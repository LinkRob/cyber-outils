The {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow}} is the {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} once used before he was defeated by {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> This {{Term|BotW|Bow}} belongs to the {{Term|BotW|Rito Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> It is said that {{Term|BotW|Revali}} could fire {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} from this {{Term|BotW|Bow}} with the speed of a gale, making him adept at aerial combat.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}} claims that it is too heavy for the average {{Term|BotW|Rito}} to wield, as they would end up losing all their speed.<ref>{{Cite|That thing is way too heavy to use in aerial fighting—I'd end up sacrificing all of my speed just to take a shot.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> However, he states that {{Term|BotW|Revali}}'s strength and speed allowed him to counter the detriments of the {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now, if I had both the strength and speed of Master Revali, that would be a different story.|Teba|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}} will gift {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} the {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow}} in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}} as a reward for calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Feel free to take what you find in that treasure chest. You will get more use out of it than I.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> This is done at the suggestion of {{Term|BotW|Teba}}, who was impressed by how {{Term|BotW|Link}} handled his own {{Term|BotW|Bow}} against the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But seeing the way you handled your bow against {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}, I suggested we let you borrow the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Eagle Bow}}.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> It can be retrieved from inside a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kaneli's House|link}}.

If the {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow}} breaks or becomes irreversibly lost, {{Term|BotW|Harth|link}} can make a new {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow}} for {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he provides a {{Term|BotW|Swallow Bow|link}}, five bundles of {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you break or lose the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Eagle Bow}}, I can make a new one for you. But you will need to bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|swallow bow}}, {{Color|BotW Red|five}} bundles of {{Color|BotW Blue|wood}}, and a {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}}.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> If it was dropped somewhere but can still be reobtained, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} will instead recommend trying to find it first, as it is expensive to make.<ref>{{Cite|Where is your {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Eagle Bow}}? Did you drop it somewhere? I can make you another one, but they are expensive to make. Maybe you should try to find your old one first.|Harth|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow}} is a powerful {{Term|BotW|Bow}} for aerial combat that can fire three {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} at once, similar to the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Bow|linl}} and its counterparts.  It also contains the Quick Draw upgrade as a hidden feature. Being made of wood, it will burn if it touches {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or is equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. It will also float if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and it will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. Finally, the {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow}} cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Great Eagle Bow}} is wielded by the {{Term|HWAoC|Rito|link}} {{Term|HWAoC|Champions|link|display=Champion}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali|link}}. He wields it in several cutscenes. For example, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} uses the {{Term|HWAoC|Great Eagle Bow}} against {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}} during their first encounter. He briefly aimed it at a surge of {{Plural|HWAoC|Monster|link}} on the way back to {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Castle|link}}. {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} also used it to defend himself against {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon|link}}, which he later shot {{Plural|HWAoC|Bomb Arrow|link}}. Finally, he opened fire with it to protect {{Term|HWAoC|Teba|link}} from a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Skywatcher|link}}.
