{{Term/Store|Harker Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Harker Lake'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Harker Lake is located in the [[Faron Grasslands]]. It lies north of the [[Zonai Ruins]], and adjacent to the [[Damel Forest]]. This small finger-shaped lake has two [[Blue Lizalfos]] patrolling its waters. To its south shore is a [[Hearty Durian]] fruit tree, with another near its westernmost shore.
