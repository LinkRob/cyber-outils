{{Term/Store|Rat|Rats|Series, ALttP, TWW, TWWHD, FSA, TP, TPHD, PH, ST, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 200 {{Exp Game|ALttP, TWW, TWWHD, FSA, TP, TPHD, PH, ST, ALBW, TFH}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} They are shadow-dwelling creatures, preferring dank, maze-like sewers, holes in walls, and dark rooms.

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Rat}} can be found in the {{Term|ALttP|Castle Dungeon|link}} below {{Term|ALttP|Hyrule Castle|link}} while {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} is escorting {{Term|ALttP|Princess Zelda|link}} to the {{Term|ALttP|Sanctuary|link}}. They can also be found in various locations around {{Term|ALttP|Hyrule|link}}.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Rat}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= It's a '''Rat'''! Don't let it hit you or you will drop Rupees! Tingle loves Rupees!
}}

In {{TWW}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Rat}} can steal {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}} when attacking {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}, dropping them upon their defeat. If {{Plural|TWWHD|Bomb Flower|link}} are found in the same room, {{Plural|TWWHD|Rat}} will pick them up and toss them at {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}. These {{Plural|TWWHD|Rat}} are known as {{Plural|TWWHD|Real Bombchu|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|These creatures attack spontaneously and attempt to steal Rupees. The ones that carry bombs around are apparently known as Bombchu.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> If {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} throws {{Term|TWWHD|All-Purpose Bait|link}} at their nests, they will sell different items to him, such as {{Plural|TWWHD|Arrow|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Bomb|link}}, [[Blue Potion]], [[Red Potion]], All-Purpose Bait, and [[Hyoi Pear]].<ref>{{Cite|If you spread bait near their nest, they may share their store of treasure with you.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref>

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Plural|FSA|Rat}} are found in various levels in {{FSA}} and can be easily defeated by the {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}}. They drop {{Plural|FSA|Force Gem|link}} upon their deaths. The {{Plural|FSA|Real Bombchu|link}} also appear.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, {{Plural|TPHD|Rat}} can sometimes be found gnawing on {{Plural|TPHD|Skull|link}}. Another type of {{Plural|TPHD|Rat}}, called {{Plural|TPHD|Ghoul Rat|link}}, cling onto {{Term|TPHD|Link}}, preventing him from running and jumping off platforms. When they cling to {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}} will become uneasy, looking down nervously at the {{Plural|TPHD|Ghoul Rat}} covering {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s body. {{Plural|TPHD|Ghoul Rat}} can only be seen and destroyed when using {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s wolf senses. A {{Term|TPHD|Spin Attack|link}} can be used to remove all at once.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, {{Plural|PH|Rat}} usually appear from small holes in walls. They generally act as simple obstacles and are easily defeated in one hit.

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, {{Plural|ST|Rat}} sometimes carry a {{Term|ST|Small Key|link}}, so {{Term|ST|Link}} must defeat them in order to obtain it. If one gets near a {{Term|ST|Phantom|link}} possessed by {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}}, she will scream and cower, not being able to move again until {{Term|ST|Link}} defeats it. During the first phase of the fight with {{Term|ST|Malladus|link}}, {{Term|ST|Chancellor Cole|link}} attacks {{Term|ST|Phantom Zelda|link}} with ghostly {{Plural|ST|Rat}} that tend to attack her. If one of these {{Plural|ST|Rat}} hits {{Term|ST|Zelda}}, {{Term|ST|Chancellor Cole}} will take control of her, forcing {{Term|ST|Zelda}} to attack Link.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Rat}} appear in the Secret Passage that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} uses to get inside the {{Term|ALBW|Sanctuary}} after {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} locks the doors. {{Plural|ALBW|Dark Rat|link}}, a stronger variation of {{Term|ALBW|Rat}}, also appear mostly in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, there are two varieties of {{Plural|TFH|Rat}}; however, they have no difference except for their color. Regular grey {{Plural|TFH|Rat}} are generally found in {{Term|TFH|The Ruins|link}}, attacking the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} normally. {{Plural|TFH|Blue Rat|link}} tend to appear in the {{Term|TFH|Ice Cavern|link}}, and sometimes hide inside {{Plural|TFH|Snowball|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
A Rat appears in the reveal trailer for {{TotK}} where it was about to be consumed by {{Term|TotK|Gloom|link}}. They do not appear in the final game, however.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{FPTRR|-}}===
In {{FPTRR}}, {{Plural|FPTRR|Rat}} appear in different varieties.

[[File:FPTRR Shiyou Rat Sprite.png|left|30px]]
'''Shiyou {{Plural|FPTRR|Rat}}''' are the weakest and most common variety, found in {{Term|FPTRR|Hometown Prairie|link}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Sunshine Seashore|link}}, and the {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Hideaway|link}}. They are not aggressive and tend to avoid {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} if he goes near them. They are the weakest enemy in the game and regularly drop {{Term|FPTRR|Bone|link}}. One of the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate|link}} owns a pet {{Term|FPTRR|Rat}} called Ratty.

[[File:Big Green Rat.png|left]]
'''Chu {{Plural|FPTRR|Rat}}''' are larger, stronger versions of Shiyou {{Plural|FPTRR|Rat}} and also appear in {{Term|FPTRR|Hometown Prairie}} and {{Term|FPTRR|Sunshine Seashore}}. They are much more aggressive than the smaller variety and squeak and charge at {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} when they see him.

[[File:FPTRR Rotten Rat Sprite.png|left]]
'''Rotten {{Plural|FPTRR|Rat}}''' are found only in the {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Hideaway}}. They are dark purple in color, aggressive, and much stronger than any other kind of {{Term|FPTRR|Rat}}. They also only drop {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}} when defeated.

[[File:FPTRR Gold Rat Sprite.png|left]]
'''Gold {{Plural|FPTRR|Rat}}''' are rarely found at the {{Term|FPTRR|Hometown Prairie}} in place of Shiyou {{Plural|FPTRR|Rat}}. As with all the rare gold enemies, they drop a large number of {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}} when defeated.
{{Noncanon|End}}
