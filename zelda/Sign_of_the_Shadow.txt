"{{Term/Store|Sign of the Shadow||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Sign of the Shadow}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Gerudo Tower}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the top of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Tower|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} encounters {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} playing his accordion.<ref>{{Cite|♪Hmmm hm hmmm...♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will be deep in thought with himself.<ref>{{Cite|What if... No, but then...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> However, when he notices {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he apologizes and explains he was busy with a song his teacher, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet|link}}, wrote for the fallen hero.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm? Mm? Ah, forgive me. I was lost in a song written by my late teacher. He wrote it for the hero who fell to the calamity 100 years ago...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} was always hopeful that the hero would return, even after so long.<ref>{{Cite|So much time has passed, and yet the hero has not yet returned. But my teacher always kept faith.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Someday, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} wishes to be able to perform the song for the hero, and so he continues to practice.<ref>{{Cite|I hope to play the song for the hero someday, so I practice it often.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

Realizing he has been talking for a while, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to hear an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" pertaining to the area.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, forgive me. It seems I've prattled on... I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to listen, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will begin to sing.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's hear it.] Excellent! Without further ado...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics describe shooting an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} towards the sky when the sun is to the northwest.<ref>{{Cite|As light shines from the northwest skies,<br/>From the tower’s shadow an arrow flies.<br/>Pierce heaven’s light to reveal {{Color|BotW Red|the prize}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Kass}} does not understand the meaning behind them, he knows his teacher left a secret in the song.<ref>{{Cite|I still don't quite understand the lyrics, but knowing my teacher, they're the key to revealing a secret.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} luck on his path.<ref>{{Cite|Best of luck, and may the light illuminate your path.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} begins.

Southeast of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Tower}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}, which he must stand on. Once the sun or moon passes behind the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Tower|display=Tower}}, he must shoot an {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} towards it. This will cause {{Term|BotW|Sasa Kai Shrine|link}} to rise from the ground,<ref>{{Cite|When the light was in the northwest, you saw Gerudo Tower cast a shadow upon a pedestal. After firing an arrow toward the light from the top of that pedestal, a shrine appeared!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
