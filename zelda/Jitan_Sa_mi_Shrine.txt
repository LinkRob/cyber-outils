The {{Term/Store|Jitan Sa'mi Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Jitan Sa'mi Shrine}}<br/>Jitan Sa'mi's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Jitan Sa'mi's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The Jitan Sa'mi Shrine is located behind the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|link}} on top of {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}. It's accessed as part of the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Spring of Wisdom|link}}". {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} receives the Quest by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Medda|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, sharing his belief that since the {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link|display=princess}} endured hardship in the Spring, then the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} must have left a treasure there.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Spring of Wisdom}} the princess of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule}} used for purification is said to be somewhere on that mountain. A special fountain? That the princess had to endure hardship to visit? I bet the royal family left a treasure there!|Medda|BotW}}</ref>

When Link arrives at the Spring of Wisdom, he will find {{Term|BotW|Naydra|link}} infected by {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found no treasure but instead discovered {{Color|BotW Red|Naydra}}, the Spring of Wisdom's guardian spirit, who has been overtaken by darkness.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> The Spring's {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} asks Link to purify Naydra.<ref>{{Cite|You who have received the Spirit Orbs... Free {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra}} from this Malice. Show what your power can achieve!|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> Link must follow Naydra as it flies throughout Mount Lanayru, shooting at the glowing eyes of Malice.

After Link saves Naydra, the Goddess Statue will encourage him to shoot one final {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} at the dragon.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to your efforts, {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra}}, the spirit of Mount Lanayru, has been freed from the grips of an {{Color|BotW Red|evil power}}. But a single ceremony remains. Now... Loose your arrow through the body of {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra}} to free the spirit of this region!|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> Naydra will take off once shot, dropping one of its {{Term|BotW|Naydra's Scale|link|display=scales}} in front of Link.<ref>{{Cite|This is the spirit {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra's scale}}. It fell when your arrow struck. It serves as proof of the courage you received from the one who served the spring since ancient times.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> The Statue encourages Link to offer the Scale to the Spring,<ref>{{Cite|Come... Offer a {{Color|BotW Blue|scale}} of the blue spirit {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra}} to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Wisdom}}.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> which will open the door to Jitam Sa'mi Shrine.<ref>{{Cite|Your path has shown itself. Now go forth.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref>

This completes the Shrine Quest. As Link enters the Shrine, he is greeted by the Shrine's {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Jitan Sa'mi|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the Shrine is proof of Link's worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Jitan Sa'mi. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Jitan Sa'mi|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Jitan Sa'mi Shrine features no puzzles, simply a direct path to Jitan Sa'mi. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Frostspear|link}}. As Link approaches Jitan Sa'mi, he will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Jitan Sa'mi|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Jitan Sa'mi|BotW}}</ref>
