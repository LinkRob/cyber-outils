{{Term/Store|Flurris||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Flurris}} is a member of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}}, and can be found at the third floor of their {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link|display=Home}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} accesses the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} via the {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)|link}} in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} (a result of {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Strato|link}}), she will be blocking the stairway leading up to the fourth floor and roof.

Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} arrives at the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} via the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Flurris}} will have moved aside, giving him access to the fourth floor where {{Term|TMC|Siroc|link}} resides. She will also {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with him,<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? I hope they fit!|Flurris|TMC}}</ref> resulting in a {{Term|TMC|Golden Rope|link}} appearing in {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}.
