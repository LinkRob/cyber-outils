The {{Term/Store|Shadow Crystal|Shadow Crystals|Series, TP, TPHD}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Nintendo|page= 192}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
After completing the [[Lakebed Temple]], [[Link]] and [[Midna]] arrive at [[Lanayru (Spirit)|Lanayru]]'s [[Spirit Spring]], where they are confronted by [[Zant]]. Zant transforms Link into his [[Wolf Link|Wolf]] form and takes the [[Fused Shadow]]s from Midna. Zant summons the Shadow Crystal to display his power. In response, Wolf Link attacks Zant, only to be repelled and have the crystal forced into his forehead, leaving Link trapped in his Wolf form.

Deep within the [[Sacred Grove]] lies the [[Master Sword]], which Link and Midna find after following the [[Skull Kid]]. As Link approaches the [[Sword]], he transforms back into his human form. This breaks the curse of the Shadow Crystal, and it coalesces in Midna's hand.<ref>{{Cite|The sword accepted you as its master... This thing is the embodiment of the evil magic that Zant cast on you. It's definitely different from our tribe's shadow magic...|Midna|TP}}</ref>

By using the Shadow Crystal, Link gains the ability to transform into his Wolf form at will,<ref>{{Cite|Careful... If you touch it, you'll turn back into a beast! This thing is too dangerous... It's probably for the best if we just leave it here, huh? But on the other hand...if we kept it, you'd be able to transform into a beast anytime you wanted... Yes, since Zant was kind enough to give this to us, we should be thankful and use it all we can! If you need it, just call me. I want to keep a low profile, so I'll hide in your shadow when you're human, but I can change you whenever. You can be a wolf anytime you like!|Midna|TP}}</ref> as well as use Twilight [[Portal (Object)|Portal]]s to [[warp]] anywhere.<ref>{{Cite|Also, thanks to this thing, you can warp whenever you want by switching into wolf form...|Midna|TP}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Ambig|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
The Shadow Crystal makes an appearance in the [[Scenario]] "[[Her True Self]]". It was used to transform Midna from her cursed imp form into [[Twili Midna]] before helping Linkle save [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] in the [[Palace of Twilight (Hyrule Warriors)|Palace of Twilight]]. After the battle was won, the darkness of the [[Twilight]] continued to spread and, to prevent it from spreading further, Midna smashed the Crystal to release enough energy to contain the darkness, causing her to return to her cursed imp form.
{{Noncanon|End}}
