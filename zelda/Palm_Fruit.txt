==Locations and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Palm Fruit}}
{{Term|BotW|Palm Fruit}} can be found on {{Plural|BotW|Palm Tree|link}} in the [[Gerudo Desert]] and on the coast of [[East Necluda]]. They're also sold in the [[Kara Kara Bazaar]] by [[Emri]] as well as by the travelling merchant [[Chumin]]. Red {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} also sometimes drop Palm Fruits upon their death.

Palm Fruits can be eaten raw to restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}, but when it comes in contact with fire, it will turn into {{Term|BotW|Baked Palm Fruit|link}}, which heals for one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart}}.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> When Palm Fruit is cooked into a dish, it will increase the heart recovery provided.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, Palm Fruit can be used to make Light Yellow Dye.

When used in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, Palm Fruits are one of the many fruits that can make [[Fruit Pie]], [[Fruitcake]], [[Honeyed Fruits]], [[Simmered Fruit]], [[Steamed Fruit]], [[Fruit and Mushroom Mix]], and [[Copious Simmered Fruit]].
