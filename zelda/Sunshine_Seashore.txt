{{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Sunshine Seashore||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a location in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sunshine Seashore|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
This is the first area of the game where [[Tingle]] will encounter enemies which are beyond his capability of safely defeating alone. There is a [[Bodyguard Salon]] where he can hire [[Bodyguard]]s to help defend him against stronger enemies. Inside the oysters commonly found here, Tingle can find valuable [[Snatched Pearl]]s.

Once Tingle meets all of the [[Bridge Builders]], he can return here to find them building a bridge to a small island in an attempt to vie for the favor of their father, the foreman. Once the bridge is complete, Tingle can speak with the foreman to have the bridge builders return all the Rupees which Tingle paid them. The foreman will also give Tingle the [[Rupee Good#Pickaxe|Pickaxe]] as an apology for his children's behavior.
