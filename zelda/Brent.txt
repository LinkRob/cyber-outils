{{Term/Store|Brent||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|The Carpenters}}
{{Term|TMC|Brent}} is a {{Term|TMC|Carpenter|link}} that first appears alongside the other {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter}} repairing the northern entrance to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} is turned to stone by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}. The {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter}} are later found repairing an area in the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}. He constantly comments on his busyness.<ref>{{Cite|I’m so busy! So busy! Gotta finish here, so I can go repair the steps next!|Brent|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Brent}} can be found in the {{Term|TMC|Carpenters Shop|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} for the remainder of the game. He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? I hope they fit!|Brent|TMC}}</ref>
