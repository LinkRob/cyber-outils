The {{Term/Store|Swordsman's Scroll 2||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Swordsman's Scroll 2 can be obtained by collecting all 20 Stamps. When Link shows the completed [[Stamp Book]] to the elderly [[Niko]], he will be awarded the scroll. Once obtained, Link will be enabled to use the Great Spin Attack.
