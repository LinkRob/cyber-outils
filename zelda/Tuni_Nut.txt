The {{Term/Store|Tuni Nut||Series, OoA}} is an [[Item]] in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|OoA|Tuni Nut|This nut is the symbol of Symmetry City — a city found in a distant corner of the world. If anything happens to this nut, the city will fall into a state of severe imbalance.}}
During the past, [[Veran]] cracks the Tuni Nut, making [[Symmetry Village]] fall into a state of imbalance.<ref name= "Manual"/> When [[Link]] attempts to reach to [[Symmetry City]] during the present, he instead finds the [[Symmetry City Ruins]]. After retrieving the [[Cracked Tuni Nut]] from the past, Link proceeds to restore it with [[Patch]]'s [[Patch's Crazy Cart|ceremony]]. After placing the restored Tuni Nut on its pedestal inside the [[Middle House]], Symmetry City is also restored.

==Nomenclature==
Tuni Nut is a {{Wp|palindrome}}, fitting with the theme of Symmetry Village. This is also true of its Japanese name when written phonetically with Hiragana. "Tu" also sounds like "two", and "ni" means "two" in Japanese, making the nut's name two twos, which could also refer to the town's bilateral symmetry.
