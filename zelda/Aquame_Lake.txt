{{Term/Store|Aquame Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Aquame Lake'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Aquame Lake is located in [[Hyrule Field]]. It is fed by the [[Regencia River]] to the north and partly surrounds the [[Coliseum Ruins]]. A stone bridge, the [[Aquame Bridge]], crosses over the [[Lake]] from the north and connects to the Coliseum Ruins. Close to the Lake's eastern shore are the ruins of a dilapidated village.

In the waters near the point where the Regencia River meets Aquame Lake, [[Link]] can find a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing an [[Opal]].

A [[Korok]] can be found by lifting a [[Rock]] on the cliff just west of the Aquame Bridge.
