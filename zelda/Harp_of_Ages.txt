The {{Term/Store|Harp of Ages||Series}} is an {{Term|OoA|Item|link}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 126}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Harp of Ages|Use this harp to move back and forth between the present and the past. Playing tunes on the harp allows you to travel through time.}}
The Harp of Ages is a mythical instrument that belonged to [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]], the Oracle of Ages. [[Link]] finds the Harp in a secret room of Nayru's house where an imprint of Nayru teaches him the "[[Tune of Echoes]]". This apparition hoped that a hero had found the Harp and would use it to save the land. The Harp of Ages allows Link to control the currents of time to [[Warping|Warp]] between the past and present ages of [[Labrynna]] by playing certain tunes on it. The three tunes Link learns to play on the Harp are the [[Tune of Echoes]], the [[Tune of Currents]] and the [[Tune of Ages]]. Playing any tune on the Harp of Ages will also defeat any [[Pols Voice]] on the screen. If Link attempts to travel through time in certain areas, he will be sent back "by a strange force."<ref>{{Cite|You were sent back by a strange force!|N/A|OoA}}</ref> These areas are [[Yoll Graveyard]], the [[Sea of No Return]] and the [[Black Tower]] grounds. He also cannot travel through time inside dungeons. This exists to prevent [[wikipedia:Sequence breaking|sequence breaking]].

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{OoA|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{OoA|Himekawa}}, the Harp of Ages is first used by Nayru. The Oracle is seen strumming the Harp within a glade, but stops once she senses light and darkness approaching together.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Light and darkness approach together...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 24}}</ref> Nayru uses the Harp of Ages to see Link's future, telling the hero that he will be remembered throughout time by generations.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Your story is told... Generation after generation... For centuries... Passed down...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 26}}</ref> [[Veran]], who was possessing [[Impa]], instead takes control of Nayru and uses her to enter [[Labrynna]]'s past in an effort to erase Link.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Now... To the past... In order to erase you, Link!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 30}}</ref> The Harp of Ages is left behind.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= All that's left... Is this harp.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 41}}</ref> [[Ralph]] fights Link for the instrument, as he wants to use it to rescue Nayru.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I can't leave the Harp of Ages to a pipsqueak like you! I'm gonna save Nayru! Now give it to me!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 41}}</ref> Ralph unintentionally strums the Harp, allowing Link to travel back in time without him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Wait right here. I'll be back with Nayru! Buh-Bye!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 43}}</ref> 

While Link fought against [[Queen Ambi]]'s soldiers, one of the knights took the Harp of Ages from Link and used it to pay his bar tab.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... A soldier took it from Link, then used it to pay his bar tab that night...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 85}}</ref> The owner of the bar sold it to a merchant, who gave it to an aristocrat.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The owner of the tavern sold it to a rich merchant... And from the merchant it went to an aristocrat...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 85}}</ref> Since then, the Harp was passed on for centuries until it was in a [[Tokay]]'s shop in present day Labrynna.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... And passed from hand to hand across the centuries... to present day.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 85, 86}}</ref> Ralph happens to be at the shop searching for a time travel device and recognizes the Harp of Ages.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= A time travel device? I don't know... How about this? It's hundreds of years old, but good as new! It's defied the ravages of time and takes you back to... I something wrong, Sir?|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 86}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= IT'S A MIRACLE! I'M COMING, NAYRU!!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 86}}</ref> He uses the Harp to enter Labrynna's past, landing on top of Link.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link! If you're here... I must have made it to the past.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 98}}</ref> Ralph sees his friend Nayru and rushes to her,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Nayru! Is that Nayru?!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 97}}</ref> unaware that she is still possessed by Veran. Link thrusts his hand out to stop Ralph and accidentally strums the Harp of Ages, which transports the two onto a deserted island. Link and Ralph are brought aboard the [[Cap'n]]'s ship, who orders Ralph to play the Harp of Ages.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= A harp! Play me a song!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 107}}</ref> Ralph plays a {{Wp|waltz}} for the Captain and is surprised that the Harp of Ages is not causing a time trip.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Oh! A waltz! A rondo!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 108}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Huh?! The harp isn't causing a time trip?|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 108}}</ref> Eventually Ralph and Link leave the pirate ship and use the Harp of Ages to return to Labrynna.

After Queen Ambi has been possessed by Veran, the newly freed Nayru sends Link, Ralph and [[Sir Raven]] to Labrynna's present. After forming a plan to defeat Veran, the Oracle of Ages uses the Harp of Ages to return to Labrynna's past. Nayru reveals that [[Roperi]]'s plant is a [[Mystery Tree]] sprout, which will produce Veran's weakness, [[Mystery Seed]]s.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... Demons that posses people can't stand Mystery Seeds.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 123}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link, this is a young Mystery Tree. It will grow Mystery Seeds.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 154}}</ref> Using the Harp of Ages, Nayru speeds the sprout's growth and gives the seed to Sir Raven and Link to defeat Veran.

At the manga's end, Nayru uses the Harp of Ages to return Link, Ralph and herself to modern day Labrynna.
{{Noncanon|End}}
