"{{Term/Store|The Crowned Beast||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Crowned Beast}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Rabia Plain}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
On {{Term|BotW|Rabia Plain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} singing about a "crown of bone" near a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Crown of bone...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He is shocked upon being interrupted,<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Pardon me! I didn't think anyone was around...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> but his demeanor becomes friendly once he recognizes {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, I knew we'd meet again!<br/>Though, I never imagined it would be here...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that he knows an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" about the area,<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to listen.<ref>{{Cite|Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to hear the song, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will sing it for him.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's hear it.] Excellent! Without further ado...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics of {{Term|BotW|Kass}}'s song describe placing a beast with a bone crown upon a throne, which will reveal an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A beast that wears a crown of bone,<br/>Prancing through the lush green.<br/>Mount the beast upon its throne,<br/>For only then the shrine is seen.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Kass}} believes that the area is correct, he does not know what kind of creature wears a "crown of bone".<ref>{{Cite|I suppose "the lush green" could refer to this place. But what sort of beast wears a "crown of bone"?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He knows there is a secret, but he is not sure he can solve it.<ref>{{Cite|There's definitely a secret hiding here, but it's beyond me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well on his journey,<ref>{{Cite|May the light illuminate your path.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} begins.

To unearth the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs to successfully mount and tame a {{Term|BotW|Mountain Buck|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The beast wearing a crown of bone was a mountain buck.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> These {{Plural|BotW|Deer|link}} can be found throughout {{Term|BotW|Rabia Plain}}, but {{Term|BotW|Link}} must have a high {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} level in order to approach one without it running away. After successfully taming a {{Term|BotW|Mountain Buck}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ride it onto the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}, which will activate it, and {{Term|BotW|Mezza Lo Shrine|link}} to emerge from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|As the song instructed, being atop a mountain buck while it stood on the pedestal caused the shrine to appear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
