'''[[wikipedia:Cat|Cats]]''' are recurring {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 64 {{Exp Game|Series, TMC, TP, TPHD}}}}</ref> They commonly appear in towns or other populated areas. They make their first appearance in {{OoT}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT|-}}, the one and only cat in-game can be found at [[Granny's Potion Shop]], being pet by [[Granny]] herself.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Main|Mittens}}
In {{OoS}}, a cat named {{Term|OoS|Mittens|link}} plays a part in the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}, where [[Link]] must lure her down from a {{Term|OoS|Tree|link}} with a {{Term|OoS|Fish|link}}. In return, Link will get the {{Term|OoS|Megaphone|link}} from her owner, the {{Term|OoS|Old Man (Oracle of Seasons)|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Scratcher, Purry}}
Cats in {{TMC}} will attack Link while he is {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized. They are most prevalent around {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
[[File:TP Link (Cat) Carrying Greengill Screenshot.jpg|thumb|[[Link (Cat)|Link the Cat]] carrying a Fish]]
In {{TP}}, many cats appear throughout {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}}. They will pounce and attack nearby {{Plural|TPHD|Cucco|link}}. [[Link (Cat)|One particular cat]] is owned by {{Term|TPHD|Sera|link}} in {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}} and is named after [[Link]].{{Fact}} In order to buy the {{Term|TPHD|Slingshot|link}} from {{Term|TPHD|Sera's Sundries|link}}, Link must catch a {{Term|TPHD|Fish|link}} for Link the Cat. This is due to the cat running away after being scolded for eating a {{Term|TPHD|Fish}} intended for the family's dinner.<ref>{{Cite|You... You didn't happen to see my little {{Color|TPHD Red|cat}} out there, did you? He ate the fish we were going to have for supper last night, and I gave him a good scolding...but then he went out and hasn't returned... I'm so fraught with worry for him... I've exhausted myself...|Sera|TPHD}}</ref> {{Term|TPHD|Sera}}, exhausted from worry, is unable to run her shop while her Cat was missing.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhhh... I'm terribly sorry...but my mind is so filled with thoughts of my little cat that I simply can't deal with customers right now...|Sera|TPHD}}</ref> After a {{Term|TPHD|Fish}} is given to {{Term|TPHD|Link (Cat)}} the Cat, he returns to {{Term|TPHD|Sera's Sundries}}, allowing the hero to shop at {{Term|TPHD|Sera's Sundries}} again.

A group of cats gather around {{Term|TPHD|Jovani|link}}'s house in {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle Town|link}} that can be seen playing with balls. The leader of these cats is {{Term|TPHD|Jovani}}'s Cat, {{Term|TPHD|Gengle|link}}. When {{Term|TPHD|Jovani}} sold his soul to a {{Term|TPHD|Poe|link}} and turned into gold, {{Term|TPHD|Gengle}} was also cursed and turned to gold, petrifying him to his owner's head.<ref>{{Cite|I can't move... I can't go see my girlfriend... My pet cat {{Color|TPHD Animal Green|Gengle}} is frozen on my head... I don't think I could be more miserable.|Jovani|TPHD}}</ref> Collecting the {{Plural|TPHD|Poe Soul|link}} of 20 or more Poes will free {{Term|TPHD|Gengle}} from his curse.<ref>{{Cite|Can you find and defeat the {{Color|TPHD Red|20 ghosts that lurk in the dark}} for me? I think if you can free the pieces of my {{Color|TPHD Red|soul}} from the {{Color|TPHD Red|ghosts}} that hold them, I'll be free...|Jovani|TPHD}}</ref> After being freed from the curse, {{Term|TPHD|Gengle}} thanks {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} and then ponders what {{Term|TPHD|Jovani}} is going to do with his money.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Animal Green|Thanks for all your help! All this money... What are we going to do with it?}}|Gengle|TPHD}}</ref>

{{Term|TPHD|Louise|link}}, a cat owned by {{Term|TPHD|Telma|link}} in {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle Town}}, assists {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} on multiple occasions. After {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}} attacks Link and {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}}, before stealing the {{Plural|TPHD|Fused Shadow|link}}, Link searches for a way to take the injured {{Term|TPHD|Midna}} to {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Louise}} helps the hero by showing him a way through the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Animal Green|Here, get in through the}} {{Color|TPHD Red|window}}{{Color|TPHD Animal Green|. After confronting {{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville}}, he tells Link that the {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Statue}} was stolen after he spilled medicine on it.<ref>{{Cite|Look, lad, if I'd been able to sell the {{Color|TPHD Red|wooden statue}} the {{Color|TPHD Red|young lady}} had, I would've been able to get the money... In the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village|link}}, twenty cats return to the village after Link defeats the {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin|link}} occupying it. The {{Term|TPHD|Cucco Leader|link}} who looks after the cats asks {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} to play {{Term|TPHD|Cat Hunt|link}} by speaking to each one of them.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Animal Green|So perhaps you could...you know...just...play with them?}} [Yes] {{Color|TPHD Animal Green|Aww, thanks so much... Cats are forgetful.}}|Cucco Leader|TPHD}}</ref> He is given a {{Term|TPHD|Piece of Heart|link}} after successfully talking to all 20 Cats.

==={{SS|-}}===
{{Main|Remlit}}
In {{SS}}, while not technically felines, cat-like creatures called Remlits roam {{Term|SS|Skyloft|link}}. At night, {{Plural|SS|Remlit}} become aggressive enemies and will seek to harm Link. A particular {{Term|SS|Remlit}} named {{Term|SS|Mia|link}} belongs to Zelda and {{Term|SS|Gaepora|link}}, her father. Link can help find the Remlit at the beginning of the game.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, cats are found in various places in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}. Many appear in {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}, but two are found outside, lounging on a bench and a roof, respectively. The cat on the bench will meow when talked to without the {{Term|TFH|Cheetah Costume|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Meowwww! Mew mew meow!|Cat|TFH}}</ref> It is to this cat that the {{Term|TFH|Beret Boy|link}} will read Volume 6 of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands Diary|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mimi lent me a book! I was so happy, I read it to a cat.|Beret Boy|TFH}}</ref> Apparently the cat enjoyed listening to it.<ref>{{Cite|The cat liked it! Do you want to hear it too?|Beret Boy|TFH}}</ref> When Link looks in the {{Term|TFH|Mirror|link}} in {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}, he can see a cat sitting behind him near the rack of clothes. Strangely, however, it can only be seen in this {{Term|TFH|Mirror}}, and cannot be approached. If Link wears the [[Cheetah Costume]], he is able to understand the language of Cats.<ref>{{Cite|Perhaps if I wore a catlike outfit, I could understand their ways! Hmm...|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TFH|publisher= Prima|edition= Collector's Edition|page= 227}}</ref>

Cats that sit outside on the bench west of {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's}} will tell Link that the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} adjusts his prices whenever he is in a good mood,<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Hello, meow! I'm a cat! You've probably noticed that already, meow. The human princess always knew just how to make us cats purr purr purrr. Le sigh, meow. Hey! Since you're trying to help my best human, I'll give you some advice! The Street Merchant is super finicky, meow. When he's in a good mood, he'll pay more for the same item. It's easy to tell when he's in high spirits if you chat with him. Hope that helps, meow!|Cat|TFH}}</ref> offering double for buying Link's {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}}. These cats also emulate {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture|link}}'s habit of slipping into a mix of English and French. The cat sitting on the couch facing the dressing room claims to be {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}}'s guardian, as well as the guardian of the store owner's secrets, freely gossiping about {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}}'s relationship with her sister, [[Lady Maud|Lady Muad]], the main antagonist.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, stranger! Gimme some fish! Just kidding, nya ha. Wait a minute... Do you actually understand what I'm saying?! That's amazing, meow! I'm the guardian of this store. ==={{EoW|-}}===
[[File:EoW Cats.png|thumb|The many colors of cats in {{EoW|-}}]]
In {{EoW|-}}, many cats can be found across Hyrule, however most can be found at major settlements such as Kakariko. Like the game in general, cats have a rather "chibi" appearance.

After acquiring cat cloths, Princess Zelda can talk to any of the cats across Hyrule to acquire gossip.

==Trivia==
*The balls which cats in {{TP}} play with use the same model as the {{Plural|TPHD|Bomb|link}} that {{Plural|TPHD|Bomskit|link}} drop.
*Humorously, in {{TWW}}, during the [[Side Quest|sidequest]] in [[Windfall Island]] where Link quietly follows [[Mila]], whenever she says "...''Who is that?''", the young hero [http://noproblo.dayjo.org/ZeldaSounds/WW_New/WW_Link_Meow.wav meows like a cat], before progressing further. This has become a popular meme among fans of the series.
