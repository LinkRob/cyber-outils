==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Highland Sheep}}
Highland Sheep are often found in pastures with {{Plural|BotW|Hateno Cow|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|White Goat|link}}, particularly at [[Hateno Pasture]] and at certain {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}}. They are herbivorous and will eat certain [[Material]]s, such as herbs, fruits, and vegetables, if they are nearby. Their wool is harvested to make clothing and bedding.<ref>{{Cite|Once the fluffy wool grows out, it's harvested and used to make clothing and bedding.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>
