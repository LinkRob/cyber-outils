The '''Steel Trap''' is an item from {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Steel Trap|A Steel Trap with sharp, pointy teeth. It doesn't work anymore and is too dangerous to use.<ref name="Steel Trap">{{Cite|A Steel Trap with sharp, pointy teeth. It doesn't work anymore and is too dangerous to use.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Steel Trap is the reward for completing [[Acorn Gathering]] in under 40 seconds. It is one of the items required to make the [[High-Teku Baba]] [[Automaton]] and to complete the [[Endless Stomach!]] [[Side Quest]].
