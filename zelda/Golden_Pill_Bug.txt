==Location and Uses==
[[File:Pill Bug (M).png|left]]
[[File:Pill Bug (F).png|left|47px]]
The male Pill Bug can be found southeast of the bridge in the center of [[Kakariko Gorge]]. The female Pill Bug can be found among tall grass in the northwest of Kakariko Gorge. They can be given to [[Agitha]] in exchange for different items, such as a bigger [[Wallet]], [[Rupee]]s, or her [[Miiverse Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}. If collected a second time, they will drop a [[Heart]].
