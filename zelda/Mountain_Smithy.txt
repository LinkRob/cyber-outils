The {{Term/Store|Mountain Smithy||Series, MM}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mountain Smithy ahead. We sharpen any sword. We work hard so your sword works hard for you!|Sign|MM}}</ref>

==Features and overview==
[[File:Mountain Smithy exterior.jpg|thumb|left|Link facing the outside of the Mountain Smithy in {{MM|-}}]]
Located at [[Mountain Village]], the Smithy is run by [[Zubora]] and [[Gabora]], both local blacksmiths. Here, they charge a fee of 100 [[Rupee]]s to upgrade and sharpen [[Sword]]s.<ref name=sword>{{Cite|All right...To reforge your sword, it will cost you 100 Rupees. It'll be ready at sunrise. You'll have to let us hold onto your sword until then. Keep in mind that after you use your reforged sword 100 times, it will lose its edge and it'll be back to its original sharpness... So, would you like your sword reforged for 100 Rupees?|Zubora|MM}}</ref>

When [[Link]] first visits the Smithy, Zubora and Gabora are not able to perform any forging due to the hearth being frozen over, which is caused by the abnormal winter that has overcome [[Snowhead]].<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, we're not doing any business right now. It's because of this abnormal cold snap we've been having. See? Our hearth has been frozen over!|Zubora|MM}}</ref> When Link defeats [[Goht]] inside the [[Snowhead Temple]], spring returns to the area, unfreezing the hearth and allowing Zubora and Gabora to start working again. Alternatively, the ice blocking the hearth can also be melted by shooting a [[Fire Arrow]] at it or by pouring some [[Hot Spring Water]] on it.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You've done me a great favor! Now, then... This hearth can finally go to work for me! Heh-heh-heh-heh... Thanks.|Zubora|MM}}</ref>

With the hearth active again, Link can upgrade the [[Kokiri Sword]] to the [[Razor Sword]] by paying a fee of 100 Rupees; however, since the process takes a whole day, Link will have to leave his sword with the duo until then. Oddly enough, he can give the smith his sword as late as a second before the dawn of the 2nd and 3rd days to get it upgraded.<ref name=sword/> Once Link receives the Razor Sword, he can bring a [[Bottle]] of [[Gold Dust]] to have the sword upgraded for free to its maximum capability: the [[Gilded Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|Why, if it isn't gold dust! And it's even top quality!!! Why, even if I use it to reforge your sword, there'll still be some left... All right! Just for you, I'll do this for free. But don't tell anyone!|Zubora|MM}}</ref> The process of upgrading the Kokiri Sword to the Gilded Sword has to be done in the same three-day cycle since playing the "[[Song of Time]]" will cause the Razor Sword to revert to the Kokiri Sword; moreover, it has to be started on the [[First Day]] since Zubora and Gabora do not take any orders on the [[Final Day]].<ref>{{Cite|Sorry...It would take until tomorrow morning to sharpen your sword, and we're not taking any more orders today. ...Why, you ask? Because tomorrow is the town's carnival, and you can't work during the carnival, can you?|Zubora|MM}}</ref>
