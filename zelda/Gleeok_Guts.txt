==Overview==
{{Term|TotK|Link|link}} meets a couple named Malena and Platt near a quarry in {{Term|TotK|Gerudo Canyon|link}}. Malena's husband fell ill and assumes that the medicinal properties of a {{Term|TotK|Gleeok Guts|link}} could cure him. But as {{Plural|TotK|Gleeok|link}} are fearsome monsters, she has trouble steeling herself to bring one down.

Link eventually bring Malena a Gleeok Guts, to her relief. Using it, she was able to create a medicine and cure her husband. She thanked Link by giving him a {{Term|TotK|Gold Rupee|link}} as a reward, then the two headed out.
