==Biography==
Moe is the name of a [[Moblin]] who lives in the [[Forsaken Fortress]].<ref name="Letter"/> While [[Maggie (The Wind Waker)|Maggie]] was held captive there, she had met Moe, who apparently liked her enough to give her many [[Skull Necklace]]s.<ref>{{Cite|He was so kind to me in the Forsaken Fortress. That sweet boy who gave me all those expensive necklaces when I fled from that cursed island...|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> It was thanks to these gift necklaces that Maggie and her [[Maggie's Father|father]], once she escaped from the fortress, were able to become rich overnight by selling them.<ref>{{Cite|Why, she even returned with a huge load of lovely, glittering, delicious treasure. Thanks to her little incident, we've become filthy rich overnight! And I mean FILTHY!|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It was by selling these for lots of cash that my father and I were finally able to escape from poverty!|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> Although even with her newly found lavish lifestyle, Maggie misses Moe and grew depressed.<ref>{{Cite|And to think that I would ever be able to live in such a beautiful, opulent house... It's like a dream come true! Hmmmm... But no! NO! It's a nightmare! Without HIM by my side, it's like a rainstorm rages in my heart...|Maggie|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|When I think of my precious little Moe... Troo di loo la la! I can't sleep at night... Why doesn't he write me back?|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> Since her father will not allow her to leave the property, she requests [[Link]] to send Moe [[Maggie's Letter|her letter]] in her stead.<ref>{{Cite|My father has been so strict with me lately. He won't let me out of the house! That's why I need you to take this letter -this note to Moe- and drop it into the postbox for me. Please, troo di loo la!|Maggie|TWW}}</ref>

While Link does not actually meet Moe, he may receive [[Moblin's Letter|his letter]] to deliver to Maggie. After being kicked out of the [[Auction House]], [[Ilari]] the postman asks Link to deliver the letter for him.<ref>{{Cite|I came to deliver a letter addressed to Maggie from a Moblin named Moe, but as you saw, the girl's stubborn father won't permit me to give it to her.|Ilari|TWW}}</ref> When Maggie reads Moe's letter, it is revealed that he likes her so much that he wants to "eat her for dinner."<ref>{{Cite|This is Moe. I... like... you... ...Ma...ggie... ...so...much...that... ...I...want...to... ...eat you...for dinner.|Moblin's Letter|TWW}}</ref> Maggie interprets this as a wedding proposal, but decides to stay in [[Windfall Island|Windfall]] with her father.<ref>{{Cite|Did you hear that? Those words? This means... I mean, it must mean... Can it be...?!? Is this his marriage proposal?|Maggie|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whenever I think of my dear father, I lose my resolve to leave home...|Maggie|TWW}}</ref>
