The {{Term/Store|Dragon Sculpture||Series, SS, SSHD}} is an {{Term|SSHD|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|SSHD Gold|Dragon Sculpture}}! It is a carving of a coiled dragon made out of dazzling gold.|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|SSHD|Dragon Sculpture}} is a {{Term|SSHD|Puzzle Key|link}} found inside the {{Term|SSHD|Earth Temple|link}}. {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} maneuvers up a winding pathway of stone carved to resemble a {{Term|SSHD|Dragon|link}}; when he retrieves the {{Term|SSHD|Dragon Sculpture}} from the {{Term|SSHD|Treasure Chest|link}} at the end of the path and begins walking back down, the mouth of the {{Term|SSHD|Dragon}} releases {{Term|SSHD|Scaldera|link}} in the form of a massive {{Term|SSHD|Boulder|link}} that chases him down. As he dives aside, the {{Term|SSHD|Boulder}} continues rolling and lodges itself into the mouth of another {{Term|SSHD|Dragon}} carving, stopping the flow of {{Term|SSHD|Lava|link}} and allowing {{Term|SSHD|Link}} to access the {{Term|SSHD|Locked Door}}.
