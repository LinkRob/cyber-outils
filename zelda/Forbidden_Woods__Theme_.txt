"{{Name|Japanese|Forbidden Woods}}" is a [[Song]] in {{TWW}}.

==Overview==
"Forbidden Woods" is associated with the eponymous location, the [[Forbidden Woods]].

It is the 60th track on Disc 1 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker Original Sound Tracks]]''.

==Trivia==
*"Forbidden Woods" contains the melody that plays on the {{Term|TLoZ|Recorder|link}} in {{TLoZ}}.{{Fact}}
