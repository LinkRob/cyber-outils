==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Stamella Shroom}}
Stamella Shrooms can be found growing near trees. Due to their bright green coloration, they can difficult to spot among grass. They can also be purchased at the [[Spore Store]] in the [[Great Hyrule Forest]] or from [[Bugut]] the travelling merchant. Stamella Shrooms can be eaten as is to restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} in [[Link|Link's]] [[Life Gauge]], but can also be [[Cooking|Cooked]] into more fulfilling [[Food]]s that also restore the [[Stamina Wheel]].<ref name="Inventory"></ref> When a {{Term|BotW|Stamella Shroom}} is exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}, it turns into a {{Term|BotW|Toasty Stamella Shroom|link}}. They can be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to create Green [[Dye]].

Stamella Shrooms, as well as other mushrooms, can be used in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Fragrant Mushroom Sauté]], [[Fruit and Mushroom Mix]], [[Glazed Mushrooms]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], a [[Mushroom Omelet]], [[Mushroom Rice Balls]], [[Mushroom Risotto]], a [[Mushroom Skewer]], [[Copious Mushrooms Skewers]], [[Salt-Grilled Mushrooms]], and [[Steamed Mushrooms]].
