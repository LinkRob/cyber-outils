==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Mountain Goat}}
{{Plural|BotW|Mountain Goat}} are common {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} that primarily inhabit open fields in hilly and mountainous areas, such as [[Hyrule Ridge]] and the [[Great Hyrule Forest]]. They will flee if they see {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} or monsters, but if approached from the front they may attempt to charge the player down. They will only attempt to do so once, and will flee afterwards regardless of whether they successfully damaged Link or not. When killed, they drop {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}}.
