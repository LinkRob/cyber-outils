{{Term/Store|Ripped and Shredded||Series, BotW}} is an [[Armor]] Shop in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have the privilege of standing in {{Color|BotW Blue|Ripped and Shredded}}, my armor shop. How can I help ya?|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Ripped and Shredded is a small store in [[Goron City]] that sells Armor. It is managed by [[Rogaro]] and [[Pyle]], who stands outside the shop to direct visitors to it.<ref>{{Cite|You're a traveler? In that case, I'll take ya somewhere nice. Follow me. [...] This is it. The {{Color|BotW Blue|armor shop}}.|Pyle|BotW}}</ref> Pyle states that ever since the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} appeared, the Region has grown much hotter, and so the store has adapted in selling the fireproof [[Flamebreaker Set]].<ref>{{Cite|Recently, somethin' called the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}} showed up on {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}. Since then, it's gotten a lot hotter here. That's why we're sellin' {{Color|BotW Red|fireproof armor}}.|Pyle|BotW}}</ref> The shop's [[Armor]] is supplied by the blacksmith, [[Rohan]],<ref>{{Cite|Oh... We're actually all sold out! We'll have to get more stock from old man Rohan.|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref> and is crafted from [[Gem]]s mined in the Region.<ref>{{Cite|I recommend the {{Color|BotW Blue|flamebreaker boots}} over all else. They're made using {{Color|BotW Blue|gems}} mined from this very soil. As you'd expect, they have great {{Color|BotW Red|flame resistance}}. Give 'em a try! They're the envy of famous warriors the world over!|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref>

As with all stores, Link can also sell his items to Rogaro as well.
