'''{{SSB|-}}''' is a fighting game released for the [[Nintendo 64]]. It is the first game in the {{SSB|-}} series, followed by its sequel {{SSBM}} for the [[Nintendo GameCube]].  It features a character, stage, and item from {{TLoZ|Series}}, alongside those of other popular [[Nintendo]] series.

==Relevant to {{TLoZ|-}}==
{{SSB|-}} features [[Link]] as a playable character based on his appearance in {{OoT}}.

Link's moveset includes many attacks and items from ''The Legend of Zelda'' series, including the [[Master Sword]] for most of his smash attacks, the [[Spin Attack]] as his recovery, the [[Jump Thrust]] and [[Down Thrust]], the [[Hookshot]] as his grab, and the [[Boomerang]] and [[Bomb]]s as projectiles. Link also holds the [[Hylian Shield]], although it cannot be used to protect him, a feature that was not added until {{SSBM|-}}.

Link's costumes include the [[Kokiri Tunic]] as his main costume and the [[Goron Tunic]], [[Zora Tunic]], and a lavender tunic based on the [[Blue Ring]] palette swap in {{TLoZ}} as his alternative costumes.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Exp|赤服：２Ｐ用＆赤チーム（ゴロンの服：６４ゼルダの伝説）|Red clothing: for 2P & red team (Goron clothes: 64 The Legend of Zelda)}}<br/>{{Exp|青服：３Ｐ用＆青チーム（ゾーラの服：６４ゼルダの伝説）|Blue clothes: for 3P & blue team (Zora clothing: 64 The Legend of Zelda)}}<br/>{{Exp|薄紫服：４Ｐ用（青リング装着時：ＦＣゼルダの伝説）|Light purple clothes: for 4P (blue ring when worn: FC Legend of Zelda)}}|author= |published= |retrieved= November 29, 2015|url= http://www.nintendo.co.jp/n01/n64/software/nus_p_nalj/smash/Color.html|title= 色ちがいのひみつ|site= Nintendo Japan|type=}}</ref>

[[Hyrule Castle#Super Smash Bros.|Hyrule Castle]] appears as a stage taking place on the roof. Frequently, small tornadoes appear through the stage, throwing upward any character that gets trapped in them.

[[Heart Container]]s appear as recovery {{ssb|item}}s, removing all damage from a character.

===Triva===
*This game remains the only game in the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series where Final Destination and Battlefield cannot be played on outside Classic Mode.
* In ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'' and ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', the characters are trophies that have been brought to life, as demonstrated in their intro sequences and continue screens. This is not the case in ''Super Smash Bros.'', in which the characters are instead toys that have been brought to life by Master Hand, and the stages are dioramas.
