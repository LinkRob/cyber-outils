{{Term/Store|Mazli||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mazli|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Mazli}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} who guards the entrance to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. He carries a {{Term|BotW|Feathered Spear|link}}, and he patrols closer to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} than his fellow guard, {{Term|BotW|Gesane|link}}.

Upon meeting {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} claims that he would like to show him around, but now is not a good time.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm? Are you a traveler? I'd like to show you around, now's not really a good time.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks where he is, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} responds that he is in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, but they are not in welcoming mood.<ref>{{Cite|[Where am I?] This is Rito Village, but... Well, you could say we're not in a welcoming mood.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> To learn more, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the rest of the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}}'s inhabitants.<ref>{{Cite|Talk to the folks in the village to hear all about it.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> When asked what is going on, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} faces the sky toward {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}, explaining that it showed up a while back and shot down the {{Term|BotW|Rito}} warriors who flew up to check it out.<ref>{{Cite|Did you see the {{Color|BotW Blue|beast in the sky}}? That's {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}. It showed up a while back. The Rito warriors who flew up to check it out were shot down.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> Although it is said to have once been a divine being that protected the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}}, it does not seem very divine to him.<ref>{{Cite|They say it used to be a {{Color|BotW Blue|divine being}} that protected the village, but it doesn't seem very divine to me.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} is thankful it only shoots at anything flying in the sky, but it is still demoralizing for the {{Term|BotW|Rito}} to have to fly so low to the ground.<ref>{{Cite|Thankfully, the thing only shoots at anything flying high up above the village. But still... It is pretty demoralizing  for us Rito to have to fly so low to the ground.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} leaves, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} allows him to do so.<ref>{{Cite|Don't let me keep you.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} again, he will recognize him and state that there are no new happenings.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... It's you. Things are the same as always around here.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> After starting the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} is he knows {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} claims that {{Term|BotW|Teba}} has left {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Do you know Teba?] If you're looking for Teba, he's not in the village.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> He directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Gesane}}, who might know where {{Term|BotW|Teba}} is.<ref>{{Cite|I bet Gesane knows where Teba is. Gesane is probably patrolling the bridges to the mainland.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} has been appeased, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has come to see the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let me guess—you've come to see {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|display=Vah Medoh}} has been perched above {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, and it has not moved since then.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} is perched above the village now. It has not moved an inch since it stopped there.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} is surprised that the spire there can support its weight.<ref>{{Cite|I am surprised that rock up there can support all that weight.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}, which is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} along the edge of {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}}. When asked why he is there, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} explains that {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}} told him to keep watch on {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Dragon|link}} that appears in the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|display=Canyon}}.<ref>{{Cite|[What brings you here?] I came here because the elder tasked me with keeping watch on the {{Color|BotW Blue|dragon}} that appears around here.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> Due to the frequency in sightings of {{Term|BotW|Dinraal}}, the citizens of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} have become worried it will cause them trouble.<ref>{{Cite|It's been appearing quite frequently these days. Everyone's worried it's going to cause trouble for the village.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> He does not blame them for being worried, since {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} left them all on edge.<ref>{{Cite|After the incident with {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}, I can't blame them for worrying.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> While everything seems fine, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} plans on continuing his watch to be sure.<ref>{{Cite|So far everything seems fine. However... Just to be safe, I'll be scoping things out for a while yet.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> He recalls that {{Term|BotW|Dinraal}} flies through {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon}} every night, and he finds the way its horns glow to be captivating.<ref>{{Cite|Come to think of it...the {{Color|BotW Blue|dragon}} always flies through this valley at night. When it does, its {{Color|BotW Red|horns light up}}. There's something about that light that's truly captivating.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Dinraal}} passes by, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} comments that he believes it to be friendly due to how tranquil it is.<ref>{{Cite|The elder requested that I watch for the {{Color|BotW Blue|dragon}} that appears around here. It's colossal for sure, but if you look closely, it seems so tranquil. I can't help but believe it means us no harm.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> He then points out the glow of its horns to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, once again stating that it is captivating.<ref>{{Cite|...Look! Do you see? Its {{Color|BotW Red|horns light up}}. There's something about that light that's truly captivating.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} is still fearful of {{Term|BotW|Dinraal}}'s size, even though he knows it will not hurt him.<ref>{{Cite|Still... When I'm near something that enormous, my body shivers of its own accord, even if my mind knows it won't hurt me.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has unearthed {{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mazli}} notices that {{Term|BotW|Dinraal}}'s horns have stopped glowing and wonders what it means.<ref>{{Cite|Come to think of it...the horns don't glow anymore. I wonder what that means...|Mazli|BotW}}</ref>
