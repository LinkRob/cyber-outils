{{Term/Store|True Strength||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 69 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|True Strength|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|True Strength}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given twelve minutes to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Frost Talus|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Lynel|link}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} spawns, only the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox}} is close enough to attack it, but bringing it down to a fourth of its health will cause the other two to begin an assault on the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. If the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox}} is not damaged, the {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus}} will begin an assault 30 seconds after the start of the mission and the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Lynel}} will begin an assault after 60 seconds. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
