==Biography==
==={{OoS|-}}===
Dr. Left is a [[Cucco]]-studying biologist living in his {{Term|OoS|Biologist's Home|link|display=Home}} in the southeastern portion of [[Horon Village]]. In return for lighting his [[torch]] with [[Ember Seed]]s, he rewards [[Link]] with a [[Cuccodex]],<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... The light in here is too dim to read by. Can anyone light my fire? [...] Splendid! I can finally focus! Here! Take this Cuccodex!|Dr. Left|OoS}}</ref> the first item in the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}. Whenever Link speaks with him afterwards, the doctor will feed him seemingly random facts about characters or monsters found throughout [[Holodrum]]. All his tidbits of knowledge begin with the characteristic phrase, "My books tell me..."

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Dr. Left}}

{{Term|TMC|Dr. Left}} appears in {{TMC}} as a researcher of the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am a researcher of Picori legend and lore. My name is {{Color|TMC Blue|Dr. Left}}.|Dr. Left|TMC}}</ref> He is in possession of an overdue {{Term|TMC|Library Book|link}} titled {{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori|link}}, which {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must locate within his {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House|link|display=House}} and return to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The next overdue book is... Let's see... Ah, yes! Of course...{{Color|TMC Red|Legend of the Picori}}! It's been out forever! It was checked out by a somewhat {{Color|TMC Blue|absentminded scholar}}, I believe...|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> Before {{Term|TMC|Link}} has to find the overdue {{Term|TMC|Library Book}}, {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left}} will not allow him into his {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House|display=House}}, as he is absorbed in his study of the {{Term|TMC|Minish}}.<ref>{{Cite|What is all of this!? I'm busy, don't you see that? Do not disturb my research! If you have no business, begone! You heard me!|Dr. Left|TMC}}</ref>

Ironically, {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left}} is oblivious to the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} living within his own home.<ref>{{Cite|The more I study the Minish, the closer I feel to them! It's almost as if they were right here with me, all the time...|Dr. Left|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link}} returns {{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori}}, {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left}} will part with some seemingly valuable and exclusive information on the {{Term|TMC|Minish}}, gleaned from hours of study.<ref>{{Cite|You found and returned the book for me, did you? I'm very grateful. Here, let me tell you something interesting as thanks. You see, the Minish did not always live in our world. No, they came through a secret portal many ages ago from their world to ours! But don't tell anyone! It's a valuable bit of knowledge that only I know! And I gleaned it only after hours of study from an incredibly old book.|Dr. Left|TMC}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*{{Term|Series|Dr. Left}} bears a strong resemblance to {{Term|LANS|Mr. Write|link}} from {{LA}}. Both characters may have been inspired by Dr. Wright, an advisor from ''SimCity'' for SNES and ''SimCity 64''.
*In {{OoA}}, {{Term|OoA|Dr. Troy|link}} plays a similar role to that of {{Term|OoS|Dr. Left}} in {{OoS}}; he will offer {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} information about various characters and monsters throughout {{Term|OoA|Labrynna|link}}. {{Term|OoA|Dr. Troy}} speaks of the very same monsters as {{Term|OoS|Dr. Left}} with the exception of the {{Plural|OoS|Golden Monster|link}}, which do not appear in {{OoA|-}}.
*Unlike almost all other {{Plural|TMC|Hylian|link}} in {{TMC}}, he refers to the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} as "Minish" rather than "Picori". This is likely to demonstrate his knowledge of them compared to others.
