{{Term/Store|Sacred Shield|Sacred Shields|Series, SS, SSHD}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 137 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWDE}}

==Locations and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
{{Lacking Sources|SSHD}}
In {{SS}}, {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Shield}} can be bought from the {{Term|SSHD|Gear Shop|link}} in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}} for 500 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}} after {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} has defeated {{Term|SSHD|The Imprisoned|link}} for the first time. They are immune to {{Term|SSHD|Fire|link}}, {{Term|SSHD|Electricity|link}}, and {{Plural|SSHD|Curse|link}}. Although their durability bar is shorter than the other {{Plural|SSHD|Shield|link}}s, {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Shield}} have a special ability to recover themselves over a period of time. By {{Term|SSHD|Upgrading|link}} a {{Term|SSHD|Sacred Shield}}, it will turn into a {{Term|SSHD|Divine Shield|link}} and later, the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Shield|link}}. Cursed enemies like the {{Term|SSHD|Cursed Bokoblin|link}} are especially averse to the {{Term|SSHD|Sacred Shield}}, and brandishing it at them will cause them to retreat without attacking. The symbol on the {{Term|SSHD|Sacred Shield}} resembles a {{Term|SSHD|Loftwing|link}} talon.
