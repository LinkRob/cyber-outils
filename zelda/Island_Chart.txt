The {{Term/Store|Island Chart||Series, OoA}} is an {{Term|OoA|Item|link}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The island chart is obtained from [[Tingle]]. It is needed to progress further in the story line, as [[Rafton]] will not make the raft to head to [[Crescent Island]] until Link has obtained the chart.
