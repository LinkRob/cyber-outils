The {{Term/Store|Age of Burning Fields||Series, BotW}} is an event in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|I didn't come along until everything was already over... I was born during the Age of Burning Fields.|Uma|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Age of Burning Fields}} began at the beginning of the {{Term|BotW|Era of the Wilds|link}}, immediately following the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}. Following the destruction throughout {{Term|BotW|Necluda|link}} as a result of the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}' attack, the residents of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} were unable to farm in the {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yes... When I was a child, this whole region wasn't in a state fit to be sown, much less harvested.|Uma|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Age of Burning Fields|display=Age}} ended once {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}} was able to start growing plants, making it self-sufficient.<ref>{{Cite|By the time I was old enough to be aware of it, the plants around Hateno Village were budding... We were self-sufficient.|Uma|BotW}}</ref>
