{{Term/Store|Fort Hateno||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Fort Hateno'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}} is located in [[East Necluda]], demarcating the border between East and [[West Necluda]]. The surrounding cliffs, [[Cliffs of Quince]] to the east, make {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}} a small enclosure. {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} can be found on the lower levers of the cliffs. Much of the ground level is covered in {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}, with a lone [[Lodge]] hiding among their shade, and a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} at the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|display=Fort}}. The [[Squabble River]] hugs the cliff in the south, and the broken remains of a {{Term|BotW|Wagon|link}} can be found along the road leading through the {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|display=Fort}}. Near this {{Term|BotW|Wagon}} sits a small camping spot with a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Box|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Barrel|link}}, and a [[Garbage Dump]]. A lone [[Chuchu]] can be found hiding inside the {{Term|BotW|Barrel}}.

During the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}} served as a tactical defense point for the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} army.<ref name="CaC 384">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 384}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} attempted to flee to {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}} but were met with {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} at [[Blatchery Plain]].<ref name="CaC 384"/> {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}} was ill-equipped for serious combat, but the surrounding terrain impeded the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}' attacks upon the {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|display=Fort}}.<ref name="CaC 385">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 385}}</ref> The combined efforts of {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}, the militia from the citizens of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} forces stationed at {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}} led to the successful defense of the {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|display=Fort}}, narrowly sparing those living east of its walls.<ref name="CaC 384"/><ref name="CaC 385"/> Following the victory at {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}}, minor repair efforts were carried out to restore its fortifications, however these repairs were in a very limited capacity as those trained in stonemasonry were lost during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}.<ref name="CaC 385"/> Despite the successful defense of {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}}, a small number of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} still roamed the land west of the {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|display=Fort}} for 40 or 50 years.<ref name="CaC 385"/> After developing the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow|link}}, [[Robbie]] used them to rid the territory of the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} to atone for his failure to sufficiently support {{Term|BotW|Link}} during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}.<ref name="CaC 385"/> As a result of the battles with {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}, the remains of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} that can be found at {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}} can be searched for [[Ancient Material]]s.

The {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} travelers, [[Agus]], [[Celessa]], [[Joute]], [[Teli]], and [[Toma]], can be found traveling along the road leading through the {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|display=Fort}}. [[Calip]] and [[Garill]] can be seen living at the {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|display=Fort}}.

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an [[Iron Sledgehammer]] sits atop a {{Term|BotW|Zonai Tower|link}} behind {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}}'s wall. Two more stone {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found under some {{Plural|BotW|Rock|link}}, one containing {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}}, the other an [[Emblazoned Shield]].

To the northern part of the area hides a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} in a [[Natural Hiding Place]], where {{Term|BotW|Link}} must move a metal door with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} before removing the {{Term|BotW|Rock}} to get the {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}. Another {{Term|BotW|Korok}} puzzle can be found on top of the stone gates. Standing next to a [[Pinwheel]], {{Term|BotW|Link}} must successfully shoot two {{Term|BotW|Acorn|link}}-shaped {{Plural|BotW|Pot|link}} that are seemingly being thrown above the wooded area before he is rewarded.
