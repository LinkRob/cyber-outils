The {{Name|Fan|{{Term/Store|Subrosian Smelter||Series}}}} is a character in {{OoS}}.{{Fact}} They blow up the obstruction which blocks the [[Temple of Seasons#Accessing the Autumn Tower|Tower of Autumn]]. If [[Link]] can get them [[Ore|Red Ore]] and Blue Ore, then they can make Hard Ore, which can be used to forge either the [[Iron Shield]] or the [[Mirror Shield]], depending on the circumstances.

The Subrosian Smelter, like the rest of [[Subrosian|their people]], is a big fan of dance, shown best as they dance while the Hard Ore is being created. They are one of the few Subrosians who are happy with the [[Temple of Seasons]] having fallen into Subrosia, saying it has resulted in more ore being harvested than normal since its arrival.
