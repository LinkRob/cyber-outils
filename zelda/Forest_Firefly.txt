{{Term/Store|Forest Firefly|Forest Fireflies|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are items in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> It is a unique species of [[Bug#Firefly|Firefly]].

==Location and Uses==
The Forest Firefly is indigenous to the [[Forest Haven]].<ref>{{Cite|Northeast of here is the Forest Haven... They say a truly unique kind of firefly is indigenous to that place. You heard of it, small fry?|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> It can be caught in an [[Bottle|Empty Bottle]],<ref>{{Cite|You caught a Forest Firefly in your bottle! A strange light emanates from this mystical creature...|N/A|TWW}}</ref> and, if brought to the pictographer [[Lenzo]], can be used to alter a [[Picto Box]] into a [[Picto Box#Deluxe Picto Box|Deluxe Picto Box]].<ref>{{Cite|For ages, it has been said that the Forest Fireflies possess a mystical power. For your efforts, I shall place this one inside your box! Come on now, hand me your Picto Box, if only for a moment...|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> It is said to glow in seven different colors.<ref>{{Cite|It emits bursts of light in seven colors! Supposedly it's as beautiful as a rainbow!|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> In the [[Second Quest]], this item becomes useless entirely, as the Deluxe Picto Box is already available since the start of the game in this mode.

In {{TWWHD}}, the Forest Firefly is never needed to make the Deluxe Picto Box, and in turn Lenzo will not mention it during Link's training to be his [[Lenzo's Research Assistant|Research Assistant]]. The Forest Firefly can still be found in [[Forest Haven]] however, and captured with an empty bottle. Link can show Lenzo the firefly, and will be told that it was once needed in order to create the Deluxe Picto Box.<ref>{{Cite|What a curious luminescence... Is that truly one of the long-fabled Forest Fireflies from Forest Haven? My fine assistant, Link... For ages, it has been said that the Forest Fireflies possess a mystical power. In the old days, they were required for making colored pictographs... but no longer.|Lenzo|TWWHD}}</ref> [[Lenzo]] will also reward Link's hard work by accepting the firefly and giving him a [[Joy Pendant]] in return.<ref>{{Cite|I'd hate to see you leave with nothing after you went to so much trouble to show it to me. I offer you this! The elderly lady next door tells me it has value, of sorts.|Lenzo|TWWHD}}</ref>
