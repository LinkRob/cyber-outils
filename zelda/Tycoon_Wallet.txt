The {{Term/Store|Tycoon Wallet||Series, SS}} is an item in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Tycoon Wallet is given to {{Term|SS|Link|link}} by [[Batreaux]] after collecting all 80 [[Gratitude Crystal]]s scattered around [[Skyloft]] and giving them to him. It can hold up to 9,000 Rupees on its own, or up to 9,900 with the [[Extra Wallet]]s.
