==Location and Uses==
Blackened Crabs are the result of either {{Plural|Series|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, {{Plural|Series|Ironshell Crab|link}}, or {{Plural|Series|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}} that have made contact with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. Blue {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} also sometimes drop them when killed. Blackened Crabs have soft, flaky flesh, and smell faintly of charred shell. It is said to assault all five of your senses in the best ways.<ref name="Inventory"/> Eating one will restore one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}.
