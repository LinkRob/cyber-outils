The {{Term/Store|Bombos Medallion||Series, ALttP, FSA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 118 {{Exp Game|ALttP, FSA}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Bombos|Only the Hero bearing the Master Sword can retrieve this medal. The magic of this medallion sets off explosive forces around you which engulf the enemy in flames.}}
In {{ALttP}}, the Bombos Medallion can be found in the [[Desert of Mystery]], high upon a cliff that can only be reached by traveling to the [[Dark World]] and using the [[Magic Mirror]] near the swamp. It is obtained via a green tablet which can only be read with the [[Book of Mudora]]. The spell is cast once the [[Master Sword]] is raised,<ref>{{Cite|Hold up the Master Sword and you will get the magic of Bombos.|Stone Tablet|ALttP}}</ref> with the tablet breaking afterwards. The resulting effect of the spell first being cast also ended up obliterating a nearby [[vulture]]. The Medallion then falls from the sky.

The Bombos Medallion is an activatable item that consumes 1/4th of the magic bar when used.

When Link activates the Bombos Medallion, it triggers the animation of an enchanted [[Spin Attack]], then [[Link]] sends out a ring of fire followed by a series of small explosions in all directions.<ref>{{Cite|This is the Bombos Medallion! Its magic makes the ground explode with power! Watch your Magic Meter!|N/A|ALttP}}</ref> This causes all enemies to become inflamed, as if they have been struck with the [[Fire Rod]]. This is the only Medallion not required to progress through the game. Note that while the visual effect is identical to that of the [[Fire Rod]], the Bombos Medallion deals far more damage in a single use, enough to break [[Kholdstare]]'s ice barrier in one hit, whereas the [[Fire Rod]] would take multiple uses, and also, as a matter of fact, cost a greater quantity of total [[Magic]] to break said barrier.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Manual|FSA|Bombos Medallion|Annihilate all enemies on the screen.}}
In {{FSA}}, the {{Term|FSA|Bombos Medallion}} triggers a rain of fire which defeats every enemy on the screen. It appears in the {{Term|FSA|Death Mountain Foothills|link}}, where it can be used to clear an area filled with {{Plural|FSA|Buzz Blob|link}}. The {{Term|FSA|Bombos Medallion}} appears again in {{Term|FSA|The Field|link}}, where it can be used to defeat three waves of {{Plural|FSA|Enemy|link}}.{{Which}}

The {{Term|FSA|Fairy Queen|link}} can cast Bombos without having to use the {{Term|FSA|Bombos Medallion}}, as shown in the {{Term|FSA|Hyrule Castle|link}} stage when a horde of {{Term|FSA|Soldier (Enemy)|link}} attempt to ambush her and the {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}}.
