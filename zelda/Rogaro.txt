{{Term/Store|Rogaro||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rogaro|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Rogaro}} is the owner of the {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} shop {{Term|BotW|Ripped and Shredded|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have the privilege of standing in {{Color|BotW Blue|Ripped and Shredded}}, my armor shop. How can I help ya?|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref> He sells the {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Set|link}}, and highly recommends it to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if he intends on scaling {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can't go out dressed like that! If ya don't hurry and change your gear, you're gonna pass out from the heat!|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But ya did a great job fireproofing! Like that, you could even make it to {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}, or anywhere really.|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref> Rogaro states that the Armor he sells are intended for tourists, as {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}} do not normally wear clothes.<ref>{{Cite|Funny thing is, Gorons normally don't wear anything at all. We started sellin' {{Color|BotW Red|flame-resistant armor}} for the tourists. I recommend the {{Color|BotW Blue|flamebreaker boots}} over all else. They're made using {{Color|BotW Blue|gems}} mined from this very soil. As you'd expect, they have great {{Color|BotW Red|flame resistance}}. Give 'em a try! They're the envy of famous warriors the world over!|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} talks to {{Term|BotW|Rogaro}} without any {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Armor|link}} on, his eyes widen and he urges {{Term|BotW|Link}} to quickly buy his gear, telling him that it is not a good time to talk or be casual.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! You can't be casual about stuff like that! Hurry up and buy some gear!|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This doesn't seem like a good time to talk...|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can say good-bye all ya want, but you'll still need to change your gear! Go on, make it snappy!|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref> Like many other shop owners, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also sell items to {{Term|BotW|Rogaro}} if needed. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to sell anything without any flame protection, {{Term|BotW|Rogaro}} tells him to do so quickly.<ref>{{Cite|Got it. Let's make this quick!|Rogaro|BotW}}</ref>
