{{Term/Store|Haunted Grove||Series, ALttP, ALBW}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP|-}}, [[Link]] can find the [[Flute Boy]] playing for wild [[animals]] in this area before mysteriously disappearing when approached.

When Link enters the [[Dark World]] equivalent of the Haunted Grove, he finds the Flute Boy transformed into a strange creature as a result of the [[Triforce|Golden Power]]'s effects.<ref>{{Cite|After wandering into this world I turned into this shape.|Flute Boy|ALttP}}</ref> The Flute Boy tells Link that he buried his [[Flute]] with some flower seeds in the [[Light World]]'s Haunted Grove and gives Link the [[Shovel]] to search for it. Link can dig near the top left corner of the Haunted Grove to find the flute.

When he brings the flute back to him in the Dark World, the Flute Boy says he can't play it anymore,<ref>{{Cite|But it looks like I can't play my flute any more. Please take it.|Flute Boy|ALttP}}</ref> asks him to give it to his [[Man in Tavern|father]] in Kakariko Village,<ref>{{Cite|If by chance you go to the village I lived in, please give it to a tired old man.|Flute Boy|ALttP}}</ref> and asks to hear its melody one more time.<ref>{{Cite|Please let me hear the sound of the flute one last time...|Flute Boy|ALttP}}</ref> When Link plays the Flute, the boy petrifies into a [[tree]] and his apparitions cease to exist in the Haunted Grove.

After [[Ganon]]'s defeat, the Flute Boy returns,<ref>{{Cite|FLUTE BOY PLAYS AGAIN - HAUNTED GROVE|Ending Text|ALttP}}</ref> and continues to play there, even six years later, during the events of ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|Ancient Stone Tablets]]''.{{fact}}

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW|-}}, the grove is initially filled with wild animals early in the game. After a while, Link can find [[Gulley]] here, playing with the animals, something he seems to do in his free time.

Later on, Link finds the [[Blacksmith's Wife]] searching for Gulley, and heads to the grove to find him, but only finds [[Pouch|Gulley's bag]] on the stump, implying that this was place where he was caught and turned into a [[Painting]] by [[Yuga]]. Upon finding the Pouch, Link is found by the Blacksmith's Wife, who lets Link keep the Pouch, giving him an extra slot to which he can equip an extra item from his [[Inventory]].

After Yuga merges with Ganon, the grove becomes filled with enemy [[Soldier (Enemy)|Soldier]]s who attack Link whenever they see him.
