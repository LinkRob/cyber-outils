'''[[Link]]''' is generally seen as the main protagonist of ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' series, but he has visited the [[w:c:mario:Mushroom Kingdom|Mushroom Kingdom]] and nearby areas on occasion. The first time Link appears in a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game is ''[[w:c:mario:Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars|Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==''Donkey Kong'' appearances==
====''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest==== 
[[File:Cranky'sVideoGameHeroes.png|thumb|Link comes in third place as one of Cranky's Video Game Heroes.]]
In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'', Link was ranked third in [[Cranky Kong]]'s list of Video Game Heroes. [[Mario]] came in first place, while [[Yoshi]] came in second place. The heroes were ranked according to how many of Cranky's hidden [[DK Coin]]s they discovered while progressing through the levels and defeating [[w:c:donkeykong:Kaptain K. Rool|Kaptain K. Rool]]. By collecting more DK Coins than him (19), [[Diddy Kong]] knocks Link off the leader board, ranking Diddy in third place.

In the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake, Link not only switches positions with Yoshi, putting Link in second place, but has 50 Video Game Hero Coins, given that more have been added in general. If Diddy manages to obtain more Video Game Hero Coins, Link is shifted down to third place.

====''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!====
According to [[Bazaar]] the Bear, Link visited [[w:c:donkeykong:Bazaar's General Store|Bazaar's General Store]] before the events of ''[[w:c:donkeykong:Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!|Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''. While he was there, Link asked Bazaar who owned the castle in the northern part of the [[w:c:donkeykong:Northern Kremisphere|Northern Kremisphere]]. However, Link did not have the two [[w:c:donkeykong:Bear Coin|Bear Coins]] Bazaar required before he would tell his story about the castle. Link did have five hundred Rupees though, and Bazaar agreed to take them. Unfortunately for Link, Bazaar did not know who owned the castle; Bazaar merely knew there was a no trespassing sign on the castle grounds. Distraught, Link left the store, grumbling about how Bazaar's Seashells were the wrong shape. (This is undoubtedly a nod to Link's quest for Secret Seashells in the Game Boy's ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening]]''.

The dialogue referencing Link is not in the Game Boy Advance remake.

===''Donkey Kong Land 2''===
Link retains his spot and number of Video Game Hero Coins on the Video Game Heroes podium in the ending of ''[[w:c:donkeykong:Donkey Kong Land 2|Donkey Kong Land 2]]''.

==''Mario'' appearances==
===''Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up''===
[[File:Link on WIGU Chef page.png|thumb|left|200px|On the Chef page]]
[[File:Link on WIGU Travel page.png|thumb|200px|On the Travel page]]
Link is featured on two coloring pages in ''[[w:c:mario:Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up|Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up]]''. He is the very feature of the "Travel Guide" page, where Link runs his own travel agency. Link is also on the "Chef" page, where [[Princess Peach|Waitress Toadstool]] takes his order for a pasta dish as [[Mario|Chef Mario]] prepares it nearby.

===''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars===
[[File:SMRPG Link in Rose Town Inn.png|thumb|left|Link gets some much needed rest at the [[Rose Town]] [[Inn]].]]
[[File:SMRPG 2023 Link Cameo.jpg|thumb|Link sleeping in one bed while Mario sleeps in one nearby in the ''Super Mario RPG'' remake.]]
During the events of ''[[mariowiki:Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars|Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', Link could be found taking a break from battling [[Ganon]] and actually resting at the [[w:c:mario:Rose Town|Rose Town]] Inn. In fact, the Hyrulian hero is a heavy sleeper; he won't even awaken when spoken to (this is likely a nod to Link's constant portrayal as a silent protagonist). However, a strange but familiar melody could be heard whenever anyone tried to talk to the slumbering Link.

===''Mario Golf'' (Nintendo 64)===
Link's name appears on the scorechart of ''[[w:c:mario:Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]].

===''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''===
Link is included as a downloadable playable racer in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. He is designed after his appearance in {{SS}} and is a heavyweight racer. Link has special tires named the [[Triforce Tires]] and a [[glider]] named the [[Hylian Glider]], respectively. A racing suit based on Link's appearance is also unlockable for [[Mii]] characters by scanning a Link-related [[amiibo]].

===''Super Mario Maker''===
In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', there are Mystery Mushroom costumes available of Link (using his sprite from the original game), Toon Link, Wolf Link and Totem Link. The former three could be unlocked by scanning matching amiibos, and the former two could alternately unlocked as random prizes for completing a 100-Mario Challenge (on Normal or harder for Toon Link). Wolf Link and Totem Link each were featured in special Event Courses that could be used to unlock them.

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', an update made in December 2019 added the Master Sword as a power-up, which turns Mario and other playable characters into an 8-bit version of Link, using sprites derived from the original ''The Legend of Zelda''. In this form, Link can attack with his swords, arrows and bombs.

==Nintendo crossovers==
===''Club Nintendo''===
[[File:LinkCN.jpg|thumb|100px|Link as seen in "Mario in Mariozilla".]]
Link also made several appearances in the Mario comic series of the German [[Club Nintendo|Club Nintendo magazine]]. He debuted in "[[Super Mario: Die Bescherung]]", celebrating a Christmas party in [[Mario's House|Mario and Luigi's house]]. His role in this story is minor, though he has a brief speaking part at the end, mistrusting the realness of [[Santa Claus]].

In "[[Mario in Mariozilla]]", he is seen in the [[Empire State Building]], studying a map together with [[Dr. Light]], who lives there. Link's actual role is unknown, though it appears that he works as Light's assistant. He also comments on a "magic cap" Light gave to Mario in order to make him incognito - actually a simple paper bag pulled over his head - in the way that it "becomes" him.

Link, alongside Kirby, was Mario's ally in the comic "[[Super Mario in Die Nacht des Grauens]]". During the events of this story, he lives in a [[Brooklyn]] [[Nintendo Skyscraper|skyscraper]] and fights against [[Abigor]] and his demonic fellows who plan to take over the building. He does so by making use of a magic serum which turns him and his allies into aggressive ghostbusters. In this form, he has green skin, black armor and a fire sword.

===''Nintendo Monopoly''===
Link appears in [[Nintendo Monopoly]]. He takes the place of Pennsylvania Avenue, and costs $320.

===''Super Smash Bros.''===
[[File:SSB Link Artwork.png|thumb|left|x170px]]
In the first ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'', he was the first character fought in single player mode, on [[Hyrule Castle]]. 
{{clear|left}}
===''Super Smash Bros. Melee''===
[[File:SSBM Link Artwork.png|thumb|left|x200px|Link as he appeared in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'']]
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', Link is fought in the [[Underground Maze]] in Adventure Mode. Link is fought many different times (wearing different tunics each time) as fighters search for the Triforce to end the level.

Link's Bomb attack is not as strong as throwing the [[Bob-omb]] item, but can still do medium damage to the opponent. His Bow and Arrows, as well as his Boomerang, are his main long range attacks. At close range, Link can use the Master Sword to attack and the Hylian Shield to defend. He uses his Hookshot as grab move, which can be attached to the stage to prevent him from falling. A younger version of Link, called [[Young Link]], is also playable in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'', much in the same manner that [[Mario]] and [[w:c:mario:Baby Mario|Baby Mario]] often appear in the same game. Additionally, a doppelgänger of Link, known as [[Dark Link]], can be fought in the game.
{{clear|left}}
===''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''===
Link was one of the first characters to be announced for ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. Link's [[Final Smash]] is the [[Triforce Slash]]. His moveset has been altered to match his appearance from ''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess'', which gives him the Gale Boomerang as well as a chargable Spin Attack. Link's grab move, the Clawshot, can be used as a [[tether recovery]]. Link has a new voice actor in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', [[Akira Sasanuma]], who voiced him in ''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess''. Dark Link will also be in this game, as one of Link's Alternative Costumes. [[Toon Link]], the Link from ''Wind Waker'' will also appear in the game, with a similar moveset to Link's.

====Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary====
[[File:Link SSBB SSE introduction snapshot.png|thumb|left|Link's snapshot in The Subspace Emissary]]
Link walks through a forest. There he finds the Master Sword. Taking the Master Sword in hand in a forest, Link meets up with [[Yoshi]], forming an alliance. They later meet [[Mario]] and [[Pit]], though under circumstances where Mario/Link defeats an evil copy of [[Zelda]]/[[Peach]], leading to a fight. The two groups come to an understanding (and [[Kirby]] joins up as well) and unite as they see [[King Dedede]] driving off with [[Ness]], [[Luigi]], and the captured Princess.

They come to Dedede's base, only to find that [[Bowser]] beat them to it, taking the Princess off in his [[Koopa Clown Car]]. The team grows in size as they meet with [[Ike]], [[Marth]], [[Lucas]], the [[Pokémon Trainer]], and the [[Ice Climbers]]. The remaining heroes join them as they enter Subspace. Link is among the many that falls to [[Tabuu]]'s [[Off Waves]], only to be revived by Kirby. He and Zelda come across [[Ganondorf]]'s trophy and revive him, forming a temporary alliance as everyone teams up to defeat Tabuu.

Link reappears as a playable [[fighter]] in {{SSBU}}. His appearance and moves are now based on those in {{BotW}}. Although {{BotW|-}} gives Link a completely different arsenal of moves and abilities unlike prior games, his moveset in {{SSBU|-}} is still the same, aside from his new Final Smash. Toon Link, as well as Young Link, makes a return, making three different Links available.

Link appears as a [[Fighter Spirit]] that can be obtained by either clearing "[[A Quest to Seal the Darkness]]", Link's [[Classic Mode]] Route, or by purchasing it from the [[Shop#Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|Shop]] for G 300. Link also appears as the [[Primary Spirit#Link (The Legend of Zelda)|Link {{Small|(The Legend of Zelda)}}]] [[Primary Spirit]], the [[Wall-Merged Link]] Primary Spirit, the [[Primary Spirit#Link (A Link Between Worlds)|Link {{Small|(A Link Between Worlds)}}]] Primary Spirit, as part of the [[Spirit#Alfonzo & Engineer Link|Alfonzo & Engineer Link]] [[Support Spirit]], and as part of the [[Primary Spirit#Totem Link|Totem Link]] Primary Spirit.

The Link {{Small|(The Legend of Zelda)}}, Wall-Merged Link, Alfonzo & Engineer Link, and Totem Link spirits can be obtained through [[Spirit Battle]]s. The Link {{Small|(A Link Between Worlds)}} spirit can be obtained by leveling the Wall-Merged Link spirit to Level 99 and enhancing it.

==''WarioWare'' series==
Link has appeared in several ''[[WarioWare]]'' games. He usually is a playable character in [[9-Volt]]'s and [[18-Volt]]'s micro-games. In the micro-games, the player has to control the hero, helping him avoid the enemies on the screen by moving him safely into a cave.

==''Yoshi's Woolly World''==
In ''Yoshi's Woolly World'', there are Yoshi designs based on the appearances of Link and Toon Link that can be unlocked by scanning the respective amiibo. A design based on Wolf Link is also available in ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''.

==Trivia==
*Link, along with many other non-''Mario'' Nintendo characters, was meant to appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', at the [[w:c:mario:Starbeans Cafe|Starbeans Cafe]]. He would try one of the drinks that Mario and Luigi got and reward them with an item called "Triforce". He was replaced by [[E. Gadd]] in the final version, although his Triforce reward (albeit redesigned) survived as the professor's [[w:c:mario:Great Force|Great Force]] invention.
*The [[Ghosts of Christmas#Ghost of Present|Ghost of Present]] in the ''Club Nintendo'' comic "[[Warios Weihnachtsmärchen]]" resembles Link.
*In the ''Club Nintendo'' comic "[[Super Mario: Die Verwandlung]]", Dr. Light constantly mistakes Mario for Link. One of these confusions also implies that Link has undergone an esthetic surgery by Light.
