The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Bridle|Gerudo Bridle|Series, HWAoC|plural}} is a {{Term|Series|Weapon|link}} used by {{Term|HWAoC|Riju|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gerudo Bridle}}<br/>A common bridle used for riding sand seals. The Gerudo sometimes bejewel these to adorn their tusked steeds.|Weapons|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
When Riju is unlocked as a playable character, the Gerudo Bridle can be acquired in various missions whether or not she is used for that mission. Upon arrival in the past, Riju and Patrica ram into Thunderblight Ganon just as it was about to brutally electrocute Urbosa to death.
