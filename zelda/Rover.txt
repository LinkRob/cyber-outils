==Characteristics==
{{Term|Series|Smasher}} is a mysterious round creature with dog-like behavior. It attacks by picking up a heavy {{Term|Series|Ball|link}} and throwing it at {{Term|Series|Link|link}}, who must pick it up with his {{Term|Series|Power Bracelet|link}} and throw it back at {{Term|Series|Smasher}} before the {{Term|Series|Boss}} is able to retrieve it.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Term|LADX|Smasher}} is found in a hidden room in {{Term|LADX|Face Shrine|link}}, in one of the "eyes" on the {{Term|LADX|Dungeon}} map. The only way to access this room is to open the wall with {{Plural|LADX|Bomb|link}}. After defeating {{Term|LADX|Smasher}}, a warp to the entrance of the {{Term|LADX|Dungeon}} will appear and {{Term|LADX|Link}} can pass through to a room that contains a secret passageway deeper into the {{Term|LADX|Dungeon}}. However, if he cannot find the passageway, the rooms will loop endlessly. {{Term|LADX|Smasher}} later appears in {{Term|LADX|Turtle Rock|link}} along with other {{Plural|LADX|Sub-Boss}}, where it will continue to reappear even after being destroyed.
{{Clear}}

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Term|OoA|Smasher}} is found as the {{Term|OoA|Sub-Boss}} of the {{Term|OoA|Crown Dungeon|link}} on {{Term|OoA|Rolling Ridge|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 93}}</ref> The only major difference from its {{LA|-}} incarnation is that the {{Term|OoA|Ball}} will disappear after a short while after being thrown and reappear closer to {{Term|OoA|Smasher}}.
