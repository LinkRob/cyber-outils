{{Term/Store|Secret Medicine|Secret Medicines|Series, LA, LADX, LANS|plural}} are items in {{LA}}.<ref name="Obtainment">{{Cite|You found some {{Color|LANS Pink|secret medicine}}! But you've already got some on, so let's put this back.|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|LA|Secret Medicine|If you have this in your possession when all of your Heart Containers are empty, they will be fully restored.}}
{{Term|LANS|Link|link}} can purchase {{Plural|LANS|Secret Medicine}} from {{Term|LANS|Tracy|link}} at varying prices. In {{LA|DX}} and {{LA|NS}}, they can also be found inside the final three dungeons. {{Plural|LANS|Secret Medicine}} will activate automatically when {{Term|LANS|Link}} loses all of his {{Plural|LANS|Heart|link}}, completely refilling them before disappearing from the inventory.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= LA|page= 19}}</ref> Only one {{Term|LANS|Secret Medicine}} can be carried at a time. According to {{Term|LANS|Tracy}}, they are rubbed on {{Term|LANS|Link}} when purchased. If Link is to be killed by the [[Town Tool Shopkeeper]] for stealing an item from the [[Town Tool Shop]], the Secret Medicine cannot revive Link.

In {{LA|NS|-}}, if {{Term|LANS|Link}} already has a {{Term|LANS|Secret Medicine}} in his inventory and then finds another one inside a {{Term|LANS|Treasure Chest|link}}, he will put it back inside.<ref name="Obtainment"/>
