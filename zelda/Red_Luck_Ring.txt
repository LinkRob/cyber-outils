The {{Term/Store|Red Luck Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Red Luck Ring<br/>1/2 damage from spiked floors|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Red Luck Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be obtained by giving a [[Goron]] inside a cave in [[Rolling Ridge]] during the past 20 [[Ember Seed]]s and 20 [[Bomb]]s. If [[Link]] returns later, he can choose between two [[Treasure Chest]]s containing the Ring. Additionally, it can be obtained by scoring 350 points at the [[Shooting Gallery]] in [[Lynna Village]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it reduces damage taken from [[Spiked Floor]]s.<ref name="Description"/> The Red Luck Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
