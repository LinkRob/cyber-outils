The following is a list of {{Plural|TotK|Enemy|link}} known to appear in {{TotK}}.

==Gloom enemies==
Many enemies have a variety that appears in [[The Depths]], covered in [[gloom]]. These varieties do gloom damage when they strike you physically or throw rocks at you.

* Bokoblin (all variants)
* Lizalfos (all variants)
* Moblin (all variants)
* Gibdos (all variants)
* Boss Bokoblin (all variants)
* Horriblin (all variants)
* Lynel (all variants)
* Fire Like
* Evermeans (they resemble Depths trees and have different drops)
* Stalkoblin
* Stalizalfos
* Stalnox
* Talus (all variants)
* King Gleeok (one only)

==Armored enemies==
Additionally, some enemies are '''Armored'''. These enemies must first be struck with blunt damage (eg: [[Cobble Crusher]], [[Horriblin Horn]]) or explosive damage (eg: [[Bomb Flower]]s). This will destroy their Armor health (in yellow). Once the Armor is gone, they can be attacked normally to take down their health. They will usually drop an [[Armor Shard]] upon death which Link can't pick up into his inventory, but he can fuse it to weapons/shields.

* Bokoblin (all variants)
* Lynel (all variants)
