The {{Term/Store|Dah Hesho Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Dah Hesho Shrine}}<br/>A Minor Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Minor Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Dah Hesho Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}} on a cliff south of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}} and overlooking {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dah Hesho|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Dah Hesho. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Dah Hesho|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Dah Hesho Shrine}} has {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} face a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}}, which is armed with an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe|link}}. The arena has several stone pillars that can be destroyed by {{Plural|BotW|Bomb|link}} or by the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}} spinning into them during one of its attacks.

Once the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}} is defeated, a portcullis will open that leads to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}. Here {{Term|BotW|Link}} will meet the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, {{Term|BotW|Dah Hesho}}, who will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Dah Hesho|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Dah Hesho|BotW}}</ref>
