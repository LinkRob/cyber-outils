{{Term/Store|White Zol|White Zols|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: White Zol|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|White Zol}} are a strong variety of {{Term|CoH|Zol|link}}, possessing a total of 3 {{Plural|CoH|Heart|link}} but cannot attack {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} as they are stationary and cannot move. Defeating them will make them split into four {{Plural|CoH|White Gel|link}}, quickly outnumbering {{Term|CoH|Cadence}}, {{Term|CoH|Link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}} if they are not careful.
