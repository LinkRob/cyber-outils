== Biography ==
Goronu is a necromancer and a minion of [[Ganon]]. He is first encountered at his Face of Evil, the {{Term|TFoE|Goronu (Stage)|link}} area. As [[Link]] enters his chamber, he raises the dead as [[Stalfos]] to attack the living.<ref>{{Cite|Wake up Sleepy Bones! Find the living and cut their vile throats!|Goronu|TFoE}}</ref> He is defeated by several [[Firestone]]s, and he dissolves in flames.

He is later encountered again at Fortress Centrum when Link goes to find the [[Crystal of Reflection]]. He finds [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] asleep there, but is revealed to actually be Goronu in disguise in an attempt to ambush Link. He is fought and defeated again the same way.
