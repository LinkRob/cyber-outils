{{Term/Store|Mallara||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 294}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Her name is {{Color|SS Blue|Mallara}}, and she is quite an optimist. She is the mother of {{Color|SS Blue|Pipit}}, the Knight Academy student.</p><p>The opposite of her son, she can be quite haphazard. She is famed for her dislike of chores, especially cleaning, even though she prefers a clean house.</p>
}}

Mallara is the mother of [[Pipit]]. She is reputed for her dislike of doing housework,<ref name="Dislike"/> and as such, their house is always in a perpetual state of untidiness.<ref>{{Cite|This is {{Color|SS Blue|Pipit}}'s house. His mother, {{Color|SS Blue|Mallara}}, can't stand cleaning up, so their house is always in various states of uncleanliness.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

Mallara spends most of her time at home, but, despite being there all the time, never bothers to clean it.<ref>{{Cite|It's just that I hate cleaning, you know. It would be great if someone would clean the place up for me...|Mallara|SS}}</ref> After completing the [[Lanayru Mining Facility]], she will ask [[Link]] to clean it for her by using the [[Gust Bellows]] to blow away all the dust.<ref>{{Cite|Pipit is always telling me to do some dusting around here, but I just can't bring myself to do it. That's why I wanted to ask you if you wouldn't mind cleaning it for me.|Mallara|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Most of the time I just wait for a good strong wind to come along and {{Color|SS Red|blow the dust away}}. So look, I don't really care how you get the job done--I just want you to {{Color|SS Red|blow it all away}} for me.|Mallara|SS}}</ref> If he does, he will receive five [[Gratitude Crystal]]s the first time he completes the task. From that point on she will pay Link 20 [[Rupee]]s to clean the house.<ref>{{Cite|...I can't let you go without your reward. Watch out--there's some big money coming... BAM, {{Color|SS Red|20 Rupees}}!|Mallara|SS}}</ref> She will ask Link not to tell Pipit this, however.<ref>{{Cite|Don't tell Pipit about this. He'll only get angry at me for spending money on something he thinks I should be doing myself.|Mallara|SS}}</ref>

If Link returns at night, he will unintentionally overhear Pipit reprimanding her for spending his hard-earned money, which she could use to buy bread, on having someone else do a job she could do herself.<ref>{{Cite|It's been obvious lately that you've been {{Color|SS Red|giving someone Rupees}} to clean the house! I gave you that money so you could buy some bread!|Pipit|SS}}</ref> However, undeterred, she will continue to ask Link to clean for her.<ref>{{Cite|Hi there, Link. Pipit is really angry with me... He said I shouldn't be spending our money on a housekeeper.... But...you're not exactly a housekeeper, are you, Link? That makes it OK, doesn't it? Anyway, that's good enough for me. As far as I'm concerned, I'll pay you to clean my house anytime it gets dusty.|Mallara|SS}}</ref> Once the chore is completed, Link can speak to her to acquire his 20 Rupees, or choose to leave, in essence doing the job for free, though there is no benefit to doing so.
