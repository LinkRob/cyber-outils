The {{Term/Store|Gnat Hat||Series, FS}} is an {{Term|FS|Item|link}} in {{FS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|FS|Gnat Hat|Wearing this hat shrinks you down in size! There are some areas that only insect-sized players can enter.}}
The Gnat Hat allows Link to instantly shrink down to the size of a tiny gnat and back again. There are some areas only insect sized beings can enter. The Gnat Hat functions similarly to [[Ezlo]] in {{TMC}}, where he aids in shrinking [[Link]] down to the size of the [[Minish]]. The [[Mage's Cap]] created by Ezlo, also in {{TMC|-}}, has a similar appearance.
