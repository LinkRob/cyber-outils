==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Sunset Firefly}}
Sunset Fireflies can be found flying around during the night, particularly noticeable by their glow.<ref name="Inventory"/> When combined with {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} in a [[Cooking Pot]], the result will be a [[Sneaky Elixir]], which will temporarily increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} [[Stealth]] when consumed.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] as an ingredient for Purple [[Dye]].

If Link enters [[Lasli|Lasli's]] home during the evening, she will express remorse over being unable to gather any fireflies due to the roads being dangerous at night.<ref>{{Cite|They say the roads are dangerous at night, so I have to come straight home without searching for {{Color|BotW Red|fireflies}}...|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> After talking to her, Link will receive the [[Side Quest]] "[[By Firefly's Light]]", which requires him to obtain five Sunset Fireflies. Releasing the fireflies in front of Lasli will end the quest, and she will reward him with fifty {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.

Five Sunset Fireflies, as well as five {{Plural|Series|Blue Nightshade|link}}, are needed to upgrade each piece of the [[Stealth Set]] to its second tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Stealth Set''': 15 (0, 5, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
