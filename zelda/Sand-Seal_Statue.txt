==Locations and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Sand-Seal Statue}} are realistic statues of {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal|link}}. While they are mostly made of stone, the saddles featured on these statues are created out of metal. They are used to mark the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth|link}} on the edges of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref name="Frelly"/> As a result of this, the {{Plural|BotW|Sand-Seal Statue|display=Statues}} have been placed on their roofs.
