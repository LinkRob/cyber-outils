The {{Term/Store|Great Deku Tree's Navel||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Zzz...<br/>Oh, Mr. Hero. Welcome to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Deku Tree's Navel}}.|Pepp|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Great Deku Tree's Navel is the hollow center of the [[Great Deku Tree]]. The Navel is made up of a single hollowed out center with three rooms extending outward in the three compass directions other than the entrance.

The Navel is inhabited by three [[Korok]]s. [[Pepp]] is a Korok who runs an inn inside of the Tree, allowing [[Link]] to sleep and regain his health without paying any money. There are also two shops inside the Tree. The first is the [[Spore Store]], run by [[Natie]], which sells [[Mushroom]]s. The second is the [[General Shoppe]], which includes assorted adventure items such as [[Shock Arrow]]s and [[Apple]]s.

The center of the Navel contains a [[Goddess Statue]], [[Cooking Pot]], and a [[Soup Ladle]]. There is also a fire in the center of this clearing, and a Korok named [[Peeks]] can be found dancing around it. If Link talks to Peeks, Peeks will give Link the [[Side Quest]] "[[Legendary Rabbit Trial]]", where Link must show Peeks a picture of a [[Blupee]].

== Tears of the Kingdom ==
There is now a fourth chamber that contains the Great Deku Tree Chasm with a [[Phantom Ganon]] boss fight.
