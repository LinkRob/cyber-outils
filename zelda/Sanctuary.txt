==Features and overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:ALttP sanctuary 1.png|left|200px|thumb|The sanctuary as seen in ''A Link to the Past'']]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', the {{Term|ALttP|Sanctuary}} is the home of the {{Term|ALttP|Loyal Sage|link}} located north of {{Term|ALttP|Hyrule Castle|link}}. After [[Link]] rescues [[Princess Zelda]] from her prison in Hyrule Castle, he uses the [[Sewer Passageway]] to take her to the Sanctuary. She remains there with the Loyal Sage, hiding from [[Agahnim]].<ref>{{Cite|The two of you cannot stay together, so I shall hide Princess Zelda here.|Loyal Sage|ALttP}}</ref> However, when the young hero withdraws the [[Master Sword]] from its [[Pedestal of Time|pedestal]], the Sanctuary is suddenly attacked by [[soldier]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Link! Help! The soldiers are coming to the Sanctuary! AIEEEEEEE! ... ... ...|Princess Zelda|ALttP}}</ref> When Link reaches the Sanctuary, he finds the wounded Loyal Sage lying on the ground. The sage informs him that the princess was kidnapped and taken back to the castle so Agahnim could use her to open the [[Sage|Seven Sages']] seal.<ref>{{Cite|Link! You are a second too late. I have failed... Zelda... The soldiers have abducted her. They have taken her to the castle. You must find her before the wizard works his magic. Please...You are our only hope...|Loyal Sage|ALttP}}</ref>

The Sanctuary is last seen during the ending credits, showing that the Loyal Sage has fully recovered from his wounds.

==={{TP|-}}===
[[File:TP Sanctuary.png|thumb|right|227x227px|The rundown Sanctuary found in Kakariko]]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', the {{Term|TPHD|Sanctuary}} is located in [[Kakariko Village]], {{Exp|GCN version; northwest in the Wii version|northeast}} of [[Malo Mart]] and {{exp|GCN version; west in the Wii version|east}} of the [[Kakariko Village Graveyard|graveyard]]. [[Renado]] lives here with his daughter, {{Term|TPHD|Luda|link}}. The children of [[Ordon Village]], [[Colin]], [[Talo]], and {{Term|TPHD|Beth|link}}, take refuge here after being kidnapped by [[King Bulblin]].

The Sanctuary consists of one round room with an effigy of the [[Spirits of Light|Light Spirit]] [[Eldin (Spirit)|Eldin]] in the center. A small fire burns next to the statue. Four torches line the walls, each within an alcove. The sanctuary may seem small at first glance; however, it has a large cellar which can be accessed by lighting all the torches in the room. This causes the statue of Eldin to shift, revealing the entrance to the cellar.<ref>{{Cite|The entrance to the cellar is designed to open when all of the candles have been lit...|Renado|TP}}</ref> At the far end of the cellar is a scaffolded shaft that leads up into the [[Kakariko Village Graveyard]]. Within the cellar there is also a large room blocked by an [[Owl Statue]].

Link first visits the Sanctuary as a [[Wolf Link|wolf]] while Kakariko Village is covered in [[Twilight]], during his search for Eldin's [[Tears of Light]]. Here, he discovers that Renado has been taking care of the Ordon children all along, protecting them from the [[Shadow Beast]]s that prowl the village.<ref>{{Cite|We are safe as long as we remain in here, child. Be at ease.|Renado|TP}}</ref> Later on in the game, Link returns to the Sanctuary, where Renado and [[Gor Coron]] tell Link how to [[Retrieval of Ilia's Memory|retrieve Ilia's memory]].<ref>{{Cite|Fortune has smiled upon us! If things go well, we should be able to help Ilia recover her memory! The Goron elder believes that to help her regain her lost memories, we must piece together the fragments of her past... Which means... If we work our way backward from the very place where she lost her memory, we may find something that will restore it.|Renado|TP}}</ref> After the young hero obtains [[Ilia's Charm]] from [[Impaz]] in the [[Hidden Village]], Link must show it to Ilia at the Sanctuary, where she will recover her memory and thus give Link the [[Horse Call]].

[[Shad]] can also be found in the cellar after Link completes the [[Temple of Time (Dungeon)|Temple of Time]]. Once the young hero obtains the [[Ancient Sky Book]] from Impaz, he must show it to Shad, who will read it and cause for the [[Dominion Rod]] to reactivate.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? Ah! It's Sky Writing! Where in blazes did you get this? You don't suppose this could be the word that breaks the seal...|Shad|TP}}</ref> When Link obtains the missing [[Sky Character]]s and completes the Ancient Sky Book, Shad will able to read it fully, thus permitting Link to move the Owl Statue. Doing so reveals the secret room in the Sanctuary's cellar.<ref>{{Cite|Is this that book from last time? ...Hm? Are there more letters in the word than the last time I looked at this? How did you investigate the... Link, this is amazing! Amazing! Now THIS must be the word we need! Let's try it immediately! Ah! It has taken on the same shape as the other statues... What can this mean?|Shad|TP}}</ref> It is here that the broken [[Sky Cannon]] can be found, which Link must [[Warping|warp]] to [[Lake Hylia]] for [[Fyer]] to fix.<ref>{{Cite|That seems to be broken, so you'd do well to show it to a cannon expert first, I should say!|Shad|TP}}</ref>

==={{ALBW|-}}===
[[File:ALBW Priest.png|thumb|233x233px|left|{{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Sanctuary}} interior in ''A Link Between Worlds'']]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', the {{Term|ALBW|Sanctuary}} in [[Hyrule]] is located north of [[Hyrule Castle]] and northeast from the [[Blacksmith's Forge]]. Link must travel to the Sanctuary in order to deliver the [[Forgotten Sword]] to the [[Captain]]. The interior of the Sanctuary is accessible only via a secret passage located in the [[Graveyard]] to the east of the Sanctuary,<ref>{{Cite|Dampé once heard the Priest talking about a secret way into the Sanctuary, hidden right in my Graveyard.|Dampé|ALBW}}</ref> which Link must navigate since the doors are locked upon [[Yuga]]'s entrance.<ref>{{Cite|Th-th-the doors are shut tight. Can't get them open|Dampé|ALBW}}</ref> The secret passage contains the [[Lantern|Lamp]],<ref>{{Cite|You got the lamp!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> an item used to provide light in the passageway. Upon Link's arrival inside the Sanctuary, Yuga attacks [[Seres]], turning her into a [[painting]].

In [[Lorule]], the Sanctuary is replaced by a cave containing [[Master Ore]]. It is inhabited by the [[Philosopher]]. Since the Sanctuary is not reachable from other [[Fissure]]s to Lorule, Link must use the Fissure located inside the Hyrulean Sanctuary to reach the cave in Lorule. In order to access the [[Treasure Chest]] containing the Master Ore, Link must navigate a passage similar to the one found in Hyrule. At the end of the passage, Link enters the cave on a ledge and must [[Wall Merging|merge]] on the wall to reach the Ore.

==={{EoW|-}}===
[[File:EoW Sanctuary Interrior .jpg|200px|thumb|Inside of ''Echoes of Wisdom''’s Sanctuary]]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]'', the Sanctuary, located north of Hyrule Castle, has been entirely consumed by a [[rift]]. Once [[Princess Zelda]] and [[Tri]] mend the rift, the former can speak with the priest to hear rumors from across Hyrule.

== Trivia ==
[[File:LinkPraying.png|thumb|''A Link to the Past'' artwork of Link praying to a crucifix in the Sanctuary]]

* Interestingly, in the Japanese strategy guide book of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', a rare artwork can be seen showing [[Link]] inside Sanctuary privately praying to [[wikipedia:Jesus_in_Christianity|Jesus Christ]] on a cross, known as a [[wikipedia:Crucifix|crucifix]], a reference to [[wikipedia:Christianity|Christianity]] beliefs. This is supported by the fact that in that religion, the Sanctuary itself is known as a church.
** Although it is implied this religion was once planned for the franchise as seen with early [[Shield|Shields]] depicting the [[wikipedia:Christian_cross|Christian cross]] and the [[wikipedia:Bible|Bible]] ([[Book of Magic]]) was used as an item in the original ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', it must be noted that this no longer applies to current ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|series]] canon, as since ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', [[Nintendo]] removed all real-world connections and created its own fictional culture to avoid [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda series|controversy]].

==Nomenclature==
In the Japanese version of {{ALttP|-}}, the {{Term|ALttP|Sanctuary}} is named {{Romanize|教会|Kyōkai|Church|2}}. [[Nintendo of America]] changed it to {{Term|ALttP|Sanctuary}} in accordance with its policy on religious content.{{Fact|ALttP}} Nintendo of Europe mostly followed suit for its localizations, with the exception of the German one, which uses the term Kathedrale (''Cathedral'').
