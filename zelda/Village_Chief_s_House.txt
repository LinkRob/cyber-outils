The {{Term/Store|Village Chief's House||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 267}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House}} is found in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, housing {{Term|BotW|Reede|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Clavia|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Karin|link}}. The family sleeps inside at night, and it is also where they take refuge during {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}. It is the largest house in the village.

Outside of the {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House}} is the town's {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}}, at which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can exchange four {{Plural|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for a {{Term|BotW|Heart Container|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Stamina Vessel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It appears you have claimed {{Color|BotW Red|four}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Spirit Orbs}}. In exchange for {{Color|BotW Red|four}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Spirit Orbs}}, I will amplify your being. So tell me what it is that you desire.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> There is also a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can sit at to pass the time, as well as an {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}} {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}}. The four {{Plural|BotW|Jar|link}} next to the front door can be broken for supplies, as well. The {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House|display=House's}} shed has a {{Term|BotW|Farmer's Pitchfork|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Farming Hoe|link}}, while a {{Term|BotW|Hightail Lizard|link}} can be found behind the building.

In the loft, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can read the {{Term|BotW|Girl's Notebook|link}}, which details a recipe for {{Term|BotW|Pumpkin Stew|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And there you have it! A tasty {{Color|BotW Blue|pumpkin stew}}!|Girl's Notebook|BotW}}</ref> Below the loft is a basket, which contains two {{Plural|BotW|Apple}}.
