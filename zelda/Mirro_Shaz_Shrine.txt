The {{Term/Store|Mirro Shaz Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Mirro Shaz Shrine}}<br/>Tempered Power}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Tempered Power''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The Shrine is located at {{Term|BotW|Pico Pond|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. It is unguarded and sits in plain sight north of the pond. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mirro Shaz|link}}, who introduces the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Mirro Shaz. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Mirro Shaz|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
There are three parts to {{Term|BotW|Mirro Shaz Shrine}}'s Trial. The first two parts must be completed to finish, while the third optional part rewards a treasure.

The first part of the Trial consists of a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Iron Sledgehammer|link}}, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}}. Using the {{Term|BotW|Iron Sledgehammer}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must line up directly behind the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}, facing toward the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} in the water. Using {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to hit it five times with the Iron Sledgehammer, which launches it towards the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. Hitting the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} too few or too many times will cause it to be launched either short or far of the goal, landing in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. If performed correctly, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} will land in the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}, unlocking the second part of the trial. The {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} resets the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} if the goal is missed.

In the second part of the Trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a stone block on a rail, at the end of which is a stone slab. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use {{Term|BotW|Stasis}} on the stone block and hit it straight up toward the stone slab. This will change the orientation of the slab, turning it into a ramp and allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to access the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}.

Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Mirro Shaz}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} grants {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Mirro Shaz|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Mirro Shaz}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Mirro Shaz|BotW}}</ref>

To the left of the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, there is a path that leads down to the third and final optional part of the Trial. The third part of the Trial has a similar set up to the first, with a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Iron Sledgehammer}}, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}}. Another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} is stuck behind a portcullis. 

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can apply the same tactics to hit the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} into the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. However, this time the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} must be hit exactly five times with the {{Term|BotW|Iron Sledgehammer}} and once with a smaller {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}. When this is performed correctly, the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}} will activate and raise the portcullis, allowing Link access to the final Treasure Chest. Inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain a {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}.
