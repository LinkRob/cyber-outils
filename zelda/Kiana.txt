==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kiana}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman who lives in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}. From 5:00 AM to 3:00 PM, {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} can be found standing next to a clothes line outside her home, humming to herself as she works.<ref>{{Cite|Humm humm...|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to her, she asks if he is a traveler.<ref>{{Cite|Are you a traveler?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> She explains that {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village}} is a small, proud fishing town.<ref>{{Cite|This is Lurelin Village. We're a small fishing town and proud of it!|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> Her husband, {{Term|BotW|Sebasto|link}}, is a fisherman, and she believes her sons {{Term|BotW|Kinov|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Zuta|link}} will follow in her footsteps one day.<ref name="Kiana"/>

Turning to {{Term|BotW|Kinov}}, {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} asks him what she should make for dinner.<ref>{{Cite|What should I cook for tonight's dinner?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kinov}} requests {{Term|BotW|Seafood Paella|link}} because he likes the way his mother makes it.<ref>{{Cite|Make your {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}}! It's so good and yummy and greet!|Kinov|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} decides to go along with his suggestion.<ref>{{Cite|That settles it! I'll make some {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}}.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> However, she admits to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she is missing two ingredients for this recipe: {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We seem to be a bit short on ingredients though. We'll need {{Color|BotW Red|goat butter}} and a {{Color|BotW Red|hearty blueshell snail}}.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref>

Since she is so busy with housework, {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} is unable to shop for the ingredients herself.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so busy with housework that I don't have time to go shopping for ingredients.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he could retrieve them for her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I know! Could you go and get me the ingredients we're missing?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} informs him that she will be waiting.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure.] Really? I'm happy to hear that. I'll be waiting!|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> After she finishes speaking, the "{{Term|BotW|What's for Dinner?|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has the ingredients that {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} needs, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} to give them to her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You have the ingredients! Can I have them?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to hand them over, {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} will take the ingredients and start {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure.] All right, I'll take those, then. Now to start cooking.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> To thank {{Term|BotW|Link}}, she gives him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a little something...|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> She also invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to eat with her family, since he was able to find the ingredients she was missing.<ref>{{Cite|Since you found the missing ingredients, why don't you eat with us? It'll be done soon.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Seafood Paella}} of his own to eat, encouraging him to dig in.<ref>{{Cite|Go on, dig in! It's deeeee-lish!|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

Starting at 5:00 AM, {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} stands in front of the clothes line, where she begins to debate what she should make for dinner.<ref>{{Cite|Now what should I cook for tonight's dinner?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> From 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM, {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} sits at a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} next to her home, which she welcomes {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use.<ref>{{Cite|Do you cook? Feel free to use our cooking pot if you need it!|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> As her family begins to gather to eat, she reveals that she made {{Term|BotW|Seafood Paella}} for dinner.<ref>{{Cite|We're havin' {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}} tonight!|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> If it ever {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Rains}} while she is still outside, {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} will run to her house with her hands on her head.

At 9:00 PM, {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} will walk into the house next to her own to put her sons to bed.<ref>{{Cite|Can't talk now. It's past the kids' bedtime. Whatever you need will have to wait until tomorrow.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> She watches over {{Term|BotW|Kinov}} and {{Term|BotW|Zuta}} as they sleep, where she warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be careful not to wake them.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Shhhh! You'll wake the kids.}}|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> She goes to sleep in her house at 10:00 PM.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, don't you think it's a little late to be making small talk? We're going to bed. Come back tomorrow.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Zzz...|Kiana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Mmm...|Kiana|BotW}}</ref>
