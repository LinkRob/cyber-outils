The {{Term/Store|Dueling Peaks Tower||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dueling Peaks Tower|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Tower}} is a [[Sheikah Tower]] that is located at the base of the [[Dueling Peaks]] in [[West Necluda]]. The base of the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Tower|display=Tower}} is set in the [[Squabble River]], where it is surrounded by rock pillars that jut towards the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Tower|display=Tower}}. Scanning the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Tower|display=Tower}} adds the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Tower Region|link}} to its {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}.

There is a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a [[Soldier's Spear]] behind a bombable wall to the east of the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Tower|display=Tower}}.
