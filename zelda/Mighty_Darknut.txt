{{Term/Store|Mighty Darknut|Mighty Darknuts|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are mini-bosses and enemies in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 192}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TWW|Mighty Darknut}}
Mighty Darknuts are captain-rank [[Darknut]]s that are stronger and more heavily-armored than other Darknuts.<ref name="Figurine">{{Cite|These are Darknut captains. Some wear capes that must be burned off before their armor straps can be cut.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> All Mighty Darknuts are equipped with red capes, which must be burned or cut off before [[Link]] can attack the armor straps that hide underneath.

Four Mighty Darknuts can be found on the final floor of the [[Savage Labyrinth]], which can only be accessed after getting the [[Mirror Shield#The Wind Waker|Mirror Shield]] in the [[Earth Temple#Wind Waker|Earth Temple]]. Two more Mighty Darknuts appear in the [[Master Sword]]'s chamber after returning to [[Hyrule Castle#The Wind Waker|Hyrule Castle]], and an additional two guarding the door to [[Puppet Ganon]] in [[Ganon's Castle#The Wind Waker|Ganon's Tower]].

==Trivia==
*Although the Nintendo Gallery mentions that only "some" wear capes,<ref name="Figurine"/> only caped Darknuts are considered to be acceptable for a Mighty Darknut [[Figurine]].
