==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Pumpkin Stew}} are meals that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Fresh Milk|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The only way to make regular {{Term|BotW|Pumpkin Stew}} is to add ingredients to the recipe that contradict the tough effect. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat them to restore some of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These meals are favored in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Girl's Notebook|link}}, which can be found in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, details the recipe for Pumpkin Stew. The author describes it as a secret recipe that will surprise the most knowledgeable of fathers, instructing the reader to start with some Fortified Pumpkins.<ref>{{Cite|For a secret recipe that'll surprise even the most knowledgeable of dads, start with some {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkins}}.|Girl's Notebook|BotW}}</ref> Next, she suggests to add Tabantha Wheat for a "wheaty flavor", and then to let the Fresh Milk and Goat Butter simmer.<ref>{{Cite|Throw in some {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}} to give the stew a nice wheaty flavor, then let some {{Color|BotW Blue|fresh milk}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}} simmer.|Girl's Notebook|BotW}}</ref> The recipe is concluded by telling the reader they should now have Pumpkin Stew.<ref>{{Cite|And there you have it! A tasty {{Color|BotW Blue|pumpkin stew}}!|Girl's Notebook|BotW}}</ref>

Only one variation of {{Term|BotW|Pumpkin Stew}} can be made:

* '''Tough Pumpkin Stew''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Pumpkin Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Simply simmer a fortified pumpkin to make this dish. A favorite in Kakariko Village.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using the default recipe. When these stews are eaten, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will receive a boost to his defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
The recipe for {{Term|HWAoC|Pumpkin Stew}} is earned by completing the {{Term|HWAoC|Side Quest|display=Quest|link}} "{{Term|HWAoC|The Pumpkin Hunt|link}}". It is made using three {{Plural|HWAoC|Fortified Pumpkin}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Tabantha Wheat}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Fresh Milk}}, and three {{Plural|HWAoC|Goat Butter}}. When prepared before a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} or {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} will recover health for every {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} KO.
{{Noncanon|End}}
