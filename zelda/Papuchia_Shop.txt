{{Name|Fan|Papuchia Shop}} is a location in {{ST}}. Situated in the southwest section of [[Papuchia Village]], it is a small shop that offers a variety of common items for sell, and it's the only shop in the game where [[arrow]]s can be bought. It also offers [[bomb]]s at one of the cheapest prices available. The shopkeeper is {{Term|ST|Kogane|link}}.
