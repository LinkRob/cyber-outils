The '''Fairy's Scroll''' is an {{Term|TCT|Item|link}} in {{TCT}}.{{Name Ref|TCT}}

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TCT|Fairy's Scroll}} is one of the main {{Plural|TCT|Item}} in the book since it is required to figure out what three objects are necessary to free {{Term|TCT|Link|link}} from inside the {{Term|TCT|Crystal}}.

{{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|link}} can only obtain the {{Term|TCT|Fairy Scroll}} if she used the {{Plural|TCT|Magic Arrow|link}} to defeat one of the {{Plural|TCT|Gibdo|link}} inside the {{Term|TCT|Cavern (The Crystal Trap)|link}}. From the corpse of the {{Term|TCT|Gibdo}} appears a {{Term|TCT|Blue Fairy|link}} who explains that she was being kept a prison by the foe.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Above the ashes, a blue glow floats in the air. 'A fairy!' Zelda cries with joy. 'Yes, a fairy,' says the little sprite, beating her wings above the gibdo's ashes. 'And one that's glad to be rid of that putrid gibdo. He's been holding me prisoner for I don't know how long!' |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 19}}</ref> {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}} informs the {{Term|TCT|Blue Fairy|display=Fairy}} about her quest, and although the {{Term|TCT|Blue Fairy}} says she cannot stay with the {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|display=Princess}}, she gives her the {{Term|TCT|Fairy Scroll}} and promptly disappears.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'I'm sorry I can't stay with you, but maybe this will help.' She drops a scroll at Zelda's feet. 'What is it?' Zelda asks. But the fairy doesn't answer. 'Good luck!' she cries. Then she vanishes. |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 19}}</ref> {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}} picks it up to find a riddle within the {{Term|TCT|Fairy Scroll|display=Scroll}}, which says, "Look for something hotter than fire, colder than ice, and sweeter than sugar."<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Zelda picks up the scroll. 'It's a riddle,' she says. 'I wonder what it means?' 'Look for something hotter than ___, colder than ___, and sweeter than ___.' |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 19}}</ref>

As {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}} continues her journey, she will find the three {{Plural|TCT|Item}} that the {{Term|TCT|Fairy Scroll}} was referring to. In order, they are the {{Term|TCT|Magnifying Glass|link}}, {{Term|TCT|Ganon|link}}'s {{Term|TCT|Handprint|link}}, and {{Term|TCT|Magic Honey|link}}. If the {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|display=Princess}} fails to obtain all three {{Plural|TCT|Item}}, she will be unable to rescue {{Term|TCT|Link}} and will ultimately cause his demise.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Slowly the crystal changes color. It becomes darker and darker until Zelda can no longer see Link inside. The crystal is turning to stone! [...] 'He's gone forever. You failed, little princess,' he mocks her. 'You failed!' |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 28}}</ref>
