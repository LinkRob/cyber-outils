{{Term/Store|Training||Series, TMC}} is a mechanic in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|So? Will you accept this new {{Color|TMC Blue|training}}?|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Training}} is used by the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}} to teach {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} new {{Plural|TMC|Sword Technique|link}}, and comes in two forms. The first (used by {{Term|TMC|Grayblade|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Grimblade|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade I|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Waveblade|link}}) involves the use of each {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|display=Brother}}'s {{Term|TMC|Possession Technique|link}} to possess {{Term|TMC|Link}} and demonstrate the {{Term|TMC|Sword Technique}}. The second form is visualization training (used by {{Term|TMC|Greatblade|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Scarblade|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Splitblade|link}}),<ref>{{Cite|I take visualization {{Color|TMC Blue|training}} very seriously, I'll have you know!|Scarblade|TMC}}</ref> where the ability is simply imparted to {{Term|TMC|Link}} without a demonstration.
