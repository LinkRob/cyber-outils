The {{Term/Store|Ghost Lantern||Series, TPHD}} is an item in {{TPHD}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|TPHD}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Ghost Lantern is received from [[Jovani]] after obtaining 20 [[Poe Soul]]s.<ref>{{Cite|It must be though finding those creepy crawlies, right? Take this Lantern with you!|Jovani|TPHD}}</ref> When equipped, it lights up if an [[Imp Poe]] is in the area highlighted in the map or within a {{Term|TPHD|Cave|link}} that can be entered in that area.<ref>{{Cite|Ghost Lantern<br/>A mysterious lantern that reacts to ghosts.<br/>◆ Equip it with {{Icon|TPHD Y}}, {{Icon|TPHD X}}, or {{Icon|TPHD R}}. It doesn't use oil.<br/>◆ It's normally dark but will light up day or night if a ghost is nearby.<br/>◆ It will stay lit throughout complicated areas like dungeons if there is a ghost to be found.|Inventory|TPHD}}</ref> In dungeons, it lights up if there are Imp Poes anywhere inside. Once all the Imp Poes in the area or dungeon are defeated, the Ghost Lantern goes out. It can also be used during the day, does not require [[Lantern Oil]] to work, and cannot be used to light {{Plural|TPHD|Torch|link}}.
