{{Term/Store|Vaati Reborn||Series, TMC}} is a {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Vaati Reborn|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|TMC|Vaati Reborn}} is {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}'s first form. His main attacks are shooting laser beams from the small eyes orbiting around him, and casting fire at {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}. He also often teleports around the room to evade {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s attacks. {{Term|TMC|Link}} must destroy all of the eyes that orbit around {{Term|TMC|Vaati Reborn}} to reveal his weak spot: an open eye in his chest. After dealing enough damage, {{Term|TMC|Vaati Reborn}}'s attacks will become more intense, he will teleport more often, and black energy spheres will appear around the eyes, protecting them from damage until {{Term|TMC|Link}} uses the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}} to suck them in.

After enough damage is dealt, {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms into his second form, {{Term|TMC|Vaati Transfigured|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The boss music for Vaati reborn is the same music that played when Vaati broke the Picori Blade in the beginning of the game.
**The theme itself is a remix of the theme used for Vaati's second form in ''Four Sword''.
*If the player fights Vaati Reborn after the latter successfully drains Zelda's Light Force, he will be rendered unwinnable.
