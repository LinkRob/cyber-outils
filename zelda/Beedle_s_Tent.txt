{{Term/Store|Beedle's Tent||Series, SSBU}} is a location in {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite|Beedle's Tent|N/A|SSBU}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Beedle's Tent is a facility that is operated by [[Beedle]]. It is located in the wooded area to the west, and features {{Ssb|Snack}}s, {{Ssb|Support Item}}s, and {{Ssb|Skill Sphere}}s, along with [[Primary Spirit]]s and [[Support Spirit]]s based on living creatures.<ref>{{Cite|Adventure: Store Inventory (Pt. 1)<br/>In Sheldon's store, it's easier to find spirits related to weapons. In Anna's store, you'll find more spirits with equipment, and in Beedle's store, you'll find more living creatures.|Tips and Tricks|SSBU}}</ref> Beedle's Tent is only accessible after the Beedle [[Master Spirit]] has been obtained.<ref>{{Cite|Beedle's location is now open!|N/A|SSBU}}</ref> Afterwards, Beedle's Tent can be accessed from its location in the [[World of Light]] or from the Shopping section of the Collection sub-menu in the Spirits menu.
