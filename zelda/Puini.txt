'''Puini''' is {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Sir Raven|link}}'s horse in the {{OoA|Himekawa}}. Although a minor character, it is instrumental in the defeating the sorceress {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran|link}}. The horse accompanies Raven on all of his travels and resides with him in the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Hidden Village|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} is first seen when {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Sir Raven|link}} returns from speaking with {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Queen Ambi|link}} and {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Nayru (Oracle)|link}}, the latter who is currently possessed by {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran|link}}. Both {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} and Raven sense {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link|link}} following them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I sense him, too, Puini...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 64}}</ref> Raven dismounts the steed and both disappear around a building. {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} follows them and is confused to see Raven fishing.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Why's he just sitting there fishing?|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 65}}</ref> {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} notices {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} and nuzzles the hero while emitting a neigh, alerting Raven to his presence.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Uh oh! He saw me!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 65}}</ref> After a brief confrontation between the two, Raven allows {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} to mount {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} with him. {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} brings the two knights to the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Hidden Village|link}}, where Raven has brought individuals who are in danger of execution.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... But before they can be executed, I bring them here.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 82}}</ref> Raven tethers {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} as he leaves to check the fields,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Wait here a minute. I need to go look at the fields...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 72}}</ref> leaving the horse with {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} and {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Roperi|link}}. {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} is seen later on when Raven rides it to {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Queen Ambi}}'s {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Black Tower|link}}, where a fight has started with the workers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Raven! Fights are breaking out at the tower! The workers are growing angry!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 89}}</ref> Once the rebellion has been quelled, Raven dismounts {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} and leads it back to the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Hidden Village}}, unaware that {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran}} is following them. {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran}} then possesses {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Ralph|link}} and uses him to burn Raven's {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Mystery Seed|link}}, which are her fatal weakness.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... Demons that possess people can't stand Mystery Seeds.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 123}}</ref> She sends {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Ramrock|link}} to destroy the village.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Rise, Ramrock! Veran, the Sorceress of Shadows, commands it!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 127}}</ref> After {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} defeats it, {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Queen Ambi}}'s army arrives and captures Raven. {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} spots {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} tied to a wooden pole and rides him with {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Ralph}} to {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Sir Raven}}'s rescue. The three arrive in time to save Raven from execution, but {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran}} releases {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Nayru (Oracle)}} and possesses {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Queen Ambi}} instead. {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Nayru (Oracle)}} awakens and transports the group to modern day {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Labrynna|link}}, sending them back after a plan is formed. {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Nayru (Oracle)}} speeds the growth of {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Roperi}}'s {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Mystery Tree|link}} sprout so it will grow the needed {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Mystery Seed}} to defeat {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran}}. Despite this, {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Ralph}} still leaves to confront {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran}} on his own. When the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Mystery Tree}} produces a {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Mystery Seed}}, {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Sir Raven}} orders {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} to take {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} as his mount.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Take my horse!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 158}}</ref> {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} and the knights rush to the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Black Tower|link}} and use the Seed against {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran}}.

After {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Veran}} and {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Ganon|link}} are defeated, {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} prepares to return to his own time, at which point {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} expresses his desire to accompany {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You want to go with him?|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 190}}</ref> Raven acknowledges his steed's choice and presents {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} to {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} as a parting gift.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Puini wants to accompany you.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 190}}</ref> {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} rides {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} to his grandparents' farm, where his grandfather remarks that {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Puini}} is a fine steed.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You've even got a fine horse!...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 192}}</ref>

==Trivia==
