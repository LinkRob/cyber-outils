'''''Mini Guide - The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild''''' is a digital guide published by [[Piggyback Interactive Limited]].<ref name="Listing Info"/>

==Features==
This Mini Guide for {{BotW}} was released as a [[My Nintendo Rewards|My Nintendo Reward]] to be purchased for 75 platinum points. The guide contained the first chapter of "[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild — The Complete Official Guide]]" guide for {{BotW|-}} and was released in English, French, and Spanish.<ref name="Listing Info"/><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This official digital mini-guide for the Legend of Zelda™: Breath of the Wild game is just the thing to help you get started on this epic adventure. This digital guide contains the first chapter of [https://www.amazon.com/Legend-Zelda-Complete-Official-Collectors/dp/1911015222 The Complete Official Guide] and offers a sneak-peek of game features, including combat, landmarks, commands, and puzzle features.|author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= August 24, 2022|url= https://my.nintendo.com/rewards/823e7e04555dac0f|title= Mini Guide - The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|site= my.nintendo.com|type= }}</ref>

A version of the Mini Guide was also available for 10 gold points until April 1, 2019, and included German and Italian translations.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= September 19, 2022|url= https://my.nintendo.com/rewards/849481ac511151b2|title= Mini Guide - The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|site= my.nintendo.com|type= }}</ref>
