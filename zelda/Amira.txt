{{Term/Store|Amira||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Amira|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Amira}} is a rather unfriendly, gossip loving woman who resides in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} with her family.<ref name="Family"/>. At nine in the evening, {{Term|BotW|Amira}} heads to sleep on the top floor of the {{Term|BotW|East Wind|link}} with her children.<ref>{{Cite|Excuse me, but I'm going to bed. Can you please get out of my way?|Amira|BotW}}</ref> She is clearly tired and sighs when Link attempts to converse with her, irritated with the company.<ref>{{Cite|Some of us have things to do, y'know? Would you mind leaving me alone?|Amira|BotW}}</ref> She is an uneasy sleeper, often grunting or mumbling as she dreams.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Urk...}}|Amira|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...Whozat? {{Small|Zzzzzrrmblle...}}|Amira|BotW}}</ref> If Link tries to speak with her in the morning, she asks him to leave her alone.<ref>{{Cite|Um... Would you mind not following me?|Amira|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...What? Go hassle someone else.|Amira|BotW}}</ref>

During the day, {{Term|BotW|Amira}} be found chatting with fellow villager {{Term|BotW|Nikki|link}} at the water trough next to the {{Term|BotW|East Wind|link}}. However, she despises others who try to listen in on her conversation and will harshly tell {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} off if he tries to speak with her.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Captain Snoop! You want to get out of my personal space or what? Ever heard of the concept of a PRIVATE conversation?|Amira|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to speak to Nikki instead, Amira admonishes him for trying to eavesdrop.<ref>{{Cite|Honestly, young people these days... What kind of scamp just {{Color|BotW Red|eavesdrops}} like it's no big deal? How rude.|Amira|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Amira}} make it to the trough before {{Term|BotW|Nikki}}, she will say that she is waiting for someone and ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to leave.<ref>{{Cite|I'm waiting for someone.<br/>And it's not you. Can you go away?|Amira|BotW}}</ref> To listen to their conversation, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must hide out behind the trough. They talk about a {{Term|BotW|Purah|link|display=little girl}} who has veen spotted at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, and that reminds me, my little one was gabbing about something interesting the other day... Apparently a {{Color|BotW Red|young girl}} was seen going in and out of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab}}.|Nikki|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Amira}} thinks that perhaps the people living in the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|display=Lab}} adopted her,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, weird. Think they adopted or something?|Amira|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Nikki}} disagrees.<ref>{{Cite|But the {{Color|BotW Blue|director}}...just doesn't seem the adopting type, you know?|Nikki|BotW}}</ref>

Like most residents of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Amira}} will have a reaction if {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her while carrying the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}. She notes that it is dangerous and says that he should not run around with it.<ref>{{Cite|What do you have there?<br/>Um, that's really dangerous.<br/>You shouldn't run around with that.|Amira|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her again, she scolds him for carrying something so dangerous and tells him to put it away.<ref>{{Cite|That thing you're holding... It's dangerous! So put it away!|Amira|BotW}}</ref>
