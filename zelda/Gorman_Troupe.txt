The {{Term/Store|Gorman Troupe||Series, MM, MM3D}} is group of carnival entertainers who were scheduled to perform at the [[Carnival of Time]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Madame Aroma, I am Gorman. It has been some time... Thank you for letting us perform at this year's Carnival of Time.|Gorman|MM}}</ref> However, since [[Lulu]] of [[The Indigo-Go's]] has lost her voice, Toto must cancel their performance at the carnival,<ref>{{Cite|Actually, there's been an unusual accident in Great Bay... And due to this unusual accident, Lulu, the diva in The Indigo-Go's, has lost her voice. The details are quite long, so I'll spare you. At any rate... I must cancel our performance at this year's Carnival of Time.|Toto|MM}}</ref> and as such, [[Madame Aroma]] is forced to cancel the Gorman Troupe's opening performance as well.<ref>{{Cite|The opening performance I've asked you to do...They've canceled! Just now!|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref> [[Gorman]], the troupe leader,<ref>{{Cite|Gorman, leader of a traveling troupe / Has fine clothes, but a frightful face|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> does not tell the rest of the members about their acts being canceled, drowning his sorrows at the [[Milk Bar]] instead,<ref>{{Cite|Though we're running out of time, the troupe leader won't wake up. In times like this, it means the leader has something he's hiding from us.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> and so the group continues rehearsing for their routines. The members of the troupe are all staying at the [[Stock Pot Inn]] during their [[Clock Town]] visit.

They are led by the Circus Leader, Gorman. Other members include:

*[[Rosa Sisters|Judo and Marilla Rosa]] are twin sisters and dancers of the troupe.<ref>{{Cite|Rosa Sisters, twin dancers / Members of the Gorman Troupe|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> During the day, they are both seen pacing the floors of the Stock Pot Inn, trying to come up with new dance moves for their unfinished choreography.<ref>{{Cite|Doot-doo-dooo... No time is left. The dance isn't finished yet. The song is now done, but the dance is not.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> At night, they are practicing their routine in [[West Clock Town]], and [[Link]] can help them learn Kamaro's dance by wearing [[Kamaro's Mask]].
*[[Guru-Guru]] is the musician of the group.<ref>{{Cite|Guru-Guru / Musician in the Gorman Troupe|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref>  He is found practicing in his room of the Stock Pot Inn during the day or by the [[Laundry Pool]] at night. Guru-Guru wishes someone would teach the Rosa Sisters some new dance moves,<ref>{{Cite|I wish someone would teach them one...A fancy step for these girls. ...Do-dee-do...|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> and he also rewards Link with the [[Bremen Mask]] for listening to his tale.<ref>{{Cite|I wanted it because it was the leader's mask... But I no longer need it... I give it to my guest.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref>
*[[Twin Jugglers]] are the jugglers of the troupe and bring comic relief to their acts.<ref>{{Cite|We're the twin jugglers from the Gorman Troupe!|Twin Jugglers|MM}}</ref> They practice in [[East Clock Town]] and can be seen playing cards in their Stock Pot Inn room during the night. The Twin Jugglers are the only members of the troupe who do not have their own [[Bombers' Notebook]] entry.

==Trivia==
*Following their appearance in {{MM|-}}, a [[Troupe of Performers]] appeared in {{OoS}}, and another one in {{TP}}.
