The '''Slumber Dojo''' is a location in {{EoW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Slumber Dojo}}|Map|EoW}}</ref>

==Overview==
===''Echoes of Wisdom''===
The Slumber Dojo can be found in [[Kakariko Village]], where Zelda can enter dreams to get rewards if she manages to complete the dream within the goal time.

Special rewards are also given to Zelda when she completes certain numbers of dreams. With 2 dreams complete, she receives a [[Piece of Heart]]. With 4 dreams completed, Zelda will be given the [[First Mastery]]. When Zelda completes 6 dreams, she is given the [[Heirloom Katana]], which she needs for the [[Chop 'em in Two!]] quest. When she finishes 8, she gets the [[Second Mastery]], and will be given another [[Piece of Heart]] once she completes 11 dreams. Once Zelda finishes 14 dreams, she will receive the [[Final Mastery]] and she will be able to enter the 15th dream, [[The Final Titans' Gathering]]. Once she completes the final dream, she will be awarded the [[Green Tunic]].
