==Overview==
At the {{Term|TotK|Great Plateau|link}}'s {{Term|TotK|Temple of Time|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} heard a godly voice coming out from a large {{Term|TotK|Goddess Statue|link}}. It gave Link a task, to find four eyes on the surface of the Great Plateau, throw them into the {{Plural|TotK|chasm|link}}, and bring the eyes to them. They will reward Link with a blessing for bringing their eyes back.

Link ventured around the Great Plateau and finds the missing eyes. He threw them all down into the nearest chasms and took them down the rail tracks and to the source of the voice: A large {{Term|TotK|Bargainer Statue|link}}. Once all the eyes are returned, it expressed its gratitude and explained why their eyes were removed.<ref>{{Cite|In ancient times, my eyes were stolen and sealed away. For eons I have awaited one who would hear my voice and return them to me... And here you are.|Bargainer Statue|TotK}}</ref>

As promised, the statue gave Link a blessing:<ref>{{Cite|As promised, I will repay your kindness by bestowing good fortune upon you.|Bargainer Statue|TotK}}</ref> A choice of either a {{Term|TotK|Heart Container|link}} or a {{Term|TotK|Stamina Vessel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your being overflows with vigor. There is no need for more <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Stamina Vessels</font>.|Bargainer Statue|TotK}}</ref>
