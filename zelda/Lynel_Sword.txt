{{Plural|BotW|Lynel Sword}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} wielded solely by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}. They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} crafted by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Though most {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} will increase in tier as {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} defeats more {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Lynel}} on {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain|link}} will always remain a regular {{Term|BotW|Lynel}}. This makes it a permanent source of {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Sword}} and other low tier {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, though the {{Term|BotW|Lynel}} on {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain}} remains the same, all other {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} are tiered up to {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}}. However, {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel}} will still make use of {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Sword}}, meaning they are still obtainable.

Because they are made for smashing, {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Sword}} are heavier than standard swords.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Sword}} can conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} due to being made of metal. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, but they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
