{{Term/Store|Bazz||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Bazz|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Bazz}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} and the captain of the guard of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}.<ref name="Captain">{{Cite|Thanks to that, I made great progress with my sword training, and now I am captain of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} guard!|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> He wields a {{Term|BotW|Silverscale Spear|link}}. He is the son of {{Term|BotW|Seggin|link}},<ref name="Seggin"/> and when he was a child, he was the leader of the Big Bad Bazz Brigade. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} taught {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} swordsmanship when he was young.<ref name="Blade">{{Cite|A hundred years ago, you frequently trained me in the art of the blade. Remember?|Bazz|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Bazz}}, he will welcome him to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} and explain that they have been looking for a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} like him.<ref>{{Cite|Hello, and welcome! {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} is currently searching for a Hylian like you.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> He mutters to himself that {{Term|BotW|Link}} seems familiar, believing that they have met a long time ago.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|That said, this Hylian... I get the feeling I've seen him before... Perhaps a long time ago...}}}}|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> 

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} a second time, he is certain that he remembers who {{Term|BotW|Link}} is.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Now I remember! This person is...}}}}|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> However, due to the shorter lifespan of {{Plural|BotW|Hylian}}, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} decides to test him in order to be sure.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|No, but Hylians don't live more than 100 years... I'd better ask, just to be sure...}}|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he knows the Bigg Badd Bazz Brigade password, starting with, "Fluffy white clouds! Clear blue..."<ref>{{Cite|I must ask something of you, traveler. If you know it, speak it now. What is the Big Bad Bazz Brigade password? Fluffy white clouds! Clear blue...|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} successfully guesses the word "Zora", {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} will be convinced that the person he is speaking to is {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yes! There is no mistaking it! You are, indeed, the real Master Link!|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms that he is, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} excitedly proclaims that he knew it.<ref>{{Cite|[It's true.] I knew it! The only people who know that password are members of the Big Bad Bazz Brigade!|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} denies this, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} refutes him, saying only a member of the Big Bad Bazz Brigade could know the password.<ref>{{Cite|[You're wrong.] Nope! The only people who know that password are members of the Big Bad Bazz Brigade!|Bazz|BotW}}</ref>

Noticing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} looks the same as he did 100 years ago, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he has been doing.<ref>{{Cite|That said, you haven't changed at all... But what have you been doing for the last 100 years?|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells him that he was sleeping, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} refuses to believe anyone could sleep so long.<ref>{{Cite|[I was sleeping.] As though you could sleep for that long. Hmph.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is unable to come up with an answer, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} assumes he was doing something important.<ref>{{Cite|[Umm...] Special circumstances, I take it? Well, I suppose a lot had happened in the last 100 years.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> Either way, he repeats that {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} is in need of the help of a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}}, and he urges {{Term|BotW|Link}} to meet with {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway... I am glad to see you here, Master Link. {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} is in dire need of a brave Hylian like yourself. I would ask that you meet with {{Color|BotW Red|King Dorephan}} upstairs. Right away, please.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} has confirmed {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s identity, he will answer questions for him.<ref>{{Cite|Now then, Master Link... What do you need from me?|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> When asked about {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}}, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} explains that he is located upstairs.<ref>{{Cite|[King Dorephan?] {{Color|BotW Red|King Dorephan}} is upstairs.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> He is certain that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must remember him, revealing that he is the father of {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You surely remember him, yes? He is Lady Mipha's father!|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> When asked this question after {{Term|BotW|Link}} has met with {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}}, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} reminds him that {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|display=King}} is the father of both {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} and {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[King Dorephan?] He is the father of Princess Mipha and Prince Sidon.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> He also reveals that things have been difficult for the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} since {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} was defeated by {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Princess Mipha fell to {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}}... 100 years ago. It has certainly been difficult ever since.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref>

After hearing her name from {{Term|BotW|Bazz}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to ask him about {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}. {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} is shocked to hear that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has forgotten {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}.<ref>{{Cite|Master Link... Do not tell me you have forgotten Lady Mipha...|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> He shares that {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} was a princess who was admired and beloved by the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lady Mipha was admired by everyone in the domain. She was the beloved princess of the Zora.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> He starts to elaborate on {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} and {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s relationship,<ref>{{Cite|But then... Do you really not remember? Lady Mipha was...|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> but he decides it would be better if he heard it from {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}}, instead.<ref>{{Cite|... Actually, I think it would be best if you heard this from {{Color|BotW Red|King Dorephan}}.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref>

Asking who {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} is causes him to introduce himself,<ref>{{Cite|Me? I am Bazz.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> revealing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} trained him when he was younger.<ref name="Blade"/> He credits his father {{Term|BotW|Seggin}} surviving the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref name="Seggin"/> He also laments that {{Term|BotW|Seggin}} has not changed much, revealing that he has begun training to increase his resistance to {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}'s sake.<ref>{{Cite|However, the Demon Sergeant... Sorry, the FORMER Demon Sergeant... Well, he is as he ever was. He is, even now, training to extremes by poking {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}} to up his tolerance. He does so for Lady Mipha's sake.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} typically avoid {{Term|BotW|Electricity}}, and he is afraid that {{Term|BotW|Seggin}} may reach his limit soon.<ref>{{Cite|Electricity is banned for all Zora, and for good reason. Although he is adamant, I am sure even he has his limits.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks this question after speaking to {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}}, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} reveals that he was able to become the captain of the guard due to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s training.<ref name="Captain"/> He considers himself to be in {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s debt because of this.<ref>{{Cite|I am in your debt, Master Link.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref>

After the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for saving {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|Master Link! Thank you!|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> While he believes that calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} should have been his job as the captain of the guard, he us unable to handle {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|As captain of the guard, that should've been my responsibility. But {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}} are the one thing I just can't handle.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> However, even though the trouble has passed, he plans to continue protecting {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|Even though {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} has been appeased, I must not let my guard down... I must continue to serve and defend this domain as I always have!|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> He can also be seen in the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}} as {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}}.

During "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song|link}}", which is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} can be found in {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana|link}} alongside his father, {{Term|BotW|Seggin}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches him, he remarks how rare it is to encounter a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} where they are.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? It is rare to see a Hylian here...|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> Upon recognizing {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} warmly greets him, and he thanks him again for calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, how silly of me! It is none other than Master Link! I am happy to see you, old friend. I must sincerely thank you for your help with that {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}} mess.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> He warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that blue-eyed {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} have appeared in {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana}}, which he has heard were last seen during {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}'s trial.<ref>{{Cite|While you are here, I should warn you... {{Color|BotW Red|Blue-eyed Guardians}} have appeared in {{Color|BotW Blue|Upland Zorana}}. Apparently, those things last appeared when {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Mipha}} challenged her sacred trial.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Seggin}} claims that they are harmless, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} has decided to remain alert.<ref>{{Cite|My father insists they are no cause for alarm, but I am on high alert just in case.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has defeated the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} notices the emergence of {{Term|BotW|Sato Koda Shrine|link}}, which confirms to him that they were related to the trial.<ref>{{Cite|When those {{Color|BotW Blue|blue-eyed Guardians}} left, a {{Color|BotW Red|strange shrine}} appeared. I knew those {{Color|BotW Blue|Guardians}} were related to the trial!|Bazz|BotW}}</ref>
