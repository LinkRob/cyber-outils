{{Term/Store|Nackle||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nackle|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Nackle}}. He is a member of a highly civilized race of moles called the Mogma. All Mogmas have a deep and abiding love of treasure.
}}

Nackle is a green-haired [[Mogma]] found in a {{Term|SS|Cave|link}} in [[Eldin Volcano]]. When Link stumbles into him, he is expressing his dismay that [[Bokoblin]]s have taken over their territory.<ref>{{Cite|Grrr... Those creeps just shimmied on in and took over our territory.|Nackle|SS}}</ref> In return for clearing out the Bokoblins, he gives Link the [[Digging Mitts]].<ref>{{Cite|In that case, take these things! They're something pretty special, if I say so myself.|Nackle|SS}}</ref>

== Nomenclature==
Nackle’s name is derived from the metal {{Wp|nickel}}.

Nackle’s and [[Tyto]]'s names were interchanged in the North American, Latin American Spanish, and French localizations. His Japanese name, {{Romanize|ニケル|Nikeru|}}, is derived from {{Romanize|ニッケル|nikkeru}}, Japanese for {{Wp|nickel}}. His name is also derived from nickel in the Canadian French and German versions.
