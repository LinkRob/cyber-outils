The '''Lockup Key''' is an Item in {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{hatnote|”The key to the Deku Scrub lockup, where the no-gooders of the Faron Wetlands go.” — [[Key Items in Echoes of Wisdom|Key Items]]<ref name="Lockup Key">{{Cite|The key to the Deku Scrub lockup, where the no-gooders of the Faron Wetlands go.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}

During the [[A Rift in the Faron Wetlands]] [[Main Quest]], after mending Blossu’s [[rift]], [[Princess Zelda]] is imprisoned in the [[Deku Scrub lockup]], but can use the [[Tri Rod]] to obtain the Lockup Key and escape to recover her items.
