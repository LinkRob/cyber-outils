==Location and Uses==
By drinking Sneaky Elixirs, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can receive a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They'll calm his nerves and silence his footfalls, and he'll be able to move undetected amongst monsters and animals.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Sneaky Elixirs can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|Series|Sunset Firefly|link}}

By adding any of these sneaky ingredients, Link can make a Sneaky Elixir that will also heal him:

* {{Term|Series|Silent Shroom|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Blue Nightshade|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Silent Princess|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Stealthfin Trout|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Sneaky River Snail|link}}
