{{Term/Store|Sheikah Stone|Sheikah Stones|Series, OoT3D, MM3D, SS, CoH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="HH 25"/><ref name="CoH Doors"/> They give [[Link]] [[Vision]]s and hints on how to solve puzzles, find secrets, and defeat [[Boss]]es on his adventure, provided he has reached a point in his quest close to accessing the subject.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|The Great Deku Tree said they help people who are lost or troubled to find their way again. If there's ever anything you need to know, you should try asking one for help.}}|Navi|OoT3D}}</ref>
==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT3D|-}}===
Sheikah Stones have a passage at the bottom for entry in {{OoT3D}}. One Sheikah Stone can be found outside [[Link's House]] and another one in the [[Temple of Time]], as these are the two starting points upon resuming a saved game. If Link talks to one, he will have the option to crawl inside it to bring up the Visions screen. Both Sheikah Stones are absent in the {{MQ}} version of the game.

==={{MM3D|-}}===
A Sheikah Stone appears inside the [[Clock Tower]] in {{MM3D}}. If Link talks to it, he will have the option to crawl inside it to bring up the Visions screen.

==={{SS|-}}===
After Link obtains the [[Practice Sword]] in {{SS}}, a Sheikah Stone will appear and talk to him outside the [[Sparring Hall]]. From then on, the hero can summon a [[Gossip Stone]] by playing the "[[Ballad of the Goddess]]" wherever a group of [[Blessed Butterflies]] appear.

If Link is playing in [[Hero Mode]], the Sheikah Stone will provide him all of the hints upon their first encounter.<ref>{{Cite|I'm a stone of few words so I'll get right to it. I can see you're playing in {{Color|SS Red|Hero Mode}}, so clearly you've mastered all the puzzles out there before. I think that's pretty impressive! Tell you what, I'm going to go ahead and give you access to {{Color|SS Red|all the hints}} right from the start.|Sheikah Stone|SS}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Plural|CoH|Sheikah Stone}} are multifunctional objects in {{CoH}}. When approached, {{Plural|CoH|Sheikah Stone}} will activate and can be used as [[Warping|Warp]] points for the [[Lute]]. After either {{Term|CoH|Link|link}} or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} has been awakened by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, either one of them can be change spot with her by interacting with any {{Term|CoH|Sheikah Stone}}. Other {{Plural|CoH|Sheikah Stone}} can be used to open doors.<ref name="CoH Doors"/>

Some {{Plural|CoH|Sheikah Stone}} require certain conditions to be met before they can be activated, such as defeating all enemies on the screen.<ref>{{Cite|This {{Color|CoH Yellow|Sheikah Stone}} looks a little different.
