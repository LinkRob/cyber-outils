'''''The Legend of Zelda Series for Piano''''' is a sheet music book for piano released on December 16, 2011. Containing 84 pages, the book features 33 songs from almost every single ''The Legend of Zelda'' game released at the time, ranging from the original {{TLoZ}} to {{ST}}.
