Gortram is a large, bearded [[Goron]] who has been living alone in the [[Lanayru Shipyard]] for ages. However, he does not mind this as he gets to ride the mine carts all day long.<ref>{{Cite|I have been here alone for...well, who knows how long. Years. But I get to ride these carts every day, and I never get bored of it!|Gortram|SS}}</ref> He is also an entrepreneur, and runs the [[Rickety Coaster]] mini-game. However, no one ever comes to ride the mine carts.<ref>{{Cite|I am here every day running this coaster, but you are the only one who ever shows up. Could you spread the word? It is lonely out here. And...dry.|Gortram|SS}}</ref>

Gortram is first encountered when Link visits the mine cart station en route to the Shipyard, which is said to hold more clues about the [[Sandship]]. Gortram agrees to allow Link to ride the mine carts, but only after explaining how to ride them safely.<ref>{{Cite|This station here was used by folks heading into the old mines. People still ride the mine carts all over the place, though! I am probably supposed to tell you there is some "safety information" about using the carts posted on the wall over there. Cart riding can be, uh...dangerous at times, so I would suggest you give that stuff a quick read.|Gortram|SS}}</ref> The Goron also asks Link to tell more people about the Rickety Coaster so more people will visit the  mine track. 

After Link defeats [[Tentalus]] and tempers his Sword with the power of [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]]'s [[Sacred Flames|Sacred Flame]], he can return to the Shipyard to find that Gortram has set up a new mini-game requiring Link to race around the Rickety Coaster. He gives Link the option of choosing two tracks: the "Scary" and "Heart-Stopping" Tracks. The Scary Track requires Link to beat a certain time, and upon doing so will earn [[Rupees]] or [[Treasure]] as a reward. On the other hand, the "Heart-Stopping" Track requires that Link beats Gortram's time of 1:05. If Link manages to do so, Gortram will reward him with a [[Piece of Heart]].

==Trivia==
*If Link sits on the stool next to Gortram, he will complain and tell him it is his seat.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, that is my seat! Nobody sits their rear end on my seat but me, got it?|Gortram|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Gortram's name is a portmanteau of Goron and {{Wp|tram}}, a type of rail car.
