The {{Term/Store|Kochi Dye Shop||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|- Kochi Dye Shop -<br/>We live to dye!|Sign|BotW}}</ref> also known as the '''Dye Shop''',<ref>{{Cite|Dye Shop|Map|BotW}}</ref> is a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.

==Features and Overview==
Kochi Dye Shop is the only Dye Shop in [[Hyrule]], it is located in [[Hateno Village]]. Inside the Kochi Dye Shop, [[Link]] can change the color of his [[Armor]].<ref>{{Cite|There's a store in Hateno Village—the {{Color|BotW Blue|Kochi Dye Shop}}—where you can dye your clothes!|Saula|BotW}}</ref> [[Sayge]] operates the Dye Shop, while his wife, [[Senna]], greets visitors outside.<ref>{{Cite|I'm the hardest working greeter there is. Though it hardly seems like work when the weather's so nice...|Senna|BotW}}</ref> Sayge offers to dye Link's entire outfit<ref>{{Cite|Get ready! Because we are going to dye all the clothes you're wearing at once!|Sayge|BotW}}</ref> for 20 [[Rupees]]. Link must also provide five [[Materials]] to create the [[Dye]]. Sayge can also restore pieces of Armor to their original color. In {{TotK}}, Sayge also offers to customize Link's Paraglider for the same fee, if Link provides him with the appropriate [[Fabric]].

The corner of the building serves as a sleeping area for the family. {{Term|BotW|Sefaro|link}}, the son of Sayge and Senna, sleeps at the Kochi Dye Shop at night.
