The {{Term/Store|Ghast|Ghasts|Series, CoH}} is an enemy in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Ghast|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The {{Term|CoH|Ghast}} is a type of wraith-like enemy that only appears after its {{Term|CoH|Ghost|link}} form has been hit. It is responsible for putting a spell on the {{Term|CoH|Prince|link}}, and defeating it will lift the curse on him and cause him to awaken from his slumber. Defeating it is required for obtaining the {{Term|CoH|Flippers}} in {{Term|CoH|Story Mode|link}}.
