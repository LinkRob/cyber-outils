'''Crystal Shards''' are obstacles in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Crystal Shards are hazards encountered only in the [[Shrine of Air]]. They spawn on the sides of a room endless and fly after [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]. However, she can use her [[Magical Shield|Magic Shield]] to protect against their attacks. A Crystal Heart deals a [[Heart]] of damage on Zelda upon hitting her.

Despite being vastly different, Crystal Shards function identically to [[Fireball]]s, except the latter inflicts two Hearts of damage.
