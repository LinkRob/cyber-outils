The {{Term/Store|Item Shop|Item Shops|Series, ALBW}} is the name of two {{Plural|ALBW|Shop|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALBW|publisher= Prima|page= 292}}</ref> One is located in {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}, and its {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} counterpart is located in {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Plural|ALBW|Item Shop}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} are run by their respective worlds' {{Plural|ALBW|Item Seller|link}}. The two {{Plural|ALBW|Shop}}' inventories and prices are identical to those of the two {{Plural|ALBW|Lakeside Item Shop|link}}.

==={{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}===
{{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Item Shop}} is actually owned by the {{Term|ALBW|Runaway Item Seller|link}}, but his wife is managing the {{Term|ALBW|Item Shop|display=Shop}} since he ran away following an argument.<ref>{{Cite|You know, this is actually my husband's store. But he and I...well, we had a little argument.|Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Anyway, he wandered off and hasn't come back. He's SO sensitive, that man. Where he is, I have no idea!|Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref>
