The {{Term/Store|Beaver Bro|Beaver Bros.|Series, MM3D|plural}} are characters in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Race the Beaver Bros.!|Bombers' Notebook|MM3D}}</ref> Their names are not mentioned in-game despite the young one being called '''Shtick''' (the one carrying branches) and the older one '''Twig''' (with the lifebelt).<ref name=":0" />

==Biography==
[[File:BeaverBrothers.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A screenshot of the Beaver Bros. in {{MM|-}}]]
The {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver Bros.}} swim around in the {{Term|MM3D|Waterfall Rapids|link}} at the source of the river in the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay|link}}. They consider {{Plural|MM3D|Bottle|link}} to be valuable items and will not give them up easily.<ref>{{Cite|To us, empty bottles are a treasure. I can't give you one just because you beat my little brother.|Big Brother Beaver|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Mikau|link}} tried to race them in order to obtain a {{Term|MM3D|Bottle}} from them, but ultimately failed.<ref>{{Cite|You again? You can't fool me with your green clothes! You want an empty bottle, don't you? You never learn your lesson!|Little Brother Beaver|MM}}</ref> Thus, when [[Link]] first meets them as {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link|link}}, they mistake him for the guitarist of {{Term|MM3D|The Indigo-Go's|link}}.

In order to obtain their {{Term|MM3D|Bottle}}, Link will have to challenge the {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver Bros.|display=Bros.}} separately in a {{Term|MM3D|Beaver Race|link}}. First, the {{Term|MM3D|Little Brother Beaver|link}} will challenge the young [[Hero of Time]], and if he succeeds, he will call his {{Term|MM3D|Big Brother Beaver|link}} to race against Link.<ref>{{Cite|Uh...that was perfect. That's not good... [...] I called you, big brother! Actually...|Little Brother Beaver|MM}}</ref> This time, {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link}} will have to go through even more rings in order to win against the {{Term|MM3D|Big Brother Beaver|display=Big Brother}}, and upon completion, the two {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver|link}} will begrudgingly reward {{Term|MM3D|Link}} with a {{Term|MM3D|Bottle}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's nothing we can do, little brother. We underestimated you.|Beaver Bros.|plural=yes|MM}}</ref>

{{Term|MM3D|Link}} can race again to win a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}} from them, for they do not have any more {{Plural|MM3D|Bottle}}.<ref>{{Cite|But we don't have any more empty bottles...do we, little brother?|Big Brother Beaver|MM}}</ref> This time, the time limit is shortened for both {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver Race|display=Races}}, and if {{Term|MM3D|Link}} succeeds, the {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver Bros.}} will hand over a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart}} in hopes that he will finally leave them alone.<ref>{{Cite|If we give THAT to him, I wonder if he'll leave us alone, little brother.|Big Brother Beaver|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please! Let it be over.|Beaver Bros.|plural=yes|MM}}</ref>

If Link attempts to race the Beaver Bros. once more, there will no longer be any more rewards. He merely has to keep up, get the rings, and cross the finish line within the allotted amount of time.
