'''Holmill''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Holmill|This monster digs holes in soft earth. Sometimes these holes can be entrances to hard-to-reach places.<ref name="Holmill">{{Cite|This monster digs holes in soft earth. Sometimes these holes can be entrances to hard-to-reach places.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Holmills are [[mole]]-like monsters that pop in and out of holes in the ground. Their holes can act as pits or passages to inaccessible areas. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Holmill [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], which costs three Echo energy to summon.
