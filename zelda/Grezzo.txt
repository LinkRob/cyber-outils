'''Grezzo''' is a Japanese video game developer founded in 2006 and named after the Italian phrase ''Diamante Grezzo'', meaning "Diamond in the rough".<ref name= "About"/><ref name= "Message">{{Cite Web|retrieved= June 01, 2019|url= http://www.grezzo.co.jp/en/about/message|title= Message from CEO|site= GREZZO Co, Ltd.}}</ref> Besides their original titles, Grezzo has developed and co-developed several games for and with [[Nintendo]], including various remakes and an original entry in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

Grezzo's CEO is {{wp|Koichi Ishii}},<ref name= "Message"/> and as of 01 April, 2019, the company has 62 employees.<ref name= "About"/>
