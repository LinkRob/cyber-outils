The {{Term/Store|Lover's Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lover's Pond'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond}} is a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}-shaped pond located atop {{Term|BotW|Tuft Mountain|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}, west of {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= If you run your horse west from Lurelin Village, you will arrive at Tuft Mountain.|magazine= Nintendo Magazine|volume= |issue= |date= Winter 2021|page= 17 (English)}}</ref> It is the main location of the "{{Term|BotW|A Gift of Nightshade|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond}} is said to show people to their soulmate and as such has become a legend.<ref>{{Cite|This IS the pond of legend, is it not? The one where a person finds true love?|Perda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= The heart-shaped lake on the top of the mountain is a legendary love spot that is said that one can find their true love.|magazine= Nintendo Magazine|volume= |issue= |date= Winter 2021|page= 17 (English)}}</ref>

A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Throwing Spear|link}} sits in the middle of it.
