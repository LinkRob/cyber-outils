"{{Term/Store|The Road to Respect||Series, BotW}}" is a [[Side Quest]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Road to Respect}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Goron City}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can meet {{Term|BotW|Fugo|link}}, who explains he the house they are in front of belongs to the {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} artisan {{Term|BotW|Rohan|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the house of the distinguished Goron artisan...Master Rohan!|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} explains that his master is responsible for keeping the ancient art of blacksmithing alive.<ref>{{Cite|[Who's Rohan?] Master Rohan is a godly blacksmith. He's responsible for keepin' the ancient arts alive!|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> As part of his strict training, {{Term|BotW|Rohan}} gave {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} the task of defeating every {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus|link}} near {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|He's really strict with everyone—me included. In my case, he told me to beat every {{Color|BotW Blue|Igneo Talus}} around Goron City as part of my training.|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> However, he has been unable to reach the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus}} at {{Term|BotW|Darunia Lake|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But I can't get to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Igneo Talus}} at {{Color|BotW Blue|Darunia Lake}}. *siiiigh*|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> He openly wishes that someone else could go in his place,<ref>{{Cite|If only someone would go in my place...|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> which begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

Following his suggestion, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus}} buried on the western shore of {{Term|BotW|Darunia Lake}}. It will rise out of the ground when {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches and begin to attack. Upon defeating it, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must return to {{Term|BotW|Goron City}} to tell {{Term|BotW|Fugo}}.<ref>{{Cite|You defeated the {{Color|BotW Red|Igneo Talus}} at {{Color|BotW Red|Darunia Lake}}! Go and tell Fugo!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Fugo}} is surprised to hear that {{Term|BotW|Link}} defeated the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I beat an Igneo Talus.] Whaaaa—?! You say you beat the {{Color|BotW Blue|Igneo Talus}} at {{Color|BotW Blue|Darunia Lake}}?!|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> Deciding to strike a deal, {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} offers to pay him 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} in exchange for the achievement.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm...I have an idea. How about ya sell me that achievement for a cool {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}}?|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts this, {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|YESSS! The deal is done! Here ya go, brother!|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> He thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} and expresses his excitement to tell {{Term|BotW|Rohan}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks! Thanks so much! I can't wait to tell Master about the {{Color|BotW Blue|Igneo Talus}}!|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete.
