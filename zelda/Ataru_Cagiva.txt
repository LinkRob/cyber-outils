'''Ataru Cagiva''' ({{Romanize|かぢば あたる|Kajiba Ataru}}) is a [[manga]] artist.

==Biography==
{{Stub}}
Cagiva is known to have illustrated two {{TLoZ|Series|-}} manga: ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Cagiva)|Link's Awakening]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past (Cagiva)|A Link to the Past]]'', released in 1994 and 1995, respectively. He has also worked on other manga such as ''{{Wp|The Legend of Dragoon|The Legend of Dragoon}}''.
