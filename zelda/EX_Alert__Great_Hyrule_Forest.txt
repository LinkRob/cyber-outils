{{Term/Store|EX Alert: Great Hyrule Forest||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 78 {{Term|HWAoC|EX Vicious Monster Battle|link}} in {{HWAoC}} included in the first downloadable content pack, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Alert: Great Hyrule Forest|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} must find the vicious monsters without their location on the map. They can spawn in a limited number of possible locations, and if a {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} approaches the wrong one, another {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} will spawn in the vicious monsters' place. While the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} fights the vicious monsters, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} will appear to fight as well.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the vicious monsters are defeated, and can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of two {{Plural|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Courser Bee Honey|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Rock Salt|link}}, 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
