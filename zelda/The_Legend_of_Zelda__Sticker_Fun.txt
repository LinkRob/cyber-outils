'''The Legend of Zelda: Sticker Fun''' is a sticker book published by the Western Publishing Company in 1989. It features a story loosely relating to [[The Legend of Zelda]], including many enemies from the video game.  The book also contains 43 stickers for the reader to stick to the 16 coloring pages. Each coloring page contains one or two sentences of the story and multiple blank areas for the reader to place the sticker, along with uncolored pictures for the reader to fill in. The back cover of the book also contains a maze.

==Story Transcription==
This is the peaceful kingdom of [[Hyrule]]. [[Ganon]], the Prince of Darkness, sent his army to attack Hyrule and steal the [[Triforce of Power]]. [[Princess Zelda]] hid the eight pieces of the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] in the underground labyrinths. Princess Zelda sent her nursemaid, [[Impa]], to find someone brave enough to rescue the kingdom. Ganon, angry that he had been cheated out of the Triforce of Wisdom, put Princess Zelda in prison. [[Link]] arrived just in time to save Impa from the evil warriors. Impa told Link the story of Zelda. He promised to save her. A merchant in a cave gave Link a [[sword]] to use for protection on his adventure. Link used his sword to defeat the [[Molblin]]s [sic]. He earned a blue ruby [sic] and a heart. The [[fairy]] in the lake gave Link many red hearts. Link's sword was no match for [[Zola]]. Link ducked out of the way and went to buy more weapons. Link went to the merchant to buy more weapons. After defeating all the [[Keese]]s [sic], Link found a [[compass]] and a [[map]] to help him search for the Triforce pieces. Link disarmed the [[Stalfos]]es and found a key to unlock doors in the labyrinth. Link defeated [[Aquamentus]] in order to enter the room where the Triforce was hidden. After Link found all eight parts of the Triforce of Wisdom, he set off for [[Death Mountain]] to defeat Ganon and rescue Princess Zelda.
