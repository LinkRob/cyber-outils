{{Term/Store|Mushroom Omelet|Mushroom Omelets|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Mushroom Omelets are meals made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}}, a [[Bird Egg]], [[Goat Butter]], and [[Rock Salt]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The regular version can be made either by using {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}}, which offer no status effects, or by using multiple mushrooms with differing effects. Eating a Mushroom Omelet will heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The fluffy texture is considered to be a highlight of Mushroom Omelets.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Either by using certain mushrooms or through the additional of more ingredients, Link can create several variations of Mushroom Omelet:

* '''Hearty Mushroom Omelet''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Mushroom Omelet''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}} or other energizing ingredients. Eating these omelets will refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Mushroom Omelet''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}} or other enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled when he eats these.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Mushroom Omelet''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}} or other hasty ingredients. Consuming these will increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Mushroom Omelet''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}} or other spicy ingredients. Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when he eats these omelets.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Mushroom Omelet''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}} or other electric ingredients. When eaten, Link will be granted one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Mushroom Omelet''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}} or other mighty ingredients. By eating these omelets, Link's attack power will be increased.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Mushroom Omelet''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}} or other tough ingredients. When eaten, they'll boost Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Mushroom Omelet''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}} or other sneaky ingredients. Eating them will boost Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Mushroom Omelet''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Mushroom Omelet}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The fluffy texture of this omelet is one of the great joys of this dish, as well as life.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}} or other chilly ingredients. Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]] if he eats these omelets.<ref name="Chilly"/>
