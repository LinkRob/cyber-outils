The {{Term/Store|Akkala Wilds||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Akkala Wilds|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Wilds}} are located in {{Term|BotW|Deep Akkala|link}}, west of {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights|link}}. They span all the way to the edge of {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}}. Several {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}}, including {{Plural|BotW|Rare Ore Deposit|link}}, are found along the cliffs on its southern border. There are several small forests among the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Wilds}}, which are filled with {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom|link}} and other {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}. In addition, many deactivated {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}} are found in the area, which can be scavenged for {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}} will fly over the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Wilds}} on occasion. While it does not get close enough to the ground for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to reach, it does cause a strong {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}} to sweep over the area.

A single {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}}, filled with a variety of {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}, is found in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Wilds}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} defeats all of the {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, he will be able to open their {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Drillshaft|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} also patrols the eastern side of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Wilds}}.

While the "{{Term|BotW|Xenoblade Chronicles 2|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is active, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can stand on the left eye of {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link}}, causing a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} to fall from the sky and land in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Wilds}}. This {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} contains the {{Term|BotW|Salvager Vest|link}}.

Six {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found hidden in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Wilds}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} finds these {{Plural|BotW|Korok}}, they will give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref> They are in the following locations:

* Under a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}}, located in a dead {{Term|BotW|Tree}} on the western side of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Wilds}}.

* A {{Term|BotW|Rock Pattern|link}}, south of the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}.

* Under a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot}}, which is concealed by a {{Term|BotW|Boulder|link}} southeast of the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}.

* Under a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot}}, hidden by a rock slab east of {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake}}.

* Hitting a {{Term|BotW|Boulder}} from {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights}} into a hole in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Wilds}}. This task may cause {{Term|BotW|Link}} to pass through the territory of the {{Term|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel}}.

* At the end of a {{Term|BotW|Flower Trail|link}}, east of the ruins.
