{{Term/Store|Diamond Steppe Island||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (F1) is a diamond-shaped island in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features==

On this island, [[Link]] must use the [[Hookshot]] to reach the top of the island and enter a maze with many [[Warp Jar]]s. The reward there is the [[Special Charts#Ghost Ship Chart|Ghost Ship Chart]], which marks the island appearances of the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship|link}} on certain moon phases. After obtaining the Ghost Ship Chart, a glowing ship symbol will appear at night on the [[Sea Chart]]. Coincidentally, the island itself is one of the seven recurring destinations of the Ghost Ship.

There is also one eight-eyed [[Big Octo (Great Sea)|Big Octo]] which Link can destroy for 100 [[Rupee]]s. In addition, beneath the waves is a chest with a [[Heart Container|Heart Piece]] that can be located after opening a [[Treasure Chart]] from [[Windfall Island]].

==Trivia==
*The layout of the island's underground maze is similar to the grotto found on [[Needle Rock Isle]].
*At a certain angle, the island looks somewhat like a ship itself.
*The island's English name is likely a mistranslation of its Japanese name (see [[Diamond Steppe Island#Nomenclature|below]]), confusing the homophones ''steppe'' and ''step''. The Japanese name for the island completely lacks the word for "steppe", 草原 ''sōgen'', instead using the word 段 ''dan'', which means "step" (as of stairs), which far better describes the island's geography.
