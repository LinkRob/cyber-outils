{{Term/Store|Guardian Graveyard||HWAoC}} is a level 60 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Guardian Graveyard|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Graveyard}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Terrako Component|link}}, and three {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}}'s objective is to defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} within twelve minutes. Another {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} spawns further west when one of the two is brought down to about half health, and immediately advances towards {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}}. Once all three are killed, a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}} spawn near the southern end of the map, and advance toward {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} as well. When one is killed, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian|link}} and an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Guardian|link}} spawn in different areas and do the same. Once all {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} that have spawned so far are defeated, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}} spawn, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends upon their defeat. Aside from the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}}, there are {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, and some {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok|link}} scattered around the map.
