==Biography==
{{Term|ALBW|Sabro}} is a baby {{Term|ALBW|Turtle|link}} who resides in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake|link}} with his {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle|link}} and his brothers, {{Term|ALBW|Tallo|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Jillo|link}}. He is the smallest of the three brothers, and is not yet very coordinated.<ref name="fall"/>

When {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} first arrives at {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake}}, he can encounter {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle}}, who pleads for his help in finding her lost babies.<ref>{{Cite|Oh no! I got separated from my little turtles. You have to help me look for them—you just have to!|Mama Turtle|ALBW}}</ref> {{Term|ALBW|Sabro}} can be found on a patch on land to the south-west of the lake, wriggling helplessly on his back. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can approach the {{Term|ALBW|Turtle}} and pick him up, tossing him back into the lake. {{Term|ALBW|Sabro}} thanks {{Term|ALBW|Link}} for the help and swims off to rejoin his mother.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks! Oh, that was just horrifying! I couldn't roll back over! I hope my mom isn't too worried about me!|Sabro|ALBW}}</ref> Once all three baby {{Plural|ALBW|Turtle}} have been rescued, they all climb onto {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle}}'s back, forming a stack that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can stand on to receive a ride around {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake}}, including access to {{Term|ALBW|Turtle Rock|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found all of my babies! Thank you so much! I'm sorry I don't have anything I can offer you as a reward. If you need a ride somewhere, you can go ahead and hop on, though!|Mama Turtle|ALBW}}</ref>
