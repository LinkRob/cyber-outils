==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|BotW}}
{{Term|BotW|Dalia|link}} is upset that her garden cannot grow because of all the {{Term|BotW|Trash}} in the water. She gives {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Mystery Polluter|link}}," asking him to find out who is doing the polluting. {{Term|BotW|Link}} finds out that the {{Term|BotW|Trash}} is {{Term|BotW|Hydromelon|link}} rinds thrown out by {{Term|BotW|Calyban|link}}, who is sitting on one of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}'s walls. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has collected and given 10 {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Calyban}}, she will stop polluting the waterway with her {{Term|BotW|Trash}}.
