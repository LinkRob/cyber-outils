The {{Term/Store|Average Middle-Aged Man||Series, ALttP}} is a character in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 388 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The man is a former [[Thief]] who specialized in picking [[lock]]s who now lives at the entrance of the [[Desert of Mystery]] under the guise of being a ordinary man in silent meditation, sitting by a sign that tells people to leave him be. If Link picks up or slices his [[Sign]], the man will break his silence and follow Link until he leaves the area.<ref>{{Cite|Why did you take my sign? It says plain as day to just leave me alone! Sheeesh!|Average Middle-Aged Man|ALttP}}</ref>

After hearing about the "average" man from a [[Storyteller|former thief]] located in a cave near the [[Ice Palace]] or from a [[Thief]] in the [[Lost Woods]],<ref>{{Cite|Heh heh. Thank you. To tell you the truth, I used to be a thief in the Light World... some of my fellow thieves went into hiding because they were afraid of being caught. One of them was a master locksmith, but now he is hiding the fact that he was a thief... by pretending to be a strange middle-aged guy! Ha ha ha...|Storyteller|ALttP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey kid, this is a secret hide-out for a gang of thieves! Don't enter without permission! By the way, I heard that one of our ex-members is staying at the entrance to the desert.|Storyteller|ALttP}}</ref> Link can return to him with the [[Treasure Chest#Varieties|Locked Chest]] found in the [[Village of Outcasts]]. The man offers to open the Chest for Link in exchange for him not telling anyone about his true identity.<ref>{{Cite|I heard that you know I used to be a thief, right? Well, I'll open a chest for you. Will you keep it secret from everyone else? Would you please promise?|Average Middle-Aged Man|ALttP}}</ref> He gives Link the [[Bottle|Magic Bottle]] that was inside in exchange for Link's silence. Afterwards, he will not care whether or not his sign is taken by Link.<ref>{{Cite|Remember, you promised... Don't tell to anyone.|Average Middle-Aged Man|ALttP}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The man asking Link to keep his identity a "secret from everyone" is a reference to the famous quote from {{TLoZ}}.
*During the ending credits, the man can be seen back to his old ways trying to pick locks in the [[Desert of Mystery]].
