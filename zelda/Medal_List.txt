The {{Term/Store|Medal List||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} is an interface in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Medal List|Gallery|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|Medal List}} is a menu that displays all {{Plural|HWDE|Medal|link}} that have been collected by the {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}}, along with the requirements for obtaining each respective {{Term|HWDE|Medal}}. The {{Term|HWDE|Medal List}} also displays all uncollected {{Plural|HWDE|Medal}}, with some showing the requirements for unlocking the {{Term|HWDE|Medal}}, and others not. Every unlocked {{Term|HWDE|Medal}} will not have its name displayed until it has been unlocked.<ref>{{Cite|??????????|Medal List|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Medal List}} can be accessed from the {{Term|HWDE|Gallery|link}}.
