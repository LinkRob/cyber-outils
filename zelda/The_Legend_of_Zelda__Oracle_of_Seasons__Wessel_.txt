'''''The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons''''' is a multi-ending chapter book based on the [[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|video game of the same name]]. It is a companion to the {{OoA|Wessel}} book. Despite some small differences in the story, it generally follows the plot of the video game accurately. In the center of the book are eight full-color pages of official game artwork.

The book is fairly short and skims over many events in the game, covering only four dungeons out of eight and the final battle against [[General Onox]]. The skipped dungeons are mentioned, but not described in great detail. The book also describes the order for the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}.

Like in the {{OoS|Himekawa}}, {{OoS|-}} is shown to be the first game chronologically in {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}, followed by {{OoA|-}}. Although it is based on a canon game, the book is generally regarded as non-canon. 

==Differences==
Although based on the same adventure, the plotlines of the game and the book differ slightly.

*Link speaks quite frequently, like in the {{OoS|Himekawa}}.
*Depending on the reader's choice, the [[Sword & Shield Maze]] may become the seventh dungeon, while [[Explorer's Crypt]] may become the eighth.

==Trivia==
*Link names [[Bipin]] and [[Blossom]]'s [[Bipsom|son]] "Bipsom" if the reader chooses to enter their home in the story.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Put on the spot, Link thought for a moment and said, "Why don't you name him Bipsom, after both of you?"|book= OoS (Wessel)|publisher= |page= 52}}</ref>
*In the book, [[Ricky]] is the animal companion which Link meets.
