'''Final Smashes''' are recurring attacks in {{SSB|Series}}.

==Overview==
A Final Smash is an elaborate and powerful attack that can be used after grabbing an object called a Smash Ball, but can only be used once per Smash Ball. While performing a Final Smash, many characters gain auras around their body and the background darkens.

==={{SSBB|-}}===
[[Link]] and [[Toon Link]] share a Final Smash in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', the [[Triforce Slash]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] and [[Sheik]] both use the [[Light Arrow]]s. [[Ganondorf]]'s Final Smash is [[Beast Ganon]], in which he transforms into [[Ganon]], resembling Ganon's appearance from {{TP}}.

==={{SSB4|-}}===
[[Link]] and Toon Link use the [[Triforce Slash]] once more in {{SSB4}}. Zelda and her alter ego, Sheik, use the Light Arrows. Ganondorf retains his Final Smash, Beast Ganon.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
In {{SSBU}}, [[Link]], Zelda, and Sheik receive new Final Smash that are better suited for them. In reference to [[Link]] being from {{BotW}}, his new Final Smash is the [[Ancient Bow and Arrow]] where [[Link]] fires an [[Ancient Arrow]] at his opponents straightforwardly. Zelda's new Final Smash is the [[Triforce of Wisdom]], in which she casts a sealing Magic that pulls nearby enemies into the Triangle grip dealing damage. If a fighter's percentage is 100 or higher, it is an automatic defeat. Ganondorf's Final Smash is [[Ganon, the Demon King]], which is similar to the Beast Ganon Final Smash, except Ganondorf now transforms into Ganon from {{OoT}}. Toon Link continues to use the [[Triforce Slash]] for his Final Smash, and [[Young Link]] was given his own variation of the move. Sheik received a different Final Smash called the [[Sheikah Dance]], in which she rushes at an opponent, and upon catching one, a [[Sheikah]] symbol will flash on the target before a series of slashes occur.

==Easy Final Smash==
An '''Easy Final Smash''', also known as a '''Pity Final Smash''', randomly occurs when a fighter has been KO'd a numerous amount of times and has respawned on stage with the ability to perform a Final Smash. Usually, in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', a [[Fighter]] would have to be at least 5 points behind the Fighter in the lead. If a Smash Ball is on the battlefield already or if a Fighter already obtained one, an Easy Final Smash will not occur.

==Final Smash Meter==
{{Stub|SSBU}}
{{SSBU|-}} adds a unique mechanic called the {{ssb|Final Smash Meter}} where [[fighter]]s can charge a meter until it is full to unleash a weaker version of their Final Smash. The Meter can be charged by dealing and taking damage. With this rule, it is possible for more than one fighter to have their Final Smash abilities at the ready to be activated, however, each Final Smash must happen one at a time. When a Fighter claims a Smash Ball, it will instantly fill up the meter and reset after being used.
