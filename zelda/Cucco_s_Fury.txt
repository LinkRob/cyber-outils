{{Term/Store|Cucco's Fury||Series, HW, HWDE}} is a game mode in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Big|Cucco's Fury}}'''|N/A|HW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Cucco's Fury is a separate part of {{Term|HWDE|Challenge Mode|link}} identical to {{Term|HWDE|Ganon's Fury|link}}, which instead features a giant [[Cucco#Hyrule Warriors|Cucco]] as a playable character. This mode is unlocked after completing Survival Battle Lv. 3 in Ganon's Fury with an A-Rank, or completing Survival Battle Lv. 4 in Ganon's Fury with an A-Rank in the [[Hyrule Warriors: Definitive Edition|Definitive Edition]].  This mode features the same types of battles as in Ganon's Fury. As with Ganon, Cucco is able to perform attack combinations, execute Special Attacks and Focus Spirit, and level up.
