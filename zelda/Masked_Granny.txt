The {{Term/Store|Masked Granny||Series}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography==
One of a masked cult led by the [[Masked Elder]] and first spotted on [[Stalbind]]'s defeat<!--Either that or one Dark World boss defeat-->, the Masked Granny serves as the Masked Elder's housekeeper.  She states that being the Masked Elder's housekeeper does not give her the right to complain, asking [[Link]] to scram as she tends to business.

The Masked Granny also has a tendency to bite her tongue.
