The {{Term/Store|Recruit's Sword|Recruit's Swords|Series, ST}} is a [[sword]] in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 135}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Recruit's Sword}} resembles the {{Term|TWWHD|Hero's Sword|link}} and {{Term|PH|Oshus's Sword|link}} from the game's predecessors, {{TWW}} and {{PH}} respectively. This dangerous yet unremarkable weapon is obtained in the training room of {{Term|ST|Hyrule Castle|link}}, as {{Term|ST|Link}} requires a {{Term|Series|Sword}} in order to make his way from the {{Term|ST|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}'s back door toward the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits|link}}, through a pathway full of enemies.<ref>{{Cite|There are all sorts of strange beasts on the other side of this door. It's not the kind of place you want to go without a sword!|Soldier|ST}}</ref> [[Russell]], the captain of the Hyrule Castle [[Soldier|Guards]], gives it to Link after he successfully completes a basic sword training exercise.<ref>{{Cite|Some swordsman you are, running around with no sword! There's something fishy about this... Are you really a new recruit? Heh heh heh! Your eyes tell the whole story! All right! Take this one!|Russell|ST}}</ref> It is eventually replaced by the {{Term|ST|Lokomo Sword|link}}.

While using the {{Term|ST|Recruit's Sword}}, {{Term|ST|Link}} must collect three {{Plural|ST|Tear of Light|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits}} to be able to strike a {{Term|ST|Phantom|link}} in the back.<ref>{{Cite|Hitting the Phantom in the back with your sword should stop it for a while... But first, you need to gather all three of the Tears of Light.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> This will stun the creature and will allow {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link}}'s spirit to control it while in that state. After {{Term|ST|Link}} obtains the {{Term|ST|Lokomo Sword}}, he no longer needs to collect {{Plural|ST|Tear of Light}}, as the {{Term|ST|Lokomo Sword|display=Sword}} itself is infused with enough energy to attack the {{Plural|ST|Phantom}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your new sword is filled with energy. That means you don't need to grab Tears of Light to attack the Phantoms.|Zelda|ST}}</ref>
