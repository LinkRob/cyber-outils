The {{Term/Store|Royal Crest|Royal Crests|Series}},{{Exp Game|Series}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 15}}</ref> also known as the '''Wingcrest''',{{Exp Game|Series}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 395}}</ref> is a common depiction of the [[Triforce]] above a [[Loftwing]]. Succeeding a [[Goddess Crest|much older crest]] used in the same land during [[Era of the Goddess Hylia|ancient times]] by the [[Hylia|Goddess Hylia]], it serves as the official crest of the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]] and can be found in many places throughout the kingdom of [[Hyrule]]. It appears in places such as the various incarnations of [[Hyrule Castle]], [[Temple of Time]], and the {{Term|TPHD|Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|link}}. It can also be found on walls, banners, and [[Soldier|Guard]] uniforms.

The Goddess Hylia's crest was in the shape of a bird and the people of [[Skyloft]], descendants of the people who lived among Hylia and the ancestors of the [[Hylians]], rode giant birds and used the same symbol. Because of this, both the {{Term|Series|Royal Crest}} and the [[Hylian Crest]] incorporate elements of the Goddess Crest.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The goddess Hylia had a birdlike crest, and the people of Skyloft rode giant birds. The crest of the royal family incorporates the historically important bird, as well as the three triangles of the Triforce.|book= HH|publisher= |page=77}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
The {{Term|OoT|Royal Crest}} makes several appearances in {{OoT}}. It can be found on the Guard uniforms, above the drawbridge leading to the [[Market]], on the tombstone of the [[Royal Family's Tomb]], and in the [[Castle Courtyard]].

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Term|TWWHD|Royal Crest}} can be seen atop the gates of {{Term|TWWHD|Hyrule Castle|link}}, below the statue of the [[Hero of Time]], around the walls of {{Term|TWWHD|Hyrule Castle}}, and used in Rauru's {{Term|TWWHD|Stained Glass|link}} window. They also appear in certain rooms that contain a [[Triforce Chart]], such as the one in the [[Islet of Steel]]. A giant {{Term|TWWHD|Royal Crest}} is depicted in the boss room of the [[Wind Temple]].

==={{TMC|-}}===
The {{Term|TMC|Royal Crest}} appears above the bell in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} and is shown several times throughout Hyrule Castle in {{TMC}}. It can also be seen in the [[Royal Crypt]] where members of the Royal Family of Hyrule are buried.

==={{TP|-}}===
[[File:Golden Goddesses.jpeg|The {{Term|TP|Royal Crest}} surrounded by the depiction of the Golden Goddesses from {{TP|-}}|thumb|right]]
The {{Term|TPHD|Royal Crest}} makes many appears on banners, flags, doors, and other engravings in Hyrule Castle and on the uniforms of the [[Soldier|Castle Guards]] in {{TP}}. It is predominately displayed all over [[Hyrule Castle Town#Twilight Princess|Hyrule Castle Town]], appearing in the designs of various doors found throughout the town and near the town's entrance, as well being in the fountain of the central square. In the [[Gerudo Desert]], a partially broken {{Term|TPHD|Royal Crest}} can be seen at the entrance of the {{Term|TPHD|Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|link}} and it appears within the complex itself. Atop the Grounds, in the [[Mirror Chamber]], versions of the crest with the [[Medallions|six Medallions]] replacing the Triforce appear.

==={{ST|-}}===
A [[Spirits of Good#Symbol of the Spirits|new crest]] appears in {{ST}}, in the re-established kingdom of Hyrule that was founded by [[Tetra]] and [[Tetra's Pirate Crew|her pirate crew]] one hundred years prior to the events of {{ST|-}}. This crest features a [[Force Gem#Spirit Tracks|Force Gem]] instead of the Triforce in its design. It is visible on the front of the [[Spirit Train]] as well as on [[Anjean]]. It appears to be the national symbol of the [[new Hyrule]] to distinguish it from the ancient one destroyed during the [[Great Flood]] that took place between the [[Zelda Timeline#Adult Timeline|Adult Timeline ending]] of {{OoT|-}} and {{TWW|-}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
The {{Term|ALBW|Royal Crest}} appears throughout Hyrule in {{ALBW}}. Also introduced in this game is [[Lorule]]'s {{Term|ALBW|Royal Crest}}, which is similar but has an inverted Triforce, no talons, and three feathers instead of the four in Hyrule's version.
