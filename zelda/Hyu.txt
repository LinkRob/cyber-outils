{{Term/Store|Hyu|Hyu|Series, ALttP, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 185 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, Hyu can be found in the [[Village of Outcasts]], [[Skeleton Forest]], and by the [[Graveyard]]. They float around but do not directly move towards [[Link]]; however, they cause damage to Link if touched.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, Hyu are found in {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}, the [[Skull Woods Region]], and the [[Treacherous Tower]]. Inside the Tower, eight Hyu appear in the Advanced Tower on the 6th floor after defeating four [[Poe]]s.

==Other Appearances==
{{Ambig|Start}}
==={{AST|-}}===
In {{AST}}, Hyu appear in the Graveyard near the Sanctuary when pushing certain tombstones. Once summoned, they fly sporadically around the area.
