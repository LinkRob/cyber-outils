==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Scroll of Freezing}} are limited-use items that can encase nearby enemies in blocks of ice, temporarily stalling their movement and leaving them vulnerable. However, the scrolls can only be used once and only a maximum of one {{Term|CoH|Scroll of Freezing}} can be stored in the hero's inventory. Collecting multiple {{Plural|CoH|Scroll of Freezing}} will not stack, meaning that collecting multiple scrolls of the same type will not add more scrolls to the hero's inventory, forcing the heroes to be carefully manage how they use them and when they should pick up extras if there are any present.

They can be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}}, or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or as a random reward after destroying a stack of books with an explosive.
