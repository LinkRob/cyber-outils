The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Mesa||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gerudo Mesa|Map|TP}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{TP|-}}===
The Gerudo Mesa is a {{Wp|mesa}} situated in the {{Exp|GCN version; southeast in the Wii version|southwest}} corner of the [[Gerudo Desert]]. When a portion of the [[Bridge of Eldin]] is [[Warping|warped]] from its proper location, it lands in the mesa. Once it is taken back to its proper place with the help of [[Midna#Abilities|Midna's powers]], the entrance to the [[Cave of Ordeals]] is revealed. There are also [[Shadow Beast]]s on the mesa which leave a portal behind once they are defeated, which is necessary to transport the bridge segment, and also one of the sixty [[Poe]]s scattered around [[Hyrule]].

Aside from the [[Oasis Flight]], warping to the Gerudo Mesa is the only way to access the Gerudo Desert.
