==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Voltfin Trout}}
Voltfin Trout can be found swimming in the waters of [[Hyrule Ridge]] and the [[Tabantha Frontier]]. Like other fish, they can also be dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Octorok|link}}, specifically {{Plural|BotW|Forest Octorok|link}} near [[Thundra Plateau]]. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat one raw to restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Voltfin Trout exposed to [[Ice]] become [[Frozen Trout]], while ones exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} turn into [[Roasted Trout]]. [[Cooking]] with Voltfin Trout results in meals that grant Link temporary Shock Resistance.<ref name="Inventory"/>

During the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, [[Walton]] will ask Link to bring him several items that correspond to his riddles. The fourth riddle he tells asks for something with scales and a tail that will zap people, and refers to it as a shocking swimmer.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Its scales and tail will zap you, no doubt}}! This shocking swimmer is called a...|Walton|BotW}}</ref> When Link placed a Voltfin Trout on the leaf, Walton will cheer that he figured it out, and then move on to the next riddle.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Voltfin trout!}} That's it! THAT'S IT! You got it!}}|Walton|BotW}}</ref>

In a [[Cooking Pot]], Voltfin Trout and other fish can be used to make a [[Fish Skewer]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], and [[Salt-Grilled Fish]] in a [[Cooking Pot]]. They're also among the seafood that can make [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Meunière]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]].
