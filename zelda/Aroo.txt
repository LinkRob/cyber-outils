{{Term/Store|Aroo||Series, PH}} is an [[Anouki]] who lives in the [[Anouki Estates]] on the [[Isle of Frost]] in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Right before [[Link]] lands on the Isle of Frost, a [[Yook]] violates the peace agreement between the Yooks and [[Anouki]] and kidnaps Aroo.<ref>{{Cite|That was the deal, but now some tricky Yook has snuck over here. And he's taken one of my villagers! Now he's pretending to be him and living with us! ...All comfortable-like!|Island Chief|PH}}</ref> He then disguises himself as Aroo and sets the whole village into an uproar because no one knows who the Yook disguised himself as. The Yook disguised as Aroo tells Link that [[Kumu]] is the liar,<ref>{{Cite|My name's Aroo. Between you and me, Kumu is lying. And I'll leave it at that.|Yook|PH}}</ref> but Kumu and [[Gumo]] warn Link that Aroo is the one who is lying.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Kumu. See, either Mazo or Aroo is lying to you... And that's the truth!|Kumu|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My name's Gumo. Either Fofo or Aroo has gotta be lying to you. I just wanted to tell you that...|Gumo|PH}}</ref> Link discovers that what appeared to be Aroo was actually a Yook, because logic deduced that it was lying.<ref>{{Cite|...Hey! How'd you figure it out? And I thought I had an amazing costume.|Yook|PH}}</ref> After Link restores peace by beating [[Gleeok]] in the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}} and frees Aroo, the Anouki is so grateful that he sends Link a letter containing a [[Wisdom Gem]].<ref name="letter"/>
