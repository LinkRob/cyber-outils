{{Term/Store|Sentry|Sentries|Series, BotW|plural}} are enemies in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Sentry}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Sentry}}
{{Plural|BotW|Sentry}} are a type of flying {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}}, similar to {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher|link}}, that only appear on {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} dispatches {{Plural|BotW|Sentry}} as {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}} approach {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain}} to hinder them as they attempt to disable the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|These sentries dispatched from Divine Beast Vah Rudania are equipped with searchlights that can spot intruders.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh... Yikes... A {{Color|BotW Blue|sentry}} is always a nuisance, no matter where it is or how many of them there are.|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Sentry}} use red searchlights along the path up the mountain which, should {{Term|BotW|Link}} or {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} be spotted, will cause the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} to furiously unleash {{Plural|BotW|Magma Bomb|link}} at them.<ref>{{Cite|If one finds you, {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}} will go nuts, which will cause {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}} to erupt, goro. I...always get spotted, and then {{Color|BotW Blue|magma bombs}} come raining down one after another.|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Plural|BotW|Sentry}} have a high amount of health at 1000, they are extremely weak to {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} and physics damage and are one of the few {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} that can be taken down with a single {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}}. When defeated, they will drop a random {{Term|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}.

==Trivia==
*Even though {{Plural|BotW|Sentry}} are under the control of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}'s full power does not activate when near one.{{Verify|BotW}}
