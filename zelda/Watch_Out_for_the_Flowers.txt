"{{Term/Store|Watch Out for the Flowers||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Watch Out for the Flowers}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Hylia River}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
On the {{Term|BotW|Floret Sandbar|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Hila Rao Shrine|link}} surrounded by {{Plural|BotW|Flower|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Magda|link}}, who is nearby, explains that she planted them because she thought they would make the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} look nice.<ref>{{Cite|Look! Aren't they lovely? I wanted to see how the shrine would look with flowers around it, and it's wonderful!|Magda|BotW}}</ref> She invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to look at the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}}, but she warns him not to step on them.<ref>{{Cite|Please, feel free to take a closer look. Just don't step on them!|Magda|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} steps on the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}} before speaking to {{Term|BotW|Magda}}, she will yell at him to stop.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! What do you think you're doing?! {{Color|BotW Red|You must not harm the flowers}}!|Magda|BotW}}</ref> She shares that she planted all of the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}} and informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he should not hurt them.<ref>{{Cite|I planted flowers around the shrine. Please be careful where you step. You don't want to hurt them, do you?|Magda|BotW}}</ref>}} When she is finished speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

The first time {{Term|BotW|Link}} steps on the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}}, {{Term|BotW|Magda}} will shout that they are screaming out in pain.<ref>{{Cite|No! {{Color|BotW Red|You must not hurt the flowers}}! They are screaming out in pain!|Magda|BotW}}</ref> The second time, she will shout at {{Term|BotW|Link}} to treat them well, as they are alive.<ref>{{Cite|How many times do I have to say it?! {{Color|BotW Red|Please treat the flowers with care}}! They're living things, you know!|Magda|BotW}}</ref> On the third occasion, {{Term|BotW|Magda}} will dejectedly reveal that she has lost count of how many times she has warned him.<ref>{{Cite|So many times... I've lost count of how many times I've told you... It's just so many now...|Magda|BotW}}</ref> She states her confusion at {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s lack of understanding,<ref>{{Cite|How do you not understand? Why don't you get it?|Magda|BotW}}</ref> before she suddenly shouts in rage.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|ARGGG!! NOOOOOOO!!}}|Magda|BotW}}</ref> Running through the path, {{Term|BotW|Magda}} angrily recounts that she has been tending to these {{Plural|BotW|Flower}} day after day, and yet {{Term|BotW|Link}} keeps hurting them.<ref>{{Cite|I worked day...after day...after day...to plant those flowers... Then you come and hurt them...over...and over...|Magda|BotW}}</ref> She collapses near {{Term|BotW|Link}}, but as she stands back up, she vows to make him understand the rage of the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}} and attacks him.<ref>{{Cite|I will make you understand... {{Big|the flowers' rage!!!}}|Magda|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will take damage, his minimum remaining health being a quarter of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}.

To access {{Term|BotW|Hila Rao Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must navigate the paths between the {{Plural|BotW|Flower}} without stepping on them. Along the way, {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu|link}} may appear from the ground, impeding his progress. After successfully following the path, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. Activating it will complete the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
