{{Term/Store|Saas Ko'sah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Saas Ko'sah Shrine}}<br/>A Major Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Major Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Saas Ko'sah Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Docks|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. It is initially buried underground and must be uncovered by lighting the large {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}} located in the center of a ring of smaller {{Plural|BotW|Torch}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Saas Ko'sah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Saas Ko'sah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Saas Ko'sah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Saas Ko'sah Shrine}} features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}}, wielding an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe++|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword++|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear++|link}} will rise from a pit to challenge him. Unlike most Tests of Strength, {{Term|BotW|Saas Ko'sah Shrine}} features no pillars throughout the arena. Instead, there are five metal blocks embedded into the floor, which can be lifted with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} to protect {{Term|BotW|Link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV}}.

In order to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV}}. Doing this causes the portcullis in the back of the room to open. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Saas Ko'sah}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for overcoming the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Saas Ko'sah|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Saas Ko'sah}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fade away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Saas Ko'sah|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} found behind the portcullis contains a {{Term|BotW|Flameblade|link}}.
