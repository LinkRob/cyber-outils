The {{Term/Store|Death Armos|Death Armos|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are enemies in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= {{Color|MM White|Death Armos}}, a temple guard...<br/> Look at that red mark on its chest! I know you've seen something like that before...
}}

Death Armos is an almost invincible variety of [[Armos]]. It floats in the air and attacks by slamming down to the ground in an attempt to crush [[Link]]. [[Tatl]] will draw Link's attention to the [[Red Emblem]] which appears on their chest. When a [[Light Arrow]] is shot at this crest, the Death Armos will flip upside-down. While inverted, it will continue to attack but, while doing so in this state, it will take damage. This is the only way in which it may be damaged. If Link wears the [[Stone Mask]], it will ignore him.
