"{{Term/Store|Champion Mipha's Song||Series, BotW}}" is the third {{Term|BotW|EX Recovered Memory|link}} in {{TCB}} DLC Pack for {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Champion Mipha's Song}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|EX Recovered Memory #3}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|Champion Mipha's Song (Memory)}}" is triggered by listening to {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} sing his {{Term|BotW|Champion Mipha's Song (Song)|link|display=song}} about {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪Recall the Champion of {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}}'s wrath,<br/>before she walked her chosen path.♪<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Mipha}}'s kindness was known to all,<br/>which is why the princess came to call.♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> It opens with {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} standing atop the {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls|link}} together.

{{Term|BotW|Mipha}} asks {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} who the rest of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} are.<ref>{{Cite|So, Princess... May I ask who the other chosen Champions are?|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} informs her of {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Goron vigilance, Daruk.<br/>Rito confidence, Revali.<br/>Gerudo spirit, Urbosa.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> but she is unable to bring herself to say {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s name out loud.<ref>{{Cite|And also... the Hylian with the sword that seals the darkness.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} realizes who she is talking about.<ref>{{Cite|Link.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref>

Looking down the {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls}}, {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} notices {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}} paddling at the bottom.<ref>{{Cite|Oh...|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> She encourages him to swim up and join the two of them,<ref>{{Cite|Sidon! Hurry and swim up here!|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} is worried that he is still too young to swim up the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}} on his own.<ref>{{Cite|Mipha... Perhaps he is still too young to swim up this big waterfall.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} says that one day, she will have to leave him to fight {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|display=Ruta}},<ref>{{Cite|One day, Princess... I must leave him... to face my fate with Ruta.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> at which point she jumps and slides down the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}} to Sidon.

At the bottom, {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} tells {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} to join her on the way up so he can get a feel for it.<ref>{{Cite|Join me. We'll go together so you can get a feel for it.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> While going up the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}}, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} is nervous and at first struggles to stay balanced on {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}'s back, but he is able to correct himself before they reach the top. Once they have returned to {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}, {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} cups {{Term|BotW|Sidon}}'s face, and says that if they are ever parted by fate, it will be up to him to protect {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sweet Sidon. Should fate ever part us... I'm counting on you to protect our beloved home from harm.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} asks if he understands,<ref>{{Cite|Understood?|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} nods. She tells him that she believes in him and asks if he wants to try the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}} one more time.<ref>{{Cite|I believe in you. Now, shall we try one more time?|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> His meek façade breaks as he delivers his trademark smile at her, and {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} and {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} laugh together as {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} watches on.
