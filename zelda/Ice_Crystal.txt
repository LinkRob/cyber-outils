{{Term/Store|Ice Crystal|Ice Crystals|Series, OoS|plural}} are recurring objects and items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 151 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
Ice Crystals are found in the [[Sword & Shield Maze]] in {{OoS}}. They are used to cool magma so [[Link]] can walk on it. Ice Crystals can be picked up with the [[Power Bracelet]] and thrown down holes. They can also be carried on minecarts from room to room.

==Other Appearancess==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TFoE|-}}===
[[File:Ice Crystal Icon CD-i.png|left]]
The Ice Crystal can be found in [[Serigon Caves]] in {{TFoE}}. [[Odranoel]] in the [[Harlequin Bazaar]] asks Link to bring it to him so he can prove that "the light of ice penetrates more than fire". He uses the Crystal to add a vacuum to Link's [[Lantern]], turning it into the [[Magic Lantern]].
{{Noncanon|End}}
