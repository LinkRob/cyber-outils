{{Term/Store|Red Orb Monster|Red Orb Monsters|Series, LADX|plural}} are enemies in {{LA|DX}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 196}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|LANS|Red Orb Monster}} are found exclusively in the {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon|link}}. After being hit with a {{Term|LANS|Sword|link}}, they retreat into their orbs and can be picked up by using the {{Term|LANS|Power Bracelet|link}}. {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} must throw the {{Plural|LANS|Red Orb Monster}} into their corresponding red holes.{{Note|In {{LA|NS}}, both {{Plural|LANS|Red Orb Monster}} and their corresponding holes bear geometric circles to assist with {{Wp|color blindness}}. This feature was not present in {{LA|DX}}.}}  When all the other {{Term|LANS|Orb Monster|link}} are placed correctly, they vanish thus spawning either a {{Term|LANS|Treasure Chest|link}} or a {{Term|LANS|Small Key|link}}.
