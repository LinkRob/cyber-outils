{{Term/Store|Eyeroc|Eyeroc|Series, FSA|plural}} are enemies in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 176}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Eyeroc usually only appear when a [[Switch]] is pressed or a lever is pulled. They fly in a group of four, circling the area before flying away. If the {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} defeat them all, they will drop a large [[Force Gem]]. Eyeroc can be defeated in one hit each. To defeat all of them in a single attack, the four {{Plural|FSA|Links}} can form a row and strike as one.
