{{Term/Store|Jovani's House||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 278}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Located on the south road of [[Hyrule Castle Town (Twilight Princess)|Hyrule Castle Town]], it is home to [[Jovani]], a man who has lost his soul due to his greed and is now made entirely of gold, thus unable to move any longer.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Jovani. I...became consumed by greed long ago, and sold my soul to a dark creature that did this to me...|Jovani|TP}}</ref> The house is rather small and is full of gold and other treasures; however, it cannot be entered normally since the door is locked. Instead, [[Link]] must either access it through [[Telma's Bar]] or by digging in front of the door as a [[Wolf Link|wolf]].

Link first enters Jovani's House as he is trying to take [[Midna]] to [[Princess Zelda]] via the [[Castle Town Sewers]]. As a wolf, he traverses through Telma's Bar and eventually reaches the inside of Jovani's House, where he must defeat a [[Poe]] and collect its [[Poe Soul|soul]]. Upon obtaining its soul, Jovani will regain the ability to speak and asks Link if he could collect 20 Poe Souls for him in order to break the curse.<ref>{{Cite|I can't move... I can't go see my girlfriend... My pet cat Gengle is frozen on  my head... I don't think I could be more miserable... I have to ask you a favor, doggie... Can you find and defeat the 20 ghosts that lurk in the dark for me? I think if you can free the pieces of my soul from the ghosts that hold them, I'll be free...|Jovani|TP}}</ref> Afterwards, Jovani opens a [[Treasure Chest]], which leads to the underground waterway of [[Hyrule Castle]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh, but it's raining out now, so I don't think you can go outside directly. I'll open the door to the underground waterway for you... Once the rain stops, you can come back in here by digging around the front wall where the cats gather... Understand, doggie?|Jovani|TP}}</ref>

When Link collects 20 Poe Souls and shows them to Jovani, he will be able to move again and will reward the hero with a [[Bottle]] filled with [[Great Fairy's Tears]].<ref>{{Cite|You did it! I can move again! This is all thanks to you, right? Oh, I have to thank you... Will this do?|Jovani|TP}}</ref> In {{TPHD}}, he also gives Link the [[Ghost Lantern]].<ref>{{Cite|It must be though finding those creepy crawlies, right? Take this Lantern with you!|Jovani|TPHD}}</ref> However, since 20 Poe Souls are not enough to break the curse, Link must collect all 60 Poe Souls, at which point Jovani returns to his normal self.<ref>{{Cite|But...my body is still golden... Does this mean my soul is still not whole? That's strange... Maybe we need the souls of 60 ghosts...|Jovani|TP}}</ref> He then leaves his house to drown his sorrows at Telma's Bar since his girlfriend found another lover.<ref>{{Cite|Word came in that he's at the bar... Ugh, what a nuisance...|Hylian|TP}}</ref> At this point, Link can go back to Jovani's House and talk to [[Gengle]], Jovani's cat, to receive unlimited silver [[Rupee]]s for saving their lives.<ref>{{Cite|I see... So my master got dumped, huh? Well, I guess it goes to show that for humans, it's all about money. I guess that means we don't need any more money...so you can take it all.|Gengle|TP}}</ref>
