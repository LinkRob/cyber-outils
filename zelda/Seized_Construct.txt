The '''Seized Construct''' is introduced in {{TotK}} as the [[Bosses in Tears of the Kingdom|Boss]] of the [[Spirit Temple]].

== Characteristics ==
{{Compendium|Extract|locs=Spirit Temple|loot=None|name=Seized Construct|desc=The construct that Mineru originally intended to house her spirit. It was changed into this ominous form by the magic power of the Demon King. Originally intended for construction, it is highly durable and able to withstand harsh conditions.|numbermaster=197}}

The Seized Construct is fought in the Spirit Temple as the last obstacle between [[Link]], [[Mineru]] (in a robot body), and Mineru's [[Secret Stone]]. The Seized Construct is a [[construct]] robot very similar to the one Mineru inhabits, which she says is a prototype she built long ago. It is under [[Ganon|Ganondorf's]] control and overflowing with [[Gloom]] as a result.

==== First Phase ====
The Seized Construct is fought in the Spirit Temple, in a zone that resembles a boxing arena covered in Gloom. Link must stay on Mineru's Construct during the fight to avoid Gloom damage. It uses a vast array of Zonai devices that it fuses to its own body to deal damage.

In the first half of the fight, the Seized Construct can do a handful of attacks such as leaping backwards and firing cannons, stabbing with shock emitters, and shielding itself from damage. The easiest way to damage at this stage it is if Link attacks it with Mineru's fists and continuously knocks the Seized Construct backwards until it makes contact with the electrified fence surrounding the arena. Link can use the [[Fuse]] ability to attach various objects and weapons to Mineru's arms, making it easier to knock the Seized Construct into one of the fences.

==== Second Phase ====
When the Seized Construct has lost half of its health, the second phase of the fight begins.  It activates two additional attachment points and fuses fans to its arms, allowing it to hover. The strategy to defeat it is largely the same as the first part of the battle, but the Seized Construct fights in a more aggressive and complex manner. 

At the end of the fight, Link is rewarded with a [[Heart Container]] and [[Mineru's Vow]], an ability that allows him to summon Mineru's Construct at any time.

'''War in the Depths of Hyrule'''

During this battle leading up to the fight against Demon King Ganondorf, if the player did not complete the Spirit Temple beforehand, the player will be forced to fight the boss without Mineru's Construct, changing the flow of the battle significantly.

The arena will no longer be covered in Gloom, and Link must stun the Seized Construct using his own weapons and launch it into the electric barriers. Elemental attacks are particularly effective at stopping its hovering attack.
