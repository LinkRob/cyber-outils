The {{Term/Store|Southern Oasis||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Southern Oasis'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Southern Oasis is located south of [[Gerudo Town]] in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. It is a mesa-like oasis south of [[Gerudo Town]].

A [[Molduga]] prowls the sands around the Oasis.

A [[Treasure Chest]] containing 10 [[Bomb Arrow]]s can be found atop the Oasis.

A Korok can be found atop one of the Palm Trees in the Oasis.
