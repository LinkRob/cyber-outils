{{Term/Store|Greyson||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Greyson|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Greyson}} is a {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} miner who works at the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. During the day, {{Term|BotW|Greyson}} mines alongside {{Term|BotW|Dorill|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Jengo|link}} while his younger brother, {{Term|BotW|Pelison|link}}, plays with {{Term|BotW|Axyl|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Greyson}} rests for the night in front of the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|display=Mine's}} {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} while {{Term|BotW|Bohrin|link}} resumes his night shift. However, he expresses to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that he is tired of digging and wishes he could find a job where he could use his strength to make a difference in people's lives.<ref>{{Cite|I'm a digger here, as you can see. I dig and I dig... Day in and day out... To be honest, I'm sick of it. I feel like...maybe I'm not meant to toll away in obscurity like this. *sigh* Is there a job out there for me that'd let me use my {{Color|BotW Red|strength}} to make a difference?|Greyson|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} meets with {{Term|BotW|Hudson|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, he is eventually asked to find a {{Term|BotW|Goron}} who would be able to remove boulders to clear the land for further development.<ref>{{Cite|These boulders are driving me nuts. I want to move them out of the way so we can develop the land underneath. It'd really help me out if you could find someone with enough {{Color|BotW Red|brute physical strength}} to bust them apart. Yeah, if we're talking {{Color|BotW Red|brute physical strength}}, and we are, a {{Color|BotW Red|Goron}} would be my first choice...|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> However, due to the strict naming policy of {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Goron}}'s name must end in "son."<ref>{{Cite|Oh, and one last wrinkle... Yeah, in accordance with the official {{Color|BotW Blue|Bolson Construction}} policy, that someone's name must end in "son." If you run into anyone that fits with what I've said, send them here to {{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}} for me.|Hudson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can approach {{Term|BotW|Greyson}} during his break and offers him the job, who is interested to hear that there is a place out there that needs him.<ref>{{Cite|Tarrey Town? Never heard of it. What's the deal with that place? Hmm... Hrmm... {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}}... {{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}}... So there is somewhere out there that needs me...|Greyson|BotW}}</ref> Deciding that {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} is not too far, he decides to embark to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}} with {{Term|BotW|Pelison}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm, {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} isn't too far from here. All right! I'm gonna do it! I'm gonna strike while the iron is hot! Thanks for lettin' me know, brother! Pelison and I are gonna head there right away! While I'm at it... Pelison! We're gonna leave!|Greyson|BotW}}</ref> The two brothers then walk out of the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine}} together.

At {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} finds that {{Term|BotW|Greyson}} has accepted the job and is much happier now.<ref>{{Cite|Yo! Hey! It's you! As you can see, I'm workin' real hard! Hudson praises me a ton for breakin' these rocks. We're findin' some nice ore too!|Greyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My brother should be somewhere around here. He's so much happier than he was at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Southern Mine}}! He gets praised a ton for how fast he can move rocks. I'm sure that makes him extra happy!|Pelison|BotW}}</ref> As the boulders he breaks contain {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}}, his brother {{Term|BotW|Pelison}} has also opened {{Term|BotW|Ore and More|link}} to sell the Gems they find.<ref name="Pelison"/><ref>{{Cite|Once we started breakin' up the rock here, we found that it's rich in {{Color|BotW Blue|ore}}. So I've decided to open up shop! I'm not sure why we have so much ore here, but maybe it's because we're close to {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}. Anyway, these here are freshly mined. Be sure to buy a whole bunch!|Pelison|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Greyson}} will continue to break the boulders in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}} as {{Term|BotW|Link}} proceeds with the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}. A house will replace a boulder every time {{Term|BotW|Link}} refers another person to the {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|display=Town}}. 

Once all of the rocks have been cleared, {{Term|BotW|Greyson}} will stand in the center of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}} as he was told to.<ref>{{Cite|Yo! I'm all done breakin' the rocks! I was told to stand here... Is somethin' supposed to happen?|Greyson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Greyson}} is one of the guests present at {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} and {{Term|BotW|Rhondson|link}}'s wedding. He agrees with the vow that {{Term|BotW|Rhondson}} should name her children in accordance to the {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction}} naming policy.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|I'll take that vow!}}|Greyson|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Greyson}} later comments that he did not know there was a wedding planned, and voices his disappointment that he was never told despite having known {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} for a long time<ref>{{Cite|I was just standin' around when a wedding started outta nowhere and took me by surprise! I've known Hudson for ages, and he never said a word about it. Can you believe that?!|Greyson|BotW}}</ref> Since the wedding was nice, though, he decides everything is alright.<ref>{{Cite|Well, it was a nice wedding ceremony, so I guess all's well that ends well!|Greyson|BotW}}</ref>

After the wedding, {{Term|BotW|Greyson}} will no longer have any work to do and decides to relax.<ref>{{Cite|That sure was a nice wedding ceremony. Well then... There's nothin' left for me to do here, so I'm gonna take it easy!|Greyson|BotW}}</ref> He can be found in front of the {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}'s {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} and sitting beside {{Term|BotW|Ore and More}} at night.
