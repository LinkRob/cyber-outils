The {{Term/Store|Gorman Brother|Gorman Brothers|Series|plural}} are three brothers in {{MM}}.{{Fact}}

Two of them, referred to as the {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}} by the {{Term|MM3D|Bombers' Notebook|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Gorman Brothers / Horse trainers. Suspicious...|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> are found on a racing track off {{Term|MM3D|Milk Road|link}} called the {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Track|link}}, while the other one, known only as {{Term|MM3D|Gorman|link}}, is the leader of the {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Troupe|link}}.

==Biography==
===The Gorman Brothers===
[[File:MM3D Gorman Brothers Notebook Portrait.png|left|One of the Gorman Brothers' picture, from the Bombers' Notebook in {{MM3D|-}}]]
The two {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}}s are known for their devious nature.<ref>{{Cite|Heh, heh...Much obliged. From here on out the milk business will be ours!|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> They do everything in their power to hinder the competing {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|link}}, such as breaking bottles of {{Term|MM3D|Milk|link}} to prevent them from being delivered; however, {{Term|MM3D|Cremia|link}} remains oblivious as to the perpetrators of these acts.<ref>{{Cite|I'm trying to take care of the ranch, but things have been getting unstable lately...the cows always seem bothered and frazzled, and I'm finding broken bottles everywhere... Who's doing it, I don't know...|Cremia|MM}}</ref> It is also likely that the two brothers are the ones responsible for blocking the path to the {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|display=Ranch}} with a giant {{Term|MM3D|Boulder|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Someone was up to some no-good mischief and blocked the road to Romani Ranch.|Carpenter|MM}}</ref> preventing {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch}} from bringing supplies to the town's {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh my! Did you come from town? ...Then that means... Milk Road is open to traffic! Good! I can deliver milk to town now!|Cremia|MM}}</ref>

The {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}} are dismissive of those who show up at the {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Track}} without a horse.<ref>{{Cite|You got no business here if you got no horse. Now beat it! Scram! I told you this is a horse training center! It means nuthin' if you got no horse! Got it?|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> Moreover, they continue to demonstrate their malicious intents by suggesting that if the visitor is in need of a horse, they could just steal a horse from {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch}} as the act would likely go unnoticed.<ref>{{Cite|This is just between you and me, but if you need a horse, there are a bunch next door at Romani Ranch. Some young girl's running it. I bet she wouldn't notice if ya stole yourself one!|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> If {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} is wearing the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Mask|link}}, they will be terrified of him and will tell him that even though he cannot race without a horse, he is free to look around.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, sir. Heh, heh...What brings you here? We're the Gorman Brothers and this is our horse training center...Without a horse...you...Now, now...Don't get angry. All right, if you don't have a horse, go ahead and take a look around.|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> They also sell {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} for 50 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee|link}} and mention that the {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} produced at {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch}} is watered down and not worth drinking, while claiming that theirs is always fresh and full of vitamins.<ref>{{Cite|Won'tcha buy some fresh milk? This is just between you and me... That milk they produce down yonder at Romani Ranch is watered down a bit. It's not worth drinkin'. We don't do that kinda thing here. Ours is the real deal. It's always fresh and full of vitamins. It's true! I'll sell it cheap just for you. How about it?|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> Unlike {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch}}, the {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}} do not show hospitality to the citizens who are fleeing from {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}} during the {{Term|MM3D|Final Day|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What? Did you come runnin' here from town? Well it's been only that type of folk coming 'round here lately. Sorry, but this is no place for you to take shelter.|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref>

When {{Term|MM3D|Link}} first meets the brothers, they treat him with a cocky and arrogant attitude as they make fun of the size of his horse, {{Term|MM3D|Epona|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Is that pathetic thing your horse? I would've guessed that little thing was a mule fer sure, ain't that right, little brother?|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> They challenge him to a race for 10 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}}, confident that they will win.<ref>{{Cite|This guy's funny! Why don't you give us 10 Rupees for the chance to race against us?|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> When {{Term|MM3D|Link}} beats them, they hand over the {{Term|MM3D|Garo's Mask|link}}, so long as the young hero promises to not tell anyone where he got it from.<ref>{{Cite|We gotta give him something. Well, he's a kid, so what could he do with it? It should be fine... In exchange, you can't tell anyone where you got this!|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref>

If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} helped {{Term|MM3D|Romani|link}} defend the {{Plural|MM3D|Cow|link}} from the {{Plural|MM3D|Ghost (Majora's Mask)|link}} on the night of the {{Term|MM3D|First Day|link}} and accepts to accompany {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} on the {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} delivery to town on the {{Term|MM3D|Second Day|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm going by wagon into town. Would you like a ride? I'll be leaving about 6 o'clock this evening, so you can join me if you'd like.|Cremia|MM}}</ref> the {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|display=Ranch}} owner and {{Term|MM3D|Link}} discover that the road to {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}} has been blocked, most likely due to the {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}}. As such, they are forced to take a detour through the {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Track}}.<ref>{{Cite|First Milk Road is blocked by a boulder and now... We have to take this detour through ugly country...|Cremia|MM}}</ref> The {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}}, wearing {{Plural|MM3D|Garo's Mask}} while riding on their horses, chase the wagon and try to break the cargo of {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} bottles that {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} is trying to deliver to the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you ready, boy? I'm going to try to get us through here as fast as I can! If any pursuers come from behind, chase them off with your arrows. They may be after my cargo of milk bottles.|Cremia|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Link}} chases them off using his {{Term|MM3D|Bow|link}} and {{Plural|MM3D|Arrow}}, and the pair is able to escape the {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}} without any major harm done. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} succeeds in protecting the {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} from the brothers, they will not appear on the {{Term|MM3D|Final Day}}, and instead a sign will be present at the {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Track}} stating that riding lessons have been temporarily suspended.<ref>{{Cite|Riding lessons have been temporarily suspended. The Gorman Bros.|Sign|MM}}</ref> However, if he fails to protect the {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} delivery, the {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}} will have taken over the {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} business.<ref>{{Cite|We're the only place you'll be able to get milk from!|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref>

If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} talks to them using the {{Term|MM3D|Troupe Leader's Mask|link}}, the {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}} will get sentimental and talk about their middle brother, {{Term|MM3D|Gorman|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Grrrr... We're the notorious Gorman Brothers! We can't get all sappy over stuff like this!|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So, he's gone into the world of entertainment... That younger brother of mine is really struggling...|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> They will even show a sign of remorse for all the malevolent deeds they have done, but then mention that they cannot get emotional over such things. Thus, they ask {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to stop making them feel uncomfortable and to take the {{Term|MM3D|Troupe Leader's Mask|display=Mask}} off.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry, but take that mask off. I can't get anything done when you gaze at me with that mask on.|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> Wearing the {{Term|MM3D|Troupe Leader's Mask}} also prevents them from attacking {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}}'s wagon on the night of the {{Term|MM3D|Second Day}}.

===Gorman===
{{Main|Gorman}}
[[File:MM3D Gorman Notebook Portrait.png|left|Gorman's picture from the Bombers' Notebook in {{MM3D|-}}]]
The middle sibling of the {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}},<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, I'm just the despised second son!|Gorman|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Gorman}}, seems to be kinder and more benevolent than his brothers, despite that he might not show it when {{Term|MM3D|Link}} first talks to him.<ref>{{Cite|Bah! Bother! Bother! I'm busy!|Gorman|MM}}</ref> Unlike his brothers, he was no good at handling and taking care of horses,<ref>{{Cite|I can't handle horses like my older brother! I can't take care of horses like my younger brother!|Gorman|MM}}</ref> so he left the ranch life to join the world of show business after hearing the [[Lulu's Mother|mother]] of {{Term|MM3D|Lulu|link}} sing the famous melody of "{{Term|MM3D|Ballad of the Wind Fish|link}}".<ref>{{Cite|I left the ranch, came to know the world of show business... Traveled around... But for what? For nothing!|Gorman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I entered show business because I heard this song at the Carnival of Time long ago... By doing performances, I thought maybe I'd get to meet the singers of that song...|Gorman|MM}}</ref>

If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} comforts him by playing the "{{Term|MM3D|Ballad of the Wind Fish}}" during a sound check at the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar}}, he gives the young hero the {{Term|MM3D|Troupe Leader's Mask}} as a reward for helping him remember why he joined the world of show business.
