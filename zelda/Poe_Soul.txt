{{Term/Store|Poe Soul|Poe Souls|Series, TFH|plural}} are a recurring items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 134"/> They are the remnants of [[Poe]]s after they have been slain.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT3D Poe Soul Icon.png|left]]
In {{OoT}}, a defeated [[Poe]] will drop its lamp to the ground, revealing a violet Poe Soul encased within, while a [[Big Poe]] will leave behind an orange '''Big Poe Soul'''. These can be optionally snatched up in an empty [[Bottle]], and will disappear if left untouched. Consuming a Poe Soul will randomly either restore a portion of [[Link]]'s [[Heart]]s or deplete them until he only has a partial Heart remaining.

Trapped souls can also be sold to the [[Poe Collector]] for 10 [[Rupee]]s for a Poe Soul or 50 Rupees and 100 Points for a Big Poe Soul. If Link sells all 10 Big Poe Souls to the Poe Collector, he will reward him with an empty Bottle at 1,000 Points. The 10 Big Poes at [[Hyrule Field]] will only appear if Adult Link is riding [[Epona]] and passes their spawn point. He must then shoot them down with the [[Bow]] to collect their souls. The various Poe Souls contained by the Poe Collector can be seen attempting to escape their cages and after some time, he whips his stick, locking the souls back in.

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM3D Poe Icon.png|left]]
In {{MM}}, defeated Poes also drop their lamps to the ground, revealing a Poe Soul encased within. They can also be snatched up in an empty Bottle, and will disappear if left untouched. Consuming a Poe Soul will cause the same effects as in {{OoT|-}}. A Big Poe is found [[Beneath the Well]] in [[Ikana Canyon]], revealing a Big Poe Soul when defeated. The Big Poe Soul must be given to a [[Gibdo]] in order to proceed through the well. It can also be sold at the [[Curiosity Shop]] for 200 Rupees. Another Big Poe appears [[Beneath the Graveyard]] on the night of the [[Final Day]], after Link has uncovered three blue flames by helping [[Dampé]] dig patches of dirt. It also leaves a Big Poe Soul behind upon defeat.

==={{TP|-}}===
[[File:TPHD Poe Soul Icon.png|50px|left]]
In {{TP}}, Link can collect up to 60 Poe Souls throughout [[Hyrule]] in order to remove the curse on [[Jovani]]. Imp Poes can only be seen and defeated as [[Wolf Link]]. Collecting 20 Poe Souls for Jovani will lift part of his curse, earning Link a Bottle of [[Great Fairy's Tears]], and the [[Ghost Lantern]] in {{TPHD}}. In the HD version, the number of Poe Souls collected and remaining in each area is marked on the Map screen when the Portal view option is off. Collecting all 60 Poe Souls will completely lift Jovani's curse, rewarding Link with 200 Rupees. Continuing the quest by visiting Telma's Bar and speaking with Jovanni there will allow Link to earn 200 more Rupees indefinitely each time he speaks to [[Gengle]], once per visit to Castle Town.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, {{Plural|TFH|Poe Soul}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Ruins|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Illusory Mansion|link}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Poe Soul}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Palace Noir|link}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Poe Soul}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Palace Noir}}''', ''"Transport the orb quickly!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Poe Soul}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Lone Labyrinth|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Poe Soul}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Lone Labyrinth}}''', ''"Guard the orb!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Poe Soul}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Poe Soul}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Poe Soul}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Light Armor|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Serpent's Toga|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 134"/>

== Trivia ==

* Although Poe Souls in the [[Nintendo 64]] installments and their remakes on the [[Nintendo 3DS]] can be used to slightly regain [[Life Gauge|health]] (or lose some) at random, the action of [[Link]] consuming the remains of [[Poe|Poes]] in the same manner as [[Milk]] or a [[Potion]] is considered by some fans as one of the darkest in the series, as he is indeed eating a spirit.
* The souls of [[Big Poe|Big Poes]] cannot be consumed by Link.
* Poe Souls in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' notably have different colors and expressions. Regular Poes are purple with a sad face, the Big Poes are yellow with a maniacal look, and the [[Composer Brothers]] for the former game are green with a happy face.
* The [[Poe Sisters]] are the only type of Poe not to have a soul, as they leave the flames they have stolen in the [[Forest Temple]] when defeated.
* With the exception of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess,]]'' Poe Souls shares the same graphics as fire seen on torches.
* When the soul of a regular or Big Poe is captured in ''Ocarina of Time'', the screeching sound of the Poe Sisters [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_PoeSister_Die.wav dying] is heard. If Link refuses to capture it and the soul disappears, the [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Poe_Laugh.wav laugh of the Poe] can be heard instead (which is actually a sped-up version of [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Ganondorf_Laugh.wav Ganondorf's laugh]).
* The Poe Souls of the Composer Brothers cannot be captured, as they immediately disappear after speaking to Link.
