{{Term/Store|Golden Monster|Golden Monsters|Series, OoS, TMC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= The Four Golden Monsters |game= OoS |guide= Versus |page= 55 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC |publisher= Nintendo |page= 92}}</ref>{{Name Ref|BotW}} They are much more difficult to defeat than their regular counterparts, with improved speed, power or health. In return, the reward for defeating these rare monsters is substantially higher. Their appearance is usually tied to the activation of an event, such as a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you ever seen a golden monster? When you fuse Kinstone pieces, you might get the rare chance to meet one.|Swordsman Newsletter 4|TMC}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:Golden Old Man.png|left|32px|The Old Man in gold robes]]
Four different {{Plural|OoS|Golden Monster}} will appear around various regions of {{Term|OoS|Holodrum|link}}. When all four have been defeated, {{Term|OoS|Link}} can speak to the golden-robed {{Term|OoS|Old Man|link}} living in the cave accessible in {{Term|OoS|Summer|link}}, when the water around {{Term|OoS|Gnarled Root Dungeon|link}} recedes. {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} receives a {{Term|OoS|Magical Ring|link}} which, when appraised by {{Term|OoS|Vasu|link}}, becomes the powerful {{Term|OoS|Red Ring|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 17}}</ref>

They are {{Plural|TMC|Octorok|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Tektite|link}}, and {{Plural|TMC|Rope|link}}. After one is defeated, they will drop a large sum of {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}} in {{BotW}}, {{Plural|BotW|Golden Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Golden Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lynel|link}} appear as a stronger variant than silver enemies. According to the [[Hyrule Compendium]], {{Plural|BotW|Golden Monster}} are silver enemies that underwent a mysterious transformation after being struck by lightning.<ref>{{Cite|It is said they are actually Silver <nowiki>[Monsters]</nowiki> who mysteriously transformed after being struck by lightning.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Unlike the other variants, {{Plural|BotW|Golden Monster}} have a stronger resistance to status effects, such as Shock, [[Ice]], and [[Fire]]. They can recover from being shocked faster, thaw out of ice faster, and are not as susceptible to flames.
