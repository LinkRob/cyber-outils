'''Seahats''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 202}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Tingle
|quote1= <p>It's a '''Seahat'''!</p><p>Hit it with a projectile weapon!</p>
}}

Seahats are large, bulbous creatures with spiny undersides that both inhabit and roam the [[Great Sea]]. Seahats are the marine, [[fish]]-like counterpart of the earthly, [[plant]]-like [[Peahat]]s, with both having "-hat" as the suffix of their name and propeller-like appendages to hover around.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW|-}}, Seahats are usually found together in large numbers off the coast of islands such as the [[Boating Course]], [[Southern Triangle Island]], [[Cyclops Reef]], and [[Horseshoe Island]]. Seahats usually attack [[Link]] if he is on the [[King of Red Lions]] and usually ignore him otherwise. A Seahat attacks by first dipping into the water, up to its nostrils, and then propelling itself at Link while making a loud whirring sound. If the Seahat hits the King of Red Lions, Link is knocked overboard.

Seahats do not attack Link while he is on land, although they may still float towards him and unintentionally damage him as a result of hovering back and forth. Seahats do not follow Link too far from the island or area where they are found, opting to rejoin their group after traveling a certain distance.

Much like Peahats, striking a Seahat with a [[Boomerang]] cuts off its propeller-fins, leaving it to swim helplessly in the water. The fins grow back after a short period, but if a Seahat is struck by a Boomerang again, it becomes defeated. Seahats can be quickly defeating from either an [[Arrow]] or by firing a [[Bomb]] at it from the [[Cannon]].

If a Seahat's propeller-fins are chopped off while it is hovering above land, a glitch may occur where the Seahat will be frozen on the spot (see [[Glitches in The Wind Waker#Sea Hat Glitch|Seahat Glitch]]).
