'''Little Froxes''' are [[Enemy|Enemies]] in {{TotK}}.

==Appearance==
Little Froxes have a black body reminiscent of a [[frog]], with a single eye in the center. Like its name would suggest, it appears to be a young, diminuitive form of a [[Frox]].

==Characteristics==
==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Compendium|number=184|name=Little Frox|desc= A very young Frox. Makes up for its diminutive size with powerful legs and by attacking in large numbers.|locs=The Depths|loot=Giant Brightbloom Seed, Zonaite, Brightbloom Seed}}
Little Froxes can be found in [[The Depths]] between 2PM to 8PM (measured in the Great Hyrule Forest) in small packs roaming around. When disturbed, they will make a shout to alert the rest of their pack, and then engage [[Link]]. They attack by jumping on him, uniquely being one of the few creatures in the Depths (alongside [[Flux Construct|Flux Constructs]] and their bigger relatives) that is not afflicted by Gloom.

Little Froxes are attracted to [[Brightbloom Seed|Brightbloom Seeds]] and will eat them, though this will make them vulnerable to Link's attacks since they are distracted eating them.
