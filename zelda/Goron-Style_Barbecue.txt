{{Term/Store|Goron-Style Barbecue||HWAoC}} is a level 50 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Goron-Style Barbecue|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Goron-Style Barbecue}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Opal|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Endura Carrot|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Courser Bee Honey|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within six minutes. At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, four {{Plural|HWAoC|Goron Captain|link}} also spawn alongside the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. After the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches 50 KOs, a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} spawns, and when it is killed a gate opens. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} moves past the gate, magma bombs rain down on them until they reach another gate, where a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} will spawn when they get there. After 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are killed, an {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus}} spawns and the timer resets to five minutes. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus}} is defeated.
