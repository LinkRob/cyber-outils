==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Tri Suit is tres special. If three heroes wear this at the same time... You'll have to wait and see!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Tri Suit}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} for 300 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} and five {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token|link}}. Its actual abilities are left a mystery before it is purchased, with various descriptions hinting that "good things happen" when three heroes wear the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} together.<ref>{{Cite|They say good things happen if three heroes wear the Tri Suit. I bet it's something cool!|Madame Couture's Apprentice|TFH}}</ref>

When all three {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} wear the {{Term|TFH|Tri Suit}}, they gain the combined abilities of the {{Term|TFH|Energy Gear|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Legendary Dress|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Lucky Loungewear|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Rupee Regalia|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit}}. Each {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s {{Term|TFH|Energy Gauge|link}} is increased by 50%, they automatically dodge 25% of hits, and the drop rates are increased for {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} appearing from broken {{Plural|TFH|Jar|link}}, cut grass, and defeated [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]].<ref>{{Cite|When three are worn, heart and Rupee drop rates go up. Energy gauges go up 50%.|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref>

Any {{Plural|TFH|Links}} who join the game through [[Download Play]] will be unable to obtain the {{Term|TFH|Tri Suit}}. Though the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} appears in the {{Term|TFH|Catalog|link}}, it is impossible to collect the required {{Plural|TFH|Material}} in this mode.

The design of the {{Term|TFH|Tri Suit}} is loosely based on the ''{{Wp|Super Sentai}}'' series, which was adapted in the United States as ''{{Wp|Power Rangers}}''.
