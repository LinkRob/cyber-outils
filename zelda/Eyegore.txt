They are large, {{Wp|Cyclops}}-like creatures with tough defenses that make them impervious to most attacks, with their key weak spot being their single eye. {{Plural|Series|Eyegore}} are weak to projectile weapons, especially to the {{Term|Series|Bow|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Arrow|link}}, which in some cases are the only weapons that will inflict any damage onto them.

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Eyegore}} can be found in the {{Term|ALttP|Eastern Palace|link}} and the {{Term|ALttP|Desert Palace|link}}, and later in {{Term|ALttP|Ganon's Tower|link}}. They stand still until they are approached, when they open their eye and charge at {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} for a brief moment. Only when their eye is open are they vulnerable to attacks. {{Plural|ALttP|Eyegore}} are split into two groups; {{Plural|ALttP|Green Eyegore|link}} and {{Plural|ALttP|Red Eyegore|link}}. {{Plural|ALttP|Green Eyegore}} can be defeated with just the {{Term|ALttP|Sword|link}}, but they are durable and require many hits to defeat. An {{Term|ALttP|Arrow|link}} or a thrown {{Term|ALttP|Jar|link}} straight in the eye defeats them instantly. {{Plural|ALttP|Red Eyegore}} are completely immune to all attacks even when their eye is open, except to {{Plural|ALttP|Arrow}}. Two {{Plural|ALttP|Arrow}} will defeat {{Plural|ALttP|Red Eyegore}}.

Decorative {{Term|ALttP|Eyegore}} statues also appear in various dungeons in both the {{Term|ALttP|Light World|display=Light}} and {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}}, and one in particular is key to solving a specific puzzle. Some of them also hold {{Plural|ALttP|Telepathy Tile|link}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= It's an {{Color|MM White|Eyegore}}.<br/> Simply attacking it is no good. I say you should pay attention to the subtle changes in its eye.
}}

In {{MM}}, {{Plural|MM3D|Eyegore}} are hulking monsters with slender bodies and large heads, which consist solely of their eye. Only two of them appear in the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|link}}. They stand dormant until approached. Once awake, they will creep forward and fire beams at {{Term|MM3D|Link}} from their single yellow eye. They will also slam the ground with their fists to send rocks flying at him. When {{Term|MM3D|Link}} has been warded off, they will quickly retreat to their point of origin and become dormant again. {{Plural|MM3D|Eyegore}} can be stunned by aiming the {{Term|MM3D|Hero's Bow|link}} or {{Term|MM3D|Hookshot|link}} at their eye, thus leaving them open to further attack. They can only be damaged when their eye is yellow.

==={{FS|-}}===
In {{FS}}, {{Plural|FS|Eyegore}} appear as stone statue-like monsters with horns and a prominent single eye. Initially, they are dormant, but awaken and hop after {{Term|FS|Link}} when approached. They can appear in any stage, including the {{Term|FS|Realm of Memories|link}} and {{Term|FS|Hero's Trial|link}} in {{FSAE}}.

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, {{Plural|FSA|Eyegore}} will awaken and charge at the {{Plural|FSA|Link}} in short intervals when approached. They can only be attacked by shooting {{Plural|FSA|Arrow}} into their eyes. {{Plural|FSA|Eyegore}} can only be found in the {{Term|FSA|Desert Temple (Four Swords Adventures)|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Eyegore Statue}}
{{Plural|TMC|Eyegore Statue}} appear throughout the {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}} in {{TMC}}. They stand stationary in a dormant state, often blocking the path to other areas of the marsh. They will only activate once shot in the eye with an {{Term|TMC|Arrow}} from {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Bow}}, after which they will attack by jumping towards him. They are slow, as they must hop in place to turn around. {{Plural|TMC|Eyegore Statue}} can be destroyed by shooting them in the eye with three {{Plural|TMC|Arrow}}, not counting the first {{Term|TMC|Arrow}} used to activate them. A single {{Term|TMC|Light Arrow|link}} will also destroy them. {{Plural|TMC|Eyegore Statue}} will often drop a bundle of {{Plural|TMC|Arrow}} once defeated. {{Term|TMC|Link}} can also simply run past the {{Term|TMC|Eyegore Statue|display=Statue}} once it jumps out of the way.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, just like {{ALttP|-}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Eyegore}} are split into three groups; {{Plural|ALBW|Green Eyegore|link}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Blue Eyegore|link}}, and {{Plural|ALBW|Red Eyegore|link}}. {{Plural|ALBW|Eyegore}} stand dormant until {{Term|ALBW|Link}} comes near, when they awake and quickly charge at him. They, however, no longer require the {{Term|ALBW|Bow}} to be defeated and can be attacked with any conventional weapon. Their eyes also brightly glow in pitch dark spaces whenever they are open. A group of five {{Plural|ALBW|Red Eyegore}} are also fought as {{Plural|ALBW|Sub-Boss|link}} in a dark room of the {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Hideout|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
Eyegore refers to the monster's single eye. Rocklops is a combination of rock and cyclops, referring to the monster's stone-like defenses and being a cyclops-like creature.

Its Japanese name, {{Romanize|アイゴール せいきぞう|Aigōru Seikizou}}, is a pun. ''Seikizou'' is a combination of {{Romanize|生気|Seiki}} ("living"), and {{Romanize|石像|Sekizou}} ("stone statue").
