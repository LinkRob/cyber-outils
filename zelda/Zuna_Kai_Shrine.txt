The {{Term/Store|Zuna Kai Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Zuna Kai Shrine}}<br/>Zuna Kai's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Zuna Kai's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai Shrine}} is located on the eastern eye of {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Deep Akkala|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Jerrin|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} will direct {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake}}'s left eye after mentioning she has focused a lot of her research on {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm an amateur scholar of ancient history. To be honest...I've been focusing a lot of my research on {{Color|BotW Blue|shrines}}. ...Oh, that reminds me! Link, there's somewhere you need to go. Umm... The {{Color|BotW Red|left eye of Skull Lake}}!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> She will then tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} that it holds an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine }}, but it will be challenging to reach.<ref>{{Cite|I know there's a {{Color|BotW Blue|shrine}} there, but it's a challenge to get to it... The {{Color|BotW Red|left eye}} of {{Color|BotW Red|Skull Lake}} is higher up. The right one is lower down. If you run into {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}, you're on the wrong eye!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> This initiates "{{Term|BotW|The Skull's Eye|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} can be found atop a spire on the eastern island of {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake}}, which is the {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|display=Skull}}'s left eye. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the top of the spire and activates {{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai Shrine}}, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.<ref>{{Cite|You gained entry into the ancient shrine atop the left eye of Skull Lake.<br/><br/>You understand now what Jerrin meant when she said that the real trial was getting there.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Zuna Kai. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Zuna Kai|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Flameblade|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Zuna Kai|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai}} wishes him well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Zuna Kai|BotW}}</ref>
