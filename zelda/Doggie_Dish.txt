The {{Term/Store|Doggie Dish|Doggie Dishes|Series, TMC}} is an object in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|I believe there should be a {{Color|TMC Blue|doggie dish}} for my dog back at my house. Just face the {{Color|TMC Blue|dish}} and press {{Icon|TMC R}} to dump the food into little Fifi's bowl.|Stockwell|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Doggie Dish}} is located inside {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's House|link}} in the northern area of {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} attempts to take the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link|display=Bottle}} of {{Term|TMC|Dog Food|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Stockwell|link}} asks him to feed {{Term|TMC|Fifi|link}}, allowing him to keep the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle}} when he has finished.<ref>{{Cite|That bottle contains food for my dear little dog, Fifi... What? You just want the {{Color|TMC Red|bottle}}? That's highly unusual... Well, fine. If you feed Fifi, then I guess {{Color|TMC Blue|you can have the bottle}}.|Stockwell|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Spare Key|link}} and passes through {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}, he can access {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's House}}, where he can dump the {{Term|TMC|Dog Food}} into the {{Term|TMC|Doggie Dish}}, feeding {{Term|TMC|Fifi}} and keeping the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle}} for himself.
