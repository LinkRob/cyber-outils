==Location and Uses==
The Salvager Trousers are a piece of [[Armor]] in {{BotW}}. They are obtained from a [[Treasure Chest]] as part of the "[[Xenoblade Chronicles 2|[Xenoblade Chronicles 2]]]" [[Side Quest]]. The Chest crashes on a plateau on the southern side of the [[Hebra East Summit]] after [[Link]] looks to the southeastern sky from the [[Hebra Peak]] during the night. After obtaining the Salvager Trousers, [[Granté]] will begin selling them for 50 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, the Salvager Trousers provide one level of Swim Speed Up. The Salvager Trousers cannot be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], nor can they be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
