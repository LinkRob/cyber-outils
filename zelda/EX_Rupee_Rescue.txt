{{Term/Store|EX Rupee Rescue||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 85 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Rupee Rescue|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the first wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Rupee Rescue}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given eight minutes to collect as many {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}} as possible. The original objective is 100, although it raises infinitely as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches it. There are {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Keese|link}}, a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} scattered around the map, with the defeat of some {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} required to open gates. All {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} drop at least one {{Term|HWAoC|Rupee}} when defeated, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} drop {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee}} of higher value, and after a certain amount of time, an abnormally large {{Term|HWAoC|Chuchu|link}} spawns, which drops a {{Term|HWAoC|Gold Rupee|link}}. A very large {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu}} spawns as well, and leave if not approached for too long. The same happens again later, although what spawns is one abnormally large {{Term|HWAoC|Chuchu}} of each element. {{Plural|HWAoC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee}} are also scattered around the map, and jars and {{Plural|HWAoC|Wooden Box|link}} may drop an unusually large amount of {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee}}. In this {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, healing {{Plural|HWAoC|Item|link}} also never appear.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Mighty Banana|link}}, and 2,000 {{Term|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.
