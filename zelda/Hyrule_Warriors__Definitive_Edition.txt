'''{{HW|DE|-}}''' is a port and adaptation of {{HW}} and {{HW|L}} for the [[Nintendo Switch]]. It includes all previously released content from both games, including every map, character, mission, and downloadable content.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This new version brings every map, character, and mission from both the Wii U game and the Nintendo 3DS game together, including all the original premium downloadable content.|author= Nintendo|published= January 11, 2018|retrieved= January 11, 2018|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_u7g5BlfiY|title= Nintendo Direct Mini 1.11.2018|site= Youtube.com|type= video}}</ref> This puts the amount of playable [[Warrior]]s at a total of 29.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= That means you can play Hyrule Warriors anytime, anywhere, with anyone using 29 playable characters.|author= Nintendo|published= January 11, 2018|retrieved= January 11, 2018|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_u7g5BlfiY|title= Nintendo Direct Mini 1.11.2018|site= Youtube.com|type= video}}</ref> The game features 1080p HD resolution in TV mode and 2 player split-screen multiplayer on one Nintendo Switch console both in TV mode and on the device itself, and can be played with a single Joy-Con controller.<ref name="1080p">{{Cite Web|quote= Enjoy up to 1080p HD graphics in TV mode and 2 player multiplayer on a single system by sharing Joy-Con controllers.|author= Nintendo|published= January 11, 2018|retrieved= January 11, 2018|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_u7g5BlfiY|title= Nintendo Direct Mini 1.11.2018|site= Youtube.com|type= video}}</ref>

==Revisions==
===Story===
{{Main|Hyrule Warriors#Story, Hyrule Warriors Legends#Story}}
{{HW|DE|-}} includes all the story content from both {{HW|-}} and {{HW|L|-}}. This includes all the [[Scenario]]s featured in [[Legend Mode]], including the Scenarios from "[[Cia's Tale]]", "[[Linkle's Tale]]", and the "[[Legend Mode#Wind Waker|Wind Waker]]" epilogue.

===Gameplay===
{{Main|Hyrule Warriors#Gameplay, Hyrule Warriors Legends#Gameplay}}
{{HW|DE|-}}'s gameplay is identical to {{HW|-}} and {{HW|L|-}} including all Modes, [[Warrior]]s and Stages from those games. In addition, the game adds new [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] [[Costume]]s based on their appearance in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Plus, we're adding in Link and Zelda outfits based on The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild.|author= Nintendo|published= January 11, 2018|retrieved= January 11, 2018|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_u7g5BlfiY|title= Nintendo Direct Mini 1.11.2018|site= Youtube.com|type= video}}</ref>

==Game Information==
===Development===
{{HW|DE|-}} was announced in a [[Nintendo Direct Mini]] on January 11, 2018. Like {{HW|-}} and {{HW|L|-}}, it was developed by Koei Tecmo Games Co., Ltd.<ref name="Tecmo">{{Cite Web|quote= {{Typo|Tecmo Koei|Koei Tecmo}} Games Co., Ltd.|author= Nintendo|published= January 11, 2018|retrieved= January 11, 2018|url= https://www.nintendo.com/games/detail/hyrule-warriors-switch|title= Hyrule Warriors: Definitive Edition|site= Nintendo.com|type= }}</ref>

===Release===
{{HW|DE|-}} was released on March 22, 2018 for Japan and May 18, 2018 for North America and Europe.<ref name="JP Release"/><ref name="NA Release"/><ref name="EU Release"/>

===Pre-Order Bonuses===
The Nintendo Official UK Store bundled pre-orders of {{HW|DE|-}} with a free [[Green Rupee]] Light by Merchoid. When the Green Rupee Light sold out, the game was then bundled with a [[Triforce]] Light.
