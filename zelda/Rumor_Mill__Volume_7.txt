The {{Term/Store|''Rumor Mill: Volume 7''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor Mill: Volume 7|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill: Volume 7}} is located on the top bunk of the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn|link}}. In this volume of the {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} recounts rumors about the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} and their secrets hidden within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}. Though she would never confirm these rumors herself due to the danger of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}, she still gives it five out of five stars.<ref>{{Cite|But there's no way I'm going anywhere near a place as dangerous as Hyrule Castle. No way! Don't let that scare you, though... Traysi's Recommendation: ★★★★★|Rumor Mill: Volume 7|BotW}}</ref>
