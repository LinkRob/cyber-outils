{{Term/Store|Unnatural Disaster||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 80 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Unnatural Disaster|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Unnatural Disaster}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given eleven minutes to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Frost Talus|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Igneo Talus|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Lynel}}. They spawn in that order, and each one spawns after the previous one is killed. It can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ancient Core|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Dark Tunic|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
