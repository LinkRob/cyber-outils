==Biography==
{{Term|LADX|BowWow}} is one of {{Term|LADX|Madam MeowMeow|link}}'s three pets, being the biggest of them and the only one kept outdoors. Like the {{Plural|LADX|Chain Chomp|link}} of {{ALttP}}, {{Term|LADX|BowWow}} charges at {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} when he approaches, although he is completely harmless. When {{Term|LADX|Link}} comes back from {{Term|LADX|Toronbo Shores|link}} after completing {{Term|LADX|Tail Cave|link}}, a pair of {{Plural|LADX|Quadruplet|link}} near the {{Term|LADX|Village Library|link}} will inform him that {{Term|LADX|BowWow}} has been kidnapped by a gang of {{Plural|LADX|Moblin|link}}.<ref name="Kids"/><ref name="Guide 32">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 32}}</ref> {{Term|LADX|Link}} can go to {{Term|LADX|Madam MeowMeow's House|link}} in {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village|link}} only to find her panicked and begging for help.<ref>{{Cite|AIEEEEEEEE! It's terrrrible! My BowWow was dognapped by... Mo-mo-MOBLINS!! OHHH! AHHHH! Please! Somebody help my poor BowWow!!|Madam MeowMeow|LADX}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* A {{Term|LADX|Signpost|link}} near the western entrance of {{Term|LADX|Goponga Swamp|link}} warns adventurers to keep out due to the imminent danger that it represents, except for {{Term|LADX|BowWow}}.<ref>{{Cite|DANGER! Keep out! (Except BowWow)|Signpost|LADX}}</ref> This is because he is the only one able to eat the {{Plural|LADX|Goponga Flower|link}} present in {{Term|LADX|Goponga Swamp}}.
* The Japanese name for BowWow and Chain Chomp is Wanwan (ワンワン), the Japanese onomatopoeia for a barking sound, making it a inadvertently literal translation of the Mario enemy's name.
* BowWow sometimes telling Link to dig in certain spots while barking, which indicates where a {{Term|LADX|Secret Seashell}} is hidden, may be a reference to the Japanese folk tale ''{{Wp|Hanasaka Jiisan}}'', where an old man's dog tells its owner to dig in a certain spot while barking, which leads to him uncovering gold coins.
* When Link first meets BowWow, he strains against his post attempting to attack him, but after Link has returned him to his master, he will jump up and down in glee, attempting to play with him.
