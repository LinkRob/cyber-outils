{{Term/Store|Ilari||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Ilari}}
Ilari was born on [[Dragon Roost Island]]. He is a Rito postman, and can usually be found at the highest balcony of the island. He tells [[Link]] about [[Flight Control Platform]] before taking off.

After Link sends [[Maggie's Letter]] to the [[Postbox]], Ilari is seen the next day on [[Windfall Island]], where he and [[Maggie's Father]] are seen arguing inside the newly-rich homeowner's house.<ref>{{Cite|Silence! I'm telling you we don't allow postmen here! Begone! Begone!|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Don't be ridiculous! I went through great trials and tribulations to go all the way to the Forsaken Fortress and return with Moe's letter...|Ilari|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|SILENCE! Begone! Begone!|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref> Maggie's Father refuses to let the postman deliver the [[Moblin's Letter]] to {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie|link}} and will not listen to reason.<ref>{{Cite|Then, please! Be reasonable! Can you at least sign for the letter?!|Ilari|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|SILENCE! Begone! Begone!|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Just acknowledge I was here!|Ilari|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|SILENCE! Begone! Begone!|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I came to deliver a letter addressed to Maggie from a Moblin named Moe, but as you saw, the girl's stubborn father won't permit me to give it to her.|Ilari|TWW}}</ref> Frustrated, Ilari leaves to the [[Cafe Bar]] to calm down.<ref>{{Cite|RAAAAAAWRK! Foolish man! Now you've made me angry! I cannot even look you in the face any longer, or I fear I might... ...I must go somewhere to cool my feathers!|Ilari|TWW}}</ref> There, he explains to Link the hardships he experienced to obtain the Letter, and feels he is a failure for failing to deliver it.<ref>{{Cite|I went to great trouble to go all the way to the Forsaken Fortress, and it was a most frightening ordeal, let me tell you! ...If I don't deliver this letter, then I am a failure of the delivery trade...|Ilari|TWW}}</ref> Link can help this troubled postman by agreeing to deliver the letter for him.<ref>{{Cite|I have an idea. That old fool may not permit postmen on his premises... But surely he would allow YOU in the place, would he not? Could you deliver this letter to Maggie in my stead?|Ilari|TWW}}</ref>

He has a difficult time remembering people's faces.
