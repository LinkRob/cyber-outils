The {{Term/Store|Rental Hammer||Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Ravio]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with [[Element|Darkness]].

==Variations==
The Level 1 Rental Hammer is the '''Wooden Hammer'''. This is unlocked with Ravio when the [[A Link Between Worlds Pack]] DLC is purchased in {{HW|-}} and {{HW|L}}. In {{HW|DE}}, both Ravio and the Wooden Hammer are unlocked as a Reward for Battle Victory in [[Lorule Map/Hyrule Field (Hyrule Warriors: Definitive Edition#Battle F5|Battle F5]] of the [[Lorule Map]]. It is modeled after the [[Hammer]] in {{ALBW}}, like the Hammer item in {{HW|L|-}} and {{HW|DE|-}}. However, it is worn around the edges, differentiating the two. This also results in it resembling the Hammer from {{PH}}, but without the handle sticking out on top as far

The Level 2 Rental Hammer is the '''White Bunny Hammer'''. In {{HW|-}}, this is unlocked with Ravio and the other tiers but must be obtained from a Weapon Bag. In {{HW|L|-}}, this is unlocked by getting an A Rank in the Lorule Map Battle E3 in [[Adventure Mode]]. As it's name implies, this Hammer is white and has a pair of white bunny ears on the top, based on the bunny ears on top of the [[Rented Hammer]] from {{ALBW|-}}. The Hammer also has a brown grip, a red band around the edge and a swirl on the faces of the Hammer.

The Level 3 Rental Hammer '''Nice Hammer'''. In {{HW|-}}, this is unlocked with Ravio and the other tiers but must be obtained from a Weapon Bag. In {{HW|L|-}}, this is unlocked by getting an A Rank in the Lorule Map Battle A1. This Hammer is modeled after its [[Nice Hammer|namesake]] and features the [[Triforce#Lorulean Triforce|Lorulean Triforce]] on the Hammer's faces. The Hammer's handle has also been replaced with a shorter version of [[Princess Hilda]]'s Staff.

The Level 4 Rental Hammer '''Nice Hammer +''', which can only be found in {{HW|L|-}}. It is unlocked by getting an A Rank in the Lorule Map Battle F8. This is visually identical to the Nice Hammer but is much stronger.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Ravio readies his Bow with three [[Bomb Arrow]]s and fires them in a spread in front him to knock enemies into the air, then tosses his [[Gale Boomerang]] to sweep them up with two tornadoes from the sides. The tornadoes then collide and form into a single, larger one in front of Ravio while he gives applause.
