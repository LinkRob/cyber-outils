'''Tingle's Uncle''' is a minor character in {{FPTRR}}. He wears a suit similar to {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}'s, although it is white.

Once {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} obtains the {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Master Rupee|display=Master Rupee|link}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle's Uncle}} will appear next to the Tower. He will try to sell {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} the Staff Roll. Once {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} buys the Staff Roll, he will then float off, and only appear during the credits after the game has been completed.
