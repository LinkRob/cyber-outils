==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Cool Blue Summer}}" is an event wherein a greater number of blue {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Blue-colored spirits are keeping it fresh on the Spirit Board during this event!|author= Nintendo |published= July 26, 2022 |retrieved= August 13, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/9d4b112a/index.html |title= Event: Cool Blue Summer |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
