{{Term/Store|Desert Palace||Series, ALttP, ALBW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Dungeon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 147 {{Exp Game|ALttP}} & 159 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
====Entrance to the {{Term|ALttP|Desert Palace|display=Palace}}====
The palace is located in the south-west corner of [[Hyrule]] in the [[Desert of Mystery]]. Access is granted only by using the [[Book of Mudora]] to read the inscription on the stone that is placed on a small square with three statues surrounding it. The tablet instructs Link to wish for the way forward to open. Link raises his eyes upward and appears to cross himself before joining his hands in prayer. When he finishes, the statues revolve 180 degrees around the platform, opening a pathway to the {{Term|ALttP|Desert Palace|display=Palace}} entrance.

====Themes and Navigation====
Many of this dungeon's rooms have sand floors, with enemies that hide inside quicksand which attack [[Link]] by firing projectiles at him. It is also the first time [[Beamos]] are found. Interestingly, most of the rooms are accessible at first, and some of them lead to alternate exits. The main treasure is the [[Power Glove]], which allows Link to lift boulders. The boss is a group of three [[Lanmola]]s. After defeating them, Link receives a [[Heart Container]] and the [[Pendants of Virtue#Pendant of Power|Pendant of Power]].

Around three-fourths of the way through the dungeon, Link must actually exit through one of the outer stone heads and use the [[Power Glove|Power Glove]] to venture toward the second floor, where the bosses are found a few rooms further. This feature of exiting a dungeon entirely in order to progress further is shared with the [[Skull Woods (A Link to the Past)|Skull Woods]] in the same game, the [[Spirit Temple]] in {{OoT}}, and [[Mermaid's Cave]] in {{OoA}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
====Entrance to the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace|display=Palace}}====
Despite being a {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}} from which one of the {{Term|ALBW|Sage|link|display=Seven Sages}} is rescued, the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace}} rests in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}, as opposed to the other six {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon}} found in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}. It is the only one of these {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon}} that cannot be accessed before the others, due to the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod}} being rented out to {{Term|ALBW|Osfala|link}}, who must first be rescued from the {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Hideout|link}}. To access the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} must go to a small, cavernous area between the {{Term|ALBW|Southern Ruins|link}} of {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Desert of Mystery}}, then travel through a {{Term|ALBW|Fissure|link}} to {{Term|ALBW|Misery Mire|link}}. From there, other {{Plural|ALBW|Fissure}} lead him back to the upper areas of the {{Term|ALBW|Desert of Mystery|display=Desert}}, which can then be crossed using the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod}}, leading to the {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}}'s entrance.

====Themes and Navigation====
Many of the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace}}'s rooms are filled with sand. The {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod}} is required in order to create pathways for {{Term|ALBW|Link}} to cross, whether on foot or by [[Wall Merging]]. There are also numerous sand-based puzzles, like elevating a {{Term|ALBW|Beamos|link}} with the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod}} so its beams can hit {{Plural|ALBW|Shock Switch|link|display=Switches}}, filling a room with sand in order to reach otherwise inaccessible areas, and moving {{Plural|ALBW|Gimos|link|display=Gimos Statues}} to activate {{Plural|ALBW|Floor Switch|link}}. In a later room, rolling {{Plural|ALBW|Ball|link}} must be guided toward cracked and breakable blocks using the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}} contains the {{Term|ALBW|Titan's Mitt|link}}, which enables Link to lift large boulders that block certain doors and entrances.

The {{Plural|ALBW|Sub-Boss|link}} of the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace}} are a series of 32 {{Plural|ALBW|Flying Tile|link}} followed by a pair of {{Plural|ALBW|Devalant|link}}, which become regular enemies later on in the {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}}. There are two points in which {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must exit the {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}} and enter another area to proceed; first to reach the second floor where the {{Plural|ALBW|Sub-Boss}} await, and again beyond the {{Term|ALBW|Big Key|link}} door, where a {{Term|ALBW|Fissure}} leads to the {{Term|ALBW|Boss|link}}'s enclosure in {{Term|ALBW|Misery Mire}}. Once {{Term|ALBW|Link}} defeats {{Term|ALBW|Zaganaga|link}}, he will be rewarded with a {{Term|ALBW|Heart Container|link}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Portrait|link}} of the {{Term|ALBW|Sage}}, {{Term|ALBW|Irene|link}}.

The {{Term|ALBW|Compass|link}} is in the northwestern first floor room in an easily accessible {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Chest|link}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Big Key}} is on a platform in the central eastern room on the second floor. It cannot be immediately accessed; {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must first raise the sand level in the room to the north, then return and use the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod}} to get to the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}.

The {{Term|ALBW|Titan's Mitt}} is in the central northern room of the first floor. To access it, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must pull a {{Term|ALBW|Lever|link}} on the west side of the room to raise the sand level. He must then press a {{Term|ALBW|Switch}} on the north platform of the room to open the wall around the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Chest|display=chest}}. Then, using a combination of the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod}} and {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging}} to get behind the torrent of sand, he can access the {{Term|ALBW|Titan's Mitt|display=Mitt}}.

==Trivia==
*In {{ALttP}}, this is one of the few dungeons which Link is required to leave and re-enter in order to complete it.
*In {{ALttP|-}}, once Link reads the inscription in front of the Palace and the statues move, he cannot leave the dungeon through the main entrance.  He must find an alternate exit if he wishes to leave.
*The [[Spirit Temple]] in {{OoT}} borrowed elements from this dungeon such as the dungeon setting, the multiple entrances and the dungeon treasure being the [[Silver Gauntlets]], very similar items to the Power Gloves.
*The [[Desert Temple (Four Swords Adventures)|Desert Temple]] from {{FSA}} is extremely similar to the Desert Palace, with almost identical architecture and enemies. Their names are the same in Japanese, so they are possibly intended to be the same location.
*The [[Stone Tower Temple]] of {{MM}} is strikingly similar to the Desert Palace concerning its architecture, desert location, and boss ([[Twinmold]] and [[Lanmola]] being nearly identical in physical appearance, surroundings, mannerisms, and weaknesses).
*The Desert Palace makes a cameo appearance in {{EoW}} in the same area as it was in {{ALttP}} and {{ALBW}}. but since {{EoW|-}} take places long after both games, the entire palace, now known as the Desert Temple, is found in a state of decay, buried in sands and its entrances blocked off.
