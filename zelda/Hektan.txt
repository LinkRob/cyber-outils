==Characteristics==
{{Term|TWoG|Hektan}} is a wizard and a minion of {{Term|TWoG|Ganon|link}}. He was ordered by {{Term|TWoG|Ganon}} to keep {{Term|TWoG|Lord Kiro|link}}, who had tried to stop {{Term|TWoG|Ganon}} from capturing {{Term|TWoG|King Harkinian|link}}, imprisoned in chains.<ref name="Chains"/> {{Term|TWoG|Hektan}} kept him in {{Term|TWoG|Dodomai Palace|link}}, {{Term|TWoG|Duke Onkled|link}}'s castle, where {{Term|TWoG|Princess Zelda|link}} later confronts him. If {{Term|TWoG|Zelda}} speaks to {{Term|TWoG|Impa|link}} inside the palace, they witness {{Term|TWoG|Hektan}} demonstrating his abilities by repeatedly disappearing and reappearing before destroying an {{Term|TWoG|Armos|link}} in a single blast. {{Term|TWoG|Zelda}} however prevails and defeats him, and he dissolves into a pool of bubbling liquid and leaves behind a beating {{Term|TWoG|Life Heart|link}}. Though he calls the Princess out for killing him, Zelda simply responds by declaring it a good deed.

{{Term|TWoG|Hektan}} is capable of unleashing fireballs to attack {{Term|TWoG|Zelda}}. He is weak to the {{Term|TWoG|Sword|link}}, and a single slash will defeat him. Although while he seems to possess the ability to vanish in one of his cutscenes, he remains visible throughout the battle.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|TWoG|Hektan}} is similar to {{TFoE}} boss {{Term|TFoE|Harlequin|link}}. Both bosses demonstrate the ability to disappear and reappear in their cutscenes, yet do not actually possess this ability in their boss battles.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|TWoG|Hektan}} may be derived from {{Wp|Hecate}}, the Greco-Roman goddess of witchcraft.
