==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Gale Seeds|Gale Seeds produce miniature tornadoes when cracked open. These winds can carry you directly to any Mystical Tree you've found. If you shoot a Gale Seed at an enemy, the enemy will be blown far away.}}
{{Plural|OoS|Gale Seed}} are {{Plural|OoS|Mystical Seed|link}} that grow on {{Plural|OoS|Gale Tree|link}} in the Summer. They produce miniature tornadoes when cracked open. These tornadoes can {{Term|OoS|Warping|link|display= Warp}} {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} to any {{Term|OoS|Mystical Tree}} he has found.<ref>{{Cite|The mysterious tree on the edge of town bears Gale Seeds. They'll take you anywhere in a flash! They're a big help!|N/A|OoS}}</ref> When shot, they will blow almost any enemy away.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Gale Seeds|Gale Seeds produce miniature tornadoes when cracked open. These winds can carry you directly to any Mystical Tree you've found. If you shoot a Gale Seed at an enemy, the enemy will be blown far away.}}
{{Plural|OoA|Gale Seed}} are {{Plural|OoA|Mystical Seed}} that grow on {{Plural|OoA|Gale Tree|link}}. They produce miniature tornadoes when cracked open. These tornadoes allow {{Term|OoA|Link}} to {{Term|OoA|Warping|display= Warp}} to any {{Term|OoA|Mystical Tree}} he has found. When shot, they will blow almost any enemy away.
