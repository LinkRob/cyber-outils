{{Term/Store|Giant's Knife|Giant's Knives|Series, OoT, OoT3D, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Giant's Knife|Smithy (Hyrule Warriors)|HWDE}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:GiantsKnife.jpg|thumb|249x249px|The Giant's Knife being obtained in {{OoT|-}}]]
In order to obtain the Giant's Knife in {{OoT}}, [[Link]] must open [[Medigoron]]'s shop with his [[Bomb]]s as a child. When spoken to as a child, Medigoron tells the young hero that he is working on "something really cool," but that it will take five to six years to complete.<ref>{{Cite|I'm working on something really cool right now! But I think it's going to take a while... If you can wait five or six years, it should be ready. OK?|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref> When Link returns as an adult, the Giant's Knife will be finished,<ref>{{Cite|I just completed a small weapon. How about it...|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref> and it may be purchased, but it is very expensive - at the cost of 200 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|How about buying this knife for 200 Rupees?|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref>
[[File:OoT Giant's Knife breaking.png|thumb|258x258px|The Giant's Knife easily breaking apart as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
The Giant's Knife is a very large sword that looks identical to the [[Biggoron's Sword]] and shares the same raw power with double the strength of the [[Master Sword]]. However, due to the horrifically slow and cheap construction by Medigoron, the blade is extremely fragile in complete contrast to the Biggoron's Sword, and will entirely break off after just 8 hits or immediately if Link strikes a solid wall,<ref>{{Cite|It looks like there is a problem with its durability... But still...|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref> turning it into the "'''Broken Giant's Knife'''". Unlike a broken [[Deku Stick]], however, the Broken Giant's Knife can still be used in combat, but its strength is severely reduced to only 1/4 its normal amount, consequently the same as the [[Kokiri Sword]], and its reach will be drastically shorter. While wielding the Broken Giant's Knife, the [[Spin Attack]] cannot be used. The Giant's Knife can be replaced but will cost another 200 Rupees with the same problems.

The Giant's Knife can only be used by Link as an adult and must be held with two hands, thereby precluding the use of a [[Shield]], even when broken.

With all of the aforementioned downsides, although powerful, the Giant's Knife is one of the most unreliable weapons in {{TLoZ|-}} series.

==Trivia==
*The Giant's Knife is tied with the [[Biggoron's Sword]] as the strongest weapon in {{OoT}}, though its durability is at a major disadvantage in comparison. Because of this, the rare [[Sword]] is mostly forgotten by fans of the ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' community.
*Strangely, if [[Link]] were to avoid ever obtaining a Giant's Knife and then were to instead skip to getting the Biggoron's Sword, [[Medigoron]] will still talk to Link as if he has a working, non-broken Giant's Knife even though it is in fact the Biggoron's Sword. This is likely due to the fact both weapons share the same model.[[File:OoT3D Broken Goron's Sword Model.png|thumb|141x141px|The Broken Goron's Sword, which appears like a shattered Giant's Knife, as seen in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'']]
*Although the [[Broken Goron's Sword]] looks identical to a broken Giant's Knife and likewise have a similar name, this item cannot be used for combat, as it is meant for trading to [[Biggoron]] for himself to obtain the [[Prescription]] during the [[Trading Quest]]. It is actually a broken ''Biggoron's Sword''.
*Considering most real-world swords in the medieval era take about 2 weeks on average to make (5 hours to a day today), while the Giant's Knife took a staggeringly long 5 or 6 years, it is quite clear Medigoron is not very skilled as a blacksmith, as explained by his older brother.
*The Giant's Knife able to have its blade broken off in {{OoT|-}} was met with shock among fans at the time, due to {{TLoZ|-}} series itself being primarily associated with swords (as they are Link's main weapon alongside the [[Medieval elements in The Legend of Zelda series|medieval theme]]) and had never occurred in any media within the franchise prior to the game's release.
*{{OoT|-}} contains unused text for the Giant's Knife, meant to be displayed when it is exchanged for the Biggoron's Sword, provided that Link possesses the Giant's Knife. The final version makes no mention of the replacement, and instead the Giant's Knife text is simply discarded.<ref>{{Cite|You traded the Giant's Knife for the Biggoron's Sword! This blade was forged by a master smith and won't break!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> This text is used in {{OoT3D}}, however.
*The Giant's Knife, alongside with the [[Deku Stick]], are the first items in the series that will break or wear down after regular use. It is succeeded by the [[Razor Sword]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|{{MM|-}}]], and the items Link can obtain in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.
*As several unused animations of Link wielding the Biggoron's Sword exist in {{OoT|-}}, it is likely the Giant's Knife shares them.
*The Razor Sword in {{MM|-}} shares the same [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_GiantsKnife_Break.wav sound effect] as the Giant's Knife breaking when the blade becomes dull after one hundred strikes.
*The [[Decayed Master Sword]] in ''Tears of the Kingdom'' is very similar to the broken Giant's Knife in that the sacred weapon indeed has its blade broken off by the [[Gloom]], and consequently had bare minimum damage of 1 and drastically reduced reach.
*The French localization refers the Giant's Knife as '''Blade of the Giants'''.
