'''Castle''' may refer to anyone of these structures found in ''[[The Legend of  Zelda (Series)| The Legend of Zelda series]]'':
*'''[[Ancient Castle of Ikana]]''', located deep in [[Ikana Canyon]], and is home to the long dead king of the  Ikana kingdom, [[Igos du Ikana|Igos du Ikana]].
*'''[[Ganon's Castle]]''', the final dungeon in {{OoT}}.
*'''[[Hyrule Castle]]''', the home of the royal family of [[Hyrule]], usually including [[Princess Zelda]] and the normally unseen [[King of Hyrule|King]] of [[Hyrule]].
**'''[[Dark Hyrule Castle]]''', the dark version of Hyrule Castle created by [[Vaati]] in {{TMC}}.
*'''[[Kanalet Castle]]''', located in the center of [[Koholint Island]] in {{LA}}.
*'''[[North Castle]]''', the area in {{TAoL}} where the game begins.
*'''[[Onox's Castle]]''', located in the middle left hand side of [[Holodrum]], and is the lair of the final boss of {{OoS}}.
*'''[[Agitha's Castle]]''', home to the princess [[Agitha]] in {{TP}}.
*'''[[Castle]]''' a location in {{FPTRR}}.
