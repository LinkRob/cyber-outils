{{Term/Store|Inga||Series, OoS}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref> also known as '''Mrs. Ruul''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref> is a character in {{OoS}}.

==Biography==
Inga lives at her villa in the [[Holodrum Plain]]. She trades [[Link]] her [[Iron Pot]] for the [[Ghastly Doll]]. Her villa can be accessed when Link gets [[Ricky]], [[Dimitri]], or [[Moosh]] as a partner. When Inga is first met, she complains that it is so hot that even her {{Term|OoS|Soothing Pool|link}} cannot cool her down.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, it's so hot! Even sitting by my prized Soothing Pool doesn't help! I need something creepy to send chills down my spine...|Inga|OoS}}</ref> Link can give her the Ghastly Doll, which she remarks is so ghastly that it gives her the chills.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! That doll! It's so ghastly it gives me chills!|Inga|OoS}}</ref> She is the wife of [[Mayor Ruul]].
