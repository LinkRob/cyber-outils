{{Term/Store|Fairy Fountain: Desert Sands||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fairy Fountain: Desert Sands|N/A|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
This side quest is considered the last of the Fairy Fountain quests, after [[Fairy Fountain: Grassy Plains]] and [[Fairy Fountain: Deep Snows]], although the side quests can be completed in any order.

This side quest has a time limit of 13:00 minutes. It first starts with the shadowed figure asking the character to bring 600 [[Rupee|rupees]] by getting them from the [[Yiga|Yiga Clan]].

The [[Yiga Footsoldier|Yiga Footsoldiers]] will only drop a Green Rupee(worth 1 rupee), while the [[Yiga Blademaster|Yiga Blademasters]] drop a Purple Rupee(worth 50 rupees). After this, a [[Blue Moblin]] must be defeated in order to open a gate, and then a [[Stone Talus]] to make more Yiga spawn.

Once the targeted position is reached, the shadowed figure takes all the rupees collected, and will reveal herself as the Great Fairy [[Tera]]. Once defeated, if this was the character's first try, then he/she will get 1 [[Ethereal Stone]], 3 [[Fairy|Fairies]], 10 [[Raw Whole Bird]], and 500 rupees.

If all of the Fairy Fountain quests are completed, then the character will be able to do [[Fairy Fountain: Battlefield]]
