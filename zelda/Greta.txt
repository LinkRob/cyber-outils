{{Term/Store|Greta||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Greta|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Greta}} is the proprietor of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club|link}}, located in the building behind {{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} initially tries to enter the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club}}, {{Term|BotW|Greta}} will speak from behind the door and ask for the password.<ref>{{Cite|...What's the password?|Greta|BotW}}</ref> If Link guesses the password wrong, he will not be able to try again until the next day.<ref>{{Cite|If you get our password wrong, you have to wait until the next day to try again.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> Greta will then tell Link to go drink milk at {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}} in an attempt to get him to go away.<ref>{{Cite|Go drink milk in the bar or something!|Greta|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Secret Club's Secret|link}}".

Once Link provides the correct password to Greta, he is allowed entry to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club}}.<ref>{{Cite|All right... You can come in.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> Upon entering the Secret Club, {{Term|BotW|Greta}} notices that she has not seen Link before.<ref>{{Cite|Hey... I haven't seen your face around here before...|Greta|BotW}}</ref> She comments that Link is a rare visitor and promises not to ask any questions if he does the same.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Heh heh heh... Looks like we've got ourselves a rare visitor. I guess I have a suspicious mind... So maybe you don't ask too many questions, and I won't either.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> Greta then accepts Link as a member to the Club and the Side Quest is completed.<ref>{{Cite|You're a member here now, so thanks for your patronage.|Greta|BotW}}</ref>

If Link speaks to Greta, she comments that it is always "good evening" in the Gerudo Secret Club.<ref>{{Cite|Elsewhere it might be sav'otta or sav'aaq, but here it's always sav'saaba.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> If Link asks what the place is, she explains that it is the Gerudo Secret Club, or "GSC" for short.<ref>{{Cite|This is the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Secret Club}}, or {{Color|BotW Blue|GSC}} for short.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> She sells the {{Term|BotW|Desert Voe Set|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set|link}} and tells Link that it is the only {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}} in Gerudo Town that sells men's clothing.<ref>{{Cite|This is the only shop in Gerudo Town where someone could buy clothes meant for a voe to wear.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> While it is illegal to sell men's clothing in Gerudo Town, there is a high demand for it that {{Term|BotW|Greta}} recognizes.<ref>{{Cite|Despite it being illegal to sell voe clothing in this town, there is a high demand for such things.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> Greta comments that she has never seen a man disguise himself as a woman like Link has.<ref>{{Cite|I've never seen a voe disguise himself as a vai like you have, though...|Greta|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reacts with shock, she mentions that they both have something to hide and suggests they move on to business.<ref>{{Cite|We each have something to hide, I suppose. Let's ignore that and move on to business.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} is done speaking with her, {{Term|BotW|Greta}} assures him that his secret is safe with her.<ref>{{Cite|Haha, don't worry... Your secret is safe with me.|Greta|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Greta}} admits to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the sunlight does not agree with her, as she has not been out in the day for years.<ref>{{Cite|The bright light of the sun doesn't agree with me. I haven't paid my respects to it for many years...|Greta|BotW}}</ref> If Link ends the conversation, Greta asks if he has ever noticed how beautiful light is in the dark.<ref>{{Cite|Have you ever noticed how the darker it is, the more beautiful the light becomes? Isn't it great, young voe? Er, young vai?|Greta|BotW}}</ref> When examining the Radiant Set, Greta will tell Link that it is made using {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh ho. That's a special one, made using {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stone}}.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> She then informs Link that he must also trade her Luminous Stones when buying the Set, in addition to a handling fee.<ref>{{Cite|If you're interested, you'll need {{Color|BotW Red|to trade me the materials}}. Oh, and there's a handling fee, too.|Greta|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks to sell his {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Greta}}, she agrees, though she will refuse to pay more than another trader would.<ref>{{Cite|I suppose I could. But I won't be paying more than any other trader would.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> However, she is still interested in finding something rare, should {{Term|BotW|Link}} have any.<ref>{{Cite|Though I'd love to pick up something rare, if you've got anything like that...|Greta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'd be happier if it were something more uncommon...|Greta|BotW}}</ref> She has not done much traveling, so she appreciates being sold rare goods.<ref>{{Cite|I haven't traveled very much, so you'd really help me out by selling me a lot of stuff.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Greta}} appreciates a good deal,<ref>{{Cite|Pretty good deal, heehee. Sarqso.|Greta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Heehee, first-class deal. Sarqso.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> and she is glad to purchase unique {{Plural|BotW|Item}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Heehee, if it's OK with you, I'd gladly take something off your hands.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> She may occassionally mention how she wanted the product that {{Term|BotW|Link}} just sold to her.<ref>{{Cite|I was just thinking about how I wanted one of these. Sarqso.|Greta|BotW}}</ref>
