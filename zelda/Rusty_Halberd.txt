{{Plural|BotW|Rusty Halberd}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}, particularly around ruins. They were potentially used by {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}}, before they deteriorated due to exposure to the elements.<ref name="Inventory"/> Because of their rusted nature, they have a very low durability.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Rock Octorok|link}} will inhale {{Plural|BotW|Rusty Halberd}} that are placed in front of them, spitting out a cleaned counterpart.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Item}} has a 50% chance to be a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Spear|link}}, a 35% chance to be a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Spear|link}}, a 10% chance to be a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Halberd|link}}, and a 5% chance to be a {{Term|BotW|Royal Halberd|link}}.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Rusty Halberd}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. However, they are unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Rusty Halberd}} can be manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Rusty Halberd}} are part of the archetype of equipment known as {{Term|BotW|Rusty Gear|link}}, which is a group of deteriorated {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} with low durability.<ref name="CaC"/>
