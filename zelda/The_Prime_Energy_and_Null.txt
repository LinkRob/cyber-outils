"'''The Prime Energy and Null'''" is a [[Main Quest]] in {{EoW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Prime Energy and Null}}<br/>Hyrule Castle|[[Adventure Log]]|{{EoW}}}}</ref>

==Overview==

===Objectives===
{{Quest Objective|Start}}
{{Quest Objective|Entry|1|The Eternal Forest watched over by the sacred tree contains the path to the Prime Energy. The forest is northwest of Hyrule Castle. Head there to find the hero and stop Null.}}
{{Quest Objective|Entry|2|The following message was inscribed on the stone monument in the Eternal Forest: "The path will open when the six pedestals are crowned. To crown each pedestal, demonstrate the power of echoes, and prove your wisdom.}}
{{Quest Objective|Entry|3|A path leading deeper into the Eternal Forest opened after placing ancient orbs on the six pedestals. General Wright and his soldiers parted ways with Zelda, returning to Hyrule Castle.}}
{{Quest Objective|Entry|4|You met the sacred Deku Tree deep within the Eternal Forest. The echo of Zelda deceived the Deku Tree and is heading toward the Prime Energy.}}
{{Quest Objective|Entry|5|The Prime Energy split into three—one piece went to Zelda, and another went to the echo of Zelda. It seems the Zelda echo has an idea about where the last piece may be.}}
{{Quest Objective|Entry|6|The echo of Zelda escaped into the darkness beyond. She still holds the last part of the Prime Energy.}}
{{Quest Objective|Entry|7|After returning all of the weapons of might back to Link, he opened up a path forward. Work with Link to retrieve the piece of the Prime Energy from Null.}}
{{Quest Objective|End}}
