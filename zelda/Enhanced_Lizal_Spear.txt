{{Compendium/Extract|Enhanced Lizal Spear}}
{{Plural|BotW|Lizal Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} for both fishing and combat.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can commonly be found on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} and in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, where they are often wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Lizalfos|link}}. They can also be found in the hands of {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Spear|display=Spears}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Because of their metallic spearheads, {{Plural|BotW|Enhanced Lizal Spear}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up again using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. In addition, they will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
