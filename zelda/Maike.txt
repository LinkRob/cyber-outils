{{Term/Store|Maike||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Maike|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Maike}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} merchant who runs a {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}. She sells {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Maike}} during the day, she will attempt to say "good morning" in the {{Term|BotW|Hylian (Language)|link}} language, and she explains that the appropriate word in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)|link}} is ''sav'otta''.<ref>{{Cite|Good...morning... Heh, that's how you say it where you're from, isn't it? Here, we say "sav'otta."|Maike|BotW}}</ref> At night, she will try to say "good evening" in {{Term|BotW|Hylian (Language)}}, and she likewise explains that the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}} say ''sav'sabba'' instead.<ref>{{Cite|Good...evening... Heh, that's how you say it where you're from, isn't it? Here, we say "sav'sabba."|Maike|BotW}}</ref> She also incorrectly says "welcome" as "well come" and explains that they say ''vasaaq'' in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well...come... Did I get that right? Isn't that how you say it where you're from? Here, we say "vasaaq."|Maike|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Maike}} reveals that her younger sister, {{Term|BotW|Estan|link}}, runs a butcher shop {{Term|BotW|Stall|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, and that he should visit her since she knows {{Term|BotW|Hylian (Language)}} well.<ref name="Sister"/> However, {{Term|BotW|Maike}} quickly realizes that as a man, {{Term|BotW|Link}} would not be allowed to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to do so.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, of course. You're a voe, so you won't even be able to get past the front gates...|Maike|BotW}}</ref>

During the {{Term|BotW|Forbidden City Entry|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Maike}} if she has seen anyone suspicious in {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}}.<ref>{{Cite|You want to know if I've seen a suspicious-looking male merchant?|Maike|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Maike}} will be unable to recall someone of that description, and since she spends all her time at her {{Term|BotW|General Store}}, she is unsure how she would be able to spot a {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Suspicious, huh... Hmmmm... Ahh, I dunno. It's hard to say. I spend all my time right here, and I don't know how I'd tell if someone was a merchant...|Maike|BotW}}</ref> She suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} ask one of the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Merchant}} who are camping at {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}} instead.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, there are Hylian merchants here, too. Why don't you go ask one of them?|Maike|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} walks near her wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} she will remark that the outfit suits him.<ref>{{Cite|Those clothes suit you.|Maike|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her, she will again tell him about {{Term|BotW|Estan}}.<ref>{{Cite|My kid sister runs a butcher shop in {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}. She's traveled all over, so she knows your language pretty well.|Maike|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Maike}} calls {{Term|BotW|Estan}} cute, and she encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to say hello to her.<ref>{{Cite|She's pretty cute too, so if you stop by {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}, you should say hello to her, at least.|Maike|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys {{Term|BotW|Maike}}'s wares, she will attempt to thank him in a way that would be familiar to {{Plural|BotW|Hylian}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank...you! Sarqso!|Maike|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks...boss... Hehe, that's how they say it where you're from, right?|Maike|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her after buying all of her stock, {{Term|BotW|Maike}} will thank him for shopping.<ref>{{Cite|Thank...you...for...shopping... That's how they say it where you're from, right?|Maike|BotW}}</ref> Like other proprietors of {{Plural|BotW|General Store}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can sell his own {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Maike}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, sure thing. So what do you want to sell me?|Maike|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll buy as much as you've got!|Maike|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If there's anything you want to sell, don't hesitate to talk to me!|Maike|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} does so, she will be appreciative of whatever she buys from him.<ref>{{Cite|Sarqso. Good, good.|Maike|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Nice, nice. Sarqso.|Maike|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sarqso! Is that all?|Maike|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, she invites him to come back in the future.<ref>{{Cite|Come back anytime!|Maike|BotW}}</ref>
