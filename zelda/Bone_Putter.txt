{{Term/Store|Bone Putter|Bone Putters|Series, LADX|plural}} are enemies in {{LA|DX}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|LANS|Bone Putter}} are found exclusively in the bonus {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon|link}}, where they appear in rooms with the colored-platforms. {{Plural|LANS|Bone Putter}} come in two varieties: winged and wingless. Wingless {{Plural|LANS|Bone Putter}} are harmless and will not attack {{Term|LANS|Link|link}}, as they will just jump around and even sometimes fall to their death as they make the colored-platforms disappear. The winged versions will sometimes accompany them, dropping {{Plural|LANS|Bomb|link}} on various places. {{Plural|LANS|Bone Putter}} can easily be defeated with the use of a {{Term|LANS|Sword|link}}.
