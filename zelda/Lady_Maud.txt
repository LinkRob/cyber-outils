== Physical Description ==
Lady Maud is a slender, middle-aged woman with fair skin, short blonde hair, and frown lines around her mouth.

She has a light purple tight dress with a pink striped print, wears large sunglasses that cover her eyes, a white hat with a pink ribbon, a gorget with ribbons around the neck, and white boots.

== Personality ==
Lady Maud has a narcissistic, selfish, and vain personality, because she believes that only her criteria on fashion is valid and that the opinion of others is incorrect. She also has an envious and sadistic side, which led her to curse [[Princess Styla]]. Lady Maud also shows an irate personality, since she would get angry and throw tantrums when her plans did not go as she wanted.

== Abilities ==
Lady Maud is a very powerful human witch, her [[magic]] is mainly based on sewing as she is able to create embroidery of monsters and give them life and powers with her magic, she can also use her parasol as if it were a magic wand and shoot beams of light and electric rays from it.

== Game Mode ==
{{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} is fought as the [[Final Boss]] in the {{Term|TFH|Sky Temple|link}}. Prior to the start of the fight, she will rid the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} of their {{Plural|TFH|Item|link}} picked up from the {{Term|TFH|Level}}.<ref name= "Items"/>

During the first phase, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} will stand in the middle of a raised altar while a magical floor barrier circles around her. To pass the barrier, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must form a {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}} and throw each other over. However as soon as the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are on the other side of the barrier, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} will retaliate by throwing parasols at them. One {{Term|TFH|Links}} must then be thrown onto {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}}'s platform; however, she will push him away with her parasol. The thrown {{Term|TFH|Links}} must be quick to attack her with the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} before she can push him back. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can also wait until she is distracted while throwing parasols and throw one {{Term|TFH|Links}} behind her. Eventually, after taking enough damage, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} will begin to throw a fit as her platform lowers and the barrier dissipates. She will then charge after the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} while waving her parasol wildly. One {{Term|TFH|Links}} can lure her away while the other {{Plural|TFH|Links}} attack from behind. After several more hits, she will disappear and begin the next phase.

In the second phase, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} will transform into a new, more powerful outfit. The platform beneath her will drop out, forming a hole in the center of the battle arena, and she will float in the air and send orbs of electrical energy at the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}. The color of the orb will match the tunic of the {{Term|TFH|Links}} that it targets. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} play a form of {{Term|Series|Dead Man's Volley|link}}, where after hitting the orb, it will turn yellow and fly back towards {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}}, resulting in the orb being volleyed between her and the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} at increasing speeds. If the orb is not struck swiftly enough by the targeted {{Term|TFH|Links}}, it will bounce upward and change color to that of another {{Term|TFH|Link}}, who must then be the one to return the volley. If the {{Plural|TFH|Link}} miss the orb altogether, it will land on the ground and shock them, causing damage. Once the orb has been volleyed enough times, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} will catch the orb, spin around, and throw it back in a much larger and faster form. If this final orb is returned to {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}}, it will shock her and knock her back onto the ground. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can then strike her with their {{Plural|TFH|Sword}} until she recovers. After enough damage, the third phase will start.

In the third phase, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} lowers herself down the pit she created in the second phase. She then rises wearing an elaborate dress that greatly increases her height. She will then summon colored, open parasols that slowly float around her. She will use these parasols to throw at the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}. However, when not in use, these parasols double as platforms. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can form a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} to reach the floating parasols, however, only the {{Term|TFH|Links}} whose tunic matches the parasol's color will be able to land on it. (Any {{Term|TFH|Links}} can stand on a white parasol.) If the {{Term|TFH|Links}} stands too long on a parasol, it will close beneath him. In order to attack {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}}, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must form a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} of three and have either the bottom {{Term|TFH|Links}} throw the middle {{Term|TFH|Links}} onto a large parasol, or the middle {{Term|TFH|Links}} throw the top {{Term|TFH|Links}} onto a smaller, higher parasol. The top {{Term|TFH|Links}} can then strike {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} with his {{Term|TFH|Sword}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must also be careful of electricity striking the parasols. Eventually, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} will open the wings of her outfit as she throws the parasols to the floor like darts, which will shock the area that they land on. The parasol platforms will eventually reappear.

After several more hits, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} will elevate the platform beneath her, making her even taller than before. The rotating parasols will periodically lower to the ground before returning to {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}}. This allows the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to ride the parasols without having to throw each other. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can form a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} of three and have the bottom {{Term|TFH|Links}} step onto a parasol that matches. With their combined height, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can strike {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|display=The Lady}} and finally defeat her.

==Nomenclature==
{{Wp|Maud (given name)|Maud}} is an Old German feminine given name, which means "powerful battler". Maud is also the name given to grey plaids once worn by shepherds in Scotland. It also has a similar pronunciation to {{Wp|Mod (subculture)|Mod}}, a 1960's British subculture and style of fashion of which Lady Maud's apparel resembles, as well as the word mode, which means "fashion, style."
