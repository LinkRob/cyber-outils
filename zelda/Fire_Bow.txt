'''Fire Bow''' is a special move appearing only in the {{SSB|Series}}. It first appears in {{SSBM}}.

==Overview==
Fire Bow is the neutral special move of [[Young Link]] in both {{SSBM}} and {{SSBU}} and one of the custom variants of [[Toon Link]]'s [[Bow]] move in {{SSB4}}. When used, Young Link or Toon Link shoots a [[Fire Arrow]] from their Bow. Using Fire Bow does not inflict as much damage as [[Link]]'s [[Bow]], nor does it travel as far, but it does burn the enemy. If the player holds the B button, they can charge the Fire Bow and relatively control how far it shoots a Fire Arrow.

If [[Kirby]] inhales Young Link, he can use the ability.
