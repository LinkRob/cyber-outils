==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Plural|TLoZ|Red Darknut}} are weaker than {{Plural|TLoZ|Blue Darknut|link}} in {{TLoZ}}. They are able to shield themselves from any attack and are thus invulnerable if attacked from the front.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= The knight who lives in the labyrinths. He has lots of attacking power. He repels Link's attacks from the front with his shield.|game= TLoZ|page= 36}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} can harm them by attacking them from the side or behind with either his {{Term|TLoZ|Sword|link}} or a {{Term|TLoZ|Bomb|link}}. {{Plural|TLoZ|Red Darknut}} cannot be stunned by the {{Term|TLoZ|Boomerang|link}} and they cannot be harmed by the {{Term|TLoZ|Magical Rod|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Arrow|link}}, or {{Term|TLoZ|Fire|link}}. They have a health of four. When defeated, {{Plural|TLoZ|Red Darknut}} may drop either a {{Term|TLoZ|Heart|link}}, one {{Term|TLoZ|Rupee|link}}, four {{Plural|TLoZ|Bomb}}, or a {{Term|TLoZ|Clock|link}}.{{Note|If {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} defeats 10 enemies without getting hit or teleporting with the {{Term|TLoZ|Recorder|link}}, then the 10th enemy defeated will drop five {{Plural|TLoZ|Rupee|link}}. However, if the 10th enemy is defeated with a {{Term|TLoZ|Bomb|link}}, then it will drop four {{Plural|TLoZ|Bomb}}. If {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} defeats 16 enemies without getting hit or teleporting, then the 16th enemy will drop a {{Term|TLoZ|Fairy|link}}. After the 16th enemy, {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} must get hit and reset the counter to zero in order to achieve this effect again. Defeating {{Plural|TLoZ|Armos|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Like Like|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Keese|link}}, or {{Plural|TLoZ|Gel|link}} will not be counted.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= n.a. |published= n.d. |retrieved= August 17, 2017 |url= http://redcandle.us/The_Legend_of_Zelda#Forced_drops |title= Forced Drops |site= redcandle.us}}</ref>}}

{{Plural|OoS|Red Darknut}} appear in {{OoS}} with a similar appearance and behavior to {{Plural|LANS|Darknut|link}} from {{LA}}, attacking by throwing spears or charging at {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} with their sword.

{{Stub|OoA}}

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Red Dark Nut}}
{{Plural|TMC|Red Darknut}} are commander-rank {{Plural|TMC|Darknut|link}} who possess greater strength and endurance than the standard variant in {{TMC}},<ref name="TMC Figurine"/> though they have the same attack patterns. A {{Term|TMC|Red Darknut}} appears as the {{Term|TMC|Sub-Boss|link}} of the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}. Two {{Plural|TMC|Red Darknut}} later appear as enemies in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} defeats the {{Term|TMC|Black Knight|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
Red Darknuts make their first 3D appearance in [[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|''Twilight Princess'']], being fought the same way as regular Darknuts. They are found in the [[Cave of Ordeals]]'s 48th and 49th floors, alongside 2 [[Aeralfos]] in the former and 1 Darknut and a [[Green Darknut]] in the latter.

==Other Appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
[[File:CoH Red Darknut Sprite.png|left]]
{{Plural|CoH|Red Darknut}} appear as {{Plural|CoH|Sub-Boss|link}} in {{CoH}} where they are the second strongest variety of {{Term|CoH|Darknut|link}}. Although their movement is limited to the beat of the music, they can move on every single beat, rendering them faster than most enemies and {{Plural|CoH|Sub-Boss}}. They also carry a shield that protects them from frontal attacks and can push the heroes backwards as they move, making them dangerous in areas where they can pin the heroes against walls or push them into holes and other hazards. Every few beats, they will lower their shield which will temporarily expose them to attack. When their shield is down, they will only swing their sword if the hero is either standing still or tries to run to their sides.

When the opportunity to strike the heroes arises, they will hold out their sword for one beat and 5 warning tiles will flash in a horseshoe formation before they swing their sword on the second beat, dealing damage to anything within 3 spaces in front of them and anything that was already standing at their sides.

Equipping the {{Term|CoH|Ring of Piercing|link}}, the {{Term|CoH|Great Bow|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Fragarach|link}} will enable the heroes to attack them through their shields.
{{Ambig|End}}
