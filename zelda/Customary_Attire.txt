The '''Customary Attire''' is an [[Outfit]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
[[Princess Zelda]]'s iconic attire, it's her usual outfit when there's no need for her to wear anything else.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Customary Attire|A beautiful and elegant dress fit for a princess. Wearing it makes you feel regal.<ref name="Customary Attire">{{Cite|A beautiful and elegant dress fit for a princess. Wearing it makes you feel regal.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}

Zelda wears this Outfit when she is kidnapped by [[Echo Ganon|a strange blue demon]], and after she returns home. She is forced to discard the Outfit and assume a [[Disguise]] when [[Dark Echo|impostors]] of [[King of Hyrule#Echoes of Wisdom|her father]], [[General Wright]], and [[Minister Lefte]] frame her as the culprit behind the [[Rift]]s.

After the impostors are defeated, Zelda regains the Outfit alongside the [[Royal Travel Attire]], though she does keep the Disguise as well.

==Trivia==
*[[Tekom]] will always assume this appearance even if Zelda isn't using the Outfit when [[Null]] ambushes her.
