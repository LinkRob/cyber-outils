==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Super Smash Sisters}}" is an event wherein a greater number of heroine-themed {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Heroines are taking their place in the spotlight on the Spirit Board! Defeat them in battle to earn more gold than usual.<br/><br/>Also, for this time only, the new spirit Peachette will make an appearance. You don't want to miss out on that! |author= Nintendo |published= May 29, 2019 |retrieved= June 2, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/0e061fe4/index.html |title= Event: Super Smash Sisters - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
