{{Term/Store|Vegetable Omelet|Vegetable Omelets|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Vegetable Omelet}}|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Vegetable Omelets by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Bird Egg|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}}, and any vegetable, herb, or flower together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made either by using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb|link}}, which have no status effects, or by using ingredients with contradicting effects. Eating them will heal some of Link's {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The use of eggs and chopped vegetables add to the nutritional balance.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Several different variations of Vegetable Omelets can be made, depending on the type of vegetable, herb, or flower used:

* '''Hearty Vegetable Omelet''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. By eating them, all of Link's Hearts will be healed and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Vegetable Omelet''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using energizing ingredients. They can be eaten to partially refill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Vegetable Omelet''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}} or other enduring ingredients. When eaten, they'll completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Vegetable Omelet''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}}, or other hasty ingredients. These omelets will provide Link with a movement speed when he eats them.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Vegetable Omelet''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}} or other spicy ingredients. Consuming them will grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Vegetable Omelet''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}} or other electric ingredients. If eaten, they'll give Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Vegetable Omelet''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}} or other mighty ingredients. Eating these omelets will provide Link with a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Vegetable Omelet''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}}, or other tough ingredients. They will grant Link a boost to his defense when he eats them.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Vegetable Omelet''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. By eating them, Link will receive a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Vegetable Omelet''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Vegetable Omelet}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This home-style dish mixes fluffy eggs with chopped vegetables for nutritional balance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}} or other spicy ingredients. They can be consumed to grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Chilly"/>
