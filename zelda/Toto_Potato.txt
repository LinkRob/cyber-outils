The '''Toto Potato''' is a plant in {{OoA|Himekawa}}.{{Name Ref|OoA (Himekawa)}}

==Location and Uses==
When {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link|link}} is brought to the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Hidden Village}} by {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Sir Raven|link}}, he recognizes the {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Toto Potato}} among the crops.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Oh! Toto Potatoes! I've seen them before! |book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= VIZ Media |page= 78}}</ref> {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Sir Raven}} tells {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} the {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Toto Potato|display=Potatoes}} originally did not grow in {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Labrynna|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= They didn't originally grow here. |book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= VIZ Media |page= 78}}</ref> He had brought them from {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Hyrule|link}} in a seed tuber.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I BROUGHT A SEED TUBER FROM HYRULE AND PLANTED THEM HERE. |book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= VIZ Media |page= 78}}</ref> He states that it has taken three years for the {{Plural|OoA (Himekawa)|Toto Potato|display=Potatoes}} to grow.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= IT'S TAKEN THREE YEARS... |book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}} |publisher= VIZ Media |page= 78}}</ref>
