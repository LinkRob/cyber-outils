'''Lenzo's Research Assistant''' is a sidequest in {{TWW}}, and the precursor to the {{Term|TWWHD|Nintendo Gallery|link}} sidequest (also found in this game). It starts in {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}: When {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo|link}} finds {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} in his house for the first time, he doesn't show any trust in him, since his {{Term|TWWHD|Picto Box|link}} was stolen by a thief who had escaped the jail previously; but when {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} retrieves it, {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo}} not only thanks him, but also lets him keep the {{Term|TWWHD|Picto Box}} and, additionally, shows him his repertoire of pictographs. In a later encounter, {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo}} has {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} complete certain objectives. This sidequest ultimately leads to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} obtaining the {{Term|TWWHD|Deluxe Picto Box|link}}.

==Event Procedure==
===Exercise One – A Love Letter===
[[File:TWW Garrickson Figurine Model.png|85px|left]]
{{Term|TWWHD|Link}}s' first task revolves around a man who sends love letters to an unknown person, only to find that none of his feelings are reciprocated. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} stakes out the {{Term|TWWHD|Postbox|link}}, remains out of sight, and waits for {{Term|TWWHD|Garrickson|link}} to deliver his love letter. He then takes the pictograph and brings it to {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo}} for the next challenge.
{{Clear}}

===Exercise Two – The Face of Fear===
[[File:TWW Gossack Figurine Model.png|80px|left]]
{{Term|TWWHD|Link}}s' second task revolves around a man who fails to face his own fears. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} must scare {{Term|TWWHD|Gossack|link}}, the man sitting at the table at the {{Term|TWWHD|Cafe Bar|link}} who is afraid of earthquakes, by hitting a wall with a {{Term|TWWHD|Roll Attack|link}} or knocking dishes off of the table, and snap a picture while the man is trembling. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can then bring the pictograph to {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo}} for the next challenge.
{{Clear}}

===Exercise Three – The Secret Couple===
[[File:TWW Anton Figurine Model.png|left|80px]]
[[File:TWW Linda Figurine Model.png|right|40px]]
{{Term|TWWHD|Link}}s' third and final task revolves about a gentleman and a lady: Both are in love with each other, yet are unaware that their feelings are requited. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} waits for {{Term|TWWHD|Anton|link}}, the man strolling through town, to look at {{Term|TWWHD|Linda|link}} as he's passing by, the woman in the orange dress near the {{Term|TWWHD|School of Joy|link}}. He snaps a picture showing them together and brings the picture to {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo}}. The reward from {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo}} is 20 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}}, but if {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} chooses to help the couple further, they will reward him with a {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart|link}}.
{{Clear}}

===Final Ingredient===
[[File:TWWHD Forest Firefly Artwork.png|x70px|left]]
[[File:TWWHD Deluxe Picto Box Artwork.png|70px|right]]
{{Term|TWW|Lenzo}}'s last task for {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} is to bring him a {{Term|TWW|Forest Firefly|link}} from {{Term|TWW|Forest Haven|link}}. The luminiscent insect can be found a few meters away from {{Term|TWW|Hollo's Forest Potion Shop|link}}, but {{Term|TWW|Link}} will need an empty {{Term|TWW|Bottle|link}} to take it with him.

In {{TWWHD}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo}} does not need the {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Firefly}} to create the {{Term|TWWHD|Deluxe Picto Box}}, and will accept {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} as his apprentice after the third exercise. He will not mention anything about the {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Firefly}}, but will accept the Firefly if {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} shows him one, giving him a {{Term|TWWHD|Joy Pendant|link}} in return.
{{Clear}}

==Aftermath==
The reward for all of this trouble is the {{Term|TWWHD|Deluxe Picto Box}}, capable of taking Pictographs with color, unlocking the {{Term|TWWHD|Color Pictography Quests|link}} and the {{Term|TWWHD|Nintendo Gallery|link}} sidequests. Additionally, after {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} clears the {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}}, he can purchase ''Legendary Pictographs'' from {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo}}, so that they can be turned into figurines, as part of the {{Term|TWWHD|Nintendo Gallery}} completion.
