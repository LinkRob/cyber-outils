The {{Term/Store|Receptionist||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The young lady serves as [[Mayor Dotour]]'s secretary at his [[Mayor's Official Residence|residence]]. She is responsible for directing people either to the Mayor's office or [[Madame Aroma|his wife]]'s drawing room. She will ask [[Link]] if he has any business with the Mayor or his wife,<ref>{{Cite|Welcome. Do you have some business?|Receptionist|MM}}</ref> and gives him directions to both of them when Link speaks to her. She explains that the Mayor is in the middle of a meeting (or that it just ended if Link has helped the Mayor do so).<ref>{{Cite|The room on the left is the Mayor's room... It sounds like they're having some kind of meeting, I think.|Receptionist|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The room on your left is the Mayor's Office... It sounds like their... whatchamacallit... meeting ended. A lot of tough guys walked out.|Receptionist|MM}}</ref> She will also warn Link not to enter Madame Aroma's drawing room without an appointment else she will get mad.<ref>{{Cite|The room on your right is the drawing room and it's also Madame Aroma's office. Don't go in there without an appointment. The lady will get mad at you.|Receptionist|MM}}</ref> If Link speaks to her a second time, she will ask if he wants to hear her directions again.<ref>{{Cite|...My directions... Do you want to hear them again?|Receptionist|MM}}</ref> Apparently quite a few people come to her asking about [[Kafei]]. She seems to think that the Mayor's son intentionally broke his engagement with [[Anju]] and that people should let him do what he wants.<ref>{{Cite|Ohh, how many people are gonna ask me that? That lady is so persistent. Soooo, doesn't Kafei want to break off his engagement to Anju? Why not let him? That's what he wants... Some people...|Receptionist|MM}}</ref> During the [[Final Day]], she will flee from [[Clock Town]] like most of the residents.

==Trivia==
*The only Mask she will comment on is [[Kafei's Mask]].
*The Receptionist is one of the few characters in {{MM|-}} that does not have a counterpart in {{OoT}}.
