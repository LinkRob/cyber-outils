In {{EoW}} the '''Cryptic Cavern''' is a mini-dungeon located in the [[Gerudo Desert]], serving as a precursor to the [[Gerudo Sanctum|Gerudo Sanctum Dungeon]]. Access is granted after obtaining permission from the Gerudo Town chief, which becomes possible upon completing the “Stilled Desert Temple Ruins Rift” during the "A Rift in the Gerudo Desert" quest. 

Inside the cavern, [[Link]] encounter various puzzles and [[enemies]]. Notably, players can obtain the Wind Cannon Echo within the cavern, essential for progressing through the dungeon and useful for acquiring a Heart Piece southwest of Gerudo Town.
