The {{Term/Store|Sword of the Six Sages|Swords of the Six Sages|Series, BotW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} and {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Sword of the Six Sages}}<br/>The Six Sages are said to have forged this longsword to seal a demon king in the world where the hero fought against the beasts of twilight. The blade shines with a holy luster.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|Series|Sword of the Six Sages}} goes unnamed in {{TP}}, {{SSBB}}, and {{SSB4}}.

==Description==
The Sword of the Six [[Sage|Sages]] is a two-handed, totally white sword. Its guard is ornately crafted and shaped, and the handle has distinct ridges, to provide its wielder with a stronger grip. [[Ganondorf]] is seen with a [[scabbard]] for the sword, but it is considerably cruder in design and is wrapped in bandages. The Sage of Water was able to levitate it by thought alone, as shown in his [[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|botched execution]] of Ganondorf. The King of Evil can freely switch between using one hand or two in battle, presumably by virtue of his unnatural strength. Ganondorf also implies that, at least during the time he wielded it, the Sword of the Six Sages was capable of umbrakinetic abilities.<ref name="blot out the light forever" />

==Location and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
Prior to the events of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', the Six Sages forged the {{Term|TPHD|Sword of the Six Sages}} to execute the [[Gerudo]] King, Ganondorf, due to Link the [[Hero of Time]] warning [[Princess Zelda]] about Ganondorf's true plan after the conclusion of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''.<ref name="Inventory"/> When this capital punishment took place years later, the Six {{Plural|TPHD|Sage}} gathered at the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Chamber|link}} with {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} in chains. One of the Sages, the {{Term|TPHD|Sage of Water|link}},{{Fact|TPHD}}<!-- Needs a reference that this was the Sage of Water -->attempted to execute {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} by thrusting the {{Term|TPHD|Sword of the Six Sages}} directly into his chest, likely hitting vital organs. This weapon, however, though it contains the power of [[light]], is apparently not as effective as that of the [[Master Sword]], as the {{Term|TPHD|Triforce of Power|link}} within {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} kept him alive, allowing him to break free from his chains, and then kills the Sage of Water, before withdrawing the sword from his body. The other {{Plural|TPHD|Sage}} promptly banish {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} to the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight Realm|link}}, but also unintentionally send the {{Term|TPHD|Sword of the Six Sages}} there in the process, allowing {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} to collect the sword and keep it for himself. At some point, he even crafted a scabbard for the sword and took to carrying it as a trophy.

While this sword indeed failed to kill {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}}, it did inflict a glowing wound on him that would not heal, which later acts as a major weak point that is also present on {{Term|TPHD|Dark Beast Ganon|link}}, which proved to be an advantage for [[Link]]. {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} uses the {{Term|TPHD|Sword of the Six Sages}} for his final battle with the Hero of Twilight, denouncing the sword for its origin and initial purpose, but therefore wishing to blot out the light forever with it.<ref name="blot out the light forever">{{Cite|Would you hear my desire? To take this foul blade...and use it to blot out the light forever!|Ganondorf|TP}}</ref> {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} ultimately loses the duel, as Link repeatedly overpowers him using the [[Hidden Skill|Hidden Skills]] taught by the [[Hero's Spirit]] (the Hero of Time himself as a restless ghost) and plunges the Master Sword directly into his wound with an [[Ending Blow]], killing the villain slowly. Link himself never wields the {{Term|TPHD|Sword of the Six Sages}} in {{TP|-}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Sword of the Six Sages}} appears in {{BotW}} as an exclusive {{Term|Series|amiibo|link}} {{Term|BotW|Item|link}}. It has a rare chance of appearing in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} after scanning the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] {{Term|Series|Ganondorf|link}} {{Term|Series|amiibo}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, although only after {{Term|BotW|Link}} has tamed at least one of the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Sword of the Six Sages}} functions as a two-handed {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, and its description refers to it as a longsword.<ref name="Inventory"/> Additionally, its sheath is the same one used by {{Term|BotW|Ganondorf}} in {{TP|-}}. It shines with a holy luster that causes it to glow in dark areas such as the {{Term|TPHD|Thyphlo Ruins|link}}. Link can acquire replacements via using the {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} {{Term|Series|amiibo}} if it breaks or if it is lost.

Being made of metal, like any other blade, the {{Term|BotW|Sword of the Six Sages}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if it is equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. It will also sink if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but it can be picked back up and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. If it is exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, it will not burn as wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} do.

==={{TotK|-}}===
A variation of the Sword of the Six Sages returns in {{TotK}} in the form of the '''Dusk Claymore'''. It has a slightly lower base attack of 38, but it can be fused with materials to boost its offensive power. It is found at the [[Thyphlo Ruins]] after completing all quests there, which requires the powers of the first four Sages. If lost or broken, it can be bought back from Barganing Statues in [[the Depths]] using [[Poe]]s.

==Trivia==
*While {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} is wielding the sword in {{TP|-}}, the color changes from white to a glowing red. While this may be just due to the lighting, its similar coloration in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' could mean the color change is intentional.
*Ganondorf surviving an otherwise fatal blow by the Sword of the Six Sages could be a reference to himself as his actual form, [[Ganon]], lived through his brutal defeat by Link the Hero of Time in ''Ocarina of Time'' via stabbed in his mouth or forehead with the Master Sword (despite other cases he did die by the Blade of Evil's Bane in his human form).
