{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}} within "A Minor Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. They also have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn when the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. They were made with the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They have the special ability to deflect the beams of {{Plural|Series|Guardian Scout|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>

As they are made with {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield}} will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. In addition, they will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also cannot be manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
