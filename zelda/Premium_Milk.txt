{{Term/Store|Premium Milk||Series, ALBW}} is an item in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 135}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Premium Milk is a special drink in the [[Kakariko Village]] [[Milk Bar]] that is not normally served to its patrons.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! Care for an icy-cold glass of--! Whuh? You heard about premium milk? How'd you find out about the good stuff?|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</ref> Link receives Premium Milk by speaking to the [[Milk Bar Owner]] after receiving the [[Letter in a Bottle]] from [[Lake Hylia]]. The Milk Bar Owner explains that the letter is from a regular customer of his, the [[Bouldering Guy]], and requests that the young hero brings him a [[Bottle]] of Premium Milk to heal the boulderer.<ref>{{Cite|I see... A message in a bottle. And from someone stranded up on the mountain? Could only be one guy. He's a tricky customer. Sorry to ask, but could you take this premium milk to him? I bet it would help him heal up in no time.|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</ref> If Link tries to drink this Bottle, he will be stopped and reminded that he must deliver it instead.<ref>{{Cite|Don't drink this last bottle of precious premium milk. Deliver it!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> When Link brings the Premium Milk to the Bouldering Guy, he eagerly takes it from the young hero,<ref>{{Cite|Ugh, how embarrassing... I was bouldering along just fine when I slipped and fell. I twisted my ankle. Now I'm stuck. Hey, guy. W-w-wait! Is that what I THINK it is? Milk? Ice-cold milk! And not just any milk, but some premium milk? GIMME!|Bouldering Guy|ALBW}}</ref> drinking it all.<ref>{{Cite|Ahhh! That really hit the spot--best milk in the kingdom, right? Uh, wait. Oops. Did I drink the whole thing? Sorry, friend. And after you came all this way. Well, how about doing me another favor? Here, take this garbage away.|Bouldering Guy|ALBW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Although attempting to drink the milk normally results in a message saying to deliver it instead, the Premium Milk can be drunk during [[Shadow Link Battle]]s over [[StreetPass]]. It functions the same as regular [[Milk]] and, like all items used in StreetPass battles, it is restored following the end of the battle.<ref>{{Cite Web|url=http://www.gamefaqs.com/3ds/711412-the-legend-of-zelda-a-link-between-worlds/faqs/68297|title=StreetPass Guide by TourianTourist|site=GameFAQs|type=}}</ref>
