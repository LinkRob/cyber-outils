The {{Term/Store|Deku Scrub Playground||Series}} is a {{Term|MM3D|Mini-Game|link}} in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Deku Scrub Playground}} down this hole.|Sign|MM3D}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The Deku Scrub Playground is located in [[North Clock Town]] and is specifically for [[Deku Scrub]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Ehhhh, I'm sorry... Only Deku Scrubs can play here.|Deku Scrub|MM}}</ref> [[Deku Link]] can reach the playground by using a [[Deku Flower]] near the entrance to the [[Great Fairy Fountain]] in order to fly above the fence, or by simply climbing the fence as human. 

The objective of the game consists of jumping from platform to platform by using Deku Flowers in order to obtain all of the [[Rupee]]s scattered across them. If Deku Link successfully gathers all of the Rupees without falling to the floor within the time limit, he will be rewarded with 50 Rupees. In {{MM3D}}, he is rewarded with a [[Fishing Hole Pass]] instead.

The speed of the platforms increase each day. On the [[First Day]], the platforms move vertically. On the [[Second Day]], they move horizontally around the area's center. On the [[Final Day]], some move vertically and others move horizontally, but all of them will move faster. If Link sets a new record for three days straight, he ultimately wins a [[Piece of Heart]], although he is also barred from participating again.<ref>{{Cite|...Amazing. A new record... And for three days straight?!? Wow...We've been had. Well, you're obviously a pro, and pros can't play here. Take this and don't come back.|Deku Scrub|MM}}</ref>
