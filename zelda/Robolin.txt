The '''Robolin''' is an [[Automaton]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Robolin|A deadly mechanical sword fighter whose flashing blade can take out whole groups of enemies.<ref name="Robolin">{{Cite|A deadly mechanical sword fighter whose flashing blade can take out whole groups of enemies.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Roboblins are [[Moblin]]-like Automatons created by [[Dampé]]. They are obtained as a reward for completing the [[Chop ’em in Two!]] [[side quest]], which requires the [[Heirloom Katana]] and [[Moblin]] echo to complete.
