==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Mighty Bananas}}
Mighty Bananas can be found growing in the tropical forests of [[Faron (Region)|Faron]] and on [[Eventide Island]]. They are also sold by [[Emri]] in [[Kara Kara Bazaar]] and dropped by Red {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}}. Eating one raw heals half of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}. When cooked, the resulting [[Food]] provides [[Link]] with attack buffs.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> Mighty Bananes exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} become [[Roasted Mighty Bananas]]. They can be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Yellow [[Dye]].

Mighty Bananas are one of three [[Material|foodstuffs]] favored by the [[Sheikah]], with the others being [[Swift Carrot]]s and [[Fortified Pumpkin]]s. They are the clear obsession of the [[Yiga|Yiga Clan]], as their hideout contains many mighty bananas and all defeated [[Yiga Footsoldier]]s and [[Yiga Blademaster|Blademasters]] drop them. Yiga Blademasters will also eat them when given the opportunity, which is a good distraction technique for sneaking past them in the Yiga Clan Hideout. Interestingly, [[Monk Maz Koshia]] will also stop fighting and eat them should Link provide him with some, showing that {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas}} have been enjoyed by {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} for millennia.

Mighty Bananas are required to make [[Fried Bananas]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Along with other fruits, Mighty Bananas can be used to make [[Fruit Pie]], [[Fruitcake]], [[Honeyed Fruits]], [[Simmered Fruit]], [[Steamed Fruit]], [[Fruit and Mushroom Mix]], and [[Copious Simmered Fruit]].
