{{Term/Store|Hylian Shield|Hylian Shields|Series, OoT, OoT3D, TP, TPHD, SS, SSHD, ALBW, BotW|plural}} are recurring [[item]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 127 {{Exp Game|OoT, TP, SS, ALBW}}}}</ref><ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Hylian Shield}}<br/>A shield passed down through the Hyrulean royal family, along with the legend of the hero who wielded it. Its defensive capabilities and durability outshine all other shields.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The Hylian Shield goes unnamed in {{HWAoC}}. It is the trademark [[Shield]] of [[Link]] since {{OoT}}.

==Location and uses==
[[File:OoT3D Link Shielding Render.png|left|thumb|''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'' render of Link defending with the Hylian Shield]]
It is the traditional [[Shield]] borne by the [[Knights of Hyrule]] and is commonly used by [[Link]] in 3D ''Zelda'' games and spin-offs alongside the [[Master Sword]] since its first appearance in {{OoT}}, making the Hylian Shield itself a signature image for the hero like the sacred blade.<ref name=":0">{{Cite|This is a big, heavy Shield just like the ones Hylian Knights use. It can stand up to flame attacks!|Bazaar Owner|OoT}}</ref> Unlike the less advanced [[Wooden Shield]]s, the Hylian Shield will not burn upon contact with fire. Though it is made of steel, it is light and sturdy, an optimal weight for a fighter with a one-handed weapon.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Though it is made of steel, it is light and sturdy, an optimal weight for a fighter with a one-handed weapon.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=84}}</ref>

Throughout {{TLoZ|Series}}, the Hylian Shield has had minimal differences to its overall design, always bearing the symbol of the [[Triforce]] and a red [[Hylian Crest]] below it that represents the goddess [[Hylia]] and the [[Crimson Loftwing]] that helped Link at the founding of [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Our hero, who rescues the land that will later become Hyrule, rides a red bird. This is why the mark of a bird appears on his shield. However, since the design had already appeared, was this some herald of fate to come? Loftwings are modeled after birds called shoebills, and the idea for their curled tails came from the designer's pet shiba - Hiraoka, designer |book= HH|publisher= |page= 9}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The bird-like design that is painted to look as though it is protecting the Triforce is the mark of the goddess Hylia and also depicts the brilliant red Loftwing once ridden by the hero.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=84}}</ref> The first Hylian Shield was a legendary shield guarded by the Thunder Dragon [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]], who created the [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|Lightning Round]] to judge whether someone would be worthy of wielding the Shield, in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, yes... The Thunder Dragon. The goddess gave the Thunder Dragon the power to protect the desert. This dragon possesses a legendary shield. It is truly one of its most prized treasures. It is said that the Thunder Dragon delights in testing those with promise in the hopes of someday finding a worthy recipient for the shield.|Eldin (Dragon)|SS}}</ref> Although this shield is an important defensive item for Link, it is surpassed by the [[Mirror Shield]] in some games.

==Mainline Appearances==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Main|Shield}}
While the Hylian Shield does not exist in {{LA}} nor its ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|DX]]'' version (though technically did debut by the latter's release), it was given an appearance for the [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Nintendo Switch remake]] of the game. It can received from [[Tarin]] after [[Link]] wakes up on [[Koholint Island]].

==={{OoT|-}}===
In its debut in the long-running series, the Hylian Shield can either be purchased or found by [[Link]] as a child in {{OoT}}. Emblazoned with [[Hylian]] artwork, complete with a [[Crimson Loftwing]] and the [[Triforce]], the Hylian Shield is far too large for Child Link to wield on his arm unlike the [[Deku Shield]] and functions more like a turtle's shell. After his seven year sleep and becoming the [[Hero of Time]], Link can use it as his standard [[Shield]]. The Hylian Shield is later surpassed by the [[Mirror Shield]], which is only usable by Adult Link as this particular shield is even larger in comparison.
[[File:OOT Child Link with Hylian Shield.webp|thumb|209x209px|Link as a child wearing the Hylian Shield in {{OoT|-}}, covering over half of his body]]
The Hylian Shield becomes almost necessary to survive a visit to the [[Great Fairy of Magic]] in [[Death Mountain]], as Child Link's 'turtle shell' use of the Shield protects from recurrent flaming rock eruptions. It is also not prone to the risk of being burnt by particular fire-borne enemies, such as the [[Fire Keese]]. The Hylian Shield, like other shields, can be eaten by a [[Like Like]] if it swallows Link. Child Link's use of the Hylian Shield limits mobility while defending and does not provide as much adequate protection from the front as the Deku Shield (and the Hylian Shield itself as an adult) and is similarly unable to reflect [[Deku Scrub]] and [[Octorok]] attacks.

The Hylian Shield can be bought for 80 [[Rupee]]s at the [[Bazaar]] in [[Hyrule Castle Town]]. However, [[Death Mountain Trail Gatekeeper]] can offer Link a discount in correspondence with the [[Bazaar Owner]]. If Link speaks to the guard and then attempts to purchase the Hylian Shield at normal price at the Bazaar in the [[Market]], the shop owner will give Link a discount of random values in intervals of 5 Rupees between a total price of 45 to 75 Rupees.

Alternatively, Link can find a free Hylian Shield if he opens a certain grave at night in the [[Kakariko Village Graveyard]].

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the Hylian Shield is an indestructible type of [[Shield]], unlike the [[Wooden Shield]] and [[Ordon Shield]], because it's made of metal. However, it is unable to catch [[Arrow]]s that strike it, merely deflecting them instead.

The Hylian Shield becomes available at the newly established [[Malo Mart]] in [[Kakariko Village]] for 200 [[Rupee]]s, before entering the [[Goron Mines]], where the durability of the Hylian Shield is designed to assist in the exploration of the location. The Hylian Shield can also be bought from a [[Goron]] in Hyrule Castle Town at a slightly higher price of 210 Rupees.

Its appearance is largely similar to its {{OoT|-}} appearance, with some minor design changes. Unlike {{OoT|-}}, the metal frame is thinner and the numerous rivets are replaced by three large bolts. The Triforce symbol has become smaller, now with metal framing it. In contrast, the [[Wingcrest]] is noticeably bigger and takes up the entire bottom half of the Hylian Shield. The inverted triangle at the bottom has been removed and two metal decals take its place.

==={{SS|-}}===
As with all other Shields in {{SS}}, the Hylian Shield is controlled by using the [[Nunchuk]] (similarly to the [[Wii]] version of {{TP|-}}): by quickly thrusting the Nunchuk forward, Link will raise his Shield and use it to defend himself against enemy attacks. Unlike other Shields in the game or its previous appearances, this Hylian Shield is imbued with heroic power, making it indestructible, with its durability bar permanently filled with a glowing blue color to signify this. Also unlike its past appearances, the Hylian Shield cannot be obtained early on in the game, due to the nature of normal Shields as potentially temporary items with limited durability. It is the reward from the Thunder Dragon [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]]'s [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|Lightning Round]] if Link wins eight fights consecutively and then quits.

The Hylian Shield's design originally was largely similar to that of {{TP|-}}, albeit with brighter colors more akin to the original {{OoT|-}} design. Sometime prior to E3 2011, the design was altered, removing the silver rim around the Triforce and the metal ornamentation beneath the Crimson Loftwing design.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
The Hylian Shield is found in [[Turtle Rock]] in {{ALBW}}. When obtained, it replaces the standard Shield once obtained. Unlike the standard Shield, it can block magical attacks such as the beams fired by [[Beamos]], as well as block the [[Lynel]]s' fire breath,<ref>{{Cite|You got the Hylian Shield! This can protect you against magical attacks.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> although it cannot reflect them back.

Although the Shield is referred to as a Hylian Shield and bears the Hyrulean Triforce, it is not known or explained how it came to be housed in a [[Lorule]]an dungeon.

The Hylian Shield is located in a [[Treasure Chest]] in the [[Lockup]] of [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]], guarded by a [[Stalnox]].<ref>{{Cite|- Test of the Royal Guard -<br/>Strike down the giant foe to become recognized as a knight of the kingdom.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> It is said to have been passed down through the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]] along with legends of the hero.<ref name="Inventory"/> The defensive capability and durability of this [[Shield]] are known to outshine all others.<ref name="Inventory"/> It is the strongest and most durable shield in the game, having a durability of 800 (up to 1160 with Durability Up +) and Defense rating of 90 (up to 144 with Guard Up +). Unlike {{SS|-}}, the Hylian Shield is capable of breaking like other Shields. Once obtained within Hyrule Castle, it can be purchased from [[Granté]] in [[Tarrey Town]] for 3,000 Rupees, provided Link does not have one already. However, when purchased here, it does not come with a chance of being granted modifiers.

When spoken to at [[Trilby Plain]], [[Russ]] of the [[Shield Breakers]] will explain that all shield surfers have heard of and covet the Hylian Shield.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Speaking of not breaking, have ya heard about the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian shield}}? Everyone in the surfing scene covets that bad boy.|Russ|BotW}}</ref> He believes it to have been commissioned by the [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Family]], and its durability makes it perfect for shield surfing.<ref>{{Cite|It's clearly commissioned by the royal family or somethin'! It's sturdiness makes it perfect for shield surfing too.|Russ|BotW}}</ref> The traveler [[Baumar]] has also heard about the Hylian Shield's durability.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently, there's a totally durable shield called a {{Color|BotW Red|Hylian shield}} somewhere in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. Sounds gnarly, dude!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref>

The Hylian Shield, being made of metal, will attract [[Lightning]] if it is equipped during a [[Thunderstorm]] and conduct [[Electricity]]. It will also sink if it is dropped in [[Water (Terrain)|Water]], but it can be picked up and manipulated with the [[Magnesis]] [[Rune]]. Finally, it will not burn if it is exposed to [[Fire]] or a [[Scorching Climate]].

==={{NL|-}}===
In {{NL|-}}, the first player wields a bow (or optionally a sword and shield if playing singleplayer) and the other one to four players wield swords and shields. The swords resemble the Master Sword, while the shields resemble the Hylian Shield. However, the shields' (and swords') base color changes to match the color of the player wielding them (with the exception of the first player in singleplayer, where the sword and shield will be colored blue, despite the first player being green). The shield's design is much more simplistic than the majority of its canon appearances, and is flat across the top and lacks the curved edges on the bottom as well as the metal designs above the Triforce crest. The back of the shield is made of wood, similar to its in-game appearance in Ocarina of Time. Similar to its functionality in Skyward Sword, the shield is controlled using the Nunchuk and can block melee attacks as well as projectiles.

==Trivia==
*The Hylian Shield is one of the most widely recognized defensive items in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] or the video game industry and is sometimes referenced in [[The Legend of Zelda in Popular Culture|popular culture]], similarly to the [[Master Sword]].
*Despite being the primary shield of the [[Knight of Hyrule|Knights of Hyrule]], they are not visibly seen wielding the Hylian Shield in any of the main games, as this is only being mentioned.<ref name=":0" />
**However, as [[Link]] himself is such a knight in {{BotW}} as well as ''[[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity]]'', this is technically a case.
*While parts of the back of the Hylian Shield are made with wood, most of the official artwork for [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|{{OoT|-}}]] depicts the shield made entirely of metal.[[File:OoT English Logo.png|thumb|195x195px|The logo of {{OoT|-}} features the Hylian Shield]]
*Alongside the Master Sword, the Hylian Shield is part of the title logo of {{OoT|-}}.
*Although the Hylian Shield was said to be indestructible in {{TP}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Swor]][[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|d]]'', it can be destroyed in {{OoT|-}} and {{BotW|-}}. Therefore, this is not technically true.
*Contrary to popular belief, it is not necessarily required for Link as a child to obtain and u''<nowiki/>''se the Hylian Shield to reach the summit of [[Death Mountain]] safely at [[Death Mountain Trail]] in {{OoT|-}}. It is possible to avoid being hit with falling rock debris by running backwards with [[Targeting|Z-Targeting]], due to the faster movement speed.
*The [[Hero's Shield]] seen in some games such as {{MM}} and {{TWW}} bare a close resemblance to the Hylian Shield.[[File:OOT Dark Link Hylian Shield.png|thumb|166x166px|Dark Link's "Hylian Shield" as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
*[[Dark Link]] appears to wield the Hylian Shield in {{OoT|-}}, although he strangely never uses it to block Link's attacks but rather with his sword (itself appearing to be the Master Sword). In the original [[Nintendo 64]] version, due to hardware limitations, the model of the shield, like the character himself, has basic textures and blending into his arms, appearing matte black with no symbol on the front. In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'', however, it has a more proper look as a darker version of the traditional Hylian Shield.
*The Hylian Shield covering more than half of Child Link's body in {{OoT|-}} is very similar to the [[Mirror Shield]] from {{aLttP}}, although in the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]] title the young hero carries that equipment like weaker shields.
**It could also possibly be a reference to the [[mariowiki:Hammer_Suit|Hammer Suit]] in ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros._3|Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' from the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series, as [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] in this form is known to crouch forward for defense and have his back protecting him from fireballs like a turtle shell similarly to most [[mariowiki:Koopa|Koopas]].
**Link's [[Deku Link|Deku]] and [[Goron Link|Goron]] forms' defense in {{MM|-}} functions in a nearly identical manner.
**The turtle shell-like usage by Link's young self in the game has become a popular meme among fans due to its rather comical appearance on the hero's back compared to the [[Deku Shield]] and is therefore equipped by players more frequently.
*In ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', an unlockable weapon, [[Soul Edge (Complete)]], itself the completed state of [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Edge Soul Edge] in the form of a [[Sword]] and [[Shield]] for Link, has its latter equipment being a demonic version of the Hylian Shield, in spite of not being identical in shape.
