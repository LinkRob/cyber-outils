==Location and Uses==
Roasted Carps are the result of {{Plural|Series|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|Series|Armored Carp|link}}, or {{Plural|Series|Sanke Carp|link}} that have made contact with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. Blue {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} may also drop them when killed by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. The roasting process is said to remove some of the stench of raw carp.<ref name="Inventory"/> Link can restore one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} by eating a Roasted Carp.
