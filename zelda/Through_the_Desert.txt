{{Term/Store|Through the Desert||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 24 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Through the Desert|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Through the Desert}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must reach {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Town|link}}, starting from the northeast corner of the map. After the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} walks a short distance, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain|link}} spawn, along with a monster horde to attack each one of them. The difficulty of the monster hordes increases the farther the captain is away from the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. The first captain is attacked by a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}}, the second by a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}}, and the third by a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, aided by weaker {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} of its type. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must rescue all three of the captains to continue. When he or she does, a {{Term|HWAoC|Molduga|link}} spawns, and three rescued captains aid the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} in defeating it. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Molduga}} is defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hydromelon|link}}.
