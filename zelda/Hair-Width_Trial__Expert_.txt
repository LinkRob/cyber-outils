{{Term/Store|Hair-Width Trial: Expert+||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 57 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hair-Width Trial: Expert+|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Hair-Width Trial: Expert+}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} loses all of their health in one hit and no healing items appear on the battlefield. To complete the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Hinox|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Lynel|link}} within eight minutes, with the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Lynel}} the farthest from the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} and on the opposite side of the map. Other {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, like {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Forest Octorok|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Rock Octorok|link}} are scattered around the map as well, while groups of {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}} can be found surrounding the {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Lynel}}.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Endura Carrot|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Barbarian Leg Wraps|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}. 

A {{Term|HWAoC|Korok|link}} can be found in an area behind a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok}}, not far from the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s starting point. When talked to, they award the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} with a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}}.
