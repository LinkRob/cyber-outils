The '''Clockwork Bangle''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Clockwork Bangle|A simple accessory crafted by Dampé. It helps you wind the clockwork key faster.<ref name="Clockwork Bangle">{{Cite|A simple accessory crafted by Dampé. It helps you wind the clockwork key faster.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Clockwork Bangle can be purchased from [[Dampé]] after constructing each type of [[Automaton]]. While equipped, it reduces the windup time on automatons.
