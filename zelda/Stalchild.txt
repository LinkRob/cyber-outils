{{Term/Store|Stalchild|Stalchildren|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, FS, TP, TPHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 206 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, FS, TP, TPHD}}}}</ref>{{Note|They are referred to as '''Stalkins''' in {{Guide|TP|Prima}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|guide= Prima|page= 20}}</ref> However, as the enemy's name was officially stated in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Stalchild}}<br/>Don't be afraid of the Stalchild! Just attack it repeatedly!
}}

Stalchildren appear in [[Hyrule Field]] in {{OoT}}. They spawn only at night while [[Link]] is a child and disappear at sunrise. They will not appear if Link is wearing the [[Bunny Hood]] or if he walks on the [[Road]]. Stalchildren attack in pairs, and if one falls before the other, another Stalchild may spawn in its place if Link takes too long to destroy the other. Each time 10 Stalchildren are defeated, a larger one will spawn alongside a regular sized Stalchild. Stalchildren can be defeated with two hits, regardless of size. If Link attacks a Stalchild from the front, it will lose its head and begin wandering around blindly.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= It's a {{Color|MM White|Stalchild}}. They were the cursed soldiers of the Kingdom of Ikana...What are you gonna do?
}}

[[File:MM3D Stalchild Notebook Portrait.png|left]]
Stalchildren are of the [[Ikana Kingdom|Ikana]] race in {{MM}}. They haunt the [[Ikana Graveyard]] and are the fallen soldiers of the [[Ikana Kingdom]].<ref>{{Cite|It's a Stalchild. They were the cursed soldiers of the Kingdom of Ikana...What are you gonna do?|Tatl|MM}}</ref> Their general is [[Captain Keeta]], but they will mistake anyone that wears the [[Captain's Hat]] for their leader.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Oh, Captain! E-excuse me, sir!|Stalchild|MM}}</ref> By wearing the Captain's Hat, the Stalchildren will ask Link whether they should open or guard the grave, mentioning that they already had an order from Captain Keeta to guard it.<ref>{{Cite|Aah! Captain, sir!!! Excuse me, sir! Everyone, give the captain your attention! We haven't seen you in some time, captain, sir! As per your orders, sir, we've been guarding these graves closely. We have all been expecting your next order, sir.|Stalchild|MM}}</ref> In the [[Oceanside Spider House]], Stalchildren will reveal the solution to the house's puzzle, ultimately leading to a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Captain, sir! It's been a long time since we've seen you! As you have ordered, sir, we are investigating the secrets of this place.|Stalchild|MM}}</ref>

==={{FS|-}}===
{{Plural|FS|Stalchild}} act similarly to {{Plural|OoS|Stalfos|link}} in {{OoS}} and have four distinct behaviors, albeit without color variations to differentiate between them. {{Plural|FS|Stalchild}} may simply walk around like {{Plural|OoS|Blue Stalfos|link}}, throw bones like {{Plural|OoS|Red Stalfos|link}}, throw bones and dodge attacks like {{Plural|OoS|Orange Stalfos|link}}, or attempt to crush the {{Plural|FS|Links|link}} like {{Plural|OoS|Green Stalfos|link}}.

Purple {{Plural|FS|Stalchild}} appear in the {{Term|FS|Hero's Trial|link}} as part of the silver door levels. While they may have any of the behavior patterns of normal {{Plural|FS|Stalchild}}, they cannot be outright defeated. When hit, they will instead crumble into a pile of bones and revive a short time later.

==={{TP|-}}===
Stalchildren appear in the [[Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|Arbiter's Grounds]], the [[Cave of Ordeals]] and the [[Hyrule Castle (Twilight Princess)|Hyrule Castle]] graveyard in {{TP}}. They emerge from the ground in large groups and attack with small spears. They can be destroyed with almost any weapon, though Link's [[Sword]] is the most effective, especially when the [[Spin Attack]] is utilized.

==Other Appearances==
{{Ambig|Start}}
==={{LCT|-}}===
Stalchildren appear in the "Gerudo Stalfos: Defender" Stage of Level 1 and "Hyrule Castle: Defender" Stage of Level 4 in {{LCT}}. They rise from the ground to attack Link with their spears, along with Stalfos. Stalchildren can be taken out with one hit, but will swarm in massive numbers as time runs out.
{{Ambig|End}}

{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{OoT|-}} (Himekawa)===
A Stalchild makes a brief appearance in the {{OoT|Himekawa}}, being burned to a crisp by a young [[Volvagia]] before it became a follower of [[Ganondorf]].

===''Sonic Lost World''===
Stalchildren appear in [[The Legend of Zelda Zone]], attacking Sonic by slowly wandering toward him. They can easily be defeated by any of his moves, such as the Homing Attack.

==={{HW|-}}===
Stalchildren appear as foot soldiers that group in large numbers in {{HW}}. In [[Legend Mode]], they appear in the Scenarios "[[The Sheikah Tribesman]]", "[[The Water Temple]]", "[[Liberation of the Triforce]]", and "[[Darkness Falls]]".

Helmeted Stalchildren with broken armor appear in occupied [[Keep]]s and [[Outpost]]s. [[Summoner]] Stalchildren also appear as a variant that summons reinforcements, and wear purple hoods and carry staves. Unit troops are led by Stalchild [[Captain (Hyrule Warriors)|Captain]]s, which are actually [[Stalfos]] in appearance. The same type of Captain also appears as a Keep Boss variant that guards Keeps and Outposts. Stalchild Captains occasionally drop the [[Monster Tooth]] Bronze [[Material]] when defeated.
{{Noncanon|End}}
