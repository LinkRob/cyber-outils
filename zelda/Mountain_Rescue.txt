{{Term/Store|Mountain Rescue||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 25 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mountain Rescue|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Mountain Rescue}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given six minutes to rescue four captains. There is one in each corner of the map. The {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain|link}} in the top right corner is saved by defeating an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, in order to open a gate that blocks her from entering any other part of the map. The {{Term|HWAoC|Goron Captain|link}}, located in the lower-left hand corner, is saved by defeating an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin}} that spawns when a {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches him. The {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}} located in the lower-right hand corner is saved by defeating an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}. The final victim, the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain|link}}, is in the same situation as the {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain}}, but is being guarded by a {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus|link}} instead. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when all four captains are saved. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, ten {{Term|HWAoC|Sizzlefin Trout|link}}, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Climber's Bandanna|link}}.
