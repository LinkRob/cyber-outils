==Location and Uses==
Glass Greatshovels are strong yet brittle {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} that can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, dealing 3 damage to three enemies in front of her in a sweeping motion.<ref name="Obtention"/> As glass weapons, they cannot be created or infused with any materials, and thus can only be found as loot from {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or slain enemies.
