{{Term/Store|Dubious Food||Series, BotW}} is a recurring {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Dubious Food}}<br/>It's too gross to even look at. A bizarre smell issues forth from this heap. Eating it won,t hurt you, though...probably.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Dubious Food}}<br/>It's too gross to even look at. A bizarre smell issues forth from this heap. Eating it won't hurt you, though...probably.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref> It is shown to be green and purple, with a bone sticking out. The food is censored, hence the name.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Dubious Food can be made from several different {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} combinations:

* Food with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} or critters, but not both

* Monster Parts on their own

* Critters on their own

* Any recipe that would produce an Elixir without an effect (e.g. monster part + Chilly critter + Spicy critter)

* Seasonings without Food

Dubious Food is noted to be too gross to look at and emits a bizarre smell.<ref name="Inventory"/> However, it can restore a random amount of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} depending on the ingredients used.

[[Moza]], who can be found near [[Ishto Soh Shrine]], will give Link three recipes, two of which result in Dubious Food. One is her ancient meat dish, which involves cooking meat with a part from a {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sure thing! I'll happily teach you the recipe for my ancient meat dish. First, prepare the meat you want to use. I recommend either bird meat or steak. And then you cook it with something from a broken Guardian... {{Big|AN ANCIENT PART!!}}|Moza|BotW}}</ref> When Link calls this gross, she'll scold him, saying the only way to cook meat is something with history.<ref>{{Cite|[That sounds gross.] Hey! That's not nice! Cooking delicious meat with something that smacks of history is the only way!|Moza|BotW}}</ref> She asks Link how else she's supposed to make her ancient meat dish.<ref>{{Cite|How else would one create an ancient meat dish the likes of which no one has ever seen before?!|Moza|BotW}}</ref> However, Moza admits to Link that her only results have been Dubious Food.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, what I always end up making is {{Color|BotW Blue|dubious food}}... Though I don't see what's so dubious about it! Even when I use ingredients I'm certain will be perfect, there are lots of times I still mess it up. Cooking is complicated.|Moza|BotW}}</ref>

For her other dish, the ultimate survival dish, Moza tells Link to defeat [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monsters]] and to put their parts in a Cooking Pot.<ref>{{Cite|Sure thing! I'll happily teach you the recipe for my ultimate survival dish! First, you'll have to defeat a monster!! After that, just find yourself a cooking pot and toss in... {{Big|tons of monster parts!!}}|Moza|BotW}}</ref> This time, if Link calls her recipe gross, she'll say that you have to be willing to eat anything to survive in the wild.<ref>{{Cite|[That sounds gross.] {{Big|In order to survive out in the wild, you've got to be willing to eat anything! That's the idea behind my ultimate survival dish!!}}|Moza|BotW}}</ref> Though she always ends up with Dubious Food, she asserts she won't achieve her goal unless she makes some sacrifices.<ref>{{Cite|I may end up with nothing to eat but {{Color|BotW Blue|dubious food}}...but I won't achieve my goal unless I make some sacrifices!|Moza|BotW}}</ref> She also admits to Link that she's not sure what's so dubious about using Monster Parts.<ref>{{Cite|I don't see what's so dubious about using monster parts, though... Even when I used ingredients I'm certain will be perfect, there are lots of times I still mess it up. Cooking is complicated.|Moza|BotW}}</ref>
