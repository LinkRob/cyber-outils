==Biography==
{{Term|LANS|YipYip}} is a small pet {{Term|LANS|Chain Chomp|link}} owned by [[Madam MeowMeow]] in [[Mabe Village]].<ref name="E 233"/> She resides in the [[Doghouse]] that is attached to {{Term|LANS|Madam MeowMeow's House|link}}.<ref name="E 136"/> Unlike the other {{Term|LANS|Chain Chomp}} pets, {{Term|LANS|YipYip|link}} is able to speak fluently.

{{Term|LANS|YipYip}} is obsessed with glitz and fashion, desiring to be adorned with accessories and jewelry.<ref>{{Cite|Make-up! Jewels! Dresses! I want it all! Sigh... And some new accessories would be nice...|YipYip|LADX}}</ref> If {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} possesses the {{Term|LANS|Ribbon|link}}, {{Term|LANS|YipYip}} will notice the accessory and beg to have it, offering to trade her can of {{Term|LANS|Dog Food|link}} in exchange.<ref>{{Cite|Make-up! Jewels! Dresses! I want it all! Sigh... And some new accessories would be nice... Oh! That Ribbon! I need it! Will you trade for my dog food?|YipYip|LADX}}</ref> If {{Term|LANS|Link}} refuses to make the trade, {{Term|LANS|YipYip}} becomes very angry at him.<ref>{{Cite|Eh?! I can't believe it! You are the worst!!|YipYip|LADX}}</ref> Even after giving her the {{Term|LANS|Ribbon}}, {{Term|LANS|YipYip}} continues to insatiably lament for more accessories.

A {{Term|LANS|Figure|link}} of {{Term|LANS|CiaoCiao}} can be won by {{Term|LANS|Link}} in the {{Term|LANS|Trendy Game|link}}.
