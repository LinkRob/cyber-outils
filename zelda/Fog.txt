{{Term/Store|Fog|Fog|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}}, also known as '''Mist''',<ref>''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess — PRIMA Official Game Guide|PRIMA Official Game Guide]]'' (Prima Games) pg. 63</ref> is a recurring trap in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD |publisher= Prima |page= 19}}</ref>

==Appearances==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Paralyzing Fog}}
{{Term|TWWHD|Paralyzing Fog}} can be found in certain areas of the {{Term|TWWHD|Earth Temple|link}}. If {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} comes into contact with it, he will become {{Term|TWWHD|Curse|link|display= Cursed}}, meaning that none of his items work, including his {{Term|TWWHD|Master Sword|link}}. While affected, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} is surrounded by a concentrated smog, his body flashes purple, and he acts disoriented or weakened. This is markedly similar to being cursed by a {{Term|TWWHD|Blue Bubble|link}}. The curse will not be lifted unless he gets out of the {{Term|TWWHD|Paralyzing Fog}} or finds a {{Term|TWWHD|Light Beam|link}} to take refuge under.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, After Link is transformed into [[Wolf Link]], purple {{Term|TPHD|Fog}} appears in {{Term|TPHD|Faron Woods|link}}. Link initially crosses the foggy portion of the woods by jumping across tree branches with the help of [[Midna]]. This fog can be temporarily cleared away by holding and swinging a [[Lantern]], but if Link is not careful and falls in, he will faint and respawn where he was earlier. After re-entering the area in human form, a [[Monkey]] takes Link's Lantern and guides him through the fog to the [[Forest Temple]]. This fog remains here for the rest of the game.{{Fact}} 

{{Main|Dark Fog}}
In the {{Term|TPHD|Palace of Twilight|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Dark Fog}} appears as a trap that will automatically turn {{Term|TPHD|Link}} into Wolf Link. {{Term|TPHD|Dark Fog}} was made by {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}} and large amounts of {{Term|TPHD|Dark Fog}} appear when {{Term|TPHD|Phantom Zant|link}} is defeated.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Blue|This black fog is made up of shadow crystals created by Zant. They blot out light...}}|Midna|TPHD}}</ref>

==={{ST|-}}===
A poisonous Fog appears in {{ST}} whenever a [[Vengas]] is defeated inside the {{Term|ST|Forest Temple|link}}. If Link comes in contact with the impenetrable Fog, he will be hurt by it until he moves away. The [[Whirlwind]] is capable of blowing the Fog away.
