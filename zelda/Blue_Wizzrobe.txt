==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
Blue Wizzrobes are usually found in the same dungeons as their [[Red Wizzrobe|red counterparts]]. They try to walk toward [[Link]] and, if something is in the way, they will teleport through it. Unlike Red Wizzrobes, you can actually see him while he's teleporting. If he is in front of Link, he will shoot lots of beams. Having said beams deal a lot of damage, many players find this annoying.
