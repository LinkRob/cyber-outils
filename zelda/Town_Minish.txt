{{Term/Store|Town Minish|Town Minish|Series, TMC|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref> also known as '''Town Picori''',<ref>{{Cite|Town Picori|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> are a type of {{Term|Series|Minish|link}} in {{TMC}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Town Picori}}
The {{Plural|TMC|Town Minish}} are a type of {{Plural|TMC|Minish|link}} that moved from the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}} to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} due to their love for {{Plural|TMC|Human|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|These Minish like humans so much that they moved from Minish Village into Hyrule itself!|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}}, the twin brother of {{Term|TMC|Gentari|link}}, serves as their elder, and a small group of them in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}} are also referred to as {{Plural|TMC|Library Minish|link}}.

{{Plural|TMC|Town Minish}} can be found scattered across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}, particularly in buildings. Unlike the {{Plural|TMC|Forest Picori}}, the color of their clothing is varied; they can wear blue, green, pink, red, or yellow, although yellow-clothed {{Plural|TMC|Minish}} are a rarity.<ref>{{Cite|Yellow is a rare color for us Minish. There’s a good side and a bad side to being rare, though. Two yellow ones rarely ever show up in the same place, so they never meet up!|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref>

Some {{Plural|TMC|Town Minish}} help the residents of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} with various tasks; a group of them consistently aid {{Term|TMC|Rem|link}} in making shoes, due to his frequent falling asleep while working.<ref>{{Cite|Ho, ho! You need {{Color|TMC Red|Pegasus Boots}}? Why, you're just in time! We made a pair just now while {{Color|TMC Green|Rem}} was sleeping. But only {{Color|TMC Green|Rem}} can put the finishing touches on them...|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> In addition, any {{Plural|TMC|Town Minish|link}} is a potential character to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Let's give it a try!|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref>
