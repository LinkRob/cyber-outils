{{Term/Store|?||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a location in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|?|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
As an adult, {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} can find {{Term|OoT3D|Dampé|link}}'s grave in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village}}'s {{Term|OoT3D|Graveyard|link}}. If {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} moves the tombstone, he will find a secret entrance. Inside, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} will meet {{Term|OoT3D|Dampé's Ghost|link}}, who challenges him to a race. If {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can keep up with him, he will receive the {{Term|OoT3D|Hookshot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hehehe, young man... You were very quick to be able to keep up with me! Hehehe! As a reward, I'm going to give you my treasure. It's called the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Hookshot}}!|Dampé's Ghost|OoT3D}}</ref> If {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} challenges {{Term|OoT3D|Dampé's Ghost}} once again and reaches the end under one minute, he will be rewarded with a {{Term|OoT3D|Piece of Heart|link}}.

After reaching the end of the grave's interior, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} must play the "{{Term|OoT3D|Song of Time|link}}" to remove a block in order to exit the grave and reach an upper section of the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill|link}}'s interior. From there, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can reach another {{Term|OoT3D|Piece of Heart}} by jumping on the moving platforms.

==Trivia==
*The original {{Term|OoT3D|Crest of the Gerudo|link}} can still be found inside {{Term|OoT3D|Dampé|link}}'s grave in the [[Nintendo GameCube]] versions of the game and was present in the original version as well.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= 
aleckermit|published= December 27, 2009|retrieved= February 5, 2020|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kfihH92Ukqk|title= OoT: Un-Replaced Crescent Moon Symbols in Dampe's Grave|site= YouTube|type= video}}</ref> The reason for the symbol remaining present in the textures was because this map was originally a part of the [[Gerudo Training Ground]], but was made separate at some point in development. It was replaced with the newer version in the Virtual Console and with a floral pattern in {{OoT3D}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= It's worth noting that the editors neglected to replace the old symbols in the beginning and ending rooms of the Dampe grave race until the 3DS release which substituted them with floral engravings.|author= |published= n.d.|retrieved= February 5, 2020|url= https://www.zeldaspeedruns.com/oot/generalknowledge/version-differences|title= Version Differences|site= ZeldaSpeedRuns|type= }}</ref>
