'''"Light Spirit's Elegy"''' is a [[Song]] in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
"{{Term|TPHD|Light Spirit's Elegy}}" plays when one of the {{Term|TPHD|Spirits of Light|link}} is speaking to {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}. It is the 16th track in Disc 1 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Original Soundtrack]]''. "{{Term|TPHD|Light Spirit's Elegy}}" is melodically similar to the song [[Selection Menu]].{{Fact}}
