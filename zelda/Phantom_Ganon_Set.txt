The {{Term/Store|Phantom Ganon Set||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} set in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 191}}</ref> It's only available in BoTW when [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC Pack is active.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Set}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} set comprised of the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Skull|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Armor|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Greaves|link}}. They can be obtained through the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Dark Armor|link}}", which is available after reading {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The evil spirit's armor set is hidden within {{Color|BotW Red|Faron's sea of trees}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at the highest of three waterfalls north of Lake Floria}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at the bridge between small waterfalls at Floria River}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the broken stone bird of Ebara Forest}}.|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> The pieces of the set can be found in the following locations in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Woods|link}}.
* Within {{Term|BotW|Corta Lake|link}}
* Under {{Term|BotW|Sarjon Bridge|link}}
* In {{Term|BotW|Ebara Forest|link}}

Each individual piece of the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Set}} will increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} when worn. The full set can be worn to give Link the {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link|display=Disguise|section=Disguise}} {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link}} against {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Stalmoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Stalizalfos|link}}. He'll also receive the {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link|display=Bone Atk. Up|section=Bone Atk. Up}} Set Bonus, which will increase his attack when using bone weaponry.

The Phantom Ganon Set is unable to be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}. Similarly, it can't be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. Characters who see Link wearing the Phantom Ganon Set will be temporarily frightened. It is modeled after {{Term|OoT3D|Phantom Ganon|link}} from {{OoT}}.
