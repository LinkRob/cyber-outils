{{Term/Store|Scorching Climate|Scorching Climates|Series, OoT, BotW, TotK|plural}} are recurring areas in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|OoT, BotW, TotK}} In these areas, {{Term|Series|Link|link}} will burn unless he has fireproof equipment on.

== Overview==
A notable feature of the {{Term|Series|Death Mountain|link}} vicinity, a {{Term|Series|Scorching Climate}} is an area that is so hot that {{Term|Series|Link}} cannot remain in them indefinitely without protective equipment. Remaining too long in a {{Term|Series|Scorching Climate}} will, without exception, result in {{Term|Series|Link}}'s death.

{{Stub}}

=={{OoT}}==
Found in the {{Term|OoT|Death Mountain Crater|link}}, the {{Term|OoT|Fire Temple|link}}, and the Fire Medallion path of Ganon's Castle, {{Term|OoT|Link}} requires a {{Term|OoT|Goron Tunic|link}} to remain in a {{Term|OoT|Scorching Climate}} indefinitely,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Link, it's so hot in here! We can't stay in here for long!}}|Navi|OoT}}</ref> elsewise a [[Time#Countdowns_and_Timers|countdown timer]] will start based on {{Term|OoT|Link}}'s current health. Gaining more {{Term|OoT|Heart Container|link}}s, as well as filling up any empty ones whilst outside the hot zone, will allow {{Term|OoT|Link}} more time. If the timer runs out, {{Term|OoT|Link}} will burst into flames and perish instantly.

{{Stub}}

=={{BotW}}==
Within {{Term|BotW|Eldin|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain}} area, {{Term|BotW|Link}} encounters two types of {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|plural}} and must have at least one level of {{Term|BotW|Flame Guard|link}} in order to survive.{{Fact}} He can obtain this by either consuming a sufficiently potent {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}} or as many pieces of {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Armor|link}} as he needs.

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} ascends {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain}} he finds the air getting even hotter, and must apply a second level of {{Term|BotW|Flame Guard}} as just the one will no longer suffice.{{Fact}}

If Link submerges himself into the [[Hot Spring]]s, he can cool himself from the Scorching Climate so long as he remains in there, and for a short period after he emerges while still soaked in spring water.

{{Stub}}

=={{TotK}}==
While Death Mountain no longer has its {{Term|TotK|Scorching Climate}}, most Eldin caves and its counterpart in {{Term|TotK|The Depths|link}}, which includes the {{Term|TotK|Fire Temple|link}}, does. In addition, the sources of the various {{Term|TotK|Hot Spring|link}}s in Hyrule are from waterfall-like lava flows that come with {{Term|TotK|Scorching Climate|plural}}.{{Fact}} North of the Fire Temple requires level two.{{Fact}}

Link can temporarily avert the Scorching Climate heat by applying water onto himself, such as using a [[Water Fruit]] or [[Sidon's Vow]].
