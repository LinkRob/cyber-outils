==Characteristics==
Electric Lynels are [[Lynel|Lynels]] that are given elemental properties due to the [[Calamity Ganon]] of the future and the [[Calamity Ganon]] of the past being in the same time zone. Electric Lynels have dark green skin with electricity surrounding them. They have yellow horns and grey fur with glowing red eyes. Some of the Electric Lynel attacks feature electric shock waves, similar to [[Electric Moblin|Electric Moblins]]. Electric Lynels use a [[Savage Lynel Spear]] unlike regular Lynels in the game. Water fighters like [[Mipha]] and [[Sidon]] are weak to it and take more damage. Its unique attacks are as follows: 

* Causing a lightning strike after stabbing it's spear in the ground 
* Causing a lightning explosion 
* Firing off electric balls instead of fireballs
