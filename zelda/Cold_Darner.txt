==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Cold Darner}}
{{Plural|BotW|Cold Darner}} can be found in cold regions of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, such as {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. They can also be purchased from traveling merchants, such as {{Term|BotW|Maypin|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}}, {{Plural|BotW|Cold Darner}} can be made into {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}} that provide {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Cold Darner}} can be used to make {{Term|BotW|Blue Dye|link}}.

As part of the "{{Term|BotW|Little Sister's Big Request|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must ask [[Gleema]] what the insect she wants to see is.
