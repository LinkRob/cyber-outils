'''Fireworks''' are a type of [[Item]] in {{EoW}} and {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Firework|Kitchen|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Firework|This device, crowning achievement of its Goron artisan, launches three fireworks in a row.<ref name="EoW Firework">{{Cite|This device, crowning achievement of its Goron artisan, launches three fireworks in a row.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Firework [[Echo]] can be learned in [[Goron City]] after completing [[The Fireworks Artist]] [[Side Quest|side quest]]. It is required for the [[Explosions Galore!]] quest to obtain the [[Tocktorok]] [[automaton]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their Echo using the [[Tri Rod]], which can be summoned for two Echo energy.
