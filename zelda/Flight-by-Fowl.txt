==Location and Rules==
Flight-by-Fowl is the main attraction of [[Fyer & Falbi's Watertop Land of Fantastication]] and can be found in a hut at the top of [[Lake Hylia]].<ref>{{Cite|Lake Hylia's Newest Hangout:<br/>{{Color|TPHD Blue|Fyer & Falbi's Watertop<br/>Land of Fantastication}}<br/>Head right {{Icon|TP Arrow Right}} for the<br/>{{Color|TPHD Green|Flight-by-Fowl}} main attraction.|Sign|TPHD}}</ref> [[Link]] can reach it by either paying 10 [[Rupee]]s to [[Fyer]] for a cannon ride or by entering through [[Hyrule Field]]. To start the mini-game, Link must pay 20 Rupees to the owner [[Falbi]].

Once paid, Link can pick one of the [[Cucco]]s in the corral and then jump off of a ledge located above Lake Hylia. Link must collect Rupees by gliding with the Cucco until landing on the [[Isle of Riches]] located on the Lake. The Isle has six floors with various [[Treasure Chest]]s filled with different types of Rupees and a [[Piece of Heart]]. In {{TPHD}}, the [[Cucco Stamp]] is found in a Chest instead of 50 Rupees. A [[Poe]] can also be found during night on the second floor. A moving platform is located at the top, which can be stopped by targeting the weather vane on top of Falbi's hut with the [[Gale Boomerang]].

Link can also reach several other areas in Lake Hylia by playing the mini-game. These include a {{Term|TPHD|Cave|link}} on a ledge below Falbi's hut and two Treasure Chests above the entrance to [[Spring of the Spirit Lanayru]].
