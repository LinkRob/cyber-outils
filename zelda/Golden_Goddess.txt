The {{Term/Store|Golden Goddess|Golden Goddesses|Series, SS|plural}} are recurring [[deities]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 11}}</ref> They are [[Din (Goddess)|Din]], the Goddess of Power, [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]], the Goddess of Wisdom, and [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]], the Goddess of Courage, the chief gods and creators of the land that becomes the [[Hyrule|Kingdom of Hyrule]] and the [[World of The Legend of Zelda|extended world]] that it resides in, and are also sisters.<ref name=gdt/> Though never directly seen in any installment of the franchise, it is understood that the Golden Goddesses have always existed, and that they are omnipotent and eternal. Though the Golden Goddesses are the most important gods of the world, they are mentioned only by few characters in the series, and knowledge of them has been shown to be limited to the people of Hyrule alone, most races worshiping their own patron deities. Signs of their existence had been visible in parallel worlds such as [[Termina]] and the [[World of the Ocean King]] also, implying that they may have created those worlds as well.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Though the waters of the Ocean King exist in a different world from that of Tetra, Link, and the pirates, the crests of Hyrule’s three gods can still be found in the Ocean King’s temple. Because of this, it is highly possible that the waters of the Ocean King were created by the same gods as Hyrule.|book=HH|page= 130}}</ref>

As seen in {{OoT}} and {{TWW}}, each of the three Goddesses is associated with a characteristic, common element and color: Din is power, fire and red; Nayru is wisdom, water and blue; Farore is courage, wind and green. They are the creators of the sacred [[Triforce]] that is made up of their three respective associated characteristics of power, wisdom and courage.

==Appearances==
Although the Goddesses have never physically appeared to this date, they are sometimes implied to be influencing the events that take place throughout the series. The three deities of power, wisdom, and courage were first mentioned in the instruction manual of {{ALttP}}, but they were not yet given names. They were first called by their individual names and titles in {{OoT}} and appeared in a flashback as flaming golden feminine humanoid figures as they created the world from chaos. Their [[Marks of the Goddesses|symbols]] make an appearance in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Oracles of Ages]]'' representing three tunes of the [[Harp of Ages]], as do the [[Oracle]]s that are named after them. They were mentioned in {{TWW|-}} as putting the young [[Link#The Wind Waker|Hero of Winds]] through trials to test his worthiness as a successor of the legendary [[Hero of Time]],<ref>{{Cite|Only one who is able to overcome the trials that await here will be acknowledged by the gods to be a true hero. [...] You must rise above the trial of the gods!|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> and three colored statues (red, blue and green) depicting each one of them appears with their associated symbols. The Goddesses also play an indirectly important role in {{SS}}, where it is revealed that they personally placed the [[Triforce]] in the care of the goddess [[Hylia]] in the [[Zelda Timeline#The Era of the Goddess Hylia|ancient past]].<ref>{{Cite|They did all this in their lust to take the ultimate power protected by Her Grace, the goddess... Handed down by the gods of old, this power gave its holder the means to make any desire a reality. Such was the might of the ultimate power that the old ones placed it in the care of the goddess.|N/A|SS}}</ref>

==History==
===Creation of the World===
[[File:GoddessesDepartOoT.png|thumb|right|309x309px|The Golden Goddesses shown leaving Hyrule following its creation, as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
Before time began and before spirits and life existed, the universe was in chaos. The three Golden Goddesses descended upon the chaos and began the creation of the world, each of them creating a different facet of the realm. Din created the material realm, Nayru gave the realm law and order, and Farore created all life forms that would follow the said order.<ref name=gdt>{{Cite|Before time began, before spirits and life existed... Three golden goddesses descended upon the chaos that was Hyrule... Din, the Goddess of Power... Nayru, the Goddess of Wisdom... Farore, the Goddess of Courage... Din... With her strong flaming arms, she cultivated the land and created the red earth. Nayru... Poured her wisdom onto the earth and gave the spirit of law to the world. Farore... With her rich soul, produced all life forms who would uphold the law.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref>

With their labors completed, the Goddesses departed for the heavens, but not before leaving behind the Triforce, three sacred golden triangles representing a small portion of the essence of the goddesses.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=After the gods had finished their work, they left the world, but not before creating a symbol of their strength, a golden triangle known as the Triforce. A small but powerful portion of the essence of the gods was held in this mighty artifact, which was to guide the intelligent life on the world of Hyrule.|game= ALttP|page=3}}</ref><ref name=fsmanual>{{Cite Manual|quote=Aeons ago, the Deities of Power, Courage, and Wisdom descended to the world of chaos. They created the world that we know and left behind a symbol of their strength, the golden emblem known as the Triforce, which they hid in the Golden Land.|game= ALttP&FS|page=14}}</ref> Because the power of the Triforce was so great, they trusted the sacred relic to the careful hands of the goddess Hylia so that she could protect it from those who would misuse its almighty power (as often seen with [[Ganon]]).<ref>{{Cite|Handed down by the gods of old, this power gave its holder the means to make any desire a reality. Such was the might of the ultimate power that the old ones placed it in the care of the goddess.|N/A|SS}}</ref> Since Hylia herself was a goddess, the Triforce was in safe hands, as only mortals would be able to use its power. The Goddesses entrusted the protection of the land they created to Hylia and a legion of spirits and fairies.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Upon their departure to the heavens, the three goddesses entrusted another goddess, Hylia, and a legion of spirits and fairies with protecting the land they had created.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=12}}</ref>
[[File:ALttP Triforce Sacred Realm Artwork.png|thumb|259x259px|The Sacred Realm is where the Golden Goddesses' great creation, the Triforce, rests, as seen in an artwork of {{aLttP|-}}]]
The resting place of the Triforce has become the [[Sacred Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|And, the resting place of the triangles has become the Sacred Realm.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref>

Besides the Triforce, the Golden Goddesses were also responsible for the creation of the [[Secret Stone]]s, which they specifically entrusted to the [[Zonai]] via Hylia.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Origin Era, Creation of Heaven and Earth: "The three goddesses created Hyrule. The Secret Stones are created."|book=''Tears of the Kingdom'' Master Works|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=370-371}}</ref>

It would later transpire that a major part behind why the Golden Goddesses created Hyrule was specifically to contain an entity from the primordial chaos within the universe known as [[Null]], who if left unchecked would have consumed any and all life that was in the void.

===Interventions in Hyrule===
Although the Goddesses' morals and standards are never specifically stated anywhere in the games, they have intervened in affairs numerous times after the creation of the world but always for a good cause.

====Age of Twilight====
In the Child Timeline, after Ganondorf's attempt at overthrowing the [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Family]] was exposed via information from the young Hero of Time, he was sentenced to be [[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|executed]]. Although the ancient Six Sages stabbed him with a [[Sword of the Six Sages|sword of light]], he unexpectedly gained the [[Triforce of Power]] in what was described only as "a divine prank" and survived the otherwise fatal blow,<ref name="divine prank">{{Cite|Yet...By some divine prank, he, too, had been blessed with the chosen power of the gods.|Sage|TP}}</ref>  thus forcing them to seal him away into the [[Twilight Realm]] via the [[Mirror of Twilight|Twilight Mirror]], although not before he successfully slew the Sage of Water with a punch.

==The Three Goddesses==
===Din, The Goddess of Power===
{{Main|Din (Goddess)}}
{{quote|Din... With her strong flaming arms, cultivated the land and created the red earth.|[[Great Deku Tree]]}}
[[File:OoT Din (better image).png|thumb|320x320px|Din, as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
'''Din''' created the bare earth and shaped the featureless land, forming its topography. Her labors produced the towering mountains, profound chasms, and great expanses of Hyrule's topography. 

With her work completed, Din formed the [[Triforce of Power]], the embodiment of her supremacy. When the Demon King [[Ganon#Ocarina of Time|Ganon]], as [[Ganondorf]], sought to abstract the Golden Power from its resting place, he failed to procure the sacred Triforce in its entirety due to his unbalanced heart, and it split into its three separate elements. Believing in his heart that power was the most important aspect of the Triforce, he was ultimately left with the Triforce of Power, and therefore the blessing of Din. Despite this, Ganon was not satisfied, and is determined to steal every Triforce piece and receive the full blessings of all three Golden Goddesses rather than only Din.

===Nayru, The Goddess of Wisdom===
{{Main|Nayru (Goddess)}}
{{quote|Nayru... Poured out her wisdom onto the land, and gave it the spirit of law.|[[Great Deku Tree]]}}
[[File:Nayruoot.jpg|thumb|392x392px|right|Nayru, as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
'''Nayru''' is the source of the laws that govern the realm in which Hyrule exists. Once her sister Din had completed the creation of the physical realm, Nayru began establishing order within the realm. She illuminated the firmament of the sphere with her divine wisdom, and founded the fundamental laws of the realm. 

Once she had finished, Nayru formed the [[Triforce of Wisdom]], the embodiment of her omniscience. Whomever would possess the Triforce of Wisdom would be instilled with the essence of Nayru, and her blessing, granting them wisdom unparalleled. When the sacred Triforce was split into its three virtues, the Triforce of Wisdom eluded Ganon, and sought a worthy holder, namely, the Princess of Destiny, [[Princess Zelda]].
===Farore, The Goddess of Courage===
{{Main|Farore (Goddess)}}
{{quote|Farore... With her rich soul, produced all life forms who would uphold the law.|[[Great Deku Tree]]}}
[[File:Faroreoot.jpg|thumb|394x394px|right|Farore, as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
'''Farore''' is the source of all life that exists within the Hyrulean realm. Once her sisters had finished creating a realm suitable for sustaining life, Farore created the life forms that would inhabit it. She breathed life onto the barren earth and into the seas, creating grasses, trees, vines, and all other manner of vegetation, also forming all of the diverse people of Hyrule. 

With her works completed, Farore drew a portion of her divine spirit, and produced the [[Triforce of Courage]]. The holder of the Triforce of Courage would be imbued with the blessings of Farore, and valor unrivaled by any other. When the Triforce divided into its three aspects, the Triforce of Courage eluded Ganon and was bestowed upon the Hero, [[Link]], as a merit of his great courage.

Various items, people, and deities found throughout the series are named after the Golden Goddesses. Each of the Goddesses also have a [[Marks of the Goddesses|crest]] that represents them, which can be found in places and items related to them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Din, Nayru, and Farore each left a crest behind. Each of these symbols—the crests of power, wisdom, and courage—can be found in a location related to its respective goddess.|book=HH|page= 70}}</ref>

The first of the items are the three magic abilities obtained in {{OoT}}: [[Din's Fire]], [[Farore's Wind]], and [[Nayru's Love]], granted to Link by the [[Great Fairy|Great Fairies]]. In {{TWW}}, it is said that [[Din's Pearl]], [[Farore's Pearl]], and [[Nayru's Pearl]] were handed down to the [[Rito]]s, [[Korok]]s, and [[Jabun]] by the Goddesses themselves, respectively.<ref>{{Cite|The water spirit Jabun was kind enough to give you this jewel, a treasure of one of the goddesses!|N/A|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You received Din's Pearl! This is the gem the Ritos claim was passed down from the great goddess!|N/A|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You received Farore's Pearl! The earth spirit, the Deku Tree, has seen fit to give you this jewel, a treasure of the goddess, Farore.|N/A|TWW}}</ref> These three pearls, known as the [[Goddess Pearls]], are each placed on the three statues resembling the Golden Goddesses located in each of the [[Triangle Islands]] found across the Great Sea to rise the [[Tower of the Gods]] from beneath the waves.<ref>{{Cite|Wandering traveler who seeks the guidepost of the goddesses... Place the pearl you hold here.|Goddess Statue|TWW}}</ref> Atop the [[Hyrule Castle]] from {{TP}} is a statue depicting the Goddesses surrounding the [[Triforce]]. Three songs, "[[Din's Power]]", "[[Farore's Courage]]", and "[[Nayru's Wisdom]]" in {{SS}} are learned in the [[Isle of Songs]], which are necessary to obtain the three [[Sacred Flames]], each also named after the Goddesses, that will upgrade the [[Goddess Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|Ah, sorry to disappoint you, boy, but for now your sword lacks the power necessary to awaken the gate. Faron Woods...Eldin Volcano...and Lanayru Desert... A sacred flame is hidden somewhere in each of these lands. Seek them out, and purify your sword in their heat. Only after your blade has been tempered by these three fires will it be fully imbued with the great power for which you search.|Impa|SS}}</ref>

[[Din (Oracle)|Din]], [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]], and [[Farore (Oracle)|Farore]] from {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, who are the Oracle of Seasons, Ages, and Secrets respectively, share the names of the goddesses and also share physical similarities to the statues depicted in {{TWW|-}}. It is also implied that both the dragons from {{SS|-}} and the Light Spirits from {{TP|-}}, Eldin, Faron, and Lanayru, are references to the Goddesses, with two of the dragons bearing the symbol of the goddess they are associated with on their forehead. Because of this, the [[Silent Realm]]s of the three regions in the former game feature the names of the Goddesses, each of them testing one of the Goddess' attributes depending on the realm. Farore's Silent Realm tests the limits of Link's courage, Nayru's Silent Realm tests the limits of his wisdom, and Din's Silent Realm tests the limits of his power.<ref>{{Cite|This particular trial, Farore's Silent Realm, tests the limits of your courage.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is the second trial, known as Nayru's Silent Realm. It will test your wisdom, Master.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You stand within Din's Silent Realm, the third trial. It will test the limits of your power.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Likewise, each of the [[Sacred Tears]] bear their name.<ref>{{Cite|You got a Tear of Farore! This object's power halts the movements of the Guardians for just 90 seconds. Collect all 15 to pass the trial!|N/A|SS}}</ref> Three [[Sacred Flames]] sharing their names and bearing their associated symbols, characteristics and colors are used to forge the [[Goddess Sword]] into the legendary [[Master Sword]] and activate the [[Gate of Time]] that also bears their symbols in the [[Temple of Hylia]]. 

In {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', each Goddess has a corresponding [[dragon]] that shares their name and associated element. Numerous locations in the games also have the Goddesses as their namesake, typically corresponding to a goddess's associated element; [[Faron (Region)|Faron]] consists primarily of rainforests and grassland, [[Eldin (Region)|Eldin]] contains Death Mountain, and [[Lanayru (Region)|Lanayru]] is snowy and mountainous with many waterfalls.

In [[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|{{EoW|-}}]], when Princess Zelda completes each of the [[Dungeons in Echoes of Wisdom|dungeons]] corresponding to each of the goddesses, the Golden Goddesses themselves talk to Zelda and give her a Sanctum. It is required to have all three Sanctums in order to get to the place where the [[Triforce]] is.

==Physical Appearance==
[[File:ALttP Goddesses Artwork.png|thumb|right|276x276px|A depiction of the Golden Goddesses in {{ALttP}}]]
In an artwork of {{aLttP|-}}, the Golden Goddesses resemble Hylia/Princess Zelda, as each has blonde hair and light skin. In their first appearance, in {{OoT}}, the Goddesses Din, Nayru, and Farore are depicted as golden figures surrounded by a colorful red, blue, and green auras, respectively. However, in later titles, monuments representing the Goddesses (such as the statues of the Golden Goddesses in the throne room of [[Hyrule Castle]] in {{TP|-}}) give a more detailed appearance. The Goddess Statues in {{TWW|-}} appear similar to the Oracles [[Din (Oracle)|Din]], [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]], and [[Farore (Oracle)|Farore]] from the ''Oracle'' games, who are all humans named after the Goddesses and have some resemblance to themselves.

== Trivia ==

* The three colors that represent the Golden Goddesses, red (Din), blue (Nayru), and green (Farore), are interestingly the primary colors of visible light. When these colors are combined, a white light will result, much like how when the three goddesses combined their essences, as seen in some games such as {{OoT}} and {{TWW}}, the yellow or golden [[Triforce]] appears.
* It is likely that the Golden Goddesses are triplets. In their appearances based on official artwork from {{aLttP}} as well as their likeness in {{OoT|-}} and {{TP|-}}, aside from their signature colors to distinguish them, the deity sisters are identical. However, in {{TWW|-}}, the statues depicting the goddesses are distinctively different from each other in appearance.
* The [[Master Sword]] likely has connection with the Golden Goddesses in that for [[Link]] to have the right to use the sacred blade, he must complete great trails that test his power, wisdom, and courage. In addition, in {{SS|-}}, the [[Sacred Flame|Sacred Flames]] were used on the Goddess Sword to make it become the Master Sword.[[File:OoT Link next to Nayru.png|thumb|248x248px|Adult Link next to Nayru in a modified gameplay of {{OoT|-}}]]
* In {{OoT|-}}, through the use of ROM hacking, it is possible to make Link be up-close to one of the Goddesses. The deities are much larger than the hero in scale and if set to become stationary, their models are shown to slowly turn counter-clockwise. They also do not have hit detection, as they are not intended to be encountered in the main storyline.
* Although their age is unknown and are equal in power as a trinity, it is possible that Din is the eldest sister, or at least the leader of the Golden Goddesses, as she is the first in order.
* {{OoT|-}} and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|3DS remake]] remains the only case in the series so far where Link uses a form of the Goddesses' divine powers as an item, through [[Din's Fire]], [[Farore's Wind]], and [[Nayru's Love]], respectively.
* Coincidentally, due to their appearance in {{OoT|-}} and Din's color being pink in-game rather than red, some fans of the series find the Golden Goddesses similar to superheroine characters [https://powerpuffgirls.fandom.com/wiki/Blossom_(1998_TV_series) Blossom], [https://powerpuffgirls.fandom.com/wiki/Bubbles_(1998_TV_series) Bubbles], and [https://powerpuffgirls.fandom.com/wiki/Buttercup_(1998_TV_series) Buttercup] of the popular Cartoon Network TV series ''[https://powerpuffgirls.fandom.com/wiki/The_Powerpuff_Girls_(1998) The Powerpuff Girls]'' (which was released at the same year in 1998), who each can fly at high speed with identical colored trails respectively to Din, Nayru, and Farore, and are also triplet sisters, themselves.
** Ironically, the show itself has a [[The Legend of Zelda in Popular Culture|pop culture reference to {{OoT|-}}]], in the season 3 episode "Child Fearing", where it shows Link and [[Navi]] being in a dungeon and the Mayor having trouble controlling the main character.
