{{Term/Store|Fado||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 276 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Fado is responsible for running the [[Ordon Ranch]]. He teaches [[Link]] to ride [[Epona]] by setting up fences for practice.<ref>{{Cite|When y'all are approachin' a fence, use A to dash at the right moment and y'all can jump the middle of the fence. Got that, bud?|Fado (Twilight Princess)|TP}}</ref> As [[Ordon Goat|goats]] never listen to him,<ref>{{Cite|I tell you, though, these scamps won't listen to a word I say, so I got no one else to turn to.|Fado (Twilight Princess)|TP}}</ref> he often asks Link to help him by herding them.<ref>{{Cite|Link, Epona... Look, I'm awful sorry to have to ask y'all to do this for me.|Fado (Twilight Princess)|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OK, then! Herd them goats into the barn with Epona!|Fado (Twilight Princess)|TP}}</ref> After Link restores light to the [[Eldin Province]] and gets Epona back, Fado gives him a [[Piece of Heart]] if he herds 20 goats into the barn in less than three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|Hoo! Now, THAT is dedication! Wantin' to do the job perfect!|Fado (Twilight Princess)|TP}}</ref>

Fado is last seen during the ending credits calling to Link's empty house.

==Other Appearances==
