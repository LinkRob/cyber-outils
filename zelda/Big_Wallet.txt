{{Term/Store|Big Wallet|Big Wallets|Series, TMC, TP, TPHD, SS|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 117 {{Exp Game|TMC, TP, SS}}}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
==={{TMC|-}}===
[[File:TMC Big Wallet of Fairy Fountain.png|thumb|left|200px|Link obtaining the Big Wallet from the Great Butterfly Fairy in {{TMC|-}}]]
Three Big Wallets can be obtained in {{TMC}}. One can be purchased from [[Stockwell's Shop]] for 80 [[Rupee]]s;<ref>{{Cite|Only 80 Rupees for a {{Color|TMC Red|wallet}}. It can hold a king's ransom in Rupees!|Stockwell|TMC}}</ref> one can be found in a [[Treasure Chest]] in [[Lon Lon Ranch]], in a pool of water that drains away after [[Link]] [[Kinstone Fusion|Fuses]] [[Kinstone Piece]]s with [[Mayor Hagen]]; one is given by the [[Great Butterfly Fairy]] in [[Minish Woods]].

The first Big Wallet obtained increases the maximum amount of Rupees that Link can hold from 99 to 300; the second Big Wallet increases the maximum amount to 500; the third and final Big Wallet increases the maximum amount to 999.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the Big Wallet is the first upgrade to the Wallet, and it can hold up to 600 Rupees. [[Agitha]] gives Link the Big Wallet after he gives her a [[Golden Bug]].

In {{TPHD}}, the Big Wallet can hold up to 1,000 Rupees, originally the maximum capacity of the [[Giant Wallet]], which had its maximum capacity increased to 2,000 Rupees.

==={{SS|-}}===
The Big Wallet is the second Wallet upgrade in {{SS}}. It can hold up to 1,000 Rupees. Link can receive the Big Wallet from [[Batreaux]] after giving him thirty [[Gratitude Crystal]]s.
