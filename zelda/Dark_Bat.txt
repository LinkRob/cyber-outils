'''Dark Bats''' are shadowy bats found in the {{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine}} of {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Characteristics==
They have a similar appearance to {{Plural|FPTRR|Keese|link}} and {{Plural|FPTRR|Bad Bat|link}} and are accompanied by other "Dark" shadowy enemies such as {{Term|FPTRR|Dark Nyuudou|link}} in the dungeon. The weakest and most common enemy found in the dungeon, {{Plural|FPTRR|Dark Bat}} are the only monster to remain after its completion. They are moderately strong if {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} is alone, but {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo|link}} will help {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} make short work of them. As with other dungeon enemies, they will only drop {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} when defeated.
