== Features and overview ==
Caves are openings above the ground that appear on the [[Surface]] and high in the [[Sky]] in places like [[Skyloft]] and the [[Great Sky Island]]. While caves are typically shallower than [[dungeon]]s, in some instances, there are dungeons also called caves or caverns.

==Mainline appearances==
=== {{TLoZ|-}} ===
In {{TLoZ}}, the people [[Link]] meets in the part of [[Hyrule]] he explores reside in caves. Link receives assistance from merchants and {{Plural|TLoZ|Old Man|link}} in the caves,<ref name="z1 manual"/> including receiving {{Plural|TLoZ|Sword|link}} which he can equip.<ref name="sword"/>
=== {{TAoL|-}} ===
In {{TAoL}}, caves connect Hyrule's towns with one another<ref name="trophy">{{Cite Book|quote=Taking back the trophy that was stolen by a Goriya unlocks the ability to learn Jump magic. Without this ability, it is not possible to progress through the cave beyond Ruto. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=108 }}</ref> and with palace [[dungeon]]s.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=This palace built on a small island can be reached via a cavern south of the King's Tomb. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=146 }}</ref> It is required for Link to progress through caves in his quest past obstacles using abilities such as with the Jump spell.<ref name="trophy"/> Caves, including the cave at the end of Death Mountain's maze of caverns, also hold treasures in them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=A maze of caverns and multiple routes makes exploring the depths of Death Mountain particularly perilous. While not a dungeon proper, if Link reaches Spectacle Rock, he'll receive the Hammer, essential for clearing boulders and brush. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=146 }}</ref>
