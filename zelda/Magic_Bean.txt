{{Term/Store|Magic Bean|Magic Beans|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They can be planted in soft [[soil]],<ref>{{Cite|Set a bean to (C) and use it on the dirt right here.|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you want to plant beans, go around and look for soft soil. Chomp chomp chomp.|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref> where they will eventually grow into a magic plant that can transport [[Link]] to otherwise unreachable places.<ref>{{Cite|You got a Magic Bean! Find a suitable spot for a garden and plant it with (C). Then, wait for something fun to happen!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> 

==Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Magic Beans can be purchased from the [[Bean Seller]] sitting near the [[Zora's River]].<ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... How about some Magic Beans?|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref> The price of the beans starts at 10 [[Rupee]]s and increases by 10 each time Link buys one.

Soft soil only appears during the child era, so Link can only plant a Magic Bean as a child. After seven years, the bean will grow into a leaf that grants Link access to certain areas otherwise impossible to reach. Some of these areas, however, can be accessed by summoning [[Pierre]] using the "[[Scarecrow's Song]]."

[[Young Link]] can play the [[Song of Storms]] near a bean seedling, which will make it grow and release a bunch of [[Fairy|Fairies]] before going back to normal.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Link can hold a maximum of 20 Magic Beans.

Link cannot purchase Magic Beans until he has learned how to use them from the [[Bean Seller]]. The Bean Seller is located inside a [[cave|hole]] at the end of the east side of the [[Deku Palace]] garden (in {{MM3D}}, this hole has a second entrance, from the outside of the palace on the east side). Upon teaching Link to use Magic Beans, he will give Link one as a free sample. After teaching how to use them, he will sell Magic Beans for 10 Rupees each.

The Deku-friendly [[Business Scrub]] (who is initially located in the [[Southern Swamp]] next to the [[Swamp Tourist Center]], but will move to [[South Clock Town]] if Link gives him the Town Title Deed) sells Magic Beans exclusively to [[Deku Scrub]]s for 10 Rupees each.

In {{MM3D}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Trading Post|link}} sells Magic Beans for 100 Rupees each.

The beans will fully grow when they are watered with [[Bottled Water|Spring Water]]<ref>{{Cite|Do you need any Magic Beans? They sprout leaves as soon as you water them. That they do, they do.|Bean Seller|MM}}</ref> or the rain from the [[Song of Storms]].

Soft soil appears in various locations in [[Termina]]. Growing Magic Beans usually takes Link to areas with a Piece of Heart. Planting a Magic Bean in the Deku Palace, however, is necessary to progress through the game.
