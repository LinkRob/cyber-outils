==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Endura Carrot}}
{{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot}} are found at {{Term|BotW|Malanya Spring|link}}, near the peak of {{Term|BotW|Satori Mountain|link}}, and at the edges of {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. Eating an {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot}} will heal {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} for two {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot}} can also be roasted by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Endura Carrot|link}}. They can also be cooked into a meal using a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to temporarily increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s maximum {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"></ref>

Like {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot}} can be fed to {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} to increase their {{Term|BotW|Bond|link}}. Unlike other {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}} that {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} can eat, {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot}} will temporarily increase the {{Term|BotW|Horse}}'s number of {{Plural|BotW|Spur|link}} granting them three extra yellow {{Plural|BotW|Spur}} which have a carrot icon next to them. After using up normal {{Plural|BotW|Spur}} and any granted by the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Bridle|link}}, these extra {{Plural|BotW|Spur}} can be used. However, they are lost afterwards. {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Endura Carrot}} have the same effect if eaten off the ground by a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}. If the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}} eats an {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot}}, only its {{Term|BotW|Bond}} will be increased, as it has no {{Plural|BotW|Spur}}.

An {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot}} is required to revive a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} via the Horse God, {{Term|BotW|Malanya|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I may not look the part, but I am the {{Color|BotW Red|god of horses}}. I need that uh...that thing... A kind of carrot... One I've only eaten once in all my many years... An {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|endura carrot}}!}} That is what I ask of you. Fulfill this request and I will consider helping you.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Huh, you... If you would like to revive a horse, you will need an {{Color|BotW Blue|endura carrot}}. I assume you brought an {{Color|BotW Blue|endura carrot}}?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to give him an {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot}}, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will agree to revive one of his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I have one now!] I see you have an {{Color|BotW Blue|endura carrot}}. Allow me to revive your horse.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> They can also be used at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} as an ingredient for {{Term|BotW|Light Yellow Dye|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
In {{TotK}}, Endura Carrots no longer appear at Great Fairy Fountains or Malanya Spring. One Endura Carrot can be found at pink-colored trees all around Hyrule. Like the previous game, Endura Carrots temporarily increase maximum stamina when cooked in a meal.
