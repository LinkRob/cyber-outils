{{Term/Store|Dr. Troy||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> He is an experienced biologist who has traveled the world in his study of [[animals]].<ref>{{Cite|Hoh hoh! I am Troy. I am a dedicated and hardworking biologist.|Dr. Troy|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hoh hoh! I am Troy! In my younger days, I traveled the world and saw many animals.|Dr. Troy|OoA}}</ref>

==Biography==
Dr. Troy resides in northwestern [[Lynna City]]. He will inform [[Link]] on various creatures in the game. Once Link defeats [[Veran]], Dr. Troy will travel to the [[Target Carts]] game on [[Rolling Ridge]] to study [[Goron]]s.<ref>{{Cite|I've come to Rolling Ridge to study the Gorons and their games.|Dr. Troy|OoA}}</ref> 

In a [[Linked Game]] in {{OoS}}, Dr. Troy's student will appear in [[Holodrum]] next to [[Guru-Guru]] on top of the [[Windmill]] near [[Horon Village]].<ref>{{Cite|My student is studying hard in Holodrum.|Dr. Troy|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I am the senior pupil of Troy, Labrynna's biologist!|Hylian|OoS}}</ref> He will ask Link to tell a [[Troy Secret|Secret]] to his teacher in [[Labrynna]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm very busy, so I want you to tell this secret to Dr. Troy up on Rolling Ridge in Labrynna!|Hylian|OoS}}</ref> Once Link tells the Secret to Dr. Troy, the biologist will thank Link for the update, and will offer him a "gift" if he can get a perfect score in the Target Carts mini-game.<ref>{{Cite|Thankyou for updating me. Let me offer you something in return... But not for free! If you score high enough in that game, you can have a gift, OK?|Dr. Troy|OoA}}</ref> After successfully completing the mini-game, Dr. Troy will give Link the [[Bombchu]]s.
