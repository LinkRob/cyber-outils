==Overview==
==={{LA|NS|-}}===
In {{LA|NS}}, {{Term|LANS|Hero Mode}} is available at the beginning of the game and can be selected upon the creation of a new save file. In it, enemies deal double damage and {{Plural|LANS|Heart|link}} do not appear. Mini-Bosses will also not yield {{Plural|LANS|Fairy|link}} upon defeat. The icon of a monster appears on the game file and on the bottom left corner of the screen when playing in {{Term|LANS|Hero Mode}}.

==={{TWWHD|-}}===
In {{TWWHD}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Hero Mode}} is available at the beginning of the game and can be selected upon the creation of a new save file. In it, enemies deal double damage and {{Plural|TWWHD|Heart}} do not appear.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= In Hero Mode, you take double damage and won't be given recovery hearts at any point.|author= Nintendo|published= August 28, 2013|retrieved= |url= http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HsZDa2nBVgY|title= WiiU - The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD - Hero Mode Trailer|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Hero Mode}} can be activated or deactivated at any point through the file selection screen.

==={{TPHD|-}}===
In {{TPHD}}, {{Term|TPHD|Hero Mode}} is available at the beginning of the game and can be selected upon the creation of a new save file. In it, enemies deal double damage and {{Plural|TPHD|Heart}} do not appear. Similarly to the [[Wii]] version of {{TP}}, the entire map is flipped horizontally while in {{Term|TPHD|Hero Mode}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, {{Term|SS|Hero Mode}} is unlocked after completing the game and can be selected upon the creation of a new save file from the previous one.<ref>{{Cite|I can see you're playing in Hero Mode, so clearly you've mastered all the puzzles out there before.|Sheikah Stone|SS}}</ref> In it, cutscenes can be skipped, enemies deal double damage, and {{Plural|SS|Heart}} and {{Plural|SS|Heart Flower|link}} do not appear unless {{Term|SS|Link|link}} is holding the {{Term|SS|Heart Medal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have elected to engage Hard Mode, so hearts and heart flowers will not appear. I recommend utilizing potions.|Fi|SS}}</ref> The current amount of {{Term|SS|Treasure|link}}s and {{Term|SS|Insect|link}}s from the previous game are retained. In addition, actions that consume stamina or air will deplete the {{Term|SS|Stamina Gauge|link}} and {{Term|SS|Oxygen Gauge|link}} faster. Furthermore, all the hints from {{Plural|SS|Sheikah Stone|link}} are available from the beginning.<ref>{{Cite|Tell you what, I'm going to go ahead and give you access to all the hints right from the start.|Sheikah Stone|SS}}</ref> The battle against the Demon Horde, the third battle against {{Term|SS|Ghirahim|link}}, and {{Term|SS|Demise|link}} can also be fought in the {{Term|SS|Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|link}}. Finally, the {{Term|SS|Skyward Strike|link}} keeps its powered-up form and, at the point in the game when it would have been powered up, the charge time gets removed completely.

In {{SSHD}}, the save slot for Hero Mode can be chosen at will, and the Sheikah Stone is not available due to its removal from the HD version. In addition, the ability to skip cutscenes is no longer exclusive to Hero Mode.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, {{Term|ALBW|Hero Mode}} is unlocked after completing the game and can be selected upon the creation of a new save file. In it, enemies deal four times more damage. Unlike other games, {{Plural|ALBW|Heart}} appear normally. Additionally, {{Term|ALBW|Ravio|link}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Diary|link}} can be found inside the {{Term|ALBW|Vacant House|lik}}, and there is an extra scene after Link deposits the Master Sword in the pedestal, with the sages cheering.

==={{EoW|-}}===
In {{EoW}}, Hero mode is a difficulty option that can be turned on at anytime by going to the options menu. Enemies deal increased damage, Hearts no longer drop, and some item effects have no effect.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW}}, {{Term|HW|Hero Mode}} appears as a {{Term|HWDE|Difficulty|link}} setting for Scenarios in {{Term|HW|Legend Mode|link}}. It is unlocked after completing the original Scenarios in any other {{Term|HWDE|Difficulty}}. In higher {{Term|HWDE|Difficulty}} levels, enemies deal more damage and have increased defenses. Bonuses are also given by clearing Legend Mode Scenarios in a higher {{Term|HWDE|Difficulty}}, such as an increase in {{Plural|HW|Rupee|link}} gained. The Hard Mode {{Plural|HW|Gold Skulltula|link}}, also unlocked after completing the original {{Plural|HWDE|Scenario}}, can only be found by playing through the {{Plural|HWDE|Scenario}} in either Hard or {{Term|HW|Hero Mode}} difficulty in addition to fulfilling their other requirements. 
{{Noncanon|End}}
