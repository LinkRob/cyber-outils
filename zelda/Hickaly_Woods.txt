The {{Term/Store|Hickaly Woods||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hickaly Woods'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Hickaly Woods are located in [[West Necluda]], southeast of [[Dueling Peaks Stable]].

[[Link]] can find a [[Stalnox]] in a clearing in the northern section of the Woods.

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Purple Rupee]] can be found near the northeastern edge of the Woods, hidden behind two bombable walls at the base of a small rocky hill.

A [[Korok]] can be found by lifting a [[Rock]] hidden in a hollow [[Tree Stump]] on the Woods' western edge near the river.
