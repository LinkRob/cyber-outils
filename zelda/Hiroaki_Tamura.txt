'''Hiroaki Tamura''' is a graphic artist, designer, and planner at [[Nintendo]].

==Biography==
{{Stub}}
Hiroaki Tamura has worked on at least 7 games, having done both game design and graphics work for both the Pokemon franchise and the Legend of Zelda franchise.
Non-Zelda games he has worked on include<ref>https://www.mobygames.com/person/193357/hiroaki-tamura/credits/</ref>:

* Product Tester for ''Pokemon Crystal Version'' (2000)
* Graphic Artist for ''Pokemon Puzzle Collection'' (2001)
* Graphic Artist for ''Pokemon Pinball mini'' (2001)
* Main Designer for ''Pokemon Tetris'' (2002)
* Designer of Snorlax's Lunch Time for ''Pokemon Channel'' (2003)
