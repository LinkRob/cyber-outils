The {{Term/Store|Mogma Mitts|Mogma Mitts|Series, SS}} are an item in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Digging Mitts can be upgraded to the Mogma Mitts. They are received from [[Silva]] after saving him in the [[Fire Sanctuary (Skyward Sword)|Fire Sanctuary]]. The mitts allow Link to burrow underground through patches of soft sand. While digging underground, Link can break rocks, set off [[Bomb]]s, hit [[switch]]es, and attack [[Moldorm]]s.
