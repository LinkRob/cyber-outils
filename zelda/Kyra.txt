{{Term/Store|Kyra||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kyra}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} who can be found traveling the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. In particular, she goes back and forth between the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. Her goal is to find her dreams while wandering the world.<ref name="Kyra">{{Cite|I'm wandering the world, learning what I can and seeking my dreams... And I can tell, just from looking at you...You have a place in my dreams, don't you? Did we meet for a reason?|Kyra|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, to live my dreams...|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}}, she stops to eat a snack.<ref>{{Cite|I'm on a snack break right now. Food just tastes better when you're lounging at the Kara Kara Bazaar.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} talks to {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} without wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}, she mutters to herself to stay calm, remember her training, and greet a man like anyone else.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|OK... It's a voe. You can handle this. Deep breaths, Kyra. Remember your training. First... You greet the voe like anyone else.}}|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> She then introduces herself to {{Term|BotW|Link}} while stuttering nervously.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'otta! N...nice to meet you! I'm Kyra. From the...the... From the Gerudo!|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she is wandering the world in search for her dreams, and she wonders if he has a place in them.<ref name="Kyra"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} that she is his "dream girl," she rejects this and remembers that her grandmother warned her not to waste her time on men who use such phrases.<ref name="Dream"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} claims to simply be taking a walk, {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} decides she can stop wasting her time with him.<ref>{{Cite|[Just a guy takin' a walk.] Oh, is that how it's gong to be? Then I can stop wasting my time with you.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} talks to {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} again, she is upset that he is disrupting her "personal journey of learning and discovery."<ref>{{Cite|You again? I'm on my personal journey of learning and discovery, so it would be great if you could not disrupt it...|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> Kyra asks Link once more if he thinks he is a part of her destiny.<ref>{{Cite|Unless... Do you think you're part of my destiny? My dream life?|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} calls her his "dream girl" this time, she says that her grandmother was right about men, telling {{Term|BotW|Link}} to leave her alone.<ref>{{Cite|[You're my dream girl!] Hmm... My vaba was right about you voe. "Dream girl..." Nah, I don't have time to waste on you. Get out of here.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} rejects her, {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} wonders what the point of talking to him is.<ref>{{Cite|[Just a guy takin' a walk.] I hate to be rude, but... What's the point in talking to you anymore?|Kyra|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kyra}}'s interactions with {{Term|BotW|Link}} change if he is wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}}. In {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, she compliments his outfit and says that it must drive men wild.<ref>{{Cite|Pretty clothes. You don't see my kinswomen wearing that style often. I bet it drives voe out in the world wild.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} goes on to say that she is the perfect age to travel, but she has barely explored the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} because she prefers being around the familiar.<ref>{{Cite|I'm at the perfect age to travel and see what the world holds, myself, but I've barely explored this desert...|Kyra|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Adventures won't come and find me, I know. I have to put myself out there... I like staying around the familiar, I guess.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} recommends that {{Term|BotW|Link}} travel the world so that he might meet a nice man.<ref>{{Cite|You, though... You should see the world. Meet new people. Maybe you'll meet a nice voe! Sav'orq, traveler!|Kyra|BotW}}</ref>

While staying at {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} thinks that if men were allowed inside, they would be able to share their adventures with its inhabitants.<ref>{{Cite|It occurs to me sometimes... If we got rid of the law keeping voe out of Gerudo Town, they could come to town with tales of their adventures...|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> However, she also notes that younger {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} women like the law, as it gives them an excuse to leave and see the world.<ref>{{Cite|Then again, the younger vai like the law. It gives them an excuse to get out and see the world, you know?|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} sometimes wishes men could enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, she acknowledges that there are advantages to keeping them banned.<ref>{{Cite|Sometimes I wish voe were allowed in town, but banning them does have some advantages...|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> She thinks that the outside world is interesting, but if she had heard about it from a traveler, she never would have left {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to experience it for herself.<ref>{{Cite|The outside world can be an interesting place, and I'd probably never have left town if I could just hear about it.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kyra}} regrets not learning to {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} surf when her grandmother told her to.<ref name="Vaba"/> She has trouble walking through the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} on her own, and she dreams of meeting someone that she can ride with.<ref>{{Cite|The desert sands are hard on my feet. I'd love to use a sand seal, but... I never actually learned the trick to sand-seal surfing. Maybe one day I'll meet someone who really knows how to do it. Then I can catch a ride and travel with ease!|Kyra|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The desert sands are hard on my feet. Maybe one day someone who can sand-seal surf will carry me about...|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} has been appeased, she warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|display=Desert}} can still be dangerous, and it can be easy to avoid threats with a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}.<ref>{{Cite|Even with the Divine Beast appeased, these sands are dangerous. Move fast, or the monsters will catch you. It's easiest to give them the slip when you're sand-seal surfing, but I never really learned how.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kyra}} appreciates {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}}, often wishing to be there when she is in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kara Kara Bazaar is sounding real nice right about now, but...|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> However, since she would have to walk there, she does not feel like making the journey.<ref>{{Cite|I can't sand-seal surf, so I'd have to walk, and I'm not feeling up to that right now.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I can't sand-seal surf, so I'd have to walk, and I'm not feeling up to that right now.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> When she arrives in {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}}, she remarks that there is no better place to relax, as it has cool {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} and green plants.<ref>{{Cite|There's no better place to relax that Kara Kara Bazaar. Cool water, green plants—this place has it all.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> She tries to visit as much as she can.<ref>{{Cite|I come here whenever possible. I just can't get enough of it!|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> While she is there, she resolves to start living her daydreams.<ref>{{Cite|My daydreams keep getting more elaborate. I'd better get down to it and start living them!|Kyra|BotW}}</ref>

On her way back to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the best way to avoid the {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}} is with a {{Term|BotW|Hydromelon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|In the worst of the desert heat, I have some hydromelon. There's nothing better for cooling off.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> When she was younger, she would never leave {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} without them.<ref>{{Cite|I would never go outside the town walls without some when I was a young vai.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> On occasion, she also remarks that she feels as though she is being baked by the sun.<ref>{{Cite|I'm baking in this sun...|Kyra|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves {{Term|BotW|Kyra}} from being attacked by [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|enemies]], she will thank him, noting he is stronger than she thought.<ref>{{Cite|Sa'oten! You're stronger than I gave you credit for.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Chilly Elixir|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, right... You should have this. You fought well.|Kyra|BotW}}</ref>
