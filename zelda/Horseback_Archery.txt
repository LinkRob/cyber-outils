{{Term/Store|Horseback Archery||Series, BotW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Minigame|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 332}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Horseback Archery}} goes unnamed in {{MM}}.

==Location and Rules==
==={{MM|-}}===
In order to prevent the {{Plural|MM3D|Ghost (Majora's Mask)|link}} from stealing the {{Plural|MM3D|Cow|link}} from {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Romani was practicing... For tonight... Tonight...|Romani|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|They}} are coming...|Romani|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|They... They come at night...every year when the carnival approaches... They come riding in a bright, shining ball. A whole lot of them come down... And then... They come to the barn...|Romani|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My older sister won't believe me... But Romani must protect the cows!|Romani|MM3D}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Romani|link}} appoints {{Term|MM3D|Link}} as her assistant. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} agrees to help her, she explains her strategy and tasks {{Term|MM3D|Link}} with riding the {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|display=Ranch}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Horse}}, {{Term|MM3D|Epona|link}}, to destroy the 10 {{Term|MM3D|Ghost (Majora's Mask)}}-shaped {{Plural|MM3D|Balloon|link}} that she places across the {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|display=Ranch}} in under 2 minutes.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Grasshopper, I'm recruiting for an assistant right now! You're a boy--won't you try?|Romani|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Green|[Yes]}} Great! Now that's the spirit, Grasshopper! OK then, I'm going straight into my strategy!|Romani|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|They'll appear all over the ranch... They'll aim for the barn and approach it slowly, so hit them with {{Color|MM3D Red|arrows}} so they can't get in! You got that? And you mustn't {{Color|MM3D Red|leave}} the ranch!|Romani|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Grasshopper! Let's practice right away!|Romani|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There are {{Color|MM3D Red|10}} ghost-shaped {{Color|MM3D Red|balloons}} in the ranch, so hurry and burst them all. If you take more than {{Color|MM3D Red|2 minutes}}, you're out.|Romani|MM3D}}</ref>

During the {{Term|MM3D|Minigame}}, red-colored dots will appear on the {{Term|MM3D|Map Screen|link}}, indicating the positions of the remaining {{Plural|MM3D|Balloon}}. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} is unable to dismount {{Term|MM3D|Epona}} during the {{Term|MM3D|Minigame}}. Likewise, a {{Term|MM3D|Gate|link}} will be set up at the entrance to {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch}} to prevent {{Term|MM3D|Link}} from escaping. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} speaks to {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} during the {{Term|MM3D|Minigame}}, she will laugh and remark on how confident he is.<ref>{{Cite|Confident, aren't you?|Romani|MM3D}}</ref>

Upon first completing the {{Term|MM3D|Minigame}} within the time limit, {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} will tell {{Term|MM3D|Link}} that he and {{Term|MM3D|Epona}} work perfectly together.<ref>{{Cite|OK! You're done!|Romani|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You two work perfectly together!|Romani|MM3D}}</ref> She then decides to teach {{Term|MM3D|Link}} "{{Term|MM3D|Epona's Song|link}}" so that he can call upon the {{Term|MM3D|Horse}} when he needs.<ref>{{Cite|I'll teach Romani's horse-calling song to you, Grasshopper. Now you two keep getting along, and go practice some more!|Romani|MM3D}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} does not beat the current record but completes the practice in under 2 minutes, {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} will simply tell him that he did great.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|XX:XX.XX}}! That's great.|Romani|MM3D}}</ref> If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} beats {{Term|MM3D|Romani}}'s record of 1 minute,<ref>{{Cite|The current record is {{Color|MM3D Red|01:00.00}}. Are you ready?|Romani|MM3D}}</ref> she will congratulate him instead.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|XX:XX.XX}}?!? Amazing! It's a new record!|Romani|MM3D}}</ref> Additionally, if {{Term|MM3D|Link}} fails to complete the practice in the time limit, she will dismiss the result as a consequence of his inexperience.<ref>{{Cite|OK! Time's up!|Romani|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Too bad. Well, that's how it goes at first, Grasshopper.|Romani|MM3D}}</ref>}} Afterwards, {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} will caution {{Term|MM3D|Link}} that unlike the practice {{Plural|MM3D|Balloon}}, the real {{Plural|MM3D|Ghost (Majora's Mask)}} will keep appearing even after being destroyed and that they must hold out until sunrise at 5:00 AM.<ref>{{Cite|When your arrows hit these, they burst into nothing, but the real ones will keep popping up! If they get in the barn, we lose! They run away at first {{Color|MM3D Red|light}} of the {{Color|MM3D Red|sun}}, so we'll have to keep fending them off until then!|Romani|MM3D}}</ref>

The {{Term|MM3D|Horseback Archery}} {{Term|MM3D|Minigame|link}} can be attempted multiple times during a cycle, although {{Term|MM3D|Link}} will not receive any additional prizes for practicing. It can only be attempted on the {{Term|MM3D|First Day|link}} as the {{Plural|MM3D|Ghost (Majora's Mask)}} will either abduct {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} if {{Term|MM3D|Link}} fails to fend them off, or there will be no need with the threat of invasion gone on subsequent days.

==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Horseback Archery}} {{Term|BotW|Minigame|link}} is operated by {{Term|BotW|Jini|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Mounted Archery Camp|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches him, he asks how confident he is in his ability to fight on horseback.<ref>{{Cite|Hello there, young man. I can tell just by looking that you're a trained warrior. But tell me this: How confident are you in your ability to fight on horseback?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not confident, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} expresses concern and asks if he would like to run some drills.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... I don't like the sound of that. The monsters out this way are strong critters. You're sure you don't want to {{Color|BotW Red|drill}} with me some?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is confident, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} asks if he is willing to prove it.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah? You got the skills to back that confidence up? Nothing's more dangerous than a man who thinks he can handle the worst, only to fall apart when the time comes. What do you think? Are you confident enough to {{Color|BotW Red|prove you got the right stuff}}?|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Jini}} how the drills work. {{Term|BotW|Jini}} explains that {{Term|BotW|Horseback Archery}} was invented somewhere in the east,<ref>{{Cite|Ever heard of {{Color|BotW Red|horseback archery}}? They invented it way out east somewhere.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> and it requires a lot of coordination.<ref>{{Cite|It takes a lot of coordination to shoot targets with a bow and arrow from the back of a galloping horse.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He claims his drill will be a good way to practice fighting mounted [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monsters]].<ref>{{Cite|I expect you'll run into mounted varmints now and again, so this'll be a good way to get some practice in.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not riding a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will notice.<ref>{{Cite|Now...for a young man interested in horseback archery, you seem to be in want of a horse. That's no way to {{Color|BotW Red|drill}}.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to retrieve one of his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} from {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}} nearby and to come back riding it.<ref>{{Cite|Head down that path there, and you ought to come across a {{Color|BotW Red|stable}}. Fetch your steed from them if you got one. Get a horse, mount up, then come talk to me {{Color|BotW Red|when you're riding high in the saddle.}}|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to run a drill, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} reminds him that he can not do so without a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Can't drill without a {{Color|BotW Red|bow and arrows}}. I'd think that's just common sense.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He offers to sell {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} and do other kinds of business with {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you need some, I can sell you a few of mine. Or I'm open for any sort of business! What do you say?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take his time as he browses his wares.<ref>{{Cite|I have an impressive collection here! Take your time!|Jini|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} denies him, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} says that they will see how well he does on the field with his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Suit yourself. We'll see how well you and your horse act as a team on the field.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

The price to run a drill is 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Now...I'm loath to ask, but would you mind forking over {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} to cover some of my costs?|Jini|BotW}}|</ref> though {{Term|BotW|Jini}} guarantees they are worth twice as much.<ref>{{Cite|Much obliged, young man. I guarantee my {{Color|BotW Red|drills}} are worth twice what you pay.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses to pay, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} explains that while he is happy to provide his {{Term|BotW|Horseback Archery}} drills, he has needs to meet.<ref>{{Cite|This here's a service I'm happy to provide, but I can't do it for free. Don't judge a man for his needs.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

The goal of the {{Term|BotW|Minigame}} is to pop as many of the {{Plural|BotW|Balloon|link}} floating in the {{Term|BotW|Parache Plains|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll explain a bit before we get started. You see the {{Color|BotW Red|targets}} floating out there? I'll judge your skill by {{Color|BotW Red|how many of 'em you pop with your arrows}}.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Jini}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} not to worry about time, as he will let him know when it is up.<ref>{{Cite|Don't you worry about when time's up. I'll let you know.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> In addition, he informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he is not allowed to dismount his {{Term|BotW|Horse}} during the drill.<ref>{{Cite|Suppose this goes without saying but you get off your horse, and we're done here. No way to practice shooting from horseback when you're standing around on foot, after all.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He promises {{Term|BotW|Link}} some {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear|link}} if he can break more than 20 {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tell you what... You break {{Color|BotW Red|more than 20}} targets, and I'll hand over my {{Color|BotW Red|horse gear}}|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Jini}} during the {{Term|BotW|Minigame}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? What's the matter?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says it is nothing, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} says no one likes a quitter and he should continue with the drill.<ref>{{Cite|Good. No one likes a quitter. There's still time, so ride hard and shoot straight. Until then, ride hard and shoot straight.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks to forfeit the run, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} tells him he will not get his {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} back and asks if he still wants to quit.<ref>{{Cite|That's fair. Course, you won't get your money back, if that's what you're after. Still planning to quit?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses to quit, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} figures that he must have his reasons and encourages him to come again.<ref>{{Cite|I suppose you have your reasons. Hope to see you again someday.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

Once time runs out, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} calls {{Term|BotW|Link}} back to the {{Term|BotW|Mounted Archery Camp}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hold it! Head on back here.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} hit less than 11 {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}}, he suggests trying to shoot while the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} is trotting instead of galloping.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... You only hit {{Color|BotW Red|[X]}}... Nothing to write home about. Might want to shoot from {{Color|BotW Red|a trot}} some more before you jump straight into firing at a gallop.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> There is no reward. Between 12 and 19 {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}}, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} is not convinced of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s skill and reminds him to keep his eye on the targets.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... Well, you hit {{Color|BotW Red|[X]}}... Not bad, but I'm not rightly convinced you're ready to raise the stakes. Hitting your mark at a gallop is no small potatoes. Make sure to {{Color|BotW Red|keep your eye}} on the target.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} 15 {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a reward for seeing it through. Keep drilling and you'll get it.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} pops between 20 and 22 {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}}, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} says he has the potential to be among the best archers and gives him the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bridle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm...{{Color|BotW Red|[X] targets}}... You've got some fire in you, young man. The best archers say {{Color|BotW Red|targets seem to freeze when they concentrate on them}}. I think you could reach that level too. Here's a gift to help you on your travels. Hope you find a good use for it.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} already has the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bridle}}, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will instead give him 10 {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}.}} Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} hits between 23 and 25 targets, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will say he has not seen someone so young with such talent in a long time.<ref>{{Cite|Hm...{{Color|BotW Red|[X] targets}}... It's been a dog's age since I've seen such talent in someone so young.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He does not believe that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is actually over 100 years old and gives him the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Saddle|link}}, as he no longer needs it.<ref>{{Cite|What do you mean you're not that young? Over 100 years old? Go ahead, pull the other one. Anyway, this gift is my thanks for putting on a good show. I'm past needing it, and I think you should have it.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} already has the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Saddle}}, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will instead give him 20 {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow}}.}}

After each drill {{Term|BotW|Link}} runs, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} asks if he wants to go again.<ref>{{Cite|Well? You want to {{Color|BotW Red|drill}} some more?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} reminds him not to be a stranger and to come back.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm in a hurry...] Don't be a stranger, young man. I'll be waiting here for you, come wind or storm.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} says he made the right call and asks for 20 more {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sounds like the right call to me. The price hasn't gone down any—{{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}, same as ever. That fine with you?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has paid, he goes to fix the targets that were destroyed in the previous run.<ref>{{Cite|I'll go fix up the targets. Shouldn't be long.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He is able to run a drill once they are fixed.<ref>{{Cite|All done now. Let's get drilling again.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>
