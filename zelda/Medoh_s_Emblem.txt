{{Term/Store|Medoh's Emblem|Medoh's Emblems|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Medoh's Emblem}}<br/>The commemorative symbol of a wind trial's completion by one who wishes to control a Divine Beast. ==Location and Uses==
Medoh's Emblems are obtained as part of the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song|link}}" from {{TCB}} DLC Pack. They're gifted to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} by the {{Plural|BotW|Monk|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kiah Toza Shrine|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Noe Rajee Shrine|link}} once he completes their trials.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Medoh's Embelm.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> These Emblems are commemorative symbols of the completion of a wind trial.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Once Link has collected all three of Medoh's Emblems, the final Monk will instruct him to head to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Medoh...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Once there, the Emblems will be taken from him, and he will fight {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon|link}} again in the [[Illusory Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the one who approaches this Divine Beast... In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial. In exchange for Medoh's Emblems... you will be granted the chance to tread through the realm of memories. Those who lack determination will find this trial unforgiving. Do not take this place lightly nor dismiss it as merely a world within your mind. The truth is much deeper than you know.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, Link will be able to challenge Windblight Ganon at any time.
