{{Term/Store|EX Coliseum: Master||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 84 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Coliseum: Master|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the second wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian of Remembrance|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Coliseum: Master}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given 14 minutes to defeat five {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. Only one {{Term|HWAoC|Stalnox|link}} spawns at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, although a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Frost Talus|link}} spawns after it is killed. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} kills the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Frost Talus}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Lynel|link}} spawn. When they are killed, {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon}} spawns. When {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon}} is defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. 

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ruby|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Opal|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Durian|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Safflina|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
