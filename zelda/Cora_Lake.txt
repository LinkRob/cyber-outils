{{Term/Store|Cora Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Cora Lake'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Cora Lake}} is located south of the {{Term|BotW|Lake Tower|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Sea|link}}. It is surrounded by cliffs on all sides, leaving lower than the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}} nearby. The {{Term|BotW|Cora Lake|display=Lake}} is the source of the {{Term|BotW|Menoat River|link}}, which feeds into the {{Term|BotW|Faron Sea}}.

During the "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Garb of Winds|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, which is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is tasked with finding the [[Hero of Winds]]' garb, which was stolen from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The royal family of Hyrule collected historically significant outfits, but they were stolen by a bandit named Misko. One of the items stolen was the blue garb of the Hero of Winds.<br/><br/>Somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Deya Village Ruins}} is a journal that places the location of this treasure...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After reading {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2|link}}, he learns that it is located "where the {{Term|BotW|Menoat River}} was born."<ref>{{Cite|The blue garb of the hero who controlled the wind and travelled the ocean is {{Color|BotW Red|where the Menoat River was born}}.|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> In the cave opening on the west side of {{Term|BotW|Cora Lake}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find an EX {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the {{Term|BotW|Island Lobster Shirt|link}}, which can be pulled from the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found the treasure that was hidden at Cora Lake and obtained the {{Color|BotW Red|Island Lobster Shirt}}!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
Two {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found at {{Term|BotW|Cora Lake}}. The first can be found by completing the {{Term|BotW|Cube Pattern|link}} on its eastern cliff. The metal cube required to solve this {{Term|BotW|Cube Pattern|display=Pattern}} is beneath the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}, near the rock outcroppings. The second {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found under a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}}, which serves as a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}} overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Cora Lake|display=Lake}}'s western edge. After these {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} are located, they will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
