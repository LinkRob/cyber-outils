{{Term/Store|Teba's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 40 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Teba's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Teba's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}}, and more will spawn when {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}} reaches 80 and 160 KOs. Once 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} spawns alongisde {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}} and the time limit is taken away. The {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} must be defeated for the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} to end.
