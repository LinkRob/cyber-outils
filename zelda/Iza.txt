{{Term/Store|Iza||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 276}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TPHD|Iza}} is the owner and operator of the {{Term|TPHD|Boat Rental Cabin|link}} in the {{Term|TPHD|Lanayru Province|link}}, home of the {{Term|TPHD|Rapids Ride|link}} {{Term|TPHD|Mini-Game|link}}. Although she is nice and kind to [[Link]], her brother [[Coro]] mentions on how [[Wolf Link]] does not even compare to how scary she is.<ref name="Coro"/> Moreover, Iza's [[Zora]] part-time worker comments that she has a short temper, also having a tendency to bribe people with whatever is lying around.<ref>{{Cite|When we were closed, my boss's temper only got worse and worse... It was a rough time for us all...|Zora|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes, she tends to bribe people with whatever's lying around... So stingy, really...|Zora|TP}}</ref>

When Link first meets Iza, she will not let him rent a boat, saying that they are temporarily closed.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... A customer? Sorry, but we're temporarily closed.|Iza|TP}}</ref> However, after Link defeats the [[Shadow Beast]]s that appear near her {{Term|TPHD|Boat Rental Cabin|display=Cabin}}, she invites him inside.<ref>{{Cite|Well, what are we standing around jawing for? Come on inside!|Iza|TP}}</ref> She explains to him that she cannot rent boats because of an earlier rockfall damming up the river. Therefore, she asks Link for help.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I USED to rent boats here, but then we had a rockfall and it dammed the river up. Yeah... I could sure use a helpful guy around at times like this... Yup, a handy guy would sure be a big help around here.|Iza|TP}}</ref> She gives Link a [[Bomb Bag]] and [[Arrow]]s to make [[Bomb Arrow]]s to destroy the boulders.<ref>{{Cite|I'll give you some bombs and arrows, so you just put 'em together and shoot bomb arrows at that thing.|Iza|TP}}</ref>

During the ending credits, Iza, [[Hena]], and [[Coro]] can be seen together relaxing at the [[Fishing Hole]].

==Trivia==
*If Link breaks the yellow jars hanging inside the {{Term|TPHD|Boat Rental Cabin}}, {{Term|TPHD|Iza}} will demand that Link pays for them.<ref>{{Cite|COM! PEN! SA! TION! 10 Rupees!|Iza|TP}}</ref> She will also refuse service to Link until he agrees to pay 10 [[Rupee]]s for them.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I can keep this up indefinitely until you pay, so I hope you're ready for the long haul, pal!|Iza|TP}}</ref>
*After defeating the nearby [[Shadow Insect]] when the area is still covered in [[Twilight]], Iza mentions how she wishes for a shiny prince to come by and solve all her problems. She does so while looking towards the sky with her hands together, then slumping down shaking her head.<ref>{{Cite|Golly.. What am I going to do?! The water's back, and I still can't get back in business... I wish some shiny prince would just show up and fix it all... ...Ah well.|Iza|TP}}</ref>
