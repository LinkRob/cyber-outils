{{Term/Store|Danton||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Danton|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Danton}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who resides at {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}}. During the day, he will sit beside his {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} outside of the {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|display=Stable}}. He warns {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that he should make {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link|display=Cold Resistant}} {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} before heading into the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you plan to roam the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}}, you should make some food to help you withstand the extreme cold.|Danton|BotW}}</ref> He allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use his {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}, as leaving for such a journey unprepared would be unacceptable.<ref>{{Cite|Feel free to use my cooking pot outside. Going to a snowy mountain unprepared is unacceptable for a true adventurer!|Danton|BotW}}</ref> If it starts to snow, {{Term|BotW|Danton}} will retreat inside the {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|display=Stable}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Danton}}, he will talk about how great something must be before asking if he knows what he is talking about.<ref>{{Cite|It must be so great! You do know what I'm talking about, right?|Danton|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} explains that he does not know, {{Term|BotW|Danton}} reveals he is talking about the {{Term|BotW|Sword that Seals the Darkness|link}}, which legends state has been hidden somewhere in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Not a clue.] I'm talking about {{Color|BotW Red|the sword that seals the darkness}}, of course! Legend has it that it's hidden somewhere in Hyrule!|Danton|BotW}}</ref> He is enamored by the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|display=Sword}} and dreams of owning it.<ref>{{Cite|[Of course!] I knew you would! It's every dreamer's dearest dream, isn't it? Having a sword that shoots light all like WHAM and mows down all kinds of monsters like KAPOW would be amazing! I mean, even the name—{{Color|BotW Red|the sword that seals the darkness}}—is just the coolest thing ever!|Danton|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A single swing shoots out sacred light beams...mows down wicked monsters... and restores peace and stuff... Ahhhh, it's every dreamer's dearest dream come true, I tell ya!|Danton|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him again, {{Term|BotW|Danton}} will imagine what it would be like if he found the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}},<ref>{{Cite|What if I were the one to find {{Color|BotW Red|the sword that seals the darkness}}? Yeah, what if?! That'd be swell...|Danton|BotW}}</ref> claiming he would use it to defeat every monster in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains}}.<ref>{{Cite|I could use the sword's sacred light to beat the stuffing out of every single monster on the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}}!|Danton|BotW}}</ref> Even while sleeping, {{Term|BotW|Danton}} dreams about the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|display=Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Sword that...seals the darkness... Zzz...}}|Danton|BotW}}</ref>

After obtaining the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can show it to {{Term|BotW|Danton}}.<ref>{{Cite|[This sword here?] HUH?! That's the {{Color|BotW Red|sword the seals the darkness}}? I can't believe it!|Danton|BotW}}</ref> Once he calms down, {{Term|BotW|Danton}} will note that the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|display=Sword}} is not as big or extravagant as he thought it would be,<ref>{{Cite|Deep breaths... Deeeeep breaths... OK. I see, I see... Hmmm... Honestly, it's a tad slimmer than I imagined. Since it's the legendary sword, I thought it would be as big as me! With, like...jewels everywhere.|Danton|BotW}}</ref> but he still finds it amazing and says that he will never forget the day he saw it.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, it's still amazing, though. I can feel the power. Hey, thanks for showing that to me! I'll never forget this day!|Danton|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Danton}} again, he will state that according to legend, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is the chosen hero.<ref>{{Cite|If the legends are true, the {{Color|BotW Red|sword that seals the darkness}} is the blade of the chosen hero... Are YOU that hero?!|Danton|BotW}}</ref> In that scenario, {{Term|BotW|Danton}} makes {{Term|BotW|Link}} promise he will never give up, despite all of the challenges he will have to face.<ref>{{Cite|If so, you'll likely face many hardships in your life...but never give up!<br/>Promise me that.|Danton|BotW}}</ref>
