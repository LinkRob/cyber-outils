{{Term/Store|Manbo||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Manbo|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

==Biography==
Manbo is the child of the {{Term|LANS|Sun Fish|link}} and resides in a cave to the west of [[Angler's Tunnel]],<ref name="Manbo">{{Cite|I am Manbo, child of the Sun Fish! Have you got an Ocarina?|Manbo|LADX}}</ref> along with two smaller fish. If [[Link]] has an [[Ocarina]] when he talks to Manbo, he offers to perform the song known as "[[Manbo's Mambo]]."<ref name="Manbo"/><ref>{{Cite|Very well... Glub Blub Bloop!|Manbo|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ahaha! Then I can teach you my song! Bloop!|Manbo|LADX}}</ref> After the performance is over, Link learns the song on his Ocarina. If Link is outside of a Dungeon, the song will [[Warping|warp]] him to [[Manbo's Pond]], next to [[Crazy Tracy]]'s Shop. If Link is in a Dungeon, it will warp him to the entrance.<ref>{{Cite|I am Manbo, child of the Sun Fish! When you play my Mambo, you can warp to Manbo Pond! Try this tune in the dungeons, too! Cha-cha-cha!|Manbo|LADX}}</ref>
