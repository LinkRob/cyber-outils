The {{Name|Fan|Pillar Crossroads}} is a location featured in {{TCT}}. [[Princess Zelda]] stumbles into the crossroads after she leaves the [[Midoro Palace]]. Here, she finds tall stone pillars lying in a ring all around with strange writing on them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=After a little ways, the road veers left and runs into a large flat space. Tall stone pillars lie crumbling in a ring all around. 'It's a crossroads,' Zelda says. 'Those pillars are signposts. But I don't recognize the language they're written in.'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=17}}</ref> With three columns all pointing in different locations in which Zelda could go to, each column has a drawing pertaining to its area. For example, the road that leads into the [[Forest (The Crystal Trap)|Forest]] has a worn picture of trees and a deer.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=She walks up to one of the columns and sees a worn picture carved on its side. She can just make out trees and a [[deer]]. 'This road must lead into the forest,' she says to herself.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=30}}</ref>

The three roads that Zelda can take are the Forest, [[Desert (The Crystal Trap)|Desert]], or the [[Swamp (The Crystal Trap)|Swamp]]. Whichever path Zelda takes will lead her to obtain a [[Large Brass Key]]; however, the most recommended path is the Forest path, since there she will meet a [[Tree Witch]] who will give her the [[Witch's Ring]], required to obtain one of the three items necessary to free [[Link]] from the [[Crystal]] as dictated in the [[Fairy's Scroll]]: the [[Magnifying Glass]].
