{{Manual|EoW|Hyrule Castle Pot|An elegant pot from Hyrule Castle, clearly made with love and great effort.<ref name="Hyrule Castle Pot">{{Cite|An elegant pot from Hyrule Castle, clearly made with love and great effort.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Hyrule Castle Pots are objects primarily located within [[Hyrule Castle]] and the [[Hyrule Castle Underground]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the [[Echo]] after using the [[Tri Rod]] on one, allowing her to summon pots as needed. She can hide inside them to sneak past enemies or throw them to create distractions.
