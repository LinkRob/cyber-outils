{{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Bat}} are upgraded {{Plural|BotW|Boko Bat|link}} that {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} decided to attach sharp spikes to.<ref name="Inventory"/> Despite their simplicity, skilled fighters can cause a good amount of damage using these {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly used by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}. They also have a chance of appearing inside {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn after scanning the {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Bat|display=Bats}} are part considered {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}}, an equipment archetype that includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Due to being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Bat}} will float when they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. However, will burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. The do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, but they can not be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
