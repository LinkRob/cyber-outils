==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|OoA|Vine Sprout}} are sprouts that appear in the past on {{Term|OoA|Crescent Island|link}}. If {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} places a {{Term|OoA|Vine Sprout}} up beside a suitable wall, the {{Term|OoA|Vine Sprout}} will grow into {{Plural|OoA|Vine|link}} in the present.<ref name="Tokay"/><ref>{{Cite|It takes a long, long time for a tree to grow. The same goes for that vine sprout.|Tokay|OoA}}</ref> If left out in the open, they will wither and die instead.
