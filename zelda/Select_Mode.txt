The {{Term/Store|Select Mode||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE, CoH}} menu is a recurring interface in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|HWDE Red|Select Mode}}}}|N/A|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Select Mode}}|N/A|CoH}}</ref>


==Overview==
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Select Mode}} menu has 6 submenus and modes to choose from.{{Note|The {{Term|HWDE|Select Mode}} has 7 submenus if playing on {{HW|L}} or {{HW|DE}} as this adds the {{Term|HWDE|My Fairy|link}} menu.}} {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode|link}}, where the player can play the story of {{HW|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|Experience the story of legends.|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Free Mode|link}}, where the player can play any completed {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} from {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode}} with any {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}}.<ref>{{Cite|Replay any scenario with any unlocked warrior.|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}, where the player can play a version of {{HW|-}} similar to the original {{TLoZ}}.<ref>{{Cite|Find and defeat the Dark Ruler of Hyrule.|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Challenge Mode|link}}, where the player can take on a number of challenges with any {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}}.<ref>{{Cite|This mode challenges you with unusual battles!|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|My Fairy|link}}, a menu only found in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}} where the player can tend to their {{Plural|HWDE|Companion Fairy|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nurture and raise your own companion fairies.|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Gallery|link}}, where the player can view their unlocked collectables.<ref>{{Cite|View collectables.|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref> And finally, the {{Term|HWDE|Settings|link}} where the player can adjust the games settings to their preference.<ref>{{Cite|Adjust game settings.|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref>
