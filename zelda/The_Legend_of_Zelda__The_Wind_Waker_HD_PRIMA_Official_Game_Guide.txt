'''''The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD PRIMA Official Game Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{TWWHD}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 352 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{TWWHD}}, a listing of {{Term|TWWHD|Locations in The Wind Waker|display=islands|link}}, and a checklist of {{Plural|TWWHD|Item|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Song|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Mini-Game|link}}, and collectibles.
