The {{Term/Store|''History of the Zora, Part Six''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|History of the Zora, Part Six<br/>Divine Beast Vah Ruta<br/>As told by King Dorephan|History of the Zora, Part Six|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|History of the Zora, Part Six}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|link}} at the far end of {{Term|BotW|Ruto Mountain|link}}, towards {{Term|BotW|Rutala Dam|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must read it as part of the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}".

The story of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} is inscribed on this {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monument}}. When {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|display=Vah Ruta}} was first unearthed, {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} hurried to see it.<ref>{{Cite|When the Divine Beast Vah Ruta was first discovered in Zora's Domain, my daughter Mipha hurried to see it.|History of the Zora, Part Six|BotW}}</ref> Those who saw her that day saw a sparkle of excitement in her eyes, as opposed to her usual calm demeanor.<ref>{{Cite|Those present that day say they saw an unusual sparkle of excitement in Mipha's normally calm eyes as she beheld Ruta.|History of the Zora, Part Six|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} spoke to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|display=Vah Ruta}}, it was as though they were friends, and she was excited when she was chosen to pilot the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The princess spoke of the Divine Beast as she would a friend and was overjoyed when she was chosen to pilot Ruta.|History of the Zora, Part Six|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} later regretted this following the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I thought nothing of it at the time, but given the events that followed, I now regret allowing this to happen.|History of the Zora, Part Six|BotW}}</ref> He has been consumed by guilt, and he prays every day for {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}'s soul to know peace.<ref>{{Cite|I have spent many long years consumed by guilt. My dearest wish is that her soul will know peace. I pray for it every day.|History of the Zora, Part Six|BotW}}</ref>
