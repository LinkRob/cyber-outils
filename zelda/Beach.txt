The {{Term/Store|Beach||Series, CoH}} is a location in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Beach|Map|CoH}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|CoH|Beach}} is a region that appears along the edges of {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}, acting as the border between land and ocean. Though its placement is random, it will only appear on the eastern, southern, or western edges of the world and always directly adjacent to each other. They serve as the location of a beach hut, which houses a {{Term|CoH|Zora|link}} merchant who sells the {{Term|CoH|Snorkel|link}} and an {{Term|CoH|Bottle|link}}.

{{Plural|CoH|Sub-Boss}} such as {{Plural|CoH|Blue Daira|link}} and {{Plural|CoH|Daira|link}} can be found here.
