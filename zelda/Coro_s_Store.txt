{{Term/Store|Coro's Store||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 26}}</ref> Situated in the southern portion of the {{Term|TPHD|Faron Woods|link}}, it is a small {{Term|TPHD|Shop|link}} owned by {{Term|TPHD|Coro|link}} who sells {{Term|TPHD|Lantern Oil|link}} for just twenty {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}}. 

==Features and Overview==
When {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} first talks to {{Term|TPHD|Coro}} in his {{Term|TPHD|Shop}}, he will give the young hero a free {{Term|TPHD|Lantern|link}} filled with {{Term|TPHD|Lantern Oil}} to boost his sales.<ref>{{Cite|Here! Go on, guy. Take this! See, I sell lantern oil here... I'm trying to drum up sales by giving away free lanterns! It's a business tactic, guy!|Coro|TP}}</ref> Later on, {{Term|TPHD|Coro}} will offer the young hero a special offer of a {{Term|TPHD|Bottle|link}} filled with {{Term|TPHD|Lantern Oil}} for 100 {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|And check this out! I have a special, one-time offer of a bottle full of lantern oil for only 100 Rupees! How 'bout it, guy? What a bargain!|Coro|TP}}</ref> {{Term|TPHD|Link}} may speak to {{Term|TPHD|Coro}} from that point on to get his {{Term|TPHD|Lantern}} refilled for just twenty {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Your lantern's out of fuel, guy! If you don't refuel it, you'll regret it later! Trust me on this one. So I can fill it to the brim with oil for just 20 Rupees!|Coro|TP}}</ref>
