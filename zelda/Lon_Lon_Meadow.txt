{{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Lon Lon Meadow||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a location in the First Continent in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lon Lon Meadow|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The area is inhabited mostly by cows with alien faces, but also by walking mushrooms, [[Oinker]]s, [[Mole]]s and several other creatures. There is no dungeon in this area. This is the first area where [[Tingle]] encounters Oinkers. Tingle can find [[Missy (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|Missy]], who has lost her [[Girl's Glasses|Glasses]] here.

===Local Specialty===
An important ingredient in cooking, Sweet Potatoes, can be found here. Lon Lon Meadow's local specialty is [[Recipes#List of Recipes|Lon Lon Ketchup]]. The recipe for Lon Lon Ketchup is as follows:
*[[Aroma Toadstool]] X 2
*[[Crisp Tomato]] X 5
*[[Sweet Potato]] X 7
