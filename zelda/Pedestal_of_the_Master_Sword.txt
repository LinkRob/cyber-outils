The '''Pedestal of the [[Master Sword]]''',{{Exp Game|ALttP, OoT, TP, BotW}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Pedestal of the Master Sword |book= E |publisher= |page= 60 {{Exp Game|ALttP, OoT}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Pedestal of the Master Sword |book= E |publisher= |page= 58 {{Exp Game|OoT, TP}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 227}}</ref> also known as the '''Pedestal of Time''',{{Exp Game|OoT, TP}}<ref name="Chamber of Sages">{{Cite|I am {{Color|OoT3D Red|Rauru}}, one of the ancient Sages... Ages ago, we ancient Sages built the Temple of Time to protect the entrance to the Sacred Realm... This is the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Chamber of Sages}}, inside the Temple of Light... The Temple of Light, situated in the very center of the Sacred Realm, is the last stronghold against Ganondorf's evil forces. The {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Master Sword}}—the evil-destroying sword that you pulled out of the Pedestal of Time—was the final key to the Sacred Realm. Link... Don't be alarmed... Look at yourself...!|Rauru|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 27 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref> and the {{Term/Store|Sacred Pedestal||HW}},{{Exp Game|HW}}<ref>{{Cite|Pull the {{Color|HW Green|Master Sword}} from the {{Color|HW Green|Sacred Pedestal}}!|N/A|HW}}</ref> is a recurring {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is the sacred location in [[Hyrule]], often in the [[Lost Woods]] or [[Hyrule Castle]], where the Master Sword rests, until the time of destiny comes for [[Link]] to obtain it in order to save the land. The Pedestal of the Master Sword goes unnamed in {{TWW}}, {{SS}}, {{ALBW}}, {{AST}}, and the {{OoT|Himekawa}}. Like the Master Sword itself, the Pedestal makes its first appearance in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]''. 

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
The Pedestal of the Master Sword is situated in the [[Sacred Grove]] deep within the [[Lost Woods]] in {{ALttP}}. In order to draw the Sword, [[Link]] must prove himself worthy of wielding it by presenting the three [[Pendants of Virtue]], as instructed in the {{Term|ALttP|Mudoran|link}} text inscribed on the Pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|The Hero's triumph on Cataclysm's Eve wins three symbols of virtue. The Master Sword he retrieves, keeping the Knight's true line.|Pedestal of the Master Sword|ALttP}}</ref> In order to read this text, Link must use the [[Book of Mudora]] to translate the words into legible characters.

After defeating [[Ganon]] and saving [[Hyrule]], Link returns the Master Sword to its Pedestal in the hopes that it would rest forever.

==={{OoT|-}}===
The Pedestal of the Master Sword sits behind the [[Door of Time]] in the [[Temple of Time]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''. During the [[Era of Chaos]], [[Rauru]] constructed the Temple of Time, placing the Master Sword within its Pedestal. The Sword and its Pedestal were designed to seal the entrance to the [[Sacred Realm]], which housed the [[Triforce]].<ref name="Chamber of Sages"/> If the Master Sword is drawn from the Pedestal, the Temple of Time will connect with the [[Temple of Light]] at the heart of the Sacred Realm, allowing access to the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 26}}</ref>

Following the [[Hyrulean Civil War]], [[Ganondorf]] enacted a plot to enter the Temple of Light and steal the Triforce for his own desires. As he was unable to obtain the keys to the Door of Time, the [[Spiritual Stone]]s and [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]], he waited for Link to pass through the Door of Time and [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_MasterSword_Pedestal_Clank.wav draw] the Master Sword from its Pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|Geh heh heh! Excellent work! As I thought, you held the keys to the Door of Time! You have led me to the gates of the Sacred Realm... Yes, I owe it all to you, kid!|Ganondorf|OoT3D}}</ref> Due to Link's young body being incapable of wielding the Master Sword and therefore not yet worthy of his title as the "Hero of Time", his soul was held in the Sacred Realm for seven years.<ref name="Page 236">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 236}}</ref> When he eventually awoke as the legendary [[Hero of Time]], Rauru met with him and explained the dire status of [[Hyrule]], setting him on his quest to awaken the [[Sage]]s trapped in the various Temples across the land.<ref>{{Cite|Keep my spirit with you... Find the power of the other Sages, and add their might to your own!|Rauru|OoT3D}}</ref>

After Link frees [[Saria]] from the depths of the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}}, [[Sheik]] informs him that the Master Sword is capable of transporting him backwards through [[Time]] when it is [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_MasterSword_Pedestal_Slide.wav plunged back] into its Pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|You destroyed the wicked creatures that haunted the temple and awakened the Sage... But there are still other Sages who need your help. In order to awaken all the other Sages, you must become even more powerful. You must travel over mountains... under water... and even through time...|Sheik|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you want to return to your original time, return the Master Sword to the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Pedestal of Time}}. By doing this, you will travel back in time seven years...|Sheik|OoT3D}}</ref> He informs Link that in order to free all of the Sages, the young hero must make use of this ability. Specifically, Link must drive the Master Sword into the Pedestal in order to learn the "[[Song of Storms]]", obtain the [[Lens of Truth]] from the [[Bottom of the Well]], and retrieve the [[Silver Gauntlets]] from the [[Spirit Temple]].

In the branch of the [[Timeline]] that [[Ganon]] is defeated, [[Princess Zelda]] uses the Ocarina of Time to permanently send Link back to his original time seven years prior, placing him before the Master Sword in its Pedestal.<ref name="Page 236"/>

==={{TWW|-}}===
The Master Sword and its Pedestal both rest beneath [[Hyrule Castle]] in {{TWW}}. Inside the Pedestal, the Master Sword holds the Castle in perpetual stasis, sealing the evil that invaded during the [[Era without a Hero]]. After proving himself worthy of wielding the Blade of Evil's Bane in the [[Tower of the Gods]], Link travels to the sunken Castle and draws the Master Sword from its Pedestal. As both the Master Sword and its Pedestal served as the only seal on Ganondorf and his monsters, time began to flow as soon as the Master Sword was drawn.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 70}}</ref>

Unlike other games in the franchise, this Pedestal of the Master Sword does not inherently restore the [[Power to Repel Evil]] to the Blade of Evil's Bane, and thus it requires prayer from [[Sage]]s to imbue the Sword with the power of the gods.<ref>{{Cite|Link, I need you to lend me your strength in this dark hour. Without any Sages to offer prayers to the gods, the Master Sword slowly lost its power as it sat within the Pedestal of the Master Sword.<ref>{{Cite|Link, listen well... ==={{TP|-}}===
The Pedestal of the Master Sword sits in the ruins of the [[Temple of Time (Dungeon)|Temple of Time]] in the [[Sacred Grove]] in {{TP}}. After Link is cursed to permanently be in [[Wolf Link|Wolf]] form, Princess Zelda instructs him to travel to the Sacred Grove to find the Master Sword.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 274}}</ref> As the Master Sword can banish evil magic, the curse on the Hero of Twilight is dispelled immediately as he approaches the Pedestal of the Master Sword, enabling him to withdraw the Sword. After passing through the [[Door of Time]] into the past, Link can temporarily return the Master Sword to its Pedestal in order to access the depths of the Temple of Time.

Following [[Ganondorf]]'s defeat and the destruction of the [[Mirror of Twilight]], the Master Sword is returned to its Pedestal in the Sacred Grove.

==={{SS|-}}===
After [[Link]] successfully defeats [[Demise]], the latter is sealed away within the [[Master Sword]]. With her role fulfilled, a Pedestal appears within the [[Temple of Hylia]] in the distant past and [[Fi]] requests that her servitude to the [[Hylia's Chosen Hero|Legendary Hero]] be concluded by means of driving the Sword into the Pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|Master Link, you have successfully protected the goddess reborn and defeated Demise, fulfilling your role as the {{Color|SS Red|hero}} of legend. My purpose here is complete. Therefore, I ask you to dissolve our arrangement as master and servant.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Drive the sword into the pedestal before you, and I will return to the sword to enter a sleep without end.|Fi|SS}}</ref> This allows Fi to rest indefinitely within the Master Sword, officially marking the end of [[Hylia]]'s mission.<ref>{{Cite|Master, you have achieved the purpose you were chosen to fulfill. Please, set the sword in the pedestal and bring the goddess's mission to an end.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Now, Master. It is time to conclude our necessary companionship.|Fi|SS}}</ref> The Pedestal of the Master Sword remains undisturbed through to the end of the Sky Era, containing Demise and Fi's sleeping spirit within the Master Sword. However, at some point, the curse set by Demise allows his evil to escape, eventually allowing him to be reborn and escape from the Master Sword's seal.

==={{ALBW|-}}===

Claim what is rightfully yours!|Sahasrahla|ALBW}}</ref> As in the [[Era of Light and Dark]], in order to draw the Master Sword from its Pedestal, the three [[Pendants of Virtue]] must be presented to the Pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|Now that you have all three {{Color|ALBW Blue|Pendants of Virtue}}, you have proven yourself worthy of the {{Color|ALBW Blue|Master Sword}}.|Sahasrahla|ALBW}}</ref> [[Sahasrahla]] tasks Link with the duty of finding the Pendants of Virtue and the Pedestal of the Master Sword in order to break the evil [[Barrier]] surrounding [[Hyrule Castle]].<ref>{{Cite|Listen well, Link. After wishing upon [[Hyrule]]'s [[Triforce]] to restore [[Lorule]]'s Triforce, Link returns to the Sacred Grove to place the Master Sword back in its Pedestal once more.

==={{BotW|-}}===
The Pedestal of the Master Sword is located in the [[Korok Forest]] deep within the [[Lost Woods]] in {{BotW}}. It sits beneath the [[Great Deku Tree]], where it draws from the energy of the land to restore the [[Master Sword]] after it is damaged.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The Master Sword has averted many calamities alongside the hero, and the pedestal that restores the sword from the damage it incurs in those battles is located in Korok Forest, which brims with the energy of the land. Perhaps by design of the Goddess, beside the pedestal a single young cherry tree grew. The young tree bathed in the energy flowing through the land and, after an eternity had passed became the guardian of the blade. How many sacred maidens and heroes opposing countless calamities has this ancient tree witnessed? |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 229}}</ref>

At around the age of 12 or 13, Link made his way to the Korok Forest and drew the Master Sword from its Pedestal.<ref name="Page 376">{{Cite Book|quote= It is said that the legendary Master Sword can only be wielded by a hero chosen by the sword itself, and its whereabouts had long been a mystery. The details of how Link obtained the sword a hundred years ago have been lost to the mists of time, but since he was in possession of it for a number of years prior to becoming a Champion, he was likely around twelve or thirteen years old when it happened. |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 376}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|After 100 years, I'd nearly given up hope on seeing you again.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref>

Just after the start of the second [[Great Calamity]], Link fell in battle outside [[Fort Hateno]] and the Master Sword sustained great damage from fighting against [[Guardian]]s. The Master Sword then spoke to [[Princess Zelda]], informing her that the [[Hylian]] [[Champion]] could still yet be saved if he is put in the [[Slumber of Restoration]] in the [[Shrine of Resurrection]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= A voice from within the Master Sword called out to Princess Zelda after Link collapsed. Zelda was able to hear it because her power had awoken. The sword offered guidance and helped the princess save Link from the brink of death. Though Zelda knew Link would likely lose his memories in the healing process, the sword persuaded her to send the hero to the Shrine of Resurrection. |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 388}}</ref> Instructing [[Sheikah]] servants to transport the near-lifeless hero there in her stead, Zelda made her way to the Korok Forest to place it into its Pedestal once so that both the Sword and its master would be revitalized.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 389}}</ref>

After awakening from the Slumber of Restoration, Link can return to the Korok Forest and attempt to withdraw the Master Sword from its Pedestal once more. However, as wielding the Master Sword requires great physical strength, Link is incapable of drawing the Sword from its Pedestal until he has at least 13 [[Heart]]s.

Provided [[The Master Trials]] DLC Pack is downloaded, after completing the [[Main Quest]], "[[The Hero's Sword]]", Link will be telepathically contacted by a [[Monk]] who will invite him to the Korok Forest to test the Champion's courage.<ref>{{Cite|Chosen one... The hero called Link... The time has come. You are now ready to test the limits of your courage. At the place where you reunited with the Master Sword, the sacred Korok Forest... Go there and heed the Great Deku Tree's words. Do so, and a glorious trial will be bestowed upon you.|Sword Monk|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "[[EX Trial of the Sword]]" [[Side Quest]]. If Link follows the Monk's advice, the Great Deku Tree will greet him and surmise that Link has been summoned for the [[Trial of the Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|Hm? What's this? A voice beckoned you here? I see. So the time has come. I shall now tell you all I know about the arduous trial that awaits you.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> The Great Deku Tree will explain that in order to fully awaken the Master Sword's power, Link must prove his courage in this Trial.<ref>{{Cite|That sword you have reunited with...the Master Sword...it has yet to realize its true splendor.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This trial shall test your courage to ensure you are worthy of this splendor. It is called the Trial of the Sword. It will challenge you as you have never been challenged before.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> Recognizing that Link has accepted the challenge, the Great Deku Tree will advise the Hero of the Wild to plunge the Master Sword back into its Pedestal to visit the [[Sword Monk's Shrine]].<ref>{{Cite|...Hm. Unless my eyes deceive me, you seem quite willing to take on this trial. A hero through and through! Now, Link... Place the Master Sword in its pedestal.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> By completing each stage of the Trial of the Sword, the Master Sword will grow in strength.<ref>{{Cite|To the one chosen by the sacred blade... I commend your efforts in this trial. You are now strong enough to wield more of the Master Sword's power than ever before. Courageous hero...chosen by the blade... Take hold of the Master Sword.|Sword Monk|BotW}}</ref> Replicas of the Pedestal of the Master Sword can be found at the end of each Trial inside the Sword Monk's Shrine. By withdrawing the Master Sword from them, Link can be returned to the Korok Forest to claim the newly-strengthened Master Sword.

==Trivia==
[[File:ALTTP-Master Sword3.png|thumb|234x234px|The Master Sword on its pedestal at the ending of ''A Link to the Past'']]
*The [[Master Sword]] resting on its pedestal for [[Link]] to obtain it for important manners and then putting it back once he accomplishes his mission since ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' is widely known as one of the most iconic scenes in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]. Many fans consider it to be a modern retelling of the [[wikipedia:Excalibur|Excalibur]] mythical sword told in British legends of King Arthur by [[Nintendo]].
*Four pedestals similar to the Pedestal of Time appear in the [[Palace of the Four Sword]], the dungeon exclusive to the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|Game Boy Advance version of ''A Link to the Past'']].
*In ''A Link to the Past'', when Link must search for the Master Sword, [[Fake Master Sword|fake blades on pedestals]] are found throughout [[Lost Woods]], resembling the actual Blade of Evil's Bane.[[File:OOT Master Sword landing perfectly on ground near Zelda.png|thumb|247x247px|The Master Sword at a very similar position as when on its pedestal after being knocked by Ganon in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|remake]] as well as its [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|manga adaptation]], during the final battle against [[Ganon]] at [[Ganon's Castle]], although somewhat unrealistic, when the Master Sword was knocked into the air, it is seen swinging sideways and then lands onto the ground near [[Princess Zelda]] behind the fire barrier, with the blade hitting squarely at an identical position as if it was on its pedestal, which was met with shock by fans. This is the first time in the franchise where Link was to lose a [[Sword]] by an enemy.
**A similar case in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'' is seen when [[Ganondorf]] rushes and knocks Link out before its own final battle, where the weapon flies into the air and nearly lands on Zelda's head, but the Master Sword didn't hit the ground perfectly straight but rather at an angle.
**Coincidentally, in the ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'' fighting game by Namco, when Link or other playable characters defeat its main antagonist Inferno, the titular [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Calibur Soul Calibur], a spirit sword with similar powers to that of the Master Sword and [[Four Sword]], can be seen falling to the volcanic ground in a downward position like the Blade of Evil's Bane from ''Ocarina of Time''.
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', the cutscene of Link drawing the Master Sword will always show him in his default [[Green Clothes|green attire]] and no [[Iron Boots]], regardless of whatever he was previously wearing when last human. His shield, however, will change depending on whichever the player last had equipped.
*During development of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'', the Master Sword's pedestal from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'' was used instead of the [[Rock]]s where [[Korok]]s hide.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Can you guess the original concept behind this stone object? [...] That stone object was actually the pedestal where the Master Sword was located in The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword for Wii.|author= |published= |retrieved= June 25, 2017|url= http://zelda.com/breath-of-the-wild/news/dlc-pack-one-countdown-korok-mask/|title= Countdown to DLC Pack 1: The Master Trials|site= Zelda.com|type=}}</ref>
*In ''A Link to the Past'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', as well as ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|BS Ancient Stone Tablets]]'', at the ending of these games, when the Master Sword in its upgraded forms, the [[Master Sword Lv2|Tempered]] or [[Master Sword Lv3|Golden Sword]], is returned to its pedestal, for unknown reasons, the blade reverts back to its standard shape.
*Although unseen, the Master Sword is shown being pulled from its pedestal by Link in the opening scene of ''SoulCalibur II'' ([[Nintendo GameCube]] version only).
*In the beta version of ''Ocarina of Time'', Link was originally going to have a more difficult time pulling the Master Sword off its pedestal as a child.
*The music jingle of whenever Link opens a large [[Treasure Chest]] in the 3D games is a heavily remixed version of the famous theme of the Master Sword being obtained by the main protagonist.
*When Child Link pulls the Master Sword out of its pedestal in ''Ocarina of Time'', the [[Triforce]] symbol on the floor can be seen glowing.
*Although not technically its pedestal, in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', when Link obtains the Master Sword, he pulls it from the [[Light Dragon]]'s head (who is actually Princess Zelda under [[Draconification|draconifcation]]) in a very similar manner as seen in its prequel ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]''. Both of those cases had Link strain with difficulty.
*Due to ''Ocarina of Time''<nowiki/>'s time-traveling gameplay mechanic and story, this title remains the only game in the franchise where the theme of the Master Sword can be heard many times if chosen by the player.
**''The Wind Waker'' has it <nowiki/>played three times, when the Master Sword is obtained and restoring its [[Power to Repel Evil]] after completing the [[Earth Temple|Earth]] and [[Wind Temple]].
*If cheats are used to make Link bypass the [[Soldier]] guards at the very beginning of ''A Link the Past'' and immediately enters the [[Lost Woods]], the pedestal of the Master Sword has the legendary blade itself missing and cannot even read the [[Mudoran]] inscription, due to the fact the player is not supposed to be there this early.
