'''''How to Draw The Legend of Zelda''''' is a book containing step-by-step instructions on how to draw various characters and enemies from the ''Legend of Zelda'' series. All of the character poses in the book are based on official illustrations from {{OoT}} and {{MM}}. The book also contains an introductory section with some basic background information on the ''Legend of Zelda'' series and the premise of the book.

The following characters are included in ''How to Draw The Legend of Zelda'': [[Kaepora Gaebora]], [[Princess Zelda]], [[Saria]], [[Young Link]], Adult [[Link]], "Link in Action", [[Ganondorf]], [[Hylian]] [[Soldier]], [[King Dodongo]], a [[Goron]], [[Queen Gohma]], a [[Tektite]], Link as a [[Deku Scrub]], [[Volvagia]], and a [[Lizalfos]].
