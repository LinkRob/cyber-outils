As in the grand majority of video games, '''voice acting''' is an integral part of the plot, providing a human-like interface and relatable sound to in-game characters and beings. {{TLoZ|Series}}, unlike most other [[Nintendo]] franchises, is known for its overall lack of voice acting, with the exception of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'' and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|sequel]]. However, small voice clips recorded by Japanese voice actors and actresses are used for main and non-player characters since {{OoT}} for the [[Nintendo 64]]. Since these voice clips contain few legitimate words, most of them are kept for all language releases. Non-canon media occasionally contains full voice acting, which is dubbed over for different language releases. The earliest games prior to ''Ocarina of Time'', due to hardware limitations, have certain characters only possibly heard by taking damage or laughing, which used generic sound effects.

==Link==
The main protagonist, [[Link]], is perhaps the most famous in the franchise in terms of voice acting. For the most part, Link himself does not speak, but rather grunts, exclaims, and makes other throttal sounds during different situations, whether it be in battle or in conversation, similar to certain other central Nintendo protagonists in various franchises such as [[Mario]], [[metroidwiki:Samus_Aran|Samus]], [[pokemon:Red|Red]], and few others. Since the debut of the series in 1986 with ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' on the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]], Link has yet to receive proper voice acting, as he does not talk even in the most recent installments. However, the exclamations Link provides during combat are in fact real human voices.

[[File:Fujiko Takimoto.webp|thumb|113x113px|Fujiko Takimoto, the voice actress of the young Hero of Time]]
'''Fujiko Takimoto''' is the original voice actress that voiced [[Young Link]], [[Deku Link]] and [[Zora Link]] in ''Ocarina of Time'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', and ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'', as well as later young incarnations of Link from {{FS}}, {{TMC}} and the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake of {{ALttP}} (due to her voice clips from ''Ocarina of Time'' being reused). She also voiced {{Wp|Taki (Soul Calibur)|Taki}} in the ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'' for the Japanese language.
[[File:Nobuyuki Hiyama.webp|thumb|133x133px|Nobuyuki Hiyama, the voice actor of the adult Hero of Time|left]]
'''Nobuyuki Hiyama''' is the original voice actor that voiced Adult Link and [[Dark Link]] in ''Ocarina of Time'', ''Super Smash Bros.'', ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'', and ''SoulCalibur II''. He is also credited in voicing [[Fierce Deity Link]] in {{MM}}, as well as {{Wp|Nightmare|Nightmare/Siegfried Schtauffen}} and {{Wp|Yoshimitsu}} in the [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_series ''Soulcalibur'' series] for the Japanese language. Because Link in {{OoT|-}} required different speech registers given two time periods unlike other games in the franchise, a unique voice actor was used for each era of the [[Hero of Time]]'s life in the [[Nintendo 64]] title, with Takimoto and Hiyama, respectively.

[[File:P9741-6.jpg|thumb|Sachi Matsumoto, the voice actress of the Hero of Winds]]
Link was given much more expression in {{TWW}}, given the art style and the storyline, and hence was given a new voice than that of {{OoT|-}}. '''Sachi Matsumoto''' is the voice actress who voiced Link in {{TWW}}, him and [[Shadow Link]] in {{FSA}}, {{PH}}, and [[Toon Link]] in {{SSBB}}. She also voiced the [[Skull Kid]] in ''Majora's Mask'' as well as [[Aryll]] in ''The Wind Waker''.

[[File:P7666-5.jpg|thumb|Akira Sasanuma, the voice actor of the Hero of Twilight|left]]
{{TP}} is a title that received yet another voice actor by the name of '''Akira Sasanuma''' to portray Link, also in {{SSBB|-}}. '''Takashi Ōhara''' voiced Link in {{SS}}. '''Mitsuki Saiga''' voiced Link (as well as [[Ravio]], due to his parallel relationship to Link) in {{ALBW}}.

In non-canon media, '''Jonathan Potts''' is the voice actor that portrays Link in {{TLoZ|TV Series}}, while '''Jeffrey Rath''' provides actual speech to the young hero during the infamous cutscenes of the unsuccessful [[Philips CD-i]] titles, {{TFoE}} and {{TWoG}}. In {{HW}}, the adult Link was voiced by '''Yūki Kaji'''.

==Princess Zelda==
[[File:Jun Mizusawa.png|thumb|112x112px|Jun Mizusawa, the original voice actress of Princess Zelda]]
In terms of [[Princess Zelda]], the list of voice actors is much smaller, mainly because aside from significantly less screen time or action compared to Link in most games, '''Jun Mizusawa''' provided voice acting for the princess not only in {{OoT|-}}, but {{TP|-}}, {{SSBM|-}}, and {{SSBB|-}}. Mizusawa is also credited with the voicing of [[Sheik]] and [[Malon]] in {{OoT|-}}.

{{TWW|-}} changes to '''Hikari Tachibana''' as the voice of Princess Zelda/[[Tetra]].

'''Yu Shimamura''' voiced Zelda in {{SS|-}}, and reprises the role in the Japanese version of {{BotW|-}} and ''Tears of the Kingdom'', this time speaking actual words. In other versions of {{BotW|-}}, Zelda has been dubbed by '''Patricia Summersett''' (English), '''Jessica Ángeles''' (Mexican Spanish), '''Nerea Alfonso''' (European Spanish), '''Adeline Chetail''' (French), and '''Julia Casper''' (German).

'''Ayumi Fujimura''' gives her voice in {{ALBW|-}}.

In terms of non-canon media, '''Cyndy Preston''' provides Zelda with legitimate words in ''The Legend of Zelda'' TV series, as does '''Bonnie Jean Wilbur''' in the Philips CD-i titles, ''Link: The Faces of Evil'' and ''Zelda: The Wand of Gamelon''. In {{HW|-}}, she is voiced by ''Saori Seto''.

==Ganon/Ganondorf==
[[File:Takashi Nagasako.jpg|thumb|153x153px|Takashi Nagasako, the original voice actor of Ganondorf]]
The voice acting for [[Ganon]] is even more slim than Princess Zelda, given his own screen time in every ''Zelda'' title to date being particularly narrow and most games have the main antagonist simply roar or laugh. As his human form, [[Ganondorf]], for the most part, '''Takashi Nagasako''' provided the voice of the [[Gerudo]] warlock in ''Ocarina of Tim''e, ''The Wind Waker'', ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'', and ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''. Nagasako also provides the voice of the infamous [[Iron Knuckle (Boss)|Iron Knuckle]] and indirectly the [[Poe]] and [[Garo Master]] enemies in Nintendo 64 ''Zelda'' games (the latter two has their laugh being a sped up version of Ganondorf's). In ''Twilight Princess'' and ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', he was played by '''Hironori Miyata''', and '''Len Carlson''' takes over the voice of the Evil King in the [[The Legend of Zelda (television series)|TV series]]. '''Matthew Mercer''' takes on the role of Ganondorf in ''Tears of the Kingdom'' (English).

==Midna==
[[File:P12603-1.jpg|thumb|Akiko Koumoto, the voice actress of Minda|left]]
'''Akiko Koumoto''' is the Japanese voice actress who voiced the imp-like ''Twilight Princess'' character [[Midna]]. Strangely, Midna herself only speaks gibberish. Some have been able to make words out of Midna's gibberish, although these are strings of words without any meaning. It has been speculated by fans that Midna's in-game speech is indeed English, but with all the words mixed and reformatted to make it sound like gibberish.

==Non-Canon Media==
===Other Voices in the TV Series===
Because ''The Legend of Zelda'' TV series enabled voice acting across the board, characters like Link, Zelda, and even objects like the [[Triforce of Power]] and [[Triforce of Wisdom|Wisdom]] are given speech where they otherwise would not, had it been a video game. '''Elizabeth Hanna''' and '''Allan Stewart Coates''' are the actors who give words to the Triforce of Power and Wisdom, respectively, and '''Colin Fox''' is the man providing the voice to [[King of Hyrule|King Harkinian]].

===Other Voices in the Philips CD-i Titles===
Other voice actors credited with contributing their words to characters from ''Link: The Faces of Evil'' and ''Zelda: The Wand of Gamelon'' are '''Jeffrey Nelson, Mark Berry, Natalie Brown, Karen Grace, Josie McElroy, Marguerite Scott''' and '''Paul Wann'''.

== Trivia ==

* Among [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]' community, although every voice actor/actress in the core series are widely praised, the performances of Fujiko Takimoto and Nobuyuki Hiyama for [[Link]], Jun Mizusawa for [[Princess Zelda]], and Takashi Nagasako for [[Ganon]] are seen as the most iconic in the franchise and other media.
* Alongside playing Taki from the ''Soul'' series, Fujiko Takimoto is also known as the Japanese voice actress of [https://hellokitty.fandom.com/wiki/Badtz-Maru Badtz-Maru] and [https://hellokitty.fandom.com/wiki/Lloromannic Cherry] from Sanrio's [https://hellokitty.fandom.com/f ''Hello Kitty''] series. In addition, she also voiced young [[pokemon:Mewtwo|Mewtwo]] from the [[pokemon:Pokémon_(franchise)|''Pokémon'']] franchise, Suguru Misato from ''Mahoromatic'', young [https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi Asuma Sarutobi] from the [https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_(series) ''Naruto''] series, and more.
* Nobuyuki Hiyama, due to his famous roles as heroic protagonists in his career, is not only widely recognized as the voice of Adult Link in the [[Nintendo 64]] ''Zelda'' games, but also his portrayal of [https://yuyuhakusho.fandom.com/wiki/Hiei Hiei] from the [https://yuyuhakusho.fandom.com/wiki/YuYu_Hakusho ''YuYu Hakusho'' series], Gai Shishioh from ''King of Braves GaoGaiGar'', and more.
* In addition to Ganondorf, Takashi Nagasako is also a longtime former voice actor of [[mariowiki:Donkey_Kong_(character)|Donkey Kong]] from the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise as well as the Japanese voice of [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Big_the_Cat Big the Cat] from Sega's [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_series ''Sonic the Hedgehog''] series.
* ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'' is widely believed by the fandom that it is the first mainline game to have Link speak words in any form. On the contrary, in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', when the [[Hero of Time]] is defeated and the player receives a [[Game Over]], the main protagonist can actually be heard saying "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_AdultLink_Choke2.wav ''Yuck!'']" as he collapses, predating the [[Nintendo GameCube]] title. In addition, when he takes fall damage as an adult, he also appears to be stammering in both anger and pain.
* At one point of development for ''Ocarina of Time'', [[mariowiki:Charles_Martinet|Charles Martinet]], the longtime original voice of [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and few other characters in the ''Super Mario'' series, requested [[Shigeru Miyamoto]] to be the voice of Link, in which the creator of both flagship [[Nintendo]] video game franchises refused the offer, as he doesn't want ''The Legend of'' ''Zelda''<nowiki/>'s main character to have a speaking role in the core series like Mario does.
* There is a speculation by fans that Sachi Matsumoto voices the [[Gerudo]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]].'' For example, when they are heard screaming when they're chased by [[Giant Bee|Giant Bees]] or sucked by the dragon cloud at [[Great Bay Temple]], they sound very similar to Link the Hero of Winds. This is however not confirmed by Nintendo.
* The majority of voice clips of Link's [[Transformation|transformations]] (except [[Goron Link]]) and his evil shadow [[Dark Link]] in the N64 titles were reused by Fujiko Takimoto and Nobuyuki Hiyama for the hero himself but are altered to fit the characters.
