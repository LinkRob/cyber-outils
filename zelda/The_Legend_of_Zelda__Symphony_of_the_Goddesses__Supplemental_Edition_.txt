'''''The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses (Supplemental Edition)''''' is a sheet music book released by Alfred. It serves as an extension to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses (Sheet Music)|Symphony of the Goddesses]] sheet music book, with music from {{SS}} and {{BotW}}. It contains 28 pages and seven total songs across two new movements.
