The {{Term/Store|Sha Warvo Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Sha Warvo Shrine}}<br/>Path of Hidden Winds}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Path of Hidden Winds''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Sha Warvo Shrine is located on {{Term|BotW|Dronoc's Pass|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, south of the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sha Warvo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Sha Warvo. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Sha Warvo|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Sha Warvo Shrine features several platforms with fans floating above a chasm. Using the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} above the fans allows Link to glide upwards and over to other platforms.

A {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}} is waiting on a small platform, along with a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} to the left of the second fan. After gliding up the third fan, a moving platform will back and forth from a ladder to the next platform with a fan.

The platform with the fifth fan is surrounded by a tall wall. The fan can be accessed by a small hole in the wall farthest away from the platform with the Monk.

In order to complete the Trial, Link must simply glide over to the platform where Sha Warvo waits. He will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Sha Warvo|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Sha Warvo|BotW}}</ref>

On a small platform far away from all other platforms and near the top of the Shrine is a Treasure Chest containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bow|link}}.
