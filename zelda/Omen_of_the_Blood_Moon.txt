==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|Omen of the Blood Moon}}" plays whenever a {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon|link}} is in the sky from 11:30PM to 12:00AM. It also plays during the {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}, "{{Term|Series|A Premonition|link}}".

"{{Term|BotW|Omen of the Blood Moon}}" is track 12 in Disc 4 of {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Original Soundtrack|link}}.
