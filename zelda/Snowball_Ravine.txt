{{Term/Store|Snowball Ravine||Series, TFH}} is a {{Term|TFH|Level|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=160 }}</ref> It is the second {{Term|TFH|Level}} in the {{Term|TFH|Ice Cavern}} area.

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|TFH|Snowball Ravine}} is a snow-covered, mountainous landscape, so named because of the large {{Plural|TFH|Snowball|link}} that roll down the sides of the mountain. Littered across the ravine are snow mounds, which may be cut by the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}, or melted with the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves|link}} obtained in the {{Term|TFH|Level|link}}. The mounds may contain {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}}, {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}, or {{Plural|TFH|Enemy|link}} such as {{Plural|TFH|Rat|link}}. The giant {{Plural|TFH|Snowball}} can likewise also be melted away with the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}}. Similarly, there are gigantic frozen boulders as well that roll down the ravine, however these cannot be melted. The {{Term|TFH|Level}} also features icy walkways over bottomless pits that provide little traction, making the {{Term|TFH|Cozy Parka|link}} helpful in this {{Term|TFH|Level}}.

In '''{{Term|TFH|Stage|link}} 1''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} begin at a small area leading up to several frozen stairways. Giant, frozen {{Plural|TFH|Boulder|link}} will occasionally roll down these stairs. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can find {{Term|TFH|Boomerang|link}} and a pair of {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}} further up the stairs. Another set of stairs to the east leads to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}}. Giant {{Plural|TFH|Snowball}} and frozen {{Plural|TFH|Boulder}} will roll down these stairs as well, but the former can be melted away with the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}}. At the top of the stairs are three ledges that the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must cross by forming a {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}} and throwing each other across. The {{Term|TFH|Links}} who is left behind can be reunited with the others by throwing the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang}} at him to carry him over. The {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} is found at the end.

In '''{{Term|TFH|Stage}} 2''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} appear over a deep chasm. A pair of {{Plural|TFH|Torch|link}} must be lit to activate a moving, ice bridge. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} then walk across an icy walk way, and choose either the left or right paths by throwing themselves across. On the left side is a snowy ledge with two {{Plural|TFH|Switch|link}}, which lowers an obscuring platform in front of unlit {{Plural|TFH|Torch}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must then light these {{Plural|TFH|Torch}} using the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}} and the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang}} to carry flames over. On the right side is an icy platform where {{Plural|TFH|Hardhat Beetle|link}} attempt to push them off. A {{Term|TFH|Bomb Flower|link}} grows on this platform, which must be thrown to destroy a cracked block atop a tall {{Term|TFH|Torch}}. This requires that the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} quickly form a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} to reach. Once destroyed, the top {{Term|TFH|Links}} can then light the two {{Plural|TFH|Torch}} with the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}}. Doing both tasks activates the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} and creates an ice bridge to it.

'''{{Term|TFH|Stage}} 3''' is a snowy mountain road where giant {{Plural|TFH|Snowball}} roll down the path. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must walk this road while avoiding the {{Plural|TFH|Snowball}} and {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}} along the way. Eventually the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} come to a stairway that goes up, but has {{Plural|TFH|Snowball}} rolling down in their direction. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must move up this path by melting any {{Plural|TFH|Snowball}} that come down. Moving west, the {{Plural|TFH|Link}} again avoid {{Plural|TFH|Snowball}} and frozen {{Plural|TFH|Boulder}} while making their way through. At the end is a lift that can be activated by lighting an overhead {{Term|TFH|Torch}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} then move eastward past more {{Plural|TFH|Snowball}} and {{Plural|TFH|Boulder}} to where the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} is.

'''{{Term|TFH|Stage}} 4''' is where {{Term|TFH|Freezlord|link}} is fought. Icicles can be melted prior to the fight for {{Plural|TFH|Heart}}.
