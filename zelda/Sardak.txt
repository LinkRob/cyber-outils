The '''Sardaks''' are the mini-bosses of the {{Term|RTBToL|Shrine of Earth|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= The South Sardak can only be killed with the Jade Ring.|author=|published=|retrieved=|url= http://www.cdinteractive.co.uk/forum/viewtopic.php?t=22&sid=3ae463a956cde8573ee3c34902be9c9e|title= Philip's CD-i Forum|site= CDinteractive.co.uk}}</ref>

They serve {{Term|RTBToL|Llort|link}}, and are invulnerable to every weapon except for the {{Term|RTBToL|Spells of Zelda's Adventure|section=Jade Ring|display=Jade Ring|link}}. {{Term|RTBToL|Princess Zelda|link}} must defeat every one of them to remove the {{Term|RTBToL|Barrier|link}} in front of {{Term|RTBToL|Llort}}'s room. They come in three colors: Blue, Red and Yellow, with Blue being the weakest (taking 2 hits to defeat) and Red and Yellow being the strongest (taking 4 hits each to defeat).
