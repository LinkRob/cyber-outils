The {{Term/Store|Gorman Track||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 246}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Gorman Track is a racetrack run by the [[Gorman Brothers]] situated near [[Milk Road]]. Only horses can be raced on the track, and so [[Link]] cannot race there without [[Epona]].<ref>{{Cite|You know what this place is? This is the Gorman Brothers' horse training center! You got no business here if you got no horse. Now beat it! Scram!|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> The prize for winning the race  is the [[Garo's Mask]], won only if Link can beat both Gorman Brothers in a race.

After Link helps [[Romani]] save the cows from the [[Ghost (Majora's Mask)|Ghost]]s and accepts to accompany [[Cremia]] in her [[milk]] delivery to [[Clock Town]],<ref>{{Cite|I'm going by wagon into town. Would you like a ride? I'll be leaving about 6 o'clock this evening, so you can join me if you'd like.|Cremia|MM}}</ref> the ranch owner and Link discover that the road to town has been blocked, most likely by the Gorman Brothers. As such, they are forced to take a detour through the Gorman Track.<ref>{{Cite|First Milk Road is blocked by a boulder and now... We have to take this detour through ugly country...|Cremia|MM}}</ref> The Gorman Brothers, wearing Garo's Masks while riding on their horses, chase the wagon and try to break the cargo of milk bottles that Cremia is trying to deliver to the [[Milk Bar]].<ref>{{Cite|Are you ready, boy? I'm going to try to get us through here as fast as I can! If any pursuers come from behind, chase them off with your arrows. They may be after my cargo of milk bottles.|Cremia|MM}}</ref> Link then chases them off using his [[Bow]] and [[Arrow]]s. If he succeeds, he is rewarded with [[Romani's Mask]] from Romani herself.

The Gorman Brothers also sell milk for 50 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Won'tcha buy some fresh milk? [...] 50 Rupees will do ya for one
drink!|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> In {{MM3D}}, Link can receive a [[Bottle]] with [[Mystery  Milk]] from them after talking to [[Gorman]] during the [[Second Day]]. Link must then deliver the milk to Gorman in less than two minutes so he can keep the empty Bottle.
