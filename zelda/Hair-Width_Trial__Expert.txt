{{Term/Store|Hair-Width Trial: Expert||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 54 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hair-Width Trial: Expert|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 


==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Hair-Width Trial: Expert}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} loses all of their health in one hit and no healing items appear on the battlefield. To complete the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, and a group of three {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin}} is surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Chuchu|link}}, while groups of {{Plural|HWAoC|Keese|link}} can be found around the map as well. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Meat|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Drumstick|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Star Fragment|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
