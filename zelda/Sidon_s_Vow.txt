|name=Vow of Sidon, Sage of Water}}The '''Vow of Sidon, Sage of Water''' is a [[Key Items in Tears of the Kingdom|Key Item]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'' that is used to summon or dismiss the avatar of [[Tulin|Sidon]].

It is obtained after completing the [[Sidon of the Zora|Sidon of the Zora]] questline.

After colecting 4 fragments of sage's will and talking to the [[Goddess Statue]] you can exchange them for an upgraded Solemn Vow of Sidon that increases his avatar's attack power.

The avatar of Sidon can't be summoned in the [[The Depths|Depths]] beneath [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] and in towns and villages.
