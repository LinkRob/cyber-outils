{{Term/Store|Emri||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Emri|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Emri}} manages a small {{Term|BotW|Fruit|link}} shop in the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}. She is an elderly {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} with a grumpy attitude.<ref name="Vai"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Small|...Ugh, how can they make an old vai work this late?}}|Emri|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What do you want? Do you still need something from me?|Emri|BotW}}</ref> She sells {{Term|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Palm Fruit|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Emri}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that she offers more {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon}} than one could find in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Oh, vasaaq... I've got plenty of {{Color|BotW Blue|hydromelons}}... I bet more than you'll find in {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}. Why not buy a bunch while you're here?|Emri|BotW}}</ref> She also says that it is foolish to cross the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} without cooling {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Trying to cross the desert without using {{Color|BotW Blue|cooling cooking}}? That seems like a foolish thing to do, if you ask me...|Emri|BotW}}</ref> She does not see what is so amazing about the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|display=Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|And really, what's so amazing about such a hot place, anyway?|Emri|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Emri}} with the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} on, she calls him an oddball for wanting to wear {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} outfits.<ref>{{Cite|You're an oddball for wanting to wear Gerudo outfits... Anyway, you need anything else from me?|Emri|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Emri}} complains when {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys a small amount of produce, saying he should buy them all at once.<ref>{{Cite|If you're going to buy a bunch, you should just buy them all at once.|Emri|BotW}}</ref> However, when {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys out one of {{Term|BotW|Emri}}'s products, she complains that she will have to get more.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh, now I've got to go get more... {{Small|What a drag...}}|Emri|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} declines to make a purchase, {{Term|BotW|Emri}} tells him to leave if he is going to jerk her around.<ref>{{Cite|Get out of here if you're just gonna jerk me around!|Emri|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can sell {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Emri}},<ref>{{Cite|You still need to sell anything?|Emri|BotW}}</ref> though she complains about it and tells him to hurry up.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* Why do you come here to do that? Hurry it up, would you?|Emri|BotW}}</ref> She continuously asks if he is done yet.<ref>{{Cite|All right... Are you satisfied now?|Emri|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Are we done here?|Emri|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} is finished selling, {{Term|BotW|Emri}} says that she wishes he would sell his {{Plural|BotW|Item}} somewhere else next time.<ref>{{Cite|Are you finished? I wish you would do that somewhere else next time...|Emri|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Emri}} while searching for the {{Term|BotW|Vilia|link|display=man}} who discovered a way into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Emri}} will note that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has a look on his face like he needs to talk to her.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? What's the matter? You've got this look on your face that almost screams, "I gotta talk to you right now."|Emri|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} exits the conversation, she asks what his deal is,<ref>{{Cite|What's your deal? If you don't need anything, don't make that "I need to talk to you" face.|Emri|BotW}}</ref> but she is glad that she is not getting dragged into the drama.<ref>{{Cite|Whatever... I should probably just be glad I'm not getting dragged into every little bit of drama.|Emri|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about suspicious merchants, {{Term|BotW|Emri}} thinks for a moment and says that every merchant that is not a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} is suspicious to her.<ref>{{Cite|A suspicious merchant, huh... From my point of view, pretty much any merchant that isn't Gerudo looks suspicious, you realize?|Emri|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Emri}} then remembers a particular person, and she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} of a female merchant who frequents the {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Still... Yeah, I can think of someone...|Emri|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That one vai merchant in full Gerudo garb... She frequents the {{Color|BotW Red|general store}} a lot.|Emri|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Emri}} says that she is not going to poke her nose in someone's business and finishes speaking.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure there must be more to her story. But what do I know? Plus, I'm not going to poke my nose in another's business.|Emri|BotW}}</ref>
