{{Term/Store|Twinmold||Series, MM, MM3D}} is the [[Boss]] of [[Stone Tower Temple]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 210}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Twinmold's Remains: The remains of the boss in Stone Tower Temple.|Inventory|MM}}</ref> The colossal centipede-like monster is responsible for all of the curses placed on [[Ikana Canyon]],<ref>{{Cite|I wish for you to go to the temple in this land and sever the root of the evil curse that torments us.|Sharp|MM}}</ref> and is keeping the last of the [[Four Giants]] captive. Twinmold's body is protected by an exoskeleton that leaves only its head and tail exposed.<ref>{{Cite|Just its head and tail aren't protected by its hard exoskeleton, so aim carefully.|Tatl|MM}}</ref>

Twinmold's Boss room is located above the room where the [[Garo Master]] is fought. To defeat Twinmold, [[Link]] must attack its vulnerable heads and tails. The most effective method for defeating Twinmold is to use the [[Sword]] while wearing the [[Giant's Mask]], which allows Link to grow to the size of Twinmold. However, Link will not be able to use [[Targeting System|Z-Targeting]] while using the Giant's Mask. As the mask quickly drains Link's [[Magic Meter]], Link can replenish it by destroying the pillars found throughout the area, which contain [[Magic Jar]]s. It is possible to defeat Twinmold without the Giant's Mask by shooting [[Arrow]]s at Twinmold's weak points. [[Ice Arrow]]s are effective against Twinmold's red tail, as are [[Fire Arrow]]s against its blue tail. It can also be defeated by using [[Spin Attack|Spin Attacks]] right next to them as the gigantic beasts rise out of the sand.

If Link strays too far from the initial platform during the battle, even if he is wearing the Giant's Mask, he will immediately sink into the sand (the boundaries can be seen with the outer area flowing faster than the arena). This causes him to be returned to the dungeon's entrance. Twinmold may fly past this boundary in an effort to lure Link into the trap.

Once Link has dealt sufficient damage to one half of Twinmold, it will fly around in agony before its body will explode, with its head crashing into the sand. Once both parts of Twinmold are defeated, Link will receive a [[Heart Container]] and [[Twinmold's Remains]], and freeing the final Giant who was trapped in Twinmold's body.<ref>{{Cite|You have just freed the innocent spirit that this dark mask had kept imprisoned within the body of evil Twinmold.|N/A|MM}}</ref> Twinmold's defeat lifts the curse on Ikana, causing the undead monsters brought to life by the curse to disappear.

In {{MM3D}}, the boss battle functions very differently from the original game. Twinmold's spikes are colored blue and red. The blue Twinmold must be defeated by shooting Arrows at the eyes beneath it. This will make it fall to the ground and reveal its main eye. Once defeated, a [[Treasure Chest]] containing the Giant's Mask appears in the center of the arena. The red Twinmold can shoot fireballs and summon [[Moldbabies]], which leave [[Heart]]s and Magic Jars when defeated. Moldbabies can be defeated easily by bashing the ground by holding the B Button as a giant.

While in giant form, Link can throw punches and roll in order to hit and stun Twinmold. In order to stun Twinmold, it has to be hit ten times or hit with three combos to knock him down while he is flying. If Twinmold burrows while flying, the stun counter will reset. Alternatively, Link can throw one of the two giant [[boulder]]s at Twinmold to stun it immediately. When stunned, Link can grab its tail and spin it around, leading to a whip onto the ground, or choke it by grabbing its head. When spinning or choking Twinmold, more damage is dealt when spinning the Circle Pad around.

== Trivia ==

* Twinmold's boss battle and defeat is similar to [[Volvagia]]'s from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', and would be reused for [[Molgera]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]''.
* In ''Majora's Mask 3D'', Giant Link grabbing the Red Twinmold by the tail and spinning it around resembled a common move Mario used to defeat Bowser since ''Super Mario 64''.

==Nomenclature==
Twinmold's name may be a reference to [[Moldorm]]s, with "Twin" referring to the fact that there are two of them.
