{{Term/Store|Ordon Shield|Ordon Shields|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=133 {{Exp Game|TP, TPHD}} }}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWDE}}

==Location and Uses==
{{Lacking Sources|TP}}
During the night of the invasion on {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}}, the villagers plan on using the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Shield}} and the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Sword|link}} that {{Term|TPHD|Rusl|link}} crafted to fend off the {{Plural|TPHD|Enemy|link}}. To meet {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}}'s demands, {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} enters {{Term|TPHD|Jaggle|link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Pergie|link}}'s house from the roof and steal the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Shield}} from the wall of his {{Term|TPHD|Storage Loft|link}}. Since this {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Shield}} is made out of wood, it can catch on {{Term|TPHD|Fire|link}}. If this happens and the {{Plural|TPHD|Fire}} are not put out in time, {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} will be [[Permanently Missable Items#Ordon Shield|permanently]] unable to obtain another one. If the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Shield}} is destroyed, a visually different, yet functionally identical {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Shield|link}} can be bought from the {{Plural|TPHD|Shop|link}} at {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village|link}} or {{Term|TPHD|Death Mountain|link}} as a replacement. {{Plural|TPHD|Arrow|link}} fired from {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin Archer|link}} will get stuck in the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Shield}} when it is used to block them and can be collected by {{Term|TPHD|Link}} when he puts the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Shield}} away.
