==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|Possessed Naydra}}" plays when in the vicinity of {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}-infected {{Term|BotW|Naydra|link}} during "{{Term|BotW|The Spring of Wisdom|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. It is a distorted, otherworldly rendition of "{{Term|Series|Attendant of the Sacred Spring|link}}" with trailing notes. Like its counterpart, the song contains elements of "{{Term|Series|Between the Dragons|link}}" from {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 龍の間 |book= {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Original Soundtrack|link}} booklet |publisher= Nintendo Co., Ltd. |page= 17}}</ref>

"{{Term|BotW|Possessed Naydra}}" is track 10 on Disc 4 of {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Original Soundtrack|link}}.
