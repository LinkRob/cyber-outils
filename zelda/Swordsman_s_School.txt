The {{Term/Store|Swordsman's School||Series}},<ref>{{Cite|Swordsman's School|N/A|MM3D}}</ref> also known as the '''Mighty Training Center''',<ref>{{Cite|Enter all disciples and those who choose to be enlightened with the way of the sword. / Mighty Training Center|Sign|MM}}</ref> is a location in {{MM}}.

==Overview==
The school is located in [[West Clock Town]]. It is operated by the [[Swordsman]] and is open 24 hours.<ref>{{Cite|This training center has friendly, polite, 24-hour, one-on-one training that will noticeably improve your sword skills!|Swordsman|MM}}</ref>

The Swordsman does not physically participate in training sessions. Rather, he raises logs from out of the floor to serve as targets. He remains sitting cross-legged on a raised platform at far end of the hall. To his left is a [[Cow Figurine]], to his right a gong that yields small amounts of [[Rupee]]s{{How Many}} when struck with different attacks.{{which}} 

Behind the Swordsman is a trophy shelf and a wooden placard that reads, {{Romanize|剣道場||Kendo Hall|2}}.{{Verify|MM}} Destroying the placard reveals a secret room behind it. There are 5 [[Pot]]s in the secret room containing a total of 25 Rupees—one Blue Rupee in each.{{Verify|MM}}

After midnight on the [[Final Day]], there is a [[Sign]] where the Swordsman usually sits. It reads, "''I'll be on vacation for a short while. Please don't look for me.''" During that time, the Swordsman is in fact cowering in a corner of the secret room.<ref>{{Cite|Uuuuurgh...I'm scared! I can't take it! I don't want to die!|Swordsman|MM}}</ref>

==Courses==
The Swordsman's School offers two [[Sword Training]] courses:
* Novice Course, a tutorial which costs 1 Rupee.
* Expert Course, a mini-game which costs 10 Rupees.

===Novice Course===
The Novice Course is a [[Sword]]-fighting tutorial. To complete it, Link must perform the following moves in order: [[Sideways Jump]], [[Roll Attack]], [[Backflip]], [[Horizontal Cut]], [[Vertical Cut]], [[Thrust]], [[Jump Attack]].

===Expert Course===
The Expert Course is a mini-game where one earns points by slashing 10 logs in succession. One earns 3 points for slicing a log lengthwise with a Jump Attack, and 1 point for every other attack.{{Verify|MM}} There is a [[Piece of Heart]] reward for earning 30 points by slicing all 10 logs with the Jump Attack.

==Nomenclature==
The Japanese name of the Swordsman's School, {{Romanize|剣道場|Kendō Jō|2}}, is inscribed on a placard at the back of the hall. In {{MM3D}}, this name is also inscribed in [[Hylian Language|Hylian]] on a sign above where the Swordsman sits.
