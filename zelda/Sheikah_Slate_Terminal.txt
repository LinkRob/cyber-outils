{{Term/Store|Sheikah Slate Terminal|Sheikah Slate Terminals|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 217}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Slate Terminal}} are pieces of {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can activate with his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. They are short pedestals, similar to {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|link}}, though they are unable to hold the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.

In the second chamber of the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Terminal}}. The voice of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} instructs him to hold up his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} to it in order to reveal his path forward.<ref>{{Cite|Hold the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} up to the pedestal. That will show you the way.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} does so, the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Terminal|display=Terminal}} will authenticate and confirm the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Authenticating... Sheikah Slate confirmed.}}|Sheikah Slate Terminal|BotW}}</ref> The door to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}} will then open, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to explore the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}.

Each {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} has a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Terminal}} outside of it. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} activates these {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Slate Terminal}}, they will confirm his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate confirmed.}}|Sheikah Slate Terminal|BotW}}</ref> They will add the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine's}} {{Term|BotW|Travel Gate|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}.<ref name="Travel Gate">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Travel Gate registered to map.}}|Sheikah Slate Terminal|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will also be granted access to the interior of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Access granted.}}|Sheikah Slate Terminal|BotW}}</ref> There are {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Slate Terminal}} at the entrance to {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} as well, which will also activate a {{Term|BotW|Travel Gate}}.<ref name="Travel Gate"/>
