==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Energizing Honey Crepes are dishes made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Fresh Milk]], [[Bird Egg]], [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Cane Sugar]], and [[Courser Bee Honey]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. They can be eaten to refill two fifths of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} and heal ten of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> The honey drizzled over these crepes bring out their natural sweetness and flavor.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Since five ingredients are necessary to make Honey Crepes, only their energizing version can be made.
