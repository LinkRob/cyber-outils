The '''Insect Heads''' are mini-bosses found in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} They are found in three locations around the {{Term|FPTRR|Gooey Swamp|link}} but can only be reached via the {{Term|FPTRR|Insect Cavern|link}}. Their abdomens are found within the cavern and emit a noxious gas which causes {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} to lose {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}. They also block his path to other sections of the {{Term|FPTRR|Dungeon|link}}.

To gain passage through the cavern, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} must destroy the {{Plural|FPTRR|Insect Head}} by throwing a total of three {{Plural|FPTRR|Bomb Fruit|link}} at each.
