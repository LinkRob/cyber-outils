The {{Term/Store|Life Dew||Series, FPTRR}} is an item in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|FPTRR}}
Life Dew is found deep within the [[Deku Temple]]. The [[Deku Tree]] requests that Tingle bring this dew and use it on his heir, so that the sapling may regain his strength, and carry on the Deku Tree's legacy.
