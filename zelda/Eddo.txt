{{Term/Store|Eddo||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 282}}</ref>

==Biography==
Eddo is an inventor who runs a [[Eddo's Garage|ship Garage]] at [[Cannon Island]].<ref>{{Cite|Hello, there! You've come to Eddo's Garage...that is, Master Eddo's shop!|Fuzo|PH}}</ref> If [[Link]] takes the route around the island that his apprentice, [[Fuzo]], recommends to him,<ref>{{Cite|If you really, really, really want a cannon, then go speak with the master. You can go around the island to reach the back door.|Fuzo|PH}}</ref> the hero will find Eddo in his garage. Eddo introduces himself as the owner of the shop and asks Link what he wants.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, yes...I'm the owner of this shop, Eddo! What do you want?|Eddo|PH}}</ref> He is impressed that Link would brave the dangerous isle for one of his [[Cannon#Phantom Hourglass|Cannons]].<ref>{{Cite|What?! You demand a cannon? Heh heh...you impress me. You'd brave this dangerous isle for one of my cannons.|Eddo|PH}}</ref> Eddo lets Link know his cannons are works of explosive art and are crafted from choice parts.<ref>{{Cite|Then know that my cannons are works of explosive art, crafted from choice parts!|Eddo|PH}}</ref> He says the hero will have to pay a large amount of [[Rupee]]s for one and asks if he has the guts to hear how much.<ref>{{Cite|So you'd better believe that you're going to cough up big Rupees for one. Have the guts to hear how much?|Eddo|PH}}</ref> Eddo says not to blame him for dropping a load of Rupees in his shop and reveals the Cannon will cost fifty Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|It's going for the steep price of...50 Rupees!|Eddo|PH}}</ref> Eddo asks Link if he is big enough to give up fifty whole Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|You big enough to pony up 50 whole Rupees?|Eddo|PH}}</ref> Eddo goes on to say fifty Rupees can buy a good life on the isle and he could not blame him if he is unable to pay.<ref>{{Cite|I mean, 50 Rupees would buy you a whole lot of good life on this isle! No one could blame you if you can't pay!|Eddo|PH}}</ref> If Link agrees to the purchase, Eddo asks in surprise where he got fifty Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|What?! Where'd you get 50 Rupees?!|Eddo|PH}}</ref> He continues that is does not matter as long as Link coughs up the cash and gives the Cannon to him.<ref>{{Cite|Doesn't matter, I guess, just as long as you cough up the cash! OK, take it!|Eddo|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|PH Red|cannon}}! This part from {{Color|PH Blue|Eddo}} fits on the deck of your ship!|N/A|PH}}</ref> Link can use the Cannon to defend himself on the [[Great Sea]].<ref name="Guide 13">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 13}}</ref> Eddo says he is next going to devise a [[Salvage Arm]] that can pull treasure from the seafloor.<ref>{{Cite|Next I'm gonna devise a {{Color|PH Red|Salvage Arm}} that can pull treasure from the seafloor!|Eddo|PH}}</ref> Eddo bids Link farewell and tells him to use the door in the back to return to the dock.<ref>{{Cite|See you later, shrimp. Just use that door there to get back to the dock!|Eddo|PH}}</ref> Later, he sells Link the Salvage Arm,<ref name="Guide 13"/> with the price depending on how loud he yells for it (by using the [[Nintendo DS]] microphone).<ref>{{Cite|Butter me up all you want, but it's gonna cost you big-time! So! Show me how much you want the Salvage Arm! SHOUT WITH SOME GUSTO!|Eddo|PH}}</ref>
