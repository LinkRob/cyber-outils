The {{Term/Store|Fairy of Winds||Series, TP, TPHD}} is the [[Fairy]] who resides inside the [[Gale Boomerang]] in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am the Fairy of Winds who resides in this boomerang.|Fairy of Winds|TP}}</ref> They give the Boomerang its eolic properties.

==Biography==
After [[Ook]] is defeated in the {{Term|TPHD|Forest Temple|link}}, the monkey escapes, leaving the [[Gale Boomerang]] behind. The Boomerang then hovers on its own and reveals the spirit within the item as the Fairy of Winds, who thanks [[Link]] for having freed them from evil.<ref>{{Cite|You have freed me from evil, and I now have my true power back.|Fairy of Winds|TP}}</ref> With their true power returned, they ask for Link to take the Boomerang with him and use their power and blessing to aid him in his quest.<ref>{{Cite|Please... Take it with you, use it to aid your quest, and may both my power and my blessing go with you.|Fairy of Winds|TP}}</ref> The Fairy of Winds is never seen and is only spoken to once when Link first receives the Gale Boomerang.
