"{{Term/Store|The Two Rings||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Two Rings}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|West Hyrule Plains}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the end of {{Term|BotW|Jeddo Bridge|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} singing among the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Ring|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|West Hyrule Plains|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Kass}} is shocked when {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him, as he was lost in the song written by his teacher, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet|link}}, and did not hear him approach.<ref>{{Cite|... Oh! I did not hear your approach. I was lost in this song written by my late teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Though the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} passed away several years ago, the song {{Term|BotW|Kass}} was playing was the last one he taught him.<ref>{{Cite|He passed away several years ago, you see... And this was the last song he taught me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Realizing he has gotten carried away, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} apologizes and blames nostalgia.<ref>{{Cite|Excuse me.<br/>I fear I may have gotten carried away. Nostalgia will do that to a person.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kass}} reveals that he knows an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" about the area and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to hear it.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to listen, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will follow through with his offer.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's hear it.] Excellent! Without further ado...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics describe an {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} threading two rings, which will cause an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} to appear.<ref>{{Cite|When a single arrow threads two rings,<br/>the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} will rise like birds on wings.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After singing, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} wonders if the rings mentioned in the song refer to the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Ring}}.<ref>{{Cite|Do you think the rings in the song refer to these oddly shaped rocks? And what of the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> While he can see that there is a mystery in the area, he has a feeling that the answer is beyond him.<ref>{{Cite|There's a mystery to be uncovered here, that's for sure. And I get the feeling the truth is beyond my wildest imagination.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well,<ref>{{Cite|May the light illuminate your path.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.{{Note|It is possible to immediately complete this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} by unearthing {{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze Shrine}} before speaking to {{Term|BotW|Kass}}.}}

There are 12 {{Plural|BotW|Stone Ring}} scattered across the area, extending as far as the {{Term|BotW|Jeddo Bridge}}. Some of them are on the ground while others are upon raised elevations. Most of the rock formations are in close proximity to one another, though only a few are in clear alignment. While using a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} with long range, it is possible to sit within the ring of a ring-shaped rock and shoot through the ring of another rock. Upon successfully shooting an {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} through two {{Plural|BotW|Stone Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze Shrine|link}} will raise from the ground nearby.<ref>{{Cite|An ancient shrine appeared when you loosed a single arrow through two ring-shaped rocks.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.

==Trivia==
* Upon completing this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Kass}} once more. He will compliment him for his ability to solve the riddle before wishing him luck on his journey.<ref>{{Cite|That was...stupendous. I suppose I should have expected nothing less. No doubt I shall see you again. May the light illuminate your path.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> 
* {{Term|BotW|Kass's Journal|link}}, which is found in a hut on {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff|link}}, hints at the location of this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|"The ancient song of the {{Color|BotW Blue|West Hyrule Plains}}."|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} recounts that there are many rocks with holes in them near {{Term|BotW|Jeddo Bridge}}.<ref>{{Cite|On the south side of the West Hyrule Plains, there is a bridge called {{Color|BotW Blue|Jeddo Bridge}}. Near this bridge, there are a number of circular rocks with holes in their centers.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> The rocks are mentioned in an ancient song, which he hopes to learn more about.<ref>{{Cite|Peculiarly enough, these rocks are apparently mentioned in an ancient song from the area. It sounds rather odd, but I hope to find out more about this ancient rock song.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref>
