{{Term/Store|Shiro||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
Shiro is a [[Soldier]] who is encountered in [[Ikana Canyon]]. He is so plain and boring that, even though he constantly waves his arms around, he is unnoticeable and invisible.<ref>{{Cite|I'm shocked. You're the first person who's ever spoken to me. I've been here for many years, waving my arms around and asking for help, but everyone ignores me and passes me by. It's 'cause I'm about as impressive as a stone, right?...I'm used to it, though.|Shiro|MM}}</ref> [[Link]] can find the injured Soldier while using the [[Lens of Truth]].<ref>{{Cite|If you use the Lens of Truth near here and speak to the man you find... you may earn a mask that can enable you to blend into backgrounds and move about without being noticed.|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> After being healed with a [[Red Potion|Red]] or [[Blue Potion]], he gives Link the [[Stone Mask]].<ref>{{Cite|Umm...I have a request...Can you give me some medicine that would make even an unnoticeable stone like me feel better? [...] Ah, that...Is that it? ...I feel better, I think. Thanks... Oh...here... In gratitude... Now...Let me practice standing out...|Shiro|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, he is instead found in the center of the [[Pirates' Fortress]] next to its lookout tower, which he infiltrated in an attempt to stand out.<ref>{{Cite|I thought I'd maybe stand out if I did something brave, so I infiltrated the Pirates' Fortress.|Shiro|MM3D}}</ref> [[Tatl]] senses his presence when nearby, and alerts Link.<ref>{{Cite|Hey... Did you just call me? That's strange. I could have sworn someone here just called to me...|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref> After assisting him in the same manner as in the original game, he runs out of the fortress through the nearest doorway. He can then be found on the ledge above the area with the Pirates on boats at a loss on how to proceed.<ref>{{Cite|..How will I ever get home? My armor's so heavy I'd sink like... well, like a stone.|Shiro|MM3D}}</ref> When spoken to, he will reassure Link that he does not need further help.<ref>{{Cite|Aah, I'm sorry. This isn't your problem. Don't worry, I'll be fine...|Shiro|MM3D}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Shiro means white in Japanese, which can also refer to being plain or blank, referring to Shiro's predicament of being as plain as stone.
