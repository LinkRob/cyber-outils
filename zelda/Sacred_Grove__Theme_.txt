"'''Sacred Grove (Theme)'''" is a [[Song]] in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
The track is associated with the eponymous location, {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Grove}}. It is a remixed version of "{{Term|Series|Saria's Song|link}}" with the dungeon music from the original {{TLoZ}} as a harp backing track.

The song appears as Track 13 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Original Soundtrack]]'' and Track 17 in Disc 2 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Sound Selection]]''.
