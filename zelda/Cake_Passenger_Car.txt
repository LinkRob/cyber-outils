The {{Term/Store|Cake Passenger Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cake Passenger Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Cake Passenger Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Palace Dish|link}}, three {{Plural|ST|Dragon Scale|link}} and four {{Plural|ST|Ruto Crown|link}}. Its design resembles a square piece of cake topped with whipped cream and various slices of fruit, and its description reads "Anybody would be happy to ride in a car this sweet!"

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Cake Passenger Car}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Sweet Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
