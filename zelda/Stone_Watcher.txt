==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
Unlike general [[Rock|rocks]] in some locations that usually can be destroyed by [[bombs]], {{Plural|TWWHD|Stone Watcher}} are rather carved rocks that can only be lifted by using the {{Plural|TWWHD|Power Bracelet|link}}, and they trigger the sound effect everytime they're destroyed outside of a destruction cutscene event. None of the stone watchers throughout gameplay drop any items, nor spawn enemies from underneath (i.e. [[Chuchu]]) when destroyed. There are four {{Plural|TWWHD|Stone Watcher}} located on the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}, most of them covering {{Plural|TWWHD|Secret Cave|link}}. One can be found in {{Term|TWWHD|Fire Mountain|link}} blocking a shortcut to the exit. Another one can be found on {{Term|TWWHD|Stone Watcher Island|link}}. One more can be found on {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}} blocking the entrance to the {{Term|TWWHD|Savage Labyrinth|link}}. The final one can be found on {{Term|TWWHD|Headstone Island|link}} blocking the entrance to the {{Term|TWWHD|Earth Temple|link}}. 

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|L|-}}/{{HW|DE|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW|L}}, {{Plural|HWL|Stone Watcher}} appear blocking the path to new areas on the map. They can only be destroyed by shooting them with a {{Term|HWL|Warship|link}}, which can only be activated by hitting a peg next to them with the {{Term|HWL|Hammer|link}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
