The {{Term/Store|Hawkeye||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an item in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 127}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Hawkeye can only be purchased at [[Malo Mart]] in [[Kakariko Village]] after completing [[Talo]] and [[Malo]]'s [[Bow]] {{Term|TPHD|Mini-Game|link}}. Once [[Link]] completes the [[Goron Mines]], he can talk to Talo to start the sidequest, which consists of hitting three targets scattered around Kakariko Village using the Bow. Successfully hitting the three targets yields the hero a [[Piece of Heart]], and the Hawkeye will also be available for purchase at Malo Mart for 100 [[Rupee]]s.

The Hawkeye allows Link to see farther away than usual. Link can combine it with the Hero's Bow to use it like a {{wp|sniper rifle}}. It is an optional object and is not required to complete the game.
