{{Term/Store|Pierre Plateau||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Pierre Plateau'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Pierre Plateau}} is a section of highlands that is located in [[East Necluda]]. {{Term|BotW|Pierre Plateau}} overlooks the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - West Gate|link}} to the north and is bordered by {{Term|BotW|Bonooru's Stand|link}} to the south and the [[Phalian Highlands]] to the southeast. {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} can be found along the ridges of {{Term|BotW|Pierre Plateau}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found by completing a [[Cube Pattern]] at the northern base of {{Term|BotW|Pierre Plateau}}.
