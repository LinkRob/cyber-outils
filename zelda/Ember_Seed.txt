{{Term/Store|Ember Seed|Ember Seeds|Series, OoS, OoA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 122 {{Exp Game|OoS, OoA}}}}</ref>{{Fact}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Ember Seeds|Flames shoot out of this strange seed when it is cracked open. Use Ember Seeds whenever you want to light a fire.}}
In {{OoS}}, {{Plural|OoS|Ember Seed}} are {{Plural|Series|Mystical Seed|link}} that grow on {{Plural|OoS|Ember Tree|link}} during the winter. They ignite when cracked open. They can be used to light {{Plural|Series|Torch|link}}, burn shrubs, and cause enemies to spontaneously combust. If {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} uses one on a {{Term|OoS|Gibdo|link}}, its wrappings will burn off, revealing the {{Term|OoS|Stalfos|link}} underneath. They retain the same functions when used as projectiles.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Ember Seeds|Flames shoot out of this strange seed when it is cracked open. Use Ember Seeds whenever you want to light a fire.}}
In {{OoA}}, {{Plural|OoA|Ember Seed}} are {{Plural|Series|Mystical Seed}} that grow on {{Plural|OoA|Ember Tree}}. They ignite when cracked open. They can be used to light {{Plural|Series|Torch}}, burn shrubs, and cause enemies to spontaneously combust. If {{Term|OoS|Link}} uses one on a {{Term|OoS|Gibdo}}, its wrappings will burn off, revealing the {{Term|OoS|Stalfos}} underneath. They retain the same functions when used as projectiles.
