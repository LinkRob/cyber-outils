The {{Name|Hylian|{{Term/Store|Diary||Series, ALBW}}}} is an object in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{HylianALBW2|Diary}}|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Diary}} can be found in the back of the {{Term|ALBW|Vacant House|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} while playing in {{Term|ALBW|Hero Mode|link}}. It has a purple {{Term|Series|Rabbit|link}} symbol on its cover, implying that it belongs to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio|link}}. The contents of the {{Term|ALBW|Diary}} reveal his feelings of cowardice and how he knew that {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|link}} was being manipulated by {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} from the start, planning to stop a disaster from happening with his remaining magic.
