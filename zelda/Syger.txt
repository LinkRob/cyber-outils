{{Term/Store|Syger|Sygers|Series, OoS}} is the {{Term|OoS|Sub-Boss|link}} of [[Unicorn's Cave]], the fifth dungeon of {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 39}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Syger rolls into a spiked ball and rolls across the room in an attempt to harm Link. There is a brief window between these attacks where Link can attack Syger's weak point, the red ball-shaped tip of his tail, with his [[sword]]. 

==Nomenclature==
"Syger" appears to be a shortened version of ''{{Wp|sabre-toothed tiger}}''.
