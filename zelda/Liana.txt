{{Term/Store|Liana||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Liana|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Liana}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She carries a {{Term|BotW|Moonlight Scimitar|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Liana}}, she asks what an outsider is doing in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}.<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing here, outsider?! This is the barracks for Gerudo soldiers!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She informs him that they are on high alert, and she suggests that he instead check out the market.<ref>{{Cite|We're on high alert right now. If you want to do some sightseeing, go check out the market instead!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what happened, {{Term|BotW|Liana}} begins to explain, but she quickly realizes that she should not tell an outsider anything involving their security.<ref>{{Cite|Well, you see... Wait, why would I tell an outsider like you about important matters involving the security of our people?!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She mentions that she could tell him if he had permission from {{Term|BotW|Teake|link|display=Captain Teake}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you had permission from {{Color|BotW Blue|Captain Teake}}, that'd be a different story...|Liana|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Liana}} after meeting with {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}, she notices that she has not seen {{Term|BotW|Barta|link}} recently and wonders where she is.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, I haven't seen Barta. Where's she gone off to?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leena|link}} explains that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} went to scout out the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}} two days ago, but she does not know what happened to her.<ref>{{Cite|She said she was going to scout the thieves' hideout, but then she left before we could say anything. Now that I think about it, she's been gone for two days. I wonder what happened to her...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> Angered, {{Term|BotW|Liana}} asks why she let {{Term|BotW|Barta}} go by herself,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|You— Are you serious?!}} Why did you let her go by herself?! And why did you wait two days to report this?!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Leena}} apologizes for.<ref>{{Cite|I'm— I apologize... She said she just wanted to check it out and would be careful, so I thought...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is upset that they now have to retrieve both the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Barta}}.<ref>{{Cite|Uuugghhh. As if we aren't busy enough searching for the chief's heirloom, now we have to find {{Color|BotW Blue|Barta}} too...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She is fearful that, if {{Term|BotW|Teake}} were to find out, they would be punished with 100 laps around the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If the captain hears about this fiasco, she'll punish us for sure! Probably 100 laps around Gerudo Desert...again.|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotta|link}} asks what they should do,<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Oh, no. What should we do?|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> causing {{Term|BotW|Liana}} to yell that she is thinking about it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|I'm thinking about it!}}|Liana|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, he can return to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}} to find {{Term|BotW|Liana}} yet again shocked that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} failed to return.<ref>{{Cite|Whuh... Whah... {{Big|WHAAAAT?!}} Barta didn't come back AGAIN?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} explains that she went to investigate the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid this time she went to investigate the {{Color|BotW Blue|leviathan bones}} in the southwest...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Leena}} claims that she tried to stop her.<ref>{{Cite|I... I tried to stop her...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} points out that this did not work, since {{Term|BotW|Barta}} is clearly not with them.<ref>{{Cite|But obviously it didn't work because she's not here!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leena}} and {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} reveal that they did not think {{Term|BotW|Barta}} would actually go, due to the {{Term|BotW|Molduga|link}} and the intense {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}} in the area.<ref>{{Cite|Well, yeah... But... Everyone knows that area is the domain of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga}}, so who would figure she'd even go close to there?|Leena|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes, nobody would dare! And not only that, but it's an {{Color|BotW Blue|extremely hot zone}}, so we wouldn't last long there in our gear...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is only angered further by this, reminding them that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} walks into danger unknowingly.<ref>{{Cite|You... You {{Big|IDIOTS!}} That scatterbrain wouldn't even realize she was walking into danger! I mean, you know that, right?! Why, it was just the other day she ran off and got captured by the Yiga!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She resigns herself to the fact that {{Term|BotW|Barta}}, {{Term|BotW|Leena}}, and {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} will not change.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh... I guess Barta will be Barta, and you all aren't going to change...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> However, she still wonders why {{Term|BotW|Barta}} gets herself into trouble so often.<ref>{{Cite|But still, why does she have to go and get in trouble like this all the time...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> After witnessing this exchange, "{{Term|BotW|The Search for Barta|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Liana}} during the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, he can overhear her scolding {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} and {{Term|BotW|Leena}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've had it up to here with you lot! You're walking embarrassments! And now Barta has gone missing...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She is scared of what will happen if {{Term|BotW|Riju}} finds out what has happened.<ref>{{Cite|If the chief finds out, what do you think she's going to say?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She then mutters to herself that she needs to do something before anyone else finds out.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Must do something before we get found out...}}}}|Liana|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Barta}} returns to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}} once again, {{Term|BotW|Liana}} commends {{Term|BotW|Link}}, revealing that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} told her about how he helped her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! What a courageous vai! I heard from Barta! She said you found her!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She compares him to a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}, claiming that if he were a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}, she would want him as part of her troop.<ref>{{Cite|I told her you were as dependable as a Champion! If you were a Gerudo vai, I'd want you in my troop!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is relieved that the situation has been resolved before {{Term|BotW|Teake}} could punish her for it.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, close one, huh? Looks like I got out of this without getting chewed out by our troop leader!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, telling him to buy something nice at the market.<ref>{{Cite|You must be worn out, right? Here, take this as thanks! Buy yourself something nice in the market!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} talks to {{Term|BotW|Liana}} after calming the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, she admits that she dismissed Link's abilities too quickly.<ref>{{Cite|I may have dismissed you too hastily before. You have many promising qualities...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> After completing "{{Term|BotW|The Search for Barta}}", she may also remind him to use his reward and enjoy {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|You should use your reward from earlier and enjoy what our town has to offer.|Liana|BotW}}</ref>
