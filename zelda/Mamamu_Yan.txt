{{Term/Store|Mamamu Yan||Series, MM, MM3D, OoA}},{{Exp Game|MM, OoA}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM}} & 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Pooch Lady||OoT, OoT3D}},{{Exp Game|OoT}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT|publisher= Prima|page= 18}}</ref> is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
Although unnamed in {{OoT}}, the Pooch Lady is a woman in [[Hyrule Castle Town]] who has lost her [[Dog]], [[Richard (Ocarina of Time)|Richard]].<ref>{{Cite|I can't find my puppy anywhere! I need you to look for it! Please!|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref> If [[Link]] returns the correct [[Dog]], she will reward him with a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|Richard! This is my little Richard! Thank you! Thank you! I'll give this to you as a reward!|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref> Arrogantly, she seems to think Richard is above all the other Dogs in Hyrule Castle Town.<ref>{{Cite|My precious little puppy's fur coat is very special compared to the other mutts around here!|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My precious puppy can run faster than any of the other mutts around here!|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref> The Pooch Lady is one of the survivors who flee to [[Kakariko Village]] after Hyrule Castle Town was destroyed during Link's absence. She is housed by the [[:File:OoT GenerousWoman2.png|generous woman]] who runs the boarding house, the same place [[Talon]] flees to when he is driven away from [[Lon Lon Ranch]].

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Mamamu Yan owns the [[Doggy Racetrack]] south of [[Romani Ranch]], which is open from 6 AM to 8 PM.<ref>{{Cite|Mamamu Yan's / Doggy Racetrack / 6 a.m. to 8 p.m.|N/A|MM}}</ref> Here, Link can place a bet on the winning Dog in a race. If the Dog he chooses places first, he will win triple his bet. If the Dog places second, he will win double the bet. For third to fifth places, he will break even. Any place lower wins nothing, and Link loses the money he bet.<ref>{{Cite|If the dog you brought me takes 1st place, you get triple your bet. 2nd place gets double. If the dog you've chosen places 3rd through 5th, you'll break even and get back however much you bet. If your dog does any worse than that, you'll lose whatever you bet.|Mamamu Yan|MM}}</ref> If Link wins over 150 [[Rupee]]s at the Doggy Racetrack, Mamamu Yan will also give him a Piece of Heart.<ref>{{Cite|N-N-Nice going! You've won over 150 Rupees, so I have to give you a bonus prize, too!|Mamamu Yan|MM}}</ref>

==={{OoA|-}}===
Mamamu Yan is part of the [[Trading Quest]], giving Link the [[Dumbbell]] in return for the [[Doggie Mask]].<ref>{{Cite|That [[File:OoA Doggie Mask Sprite.png|15px]] mask!!! It fits my dog perfectly! Let me have that [[File:OoA Doggie Mask Sprite.png|15px]] mask!|Mamamu Yan|OoA}}</ref>

In a [[Linked Game]], Mamamu Yan's mother will appear in [[Spool Swamp]] in western [[Holodrum]] in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm the mother of Mamamu Yan, Labrynna's top dog breeder.|Mamamu Yan's Mother|OoS}}</ref> She will ask Link to tell a secret to her daughter in [[Labrynna]].<ref>{{Cite|Can you tell this secret to my daughter, Mamamu Yan, in Labrynna?|Mamamu Yan's Mother|OoS}}</ref> After telling the secret password to Mamamu Yan, she will ask Link to help her find her lost Dog.<ref>{{Cite|My precious dog has gotten lost. He's somewhere in town, so I'd like you to get him. He's so delicate. You carry him properly. There is a huge reward if you find him!|Mamamu Yan|OoA}}</ref> After returning the Dog, which can be found roaming [[Lynna City]], Mamamu Yan will reward Link with a [[Magical Ring]], which becomes the [[Snowshoe Ring]] once appraised by [[Vasu]].<ref>{{Cite|My precious pup! Where were you? Thank you for bringing him home safely! Here is your reward! Don't forget to take it to the jeweler for appraisal!!!|Mamamu Yan|OoA}}</ref>
