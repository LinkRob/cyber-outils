{{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Boko Shield|link}} that are made using slightly stronger wood and reinforced with {{Term|BotW|Animal|link}} bones.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly used by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}. They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> If scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Spiked Boko Shield}}.

Because of their wooden composition, {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Shield}} will burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. However, they float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} like metal {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} do. Finally, they are unable to be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
