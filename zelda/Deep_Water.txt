'''Deep Water''' is a recurring obstacle in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and uses==
Deep Water appears in every game of {{TLoZ|-}} series. Most installments allow Link to swim, usually immediately or after obtaining the [[Flippers]], though in {{TLoZ}}, {{TAoL}}, {{PH}}, and {{ST}}, Link cannot swim at all.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page=10}}</ref> While swimming above the surface Link is unable to use any of his items or weapons.

In {{LA}}, {{OoA}}, {{OoS}}, and {{FSA}}, underwater swimming switches to a 2-D side-scrolling view and allows Link to make sword swipes. In {{OoT}} and {{TP}}, [[Iron Boots]] can be equipped to sink to the ground and select items such as the [[Hookshot]] can be used. Staying underwater for prolonged periods of time requires a [[Zora Tunic]] in {{OoT|-}}, a requirement to completing the [[Water Temple]]. In {{MM}}, the requirement is instead [[Zora Mask]], while in {{TP}}, the requirement is [[Zora Armor]]. [[Beaver Race]] is a minigame directly related to swimming.

In {{TMC}}, [[Shallow Water]] relatively becomes Deep Water while Link is [[Minish]]-sized.

In {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}, Link's [[Stamina Wheel]] depletes when swimming in deep water, and Link will instantly drown if he enters water with an empty Stamina Wheel.
