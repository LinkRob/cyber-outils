{{Term/Store|Monita||Series, NL}} is the robotic host that runs {{NL}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to {{Color|NL Blue|Nintendo Land}}! I am {{Color|NL Blue|Monita}}, your park guide.|Monita|NL}}</ref>

==Biography==
Upon first meeting {{Term|NL|Monita}}, she is trapped inside a {{Smw|? Block}}, and asks the player to let her out.<ref>{{Cite|Um, excuse me. Would you mind letting me out of here? Just {{Color|NL Blue|touch the box}} on the {{Icon|NL Gamepad}}.|Monita|NL}}</ref> She then introduces the basic gameplay mechanics used to navigate the plaza and the {{Term|NL|Central Tower|link}}. She will also make comments on the different prizes unlocked in the plaza.

==Nomenclature==
"{{Term|NL|Monita}}" is likely a play on the word "monitor".
