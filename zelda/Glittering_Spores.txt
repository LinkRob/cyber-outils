{{Term/Store|Glittering Spores||Series, SS}} are an item in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Glittering Spores are a special form of [[Mushroom Spores]] that can be obtained by slashing glittering [[mushroom]]s. Unlike Mushroom Spores, Glittering Spores allow five uses. If sprinkled on dropped items like [[Rupee]]s or [[Heart]]s, they can upgrade them to more valuable Rupees or [[Fairy|Fairies]]. If sprinkled on enemies, most of them will become stunned.
