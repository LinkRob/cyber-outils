The {{Term/Store|Fisherman's Hut||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Fisherman's Hut}}<br/>Rare fish inside!|Sign|MM3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Hut is situated in [[Great Bay Coast]], and it serves as the home of the [[Fisherman#Majora's Mask|Fisherman]], who seems to have a sort of obsession with the [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirates]] from the [[Pirates' Fortress]].

Inside the Hut is a rare fish called a [[Seahorse]].<ref>{{Cite|It's a rare fish, isn't it? It's called a sea horse.|Fisherman|MM}}</ref> The Fisherman offers to give [[Link]] the Seahorse in return of a [[Pictograph]] of a Gerudo Pirate.<ref>{{Cite|If you want that sea horse, bring me a pictograph of a female pirate.|Fisherman|MM}}</ref> 
Once Link gives him the Pictograph, the Fisherman will store the Seahorse in one of the young hero's empty [[Bottle]]s. The Seahorse must then be taken to the waters of [[Pinnacle Rock]] in order to retrieve the three [[Zora Egg]]s from the [[Deep Python]]s.
