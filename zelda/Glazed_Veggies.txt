{{Term/Store|Glazed Veggies|Glazed Veggies|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}} .<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Glazed Veggies}}<br/>Don't like the taste of vegetables? Simply sauté them in honey for a salty-sweet flavor!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Glazed Veggies are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any vegetable, herb, or flower in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} with [[Courser Bee Honey]]. Regular Glazed Veggies can be made by using ingredients with a different effect than the Courser Bee Honey. When eaten, they'll restore some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Glazed Veggies are vegetables sautéed with honey for a salty-sweet flavor, which makes them ideal for those who dislike the taste of vegetables.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Due to the limitations of their recipe, only one variation of Glazed Veggies exists:

* '''Energizing Glazed Veggies''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Glazed Veggies}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Don't like the taste of vegetables? Simply sauté them in honey for a salty-sweet flavour.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb|link}}, which doesn't have any effects. Consuming these veggies will refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>
