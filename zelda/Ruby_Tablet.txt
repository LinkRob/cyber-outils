The {{Term/Store|Ruby Tablet||Series, SS, SSHD}} is an item in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|SSHD Gold|Ruby Tablet}}! The weathered surface of this heavy stone tablet feels very old.|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Ruby Tablet is found in [[Skyview Spring]], located behind the room where Link fights [[Ghirahim]] in [[Skyview Temple]]. Like the altar on which the tablets are placed, it is revealed by hitting the Goddess Crest on Skyview Spring's altar with a Skyward Strike. After Link has done this, Fi will relay a message from the gods of old to him and then materialize the tablet.<ref>{{Cite|Master, I have a message written in the language of the gods of the old. Allow me to translate for you.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|From the edge of time I guide you, the one chosen to carry out the goddess's mission. The spirit maiden who descended from the clouds must travel to two sacred places to purify her body. You stand in one of these places: Skyview Spring. The other is known as the Earth Spring. This second spring is hidden away deep within the scorched earth of Eldin. The spirit maiden, ever mindful of the heavy task entrusted to her, has set out to this second sacred place.|Fi|SS}}</ref> When the Ruby Tablet is returned to the altar inside the Chamber of the Sword, a cloud rift with a red light pillar appears in the northern part of The Sky. This marks the way to Eldin Volcano.
