{{Term/Store|Robowear||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|Robowear is fashion of the future! It also improves your Gripshot. The future is here, and it's tres fab!}}

{{Term|TFH|Robowear}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Desert Temple|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}. Wearing it improves the firing speed and power of the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot|link}}.

With the additional power afforded by {{Term|TFH|Robowear}}, the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} will pass through [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]] to its full extension rather than retracting immediately, allowing it to hit multiple Enemies.<ref>{{Cite|Powers up the Gripshot so that it goes right through your foes. Gripping!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> It will deal damage to Enemies in addition to dizzying them, and will pull larger Enemies toward {{Term|TFH|Link}} where the regular {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} is unable to. Smaller Enemies that can be pulled by the regular {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}}, such as {{Plural|TFH|Keese|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Water Tektite|link}}, will be instantly defeated by {{Term|TFH|Robowear}}'s {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}}.

Although the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}}'s extension length is not increased by {{Term|TFH|Robowear}}, it has a slightly longer range due to its larger pincer.
