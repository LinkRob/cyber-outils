==Features and Overview==
The Rollin' Inn is a lodge in [[Goron City]]. It is managed by the [[Goron]]s [[Tray]], who runs the front desk, and [[Volcon]], who stands outside advertising the Inn and who also gives massages to its customers.<ref name="Rollin' Inn"/> Link can sleep in a regular bed to fully recover his health for 20 [[Rupee]]s. Rollin' Inn also offers a special bed and massage package that costs 80 Rupees, which will fully recover Link's health and also give him three temporary {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} and a single temporary [[Stamina Wheel]].<ref>{{Cite|A regular bed will cost ya {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}. If ya wanna throw in a Goron massage, that'll run ya {{Color|BotW Red|80 rupees}}.|Tray|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|New to the art of Goron massage, I take it? That's OK. First time for everything, as they say. A Goron-style massage is done on top of a hard rock bed. Pressure is applied to your body to squeeeeze the tension out. But I'll tell ya, it doesn't hurt one bit. Mostly. Honestly, I doze off sometimes! When it's all over, you'll sleep like a rock and feel great when ya wake up. You'll be all limber and full of energy!|Tray|BotW}}</ref>

During the night, the [[Gerudo]] traveler [[Ramella]] can be found sleeping in one of its beds, dreaming of Jewels.<ref>{{Cite|<small>{{Color|BotW Blue|Jewels}}... Zzz...</small>|Ramella|BotW}}</ref>
