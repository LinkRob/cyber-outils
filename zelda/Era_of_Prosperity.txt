The {{Term/Store|Era of Prosperity||Series, BotW}} is an [[Era]] in the [[Zelda Timeline]].<ref name="CaC 360">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 360}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Era of Prosperity}} began with the development of {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} 10,000 years before the events of {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC 360"/> Due to their advanced {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|display=Technology}}, the people of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} were protected from threats such as {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} and lived peacefully.<ref>{{Cite|Hyrule was then bolossoming as a highly advanced civilization. Even the most powerful monsters posed little threat to the denizens of the realm.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The kingdom of Hyrule was once a land of lasting peace / A culture of such strength and wit, that suffering did cease.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

===East Reservoir Lake===
{{Main|East Reservoir Lake}}
Every 10 years, the {{Term|BotW|Region|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)|link}} experiences heavy {{Term|BotW|rain|link}} that causes the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}} to flood.<ref>{{Cite|Once every 10 years, the Lanayru region experiences unusually heavy rainfall. The Zora River flooded every time.|History of the Zora, Part Two|BotW}}</ref> During the {{Term|BotW|Era of Prosperity}}, this would cause damage to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} and wash away the {{Plural|BotW|Zora|link}} who resided there.<ref>{{Cite|The tides damages not only our domain but our people, washing away poor souls and causing great suffering and disarray.|History of the Zora, Part Two|BotW}}</ref> After seeking aid from the {{Term|BotW|King of Hyrule|link}}, the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} joined forces to create the {{Term|BotW|Rutala Dam|link}} and {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Zora king of that time, after seeking aid from the king of Hyrule, rode out to see what could be done. By joining the architectural genius of the Zora and Hyrule's technological prowess, East Reservoir Lake was swiftly built.|History of the Zora, Part Two|BotW}}</ref> This project was completed in a single year, and it halted the floods that would plague {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}} from the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}.<ref name="CaC 362">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 362}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks to this fruitful partnership, Hyrule was no longer plagued by these devastating floods.|History of the Zora, Part Two|BotW}}</ref> Since then, the {{Term|BotW|Zora}} kings have maintained the level of the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake|display=Reservoir}}.<ref>{{Cite|In gratitude, the Zora king promised the king of Hyrule to manage the reservoir level to protect all of Hyrule from floods. Each Zora king since has kept that oath, spanning 10,000 years. That is why the reservoir signifies our bond with Hyrule.|History of the Zora, Part Two|BotW}}</ref>

===First Great Calamity===
{{Main|Great Calamity}}
[[File:BotW Calamity Ganon Tapestry.png|thumb|left|250px|The Battle against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}]]
{{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}} began to prepare for possibility of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}'s return.<ref>{{Cite|The people thought it wise to utilize their technological prowess to ensure the safety of the land, should Calamity Ganon ever return.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But Ganon lurked beneath the surface, strengthening its jaws / So the ancient people of Hyrule set out to help the cause.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> This involved the construction of the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}, which were designed to participate in the battle against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|They constructed four mechanical wonders that came to be known as the Divine Beasts. They also built a legion of autonomous weapons called Guardians.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Their efforts bore fruit in an automated force,<br/>To help avert Calamity by sealing it at its source.<br/>Four giant behemoths for which power never ceased,<br/>Each of these titans was called a "Divine Beast."<br/>And free-willed machines that hunted down their prey / These Guardians were built to last so they coud join the fray.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Four {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} were chosen to pilot the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Divine Beasts were piloted by four individuals of exceptional skill from across the land.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|To guide the beasts in battle, warriors were needed,<br/>So four Champions pledged to see Ganon defeated.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> With their {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link|section=Ten Thousand Years prior to Breath of the Wild|display=Princess}}, and {{Term|BotW|Link|link|section=The Hero 10,000 Years Prior to Breath of the Wild|display=Hero}}, {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}} was prepared to face {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|And thus, the plan to neutralize Ganon was forged.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Divine Beasts, Guardians, princess, and knight,<br/>Their plan to rout Ganon was looking airtight.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} inevitable emerged, {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}} was prepared to face him.<ref>{{Cite|Upon Ganon's inevitable return to Hyrule, the princess and the hero fought alongside these four Champions against this ancient evil.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And when Calamity Ganon reared its head, Hyrule rose against it / The optimism of Hyrule all the more incensed it.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|section=Ten Thousand Years prior to Breath of the Wild|display=Princess}} and the {{Term|BotW|Link|section=The Hero 10,000 Years Prior to Breath of the Wild|display=Hero}} were protected by the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} while the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} attacked {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Guardians were tasked with protecting the hero as the Divine Beasts unleashed a furious attack upon their terrible foe.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ganon raged in its assault, boiling with hate,<br/>It gnashed its teeth and thrashed about, but it was all too late.<br/>The Guardians kept the heroes safe through every hour...<br/>The Divine Beasts unleashed attacks that weakened Ganon's power.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After the final blow from the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|section=Ten Thousand Years prior to Breath of the Wild|display=Princess}} used her sacred power to seal {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} once more.<ref>{{Cite|And when the hero wielding the sword that seals the darkness delivered his final blow... The princess used her sacred power to seal away Calamity Ganon.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The hero with the sealing sword struck the final blow,<br/>And the holy power of the princess sealed Ganon so.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

===Banishment of Ancient Technology===
Fearing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}} of the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah}}, the {{Term|BotW|King of Hyrule}} and the rest of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} sought to get rid of it.<ref name="CaC 362"/><ref>{{Cite|We of the Sheikah tribe have long been heralded as a people of great wisdom. Our technology became the key to sealing {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}} away during the Great Calamity, some ten thousand years ago. At one point, our technology was praised as the power of the gods...but eventually the people turned on it. Turned on us. Our creations came to be viewed as a threat to the kingdom. The Sheikah became outcasts, forced into exile.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> Some {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah}} willingly parted ways with their {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|display=Technology}}, living normal lives afterwards and settling in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}.<ref name="CaC 362"/><ref>{{Cite|Some, like us, chose to cast off our technological advances and strove to live normal lives.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> However, others became hateful of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}} and formed the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}}, which swore loyalty to {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|Others formed a hatred toward the kingdom that shunned them. These sad souls swore their allegiance to {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}}. They now call themselves the {{Color|BotW Blue|Yiga Clan}}.|Cado|BotW}}</ref>

All forms of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}} were buried. However, the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah}} of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}} kept secret records pertaining to their locations. This included {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, which were hidden in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, respectively.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 375}}</ref> Other records referred to the types of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Giant Column|link}} hidden below {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|...But we have still yet to find all of the Guardians. Records mention a greater number of them—and even other types. They are said to be stored in five giant columns that rest beneath Hyrule Castle. The thing is... No matter how I search beneath the castle, I can't seem to locate these columns. They must be buried deep.|Zelda's Research Notes|BotW}}</ref>

===Second Great Calamity===
[[File:BotW Sacred Ground Champions.png|thumb|right|250px|{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}}]]
100 years before the beginning of {{BotW|-}}, a fortune-teller predicted the revival of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref name="CaC 362"/><ref>{{Cite|Stories of Ganon were passed from generation to generation in the form of legends and fairy tales. But there was also...a prophecy. "The signs of a resurrection of Calamity Ganon are clear. And the power to oppose it lies dormant beneath the ground."|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Following this advice, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} began mass excavations across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}, which uncovered {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}, several {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, and the four {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|We decided to heed the prophecy and began excavating large areas of land. It wasn't long before we discovered several ancient relics made by the hands of our distant ancestors. These relics, the Divine Beasts, were giant machines piloted by warriors. We also found the Guardians, an army of mechanical soldiers who fought autonomously.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} and the bearer of the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, were identified as the two who would seal away {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|We also learned of a princess with a sacred power and her appointed knight, chosen by the sword that seals the darkness. It was they who sealed Ganon away using the power of these ancient relics. One hundred years ago, there was a princess set to inherit a sacred power and a skilled knight at her side.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} were chosen as {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} and pilots of the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|We selected four skilled individuals from across Hyrule and tasked them with the duty of piloting the Divine Beasts.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

The second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}} occurred on the day of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}'s 17th birthday. Before the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} could return to the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}, {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} corrupted both them and the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} with his {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Blight Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|He appeared from deep below Hyrule Castle, seized control of the Guardians and the Divine Beasts, and turned them against us.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> During this attack, {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town|link}} was destroyed, the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} were killed, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} collapsed while protecting {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Champions lost their lives. Those residing in the castle as well. The appointed knight, gravely wounded, collapsed while defending the princess... And thus, the kingdom of Hyrule was devastated absolutely by Calamity Ganon.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

After unlocking her powers at {{Term|BotW|Blatchery Plain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} returned to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}} to face {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} on her own.<ref>{{Cite|However... The princess survived... to face Ganon alone.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} was taken to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Take Link to the Shrine of Resurrection. If you don't get him there immediately, we are going to lose him forever! Is that clear?!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And the courageous knight who protected her right up to the very end... That knight was none other than you, Link. You fought valiantly when your fate took an unfortunate turn. And then, you were taken to the Shrine of Resurrection. Here you now stand revitalized, 100 years later.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Dr. Purah and I had already taken Link to the Shrine of Resurrection and left him to recover.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} constrained {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} within the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}}, which is part of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}.<ref>{{Cite|Even now, as she works to restrain Ganon from within Hyrule Castle, she calls out for your help.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>
