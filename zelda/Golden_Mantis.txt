{{Term/Store|Golden Mantis|Golden Mantises|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are items in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 66}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[File:Mantis (M).png|left]]
[[File:Mantis (F).png|left]]
The male Golden Mantis can be found at the northern portion of the [[Great Hylian Bridge]]. The female Golden Mantis is located at the southern portion of the Bridge. [[Link]] must use the [[Clawshot]] or the [[Gale Boomerang]] to reach them. They can be given to [[Agitha]] in exchange for different items, such as a bigger [[Wallet]], [[Rupee]]s, or her [[Miiverse Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}. If collected a second time, they will drop a [[Heart]].
