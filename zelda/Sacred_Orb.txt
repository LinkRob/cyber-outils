==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}} is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} that is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack. It has been treasured and protected by the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo|link}} for generations.<ref name="Riju"/> During the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can hear the part {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song|link}} left by {{Term|BotW|Kass|link|display=Kass's}} teacher that references {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link|display=Urbosa's}} trials.<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Urbosa}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> One of the lyrics mentions the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}}, instructing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to throw it underground.<ref>{{Cite|♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|throw the orb underground}}.<br/>Champion, trials abound!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}} about the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}}. {{Term|BotW|Riju}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to help them retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}} from the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link... Would you be willing to help us recover a sacred treasure from those fiends once more?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> If he does so, she will allow him to use it in order to complete the trial.<ref>{{Cite|In return, you may use the {{Color|BotW Red|treasured orb}} however you please to complete the trial.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}}, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} wishes him luck in finding it.<ref>{{Cite|Perhaps it is fate, as that {{Color|BotW Red|orb}} was made to be used for the very trial you are challenging. The front entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout}} through {{Term|BotW|Karusa Valley|link}} is inaccessible at this time. Therefore, in order to find the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must enter through the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. At this back entrance, {{Term|BotW|Mils|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mina|link}} will be scouting the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|display=Hideout}}. The pair followed the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}} back to this location with the intention of stealing and selling the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Are you looking for treasure too? The {{Color|BotW Red|Yiga Clan}}... They took one of the Gerudo Tribe's treasures in there, a {{Color|BotW Red|sacred orb}} or something. The {{Color|BotW Red|orb they stole from the Gerudo tribe}} is in their second floor storage area.|Mils|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Mils}} considers taking it to be too great of a risk.<ref>{{Cite|But getting there is too dangerous. Staying alive is essential to being a great treasure hunter!|Mils|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mina}} suspects that the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}} are planning to throw the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}} into the bottomless pit in accordance with an old legend.<ref>{{Cite|Wait. Inside the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout}}, {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Blademaster|link}} have returned to their patrols. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must either defeat them and the {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} they summon or sneak past them. Afterwards, he will be able to leave the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|display=Hideout}} with the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}}. {{Term|BotW|Mils}} is surpised that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was able to steal the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}},<ref>{{Cite|Did you really go in there... and nab {{Color|BotW Red|that orb}}? To complete the trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must throw the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}} into the nearby pit. This will cause {{Term|BotW|Kihiro Moh Shrine|link}} to emerge from the ground nearby. {{Term|BotW|Mina}} scolds {{Term|BotW|Link}} for wastefully throwing away the treasure,<ref>{{Cite|You're throwing away treasure?!
