{{Term/Store|Torch of Wisdom|Torches of Wisdom|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.{{Name Ref|CoH}}

==Location and Uses==
Torches of Wisdom are a type of {{Term|CoH|Torch|link}} that {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} can use to see the items inside {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}}, bushes, columns of dirt, and pots without the need to break them open.<ref>{{Cite|Reveals all items, even ones inside chests and other objects. Can burn stumps and light up braziers.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> Like other torches, they also allow visibility within dark areas and burn away stumps, but at the cost of durability unless the {{Term|CoH|Glass Ring of Preservation|link}} is equipped.

They can be found either from a red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest}} or bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} for 60 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}}.
