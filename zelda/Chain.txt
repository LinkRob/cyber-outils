'''Chain''' is [[Sheik]]'s side special move in {{SSBM}} and {{SSBB}}.

==Overview==
When used, Sheik pulls out a metal chain out in front of herself, the direction of which can be determined using the Control Stick. If the chain hits at the tip on an opponent, it inflicts electric damage.

Since {{SSB4}}, Sheik has [[Burst Grenade]] as her side special move instead.
