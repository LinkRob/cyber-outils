==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Upon death, Raw Bird Thighs can be dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Rainbow Pigeon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hotfeather Pigeon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|White Pigeon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Islander Hawk|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Eldin Ostrich|link}}. The travelling merchant [[Mezer]] sells Raw Bird Thighs near [[Dueling Peaks Stable]]. When eaten, Raw Bird Thighs will heal {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} for one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. Raw Bird Thighs can be grilled by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} for {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Bird Thigh|link}} and chilled with [[Ice]] to create {{Plural|BotW|Frozen Bird Thigh|link}}.

Raw Bird Thighs are required to make [[Prime Poultry Curry]] and [[Prime Poultry Pilaf]]. Either Raw Bird Thighs or [[Raw Prime Meat]] is needed to make [[Prime Meat Stew]], [[Prime Meat and Rice Bowl]], [[Salt-Grilled Prime Meat]], and [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]]. As is the case with all types of meat, they can also be used to make [[Creamy Meat Soup]], [[Glazed Meat]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Meat Pie]], a [[Meat Skewer]], [[Copious Meat Skewers]], [[Meaty Rice Balls]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Steak]], [[Steamed Meat]], and a [[Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin]].
