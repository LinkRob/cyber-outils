The {{Term/Store|Couple's Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's the {{Color|MM Red|Couple's Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>That is truly a very fine mask. It's overflowing with powerful feelings of love and gratitude.</p>}}

The Couple's Mask is obtained by completing the reuniting [[Kafei]] and [[Anju]] sidequest. If worn in the [[Mayor's Official Residence]] during a meeting in any of the three days, [[Mayor Dotour]] will end the meeting and give [[Link]] a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|The Couple's Mask. What a nice reminder...So, a young couple has been married. [...] Thank you for allowing me to put an end to all that pointless bickering. [...] Here is a token of my gratitude.|Mayor Dotour|MM}}</ref>

The Couple's Mask is a sign of two people dedicating their lives to each other by creating two wedding masks and combining them to form the Mask, a ritual which signified that the two people are now a couple.<ref>{{Cite|Let us exchange the promised masks. We have exchanged our oaths and have become a couple. You are our witnesses. Please accept this mask.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> It is formed through the merging of the [[Sun Mask|Sun]] and [[Moon Mask]]s. The merging happens if the two lovers press each mask against the other.
{{Clear|right}} 

==Trivia==
*The Couple's Mask is one of the few non-unique masks in the game. The other examples are the [[Keaton Mask]], which each of the [[Bombers Secret Society of Justice|Bombers]] claim to have; [[Romani's Mask]], which is owned by all customers of [[Latte]]; the [[Garo's Mask]], which the [[Gorman Brothers]] (excluding the middle brother) own; and [[Kafei's Mask]], which Anju also has.
