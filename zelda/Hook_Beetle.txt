The {{Term/Store|Hook Beetle||Series, SS, SSHD}} is an {{Term|SSHD|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 127 }}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The standard {{Term|SSHD|Beetle|link}} can be upgraded to the {{Term|SSHD|Hook Beetle}} in the {{Term|SSHD|Lanayru Desert|link}} by an {{Term|SSHD|LD-301|link}} series of {{Term|SSHD|Ancient Robot|link}}, adding a mandible that is able to carry items like {{Plural|SSHD|Bomb|link}} or [[Ampilus Egg|Ampilus Eggs]] and drop them anywhere.<ref>"''You got the Hook Beetle! The big pincers enable you to pick up and carry things with it!''" ''—'' N/A ''([[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword HD|Skyward Sword HD]])''</ref>

==Trivia==
*{{Plural|SSHD|Bokoblin|link}} are afraid of the {{Term|SSHD|Hook Beetle}} and will run away from it. {{Plural|SSHD|Red Bokoblin (Archer)|link|display= Archer Bokoblins}} will attempt to shoot it at first sight.
*The {{Term|SSHD|Hook Beetle}} cannot be used during the {{Term|SSHD|Horde Battle|link}}.
