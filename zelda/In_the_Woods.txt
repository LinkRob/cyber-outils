{{Term/Store|In the Woods||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|In the Woods|Map|SSHD}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|SS}}
{{Term|SS|In the Woods}} is located within the {{Term|SS|Faron Woods|link}}, accessible after passing through {{Term|SS|Faron Woods Entry|link}} at its west. It contains the {{Term|SS|Great Tree|link}} and leads to {{Term|SS|Lake Floria|link}} to the southeast and the {{Term|SS|Viewing Platform|link}} to the north.
