{{Plural|BotW|Falcon Bow}} are highly refined {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} made by master {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} craftsmen.<ref name="Inventory"/> These {{Plural|BotW|Bow}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Rito Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> They have a quick rate of fire, which {{Term|BotW|Rito}} warriors favor for use in aerial combat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Falcon Bow}} are found both in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} and in the hands of {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} and in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. During the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}} will allow {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to take a {{Term|BotW|Swallow Bow}} he has modified from a nearby {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} for their fight against {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Inside that treasure chest is a {{Color|BotW Blue|bow}} I've modified. Take it, and let me know when you're ready.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> A Falcon Bow that respawns every [[Blood Moon]] can be found laying on the wall in the Barracks section of [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]].

The following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Falcon Bow}} when they are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Link}} (Archer) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Falcon Bow}} will burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. In addition, they will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. Finally, they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
