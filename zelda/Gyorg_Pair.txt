The {{Term/Store|Gyorg Pair||Series, TMC}} is the collective name given to the {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Female|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Male|link}}, the {{Plural|TMC|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gyorg Pair|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Gyorg Pair}} was referred to as the '''Gyorgs''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 77}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{TMC}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Gyorg Pair}}
The {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Pair}} appears at the end of the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}} at the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}. The battle takes place mid-air, forcing {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to jump from one {{Term|TMC|Gyorg}} to the other by using {{Term|TMC|Roc's Cape|link}}. As soon as {{Term|TMC|Link}} enters the battle room, he lands on the back of the {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Male|link}}, who will deposit him onto the back of the {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Female|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Link}} must multiply by charging the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} in order to attack the multiple eyes of the {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Female|display=Female}}. The {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Male|display=Male}} will fire projectiles at {{Term|TMC|Link}} to prevent this. While riding the {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Male}}, he will attack with his tail to knock {{Term|TMC|Link}} off. Eventually, the {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Female}} will also release a swarm of green {{Term|TMC|Gyorg}} children. Their defeat earns {{Term|TMC|Link}} the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}.
