"{{Term/Store|The Gut Check Challenge||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Gut Check Challenge}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Gut Check Rock}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the top of {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr Shrine|link}}, though his entrance his blocked by {{Term|BotW|Bayge|link}} of the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches him, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} reveals that his training ground, and it is where he and his brothers come to build up their muscle.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|PUMP IT UP, BROTHER!}} By the gods, I love me some muscle. This right here is our hard-core training ground, string bean. This is where we come to get RIPPED. AND. SHREDDED. It's where Goron boys become Goron men, brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already completed the "{{Term|BotW|Test of Will|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will recognize and welcome him instead.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you're the guy who won the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}}! Welcome to our Goron training ground, the home of... {{Big|Gut Check Challenge!!}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>}} If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} to move out of his way, he will refuse.<ref>{{Cite|[Um...please move.] {{Big|I AIN'T MOVIN' A MUSCLE, BROTHER! Not a single, well-defined muscle!}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Travel Gate|link}} has been designated as a sacred spot by the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} for those who have completed the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is a special platform! Sacred even! Only real Goron men, as decided by us three Goron Blood Brothers, are allowed.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This right here is a special platform that's only for those who've conquered the {{Color|BotW Red|Gut Check Challenge}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He is also hesitant to interrupt his brother {{Term|BotW|Kabetta|link|display=Kabetta's}} training.<ref>{{Cite|Besides, my littlest bro is in the middle of trainin' right now. A brother does NOT interrupt a brother's trainin', brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

However, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} notes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to be a man of action, which he likes.<ref>{{Cite|But I gotta say...ya do seem like a real man of action. Almost like you have a Goron air about ya. I like it!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already completed the "{{Term|BotW|Test of Will}}", {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will instead decide to make an exception for him.<ref>{{Cite|That said...you ARE the guy who won the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}}. So I guess my question is...|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>}} He decides to allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}}, and if he clears it, he promises to move out of the way.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|SO WHAT ARE YA WAITIN' FOR, BROTHER?! GET TRAININ'!}} Yeah that's it! {{Color|BotW Red|If you can clear our hard-core training program, I'll move outta your way}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will be impressed and tells him to prepare.<ref>{{Cite|[Count me in, brother!] Ha! Good answer, brother! We're gonna shred you to pieces, little man. Prepare your tiny self!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Bayge}} decides to explain their training to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|OK, lemme explain how this hard-core training works. The rules are dead simple. Simple as a deadlift, brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He must climb from the bottom of {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}} all the way to the top within the time limit set.<ref>{{Cite|Startin' from the bottom, ya gotta climb back up here within the time limit.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Along the way, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must also pick up {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}},<ref>{{Cite|BUT! Don't fool yourself into thinking it's only a climbin' challenge, little man. You've gotta {{Color|BotW Red|pick up rupees}} as ya go!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> though he does not specify how many are necessary.<ref>{{Cite|Get up the cliff within the time limit, and grab a {{Color|BotW Red|passable number of rupees}}. Do that, and you pass the test!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} run out of time or give up, he will have to return any {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} he has collected.<ref>{{Cite|If ya run outta time or decide to give up halfway through your climb... {{Big|You'll have to return all the rupees ya picked up! That's life, brother.}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> This {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge|display=Challenge}} will push {{Term|BotW|Link}} past his physical, mental, and emotional limits.<ref>{{Cite|We're gonna help ya push past your limits, brother! Your physical, mental, AND emotional limits!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} decides it is finally time to begin,<ref>{{Cite|OK, enough messin's around. It's time for the hardest of hard-core feats... {{Big|GUT CHECK CHALLENGE!}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> which initiates the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

At this point, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} mentions the cost of 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} per attempt.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Almost forgot a tiny detail, tiny man. No train, no gain. And no pay, no stay! So yeah, it's {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} per attempt.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to pay, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} tasks {{Term|BotW|Link}} with collecting 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's do this thing!] You've gotta collect {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}} as ya climb. A hard-core Goron man should be able to handle that no problem!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He also informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will fail if he does not collect enough, even if he makes it up in time.<ref>{{Cite|Even if ya reach the goal before time runs out, if ya don't grab enough rupees, ya fail! DON'T FAIL, BROTHER!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> With these reminders out of the way, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} officially starts the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}}.<ref>{{Cite|Got it? Good. Then it's finally time for... {{Big|GUT CHECK CHALLENGE!! GOOOO!}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

After successfully clearing the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} happily accepts Link as an honorary fourth Blood Brother.<ref>{{Cite|UNBELIEVABLE!! You've passed our training with the grit of a true Goron, my brother! In fact...you've earned the right to call yourself...the fourth Goron Blood Brother!!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You've passed our training with the grit of a true Goron, my brother!! Now that you've managed to clear our extreme training AND the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}}... You've earned the right to call yourself... the fourth Goron Blood Brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to stand on their sacred platform.<ref>{{Cite|As promised, you are now allowed to stand on this sacred platform of Goron manliness. A real honor, I know.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> As he is now officially a {{Term|BotW|Goron}} brother, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} welcomes {{Term|BotW|Link}} to their family.<ref>{{Cite|You're officially one of us now! Our true Goron brother, brother! Welcome to the family!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will now be able to challenge the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}} whenever he likes, in addition to trying a harder course.<ref>{{Cite|And good news, fourth brother! That means you can do the GUT CHECK CHALLENGE all you want! As a Goron Blood Brother, you're ready for the HARD stuff! Just lemme know when you're pumped enough to try it!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
