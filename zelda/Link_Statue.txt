==={{OoA|-}}===
Once {{Term|OoA|Link}} defeats {{Term|OoA|Veran|link}} atop the {{Term|OoA|Black Tower|link}} in {{OoA}}, peace returns to {{Term|OoA|Labrynna|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Link. Thanks to you, Lynna City is a peaceful place once again!|Nayru (Oracle)|OoA}}</ref> Afterwards, Link is treated with utmost respect by the inhabitants of {{Term|OoA|Lynna City|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You! Kids aren't allowed in... Sir Link! Excuse me!|Guard|OoA}}</ref> Statues of Link are placed within the village,<ref>{{Cite|You look just like the statue outside... Say! Are you Link?!? I never thought I'd actually get to see you!|N/A|OoA}}</ref> which can be seen in the present age as well. The statue can be seen in the credits of {{OoA|-}}, with {{Term|OoA|Queen Ambi|link}} overseeing its construction.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, a statue of the [[Hero of Time]] - the Link from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', appears in {{Term|TWWHD|Hyrule Castle|link}} deep below the surface of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}. It is a monument to the Hero, and hides the basement chamber where the {{Term|TWWHD|Master Sword}} lies. The statue can be moved after Link, the Hero of Winds, completes a puzzle, granting the young hero access. Once Link exits the chamber with the [[Master Sword]], the statue moves back into place.

After Link and {{Term|TWWHD|Tetra|link}} flee from {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}}, the statue is once again moved, so that they may enter the chamber. Once in the chamber, Link and {{Term|TWWHD|Tetra}} meet {{Term|TWWHD|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|link}}, who reveals that he is the {{Term|TWWHD|King of Red Lions|link}}. Alongside this, {{Term|TWWHD|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|display=Daphnes}} also reveals that {{Term|TWWHD|Tetra}} is his descendant, {{Term|TWWHD|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You are the true heir of the royal family of Hyrule...the last link in the bloodline. You are Princess Zelda.|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref> After learning of her true identity and destiny, Tetra as Zelda decides to wait inside the basement chamber until Link obtains the {{Term|TWWHD|Triforce of Courage|link}} and restores the {{Term|TWWHD|Master Sword}} to its full power.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda... It is far too dangerous for you to join us in this task. Fortunately, this sacred chamber is not yet known to Ganon. It is my wish that you wait here in hiding until we return.|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref>

Once Link has gained the power to defeat {{Term|TWWHD|Ganondorf|link}}, he returns to find that the statue has been destroyed in half and the chamber left open. As he heads inside, the young hero finds that {{Term|TWWHD|Princess Zelda}} has been kidnapped by {{Term|TWWHD|Ganondorf}}, who leaves Link to fight two {{Plural|TWWHD|Mighty Darknut|link}} inside the chamber.
{{Clear|left}}

==={{TPHD|-}}===
On the 40th and final floor of the {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Shadows|link}} is a large, stone statue of {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}} based on the {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} [[amiibo]]. On top of {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}}'s head is a secret [[Digging|dig spot]] containing an {{Term|TPHD|Orange Rupee|link}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Main|Tri Force Hero Statue}}
A statue of three {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} in a {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}} formation lies at the center of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}, in recognition of the legendary heroes who arrive whenever the Kingdom is in peril.<ref>{{Cite|When the kingdom of Hytopia falls into despair, three courageous heroes shall ever arrive to save the day...|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> This Statue serves as the main {{Term|TFH|Saving}} point in {{TFH}}.

Inside {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}, three busts of individual {{Plural|TFH|Links}} stand in a row beside the entrance to the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}}.
