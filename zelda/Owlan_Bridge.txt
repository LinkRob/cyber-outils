{{Term/Store|Owlan Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Owlan Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Owlan Bridge is located between [[Hyrule Field]] and [[West Necluda]]. It is a battered up wooden bridge that passes over [[Hylia River]]. 

A wooden platform held aloft by [[Sky Octorok]]s appears due south of the Bridge in [[Master Mode]], bearing a metal [[Treasure Chest]] which contains a [[Knight's Bow]].

A [[Korok]] can be found by breaking an [[Acorn]]-shaped {{Term|BotW|Vase|link}} suspended from a chain under the Bridge.
