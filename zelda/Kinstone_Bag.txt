The {{Term/Store|Kinstone Bag||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Manual|TMC|Kinstone Bag|Store your Kinstone pieces in this handy little bag.}}
The {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag}} is used to carry {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}}. It is received in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} after the completion of the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}. Over the course of the game, the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|display=Bag}} will grow and bulge as {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} collects more {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}. Despite this, the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag}} has an infinite {{Term|TMC|Kinstone}} carrying capacity. The {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag}} will be turned into the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Trophy|link}} by the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Siblings?|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone}} with all one hundred {{Term|TMC|Kinstone}}-holding characters in the game.
