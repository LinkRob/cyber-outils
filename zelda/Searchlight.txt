{{Term/Store|Searchlight|Searchlights|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD|publisher= Prima|page= 72}}</ref>{{Name Ref|FSA}} They are devices used by enemies to detect Link's presence. They appear in the [[Forsaken Fortress]] in {{TWW}} and in the [[Infiltration of Hyrule Castle]] stage in {{FSA}}. Both places are, fittingly, locations Link tries to infiltrate. Link must use [[Stealth]] to avoid being seen by the Searchlights. 

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:TWW Forsaken Fortress.png|thumb|right|The Forsaken Fortress is well guarded thanks to the Searchlights]]
Searchlights are first seen in {{TWW}} as a part of the [[Forsaken Fortress]]'s defense system. There are, in total, three pairs of Searchlights in the Forsaken Fortress (making six in total). Each pair of Searchlights is located on a turret of the fortress and is manned by a single [[Bokoblin]].<ref>{{Cite|There's a monster running the searchlight up there!|Tetra|TWW}}</ref> Should the Bokoblin encounter a distraction, it will lift the Searchlight to the skies before leaving its post. It will return to its post as soon as the distraction disappears. If the Bokoblin is killed, the Searchlight will be permanently disabled.<ref>{{Cite|If you can slay the monster, then just maybe it'll shut the searchlight down.|Tetra|TWW}}</ref>

It is because of the Searchlights that Link is sent into the Forsaken Fortress via catapult on his first visit. If Link is seen by the Searchlights while sneaking around the Fortress, an alarm is sounded and Link will be thrown into a jail cell.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 38}}</ref> On his second visit to the Forsaken Fortress (when Link has his [[Sword]] to defend himself), [[Bomb]]s will fire at Link and [[Miniblins]] will begin to attack him if he is detected by a Searchlight.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 79}}</ref> If Link hides underneath a [[Barrel]] and remains still, the Searchlights will pass over without detecting him.

One of the Searchlights shines directly on the ledge beneath the window of the chamber where [[Aryll]], {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie|link}}, and [[Mila]] are held captive. Link must disable that Searchlight to sidle across the ledge and reach the upper section of the Forsaken Fortress. Disabling the other two pairs of Searchlights is optional. 

==={{FSA|-}}===
Searchlights appear at the beginning of the [[Infiltration of Hyrule Castle]] stage in {{FSA}}. They peer over the [[Hyrule Castle|Castle]] walls as the [[Links]] try to find a way into the castle. Much like in {{TWW|-}}, if one of the Links is spotted, an alarm will be sounded and Link will be thrown in jail.

The Links can avoid detection from the Searchlights by diving underwater as it passes over them. If a [[Jar]] is shattered, the Searchlights will promptly converge at the source of the sound. Although this may cause the Links to be captured, it can also be used as a decoy to sneak past the Searchlights; if it is thrown away from the Links it will create a distraction for them when it shatters.
