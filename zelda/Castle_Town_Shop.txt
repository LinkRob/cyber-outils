{{Term/Store|Castle Town Shop||Series, ST}} is a location in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|We have anything you might need here! ～Castle Town Shop～|Sign|ST}}</ref> It is a small shop that offers basic items to its customers, and it is the first shop where a [[Shield]] can be bought. It also offers a few [[Treasure]]s for sale. It is owned and operated by {{Term|ST|Shitate|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
The Shop is located in the northeast section of [[Hyrule Castle Town#Spirit Tracks|Hyrule Castle Town]]. The Treasures offered in the Castle Town Shop are medium-rare Treasures such as [[Ruto Crown]]s and [[Pirate Necklace]]s, all of which can be bought for 300 [[Rupee]]s each. Only two Treasures are available at any time, and the ones it offers are random. After Link buys one Treasure from the store, it will take a day for the shop to restock with a new, random Treasure.
