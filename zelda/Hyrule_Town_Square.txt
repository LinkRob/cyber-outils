{{Term/Store|Hyrule Town Square||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 37}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square}} is located in the central area of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. It is surrounded by several shops, including the {{Term|TMC|Bakery|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Chest Mini-Game Shop|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Rem's Shoe Store|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Simon's Simulations|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|link}}, as well as the {{Term|TMC|Music House|link}} owned by {{Term|TMC|Herb|link}}.

In addition, the central area of the square is home to a number of stalls and vendors, including {{Term|TMC|Brocco|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Pina|link}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} uses the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}} to clean the stalls of dust, {{Term|TMC|Beedle|link}} will eventually arrive in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} to sell {{Term|TMC|Picolyte|link}}. If the dust is cleared and {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with a {{Term|TMC|Goron|link}} in the cave near {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Goron Merchant|link}} will also arrive and begin selling {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}.
