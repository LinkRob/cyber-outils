{{Term/Store|Sun Pattern|Sun Patterns|Series, MM|plural}},{{Exp Game|MM3D}}<ref>{{Cite|Canyon, #4: Sun Pattern A|Sheikah Stone|MM3D}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Sun Switch|Sun Switches|OoT|plural}}{{Exp Game|OoT3D}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 85}}</ref> are recurring objects in {{TLoZ|Series}}. {{Plural||Sun Pattern}} go unnamed in {{TWW}} and {{HW|L}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Plural|OoT|Sun Pattern}} are [[Switch]]es that can be found in the [[Spirit Temple]] and [[Inside Ganon's Castle]] in {{OoT}}. They can be activated by shining a [[Light Beam]] on them. If a {{Term|OoT|Sun Pattern}} is not the direct path of a Light Beam, it can be  reflected at the {{Term|OoT|Sun Pattern}} by rotating a [[Mirror (Object)|Mirror]] or using the [[Mirror Shield]]. Upon being activated, {{Plural|OoT|Sun Pattern}} will either brighten up and smile or they will burn up and disappear. Some {{Plural|OoT|Sun Pattern}} may be trapped and cause enemies such as [[Wallmaster]]s to appear when activated.

==={{MM|-}}===
Sun Patterns can be found in the [[Stone Tower Temple]] in {{MM}}. As well as being activated by shining Light Beams at them, Sun Patterns can also be activated by shooting them with [[Light Arrow]]s. Some Sun Patterns will burn up and disappear when activated, the rest will brighen up and smile.

In {{MM3D}}, the appearance of Sun Patterns were changed to more closely resemble [[Eyeball Switch]]es in a sun. When activated, the Sun Patterns brighten up and close their eye. 

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Plural|TWW|Sun Pattern}} often appear in pairs in the [[Earth Temple (The Wind Waker)|Earth Temple]] in {{TWW}}. Each pair of {{Plural|TWW|Sun Pattern}} appear in a large statue designed to appear as its eyes. They can be activated by shining Light Beams on them, which can be done by reflecting light with the [[Mirror Shield]] or using [[Medli]]'s [[Rito Harp]]. Activating a {{Term|TWW|Sun Pattern}} causes half of the face of the statue they are in to brighten up but the {{Term|TWW|Sun Pattern}} will deactivate and the statue will darken if the Light Beam stops shining on the {{Term|TWW|Sun Pattern}}. If both {{Plural|TWW|Sun Pattern}} are activated, the face of the statue will light up, creating a glow that dissipates [[Dark Fog]] and can cause walls weak to Light Beams to crumble. When the statue creates the glow, the {{Plural|TWW|Sun Pattern}} will no longer deactivate and bars of a door will be raised or a series of platforms will lower to form a stairway.

A lone {{Term|TWW|Sun Pattern}} appears in the room where Link obtains the [[Mirror Shield]]. Shining a Light Beam at it will raise the bars on the door, allowing Link to leave.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
{{Plural|HWL|Sun Pattern}} appear in the [[Wind and Earth Temples]] Stage in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}}. Unlike other {{Plural|HWL|Sun Pattern}}, they cannot be activated and instead appear in pairs resembling the eyes of the statues in the nooks in the walls around the Faces Keep.
{{Noncanon|End}}
