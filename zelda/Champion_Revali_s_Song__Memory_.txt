"{{Term/Store|Champion Revali's Song||Series, BotW}}" is the first {{Term|BotW|EX Recovered Memory|link}} in {{TCB}} DLC Pack for {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Champion Revali's Song}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|EX Recovered Memory #1}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|Champion Revali's Song (Memory)}}" is triggered by listening to {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} play the {{Term|BotW|Champion Revali's Song (Song)|link|display=song of the same name}}, in which he describes the events of the {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪Recall {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}'s Champion of flight,<br/>before he fell to his fateful plight.♪<br/>♪{{Color|BotW Blue|Revali}}'s feat was a wonder to see,<br/>so the princess made her plea.♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> It opens with {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} riding her {{Term|BotW|White Horse|link}} up to the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|link}} with two guards. There, she encounters {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}}, where he activates his {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale|link|display=Gale}} and uses it to fly up into the sky. However, he ends up thrown off and gets caught in the whirlwind, and he crashes to the ground.

Picking himself up, {{Term|BotW|Revali}} reprimands himself, saying it is not enough and that he needs to stay in the eye of the whirlwind.<ref>{{Cite|Not enough. I must stay in the eye of the whirlwind.... must push myself harder...|Revali|BotW}}</ref> However, he notices {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} watching and reminds her that it is rude to eavesdrop.<ref>{{Cite|You know, your highness... it's rude to eavesdrop.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} apologizes, saying the citizens of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}} told her he could be found there.<ref>{{Cite|My apologies. I went to the village, and I was told I could find you here.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Revali}} deduces that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} has come to recruit him in the fight against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have need of me. To defeat Calamity Ganon.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> He tells her that it will be his pleasure to slay the beast.<ref>{{Cite|To slay the beast once and for all... It will be my great pleasure.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> Joyfully, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} starts to thank {{Term|BotW|Revali}} and assure him of their ability to defeat the {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Calamity}} if they work together.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Revali. If we work together, I'm certain we'll be able to defeat—|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> but before she can finish, {{Term|BotW|Revali}} interrupts her.<ref>{{Cite|However!|Revali|BotW}}</ref>

Once again, {{Term|BotW|Revali}} activates his {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale|display=Gale}}, but this time he is able to fly to its height without falling. Once he is airborne, he locks onto three of the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Target|link}}. He dives down and nocks three {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}, destroying all of the {{Plural|BotW|Target}} at once. Landing on the rocks at the bottom of the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|display=Range}}, he uses his {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale|display=Gale}} once more to soar around, blowing up all of the {{Plural|BotW|Target}}.

{{Term|BotW|Revali}} lands outside of the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range}}'s hut, and he asserts to {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} that he will play the biggest role in helping the {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=knight}} with the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link|display=darkness-sealing sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|I know I play the biggest part in helping that, ahem... that little knight with the darkness-sealing sword. Correct?|Revali|BotW}}</ref> He tells her that if {{Term|BotW|Link}} loses his confidence after seeing him in action not to come crying to him.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if he loses his confidence after seeing me in action... don't come crying to me.|Revali|BotW}}</ref>
