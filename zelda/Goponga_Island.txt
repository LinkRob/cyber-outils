{{Term/Store|Goponga Island||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Goponga Island'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Goponga Island is located in the [[Lanayru Wetlands]]. A large portion of the Goponga Village Ruins are located here. Several Lizalfos and an Electric Wizzrobe reside here.

==Nomenclature==
Goponga Island derives its name from [[Goponga Swamp]].
