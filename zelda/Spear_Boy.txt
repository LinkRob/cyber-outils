The {{Term/Store|Spear Boy||Series}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Biography==
The {{Term|ALBW|Spear Boy}} is initially in charge of guarding the {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Hideout|link}}, and is presumably the one who asks anyone trying to enter their knowledge of the band's rhyme. Once {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} gains access to the Hideout, he will think {{Term|ALBW|Link}} to be a new recruit come to relieve him of guard duty. The {{Term|ALBW|Spear Boy}} then leaves the Hideout for some fresh meat and fresh air, thinking that the {{Term|ALBW|Bard|link}} could put his words to song.

Despite him hinting heading toward the {{Term|ALBW|Milk Bar|link}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Spear Boy}} is never seen again after leaving the {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Hideout}}.
