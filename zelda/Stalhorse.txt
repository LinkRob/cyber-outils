==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Stalhorse}}
A {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}} is an undead skeletal {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} reanimated by {{Term|BotW|Ganon|link}}'s magic.<ref name="Compendium">{{Cite|This skeletal horse is ridden by monsters. It was once a regular horse, but Ganon's power revived it from death. It cannot maintain its bone structure in the daytime.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Plural|BotW|Stalhorse}} behave and function like regular {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} and pose no threat to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. They only appear at night, as they cannot maintain their bone structure during the day.<ref name="Compendium"/> If a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}} is present as the sun rises, it will fall to pieces and disappear. They are usually encountered with {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} riding on their backs.

Despite its lack of stomach, {{Plural|BotW|Stalhorse}} can eat both {{Plural|BotW|Carrot|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}} like a living Horse, though they cannot be registered at a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|AAAH! What the— What is that thing? Why are its eyes...glowing? Sorry, buddy... This is a stable for horses, not nightmares.|Embry|TotK}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to register one at a {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} owner will call it a monster and refuse to board it, as it might eat the other {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|What in Hylia's name?! That's a monster, not a horse! Sorry, but we can't board that... thing here. It might eat the other horses.|Varke|BotW}}</ref> However this is apparently superstition, as Stalhorse are shown to behave identically to their living counterparts. Stalhorses will not harm any Horses tamed by Link, and wild Horses will not flee when they detect them. Domesticated livestock such as cows, sheep, goats, and {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Retriever|link}} will flee from Stalhorses due to their monstrous appearance. Wearing the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Set|link}} causes {{Plural|BotW|Stalhorse}} to run at high speeds when ridden.

{{Plural|BotW|Stalhorse}} serve a role in the "{{Term|BotW|Stalhorse: Pictured!|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Juannelle|link}} is spoken to at {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}}, she will state that she has heard there is a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}} is in the area, but it is too cold for her to search for it.<ref>{{Cite|I heard there was a {{Color|BotW Red|Stalhorse}} in this area, but I can't take it any longer! It's too cold to look anymore!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} will offer to give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a reward if he brings her some notes or a picture of the {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Can you handle this task for me? I'll even reward you! All I need is a picture! Or some notes!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> Based on the rumor, she advises {{Term|BotW|Link}} to try and find a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}}, along with its {{Term|BotW|Stalkoblin}} rider in the {{Term|BotW|North Tabantha Snowfield|link}},<ref>{{Cite|There's a rumor that it can be found with Stalkoblins in the {{Color|BotW Blue|North Tabantha Snowfield}}.|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> but states that a picture of a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}} from any location would work. If a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}} is shown to {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}}, she will be startled by its appearance, saying that it looks like a monster. She then rewards {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is complete.<ref>{{Cite|Wha-? This is the {{Color|BotW Red|bony horse}}? That {{Color|BotW Blue|Stalhorse}}... It's a monster!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref>

If [[Link]] speaks to [[Baddek]] while he's on his way to [[Serenne Stable]] from the [[Breach of Demise]], he mentions that he once rode a boney horse he encountered in the [[Tabantha Tundra]] and notes it was a surprisingly comfortable mount.<ref>{{Cite|I love every little thing about horses. I love their fur, the way they smell, their bones even. Speaking of bones, I once rode a horse made of nothing but bones! No lie! It was way up in the snowy mountains. Was I scared, you ask? Nope. I could never be scared of a horse. That bony horse ran really well, and it was actually a surprisingly comfortable ride.|Baddek|BotW}}</ref> Like [[Konba]], Baddek considers the Stalhorse to be a type of Horse.
