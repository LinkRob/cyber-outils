The '''Subspace Army''' is an evil organization made of formless beings in [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]] of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. They are the main antagonists, supposedly led by the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Ancient Minister|Ancient Minister]]. It is later revealed that he was forced to lead the Subspace Army by [[Ganondorf]] and fellow ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' antagonists [[Bowser]] and [[Wario]], all of whom did so under the orders of [[w:c:supersmashbros:Master Hand|Master Hand]], ultimately revealed to be under the control of the Subspace Army's true leader, [[w:c:supersmashbros:Tabuu|Tabuu]].

The Subspace Army's main troops are a group of small shadow beings, known as [[w:c:supersmashbros:Primid|Primids]], which are formed out of [[w:c:supersmashbros:Shadow Bug|Shadow Bug]]s, like many other enemies. Their goal was to pull the entire [[w:c:supersmashbros:World of Trophies|World of Trophies]] into [[w:c:supersmashbros:Subspace|Subspace]] through the use of a [[w:c:supersmashbros:Subspace Bomb|Subspace Bomb]].

Ganondorf is the only villain of {{TLoZ|Series}} who is part of the Subspace Army. He does not command any of his troops, unlike Bowser, whose [[Goomba]], [[w:c:mario:Koopa Troopa|Koopa Troopa]], [[w:c:mario:Koopa Paratroopa|Koopa Paratroopa]], [[w:c:mario:Hammer Bro|Hammer Bro]], and [[w:c:mario:Bullet Bill|Bullet Bill]] minions are all encountered as enemies at some point. The first boss, [[Petey Piranha]], is also part of the Subspace Army, and he has trapped both [[Princess Peach]] and [[Princess Zelda]] during the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Midair Stadium|Midair Stadium]] at the start.

The Subspace Army commands a large assortment of weapons and doomsday devices. The only one that Ganondorf is seen using is the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Subspace Gunship|Subspace Gunship]], a flying fortress that he and Bowser use to create voids to Subspace without using a Subspace Bomb. Bowser and Wario have been shown to use more weapons, such as the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Dark Cannon|Dark Cannon]], which converts [[fighter]]s into [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|trophy]] form, and the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Cargo|Cargo]] as a form of transport.
