{{Term/Store|Shurmak||Series, ZA}} is a character in {{ZA}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am Shurmak your guide.|Shurmak|ZA}}</ref> She is [[Princess Zelda]]'s guide through [[Tolemac]], and gives her advice on navigating the land and its shrines.

==Biography==
Shurmak is a woman who lives in Tolemac. She can be found at the [[Great Dry Canyon]] in central Tolemac, where she sits on a red couch. Many years ago, she had sent [[Gaspra]] a scroll warning him that [[Ganon]] had captured [[Link]] and stolen the seven [[Celestial Sign]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, the scroll of Shurmak, bearer of sad news these many years ago. And so it was that Gannon, Lord of Darkness, had taken over Tolemac. He has stolen the treasured celestial signs and captured Link! A brave warrior will have to be found to face this evil monster.|Gaspra|ZA}}</ref> Upon Zelda's arrival to Tolemac to find the Celestial Signs and rescue Link, Shurmak serves as her guide throughout the land, whom she has known since Zelda was a child.<ref>{{Cite| I have known you since you were a child.|Shurmak|ZA}}</ref> Shurmak communicates with Zelda telepathically from afar, and offers her sage advice and riddles on exploring the land and its shrines. At the beginning, she urges that Zelda find her Wand near the [[Vision Henge]] before venturing any further.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda, do not be hasty! There is something you must have nearby here. Exploration is the key to your success. [...] Zelda, Zelda! Do not roam unprotected. Look nearby for something to aide you.|Shurmak|ZA}}</ref> She also gives Zelda a riddle on where to find the first shrine, the [[Shrine of Earth]].<ref>{{Cite|Go now to the one who has a bed but never sleeps, where by its side the red willow weeps, and to its mouth that never speaks.|Shurmak|ZA}}</ref>

Shurmak had also employed several other residents of Tolemac to assist Zelda, including [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Sir Basil|Sir Basil]] and [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Madame Kriggle|Madame Kriggle]],<ref>{{Cite|Hail Princess Zelda, friend of Shurmak! I am Sir Basil of Tolemac, and Shurmak has sent me to assist you.|Sir Basil|ZA}}</ref> whom she is an old friend of.<ref>{{Cite|My old friend Shurmak has told me of your coming. I have awaited you many moons and suns.|Madame Kriggle|ZA}}</ref> Shurmak had also once helped [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Yvonne|Yvonne]], so she in turn helps Zelda.<ref>{{Cite|I believe Shurmak has graced you with her friendship. She helped me once, so I am going to help you.|Yvonne|ZA}}</ref>

According to a [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Link's Guide|woman]] in the [[Vendoss]] swamp, there is some envy between Shurmak and Gaspra.<ref>{{Cite|You must heed both Shurmak and Gaspra. There is some envy between them, but it is a small thing and they both fight for the right in different ways.|Link's Guide|ZA}}</ref>
