{{Term|TWWHD|Five-Eye Reef}} is a minor island located north of {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}}, at F2. It has five round reefs in the center, giving it its name. After the {{Term|TWWHD|Helmaroc King|link}} is defeated in {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Warship|link}} and wall-mounted {{Plural|TWWHD|Cannon (Enemy)|link}} will appear at the island. When {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} destroys all of them, a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest|link}} appears containing a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chart|link}}.

A {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest|link}} containing 200 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}} can also be found by using one of the {{Plural|TWWHD|Treasure Chart}} found in the {{Term|TWWHD|Earth Temple|link}}.
