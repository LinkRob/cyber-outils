{{Term/Store|Red Stalfos|Red Stalfos|Series, TAoL, ALttP, LA, LADX, LANS, OoS, OoA, TMC, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 207 {{Exp Game|TAoL, ALttP, TMC}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|LADX, OoS, OoA, ALBW, TFH, AST}} {{Plural|Series|Red Stalfos}} go unnamed in {{LA|NS}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
Red and [[Blue Stalfos|Blue]] appear, with the blue variant being the stronger one.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
Red Stalfos are common enemies, first appearing in the [[Tower of Hera]] and appearing in many [[Dark World]] dungeons afterwards. They jump back when [[Link]] swipes his sword at him, and throw a bone at him.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Stub|LA}}

==={{OoS|-}}===
Red Stalfos will jump to avoid [[Link]], but do not throw bones.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Stub|OoA}}

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Stalfos}}
{{Plural|TMC|Red Stalfos}} appear in {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}} in {{TMC}}, specifically the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}. Unlike their {{Term|TMC|Blue Stalfos|link|display=Blue}} counterparts, they fling bone projectiles at {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} and jump less frequently.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Plural|ALBW|Red Stalfos}} appear in the {{Term|ALBW|Tower of Hera|link}} in {{ALBW}}. {{Plural|ALBW|Red Stalfos}} will attack {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} by running up and hitting him, or throwing bones as a ranged attack. Their ability to throw bones makes them different and more dangerous than their weaker variant, {{Plural|ALBW|Stalfos|link}}. In the {{Term|ALBW|Tower of Hera}}, they appear as a {{Term|ALBW|Sub-Boss|link}} that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must fight to progress through the {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon|link}}. When {{Term|ALBW|Link}} enters the room on the sixth floor, the door which he entered from will shut and lock. Five {{Plural|ALBW|Red Stalfos}} will stand from the piles of bones that they previously were, and attack {{Term|ALBW|Link}}. Once {{Term|ALBW|Link}} defeats all the {{Plural|ALBW|Red Stalfos}}, the doors will unlock and a {{Term|ALBW|Portal (Object)|link}} will open on the ground, which will transport him to and from the entrance of {{Term|ALBW|Tower of Hera}}.
