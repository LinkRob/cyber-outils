The {{Term/Store|Gold Joy Ring||Series, OoA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Gold Joy Ring<br/>Find double items|Inventory|OoA}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Gold Joy Ring can be found behind a bombable wall in a cave at [[Rolling Ridge]] during the present. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee|Rupees]]. When worn, it doubles the value of picked up [[Rupee|Rupees]], [[Heart|Hearts]], [[Ore|Ores]], [[Bomb|Bombs]], and [[Mystical Seed|Mystical Seeds]]. The Gold Joy Ring can be transferred to {{OoS}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
