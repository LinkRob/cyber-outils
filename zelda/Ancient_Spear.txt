{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that can be made at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} using the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Exchange for Ancient Spear?|Ancient Oven|BotW}}</ref> To make one, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will need to provide 15 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Gear|link}}, five {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shaft|link}}, and two {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Core|link}}. They are the result of countless hours of research into {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} done by {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> This is common among many pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}, the equipment archetype to which {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Spear}} belong.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref> The spearhead glows and has incredible piercing power.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Spear}} will deal additional damage when they are used against {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}. They will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, will float on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and cannot burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
