{{Term/Store|Spike Roller||Series, LANS}} is the {{Term|LANS|Sub-Boss}} of {{Term|LANS|Tail Cave|link}} in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= SPIKE ROLLER |author= Nintendo |published= n.d. |retrieved= September 17, 2019 |url= https://www.zelda.com/links-awakening/characters/ |title= Characters – The Legend of Zelda ™: Link’s Awakening game for the Nintendo Switch™ system – Tarin, Marin, and more! |site= Zelda.com |type= }}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Spike Roller}} was referred to as {{Term/Store|Rolling Bones||LA, LADX}} in all versions of {{LA}} prior to {{LA|NS}}. However, as this contradicts the name given in {{LA|NS}}, it is no longer considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
To defeat {{Term|LANS|Rolling Bones}}, {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} can use the {{Term|LANS|Roc's Feather|link}} to jump over the {{Term|LANS|Spiketrap|link}} that he pushes in his direction. Then he must attack him with the {{Term|LANS|Sword}} as many times as possible before he can get back behind the {{Term|LANS|Spiketrap}}. Defeating {{Term|LANS|Rolling Bones}} will open the way to the {{Term|LANS|Boss}}, {{Term|LANS|Moldorm (Boss)|link}}, and open a warp back to the entrance of the {{Term|LANS|Dungeon}}.

{{Term|LANS|Rolling Bones}} also appears as one of many previous {{Plural|LANS|Sub-Boss}} in {{Term|LANS|Turtle Rock|link}}, and can be defeated instantly if hit by the Boomerang. If defeated in {{Term|LANS|Turtle Rock}}, he will return like a regular enemy.
