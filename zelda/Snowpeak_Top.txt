{{Term/Store|Snowpeak Top||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Snowpeak Top|Map|TPHD}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
As its name implies, {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Top}} is the highest point of {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak|link}} that {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can reach in the {{Term|TPHD|Peak Province|link}}. It can be accessed by going through a small cavern in the upper section of the mountain, past the icy lake.

Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} reaches {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Top}} after having followed the {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish Scent|link}}, a {{Term|TPHD|Portal (Object)|link}} will open up and {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast|link}}, along with {{Term|TPHD|Ice Keese|link}}, will promptly attack him. Defeating these creatures will allow {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to see a lone tree atop the hill, along with {{Term|TPHD|Yeto|link|display= a beast}} holding a large {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish|link}}. When {{Term|TPHD|Link}} speaks to him, the beast will introduce himself as {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}}. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} tells him of his quest to find a {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Shard|link}} from the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror of Twilight|link}}, to which {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} mentions that he had found a {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Shard}} earlier on. {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} invites {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to his {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins|link|display= mansion}} to see if the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Shard}} is the one he is looking for.<ref>{{Cite|...Uh. You look for mirror in such faraway place... But you make good climb! And you lucky to meet me! I found shiny mirror piece. Same mirror you look for, uh? Uh! You come to house and see yourself!|Yeto|TP}}</ref> However, since his home is located at the bottom of the mountain, {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} tells {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to follow him by using a frozen leaf from the nearby tree to participate in a {{Term|TPHD|Snowboard Race|link}} down the mountain.<ref>{{Cite|My house far away... We slide there, uh? Do like me! Come!|Yeto|TP}}</ref>

Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} defeats {{Term|TPHD|Blizzeta|link}} and retrieves the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Shard}} from the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins}}, {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} and {{Term|TPHD|Yeta|link}} will be on {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Top}} to race {{Term|TPHD|Link}}, rewarding him with a {{Term|TPHD|Piece of Heart|link}} if he bests both {{Plural|TPHD|Yeti|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You take this as token of respect for sled skills, uh! ...And no tell husband! You come back and race anytime, uh!|Yeta|TP}}</ref>
