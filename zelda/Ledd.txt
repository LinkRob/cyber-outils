{{Term/Store|Ledd||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 295}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Ledd}}. He is a member of a highly civilized mole race called the Mogma. Their primary habitat is underground. All Mogmas have a deep and abiding love of treasure.
}}

Ledd is a [[Mogma]] treasure hunter. He accompanies [[Cobal]] on their treasure hunts, but appears to be somewhat abusive toward him, referring to him as "{{Wp|Igneous rock|igneous}}" as an insult.<ref>{{Cite|Whaddya think, igneous? We sneak inside and take all the treasure we can carry!|Ledd|SS}}</ref> According to Cobal, Ledd is afraid to dig in the dark.<ref name="Dark"/>

[[Link]] encounters Ledd on [[Eldin Volcano]] while waiting to ambush the [[Bokoblin]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry 'bout that, pal. These monsters showing up and messing with our turf has got me on edge.|Ledd|SS}}</ref> He tells the young hero that he saw a [[Impa|person dressed in black]] running towards the Volcano, mistaking it for his [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|friend]].<ref>{{Cite|So that must have been your pal that passed by earlier, {{Color|SS Red|sprinting by}} without so much as a glance in this direction. It was sorta a blur of movement, so I didn't get a real good look, but I knew it wasn't one of those red creeps.|Ledd|SS}}</ref> Link encounters him again in the [[Earth Temple (Skyward Sword)|Earth Temple]] after Ledd had lost his [[Bomb Bag]] while running away from two [[Lizalfos]].<ref>{{Cite|I was behind that rock looking for treasure when those creeps showed up. I panicked and hit the road, but it looks like I dropped it.|Ledd|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's a {{Color|SS Red|bag for carrying around bomb flowers}}. I can't imagine not having one. Oh, hey...I guess I don't have to imagine it, 'cause I lost mine! Anyway, it's back there... Do me a favor and get my {{Color|SS Gold|Bomb Bag}} back, will you?|Ledd|SS}}</ref> He asks Link to retrieve it for him, and decides to give it to him once Link has defeated the Lizaflos, after Link asks him to borrow it in order to help him find Zelda.<ref>{{Cite|What's this, now? You want to borrow it so you can go deeper into the volcano? Oh, right. You're looking for your friend. You sure have put yourself out there, getting this far...|Ledd|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm not letting you borrow my bag, though. Nope. I'm GIVING it to you! Just don't blow yourself up...|Ledd|SS}}</ref>

Later, Ledd appears near the entrance to the [[Fire Sanctuary (Skyward Sword)|Fire Sanctuary]]. He suggests that Link travel to [[Lake Floria]] to retrieve enough water to douse a large wall of flames blocking the entrance to the dungeon.<ref>{{Cite|What? There's some fire blocking your path? Why don'tcha just pour some water on it and move on?|Ledd|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you need lots of the wet stuff, you should head over to {{Color|SS Red|Lake Floria}}. I think you'll find what you need.|Ledd|SS}}</ref>
