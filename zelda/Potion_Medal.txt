The {{Term/Store|Potion Medal||Series, SS}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 134}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Potion Medal can be obtained from a [[Goddess's Treasure Chest|Goddess's Chest]] found within the [[sky]]. While [[Link]] is carrying it in his [[Pouch|Adventure pouch]], [[Potion|Potions]] he drinks will have their effect time extended. This has no use with instant use potions, such as [[Heart Potion|Heart]] or [[Revitalizing Potion|Revitalizing Potions]]. Like other pouch items, it has no effect during the [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|Lightning Rounds]].
