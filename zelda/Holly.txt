{{Term/Store|Holly||Series}} is a character in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |publisher= Nintendo|page= 127}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|OoS|Holly}} resides in her {{Term|OoS|Holly's House|link|display=House}} in the {{Term|OoS|Woods of Winter|link}}. To access the {{Term|OoS|Snake's Remains|link}}, {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} must acquire a {{Term|OoS|Shovel|link}} from her.

The door to {{Term|OoS|Holly's House}} is blocked by snow. To enter the {{Term|OoS|Holly's House|display=House}}, {{Term|OoS|Link}} must climb a platform and enter via the chimney. Upon entering, the startled {{Term|OoS|Holly}} first confuses {{Term|OoS|Link}} for {{Term|OoS|Santa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|AHH!!! You startled me! Winter sets in all of a sudden, then down my chimney comes... You're not Santa!|Holly|OoS}}</ref> She then demands that he clear the snow from her walkway, allowing him to keep the {{Term|OoS|Shovel}} when he is finished.<ref>{{Cite|Now take this shovel and clear the snow from my walkway! Keep the  shovel when you're done.|Holly|OoS}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* It is only ever possible to enter {{Term|OoS|Holly's House}} during the {{Term|OoS|Rod of Seasons|link|display=Winter}}. 
* In the British English version of the game, {{Term|OoS|Holly}} mistakes {{Term|OoS|Link}} for a chimney sweep instead of {{Term|OoS|Santa}}.<ref>{{Cite|AHH!!! You startled me! Winter sets in all of a sudden, then down my chimney comes... You're no chimney sweep!|Holly|OoS|British English version}}</ref>
