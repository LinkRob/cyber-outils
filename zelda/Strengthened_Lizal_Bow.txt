{{Plural|BotW|Strengthened Lizal Bow}} are upgraded {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Bow|link}} with a grip reinforced by metal.<ref name="Inventory"/> Their wooden body is made from flexible {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} that grow near {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, increasing their destructive power.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are commonly used by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Lizalfos|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} and in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, but they are also found in the hands of {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}}.

While they do have metallic components, {{Plural|BotW|Strengthened Lizal Bow}} are considered wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} in the game. Therefore, they will burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They also float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. Finally, they cannot be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
