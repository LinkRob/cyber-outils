==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|High Ground}} is a cliff located behind the {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. It is the site of the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|display=Village}}'s {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace|link}}. It also houses many of the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|display=Village}}'s crop fields, as well as the {{Plural|BotW|Scarecrow|link}} that guard them. During the day, {{Term|BotW|Reede|link}} tends to one of these fields. After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has spoken with {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} for the first time, the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} will start to appear on the {{Term|BotW|High Ground}} during the night.<ref>{{Cite|Fang and Bone (only open at night)|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|Locked Mementos|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} will send {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the {{Term|BotW|High Ground}} to retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let me explain what this errand entails. You know the unlit {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}} on the wall just outside this laboratory? Could you please bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} in town? Oh, and also use it to light our {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}}?|Purah|BotW}}</ref> After igniting a flammable {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can light the {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|link}} found around the {{Term|BotW|High Ground}}.
