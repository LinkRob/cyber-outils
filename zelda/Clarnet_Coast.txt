{{Term/Store|Clarnet Coast||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Clarnet Coast'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Clarnet Coast is located along the [[Necluda Sea]] in [[East Necluda]]. Clarnet Coast is a narrow beach stretching across the Necluda Sea. It features many [[Palm Tree]]s as well as a {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}}. At this {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}, Link can find an unlit [[Campfire]] as well as [[Barrel]]s and [[Wooden Box]]es that contain [[Apple]]s, [[Hearty Durian]]s, [[Hydromelon]]s, [[Mighty Bananas]], [[Palm Fruit]]s, and [[Voltfruit]]s. A dock sits at the far western side of Clarnet Coast. Around this dock, Link can find a lit [[Campfire]] as well as Barrels, {{Plural|BotW|Vase|link}}, and Wooden Boxes containing [[Arrow]]s, Apples, Hydromelons, Palm Fruits, and Voltfruits.

A metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Silver Rupee]] can be found inside the aforementioned {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}. A second metallic Treasure Chest containing an [[Amber]] sits in the water beside the western dock.
