{{Term/Store|Traveler's Shield|Traveler's Shields|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Traveler's Shield}}<br/>A sturdy shield loved by many an adventurer. It is made of animal hide and sturdy wood and is best suited to defending against weak monsters or animals.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Traveler's Shield}} go unnamed in {{HWAoC}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Traveler's Shield}}
{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Shield}} are simple, mid-grade {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}}. They are beloved by many travelers and are used for protection against weak monsters and {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}}.<ref name="Inventory" /> The {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Shield}} is also a part of an unnamed set of equipment used by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} in promotional media, alongside a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Bow|link}}.

There are many places to find a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Shield}}, most notably at enemy camps on the beaches surrounding {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. They are also given out by {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} as a reward for completing the {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} with a time between 2:30 and 2:45.<ref>{{Cite|A shiny new record deserves a shiny new shield. Here y'go!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

The following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Shield}} when they are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} ({{TLoZ}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Toon Zelda|link}} ({{TWW}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} ({{TP}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} ({{SS}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Being made from {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Shield}} will start to burn if exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. Since they are not made of metal, they do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and can not be manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. When dropped into {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Shield}} will float.

{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Shield}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear|link}} archetype of equipment, which are all said to be used to defend travelers throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref> A {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Shield}} is part of an unnamed set of equipment used by {{Term|BotW|Link}} in promotional media, alongside a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Bow|link}}.
