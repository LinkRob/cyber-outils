{{Term/Store|Tile|Tiles|Series, TMC|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC |publisher= Nintendo |page= 84}}</ref>

==Overview and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Tile}} appear in two different rooms within {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}. Both rooms require {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to maneuver across a cluster of {{Plural|TMC|Tile}} without stepping on the same {{Term|TMC|Tile}} twice. Whenever {{Term|TMC|Link}} steps on a {{Term|TMC|Tile}}, it turns from blue to red. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} steps on a red {{Term|TMC|Tile}}, no more blue {{Plural|TMC|Tile}} will be able to turn red, and {{Term|TMC|Link}} must use a nearby {{Term|TMC|Floor Switch|link}} to reset the puzzle. After all the blue {{Plural|TMC|Tile}} are stepped on and turn red, the door to the next room opens.
