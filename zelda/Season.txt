{{Term/Store|Season|Seasons|Series, OoS|plural}} are a recurring element in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Want to learn about {{Color|OoS Red|seasons}}?|Know-It-All Bird|OoS}}</ref> Though most games seem to feature either spring or summer, Seasons play a major part in {{OoS}} and a significantly more minor role in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:Four Seasons (Full).png|thumb|right|200px|<center>All four Seasons in {{OoS|-}}</center>]]
All four Seasons play a critical role for [[Link]] in {{OoS}}. As Link arrives in [[Holodrum]], [[Din (Oracle)|Din]], the Oracle of Seasons, becomes kidnapped by [[General Onox]]. This throws the Seasons into chaos,<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=When Onox carried off the Oracle of Seasons from the land of Holodrum, the seasons fell into chaos and the land was deprived of the bountiful gifts of nature.|game= OoS|page=5}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|As you know, if I imprison the Oracle of Seasons and bury the temple that houses the Seasons Spirits, the seasons of Holodrum will be cast into chaos, the bountiful gifts of nature will rot, and all living things perish!!! That is the world of darkness that I long for!|Onox|OoS}}</ref> and casts the [[Temple of Seasons]] down into [[Subrosia]].<ref>{{Cite|Down, Temple of Seasons!!! Spring, summer, fall, winter... Fall into chaos!|Onox|OoS}}</ref> In Subrosia, Link is guided to the [[Rod of Seasons]] by the [[Season Spirits]].<ref>{{Cite|Get the Rod of Seasons and visit us who wait in each of the four corner towers!|Spirit of Winter|OoS}}</ref> The Rod of Seasons can be used to control the Seasons after being empowered by each of the four Season Spirits in the four towers positioned at each corner of the Temple of Seasons. The first Season that Link obtains is Winter, granted by the Spirit of Winter.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for coming, child of the crested hand. The Spirit of Winter has been waiting for you. Din is our dear friend... Now, let me bestow the power of the Spirit of Winter on the rod you hold! When you swing the rod from atop a stump, the seasons shall change.|Spirit of Winter|OoS}}</ref>

In {{Term|OoS|Winter|link}}, some trees lose their leaves and become thinner, allowing Link to pass under them.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=Winter brings heavy snows and cold temperatures that can freeze lakes and rivers. In winter, some trees will be completely free of leaves.|game= OoS|page=32}}</ref> Some ponds, lakes, and rivers freeze over, allowing Link to walk over them and [[Snow]]drifts may pile up in some places. They can act as a bridge between two places, or as a barrier, preventing [[Link]] from continuing.

In {{Term|OoS|Summer|link}}, some ponds, lakes, and rivers dry up, allowing Link to explore the riverbed.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=In summer, some lakes dry up under the hot sun. Many plats like vines grow particularly well in this season.|game= OoS|page=32}}</ref> Additionally, vines grow in vertical crevices in walls, allowing Link to climb them to access higher places.

In {{Term|OoS|Spring|link}}, some ponds, lakes, and rivers flood from melting ice, raising the water level and covering brambles and other barriers, allowing some waterways to be navigable without the [[Flippers]].<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=Spring is the season when snow and ice thaw and many flowers start to blossom. The buds of some flowers are hard as rocks until they blossom in spring.|game= OoS|page=32}}</ref> Flowers that were rock hard during the other seasons bloom, and regular flowers can now be chopped down. Also in some places, [[Blast Bloom]]s flourish, which can be used to rocket Link into the air, granting him access to high ledges.

In {{Term|OoS|Autumn|link}}, [[Rock Mushroom]]s that were rock-hard during the other Seasons are now ripe for picking when equipped with the [[Power Bracelet]].<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=Autumn leaves fall to the ground and cover holes. In this season, you can even pick mushrooms that are rock-hard the rest of they year.|game= OoS|page=32}}</ref> Leaves fall and cover holes in the ground, which Link can walk over. However, if he stands on a hole for too long, he will fall through.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, seasonal changes only affect [[Hena]]'s [[Fishing Hole]]. Each time Link enters the Fishing Hole, the Seasons will advance, starting from Spring. The Seasons affect which types of [[Fish]] are available, as the [[Hylian Loach]] can only be found during the summertime.
