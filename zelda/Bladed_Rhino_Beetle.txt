==Locations and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Bladed Rhino Beetle}}
{{Plural|BotW|Bladed Rhino Beetle}} can be found on trees in the region of {{Term|BotW|Necluda|link}}. They can be combined with {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make a {{Term|BotW|Mighty Elixir|link}}, which increases attack damage when consumed.<ref name="Inventory"/> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Bladed Rhino Beetle}} in his inventory, {{Term|BotW|Beedle}} will offer to trade it in exchange for a random {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Elixir|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Beedle loves {{Color|BotW Blue|bladed rhino beetles}}!
