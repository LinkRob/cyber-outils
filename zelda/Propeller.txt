The {{Term/Store|Propeller||Series, SS}},<ref>{{Cite|Propeller|N/A|SS}}</ref> also known as the '''Windmill Propeller''',<ref>{{Cite|We now have the means to bring the propeller back to Skyloft. You may immediately begin your search for the {{Color|SS Blue|Windmill Propeller}}.|Fi|SS}}</ref> is a quest item in {{SS}}. It is necessary to operate one of the [[Skyloft]] windmills and thus eventually lead to opening the path to the [[Thunderhead]].<ref>{{Cite|I highly suggest you retrieve the {{Color|SS Red|windmill propeller}} from the land below to reorient the windmill.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The two windmills in [[Skyloft]] can be turned using the propellers attached to the back of them so that they can catch the wind no matter the direction it is blowing.<ref>{{Cite|Pretty smart design, I gotta say. See, you can turn the windmill so that it can always catch the wind no matter which way it's blowing.|Jakamar|SS}}</ref> However, the eastern windmill is missing the propeller, having dropped down beneath the clouds ages ago.<ref>{{Cite|But... Well, there used to be this little {{Color|SS Red|propeller}} attached to the windmill so that you could turn it. The thing flew off ages ago. It must have dropped down off the edge, down beneath the clouds.|Jakamar|SS}}</ref> By using the [[Dowsing]] ability, Link can find the propeller near the [[Earth Temple (Skyward Sword)|Earth Temple]] entrance and call [[Scrapper]]. Once the propeller is brought to Skyloft, [[Jakamar]] will reattach it to the windmill and make it operational again.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Somehow I get the feeling this is all some elaborate prank, but I guess I'll try to fix this thing... This goes in here... And I just crank that a few clicks... Phew! That ought to do it! If you can find some way to spin the propeller, you can probably turn the windmill around.|Jakamar|SS}}</ref>
