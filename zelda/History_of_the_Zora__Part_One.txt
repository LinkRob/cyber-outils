The {{Term/Store|''History of the Zora, Part One''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|History of the Zora, Part One<br/>The Eternal Zora's Domain<br/>As told by King Dorephan|History of the Zora, Part One|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|History of the Zora, Part One}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|link}} that can be found west of {{Term|BotW|Luto's Crossing|link}}, along the path leading towards {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is required to read it as part of the "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument}} recounts the creation of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} 10,000 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|Seeking a bounty of such water, the Zora gathered there. Thus, as the legends go, the domain was born 10,000 years ago.|History of the Zora, Part One|BotW}}</ref> As one giant sculpture, the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}} has drawn admirers from all over the world and become a symbol of {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} pride.<ref>{{Cite|The domain is one giant sculpture, a feat of architecture that has drawn admirers the world over. Our great domain will ever stand as a hallmark of the esteemed artists who made it, an eternal symbol of Zora pride.|History of the Zora, Part One|BotW}}</ref>
