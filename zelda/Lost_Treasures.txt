{{Term/Store|Lost Treasures||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 46 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lost Treasures|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Lost Treasures}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given six minutes to find a {{Term|HWAoC|Treasure Chest|link}}. As the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} moves south to look for it, a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} spawns, alongside a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the center of the map, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}} spawns with another group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}}, along with some {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Keese}} in other places around the center. A few {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok|link}} disguised as {{Plural|HWAoC|Treasure Chest}} are scattered around the map as well. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the eastern side of the map, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} spawns, surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Lizalfos|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Treasure Chest}}'s location is randomized, and when approached by the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel|link}} spawns next to it and the time limit is taken away. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel}} is defeated. {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}.
