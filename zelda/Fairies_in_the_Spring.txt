'''"Fairies in the Spring"''' was the eleventh episode in {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. It first aired in the United States and Canada on November 17, 1989.

==Plot==
:''See also: [[Fairies in the Spring/Episode transcript|Episode transcript]]''
[[File:Water Monsters (Fairies in the Spring).png|thumb|left|Two crab-like water monsters attack Link and Zelda]]
It is summertime in [[Hyrule]] and [[King Harkinian]] wants to build a [[Royal Water Park]] for all the people in his kingdom to enjoy.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Most certainly. Still, my Royal Water Park should be completed soon, then all the kingdom will have some place to cool off and have fun.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> However, as the construction is nearing completion, [[Water Monster|strange water creatures]] begin to attack the workers.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Yeah, the king's water park is almost done! We were sure lucky to  find that underground spring.|name=Workers' Boss|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> The workers' boss reports the incident to the king and Link and Zelda promptly go to the water park to investigate.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Never saw anything like it, your majesty. Scared off all my crew  and took the whole place!|name=Workers' Boss|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> When they arrive, the duo are attacked by a water monster and later Zelda is almost drowned by an [[eel]], but both are easily defeated. When they resurface, two crab-like monsters grab them and once the heroes zap them both,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I can't...reach mine either! Oh but, we can zap each other's!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> they see that the King has arrived.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Well, well. Enjoying the park, eh? Good, good. That's  what it's for.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> Just as Zelda warns him not to stand near the water,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Daddy, please! Don't stand so close to the water.|name=Princess Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> a tentacle comes out of the water and grabs him, which takes him deep underwater. Link dives in to the pool but is unable to find neither the king nor the monster.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Sorry, Zelda. He's...gone, too.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref>

Link and Zelda return to [[North Castle]] and ask the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] for advice,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Please, Triforce, my father has been captured, but we don't know  who did it or where he is!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> who tells them to keep looking underwater.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=To find your father, royal daugther, you must  search beneath the water.|name=Triforce of Wisdom|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> Zelda takes the Triforce of Wisdom with her and,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I have a feeling we may need the Triforce's magic for this  adventure, Link. Let's go!|name=Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> when they arrive back at the park, she uses the Triforce of Wisdom's powers to allow them to breathe underwater.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=We may have to stay underwater for a while. These magic helmets will allow us to breath.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> Link and Zelda dive in, and when they find a grate that they can go through, a [[Giant Fish|large fish]] suddenly attacks them and grabs the Triforce, which Zelda holds on to. Eventually, Link catches up to them and zaps the creature with the help of Zelda, and when they finally resurface, they find themselves in a familiar place but cannot figure out why they recognize it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=This place...seems familiar.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> Just then, several beams come out of nowhere and land into the water, which makes three water monsters appear. Link easily [[Sword Beam|zaps]] all of them but just as he defeats them, they keep respawning.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Where are all these monsters coming from?|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> As Link is fighting the creatures, Zelda notices that the beams creating the monsters are coming from a tree and, upon further investigation, discovers that the monsters were being created by [[Spryte's Sister|Spryte's sister]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=That's right! Spryte's my sister. And you must be Zelda. And  Link.|name=Spryte's Sister|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> Zelda then realizes that they are at the [[Fairy Spring]] that [[Spryte]] told her about,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=A fairy! Why, this must be the Fairy Spring that Spryte told me  about.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> and Spryte's sister explains that someone was draining their spring, which was the reason they were creating the water creatures.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=We didn't know who you were. All we knew was somebody  was draining our spring.|name=Spryte's Sister|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> King Harkinian, who was talking with the [[King Oberon|Fairy King]] all along, then comes out and informs to Zelda that their water park was inadvertantly taking water from the fairies' water supply, but he has already explained the situation to the Fairy King and all is forgiven.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=That's right, my dear. I'm afraid our water park was hooked into the fairy water supply. And the fairy king here got  worried. Perfectly natural for them to defend it, eh? [...] Not a bit, my dear! We'll just use magic ponds to move  our water. Once all the pools are full, we won't need to use any more!  I was just explaining that to Oberon here. He's not worried anymore.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref>

The construction on the water park is finally completed, and both the humans and the fairies enjoy it together. Link once again tries to get Zelda to kiss him, but when she complies, Spryte interrupts them with her return from her vacation.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Spryte, my daughter! You're back from your trip!|name=King Oberon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref>

==Trivia==
This is the only episode in the series where [[Ganon]] and his minions are not shown at all. 

This is the last appearance of [[King Harkinian#The Legend of Zelda TV Series|King Harkinian]] in the show.
