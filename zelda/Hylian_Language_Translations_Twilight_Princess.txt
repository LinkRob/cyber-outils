TITLE : Hylian Language Translations/Twilight Princess

<noinclude>{{TLoZ|colon}} {{TP}} introduces a new form of [[Hylian (Language)|Hylian text]] that is a cipher based on the Latin alphabet. It translates directly to English in most cases, and occasionally to Japanese. The previous form of Hylian text, introduced in {{TWW}}, also makes an appearance in the {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village Graveyard|link}}.

Images from {{TPHD}} are shown where available. For Hylian text added or altered in that version, see [[Hylian Language Translations/Twilight Princess HD]].</noinclude>
