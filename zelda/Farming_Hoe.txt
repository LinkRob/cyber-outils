{{Plural|BotW|Farming Hoe}} are a tool used by farmers to till their fields, and as a result are found near {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} settlements and ruins in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Regular {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} may also wield them as {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}. They can be used as two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, though their application in battle is untested.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Farming Hoe}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Daily Use Goods|link}} equipment archetype, which is a series of tools that double as {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>

Due to their metallic components, {{Plural|BotW|Farming Hoe}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. This also makes them magnetic, which means they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. They sink if they are dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
