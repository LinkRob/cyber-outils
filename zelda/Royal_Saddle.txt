The {{Term/Store|Royal Saddle||Series, BotW}} is an item in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Saddle}}<br/>The shimmering emblem design of this saddle signifies its origins with the Hyrulean royal family. They say Princess Zelda herself once used this saddle for her own beloved horse.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Royal Saddle is a [[Saddle]] that can be obtained from [[Toffa]] by completing the [[Side Quest]] "[[The Royal White Stallion]]". Prior to the second [[Great Calamity]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] equipped her {{Term|BotW|White Horse|link}} with the custom-made Royal Saddle and its counterpart, the [[Royal Bridle]], as part of an attempt to bond with her [[Horse]].<ref>{{Cite|At first, I wasn't sure if I should outfit him with all of the royal gear. I thought maybe he should have to earn it first. But it works! He wears it like a true natural.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm trying to be a bit more empathetic. Benefit of the doubt, you know?|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Following the Great Calamity, Toffa's grandfather inherited the Royal Saddle and passed it down to Toffa.<ref>{{Cite|What? You brought the royal family's {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}? Well I'll be... Grandpa wasn't lying! Son, you just made my life. Staying alive in this godforsaken land may just have been worth it after all. I've got a surprise for you. My grandpa handed this down to me, and now I'm handing it down to you. I present to you...the royal family's saddle and bridle! Custom fitted for the white royal horse!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> By registering the White Horse at a Stable and showing it to Toffa, the elderly man will gift Link the Royal Saddle and equip it onto the Horse, even if the [[Bond]] between Link and the White Horse is weak.

The Saddle can be equipped to any other Horse, except for [[Epona]] and the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}, after Link maxes out the Bond with his Horse and speaking to [[Canni]] at the [[Outskirt Stable]], {{Term|BotW|Phanna|link}} at the [[Highland Stable]], [[Breen]] at the [[Woodland Stable]], and {{Term|BotW|Tenne|link}} at the [[South Akkala Stable]].<ref>{{Cite|Change to {{Color|BotW Red|Royal Saddle}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref>

The Royal Saddle can be seen equipped to Zelda's White Horse during the special cutscene that plays after Link completes the "[[Captured Memories]]" and "[[Destroy Ganon]]" [[Main Quest]]s.
