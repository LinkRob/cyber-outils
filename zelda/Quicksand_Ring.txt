The {{Term/Store|Quicksand Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Quicksand Ring<br/>No sinking in quicksand|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Quicksand Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. In {{OoS}}, the Ring can also be found inside a cave in [[North Horon]] during [[Autumn]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be won in the [[Big Bang Game]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it prevents [[Link]] from sinking in quicksand.<ref name="Description"/> The Quicksand Ring can be transferred between games talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
