{{Term/Store|Kortz||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 295}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Kortz}}. He is a member of a highly civilized race of moles called the Mogma. All Mogmas have a deep and abiding love of treasure.
}}

Kortz is a brown-haired [[Mogma]]. He teaches Link about [[Bomb Flower]]s and that a [[Bomb Bag]] is needed to carry them.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|SS Gold|Bomb flowers}}. You're joking, right? Those are them growing right over there.|Kortz|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You, uh...know you don't have a {{Color|SS Gold|Bomb Bag}}, don'tcha? Without a {{Color|SS Gold|Bomb Bag}}, you can't safely carry {{Color|SS Gold|bomb flowers}} with you when you're out and about. You won't find a {{Color|SS Blue|Mogma}} without one.|Kortz|SS}}</ref> Kortz is later seen searching for treasure at the [[Skyview Temple]] when Link returns to find [[Sacred Water]].

If Link sits on the stool in one of the [[Bokoblin]] houses in the underground caves of [[Eldin Volcano]], Kortz will humorously welcome Link to his new home while justifying why it is his new house.  However, Kortz will admit that the house is not actually his and that he just wanted to know what it is like to welcome guests to a home.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Welcome to my place! Make yourself at home. What? Of course this is my house! OK, OK, those red creeps built it, but... They split, right? So what's the harm? Us {{Color|SS Blue|Mogmas}} live in the ground so we don't have what you would call "houses". I just wanted to see what it felt like to say "Welcome to my place!"|Kortz|SS}}</ref> If Link sits on the same stool for a second time, Kortz will warn Link to keep the house clean and not to "roll around", alluding to how if Link rolls into some of the house's structures on the walls, a [[Rupee]] may pop out.  These structures can also be found by [[Dowsing]] for Rupees.

==Nomenclature==
Kortz's name is derived from the mineral {{Wp|quartz}}. His Japanese name, {{Romanize|ロズ|Rozu|Rose}}, and Latin American Spanish name, Rosso, which is Italian for red, may refer to his reddish-brown hair. [https://www.mindat.org/show.php?id=3456 Rose quartz] is also a rather common mineral with a pale red to pinkish hue, and his name in both Japanese and English may derive from this source.
