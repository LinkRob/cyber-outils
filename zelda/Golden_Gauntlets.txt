The {{Term/Store|Golden Gauntlets|Golden Gauntlets|Series, OoT, OoT3D|plural}} are a recurring [[item]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref> It is the upgrade of the [[Silver Gauntlets]].

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT Link throwing pillar with Golden Gauntlets.gif|thumb|343x343px|The Golden Gauntlets in action, showing Link lifting and throwing the granite pillar outside Ganon's Castle to access the Great Fairy's Fountain]]
The Golden Gauntlets are located in the Shadow Barrier room of [[Ganon's Castle]]. They replace the Silver Gauntlets when obtained. This handgear is considerably stronger,<ref>{{Cite|You found the Golden Gauntlets! You can feel even more power coursing through your arms! Grab with (A) and lift stuff up!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> as when worn, [[Link]], the [[Hero of Time]], gains incredible superhuman strength rivaling that of [[Ganondorf]] using the [[Triforce of Power]], able to lift certain things that were previously impossible, such as giant, black, extremely heavy [[wikipedia:Granite|granite]] pillars, and throw them great distances. Despite this, however, he still cannot move when carrying gray boulders and does not affect carrying the [[Megaton Hammer]] or [[Biggoron's Sword]] with both hands like with the previous Silver variant. One of these pillars obstructs the door to the Light Barrier room, forcing Link to use them to clear the path. They are also required in the Fire Barrier room to lift and throw another pillar to obtain two [[Rupee|Silver Rupees]].

A third pillar appears outside of Ganon's Castle, in the region formerly housing the [[Great Fairy#Ocarina of Time|Great Fairy of Magic]] on the path to [[Hyrule Castle]]. When removed, the entrance to a [[Great Fairy Fountain|Great Fairy's Fountain]] will be revealed, housing the [[Great Fairy of Courage]]. When summoned, she provides him with {{Term|OoT3D|Defensive Power|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Welcome Link! I am the Great Fairy of Courage! I'm going to enhance your defensive power. Receive it now!|Great Fairy of Courage|OoT}}</ref> halving all damage taken.<ref>{{Cite|Your defensive power has been enhanced! Damage inflicted by enemies will be reduced by half.|Great Fairy of Courage|OoT}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* The Golden Gauntlets are based on the [[Titan's Mitt]] from {{aLttP}}.
* For unknown reasons, in both {{OoT|-}} and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|remake]], during the ending cutscene of when [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] say their goodbyes after defeating [[Ganon]], the Golden Gauntlets were removed from the former's hands.
* The Golden Gauntlets are widely considered to be the most potent strength-enhancement item in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]], and because of this, there have been some fans who wished to see Link punch enemies - especially his archenemy Ganon, with this special equipment.
* Being an igneous rock, granite, the mineral found on the large pillars for Link to lift and throw with the Golden Gauntlets, is one of the densest types of rocks. It can weigh 180 pounds per cubic foot and has a density rating of 2.8 g/cm³. However, it is not one of the heaviest natural rocks in the real-world, as peridotite, an ultramafic, in comparison has a density of 3.4 g/cm³.
