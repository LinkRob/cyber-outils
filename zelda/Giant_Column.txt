The {{Term/Store|Giant Column|Giant Columns|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|series}}.<ref name="CaC 235">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 235}}</ref> The {{Plural|Series|Giant Column}} go unnamed in {{HWAoC}}.

==History==
The five Giant Columns were created by the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} alongside the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} to aid in the battle against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} during the events of the first [[Great Calamity#First Great Calamity|Great Calamity]].{{Fact}} Their purpose was to store {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} and would automatically emerge from below {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} upon {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}'s return to Hyrule.<ref name="CaC 235" />

When {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} returned during the events of the second [[Great Calamity#Second Great Calamity|Great Calamity]], he took over the columns and all {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} within them. They have since remained visible above ground around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} in a pentagon-shaped formation.

==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Being structures built by the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}}, they have a similar appearance to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}}'s other creations, such as {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}}. As such, their surfaces are not naturally scalable by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, though it is possible to climb them using the protruding patterns as makeshift platforms. {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} were stored inside the columns and emerged from the constellation points.<ref>{{Cite|Robbie has restored mobility to many of the Guardians we've excavated. ...But we have still yet to find all of the Guardians. Records mention a greater number of them—and even other types. They are said to be stored in five giant columns that rest beneath Hyrule Castle. The thing is... No matter how I search beneath the castle, I can't seem to locate these columns. They must be buried deep. Were they perhaps designed to sense the appearance of Calamity Ganon and to only activate upon his return?|Zelda's Research Notes|BotW}}</ref> At the top of each column lies a broken-down {{Term|BotW|Guardian}}, which all yield an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Material|link}} upon examination.
