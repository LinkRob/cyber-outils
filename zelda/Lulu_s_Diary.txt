==Location and Uses==
[[Lulu]]'s Diary is found in her room in [[Zora Hall]]. Because it is written in [[Zoran Script]], [[Link]] can only read it while in his [[Zora Link|Zora form]].<ref>{{Cite|Can you read Zoran script?|Tatl|MM}}</ref>

The Diary contains three entries, in which Lulu divulges the mysterious series of events involving her [[Zora Egg|eggs]]. The first entry describes Lulu's efforts to discover why she has lost her voice, which she had kept secret from [[Mikau]] for two days. [[Evan]], the band leader, recommends she take her eggs to the {{Term|MM3D|Marine Research Lab|link}}, which she intends to do.<ref name="Eggs"/> In the second entry, Lulu describes her brief struggle with the [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirates]] in the night, who stole her eggs before she could bring them to the Research Lab.<ref>{{Cite|Such a terrible thing has happened today that I don't even know where to begin writing. I heard a sound late at night, and when I opened my eyes, I saw strangers in my room. I tried to fight, but they were able to steal my precious eggs. It seems I lost consciousness after that.|Lulu's Diary|MM}}</ref> Finally, in the third entry Lulu writes that she told her predicament to Mikau, who she fears will react rashly.<ref>{{Cite|Today, I told everything to Mikau, the one person whom I didn't want to know about it. At first, I was too embarrassed and too sad to do anything. And with the words that Mikau said at that moment, I felt that all hope had been lost. But please, Mikau, I'm begging you, don't do anything rash.|Lulu's Diary|MM}}</ref> It was undoubtedly this conversation that led Mikau to confront the Gerudo Pirates and, ultimately, lose his life.
