{{Term/Store|EX Ancient Threats||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 55 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Ancient Threats|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It is only available as part of the first wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Ancient Threats}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given six and a half minutes to defeat nine {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} in the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} are scattered along a path that spans the entire map. The nine include a group of three {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}} and a pair of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}}, located on opposite ends of the path next to one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}. The other four are a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}} pair in the center of the path, and the last two are {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when all nine are killed. Other {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} include a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}. A second and third {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin}} appear when one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}} and when one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}} is defeated. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of 15 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rock Salt|link}}, 15 {{Plural|HWAoC|Smotherwing Butterfly|link}}, and 15 {{Plural|HWAoC|Fireproof Lizard|link}}.
