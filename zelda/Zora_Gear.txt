The {{Term/Store|Zora Gear|Zora Gear|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|Series|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Zora Gear}} is weaponry crafted by the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}}. It is made out of a particular type of silver metal that grants it increased durability and lightness.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Zora Sword}}<br/>The ornamentation that adorns this blade is a traditional Zora design. It's forged from a very durable and rust-proof metal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Zora Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Zora Spear}}<br/>This spear is a Zora's weapon of choice. It's lighter than it looks due to being made from a special metal and is used by the Zora for both fishing and protecting their domain.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Silver Bow">{{Cite|{{Big|Silver Bow}}<br/>A bow favored by the Zora for fishing. It doesn't boast the highest firepower, but the special metal it's crafted from prioritizes durability.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> In addition to combat, some of these items are traditionally used for fishing.<ref name="Zora Spear"/><ref name="Silver Bow"/>

Since all pieces of {{Term|BotW|Zora Gear}} are made out of metal, they will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, {{Term|BotW|Zora Gear}} will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
