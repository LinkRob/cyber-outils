'''Queen Seline''' is a character appearing in the {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}. She is the monarch of the kingdom of [[Calatia]] and seems to be close friends with [[King Harkinian]].<ref name=monarch>{{Cite Book|quote=May I present her most serene royal highness, Seline, beloved monarch of my homeland, Calatia.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=2}}</ref><ref name=friends>{{Cite Book|quote=Seline...? Seline! It's been...SIXTEEN YEARS! You haven't changed a bit! No...you're even more beautiful!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=3}}</ref> Seline is also very fond of [[Princess Zelda]], who she first met when Zelda was a child.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=You were too young to remember...|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page= }}</ref><ref name=quote>{{Cite Book|quote=I care for you more than you'll ever know. Grow up strong and wise. You will be queen of this land someday.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref> Possessing great magical powers that can easily destroy [[Ganon]]'s minions,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Your Majesty...why didn't you use your magic spells to defend yourself against Ganon's creatures?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=3}}</ref> Queen Seline is a fair and benevolent ruler who is beloved by the Calatians. She is heavily featured in ''[[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 4#Queen of Hearts|Queen of Hearts]]'' and ''[[Coming Home]]'', which are her only two appearances in the comic.

==Appearances and Biography==
===''Queen of Hearts''===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 4#Queen of Hearts|Queen of Hearts]]'', Seline's magical powers mysteriously disappear, and her search for them leads her to the kingdom of [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Some weeks ago, my powers disappeared. My search to regain them led me here.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=3}}</ref> Unfortunately, as she is traveling through the [[Death Mountain]] region, she is ambushed by a [[Wizzrobe]] and two [[Daira]]s. Link and Zelda soon arrive to the scene and rescue the queen, where Seline explains her situation. When the two heroes take Seline back to the [[North Palace]] to meet with King Harkinian,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=If you feel up to it, Seline, I'll introduce you to my father, the king...|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=3}}</ref> it is revealed that the two of them already know each other,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Thank you, Zelda, but that won't be necessary.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=3}}</ref> and both Harkinian and Seline are delighted to see one another again after sixteen years.<ref name=friends/> They spend most of the next day together,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Oh...can't  that wait, darling? I'm spending the day with Seline. It's been sixteen  years, you know! So much to catch up on!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=3}}</ref> which causes for Princess Zelda to become jealous of Seline.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Humph...doesn't she have her OWN realm to get back to?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page= }}</ref> 

Soon after Ganon's minions begin to head towards the castle, and Seline, regretting her choice of having spent so much time dawdling,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=No! ...Oh, no! I've been a fool to tarry here! They moved more swiftly than I thought possible!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> rushes off to seek the knowledge of the Triforce of Wisdom. When Link and Zelda realize that the sacred relic is gone, Zelda accuses Seline of stealing it,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Seline!  I knew it! You thought you could come in here, trick us into trust you  and then, while WE were defending the palace, you'd steal the Triforce  from under our noses!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> but the queen explains that she is not stealing the Triforce and that she merely came to Hyrule to seek its wisdom.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I am not stealing the Triforce. I came to Hyrule to seek its wisdom.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref>  She elaborates that Ganon has sealed her magic powers in a magic  container, and that the Triforce said that Zelda must accompany her to retrieve her powers, for otherwise, Seline will fail.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Ganon  has stolen my magic powers and sealed them in a magic container. Zelda,  the Triforce said that you must come with me to recover my powers,  otherwise I will fail. I've brought your horse. You must TRUST me, my  child!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> Despite her pleas, Zelda refuses to go with her.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I'm NOT your child! You must think I'm some kind of idiot! You expect me to trust you after you stole the Triforce?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page= }}</ref>  The queen continues to insist since now that Ganon is planning to use  her to take over Calatia, he will surely use her kingdom to take over  Hyrule as well.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Ganon's  army is pursuing me! He'll use me to take over Calatia. Once he  controls Calatia and its vital mountain passes, he'll use Calatia to  take over Hyrule!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> Just then, Link offers his assistance,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I'll go with you, Seline.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> and although Seline tells him that without Zelda's help they will fail due to the Triforce's prediction,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=No, brave Link! The Triforce said that if you go, you will fall in battle! Without Zelda, I am doomed!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> the young hero insists and the two of them begin to gallop away with Link clearing the bridge of Ganon's army to allow the queen to go through.

Eventually, Link and Seline stumble into a cave near the [[North Palace]],<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Later, in a mysterious cave to the south west of the North Palace...|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=8}}</ref> where they are trying to break open the magic container being guarded by a Wizzrobe and a horde of Ganon's minions so that Seline's powers will return.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The magic container is the key--! If we can break it open, my powers will return!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=8}}</ref>  Link is then heavily injured and faints, but before the Wizzrobe can do  anything, Zelda appears and shoots an arrow at the magic container,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Get away from my friends, you creep!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=9}}</ref> restoring Seline's powers and allowing her to easily defeat the army of enemies.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Ah! My powers have returned! Now then, little wizard....it's time for you to learn about REAL magic.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=9}}</ref>  Back at the palace, Seline says her goodbyes to King Harkinian, but  before she departs, she asks Zelda what made her trust her and go to the  cave.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Zelda, what made you come to the cave? What made you trust me?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref>  The young princess apologizes for her behavior, saying that everything  Seline did was out of love and Zelda was just being jealous of her.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I  thought of the way YOU acted. And the way I acted. Everything you did  was out of LOVE and everything I did was because of jealousy.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref> Seline returns Zelda's hug and reminds her to stay strong so that she will be the queen of Hyrule someday.<ref name=quote/> The story ends with Seline riding away as Zelda asks her father if she will ever be a great queen like her.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Do you think I could be a great queen like her someday?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref>

===''Coming Home''===
In ''[[Coming Home]]'', Queen Seline is suddenly attacked by an evil clone of her, which defeats her with her own powers and locks her up in the tower of the palace of Calatia.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I was set upon and defeated by someone who looked exactly like me AND had all my powers. Every magic spell I used--it  threw back at me! Now this...thing, disguised as YOU, Link, has taken  over the kingdom. My people are suffering under his evil rule!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> The clone, who turns out to be [[Shadow Link]], then takes over the queen's kingdom and begins to make her people suffer under his rule. When Link arrives to Calatia and is locked up along with Seline, she quickly explains the situation to the young hero until a Daira comes and takes both Seline and Link to Shadow Link, where he sentences both of them to execution.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=My  people! Now all will see what happens to those who defy the NEW king of  Calatia! [...] Silence! I am the king! You have committed treason  against me! There can be but ONE penalty! Executioner! Stand forth!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page= }}</ref> The queen then halts him and demands her right of a life trial since it  is Calatian Law that anyone sentenced to death has the right to trial by  combat to prove their innocence.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Hold  fast! I, Queen Seline, demand my right of LIFE TRIAL! By Calatian law,  anyone sentenced to death has the right to trial by combat to prove  their innocence!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=4, 5}}</ref> Shadow Link accepts her challenge and, in accordance with the ancient laws, Seline chooses Link to fight in her place,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=In accordance with the ancient laws...I choose this man to fight in my place, as my champion!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> to which Link agrees.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=What? I...uh...I mean...so be it.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref>

The next morning, however, Link wakes up to realize that his Triforce of Courage has left his heart,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It's gone! The Triforce of Courage--it's left me!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=8}}</ref> and Seline explains that it is due to it being the [[Day of the Triforce]], which is when all three Triforce pieces disappear once every decade for one full day.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Yes...it  is the Day of the Triforce. Once each decade, for one day, from dawn to  dusk, all three Triforces...Wisdom, Power, and Courage disappear. No  one knows wither or wherefore...just that it is so.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=8}}</ref> The young hero is discouraged since he cannot hope to have any chances against Shadow Link,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=How...how can I win...without the Triforce? What chance do I have...|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=8}}</ref> but the queen tells him that she has faith in him, and not the Triforce of Courage.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I have faith in YOU, Link. Not the Triforce of Courage.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=8}}</ref> Eventually, Link ends up winning against Shadow Link in the name of Seline, and the queen commends him for his great courage.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Even without the Triforce, Link, your courage is your greatest power!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref>
