{{Term/Store|Deku Scrub|Deku Scrubs|Series, OoT, MM, OoS, OoA, TMC, FSA, TFH|plural}} are a recurring tribe in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 50 {{Exp Game|MM, FSA}}, 169 {{Exp Game|TMC}}, 174 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoS}}, 210 {{Exp Game|TFH}}}}</ref> They are small forest creatures as well as enemies that inhabit [[Hyrule]], [[Termina]], and several other countries. They make their first appearance in {{OoT}}.

They are plant-based creatures that have skin seemingly made of wood and hair and "clothing" made of leaves and flowers,<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= It is susceptible to fire, since it originates from plants.|game= MM|page= 25}}</ref> and spit [[Deku Nut]]s at their enemies when threatened. They usually appear in a small, flower-like tuft of foliage which they hide within when threatened. They normally appear as enemies, but despite their antagonistic nature some tend to be friendly, at least when they believe it to be to their own benefit. It is rumored that a Deku Scrub's disposition can change drastically depending on the type of tree they are born from.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Though they are more hostile than not, they have been know to trade with Hylians. It is said that the disposition of a Deku Scrub differs greatly based on the type of tree they are born from. |book= E |publisher= |page= 50}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
As enemies, Deku Scrubs tend to hide in grassy or flower shrubs, and pop out when provoked to spit a Deku Nut at [[Link]]. When approached they will quickly retreat into their shrubs where they cannot be touched. In {{MM}}, they sometimes hide in [[Deku Flower]]s instead, which can be used after their defeat. While they cannot be harmed by direct combat, they are susceptible to long-range weapons, but can also be defeated by using a [[Shield]] to deflect their Deku Nuts back at them. When harmed, the Deku Scrub will be quick to surrender. This same strategy is also applied to the other variations of the Deku Scrub, including [[Business Scrub]]s and [[Mad Scrub]]s, which will jump out of their shrubs when first harmed.

Dekus can deal with water, but if they happen to venture in deep areas they will hop on the water to survive drowning. Like plants, too much water can hurt them. Luckily, Dekus are usually light enough to stand on [[Lily Pad]]s, granting safer travel over the swampy waters that the Dekus of Termina call home. In his [[Deku Link|Deku form]], Link is able to skip across water, but cannot swim. Dekus are also highly flammable, and rightly so, as they are wood-based creatures.

Some Deku Scrubs in {{MM|-}} also have propellers on their heads which allows for flight.

===Business Scrub===
{{Main|Business Scrub}}
[[File:OoT Business Scrub Model.png|thumb|A Business Scrub as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
Business Scrubs are Dekus that have taken to selling certain items, but they will only offer these items once Link defeats them. In {{OoT|-}}, Business Scrubs appear within the [[Lost Woods]] but are also common in many [[cave]]s and secret holes in the rest of [[Hyrule]]. While most of their deals are extortionate, some of them will offer important items such as a [[Piece of Heart]] or even the ability to carry more [[Deku Stick]]s or Deku Nuts.

===Mad Scrub===
{{Main|Mad Scrub}}
[[File:OoT Mad Scrub Model.png|thumb|A Mad Scrub as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
Mad Scrubs are a stronger and far more aggressive variation to the regular Deku Scrub. In {{OoT|-}}, they appear in the [[Sacred Forest Meadow]] when Link is young, and also in [[Kokiri Forest]] as the [[Hero of Time]]. In {{MM|-}}, they appear in the [[Deku Palace]] and [[Woodfall]]. Unlike regular Dekus, they attack in three Deku Nut bursts, and will make no attempt to bargain with Link and can be destroyed in battle.

===Totem Deku===
{{Main|Totem Deku}}
Totem Dekus are a variation that appear exclusively in {{TFH}}. They are similar to ordinary Deku Scrubs, but commonly have segmented bodies that make them appear much taller, leaving their vulnerable heads out of reach. The [[Links]] can reach and attack them by forming a [[Totem]].

==Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Deku Scrub}}<br/>It will hide in the grass if you get close to it. Bounce the nuts it spits back at it!

Deku Scrubs first appeared in {{OoT}}, where they primarily appear as enemies [[Inside the Deku Tree]] where they are subordinate to [[Gohma]].<ref>{{Cite|How did you know our secret?! How irritating! It's so annoying that I'm going to reveal the secret of Queen Gohma to you! In order to administer the coup de grace to Queen Gohma, strike with your sword while she's stunned. Oh, Queenie... Sorry about that!|Deku Scrub|OoT}}</ref> Here [[Link]] learns of his ability to deflect their nuts back at them with his shield for the first time. Unlike other enemies, once struck they will not be destroyed, and will run around in a panic. When Link approaches, they will plead for mercy and offer him some advice to progress through the dungeon.

A group of three Deku Scrubs appear prior to Gohma's room, guarding the doorway. Unlike previous Deku Scrubs, these enemies will become stunned when hit, and will not give up unless Link defeats them in the correct order, a secret revealed by Deku Scrub defeated previously in the dungeon. This can be remembered using the phrase "twenty-three is number one".<ref>{{Cite|Please forgive me, master! I'll never do it again! If you spare me, I'll teach you something cool. You will never beat my brothers up ahead unless you punish them in the proper order.The order is... 2  3  1 Twenty-three is number one! Do you think I'm a traitor?|Deku Scrub|OoT}}</ref> When Link beats them, they reveal to Link the strategy to use in order to defeat Gohma. In the {{MQ}} version of the game, the Deku Scrub that reveals the correct order is replaced by a [[Mad Scrub]], and the order to defeat the three Deku Scrubs is changed to 3 1 2. Link can only discover this new order by trial and error.

Deku Scrubs also appear in the [[Lost Woods#Ocarina of Time|Lost Woods]], most notably in the [[Forest Stage]]. Here, a group of Deku Scrubs will judge the face of any visitor who stands on the stage, and reward them depending on what they think of it. This reward varies from [[Rupee]]s, nothing at all, or a hail of Deku Nuts. If Link visits with the correct [[Mask]], he will meet a large Deku Scrub who will offer him a greater reward. According to him, the [[Skull Mask]] looks like the Deku's sacred forest totem.<ref>{{Cite|All of the young Deku Scrub brothers agree...you look exactly like our sacred forest totem! As an offering from us, please accept these Deku Sticks. We will also enhance your carrying skills! Abracadabra! Alakazaaaam!|Deku Scrub|OoT}}</ref> 

One normal Deku Scrub also appears in the Lost Woods making his home in the top of a tree from which hangs a small drum-like target. If Link hits the center of the target three times in a succession, he will be granted a larger [[Bullet Bag]].<ref>{{Cite|Cool! You're great! You scored three perfect bull's-eyes! I have to give a neat present to such a wonderful person! Please take it!|Deku Scrub|OoT}}</ref>

==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:Deku Scrub OOS.png|left]]
In {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, Deku Scrubs are scattered throughout [[Labrynna]] and [[Holodrum]]'s lands and caves. Most of them are non-violent Business Scrubs that trade freely with Link. Some sell staples such as [[Mystical Seed]]s and [[Bomb]]s, though most Business Scrubs sell Shields. The largest population of Deku Scrubs appears in a forest in western Labrynna in the past, appropriately named [[Deku Forest]]. 

Of the two [[Game Boy Color]] games, ''Seasons'' is the only one to feature hostile Deku Scrubs. These Scrubs behave similarly to the ones in {{OoT|-}}; they shoot seeds at Link from afar and hide themselves when he approaches. Deflecting their seeds back at them with the shield will cause them to surrender, at which point they will offer Link helpful advice.<ref>{{Cite|I surrender! In return, I offer my advice! If you are lost in the woods, travel west, then south, east and north, taking each step in a warmer season.|Deku Scrub|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I surrender! In return, here's a tip! On the summit of the mountain in the east are Spring Bananas! Many people will want them!|Deku Scrub|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I surrender! In return, I offer my advice! Even if you have a key, you still need a keyhole! Foolish brother! The keyhole is on the summit!|Deku Scrub|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I surrender! In return, I offer my advice! Even if you have a keyhole, you still need a key. Moronic brother! The key is in the valley in the west.|Deku Scrub|OoS}}</ref> Two such Scrubs appear on [[Mt. Cucco]], one of which has a [[sign]] next to it that forbids the use of shields and deflecting seeds, comically revealing its weakness.<ref>{{Cite|No shields!!! No deflecting seeds allowed!|Sign|OoS}}</ref>

There are also three friendly Deku Scrubs who have roles in [[sidequest]]s. One of them is located in the [[Lost Woods]] and plays a role in the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}. After playing the [[Phonograph]] for it, it will tell Link how to find his "Secret Spot," where the final reward for the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest}}, a greater [[Sword]], is located.<ref>{{Cite|Dee! What a tune! I love it! I'll teach you the way to my Secret Spot! If temperatures rise as you go far to the west, you'll find it!|Deku Scrub|OoS}}</ref> In a [[Linked Game]] of {{OoA}}, a friendly Deku Scrub appears in Deku Forest. He can be heard singing a "secret" (the [[Linked Game/Secrets#Deku Secret|Deku Secret]]) called "Love the Seed," which is apparently quite popular among Dekus.<ref>{{Cite|Love, love the seed! This secret is all the rage among Deku Scrubs now! Want to hear the rest?|Deku Scrub|OoA}}</ref><ref name="Love the Seed">{{Cite|I can't remember the last half of "Love the Seed," the hit secret among us Deku Scrubs. "Love, love the seed!" What's the next part? Do you know it?|Deku Scrub|OoS}}</ref> Link tells the secret to another singing Deku that appears in a cave west of [[Sunken City]] in Holodrum,<ref>{{Cite|The Deku Scrub west of Sunken City in Holodrum loves this secret! I do, too! You should pay him a visit!|Deku Scrub|OoA}}</ref> who also loves the secret song but cannot remember the last half.<ref name="Love the Seed" /> As thanks, the Deku increases the size of Link's [[Seed Satchel]].<ref>{{Cite|Yes! Yes! That's it! It touches me everytime I hear it! In return, I'll make it so you can carry more seeds! Come back with your Seed Satchel full with all five kinds of seeds!|Deku Scrub|OoS}}</ref>

The Deku Scrubs of {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}} are unique in that they appear to be individualistic creatures, given that most of them remain distanced from one another and they are never seen to interact. In fact, the singing Deku Scrubs are the only ones that refer to others of their own kind. The supposedly popular Deku song, "Love the Seed," is the only indication that these Deku Scrubs share any form of culture. Only the friendly Dekus that are not Business Scrubs demonstrate musical inclination, however.

These Deku Scrubs were awoken from their slumber by [[Ganon]] transforming the forest into the Lost Woods.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to Ganon, we were awakened from our slumber!|Deku Scrub|FSA}}</ref> They look forward to the expansion of the Lost Woods to cover all the forests of Hyrule, at which point they can travel freely.<ref>{{Cite|Soon, Lord Ganon's power will change all of Hyrule's forests into Lost Woods! If you wander lost here for too long, you too can become a Deku Scrub!|Deku Scrub|FSA}}</ref> Despite being tied to the woods, they are able to travel beyond the forest using underground passageways,<ref>{{Cite|Make sure that all who enter the forest remain here! Lord Ganon's orders! But we're trying to build an underground road that will let us go anywhere... Anyway, we've hidden every single shovel we could find! If there are no holes, no one will ever find our road. We're absolutely brilliant!|Deku Scrub|FSA}}</ref> and later appear in [[Frozen Hyrule]] where they have taken to a new game they call [[Terrorpin Hockey]].<ref>{{Cite|Oopipi.... I've done it! ==={{TMC|-}}===
The Deku Scrubs of {{TMC}} are all Business Scrubs. They spit seeds as well, but mean no harm by it; they merely have a natural tendency to spit seeds when they speak.<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, we scrubs have a tendency to spit nuts when we speak! Everyone got scared and ran away, and no one would buy anything! This is a terrible place to do business! I'm going back to my cave!|Business Scrub|TMC}}</ref>
==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, Deku Scrubs appear on top of a [[Totem]] containing one, two, or three scrubs. The Links must use the totem formation to defeat any that are beyond their reach. However, the Deku Scrubs will burrow back into the ground if approached, so the Links must use a ranged weapon (such as the [[Bow]]) to defeat them.

[[File:EoW Deku.jpg|thumb|Deku Scrub's eating “Cotton Candy”]]
Deku scrubs can be found in the [[Faron Wetlands]] eating cotton candy that turns out to be made of spider webs, oozing from the rifts.

==Other Appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Plural|CoH|Deku Scrub}} appear as both enemies and allies in {{CoH}}, where they are hidden throughout {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}. While they are stationary enemies, their attack pattern is based on the beat of the music, appearing from their hiding spots only when the heroes are within their line of sight for a total of three beats. Once exposed, they will fire a projectile at them on the second beat before returning underground on the fourth beat. Like their previous incarnations, their projectiles can be reflected back at them either with the {{Term|CoH|Shield|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Nayru's Love|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Torch of Reflection|link}}. However, their projectiles can also be used to deal damage to other monsters and enemies that walk within their line of fire, including other {{Plural|CoH|Deku Scrub}}.

Upon defeat, all enemy {{Plural|CoH|Deku Scrub}} will drop a {{Term|CoH|Deku Seed|link}}, which can be given to {{Term|CoH|Tingle|link}}. Giving him 10 {{Plural|CoH|Deku Seed}} will have him cut the hero's {{Term|CoH|Stamina|link}} in half in order to grant them more magic power, while giving him 20 {{Plural|CoH|Deku Seed}} will add the friendly {{Term|CoH|Deku Scrub}} {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} to the team. There are a total of 21 to 22 {{Plural|CoH|Deku Scrub}} within {{Term|CoH|Hyrule}}, with 12 randomly scattered across the open landscape and 8 hidden in a cave behind a cracked wall within the {{Term|CoH|ReDead|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Poe|link}} section of the {{Term|CoH|Lost Woods}}. The final {{Plural|CoH|Deku Scrub}} appear within {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}'s and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}'s starting areas, which are {{Term|CoH|Link's House|link}} and the room outside {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}}'s bedroom in {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle|link}} respectively, though the latter only appears if {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} had woken up {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}} before {{Term|CoH|Link}}.

{{Term|CoH|Yves}} is the only {{Term|CoH|Deku Scrub}} that is friendly to the heroes, greeting them as soon they arrive to {{Term|CoH|Tingle}}'s Hut within the {{Term|CoH|Lost Woods|link}}. He dreams of becoming an adventurer when he grows up, but believes that he can't become one due to a lack of arms.<ref>{{Cite|I've always wanted to be a hero like you... but I can't even hold a sword. Oh, well... A scrub can dream...|Yves|CoH}}</ref> Despite this, he joins in on their quest once at least 20 {{Plural|CoH|Deku Seed}} are given to {{Term|CoH|Tingle}}. But being a young {{Term|CoH|Deku Scrub}} with no arms, he can't use any weapons and only possesses one {{Term|CoH|Heart|link}}, making him vulnerable to damage. However, he can avoid attacks by burrowing underground or attacking enemies from afar with his {{Term|CoH|Deku Spit|link}} ability, allowing him to fire projectiles at the cost of {{Term|CoH|Stamina}}.

Despite being of the same race, other {{Plural|CoH|Deku Scrub}} will still fire their projectiles at {{Term|CoH|Yves}} just like they would with any other hero.

==Trivia==
*Ironically, in the Japanese releases, Deku Scrubs are known as '''Deku Nut''', sharing the same name as the [[Deku Nut|item]] they spit at [[Link]].
*Deku Scrubs can be seen as a parallel to the [[mariowiki:Snifit|Snifit]] and [[mariowiki:Birdo_(species)|Birdo]] enemies from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], as both are known to have nose-like mouths capable of spiting large objects at their foes.
*Deku Scrubs have an unusual way of speaking that often involves making the noise "pi" after sentences. This was removed from the translations of most games, but was left intact in some instances, such as in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]''.
*The hint for defeating the Deku Scrub triplets guarding the boss room [[Inside the Deku Tree]] in Japanese is {{Romanize|「ニイさんイチバン」|niisan ichiban|Twenty-three is number one}},<ref>{{Cite|三つ子デクナッツは、決まった順にやっつけないと復活しちゃうッピ。その順番は… 2 3 1 「ニイさんイチバン」だッピ！|Deku Scrub|OoT}}</ref> which is actually a play on words: "niisan ichiban" could either read "Two three number one" or "Older brother first."
*Initially during development, Deku Scrubs started out as a character named "Hobbit" {{Romanize|ホビット|Ho bitto|Hobbit}}, inspired from {{Wp|J. R. R. Tolkien}}'s ''{{Wp|The Lord of the Rings}}''.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote=-宮本さんのお気に入りのキャラは?  '''[[Miyamoto|宮本いまは:]]'''「デクナッツ」ですね。実  ようしよ は「ホビット」っていうキャラが仕様書  にあって、で、そい が話をしたりモノ |magazine=''64 Dream''|publisher=MyCom |date=January 1999 |page=93 |url=https://archive.org/details/64dream-january-1999-ozidual/64Dream%201999%2001%20%28J%20OCR%29/page/n96/mode/1up}}</ref>
