The {{Term/Store|Zora Greaves||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} are a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Zora Greaves}}<br/>These greaves have been passed down among the Zora for generations. It's been said they're crafted using dragon scales. Equip them to swim faster.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Zora Greaves}}<br/>These greaves have been passed down among the Zora for generations. It's been said they're crafted using dragon scales. They reportedly increase the wearer's swim speed.|Allies|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Zora Greaves are given to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} by [[Laflat]] as a reward for completing the "[[Lynel Safari]]" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. Zora Greaves were originally made by ancient craftsman on behalf of an old [[Zora]] king.<ref>{{Cite|These {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora greaves}} were once made by order of an ancient Zora king. Now they're fairly common.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> After the dam for [[East Reservoir Lake]] was finished, Zora began making them for visiting {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} as a sign of friendship.<ref>{{Cite|After {{Color|BotW Blue|East Reservoir Lake}} was finished, we made Zora greaves as a token of friendship for Hylians who came to visit.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> The Zora Greaves that Laflat gives to Link were the last pair left in her father's armor shop after it closed.<ref>{{Cite|These Zora greaves here were the last pair left in the stock room when he closed up shop.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>

Wearing the Zora Greaves will increase Link's swim speed. They can also be worn with the rest of the [[Zora Set]], the [[Zora Helm]] and [[Zora Armor]], to give Link the Swim Dash Stamina Up [[Set Bonus]]. They're able to be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} and [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
In Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity, the Zora Greaves have the same design as their version in Breath of the Wild. They can be obtained by completing the [[Armor for Whom?]] quest. When unlocked, the Zora Greaves become part of Link's attire and can be worn in almost any scenario or challenge. They can also be dyed at the Kochi Dye Shop.
{{Noncanon|End}}
