==Location and Uses==
[[File:Snail (M).png|left]]
[[File:Snail (F).png|left|47px]]
The male Snail can be found under a ruined stairway in the [[Sacred Grove]] The female Snail can be found within the {{Term|TP|Temple of Time|link}} in the past. They can be given to [[Agitha]] in exchange for different items, such as a bigger [[Wallet]], [[Rupee]]s, or her [[Miiverse Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}. If collected a second time, they will drop a [[Heart]].
