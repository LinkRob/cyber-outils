The "{{Term/Store|Song of the Hero|Song of the Hero|Series, SS, SSHD, BotW}}" is a recurring {{Term|Series|Song|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|SSHD Gold|Song of the Hero}} is now complete!|N/A|SSHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= "Song of the Hero" by the Court Poet|book= CaC |publisher= |page= 390}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{SS|-}}===
The {{Term|SSHD|Song of the Hero}} is learned in four parts: one from {{Term|SSHD|Faron (Dragon)|link}}, one from {{Term|SSHD|Lanayru (Dragon)|link}}, one from {{Term|SSHD|Eldin (Dragon)|link}}, and one from {{Term|SSHD|Levias|link}}. It is used to open the {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft|link}} {{Term|SSHD|Silent Realm|link}}.

====Lyrics====

==={{BotW|-}}===
After {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} has returned to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, he will inform {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that there is a song he wants him to listen to.<ref>{{Cite|I'm taking a moment's rest from my tireless search for the ancient songs to perform in my hometown. ...By the by, I've something I must ask of you. I've a song that I need you to hear. I wish to speak with you, so this will take some time. Is that all right?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to listen, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} reveals that he wanted to tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} about his teacher, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[That's fine.] Thank you ever so much. I wanted to talk to you about my teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} shares that the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} held the belief that {{Term|BotW|Link}} would one day return to defeat {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, and he turned that belief into a song.<ref>{{Cite|...My teacher believed a hero would appear to beat back the Calamity. He poured that belief into a song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} then proceeds to play the "{{Term|BotW|Song of the Hero}}" for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.

Once the "{{Term|BotW|Song of the Hero}}" has been performed, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} asks on behalf of the departed {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} will accept it.<ref>{{Cite|So...appointed knight. Will you accept this song from my departed teacher?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} does, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} thanks him.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] Thank you so much! I knew you would. I was sure of it.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>
