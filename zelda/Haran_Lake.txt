{{Term/Store|Haran Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Haran Lake'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Haran Lake}} is located in the [[Faron Grasslands]] between [[Harfin Valley]] and [[Fural Plain]]. Not far east of {{Term|BotW|Haran Lake}}, the [[Highland Stable]] resides nearby. The {{Term|BotW|Haran Lake}} is not deep enough for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to swim in and doesn't go far past his knees. Several {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} stand tall around the perimeter of the {{Term|BotW|Haran Lake|display=Lake}}.
