{{Term/Store|Blacksmith|Blacksmiths|Series, ZA|plural}} are enemies in {{ZA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Begone! I, Blacksmith, have my work to do! No time for intruders!|Blacksmith (Enemy)|ZA}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Two Blacksmiths appear early in the [[Shrine of Fire]]. They can both be seen working at some anvils in separate chambers, and immediately threaten and attack [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] once she appears. Both Blacksmiths attack by throwing spears at Zelda, which they have crafted themselves.<ref>{{Cite|Feel the tip of my spears and tell me they are the finest in Tolemac!|Blacksmith (Enemy)|ZA}}</ref> They take three hits of the [[Wand]] to defeat. The second Blacksmith drops the Shrine's [[Compass]] once he is defeated.
