{{Term/Store|Deku Link||Series, MM, MM3D}} is one of the forms [[Link]] turns into in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 187 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> He is forcefully transformed into this form by the [[Skull Kid]] at the beginning of the game, and the hero can later transform at will by wearing the [[Deku Mask]]. It is one of five [[Transformation#Majora's Mask|Mask transformations]] in the game.

==Transformation==
{{Main|Retrieval of the Ocarina of Time}}
[[File:Deku link reflection.jpg|right|thumb|Deku Link seeing his reflection after Link's forced transformation by the Skull Kid|288x288px]]
After stumbling through the [[Lost Woods]] in pursuit of the [[Skull Kid#Mjora's Mask|Skull Kid]] after being ambushed by him alongside the Fairy siblings [[Tatl]] and [[Tael]], [[Link]] falls through a large hole leading to the parallel world of [[Termina]]. At the end of the hole, Link finds Skull Kid wearing the evil Majora's Mask, possessed by its influence. He eerily laughs and transforms the young [[Hero of Time]] into a [[Deku Scrub]].<ref>{{Cite|Hee, hee! Now, that's a good look for you! You'll stay here looking that way forever! |Skull Kid|MM}}</ref>Link tries to chase after the Skull Kid for payback but is stopped by Tatl (leaving the Skull Kid and her brother behind). He then leaves through the door along with Tatl catching up to him. She agrees to be his partner until they catch the Skull Kid. The [[Happy Mask Salesman]] later finds Link and informs him that he can be returned to his original form if the [[instrument]] stolen from him, the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}}, is retrieved. Link accepts the quest and explores [[Clock Town]] over the course of [[Time|three days]] and, after interacting with various townsfolk, manages to confront the Skull Kid atop the [[Clock Tower]]. He quickly uses a [[Bubble Blast]] to knock the sacred Ocarina out of Skull Kid's hands, scoops it up, and begins to play (not before memorizing a flashback of [[Princess Zelda]] of himself leaving Hyrule). Link's Deku form causes the Ocarina of Time to appear as the [[Deku Pipes]].<ref>{{Cite|Huh? When did you get that instrument?!?|Tatl|MM}}</ref> Undeterred, Link plays the "[[Song of Time]]" to travel back in time to the first day.

He then presents the recovered instrument to the Happy Mask Salesman, who teaches Link the "[[Song of Healing]]." The magical properties of this song ease Link's soul, and he returns back to his human form. The eradication of the curse causes the Deku Mask to materialize, granting Link the ability to transform into a Deku Scrub at will when worn. Link then starts his quest to find a way to retrieve the Majora's Mask that is manipulating the [[Moon#Majora's Mask|Moon]] to crash into Termina. Link himself would later find several more Masks that would also make him change into different forms similar to that of the Deku Mask by healing souls, specifically the [[Goron Mask|Goron]] and [[Zora Mask]].

Link's new form is required while he is exploring the [[Southern Swamp]]. With it, he can hop across the toxic water, infiltrate the [[Deku Palace]], reveal the entrance to [[Woodfall Temple]], and explore and conquer the temple itself.

Deku Link is the incarnation of the [[Deku Butler's Son]], who was turned into a tree while hunting for mushrooms in the forest,<ref>{{Cite|My son often used that mask to find mushrooms in the forest.|Deku Butler|MM}}</ref> possibly as a curse by the possessed Skull Kid.<ref>{{Cite|It's strange, but the way you look right now sort of looks like this tree... |Tatl|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link's transformation into a Deku Scrub is caused by the spirit of a member of the Deku tribe that died in the forest connecting Hyrule and Termina.|book=HH|page=112|publisher=Dark Horse Books}}</ref>This also explains the form's sad expression, unlike other transformations.

==Abilities==
While in his Deku form, Link gains many unique abilities which can be used to fight enemies and to solve [[Puzzle|puzzles]]. These abilities prove invaluable in several locations throughout [[Termina]].

===Water Hopping===
Deku Link is incapable of swimming, and thus instead travels on the surface of bodies of water through a series of hops. He can bounce up to five times on a water's surface, with the fifth bounce significantly higher than the previous four. If Deku Link falls into the water after the fifth bounce, he will sink and reemerge back onto the ground from where he was last standing (with a [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_Whoops.wav comical sound effect] being heard). This ability prevents Deku Link from being damaged by {{Term|MM3D|Toxic Water|link}} or from being affected by the speed or direction of the current. This ability is primarily employed in areas with Toxic Water, such as the Southern Swamp and the Woodfall Temple. In {{MM3D}}, Deku Link requires extra speed to hop farther before performing a [[Spin Attack]].

Unlike normal Link, {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link}} can stand on {{Plural|MM3D|Link-Eating Plant|link}}.
[[File:Deku flying.png|right|frameless|249x249px|Deku Link flying]]

===Deku Flower flight===
By burrowing into a [[Deku Flower]], Deku Link can launch himself into the air in brief flight and drop [[Deku Nut]]s onto enemies.<ref>{{Cite|All right, listen up! If you press and hold (A) as a Deku Scrub while standing on a Deku Flower, you can dive into it. If you wait a bit before releasing (A), you'll launch out of the flower. Press (A) while flying to descend.|Tatl|MM}}</ref> The elevation and duration of the flight varies from flower to flower, though any flight can be extended if Deku Link passes through a powerful air stream. As well as granting Link access to previously inaccessible areas, launching from a Deku Flower is also a powerful attack move. This is necessary to defeat enemies such as [[Snapper (Turtle)|Snapper]]s, and can also be used to defeat the boss of the Woodfall Temple, [[Odolwa]].

===Bubble Blast===
{{main|Bubble Blast}}
[[File:MM Bubble Blast.png|right|frameless|Deku Link using the Bubble Blast]]
This power is granted to Deku Link after he restores the body of the [[Great Fairy]] in [[North Clock Town]]. It allows Link to release powerful bubbles at the expense of costing [[Magic]].<ref>{{Cite|You've been granted Magic Power! In your current form, press (B) to shoot bubble blasts. Press and hold (B) to blow a big bubble. Release (B) to shoot it. Your Magic Power decreases when you shoot. Replenish it with Magic Jars and Potions.|N/A|MM}}</ref> This attack is useful in breaking airborne objects and defeating flying enemies. The disadvantage is that the range and speed is inefficient compared to that of [[Arrow]]s. In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] remake, a sight was added when going into first-person perspective. Also, the bubble does not burst if charged for too long, its charging time is decreased, and it travels faster in a straight line, making it more reliable to defeat some enemies.

===Spin Attack===
[[File:Deku Link Spin.png|right|frameless|167x167px|Deku Link spinning]]
By tapping the [[Controller Buttons#A Button|A-Button]] while moving, Deku Link will twirl on one foot at an accelerated pace, resembling the traditional Spin Attack. This spin can stun enemies and break weak objects such as [[pot]]s, although it is rarely powerful enough to defeat an enemy. In {{MM3D|-}}, the tip of Deku Link's hat transforms into a root when using the Spin Attack.

===Deku Shield===
Deku Link possesses a unique [[Shield]] in the form of a large [[Deku Nut]] husk. It is similar to [[Goron Link]]'s shield in that it renders Link immobile, but is also impervious to most attacks from any direction (excluding fire-based attacks). This defense is also reminiscent of Child Link's attempt to use the [[Hylian Shield]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|{{OoT|-}}]].

===Other Abilities===
[[File:DekuLink on a LilyPad.png|thumb|Deku Link standing on a Lily Pad in the Southern Swamp.|223x223px]]
Due to Deku Link's light weight, he is able to stand on [[Lily Pad]]s without them sinking below him. This ability makes the exploration of {{Term|MM3D|Toxic Water}} in the Southern Swamp possible. However, this ability also makes it impossible for Deku Link to press down most [[Floor Switch]]es. His small size also makes him too short to grab and climb onto most ledges.

As Deku Link is made of wood, he is especially vulnerable to fire-based attacks, similar to [[Zora Link]]. Mere contact with a flame of any sort will typically be enough to instantly knock him out, and he reemerges from where he last entered the area.

==Instrument==
[[File:MM Deku Pipes Model.png|right|195x195px]]
While in Deku form, Link's Ocarina of Time takes the form of the [[Deku Pipes]]. This instrument is known for its high volume.<ref>{{Cite|Wait...You don't happen to have something that can make a lot of  noise...like a loud instrument with sound that carries a long way?|Monkey|MM}}</ref> It is required to play the "[[Sonata of Awakening]]" with maximum effect.

The Deku Pipes are primarily used to raise the Woodfall Temple from the swamp, granting access to it.

==Trivia==
[[File:MM Deku Link shrugging to the player.gif|thumb|239x239px|Deku Link breaking the 4th wall]]
*Toward the beginning of {{MM}}, {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link}} briefly breaks the [[wikipedia:Fourth_wall|fourth wall]] when, incredulous at {{Term|MM3D|Tatl|link}}'s sudden change of heart, he turns toward the player, shakes his head, and shrugs.
**[[Link]] also turns and shrugs if he attempts to play a song for [[Pierre]] and then cancels, though the camera must be set at the correct angle (facing at his right) to make him appear to break the 4th wall.
*Despite Link being canonically left-handed (or possibly ambidextrous), when Deku Link opens [[Treasure Chest]]s, he lifts their contents with his right hand.
*Deku Link is the only one of Link's transformations in the game (excluding the [[Fierce Deity Link|Fierce Deity]]) that cannot press down a [[Floor Switch|floor switch]], making his "[[Elegy of Emptiness]]" statue useless.
*Deku Link is the shortest among all of Link's five transformations in {{MM|-}}, with the height of a small child. Because of this, certain characters, particularly the [[Soldier|Soldiers]] and the [[Man from Trading Post|two]] [[Part-Timer|employees]] of the [[Trading Post]], view Link in this form as a lost [[Deku Scrub]] kid.
[[File:Deku Link concept 2.jpg|thumb|224x224px|Concept art of Deku Link wearing the scrapped Link Mask]]
*One of the concept artwork of Deku Link seen in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]]'' shows him appearing to wear an [[Unused Content|unused]] mask strongly resembling Link's normal face. This particular mask, though not released in the final game (but still remains in the game's code), is actually a scrapped transformation mask that was likely originally going to make Link change into his adult self seen in {{OoT}}, before being replaced by the [[Fierce Deity Mask|Fierce Deity's Mask]].
*During development, while thinking in an event for the Deku, [[Eiji Aonuma]] had a nightmare about being chased by one. The next day, [[Takumi Kawagoe]] showed him a movie he created for the Deku which was exactly as Aouma's dream.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= I was thinking about an event for the Deku, and have been trying to figure out what to do with it. I thought of it at home, and Dekus appeared in my dream. I woke up screaming! I went to work the next day and that's when (Takumi) Kawagoe-san told me that he finished making a movie for the Dekus, so I had him show it to me....and that movie was exactly like my dream!|name= Eiji Aonuma|url= http://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/#/3ds/majoras-mask-3d/0/0|title= Iwata Asks: The Legend of Zelda: Majora’s Mask 3D}}</ref> This scene was later ultimately used for Link's initial transformation into a Deku by [[Skull Kid]]'s curse.
