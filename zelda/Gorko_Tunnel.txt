{{Term/Store|Gorko Tunnel||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gorko Tunnel|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Gorko Tunnel is an underground passage located in the northeastern end of the gorge between [[Gortram Cliff]] and [[Gorko Lake]], in [[Eldin Canyon]]. The Tunnel is said to conceal the "secret of the hero."<ref>{{Cite|I wonder when my little brother will come home... He can't really be searchin' for the {{Color|BotW Red|secret of the hero}} in the depths of {{Color|BotW Blue|Gorko Tunnel}}... Right?|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> [[Gonguron]], the brother of [[Bladon]], can be found inside the Tunnel excavating it to uncover its secret. A lit [[Cooking Pot]] for [[Cooking]] can also be found inside. 

As part of the [[Side Quest]] "[[A Brother's Roast]]", Bladon asks Link to go to Gorko Tunnel to find his brother. There it is discovered that Gonguron has collapsed from hunger and cannot continue excavating.<ref>{{Cite|<nowiki>*cough* *wheeze*</nowiki> Phew! I'm spent. To think that somewhere behind all this rubble is the {{Color|BotW Red|secret of the hero}}... I know I'll become really great and important if I can find it! My only problem is that I don't have the strength to go on. My belly's empty and and rumblin' like mad! What to do...|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> Bladon, who arrives shortly after, asks Link to bring back a [[Rock Roast]] from Gortram Cliff so he can cook it for his brother.<ref>{{Cite|I need you to do me a favor! As a brother, I can't just sit idly by. I have to help him! Exit here and head {{Color|BotW Red|down the hill}} to Gortram Cliff. There you can find {{Color|BotW Red|rock roasts}} rollin' around. I need ya to please grab one of 'em for me.|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> After Gonguron eats the cooked Rock Roast, he finishes excavating the Tunnel, and it is discovered that the secret is the [[Kayra Mah Shrine]].<ref>{{Cite|WOW! There it is! The {{Color|BotW Red|hero's secret}} has revealed itself to me!! <small>Weird... I feel like I've seen somethin' like this before in Goron City...</small>|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Gorko Tunnel's name is derived from [[Gorko]], the [[Goron]] from {{SS}}.
