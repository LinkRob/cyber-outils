{{Term/Store|Like the Wind||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Like the Wind|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Like the Wind}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, with {{Term|HWAoC|Revali|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Teba|link}} as its pilots. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Chillfin Trout|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Chillshroom|link}}, and 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

Throughout the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to defeat all {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} on the map five minutes. There are only ten at first, although five more spawn when five, and fourteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} are defeated. Other {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} in the area include {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|White-Maned Lynel|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} and many {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once all 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} are defeated.
