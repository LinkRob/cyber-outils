==Trivia==
* {{Term|BotW|Bolson}}'s profession, demeanor, and attire are an allusion to the {{Plural|OoT3D|Carpenter|link}} in the Japanese version of {{OoT}}. In it, the {{Plural|OoT3D|Carpenter}} would aimlessly prance around {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}} and notably employ characteristically feminine speech.<ref>{{Cite|{{Romanize|アタイ、大工のイチロー。|Atai, Daiku no Ichirō.|I'm the carpenter Ichiro}}|Ichiro|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Romanize|あたしたち　一日中この村で　家　建ててんの。|Atashitachi ichinichijū kono mura de ie tatete n no.|We are building houses in this village all day long.}}|Carpenter|OoT}}</ref> Additionally, one of the {{Plural|OoT3D|Carpenter}} refers to {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} as a {{Romanize|cute boy|カワイイ　ボーヤ|Kawaī Bōya|3}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Romanize|カワイイ　ボーヤ！助けてくれて　サンキュー！。|Kawaii Bōya! Tasuketekurete Sankyū!|Cute Boy! Thank you for rescuing me!}}|Jiro|OoT}}</ref> These are stereotypical traits of gay men in Japanese media that weren't properly adapted for the English versions. In the English versions of {{BotW|-}} however, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}}'s stereotypical characteristics were retained. {{Term|BotW|Bolson}}'s tiger-striped collar, leg wrappings, and blue jacket are also likely a reference to the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Craftsman|link}} from {{OoT|-}}.
** Additionally, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} was originally going to have a larger build which closely resembled the body shape of the Master Craftsman.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 96}}</ref>
* If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} while not wearing any {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} that covers his torso, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} will become excited and call him "perky".<ref>{{Cite|Whoa my! ♪ Aren't you perky? ♪ Well then...|Bolson|BotW}}</ref>
