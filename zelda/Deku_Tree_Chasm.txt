'''The Deku Tree Chasm''' is a location in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]. It is inside the [[The Great Deku Tree|Great Deku Tree]] and was caused by [[Ganondorf|Ganondorf's]] [[Gloom]] entering the [[Lost Woods]] and reaching [[Korok Forest]]. During this time, the [[Korok]]s are paralyzed in place and the Deku Tree is [[Gloom-Borne Illness|sick with Gloom]]. [[Link]] comes up from the Depths and finds the Deku Tree in pain because its stomach is not well. If Link skydives down this Chasm, he can find Gloom Spawn inside. Link defeats them and [[Phantom Ganon]], the Scourge of the Deku Tree, appears. Link battles it and defeats it and the Koroks come down to see what's going on.

After doing this, Link can accept the [[Recovering the Hero's Sword]] main quest in order to get the [[Master Sword]]. This chasm is stated to actually be the Deku Tree's stomach which is what he states.

== Trivia ==

* In order to get into the Deku Tree Chasm, Link has to come from Korok Grove in the Depths
* The Deku Tree Chasm was opened by Gloom, explaining Phantom Ganon
* The Phantom Ganon here has more health than the average overworld versions
