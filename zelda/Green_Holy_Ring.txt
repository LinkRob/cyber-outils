The {{Term/Store|Green Holy Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Green Holy Ring<br/>No damage from electricity|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Green Holy Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. In {{OoS}}, it can also be obtained in the [[Treasure Grove]] by following the [[Strange Brothers]]. In {{OoA}}, the Ring can also be found on the shore south of [[Fairies' Woods]], reached through the [[Zora Seas]]. Aditionally, it can be obtained by getting a perfect score at the gold level of the [[Goron Dance]] minigame. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it negates all damage from electricity.<ref name="Description"/> The Green Holy Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
