{{Compendium/Extract|Lizal Tri-Boomerang}}
{{Plural|BotW|Lizal Tri-Boomerang}} are {{Plural|BotW|Boomerang|link}} with three blades that are typically carried by {{Plural|BotW|Black Lizalfos|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are also used by {{Term|BotW|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Golden Lizalfos|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}}. They can be thrown to attack {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} at a range, but they must be caught before returning to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} or else he will take damage. Alternatively, they can be used as melee {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Boomerang}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are commonly found in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}.

As metallic {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|}}, {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Tri-Boomerang}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, but they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
