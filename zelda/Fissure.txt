{{Term/Store|Fissure|Fissures|Series, ALBW|plural}} are cracks on walls that serve as the means to traverse between [[Lorule]] and [[Hyrule]] in {{ALBW}}.{{Fact}} They appear as a bizarre crack-shaped pattern on walls or flat surfaces. In Hyrule, they seem to release the dark smoke, while in Lorule they seem to emit light. Their icon will appear on the map when Link travels through them.

==History==
Fissures are first mentioned by [[Hilda]] after [[Link]] is awakened. According to Hilda, Fissures appeared due to the actions of [[Yuga]], and now link both worlds.<ref>{{Cite|Can you hear me, Hero of Hyrule? It is I, Princess Hilda. I wanted to warn you about the kingdom in which you've found yourself. My Lorule may remind you of your own home. But, in fact, our kingdoms are as different as night and day. Worlds apart, as they say. But Yuga's scheme has forced our two kingdoms close together. So close, they are now connected.|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref> However, Hilda later mentions that there was a single crack in a large stone slate found within Lorule's [[Sacred Realm]] which connected both worlds even before Yuga's actions. She later states that Yuga was the one which discovered this Fissure.<ref>{{Cite|Yuga discovered that there was a strange crack in this grim slate... Through it, we could sense that there was another world beyond ours... a place where the Triforce still existed.|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref>

Often, when Link first emerges into Lorule from certain Fissures located in Hyrule, Hilda will speak to the young hero telepathically and warn him of the dangers that plague the area.
