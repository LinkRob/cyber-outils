{{Term/Store|Dark Ore||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|ST|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Found only in the {{Term|ST|Dark Ore Mine|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Fire Realm|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Dark Ore}} costs 200 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}} for ten pieces.<ref>{{Cite|Dark Ore is 200 Rupees. Want to buy some?|Goron|ST}}</ref> The ore is said to shine in the dark, but begins to disappear at a fast rate when exposed to sunlight. As such, {{Term|ST|Link|link}} must quickly transport it aboard the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} to its destination before it completely disappears.<ref>{{Cite|The Dark Ore we mine here disappears when exposed to sunlight. So we work all day in the dark like this. I so need to go out and get some sun!|Goron|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, kid! You ever heard of Dark Ore? It shines in the dark and melts if sunlight hits it! Strange, eh?|Linebeck III|ST}}</ref>

{{Term|ST|Link}} can purchase ten pieces of {{Term|ST|Dark Ore}} and take them to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}} at the {{Term|ST|Trading Post|link}}. If {{Term|ST|Link}} can successfully bring at least five of these pieces to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|display=Linebeck}}, he will reward the young hero with a {{Term|ST|Force Gem|link}}, which will energize new tracks in the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm|link}} allowing him to gain access to the {{Term|ST|Lost at Sea Station|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wh-what's this? You actually brought me some Dark Ore?! Hyah hah hah! Well, fork it
over then! ...You are going to give it to me, right?|Linebeck III|ST}}</ref> To transport the {{Term|ST|Dark Ore}} quickly to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}, {{Term|ST|Link}} must travel through the cave on the east side of the {{Term|ST|Fire Realm}}, where a large {{Term|ST|Rocktite|link}} and its small children lay in wait. If these monsters are able to attack the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}, some of the {{Term|ST|Dark Ore}} will be lost, rendering {{Term|ST|Link}} unable to fulfill {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|display=Linebeck}}'s request, though he will still be rewarded with 100 {{Plural|ST|Rupee}} for the remaining pieces.

Later, if {{Term|ST|Link}} brings another five or more pieces of {{Term|ST|Dark Ore}} to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}, he will be rewarded with a valuable {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}.

==Trivia==
* {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}'s use for {{Term|ST|Dark Ore}} is never explained in the game.
