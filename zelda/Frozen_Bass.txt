==Location and Uses==
Frozen Bass are made when either a [[Hyrule Bass]] or a [[Staminoka Bass]] are frozen by [[Ice]]. When eaten, it will restore two {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. It will also grant him a minute of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"/>
