== Trivia ==

* The Beaver Bros. can be seen as a parallel to the Mario Bros. from the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise, due to their physical proportions. However, [[Mario]] is the older twin brother while [[Luigi]] is younger.
* His name (and his older brother's, [[Big Brother Beaver|Twig]]) were first mentioned by [https://nintendo.fandom.com/wiki/Satomi_Asakawa Satomi Maekawa], a character designer at the time, in The 64 Dream magazine issue #46 from 2000.<ref name=":0" />
