==Biography==
Alora lives in a tall house in the area surrounding [[Toyku Lighthouse]]. Her house was invaded by monsters, leaving it ruined and forcing her to hide beneath a flight of stairs in the basement. She asks [[Link]] to retrieve her stolen [[Necklace]], which was taken from her by a [[Gleeok]] in [[Spearfish Falls]]. After Link defeats the Gleeok and returns her Necklace, she rewards him with a big kiss and a [[Canteen]].

She appears to unnerve Link slightly, likely due to her quasi-romantic advances. She mentions that she is or was formerly married, but claims that her husband was turned into an [[Abominom]].<ref>{{Cite|My husband gave me this. He's an Abominom now.|Alora|TFoE}}</ref> Her Necklace was a gift from her husband.
