The "{{Term/Store|Frog's Song of Soul||Series, LA, LADX}}" is a [[Song]] in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 96">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Frog's Song of Soul}} was referred to as the '''Frog Song of Soul''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Frog Song of Soul}}<br/>While adventuring on the island of Koholint, Link learned to play the Frog Song of Soul from Mamu the frog. When played, its magical power was strong enough to resurrect the dead.|retrieved= January 22, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160307135902/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/f.jsp#FrogSongofSoul|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It has the ability to resurrect the dead or awaken the asleep.<ref name="E 96"/>

==Location and uses==
Link can learn the song from [[Mamu]] and his [[Frog]] choir by completing the puzzle in [[Signpost Maze]] after obtaining the [[Hookshot]] in [[Catfish's Maw]].<ref>{{Cite|'BRRING! BRRING! Yeah, this is Ulrira! You are starting to like music, eh? Well, a frog named Mamu, who lives in the Signpost Maze, might know some new songs, but he charges a lot to play them! Bye! CLICK!'|Grandpa Ulrira|LADX}}</ref> However, according to Mamu, the song is a previously unreleased cut, and Link will have to pay 300 [[Rupee]]s in order to listen to it.<ref>{{Cite|Ribbit! Ribbit! I'm Mamu, on vocals! But I don't need to tell you that, do I? Everybody knows me! Want to hang out and listen to us jam? For 300 Rupees, we'll let you listen to a previously unreleased cut! What do you do?|Mamu|LADX}}</ref> Its main purpose is to revive the [[Flying Rooster]] whose skeleton lies under the [[Weathercock]] in [[Mabe Village]],<ref>{{Cite|Here Sleeps The Flying Rooster|Weathercock|LADX}}</ref> granting Link access to the majority of the [[Tal Tal Mountain Range]] and [[Eagle's Tower]]. Later on during his quest, Link must play the song once more in order to awaken [[Turtle Rock (Boss)|Turtle Rock]], subsequently opening the entrance to [[Turtle Rock|the dungeon of the same name]].<ref>{{Cite|'BRRING! BRRING! Hi, it's Ulrira! The head of the turtle is in your way? Put life into it and it will move! It's true! True! Bye! CLICK!'|Grandpa Ulrira|LADX}}</ref>
