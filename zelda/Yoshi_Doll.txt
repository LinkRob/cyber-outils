The {{Term/Store|Yoshi Doll||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an [[item]] in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|LANS Pink|Yoshi doll}}! You can't count how many games you've seen him in now!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
{{Manual|LA|Yoshi Doll|This small doll is a miniature image of the most popular character on Koholint Island. Everyone wants a Yoshi Doll! Where on earth are you going to get hold of this doll?! And once you have one, what can you do with it?}}
The Yoshi Doll is the first item in the game's [[Trading Quest]]. It is a toy created in the likeness of [[Yoshi]] from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. The Yoshi Doll is won as a prize from the [[Trendy Game]] in [[Mabe Village]], wherein [[Link]] must operate the game's claw and grab the doll. Unlike the other item prizes in the Trendy Game Shop, the Yoshi Doll sits in the middle of the platform off of the conveyor belt.

The Yoshi Doll can be given to [[Mamasha]] in Mabe Village, who asks if she can give it to her baby.<ref>{{Cite|Because they all look alike, even I am sometimes confused. . . By the way, my baby wants a Yoshi Doll. I saw one at the Trendy Game, but I couldn't get it.|Quadruplets' Mother|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! Will you give that doll to my baby?!|Quadruplets' Mother|LADX}}</ref> In exchange, Link is given the {{Term|LANS|Ribbon|link}}, the next item in the Trading Sequence.

In {{LA|-}} and {{LA|DX|-}}, the text for obtaining the Yoshi Doll is "Recently, he seems to be showing up in many games!", reflecting how Yoshi had begun to appear in several ''Mario'' games since debuting in ''[[w:c:mario:Super Mario World|Super Mario World]]''. Several years later, with the release of the [[Nintendo Switch]] remake, the text reads, "You can't count how many games you've seen him in now!" since Yoshi had appeared in a very large variety of ''Mario'' and ''[[w:c:mario:Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' games by that point.
