"{{Term/Store|The More the Merrier||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: The More the Merrier}}<br/>01/22/2019 |author= Nintendo |published= January 22, 2019 |retrieved= January 25, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/c60c8f4a/index.html |title= Event: The More the Merrier - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|The More the Merrier}}" is an event wherein a greater number of {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} featuring two or more characters randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Multiple-character spirits have ganged up on the Spirit Board! Defeat them to receive more snacks than usual.<br/><br/>Legend-class spirits Donkey Kong & Lady, Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres, as well as Ace-class spirit Octoling Girl and Octoling Boy will appear for a fixed time. Good luck! |author= Nintendo |published= January 22, 2019 |retrieved= January 25, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/c60c8f4a/index.html |title= Event: The More the Merrier - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
