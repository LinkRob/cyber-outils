{{Plural|BotW|Eightfold Longblade}} are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} passed down through the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} tribe.<ref name="Inventory"/> This makes them part of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 170}}</ref> They have a sharp edge that makes them ideal for slicing.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly found in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}, particularly around {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. They also have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} spawned after scanning the {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Sheik|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

Like the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}}-made {{Term|BotW|Windcleaver|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Eightfold Longblade}} has a charged attack distinct from other two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}. Rather than spinning, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will draw the {{Term|BotW|Eightfold Longblade|display=Longblade}} back before slashing forwards with considerable speed, knocking large {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} over and sending smaller ones flying. Even if the weapon does not strike something directly, it will lose [[Durability]] from releasing the projectile.

Since they are made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Eightfold Longblade}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, and they will sink if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Finally, they will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Eightfold Longblades reappear in ''Tears of the Kingdom'', being wielded by [[Yiga Blademaster]]s in battle, though Link can obtain them if he defeats them. Like other weapons in the game, they have visible decay from [[Ganon|Ganondorf]]'s [[Gloom]], and can be [[Fuse|fused]] with other weapons and materials to enhance their potential. The weapon can unleash a ranged wind slash if Link has a high amount of {{Term|TotK|Life|link|display=health}} remaining. If fused with an elemental item, the weapon can unleash elemental projectiles. An intact Eightfold Longblade may be found in {{Term|TotK|The Depths|link}}.
