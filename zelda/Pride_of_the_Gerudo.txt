==Overview==
{{Term|TotK|The Missing Owner|link|display=As thanks for saving her}}, Isha disclosed that she is the only known Gerudo who can craft the {{Term|TotK|Arms of the Seven|link}}: {{Term|TotK|Scimitar of the Seven|link}} and {{Term|TotK|Daybreaker|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They are a sword and shield passed down by the Gerudo, and they can only be wielded by a chosen individual. I'm talking about the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Scimitar of the Seven</font> and the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Daybreaker</font>, of course. They're crafted using a special technique known only to me.|Isha|TotK}}</ref> A set of gear only wielded by chosen individuals and Isha wishes to impart them to {{Term|TotK|Link|link}}. To make these, she will need four {{Plural|TotK|diamond|link}}, ten {{Plural|TotK|flint|link}}, a {{Term|TotK|Gerudo Scimitar|link}}, and a {{Term|TotK|Gerudo Shield|link}}.

After Link bring all these materials to Isha, she created the Arms of the Seven and gave them to Link.
