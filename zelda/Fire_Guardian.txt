{{Term/Store|Fire Guardian|Fire Guardians|Series, HWAoC|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
Fire Guardians are Guardians enhanced with fire. Their attacks are as follows:

* Firing a laser
* Swinging one of their legs to scratch its enemies
* Jumping up, then slamming down for a fiery explosion
* Spinning their legs, then charging forwards
* Rearing back, with two feet up, then slamming them down, causing small explosions around itself
* Rearing back while charging, with two feet up, then slamming them down, firing a fireball
