{{Term/Store|Jules||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']].<ref name=":0">{{Cite|N/A|Jules|BoTW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|N/A|Jules|ToTK}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Jules}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} girl who can be found in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Jules}} visits many locations in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}} throughout the day, such as outside of the {{Term|BotW|Jewelry Shop|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has not yet completed the "{{Term|BotW|Tools of the Trade|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Jules}} comments that she does not see any accessories on display and questions if the {{Term|BotW|Jewelry Shop|display=Shop}} is open or not.<ref name=":1">{{Cite|{{Small|Huh? This is an accessory shop, isn't it? I don't see any accessories on display. Are they not open yet?}}|Jules|BotW}}</ref> If the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed, {{Term|BotW|Jules}} stands outside and decides that it is not her time to own jewelry, as she lacks the {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} needed and outright buying it from the store is expensive.<ref name=":2">{{Cite|{{Small|The accessories here sure are pretty... But I don't have any of the materials to trade, and buying one outright would be so expensive... I guess it's just not time for me to own one...}}|Jules|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Jules}} while she is standing at {{Term|BotW|Ardin|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stall|link}}, which sells a variety of {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Jules}} will contemplate whether she should buy some {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}} or some {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}}.<ref name=":3">{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Blue|Chillshrooms}} are great, but the nights here are so cold... Maybe I should get some {{Color|BotW Blue|sunshrooms}} instead...}}|Jules|BotW}}</ref> When the {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} is sold out, she buys into {{Term|BotW|Ardin}}'s lie that she is selling {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom}} that only beautiful people can see.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Mushrooms that only beautiful people can see... I think...maybe I'm starting to see something?}}|Jules|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her while she is in the center of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}} or in {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}}, she will ask if {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} are the clothes sold at {{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion|link}} and mentions that she was thinking about buying some herself.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, aren't those clothes sold at Fashion Passion? I was thinking about buying some myself...|Jules|BotW}}</ref> She also asks if the clothes are comfy.<ref>{{Cite|How do they feel? Comfy?|Jules|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Jules}} is suspicious that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a boy and asks if he is secretly in disguise.<ref>{{Cite|Say, are you, uhh... I really don't know how to say it... Might you be... {{Small|A boy?}}|Jules|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will react in surprise after having his cover blown, confirming her suspicions. She compliments {{Term|BotW|Link}} on his ability to get into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} without anyone noticing and assures him that she will keep the secret between them.<ref name="Vai"/> {{Term|BotW|Jules}} considers learning the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)|link}} language while she is in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} and again compliments {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he makes a convincing girl.<ref>{{Cite|Might as well learn as much of the Gerudo language as I can while I'm here, right? {{Small|Hehe, I have to say... You really make a convincing girl... I know your secret, and I'm still fooled every time you walk up!}}|Jules|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Jules}} while she is in {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}}, she expresses how refreshing her drink is.<ref>{{Cite|Ahhhh! This really hits the spot! Nothing like a cool drink to take some of the edge off the heat.|Jules|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Jules}} explains that all of the drinks made at {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}} are made from the {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|link}}, which is the reason it is possible to have cold drinks in the middle of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|All of the drinks here are made using ice from the nearby {{Color|BotW Blue|icehouse}}. That's why we're able to have ice-cold drinks, even in the middle of the desert!|Jules|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Jules}} comments that her drink is the {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit|link}}, which is the signature product of the {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|display=Canteen}}.<ref>{{Cite|The name of this drink is {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}. It's the signature product of this store.|Jules|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|display=Icehouse}}, {{Term|BotW|Jules}} explains that it is an underground {{Term|BotW|Ice}} depository that harvests its supply from the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and stores it.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|icehouse}} is an ice depository that was made by digging a hole in the ground. It sounds like they cut ice from the nearby {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Highlands}}, take it there, and then store it.|Jules|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks where it is, she tells him to go past the ruins to the north of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} and he will find it.<ref>{{Cite|Go out the main gate and head left. First you'll see some ruins... Just go past them and you'll see the {{Color|BotW Blue|icehouse}}.|Jules|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} warns {{Term|BotW|Jules}} not to drink too much, she assures him that she is fine and is only going to have one more drink.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I'm fine! I'm only gonna have one more.|Jules|BotW}}</ref> Before the conversation ends, {{Term|BotW|Jules}} struggles to remember the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)|display=Gerudo}} word for "good-bye" until finally recalling that it is "sav'orq."<ref>{{Cite|Oh, darn... What was it... Good-bye... Good-bye... Sav... Oh! Sav'orq!|Jules|BotW}}</ref>

In ''Tears of the Kingdom'', Jules now works as an assistant at the [[Hotel Oasis]].<ref>{{Cite|Originally I was just another customer coming here to relax, but I got hooked... Now I work here as an assistant, ha!|Jules|ToTK}}</ref> She praises [[Romah]]'s relaxation techniques<ref>{{Cite|The owner of Hotel Oasis, {{Color|BotW Red|Romah}}, is known by vai the world over! Her special relaxation technique can not only improve the condition of your skin... but it is known to {{Color|BotW Red|awaken dormant powers}} inside you for a time, as well!|Jules|ToTK}}</ref>, and also recommends [[Olu]]'s spa plan, although she comments that it's less reliable.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Olu}} is apprenticing under {{Color|BotW Red|Romah}}. She's the only vai who has ever had the opportunity to inherit Romah's special technique. But... she's still learning. The effects of her spa plan are a bit less reliable, you might say.|Jules|ToTK}}</ref>
