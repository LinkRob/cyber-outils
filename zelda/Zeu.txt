'''Zeu''' is a character in the {{TP|Himekawa}}.{{Name Ref}}

==Biography==

[[File:Boys sparring.png|200px]]

Before the events of the [[manga]] took place, Zeu lived in a city that guarded the border of a [[Gerudo_Desert|desert]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= My hometown was close to the desert-A city that guarded the border.|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 139}}</ref> He would practice sword fighting with the other boys to defend [[Hyrule]]'s borders.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= When we grow up, we'll defend Hyrule's borders!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 139}}</ref><ref name="Boy"/>

As his friends [[Link]] and [[Rioma]] train, he walks up to them and says they should stop for the day.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Let's stop for the day!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 140}}</ref> He laughs as Rioma complains about Link being difficult to fight because he is left handed,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= It's hard to fight Link! He's left handed!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 140}}</ref> the other youth stating he would do the same right handed against him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I'd do the same right handed against you, Rioma!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 140}}</ref> 

Zeu is shown again when [[Darpa]] takes he, Link, and Rioma to see the [[Gaurof Sword]]. Zeu watches as Darpa ignores Rioma's reminder that they have been told not to go near the sword by parents and teachers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Let's not do this, Darpa. We've been told not to go near that since we were kids. <small>By parents and teachers...</small>|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 141}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= If you're scared of getting in trouble, go back!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 141}}</ref> While Darpa's efforts to pull the Gaurof Sword out of its stone are proved to be in vain,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Umph! Umph! Rrgh! I can't pull it out!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 142}}</ref> Zeu insults him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You suck!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 142}}</ref> Zeu observes as Link tries next.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Tsk! Hey, Link! Give it a try!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 142}}</ref> Link succeeds, but he and the other boys, along with the entire city, are pulled into the stone with Link as the only survivor.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Darpa! Rioma! Zeu! It's pulling us in!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 145}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... Everyone...Got sucked into the darkness. Why did I alone survive? I don't know!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 149}}</ref>

In the second volume of the manga, Zeu, Darpa, and Rioma are shown when [[Wolf Link]] has a flashback about his younger days with his friends, feeling remorse that he and the others were not able to protect [[Princess Zelda]] as they trained for.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= "In the future, we'll defend Hyrule's borders!"|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 79}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Back home...I went through tough training so I'd be able to fight for my country in a crisis... We all...Believed that. But now the threat is real. I was supposed to protect her! But our home has disappeared... Our very lives have disappeared...And I stand before Princess Zelda not as a person...But as a beast!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 79}}</ref>
