==Overview==
==={{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}===
{{Plural|OoS|Ring Fortune}} can be performed by the {{Term|OoS|Blue Snake|link}} at {{Term|OoS|Ring Shop|link}}. By connecting {{OoA}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Seasons]]'' with a Game Link cable, then simultaneously speaking to the Blue Snake in both games, a Ring Fortune is performed. Ring Fortunes give both games a new Magic Ring to be appraised, as a sign of "friendship".<ref name="LinkBasics"/>

No rings can be obtained exclusively through this method, therefore Ring Fortunes are not required to obtain all the Magic Rings. Ring Fortunes can be performed with any two {{OoA|-}} and {{OoS|-}} game files, although they cannot be performed with two identical games (two ''Ages'' or two ''Seasons).
