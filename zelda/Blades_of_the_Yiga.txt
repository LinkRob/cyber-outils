{{Term/Store|Blades of the Yiga||Series, BotW}} is the seventh {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Blades of the Yiga}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #7}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link}} discovers this {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory}} at {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. Within, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} is attacked by several {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} before {{Term|BotW|Link}} jumps in to rescue her from them. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} guards her from the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}} attackers, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} stares up at him with awe.
