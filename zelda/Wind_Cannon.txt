'''Wind Cannon''' are objects from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Wind Cannon|An arcane device that continually blows a strong wind straight ahead that can disperse sand or extinguish flames.<ref name="Wind Cannon">{{Cite|An arcane device that continually blows a strong wind straight ahead that can disperse sand or extinguish flames.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Wind Cannons are objects found in caves near the [[Gerudo Desert]]. They create gusts of wind that can blow away sand, extinguish flames, or push enemies and objects. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], allowing her to summon them for two [[Echo]] energy.
