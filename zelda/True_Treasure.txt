==Overview==
{{Incomplete}}
{{Term|TotK|Link|link}} finds Kodah at <MISSING> inn where she disclosed that Sasan and Finley found an ancient text about a "shrine" at Tarm Point. Under the impression there's treasure, Finley set out for Tarm Point and Sasan followed her. Kodah is worried about the two and asked Link to check on them there.

Upon reaching Tarm Point, Link finds a hidden cave. Entering, he found Sasan. He said that Finley went ahead to look for treasure but Sasan was left behind because he can't swim. There is a raft he could use but the cave's water level fluctuate rapidly and has trouble navigating the rocks.
