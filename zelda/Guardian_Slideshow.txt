"{{Term/Store|Guardian Slideshow||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Guardian Slideshow}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Puffer Beach}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Puffer Beach|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will meet a woman named {{Term|BotW|Loone|link}} caressing an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} that she has named {{Term|BotW|Roscoe|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your skin is so smooth and gorgeous, my little Roscoe...|Loone|BotW}}</ref> When she notices {{Term|BotW|Link}} watching the two of them, she calls {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}} shy for wanting to hide away.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Someone's watching us? Oh, my sweet Roscoe! You're so shy, aren't you? YES, YOU ARE! Why not proudly show off your beaming beauty, my darling? You really MUST come out of your shell.|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Interpreting that {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}} suspects {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants something, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} speculates that he may be after her {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? The person watching looks like he wants something? Maybe he's after... *GASP* Is he after you?!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> She frantically assures {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}} that she would never let it go, as she is in love with it.<ref>{{Cite|Don't you worry, my sweet Roscoe! I would never ever EVER let you go! I'm head over heels for my special friend who's been around since ancient times! You're so ancient and special, Roscoe! YES, YOU ARE!!|Loone|BotW}}</ref>

However, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} quietly admits that her heart actually belongs to {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Sorry, my sweet Roscoe, but I fibbed a little... The truth is...}} {{Big|I wanna see Guardians!}}|Loone|BotW}}</ref> In particular, she wants to see flying {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}, small {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} that walk around, all of which must be active.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Flying ones}}! {{Color|BotW Red|Small ones}}! {{Color|BotW Red|Ones that walk around}}! I wanna see all the Guardians in all the world!! Not the ones around here that have rusted away, either. I wanna see {{Big|fresh and lively Guardians!}}|Loone|BotW}}</ref> In the scenario that she would see these {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} confesses that she may abandon {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I ever do get to see them... I may just have to leave you, my friend.|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Due to how dangerous they are, though, she does not think this could ever happen.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, but don't you worry! Getting near a Guardian is too dangerous for me. {{Color|BotW Red|Flying ones}}, {{Color|BotW Red|small ones}}, {{Color|BotW Red|ones that walk}}... I'm afraid seeing Guardians like that is nothing more than a dream within a dream for me...|Loone|BotW}}</ref> When she finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

Using the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can take {{Plural|BotW|Picture|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}}. Back at {{Term|BotW|Puffer Beach}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can offer to show these {{Plural|BotW|Picture}} to {{Term|BotW|Loone}}, she will initially tell him not to bother her and {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll show them to you.] Hm? Someone's speaking? You'll...show me? A Guardian? Hey, you! Could you please not bother my Roscoe and me?|Loone|BotW}}</ref> She does not believe that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is has a way to show her anything.<ref>{{Cite|It's not like you can show me a Guardian or anything...|Loone|BotW}}</ref>

First, {{Term|BotW|Link}} presents the {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher}}, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} will feel amazed to see its beauty.<ref>{{Cite|That image... That's a flying Guardian! Wow, I feel alive with terror just from gazing at its beautiful form!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Next, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} sees the {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout}}, which she thinks is adorable.<ref>{{Cite|A small Guardian! Awww, it looks so vicious for its tiny size! Adorable!!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> After seeing both, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is about to show her the final {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} she wants to see.<ref>{{Cite|Hold on... If you've been able to show me two types, then that must mean...|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Seeing the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker}} {{Term|BotW|Picture}}, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} will consider it to be exhilaratingly terrifying.<ref>{{Cite|A walking Guardian! I can't believe it! Nothing could be more dangerous! What an exhilaratingly scary sight!|Loone|BotW}}</ref>

Satisfied with what she has seen, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} demands {{Term|BotW|Link}} to give her the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} so she can continue to look at the {{Plural|BotW|Picture}}.<ref>{{Cite|Phew... What a joy. Hey, you! Give me that box, OK?! I wanna look at those images every single day!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, she resigns herself to memorizing the {{Plural|BotW|Picture}} instead.<ref>{{Cite|[I can't do that.] You can't? I see... How unfortunate. I guess I'll just have to burn those images into my imagination.|Loone|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks for {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}} the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} allows him to have it, as she has gotten over her obsession.<ref>{{Cite|[Can I have that orb?] Hm? Oh, you mean Roscoe? Sure, that weird sphere thing is all yours. Really, I'm over it.|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Now, all she cares about is {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, I'm all about Guardians now! If I close my eyes, I can see them in my imagination! It's glorious!|Loone|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} will now be able to pick up the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} and place it into the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} nearby.<ref>{{Cite|You've decided to go place it in the {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}}.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> Once he does so, {{Term|BotW|Shoqa Tatone Shrine|link}} will rise from the sand, which will complete the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|When you placed the orb into the pedestal, an ancient shrine appeared!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
