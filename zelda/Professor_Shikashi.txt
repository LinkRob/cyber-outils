{{Term/Store|Professor Shikashi||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Professor Shikashi}} goes unnamed in {{OoT}}.

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
Professor Shikashi appears as an elderly man in {{OoT}}. He is first seen in the [[Market]], where he talks about the [[Sheikah]] when [[Link]] is a child.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard the legend of the "Shadow Folk"? They are the Sheikah...the shadows of the Hylians. They say they swore allegiance to the King of Hyrule and guarded the Royal Family. But with the long peace, no one has seen a Sheikah around here for a long time.|Old Man|OoT}}</ref> Seven years later, he moves to [[Kakariko Village]] following the destruction of Hyrule Castle Town. He talks about the [[House of Skulltula]] and the history of the [[Lens of Truth]] when Link is an adult.<ref>{{Cite|Folks around here tell of a fabulously rich family that once lived in one of the houses in this village... But they say that the entire family was cursed due to their greed! Who knows what might happen to those who are consumed by greed...|Old man|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A long time ago... There was a man in this very village who had an eye they said could see the truth! Now usually, you have to train your mind's eye most strenuously to actually see the truth... But this fella, no, they say he had a different way of doing things... His house stood where the well is now...|Old Man|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
Professor Shikashi is a wise old man who spends his time in the [[Astral Observatory]] in {{MM}}, looking at the stars. He opens his observatory and the path leading to it from [[Clock Town]] to the [[Bombers Secret Society of Justice|Bombers]], so that they may use it as their hideout.<ref>{{Cite|We Bombers have a hideout that leads to the observatory outside town.|Jim|MM}}</ref> Professor Shikashi is also the one who informs Link about the importance of the [[Moon's Tear]].<ref>{{Cite|That is one of the lunar rocks that has been blazing from the surface of the moon lately. They fall from what looks to be the moon's eye, so I call them Moon's Tears. They are rare stones, valued by many in town.|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref> Like many others in [[Termina]], Professor Shikashi also had a disruptive encounter with the [[Skull Kid]] before Link's arrival.<ref>{{Cite|Your manners seem much better than those of your mischievous friend from the other day. Hrnf, hrnf, hrnf! That ill-mannered troublemaker from the other day said he'd break my instruments...|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref> He is also very concerned about the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]]'s slow descent to Termina.<ref>{{Cite|But lately, there is something quite strange with the moon. Strange, indeed... It seems as though the moon is being pulled down by something and is slowly drawing closer.|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref>
