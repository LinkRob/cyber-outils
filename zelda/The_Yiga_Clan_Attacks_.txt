There are 8 Korok Seeds to be found throughout "The Yiga Clan Attacks!".

There are 8 Treasure Chests to be found throughout "The Yiga Clan Attacks!".

== Battlefield-Specific Materials ==
The Materials that may appear throughout "The Yiga Clan Attacks!" are Blue Nightshade, Mighty Carp, Mighty Thistle, Stamella Shroom, and Rugged Rhino Beetle.
