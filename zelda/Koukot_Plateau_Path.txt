The {{Term/Store|Koukot Plateau Path||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Sesami">{{Cite|We were all walking together when some monsters suddenly jumped us near the {{Color|BotW Blue|Koukot Plateau path}}. I took off running and didn't stop until I got here. I thought my friends were right behind me, but when I turned around, they were nowhere to be found.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau Path}} is a series of scaffolding found along the eastern side {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau|link}}. It hangs over the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Pass|link}}, and it is comprised of multiple levels that can be traversed using {{Plural|BotW|Ladder|link}}. On its northern side, it connects to {{Term|BotW|Mount Nabooru|link}}.

At {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can meet {{Term|BotW|Sesami|link}}, who claims he was separated from his friends while they were on the {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau Path}}.<ref name="Sesami"/> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to direct his friends to the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} if he encounters them,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, if you happen to run into any of my friends on the road, please let them know Sesami is waiting for them here.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> which begins the "{{Term|BotW|Missing in Action|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. On the {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau Path}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will encounter {{Term|BotW|Oliff|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Flaxel|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Canolo|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Palme|link}}, who must be rescued from the {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}} that are attacking them.<ref>{{Cite|Wh-what wonderful form! I've never seen anyone fight like that! My name is Oliff. I'm a decent fighter, but I was caught off guard while traveling with my friends... The monsters vastly outnumbered us, so I thought it unwise to challenge them. I hate to think what would've happened had you not shown up. By the Goddess, I'm grateful...|Oliff|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks for the assist, pal. That could've been the end of me. Boy, I'm gonna be sore tomorrow. Filthy bottom-feeders...|Flaxel|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wait... Am I...saved?|Canolo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Huh? I'm... I'm alive? But...the monsters... Did you get rid of them? That's incredible! YOU'RE incredible! You might even be braver than Sesami!|Palme|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} informs them of {{Term|BotW|Sesami}}'s whereabouts, they will leave the {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau Path}} to join him.<ref>{{Cite|[So...] Sesami is waiting for me? Ah, I'm glad to hear he's safe. I shall hurry off to the Gerudo Canyon Stable where he waits.|Oliff|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[So...] So Sesami was kicking his feet up in the stable while I was being tortured by those sorry sacks?! Oh, I'm going to go give him a piece of my mind, all right. A piece of my fist too!|Flaxel|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[So...] At least I'm safe now. No thanks to Sesami. As soon as the monsters showed up, he ran away as fast as he could and left us for dead. Who does that? Especially to a girl! He's at the Gerudo Canyon Stable? If he thinks I'm going to forgive him for this, he's got another thing coming!|Canolo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[So...] Sesami...is waiting at the stable? He didn't come to save me? But he said he'd mastered the martial arts. Did he...lie to me about that? Nah...this must be a misunderstanding.|Palme|BotW}}</ref>

Two {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found on the {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau Path}}. The first is located on its northern end, and it contains a bundle of five {{Plural|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}}. The second is found on the {{Term|BotW|Mount Nabooru}} side of the {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau Path|display=Path}}, and within, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.
