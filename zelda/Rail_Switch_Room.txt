==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|OoS}}
The {{Term|OoS|Rail Switch Room}} is a room in the {{Term|OoS|Dancing Dragon Dungeon|link}}. It contains four {{Plural|OoS|Keese|link}}, a {{Term|OoS|Rail Switch|link}}, and a switchable {{Term|OoS|Mine Cart|link}} track. The northwest and southeast corners of the {{Term|OoS|Rail Switch Room|display=Room}} are submerged in {{Term|OoS|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
