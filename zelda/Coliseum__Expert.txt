==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Coliseum: Expert}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given six minutes to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Hinox}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Black Hinox}} spawns first, and after it is killed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Barbarian Armor|link}}.
