The {{Term/Store|Eastern Hills||Series, TMC}} are an area in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271{{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills}} are located in eastern {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. They connect to three other areas: {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}} to the north, {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}} to the east, and {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} to the west.

There is a cave here that can be blasted open with a {{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}}, and houses a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}}. Also located in this area is the farm of {{Term|TMC|Eenie|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Meenie|link}}, both of whom {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can [[Kinstone Fusion|fuse]] {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with.

In the lower left hand corner there is a {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}}. Fusing {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with this {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} causes a beanstalk to grow in the north of the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills}}, yielding {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell}} and a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}. There is also a hidden cave, in which a {{Term|TMC|Mysterious Wall|link}} awaits {{Term|TMC|Kinstone}} fusion. This fusion causes another {{Term|TMC|Goron|link}} to appear at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}.
