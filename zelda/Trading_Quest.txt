{{Term/Store|Trading Quest|Trading Quests|Series, LANS|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= THE TRADING QUEST|author= |published= |retrieved= September 21, 2019|url= https://www.zelda.com/links-awakening/koholint-island/|title= Explore Koholint Island, the setting of the Legend of Zelda: Link’s Awakening game.|site= Zelda.com|type= }}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Trading Sequence|Trading Sequences|MM|plural}},<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= MM|page= 33}}</ref> are recurring sidequests in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Overview==
By trading a long list of items, the player will eventually be rewarded with a useful item such as a better [[sword]], new tools or even numerous [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]]. However, due to their status as sidequests, they are not necessary to complete the game, except in the case of {{LA|-}}, where several items, including the final reward, are mandatory.

As Link tries to help a character, he may receive an item that becomes useful for someone else; when Link gives the newer item to the right character, he receives in turn another item that is destined to yet another character; a trade sequence takes form when this process is repeated continuously, thus resulting into a long string of exchanges that ultimately lead to a powerful item. For natural reasons, the traded items generally have one-time uses, and therefore one-time appearances.

==Trivia==
*The {{Plural|Series|Trading Quest}} were inspired by the Japanese Buddhist tale, "{{Wp|Straw Millionaire}}".<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= We had (Kensuke) Tanabe-san join early on. He thought up the sub-events and stuff like the "Straw Millionaire" parts. |author= Takashi Tezuka |published= n.d. |retrieved= January 24, 2020 |url= http://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/#/ds/zelda/1/1 |title= Iwata Asks : The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks : Kirby and Chomps in ... |site= Nintendo.com |type= }}</ref> "Straw Millionaire" is a tale about a poor man who becomes rich through trading various items.
