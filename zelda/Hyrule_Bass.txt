==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Stub|OoT}}

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Stub|TPHD}}
Hyrule Bass can be caught at the [[Fishing Hole]] and [[Eldin Spring]]. When caught on the hook they may leap out of the water in an attempt to break free.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hyrule Bass}}
In {{BotW}}, Hyrule Bass are commonly found in rivers and lakes all over Hyrule.<ref name="Inventory"/> [[Link]] can eat them raw to restore a [[Life Gauge|Heart]], or he can use them in cooking to gain more hearts. When used in cooking, they provide no effects to Link when eaten unless cooked with another [[Material]]. It becomes a [[Roasted Bass]] when cooked and a [[Frozen Bass]] when chilled. If a Hyrule Bass avoids getting caught it will eventually mature into a [[Staminoka Bass]].<ref name="Staminoka">{{Cite|{{Big|Staminoka Bass}} This Hyrule bass got to be the biggest fish by never getting caught (until now). Its long life results in a cooked dish that will restore a lot of stamina.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

Along with other fish, Hyrule Bass are able to be [[Cooking|cooked]] in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make a [[Fish Skewer]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], and [[Salt-Grilled Fish]]. They're also counted among the seafood that can be used to make [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Meunière]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]].

Five Hyrule Bass, as well as five {{Plural|Series|Lizalfos Talon|link}}, are needed to upgrade each piece of the [[Zora Set]] to its second tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
* Total for '''Zora Set''': 15 (0, 5, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
