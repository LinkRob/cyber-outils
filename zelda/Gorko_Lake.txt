{{Term/Store|Gorko Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gorko Lake|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Gorko Lake is a tiny lake of lava in [[Eldin Canyon]], located to the southeast of [[Goron City]]. The Lake is otherwise isolated with the exception of an inflow of lava that falls from the lava moat surrounding [[Death Mountain]].

The Lake has a small ledge above it, which is host to several [[Luminous Stone Deposit]]s. An [[Igneo Talus]] is buried in the ledge.
