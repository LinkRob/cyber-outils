{{Term/Store|Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer|Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewers are meals made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Raw Gourmet Meat]] with [[Goron Spice]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Eating them will restore some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Due to their rich aroma and the quality of their meats, these skewers are considered to be in a league of their own.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Additional ingredients can be added to Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewers, which could result in one of the following versions:

* '''Hearty Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. When eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. They refill a part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} when consumed.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. These skewers completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. Link receives a boost to his movement speed when he eats these.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] temporarily.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Link can eat them to temporarily boost his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. When eaten, Link's defense will be temporarily increased.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. They increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short while when eaten.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The rich aroma and juicy texture of this high-quality meat puts it in a league of its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding cold ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's [[Heat Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Chilly"/>
