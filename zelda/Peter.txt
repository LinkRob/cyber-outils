{{Name|Japanese|Peter}} is a character in {{RTBToL}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|RTBToL|Peter}} is a corn farmer located in {{Term|RTBToL|Page 3|link}}. He lives in a small farmhouse with his daughter, {{Term|RTBToL|Sensei|link}}, and a talking doll, {{Term|RTBToL|Corn Boy|link}}. His daughter is leaving home and is one of {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}}'s love interests. {{Term|RTBToL|Peter}}'s leg was injured when {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki|link}}'s spaceship crash-landed in his field, smashing into his tractor.

For {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} to move on to {{Term|RTBToL|Page 4|link}}, he must help {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki}} leave in her ship. He must do several things to complete this task, which includes replacing {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki}}'s Batteries and oiling her. After {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} helps {{Term|RTBToL|Peter}} out, he takes him, {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi|link}}, and {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki}} to {{Term|RTBToL|Page 4}}.
