{{Term/Store|Princess Styla||Series, TFH}} is the cursed daughter of {{Term|TFH|King Tuft|link}}, sovereign of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 305}}</ref>

== Biography ==
Prior to the events of the game, {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}} was a beautiful princess adored by all in the kingdom of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}}.<ref>{{Cite|Once upon a time there was a kingdom called Hytopia. At the heart of this fashion-obsessed land was the lovely Princess Styla. The people adored their fair princess... |N/A|TFH}}</ref> Although adored by many, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link|display=Lady Maud}}, the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}} witch, did not and despised her for her unbearably cute fashion sense.<ref>{{Cite|... but the Drablands witch despised the girl's unbearably cute fashion sense.|N/A|TFH}}</ref> The witch had sent her a beautifully wrapped gift, which {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}} could not resist opening.<ref>{{Cite|One fateful day, a beautifully wrapped gift arrived at the castle. The sender was unknown, and the box was brimming with an aura of mystery. The princess simply had to open it! Naturally, this was a HUGE mistake.|N/A|TFH}}</ref> Upon opening it, however, she was enveloped in purple smoke that cursed her to wear an irremovable, ugly jumpsuit.<ref>{{Cite|When the smoke cleared, Styla was met with a terrible sight, followed by the witch's voice in her pretty head: "A perfect outfit for a perfect princess." This cursed outfit of ultimate ugliness could not be removed no matter how the princess tried.|N/A|TFH}}</ref> Humiliated and miserable, the princess isolated herself by locking herself in her room and the rest of the kingdom wept for her fate.<ref name= "Humiliation"/> In response to her curse, the people of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}} grew afraid of fashion, in fear that they would be cursed next. In agony, her father {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} issued a request for worthy heroes who could go into the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}}, find the witch, and save his daughter.

{{Term|TFH|Link|link}} meets {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}} and {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} when the King summons him to his throne room after he clears the [[Drablands#Woodlands|Woodlands]] {{Term|TFH|Area}} of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}}. She is far too upset to talk to him, however, and soon retreats to her room. Although isolating herself, {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}} can occasionally be seen hiding around the town of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}}, secretly rooting for {{Term|TFH|Link}}. She quickly runs away once he spots her. The townsfolk however believe her to be a black ghost, and hope to take a picture of her.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Mr. Guard...<br/>Have you heard about a black ghost wandering around town lately?|Mrs. Pompadour|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Have you heard those rumors about the black ghost? I actually SAW it!<br/>Sadly, no one believes me. I'd take a picture by pressing {{Icon|TFH X}} and going to Miiverse, but...<br/>Well, it's too scary to go it alone! That's why I was just asking this kind guard to accompany me.|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref>

After the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} defeat {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}} and finds the last of her {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Link}} quickly returns to {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture|link}} to tailor the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble|link}}, an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can lift the curse off of {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now that you have some of The Lady's materials, it's time I showed you this little piece of work. I call it... The Lady's Ensemble! If you wear this and visit the princess, you should be able to lift her curse! All without defeating The Lady.|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> {{Term|TFH|Link}} hurries back into the castle wearing the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}, and uses its magic power to remove {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}}'s curse. Returned to her former, beautiful self, {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}} gives {{Term|TFH|Link}} her gratitude, and {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} holds a big ceremony to celebrate. After the festivities, {{Term|TFH|Link}} returns to {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}}'s throne room for his reward, only to be given {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}}'s {{Term|TFH|Cursed Tights|link}}. {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}} expresses that she misses the tights a little and how they had fit snug around her, but much prefers her old dresses. During the ceremony, the {{Term|TFH|Photo Bro|link}} had filmed the entire event and had given it as a gift to {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}}.<ref>{{Cite|You should know that the Photo Bro filmed the whole affair. He presented it to the princess as a present! If you ever wish to revel in that magnificent ceremony once more, go speak with the princess.|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> {{Term|TFH|Link}} can speak with her whenever he wishes to rewatch the ceremony. With {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}}'s curse lifted, she will no longer hide around the town of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}}.

== Physical Description ==
Styla is a very beautiful girl with pale skin, a small nose, large black eyes with blue reflections, and curly golden-blonde, leg-length hair, with two braided locks falling to her shoulders.

She wears a long princess dress with different shades of lilac, pale yellow and orange colors, she also wears long pale yellow gloves and has a large yellow flower on the right side of her head.

== Personality ==
Styla is a girl with a pure heart because she always watches over the welfare of her subjects. She also loves fashion and everything that is adorable.
