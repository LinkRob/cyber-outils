The {{Term/Store|Rota Ooh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Rota Ooh Shrine}}<br/>Passing of the Gates}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Passing of the Gates''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Rota Ooh Shrine}} is located on a hill to the west of {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rota Ooh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Rota Ooh. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Rota Ooh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} features a barred wall that is rotated by a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}} when hit. When the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} is hit, the wall rotates upward and returns to its original position when the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} is hit again. The wall also has a door that barricades the other half of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can reach the other side by navigating through a waterhole and using the {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, where an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} behind a cell can be found. On the wall is a rectangular basket that will empty any contents to the other side when rotated. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the basket and {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} in conjunction to transport the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} to the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. Doing so activates a {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}} that can be used to send {{Term|BotW|Link}} into the air.

Rotating the wall also lets {{Term|BotW|Link}} access to a narrow corridor to the left, where a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}} can be found. Aside from letting {{Term|BotW|Link}} reach the other side of the wall, opening the door also allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} be sent through it with the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}.

Upon unlocking the door and activating the {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can then propel himself upward over the top of the wall. However, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is incapable of reaching {{Term|BotW|Rota Ooh}}'s {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} while the wall is rotated, requiring the wall's position to be reset before landing atop it.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, {{Term|BotW|Rota Ooh}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Rota Ooh|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Rota Ooh}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Rota Ooh|BotW}}</ref>

A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Feathered Edge|link}} can be found atop a ledge at the back of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}.
