'''Feather''' is a spell in {{ZA}}.

==Overview==
The Feather spell launches a spread of feathers, which spread out as they travel. It costs 2 [[Rupee]] to use. The Feather Spell is given to [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] by [[Waldensop]] in the [[Forest of Torian]] as a token of his affection.

The Feather once belonged to [[Aviana]], who fears that her own power could be turned against her. As such, the Feather is the only Spell that can defeat her.
