==Overview==
Fireproof is a [[Set Bonus]] granted by the complete [[Flamebreaker Set]] after each piece has been enhanced at least twice by a [[Great Fairy]]. Fireproof functions similarly to [[Flame Guard]] with the added bonus of making Link totally impervious to flames, viscous lava formations, and fire-based attacks. Despite this, Link is still damaged by bodies of free-flowing lava, direct strikes from flaming enemies, and [[Magma Bomb]]s, though none of these will cause him to catch fire.
