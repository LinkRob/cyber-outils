{{Term/Store|Eyesoar||Series, OoA}} is the {{Term|OoA|Boss}} of {{Term|OoA|Skull Dungeon|link}}, the fourth {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 87}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|OoA|Eyesoar}} appears as a flying eyeball with four smaller eyes that orbit around it, similar to a {{Term|Series|Patra|link}}. As {{Term|OoA|Eyesoar}} gradually chases {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} around the room, its orbiting eyes will increase and decrease their radius to cover more ground. These smaller eyes can be defeated with several strikes from {{Term|OoA|Link}}'s {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}}, but will quickly regenerate. To harm this {{Term|OoA|Boss}}, {{Term|OoA|Link}} must use the {{Term|OoA|Switch Hook|link}} so that it penetrates its defenses and hits {{Term|OoA|Eyesoar}} directly. This will dizzy the {{Term|OoA|Boss}} and scatter the smaller eyes briefly, allowing {{Term|OoA|Link}} to strike {{Term|OoA|Eyesoar}} with his {{Term|OoA|Sword}} before they return to formation. After taking enough damage, {{Term|OoA|Eyesoar}} will be defeated, leaving behind a {{Term|OoA|Heart Container|link}} and opening the door to the {{Term|OoA|Burning Flame|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|OoA|Eyesoar}} shares its Japanese name with {{Term|Series|Patra|link}}, implying that they may be the same creature. Its English name is a play on the words ''soar'' and ''eyesore''.
