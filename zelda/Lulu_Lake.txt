{{Term/Store|Lulu Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lulu Lake'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Lulu Lake is a small lake in the [[Lanayru Great Spring]]. It is located east of [[Mikau Lake]] and is one of the shortcuts to [[Ploymus Mountain]].<ref>{{Cite|OK, Link. Let's get moving. The fastest way to {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}} is to ascend the {{Color|BotW Red|waterfall east of the domain}}! That Zora armor should come in handy. Just {{Color|BotW Red|swim to the waterfall basin}}, and then go up the waterfall from there! I'll wait at {{Color|BotW Blue|East Reservoir Lake}}, right by the Divine Beast. Gather {{Color|BotW Red|at least 20}} {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}, and then come join me!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> South of Lulu Lake is a [[Stone Monument]] depicting part of the [[History of the Zora]]. Lulu Lake is one of the locations that is affected by the constant [[Rain]] generated by {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}.

Lulu Lake is one of the places where one of the Trials for the "[[EX Champion Mipha's Song]]" [[Main Quest]] in [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC Pack takes place. In the Trial, Link must ascend the waterfall and pass through the [[Glowing Ring]]s leading through Lulu Lake to the base of Ploymus Mountain.<ref>{{Cite|The scattered One-Hit Obliterator led you to a location where a stone monument with a map of trials appeared. Kass's song seems related...<br/><br/>One, {{Color|BotW Red|find what the light's path shows}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|conquer ancient foes}}.<br/>Three, {{Color|BotW Red|chase rings of the waterfall}}.<br/>Champion, the trials call!<br/><br/>You still have {{Color|BotW Red|three}} trials to complete.|EX Champion Mipha's Song|BotW}}</ref>

In [[Master Mode]], a metal [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Silver Bow]] can be found atop a platform suspended by [[Sky Octorok]]s above the Lake.
