The '''High-Teku Baba''' is an [[Automaton]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|High-Teku Baba|This automaton has a big, clockwork appetite. It swallows up any monster it can reach in one gulp.<ref name="High-Teku Baba">{{Cite|This automaton has a big, clockwork appetite. It swallows up any monster it can reach in one gulp.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
High-Teku Babas are [[Baba]]-like Automatons created by [[Dampé]]. They are obtained as a reward for completing the [[Endless Stomach!]] [[side quest]], which requires the [[Steel Trap]] and [[Deku Baba]] echo to complete.
