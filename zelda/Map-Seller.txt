The {{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Map-Seller||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a character in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Map-Seller|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Map-Seller is an elderly woman whose husband has passed away.<ref>{{Cite|Here lies a Master Mapper. RIP.|Husband's Grave|FPTRR}}</ref><ref name="Master Mapper"/> Because of her husband's death, she took up the profession of map-making, but is having a difficult time creating maps because her hands shake. When she meets Tingle, she asks him for help drawing maps for her.<ref>{{Cite|You're just a passer-by but if you can help me, I'll reward you! Would you help me draw my map?|Map-Seller|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you, young man! As my hands shake, I can't draw properly. Here you are! The map of the Hometown Prairie!|Map-Seller|FPTRR}}</ref> After Tingle accepts, the Map-Seller asks him to draw her husband's grave on the map to make it current.<ref>{{Cite|This map is not yet complete. That grave between the trees hasn't been drawn on the map yet. If you'd be so kind as to draw it on the map for me...|Map-Seller|FPTRR}}</ref> Afterwards, the Map-Seller offers to reward Tingle for drawing landmarks on the map.<ref>{{Cite|Show me! I say, young man! This is wonderful! You've got potential! If you find anything else that isn't yet mapped please draw it on the map! If you bring it to my shop in the town, I'll reward you handsomely! Good luck!|Map-Seller|FPTRR}}</ref>

The Map-Seller can later be seen at her shop, where she tells Tingle that she does not have any maps for sale and asks him to keep making maps so that she can sell them.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, it's you! Thank you for your help drawing that map... This is my little shop. Well, I say shop... There are no maps for sale... If you would be kind enough to keep making maps for me... I'll be able to get this shop up and running, just like the old days. Naturally, I'll reward your hard work with rupees. Good luck, young man!|Map-Seller|FPTRR}}</ref> Once Tingle completes the map for Hometown Prairie, she thanks him and buys the map back for 100 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|I say, young man! This is remarkable! The map is finished for Hometown Prairie! Now I have a map to sell in my shop! It's almost like a real map shop again! I'm sure my husband is smiling up in heaven! Now I'm going to buy back the map of Hometown Prairie. Here are 100 rupees! Come back anytime!|Map-Seller|FPTRR}}</ref> Afterwards, she declares that her map shop is now open for business.<ref>{{Cite|The map shop is open for business! Oh, it feels so good to say that! get your maps here! Maps, anyone?|Map-Seller|FPTRR}}</ref> From Sunshine Seashore onwards, Tingle can purchase the incomplete maps for each area from the [[Look-What-I-Found Kid]], enabling him to map more locations and claim more rewards from the Map-Seller.

After Tingle maps every location and buys every map back from the Map-Seller, she passes away, thanking Tingle for his services. She also gives him the Master Pen, one of the [[Rupee Goods]].
