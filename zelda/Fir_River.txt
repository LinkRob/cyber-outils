{{Term/Store|Fir River||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Fir River'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Fir River is located in [[East Necluda]]. The Fir River is fed by [[Lake Jarrah]] and flows into the [[Squabble River]]. Along its banks, Link can find several [[Ore Deposit]]s. A cluster of boulders has landed over the body of the Fir River in one segment, and though this doesn't inhibit the flow of the River, it does prevent Link from sailing down the River with the Raft from Lake Jarrah.

The Fir River runs beside the [[Equestrian Riding Course]] and eventually cuts into the [[Cliffs of Quince]], where it becomes the [[Squabble River]].
