The {{Term/Store|Graveyard Key||Series, OoA, TMC}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126 {{Exp Game|OoA, TMC}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoA|-}}===
The {{Term|OoA|Graveyard Key}} is found in a secret cave in the southern part of {{Term|OoA|Yoll Graveyard|link}} in {{OoA}}. It is needed to open a gate in the northern part of the {{Term|OoA|Yoll Graveyard|display=Graveyard}} to access the eastern part and reach the {{Term|OoA|Spirit's Grave|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Dampé|link}} the gravedigger gives the {{Term|TMC|Graveyard Key}} to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} while he is traveling through the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|My name's Dampé. I'm the {{Color|TMC Green|gravedigger}} here. What can I do for you? What's that? You say you were called here by the ghost of a king? That'd be the ancient king of Hyrule who rests here. I've seen him myself. Well, if the king has called you, I can't see any reason not to let you in... Here, take this {{Color|TMC Red|graveyard key}}.|Dampé|TMC}}</ref> As {{Term|TMC|Link}} steps out of {{Term|TMC|Dampé}}'s house, a {{Term|TMC|Takkuri|link}} steals it. By knocking the {{Term|TMC|Graveyard Key|display=Key}} loose from a [[Tree]] in the same area, {{Term|TMC|Link}} is able to reacquire it and open the locked gate.
