{{Name|Fan|Makar's Violin}} is an item featured in {{TWW}}.{{Name Ref|TWW}} As its name implies, it is a violin that belongs to [[Makar]]. Makar practices playing his Violin year round so that he is prepared for the annual [[Korok Ceremony]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm practicing a new song for next year's ceremony. And I need privacy! It isn't any fun if everyone knows in advance what song I'll play.|Makar|TWW}}</ref> Makar's Violin plays an important role in the game due to it being required to awaken Makar as a [[Sage]] as well as restoring the [[Master Sword]]'s [[Power to Repel Evil|true power]].

==History==
Makar received his Violin at birth as a gift from the [[Great Deku Tree]], likely foreseeing Makar's destiny.<ref>{{Cite|This instrument is the one the Great Deku Tree gave to me on the day of my birth.|Makar|TWW}}</ref>

[[File:TWW-WindSageSuccession.jpg|thumb|left|Fado and Makar playing the "Wind God's Aria" on their Violins]]
After [[Link]] meets [[Fado (The Wind Waker)|Fado]], the former Sage of Wind before [[Ganondorf]] murdered him, he informs the young hero that he must look for the one who carries the same sacred instrument as him, a Violin, and play the "[[Wind God's Aria]]", the melody that will awaken the new Sage of Wind.<ref>{{Cite|You wish to return the power to your blade, don't you? In that case, look for the one who has the same instrument that I hold, and teach him the song you just played. Once you do, I'm certain the holder of the instrument will feel the blood of the sages awaken within his veins.|Fado (The Wind Waker)|TWW}}</ref> The young hero then travels to the [[Forest Haven]], where he finds Makar practicing his Violin in a cavern hidden behind a waterfall. As instructed by Fado, Link conducts the "Wind God's Aria" as Makar plays along with his Violin, which causes Makar to realize his fate.<ref>{{Cite|This sacred song that you've conducted has awakened me... I know myself now! I am the sage of the Wind Temple...|Makar|TWW}}</ref> Makar then urges Link to take him to the [[Wind Temple]] to thus complete his duty as the Sage of Wind.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Link, please! You must take me with you to the Wind Temple.|Makar|TWW}}</ref>

Inside the Wind Temple, Makar's Violin plays an important role of opening up blocked doorways when Makar plays the "Wind God's Aria" in front of them.

After defeating [[Molgera]], Link conducts the Wind God's Aria for Makar as he plays the song on his Violin. As they play it, Fado temporarily appears next to Makar to play along. This restores the remaining power of the Master Sword, to which Makar takes notice and begins to cry with joy that he was able to help restore the Master Sword and please his ancestors.<ref>{{Cite|Link, I have fully restored the power to repel evil to your Master Sword...And my ancestors are most satisfied.|Makar|TWW}}</ref> Makar remains in the Wind Temple to assure the power to repel evil stays in the Master Sword, which is done so by continuing to play his Violin.

==Trivia==
*Makar plays his Violin in a similar fashion as a cello is played, but this is only because of his size.
*As an Easter Egg, two pictures appear on the back wall of the [[Milk Bar]], one of a harp and one of a Violin side-by-side in {{ALBW}}, seemingly a reference to the two instruments used by the [[Sage]]s in {{TWW}}.
