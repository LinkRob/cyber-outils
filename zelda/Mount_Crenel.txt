{{Term/Store|Mount Crenel||Series, TMC}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> also called '''Mt. Crenel''',<ref>{{Cite|Mt. Crenel|N/A|TMC}}</ref> is an area in {{TMC}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}} is a craggy, mountainous area located in northwestern {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. It connects to one other area: {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel's Base|link}} to the south.

===Inhabitants===
{{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}} is home to the {{Term|TMC|Mountain Minish|link}}, among whom is the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} metal smith {{Term|TMC|Melari|link}} and his seven apprentices. The {{Term|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy|link}} is also found here, providing {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} with a larger {{Term|TMC|Bomb Bag|link}}, as well as the reclusive {{Term|TMC|Crenel Hermit|link}}; both live high up on the {{Term|TMC|Crenel Wall|link}}. Lastly, one of the nine {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Grayblade|link}}, who teaches {{Term|TMC|Link}} the {{Term|TMC|Roll Attack|link}}, is also found by scaling the walls of {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}} on the southeastern side.

===Scaling the Mountain===
[[File:TMC Mount Crenel's Base 3.png|left|thumb|The base of {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}}, by some beanstalks]]
{{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}} employs the use of blue and green beanstalks to ascend further up the mountainside, the green of which cannot be grown with simple water, but must be supplied with {{Term|TMC|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water|link}} gathered from the natural hot spring at the base of the mountain. The {{Term|TMC|Grip Ring|link}}, found in a cave opened with {{Plural|TMC|Bomb|link}} and bought from the {{Term|TMC|Business Scrub|link}} within, is used extensively and is required to scale the vertical, rocky walls of {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}}. A unique feature of {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} is also introduced in this area, as {{Term|TMC|Link}} needs to cross cliffs via small {{Plural|TMC|Updraft|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}} can remove spiky obstacles and uncover enemies hidden beneath rocks. 

====Crenel Wall====
The most significant area of {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}} is the {{Term|TMC|Crenel Wall}} region, an enormous expanse of vertical rock face that can only be climbed with the aid of the {{Term|TMC|Grip Ring}}. With the {{Term|TMC|Grip Ring}} in hand, {{Term|TMC|Link}} will be able to reach the summit of the mountain via its only route; without it, {{Term|TMC|Link}} will not be able to reach the summit any other way. Like the climb to the apex of {{Term|OoT|Death Mountain|link}} in {{OoT}}, falling {{Plural|TMC|Boulder|link}} threaten any safe passage to the mountaintop.

===Areas of Interest===
{{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}} was once home to the {{Plural|TMC|Goron|link}} before their numbers began to dwindle. The mountain is full of {{Plural|TMC|Tektite|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Peahat|link}}, and falling {{Plural|TMC|Boulder}}, endangering any who venture the risky ascent to the summit. Near the top of the mountain is a human mine, which is the second {{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}} called the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}}. In this {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} receives the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} is {{Term|TMC|Gleerok|link}}, and upon his defeat {{Term|TMC|Link}} receives the {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link}}, the second of the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}}, and a full {{Term|TMC|Heart Container|link}}. This area is also notable for its many secrets, including {{Plural|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Fairy Fountain|link}}.
