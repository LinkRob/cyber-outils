{{Term/Store|Jogo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Jogo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Jogo}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} man visiting {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}} alongside his wife, {{Term|BotW|Juney|link}}, for their honeymoon.<ref name="Honeymoon"/> The two of them are staying at the {{Term|BotW|Swallow's Roost|link}}, where he can be found throughout the day. He wears a {{Term|BotW|Snowquill Tunic|link}} and a pair of {{Term|BotW|Snowquill Trousers|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Jogo}}, he asks if he has also come to stay in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|So are you staying in Rito Village too?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> He is relieved to meet someone else who appreciates the town, admitting that he appreciates its simplicity.<ref>{{Cite|I see. Nice to meet a fellow admirer. I always liked the simple nature of this village.|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> However, he regrets choosing it for his honeymoon, since {{Term|BotW|Juney}} has been complaining about the cold and lack of activities.<ref>{{Cite|Although, I'm starting to regret choosing it as my honeymoon destination. It's really cold here and there's not much to do, so my {{Color|BotW Blue|wife}} is complaining nonstop. She won't even talk to me right now.|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> He fears that she will divorce him if he is unable to make amends with her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, man. If this doesn't work, she's going to divorce me for sure!|Jogo|BotW}}</ref>

In order to make {{Term|BotW|Juney}} happy, {{Term|BotW|Jogo}} needs to make her {{Plural|BotW|Baked Apple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I need to make some {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apples}} to keep my wife happy...|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, he does not have the {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}} he would need to start a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But I don't have any {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}} to start a fire...|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> Once he finishes speaking, "{{Term|BotW|The Spark of Romance|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Jogo}} with some {{Term|BotW|Flint}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Jogo}} will be able to smell it.<ref>{{Cite|That unmistakable, slightly acrid scent... You have some {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}} on you, don't you?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would sell it to him.<ref name="One">{{Cite|Can you sell me {{Color|BotW Blue|a piece}}?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Jogo}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as payment.<ref>{{Cite|Th-thanks! As promised, here's your payment.|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> He is happy to be able to make {{Plural|BotW|Baked Apple}} for {{Term|BotW|Juney}}.<ref>{{Cite|A-all right! Now I can make some {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apples}}.|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> Since he will need to make more {{Plural|BotW|Baked Apple}} in the future, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring him {{Term|BotW|Flint}}.<ref>{{Cite|I still need to make more {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apples}} to keep my wife happy, so please bring me more {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}!|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can continue to sell {{Term|BotW|Flint}} to {{Term|BotW|Jogo}} so long as he has at least one in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory}}. Depending on how many pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} has, {{Term|BotW|Jogo}} will buy them in groups of one,<ref name="One"/> five,<ref>{{Cite|Can you sell me {{Color|BotW Blue|five}} of those?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> 10,<ref>{{Cite|Can you sell me {{Color|BotW Blue|10}} of those?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> 30,<ref>{{Cite|Can you sell me {{Color|BotW Blue|30}} of those?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> 50,<ref>{{Cite|Can you sell me {{Color|BotW Blue|50}} of those?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> or 100.<ref>{{Cite|Can you sell me {{Color|BotW Blue|100}} of those?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref>
