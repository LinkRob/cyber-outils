{{Term/Store|Skullfish|Skullfish|Series, MM, MM3D, TP, TPHD, TFH, HWL, HWDE, CoH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 203 {{Exp Game|MM, MM3D, TP, TPHD, TFH}}}}</ref>{{Fact}}<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Skullfish|Game Over|CoH}}</ref> They are skeletal fish that are similar in behavior to piranhas.

==Characteristics==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= Isn't that a {{Color|MM White|Skullfish}}?<br/> Look, don't ask me about everything, all right?
}}

{{Plural|MM|Skullfish}} live in the seas around {{Term|MM|Great Bay|link}}, as well as in the water just outside of the {{Term|MM|Goron Village|link}} after defeating {{Term|MM|Goht|link}} in {{MM}}. They spawn at certain intervals within the Bay and swim towards {{Term|MM|Link|link}}, biting him if he does not raise his defenses. {{Term|MM|Zora Link|link}}'s electrical shield attack can be used to defeat them. In {{MM3D}}, they can also be caught in the {{Term|MM3D|Ocean Fishing Hole|link}} by wearing the {{Term|MM3D|Captain's Hat|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Plural|TP|Skullfish}} appear in the {{Term|TP|Lakebed Temple|link}}, as well as several secret {{Plural|TP|Cave|link}} around {{Term|TP|Hyrule Field|link}} in {{TP}}. They can be caught with the {{Term|TP|Fishing Rod|link}}, and will even drop a {{Term|TP|Heart|link}} when released, although they are forbidden fish and will not be recorded in the {{Term|TP|Fish Journal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You caught a skullfish! It looks stronger than it is... This fish is forbidden, so it won't be added to your fish journal.|N/A|TP}}</ref>

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Skullfish}} are found exclusively in the {{Term|TFH|Water Temple|link}} in {{TFH}}. They viciously attack any {{Term|TFH|Link}} they find swimming near them, and will leap out of the water to get to {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} standing on raised platforms. To defeat them, they can be slashed with the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} while they leap over a {{Term|TFH|Link}}, or dredged from the water with the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|link}}. They can then be attacked as a normal enemy. In this appearance, they are green rather than bone-colored.

There is also a desert variant of this enemy called the {{Term|TFH|Sandfish|link}}, which instead inhabits the sand of [[The Dunes]], but behaves identically.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|L|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:HWDE Skullfish Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, {{Plural|HW|Skullfish}} appear as Weird-type {{Term|HW|Food|link}}. They are imbued with the Darkness {{Term|HWDE|Element|link}}. When consumed by a {{Term|HW|Companion Fairy|link}} in the {{Term|HW|Dining Room|link}} of the {{Term|HW|My Fairy|link}} mode, {{Plural|HW|Skullfish}} can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.
{{Noncanon|End}}
{{Clear}}

==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
[[File:CoH Skullfish Sprite.png|left]]
{{Plural|CoH|Skullfish}} can be found swimming in deep pools of water in {{CoH}}. They move to the beat of the music and move every two beats, always crouching within the water for one beat before moving to an adjacent tile on the next beat. They will typically try to attack {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} at close range. They can be lured into shallow water, where they will beach themselves and flop around helplessly for seven beats before they jump back into the water. Occasionally, they will leap into the air before diving back into the water again.
{{Ambig|End}}
