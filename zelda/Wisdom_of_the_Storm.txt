{{Term/Store|Wisdom of the Storm||HWAoC}} is a level 51 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wisdom of the Storm|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Wisdom of the Storm}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed only by {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Safflina|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Voltfruit|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Endura Carrot|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}'s objective is to rescue two {{Plural|HWAoC|Goron Captain|link}} by defeating their {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. The first is being attacked by a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}, and the second two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}. Other {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, such as {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Pebblit|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}} are scattered around the map as well. When the {{Plural|HWAoC|Goron Captain}} are saved by {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}, one is surprised to not see {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo|link}} with her. {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}'s objective then becomes to find and save {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}}, who is fighting a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Hinox|link}} by himself. When it is defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.
