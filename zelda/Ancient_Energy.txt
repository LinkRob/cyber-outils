{{Term/Store|Ancient Energy|Ancient Energies|Series, BotW}} is an element in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC 404">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 404}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|BotW}}
{{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy}} resembles a glowing, highly viscous fluid. It is the mysterious source of energy for the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 405}}</ref> It can be found under {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, just beyond {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}} and in {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Deep Akkala|link}}.<ref name="CaC 404"/><ref>''"We found large amounts of ancient energy in the Necluda and Akkala regions." — [[Robbie's Memoirs]], Page 1 ([[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]])''</ref>

The final Battle against [[Dark Beast Ganon]] implies, that Ancient Energy is sacred in origin. Dark Beast Ganon can only be harmed by [[Sword Beam|Sword Beams]] from the [[Master Sword]], from [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]] fired from the [[Bow of Light]] or [[Twilight Bow]] and from [[Ancient Arrow|Ancient Arrows]].
