==Characteristics==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= That's a {{Color|MM White|Red Chuchu}}. It's nothing really to be afraid of! It's not unusual to find something in its stomach that might be of use.
}}

In {{MM}}, {{Plural|MM3D|Red Chuchu}} are commonplace to {{Term|MM3D|Termina Field|link}}, and carry {{Plural|MM3D|Heart|link}} in their bodies which they drop when deafeated.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|ChuChu}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= <p>A '''Red ChuChu'''!</p><p>Pulverize it and collect red Chu Jelly!</p>
}}

In {{TWW}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Red Chuchu}} are among the most common encountered type of {{Plural|TWWHD|Chuchu|link}}, and can be found on {{Term|TWWHD|Pawprint Isle|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Cavern|link}}, as well as {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Haven|link}} and the {{Term|TWWHD|Earth Temple|link|display= Earth}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Wind Temple|link|display= Wind}} {{Plural|TWWHD|Temple|link}}. They are the lowest form of {{Term|TWWHD|Chuchu}} that possess no unique traits, and are thus the easiest to slay. Sometimes forming groups of three, their attack deals little damage. As simple as they are, they are valued as they can be slain for {{Term|TWWHD|Red Chu Jelly|link}}, which can make health-replenishing {{Plural|TWWHD|Red Potion|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Chuchu}}
In {{TMC}}, {{Plural|TMC|Red Chuchu}} are common to {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}. They are the most basic varieties, and attack simply by jumping. {{Plural|TMC|Red Chuchu}} are often found in small groups.

{{Plural|TMC|Red Chuchu}} also come in an additional variant called {{Plural|TMC|Rock Chuchu|link}}. Appearing in {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}, they wear a rocky helmet over their face. When their helmets are destroyed, they turn into regular {{Plural|TMC|Red Chuchu}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Stub|TP}}

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, {{Plural|PH|Red Chuchu}} can be found in mountainous regions as well as most {{Plural|PH|Dungeon|link}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, {{Plural|ST|Red Chuchu}} are the most basic and common variety, appearing as early as the [[Tunnel to the Tower|Tunnel to the Tower of Spirits]]. 

{{Plural|ST|Helmet Chuchu|link}} are {{Plural|ST|Red Chuchu}} that wear protective helmets.

==={{SS|-}}===
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Target lock: {{Color|SS Blue|Red Chuchu}}</p><p>The red Chuchu has an exceedingly high body temperature. Avoid all physical contact, or your clothing and shield may burst into flames.</p><p>In the event you are set on fire, I recommend you quickly extinguish the flames by performing a roll.</p>
}}

In {{SS}}, {{Plural|SS|Red Chuchu}} are most commonly found at the {{Term|SS|Eldin Volcano|link}} area and can ignite {{Term|SS|Link|link}} on contact with their extremely high body temperature. They are also no longer the weakest Chuchu variety, as that role has been taken up by the [[Green Chuchu]]s.
