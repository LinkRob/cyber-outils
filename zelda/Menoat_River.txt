The {{Term/Store|Menoat River||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Menoat River'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Menoat River}} starts directly underneath the {{Term|BotW|Lake Tower|link}} and feeds into the {{Term|BotW|Faron Sea|link}}, south of {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}. At the head of the {{Term|BotW|Menoat River|display=River}} is {{Term|BotW|Cora Lake|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Menoat River}} then curves south through {{Term|BotW|Harfin Valley|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Parache Plains|link}} for a short time, before finally ending at the {{Term|BotW|Faron Sea}}.

During the "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Garb of Winds|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, which is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is tasked with reading {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2|link}} to find the location of the {{Term|BotW|Island Lobster Shirt|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The royal family of Hyrule collected historically significant outfits, but they were stolen by a bandit named Misko. One of the items stolen was the blue garb of the Hero of Winds.<br/><br/>Somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Deya Village Ruins}} is a journal that places the location of this treasure...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Menoat River}} is mentioned in this {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal|display=''Journal''}}, as it describes the {{Term|BotW|Island Lobster Shirt|display=Shirt}} being at the location "where the {{Term|BotW|Menoat River}} was born."<ref>{{Cite|The blue garb of the hero who controlled the wind and travelled the ocean is {{Color|BotW Red|where the Menoat River was born}}.|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref>

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
A {{Term|BotW|Circle|link}} of {{Plural|Series|Lily Pad|link}} can be found near the beginning of the {{Term|BotW|Menoat River}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} dives into this {{Term|BotW|Circle}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
