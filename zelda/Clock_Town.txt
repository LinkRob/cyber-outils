{{Term/Store|Clock Town||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 110 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Clock Town is the capital and largest city in [[Termina]] and is the region's center of government and commerce. The town consists of four main sections, each named after one of the four cardinal directions on a compass, as well as a small area known as the [[Laundry Pool]]. Clock Town is located right in the center of both [[Termina Field]] and all of Termina. 

Clock Town is completely surrounded by tall walls. There are only four entrances into the town, each facing a different compass direction, and they are all constantly guarded by [[Soldier]]s. However, there is a small, secret entrance through a tunnel from [[East Clock Town]] to the [[Astral Observatory]]. However, this entrance does not make the Town particularly vulnerable due to the tall fence that surrounds the Observatory. Only [[Deku Link]] can get in by using a [[Deku Flower]].

Clock Town is a very lively place. Many walls are painted with colorful patterns, and there are shops offering many wares and services. The annual [[Carnival of Time]] draws huge crowds of tourists.<ref>{{Cite|I'm terribly sorry! There are no vacancies today... We've been booked solid with reservations. It's always this way before the carnival. I'm sorry.|Anju|MM}}</ref> However, when [[Link]] the [[Hero of Time]] arrived in Termina, Clock Town was on the verge of [[Terminan Apocalypse|destruction]] by the falling [[Moon (Object)|Moon]].<ref>{{Cite|Come back to what?? Won't this town be crushed beneath the moon the morning after tomorrow?|Anju's Mother|MM}}</ref> This caused a large number of the townsfolk to flee, and the Town continued to empty as the days went by.<ref>{{Cite|It looks like this is it for this town, you know. You saw the moon, didn't you? It's gotten so huge. All the townsfolk have fled. You should flee, too. Far away...|Guard|MM}}</ref>

===Administration===
Clock Town is run by [[Mayor Dotour]], who makes important decisions from [[Mayor's Official Residence|his home]] in East Clock Town.<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Dotour Mayor of Clock Town.  V.I.P.|N/A|MM}}</ref> The Mayor's wife, [[Madame Aroma]], is in charge of scheduling performances for the Carnival of Time.<ref>{{Cite|Madame Aroma, the Mayor's Wife In charge of town performances|N/A|MM}}</ref> Other important figures in town are the Head of the Town Guard, [[Captain Viscen]],<ref>{{Cite|Captain Viscen! ...The Mayor's orders are absolute!|Guard|MM}}</ref> and the Head of the [[Carnival Committee]], [[Mutoh]].<ref>{{Cite|Urgently Recruiting! Workers wanted to build the festival tower for the carnival. See Mutoh.|N/A|MM}}</ref>

==The Carnival of Time==
[[File:MM3D Carnival of Time Story 3D.jpg|thumb|259x259px]]
{{Main|Carnival of Time}}
Every year, people from all over Termina come to Clock Town to celebrate the Carnival of Time. It is celebrated when the sun and the moon align to pay homage to the never-ending progress of both nature and time, and to request fruitfulness for the next year.<ref>{{Cite|Each year, the season of harmony begins when the sun and moon are in alignment. Paying homage to the way that both nature and time are tirelessly in the process of progressing... the Carnival of Time is when the peoples of the four worlds celebrate that harmony and request fruitfulness for the year.|Anju's Grandmother|MM}}</ref> It is customary to celebrate the Carnival wearing a hand-carved [[Mask]] that resembles the [[Four Giants]], the guardian spirits of Termina.<ref>{{Cite|For ages, people have worn masks resembling the giants who are the gods of the four worlds.|Anju's Grandmother|MM}}</ref>

==Significant Districts==
===South Clock Town===
[[File:MM3D South Clock Town.png|thumb|238x238px]]
{{Main|South Clock Town}}
South Clock Town is considered to be the main hub of the Town, due to its large size and the fact that it connects to every other section. It is also the location for the namesake and the main structure of Clock Town, the [[Clock Tower]].<ref>{{Cite|Have you been to the Clock Tower plaza yet?|Anju|MM}}</ref> In this area, two [[Treasure Chest]]s can be found.

In {{MM3D}}, the [[Clock Town Bank]], originally in West Clock Town, is relocated at the back of the Clock Tower.

===West Clock Town===
[[File:MM3D West Clock Town.png|thumb|238x238px]]
{{Main|West Clock Town}}
West Clock Town forms Clock Town's business district. All of the town's shops are located along its winding road, including the [[Bomb Shop#Majora's Mask|Bomb Shop]] and the [[Curiosity Shop]], which is only open after 10:00 P.M. West Clock Town is also the location of the [[Post Office]], [[Lottery Shop]], {{Term|MM3D|Trading Post|link}}, and [[Clock Town Bank]] in {{MM|-}}. The [[Swordsman's School]] can also be found here for those who wish to improve their [[Sword]] skills. At night, the [[Rosa Sisters]] practice their Carnival dance at the northern end of West Clock Town.<ref>{{Cite|They're twins just like us! Can you believe it? I think they're practicing somewhere in West Clock Town right now.|Twin Jugglers|MM}}</ref>

===East Clock Town===
[[File:MM3D East Clock Town.png|thumb|242x242px]]
{{Main|East Clock Town}}
East Clock Town is where the main tourist attractions of the city are located, as well as the [[Stock Pot Inn]] for any travelers who wish to stay in town. Most of the businesses are minigames such as [[Honey & Darling's Shop]] and the [[Town Shooting Gallery]]. However, there is also the [[Milk Bar#Majora's Mask|Milk Bar]], a place for townsfolk and tourists alike to congregate and chat, as well as a venue for bands such as [[The Indigo-Go's]] to perform. The raised northern section is the location of the Mayor's Official Residence, as well as the entrance to the secret tunnel leading to the [[Astral Observatory]].

This is also where the first [[Stray Fairy]] is located at night.

===North Clock Town===
[[File:MM3D North Clock Town.png|thumb|240x240px]]
{{Main|North Clock Town}}
North Clock Town is the least busy section in town, which has no buildings. It appears to be the town's park as it is mostly covered in grass. There is also what appears to be a small play area for children in the northeast corner, and the [[Deku Scrub Playground]] is located down a hole in the southwest, right next to the entrance to Clock Town's [[Great Fairy's Fountain]].<ref>{{Cite|Please, hear my plea! The masked Skull Kid has broken me apart and scattered my pieces! Please find a way to return me to the Fairy Fountain in North Clock Town.|Stray Fairy|MM}}</ref> North Clock Town has a reputation as an area where muggings occur. This is proven true when the thief [[Sakon]] attempts to steal the Bomb Shop's bag from the [[Old Lady from Bomb Shop|Old Lady]] during Link's first night in Termina.

===Laundry Pool===
[[File:MM3D Laundry Pool.png|thumb|237x237px]]
{{Main|Laundry Pool}}
The Laundry Pool is the smallest section of Clock Town, located in the southwest corner. At first sight it looks like a rather insignificant place, but becomes an important location in the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest. It features an entrance to the [[Curiosity Shop]]'s backroom where [[Kafei]] lives inside,<ref>{{Cite|Kafei Strange youth seen near Laundry Pool|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> a member of the [[Frog Choir]], and a bench where [[Guru-Guru]] sits at night.

This is also where the first Stray Fairy is located during the day.

==Trivia==
*Unlike all other places in [[Termina]], each day in Clock Town has a different variation of the same melody. The [[First Day]]'s theme features a fiddle playing the melody in a whimsical manner. The [[Second Day]] features the melody on an [[Ocarina]] and fiddle, and is similar to the First Day's theme, albeit with fewer instruments playing at a time and the pace is slightly faster. The [[Final Day]] features a variation on ocarina that is far more frantic than the previous two days and has an occasional diminished harmony in the background that gives a very ominous and desperate tone, due to the now-close [[Moon]] about to [[Terminan Apocalypse|crash]] into the town.
**If the [[Glitches in Majora's Mask|4th Day Glitch]] is performed to make [[Link]] enter the [[New Day]] without the limitations of the three-day cycle, the music in Clock Town will play as if in the First Day.
[[File:Clock Tower 2.jpg|thumb|254x254px|The Clock Tower's Carnival of Time cutscene in {{MM|-}}]]
*During the Eve of the [[Carnival of Time]], normally, a cutscene will play showing the Clock Tower lowering is head to reveal a staircase, allowing Link to enter the top to face the [[Skull Kid]]. However, this only occurs when the young hero is currently in the South area of the town. If he were to be in the West, North, or East sides of Clock Town as well as the Laundry Pool, the game will continue to run in real-time as the tower changes its shape, similar to as seen in [[Termina Field]].
