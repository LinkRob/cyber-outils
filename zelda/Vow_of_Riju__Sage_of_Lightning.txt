The {{Term/Store|Vow of Riju, Sage of Lightning||TotK}} is a [[Key Items in Tears of the Kingdom|Key Item]] in {{TotK}}.

==Location and Uses==
The vow is used to summon or dismiss the [[Sage Avatar|avatar]] of [[Riju]]. It is obtained after completing the [[Riju of Gerudo Town]] quest line.

[[Link]] may exchange four [[Sage's Will]]s at any [[Goddess Statue]] to upgrade the Vow into a [[Solemn Vow of Riju, Sage of Lightning]], which grants the avatar increased attack power when assisting in battle and a slightly shorter ability recharge.

The avatar of Riju cannot be summoned in [[Beneath Hyrule Castle]], [[Town]]s, and [[Stable]]s.
