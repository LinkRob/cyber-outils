'''''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment''''' is a title for the [[Nintendo Switch 2]]. It released on November 6th, 2025.{{Exp|Is that worldwide or one region? If the latter, when did it release in other regions?}} The game is the third title released under the [[Hyrule Warriors Series|''Hyrule Warriors'' label]] and is set in the world of {{TotK}} during the [[Imprisoning War (Tears of the Kingdom)|Imprisoning War]], an unknown amount of time ago.

==Story==
===Chapter 1===
Shortly before [[Ganondorf]] begins his campaign for power, Zelda meets Rauru and Sonia atop the Great Plateau.
{{stub}}

==Gameplay==
The gameplay is very similar to {{HWAoC}}'s, with a large amount of enemies and mini-bosses on a open battlefield. The setting is in an ancient version of Hyrule, based on the lore of {{TotK|-}}.

===Controls===
The player is able to control different characters throughout the game, each of which having their own unique attacks and abilities.
Characters can use different combinations of 'Normal Attacks' and 'Strong Attacks' to perform different combat actions and damage outputs. Players can also use different [[Zonai Devices]] seen in {{TotK}}, as well as separate Special Actions used to counter special attacks from enemies. As you defeat more enemies, a 'Special-Attack Gauge' charges up. Triggering it initiates a cinematic animation in which you can deal large damage to enemies. Another gauge also charges, called the 'Sync-Strike Gauge' and when two characters have their Sync-Strikes charged, they can attack together and uniquely affect each other's attacks.
