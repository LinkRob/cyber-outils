{{Term/Store|Greatfish Isle||Series, TWW, TWWHD}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref> (D2) also formatted as '''Great Fish Isle''',<ref>{{Cite|Oh, hey... I hear traveling merchants have appeared on Great Fish Isle and Bomb Island, too...|Wandering Merchant|TWW}}</ref> is an island of the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}}. Once the location of a peaceful village of [[Hyrulean]]s and [[Jabun]], it is now a horrific wreck after being demolished by [[Ganon|Ganondorf]]. Jabun fled the island and headed for [[Outset Island]]. Now, the only residents are a [[Traveling Merchant]] and the [[Korok]] [[Linder]].

==Features and Overview==

Despite its destruction, the island still holds two [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]], and is one of the recurring destinations of the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship|link}}. One of the Pieces of Heart can be found in a small indent by flying with the Deku Leaf directly northwest from the small spiral island chunk atop where the Korok rests. The other Heart Piece is given to Link from the Merchant in exchange for the [[Decorations|Shop Guru Statue]].

Like [[Dragon Roost Island]], the island, when Link hasn't received [[Nayru's Pearl]], has a spinning ring cloud above.

Beneath the waves is a fragment of the [[Triforce]], available after finding and opening the [[Triforce Chart]] from the [[Islet of Steel]] (which is located one square south).

Before the [[Great Flood]], Greatfish Isle was [[Lake Hylia]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 59}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* During the cutscene when Quill informs Link that Jabun moved to Outset Island, the water is transparent.
* As it is one of the islands where the Ghost Ship appears, Greatfish is drawn on the Ghost Ship Chart, but interestingly, it will appear on the chart in its current torn-apart state as opposed to what it used to look like (despite the chart reportedly being quite old). This was, no doubt, done for the player's benefit.
