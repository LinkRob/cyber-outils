==Location and Uses==
Frozen Carps are made by exposing [[Mighty Carp]], [[Armored Carp]], or [[Sanke Carp]] to [[Ice]]. Their icy texture makes them a delicacy.<ref name="Inventory"/> Eating one will restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and grant him temporary [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"/>
