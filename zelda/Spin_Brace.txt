The '''Spin Brace''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Spin Brace|A sturdy but light shoulder pad. Enables you to knock back foes that you hit with a spin.<ref name="Spin Brace">{{Cite|A sturdy but light shoulder pad. Enables you to knock back foes that you hit with a spin.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Spin Brace is rewarded for defeating the [[Darknut Lv. 3]] found in the East Ruins. Equipping it increases [[Princess Zelda]]’s spin knockback.
