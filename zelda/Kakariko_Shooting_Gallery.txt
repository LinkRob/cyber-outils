{{Term/Store|Kakariko Shooting Gallery||Series, CoH}} is a location in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Kakariko Shooting Gallery! It's 20 rupees per game.|Hylian|CoH}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
This {{Term|CoH|Mini-Game|link}} gives the contestant 50 beats - roughly 16 seconds - to hit cardboard cutout targets based on enemies from Crypt of the Necrodancer. Earning at least 12 points rewards them with a {{Term|CoH|Piece of Heart|link}} the first time, and 20 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}} on subsequent attempts. Earning at least 20 points rewards them with a {{Term|CoH|Quiver|link}}, and 50 Rupees on subsequent attempts.
