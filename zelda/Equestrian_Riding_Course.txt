The {{Term/Store|Equestrian Riding Course||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|BotW Arrow Left}} Hateno Village<br/>Equestrian Riding Course {{Icon|BotW Arrow Right}}|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Equestrian Riding Course}} is located in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. It was once used for riding {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} and practicing archery, as evidenced by the many {{Plural|BotW|Target|link}} located among the ruins, though it has since been inhabited by monsters. Among the ruins in the center of the track, there is a lit {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}}, which the monsters sleep around at night.

Southwest of the {{Term|BotW|Equestrian Riding Course|display=Riding Course}}'s track is a small hut which houses a wooden {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing 10 {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, a metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Eightfold Longblade|link}} appears atop a wooden platform held aloft by {{Plural|BotW|Sky Octorok|link}} above the {{Term|BotW|Equestrian Riding Course|display=Riding Course}}.

Destroying all of the {{Plural|BotW|Target}} on the track will cause a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}}-themed {{Term|BotW|Balloon|link}} to appear over the hut. If this {{Term|BotW|Balloon}} is destroyed, a {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will appear and give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}} for finding him. A second {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found on the portion of land located furthest south of the track. Here, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must move a large, metal door with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} and lift a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}} to uncover the {{Term|BotW|Korok}}.
