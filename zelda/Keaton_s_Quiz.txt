{{Term/Store|Keaton's Quiz||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a minigame in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a perfect score on Keaton's Quiz! This was added to your {{Color|MM3D Red|notebook}}!|N/A|MM3D}}</ref>

==Overview==
Keaton's Quiz is a series of five questions given to Link by any [[Keaton]] found by cutting clumps of [[Moving Grass]] while wearing the [[Keaton Mask]]. There are a total of thirty questions that could possibly be asked by a Keaton, with three possible answers. If Link answers them all correctly, he will obtain a [[Piece of Heart]]. However, if Link fails to answer the questions correctly, the Keaton will disappear. In order to try again, Link must start a new three-day cycle.

In {{MM}}, choosing to summon a Keaton again and answering the five questions after receiving the Heart Piece will yield a [[Red Rupee]] as a reward. However, in {{MM3D}}, answering the five questions again will instead yield a [[Fishing Hole Pass]]. Should Link remove the Keaton Mask before speaking to the Keaton, it will disappear, though it can be summoned again upon donning the Mask.
