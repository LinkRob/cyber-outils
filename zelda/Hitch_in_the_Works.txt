'''"Hitch in the Works"''' is the tenth episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}, but the twelfth in production order. It was first aired in the United States and Canada on November 10, 1989.

==Plot==
:''See also: [[Hitch in the Works/Episode transcript|Episode transcript]]''
{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} attempts to sneak out of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|North Castle|link}} in order to go {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fishing|link}}, but {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda|link}} runs into him and informs him that he needs to do chores around the castle if he wants to earn his keep since {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}} hasn't attacked in weeks.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=No! Ganon hasn't attacked in weeks and it's time you started earning your keep. There's chores to be done.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> While filling the castle's lamps with oil, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} spies a strange mechanical robot that takes a few steps toward him and promptly shatters; much to the dismay of its {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Doof|link}} the handyman. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Doof}} tells {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} that the robot was supposed to be his assistant.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, it was supposed to be my assistant. Help with the chores. Didn't work though.|name=Doof|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} encourages him to try again so that the young hero will do less work,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hey, don't give up yet. Any adventure that means doing less work is ok with me. Go on, give 'er another try.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> but {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Doof}}'s magic only causes the robot to go haywire, using a mop as a pogo stick and making a mess.

While {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Doof}} are chasing the robot, some of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}'s {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin|link}} infiltrate the castle by using a secret trapdoor under the floor tiles. The {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} break {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Doof}}'s robot again,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, darn it! I just fixed that!|name=Doof|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> and during their battle with {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}, ensnare him in a net. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Doof}} attempts to help by casting a spell on the robot again, allowing {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} enough time to defeat the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} while they are distracted by it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, I must help Link! Alakazoo!|name=Doof|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> Unfortunately, a hit from a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}}'s {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Boomerang|link}} causes the robot to collapse once more, knocking {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} unconscious. When he wakes up, he is being scolded by Zelda for making such a mess and sleeping on the job.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=You were sleeping on the job, THAT'S what happened.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> He tries to tell her about the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} he defeated,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= No, no. I remember now! There was a bunch of Moblins. Like 50 or 60 of them, yeah! And I bravely and heroically fought them off. So, uh, yeah.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> but she doesn't believe him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=A likely story. I don't see any Moblins. All I see is a bunch of junk on the floor that needs to be picked up. So get to it, ''hero''. And no more naps, ok?|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} then asks {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Doof}} if he can conjure up some fake {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} so he can "rescue" Zelda and get out of doing the chores.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Say, Doof. Could you make up some fake Moblins, that look real, I mean. [...] That way, see, I can save Zelda from the fake Moblins and get out of doing these stupid chores.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> However, Zelda overhears their discussion as she was cleaning a nearby door.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hmm...that's what YOU think, Link.|name=Princess Zelda|episode=10}}</ref>
[[File:Ceremony 2 (Hitch in the Works).png|thumb|right|Link stops the wedding]]
Meanwhile, in the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} reveals his plans to put a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Jewel of Control|link}} on Zelda, forcing her to marry him and make him ruler of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I wanted that princess. But once I place this Jewel of Control around her neck, the princess will do anything I say--even "Marry me!" Aha ha ha ha! And make ME ruler of all Hyrule! Aha ha ha ha!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> Back at the castle, the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} catch Zelda and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} off-guard, because Link assumes Doof has made his move while Zelda nonchalantly remarks that Moblins are attacking.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=If Link thinks his fake Moblins will scare me, he's in for a surprise.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Those Moblins are real!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> When they carry her off, Link realizes that was not part of the plan. Despite {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}'s attempts to stop the genuine {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}}, Zelda is taken to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}'s lair where he places the Jewel around Zelda's neck, forcing her to say yes to his marriage proposal.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=What?! If you think I'm gonna marry you, pigface, you're--absolutely right. I WILL marry Ganon.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref>

After defeating several {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} makes his way into {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}'s lair and interrupts the ceremony, only to discover that Zelda does not want to be rescued.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=No. I'm marrying Ganon.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} tells him that she is under his control, and then summons a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Gleeok|link}} from the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Evil Jar|link}} to attack {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=She's under my control, hero! And as for you, you have your own problems!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} fires three {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam|link}} at the monster's necks, but the heads separate and come after him. He skillfully dodges their attacks, causing them to fly straight into {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}. Back above ground, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} tries to get Zelda to kiss him for rescuing her, but the princess blames the debacle on {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}, who was trying to scare her with fake {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=No! I just remembered, this is all YOUR fault! [...] Yes! If you hadn't been trying to scare me with those fake Moblins, this never would've happened!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> She then promptly screams and jumps into {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}'s arms when she sees one, which turns out to be {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Doof}}'s fake {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} after all.
