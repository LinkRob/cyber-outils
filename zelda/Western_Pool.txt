The {{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Western Pool||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a small pond near [[Tingle]]'s house in {{FPTRR}}.<ref name="Uncle Rupee">{{Cite|Hey! Tingle! Yes, you...the 35-year old man over there... The daily grind has got you down... ...but you can escape this dull existence... If you don't want to miss this chance... ...get to the Western Pool!|Uncle Rupee|FPTRR}}</ref>

==History==
Tingle is called to the Western Pool by Uncle Rupee, who promises the middle-aged adventurer a more exciting life.<ref name="Uncle Rupee"/> Once Tingle arrives and touches the surface of the pool's water, he is greeted by Uncle Rupee, who tells Tingle of [[Rupeeland]] and explains the role of Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome Tingle. My name is Uncle Rupee. I am a Rupee Master. Rupees are money, the things which make this world go round! As a Rupee Master I'm an important person! You can tell, right? I am going to tell you about the most amazing place in the world... Its name is... Rupeeland! In Rupeeland you can live the life you've always dreamed of! It's a true paradise on earth! In Rupeeland you will eat nothing but the finest food! Work? Study? In Rupeeland those words mean nothing! Beautiful women are waiting for you in Rupeeland!|Uncle Rupee|FPTRR}}</ref> After telling Tingle of Rupeeland's wonders, Uncle Rupee asks Tingle if he would like to go to Rupeeland.<ref>{{Cite|How about it? Does Rupeeland sound like somewhere you want to go?|Uncle Rupee|FPTRR}}</ref> If Tingle accepts, Uncle Rupee will zap the middle-aged adventurer, donning him in green clothing,<ref>{{Cite|I can tell you really like the sound of Rupeeland! Without further ado, let's get you ready for your adventure!|Uncle Rupee|FPTRR}}</ref> rechristening the adventurer as "Tingle".<ref>{{Cite|That's quite a transformation! From this day forth you will be known as Tingle!|Uncle Rupee|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Name]? You won't need that silly name anymore! You're Tingle!|Uncle Rupee|FPTRR}}</ref> Uncle Rupee will then grant Tingle 100 Rupees,<ref>{{Cite|There you go! don't spend it all at once! I gave you 100 rupees to help you on your way! For you, Tingle, rupees are extremely important! Only rupees can lead you to Rupeeland!|Uncle Rupee|FPTRR}}</ref> prompting him to throw some of them into the pool.<ref>{{Cite|Let's start by throwing some rupees into the pool!|Uncle Rupee|FPTRR}}</ref>

Once Tingle throws enough [[Rupee]]s into the pool, it grows into a [[Rupee Tower|Tower]] which continues to grow as Tingle adds more Rupees. This Rupee Tower allows Tingle to travel farther across the world with the aid of his [[Tingle Balloon|Balloon]].
