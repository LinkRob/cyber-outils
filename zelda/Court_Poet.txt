The {{Term/Store|Court Poet||Series, BotW}} is a character mentioned in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= "Song of the Hero" by the Court Poet|book= CaC |publisher= |page= 390}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} was a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} who formerly served the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Sheikah"/> He was close in age to {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|At the time, there was a beautiful princess in the royal family, quite close to my teacher in age, apparently.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Due to his knowledge on ancient civilizations, he accompanied her on surveys of ancient {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} ruins.<ref>{{Cite|You see, my teacher was the court poet for the Hyrulean royal family and also a member of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah tribe}}. He was well versed in ancient civilizations and surveyed ruins in the company of the princess, it seems.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

Despite knowing it was doomed, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} fell in love with {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|Though he must have known it was doomed to be unrequited, my teacher fell in love with her...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} was supposedly in love with her knight, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But the princess herself only had eyes for her escort, he own knight attendant.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} was neither nobility nor royalty, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} was jealous of him.<ref>{{Cite|My teacher was consumed by jealousy... He fumed that the knight was neither nobility nor a royal himself.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

When the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}} struck, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} fled to {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|My teacher fled the Calamity and returned to his hometown of Kakariko Village.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> On the way, he witnessed {{Term|BotW|Link}} sacrifice himself at {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|link}} to protect {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|But on his way, he witnessed the princess's knight sacrifice himself to protect the princess.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} explained what happened to him, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} decided to seek out the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song|link}} of the hero who defeated {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} in the past to be passed down to {{Term|BotW|Link}} upon his return.<ref>{{Cite|The elder of Kakariko Village, Impa, explained these cirumstances to him, and his mind was made up. He would seek the songs of the hero who sealed the Calamity in an age past... so they could be passed on to the knight once he returned.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> His desire was that they would assist {{Term|BotW|Link}} when the time came for him to save {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref name="Kass">{{Cite|All so that the princess might be saved... Those were my teacher's last words, passed on now through me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

After the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} believed so much that {{Term|BotW|Link}} would return that he wrote a song, entitled the "{{Term|BotW|Song of the Hero|link}}".<ref>{{Cite|And then the Calamity struck. ...My teacher believed a hero would appear to beat back the Calamity. He poured that belief into a song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> During this time, he took on {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} as a student in order to pass down the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song}}, hoping that he would one day be able to share them with {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref name="Kass"/> He shared his memories of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} with {{Term|BotW|Kass}}, which brought him much joy.<ref>{{Cite|Those memories seemed to bring him a great deal of joy when he would speak of them.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My teacher would often speak of the princess's beauty. I would love to meet her and craft a song worthy of her.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

Unfortunately, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} passed away before {{Term|BotW|Link}} awoke from the {{Term|BotW|Slumber of Restoration|link}}. Before he passed, he made {{Term|BotW|Kass}} promise to share his song with {{Term|BotW|Link}}. Due to this, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} is unable to return to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}} until he has fulfilled his teacher's promise.<ref>{{Cite|But you see, I cannot go home until I fulfill my promise to my teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After singing the song for {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} asks on behalf of his teacher if he will accept it.<ref>{{Cite|So...appointed knight.<br/>Will you accept this song from my departed teacher?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts the song, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will thank him.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] Thank you so much! I knew you would. I was sure of it.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} sets out to complete a song that the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} left unfinished before his passing.<ref>{{Cite|[Unfinished song?] Yes. The song is mostly complete. However... My teacher left this world without finishing the {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The song was written in honor of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} and the trials they faced.<ref>{{Cite|...So? Did the {{Color|BotW Blue|verse}} move you? It was written in honor of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} challenging the trial.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> There are verses dedicated to {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Mipha}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Revali}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Urbosa}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} decides to journey to the locations mentioned in the lyrics in hopes of finishing the song.<ref>{{Cite|I wish to weave the missing pieces together and finish the song. That's why I'm roaming the areas mentioned in the lyrics. Ah, sweet joy! How glorious to sing this song in the very land it describes...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After completing the song, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} dubs it "{{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad (Song)|link}}".
