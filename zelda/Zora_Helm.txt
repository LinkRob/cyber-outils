==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Zora Helm is a piece of [[Armor]] that can be found in [[Toto Lake]]. Wearing it will increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} swim speed in the water and allow him to execute the Water [[Spin Attack]].<ref name=Inventory"/> The Zora Helm, alongside the [[Zora Armor]] and [[Zora Greaves]], compose the [[Zora Set]]. It can be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} or [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
In Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity, the Zora helm can be obtained by completing the [[Mipha, the Zora Princess]] scenario. After getting it, it becomes part of Link's attire and can be worn in any mission Link is in.
