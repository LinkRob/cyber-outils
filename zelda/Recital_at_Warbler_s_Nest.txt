The sisters each sing one note in a particular order: {{Term|BotW|Cree}}, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}, {{Term|BotW|Genli}}, {{Term|BotW|Notts}}, and lastly {{Term|BotW|Kotts}}. The pillars at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}} all correspond to one of them, the number of spires on top of it respresenting both their place in line and their birth order. While standing on the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must swing the {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf}} in the pattern given to him by the sisters, this being four, five, three, one, and two. This will cause {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine|link}} to rise from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|You solved the mystery behind the Rito Village song and Warbler's nest, uncovering an ancient shrine in the process!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

Turning around, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} beckons her sisters to look at the {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine|display=Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! Hey look, guys!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cree}} is confused and wonders whether the "weird-looking house" was always there,<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Was that weird-looking house always there?|Cree|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} recognizes it as an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|No, that's that "shrine" thing that the elder was talking about!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Genli}} remarks how cool it is that {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}} had such a secret,<ref>{{Cite|So cool! Who knew Warbler's Nest was keeping a secret like that!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> and so {{Term|BotW|Notts}} decides they have to tell their mother,<ref>{{Cite|Let's tell Mom!|Notts|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} agrees with her,<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, let's!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> turning back to say goodbye to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|See ya later!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} challenges her sisters to a race,<ref>{{Cite|Last one to mom is an unhatched egg!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ready, set...GO!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> and so the five of them fly off back to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}. Once they are gone, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to access {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine}}.
