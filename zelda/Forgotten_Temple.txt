The {{Term/Store|Forgotten Temple||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}} and [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']].<ref>{{Cite|'''Forgotten Temple'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==

=== {{BotW|-}} ===
The Forgotten Temple is located in the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Temple}} is an ancient temple devoted to {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}} hidden at the far northern end of the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}}. Several {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}} can be found along the Forgotten Temple's grounds, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link|display=Paraglide}} above the chamber walls inside the Temple though the place is filled with [[Guardian|Decayed Guardians]] who will shoot Link on sight. The {{Term|BotW|Rona Kachta Shrine|link}} sits at the end of the Temple, before a giant {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}}. This Goddess Statue is the oldest and largest of the Statues throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Head to the Forgotten Temple where the oldest statue of the Goddess stands to see what they have left for you there.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> as well as the largest that Link can find in his journey. At certain times, {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}}; one of the three roaming dragons can be seen flying above the temple.

The Forgotten Temple is where Link is directed by the {{Plural|BotW|Monk|link}} upon completing the primary 120 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} "{{Term|BotW|A Gift from the Monks}}".<ref>{{Cite|You have at last conquered all of our trials. Having fulfilled our purpose, we monks bestow upon you this... The warrant of the true hero. You must now depart for the Forgotten Temple.|Rona Kachta|BotW}}</ref> If Link travels to the Forgotten Temple after speaking to the final Monk, three stone {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} will appear on the platform in front of the Goddess Statue, gifting Link with all three pieces of the {{Term|BotW|Hero of the Wild Set|link}}.

A Korok can be found hiding underneath a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}} on a ledge behind the Goddess Statue.

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
Some time after {{BotW|-}}, the Forgotten Temple is still in ruin and no effort has been made at reconstruction. All of the Decayed Guardians and updrafts from the previous game are gone, and there are a few [[Bokoblin]] camps instead. 

The large Goddess Statue has been toppled due to the [[Upheaval]]. This reveals an open door behind the Statue, leading to a [[Zonai]] structure, which contains the locations of all geoglyphs in Hyrule, and the order to find them in. It also contains the grave of [[Queen Sonia|Sonia]], the first Queen of Hyrule, surrounded by Sundelions. 

The Forgotten Temple is also seen in Memories 12 and 13, "The Sages' Vow," and "A King's Duty." In "The Sages' Vow." [[King Rauru|Rauru]] leads the [[Sage|sages]] of Hyrule to the Zonai structure at the back of the temple, presenting them with [[Secret Stone|Secret Stones]] so that they may fight alongside him as sages. In "A King's Duty," Rauru mourns over the grave of Sonia, his wife, and comforts Zelda about the coming [[Imprisoning War (TotK)|battle]] with [[The Demon King|Demon King Ganondorf]].

== Trivia ==

* The design of the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Temple}} is inspired by the {{Term|SS|Sealed Temple|link}} from {{SS|-}} and features similar architecture to the Springs of Wisdom, Courage, Power, as well as Lanayru Road.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Since it is a building that represents Hyrule's ancient past, the design incorporates construction elements from Skyward Sword in a similar way to the Springs of Wisdom, Courage, and Power, as well as Lanayru Road.|book=CaC|page=312}}<br /></ref>
