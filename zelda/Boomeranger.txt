The {{Term/Store|Boomeranger||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 118}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Boomeranger improves le Boomerang. If you're into that sort of thing, my pet.}}

The {{Term|TFH|Boomeranger}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}} has been completed. It dresses {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} in nonspecific tribal garments including a tooth necklace, horned skull hat, and red face paint.

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Boomeranger}} improves several abilities of the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang|link}}:
*Making it larger, with an increased hit radius.
*Dealing damage to [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]] in addition to knocking them dizzy.
*Passing through Enemies instead of simply rebounding off of them, allowing it to hit multiple Enemies or to hit the same Enemy multiple times.<ref>{{Cite|Increase Boomerang range and power, and make your 'rang go through enemies!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref>
*Pulling in both of the other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} instead of only one.<ref>{{Cite|Rule the 'rang!<br/>♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦<br/>Make Boomerangs pass through foes. Use on enemies or to bring the other players closer.|Catalog|TFH}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*An early version of the {{Term|TFH|Boomeranger}} found in the E3 2015 demo featured traditional Native American garb, including a feathered headdress.
