'''Tweelus''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Tweelus|This fire-resistant rock monster's hard exterior is still vulnerable to impact. It has a weakness for Rock Roast.<ref name="Tweelus">{{Cite|This fire-resistant rock monster's hard exterior is still vulnerable to impact. It has a weakness for Rock Roast.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Tweelus are fire-resistant rock creatures that blend into their surroundings. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Tweelus [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of two.
