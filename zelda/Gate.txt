{{Term/Store|Gate|Gates|Series, ST|plural}} are recurring objects and items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 41 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Main|Moon Gate}}
In {{FSA|-}}, {{Plural|FSA|Moon Gate}} are portals between {{Term|FSA|Hyrule|link}} and the {{Term|FSA|Dark World|link}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
===={{Term|ST|Gate to the Fire Realm}}====
{{Main|Gate to the Fire Realm}}
In {{ST}}, the {{Term|ST|Gate to the Fire Realm}} allows access to the {{Term|ST|Fire Realm|link}}.

===={{Term|ST|Gate to the Forest Realm}}====
{{Main|Gate to the Forest Realm}}
In {{ST|-}}, the {{Term|ST|Gate to the Forest Realm}} allows access to the {{Term|ST|Forest Realm|link}}.

===={{Term|ST|Gate to the Ocean Realm}}====
{{Main|Gate to the Ocean Realm}}
In {{ST|-}}, the {{Term|ST|Gate to the Ocean Realm}} allows access to the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm|link}}.

===={{Term|ST|Gate to the Snow Realm}}====
{{Main|Gate to the Snow Realm}}
In {{ST|-}}, the {{Term|ST|Gate to the Snow Realm}} allows access to the {{Term|ST|Snow Realm|link}}.

===={{Term|ST|Spacetime Gate}}====
{{Main|Spacetime Gate}}
In {{ST|-}}, {{Plural|ST|Spacetime Gate}} appear in pairs, allowing {{Plural|ST|Train|link}} that passes through it to warp to its associated {{Term|ST|Spacetime Gate}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
===={{Term|SS|Gate of Time}}====
{{Main|Gate of Time}}
In {{SS}}, the {{Term|SS|Gate of Time}} is used to travel between eras.

===={{Term|SS|Trial Gate}}====
{{Main|Trial Gate}}
In {{SS|-}}, {{Plural|SS|Trial Gate}} are portals that allow access to the {{Term|SS|Silent Realm|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
===={{Term|BotW|Champion's Gate}}====
{{Main|Champion's Gate}}
In {{BotW}}, the {{Term|BotW|Champion's Gate}} is a location in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}.

===={{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - East Gate}}====
{{Main|Lanayru Road - East Gate}}
In {{BotW|-}}, the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - East Gate}} is a location in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}.

===={{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - West Gate}}====
{{Main|Lanayru Road - West Gate}}
In {{BotW|-}}, the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - West Gate}} is a location in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda}}.

===={{Term|BotW|Travel Gate}}====
{{Main|Travel Gate}}
In {{BotW|-}}, {{Plural|BotW|Travel Gate}} are objects allowing {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to warp to using the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
===={{Term|HWDE|Gate of Souls}}====
{{Main|Gate of Souls}}
In {{HW}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Gate of Souls}} are connections to other eras and can be manipulated to summon monsters, people, and locations from other times. It is also {{Term|HWDE|Lana}}'s level three {{Term|HWDE|Summoning Gate|link}}.

===={{Term|HWDE|Gate of Souls +}}====
{{Main|Gate of Souls +}}
In {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Gate of Souls +}} is {{Term|HWDE|Lana}}'s level four {{Term|HWDE|Summoning Gate}}.

===={{Term|HWDE|Gate of Tides}}====
{{Main|Gate of Tides}}
In {{HW|L|-}} and {{HW|DE|-}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Gate of Tides}} is {{Term|HWDE|Lana}}'s level four plus {{Term|HWDE|Summoning Gate}}.

===={{Term|HWDE|Gate of Time}}====
{{Main|Gate of Time}}
In {{HW|-}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Gate of Time}} appears in the {{Term|HWDE|Sealed Grounds (Hyrule Warriors)|link}} and as {{Term|HWDE|Lana|link}}'s level one {{Term|HWDE|Summoning Gate}}.

===={{Term|HWDE|Guardian's Gate}}====
{{Main|Guardian's Gate}}
In {{HW|-}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Guardian's Gate}} is {{Term|HWDE|Lana}}'s level two {{Term|HWDE|Summoning Gate}}.

===={{Term|HWDE|Summoning Gate}}====
{{Main|Summoning Gate}}
In {{HW|-}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Summoning Gate}} is {{Term|HWDE|Lana}}'s {{Term|HWDE|Weapon|link}}. Initially appearing as the {{Term|HWDE|Gate of Time}}, it can level up to become other types of {{Plural|HWDE|Gate}}: {{Term|HWDE|Guardian's Gate}}, {{Term|HWDE|Gate of Souls}}, {{Term|HWDE|Gate of Souls +}}, and {{Term|HWDE|Gate of Tides}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
