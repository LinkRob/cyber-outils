The '''[[wikipedia:GameCube|Nintendo GameCube]]''' is a console system developed by [[Nintendo]], and released towards the end of 2001. It is the company's fourth home console released internationally and the first to use CDs to store games. Part of the "[[wikipedia:Sixth_generation_of_video_game_consoles|128-bit era]]" alongside the [[Game Boy Advance]], it competed with the [[wikipedia:Dreamcast|Sega Dreamcast]], [[wikipedia:PlayStation_2|Sony PlayStation 2]], and [[wikipedia:Xbox_(console)|Microsoft Xbox]]. The GameCube's original name was to be "Dolphin", as noted references appear in games such as ''[[w:c:mario:Super Mario Sunshine|Super Mario Sunshine]]''. The GameCube has room for two Memory Cards and four controllers. It has three buttons on top: Open, Reset, and Power. It has two serial ports and one hi-speed port on the bottom; their respective functions remain a mystery. The Nintendo GameCube utilizes mini-disks. The system was discontinued in 2007, and is Nintendo's least successful home console before the [[Wii U]], selling 21.74 million units worldwide.

There are four main colors to the Nintendo GameCube - Purple, Black, Orange, and Silver. Purple is the original color, seen in advertisements, the trophy in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', and other places. Silver was released after the first three colors.

Several popular ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' titles were produced for this system, especially {{TWW}} and {{TP}}, but the first Nintendo GameCube title to feature ''Zelda'' content in any capacity is the crossover fighting title ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' (its best-selling title). The counterpart ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise also had its share of successful titles like ''Super Mario Sunshine'', ''[[w:c:mario:Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door|Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', and ''[[w:c:mario:Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''.

An add-on accessory for the Nintendo GameCube known as the [[Game Boy Player]], was released in 2003. The Game Boy Player allowed people to play [[Game Boy]], [[Game Boy Color]], and [[Game Boy Advance]] games on their television screen through their GameCube.

The GameCube's predecessor is the [[Nintendo 64]], and it's proceeded by the [[Wii]].

===GameCube Controller===
[[File:GameCube Controller.png|thumb|The '''Nintendo GameCube Controller'''; The standard controller for playing Nintendo GameCube games.]]

The '''Nintendo GameCube Controller''' is the standard controller for the Nintendo GameCube. It consists of several buttons, of many types. The Nintendo GameCube gets shipped with controllers capable of rumble effect. These controllers can also be used to play some Virtual Console games on the Wii, and is also one of the four controller types available in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

== Details ==
*MPU ("Microprocessor Unit")*: Custom IBM Power PC "Gekko" 
*Manufacturing process: 0.18 micron IBM copper wire technology 
*Clock frequency: 485 MHz
*CPU capacity: 1125 Dmips (Dhrystone 2.1) 
*Internal data precision: 32-bit Integer & 64-bit floating-point
*External bus: 1.3GB/second peak bandwidth (32-bit address space, 64-bit data bus 162 MHz clock) 
*Internal cache L1: instruction 32KB, data 32KB (8 way) L2: 256KB (2 way) 
*System LSI: Custom ATI/Nintendo "Flipper" 
*Embedded frame buffer: Approx. 2MB sustainable latency : 6.2ns (1T-SRAM) 
*Embedded texture cache: Approx. 1MB sustainable latency : 6.2ns (1T-SRAM) 
*Texture read bandwidth: 10.4GB/second (Peak) 
*Main memory bandwidth: 2.6GB/second (Peak) 
*Pixel depth: 24-bit color, 24-bit Z buffer 
*Image processing functions: Fog, subpixel anti-aliasing, 8 hardware lights, alpha blending, virtual texture design, multi-texturing, bump mapping, environment mapping, MIP mapping, bilinear filtering, trilinear filtering, anisotropic filtering, real-time hardware texture decompression (S3TC), real-time decompression of display list, HW 3-line deflickering filter.

== Trivia ==

* The music theme of the Nintendo GameCube's main menu screen is actually the [[wikipedia:Famicom_Disk_System|Family Computer Disk System]]'s theme, albeit playing very slowly. If sped-up 16 times, it will become identical.[[File:SCII Link fighting Nightmare.jpg|thumb|214x214px|Link as he appears in ''SoulCalibur II'', fighting Nightmare in the GameCube release]]
* [[Link]], alongside [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Princess Peach]] are known to have special guest appearances in a very few third-party games of the [[wikipedia:Sixth_generation_of_video_game_consoles|sixth-generation era]]. He appears in Namco's fighting game ''[https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soulcalibur_II SoulCalibur II]'' while the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters appear in Electronic Art's ''[[wikipedia:SSX_on_Tour|SSX on Tour]]'' and ''[[wikipedia:NBA_Street_V3|NBA Street V3]]'' sport games, each exclusively for the Nintendo GameCube. Link's case was met with critical acclaim.
* In ''[[mariowiki:Mario_Kart:_Double_Dash!!|Mario Kart: Double Dash]]'' of the [[mariowiki:Mario_Kart_(series)|''Mario Kart'' series]], one of the battle courses is the Nintendo GameCube itself.
* Although the Nintendo GameCube is known to be durable, it has been noted by fans to have a design flaw where its internal compactors quickly wear out over use, causing the system to have trouble reading games even if the disk is undamaged or clean until it gets repaired. In some cases, they have to wait between 10-30 minutes for it to "warm up" before its able to play games.
