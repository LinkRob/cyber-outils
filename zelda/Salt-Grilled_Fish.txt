{{Term/Store|Salt-Grilled Fish|Salt-Grilled Fish|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Salt-Grilled Fish can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} together with [[Rock Salt]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The default version can only be made either by using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}}, both of which offer no status effects, or by using two Fish with contradictory effects. Eating these fish will heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. To make them, the whole fish must be rolled in Rock Salt before it's grilled.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on both the type of Fish used and whether any additional ingredients are added, several different variations of Salt-Grilled Fish can be made:

* '''Hearty Salt-Grilled Fish''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed, and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Salt-Grilled Fish''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}} or other energizing ingredients. Consuming them will refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Salt-Grilled Fish''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using enduring ingredients. They can be eaten to completely replenish and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Salt-Grilled Fish''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using hasty ingredients. Link's movement speed will be increased by eating these meals.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Salt-Grilled Fish''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} or other spicy ingredients. One level of [[Heat Resistance]] will be granted to [[Link]] if he eats them.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Salt-Grilled Fish''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}} or other electric ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Salt-Grilled Fish''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, or other mighty ingredients. By eating this fish, Link will receive a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Salt-Grilled Fish''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, or other tough ingredients. They will grant Link a boost to his defense if he eats them.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Salt-Grilled Fish''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}} or other sneaky ingredients. If Link eats them, he will receive a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Salt-Grilled Fish''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Salt-Grilled Fish}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A simple dish made by rolling a whole fish in natural rock salt before grilling it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}} or other chilly ingredients. Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]] if he eats these fish.<ref name="Chilly"/>
