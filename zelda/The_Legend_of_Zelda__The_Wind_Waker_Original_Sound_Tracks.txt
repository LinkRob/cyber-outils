==Overview==
''The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker Original Sound Tracks'' was released in March 19, 2003, the album consists of two CDs that contain 133 synthesized tunes from the game. It was published by Scitron Digital Content, being composed by [[Kenta Nagata]], [[Hajime Wakai]], [[Toru Minegishi]] and [[Koji Kondo]].
