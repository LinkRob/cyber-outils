The {{Term/Store|Keh Namut Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Keh Namut Shrine}}<br/>Cryonis Trial}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Cryonis Trial''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Keh Namut Shrine}} is located on {{Term|BotW|Mount Hylia|link}} atop the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. It is one of the four {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must complete during "{{Term|BotW|The Isolated Plateau|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Old Man explained that there are a total of four Shrines on the plateau. He said that he will give you the Paraglider once you have acquired the Spirit Orb from all the of the Shrines.<br/>He told you to survey the plateau from the top of the tower to find the remaining shrines. He also pointed out that you can use your Sheikah Slate if you need to travel quickly.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> The surrounding area is a {{Term|BotW|Freezing Climate|link}}, so {{Term|BotW|Link}} will need either a {{Term|BotW|Warm Doublet|link}} or spicy {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} to reach it. When he enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Keh Namut|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Keh Namut. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Keh Namut|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The first chamber in {{Term|BotW|Keh Namut Shrine}} contains a pool of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}}, the latter of which has a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|link}} below it. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} inserts his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} into the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|display=Pedestal}}, the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} will authenticate it before distilling a {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling rune...}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> A drop of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy|link}} will fall from the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} and onto the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}, granting {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Rune extracted.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

With {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to make a pillar of {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} in the nearby pool of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}. He can then climb the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} pillar, allowing him to access the ledge above it. In the next room, the floor is coated with {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}, but the way forward is blocked by a portcullis. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can raise the portcullis by summoning a {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} pillar underneath it.

The third chamber has a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must either avoid or defeat. Past this is a stone slab, which is balanced upon a pillar at its center. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} raises a {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} pillar underneath one of its ends, it will turn into a walkway leading to the highest level of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. The {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} is accessible from here.

If this is the first {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed, {{Term|BotW|Keh Namut}} will explain that they are a {{Term|BotW|Monk}} of {{Term|BotW|The Goddess Hylia|link}}, dedicated to helping defeat {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have proven to possess the resolve of a true hero. I am Keh Namut, the creator of this trial. I am a humble monk, blessed with the sight of Goddess Hylia and dedicated to helping those who seek to defeat Ganon.}}|Keh Namut|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s arrival fulfills {{Term|BotW|Keh Namut}}'s purpose, and the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|With your arrival, my duty is now fulfilled. In the name of Goddess Hylia, allow me to bestow this gift upon you... Please accept this Spirit Orb.}}|Keh Namut|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Keh Namut|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Keh Namut}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Keh Namut|BotW}}</ref>

One a ledge near the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I}} is a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. Using a {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} block, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can climb up to the ledge and open the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}. Inside, he will find a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Spear|link}}.
