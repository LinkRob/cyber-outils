The {{Term/Store|Rok Uwog Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Rok Uwog Shrine}}<br/>Power of Reach}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Power of Reach''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Rok Uwog Shrine}} is located under a rocky canopy in the northwestern part of the {{Term|BotW|Pikida Stonegrove|link}}, which is part of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rok Uwog|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Rok Uwog. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Rok Uwog|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Rok Uwog Shrine}} features multiple chambers. The primary chamber has a locked door to Rok Uwog on the right, a barred window on the left, vines on the walls and {{Plural|BotW|Torchlight|link}} around the room. Behind some vines near the barred window is two {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}} in a gap in the wall, which leads to the second chamber.

A {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}} wielding a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear|link}} is waiting in the second chamber. This chamber also contains a ramp being held up by a metal block and a wooden box. Lowering the ramp allows Link to advance into a corridor where another {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}} wielding a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear}} is waiting.

At the end of the corridor is a barred window and a portcullis to the next chamber. The next chamber has two {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}} on top of a {{Term|BotW|Switch|link}}, a ladder leading back to the primary chamber, and a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. To complete the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must remove the {{Plural|BotW|Barrel}} from the {{Term|BotW|Switch}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has done so, the portcullis will rise, allowing him to obtain the {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}} inside the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Small Key}} can be used to open the locked door in the primary chamber, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to speak to {{Term|BotW|Rok Uwog}}. They give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Rok Uwog|BotW}}</ref> They will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Rok Uwog|BotW}}</ref>

On a ledge on the right side of the wall facing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}'s entrance is a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Drillshaft|link}}.
