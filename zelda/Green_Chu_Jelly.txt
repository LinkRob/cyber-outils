{{Term/Store|Green Chu Jelly|Green Chu Jelly|Series, TWW, TWWHD, TP, TPHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126 {{Exp Game|TWW, TP}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Term|TWWHD|Green Chu Jelly}} is dropped by {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Chuchu|link}}. If {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} gives fifteen {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Chu Jelly}} to {{Term|TWWHD|Doc Bandam|link}} in {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}'s {{Term|TWWHD|Chu Jelly Juice Shop|link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Doc Bandam}} will start selling {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Potion|link}} for either ten {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}} or for trading five {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Chu Jelly}}.{{Fact|TWWHD}} Additionally, {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Chu Jelly}} can be sold to {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle|link}} for 1 {{Term|TWWHD|Rupee}} each.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Term|TPHD|Green Chu Jelly}} is left behind after defeating a {{Term|TPHD|Green Chu|link}} in the [[Wii]] version of {{TP}} and in {{TPHD}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= SuperZambezi|published= January 19, 2019|retrieved= January 31, 2020|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ILM5DkuARNE|title= Green Chu Jelly - Zelda: Twilight Princess Wii + HD|site= YouTube|type= video}}</ref> It can be scooped up with an {{Term|TPHD|Empty Bottle|link}} and has no effect when drank. When obtained, no text will appear in the Wii version; however, text was added in {{TPHD|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|You put {{Color|TPHD Animal Green|green Chu Jelly}} in your bottle! It doesn't look all that tasty...|N/A|TPHD}}</ref>

In the [[Nintendo GameCube]] version, since the merge of a {{Term|TPHD|Blue Chu|link}} with a {{Term|TPHD|Yellow Chu|link}} will result in a {{Term|TPHD|Purple Chu|link}},<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= WhiteWolfos14|published= n.d.|retrieved= January 31, 2020|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ETChA6zIGKs|title= TP: Green Chu Jelly not obtainable on GC|site= YouTube|type= video}}</ref> {{Term|TPHD|Green Chu Jelly}} cannot be obtained.
