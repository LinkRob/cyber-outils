{{Term/Store|Purple Puffstool|Purple Puffstools|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Purple Puffstool|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Purple Puffstools are are stationary enemies that release hazardous spores once every three beats, crouching on the second beat before release. They are often found near {{Term|CoH|Puffstool|link}}, making treading around them difficult due to their different beat patterns.

==Trivia==
They share their color scheme and attack pattern with the Purple Mushroom enemies from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
