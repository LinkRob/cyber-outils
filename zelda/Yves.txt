==Biography==
{{Term|CoH|Yves}} lives inside a tree hut along with his friend {{Term|CoH|Tingle|link}} in the {{Term|CoH|Lost Woods|link}}, where he longs to be an adventurer.<ref>{{Cite|As thanks, Tingle's friend {{Color|CoH Blue|Yves}} will join you! He's always wanted to be an adventurer.|Tingle|CoH}}</ref> He can be unlocked as a playable character after 20 {{Plural|CoH|Deku Seed|link}} have been collected and given to {{Term|CoH|Tingle}}. {{Term|CoH|Yves}} has no arms, and therefore cannot attack using normal weapons.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Blue|Yves}} {{Icon|CoH Yves}} joined your party!<br/>{{Color|CoH Blue|Yves}} has ambitions in becoming a hero, and he won't let a lack of arms get in his way!|N/A|CoH}}</ref> Instead, he can only bump into enemies, fire {{Plural|CoH|Deku Seed}}, or hide by burrowing underground.<ref name="Description"/> {{Term|CoH|Yves}} is also extremely vulnerable as he possesses only one {{Term|CoH|Heart|link}}.
