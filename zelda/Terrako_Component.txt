{{Term/Store|Terrako Component|Terrako Components|HWAoC|plural}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Item|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.

==Overview==
{{Plural|HWAoC|Terrako Component}} are parts of the broken {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako|link}}, which are scattered around {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule|link}} after its battle with {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|link}}. Their appearance resembles that of a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}'s upper leg, albeit "with a special shape" exclusive to {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}}. An {{Term|HWAoC|Ancient Gear|link}} and two {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Screw|link}} are also shown within the in-game sprite.

{{Plural|HWAoC|Terrako Component}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Key Item|link}}, and thus cannot be sold to merchants or shop owners. There are a total of 50 {{Plural|HWAoC|Terrako Component}} scattered around {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule}}, obtained by completing all of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Side Quest|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} that are unlocked after completing {{Term|HWAoC|The Future of Hyrule|link}}.

{{Plural|HWAoC|Terrako Component}} are used to complete {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako's Return|link}}; upon its completion, {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} becomes a playable character.
