{{Plural|BotW|Boko Spear}} are {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}} that can commonly found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. They are used primarily by regular {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} in these areas. They belong to to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Carved from {{Plural|BotW|Tree Branch|link}}, these {{Plural|BotW|Boko Spear|display=Spear}} are also used by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} to skewer and {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cook|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> The {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}} recommends throwing them if they are close to breaking.<ref name="Inventory"/> Scanning the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Term|BotW|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} has a chance of spawning a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Boko Spear}}.

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Boko Spear}} will float on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} if they are dropped. They will also burn if exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}, which is a tactic used by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} near {{Plural|BotW|Campfire|link}}. Finally, they do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
