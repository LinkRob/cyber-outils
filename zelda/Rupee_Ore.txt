==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SSHD|Rupee Ore}} can be found along the walls of the {{Term|SSHD|Thrill Digger|link}} {{Term|SSHD|Mini-Game|link}} cave in the {{Term|SSHD|Eldin Volcano|link}}. By shooting at them with either the {{Term|SSHD|Slingshot|link}} or the {{Term|SSHD|Bow|link}}, they will occasionally drop a {{Term|SSHD|Rupee}} that {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} can pick up.<ref name="Name"/> There are three colors of {{Term|SSHD|Rupee Ore}}, with each color corresponding to the type of {{Term|SSHD|Rupee}} that can be dropped. Green {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee Ore}} can drop Green {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee}}, Blue {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee Ore}} can drop Blue {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee}}, and Black {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee Ore}} can drop {{Plural|SSHD|Rupoor|link}}.
