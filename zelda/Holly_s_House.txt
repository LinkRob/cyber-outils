==Features and Overview==
{{Term|OoS|Holly's House}} is a {{Term|OoS|House|link}} in the {{Term|OoS|Woods of Winter|link}}. It is inhabited by {{Term|OoS|Holly|link}}, and contains a large fireplace, two tables, a {{Term|OoS|Bed|link}}, and four {{Plural|OoS|Pot|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} first enters the {{Term|OoS|Holly's House|display=House}} by falling through the chimney off of a snowbank, startling {{Term|OoS|Holly}} and making her think he is {{Term|OoS|Santa|link}}. She has him clear the snow from her walkway, in exchange for letting him keep the {{Term|OoS|Shovel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|AHH!!! You startled me! Winter sets in all of a sudden, then down my chimney comes... You're not Santa! You don't scare me! Now take this shovel and clear the snow from my walkway!
