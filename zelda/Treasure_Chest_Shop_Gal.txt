The {{Term/Store|Treasure Chest Shop Gal||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Treasure Chest Shop Gal runs the [[Treasure Chest Game#Majora's Mask|Treasure Chest Shop]] in [[East Clock Town]]. She is rather flirtatious, and changes the cost and prize of the game depending on what [[Transformation#Majora's Mask|form]] Link takes. She refers to Link in his [[Hylian]] form as the "kid collecting masks" and charges him 20 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Come in! Oh, is the kid collecting masks? How about it? Wanna play? For you, it's 20 Rupees.|Treasure Chest Shop Gal|MM}}</ref> She says [[Deku Link]] is "cute" and charges him 10 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, what a cute little customer! So, do you want to play? For you, it's only 10 Rupees.|Treasure Chest Shop Gal|MM}}</ref> She describes [[Goron Link]] as fit and charges 30 Rupees to play.<ref>{{Cite|Well, aren't you a fit fellow! Wanna play? For you, it's 30 Rupees.|Treasure Chest Shop Gal|MM}}</ref> Finally, she says [[Zora Link]] is handsome and charges him only 5 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Well, hello there, handsome! Wanna play? Hey...For you, it's only 5 Rupees.|Treasure Chest Shop Gal|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, her dialogue with Goron Link is changed. She now calls him handsome, complimenting his strong arms and saying he is her type.<ref>{{Cite|Well, HELLO there, handsome! My, what strong arms you have. Heehee. So, wanna play?|Treasure Chest Shop Gal|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, really? That's too bad! And you're just my type, too...|Treasure Chest Shop Gal|MM3D}}</ref> However, she still charges him 30 Rupees.
