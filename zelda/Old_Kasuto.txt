==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TAoL|Old Kasuto}} was attacked and destroyed by monstrous {{Plural|TAoL|Moa|link}}, forcing its people to flee,<ref>{{Cite|WE HAD TO FLEE KASUTO.|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref> although one {{Term|TAoL|Old Man|link}} stayed behind.<ref>{{Cite|THE OLD MAN WHO REMAINS HAS MAGIC.|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref> Upon {{Term|TAoL|Link|link}}'s first arrival to {{Term|TAoL|Old Kasuto}}, the {{Plural|TAoL|Moa}} who have taken over the town are invisible to him, and only by possessing the {{Term|TAoL|Cross|link}} can the young hero see these flying creatures. The {{Term|TAoL|Old Man}} who is left here informs {{Term|TAoL|Link}} that the town is deserted, and that he must look in the woods to the east in order to find the new village of {{Term|TAoL|Kasuto}}.<ref>{{Cite|THE TOWN IS DEAD. LOOK EAST IN WOODS.|Old Man|TAoL}}</ref> Once {{Term|TAoL|Link}} has obtained all of the eight {{Plural|TAoL|Magic Container|link}}, the {{Term|TAoL|Old Man}} will reward him with his most powerful magic yet, the [[Magic of The Adventure of Link#Thunder|Thunder]] spell. {{Term|TAoL|Link}} can also find a hint on the location of the {{Term|TAoL|Magical Key|link}} written on a wall in one of the empty houses.<ref>{{Cite|MAGIC KEY READS... TREASURE OF KASUTO|N/A|TAoL}}</ref>
