In {{HW}}, the '''Gauntlets''' are a class of Weapon used by Link. They are associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Lightning}}.

The Gauntlets are paired with a version of the [[Ball and Chain]] as a weapon, which is powerfully swung and tossed to attack enemies. The Gauntlets also enable Link to lift heavy objects, including [[Pot]]s, [[Bomb Flower]]s, and even enemies. He is also capable of summoning large pillars of stone (identical to the ones that the Golden Gauntlets are able to lift in {{OoT|-}}) to slam or swing into enemies as a combo attack. Due to its elemental nature, the Gauntlets also hold an electrical charge which can be used to attack enemies with or charge the Ball and Chain.

====Variations====
The Lv.1 Gauntlets are the '''Silver Gauntlets'''. It is paired with the Ball and Chain from {{TP}}.

The Lv.2 Gauntlets are the '''Golden Gauntlets'''. It is paired with a golden, spiked version of the Ball and Chain.

The Lv.3 Gauntlets are the '''Power Gloves'''. It is paired with a [[Chain Chomp]] as its Ball and Chain, which bites into enemies and the ground as it is thrown.

The Lv.★ Gauntlets is the '''8-Bit Power Bracelet''', which is only available in {{HW|-}} and {{HW|DE}}. Its gloves are paired with [[Power Bracelet]]s, whose appearance are based from {{TLoZ}}. The 8-Bit Power Bracelet is also accessorized with the [[Raft]] from {{TLoZ|-}} as the Weapon's version of the Ball and Chain. In {{HW|DE|-}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Power Bracelet replaces the Burning Gloves when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.

The Lv.4 Gauntlets are the '''Power Gloves +''', which are only available in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE|-}}. They appear identical to the Power Gloves but are a much stronger Weapon.

The Lv.4+ Gauntlets are the '''Burning Gloves''', which only appear in {{HW|L|-}} and {{HW|DE|-}}. As a Multi-Element Weapon, the Burning Gloves are also associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Fire}}. The Chain Chomp and the Gloves both have a metallic red finish.

=====Special Attack=====
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Link slams the iron ball into the ground, then jumps into the air as he pulls the chain, causing the world to briefly flip upside-down. The world is then flipped back into place and damages enemies nearby. This attack damages a moderate range in all directions around Link.
