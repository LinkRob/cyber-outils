{{Term/Store|Fado||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|OoT3D|Fado (Ocarina of Time)}} is a blonde {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri|link}} girl who lives in {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Forest|link}}. She seems to have a fascination with the {{Term|OoT3D|Lost Woods|link}} and a bond of sorts with {{Term|OoT3D|Mido|link}}.{{Fact}}

When {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} is a child, he can find {{Term|OoT3D|Fado (Ocarina of Time)}} standing atop a lookout post, accessed by bridges leading from the roof of {{Term|OoT3D|Saria's House|link}}. Here, she teaches {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} how to look around in the first-person view,<ref>{{Cite|Hee hee hee! You came all the way up here? You're a real man! Look! Isn't this view pretty? Change your viewpoint with {{Icon|OoT3D View}} so you can look around the forest with {{Icon|OoT3D Circle Pad}}.|Fado (Ocarina of Time)|OoT3D}}</ref> and warns him about the {{Term|OoT3D|Lost Woods}}' dangers.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to go to the Lost Woods, you'd better equip the right gear! Hee hee!|Fado (Ocarina of Time)|OoT}}</ref>

If {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} returns to {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Forest}} as an adult, {{Term|OoT3D|Fado (Ocarina of Time)}} will no longer be found in her home village, instead being encountered in the {{Term|OoT3D|Lost Woods}} during the [[Trading Quest]] to obtain {{Term|OoT3D|Biggoron's Sword|link}}. When {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} brings the {{Term|OoT3D|Odd Poultice|link}} for the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Craftsman's Son|link}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Fado (Ocarina of Time)}} appears instead, demanding that {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} give the medicine to her since it was made of forest {{Plural|Series|Mushroom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That medicine is made of forest mushrooms. Give it back!|Fado (Ocarina of Time)|OoT}}</ref> In return, she gives {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} the {{Term|OoT3D|Poacher's Saw|link}} that the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Craftsman's Son}} left behind. She wonders aloud about the young man's fate, mentioning that any non-{{Plural|OoT3D|Kokiri}} that enter the {{Term|OoT3D|Lost Woods}} wind up turning into {{Plural|OoT3D|Stalfos|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That guy isn't here anymore. Except for the Kokiri, people who come into the forest end up lost. They all become Stalfos. That's why he's not here anymore. Only his saw is left. Hee hee.|Fado (Ocarina of Time)|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Heh heh heh. Are you going to become one...too? Heh heh!|Fado (Ocarina of Time)|OoT3D}}</ref> Following this interaction, {{Term|OoT3D|Fado (Ocarina of Time)}} is never seen again.

==Trivia==
[[File:FadoOOT.jpg|thumb|right|The Fado look-alike's appearance]]
*In early screenshots of {{OoT}}, a female Kokiri with a face very similar to Fado's appears on the {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Shop|link}}'s awning. In the final game, a more generic {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri}} girl occupies this spot.
*Based on investigation into early versions of {{OoT|-}}, it is speculated that {{Term|OoT3D|Fado (Ocarina of Time)}} was planned to be one of the {{Plural|OoT3D|Sage|link}}, possibly related to a wind-based temple that was eventually replaced with the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}}. This theory is further supported by a [[Fado (The Wind Waker)|Kokiri character of the same name]] later appearing as a {{Term|TWWHD|Sage}} in {{TWW}}.
*In the original version of {{OoT|-}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Fado (Ocarina of Time)}} has blue eyes similar to other female {{Plural|OoT3D|Kokiri}}. In {{OoT3D}}, however, she has a unique eye color: the upper halves of her irises are a Bluebonnet, while the lower halves are a light indigo.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|OoT3D|Fado (Ocarina of Time)}}, along with {{Term|OoT3D|Mido|link}}, were named after the {{Wp|solfège}} syllables in the {{Wp|Scale (music)|music scale}}.
