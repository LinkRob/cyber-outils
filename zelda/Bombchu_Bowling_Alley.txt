The {{Term/Store|Bombchu Bowling Alley||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a location and {{Term|OoT3D|Mini-Game|link}} and in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to our cutting-edge amusement center: The Bombchu Bowling Alley!|Bombchu Bowling Alley Operator|OoT}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The entrance to the {{Term|OoT3D|Bombchu Bowling Alley}} is located west of the central area of {{Term|OoT3D|Hyrule Castle Town|link}}. This {{Term|OoT3D|Mini-Game|link}} is only available after defeating {{Term|OoT3D|King Dodongo|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Dodongo's Cavern|link}}. The object is to aim a {{Term|OoT3D|Bombchu|link}} past {{Plural|OoT3D|Cucco|link}} and a {{Term|OoT3D|Blade Trap|link}} into a hole.<ref>{{Cite|OKAY!! Aim for the hole in the center and let Bombchu go! You get ten tries. Ready... LET'S BOWL!|Bombchu Bowling Alley Operator|OoT}}</ref> Prizes rotate seemingly randomly and are only revealed after paying, but before beginning the actual game.<ref>{{Cite|Do you want to know what you can win? Well, it's a secret. I can't tell you until you've paid to play.|Bombchu Bowling Alley Operator|OoT}}</ref> Prizes include a {{Term|OoT3D|Purple Rupee|link}}, a {{Term|OoT3D|Piece of Heart|link}}, 10 {{Plural|OoT3D|Bomb|link}}, 10 {{Plural|OoT3D|Bombchu|link}}, and a {{Term|OoT3D|Large Bomb Bag|link}}.

In {{OoT3D}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Bombchu Bowling Alley}} instead has blue neon lighting. A newspaper shows the {{Term|OoT3D|Bombchu Bowling Alley Operator}}, and several posters depict a {{Term|OoT3D|Goron|link}} and the {{Plural|MM3D|Mask|link}} from {{MM}}.
