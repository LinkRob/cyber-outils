==Overview==
{{Plural|Series|Item}} are anything that is stored within a character's {{Term|Series|Inventory|link}}, often usable multiple times. {{Plural|Series|Item}} are separate from {{Plural|Series|Object}}, which are things that exist outside of {{Plural|Series|Inventory}}. {{Plural|Series|Item}} do not necessarily have to be visible within the {{Term|Series|Inventory}} to be considered {{Plural|Series|Item}}.

Some {{Plural|Series|Item}} may also overlap with characters, enemies, or {{Plural|Series|Object}}. For instance, characters such as {{Term|TAoL|Child|link}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Princess|link}}, enemies such as {{Plural|ALBW|Bee|link}} and {{Plural|TPHD|Bombling|link}}, and objects such as {{Plural|Series|Torch|link}} also double as {{Plural|Series|Item}}.

==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Main|Items in The Legend of Zelda}}
Five different types of {{Plural|TLoZ|Item}} are utilized in {{TLoZ}}. Within the {{Term|TLoZ|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} can select and use up to eight {{Plural|TLoZ|Item}} at any given time. {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} also has access to six other items which activate at contextually specific times, such as the {{Term|TLoZ|Ladder|link}}, which is used whenever {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} approaches small gaps. Other {{Plural|TLoZ|Item}} of this type include the {{Term|TLoZ|Raft|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Book of Magic|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Magical Key|link}}, and the {{Term|TLoZ|Power Bracelet|link}}.

{{Term|TLoZ|Link}}'s {{Term|TLoZ|Inventory}} also contains a space for the {{Term|TLoZ|Triforce Shard|link}} of the {{Term|TLoZ|Triforce of Wisdom|link}}, which is used in the final battle against {{Term|TLoZ|Ganon|link}}.

The fourth set of {{Plural|TLoZ|Item}} are those displayed within the {{Term|TLoZ|HUD|link}}. These are {{Plural|TLoZ|Rupee|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Small Key|link}}, and {{Term|TLoZ|Link}}'s {{Plural|TLoZ|Heart|link}} within the collective {{Plural|TLoZ|Life Gauge|link}}. Like the second set of {{Plural|TLoZ|Item}}, these are also used under certain conditions, such as when purchasing other {{Plural|TLoZ|Item}} or taking damage.

Though they do not appear directly in his {{Term|TLoZ|Inventory}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} can also obtain other {{Plural|TLoZ|Item}} such as the {{Term|TLoZ|Sword|link}}, which is automatically equipped to {{Term|TLoZ|Link}}.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Main|Items in Four Swords Adventures}}
{{Stub|FSA}}
{{Plural|FSA|Item}} are found on {{Plural|FSA|Item Podium|link}} in {{FSA}}; several of them are located in each {{Term|FSA|Stage|link}}. They store {{Plural|FSA|Item}} for {{Term|FSA|Link|link}} to obtain, since he may only hold one {{Term|FSA|Item}} at once. Once {{Term|FSA|Link}} takes the currently-stored {{Term|FSA|Item}}, his previous {{Term|FSA|Item}} will replace it.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Items in The Minish Cap}}
There are three main categories of {{Plural|TMC|Item}} in {{TMC}}: traditional {{Plural|TMC|Item}} that {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can use to overcome obstacles in his adventure (appearing in the {{Term|TMC|Inventory|link}}), quest-related {{Plural|TMC|Item}} that are usually delivered or given to other characters to advance the story (appearing on the {{Term|TMC|Quest Status|link}} screen), and collectible {{Plural|TMC|Item}} such as {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}.

Several {{Plural|TMC|Item}} (the {{Term|TMC|Smith's Sword|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Small Shield|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Boomerang|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Bow|link}}) have different variations or upgrades that can be obtained, though most of them are optional and are not required to complete the game.
