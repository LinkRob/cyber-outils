{{Term/Store|''The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom OST''||Series, TotK}} is the original soundtrack for {{TotK}}. The original soundtrack was officially released on July 31st, 2024.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= An original soundtrack CD of “The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom” will be released by Nippon Columbia on Wednesday, July 31. (Translated from DeepL, original text reads: "『ゼルダの伝説　ティアーズ オブ ザ キングダム』のオリジナルサウンドトラックCDが、日本コロムビアさんより7月31日（水）に発売されることが決まりました。"|published= May 10, 2024 |retrieved= May 10, 2024 |url= https://www.nintendo.com/jp/topics/article/5b23e15b-71df-4877-8164-12c36cb36a84 |site= nintendo.com}}</ref>

There are a total of 344 tracks spanning 9 discs, plus an additional USB disc included with the Special Edition.
