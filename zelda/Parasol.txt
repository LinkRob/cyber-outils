The {{Term/Store|Parasol||Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Agitha]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Light}}. The Parasol, along with her basket containing an assortment of collected bugs in [[Bottle]]s, allows Agitha to summon gusts of wind as well as several [[Golden Bug]]s as part of her moveset. The Bugs that appear include [[Golden Bug#Butterfly|Golden Butterflies]] and [[Golden Bug#Beetle|Golden Beetles]].

==Variations==
The default Parasol is the '''Butterfly Parasol'''. The second form of the Parasol is the '''Luna Parasol''', followed by the '''Princess Parasol'''.

The Lv.★ form of the Parasol is the '''8-Bit Rupee''', which is modeled after the Blue [[Rupee]] from {{TLoZ}}. The basket that Agitha carries is also filled with Blue Rupees. It was added to {{HW|-}} with the {{TP|-}} Pack DLC. In {{HW|DE}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Rupee replaces the Incandescent Parasol when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.

In {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE|-}}, the fourth form of the Parasol is the '''Incandescent Parasol''', which is associated with both the Elements of Light and {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Fire}}.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Agitha floats into the air with the wings on the back of her dress transformed into gigantic Golden Butterfly wings. With a giggle, she gives the wings a mighty flap, blowing out a column of swirling air in front of her, then drops to the ground. This attack deals damage in a wedge-shaped region ahead of Agitha, and carries no Element.{{verify}}
