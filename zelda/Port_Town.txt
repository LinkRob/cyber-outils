{{Term/Store|Port Town||Series}} is a location in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} It is located east of [[Hometown Prairie]] and it is filled with shops.

==Features and Overview==
Port Town is divided in three screens. The first one contains the guard post, a tavern, a fountain, and a [[Jeweller|Jeweler shop]]. The fountain is the residence of the town's guardian [[Fairy]].

===Port Town Guard===
Before [[Tingle]] can gain passage into Port Town, he must pay a fee to the Port Town Guard.<ref>{{Cite|I am the town's Guard! My job is to stop anyone suspicious! Anyone who pays the toll is welcome in this town!|Guard|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That will do nicely, sir! Welcome to the town! You may pass!|Guard|FPTRR}}</ref> After Tingle pays the Guard, he will tell the middle-aged adventurer about the town for a small toll.<ref>{{Cite|Today there is nothing unusual to report, sir!|Guard|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|How much will you pay me, sir?|Guard|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Welcome, sir. This is Port Town, where rupees rule supreme! All of the townsfolk love rupees. The more rupees you pay, the more they will tell you! That's all, sir!|Guard|FPTRR}}</ref>

===Tavern===

===Fountain===
The fountain is the residence of Port Town's guardian Fairy. After Tingle has completed the [[Hero's Shrine]], [[Dave]] can be found beside it, talking of his brother.

===Chef===
Tingle can pay a toll to the Chef,<ref>{{Cite|Anyone with rupees is okay with me... No rupees? No good...|Chef|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|From this moment on, you and I are friends!|Chef|FPTRR}}</ref> who will purchase food from the middle-aged adventurer and provide Ingredient tips.

===Armourer===
{{Main|Armourer}}
Until he is paid a small fee, the Armourer will treat Tingle rudely.<ref>{{Cite|Who do you think you are? Don't you know how things work round here?|Armourer|FPTRR}}</ref> After Tingle pays him, the Armourer will sell him weapons, shields and armor.<ref>{{Cite|I think you and I are going to get along just fine! Come and visit my shop anytime!|Armourer|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Weapons! Shields! Armour! I love them all!</ref> Tingle can alternatively sell these materials to the Armourer instead.

Tingle can purchase an Empty Jar from the Armourer after selling fireworks to him.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! Now those are some real pretty fireworks! I'll be happy to buy these from you, so keep them coming! Oh, one more thing... I'm sure I have an empty jar lying around. If you want to know more I'm always happy to talk.|Armourer|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Want an empty jar? I'll sell you one! But it's going to cost more than I gave you for the fireworks!|Armourer|FPTRR}}</ref> While Tingle can bargain a price for it, the Armourer will keep any amount of [[Rupee]]s offered to him, even if it's too low.<ref>{{Cite|How much will you pay? A word of warning: Even if your offer is too low, I'm still going to take the rupees! So what's your offer?|Armourer|FPTRR}}</ref>

===Jeweller===
{{Main|Jeweller}}
For an initial small fee, the Port Town Jeweller will purchase gems from him.<ref>{{Cite|Let me see how brightly your heart sparkles!|Jeweller|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Year heart sparkles like the very finest rupee! I'm the Jeweller. If you find any gems, I'll value them for you!|Jeweller|FPTRR}}</ref>

===Tailor Shop===
The Tailor Shop is run by [[Chiko's Mum]]. When Tingle first speaks to her, she will tell him about her daughter's affinity for Fairies for a small fee.

===Map-Seller===
{{Main|Map-Seller}}
The Map-Seller gives Tingle a [[map]] of Homestead Prairie, tasking the middle-aged adventurer with the job of marking the location of significant landmarks so she may sell her maps.

===Playground===
The Playground is a small area reserved for Port Town's children. The children are among the few that are not affected by the power of [[Uncle Rupee]] that is enhancing the greed of everyone (including the rest of Port Town).
