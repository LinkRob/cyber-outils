{{Term/Store|Grizzlemaw Bear|Grizzlemaw Bears|Series, BotW}} are [[Creature]]s in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Grizzlemaw Bear}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Grizzlemaw Bear}}
Grizzlemaw Bears are a rarer and stronger variant of the more common [[Honeyvore Bear]]s. They are physically distinguished by their darker fur and gray muzzles.<ref>{{Cite|This breed of honeyvore bear is distinguished by its gray fur.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They are highly aggressive and territorial animals, and are considered to the most dangerous wild animals native to [[Hyrule]] during the [[Era of the Wilds]].<ref>{{Cite|Even more ferocious than their relatives, they're commonly believed to be the most dangerous wild animal.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They can be found in the [[Hebra Mountains]] and [[Gerudo Highlands]].

Grizzlemaw Bears chiefly inhabit snow-covered highland areas, such as the [[Hebra Mountains]] and [[Gerudo Highlands]]. They're one of a few animals that can be ridden and tamed by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, but they can't be registered at a [[Stable]]. {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} can also be found riding Grizzlemaw Bears in the Gerudo Highlands. When killed, they drop [[Raw Prime Meat]], [[Raw Gourmet Meat]], and [[Hearty Salmon]].
