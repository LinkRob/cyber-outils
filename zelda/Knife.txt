The '''Knife''' is an item in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Knife is obtained after [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] defeats both [[Goriya]]s in a room of the [[Shrine of Illusion]]. The Knife is used by Zelda in the same room to cut open the wall canvas so that she can progress. The Knife has to be used each time Zelda enters the room, as the hole created by her seals up after she leaves.
