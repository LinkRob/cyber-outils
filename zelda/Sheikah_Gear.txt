The {{Term/Store|Sheikah Gear|Sheikah Gear|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 170}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Sheikah Gear}} is a series of weaponry made using techniques passed down within the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link|display=Sheikah Tribe}}, including {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}.<ref name="Eightfold Blade">{{Cite|{{Big|Eightfold Blade}}<br/>A single-edged sword traditional to the Sheikah tribe. Forged using ancient technology, it's said to be among the sharpest conventional weapons ever made.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Eightfold Longblade">{{Cite|{{Big|Eightfold Longbalde}}<br/>A single-edged sword seldom seen in Hyrule. This weapon is passed down through the Sheikah tribe and has an astonishingly sharp edge ideal for slicing.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Edge of Duality}}<br/>A curious double-edged sword crafted using Sheikah technology. It was originally made for Hyrulean knights unfamiliar with single-edged blades.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Serpentine Spear}}<br/>The spearhead of this weapon is uniquely Sheikah in design. Spear masters of the Sheikah tribe can use the crescent shape to snag their opponents and deliver brutal cuts.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Phrenic Bow}}<br/>A bow passed down through the Sheikah tribe. Concentrating before drawing the string will allow you to target distant enemies as easily as those nearby.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Their blades are notable for possessing a single cutting edge and for being remarkably sharp.<ref name="Eightfold Blade"/><ref name="Eightfold Longblade"/>

When scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Sheik|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a piece of {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Gear}}.

While the {{Term|BotW|Phrenic Bow|link}} and [[Shield of the Mind's Eye]] are wooden, all {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Gear}} blades are made out of metal. This means that they will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. Finally, the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Gear}} will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
