The {{Term/Store|Ordon Sword||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a [[Sword]] in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133}}</ref> Forged by [[Rusl]], it is inlaid with the horns of an [[Ordon Goat]].<ref>{{Cite|'''Ordon Sword:''' Rusl crafted this fine sword. It is inlaid with Ordon goat horns. Swing with [B] or sheathe with [A].|N/A|TP}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Ordon Sword was the blade that [[Link]] was meant to bring to [[Hyrule]] to present to the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]], before he was dragged into the [[Twilight]]. There, after meeting [[Midna]], she tells Link that he must find her a suitable Sword and a [[Shield]]. Link finds the [[Ordon Shield]] in [[Jaggle]] and [[Pergie]]'s house in [[Ordon Village]], and obtains the Ordon Sword in Rusl's house. Since Link cannot use the Sword in [[Wolf Link|wolf form]], Midna holds onto them for him after finding the weapons to be useless to her. 

The Sword and Shield are automatically equipped when Link returns to his human form. If Link talks to {{Term|TPHD|Uli|link}} after he returns to human form, she will comment that  "monsters" stole the Sword and that Link got it back from them, not knowing that it was he who took it while in wolf form.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Link... That sword... The evil creatures stole it from Rusl...but you... You got it back?! How wonderful! I know it is safe in your hands... And you need it now, yes? Please keep using it, Link.|Uli|TP}}</ref> Link can equip this Sword anytime he wishes, even after he has acquired the [[Master Sword]]. The Ordon Sword, however, has no effect on [[Zant]] or [[Ganondorf]].

Rusl uses a Sword similar to the Ordon Sword as his weapon at the beginning of the game, and when he meets up again with Link as part of [[The Resistance]].
