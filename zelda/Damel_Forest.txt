The {{Term/Store|Damel Forest||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Damel Forest'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Damel Forest}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}. It lies north of both the {{Term|BotW|Zonai Ruins|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Harker Lake|link}}. Approaching from the south, the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Damel Forest}} is flanked by two large, carven stone pillars. An ancient road of flagstone and dirt passes through these into a wide clearing, where several {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} are camped. There are a handful of {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}} present in the clearing, gathered together on its western flank. On nearly all sides, the forest is thick with vegetation. To the west lie the tall {{Term|BotW|Farosh Hills|link}}, forming a natural barrier. It is often overcast or rainy in this particular environment; the ground is saturated as a result, evidenced by the presence of many puddles. Northward and then east, past the first {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}} and the clearing sits a second clearing space. Here is another {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}, coupled with a handful of metal {{Plural|BotW|Crate|link}} strewn on the ground. This second camp boasts a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} near a small pond. From here, the old forest road veers to the southeast, where it meets the winding {{Term|BotW|Dracozu River|link}}. Its path is watched over by several stone {{Term|BotW|Dragon|link}} statues. Another wooded hill encircles from the north and boasts similar fare. From this vantage point, the grand ruins at {{Term|BotW|Dracozu Lake|link}} are visible. In a small cave blocked with a breakable rock wall on top of the wooded hill, on its eastern flank, are two regular {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} and one {{Term|BotW|Rare Ore Deposit|link}}.

The first aforementioned {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}} has a wooden {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a bundle of {{Plural|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}},{{How Many}} as well as a remote, metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} on a raised platform containing a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Spear|link}}. The second camp has a raised platform housing a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, which contains a single {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.
