{{Term/Store|Bit|Bits|Series, TAoL|plural}} are enemies in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 167}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|TAoL|Bit (Red) Bot (Blue)|A jelly-like monster that wobbles about. Watch out! Bot jumps.}}
Bits are red jelly-like monsters, similar to their blue counterpart, the [[Bot]]s. They are short, meaning [[Link]] has to crouch to strike them. They are weak and will not cause much damage. Link can defeat them with two strikes of the Sword. Unlike Bots, they do not jump, instead, they wiggle like Bots do before jumping. They move faster along the ground than Bots. They are much more rare than Bots, only found in the areas surrounding the [[North Palace]] and certain caves in the [[Death Mountain]] area.

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese version of the game, more Bits appear in the enemy encounters on the plains, and they give 1 experience rather than 2 like in other versions of the game.
