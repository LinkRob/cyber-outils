{{Term/Store|Waveblade||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Waveblade}} is the fourth youngest member of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}. He can be found in {{Term|TMC|Waveblade's Dojo|link}}, located on the southwest side of {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, which is accessible after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}. He teaches the {{Term|TMC|Peril Beam|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now you will learn a dangerous technique for desperate times! The {{Color|TMC Red|Peril Beam}}!|Waveblade|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Waveblade}} will not train {{Term|TMC|Link}} unless he has more than ten {{Plural|TMC|Heart Container|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It seems you are not ready to train here! Come back when you have {{Color|TMC Red|more than 10 hearts}}!|Waveblade|TMC}}</ref> There is a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} inside his dojo.

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Waveblade}}, opening a waterfall in {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}} and revealing {{Term|TMC|Greatblade|link}}'s dojo.
