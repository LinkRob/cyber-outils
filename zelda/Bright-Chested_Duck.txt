==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Bright-Chested Duck}}

Bright-Chested Ducks are common [[bird]]s which can be found in small flocks in bodies of water throughout [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|These birds can be found on waterfronts all over Hyrule.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They spend most of their time in the water, paddling with their webbed feet, and occasionally dive the fronts of their bodies underwater to feed. When startled, the flock will take to the wing and fly away. Their meat is very fatty, and is considered to be high-quality food.<ref>{{Cite|Their fatty meat is high quality.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When killed, they drop a [[Raw Bird Drumstick]].
