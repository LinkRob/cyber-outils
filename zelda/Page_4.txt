==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|RTBToL}}
Page 4 is a dark forest, which [[Peter]] takes Tingle to. There are several abandoned buildings located throughout it, and the wildlife abounds. This page is where Tingle first meets [[Lion]], who joins his part at the end of the page. [[Segāre]] also makes his first appearance when he cuts down the rope bridge that Tingle needs to cross.
