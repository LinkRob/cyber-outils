{{Term/Store|Blob|Blobs|Series, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|Enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 167 {{Exp Game|ALBW, TFH}}}}</ref> They are non-electric variants of [[Buzz Blob]].

==Characteristics==
Blobs appear as slow-moving piles of a jelly-like substance which resemble the green [[Buzz Blob]] from games prior to {{ALBW}}. Following the introduction of this non-electric variety, the original Buzz Blobs gain a yellow glow and emit sparks to distinguish themselves.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW|-}}, Blobs appear mainly in grassy areas of [[Hyrule]], such as the [[Lost Woods]], the [[Graveyard]], the [[Southern Ruins]], and the forests near [[Link's House]]. Blobs pose little threat to [[Link]] and can be dispatched with two hits from the [[Forgotten Sword]] or one hit from the [[Master Sword]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, Blobs appear in various [[Level]] of the [[Woodlands]] and [[Riverside]]. They frequently appear in groups, by popping up from inside the ground when the [[Links]] approach them. They will chase the Links more aggressively than in previous appearances, but are otherwise easy to dispatch with two hits from Link's basic [[Sword]]. During certain [[Drablands Challenge]], many Blobs are replaced by [[Buzz Blob]].

Blobs also appear in the [[Forest Zone]] of the [[Den of Trials]], along with a stronger, purple variant known as Dark Blobs, which can temporarily [[Curse]] the Links upon dealing damage.
