'''Darknut Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Darknut Lv. 2|This elite monster wears bright armor that protects against most attacks. Its powerful axe cleaves through foes.<ref name="EoW Darknut Lv. 2">{{Cite|This elite monster wears bright armor that protects against most attacks. Its powerful axe cleaves through foes.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Darknut Lv. 2 are stronger, blue-armored versions of the regular [[Darknut]], with increased health and damage. When summoned as an [[Echo]], they have an Echo cost of four.

The Darknut Lv. 2 also comes in a [[Dark Echo|Dark variant]] upon coming in contact with a [[Tecuum]].
