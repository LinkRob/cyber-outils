{{Term/Store|Ambrosia Lite||Series, TLoZ (Valiant Comics)}} is an item in {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}.<ref name="Spilled">''Hey! You [Daira] spilled my Ambrosia Lite!'' - Link (''Nintendo Comics System Issue #7'')</ref>

==Location and Uses==
It is a [[Calatia]] special that is brewed on [[Arn]] and [[Medilia]]'s farm.<ref>''Calatia's favorite drink'' - Ambrosia Lite Advertisement (Valiant Comics #5'')</ref><ref>''From the vineyards of Arn and Medila'' - Ambrosia Lite Advertisement (Valiant Comics #5'')</ref> It is first introduced in a bonus part of Issue #5. [[Link]] is a fan of it.<ref>''Link loves it!'' - Arn (Valiant Comics #5'')</ref> The beverage is consumed by both humans and monsters,<ref>''Whether you're a dashing adventurer or a big ugly monster... the great taste of Ambrosia Lite<sup><small>tm</small></sup> satisfies everyone!'' - Ambrosia Lite Advertisement (Valiant Comics #5'')</ref> and an advertisement indicates that it is a warrior's drink. The advertisement also displays a [[Daira]] in a suggestively provocative position.

In Nintendo Comics System Issue #7, the second bonus story titled ''The Perfect Date 2'' covers a rather disastrous date with Link and [[Princess Zelda]], in which Link engages in a bar fight after a Daira knocks over his Ambrosia Lite.<ref name="Spilled"/>
