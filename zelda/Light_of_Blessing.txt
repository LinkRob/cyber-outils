==Location and Uses==
Light of Blessings are crystals obtained from completing [[Shrine of Light]]s. [[Link]] can pray to [[Goddess Statue]]s to upgrade either his total [[Heart]]s or [[Stamina Vessel]]s in exchange for four Light of Blessings. There are a total of 152 Light of Blessings to be obtained, which allows Link to receive 38 upgrades.
