'''Sunken Treasures''' are recurring [[item]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They first appear in {{TWW}} and return in {{PH}} and {{BotW}}.

==Location and uses==
Sunken Treasure is a type of [[treasure]] recovered from the ocean floor, usually being contained inside of a [[Sunken Chest]].
