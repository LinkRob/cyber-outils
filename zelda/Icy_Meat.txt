==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Icy Meat}} are the result of {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}} that has been frozen by {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}. They can also be found in {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. Eating one will restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and grant temporary {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>
