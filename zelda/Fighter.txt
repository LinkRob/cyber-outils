{{Term/Store|Fighter|Fighters|Series, SSBU|plural}} are recurring characters in {{TLoZ|Series}}, all originally from their own individual franchises. In Super Smash Bros., they are who the player controls and fights as. Many characters from {{TLoZ|-}} make appearances as playable characters in Super Smash Bros., such as Link and Zelda. Although no {{Plural|Series|Fighter}} not debuting in {{TLoZ|-}} have any roles story-wise, there have been some that were present in the games as Easter eggs.

==Overview==
==={{SSB|-}}===
In {{SSB|-}}, the only character from {{TLoZ}} who appears on the roster is Link. The character other than Link on the Smash 64 roster who made cameo appearances in Zelda games to be released on the [[Nintendo 64|same console]] is Mario. He was an Easter egg found in {{OoT}} in a portrait alongside other stars from his franchise and {{MM}} as one of the [[Happy Mask Salesman]]'s {{Plural|MM3D|Mask|link}} on his pack.
