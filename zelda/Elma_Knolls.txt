The {{Term/Store|Elma Knolls||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Elma Knolls'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Elma Knolls are a plateau in the [[Great Hyrule Forest]] that is situated south of [[Aldor Foothills]] and east of [[Lake Mekar]]. The Knolls create a series of flat rocks leading to a top plateau.

On top of the Knolls are two [[Korok]]s. The first is located in the center of the plateau and requires [[Link]] to stand on a [[Tree]] stump with a small pinwheel placed on it. Once he does so, he must then shoot three [[Balloon]]s flying around the surrounding Trees. Upon successfully doing so, the Korok will reveal himself and give Link a [[Korok Seed]] for finding him. The second Korok, in an eastern, lower section of the plateau, is simply hiding under a [[Rock]] within a ring of dead Trees.

==Nomenclature==
Elma Knolls is named for [[Elma]], a [[Korok]] from {{TWW}}.
