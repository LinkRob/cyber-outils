{{Term/Store|Spicy Elixir|Spicy Elixirs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Elixir}}<br/>Warms your body from its core, increasing your resistance to cold environments. ==Location and Uses==
Drinking Spicy Elixirs will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}} by warming his body to the core.<ref name="Inventory"/> They're useful in cold environments and snow-covered mountains,<ref name="Inventory"/> such as {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}.

Spicy Elixirs can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|Series|Summerwing Butterfly|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Warm Darner|link}}

By adding any of these spicy ingredients, Link can make a Spicy Elixir that will also heal him:

* {{Term|Series|Spicy Pepper|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Sunshroom|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Warm Safflina|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Sizzlefin Trout|link}}
