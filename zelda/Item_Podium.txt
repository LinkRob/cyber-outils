==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|FSA|Item Podium|You'll find many different items on these stands. Press the A Button to pick up the item on the podium or exchange it with {{Typo|item|the item}} you are holding. You can also use them when you want to switch items with a friend.}}
Several {{Plural|FSA|Item Podium}} are located in various locations in each {{Term|FSA|Stage|link}}. They store {{Plural|FSA|Item|link}} for {{Term|FSA|Link|link}} to obtain, since he may only hold one {{Term|FSA|Item}} at once. Once {{Term|FSA|Link}} takes the currently-stored {{Term|FSA|Item}}, his previous {{Term|FSA|Item}} will replace it.
