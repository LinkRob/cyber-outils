The {{Term/Store|White Sword-Giving Old Man||Series, TLoZ}} is a character in {{TLoZ}}.<ref name="E 219"/>

==Biography==
The {{Term|TLoZ|White Sword-Giving Old Man}} is found in a cave in the northeast of {{Term|TLoZ|Hyrule|link}} near a waterfall, which is guarded by a {{Term|TLoZ|Lynel|link}}. He will provide {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} with the {{Term|TLoZ|White Sword|link}} once he has a minimum of five {{Plural|TLoZ|Heart Container|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|MASTER USING IT AND YOU CAN HAVE THIS.|White Sword-Giving Old Man|TLoZ}}</ref>
