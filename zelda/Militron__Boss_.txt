{{Term/Store|Militron||Series, TFoE}} (pronounced {{IPA|ˈmɪlɪtɹɒn}} {{Respell|MI|li-tron}})<ref>{{Cite|Look! How Militron makes his warriors.|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref> is the third boss in {{TFoE}}. He is located in the {{Term|TFoE|Militron (Stage)|link}} Stage, and is encountered again at [[Ganon's Lair]].

==Biography==
Militron is an armored knight who is a minion of [[Ganon]]. He is first encountered at his Face of Evil, the {{Term|TFoE|Militron (Stage)|link}} area. He possesses the ability to turn [[Koridian]]s into [[Armos|stone soldier]]s by crushing them in his hand and exposing them to fire, which he then sends to attack [[Link]].<ref>{{Cite|Worthless Koridian, you must be hardened with fire! Go and kill!|Militron (Boss)|TFoE}}</ref> When Link defeats Militron, his armor explodes, revealing himself to be nothing more than a fat old man. He then makes his escape, mortified by his exposure.<ref>{{Cite|Oh my goodness, this is awful!|Militron (Boss)|TFoE}}</ref>

He is encountered again in [[Ganon's Lair]], and is fought the same way.
