{{Term/Store|Lake Akkala||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lake Akkala'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala}} is the largest lake in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} Region. It is located in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}, where it is fed by the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Falls|link}}. The western shore of {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala}} is flanked by the {{Term|BotW|Torin Wetland|link}}. A peninsula cuts into {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala}}, connecting {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}} with the rest of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands}} to the east. The southern side of the [[Lake]] contains underwater ruins, which hide three metallic {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. The {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} contain five {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Silver Bow|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Silver Shield|link}}. Another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found in the waters beneath the middle waterfall of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Falls}}, containing a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}, valued at 300 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. A second {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} that rests in the water north of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Raft|link}} can be found on the northern bank of the Lake. Near this {{Term|BotW|Raft}} is a sunken wagon, which serves no purpose. {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone Deposit|link}} can be found along the banks of {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala}}. {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}}'s shop, the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}}, can be found underneath the land bridge connecting to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}} after 9:00 at night once {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has met {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link}}.
