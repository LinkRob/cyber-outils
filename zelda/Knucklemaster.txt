'''Knucklemaster''' is a [[boss]] in {{ALBW}}<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=188 }}</ref> and is one of the seven bosses in which the Player(s) can fight in any order in the second act of the game. It appears as a hand encased in a metal gauntlet, with an eye in its palm.

==Characteristics==
[[File:ALBW Knucklemaster Screenshot.png|thumb|left|250px|A screenshot of Knucklemaster in {{ALBW|-}}]]
Knucklemaster's main attack involves clutching into a fist and ramming [[Link]]. This attack can only be avoided by jumping out of the way with the [[Tornado Rod]], or [[Wall Merging]]. This causes Knucklemaster to crash into the wall and get stunned, exposing its vulnerable eye. After recovering, it will attempt to squash Link repeatedly. This is not only dangerous on its own, but it also destroys the fragile parts of the floor, making it harder to maneuver. After sustaining enough damage, Knucklemaster will change colors, get faster and attempt to squash Link more often, but the strategy to defeat it remains the same. Upon the monster's defeat, a passageway will be revealed, leading to Seres's [[Painting]].
{{clear|left}}
==Nomenclature==
Knucklemaster is a portmanteau of [[Iron Knuckle]] and [[Wallmaster]].

== Trivia ==

* Knucklemaster has the distances for being the only Eye-Based boss in the second act of ''The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds'' to not utilize any projectile-based attacks. [[Arrghus]] can launch Arrgi with energy and later on fire energy lasers that will boil the water beneath the Player(s), and [[Dharkstare]] can launch out three orbs of Ice, which will form a triangle around Link and even firing the orbs like projectiles at Link.
* During the phases were Knucklemaster use's it's hand slam move, a lot of Players have found an easy exploit as to not have it destroy any parts of the floor. The Player(s) can simply merge to a wall with no broken floor and have the Knucklemaster preform the hand slam move all the while merging with the wall with no broken floor to not only avoid damage, but also have it were Knucklemaster does not destroy any parts of the floor.
* Knucklemaster shares a strong reassembles to Master Hand from the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series, another franchise created by Nintendo.
* Knucklemaster has the second highest HP out of any boss in the game, having 84 HP in total.
