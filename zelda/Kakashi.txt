{{Name|Japanese|Kakashi}} is a character in {{RTBToL}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi}} is one of {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}}'s partners in the picture book world, beneath {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki|link}} and [[Lion (Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love)|Lion]]. The cheerful scarecrow is mentally lacking and is in search for intelligence. He's the smallest of {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}'s three friends and is able to get into small spaces due to his size. {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi}} joins {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}'s quest on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 2|link}}. On {{Term|RTBToL|Page 11|link}}, he is kidnapped by {{Term|RTBToL|Segāre|link}}, and hidden in a cave behind {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|link}}.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi}} is based on the {{wp|Scarecrow (Oz)|Scarecrow}} from ''{{wp|The Wizard of Oz}}''.
