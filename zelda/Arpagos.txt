{{Term/Store|Arpagos|Arpagos|Series|plural}} are recurring flying dragon-like enemies in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|TFoE, TWoG}} Specifically, they appear in [[Link: The Faces of Evil|The Faces of Evil]] and [[Zelda: The Wand of Gamelon|The Wand of Gamelon]], and are the most common flying enemy.

==Characteristics==
Arpagos are airborne enemies that attack [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] from above. As with most enemies in the games they appear, they have a tendency of spawning frequently and in large flocks. They have little health, but their speed, numbers and flight still make them a threat, especially while platforming. In {{TFoE|-}}, the [[Bell#The Faces of Evil|Bell]] can briefly freeze them when rung. The [[Flute#The Wand of Gamelon|Flute]] in {{TWoG|-}} has an identical effect on them. In {{TWoG|-}} only, Zelda can receive free [[Bread#The Wand of Gamelon|Bread]] from [[Harbanno]] the baker in [[Sakado]] after rescuing him from [[Tykogi Tower]], which can be thrown to distract Arpagos for a brief period of time.

Two items in {{TWoG|-}} are related to these enemies. The [[Arpagos Egg]] can be found in the tower behind [[Mayor Cravendish]]'s house in Sakado and is given to the [[Hungry Girl]] in [[Kobitan]] in exchange for the Flute. The [[Arpagos Feather]] can be found in [[Gobiyan Ship]], and is given to [[Myra]] in [[Hanyu Forest]] to combine with Zelda's [[Shroud]] to make the [[Magic Cloak]].
