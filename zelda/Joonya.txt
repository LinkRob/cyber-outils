==Biography==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Stub|LA}}
{{Term|LANS|Joonya}} is one of {{Term|LANS|Mamasha|link}}’s and {{Term|LANS|Papahl|link}}’s five children.<ref>{{Cite|Yep! Those're my boys! I'm Papahl. Pleased ta meetcha!|Papahl|LANS}}</ref> With the exception of his baby sibling {{Term|LANS|Joonya}} and his brothers look almost identical to each other, a fact that sometimes confuses their mother.<ref>{{Cite|With four boys who look alike, even I get confused sometimes...|Mamasha|LANS}}</ref>

Joonya can normally be seen tossing a ball back and forth with his brother {{Term|LANS|Kidoh|link}} at the southern entrance to {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|link}}. However, once Link returns after having completed {{Term|LANS|Tail Cave|link}}, {{Term|LANS|Joonya}} and his brother {{Term|LANS|Kidoh|link}} will rush to meet him and in a panic, and will inform him that while he was gone a gang of {{Plural|LANS|Moblin|link}} came to the village<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, it is! The {{Color|LANS Pink|moblins}} came to the village!|Joonya|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yeah, that's right! A whole gang of {{Color|LANS Pink|moblins}}!|Kidoh|LANS}}</ref> and did something at {{Term|LANS|Madam MeowMeow's House|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Then... It's for real! They all went to the house...|Joonya|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yeah, that house, and then they did something at {{Color|LANS Blue|BowWow's house}}!|Kidoh|LANS}}</ref> though their panicked state prevents them from being able to give him any more information.<ref>{{Cite|It might be faster to find out for yourself what happened!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

Like all his siblings {{Term|LANS|Joonya}} will give {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} useful information that he himself does understand.<ref>{{Cite|I heard you can press {{Icon|LANS -}} to look at the island map... Not only that, you can stick pins in it with {{Icon|LANS A}} and remember stuff with {{Icon|LANS X}}. But I don't understand what they mean by that...|Joonya|LANS}}</ref>
