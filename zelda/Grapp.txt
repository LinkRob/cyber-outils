{{Term/Store|Grapp||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Grapp|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
Grapp is an elderly {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} who is found at the {{Term|BotW|Goron Hot Springs|link}} sitting in one of its pools. He dislikes being disturbed while taking he is taking his bath.<ref>{{Cite|Who's there?! Who's disturbing my bath?|Grapp|BotW}}</ref> His grandson {{Term|BotW|Dugby|link}} competes with him to see who can stay in the {{Plural|BotW|Hot Spring|link}} the longest.<ref name="Dugby"/> Grapp expresses that he feels the water there is only lukewarm and would like to bathe in much hotter water, but is unable to due to the {{Plural|BotW|Magma Bomb|link}} ahead.<ref>{{Cite|Lukewarm. I'd like to find some reeeally hot water to relax in...but those fallin' rocks make it way too dangerous.|Grapp|BotW}}</ref>

Grapp warns {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to stay away from the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Eldin|link}}, as the area past that point is incredibly hot.<ref>{{Cite|But a Hylian like you should stay away from any place hotter than here. That goes double for the area past the {{Color|BotW Red|Bridge of Eldin}}. It's scorching there!|Grapp|BotW}}</ref> However, he also admits that the Bridge is inaccessible anyway as {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}} lowered it to prevent the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link|display=Divine Beast Vah Rudania's}} advance.<ref>{{Cite|In any case, gettin' across the {{Color|BotW Red|Bridge of Eldin}} is impossible right now. The {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} probably raised {{Color|BotW Red|the bridge}} to keep {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}} from comin' over here.|Grapp|BotW}}</ref>

If Link is wearing at least two pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Set|link}}, Grapp will be impressed with him.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, I don't see many tourists like you equipped to stand up to this heat. I'm impressed!|Grapp|BotW}}</ref> However, he warns him to stay away from Vah Rudania regardless.<ref>{{Cite|Even still, don't you go near {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}}. If ya do, no matter how well prepared ya are... you'll be burned to ashes!|Grapp|BotW}}</ref> After Link has completed the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, Grapp will encourage him to spend as much time in {{Term|BotW|Hot Spring}} as he would like.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}} has settled down, so feel free to take your time soakin' in the hot springs.|Grapp|BotW}}</ref>
