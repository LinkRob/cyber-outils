{{Plural|BotW|Forest Dweller's Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} made by {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} specifically for {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} to use.<ref name="Inventory"/> This is common among {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear|link}}, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Korok}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> They are only found in three locations in and around the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}, though an additional one can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Floria Falls|link}}. They are made from the finest hard wood in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, making them deceivingly sturdy.<ref name="Inventory"/>

As part of "{{Term|BotW|The Test of Wood|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Damia|link}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Shield}}, {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Sword|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Bow|link}} he must use to fight his way to the {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But it's not complicated—all you need to do is equip the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok weapon set}} and reach the shrine at the back of the area.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> If he switches out or breaks any of these {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}}, the trial must be restarted.<ref>{{Cite|You have to keep the {{Color|BotW Red|sword}}, {{Color|BotW Red|bow}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|shield}} equipped the whole time. If they break or you unequip them, you're out!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Forest Dweller's Shield}} will burn to a crisp if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} drops them in {{Term|BotW|Water|link}}, they will float. They also do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} in {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} like a metal shield would.
