The {{Term/Store|Qaza Tokki Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Qaza Tokki Shrine}}<br/>Qaza Tokki's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Qaza Tokki's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Qaza Tokki Shrine is located  in the northeast of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, in the center of the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth|link}}. Upon entering the labyrinth, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is greeted by a mysterious voice that informs him he will receive a blessing for navigating the maze,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You who have reached this point... Proceed onward through the maze and you will receive a blessing as reward...}}|Qaza Tokki|BotW}}</ref> initiating the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Trial on the Cliff|link}}". The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is initially surrounded by portcullises, preventing Link from easily reaching it.

After successfully making his way through the labyrinth, Link finds some ladders and a tunnel that takes him to a walkway above the Shrine, allowing him to drop down and enter. As Link enters the Shrine, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Qaza Tokki|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the Shrine is proof of Link's worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Qaza Tokki. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Qaza Tokki|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Qaza Tokki Shrine features no puzzles, simply a direct path to Qaza Tokki. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Term|BotW|Barbarian Leg Wraps|link}}. As Link approaches Qaza Tokki, he's given a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the Shrine.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Qaza Tokki|BotW}}</ref> Qaza Tokki wishes Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Qaza Tokki|BotW}}</ref>
