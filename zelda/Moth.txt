{{Term/Store|Moth|Moths|Series, MM, MM3D, OoS, OoA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''''Moth'''''|book= E |publisher= |page= 194}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Moth}} were referred to as '''Butterflies''' in {{Guide|OoA|BradyGames}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA |publisher= BradyGames |page= 30 |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Plural|MM3D|Moth}} can be found throughout {{Term|MM3D|Woodfall Temple|link}} in {{MM}}. They are commonly seen flying around lit {{Plural|MM3D|Torch|link}}, at which time they pose no threat to {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}}. However, if he uses the {{Term|MM3D|Torch}} to light a {{Term|MM3D|Deku Stick|link}}, they will follow the lit {{Term|MM3D|Deku Stick}} and attack {{Term|MM3D|Link}} if they come close enough. {{Plural|MM3D|Moth}} will continue to follow {{Term|MM3D|Link}} until either the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Stick|display=Stick}} burns up completely or it is put away.

{{Plural|MM3D|Moth}} are also summoned by {{Term|MM3D|Odolwa|link}}. In this battle, they are much more aggressive and will actively seek out {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to harm him. If he dons the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Mask|link}} and transforms into {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link}}, they will still follow him but will be unable to damage him.{{Verify|MM}} {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can defeat the {{Plural|MM3D|Moth}} by throwing a {{Term|MM3D|Bomb|link}} or picking a {{Term|MM3D|Bomb Flower|link}}, which will cause the {{Plural|MM3D|Moth}} to flock to the explosive. Alternatively, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can wait until {{Term|MM3D|Odolwa}} creates a ring of fire, which will cause the {{Plural|MM3D|Moth}} to burn up. 

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Plural|OoS|Moth}} appear during the battle with {{Term|OoS|Mothula|link}} in the {{Term|OoS|Poison Moth's Lair|link}} in {{OoS}}. They fly around the room, attempting to hit {{Term|OoS|Link}} and push him down the holes, thus forcing {{Term|OoS|Link}} to restart the battle.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Plural|OoA|Moth}} are summoned by the {{Term|OoA|Shadow Hag|link}} in the {{Term|OoA|Moonlit Grotto|link}} in {{OoA}}. Once all of her shadows merge back into one, she releases these bugs to prevent {{Term|OoA|Link}} from shooting her with the {{Term|OoA|Seed Shooter|link}}.
