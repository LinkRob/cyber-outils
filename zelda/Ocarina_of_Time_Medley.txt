The "{{Term/Store|Ocarina of Time Medley||Series, SSBfN3DS, SSBfWU, SSBU}}" is a [[Song]] in {{SSB4}} and {{SSBU}}.

==Overview==
==={{SSB4|-}}===
The "{{Term|SSBfWU|Ocarina of Time Medley}}" is composed of nine Songs from {{OoT}}. In {{SSB4|3DS|-}}, it plays on the [[Gerudo Valley]] stage. In {{SSB4|Wii U|-}}, it plays on the [[Bridge of Eldin]] and [[Pirate Ship]] Stages.

The "{{Term|SSBfWU|Ocarina of Time Medley}}" is the ninth track in disc 2 of ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS/Wii U: A Smashing Soundtrack''.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
The "{{Term|SSBU|Ocarina of Time Medley}}" plays in any Stage related to {{TLoZ|Series}}.
