==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Courser Bee Honey}}
In {{BotW}}, {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee Honey}} resembles beehives and can be found on various {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which resemble {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Tree}} but bear no fruit. It can be found in various regions, but appear a fair bit in places such as {{Term|BotW|Necluda|link}}, with three being found directly next to {{Term|BotW|Calip|link|display=Calip's}} {{Term|BotW|House|link}}. Each hive is guarded by a swarm of {{Plural|BotW|Courser Bee|link}} that will do a quarter {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} of damage and take two hits to scare off. They can be eaten to restore two {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cooked}} they provide {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}} that restore {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Courser Bee Honey}} can also be used at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} to make {{Term|BotW|Orange Dye|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Koko's Specialty|link}}" requires {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to retrieve {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee}} Honey for {{Term|BotW|Koko|link}} so she can make a {{Term|BotW|Honeyed Apple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What should Koko make for dessert? {{Big|OH!}} Koko will cook a {{Color|BotW Blue|honeyed apple}}! Mother loved those! It melts in your mouth and is sooo delicious!|Koko|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Let's see... the ingredients are... an {{Color|BotW Blue|apple}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|courser bee honey}}... {{Big|Hrm? ...{{Color|BotW Blue|Courser bee honey}}? OH, NO!!}} Koko is all out of {{Color|BotW Blue|courser bee honey}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} delivers the {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee Honey}} and {{Term|BotW|Koko}} makes her desert, she will thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} and give him an {{Term|BotW|Energizing Honeyed Apple}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's all thanks to you! Please, take this!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

==={{TotK|-}}===
In {{TotK}}, Courser Bee Honey can be used as a weapon. When thrown, it summons bees from its point of impact that will attack the nearest target, ''including'' {{Term|TotK|Link|link}}. It can be {{Term|TotK|Fuse|display=fused}} with a weapon to summon bees with each swing. It can also be fused to an arrow to summon bees from a distance.
