The {{Term/Store|Fairies' Woods||Series, OoA}} is a location in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 255}}</ref> It is located directly to the west of [[Lynna City]]. This area only appears in the present; its past equivalent is [[Deku Forest]].

==Overview==
Similarly to the [[Lost Woods]], they change according to what area [[Link]] is in and which direction he decides to go. The Fairy Woods contain a Hide 'n' Seek game in which the young heroes has to look for three [[Stray Fairy|Stray Fairies]] (a <font color=yellow>yellow</font>, a <font color=pink>pink</font>, and a <font color=lightblue>blue</font> one), that are similar in appearance to those that appeared in {{MM}}, in a supernatural game of [[Hide and Seek]] to be granted access to the [[Wing Dungeon]].<ref>{{Cite|This is our playground! Won't you play hide 'n' seek? If you can't find the three of us, you can't go on! We'll hide, so try to find us!|Fairy|OoA}}</ref>. When Link beats the fairies at their game, they set the forest back in order and allow the young hero to enter the dungeon.

Later on in the game, one of the [[animals]] will become lost in the woods. Which one it is depends on if Link has a [[Strange Flute]] and which flute he has. Once Link finds the animal, it will become Link's companion and he will receive the corresponding flute.
