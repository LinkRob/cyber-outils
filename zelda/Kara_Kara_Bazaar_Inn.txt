The {{Term/Store|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 323}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn}} is an {{Term|BotW|Inn|link}} located in the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. It is managed by the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} {{Term|BotW|Kachoo|link}}. During {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link|display=Divine Beast Vah Naboris's}} rampage, {{Term|BotW|Kachoo}} will complain that it has caused the {{Term|BotW|Inn|link}} to lose business.<ref>{{Cite|Grr... We've lost a lot of business ever since that {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} started stomping out in the desert.|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> While the stomping is loud, she encourages {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to stay in one of their comfortable beds.<ref>{{Cite|The sound of the Divine Beast walking might be loud, but the beds are still comfy. Do you want to stay?|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> She is also relieved once the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} has been calmed, as patrons can now sleep without being disturbed.<ref>{{Cite|With the Divine Beast gone, you can sleep soundly without being woken up by constant stomping. Want to stay?|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can pay 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} for a regular bed, but he can also get a soft bed for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|BotW Blue|regular bed}} is {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}. A {{Color|BotW Blue|soft bed}} is {{Color|BotW Red|40 rupees}}.|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> The soft beds used by the {{Term|BotW|Inn}} are made using {{Term|BotW|Cucco|link}} feathers and have been designed to leave their users full of energy.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|soft bed}} has been carefully crafted from Cucco feathers. It's been designed to leave you full of energy when you wake.|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> Choosing this bed will provide {{Term|BotW|Link}} with temporary {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} after sleeping. The {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn}} also offers a mandatory wake-up shout, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} can choose to happen in the morning, at noon, or in the evening.<ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|BotW Blue|regular bed}} it is. We offer a mandatory wake-up shout. When would you like it?|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|One {{Color|BotW Blue|soft bed}} coming up! We offer a mandatory wake-up shout. When would you like it?|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kachoo}} will shout a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)|link}} greeting at {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he awakens.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Sav'otta!}}|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Sav'aaq!}}|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Sav'saaba!}}|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref> She wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} safety on his travels before bidding him farewell.<ref>{{Cite|I hope you slept well.<br/>Take care and safe travels to you!|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hope you slept well.<br/>It's cold outside, so travel safe!|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wasn't that bed the softest?<br/>Take care and safe travels to you!|Kachoo|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} is active, {{Term|BotW|Konora|link}} will reside at the {{Term|BotW|Inn}}.<ref>{{Cite|That {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}... It feels like it gets a little bit closer every day.|Konora|BotW}}</ref> She has been unable to return to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I wonder how long it will be before I can get back to {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}.|Konora|BotW}}</ref> as she is afraid of getting zapped by the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The thought of getting zapped is terrifying. I can't risk traveling to {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}...|Konora|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill: Volume 7|link}} is found on one of the top bunks in the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn}}. It details various secrets of the {{Term|Series|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} hidden in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Secret of the Royal Family of Hyrule}}<br/><br/>I heard a rumor that there's something to be found inside Hyrule Castle...|Rumor Mill: Volume 7|BotW}}</ref> There is also a poster on the wall of the {{Term|BotW|Inn}}, which depicts the recipe for a {{Term|BotW|Chilly Fruit Pie|link}}. The poster lists the ingredients as {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Hydromelon|link}}.

During the "{{Term|BotW|Forbidden City Entry|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shaillu|link}} mentions that there is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman who likes to relax on the roof of the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a {{Color|BotW Red|Hylian vai}} who likes to relax on the roof. Though in the afternoon, it's probably too hot for up there.|Shaillu|BotW}}</ref> There, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Vilia|link}}, who asks if he needs anything from her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my. What a lovely young lad you are. Do you need something from me?|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> Though she does not know anything about a man sneaking into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}},<ref>{{Cite|What's that? You've heard about {{Color|BotW Red|a man}} who snuck into {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}? Oh, I don't know anything about him... Sorry I couldn't be more help.|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> if {{Term|BotW|Link}} calls her beautiful, she realizes her style of clothing would look nice on him.<ref>{{Cite|[You're very beautiful!] Oh, such a nice thing to say! And I'm sure you'd agree that these clothes only help my looks, right? Now that I think about it, this style would look quite fetching on you...|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> She offers to sell {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} for 500 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'd have to charge you, of course, but I bet people would see you completely differently if you wore them! What do you think? Do you want to buy them for...{{Color|BotW Red|500 rupees}}?|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to the roof to speak to {{Term|BotW|Vilia}}, who can sell him more clothes or change him into the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}}.

===Shaillu's General Store===
At the front of the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn}}, {{Term|BotW|Shaillu}} runs a {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} specializing in {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}. She suggests that, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants to buy something else, he should check out {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|I only sell arrows. They're on the counter to your left. If you're looking for something else, try {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}.|Shaillu|BotW}}</ref> However, she realizes that, as a man, he would not be allowed inside.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, right... Voe aren't allowed in {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}.|Shaillu|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} is also able to sell {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} to her.<ref>{{Cite|So... What do you want to sell?|Shaillu|BotW}}</ref>
