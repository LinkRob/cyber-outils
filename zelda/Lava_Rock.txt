'''Lava Rock''' is an [[Object]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Lava Rock|Eldin Volcano is home to many rocks like this one. It is heat resistant and won't crumble, even in lava.<ref name="Lava Rock">{{Cite|Eldin Volcano is home to many rocks like this one. It is heat resistant and won't crumble, even in lava.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Lava Rocks are large, heat-resistant rocks found floating around [[Eldin Volcano]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]]. They cost four Echo energy to summon, remain stable on lava, and can be used with Lava Geysers to reach higher areas.
