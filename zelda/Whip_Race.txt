The '''Whip Race''' is a [[Mini-Game]] in {{ST}}.{{Name Ref|ST}}

==Location and rules==
The Whip Race is run by [[Wood (Character)|Wood]]. Located in the backside of Whittleton which can only be reached by using the [[Whip]], [[Link]] can play it for 50 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Try your hand at a whip race? It's just 50 Rupees!|Wood|ST}}</ref> The object of the game is to swing from pole to pole with the help of the Whip and make it to the finish line as fast as possible.<ref>{{Cite|There's only one rule. Use your whip to swing across to the finish line. If you get a good time, you receive a prize!|Wood|ST}}</ref> As Link is making his way to the finish line, he will encounter thorn trees that will impede his way; as such, he must remove the thorns using the Whip to continue his path.

==Prizes==
If Link gets a time between 1:20 and 1;30, he will obtain a Bomb Bag upgrade. By getting to the finish line in less than 1:15, he will receive a Heart Container. Afterward, Link can win [[treasure]]s depending on the time he obtains:

* Above 1:30 - common Treasure, such as a [[Wood Heart]] or a [[Stalfos Skull]].
* Between 1:15 and 1:30 - a medium-rare Treasure, such as a [[Pirate Necklace]] or [[Dark Pearl Loop]]s.
* Between 1:05 and 1:15 - a moderately rare treasure, such as an [[Ancient Gold Piece]], [[Goron Amber]] or [[Mystic Jade]].
* Under 1:05 - a rare treasure, either a [[Regal Ring]] or an [[Alchemy Stone]]. The price of the minigame is also reduced to 25 Rupees; if Link is carrying less than 25 Rupees, he will be allowed to play for free, on the condition that his time "knocks his socks off", according to Wood. However, this condition is not enforced, and unlimited free games can be played, winning many Treasures, including the rarest ones.
