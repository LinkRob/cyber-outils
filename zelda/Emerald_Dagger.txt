==Location and Uses==
Emerald Daggers are a type of {{Term|CoH|Dagger|link}} that deal 1 damage to a single enemy, along with poisoning them temporarily. They can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}, and can only be acquired either after they infuse the Dagger with an {{Term|CoH|Emerald|link}} at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, or if either them or {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} opens the blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} already containing one in the {{Term|CoH|Lost Swamp|link}}.

They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Emerald Short Sword|link}}.
