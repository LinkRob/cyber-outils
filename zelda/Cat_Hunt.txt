{{Term/Store|Cat Hunt||Series, TP}} is a {{Term|TPHD|Mini-Game|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 369}}</ref>

==Location==
After [[Link]] routs the [[Bulblin]]s in the [[Hidden Village]], talks to [[Impaz]], and [[Retrieval of Ilia's Memory|helps Ilia recover her lost memories]] in [[Kakariko Village]], he can return to the ruined town to find it inhabited by a vast population of [[cat]]s. The young hero transforms into [[Wolf Link]] to communicate with one of them, and is told to speak to the [[Cucco]] (referred to as the ''Cucco Leader'')<ref>{{Cite|Do something fun with us! Ask the Cucco leader about it!|Cat|TP}}</ref> behind one of the {{exp|GCN version; eastern in the Wii version|western}} houses. Once he locates the Cucco, he proposes a challenge to the lycanthrope: to talk to every single cat living in the village.<ref>{{Cite|30... No, more like 20...That's how many cats have made it back here to the village...So perhaps you could...you know...just...play with them? Aww, thanks so much... They're all so lonely... Perhaps you could talk to every one of them?|Cucco Leader|TP}}</ref>

==Rules==
After the minigame starts, Link (always in wolf form) must start speaking to every cat he finds, and it seems that they realize the order in which they are found,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TP Red|First}} {{Color|TP Animal Green|one, meow!}}|Cat|TP}}</ref> and they remember whether or not they already spoke to Link (as long as he is still playing with them, of course).<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TP Animal Green|You already talked to me, meow!}}|Cat|TP}}</ref> Some cats are wandering in the outer land, and others are inside the houses. When Link speaks with the 20th cat, he is told to return to where the Cucco is to be rewarded. The prize is a [[Piece of Heart]], which waits for its new owner near the entrance to Impaz's house. Completing the minigame for a second time will make a {{Term|TPHD|Red Rupee|link}} appear buried in the ground near {{Term|TPHD|Impaz}}'s house.

If Link leaves the village during the minigame, the cats to which he spoke will forget that Link spoke to them, which also means that he will have to restart the challenge. The same thing happens if he howls in front of the [[Howling Stone]] located near the Cucco's whereabouts.<ref>{{Cite|...But if you leave the village, it will be like you never talked to any of them, do you understand? Cats are forgetful.|Cucco Leader|TP}}</ref>

==Trivia==
The music heard during the mini-game is the same music heard while Link assaults the village to remove the Bulblins.
