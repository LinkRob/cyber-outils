{{Plural|BotW|Zora Spear}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} made by the {{Plural|BotW|Zora|link}}, crafted from a specific type of metal that makes them particularly light for their size.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the equipment archetype known as {{Term|BotW|Zora Gear|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref> Despite their limited damage output, they are used by the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} for protecting themselves and for fishing.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are used by the female guards of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dunma|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gaddison|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Torfeau|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}} also use them. The {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear}} when it is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Dento|link}} requires a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear}} to recreate both of his special {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} crafts at the {{Term|BotW|Hammerhead|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} wants a new {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dento}} will ask for a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear}} and five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}} to make a new one.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|ceremonial trident}}... I somehow felt it had disappeared from this world. Shall I remake it for you?|Dento|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In that case, I will need a {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora spear}} and {{Color|BotW Red|five pieces}} of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}...|Dento|BotW}}</ref> The same recipe is required for remaking the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}}, with the addition of a single {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}... I somehow felt it had disappeared from this world. Shall I remake it for you?|Dento|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In that case, I will need a {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora Spear}}, {{Color|BotW Red|one}} {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|five pieces}} of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}.|Dento|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Zora Spear}} are made out of metal, so they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. However, they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Zora Spear}} will also conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
