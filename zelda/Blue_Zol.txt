==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
Slimes are what certain enemies will transform into after sprinkling [[Magic Powder]] on them, or using the [[Quake Medallion]] in {{ALttP}}. Link can easily dispatch them with one slash of his sword. Magic Powder is usually only effective on certain weaker enemies however, while the Quake magic will transform medium strength enemies into Slimes. Transforming a [[Deadrock]] into a Slime is the only way to defeat it. A Slime's behavior is very similar to that of a [[Zol]].

==={{FSA|-}}===
Blue Zols appear in {{FSA}}, now resembling a typical {{Term|FSA|Zol}}. One is first found in [[Lake Hylia (Four Swords Adventures)|Lake Hylia]], while more are found in the [[Tower of Winds]]. Like in {{ALttP|-}}, they are more commonly found by using the Quake Medallion, first shown in the [[Death Mountain Foothills]] on an army of [[Hinox]]es, and later, during {{Term|FSA|Frozen Hyrule (Stage)|link}} on {{Plural|FSA|Soldier (Enemy)|link}}, [[Bow Soldier]]s and a Green [[Chief Soldier]]. They can then be easily defeated by the Links.
