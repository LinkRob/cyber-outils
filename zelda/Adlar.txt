{{Term/Store|Adlar||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Adlar}} was referred to as '''Adler''' on the {{Term|OoA|World Map Screen|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoA Map Past|Ancient Adler's House}}|World Map Screen|OoA}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
[[File:OoA Ancient Adler's House Interior.png|thumb|left|x120px|Adlar in his house]]
Adlar lives in a {{Term|OoA|Ancient Adler's House|link|display=House}} in [[Lynna Village]] in the past, on the west side of the river that flows through town. He was the adviser to [[Queen Ambi]] for 50 years,<ref name="Advisor" /> as well as her friend from the time she was a child.<ref>{{Cite|I have known the Queen since she was young.|Adlar|OoA}}</ref> However, when the [[Nayru (Oracle)#Possessed Nayru|Possessed Nayru]] arrived, Ambi stopped listening to his advice.<ref>{{Cite|She always held the people foremost in her heart. But since she took that girl Nayru in, she has changed completely... She quit listening to my advice.|Adlar|OoA}}</ref> Shortly after, Nayru suggested that Adlar be banished,<ref>{{Cite|It was that girl who suggested I be banished, but...|Adlar|OoA}}</ref> and he was chased from the palace.<ref>{{Cite|I have since been chased from the palace and am now retired.|Adlar|OoA}}</ref> He gives [[Link]] helpful advice when he first visits the village, and wishes him well on his quest, as he understands that something is wrong.<ref>{{Cite|I can only see her as a monster in human guise. I wonder if the Queen is all right... |Adlar|OoA}}</ref>
