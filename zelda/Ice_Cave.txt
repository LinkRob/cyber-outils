The {{Term/Store|Ice Cave||Series, ALttP}} is a location in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP|publisher= Nintendo|page= 111}}</ref> It is a mysterious frozen cave on the eastern side of [[Lake Hylia]].

==Features==
Despite being a small location that is easily missed on Link's quest, the Ice Cave is very significant to the completion of his adventure, as it contains the [[Ice Rod]]. Link is first told about the cave by [[Sahasrahla]], who informs him a very important item is found there that he must obtain.<ref>{{Cite|A helpful item is hidden in the cave on the east side of Lake Hylia. Get it!|Sahasrahla|ALttP}}</ref> At first, Link will only locate a single entrance that leads to a solid wall; using a [[Bomb]] to blow a hole in this wall will reveal a beautiful [[Fairy Fountain]] in the middle of the frozen cave. Passing through another passageway he will find a number of unmovable blocks barring his way to a [[Chest|treasure chest]]. To reach this chest, which contains the Ice Rod, Link will have to leave the cave and blow open another entrance near the first.

==The Golden Bee==
The Ice Cave contains a sole enemy; the [[Golden Bee]] (also known as the Good Bee), which hides on the [[Fairy]] statue in the fountain.<ref>{{Cite|Check out the cave east of Lake Hylia. Strange and wonderful things live in it...|Thief|ALttP}}</ref> If Link uses the [[Pegasus Boots]] to perform a [[Dash Attack]] into this statue, the bee will reveal itself and attempt to attack Link. By catching the sparkling insect in a Bottle, it will become a faithful companion if Link releases it in battle. In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of the game, he can also take one of these Golden Bees to a shop in the [[Village of Outcasts]]. By showing it to the owner, he will then sell Golden Bees to Link any time he wishes to purchase one. Link can also return to the Ice Cave to obtain another at any time.
