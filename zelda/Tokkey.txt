{{Term/Store|Tokkey||Series, OoA}} is an eccentric researcher in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254}}</ref> He lives in [[Labrynna]], in a [[cave]] hidden through the lake on [[Talus Peaks]] (near [[Symmetry Village|Symmetry City]]).

==Biography==
Tokkey is trying to create a tune that will cause the one who plays it to revert from the past age to the present age. However, his research lacks a certain tune that will give his tune time-traveling abilities. He knows a riddle that tells him what the tune is, but he cannot seem to figure it out.<ref>{{Cite|I am Tokkey. I research time. After many years of research, I'm one step away from perfecting a tune that lets one move forward in time, but it seems I've hit a roadblock. "Echoes produce waves..." What could it mean?|Tokkey|OoA}}</ref> If [[Link]] plays the correct tune for him on the [[Harp of Ages]], it helps him complete his research. The research yields the "[[Tune of Currents]]".<ref>{{Cite|Ye of many tunes, I grant you a new tune! Time flows on when you play the Tune of Currents!|Tokkey|OoA}}</ref>

After he composes the "Tune of Currents", Tokkey leaves his cave.
