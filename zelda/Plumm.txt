{{Term/Store|Plumm||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 277{{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Plumm is a {{Wp|myna}}-like bird that appears whenever [[Link]] approaches a patch of [[Hawk Grass]] at [[Lake Hylia]]. She is friends with another [[Bird]] named [[Trill]].<ref name="TPHD Guide 15"/> If Link speaks to her while in [[Hylian]] form, Plumm will say that she does not speak to humans.<ref>{{Cite|Plumm has no need to speak to humans...|Plumm|TP}}</ref> However, if Link is in [[Wolf Link|wolf form]], she will ask him to play a {{Term|TPHD|Mini-Game|link}} called {{Term|TPHD|Fruit-Pop-Flight|link}}.<ref name="TPHD Guide 15"/> She is somewhat rude, telling Link what a poor job he does if he loses the mini-game.<ref>{{Cite|That was so...lame!|Plumm|TP}}</ref> She is amazed when he wins, and will give Link a prize for winning, clearly stating that she is not giving it to him because she likes him.<ref>{{Cite|What?! No way! [X] points?! You got more than 10,000 points... Wow. Here, take this. But... Don't think I'm giving you this because I like you or anything!|Plumm|TP}}</ref>
