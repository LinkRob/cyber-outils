The {{Term/Store|Pegasus Boots|Pegasus Boots|Series, LA, LADX, LANS, FSA, TMC, ALBW}},{{Exp Game|LA, FSA, TMC, ALBW}}<ref name="E 133">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133{{Exp Game|ALttP, LA, FS, FSA, TMC, ALBW}}}}</ref> also originally known as the {{Term/Store|Pegasus Shoes|Pegasus Shoes|ALttP, FS}},{{Exp Game|ALttP, FS}}<ref name="E 133"/> are a recurring item in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They allow {{Term|Series|Link|link}} to {{Term|Series|Sprinting|link|display=Sprint}} forward at superhuman speeds. The increased speed not only allows him to travel across the land quickly, but to use [[Dash Attack]]s and bash into various objects, like [[tree]]s, in order to knock out their contents.

==Location and uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Pegasus Shoes|This magic footwear has been handed down by the family of one of the Seven Wise Men. If you wear these shoes, you can run incredibly fast. You will get the Pegasus Shoes from the wise man Sahasrahla.}}
{{Manual|ALttP&FS|Pegasus Shoes|These allow the wearer to run at great speed.}}
In {{ALttP}}, Link receives the Pegasus Shoes from [[Sahasrahla]] after retrieving the [[Pendant of Courage]] from the [[Eastern Palace]]. They must be used to obtain the [[Book of Mudora]] by bashing into a bookshelf inside the [[library]]. If Link charges into a tree, it may drop [[apple]]s, [[bee]]s, a [[Fairy]], or a [[Bomb]] while in the [[Dark World]]. Piles of rocks and some cracked walls can also broken by bashing into them.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Pegasus Boots|This magical pair of footwear is another legendary treasure. These Boots allow you to run at incredible speeds and ram into objects with great force. Press and hold the Button to start running. If you press the +Control Pad in a direction other than the one you are running in, you will come to a halt.}}
In {{LA}}, the Pegasus Boots are found inside the [[Key Cavern]]. When combined with the [[Roc's Feather]], they can increase Link's jump distance. If the [[Sword]] is equipped while dashing, Link can perform a dash attack, which can damage enemies, cut through grass, and destroy crystal-shaped rocks. The Pegasus Boots are also required to defeat [[Slime Eye]], the boss of the Key Cavern. Unlike other games, Link does not necessarily use his Sword while he is sprinting via the Boots.

==={{FS|-}}===
{{Manual|FS|Pegasus Shoes|These legendary shoes allow you to run fast and dash up slippery slopes.}}
In {{FS}}, the Pegasus Shoes are required to defeat [[Dera Zol]], the boss of the [[Talus Cave]]. While dashing, the [[Links]] can change directions instead of going straight forward.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Manual|FSA|Pegasus Boots|Press the A Button repeatedly for a special dash. Hm... What happens if you dash square into a tree?<br/>{{Big|Lev. 2}}<br/>While you are dashing, you will jump right over most holes.}}
In {{FSA}}, the Pegasus Boots also allow the Links to change directions while dashing. Dashing with them flips [[Terrorpin]]s upside down. If the Pegasus Boots are given to a [[Great Fairy]], they will be upgraded to Level 2. The upgraded boots allow the Links to run over large gaps, granting access to otherwise unreachable areas. 

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, Link receives the Pegasus Boots after helping [[Rem]] and his [[Minish]] helpers create them. They are required to cross [[Castor Wilds]], thus gaining entrance to the [[Fortress of Winds]]. The Pegasus Boots can be used to knock masks off walls, run through grass, and knock some trees down. By obtaining the [[Dash Attack]] [[Tiger Scroll]], Link can perform said technique when equipping the Pegasus Boots and the sword at the same time.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, the Pegasus Boots only appear in the [[multiplayer]] [[Battle Mode]]. In it, Link or a [[Phantom]] can take the Pegasus Boots power-up to increase their speed for a short time.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, the Pegasus Boots can be obtained by [[Wall Merging|merging]] into a wall behind to the [[Shady Guy]] in [[Kakariko Village]] after he has stolen the [[Smooth Gem]]. Once caught, he will apologize for taking the stone and then proceed to give Link the boots. They can be used to bash into objects such as trees and pillars, knocking down lost [[Maiamai]]s hidden on them.

In {{AST}}, the Pegasus Boots are especially effective, as the [[Player|BS-X avatar character]] can freely change direction as they perform the Dash Attack.

{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}} and {{HW|L}}, the Pegasus Boots appear as the third tier of [[Linkle]]'s [[Boots#Hyrule Warriors|Boots]] weapon set. [[Young Link]] also uses winged boots momentarily for his sixth Strong Attack combo to move around quickly. These boots bear a resemblance to both the Pegasus Boots and the [[Hover Boots]].
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
[[File:HW Young Link - Hover Boots.png|right|thumb|244x244px|Young Link using a pair of winged boots in {{HW|-}}]]
*In the Namco fighting game ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', Link is able to use the Dash Attack as a special move. However, he is never actually wearing the Pegasus Boots and the way he runs is merely himself briefly charging forward.

==Nomenclature==
The Pegasus Boots are based on the eponymous horse from the ancient Greek Mythology. In it, {{wp|Pegasus}} was a winged horse, famous for his running speed. The horse was sired by the Greek God of Oceans and Horses, Poseidon, and was famous for being one of the fastest mortal horses in the known world.
{{Nomenclature}}
