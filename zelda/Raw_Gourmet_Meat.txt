==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}/{{TotK|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Raw Gourmet Meat}} are dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Water Buffalo|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Snowcoat Fox|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Maraudo Wolf|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Wasteland Coyote|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Cold-Footed Wolf|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Tabantha Moose|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Great-Horned Rhinoceros|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Honeyvore Bear|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Grizzlemaw Bear|link}} when they are killed. When eaten, Raw Gourmet Meat heals three {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}}. Raw Gourmet Meat can also be roasted by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to make [[Seared Gourmet Steak]] or frozen to make [[Icy Gourmet Meat]].

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to [[Trott]] at [[Outskirt Stable]], he will express a desire for Raw Gourmet Meat.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, what I wouldn't do for some fresh-caught raw gourmet meat...|Trott|BotW}}</ref> This will activate the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "[[A Rare Find]]". If Link gives him some, he will be paid 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, you don't have to tell me twice! I'll take one! Does {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}} work for you?|Trott|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, Link can give Trott Raw Gourmet Meat at any time for 100 Rupees.

When making meals in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, Raw Gourmet Meat is required to make [[Gourmet Meat Curry]], a [[Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl]], and a [[Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer]]. Raw Gourmet Meat or a [[Raw Whole Bird]] are needed to make [[Gourmet Meat Stew]], [[Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat]], and [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]]. Like other meats, they can be used to make [[Creamy Meat Soup]], [[Glazed Meat]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Meat Pie]], a [[Meat Skewer]], [[Copious Meat Skewers]], [[Meaty Rice Balls]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Steak]], [[Steamed Meat]], and a [[Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin]].
