{{Term/Store|Green Kinstone|Green Kinstones|Series, TMC|plural}} are items in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC |publisher= Nintendo |page= 104}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Green Kinstone}} are the result of a successful {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusion}} of two green {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}. There are 49 possible {{Term|TMC|Green Kinstone}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusions}} to be made throughout {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}, detailed below.
