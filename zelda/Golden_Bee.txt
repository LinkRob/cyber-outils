The {{Term/Store|Golden Bee|Golden Bees|Series, ALttP, ALBW}},{{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}<ref name="E 125">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}}}</ref> also known as the '''Good Bee''',{{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}<ref name="E 125"/> is a recurring animal and item in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
The {{Term|ALttP|Golden Bee}} appears only in one area in {{ALttP}}. Like a normal [[Bee]], it can be caught and used to defeat enemies, and every boss except [[Mothula]] is immune to it. However, compared to regular Bees, it will remain by Link's side for a much longer period of time. To get it, Link must go to east side of [[Lake Hylia]] and enter the [[Ice Cave]] found there then use a [[Bomb]] inside to find a [[Fairy Fountain]]. There, Link has to [[Dash Attack|Dash]] into the Fairy statue with the [[Pegasus Boots]] and the Golden Bee will appear. Link then must put it into a [[Bottle]] using the [[Bug Net#A Link to the Past|Bug Catching Net]]; unlike normal Bees, this rare kind leaves a sparkling trail as it flies and it will not fly away from Link's side until it has attacked many more enemies than the normal variety. Link can usually catch it again to reuse it, making it a valuable ally. If it does leave, Link can return to the Ice Cave to catch another.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
The Golden Bee is found the same places as normal Bees (in grass and bushes), but it is much more rare. Golden Bees are marked by their golden aura when they fly.<ref>{{Cite|You caught a golden bee! It's so bright and buzzy!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got a golden bee! It shines so bright and beautiful. Still..."bee" careful!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> A Golden Bee can also be purchased from a man in the forest surrounding Lost Woods for 888 [[Rupee]]s.

When {{Term|ALBW|Link}} gives a {{Term|ALBW|Golden Bee}} to the {{Term|ALBW|Bee Guy|link}}, he receives the {{Term|ALBW|Bee Badge|link}} in return. Additional {{Plural|ALBW|Golden Bee}} can be sold to the {{Term|ALBW|Bee Guy}} for 300 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}} each. 

==Trivia==
*In {{ALttP|-}}, the only boss that Bees and Golden Bees can harm is [[Mothula]]. A single Golden Bee is not quite strong enough to defeat the monster on its own, however.
*In both the GBA and SNES versions of {{ALttP|-}}, if Link goes to [[Kakariko Village]] and lets a Golden Bee loose near the [[Street Merchant]], he will buy it for 100 Rupees.
*In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of {{ALttP|-}}, there is a shop that has a Golden Bee on display in the [[Village of Outcasts]]. However, Link cannot purchase it until he brings one of his own from the Ice Cave to the shopkeeper. The [[Fairy]] and normal Bee in the same shop are purchasable by default.
*The purchasing price of a Golden Bee in {{ALBW|-}}, 888 Rupees, is based on a pun in Japanese which has no English equivalent; the Japanese words for "bee", {{Romanize|蜂|Hachi}}, and "eight", {{Romanize|八|Hachi}}, are homophones.

==Nomenclature==
In the version of {{ALttP|-}} for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]], the {{Term|Series|Golden Bee}} was mistranslated from Japanese as "Good Bee". The name was corrected in {{ALttP&FS|-}}.<ref name="E 125"/>
