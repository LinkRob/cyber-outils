{{Term/Store|Barkle||Series, FPTRR}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR, RTBToL, SSBU}} He is [[Tingle]]'s intelligent pet [[Dog]].

==Biography==
[[File:Barkle.gif]]
[[Tingle]] first meets Barkle at [[Sunshine Seashore]] on the [[First Continent]], where he can sell to Barkle all of his bones for two [[Rupee]]s each and the dog will leave the area to fetch items for Tingle. When Tingle meets up with the skeleton {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate|link}} in [[Cape Treasure]], Barkle shows up and scares the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}}, giving Tingle access to their {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Ship (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|link}} and an opportunity to join them. While later exploring their [[Pirate Hideaway]], Tingle is informed of a vicious monster attacking the cook's assistant in the kitchen. Tingle is sent to deal with the monster, only to find out that it is only Barkle. Barkle leaves the hideout shortly after.

Barkle follows Tingle home and stumbles upon the [[Rupee Tower]], and is transformed into a [[Tingles|Tingle]] version of himself. Barkle then chooses to live with Tingle.

Barkle also appears in the opening scene of {{RTBToL}} before Tingle is sucked into the storybook.

==Trivia==
*Barkle's name is a portmanteau of "bark" and "Tingle". This play on words is also present in other languages. Namely, {{Romanize|ワンクル|Wankuru}} in Japan ("''wan wan''" is a Japanese onomatopoeia for barking, "Chin''kuru''" is Tingle's Japanese name) and "Guagle" in Spain (Guau being a Spanish onomatopoeia for barking).
