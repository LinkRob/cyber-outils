The {{Term/Store|Mermaid Key||Series, OoA}} is an item in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 131}}</ref> It is the key to {{Term|OoA|Mermaid's Cave|link}} and exists in both time periods, as the {{Term|OoA|Old Mermaid Key|link}} for the present and simply the Mermaid Key for the past. Both keys are obtained in a similar fashion.

==Location and Uses==
The Mermaid Key is also obtained through a side-quest. Where the [[Target Carts]] mini-game is in the present, in the past there is the [[Shooting Gallery|Goron Shooting Gallery]], much like the shooting gallery in [[Lynna Village]].<ref>{{Cite|This is Goron Gallery! Our game was a big hit in Lynna Village!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> If Link achieves a score of 100 points or more, he will receive the [[Lava Juice]] as a prize.<ref>{{Cite|Right now, we're offering Lava Juice to one player with an excellent score!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Next, in a cave near the summit of [[Rolling Ridge]], Link will meet a Goron who happens to be an acquaintance of the [[Graceful Goron]].<ref>{{Cite|Don't tell anyone, but I'm friends with the famous Graceful Goron. Really!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> If Link already has the old version of the key, the Goron will tell him that it is the Graceful Goron who owns the Mermaid Key. In exchange for the Lava Juice, the Goron will offer to introduce Link to the Graceful Goron with the [[Letter of Introduction]].<ref>{{Cite|I don't know if it will work, but if you give me your Lava Juice, I'll introduce you to the Graceful Goron!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Once it is shown to the Graceful Goron in the past, he will agree to give Link the Mermaid Key, but only if Link proves himself by matching his skills in a [[Goron Dance|dancing contest]]. Once this is done, Link will be the proud owner of both Mermaid Keys, leaving [[Mermaid's Cave]] waiting for him.
