{{Term/Store|Zant Mask|Zant Masks|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 213">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 213}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Zant Masks are enlarged versions of [[Zant]]'s helmet<ref name="E 213"/> and are immobile except for their ability to teleport.<ref name="TPHD Guide 21">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TPHD|publisher= Prima|page= 21}}</ref> They open their mouth guard to fire blasts of dark magic at [[Link]], which can be dodged or deflected using Link's [[Shield]].<ref name="TPHD Guide 21"/> If Link is successful at deflecting the blast back at the entity, it will be stunned for a short period, leaving it open to attacks.

[[Arrow]]s are ineffective against them, as they will just reflect off the mask's material. With the [[Master Sword]], they can be killed with a trio of slashes, but after the sword has been [[Master Sword#Light-infused Master Sword|infused with light]], one is sufficient to vanquish them.

Some of them attack alone, but others come in groups. Some groups appear in "waves", that is, Link defeats a few and a few more appear. The defeat of all Masks in an area always results in one or more important [[chest]]s appearing, which usually contain [[Key]]s, so these enemies cannot be avoided.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, Zant Maskes appear as part of Zant's moveset with the [[Scimitars]]. Zant summons it to fire Twilight energy balls against his enemies as it rapidly spins around. It is also used in his [[Focus Spirit Attack]], in which he summons a giant Zant Mask to knock his enemies back.
{{Noncanon|End}}
