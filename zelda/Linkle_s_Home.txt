==Features and Overview==
{{Term|HWDE|Linkle's Home}} is the home of the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior|link}}, {{Term|HWDE|Linkle|link}} and her grandmother. {{Term|HWDE|Linkle's Home}} contains a small ranch where {{Term|HWDE|Linkle}} cares for a few {{Plural|HWDE|Cucco|link}} with her grandmother.<ref name="Prima"/> {{Term|HWDE|Linkle's Home}} is seen during the opening cutscene of "{{Term|HWDE|The Girl in the Green Tunic|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} where {{Term|HWDE|Linkle}} awakens to hear of {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Castle|link}} being attacked by monsters.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard?
