==Characteristics==
Omuai are the minibosses of [[Poison Moth's Lair]]. The trio emerge at random from various submerged pits in the room and shoot fireballs at [[Link]]. To defeat them, Link must lift them out of the water with the [[Power Bracelet]] and attack their exposed bodies with his [[Sword]]. When out of the water, Omuai will flop around in an attempt to harm Link.
