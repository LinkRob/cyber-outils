The {{Term/Store|Great Plateau Tower||Series, BotW, SSBU}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''''Great Plateau Tower'''''|Map|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|'''Great Plateau Tower'''|N/A|SSBU}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Great Plateau Tower}} was referred to as the '''Resurrection Tower''' in the E3 2016 demo for {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Resurrection Tower}}<br/>Central Hyrule|Map|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo |published= June 14, 2016 |retrieved= June 8, 2020 |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ze05xdORvYs?t=141 |title= The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild - Exploration Gameplay - Nintendo E3 2016 |site= YouTube |type= Video}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in the final build of the game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
[[File:BotW Sheikah Tower Rising.png|thumb|right|300px|The Great Plateau Tower rising from the ground]]
The {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower}} is the first {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} activates in {{BotW}}. Though the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|display=Tower}} was buried beneath the earth, a portion of it became exposed when {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}'s resurrection shook the earth.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 404}}</ref> After awakening from the {{Term|BotW|Slumber of Restoration|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} instructs him to follow the marker displayed on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|... Link... Link. Head for the point marked on the map in your Sheikah Slate.|???|BotW}}</ref> This marker leads {{Term|BotW|Link}} to a {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}} in the buried remains of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower}}. By activating the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} causes the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|display=Tower}} to rise from the ground,<ref>{{Cite|Sheikah Tower activated. Please watch for falling rocks.|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> activating 120 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} and causing the remaining {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower}} to rise as well.<ref>{{Cite|Do you see that structure there? The one shining with a strange light? It began glowing at the exact moment those towers rose up from the ground.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> After it has risen, a {{Term|BotW|Travel Gate|link}} will be activated atop the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|display=Tower}} and the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} atop the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower}} will begin distilling regional data of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower Region|link}} onto {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}, granting him a {{Term|BotW|Map}} of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link|display=Plateau}}.<ref>{{Cite|Distilling local information...|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Regional map extracted.|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> In response to this, {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} will assume an ethereal form and begin rampaging around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} before being subdued by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}'s sealing power. From atop the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|display=Tower}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can hear {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} pleading him to remember his mission to destroy {{Term|BotW|Ganon|link}} before her power wanes.<ref>{{Cite|Remember... Try... Try to remember... You have been asleep for the past 100 years.|???|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The beast... When the beast regains its true power, this world will face its end. Now then... You must hurry, Link. Before it's too late...|???|BotW}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSBU|-}}===
The {{Term|SSBU|Great Plateau Tower}} appears as a {{Term|SSBU|Stage|link}} in {{SSBU}}. The top of the {{Term|SSBU|Great Plateau Tower|display=Tower}} can be destroyed by attacks or {{Plural|SSBU|Fighter|link}} bouncing against it, though it will regenerate after a period of time. While the roof of the {{Term|SSBU|Great Plateau Tower}} is destroyed, a platform will appear in its place. The {{Term|BotW|Old Man|link}} from {{BotW}} can be seen {{Term|SSBU|Paraglider|link|display=Paragliding}} in the background of the {{Term|SSBU|Stage}}, and will occasionally glide down onto the {{Term|SSBU|Great Plateau Tower|display=Tower}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In {{SSBU}}, the platform that replaces the roof of the {{Term|SSBU|Great Plateau Tower}} bears text in the [[Sheikah (Language)]] which says "''SMASHBROS''", and on the side of the roof, text states "''S HEIKAHTOWE R''."{{Exp|The pillars connected to the roof split the S and R from the rest of the text}}
