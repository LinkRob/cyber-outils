The {{Term/Store|''Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason}} is the final {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|link}}, located southeast of {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I won't need to roll the dice of hearts for this one. The remaining one is here. At the southeastern cliff of {{Color|BotW Blue|Upland Zorana}}!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must read it as part of the "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

This {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monument}} recounts the stonemason's work creating the rest of the {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monuments}}. Initially, they were commissioned to create only one {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monument}}, but when they saw the volume of writings {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} wanted preserved, they ended up having to split it among seven.<ref>{{Cite|What an honor it was to receive a personal request from King Dorephan to craft a historical stone monument! I did not realize how much content he'd give me, though... It certainly exceeded the line limits of a single monument. I suppose I could have just shortened the text, but it felt wrong to tamper with our great king's words. Thankfully, I was able to split it all between seven monuments to ensure that every word was preserved.|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref> They also added two more {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monuments}}, depicting the achievements of both {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} and {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link|display=Prince Sidon}}.<ref>{{Cite|While I was at it, I thought...why not add two of my own? And so I created one for King Dorephan and one for Prince Sidon. True, this is outside the scope of my commission... But I believe their triumphs deserve as much! But why stop at that...|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref> While they were doing so, they decided to add one final {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monument}} describing their own work.<ref>{{Cite|Why, indeed! My achievement surely deserves remembrance too. That is how a commission of one became 10.|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref> They finish out by stating that as long as they remember to sign the {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monuments}}, their name will be known forever.<ref>{{Cite|Still, it is worth it! So long as I remember to sign these monuments, my name will be remembered forever. As it should be.|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref>
