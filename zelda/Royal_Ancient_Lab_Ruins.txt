The {{Term/Store|Royal Ancient Lab Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Royal Ancient Lab Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Royal Ancient Lab Ruins}} are located in the {{Term|BotW|North Hyrule Plain|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. They are the remains of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Research Institute|link}} that was destroyed during the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 396}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found by following a {{Term|BotW|Flower Trail|link}} from the north of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Ancient Lab Ruins|display=Ruins}} to a southeastern wall.
