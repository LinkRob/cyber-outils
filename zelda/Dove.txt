{{Term/Store|Dove|Doves|Series, ST|plural}} are a type of [[Creature]] in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 63}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ST|Dove}} are common {{Plural|ST|Bird|link}} that can be found throughout {{Term|ST|New Hyrule|link}}. They will often flock together on the ground, fleeing from {{Term|ST|Link|link}} if he gets too close to them. If he uses the {{Term|ST|Spirit Flute|link}} to play the "{{Term|ST|Song of Birds|link}}" when {{Plural|ST|Dove}} are nearby, they will settle on and around him, staying there until he moves, by which point they will all fly away with the exception of the {{Term|ST|Dove}} perched on his hat. This {{Term|ST|Bird}} will remain there until {{Term|ST|Link}} either rolls, performs a {{Term|ST|Spin Attack|link}}, hits something hard with his {{Term|ST|Sword|link}}, or leaves the area.

{{Plural|ST|Dove}} are the symbol of the {{Plural|ST|Royal Engineer|link}}, therefore, a picture of a {{Term|ST|Dove}} can be seen on the hat of the {{Term|ST|Engineer's Clothes|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link is well known for his green clothing, a look he has sported throughout time. But in Spirit Tracks, he dons a blue uniform with a red cap instead. The cap is decorated with a dove, the symbol of the Royal Engineers. Playing the Song of Birds will call a dove to roost!|Trophy|SSBfN3DS}}</ref>
