{{Term/Store|Fireball|Fireballs|Series, HW, HWDE, CoH|plural}} are recurring objects in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Ganon's Fury">{{Cite|Shoot a {{Color|HW Green|Fireball}} from your right hand. This can put {{Color|HW Green|Argorok}} into a downed state.|Tutorial|HW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWDE}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Fireball}}<br/>Launches three magic fireballs in the direction you are facing.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Fireball}} go unnamed in {{HW|L}}.

==Spinoff appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{ZA|-}}===
[[File:ZA Fireball Sprite.png|frame|left]]
Fireballs in {{ZA}} are obstacles encountered in the [[Shrine of Fire]]. They function identically to [[Crystal]]s, and spawn from sides of the room before launching at [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]. Fireballs are stronger than Crystals, as they can inflict two [[Heart]]s of damage on Zelda, rather than just one.

==={{HW|-}}===
Fireballs are [[Boss Attack Item]]s obtained by defeating [[King Dodongo]] in [[Ganon's Fury]].<ref>{{Cite|By picking up the {{Color|HW Green|Boss Attack items}} dropped by defeated Giant Bosses, you gain the ability to use {{Color|HW Green|Boss Attacks}} until the battle ends. By hitting each Giant Boss with the Boss Attack it's {{Color|HW Green|weak to}}, you can force the boss into a {{Color|HW Green|downed state}}.|Tutorial|HW}}</ref> They are effective against [[Argorok]].<ref name="Ganon's Fury"/> Fireballs are also used by King Dodongo and [[Ganon]] as [[Boss Attack]]s, which can be countered with [[Bomb]]s.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Crossover appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
[[Octavo]] is capable of summoning three Fireballs as an attack. In [[Story Mode — Octavo]], Octavo can find the Fireball [[Spell]], which allows him to summon three Fireballs at the cost of his [[Stamina]].<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|CoH Yellow|fireball}} spell! It's automatically assigned to {{Icon|CoH L}}, and only {{Icon|CoH Octavo}} can use it. Press {{Icon|CoH L}} to launch three magic fireballs in the direction you are facing.|N/A|CoH}}</ref> As he is the [[Future World]] counterpart of Octavo, [[The NecroDancer]] is also capable of summoning Fireballs to attack.

When Octavo finds the Fireball Spell, a [[Fate]] appears to chastise his foolish behavior.<ref>{{Cite|Fool! Look at you, flittering about without a care for the present. There is more to a journey than the destination...|Fate|CoH}}</ref>
{{Ambig|End}}
