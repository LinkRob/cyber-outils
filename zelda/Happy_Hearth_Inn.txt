The {{Term/Store|Happy Hearth Inn||Series, TMC}} is located in the eastern area of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Figurine|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn}}
The {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|display=Inn}} opens when {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}}. It serves as an area where {{Term|TMC|Link}} can heal all his {{Plural|TMC|Heart|link}} by sleeping, similar to his {{Term|TMC|Link's House|link|display=House}}; however, {{Term|TMC|Link}} will receive a gift for staying at the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|display=Inn}},<ref>{{Cite|Hi there, and welcome to the Happy Hearth! Need a rest, kid? Well, we can take care of that &ndash; and even throw in a present to boot!|Emma|TMC}}</ref> which is always a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|Emma|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|display=Inn}} clerk, allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to choose which room to stay in, so as long as he pays first.<ref>{{Cite|All payment up front, please! Thanks! Enjoy your stay!|Emma|TMC}}</ref> The three rooms vary in size and price, as well as the resulting {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} stays in the cheap room (50 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}),<ref>{{Cite|...Right. 50 Rupees. The cheap room it is, then.|Emma|TMC}}</ref> he will receive a green {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}}; in the regular room (200 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}}),<ref>{{Cite|Yessir! 200 Rupees gets you a regular room!|Emma|TMC}}</ref> a blue one; and in the luxury room (400 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}}),<ref>{{Cite|Well now! Come from a good family, do we? A luxury room for 400 Rupees!|Emma|TMC}}</ref> a red one.

This building is also where {{Term|TMC|Din (Oracle)|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Nayru (Oracle)|link}} can be found. By [[Kinstone Fusion|fusing]] {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone}} with them, their sidequest can be started, which involves finding two of the three sisters a house. As only two houses are available, one sister will be left at the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|display=Inn}}.

There is also a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} on the balcony of the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|display=Inn}}. This is accessible once {{Term|TMC|Link}} has completed the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, as the door can only be unlocked by lighting the two {{Plural|TMC|Torch|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} contains 200 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}}. 

In the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|display=Inn}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can fuse {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone}} with {{Term|TMC|Emma}}, as well as the two residents, {{Term|TMC|Bindle|link}} (standing on the bottom floor) and {{Term|TMC|Satchel|link}} (sitting on a chair on the second floor). He can also fuse {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone}} with {{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Joel|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Lolly|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Zill|link}}, who are sometimes found here.
