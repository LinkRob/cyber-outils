{{Term/Store|''Super Rumor Mill EX''||Series, BotW}} is a series of {{Plural|BotW|Book|link}} in {{TMT}} and {{TCB}} DLC packs for {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor has it there's an item called the Korok Mask that has the power to alert its wearer when a Korok is hiding nearby.<br/><br/>Apparently the {{Color|BotW Red|Woodland Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare mask.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

=='''Features and Overview'''==
Four volumes of {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill EX}} can be found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. Similar to the standard {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill|link}} issues, the series is written by {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Traysi here! Awake or asleep, my quest
for juicy rumors never rests! EVER.|Super Rumor Mill V1|BotW}}</ref> Each volume covers a topic of local gossip and is recommended with a 5-star rating.

Upon downloading {{TMT}} DLC pack, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is tasked with tracking down the first two issues of the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill EX}} as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Strange Mask Rumors|link}}" and "{{Term|BotW|EX Teleportation Rumors!|link}}" {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

Similarly, {{TCB}} DLC pack tasks {{Term|BotW|Link}} with tracking down the last two issues as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Ancient Horse Rumors|link}}" and "{{Term|BotW|EX Royal Guard Rumors|link}}" {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest}}.
