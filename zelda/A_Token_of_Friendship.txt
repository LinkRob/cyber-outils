==Overview==
{{Term|TotK|Yona|link}} tells {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} that she and the {{Term|TotK|Zora|link}} were going to present the {{Term|TotK|Zora Greaves|link}} as a gift to him. However, she and her colleagues were unable to find the Zora Greaves. She last noted they should be somewhere in {{Term|TotK|Ancient Zora Waterworks|link}}.

Looking around the ancient Zora ruins, Link found a secret hole from the now-drained ruins, which lead him to a large cave. From behind a waterfall, Link found the Zora Greaves inside a treasure chest.
