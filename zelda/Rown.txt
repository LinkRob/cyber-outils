==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Rown}}
Rown is a [[Korok]] who lives in the [[Forest Haven]]. Before the Forest Ceremony, Rown stands on a ledge with an arrow of grass pointing to the exit to the Forbidden Forest. After the Forest Ceremony, Rown goes to the [[Mother & Child Isles|Mother and Child Isles]] to plant new [[Tree]]s.
