The {{Term/Store|Ninja Gi||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Ninja Gi is an import. You'll look dashing... and also do triple damage WHILE dashing!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Ninja Gi}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes {{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}. When {{Term|TFH|Link}} wears the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}, he is able to {{Term|TFH|Dash Attack|link}} instantly by pressing the L or R button, skipping the usual windup period that requires the player to hold the button momentarily.<ref>{{Cite|Increase dash attack power and speed. Hold {{Icon|TFH L}}/{{Icon|TFH R}} and go, go, go!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> When the {{Term|TFH|Dash Attack}} using the {{Term|TFH|Ninja Gi}} connects with an [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemy]], it deals three times the damage of a regular {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} strike.<ref>{{Cite|The Ninja Gi triples your dash attack power. That's nothing to sneeze at, doncha know!|Madame Couture's Apprentice|TFH}}</ref> The {{Term|TFH|Ninja Gi}} also changes {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s {{Term|TFH|Sword}} into one resembling a {{Wp|Ninjatō|ninjatō}}.
