The "{{Term/Store|Sun's Song||Series, OoT, OoT3D}}" is a recurring Song in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref> The "{{Term|Series|Sun's Song}}" goes unnamed in {{MM}}, {{TWW}}, {{TP}}, {{SSBB}}, {{SSB4}}, and {{SSBU}}.

==Learning Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT3D Sun's Song Icon.png|left]]
The song is composed by the [[Composer Brothers]], [[Flat]] and [[Sharp]].<ref>{{Cite|Something else is inscribed on the tombstone... It's the {{Color|OoT3D Yellow|secret melody}} of the Composer Brothers!|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref> Assigned by the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]] to study the mystic powers of the family, this melody is the result of Flat and Sharp each studying a different song: one to summon the sun, and another to summon the moon.<ref>{{Cite|We brothers also served the Royal Family, and were assigned to study the hereditary mystic powers of the family. Though we never could figure out the power of the Triforce, we had almost completed our study of controlling time with the tones of ocarinas. Uh, I mean... Actually, we did complete that study!|Composer Brothers|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|To tell the truth, each of us was studying a different song, one to summon the sun and another to summon the moon.|Composer Brothers|OoT}}</ref>

The "Sun's Song" can be learned from entering the [[Royal Family's Tomb]] in the [[Graveyard]] of [[Kakariko Village]]. However, Link can only enter the inside of the tomb after playing [[Zelda's Lullaby]] on top of the [[Triforce]] mark. The notes for the "Sun's Song" are scratched on a wall at the end of the crypt along with a poem.<ref>{{Cite|The rising sun will eventually set, A newborn's life will fade. From sun to moon, moon to sun... Give peaceful rest to the living dead.|N/A|OoT}}</ref> When played on either the [[Fairy Ocarina]] or the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}}, the "Sun's Song" will allow [[Link]] to change night to day, or day to night.<ref>{{Cite|Like I told you before, with that song, you can turn day to night or night to day whenever you want. When you're in darkness, that  song should bring the sun's light to you. You should try it.|Composer Brothers|OoT}}</ref> It can also freeze [[ReDead]]s and [[Gibdo]]s inside the room within which the melody is played.<ref>{{Cite|Restless souls wander where they don't belong, bring them calm with the {{Color|OoT3D Yellow|Sun's Song}}.|N/A|OoT}}</ref> If Link plays the song near certain areas on the ground in which [[Navi]] turns green, a special type of [[Fairy]] will appear that refills Link's magic meter.

When this melody is played, one of two things can happen to the surrounding area and Link's location. In areas where time flows normally, Link will remain in his current location, whereas in locations where time normally stands still, Link is returned to the entrance to that area. Examples of these effects are [[Hyrule Field]] and [[Kakariko Village]], respectively. When the "Sun's Song" is played in Hyrule Field, Link stays where he is, and the sun quickly sets before the moon is summoned. When this melody is played in Kakariko Village, however, the screen goes black, and when Link is visible again he is at the entrance of Kakariko Village. This song is especially useful for completing the [[Gold Skulltula]] token side quest, as they are only found on the overworld during night.

Since its introduction in {{OoT|-}}, the "Sun's Song" has become the song to signify morning in several games.{{Which}}

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Stub}}
The notes of the "{{Term|MM3D|Sun's Song}}" signify morning before the a given area's main theme begins. While the {{Term|MM3D|Song|link}} is not playable by Link without the use of glitches, its function of skipping to the next morning or night is fulfilled by the "{{Term|MM3D|Song of Double Time|link}}."

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Stub}}
A more basic version of the "Sun's Song" appears in {{TWW}}, known as the "[[Song of Passing]]," which serves the same function as the "Sun's Song."

==Trivia==
*The Sun's Song is heard at the beginning of the [[Hyrule Field]], [[Termina Field]] and "[[Clock Town (Day 1)]]" themes.
*It is possible that the Sun's Song was supposed to appear in {{MM}} due to it being found in the files and may have been replaced by the "[[Song of Double Time]]," given their same functions. Unlike the "Song of Double Time," the "Sun's Song" can be used on the Night of the [[Final Day]] and will result in a Game Over if Link does not stop it by going into another area or quickly playing the "[[Song of Time]]."<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1MIa_sat8M4 Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (N64) - Beta Code Fun - Sun's Song & Saria's Song] YouTube</ref>
