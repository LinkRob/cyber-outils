The {{Term/Store|Know-It-All Brother|Know-It-All Brothers|Series, OoT, OoT3D|plural}} are three [[Kokiri]] brothers in {{OoT}}.<ref name="Sign">{{Cite|Visit the Know-It-All Brothers for any of your Swordfighting Questions.|Sign|OoT}}</ref>

==Biography==
All three brothers are very intelligent, and they serve as the instructors of {{OoT|-}}<nowiki>'</nowiki>s game mechanics, presenting them clearly without patronizing the listener (one of them even prepares Link for the lengthy lecture he is about to begin).<ref>{{Cite|If you want to know about the map and items, just ask me. But don't ask unless you want to hear a long explanation.|Know-It-All Brother|OoT}}</ref> They hang out inside their house near the [[Forest Training Center]], always ready to help out inexperienced players, giving out somewhat useful information on how to use the sword.

Their physical figure is that of a male Kokiri model: typical Kokiri apparel with hats pointing up, locks of hair covering their eyes, and a mellow smile with a slight overbite. Their guardian fairies change color, much like that of other Kokiri.

The Know-It-All Brothers will comment on problems in the [[Kokiri Forest]] in Link's adult years;<ref>{{Cite|Since the Great Deku Tree withered, more meanies have been appearing in the forest... I'm scared!|Know-it-all Brother|OoT}}</ref> though after the forest temple has been completed, they will comment on its improvements.<ref>{{Cite|Since a fair wind has begun to blow, I'm sure Link will return soon!|Know-It-All Brother|OoT}}</ref> 

==Trivia==
*Given that they live near the Forest Training Center and that the [[Treasure Chest|Kokiri Sword's chest]] sits near a [[sign]]post advertizing the Know-It-All Brothers' services,<ref name="Sign"/> it is possible that the Know-It-All Brothers own or manage the training center. Thus, it is also possible that the Kokiri in the Training Center is actually a fourth Know-It-All Brother.
