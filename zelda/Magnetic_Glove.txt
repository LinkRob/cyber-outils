{{Term/Store|Magnetic Glove|Magnetic Gloves|Series, OoS, FS|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 131 {{Exp Game|OoS, FS}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Magnetic Gloves|The power of the Magnetic Gloves can pull metal objects to you or push them away. You may find the gloves have other uses, too.}}
[[File:Magnetic Glove South.png|left]]
The {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Glove}} are found in the {{Term|OoS|Unicorn's Cave|link}} in {{OoS}}. They are used to defeat {{Plural|OoS|Magunesu|link}} and {{Plural|OoS|Iron Mask|link}}. The {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Glove}} are also used to solve puzzles, cross chasms with {{Plural|OoS|"S" Stone|link}} and {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Block|link}}, and draw large metal orbs to slay {{Plural|OoS|Enemy|link}}. These {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Glove|display=Gloves}} are used extensively in the boss fight against {{Term|OoS|Digdogger|link}}, wherein {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} can use the {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Glove}} to push and pull a spiked ball to hit {{Term|OoS|Digdogger}} in the eye. When it splits into smaller eyes, {{Term|OoS|Link}} can use the spiked ball to hit them before they reform.

==={{FS|-}}===
{{Manual|FS|Magnetic Glove|With this item equipped, you can use magnetic power to repel yourself from or pull yourself to a nearby magnetic block. Every time you press the A Button, the glove reverses polarity. You can also use this item to attract other players.}}
[[File:MagnetBlueFS.png|left]]
{{Plural|FS|Magnetic Glove}} can be used to pull or push other {{Plural|FS|Links|link}} or to pull a {{Term|FS|Links}} to a magnetic block in {{FS}}. Each time the A-Button is tapped, the polarity of the {{Term|FS|Magnetic Glove}} is reversed.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= With this item equipped, you can use magnetic power to repel yourself from or pull yourself to a nearby magnetic block. Every time you press the A Button, the glove reverses polarity. You can also use this item to attract other players.|game= FS|page= 43}}</ref>
