'''''The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap — The Official Nintendo Player's Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Nintendo Power]] for {{TMC}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 112 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{TMC}}, along with collectibles and secrets such as {{Plural|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}}, and {{Plural|TMC|Figurine|link}}.
