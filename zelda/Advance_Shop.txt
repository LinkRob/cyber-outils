The {{Term/Store|Advance Shop||Series, OoS, OoA}},<ref name="OoS">{{Cite|Advance Shop & Know-It-All Birds' Hut|Sign|OoS}}</ref><ref name="OoA">{{Cite|Advance Shop & Shooting Gallery|Sign|OoA}}</ref> also known as the '''100 Rupee Advance Shop''',<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, sir... to the 100 Rupee Advance Shop.|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref> is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is a unique store as it can only be accessed when the game is played on a [[Game Boy Advance]].<ref>{{Cite|We're only found on Game Boy Advance!|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The shop is adjacent to the [[Lynna Village]] [[Shooting Gallery]] in {{OoA|-}},<ref name="OoA"/> and adjacent to the [[Know-It-All Birds' Hut]] in {{OoS|-}}.<ref name="OoS"/> It is not available in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] Virtual Console or [[Nintendo Switch Online]] version of either game, as the 3DS uses the [[Game Boy Color]] emulator for the game.

The Advance Shop is run by [[Stockwell]], who is amazed that [[Link]] managed to find the store.<ref>{{Cite|I'm amazed you found us!|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref> Every item sold in the Advance Shop costs 100 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Everything is 100 Rupees. Don't let our secret out!|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref> The shop's merchandise consists of a [[Gasha Seed]] and two [[Magic Ring]]s. Once appraised, the first ring, called an Advance Ring in the shop, will become the GBA Time Ring in {{OoA|-}}, or the GBA Nature Ring in {{OoS|-}}. If Link buys every item, Stockwell has nothing more to sell.<ref>{{Cite|You've bought it all, so I'm closing shop.|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref>
