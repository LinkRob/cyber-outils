{{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Spear}} are upgraded {{Plural|BotW|Boko Spear|link}} that have been enhanced using {{Term|BotW|Animal|link}} bones.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are used most often by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Blue Moblin|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipent, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} made by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Known to be light and easy to use, they deal a decent amount of damage.<ref name="Inventory"/> When scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} {{Term|BotW|Amiibo|link}} has a chance of spawning a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Spiked Boko Spear}}.

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Spear}} will float in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} if they are dropped. They will also burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}.
