{{Term/Store|Buliara||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Buliara|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Buliara}} is the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} captain of the guard who accompanies {{Term|BotW|Riju|link|display=Lady Riju}}, Chief of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I thought I could relax at last, now that Buliara has come of age. But then something like this happens...|Rima|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|When I was captain of the guard, such a failure would have been absolutely unthinkable. It's just pathetic.|Rima|BotW}}</ref> Throughout the day, she can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} To the right of Riju of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She is known as the best spear master in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Buliara is the best spear master in the whole town, of course!|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, she carries a {{Term|BotW|Golden Claymore|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Riju}} notes in {{Term|BotW|Chief's Diary|link|display=her diary}} that {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} was the one who informed her of her mother's passing and her new role as Chief.<ref>{{Cite|Buliara told me today that Mother has passed on and gone to a better place... She said that my mother's death means I am now the chief of the Gerudo people.|Chief's Diary|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Riju}} left {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to investigate {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} was the one who rescued her when she got too close.<ref>{{Cite|I turned Patricia toward home, but a monster spooked her, and she fled directly toward Naboris again... The next thing I remember is Buliara standing over me, her face full of worry.|Chief's Diary|BotW}}</ref> Since then, she has not left {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s side, blaming herself for the theft of the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ever since I went to investigate the Divine Beast, Buliara has made a point of never leaving my side. Even now, with such a valuable heirloom stolen by thieves, she refuses to tear herself away from me even for a moment. She takes all the blame for the theft, however. Nothing can change her mind about being responsible.|Chief's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first meets {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} at the start of the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} when he first enters the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}} disguised as a woman. {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} will stop {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he approaches {{Term|BotW|Riju}} on her {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|link|display=throne}} and demands him to explain his business here.<ref>{{Cite|You stand before {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Riju}}, chief of the Gerudo! Declare your business, but come no closer!|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Riju}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Link}} for some time, she will mention to {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} that she recalls her mother speaking of a fallen swordsman that was placed into a deep sleep by the {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link|display=Princess of Hyrule}} when the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}} happened.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Buliara, a memory just jumped into my head—something my mother spoke of. When the Calamity happened, the princess of Hyrule placed a fallen swordsman into a deep sleep.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She also recalls that the swordsman's name was {{Term|BotW|Link}}, hesitantly wondering if the {{Term|BotW|Link}} before her is that same swordsman.<ref>{{Cite|That swordsman, much like our new friend here, was named Link...though it always seemed more legend than fact.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not carrying the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link|display=legendary sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} will point out that Link is not carrying the sword from those same legends.<ref>{{Cite|But, Lady Riju! Do those same stories not also say that the Hylian Champion carried a {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}}? I see no such weapon in this person's care.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> After more conversation, {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} suddenly notices that Link is actually a man.<ref>{{Cite|...I don't remember ever hearing of a Hylian vai among the Champions. Wait a moment... You're a voe!|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Riju}} states that it is a great crime for a man to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, but she dismisses this due to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s status a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A voe within our walls is a great crime. But a voe who is a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}}...|Riju|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Riju}} soon reasons that if {{Term|BotW|Link}} truly is a {{Term|BotW|Champions}}, then he may be able to enter {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} and calm its anger.<ref>{{Cite|If you truly are a Champion, perhaps you will be able to enter {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} and calm its anger|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Buliara}} urges {{Term|BotW|Riju}} not to trust a stranger with such an important task,<ref>{{Cite|Lady Riju! I don't see how you can trust a complete stranger with something as important as this task.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> suggesting that he prove himself worthy by recovering the stolen {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}.<ref>{{Cite|Perhaps if this person were first to prove his worth by recovering your stolen Thunder Helm...|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Riju}} agrees with this suggestion, and {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} assures her that recovering the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} should be an easy task for {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is truly a {{Term|BotW|Champions}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, yes... An interesting suggestion.|Riju|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You needn't worry, Lady Riju. If he truly is a Champion, this should be an easy task for him.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Teake|link|display=Captain Teake}} in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}} nearby can tell him about the thieves who stole the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}.<ref>{{Cite|Our soldiers can tell you all you need to know about the thieves who stole Lady Riju's heirloom. Head through this arch. It will lead you to the barracks. Then, seek our {{Color|BotW Blue|Captain Teake}}, and ask for a full report.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} again, she asks him what is wrong and says that if he is truly the {{Term|BotW|Champions}} of legend, the task should be simple.<ref>{{Cite|What's wrong? If you truly are the Champion of legend, recovering the chief's heirloom should be simple.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> She then reiterates that he should see {{Term|BotW|Teake|display=Captain Teake}} to learn more about the thieves and comments that this is his chance to show them what he is capable of.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to know more about the thieves, then you should speak to {{Color|BotW Blue|Captain Teake}}. Just head down those stairs.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Use this chance to show us what you're capable of!|Buliara|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns with the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}, {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} will direct him to {{Term|BotW|Riju}}, saying she is on the second floor.<ref>{{Cite|If you are looking for {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Riju}}, she's on the {{Color|BotW Red|second floor}}.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> She notes the special permission he has received to be able to enter {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom|link}}, and she reminds him to be on his best behavior.<ref>{{Cite|Normally people aren't allowed into her sleeping quarters, but she has given you permission it seems... Be on your best manners. If she deems you to be a disturbance to her, it will not end well for you!|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> During this time, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} notes in the {{Term|BotW|Chief's Diary}} how {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} trusts in {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s abilities and does not mind that he is a man, which is a surprise to her.<ref>{{Cite|My prayers were answered! One of the Champions of old has appeared! Buliara was wary, but he has proven himself by taking our heirloom back from the thieves and calming Naboris. She now seems to trust his abilities. She even seems to not mind that he is a voe. She's so focused and serious. Always by the book. I'm surprised she's OK with him.|Chief's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} calms {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}, {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} tells him that they are indebted to him.<ref>{{Cite|A lot happened, but... We are truly indebted to you.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> She also informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} is his ally now,<ref>{{Cite|If you ever find yourself in trouble, you are always welcome to come here. Everyone in this town is your ally now.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> but she warns him that he should still hide that he is a "voe".<ref>{{Cite|However, you still mustn't let anyone discover that you are actually a voe.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref>

When {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack is active, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} about the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb|link}}, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} guarded by the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}. Though she is shocked he knows about it,<ref>{{Cite|What? How do you know about that?!|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} admits that it has been stolen by the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|treasure of my people}}... The {{Color|BotW Red|sacred orb}} that we have guarded for generations is gone. Another precious item stolen by the {{Color|BotW Red|Yiga Clan}}.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> She also shares rumors that the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}} have begun regrouping at the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Those fiends! I thought they had been wiped out, though I had heard rumors of them regrouping at their base.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can recreate the {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker|link}} if they are broken by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Buliara}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion's arms}} are treasures of the Gerudo, much like the Thunder Helm. If anything happens to them, you must let me know immediately.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> She will make a new {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} can supply her with one {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}, five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Scimitar of the Seven}}, huh? To make it, I'll need {{Color|BotW Blue|one diamond}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|five}} pieces of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}, and a {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo scimitar}}.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> To make a new {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker}}, {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} requires one {{Term|BotW|Diamond}}, five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Daybreaker}}, huh? To make it, I'll need {{Color|BotW Blue|one diamond}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|five}} pieces of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}, and a {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo shield}}.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref>
