'''Training Construct''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{TotK}} commonly found in [[Shrine of Light|Shrines of Light]], specifically the Combat Training shrines.
==Characteristics==
{{Compendium|name= Training Construct|number=159|desc= The Zonai employed advanced technology and magic to create this instructional construct. Its color indicates that it is equipped with special combat-training functions. It is even capable of using Fuse to modify and strengthen its weapons.|locs= Unknown|loot= Zonai Charge}}
Training Constructs are a type of [[Captain Construct]] designed to train individuals who enter the Shrines of Light. This [[Construct]] behaves similarly to a regular Captain Construct, except it is designed to help the player learn combat moves they can do, like [[Perfect Dodging]] and [[Sneakstrike]]. Because of this, they only do moves that allows the player to do the move the Shrine is teaching. The Constructs do not get damaged by any forms of attacking, except any related to the move being learned and is only defeated when the Shrine ends. An example of this is the Construct in the [[Kyononis Shrine]], which is immune to any forms of damage except those from Perfect Dodges and [[Perfect Guard|Perfect Guards]].
