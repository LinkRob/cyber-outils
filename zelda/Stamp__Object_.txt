{{Term/Store|Stamp|Stamps|Series, BotW}} are objects in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Stamp|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|BotW}}
Stamps are used on the [[Map]] to add waypoints. There are nine available types of Stamps, each representing key elements of the game. A total of 100 Stamps can be placed at once on the map.
