{{Term/Store|Jengo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Jengo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Jengo}} is a {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} miner who works for the {{Term|BotW|Goron Group Mining Company|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. During the day, {{Term|BotW|Jengo}} mines alongside {{Term|BotW|Dorill|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Greyson|link}} for {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}}. At night, {{Term|BotW|Jengo}} takes his break next to the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} while {{Term|BotW|Bohrin|link}} works the night shift.<ref>{{Cite|♪Break time!♪|Jengo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Jengo}} is focused on his work, so much so that he is startled when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches him during this time.<ref>{{Cite|Bah! You scared me! What is it?!|Jengo|BotW}}</ref> He prefers not to be disturbed until he is on his break.<ref>{{Cite|I need to concentrate while I'm workin'. If you wanna talk, come see me when it's {{Color|BotW Red|nighttime}}.|Jengo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well then... Time to work. Don't talk to me while I'm workin', OK? I need to concentrate.|Jengo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} keeps speaking to him, he informs him that it is not yet time for his break.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, it's you again. It's not time for me to stop workin' yet, ya know...|Jengo|BotW}}</ref> When he is finished with work for the day, {{Term|BotW|Jengo}} asks that {{Term|BotW|Link}} wait until he sits down before they talk.<ref>{{Cite|If you wanna talk, lemme sit down a bit first, OK?|Jengo|BotW}}</ref>

During {{Term|BotW|Jengo}}'s break, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to ask him questions.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, yeah. What is it?|Jengo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Anything else you want to ask?|Jengo|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} ask about {{Term|BotW|Jengo}}, he will introduce himself himself.<ref>{{Cite|[Who are you?] Me? I'm Jengo.|Jengo|BotW}}</ref> He explains that because he and the other {{Plural|BotW|Goron}} are digging up {{Plural|BotW|Gem}}, {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}} is able to prosper.<ref>{{Cite|I'm a proud Goron of Goron City. If we dig up {{Color|BotW Blue|ore}} like this, Goron City will prosper. Everyone wins!|Jengo|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about their location, {{Term|BotW|Jengo}} explains that they are at the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine}}.<ref>{{Cite|Take a look around. This is where we dig up {{Color|BotW Blue|ore}}. We call it the Southern Mine.|Jengo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} has not been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Jengo}} shares that the {{Term|BotW|Goron Group Mining Company}} used to mine at the {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain Summit|link}} before the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} appeared, forcing them to move to the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine}}.<ref>{{Cite|But right now, we've got that {{Color|BotW Blue|lizard monster}} sittin' up on {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}. Normally we can pull up good {{Color|BotW Blue|ore}} from near the mountain peak... But for now, we have to mine here instead.|Jengo|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to speak with {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}}, the boss of the {{Plural|BotW|Goron}}, for more information.<ref>{{Cite|You should ask the {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} for more details. He's in Goron City.|Jengo|BotW}}</ref> After the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Jengo}} excitedly reveals that {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} has instructed them to start mining again.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|lizard monster}} on {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}} calmed down, so the {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} has instructed us to start mining this site again!|Jengo|BotW}}</ref>
