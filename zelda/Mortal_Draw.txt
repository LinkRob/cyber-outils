The {{Term/Store|Mortal Draw||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an ability in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Very well. My fifth hidden skill is... The {{Color|TPHD Red|mortal draw}}! Let it be hewn into your mind!|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TPHD|Mortal Draw}} is the fifth {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill|link}} that {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can learn. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can learn this {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}} by first finding a {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone|link}} to awaken the [[Hero's Spirit|Golden Wolf]].<ref>{{Cite|Search for the statues that howl with the {{Color|TPHD Red|sound of the wind}}. Seek the sound that calls the spirit of the beast to awaken me again.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref> The {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone}} needed to learn the {{Term|TPHD|Mortal Draw}} can be found in southern {{Term|TPHD|Lake Hylia|link}}, high up on a ledge. Because {{Term|TPHD|Link}} must climb a ladder to reach this {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone}}, he must be able to transform into {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} and back at will, so this {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}} is impossible to get before obtaining the {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword|link}}. After howling with the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}}, the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} instructs {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to find him while in human form.<ref>{{Cite|Let teachings of old pass to you... Take sword in hand and find me...|White Wolf|TPHD}}</ref> The Golden Wolf also marks the location of where he can be found on {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Map|link}}. Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} returns to human form, he can find the Golden Wolf waiting along the path to the {{Term|TPHD|Desert Caravan|link}}, south of the {{Term|TPHD|Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|link}}. Approaching the Golden Wolf will return {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to the clouded area where the {{Term|TPHD|Hero's Spirit}} can teach {{Term|TPHD|Link}} the {{Term|TPHD|Mortal Draw}}.

To perform the {{Term|TPHD|Mortal Draw}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} must stand still with his {{Term|TPHD|Sword|link}} sheathed without Z-Targeting his opponent. Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s foe gets close enough, {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s hand will move to his hilt and the Action Button will change to "Draw", which causes {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to quickly draw his {{Term|TPHD|Sword}} and perform a devastating blow to the nearest enemy.<ref>{{Cite|You must sheathe your sword and cast aside the most basic of sword skills, the {{Icon|TPHD ZL}}-target. You must wait until your foe is upon you… Then, before the enemy can see through your ruse, you must quickly draw your blade with {{Icon|TPHD A}}.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref> This will instantly kill the enemy, no matter the hits taken beforehand. The {{Term|TPHD|Mortal Draw}} does not work on {{Plural|TPHD|Boss|link}} and most {{Plural|TPHD|Middle Boss|link}}. Despite the Spirit's claim that 'there is no defense' to the Mortal Draw, several enemies can indeed dodge or block it, such as Dynalfos or Darknuts.
