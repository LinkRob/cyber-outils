"{{Term|TotK|{{Page Name}}|Series, TotK}} I and II" are a series of {{Plural|TotK|Side Quest|link}} in {{TotK}}.

== Overview ==
=== Part 1 (I) ===
{{Term|TotK|Symin|link}} is teaching children at the school of {{Term|TotK|Hateno Village|link}} about history of {{Term|TotK|The Calamity|link}}. However the children are too disinterested believing Symin's lecture. Admitting the dullness of the lesson, Symin may elaborate into detail if he had a reference relating to the Calamity, which all the way back in his hometown {{Term|TotK|Kakariko Village|link}}.

{{Term|TotK|Link|link}}, who was there observing the class, decided to help with Symin's lecture. He went all the way to Kakariko Village to find anything relating to The Calamity. He found a painting of it in the Chief's house and took a {{Term|TotK|Camera|link|display=picture}} on his {{Term|TotK|Purah Pad|link}}. He then brought the picture to the classroom and showed it to the children, delighting them.

With the children now intrigued, Symin was able to finish his history lesson about the Calamity. He thanked Link for the assist and gave him 10 {{Plural|TotK|Hylian Rice|link}}. He then proceeds to teach the class the next lesson.

=== Part 2 (II) ===
Symin's teaches a lesson about home economics. He lectures to the students about {{Term|TotK|cooking|link}} food. He gives a student a question involving Hylian Rice and {{Term|TotK|Monster Extract|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Take Goron spice, Hylian rice...and mix in...<font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">monster extract</font>. What do you get then?|Symin|TotK}}</ref> but they were unable to answer the question due to lack of insight.<ref>{{Cite|Azu: Monster what?!<br>Karin: Um, aren't monster parts only used for medicines?<br>Aster: Yeah! If you try to cook with monster parts, it's a disaster every time!<br>Sefaro: No one would eat curry made with monster stuff! I call trick question!|Various|TotK}}</ref> He corrected the answer results in {{Term|TotK|Monster Curry|link}},<ref>{{Cite|In fact, combining monster extract, Goron Spice, and Hylian rice makes <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">monster curry</font>.|Symin|TotK}}</ref> but the students were unable to believe him due to the complexity of the lecture.<ref>{{Cite|Hm, this lesson may be too advanced.|Symin|TotK}}</ref>

To prove what Symin meant, Link heads to a {{Term|TotK|Cooking Pot|link}} to use Monster Extract to cook the aforementioned Monster Curry. Then he brought it to the children as proof. The children tasted the curry and found it surprisingly tasty. Having believed the monster extract, Symin was able to finish his lecture.

Symin thanked Link for the assist again. With the students now looking up to Link at lessons, Symin gives him access to the school's field where he can use it to cultivate plants.
