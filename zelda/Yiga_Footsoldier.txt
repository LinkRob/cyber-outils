{{Term/Store|Yiga Footsoldier|Yiga Footsoldiers|Series, BotW, TotK, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring enemies in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Yiga Footsoldier}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TotK, SSBU, HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Yiga Footsoldier}}
Yiga Footsoldiers are members of the [[Yiga|Yiga Clan]] who follow their leader, [[Master Kohga]].<ref>{{Cite|Begone, enemy of my maaaaster!|Traveler|BotW}}</ref> They are always hunting [[Link]] down, attempting to assassinate him and stop his quest to defeat [[Calamity Ganon]]. Yiga Footsoldiers can be found in various places throughout Hyrule, usually appearing disguised as travelers, revealing themselves as Yiga Footsoldiers after Link speaks to them.

After defeating Kohga, Yiga Footsoldiers might also appear out of thin air to ambush Link.<ref>{{Cite|I'll send you to join Master Kohga!|Traveler|BotW}}</ref> They will sometimes attempt to entice Link by selling Mighty Bananas,<ref>{{Cite|Just take a look at these fine bananas! Buy them up quick before you've missed your chance!|Traveler|BotW}}</ref> only to attack him regardless. Yiga Footsoldiers will often teleport around the area, levitating and shooting Link with a Duplex Bow. If wielding either a Vicious Sickle or a Demon Carver, they will teleport above Link and [[Down Thrust]] into the ground with their weapon, telegraphing the attack with a glowing red ring of nine [[Hylian Language#Sixth Hylian Script|Hylian letters]]. These letters parallel the nine [[Sheikah (Language)|Sheikah letters]] preceding some of [[Monk Maz Koshia]]'s attacks.

While infiltrating the Yiga Clan Hideout, [[Yiga Blademaster]]s will summon two Yiga Footsoldiers if Link is spotted. When defeated, Yiga Footsoldier will teleport away, dropping their weapon and leaving behind a random amount of Mighty Bananass and Rupees.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Yiga Footsoliders reappear in {{TotK|-}}, now wielding [[Eightfold Blade]]s instead of their Vicious Sickles and/or Demon Carvers, though they retain their Duplex Bows and disguising abilities, now with the addition of Researchers and Fancy People, in addition to a [[Cucco]] and Zelda herself, but otherwise act the same as in ''Breath of the Wild'' and are found in [[The Depths]] and all over Hyrule. They can also ride [[Zonai]] vehicles such as [[Big Rig]]s, [[Monocycle]]s with either [[Flame Emitter|Flame]], [[Frost Emitter|Frost]], and/or [[Shock Emitter|Shock Emitters]], [[Gloomdredger|Gloomdredgers]], Tanks, and [[Headlight Raft]]s. Link can disguise himself as a Yiga Footsoldier with the Yiga Armour Set, causing them to mistake him for a new recruit blowing their sneak attacks.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSBU|-}}===
Though Yiga Footsoldier's do not appear directly in {{SSBU}}, items resembling the attire worn by Yiga Footsoldiers known as the [[Yiga Clan Mask]] and the [[Yiga Clan Outfit]] appear as a [[Mii Headgear]] for all [[Mii Fighter]]s and a [[Mii Outfit]] for [[Mii Swordfighter]]s respectively. Mii Swordfighters disguised as Yiga Footsoldiers appear in [[Sheik#Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|Sheik]]'s and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]'s [[Classic Mode]] Routes, "[[Masquerade]]" and "[[Wisdom Prevails]]", with the latter appearance referencing the Yiga Footsoldiers' goal of assassinating Zelda in {{BotW}}.

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
Yiga Footsoldiers retroactively debut in {{HWAoC}}, now having voice acting and are fought both in [[Gerudo Town]] and the [[Gerudo Desert]], and several other scenarios. They appear as the weakest soldiers of the Yiga Clan.  Two Yiga Footsoldiers are also seen kneeling before [[Astor]] in the [[Lost Woods]]. On top of that, there is a new variant that stands at about twice the size of a regular Yiga Footsoldier, called Yiga Captains. Each can switch between their Vicious Sickle and Duplex Bow at will and can sustain much more damage; they act as [[Captain (Hyrule Warriors)|Keep Bosses]]. Later in the game, they are sacrificed to revive the Blight Ganons [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|after they failed to kill the Champions]], causing the Yiga Clan to join their former enemies to take down Astor and later, Ganon. They disguise themselves as Hylain Soldiers during "[[The Yiga Clan Attacks!]]" and even slip into their old Sheikah professions to infiltrate the [[Royal Ancient Lab Ruins|Royal Ancient Lab]] in an attempt to stop [[Robbie]] and [[Purah]] from learning the full results of analyzing [[Terrako]].<ref>{{Cite|I was too careless. This is bad.|Robbie|HWAoC}}</ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*It is possible that the Yiga Footsoldiers were inspired by the [[Ache]] from {{TAoL}}, since they both disguise themselves as travelers or townspeople before attacking when approached by {{Term|Series|Link}}.
*Unlike most enemies in Breath of the Wild, Yiga Footsoldiers do not die when they are defeated. 
*It is possible to cause Yiga in Breath of the Wild to run around Link by using Stasis+ on them during their dash attack and moving to a set spot. Their turning angle will result in an endless circle.
