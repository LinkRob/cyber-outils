==Biography==
Horgum is an elderly man who resides in the Toyku Lighthouse, and is possibly its care taker. He gives [[Link]] a [[Lantern]] so he may light his way through [[Koridai]]'s darkness.<ref>{{Cite|Gets mighty dark with all the evil about. Keep this lantern full, it will light your way.|Horgum|TFoE}}</ref> If spoken to again, he comments that there are not many [[Koridian]]s left on the island, and adds that [[Droolik]] around the Face of {{Term|TFoE|Glutko|link}} is among the few left.<ref>{{Cite|Not many left, Link. There's Droolik, 'round the side of Glutko.|Horgum|TFoE}}</ref>
