{{Term/Store|Hammerwear||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|Hammerwear is ideal for lovers of Magic Hammers. It's popular with turtle enthusiasts, oddly enough...}}

{{Term|TFH|Hammerwear}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}. It dresses {{Term|TFH|Link}} in a turtle shell, helmet, and shoes identical to those of a {{Smw|Hammer Bro}} from the ''{{Smw|Mario (franchise)|Mario}}'' series.<ref>{{Cite|Did you use a koopon to buy that? I mean, a coupon? ♥|Stylish Woman|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hammerwear ups the power of Magic Hammers. It looks kind of... familiar. Maybe my bro has one?|Madame Couture's Apprentice|TFH}}</ref> Wearing {{Term|TFH|Hammerwear}} increases the size, swinging speed, and power of the {{Term|TFH|Magic Hammer|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Increases the power, speed, and range of the Magic Hammer. Hammer away!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> as well as the radius of the shockwave created when it strikes the ground. The design of the {{Term|TFH|Magic Hammer|display= Hammer}} also changes to that of a {{Smw|Sledge Bro}}'s weapon.

==Trivia==
*The description for {{Term|TFH|Hammerwear}} in {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's}} shop contains the phrase "Time to hammer!".<ref>{{Cite|Time to hammer!<br/>♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦<br/>Swing farther and deal more damage. Swing your Hammer like it's nothing. Nothing, I say!|Catalog|TFH}}</ref> This may be a reference to the phrase "Hammer time", popularized by hip hop artist {{Wp|MC Hammer}}.
