'''Flux Construct II''' is a [[Boss]] from {{TotK}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Compendium|name= Flux Construct II|number=161|desc= A construct composed of interconnected blocks, made by the Zonai with advanced techniques and magic. This model has been upgraded to process complex commands and assume modified shapes. To provide sufficient energy to power its gigantic form, this construct contains a massive store of Zonai charge.|locs= Lanayru Sky Archipelago, Mount Lanayru Depths|loot= Zonai Charge, Large Zonai Charge}}
Flux Constructs II are Constructs made of floating cubes held together by [[Zonai]] magic. They can change forms, including a humanoid shape, a giant cube, and a flying form that can be [[Ascend|Ascended]] through.

Flux Constructs II fly too high for Ascend, so Link must use [[Recall]] on a fired cube while standing on it to reach them. Their cube form aggressively slams down, while their humanoid form attempts to trample him. When defeated, they drop [[Zonai Capsule|Zonai Capsules]], [[Zonai Charge|Zonai Charges]], and [[Flux Construct II Core|Flux Construct II Cores]].
