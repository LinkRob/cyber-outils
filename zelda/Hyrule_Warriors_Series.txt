The '''{{HW|-}} Series''' refers to several spin-off titles in {{TLoZ|series}}:

*'''{{HW}}''', the first and original game in the series for the [[Wii U]].
*'''{{HW|L}}''', an enhanced port of the original game with added features, characters and Scenarios for the [[Nintendo 3DS]].
*'''{{HW|DE}}''', a fully enhanced port from both games with all the features, characters and Scenarios for the [[Nintendo Switch]].
*'''{{HWAoC}}''', a ''Warriors''-style game based on {{BotW}}.
