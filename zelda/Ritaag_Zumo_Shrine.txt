The {{Term/Store|Ritaag Zumo Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ritaag Zumo Shrine}}<br/>Ritaag Zumo's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Ritaag Zumo's blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Ritaag Zumo Shrine}} is located at the center of the {{Term|BotW|Rist Peninsula|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Sea|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is buried underground next to a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|link}} among the ruins near the base of the {{Term|BotW|Rist Peninsula|display=Peninsula}} states that only a hero with the nearby {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} will be granted a Trial.<ref>{{Cite|Only a hero bearing this {{Color|BotW Red|ancient orb}} shall be granted the right of {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} on Rist Peninsula.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> Reading this {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|display=Tablet}} begins the "{{Term|BotW|Into the Vortex|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

To complete the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} and reveal {{Term|BotW|Ritaag Zumo Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must carry the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} to the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} at the center of {{Term|BotW|Rist Peninsula}}. Once the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} is placed into the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}'s {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ritaag Zumo Shrine}} will rise up from the ground, and the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.<ref>{{Cite|You discovered the hero's trial by carrying the orb to the center of Rist Peninsula.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ritaag Zumo|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} is proof of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ritaag Zumo. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Ritaag Zumo|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Ritaag Zumo Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Ritaag Zumo}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Ritaag Zumo}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will congratulate him for reaching the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule.}}|Ritaag Zumo|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, they give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ritaag Zumo|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ritaag Zumo|BotW}}</ref>
