'''"The White Knight"''' is the third episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}} to be broadcast, but was the ninth in production order.

==Plot==
:''See also: [[The White Knight/Episode transcript|Episode transcript]]''
[[File:Facade Link Zelda (The White Knight).png|thumb|left|Prince Facade meets Link and Zelda]]
While {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}} are in an unnamed village of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Hyrule|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Tinsuit|link}} rise from the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}} and begin to attack the young hero and the princess.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Tinsuits! My favorite playmates!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> Although {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda are successful in defeating several {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Tinsuit}},<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Thanks. Ah-hah! Now for that Ruby.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> one of them calls an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Octorok|link}} from the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}} and grabs the now-swordless {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} since Zelda has run out of ammunition for her {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Crossbow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Zap him, princess!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The bow's empty!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> Suddenly, a man known as {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Prince Facade|link}} appears just in time to help {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda defeat the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Octorok}} with a quick hit from his {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Crossbow}}, sweeping Zelda off her feet.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= I am Prince Facade from Arcadia. I have traveled the world and viewed its wonders but none have been your match in beauty.|name=Prince Facade|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}, used to being the hero, doesn't like being outdone by this handsome stranger, and quickly becomes jealous. Meanwhile, Zelda is enchanted by Facade and begins to disregard {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}},<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Uh, Link! Where are your manners? Stand up and meet Prince Facade! This is my friend, uh, well, an acquaintance--somebody I know slightly--his name's Stink.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> leaving him to walk back to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|North Castle|link}} while she and the prince go by horse.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, I'm sure you'll be fine, Link! [...] See you back at the castle, Link!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I'm afraid [my horse] can't carry three.|name=Prince Facade|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref>
[[File:Link dressed up (The White Knight).png|thumb|right|Link's new outfit|150px]]
When they arrive back at the castle, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian|link}} and Zelda are fascinated by Facade and his tales of heroism, with the princess not even acknowledging {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}'s existence.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Well, there's Link, of course, but he's not in the prince's class. Prince Facade is wonderful.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}, feeling left out,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I swear... It's like Zelda doesn't even know I exist anymore.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> decides to try and match Facade, and attempts to dress more like him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=No! I'll handle it myself. Did you get those clothes I wanted? [...] Zelda wants a prince, she'll get a prince.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> What he fails to realize, despite {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte|link}}'s warnings,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I did, but I think you're making a mistake, Link.|name=Spryte|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> is that the outfit he has chosen looks completely ridiculous.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Link, what ARE you wearing?|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> When {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} makes his appearance in front of Zelda and the King, he embarrasses himself further by tripping over. They burst out laughing, causing {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} to storm out, angry and humiliated.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=She laughed...she laughed at me! She laughed!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> Deciding that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Prince Facade}} can protect the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce|link}} of Wisdom for Zelda, he leaves the castle.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Save your breath, Spryte! I'm gone! I'm Zelda wants her Triforce protected, her prince precious can do it. I just quit! [...] Let's hit the road, Katherine. This place makes me sick.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref>

Meanwhile, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}}, having learned that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Prince Facade}} has arrived in {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Hyrule}}, knows that the Prince has a fatal flaw - his extreme vanity - and decides to take advantage of this in his latest bid to steal the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=So...Prince Facade, the sickenly handsome face of Hyrule, is he. Hmm...this could be most fortunate. I've dealt with Prince Facade before, and I know his weakness. He's brave enough, but he's vain, very vain! Proud of his looks. He'd never let himself get dirty, not even to save the princess! So...if things work out as I plan, this could be my opportunity to capture Princess Zelda, and conquer all of Hyrule!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> When {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} leaves the castle, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} summons several {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Enemy|link}} from the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Evil Jar|link}} to cause chaos in the castle. {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Tektite|link}} arrive and attack Facade while {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin|link}} throw Zelda out the window, where a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zola|link}} readily catches her. The {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zola}} carries Zelda out of the castle, with Facade in pursuit, but when the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zola}} jumps into the water, Facade hesitates on the bank, not wanting to get his clothes wet and muddy.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, mud... No, I'm afraid not. I DO have a reputation to maintain.|name=Prince Facade|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> Zelda screams for him to save her, but he refuses, unwilling to get dirty.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Sorry, princess. No can do, I'm afraid. These are my hero's clothes, and heroes do not simply get dirty.|name=Prince Facade|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref>

However, all is not lost as {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} is still in earshot of Zelda's screams.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=She's in trouble! I, well... Aw, heck. I'm such a sucker.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> He mutters that Zelda is not worth saving, but he'll do it anyway, and hurries to save the princess.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hold it right there, Zola. She's not worth saving, but I'm doing it anyway.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> He defeats the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zola}} with three {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam|link}} and rescues Zelda.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hey, it's a dirty job, princess, but somebody's gotta do it.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Prince Facade}} quickly tries to make up with the angry Zelda,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Zelda, you're safe! I'm so glad, really. I would have done it myself, but you know how it is.|name=Prince Facade|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> but she has none of it, and makes him fall into the mud. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda walk back towards the castle with the young hero once again trying to get the princess to kiss him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=You're my kind of girl, princess. Kiss me.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=3}}</ref> As they leave, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Prince Facade}} throws up his hands in surrender, ending the episode.
