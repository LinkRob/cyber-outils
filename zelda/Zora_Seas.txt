The {{Term/Store|Zora Seas||Series, OoA}} constitute a vast sea in the land of [[Labrynna]] in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 255}}</ref> As the name suggests, the Zora Seas are home to the [[Zora]]s (most of which are found within the [[Zora Village]]). The Zora Seas are found to the west of the [[Sea of Storms]], from whence the water flows. To the north is the [[South Shore]] and the [[Fairies' Woods]]. The only noticeable landmark of the Zora Seas is the [[Eyeglass Island Library]].

==Overview==
[[File:ZoraSeasFilthy (Past).png|thumb|170px|left|The filthy Zora Seas in the past]]
When [[Link]] first arrives at the Zora Seas in search of the seventh [[Essences of Time|Essence of Time]], he will find the entire sea polluted with {{Term|OoA|Toxic Water|link}}, causing much discomfort among the [[Zora]]s,<ref>{{Cite|The seas are filthy--koff! Koff! I can't swim in peace.|Zora|OoA}}</ref> and blocking off much of the sea. When Link arrives at the palace of the [[King Zora]] in [[Zora Village]], the throne will be empty, the palace abandoned. Link learns that, long ago, their king perished of an unknown illness, leaving the Zoras without a king.<ref>{{Cite|This was the palace of our ruler, King Zora, but long ago the king fell ill. Ever since, we've continued on with no king.|Zora|OoA}}</ref> The young hero then travels to the past, where he finds the king near death due to an illness for which the Zoras cannot find a cure.<ref>{{Cite|Our ruler, King Zora, suddenly fell ill... We've done all that we can, but his condition does not change.|Zora|OoA}}</ref> Thankfully, Link is able to cure the Zora King by giving him a [[Magic Potion]], thus saving his life.<ref>{{Cite|We believe we need a special medicine called a Magic Potion to cure King Zora's illness.|Zora|OoA}}</ref> Atferwards, the King Zora expresses his gratitude towards Link, promising to never forget his good deed. The king also confirms that his illness was indeed caused by the filthy sea water.<ref>{{Cite|Filthy water has been flowing in from the east lately. Perhaps that is what made me ill. Anyhow, I'll never forget your deeds!|King Zora|OoA}}</ref>

Returning to the present, Link will find the king alive and well. However, [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]], the guardian of the Zoras (who holds the Essence of Time inside him), has also fell ill to the {{Term|OoA|Toxic Water}}. The King Zora will only give Link permission to enter Jabu-Jabu once he has cleansed the seas and cured Jabu-Jabu.<ref>{{Cite|Great healer! What do you ask? You wish to enter Jabu-Jabu? My permission is needed to enter Jabu-Jabu, but I can't permit just anyone to see our guardian. Anyhow, the filthy seawater has left Jabu-Jabu near death. If you say you must, then find a way to get rid of this filthy seawater and save Jabu-Jabu.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> To aid him on his quest, King Zora grants the young hero the key to the [[Eyeglass Island Library]], in the hopes that he can research the cause of the {{Term|OoA|Toxic Water}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the key to Eyeglass Isle Library east of here. It is an ancient library, so I do not know if this key still works, but perhaps you can research why the seawater is so filthy. I trust you to save Jabu-Jabu.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref>

Once Link opens the library in the past, he will learn of a [[Fairy Queen]] who protects the seas from evil. However, the fairy, who lived on an island west of the library, has supposedly disappeared.<ref>{{Cite|The Fairy Queen used to live on the island west of here, but she vanished recently... Surely the Fairy Queen could do something for these seas...|Old Man|OoA}}</ref> Some inhabitants of the library have reported seeing a [[Octorok|strange beast]] inside the cave where the fairy used to live.<ref>{{Cite|Those who have seen a strange beast on the isle to the west say the beast is always there, never attacks, and just stares as if asking for help. Some even claim it speaks. It's nonsense!|Old Man|OoA}}</ref> When Link goes to investigate for himself, he discovers that the strange beast is actually the [[Fairy Queen]], who has been cursed by [[Veran]]. Veran's curse is also the cause of the {{Term|OoA|Toxic Water}}. The Fairy Queen will ask Link to bring her some [[Fairy Powder]] to cure her.<ref>{{Cite|Please listen, young one! You may not believe this, but I am the Queen Fairy, guardian of this sea! The curse of an evil one named Veran has made me appear so! The magic of Fairy Powder can turn me back to normal. Veran has poisoned the sea. Soon all living things in it will perish! Please go find some Fairy Powder!|Fairy Queen|OoA}}</ref> 

The Fairy Powder is found in the back room of the Eyeglass Island Library in the past.<ref>{{Cite|This is the Eyeglass Isle Library. Fairy Powder is said to be stored in back, but I've yet to see it.|Human|OoA}}</ref> To obtain it, Link must solve a puzzle which requires that he have the [[Book of Seals]],<ref>{{Cite|Fairy Powder is locked away in back, but without another Book of Seals, no one can reach the powder|Old Man|OoA}}</ref> found in the back room of the library back in the present.<ref>{{Cite|There used to be something in back for sealing things away, but now there's just one crinkled old book.|Human|OoA}}</ref>

With the Fairy Powder, Link is able to cure the Fairy Queen, who will then cleanse the seas.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, young one! Now I can cleanse the seas of evil!|Fairy Queen|OoA}}</ref> Link then gains permission from the [[King Zora]] to enter [[Jabu-Jabu's Belly]].<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, our seawater is clean again, and Jabu-Jabu's health has returned. All is well. I never go back on my word. I dub you Zora Hero and permit you to enter Jabu-Jabu. You may go see Jabu-Jabu.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref>
