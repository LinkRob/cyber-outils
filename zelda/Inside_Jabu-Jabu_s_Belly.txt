{{Term/Store|Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly||Series, OoT, OoT3D}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 148}}</ref> also known as '''Lord Jabu-Jabu's Belly''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 208}}</ref> is the third {{Term|OoT3D|Dungeon|link}} in {{OoT}}. It is also the final dungeon for [[Link]] as a child.

==Entrance to the Belly==
The dungeon, unlike most others throughout the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|franchise]], is set inside the body of [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]], who spends every day on [[Zora's Fountain]]. To enter, [[Link]] must drop a [[Fish]] from his [[Bottle]] in front of Jabu-Jabu, causing him to widely open his mouth and suck both the fish and Link inside. His mission here, while he is looking for the third [[Spiritual Stone]], the [[Zora's Sapphire]], is to rescue [[Princess Ruto]], as requested by her father [[King Zora]].<ref>{{Cite|The evidence seems clear. Of course, you'll go find Ruto. You can pass through here to the altar of Lord Jabu-Jabu.|King Zora|OoT}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
[[File:OoT Link carrying Princess Ruto.png|left|thumb|232x232px|Gameplay screenshot of Link carrying Princess Ruto in one of the dungeon's upper rooms]]
While inside, Link must initially avoid the vast variety of parasitic enemies, including an especially [[Parasitic Tentacle|large but colorful tentacle-like organism with electrified appendages in the shape of unstable pillars]] and jellyfish-like creatures called [[Bari]] and [[Biri]],<ref>{{Cite|Lord Jabu-Jabu is very strange today... There are electrified jellyfish and strange holes around...|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref> since they are hazardous to kill without the [[Boomerang]], the key weapon found inside the dungeon. Shortly thereafter, Link locates Princess Ruto inside what appears to be the stomach, who initially refuses to accept any help from the young [[Hero of Time]],<ref>{{Cite|You! Who are you?! I am Ruto, Princess of the Zoras. What?! Are you saying my father asked you to come here to save me? I'd never ask anyone to do such a thing! "Letter in a Bottle?"  I have no idea what you're talking about! My father is worried about me? I don't care! Anyway, I can't go home right now. And you... Get out of here! understand?!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref> and accidentally falls into a hole.<ref>{{Cite|Ooooh Noooooo!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref> Once found again by Link, she commands him to help her recover the Spiritual Stone, but will not move on her own. The young hero must carry Ruto throughout many rooms to help her find it.<ref>{{Cite|You're that worried about me? Then I will give you the honor of carrying me! However...I won't leave until I find the thing I'm looking for. You'd better believe me!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref> These rooms include areas where Link must fight the numerous tentacles with the help of the Boomerang, so that new certain areas can be explored. He eventually enters a large room that has a central platform with the Zora's Sapphire on it. When thrown to the platform, the young [[Zora]] princess obtains it for herself.<ref>{{Cite|That's it! That's what I've been looking for!Throw me up there! Onto the platform!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref>

After taking the Zora's Sapphire, the platform abruptly rises with Ruto still on it, and Link is ambushed by a [[Big Octo]] that must be defeated in order to continue.<ref>{{Cite|Oh my goodness! I finally found... My mother's stone... [...] Keeeyaaaah! What is this?! An octopus?!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref> After destroying the aquatic monster, the princess is nowhere to be found, and Link continues until he locates the creature causing Jabu-Jabu's discomfort: an electrical parasite called [[Barinade]], the dungeon's [[Boss]]. Link eventually defeats Barinade, rescues Princess Ruto (who is visibly shaken and scolds him), and receives the Zora's Sapphire as a reward, not before almost kissing him and declares the Hero of Time her future husband.<ref>{{Cite|Well, anyway, you saved me, so I guess I'll reward you. What do you wish? Just tell me... [...] My mother gave it to me and said I should give it only to the man who will be my husband. You might call it the Zora's Engagement Ring! All right! I'll give you my most precious possession: Zora's Sapphire!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref>

This dungeon has many strange changes in the ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Master Quest|Master Quest]]'' version of the game. Instead of uvula-like switches on the ceilings of Jabu Jabu's intestinal tract, there are now [[Cow]] head [[Switch|switches]] attached to walls that operate the opening of doors. As for [[Enemy|enemies]], [[Lizalfos]] and [[Like Like]]s were added to several rooms, the [[Parasitic Tentacle]]s were dispersed evenly and in larger quantities throughout Jabu-Jabu's insides, and invisible [[Keese]] were supplied to one room that includes an extra [[Gold Skulltula]]. Only three [[Tailpasaran]]s appear in an optional room with a Gold Skulltula, and [[Business Scrub]]s were deleted from the ''Master Quest'' version. In terms of [[Item|items]], the [[Dungeon Map]], [[Compass]], and [[Boomerang]] can be obtained much earlier; the room that contained the Boomerang in the original version now contains the Compass, and vice versa. Other changes include the placement of a Cow in a room, the addition of a water pillar in the suckhole room, and the presence of [[Bombchu]]s, which are used to complete various new puzzles throughout the dungeon. Princess Ruto is utilized much less as a gameplay mechanic, as she is only required to hit one switch.

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese release, the dungeon is called '''Inside Lord Jabu-Jabu'''.
*If [[Link]] attempts to slash the organic walls with his [[Sword]], blue blood can be seen being splattered as with some enemies and certain rooms temporally pulse faster. This also applies to a few items like the [[Slingshot|Fairy Slingshot]].
*If Link were to drop or throw [[Princess Ruto]] in front of enemies, it is possible to defeat them.
*The three [[Parasitic Tentacle|Parasitic Tentacles]] that Link must defeat in order to progress to certain doors of Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly (which [[Navi]] calls them "[color] slimy thing") come in the colors red, blue, and green, respectively. Coincidentally, their hues match Link's respective [[Clothes|Tunics]] he wears throughout the game as well as the [[Spiritual Stone|Spiritual Stones]] and [[Golden Goddess|Golden Goddesses]].
*A remixed version of the dungeon's theme was used in {{TWW}} as [[Jabun]]'s theme.
*In certain areas of the dungeon, specifically at area leading to the three rooms of the Parasitic Tentacles, if Link were to remain still, the floor will slowly move him to where the walls lead.
*It is possible to [[Permanently Missable Items|permanently lose out the Dungeon Map]] if the player doesn't collect it after defeating the red Parasitic Tentacle and continues through the dungeon until the Octorok, when Ruto is no longer available to use to hold the switch down, making it impossible to re-enter the room.
