{{Term/Store|Puzzle|Puzzles|Series|plural}} are a recurring aspect of {{TLoZ|Series}},{{Fact}} particularly in [[Dungeon]]s. They are some of ''The Legend of Zelda'' series' main trademarks, and some of the more simplistic puzzles are repeated throughout the series, of which the Block, Enemy, Return, Switch, Target, and Torch are the most common.

==Types of Puzzles==
===Block Puzzle===
[[File:BlockPuzzleWW.png|left|thumb|150px|Link moving Blocks around in {{TWW|-}}{{'}}s Tower of the Gods]]
The '''Block Puzzle''' involves moving [[Block]](s) into a certain position, or the rearranging of blocks to form a picture. This may be to press a switch, or to create a step which can be used to gain high ground, etc. The [[Poe Sisters|Poe Sister]], [[Amy]], from the Forest Temple in {{OoT|-}} incorporates the block-picture approach in her defeat. A variation of this puzzle, as seen in {{OoA}}, is to push a block in a way that a certain color is on top, and the block itself in a hole, igniting a flame.
{{clear}}

===Enemy Puzzle===
The '''Enemy Puzzle''' puzzle involves a room or area in which one or several enemies must be defeated to move on. In {{TLoZ|Series}}, this puzzle takes three forms: the multi-pronged strike, the miniboss puzzle, and the boss puzzle. 

[[File:Darknut-twilight.jpg|right|thumb|150px|Link against a mini-boss [[Darknut]] in {{TP|-}}]]
*The {{Name|Fan|Multi-Pronged Strike}} enemy Puzzle pits Link against multiple foes in a single room, often enemies with collective strength much lower than that of [[Link]]. Seen in the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}} of {{OoT|-}}, and in the [[Lakebed Temple]] of {{TP}}, all of these enemies must be defeated for the enemy puzzle to be accomplished. Sometimes, such puzzles will keep a reward or door hidden unless the foes in the room are defeated in a certain order.
*The '''Miniboss''' enemy puzzle is seldom seen outside of dungeons because they involve the defeat of a single, significant enemy, often carrying or barring access to a dungeon item. Some examples include [[Death Sword]] from the [[Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|Arbiter's Grounds]] and [[Ook]] from the Forest Temple in {{TP|-}}.
*In the '''[[Boss]]''' puzzle, a large, strong monster that holds a [[Heart Container]] prevents [[Link]] from finishing the temple until it is defeated. This type of enemy puzzle is only seen in dungeons: examples include [[Gleerok]] from {{TMC}} and [[Gyorg (Boss)|Gyorg]] from {{MM}}.

===Return Puzzle===
One of the most commonly reccurring puzzles is the '''Return Puzzle''', a puzzle in which a player must leave a room and return later with an extra item or key, making that room passable. The return puzzle most often exists in dungeon rooms with many exits.

===[[Switch]] Puzzle===
[[File:Gerudo Symbol Switch.jpg|right|thumb|150px|Link pressing down a Floor Switch]]
'''Switches''' in the form of levers and buttons are prevalent in the series.
*'''Lever''': Needs to be pushed or pulled. Some inaccessible levers require use of [[Hookshot]] (pull), [[Seed Shooter]] (push), or [[Bomb]]s (when timing is needed).
*'''Button''': Many varieties exist, but can be broken down to positive and negative actions. Positively activated buttons require a weight to be added or placed upon it. Negatively activated buttons require a weight to be removed from it.
{{clear}}

===Target Puzzle===
[[File:Arrow puzzle MM.jpg|left|thumb|150px|Link shooting an eye switch with a Fire Arrow in {{MM|-}}]]
The '''Target Puzzle''', simply involves shooting a certain switch to open a door. For example, shooting a golden eye above a door, with a projectile, can open that door. Sometimes the target moves, or Link is on a moving platform, making this puzzle more difficult.

'''Projectile Items Necessary to Complete Target Puzzles:'''
*[[Bow]] and [[Arrow]]
*[[Slingshot]]
*[[Crossbow]] 
:::Note: The Hookshot, [[Clawshot]], and other similar items do not work when it comes to target puzzles, as they are not true projectile weapons.
{{clear}}

===Torch Puzzle===
[[File:OOT3D Torch Puzzle.png|right|thumb|195px|Link lighting Torches with a Deku Stick in {{OoT3D|-}}]]
The '''Torch Puzzle''' involves the lighting of one or more [[torch]]es by fire in order to receive access to another room, treasure chest, or the like.

'''Viable Ways to Complete Torch Puzzles''':
*[[Fire Arrow]]s
*[[Din's Fire]]
*Shooting a regular arrow through fire in the direction of a torch
*Lighting a [[Deku Stick]] from another fire source, to set a torch alight
*Using the [[Lantern]]

{{Clear}}

==Trivia==
*Although many temples and dungeons in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series incorporate at least one of these techniques, the temples of {{OoT|-}}, {{MM|-}}, and {{TMC|-}} make use of all of them.
