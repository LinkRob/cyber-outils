==Overview==
Fugo the blacksmith apprentice is having a falling out with Rohan, his master. Since the [[Marbled Rock Roast|marbled rock roasts]], Fugo had sold the materials needed to craft the {{Term|TotK|Boulder Breaker|link}}. Because the two have no crafting materials to work with, Rohan barred Fugo which has left him speechless.

To make up for Fugo's mistake, {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} replaced the lost materials for Fugo. Delighted to have them, Fugo proceeds to forge a Boulder Breaker to prove Rohan his spirit as a blacksmith. Upon presentation, Rohan was content of Fugo's handiwork. Seeing he has mastered his craft, Rohan permitted Fugo to inherit his blacksmith.

As thanks for helping him out, Fugo gave Link the Boulder Breaker he just made.
