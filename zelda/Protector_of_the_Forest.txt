{{Term/Store|Protector of the Forest||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Protector of the Forest|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Protector of the Forest}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, with {{Term|HWAoC|Revali|link}} as its pilot. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Prime Meat|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Thigh|link}}, and 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

Throughout the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}}'s objective is to defeat 2,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. The different {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} include {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Hinox|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Lynel|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}} and many {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}} scattered around the map. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 2,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
