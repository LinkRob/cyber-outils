'''Jovan''' is a character who is introduced in the {{OoS|Himekawa}}. He is a member of the {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Troupe of Performers|display=Troupe of Traveling Performers|link}} along with {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din (Oracle)|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Impa|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Parot|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Rishu|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Mel and Rom|link}}. He is the boss of the troupe and offered for {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link|link}} to participate during their performances, which {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} tried out and continued doing. He is rather greedy, as he tries to bully {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} into keeping it up when he is having second thoughts. One of the performers is his son, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Rishu}}.
