{{Spoiler Alert}}
'''Feathered Fugitives''' is a [[Side Quest]] in {{TotK}}.
<div style="clear:both;"></div>

==Overview==
[[Teli]], of [[Agus|Agus's]] group, suddenly scared off some [[Cucco|Cuccos]] when he tried to use them for [[food]]. He needs [[Link]]'s help to find them before the [[Riverside Stable|stable]] staff find out.

You can find the missing Cuccos: One inside the stable, on top of the stable's effigy, next to a theater stage, in a [[tree]], and stranded on a [[river]] [[rock]]. After the Cuccos were returned, Teli thanked Link for the assistance and gives him a [[Silver Rupee]] as a reward.
