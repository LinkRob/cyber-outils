==Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Grayblade|link}} uses the {{Term|TMC|Grayblade Possession Technique}} to teach {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} the {{Term|TMC|Roll Attack|link}} by taking control of his body and showing him how the move is performed.<ref>{{Cite|But one must feel the technique, not just hear about it! That's why I will now possess your body so as to demonstrate the technique!|Grayblade|TMC}}</ref> After this, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must repeat the action he was shown in order to earn the {{Term|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}} for the ability.
