{{Term/Store|Cojiro||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an animal and item in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|OoT3D|Cojiro}} is received in {{OoT}}'s {{Term|OoT3D|Trading Quest|link}} from the {{Term|OoT3D|Cucco Lady|link}} after waking up {{Term|OoT3D|Talon|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}}. Unlike most other {{Plural|OoT3D|Cucco|link}}, its feathers are entirely blue. {{Term|OoT3D|Cojiro}} originally belonged to her brother, the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Craftsman's Son|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Its name is {{Color|OoT3D Red|Cojiro}}, and it used to be my brother's Cucco. Its blue body is quite charming. It's so cute!|Cucco Lady|OoT3D}}</ref> During the seven years of {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}}'s absence, {{Term|OoT3D|Cojiro}} was given to the {{Term|OoT3D|Cucco Lady}} and the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Craftsman's Son}} disappeared from the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|display=Village}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Cojiro}} hasn't crowed since.<ref>{{Cite|Since my brother has gone, it's strange, but Cojiro has stopped crowing.|Cucco Lady|OoT3D}}</ref> Taking it to the {{Term|OoT3D|Lost Woods|link}}, where the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Craftsman's Son}} disappeared to, causes it to finally crow once more. Upon showing Cojiro to him, the Master Craftsman's Son exclaims that it can usually only be tamed by a "nice person",<ref>{{Cite|Cojiro? Why? Normally only a nice guy like me can tame you...|Master Craftsman's Son|OoT3D}}</ref> after which he gives {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} the {{Term|OoT3D|Odd Mushroom|link}} in exchange for it.
