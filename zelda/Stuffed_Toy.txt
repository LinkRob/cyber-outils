'''Stuffed Toy''' is an Object and [[Echo]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Stuffed Toy|A cute little plush guy. For creepy, unknown reasons, ghosts are drawn to it.<ref name="Stuffed Toy">{{Cite|A cute little plush guy. For creepy, unknown reasons, ghosts are drawn to it.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Stuffed Toy is a bear stuffed animal found in the [[Graveyard]] beneath the [[Northern Sanctuary]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]] after defeating the surrounding [[Ghini]]. When summoned, they cost one Echo energy and can be used to lure ghost-like enemies such as Ghinis.
