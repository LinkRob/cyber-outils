The {{Term/Store|Delivery Bag|Delivery Bags|Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an item in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> It is used for carrying an array of items.<ref name="TWW Guide 12">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Nintendo|page= 12}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Delivery Bag is an item received on [[Dragon Roost Island]] from [[Quill]],<ref name="TWW Guide 12"/> the [[Rito]] postman. Quill can be seen wearing a bag that is identical to the Delivery Bag on his back, suggesting that it may be a common item for the Rito [[Island Postal Service]]. It is first used to carry [[Father's Letter]] to [[Komali]], but is used more commonly to carry the [[Decorations]] during the [[Island Trading Sequence]].<ref name="TWW Guide 12"/>

The Delivery Bag is also used to deliver other [[Letter|letter]]s,<ref name="TWW Guide 31">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Nintendo|page= 31}}</ref> such as [[Maggie's Letter]] and [[Moe's Letter]] in the Maggie and Moe sidequest and [[Baito]]'s letter. Letters can be retrieved from [[Mailbox|red mailboxes]] around the [[Great Sea]], which can be seen bouncing around if a letter is ready for pick up.<ref name="TWW Guide 31"/> Lastly, this is where the [[Cabana Deed]] is stored.
