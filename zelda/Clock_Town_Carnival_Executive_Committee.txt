The {{Term/Store|Clock Town Carnival Executive Committee||Series, MM, MM3D}} is the dedicated group responsible for the festivities that take place in [[Clock Town]] every year during the [[Carnival of Time]].<ref name=fireworksshow>{{Cite|The door to the Clock Tower stairway opens at midnight on the eve of the carnival. A Fireworks show will mark the occasion.<br/>Clock Town Carnival Executive Committee|Sign|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Clearly, it's your job to ensure the carnival's operation, but that's if people are here for it!|Captain Viscen|MM}}</ref> Members include Clock Town councilmen, such as [[Mutoh]],<ref>{{Cite|If the soldiers wish to run, then run, Viscen! We councilmen will stick to tradition.|Mutoh|MM}}</ref> the town [[Carpenters]], and [[Madame Aroma]]. The committee ensures the smooth operation of the carnival traditions, including construction of a festival tower,<ref>{{Cite|Urgently Recruiting! Workers wanted to build the festival tower for the carnival. See Mutoh.|N/A|MM}}</ref> town performances,<ref>{{Cite|If you need to speak to me about the carnival performances, make an appointment, please.|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref> and a fireworks show.<ref name=fireworksshow/>

==Overview==
Two Committee Members, Mutoh and one of his apprentices, are locked in a heated debate in [[Mayor Dotour]]'s office during the three days [[Link]] is in [[Termina]]. As [[Captain Viscen]] and another member of the town's defense unit push for an evacuation,<ref>{{Cite|This carnival will be a success! I've never heard of a defense unit abandoning its town!|Mutoh|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Most of the townsfolk already have taken shelter without waiting for the Mayor's orders. The only ones left are public servants and committee members... Mr. Mayor and Carnival Committee Members, please order those who remain to evacuate!|Captain Viscen|MM}}</ref> Mutoh and his apprentice insist on celebrating the carnival as planned.<ref>{{Cite|You cowards! Do you actually believe the moon will fall? The confused townsfolk simply caused a panic by believing this ridiculous, groundless theory. The soldiers couldn't prevent the panic, but outside the town walls is where the danger is! You want answers? The answer is that the carnival should not be canceled!|Mutoh|MM}}</ref>

Meanwhile, Madame Aroma, who is responsible for coordinating the carnival's performances,<ref>{{Cite|Madame Aroma, the Mayor's Wife<br/>In charge of town performances|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> is forced to cancel the scheduled entertainment provided by [[The Indigo-Go's]], and the opening act by the [[Gorman Troupe]], due to the loss of [[Lulu|the diva's]] voice.<ref>{{Cite|Actually, there's been an unusual accident in Great Bay... And due to this unusual accident, Lulu, the diva in The Indigo-Go's, has lost her voice.|Toto|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The details are quite long, so I'll spare you. At any rate... I must cancel our performance at this year's Carnival of Time.|Toto|MM}}</ref>

By the [[Third Day]], the arrangements for the [[Carnival of Time]] are looking grim. All of the entertainment has been canceled, and the festival tower is incomplete because the craftsmen have fled.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Deku kid! You waitin' for us to finish the bridge from the festival tower to the clock tower entrance up there? Sorry! My apprentices panicked and ran away! This is all we get for a festival tower. It's not quite tall enough, is it? Sheesh! Even if we went to the top of the festival tower, we wouldn't be able to get up to the clock tower entrance! I apologize for all this. I wish I could scare that moon away!|Mutoh|MM}}</ref> At midnight, the Fireworks show, which is probably supplied by the Clock Town [[Bomb Shop]],<ref>{{Cite|There'll be a fireworks show on the night before the carnival. You should go see it. ...Come again.|Bomb Shop Owner|MM}}</ref> goes on as planned, and the door to [[Clock Tower]] opens, but the descending [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] has scared off any potential tourists and most of the citizens and public servants.<ref>{{Cite|At this time every year, we are overrun by tourists! So why is the town empty?|Captain Viscen|MM}}</ref>

If Link manages to defeat [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]] before the Moon crushes Termina, the Carnival can proceed on the [[New Day]]. The Gorman Troupe and The Indigo-Go's perform in the [[Milk Bar]], Latte, the town's best venue.<ref>{{Cite|We were scheduled to do a show, but it got canceled. This place has a reputation for being a great live venue, so it's a shame.|Toto|MM}}</ref>
