{{Term/Store|Smash Boost Power||Series, HWL, HWDE}} is an ability in {{HW|L}}.<ref>{{Cite|Smash Boost Power|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
Smash Boost Power activates when two or more {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} gather next a [[Giant Boss]].<ref>{{Cite|If {{Color|HWDE Green|multiple playable warriors gather near a Giant Boss}}, they'll receive all kinds of effects. The more warriors gathered, the stronger the effect becomes!|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> It can be first activated against [[Manhandla]] in "[[The Sorceress of the Valley]]" after [[Lana]] tells the other Warriors to gather around it.<ref>{{Cite|I hav an idea! Everyone gather around the threat!|Lana|HWDE}}</ref> If the Warriors gather near Manhandla, Lana uses her [[Magic]], enhanced by the [[Valley of Seers]],<ref>{{Cite|Wow, it's amazing what sort of magic I can tap into when I'm in the Valley of Seers!|Lana|HWDE}}</ref> to pool the strength of the Warriors together,<ref>{{Cite|I'll use my magic to pool together everyone's strength. We'll be unstoppable!|Lana|HWDE}}</ref> activating Smash Boost Power. After defeating Manhandla, whether Smash Boost Power was activated or not, the ability will activate any time multiple {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} gather around a Giant Boss.

When Smash Boost Power activates by gathering two Warriors, 50% of the [[Magic Gauge]] of the gathered Warriors will be filled and Boss Magic will be absorbed, allowing the Warriors to fill their Magic Gauge by attacking enemies.<ref>{{Cite|If {{Color|HWDE Green|two playable warriors}} get together, 50% of their {{Color|HWDE Green|Magic gauge will instantly fill}}! Plus, {{Color|HWDE Green|you will be able to refill the Magic gauge just by attacking enemies}}!|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> Gathering three {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} causes the [[Weak Point Gauge]]s of nearby Giant Bosses to deplete faster while gathering four Warriors causes Giant Bosses to receive more damage.<ref>{{Cite|When {{Color|HWDE Green|three playable warriors}} gather, nearby {{Color|HWDE Green|Giant Bosses' weak point gauges will deplete faster}}. When {{Color|HWDE Green|four playable warriors}} gather, the {{Color|HWDE Green|Giant Bosses will receive greater amounts of damage}}.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>
