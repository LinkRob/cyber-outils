{{Term/Store|Deku Like|Deku Likes|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are stationary, carnivorous plants that share traits with [[Deku Baba]]s and [[Like Like]]s. They are found in the Forest temple.

==Characteristics==
Deku Likes are found only in the {{Term|TPHD|Forest Temple|link}} and [[City in the Sky]], and appear to form a symbiotic relationship with [[Big Baba]]s. To defeat a Deku Like, [[Link]] must throw a [[Bombling]] or a regular [[Bomb]] into its mouth. It will then chew on the explosive, causing it to blow up, and the Deku Like will wither away. If Link falls down from above into one of these plants, the Deku Like will swallow him whole, consuming one [[Heart]] and then spitting him out onto the ground nearby. Only four of these plants appear in the course of the game (not including those attached to Big Babas); three of which are found within the Forest Temple.
