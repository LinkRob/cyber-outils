The {{Term/Store|Boating Course||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (G6) is an island in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features==

The island is comprised of two tiny [[island]]s on the [[Great Sea]]. One island has a [[Secret Cave]] with the [[Submarine Chart]]; the other is home to [[Loot]] the sailor, who runs the Yacht Game in the waters surrounding the islands. It is located south of the [[Forest Haven]].

For a fee of thirty Rupees, [[Link]] can use [[King of Red Lions|his boat]] to race against time and collect as many [[Rupee]]s as he can. The course consists of two types of floating barrels: barrels that float horizontally and those that float vertically. Link's boat can ride over the horizontally floating barrels, but has to jump over the vertically floating ones. Large amounts of money can be accumulated here, but the time limit will be reduced by 10 seconds each time the game is played, up until it has been brought down to two minutes.
