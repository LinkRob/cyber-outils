It is usually located somewhere within the kingdom of [[Hyrule]].

==Features and Overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
Link's House is located in a somewhat central location of Hyrule, with [[Hyrule Castle]] nearby in the north in {{ALttP}}. It is a simple building, containing basic, wooden furniture, and is home to both Link and [[Link's Uncle|his uncle]]. Next to Link's bed, which is situated in the house's northwest corner, are three [[Pot]]s that each contain a recovery [[Heart]]. A [[Treasure Chest]] located in the southeast corner of the house contains the [[Lantern]], which will likely be the first item that Link collects in his quest. However, if the Treasure Chest is opened after already receiving the Lantern elsewhere, it will instead contain a Blue [[Rupee]].

At the beginning of the game, Link receives a telepathic plea for help in his sleep from [[Princess Zelda]], who mentions that she has been locked in the dungeon of Hyrule Castle by [[Agahnim]].<ref>{{Cite|Help me... Please help me... I am a prisoner in the dungeon of the castle. My name is Zelda. The wizard, Agahnim, has done... something to the other missing girls. Now only I remain... Agahnim has seized control of the castle and is now trying to open the seven wise men's seal.... ... I am in the dungeon of the castle. Please help me...|Princess Zelda|ALttP}}</ref> He wakes up in time to see his uncle about to leave the house, armed with a [[Sword]] and [[Shield]]. Upon seeing that Link is awake, Link's uncle promises that he will be back by morning and tells Link not to leave the house.<ref>{{Cite|Link, I'm going out for a while. I'll be back by morning. Don't leave the house.|Link's Uncle|ALttP}}</ref> Link, however, disobeys his uncle's orders and leaves to investigate the castle. Later, during the game's end credits, Link is shown to be practicing sword fighting with his uncle outside of their home.

Link's House is one of the locations that he can choose to start in when continuing the game, provided that Link is in Hyrule and not the [[Dark World]]. Later, after obtaining the [[Flute]], it also becomes one of the locations that Link can be transported to by the [[Flute Boy's Bird]].

In the Dark World, the [[Bomb Shop]] corresponds to where Link's House would be located.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{OoT}} begins with Link being woken up inside his house from a nightmare involving [[Princess Zelda]] by [[Navi]] the [[Fairy]], on the orders of the [[Great Deku Tree]]. Here at Link's House in the [[Kokiri Forest]] is where the two companions first meet, although Navi's first insistence is that Link leaves the house to visit the Deku Tree with haste. The house is more or less irrelevant in the game after a point, although an interesting feature that keeps its use alive is a scoreboard within the room, with which Link can inspect his achievements in various mini-games throughout Hyrule. 

When loading a saved game, young Link will begin in his home if the game was saved while he was not in a dungeon. Link can obtain a [[Cow]] that stays in his house after beating [[Malon]]'s record of the obstacle course at [[Lon Lon Ranch]] as an adult.<ref>{{Cite|You did it! ... I have to give you a present to commemorate your new record! The present is a little too heavy to give to you here, so I'll have it delivered to your house. Bet you can't wait to see it! Hee hee!|Malon|OoT}}</ref>

In {{OoT3D}}, Link has the option to sleep in his bed in the house. By doing this he can either recover his health, or access the [[Boss Challenge]] Mode, where he can refight any boss which he has defeated in his quest. A [[Sheikah Stone]] can also be found just outside his house.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, Link and his younger sister, [[Aryll]], live with [[Link's Grandma|their grandmother]] in a house on [[Outset Island]]. Located at the easternmost point of the island, it is a two-story home with a kitchen and sleeping area on the first floor, with its second floor serving as a loft to keep the family's [[Hero's Shield|shield]].

Intitially, Link is not at home, having fallen asleep at [[Aryll's Lookout]] a day earlier. Aryll soon summons her brother to the house so he can receive the [[Hero's Clothes]] from his grandmother. The house becomes fundamental to more elements of the game's plot than most other games in the series, with Link having to return on various occasions to find the Hero's Shield and to release a [[Bottle]]d Fairy onto his ailing grandmother,<ref>{{Cite|In fact, it might not be a bad idea for you to visit your hometown and family again after such a long time away.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Even a simple little island like this has to have something you can use as a shield.|Tetra|TWW}}</ref> resulting in her infinite offerings of free, revitalizing [[Elixir Soup]].<ref>{{Cite|You got Elixir Soup! This healthy soup your grandmother makes replenishes all of your life energy and magic power!|N/A|TWW}}</ref> A crawl space can also be found under the house, containing a Chest with an Orange Rupee.

Later in the game, Link also receives the [[Cabana Deed|Deed]] from [[Mrs. Marie]] to the Cabana on the [[Private Oasis]], thereby making it his.<ref>{{Cite|I own my very own cabana in a tropical oasis down south. But lately I haven't made use of it at all, so you can have it!|Mrs. Marie|TWW}}</ref> There he gains access to a labyrinth beneath the island, which houses a [[Triforce Chart]]. Link can also play a [[Sliding Puzzle]] and earn [[Rupee]]s as a reward in the Cabana.

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} lives with his grandfather, {{Term|TMC|Smith|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}'s famed swordsmith.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|TMC Red|Smith's Sword}}! It's a sharp blade made by your grandfather.|N/A|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The finest swordsmith in Hyrule. As a young man he was a great swordsman.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> The two-storied home doubles as a smith's forge in addition to being a residence. It has a similar appearance to its {{ALttP|-}} incarnation. It rests on a plateau in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} seems familiar with this home, as she is {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s childhood friend and visits early into the story to awaken {{Term|TMC|Link}} for the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Where's Link? The whole town is bustling for the annual Picori Festival! I thought he and I might go together. Would you mind terribly?|Princess Zelda|TMC}}</ref> Later in {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s journey, he has the option to stay here for the night to recover lost {{Plural|TMC|Heart|link}}, as well as {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Smith}}, who can always be found at home smelting.<ref>{{Cite|You want to fuse Kinstones? OK! Let's do this!|Smith|TMC}}</ref>

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, Link lives in a tree house in [[Ordon Village]] that has a similar appearance to Link's house in {{OoT|-}}. It contains a basement and a few platforms with ladders, and at the top there is a window which Link can look through. A pot of boiling soup can be found over a burning fire that is kept lit throughout the game. There is also a green flag with a triangular symbol, similar to the [[Triforce]], above the house's door. The basement contains a Treasure Chest with 50 Rupees in it, though the room is so dark that it may require the [[Lantern]] to find it. Also in the basement is a large mirror which Link can see his reflection in. Near the beginning of the game, [[Rusl]] will deliver the [[Wooden Sword]] to Link's House.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, Link's House appears in the same location it was in {{ALttP|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|Say what? This is your house? Looked empty-ish to me.|Ravio|ALBW}}</ref> However, it is much smaller and Link is the only person to live there at first. [[Ravio]] moves in shortly after Link's failed confrontation with [[Yuga]], and later sets up a [[Ravio's Shop|rental shop]] within the home.<ref>{{Cite|Is it okay if I stay here for a while? Just a couple of days, I promise!|Ravio|ALBW}}</ref> Link can return here at any time to rent items from Ravio. Eventually, Ravio allows Link to purchase the items he has for rent.<ref>{{Cite|I still haven't found anywhere else to stay. So I spruced things up in here. I've made it into a nice little shop for myself. Pretty great, isn't it?|Ravio|ALBW}}</ref> {{MM|Object}}, or possibly a replica of it, hangs on the wall of Link's House in {{ALBW|-}}. Though it serves no purpose in the story or regular gameplay, Link can [[Wall Merge|merge]] into the wall behind the mask to make it appear as though he is wearing it.

[[Lorule]]'s equivalent of Link's House is the {{Term|ALBW|Vacant House|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
In {{BotW}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can discover a {{Term|BotW|House|link}} that is currently in the process of being demolished by the {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction Company|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. If the boss, {{Term|BotW|Bolson|link}}, is spoken to, he will offer to let {{Term|BotW|Link}} buy the {{Term|BotW|House}} for 3000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, plus 30 bundles of {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}}. This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Hylian Homeowner|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. The building comes with a {{Term|BotW|Weapon Rack|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} as a housewarming gift. It is implied that Link actually owned the house 100 years before the events of the game.<ref>{{Cite|The old owner apparently went off to {{Color|TMC Blue|the castle}} to report for service. Never came back, never wrote, so away it goes!|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

After the {{Term|BotW|Link's House|display=House}} is purchased, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can buy additions such as a door, a {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}}, additional lighting, and {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} Racks, as well as having {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} decorate the exterior. Upon completion of the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} from {{TCB}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} obtains a {{Term|BotW|Picture of the Champions|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can hang the {{Term|BotW|Picture of the Champions|display=Picture}} up in his {{Term|BotW|Link's House|display=House}} upstairs on the wall near his {{Term|BotW|Bed}}. 

Sometime before the events of Tears of the Kingdom, Link's House changed ownership and is now referred to as "Zelda's Home". The inside has been mostly redecorated, save for the framed picture of the Champions from the DLC upstairs. The well in back has also become a place for Zelda to retreat to when she wishes to study in peace as players can find her journal about rebuilding Hyrule, drawings of her from the students of Hateno Village's school and a hairband accessory for Link to wear, allowing him to revert his hair to its old style from the prior game. Link can buy and construct a new home outside of Tarrey Town that includes more such as a stable for a registered horse, a hanging frame to display pictures taken with the Purah Pad, a kitchen and a goddess statue for increasing his Heart Containers or Stamina bar.
