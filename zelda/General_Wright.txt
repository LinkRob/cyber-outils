General Wright is a key character in {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
General Wright is the head of the Royal Guard. He serves the [[King of Hyrule#Echoes of Wisdom|King of Hyrule]] alongside [[Minister Lefte]].

After [[Princess Zelda]] returns safely to [[Hyrule Castle]], the four discuss strategies to address the Rifts appearing across Hyrule. Shortly after they finalize a plan, a Rift opens and engulfs Wright, the King, and Minister Lefte. From the Rift, a dark version of General Wright emerges, supporting the King's claim that Zelda is responsible for the Rifts.

Later, Zelda finds the real Wright in the Rift at [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]]'s Den, and he is rescued when it is closed. He and Lefte then assist in evacuating [[Hyrule Castle Town|Castle Town]] when the Rift in the castle consumes most of it, while Zelda enters the Rift to close it as she did with [[Jabul Waters]], [[Gerudo Desert#Echoes of Wisdom|Gerudo Desert]], and [[Suthorn Forest]].

In a hidden area of the castle, Zelda finds the [[Tecuum]] that were impersonating the King, Lefte, and Wright. Frustrated at Zelda's interference, the three merge into [[Echo Ganon|an Echo of Ganon]], but this is defeated.

After this, Wright remains at the Castle with Lefte and the King, offering information{{Clarify|EoW|reason=What info does he offer?}} to help Zelda with her quest.

Later, when Zelda has obtained the three Sanctions to claim the [[Triforce|Prime Energy]], Wright takes a group of soldiers to the [[Eternal Forest]] to secure the area for Zelda, but are attacked by [[Tekom|an Echo of her]] due to being too hasty to hear the briefing on this. After Zelda opens the way to [[The Great Deku Tree]], Wright returns to the castle since only Zelda, possessing the Sanctions, is permitted to proceed onward.
