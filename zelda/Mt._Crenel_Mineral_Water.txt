{{Term/Store|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water||Series, TMC}} is an item in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TMC|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water}} can be found on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}. It is a green-colored type of water that can be scooped with an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}} from a pool found in the mountain. {{Term|TMC|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water}} can be used to grow green beanstalks found in the area. It can also be delivered to a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}, who uses it to create {{Term|TMC|Blue Picolyte|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am working on my next Pico Bloom enhancement... All I need is some {{Color|TMC Red|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water}} to complete it.|Minish|TMC}}</ref>
