{{Term/Store|Princess Ruto||Series, OoT, OoT3D}},{{Exp Game|OoT}}<ref name="E 238"/> also known as simply {{Term/Store|Ruto||HW, HWL, HWDE}},{{Exp Game|HW}}<ref>{{Cite|Ruto|View Warriors|HWDE}}</ref> is a recurring [[Zora]] character in {{TLoZ|Series}}. She is the daughter and only child of [[King Zora]], the ruler of the Zora population residing in [[Hyrule]], and serves as the attendant of the Zoras' patron deity [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]], preparing his meals.<ref>{{Cite|Zora's Fountain is the source of the river. Lord Jabu-Jabu lives in the fountain. Lord Jabu-Jabu is the patron deity of the Zoras. Princess Ruto is in charge of preparing his meals, morning and night.|Zora|OoT}}</ref> Despite her royal beauty as the princess of the Zoras, as a child, Ruto appears to be a tomboy and is shown to be very strong-willed and stubborn even in the face of danger, a trait she would temper and yet benefit from in her adult years when danger once again threatened her people.<ref name=Quote4>{{Cite|We Zoras all respect the great King Zora! He has the dignity of a king. Oh yeah, he does. The grand King Zora has a Princess named Ruto. She's an only child. She's also a wild tomboy, and it seems like she's always causing the King a lot of trouble!|Zora|OoT}}</ref> Though she initially shows a selfish streak in her youth,<ref name=":0">{{Cite|They say that Ruto, the Zora Princess who is known for her selfish nature, likes a certain boy...|Gossip Stone|OoT}}</ref> she grows to become much more benevolent in her adult years, especially after she is awakened to the knowledge that she is the [[Sage|Sage of Water]] that guards the [[Water Temple]] beneath [[Lake Hylia]].

It is implied that Princess Ruto's mother passed away some time before the events of {{OoT|-}}, though she gave Ruto the [[Zora's Sapphire]] at some point in her youth. Ruto's mother told her that the [[Spiritual Stone]] serves as the so-called "Zora's Engagement Ring" by tradition, and instructed her to give it to the man that would one day become her husband, who happens to be [[Link]], the [[Hero of Time]].<ref name="OoT">{{Cite|My mother gave it to me and said I should give it only to the man who will be my husband. You might call it the Zora's Engagement Ring!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT Princess Ruto Child Artwork.png|left|thumb|298x298px|Princess Ruto as she appears in her childhood]]

Once outside Lord Jabu-Jabu's belly, standing on a branch-like log, Ruto's demeanor toward Link changes entirely, with the Zora princess having grown quite fond of him, even almost kissing him before the young hero falls into the water startled, and asking Link what reward he would ask for rescuing her and helping her recover the Spiritual Stone.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, hi Link! Despite this, Link is unaware of his engagement to Princess Ruto, only knowing that he had finally obtained the three Spiritual Stones to complete [[Princess Zelda]]'s request.<ref>{{Cite|Her most precious possession? You don't know what she's talking about, but you've finally collected all three Spiritual Stones!! Go back to see Princess Zelda!|N/A|OoT}}</ref>

[[File:Ruto Awakens.png|thumb|left|303x303px|Princess Ruto awakening as the Sage of Water and telling Link their marriage has to be on hold]]
Seven years after Link is placed into suspended animation by the [[Master Sword]], evil returns to Zora's Domain when [[Ganondorf]] uses the power granted to him by the [[Triforce of Power]] and causes the entire area and its inhabitants to be frozen under a thick layer of [[Ice]]. This fate also befalls Ruto initially, but she is rescued from beneath the cursed ice by [[Sheik]]. She then goes to the [[Water Temple]] in the hope of finding a way to save her people from being eternally frozen.<ref>{{Cite|We meet again, Link... Although the Zora princess is quite happy to see her fiancé again, she is aware that this is not a time to talk about love. The hero does not see Ruto again until he defeats the Giant Aquatic Ameoba [[Morpha]], the monster responsible for the sad state of both Zora's Domain and the nearly dry [[Lake Hylia]]. Inside the [[Chamber of Sages]], Link learns that Ruto is the new [[Sage of Water]] that guards the Water Temple. Ruto again appears within the water room inside [[Ganon's Castle]] after Link dispels the [[Water Barrier]] protecting the castle, where she urges the Hero of Time onward to rescue the captive seventh [[Sage]] and their leader, Princess Zelda herself. After Link ultimately defeats Ganondorf, both in his [[Gerudo]] form and as the Demon King [[Ganon]], Ruto combines her power with that of the other five Sages to cast the King of Evil back into the tainted [[Sacred Realm]] and to create a powerful seal that would imprison him there for a very long time. She is last seen alongside several of the other Sages atop [[Death Mountain]], overlooking a jubilant Hyrule now freed of Ganon's evil tyranny by the Hero of Time and fellow princess Zelda. 

Although Ruto finally managed to fulfill her duty as a Sage at this point, Link had been sent back to the past by Zelda and therefore no longer existed in their timeline. Thus, the Zora Princess was sadly unable to marry the Hero of Time.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{OoT|-}} (Himekawa)===
Princess Ruto largely fills the same role in the {{OoT|Himekawa}} as she did in the actual game, though there are some slight differences that can be seen in her character when comparing the two. In the story of the manga, Ruto is swallowed by Jabu-Jabu intentionally, as she hopes to escape an engagement to a suitor presented to her by her father whom she deems unacceptable. After Link saves her, she grows quite fond of him and grants him the Zora's Sapphire in rebellion against the marriage her father was attempting to arrange for her. As in the game, she comes to truly believe she and Link are destined for marriage following these events. In this take on the story, she seems to be far less tolerant of her father, ill concealing that she considers him annoying. She also makes a habit of saying 'Zora' at the end of her sentences. When she becomes the Sage of Water in her adulthood and tells Link their vows must be on hold, the hero expresses minor disappointment in response, but continues his destiny to save Hyrule from Ganondorf.

==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, Princess Ruto appears as a playable Warrior. She is based on her slightly redesigned adult form in {{OoT3D}}. She wields the [[Zora Scale]], which she uses as a medium for water magic. Similarly to [[Lana]], she can be considered a wizardly character, as her moveset often consists of area-of-effect attacks, such as forming whirlpools, a giant sphere of water which she uses to trap her enemies and unleashing wide splashes. She has a unique mechanic where executing her combos causes a blue gauge to fill. At any point, she can use the gauge to fire off long-range waves of water that plow through enemies.

In [[Legend Mode]], Princess Ruto first appears in the Scenario "[[Land of Myth]]", where she has been taken hostage by [[Darunia]] and held in [[Death Mountain (Hyrule Warriors)|Death Mountain]]. The [[Hyrulean Forces]], led by [[Impa]] and [[Sheik]], come to her rescue and defeat Darunia. It is then discovered that Darunia was being manipulated by an outside force. Princess Ruto, along with Darunia, then accompany the Hyrulean Forces as they venture to locate and close the [[Gate of Souls]] in [[Lake Hylia (Hyrule Warriors)|Lake Hylia]] in the Scenario "[[The Water Temple]]". Princess Ruto assists the Hyrulean Forces by opening the floodgates allowing passage to the Water Temple. She then shortly leaves to find the [[Lens of Truth]], in order to reveal the true identity of the false [[Princess Zelda]]. She is however soon captured by Zelda. After being rescued, she hands the Lens of Truth to Sheik before retreating from the battle.

Ruto otherwise does not play a large role in Legend Mode by default. She, however, will appear in place of Zelda, Sheik, or Darunia should any one of them be chosen in a Scenario they appear in. In "[[The Sacred Sword]]", Ruto will be the one to activate the Fire and Water Statue if Darunia is being played as. In "[[Liberation of the Triforce]]", Ruto will be the one to grant Link the [[Light Arrow]]s during the final battle if Zelda is picked.

When defeated in battle, Princess Ruto may occasionally drop her [[Ruto's Earrings|Earrings]] as a silver [[Material]], and rarely her [[Ruto's Scale|Scale]] as a gold Material.

Young Princess Ruto appears as a [[Spirit]] in {{SSBU}}.

== Reception ==
[[File:OoT Child Princess Ruto rude to Link.png|thumb|317x317px|Princess Ruto's rather hot-headed personality as a child has received outrage among fans in spite of her becoming more mature when she grows into an adult in {{OoT|-}}]]
Like [[Navi]], Princess Ruto is one of the most disliked characters of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], specifically when she was a child in ''Ocarina of Time''. This is primarily due to her strong-willed behavior and short temper in the face of danger comparable to that of [[Tatl]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]''<ref name="OoT Ruto Intro" /><ref name=":1" /><ref name=":3" /> and the requirement of carrying her across [[Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly]].<ref name=":2" /> Because of this, alongside the [[Dungeon]] itself being claimed to be tedious not unlike the [[Water Temple]] where Ruto is once again found as an adult, many players call the character a "spoiled brat", a mean "fish girl", or a horrible princess for her kind. Others often claim she is arguably the most annoying female character of the franchise and wanted to "beat the crap out of her". In addition, the idea of [[Link]] - a [[Hylian]], being canonically a possible husband of a [[Zora]]<ref name=":4" /> is not without [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda series|controversy]], especially since this was spoken by Ruto herself at a young age after the [[Hero of Time]] rescues the princess,<ref name="OoT" /> and the hero himself was never aware of being her fiancé in-game (implying he was "forced" to marry her). Some also utterly believe two separate races/species are not compatible even though she is a humanoid and therefore view Link and Princess Ruto together in a romantic relationship as "creepy", unlike with [[Princess Zelda]] or [[Malon]]; This is reflected on social media based on the ''Zelda'' franchise where fans commonly depict the main protagonist being very uncomfortable with the Zora princess, refuse of getting married (or having it botched), or herself stalking the hero. Despite this, however, similarly to the aforementioned [[Fairy]], the character actually does have a small fanbase among the community today due to her unique royal beauty appearance compared to most other Zoras especially as an adult or otherwise accepting the lore, as well as Ruto's role as a Warrior in {{HW}}.

Ironically and perhaps strangely, Link's relationship with [[Saria]], who is a [[Kokiri]] and doesn't age into an adult, is significantly less criticized in comparison.

==Trivia==
*Ruto, like the other [[Sage|Sages]] from {{OoT}}, shares her name with a [[Town of Ruto|town]] from {{TAoL}}.
*She is the first female character to explicitly fall in love with [[Link]] in the core series.
*As seen in official artwork and her original [[Nintendo 64]] model, as well as the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|{{OoT|-}} manga adaptation]] by [[Akira Himekawa]], Ruto does not appear to be wearing any clothing, including as an adult, revealing her breasts. In the [[Nintendo 3DS|3DS]] [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|remake]] and ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'', as this was deemed inappropriate, she was slightly redesigned so her fins cover them up and drape past her waist to avoid controversy.
**She is not the first female character to have such an explicit case, as the [[Hippo Model]] and [[Mermaid]] in the Japanese version of {{LA}} also revealed themselves to be nude, and were therefore changed in the international release.
*Princess Ruto is the first and one of few female [[Zora|Zoras]] to appear in {{TLoZ|Series}}, as the vast majority of the race are male.
*Princess Ruto in ''Ocarina of Time'' shares the same voice actress as [[Princess Zelda]] - [[Jun Mizusawa]]. In ''Hyrule Warriors'', however, she was voiced by Ikumi Nakgami.
*According to Yoshiki Haruhana, a character designer for {{OoT|-}}, Princess Ruto's design, specifically her head, is based on a [[wikipedia:Hammerhead_shark|hammerhead shark]], and in the original N64 version, the holes on the sides of her head as a child are actually an extra set of eyes (implying she can see almost all directions), before they move inside and leave blowholes as an adult. Official artwork and ''Ocarina of Time 3D'', however, show those eyes the same regardless of age. Due to their shiny appearance, however, some fans otherwise believed them to be gems, instead. As Ruto's [[Terminan]] counterpart [[Lulu]] shares a very similar appearance to herself, this is also likely the case.
*After Link speaks to Ruto after meeting her at the [[Water Temple]] and the player immediately looks up toward her from directly below as she swims above to lead the hero to the dungeon's upper levels, the Zora princess can be seen indeed vanishing.
*If Link were to say "''Nothing, really''" to Princess Ruto during when she asks him what does he wishes for his reward after rescuing her, she will suggestively say to him not to be shy and she can tell what he is thinking, and will still grant the [[Spiritual Stone of Water|Zora's Sapphire]] to the young hero as if he desired for it.
*Due to Link's seemingly romantic relationships with two different princesses in {{OoT|-}} - Ruto and Zelda <ref name=":5" />, as well as [[Saria]] and [[Malon]], it is widely believed by fans that the [[Hero of Time]] is in a [[wikipedia:Love_triangle|love triangle]], despite he is technically engaged with Princess Ruto upon obtaining the Spiritual Stone of Water.[[File:Ruto gets the Zora's Sapphire.png|thumb|275x275px|Young Ruto doing the famous "got item" act Link does in the franchise, as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
*Princess Ruto is the first character other than Link in the series (excluding the [[Player|BS-X avatars]] for the Japan-only [[Satellaview]] games) to perform the iconic "got item" jingle while holding the item in the air, as seen when she obtains her Zora's Sapphire as a child in Jabu-Jabu's Belly.
**She is also the first besides the hero himself to [http://noproblo.dayjo.org/ZeldaSounds/OOT/OOT_Ruto_Scream.wav scream] when falling from a great height, though unlike Link, Ruto doesn't take damage.
*Just before the [[boss]] battle against [[Morpha]] in the Water Temple, a voice is heard warning Link about the water not being normal. Although commonly assumed to be [[Navi]], it is implied the voice actually belonged to Princess Ruto, as the speech text is white rather than blue (with it being more explicit in the Japanese version where the voice used speech quirks exclusive to Ruto).{{fact}}
*If Link were to wear the [[Zora Mask]] and talk to Princess Ruto, she will react with excitement, saying to the hero that he is ready to become a Zora and requests would he agree to marry her now (the options box won't appear, however).
**Ironically, the hero can [[Transformation|transform]] into a [[Zora Link|Zora]] by the similarly named Zora's Mask in {{MM}}. Even more so is that the mask's basis, [[Mikau]], was the possible husband of Ruto's Terminan counterpart Lulu.
*Ruto as a child shares the same [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Ruto_Laugh.wav giggling sound effect] as fellow Sage Saria.
*It is possible that Princess Ruto was inside [[Barinade]] during the boss battle in Jabu-Jabu's Belly, as when it was defeated, she can be seen rising out of the ground, and explaining her shaken behavior toward Link.
*[[Mipha]], Princess Ruto's descendant in {{OoT}} and one of the [[Champions]], is a reference to the character in that she is herself another Zora princess who falls in love with Link, that case the Hero of the Wild incarnation.
*In the {{OoT|-}} manga, Princess Ruto as an adult completely assists Link in the Water Temple and even fights Morpha alongside him. During the battle, she can produce a force field made of water.[[File:MM Princess Ruto in Clock Town (rescaled).webp|thumb|246x246px|Adult Princess Ruto in {{MM|-}} through debug cheats]]
*Interestingly, although Princess Ruto does not play any role whatsoever in {{MM|-}}, she as an adult can be spawned through ROM hacking, as her model remains in the game's code. This is because it itself reus<nowiki/>''<nowiki/>''es ''Ocarina'' ''of Time'''s engine and Ruto was most likely set by the developers as the basis for her counterpart Lulu's design. It is unknown if this applies to {{OoT3D|-}}.
**In ''Hyrule Warriors'', Ruto herself does have an unlockable costume based on Lulu's.
*Princess Ruto, alongside Princess Zelda and Malon, are the only three characters from Link's childhood to grow up into an ad<nowiki/>ult once he becomes the Hero of Time.
*Ruto's near-kiss towards Link upon rescuing her from inside [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]] is possibly a parallel to [[Princess Peach]] of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] when saved by [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]], as she herself is indeed a princess.
**It could also be a reference to {{TAoL}}<nowiki/>'s ending, where Princess Zelda is seen giving Link direct affection.
*In the original N64 version of {{OoT|-}}, as an adult, Princess Ruto's fins periodically move. However, they remain still in {{OoT3D|-}}.
*Princess Ruto's personality of being a tomboy is a coincidence to Princess Zelda's (and possibly [[mariowiki:Princess_Daisy|Princess Daisy]] from the ''Mario'' series), though in her case it is shown more strongly.
*Notably, as the Spiritual Stone of Water is the "Zora's Engagement Ring", when Princess Ruto gives it to Link, she tells the young hero off-screen to not tell her [[King Zora|father]]. This implies that she was not supposed to vow her marriage to an individual that is not of her race, since the main protagonist himself is a Hylian. Of course, the Zora leader does not know about this for the rest of the game.
**In the manga, she does tell King Zora that she had given the Zora's Sapphire to someone but never said it was Link, in which he reacted in shock.
*Ruto's "[http://noproblo.dayjo.org/ZeldaSounds/OOT/OOT_Ruto_Throw.wav ''Eep!'']" sound when she is thrown by Link in Jabu-Jabu's Belly as a child is shared with Navi of when the [[Fairy]] hits the wooden wall in [[Kokiri Forest]] heading toward [[Link's House|Link's house]] in the cutscene of being sent by the [[The Great Deku Tree|Great Deku Tree]].
*In {{OoT3D|-}}, in the cutscene where young Princess Ruto attempted to kiss Link after saving her, she was moved slightly further back from him compared to the original N64 version to be less apparent.
*Despite this never occurring in any official media based on {{OoT|-}}, if Link the Hero of Time were to actually marry Ruto, due to her title as the princess of the Zoras, it's likely he himself would become their prince.
