'''Caromadillo Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Caromadillo LV. 2|A powerful monster that hurls itself with incredible momentum, flying over holes with ease.<ref name="Caromodillo LV. 2">{{Cite|A powerful monster that hurls itself with incredible momentum, flying over holes with ease.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Caromadillo Lv. 2 is a tougher variant of the standard Caromadillo, with higher health and damage. Zelda can learn their [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], and summoning one costs three Echo energy.
