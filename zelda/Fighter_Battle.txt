'''Fighter Battles''' are special Battles in {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fighter Battle<br>The captive fighters are being copied to create puppet fighters. The only way to free them from Galeem's control is to defeat them in battle!|N/A|SSBU}}</ref>

==Overview==
Fighter Battles are accessible via [[Adventure Mode]]. Fighter Battles function similarly to [[Spirit Battle]]s, wherein a [[Fighter]] is held captive by {{Ssb|Galeem}} in order to clone them to create [[Puppet Fighter]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Galeem copied the fallen fighters, creating empty puppets in their likenesses...|N/A|SSBU}}</ref> They must be defeated in order to be freed from Galeem's control, after which they are added to the roster of available Fighters.
