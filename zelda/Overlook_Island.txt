{{Term/Store|Overlook Island||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (A7) is a location in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Fishman|Hey, small fry, do you know about the golden Triumph Forks? They're there, I tell you... On that island... The chart's there, is what I mean to say. But the cliff there rises sharply, and even if  you wanted to climb it, there aren't any ledges or footings to grab onto. So?  What are you going to do, small fry?}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= <p>Overlook Island</p><p>It smells of old, stinky chart around there. Mr. Fairy! Find it! Find it! If you find it, bring it to me. Thanks, sir!</p>
}}

The island consists of tall, interconnected, brick structures with flat, grassy roofs. Palm trees grow on the roofs, which can be used as targets for [[Hookshot]]. Several [[Warship]]s patrol the waters surrounding the island. On the tallest structure is a secret {{Term|TWWHD|Cave|link}} housing a [[Triforce Chart]].

By opening the [[Treasure Chart]] found in [[Two-Eye Reef]], the [[Secret Cave Chart]] can be unveiled from the deep waters surrounding the isle.
