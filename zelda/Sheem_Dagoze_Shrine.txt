{{Term/Store|Sheem Dagoze Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Sheem Dagoze Shrine}}<br/>Moving in Parallel}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Moving in Parallel''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|West Hyrule Plains|link}}. Initially, {{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze Shrine}} is buried underground, though {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} can be found nearby. He will ask {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if he wants to hear an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" passed down in the region,<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which describes threading two rings to reveal an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When a single arrow threads two rings,<br/>the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} will rise like a bird on wings.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Listening to Kass activates the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Two Rings|link}}".

To unlock the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must shoot an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} through two of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Ring|link}}. Once this is done, {{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze Shrine}} will rise from the ground, and the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters, he is greeted by {{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} who designed the eponymous {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Sheem Dagoze. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Sheem Dagoze|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze Shrine}} features a single puzzle immediately after the entrance, in which two {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} rest on platforms above a series of ramps. Beneath the ramps are a pair of {{Plural|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|link}} that the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} must reach to activate.

Two {{Plural|BotW|Floor Switch}} on the platform each operate two tilting platforms. The left {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|display=Switch}} operates the flat top-left and bottom-left platforms, tilting them to the right, whereas the right {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|display=Switch}} operates the flat top-right platform and the bottom-right platform tilted to the right, tilting them to the left. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} messes up and sets both {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} in the same {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|display=Socket}}, the {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|display=Socket}} will tip forward and dump the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orbs}} out, resetting the puzzle.

Activating both {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|display=Sockets}} opens the nearby portcullis to {{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze}}. Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will present {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Sheem Dagoze|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Sheem Dagoze|BotW}}</ref>

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Great Thunderblade|link}} can be found behind the ramp on the bottom left. A nearby {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|display=Switch}} activates a moving platform that moves between the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} and the initial platform.

==Trivia== 
* In some cases, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} that has been correctly placed in a {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket}} will stay slotted, even after the other {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} has been moved to the same side, causing the {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|display=Socket}} to restart and tip the platform.
