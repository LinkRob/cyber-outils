{{Term/Store|Fifi||Series, TMC}} (known as '''Borken''' in the PAL English version) is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 270"/> He is {{Term|TMC|Stockwell|link}}'s pet {{Term|TMC|Dog|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Fifi}} lives in {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's House|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Stockwell}} claims he is too busy to feed him,<ref>{{Cite|He complains that he's been too busy lately to see his dog, Fifi.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> so when {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} tries to take the {{Term|TMC|Dog Food|link|display=Bottle of Dog Food}} behind the counter of {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Stockwell}} agrees to let him keep the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}} if {{Term|TMC|Link}} feeds {{Term|TMC|Fifi}} in his stead.<ref>{{Cite|If you feed Fifi, then I guess you can have the bottle.|Stockwell|TMC}}</ref> Emptying the {{Term|TMC|Dog Food}} into {{Term|TMC|Fifi}}'s {{Term|TMC|Doggie Dish|link}} will give {{Term|TMC|Link}} the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle}}; it cannot be used or emptied anywhere else, as the {{Term|TMC|Dog Food|display=Bottle of Dog Food}} appears as a quest {{Term|TMC|Item|link}} rather than a usable {{Term|TMC|Item}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bottle of Dog Food|Quest Status|TMC}}</ref>

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} talks to {{Term|TMC|Fifi}} while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, he complains about how his master never has time to play with him.<ref>{{Cite|My master, Stockwell, has been coming home far too late to play with me... I'm so lonely.|Fifi|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with him, causing a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} to appear near {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's Lakeside Cabin|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia}} with a blue {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} inside.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|Series|Fifi}} resembles dogs of the {{Wp|Maltese (dog)|Maltese}} or {{Wp|Yorkshire Terrier}} breed.
*Although a few other {{Plural|TMC|Dog|link}} appear in {{TMC}}, {{Term|TMC|Fifi}} is the only {{Term|TMC|Dog}} of his kind, the others being of a larger breed.
