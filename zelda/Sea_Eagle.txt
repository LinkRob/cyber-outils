'''Sea Eagles''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Sea Eagles are powerful bird enemies encountered only in the [[Seacoast Plain]]. They are relatives of [[Romraven]]s. If [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] has no [[Celestial Sign]]s, Sea Eagles are invincible, but even as she gains more, Sea Eagles still require a number of hits to defeat. If Zelda has four or fewer Celestial Signs, a Sea Eagle inflicts a [[Heart]] and a half of damage on her, but if she has five Celestial Sign, Sea Eagles inflict only a Heart of damage on her.

There sprites are the same as Romravens but inverted
