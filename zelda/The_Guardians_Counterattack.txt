{{Term/Store|The Guardians Counterattack||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 54 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Guardians Counterattack|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|The Guardians Counterattack}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} are given 20 minutes to defeat 12 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. The 12 consist of one {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}}, one {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Guardian|link}}, one {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}}, and six {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}. One {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} will spawn northeast, one in a building near the center of the map, and one just below the center of the map, next to an elemental {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian}} spawns in the center of the map, the {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Guardian}} in the northeast, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian}} just below the center, although underneath the bridge on which the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian}} spawns. The last nine {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are split into three groups, each containing a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}} and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}}. One group is located near the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian}}, one in the northwestern corner, and the last one just south of the second group. As time passes by, new {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} will spawn near the area of the first three. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any three {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Ancient Core|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Giant Ancient Core|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
