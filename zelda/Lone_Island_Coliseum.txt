The Lone Island Coliseum is a location in {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lone Island Coliseum'''|Map|TotK}}</ref> Upon entering, the gate will close behind [[Link]] and another will open, revealing [[Bokoblin]]s. Link must defeat all waves of bokoblins for the gate to open, and to be able to open the [[Treasure Chest]] in the middle of the Coliseum, rewarding Link with [[Midna's Helmet]].
