{{Term/Store|Tabantha Bridge Stable||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tabantha Bridge Stable|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable}} is located in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}, just east of the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Great Bridge|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Shae Loya Shrine|link}} is just south of it, providing a convenient {{Term|BotW|Travel Gate|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} when activated. The {{Term|BotW|Stable}} contains a number of cargo containers just outside of it, with a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword|link}} resting against a crate with a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Bow|link}} sitting atop it, along with two bundles of {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}}. Another crate nearby has three {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper|link}} on top of it. In front of a covered wagon rests a {{Term|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} can be found to the right of the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} entrance near another cluster of crates, one of which has three {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry|link}} for the taking. Two {{Plural|BotW|Torch|link}} can be picked up, one in a stacked barrel and another on a crate by the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}. A livestock pen containing three {{Plural|BotW|White Goat|link}} is found to the left of the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} yurt surrounding a mushroom tree. Two more {{Term|BotW|wood}} bundles are behind the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} yurt on a cart. 

The {{Term|BotW|Stable}} is run by {{Term|BotW|Dabi|link}}, who mans both its {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} services and lodging. As with all other {{Plural|BotW|Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} can occasionally be found here selling his wares. {{Term|BotW|Toren|link}} can be found near the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}, who initiates the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|A Gift for the Great Fairy|link}}". The children {{Term|BotW|Ena|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kenyo|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Sho|link}} all live here. {{Term|BotW|Banji|link}}, frequently inspecting the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}'s cargo, can offer {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} directions into surrounding areas. During the Quest "{{Term|BotW|Captured Memories|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Pikango|link}} can be found at {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable}}, and will assist {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has completed the Quest "{{Term|BotW|Find the Fairy Fountain|link}}".

Inside the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} there is a recipe for {{Term|BotW|Hearty Elixir|link}} on the wall.
