{{Term/Store|Freezard Water|Freezard Waters|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 124"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Freezard Water}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Ice Cavern|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Frozen Plateau|link}}''', ''"Guard the orb!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Freezard Water}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Snowball Ravine|link}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Freezard Water}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Silver Shrine|link}}''', ''"Win without using a sword!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Freezard Water}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Silver Shrine}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Freezard Water}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Ice Temple|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Freezard Water}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Ice Temple}}''', ''"Don't get hit by snowballs!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Freezard Water}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Freezard Water}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make {{Term|TFH|Hammerwear|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Tingle Tights|link}}.<ref name="E 124"/>
