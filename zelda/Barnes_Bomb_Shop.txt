{{Term/Store|Barnes Bomb Shop||Series, TPHD}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Red|Barnes Bomb Shop}}<br/>- Buy or Sell -<br/><br/>Occasional Holidays|Sign|TPHD}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{TP|-}}===
Barnes Bomb Shop is located in [[Kakariko Village]], and is owned by {{Term|TPHD|Barnes|link}}. At first, Barnes cannot sell Link any [[Bomb]]s because his suppliers, the Gorons, have broken off relations with the Village.<ref>{{Cite|Hey now! It's you. Yeah, so, I've been thinkin' 'bout gettin' things sorted out so I can go ahead and reopen my shop... ...Thing is, I can't get the materials I need from the Gorons anymore... Man, I ain't got a CLUE what to do!|Barnes|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Cripes, those danged Gorons... They're sure takin' their sweet time gettin' me my materials!|Barnes|TP}}</ref> Link must earn the trust of the Gorons before they will begin supplying Barnes with the materials he needs to make them.<ref>{{Cite|Hey now, bud! How 'bout this, now? Yep, I finally managed to get my numbers together and open up shop again. Well, sort of... I guess I've only got one type of bomb, but hey, that's one more than none, right? If only my storehouse had survived...|Barnes|TP}}</ref>

Initially, Barnes only sells regular Bombs, and informs Link that they can be used to create [[Bomb Arrow]]s.<ref>{{Cite|That's right! Now you'll get to taste the true height of perfection with my finely-crafted bombs! Oh... And you can combine bombs and arrows to make bomb arrows for when you need ranged explosives! I'll send you a letter with the details later so you can read about it at your leisure.|Barnes|TP}}</ref> Later on, he will then sell [[Water Bomb]]s,<ref>{{Cite|I've got a deal for you: a bomb bag stuffed with water bombs! Yeah, this premium kit is yours for only 220 Rupees! How 'bout that, now?|Barnes|TP}}</ref> and eventually, [[Bombling]]s. Barnes will also buy back Bombs from any of the Bomb Bags Link has for a deflated price.<ref>{{Cite|Head to the counter next to this one to use our buyback program. You got any bombs you ain't gonna use, we'll take 'em off your hands.|Barnes|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A very good day to you, sir! Welcome to the incredibly fancy bomb buyback counter!|Barnes|TP}}</ref> However, if Link wishes to sell his Bombs, he must sell all of the Bombs in the selected Bomb Bag. This is sometimes necessary, as Link can only store one type of Bomb in each Bomb Bag. Barnes also offers Link his first Bomb Bag for 120 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|What's this now? You ain't got a bomb bag for carryin' bombs, huh? I've got a special offer goin' right now: my bomb bag premium kit, which comes with bag and bombs, just 120 Rupees! How 'bout that?|Barnes|TP}}</ref>

Around the store are many warning signs that say not to use any flammable objects in the store.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|TPHD Red|FLAMMABLE}}}}<br/>{{Small|Lanterns strictly prohibited!}}|Sign|TPHD}}</ref> If Link uses his [[Lantern]], Barnes will slam his fist on the counter in front of him, causing a stream of water to fall directly on top of Link. If Link pulls out his Lantern in front of Barnes, the proprietor will reprimand him.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Hey now! You insane, bud?! That's dangerous! Don't go lightin' fires in the middle of my shop!|Barnes|TPHD}}</ref>
