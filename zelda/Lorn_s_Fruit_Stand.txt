==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Lorn's Fruit Stand|link}} is a {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} located in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. It is operated by {{Term|BotW|Lorn|link}}. At night, {{Term|BotW|Lorn}}'s daughter {{Term|BotW|Dalia|link}} sleeps here. {{Term|BotW|Emri|link}}, a fellow fruit merchant, competes with {{Term|BotW|Lorn}}, whether {{Term|BotW|Lorn}} knows it or not.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Oh, vasaaq... I've got plenty of {{Color|BotW Blue|hydromelons}}... I bet more than you'll find in {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}. Why not buy a bunch while you're here?|Emri|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} frees {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Lorn}} remarks on how {{Term|BotW|Patricia|link}} was involved in the act and adds that she feeds her fruit to {{Term|BotW|Patricia}} whenever possible.<ref name="Brand"/> Realizing this opportunity for promotion, {{Term|BotW|Lorn}} muses about rebranding her {{Term|BotW|General Store}} to associate it with the heroic {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}}.<ref name="Brand"/>
