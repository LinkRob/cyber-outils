{{Term/Store|EX Road to Akkala Citadel||HWAoC}} is a level 50 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Road to Akkala Citadel|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the first wave of the game’s {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Road to Akkala Citadel}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Wildberry|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Voltfruit|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

For the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to escort a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}} and their allies to a Targeted Position located in the northeast corner of the map. At first, there is only a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} being escorted, and only three {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} and a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}} stand in the way. When they are defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} will then advance further throughout the map, being blocked by only a small amount of {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}}. At around the center of the map, if the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} moves south, he or she will also find a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} by itself. If the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} moves north from there, he or she will encounter a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese|link}}. From the center of the map, the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} moves east, blocked by a handful of {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} will eventually reach a {{Term|HWAoC|Goron Captain|link}}, who has been fighting {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} since the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} arrives, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}} also spawn, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} in the south will start moving toward the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. After they are defeated and the captains move east, they will find a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Chuchu|link}}, until eventually they reach a {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain|link}} being attacked by a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}} and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} is reached, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} spawn in the northern part of the map and advance towards the captains. When the two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe}} are defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blizzrobe|link}} spawns that must be defeated for the captains to move forward. From there until the Targeted Position, only a small group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Keese|link}} stand in the captains' way. When all three of the captains reach the Targeted Position, a large {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Chuchu}} spawns. When that is defeated, a large {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu|link}} spawns. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu}} is defeated, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} spawn, surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin}}. When one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} is defeated, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Lynel|link}} spawns. Once all five of those {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} are defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.
