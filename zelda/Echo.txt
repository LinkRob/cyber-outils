:''Not to be confused with [[Dark Echo|Dark Echoes]].''
'''Echoes''' are enemies and objects in {{EoW}} created by [[Princess Zelda]] with the [[Tri-Rod]], a gift from [[Tri]].<ref name="Tri Rod">{{Cite|Tri loaned you this mystical rod. With its power, you can echo things you've learned.|Key Item|EoW}}</ref><ref name="Tri Speak">{{Cite|Did you learn to echo that monster? Defeating new monsters leaves a shimmer, which allows you to learn about them like you just did.|Tri|EoW}}</ref> Certain enemies, like [[Tecuum|Tecuums]], can also create Echoes. Victims of [[Rift|rifts]] may be replaced by [[Dark Echo|imposters]] who mimic their lives and manipulate others.
