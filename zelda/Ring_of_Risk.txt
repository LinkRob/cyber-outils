{{Term/Store|Ring of Risk|Rings of Risk|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Ring of Risk}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Ring of Risk}} are equipment that doubles the user's attack damage when they are at low health.<ref>{{Cite|Doubles damage dealt when at 1 or fewer hearts remaining.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or slain minibosses, though a free {{Term|CoH|Ring of Risk}} can also be found at the top of a small fountain that's randomly located within the {{Term|CoH|Forest|link}} biome.
