{{Term/Store|Dordung Cave||Series, TWoG}} is a location in {{TWoG}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Dordung Cave}}|Map|TWoG}}</ref> It is unlocked after completing [[Tykogi Tower]].

==Features and Overview==
Dordung Cave is a ravine and cave complex in [[Gamelon]], located in a small mountain peak next to the forest valley that borders the area. The ravine is inhabited by [[Jawbee]]s that attack [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] continuously, along with a few [[Dodongo]]s that patrol the ravine grounds. At the far end of the ravine is the entrance to the Cave itself.

The Cave inside is pitch dark, which lighting with the [[Lantern]] reveals large concentrations of green crystals forming on the walls. Moving [[Gleeok|Gleeok Head]]s inside attempt to pelt Zelda with fireballs. At the top of the cavern is a [[Gleeok]]. Upon its defeat, Zelda receives the [[Heat Crystal]] that is left behind. A [[Triforce Map]] at the end of the Cave takes Zelda back to the map screen, and upon completion of the stage, [[Dodomai Palace]] is unlocked.
