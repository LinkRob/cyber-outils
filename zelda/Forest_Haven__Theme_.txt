"Forest Haven" is a [[Song]] in {{TWW}}.

==Overview==
"Forest Haven" is associated with the epynomous location, {{Term|TWW|Forest Haven|link}}. It plays on the exterior of {{Term|TWW|Forest Haven}}.

It is the 56th track on Disc 1 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker Original Sound Tracks]]''.

==Trivia==
*"Forest Haven" is a slow-paced remix of "[[Kokiri Forest (Theme)|Kokiri Forest]]", the music that plays in the [[Kokiri Forest|location of the same name]], from {{OoT}}.
