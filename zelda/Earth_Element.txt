The {{Term/Store|Earth Element||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 122 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Earth Element}} is the first of the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}} obtained by {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, found within {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}. After defeating the {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|display=Element}} and continues his journey to find the others. Once he has obtained the {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link}}, he travels to the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} to infuse the two {{Plural|TMC|Four Elements|display=Elements}} into the reforged {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}}, turning it into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword (Two Elements)|link}} and granting him the ability to split himself in two.
