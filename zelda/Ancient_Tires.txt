==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|MK8D|Ancient Tires}} are the [[mariowiki:Tire|tires]] that accompany the {{Term|MK8D|Master Cycle Zero|link}} and the {{Term|MK8D|Paraglider|link}} in {{MK8|D}}. They resemble the tires used by the Master Cycle Zero in {{BotW}}.

The {{Term|MK8D|Ancient Tires}} are obtained immediately after downloading the version 1.6.0 update.
