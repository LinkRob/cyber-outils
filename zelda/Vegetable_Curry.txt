{{Term/Store|Vegetable Curry|Vegetable Curries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Vegetable Curry}}<br/>This healthy curry is popular for its mild flavor and moderate spiciness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Vegetable Curries are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice|link}}, and either a {{Term|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made by using multiple vegetables with differing effects. Eating them will heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These curries are healthy, and their mild flavor and spiciness make them popular.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on the type of vegetable used, Link can make the following variations of Vegetable Curry:

* '''Enduring Vegetable Curry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Vegetable Curry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This healthy curry is popular for its mild flavor and moderate spiciness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using Endura Carrots. When eaten, these curries will completely restore and overfill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Vegetable Curry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Vegetable Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This healthy curry is popular for its mild flavor and moderate spiciness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using Swift Carrots. By eating them, Link will receive a boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Tough Vegetable Curry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Vegetable Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This healthy curry is popular for its mild flavor and moderate spiciness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using Fortified Pumpkins. They can be eaten to grant Link a boost to his defense.<ref name="Tough"/>
