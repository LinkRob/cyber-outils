The {{Term/Store|Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|BotW}}
The {{Term|BotW|Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)}} is a branch of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)}} is located in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}. It is established when [[Fyson]], a [[Rito]] whose mother [[Misa]] runs the flagship store, is recruited to help develop Tarrey Town. It sells both {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}. The {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} here are usually cheaper than other {{Plural|BotW|Shop|link}}, as it sells {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} for about 87% of the price compared to other {{Plural|BotW|Shop}}.

In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', the store, this time with no specified name, is relocated to to the northeast part of Tarrey Town, next to [[Pelison]]'s Break-a-Part store. Aside from arrows, the store's stock is completely different.
