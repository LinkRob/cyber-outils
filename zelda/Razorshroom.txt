==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Razorshroom}}
{{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom}} are found below trees in the [[Great Hyrule Forest]] and on the [[Tabantha Frontier]]. They're also available to purchase at the [[Spore Store]] or from [[Ardin]] in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. They can be eaten raw to restore a half of a {{Term|BotW|Heart}}, or they can be roasted by direct flames to become {{Plural|BotW|Toasty Razorshroom|link}}. If one or more {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom}} are {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}} into a dish in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, the resulting meal will increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s attack power temporarily.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom}} are one of the ingredients that can be used to make {{Term|BotW|Crimson Dye|link}}.

Many mushrooms, Razorshrooms included, can be used in a Cooking Pot to make [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Fragrant Mushroom Sauté]], [[Fruit and Mushroom Mix]], [[Glazed Mushrooms]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], a [[Mushroom Omelet]], [[Mushroom Rice Balls]], [[Mushroom Risotto]], a [[Mushroom Skewer]], [[Copious Mushrooms Skewers]], [[Salt-Grilled Mushrooms]], and [[Steamed Mushrooms]].
