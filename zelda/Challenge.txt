==Overview==
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
Besides the [[Scenario]]s that make up the main story, there are Challenges in {{HWAoC}}. These Challenges yield [[Material]]s after clearing them for the first time. {{PotA}} and {{GoR}} add [[EX Vicious Monster Battle]], a type of respawning Challenge where the chosen [[Warrior]]s face a certain [[Vicious Monster]]. In [[Divine Beast Battle]]s, one of the four [[Divine Beast]]s is piloted, instead of controlling a standard Warrior.
