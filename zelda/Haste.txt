{{Term/Store|Haste||Series, CoH}} is an ability in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Haste}}<br/>Allows {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}} to move without following the beat for a short time.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|CoH|Haste}} is a {{Term|CoH|Spell|link}} that {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} can find in {{Term|CoH|Story Mode — Octavo|link}}. When cast, {{Term|CoH|Haste}} allows {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} to move without regard to the {{Plural|CoH|Beat Bar|link}} for a limited time at the cost of his {{Term|CoH|Stamina|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|CoH Yellow|haste}} spell! It's automatically assigned to {{Icon|CoH R}}, and only {{Icon|CoH Octavo}} can use it. Press {{Icon|CoH R}} to give {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}} the ability to move and act without being restricted by the beat. It will wear off after a few beats.|N/A|CoH}}</ref> As he is the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}} counterpart of {{Term|CoH|Octavo}}, {{Term|CoH|The NecroDancer|link}} is also capable of casting {{Term|CoH|Haste}}.

When {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} finds the {{Term|CoH|Haste}} {{Term|CoH|Spell}}, a {{Term|CoH|Fate|link}} appears to chastise his foolish behavior.<ref>{{Cite|Fool! You think it's that easy to change your fate? If only you could see the tangled web that is your future...|Fate|CoH}}</ref>
