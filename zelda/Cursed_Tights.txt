The {{Term/Store|Cursed Tights||Series, TFH}}, initially called the '''Kinda Cursed Tights''' when received, are an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|A curse or a blessing? Either way, they certainly are cutting edge, aren't they, dahling?}}

The {{Term|TFH|Cursed Tights}} are the ugly and irremovable {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that was foist upon {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}} by {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link}} prior to the events of the game.<ref>{{Cite|When the smoke cleared, Styla was met with a terrible sight, followed by the witch's voice in her pretty head: "A perfect outfit for a perfect princess." This cursed outfit of ultimate ugliness could not be removed no matter how the princess tried.|N/A|TFH}}</ref> Once {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} is able to remove the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} by lifting its curse, {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}} and {{Term|TFH|King Tuft|link}} present it to him as his promised reward.<ref>{{Cite|[Link]! Please take this as well. As promised, one fully swell reward!<br/>How's that? You must be overjoyed to receive such a one-of-a-kind collector's item! Gahahaha!|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref>
Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Cursed Tights}} causes {{Term|TFH|Link}} to take double damage from [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]] and most Traps, but allows him to automatically dodge 50% of hits.<ref>{{Cite|Slightly used tights!<br/>♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦<br/>Luckier than Lucky Loungewear... But you'll receive double damage. Curses!|Catalog|TFH}}</ref> The resulting average of these two effects is that {{Term|TFH|Link}} takes the same amount of damage as usual, though subject to chance.

Any {{Plural|TFH|Links}} who join the game through [[Download Play]] will be unable to obtain the {{Term|TFH|Cursed Tights}}, since they cannot leave the [[Multiplayer]] lobby to speak to {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}}.
