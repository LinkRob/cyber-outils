{{Term/Store|Stable|Stables|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring locations in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Stables are <ref>Mr. Aonuma's Tears of the Kingdom Gameplay Demonstration - YouTube, published March 28, 2023.</ref> yurt-like structures found throughout [[Hyrule]]. They are characterized by their giant, [[Horse]]-shaped rooftop decorations.
Stables are places where travelers, such as [[Link]], can register and board [[Wild Horse]]s that they have tamed. In addition, these travelers can seek lodging inside the Stable's main room.<ref>{{Cite|Do you...know how to catch a wild horse? [I know how.] Oh, pardon me. Let me start over... Welcome to our {{Color|BotW Blue|stable}}. If you've come with a wild horse, you can register it with us here. Or if you want to stay for a {{Color|BotW Red|rest}}, please head to the counter inside.|Embry|BotW}}</ref> The owner of a Stable can instruct Link on how to capture Wild Horses, should he need to know.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to my {{Color|BotW Blue|stable}}. We deal in all things horse related. We also double as an inn. Huh... I haven't seen your face before. Do you know about the {{Color|BotW Blue|stable}} system? [I don't.] Then allow me to explain.|Embry|BotW}}</ref> All Stables are connected through a network across [[Hyrule]], allowing Link to call a registered Horse to any of the Stables he visits.<ref>{{Cite|You can board your horse companions with us and then summon them as you please from our network of stables.|Embry|BotW}}</ref>

To register a Horse, Link must first catch and domesticate a Wild Horse and bring it to a Stable before speaking to the Stable owner.<ref>{{Cite|You may have come across {{Color|BotW Red|wild horses}} during your travels... If you catch one and ride it here to register it, it will become your horse companion. One person can board up to five horses, and you're able to swap horses in and out as you please.|Embry|BotW}}</ref> It is not necessary to ride a Horse while speaking to the Stable owner to register it, as Link can simply bring the Horse near the Stable and speak to the owner. During the registration process, Link is able to view the Horse's qualities, which are based on Strength, Speed, Stamina, Temperament, and Bond to Link, as well as the Horse's headshot.<ref>{{Cite|So what'll it be? [Register horse.] Gotcha. You sure this is the one? [Yes.] Not a problem.|Embry|BotW}}</ref> He is also able to name his Horse at this point, provided the name fits within a nine character text limit. Stables only allow patrons to carry a maximum of five Horses, though Horse's can be swapped at any time. Stables charge a fee of 20 [[Rupee|Rupees]] each time a Horse is registered to fund their conservation efforts as well as cover the cost of a [[Saddle]] and [[Bridle]], which, once paid for, will be automatically put on Link's Horse.<ref>{{Cite|So what'll it be? [Register horse.] Gotcha. You sure this is the one? [Yes.] Not a problem. We charge a registration fee to help support our conservation efforts. The fee also gets you a locally made {{Color|BotW Blue|saddle}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|bridle}} for your horse. The fee is {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}. Is that OK? [Yes.] Thanks. Please enter a name for your horse.|Embry|BotW}}</ref> After registering a Horse, Link is given the option to board it or to take it with him.<ref>{{Cite|Are you sure you want to name this horse {{Color|BotW Red|[NAME]}}? [Yes.] Your registration is complete. Would you like to take your new horse with you? [Board horse.] Thank you. We'll see you next time.|Embry|BotW}}</ref>

Stables also serve as hubs for the various people of Hyrule, such as the merchant [[Beedle]], or various characters who can provide [[Side Quest]]s.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= It's pretty clear that the stables in this world, this wild world, are places where human beings congregate. People—of all races, really—come here, and as you see, I'm just talking to this guy. There's a lot you can learn about the world and a lot that you might need to do in the world when you interact with all the people in the stables.|name= Nate Bihldorff|url= https://youtube.com/watch?v=qadzCB3CLlI?t=4h31m48s|title= Nintendo Treehouse Live with Nintendo Switch}}</ref>
