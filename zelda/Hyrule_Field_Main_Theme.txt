'''"Hyrule Field Main Theme"''' is a [[Song]] in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
"{{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Field Main Theme}}" plays in {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Field|link}} during the day. It is the 23rd track in Disc 1 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Original Soundtrack]]''. When an enemy approaches, "{{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Field Main Theme}}" continues playing, rather than changing to {{Term|TPHD|Battle|link}}.

== Remixes ==
This is the main theme of the game, and parts of it reappear in many different places.
