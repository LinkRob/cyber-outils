==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Radiant Mask}} is a piece of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} which can be purchased at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} after "{{Term|BotW|The Secret Club's Secret|link}} " {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} has been completed. Fashioned using {{Term|BotW|Dye}} made from {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Mask|display= Radiant Mask's}} skeletal markings glow when worn at night.<ref name="Inventory"/> The {{Term|BotW|Radiant Mask}} can be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} and can be {{Term|BotW|Dye|link|display= Dyed}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Radiant Mask}}, alongside the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Shirt|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Radiant Tights|link}}, compose the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set|link}}.

When each piece of the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set}} has been enhanced at least two times by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy}} and the full {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set|display= Set}} is worn, it provides a "Disguise" and "Bone Atk. Up" {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link}}. Similar to disguise masks sold by {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set}} allows {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to blend in with {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Stalmoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Stalizalfos|link}}. In addition, the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set}} causes attacks using {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link|display= Bone Weapons}} (such as the {{Term|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Club|link}}) to deal an increased amount of damage.
