{{Term/Store|Shield-Surfing||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Piggyback">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 335}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can access the {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} by talking to {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Selmie's Spot|link}}. When spoken to, she will offer to assist {{Term|BotW|Link}} in refining his {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}} skills.<ref>{{Cite|But if you don't mind a few pointers from a has-been, I'd be happy to critique your technique.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts and pays her 20 {{Term|BotW|Rupee|link}} fee, she will take him outside of her cabin to begin his training.<ref>{{Cite|Smart choice. I always say, if you're not willing to put your money where your mouth is, how serious are you really? Then let's get straight to the slopes. Follow me!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

At the top of the slope, {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants her to explain {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}}.<ref>{{Cite|Do you need a rundown on how this works?|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> She informs him that his goal is to reach the bottom of the hill as quickly as possible and that he can ride his {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}} by hopping onto it in the middle of a jump.<ref>{{Cite|Shield surfing is all about how fast you can make it to the bottom of the hill. Quick check—you do know how to surf, right? Just hold out your shield, and then hop onto it in mid-jump.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

===Beginner Course===
The '''Beginner Course''' is the first course {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} offers to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, which is focused on the fundamentals of {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}} downhill.<ref name="Piggyback"/><ref>{{Cite|What I suggest is starting simple with a basic course, so we can take a look at your downhill fundamentals.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> When running this course, she does not care if {{Term|BotW|Link}} dismounts from his {{Term|BotW|Shield}} at any point.<ref>{{Cite|For this course, I don't care if you stay on your shield or not. You can go so fast that you start to fly for all I care.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s goal is a land bridge at the other end of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Tundra|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Straight down from here, you'll see a big bridge. Why don't you start by surfing your way there?|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> Though this course is simple, {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} believes it will help her understand {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s experience.<ref>{{Cite|It's a pretty no-frills course, but it'll help me get a bead on where you're at with your technique.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is successful, she promises him a more advanced course.<ref>{{Cite|If I dig the way you surf, I'll let you advance to the next step. But we can go down that hill when we get to it.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes the Beginner Course in less than 45 seconds, {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} will allow him to try a more difficult course.<ref name="Selmie">{{Cite|I'd like to see what you can do on a harder slope now. You can consider the {{Color|BotW Red|advanced course}} open for business!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> Similarly, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes the course between one minute and 45 seconds, {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} will ask if he is ready to move on.<ref>{{Cite|You clocked in at {{Color|BotW Red|XX:XX.XX}}... Pretty average time, all told. I'm not too keen on letting you move on, but... Well, I'll make it your call. You comfy where you are, or do you want to push yourself ahead anyway?|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to push forward, {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} instead asks what {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}} means to him.<ref>{{Cite|[Do your worst!] Hmmm. Let's put the test aside for now and just ask you straight out... What does {{Big|shield surfing}} really mean to you?|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds that it is his life, she is disappointed in him and tells him to try the Beginner Course again.<ref>{{Cite|[Shield surfing is life!] Life? It's your whole life? That's your answer? *sigh* Slipping, crashing, and falling over cliffs is no kind of life for anybody. Trust me—I know. If that's how you see things, you're in no shape to advance yet. Sorry, but I'll have to ask you to {{Color|BotW Red|do the basic course again}}.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s answer is a shrug, {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} finds wisdom in this and allows him to move on.<ref>{{Cite|[*shrug*] Uh...are you pulling my leg? Though... These days I'd probably answer the same way. Shield surfing is how we express a passion that we can't put into words. Surfing is its own meaning. Funny, I started out thinking I'd teach you a few tricks, but I ended up learning something pretty important. I think you'll be just fine on the {{Color|BotW Red|advanced course}}. But don't make me regret that decision, OK? Haha...|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

===Advanced Course===
Upon completion of the Beginner Course, {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to attempt the '''Advanced Course'''.<ref name="Selmie"/>For this course, {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s goal is go past the land bridge and towards a pair of {{Plural|BotW|Big Flag|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|First, {{Color|BotW Red|go straight}} under the same bridge that was your goal for the beginner's course. Once you're through there, make a {{Color|BotW Red|sharp turn}} before going down a {{Color|BotW Red|long straightaway}}. You should be able to see a pair of {{Color|BotW Red|big flags}} marking the finish line. Head for those and you can't go wrong.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} will bring {{Term|BotW|Link}} back to the top of the hill if he ends up off-course.<ref>{{Cite|If you do manage to veer too far off course, I'll just drag you back here.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> She promises {{Term|BotW|Link}} special {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} as a reward for beating certain times.<ref>{{Cite|Once you clear the advanced course, you can earn special shields for beating certain times. How's that for incentive?|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Shield}} {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} is full, {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} will refuse to start the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} until he has space for one.<ref>{{Cite|Except...it looks like you have all the shields you can carry. Ditch one or I can't give you a proper reward!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>}}
