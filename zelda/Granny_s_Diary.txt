==Location and Uses==
The diary belonging to [[Granny]] can be found in the lower bedroom of the [[Stock Pot Inn]]. It is the only diary in {{MM|-}} in which the entries changes from the [[First Day|First]] to the [[Second Day]]. Though she appears to be senile, often referring to people by the name of her late son, [[Tortus]],<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Tortus. Did you finish helping father? Then let mother read you a story.|Granny|MM}}</ref> the entries in her diary present evidence that her mind is very much intact.

In the entry that can be read on the First Day, Granny laments [[Anju]]'s horrible cooking, and writes that she has thought of a way to get by without eating the next day.<ref name="Cooking"/> If [[Link]] returns to the Stock Pot Inn at lunch time on the Second Day, he can witness Anju trying to bring her Grandmother lunch, and her getting out of eating by feigning senility.{{Ref Cluster|<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Tortus. I've already had lunch.|Granny|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Grandmother...I am Anju! Tortus was my dad... And you haven't had lunch yet!|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I've already had lunch. Now be quick and take that away.|Granny|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Not eating is bad for you. Please eat...|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Didn't I say that I already ate lunch, Tortus?!? Impossible child!|Granny|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Then don't eat my food. I give up...|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whew!|Granny|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|"Whew?"|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Er... W-W-Whewwwould you like me to read you  a story?|Granny|MM}}</ref>}} In the entry that can be read on the Second Day, Granny writes that the family has decided to take refuge from the imminent [[Terminan Apocalypse|destruction]] of [[Termina]] by the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] at [[Romani Ranch]], and that they will be fleeing [[Final Day|the next day]]. Lastly, she writes that she plans to take some of her [[Gampy|late husband]]'s favorite books.<ref>{{Cite|Granny's Diary<br/>We're going to take refuge at Romani Ranch tomorrow. I guess it really will fall. Leaving my lifelong home and fleeing is unbearable, but nothing can be done... I think I'll take several of Gampy's favorite books.|Granny's Diary|MM}}</ref> Her evident awareness of the turmoil going on around her and that her family will be fleeing her lifelong home, probably for good, is further proof that her senility is merely a ruse.
