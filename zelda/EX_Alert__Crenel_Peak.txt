{{Term/Store|EX Alert: Crenel Peak||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 78 {{Term|HWAoC|EX Vicious Monster Battle|link}} in {{HWAoC}} included in the first downloadable content pack, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Alert: Crenel Peak|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, who are surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}}. After their defeat, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s goal is to capture two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}}, and when the second is captured, a gate opens to an area in which the vicious monster appears. Defeating the vicious monster is the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s final objective. While the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} fights them, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, an {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} also spawn also spawn and attack them. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the vicious monster is defeated, and can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of a {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Mighty Porgy|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Armored Porgy|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Maintenance Oil|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
