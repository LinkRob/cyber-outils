==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} was a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} who lived at {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}} with his mate {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Monkton|link}} when he was a child. {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} recalls a time when he went to play with {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} out at the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth|link}}, while he also played with {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}} by the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Temple|link}}.<ref name="Monkton"/>

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} asks to hear about {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}}, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} explains that the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth}} is a huge maze at the edge of the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tundra|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Tell me about Harry.] There's an {{Color|BotW Red|enormous maze}} beyond the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha Tundra}} just north of here. I don't know who built it or when.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> When he was a child, the adults warned him not to approach it, as it was too dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|The adults always said not to go near it. They said it was too dangerous there.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> One day, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} sent {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} into the maze to test his courage,<ref>{{Cite|One day, I urged {{Color|BotW Blue|Harry}} to go into the maze as a test of courage.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> but he never returned.<ref name="Him"/> All these years later, the memory still breaks his heart.<ref>{{Cite|To this day, the mere memory breaks my heart.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref>

In time, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} reveals that he was able to overcome his grief over losing both {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} and {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}}.<ref>{{Cite|As I grew older, I learned to overcome my grief over losing them. You can't change the past, only the future.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> While they never returned, two of their descendants still reside in the stall next to {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}}.<ref name="Horse"/>
