The {{Term/Store|Red Bomb||Series, OoS}},<ref>{{Cite|OK, pick up this {{Color|OoS Red|Red Bomb}}|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> also known as the '''Big Bomb Bag''',<ref name="Big Bomb Bag">{{Cite|{{Color|OoS Red|Big Bomb Bag}}<br/> I'll trade for {{Color|OoS Red|10 Bombs}} and {{Color|OoS Red|50 Ore Chunks}}|Subrosian|OoS}}.</ref> is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{OoS}}.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoS|Red Bomb}} is sold at the {{Term|OoS|Subrosia Market|link}} under the name "Big Bomb Bag".<ref name="Big Bomb Bag"/> It costs 10 {{Plural|OoS|Bomb|link}} and 50 {{Plural|OoS|Ore Chunk|link}}. If {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} purchases it, the shop owner instructs {{Term|OoS|Link}} to pick it up. The {{Term|OoS|Red Bomb}} explodes harmlessly in {{Term|OoS|Link}}'s hands, at which point his {{Term|OoS|Bomb}}-carrying capacity is increased by 20.
