{{Plural|BotW|Boko Shield}} are {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}-made {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} that are made by attaching a handle to a flat piece of bark.<ref name="Inventory"/> Due to the low level of craftsmanship that went into them, they do not last very long in combat.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly wielded by regular {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}, particularly on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}, as well as in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Shield}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Boko Shield}} are made of wood and will consequently burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They also float when dropped in water, and they do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} like metal {{Plural|BotW|Shield}} do.

If scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Term|BotW|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Boko Shield}}.
