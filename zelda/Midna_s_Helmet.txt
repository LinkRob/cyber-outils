==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Midna's Helmet}} is a {{Term|BotW|Armor|link|display=headpiece}} included in {{Term|BotW|The Master Trials|link}} DLC pack. As part of the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Twilight Relic|link}}," {{Term|BotW|Midna's Helmet}} can be located buried within the {{Term|BotW|Sage Temple Ruins|link}} where {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is required to use {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} to unbury a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Term|BotW|Midna's Helmet}}. Once acquired, {{Term|BotW|Granté|link}} will begin selling {{Term|BotW|Midna's Helmet}} for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, Furthermore it cannot be {{Term|BotW|Dye|link|display=Dyed}}, or enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Midna's Helmet}} has a defense of 7 and grants one level of {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link|display=Guardian Resist Up|section=Guardian Resist Up}}.
