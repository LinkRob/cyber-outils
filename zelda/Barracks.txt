The {{Term/Store|Barracks||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|You should head over to the {{Color|BotW Blue|barracks}} if you want to learn more...|Reeza|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Barracks}} are located in the western corner of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. They house the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldiers, who can be seen training within.<ref name="Liana">{{Cite|What are you doing here, outsider?! This is the barracks for Gerudo soldiers! We're on high alert right now. If you want to do some sightseeing, go check out the market instead!|Liana|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Teake">{{Cite|What are you doing in here?! This is the barracks for the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}}'s guards, not a place for tourists to gather. We're on high alert after the recent theft, so you should clear out.|Teake|BotW}}</ref> From the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}, {{Term|BotW|Ashai|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Relationship Classroom|link}} can be accessed through a doorway at the bottom of a staircase. At the top of this staircase, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can enter the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} initially enters the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}, the soldiers are on high alert due to a recent theft of the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}, which they refuse to discuss with outsiders.<ref name="Liana"/><ref name="Teake"/><ref>{{Cite|Can't you tell that we're on high alert right now?! And no, you can't help! Outsiders must stay out of it!|Babi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You again?! How many times do I have to tell you before you get it?! Get. Out. Of. Here!|Teake|BotW}}</ref> Two soldiers, {{Term|BotW|Smaude|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Marta|link}}, are being subjected to intense training by {{Term|BotW|Babi|link}} since the thieves managed to slip past them.<ref>{{Cite|Those thieves sure have guts, scheming against us like that. We'd never have lost in a fair fight. {{Small|But this training... It's so tough...}}|Smaude|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|*pant pant* Babi's training is really tough, but... It's our fault the thieves were successful. We have no room to complain.|Marta|BotW}}</ref>

While meeting with {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}} suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} prove himself by recovering the stolen {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lady Riju! I don't see how you can trust a complete stranger with something as important as this task. Perhaps if this person were to first prove his worth by recovering your stolen Thunder Helm...|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> To this end, she allows the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} soldiers in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}} to speak freely about the theft to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Our soldiers can tell you all you need to know about the thieves who stole Lady Riju's heirloom. Head through this arch. It will lead you to the barracks. Then, seek out {{Color|BotW Blue|Captain Teake}}, and ask for a full report.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> In the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}, the captain of the guard, {{Term|BotW|Teake|link}}, will explain the details of the theft once {{Term|BotW|Link}} reveals that {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} sent him.<ref>{{Cite|What? Buliara sent you? My apologies! I'm the officer in charge of the soldiers, Captain {{Color|BotW Blue|Teake}}. The chief's heirloom was stolen by thieves belonging to the Yiga Clan the other day, so everyone is on edge... We've determined that the Yiga's hideout is in {{Color|BotW Red|Karusa Valley}}, but... The soldiers are all exhausted, so I was just thinking about requesting some extra help...|Teake|BotW}}</ref> She then commands her soldiers to offer whatever information they have to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hey, everyone! Listen up!}} This Hylian vai has agreed to help us, so offer her any information you can!|Teake|BotW}}</ref> Turning back to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, she tells him to consider himself part of their unit, and he should ask the soldiers any questions he has.<ref>{{Cite|Consider yourself a part of our unit! If you have any questions, my troops will be happy to help.|Teake|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Liana|link}} during this time, he will overhear her wondering where {{Term|BotW|Barta|link}} has gone.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, I haven't seen Barta. Where's she gone off to?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leena|link}} reveals that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} went to scout out the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}} two days prior, leaving before anyone could stop her.<ref>{{Cite|She said she was going to scout the thieves' hideout, but then she left before we could say anything. Now that I think about it, she's been gone for two days. I wonder what happened to her...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is shocked that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} has been gone for so long without anyone informing her.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|You— Are you serious?!}} Why did you let her go by herself?! And why did you wait two days to report this?!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leena}} apologizes, recalling that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} claimed she would be careful.<ref>{{Cite|I'm— I apologize... She said she just wanted to check it out and would be careful, so I thought...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} expresses her frustration that they have to search for both the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} and {{Term|BotW|Barta}}, and she is afraid of {{Term|BotW|Teake}} punishing them if she hears about what happened.<ref>{{Cite|Uuugghhh. As if we aren't busy enough searching for the chief's heirloom, now we have to find {{Color|BotW Blue|Barta}} too... If the captain hears about this fiasco, she'll punish us for sure! Probably 100 laps around Gerudo Desert...again.|Liana|BotW}}</ref> Realizing the gravity of the situation, {{Term|BotW|Kotta|link}} asks what they should do.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Oh, no. What should we do?|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} yells that she is thinking about it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|I'm thinking about it!}}|Liana|BotW}}</ref>

After the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}} to find that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} has disappeared again.<ref>{{Cite|What happened to Barta?|Liana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whuh... Whah...<br/>{{Big|WHAAAAT?!}}<br/>Barta didn't come back AGAIN?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} explains that she left to investigate the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton|link}}, southwest of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid this time she went to investigate the {{Color|BotW Blue|leviathan bones}} in the southwest...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leena}} claims she tried to stop her,<ref>{{Cite|I... I tried to stop her...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Liana}} points out that this obviously did not work.<ref>{{Cite|But obviously it didn't work because she's not here!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> After witnessing their exchange, "{{Term|BotW|The Search for Barta|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves {{Term|BotW|Barta}} from her exhaustion, she considers resting at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton}} longer before she returns to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hahh... Anyway, I'm absolutely beat. Maybe I'll rest just a little longer and then head home.|Barta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hah...Anyway, I'm totally exhausted. Maybe I'll rest just a little longer then head home.|Barta|BotW}}</ref> However, when she hears how angry {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is, she decides to leave right away.<ref>{{Cite|[Your boss was MAD.] Huh? So...they knew I snuck off... {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|...Oh, I am in such trouble...}}}} Yep, looks like I'd better go on agead. See you later and sav'orq!|Barta|BotW}}</ref> Back in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}, {{Term|BotW|Liana}} will commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for helping {{Term|BotW|Barta}}, giving him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! What a courageous vai! I heard from Barta! She said you found her! I told her you were as dependable as a Champion! If you were a Gerudo vai, I'd want you in my troop! Anyway, close one, huh? Looks like I got out of this without getting chewed out by our troop leader! You must be worn out, right? Here, take this as thanks! Buy yourself something nice in the market!|Liana|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Malena|link}} frequents the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}} during the day, where she begs the guards for help.<ref>{{Cite|Someone...|Malena|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to, she reveals that her husband has fallen ill, and he can only be cured with {{Term|BotW|Molduga Guts|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I... I finally found the voe of my dreams, and I just recently married him... I thought I had finally found happiness... But then...my husband fell ill with a rare disease... One difficult to cure. The only thing that can save him is the {{Color|BotW Blue|innards of a Molduga}}...|Malena|BotW}}</ref> She returned to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to ask the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} soldiers for help, but they were unable to offer her any.<ref>{{Cite|But I would never be able to take down such a creature myself... I asked the soldiers for help, but... They said they have serious issues to deal with! They had no time for my plight! They turned me away! But then, why wouldn't they? The {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga}} is a menacing beast... None who face one escape unscathed... So perhaps it's foolish to expect anyone to stick their neck out for a stranger... I thought I'd finally gotten out of this town... Never thought I'd have to come crawling back...|Malena|BotW}}</ref> After speaking to her, the "{{Term|BotW|Medicinal Molduga|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will begin.
