'''''{{Name|Japanese|The Legend of Zelda Gaiden: Kwōku and the Fairy Queen}}''''' is a one-shot [[manga]] story based on {{TLoZ|Series}}. It was drawn and written by Toshihiro Ono and was published in the February 1992 issue of the Japanese magazine ''{{Wp|CoroCoro Comic|Bessatsu CoroCoro Comic Special}}''.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= n.d. |retrieved= July 27, 2019 |url= http://www5d.biglobe.ne.jp/~m_yokko/92b.htm |title= Bessatsu CoroCoro Comic Special 1992 |site= CoroCoro Comic Shiryō-hen |type=}}</ref> As a bi-monthly publication, Bessatsu CoroCoro Comic Special's cover date is always ahead of the actual release date. As such, this issue would have been released late December 1991. It has never been reprinted.

==Overview==
This manga is a single chapter story, created to promote {{ALttP}}, which had recently been released in Japan. The story is wholly original, written as a side story (''Gaiden'') to the plot of {{ALttP|-}}. It was Toshihiro Ono's first professionally published manga.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= n.d. |retrieved= July 27, 2019 |url= http://www.manga-gai.net/column/shitumon/shitumon_ono.html |title= The 41st Guest / Mr. Toshihiro Ono|site= Manga-Gai.net|type=}}</ref>
