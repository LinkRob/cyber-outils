The {{Term/Store|Shoda Sah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Shoda Sah Shrine}}<br/>Impeccable Timing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Impeccable Timing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Shoda Sah Shrine}} is located in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. The Shrine is hidden behind the highest of the {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Floria Falls|link}}, which flows out from {{Term|BotW|Riola Spring|link}}. There is a path next to the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}} that leads to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shoda Sah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Shoda Sah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Shoda Sah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Shoda Sah Shrine}} features one large chamber containing a large pool of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. There is a bridge across the center of the room flanked by two {{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}. The one on the right features no obstacles, while the one on the left has two moving platforms the float in front of it vertically. There are also two slanted {{Plural|BotW|Launcher|link}} opposite of each {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. Link must use the {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} to launch one of the five {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} into the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}.

Each {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}} that is activated results in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} being sent fling from a {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} on the opposite side of the room. Inside the right platform's {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} are five {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}}. Inside the left platform's {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} is a {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}}, which is needed to unlock the door at the end of the bridge.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} opens the door, he will find the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Shoda Sah}}. {{Term|BotW|Shoda Sah}} will congratulate {{Term|BotW|Link}} for completing the Trial, giving him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Shoda Sah|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Shoda Sah|BotW}}</ref>
