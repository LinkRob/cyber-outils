The {{Term/Store|Nice Sand Rod||Series, ALBW}} is an item in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Once Link acquires the three [[Pendants of Virtue]], he can buy the [[Sand Rod]] from Ravio for 800 Rupees. Once he owns the rod, Link can then find and bring 10 [[Lost Maiamai|Lost Maiamais]] to [[Mother Maiamai]] to get her to upgrade the [[Sand Rod]] into the [[Nice Sand Rod]].

The [[Nice Sand Rod]] is much the same as the [[Sand Rod]], except that it is enhanced so that the sand pillars from the Rod will not disappear over time. Instead, the Rod has to be used again in order to collapse them.{{Stub|ALBW}}
