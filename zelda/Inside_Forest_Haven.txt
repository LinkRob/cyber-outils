"Forest Haven" is a [[Song]] in {{TWW}}.{{Name Ref}}

==Overview==
"Inside Forest Haven" is associated with the epynomous location, {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Haven|link}}. It plays in the interior of {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Haven}}.

It is the 57th track on Disc 1 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker Original Sound Tracks]]''.

==Trivia==
*"Inside Forest Haven" contains a remix of part of the music of [[Kokiri Forest]] and "{{Term|Series|Saria's Song|link}}" from {{OoT}}.
