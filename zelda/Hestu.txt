{{Term/Store|Hestu||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hestu|N/A|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Hestu" /> He is a [[Korok]] and a musician, who plays the [[Hestu's Maracas|Maracas]]. He uses these to expand [[Link|Link's]] inventory in exchange for [[Korok Seed|Korok Seeds]]. To fully expand his inventory, Link needs to find 441 Korok Seeds. However, in {{BotW}}, there are 900 Korok Seeds hidden in [[Hyrule]], thus the other 459 have no value other than as collectibles.

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
When Link first encounters a Korok, it will believe that it has been found by Hestu before realizing that it is mistaken.<ref>{{Cite|Yah-ha-ha! You found me! Huh? You're not Hestu!|Korok|BotW}}</ref> The Korok will then give Link a Korok Seed ask him to return it to Hestu.<ref name="Return Seed" /> From this point forward, Koroks will continue to give Link Korok Seeds once he finds them.

Initially, Hestu can be found on the road between [[Kakariko Bridge]] and [[Kakariko Village]]. Hestu is surprised that Link can see him,<ref>{{Cite|You! You can see me?!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> saying that the last time anyone was able to see him was 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|It's been 100 years since anyone has been able to see me!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> He introduces himself and asks Link for assistance.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Hestu, and I need your help!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> He will tell Link that his [[Hestu's Maracas|Maracas]] were stolen from him by a group of monsters.<ref>{{Cite|Those monsters over there stole my beloved {{Color|BotW Red|maracas}}!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Hestu explains that he is powerless without them,<ref>{{Cite|I can't use my {{Color|BotW Blue|powers}} without them. Shoko...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> and so he asks Link to retrieve them in his stead.<ref>{{Cite|So please! PLEASE get my {{Color|BotW Red|maracas}} back from them!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> This dialogue will trigger "[[The Priceless Maracas]]" [[Side Quest]], in which Link will have to retrieve Hestu's Maracas from a [[Treasure Chest]] in a nearby [[Monster Stronghold]]. If spoken to again, Hestu will ask if he completed the task and hopes the monsters have not been using them.<ref>{{Cite|Soooooooo... Did you get my {{Color|BotW Red|maracas}} back yet? They better not be shaking them.|Hestu|BotW}}</ref>

Once Link retrieves Hestu's Maracas, he is overcome with joy and asks for them back.<ref>{{Cite|SHALA-KALA! Those are... Those are my {{Color|BotW Red|maracas}}! Please giiiiiive them to meeeeeeeee!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> When Link} returns them, Hestu can tell something is wrong and realizes that the Korok Seeds inside them are gone.<ref>{{Cite|But wait! There's something wrong with my maracas! The {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seeds}} inside are gone! How am I supposed to dance now? Shoko...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Hestu will then explain that if he had even one Korok Seed, he would be able to sing, dance, and use his powers to expand Link's [[Inventory]].<ref>{{Cite|If I had just {{Color|BotW Red|one Korok seed}}, I could sing, dance and use my {{Color|BotW Blue|powers}} to help you. You see, I have the power of {{Color|BotW Blue|inventory}} expansion! So let me know if you find some {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seeds}}!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Hestu can upgrade Link's [[Weapon]] stash, [[Bow]] stash, or [[Shield]] stash. Hestu states that the other Koroks must have taken the Seeds, and he is surprised to find that he was the target of such a prank.<ref>{{Cite|The children of the forest must have taken the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seeds}} from my beloved maracas. I never thought I would be the target of one of their pranks. Shoko...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref>

If Link has Korok Seeds with him, Hestu will be able to smell them.<ref>{{Cite|*sniff* But wait... *sniff sniff* Do you have a {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seed}}?! You do! You got a {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seed}} from one of the forest children!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> For the first expansion of Link's Inventory, Hestu asks for a single Korok Seed.<ref>{{Cite|If you'll give me just {{Color|BotW Red|one Korok seed}}, I'll make your {{Color|BotW Blue|inventory}} bigger in return! |Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Should Link refuse, Hestu will respond with a dejected sound.<ref>{{Cite|Shoko...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> If Link agrees to the trade, Hestu will ask which stash he would like to upgrade.<ref>{{Cite|SHALAKA! So which {{Color|BotW Blue|stash}} do you want more slots in?|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Every time Hestu upgrades a stash, he will sing the tune that plays with it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|♪Dah-na-na-na-naaaaaaaa!♪}}|Hestu|BotW}}</ref>

Hestu will only allow Link two upgrades before announcing that he needs to return to the [[Korok Forest]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Look at the time! I need to get back before {{Color|BotW Red|Grandpa}} yells at me. Shoko... Sorry, I have to go. But I still need Korok seeds, so bring some to {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Forest}} if you find any!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Hestu thanks Link for his help and promises to see him off before leaving.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for bringing my maracas back! Once I see you off, I'm going back home to Korok Forest!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Before he leaves, Link can talk to him to learn more about the Koroks. If asked about the Korok Forest, he will recite the directions he was told: follow the [[Hylia River]] north.<ref>{{Cite|Korok Forest... It should be somewhere north of here. {{Color|BotW Red|Grandpa}} told me, "If you ever get lost, follow the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylia River}} and continue north." That's what he said, and Grandpa is no liar.|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Asking about Hestu himself will have him reintroduce himself and state that he is the most celebrated musician in the Korok Forest.<ref>{{Cite|My name is {{Color|BotW Blue|Hestu}}! The most celebrated musician of Korok Forest! Shalaka lala!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Should Link ask about Korok Seeds, Hestu will hesitate in his explanation before giving an excuse to not continue, saying his voice is blown out.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Korok seeds}}...come from the Korok children...and they're a...little bit... ... Sorry... I blew out my voice singing...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref>

Hestu does travel north, but he becomes lost along the way. While Hestu is lost, Link can find him [[Riverside Stable]], [[Wetland Stable]], and [[Woodland Stable]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm trying to get back to Korok Forest, but I've lost my way...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> During these encounters, Link can obtain four more stash upgrades before Hestu remembers the way back.<ref>{{Cite|I... I think I remember how to get back to Korok Forest!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Maybe I'll head back now. I bet {{Color|BotW Red|Grandpa}} is waiting for me.|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> If Link makes it into the Korok Forest before getting these four upgrades, Hestu will appear there straight away and will no longer appear at any [[Stable]]s.

When Link speaks to Hestu in the Korok Forest, he will be excited and reintroduce himself.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|♪Shalaka!♪}} It's you! We meet again! What's with the silent treatment? Don't you remember me? I'm the musician of Korok Forest, Hestu!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> He informs Link that it is impressive he was able to make it through the [[Lost Woods]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm impressed you found your way through the Lost Woods and made it here...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> In Korok Forest, Hestu will offer Link the maximum amount of Inventory upgrades available. 

Once Link has located all 900 Koroks hidden throughout [[Hyrule Kingdom]], he will be instructed to share the news with Hestu.<ref>{{Cite|You've found all of the hidden Koroks! You should tell Hestu the good news!|N/A|BotW}}</ref> Hestu excitedly informs Link that the Koroks have all returned to Korok Forest.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hey! Guess what!}} All of the forest children returned to Korok Forest!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> He claims that Link must have had a lot of people helping him search for the Koroks.<ref>{{Cite|You must have had an army of people searching for them, huh?|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> When Link instead explains that he found them all on his own, Hestu will be impressed.<ref>{{Cite|[Nope, just me.] {{Big|NO WAY! Fwahahahaha!}} ♪NAAAAAAAAICE!♪|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Since Link has already maximized his Inventory, he will be given [[Hestu's Gift]] as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|You've maxed all of your {{Color|BotW Blue|stashes}}! So let me give you this!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Hestu asks Link if he likes his Gift.<ref>{{Cite|So? Do you like it?|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> He invites Link to keep visiting him to watch his dances.<ref>{{Cite|I hope you'll still visit me. You can watch me dance whenever you want! ♪Shalaka!♪|Hestu|BotW}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
Hestu first appears in the "[[Freeing Korok Forest]]" [(Scenario]] after the characters admit they might have gotten lost in the [[Lost Woods]]. He then becomes a playable character after it is revealed that a [[Malice]] swamp is blocking the way to [[Korok Forest]]. His main weapons consist of [[Maracas]] and [[Shaka Maracas]].
{{Ambig|End}}

==Trivia==
*If Link talks to Hestu while wearing no [[Armor]], Hestu will humorously react to Link's "nekkidness" before gleefully celebrating that he and Link are naked together.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Shaka! Shakaka! You are nekkid! I am nekkid!}} We are united in exposure! ♪Nekky nokky nakey!♪|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Due to Hestu's mirth, Link will be unable to receive any upgrades until he puts some Armor on.<ref>{{Cite|♪Nakey nakey!♪<br/>♪Nekky nokky nakey!♪|Hestu|BotW}}</ref>
