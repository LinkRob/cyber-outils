==Location and Uses==
==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Keese Wing}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Volcano|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Hinox Mine|link}}''', ''"Don't pop any balloons!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Keese Wing}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Den of Flames|link}}''', ''"Avoid the volcanic rocks!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Keese Wing}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Fire Temple|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Keese Wing}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Fire Temple}}''', ''"Only Bombs-no swords!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Keese Wing}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Keese Wing}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Energy Gear|link}} {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 128"/>

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, Keese Wings are commonly dropped by [[Keese]] when defeated. As is the case with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make all kinds of [[Elixir]]s. They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Gray [[Dye]] or exchanged for [[Mon]] at the [[Fang and Bone]]. There are three variations of Keese Wings, which are [[Fire Keese Wing]]s, [[Electric Keese Wing]]s, and [[Ice Keese Wing]]s, which come from the relating variation of species.

Two sets of [[Armor]] require Keese Wings for upgrades at {{Plural|Series|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. A total of three Keese Wings, as well as three [[Rushroom]]s, are needed to upgrade each piece of the [[Climbing Set]] to its first tier. Meanwhile, the [[Soldier's Set]] requires Keese Wings and [[Moblin Guts]] to reach its second tier.

* Total for '''Climbing Set''': 9 (3, 0, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Soldier's Set''': 15 (0, 5, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
