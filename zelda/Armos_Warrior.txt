The {{Term/Store|Armos Warrior||Series, OoA}} is the {{Term|OoA|Sub-Boss}} of {{Term|OoA|Skull Dungeon|link}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 86}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The {{Term|OoA|Armos Warrior}} is a variant of an {{Term|OoA|Armos|link}} that boasts a huge shield that protects it from any damage, and a sword that it directs seemingly telepathically. As the {{Term|OoA|Armos Warrior}} runs around the room in a rectangular path, occasionally changing direction, its sword takes flight and begins its own assault on {{Term|OoA|Link|link}}. The sword will spin in place like a fan before launching toward {{Term|OoA|Link}}, who must move to avoid the weapon. It then stops for moment and repeats the attack. {{Term|OoA|Link}} must position himself so that the spinning sword collides with the {{Term|OoA|Armos Warrior}}, causing a crack in its shield.

After being hit by its own sword three times, the {{Term|OoA|Armos Warrior}}'s shield breaks, angering the {{Term|OoA|Armos Warrior|display=Armos}}. It then resorts to charging at {{Term|OoA|Link}} repeatedly, stunning itself momentarily when it slams into a wall. {{Term|OoA|Link}} can take this brief opportunity to strike the {{Term|OoA|Armos Warrior}} with his {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}}. After taking enough damage, it will be defeated, leaving behind a {{Term|OoA|Fairy|link}} and a portal to the {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}}'s entrance.
