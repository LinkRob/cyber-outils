==Overview==
==={{PH|-}}===
{{Lacking Sources|PH}}
After {{Term|PH|Link}} rescues {{Term|PH|Linebeck|link}} from the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}} and meets him back at his ship, {{Term|PH|Oshus|link}} arrives and tells the young hero that he should go to the {{Term|PH|Isle of Ember|link}} and talk to {{Term|PH|Astrid|link}} for clues on how to find the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}. {{Term|PH|Linebeck}} agrees to go with both {{Term|PH|Link}} and {{Term|PH|Ciela|link}} on their quest to travel around the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King}} and rescue {{Term|PH|Tetra|link}}. 

The {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} is controlled by drawing its path on the bottom screen of the [[Nintendo DS]]. While out on the sea, there will be an arrow at the bottom of the screen which can be pressed to make the ship jump and evade {{Plural|PH|Sea Trap|link}}. The side of the touch screen has a gear with the words "Go" and "Stop" to halt the ship if necessary. When {{Term|PH|Link}} buys the {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}} from {{Term|PH|Eddo|link}}, it will be immediately installed onto the ship, where {{Term|PH|Link}} will have an infinite amount of {{Plural|PH|Bomb|link}} to destroy enemies out on the sea by just tapping the monster. Eventually the {{Term|PH|Salvage Arm|link}} will also get installed on the ship to obtain treasure from deep beneath the waves.

When at sea, the ship's endurance meter appears as a green {{Term|PH|Life Gauge|link}} on the top screen. If the ship runs out of hearts, it will be damaged beyond repair and sink, resulting in a [[wikipedia:Destroyed|Game Over]]. Docking the ship replenishes any hearts lost during travels. The {{Term|PH|Salvage Arm}}, however, must be repaired by {{Term|PH|Gazpacho|link}} in the {{Term|PH|Shipyard|link}} on {{Term|PH|Mercay Island|link}}. The ship can also be customized with different {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}} by talking to {{Term|PH|Gazpacho}}.

In order to dock on an island, the ship's destination must be drawn and touch the anchor symbol that is situated next to the island. Once done so, the ship will automatically follow the path drawn and land on the island as it is nearing to it.

During the battle with {{Term|PH|Bellum|link}}, the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} gets completely destroyed after {{Term|PH|Link}} and {{Term|PH|Linebeck}} defeat the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}. Once the {{Term|PH|Ocean King|link}} returns to his original form, {{Term|PH|Linebeck}}, instead of asking for treasure, asks for his ship to be restored. The {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} is last seen during the game's ending when {{Term|PH|Link}} watches it sailing away without any visible damages.

==={{HW|L|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW|L}}, {{Plural|HWDE|S.S. Linebeck}} appear as {{Plural|HWDE|Item Card|link}} on the {{Term|HWDE|Grand Travels Map|link}} in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}. The {{Term|HWDE|Grand Travels Map}} was not included with the {{Term|HW|Phantom Hourglass & Spirit Tracks Pack|link}} for the original {{HW}}, so {{Plural|HWDE|S.S. Linebeck}} weren't introduced until the {{Term|HWL|Phantom Hourglass & Spirit Tracks Pack}} released for {{HW|L|-}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Phantom Hourglass & Spirit Tracks Pack}}<br/>This pack includes:<br/>New playable character: Toon Zelda<br/>New weapon: Sand Wand for Toon Link|author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= November 30, 2021|url= https://www.zelda.com/hyrule-warriors/wiiu/dlc/|title= Expand your adventure with dlc packs|site= Zelda.com|type= }}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= A new map for Adventure Mode|author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= November 30, 2021|url= http://web.archive.org/web/20160904032504/http://www.zelda.com/hyrule-warriors-legends/#buynow/1|title= Individual Packs|site= Zelda.com|type= web archive}}</ref> Because {{HW|DE}} came with all previous DLC packs included, {{Plural|HWDE|S.S. Linebeck}} are available from the moment {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode}} is unlocked.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This new version brings every map, character, and mission from both the Wii U game and the Nintendo 3DS game together, including all the original premium downloadable content.|author= Nintendo|published= January 11, 2018|retrieved= January 11, 2018|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_u7g5BlfiY|title= Nintendo Direct Mini 1.11.2018|site= Youtube.com|type= video}}</ref>

{{Plural|HWDE|S.S. Linebeck}} can be obtained either by purchasing them from the {{Term|HWDE|Item Card Shop|link}} for 20,000 {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee|link}}, or by getting one as a battle reward. Because the {{Term|HWDE|Grand Travels Map}} is split between {{ST}} and {{PH}}-based sections, {{Plural|HWDE|S.S. Linebeck}} only appear as victory rewards of battles to the right on the pink dividing line. When a {{Term|HWDE|S.S. Linebeck}} is used, every battle across the {{Term|HWDE|Grand Travels Map}} will have less of a KO requirement for certain battle ranks, which makes it easier for {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} to achieve higher ranks for less kills.<ref name="HWDE Tutorial"/><ref>{{Cite|An {{Color|HWDE Green|Engine Power}} is affecting the entire Grand Travels Map!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Captain Power|link}} provided by the {{Term|HWDE|S.S. Linebeck}} runs for three battles.

{{Plural|HWDE|S.S. Linebeck}} can be traded for a random delicious-type {{Term|HWDE|Food|link}} at the {{Term|HWDE|Linebeck Trading Company|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|On map squares with the Linebeck Trading Company, you can {{Color|HWDE Green|swap one Item Card for food}}.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> While searching a map space with the {{Term|HWDE|Linebeck Trading Company}}, any {{Term|HWDE|Item Card}} can be traded by selecting it.<ref>{{Cite|We have a deal!<br/>I'll trade you some food for that Item Card!|Linebeck Trading Company|HWDE}}</ref> After a single trade, the company will disappear from the space.<ref>{{Cite|The Linebeck Trading Company disappears from the square after just a single trade but will pop up again after a certain number of battles.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> During a {{Term|HWDE|Two-for-One Sale|link}}, two delicious {{Plural|HWDE|Food}} will be given instead of just one.<ref>{{Cite|The Linebeck Trading Company will occasionally hold a {{Color|HWDE Green|Two-for-One Sale}}. During these promotions, trading an Item Card will get you double the usual amount of food in return.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>
