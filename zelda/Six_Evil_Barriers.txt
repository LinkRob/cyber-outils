The {{Term/Store|Six Evil Barriers||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} are objects in {{OoT}}.<ref name="Bridge">{{Cite|Link...can you hear me? It's Rauru, the Sage. We six will gather our power to create a bridge to the castle where Ganondorf dwells... The castle's keep, which is known as Ganon's Tower, is protected by {{Color|OoT3D Red|six evil barriers}}. Bring down the six barriers, and save Princess Zelda!|Rauru|OoT3D}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Six Evil Barriers}} are located {{Term|OoT3D|Inside Ganon's Castle|link}}, where they protect Ganon's Tower. In order to access {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon's Tower}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} must infiltrate each {{Term|OoT3D|Six Evil Barriers|display=Barrier}}'s room, denoted by their corresponding {{Plural|OoT3D|Sage Medallion|link}}, and free the {{Plural|OoT3D|Sage|link}} inside.<ref name="Bridge"/> Once all of the {{Term|OoT3D|Six Evil Barriers}} are dispelled, the force protecting the entrance to {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon's Tower}} will dissipate.
