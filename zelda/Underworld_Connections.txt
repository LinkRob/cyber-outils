'''Underworld Connections''' is the eighth episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. It first aired in the United States and Canada on October 27th, 1989.

==Plot==
:''See also: [[Underworld Connections/Episode transcript|Episode transcript]]''
[[File:Zelda (Underworld Connections).png|thumb|left|Zelda rescues Link]]
[[Link]], while sleepwalking, shoots an [[arrow]] tied to a rope across to [[Princess Zelda]]'s bedroom, but is woken up by the princess before he is able to reach the other side.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Sleepwalking? Sure! You were trying to sneak a quick kiss!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> After speaking of his true feelings for Zelda,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Anyway, princess, can you blame me? Whether I'm awake or asleep, you're the girl of my dreams.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> she leans over to give the young hero a kiss but the two are suddenly interrupted when Zelda notices three [[Vire]]s flying towards the chamber where the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] is kept.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Link, look! Those Vires, they're after the Triforce!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> Zelda realizes that they plan to blow up the Triforce with a [[bomb]] so that they will be able to carry it back to the [[Underworld]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Not if they blow it apart. That one's got a bomb!|name=Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> Although Link rushes over to stop the monsters,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=On my way, princess!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> he is knocked over by the explosion of the bomb and is rescued just in time by Zelda. The young duo make it in time to zap one of the Vires carrying a shard of the Triforce, but the other two manage to get away into the Underworld.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=They're getting away! Stop them!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> Thinking quickly, Zelda remembers that, since all of the pieces of the Triforce are magically linked, if Link shoots a [[Sword Beam|sword beam]] into the shard that they currently have, it will go through the two others and zap the Vires.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I got it! Since all of the pieces of the Triforce are magically linked, if you shoot a zap into this one, it'll go through to the others and zap the Vires!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> The two Vires are effectively defeated, but due to the foes taking separate ways to the Underworld, one of the pieces falls into the [[Black Falls]] and another one into a lava area.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= I hope so. Yes, I think so. Oh, see? This side feels wet and this side--ow!--feels hot. So that means one piece must have fallen somewhere wet, and the other piece is somewhere hot.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> 

The two heroes grab their shard of the Triforce and begin to head towards an Underworld entrance. Zelda uses the Triforce's magic to dry up a puddle covering the entrance<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Watch. As I said, underworld entrance. C'mon, Link!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> and utilizes the [[Magic Compass]] to know which way to go.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= The Magic Compass says...that way!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> As they make their way to the first Triforce shard, [[Rope]]s attack Link, but after he zaps a few of them, Zelda tells him to stop using his [[Crissword|sword]] since otherwise Ganon will know their location.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Link! Stop zapping those Ropes! Ganon will know we're here!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> The pair eventually arrives at the Black Falls, where Link dives in to recover the Triforce shard;<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Definitely. We knew it was some place wet, and this is the wettest place around. Dive in and find it.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> however, as he's about to grab it, an [[Aquamentus]] attacks him and takes him to the surface while Link is riding on top of it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Zelda! Help! This monstrous Aquamentus wants me for lunch!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> Zelda then grabs a [[bomb]] from Link's pouch and throws it at the Aquamentus, which promptly defeats it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Bombs away!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> With only one shard left, Link and Zelda head off in the direction that the Magic Compass points to, which is a room full of lava pits. Equipping himself with the [[Magical Shield]] to defend himself from the rising fire flames, Link eventually finds the last shard, but just as he's about to pick it up,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ah, there it is! The last piece of the Triforce|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> Ganon appears and confronts the young hero, telling him that the shard is his.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=You and Zelda are my prisoners, and that piece is mine!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> Link, knowing that the Triforce piece is burning hot,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hey, you want this piece, Ganon? You can have it!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> throws it to Ganon's hands, but the evil wizard immediately throws it back when he burns his hands holding it. The young hero then gives it over to Zelda, who finally reunites the Triforce of Wisdom;<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hurray! The Triforce is whole again!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> however, she begins to run away when Ganon throws fire flames in her direction. Link then uses his shield to "flame surf" and grabs Zelda on his way out the Underworld, which they safely exit just before the fire reaches them.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Yahoo! Flame surfing!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> The episode ends with Link trying to get a kiss from Zelda, only to be rejected yet again.
