{{Term/Store|Glass Dagger|Glass Daggers|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Glass Dagger}} {{Icon|CoH Zelda}}{{Icon|CoH Cadence}}<br/>Deals {{Color|CoH Red|3 damage}} to a single enemy in front of you. Breaks if you get hit.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Glass Daggers are strong yet brittle {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} of the {{Term|CoH|Dagger|link}} variety, which always deal 3 damage to a single enemy. Like all daggers, they can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. They can only be found as loot from slain enemies, or from {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} opened by either Cadence, Zelda, or Yves.

They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Glass Short Sword|link}}.
