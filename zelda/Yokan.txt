==Biography==
Yokan is a fisherman who had went fishing at the coast of [[Ahitaru]]. For unknown reasons, he became trapped in the head of a giant fish skeleton, whose socket was blocked by a boulder.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, there's a fisherman trapped in the fish head.|Water Lady|TWoG}}</ref> Zelda is informed of him by the [[Water Lady]], and she leaves to rescue the fisherman by blowing up the boulder with either [[Bomb]]s or the [[Power Glove#The Wand of Gamelon|Power Glove]]. Yokan thanks her for saving him, and decides to quit fishing. He tells her to come see him in [[Sakado]].<ref>{{Cite|Thanks, you saved me! That's the last time I'm going fishing. See you in Sakado!|Yokan|TWoG}}</ref> As he leaves, he drops a [[Key]] that opens Sakado's smithy.

There, Zelda finds that he got a new job as the town blacksmith. He offers to temper her Sword if she brings him a [[Heat Crystal]].<ref>{{Cite|Look, I got a new job! Hey, bring a Heat Crystal. I'll fix up your Sword!|Yokan|TWoG}}</ref> After Zelda brings him one, he tempers her [[Power Sword]] into a much stronger form, and demonstrates its new strength by firing a blast of energy with it.
