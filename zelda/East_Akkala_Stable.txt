{{Term/Store|East Akkala Stable||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|East Akkala Stable|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} located in {{Term|BotW|Deep Akkala|link}}, and is managed by {{Term|BotW|Rudi|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Katosa Aug Shrine|link}} resides next to it.{{Where}} The {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable}} is also home to the area-exclusive {{Term|BotW|Akkala Bun|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Helloooo! Welcome to {{Color|BotW Blue|East Akkala Stable}}! The premier stable of the east! By the way, good sir... The outside is crunchy while the inside is chewy. Take one bite, and your {{Color|BotW Red|energy will go through the roof}}! Go on! Try one of our {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala buns}}! They’re a delicacy straight from the {{Color|BotW Blue|East Akkala Stable}}!|Khini|BotW}}</ref> an unobtainable item that can be bought and eaten for 10 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Khini|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has already located the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Pikango|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, he can be found here as well. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also receive the {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Spring of Power|link}}" from {{Term|BotW|Nobo|link}}, who is staying at the stable, and "{{Term|BotW|A Shady Customer|link}}" from {{Term|BotW|Hoz|link}}, who is employed by the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} as security. If the quest "{{Term|BotW|Robbie's Research|link}}" has been completed {{Term|BotW|Aya|link}} will ask him to light the nearby lantern with a {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}, rewarding him based on the speed of his work.{{How Many}}

Inside {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable}} there is a recipe for {{Term|BotW|Meat and Rice Bowl|link}} on the wall and the fourth volume of the {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill|link}} open on a table.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} feeds {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable}}'s resident {{Term|BotW|Hylian Retriever|link}} three times it will lead him to a metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing ten {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}}.{{Where}}

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Bow|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe|link}} around the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.{{Where}}
