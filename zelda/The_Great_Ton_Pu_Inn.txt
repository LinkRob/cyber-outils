{{Term/Store|The Great Ton Pu Inn||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|- The Great Ton Pu Inn -|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Great Ton Pu Inn is an [[Inn]] nestled in the eastern portion of [[Hateno Village]]. It sits along the main pathway that leads up to the [[Hateno Ancient Tech Lab]]. Bright blue banners along the building's exterior adorned with an image of a crescent moon mark the location.

[[Prima]] is the innkeeper, and the subject of {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|A Gift for My Beloved|link}}".<ref>{{Cite|Prima is the clerk over at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Ton Pu Inn}}.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> The Inn offers a regular bed for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, and a soft bed for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} which provides a "restorative experience", granting [[Link]] an {{Term|BotW|Extra Heart|link}} in addition to regenerating his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A regular bed is {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}. Or, if you’re looking for a more relaxing and restorative mind and body experience... You can have one of our extra-soft downy beds for {{Color|BotW Red|40 rupees}}.|Prima|BotW}}</ref> If Link chooses the soft bed, Prima will tell him he looks well-rested when he wakes.<ref>{{Cite|You look very well rested.|Prima|BotW}}</ref>

[[Leop]], a [[Hylian]] resident of Hateno Village, claims the Inn is the best place in the Village to rest, and stands outside the entrance greeting passerby.<ref>{{Cite|Best place to rest up in all of Hateno Village is {{Color|BotW Blue|Ton Pu Inn}}!|Leop|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Come on in!|Leop|BotW}}</ref> [[Worten]], a traveler, can be found on the Inn's porch in fair weather, or upstairs sleeping at night. [[Teli]], a traveling merchant that trades [[Guardian]] parts, will occasionally visit the Inn as he passes through the Village.
