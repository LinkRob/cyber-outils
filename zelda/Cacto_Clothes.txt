The {{Term/Store|Cacto Clothes||Series, TFH}} are an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 119}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Cacto Clothes are like a rose... gorgeous but YOUCH! They hurt foes who dare to touch your wear.}}

The {{Term|TFH|Cacto Clothes}} are an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}, at a cost of one {{Term|TFH|Supple Leaf|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Thornberry|link}} and 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. They become available for purchase after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} dresses {{Term|TFH|Link}} in a green bodysuit covered in large spines, to which the {{Term|TFH|Stylish Woman|link}} jokes that he's "playing hard to get".<ref>{{Cite|I've heard of playing hard to get, but this is a bit excessive... ♥|Stylish Woman|TFH}}</ref>

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Cacto Clothes}} causes {{Term|TFH|Link}} to automatically deal two {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} strikes' worth of damage to any enemy he comes into contact with. {{Term|TFH|Link}} will also take damage as usual, except from small enemies that are pulled into him using the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot|link}}. Enemies will not be harmed if {{Term|TFH|Link}} only touches their shield or outstretched weapon. The other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} are unaffected by the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}, except during {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} battles, where it deals one {{Term|TFH|Heart}} of damage on contact. The opposing {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can still {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}} the {{Term|TFH|Cacto Clothes}}' wearer if done from a step away.

The {{Term|TFH|Cacto Clothes}} will also cause {{Plural|TFH|Squiddy|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|link}} to explode instantly on contact. Ironically, they will not cause the balloons used in certain {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} to pop. If a {{Term|TFH|Doppel|link}} wearing the {{Term|TFH|Cacto Clothes}} is thrown, it will have no effect on enemies or objects (such as balloons or {{Plural|TFH|Shock Switch|link}}), whereas it would with any other {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}.
