'''Sword Moblin Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Sword Moblin Lv. 2|This strong Moblin is common all over Hyrule. It blocks with its shield but struggles when its attacks are blocked.<ref name="EoW Sword Moblin Lv. 2">{{Cite|This strong Moblin is common all over Hyrule. It blocks with its shield but struggles when its attacks are blocked.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Sword Moblin Lv. 2 are stronger versions of [[Sword Moblin|Sword Moblins]] found throughout [[Hyrule]]. They have higher health, deal more damage, and often appear in Monster Camps alongside other [[Moblin]] types. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which costs four Echo energy to summon.
