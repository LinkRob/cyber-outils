{{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow}} are a powerful type of {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} found within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow}}, spawns above the fireplace in {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room|link}}. Another one spawns inside the {{Term|BotW|Square Spiral Staircase|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series|link}} equipment archetype, which is associated with the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref> Prior to the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow}} were made by the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} using {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} to fight {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow}} make a trade between firepower and {{Term|BotW|Durability}}, as they have a higher strength but has lower {{Term|BotW|Durability}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Parcy|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}, she will tell him about the {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} once used by the {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}, expressing sadness she could never find any of them.<ref>{{Cite|Have you gone to the giant castle north of here? That's Hyrule Castle. It's said to have fallen to ruin over a hundred years ago. I used to sneak in there to salvage ancient treasures, but it's too dangerous to go in there now. These days it's surrounded by an eerie mist and savage machine-like creatures. Back when it was safer, I went in looking for this ultra-rare equipment that once belonged to the {{Color|BotW Red|royal guard}}. It's legendary among collectors, but I was never able to find any...|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> This will begin "{{Term|BotW|The Royal Guard's Gear|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow}} are one of the {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} can bring {{Term|BotW|Parcy}}. She will recognize it immediately, asking to see it.<ref>{{Cite|Wait just a minute! Can you show me that bow you've got there?!|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} allows her to, she will thank him and express a desire to find her own pieces of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you. You have shown me something truly spectacular. I need to go out and find one of these myself.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Parcy}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a token of my appreciation.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> Following this exchange, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can trade {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow}} to {{Term|BotW|Parcy}} for a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Ruby|link}}, or a {{Term|BotW|Sapphire|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, that bow... Will you trade it for a topaz?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, that bow... Will you trade it for a ruby?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, that bow... Will you trade it for a sapphire?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow}} are made of metal, and will therefore conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, they will not burn if they touch {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or if they are exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
