{{Term/Store|Daily Riches||Series, TFH}} is a shop and a minigame in [[Hytopia]] in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=306 }}</ref> It is a minigame where once per day, [[Link]] may pick from a choice of four [[Treasure Chest]]s and try his luck for a random, hidden piece of [[Material]].<ref>{{Cite|Try your luck, but only once a day. Will you get lucky? It's hard to say! Didn't win? Tomorrow you may! Welcome to the Daily Riches shop! Go on, try your luck!|Daily Riches Owner|TFH}}</ref>

==Rules==
Once per day in real-time, Link may pick one of four Treasure Chests for free, of which one of them contains a pre-selected, random piece of Material. Link is informed what kind of Material he may receive before the game starts. If Link opens a wrong chest, he will receive a [[Freebie]] Material instead, which is exclusive to the shop. Once Link opens a chest, he will not be able to open another one until the next day.

If Link returns for every consecutive day, he will find the chests he last opened will still be there, reducing the number of chests left to open and thus increasing his odds of finding a rare Material. This will continue up until the fourth day, when Link is guaranteed to win a rare Material with only one chest remaining.<ref>{{Cite|In fact, if you come here four days in a row... you're guaranteed to win!|Daily Riches Owner|TFH}}</ref> Once the prize is won, Link will be congratulated and the chests will reset the next day. The chests will also reset if Link misses a day.

If Link is unhappy with the prize, he can speak to the [[Daily Riches Owner|owner]] and pay him 50 [[Rupee]]s to reset the chests and offer a new prize.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? What's up, fella? Unhappy with today's lot? The game's free to play, but to change the prize, you'll have to pay. Calm down, fella! Changing the prize and resetting the treasure chests only costs 50 Rupees! Whaddya think? [Change the prize!] You can still only open one chest per day! That's not gonna change, so hope that's OK... [That's fine.] Here you are, fella! Don't ever say I never did ya any favors.|Daily Riches Owner|TFH}}</ref> Link can repeatedly ask the owner to change the prize for 50 more Rupees each time. However, if Link already opened a chest that day, he will still not be able to open another one until the next day.

After Link opens a total of five wrong chests, excluding the fourth's day guaranteed win, the owner will reward Link's bad luck with an order to [[Madame Couture's]] to offer the [[Dapper Spinner Kit]].<ref>{{Cite|You lost FIVE times... Impressive! I have a special gift to acknowledge such spectacularly bad luck. In anticipation of such an occasion, I've ordered a special outfit from the one and only Madame. She calls it... The Dapper Spinner!|Daily Riches Owner|TFH}}</ref>

Following the 2.0.0 update, the chests have been made "peek-proof" and resistant to the effects of [[Linebeck's Uniform]], thus preventing Link from cheating at the minigame.<ref>{{Cite|They say there's an outfit out there that lets you see inside treasure chests before opening them... What a monstrosity! It's a good thing I invested in these peek-proof chests. Worth every Rupee.|Daily Riches Owner|TFH}}</ref>
