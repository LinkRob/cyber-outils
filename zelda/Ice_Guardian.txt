{{Term/Store|Ice Guardian|Ice Guardians|Series, HWAoC|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
Ice Guardians are Guardians enhanced with ice. Their lights are all light blue(Unlike the base malice purple) except their eye, which is more purple. Their attacks are as follows.

Firing a laser

Swinging a leg(if they can stand up)

Jumping up, then slamming down, which generates ice flooring(If they can stand up)

Spinning their legs, then charging forwards(If they can stand up)

Rearing back, with two legs, then slamming them down, causing ice explosions(If they can stand up)
