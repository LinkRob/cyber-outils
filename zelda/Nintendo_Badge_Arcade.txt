'''''Nintendo Badge Arcade''''' is a {{wp|Free-to-play}} application for the [[Nintendo 3DS]]. It involves the player playing a selection of {{wp|Claw crane}} games that can be used to decorate the HOME Menu of the Nintendo 3DS. More badges were occasionally released through weekly updates, until new badge additions were discontinued on May 26th, 2017 in Japan,<ref>{{Cite Web|author= Matthew Weidner|retrieved= December 16, 2017|url= https://nintendowire.com/news/2017/05/11/japanese-badge-arcade-coming-end-month/|title= Japanese Badge Arcade coming to an end this month|site= Nintendo Wire|type=}}</ref> on June 22nd, 2017 in North America,<ref>{{Cite Web|author= Daniel Dell-Cornejo|retrieved=December 16, 2017|url=https://nintendowire.com/news/2017/06/16/north-american-badge-arcade-end-new-badge-releases/|title=North American Badge Arcade to end new badge releases|site=Nintendo Wire|type=}}</ref> and on June 23rd, 2017 in Europe.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Europe will see its last update on June 23, 2017, while North America will be ending a day earlier on June 22.|author= Emilie Aul|published= June 19, 2017|retrieved= December 17, 2017|url= https://zeldauniverse.net/2017/06/19/updates-to-the-nintendo-badge-arcade-will-soon-end-in-europe-and-north-america/|title= Updates to the Nintendo Badge Arcade will soon end in Europe and North America|site= Zelda Universe|type= }}</ref>

Corresponding to the closure of the Nintendo 3DS and Wii U eShops on March 27th, 2023, the selection of badge catchers no longer rotates daily, and the ability to purchase plays was deactivated. Free plays can still be won through the daily practice catcher, and all previously-collected badges can still be accessed.

==Relevance to ''The Legend of Zelda''==
The following {{TLoZ}} games have had sets of badges based on sprites and artwork:
*{{TLoZ}}
*{{TAoL}} (As part of the ''[[NES Remix]]'' set)
*{{ALttP}}
*{{TWWHD}}
*{{ALBW}}
*{{MM3D}} (Some characters' artwork is from {{MM|-}})
*{{TFH}}
*{{TPHD}} (Some characters' and items' artwork is from {{TP|-}})
*{{BotW}}

The now-permanent selection of badge catchers includes three catchers from the {{MM3D|-}} set and two from the {{ALBW|-}} set. Several other sets contained {{TLoZ|-}}-related badges. A set titled "{{TLoZ|-}} - 30th Anniversary" contained artwork of Link from nearly every game in the series, while badges with pixelated sprites of various {{TLoZ|-}} characters were available in the {{SMM|-}} set.<ref>{{Cite Web|author=Brian|retrieved=November 5, 2015|url=https://nintendoeverything.com/collectible-badge-center-adds-zelda-wind-waker-hd-swapnote-nikki-and-other-badges/|title=Collectible Badge Center adds Zelda: Wind Waker HD, Swapnote Nikki, and other badges|site=Nintendo Everything|type=}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|author=Brian|retrieved=November 5, 2015|url=https://nintendoeverything.com/collectible-badge-center-update-zelda-tri-force-heroes-kirby/|title=Collectible Badge Center update – Zelda: Tri Force Heroes, Kirby|site=Nintendo Everything|type=}}</ref> Several badges of {{TLoZ|-}} game cartridges could be found in the "Pixel Collection" set, and several badges from the "amiibo" set featured {{TLoZ|-}} characters. Five badges from the {{TLoZ|-}} set and two badges of [[Famicom Disk System|FDS]] Disk Cards from the "Pixel Collection" set are unavailable outside of Japan.

[[File:NBA Exclusive Theme.png|right|thumb|200px|Arcade Bunny advertising an exclusive HOME Menu theme]]
Some "collaboration" badges were also available, featuring characters from ''[[Animal Crossing]]'', ''Swapdoodle'', the ''[[WarioWare]]'' series, and ''Nintendo Badge Arcade'' itself dressed as characters from {{TLoZ|-}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|author=Lite Agent|published= December 23, 2016|retrieved=January 5, 2018|url=http://www.perfectly-nintendo.com/nintendo-jp-news-dec-23-nintendo-badge-arcade-splatoon-koshien-2017-kirby-pendant/|title=Nintendo JP News (Dec. 23): Nintendo Badge Arcade / Splatoon Koshien 2017 / Kirby pendants|site=Perfectly Nintendo|type=}}</ref> Link also appeared riding the [[Master Cycle]] in the {{MK8|-}} set, as a puppet in the ''Cat Mario'' set,<ref>{{Cite Web||retrieved=March 13, 2018 |url=https://nintendo-badge-arcade.wikia.com/wiki/Cat_Mario |title=Cat Mario |site=Nintendo Badge Arcade Wikia }}</ref> and as badges within badges in the ''Nintendo Badge Arcade'' set. Several sets of ''Zelda'' game badges also included "launcher" badges, which resemble the icons of various built-in Nintendo 3DS apps, remade in the style of those games.

Link also appears on the application's title screen in his {{TWWHD|-}} appearance as part of the Badge Arcade's marquee, and on the loading screen in his {{TLoZ|-}} appearance. Once loaded, the application is hosted by an anthropomorphic pink rabbit named Arcade Bunny, who would often make spoken references to {{TLoZ|-}} when introducing new sets of badges or showcasing the customized HOME Menus that users had shared on [[Miiverse]]. Arcade Bunny has made use of quotes such as [[Iconic Quotes from The Legend of Zelda#.22I am Error.22|"I am Error"]] in his greeting monologues,<ref>{{Cite Web|author= ZeldaFan4AwhileAnyway|published= July 5, 2016|retrieved= February 10, 2018|url= https://imgur.com/gallery/vG7TEwT|title= Nintendo Badge Arcade 2016-04-07 Arcade Adventure|site= Imgur}}</ref> and would frequently comment on his favorite characters and other aspects of the series.

Several of the badge catchers included gimmicks which recreate or allude to gameplay elements from the original games. For instance, in a catcher set inside a [[Dungeon]] from {{TLoZ|-}}, the badge of [[Aquamentus]] could only be collected by knocking the badge of Link's [[Sword Beam]] into it from the opposite side of the screen. A few other {{TLoZ|-}} catchers mimicked the layout of the [[Shop]]s found inside caves. Another catcher based on {{MM3D|-}} features several [[Mask]] badges along the floor and a badge of the [[Moon]] suspended higher up; moving the claw too far will cause the Moon badge to fall onto the Masks, making them more difficult to collect.

An exclusive {{ALBW|-}} HOME Menu Theme, featuring a brick wall suitable for [[Painting]]-style badges, could only be purchased through the application.<ref>{{Cite Web|published= November 17, 2015|retrieved= December 14, 2019|url= https://imgur.com/a/vHmst|title= All Nintendo Badge Arcade Themes (11/17/15)|site= Imgur}}</ref>
