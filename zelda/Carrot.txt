{{Term/Store|Carrot|Carrots|Series, FSA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119 {{Exp Game|FSA}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWL, HWDE}}

==Uses==
==={{OoT|-}} and {{MM|-}}===
[[File:OoT3D Carrot Icon.png|left]]
In {{OoT}} and {{MM}}, a meter consisting of six {{Term|OoT3D|Carrot}} appears while riding a {{Term|OoT3D|Horse|link}}. The number of remaining {{Plural|OoT3D|Carrot}} represents the number of times {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} can whip the {{Term|OoT3D|Horse}} to make it gallop.<ref>{{Cite|Use {{Icon|OoT3D Circle Pad}} to make it run. Once it starts running, press {{Icon|OoT3D A}} to tap it with the whip. It will speed up!|Ingo|OoT3D}}</ref> {{Plural|OoT3D|Carrot}} successively regenerate 3 seconds after the last whipping. If all 6 {{Plural|OoT3D|Carrot}} are exhausted, they regenerate simultaneously after roughly 7 seconds.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Manual|FSA|Carrot|Ride on Epona. You are invincible while you are riding. If you collide with enemies, you can knock them down and scatter Force Gems.}}
In {{FSA}}, Carrots can be collected to increase the speed of the [[Links]]' [[Horse]]s. Obtaining a Carrot without a Horse will instantly grant the Links a Horse. Carrots only appear in [[The Field]] stage and the [[Tingle's Tower]] minigame, Bucking Broncos.

==={{BotW|-}}===
[[File:BotW Endura Carrot Icon.png|left|32px]]
{{Main|Swift Carrot, Endura Carrot}}
In {{BotW}}, Carrots appear in two different varieties, [[Swift Carrot|Swift Carrots]] and [[Endura Carrot|Endura Carrots]]. Uncooked Swift Carrots restore half of a heart each. When cooked into a dish, they add a "Hasty" bonus which temporarily increases Link's movement speed. Uncooked Endura Carrots restore two hearts each and when cooked into a dish, grant Link a "Enduring" bonus which gives him extra maximum [[Stamina]]. Swift Carrots look like ordinary orange Carrots, while Endura Carrots are bifurcated with a lighter orange hue. Endura Carrots will also temporarily grant {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} three extra yellow Spurs with a carrot icon next to them. These extra "Carrot" Spurs act similarly to Link's extra Stamina Wheels granted via "Enduring" dishes and {{Plural|BotW|Enduring Elixir|link}} as they are lost once used and can only be used after other Spurs have been used up including those granted by the [[Ancient Bridle]]. Roasted Endura Carrots have the same effect if eaten off the ground by Horses. Horses can also eat Swift Carrots but this only increases their Bond. Additionally the [[Giant Horse]] can eat both types but Endura Carrots only increase its Bond as it has no Stars in Stamina thus no Spurs.
==={{TotK|-}}===
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Carrot|Harvested fresh from a field northwest of Hyrule Castle, this carrot is quite sweet. Horses come running for them.<ref name="EoW Carrot">{{Cite|Harvested fresh from a field northwest of Hyrule Castle, this carrot is quite sweet. Horses come running for them.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Carrots are vegetables grown in the [[Carrot Patch]] near [[Hyrule Castle]]. During the [[Impa’s Gift]] [[Side Quest]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can use the [[Tri Rod]] to learn the Carrot [[Echo]]. This Echo costs one energy to summon and can be used to call [[Zelda’s horse]] to her location.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|L|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:HWDE Carrot Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, Carrots appear as Plant-type [[Food#Hyrule Warriors|Food]]. They are imbued with the Lightning {{Term|HWDE|Element|link}}. When consumed by a [[Companion Fairy]] in the [[Dining Room]] of the [[My Fairy]] mode, Carrots can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.
{{Noncanon|End}}
