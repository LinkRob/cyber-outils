The '''Brotherhood of Underworld Monsters''' is an organization made up by [[Ganon]]'s minions that were tired of having to take the blame for his failed plans at retrieving the [[Triforce of Wisdom]].<ref name=moblin>{{Cite Episode|quote=We tired of your plans, Ganon. So now, laugh's on you!|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> They are commanded by a [[Moblin]].

==History==
[[File:Moblin (The Moblins are Revolting).png|thumb|left|The Moblin that leads the Brotherhood of Underworld Monsters]]
After two Moblins and two [[Goriya]]s once again fail to obtain the Triforce of Wisdom from [[Link]], Ganon berates them and electrocutes them with his magic for their failure.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Silence! Maybe that will shock some sense into you!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> One of the Moblins then complains as to why they have to be punished for it when it was Ganon's plan in the first place,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=That not fair. It HIS stupid plan.|name=Moblin|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> and the other three monsters agree with him, with one Goriya saying that they should kick him out since they can do better than him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Uh, why? We can do better. Let's kick him out!|name=Goriya|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> Later, when Ganon returns to his lair and shows a Moblin on how his new [[Capture Staff]] works, one of the Moblin steals the Capture Staff and traps Ganon inside the indestructible bubble as he reveals that the monsters are sick of his plans.<ref name=moblin/> With Ganon now trapped, the Moblin pushes him into the [[Underworld#Bottomless Pit|Bottomless Pit]] to prevent him from ever touching the [[Triforce of Power]] and bursting the bubble.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=No, no! Not the Bottomless Pit! Noooo!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> The Moblin leader proceeds to free all of the monsters trapped within the [[Evil Jar]] and gives them the news that Ganon is gone, to which they all answer with a cheer as they realize that they are in charge now.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=He gone. Everybody out.|name=Moblin|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> Unfortunately, the Moblin leader hands the Capture Staff to another Moblin, specifically telling him not to drop it,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Here, hold this, and DON'T drop it.|name=Moblin|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> but the bumbling foe misunderstands the message and breaks the staff,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Don't what? [...] Whatever you say.|name=Bumbling Moblin|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> much to the leader's dismay.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh! Now I know how Ganon feel!|name=Moblin|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref>

As their first plan, the Moblin leader commands that they must get revenge on Link and Zelda.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=And first thing we do is get revenge on Link and Zelda.|name=Moblin|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> They deliver a message to Princess Zelda, telling her to "surrender or be destroyed,"<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=It says, "Princess Zelda, surrender or be destroyed! Signed, the Brotherhood of Underworld Monsters."|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> and when she and Link look outside, they realize that the minions have laid siege to [[North Castle]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ganon's monsters have surrounded the castle!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> However, they soon find out that they pose no threat since their army is disorganized as they are botching their attempts to attack the castle by accidentally shooting their own troops and not following the Moblin leader's orders properly.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= I see what you mean, princess! These dimwits are beating themselves!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=No! Wait! It too far! No! Wait, I said! Oh, you idiots!|name=Moblin leader|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oops! This job harder than I thought.|name=Moblin leader|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> Eventually, the monsters wear themselves out and, having accomplished nothing meaningful, Ganon teleports them back to the [[Underworld]] when he manages to free himself.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Uh-oh, somebody let the boss out.|name=Moblin leader|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref>
