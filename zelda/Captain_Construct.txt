The '''Captain Construct''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{TotK}}.
==Characteristics==
Captain Constructs are [[Construct|Constructs]] known for commanding [[Soldier Construct|Soldier Constructs]] in battle. These constructs are capable of [[Fuse|Fusing]] Objects and [[Zonai Device|Zonai Devices]] to their equipment, enhancing their combat capabilities. Like Soldier Constructs, they will immediately attack [[Link]] on sight if spotted.
