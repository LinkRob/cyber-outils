{{Term/Store|Daily Drills: Zora||HWAoC}} is a level 21 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Daily Drills: Zora|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Daily Drills: Zora}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} except for {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to capture an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} within five minutes. More {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} soldiers spawn when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches 15 KOs, and continue to spawn as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} defeats more {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} loses half of its health, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} spawns alongside two {{Plural|HWAoC|Zora Captain|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} defeats all three of them.
