{{Term/Store|Bug Rock||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bug Rock|Map|SSHD}}</ref> It is a small island inside the {{Term|SSHD|Thunderhead|link}}, inhabited by a large number of {{Plural|SSHD|Bug|link}}. It is also the location of {{Term|SSHD|Strich|link}}'s minigame {{Term|SSHD|Bug Heaven|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|SSHD|Bug Rock}} is a small island whose main inhabitants consist of every type of {{Term|SSHD|Bug|link}} found in {{SS|-}}. It features a wide range of habitats suitable for {{Plural|SSHD|Bug}}, including a ruined puddle-filled cellar, and a wide variety of flora. The minigame in the area becomes available after {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} defeats {{Term|SSHD|Bilocyte|link}}, freeing {{Term|SSHD|Levias|link}} from the parasite's control. He can visit the {{Term|SSHD|Bug Rock}} to find {{Term|SSHD|Strich|link}}, who says that he got lost while flying his {{Term|SSHD|Loftwing|link}} into a thick fog and ended up on the island. However, {{Term|SSHD|Strich}} notes that after looking around the island, he came to the conclusion that the island was a {{Term|SSHD|Bug}} paradise. The {{Term|SSHD|Bug}} enthusiast informs {{Term|SSHD|Link}} that he decided to use the island as a place to raise and catch bugs.<ref>{{Cite|Link? I wasn't expecting to see you here. I flew into this thick fog and ended up on this island. But once I got here, I looked around and realized...this is some kind of insect paradise! So I decided to go after my dream and raise a bunch of bugs here. Now I can spend my days catching all the bugs I want! You want to try catching some bugs? I'll release a few for you, so why don't you give it a shot?|Strich|SS}}</ref>

===Bug Heaven===
{{Term|SSHD|Strich}} offers to release some of the bugs for {{Term|SSHD|Link}} to catch and win as prizes in a minigame known as {{Term|SSHD|Bug Heaven|link}}. The game requires {{Term|SSHD|Link}} to have a {{Term|SSHD|Bug Net|link}}, and there are two levels that can be selected to play in the bug-catching competition. One level is the easier "Bug Beginner" and costs 20 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}}. The harder level, "Bug Wrangler," costs 50 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|I have come up with two levels for my bug-catching competition: an easy Bug Beginner level and a Bug Wrangler level for real bug masters like me. Since you aren't a bug lover, I'll charge you 20 Rupees for the Bug Beginner level and 50 for the Bug Wrangler level. Do you want in on this?|Strich|SS}}</ref>
