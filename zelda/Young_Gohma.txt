==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Gohma Larva}}<br/>Look out when it gets ready to pounce!
}}

Gohma Larvae appear [[Inside the Deku Tree]] in {{OoT}}. They hatch from [[Gohma Egg]]s found hanging on the ceiling. [[Link]] can prevent them from hatching by destroying the Eggs with the [[Fairy Slingshot]] while still on the ceiling. During the dungeon's boss fight against [[Gohma#Ocarina of Time|Queen Gohma]], she will hatch some of the Larvae to distract Link.

Gohma Larvae also appear in [[Dodongo's Cavern]] in {{MQ}}.

==={{OoS|-}}===
Gohma Larvae appear in the [[Dancing Dragon Dungeon]] during Gohma's boss fight in {{OoS}}. Once Link destroys Gohma's claw, she will occasionally fire Larvae at him. They can be defeated with the [[Sword]].

==={{TP|-}}===
Young Gohma appear in the [[Temple of Time (Dungeon)|Temple of Time]] in {{TP}}. They leap towards Link trying to bite him with their pincers. They can be defeated with the Sword or with the [[Hero's Bow]] from afar. Once the [[Dominion Rod]] is obtained, Link can crush the Young Gohma by using a statue's hammer.
