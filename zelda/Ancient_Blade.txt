== Location and Uses ==
Ancient Blade is an item used for weapon {{Term|TotK|Fuse|link|display=Fusing}}. It functions the same as {{Plural|TotK|Ancient Arrow|link}} from {{BotW}}.

When attached to a weapon, if the weapon strikes an enemy,{{Verify}} it will instantly defeat them in one hit by obliteration. However, it will also destroy ''everything'' the target was carrying with them — including {{Plural|TotK|Weapon|link}} and potential {{Term|TotK|material|link}} drops.

The item has a high affinity when fused with {{Plural|TotK|Arrow|link}}, as it's designed to function as an arrowhead. Despite this, it will still work the same with a regular weapon.{{Verify}} It can also be thrown like any material, and if it hits an enemy, it will destroy the enemy as well.

Ancient Blades may be found in a few selection of {{Plural|TotK|Treasure Chest|link}} around the world, primarily in {{Plural|TotK|Cave|link}}. It can also be bought from a unique {{Term|TotK|Steward Construct|link}} named "Mining Construct" in the {{Term|TotK|Spirit Temple|link}} for 50 {{Term|TotK|Zonaite|link}} each.

== Trivia ==
* Like the {{Plural|TotK|Ancient Arrow|link}}, it extends its blue glowing blade when deployed.
* Given that the Ancient Blade's design is of {{Term|Series|Sheikah|link}} origin, but reproduced by a {{Term|TotK|Zonai|link}} Steward Construct, it is unknown whether this iteration is related to the original Ancient Arrow design, or whether the Zonai had found a way to reverse-engineer the somewhat modern design.
