The {{Term/Store|Phalian Highlands||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Phalian Highlands'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Phalian Highlands}} are located in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}} sleeps at the center of the {{Term|BotW|Phalian Highlands|display=Highlands}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, it is replaced with a {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}}.

An {{Term|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} can be found along the eastern hillside.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found hiding southeast of the {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}}. He will appear if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} completes a circle of {{Plural|BotW|Rock|link}}.
