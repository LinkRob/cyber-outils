==Location and Uses==
==='''{{BotW|-}}'''===
{{Compendium/Extract|Electric Darner}}
{{Plural|BotW|Electric Darner}} can be found on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} and in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} also sells them at {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}. They can be used with {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make an {{Term|BotW|Electro Elixir|link}}, which will temporarily provide Shock Resistance to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> The {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} accepts {{Plural|BotW|Electric Darner}} as an ingredient for {{Term|BotW|Yellow Dye|link}}.

During the "{{Term|BotW|Little Sister's Big Request|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} finds out that {{Term|BotW|Gleema|link}} likes dragonflies, despite her sister's fear of them.<ref>{{Cite|I once saw pictures of a {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, a {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, and an {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}} in a book... But when I showed them to Jana, she said their eyes were creepy and got mad.|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Jana|link}} hears this, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to obtain an {{Term|BotW|Electric Darner}} and other dragonflies for {{Term|BotW|Gleema}}'s birthday on her behalf.<ref>{{Cite|Well, this is her birthday wish... I didn't have a choice. Bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}}, please.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> Upon delivery, however, {{Term|BotW|Jana}} will become disgusted by the dragonflies and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to give them to {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} for her.<ref>{{Cite|BAH! I CAN'T DO THIS! Please give them to her...just...give them to Gleema yourself!|Jana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Gleema}} will be excited about her dragonflies and rush to show {{Term|BotW|Jana}},<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Big Sis! Lookee, lookee! It's a real life {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}! And a {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}} and an {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}} too!! I'll bring 'em over so you can see!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref> which causes her sister to shriek in terror.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|HEY! Cut it out!! EEEEEEK!!}}|Jana|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, she will give him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Now then... I must thank you for catching those {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}. Here, take this.|Jana|BotW}}</ref>
