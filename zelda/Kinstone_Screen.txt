The {{Term/Store|Kinstone Screen|Kinstone Screens|Series, TMC}} is the location where {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can perform {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} with other characters in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, yes. That's it. Press {{Icon|TMC L}}! Then you can see the {{Color|TMC Blue|Kinstone screen}}.|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Screen}} is accessed by pressing the {{Term|TMC|L Button|link}} while facing a character with a thought bubble over their head, triggering a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}}.

On the left side of the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Screen}}, all of the {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} that {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} currently has are displayed in a wheel that can be rotated with the {{Term|TMC|Control Pad|link}}. On the right side, a moving sprite of the character with which {{Term|TMC|Link}} is trying to perform a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion}} is accompanied by the single {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} in their possession. Both {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s and the character's names are displayed on the bottom of the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Screen}}.

When the player selects a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} from those available in the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}}, the two {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|display=Pieces}} move toward each other in an attempt to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}}; if they do not match, they will strike against one another and return to their original positions, but if they do match, the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}} will be completed, and the game will transition away from the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Screen}} to view the effect of the successful {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}}.
