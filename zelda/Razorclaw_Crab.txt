==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Razorclaw Crab}}
Razorclaw Crabs can be found near water, usually along beaches in the [[Necluda Sea]] and [[East Necluda]]. They will scurry away if approached, but can be easily caught by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} when snuck up on. Eating one raw will restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to [[Link|Link's]] {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, but they can also be exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to make [[Blackened Crab]] or chilled by [[Ice]] to make [[Frozen Crab]]. Meals made in a [[Cooking Pot]] using Razorclaw Crabs will temporarily increase Link's attack damage.<ref name="Inventory"/>

When [[Cooking]], either a Razorclaw Crab or any other crab is required to make a [[Crab Omelet with Rice]], [[Crab Risotto]], [[Crab Stir-Fry]], and [[Salt-Grilled Crab]]. Any crab, a [[Sneaky River Snail]], or a [[Hearty Blueshell Snail]] is needed to make a [[Seafood Skewer]]. They also count as seafood when making [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Meunière]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]].
