'''Storm''' is an animal in [[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)]].{{Name Ref|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)}} 

==Biography==
{{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Storm}} helps Zelda leave {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Hyrule|link}}'s {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|North Palace|link}} when she attempts to leave {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Hyrule}} in order to keep the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Triforce|link}} of Wisdom from the reach of {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon|link}}.<ref>''Zelda said that the only way to keep the Triforce of Wisdom safe from Ganon is for her to take it far away and never return!'' - King Harkinian (''Valiant Comic Issue #1'')</ref> However, she is spotted and pursued by a group of {{Plural|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Daira|link}}. As the {{Plural|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Daira}} give chase, a {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Deeler|link}} drops from a {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Tree|link}} and spooks {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Storm}}.<ref>''A Deeler! Whoa, Storm!'' - Zelda (''Valiant Comic Issue #1'')</ref> In the process, Zelda is thrown from {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Storm}}'s saddle, and {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Storm}} proceeds to run off. 

It is not seen when {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Storm}} recovered, but Zelda is later seen riding him with {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link|link}} on the way to the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Harbor Town of Mido|link}}. It is assumed that the horse was either sold in {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Mido}}, or was given to others to care for as he does not accompany {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}} and Zelda for the remainder of their journey.
