==Characteristics==
Ropes are poisonous [[snake]]s that charge at any target posing a threat to their territory.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= It senses other creatures quickly and suddenly comes after them (and Link, for that matter!) once it has found them out. It hasn't got a lot of attacking power. |game= TLoZ |page= 35}}</ref><ref>{{Cite manual|quote= A poisonous snake that has made the labyrinths its home. |game= TLoZ |page= 35}}</ref> They are easily dispatched with most conventional weaponry like the [[Sword]] and [[Bow]]. Unlike most enemies throughout {{TLoZ|Series}}, {{Plural|Series|Rope}} have changed relatively little in attack pattern and appearance.

Some variations of Ropes do exist; [[Skullrope]] appear in {{ALttP}}, {{ALBW}}, and {{TFH}}, while [[Golden Rope]] appear in {{TMC}}.

==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Rope|A poisonous snake that has made the labyrinths its home. It senses other creatures quickly and suddenly comes after them (and Link, for that matter!) once it has found them out. This one hasn't got a lot of attacking power.}}
Ropes appear in red and flashing varieties in {{TLoZ}}. When defeated, they may drop either a [[Heart]], one [[Rupee]], five Rupees, or a [[Clock]].{{Note|If [[Link]] defeats 10 enemies without getting hit or teleporting with the [[Recorder]], then the 10th enemy defeated will drop five Rupees. However, if the 10th enemy is defeated with a [[Bomb]], then it will drop four Bombs. If Link defeats 16 enemies without getting hit or teleporting, then the 16th enemy will drop a [[Fairy]]. After the 16th enemy, Link must get hit and reset the counter to zero in order to achieve this effect again. Defeating [[Armos]], {{Plural|TLoZ|Like Like|link}}, [[Keese]], or [[Gel]] will not be counted.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= n.a. |published= n.d. |retrieved= August 17, 2017 |url= http://redcandle.us/The_Legend_of_Zelda#Forced_drops |title= Forced Drops |site= redcandle.us}}</ref>}}

==Other Appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}} TV Series===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:Ropes (Underworld Connections).png|thumb|right|Ropes, as seen in the animated series]]
Ropes appear in the "[[Underworld Connections]]" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. When [[Link]] opens a door in the [[Underworld]] to find a shard of the [[Triforce of Wisdom]], various Ropes lunge towards him. After Link uses a [[Zap]] on a few of them with his [[Crissword]], Princess Zelda tells him to stop, as [[Ganon]] will be able to discern their location.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Link! Stop zapping those Ropes! Ganon will know we're here!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> Link, who is entangled by two Ropes, is set free by Princess Zelda when she gets out a [[Boomerang]] and uses it to defeat them,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Oh, I suppose I better get you loose.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=8}}</ref> allowing her and Link to proceed through the Underworld, though not before Link picks up a [[Bomb]] that was dropped by one of the Rope.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{TLoZ|-}} (Valiant Comics)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Although Ropes do not appear directly in the [[Valiant Comics]], they are mentioned in the [[Day of the Triforce]] story. When Princess Zelda and [[Cassiopia]] arrive at the [[Water Town of Saria]], Cassiopia's husband comes and brings their son, [[Rus]], telling them that he has been bitten by a Rope when he was trying to escape the [[Lynel]] that had attacked him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=This is what I tried to tell you! The child was bitten by a rope snake while trying to escape the Lynels!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> It is explained that a Rope's bite is lethal and cannot be cured by normal healing;<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He is beyond by healing powers! He will not last beyond sundown!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> as such, it requires a [[Fairy]]'s magical powers to be properly cured.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I can go to the Magic Spring and bring one of the fairies back with me to cure him! Their power to heal is even greater than yours, Cassiopia!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{TCT|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Princess Zelda can encounter a Rope in {{TCT}} if she does not have the [[Large Brass Key]] necessary to open the [[Iron Door]]. Although she will try to attack it with her [[Zelda's Bow]], the Rope will swiftly attack her, leading the book to a [[Game Over]] as Princess Zelda screams for help.<Ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Zelda turns just as a huge snake-like neck rises up from a hole in the ground. 'A rope snake!' she cries. The giant serpent slithers forward, its cold eyes flashing and its great, forked tongue whipping out between its fangs. Zelda grabs for her bow, but before she can get it, the monstrous snake throws a coil around her arms. 'Help!' she screams.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=52}}</Ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}
