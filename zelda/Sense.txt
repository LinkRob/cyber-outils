==Overview==
When {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} transforms into {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}, he gains the use of greatly enhanced {{Plural|TPHD|Sense}}. This ability activates a special combination of enhanced visual and olfactory perception. Visual range is reduced, but Link is able to detect many otherwise hidden things. The Sense ability is notably similar to the power of the {{Term|Series|Lens of Truth|link}} from previous titles, though it can be used indefinitely and does not use up any resources such as the {{Term|Series|Magic Meter|link}}.

Wolf Link's enhanced {{Plural|TPHD|Sense}} have a number of vital applications throughout the game. The first use, taught by {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}} while in the {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town Sewers|link}},<ref name="hone" /> is to detect the {{Term|TPHD|Spirit|link}} forms of the people of {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}} trapped within the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight|link}}. Appearing as a floating greenish flame, these {{Plural|TPHD|Spirit}}' true forms can be seen by activating the {{Plural|TPHD|Sense}}, enabling {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} to listen to their thoughts. As {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} is unable to communicate directly with the {{Plural|TPHD|Spirit}}, this enables him to gain useful information from them about recent events. {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} must also use his {{Plural|TPHD|Sense}} to see and defeat certain enemies, such as {{Plural|TPHD|Ghoul Rat|link}} and {{Plural|TPHD|Poe|link}}.

Another application of the {{Term|TPHD|Sense}} ability is the detection of special patches of ground found all across {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule}}. These appear as patches of sparkling light where {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} can dig, usually unearthing items such as {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}} or {{Plural|TPHD|Heart|link}}. Some of the patches are larger, and can lead to different areas within dungeons, or to {{Plural|TPHD|Cave|link}}.
