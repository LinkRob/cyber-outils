{{Term/Store|Lava Drop|Lava Drops|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 129"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Lava Drop}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Ruins|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Palace Noir|link}}''', ''"Transport the orb quickly!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Lava Drop}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Lone Labyrinth|link}}''', ''"Guard the orb!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Lava Drop}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Lava Drop}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Grim Temple}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Lava Drop}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Lava Drop}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Fire Blazer|link}}.<ref name="E 129"/>
