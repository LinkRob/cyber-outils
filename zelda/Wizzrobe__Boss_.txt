==Characteristics==

In {{MM}}, {{Plural|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} are cruel spellcasters that resemble {{Wp|goblin}}-like old men who wear simple cloth and carry a staff.<ref name="MM3D Prima 40"/> One appears as the {{Term|MM3D|Mini-Boss|link}} of {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead Temple|link}}, another appears in the {{Term|MM3D|Ancient Castle of Ikana|link}}, and a third appears in the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|link}}. A fourth {{Term|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} also appears in the {{Term|MM3D|Secret Shrine|link}}.

{{Plural|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)|link}} are encountered in rooms with pads across the room, which they teleport between before eventually stopping to dance and cast an attack.<ref name="MM3D Prima 40"/> {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} must attack it while it is dancing before it can cast its spell. This is easiest with the {{Term|MM3D|Bow|link}}, especially in later {{Plural|MM3D|Dungeon|link}} where it emerges from a pad that is out of reach. After enough damage is dealt,{{How Many}} it creates illusionary copies of itself that rush around the room.<ref name="MM3D Prima 40"/> To defeat the {{Term|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must find the real one and attack it before it can strike back. As only the real {{Term|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} will attack, it can be found by looking for the one that is dancing. The real {{Term|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} also appears on the {{Term|MM3D|Map|link}}. {{Term|MM3D|Tatl|link}} can also target the real one if {{Term|MM3D|Link}} is close enough.

The {{Plural|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} that appear in {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead Temple}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Secret Shrine}} cast {{Term|MM3D|Ice|link}} magic, whereas the {{Plural|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Ancient Castle of Ikana}} and {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple}} wield {{Term|MM3D|Fire|link}} magic.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM3D|publisher= Prima|page= 285}}</ref> The battle in the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple}} also involves fiery rocks that fall from above. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can use the {{Term|MM3D|Mirror Shield|link}} to reflect their spells. Defeating the {{Term|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} in {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead Temple}} rewards {{Term|MM3D|Link}} with the {{Plural|MM3D|Fire Arrow|link}},<ref name="MM3D Prima 344"/> and defeating the one in {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple}} reveals a {{Term|MM3D|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|MM3D|Stray Fairy|link}}.<ref name="MM3D Prima 302"/> The {{Term|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Secret Shrine}} leaves a {{Term|MM3D|Treasure Chest}} with a {{Term|MM3D|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref name="MM3D Prima 311"/> Once it is defeated, along with the other three {{Plural|MM3D|Mini-Boss}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Secret Shrine}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} receives a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Ancient Castle of Ikana}} does not leave a {{Term|MM3D|Treasure Chest|link}} when beaten, however defeating it makes it possible to proceed to the roof, where it is possible to blast a hole with a {{Term|MM3D|Powder Keg|link}} to let sunlight in.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Wizzrobe}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= Get the '''Wizzrobe''' first, or there'll be no end to these attacks!

In {{TWW}}, a more powerful version of the regular Wizzrobe appears as the {{Term|TWWHD|Wind Temple|link}}'s Mini-Boss. It wears an orange cloak and an {{wp|Inca civilization|Incan}}-like crown. It has the power of summoning other Wizzrobes (although it does this only once at the start of the battle),<ref>{{Cite|Wizzrobe (Boss) - Habitat: Wind Temple - Magic: Summons - These high-level mages use the magic of summoning to aid them in battle. They are the only Wizzrobes that can summon other Wizzrobes, which makes them formidable.|Wizzrobe (Boss) figurine|TWW}}</ref> as well as other enemies such as {{Plural|TWWHD|Darknut|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Kargaroc|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Keese|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Morth|link}}, and {{Plural|TWWHD|Peahat|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 101}}</ref>

Although it has more health than its lesser summoning counterpart, this Wizzrobe can be defeated in a single hit if it's are frozen with an {{Term|TWWHD|Ice Arrow|link}} and then tossed at a wall, crushed with the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer|link}}, or shot with a [[Fire Arrow]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TWoG|-}}===
[[File:Wizzrobe WoG.png|left]]
In {{TWoG}}, a {{Term|TWoG|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} appears as the fourth {{Term|TWoG|Boss|link}}. He appears as a green-skinned wizard wearing a purple robe and is located in the {{Term|TWoG|Shutoy Lake|link}} tower, where he is holding {{Term|TWoG|Lady Alma|link}} captive.<ref>{{Cite|Oh dear, Wizzrobe has captured Lady Alma.|Impa|TWoG}}</ref> In defiance, {{Term|TWoG|Lady Alma}} says that she is not afraid of him, to which {{Term|TWoG|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} responds by casting a spell that buries her in stones.

{{Term|TWoG|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} casts magic spells at {{Term|TWoG|Zelda|link}}, and cannot be attacked by normal means. He can only be defeated by reflecting his disintegration spell back at him using the {{Term|TWoG|Reflecting Shield|link}}. Upon his defeat, he drops a {{Term|TWoG|Key|link}} that opens the door to the chamber where {{Term|TWoG|Lady Alma}} is imprisoned.

==={{BQ|-}}===
In {{BQ}}, {{Term|NL|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} resembles a giant bird with torn clothing and first appears as a {{Term|NL|Boss}} in {{Term|NL|Death Mountain|link}} Climb. It throws {{Plural|NL|Keese|link}} at the player, and then a ball. The swordsmen have to swipe their {{Plural|NL|Sword|link}} at the ball to volley it back. The ball will change color, which means it will go to a different swordsmen. Once the ball has been volleyed enough,{{How Many}} it deals a hit. The archer can also hit {{Term|NL|Wizzrobe (Boss)}} with {{Plural|NL|Arrow|link}} to deal damage.
{{Noncanon|End}}
