{{Term/Store|Knight's Sword|Knight's Swords|Series, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are {{Plural|HWDE|Item|link}} in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Knight's Sword|Smithy|HWDE}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|HWDE|Knight's Sword}} are used by {{Plural|HWDE|Soldier|link}} and {{Plural|HWDE|Hylian Captain|link}} of {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule|link}}. They come with a shield and are modified to be used by left-handed {{Plural|HWDE|Soldier}}.<ref>{{Cite|A sword-and-shield combo modified for use by left-handed warriors.|Check Combos|HWDE}}</ref> Once a {{Term|HWDE|Trainee|link}} reaches the status of {{Term|HWDE|Soldier}}, they are given a {{Term|HWDE|Knight's Sword}}. In the first {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}}, "{{Term|HWDE|The Armies of Ruin|link}}," a horde of {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy|link}} attacks {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Castle|link}} and the {{Plural|HWDE|Trainee}} are told to stay inside.<ref>{{Cite|A horde of monsters is marching toward Hyrule Castle!|Soldier|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So...I guess we just wait to hear what happened?|Trainee|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Link|link}}, a {{Term|HWDE|Trainee}} at the time, disobeys and takes a {{Term|HWDE|Knight's Sword}} to join the fight.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Link! What d'you think you're doing with that sword?!|Trainee|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 372 & 380}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Link}} then uses the {{Term|HWDE|Knight's Sword}} to fight until either unlocking the {{Term|HWDE|White Sword|link}} or obtaining the {{Term|HWDE|Master Sword|link}}.

{{Plural|HWDE|Knight's Sword}} are the base {{Term|HWDE|Weapon|link}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Hylian Sword|link}} archetype used by {{Term|HWDE|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 8}}</ref> They are unlocked from the start upon starting the "{{Term|HWDE|The Armies of Ruin}}" {{Term|HWDE|Scenario}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= '''Knight's Sword'''<br/>This is Link's starting weapon. |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 9}}</ref> {{Plural|HWDE|Knight's Sword}} have a base attack damage of 80, increasing by 10 percent for each star in the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}'s star rating;<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= Each added star adds an additional 10 percent attack damage to a weapon.|game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 8}}</ref> {{Plural|HWDE|Knight's Sword}} can have a range of 80, 88, 96, 104, 112, and 120 attack damage. They have a one {{Term|HWDE|Skill|link}} slot available (which can be increased when obtained while using a {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} with a "Slots+" {{Term|HWDE|Skill}}), and are {{Term|HWDE|Light|link}}-type {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}}, meaning that the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} will increasingly deal more damage while repeatedly attacking a group of {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}}.<ref>{{Cite|Light elemental attacks gradually become stronger as you make consecutive attacks on groups of enemies.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

{{Plural|HWDE|Knight's Sword}}, at their base tier of no stars and {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}}, are worth 1,000 {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee|link}}.<ref name="Sell 1000"/> Each star in the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}'s star rating increases the value of the {{Term|HWDE|Knight's Sword}} by 100 {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee}}, with a max of 1,500 {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee}} for a five-star {{Term|HWDE|Knight's Sword}}. Depending on the {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} that the {{Term|HWDE|Knight's Sword}} holds, the value will increase by a differing number of {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee}}.

{{Plural|HWDE|Knight's Sword}} start with a default of five {{Plural|HWDE|Regular Attack|link}}, three {{Plural|HWDE|Combo Attack|link}}, and one {{Term|HWDE|Strong Attack|link}}. Four more combinations can be unlocked in the {{Term|HWDE|Badge Market|link}} under the {{Term|HWDE|Kokiri Sword|link}} I, II, III, and IV {{Plural|HWDE|Badge|link}}. {{Term|HWDE|Kokiri Sword}} I unlocks two more {{Plural|HWDE|Regular Attack}};<ref>{{Cite|New Combo Attack for Hylian Sword:<br/>{{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}}|Badge Market|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Kokiri Sword}} II unlocks an extra {{Term|HWDE|Combo Attack}} by holding X (Y when use the Zelda Style) after any previous {{Term|HWDE|Combo Attack}};<ref>{{Cite|New spin attack for the Hylian Sword:<br/>Hold {{Icon|HWDE X}} after a Combo Attack.|Badge Market|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Kokiri Sword}} III unlocks one more {{Term|HWDE|Regular Attack}}, bringing the number to nine;<ref>{{Cite|New Combo Attack for Hylian Sword:<br/>{{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}}|Badge Market|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Kokiri Sword}} IV unlocks a new {{Term|HWDE|Combo Attack}} after the fourth {{Term|HWDE|Regular Attack}}.<ref>{{Cite|New Combo Attack for Hylian Sword:<br/>{{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE Y}} {{Icon|HWDE X}}|Badge Market|HWDE}}</ref> When using the {{Plural|HWDE|Knight's Sword}}, the series of {{Plural|HWDE|Regular Attack}} will produce a series of slashes in all different directions, slowly advancing forwards while doing so. The slashes hit in a 180-degree arc in the direction that {{Term|HWDE|Link}} is facing. A {{Term|HWDE|Spin Attack|link}} can be performed by using a {{Term|HWDE|Strong Attack}}. If the {{Term|HWDE|Strong Attack}} button is tapped, then a weak {{Term|HWDE|Spin Attack}} will be performed. If the button is held and then released, the {{Term|HWDE|Spin Attack}} performed will gain power and have a larger radius.<ref>{{Cite|Charge energy by holding down the {{Color|HWDE Green|Strong Attack button}}, and then release for a powerful Spin Attack!|Check Combos|HWDE}}</ref> Performing a {{Term|HWDE|Regular Attack}} and then a {{Term|HWDE|Combo Attack}} will have {{Term|HWDE|Link}} slash upwards, rising into the air. While in the air, three {{Plural|HWDE|Regular Attack}} can be performed before falling back to the ground. After the {{Plural|HWDE|Regular Attack}}, a {{Term|HWDE|Combo Attack}} can be used to smash back to the ground, creating a damaging shock wave. If the {{Term|HWDE|Combo Attack}} button is held, a small {{Term|HWDE|Spin Attack}} will be charged, launching {{Term|HWDE|Link}} forwards when used.

==Trivia==
