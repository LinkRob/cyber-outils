The {{Term/Store|Gorae Torr Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Gorae Torr Shrine}}<br/>Gorae Torr's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Gorae Torr's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr Shrine}} is located on top of {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock|link}} in northeastern {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}}. It is initially blocked by {{Term|BotW|Bayge|link}} of the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers|link}}, as the {{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr Shrine|display=Shrine}}'s {{Term|BotW|Travel Gate|link}} is considered sacred to the group.<ref>{{Cite|I AIN'T MOVIN' A MUSCLE, BROTHER! Not a single, well-defined muscle! This is a special platform! Sacred even! Only real Goron men, as decided by us three Goron Blood Brothers, are allowed.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> His refusal initiates "{{Term|BotW|The Gut Check Challenge|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will only move once {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has completed the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}, in which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must climb the pinnacle while collecting at least 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} in under three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|But I gotta say...ya do seem like a real man of action. Almost like you have a Goron air about ya. I like it! SO WHAT ARE YA WAITIN' FOR, BROTHER?! GET TRAININ'! Yeah that's it! {{Color|BotW Red|If you can clear our hard-core training program, I'll move outta your way}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has done so, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will move out of his way and allow him access to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|As promised, you are now allowed to stand on this sacred platform of Goron manliness. A real honor, I know.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he will be greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Gorae Torr. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Gorae Torr|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr Shrine}} is a single and straight-forward room that immediately leads to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr}}. A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in the center of the room contains a {{Term|BotW|Great Frostblade|link}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr}}, he is presented with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for arriving at the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule.}}|Gorae Torr|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fade away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Gorae Torr|BotW}}</ref>
