==Biography==
Doc Bandam is the eccentric man who owns the [[Chu Jelly Juice Shop]] on [[Windfall Island]] and a self proclaimed genius and true artist.<ref>{{Cite|That doesn't surprise me. Of course, not many things do. For I am a genius.|Doc Bandam|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But I digress, and the game is afoot! For when it comes to making potions, I am a true artist!|Doc Bandam|TWW}}</ref> He supplies the [[Great Sea]] with medicines derived from [[Chu Jelly]]. Doc was born on Windfall and frequently takes trips to [[Pawprint Isle]] to collect samples of Chu Jelly from the many [[Chuchu|ChuChu]]s that live there.<ref>{{Cite|This is an extremely rare color of Chu Jelly! ...Perhaps it indicates a new breed!? What a fascinating jelly development! I've never seen one of this particular hue on Pawprint Isle, a small atoll famous for its robust Chu population...|Doc Bandam|TWW}}</ref>

He loves his work, which he began ten years ago,<ref>{{Cite|It's been ten years since I began my now legendary studies of the Chu Jelly.|Doc Bandam|TWW}}</ref> and wants people to donate Chu Jelly to him so that he can research new [[potion]]s.<ref>{{Cite|If you get your hands on any Chu Jelly, please! I beg of you! Bring it to my shop!|Doc Bandam|TWW}}</ref> Every time [[Link]] gives him more Chu Jelly, he will give him a free sample of the new potion as long as he has en empty bottle: Red, Green, or Blue potion, depending on what type of jelly Link gives him.<ref name=five>{{Cite|For every five glops of Chu Jelly you bring me, I'll give you one free potion. Come back after you collect more of that wonderful, gooey, potion-making stuff!|Doc Bandam|TWW}}</ref> Even if he has already made that type of potion, he will still give Link a free sample. While Link only has to bring him five Chu Jellies in order for Doc to concoct a potion he has made before,<ref name=five/> he needs to bring him fifteen if he is to make a Green or Blue potion for the first time.

[[Hollo]] of the [[Forest Haven]] can also make Link Blue potions, but he requires only four [[Boko Baba Seed]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Even after the Korok ceremony, Hollo remains in the Forest Haven researching potion-making. He'll make a potion that replenishes both life energy and magic power for you if you bring him Boko Baba seeds.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref>
