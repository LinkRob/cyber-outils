{{Term/Store|Death Mountain Summit||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Famous">{{Cite|100 famous mountains of Hyrule: #1<br/>Death Mountain Summit|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Death Mountain Summit is the highest point of [[Death Mountain]]. Its location is marked by a signpost denoting it as one of [[Hyrule]]'s one hundred famous mountains.<ref name="Famous"/> After Link has defeated [[Fireblight Ganon]] and activated {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}'s Main Control Unit, [[Daruk]] operates Vah Rudania to climb up to the Summit and take aim at [[Hyrule Castle]]. From there on, the Summit becomes a new Travel point via Vah Rudania's icon on the map. Link will not be able to approach Vah Rudania, however, due to it being too dangerous.

[[Bohrin]] states that the [[Goron]]s used to mine at the Summit, but were forced to relocate to the [[Southern Mine]] when Vah Rudania began launching [[Magma Bomb]]s.<ref>{{Cite|We used to mine by {{Color|BotW Blue|the summit}}, but with that {{Color|BotW Blue|lizard monster}} launchin' those {{Color|BotW Blue|magma bombs}} at everything...|Bohrin|BotW}}</ref>
