==Overview==
Walton, a {{Term|TotK|Korok|link}} above the {{Term|TotK|Deku Tree|link}}, is sad that nobody wants to play his treasure hunt game, thinking it could be difficult. {{Term|TotK|Link|link}}, who is there, plays his game to help test it out for him. Walton wants him to found two items: A {{Term|TotK|Forest Dweller's Sword|link}} and a {{Term|TotK|Forest Dweller's Spear|link}}. He hid them somewhere atop of the Deku Tree.

Link finds the two items on top of the tree and brings them back to Walton. Impressed, he wants him to try the second part of the treasure hunt and find a {{Term|TotK|Forest Dweller's Shield|link}}, hidden at the bottom (but not inside) of the Deku Tree. After finding the shield, Link shows it to Walton. He thanked Link for his help and opinion on the treasure hunt and gave him a {{Term|TotK|Forest Dweller's Bow|link}}, and the items he found, as rewards.

==Coordinates==
The items are located at these coordinates:
* 417, 2173, 242: Forest Dweller's Sword
* 406, 2198, 229: Forest Dweller's Spear
* 422, 2157, 158: Forest Dweller's Shield
