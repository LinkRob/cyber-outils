==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Wildberry}}
{{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} are found growing on bushes in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}},<ref name="BotW Inventory"/> or they can be dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} when defeated. They can be eaten raw to restore half of a {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link|display=Heart}}. If a {{Term|BotW|Wildberry}} is roasted over a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} or dropped in a hot location like {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}, it becomes a {{Term|BotW|Roasted Wildberry|link}}, which doubles its healing properties to heal a full {{Term|BotW|Heart}} of the {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge}}. At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} are one of the ingredients that can make {{Term|BotW|Peach Dye|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} are necessary to make a {{Term|BotW|Wildberry Crepe|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Either a {{Term|BotW|Wildberry}} or an {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}} is needed to make {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake|link}}. Similar to other fruits, {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} can be used to make {{Term|BotW|Fruit Pie|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake}}, {{Term|BotW|Honeyed Fruits|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Simmered Fruit|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Steamed Fruit|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fruit and Mushroom Mix|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Copious Simmered Fruit|link}}.

As part of the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Mystery Polluter|link}}," {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must give {{Term|BotW|Calyban|link}} 10 {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} in order to get her to stop polluting the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} trough in {{Term|BotW|Dalia|link}}'s garden.<ref>{{Cite|Just once...I'd like to eat a {{Color|BotW Blue|wildberry}}. You bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|wildberry}}, and I can hold off on eating so many melons.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Now that I think on it, there's barely a point if you only bring me one. Let's see... Yes, I'll need at least {{Color|BotW Red|10}}...|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} gives her the {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}}, {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} will help {{Term|BotW|Dalia|link}} start a {{Term|BotW|Wildberry}} garden in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, which completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|But...now I feel bad for the little vai. Now that my craving's been satisfied, I realize just how horribly selfish I've been... Shame on me. I'm going to go help clean up the trash!
