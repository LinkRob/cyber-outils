The {{Term/Store|Creation of the World||Series}},<ref name="E 11">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 11}}</ref> also known as the '''Creation Myth''',<ref name="E 12">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 12}}</ref> is an in-universe myth within {{TLoZ|Series}} about how the land of Hyrule was created by the three [[Golden Goddess|Golden Goddesses]]. The original version of this myth appears in the {{aLttP}} instruction manual.<ref name="ALttP Manual">{{Cite manual|quote=After the gods had finished their work, they left the world, but not before creating a symbol of their strength, a golden triangle known as the Triforce.|game=ALttP|page=3}}</ref> This myth would be retold in {{OoT}}, and again in an abbreviated form in {{TP}}, as well as expanded in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom]]''. 

==Overview==
During the {{Term|Series|Creation of the World}}, the three {{Plural|Series|Golden Goddess|link}} created the land of Hyrule (not to be confused with the {{Term|Series|Hyrule|link}}, which exists within the land of Hyrule).<ref name="E 11" /> {{Term|Series|Din (Goddess)|link}} created the land, {{Term|Series|Nayru (Goddess)|link}} created order, and {{Term|Series|Farore (Goddess)|link}} created all life.<ref name="E 12" /> The Goddesses then departed for the heavens. The {{Term|Series|Triforce|link}} was formed at the point where the goddesses left the world, and its resting place became the {{Term|Series|Sacred Realm|link}}.<ref name="ALttP Manual" /><ref>{{Cite|The three great goddesses, their labors completed, departed for the heavens. And golden sacred triangles remained at the point where the goddesses left the world. Since then, the sacred triangles have become the basis of our world's providence. And, the resting place of the triangles has become the Sacred Realm.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The lands where the goddesses descended came to be known as the Sacred Realm.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TPHD}}</ref>

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Great Deku Tree|link}} recounts the full "{{Term|Series|Creation of the World}}" shortly before his death, after {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} defeats {{Term|OoT3D|Gohma|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, {{Term|TPHD|Lanayru (Spirit)|link}} tells {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} a shortened version along with the {{Term|Series|Banishment of the Twili|link}}, after {{Term|TPHD|Link}} collects all the {{Plural|TPHD|Tear of Light|link}} in the {{Term|TPHD|Lanayru (Region)|link|display= Lanayru region}}

==={{EoW|-}}===
In {{EoW}}, the events of the creation of the world myth are revisited where it was revealed that the Golden Goddesses essentially created  Hyrule as a prison to seal away the primordial cosmic entity known as [[Null]] due to its presence threatening to erase anything that attempted to form.
