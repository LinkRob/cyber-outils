'''Toon Link''' is a short-hand moniker for [[Link|Link's]] occasional "cartoon" appearance originating from {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 410}}</ref> The name first appeared in {{SSBB}}. 

==Biography==
Toon Link is a composite character used to represent multiple incarnations of Link in crossover works featuring elements from {{TLoZ|Series}}. Toon Link is primarily based on Link from {{TWW|-}}, though elements from other incarnations of Link may also appear.

==={{SSBB|-}}===
In {{SSBB|-}}, Toon Link is a secret character, replacing [[Young Link]] from the previous series entry, that can be unlocked by clearing [[The Subspace Emissary]] and clearing {{ssb|Classic Mode}} with Link on any difficulty, by going through the hidden door in {{ssb|The Forest}} stage after clearing The Subspace Emissary, or by participating in 400 Brawls. His stage is the [[Tetra's Ship#Super Smash Bros. Brawl|Pirate Ship]] from {{TWW|-}}. He is very similar to his older counterpart, even having the [[Triforce Slash]] as his [[Final Smash]].

Most of Toon Link's special moves are the same as his adult counterpart, with a few differences. His [[Hookshot]], which serves as his {{ssb|grab move}} and can be used as a {{ssb|tether recovery}}, has a shorter range. His {{ssb|neutral special move}}, the [[Hero's Bow]], also does not travel as far as adult Link's and travels at a slower speed, but it has a higher rate of fire. Toon Link's {{ssb|side special move}} is a standard [[Boomerang]], and his {{ssb|up special}} as well as his {{ssb|recovery move}} is the [[Spin Attack]]. The latter one varies with adult Link's in that it can trap any nearby foes in a cyclone of slashes. His {{ssb|neutral aerial}} attack is a quick back-and-forth sword slash as opposed to a jumping kick, while his {{ssb|Forward aerial|forward}} and {{ssb|Back aerial|back aerials}} strike only once instead of twice. His {{ssb|down aerial}} attack is a version of the [[Down Thrust]] that stalls briefly in midair and falls straight downward, as opposed to adult Link's which retains his jumping momentum. The first strike of his {{ssb|forward smash}} attack barely launches the opponent, though this allows him to easily combo into the second strike—a trait later adopted by Link in the [[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U|sequel]]—and his {{ssb|up smash}} strikes only once instead of three times. He also has the ability to wall-jump.

Toon Link has three alternate colors that resemble the [[Links]] from {{FS|-}}; red, blue, and purple. He also has an alternate color that resembles Link from {{TLoZ}}, and one with darker colors and red eyes that resemble [[Dark Link]]. His {{ssb|victory pose}} will either depict him conducting a song with the [[Wind Waker (Item)]], chasing and catching a [[Pig]], or chasing a Pig but tripping and falling.

==={{HW|-}}===
Toon Link appears a playable [[Warrior]] in {{HW}}. He is a speedy fighter who can generate wind-based attacks with his [[Light Sword]] Weapon or sand-based attacks with his [[Sand Wand]] Weapon. Toon Link can be unlocked with his Light Sword Weapon in {{HW|-}} if the Legends Character Pack DLC is purchased whereas he is unlocked in [[Adventure Mode]] in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}}, as a Reward for Battle Victory in [[Adventure Map/Hyrule Field (Hyrule Warriors Legends)#Battle G9|Battle G9]] on the [[Adventure Map]] in {{HW|L|-}} and in [[Great Sea Map/Forsaken Fortress (Hyrule Warriors: Definitive Edition)#Battle C10|Battle C10]] on the [[Great Sea Map]] in {{HW|DE|-}}. In {{HW|-}}, it is possible to unlock Toon Link with the Sand Wand instead of the Light Sword if the {{PH|-}} & {{ST|-}} Pack DLC is purchased before the Legends Character Pack.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Small|If you have not previously purchased the Legends Character Pack or used the download code provided with Hyrule Warriors: Legends, then you will download a version of Toon Link who will only be able to use this new weapon.<br/><br/>If you purchase the Legends Character Pack or use the code after downloading this DLC Pack, then Toon Link will also be able to use his Hero's Sword weapon.}}|author= Nintendo UK|published= n.d.|retrieved= November 3, 2018|url= https://www.nintendo.co.uk/Games/Nintendo-3DS/Hyrule-Warriors-Legends-1025970.html#Downloadable_content|title= Hyrule Warriors: Legends|site= Nintendo UK|type= }}</ref> Much like [[Link]] and [[Young Link]], Toon Link does not speak. However, instead of having [[Proxi]] speak for him on his behalf, his younger sister [[Aryll]] speaks for him using the [[Pirate's Charm]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Koei Tecmo|published= January 15, 2016|retrieved= January 18, 2016|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kuzx7OKR40Y&feature=youtu.be&t=6m9s|title= ~やりこみ要素紹介編~『ゼルダ無双 ハイラルオールスターズ』妖精プロクシィと一緒にゲームプレイ！|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref> Despite being featured as one of the additional Warriors alongside the other {{TWW|-}} characters, Toon Link never appears in [[Legend Mode]].

One of Toon Link's moves is flipping in the air and over some enemies as if he has the [[Roc's Feather]]. While in the air, Toon Link can attack enemies with the [[Deku Leaf]].

In the {{PH|-}} & {{ST|-}} DLC pack, he gains an additional weapon in the form of the Sand Wand from {{ST}}. In addition to tossing sand cubes at his enemies, he also rides the [[Spirit Train]] for many of his Combo attacks.

When defeated, Toon Link may drop the [[Pirate's Charm]] and the Island Outfit as his Silver and Gold [[Material]], respectively.

==={{SSB4|-}}===
Toon Link reappears as a Fighter in {{SSB4}}. His design is more brightly-colored but significantly less detailed than his {{SSBB|-}} appearance, though it still has greater detail than his cel-shaded appearance in {{TWW|-}}. He gains two new alternate colors based on Link's appearances in {{ALttP}} and {{ALBW}}. His move set features only minor changes from {{SSBB|-}}; most notably an increased Hookshot range, Bombs no longer damaging himself when hitting an opponent at close range, and his Down Thrust passing through opponents rather than bouncing off them.

With the introduction of Custom Moves, all of Toon Link's special attacks can be switched out for alternate versions that mainly feature trade-offs between range and power. His Hero's Bow can be modified to shoot [[Fire Arrow]], which travel a shorter distance but leave a fire burning for several seconds where they land, or Piercing Arrows, which travel in a straight path and can pass through multiple opponents but deal less damage. The Boomerang can become the Floating Boomerang, which has a larger hit radius and slows down upon its return, or the High-Speed Boomerang, which travels faster and passes through opponents, but has a smaller hit radius and deals less damage. The Bomb can become the smaller Time Bomb, which only explodes after a two-second fuse and can launch opponents much further despite dealing less damage, or the larger Short-Fused Bomb, which deals greater damage but explodes almost immediately after pulling it out. The alternate versions of the Spin Attack are the Sliding Spin Attack, which mostly travels horizontally but does not trap the opponent with multiple hits, and the Flying Spin Attack, which covers less vertical distance but finishes with a strong upward slash, regardless of whether the attack is performed on the ground or in midair.

Another cartoon-styled Link, wearing the [[Engineer's Clothes]], makes a background appearance as the train conductor in the [[Spirit Train]] stage. If any players battle as Toon Link on this stage, the conductor will be replaced by [[Alfonzo]], suggesting that it may actually be the same Toon Link.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
Toon Link reappears as a [[Fighter]] in {{SSBU}}. His move set features several minor changes {{SSB4|-}}, as well as some more notable changes, such as his dash attack receiving greatly increased speed and range, but his forward smash being slower and reduced to only a single hit. His Spin Attack is similarly faster and more powerful, while his Bombs can be more easily deflected by other projectiles.

Toon Link also appears as a [[Fighter Spirit]] that can be obtained by either clearing "[[The Teamwork of Courage]]", Toon Link's [[Classic Mode]] Route, or by purchasing it from the [[Shop#Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|Shop]] for G 300.

==Trivia==
*In the {{SSB|-}} series, Toon Link's eyebrows cannot be seen through his hair, as they can in the gameplay or official artwork of every other cel-shaded Link appearance.
*Toon Link's [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|Trophy]] in the North American version of {{SSB4|3DS|-}} contains an error. Its description refers to "''The Legend of Zelda: Wind Waker''" without the second "''The''".<ref>{{Cite|This cartoonish version of Link is how he appeared in ''The Legend of Zelda: Wind Waker'' and a few other titles.|Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|SSBfWU}}</ref>
