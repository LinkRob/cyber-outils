{{Term/Store|Ordon Woods||Series, TPHD}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ordon Woods|Map|TPHD}}</ref>

==Features==
{{Term|TPHD|Ordon Woods}} is a small forest situated just north of {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}}, containing the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Spring|link}} which serves as the home of the {{Term|TPHD|Spirit of Light|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Ordona (Spirit)|link}}. North of the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Spring}} is a rope bridge, spanning across a large gorge, connecting the {{Term|TPHD|Ordona Province|link}} to the {{Term|TPHD|Faron Province|link}}, thus leading to the much larger {{Term|TPHD|Faron Woods|link}}. With the exception of the {{Term|TPHD|Bulblin|link}} attack, the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Woods}} is notable in that it is one of the few non-village places in the game that is free of {{Plural|TPHD|Enemy}}. 

Despite it being a small location, the woods serve an important purpose in the game since it is here that {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} learns about the four {{Plural|TPHD|Spirit of Light|link}} that guard {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}} as well as {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}}'s intentions to rule the world.<ref>{{Cite|[...]The blight will not stop with Hyrule. Before long, the entire world of light will fall into the hands of the king who rules the twilight. To save this land from the king of twilight, the lost light must be recovered. The three light spirits who have lost their light must be revived. [...] Unless... If you were to return to Faron Woods where you were first transformed... If you were to revive the light spirit... There, by the power of the light spirit, you may find the means to regain your other state of being...|Ordona (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> Moreover, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} also learns the second {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill|link}} in the game here: the {{Term|TPHD|Shield Attack|link}}.
