{{Term/Store|Mystery Extract|Mystery Extracts|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 132"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Mystery Extract}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Ruins|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Illusory Mansion|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Mystery Extract}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Illusory Mansion}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Mystery Extract}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Palace Noir|link}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Mystery Extract}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Lone Labyrinth|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Mystery Extract}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Mystery Extract}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Fire Blazer|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Serpent's Toga|link}}.<ref name="E 132"/>
