'''Roperi''' is a character in the {{OoA|Himekawa}}. 

==Biography==
Roperi is first shown when Sir Raven escorts [[Link]] to a [[Hidden Village#Oracle of Ages (Himekawa)|Hidden Village]] behind a graveyard. Roperi is very excited because the seed Raven gave her sprouted.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The seed Raven gave me sprouted!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 71}}</ref> She goes to tell Raven this,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I've got to tell Raven. He'll be so happy!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 71}}</ref> but is not able to because he needs to check the fields and asks her to watch Link.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... I need to go look at the fields. Roperi, would you look after him?|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 72}}</ref> Despite Link's efforts to befriend her, she is upset at the hero for ruining her chance to give Raven her big news.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Who're you smiling at? You ruined my telling Raven about the sprout!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 72}}</ref> It is when Link asks about Sir Raven that she reveals how much she admires the knight.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... I've never seen a knight like him... he's an absolute model knight.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 73}}</ref> Roperi's information on Raven is what leads Link to realize that Sir Raven is his ancestor.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Model knight?! NOW I REMEMBER!... he's my ancestor?!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 73}}</ref> Later on, she is seen sitting alone on a stone stairway holding the sprout. Link greets her,<ref name="Roperi"/> but Roperi is still annoyed that Link is with Raven.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Raven! Oh, he's still with that guy...|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 80}}</ref> Trying to avoid him, she trips and her seed pot spills. As she blames Link and cries,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Don't touch it! This is your fault!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 80}}</ref> Link quickly places the sprout back in the pot. Sir Raven then compliments her for taking care of the plant so well.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Your plant's got strong roots... like you, Roperi! Good job!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 81}}</ref> Roperi comes with Link and Raven to observe the Black Tower. As the two knights study the Tower, Roperi admires her sprout, claiming that it is taller than the Tower.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= My tree sprout is taller than the tower!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 87}}</ref> When Raven announces his plans to quell the fighting at the Tower, she pleads to go with him to make sure that her {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Jacobi|link|display=father}} is well,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I... I want to go, too! I'm worried about my dad...|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 89}}</ref> although Raven tells her to stay behind because her sprout will need to be replanted.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Roperi, you must wait here. Your sprout will need to be re-potted soon.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 89}}</ref> 

While Sir Raven is at the Black Tower, Roperi stares at her plant.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Roperi, are you still staring at that plant?|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 118}}</ref> She shows [[Roperi's Mother|her mother]] the bud it sprouted<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Look, Mom!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 118}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Oh, there's a bud!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 118}}</ref> and wishes her father were there to see it bloom.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I hope Dad comes home in time to see the flower bloom.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 118}}</ref> Roperi's Mother says Link and Raven will help him and to believe in them and wait.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link and Raven are sure to help him. Believe in them, and wait.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 118}}</ref>

Sometime later, Raven, Link, and [[Ralph]] enter the Hidden Village. Ralph has been possessed by [[Veran]]. Roperi notices that something is wrong with him,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link, who is that? I don't like him. He's scary.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 122}}</ref> but Link brushes off her concern.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Who? Ralph? Don't worry! He may be stupid, but he's all right.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 122}}</ref> After Veran uses Ralph to burn Sir Raven's [[Mystery Seed]]s, her fatal weakness, she sends [[Ramrock]] to destroy the Village.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Rise, Ramrock! Veran, the Sorceress of Shadows, commands it!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 127}}</ref> In an attempt to save the crops, Roperi runs in front of the monster, yelling at it to stop.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Stop! Stay off our field! Don't step on it!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 129}}</ref> Before Roperi can be harmed, Link destroys the beast. Roperi hugs Link gratefully, calling him a tiny hero.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= A tiny hero!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 130}}</ref> Even with the monster gone, Queen Ambi's soldiers attack the villagers. Though he is wounded, Sir Raven protects the villagers, until archers threaten to shoot them all,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Surrender or we'll turn you into pincushions!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 136}}</ref> including Roperi. Raven surrenders and is captured. Roperi bursts into tears, watching as Link, Ralph, and [[Puini]] rush to Raven's rescue. 

When Raven has been rescued, he, Ralph, Link, and [[Nayru]] find Roperi hiding in a cave with the other villagers. She explains that the rest of the villagers were taken to the Tower.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= They captured everyone and took them to the tower!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 153}}</ref> Nayru reveals that Roperi's sprout is actually a young [[Mystery Tree]],<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link, this is a young Mystery Seed Tree...|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 154}}</ref> their only hope to defeat Veran. Roperi stays by Nayru as she uses the [[Harp of Ages]] to speed the sprout's growth. Once it produces a Mystery Seed, Link and Raven use it against Veran. After the following battles, Roperi is seen finally reuniting with her father. As Link readies to return to modern day [[Labrynna]], Roperi asks him to not leave.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link, don't go!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 188}}</ref> Link explains that she does not need him anymore, because her family is reunited.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Roperi, you got your father back. You don't need me...when you've got your whole family.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 188}}</ref> Again crying, Roperi thanks Link,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I guess... thank you, Link!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 188}}</ref> kissing him on the cheek.
