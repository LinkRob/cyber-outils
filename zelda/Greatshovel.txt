{{Term/Store|Greatshovel|Greatshovels|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Greatshovel}}<br/>Deals {{Color|CoH Red|1 damage}} to three adjacent enemies ahead of you and digs.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Greatshovel}} are {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} that can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}. They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Broadsword|link}} by dealing damage to three enemies ahead of Cadence, but with the addition of digging up to three tiles at once. They can be upgraded with various materials at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} in exchange for {{Plural|CoH|Diamond|link}}.
