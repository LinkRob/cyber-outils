{{Term/Store|Calamity Strikes||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
Before the {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda|link}} gets ready to go to {{Term|HWAoC|Mount Lanayru|link}} on her 17th birthday so that her power to seal {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|link}} would awaken.<ref>{{Cite|All of the countermeasures to Calamity Ganon were in place—except one. The princess had not yet awakened her sealing power. In hopes of preventing her kingdom's destruction, Zelda resolved to train at the Spring of Wisdom. Souls younger than 17 years were forbidden from setting foot on Mount Lanayru, where the Spring of Wisdom lay, as they lacked the spring's namesake. Zelda made plans to ascend as soon as she was of age.|N/A|HWAoC}}</ref> However, before they can depart, {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon}} awakens.<ref>{{Cite|Well then, we seem to have been taken by surprise.|Urbosa|HWAoC}}</ref>

{{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} realizes that her father, {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam|link}}, is in {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Castle|link}}, and the party goes to rescue him.<ref>{{Cite|Father will still be within the sanctum. Quickly! There's not a moment to lose!|Zelda|HWAoC}}</ref> While running towards the castle, {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|link}} captures the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} and sends two {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Stalker|link}} after the party.<ref>{{Cite|Calamity Ganon has taken control of the Guardians?!|Zelda|HWAoC}}</ref> After reaching the {{Term|HWAoC|Sanctum|link}}, they find a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Stalker}} attacking a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}}. Defeating the {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}}, two more {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Stalker}} drop from the ceiling. After again defeating these {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}}, they ask the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} where {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} went; the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} explains that he fled through the back entrance.<ref>{{Cite|The king fled through the back entrance. We were cut off from the others amid the chaos.|Hylian Captain|HWAoC}}</ref> They follow him through the entrance in hopes of coming together.

After beating several monsters, they find {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}}. However, he then gets surrounded by three {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Stalker}}, and tells {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} to leave with {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}. {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} does as he is ordered, while {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} keeps telling {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} to let go. Three {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} lasers could be heard in the distance, telling the party that {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} was killed.
