The {{Term/Store|Gear of the Protector|Gear of the Protector|Series, HWAoC|plural}} is a {{Term|Series|Weapon|link}} used by {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gear of the Protector}}<br/>A gear that can be incorporated into Terrako's workings. No detailed information is available, but is is clear that the part contains immeasurable energy.|Weapons|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==

The Gear of the Protector is acquired whenever a {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} or a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} is completed with Terrako as one of the characters participating in them. It can also be acquired in the {{Plural|HWAoC|Chest|link}} of when a strong foe is defeated or when an outpost is captured, when Terrako participates in the scenario or challenge. Gears of the Protector can be used to fuse into other weapons used by {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako|link}} to form stronger ones.
