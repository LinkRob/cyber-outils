[[File:Magician Contest (The Ringer).png|thumb|200px|right|The Magician's Contest]]The '''Magician's Contest''' is an event featured in "[[The Ringer]]" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}.<ref name=judge>{{Cite Episode|quote=The Triforce must be guarded, and I'm already late to judge the magician's contest.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> Taking place inside the [[North Castle]], it is being judged by [[Princess Zelda]] and is only meant for amateur magicians.<ref name=judge/><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Are you an amatuer magician then?|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref>

At the start of the contest, an old wizard shows the princess a spell that he uses in his garden, which makes a tomatoe grow instantly;<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=And this is a spell I use in my garden, your Highness. Watch.|name=Old Wizard|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> however, the tomatoe grows bigger until it explodes all over the wizard and Zelda. At that moment, [[Ganon]] arrives, who had disguised himself to enter the contest and cause a distraction to retrieve the [[Triforce of Wisdom]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Obviously, if I want the Triforce of Wisdom, I'm going to have to get it myself. There is a contests of amateur magicians in Hyrule today. I shall enter that contest!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=13}}</ref> When Zelda asks him if he is an amateur magician, the evil wizard answers by making a bird appear out of his hands, which the princess approves of, but asks that he wait his turn.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Fine, wait your turn.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> Zelda continues to judge the contest while Ganon makes the bird turn into an [[Ache]] and orders it to go see who is guarding the Triforce of Wisdom.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Quickly, fly to the tower if anyone guards the Triforce of Wisdom.|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> Meanwhile, another magician shows Zelda the spell that removes the stinkiness from his socks.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=This is my best spell, your Highness, it removes the stinkiness from my dirty socks.|name=Amateur Magician|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> The Ache eventually returns and informs Ganon that [[Link]] is guarding the Triforce,  and when one of the magicians makes a lizard appear on Zelda's hand, Ganon uses his magic to turn the lizard into a [[Dragon (Animated Series)|fire-breathing dragon]] that promptly turns towards the princess, thus permanently ending the contest for the rest of the episode.
