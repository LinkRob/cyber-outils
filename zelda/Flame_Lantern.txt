The {{Term/Store|Flame Lantern||Series, TMC}} is an item in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|TMC Red|Flame Lantern}}! This handy item lights up the night! Press the button to turn it on or off.|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern}} is found in the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} defeats the {{Term|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern}} can be activated (and deactivated) by pressing the button to which it is assigned; it will immediately deactivate if {{Term|TMC|Link}} falls into a body of water.

When activated, it lights up a moderate area around {{Term|TMC|Link}}, allowing him to see in pitch-black rooms. If the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern}} makes contact with small or medium-sized blocks of {{Term|TMC|Ice|link}}, it will cause them to melt. Similarly, {{Term|TMC|Fire|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern}} will burn away webs and {{Term|TMC|Grass|link}}; setting a patch of {{Term|TMC|Grass}} alight will cause {{Term|TMC|Fire}} to spread to each adjacent patch (excluding diagonals). Destroying {{Term|TMC|Grass}} in this manner prevents any {{Plural|TMC|Item|link}} from dropping, as opposed to cutting it down with a {{Term|TMC|Sword|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern}} can also be used to transfer Fire to {{Plural|TMC|Torch|link}}.

When used on {{Plural|TMC|Gibdo|link}}, it will burn their bandages and turn them into {{Plural|TMC|Stalfos|link}}. All types of {{Plural|TMC|Wizzrobe|link}} will instantly die upon contact with the flames from the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern}}. {{Plural|TMC|Spear Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Bow Moblin|link}} also take damage from the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern}}, and it is necessary to defeat the {{Term|TMC|Big Octorok|link}}, whose flower-tipped tail is susceptible to {{Term|TMC|Fire}}.
