{{Term/Store|Mipha's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 6 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mipha's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Mipha's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Luminous Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Bass|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}}, although several {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} spawn as {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} enters certain parts of the field. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated. 

A {{Term|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}} can also be found in an isolated part of the map.
