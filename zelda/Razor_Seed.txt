{{Term/Store|Razor Seed|Razor Seeds|Series, FS|plural}} are {{Plural|FS|Item|link}} in {{FS}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|FS|Razor Seeds|These powerful seeds increase your attack powers when you collect them.}}
Razor Seeds can be found by cutting [[Grass]]. When picked up, Razor Seeds will double [[Link]]'s attack power until the end of the current Stage.<ref>{{Cite|The mystical seeds you collect will boost your power by up to two levels. {{Color|FS Orange|Razor Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Razor Seed}} add sword power. {{Color|FS Orange|Armor Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Armor Seed}} add defense. {{Color|FS Orange|Pegasus Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Pegasus Seed}} add speed.|Stray Fairy|FS}}</ref> Only two Razor Seeds per [[Links|Link]] can be active at a time.
