==Characteristics==
Bomb Bokoblins appear as enemies in the [[Future World]] during the events of [[Cadence of Hyrule: Crypt of the NecroDancer feat. The Legend of Zelda]]'s [[Symphony of the Mask]]. They carry around [[Bomb]]s which can be dropped when attacked by the player, after which they act like normal Bokoblins, though the Bomb can hurt the player as well as nearby enemies. When defeated, Bomb Bokoblins themselves drop Bombs, which can be useful during the [[King Dobongo]] boss fight in the [[Gerudo Arena]].
