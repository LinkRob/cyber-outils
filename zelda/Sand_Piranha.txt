'''Sand Piranha''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Sand Piranha|Vigorous and quick, this fishlike creature swims through sand with ease. It cannot survive in water, however.<ref name="Sand Piranha">{{Cite|Vigorous and quick, this fishlike creature swims through sand with ease. It cannot survive in water, however.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Sand Piranhas are the desert variant of the standard [[Piranha]], found in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Sand Piranha [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of two.
