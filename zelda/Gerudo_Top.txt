The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Top||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gerudo Top}}<br/>Standard Gerudo garb popular for its open, breathable design and for the traditional Gerudo markings that adorn it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gerudo Top}}<br/>Standard Gerudo garb popular for its open, breathable design and for the traditional Gerudo markings that adorn it.|Allies|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Top}} can be acquired from {{Term|BotW|Vilia|link}} on top of the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}}, who sells the top along with the rest of the set for 600 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} as part of the {{Term|BotW|Forbidden City Entry|link}} {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}. It can also be purchased by itself at {{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} for 180 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}. It provides {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with 3 defense, and if the entire {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} is worn, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have level 1 [[Heat Resistance]], even though the pieces of the set cannot be upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
