==Location and uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT Deku Seeds obtained.jpg|thumb|left|Link obtaining Deku Seeds for the first time in {{OoT|-}}]]
In {{OoT}}, Deku Seeds are used as ammunition for the [[Fairy Slingshot]].<ref>{{Cite|These are small, hard seeds that you can use as bullets for your Slingshot.|N/A|OoT}}</ref> They are held in the [[Bullet Bag]], which can be upgraded to hold 40 and 50 Deku Seeds. They can be found when cutting grass.

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, Deku Seeds are used as ammunition for the [[Slingshot]]; however, it has unlimited ammunition, as there are no Deku Seeds to be collected and no bag to hold them.
{{clear|left}}
==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, Pumpkin Seeds can be used as ammunition for the Slingshot. [[Link]] receives 50 pellets when he first buys the Slingshot from [[Sera]] in [[Ordon Village]], but must use Pumpkin Seeds as replacements once they are used up. They can be found by smashing [[Pumpkin]]s or slaying [[Deku Baba]]s and throwing their pods. A maximum of 50 ammunition can be held at a time.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, Deku Seeds are used as ammunition. They can be found by defeating [[Deku Baba]]s, rolling into [[Tree]]s or using the [[Digging Mitts]] in soft [[Soil]]. [[Link]] can also buy ten Deku Seeds for 20 [[Rupee]]s at the [[Gear Shop]].<ref>{{Cite|I see you have an interest in {{Color|SSHD Gold|10 Deku Seeds}}! They are truly the perfectly matched projectile for a slingshot. They could be yours for {{Color|SSHD Red|20 Rupees}}. Care to buy them today?|Rupin|SSHD}}</ref>
