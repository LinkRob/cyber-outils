{{Term/Store|Blue Stalfos|Blue Stalfos|Series, TAoL, ALttP, OoS, OoA, TMC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 207}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
[[Red Stalfos|Red]] and Blue appear, with the blue variant being the stronger one.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
Blue Stalfos are the most simple, as they will only attempt to jump away from [[Link]]'s attacks. They can be defeated with very quick Sword strikes, especially when they are cornered to a wall, although it usually takes a couple strikes to defeat them. They can also be defeated instantly by picking up and throwing a nearby [[pot]] at them, which they will not dodge. They signal their movements by looking in the direction they are going to move next when they stop.

==={{OoS|-}}===
Blue-colored Stalfos are the most basic Stalfos who simply walk around and pose little threat.

==={{OoA|-}}===
A unique [[Blue Stalfos (Sub-Boss)|Blue Stalfos]] appears as the mini-boss of the [[Ancient Tomb]] in {{OoA}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Stalfos}}
{{Plural|TMC|Blue Stalfos}} are found in {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}} in {{TMC}}, specifically the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}. They jump in the air and attempt to land on {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}; sometimes they masquerade as {{Plural|TMC|Skull|link}}, leaping up when {{Term|TMC|Link}} approaches.

Whenever a {{Term|TMC|Gibdo|link}} is burned with the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Blue Stalfos}} emerges from its bandages.
