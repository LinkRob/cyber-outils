{{Term/Store|Seafood Skewer|Seafood Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Seafood Skewer}}<br/>The natural water in this medley of seafaring creatures makes for a delicious broth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Seafood Skewers are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} at least one [[:en:Crab#Breath_of_the_Wild|crab]] or snail in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make their default version can be made by utilizing seafood that has contradicting effects. When eaten, they'll restore some of Link's {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The natural water in this meal creates a delicious broth.<ref name="Inventory"/>

To achieve a status effect from Seafood Skewers, several ingredients of the same effect must be utilized.

* '''Hearty Seafood Skewer''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Seafood Skewer}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The natural water in this medley of seafaring creatures makes for a delicious broth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using any combination of {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}. They will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts when eaten.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Seafood Skewer''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Seafood Skewer}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The natural water in this medley of seafaring creatures makes for a delicious broth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}. Eating them will partially refill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Mighty Seafood Skewer''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Seafood Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The natural water in this medley of seafaring creatures makes for a delicious broth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using any combination of {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Seafood Skewer''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Seafood Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The natural water in this medley of seafaring creatures makes for a delicious broth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using any combination of {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}. Link will gain a boost to his defensive abilities when he eats these skewers.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Seafood Skewer''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Seafood Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The natural water in this medley of seafaring creatures makes for a delicious broth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}}. By eating them, Link will receive a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities.<ref name="Sneaky"/>
