{{Term/Store|Inogo Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Inogo Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|BotW}}
Inogo Bridge is a bridge that spans the [[Zora River]] that is located in the [[Lanayru Great Spring]]. It is one of the two places where [[Link]] can meet [[Sidon]].

Multiple [[Zora]] can point out Inogo Bridge before [[Link]] meets [[Sidon]]. These [[Zora]] are [[Tona]], [[Ledo]], [[Gruve]], [[Torfeau]], [[Tottika]], and [[Tula]].
