==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Raw Bird Drumsticks are dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Wood Pigeon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mountain Crow|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Chested Duck|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Winged Heron|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Pink Heron|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Seagull|link}} when they are killed. They can also be purchased from the travelling merchant [[Mezer]]. Raw Bird Drumsticks restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} when eaten. They can be exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Bird Drumstick|link}} or chilled by [[Ice]] to make {{Plural|BotW|Frozen Bird Drumstick|link}}.

When making [[Food]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, a Raw Bird Drumstick is required to make [[Poultry Pilaf]] and [[Poultry Curry]]. Either [[Raw Meat]] or a Raw Bird Drumstick can be used to make [[Meat Stew]] and [[Salt-Grilled Meat]]. They're also among the meats that can be used to make [[Creamy Meat Soup]], [[Glazed Meat]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Meat Pie]], a [[Meat Skewer]], [[Copious Meat Skewers]], [[Meaty Rice Balls]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Steak]], [[Steamed Meat]], and a [[Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin]].
