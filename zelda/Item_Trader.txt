The {{Term/Store|Item Trader||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233}}</ref>{{Name Ref|LANS}}

==Biography==
The {{Term|LADX|Item Trader}} is found in a cave on the southern portion of {{Term|LADX|Toronbo Shores|link}}. {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} can only see and speak with him after obtaining the {{Term|LADX|Magnifying Lens|link}}. At this point, the {{Term|LADX|Item Trader}} offers {{Term|LADX|Link}} to trade a {{Term|LADX|Boomerang|link}} he found on the beach for the item on his B Button.<ref>{{Cite|I found a good item washed up on the beach... I'll trade it to you for what you have in your B Button... |Item Trader|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Boomerang in exchange for the item you had.|N/A|LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LADX|Link}} can trade the {{Term|LADX|Boomerang}} back to the {{Term|LADX|Item Trader}} at any time to recover his item.<ref>{{Cite|Okay, let's do it! When you don't want the Boomerang any more, come back! |Item Trader|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The item came back to you. You returned the Boomerang. |N/A|LADX}}</ref> The {{Term|LADX|Item Trader}} begs {{Term|LADX|Link}} to give the {{Term|LADX|Boomerang}} back to him after the trade.<ref>{{Cite|Give me back the Boomerang, I beg you! I'll return the item you gave to me!|Item Trader|LADX}}</ref> Not all items can be traded for the Boomerang.<ref>{{Cite|Ah... Don't give me that item... How about something else? |Item Trader|LADX}}</ref> He refuses to take any item with multiple levels (sword, shield, power bracelet), an ammo count (magic powder, bombs, bow), or the ocarina.
