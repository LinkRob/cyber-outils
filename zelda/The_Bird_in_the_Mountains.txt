"{{Term/Store|The Bird in the Mountains||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Bird in the Mountains}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Rito Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can meet a young {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} named {{Term|BotW|Molli|link}}, who asks him if he wants to hear a story.<ref>{{Cite|Want me to tell you a story?|Molli|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, she tells of a time her {{Term|BotW|Grandpa|link}} stopped at a {{Term|BotW|Cedar Tree|link}} while {{Term|BotW|Climbing|link}} a mountain, and he looked down to the northwest.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* Once upon a time... My grandpa stopped at a {{Color|BotW Red|big tree}} while he was climbing a big mountain and looked below him to the northwest.|Molli|BotW}}</ref> When he did, he saw a huge white {{Term|BotW|Bird|link}} with its wings spread out.<ref>{{Cite|When suddenly he saw {{Color|BotW Red|a HUGE snow-white birdie}} with its wings spread wide!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> He flew after the {{Term|BotW|Bird}}, at which point he noticed something inside of its stomach.<ref>{{Cite|My grandpa took off {{Color|BotW Red|flying after the snow-white birdie}}. And as he got a little closer to the white birdie, Grandpa saw {{Color|BotW Red|something SUPER important}} inside its belly!|Molli|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Molli}} pauses when she is about to reveal what the {{Term|BotW|Bird}} swallowed.<ref>{{Cite|The birdie had swallowed—!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks her what it was, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} admits that she gets sleepy at this part of the story and does not remember.<ref>{{Cite|[Well? What was it?!] {{Small|...I always get sleepy around that part of the story...so I don't remember...}}|Molli|BotW}}</ref> However, she does know where the {{Term|BotW|Cedar Tree}} is,<ref>{{Cite|OH! I know where the {{Color|BotW Red|big tree}} is, though!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> pointing out a {{Term|BotW|Cedar Tree}} on {{Term|BotW|Talonto Peak|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's over there! You see it? Grandpa said he saw the {{Color|BotW Red|snow-white birdie}} from THAT tree!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Molli}} wonders what the {{Term|BotW|Bird}} swallowed, and she laments being unable to ask her grandfather now.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm hmm hmm... Now what did that snow-white birdie swallow? I can't hear the story from Grandpa anymore... *sniff*|Molli|BotW}}</ref> When she finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} begins.{{Note|It is possible to immediately complete this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} by finding {{Term|BotW|Mozo Shenno Shrine}} before speaking to {{Term|BotW|Molli}}.}}

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Climbing|display=Climbs}} {{Term|BotW|Talonto Peak}} and looks to the northwest, he will see {{Term|BotW|Biron Snowshelf|link}}, which looks like the silhouette of a {{Term|BotW|Bird}}. As he {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link|display=Paraglides}} towards it, the glow of {{Term|BotW|Mozo Shenno Shrine|link}} will become visible from a cave below the {{Term|BotW|Biron Snowshelf|display=Snowshelf}}. Once the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is activated, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
