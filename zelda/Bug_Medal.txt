The {{Term/Store|Bug Medal||Series, SS}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 119}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==

The Bug Medal can be bought from {{Term|SS|Beedle's Air Shop|link}} after buying the [[Bug Net]]. While [[Link]] has one in his [[Pouch|Adventure Pouch]], you can see all the [[Insect|bugs]] within the area on your [[map]]. Like other pouch items, this has no effect in the [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|Lightning Round]].
