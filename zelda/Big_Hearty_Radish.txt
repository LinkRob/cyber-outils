==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Big Hearty Radish}}
{{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish}} are flora that are found in various grassy areas of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, characterized by their forked and flowering stalk. The vegetable itself cannot be seen until it is picked, as it is a root. As their name implies, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish}} are larger versions of the {{Term|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish}} can be eaten raw to restore four {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. If a {{Term|BotW|Big Hearty Radish}} is roasted over a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}}, it becomes a {{Term|BotW|Roasted Big Radish|link}}. If a {{Term|BotW|Big Hearty Radish}} is {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, the resulting {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} will increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} maximum {{Plural|BotW|Heart}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish}} are one of the ingredients that can be used to make {{Term|BotW|Peach Dye|link}}.

The following meals either require or can optionally use {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish}} in their recipes:
