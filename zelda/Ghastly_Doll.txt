The {{Term/Store|Ghastly Doll||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref name="E 124"/>

==Location and Uses==
To obtain the {{Term|OoS|Ghastly Doll}}, {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} must run into {{Term|OoS|Maple|link}} while possessing the {{Term|OoS|Lon Lon Egg|link}} which she will trade for the {{Term|OoS|Ghastly Doll}}.<ref name="E 124"/><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 130 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref> {{Term|OoS|Link}} can then trade the {{Term|OoS|Ghastly Doll}} to {{Term|OoS|Mrs. Ruul|link}} to receive the {{Term|OoS|Iron Pot|link}}.<ref name="E 124"/>
