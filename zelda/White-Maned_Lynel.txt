{{Term/Store|White-Maned Lynel|White-Maned Lynels|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring enemies in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|White-Maned Lynel}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|White-Maned Lynel}}
White-Maned Lynels are the white and black-colored variant of [[Lynel]]s. They have been in [[Hyrule]] since ancient times and only a few amount of witnesses of this breed survive to tell the tale.<ref>{{Cite|These fearsome monsters have lived in Hyrule since ancient times.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There are so few eyewitness accounts of this breed because a White-Maned Lynel is not one to let even simple passersby escape with their lives.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Like other Lynels, it will often refrain from attacking for a short period of time after spotting Link, instead opting to stare down its opponent. However, they start their battle by roaring out loudly and slamming its Weapon down to causing a fiery explosion around itself. Being a higher tier variant from the [[Blue-Maned Lynel]]s, it is known to carry a stronger set of "Savage" Lynel gear, such as a [[Savage Lynel Sword]], [[Savage Lynel Shield]], [[Savage Lynel Bow]], [[Savage Lynel Spear]], and a [[Savage Lynel Crusher]]. A few White-Maned Lynels, alongside some [[Bokoblin|Bokoblins]] and [[Blue Bokoblin|Blue Bokoblins]], are seen lying defeated by Link in [[A Premonition]] [[Memory]].

In [[Master Mode]], they carry "Mighty" Lynel gear. One also appears on the [[Great Plateau]] carrying a Savage Lynel Crusher and Savage Lynel Bow.

==={{TotK|-}}===
White-Maned Lynels reappear in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', acting the same as in ''Breath of the Wild'', but can also attack Link with their new horns. One is summoned alongside a [[Blue-Maned Lynel]], a [[Silver Lynel]], and some Bokoblins and [[Moblin|Moblins]] by [[Ganon|Ganondorf]] after he becomes the Demon King.
