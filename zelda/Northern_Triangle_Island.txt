{{Term|TWWHD|Northern Triangle Island}} is an {{Term|TWWHD|Island|link}} of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}, located on square C4 to the south of {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}. It is part of a trio of triangle-shaped {{Plural|TWWHD|Island}} that in turn form the inner part of a {{Term|TWWHD|Triforce|link}} when lines are drawn on a map connecting each in conjunction with the {{Plural|TWWHD|Island}} that house the {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}}.

The location of {{Term|TWWHD|Northern Triangle Island}} is strategic as a {{Term|TWWHD|Statue|link}} depicting the {{Term|TWWHD|Golden Goddess}} {{Term|TWWHD|Din (Goddess)|link}} awaits the placement of {{Term|TWWHD|Din's Pearl}}. When {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} approaches the {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}} while holding {{Term|TWWHD|Din's Pearl}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}}'s eyes glows a reddish hue and it prompts him to place the {{Term|TWWHD|Din's Pearl|display=Pearl}} in its hands.<ref>{{Cite|Wandering traveler who seeks the guidepost of the goddesses... Place the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|pearl}} you hold here.|Statue|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You placed {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Din's Pearl}} in the ancient statue's hands.|N/A|TWWHD}}</ref> Afterwards, it will continue to glow until all three {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl}} have been placed in their respective {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}}'s hands, at which point they will come to life and emit beacons toward the other {{Plural|TWWHD|Triangle Island|link}}. This raises the {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}} from the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121}}</ref>

{{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} can also use the {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chart|link}} he receives from {{Term|TWWHD|Six-Eye Reef|link}} to obtain the {{Term|TWWHD|Big Octo Chart|link}} here. It is one of the {{Plural|TWWHD|Island}}, along with {{Term|TWWHD|Shark Island|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Mother and Child Isles|link}}, where {{Plural|TWWHD|Cyclone|link}} rage until {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} defeats {{Term|TWWHD|Cyclos|link}}.

==Trivia==
* Before all three of the {{Plural|TWWHD|Statue|link}} are activated by their respective {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}}, they are shown with unique characteristics that their counterparts lack. The {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}} of {{Term|TWWHD|Din (Goddess)|link}} possesses a nose, whereas the {{Plural|TWWHD|Statue}} of {{Term|TWWHD|Nayru (Goddess)|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Farore (Goddess)|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Southern Triangle Island|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Eastern Triangle Island|link}} possess a mouth and ears, respectively. These features correspond to three kinds of Pikmin from the ''Pikmin'' series, as each Pikmin has a feature that the others do not: [[w:c:Pikmin: Red Pikmin|Red Pikmin]] have noses, [[w:c:pikmin: Blue Pikmin|Blue Pikmin]] have mouths, and [[w:c:pikmin: Yellow Pikmin|Yellow Pikmin]] have ears.
