{{Term/Store|Damia||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Damia|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Damia}} is a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} that lives in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can meet with {{Term|BotW|Damia}} by following the eastern path out of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|display=Forest}}. {{Term|BotW|Damia}} has been expecting {{Term|BotW|Link}} and asks if he is ready for the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood|link}}, since it is the hardest of the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hero guy! I was told you'd be coming. But do you really think you're ready for the {{Color|BotW Red|Test of Wood}}?! Some say it's the hardest of the Korok Trials.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> He explains that the only thing {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs to do is equip the {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear|link}} and reach the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But it's not complicated—all you need to do is equip this {{Color|BotW Red|Korok weapon set}} and reach the shrine at the back of the area.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Damia}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is ready to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you ready to try it?|Damia|BotW}}</ref> Agreeing initiates "{{Term|BotW|The Test of Wood|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} congratulates him.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Mr. Swordsman! Congrats on completing the {{Color|BotW Red|Test of Wood}}! Wasn't too hot for you, was it?|Damia|BotW}}</ref> He then offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} another trial, the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}, which has a shorter time limit.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if you thought that was fun, how about taking a crack at a trial that'll really turn up the heat? Don't worry, Mr. Hero, you'll be doing the same thing as before! The only different is how long you have to do it.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} can make it to {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan Shrine|link}} within five minutes, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} promises him a prize.<ref>{{Cite|If you can make it to the goal within {{Color|BotW Red|five minutes}}, you'll get a prize!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> However, he requires a security deposit of 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, which covers the cost of gear rental.<ref>{{Cite|I'll just need a security deposit of {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} to cover the cost of the gear rental. Sound fair?|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} explains that the rules are the same as before.<ref>{{Cite|The rules are the same as before. Equip the {{Color|BotW Red|sword}}, {{Color|BotW Red|bow}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|shield}}, and make it to the shrine in the back.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> He then goes to set everything up.<ref>{{Cite|I'll get everything ready for you!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan Shrine|display=Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} shouts that he reached the goal.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gooooooooal!}}|Damia|BotW}}</ref> The prize for making it in under five minutes is a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Damia}} hopes {{Term|BotW|Link}} likes.<ref>{{Cite|All right! You made it here {{Color|BotW Red|within five minutes}}, so you get a prize!|Damia|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ta-daaa! Congrats, Mr. Hero! Hope you like it!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} breaks his record, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} asserts that he knew {{Term|BotW|Link}} would be good at the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, a new record! Congrats, Mr. Hero! I knew you'd be good at this!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to let him know if he wants to come back and break his record.<ref>{{Cite|Let me know if you wanna try to break your record!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is done playing, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will take his {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear}} back and return him to the start.<ref>{{Cite|Oops, I almost forgot! I need to collect that gear you borrowed. I'll go ahead and take you back to the start, too. Hold on tight! Yaaaa-hooooooeeeyyy!!!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>
