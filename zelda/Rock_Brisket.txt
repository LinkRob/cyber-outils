{{Term/Store|Rock Brisket||Series, OoA}}, also known as '''Rock Sirloin''',<ref name="Sirloin">{{Cite|Rock Sirloin: A tasty treat.|Inventory|OoA}}</ref> is a quest item in {{OoA}}.

==Location and Uses==
Rock Brisket is earned by successfully hitting all twelve targets in the [[Target Carts]] mini-game. The Rock Brisket is given to the hungry [[Goron]] guarding the stairs to the upper [[Rolling Ridge]] area in the present,<ref>{{Cite|For generations, my ancestors have sat here guarding the peace on Rolling Ridge. I can't leave, but I'm so hungry I could starve...|Goron|OoA}}</ref> in return for his ancestors' [[Goron Vase]].<ref>{{Cite|Whoaaa! You have prime Rock Brisket! Please! Let me have that Rock Brisket! I'll trade my ancestors' Goron Vase for it!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Ironically, the vase must be given to the Goron Guard's ancestor in the past,<ref>{{Cite|What a fine Goron Vase! Please! Let me have that vase!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> who would like a heirloom to give to his descendants to teach them to value life.<ref>{{Cite|I need an heirloom that will teach my descendants to value life...|Goron|OoA}}</ref>

The Rock Brisket is an item in a side-quest [[Link]] must complete to obtain the [[Mermaid Key|Old Mermaid Key]], which allows him to access [[Mermaid's Cave]].

==Nomenclature==
