{{Term/Store|Bargoh||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bargoh|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Bargoh}} is a {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} who stands watch between {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine|link}} near the {{Term|BotW|Shae Mo'sah Shrine|link}}. As a guard, he carries a {{Term|BotW|Cobble Crusher|link}} with him and wears a {{Term|BotW|Forehead Protector|link}}. He is tasked with making sure that monsters do not enter the {{Term|BotW|Goron City|display=City}}, as they have started to appear following the emergence of the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ever since that {{Color|BotW Blue|big lizard}} appeared on {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}, there've been lots of monsters in this area. I'm here to make sure they don't come anywhere near us!|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tells {{Term|BotW|Bargoh}} he is lost, he will suggest climbing up high to get a feel for his surroundings.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm lost...] I guess if you're not used to this area, it's easy to lose your way... When that happens, try findin' a spot that's high up to scope things out.|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref>

As part of his job, {{Term|BotW|Bargoh}} stops people from heading to the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine}} due to its danger.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! It's a Hylian! That's unexpected. What are ya doin' here, little guy? There's only an abandoned mine past this point. That and lots of monsters. In other words, it's dangerous!|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref> However, he allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go through, as {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}} sent him.<ref>{{Cite|What's a Hylian like you doin' tryin' to sniff out Yunobo? HUH? The Boss asked you to?! Well, if the Boss sent ya, I have no choice but to cooperate!|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} may speak with {{Term|BotW|Bargoh}} to learn the whereabouts of {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}}, who passes him several times over the course of the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. At first, he will direct {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're lookin' for Yunobo, he went to the {{Color|BotW Red|Abandoned North Mine}}.|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref> Later, he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} is at the {{Term|BotW|Boss's House|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're lookin' for Yunobo, he's at the {{Color|BotW Red|Boss}}'s house.|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} leaves, however, {{Term|BotW|Bargoh}} will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he is at the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Eldin|link}}, which he gives him directions to.<ref>{{Cite|If you're lookin' for Yunobo, he went to the {{Color|BotW Red|Bridge of Eldin}}. You'll find it waaay down this road, over the hill and past the bridge. It's past the area that's past the area that's past the Goron Hot Springs. That's where it is.|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Bargoh}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that Yunobo has returned to {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yunobo went back to {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}.|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref> He also asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if something happened on {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}, since it looked like {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} had been through something.<ref>{{Cite|By the way... Did somethin' happen at {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}? By the look on Yunobo's face... I dunno, it just seems like he's been though somethin'!|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* {{Term|Series|Bargoh}} is one of several characters that will hit {{Term|Series|Link}} back if he attacks them with a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Don't cross me!|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What are you doing?!|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You want to fight?!|Bargoh|BotW}}</ref> These attacks deal no damage.
