The {{Term/Store|Spooky Mask||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an item in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref> It is a [[Mask]] part of the [[Mask Trading Sequence]].

==Location and Uses==
The Spooky Mask is the third Mask sold, after the [[Keaton Mask]] and [[Skull Mask]]. In order to progress the trading sequence, the Spooky Mask is sold to [[Dampé]]'s admiring fan, a [[Graveyard Boy|young boy]], at the [[Graveyard|Kakariko Graveyard]]. The boy will mistake the masquerading [[Link]] for Dampé.<ref>{{Cite|Gyaah! It's Dampé the gravekeeper!|Graveyard Boy|OoT}}</ref> After realizing it was just a Mask, the boy will buy it for 30 [[Rupee]]s, the same amount that Link paid.<ref>{{Cite|You sold the 30-Rupee mask to a kid for full price?! And he didn't mind paying at all!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> After selling the Spooky Mask, Link can return to the [[Happy Mask Shop]] and receive the [[Bunny Hood]].

Like all Masks, the Spooky Mask can be worn by Link to receive new messages from people, notably by scaring them. It also possesses the unique side-effect of glowing in the dark at night. If Link talks to [[Lake Scientist|the scientist]] at the [[Lakeside Laboratory]] while wearing the Mask, he will tell Link that it was carved from wood taken from a coffin.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmm... Let's see... Yes, I'm quite sure of it... That was manufactured from a plank from a coffin. Yes, it was!|Lake Scientist|OoT}}</ref> Dampé, meanwhile, will ask Link if he knows what kind of Mask it is, and why he is still wearing it if he does.<ref>{{Cite|Do you know what kind of mask you're wearing...? And you're still wearing it?|Dampé|OoT}}</ref>

[[ReDead]]s have a Mask on their faces that is almost identical to the Spooky Mask.
