{{Term/Store|Fairy Fountain: Grassy Plains||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fairy Fountain: Grassy Plains|N/A|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
Fairy Fountain: Grassy Plains is located in [[Hyrule Field]] after completing [[Offering Help on the Road]].

This is a side quest that involves the character completing it in within 7:00 minutes. It is unlocked along with [[Fairy Fountain: Deep Snows]] and [[Fairy Fountain: Desert Sands]].

The character is heading towards the targeted position. However, the character then meets 2 [[Fire Moblin|Fire Moblins]] and 1 [[Ice Moblin]]. After the character defeats them, the character will be able to open three [[Treasure Chest|treasure chests]], where the three chests combined will allow the player to obtain 900 [[Rupee|rupees]]. The timer will still be continuing.

The character will continue heading for the targeted position, but will then be required to defeat a [[Hinox]]. After defeating the Hinox, the character will again see 3 treasure chests, containing a total of 900 rupees. The character should now have obtained 1,800 rupees in the side quest.

After reaching the targeted position, the character should see something similar to a flower. The flower-like thing will then open, to reveal the [[Great Fairy]] [[Cotera]]. She will be very large, and the player will have to defeat Cotera. At this point, the timer should have stopped. Note that once the character meets Cotera, he/she will lose all 1,800 rupees gained.

Once  completed, if this was the character's first try, he/she will get one [[Ethereal Stone]], 3 [[Fairy|Fairies]], and 10 [[Raw Gourmet Meat]], along with completing one of the three Fairy Fountain side quests. If he/she did not complete this on his/her first try, he/she will complete one of the three Fairy Fountain side quests.
