The {{Term/Store|Witch-Hunting Brigade||Series, TFH}} is the group of heroes dedicated to saving the kingdom of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref name="Commander">{{Cite|Allow me to introduce myself. I am {{Color|TFH Blue|Sir Combsly}}, commander of the {{Color|TFH Blue|witch-hunting brigade}} for the great kingdom of Hytopia.|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref>

==Overview==
Prior to the events of {{TFH|-}}, {{Term|TFH|King Tuft|link}} created the {{Term|TFH|Witch-Hunting Brigade}} after his daughter, {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}}, opened a cursed package from the witch of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link}}. As a result, {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}} and its people fell into despair from the fear of being fashionable.<ref>{{Cite|The worst part is that, ever since {{Color|TFH Blue|Styla}} became cursed, Hytopia and its people have fallen into despair. Everyone is afraid of fashion now, lest they become cursed as well. Hytopia has never looked so drab...|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> The King placed a {{Term|TFH|Sign|link}} at the village's entrance in hopes of finding heroes to fulfill the roles of the legendary {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero|link}} and save {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}} from its predicament.<ref>{{Cite|WANTED: TRI FORCE HEROES!!|Sign|TFH}}</ref>

The commander of the Brigade, {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly|link}},<ref name="Commander"/> meets with prospective heroes inside {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}. Having seen many insufficient heroes already, {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} is immediately impressed by {{Term|TFH|Link|link}}'s heroic {{Term|TFH|Three Noble Attributes of Courage|link|display=Attributes}} and enlists him in the Brigade,<ref>{{Cite|I must say...I've seen many would-be heroes in my time. Too many, in fact. But none of them boasted the perfect trifecta of heroic features as you do. We're happy to have you on our side!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> providing him with the {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} needed to tailor a {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic|link}}. He then informs {{Term|TFH|Link}} of the Brigade's unbreakable rules; namely, that the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}} are too dangerous to visit alone, and that the young hero must be matched with other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} in [[Multiplayer]], or make use of {{Plural|TFH|Doppel|link}} in order to set out on his adventure.<ref>{{Cite|To start, let me fill you in on the {{Color|TFH Blue|unbreakable rules}} of my brigade. The {{Color|TFH Blue|Drablands}} are home to the {{Color|TFH Blue|witch}}. It's literally too dangerous to go alone, so you'll need a team of three.|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref>

{{Term|TFH|Madame Couture|link}} is implied to be a member of the {{Term|TFH|Witch-Hunting Brigade}}, through her willingness to create powerful {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}} for the Brigade's heroes,<ref>{{Cite|Still, enough is enough! I'll do all I can to support any hero who has what it takes to defeat that pesky {{Color|TFH Blue|witch}}.|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> and her close communication with {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, you must be the one {{Color|TFH Blue|Sir Combsly}} told me about. Hmm...|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TFH Blue|Madame}} told me everything. I never would have guessed she and the {{Color|TFH Blue|witch}} are sisters... Such a sad tale.|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> She initially doubts that {{Term|TFH|Link}} could be the fated hero due to his [[Bear Minimum|poor fashion sense]],<ref>{{Cite|Someone like you, who cares nothing for style, is our hero?
