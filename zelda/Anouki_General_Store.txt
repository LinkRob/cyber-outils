The {{Term/Store|Anouki General Store||Series, ST}} is a location in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Guy! This here is the Anouki General Store! We got everything ya need. Check it out!|Akin|ST}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|ST|Anouki General Store}} is located in the [[Snow Sanctuary]], it is a small shop that offers basic items to its customers, being the only shop in the game where a [[Heart Container]] can be bought. The owner is a {{Term|ST|Akin|link}}.

After [[Link]] buys the [[Heart Container]], the shop will replace it with a random medium-rare [[treasure]] such as a [[Dragon Scale]] or a [[Ruto Crown]], any of which can be bought for 300 Rupees. Only one treasure is available at any time, and the one it offers is random. After Link buys the treasure from the store, it will take a day for the shop to restock with a new, random treasure.
