The {{Term/Store|Pumaag Nitae Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Pumaag Nitae Shrine}}<br/>A Minor Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Minor Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Pumaag Nitae Shrine is located at the border of the {{Term|BotW|Pagos Woods|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Finra Woods|link}} in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pumaag Nitae|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Pumaag Nitae. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Pumaag Nitae|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Pumaag Nitae Shrine features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. As Link approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}}, wielding a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Shield|link}}, will rise from a pit to challenge Link. In order to reach Pumaag Nitae, Link must defeat the Guardian Scout II to cause a portcullis separating him from the Monk to rise.

Once Link has done so, Pumaag Nitae will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Pumaag Nitae|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, Pumaag Nitae will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Pumaag Nitae|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the portcullis contains a {{Term|BotW|Boomerang|link}}.
