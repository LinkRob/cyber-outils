==Location and uses==
The Red Potion is a [[Potion]] used to replenish [[Link]]'s [[Life Gauge]]. In its early appearances, the Red Potion replenishes all of Link's [[Heart]]s, much like a [[Life Potion]], but in later appearances, it restores only up to six or eight [[Heart]]s. Like the [[Green Potion|Green]] and [[Blue Potion|Blue Potions]], Link is required to have an [[Empty Bottle]] to contain the Red Potion.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP|-}}, Red Potions can be bought from the [[Witch's Hut]] for 120 [[Rupee]]s. The shopkeeper allows Link to instantly take a sample of the Red Potion in the shop for free, and it fully restores his health, like the product itself.

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Red Potions are single-use items that fully restore Link's health, just like in the previous game. They are sold by various [[Business Scrub]]s, and they are also sold at the [[Potion Shop]] at 30 Rupees each; the [[Goron Shop]] at 40 Rupees each; and at the [[Zora Shop]] at 50 Rupees each.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Red Potions are based on their appearance in {{OoT|-}}, but have their role expanded upon. Red Potions function identically as before, with the added effect of being able to remove the curse that [[Blue Bubble]]s inflict upon Link.

Red Potions are sold in several locations, from cheapest to most expensive: the [[Magic Hags' Potion Shop]] for 20 Rupees; the [[Trading Post]] for 30 Rupees; the [[Goron Shop]] for 50 or 80 Rupees; and [[Zora Hall]] for 60 Rupees. Conversely, Link can sell a Red Potion at the [[Curiosity Shop]] for 20 Rupees, the same price as it costs in the Magic Hags' Potion Shop.

Though Link usually has to pay for Red Potions at the Magic Hags' Potion Shop, [[Kotake]] will give him a free bottle of Red Potion to heal [[Koume]] in the [[Woods of Mystery]]. Link can optionally give a Red Potion to [[Shiro]] in exchange for the [[Stone Mask]].

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, Red Potions fully restore Link's health, as in previous games. Red Potions are now composed of [[Red Chuchu Jelly]], and [[Chu Jelly Juice Shop]], Link can pay 20 [[Rupee]]s to have [[Doc Bandam]] convert Red Chuchu Jelly into a Red Potion. Red Potions can also be bought from [[Beedle's Shop Ship]] at 30 Rupees each.

==={{TMC|-}}===
Red Potions restore all of Link's hearts in {{TMC}}. Link can purchase a Red Potion from [[Syrup]] in the [[Witch's Hut]] for 150 Rupees, although only after he fuses [[Kinstone]]s with the [[Forest Picori]] in [[South Hyrule Field]]

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, unlike previous installments, Red Potions restore only up to six hearts. Just like in {{TWW|-}}, it can be bought from Beedle's Shop Ship, at the same price of 30 Rupees.

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, Red Potions are functionally identical to their appearance in {{PH}}, even costing 30 Rupees as before, but they can only be bought deom the [[Castle Town Shop]].

==={{SS|-}}===
The Heart Potion, as it is called in {{SS}}, can restore up to eight hearts. It can first be [[Upgrading|upgraded]] to the [[Heart Potion+]], which is functionally identical to the standard Heart Potion's appearances prior to {{PH|-}}, and upgraded a second time to the [[Heart Potion++]], which retains its functionality but can be used twice.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
Red Potions in {{ALBW}} can restore up to eight hearts, just like in {{SS|-}}. Link can purchase it at the [[Witch's House]] for 50 Rupees, provided he has an [[Empty Bottle]] to store it inside of.

==={{EoW|-}}===

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
Red Potions are a usable item in {{HW}} and its reissues, {{HWL}} and {{HWDE}}.
