{{Term/Store|Cross the Volcano||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 17 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cross the Volcano|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Cross the Volcano}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given five minutes to reach a Targeted Position in the eastern-most section of the map, starting on the opposite side. There is nothing blocking the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} from reaching the center of the map, aside from a handful of {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} walks close to the center of the map, a gate closes and a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} spawns. The gate can only be opened by defeating it. If the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} goes south after passing through the gate, he will find an area containing an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}}. Eventually, after following a path containing a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} will reach a dead end, and when he or she does, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} spawns. When it is defeated, a drawbridge lowers, allowing the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} to advance. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} then follows a long path to the other end of the map, scattered with {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Pebblit|link}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the Targeted Position, they are given the task of killing an {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus}} is defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Ruby|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Summerwing Butterfly|link}}.
