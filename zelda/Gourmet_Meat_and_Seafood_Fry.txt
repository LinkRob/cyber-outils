As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fries are meals made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either [[Raw Gourmet Meat]] or a [[Raw Whole Bird]] with any seafood together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made either by using seafood that lacks any effect, such as {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}}, or by using multiple pieces of seafood with different effects. Eating these dishes will heal several {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fries are made using the best cuts of meat and seafood and are considered to be as delicious as they are filling.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on the type of seafood used and whether any additional ingredients are added, several different variations of Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry exist:

* '''Hearty Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A marriage of the choicest cuts of meat and seafood. As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When eaten, they'll completely heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A marriage of the choicest cuts of meat and seafood. As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}, or other energizing ingredients. If they're eaten, they'll replenish a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A marriage of the choicest cuts of meat and seafood. As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using enduring ingredients. They completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when they're eaten.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A marriage of the choicest cuts of meat and seafood. As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using hasty ingredients. Consuming these fries will temporarily boost Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A marriage of the choicest cuts of meat and seafood. As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} or other spicy ingredients. Link gains a level of [[Cold Resistance]] when he eats these.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A marriage of the choicest cuts of meat and seafood. As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}} or other electric ingredients. Eating these will increase Link's [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A marriage of the choicest cuts of meat and seafood. As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, or other mighty ingredients. When eaten, Link's attack power will be boosted for a short period of time.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A marriage of the choicest cuts of meat and seafood. As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}, or other tough ingredients. They increase Link's defense when they're eaten.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A marriage of the choicest cuts of meat and seafood. As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}} or other sneaky ingredients. If these dishes are eaten, they'll temporarily increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A marriage of the choicest cuts of meat and seafood. As delicious as it is filling!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}} or other cold ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] temporarily.<ref name="Chilly"/>
