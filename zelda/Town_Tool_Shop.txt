The {{Term/Store|Town Tool Shop||Series, LA, LADX}} is a {{Term|LANS|Shop|link}} in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Town Tool Shop|Atlas Of Koholint Island|LADX}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Manual|LA|Town Tool Shop|This shop has many goods that will come in handy during your adventure. You can make your purchase by bringing the item you want to the cash register.}}
The Town Tool Shop is the local Shop of {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|link}}. It is possible for {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} to {{Term|LANS|Thievery|link|display= steal}} items from the {{Term|LANS|Shop}} by either distracting the {{Term|LANS|Town Tool Shopkeeper|link|display= Shopkeeper}} or by using the {{Term|LANS|Pegasus Boots|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Guess what? You got it for free. Are you proud of yourself?|Town Tool Shopkeeper|LADX}}</ref> However, upon re-entry, the {{Term|LANS|Town Tool Shopkeeper|display= Shopkeeper}} will kill {{Term|LANS|Link}} for stealing from him.<ref>{{Cite|I wasn't kidding when I said pay! Now, you'll pay the ultimate price!!|Town Tool Shopkeeper|LADX}}</ref> In addition, every character will refer to {{Term|LANS|Link}} as "THIEF" from that point on.
