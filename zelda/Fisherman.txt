{{Term/Store|Fisherman|Fishermen|Series, LA, LADX, LANS, MM, MM3D|plural}} are recurring characters in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fisherman|N/A|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, there are two {{Plural|LADX|Fisherman}}. One of them can be found in {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village|link}} and will allow {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} to go {{Term|LADX|Fishing|link}} for 10 {{Plural|LADX|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|How about some fishing, little buddy? I'll only charge you 10 Rupees...|Fisherman|LADX}}</ref> The second one can be found underneath a bridge in {{Term|LADX|Martha's Bay|link}} and has lost his {{Term|LADX|Fishing Hook|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's no use, little buddy! A fish took my hook... I keep casting my line into the water, but I haven't got a bite... I thought this would happen...|Fisherman|LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LADX|Link}} can give him the {{Term|LADX|Fishing Hook}} {{Term|LANS|Grandma Yahoo|link}} finds while sweeping the river bank in exchange for his next catch,<ref>{{Cite|Okay! In return you can have this fishing hook I found when I swept by the river bank!|Grandma Ulrira|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! What is that you have in your hand? It's not a fishing hook, is it? You had better let me have it. I'll give you my next catch if you let me have it...|Fisherman|LADX}}</ref> which happens to be the {{Term|LADX|Necklace|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|All right, boy, feast your eyes on a display of fishing skill! [...] Whoooah nellie!! It's a big one!! Big! Big! Yaaah!|Fisherman|LA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The {{Icon|LADX Fishing Hook}} became a necklace {{Icon|LADX Necklace}}! L-l-lucky!|N/A|LADX}}</ref>

In {{LA|DX}}, he appears in a {{Term|LADX|Photo|link}} in {{Term|LADX|The Travels of Link|link}} album.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Fisherman}} is found in the {{Term|MM3D|Fisherman's Hut|link}} at the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Coast|link}}. He tells {{Term|MM3D|Link}} that he has been fishing for 30 years and that he is a better {{Term|MM3D|Fisherman}} than the {{Plural|MM3D|Zora|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've been catching fish in these seas for 30 years. When it comes to catching fish, I'm even better than the Zoras.|Fisherman|MM}}</ref> However, he claims that he has not been able to catch many {{Plural|MM3D|Fish|link}} lately because of the warm and murky waters.<ref>{{Cite|Lately, the seawater has gotten really warm. It hasn't helped my fishing at all...|Fisherman|MM}}</ref> He also mentions that he used to use a {{Term|MM3D|Hookshot|link}} to catch {{Plural|MM3D|Fish}} off the coast, but he was suddenly attacked by {{Term|MM3D|Gerudo|link|display= female pirates}} who took away the {{Term|MM3D|Hookshot}}, claiming that it was a legendary treasure.<ref>{{Cite|See, it was long ago when I used to use this thing called a hookshot to catch fish off the coast... But I was attacked by these pirates, and they took it away with them. They say... it's a legendary treasure..|Fisherman|MM}}</ref> If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} inspects the {{Term|MM3D|Seahorse|link}} in his house, the {{Term|MM3D|Fisherman}} will say that it is a rare specimen, and that he was planning to sell it at the carnival. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} brings him a {{Term|MM3D|Pictograph|link}} of a {{Term|MM3D|Gerudo}} pirate from the {{Term|MM3D|Pirates' Fortress|link}}, he will give him the {{Term|MM3D|Seahorse}} in exchange.<ref>{{Cite|It's a rare fish, isn't it? It's called a sea horse.|Fisherman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you want that sea horse, bring me a pictograph of a female pirate.|Fisherman|MM}}</ref>

After completing the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple|link}} and defeating {{Term|MM3D|Gyorg (Boss)|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Fisherman}} will relocate to a small island near the {{Term|MM3D|Pirates' Fortress}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now that the seas are back to normal, I've started a little business aimed at tourists.|Fisherman|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Link}} cannot reach the island unless he rides on a nearby motorboat and then {{Term|MM3D|Hookshot}} over to the island. He will let {{Term|MM3D|Link}} play the {{Term|MM3D|Fisherman's Jumping Game|link}}, in which he has to jump across several islands, trying to land on the island with a lit {{Term|MM3D|Torch|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to light the torches on each of the surrounding four islands in a particular order. Jump to the island that has the lit torch. If you can jump to it before the torch goes out, you'll get one point!|Fisherman|MM}}</ref> If the young hero obtains at least 20 points, he will receive a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}} as a prize from the {{Term|MM3D|Fisherman}}. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} wins, the {{Term|MM3D|Fisherman}} will tell him that he regrets having made the game so easy, saying that he was thinking of buying a big ship but cannot anymore because he might go bankrupt.<ref>{{Cite|Uh-oh! That was a little too easy... Oh well, here's your prize! And I was thinking of saving up to buy a big ship... Now it looks like I may go bankrupt!|Fisherman|MM}}</ref>
