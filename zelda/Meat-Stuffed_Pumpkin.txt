{{Term/Store|Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin|Meat-Stuffed Pumpkins|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin}}<br/>This hollow, meat-filled fortified pumpkin is a local specialty or Kakariko Village.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Meat-Stuffed Pumpkins can be {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=cooked}} by combining a {{Term|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}} with any type of meat in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made by adding a third ingredient with an effect other than tough. By eating them, some of {{Plural|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} will be restored to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These meals are a specialty of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

After completing {{Term|BotW|Koko|link|display=Koko's}} first two quests, Link can speak to her again to find that she wants to make {{Term|BotW|Dorian|link|display=her father's}} favorite meal, Tough Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin.<ref>{{Cite|What should Koko make now... {{Big|OH!}} Koko will make Father's favorite! {{Color|BotW Blue|Tough meat-stuffed pumpkin}}!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, while going over the recipe, Koko realizes that she doesn't have any {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let's see... The ingredients are... {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkin}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|raw meat}}. Hrm? ...{{Color|BotW Blue|Raw meat}}? {{Big|OH, NO!!}} Koko is out of {{Color|BotW Blue|raw meat}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Koko Cuisine|link}}". Link can bring her Raw Meat, which Koko will be thankful for<ref>{{Cite|Is this...{{Big|raw meat?!}} Are you sure about this? Really? Thank you so much! Koko means it! Stay right there for a second!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> After making it, Koko will be pleased with the result, saying it's just like her mother made and giving some to Link as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Mmm! What a scrumptious smell! Oh, and that savory meat pairs SO well with the sweetness of the pumpkin! This is just how Mother made it! Here, have some! It's so good!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

Only one variation of Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin exists:

* '''Tough Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This hollow, meat-filled fortified pumpkin is a local specialty or Kakariko Village.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using the default recipe. Eating these pumpkins will increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/>
