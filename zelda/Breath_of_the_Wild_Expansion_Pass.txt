The {{Term/Store|''Breath of the Wild Expansion Pass''||Series, BotW}} is a downloadable content pass for {{BotW}}.{{Fact}}

==Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Breath of the Wild Expansion Pass}} contains both waves of downloadable content for {{BotW|-}}: {{TMT}} and {{TCB}}. It is the only way of obtaining these packs. It also includes a purchase bonus in the form of three {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} scattered around the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}, containing a {{Term|BotW|Ruby|link}}, ten {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}, and the exclusive {{Term|BotW|Nintendo Switch Shirt|link}}.
