{{Term/Store|Lake Illumeni||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lake Illumeni'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Lake Illumeni is located in the [[Tabantha Frontier]] between the [[Gerudo Highlands]] and the [[Tanagar Canyon]]. Lake Illumeni is a small lake surrounded by a small forest and a cliff. Inside the small forest, old ruins can be found scattered throughout the forest.
[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
East of the lake in the forest, a [[Korok]] can be spotted hiding in a group of flowers near some ruins. [[Link]] must walk through the patches of flowers going from one flower, up to five flowers to make the Korok appear.
