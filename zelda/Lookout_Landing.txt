'''Lookout Landing''' is a settlement in {{TotK}}, located right in front of [[Hyrule Castle Town Ruins|Hyrule Castle Town]]. This is the first settlement [[Link]] visits in {{TotK|-}}. The settlement was built over the [[Sacred Ground Ruins]] by [[Hudson Construction]] as an encampment to find out what was happening on the levitating [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle.]] This is the place [[Link]] gets the main quest [[Find Princess Zelda]].

This is also the place [[Link]] activates and discovers [[Skyview Tower|Skyview Towers]].
