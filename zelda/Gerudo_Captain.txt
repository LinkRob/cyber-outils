{{Term/Store|Gerudo Captain|Gerudo Captains|Series|plural}} are [[Enemies]] in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Improve}}
Gerudo Captains appear in The Champion Urbosa, where they are initially tricked into helping [[Master Kohga]], then ally with [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]. They also appear in Down with the [[Yiga Clan]], where they have infiltrated the hideout with varying degrees of success. As well, they appear in Air and Lightning, All Hyrule United and The Final Battle. They, like [[Captain (Hyrule Warriors)|Hylian Captains]], can use all weapons that [[Link]] uses.
