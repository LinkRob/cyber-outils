{{Term/Store|Lake Hylia North||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lake Hylia North|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Lake Hylia North is a small region of land located in [[Hyrule Field]]. It is positioned north of [[Lake Hylia]] and due south of the [[Outpost Ruins]].

A [[Stone Talus (Luminous)]] sits on the hill between the Lake and the [[Outpost Ruins]]. In [[Master Mode]], it is a [[Stone Talus (Rare)]] instead.
