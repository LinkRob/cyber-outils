==Location and Uses==
Shards of Naydra's Fang can be gathered by shooting the spirit [[Naydra]] in the face. The spot it was shot will glow, and the Shard will fall to the ground. [[Cooking]] with a Shard of Naydra's Fang will always result in [[Dubious Food]]. They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] as an ingredient to make Light Blue [[Dye]].

Two Shards, as well as five {{Plural|Series|Energetic Rhino Beetle|link}}, are needed to upgrade the [[Tunic of the Wild]] to its third tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. Another Shard of Naydra's Fang can be used to upgrade the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Armor|link}} to its third tier.

* Total for '''Fierce Deity Armor''': 1 (0, 0, 1, 0)

* Total for '''Tunic of the Wild''': 2 (0, 0, 2, 0)
