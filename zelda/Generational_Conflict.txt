==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Generational Conflict}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} are given ten minutes to capture an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} by defeating their relative from the past. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are also separate from each other, so that each {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} can only capture the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} they spawn in. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} is completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, one {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, and 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee}}.
