{{Term/Store|Lookout Platform|Lookout Platforms|Series|plural}} are locations in {{TWW}}.{{Name Ref|TWW, TWWHD}}

==Overview==
In the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}, many Lookout Platforms protrude from the water. They can be found using the {{Term|TWWHD|Platform Chart|link}}, and are inhabited by {{Plural|TWWHD|Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Kargarok|link}} and rarely, {{Plural|TWWHD|Wizzrobe|link}}. 

While they mostly appear on their own, there are occasionally clusters of two or three platforms, which tend to have more valuable treasure. Some Platforms will only yield their treasure when all of their mounted cannons and enemies are destroyed first.
