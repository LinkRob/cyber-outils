==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
Helmaroc Plumes are [[Treasure#Phantom Hourglass|Treasure]]s in {{PH}}. Described as flamboyant feathers dropped by [[Helmaroc King|Helmaroc]] birds,{{Fact}} they can be sold on [[Mercay Island]] to the [[Teller of Treasures]] for a random price. They can also be sold to a man on the [[Ho Ho Tribe]]'s Traveling Ship for a random amount, often more than 100 [[Rupee]]s each.
