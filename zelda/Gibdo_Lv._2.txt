'''Gibdo Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Gibdo Lv. 2|Terrifying and driven by vengeance, this bandaged horror transforms into a ReDead when set on fire.<ref name="Gibdo Lv. 2">{{Cite|Terrifying and driven by vengeance, this bandaged horror transforms into a ReDead when set on fire.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Gibdo Lv. 2 is a more powerful variant of the standard [[Gibdo]], featuring enhanced durability and stronger attacks. When exposed to fire, it sheds its wrappings and transforms into a [[ReDead]]. They have a [[Echo]] cost of four when summoned.

==Trvia==
*The burning of a Gibdo Lv. 2 into a ReDead when burned is reminiscent of {{MM|-}}, where setting a Gibdo on fire similarly reveals a ReDead beneath its wrappings.
