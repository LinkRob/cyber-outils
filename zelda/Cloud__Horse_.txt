{{Term/Store|Cloud|Clouds|Series, TSP, SSBfN3DS, SSBfWU, SSBU}} is a horse in {{TSP}}.{{Name Ref|TSP, SSBfWU, SSBU}}

==Biography==
Cloud is [[Link]]'s horse and is first mentioned in the first page of the book as Link and her are traveling through the [[Midoro Swamp]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=There's no time for detours, Link says, and spurs Cloud into the Midoro Swamp.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 1}}</ref> Throughout the book, Cloud provides easy transportation for Link, especially when traveling to the [[Water Town of Saria]] to retrieve the [[Mirror of Truth]]. Cloud also served as a quick means of escape when Link was ambushed by the Moblins in Midoro Swamp later in the book.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Woops! I think it's time for a fast exit now, he says. He urges Cloud to a gallop and they race out of the swamp, leaving the howling moblin behind.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 41}}</ref> 

Towards the end of the book, when [[Charles of Moria]] is revealed to be [[Ganon]], Link mounts Cloud to follow Charles's tracks into the Midoro Swamp, where the young hero successfully defeats Ganon and safely retrieves the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] with a small aid from his horse.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link leans forward in the saddle and squeezes his horses's sides with his knees. She stretches her legs and runs as fast as she can across grassy pastures, down dirt roads, through small towns. Link follows Charles's tracks to the Midoro Swamp.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 48}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link jumps to his feet on Cloud's back, does a somersault over the swarm of Moblin and lands in front of Charles.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 96}}</ref>
