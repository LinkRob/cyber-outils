{{Term/Store|Spoil|Spoils|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are items in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref> They are stored in the [[Spoils Bag]].

==Location and Uses==
Spoils are occasionally dropped by some enemies when defeated. A defeated enemy may also leave behind a [[Treasure Sphere]], which encases several Spoils. They can also be stolen from enemies by using the [[Grappling Hook]] or found inside [[chest]]s. Spoils can be sold at any [[Beedle's Shop Ship]].
