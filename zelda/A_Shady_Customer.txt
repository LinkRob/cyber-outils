"{{Term/Store|A Shady Customer||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Shady Customer}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|East Akkala Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}}, a mercenary guard hired to patrol the area, {{Term|BotW|Hoz|link}}, tells {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} how dangerous the area has gotten lately.<ref>{{Cite|Geez, it's gotten dangerous around here lately!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, he shares a rumor of a suspicious character named {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}}, who has been spotted running a business known as {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} during the night.<ref>{{Cite|[It has.] Yes! Recently I've been hearing about a suspicious guy named {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}... He runs a shop called {{Color|BotW Blue|Fang and Bone}}. They say he goes around pushing some kind of cart {{Color|BotW Red|night after night}}.|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> Due to his activities, the people around {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable}} have become uncomfortable.<ref>{{Cite|He's been making people around here uncomfortable with his suspicious and otherworldly activity...|Hoz|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Hoz}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he knows {{Term|BotW|Kilton}}.<ref>{{Cite|Do you know...{{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}?|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that he does, {{Term|BotW|Hoz}} is shocked to hear that {{Term|BotW|Link}} knows something.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes, I do.] Whaaa? You've heard something?!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that he does not, {{Term|BotW|Hoz}} is disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|I see...|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> Either way, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to tell him of any suspicious people he sees, specifically {{Term|BotW|Kilton}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if you see any suspicious people, please tell me {{Color|BotW Red|what they look like in detail}}! Really! If you see this {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}} character, please tell me all the {{Color|BotW Red|juicy details}}. I'm counting on you!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can then take a photo of {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} using the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} has not been met yet, he can be found in the eye of {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link}} during the night. However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already spoken to him, he can be found outside of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}.

Once this is done, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Hoz}} at the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable}} and show him the picture.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? You've got information about {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}? Allow me to take a look...|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> Upon seeing it, {{Term|BotW|Hoz}} remarks that {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} looks strange.<ref>{{Cite|Heheheh. I see... How strange.|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> He thanks Link for helping to hunt him down, giving him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Seriously, I can't thank you enough! This will surely help me hunt him down. Really, I must thank you somehow!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Hoz}} returns to his duty guarding the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Well, I must return to my mission now. Farewell!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> which completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
