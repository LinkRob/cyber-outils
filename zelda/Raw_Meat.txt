==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Raw Meat is dropped by {{Plural|Series|Woodland Boar|link}}, {{Plural|Series|Mountain Goat|link}}, and {{Plural|Series|Grassland Fox|link}} when they are killed. It's also sold by two separate travelling merchants, those being [[Cambo]] and [[Mezer]]. Raw Meat can be eaten to restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} of healing. It can be cooked by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to make [[Seared Steak]] or frozen with [[Ice]] to create [[Icy Meat]].

As part of the [[Side Quest]] "[[Koko Cuisine]]", {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must gather Raw Meat for [[Koko]] so she can make [[Tough Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin]].<ref>{{Cite|What should Koko make now... {{Big|OH!}} Koko will make Father's favorite! {{Color|BotW Blue|Tough meat-stuffed pumpkin}}!|Koko|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Let's see... The ingredients are... {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkin}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|raw meat}}. Hrm? ...{{Color|BotW Blue|Raw meat}}? {{Big|OH, NO!!}} Koko is out of {{Color|BotW Blue|raw meat}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> When Link brings her Raw Meat, Koko will be excited and reward Link with Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin.<ref>{{Cite|Mmm! What a scrumptious smell! Oh, and that savory meat pairs SO well with the sweetness of the pumpkin! This is just how Mother made it! Here, have some! It's so good!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

A Raw Meat must be used in order to make a [[Spiced Meat Skewer]], [[Meat Curry]], and a [[Meat and Rice Bowl]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Either a Raw Meat or a [[Raw Bird Drumstick]] is required to make [[Meat Stew]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], and [[Salt-Grilled Meat]]. They're also one of several meat items that can be used to make [[Creamy Meat Soup]], [[Glazed Meat]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Meat Pie]], a [[Meat Skewer]], [[Copious Meat Skewers]], [[Meaty Rice Balls]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Steak]], [[Steamed Meat]], and a [[Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin]].
