{{Term/Store|Rupee Wraith|Rupee Wraiths|Series, FS|plural}} are enemies in {{FS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 249}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|FS|Rupee Wraith}} can appear when opening {{Plural|FS|Treasure Chest|link}}, pursuing the {{Term|FS|Link|link}} that opened it. If a {{Term|FS|Link}} comes in contact with it, he will start losing {{Plural|FS|Rupee|link}} in quantities of one, then five, and finally 20. Touching one of the other {{Plural|FS|Link}} will pass the {{Term|FS|Rupee Wraith}} to them. Eventually, the {{Term|FS|Rupee Wraith}} will vanish after a few seconds.

In {{FSAE}}, a {{Term|FS|Rupee Wraith}} can be dispatched quickly by switching control to the other Link not being attacked.

==Trivia==
*In {{TMC}}, the {{Term|TMC|Ghost|link}} that haunts {{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}} shares the same sprite as the Rupee Wraith.
*The {{Term|TMC|Big Octorok|link}} Boss from {{TMC|-}} also produces the same squealing sound as Rupee Wraiths.
