The {{Term/Store|Teacher||Series, ST}} is a character in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 288}}</ref> As his name would suggest, he is [[Princess Zelda]]'s personal teacher. Teacher is very mindful of his student's safety, and panics when Zelda's body is taken.<ref name="quote 1">{{Cite|WHAT?! So...what you're saying is...the princess has been kidnapped! This is terrible! I must inform the princess at once! ...Ah, yes, that's right. She's not here. Well, I'm at a loss as to what to do. Hmm... What to do... What to do...|Teacher|ST}}</ref> This panic solidifies into a resolve to find the princess.<ref>{{Cite|What to do, what to do... Oh, dear, what a mess! I must find the princess! Oh, but where do I look?|Teacher|ST}}</ref>

==Biography==
[[File:Teacher.png|thumb|left|Teacher presenting Princess Zelda the [[Engineer Certificate|Royal Engineer's Certificate]]]]
Teacher is first seen attending [[Link]]'s [[Royal Engineer]] Graduation Ceremony. He appears again in the guard house when Link regains consciousness next to [[Alfonzo]] following the events leading to [[Princess Zelda]]'s capture.<ref>{{Cite|I'm glad to see you're finally awake. There was some hubbub outside. When I went to investigate, people said that the Spirit Tracks were disappearing... At that point, the guards brought you and Alfonzo in from outside. Both of you were out cold, so I've been waiting for you to come to. Now tell me, what happened?|Teacher|ST}}</ref> At his request, Link tells him the events of Zelda's capture.<ref>{{Cite|So, what you're saying is... The chancellor is some kind of monster? And he kidnapped Princess Zelda?|Teacher|ST}}</ref> Incredulous, he falls into a state of panic, at a loss for what to do.<ref name="quote 1"></ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, what should I do? Perhaps I should talk to the chancellor. But he's not here either. Not to mention...he could very well be a monster. What a terrible mess...|Teacher|ST}}</ref>
Although having initially appeared to believe Link's story, Teacher falls into a state of denial later on, convinced that the princess is merely "wandering somewhere".<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure she's just out wandering somewhere.|Teacher|ST}}</ref> To Zelda's dismay, he scoffs at Link for saying that the princess' spirit had separated from her body and that she was actually standing right next to him.<ref>{{Cite|You mean the princess's spirit is here right now? You talk as if she's passed away! How incredibly insensitive of you!|Teacher|ST}}</ref> Despite this, Teacher is in fact troubled by Zelda's disappearance.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, it's you again... Listen, the princess still hasn't returned. It's very troubling...|Teacher|ST}}</ref> He is in fact one of few to realize that the princess has disappeared. Not wanting to cause panic in the kingdom by sending out soldiers, Teacher is resolved to go out and search for the princess on his own, even though he admits not knowing where to begin his search.<ref>{{Cite|If I sent the soldiers out looking for her, the  kingdom would panic. I suppose I'll just have to go out and find her myself. Move aside!|Teacher|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm the only one who can save the princess! Hmm... But where do I begin looking for her?|Teacher|ST}}</ref>

Later on, Teacher dons a meager disguise and appears at the [[Hyrule Castle Town|Castle Town]] [[Train Station]]. If Link addresses him by his name, he will deny it.<ref>{{Cite|You have quite the imagination there. You must be mistaking me for someone else. I have no idea who this "Teacher" fellow is!|Teacher|ST}}</ref> He will ask Link to be chauffeured to a random location in the hopes of finding Princess Zelda.<ref>{{Cite|I'm embarking on a journey to see every station I can. Would you mind if I catch a ride on your train?|Teacher|ST}}</ref> After being brought to the location, he will pay Link his "driver's fee", which will vary depending on how well Link drove the [[Spirit Train|train]] (the maximum being 300 [[Rupee]]s). The locations Teacher will ask to be driven to are [[Aboda Village]], [[Anouki Village]], [[Papuchia Village]], and [[Goron Village]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm heading to Aboda Village. I can only hope that she's there!|Teacher|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'd like to go to Anouki Village. Perhaps I'll find her there.|Teacher|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please take me to Papuchia Village! Perhaps I'll uncover some clues there.|Teacher|ST}}</ref><Ref>{{Cite|I'll try looking in Goron Village. Perhaps there will be a hint there.|Teacher|ST}}</ref> He may also ask to be driven back to Castle Town, hoping that the princess returned in his absence.<ref>{{Cite|I'm returning home to Castle Town. I've been away far too long. Perhaps in my absence, the princess has returned too.|Teacher|ST}}</ref>   

After completing a puzzle at [[Ends of the Earth Station]], [[Princess Zelda]] mentions that even Teacher, who apparently loves puzzles, would have been stumped by it.<ref name="Puzzles"/> She says she would like to bring him to the station "when everything settles down."<ref>{{Cite|When everything finally settles down, I'd like to bring him here...|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref> Zelda appears to have a certain amount of affection for Teacher.
