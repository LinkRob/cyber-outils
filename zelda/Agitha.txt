{{Term/Store|Agitha||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 276 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TP|-}}===
Agitha is a young, self-proclaimed princess around the age of 10.<ref name="TP Guide 15">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 15}}</ref> She lives in her [[Agitha's Castle|Castle]] in [[Castle Town]].<ref name="Castle">{{Cite|I don't know if they got lost on the way or what...but they're not here, that's for sure. If you see them, would you please bring them here to {{Color|TPHD Red|Agitha's Castle}}? And tell them the princess awaits!|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> She loves to dress in gothic-style clothes and pretends to be a princess of the insect kingdom in her own little castle.<ref name="TP Guide 15"/> She had sent out invitations to the [[Golden Bug]]s to come to her ball.<ref>{{Cite|I've invited the {{Color|TPHD Red|24 golden bugs}} of the world to the ball, but…|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> There are twelve types of bugs, each species having both a male and female for 24 bugs total. She believes that they have each gotten lost on their way, so she requests that [[Link]] find all 24 Bugs for her.<ref name="Castle"/> For each Bug Link gives her, she gives him 50 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Allow me to share this happiness with you!|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> For each time he completes a pair, she gives him 100 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yes! We have a {{Color|TPHD Red|pair}}! Don't those li'l bugs look happy?! Allow me to share this happiness with you!|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> When Link gives her the first Bug, she gives him the [[Big Wallet]].<ref>{{Cite|…You actually went bug hunting for me, didn't you? To be honest, I doubted you would… I guess some might consider me a poor-mannered princess… From now on, if you bring me insects, I'll share my {{Color|TPHD Red|happiness}} with you. You can keep it in this!|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> After taking all 24 bugs to Agitha, Link receives the [[Giant Wallet]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh! This means all the bugs have made it to the ball safely! Hee hee hee… And everyone has a partner. They all look so happy… You worked very hard. Your efforts will forever be passed down in the insect kingdom, brave knight. Please accept this declaration of honorable citizenship and the memento that goes along with it.|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> If Link leaves her castle when he still has Golden Bugs on hand, she will whisper that she knows he still has some, accompanied by a low growl.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I know you have bugs…}}|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref>

In {{TPHD}}, upon delivering half of the Bugs to her, she will give Link a [[Miiverse Stamp|Stamp]] of her that can be used in [[Miiverse]]. Additionally, if Link obtains a Wallet upgrade from the [[Cave of Shadows]] before obtaining both Wallet upgrades from Agitha, she will reward him with the [[Colossal Wallet]] upon bringing her all 24 Bugs.

Although she is a bit delusional, she is very kind and caring. She is one of the few characters who is not afraid of [[Wolf Link]], but rather, asks if he would like to play with her, as she thinks he is a puppy. She apologizes for not being able to speak puppy, indicating she thinks that Wolf Link wants to tell her something.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, hello, puppy… I'm sorry, but I don't speak puppy. Do you want to play?|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> Sometimes she can be found outside the southern gates of Hyrule Castle Town during the daytime before Link has brought her all 24 Bugs; she states she is looking for bugs in the flowers. This is the only time she carries her trademark parasol and basket with her. If Link speaks to her around sunset when she is walking back to the city, she says she is heading home and if Link wants to talk to her, he should come visit her at her posh castle.<ref>{{Cite|Why, hello! I'm so sorry, but I was just finishing up here. If there's something you need, you'll simply have to visit me in my posh castle on the other side of the gate.|Agitha|TP}}</ref>

==={{HW|-}}===
Agitha is a playable Warrior in {{HW}}. She fights primarily by summoning swarms of Golden Butterflies and giant Golden Beetles with her Parasol weapon to aid her in battle.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= June 24, 2014|retrieved=June 24, 2014|url= http://www.famitsu.com/news/201406/24055797.html|title= Agitha and Lana in Hyrule Warriors|site= Famitsu|type=}}</ref>

In [[Legend Mode]], Agitha first appears in the [[Twilight Field]] Scenario, "[[Land of Twilight]]". She becomes cornered by the [[Forces|Dark Force]]s' [[Bulblin]]s, but is shortly saved by [[Lana]]. She decides to aid the [[Hyrulean Forces]] to find the land's [[Gate of Souls]] by having her [[Goddess Butterfly]] lead the way. However, it is captured by [[Midna]] (whom Agitha refers to as "Miss Kitty"), who then uses it to locate [[Cia]].<ref>{{Cite Web|published= 2014-07-01|retrieved=July 1, 2014|url= https://twitter.com/zelda_musou/status/483906964401831936 |title= 「ゼルダ無双」公式 on Twitter |site= Twitter}}</ref> The Hyrulean Forces manage to defeat her and retrieve the Goddess Butterfly. Agitha accompanies the Hyrulean Forces as they combat [[Zant]] in the [[Palace of Twilight (Hyrule Warriors)|Palace of Twilight]], and later Cia and her Dark Forces.

Agitha is unlocked as a playable character through [[Adventure Mode]], though she is available to play immediately after the first Scenario in {{HW|DE}}. She is occasionally also fought as an enemy Warrior in certain Adventure Mode Battles, or sometimes in the form of "[[Dark Link|Dark]] Agitha". She occasionally drops her [[Agitha's Basket|Basket]] as a [[Material]] when defeated, and rarely her [[Agitha's Pendant|Pendant]].

==Trivia==
*The first time Link talks to Agitha, she thinks he is an insect. The insect she calls Link depends on the tunic he is wearing. It can be a giant grasshopper,<ref>{{Cite|...Oh, boy! I thought you were a giant grasshopper!|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> jewel beetle,<ref>{{Cite|...Oh, boy! I thought you were a giant jewel beetle!|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> or scarab beetle.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh, boy! I thought you were a giant scarab beetle!|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> The third result is impossible to see in the original game because Link cannot afford the Magic Armor's 598 Rupee price without the Big Wallet. In {{TPHD}}, it's possible to obtain the Big Wallet from the [[Cave of Shadows]] to obtain the Magic Armor before talking to her.
*In {{HW|-}}, Agitha claims to have learned how to pick locks from termites.<ref>{{Cite|Tee hee! A pair of termites once taught me that little door-opening trick!|Agitha|HWDE}}</ref>
