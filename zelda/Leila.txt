{{Term/Store|Leila||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>{{Note|Leila is also called '''Lila''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 106}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given both in-game and in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Leila}} is the daughter of {{Term|TMC|Simon|link}}.<ref name= "E 271"/> She attends {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}}, where she tends the flower beds with {{Term|TMC|Berry|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? This flower bed looks a little strange.|Leila|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Leila}} can be found in {{Term|TMC|Simon's Simulations|link}}, where she stays for the remainder of the game.

She is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Let's do it!|Leila|TMC}}</ref>
