==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Red Sparrow}}
Red Sparrows live among the mountains of the [[Hebra|Hebra region]], including the [[Hebra Mountains]] proper and the [[Tabantha Frontier]].<ref name="Compendium">{{Cite|These small birds live in the Hebra region and eat mainly nuts and wild plants.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They are chiefly herbivorous in diet, and feed mainly on nuts and wild plants.<ref name="Compendium"/> Their feathers are commonly used for decorations.<ref>{{Cite|Their bright- red feathers are often used as decorations.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When killed, they drop {{Plural|BotW|Chickaloo Tree Nut|link}}.
