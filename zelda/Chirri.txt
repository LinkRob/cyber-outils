==Characteristics==

{{Plural|SS|Chirri}} are small {{Plural|SS|Bird|link}} that can be found on the {{Term|SS|Surface|link}} in places such as {{Term|SS|Faron Woods|link}}. There are three varieties: yellow, orange and blue. {{Term|SS|Link|link}} can sneak up on them and catch them with his {{Term|SS|Bug Net|link}} to obtain a {{Term|SS|Bird Feather|link}}. If the caught {{Term|SS|Chirri}} is blue, he will receive a {{Term|SS|Blue Bird Feather|link}} instead.

{{Term|SS|Groose|link}} is initially surprised to see the small {{Plural|SS|Bird}} when he lands on the {{Term|SS|Surface}} because the only ones found in the {{Term|SS|Sky|link}} are the larger {{Plural|SS|Loftwing|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|WHOA! B-birds? TINY birds?! Wh-what... ARE they?!|Groose|SS}}</ref>
