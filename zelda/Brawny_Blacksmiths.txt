"{{Term/Store|Brawny Blacksmiths||Series, HWAoC}}" is a {{Term|HWAoC|Quest|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Brawny Blacksmiths'''|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|HWAoC|Brawny Blacksmiths}}" is a {{Term|HWAoC|Quest}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must provide blacksmiths with five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ironshroom}} to assist them in their work. Afterwards, the blacksmiths are strengthened and form the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Blacksmith Guild}}, a group able to make and fuse {{Plural|HWAoC|Weapon|link}} for the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}.
