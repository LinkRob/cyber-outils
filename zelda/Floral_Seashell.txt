The '''Floral Seashell''' is an item from {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Floral Seashell|An elegant seashell with lovely colors. It will be perfect for the pendant that the Great Fairy requested.<ref name="Floral Seashell">{{Cite|An elegant seashell with lovely colors. It will be perfect for the pendant that the Great Fairy requested.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Floral Seashell is one of the items required to complete [[The Great Fairy's Request]] [[Side Quest|side quest]]. It can be obtained from the [[Sea Zora Shop]] in [[Sea Zora Village]] by trading an [[Unfortunate Smoothie]] to the shopkeeper.
