The {{Term/Store|Slime Key||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an item in {{LA}}.<ref name="Encyclopedia"/>

==Location and Uses==
Initially, the {{Term|LADX|Slime Key}} is owned by [[Prince Richard]]. When {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} travels to [[Kanalet Castle]] and speaks to him, he will ask {{Term|LADX|Link}} to bring him back five {{Plural|LADX|Golden Leaf|link}} in exchange for the {{Term|LADX|Slime Key|display=Key}}.<ref>{{Cite|Salutations! You wouldn't know by the look of me, but I used to live in the castle! My servants went berserk and I was forced to flee to my villa... So, you want the key to Ukuku Prairie, do you? I may be able to help you... Let's make... a deal, shall we? I want you to retrieve the Golden Leaf I left behind in the castle when I fled...|Prince Richard|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|At last! You've got the final Golden Leaf! Now go and see Richard about that key... |N/A|LADX}}</ref> When {{Term|LADX|Link}} returns with the five {{Plural|LADX|Golden Leaf}}, Prince Richard will let him through his back garden where an [[Owl Statue]] is located.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh! Tres Bien! I see you have recovered all of the leaves! Now, move this box and you will find your reward! |Prince Richard|LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LADX|Link}} must use the [[Shovel]] to dig at the Statue's feet and find the {{Term|LADX|Slime Key|display=Key}}.<ref name="Location"/>

By inserting the {{Term|LADX|Slime Key}} into the {{Term|LADX|Slime Keyhole|link}}, to unlock the access to the {{Term|LADX|Key Cavern}}.<ref name="Encyclopedia"/>

==Nomenclature==
The {{Term|LADX|Slime Key}} may be named after [[Slime Eye]], the [[Boss]] of the {{Term|LADX|Key Cavern|link}}, with which it bears a close resemblance.
