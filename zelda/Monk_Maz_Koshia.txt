==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} is a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} who is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, appearing during the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}'s goal is to test those who wish to undergo the [[Divine Beast Tamer's Trial]].<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I am Maz Koshia. I now address the one who wishes to master a Divine Beast...}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> He first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} inside the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}, where he bestows the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator|link}} to him as the first part of his Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To undertake this task requires unwavering dedication. That weapon defeats foes with one hit. However, the reverse is also true, as its wielder will also fall to a single strike. You can use this weapon's ability only twice within a set period of time...only when it is glowing. When enough time passes, the weapon will regain its sheen along with its power.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that to complete this part of the Trial, he must travel to the four marked locations on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and defeat the bands of {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} there.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|At the four marked locations within the Great Plateau...defeat all monsters present to prove your power. If you are not up to the challenge, leave this plateau. The weapon will return here, and the trial will end.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has done this, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} will congratulate {{Term|BotW|Link}} on his progress and reveal that this task was the first of many in the Trial.<ref name="Weapon">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I am Maz Koshia. You have done well to conquer the trials laid before you. You are Hyrule's beacon of hope. But your trails have only just begun. The divine weapon shall lead you...}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} continues, saying that the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} will guide him to the next steps.<ref name="Weapon"/> This causes the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} to split into four glowing orbs, which scatter to various parts of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} urges {{Term|BotW|Link}} to follow them in order to continue.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have proven your power, a new journey awaits. Go to the four locations that will be revealed to you, and reach for greater heights than ever before.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}," "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song|link}}," "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song|link}}," and "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}," {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} will call to {{Term|BotW|Link}} once more, instructing him to return to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}} to face his final trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I am Maz Koshia. I must commend you for conquering the trial thus far. The door to the final trial is now open at the Shrine of Resurrection. Go, and face your destiny.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} will greet him, instructing him to return the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|link}} he found it in to reveal the passageway to his {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the brave soul who wishes to master a Divine Beast... You have done well to return to this place of beginnings. You have overcome many challenges thus far. Now, allow me to bestow upon you one final trial. Return the Sheikah Slate to the terminal you first obtained it from. Do so, and the final passageway will reveal itself...}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> Upon doing so, the door closest, and the chamber begins to descend.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Descending... Please beware of tremors.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref>

Once the chamber has fully descended, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} will welcome {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}}, explaining that he can be found here.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Welcome, hero. You have overcome many trials to reach me. Now it is time to face the final trial. This trial is a complex labyrinth.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} guides {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the labyrinth's {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}}, after which he leaves {{Term|BotW|Link}} to complete the labyrinth of his own accord.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To start, you will need a map. You may obtain map information from the Guidance Stone.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> Upon reaching the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}, he once again greets {{Term|BotW|Link}}, revealing that there is one remaining part of the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have proven to possess the power of a true hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia... I offer this final trial!}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} stands up and {{Term|BotW|Warping|link|display=Warps}} the two of them away to a platform in the sky, where they engage in combat.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Let us begin.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} defeats {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}, he will shrink to his normal size and commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for overcoming all of the obstacles he faced.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You faced that challenge with great courage. You have erased all doubt from my mind. You are a true hero.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} will activate the flying structure that they are on, causing the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero|link}} to rise from within.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|As such, you were destined to take hold of this ancient masterpiece...}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} explains that the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero}} is a masterpiece of innovation that was intended for the "one true hero".<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|That which you have obtained...is a masterpiece of innovation, created for the one true hero.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> With his role served, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before returning him to the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Hero of Hyrule... May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Monk Maz Koshia}}
{{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} boasts a wide array of tactics that he may use in his fight against {{Term|BotW|Link}}. Like a {{Term|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} can magically teleport and strike {{Term|BotW|Link}} from above with his {{Term|BotW|Sword}}, or he may use a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} and shoot at {{Term|BotW|Link}} from the air. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} can use {{Plural|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}} to strike {{Term|BotW|Link}}. He's also capable of using [[Earthwake]], just like [[Yiga Blademaster]]s.

After {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}'s health has been reduced by 25%, he will retreat and split himself into nine copies. Each of these copies can damage {{Term|BotW|Link}}, though only one of them can sustain more than one hit. In order to advance, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must find the correct copy of {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} and attack him. The copies will work in unison to attack {{Term|BotW|Link}}, until they are destroyed. When all of the copies have been destroyed, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} may summon another set of them.

Once {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}'s health has been reduced to half, he will retreat again and magically enlarge himself while flying in the air. At this stage, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} can use charged laser attacks to strike {{Term|BotW|Link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} deflects one of these lasers back at {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will be temporarily stunned, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to attack. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} may also summon a series of eight metallic {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder|lin}}. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} will sequentially cast these {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder}} at {{Term|BotW|Link}} in an attempt to hurt him. Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} will begin charging a {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} storm, which will strike each of the {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder}}. Much like with {{Term|BotW|Thunderblight Ganon|link}}'s metal spikes, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} to direct one of the {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder}} near {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}, causing him to be struck by his own {{Term|BotW|Lightning}}. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} can also magically tilt the battle stage before summoning the {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder}}, which will roll towards the bottom of the slope before falling off. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} can also stomp the ground to cause a tornado to chase {{Term|BotW|Link}} around. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is hit by the tornado, he will be struck by a strong pulse from the ground.

With a quarter of his health remaining, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} will ground himself. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets close to {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}'s feet, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will stomp the ground to damage {{Term|BotW|Link}}. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} can also summon two miniature copies of himself, who will attack {{Term|BotW|Link}} in a similar manner to the second phase of the fight.

During the battle, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can distract {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} with {{Term|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}}, leaving the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} temporarily vulnerable to attack. This can only be done once.

==Strategy==
During Maz Koshia's first phase, it is best to run around, dodging and flurry rushing when the monk is vulnerable. During Phase 2, when Maz Koshia spawns his clones, stand in the center of the ring of clones. Use Urbosa's fury and the real Monk will be electrocuted. Run up to him and hit him as much as possible. Do this three times. If he still has health, then place a banana in the middle and he will try to eat it. Attack him then. During Monk Maz Koshia's final phase, equip a multishot bow such as the Savage Lynel Bow or Duplex Bow and Revali's Gale right in front of him. Go into bullet time and shoot his face as much as you can with arrows. After doing this 2-3 times, the Monk will be down.
