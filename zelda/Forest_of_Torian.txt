The '''Forest of Torian''' is a location in {{ZA}}. It is a densely wooded forest that makes up half of central {{Term|ZA|Tolemac|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|ZA|Forest of Torian}} is a large, densely wooded area that covers the northern half of central {{Term|ZA|Tolemac|link}}, and makes up most of the area in that region. It is vastly covered by trees that resemble spruce. Two river streams flow through the area; one originating from the {{Term|ZA|Forest of Canvula|link}} to the west, and one from the north. The two rivers eventually meet, before splitting off again into the {{Term|ZA|Meadow of Skotness|link}} and towards {{Term|ZA|Vendoss|link}} to the east. The Forest can be entered from the south by crossing the river from {{Term|ZA|Great Wimbich|link}}, or from the village of {{Term|ZA|Ubato Hills|link}} in the east.

The {{Term|ZA|Forest of Torian}} is the largest woodland in {{Term|ZA|Tolemac}}, and its many pathways and identical-looking trees give the Forest an almost maze-like appearance. It is most notably the location of the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Illusion|link}}, which is located in the northwest and is in the appearance of a carnival tent. To the northeast are the ruins of what appear to have been a fortress or tower. It is here that {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda|link}} meets {{Term|ZA|Characters in Zelda's Adventure|section=Sir Basil|display=Sir Basil|link}}, who gives her the {{Term|ZA|Spells of Zelda's Adventure|section=Broadsword|display=Broadsword|link}} {{Term|ZA|Spells of Zelda's Adventure|display=Spell|link}}.

Close to the Forest's center is a {{Term|ZA|Fairy Fountain|link}} housing a {{Term|ZA|Fairy|link}}, which will replenish Zelda's health when approached. On the eastern side of the north river, an underground tunnel can be found. A {{Term|ZA|Heart Container|section=Philips CD-i Games|display=Crystal Heart|link}} is found deep in its burrows.

===White Steed Lodge===
The '''White Steed Lodge''',<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote={{Exp|To defeat the Shrine King Avianna, you need [the] feather-weapon. You got this in the second region from Waldensop, who was outside of the White Steed Lodge with two horses.|Om de Shrine King Avianna te verslaan, heb je het Feather-wapen nodig. Die heb je in de tweede regio gekregen van Waldensop, die buiten de White Steed Lodge stond met twee paarden.}}|magazine= CD Interactief|volume= 3|issue= 10|date= October 1996|publisher=HUB Uitgevers|page=40-41}}</ref> also known as the '''Thieves' Den''',<ref>{{Cite|Good morn, lady. A noble person such as yourself on foot-end? If it's rest you seek, you'll get precious little comfort in this thieves' den, I'll tell you.|Waldensop|ZA}}</ref> is an inn that is located within the Forest of Torian. Similar to the {{Term|ZA|Mobilin's Head Inn|link}} in the {{Term|ZA|Plain of Andor|link}}, it is a lodge where weary travelers stop to rest, however, its guests are known to be thieves.<ref>{{Cite|Be careful if you go inside, milady! Some people in these parts would rob you the same as look you, they would.|Waldensop|ZA}}</ref> The Lodge is full when Zelda visits, however, its innkeeper says she is welcomed to stay and rest. Outside the Lodge, a pair of horses can be seen tied up.

A man named {{Term|ZA|Characters in Zelda's Adventure|section=Waldensop|display=Waldensop|link}} stands outside of the inn, and warns Zelda not to go inside in fear that she will be robbed. When spoken to a second time, he gives Zelda {{Term|ZA|Aviana|link}}'s {{Term|ZA|Spells of Zelda's Adventure|section=Feather|display=Feather|link}}, a Spell, as a token of his affection.
