==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:TWW Tingle Bomb Icon.png|left|40px]]
Tingle Bombs are special [[Bomb]]s that can be purchased from the [[Tingle Tuner]] for 10 [[Rupee]]s.<ref name="TWW Inventory"/> Tingle Bombs can be placed remotely from Link via the Tingle Tuner. When placed, Tingle will extract 10 Rupees from Link's [[Wallet]] and, should Link be standing on the designated spot, warn him to run away on the Tingle Tuner before it detonates.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|TWW TT Red|Danger, Fairy! Run!}}}}|Tingle|TWW}}</ref>

Tingle Bombs are required to reveal the [[Treasure Chest]]s housing the [[Tingle Statue]]s. After Link has collected the Tingle Statues, Tingle may drop one on Link as punishment for hitting them enough times,<ref>{{Cite|Ah ha ha ha... {{Color|TWW TT Purple|Mr. Fairy!}} Stop that! {{Color|TWW TT Red|❤}}|Tingle|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Aren't you done yet, {{Color|TWW TT Purple|Mr. Fairy}}?|Tingle|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Stop it, {{Color|TWW TT Purple|fairy}}!}}|Tingle|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|.............................................................................. That's mean.|Tingle|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Stop that! Now! Stop that! Now! Stop that! Now! Stop that! Now! Stop that! Now! This instant!|Tingle|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|TWW TT Red|Grrragh! Enough, already!}}}} It should not come to this!{{Color|TWW TT Red|❤}}|Tingle|TWW}}</ref> provided the Tingle Tuner is in use at the time. Tingle will also immediately drop a Tingle Bomb on Link if a Tingle Statue is thrown into the [[Great Sea]].

Link can use a Tingle Bomb on the [[Jar]] on the ledge to the west below the [[Savage Labyrinth]]'s entrance to cause a larger Jar to appear. The larger Jar contains an [[Orange Rupee]], valued at 100 Rupees. From that point on, both Jars will remain and reappear whenever Link revisits them.<!-- Verify that the smaller Jar needs to be destroyed. -->

Due to the Tingle Tuner being replaced by [[Tingle Bottle]]s, Tingle Bombs are not available in {{TWWHD}}. To compensate for this, regular bombs will work in place of the Tingle Bombs in {{TWWHD|-}}. However, as Tingle Bombs in classic versions of {{TWW|-}} can be used before Link has obtained regular bombs, Link can collect the [[Dragon Tingle Statue]] and [[Forbidden Tingle Statue]] before ever obtaining bombs. Link must instead return to [[Dragon Roost Cavern]] and the [[Forbidden Woods]] after obtaining Bombs to collect the respective Tingle Statues in {{TWWHD|-}}.
