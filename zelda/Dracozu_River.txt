{{Term/Store|Dracozu River||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Dracozu River'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Dracozu River}} is located in the [[Faron Grasslands]]. It is most easily accessed from the {{Term|BotW|Damel Forest|link}}. It is a meandering river typified by the presence of numerous, serpentine [[Dragon]] statues along its banks, and by its dense, tropical forest surroundings. The location is tied to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "[[The Serpent's Jaws]]", given to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}, who is found in [[Pagos Woods]], in the form of an [[Ancient Song]].<ref>{{Cite|Where the forest dragon splays its jaws, A shrine sleeps with noble cause.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Dracozu River|display=River}} itself is shaped like a Dragon.<ref>{{Cite|A traveler who got lost in Faron Woods said he saw a dragon-shaped river, which made me really curious..|Totsuna|BotW}}</ref>

A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} sits submerged at the southernmost tip of the {{Term|BotW|Dracozu River|display=River}}. A second {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} rewards the same prize; this one can be found upriver, underwater, at the river bend nearest to [[Dracozu Lake]].
