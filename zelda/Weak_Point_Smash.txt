{{Term/Store|Weak Point Smash|Weak Point Smashes|Series, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are abilities in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Once the gauge is completely reduced, you'll be able to perform a powerful {{Color|HWDE Green|Weak Point Smash}}.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Stub|HWDE}}
{{Plural|HWDE|Weak Point Smash}} are attacks that can be pulled off when the {{Term|HWDE|Weak Point Gauge}} of an {{Term|HWDE|Enemy|link}} has been fully depleted, allowing the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior|link}} to do fatal or otherwise heavy damage to the target.

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
Weak Point Smashes return in {{HWAoC}}, although the player is now required to press the X button to perform the attack once the enemy's gauge is depleted, instead of performing the attack automatically. On the other hand, the Weak Point Smash now does a lot more damage to large enemies, as well as affecting other enemies nearby, including other large enemies that have had their gauges emptied at the same time, and who will be affected as if they were directly targeted by the attack.
