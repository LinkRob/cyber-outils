The {{Term/Store|Rescue Knight|Rescue Knights|Series, SS|plural}} are an organization in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 32}}</ref>{{Name Ref|SSHD}}

==Biography==
The Rescue Knights patrol the airs above [[Skyloft]] and rescue those that fall off the edge, often giving them a stern warning to be more careful.<ref>{{Cite|Watch your step there! You can't always depend on knights like me to swoop in and save you.|Albat|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Watch your step there! Haven't they taught you at the academy that knights must always be vigilant? Sharpen up, or you'll never make it to knighthood!|Hauk|SS}}</ref> They appear at the beginning of the [[Wing Ceremony]], trailing colored smoke behind them. Only seniors who graduate from the [[Knight Academy]] are allowed to become Rescue Knights,<ref>{{Cite|All set for today's big Wing Ceremony? If you come away with a win, you'll move on up to the senior class. Then after you graduate, you get to be a rescue knight. Those knights sure do keep order here in Skyloft, and I don't have to tell you that it's a pretty big deal to become one.|N/A|SS}}</ref> but senior students at the Knight Academy such as [[Karane]], [[Pipit]] and [[Link]] get to wear knight uniforms before they graduate.<ref>{{Cite|Just think: if you win today, you'll become a senior like us and you'll be a very big step closer to graduating as a knight. You'll even get a knight's uniform like I'm wearing. Something tells me you'll be pretty handsome in it!|Karane|SS}}</ref> Only certified Rescue Knights are allowed to pilot their [[Loftwing]]s at night,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, and don't let me catch you going out flying at night. Only trained knights such as myself should be doing that. You take care now!|Kyte|SS}}</ref> and their Loftwings are equipped with a flashlight on their headgear.

==Trivia==
*[[Croo]] boasts that his grandson is a Rescue Knight.<ref>{{Cite|Don't know if you've met 'em or not, but the rescue knights save anyone clumsy enough to fall off of Skyloft. Only the Knight Academy's seniors are invited to join that group. They're the best of the best! [...] Guess I should tell you my grandson's one of 'em!|Croo|SS}}</ref> However, the identity of his grandson is unknown.
*The three Rescue Knights appear in green, red and blue attire. This is a reference to the common colors of [[Link]]'s [[Tunic]]s.
