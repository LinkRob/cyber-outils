==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SS|Spiral Ivy}} are a type of plant that typically grow on branches and form a curled shape. When struck, {{Plural|SS|Spiral Ivy}} uncurl and can be used as {{Plural|SS|Rope (Object)|link}}. {{Plural|SS|Spiral Ivy}} appear {{Term|SS|In the Woods|link}}, at the {{Term|SS|Viewing Platform|link}}, and within the {{Term|SS|Skyview Temple|link}}.
