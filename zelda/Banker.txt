The {{Term/Store|Banker||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|MM3D|Banker}} runs the [[Clock Town Bank]] in [[West Clock Town]] or [[South Clock Town]] in {{MM3D}}. He recognizes his clients by stamping them with invisible ink.<ref>{{Cite|I won't forget your deposits. Let me stamp you with my special ink. Hey, relax! It doesn't leave any marks, and it's not gonna hurt. There! Now I'll know you when I see you!|Banker|MM}}</ref> Therefore, he recognizes Link in all his forms. The {{Term|MM3D|Banker}} keeps track of Link's balance even after he plays the "[[Song of Time]]" to return to the [[First Day]].<ref>{{Cite|Excuse me, but let me take a look at you... Ah, yes...Link. If I remember, you're the little guy who deposited <Rupees>.|Banker|MM}}</ref> This is the only way Link can keep all the Rupees he has collected after traveling back in time.

The {{Term|MM3D|Banker}} awards Link with gifts based on how much the latter deposits.<ref>{{Cite|So, for a limited time, I'll give you a special gift based on how much you deposit.|Banker|MM}}</ref> These are the [[Adult Wallet]] at 200 Rupees, a 5 Rupees interest at 1,000, and finally a [[Piece of Heart]] at 5,000.<ref>{{Cite|Well, little guy, here's your special gift. Take it!|Banker|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well, little guy, I can't take any more deposits. Sorry, but this is all I can give you.|Banker|MM}}</ref> The {{Term|MM3D|Banker}} will stop taking deposits if Link reaches 5,000 Rupees in his account. The {{Term|MM3D|Banker}} charges 4 Rupees for withdrawing at night;<ref>{{Cite|...You know, at this time of day there's a 4 Rupee service charge on withdrawals!|Banker|MM}}</ref> during the day, it is free.

The {{Term|MM3D|Banker}} remains at his post until the end of the [[Final Day]], applauding Link for staying behind as well.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? You need somethin' on a day like this? You haven't evacuated yet? You're amazing, little guy!|Banker|MM}}</ref> If the hero requests a withdrawal on the Final Day, the {{Term|MM3D|Banker}} tells him not to believe the rumors about the falling [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] before proceeding.<ref>{{Cite|Look, little guy, if it's 'cause of the bad rumors going around, forget it! They're just rumors!|Banker|MM}}</ref>

The {{Term|MM3D|Banker}} is outspoken and often makes impertinent comments during transactions.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? It's a waste to take out such a tiny bit!|Banker|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Aw, you're taking out all that? If you spend it like that, it'll all be gone before you know it!|Banker|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's it? That ain't nothing at all, big spender!|Banker|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whaaat?!? You're really gonna give me that much? You're one rich little guy!|Banker|MM}}</ref> If Link attempts to overdraw his account, he will get mad at him.<ref>{{Cite|...You haven't deposited that many Rupees, so that much isn't available for withdrawal. Do the math! What are you trying to do?|Banker|MM}}</ref> When Link exceeds the deposit limit, the {{Term|MM3D|Banker}} makes a discriminatory, albeit positive, remark about his age.<ref>{{Cite|Excuuuse me! But I can't take anymore deposits! Sheesh, little guy! You're young, but you sure are serious about saving! I'm impressed!|Banker|MM}}</ref> He calls Link "little guy" rather than by name, even when speaking to [[Zora Link|Zora]] or [[Goron Link]].
