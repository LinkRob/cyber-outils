'''Desma Four-Legged Spiders''' are {{Plural|FPTRR|Enemy}} in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} They appear in {{Term|FPTRR|Desma's Labyrinth}} and are similar in appearance and behavior to {{Plural|FPTRR|Tektite|link}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|FPTRR|Desma Four-Legged Spider}} are common throughout {{Term|FPTRR|Desma's Labyrinth}} and usually appear in small groups, sometimes emerging from the ground in front of {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}}. Like {{Plural|FPTRR|Tektite}}, they move by jumping and will leap around the room until they spot {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}, at which point they will aggressively hop after him until he leaves their territory. With a {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard|link}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} should not have much trouble with these enemies, although they are moderately strong. Like other {{Term|FPTRR|Dungeon}} {{Plural|FPTRR|Enemy}}, they drop {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}.

==Trivia==
*{{Plural|FPTRR|Desma Four-Legged Spider}} not only behave like {{Plural|FPTRR|Tektite}}, they also appear solely in a mountainous region, albeit inside a {{Term|FPTRR|Dungeon}} rather than its surrounding foothills. Unlike {{Plural|FPTRR|Tektite}} they appear to have no mouths and also have a total of three eyes, with one on top of their bodies and one on their backs, rather than just one at the front. Each eye appears to focus independently.
*{{Plural|FPTRR|Dark Four-Legged Spider|link}} are similar creatures from the same game, but do not jump, and instead crawl across the ground. They also only have one eye. Whether the monsters are related or not is unclear.
