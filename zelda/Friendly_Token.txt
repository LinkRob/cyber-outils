{{Term/Store|Friendly Token|Friendly Tokens|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 124"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token}} are {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Timeless Tunic|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Tri Suit|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}. Appearing as golden coins depicting the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero|link}}, they are said to symbolize the bond between friends.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TFH Blue|Friendly Tokens}} are a symbol of the bond shared between friends. Beautiful, ain't it?!|Faux Hero|TFH}}</ref>

Prior to the Ver. 2.0.0 update, {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token}} could only be obtained by speaking to the {{Term|TFH|Faux Hero|link}} after playing the Local Play or Download Play modes with other players and completing at least one {{Term|TFH|Level|link}}. After meeting the requirements and exiting {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}, the {{Term|TFH|Faux Hero}}, who begins the game with 15 {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token}} and brags about the hero status they bring,<ref>{{Cite|I've got 15 {{Color|TFH Blue|Friendly Tokens}}. Count 'em, chum!<br/>What? Don't you have any {{Color|TFH Blue|Friendly Tokens}}, chum? Aha! Guess you're not a real hero like ME!|Faux Hero|TFH}}</ref> will give {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} one of his {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token|display=Tokens}} for each player he ventured with into the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}.

Following the Ver. 2.0.0 update, {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token}} become available from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} after the curse on {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}} has been lifted,<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Friendly Tokens can be obtained from the street vendor after Princess Styla’s curse has been lifted. |retrieved=May 24, 2019 |title=How to Update The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes |site=Nintendo Support}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whoa! You did it!!<br/>So you finally broke the princess's awful curse, did ya? Good one.<br/>This town's finally lively again, and I'm turning a fine profit. It's all thanks to you, pal.<br/>Oh, that reminds me. I've decided to finally put "those" up for sale, starting today. What are "those"?<br/>They're called...<br/>{{Color|TFH Blue|Friendly Tokens}}!!|Street Merchant|TFH}}</ref> at a cost of 2000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} apiece. However, their availability as part of the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}}'s daily stock is random.
