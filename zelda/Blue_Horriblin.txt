'''Blue Horriblins''' are a type of [[Horriblin]] in {{TotK}}. They are stronger than their standard red variant.

Upon defeat, the Blue Horriblin can drop [[Horriblin Claws]], Guts and a [[Blue Horriblin Horn]]. Common locations include Hyrule Caves and Death Mountain Depths. Blue Horriblins can be deceived by both the Majora's Mask and Horriblin Mask clothing items.
