{{Term/Store|Library Book|Library Books|Series, TMC|plural}} are {{Plural|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Library Book: A Hyrulean Bestiary|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
As their name suggests, they are three books that belong to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}. However, the books are long overdue, and {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must retrieve them from their owners in order to open the way to {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} who was able to enter the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}} long ago and now lives in the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}}.<ref>{{Cite|You what? You want to enter the Temple of Droplets? I have heard rumors that one Minish actually did find a way in, long ago... That Minish is Elder Librari, and he is in semi-retirement in the town library.|Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey, hey! This is no good! No good at all! A human borrowed a book, and now none of us can get back to Elder Librari! How will he get by all alone? I'm so worried... I'm sure the librarian knows something about where those books went... But there's nothing we wee Minish can do about it!|Minish|TMC}}</ref> Once all the books are returned to the librarian, they will be put back on the shelf, allowing {{Term|TMC|Link}} to speak to {{Term|TMC|Librari}} and receive the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}} after passing his trial.<ref>{{Cite|You know, Link, you've now returned all of our overdue books! Now I can finally get that bookshelf on the second floor in order! Hey, Sturgeon! Books to shelve!!|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref>

In order to find out who checked out the books, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must speak to {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|link}}.

==Quest Books==
==={{Term|TMC|A Hyrulean Bestiary}}===
{{Main|A Hyrulean Bestiary}}
[[File:TMC A Hyrulean Bestiary Sprite.png|left|30px]]
{{Term|TMC|A Hyrulean Bestiary}} is the first of three books that {{Term|TMC|Link}} must retrieve. {{Term|TMC|Julietta|link}} took it home in hopes of learning more about her new {{Term|TMC|Cat|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Take A Hyrulean Bestiary, for example. Been gone for ages. Checked out by a girl with a cat. She said she had a new cat at her house. Wanted to learn more about it, she did.|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} must enter the {{Term|TMC|House}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} with a purple roof and climb the chimney as while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized to walk across to the {{Term|TMC|House}} in blue. Once inside, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must push the {{Term|TMC|Library Book|display=Book}} off the shelf, allowing him to take it back to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}}.

===''Legend of the Picori''===
{{Main|Legend of the Picori}}
[[File:TMC Legend of the Picori Sprite.png|left|30px]]
The second book that {{Term|TMC|Link}} must find, the {{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori}}, is located in {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left|link}}'s {{Term|TMC|House}}. When {{Term|TMC|Link}} speaks to {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left}} about the book, he claims not to know where it is.<ref>{{Cite|My name is {{Color|TMC Blue|Dr. Left}}. You're here about that book, {{Color|TMC Red|Legend of the Picori}}, aren't you? Well, I hate to say this, but I haven't seen it in several days. Perhaps a mischievous little mouse has taken it? Well, I'm sure it's in this house somewhere. I just don't know where.|Dr. Left|TMC}}</ref> To push the {{Plural|TMC|Bookshelf|link}} in his {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House|link|display=House}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must shrink to {{Term|TMC|Minish}} size and enter the {{Term|TMC|Fountain|link}} to obtain the {{Plural|TMC|Power Bracelet|link}}. Afterwards, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can align the {{Plural|TMC|Bookshelf}} in a way that allows him to go into the rafters. {{Term|TMC|Link}} will find {{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori}} here, but he must first help a {{Term|TMC|Town Minish}} push it down below.<ref>{{Cite|Ho! Ha! Hey!! Phew... I want to return this book by dropping it down below. It's impossible without some help pushing... But my brother is so busy with a book of his own that he won't help me out!|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> Once this is done, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can retrieve the {{Term|TMC|Library Book|display=Book}} and give it back to {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)}}.

==={{Term|TMC|A History of Masks}}===
{{Main|A History of Masks}}
[[File:TMC A History of Masks Sprite.png|left|30px]]
'''{{Term|TMC|A History of Masks}}''' is the last book needed to reach {{Term|TMC|Librari}}, and it can be found in {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's Lakeside Cabin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The mayor took off for his cabin by the lake clutching a book. We saw him go... But when he came back, he didn't have the book anymore. I'm sure he just forgot it at the lake cabin.|Minish|TMC}}</ref> Located in {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, the book is on top of a bookshelf; {{Term|TMC|Link}} must push it off while {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized.
