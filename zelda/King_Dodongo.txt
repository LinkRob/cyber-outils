==Characteristics==
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|King Dodongo}}<br/>This is a huge Dodongo that eats anything! Give it a shock, and finish it off with your sword!
}}
[[File:KingDodongoOOT.png|thumb|left|Link's confrontation with King Dodongo in {{OoT|-}}|235x235px]]
[[Link]] must defeat King Dodongo in order to obtain the [[Goron's Ruby]] from [[Darunia]].

During his battle in the boss room of Dodongo's Cavern, Link falls into a deep hole containing a wide area with lava in the center. The young [[Hero of Time]] turns around as sees King Dodongo walking toward him as he goes into a defensive stance. The camera then turns to show the monster in full view as it [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_KingDodongo_Scream1.wav roars] at him. King Dodongo attacks by inhaling air and releasing a blast of damaging flame that goes around half of the room if allowed to inhale completely, which inflicts half a [[Heart Container|Heart]] in damage. As with [[Dodongo|regular Dodongos]], if Link is hit while carrying the [[Deku Shield]], it will burn and become lost. Therefore, it is advised to equip the [[Hylian Shield]] if obtained. During this time, Link can toss [[Bomb]]s, or a nearby [[Bomb Flower]], into its mouth. If successful, the bomb will explode inside, causing King Dodongo to collapse and become stunned. King Dodongo is vulnerable to [[Sword]] attacks at this time, the [[Critical Point]] of the battle. Regardless if Link does not apply enough damage or successfully strikes the boss during the Critical Point, King Dodongo will rear up, curl into a ball, and roll around the arena. If he stands close to the lava, the external walls, or hides beneath or behind the [[Shield]], Link can avoid damage. King Dodongo hits an arena wall at the end of its rampage; it unfolds and moves about until Link is close enough to target again, repeating the cycle.

When defeated, King Dodongo roars in pain and curls into a ball once more. However, it wobbles as it moves and as King Dodongo hits the wall a second time, it rolls into the lava pit. The lava hardens, trapping King Dodongo, half-submerged and burned to death. Link receives a [[Heart Container]] as a reward.

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
In {{HW}}, King Dodongo appears as a giant boss that is encountered in several [[Legend Mode]] and [[Adventure Mode]] stages. In Legend Mode, it appears as a boss in the Scenarios "[[The Armies of Ruin]]", "[[The Water Temple]]", "[[Ganondorf's Return]]", "[[The Dragon of the Caves]]", and "[[The Other Hero]]". It is not required to defeat King Dodongo in all Scenarios.

King Dodongo differs from its previous incarnations as it bears golden scales, two red horns on both sides of its head, red claws, and red crystals running along its back and joints. This could be a reference to the [[Dodongo (Boss)|Dodongo]]'s appearance in {{TLoZ}}. In "The Water Temple" and "The Other Hero" it appears more closely resembling its {{OoT|-}} appearance. King Dodongo attacks with a variety of moves such as curling and rolling into a ball, stomping its claws, roaring loudly, breathing a stream of flames, or by preparing a powerful fire blast by inhaling deeply. These attacks also harm nearby enemy troops. Because of its immense size, it is impervious to nearly all attacks. It becomes vulnerable when Bombs are thrown down its throat as it sucks in air, which stuns it and exposes its weak point gauge.

King Dodongo may drop its [[King Dodongo's Claws|Claws]] as a silver [[Material]] or its [[King Dodongo's Crystal|Crystal]] as a gold Material when defeated.
{{Ambig|End}}

==Trivia==
*In {{OoT}}, King Dodongo and [[Volvagia]] share a unique battle theme that is different from the one that plays for all other standard [[Bosses in Ocarina of Time|bosses]]. According to [[Nintendo Music]], the track's name is "Dinosaur Boss Battle".
*King Dodongo can be defeated very quickly with the use of [[Deku Stick|Deku Sticks]], as it has the lowest amount of health compared to all other bosses in the game, with just 4 hit points. Therefore, it will be slain with only two [[Jump Slash|Jump Attacks]] with said item.{{Fact|OoT3D}}<!--The previous reference here linked to a YouTube video (https://youtu.be/ZAUkdQtUtpw) that is no longer available.-->
*[[Scaldera]], a boss from {{SS}}, almost perfectly parallels with King Dodongo from {{OoT|-}}. Both serve as the second boss of their games, are large beasts, can curl up into a ball and roll around in order to move faster, and both live in a [[Death Mountain|volcano]] or [[Eldin (Region)|fiery area]] and are commonly associated with [[fire]]. Furthermore, they are also defeated by [[Link]] by throwing a [[Bomb]] into the mouth of the boss as it sucks up air to prepare an attack, allowing him to attack them while they are stunned.
*The action of King Dodongo of curling and rolling around to move around the arena faster can be considered a reference to [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog Sonic the Hedgehog] from Sega's ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_series Sonic]'' series.
*The [[Unused Content|unused]] [[Arwing]] enemy based on the ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_(series)|Star Fox]]'' series uses the sound effect of King Dodongo [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_KingDodongo_Inhale.wav inhaling] whenever it is defeated and crashes down to the ground.
