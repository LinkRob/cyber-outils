{{Term/Store|EX Alert: Forgotten Temple||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|EX Vicious Monster Battle|link}} in {{HWAoC}} included in the first downloadable content pack, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Alert: Forgotten Temple|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Alert: Forgotten Temple}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} unlocked by completing the {{Plural|HWAoC|Quest}} {{Term|HWAoC|The Ultimate Relic}} and {{Term|HWAoC|The Relic of Legend}}. In the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}}. It can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} besides {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of a {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Giant Ancient Core|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Claw|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|High-Yield Ancient Furnace|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}. Clearing the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} also unlocks {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}} as a playable {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}.
