{{Term/Store|Ecru||Series, ALBW}} is the name of one of the 20 possible {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} that can appear in {{ALBW}} without being met by {{Term|Series|StreetPass|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's Ecru!<br/>This {{Color|ALBW Blue|Shadow Link}} has come via {{Color|ALBW Red|StreetPass}} and awaits battle!|Shadow Link|ALBW}}</ref>{{Note|All Shadow Links claim to have come via StreetPass, even when they are pre-generated.}}

==Overview==
Once {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} has spoken to {{Term|ALBW|Gramps|link}} and enabled {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link Battle|link}}, a new signpost signifying the arrival of a {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} will appear at the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Meadow|link}} after every five hours of gameplay, regardless of whether or not the player has received a {{Term|Series|StreetPass|link}} tag. {{Term|ALBW|Ecru}} is one of the pre-generated {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link}} who can be met by this method.

{{Term|ALBW|Ecru}} wears a {{Term|ALBW|Green Tunic|link}} and wields a {{Term|ALBW|Shield|link}}, and is the lowest-bountied {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}} to wield the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword Lv2|link}}. He has a {{Term|ALBW|Life Gauge|link}} of eight {{Plural|ALBW|Heart}}, has 15 Medals won, carries a {{Term|ALBW|Nice Fire Rod|link}} and a {{Term|ALBW|Lamp|link}} into battle, and has an overall bounty of 360 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.
