The {{Term/Store|Kee Dafunia Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kee Dafunia Shrine}}<br/>The Melting Point}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''The Melting Point''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|Series|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia Shrine}} is located in the waters of {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Sea|link}}, just past {{Term|BotW|Ankel Island|link}}. It is one of three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} unlocked as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Muzu|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tottika|link}} on the shore of the mainland nearby. They have been tasked to watch the area by {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}}, since the signs of {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link|display=Mipha's}} trial from one hundred years ago have returned.<ref>{{Cite|We are here at the behest of Prince Sidon, who tasked us with keeping watch on this area. This is the land where {{Color|BotW Blue|Princess Mipha}} completed her sacred trial. Now, the signs that appeared 100 years ago have returned...|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Muzu}} will share with {{Term|BotW|Link}} some of {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}'s notes. which provide a hint for revealing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Princess Mipha's}} notes had this phrase... "While the {{Color|BotW Red|morning sun}} is newly born, follow the {{Color|BotW Red|path of light}} to the trial."|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> Though he is unsure of their origin, {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} repeated these words often.<ref>{{Cite|I am not sure where they came from, but she often repeated those mysterious words about the trial...|Muzu|BotW}}</ref>

At sunrise, a {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} will appear in the water between {{Term|BotW|Ankel Island}} and {{Term|BotW|Tingel Island|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must pass through the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|display=Ring}} while the sun's reflection touches it, otherwise it will disappear. Doing so will cause {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia Shrine}} to rise from the sea. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he will be greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia|link}}, who will introduce the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kee Dafunia. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kee Dafunia|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Immediately at the entrance to {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia Shrine}} is a lit, stationary {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Torch}} {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} leaning against it. The path forward is covered in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but a series of {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} blocks create a path. However, the platform past the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} is blocked by two pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ice}}, which can be melted using a {{Term|BotW|Torch}} lit by {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}.

After passing the two blocks of {{Term|BotW|Ice}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find several more blocks of {{Term|BotW|Ice}} stacked on top of a raised platform. In the center of this platform is a pillar, which connects further into the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. Standing on the nearby {{Plural|BotW|Floor Switch|link}} will cause {{Plural|BotW|Launcher|link}} to push forward new blocks of {{Term|BotW|Ice}}. Another lit {{Term|BotW|Torch}} can also be found behind this platform. By melting pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ice}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to create a staircase to the top of the pillar.

On top of this pillar is a final block of {{Term|BotW|Ice}}, which is too big for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to pick up or push. Past this is a pool of shallow {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}, above which stands the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. By melting this piece of {{Term|BotW|Ice}} to a certain point, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to bring it to the far end of the pool. Through use of the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} and the {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to {{Term|BotW|Climbing|link|display=Climb}} up to {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia}}.

Upon reaching {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for completing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero.}}|Kee Dafunia|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, they give {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Ruta's Emblem.}}|Kee Dafunia|BotW}}</ref> They tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} that collecting all three {{Plural|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|display=Emblems}} will lead to a new challenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Kee Dafunia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia}} then reveals how many are left to collect.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Kee Dafunia|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Kee Dafunia|BotW}}</ref> If this was the last {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} had to visit during "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song}}," {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia}} will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Ruta...}}|Kee Dafunia|BotW}}</ref>

By melting the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} on the rear side of the raised platform, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to access a hidden indentation. Inside is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Frostblade|link}}.
