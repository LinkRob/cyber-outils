The {{Term/Store|Kokiri Clothes||Series, TFH}} are an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Kokiri Clothes are to die for! They let you shoot three arrows at once. I'm quite gifted, no?}}

The {{Term|TFH|Kokiri Clothes}} are an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|TFH|Blob Jelly|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Armos Spirit|link}} and 100 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Kokiri Clothes}} allows {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} to shoot three {{Plural|TFH|Arrow|link}} at a time using the {{Term|TFH|Bow|link}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Arrow}} are fired in a spread shot, each about 10 degrees apart, and the amount of {{Term|TFH|Energy Gauge|link}} spent is unchanged.

The {{Term|TFH|Kokiri Clothes}} are based on the {{Term|Series|Kokiri|link}} tribe of forest-dwellers, of which {{Term|TFH|Link}} was a member in {{OoT}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} was given a more functional and protective appearance, including a pointy, [[Skull Kid's Hat|wide-brimmed hat reminiscent of the Skull Kids]], possibly due to the {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic|link}} already resembling the traditional {{Term|Series|Kokiri}} garments.
