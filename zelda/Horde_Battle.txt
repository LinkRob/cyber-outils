The {{Term/Store|Horde Battle||Series, SS}} is an event that occurs in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh-ho, a {{Color|SS Blue|horde battle}}! You'll face off against the horde of monsters that {{Color|SS Blue|Ghirahim}} summoned at the {{Color|SS Blue|Sealed Grounds}}.|Lanayru (Dragon)|SS}}</ref>

==Features==
The Horde Battle is a large-scale battle that occurs at the large spiral pit of the [[Sealed Grounds]]. The event is [[Ghirahim]]'s attempt to prevent [[Link]] from interfering with his ritual, planned to use [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]]'s soul to revive [[Demise]]. Ghirahim summons the Hordes of demons that are under his command, including all types of [[Bokoblins]], [[Shield Moblin]]s, and [[Stalfos]].<ref>{{Cite|Hear me, my hordes! The spell will soon be complete! The demon king returns! Until then, you WILL keep that whelp from interfering with my ritual. I don't care if the whole lot of you get lodged on the end of his blade. You will buy me the time I need! Do not fear him... Fear my wrath if you fail me!|Ghirahim|SS}}</ref> However, the demons fail to stop Link from reaching Ghirahim before the ritual is complete.

==Events==
The Horde Battle pits Link against Hordes of demons and involves five separate waves. Each wave increases in difficulty and includes a larger variety of enemies. Every wave ends with a barrier that will only open if Link defeats the enemies that are summoned upon arrival at the barrier.

*The first wave pits Link against a large number of regular {{Plural|SS|Red Bokoblin|link}} and ends with a barrier guarded by five red [[Bokoblin Leader]]s.
*The second wave includes weapon-wielding {{Plural|SS|Red Bokoblin}} and Bokoblins with [[Bomb]]s. The barrier at the end of the wave is protected by three [[Wooden Shield Moblin]]s.
*The third wave includes Bokoblins carrying Swords and Bombs, but also features [[Bokoblin Archers]]. The barricade is guarded by a Blue Bokoblin that runs away from Link in order to use an attack similar to Ghirahim's charge attack in the [[Skyview Temple]]. It can also run through the barricade.
*The fourth wave includes both weapon and Bomb wielding Bokoblins, Bokoblin Archers, and red Bokoblin Leaders. The barricade is guarded by two Stalfos.
*The final wave pits Link against every variety of Bokoblins, including Moblins.

After Link completes both the Horde Battle and a subsequent battle with Ghirahim, he can replay the Horde Battle as an event in [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]]'s [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|Lightning Round]].
