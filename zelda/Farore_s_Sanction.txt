'''Farore's Sanction''' is a [[Key Item]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Farore's Sanction|A mystical sanction entrusted to you by the goddess [[Farore]], given only to those who have proven their courage.<ref name="Farore's Sanction">{{Cite|A mystical sanction entrusted to you by the goddess Farore, given only to those who have proven their courage.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Farore's Sanction is a [[Key Item]] obtained after defeating [[Gohma]] in the [[Faron Temple]]. Following the battle, the goddess [[Farore]] appears to [[Princess Zelda]], granting her Farore's Sanction. It is one of the three blessings required to open the path to the [[Triforce|Prime Energy]].
