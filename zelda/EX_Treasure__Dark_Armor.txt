"{{Term/Store|EX Treasure: Dark Armor||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Treasure: Dark Armor}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Deya Village Ruins}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is obtained upon leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with finding {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2|link}}, which is rumored to be in the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Deya Village Ruins}} is a journal that places the location of this treasure ...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to activate the second stage of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} by reading {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2}} without triggering any of the loading conditions for its activation.}}

Within {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2}} are clues to the locations of six {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, each containing a priceless relic stolen from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Misko|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hid the six priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> The Journal states the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon|link|display=Evil Spirit's}} {{Term|BotW|Armor Set|link}} is hidden within the forests of {{Term|BotW|Faron (Region)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The evil spirit's armor set is hidden within {{Color|BotW Red|Faron's sea of trees}}.|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> The locations outlined are the highest of the three {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall|link}} north of {{Term|BotW|Lake Floria|link}}, at the bridge that crosses {{Term|BotW|Floria River|link}} between two small {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall}}, and near the broken stone bird in {{Term|BotW|Ebara Forest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at the highest of three waterfalls north of Lake Floria}}... {{Color|BotW Red|at the bridge between small waterfalls at Floria River}}... and {{Color|BotW Red|at the broken stone bird of Ebara Forest}}.|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Skull|link}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} submerged under the highest of three waterfalls flowing south from {{Term|BotW|Corta Lake|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Lake Floria}}. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Armor|link}} is submerged within {{Term|BotW|Floria River}} underneath {{Term|BotW|Sarjon Bridge|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Greaves|link}} are in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} buried next to a broken stone bird statue in {{Term|BotW|Ebara Forest}}. Each {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be dislodged from the ground or retrieved from the water using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to acquire the {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} within. The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed once all three pieces of the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Set|link}} have been obtained.<ref>{{Cite|You found the treasure chest at Corta Lake, Sarjon Bridge, and Ebara Forest.<br/>You obtained the {{Color|BotW Red|Phantom Ganon set}}!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
