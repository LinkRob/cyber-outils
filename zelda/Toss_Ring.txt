The {{Term/Store|Toss Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Toss Ring<br/>Throwing distance {{Icon|OoS Up Arrow}}|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Toss Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be found at the entrance to [[Mermaid's Cave]] during the present, requiring the [[Mermaid Suit]] to obtain it. It can also be won at the [[Big Bang Game]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it increases [[Link]]'s throwing distance.<ref name="Description"/> The Toss Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
