The {{Term/Store|Cloud Barrier||Series, SS, SSHD}} is an obstacle created by the goddess {{Term|SSHD|Hylia|link}} to separate the {{Term|SSHD|Surface|link}} from the {{Term|SSHD|Sky|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Until now, a {{Color|SSHD Red|cloud barrier}} created by the goddess has separated the world you know from the one below.|Fi|SSHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Because the {{Plural|SSHD|Loftwing|link}} will not fly through the {{Term|SSHD|Cloud Barrier}}, many of the {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft|link|display=Skyloftians}} are unaware of what lies below the clouds and believe it to be a forsaken and barren place.<ref>{{Cite|Some say that it's an empty, barren place, or even that there's nothing at all down below, but I just have this feeling that they're wrong. [...] The thing is, no one's ever been down there to see it, and our Loftwings won't fly through the cloud barrier.|Zelda|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is no easy task, Link. The world below is a forsaken place, and to reach it you must pierce the cloud barrier below. In living memory, no one has ever done this.|Gaepora|SS}}</ref> After {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} meets {{Term|SSHD|Fi|link}} and retrieves the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword|link}} from inside the {{Term|SSHD|Statue of the Goddess|link}}, {{Term|SSHD|Fi}} gives him the {{Term|SSHD|Emerald Tablet|link}} to illuminate a path through the clouds to reach the {{Term|SSHD|Faron Woods|link}} and thus begin his search for {{Term|SSHD|Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This tablet will illuminate a path through the clouds to the land below. Take it, and place it within the altar behind me.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Each time {{Term|SSHD|Link}} places an {{Term|SSHD|Ancient Tablet|link}} in the altar inside the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Statue}}, a {{Term|SSHD|Column of Light|link}} will appear and create an opening through the {{Term|SSHD|Cloud Barrier}}, allowing {{Term|SSHD|Link}} to go through the obstacle and reach the {{Term|SSHD|Surface}} by dropping off near the column with the aid of {{Term|SSHD|Crimson Loftwing|link|display=his Loftwing}}.<ref>{{Cite|The tablet you placed in the altar has opened a small rift in the cloud barrier. You can use it to travel through the clouds to the realm below.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A column of light has appeared to the southwest of our location, and signs indicate that it has created an opening in the cloud barrier to the land below.|Fi|SS}}</ref> While the {{Term|SSHD|Emerald Tablet}} leads to the {{Term|SSHD|Faron Woods}}, the {{Term|SSHD|Ruby Tablet|link}} will open a path to the {{Term|SSHD|Eldin Volcano|link}} and the {{Term|SSHD|Amber Tablet|link}} leads to the {{Term|SSHD|Lanayru Desert|link}}.
