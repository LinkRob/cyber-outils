{{Term/Store|Hollo||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Hollo is a [[Korok]] who lives in the [[Forest Haven]]. He runs [[Hollo's Forest Potion Shop]], and can make a [[Blue Potion]] if Link gives him four [[Boko Baba Seed]]s. He is the only Korok that stays in the Forest Haven for the whole game.
