The {{Term/Store|Tail Key||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 140"/>{{Note|The Tail Key is also referred to as the '''Tail Cave Key''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|guide= Nintendo|page= 26}}</ref> However, as the in-game name takes precedence, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
The {{Term|LADX|Tail Key}} is found in a {{Term|LADX|Treasure Chest|link}} located in the [[Mysterious Woods]]. At first, {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} can not access it, as the forest is looping over itself due to [[Tarin]] being transformed into a [[Raccoon]].<ref>{{Cite|Heh heh heh ho! You're goin' ta be lost, thanks to me! Heh heh! |Tarin|LADX}}</ref> Link must sprinkle [[Magic Powder]] on Tarin to return him to his human form, thus preventing the forest to loop and being able to access the {{Term|LADX|Treasure Chest}} located to the north.<ref name="Location"/>

{{Term|LADX|Link}} can use it to open the Tail Cave's gate by inserting it into the {{Term|LADX|Tale Keyhole|link}}.<ref name="E 140"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, after arriving at the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Mysterious Forest|link}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} finds that {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Tarin|link}} is been attacked by a {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Boarblin|link}}. After attacking it with a kick, the defeated {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Boarblin}} falls on a box, crushing it. Inside, they find the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Tail Key}}. {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} then uses the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Tail Key|display= Key}} to open {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Tail Cave|link}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Nomenclature==
The Japanese name of [[Moldorm]], the [[Boss]] of the [[Tail Cave]], is ''Degu Tail'', which may explain the name of this item.
