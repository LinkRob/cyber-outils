'''Fire Octo''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Fire Octo|This Octorok variety has adapted to its volcanic habitat, using its high internal temperature to spit fiery rocks.<ref name="Fire Octo">{{Cite|This Octorok variety has adapted to its volcanic habitat, using its high internal temperature to spit fiery rocks.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Fire Octos are fiery variants of [[Octorok|Octoroks]] found on [[Eldin Volcano]] that attack from a distance by spitting fireballs at its targets. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can use the [[Tri Rod]] to learn the Fire Octo [[Echo]], which costs two Echo energy to summon.
