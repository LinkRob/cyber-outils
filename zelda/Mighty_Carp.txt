==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Mighty Carp}}
Mighty Carp can be found swimming in the waters of the [[Akkala Highlands]] and [[Lanayru Great Spring]]. There is a compound in its liver that promotes muscle growth.<ref name="Inventory"/> Like other carp, they can be exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to become [[Roasted Carp]] or chilled by [[Ice]] to become [[Frozen Carp]]. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat one to restore a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, but they can also be [[Cooking|cooked]] into a dish that will increase the damage he deals when eaten.<ref name="Inventory"/>

As is the case with other fish, Mighty Carp can be used to make a [[Fish Skewer]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], and [[Salt-Grilled Fish]]. They also count towards seafood when making [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Meunière]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]].
