This is a sub-page covering miscellaneous appearances of [[Link]] outside of {{TLoZ|Series}} and its spinoff games. For ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearances, see [[Link/Mario-related media]].

=={{SCII|-}}==
:''See also: [[Soulcalibur II/Command List]]''
[[File:SCII Link Artwork.png|right|frameless|287x287px|Artwork of Link from ''Soul Calibur II'']]
Link is a playable guest character in the [[Nintendo GameCube]] version of the Namco fighting game {{SCII}}. His appearance is taken from the adult form of the [[Hero of Time]] from {{OoT}}. Though he is voiced by [[Nobuyuki Hiyama]] like in the [[Nintendo 64]] ''Zelda'' games and the first two ''Super Smash Bros.'' titles, the actor does not provide Link a deep voice as seen in {{OoT|-}} or as [[Fierce Deity Link]] in ''Majora's Mask''; he now has a timbre relative to that of a tenor (similar to that of [[Zora Link]], without modification). His voice is the same exact tone as the character Nightmare in the Japanese voicing of the game, as well as the seiyū voice of Siegfried Schtauffen in the entire ''Soul Edge/SoulCalibur'' series. Expectedly, Link's voice remains unchanged regardless of what language voice setting it is set to. Link's moveset in ''Soul Calibur II'' is notably more aggressive than his mainline and other spinoff appearances, but [[Shigeru Miyamoto]] did not see a problem with Link appearing in what some had thought to be a "violent fighting game" since he had already been established as a fighter in the {{SSB|-}} games.{{Fact}}

Not much is revealed about this incarnation of Link in-game, but it is known that after saving Hyrule from an evil wizard who was being controlled by a fragment of [[Soul Edge]],<ref>{{Cite|Just as the people were about to lose all hope, the sun miraculously returned. Link, who was summoned by Zelda to find the cause of the disasters, successfully defeated the magician. The people of Hyrule rejoiced in the belief that eternal peace would rule. Unfortunately, this was not to be. It became clear that the magician responsible for the disasters was in fact controlled by an evil sword called Soul Edge, which existed in an another world.|N/A|Soulcalibur II}}</ref> he went on a secret quest to destroy the evil sword. Quickly pulling the [[Master Sword]] out of its [[Pedestal of Time|pedestal]], he set out to travel to this world to destroy Soul Edge with his trusty [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]] and [[Hylian Shield]].<ref>{{Cite|Knowing that the resurrection of the evil blade must be prevented at all costs, Link chose to travel to the other world and destroy Soul Edge. Link gathered his trusted Master Sword, Hylian Shield, and Ocarina, and set off on a secret journey.|N/A|Soulcalibur II}}</ref> Eventually, he successfully seals away the evil within the Soul Edge, saving his homeland and quietly leaving the world he had come upon, leaving only a faint echo of his Ocarina.<ref>{{Cite|Link came to this world in order to defeat the evil which threatened Hyrule. After a long journey, Link finally succeeded in sealing away the terrible evil. In doing so, he not only saved his homeland from peril, but also maintained the tranquility of this world. Link then quietly left this world--a faint echo of his Ocarina was all that remained.|N/A|Soulcalibur II}}</ref>

In {{SCII|-}}, Link is one of the few characters with a ranged attack, alongside other guest characters {{Wp|Necrid}} and {{Wp|Spawn (comics)|Spawn}} (the latter of which is exclusive to the Xbox version). He is the only guest character to have his own theme and more than three costumes—the same colors that were his ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' costumes and older ''Zelda'' games. His weapons are a number of weapons from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past]]'', ''Ocarina of Time'' and ''Majora's Mask'', ranging from the [[Razor Sword]] to the [[Magical Sword]] and even the [[Bug Catching Net]], and a few exclusive to the game such as the Armos Series or his own version of Soul Edge. In addition, Link's fighting style retains many of his signature moves from past games in ''The Legend of Zelda'' and {{SSB|-}} series. Some of these moves include the [[Spin Attack]], the [[Jump Attack]], the [[Down Thrust]], the [[Jump Thrust]], and the [[Dash Attack]].

The twenty-fifth puzzle in the ''Picross'' series and the fortieth puzzle in the ''Mega Picross'' series depict {{Term|MNPTP|Link}}.

==''Dynasty Warriors VS''==
A costume based on Link's appearance in {{SS|-}} is available in the ''[[w:c:koei:Dynasty Warriors|Dynasty Warriors]]'' game, ''[[w:c:koei:Dynasty Warriors VS|Dynasty Warriors VS]]'' for the 3DS. The costume also includes the True Master Sword and Hylian Shield, but they are not used in battle.

=={{CoH|-}}==
[[File:CoH Link Artwork.png|right|130px]]
Link joins [[Cadence]] and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] as a playable characters in {{CoH}}. Link appears to take design elements from The Hero of Legend that appears in {{ALttP}}, {{LA}}, {{OoA}}, and {{OoS}}.

==''Vermin''==
A remake of the Game & Watch game ''Vermin'' is included as part of the {{G&WTLoZ}}. Link replaces the protagonist, and has a design similar to that of Link in {{LA|NS}}.

==Appearances in other media==
===''Mega Man'' (Archie Comics)===
Link, or more specifically his {{OoT}} and {{MM}} depictions, were used as the template for one of [[w:c:MegaMan:Roll Light/Archie Comics|Roll Light]]'s Halloween costumes in a blog post for Ryan Jampole.<ref>http://www.themmnetwork.com/blog/2013/10/31/31-days-of-halloween-costumed-roll-by-ryan-jampole</ref> Owing to Link's silent protagonist role in the series, Roll herself doesn't actually say anything while wearing Link's outfit, with Navi speaking on her behalf.
