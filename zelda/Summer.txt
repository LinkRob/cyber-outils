{{Term/Store|Summer||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Season|link}} and mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|game=OoS|publisher=Nintendo|page=4, 25}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Summer}} goes unnamed in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{Term|OoS|Summer}}, the {{Term|OoS|Overworld|link}} color palette turns a dark green hue. Lakes and ponds dry up, with sections turning into shallow {{Term|OoS|Water (Terrain)|link}} or dry land. {{Plural|OoS|Vine|link}} will also grow up cliff faces, allowing access to higher areas.

It is the second {{Term|OoS|Season}} able to be controlled with the {{Term|OoS|Rod of Seasons|link}}, as granted by the {{Term|OoS|Spirit of Summer|link}}.
