The {{Term/Store|King of Red Lions||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}, first appearing in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
The King of Red Lions is a talking sailboat that [[Link]] meets after he is tossed out into the [[Great Sea]] upon being caught by the [[Helmaroc King]] in the first time he entered the [[Forsaken Fortress]].<ref>{{Cite|Did I startle you? I suppose that is only natural. As wide as the world is, I am the only boat upon it who can speak the words of men.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> It is with this particular boat that Link is able to move from one island to another across the ocean. Good-spirited and filled with great wisdom, the King of Red Lions speaks to Link and explains what is currently happening and what he needs to do next in order to save his younger sister [[Aryll]] and defeat [[Ganondorf]].<ref>{{Cite|In that case, I shall guide you as we go forward.. advising you on what you should do, and where you must go.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> He can glimpse Link's various adventures apart from him through the [[Pirate's Charm]], which he also uses to give the young hero hints.

The boat can be operated with multiple items in Link's arsenal, such as the [[Sail]]. When attached to the mast, it allows for easy sailing to the prevailing direction of the wind.<ref>{{Cite|You got a boat's sail! Set it to (Y), (Z), or (X), then use it while on your boat to sail across the sea.|N/A|TWW}}</ref> In {{TWWHD}}, Link can also obtain the [[Swift Sail]] from the nightly [[Windfall Auction]] at [[Windfall Island]]. The upgrade doubles the boat's sailing speed and automatically changes the direction of the wind to the boat's heading. This assures high-speed sailing in all directions and eliminates the need for the [[Wind's Requiem]] while at sea, as backlash from fans in the original [[Nintendo GameCube|GameCube]] game find the sailing too tedious. The [[Grappling Hook]] can be used with the salvage crane to retrieve items from the bottom of the Great Sea. [[Bomb]]s will give the ship a cannon to fire at enemies.

Later in the game, it is revealed that the boat is actually [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]], the current [[King of Hyrule]] who lives under the sea.<ref>{{Cite|Link, do you not recognize my voice? Indeed, I am the one who guided you here. I am the King of Red Lions.|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref> The King of Red Lions is last seen at the end of the game, lifeless, as King Daphnes himself chooses to remain in Hyrule after the final battle and drowns when the waters washed away the kingdom. The Hero of Winds is then seen departing from [[Outset Island]] in the boat to search for a new land alongside [[Tetra]] and her [[Tetra's Pirate Crew|pirate crew]]. The King of the Red Lions is nowhere to be found in the game's direct sequel, {{PH}}, however, suggesting that Link abandoned the boat in favor of a new one.

The King of Red Lions understands [[Hylian Language|ancient Hylian tongue]], and speaks so on Link's behalf in conversations with [[Valoo]] and [[Jabun]] (though unlike them, his text is always in the game's native language).

==Trivia==
*If the King of Red Lions is struck with any of Link's weapons, an [[Updraft|updraft,]] or enemy bombs, he will react in pain.
*If he remains still long enough, the King of Red Lions can be seen yawning.
*During the [[Earth Temple|Earth]] and [[Wind Temple]] quests, [[Medli]] and [[Makar]] can be seen sitting next to Link and ridding on the King of Red Lions' head, respectively.
*The design of the King of Red Lions was likely inspired by the sailboats seen in Asian cultures.
