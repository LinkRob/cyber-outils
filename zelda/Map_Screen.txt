==Overview==
The Map Screen is an interface element where the player can view the various [[map]]s throughout {{TLoZ|-}} games and its spinoffs.

==={{TMC|-}}===
The Map Screen is a [[Subscreen]] in {{TMC}}, accessed by pressing the [[Start Button]]. It displays the [[Map of Hyrule]] given to [[Link]] by [[Smith]]; as Link reaches new regions of [[Hyrule]], they appear on the Map Screen. Various characters mark the Map of Hyrule to help Link on his journey; [[Smith]] marks the location of [[Minish Woods]] so that Link can find the [[Minish]],<ref>{{Cite|I've marked the location of the forest for you. Good luck, and be careful.|Smith|TMC}}</ref> [[Gentari]] marks the location of the [[Four Elements]],<ref>{{Cite|Here, give me your map. I can mark where these {{Color|TMC Red|elements}} can be found.|Gentari|TMC}}</ref> a [[Town Minish]] marks the location of [[Witch's Hut]] so Link can wake up [[Rem]],<ref>{{Cite|If you want to wake him, you'll need to get an item from {{Color|TMC Green|Syrup}}'s hut. It's a little bit of a trek, but we'll mark it on your map for you.|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> [[Stockwell]] marks the location of his [[Stockwell's House|House]] so Link can feed [[Fifi]],<ref>{{Cite|Allow me to mark the location of my home here on your map.|Stockwell|TMC}}</ref> another Town Minish marks the location of [[Mayor Hagen's Lakeside Cabin]] so Link can retrieve [[A History of Masks]],<ref>{{Cite|You want to go to the cabin by the lake? Well, let me just mark it here on your {{Color|TMC Red|map}}.|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> and the spirit of [[Gustaf]] is able to mark the location of the [[Royal Crypt]].<ref>{{Cite|Hmph! Was that...just a dream? He said he was a king of Hyrule. Wait! Look at your {{Color|TMC Red|map}}, {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}. There's some kind of mark on it. Hm. I guess that means it wasn't a dream!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

Whenever Link enters a [[dungeon]], the Map Screen displays the [[Dungeon Map]] rather than the Map of Hyrule. Instead of navigating different areas of the [[Overworld]], the Map Screen is used to navigate between floors of the dungeon. When Link obtains the [[Compass]] in the dungeon, the Map Screen displays the location of [[Treasure Chest]]s and the [[boss]].
