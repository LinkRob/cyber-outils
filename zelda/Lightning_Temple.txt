The '''Lightning Temple''' is a dungeon in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom]]''.


== Entrance to the Temple ==

== Themes and Navigation ==
The Lightning Temple appears to be a light-themed dungeon, as many of its puzzles are related to light.

When [[Link]] and [[Riju]] enter the dungeon, they must travel through several corridors in which rolling [[Flame Ball|Flame Balls]] are dropped on them.
