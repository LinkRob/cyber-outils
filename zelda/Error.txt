{{Term/Store|Error||Series, TAoL, DotT, CoH}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 223 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref><ref name="SCRAP">{{Cite|I AM ERROR.|Sign|DotT}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
In {{TAoL}}, {{Term|TAoL|Error}} is a citizen of [[Ruto Town]]. At first, {{Term|TAoL|Error}} only introduces himself when talked to,<ref>{{Cite|I AM ERROR.|Error|TAoL}}</ref> but after {{Term|TAoL|Link|link}} meets a second man in [[Mido Town]] and is encouraged to ask {{Term|TAoL|Error}} about how to get to the [[Island Palace]],<ref>{{Cite|ASK ERROR OF RUTO ABOUT THE PALACE|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref> Error will tell Link about the passage to the palace south of the king's tomb.<ref>{{Cite|SOUTH OF KINGS TOMB IN MIDO IS A TUNNEL.|Error|TAoL}}</ref>

The sprite design for Error is used for characters in other towns who can sometimes be similarly unhelpful,<ref>{{Cite|I KNOW NOTHING.|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref> although a few of them give Link very useful hints about using the [[Fire Spell]] or finding the [[Three-Eye Rock Palace]].<ref>{{Cite|IF ALL ELSE FAILS USE FIRE|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|CALL FOR HELP AT THE THREE EYE ROCKS.|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{DotT|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Though Error doesn't directly appear in {{DotT}}, a [[Sign]] bearing his iconic quote was used as a prop during photo shoots with the [[Master Sword]] for players who failed to stop [[Ganondorf]] in the allotted time at {{DotT}} events.<ref name="SCRAP"/>
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Nomenclature==
[[File:TAoL FDS Error Speech.png|thumb|"My name is Error..."]]
Error's name is meant to form a pun with that of [[Bagu]] whose name is the Japanese spelling of a computer "bug." In the Japanese version of {{TAoL|-}}, Error makes the less vague statement {{Romanize|"My name is Error..."|Ore no na wa Erā da...|オレノナハエラーダ...}}.<ref>{{Cite|オレノナハエラーダ...|Error|TAoL}}</ref> His name is written {{Romanize|エラー|Erā}}, the Japanese way of writing the English word "error." Only Error's name properly transferred over during the English localization.
