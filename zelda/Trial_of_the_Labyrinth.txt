"{{Term/Store|Trial of the Labyrinth||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial of the Labyrinth}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Lomei Labyrinth Island}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Lomei Labyrinth Island|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Sea|link}}, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Tu Ka'loh|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}. They state that proceeding through the maze will grant him a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You who have reached this point... proceed onward through the maze, and you will receive a reward.}}|Tu Ka'loh|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

The wide open area on the {{Term|BotW|Lomei Labyrinth Island}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher|link}} regularly patrol the area. While searching the maze, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will also encounter {{Plural|BotW|Keese|link}}. Eventually, he will come upon {{Term|BotW|Tu Ka'loh Shrine|link}} at the center of the {{Term|BotW|Lomei Labyrinth Island|display=Island}}.<ref>{{Cite|When you reached the end of the maze, you found an ancient shrine waiting for you.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After activating the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
