The '''Eldin Temple''' is a [[Dungeon]] in {{EoW}}

==Entrance to the Temple==
The Eldin Temple is unlocked at the end of "The Eldin Volcano Rift" quest line, which itself only unlocks after [[Hyrule Castle]] is beaten. To get to the temple, players must navigate the crater of [[Eldin Volcano]], then traverse the [[Stilled Eldin Volcano]], at which point they will arrive at the entrance.

==Themes and Navigation==
The Eldin Temple is primarily focused on lava, fitting for a volcanic dungeon. Magma is a constant hazard, as there are entire sections filled with the substance, a room in which Zelda must outrun rising lava, and another in which the player must time their movements to avoid cascading magma.

In addition, the temple features upward wind gusts as a recurring mechanic; players must grab onto a flying echo and use the wind to glide over lava. There is also an abundance of side-scrolling sections in the Eldin Temple, more so than any other dungeon in the series.
