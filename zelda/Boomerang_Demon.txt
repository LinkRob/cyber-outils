'''Boomerang Demon''' is an enemy that appears in {{TCT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Zelda ducks just as a flaming boomerang demon rams into the wall above her.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=36}}</ref> Dwelling beneath [[Midoro Palace]], these [[boomerang]]-like enemies have small, sharp teeth and can fly and hurl themselves against their victim. [[Arrow]]s are useless against the Boomerang Demons, but weapons such as a [[Zelda's Dagger|dagger]] can easily defeat these monsters.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Drat!' she yells. 'Arrows are no good against these monsters.' She draws her dagger to fend off the weird flying goblins.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=65}}</ref>

The Boomerang Demon is only shown in the book if [[Princess Zelda]] decides to stay by [[Link]]'s side after he is encased in the crystal. She is soon attacked by one of these flying foes, which she defeats easily with the use of her dagger, but is then suddenly surrounded by them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Soon a dozen of the monsters are scattered at her feet. But faster than she can defeat them, more come swooping at her.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=36}}</ref> If Zelda chooses to stay and fight the Boomerang Demons, she will be unable to defeat them all since they now have her surrounded, eventually dying in the process.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=<nowiki>'Oh, Link,' she cries. 'Now I see Ganon was counting on our friendship to keep me here. I should have gone to get help when I had the chance. This looks like the end!'</nowiki>|book= TCT|publisher= |page=11}}</ref> However, if the princess chooses to leave the Boomerang Demon-infested room, she will eventually find a way out and escape the attack of the demons.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Thrusting again and again at the devils, she begins to grope along the stone wall in search of a way out. As she passes her hands over the walls, she finds a small hollow between the rocks. She pulls and a section of the wall opens like a door.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=90}}</ref>
