{{Term/Store|Blue Hinox|Blue Hinoxes|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|BotW|Boss|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Blue Hinox}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
Blue Hinoxes make their first appearance as a mini boss in [[Turtle Rock]] and act the same as other Hinoxes in the game, right down to requiring 2 [[Bomb]]s to defeat. It is not the dungeon's "true" mini-boss however, as it will respawn when Link leaves and later re-enters the room. Once Link obtains the [[Magic Rod]], he can make short work of it, like many other enemies in the game.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Blue Hinox}}
{{Plural|BotW|Blue Hinox}} make their first 3D appearance and are a stronger variation of the Red {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}} found in {{BotW|}}. Unlike regular {{Term|BotW|Hinox}}, they wear wooden greaves, though they can easily be burned away with flame-based weaponry.<ref>{{Cite|Blue Hinox wear greaves that can be burnt away to expose their feet.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> As is the case with all {{Term|BotW|Hinox}}, they are vulnerable to damage to their eyes. When defeated, {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox}} drop {{Plural|BotW|Hinox Toenail}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hinox Tooth}}, {{Term|BotW|Hinox Guts}}, and a variety of cooked seafood.

Several quests involve encounters with {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox}}. The {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Three Giant Brothers|link}}" has three {{Term|BotW|Hinox}} on {{Term|BotW|Mount Taran|link}} guarding {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} that must be used to unlock the {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn Shrine|link}}. Of these {{Term|BotW|Hinox}}, the Middle Kin, which can be found in {{Term|BotW|Uten Marsh|link}}, is a Blue Hinox. The "{{Term|BotW|Shrouded Shrine|link}}" quest similarly requires {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to obtain the Orb carried by a {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox}} in the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins|link}}, which is used to unlock the {{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai Shrine|link}} that lies behind the {{Term|BotW|Hinox}} itself. The "{{Term|BotW|Stranded on Eventide|link}}" quest also requires {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} from the {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox}} on {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|link}}, which is used to unlock the {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh Shrine|link}}. After freeing {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Torfeau|link}} can grant the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Giant of Ralis Pond|link}}", which requires {{Term|BotW|Link}} to defeat the {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox}} at {{Term|BotW|Ralis Pond|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can receive the {{Term|BotW|Medal of Honor: Hinox|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} by defeating all {{Plural|BotW|Hinox}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. This can only be acquired after defeating {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} and completing the "{{Term|BotW|Destroy Ganon|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Blue Hinoxes reappear in {{TotK}}, acting mostly the same as in {{BotW|-}}, but can slam their heads down on Link with their [[Blue Hinox Horn|new horns]]. One is summoned alongside several [[Bokoblin]]s and a [[Boss Bokoblin]] by Ganondorf after he becomes the Demon King by murdering [[Queen Sonia]] and steals her [[Secret Stone]], stretching afterwards as if it has awakened from a long nap.
