{{Term/Store|Anton||Series, TWW, TWWHD, TMC}} is a recurring character in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258 {{Exp Game|TWW}} & 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Term|TWW|Anton}} is a man that can be found walking around {{Term|TWW|Windfall Island|link}} in {{TWW}}. His second-favorite activity is walking as it keeps his legs in shape, while his number one hobby is actually kickboxing. Lately, he has had a crush on the young lady {{Term|TWW|Linda|link}} and, if {{Term|TWW|Link|link}} successfully introduces him to her, they fall in love and reward him with a {{Term|TWW|Piece of Heart|link}}. {{Term|TWW|Kamo|link}} becomes depressed of the fact Linda is dating someone else, which triggers a sidequest that gives {{Term|TWW|Link}} a {{Term|TWW|Treasure Chart|link}}.

{{Figurine|TWW|Anton}}

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Anton}} lives in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} in {{TMC}}. He stands outside {{Term|TMC|Borlov|link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Chest Mini-Game Shop|link}}, hoping that it will open soon so he can play and refusing to leave.<ref>{{Cite|I really wanna play a game, but this place isn’t open yet. I guess I just got here too early...|Anton|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Man! I REALLY want to get in there and play a few {{Color|TMC Blue|games}}... I can’t believe they’re still closed! I’m not going anywhere until they open!|Anton|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Chest Mini-Game Shop}} will finally open, allowing {{Term|TMC|Anton}} to start playing.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh... He’s finally opened up! I can’t wait to get crackin’!|Anton|TMC}}</ref> Despite his excitement, he will warn {{Term|TMC|Link}} about gambling.<ref>{{Cite|Don't get hooked on gambling. You always lose in the end... But people keep playing!|Anton|TMC}}</ref> He remains in the {{Term|TMC|Chest Mini-Game Shop}} through the end of the game, remarking that it is no time to play games after {{Term|TMC|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I guess this is no time for games, really, is it?|Anton|TMC}}</ref>

He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure! Let's see how well they fit!|Anton|TMC}}</ref>
