{{Term/Store|Diving||Series, LA, TMC}} is a recurring ability in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="LA Guide 58">{{Cite Guide|game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Did you know the {{Color|TMC Red|flippers}} Librari gave you let you {{Color|TMC Blue|dive}} as well as {{Color|TMC Blue|swim}}?|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Stub|ALttP}}

==={{LA|-}}===
[[File:LADX Angler Fish Battle.png|thumb|left|x120px|Link battling the Angler Fish while Diving underwater in {{LA|DX|-}}.]]
In {{LA}}, Link can use the [[Flippers]] to Dive underwater by pressing the B Button while swimming.<ref>{{Cite|You've got the Flippers! If you press the B Button while you swim, you can dive underwater!|N/A|LADX}}</ref> Link can find sunken items while Diving, such as [[Small Key]]s and [[Piece of Heart]]s.<ref>{{Cite Guide|game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 54}}</ref> Link needs to Dive to reach and battle [[Angler Fish]], the [[Boss]] of the [[Angler's Tunnel]]. Diving is also required to gain entrance to the [[Catfish's Maw]].<ref name="LA Guide 58"/>
{{Clear|left}}

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Link can press the A Button while swimming to Dive. He is initially able to be underwater for no longer than three seconds but can later obtain the [[Silver Scale|Silver]] and [[Golden Scale]]s to extend his Diving capabilities.{{Fact}}

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Link can press the A Button to Dive while in his [[Hylian]] form. As [[Zora Link]], he can swim in any direction and at a faster speed than normal. He can also walk on the bottom of the water by pressing the B Button.

==={{TMC|-}}===
After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Flippers}} in {{TMC}}, he can {{Term|TMC|Diving|display=Dive}} underwater with the B Button.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|TMC Red|flippers}}! Press {{Icon|TMC A}} to glide through the water and {{Icon|TMC B}} to dive!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Diving}} is necessary to obtain a {{Term|TMC|Small Key|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}; {{Term|TMC|Link}} can also {{Term|TMC|Diving|display=Dive}} beneath the pool of water next to {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's House|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}} to obtain a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Stub|TP}}

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, after obtaining the [[Water Dragon's Scale]], Link can Dive underwater.<ref>{{Cite|The great spirit of the {{Color|SS Blue|Water Dragon}} has provided you with the ability to swim freely underwater.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, Link can use the Zora's Flippers to Dive by pressing the A Button.<ref>{{Cite|You got Zora's Flippers! Time to jump in with both feet! Press {{Icon|ALBW A}} to dive, and press {{Icon|ALBW B}} to swim faster!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|They will allow you to swim and dive, so you may travel the rivers and roam the lakes.|Oren|ALBW}}</ref>
