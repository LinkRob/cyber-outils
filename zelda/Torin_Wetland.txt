The {{Term/Store|Torin Wetland||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.

==Features and Overview==
The Torin Wetland is located in the [[Akkala Highlands]]. The marsh is peppered with [[Guardian]] remains and a few ruins among the Trees. Three of them are still active, though one is a [[Decayed Guardian]], bereft of its legs. Defeating them at least once is the goal of the "[[Hobbies of the Rich]]" [[Side Quest]] that [[Hagie]] assigns [[Link]].

The travelers [[Stamm]] and [[Lonni]] can be found walking along the road through Torin Wetland.

[[Luminous Stone Deposit]]s can be found at the base of the cliff in the southern reaches of the Wetland.
