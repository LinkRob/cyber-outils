{{Term/Store|Mazo||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283}}</ref>

==Biography==
Mazo is an [[Anouki]] living on the [[Isle of Frost]]. It lives in the [[Anouki Estates]] and is among those [[Link]] questions while he searches for the [[Yook]] who has abducted and impersonated one of the villagers. Mazo tells Link that it and [[Dobo]] are honest,<ref>{{Cite|They call me Mazo. I don't care if you believe me, but me and Dobo only tell the truth. Just thought I'd say that.|Mazo|PH}}</ref> and Dobo is similarly confident that Mazo tells the truth.<ref>{{Cite|They call me Dobo. Mazo's real honest. The guy never lies!|Dobo|PH}}</ref>

Neither of them are lying.
