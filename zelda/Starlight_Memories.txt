==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories}} is owned by {{Term|BotW|Cara|link}} and managed by {{Term|BotW|Isha|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|With that beast causing a ruckus and the guards in an uproar over some kind of theft, things have been pretty crazy. And on top of it all, my shop...|Cara|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Flint">{{Cite|I manage a shop that specializes in handmade accessories, but I've run out of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}. I need it to make my items.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> Customers must bring the {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}} needed to craft their accessory of choice. The {{Term|BotW|Shop}} is closed when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} because they have run out of the {{Plural|BotW|Flint}} they need to make the Shop's accessories.<ref name="Flint"/> {{Term|BotW|Link}} cannot access the {{Term|BotW|Shop}} until he completes the "{{Term|BotW|Tools of the Trade|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} by giving {{Term|BotW|Isha}} the {{Plural|BotW|Flint}} she needs. {{Term|BotW|Cara}} can be found in the back of the {{Term|BotW|Shop}} and tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} many secrets about {{Term|BotW|Isha}} as thanks for the {{Plural|BotW|Flint}} he provided them.
