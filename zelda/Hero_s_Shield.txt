==Location and uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
The Hero's Shield is used by [[Link]] at the beginning of {{MM}}. It is a strong metal shield that can also be bought at the [[Trading Post]] in [[Clock Town]] for 80 [[Rupees]], in case he gets his shield swallowed by a [[Like Like]].

The Hero's Shield is permanently replaced by the [[Mirror Shield]] once Link obtains it in [[Beneath the Well]].

The shield bears many similarities to the [[Hylian Shield]]'s features, including a red bird, the blue base, the [[Triforce]], and the metal decals. However, the Hero's Shield features a simplified and abstract style in comparison. The bird's head is more hunched, and the shape cannot be seen, but its face is visible. Its wings are also much larger than its body, and point down. The Triforce has moved from above the bird to below it, greatly decreased in size.

==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:Grandma shield.jpg|right|thumb|195x195px|Link's Grandma presenting Link with the family Shield from {{TWW|-}}]]
The Hero's Shield is the basic Shield received at the beginning of {{TWW}}. It bears a slight resemblance to the Hylian Shield once used by the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Legend]]. The Shield is sturdy and provides adequate protection against most attacks, although its flimsy appearance is mocked by [[Tetra]]. Despite this, the Hero's Shield will not burn upon contact with fire like most wooden Shields.

The Shield is an heirloom belonging to Link's family.<ref>{{Cite|In the olden days, this was the day boys were finally considered to be men. They were taught the ways of the sword to prepare them for battle with their enemies. Hanging the family shield on the wall as decoration is another tradition that has been carried down from those days.|Link's Grandma|TWW}}</ref> It is said that the family Shield was the legendary Shield used by the Hero himself.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Hero's Shield! This is the legendary shield said to have been used by the hero himself!|N/A|TWW}}</ref> The Shield is given to Link by his [[Link's Grandma|Grandma]] before he leaves [[Outset Island]] to save his sister, [[Aryll]]. It is with sadness that Grandma gives Link the Shield, as it symbolizes his departure from the island to face great danger. The Hero of Winds uses the Shield throughout a large portion of the game before obtaining the [[Mirror Shield#The Wind Waker|Mirror Shield]] in the [[Earth Temple (The Wind Waker)|Earth Temple]], after which the Hero's Shield disappears from Link's inventory, presumably discarded.

The Shield is predominantly brown as opposed to the blue seen in {{MM|-}}. The Triforce is in the very center of the shield. There are long swirls surrounding it as well as three bolts at its corners. A winged creature with horns replaces the bird, but unlike the {{MM|-}} version, it faces up with wings spread out like the original Hylian Shield.

The Hero's Shield from the [[Era of the Great Sea]] appears in {{BotW}}. It is obtained randomly when 30th Anniversary series {{TWW|-}}-themed Princess Zelda amiibo is used is scanned using the [[amiibo (Rune)|amiibo]] [[Rune]]. Multiple can be obtained through repeatedly scanning the Rune. It is said this Shield was the favorite of a hero who travelled the open seas.<ref name="Inventory"/> This Shield was apparently a family heirloom, passed down through generations.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made of metal, the Hero's Shield will conduct [[Electricity]] and attract [[Lightning]] if it is equipped during a [[Thunderstorm]]. It will also sink if it is dropped in [[Water (Terrain)|Water]]. However, it can be picked up with the [[Magnesis]] Rune, and it will not burn like wooden Shields do when exposed to [[Fire]] or a [[Scorching Climate]].

==Trivia==
*Although the Hero's Shield was indeed owned by the [[Hero of Time]] in {{MM}}, the claims by [[Link]] the Hero of Winds' [[Grandma|grandmother]] that it was used by the incarnation from the [[Nintendo 64]] games is rather confusing, as {{TWW}} takes place in the Adult Timeline, while the former was in the Child Timeline according to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]]''. In {{OoT}}, no such Shield was found.
*The Hero's Shield appeared in the [[SpaceWorld 2000 GameCube Tech Demo|SpaceWorld 2000 Tech Demo]] for the [[Nintendo GameCube]]. It was however wielded by the older incarnation of Link from {{OoT|-}}.
*The [[Small Shield]] in {{TMC}}, and the Shields in {{FS}} and {{FSA}}, resemble the Hero's Shield in {{TWW|-}}.
