The '''Jade Amulet''' is a Spell in {{ZA}}.

==Overview==
The Jade Ring fires a large, green triangular bolt of magic that damages enemies. It is particularly effective against the [[Sardak]]s in the [[Shrine of Earth]]. The Jade Ring costs three [[Rupee]]s to use.

The Jade Ring is received from a [[Talking Treasure Chest]] in the Shrine of Earth.
