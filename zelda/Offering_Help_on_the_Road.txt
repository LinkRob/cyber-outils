==Overview==
Offering Help on the Road is a side quest that can be unlocked after completing the [[Scenario]], "[[Akkala Tower (Scenario)]]." It can be found in the [[Gerudo Desert]], west of [[Gerudo Town]] and the side quest requires that the character gives 4,500 [[Rupee|rupees]] in exchange for qualifying for the Trial of the Great Fairies.<ref>Materials Needed: 4,500 rupees: Quest requirements</ref> However, once you complete the side quest, the character will earn double the amount of rupees, with a total of 9,000 rupees.<ref>"The traveler came back to Gerudo Town after making the offering to the Great Fairy, and rewarded you with double what you gave him! May travelers always be so blessed."-Quest Complete Text</ref>

Once this is completed, three side quests will be unlocked: [[Fairy Fountain: Grassy Plains]], [[Fairy Fountain: Deep Snows]], and [[Fairy Fountain: Desert Sands]].
