The {{Term/Store|Tabantha Great Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Tabantha Great Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Eastern Hyrule|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Great Bridge}} spans the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon}} between {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. It is a old bridge that bears many holes which open up to a fatal fall down the canyon. Not far east of the bridge, the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable}} makes it home next to the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Great Bridge}}, and it also shares a name with the bridge.
[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
On a ridge underneath the eastern side of the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Great Bridge}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok Stump|link}} can be found waiting for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to stand on it. Once he stands on it, a ring of light appears on the other side of the canyon. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} manages to glide over to the ring of light in time, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.
