The {{Term/Store|Vow of Sidon, Sage of Water||TotK}} is a [[Key Items in Tears of the Kingdom|Key Item]] in {{TotK}}.

==Location and Uses==
The vow is used to summon or dismiss the [[Sage Avatar|avatar]] of [[Sidon]]. It is obtained after completing the [[Sidon of the Zora]] quest line.

[[Link]] may exchange four [[Sage's Will]]s at any [[Goddess Statue]] to upgrade the Vow into a [[Solemn Vow of Sidon, Sage of Water]], which grants the avatar increased attack power when assisting in battle and a slightly shorter ability recharge.

The avatar of Sidon cannot be summoned in [[Beneath Hyrule Castle]], [[Town]]s, and [[Stable]]s.
