{{Term/Store|Trill's Shop||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref name=shop>{{Cite|TRILL'S SHOP / Make payment at the box near the shopkeeper.|Sign|TP}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The shop is situated in [[Faron Woods|North Faron Woods]], very close to the {{Term|TPHD|Forest Temple|link}}.<ref name=shop/> It is a small shop owned by [[Coro]]'s pet bird, [[Trill]], that offers [[Red Potion]] and [[Lantern Oil]] only after nearby [[Bokoblin|Bokoblins]] have been defeated. After scooping the necessary refills in a [[Bottle]], [[Link]] must make the payments near Trill in a box he provides.<ref name=shop/> If Link leaves without paying, he will label Link as a "[[Thievery|thief]]" and peck him every time he enters his store.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! THIEF! STOP!|Trill|TP}}</ref> Each peck deals a quarter of a [[Heart]] of damage. The bird will stop pecking Link when he finally pays for what he took.

==Trivia==
*It is possible to steal from Trill's shop without the bird noticing. If the game is saved and restarted after Link takes the items, Link will be at the entrance of North Faron Woods, with the items in his possession.
*The way Trill calls Link "Thief" is similar to how everyone calls Link "THIEF" in {{LA}} after he steals from the [[Town Tool Shop]].
*It is possible to purchase an item by putting just one [[Rupee]] in the box. Trill will not harm Link as he leaves.
