The {{Term/Store|Island Postal Service||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is the {{Term|Series|Mail Delivery|link}} service in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the Island Postal Service! What do you have for delivery?|Postbox|TWW}}</ref> 

==Features and Overview==
===Operation===
The {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service}} is operated by the {{Term|TWW|Rito|link}}, whose unique ability to fly makes them the only suitable postmen on the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}. The {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service|display=Postal Service}} is central to the Rito way of life.<ref>{{Cite|As you can see, we of the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Rito tribe}} are profoundly connected to the sky. We make our livings on the airways.|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If this continues, the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|fledglings}} who are of age will never be able to receive scales from {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Valoo}} on {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Dragon Roost}}. They will remain {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|wingless}}, and in time, our very way of life will be threatened.|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref> They value their reputation as deliverymen over their own safety and comfort.<ref>{{Cite|I went to great trouble to go all the way to the Forsaken Fortress, and it was a most frightening ordeal, let me tell you!<br/><br/>{{Small|...If I don't deliver this letter, then I am a failure of the delivery trade...}}|Ilari|TWW}}</ref> Their aerie on {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island|link}} doubles as the {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center|link}}. 

{{Term|TWWHD|Koboli|link}} is in charge of sorting mail, whereas {{Term|TWWHD|Quill|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Ilari|link}} are responsible for collection and delivery. {{Term|TWWHD|Ilari}} is the designated postman for {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Occupation: Windfall Island Postman|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Namali|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Pashli|link}} walk around the {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center}} carrying clipboards and claim to be busy,<ref>{{Cite|It's a shame that I'm too busy to spend more time with you. You seem to be a nice enough fellow. Sorry.|Pashli|TWW}}</ref> but their occupations are not specified. The only non-{{Term|TWWHD|Rito}} staff is the part-timer {{Term|TWWHD|Baito|link}}, who {{Term|TWWHD|Koboli}} hires on to do sorting. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} himself can help with the operations of the {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service}} in two ways. The first is by playing the {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Sorting|link}} mini-game. The second is by delivering the {{Term|TWWHD|Moblin's Letter|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Ilari}}'s behalf.<ref>{{Cite|I have an idea. That old fool may not permit postmen on his premises... But surely he would allow YOU in the place, would he not? Could you deliver this {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|letter}} to {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Maggie}} in my stead?|Ilari|TWW}}</ref>

The {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service}} has {{Plural|TWWHD|Postbox|link}} installed on 19 {{Plural|TWWHD|Island}} of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea}},{{Verify|TWW}} over a third of all the islands. This includes the three major settlements and all five {{Plural|TWWHD|Fairy Island|link}}, among others. {{Plural|TWWHD|Postbox}} are sentient, talking objects that receive incoming and outgoing mail,<ref name="Incoming Mail">{{Cite|We cannot do much to help you, but we can at least do this: if we get any letters or parcels for you, we will deliver them to a {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|postbox}} near you. If you see any {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|wiggling}} postboxes, please look inside them.|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Your parcel has hereby been accepted for delivery. Thank you for using the Island Postal Service. We appreciate your business!|Postbox|TWW}}</ref> charge postage fees, process {{Term|TWWHD|Cash-On-Delivery}} parcels,<ref>{{Cite|This parcel has been sent {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Cash-On-Delivery}}.|Postbox|TWW}}</ref> and reject mail that cannot be delivered through the {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service}}.<ref>{{Cite|Krzzzzt! PAHHHFT! This item cannot be delivered.|Postbox|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Krzzzzt! PAHHHFT! This letter has no addressee written on it. Please deliver it yourself...|Postbox|TWW}}</ref> They wiggle when they have mail for {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}}.<ref name="Incoming Mail"/> The {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service}} specially delivers {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}'s mail to the {{Term|TWWHD|Postbox}} nearest him.<ref>{{Cite|We shall deliver any letters or packages addressed to you to the postbox nearest your location. You may continue your journey knowing this to be true.|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My co-workers let me in on your little secret, man... All letters addressed to you are specially delivered to the postbox closest to you! No matter what island you happen to be on! That's so cool, man!|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref>{{Note|In reality, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can retrieve his mail from any {{Term|TWWHD|Postbox}} at any time. The {{Term|TWWHD|Rito Chieftain|link}} mentions the difficulty with {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} moving around a lot, but does not explain how the {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service}} manages to address the problem.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, are you using our {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|postboxes}}? Well, as you might know, since you left the island, all {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|letters and packages addressed to you}} have been delivered to whatever {{Color|SS Red|postbox}} was nearest you. Of course, the system is not perfect—you tend to move around a lot. So be sure to check any moving postboxes you find in your travels. Do not forget!|Rito Chieftain|TWWHD}}</ref>}}

===Uses===
{{Term|TWWHD|Hoskit|link}} uses the {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service}} to send to his girlfriend the 20 {{Plural|TWWHD|Golden Feather|link}} he receives from {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to write a letter right away and send this off!|Hoskit|TWWHD|link}}</ref> When {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} forms a {{Term|TWWHD|Merchant's Oath|link}} with a {{Term|TWWHD|Wandering Merchant|link}}, they use the {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service|link}} to ship {{Term|TWWHD|Decorations|link}} to {{Term|TWWHD|Zunari's Shop|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So, from now on I shall honor our {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|merchant's oath}} by sending shipments of my product to the Windfall Island Shop. How, you ask? Why, my good friend the postman will take it there for me, so it will probably get there before you do. Just go back to Windfall and check for yourself.|Wandering Merchant|TWWHD}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Garrickson|link}} abuses the service to send unwanted love letters to an unspecified admirer.<ref>{{Cite|I am certain the object of this sadly one-sided affection is in quite a quandary over the matter. I am of the mind that I must have a word with the unwanted suitor. However, I must have physical evidence. And thus, your first task is to snap a pictograph of this individual in the act of sending the unwanted letters!|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> He is the only character other than {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} to actually be seen using a {{Term|TWWHD|Postbox}}.

The only items {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can send in the mail are the {{Term|TWWHD|Note to Mom|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie's Letter|link}}.  Various characters use the service to send letters and parcels to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}.{{Which|TWW}} {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle|link}} sends {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} an unspecified {{Term|TWWHD|Cash-On-Delivery}} parcel costing 201 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Postage due is {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|201 Rupees}}. Will you pay?|Postbox|TWW}}</ref> When purchased it turns out to be the {{Term|TWWHD|IN-credible Chart|link}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Baito}} sends a C.O.D. parcel as well,<ref>{{Cite|I'm really sorry I had to send this C.O.D., but I guess that's the price of fame!|Baito|TWW}}</ref> which strangely contains a {{Term|TWWHD|Red Rupee|link}}. The parcel costs 10 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}} (half its value).<ref>{{Cite|Postage due is {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|10 Rupees}}. Will you pay?|Postbox|TWW}}</ref>
 
===Pricing===
The postage cost at any given {{Term|TWWHD|Postbox}} varies according to how far it is from {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island}}. The postage can be 5, 10, or 20 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}}.

The {{Term|TWWHD|Postbox}} at the {{Term|TWWHD|Flight Control Platform|link}} will state that it is "quite far" from {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island}} and charge the maximum postage fee of 20 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}}.<ref name="Postage 20"/> This is in spite of the fact that {{Term|TWWHD|Flight Control Platform}} is adjacent to {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island}}. Nearly every other {{Term|TWWHD|Postbox}} in the northern half of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}} — from {{Term|TWWHD|Pawprint Isle|link}} to {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Island|link}} — charges the minimum 5-Rupee postage and states that it is near {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island}}.<ref name="Postage 5"/> This outlier aside, the postage fee is a roughly linear function of distance from {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island}}.
