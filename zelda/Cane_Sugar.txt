==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar}} is a {{Term|BotW|Material|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can purchase from {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Goron Gusto Shop|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|General Shoppe|link}}, and the travelling {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} {{Term|BotW|Yammo|link}}. At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar}} is an ingredient that can be used to make {{Term|BotW|Green Dye|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar}} is boiled, it becomes a sweet juice that can be used to make cake.<ref name="Inventory"/>
