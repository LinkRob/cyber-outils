{{Term/Store|Zol|Zols|Series, TLoZ, ALttP, LA, LADX, OoS, OoA, FS|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 214"/>

==Characteristics==
The Zol's primary habitat ranges from [[dungeon]]s and [[cave]]s in {{TLoZ}} and {{ALttP}}, to the [[Overworld]] in {{LA}}, {{OoA}}, and {{OoS}}. While Zols typically yield two [[Gel]]s when struck with a weapon, it is possible to defeat these creatures through the use of a more powerful weapon (such as the [[White Sword]] from {{TLoZ|-}}) as such so that the enemy bypasses the Gel stage altogether.

Zols from {{ALttP|-}} and {{FSA}} are the only incarnations not to experience a Gel phase when attacked. However, this is also the most abundant version, as it appears in three different colors across Hyrule and shows a difference in vitality: the standard red are the simplest, while green and yellow are the most difficult to defeat. {{LA|-}} also incorporates red and green Zols: the red Zol is the stronger of the two and can divide into Gel if struck with a weak weapon (a strong one will simply defeat it), while the green version is much less powerful and does not divide into Gel upon attack. Also, in {{LA|-}}, {{OoS}}, and {{OoA}}, only green Zols can pop out of the ground in an ambush, attacking Link in a radically different way in comparison to the commonplace, bouncing strategy of other titles. In {{ALttP|-}}, both green and yellow Zols can pop out of the ground.

As Zols are often commonplace enemies, they can be defeated with the most conventional of weaponry, including the [[Sword]], [[Bomb]], [[Boomerang]], and [[Bow]].

==Mainline appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Zol & Gel|Jelly-like monsters that walk about bouncing around in the labyrinths. When Link cuts Zol, it splits into two Gels. Gel has little attacking power, but Zol has twice the power of Gel.}}
Zols first appear in {{TLoZ}}. When defeated, Zols may drop either a [[Heart]], one [[Rupee]], five Rupees, or a [[Clock]].{{Note|If [[Link]] defeats 10 enemies without getting hit or teleporting with the [[Recorder]], then the 10th enemy defeated will drop five [[Rupee]]s. However, if the 10th enemy is defeated with a [[Bomb]], then it will drop four Bombs. If Link defeats 16 enemies without getting hit or teleporting, then the 16th enemy will drop a [[Fairy]]. After the 16th enemy, Link must get hit and reset the counter to zero in order to achieve this effect again. Defeating [[Armos]], [[Like Like]]s, [[Keese]], or [[Gel]]s will not be counted.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= n.a. |published= n.d. |retrieved= August 17, 2017 |url= http://redcandle.us/The_Legend_of_Zelda#Forced_drops |title= Forced Drops |site= redcandle.us}}</ref>}} It is notable for its palette when used in dark rooms, making it completely blend in to the background, safe for the eyes.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Zol|A weak, gelatinous monster that moves slowly by expanding and contracting.<ref name="EoW Zol">{{Cite|A weak, gelatinous monster that moves slowly by expanding and contracting.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Zols are gelatinous creatures found in the grasslands of Hyrule. When they detect a target, they jiggle before leaping toward it. Zols can be summoned as an [[Echo]], with the Echo cost of one.

Zols also have fire and water counterparts, the [[Ignizol]] and the [[Hydrozol]], respectively. The [[Buzz Blob]] also behaves very similar to an elemental Zol, being a slime creature that produces electricity. A [[Dark Echo|Dark variant]] can be encountered after coming in contact with a [[Tecuum]].

==Spinoff appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TFoE|-}}===
[[File:Bit WoG.png|frame|left]]
Zols in {{TFoE}} are depicted as purple with a pair of angry eyes. A Zol simply moves back and forth slowly. Link can defeat a Zol using his [[Sword]], but as if with most enemies, if Link touches a Zol, he takes damage.

==={{ZA|-}}===
[[File:ZA Red Zol.png|frame|left]]
Red Zols appear as enemies in the [[Shrine of Water]] in {{ZA}}. Unlike most of their other appearances, Zols do not split into Gels when defeated. At one point, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] has to defeat a group of Zols to obtain the [[Dungeon Map]] of the Shrine of Water.
