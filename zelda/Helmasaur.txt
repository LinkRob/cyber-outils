==Characteristics==
{{Plural|Series|Helmasaur}} differ in their appearances across the series, varying from {{Wp|sauria}}n, insectoid, porcine or avian-like creatures. They wear a protective mask or helmet over their faces or front bodies, and typically charge at {{Term|Series|Link|link}} when they see him, often attempting to knock him over or off platforms. Their iron masks protect them from most direct attacks to the face. However, in many games their masks can be removed, often with the {{Term|Series|Hookshot|link}} or similar items. As their backsides are often left exposed, they can also be attacked normally from behind. Alternately, some items, such as the {{Term|Series|Hammer|link}} or {{Plural|Series|Bomb|link}}, are powerful enough to penetrate their masks' defenses.

Several {{Plural|Series|Boss|link}} have also appeared in the series that take on a similar form and wear masks that must also be destroyed to defeat them, the most notable being the {{Term|Series|Helmasaur King|link}}, which is named after these creatures. Similar {{Plural|Series|Boss}} that have also appeared include the {{Term|Series|Helmaroc King|link}} and {{Term|Series|Gemesaur King|link}}.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Helmasaur|The tough, armored head of this small saurian can easily withstand sword blows. Your best chance is to attack him from behind.}}
{{Plural|ALttP|Helmasaur}} appear as orange {{wp|Sauropsida|sauropod}}-like creatures wearing green masks that obscure their entire face in {{ALttP}}. They inhabit {{Plural|ALttP|Dungeon|link}} of the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}}, specifically the {{Term|ALttP|Palace of Darkness|link}}, {{Term|ALttP|Skull Woods|link}}, {{Term|ALttP|Turtle Rock|link}}, and {{Term|ALttP|Ganon's Tower|link}}. They also appear in the {{Term|ALttP|Palace of the Four Sword|link}} in {{ALttP&FS}}. When {{Term|ALttP|Link}} enters their space, they immediately rush after him and will constantly pursue him around the entire area. They often appear in narrow spaces or in areas overhead pits, where they will try to knock {{Term|ALttP|Link}} down as they ram into him. {{Plural|ALttP|Helmasaur}}' masks protect them from being attacked directly with the {{Term|ALttP|Sword|link}}, which will simply knock them back. Their exposed backsides, however, can be struck to defeat them, although doing so is difficult due to the enemy's constant pursuit.

{{Plural|ALttP|Helmasaur}} can be killed with a few other items, including throwing {{Plural|ALttP|Skull|link}} at them, using {{Plural|ALttP|Bomb|link}}, shooting {{Plural|ALttP|Arrow|link}} with the {{Term|ALttP|Bow|link}}, or three strikes from the {{Term|ALttP|Magic Hammer|link}}. All three {{Plural|ALttP|Medallion|link}} are also effective against them, with the {{Term|ALttP|Ether Medallion|link}} turning {{Plural|ALttP|Helmasaur}} into {{Plural|ALttP|Fairy|link}}. They can also be stunned using the {{Term|ALttP|Hookshot|link}}. The masks of {{Plural|ALttP|Helmasaur}} cannot be removed or destroyed in {{ALttP|-}}.

{{ALttP|-}} also features a much bigger {{Term|ALttP|Helmasaur}}, known as the {{Term|ALttP|Helmasaur King|link}}, as the {{Term|ALttP|Boss}} of the {{Term|ALttP|Palace of Darkness}}. It also appears again in the {{Term|ALttP|Palace of the Four Sword}}.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Plural|LANS|Helmasaur}} are creatures whose faces are fully protected by steel masks in {{LA}}. They do not charge at {{Term|LANS|Link|link}}, but instead slowly move at random. Their masks protects them from nearly all attacks to the face, including the {{Term|LANS|Sword|link}}; however, their backsides are vulnerable to attack. Different from previous appearances of {{Plural|Series|Helmasaur}}, these enemies can now have their masks removed by using the {{Term|LANS|Hookshot|link}} on them when they are facing {{Term|LANS|Link}}. When their mask is removed, they become surprised and start walking much more quickly, but become completely vulnerable to all attacks from any direction. They can also be attacked with {{Plural|LANS|Bomb|link}} and {{Term|LANS|Magic Powder|link}} even when they are still masked. They appear in the {{Term|LANS|Angler's Tunnel|link}}, {{Term|LANS|Catfish's Maw|link}} and {{Term|LANS|Eagle's Tower|link}}.

{{Plural|MM3D|Hiploop}} take on a much different appearance as insect-like creatures in {{MM}}. They come in two forms: an unmasked red version, and a green version wearing a blue mask. {{Plural|MM3D|Hiploop}} are hotheaded creatures that will immediately charge at {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} when they see him, attempting to knock him off the platform they are on. Unmasked {{Plural|MM3D|Hiploop}} appear at the {{Term|MM3D|Woodfall|link}} swamp crater, where they crawl along the boardwalks and attempt to shove {{Term|MM3D|Link}} off. Since they are unguarded, they can be freely attacked with the {{Term|MM3D|Sword|link}} from any direction. {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Spin Attack|link}} will stun them, though only briefly. Their attacks can be blocked with a {{Term|MM3D|Shield|link}} to knock them back, providing an opportunity to strike back. Masked {{Plural|MM3D|Hiploop}} appear in the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|link}}, where their faces are protected by the masks they wear. They can either be attacked from behind or have their masks pulled off using the {{Term|MM3D|Hookshot|link}}.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Plural|OoS|Iron Mask}} make no effort at charging at {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} in {{OoS}}, instead wandering at their own leisure with their faces covered by steel masks. {{Plural|OoS|Iron Mask}} can be defeated by striking their exposed backsides when they turn around, although a few other items are also effective against them. {{Plural|OoS|Bomb|link}} can be used to defeat them instantly even with their masks still intact. Their masks can be removed by using the {{Term|OoS|Magnetic Glove|link}} on any polarity. They appear in {{Term|OoS|Unicorn's Cave|link}} and {{Term|OoS|Hero's Cave|link}}.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Plural|OoA|Iron Mask}} make no effort at charging at {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} in {{OoA}}, instead wandering at their own leisure with their faces covered by steel masks. As before, {{Plural|OoA|Iron Mask}} can be defeated by striking their exposed backsides when they turn around, although a few other items are also effective against them. {{Plural|OoA|Bomb|link}} can be used to defeat them instantly even with their masks still intact. Their masks can be removed by using the {{Term|OoA|Switch Hook|link}} on them, although only when they are facing {{Term|OoA|Link}}, otherwise the item will merely switch places with the enemy. They appear in the {{Term|OoA|Moonlit Grotto|link}} and {{Term|OoA|Jabu-Jabu's Belly|link}}.

==={{FS|-}}===
{{Plural|FS|Helmasaur}} take on the appearance of bird-like creatures wearing masks in {{FS}}. These {{Plural|FS|Helmasaur}} blindly charge around the area and at the {{Plural|FS|Link|link}} in a straight line. Their backsides cannot be attacked despite being exposed. They can only be defeated by using the {{Term|FS|Magnetic Glove|link}} on them, which will rip off their masks on any polarity. Once their masks are removed, the {{Plural|FS|Helmasaur}} are revealed to have parrot-like faces. In this state they will no longer charge at the {{Plural|FS|Link}}, but instead scamper about in alarm. They appear in the {{Term|FS|Chambers of Insight|link}}, {{Term|FS|Death Mountain (Four Swords)|link}}, and {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace|link}}, as well as the {{Term|FS|Realm of Memories|link}} and the {{Term|FS|Hero's Trial|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Helmasaur}}
{{Plural|TMC|Helmasaur}} have an identical appearance to the bird-like {{Plural|FS|Helmasaur}} of {{FS|-}} in {{TMC}}. As before, they constantly charge around the area whenever {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is in close proximity. They appear in the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}} and in other {{Plural|TMC|Cave|link}} around {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. Unlike the {{Plural|FS|Helmasaur}} of {{FS|-}}, these enemies' backsides are vulnerable and can be attacked with the {{Term|TMC|Sword|link}} when they are turned around. Their masks can be pulled off using the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}. {{Plural|TMC|Bomb|link}} can also be used to defeat them instantly.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Plural|TPHD|Helmasaur}} appear as animals that share traits with saurians and rhinoceros, as well as pigs, in {{TP}}. Instead of wearing a mask on their faces, {{Plural|TPHD|Helmasaur}} wear a plate of spiked armor over their heads and part of their backsides. {{Plural|TPHD|Helmasaur}} keep to themselves in small groups, but when they spot {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}, they squeal and charge at him directly until they slam into a wall. Like previous incarnations, {{Plural|TPHD|Helmasaur}} can be attacked from behind by striking their partially exposed behind. Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TPHD|Clawshot|link}}, he can use it to pull off the armor plates from {{Plural|TPHD|Helmasaur}} and freely attack them from any angle. Their plates are notably heavy, and can be used in certain instances to hold down {{Plural|TPHD|Switch|link}}. Unlike past appearances, {{Plural|TPHD|Bomb|link}} are useless in penetrating their armor. {{Plural|TPHD|Helmasaur}} can be found in the {{Term|TPHD|Lakebed Temple|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time (Dungeon)|link}}, and the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|link}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Plural|ALBW|Helmasaur}} are closely based on those from {{ALttP|-}} in {{ALBW}}, appearing as orange sauropods wearing green masks. Like their previous appearance, they will charge after {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} whenever they see him, although this time they do not make a constant effort at pursuing him and will stop attacking when {{Term|ALBW|Link}} moves too far away or becomes out of reach. Their masks protect them from frontal attacks, leaving only their backsides vulnerable; however, since they are frequently encountered in narrow pathways above pits, they can simply be shoved off the ledge by striking their guarded face to push them away. They appear in the {{Term|ALBW|Dark Palace|link}}.

A giant {{Term|ALBW|Helmasaur}} wearing a mask adorned with jewels, known as the {{Term|ALBW|Gemesaur King|link}}, also appears as the {{Term|ALBW|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|ALBW|Dark Palace}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Helmasaur}} in {{TFH}} are based on those from {{ALBW|-}}. They will charge after any of the {{Plural|TFH|Link|link}} who come near them, and will usually make an active effort in pursuing them until they move too far away. As before, they can be attacked from behind. These enemies can also have their masks pulled off their faces by using the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot|link}}, which will expose their entire body to attack. They appear in the {{Term|TFH|Floating Garden|link}} as well as the {{Term|TFH|Scorching Zone|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
The name Helmasaur is a portmanteau of "helmet", referring to its mask, and "saurian", in reference to its reptile-like shape.

{{Plural|Series|Hydrosoar|link}} are known as {{Romanize|ヒップループホバー|Hippurūpuhobā|Hiploop Hover|2}} in Japanese, connecting them to both {{Plural|Series|Helmasaur}} (known as {{Romanize|ヒップループ|Hippurūpu|Hiploop}} in Japanese) and {{Plural|Series|Water Tektite|link}} (known as {{Romanize|ホーバー|Hōbā|Hover}} in Japanese). They share their armored head and weak backside with {{Plural|Series|Helmasaur}}. Both enemies appear in the {{Term|Series|Angler's Tunnel|link}}.
