{{Term/Store|Estan||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|Estan|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Estan}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} who manages a {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. At her {{Term|BotW|Stall|link}}, she sells a variety of cooked meat. According to {{Term|BotW|Maike|link}}, a merchant at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Estan}} is her little sister.<ref name="Sister"/> {{Term|BotW|Maike}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Estan}} knows {{Term|BotW|Hylian (Language)|link}} well, and she recommends that he stop by and meet her.<ref>{{Cite|She's traveled all over, so she knows your language pretty well. You should stop by and...|Maike|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Estan}}, she greets him and asks what he eats for breakfast, lunch, or dinner depending on the time of day.<ref>{{Cite|Good mauning! What do you eat for breakfast?|Estan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Good effernoon! What do you eat for lunch?|Estan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Good ebbning! What do you eat for dinner?|Estan|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that he eats meat, {{Term|BotW|Estan}} comments that he has good taste.<ref>{{Cite|Good choice! You've got great taste!|Estan|BotW}}</ref> If Link says that he eats vegetables, she insists that he must have meant meat.<ref>{{Cite|You must mean... {{Big|Meat!!!}} That's what you meant, right? Because it's so good!|Estan|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Estan}} wonders if {{Term|BotW|Frita|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} woman who stands near her {{Term|BotW|General Store}}, has something against meat, but she mutters that this is impossible.<ref>{{Cite|That Rito vai keeps shooting me the stink eye... Does she have something against meat? {{Small|No, that's impossible...}}|Estan|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Estan}} is very grateful and thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey there, customer! Feeling so good now that the beast is calm. Sarq—I mean tanks again!|Estan|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Estan}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she once traversed across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} in search for the best meats, which allowed her to learn common words in the {{Term|BotW|Hylian (Language)}} language.<ref>{{Cite|I once traveled across all of Hyrule, searching for the best meats ever.|Estan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I learned some of the correct ways to say common Hylian words. Just the basics, like "tank you" and "good mauning." Pretty amazing, huh?|Estan|BotW}}</ref> Although {{Term|BotW|Estan}} knows some words in {{Term|BotW|Hylian (Language)}}, she usually mispronounces them, such as saying "tank you" instead of "thank you."<ref>{{Cite|Tank you! Good-bye!|Estan|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys {{Term|BotW|Estan}}'s meats, she calls it a good purchase.<ref>{{Cite|Good purchases, one and all!|Estan|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has bought all of her stock, she thanks him for doing so.<ref>{{Cite|Tank you very much.|Estan|BotW}}</ref> Not only does {{Term|BotW|Estan}} sell her wares, she will also buy {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sure! What have you got?|Estan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You sure there isn't anything else you'd like to sell?|Estan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Take a look! You sure there isn't anything else you'd like to sell?|Estan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll buy it all.|Estan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sell me all you got!|Estan|BotW}}</ref> When she accidentally says "sarqso" instead of thank you, she appears disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|Sarqso! Oh...darn...|Estan|BotW}}</ref> She is grateful for {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s patronage.<ref>{{Cite|Tank you for your patronage.|Estan|BotW}}</ref>
