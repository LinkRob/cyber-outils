The {{Term/Store|Spirit of Healing||Series, ST}} is a character in {{ST}}.<ref name="Help">{{Cite|The Spirit of Healing can't help you more than once...|N/A|ST}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|ST|Spirit of Healing}} is a {{Term|ST|Fairy|link}} who heals {{Term|ST|Link|link}} when he plays the "{{Term|ST|Song of Healing|link}}" on the {{Term|ST|Spirit Flute|link}}. Since the {{Term|ST|Song|link}} can only be used once per {{Term|ST|Dungeon|link}},<ref name="Help"/> they only appear as {{Term|ST|Link}} calls for their healing powers. They can also be called in the final phase of the battle with {{Term|ST|Malladus|link}}.

{{Term|ST|Link}} first meets them at the entrance to the {{Term|ST|Forest Temple|link}}, when he learns the "{{Term|ST|Song of Healing}}".
