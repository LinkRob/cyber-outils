The {{Term/Store|Tunic of the Sky||Series, BotW}} is a piece of [[Armor]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Tunic of the Sky}}<br/>Legend has it this armor was once worn by a hero who appeared from the sky riding atop a mighty bird. Apparently it was presented to celebrate the completion of a ceremony.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Tunic of the Sky can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the ''Skyward Sword'' Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Tunic of the Sky. It can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Tunic of the Sky, alongside the [[Cap of the Sky]] and [[Trousers of the Sky]], comprise the [[Hero of the Sky Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from ''[[Skyward Sword]]''. When it has been enhaced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
