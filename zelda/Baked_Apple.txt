{{Term/Store|Baked Apple|Baked Apples|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

==Location and Uses==
Baked Apples are the result of [[Apple]]s that made contact with [[Fire]].<ref name="Inventory"/><ref>{{Cite|An apple and an open flame make for a succulent treat.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Simple foods such as apples are fine to eat raw, but roasting them on a fire makes them tastier and more nutritious.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> They are softer and sweeter than regular Apples.<ref name="Inventory"/> By eating one, [[Link]] can restore three quarters of a [[Life Gauge|Heart]].<ref name="Inventory"/>

When Link first meets the [[Old Man]] at his [[Campfire]], he can steal his Baked Apple and have the Old Man lecture him.<ref>{{Cite|I BEG YOUR PARDON! I do believe that is my baked apple! You can't just go about taking whatever you please!|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> The Old Man will then tell Link he was only teasing him.<ref>{{Cite|Oho ho! Forgive me—I could not resist pulling your leg. Please help yourself.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

If Link speaks to [[Juney]], who can be found in [[Rito Village]], she'll explain that she's upset with her husband bringing her there for her honeymoon, and they only way she won't divorce him is if he brings her a Baked Apple.<ref>{{Cite|He already apologized, but if he doesn't want a divorce, I told him he'd better bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apple}}.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "[[The Apple of My Eye]]". Returning to Juney with a Baked Apple in your inventory will prompt her to ask for it in exchange for a present.<ref>{{Cite|*sniff sniff*<br/>I know that smell!<br/>You've got a {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apple}}! Can I have {{Color|BotW Blue|one}}? I'll give you a present!|Juney|BotW}}</ref> She'll give Link a [[Silver Rupee]] in return. Juney laments that her husband doesn't cook her Baked Apples very often.<ref>{{Cite|Now if I could only get my husband to cook me a {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apple}} every once in a while.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> She also asks Link to bring her more Baked Apples if he can.<ref>{{Cite|I've got a second stomach and a hollow leg for {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apples}}. Bring me as many as you possibly can.|Juney|BotW}}</ref>
