The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Warrior||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a [[Gerudo]] character in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo Warrior}} appears in the {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo's Fortress|link}} after [[Link]], defeats all of the four {{Plural|OoT3D|Gerudo Thief|link}} to rescue the [[Carpenter|Carpenters]]. While {{Term|OoT3D|Nabooru|link}} is away at the {{Term|OoT3D|Spirit Temple|link}}, she leaves this Gerudo in charge of the {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo's Fortress|link}}, making her technically the third-in-command of the Gerudo race, behind Nabooru and [[Ganondorf]] himself.<ref name="Exalted">{{Cite|I've seen your fine work. To get past the guards here, you must have good thieving skills. I used to think that all men, except for the great Ganondorf, were useless...but now that I've seen you, I don't think so anymore! {{Color|OoT3D Red|The exalted Nabooru}}, our leader, put me in charge of this fortress. Nabooru is the second-in-command to the great Ganondorf, King of the Gerudo Thieves. Her headquarters are in the {{Color|OoT3D Yellow|Spirit Temple}}, which is at the end of the desert. Say, you must want to become one of us, eh? All right then! You're in from now on! Take this. With it, you will have free access to all areas of the fortress!|Gerudo Warrior|OoT3D}}</ref> After Link frees all the Carpenters, the {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo Warrior}} appears to compliment him on accomplishing such a task and rewards him with the {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo Token|link}},<ref name="Exalted"/> which allows the [[Hero of Time]] to walk about freely in the {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo's Fortress}} and access the {{Term|OoT3D|Haunted Wasteland|link}} without the risk of being arrested.

==Trivia==
*Unlike other Gerudo, due to a bug as well as graphical limitations of the [[Nintendo 64]], the color palette of the {{Term|OoT|Gerudo Warrior}}'s clothing and lipstick is the same as [[Link]]'s [[Green Clothes|tunic]], and will therefore turn red or blue if he wears the [[Goron Tunic|Goron]] or [[Zora Armor|Zora Tunic]], respectively, similar to certain characters and items in {{TLoZ}} when obtaining the [[Blue Ring|Blue]] or [[Red Ring]]. She also flashes the color of the [[Bomb]] or [[Bombchu]] if the hero uses the item while on-screen.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Kazooie |published= January 19, 2011 |retrieved= February 5, 2020 |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1N1hMZMoFk4 |title= OoT: Gerudo color changing glitches |site= YouTube |type= Video}}</ref> This was fixed in PAL versions, the {{CE}} version, and {{OoT3D}}, and thus can only be seen in the original v1.0/v1.1 NTSC release.[[File:MM Aviel Model.png|thumb|221x221px|Aveil, the Gerudo Warrior's Terminan counterpart]]
*The Gerudo Warrior has a counterpart in [[Termina]] named [[Aveil]] in {{MM}}. She is the leader of the Gerudo within the [[Pirates' Fortress]].
