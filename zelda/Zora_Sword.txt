{{Plural|BotW|Zora Sword}} are one-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} fashioned by the {{Plural|BotW|Zora|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Zora Sword|display=Swords}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Zora Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref> They are forged from a type of durable and rust-resistant metal and ornamented with traditional {{Term|BotW|Zora}} designs. There is only one obtainable Zora Sword in the game, appearing in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. It is located in the same area as [[Muzu]] after the [[Divine Beast Vah Ruta|Devine Beast Vah Ruta]] has been reclaimed from [[Calamity Ganon]], on the NorthWestern bridge connecting [[Zora's Domain|Zora’s Domain]] to the mainland. There is an additional Zora Sword that appears in master mode, inside a chest.

{{Plural|BotW|Zora Sword}} are made of metal, and therefore they do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They do, however, attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they also conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. If they are dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, they will sink, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
