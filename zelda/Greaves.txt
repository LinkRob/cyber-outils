{{Term/Store|Greaves|Greaves|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Greaves}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Greaves}} are equipment that boosts the user's defense by reducing any damage done to {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} by 0.5 when they have it equipped.<ref>{{Cite|These boots increase your defense, reducing the damage you take on every hit by 0.5.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or slain minibosses, though a free {{Term|CoH|Greaves}} can always be found within one of the pots inside the {{Term|CoH|Temple of Storms|link}} lobby.

==Trivia==
They behave similarly to the Hargreaves item from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer'', another set of greaves that boosts the user's defense.
