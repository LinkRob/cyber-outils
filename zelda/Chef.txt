The {{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Chef||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a character in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Chef|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Chef owns a small restaurant in [[Port Town]] with his wife, the [[Waitress]]. When [[Tingle]] first meets The Chef, he will not speak until paid 15 [[Rupee]]s. 

Tingle can purchase several [[recipes]] from the Chef at different points during his journey, such as Beef Stew and [[Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland#Meat Soup|Meat Soup]]. He can also purchase different sizes of pots from him and his wife, which gives the ability to use larger amounts of ingredients in his recipes. 

When Tingle obtains all 28 recipes and brings them to The Chef, he will be rewarded with a rare [[Rupee Good]], the Chefs Hat.
