"{{Term/Store|Diving is Beauty!||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Diving is Beauty!}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Zora's Domain}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Once {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} arrives in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, he can find {{Term|BotW|Gruve|link}} at the top of one of the waterfalls outside the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}}. When spoken to, he recounts his story of being stuck at the top of {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Tower|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|However, while searching for a Hylian, {{Color|BotW Red|Prince Sidon}} left me stranded on a tower...|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> Eventually, he was able to muster the courage to dive into the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Instead, I worked up some fighting spirit and jumped into {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora River}} from the top of the tower. Then I came here.|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> where he saw his reflection and was deeply moved.<ref>{{Cite|I was...deeply moved seeing my reflection on the water's surface as I dove in... Yes. Deeply moved.|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquires further, {{Term|BotW|Gruve}} shares his belief that people's true colors show when they are diving.<ref>{{Cite|[Deeply moved?] It is my observation that a person's true colors shine the brightest when they dive freely into the waves!|Gruve|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is in possession of the {{Term|BotW|Zora Armor|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gruve}} notices and calls it impressive.<ref>{{Cite|Congratulations on the new {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora armor}}. It is most impressive indeed.|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> With it equipped, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is capable of swimming up {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wearing the {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora Armor}}, even a Hylian like you can {{Color|BotW Red|climb a waterfall}} and get back up here in no time at all.|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to dive into the waters below so he can see his true colors.<ref>{{Cite|That said, if you don't mind me asking, would you please show me your true colors as you dive into the water? You can dive {{Color|BotW Red|headfirst}} from here into the water below! It would be true beauty made flesh! Please consider!|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} dives into the water, {{Term|BotW|Gruve}} will share his praises with him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Blinding brightness!}} Radiant beauty!|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> He then instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to climb back up the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}} and speak to him.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* OK, now {{Color|BotW Red|climb the waterfall}} and then meet me back here!|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> At the top of the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}}, {{Term|BotW|Gruve}} says that his soul feels as though it is on fire.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Fantastic!}} Can you feel that?! My soul is on fire! It's pulsating with beauty!|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he has shown him something special and asks that he land.<ref>{{Cite|You showed me something truly special! Thank you so much! Now then, come over here real quick!|Gruve|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Gruve}} comments on how amazing {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s performance was,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|That was amazing!}} |Gruve|BotW}}</ref> saying he feels lucky to have witnessed it and is now crying tears of joy.<ref>{{Cite|I saw your dive and waterfall climb with my own lucky eyes! And now those very eyes are flooded with tears of joy!|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> He decides he must thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} and gives him five {{Plural|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yes! I must thank you somehow!|Gruve|BotW}}</ref>

Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Gruve}} feels emotionally ravaged, as he does not see such diving every day.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I feel emotionally ravaged after that! It's not every day I get to see a diving form as beautiful as yours, you know!|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will be looking forward to his next dive.<ref>{{Cite|I'll be looking forward to your next dive! I really will!|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
