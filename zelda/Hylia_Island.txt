{{Term/Store|Hylia Island||Series, BotW}} is a place in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hylia Island'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Hylia Island is a large landform partially submerged by [[Water]] in the western part of [[Lake Hylia]]. In total, 11 points can be seen peeking out of the Lake. The central point is the largest and is surrounded by 10 smaller ones. The [[Ya Naga Shrine]] can be found in the crater of the central point, and a [[Raft]] can be seen tied to the dock on the western side of the central point. A [[Zora]] named [[Mei]] can be found catching [[Fish]] on one of the eastern points of the Island before the [[Side Quest]] "[[A Wife Washed Away]]" is completed.<ref>{{Cite|I'm catching fish! They'll sell in no time flat at the general store in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}!|Mei|BotW}}</ref>

Two metallic [[Treasure Chest]]s, each containing a [[Purple Rupee]], can be found set in the ground on the northernmost and southernmost points of Hylia Island. A third metallic Treasure Chest containing a [[Phrenic Bow]] can be found in the water on the eastern side Hylia Island. A fourth metallic Treasure Chest containing a bundle of five [[Shock Arrow]]s can be found in the water north of the aforementioned Chest. A fifth metallic Treasure Chest containing a Knight's Halberd can be found in the water nestled between the two westernmost points of the Island.

A [[Korok]] can be found atop a [[Tree]] on the northwestern part of Island, who will give [[Link]] a [[Korok Seed]] in return for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
