"{{Term/Store|The Priceless Maracas||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Priceless Maracas}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|West Necluda}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
On the path between {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Bridge|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Hestu|link}}.<ref name="Piggyback 220"/> He expresses his shock that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to see him,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Shalaka?!}} You! You can see me?!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> revealing it has been 100 years since someone has done so.<ref>{{Cite|♪Shala-zah! Shala-kah!♪ It's been 100 years since anyone has been able to see me!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> After introducing himself, {{Term|BotW|Hestu}} explains that he needs {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s help.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Hestu, and I need your help!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref>

A group of {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}} have stolen {{Term|BotW|Hestu's Maracas|link}} and are hiding behind a grouping of rocks just up the path.<ref>{{Cite|Those monsters over there stole my beloved {{Color|BotW Red|maracas}}! I think they're still there on the other side of those rocks.|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Without them, {{Term|BotW|Hestu}} is unable to use his powers.<ref>{{Cite|I can't use my {{Color|BotW Blue|powers}} without them. {{Small|Shoko...}}|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to retrieve them for him,<ref>{{Cite|So please! PLEASE get my {{Color|BotW Red|maracas}} back from them!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> which begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

Past the rocks, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} comprised of three {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin}}. Once all of them have been defeated, the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Term|BotW|Hestu's Maracas}} at the back of the stronghold will be unlocked. {{Term|BotW|Hestu}} is shocked to see that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has his maracas and will ask for them back.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|SHALA-KALA! Those are...}} Those are my {{Color|BotW Red|maracas}}! Please giiiiiive them to meeeeeeeee!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Though he is happy to have them back at first,<ref>{{Cite|♪SHAAAAAA-LAAAAAH-KAAAAAAH!♪|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Hestu}} realizes that they are missing the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Seed|link}} inside of them.<ref>{{Cite|But wait! There's something wrong with my maracas! The {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seeds}} inside are gone! How am I supposed to dance now? Shoko...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed}}, {{Term|BotW|Hestu}} would be able to use his powers to expand his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I had just {{Color|BotW Red|one Korok seeds}}, I could sing, dance, and use my {{Color|BotW Blue|powers}} to help you. You see, I have the power of {{Color|BotW Blue|inventory}} expansion! So let me know if you find some {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seeds}}!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Piggyback 220"/>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} next speaks to {{Term|BotW|Hestu}}, he reveals that the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Seed}} must have been stolen by the other {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} as a prank.<ref>{{Cite|The children of the forest must have taken the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seeds}} from my beloved maracas. I never thought I'd be the target of one of their pranks. Shoko...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> However, when he realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has at least one {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed}},<ref>{{Cite|Do you have a {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seed}}?! You do! You got a {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seed}} from one of the forest children!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> he will ask for in exchange for expanding his {{Term|BotW|Inventory}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you'll give me just {{Color|BotW Red|one Korok seed}}, I'll make you {{Color|BotW Blue|inventory}} bigger in return!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to hand a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed}} over, {{Term|BotW|Hestu}} asks him which stash he would like to expand.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes, please!] SHALAKA! So which {{Color|BotW Blue|stash}} do you want more slots in?|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Hestu}} will expand {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s stashes twice, after which he realizes how late it has become.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Look at the time!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> He has to leave before {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree|link}} gets mad at him for being out so long.<ref>{{Cite|I need to get back before {{Color|BotW Red|Grandpa}} yells at me. {{Small|Shoko...}}|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever has any more {{Plural|BotW|Korok Seed}}, {{Term|BotW|Hestu}} encourages him to come to {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}} to give them to him.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, I have to go. But I still need Korok seeds, so bring some to {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Forest}} if you find any!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete.
