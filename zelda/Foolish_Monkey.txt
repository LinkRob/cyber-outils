Only those on official business may enter! ...But you may enter to see the public humiliation of the {{Color|MM3D Red|foolish monkey}} who angered our king. ==Biography==
The Foolish Monkey is a [[Monkey]] who, prior to [[Link]]'s arrival in [[Termina]], sought the aid of the [[Deku Princess]] to enter [[Woodfall Temple]] and discover the source of the poison that was infecting the [[Southern Swamp]]. Upon entering the Temple, the Foolish Monkey saw that it was infested with monsters, who captured the Deku Princess. A [[Deku Scrub]] serving the [[Deku King]] saw the Deku Princess entering the Temple with the Foolish Monkey, and later saw only the Monkey leaving the Temple.<ref name="Saw">{{Cite|I saw it! That {{Color|MM3D Red|monkey}} and the {{Color|MM3D Red|princess}} entering the {{Color|MM3D Red|temple}} at night! ...But only the monkey came back out!|Deku Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> The Deku Scrub took word of this to the Deku King, who imprisoned the Foolish Monkey, believing that he deceived the Princess and trapped her inside the Temple.

After Link arrives at the Southern Swamp and saves [[Koume]] in the [[Woods of Mystery]], he is confronted by the Foolish Monkey's brother, who was watching him. The Foolish Monkey's brother explains what happened to the Foolish Monkey and asks for Link's help,<ref name="Brother"/> as the Monkey noticed that Link is able to transform into a [[Deku Scrub]] and is therefore able to infiltrate the Deku-exclusive [[Deku Palace]]. Inside the Deku Palace, Link can see Deku Scrubs spreading rumors of the Foolish Monkey's deeds to the Deku King.<ref name="Saw"/><ref>{{Cite|Only Royal Family members can enter the sunken temple, so it's obvious that monkey was using her!|Deku Scrub|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Rumor has it that the monkey ate the princess... How frightful!|Deku Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> Infuriated by the rumors, the Deku King commands his subjects to prepare a cauldron of boiling water to torture the Foolish Monkey as punishment for tricking the Deku Princess.<ref>{{Cite|Everyone! Hurry up and get that cauldron ready!!!|Deku King|MM3D}}</ref> Inside his cage, the Foolish Monkey attempts to warn his captors that their Princess is in danger, but to no avail.<ref>{{Cite|They keep saying that I kidnapped her. No matter how many times they say it, it's not going to bring the {{Color|MM3D Red|princess}} back! If they're not careful, the {{Color|MM3D Red|princess}} will fall victim to a monster! Please, believe my words...|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref>

In order to save the Foolish Monkey, Link must enter and navigate the Deku Palace's [[Outer Garden]] to reach the inside of the Monkey's cell. Upon speaking to the Foolish Monkey inside the cage, he gives in to his fate and refuses to speak further.<ref>{{Cite|They keep saying that I kidnapped her. No matter how many times they say it, it's not going to bring the {{Color|MM3D Red|princess}} back! If they're not careful, the {{Color|MM3D Red|princess}} will fall victim to a monster! Why won't they believe what I say? ...Fine! I get it! Then I shall say nothing more to you! Punish me or do whatever else you might!|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I have nothing else to say to you Deku Scrub! Do as you will!|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> However, if Link equips the [[Deku Pipes]], the Foolish Monkey will recognize them as the same kind of instrument that the Deku Princess owns.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You have them! You do! Just like the princess... {{Color|MM3D Red|Deku Pipes}}! Those will work great!!!|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> After catching himself in his furor, the Foolish Monkey asks who Link is before explaining his situation.<ref>{{Cite|By the way...{{Color|MM3D Red|who}} are you?|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...Whoa! That's not important now... Just listen to me!|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> He asks Link to enter the Woodfall Temple on his behalf to rescue the Deku Princess.<ref>{{Cite|I was trying to find out about the poison in the swamp, so I went to {{Color|MM3D Green|Woodfall Temple}} {{Color|MM3D Red|above the waterfall}}. But the temple had become a {{Color|MM3D Red|monster's lair}}, and the princess was captured by the monster... Since the Deku King thinks I kidnapped the princess, he won't listen to a word I say. Now the princess is in trouble, so I must somehow hurry to the {{Color|MM3D Red|temple}} and save her!|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> If Link agrees, the Foolish Monkey will teach him the "[[Sonata of Awakening]]", the melody which raises the sunken Temple from the Swamp's floor.<ref>{{Cite|Do you understand my plight?|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Yes}} So then, that means... You will go in my place and save the princess!|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OK, now I'll teach you the {{Color|MM3D Red|melody}} that opens the {{Color|MM3D Red|temple}}. The princess taught it to me! We can't let them hear us, so I'll sing it softly...|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref>
[[File:MM Foolish Monkey Punishment.png|300px|thumb|right|The Foolish Monkey being suspended over a cauldron from {{MM|-}}]]
Though he attempts to teach the song quietly,<ref>{{Cite|Open your ears and listen hard!|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> the Deku King overhears the exchange and affirms it as proof that the Foolish Monkey tricked his daughter into entering the Temple.<ref>{{Cite|Everyone, did you hear that?|Deku King|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This melody, which only the Deku Royal Family knows... It proves the foolish monkey deceived the princess so he could enter the temple!|Deku King|MM3D}}</ref> The Deku King then commands his subjects to commence with the Monkey's torture,<ref>{{Cite|Everyone! Let the monkey's punishment commence!|Deku King|MM3D}}</ref> kicking Link out of the Palace in the process. As he is being dragged away, the Foolish Monkey instructs Link to take the [[Woodfall Temple Pathway]] to access the Temple and begs Link to hurry.<ref>{{Cite|You'd better take the {{Color|MM3D Red|pathway}} the Deku people use to get to {{Color|MM3D Green|Woodfall Temple}}. But you must hurry! If you don't, I will be punished by the Deku King!|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> If Link returns to the [[Royal Chamber]] after this, he can witness the Foolish Monkey being repeatedly dipped into the cauldron of boiling water.<ref>{{Cite|How about that? ...Hot, isn't it? The monkey shall suffer and suffer 'til he can suffer no more! You shall know the wrath of a king whose darling princess was taken away from him!!!|Deku King|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Suffer! I shall prolong his suffering! Foolish {{Color|MM3D Red|monkey}}!|Deku King|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ooh! Hot! Hot! St-stop it! Please!!! They keep saying that I kidnapped her. No matter how many times they say it, it's not going to bring the princess back!|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> After the Monkey is dipped in the water, he will appear lifeless and remain silent.<ref>{{Cite|.........|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref>

After Link rescues the Deku Princess and returns her to the Royal Chamber, she chastises her father for his hasty behavior and commands the Deku Scrubs to release the Foolish Monkey.<ref>{{Cite|WHAT are you doing?!? Let that monkey go this instant!!|Deku Princess|MM3D}}</ref> She then apologizes to the Monkey for his treatment,<ref name="Mr."/> which he dismisses.<ref>{{Cite|I understand, Princess. Forget about that. Has the temple been returned to normal?|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> The Deku Princess explains to him that Link cleared the evil from the Temple and returned the Southern Swamp to normal.<ref>{{Cite|Why yes, thanks to Mr. Link here. I am very, truly grateful.|Deku Princess|MM3D}}</ref> The Foolish Monkey then thanks Link for his help and rejoices in the fact that peace between the Monkeys and the [[Deku Kingdom]] has been restored,<ref>{{Cite|Really? So you're called Link, are you? Thank you for keeping your promise to me.|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> though he still senses an unknown danger.<ref>{{Cite|Link, I'm sorry for all that trouble. Now it looks like we monkeys and the Deku Scrubs can start getting along again. ...You know, the swamp may be back to normal, but something still gives me a bad feeling... I wonder what it could be...|Foolish Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> If Link speaks to the Deku Scrubs who oversaw the Foolish Monkey's torture, they will lament the fact that they were punishing an innocent Monkey.<ref>{{Cite|We were about to punish an innocent monkey!|Deku Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>

The Foolish Monkey can later be seen standing beside the Deku Princess in the credits.
