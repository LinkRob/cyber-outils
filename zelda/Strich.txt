==Biography==

Strich is one of the students of the [[Knight Academy]] in [[Skyloft]]. Alongside his friends [[Groose]] and [[Cawlin]], they have a reputation for being bullies. During the [[Wing Ceremony]] race, he and Cawlin will throw eggs at [[Link]] to help Groose win. Strich can be found in the [[Lumpy Pumpkin]] during the day, and in his and Cawlin's room at the Knight Academy at night. He has an affinity for [[Bug#Bugs in Skyward Sword|insects]] and is willing to buy them from Link.<ref>{{Cite|Look, {{Color|SS Red|I just really like bugs}}... I want to collect all kinds of bugs. Sometimes, I even dream about having, like, an entire island full of bugs...|Strich|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Link, here's the plan. {{Color|SS Red|If you find a bug, come sell it to me}}. When it comes to bugs, Rupees are no object.|Strich|SS}}</ref> However, he will only buy three varieties of insects on any given night, according to his mood.<ref>{{Cite|I won't just buy any bug, though. What I buy on any given day just depends on the {{Color|SS Red|mood}} I'm in.|Strich|SS}}</ref>

Once [[Levias]] has been released from the control of the [[Bilocyte]], Strich can be found on [[Bug Rock]] in the [[Thunderhead]], where he runs a bug-catching mini-game called the [[Bug Heaven]]. When [[Beedle]]'s [[Horned Colossus Beetle]] goes missing, Strich is revealed to have found it.<ref>{{Cite|Well... Let's just say I managed to land a legendary {{Color|SS Blue|Horned Colossus Beetle}}.|Strich|SS}}</ref> However, he refuses to return the bug unless Link collects 10 specific insects under three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|If you can pull off a time in my game that makes even me admit you are the master of bug catching, I'll give this little guy back.|Strich|SS}}</ref>
