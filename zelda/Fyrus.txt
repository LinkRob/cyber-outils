{{Term/Store|Fyrus||Series, TP, TPHD}} is the {{Term|TPHD|Boss|link}} of the [[Goron Mines]] in {{TP}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 178}}</ref> and the second overall boss of the game.

==Biography==
Following a disturbance of volcanic activity in [[Death Mountain]], [[Darbus]], the patriarch of the [[Goron]]s, visited the [[Goron Mines]] to investigate the cause as well as to check on the status of its [[Fused Shadow]]. Fyrus is the form that Darbus assumed after being transformed by its power.<ref>{{Cite|We have a treasure that was entrusted to us by the spirits, and we must protect it. Do you understand? But the moment Darbus reached out and touched the treasure...everything went wrong. He collapsed...and before our very eyes transformed into an unspeakable monster!|Gor Coron|TP}}</ref> Due to his ferocity and inability to control himself as a result of the mutation, in addition to causing destruction in the area, the Goron elders incarcerated him deep within there to prevent him from causing further damage.<ref>{{Cite|He began to rage through the mines, trailing ruin behind him...and the eruptions grew more frequent and more severe. We used all of our strength to seal him deep inside the mountain...|Gor Coron|TP}}</ref>

When [[Link]] enters Fyrus's chamber, he finds that he is chained to pillars within the room. When Fyrus detects Link, the jewel on his forehead lights up and he ignites before breaking the chains binding him. Fyrus will then attack Link with three attacks, such as using the broken chains as whips, unleasing a massive fire wave from around or by breathing fire, Fyrus must first be stunned by shooting the glowing jewel on his head with an [[Arrow]] using the [[Hero's Bow]], causing him to run around in pain for a short while. Link must then equip the [[Iron Boots]] and grab the end of one of the chains attached to Fyrus's legs and pull, causing him to fall over. Afterward, the jewel on his forehead becomes vulnerable and Link can attack Fyrus with his [[Sword]] before he recovers. Alternatively, Link can shoot Fyrus's jewel and then wait until he calms down, then shoot him again. Eventually he will fall down and can be instantly defeated with the finishing blow.

Once Fyrus is defeated, the Twilight matter from his altered form reforms into the second Fused Shadow. Link is able to look upon a confused Darbus as the Goron returns to his natural form, as well as having no recollection of what become of him.<ref>{{Cite|Uhhgh... Urrgh... ...Hmm? What am I doing here? Unngh... My head, it aches... Urrrgh... So much pain... I cannot remember anything...|Darbus|TP}}</ref> Link is also awarded with a [[Heart Container]].

==Nomenclature==
The name "Fyrus" is a portmanteau of the word "fire" and Darbus, the boss' original form. The name also sounds similar to the word "virus", playing on Darbus' mutation incited by the Fused Shadow.

== Trivia ==

* Fyrus is the first and only boss in ''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess'' to only have one phase in the battle, while the other bosses have two or more phases in their battle.
* If Link hides behind one of the many pillars in the arena before Fyrus wakes up after being knocked down, Fyrus will look around the arena to try and find Link. This will go on until Fyrus discovers him.
