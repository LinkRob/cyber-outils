{{Term/Store|Rauru||Series, OoT, OoT3D}},<ref name="E 239"/> also known as '''Kaepora Gaebora''' when in his [[Kaepora Gaebora|owl form]], is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}. He is one of the ancient [[Sage|Sages of Hyrule]], specifically the Sage of Light.

==Biography==
===Era of Chaos===
During the [[Era of Chaos]] and shortly before the [[Kingdom of Hyrule]] was established by the [[Royal Family of Hyrule|royal descendants]] of the goddess [[Hylia]]'s [[Princess Zelda|mortal incarnation]], Rauru built the [[Temple of Time]] over the remains of the [[Sealed Temple]] that was located deep within the [[Faron Woods]] in the [[Faron Province]]. In doing this, he protected the [[Triforce]] from the chaotic [[Interloper War]] being fought over its ownership at the time.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 87}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 11}}</ref> Rauru named the temple after the [[Temple of Time]] from the legends of the events of {{SS}} about a [[Link|hero]] who had wielded the [[Master Sword]] and traveled between two time periods.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 25}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 25}}</ref> He chose to remain within the Temple of Light, situated in the center of the [[Sacred Realm]], only leaving it in the form of the massive, wise and watchful owl known as [[Kaepora Gaebora]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 77}}</ref> Knowledge of the Sacred Realm passed onto the [[Sheikah]] tribe and Rauru's name faded into obscurity.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 25}}</ref>

==={{OoT|-}}===
In the events of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', after [[Link]] defeats [[Gohma]] in a failed attempt to save the [[Great Deku Tree]], he leaves the [[Kokiri Forest]] and has his first encounter with Rauru, who awaits him in his owl form as Kaepora Gaebora at the edge of the woods. The owl gives him encouragement on the adventure the Great Deku Tree has tasked him with and advises him to visit [[Princess Zelda]] at [[Hyrule Castle]] before leaving with a promise that they will meet again.<ref>{{Cite|Hoo hoot! Link... Look up here! It appears that the time has finally come for you to start your adventure! You will encounter many hardships ahead... That is your fate. Don't feel discouraged, even during the toughest times! Go straight this way and you will see Hyrule Castle. You will meet a princess there... If you are lost and don't know which way to go, look at the Map. The areas you have explored will be shown on the Map. Press START to enter the Subscreens and (Z) or (R) to find the Map. On the Map Subscreen, you will also see a flashing dot showing you which way you should go next. Did you get all that? All right then, I'll see you around! Hoot hoot hoot ho!|Kaepora Gaebora|OoT}}</ref>
[[File:MM Kaepora Gaebora Model.png|thumb|Kaepora Gaebora, Rauru's owl alter-ego, as seen in-game]]
True to his word, Kaepora Gaebora often appears to tell Link about a new area whenever the young hero comes upon it, and he picks him up and carries him twice when Link could not progress on his own. Once Link reaches the [[Spirit Temple]], after navigating between the eras of his childhood and adulthood, Kaepora Gaebora compliments him on his growth and bids farewell after giving one final piece of advice on how to defeat the evil witches, [[Twinrova]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey, what's up, Link? Surprised to see me? A long time in this world is almost nothing to you, is it? How mysterious! Even I thought that the tales of a boy who could travel back and forth through time was merely a legend. Link, you have fully matured as an adult. From now on, the future of all the people in Hyrule is on your shoulders. Maybe it's not my time anymore. Here is my last advice. Two witches inhabit this temple. In order to destroy them, turn their own magic against them. Hoo hoot! I will continue to watch you... Hoo hoo hoo hoot!|Kaepora Gaebora|OoT}}</ref>

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{ImageCaption|image=File:TWW Rauru Stained Glass.png|size=130px|align=left|caption=Rauru as seen in Hyrule Castle|captionalign=center}}
In {{TWW}}, as it takes place in the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Adult Timelime]], Rauru, like the other Sages who helped seal Ganondorf within the Sacred Realm, is memorialized within one of the stained glass windows in Hyrule Castle's basement, where the Master Sword is now enshrined. The [[Royal Crest]] is depicted directly above him.

==Trivia==
*The exact connection between Rauru and the owl Kaepora Gaebora was left up for interpretation and debate by fans from ''Ocarina of Time''<nowiki/>'s release in 1998 until thirteen years later with {{HH}} in 2011, confirmed that they are indeed one and the same.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |page= 87}}</ref>
*Rauru is the only ancient [[Sage]] among the seven in ''Ocarina of Time'' and is likely the second-in-command.
*Rauru's role of guiding [[Link]] as Kaepora Gaebora is a reference to the [[Owl|mysterious owl]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'', as well as sharing similar importance with [[Navi]] and [[Sheik]].
*Rauru, like several other characters from {{OoT|-}}, was named after a town featured in ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link]]'', in his case, [[Rauru Town]].
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'', the character [[Gaepora]] bears a great deal of resemblance and personality to both Rauru and his owl incarnation.
*A cameo of Rauru can be seen in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD|Twilight Princess HD]]'' as a painting.
