{{Term/Store|Meat and Seafood Fry|Meat and Seafood Fries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Meat and Seafood Fries can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any meat and seafood together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made by using seafood with contradicting effects. Alternatively, seafood without any effects can be used, such as {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}}. When eaten, these meals will heal some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These dishes are noted to be filling and have fresh ingredients.<ref name="Inventory"/>

In [[The Old Man's Diary|his diary]], the {{Term|BotW|Old Man|link}} writes about making Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry, claiming it restores health and keeps him warm in the cold.<ref>{{Cite|On this desolate plateau, the only pleasure that brings me comfort is cooking. And today, I outdid myself! Truly, I created the perfect dish. I call it... spicy meat and seafood fry. This recipe not only restores health, but it also keeps me warm, even when traveling in the snowy mountains.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> Using this dish, he claims he'll no longer need his [[Warm Doublet]].<ref>{{Cite|With this dish on my side, I no longer have need of that itchy {{Color|BotW Blue|warm doublet}}.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> However, on the next page, he laments the fact that he didn't write down the recipe, only remembering that he used Raw Meat and Spicy Peppers.<ref>{{Cite|I do not know how I allowed this to happen, but it seems I forgot to write down a very important recipe. I know it contained raw meat and spicy pepper. However... I simply cannot remember what else I used! My age is catching up to me.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> Though it seems unlikely to him, if anyone were to know the final ingredient, he would give them his Warm Doublet.<ref>{{Cite|Sadly, on this lonely plateau, I have only my own knowledge and memory to rely on. Still... If I did find someone who knew the missing ingredient, I would happily reward them with my {{Color|BotW Blue|warm doublet}}. However, it seems unlikely that such a miraculous wish will ever be fulfilled.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> Link can make Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry for the Old Man, and he will be rewarded with a Warm Doublet if he shares the recipe.<ref>{{Cite|Wait... Is that... That looks just like my signature perfect dish, {{Color|BotW Blue|spicy meat and seafood fry}}! But how did you-?! Well, I suppose that is not important. Can I trouble you to share the recipe?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well done! Now, please allow me to reward your culinary efforts with this warm doublet.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He will later write in his diary about how Link is a fine young man who he sees potential in, as well as transcribing the recipe.<ref>{{Cite|To my surprise and delight, I did happen upon someone who was able to re-create my spicy meat and seafood fry. I gave him my {{Color|BotW Blue|warm doublet}}, and I was happy to do so. He is a fine young man with great potential, that one. Ah, and I will now write down this perfect recipe so that I will never forget it. Place some raw meat, spicy pepper, and hyrule bass in a pot, and then turn up the heat. Voilà!|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

Based on the type of seafood used and whether additional ingredients are added, the following variations of Meat and Seafood Fry can be made:

* '''Hearty Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. These meals will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}, or other energizing ingredients. Eating them will fill a part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. When eaten, they'll completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. If eaten, they'll increase Link's speed for a short period of time.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} or other spicy ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}} or other electric ingredients. Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when he eats these meals.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, or other mighty ingredients. When eaten, they'll increase Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}, or other tough ingredients. They increase Link's defense when he eats them.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}} or other sneaky ingredients. When eaten, they boost Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Meat and Seafood Fry''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Meat and Seafood Fry}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A filling dish made by cooking fresh seafood and meat together.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}} or other cold ingredients. These meals grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] when consumed.<ref name="Chilly"/>
