The {{Term/Store|Linebeck Trading Company||Series, ST}} is a company located at the {{Term|ST|Trading Post|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What can Linebeck Trading Company help you with?|Linebeck III|ST}}</ref> It specializes in trading {{Plural|ST|Train Car|link}} for {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}, although it also buys any kind of {{Term|ST|Treasure}} from its customers. The company's president is {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}}, grandson of the company's original founder, the great captain {{Term|ST|Linebeck|link}}.

After {{Term|ST|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|ST|Regal Ring|link}} from a cave in the {{Term|ST|Trading Post}}, he will be able to bring and sell any {{Term|ST|Treasure}} to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}. If he has enough {{Plural|ST|Treasure}}, he can also trade them for new {{Plural|ST|Train Car}}.<ref>{{Cite|Still, you've got a real talent for sniffing out treasure. Ooh! Tell you what--from here on out, bring any treasure you find to me! If they impress me, I'll trade you some lovely new train cars for those trinkets! Sound like a sweet deal? That's 'cause it IS!|Linebeck III|ST}}</ref>
