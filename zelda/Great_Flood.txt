The {{Name|Fan|Great Flood}} is the name often associated with a deluge that befell the land of [[Hyrule]] many generations after the end of {{OoT}} in the [[Adult Timeline]]. Described in the introduction of {{TWW}}, it occurred when [[Ganon]] escaped from his imprisonment in the [[Sacred Realm]] and returned to Hyrule, after the departure of the [[Hero of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|The great evil that all thought had been forever sealed away by the hero...once again crept forth from the depths of the earth, eager to resume its dark designs.|N/A|TWW}}</ref>

==Overview==
===Return of Ganon===
[[File:OOT Link sent to the past by Zelda.png|thumb|289x289px|Princess Zelda sending the Hero of Time to the past, as seen at the end of {{OoT|-}}]]
In the final events of {{OoT|-}}, [[Ganondorf]] was brutally defeated by the legendary Hero of Time and sealed inside the [[Sacred Realm]], which itself was corrupted by the [[Gerudo]] king to become the [[Dark World|Evil Realm]] due to himself touching the [[Triforce]] at one point. While Hyrule enters a longtime peace, [[Princess Zelda]] permanently sends [[Link]] back to his childhood via the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]] and [[Triforce of Wisdom]] (not knowing she [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|split the timeline into two]] and Link was sent into the Child Timeline instead). This also caused the hero's [[Triforce of Courage]] to shatter apart into [[Triforce Shard|eight pieces]] and scattered throughout the land. However, this decision by the princess, in spite of her good intentions, was not exactly wise, as the Adult Timeline - the game's universe, was ultimately left without a hero. 
[[File:TWW Scroll of the Hero of Time 4.png|thumb|379x379px|Ganon breaking free from the Six Sages' seal hundreds of years after his defeat by the Hero of Time]]
Many generations after the departure of Link, the powerful seal of the [[Sage|Six Sages]] began to weaken for an unknown reason, and the evil warlock ultimately managed to escape his imprisonment as his beast form, Ganon. Now free, the villain intended to make good on his furious threat to the Hero of Time and the Seven Sages that he would escape one day to make their descendants pay dearly for his defeat and long imprisonment, due to himself still having the [[Triforce of Power]].<ref>{{Cite|Once, long ago, this land of Hyrule was turned into a world of shadows by Ganon, who sought to obtain the power of the gods for his own evil ends.|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Someday...When this seal is broken....That is when I will exterminate your descendants!!|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref> As the darkness was quickly covering the land, the people of Hyrule prayed that the Hero of Time would once again come to save them. But the legendary hero did not appear at all, as this particular Link, as well as his allies from his era, no longer exist.<ref>{{Cite|The people believed that the Hero of Time would again come to save them. ...But the hero did not appear.|N/A|TWW}}</ref> Because of this, the villain manages to completely take over Hyrule once again, as he has no opposition whatsoever.

===The Divine Deluge===
[[File:TWW Scroll of the Hero of Time 5.png|306x306px|thumb|left|The people of Hyrule pray to the Goddesses for deliverance, resulting in the Great Flood, as seen in the prologue of {{TWW|-}}]]
The current [[King of Hyrule]], [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]], attempted to use his magic to stop Ganon, but failed. Left with no other choice, the people, including the king, pleaded to the three [[Golden Goddess|Golden Goddesses]].<ref>{{Cite|My power alone could not stop the fiend, and our only choice was to leave the fate of the kingdom in the hands of the gods...|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Faced by an onslaught of evil, the people could do nothing but appeal to the gods. In their last hour, as doom drew nigh, they left their future in the hands of fate.|N/A|TWW}}</ref> The Goddesses eventually answered the kingdom's prayers, instructing those chosen to flee to Hyrule's mountaintops in order to escape the coming flood and to prevent Ganon's wish of the destruction of the land. Once the people were safely away, the Goddesses caused a gigantic torrential downpour, covering the entire land and turning the mountaintops into islands, forming the [[Great Sea]].<ref>{{Cite|When the gods heard our pleas, they chose to seal away not only Ganon, but Hyrule itself...and so, with a torrential downpour of rains from the heavens... Our fair kingdom was soon buried beneath the waves, forgotten at the bottom of the ocean. Yet all was not lost. For the gods knew that to seal the people away with the kingdom would be to grant Ganon's wish for the destruction of the land. So, before the sealing of the kingdom, the gods chose those who would build a new country and commanded them to take refuge on the mountaintops.|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref> The king chose to remain behind with his doomed kingdom. He then split the Triforce of Wisdom into two parts and instructed the [[Princess Zelda#Antediluvian Princess|princess]] of that era to keep one piece and flee to the mountaintops with her servants.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The gods chose which of the kingdom's subjects would ascend to the new land, telling them to escape to the tallest mountains. They then flooded the world, sinking Ganondorf and Hyrule to the bottom of the ocean and sealing them away. Though the king was sealed along with his kingdom, the princess took a piece of the Triforce that had been broken in two by the king and managed to escape to the ocean's surface with several of her retainers.|book= HH |publisher= |page= 123}}</ref>

Ganon's army had already infiltrated [[Hyrule Castle]] when the goddesses sealed the kingdom within an enchanted air chamber that prevented the water from destroying it. A magical seal was also cast over the [[Master Sword]] and the [[Pedestal of the Master Sword|Pedestal of Time]] that froze the entire land of Hyrule and Ganon's army, freezing them in time and draining the entire kingdom of color.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, boy... When you drew that sword of yours out of its resting place... Did you by chance notice how all of the monsters frozen in time down there suddenly began stirring again?[...] While that sword is indeed the blade of evil's bane, at the same time, it has long played another role... You see, it is also a sort of key... a most wretched little key that has kept the seal on me and my magic intact! By withdrawing the blade, you have broken that seal...|Ganondorf|TWW}}</ref>
[[File:Hero of Time Legend.png|thumb|217x217px|The legend of the Hero of Time survives the Great Flood]]

The people of Hyrule would then go on to build a new civilization on the surface of the Great Sea and, with the passing of time, largely forget the memory of the old Hyrule that still existed deep beneath the waves. However, the legend of the Hero of Time also still manages to survive.<ref>{{Cite|The memory of the kingdom vanished, but its legend survived on the wind's breath.|N/A|TWW}}</ref>

===Aftermath===
{{Main|The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker}}
[[File:Final Battle in Hyrule.jpg|261x261px|thumb|right|The final duel between Ganondorf, Link and Princess Zelda in Hyrule's final moments]]
Many centuries after the initial flood, Ganon, now back in his less powerful Gerudo form, Ganondorf, broke free of the seal binding Hyrule and returned a second time, intending to reunite the [[Triforce]] pieces to reconquer the sunken Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|Do not fear. I will not kill you... I merely have need of the power that dwells within you.|Ganondorf|TWW}}</ref> This caused an utterly new incarnation of Link and Princess Zelda to appear to challenge Ganondorf again, as had their predecessors. After eventually uniting the Triforce, and at the will of the old King rather than Ganondorf, the sacred pieces rise into the air and vanish as the air chamber above them begins to collapse, allowing the Great Sea to ultimately pour into Hyrule. After Ganondorf is finally defeated for good, Hyrule is completely swallowed by water, leaving the Hero of the Winds and Princess Zelda ([[Tetra]]) to create a new Hyrule above the waves, leading to the events of [[The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass|{{PH|-}}]], and eventually {{ST}} with their own descendants.

==Trivia==
*The [[Golden Goddess|Goddesses]] flooding [[Hyrule]] to cleanse evil from the land, at the sacrifice of most life while allowing a select few to survive, is similar to many {{Wp|flood myth}}s from real-life, specifically the [[wikipedia:Genesis_flood_narrative|Great Flood of Noah]].
*Oddly, although the "[[Song of Time]]" is normally used to make [[Link]] travel through time using the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]], [[Princess Zelda]] uses her own theme song, "[[Zelda's Lullaby]]", to send the [[Hero of Time]] into the past.
*A hint of the Great Flood's eventual occurrence can be seen in the ending of {{OoT}}, when Princess Zelda tells Link that [[Ganondorf]] was sealed into the [[Dark World|Evil Realm]], she says it will last "for a time", ''not'' permanently. With this in mind, this would make Zelda's ultimate choice of sending the Hero of Time back to his childhood especially foolish, and [[Ganon]]'s eventual escape and takeover in the Adult Timeline already foreshadowed. Of course, she herself is also not aware of Link's own longtime sufferings that would ultimately [[Hero's Spirit|haunt him]] in the events of the Child Timeline due to her actions.[[File:TWW Scroll of the Hero of Time 3.png|thumb|312x312px|The Hero of Time defeating Ganon as a child as explained in the intro of {{TWW|-}}, which does not hold true to the Era of the Hero of Time]]
*The introduction sequence of {{TWW}} explaining the events of the Great Flood showed the Hero of Time defeating Ganon as a child and riding on [[Epona]]. This is inaccurate to the events of {{OoT|-}}, as Link was actually an adult when he took down the Prince of Darkness and himself riding on his horse as a child is impossible, as Epona was too young. However, he did manage to ride her in {{MM}}, but this game takes place in the Child Timeline.
