==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Keese Eyeballs are {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by all types of {{Plural|BotW|Keese|link}} upon their defeats. Like other {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, they can be used as an ingredient to make [[Elixir]]s. However, they can also be used to make Orange [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] or exchanged for [[Mon]] at the [[Fang and Bone]].

The [[Soldier's Armor]] set requires Keese eyeball to upgrade each piece to its second tier at a [[Great Fairy Fountain#Breath%20of%20the%20Wild|Great Fairy Fountain]].

* Total for '''Soldier's Armor''': 15 (0, 5, 0, 0) x3


==={{TotK|-}}===

Keese Eyeballs can be fused with Arrows, giving them plus 1 damage and the ability to home in on nearby enemies. They are also used for Elixirs just like in Breath of the Wild.<blockquote>These mysterious orbs scan their surroundings. Take advantage of their glaring awareness, and attach one to an arrow to grant an homing quality to the arrow. Fuse Attack Power: 1.</blockquote>
