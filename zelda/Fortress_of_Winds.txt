The {{Term/Store|Fortress of Winds||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> It is large complex that was once inhabited by the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}};<ref name=weleft>{{Cite|This is the home of the Wind Tribe. We left the surface long ago. Now, we guard the entrance to the Palace of Winds... ...In this palace, the Wind Element resides.|N/A|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Although we are of the Wind Tribe, we, too, once lived on the surface. Once, the place you call the Wind Ruins was, to us, a home.|Caprice|TMC}}</ref> however, they abandoned it and traveled to the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The people who built the Wind Ruins. They now live above the clouds, suspended by their own magic ability to control the wind.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Our tribe abandoned the place you call the Wind Ruins... With our magic, we moved our palace up into the skies. We are the Wind Tribe.|Siroc|TMC}}</ref> making it serve as the third {{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}} in the game. Instead of receiving the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}} from this {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}, which the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} took with them to the skies, the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}} is obtained, allowing {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to fly to any uncovered {{Term|TMC|Wind Crest|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}.<ref name=weleft/><ref>{{Cite|I do believe this is where the Tribe of the Winds once lived. Hm... So they've left for the heavens, and taken their element with them.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Fortress==
The {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}} is located deep within the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|link}}. To access the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must bypass the {{Term|TMC|Armos|link}} statues scattered across the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins}} and make his way to the northeastern section of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|display=Ruins}}, where the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}} is situated. Shortly after entering the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}}, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} will warn {{Term|TMC|Link}} to watch his step while in the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|display=Fortress}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm. To think the ruins were hidden within this cliff the whole time! Watch your step, Link. There may be traps in here.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} is effectively a three-dimensional maze, as it has three floors and rooms on the same floor are rarely connected, more so than in most other {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon}}. Thus, it routes from one part of the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} to another involving both horizontal travel through part of a given floor, and vertical travel between floors via ladders or holes that {{Term|TMC|Link}} must fall through.<ref>{{Cite|Hmn?! Did something just fall down, or was it just my imagination?|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> Much of the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} requires the use of the {{Term|TMC|Bow|link}} to hit {{Plural|TMC|Eyeball Switch|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} in order for {{Term|TMC|Link}} to hold down multiple floor {{Plural|TMC|Switch|link}} or push large blocks, and the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}} in order to cross temporary bridges.<ref>{{Cite|Phew...! We barely made it! I was certain we'd run out of time!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> The main items of the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} are the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts|link}}, which are instrumental for navigation, as much of the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} is filled with loose earth that must be tunneled through to proceed. Sections of the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}} require {{Term|TMC|Link}} to become {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized to access other areas. A {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} requires {{Term|TMC|Link}} to shrink down and fall down from the third floor, enter a crack in the wall, and reemerge from a {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}} to obtain it.

There area total of five {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} within the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}}. On the opposite sides of the first room, there is a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} and a large green {{Term|TMC|Rupee|link}}. The middle-left room from the first room leads to an area with two {{Plural|TMC|Treasure Chest|display=Chests}} that contain {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}}. The following rooms contain {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} within {{Plural|TMC|Treasure Chest|display=Chests}} that are surrounded by loose earth. The {{Term|TMC|Compass|link}} is found in a room guarded by two {{Plural|TMC|Eyegore Statue|link}}. To activate them, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must charge the {{Term|TMC|White Sword}} and create a copy of himself to step on the two {{Plural|TMC|Switch}} in front of them. Once destroyed, the {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}} containing the {{Term|TMC|Compass}} will appear. The {{Term|TMC|Dungeon Map|link}} is located on a raised platform in the central pathway on the second floor, though {{Plural|TMC|Eyegore Statue}} block the path to it.

A {{Term|TMC|Red Dark Nut|link}} serves as one of the {{Plural|TMC|Sub-Boss}}. When defeated, a blue portal appears, leading back to the entrance of the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}. The {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts}} are located in a room behind a {{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}}-destructible wall in a narrow passageway following the {{Term|TMC|Dark Nut|link}}'s chamber. Within the same room is a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}} containing 100 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}}. The other {{Plural|TMC|Sub-Boss}} are two {{Plural|TMC|Wizzrobe|link}} aided by four malicious {{Plural|TMC|Torch|link}}, which guard a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}} with 80 {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}}. The red portal appears near the {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}}'s chamber. To activate it, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must push a lone block towards the nearest wall, making it symmetrical with the other blocks in the room. The {{Term|TMC|Big Key|link}} is located in the southeastern-most part of the second floor. To reach it, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must descend from the right hole on the eastern section of the third floor. The left hole will lead {{Term|TMC|Link}} to the left divide of the platform containing the {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}}, which {{Term|TMC|Link}} can jump down from to try again.

The {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}} is home to {{Term|TMC|Mazaal|link}}, an automated mechanism that serves to defend the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|display=Fortress}} from intruders.<ref>{{Cite|Appears in the Fortress of Winds. Constructed by the Wind Tribe to repel intruders, this sturdy machine cannot be destroyed by any external force.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> Unusual for {{TLoZ|Series}}, the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}} has a post-{{Term|TMC|Boss}} area. After defeating {{Term|TMC|Mazaal}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} ascends to the roof of the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}, where he finds the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}} and a plaque telling of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}}'s departure to the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}} and the true location of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We are the Tribe of The Winds. Long have we lived with the winds. We have mastered them. Now, we join them. Together, with the great winds at our backs, we head for the skies. Those who come seeking our power must play the notes Zeffa teaches. May they lead you onward to the power that you seek.|Stone Tablet|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I do believe this is where the Tribe of the Winds once lived. Hm... So they've left for the heavens, and taken their element with them. It looks like the Wind Element is beyond our reach for the time being. We have no choice but to press on. Let's get out of here.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Despite its name, the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}} is not wind-themed in any way whatsoever. The name refers to the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} who previously inhabited the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|display=Fortress}}.
*The {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}} is one of only two main {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon}} in {{TMC|-}} that do not reward an {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link|display=Element}} upon completion, the other being {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}.
