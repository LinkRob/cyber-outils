{{Term/Store|Cursed Bokoblin|Cursed Bokoblins|Series, SS, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 172 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Cursed Bokoblin}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They are an undead variant of [[Bokoblin]] though their appearances differ between games.

==Characteristics==
==={{SS|-}}===
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Target lock: {{Color|SS Blue|Cursed Bokoblin}}</p><p>This horrifying Bokoblin reanimates after death. Analysis indicates it fears pure, shiny items and will {{Color|SS Red|startle at the sight of a sacred shield}}.</p><p>It is able to reanimate purely through its hatred of this world...and its attachment to outlandish underpants.</p>

Undead whose hatred accords them the ability to reanimate, {{Plural|SSHD|Cursed Bokoblin}} take on a decaying appearance — with skeletal and bandaged limbs, a lone glowing yellow eye and with one of their horns chipped — and this is further exemplified by the flies swarming around them. They are primarily encountered in the deepest depths of the {{Term|SSHD|Ancient Cistern|link}}, though they also appear in the {{Term|SSHD|Sky Keep|link}}. If {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} is nearby, {{Plural|SSHD|Cursed Bokoblin}} will emerge from the ground in large groups and pursue him, attacking by latching onto {{Term|SSHD|Link}} and biting him. In addition to dealing damage, their bite also places a {{Term|SSHD|Curse|link}} on {{Term|SSHD|Link}}.

{{Plural|SSHD|Cursed Bokoblin}} recoil from shiny objects, which {{Term|SSHD|Link}} can exploit by holding up either his {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword|link}} while it is charged with the {{Term|SSHD|Skyward Strike|link}} or the {{Term|SSHD|Sacred Shield|link}} (or one of its upgrades) to keep them at bay.<ref>{{Cite|Analysis indicates it fears pure, shiny items and will {{Color|SSHD Red|startle at the sight of a sacred shield}}.|Fi|SSHD}}</ref> {{Plural|SSHD|Cursed Bokoblin}} can withstand many blows before succumbing, and they often get back up. Using {{Plural|SSHD|Bomb|link}} or performing a {{Term|SSHD|Fatal Blow|link}} will instantly defeat them, at which point they may drop an {{Term|SSHD|Evil Crystal|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Cursed Bokoblin}}
Cursed Bokoblins appear in {{BotW}} as floating heads, similar to [[Bubble]]s, and are spawned from Malice. They are only found within the four [[Divine Beast]]s and [[Hyrule Castle]], charging at Link if he is within range. The only way to stop Cursed Bokoblins from spawning is to destroy the eye that is creating them.

==Other Appearances==
{{Ambig|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
Curse Bokoblins reappear in {{HWAoC}}, spawning when [[Stalkoblin]]s are defeated. They follow the player around and charge at them. Unlike {{BotW|-}}, they are not spawned from Malice and can be permanently defeated.
