[[File:TLoZ Series Triforce of Courage Artwork.png|thumb|The Triforce of Courage resonating|337x337px]]
The '''Triforce of Courage''' is one of the three pieces of the [[Triforce]]. It embodies the essence of the Goddess of Courage, [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]], the source of all life. <ref>{{Cite|Farore, the goddess of courage... [...] With her rich soul, produced all life forms who would uphold the law.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> Despite its mystical abilities are not as known or displayed in comparison to the [[Triforce of Power]] and [[Triforce of Wisdom]] within the core series, the main protagonist, [[Link]], is almost always the Triforce of Courage's bearer. The sacred triangle, however, is more than just a mere symbol of his own exemplary courage;<ref>{{Cite|My only mistake was to slightly underestimate the power of this kid... No... It was not the kid's power I misjudged, it was the power of the Triforce of Courage!|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref> Although Link himself is not in the same level of skill at using [[magic]] like that of [[Ganon]] or [[Princess Zelda]] (who bear the Power and Wisdom pieces, respectively), as he on contrast focuses on sword fighting, the Triforce of Courage still grants him untold magical powers due to the essence of Farore - one of them being protection against potent evil magic. It is likely it also increases Link's courage even further to unnatural proportions. Though it does not affect gameplay and cannot be used as an [[item]], usually, in installments where the Triforce had split into its three component parts and when Link must face Ganon or another potentially dangerous antagonist, the hero obtains this piece of the Triforce first to make himself powerful enough to defeat them and save the land, giving it an essential role. It represents the lower-right piece of the Triforce.

Unlike its counterparts, the Triforce of Courage did not make its first appearance in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', but was rather added in its direct sequel, ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]''. This would also technically mark the debut of the completed Triforce as well.

==Mainline appearances==

=== ''The Legend of Zelda'' ===
The Triforce of Courage does not exist in the original ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]''. However, an artwork from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time — Official Nintendo Player's Guide|The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time - Official Nintendo Player's Guide]]'' referring to this game shows the fragment alongside the other sacred triangles as the full Triforce.

=== ''The Adventure of Link'' ===
[[File:TAoL Triforce of Courage.png|thumb|233x233px|The Triforce of Courage being awarded to Link by the [[Sage]] upon ultimately defeating his shadow self in ''The Adventure of Link'']]
[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|''The Adventure of Link'']] is the installment in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] where the Triforce of Courage makes its debut. Here, it is revealed that a previous [[King of Hyrule]], fearing the misuse of the Triforce, sealed the Triforce of Courage away in the [[Great Palace]], where only a hero worthy of its power would be able to obtain it after proving himself.<ref name="Quote3">{{Cite Manual|quote= |game= TAoL|page= 9-10}}</ref> [[Link]] endured the trials of the Great Palace, including defeating his [[Link's Shadow|own shadow]], and recovered the Triforce of Courage. Uniting it with the Triforce of Wisdom and Power he obtained in his previous quest to become the full Triforce, he was able to break the powerful spell cast on the sleeping [[Princess Zelda]] and finally awakens from her deep slumber. 

=== ''A Link to the Past'' ===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', the Triforce of Courage can be seen alongside its counterparts on the title screen and credits. In the events of the game, [[Ganon]] used the completed Triforce to transform the [[Sacred Realm|Golden Land]] into the [[Dark World]], while Link, after ultimately destroying the Demon King with the [[Silver Arrow]] and [[Master Sword]], also uses it to completely restore [[Hyrule]] back to normal. Therefore, like the other sacred triangles in ''The Adventure of Link'', the Triforce of Courage was never obtained or used by itself in this title, making the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]] installment the only case in the core series Link did not specifically possess it.

=== ''Ocarina of Time'' ===
[[File:Triforce of Courage in OoT.webp|thumb|327x327px|The Triforce of Courage glowing on Link's hand, as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', the Triforce of Courage plays a major role for the first time and would now become Link's standard piece for the rest of the long-running franchise. It is revealed that after [[Ganondorf]], the human form of Ganon, touched the Triforce in the [[Sacred Realm]] shortly after Link pulled the Master Sword, the Triforce itself split into three pieces. The Triforce of Courage was imparted to the main protagonist, while the other two, the Triforce of Power and Triforce of Wisdom, go to Ganondorf and Princess Zelda, respectively. During the course of the game, Link himself as the [[Hero of Time]] does not appear to know he has the blessings of the [[Golden Goddess|Goddess]] [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]] until [[Sheik]] explains the [[Sheikah/Sheikah Legends|Sheikah legends]] just before the final showdown.<ref>{{Cite|The one who holds the Triforce of Courage is... You, Link!|Sheik|OoT}}</ref> At the start of the battle against the [[Gerudo]] warlock, as the three Triforce parts resonate, Link's [[Life Gauge]] completely refills due to his piece's mystical power. The Triforce of Courage itself is also likely what protected him from the blast of Ganondorf's rage via destroying the top of [[Ganon's Castle]] upon his apparent defeat. After Ganon himself is truly defeated and sealed inside the [[Dark World|Evil Realm]], upon Princess Zelda sending Link back to his childhood for good and [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|splitting the timeline into two]], the Triforce of Courage for the Adult Timeline ultimately shatters into many pieces and scattered throughout the kingdom, similar to the Triforce of Wisdom in the case of ''The Legend of Zelda''.

Meanwhile, in the Child Timeline, Link, still the bearer of the sacred triangle, uses it as evidence to the [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Family]] at [[Hyrule Castle]] to explain Ganondorf's conspiracy and to have the [[Gerudo]] warlock eventually "[[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|executed]]". He then embarks on [[Termina|another journey]] and leaves the land of Hyrule, as seen in the game's direct sequel, {{MM}}. This causes the legendary Hero of Time to be separated from "the elements that made him a hero", and the Triforce of Courage itself to suddenly disappear (unfortunately losing its powers bestowed upon him).

=== ''Oracle of Seasons'' ===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]]'', at some point of the game, Link and [[Din (Oracle)|Din]] can be seen looking at the [[Mark of the Triforce|mark]] of the Triforce of Courage on the former's left hand.

=== ''The Wind Waker'' ===
[[File:WWHD Triforce of Courage.png|thumb|246x246px|Link holding the restored Triforce of Courage in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD|The Wind Waker HD]]'' ([[Second Quest]])]]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', long after the events of ''Ocarina of Time'', the Triforce of Courage, as [[Triforce Shard|eight shards]], remained at the bottom of the [[Great Sea]]. According to the [[King of Red Lions]] ([[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]]), despite this game technically takes place in the Adult Timeline, the result of the Triforce piece being broken is based on the Hero of Time's events in ''Majora's Mask'' rather than at the end of ''Ocarina of Time''.<ref name="Quote12">{{Cite|Once, long ago, he defeated Ganon and brought peace to the Kingdom of Hyrule... A piece of the Triforce was given to the Hero of Time and he kept it safe, much as Zelda kept hers. That sacred piece is known as the Triforce of Courage. When the Hero of Time was called to embark on another journey and left the land of Hyrule, he was separated from the elements that made him a hero. It is said that at that time, the Triforce of Courage was split into eight shards and hidden throughout the land.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> A new Link, after making the weakened Master Sword regain its [[Power to Repel Evil|power to repel evil]] during his adventure, would also eventually fully restore the sacred triangle and become its new bearer upon revealing it at the [[Tower of the Gods]], earning his title of Hero of Winds. During the final battle, Ganondorf, after easily knocking Link out, fully extracts the young hero's Triforce of Courage as well as Princess Zelda ([[Tetra]])'s Triforce of Wisdom and his own Triforce of Power to reform the complete Triforce, in order to make his wish to conquer Hyrule again, but ultimately fails when King Daphnes touched the powerful relic first and wished to the Golden Goddesses to wash away the old kingdom forever.

It is unknown who or what stored the eight shards of the Triforce of Courage inside [[Treasure Chest]]s for Link to lift them up and obtain the shattered pieces via the [[Grappling Hook]] with the King of Red Lions.

Along with the Triforce of Courage, the Triforce of Wisdom is also broken into pieces in this game - only two fragments. Here, the large piece was a necklace for Tetra until she becomes Princess Zelda when King Daphnes restores it with the other fragment.

=== ''Twilight Princess'' ===
[[File:TP Triforce of Courage glowing.png|thumb|245x245px|The Triforce of Courage resonating on Link's hand as he was grasped by a Shadow Beast in ''Twilight Princess'']]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', the Triforce of Courage can be seen on the back of Link's hand from the start (it is unknown how he earned the sacred piece). One of the known effects of the Triforce of Courage in said game is transforming Link into a [[Wolf Link|wolf]] instead of into a lost soul like every other being from the [[Light World]].<ref>{{Cite|In the land covered in twilight, where people roam as spirits, you were transformed into a blue-eyed beast... That was a sign... It was a sign that the powers of the chosen one rest within you...and that they are awakening.|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> The Triforce of Courage also repels the [[Shadow Beast]] who attempts to attack him when he is first dragged into the [[Twilight Realm]].

=== ''A Link Between Worlds'' ===
[[File:Triforce Courage Chamber SagesALBW.png|thumb|243x243px|The seven Sages awarding Link the Triforce of Courage, as seen in ''A Link Between Worlds'']]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', after [[Yuga]] fuses with Ganon to become [[Yuga Ganon]] and steals the [[Triforce of Power]], [[Princess Hilda]], while imprisoning him, instructs Link to awaken the seven [[Sage|Sages]], in which he obtains the Triforce of Courage. During the final battle, when Yuga Ganon further fuses with Hilda in [[Painting|portrait]] form, he takes away the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] as well which Hilda herself stolen from Princess Zelda. He also intends to steal Link's piece of the Triforce to make his power complete and take over [[Lorule]], but Link, using the power of the gods and the Master Sword, ultimately destroys the villain before he could do so.

=== ''Echoes of Wisdom'' ===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]'', Link, similar to in ''Ocarina of Time'', ended up getting the Triforce of Courage after [[Null]]'s attempt of using the complete Triforce (called Prime Energy in-game) to bring Hyrule into the void via [[Tekom]] failed. Tekom deduced this fragment going to Link and pursued it first, although she ultimately failed due to Princess Zelda, the holder of the Triforce of Wisdom, ultimately besting her in battle.

== Other Appearances ==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
===''[[Zelda: The Wand of Gamelon]]''===
At the start of the story, [[King Harkinian]] reassures Princess Zelda's worries of misfortune befalling him that he will take the Triforce of Courage with him to [[Gamelon]] as protection. As he was captured by Ganon anyway, and the Triforce of Courage is never mentioned again, its location and status are unknown as of the story's conclusion.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)]]''===
[[File:SSBB_Link_Triforce_Slash.jpg|thumb|244x244px|Link using Triforce Slash on Ganondorf in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
Starting with ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl|Brawl]]'', Link and his counterparts use the Triforce of Courage in their [[Final Smash]], known as [[Triforce Slash]], to bind opponents in one place while they attack furiously with their sword, dealing high damage and potentially launching them far enough to be KO'd.

===''[[Hyrule Warriors]]''===
Link is discovered to be the incarnation of the Hero when the Triforce of Courage shines from his hand during "[[The Armies of Ruin]]" as he confronts [[Wizzro]]. Impa notices this and when the Hyrulean Army falls back to Hyrule Castle to regroup, she provides him with the Hero's Tunic  as they venture forth to search for the missing Princess Zelda.

Link later loses the Triforce of Courage to Ganondorf at the end of "[[Battle of the Triforce]]" before reclaiming it in "[[Liberation of the Triforce]]" by destroying Ganon. He then continues to carry it from then onward.{{Noncanon|End}}

== Trivia ==

* Development-wise, the Triforce of Courage was not initially planned during the making of the original ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', as the [[Triforce]] itself did not start with three pieces and this part was added in its sequel. This implies the name "Triforce" originally referred to the shape of the pieces, instead of the numbers of the triangles as widely believed.
* In ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'', the Triforce of Courage was referred to as "'''No. 3 Triforce'''" or "'''No.3 Tryforce'''" in the Japanese version. However, this only applies to the original release and the latter being a mistranslation, as subsequent releases have it corrected as ''The third Triforce''.
* The Triforce of Courage is the only piece of the Triforce, by itself, never to be canonically stolen by [[Ganon]] or any other antagonist anywhere in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], unlike its [[Triforce of Power|Power]] and [[Triforce of Wisdom|Wisdom]] counterparts, although it was targeted by [[Null]] via [[Tekom]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]'', and [[Ganondorf]] did non-canonically steal the Triforce of Courage from Link in the Ganondorf chapter for ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]''.
* Unlike the Triforce of Power or Wisdom where they appear on Ganondorf and Princess Zelda's respective right hands, the Triforce of Courage is shown on Link's left hand, instead. This suggests that the marks appear on the user's dominant hand, since in {{OoT|-}}, {{TWW|-}}, and {{TP|-}}, Link is left-handed.
* In the post-[[credits]] cutscene of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', the Triforce of Courage can be seen resonating on Link's hand. Although it has never been stated outright by [[Nintendo]] about why it occurred, fans have speculated that the reason for it glowing is because when the [[Hero of Time]] returns back to his childhood into the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Child Timeline]], the other pieces from the [[Sacred Realm]] had automatically bestowed upon the young Zelda and Ganondorf, which is what would eventually led to the [[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|failed execution]] of the latter in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]''.
* Although it's unknown what inverted piece of the Triforce is shown in the artwork of the [[The Legend of Zelda: Mystical Seed of Courage|scrapped third entry of the ''Oracle'' games]] for the series (similar to as seen in ''The Legend of Zelda''), due to its title being ''Mystical Seed of Courage'', it is suggested to be the Triforce of Courage.
