{{Term/Store|Seahorse|Seahorses|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> also known as '''Gold-Colored Fish''',<ref>{{Cite|Mikau, haven't you seen any {{Color|MM3D Red|gold-colored fish}} around here?|Zora|MM3D}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Seahorse}} were referred to as '''Sea Horses''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Sea Horse}}<br/>Sea Horses are beautiful golden creatures fond of playfully swimming in the blue waters of Termina's Great Bay Coast.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160311084700/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/s.jsp#SeaHorse|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} are an animal species and a pair of characters in {{MM}}.

==Biography==
Two Seahorses live in [[Pinnacle Rock]] in the [[Great Bay]] region of [[Termina]]. One of the Seahorses was captured by the [[Fisherman]] living at the [[Great Bay Coast|coast]].<ref>{{Cite|I know it may be asking too much, but I have a request for you, brave one. Please bring back my friend who was caught by the fisherman.|Seahorse|MM}}</ref> The Fisherman will give [[Link]] the Seahorse in exchange for a good picture of one of the [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirate]]s.<ref>{{Cite|If you want that sea horse, bring me a pictograph of a female pirate.|Fisherman|MM}}</ref>

After Link gives the photo to the Fisherman, he will receive the Seahorse in exchange, who wishes to be taken to the waters near Pinnacle Rock.<ref>{{Cite|Hurry! Take me back to the waters near Pinnacle Rock...|Seahorse|MM}}</ref> Link carries it inside of a [[Bottle]]. Once in the waters of Pinnacle Rock, the Seahorse guides Link through the misty waters to the inner part of the area.<ref>{{Cite|Please follow me closely without making any missteps.|Seahorse|MM}}</ref> Here, the Seahorse explains to Link that his friend is trapped among the [[Deep Python]]s, and asks for the young hero's help.<ref>{{Cite|My friend is trapped here. Link, can you please find a way to rid the area of all the sea snakes and help my friend?|Seahorse|MM}}</ref> Once Link has killed each and every one of the sea serpents, the other Seahorse will be released. Both will thank Link for his help, and will reward him with a [[Piece of Heart]].

The Seahorse is described by the Fisherman as a rare specimen.<ref>{{Cite|It's a rare fish, isn't it? It's called a sea horse.|Fisherman|MM}}</ref> Seahorses, referred to as gold-colored fish, are known among the [[Zora]] for their familiarity with the waters around Pinnacle Rock, and are used as guides when water conditions are bad.<ref>{{Cite|Mikau, haven't you seen any gold-colored fish around here? Gold-colored fish know this area very well. I was thinking of getting one to guide me to Pinnacle Rock...|Zora|MM}}</ref>
