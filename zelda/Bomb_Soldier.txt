{{Term/Store|Bomb Soldier|Bomb Soldiers|Series, ALttP, FSA, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 168 {{Exp Game|ALttP, FSA, ALBW, TFH}}}}</ref> They are a variation of the enemy [[Soldier (Enemy)|Soldier]]s that throw [[Bomb]]s at [[Link]] as their main form of attack. In some instances, their Bombs can be thrown back at them to attack.

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, like other Soldiers, Bomb Soldiers are [[Hyrule]]an guards that have been brainwashed by [[Agahnim]] and are under his control. Bomb Soldiers situate themselves on the walls of [[Hyrule Castle]], where they throw Bombs at intruders. As they remain stationary, they can easily be defeated if Link can quickly close the gap between him and his assailant. It is possible to throw the Bombs back at them to cause damage.

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, two colored variations of the Bomb Soldier appear. The blue-colored Bomb Soldiers are the most common, whereas the green-colored Bomb Soldier is a stronger variety that appears in the [[Realm of the Heavens (Stage)|Realm of the Heavens]].

==={{ALBW|-}}===
Bomb Soldiers in {{ALBW}} are strongly based on their {{ALttP|-}} incarnation. Like other enemy Soldiers, the Bomb Soldiers were originally [[Painting]]s that were painted by and brought to life by [[Yuga]]. Much like in {{ALttP|-}}, Bomb Soldiers appear on the walls of Hyrule Castle, where they throw Bombs at Link from above. They also appear [[Inside Hyrule Castle]] as well, and toss Bombs from ledges.  While Bomb Soldiers are still relatively stationary, they will try to move closer from their post to better aim at Link. They will also attempt to back away from Link if he comes close to them. As  before, they can be easily defeated by closing in and striking back. Otherwise, Bombs can be thrown back at them or projectile weapons, such as [[Arrow]]s, can be shot at them. They can also be stunned by a wide variety of other items rented from [[Ravio]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, Bomb Soldiers appear in the [[Drablands#Fortress|Fortress]] Area of the [[Drablands]]. They appear the same as in {{ALBW|-}}, as they will actively make an effort at throwing Bombs at the [[Links]] and back away when they close in. In some instances, the Bomb Soldiers will throw Bombs from a ledge above or from over a gap, often requiring that the Links throw one Link over or form a [[Totem]] to reach the Soldier's level and attack. The Bombs they throw can also be used against them as well. In the final Stage of the [[Training Ground]], a Bomb Soldier also appears riding a [[Totem Armos]], and will actively pursue the Links while throwing Bombs.

In the [[Drablands#Sky Realm|Sky Realm]], a golden variety called '''Sky Bomb Soldiers''' also appear. This variety is much stronger than the ordinary Bomb Soldiers, and take more hits to defeat.
