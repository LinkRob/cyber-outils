The {{Term/Store|Romani Plains||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Romani Plains'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Romani Plains are located east of [[East Castle Town]] and bordered by the [[Applean Forest]] and [[Mabe Prairie]] in [[Hyrule Field]].

The remains of [[Guardian#Breath of the Wild|Guardian]]s can be found littered throughout the Plains. They can be searched for [[Ancient Material]]s.
