{{Term/Store|Amber Relic|Amber Relics|Series, SS|plural}} are items in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 115 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Amber Relics are found all over the [[Surface]]. Their primary use is for [[Upgrading|upgrading]] at the [[Scrap Shop]], but they can also be sold to [[Rupin]] during night at his home for 30 [[Rupee]]s.
