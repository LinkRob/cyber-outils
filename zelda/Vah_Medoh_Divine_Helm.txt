The {{Term/Store|Vah Medoh Divine Helm||Series, BotW}} is a piece of [[Armor]] in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Vah Medoh Divine Helm}}<br/>A treasure gifted to the one who controls Divine Beast Vah Medoh. It endows its user with enhanced abilities.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> and [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].

==Location and Uses==

=== Breath of the Wild ===
The Vah Medoh Divine Helm is obtained by scanning the [[Revali]] [[amiibo]] with the [[amiibo (Rune)|amiibo Rune]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= They'll summon materials and weapons, but unlike other amiibo, they'll conjure up headgear based on the Champions' Divine Beasts. They have functional purposes as well, and we'll have more info soon. |author= Nintendo |published= September 13, 2017 |retrieved=September 13, 2017|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ztMGTTAjsAQ |title= Nintendo Direct 9.13.2017 |site= YouTube |type= Video}}</ref> When worn, the Vah Medoh Divine Helm provides a basic level of both [[Guardian Resist Up]] and [[Cold Resistance]] and also allows Link to view an enemy's remaining health.<ref name="Facebook">{{Cite Web|quote= With the Champion amiibo coming out on November 10th, learn more about what each helm does in The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild!<br/><br/>When any of the Divine Helms are equipped with Ancient Cuirass and Ancient Greaves (All armor items need to be upgraded level 2 ★★ or above), you’ll also trigger a set bonus, Ancient Proficiency, which increases the attack power of Ancient or Guardian series weapons. All of them will increase guard from ancient energy attacks, while defense level and required materials for level-up are the same as those of Ancient Helm. Enemies' remaining HP/full HP will also be visible like the Champion's Tunic.<br/><br/>Additionally, there are specific effects for each item:<br/>- Vah Rudania Divine Helm: Flame Guard (Lv1)<br/>- Vah Ruta Divine Helm: Swim Speed Up (Lv1)<br/>- Vah Naboris Divine Helm: Electricity Resistance (Lv1)<br/>- Vah Medoh Divine Helm: Cold Resistance (Lv1) |author= Nintendo |published= November 3, 2017 |retrieved= November 3, 2017 |url= https://business.facebook.com/LegendofZelda/posts/1576388102422314 |title= With the Champion amiibo coming out on November... - The Legend of Zelda |site= Facebook |type=}}</ref> When paired with the [[Ancient Cuirass]] and the [[Ancient Greaves]] and each piece of [[Armor]] has been enhanced at least twice by a [[Great Fairy]], the three pieces of Armor provide [[Ancient Proficiency]], a [[Set Bonus]] that increases the damage dealt from the Ancient and Guardian archetypes of weapons.<ref name="Facebook"/>

The Vah Medoh Divine Helm is based on the head portion of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}, which Revali piloted as the [[Rito]] [[Champion]].

=== Tears of the Kingdom ===
In [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]] the Vah Medoh helmet can be found in a chest in North Biron shelf cave unlocked by melting a glacier.

The player can open a door leading to the chest by flooding the puzzle room in the cave.

The Vah Medoh Divine helmet also gives the same helmet to it's respective sage - [[Tulin of Rito Village|Tulin]] when worn. This also adds Tulin summoned by [[Vow of Tulin, Sage of Wind|vow of Tulin]] a lot of damage making him easily able to one shot low tier monsters.
