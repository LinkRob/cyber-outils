{{Term/Store|Sheerow||Series, ALBW}} is [[Ravio]]'s companion in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300}}</ref> It's an intelligent bird-like creature that is incapable of speech, but is able to communicate via body language.

==Biography==
Sheerow became [[Ravio]]'s companion at some point prior to meeting [[Link]]. When Link meets them, Ravio requests that they live within the young hero's home. After Link allows the duo to stay within his house, Ravio sets up [[Ravio's Shop|a shop]] inside the domicile to rent various items. After Link rents items from Ravio's Shop, Ravio explains that should the young hero die, Sheerow will retrieve them and he will have to rent them once more from the shop.<ref>{{Cite|But if you do fall in battle, my pal Sheerow here will swoop in to collect my property. Right, just like that. Swoop right in. Collect my stuff. While you...er...just lie there.|Ravio|ALBW}}</ref>

After Link rescues [[Osfala]], Sheerow flies to {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}} to retrieve the [[Sand Rod]] and returns it to Ravio's Shop, where Link may rent or purchase it. Sheerow returns to Lorule with Ravio after the final battle, and is there to witness the restoration of the kingdom's Triforce.

Sheerow possesses the ability to travel between worlds, as it can fly throughout Hyrule and [[Lorule]] with apparent ease.

==Nomenclature==
Sheerow's name in nearly every localization of the game are variations of the Japanese word {{Romanize|白|Shiro}}, meaning "white". Its English name is phonetically the same as Shiro, but may also be a corruption of {{wp|Sparrow}}, a type of bird.
