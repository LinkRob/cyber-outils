The '''Ice Climbers''' are a pair of mountain climbers named '''Popo''' and '''Nana''', from the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game ''Ice Climber''. While they do not appear in any games of {{TLoZ|Series}}, they are fighters in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. They are also the subject of a few microgames in the ''[[WarioWare]]'' series, not unlike [[Link]].
