{{Term/Store|EX Handle the Horde||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 85 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Handle the Horde|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the first wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Handle the Horde}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given seven minutes to defeat as many {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} as possible. The first objective is to defeat 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, although the number increases each time the objective is met. As more {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, stronger {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} like {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} appear. If they are defeated by the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, then many weaker {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} like {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Lizalfos|link}} appear so that the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} can reach the required amount of KOs. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} is cleared once seven minutes pass, regardless of how many {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} has defeated.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of five {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, fifteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, 1,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
