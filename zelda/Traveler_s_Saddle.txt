==Location and Uses==
A {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Saddle}} can be obtained after scanning the "Link (Rider)" [[Amiibo]]. It should be noted that when two [[Treasure Chest]]s drop after said Amiibo is used, one will contain the Traveler's Saddle while the other will contain the [[Traveler's Bridle]].

Once obtained, the Traveler's Saddle will appear in {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Key Item|link}} {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}. Link can equip it to any {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} he has a maximum Bond with by talking to workers at {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Change to {{Color|BotW Red|Traveler's Saddle}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> However, like other {{Plural|BotW|Saddle|link}}, they can't be used by [[Epona]] and the [[Giant Horse]]. Traveler's Saddles were designed for comfort and hold strong throughout all sorts of weather.<ref name="Inventory"/> Because of this, they're favored among travelers.<ref name="Inventory"/>

In certain [[Memories]], [[Link]] is shown to have a Traveler's Bridle and Traveler's Saddle equipped to his unnamed Horse during the period he served as [[Princess Zelda]]'s personal [[Royal Guard|bodyguard]].
