{{Term/Store|Honey||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM, MM3D}}}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Honey}} goes unnamed in {{OoT}}.

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Stub|OoT3D}}
{{Term|OoT3D|Honey}} is one half of the {{Term|OoT3D|Dancing Couple|link}} in {{OoT}}, though she is never referred to by name. She and {{Term|OoT3D|Darling}} can be found in [[Hyrule Castle Town]] during Link's childhood, complimenting each other during the day and complaining about Link's presence at night. At some point during the seven years Link spends in suspended animation, Honey and Darling both relocate to [[Kakariko Village]], where they can be found during Link's adulthood.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Stub|MM3D}}
{{Term|MM3D|Honey}} operates {{Term|MM3D|Honey & Darling's Shop|link}} alongside {{Term|MM3D|Darling|link}} in {{MM}}.
