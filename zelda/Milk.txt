This milk is very nutritious! Use it with {{Icon|OoT3D I}}, {{Icon|OoT3D X Yellow}}, {{Icon|OoT3D Y Yellow}} or {{Icon|OoT3D II}} to recover your life energy!|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref><ref name="E 131"/> '''Milk Bottles''',{{Exp Game|MM}}<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|MM3D Red|Milk Bottle}}! It can be stored in {{Plural|Series|Empty Bottle|link}} and usually has two helpings. Each helping recovers up to five {{Plural|Series|Heart|link}}, with the exception of {{TP}} where it only recovers three.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Term|OoT3D|Milk}} can be obtained for free from any {{Plural|OoT3D|Cow|link}} found in the game by playing "{{Term|OoT3D|Epona's Song|link}}" after he learns it.<ref>{{Cite|Moooooooooo! What a nice song... It reminds me of the pasture... That song makes me feel so good, I can produce a lot of milk! Have some of my refreshing and nutritious milk!|Cow|OoT}}</ref>

As a child, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can also buy {{Term|OoT3D|Milk}} from {{Term|OoT3D|Talon|link}} for 30 {{Plural|OoT3D|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I give ya more free of charge, Ingo will get mad at me. So I'd better sell it to you for the normal price. Wanna buy milk for 30 Rupees?|Talon|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
Like in {{OoT|-}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can get free {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} from any {{Term|MM3D|Cow}} he finds by playing "{{Term|MM3D|Epona's Song|link}}" near one after he remembers it.

It can also be purchased from the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar|link}} for 20 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}}. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can also buy a special type of {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} called {{Term|MM3D|Chateau Romani|link}} for 200 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}}, which grants him unlimited {{Term|MM3D|Magic Power|link}} for the remainder of the current cycle. This includes gameplay in the Moon should the player decide to challenge [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora]] on the [[Final Day]].

Apparently, {{Term|MM3D|Chateau Romani}} has an inebriating effect, as evidenced by {{Term|MM3D|Gorman|link}}, who overindulges after finding out his troupe's show is cancelled.<ref>{{Cite|Hey ...You! Milk... It's miiiilk... Can you get tipsy from something like milk?!? Hic!|Gorman|MM}}</ref> This may be one of the reasons it is considered a drink for adults.<ref>{{Cite|We're milking the cows tonight! It's "Chateau Romani." It's the first time I get to drink it! Until now, my sister always said "Wait until you're an adult."|Romani|MM}}</ref>

{{Term|MM3D|Link}} must protect several containers of {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} during a sidequest involving {{Term|MM3D|Cremia|link}}. When her path to {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}} is blocked, forcing her to drive through the {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Track|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can use his {{Term|MM3D|Bow|link}} or the {{Term|MM3D|Troupe Leader's Mask|link}} to ward off two masked bandits who attack her cargo. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} manages to save enough {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} containers, Cremia will reward him with {{Term|MM3D|Romani's Mask|link}}, or if he already has it, a coin toss of either 50 Rupees or a hug.<ref>{{Cite|You did it! You helped Cremia! You feel all warm and fuzzy inside! Sigh... You could get used to this!|N/A|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can obtain some {{Term|MM3D|Mystery Milk|link}} from the {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Brothers|link}} to deliver to {{Term|MM3D|Gorman}}. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} cannot drink it, and it must be delivered in a certain amount of time or it will turn into {{Term|MM3D|Moldy Milk|link}} that he must carry for the rest of the cycle. However, if Link does deliver the Mystery Milk in time, he gains an extra [[Empty Bottle|Bottle]] for his trouble.

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, {{Term|TMC|Malon|link}} goes to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} to sell {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Milk}} for 100 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} finds the {{Term|TMC|Spare Key|link}} to {{Term|TMC|Talon}}'s house. The {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Milk|display=Milk}} containers are carried on {{Term|TMC|Epona|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Goat Milk}} can be purchased in {{Term|TPHD|Sera's Sundries|link}} for 10 {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee}} and in the {{Term|TPHD|Goron Shop|link}} for 20 {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee}}. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} obtains his first {{Term|TPHD|Empty Bottle|link|display=Bottle}} of {{Term|TPHD|Milk}} from {{Term|TPHD|Sera|link}} after helping her, although it is only half-full. This {{Term|TPHD|Milk}} is also used to make {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Goat Cheese|link}}.
{{clear}}

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Term|ALBW|Milk}} is sold in the {{Term|ALBW|Milk Bar|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}} for 20 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}} in {{ALBW}}. However, after the completion of the {{Term|ALBW|Premium Milk|link}} side quest, the price is cut in half. Different from previous games, it only contains one helping.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Main|Fresh Milk}}
In {{BotW}}, {{Term|Series|Milk}} is referred to as {{Term|BotW|Fresh Milk}}. It is sold in the {{Term|BotW|East Wind|link}} general store in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} for 12 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, with a stock of three available for purchase. It can also be purchased for 12 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} from two traveling merchants, a stock of two are sold by {{Term|BotW|Agus|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}}, and a stock of 10 during Rainy Day Specials by {{Term|BotW|Yammo|link}} who travels between {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}}.

==Other Appearances==

==={{RTBToL|-}}===
[[File:TBToL Milk icon.png|left|50px]]
In {{RTBToL}}, [[Tingle]] and friends come across [[Usotami Village]] in search of [[Kakashi]], who was captured by [[Segāre]]. Once there, they will meet [[Liar]]. After a proper meeting, she gives Tingle a list of characteristics of a specific goat-like animal he need to find from a herd. When selected, Liar will milk the animal, finally giving Tingle the Milk. The Milk is used in [[Big Liar]]'s chamber, where Tingle offers the Milk to calm the monster.

{{Noncanon|Start}}

==={{SSBM|-}}===
[[Young Link]] drinks {{Term|SSBM|Lon Lon Milk}} as his {{Ssb|taunt}} in {{SSBM}}. It also appears as a [[Trophy]]. Unlike the original bottle from the N64 games, the sticker is light-yellow instead of blue, and the cow's icon is fully colored rather than being a plain white outline. It also features a new text line in the label that reads ''Kenkō ichiban!'' in Hylian script, which can be translated to "Health comes first!"
