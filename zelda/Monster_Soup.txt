{{Term/Store|Monster Soup|Monster Soups|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Monster Soup}}<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Monster Soups can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Monster Extract]], [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Fresh Milk]], and [[Goat Butter]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Due to Monster Extract being required to make them, the amount of {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} healed to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} will be random each time these soups are made. The same also applies to the strength and duration of any status effects. Since Monster Extract is used as a base, the soup is distinctly gamy.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding more ingredients, Link can create several different variations of Monster Soup:

* '''Hearty Monster Soup''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Monster Soup}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these soups will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Monster Soup''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Monster Soup}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. When eaten, a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Monster Soup''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Monster Soup}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Link can eat them to completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Monster Soup''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Monster Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Monster Soup''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Monster Soup}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Monster Soup''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Monster Soup}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Monster Soup''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Monster Soup}}<br/>>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Link can consume these soups to increase the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Monster Soup''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Monster Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. By eating them, Link can gain a boost to his defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Monster Soup''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Monster Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Monster Soup''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Monster Soup}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Using monster extract as a base, this soup's distinct gaminess is either loved or hated.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. These soups will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Chilly"/>
