In some installments from {{TLoZ|Series}}, there have been characters expressing messages or phrases that have transcended in one or another way beyond those games, becoming '''Iconic Quotes''' among fans of the franchise. There are various ways for this to happen, but the two most recognizable are:

*The phrase becomes recurring in future titles, and therefore is cited by different characters.
*For some reason, the phrase reaches a {{Wp|Internet meme|memetic}} status among fans of the series, and the game where it originates gains additional fame because of said phrase.

Below is a list of some of the most notable quotes in the series.

=="It's dangerous to go alone! Take this"==
In {{TLoZ}}, the very first item [[Link]] receives is a [[Sword]], and is given by an [[Old Man]] who lives in a cave easily seen at the start point of the game.<ref>{{Cite|IT'S DANGEROUS TO GO ALONE! TAKE THIS.|Old Man|TLoZ}}</ref> This quote has become popular among fans who played this and other [[:Category:2D Games|2D games]], and has been parodied with some image shots from the Internet. For other quotes of the Old Man, see [[Old Man#The Legend of Zelda|here]]. The advertising slogan for {{TFH}} also uses a portion of this quote, stating, "It's dangerous to go alone! So don't." In addition, the [[Sword]]'s description in {{BotW}} references this.<ref>{{Cite|A sword once wielded by a hero in an ancient age. When grasped, a strange sense of nostalgia washes over you. Take it when going alone would otherwise be dangerous.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

=="It's a secret to everybody"==
In {{TLoZ|-}}, a [[Moblin]] located in the northeastern extreme of [[Hyrule]] (inside what looks like a dungeon from the outside) will offer Link 100 [[Rupee]]s, with the only condition that this gift is kept in secret.<ref>{{Cite|IT'S A SECRET TO EVERYBODY.|Moblin|TLoZ}}</ref> The phrase "It's a secret to everybody" has become commonplace in the series, with several different characters quoting it with mild variations. These characters include [[Average Middle-Aged Man|a suspicious man]] in {{ALttP}} (when asking Link to not tell anyone that he opened a closed chest for him),<ref>{{Cite|I heard that you know I used to be a thief, right? Well, I'll open a chest for you. Will you keep it secret from everyone else? Would you please promise?|Average Middle-Aged Man|ALttP}}</ref>, a Hyrule Castle Guard at the gate in {{OoT}} (if Link speaks to him several times before collecting the third Spiritual Stone; he will let Link in for 10 Rupees) <ref>{{Cite|KEEP IT A SECRET FROM EVERYONE.|Soldier|OoT}}</ref>, [[Kafei]] in {{MM}} (when asking Link to not tell anyone who or where he is),<ref>{{Cite|Keep what we just talked about a secret from everyone.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> [[Yerbal]] in {{SS}} (when he tells him about [[Farore's Flame]]),<ref>{{Cite|All right, I'll tell you. But remember--it's a secret to everybody.|Yerbal|SS}}</ref>  the [https://zelda-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Secret_Parella Secret Parella] in {{SS}}, and the [[Thief Girl]] in {{ALBW}} after rescuing her from [[Thieves' Hideout (A Link Between Worlds)|Thieves' Hideout]], among others.

=="Grumble, Grumble"==
This phrase comes from a [[Goriya]] that obstructs the access to later rooms in {{Term|TLoZ|Level 7|link}} in {{TLoZ|-}},<ref>{{Cite|GRUMBLE,GRUMBLE...|Goriya|TLoZ}}</ref> and doesn't let Link pass if he doesn't have [[Bait|food]]. Contrary to popular belief, it's not the sound his stomach is making; the Goriya is simply mumbling.<ref name= "Grumble Grumble Translation">{{Cite Web|quote= One idea I had was that maybe “grumble” was his stomach grumbling… But nope, the Japanese text is exactly the same. He’s literally grumbling/muttering to himself. I found that surprising, I always thought this was a messed-up translation.|author= Clyde Mandelin|url= https://legendsoflocalization.com/the-legend-of-zelda/first-quest/#hungry-goriya|title= Legends of Localization, Book 1: The Legend of Zelda}}</ref> This phrase is remembered by players who hadn't managed to get past this part of the game at the time, and is also referenced in {{OoT}} by [[Mido]], who reluctantly lets the young hero see the [[Great Deku Tree]], saying at the end the same quote.<ref>{{Cite|Shoot! How did you get to be the favorite of Saria and the Great Deku Tree? Huh?! Grumble...grumble...|Mido|OoT}}</ref> [[Nintendo Power]] has also used the phrase to indicate what games on the [[Wii|Virtual Console]] aren't worth being downloaded.{{Fact}}

=="Dodongo Dislikes Smoke"==
Also in {{TLoZ|-}}, there is an Old Man in {{Term|TLoZ|Level 2|link}} that tells Link "Dodongo Dislikes Smoke".<ref>{{Cite|DODONGO DISLIKES SMOKE.|Old Man|TLoZ}}</ref> The lack of clarity on this phrase has made it popular among the fans of the series, even though it's known nowadays that it refers to the [[Dodongo]]s being vulnerable on the inside against [[Bomb]]s. In {{OoT|-}}, the boss [[King Dodongo]] from [[Dodongo's Cavern]] has the same weakness, making a reference to his {{TLoZ|-}} counterparts.

=="I am [[Error]]"==
In {{TAoL}}, there is a character in the [[Town of Ruto]] known as [[Error]]. When Link talks to him the first time, all he says is that his name is Error.<ref>{{Cite|I AM ERROR|Error|TAoL}}</ref> For a long time, fans of the series wondered the meaning of his words. The name, along with that of another character, [[Bagu|Bug]], was likely intended as a joke about computer glitches, but the romanization of the other name as "Bagu" caused it to be lost in translation. It happens that, when one of the inhabitants of the [[Harbor Town of Mido]] is spoken to, he tells Link that Error may help him find the [[Island Palace]],<ref>{{Cite|ASK ERROR OF RUTO ABOUT THE PALACE|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref> after which Error starts saying that there is a tunnel south of [[King's Tomb]] that leads to the palace; therefore, Error says his name so that Link can remember to talk to him again after visiting the town of Mido. The obscurity of the phrase "I AM ERROR" turned it into an Internet meme, and a well-known phrase among fans of the series.

This quote also appears in the game ''Super Paper Mario'' for the Nintendo Wii. In this game, before fighting the first boss, Fracktail, he says the words "I AM ERROR" along with various other computer error messages.
One international division of [[Nintendo]] also acknowledges the popularity of this quote – on Nintendo of Europe's{{Verify}} official websites, their {{Wp|HTTP 404|404 Error Message}}, which can be seen on Nintendo UK's website <span class="plainlinks">[https://www.nintendo.co.uk/404.html here]<span>, displays the message "404 – I AM ERROR  That page is missing. Perhaps it's south of King's Tomb...".<ref>{{Cite Web| quote= 404 – I AM ERROR<br/>That page is missing. Perhaps it's south of King's Tomb...| retrieved = 13:10, 28 January 2016 (UTC)| url= https://www.nintendo.co.uk/404.html| title= 404 – I AM ERROR {{!}} Nintendo| site= nintendo.co.uk}}</ref>

This phrase also appears in The Binding of Isaac. When the player teleports to an ERROR room, a special shopkeeper will have a dialogue box next to him say I AM ERROR

=="MAH BOI"==
A colloquial pronunciation of "my boy," the phrase was extracted from a dialogue between Link and [[King Harkinian]] in {{TFoE}}.<ref>{{Cite|My boy, this peace is what all true warriors strive for.|King Harkinian|TFoE}}</ref> Several other quotes in the CD-i games have gained status of memes as well; frequently, however, various YouTube videos destined to mock these games include at least once the quote "MAH BOI."

=="Well excuse me, princess"==
In numerous episodes of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}, Link protests towards [[Princess Zelda]] when he disagrees on something, or both are arguing, by saying this phrase.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Excuuuse me, princess. If I'd known you were coming, I'dve asked the Moblins to sweep up before I zapped them|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Aw, come on, Princess. I was just kidding. Well, excuuuse me, Princess!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=6}}</ref> Although the quote is not always said the same way, it has become popular among viewers of the animated series. It shows that the relationship between the two characters is somewhat less friendly than in the canon games.

=="These toys are too much for you!"==
Also in {{OoT}}, during the cutscene of when Link reaches the top of [[Ganon's Castle]], the three separate triangles of the [[Triforce]] begin to resonate as the [[Hero of Time]] watches [[Ganondorf]] play his pipe organ while Princess Zelda is captured in a [[crystal]] above him. After the [[Triforce of Courage]] and [[Triforce of Wisdom|Wisdom]] glow, Ganondorf flickers his cape as he turns around, before saying "These toys are too much for you!", followed by the [[Triforce of Power]] on his right fist glow as well, because the main antagonist believes that Link and Zelda have no proper use for their own Triforce pieces and are too much for them to handle, and therefore demands them to be given to him during the final battle.

This quote is thought to be humorous among fans, as Ganondorf dismissively calling the rather powerful pieces of the Triforce he doesn't possess "toys", due to their different role from the Power counterpart is unusual for the series, and is one of the reasons among critics for the character's high fanbase.
