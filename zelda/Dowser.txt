'''Dowser''' is a dog that appears in {{TSP}}. He is first introduced after [[Link]], [[Princess Zelda]], and [[Charles of Moria]] arrive to [[North Castle]] for the first time. Dowser comes in to interrupt the Charles in the banquet hall and licks both Link's and Zelda's face; however, when he sees Charles, he growls at him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The dog licks his face, then Zelda's. But when he comes to Charles he growls, showing teeth.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=94}}</ref> Afterward, he lunges for Charles's throat, but Zelda holds him back.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Dowser growls again. Then, suddenly, he lunges for Charles's throat! Zelda has to pull the dog back.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=94}}</ref> Dowser's sudden action makes Link wonder why Dowser, who is always nice to new people, does not like Charles.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link's brow wrinkles. An idea is brewing in his mind. 'Funny,' he thinks. 'Dowser is always nice to new people. I wonder what it is about this guy that he doesn't like? Could it be...'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=94}}</ref>

The book then offers a puzzle to give the reader a hint on what upset Dowser. When the puzzle is completed, it spells out the word "NECK," implying that the dog disliked [[Charles's Necklace|Charles's necklace]]. Sure enough, it is later discovered that Charles is actually [[Ganon]], and the necklace is what gives him power.
