==Location and Uses==
The Knight's Saddle is a type of {{Term|BotW|Saddle|link}} that's given to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Jini|link}}. It can be obtained by hitting 23-25 targets during the {{Term|BotW|Horseback Archery|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tell you what... You break {{Color|BotW Red|more than 20}} targets, and I'll hand over my {{Color|BotW Red|horse gear}}.|Jini|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hm...{{Color|BotW Red|[X] targets}}... It's been a dog's age since I've seen such talent in someone so young. What do you mean you're not that young? Over 100 years old? Go ahead, pull the other one. Anyway, this gift is my thanks for putting on a good show. I'm past needing it, and I think you should have it.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> These Saddles were used by the most elite of the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}} for their {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can have his Horses equipped with a Knight's Saddle at {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Change to {{Color|BotW Red|Knight's Saddle}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> Like other Saddles, however, workers can't put them on {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}.
