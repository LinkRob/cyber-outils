{{Term/Store|Seafood Fried Rice|Seafood Fried Rices|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Seafood Fried Rice}}<br/>Various seafood has been sautéed with rice. The stronger the flame, the tastier the dish!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Seafood Fried Rice can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|Series|Hylian Rice|link}}, {{Term|Series|Rock Salt|link}}, and either a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}} or a Porgy in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. To make the default version, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must use two seafood ingredients with contradicting effects. When they're eaten, Link will have some of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} restored to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These meals are tastier when stronger flames are used to cook them.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on the type of seafood used, the three following variations of Seafood Fried Rice can be made:

* '''Hearty Seafood Fried Rice''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Seafood Fried Rice}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Various seafood has been sautéed with rice. The stronger the flame, the tastier the dish!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using Hearty Blueshell Snails. These meals will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Mighty Seafood Fried Rice''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Seafood Fried Rice}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Various seafood has been sautéed with rice. The stronger the flame, the tastier the dish!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}. If eaten, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Seafood Fried Rice''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Seafood Fried Rice}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Various seafood has been sautéed with rice. The stronger the flame, the tastier the dish!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}. Link can eat them to receive an increase to his defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/>
