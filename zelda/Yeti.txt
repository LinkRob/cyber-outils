{{Term/Store|Yeti|Yeti|Series|plural}} are a race in {{TP}} and {{EoW}}.{{Name Ref|TP}} They are based on the mythical animal of the same name.

==Characteristics==
Yetis are covered in white fur except on their faces, hands and feet, which are a bluish gray color on males and brown on females. Male Yeti are about twice the height of an average human and have a large and beaver-like tail. The males are very strong and can easily floor a [[Hylian]]. The only known female Yeti, named Yeta, is much closer to average Hylians in size but this could possibly be because she is rather young, as implied by [[Midna]] when she refers to her as a girl rather than a woman.<ref>{{Cite|Still... I feel bad about the way we treated that girl. To think the Mirror of Twilight has the power to change people like that... This world...ALL worlds...can be cruel... Let's hurry and collect the rest of those pieces, Link! We have to, before more innocent creatures have to endure the suffering this poor girl did...|Midna|TP}}</ref> She has no tail or visible arms and her body is droopy, similar in appearance to a sweater; however Yeta could merely be wrapped in a blanket, as she is ill when [[Link]] first encounters her.<ref>{{Cite|Wife...look bad, uh? Not healthy since mirror...|Yeto|TP}}</ref>

So far, all known Yeti speak a broken variant of [[Hylian Language|Hylian]]. They are generally kindhearted and generous. Yeto and Yeta enjoy snowboarding down [[Snowpeak]] (though they call it Sledding).<ref>{{Cite|Husband love sled, he make me race lots, uh. He love race with you, too, uh!|Yeta|TP}}</ref>
