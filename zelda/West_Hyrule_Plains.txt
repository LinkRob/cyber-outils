The {{Term/Store|West Hyrule Plains||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''West Hyrule Plains'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|West Hyrule Plains}} are a series of plains located between {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field}} and the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. An unoccupied camp can be found in a small marsh in the northern Plain. A monster camp has been established in the ruins in the southern Plain. Various {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}} and boxes which contain {{Plural|BotW|Acorn|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}} can be found within this camp. South of these ruins stand several megaliths; various ring-shaped rocks which face various directions. The traveling {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} Bard, {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}, can be found atop a rock formation in the far southern reaches of the Plains. He is there in search of the source of the last {{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}} that his teacher taught him.<ref>{{Cite|I did not hear your approach. I was lost in this song written by my late teacher. He passed away several years ago, you see... And this was the last song he taught me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The aforementioned source is described by Kass as an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that can be found by threading two rings with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}. By listening to Kass recite the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Song}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} receives the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Two Rings|link}}", which leads to the {{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze Shrine|link}} in the Plains. The traveler {{Term|BotW|Chabi|link}} can be found fighting against a {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} near her bivouac.

A {{Term|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} may pass through the Plains disguised as a {{Term|BotW|Traveler|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} can be found resting in the southern Plain. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, it is replaced with a {{Term|BotW|Black Hinox|link}}.

A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}} can be found inside the ruins of a building in the monster camp in the southern Plain. A wooden {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a bundle of five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}} can be found atop the unoccupied camp in the north Plain.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found by lifting a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}} hidden underneath a large stone slab in the middle of the Plains. A second {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found by examining the {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link|display=leaves with Fairy Dust}} that are traveling along the ground southwest of the previous {{Term|BotW|Korok}}. South of that {{Term|BotW|Korok}}, a third {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found by solving the {{Term|BotW|Flower Trail|link}}. A fourth {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found by completing the {{Term|BotW|Tree Pattern|link}} west of the third {{Term|BotW|Korok}}.
