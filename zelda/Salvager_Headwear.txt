==Location and Uses==
The Salvager Headwear is a piece of [[Armor]] in {{BotW}}. It is obtained from a [[Treasure Chest]] as part of the "[[Xenoblade Chronicles 2|[Xenoblade Chronicles 2]]]" [[Side Quest]]. The Chest crashes on the southern shore of [[Lake Hylia]] after [[Link]] looks to the southern sky from the middle of the [[Bridge of Hylia]] during the night. After obtaining the Salvager Headwear, [[Granté]] will begin selling it for 50 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, the Salvager Headwear provides one level of Swim Speed Up. The Salvager Headwear cannot be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], nor can it be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
