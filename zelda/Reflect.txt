The {{Term/Store|Reflect||Series, TAoL}} magic is an ability in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 109}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Reflect}} was referred to as '''Reflect Spell''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Reflect Spell}}<br/>During Link's quest to recover the six shards of the Magic Crystal, Link frequently used the power of magical Spells. Link used the Reflect Spell to create a special barrier on his shield which could easily reflect magical attacks back to their source.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160326130959/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/r.jsp#ReflectSpell|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Overview==
The Reflect magic increases the strength of Link's shield. As its name suggests, it is used to reflect magical attacks with the [[Shield]],<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= A mysterious magic that can bounce back the magic cast by magicians in the palace. Usually shields can only deflect a magician's magic.|game= TAoL|page= 44}}</ref> and also allows the shield to block several otherwise-unblockable attacks such as fireballs, axes, and clubs. Link learns this magic from an old man in [[Mountain Town of Darunia]], after retrieving a lost child from [[Maze Island]].
