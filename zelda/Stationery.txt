The {{Term/Store|Stationery||Series, OoA}} is an item in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|OoA|Link|link}} can obtain the {{Term|OoA|Stationery}} by traveling to the {{Term|OoA|Post Office|link}} in {{Term|OoA|Lynna Village|link}} and giving the {{Term|OoA|Poe Clock|link}} to the {{Term|OoA|Postman|link}}. The {{Term|OoA|Postman}} will give him the {{Term|OoA|Stationery}} and then rush out of the {{Term|OoA|Post Office|link}}.

The {{Term|OoA|Stationery}} can be traded for the {{Term|OoA|Stink Bag}}.{{Where}}
