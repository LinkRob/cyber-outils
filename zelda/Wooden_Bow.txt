==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Wooden Bow}} is found inside a {{Term|SS|Treasure Chest|link}} on the bow of the {{Term|SS|Sandship|link}} after defeating {{Term|SS|LD-002G Scervo|link}} in {{SS}}. It can be aimed and drawn for a longer period of time to increase its overall strength. For a price, {{Term|SS|Link|link}} can [[Upgrade]] the {{Term|SS|Wooden Bow}} into an {{Term|SS|Iron Bow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Upgrade your Wooden Bow to an Iron Bow with increased attack power!|N/A|SS}}</ref>

{{Compendium/Extract|Wooden Bow}}
{{Plural|BotW|Wooden Bow}} are weak {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} that are used by weaker {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. Though they are not well suited for battle, they are known to be useful for hunting small {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made from wood, {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Bow}} will burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. If they are not put away or extinguished, they will eventually disintegrate. They also float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, which is useful for retrieving them. Finally, they do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
