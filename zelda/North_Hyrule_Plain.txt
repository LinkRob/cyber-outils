{{Term/Store|North Hyrule Plain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''North Hyrule Plain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
North Hyrule Plain is located in [[Hyrule Field]]. The [[Royal Ancient Lab Ruins]] can be found in the Plain.

A [[Korok]] can be found by rolling a [[Boulder]] down into a hole in the southeastern area of the Plain.
