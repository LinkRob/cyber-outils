{{Term/Store|Picolyte|Picolyte|Series, TMC}} are items in {{TMC}}.<ref name="Sorry">{{Cite|Oh, I'm sorry! You already have some of that kind of Picolyte!|Beedle|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TMC|Picolyte}} is made from {{Plural|TMC|Pico Bloom|link}} by a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We're doing cutting-edge research on Pico Blooms here.|Minish|TMC}}</ref> In order to grow yellow, blue, and orange {{Plural|TMC|Pico Bloom}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is asked to bring {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Milk|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water|link}}, and a {{Term|TMC|Red Potion|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I need milk to continue my improvements on this Pico Bloom.|Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I am working on my next Pico Bloom enhancement... All I need is some Crenel Hot Spring Water to complete it.|Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I am working on my next Pico Bloom enhancement... But I need a red potion to finish.|Minish|TMC}}</ref>

In {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, if {{Term|TMC|Link}} cleans the dust on the carpet by using the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Beedle|link}} will eventually start selling {{Term|TMC|Picolyte}}. Immediately after he opens his shop, {{Term|TMC|White Picolyte|display=White}}, {{Term|TMC|Red Picolyte|display=Red}}, and {{Term|TMC|Green Picolyte}} are available to purchase. The remaining colors become available once the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} grows their respective {{Term|TMC|Pico Bloom}}.

Once bought, the {{Term|TMC|Picolyte}} is stored in an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}}; however, only one of each type of {{Term|TMC|Picolyte}} can be carried at a time.<ref name="Sorry"/> When consumed, {{Term|TMC|Picolyte}} increases the rate of finding certain items, such as {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that White Picolyte is for people who want to get lucky! It makes it easier for you to find Kinstone pieces.|Beedle|TMC}}</ref> {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that Green Picolyte is for only the most serious of collectors! It will make it easier for you to find Mysterious Shells!|Beedle|TMC}}</ref> and {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that Yellow Picolyte makes it easier for you to find Rupees!|Beedle|TMC}}</ref> The effects of {{Term|TMC|Picolyte}} wear off after approximately 30 seconds.
