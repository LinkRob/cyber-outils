The {{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Icy Plain||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a location in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Icy Plain|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Icy Plain is the third area of the [[Second Continent]]. In this area a frozen lake (the ice, however, is not strong enough to carry [[Tingle]]), fierce blizzards, and a haunted graveyard can be found. Common enemies in this area are snow bunnies and penguins, and in the graveyard ghosts are fought once (for a sidequest). In the area there are several snowmen that must be marked on the map to complete and sell it for a relatively high amount of [[Rupee]]s.

===Local Speciality===
The Icy Plain's local speciality is Icy Bouillon. The ingredients for Icy Bouillon are:
*[[Devil Shell]] X 4
*[[Paralysis Lily]] X 5
*[[Paralysis Claw]] X 6
