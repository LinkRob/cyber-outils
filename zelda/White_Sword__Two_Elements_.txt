The {{Term/Store|White Sword (Two Elements)||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|White Sword (Two Elements)|Inventory|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|White Sword (Two Elements)}} is the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} after it has been infused with the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}}. It is able to create a {{Term|TMC|Links|link|display=copy}} of {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} by charging up a {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack|link}} over {{Plural|TMC|Flashing Spot on the Floor|link}} and releasing the attack. It will summon a translucent copy that mimics {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s movements until the {{Term|TMC|Split Gauge|link}} is fully depleted, {{Term|TMC|Link}} takes damage, or the copy makes extended contact with an {{Term|TMC|Object|link}} or surface, such as a wall or a {{Term|TMC|Block|link}}.

The {{Term|TMC|White Sword (Two Elements)|display=Sword}}'s hilt changes from green to red to reflect the last {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link|display=Element}} infused into the blade. It also deals more damage than the {{Term|TMC|White Sword}}.

When {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}} and infuses it into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword (Two Elements)}}, it becomes the {{Term|TMC|White Sword (Three Elements)|link}}.
