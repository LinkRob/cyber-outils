The {{Term/Store|Peace Ring||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Peace Ring<br/>No explosion if holding Bomb|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoA}}

==Location and Uses==
The Peace Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it prevents [[Bomb]]s from exploding while been hold.<ref name="Description"/> The Peace Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
