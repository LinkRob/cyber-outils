{{Term/Store|Golden Handrail|Golden Handrails|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Golden Handrail|Shipyard|PH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|PH|Golden Handrail}} are {{Plural|PH|Handrail|link}} belonging to the {{Term|PH|Golden Ship|link}} set of {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}. They are very elegantly designed.<ref>{{Cite|Note the elegant curve and design. A true masterpiece!|Shipyard|PH}}</ref> Just like most {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}}, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} can collect multiple {{Plural|PH|Golden Handrail}}. They can be obtained through various methods:
* Purchasing them from {{Term|PH|Beedle|link}} or the {{Term|PH|Masked Ship|link}}.
* Salvaging them with certain {{Plural|PH|Treasure Map|link}}.
* Salvaging them from defeated {{Plural|PH|Pirate Ship (Enemy)|link}}.
* Finding them in certain late-game {{Plural|PH|Treasure Chest|link}}.
* Scoring 2000 points or more in the {{Term|PH|Shooting Gallery|link}}.
* Trading them in {{Term|PH|Freedle|link}}'s boxes.
* Receiving them after completing four {{Plural|PH|Big Play|link}} in {{Term|PH|Battle Mode|link}}.

For every two {{Term|PH|Golden Ship}} parts attached to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}}, its stamina will be increased by one {{Term|PH|Heart}}, up to a maximum of eight {{Plural|PH|Heart}}. {{Plural|PH|Golden Handrail}} can be sold to the {{Term|PH|Treasure Teller|link}} for 1,500 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}.
