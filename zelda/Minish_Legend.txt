The {{Term/Store|Minish Legend||Series, TMC}} is the series of events taking place after {{SS}} and before {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 11}}</ref>

==History==
Long ago, during the early development of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}, the kingdom was at peace after an age of chaos. One day, the land was suddenly overwhelmed by evil creatures that descended upon it in swarms, driving the world into shadows and nearly bringing the kingdom to ruin. As {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}} neared the brink of collapse, the tiny {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} appeared from the sky and gave the {{Term|TMC|Hero of Men}} the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}} and the magical {{Term|TMC|Light Force}}. Using these two special gifts, the {{Term|TMC|Hero of Men|display=Hero}} drove the beasts back and restored light to the land of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|A long, long time ago...when the world was on the verge of being swallowed by shadow...The tiny Picori appeared from the sky, bringing the hero of men a sword and a golden light.|Prologue|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Long, long ago, and then even longer before that...Evil creatures appeared in the world of humans. They were powerful, frightening beasts, and they created such trouble! But then the Picori came from the sky, bringing humans powerful magic. With the magic light force and the Picori Blade, the beasts were repelled. And the world of humans once more knew peace, thanks to the Picori.|Marshall|TMC}}</ref>

To ensure the beasts could not bring further destruction to the land, the {{Term|TMC|Hero of Men}} sealed them all within the {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest}}. Sealing the chest with the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}} itself, the {{Term|TMC|Hero of Men}} was able to bring the destructive war to an end and brought peace to the kingdom. Meanwhile, the {{Term|TMC|Light Force}} was secretly sealed within the body of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}'s {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link|display=Princess}} to prevent its misuse, giving her and her successors magical powers that would continue across the generations.<ref>{{Cite|With wisdom and
courage, the hero drove out the darkness. When peace had been restored, the people enshrined that blade with care. And the force of the golden light, embodied in Hyrule's princess, shone forth upon the lands.|Elemental Sanctuary|TMC}}</ref>

The history of the war was documented in a series of stained-glass windows within the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} that acts as a bridge between {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}} and the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm|link}}.

==Aftermath==
{{Main|Picori Festival}}
In honor of their service to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}} in the war, the {{Term|Series|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} established the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival}}, an annual celebration to honor the {{Term|TMC|Minish}}.<ref>{{Cite|So as not to forget our gratitude, we hold this festival each year.|Marshall|TMC}}</ref> Storytellers recounted the stories of the war and the legend of the {{Term|TMC|Hero of Men}}, and the kingdom held a sword-fighting tournament, the winner of which would receive a {{Term|TMC|Smith's Sword|link}} and have the ultimate honor of approaching and touching the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}} itself.<ref>{{Cite|Whoever wins the competition earns the honor of touching the sacred blade.|Zelda|TMC}}</ref> It also became common knowledge in the stories of the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} that the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|link}}, the gateway to the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm}}, would open temporarily once a century to allow travel between the two worlds and allowing the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} to either come to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}} or return to their homeland.<ref>{{Cite|They say that, every hundred years, the Picori come to Hyrule...And this is the 100th year, to the day, since the last time the Picori were here!|Marshall|TMC}}</ref>
