The {{Term/Store|Hidden Village||Series, TP, TPHD}},{{Exp Game|TP}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 113 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|LCT, OoA (Himekawa)}} also known as the '''Bygone Village''',{{Exp Game|TP}}<ref>{{Cite|You are a tough human to make it back from the Bygone Village!|Darbus|TP}}</ref> is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is located in the [[Eldin Province]], north of the [[Bridge of Eldin]].<ref>{{Cite|If you want to find that hidden village, look for a path leading to the Lanayru province on the far side of the Bridge of Eldin.|Gor Coron|TP}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{TP|-}}===
Prior to the events in {{TP|-}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}} served as the secret home to the {{Term|TPHD|Sheikah|link}} tribe; however, due to the prolonged wars, the tribe dwindled and the town fell into decline, becoming infested with {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin|link}} and leaving only one resident, {{Term|TPHD|Impaz|link}}.<ref name=statue>{{Cite|That belonged to the tribe that protected the Hylian royal family long ago. They worked in secret, so they lived in a lonely, forgotten place. But I heard that tribe dwindled in the prolonged wars...|Gor Coron|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My name is Impaz. I'm the last resident of this poor village.|Impaz|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This village was once the secret home of a proud tribe who served the royal family...But it fell into decline, and became infested with dangerous beasts. It's become an awful place...|Impaz|TP}}</ref>

Early in {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}'s quest, entry into the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}} is blocked off due to a rockslide that happened long ago.<ref>{{Cite|But...a rockslide blocked the way to the village long ago, so you can't get there anymore.|Gor Coron|TP}}</ref> After {{Term|TPHD|Link}} shows the {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Statue|link}} to {{Term|TPHD|Ilia|link}} while {{Term|TPHD|Retrieval of Ilia's Memory|link|display= Retrieving her Memory}}, she remembers that someone saved her from monsters and is still in danger. {{Term|TPHD|Gor Coron|link}} recalls where he has seen the {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Statue}} before, which leads {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}}.<ref name=statue/> {{Term|TPHD|Link}} goes there to rescue the one who helped {{Term|TPHD|Ilia}} with the aid of {{Term|TPHD|Darbus|link}}, who breaks through the rocks to clear {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s path ahead. When {{Term|TPHD|Link}} first visits the town, it is infested by many {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin}}, all of which he must slay in order to rescue {{Term|TPHD|Ilia}}'s helper.<ref>{{Cite|There are 30... No... At most, 20 of them ahead...They are probably just a small band of survivors...Ragged remnants who have settled, leaderless, into this village. If that is all there are, little human, then you alone are more than enough for them. But you will have to defeat them ALL to save that girl's benefactor.|Darbus|TP}}</ref> The only other resident of the village, {{Term|TPHD|Impaz}}, comes out of her home after {{Term|TPHD|Link}} slays every single one of the beasts. Thanking the young hero for his help in defeating the {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin}}, she gives {{Term|TPHD|Link}} {{Term|TPHD|Horse Call|link|display= Ilia's Charm}}, an item {{Term|TPHD|Ilia}} gave to {{Term|TPHD|Impaz}} before escaping the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, but listen to me blather on! I have a favor to ask: would you help me return this to her? I think she always kept it close to her heart, but even so, she didn't hesitate to part with it in order to protect me... I believe in my heart that it's kept me safe all this time...|Impaz|TP}}</ref>

Shortly after {{Term|TPHD|Ilia}} recovers her memory with the aid of the {{Term|TPHD|Horse Call||display= Ilia's Charm}}, later revealed to be the {{Term|TPHD|Horse Call}} made for {{Term|TPHD|Link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} returns to the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}} and shows the {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod|link}} to {{Term|TPHD|Impaz}}. {{Term|TPHD|Impaz}} recognizes the {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod}} upon seeing it and gives {{Term|TPHD|Link}} the {{Term|TPHD|Ancient Sky Book|link}}, which is only to be carried by the messenger to the heavens.<ref>{{Cite|From generation to generation, my ancestors have guarded the book that, by royal decree, was to be given to the messenger to the heavens. This is that book. Please, take it. This book is written in the ancient language of Sky Writing.|Impaz|TP}}</ref> It is with the help of this book that {{Term|TPHD|Link}} eventually reaches the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|link}}.

Most of the buildings in the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}} were abandoned and ruined by the {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin}}, and as such, only {{Term|TPHD|Impaz}}'s house is still fit for residence. {{Term|TPHD|Impaz}}'s house is also home to several {{Plural|TPHD|Cat|link}} that she takes care of. In the backyard of one of the abandoned buildings, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can find a {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone|link}} as well as a {{Term|TPHD|Cucco|link}} who asks {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}, to play {{Term|TPHD|Cat Hunt|link}} with the {{Plural|TPHD|Cat}} in exchange for a {{Term|TPHD|Piece of Heart|link}}. The various stray felines wandering the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}} will play the mini-game with {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} where he has to talk to each individual {{Term|TPHD|Cat}} at least once.

==={{LCT|-}}===
In {{LCT}}, the {{Term|LCT|Hidden Village}} appears in {{Term|LCT|Level 5|link}} as a {{Term|LCT|Ranger|link}}-type {{Term|LCT|Stage|link}}. {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} freely moves through the {{Term|LCT|Hidden Village}} to kill the 25 {{Plural|LCT|Bulblin|link}} there. Most of the {{Plural|LCT|Bulblin}} are found in clusters and shoot flaming {{Plural|LCT|Arrow|link}} as soon as they spot {{Term|LCT|Link}}. Several {{Plural|LCT|Explosive Barrel|link}} can be found, which when hit, destroy everything within their vicinity. Slaying all 25 {{Plural|LCT|Bulblin}} will add a multiplier of the remaining time to the score.

==={{OoA|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{OoA|Himekawa}}, a Hidden Village is found in [[Labrynna]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= That's right. This is a hidden village.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 72}}</ref> It is not the same Hidden Village featured in {{TP|-}}.

When the sorceress [[Veran]] possesses the Oracle of Ages [[Nayru]], she travels to the past and uses her host to convince [[Queen Ambi]] to construct a [[Black Tower]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Veran possessed Nayru...And is using her abilities as the Oracle of Ages to move through time. She's going back to the past to cause trouble!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 31}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Queen Ambi suddenly decided to build a tower that reaches all the way to heaven. She's pressed all the men in Labrynna into service.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 47}}</ref> Those in the castle who grow suspicious of Nayru are fired at her suggestion.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Many people in the castle distrust Nayru. The queen keeps firing them...At Nayru's suggestion.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 82}}</ref> However, before they can be executed, [[Sir Raven]] brings them to the Hidden Village.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= But before they can be executed, I bring them here.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 82}}</ref> The other inhabitants are volunteers from [[Lynna]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Raven gathered volunteers from Lynna.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 122}}</ref> Located behind a graveyard of the supposedly dead,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= This is where you go to meet the supposedly dead... It's better that way for wanted men.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 69}}</ref> the Hidden Village rests inside of a forest.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Wow, a village in the forest behind a graveyard...Like the whole town is hiding.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 72}}</ref> The village is lead by an elder and a group of farmers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Here's a present for you, Elder!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 74}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= For farmers they look awfully tough.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 75}}</ref> Farming is the main activity in the village, with [[Toto Potato]]es as the main crop.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Oh! Toto potatoes! I've seen them before!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 78}}</ref> 

Veran discovers the Hidden Village after following Sir Raven.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Where did this secret village come from all of a sudden?|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 122}}</ref> She sends the monster [[Ramrock]] to crush the village.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Rise, Ramrock... Veran, the Sorceress of Shadows, commands it! Crush that village!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 127}}</ref> After Link defeats the monster, Queen Ambi's army arrives and lays siege to the Hidden Village. Sir Raven and the armed farmers almost win the battle until the villagers are threatened to be shot by archers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Surrender or we'll turn you into pincushions!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 136}}</ref> Since Sir Raven is captured and their settlement is destroyed, the remaining villagers who are not enslaved hide in a cave.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= They captured everyone and took them to the tower!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 153}}</ref>

The former inhabitants of the Hidden Village help in the destruction of [[Ganon]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= All the villagers, too!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 184}}</ref> The Hidden Village's elder says they will build a great city just as the built a village.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You taught us how to build a village, now we'll build a great city. Just you watch!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 186}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}} in {{TP|-}} shares a connection with {{Term|TAoL|Old Kasuto|link}} from {{TAoL}}, which is also a ghost town with a {{Term|TAoL|Old Man|link|display= Single Resident}}.
*Throughout the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}} in {{TP|-}}, several posters can be found that say the word "Wanted" written in the {{Term|TPHD|Hylian Language|link}}. The posters have a drawing of an unknown man with a mustache and goatee, along with an apparent reward of 1,000,000,000,000,000 (One Quadrillion) {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}}.
*The {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}} in {{TP|-}} also resembles a classic ghost town from {{Wp|Western (genre)|Western films}}. Its theme music resembles the score by {{Wp|Ennio Morricone}} for the classic Western films ''{{Wp|A Fistful of Dollars}}'', ''{{Wp|For a Few Dollars More}}'', and ''{{Wp|The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly}}''.
*A sign at the entrance to the {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Village}} in {{TP|-}} reads "Welcome to Old Kakarico" in the {{Term|TPHD|Hylian Language}}.
*In the French version of the game, each cat is named and has custom dialogue.
