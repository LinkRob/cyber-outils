{{Term/Store|Hyrule Castle||Series, CoH}} is a dungeon in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hyrule Castle}}|N/A|CoH}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Castle==
{{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)}} is where {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} can be found if {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} chooses to enter {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}}'s {{Term|CoH|Portal (Object)|link}}. After {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}} is awakened by the power of the {{Term|CoH|Triforce|link}} by {{Term|CoH|Cadence}}, {{Term|CoH|Impa|link}} informs {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}} that {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} kidnapped the {{Term|CoH|King of Hyrule|link}} and sealed the {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)|display=Castle}} with his {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank goodness you're awake! We need to get you out of the castle. Your father has been taken, and most of the guards no longer obey our command. The heart of the castle has been sealed by {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}}'s {{Color|CoH Yellow|Golden Lute}}. You'll need to find something capable of breaking the seal to save the {{Color|CoH Blue|king}}! Take this {{Color|CoH Yellow|telescope}}. It should help your search!|Impa|CoH}}</ref> After gifting {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}} the {{Term|CoH|Telescope|link}}, she instructs the {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|display=Princess}} to take the nearby {{Term|CoH|Dagger}} and flee the {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)|display=Castle}}.<ref>{{Cite|You must hurry, Princess. Take the {{Color|CoH Yellow|dagger}} by those crates. You can break the pots over there with it and head down the stairs.|Impa|CoH}}</ref> After pushing a {{Term|CoH|Rock|link}} upon leaving the {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)|display=Castle}}'s walls, this section of the {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)|display=Castle}} can be revisited at any time. However, the remaining portions of the {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)|display=Castle}} cannot be entered until {{Term|CoH|Octavo}}'s {{Plural|CoH|Four Champions|link}} have been defeated.<ref>{{Cite|You did it! You defeated all four of {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}}'s champions! Now we can go to {{Color|CoH Blue|Hyrule Castle}} and put an end to this!|Trill|CoH}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Four {{Plural|CoH|Sheikah Stone|link}} can be found at {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)}}. One is situated just outside the section of the {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)|display=Castle}} where {{Term|CoH|Impa}} took refuge. A second can be found just outside the main entrance to the {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)|display=Castle}}.

{{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing {{Plural|CoH|Purple Rupee|link}} and {{Plural|CoH|Orange Rupee|link}} can be found in {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)}}.
