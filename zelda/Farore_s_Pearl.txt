{{Term/Store|Farore's Pearl||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TWWHD|Farore's Pearl}}, named after {{Term|TWWHD|Farore (Goddess)|link}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Golden Goddess|link}}, is the second of three {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}} that {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} obtains. It is kept by the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Deku Tree|link}} in the {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Haven|link}}, and was initially going to be given to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} after the {{Term|TWWHD|Korok|link}}'s annual ceremony until {{Term|TWWHD|Linder|link}} arrives and tells them that {{Term|TWWHD|Makar|link}} had fallen into the {{Term|TWWHD|Forbidden Woods|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Makar...fell into the Forbidden Woods!|Linder|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|As it happens, you have come just in time for a ceremony that the Koroks hold but once every year. It is about to begin. I shall grant the pearl to you once their ceremony is complete.|Great Deku Tree|TWW}}</ref> After {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} rescues {{Term|TWWHD|Makar}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Deku Tree}} bestows {{Term|TWWHD|Farore's Pearl}} upon him.<ref>{{Cite|You have done well, Link. I must thank you for your brave deeds. Here is that which I have promised you. Please accept it.|Great Deku Tree|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It is my hope that this pearl shall lead you to a fair destiny!|Great Deku Tree|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} later places the {{Term|TWWHD|Farore's Pearl|display=Pearl}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Eastern Triangle Island|link}} to make the {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}} rise from the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}.
