==Biography==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Term|OoS|Farore (Oracle)}} appears in {{OoS}}, where she reveals the secrets obtained as a result of a {{Term|OoS|Linked Game|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|At times and places, you will hear powerful secrets. At those times, come see me. I can give shape and form to your secrets!|Farore (Oracle)|OoS}}</ref> She is hidden in the {{Term|OoS|Hall of Secrets|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the Hall of Secrets, where your secrets take form.|Farore (Oracle)|OoS}}</ref> {{Term|OoS|Farore (Oracle)}} carries a book titled "{{Term|OoS|Farore's Memory|link}}." After completing a game, or starting a password-linked game, the book appears to the left of {{Term|OoS|Farore (Oracle)}}, containing any linking passwords found so far.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Term|OoA|Farore (Oracle)}} appears in {{OoA}}, where she reveals the secrets obtained as a result of a {{Term|OoA|Linked Game|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|At times and places, you will hear powerful secrets. At those times, come see me. I can give shape and form to your secrets!|Farore (Oracle)|OoA}}</ref> She is hidden in the {{Term|OoA|Hall of Secrets|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the Hall of Secrets, where your secrets take form.|Farore (Oracle)|OoA}}</ref> {{Term|OoA|Farore (Oracle)}} carries a book titled "{{Term|OoA|Farore's Memory|link}}." ==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Farore}}

{{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)}} is described as a helpful person looking for a place to live in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|She's looking for a house in Hyrule to move into. She's a very helpful person, but people take advantage of her kind nature. This really bothers her.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} impersonates {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, she appears alongside the other {{Plural|TMC|Oracle|link}} in a room on the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}, where she hopes that they will not have to stay indefinitely.<ref>{{Cite|Staying in this inn forever would start to be a bother...|Farore (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref>

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Gorman|link}} arrives in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} seeking to rent out his {{Term|TMC|Vacant House|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow... I just had the best idea! I can rent out my house for some extra cash!|Gorman|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} speaks with {{Term|TMC|Gorman}}, he will ask {{Term|TMC|Link}} to let him know if he finds a woman willing to rent the {{Term|TMC|Vacant House|display=House}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm trying my hand at real estate. Turns out, it's a total pain. Like right now, I'm looking for a tenant, you see? But the thing is, I can only rent to a woman, single occupancy. ... If you find anyone who can rent this place, would you let me know?|Gorman|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} has the option to show any of the three {{Plural|TMC|Oracle}} the {{Term|TMC|Vacant House}}; if he chooses {{Term|TMC|Din (Oracle)|link}} or {{Term|TMC|Nayru (Oracle)|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)}} will consider visiting them.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe I should go pay her a visit sometime.|Farore (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref>

If the second {{Term|TMC|Vacant House}} is built and given to another {{Term|TMC|Oracle}}, {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)}} is left as the only one of the trio without a home, and will complains that she is alone.<ref>{{Cite|It's no fun being here alone. Maybe

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} shows either of the {{Plural|TMC|Vacant House}} to {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)}}, she will move in; he can visit her afterwards, whereupon she will thank him and offer to give him {{Term|TMC|Farore's Charm|link}} whenever he has an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}! I really love this house! If you ever need a charm, I'll give you another, so just ask!|Farore (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref> When consumed, the {{Term|TMC|Charm (The Minish Cap)|link}} increases {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s attack and defense, and turns his tunic (and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}}) purple for the duration of the effect.<ref>{{Cite|You got {{Color|TMC Red|Farore's Charm}}! Your power and guard will be boosted for a while!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> Although both attributes are increased, the offensive boost from {{Term|TMC|Din's Charm|link}} and the defense boost from {{Term|TMC|Nayru's Charm|link}} are higher.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can also {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)}} for a second time,<ref>{{Cite|Would you like to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}?|Farore (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref> resulting in a {{Term|TMC|Joy Butterfly|link}} appearing in {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}; when it is collected, {{Term|TMC|Link}} is able to dig with the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts|link}} more quickly.<ref>{{Cite|You caught a {{Color|TMC Red|Joy Butterfly}}! ==Trivia==
*There was originally planned to be a third game to accompany {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, entitled ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Mystical Seed of Courage|Mystical Seed of Courage]]'', which would have presumably featured {{Term|Series|Farore (Oracle)}} as the main focus equally as {{Term|Series|Din (Oracle)}} and {{Term|Series|Nayru (Oracle)}} were in {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}. However, [[Capcom]] ultimately decided that it would be too complex to link three different games, so they abandoned the third game and integrated {{Term|Series|Farore (Oracle)}} into the two remaining games as the "Oracle of Secrets".
*Her {{Term|TMC|Figurine|link}} description in {{TMC|-}} could be a reference to how what was expected to be her game, ''Mystical Seed of Courage'', was removed from what would have been a trilogy. The fact that {{Term|TMC|Link}} can only find houses for two of the {{Plural|TMC|Oracle}} may be a reference to this as well.
*The concept art for {{Term|Series|Farore (Oracle)}} shown in {{HH}} seems to indicate that she was originally planned to be a singer, a role assumed by {{Term|OoA|Nayru (Oracle)}} in {{OoA|-}}.
