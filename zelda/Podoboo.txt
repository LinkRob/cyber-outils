{{Term/Store|Podoboo|Podoboo|Series, LA, LADX, OoS, OoA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 198 {{Exp Game|LADX, OoS, OoA}}}}</ref> They are the same species as the [[mariowiki:Lava Bubble|Lava Bubble]]s from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]].

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
Podoboo appear in the lava-filled underground passages of [[Turtle Rock]] in {{LA}}. They constantly jump out of pools of lava that [[Link]] must pass over by jumping with [[Roc's Feather]] and using the moving lifts. They attempt to knock Link into the lava below as they leap up.

==={{OoS|-}}===
Podoboo leap out of pools of lava in an attempt to knock Link into it in {{OoS}}. He must safely clear these lava pools by using [[Roc's Cape]] and the moving platforms. Podoboo leap out of lava pools in the side-scrolling tunnel of the [[Sword & Shield Maze]]. They are accompanied by [[Keese]] as further obstructions. 

==={{OoA|-}}===
Podoboo only appear during the battle with [[Head Thwomp]] in {{OoA}}. The boss will sometimes release them out from the top of its head, more commonly when Link is above it while riding the spinning platforms.

===Other Appearances===
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Stub|CoH}}
Podoboos appear as enemies in {{CoH}} where they move to the beat of the music. They can only be found in caves and dungeons where there is a hot atmosphere, such as the [[Gerudo Ruins]]. They tend to move on every music beat, moving along the walls of a cave or dungeon, or around objects like a [[Treasure Chest]]. Touching them deals damage to [[Cadence]], [[Link]], [[Yves]], and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]].
{{Ambig|End}}
