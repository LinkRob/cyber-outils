The {{Term/Store|Hylian|Hylians|Series, BotW, CoH|plural}} (pronounced {{IPA|ˈhaɪ.li.ən}} {{respell|HY|lee|ən}}){{Fact}} are a recurring race of [[Human]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Pardon the entrance, but you're a Hylian, aren't you?|Sidon|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! You're not Hylian? So you're new here! I'm {{Color|CoH Blue|Trill}}. Nice to meet you!|Trill|CoH}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Hylian}} were referred to as '''Hyleans''' in {{Guide|OoA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 1}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} They are from the countries of [[Skyloft]], [[Hyrule]], and the latter's flooded counterpart, the [[Great Sea]]. While it is unlikely that the people of [[Termina]], a parallel world to the Kingdom of Hyrule, are also known as Hylians, they are their parallels. Descended from the people who lived among the goddess [[Hylia]] long prior to the establishment of Hyrule as a kingdom, they are a magic race with pointed ears. In the past, Hylians were able to wield magic of considerable might, but, over time, that ability has faded. However, because they are of Hylia's bloodline, the royal family, especially its princesses, are still strong in magic. In addition, some sages and shrine maidens have also been able to wield magic, an ability that brought them considerable honor in Hylian society.<ref name="E">{{Cite Book|quote=|book=E|publisher=|page=14}}</ref> By far the most famous Hylians are the [[Link|many heroes]] and [[Princess Zelda|princesses]] that share the same name as one another, the latter being of blood relation and naming tradition - Link and Zelda.

Hylians were the first race to establish organized civilization in ancient Hyrule<ref name="E"/> many years after the events of {{SS}}, upon the world formerly known only as the Surface. In {{TP}}, the [[Oocca]] are said to have aided them.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, according to legend, Hyrule was made by the Hylians, who, as we all know, are the closest race to the gods...truth be told, there's also a theory saying that in ancient times there was a race even closer to the gods than the Hylia people, and THEY created it. And they, simultaneously with the birth of the Hylia people, created a new capital, a capital that floated in the heavens.|Shad|TP}}</ref> Unlike several other races in the series, Hylians tend to live in highly populated towns or villages such as [[Hyrule Castle Town]], [[Kakariko Village]], and the [[Village of the Blue Maiden]]. Among the Hylians, there are a variety of peoples of different cultures and ethnicities.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book=E|publisher=|page=44}}</ref> Some {{Plural|Series|Hylian}} have evolved into different races, such as the {{Term|Series|Wind Tribe|link}} or the {{Term|Series|Kokiri|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book=E|publisher=|page=55}}</ref>

==Hylian Countries and Settlements==
===Skyloft===
{{Main|Skyloft}}
When the homeland of the Hylians was under a massive assault by [[Demon|demonic hordes]], they were separated from the [[Hyrule|Surface]] for thousands of years by Hylia in an attempt to protect them. Living above the clouds on a separate outcrop of earth, they named this safe haven Skyloft. In the sky, a new society flourished in peace and the majority of the Skyloft populace remained ignorant of the ravaged world below the clouds until the events of {{SS}}. It can be assumed that a portion of Skyloft's population flew down and repopulated the Surface after the events in {{SS|-}}, leading to the Hylian establishment of the Kingdom of Hyrule seen in future games.<ref>{{Cite|Look around us! As a child, I always dreamed of a world below. I wanted to see the surface with my own eyes and feel the land's warm breeze on my skin. I... I think I want to live here. I always want to feel solid ground beneath my feet, see the clouds above my head, and watch over the Triforce.|Zelda|SS}}</ref>

===Hyrule===
{{Main|Hyrule}}
[[File:BotW Zelda The Champions' Ballad Artwork.png|thumb|[[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], an important Hylian, is the princess of Hyrule]]
The most prominent and major home of the Hylians is the iconic Kingdom of Hyrule, formerly the nameless Surface. It is where the ancestors of the Hylians, the people who lived among the goddess [[The Goddess Hylia|Hylia]], resided during the [[Era of the Goddess Hylia]]. Living among “Her Grace” in the unnamed land, they had magical powers that passed down to their descendants. When a massive war broke out across the Surface and the goddess took up battle with the demon king [[Demise]], she sent the human survivors into the sky on the outcrop of earth that became known as Skyloft. It's not until after they rediscover the Surface thousands of years later that the Kingdom of Hyrule is established by [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Hylia's descendants]] and becomes the home of the Hylian race once more. Hyrule faces several subsequent invasions, namely at the hands of [[Ganon]], an incarnation of Demise's hatred.<ref>{{Cite|Though this is not the end. My hate... never perishes. It is born anew in a cycle with no end! I will rise again. Those like you... Those who share the blood of the goddess and the spirit of the hero... They are eternally bound to this curse. An incarnation of my hatred shall ever follow your kind, dooming them to wander a blood-soaked sea of darkness for all time!|Demise|SS}}</ref> The Hylians are able to suppress these attacks through their [[Hyrule#Military|military]]; their legendary hero, [[Link]]; and, occasionally, [[Golden Goddesses#Interventions in Hyrule|divine intervention]]. However, the events of {{TWW}} spell an end to Hyrule in the [[Adult Timeline]].<ref>{{Cite|Gods of the Triforce! Hear that which I desire! . . . Wash away this ancient land of Hyrule! Let a ray of hope shine on the future of the world!!!|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref> Sunk beneath the [[Great Sea]], ancient Hyrule is lost forevermore in that branch of the [[Zelda Timeline|timeline]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The curtain thus fell in the cyclical history of the sacred realm of Hyrule and the struggle for the Triforce.|book=HH|page= 129}}</ref> Following its destruction, [[Tetra]] and the [[Hero of Winds]] travel to another continent, where they establish [[New Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Hyrule vanished, and the pirates welcomed back Princess Zelda, who had once again become Tetra the pirate. Tetra, Link, and the pirates received a sendoff from the people of the islands, and set sail in search of a new world.|book=HH|page= 130}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Continuing their journey, Link, Tetra, and the pirates at last came upon a new continent. . . . Here, Tetra founded New Hyrule.|book=HH|page= 133}}</ref>

====Hylian Settlements====
'''[[Hyrule Castle]]''' appears in several incarnations and is the home of the Hylian monarch.<br/> 
'''[[Hyrule Castle Town]]''' is the capital of Hyrule and the main settlement of the Hylian people. It is never seen too far a distance from the castle.<br/>
'''[[Kakariko Village]]''' is the former home of the [[Sheikah]] tribe, but eventually was opened up to the Hylian race to make residence.<br/>
'''[[Village of the Blue Maiden]]''' is home to numerous Hylians.<br/>
'''[[Lon Lon Ranch]]''' is home to the Hylians known as [[Malon]], [[Talon]] and [[Ingo]], who provide much of the kingdom's source of milk and farming.<br/>
'''[[Hateno Village]]''' a prominent Hylian village southeast of Kakariko.<br/>
'''[[Lurelin Village]]''' a small fishing village.<br/>
'''[[Suthorn Village]]'''  a small village South of Hyrule Castle, also home to a incarnation of link.<br/>
'''[[Seesyde Village]]''' a small fishing village, in {{EoW|-}}.

===Great Sea===
{{Main|Great Sea}}

The Hylians inhabiting the Great Sea are spread out onto several islands, but live most prominently on the islands [[Outset Island]] and [[Windfall Island]]. They appear to be more advanced in the use of technology, including the [[Pictobox]] and the use of [[submarine]]s.

===Termina===
{{Main|Termina}}
The Hylians from Termina live, like their {{OoT}} counterparts, in a big, central [[Clock Town|city]]. There is only one significant difference between the Hylians from Hyrule and the ones from Termina: magic and technology. While the Hylians from Hyrule are magic-users, the Terminan Hylians focus on technology and seem to care or know little, if any, for the sacred [[Triforce]] and the [[Golden Goddesses|Three Goddesses]]. Instead, they honor the [[Four Giants]], the gods who shaped Termina and continue to guard it.

==Traits==
===Physical Appearance===
[[File:OoT Link Artwork.png|thumb|295x295px|[[Link]], the main character of the series, is a highly prominent Hylian]]
{{Plural|Series|Hylian}} have pointed ears, and generally have a wide degree of variety of skin and eye colors. The lower classes usually wear casual clothing; {{Term|Series|Hylian}} males tend to wear belted leather jerkins, and females wear long gowns in many different colors.<ref name="ALttP Guide 64">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP|publisher= Nintendo|page= 64}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Hyrulean}} symbols are often part of the embroidery in their dresses. Tall buns are common in women, while men of the working class usually wear broad-brimmed hats in order to protect their skin from the sun and the rain.<ref name="ALttP Guide 64"/> Some men also wear knee-length leather boots.<ref name="ALttP Guide 64"/>

It has been observed that the higher classes including royalty wear complex and luxurious jewelry including precious metals and gemstones such as gold and rubies.

===Culture===
{{Plural|Series|Hylian}} have a {{Wp|Middle Ages|medieval}} theme to them which fits to the [[Medieval elements in The Legend of Zelda Universe|medieval setting]] of the longtime series, especially in the case of earlier games. Many architectural elements seen in the series greatly resemble architecture from the medieval era. Clothing styles vary but are mainly consistent with the overall medieval theme. Unlike most other races, {{Plural|Series|Hylian}} are a predominantly urban race. This, however, by no means suggests that {{Plural|Series|Hylian}} are not seen elsewhere, as many people seen living on farms and other comparable non-urban locations have been {{Term|Series|Hylian}}. {{Plural|Series|Hylian}} are shown to have ranging professions, varying from farmers to merchants and bartenders. In addition, {{Plural|Series|Hylian}} are known to be highly superstitious, having a rich mythology and many legends. {{Plural|Series|Hylian}} are a very populous race and can be found all over {{Term|Series|Hyrule}}, however the majority will tend to live together in grand settlements, such as {{Term|Series|Hyrule Castle Town|link}}.

===Abilities===
One of the few known abilities that the Hylian people possess is the use of their pointed ears to receive special messages from the gods themselves.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=It was also said that their long, pointed ears enabled them to hear special messages from the gods.|game= ALttP|page=4}}</ref><ref>''"There is a story that the shame that the Gerudo felt over giving birth to the source of Calamity Ganon so long ago opened them up to listening for messages from the goddesses. So, they came to have the same long, pointed ears as the Hylians, which some believe allow them to receive special messages from the divine."'' — Hyrule Historia, Dark Horse Books, pg. 401</ref> This also includes the ability to send telepathic messages to other Hylians across time and space. This is an ability that continues to get passed unto their children, their children's children and so on.<ref name="ALttP Guide 64"/> [[Sahasrahla]] and an incarnation of [[Princess Zelda]] display this particular ability in {{ALttP}}, the former being able to communicate to [[Link#A Link to the Past|Link]] across even the void between dimensions to assist the young hero in his quest. They are also known to have incredible hearing.

==Trivia==
[[File:OoT3D Hylian Shield Model.png|thumb|The Hylian Shield from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'']]
*The [[Hylian Shield]] is heavily associated with Hylians, as it is a signature [[Shield]] used by [[Link]] and the [[Knight of Hyrule|Knights of Hyrule]].
*The name Hylian is sometimes misspelled as "Hylain" by fans and some [[The Legend of Zelda in Popular Culture|pop culture]] media.
*In {{OoT}} and {{BotW}}, [[Princess Ruto|Ruto]] and [[Mipha]]'s relationship with two different incarnations of Link, the [[Hero of Time]] and Hero of the Wild, respectively, are known to be unusual to the series' fanbase, as they are both [[Zora]] princesses explicitly falling in love with a Hylian, let alone the main protagonist, marking the first time different races of Hyrule were to be in such a case. The former, during the time of release on the [[Nintendo 64]], was met with [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda series|controversy]], as some rather want the hero to be with [[Princess Zelda]] herself or [[Malon]].
