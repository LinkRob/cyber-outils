==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Like Grains of Sand}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must continuously defeat {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. It can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, fifteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Hydromelon|link}}, fifteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Voltfruit|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} spawns in {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Town|link}}   and must defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}}. Afterwards, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} spawn, which also must be killed. Then, another {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} spawns, and after the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} defeats it, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} spawns. When killed, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} appear, and when one is defeated a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} Skywatcher takes its place. When all of them are killed, a {{Term|HWAoC|Molduga|link}} spawns. When it dies, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} concludes.
