{{Term/Store|Princess Zelda’s Room||Series, BotW}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|'''Princess Zelda's Room'''|Map|BotW}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Zelda's Room||SS}},{{Exp Game|SS}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS |publisher= Prima |page= 255}}</ref> is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}. {{Term|Series|Princess Zelda's Room}} goes unnamed in {{SSHD}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{SS|-}}===
Zelda's room in Skyward Sword is located on the second floor of [[Knight Academy]] next to [[Karane's Room|Karane's room]], which is separated from Zelda's by a wooden divider. It can only be accessed once [[Link]] has received the [[Clawshot|clawshots]] after completing the Lanayru [[Silent Realm]]. Link can use his clawshots to get inside a chimney located on the Academy roof, where he will fall into a tunnel. The tunnel contains [[Rupee|Rupees]] and a view of [[Gaepora|Headmaster Gaepora]] in the bath, and after crawling through the tunnel, Link will fall into Zelda's room through a hole in the ceiling.

Zelda's room is decorated with pink and teal floral-themed pillows, a rug, and a tapestry that match her bedspread. On one wall hangs a portrait of her and Gaepora. Potted plants and books are seen on various shelves around the room, and there is a dresser on which can be seen more books, a basket of yarn and knitting needles, a knitted [[Tingle]] doll, an in-progress knitting project, and a painted carving of her [[Loftwing]] made by Link. Another such carving can be found on her desk, where, if Link sits in the chair there, he can access Zelda's diary. 

Zelda's diary, if Link chooses to read it, says, <blockquote>''"Tomorrow's the big day! The [[Wing Ceremony]]! Finally, [Link] can take a big step toward becoming a knight.'' 

''I can't wait to see him promoted to full knighthood, but I'm a little worried he might have some trouble winning the race.'' 

''Lately, [Link] hasn't taken his flight training seriously. Someone needs to make sure he doesn't mess up his chance!'' 

''So I've made up my mind. Tomorrow I'll wake him up extra early and make sure he gets in some last-minute practice, whether he likes it or not. He has to win, or we won't be able to perform the closing ceremony together!"'' <ref>https://www.zeldadungeon.net/Resources/Textual-Resources/Zelda14-skyward-sword-text-dump.php#9</ref></blockquote>A [[Gratitude Crystal|gratitude crystal]] can be found in Zelda's room by the rug and pillows, and if Link opens her wardrobe he will find a [[Piece of Heart|piece of heart.]] {{Stub}}

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room}} is the private bedroom of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}, found on the western side of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. The entrance connecting the {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room|display=Room}} to the rest of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|display=Castle}} has collapsed. However, it is possible to access {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room}} from a consecutive series of stairs, located on a break in the road to the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room}} is a spacious chamber which has fallen into a severe state of disrepair. Various pieces of furniture stand broken and scattered across the {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room|display=Room}}, and several loose pages of notes are littered around the desk. The stairway to the upper level of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room}} has been utterly demolished, forcing {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to climb in order to reach the second floor. The upper level features very little, save for a doorway leading to a bridge that connects to {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study|link}}.

Atop the desk rests {{Term|BotW|Zelda's Diary|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} recording her inner thoughts as well as her experiences with her newly-appointed knight, {{Term|BotW|Link}}. Though she had her {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study|display=Study}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} took her research everywhere with her, including her {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room|display=Room}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 250}}</ref> The "{{Term|BotW|Father and Daughter|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} can be accessed on the bridge outside {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room}}. During this {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}}, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} confronts {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} and eventually forbids her from continuing her research.<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing out here, Zelda?|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|As the king, I forbid you to have anything to do with these machines from this moment on and command you to focus on your training.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> 

In the northwestern corner of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room}} stands an opaque glass folding screen, which hides a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.
