==Biography==
{{Stub|PH}}
{{Term|PH|Maronie}} is the wife of the {{Term|PH|Old Wayfarer|link}} and the mother of {{Term|PH|Romanos|link}}.

After being overtaken by fog in his pursuit of the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|PH|Maronie}}. She tells him about {{Term|PH|Old Wayfarer|link|display= her husband}}, who she hasn't seen in over a year. She also tells {{Term|PH|Link}} about her son {{Term|PH|Romanos|link}}.<ref name="intro">{{Cite|What?! You want to sail to the island up in the Northwestern Sea?! My husband...called that place the {{Color|PH Blue|Isle of Gust}}. He used to talk about how he had visited that island. My husband...was once content to be a fisherman, until he left this place. He sought uncharted lands. At least that's what he said when he finally left. He refused to work, instead ruining his boat by braving the {{Color|PH Red|northern fog}} repeatedly! The last time we saw him was over a year ago...My son {{Color|PH Blue|Romanos}}, who's at home right now, might know a little more. But that boy hasn't worked in a long time either. He's peeved at his dad, I think. He might get upset if you mention the {{Color|PH Blue|Isle of Gust}}...|Maronie|PH}}</ref>

She doesn't allow {{Term|PH|Link}} to enter the cave behind her home until he speaks to {{Term|PH|Romanos}}.<ref>{{Cite|Young man! You mustn't go into that cave. Dangerous monsters have been seen there lately! Now be a good boy. Don't go anywhere near it.|Maronie|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, what? Well, if my son has said it's all right... Then go on in.
