The {{Term/Store|Light Ring L-2||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Light Ring L-2<br/>Sword beams at -3 Hearts|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Light Ring L-2 can be obtained by scoring 350 points at the [[Shooting Gallery]] of [[Lynna Village]] in {{OoA}}. It is an uncommon reward, the chance for the ring you obtain to be the Light Ring L-2 is 5%.

The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it allows [[Link]] to shoot [[Sword Beam]]s with the [[Noble Sword]] or [[Master Sword]] even when missing up to three [[Heart]]s.<ref name="Description"/> The Light Ring L-2 can be transferred to {{OoS}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
