{{Term/Store|Trainee|Trainees|Series, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are a group of characters in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Trainee|N/A|HWDE}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Plural|HWDE|Trainee}} are {{Plural|HWDE|Soldier|link}} in training that work towards joining the {{Term|HWDE|Hyrulean Army|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= Only those soldiers who have passed the rigorous training at the knights academy are allowed on the battlefield as regular soldiers. |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 424}}</ref> {{Plural|HWDE|Trainee}} are only seen at the start of the first {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}}, "{{Term|HWDE|The Armies of Ruin|link}}." Before being attacked by {{Term|HWDE|Wizzro|link}} and {{Term|HWDE|Volga|link}}'s army, the {{Plural|HWDE|Trainee}} were training in {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Castle|link}}. {{Term|HWDE|Zelda|link}} and {{Term|HWDE|Impa|link}} search for the new {{Term|HWDE|Link|link|display=Spirit of the hero}}, where {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} comments that there will be no heroes among the {{Plural|HWDE|Trainee}}.<ref>{{Cite|We will find no fully realized heroes here... We'd be wiser looking elsewhere.|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> Later, while the {{Plural|HWDE|Soldier}} battle the army of {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy|link}}, the {{Plural|HWDE|Trainee}} wait inside to hear the results.<ref>{{Cite|So...I guess we just wait to hear what happened?|Trainee|HWDE}}</ref>
