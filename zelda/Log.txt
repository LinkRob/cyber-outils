==={{SS|-}}===
Eight Logs can be found in the [[Sparring Hall]] at [[Skyloft]], each marked with a different spot for [[Link]] to practice his [[Link's Sword Strike|Sword Strikes]]. If Link successfully destroys them all, [[Eagus]] will set up an additional ring of eight for Link to perform a [[Spin Attack]], then one final Log to practice the [[Fatal Blow]].
