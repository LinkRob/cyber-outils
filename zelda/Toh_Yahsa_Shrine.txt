The {{Term/Store|Toh Yahsa Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Toh Yahsa Shrine}}<br/>Buried Secrets}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Buried Secrets''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Toh Yahsa Shrine}} is initially buried beneath the ground on the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau}} is constantly under the effects of a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, which can be seen from a distance. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|display=Plateau}}, he hears the voice of a {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} presenting him with a Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You who have reached this land of thunder, I present you with a trial.}}|???|BotW}}</ref> The voice instructs him to place the four spheres found around the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|display=Plateau}} into their corresponding placeholders, which will reveal the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|When the four spirits have been settled in their proper places, they will guide you to the hero's trial.}}|???|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Trial of Thunder|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has retrieved and correctly placed the four spheres, {{Term|BotW|Toh Yahsa Shrine}} will rise from the ground, completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|You set the four spirits in their proper places based on the diagrams on the stone pillars.<br/><br/>When this was done, a shrine appeared in the middle on the Thundra Plateau!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Toh Yahsa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Toh Yahsa. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Toh Yahsa|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Toh Yahsa Shrine}}, he will be standing on a platform in front of several destructible blocks. The blocks are in a rectangular shape that is two blocks tall, five blocks long, and three blocks wide, with a "T" shape hole at the end. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} blows them up with a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}}, he can find a metallic box hidden underneath.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the metallic block to {{Term|BotW|Climbing|link|display=Climb}} to the other side of the platform , where he has access to a {{Term|BotW|Ladder|link}}. At the end of the {{Term|BotW|Ladder}} are more destructible blocks, behind which is another {{Term|BotW|Ladder}}. This {{Term|BotW|Ladder}} leads to a portcullis, and the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} that marks the end of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} can be seen past it.

Underneath the second {{Term|BotW|Ladder}}, blocked by more destructible blocks, is a {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}}. Since the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|display=Switch}} is too big for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to press down, he must use the metal block from earlier to do so. When this is done, the portcullis blocking the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}} will be raised.

After opening the portcullis, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can approach {{Term|BotW|Toh Yahsa}}, who will commend him for completing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} and grants him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Toh Yahsa|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Toh Yahsa|BotW}}</ref>

There are two {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that can be found within {{Term|BotW|Toh Yahsa Shrine}}. The first {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} can be found on a platform to the right of {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to use the metallic block to knock it onto the ground, retrieving the {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}} inside. The second {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} is hidden behind a destructible wall right below the platform {{Term|BotW|Link}} first enters on. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} blows up the wall, he can find a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing the {{Term|BotW|Rubber Armor|link}}.
