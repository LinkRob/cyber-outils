'''Darknut Lv. 3''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Darknut Lv. 3|Huge and powerful, this Darknut variant wears armor that can even protect it from arrows.<ref name="EoW Darknut Lv. 3">{{Cite|Huge and powerful, this Darknut variant wears armor that can even protect it from arrows.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Darknut Lv. 3 are stronger, silver-armored versions of the regular [[Darknut]], with increased health and damage. When summoned as an [[Echo]], they have an Echo cost of five.

The Darknut Lv. 3 also comes in a [[Dark Echo|Dark variant]] coming in contact with a [[Tecuum]].
