{{Term/Store|Harth||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Harth|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Harth}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} man who serves as {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link|display=Rito Village's}} {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} craftsman.<ref name="Craftsman">{{Cite|[You are?] I am Harth, the bow craftsman.|Harth|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Harth">{{Cite|I am Harth. Bow craft is my trade.|Harth|BotW}}</ref><ref name="CaC"/> He has been friends with {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}} since they were children.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 400}}</ref><ref name="Teba">{{Cite|Teba. Him and I go way back.<br/>As far as flying goes, he is undoubtedly the best in the village.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> When he was young, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} dreamed of being a warrior, but he ended up inheriting his family's {{Term|BotW|Bow}}-crafting business.<ref name="CaC"/><ref name="Warrior">{{Cite|My boyhood dream was to be a warrior, but I gave that up once it came time to inherit the family business.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> From this business, he learned how to recreate the {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow|link}}.<ref name="CaC"/> {{Term|BotW|Harth}} is the father of {{Term|BotW|Molli|link}}.<ref name="Molli"/>

Prior to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} arrival in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} refused to listen to {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}} when he told them only a {{Term|BotW|Champions}} could enter {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Only a chosen one, a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}}, can stop a {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}. You must enter the beast and bend it to your will. I tried explaining this to the more headstrong Rito warriors, but they wouldn't listen to reason.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> He and {{Term|BotW|Teba}} attempted to face the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} on their own, which resulted in {{Term|BotW|Harth}} becoming injured.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Teba}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Harth}} tried to confront {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}, but it did not go well, and {{Color|BotW Blue|Harth}} was injured.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Harth}} was forced to retreat because of this.<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, Harth was shot down by {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}} and had to retreat.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find him in his home, where he is in much pain.<ref>{{Cite|Owwwwwwwww...|Harth|BotW}}</ref> Though he initially welcomes {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Arrgh... Welcome. Owwww...|Harth|BotW}}</ref> he dismisses him when he asks what is wrong with him.<ref>{{Cite|[What's the matter?] No offense, but this is not your concern.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, he curses {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|display=Medoh}}.<ref>{{Cite|That cursed {{Color|BotW Blue|monster}}...|Harth|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Harth}} again, he will still welcome him.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh...<br/>Welcome...<br/>Oww...|Harth|BotW}}</ref> When asked who he is, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} introduces himself as the {{Term|BotW|Bow}} craftsman.<ref name="Craftsman"/> Due to his injury, however, he is unable to raise his arm, so his business has halted.<ref>{{Cite|But... I cannot raise my arm right now, so business is not exactly booming.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> Once again, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask him if he is alright, but this time {{Term|BotW|Harth}} admits that he is not.<ref>{{Cite|[Are you all right?] Not really...|Harth|BotW}}</ref> He regrets not listening to {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}}'s warnings about the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|If this is what we are up against, we should have put more stock in the {{Color|BotW Blue|elder}}'s words.|Harth|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}}, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} decides to tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} about what happened when he faced {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, buddy... I'm guessing you caught wind of that {{Color|BotW Blue|monster}} circling around our village. I suppose I should get you up to speed on what went down.|Harth|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses to listen, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} will call out that he still has more to say.<ref>{{Cite|[No, I'm fine.] ...Are you leaving? But I have more to say!|Harth|BotW}}</ref>}} {{Term|BotW|Harth}} reveals that the wound on his wing came from {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|display=Medoh}},<ref>{{Cite|Here goes... You see this wound here on my wing? I got shot by {{Color|BotW Blue|that thing in the sky, Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> which was supposedly once a protector of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Legend has it, that monster was once the protector of this village.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> However, when he and {{Term|BotW|Teba}} approached the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}, it unexpectedly shot at them.<ref>{{Cite|But those days are long gone—as soon as Teba and I approached the thing, it just shot at us out of nowhere.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Harth}} tried to fight back, he was too slow and ended up being hit.<ref>{{Cite|We tried to fight back, but I was too slow and got hit.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Teba}} managed to catch him before he fell out of the sky, and the pair ended up retreating.<ref>{{Cite|Thankfully, Teba caught me while I was falling from the sky. We had no choice but to retreat.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Harth}} is frightened when he thinks of what would have happened had {{Term|BotW|Teba}} not been there to save him.<ref>{{Cite|When I think about what would have happened had Teba not been there... Still gives me the bumps.|Harth|BotW}}</ref>

Now that {{Term|BotW|Link}} knows the story, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} asks if he is going to find {{Term|BotW|Teba}} and promises to tell him everything he knows.<ref>{{Cite|So you planning to go find Teba? I will tell you everything I know.|Harth|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Anything else you wanted to ask about?|Harth|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can then ask {{Term|BotW|Harth}} about {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, {{Term|BotW|Teba}}, or himself. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} shares the legend that it protected {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} before it disappeared 100 years ago, though he does not believe this to be true.<ref>{{Cite|[Who's Medoh?] Divine Beast Vah Medoh... I don't believe it myself, but some say it used to protect our village before disappearing 100 years ago.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> His injury occured when he and {{Term|BotW|Teba}} went to investigate it.<ref>{{Cite|Teba and I got to wondering about it, and when we went to investigate, it just opened fire on us...hence the injury.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> As soon as he is able, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} plans on taking a lot of {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}} up to it and destroying its {{Plural|BotW|Cannon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But as soon as I recover, I plan to fly up there with a while mess of {{Color|BotW Blue|bomb arrows}} and {{Color|BotW Red|blow its cannons to bits}}!|Harth|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Teba}}, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} explains that they have known each other for a while and that {{Term|BotW|Teba}} is the best at flying in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.<ref name="Teba"/> While {{Term|BotW|Teba}} is reckless, temperamental, and rude, he and {{Term|BotW|Harth}} look out for each other.<ref>{{Cite|His only downside is that he's reckless...and has a bit of a temper...and he can be rude. But we look out for each other.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} was disturbed by the way {{Term|BotW|Teba}} was speaking before he left.<ref>{{Cite|But the way he was talking...saying that he was going to "blow {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}} out of the sky."|Harth|BotW}}</ref> He is angered that {{Term|BotW|Teba}} intends to face {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} alone, especially since he has a wife and a child.<ref>{{Cite|He wants to go back up there...alone. Which is absolutely insane. What kind of moron with a wife and a kid does that?|Harth|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Harth}} who he is, he will explain his trade and his childhood dream.<ref name="Harth"/><ref name="Warrior"/> He also reveals that he is the father of {{Term|BotW|Molli}}, and since she is close in age with {{Term|BotW|Tulin|link}}, his family spends a lot of time with {{Term|BotW|Teba}}'s.<ref name="Molli"/> However, he stops his explanation when he realizes nothing he said was helpful to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hold on... Is any of this helpful at all?|Harth|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} will begin to work on a {{Term|BotW|Swallow Bow|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Harth}} is willing to do {{Term|BotW|Link}} the service of crafting him a new {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow}}, should he ever lose or break the one he receives from {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you break or lose the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Eagle Bow}}, I can make a new one for you.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> In this scenario, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} will need a {{Term|BotW|Swallow Bow}}, five bundles of {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}} in order to craft a new one.<ref>{{Cite|But you will need to bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|swallow bow}}, {{Color|BotW Red|five}} bundles of {{Color|BotW Blue|wood}}, and a {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}}.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> As they are expensive to make, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} suggests {{Term|BotW|Link}} try to find the {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow|display=Bow}} if he has lost it somewhere.<ref>{{Cite|Where is your {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Eagle Bow}}? Did you drop it somewhere? I can make you another one, but they are expensive to make. Maybe you should try to find your old one first.|Harth|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to take the {{Term|BotW|Swallow Bow}} from the table near {{Term|BotW|Harth}}, he will call him out.<ref>{{Cite|Hey...|Harth|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Harth}} is still injured, he will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} to keep it, as he is unable to use it.<ref>{{Cite|Heh... Take it.<br/>Not like I can use it in my condition.|Harth|BotW}}</ref> After freeing {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, however, {{Term|BotW|Harth}} allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to keep it as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Sure, go ahead an take it.<br/>Consider it thanks for conquering {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}.|Harth|BotW}}</ref>
