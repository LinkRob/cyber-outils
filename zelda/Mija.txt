{{Term/Store|Mija||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mija|N/A|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Mija}} is a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} who resides in the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}. This {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|display=Fountain}} is located south of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}, near the edge of {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala|link}}. Like the other {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy}}, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} will upgrade {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} in exchange for {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}.<ref name="Enhance">{{Cite|I can {{Color|BotW Red|enhance}} your {{Color|BotW Red|clothing}}, but I require the necessary {{Color|BotW Red|materials}} to do it.|Mija|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} comes across {{Term|BotW|Mija}}'s {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain}}, it will be closed. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|display=Fountain}}, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Mija}} asking him to listen to her story.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Boy...}} Sweet boy... Please... Listen to my story...|Mija|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} agree, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} introduces herself, explaining that her {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|display=Fountain}} was once a spring.<ref name="Mija"/> However, when travelers stopped offering {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to her, she lost her power.<ref>{{Cite|But as time passed, fewer and fewer travelers arrived to offer me rupees. As a result, my power has abandoned me.|Mija|BotW}}</ref> To restore her power, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} requires {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm nearly powerless now, so I beg your help. I need rupees to become whole again.|Mija|BotW}}</ref> Depending on how many out {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} has made offerings to, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} will ask for either 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}},<ref>{{Cite|All I need is {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}}...|Mija|BotW}}</ref> 500 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}},<ref>{{Cite|All I need is {{Color|BotW Red|500 rupees}}...|Mija|BotW}}</ref> 1,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}},<ref>{{Cite|All I need is {{Color|BotW Red|1,000 rupees}}...|Mija|BotW}}</ref> or 10,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|All I need is {{Color|BotW Red|10,000 rupees}}...|Mija|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Mija}} promises to help {{Term|BotW|Link}} once he makes an offering to her.<ref>{{Cite|Once my power has been restored, I can help you do great things, boy.|Mija|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mija}}'s hand will then emerge from her closed {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|display=Fountain}}, and she requests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} hand over his {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now... If you have rupees, hand them over to me. Quickly!|Mija|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} will be disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll keep my rupees.] That is truly tragic...|Mija|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to give her {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} will snatch them from {{Term|BotW|Link}} and thank him.<ref>{{Cite|[Here you go.] Thank you. You are too kind.|Mija|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mija}} then proclaims that her power is overflowing, and her {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain}} will bloom.<ref>{{Cite|Aah... The power... {{Big|It's overflowing!!!}}|Mija|BotW}}</ref>

With her power restored, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} will emerge from the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} of her {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ah-HAAAAAAAAAAA!!!}}|Mija|BotW}}</ref> She is surprised by {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s appearance, having expected him to be bigger than he is.<ref>{{Cite|Mmmmmm. So you're the one who released me. I was expecting someone...bigger, I guess... But a slim little lad like you will do just fine. Yes.|Mija|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} helped restore her power, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} decides to offer him a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, my power has been returned to me. And I think you'll agree—a good deed deserves a good reward.|Mija|BotW}}</ref> She informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she is capable of enhancing his {{Term|BotW|Armor}} if he provides the correct {{Plural|BotW|Material}}.<ref name="Enhance"/> The degree to which {{Term|BotW|Mija}} can enhance {{Term|BotW|Armor}} depends on how many {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy}} have had their power restored.<ref>{{Cite|Wuth the power available to me... I should be able to enhance your clothing {{Color|BotW Red|a little bit}}.|Mija|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With the power available to me... I can enhance your clothing {{Color|BotW Red|a fair amount}}.|Mija|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With the power available to me... I can {{Color|BotW Red|greatly}} enhance your clothing.|Mija|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With the power available to me... I can enhance your clothing {{Color|BotW Red|to the max}}!|Mija|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to enhance his {{Term|BotW|Armor}}, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} will ask him to choose a piece to enhance.<ref>{{Cite|[Enhance away!] Then please choose some clothes for me.|Mija|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes a selection, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} will ask him to wait for a moment.<ref>{{Cite|One moment...|Mija|BotW}}</ref> To enhance the {{Term|BotW|Armor}}, she will either blow a kiss, kiss {{Term|BotW|Link}}, hug him, or drag him into her {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain}}. Following this, she will occasionally explain {{Plural|BotW|Set Bonus|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There we go. Oh, while you're here... Some sets, if you enhance all parts of them twice, can give you a nice extra set bonus when you wear them. This isn't true of everything I can enhance, sadly...|Mija|BotW}}</ref> She then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has other pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor}} he would like to enhance.<ref>{{Cite|Any other clothing items you want me to work on?|Mija|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} run out of {{Plural|BotW|Material}} or reach the limits of {{Term|BotW|Mija}}'s power, she will invite him to come back once he has rectified this.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... I wanted to enhance other pieces of clothing, but that does not seem possible. If you get clothes and materials or if you restore my sisters' powers, then I believe I can help you. See you later.|Mija|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Mija}} without the supplies to enhance his {{Term|BotW|Armor}}, she will notice.<ref>{{Cite|...However, you are lacking any clothing that I can enhance.|Mija|BotW}}</ref> However, she will allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to view his {{Term|BotW|Armor}} enhancements to see what {{Plural|BotW|Material}} he needs.<ref>{{Cite|Here, let's take a look. Maybe I can see what's stopping me from helping.|Mija|BotW}}</ref>

If not all of the {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain}} have been opened, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} notices when {{Term|BotW|Link}} ends their conversation that her not all of her sisters' powers have been restored.<ref name="Sisters"/> She tells him that if they all regain their powers, they will be able to enhance his clothing more than she can currently.<ref name="Sisters"/> Following these encounters, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} will instead invite {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return when he has more pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor}} he wants to upgrade.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... So long for now, I suppose? Once you have some clothing you want to upgrade, come back anytime. See you later.|Mija|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes enhancing his {{Term|BotW|Armor}}, {{Term|BotW|Mija}} will dive back into her {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain}}.<ref>{{Cite|So long!|Mija|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Good-bye!|Mija|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And gone!|Mija|BotW}}</ref>
