'''Fire Fruit''' are recurring items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Locations and Uses==
==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Compendium|number=213|name=Fire Fruit|desc=A fruit that generates heat when struck. Cooked correctly, its heating properties improve blood flow and stimulate muscles to raise attack power in high temperatures.|locs=Hyrule Field, Eldin Canyon|props=Fuse Atk: 1|cookeffects=Hot Weather Attack|healing={{Damage

Fire Fruit are plants that can be found in Hyrule. When thrown or [[Fuse|fused]] to a weapon or arrow, it explodes on impact, causing all flammable materials, items or enemies to ignite.
