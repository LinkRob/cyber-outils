{{Term/Store|Splitblade||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Splitblade}} is the fourth eldest member of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}. He lives in a waterfall cave on the southern side of {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}. He will teach {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to charge his {{Term|TMC|Split Gauge|link}} more quickly.<ref>{{Cite|Mnnh! Now I will teach you how to fill your {{Color|TMC Red|Split Gauge}} faster than ever!|Splitblade|TMC}}</ref> His dojo can only be accessed once {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Grimblade|link}} and can swim to it using the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}.
