{{Term/Store|Force Like|Force Likes|Series, FSA|plural}} are enemies in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 178}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Force Likes are one of the variants of [[Rupee Like]]s. Like their parent species, a Force Like will suck the [[Force Gem]]s out of one of the [[Links]] if he comes too close. A small Force Gem is attached to its only stalk, the only part visible when it is buried in the ground; it does this precisely to appear like a real Force Gem, ambushing Link whenever he comes near. The amount of Force Gems that it steals from Link will depend on the Force Gem that it has at its top; for example, if it has a blue Force Gem at the top, it will steal five Force Gems at a time, as that is the standard amount of Force Gems obtained by a small Blue Force Gem. Link will receive all stolen Force Gems upon the creature's defeat.

At times, Force Likes will appear after a large Force Gem falls from the sky. Since they only take Force Gems away from Link, they do not cause any real damage.
