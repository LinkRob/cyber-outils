{{Term/Store|''Kass's Journal''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kass's Jorunal|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Kass's Journal}} is found within a hut on a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} located on {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff|link}}. It is written by {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} and is used to record rumors he has heard about "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|"I leave here a record of my search for the ancient songs of Hyrule."<br/>- {{Color|BotW Blue|Kass}}|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> The contents of this journal reveal all the locations {{Term|BotW|Kass}} can be found, where he will provide {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, as well as one {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.
