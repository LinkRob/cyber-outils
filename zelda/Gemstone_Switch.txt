{{Term/Store|Gemstone Switch|Gemstone Switches|Series, SS|plural}} are objects in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Order of the Four Gemstone Switches|Sheikah Stone|SS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SS|Gemstone Switch}} are lock mechanisms that can only be unlocked by striking their gemstones in the correct order. They appear twice: once in the {{Term|SS|Ancient Cistern|link}} and once in the {{Term|SS|Sandship|link}}, respectively.
