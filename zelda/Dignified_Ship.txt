{{Term/Store|Dignified Ship|Dignified Ships|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dignified Ship|Shipyard|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Dignified Ship|book= E|publisher= |page= 284}}</ref> The name refers to both the individual [[Ship Part]]s and the complete Ship.

==Location and uses==
===Ship Part===
[[File:PH Practical Ship Model.png|thumb|Practical Ship model]]
Dignified Ships are [[Hull]]s belonging to the completed Dignified Ship of the same name. They have an austere color scheme.<ref>{{Cite|A hull with a very serious red and black paint scheme.|Shipyard|PH}}</ref> Just like most Ship Parts, [[Link]] can collect multiple Dignified Ships. They can be obtained through various methods:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Methods}}

Attaching three, six, or all eight Dignified Ship parts to the [[SS Linebeck]] will increase its stamina to five, six, or seven Hearts, respectively. Dignified Ships can be sold to the [[Treasure Teller]] for either 50, 150, or 800 [[Rupee]]s. Their value is determined randomly when the [[Saving|Save]] file is created, and is the same for all Ship Parts from the Stone Ship set.

===Ship===
The Dignified Ship is the name given to the SS Linebeck once it has been outfitted with the following [[Ship Part]]s:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Dignified Ship}}

The Ship Parts can be attached to the SS Linebeck at any time by visiting the [[Shipyard]] on [[Molida Island]]. For every three parts of the Dignified Ship used, the durability of the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} is increased by one Heart. When all eight parts are used together, they increase its durability by three Hearts, up to a total of seven Hearts. Aside from this boost in durability, the Dignified Ship is merely a change in appearance to suit the player's personal tastes.
