{{Term/Store|EX The Final Battle||HWAoC}} is a level 93 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX The Final Battle|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the first wave of the game’s {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX The Final Battle}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any four {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of five {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Ironshell Crab|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Stamella Shroom|link}} and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat seven {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within 16 minutes. The seven {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are  two {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Moblin}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Igneo Talus|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Frost Talus|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Lynel|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Igneo Talus}} is in the northwest and the {{Term|HWAoC|HWAoC|Malice Frost Talus}} is in the northeast, with a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Moblin}} stationed not far in front of it. The {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}} is in the center of the map, the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox}} is in the east, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Lynel}} is in the southeast. {{Term|HWAoC|Mutated Ganon}} is also far in the southeast corner, although closed gates block the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} from reaching him. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Moblin}} are also both surrounded by three {{Plural|HWAoC|Eye of Malice|link}}. Even more, when a {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} approaches the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Igneo Talus}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Meteo Wizzrobe|link}} spawns, and when the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Frost Talus}} is approached, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blizzrobe|link}} spawns. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}} is approached, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}} spawn, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} spawns next to the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Lynel}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox}} when approached. When four {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Hollow|link}} of each Champion besides {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}} spawns and will advance towards a {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. Unlike the other {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} that spawn after the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, these {{Plural|HWAoC|Hollow}} must be defeated and the required amount of defeated {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} rises to eleven. From the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}} are also scattered around the map. When all eleven key {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, the closed gates surrounding {{Term|HWAoC|Mutated Ganon}} opens and the time limit is taken away. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when {{Term|HWAoC|Mutated Ganon}} is defeated.
