"{{Term/Store|My Hero||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|My Hero}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Outskirt Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Aliza|link}} standing under one of the {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} across the road. When he speaks to her, {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} reveals that she has been waiting for him.<ref>{{Cite|You're finally here...|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> She claims that the {{Term|BotW|Tree}} she is standing at is sacred, and she has finally met the hero of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link|display=Hyrule}} underneath it.<ref>{{Cite|So it was true! I met the hero of Hyrule under this sacred tree!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} has not yet acquired the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} will retract her claim, regardless if {{Term|BotW|Link}} affirms or denies the accusation.<ref>{{Cite|[Uh... What?] So you're not the hero of legend? I was so sure... ...But now that you mention it, you're way too scrawny to be the hero. And you don't have the {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}}.|Aliza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Yes, I am the hero.] I knew it! At long last I— ...Wait a minute! You can't fool me! Everyone knows the hero of Hyrule wields the {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}}! You don't have it!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already acquired the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} before speaking to {{Term|BotW|Aliza}}, she will discover that he is the hero regardless of how he responds to her.<ref>{{Cite|[Uh... What?] Don't try to hide it! That sword there is proof enough… you are the hero I've been waiting for! This...is a little something I've been saving for you. Please take it! |Aliza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Yes, I am the hero.] I knew it was you! That sword leaves no room for doubt. I'm so happy to finally meet you! This...is a little something I've been saving for you. Please take it! |Aliza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Bye.] Wait! I knew it was you! That sword leaves no room for doubt. I'm so happy to finally meet you! This...is a little something I've been saving for you. Please take it!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>}} {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to direct the hero to her should he encounter him,<ref>{{Cite|If you happen to meet the hero who wields the {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}} during your travels... Tell him I'm waiting for him here.|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> initiating the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

Upon retrieving the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} underneath her sacred {{Term|BotW|Tree}} and present it as proof of his status.<ref>{{Cite|So did you meet the hero of Hyrule?|Aliza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[I am he.] WHAT?! So...it WAS you!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> Recognizing the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}},<ref>{{Cite|That sword is proof enough... I'M SO HAPPY RIGHT NOW!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} will accept {{Term|BotW|Link}} as the hero she has waited for and present him with a {{Term|BotW|Star Fragment|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This... is a little something I've been saving for you. Please take it!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} is full and he is unable to receive the {{Term|BotW|Star Fragment}}, {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return to her when he has room.<ref>{{Cite|I want to give you something, but your pouch is totally full...as I would expect of a hero! Please come see me again once you have a little room in there. I'll be waiting!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>}} Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} will comment on how great it was to meet the hero, but since {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not her type, she begrudges the fact that her excitement has dissipated.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, man. It feels SO good to check that off the bucket list! But...now that I've met you, some of the excitement's gone. I mean, you're not even my type...|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> She then leaves her spot from the base of the {{Term|BotW|Tree}}, completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, buddy. Well, toodle-oo!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>
