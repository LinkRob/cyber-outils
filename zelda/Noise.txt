'''Noise''' is a spell in {{ZA}}.

==Overview==
The Noise Spell fires a sound wave projectile that is very effective against creatures with a sensitivity to sound. Noise is the only spell that can damage [[Pols Voice]]. It costs one [[Rupee]] to use.

[[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] receives the Noise spell from a woman in the village east of the [[Forest of Torian]].
