==Location and Uses==
Roasted Bird Drumsticks are the result of [[Raw Bird Drumstick]]s that have made contact with [[Fire]]. A Roasted Bird Drumstick is said to have crisp skin and a simple taste, despite its lack of seasoning.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Black Hinox|link}} when they're killed. [[Link]] can restore one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} by eating a Roasted Bird Drumstick.
