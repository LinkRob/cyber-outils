A '''Flux Construct''' I is a mini-[[boss]] from {{TotK}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Compendium|name= Flux Construct I|number=160|desc= A construct composed of interconnected blocks, made by the Zonai with advanced techniques and magic. It can rearrange the blocks to change its shape at will. To provide sufficient energy to power its gigantic form, this construct contains a massive store of Zonai charge.|locs=  West Hebra Sky Archipelago, Gerudo Highlands Depths|loot= Zonai Charge}}
'''Flux Construct I'''s are [[Construct|Constructs]] made of floating cubes held together by [[Zonai]] magic. They can change forms, including a humanoid shape, a giant cube, and a flying form that can be [[Ascend|Ascended]] through.

Their weak point is a glowing green cube that moves depending on their form. Using [[Ultrahand]] to pull it out breaks the Construct apart, leaving it vulnerable until it rebuilds.
