{{Term/Store|Bookshelf|Bookshelves|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref> {{Plural|TMC|Bookshelf}} go unnamed in {{TMC}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TMC|-}}===
There are numerous {{Plural|TMC|Bookshelf}} in buildings throughout {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} in {{TMC}}, particularly {{Plural|TMC|House|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}.

Several {{Plural|TMC|Bookshelf}} can be found on both floors of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}, one of which is home to {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Jotari|link}}, and another {{Term|TMC|Library Minish|link}}. This {{Term|TMC|Bookshelf}} can only be fully scaled while {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized and after he has returned all three overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Bookshelf}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Library|link}} and obscure hidden passageways. As the {{Plural|BotW|Bookshelf}} are metallic, they can be moved with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Bookshelf}} on the eastern side of the {{Term|BotW|Library}} covers a passage to the {{Term|BotW|Docks|link}}, while a shelf on the western side blocks a flight of stairs that lead to a small excavation site.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Have you found this excavation site yet? |author= Nintendo|published= April 27, 2017|retrieved=April 30, 2017|url= https://www.facebook.com/LegendofZelda/photos/a.171885779539227.41729.170177419710063/1389944371066689/ |title= The Legend of Zelda |site= The Legend of Zelda Facebook|type=}}</ref> A northern bookshelf also guards the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|King's Study|link}}.
