{{Term/Store|Goat|Goats|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are recurring [[Creature]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 63 {{Exp Game|Series, TP}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|LANS, BotW}}

==Appearances==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Main|Christine}}
In {{LA}}, an anthropomorphic goat named Christine lives in the [[Animal Village]]. She plays a role in the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}. Christine will give [[Link]] the {{Term|LANS|Letter|link}} in exchange for the [[Hibiscus]], which may be then delivered to [[Mr. Write]], whom she is writing to.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Main|Ordon Goat}}
In {{TP}}, Ordon Goats are the primary livestock of [[Ordon Village]], where they are mainly kept for their [[Milk]] production.{{Fact}} Ordon Goats have enormous horns that form a complete circle above their heads. This defining trait is used as a common motif around Ordon Village. The goats are notoriously stubborn and liable to escape. <ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 63 {{Exp Game|Series, TP}}}}</ref>. [[Ordona (Spirit)|Ordona]], the [[Spirit of Light|Spirit]] of [[Ordona Province]], appears to [[Link]] in the form of an Ordon Goat.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Main|Mountain Goat, White Goat}}
{{BotW}} features Mountain Goats and White Goats that appear throughout [[Hyrule]]. Mountain Goats are aggressive toward [[Link]] and are commonly found in [[Hyrule Ridge]] and the [[Great Hyrule Forest]]. They drop [[Raw Meat]] when killed. White Goats are domestic variants of Mountain Goats with white coats and a docile demeanor. They are found in pens at certain [[Stable]]s and cannot be harmed by Link. [[Goat Butter]] is produced from their milk.<ref>{{Cite|Butter made from the milk of a domesticated white goat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{RTBToL|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Stub|RTBToL}}
In {{RTBToL}}, there is a unique species of {{Plural|RTBToL|Goat}} that have large, drooping noses and woolly fleece. A herd of colorful {{Plural|RTBToL|Goat}} are kept in the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Chief}}'s room in the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|link}}. {{Term|RTBToL|Liar|link}} must milk one of these {{Plural|RTBToL|Goat}} in order to collect its {{Term|RTBToL|Milk|link}} and give to {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}}, which is then used to calm {{Term|RTBToL|Big Liar|link}}.

{{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} can also be transformed into a {{Term|RTBToL|Goat}} if he gives an incorrect answer during {{Term|RTBToL|Big Liar}}'s {{Term|RTBToL|Boss|link}} fight.
{{Noncanon|End}}
