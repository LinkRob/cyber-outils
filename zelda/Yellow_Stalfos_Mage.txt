{{Term/Store|Yellow Stalfos Mage|Yellow Stalfos Mages|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Yellow Stalfos Mage|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==

{{Plural|CoH|Yellow Stalfos Mage}} are a stronger variety of {{Term|CoH|Stalfos Mage|link}}. Like many enemies, they move to the music on every other beat, slowly making their way towards {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. If any of the heroes stand within two spaces directly ahead of them, they will stop on one beat to let out a metallic growl and use their magic on the next beat to pull the heroes towards them before attacking on the third beat. Like other {{Term|CoH|Stalfos|link}} enemies, they only appear in caves and dungeons and can be summoned by a {{Term|CoH|Yellow Sarcophagus|link}}.

==Trivia==
They behave identically to the Yellow Skeleton Mage from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
