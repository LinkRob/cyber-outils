==Location and Uses==
Baked Palm Fruits are the result of [[Palm Fruit]]s that have made contact with [[Fire]]. Baked Palm Fruits are best served right after the roasting process, once the juices emerge.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> By eating one, [[Link]] can restore one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}.
