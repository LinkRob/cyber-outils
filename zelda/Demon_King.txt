'''Demon King''' may refer to either of the following characters who hold the title:

*'''[[Ganon]]''', the main antagonist of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]].
*'''[[Malladus]]''', the primary antagonist and [[Final Boss]] of {{ST}}.
*'''[[Demise]]''', the primary antagonist and [[Final Boss]] of {{SS}}.

Although not a Demon King himself, [[Ghirahim]] adhered to a similar title called Demon Lord.

==Nomenclature==
The term '''Demon King''' (魔王, Maō) is derived from Japanese Buddhism and was a reference to [[wikipedia:Mara|Mara]], a demonic ruler sitting atop the sixth realm of desire in Buddhist mythology.

Prior to {{ST}}, the term was generally translated into varying terms, such as Prince of Darkness in {{TLoZ}}, King of Darkness in {{ALttP}}, Great King of Evil in {{OoT}}, and Dark Lord in {{TP}}.
