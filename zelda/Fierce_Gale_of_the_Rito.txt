{{Term/Store|Fierce Gale of the Rito||HWAoC}} is a level 26 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fierce Gale of the Rito|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Fierce Gale of the Rito}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Salmon|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Snowquill Headdress|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Revali}}'s objective in this {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} is to defend the Allied Stronghold {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} against many {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} for five minutes. There are four paths leading to the Allied Stronghold, and each is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} at the start. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} advance toward the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} along with some of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin}}. As each {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} is defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}} appears in their starting position alongside {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin}}, which do the same. Eventually, as {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, the same happens again, with {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Chuchu|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Keese|link}}, although only two appear. It also happens with {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, with only two spawning as well. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once either five minutes pass or each major {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy}} is defeated.
