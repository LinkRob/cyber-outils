'''[[Wikipedia:Satoru Iwata|Satoru Iwata]]''' was the current President and CEO of [[Nintendo]], promoted in 2000 by the previous President, [[w:c:nintendo:Hiroshi Yamauchi|Hiroshi Yamauchi]], before his retirement the same year.<ref name=Iwata>[http://stars.ign.com/objects/958/958699_biography.html IGN: Satoru Iwata Biography]</ref> Iwata is known for his contributions to the Nintendo subsidary, [[Wikipedia: HAL Laboratory, Inc.|HAL Laboratory, Inc.]], and is attributed to the success of the [[Nintendo Gamecube]], [[Nintendo Wii]], [[Nintendo DS]], and the latter console's launch title, ''Brain Age''.<ref name=Iwata /><ref name=Satoru>[http://cube.ign.com/articles/530/530986p1.html Profile: Satoru Iwata]</ref><ref>[http://www.gamespot.com/news/6185358.html Q&A Nintendo's Satoru Iwata]</ref>

While at HAL, Iwata worked on concept and design for the ''EarthBound'' and ''Kirby'' games, and eventually was promoted to President of the company in 1993. His work at HAL carried over to his career at Nintendo, contributing towards titles such as ''[[mariowiki:Super Mario Sunshine|Super Mario Sunshine]]'', ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox Adventures|Star Fox Adventures]]'', ''Metroid Prime'', ''Eternal Darkness: Sanity's Requiem'', ''Animal Crossing'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]''.<ref name=Iwata /> Iwata currency discusses the Wii and future Nintendo plans in his portion of the Nintendo online site, Iwata Asks. <ref name=Satoru />

Iwata died on July 11, 2015, in Kyoto, Japan, at the age of 55 years old. It's commonly believed that the [[Lord of the Mountain]] from [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]] and [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]] is a tribute to Iwata, as it resides on [[Satori Mountain]].
