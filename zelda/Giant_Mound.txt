The {{Term/Store|Giant Mound||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Kass">{{Cite|"When a lost hero calls down {{Color|BotW Red|lightning}} from the sky / The monk responds from a {{Color|BotW Red|giant mound}} on high."|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound}} is located at the edge of {{Term|BotW|Calora Lake|link}}. It rests on a cliff overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Ebara Forest|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Faron Highroad|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} arrives at {{Term|BotW|Calora Lake}}, he will find {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} there. {{Term|BotW|Kass}} offers to sing an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" about the area to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to listen, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} sings lyrics that describe a {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} responding from the {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound}} when {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} is called down.<ref name="Kass"/>

Breaking the {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound}} is required to complete the "{{Term|BotW|A Song of Storms|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. To break the {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must have it struck by {{Term|BotW|Lightning}}. This can be done by placing a metallic object, such as a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}, on top of it. Once this is done, the {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound}} will be destroyed, and {{Term|BotW|Qukah Nata Shrine|link}} will be revealed.<ref>{{Cite|By attracting a lightning strike, you shattered the large rock and revealed an ancient shrine concealed within.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
