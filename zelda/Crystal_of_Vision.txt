The {{Term/Store|Crystal of Vision||Series}} is an item in {{TFoE}}.{{Name Ref|TFoE}}

==Location and Uses==
To obtain the Crystal of Vision, you have to obtain it by killing [[Lupay (Boss)|Lupay]]. [[Gwonam]] can make the [[Lantern of Vision]] thanks to it, which helps you see invisible enemies such as the [[Sancrumi]].

It's optional, but makes the final level far easier.
