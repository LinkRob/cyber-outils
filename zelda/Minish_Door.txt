The {{Term/Store|Minish Door||Series, TMC}},<ref>{{Cite|The Minish Door opens but once in a hundred years, and it will soon close.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> also known as the '''Doorway''',<ref>{{Cite|You know, I do recall a story my grandmother once told me long ago. She said she saw a {{Color|TMC Blue|doorway}} appear in the {{Color|TMC Blue|palace courtyard}} as a child. If this door does indeed open only once a century, it may be open even now!|Minister Potho|TMC}}</ref>, is a location in {{TMC}}. It is the magical gateway between the land of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm|link}}.

==History==
When the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door}} is open, the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} can come and go between the two worlds as they please. This {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|display=Door}} also yields access to the bridge between the two worlds, the sacred {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} where the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} can be infused with the powers of the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}}. This {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|display=Door}}, located in the {{Term|TMC|Palace Courtyard|link}} of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden|link}}, opens only once a century for a very brief time, limiting the amount of travel that can take place between the two worlds. It was through this {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|display=Door}} that the evil {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} first came to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}, marking the coming of one of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}'s greatest threats second only to {{Term|Series|Ganondorf|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s master, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}}, also journeyed through this {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|display=Door}} in pursuit of {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} in an attempt to stop him from acquiring the mythical {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}} and to restore himself to his original shape after {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} stole the powerful {{Term|TMC|Mage's Cap|link}} from him and turned him into a cap himself. 

{{Term|TMC|Minister Potho|link}}'s grandmother claimed to have seen the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door}} when she was just a child in the {{Term|TMC|Palace Courtyard|display=Courtyard}} of the castle, a story {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho|display=Potho}} would later pass on to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} when he came in search of the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary}}. {{Term|TMC|Link}} would come through the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door}} several times as he gathered the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements}} and eventually recreated the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}} as the legendary {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}. After {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s defeat and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}}'s restoration to his former self, the great {{Term|TMC|Minish}} sage bid {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}} farewell as he returned to his homeland through the closing {{Term|TMC|Minish Door}}.
