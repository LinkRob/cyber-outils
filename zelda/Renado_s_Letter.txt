{{Term/Store|Renado's Letter||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an item in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
After clearing the [[Temple of Time (Dungeon)|Temple of Time]], [[Link]] returns to [[Kakariko Village]]. Once there, he receives the letter from [[Renado]], who asks him to deliver it to [[Telma]]. The letter details how to treat [[Ilia]] in order to [[Retrieval of Ilia's Memory|recover her memory]]. Link then receives in exchange the [[Invoice]] from Telma.
