==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle|link}} and her three baby {{Plural|ALBW|Turtle}}, {{Term|ALBW|Tallo|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Jillo|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Sabro|link}}, reside in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake|link}}. When {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} first encounters the {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle}}, she pleads for his help in finding the babies,<ref>{{Cite|Oh no! I got separated from my little turtles. You have to help me look for them—you just have to!|Mama Turtle|ALBW}}</ref> who must be rescued from various predicaments around the {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake|display=Lake}}. Once rescued, the {{Plural|ALBW|Turtle}} return to their mother and climb atop her back, their shells acting as platforms that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can ride on to access {{Term|ALBW|Turtle Rock|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found all of my babies! Thank you so much! I'm sorry I don't have anything I can offer you as a reward. If you need a ride somewhere, you can go ahead and hop on, though!|Mama Turtle|ALBW}}</ref>

{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSBM|-}}===
The {{Term|MM3D|Giant Turtle|link}} from {{MM}} appears in {{SSBM}} as a stage element on the {{Term|SSBM|Great Bay|link}} stage. It rises and falls through the water on the right side of the stage, and its shell and palm tree serve as extra platforms when it's above water. It may face toward the screen or with its head facing left.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
With the {{Term|SSBU|Great Bay|link}} returning as a stage for {{SSBU}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Giant Turtle}} returns as well, in the exact same role. Its design was updated to match that of {{MM3D}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
