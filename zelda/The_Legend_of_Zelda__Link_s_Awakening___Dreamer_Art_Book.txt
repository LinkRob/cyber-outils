The '''''The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening — Dreamer Art Book''''' is an art book that is included as part of [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening Dreamer Edition|Dreamer Edition]] of {{LA|NS}}.

==Overview==
''The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening — Dreamer Art Book'' features 122 pages of concept art. It includes both unused and final artwork for most features of the game, including characters, enemies, items, buildings, and dungeons. However, it does not include any artwork involving the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish|link}} or the {{Term|LANS|Shadow Nightmare|link}}.
