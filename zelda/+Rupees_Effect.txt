The {{Term/Store|+Rupees Effect||Series, LANS}} is an item in {{LA|NS}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|LANS Pink|+Rupees Effect}}!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|LANS}}
Link receives the +Rupees Effect by acquiring all 50 [[Secret Seashell|Secret Seashells]] and bringing them to the [[Seashell Mansion]]. 

When applied to a room in a [[Chamber Dungeon]], the +Rupees Effect will occasionally drop Rupees throughout the room. This will include Green Rupees, Blue Rupees, and a few Red Rupees. There is a set amount of rupees that will drop; the quantity is not endless. After this period expires, the effect will wear off and no additional rupees will drop.
