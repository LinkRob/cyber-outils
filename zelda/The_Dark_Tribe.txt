'''The Dark Tribe''' is a fan-made name for a very elusive tribe that was sealed away into the [[Dark Mirror]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]''. This Dark Tribe invaded Hyrule and was sealed, leaving the Dark Mirror that they were sealed into in the [[Temple of Darkness]]. 

=== Power of the Tribe ===
Ganondorf later steals the Dark Mirror which contains their power and summons Shadow Links. Their power of darkness is said to be in a mirror prison. This Tribe was clearly powerful as they possessed the power of Darkness such as [[Vaati]] and [[Ganon]].
