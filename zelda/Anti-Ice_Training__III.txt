{{Term/Store|Anti-Ice Training: III||HWAoC}} is a level 25 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Ice Training: II|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Ice Training: III}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Chillshroom|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Cool Safflina|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Chillfin Trout|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat four {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} within five minutes. Only one is present, but when it is killed, the other three spawn. When they are all killed, a gate opens to a {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus|link}} and nine {{Plural|HWAoC|Frost Pebblit}} that spawn in front it. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus}} is defeated.

A {{Term|HWAoC|Korok|link}} can be found near the southeastern corner of the map, and awards the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} with a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}} if found.
