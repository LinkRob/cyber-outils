==Characteristics==
To defeat a {{Term|FS|Nokkun}}, the {{Plural|FS|Links|link}} must strike them with their {{Term|FS|Sword|link}}, stunning them. Afterwards, they roll up into their shells, similar to a {{Term|FS|Hikkun|link}} or a {{Term|FS|Rollobite|link}}. Then, a {{Term|FS|Links}} must pick it up and throw it towards another {{Term|FS|Links}}, who must strike the {{Term|FS|Nokkun}} as it opens its shell to attempt to glide through the air. Like {{Term|FS|Hikkun}}, they can only be destroyed by two {{Plural|FS|Links}} working together.
