==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Rock-Hard Food}} is the result of an invalid cooking recipe. It appears when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} attempts to cook food containing {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}} or Ores. {{Term|BotW|Rock-Hard Food}} only restores a quarter of a  {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link|display=heart}} and has no effects. Eating these meals is noted to be difficult.<ref name="Inventory"/>

[[Moza]], the aspiring chef who can be found near [[Ishto Soh Shrine]], has a recipe called the lavish meat dish that will only result in Rock-Hard Food. She tells Link to prepare either bird meat or steak and then to throw in some Ore.<ref>{{Cite|Sure thing! I'll happily teach you the recipe for my lavish meat dish. First, prepare the meat you want to use. I recommend either bird meat or steak. Then you toss in some niiiice freeesh... {{Big|ORE!!}}|Moza|BotW}}</ref> If Link calls the recipe gross, she scolds him for being rude and asks how else one is supposed to be a lavish meat dish.<ref>{{Cite|[That sounds gross.] Hey! That's not nice! Cooking delicious meat with shiny ore is the only way! How else would one create a truly lavish meat dish, silly?!|Moza|BotW}}</ref> However, Moza admits that the resulting meal is always Rock-Hard Food, but she doesn't understand how it gets that rocky.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, what I always end up getting is {{Color|BotW Blue|rock-hard food}}... How did it even get that rocky anyway?|Moza|BotW}}</ref>
