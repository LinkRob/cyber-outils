{{Term/Store|Treasure Chart|Treasure Charts|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are items in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 141}}</ref> They are used by [[Link]] to help him find treasure. Such treasures include [[Rupee]]s and [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]]. The treasure is found by using the [[Grappling Hook]] in the [[Great Sea]], at the location shown on the map and marked out by a pillar of light which disappears when [[Link]] approaches. Once found, the treasure is marked on the [[Sea Chart]], to indicate that the treasure in that square is already found and any unobtained treasures are in another square. There are a total of 41 Treasure Charts in the original game. In addition to the 8 [[Triforce Chart]]s, the charts account for all 49 islands of the [[Great Sea]].

In {{TWWHD}}, five of the Triforce Charts were removed and replaced with Treasure Charts 42-46. The locations of the treasure chests remain unchanged, but they contain Rupees (and, in one case, a Piece of Heart) instead of [[Triforce Shard]]s.

==Location and Uses==
The charts are numbered in-game, though this order isn't necessarily consistent with location or availability order. The symbol [[File:HD.png|x16px]] is used to identify the Treasure Charts that appear only in {{TWWHD|-}}.
