'''''The Goddess Collection''''' is a trilogy of books featuring different type of content regarding {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is comprised of {{HH}}, {{A&A}}, and {{E}}.<ref name="Title">{{Cite Web|quote= This, the last of ''The Goddess Collection'' trilogy, which includes ''Hyrule Historia'' and ''Art & Artifacts'', is a treasure trove of explanations and information about every aspect of The Legend of Zelda universe!|retrieved= Jan.9, 2022|url= https://www.darkhorse.com/Books/3000-358/The-Legend-of-Zelda-Encyclopedia-HC|title= The Legend of Zelda Encyclopedia HC|site= Dark Horse Comics}}</ref>

==Features==
{{HH}} was originally released in Japan on December 21, 2011 celebrating the [[The Legend of Zelda 25th Anniversary|25th anniversary of the series]]. It consists of a behind-the-scenes look at {{SS}}, the full [[Zelda Timeline|timeline]], concept art, design sketches, and development information of all the main games in the series, and the {{SS|Himekawa}}.

{{A&A}} was originally released in Japan on August 26, 2016 celebrating the [[The Legend of Zelda 30th Anniversary|30th anniversary of the series]]. It features artwork released from {{TLoZ}} through {{BotW}}, spanning promotional pieces, box art, instruction booklet illustrations, and sprite art. The book also features interviews with the artists behind the series.

{{E}} is divided into three chapters spanning from {{TLoZ|-}} to {{TPHD}}. The first one is a visual encyclopedia explaining keywords like the [[Master Sword]] and [[Hyrule]]. The second one is a list of enemies, items, dungeons, and more. The third and final chapter features archives with explanatory notes and storylines of each game in the series.
