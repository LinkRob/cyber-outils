{{Term|TWWHD|Thorned Fairy Island}} is one of the islands located on the Great Sea. The entrance to the fairy shell is blocked by vines that will only disappear by hitting the three switches with the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer|link}}. Once inside, Link can receive a Quiver upgrade from the Great Fairy.

A [[Piece of Heart]] can be pulled from the bottom of the sea once Link finds and opens a [[Treasure Chart]] from the [[Wind Temple]].
