{{Term/Store|Mercay Island||Series, PH, BotW}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 113 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|'''Mercay Island'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, many earthquakes occur on Mercay Island.<ref>{{Cite|So many quakes lately.|Ciela|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We've seen so many quakes rattle the island lately, Link|Oshus|PH}}</ref> These earthquakes often cause the bridge connecting the western part to the eastern part of the Island to collapse.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, heavens! That quake just broke the bridge again! So many quakes lately, and the bridge breaks every time there's a big one!|Saya|PH}}</ref> This forces the islanders to get from one side of the Island to the other by going through an alternative path that leads to a dark cave filled with monsters.<ref>{{Cite|There's a second way to get to town, fortunately. Unfortunately, it leads through a frightening cave full of monsters.|Saya|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Be cautious, young ones. That cave up north leads to the port, it's true. But that path will be crawling with monsters.|Oshus|PH}}</ref> This happens to Link early in the game, forcing him to take a route though dangerous caves.

Link can find [[Oshus's Sword]] in the latter's storehouse,<ref>{{Cite|Grandpa uses this cave as his storehouse.|Ciela|PH}}</ref> a cave next to Oshus' house. The Sword is kept behind a locked door to prevent people from touching his belongings.<ref>{{Cite|Keep your hands off my things. Please ask first.|Oshus|PH}}</ref> In order to open the door, Link must write a certain number on a sign, this number corresponds to the number of [[Palm Tree]]s on the Island's beach: seven.<ref>{{Cite|I remember he cracks open the door by writing a number on that sign. I think he says it's the number of {{Color|PH Red|palm trees}} on the beach|Ciela|PH}}</ref>

In the main port village<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Mercay Island's port! You'd normally see sailors coming and going....|Fuchiko|PH}}</ref> on Mercay there is a [[Mercay Island Shop|shop]] selling [[Treasure]], [[Potion]]s, [[Power Gem]]s and a [[Wooden Shield]]. There is a [[Mercay Tavern|Tavern]] as well where Link must ask about [[Linebeck]]'s whereabouts; he later meets Linebeck in the Temple of the Ocean King. Additionally there is a [[Shipyard]] where Link can get the [[Salvage Arm]] repaired and change [[Ship Part]]s, giving the Ship more stamina and a different look.

Mercay Island has a smaller island off its north-eastern coast, on which Link meets [[Freedle]] and gains a [[Wisdom Gem]] as well as a [[Treasure Map]].
