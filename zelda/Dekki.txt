==Biography==
During their journey in the {{Term|FSA|Lost Woods|link}}, {{Term|FSA|Dekki}} asks help from the {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm. You're not a Deku Scrub, so why are you even here? Do you wanna help us?|Dekki|FSA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Really? Is that so? That's a big help! I was in big trouble, 'cauz there weren't enough Deku Scrubs! Now, uh...were you the one who's going to help? C'mon, let's just get started.|Dekki|FSA}}</ref> He requests them to find three objects and to show them in a specific order to {{Term|FSA|Deppi|link}},<ref>{{Cite|You ready? I'm gonna name three items, and I want you to remember each of 'em! I ain't repeating myself!! Then you go into the woods, nab 'em, and bring 'em back.
