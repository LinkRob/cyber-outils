==Biography==

Greba is the mother of [[Gondo]]. She can be mainly found washing her son's dirty laundry. However, due to the constant winds in [[Skyloft]], the clothes dry quickly.<ref name="Bio"/> She criticizes her son for his constant tinkering with his broken [[Scrapper|robot]], which once belonged to his grandfather.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I'm happy to hear that someone else believes me, but I don't think I can help you. You see, my grandpa's old robot... What's it called again... Oh, hey, that's right... I remember! His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Scrapper}}. He may not be much to look at these days... But he was an amazing robot once! When you called him, he would go anywhere and haul anything! Sadly, as you can see now... he's just another busted ol' hunk of junk...|Gondo|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Greba's name is likely derived from the {{Wp|Grebe}} bird.
