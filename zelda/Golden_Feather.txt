{{Term/Store|Golden Feather|Golden Feathers|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are items in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Golden Feathers are [[Spoil]]s obtained from [[Peahat]]s, winged [[Mothula]]s, and [[Kargaroc]]s. If Link gives 20 of them to [[Hoskit]] on the second floor of [[Dragon Roost Island]], he will receive an [[Orange Rupee]]. Afterwards, Link will receive a letter from Hoskit's girlfriend with a [[Piece of Heart]] inside.

==Trivia==
*In {{HW}}, the [[Winged Boots]], the first level of [[Linkle]]'s [[Boots#Hyrule Warriors|Boots]] Weapon class, are adorned with small wings resembling Golden Feathers.
