{{Term/Store|Gossack||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Gossack is a resident of [[Windfall Island]] who mostly stays inside the [[Cafe Bar]].

In order to obtain the [[Picto Box#Deluxe Picto Box|Deluxe Picto Box]], Link has to take a picture of Gossack displaying fear.<ref name="fear"/> To do this, Link can roll into the wall near Gossack, throw a [[Pot]] at him, or smash some cups in the café to scare him.
