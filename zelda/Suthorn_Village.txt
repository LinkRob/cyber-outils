'''Suthorn Village''' a settlement situated in southern Hyrule in {{EoW}}, bordering the [[Suthorn Forest]], between the Gerudo Desert and Faron Wetlands.

==Features and overview==
Suthorn Village is the first village that [[Princess Zelda]] visits after her [[Hyrule Castle]] escape. It is here that Link grew up and is very popular amongst the citizens for his bravery. The village is relatively small and contains only four houses, including [[Link's House]] located in the northwest corner of the village. The house has a bed, and some of Link's artifacts, including his trusty cap.

In the village center is a well that Zelda can fall into to find some pots, wooden boxes, as well as a treasure chest. There is also a child bragging about his trampoline, that Zelda can assimilate with the [[Tri Rod]]. 

Next to the Well, the [[Suthorn Village Shop]] can be found where Zelda can purchase some [[Floral Nectar]], [[Red Potion]], and a [[Piece of Heart]].
