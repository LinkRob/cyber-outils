{{Term/Store|Soka Point||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Soka Point'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Soka Point}} is a small peninsula located along the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. Around the boundary between {{Term|BotW|Soka Point}} and {{Term|BotW|Cape Cresia|link}}, there is a small {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}}. At the very tip of {{Term|BotW|Soka Point}}, a {{Term|BotW|Monument Shard|link}} sits half submerged in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} near a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}};<ref>{{Cite|Hrm...hrm... Hmm... {{Small|Some of the fragments must still be missing.}} Well, keep looking. {{Small|I wonder if the currents are strong enough to have carried a fragment all the way to Cape Cresia or Soka Point...}}|Garini|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} needs to take a picture of the {{Term|BotW|Monument Shard}} for the "{{Term|BotW|A Fragmented Monument|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you really want to help, help me find the missing fragments, will you? They'll be scattered all around. I think the fragments had the same kind of script carved into them... If you find a fragment, please draw a {{Color|BotW Red|picture}} of it and bring it to me. I'll be here finishing my examination of this stone monument!|Garini|BotW}}</ref>

A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} is buried near the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}. Slightly east off the tip of {{Term|BotW|Soka Point}}, another metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} sits underwater containing a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}}. This {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} is shown by a small oval of shallow water on the {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}. A small distance south of that {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} and even further off of {{Term|BotW|Soka Point}}, another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a second {{Term|BotW|Topaz}} is submerged in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}. This {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} is not marked on the {{Term|BotW|Map}} in any way.

At the very tip of {{Term|BotW|Soka Point}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}} under a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
