{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield++}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} that can be found in "A Major Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, where they are used by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}}. They can also be found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn when the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. They are upgraded {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield|link}} that have had their output levels boosted to the maximum.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield++}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref> Their combat abilities are said to surpass those of metallic {{Plural|BotW|Shield}}, and they are capable of deflecting the beams of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} and other {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

As they are made from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield++}} will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. In addition, they will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also cannot be manipulated by the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
