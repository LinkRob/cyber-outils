{{Term/Store|Hyrule Castle West||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hyrule Castle West|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Hyrule Castle West is located west of [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. It is an island found on the western side of Hyrule Castle and has many [[Tree]]s and two small mountains. The [[Castle Town Prison]] ruins lay to the east, between a pathway and the [[Hyrule Castle Moat]]. A cave opening can be found covered by spiky [[Vine]]s and a bombable rock wall on the west side of the southern plateau. Inside this cave, Link can find the [[Noya Neha Shrine]], [[Bomb Barrel]]s, and some metal and [[Wooden Box]]es. Along the riverbank south of this plateau, Link can find more Bomb Barrels and metal and Wooden Crates.

A [[Stone Talus (Rare)]] can be found atop the plateau in the center of the island.

An [[Archery]] [[Puzzle]] can be found on the northwest part of the island. Standing on the [[Tree Stump]] marked by a [[Pinwheel]] will start the puzzle, and an [[Acorn]]-shaped {{Term|BotW|Vase|link}} will be tossed into the air by the Trees. [[Link]] must shoot the {{Term|BotW|Vase}} with an [[Arrow]] to complete the [[Korok]]'s puzzle and receive a [[Korok Seed]]. A second Seed can be found by lifting a rock atop the southernmost plateau.
