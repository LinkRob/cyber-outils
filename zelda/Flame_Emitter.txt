The '''Flame Emitter''' is an [[Item]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom.]]<ref>"''Flame Emitter''

''A Zonai device that shoots fire. It's thought to have been originally used as protection from monsters."''</ref>

==Locations and Uses==
{{Manual|TotK|Flame Emitter|A Zonai device that shoots fire. It's thought to have been originally used as protection from monsters}}
Flame Emitters are [[Zonai Devices]] that can be used in battle by attaching them to a [[shield]] using [[Fuse]].<ref name=":0">[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2EMZzW7hj0M&ab_channel=Zeltik I Played Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom (Hands-On Preview) - Zeltik]</ref> It can also be used in collaboration with the [[Balloon]] to create a flying machine, due to the fire and hot air being emitted into the [[Balloon]].<ref name=":0" />
