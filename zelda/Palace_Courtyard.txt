==Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Palace Courtyard}} is an area located within {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}}, on the first basement floor. The {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|link}} appears within it once every one hundred years, serving as an entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} and a bridge between {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is first able to access the {{Term|TMC|Palace Courtyard}} after obtaining the newly-forged {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Melari|link}}. After searching the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}, he and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} discover the opened {{Term|TMC|Minish Door}} that only they can see (due to {{Term|TMC|Link}} being a child and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} being a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} sage) within the {{Term|TMC|Palace Courtyard|display=Courtyard}}.

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} emerge from the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} to find the {{Term|TMC|Palace Courtyard}} bleak and withered, newly populated with the cursed stone forms of two {{Plural|TMC|Vassal|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Palace Courtyard}} remains like this until {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} is able to use the {{Term|TMC|Mage's Cap|link}} to undo {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s evil and restore {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}.
