{{Term/Store|Goron Shop|Goron Shops|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are recurring locations in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:GoroncityBombshop.png|thumb|248x248px|left|The Goron welcoming Link to the Goron Shop in {{OoT|-}}]]
In {{OoT}}, the Goron Shop is on the lowest level of [[Goron City]]. It specializes in [[Bomb]]s, but it also sells a few other items, most notably the [[Goron Tunic]]. This is the only place the tunic can be acquired if [[Link]] loses his due to a [[Like Like]]. However, the shop is initially closed off by a wall that Link can open by using a [[Bomb Flower]] near. Once Link returns to Goron City as an adult, the shop will be closed off again, and it will only open after Link stops and talks to [[Link (Goron)|Darunia's son]].

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the Goron Shop is located in the [[Hot Spring]] of [[Death Mountain#Twilight Princess|Death Mountain]]. Link can access the store either by having the Gorons throw him into the upper levels and then dropping down into the Hot Spring, or by using the elevator situated where [[Gor Coron]] is. The elevator can only be activated by using the [[Iron Boots]] on the [[switch]].

== Trivia ==

* The Goron shopkeeper in {{OoT}} is the only [[Goron]] aside from [[Darunia]] and his [[Link (Goron)|son]] to never be captured by [[Ganondorf]] to feed [[Volvagia]] at the [[Fire Temple]].
* If the Fire Temple is prematurely completed before meeting Link the Goron, he will no longer gift [[Link]] this item nor open the door to the Goron Shop, making the [[Goron Tunic]] become [[Permanently Missable Items|permanently missable]]. Even if Link enters the shop as a child and attempts to purchase it, the [[Goron]] shopkeeper will refuse to sell Goron Tunics to a child.
