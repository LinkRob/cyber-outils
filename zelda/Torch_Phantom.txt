{{Term/Store|Torch Phantom|Torch Phantoms|Series, ST|plural}} are enemies in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 289}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Torch Phantoms are equipped with red armor and a flame engulfed sword. Their swords are great for illuminating dark rooms. This Phantom can light up torches, as well as stun [[Nocturn]]s that haunt the corridors in the upper dark rooms in the tower.<ref>{{Cite|Yay! I'm a {{Color|ST Light Red|Torch Phantom}}, so I've got this great sword to light the way! Now there's no need to worry about the dark!|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref>
