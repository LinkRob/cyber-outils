{{Term/Store|Peater||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 35}}</ref> He runs the [[Clean Cut]] mini-game on [[Bamboo Island]] and is the father of [[Peatrice]].

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Peater}}, a swordsman who is thought of as quite handsome. He was once a knight, and he is also the father of {{Color|SS Blue|Peatrice}}, from Item Check.</p><p>He likes to chop bamboo, and he is very protective of his daughter as he tries to keep amorous boys much like his younger self away from her.</p>
}}

When Peater is first met on Bamboo Island, Link does not seem to remember him,<ref>{{Cite|What? You tellin' me you don't know who I am?|Peater|SS}}</ref> though Peater knows the young hero.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Is that you, Link? Never thought I'd run into you here.|Peater|SS}}</ref> This prompts Peater to explain his identity, declaring he used to be "Mr. Skyloft",<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Skyloft, that was me.|Peater|SS}}</ref> and very popular.<ref>{{Cite|There was a time I was the {{Color|SS Blue|most popular guy in all of Skyloft}}. Folks were always cheerin’ me on like it was a parade. But as you know, time passes.|Peater|SS}}</ref> He also adds that Peatrice is his daughter.<ref>{{Cite|But...you might relate better if I told you I was the proud father of Skyloft's most popular girl: {{Color|SS Blue|Peatrice}}, {{Color|SS Blue|princess of the Item Check}}. How's that? Does that clear anything up for you, young feller? Ha ha ha ha!|Peater|SS}}</ref> Afterward, he can be found in his house in [[Skyloft]] at night.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for stopping by. Welcome to the home of Skyloft's most popular father-and-daughter dream team.|Peater|SS}}</ref> Due to age and stress, he claims he has lost the figure he once had,<ref>{{Cite|And I’m not the young buck I used to be. I eat when I get stressed, and I’m worried about my darlin’ daughter all the time! I mean, look at this belly!|Peater|SS}}</ref> though his reputation still stands. He continues to wear the chain mail he wore as a knight, although it does not fit correctly because of his weight gain. To train enthusiastic youths, such as he once was, he runs the Bamboo Island both as a business<ref>{{Cite|So, what's it gonna be? Want to hack some bamboo? One try will cost you {{Color|SS Red|10 Rupees}}.|Peater|SS}}</ref> and an opportunity for knights to test their skills.<ref>{{Cite|Thing is, see, it's a contest to see how many cuts you can make before it hits the ground. If you're good enough, you'll stroll out of here with a prize!|Peater|SS}}</ref> Depending on how well he does, Link will be rewarded with either nothing, 30 [[Rupee]]s, an uncommon [[Treasure#Skyward Sword|Treasure]], or a rare Treasure. If Link uses the [[Goddess Sword]], Peater will advise him that slicing bamboo would be easier with a longer blade.<ref>{{Cite|Well, look at that. Now that I take a gander at the thing, it's clear you got yourself a stubby little sword. You did mighty well considering that. See, the longer the sword you got, the easier it is to zip through that bamboo like a loaf of warm butter! If you get yourself a longer blade in the future, come back and try again. You'll be surprised what a little extra steel can do for your chopping score!|Peater|SS}}</ref>

He is extremely defensive of his daughter, cautioning Link not to get anywhere near her.<ref>{{Cite|Not that I want you anywhere near my little cupcake. No, sir! You lay a finger on her, and you'll have to buy new gloves.|Peater|SS}}</ref> Later, however, Peater will ask Link to keep any admirers of Peatrice away from her, as he cannot always remain in Skyloft,<ref>{{Cite|You got to help me out. You see anyone buzzin’ around my little flower, you give him what for and send him packin’. I’ll keep my peepers peeled too, but I can’t stand watch all day long.|Peater|SS}}</ref> oblivious to the fact that Link is the one she has fallen for. If Peatrice confesses her undying love to Link,<ref>{{Cite|Link... You came to see me. Thank you. The truth is...there's something I'm just dying to get off my chest, Link. I'm just going to ask you something point-blank, OK? (...) What do you think about me?|Peatrice|SS}}</ref> and he refuses her, Peater will enthusiastically thank Link for discouraging the interested youth in question, and give him five [[Gratitude Crystals]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Link! Have I got news for you! My sweetie daughter's given her good-for-nothin' feller the boot! Of course, I feel for her. I hate to see her blue and all, but... I'm pleased as punch the egg-swipin' scamp's out of the picture! Tell me true, Link. Are you responsible for breakin' them up? Never mind! It makes no difference to me. I'm as happy as a Loftwing in an updraft. I truly am.|Peater|SS}}</ref> If he accepts her love, Peater will be disappointed in him, but remain ignorant that Link is the one she has feelings for.<ref>{{Cite|Bamboo slivers! My darlin' daughter's got herself a feller, Link! Oh...who could it be? Who's the scamp who's stolen my muffin's heart? And you, Link! Why did I ever put my trust in you? Any more brains and I'd be as smart as a tree stump. Oh my goodness gracious... What am I supposed to do now?|Peater|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Following the bird naming patterns of most Skyloftians, Peater seems to be a corruption of "Peter" with the reference to a "peacock"
