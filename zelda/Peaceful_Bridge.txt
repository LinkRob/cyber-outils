{{Term/Store|Peaceful Bridge|Peaceful Bridges|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Peaceful Bridge|Shipyard|PH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|PH|Peaceful Bridge}} are {{Plural|PH|Bridge|link}} belonging to the {{Term|PH|Stone Ship|link}} set of {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}. They are said to offer peace and comfort.<ref>{{Cite|With its temple like serenity, it offers peace and comfort.|Shipyard|PH}}</ref> Just like most {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}}, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} can collect multiple {{Plural|PH|Peaceful Bridge}}. They can be obtained through various methods:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Methods}}

Attaching three, six, or all eight {{Term|PH|Stone Ship}} parts to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} will increase its stamina to five, six, or seven {{Plural|PH|Heart}}, respectively. {{Plural|PH|Peaceful Bridge}} can be sold to the {{Term|PH|Treasure Teller|link}} for either 50, 150, or 800 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}. Their value is determined randomly when the [[Saving|Save]] file is created, and is the same for all {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} from the {{Term|PH|Stone Ship}} set.
