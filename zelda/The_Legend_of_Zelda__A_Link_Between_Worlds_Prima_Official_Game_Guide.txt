'''''The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds Prima Official Game Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{ALBW}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The book features 320 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{ALBW}}, a listing of [[Characters in A Link Between Worlds|characters]], {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}}, and {{Plural|ALBW|Boss|link}}, maps, and a checklist of collectibles.
