{{Term/Store|Rimba Beach||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Rimba Beach'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Rimba Beach is located along the [[Necluda Sea]] in [[East Necluda]]. It is a small beach with a few [[Palm Tree]]s.

Along the Beach, [[Link]] can find a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Red Rupee]] buried in the sand. By the rocks out in the waters of the Necluda Sea sit two more metallic Treasure Chests that contain a bundle of 5 [[Ice Arrow]]s and a [[Knight's Shield]], respectively.

A [[Korok]] can be found hiding in a [[Circle]] of [[Lily Pad]]s south of the aforementioned rocks.
