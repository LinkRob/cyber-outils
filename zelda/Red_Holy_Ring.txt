The {{Term/Store|Red Holy Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Red Holy Ring<br/>No damage from small rocks|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Red Holy Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be found in an underwater cave in the [[Zora Seas]], requiring the [[Long Hook]] to reach. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it negates all damage from [[Octorok]]'s rocks.<ref name="Description"/> The Red Holy Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
