The {{Term/Store|Foothill Stable||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Foothill Stable|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}} is nestled along the main road that leads further into [[Eldin Canyon]] region through eastern {{Term|BotW|Trilby Valley|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|display=Stable}} is surrounded by rugged hills and cliffs, giving it its name. On the eastern cliff stands the {{Term|BotW|Mo'a Keet Shrine|link}}, which when activated, can be used as a convenient {{Term|BotW|Travel Gate}} to the {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|display=Stable}}. The {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}} contains every amenity characteristic to {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}}, including {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} services, providing lodging, and a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}. Several {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}} are left outside which can be broken for small {{Plural|BotW|Item}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|display=Stable}} is run by {{Term|BotW|Ozunda|link}}, who mans both its {{Term|BotW|Horse}} services and lodging. As with all other {{Plural|BotW|Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} can occasionally be found here selling his wares.

{{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} the {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} bard can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|display=Stable}}, who when spoken to, will offer to sing {{Term|BotW|Link}} a song about {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}'s past 10,000 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|I happen to know a song about the ancient hero. It was passed down to me by my teacher. Do you care to hear it?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The travelers {{Term|BotW|Boldon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dayto|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kairo|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Karsh|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Zorona|link}} all stop at {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}} on their journeys.

Inside the {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|display=Stable}} is a large {{Term|BotW|Painting|link}} depicting {{Term|BotW|Trilby Valley}} and the {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}}. One of the {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|display=Stable}}'s regulars, {{Term|BotW|Mayro|link}}, can be found admiring the {{Term|BotW|Painting}}. {{Term|BotW|Mayro}} states that it was painted by {{Term|BotW|Ozunda}}.<ref>{{Cite|This painting was made by Ozunda, the owner of this stable, or so I hear.|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> He believes that the {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|display=Stable}} depicted in the {{Term|BotW|Painting}} was painted from the southern direction.<ref>{{Cite|I think the {{Color|BotW Red|stable was painted from a southern direction}}... The composition is just so impressive...|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Painting}} provides a clue to the location of the {{Term|BotW|Tah Muhl Shrine|link}}, and speaking with {{Term|BotW|Mayro}} initiates the "{{Term|BotW|A Landscape of a Stable|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

A worker at {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}} named {{Term|BotW|Gaile|link}} sells {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}} to travelers aiming to travel through the region's hotter climate.<ref>{{Cite|That place is sheer cliff after sheer cliff, and it gets so hot past the {{Color|BotW Red|second checkpoint}} that your body will catch fire. Normally I wouldn't stop a stranger from heading to their doom, but I kinda like ya. So I guess I have no choice but to sell you these {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixirs}}. They can protect you from the burning air.|Gaile|BotW}}</ref> She will sell one {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|display=Elixir}} for 60 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, two for 110, and three for 150.
