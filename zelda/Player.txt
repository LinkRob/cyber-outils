The '''Players''' are a pair of unique [[characters]] appearing in the Japanese [[Satellaview]]-exclusive titles ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda]]'' and ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|BS Ancient Stone Tablets]]'' released in 1995 and 1997 for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super Famicom]], respectively, replacing [[Link]] as the central heroes of the two broadcast games.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= ぼく・あたし<br/>プレイヤー。|product= BSX|page= 06}}</ref>

The names and gender of the Player is dependent on the BS-X broadcast ID. The male Player wears a backwards baseball cap and has green hair whereas the female Player has red hair that is tied up with a white ribbon. As they resemble Link (especially in ''Ancient Stone Tablets''), both characters wear the same [[Green Clothes|green tunic]] as the franchise's main protagonist in the two ''BS Zelda'' games they star in with the exception of the floppy elf-like hat. Like him, they also carry their [[Sword|Swords]] on their left hand with the [[Shield]] on the right. 

The characters both originate from the ''{{Wp|BS-X}}'' cartridge for the Satellaview, in which they were a pair of avatars that users would control in a virtual town that served as the system's interactive menu, where users could download new content.

==Biography==
===''BS The Legend of Zelda''===
In ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda]]'', the Players replace [[Link]] as the protagonist, in which he is completely absent. The game is otherwise mostly then same as the original ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' released on the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] and thus follows the same events; the Player is given the task to find all of the pieces of the {{Term|BSTLoZ|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} that [[Princess Zelda]] broke to prevent [[Ganon]] from obtaining it. After finding all of the [[Triforce Shard|eight pieces]], the Player then fights Ganon in the very cave where they found the {{Term|BSTLoZ|Wooden Sword|link}} and rescues Zelda from her imprisonment afterwards upon recovering the [[Triforce of Power]] via killing the Dark Beast with the [[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]].

===''Ancient Stone Tablets''===
In ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|Ancient Stone Tablets]]'', the Players return as the game's central heroes. Set six years after ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' (ironically the same time gap as the two games' release dates in Japan) and during Link's absence, the long-time peace of the [[Hyrule|Kingdom of Hyrule]] comes to an abrupt end as {{Term|AST|Princess Zelda}} starts to have reoccurring dreams of an oncoming great evil. She and {{Term|AST|Aginah|link}}, the brother of [[Sahasrahla]], one day witness a flash of light in the forest and go to investigate where they find the Player, and take him/her to rest at {{Term|AST|Sahasrahla}}'s old hideaway. While they are both confused by the child's strange tunic matching Link's and are concerned with the original hero's long absence, Zelda begins to wonder if the child may have an extreme importance with {{Term|AST|Hyrule}}, and believes them to be the {{Term|AST|Hero of Light|link}}.

The young hero soon embarks on a unique journey to locate eight ancient {{Plural|AST|Stone Tablet|link}} to help discern the onslaught of evil. With the help of the {{Term|AST|Fortune Teller|link}}, the trio soon learn that even though Link had destroyed Ganon previously, the King of Evil's soul had lived on through his malice, had learned of the {{Term|AST|Hero of Light}}'s power and coveted it, hoping to use it to become fully reborn, and had subsequently summoned the young hero into {{Term|AST|Hyrule}}. The Player then faces the arch-villain in battle at his [[Ganon's Tower|tower]] and ultimately defeats him using the [[Master Sword]] and Silver Arrows just like Link did in the original game. With peace now restored to Hyrule once again, the Player then disappears to where they came from in a flash of light.

== Trivia ==

* As they only appeared in the extremely rare ''BS-X'' broadcast games, the Player characters do not exist in ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' canon and they themselves are considered lost [[Nintendo]] media.
* From the beginning of ''Ancient Stone Tablets'', the Players have a [[Golden Bee]] item, which is used to attack enemies.
* Excluding the [[Philips CD-i|Phillips CD-i]] games, the Players are the first playable protagonist in the franchise that is not [[Link]].
* Due to the fact the Player functions identically to Link, can wield the [[Magical Sword]] and [[Master Sword]] in ''BS The Legend of Zelda'' and ''Ancient Stone Tablets'', respectively, possess pieces of the [[Triforce]], and is capable of defeating [[Ganon]], it is likely the protagonists possesses the spirit of the [[Hylia's Chosen Hero|Legendary Hero]] much like himself.
