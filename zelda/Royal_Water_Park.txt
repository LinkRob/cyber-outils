The '''Royal Water Park''' is a location featured in the "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairies in the Spring|link}}" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}.<ref name=park>{{Cite Episode|quote= Most certainly. Still, my Royal Water Park should be completed soon, then all the kingdom will have some place to cool off and have fun.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> It is being constructed per the orders of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian|link}}, who wishes to have a place where the citizens of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Hyrule}} can cool off and have fun.<ref name=park/> 

==History==
As the construction of the water park is nearing completion, {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Water Monster|display=strange water creatures|link}} begin to attack the workers.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Yeah, the king's water park is almost done! We were sure lucky to find that underground spring.|name=Workers' Boss|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> The workers' boss reports the incident to the king and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} and Zelda promptly go to the water park to investigate.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Never saw anything like it, your majesty. Scared off all my crew and took the whole place!|name=Workers' Boss|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> When they arrive, the duo are attacked by a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Water Monster}} and later Zelda is almost drowned by an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Eel|link}}, but both are easily defeated. When they resurface, two crab-like monsters grab them and once the heroes zap them both,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I can't...reach mine either! Oh but, we can zap each other's!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> they see that the King has arrived.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Well, well. Enjoying the park, eh? Good, good. That's what it's for.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> Just as Zelda warns him not to stand near the water,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Daddy, please! Don't stand so close to the water.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> a tentacle comes out of the water and grabs him, which takes him deep underwater. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} dives in to the pool but is unable to find neither the king nor the monster.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Sorry, Zelda. He's...gone, too.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref>

When {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda ask the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce|link}} of Wisdom for advice, she tells them both to keep looking underwater.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=To find your father, royal daughter, you must search beneath the water.|name=Triforce of Wisdom|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> Using special {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic|link}} helmets made with the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}}'s powers that allow them to breathe underwater,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=We may have to stay underwater for a while. These magic helmets will allow us to breath.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> the duo dive into one of the park's pools and are attacked by a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Giant Fish|link}}, which ends up taking them into the nearby {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairy Spring|link}}. It is eventually discovered that the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairy|link}} of the spring were creating the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Water Monster}} as a means of protecting their water source that was being drained by the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Royal Water Park}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=We didn't know who you were. All we knew was somebody was draining our spring.|name=Spryte's Sister|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> The King, who was talking with the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Oberon|display=Fairy King|link}} all along, explains that their water park was unknowingly taking water from the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairy Spring}}. The Fairy King had gotten worried, but now that the king has prompted to use {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic}} ponds as their source, all is forgiven and the construction of the water park is finally completed, where both {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Human|link}} and {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairy}} can enjoy it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=That's right, my dear. I'm afraid our water park was hooked into the fairy water supply. And the fairy king here got worried. Perfectly natural for them to defend it, eh? [...] Not a bit, my dear! We'll just use magic ponds to move our water. Once all the pools are full, we won't need to use any more! I was just explaining that to Oberon here. He's not worried anymore.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref>
