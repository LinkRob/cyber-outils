{{Term/Store|Margomill||Series, ALBW}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Boss|link}} in {{ALBW}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 191}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Margomill}} was referred to as '''Moldorm''' in {{Guide|ALBW|Prima}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALBW |guide= Prima |page= 48}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} and is one of the two bosses in which the Player(s) can fight in any order during the first act of the game. It resides in the {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales|link}}, one of the dungeons located in [[Hyrule]]. In his quest to claim the [[Pendants of Virtue]], {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} must defeat this monster in order to obtain the [[Pendant of Wisdom]].

This creature consists of a giant eyeball on top of several cylindrical platforms resembling a gearbox. It is located at the top of the dungeon, and can be accessed once {{Term|ALBW|Link}} finds a [[Small Key]] and unlocks the path leading to its lair. Once defeated, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} will be rewarded with the Pendant of Wisdom.

==Characteristics==
{{Term|ALBW|Margomill}} is fought on a rectangular platform surrounded by a bottomless pit. It will spin around the room, and may push {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} into the pit if he gets too close, either by physical contact or by blowing gusts of air out of the multiple holes in its body. Link must use the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|link}} to jump on top of it, reach its eye, and attack it with the {{Term|ALBW|Forgotten Sword|link}}. After a few hits, {{Term|ALBW|Margomill}} will stack two new layers, raising its eye off the ground and putting it out of reach. In addition, the eye will close completely. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must attack the extra layers with his {{Term|ALBW|Forgotten Sword|display=Sword}}, {{Term|ALBW|Hammer|link}}, or {{Term|ALBW|Bow|link}} to destroy them, bringing the eye closer to the ground.

Once all of its layers have been destroyed, it will open its eye, making it vulnerable again. After another round of {{Term|ALBW|Forgotten Sword|display=Sword}} slashes, the boss will gain four more layers, and begin moving much faster. After destroying all of these and attacking the eye a few more times, {{Term|ALBW|Margomill}} will finally be defeated and {{Term|ALBW|Link}} will be awarded the Pendant of Wisdom.

== Trivia ==

* Margomill is very similar to [[Stallord]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess'']] and [[Skeldritch|Skeldritich]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks|''The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks'']], as much like those bosses, Margomill has the strategy of Link having knock out it's parts in order for Link to lower Margomill and deal damage to it's vulnerable eye.
** However, unlike both Stallord and Skeldritich who remain on the ground in their second phases after losing their parts, Margomill will regain back it's parts and rise higher after Link has deal enough damage.
