==Overview==
{{Incomplete}}
To find a lead on the Fifth Sage, Link headed to {{Term|TotK|Kakariko Village|link}} which may contain clues. There he found {{Term|TotK|Paya|link}} -- the village Chief -- and {{Term|TotK|Tauro|link}} -- Leader of the {{Term|TotK|Zonai Survey Team|link}}. The two are standing near a floating ruin and pondering, who claimed that {{Term|TotK|Princess Zelda|link}} ordered them and the team that they are forbidden to investigate the floating ruin, although they do not know why because she never left any additional details.{{Fact}} Because of this, the floating ruin is initially cordoned off unlike the other four ruins around the village; if Link tries to enter the area, {{Term|TotK|Calip|link}}, who is zealously keeping watch (secretly hoping that the princess will recognize his worth as a researcher if he obeys her), will reprimand him and Link will be automatically taken off the area.<!--May have broken sequence by visiting the village too early before this quest-->

After Link told the two about the "Zelda" being an impostor at {{Term|TotK|Hyrule Castle|link}}, Paya and Tauro were surprised; which they believed that the floating ruin was likely a threat to the {{Term|TotK|Demon King|link}} and used "Zelda's" position to deceive them. With the restriction order no longer coherent, Paya ordered the floating ruin to be reopened.

But with the ruin suspended in the air, Tauro could not find any way to get up or inside it. Link found his {{Term|TotK|Ascend|link|display=way up and inside}} the ruin then took a look for himself. He found a slab inside and {{Term|TotK|Camera|link|display=took a picture}} for reference. He returned to trio and showed them the writing on the slab.

Tauro deciphered some of the words, but could only make out of a "hidden key in southeast" and an unfamiliar name, "{{Term|TotK|Mineru|link}}." Paya also translated one of the writing as "dragon land." Recalled these words, Calip remarked of a {{Term|TotK|Zonai|link}} ruin somewhere in {{Term|TotK|Faron|link}}. Took all of these in account, Tauro presumed the writing indicate that the clue of the Fifth Sage led somewhere at a Zonai ruin in southeast Hyrule. Intent on investigating that ruin, Tauro and Calip headed out to setup camp near the {{Term|TotK|Skyview Tower|link}} in {{Term|TotK|Popla Foothills|link}}.

Link caught up with at the investigation camp, but found Calip there. He told that Tauro went ahead to Dracozu Lake to investigate. Catching up, Link finds Tauro inside a ruin studying an intact mural. He read it as some sort of riddle,<ref>{{Cite|This first part says, "Wear the electric garb hidden at long-necked dragons along the wide-mouthed forest serpent."|Tauro|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Next, it says... "Offer a Zonai charge to the altar at the tail."|Tauro|TotK}}</ref> but does not know how to proceed with it. He hint it may have something to do with Dracozu River.

Deducing the riddle, Link finds several similar ruins along the river and found {{Term|TotK|Charged Set|link|display=an archaic, energy-charged ritualized garment}}. Upon donning the outfit, Link reached the end of the Dracozu River, found yet another ruin, saw some form of altar, and dropped a {{Term|TotK|Zonai Charge|link}} on it.

An ancient mechanism activated within the ruin and the structure started to take form, along with nearby similar ruins. A form of electric current activated from atop these structures and after a short while, a charged lightning strike discharged from all the ruins. Above from a distance, the thunderclouds dissipated and revealed a new island.

Tauro and Calip were in the vicitiy when it happened.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Calip! Did you see that?! Lightning struck that dragon pillar too!<br>Huh? Is someone in those ruins?!}}|Tauro|TotK}}</ref> Regrouping with Link, he explained how it happened, and how another sky island appeared in the sky.<ref>{{Cite|Unbelievable...<br>A mechanism in the ancient ruins got rid of the thunderclouds and revealed some new sky islands?|Tauro|TotK}}</ref> They were amazed to hear a new discovery, but were troubled since they have no way to reach that new sky island. They decided to report to {{Term|TotK|Purah|link}}; Tauro will head back to Kakariko Village to write a report while he request Calip to look for any additional discoveries about the ruins.

To Link, the sky island that appeared in the sky could likely lead to the Fifth Sage. So he set his sights to venturing towards that new sky island.
