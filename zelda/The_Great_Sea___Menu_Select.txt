"'''The Great Sea / Menu Select'''" is a [[Song]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|SSBfWU, SSBU}}

==Overview==
"{{Term|Series|The Great Sea / Menu Select}}", as the name indicates, is a combination of two Songs from {{TWW}}: one that plays while traveling the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}} and the other that plays on the {{Term|TWWHD|File-Selection Screen|link}}.

==={{SSB4|Wii U|-}}===
"{{Term|SSBfWU|The Great Sea / Menu Select}}" plays in the {{Term|Series|Tetra's Ship|section=Super Smash Bros. Series|display=Pirate Ship|link}} and {{Term|Series|Skyloft|section=Super Smash Bros. Series|link}} {{Plural|SSBfWU|Stage|link}} in {{SSB4|Wii U}}.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
"{{Term|SSBU|The Great Sea / Menu Select}}" plays in any {{Term|SSBU|Stage}} related to {{TLoZ|-}} series in {{SSBU}}.
