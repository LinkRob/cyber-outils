{{Term/Store|Electro Elixir|Electro Elixirs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Elixir}}<br/>Grants a low/mid/high-level resistance to electricity. Useful against enemies with electrical attacks.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can drink Electro Elixirs to gain one level of {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They're known to be useful against electrical attacks.

Before parting at {{Term|BotW|Inogo Bridge|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}} will gift Link an Electro Elixir to show his faith in him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! That's right! I have something that I would like to give to you! This is just a small trinket to show that I have faith in you.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> He notes that they have no effect on {{Plural|BotW|Zora|link}}, which he theorizes is due to them being made for {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am not sure why, but its effects do not seem to work for Zora. Perhaps because it was made specifically for Hylians.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

Electro Elixirs can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|BotW|Electric Darner|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Thunderwing Butterfly|link}}

By adding any of these electric ingredients, Link can make an Electro Elixir that will also heal him:

* {{Term|BotW|Voltfruit|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Zapshroom|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}}
