"{{Term/Store|Spirits in Black||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: Spirits in Black}}<br/>02/25/2020 |author= Nintendo |published= February 25, 2020 |retrieved= August 13, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/355893a4/index.html |title= Event: Spirits in Black |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Spirits in Black}}" is an event wherein a greater number of black {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits with a black motif will make an appearance on the Spirit Board!|author= Nintendo |published= February 25, 2020 |retrieved= August 13, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/355893a4/index.html|title= Event: Smash by the Sword |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary, Ace, and Advanced {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day, with the Advanced {{Term|SSBU|Spirit}} being Lotus, who was newly added to the game at the time of the event.
