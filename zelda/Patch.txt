{{Term/Store|Patch||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254}}</ref>

==Biography==
Patch is a seemingly insane man who lives on top of [[Restoration Wall]]. He runs {{Term|OoA|Patch's Crazy Cart|link}}, a "trap, err... ceremony" to repair certain items. The two items he repairs in {{OoA}} are the [[Tuni Nut]] of [[Symmetry Village]] and the [[Broken Sword]], later the [[Noble Sword]].

In his "ceremony", Link must push [[Hardhat Beetle]]s into pits in the four corners of the room. While doing this he must also make sure a cart does not run over the item being repaired. This is done by Link standing on a [[Switch]] as the cart is about to go on the track of the item. The item's reparations are completed when all the Hardhat Beetles are gone.
