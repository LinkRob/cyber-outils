"{{Term/Store|Anything but Bugs||Series, HWAoC}}" is a {{Term|HWAoC|Quest|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Anything but Bugs'''|Gallery|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|HWAoC|Anything but Bugs}}" is a {{Term|HWAoC|Quest}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must provide a stable master with five {{Plural|HWAoC|Bladed Rhino Beetle|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Rugged Rhino Beetle|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Energetic Rhino Beetle|link}} as a birthday gift for his daughter, as he dislikes bugs and can't find them himself. After she receives the gift, the merchant {{Term|HWAoC|Cheria|link}} arrives at the {{Term|HWAoC|South Akkala Stable|link}} and will sell and buy goods from the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}.
