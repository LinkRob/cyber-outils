In ''The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom'', the “Zappy Shipwreck” is a side quest that takes place on a wrecked ship located southwest of Seesyde Village. To initiate the quest, speak with the bald man at the Seesyde Village docks, who will request your assistance in clearing a jellyfish-like monster from his ship. 

'''Locating the Wrecked Ship:'''

•	From Seesyde Village, swim southwest toward the marked shipwreck in the open water.

•	Equip Zora’s Flippers to facilitate swimming, if available.

•	Be cautious of nearby sharks during your approach.

•	Use water blocks or wooden boxes to climb onto the ship’s main deck.

•	On the deck, pull the handle at the back of the ship to reveal a staircase leading into the mini-dungeon. 

'''Navigating the Mini-Dungeon:'''

•	Inside, defeat enemies and break crates to uncover doorways.

•	Navigate through water-filled rooms and underwater sections, solving puzzles to progress.

•	Eventually, you’ll encounter the jellyfish boss.

'''Boss Strategy:'''

•	The boss is accompanied by smaller jellyfish.

•	Use ranged attacks to eliminate the smaller jellyfish first.

•	Switch to Swordfighter Form to effectively attack the main boss.

•	Defeating the boss rewards you with a Piece of Heart and rupees.

After completing the mini-dungeon, return to the ship’s owner in Seesyde Village to receive a Fairy Bottle as a reward.
