'''Temper Tweelus''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Temper Tweelus|When attacked, this rage-filled rock monster moves much faster and heats up to scorching before cooling again.<ref name="Temper Tweelus">{{Cite|When attacked, this rage-filled rock monster moves much faster and heats up to scorching before cooling again.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Temper Tweeluses are a more aggressive variant of the standard [[Tweelus]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Temper Tweelus [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of three.
