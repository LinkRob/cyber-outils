{{Term/Store|Pesto|Pestos|Series, TMC|plural}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Pesto|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Pesto}}
{{Plural|TMC|Pesto}} are around the size of normal flies. They appear in two different colors with different attack patterns: {{Plural|TMC|Red Pesto|link}} attack by flying down at {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, while {{Plural|TMC|Blue Pesto|link}} attack by dropping dirt balls onto him. There are also {{Plural|TMC|Tiny Pesto|link}} that fly around {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}, though they do not deal any damage to {{Term|TMC|Link}}.

==Trivia==
* In Japanese, Pesto's name ({{Romanize|プーン|Pūn}}) is similar to the name of {{Term|Series|Boon|link}} ({{Romanize|ブーン|Būn}}), suggesting a link between the two monsters.
