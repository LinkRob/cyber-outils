'''''The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D — Official Digital Strategy Guide for 3DS''''' is a digital strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{MM3D}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The guide has the same contents as "[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D — Prima Official Game Guide]]," containing a complete walkthrough of {{MM3D}} as well as a listing of {{Plural|MM3D|Enemy|link}}, {{Plural|MM3D|Boss|link}}, and collectibles.

It is served using Prima's in-browser digital guide service and has three sample chapters containing {{Term|MM3D|Item}} lists and a walkthrough of the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Palace|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|author= Prima Games|url=https://eguides.primagames.com/games/legend-zelda-majoras-mask-3d/guides/the-legend-of-zelda-majoras-mask-3d-eguide|site=eguides.primagames.com|title=The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D Official Digital Strategy Guide for 3DS|retrieved=September 23, 2022}}</ref> Prior to April 1, 2021, the guide was available for 20 gold points as a [[My Nintendo Rewards|My Nintendo Reward]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= September 23, 2022|url= https://my.nintendo.com/rewards/cbe83e596213aadb|title= Online Access - The Legend of Zelda: The Legend of Zelda Majora's Mask 3D eGuide|site= my.nintendo.com|type= }}</ref>
