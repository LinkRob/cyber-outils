==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}} is an [[Armor]] set consisting of the [[Gerudo Veil]], [[Gerudo Top]], and [[Gerudo Sirwal]]. The set can be purchased from {{Term|BotW|Vilia|link}} on top of the [[Kara Kara Bazaar]] for 600 [[Rupee]]s as part of the "[[Forbidden City Entry]]" [[Main Quest]]. Afterwards, all three pieces can be bought in [[Gerudo Town]] for 540 [[Rupee]]s total. Wearing the set provides [[Link]] with level 1 heat resistance and a total of 3 defense. In addition, wearing the set will disguise [[Link]] as a woman and allow him entry into [[Gerudo Town]]. It cannot be upgraded.
