==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Large Quiver}} is an upgrade to the original {{Term|OoT3D|Quiver|link}} that increases its carrying capacity from 30 {{Plural|OoT3D|Arrow|link}} to 40. It can be obtained in one of the following manners:
#As an adult, shoot all 10 targets at the {{Term|OoT3D|Shooting Gallery|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}}.
#As an adult, score at least 1,500 points at the {{Term|OoT3D|Horseback Archery Range|link}}.

Whichever upgrade is acquired second will become the {{Term|OoT3D|Giant Quiver|link}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Large Quiver}} is an upgrade to the original {{Term|MM3D|Quiver|link}} that increases its carrying capacity from 30 {{Plural|MM3D|Arrow|link}} to 40. It can be obtained in one of the following manners:
#Score 40 points at the {{Term|MM3D|Shooting Gallery|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}}.
#Achieve a perfect score at the {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Shooting Gallery|link}}.

Whichever upgrade is acquired second will become the {{Term|MM3D|Giant Quiver|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Large Quiver}} is acquired by completing the {{Term|TPHD|STAR Game|link}} for the first time, which requires the {{Term|TPHD|Clawshot|link}} found in the {{Term|TPHD|Lakebed Temple|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Large Quiver}} increases the {{Term|TPHD|Quiver}}'s capacity from 30 {{Plural|TPHD|Arrow|link}} to 60.

==={{PH|-}}===
After {{Term|PH|Link}} obtains the {{Term|PH|Bow|link}} in {{PH}}, his {{Term|PH|Arrow}} capacity can be upgraded twice by collecting two {{Plural|PH|Quiver}} in the following manners:
#Purchase at {{Term|PH|Molida's Store|link}} or {{Term|PH|Mercay Island Shop|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}.
#Score between 1,700 and 1,999 points at the {{Term|PH|Shooting Gallery|link}} on {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}.
The second of these {{Plural|PH|Quiver}} to be collected, though called "Quiver" in-game, is classified by {{E}} as a type of {{Term|Series|Large Quiver}} and allows {{Term|PH|Link}} to carry up to 50 {{Plural|PH|Arrow}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
After {{Term|ST|Link}} obtains the {{Term|ST|Bow|link}} in {{ST}}, his {{Term|ST|Arrow}} capacity can be upgraded twice by collecting two {{Plural|ST|Quiver}} in the following manners:
#Purchase at the {{Term|PH|Goron Country Store|link}} for 2,000 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}}.
#Score between 3,000 and 3,999 points at the {{Term|ST|Pirate Hideout|link}}.
The second of these {{Plural|ST|Quiver}} to be collected, though called "Quiver" in-game, is classified by {{E}} as a type of {{Term|Series|Large Quiver}} and allows {{Term|ST|Link}} to carry up to 50 {{Plural|ST|Arrow}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, a {{Term|SSHD|Large Quiver}} is a supplemental {{Term|SSHD|Quiver}} that increases {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}}'s {{Term|SSHD|Arrow|link}}-holding capacity by 15, as long as the {{Term|SSHD|Large Quiver}} is stored in the {{Term|SSHD|Adventure Pouch|link}}.

A {{Term|SSHD|Large Quiver}} can be created by [[Upgrading]] a {{Term|SSHD|Medium Quiver|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Scrap Shop|link}}, at a cost of two {{Plural|SSHD|Monster Horn|link}}, three {{Plural|SSHD|Dusk Relic|link}}, one {{Term|SSHD|Golden Skull|link}}, one {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Plume|link}}, and 100 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}}. They can also be sold at the {{Term|SSHD|Item Check|link}} for 150 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee}}.
