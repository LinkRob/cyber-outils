The '''Rabella Wetlands Skyview Tower''' is a location in {{TotK}}. The tower is covered up in Thorn Bushes, and it is always raining here.

== Activation ==
To enter the tower and activate it, {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} must use the [[Zonai Devices|building materials]] nearby to cover up the thornbushes from the rain. Link must then throw either [[Ruby]], [[Red Chuchu Jelly]], [[Fire Fruit]], or any other item that emits fire to clear the bushes and enter the tower to activate it.

== Map Addition ==
{{Term|TotK|Link|link}} can now launch out of this [[Skyview Tower]] to cue a Short Cutscene and to give Link a new section of the [[Surface]] and [[Sky]] map.
