The {{Term/Store|Coral Earring||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an item in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Coral Earring is a piece of jewelry given to [[Ralis]] by his mother, [[Rutela]]. When [[Link]] shows [[Ashei's Sketch]] of the [[Yeto|beast]] from [[Snowpeak]] to Ralis in the [[Kakariko Village]] [[Graveyard]], he instantly recognizes the red fish that the beast is holding. Ralis tells Link that the [[Reekfish]] feeds on a certain type of coral found only in [[Zora's Domain]], from which his Earring is made. He then gives the hook-shaped Earring to Link, which replaces the standard hook on his [[Fishing Rod]]. The Coral Earing can then be used to catch a Reekfish to smell its [[Reekfish Scent|Scent]] as [[Wolf Link]].
