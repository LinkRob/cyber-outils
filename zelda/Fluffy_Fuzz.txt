{{Term/Store|Fluffy Fuzz|Fluffy Fuzzes|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 123"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Fluffy Fuzz}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Ice Cavern|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Frozen Plateau|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Fluffy Fuzz}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Frozen Plateau}}''', ''"Don't get hit by snowballs!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Fluffy Fuzz}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Snowball Ravine|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Fluffy Fuzz}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 250 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Fluffy Fuzz}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Cozy Parka|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Tingle Tights|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 123"/>
