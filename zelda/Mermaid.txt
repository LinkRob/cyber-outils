The {{Term/Store|Mermaid||Series, LANS}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mermaid|N/A|LANS}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Mermaid}} was referred to as {{Term/Store|Martha||LA, LADX}} in some official material prior to {{LA|NS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|publisher= Piggyback|page= 351}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{LA|NS|-}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
[[File:LADX Martha Swimming Sprite.png|left]]
The Mermaid, named '''Martha''', inhabits {{Term|LANS|Martha's Bay|link}} on {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}. {{Term|LANS|Schule Donavitch|link}} made a statue of her called "{{Term|LANS|The Mourning Mermaid|link}}",<ref>{{Cite|An artist once asked me to pose for him, and he wanted a scale, too... Can the legend of the Magnifying Lens be true...?|Martha|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|THE MOURNING MERMAID By SCHULE ? ...A scale is missing...|Mermaid Statue|LADX}}</ref> which is placed near {{Term|LANS|Martha's Bay}}, although it is not complete.<ref>{{Cite|Ya, I am Schule Donavitch! Zee mermaid statue by zee bay iz my masterpiece! ... To tell you zee truth, zis werk iz not complete! Zee art, it'z... difficult for you to grasp, iz it not?|Schule Donavitch|LADX}}</ref> She asks [[Link]] to retrieve her lost {{Term|LANS|Necklace|link}} that was taken away by the waves.<ref>{{Cite|When I was swimming in the bay, the waves took a very important necklace from around my neck! If you find it, I will let you take a scale from my tail!|Martha|LADX}}</ref> When Link returns her the {{Term|LANS|Necklace}}, the {{Term|LANS|Mermaid}} rewards him with one of her {{Plural|LANS|Scale|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh! That's it! That's my {{Icon|LANS Necklace}}{{Color|LANS Pink|necklace}}! Give it! Give it back! I will give you a {{Icon|LANS Scale}}{{Color|LANS Pink|scale}} as I said!|Mermaid|LANS}}</ref> The young hero can place the {{Term|LANS|Scale}} on "{{Term|LANS|The Mourning Mermaid}}" statue to reveal a hidden passage where he can find the {{Term|LANS|Magnifying Lens|link}}.

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese, French, and German versions of the ''Link's Awakening'' and its ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|DX]]'' edition, [[Link]] instead has to find Martha's lost bikini top (called Pink Bra in-game).<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Simply put, she originally lost her top in the Japanese version, which is why she looks and acts so flustered. This is also why she's so quick to flee when Link tries to take a peek while diving.|author= |published= |retrieved= |url= http://artemis251.fobby.net/zelda/unused.php|title= Unused Parts|site= Artemis251's Link's Awakening Cache|type=}}</ref> This caused [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda series|controversy]], as it was most likely deemed too racy and was therefore changed in the North American version. In the original Japanese release, if Link attempts to dive near her while topless, she exclaims "キャーッ！  エッチ！  エッチー!", which translates to "''Kyaaah! Naughty! Naughty!''", indeed suggesting she was nude. In the [[Nintendo Switch]] [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], she loses her Necklace regardless of region.
**The [[Hippo Model]] also has a similar case in-game.
