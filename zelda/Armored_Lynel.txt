The '''Armored Lynel''' is a variant of [[Lynel]] in {{TotK}}. Their armor is comprised entirely of rock and can be destroyed with a [[hammer]], large weapon, arrow with a bulk attachment, or explosive. All variations of the Lynel can be armored. Armored Lynels attack in the same way as their regular counterparts.
