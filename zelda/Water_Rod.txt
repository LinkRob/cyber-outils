The {{Term/Store|Water Rod|Water Rods|Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a Water Rod! Summon water pillars with {{Icon|TFH Y}}. A splashing good time!|N/A|TFH}}</ref>

==Locations and Uses==
The {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}} allows the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} to create pillars of water that can be used as platforms, regardless of whether or not water is present. A water pillar equates to the same height as a full {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}}, and spouts for a short time before disappearing. These pillars can stun most enemies and eject aquatic enemies, such as {{Plural|TFH|Octorok|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Skullfish|link}}, out of the water. The item is also used to traverse expanses of water that cannot be crossed by swimming, and to reach heights that are too high for a normal {{Term|TFH|Totem}}, or alternately, to climb to higher platforms by elevating all three {{Plural|TFH|Links}} at once instead of leaving one behind to throw the other two. In the {{Term|TFH|Abyss of Agony|link}}, the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}}'s pillars can be used to turn water wheels in the Level's third Stage.

{{Plural|TFH|Water Rod}} are introduced in {{Term|TFH|Secret Fortress|link}}, in which all three {{Plural|TFH|Links}} acquire one. The item reappears in Abyss of Agony (two {{Plural|TFH|Water Rod|display=Rods}}), {{Term|TFH|Water Temple|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Infinity Dunes|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Dragon Citadel|link}}, and {{Term|TFH|Sky Temple|link}} (one {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|display=Rod}} per Stage). The {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}} also appears in the {{Term|TFH|Flooded Zone|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Desert Zone|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Baneful Zone|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}, as well as the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}}'s Riverside and The Ruins arenas.

The effects of the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}} are improved while wearing the {{Term|TFH|Torrent Robe|link}}. This greatly expands the radius of the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}}'s pillars.
