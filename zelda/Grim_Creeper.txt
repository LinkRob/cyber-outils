The {{Term/Store|Grim Creeper||Series, LA, LADX}} is a {{Term|LA|Sub-Boss}} and character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 183 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The {{Term|LA|Grim Creeper}} appears to be a humanoid with a skull for head, and a fondness for bats. {{Term|LA|Link|link}} encounters him midway through the {{Term|LA|Eagle's Tower|link}} {{Term|LA|Dungeon}} of {{LA}}. After a short introduction, {{Term|LA|Grim Creeper}} proceeds to use a {{Term|LA|Flute|link}} to call six {{Plural|LA|Battle Bat|link}} to attack {{Term|LA|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey runt! You think you can take me?! All right boys, get this punk out of my face!|Grim Creeper|LANS}}</ref> To defeat them, {{Term|LA|Link}} must kill the {{Plural|LA|Keese}} before they leave the screen. If he misses any {{Plural|LA|Keese}}, he has to try again. Any weapon will do, but the {{Term|LA|Sword}} is by far the easiest to use. Once all the {{Plural|LA|Battle Bat}} are destroyed in one blow, {{Term|LA|Grim Creeper}} flees, but not before vowing revenge for his beloved bats.<ref>{{Cite|You dirty rat! You k-k-k...beat my brothers! You'll pay!! I'll never forget you!|Grim Creeper|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You lousy so-and-so! Those creeps were some of my best friends! You'll pay! I'll never forget you!|Grim Creeper|LANS}}</ref> He lives up to his word in the final {{Term|LA|Boss}} battle in {{Term|LA|Eagle's Tower}}, where he sends away his remaining bats, and uses his {{Term|LA|Flute}} to summon {{Term|LA|Evil Eagle|link}} as a mount.<ref>{{Cite|BAH! I'm not going to hold back! I'm going to make you wish you were never born!!|Grim Creeper|LANS}}</ref> Upon the defeat of the {{Term|LA|Evil Eagle}}, the {{Term|LA|Grim Creeper}} uses his last breath to warn that all, {{Term|LA|Link}} included, will be lost if the {{Term|LA|Wind Fish|link}}'s wakes, as they're all in his dream.<ref>{{Cite|My energy... gone...I...lost! But you will be lost too, if the {{Color|LANS Pink|Wind Fish}} wakes! Same as me... You...are...in... his...dream...|Grim Creeper|LANS}}</ref>
