{{Term/Store|Bayge||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bayge|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Bayge}} is the eldest of the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers|link}},<ref name="Bro">{{Cite|If ya cleared my older bro's training... you must be pretty impressive!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> a group consisting of himself and his brothers, {{Term|BotW|Heehl|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Kabetta|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} and his brothers are passionate about training in difficult feats of strength and endurance.  He lives with his brothers in a house in southern {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, which is now abandoned and left with a {{Term|BotW|Sign|link}} saying they have gone to {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock|link}} to train.<ref>{{Cite|We are currently training at Gut Check Rock north of Death Mountain. Come watch us train at Gut Check Rock!<br/>- Bayge, Heehl, and Kabetta|Signpost|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Bayge}} is involved in two {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. One is the "{{Term|BotW|Test of Will}}" at {{Term|BotW|Mount Nabooru|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} and the other {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} have traveled to their ancestral training ground to train themselves to withstand the rising heat of {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is our ancestral training ground! Us three Goron Blood Brothers are in the midst of a {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's too hot to handle in Goron City...so we came here to train ourselves...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} and the others challenge {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to join them by sitting in a ring of fire to see who can withstand the longest.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, I have an idea!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Come join us!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yeah! Come try the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}} yourself...brother!|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} joins them in the ring, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will start the contest of endurance.<ref>{{Cite|OK... Now prepare yourself for the... {{Color|BotW Red|CONTEST OF ENDURANCE}}!! GOOOOOO!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} claims that it is easy, he is the last of the three to collapse.<ref>{{Cite|Piece of...cake!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, he declares {{Term|BotW|Link}} the winner.<ref>{{Cite|You're amazing! I never woulda thought the three Goron Blood Brothers could lose to you, brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

The brothers then challenge {{Term|BotW|Link}} to a harder contest, which would require him to stand on an even hotter platform.<ref>{{Cite|In fact, I'm grantin' you the highest honor—the chance to participate in the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of ULTIMATE endurance}}!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That special training area is even harder than the last ring...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will catch on {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} if he looses his focus.<ref>{{Cite|If ya lose your focus, {{Color|BotW Red|your body will catch fire}}! Make sure you're prepared for this, brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> However, he also comments that clearing this challenge is said to reveal an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's said that passin' this test will reveal a {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}...or some such!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> During this challenge, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will notice if {{Term|BotW|Link}} is wearing {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Set|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Desert Voe Set|link}}, and he refuses to start until he takes them off.<ref>{{Cite|HUH?! Is that heat-resistant gear ya have there, brother? A real Goron mane shouldn't have to rely on wussy stuff like that! Goron up and take the challenge without any help!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

After enough time passes, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will stop the contest to declare that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has beaten it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|RAAAAARRRGGGGHHH!! SO EPIC, BROTHER!!}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Once this is done, {{Term|BotW|Joloo Nah Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground nearby. {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} praises {{Term|BotW|Link}} for being able to overcome the challenge, and the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|You won the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of ultimate endurance}}, little man! You're amazing!}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Bayge}} is also involved in "{{Term|BotW|The Gut Check Challenge|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} at {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}} in northern {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} is found sitting on the steps to {{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr Shrine|link}} atop tits pinnacle. He refuses to let {{Term|BotW|Link}} approach, as the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine's}} platform is a sacred place to the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}}.<ref>{{Cite|I AIN'T MOVIN' A MUSCLE, BROTHER! Not a single, well-defined muscle! This is a special platform! Sacred even! Only real Goron men, as decided by us three Goron Blood Brothers, are allowed.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} decides to let {{Term|BotW|Link}} through if he can clear their training exercise, the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge|link}}, wherein {{Term|BotW|Link}} must climb the pinnacle while collecting enough {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But I gotta say...ya do seem like a real man of action. Almost like you have a Goron air about ya. I like it! SO WHAT ARE YA WAITIN' FOR, BROTHER?! GET TRAININ'! Yeah that's it! {{Color|BotW Red|If you can clear our hard-core training program, I'll move outta your way}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Upon clearing the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge|display=Challenge}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} happily appoints {{Term|BotW|Link}} as an honorary {{Term|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} and allows him to approach the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|UNBELIEVABLE!! You've passed our training with the grit of a true Goron, my brother! In fact...you've earned the right to call yourself...the fourth Goron Blood Brother!! As promised, you are now allowed to stand on this sacred platform of Goron manliness. A real honor, I know. You're officially one of us now! Our true Goron brother, brother! Welcome to the family!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will then offer a much harder course called the Super Gut Check Challenge.<ref>{{Cite|Lucky for you, I've prepared a training program to help you ascend to even greater heights! I call it... Super Gut Check Challenge!!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

When {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack is active and {{Term|BotW|Link}} is pursuing the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} can be found at {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already completed "{{Term|BotW|The Gut Check Challenge}}", {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} recognizes him as their fourth brother.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! It's the fourth Goron Blood Brother! The one who beat both the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}} and our {{Color|BotW Red|Gut Check Challenge}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He explains that {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} used to train at this spot, and while he does not know why, he believes it was part of his training as a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Do ya know why {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}} chose this place to train, brother? ...Neither do we! But us Goron Blood Brothers are gonna find out by completing the training ourselves! I'm sure this is some part of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}}'s secret Goron champ training. Intense!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} are training at this spot to become mighty {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}} like {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}, claiming it is their only path.<ref>{{Cite|We're gonna do all we can to be mighty Gorons just like {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}}! It's the only possible path for the Goron Blood Brothers!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} points out the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} and directs Link to stand on it.<ref>{{Cite|You see that {{Color|BotW Red|glowing ring}} in the lava? Think of it as a target, and... {{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|stand on the lava, brother}}!}} {{Small|So sweaty... Brother...}} Even Gorons can't walk on lava... I wonder if {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}} really did this training... Not that I doubt him, brother!|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} points out that choosing this spot makes {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} hard-core.<ref>{{Cite|No wonder {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}} chose this spot. It's hard-core, brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Due to the intensity of this training, he decides to take the time to strategize with the other {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}}.<ref>{{Cite|This training is gonna be rough. I can almost taste the gains! Time to strategize, brothers!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him after, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} says {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} would howl while standing on {{Term|BotW|Lava}} to sharpen his skills.<ref>{{Cite|Some say {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}} would howl while {{Color|BotW Red|standing on lava}}. It somehow helped sharpen his skills.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Bayge}} notices when {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the training due to the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}'s disappearance.<ref>{{Cite|What happened here?! I look away for one blasted second, and the {{Color|BotW Red|glowing ring}} disappears!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He is disappointed that he can no longer attempt the training.<ref>{{Cite|Now I can't try that epic training! Woe is me, brother.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} will return to {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}}.

==Trivia==
* {{Term|Series|Bayge}} is one of several characters that will hit {{Term|Series|Link}} back if he attacks them with a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}. Uniquely, he only does this on {{Term|Series|Mount Nabooru}}, as he will react with fear if {{Term|Series|Link}} attacks him on {{Term|Series|Gut Check Rock}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hold it!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Aaaah!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> These attacks deal no damage.
