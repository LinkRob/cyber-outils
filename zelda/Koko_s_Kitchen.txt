"{{Term/Store|Koko's Kitchen||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Koko's Kitchen}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Kakariko Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Koko|link}} while she is standing at one of the {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Koko}} shares her plan to make {{Term|BotW|Veggie Cream Soup|link}}, as she has heard it is nourishing and fortifying.<ref>{{Cite|Today's dinner is {{Color|BotW Blue|veggie cream soup}}! It's super nourishing and fortifying! Well, that's what they say, anyhow. It must be true!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> She shares the recipe, which is stewing a {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Fresh Milk|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}.<ref>{{Cite|All you do is toss a {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrot}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|rock salt}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|fresh milk}} in a pot and stew it up. Then, voilà! So yummy!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} panics when she realizes that she does not have any {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|...OH, NO!}} Koko is all out of {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrots}}!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> She becomes sad, recalling that she promised {{Term|BotW|Hasty Veggie Cream Soup|link}} to her sister, {{Term|BotW|Cottla|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But...Koko promised to make Cottla some {{Color|BotW Blue|hasty veggie cream soup}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can offer to help {{Term|BotW|Koko}} by fetching some {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot}}, which she will be grateful for.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll bring you some.] Thank you! So much!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Koko}} says that she only needs one and she will wait for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to get it.<ref>{{Cite|OK, Koko just needs one {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrot}}! Just one and Koko can get cookin'! Koko will be waiting for you here!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Once night comes, though, she will need to go home.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Though, once it gets dark out, Koko will have to head home...}}|Koko|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes too long, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} will go home for the night and he will have to return during the daytime when she goes back to the kitchen. Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuse to help her or withholds {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot}}, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} will call him cold.<ref>{{Cite|Oooh, that's cold...|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

The nearby {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}}, {{Term|BotW|High Spirits Produce|link}}, sells {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot}}. Several {{Plural|BotW|Merchant|link}} in the area also offer {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot}}.

{{Term|BotW|Koko}} continues to chastise herself for being so forgetful and begins to think she is a bad sister who can't look after {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} the way their mother could.<ref>{{Cite|Koko promised to make Cottla some {{Color|BotW Blue|hasty veggie cream soup}}... But silly Koko forgot the {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrots}}! Koko is supposed to take Mother's place for Cottla... Koko is a bad big sister...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has a {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} will be able to tell smell them.<ref>{{Cite|...Huh? {{Big|HUH?!}} Koko smells {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrots}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} hands one over, she happily thanks him and begins to cook.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Is that...a {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrot}}?!}} Thank you so much! So very much! Now Koko can make dinner for Cottla... Yeah! Time to get cookin'!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

As soon as dinner is finished, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} will give some of her {{Term|BotW|Hasty Veggie Cream Soup}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}} to reward him for his help, ending the Quest.<ref>{{Cite|Ooooh, it came out perfect! Here, please have some! Koko's treat!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> She goes on to say that she intends to keep experimenting in the kitchen to become an excellent cook like their mother.<ref>{{Cite|Koko wants to try cooking different things so Koko can be a great chef, just like Mother!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>
