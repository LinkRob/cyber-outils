The '''Cherry Tree Family''' is a cherry tree family appearing in {{FPTRR}}.

== Location ==
The Cherry Tree Family can be found in the [[Deku Forest (First Continent)|Deku Forest]] in the First Continent. It can be found in the north-western part of the map.

== Story ==
The Cherry Tree family plays a part in the story of [[Aba]]. It is her favorite spot, and this is where she can be found after she discovers [[Junglo]] is not her real father. The Cherry Tree Family is also an object that can be drawn on the map for a reward from the Map-Seller.
