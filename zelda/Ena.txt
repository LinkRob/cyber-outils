{{Term/Store|Ena||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ena|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Ena}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} child who lives at {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}. Throughout the day, {{Term|BotW|Ena}} follows {{Term|BotW|Banji|link}} around and assists her with chores. In the morning, they tend to the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|display=Stable's}} {{Plural|BotW|White Goat|link}}, where she encourages one of the {{Term|BotW|White Goat|display=Goats}}, Mr. Goat, to eat.<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Goat, Mr. Goat! Eat that grass, eat that grass! Fill up on that go-go grass, and then dash super go-go fast! Get it in your guts! And run like nuts! With a healthy! Heaping! Helping!|Ena|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Ena}} helps {{Term|BotW|Banji}} with the cargo,<ref>{{Cite|Car-go! Car-go! Helping out!|Ena|BotW}}</ref> she wants to know if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} keeps his own cargo in his pouch.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, mister. Do you put your cargo in your pouch?|Ena|BotW}}</ref> She then informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} of a rumor that the {{Term|BotW|Korok|link|display=forest people}} can expand one's {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} and asks if he has heard of this.<ref>{{Cite|Didja hear the rumor about how the forest people can give you a bigger inventory? Didja?|Ena|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has heard of it, {{Term|BotW|Ena}} declares the two of them to be friends, and she shares that people can collect the {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link|display=things}} forest children leave behind.<ref>{{Cite|You heard? Yaaaay! Now we're friends! Now we're friends! I hear you can collect things the forest children drop! What do you think their droppings look like?|Ena|BotW}}</ref> On the contrary, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} denies hearing the rumor, {{Term|BotW|Ena}} claims to have heard it from a {{Term|BotW|Fairy|link}} that told her to be nice to a {{Term|BotW|Hestu|link|display=big forest child}} to receive a bigger {{Term|BotW|Inventory}}.<ref>{{Cite|You haven't heard?! I heard about it from a fairy! He said, "If you're nice to a really big forest kid, he'll make your inventory bigger!"|Ena|BotW}}</ref> Despite not knowing what an {{Term|BotW|Inventory}} is, {{Term|BotW|Ena}} believes that bigger is always better.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know what an inventory is, but bigger is always better if you ask me!|Ena|BotW}}</ref> She wonders if the big forest kid ever leaves the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link|display=forest}}.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder if the big forest kid ever leaves the forest...|Ena|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Ena}} sings songs as she takes care of {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}}, one about {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} and one about poop.<ref>{{Cite|♪Horsey! Horsey! I'll make you a BED!♪<br/>Horsey! Horsey! Rest your head!♪|Ena|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|♪Poopy poop pooooop!♪|Ena|BotW}}</ref> She learned the song about {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} from a man who came through the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|display=Stable}} with a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} that she thought was cool.<ref>{{Cite|I heard that song from some guy who came by here riding a REALLY cool-looking horse!|Ena|BotW}}</ref> He also told her about a secret tunnel in the western part of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} that leads inside.<ref>{{Cite|He also said that there's a small tunnel just west of the castle entrance. And that if you go through that tunnel, you can get INSIDE the castle!|Ena|BotW}}</ref> However, the man warned her against going due to the danger of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}, which she relays to {{Term|BotW|Link}} as well.<ref>{{Cite|But the man also said not to go near the castle because it's dangerous. That goes for you too, mister!|Ena|BotW}}</ref> Despite the speed of his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Ena}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to avoid the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}.<ref>{{Cite|I heard there's a small tunnel west of the castle entrance. But no matter how fast your horse is, you shouldn't go there because it's too dangerous!|Ena|BotW}}</ref>

During the {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ena}} flees inside the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|display=Stable}}, where she sits at the end of one of the {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Kenyo|link}}. She considers bringing Mr. Goat inside.<ref>{{Cite|Rain, rain, drippity drip... I should bring Mr. Goat inside too.|Ena|BotW}}</ref> At the end of the day, {{Term|BotW|Ena}} admits she is tired before she heads to sleep.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I helped out...<br/>I...I'm sleepy...}}|Ena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Banji}} watches {{Term|BotW|Ena}} as she sleeps in one of the {{Plural|BotW|Bed}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha Bridge Stable}}, where looking at a child's sleeping face makes me happy!|Banji|BotW}}</ref>
