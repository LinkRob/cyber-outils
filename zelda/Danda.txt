{{Term/Store|Danda||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Danda|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Danda}} is an elderly {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} who manages the {{Term|BotW|Arrow Specialty Shop|link}} {{Term|BotW|Stall|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'aaq... Are you a traveler from abroad? A Hylian tourist? As you can see, this is an {{Color|BotW Blue|arrow specialty shop}}.|Danda|BotW}}</ref> She boasts that she has the best selection of {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You won't see a selection like this anywhere else.|Danda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Danda}}'s daughter works all of the time, which means {{Term|BotW|Danda}} has to look after her grandchildren, {{Term|BotW|Kalani|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Makure|link}}.<ref name="Daughter"/><ref name="Vaba"/>

As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} shops, {{Term|BotW|Danda}} asks what he thinks about her selection of {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}}.<ref>{{Cite|What do you think? We've got some pretty nice arrows here, don't we?|Danda|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys all of one type of {{Term|BotW|Arrow}}, {{Term|BotW|Danda}} calls him strange for needing so many.<ref>{{Cite|You sure do need a lot of arrows... You're a strange little vai, aren't you?|Danda|BotW}}</ref> After buying the last of her {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}}, {{Term|BotW|Danda}} promises to restock soon.<ref>{{Cite|Sarqso... I'll be sure to restock them for you.|Danda|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to after this, she comments on how long it took her to procure all of her products.<ref>{{Cite|My goodness. It took a lot of time and effort to procure all those products...|Danda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Danda}} will remark that she has never seen her selection cleared out so completely,<ref>{{Cite|For you to buy every last arrow like that, well... I don't think I've ever seen my selection cleared out so completely.|Danda|BotW}}</ref> before she thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} and tells him that she should now be able to buy her grandchildren some new toys.<ref>{{Cite|But, thanks to you, I think I'll be able to buy my grandchildren some new toys.|Danda|BotW}}</ref>

While she does sell {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} herself, {{Term|BotW|Danda}} will also buy them from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Which item?|Danda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If there's anything else you want to sell, go ahead and show it to me...|Danda|BotW}}</ref> On occassion, she will wonder why {{Term|BotW|Link}} would sell such high quality {{Plural|BotW|Item}}.<ref>{{Cite|Such quality. Why are you selling? Nice purchase for me, but...|Danda|BotW}}</ref> She may also remark that she believes one of her grandchildren will be excited to see what she has bought.<ref>{{Cite|Sarqso... I bet my grandchild will be pretty excited about this.|Danda|BotW}}</ref>
