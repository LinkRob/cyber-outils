{{Term/Store|The Master Kohga Chronicles!||HWAoC}} is a level 57 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Master Kohga Chronicles!|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|The Master Kohga Chronicles!}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}}. When completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako Component|link}}, and twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Drumstick|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}' objective is to capture two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} within ten minutes while defending two Allied Strongholds inside {{Term|HWAoC|Akkala Citadel|link}}. Both target {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} have a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}} as their boss, although the one in the south contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}} and the one in the east contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}. As for the citadel's attackers, there are two {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}} that assault the Allied Strongholds at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}. When one of the target {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} is entered, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Guardian|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} spawn around the map and attack the strongholds. When one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} is captured, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Guardian|link}} and four {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} spawn and do the same. Once both {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.
