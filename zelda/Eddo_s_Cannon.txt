==Location and Uses==
{{Term|PH|Eddo's Cannon}} is the {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}} from the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} set of {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}. It is not initially attached to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} when {{Term|PH|Link|link}} gains access to the ship. When the young hero discovers that the route to the {{Term|PH|Northwestern Sea|link}} is blocked by {{Plural|PH|Boulder|link}}, he purchases the {{Term|PH|Cannon}} from {{Term|PH|Eddo's Garage|link}} for 50 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A cannon bought from Eddo for the high price of 50 Rupees.|Shipyard|PH}}</ref> It cannot be sold to the {{Term|PH|Treasure Teller|link}} or traded to other players via [[Tag Mode]].<ref>{{Cite|That's barely worth a mention, sir! No interest...|Treasure Teller|PH}}</ref>
