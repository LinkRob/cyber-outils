The {{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Hometown Prairie||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a location in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hometown Prairie|Map|FPTRR}}</ref> It is located on the {{Term|FPTRR|First Continent|link}}.

==Features==
After {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} leaves {{Term|FPTRR|Port Town|link}}, he runs into {{Term|FPTRR|Aba|link}}, who warns him that he is going to die.<ref>{{Cite|Hey you! Do you know something? Just now, when I sensed you coming... I got a really bad feeling... My intuition is never wrong... I have to warn you... You are going to... ...DIE!|Aba|FPTRR}}</ref> She immediately retracts this as a joke, and offers to teach {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} how to fight in her {{Term|FPTRR|Aba's Battle Class|link|display= Battle Class}}.<ref>{{Cite|Don't look so scared! I was kidding! All I meant was this is a dangerous place to be strolling round... You really should enrol in "Aba's Battle Class."|Aba|FPTRR}}</ref>

==={{Term|FPTRR|Fish Rock}}===
{{Main|Fish Rock}}
{{Term|FPTRR|Fish Rock|link}} is a statue of a {{Term|FPTRR|Fish|link}} located in the north-western part of {{Term|FPTRR|Hometown Prairie}}.<ref>{{Cite|Today I think she said something about fish... She must mean that Fish Rock, north-west of Hometown Prairie...|Chiko's Mum|FPTRR}}</ref> When {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko|link}} loses her {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Doll|link}}, she tells {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} that it was eaten by a {{Term|FPTRR|Fish}}.<ref>{{Cite|Boo hoo! A fish ate my fairy!|Chiko|FPTRR}}</ref> To get the {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Doll}} back, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} must interact with {{Term|FPTRR|Fish Rock}}, which will cause it to spit out the {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Doll}}. Once {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} delivers it back to {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko}}, the young girl will show him the entrance to the {{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yippee!! My fairy! My fairy! You found my fairy! Thank you!! I'll show you something! Come to the town hero's statue! Come on! I'll go there first! I'll wait for you there!|Chiko|FPTRR}}</ref>

{{Term|FPTRR|Fish Rock}} is one of the locations that {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can mark on his {{Term|FPTRR|Map|link}} for the {{Term|FPTRR|Map-Seller|link}}. She will reward {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} with 50 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} for doing so.

==={{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine}}===
{{Main|Hero's Shrine}}
The {{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine|link}} is the location of a monument to {{Term|FPTRR|Port Town}}'s guardian, {{Term|FPTRR|Dazzle Lyphos|link}}. The statue of {{Term|FPTRR|Dazzle Lyphos}} blocks the entrance to the {{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine}}, but {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko}} shows {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} the entrance once he returns her {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Doll}}. She claims to have found it by accident when playing.<ref>{{Cite|Hello! I was playing with my fairy here the other day... She got a bit carried away and kicked the town hero... This came loose! Go through here and you can go under the ground! The Armourer said that there was something good under the ground... What can it be?! See you later!|Chiko|FPTRR}}</ref>

==={{Term|FPTRR|Husband's Grave}}===
{{Main|Husband's Grave}}
The {{Term|FPTRR|Husband's Grave|link}} is the final resting place of the {{Term|FPTRR|Master Mapper|link}}, the late spouse of the {{Term|FPTRR|Map-Seller}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hello, young man. I am the town Map-Seller! My husband was a Master Mapper, but he passed away...|Map-Seller|FPTRR}}</ref> It is the first mapping landmark that {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can draw. It is here that {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} receives the {{Term|FPTRR|Map}} of the {{Term|FPTRR|Hometown Prairie}}, after he accepts the {{Term|FPTRR|Map-Seller}}'s request for help.<ref>{{Cite|You're just a passer-by but if you can help me, I'll reward you! Would you help me draw my map?|Map-Seller|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you, young man! As my hands shake, I can't draw properly. Here you are! The map of the Hometown Prairie!|Map-Seller|FPTRR}}</ref>

==={{Term|FPTRR|Port Town}}===
{{Main|Port Town}}
{{Term|FPTRR|Port Town}} is home to many characters, such as {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko's Mum|link}}, the {{Term|FPTRR|Armourer|link}}, the {{Term|FPTRR|Jeweller|link}} and the {{Term|FPTRR|Map-Seller}}. Before {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can talk to its denizens, he must pay all of them a small fee.{{How Many}}

==={{Term|FPTRR|Rupee Tower}}===
{{Main|Rupee Tower}}
The {{Term|FPTRR|Rupee Tower|link}} is a structure that is designed to take {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} to {{Term|FPTRR|Rupeeland|link}}, as promised by {{Term|FPTRR|Uncle Rupee|link}}. It rises from the {{Term|FPTRR|Western Pool|link}} after {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} throws a certain amount of {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}} into it.{{How Many}} {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} gains the ability to travel to new locations as the {{Term|FPTRR|Rupee Tower}} continues to rise whenever {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} throws enough {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}} into it.{{How Many}} The top of the {{Term|FPTRR|Rupee Tower}} sports a diving board, which {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} must use in conjunction with his {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle Balloon|link|display= Balloon}} to descend to these new areas.

==={{Term|FPTRR|Western Pool}}===
{{Main|Western Pool}}
{{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} is called to the {{Term|FPTRR|Western Pool|link}} by {{Term|FPTRR|Uncle Rupee}}, who promises him a more exciting life. There, {{Term|FPTRR|Uncle Rupee}} tells {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} of {{Term|FPTRR|Rupeeland}} and that to enter it, he must throw {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}} into it.<ref name="Uncle Rupee">{{Cite|Hey! Tingle! Yes, you...the 35-year old man over there... The daily grind has got you down... ...but you can escape this dull existence... If you don't want to miss this chance... ...get to the Western Pool!|Uncle Rupee|FPTRR}}</ref>

===Tingle's Home===
{{Stub|FPTRR}}
{{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} begins his journey inside his home, where he is contacted by {{Term|FPTRR|Uncle Rupee}}, who is offering middle-aged adventurers entrance into {{Term|FPTRR|Rupeeland}}, instructing him to go to the {{Term|FPTRR|Western Pool}}.<ref name="Uncle Rupee"/> {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can only save by using the terminal inside his home.<ref>{{Cite|Save your game or talk with me using this terminal.|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref>

50 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}} and an {{Term|FPTRR|Empty Jar|link}} can be found in the right part of his home.

{{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can also create {{Plural|FPTRR|Recipe|link}} from {{Plural|FPTRR|Ingredient|link}} that he finds on his journey by mixing the {{Plural|FPTRR|Ingredient}} in his {{Term|FPTRR|Pot|link}}. The {{Term|FPTRR|Recipe}} for the {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle Fireworks|link}} is obtained by talking to {{Term|FPTRR|Pinkle|link}} at the terminal.<ref>{{Cite|The firework recipe is in your rucksack. Check it for details! Got the ingredients? Then try making fireworks right away! What will happen? Tee-hee!|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref>
