{{Term/Store|Green Gargoyle|Green Gargoyles|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Green Gargoyle|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Green Gargoyles are statues that appear within {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle|link}} and the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}}. As statues, they cannot move, though they will spit fireballs at anyone who passes by, dealing damage to everyone except {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}.
