The {{Term/Store|Essence of the Triforce||Series, ALttP}} is a character in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, Link... I am the Essence Of The Triforce.|Essence of the Triforce|ALttP}}</ref>

==Biography==
At the end of the game, the voice of the Triforce comes to [[Link]] and explains to him the nature of the Triforce and what Ganon’s wishes with it were prior to his defeat. It then presents the next wish to Link, who quickly complies.
