The {{Term/Store|Bottomless Swamp||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Bottomless Swamp'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Bottomless Swamp is located in [[Hyrule Field]], along the bank of the [[Hylia River]]. The Bottomless Swamp features a hostile landscape, with various hazards such as [[Bottomless Bog]]s, {{Plural|BotW|Pool of Malice|link}}, and several primitive ''{{Wp|Cheval de frise|chevaux de frise}}''. Furthermore, several dead [[Tree]]s, large skulls, and large rib bones dot the Swamp as well. In the center of the Swamp is a giant skull that serves as a monster camp, where a number of enemies patrol around it. At the southeastern reaches of the Swamp, the charred remains of a couple buildings can be found.

[[Ore Deposit]]s can be found inside the highest of the cluster of skulls. In the southernmost skull, a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Soldier's Broadsword]] can be found submerged in a [[Bottomless Bog]]. Inside the largest of the skulls is a wooden Treasure Chest containing a [[Silver Rupee]].
