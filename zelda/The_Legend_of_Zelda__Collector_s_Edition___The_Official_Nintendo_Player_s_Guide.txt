'''''The Legend of Zelda: Collector's Edition — The Official Nintendo Player's Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Nintendo Power]] for {{CE}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 160 pages, containing concise walkthroughs of {{CE}}. It includes overworld maps, dungeon maps, and item guides for {{TLoZ}}, {{TAoL}}, {{OoT}}, {{MM}}. Each game section also includes the basic controls adapted for the [[Nintendo GameCube]] controller.

The {{OoT}} section contains additional tables for collectibles and secrets such as {{Plural|OoT|Piece of Heart|link}}, {{Plural|OoT|Gold Skulltula|link}}, and {{Plural|OoT|Bottle|link}} as well as the trading sequences for the {{Term|OoT|Biggoron's Sword|link}} and {{Term|OoT|Mask of Truth|link}}. Similarly, the {{MM}} section contains tables for {{Plural|MM|Piece of Heart}}, {{Plural|MM|Bottle}}, {{Plural|MM|Mask|link}}, and {{Term|MM|Bombers' Notebook|link}} entries.
