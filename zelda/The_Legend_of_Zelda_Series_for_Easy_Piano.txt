'''''The Legend of Zelda Series for Easy Piano''''' is a sheet music book for piano released on February 22, 2012. Containing 76 pages, the book features 33 songs from most ''The Legend of Zelda'' games released at the time, ranging from the original {{TLoZ}} to {{ST}}.
