{{Term/Store|Gaddison||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gaddison|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Gaddison}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} guard for {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} who can be found guarding the eastern bridge, wielding a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear|link}}. In her youth, {{Term|BotW|Gaddison}} was part of the Big Bad Bazz Brigade with {{Term|BotW|Bazz|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Rivan|link}}.<ref name="Rivan"/> Before the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} would play with her and her friends.<ref>{{Cite|We...used to play together all the time. That was 100 years ago. My name is Gaddison.|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Gaddison}}, she reports that the east gate to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} is clear.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, sir! East gate is all clear, sir!|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> However, she quickly recognizes {{Term|BotW|Link}}, calling him a sight for sore eyes.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Well, if it isn't Master Link! Aren't you a sight for sore eyes?|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> She realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must be the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}} brought to the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Hylian that Prince Sidon brought here... Don't tell me that was you, Master Link!|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref>

However, {{Term|BotW|Gaddison}} notes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has not changed at all in 100 years,<ref>{{Cite|And what's more...you don't seem to have changed much in the last 100 years...|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> but she dismisses it as she does not have time to chat.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, but I shouldn't be making idle chitchat just now.|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> She is supposed to watching the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake|link}} as part of her job, but she asserts that she can talk to {{Term|BotW|Link}} later.<ref>{{Cite|I'm supposed to be fulfilling my officially appointed duty by keeping watch at the east gate. I must maintain my surveillance of the {{Color|BotW Blue|East Reservoir Lake}}. We can chat later.|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gaddison}} notices the clear sky wonders whether {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} managed to stop the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Link! Please take a look. For the first time in what feels like an eternity, the skies have cleared! I wonder if Prince Sidon really managed to stop Ruta's water assault...|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> She is still wary of the water level of the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake|display=Reservoir}}, even after {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|display=Ruta}} has stopped spouting water.<ref>{{Cite|That's right. I'm supposed to keep an eye on East Reservoir Lake. Ruta was spouting water, and the lake was in danger of flooding... But now, there's been no increase in water level! Still, you'd best not let your guard down. As long as Ruta is in the reservoir...|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> She shares some of the history of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, such as {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} being its 100 years ago, and wonders if {{Term|BotW|Seggin|link}} managed to fire {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}} at it.<ref>{{Cite|Ruta is its nickname. Its full and proper name is the Divine Beast Vah Ruta. If memory serves, 100 years ago Lady Mipha rode inside of it...and used the Divine Beast to fight the Calamnity. I would have thought you'd already know that, Master Link... Hmm, but Ruta's water sprouting seems to have subsided a bit... Did Seggin manage to fire those shock arrows after all?|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Gaddison}} is seen in the crowd of {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} in the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}} who watch as {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} praises {{Term|BotW|Link}}. She is grateful for all that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has done,<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for appeasing the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}!|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> as she can return to her work and {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} can receive tourists again.<ref>{{Cite|Now I can have some peace of mind and return to my mission!|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It would be best if we started getting more tourists again...|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Gaddison}} about her name.<ref>{{Cite|[Your name...?] Master Link... Indeed, my name is Gaddison. What about it?|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells her that is looking for someone like her, she asks what he needs.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm looking for you.] It seems to me that you have something on your mind... Care to let me in?|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> However, since he is looking for someone who can act as a priest,<ref>{{Cite|Yes, yes... I see... Someone whose name ends with "son" who can also act as a priest...is what you seek?|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Gaddison}} will refuse him.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry, Master Link...|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> She considers herself a blood-stained soldier who could never be a priest,<ref>{{Cite|I am a blood-stained soldier. I could never be a priest.|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> but says that {{Term|BotW|Kapson|link}} could help him.<ref>{{Cite|But back in the domain, there is {{Color|BotW Red|Kapson}}, who was a priest in the past. {{Color|BotW Red|Kapson}} should be in the {{Color|BotW Red|sleeping chamber}}. Why don't you go visit him?|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} has left, {{Term|BotW|Gaddison}} will inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he departed for {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that reminds me! The Zora whose name ends in "son," just like mine does, has left the domain. His name is Kapson. I believe he said he was heading to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} region.|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> She suggests a shortcut to {{Term|BotW|Akkala}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, telling him to climb the {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Mikau Lake|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lulu Lake|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Just climb the waterfalls at {{Color|BotW Blue|Mikau Lake}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|Lulu Lake}}. That will offer you a pretty decent shortcut to {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}}.|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref> However, she is concerned for {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} to be crossing the mountains at his age, though his good health puts her at ease.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm, but for him to be crossing the mountains at his age... Good gracious! I'm glad he's in good health, at least.|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref>
