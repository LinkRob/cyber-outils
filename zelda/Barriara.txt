==Biography==
{{Term|CoH|Barriara|link}} is the leader of the {{Term|CoH|Gerudo|link}} in {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Village|link}}.<ref name="Introduction"/> While standing outside of young {{Term|CoH|Ganondorf|link}}'s home, she was cursed into a deep sleep by {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I was caught off guard here by a strange man, Octavius? Octavoo? I'd love to teach that guy a lesson!|Barriara|CoH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}} played a song on his lute right before {{Color|CoH Blue|Barriara}} fell asleep. Maybe we could wake her up with a different song?|Gerudo|CoH}}</ref> causing her body to block the way into the prince's house and preventing the rest of the {{Term|CoH|Gerudo}} from checking in on him.<ref>{{Cite|The prince is inside, but we can't check on him while {{Color|CoH Blue|Barriara}} is still asleep... The boss is usually awake before any of us. We're not sure what happened to her.|Gerudo|CoH}}</ref>

She can be awoken by stepping on the music note-shaped buttons on the ground below her in order to wake her up with a song.<ref>{{Cite|Did you see that? The {{Color|CoH Blue|buttons}} on the ground lit up when each {{Color|CoH Blue|music note}} was played! What will happen if you recreate the tune?|Trill|CoH}}</ref> While the exact arrangement of buttons will differ per game file, the colors of the solution and the tune will always remain the same. Therefore, the sequence will always be dark green, yellow, light blue, blue, dark green, yellow, light blue, and blue, which plays the "{{Term|CoH|Gerudo Valley (Theme)|link}}" theme.

After being woken up by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}, she gives them the {{Term|CoH|Power Glove|link}} and moves out of the way.{{Note|In {{Term|CoH|Permadeath Mode|link}}, the {{Term|CoH|Power Glove|link}} is replaced with a random item.}} She also informs the heroes that there's a {{Term|CoH|Sandstone Talus|link}} guarding the entrance to {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Ruins|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Speaking of problems, there's a golem attacking anyone that goes near the Gerudo Ruins.|Barriara|CoH}}</ref> but regrettably can't help them defeat it because she has to stay in the village to watch over the young prince.<ref name="Mature">{{Cite|Unfortunately, I cannot travel to the ruins with you. I must watch over our prince until he's a little more...mature.|Barriara|CoH}}</ref> Despite her duty to watch over him, she considers the prince to be immature,<ref name="Mature"/> and likewise, {{Term|CoH|Ganondorf|link}} considers her to be foolish, indicating a strained relationship between the two.<ref>{{Cite|Hmph... I guess that fool {{Color|CoH Blue|Barriara}} finally got out of the way.|Ganondorf|CoH}}</ref>
