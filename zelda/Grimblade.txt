{{Term/Store|Grimblade||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Grimblade}} is the third eldest member of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}. He lives in a cave on southeast side of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden|link}}, and teaches {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} the {{Term|TMC|Sword Beam|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You will learn the meaning of power with this technique, the {{Color|TMC Red|Sword Beam}}!|Grimblade|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Grimblade}} will not train {{Term|TMC|Link}} unless he lights the two {{Plural|TMC|Torchlight|link}} in the room with the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But the room is dark, and I cannot see your face! We cannot train like this!!|Grimblade|TMC}}</ref> A {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} can be found inside his dojo.

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Grimblade}} to open a waterfall at {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}, revealing {{Term|TMC|Splitblade|link}}'s dojo.
