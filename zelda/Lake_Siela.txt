{{Term/Store|Lake Siela||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lake Siela'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Lake Siela is located in [[West Necluda]], northeast of [[Dueling Peaks]]. It is fed by a waterfall that starts at the base of the [[Phalian Highlands]], and passes under the [[Kakariko Bridge]]. A [[Raft]] sits at the bank on the northeastern shore near the base of the waterfall. A metal crate containing [[Arrow]]s and [[Rupee]]s sits atop the rocks protruding from Lake Siela east of Kakariko Bridge.

[[Link]] can use [[Magnesis]] near the Raft to retrieve a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing 10 [[Arrow]]s at the bottom of the river. A second metallic Treasure Chest containing an [[Opal]] can be found underwater on the eastern side of the Kakariko Bridge, near the boulders protruding from the Lake.

In the northwestern corner of the Lake, there is a small island with several [[Rock]]s on it, and to the south of this island is a circle of rocky pillars. If Link throws a Rock into this circle, a [[Korok]] will appear. Northwest of the small island there is a circle of [[Lily Pad]]s, and jumping off of a nearby cliff into this circle will cause another [[Korok]] to appear.
