{{Term/Store|Buzz|Buzz|Series|plural}},{{Exp Game|Series}}<ref name="E 170">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 170 {{Exp Game|Series, ALttP}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Bazu|Bazu|ALttP|plural}},{{Exp Game|ALttP}}<ref name="E 170"/> are giant grasshopper enemies in {{ALttP}}.

==Characteristics==
Buzz live at the top floor of the dungeon in the [[Village of Outcasts]], scurrying around the corners of the rooms and darting in and out of the sunlight. Their behavior is very similar to a [[Rat]], but they are significantly stronger, able to do two Hearts worth of damage rather than a mere quarter, and they can resist multiple Sword blows. Due to their fast speed and large numbers, they can be very dangerous to a weaker adventurer despite their size. They appear alongside [[Skullrope]] and [[Chasupa]].
