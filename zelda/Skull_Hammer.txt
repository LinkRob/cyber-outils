|comparable=[[Hammer]]<br/>[[Megaton Hammer]]|game_debut={{TWW}}}}The {{Term/Store|Skull Hammer||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an item in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer}} is obtained after defeating {{Term|TWWHD|Phantom Ganon|link}} in the {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}}. It is used to push down {{Plural|TWWHD|Switch|link}} in order to clear paths for {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} to advance. It is necessary to fully traverse the {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress}}, which is scattered with peg-shaped {{Plural|TWWHD|Switch}}. The {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer}} is also necessary to defeat the {{Term|TWWHD|Helmaroc King|link}}, as only it can break the boss's mask.

When used, the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer}} will produce a shock wave, which can stun nearby enemies. It can be used much like a {{Term|TWWHD|Sword|link}}, as {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} is able to swing it both horizontally and vertically to damage enemies. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can also perform {{Plural|TWWHD|Jump Attack|link}} and {{Plural|TWWHD|Spin Attack|link}} while wielding the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer}}. However, he cannot use a {{Term|TWWHD|Shield|link}} as it is a double-handed weapon. If {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} hits a {{Term|TWWHD|Miniblin|link}} with a vertical attack, it will be flattened. If he hits with a horizontal attack, the {{Term|TWWHD|Miniblin}} will fly a long distance in the air and explode. If he hits the ground near a {{Term|TWWHD|Miniblin}}, it will fly high in the air, coming down after a few seconds. The {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer}} can also defeat frozen enemies and the heads of {{Plural|TWWHD|Stalfos}} in a single swing.

The {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer}} is required to access the {{Plural|TWWHD|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Thorned Fairy Island|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Western Fairy Island|link}}.
