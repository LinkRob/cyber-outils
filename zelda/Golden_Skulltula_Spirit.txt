==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Plural|OoT3D|Token}} appear when a {{Term|OoT3D|Gold Skulltula|link}} is defeated in {{OoT}}. Some can be collected by just walking up to them, but some require ranged weapons to be reached. By collecting {{Plural|OoT3D|Token}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} can lift the curse of the {{Term|OoT3D|Cursed Rich Man|link}} and his sons. Each time a son is freed from the curse, they will reward {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} with an item. Once all 100 {{Plural|OoT3D|Token}} are collected, the {{Term|OoT3D|Cursed Rich Man}} will give {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} a {{Term|OoT3D|Gold Rupee|link}} each time he visits him.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Plural|MM3D|Golden Skulltula Spirit}} appear when a {{Term|MM3D|Gold Skulltula|link}} is defeated in {{MM}}. By collecting the 30 {{Plural|MM3D|Golden Skulltula Spirit|display=Spirits}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Spider House|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} can obtain the {{Term|MM3D|Mask of Truth|link}} from the {{Term|MM3D|Cursed Rich Man|link}} who was cursed when he entered the house in search of clues to become rich.<ref>{{Cite|You see, a while back, someone told me I could become rich and he gave me this mask. He said the instructions were inscribed somewhere in here, but when I went to look, I was cursed!|Cursed Rich Man|MM}}</ref> In the {{Term|MM3D|Oceanside Spider House|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can obtain the {{Term|MM3D|Giant's Wallet|link}} if he collects all 30 {{Plural|MM3D|Golden Skulltula Spirit}} during the {{Term|MM3D|First Day|link}}, with a {{Term|MM3D|Silver Rupee|link}} being awarded for completing it again on the {{Term|MM3D|First Day}} in subsequent cycles. On the {{Term|MM3D|Second Day|link}}, he will receive a {{Term|MM3D|Purple Rupee|link}}, and a {{Term|MM3D|Red Rupee|link}} on the {{Term|MM3D|Final Day|link}}.

The {{Term|MM3D|Giant's Wallet}} can be obtained on any day in {{MM3D}}. If the {{Term|MM3D|Adult Wallet|link}} has not been previously obtained, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} will instead receive it, while the {{Term|MM3D|Giant's Wallet}} will then be obtained from the {{Term|MM3D|Banker|link}}. Collecting all the {{Plural|MM3D|Golden Skulltula Spirit}} a second time at either the {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Spider House}} or the {{Term|MM3D|Oceanside Spider House}} will award Link with a {{Term|MM3D|Fishing Hole Pass|link}}.
