==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|CoH}}
{{Term|CoH|Impa's Naginata}} is a {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}} usable by most characters. Like all {{Plural|CoH|Spear}} in the game, {{Term|CoH|Impa's Naginata}} has a two-tile range, but can only hit one {{Term|CoH|Enemy|link}} at a time. It has similar effects to that of both a {{Term|CoH|Ruby Spear|link}} and an {{Term|CoH|Emerald Spear|link}}, dealing one damage and inflicting {{Term|CoH|Poison|link}} on any {{Term|CoH|Enemy}} it hits as well as healing the player by ½ a {{Term|CoH|Life Gauge|link|display=Heart}} every five kills.
