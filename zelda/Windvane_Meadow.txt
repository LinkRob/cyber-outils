{{Term/Store|Windvane Meadow||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Windvane Meadow'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Windvane Meadow is located in [[Hyrule Field]]. A [[Road]] between the [[Hyrule Garrison Ruins]] and  [[Mabe Village Ruins]] runs through the Meadow in the north and a crossroads between the Hyrule Garrison Ruins, the [[Exchange Ruins]], and a Road east towards the [[Whistling Hill]] runs through the southern portion of the Meadow. During the second [[Great Calamity]], Windvane Meadow was flooded with [[Guardian]]s that eradicated all [[Hylian]] life in and around the Meadow. The remains of a Guardian can be found in the northern part of the Meadow, which can be searched for [[Ancient Material]]s. A {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} can be found by the crossroads in the southeastern portion of the Meadow. [[Wooden Box]]es and [[Barrel]]s containing [[Apple]]s, [[Arrow]]s, and [[Hylian Shroom]]s can be found at this Encampment. This camp also features a lit [[Cooking Pot]].

Windvane Meadow is the location where [[Calamity Ganon]] reforms into [[Dark Beast Ganon]] after being defeated in the [[Astral Observatory#Breath of the Wild|Astral Observatory]]. To prevent Dark Beast Ganon from escaping and destroying [[Hyrule]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] summons [[Link]] to Windvane Meadow and erects a barrier of light to contain them both.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= {{Big|THE SITE OF THE FINAL BATTLE}}<br/>Link battles Dark Beast Ganon in the wide-open space of Windvane Meadow in Hyrule Field. Princess Zelda erects a barrier of light around them, preventing Ganon from leaving the area. |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 411}}</ref> She also bestows Link with the [[Bow of Light]] to aid him in his quest to [[Destroy Ganon]].<ref>{{Cite|I entrust you with the Bow of Light—a powerful weapon in the face of evil. Link...you may not yet be at a point where you have fully recovered your power or all of your memories... But courage need not be remembered... For it is never forgotten.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Soldier's Claymore]] can be found in the aforementioned {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}. It can only be unlocked after defeating all of the monsters in the camp.

A [[Korok]] can be found by breaking the {{Term|BotW|Vase|link}} shaped like an [[Acorn]] hanging from a broken wagon on the side of the northern Road that passes through the Meadow. Another Korok will appear after examining the [[Leaves with Fairy Dust]] moving around a small group of [[Tree]]s in the east of the Meadow. Another Korok is can be found in a [[Natural Hiding Spot]] under a [[Rock]] in a rock formation, north of the [[Central Tower]]. A Korok can be revealed by breaking another Acorn shaped {{Term|BotW|Vase}} hanging from a large Tree along the southern edge of the Meadow, north of [[Lake Kolomo]]. Following the [[Flower Trail]] that starts in the Meadow and leads west into a forest between the Meadow and the [[Exchange Ruins]] will reveal another Korok.
