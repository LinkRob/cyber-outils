'''Zoran''' is a writing form used by the [[Zora]]. The only examples of documents written using this system can be found in [[Zora Hall]] in [[Great Bay]].

==Overview==
If Link attempts to read [[Lulu's Diary]] or [[Mikau's Diary]] while in his human form, [[Tatl]] will point out that he can't read Zoran Script.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Dark Blue|Can you read Zoran script?}}|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref> While in his [[Zora Link|Zora form]], however, Link gains the physical abilities of a Zora, including the ability to read their writing. A similar phenomenon occurs when Link tries to read the unfamiliar inscriptions on [[Goron Graveyard|Darmani's grave]], but he can decipher the language by taking his [[Goron Link|Goron form]].
