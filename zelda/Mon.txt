{{Term/Store|Mon|Mon|Series, BotW}} is a type of {{Term|BotW|Currency|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="destabilize">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Mon}} is a {{Color|BotW Red|currency}} I invented to destabilize the market and fight the establishment!|Kilton|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Mon is a type of currency invented by {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} which can be obtained by exchanging Monster Parts with him.<ref name="destabilize" /><ref>{{Cite|Here's the deal: if you bring me {{Color|BotW Blue|monster parts}}, I'll exchange them for {{Color|BotW Red|mon}}.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> Mon can be used in {{Term|BotW|Kilton}}'s Shop, [[Fang and Bone]], to buy different types of [[Armor|Monster Gear]].<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, you can spend your {{Color|BotW Red|mon}} on various types of {{Color|BotW Blue|monster gear}} that I've created based on my research.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref>
