The {{Term/Store|Mail Center||Series, TWWHD}},<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD |publisher= Prima |page= 336}}</ref> also known as '''Mail Sorting''',<ref>{{Cite eGuide|quote= {{Big|Piece of Heart #5 (via Mail Sorting)}} |game= TWWHD |publisher= Prima |section= {{Small|Islands of the Great Sea}} Dragon Roost Island (F2)}}</ref> is a {{Term|TWWHD|Mini-Game|link}} in {{TWW}}.

==Overview==
The {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center (Mini-Game)}} can be found inside [[Dragon Roost Island]] on the second floor of the [[Mail Center]].

The game involves [[Link]] sorting letters by throwing them into the correct delivery box. Each letter has a logo or symbol on it and must be matched with the correct symbol inside the box.  If Link throws the wrong letter at a box, it will fall out.  Link earns one [[Rupee]] for every successful throw, and a bonus of 20 Rupees for achieving a score of 25 or higher.

The first time Link participates, he must achieve at least 25 points to win.  Some time after completing [[Dragon Roost Cavern]], [[Baito]] is hired by the [[Rito]] to work for the Island Postal Service.  If {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} manages to sort 25 or more letters once again, {{Term|TWWHD|Baito}} will ask him to put a {{Term|TWWHD|Note to Mom|link|display= letter}} in the mail to his mother talking about how great he is. Some time after sending the letter, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} will receive a reply from {{Term|TWWHD|Baito}}'s mother, {{Term|TWWHD|Kashiko|link}}, with a {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart|link}} inside.
