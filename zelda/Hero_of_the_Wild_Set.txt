The {{Term/Store|Hero of the Wild Set||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is an {{Term|Series|Armor Set|link}} in {{BotW}}, [[Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']], and {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 186}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Inventory/Set|BotW|Hero of the Wild Set}}
The Hero of the Wild Set is comprised of three pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} consisting of the {{Term|BotW|Cap of the Wild}}, {{Term|BotW|Tunic of the Wild}}, and {{Term|BotW|Trousers of the Wild}}. The three pieces will be awarded upon completing all 120 {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link|display=Shrines}}, and is the objective of the {{term|BotW|Adventure Log|link|display=Quest}} "{{Term|BotW|A Gift from the Monks|link}}". In Tears of the Kingdom, by going to the Hebra Dark Skeleton, Gerudo Dark Skeleton, and the Eldin Dark Skeleton and opening the chests inside the skulls, you will get the Cap Hero of the Wild, Tunic of the Wild, and Trousers of the Wild. The design is said to be passed down from the distant past based on a hero who is said to have a similar outfit<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=This Equipment is meant for a hero who travels the wild lands and can be acquired by conquering all of the ancient shrines. The design has been passed down from the distant past based on a hero who is said to have worn an outfit like this.|book= CaC|publisher= |page= 186}}</ref>

The set can be enhanced by visiting one of the various {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} and can be dyed at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}. When enhanced by two levels, it provides {{Effect|Master Sword Beam Up}}.
