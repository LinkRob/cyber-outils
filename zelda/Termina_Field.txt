{{Term/Store|Termina Field||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 246}}</ref> It encompasses [[Clock Town]] and has many features.

==Features and Overview==
Termina Field is the central area of the [[overworld]] of [[Termina]], and can be thought of as a reflection of [[Hyrule Field]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''. Located outside of Clock Town, it is, as its name implies, a world terminus, providing access to every major location in Termina.

This area leads into and out of the four gates of Clock Town. Termina Field also has paths leading to the other regions of Termina; the northern path leads to the [[Mountain Village]], the east path leads to [[Ikana Canyon]], the [[Road to Southern Swamp|southern area]] leads to the [[Southern Swamp]], the western area leads to [[Great Bay Coast]], and the southwest area leads to [[Milk Road]]. Milk Road, in turn, leads to both [[Romani Ranch]] and the [[Gorman Track]]. The [[Astral Observatory]] is also located close to the walls of Clock Town, but it cannot be normally entered from outside, as there are tall fences surrounding the Observatory. A [[Deku Flower]] outside the fence allows [[Deku Link]] entry from the Field, although the Observatory can also be accessed by taking an underground passageway through Clock Town.

It is impossible to enter the west and east regions without [[Epona]], as there are gates blocking the path. [[Link]] cannot enter the northern region without the [[Bow]] since the entrance of this zone is blocked by ice. The southwest area is also blocked by a giant boulder in the road that can only be destroyed by using a [[Powder Keg]], unless Link waits until the [[Final Day]], when the boulder will be removed by a man tirelessly working to demolish the boulder each day.

==Trivi==
*After being conspicuously absent from {{OoT}}, the theme of Termina Field is a remastered version of the original ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' overworld theme, which is the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|series]]' main theme.
**This theme also plays for the other regions of Termina if [[Link]] manages to restore temporary peace by completing their respective [[Dungeons in Majora's Mask|dungeons]].
*There are many ''Zelda''-related [[Easter Eggs]] hidden in Termina Field, ranging from the things Link can find through the [[Astral Observatory]] telescope, to the [[Guay]] that drops [[Rupee]]s when a song is played.
*The [[Moon]] in Termina Field is shown at a smaller scale compared to what's seen in other areas across Termina (except for the [[Clock Tower]]), which is especially noticeable in the [[First Day]]. This is because the original [[Nintendo 64]] version uses two separate models, and this area uses a simplified version of the larger one with an even angrier face.
*The music that plays in Termina Field is featured as a track in {{SSBB}} and {{SSB4|Wii U}} for the [[Bridge of Eldin]] Stage, and {{SSBU}} for any Stage related to ''The Legend of Zelda'' series. There is also an arrangement of the music featured as a track in {{SSBU|-}}.
