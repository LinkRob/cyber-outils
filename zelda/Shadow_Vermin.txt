==Characteristics==
Shadow Vermin appear as small, black, squid-like creatures that appear in areas overcast by [[Twilight]], which attack by flailing at [[Link]]. They are first encountered in the [[Castle Town Sewers]] while escaping with [[Midna]], shortly after Link was transformed into [[Wolf Link]]. Fighting them can be tricky, as they will attack while swimming across the water, and can sometimes be hard to spot until they get very close. They are quite fast, and can attack when least expected. Their behavior and small appearance is similar to that of [[Rat]]s.

Shadow Vermin also make an appearance within the [[Palace of Twilight]]. The only difference is there is no water for them to skim across.
