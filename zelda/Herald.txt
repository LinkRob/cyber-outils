'''Herald''' is a horse featured in the book {{TSP}}. He serves as [[Charles of Moria|Charles]]'s main method of transportation;<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Zelda!' Link protests, but she's already stomping toward the waiting Herald.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 47}}</ref> however, the horse is of little significance in the plot. 

In one of the many endings of the book, Link notices from a jail cell that Charles is riding away on his horse with the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] in hand. Just then, it is revealed that Herald is actually a "giant, winged lizard" in disguise.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Just as Charles is disappearing into the woods, his horse turns into a giant, winged lizard!|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 28}}</ref>
