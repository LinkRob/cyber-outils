The {{Term/Store|Temple of Light||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} became the resting place of the Triforce during the [[Era of Chaos]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page=77}}</ref><ref name="Remember">{{Cite|But, remember... Though you opened the Door of Time in the name of peace... Ganondorf, the Gerudo King of Thieves, used it to enter this forbidden Sacred Realm! He obtained the Triforce from the Temple of Light, and with its power, he became the King of Evil...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> It is the only known elemental temple that is not located directly within Hyrule. Instead, it lies in the center of the [[Sacred Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|The Temple of Light, situated in the very center of the Sacred Realm, is the last stronghold against Ganondorf's evil forces.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> It can be accessed only through the chamber of the [[Master Sword]] inside the [[Temple of Time]]. By withdrawing the [[Master Sword]] from the [[Pedestal of Time]], a portal opens to the Sacred Realm, which leads to the Temple of Light.<ref>{{Cite|The Master Sword--the evil-destroying sword that you pulled out of the Pedestal of Time--was the final key to the Sacred Realm.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Main|Chamber of Sages}}
The Temple of Light, unlike all other temples in {{OoT}}, is not an explorable area. Only one of its rooms is visited: the Chamber of Sages, which is where the Sages meet.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Main|Chamber of Sages}}
Link visits the Chamber of Sages after rescuing each of the seven Sages from the [[Painting]]s that [[Yuga]] used to trap them. Once all seven Sages are present, they reveal the [[Triforce of Courage]] to Link and create a staircase inside the chamber so that he can reach it. The Sages remain in the Chamber of Sages until the Triforce is returned to Hyrule.

The platforms the Sages stand upon spell "courage" in [[Hylian Language#A Link Between Worlds|Hylian]].
