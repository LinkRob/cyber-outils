{{Term/Store|Lake Jarrah||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lake Jarrah'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Lake Jarrah is a lake in [[East Necluda]] found just south of the [[Equestrian Riding Course]].

A metallic [[Treasure Chest]] can be found at the mouth of Lake Jarrah where it feeds into the [[Fir River]]. This Treasure Chest is buried halfway in the ground, requiring Link to use [[Magnesis]] to uncover it and obtain the [[Amber]] inside. A second metallic Treasure Chest containing five [[Shock Arrow]]s is located in the Lake's center. A third metallic Treasure Chest can be found under a sunken archway, containing an [[Opal]]. A final, wooden Treasure Chest can be found floating in the water next to the waterfall at the source of Lake Jarrah. It contains a [[Purple Rupee]], valued at 50 [[Rupee]]s.

On the southern bank of Lake Jarrah is a [[Rock]] which can be lifted to uncover a [[Korok]] who will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] in return for finding him.
