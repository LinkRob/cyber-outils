{{Term/Store|Hoiger Howgendoogen||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283}}</ref>

==Biography==
Like all members of the {{Term|PH|Ho Ho Tribe|link}}, {{Term|PH|Hoiger Howgendoogen}} originated from the land of {{Term|PH|Wayaway|link}} and came to the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}} in search of whichever {{Term|PH|Treasure|link}} is currently popular back home.<ref>{{Cite|I'm a member of the {{Color|PH Blue|Ho Ho Tribe}}. Yes, indeed! I came from the {{Color|PH Blue|country of Wayaway}}, across the ocean, looking for treasure. Certain items are treasure to the people of {{Color|PH Blue|Wayaway}}, and I'll buy them from you! I'll buy them for more than any of the general stores around here, too.|Ho Ho Tribe|PH}}</ref>

{{Term|PH|Hoiger Howgendoogen}} can be found onboard the {{Term|PH|Traveler's Ship}} belonging to the {{Term|PH|Ho Ho Tribe}}, where if spoken to, he will cryptically lament about his lost {{Term|PH|Telescope|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... I dropped {{Color|PH Red|that thing}} somewhere... Things like that are truly hard to find with the naked eye...|Hoiger Howgendoogen|PH}}</ref> which {{Term|PH|Link|link}} can find and his as part of the {{Term|PH|Trading Quest|link}}. If {{Term|PH|Link}} speaks to {{Term|PH|Hoiger Howgendoogen}} while holding his {{Term|PH|Telescope}}, {{Term|PH|Hoiger Howgendoogen}} will ask to see it.<ref>{{Cite|...Ho ho! That's a nice telescope you have there! May I see it for a moment?|Hoiger Howgendoogen|PH}}</ref> {{Term|PH|Hoiger Howgendoogen}} will recognize it immediately as it has his name written on the back. As a thanks for returning it {{Term|PH|Hoiger Howgendoogen}} will give {{Term|PH|Link}} the {{Term|PH|Guard Notebook|link}} which he had found floating in the sea.<ref>{{Cite|There! That has my name on it! Look! On the back, in really small writing! Yes, indeed! That's my real name... Hoiger Howgendoogen... I bit my tongue! ......... Regardless, this appears to belong to me. Thanks for delivering it! Please accept this gift as a symbol of my deep appreciation... Oh don't worry. It's just something that I picked up along the way...|Hoiger Howgendoogen|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I saw it floating among the waves and picked it up. I'd like to return it to the rightful owner, but who is it? What a mystery...|Hoiger Howgendoogen|PH}}</ref>
