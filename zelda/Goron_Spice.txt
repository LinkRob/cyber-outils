==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Goron Spice can be purchased from the [[Goron Gusto Shop]] in [[Goron City]]. Alternatively, it's sold by the travelling merchant [[Yammo]] near the [[Tabantha Great Bridge]]. It is made of several different spices, and the initial wave of spiciness turns into sweetness.<ref name="Inventory"/> While it can't be eaten, it can be used to make certain {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}} at a [[Cooking Pot]]. At the [[Kochi Dye Shop]], Goron Spice is one of the ingredients that can be used to make Red [[Dye]].

As part of the "[[Curry for What Ails You]]" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must give a bottle of Goron Spice to [[Lester]] at the [[Rito Stable]] so he can make [[Curry Rice]].<ref>{{Cite|Now, I have {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}} on hand, which I need to make {{Color|BotW Blue|curry rice}}, but I'm fresh out of {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}!|Lester|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you happen to get your hands on some {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}, can you give it to me?|Lester|BotW}}</ref> Upon obtaining Goron Spice, Lester will ask Link to trade it for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey... Could I ask you to part with that {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} for, say, {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}}?|Lester|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, Link can trade Goron Spice with him at any time for [[Hylian Rice]].<ref>{{Cite|Next time, I'll trade you some {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}} for it.|Lester|BotW}}</ref>

While [[Cooking]], Goron Spice is required to make [[Crab Stir-Fry]], [[Curry Pilaf]], Curry Rice, [[Fragrant Mushroom Sauté]], [[Gourmet Meat Curry]], [[Gourmet Poultry Curry]], a [[Gourmet Spiced Meat Skewer]], [[Herb Sauté]], [[Meat Curry]], [[Monster Curry]], [[Poultry Curry]], [[Prime Meat Curry]], [[Prime Poultry Curry]], a [[Prime Spiced Meat Skewer]], [[Seafood Curry]], a [[Spiced Meat Skewer]], and [[Vegetable Curry]].
