The {{Term/Store|Floodgate Key||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Floodgate Key is found in the [[Floodgate Keeper]]'s house and opens the Floodgates in [[Spool Swamp]]. [[Link]] must open the Floodgates in order to enter [[Poison Moth's Lair]].
