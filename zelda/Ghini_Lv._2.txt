'''Ghini Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Ghini Lv. 2|The largest type of Ghini, this monster has a huge eyeball and tongue to match its stature.<ref name="EoW Ghini Lv. 2">{{Cite|The largest type of Ghini, this monster has a huge eyeball and tongue to match its stature.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Ghini Lv. 2 is a tougher variant of the standard [[Ghini]], with higher health and damage. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Ghini Lv. 2 [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of three.
