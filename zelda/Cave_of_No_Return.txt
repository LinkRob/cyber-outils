The {{Term/Store|Cave of No Return||Series, FSA}} is the second stage of [[Whereabouts of the Wind]] in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 153}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Cave of No Return is found after [[Lake Hylia]] and before [[Hyrule Castle]]. Like all middle stages, the final obstacle is [[Shadow Link]]. Unique to this dungeon is the lack of adequate lighting, so the [[Links]] must put the [[Lamp]] to good use here if the young heroes are to make progress. Adding to the dark situation is the lengthy labyrinth at the level's beginnning and a [[puzzle]] at its end, both of which are under the cover of darkness. The solution to the  enchanted area at the start of the level is West, North, East, and North. If the Links choose directions incorrectly, they will end up back at the beginning of the maze.

In terms of sentient life, helpful [[Old Man|ghosts]] offer the Links advice right before the different challenges of the level. Lastly, a solitary [[Mage]] can be found at the entrance, snickering about the probability of getting lost while navigating the dark cavern beyond. Beyond that, the cavern is relatively void of life, save for the [[Rat]] and [[Whisp]] enemies that stalk the dank hallways.
