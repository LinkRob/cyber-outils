==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kodah}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} who resides at {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. She is one of the owners of the {{Term|BotW|Seabed Inn|link}}, the other being her husband {{Term|BotW|Kayden|link}}. She spends her days tending to the {{Term|BotW|Inn|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Kodah}} knew {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} prior to the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, affectionately referring to him as "Linny."<ref name="Linny">{{Cite|Yes, I'm sure of it! You're Linny! Wow, it's been a long time!|Kodah|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Yesterday">{{Cite|Has it really been 100 years? In a way, it feels like yesterday...|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> She asked {{Term|BotW|Link}} to choose between herself and {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}.<ref name="Mipha">{{Cite|A hundred years ago, I asked you... to choose between me and Mipha. I...I'm sorry about that.|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> Since then, she married {{Term|BotW|Kayden}}, and they had their daughter {{Term|BotW|Finley|link}} together.<ref name="Happy"/> When {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} began its rampage, {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} banned {{Term|BotW|Finley}} from going to the {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes|link}}.<ref name="Finley"/>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Kodah}}, she will begin to welcome him to the {{Term|BotW|Seabed Inn}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hello! Welcome to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Seabed Inn}}. Here we—|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> Recognizing {{Term|BotW|Link}}, she asks if he is "Linny" and does not wait for an answer before proclaiming that he is.<ref>{{Cite|... ... Could it be? Linny?! It is! It's Linny!!|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks, {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} only becomes more certain, remarking that it has been a long time since they last saw each other.<ref name="Linny"/> Since she thought both {{Term|BotW|Link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} had fallen to {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} begs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to tell her where he has been and what he has been doing since the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wait, but...I thought you and Mipha... fell to {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}}. And if you've been alive all this time, where have you been? What have you been doing? Please, tell me!|Kodah|BotW}}</ref>

Embarrassed, {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} apologizes for making a ruckus, as she is an adult.<ref>{{Cite|... Oh, I'm sorry... I'm an adult, and yet here I am, making a ruckus.|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> She asks if it has really been a century, because to her it feels like yesterday.<ref name="Yesterday"/> {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} states they have both been through a lot and that they should catch up sometime soon.<ref>{{Cite|We've both been through a lot, I'm sure. If you don't mind, I'd like to catch up sometime soon.|Kodah|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} again, he will be able to ask her questions.<ref>{{Cite|Hello! Wait...what was I going to say? Oh! Do you need something?|Kodah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Is there anything else you'd like to ask?|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}, {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} will be shocked to hear that he forgot about her.<ref>{{Cite|[Mipha?] Huh?! Linny... You didn't forget about Mipha, did you?|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> She explains that {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} was a princess, in addition to being {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}}'s daughter and {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}}'s older sister.<ref>{{Cite|Mipha was a princess. She was also King Dorephan's daughter and Sidon's older sister.|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> As the {{Term|BotW|Zora}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}, she was beloved by her people, and she could also {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Grace|link|display=heal}} their wounds.<ref>{{Cite|She was the Zora Champions and could heal wounds with ease. We all loved her dearly.|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> 100 years ago, {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} piloted {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}} against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}, which led to her death.<ref>{{Cite|A hundred years ago, she piloted the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}} to help fight {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}}. As for what happened next, well... I'm sure you can figure it out.|Kodah|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} who she is, she calls him terrible and introduces herself.<ref>{{Cite|You're terrible, Linny! It's me, Kodah!|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> She apologizes to {{Term|BotW|Link}} for asking him to choose between herself and {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} 100 years ago.<ref name="Mipha"/> Now, she is married to {{Term|BotW|Kayden}} and has a daughter named {{Term|BotW|Finley}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now I'm married to Kayden, and I'm a mother of one. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} again questions the name "Linny," {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} reveals that it is the nickname she gave him a century ago.<ref>{{Cite|Heh, that's the nickname I gave you 100 years ago!...|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> Though much time has passed, she still remembers that he is {{Term|BotW|Link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions}} who fought against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} with {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}.<ref>{{Cite|Though much time has passed, I still remember that your real name... is Link. And that you're the Hylian Champion! You went with Mipha to fight back against {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}}.|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the council of elders is angry about what happened, believing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} stole {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} away from them.<ref>{{Cite|You should know that the council of elders is angry about...what happened. They say "that {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}} stole Mipha away.|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} such as {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} who are around 100 years old know what it was like back then and do not believe that, since {{Term|BotW|Link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} are the ones who have held back {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|But us Zora who are around 100 years old now...we know what it was like back then. We don't believe that. You and Mipha are the Champions who held back the Calamity for all of us!|Kodah|BotW}}</ref>

When spoken to after {{Term|BotW|Link}} has appeased  {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} notices that the {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}} has stopped and wonders if it means that {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}} has ended its rampage.<ref>{{Cite|The rain has stopped... I wonder if {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} has stopped as well...|Kodah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} then notices {{Term|BotW|Link}} standing nearby and apologizes, asking if he needs something.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Sorry, Linny. Oh! Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Kodah}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for appeasing the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Linny... Thank you for appeasing the Divine Beast!|Kodah|BotW}}</ref>
