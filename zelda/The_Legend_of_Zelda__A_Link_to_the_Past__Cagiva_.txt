'''''The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past''''' is a [[manga]] by [[Ataru Cagiva]] originally serialized in ''{{Wp|Gangan Comics#Monthly_GFantasy|Monthly GFantasy}}'', a monthly manga anthology published by {{Wp|Enix|Enix Corporation}}. It was later collected into three volumes and greatly expands on the story told in {{ALttP}}.

==Trivia==
*The manga had Zelda meet Link in her bedroom before escaping with him via a secret passage. The 2020 data leak revealed that Zelda's bedroom was going to be a location in the game, implying something similar was planned.
