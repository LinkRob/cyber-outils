The {{Term/Store|Clock Shop||Series, OoS}} is a location in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Clock Shop|Sign|OoS}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|OoS|Clock Shop}} is located in the center of {{Term|OoS|Horon Village}}. Inside, {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} can find {{Term|OoS|Tick Tock}}, its proprietor. {{Term|OoS|Link}} cannot buy anything from the {{Term|OoS|Clock Shop}}, its only purpose being for the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}, where {{Term|OoS|Link}} trades {{Term|OoS|Tick Tock}} a {{Term|OoS|Wooden Bird|link}} for {{Term|OoS|Engine Grease}}. 

A patch of ground behind the {{Term|OoS|Clock Shop}} can be dug up with a {{Term|OoS|Shovel|link}} to reveal a staircase to a hidden cave. On a completed file of {{OoS}} (linked with {{OoA}}), an {{Term|OoS|Old Man|link}} will inhabit said cave. The {{Term|OoS|Old Man}} will receive a {{Term|OoS|Linked Game/Secrets|display=secret|link}} (in the form of a password) told to {{Term|OoS|Link}} by an {{Term|OoA|Old Woman|link}} near {{Term|OoA|Lynna City|link}} in a {{Term|OoA|Linked Game|link}} of {{OoA|-}}. Once having received the secret, the {{Term|OoS|Old Man}} puts {{Term|OoS|Link}} to the test by pitting him against 12 {{Plural|OoS|Enemy|link}}. If {{Term|OoS|Link}} defeats all 12 enemies in 30 seconds or less, he will reward {{Term|OoS|Link}} by upgrading his {{Term|OoS|Sword|link}} to either the {{Term|OoS|Noble Sword|link}} or the {{Term|OoS|Master Sword|link}} (if he has already obtained the Noble Sword from the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest}}).<ref>{{Cite|Oh! A message from the old hag in Labrynna... In that case, take my test. If you pass, I shall give your sword new power!|Old Man|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In a moment, 12 beasts will appear. You must slay them all in 30 seconds, without being slain yourself, of course.|Old Man|OoS}}</ref>
