{{Term/Store|Honcho||Series, ST}} is a character in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 288}}</ref> He is the leader of the {{Term|ST|Anouki|link}} race and "honcho" of {{Term|ST|Anouki Village|link}}.<ref name="Honcho"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|ST|Link|link}} seeks out {{Term|ST|Honcho}} for information on the location of the {{Term|ST|Snow Sanctuary|link}} and its {{Term|ST|Lokomo|link}} sage, {{Term|ST|Steem|link}}. At first, {{Term|ST|Honcho}} refuses to give {{Term|ST|Link}} the information as he is too busy setting up a village watch to patrol for the monsters that began to appear ever since the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} vanished.<ref>{{Cite|...Honestly, though, when those tracks disappeared, us Anoukis were shocked. Not only were the tracks gone, but monsters started poppin' up too! We're in a real bind now! There's danger around every corner! So I don't really have time to yak it up about goin' to the Snow Sanctuary.|Honcho|ST}}</ref> He will tell {{Term|ST|Link}} everything if he is able to find a patrol partner for each villager, so he gives the young hero the task to pair up all of the {{Plural|ST|Anouki}},<ref>{{Cite| But I might be able to chat more if you'd help this old- timer organize some teams. What do you say, pal?|Honcho|ST}}</ref> who are extremely picky when it comes to who they want as partners; {{Term|ST|Honcho}} mentions that he is happy to pair up with "any old {{Term|ST|Anouki}}".<ref>{{Cite|Ya think you're up to the task, buddy? Yeah, ya got a real capable look to ya. Well, do your best, but don't get worked up if the goin' gets a little tough! See, most of the villagers are a little picky about who they'll partner up with. Pairin' them off is gonna be a pain, but I think ya got it under control. Talk with folks in town, and come tell me when you've found the best pairings!|Honcho|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Link}} must then talk to each of the {{Plural|ST|Anouki}}, to figure out their preferences and pair them up accordingly.

Once {{Term|ST|Link}} shows {{Term|ST|Honcho}} his findings, he will be grateful to the young hero and unlock the path leading to the {{Term|ST|Snow Sanctuary}}. However, he warns {{Term|ST|Link}} about a "{{Term|ST|Rocktite|link|display=ginormous monster}}" that appeared in the tunnel to the {{Term|ST|Snow Sanctuary|display=Sanctuary}}, telling him to be careful.<ref>{{Cite|Technically speakin', the tunnel that goes there is still intact... But no one's used it since that ginormous monster moved in a few years back. See, us Anoukis are thinkers, not monster fighters. But I can tell you're cut from different cloth than us! Sturdier cloth! Sure, the tunnel to the Snow Sanctuary is scary, but you can handle it! Here's the entrance. If ya decide to use it, be careful in there, OK?|Honcho|ST}}</ref>

Later on, {{Term|ST|Honcho}} departs {{Term|ST|Anouki Village}} temporarily, during which time another villager pretends to be {{Term|ST|Honcho}}. {{Term|ST|Link}} can bring a {{Term|ST|Goron|link}}, who wishes to live in a colder climate, to visit the {{Term|ST|Anouki Village|display=Village}} during this time. The pretend {{Term|ST|Honcho}} then lets the {{Term|ST|Goron}} live in {{Term|ST|Honcho}}'s house, much to the dismay of the real {{Term|ST|Honcho}}, who returns to find that he is homeless.<ref>{{Cite|This is crazy! What in the world's been goin' on while I've been on watch? Some big orange monster decided to set up camp in my place! I can't exactly go home now! Where am I supposed to sleep? Tell me, where?!|Honcho|ST}}</ref>
