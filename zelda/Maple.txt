{{Term/Store|Maple||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}} & 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>{{Fact}}

==Biography==
==={{ALttP&FS|-}}===
Maple makes an appearance as a shop keeper at the potion shop, but only in the gameboy advance version, in the original she was a [[Hooded Shopkeeper]].

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Maple|Maple is an apprentice witch to her grandmother. She often sneaks away from training and flies around on the magic broom she recently learned to ride. Occasionally, she unexpectedly flies down and crashes into Link.}}
{{Manual|OoA|Maple|Maple is an apprentice witch to her grandmother. She often sneaks away from training and flies around on the magic broom she recently learned to ride. Occasionally, she unexpectedly flies down and crashes into Link.}}
{{Term|OoS|Maple}} appears in both {{OoS}} and {{OoA}} as the apprentice witch of her grandmother {{Term|Series|Syrup|link}},<ref>{{Cite|You just hit Maple, the Great Witch Syrup's apprentice!|Maple|OoS}}</ref> playing an identical role in each game. Once {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} has defeated 30 enemies (or 15 while wearing {{Term|OoS|Maple's Ring|link}}) and enters one of the predetermined areas where {{Term|OoS|Maple}} can appear, her theme song begins playing its ominous opening notes as her shadow snakes across the ground, indicating her entrance from overhead. {{Term|OoS|Maple}} then swoops in on her broomstick, making several passes around the screen. While {{Term|OoS|Link}} can try to avoid or ignore her, a collision between the two will cause {{Term|OoS|Maple}} to crash, scattering some of her belongings on the ground. These items may include {{Plural|OoS|Rupee|link}}, {{Plural|OoS|Heart|link}}, {{Plural|OoS|Bomb|link}}, {{Plural|OoS|Mystical Seed|link}}, {{Plural|OoS|Gasha Seed|link}}, {{Plural|OoS|Magic Ring|link}}, {{Plural|OoS|Potion|link|display=Magic Potions}}, and even a {{Term|OoS|Piece of Heart|link}}, which can only be collected once. The more valuable an item, the more rarely it will be dropped in the collision. Some of {{Term|OoS|Link}}'s own {{Plural|OoS|Rupee}}, {{Plural|OoS|Heart}}, {{Plural|OoS|Bomb}} and {{Plural|OoS|Mystical Seed}} may be dropped as well.

The apprentice witch then berates {{Term|OoS|Link}} for his supposed carelessness and declares that she'll take all of the dropped items for herself, including any of {{Term|OoS|Link}}'s.<ref>{{Cite|AH! All my stuff flew out of my bag! I'll just take what you dropped, too! This'll show you what happens when you make {{Color|OoS Blue|Maple}} mad!|Maple|OoS}}</ref> This begins a [[Mini-Game]] of sorts, where {{Term|OoS|Maple}} and {{Term|OoS|Link}} race around the screen to pick up as many items as possible. {{Term|OoS|Maple}} can travel slightly faster than {{Term|OoS|Link}} thanks to her power of flight, but requires an extra moment to lift up any items she hovers over, while {{Term|OoS|Link}} can simply touch an item to collect it. Once all of the items have been claimed, {{Term|OoS|Maple}} will offer a condescending remark on the young hero's performance before flying away in a huff.<ref>{{Cite|Ah ha ha ha! Now you're sorry! I have more stuff than before! See ya!|Maple|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hmm! Not bad! But that's all you get today.|Maple|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You little-- I'll remember this!|Maple|OoS}}</ref>

Though {{Term|OoS|Maple}} has little regard for {{Term|OoS|Link}}, she eventually comes to see their encounters as more of a game.<ref>{{Cite|We meet again! You know the drill! Fastest one wins. Ready..? Go!|Maple|OoS}}</ref> After eight encounters have taken place, {{Term|OoS|Maple}} will appear riding a flying vacuum cleaner instead of her broom, which allows her to fly faster, but more erratically. In a [[Linked Game]], she'll appear riding a flying saucer instead. The witch can even be encountered in the past in {{OoA|-}}, having the apparent ability to fly through {{Plural|OoS|Time Portal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ouch... Watch where you're walkin'! I came through this weird tunnel and was flyin' happily along... What are you doin' here anyway?|Maple|OoA}}</ref>

{{Term|OoS|Maple}} also plays a part in the [[Trading Quest]] of both games. In {{OoS|-}}, she requires the {{Term|OoS|Lon Lon Egg|link}} and gives {{Term|OoS|Link}} the {{Term|OoS|Ghastly Doll|link}} in return. In {{OoA|-}}, she requires the {{Term|OoA|Touching Book|link}} which she exchanges for the {{Term|OoA|Magic Oar|link}}. {{Term|OoA|Maple}} appears again in the ending of {{OoA|-}}, where she is seen listening to {{Term|OoA|Nayru (Oracle)|link}} play the {{Term|OoA|Harp of Ages|link}}.

====Item Priority====
When collecting the dropped items, {{Term|OoS|Maple}} will always fly toward the highest priority item first, even if other items are closer. {{Term|OoS|Link}} can use this knowledge to plot the best path to collect the items he desires.

==Other Appearances==
==={{OoS|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Maple plays a fairly large role in the {{OoS|Himekawa}}, alongside [[Ricky]] and [[Link]], journeying with them after meeting in the [[Temple of Seasons]]. Maple constantly says she only tags along because she wants the [[Rod of Seasons]], but after obtaining it from Link through a stroke of good luck on her part and leaving them, Maple eventually returns to Link's aid just in time to help him defeat [[General Onox]]. She is depicted in the manga as a kind witch, despite her selfishness who does not have constant effective control over most of her spells. She is essential in the defeat of Onox.
{{Noncanon|End}}
