{{Term/Store|Pina||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Pina}}
{{Term|TMC|Pina}} is a vendor in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}'s market; her stall occupies the southwestern corner of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square|link}}. She is first encountered in the southeastern stall during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, where she announces {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} as the winner of the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|display=Festival}}'s drawing.<ref>{{Cite|We have a winner! Oh, my! Princess Zelda! You won the grand prize in our drawing? Wow!|Pina|TMC}}</ref> She attempts to convince {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}} to choose either a {{Term|TMC|Heart Container|link}} or a {{Term|TMC|Big Red Rupee|link}} as her prize,<ref>{{Cite|You get your choice of any of these wonderful prizes. What will it be? First, we have...this lovely {{Color|TMC Red|heart-shaped stone}}! Next, we have... this {{Color|TMC Red|magnificent gem}}! And finally, there's... this teeny-tiny {{Color|TMC Red|shield}}. So, which one would you like? Let me guess...the jewel? Oh, but this heart stone is very cute, too. Choose whichever one you like!|Pina|TMC}}</ref> but {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}} decides on a {{Term|TMC|Small Shield|link}}, much to {{Term|TMC|Pina}}'s surprise.<ref>{{Cite|What!? Why? Why would you want that ugly thing!? What about the heart stone! It's cute! Or... Or this giant gem! It's pretty! Honestly, they're both much nicer than that nasty little shield thing. What a strange princess you are. Well... OK. Here's your teeny-tiny {{Color|TMC Red|shield}}.|Pina|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} returns to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} and obtains the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Pina}} can be found in her usual stall selling fruit.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! Fresh fruit! Get your fresh fruit here! Come on up and take a look, folks! One whiff of these sweet and delicious fruits, and you'll be sold! Come on up!|Pina|TMC}}</ref> She encourages {{Term|TMC|Link}} to buy her wares, gradually increasing her aggressive approach with threats of violence,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! Lookie, lookie! Hurry up and buy something, or I smack you!|Pina|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! Lowest prices anywhere! Hurry up and buy, or I'll pelt you with fruits! Hey, hey!|Pina|TMC}}</ref> and once claiming that vegetables (rather than the fruit she sells) are the perfect gift for any occasion.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! Veggies makes the ideal gift, perfect for all occasions! Send a few of these as a gift, and make me a happy, happy woman! Hey, hey!|Pina|TMC}}</ref>

By the time {{Term|TMC|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}} and the threat of monsters has begun to cause {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} to close down, {{Term|TMC|Pina}} attempts to sell the remainder of her wares.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! It's a closeout sale! Everything must go! Clearance prices, people! What are you waiting for!? Hey, hey!|Pina|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Pina}} disappears from {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square}} and is not seen for the remainder of the game.

{{Term|TMC|Pina}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to fuse Kinstones?|Pina|TMC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Pina}}'s name is a contraction of the word "{{Wp|pineapple}}", a fruit.
