==Tools==
===Bone Ocarina===
{{Main|Bone Ocarina}}
[[File:FPTRR Bone Ocarina Sprite.png|left]] "The pirates' ocarina. Play it at piers to call the pirate ship."<br/>
[[File:Bone Ocarina Upgrade.png|left]] "The powered-up ocarina. Allows you to discover the pirate's treasure."<br/>
The Bone Ocarina is obtained shortly after clearing the second dungeon, the [[Pirate Hideaway]] in [[Cape Treasure]]. The Bone Ocarina can be used to summon the pirates while standing on a dock. After helping the pirates for the second time, Tingle can upgrade the item and use it to open the ten hidden blue pirate treasure chests once they are found.

===Water===
[[File:FPTRR Water Sprite.png|left]] "You can use an empty jar to store water. But how can you use it?"
Water can be gathered from various sources in the game, it's mainly used for dousing enemies on fire.

===Life Dew===
[[File:FPTRR Life Dew Sprite.png|left]] "Water that plants would surely appreciate."
'''Life Dew ''' is an item found deep within the [[Deku Temple]]. The [[Deku Tree]] requests that Tingle bring this dew and use it on his heir, so that the sapling may regain his strength, and carry on the Deku Tree's legacy.

===Dirty Water===
[[File:FPTRR Dirty Water Sprite.png|left]] "This water doesn't look too clean..."
Before defeating [[Bana Bana]] only this water can be gathered from the spring inside the Deku Temple.

===Yellow Butterfly===
[[File:FPTRR Yellow Butterfly Sprite.png|left]] "A lovely butterfly with bright yellow wings."
This butterfly can be found in [[Gooey Swamp]] near the Frill Flower. The [[Chief Explorer]] will pay 5,000 Rupees for it.

===Red Butterfly===
[[File:FPTRR Red Butterfly Sprite.png|left]] "A lovely butterfly with bright red wings."
This butterfly can be found in '''Gooey Swamp''' near the Bell Bell Flower. The '''Chief Explorer''' will pay 5,000 Rupees for it.

===Blue Butterfly===
[[File:FPTRR Blue Butterfly Sprite.png|left]] "A lovely butterfly with bright blue wings."
This butterfly can be found in [[Steamy Marsh]] near the Shower Flower. The '''Chief Explorer''' will pay 5,000 Rupees for it.

===Empty Jar===
{{Main|Bottle}}
[[File:FPTRR bottle Sprite.png|left]] "An empty glass jar. It's up to you what you fill it with."
'''Empty Jars''' are items that Tingle finds throughout his journey. He can store many different things in them, such as butterflies, soups and potions.


==Items==

===Fairy Doll===
[[File:FPTRR Fairy Doll Sprite.png|left]] "This doll has seen better days, but the wear and tear shows that somebody loves it."
The '''Fairy Doll''' belongs to [[Chiko]] and is found in the mouth of the Fish Rock in [[Hometown Prairie]]. Later it becomes a [[Rupee Good]].

===Jellyfish Powder===
[[File:FPTRR Jellyfish Powder Sprite.png|left]] "A dry, white powder made from ground jellyfish."
This item is received from the {{Term|FPTRR|Engineer|link}} in the [[Pirate Hideout]] and given to a sick pirate to receive one of the pirate items.

===Red Bandana===
[[File:FPTRR Red Bandana Sprite.png|left]] "A pirate bandana. Just wearing it gives you guts and determination."
This items is one of the three pirate items Tingle has to bring the {{Term|FPTRR|First Mate|link}} to become a full member of the pirate crew. It's been handed out by the sick pirate.

===Stripy Shirt===
[[File:FPTRR Stripy Shirt Sprite.png|left]] "You'll look just like a pirate in this attractive red and white striped shirt!"
Another one of the three pirate items. Tingle receives it after saving the Cook's assistance in the '''Pirate Hideout'''.

===Telescope===
[[File:FPTRR Telescope Sprite.png|left]] "Faraway objects appear much closer with this spyglass. One of the Pirate Items."
The last of the three pirate items. It's the reward for sorting the juice barrels in the '''Pirate Hideout'''.

===Girl's Glasses===
[[File:FPTRR Girl's Glasses Sprite.png|left]] "These look like the glasses a clever young woman might wear. But whose are they?"
These glasses can be found at the [[Lon Lon Meadow]] near the marked landing spot and must be brought back to [[Missy (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|Missy]] after she is supposedly abducted by [[Oinker]]s.

===Oinker Snout===
[[File:FPTRR Oinker Snout Sprite.png|left]] "Replica snout which looks just like the real thing."
The '''Oinker Snout''' is obtained by trading [[Sweet Potato]]es to [[Dave]]. They can be used to unlock [[Oinker]] camps.

===Golden Button===
[[File:FPTRR Golden Button Sprite.png|left]] "Bright, shiny gold button."
This item is rewarded for defeating the '''Oinkers''' of '''Lon Lon Meadow'''. The [[Town Guard]] will happily buy it for 3000 Rupees.

===Aba's Knife===
[[File:FPTRR Aba's Knife Sprite.png|left]] "Inscribed on the blade: "Train them to fight bravely and fiercely!""
After [[Aba]] has an argument with [[Junglo]] the knife can be found outside of their hut in [[Deku Forest (First Continent)|Deku Forest]]. It's used to reunite her with her [[Armourer|real father]].

===Dazzling Brooch===
[[File:FPTRR Dazzling Brooch Sprite.png|left]] "Bright red fruit-shaped brooch"
Gained from the Oinker camp treasure in [[Deku Forest (First Continent)|Deku Forest]], this brooch strongly resembles a pair of cherries. I can be sold it to [[Bella]] for 4000 Rupees.

===Worn-Out Rag===
[[File:FPTRR Worn-Out Rag Sprite.png|left]] "Old, torn piece of green cloth."
This item belongs to a [[Salona]] in [[Gooey Swamp]] and is found behind a mushroom at the bottom left corner of the map. [[Chiko's Mum]] will mend it for 1,750 Rupees.

===Hood===
[[File:FPTRR Hood Sprite.png|left]] "The worn-out rag has been miraculously made into a hood on the sewing machine."
After it's been repaired it can be brought back to the naked Salona for 8,000 Rupee reward.

===Chrysalis===
{{main|Mini Death Bug Chrysalis}}
[[File:FPTRR Chrysalis Sprite.png|left]] "This Mini Death Bug chrysalis is shrivelled up, but gives off a heady smell like medicine."
The Chrysalis is obtained in the [[Insect Cavern]]. It must be given to [[Teddy Todo]] to boost his strength to continue in the dungeon.

===Aero Wing===
[[File:FPTRR Aero Wing Sprite.png|left]] "Has the inscription: "Moneybags AW-01." Looks like a part of something..."
One of the items used to repair the ballon of the [[Old Gent]] and [[Paul]]. It can be found at the [[Icy Plain]] south of the Salon and when brought back is rewarded with 5,000 Rupees.

===Vacuum Funnel===
[[File:FPTRR Vacuum Funnel Sprite.png|left]] "Has the inscription: "VF-02." Looks like a part of something..."
One of the items used to repair the ballon of the '''Old Gent''' and '''Paul'''. It can be found at the '''Icy Plain''' near the Giant Crystal and when brought back is rewarded with 5,000 Rupees.

===Hydro Crank===
[[File:FPTRR Hydro Crank Sprite.png|left]] "Has the inscription: "Moneybags HC-03." Looks like a part of something..."
One of the items used to repair the ballon of the '''Old Gent''' and '''Paul'''. It can be found at the '''Icy Plain '''by the frozen lake and when brought back is rewarded with 5,000 Rupees.

===Number Plate===
[[File:FPTRR Number Plate Sprite.png|left]] "Has the inscription: "Moneybags LP-04." Looks like a part of something..."
One of the items used to repair the ballon of the '''Old Gent''' and '''Paul'''. It can be found in the [[Gooey Swamp]] in the northern pond and when brought back is rewarded with 5,000 Rupees.

===Cloud Lamp===
[[File:FPTRR Cloud Lamp Sprite.png|left]] "Has the inscription: "Moneybags CL-05." Looks like a part of something..."

===Snow Moustache===
[[File:FPTRR Snow Moustache Sprite.png|left]] "Icy cold moustache. It is frozen solid."

[[File:FPTRR Snow Scarf Sprite.png|left]] "Icy cold scarf. Wear it and you're guaranteed to catch a cold."

[[File:FPTRR Trading Card Sprite.png|left]] "Has a picture of a monster you have never seen before. Its Power Level is 5000..."

[[File:FPTRR Royal Candy Sprite.png|left]] "High-class sweet which can only be bought from exclusive shops with very long queues."

[[File:FPTRR Star Badge Sprite.png|left]] "Badge of a character who is very popular with children. Not sure what his name is..."

[[File:FPTRR Ochre Cube Sprite.png|left]] "Yellow cube. Covered in mud."

===Blazing Cube===
[[File:FPTRR Blazing Cube Sprite.png|left]] "Red cube. Will make you hot and bothered."
This cube grows on a plant in the eastern part of '''Fairy Garden'''. It has to be offered to the Red Spring.

===Rupee Key===
[[File:FPTRR Rupee Key Sprite.png|left]] "A rupee-shaped key received from the Grand Fairy."
This key will unlock the gates of [[Auros Ruins]] and can be received from the [[Grand Fairy]] after raising the tower high enough to fly there for the first time.

===Make-Up Mirror===
[[File:FPTRR Make-Up Mirror Sprite.png|left]] "This compact make-up mirror is useful for reflecting light."
Reward for clearing the third Oinker camp in '''Auros Ruins'''. it can be sold to the [[Grand Fairy]] for up to 70,000 Rupees.

===Oinker Tiara===
[[File:FPTRR Oinker Tiara Sprite.png|left]] "Oinker brides traditionally wear this at their weddings."
This Oinker treasure is onew of the reward for defeating the [[Oinker Chief]] and can be sold to the '''Oinker Couple''' in the Salon of '''Auros Ruin'''s for 30,000 Rupees.

===White Snout===
[[File:FPTRR White Snout Sprite.png|left]] "Ghostly white Oinker nose. This can be used to open the ancient Oinker Gate..."
This snout opens the Oinker gate in '''Auros Ruins''' and is given to Tingle by the Salona if he agrees to help the '''Oinker Couple'''.

===Triangle Chip===
[[File:FPTRR Triangle Chip Sprite.png|left]] "Made of a material you have never seen before."
A '''Triangle Chip''' is an item found usually by digging in certain areas and sometimes dropped by enemies. '''Triangle Chips''' can be given to [[Madame Scratchcard]] to play a mini-game for [[Rupee]]s.

===Super Chip===
[[File:FPTRR Super Chip Sprite.png|left]] "Made of an amazing material you have never seen before."
A rarer version of the chip, the '''Super Chip''', can be found by digging or jumping in the water on the [[Third Continent]] and given to '''Madame Scratchcard''' to play a mini-game with even higher stakes.

===Star Sticker===
[[File:FPTRR Star Sticker Sprite.png|left]] "A prize sticker card with one star on it."
Found randomly with an '''Empty Jar'''. Return it to [[Dr. Bean]] for a '''Grown-Up Coral'''. 

===2-Star Sticker===
[[File:FPTRR 2-Star Sticker Sprite.png|left]] "A prize sticker card with two stars on it."
Found with a random '''Empty Jar'''. Giving it to '''Dr. Bean''' grants Tingle a '''Gold Gem''', a '''Ruby''' and a '''Topaz'''.

===3-Star Sticker===
[[File:FPTRR 3-Star Sticker Sprite.png|left]] "A prize sticker with three stars on it."
Found with the 23rd '''Empty Jar'''. Present it to '''Dr. Bean''' for the '''Crown Bottle Cap''' [[Rupee Good]].

===Dagger===
{{Main|Dagger}}
[[File:FPTRR Dagger Sprite.png|left]] "The blade's deadly glint shows that this is the real thing. Handle with care!"
This {{Term|FPTRR|Dagger}} can be found behind the western pirate gate of [[Cape Treasure]].

===Shoddy Knife===
[[File:FPTRR Shoddy Knife Sprite.png|left]] "Cheap Oinker knife with a badly rusted blade."

===Shoddy Lance===
[[File:FPTRR Shoddy Lance Sprite.png|left]] "This lance has a blade attached. Actually, it's just a sharpened stone..."
This weapon is dropped by the Oinkers of '''Lon Lon Meadow'''. The '''Armourer''' will haggle up to 230 Rupees for it.

===Shoddy Shield===
[[File:FPTRR Shoddy Shield Sprite.png|left]] "The Oinker insignia is displayed proudly on this shield. But it's very flimsy..."

===Low-Budget Mace===
[[File:FPTRR Low-Budget Mace Sprite.png|left]] "Considering how easy it is to make, this is quite a powerful mace."
This weapon is found by digging in the Oinker camp of the '''Deku Forest'''. The '''Armourer''' will haggle up to 480 Rupees for it.

===Tough Shield===
[[File:FPTRR Tough Shield Sprite.png|left]]"This shield has been well-coated in oil and is very strong."
This shield is found in the secret grotto of [[Auros Ruins]]. The '''Armourer''' will haggle up to 520 Rupees for it.

===Shoulder Spikes===
[[File:FPTRR Shoulder Spikes Sprite.png|left]] "Made from monster skin, Oinkers wear this spiky armour on their shoulders."
The Snow Oinkers of [[Icy Plain]] will drop this item, it can also be found in the secret grotto of '''Auros Ruins'''. The '''Armourer''' will haggle up to 2,900 Rupees for it.

===Hand-Sewn Robe===
[[File:FPTRR Hand-Sewn Robe Sprite.png|left]] "Looks like a normal robe but is actually delicately woven with intricate embroidery."
This item is a rare drop from Oinker Wizards in '''Icy Plain'''. [[Chiko's Mum]] will haggle up to 3,000 Rupees for it.

===Destructor Axe===
[[File:FPTRR Destructor Axe Sprite.png|left]] "Very big and heavy, just holding this axe tells you it's an awesome weapon."
This weapon can be found in the blue chest of '''Icy Plain'''. The '''Armourer''' will haggle up to 3,500 Rupees for it.

===Poker Lance===
[[File:FPTRR Poker Lance Sprite.png|left]] "This steel lance looks like it could do some damage..."
The '''Poker Lance''' is dropped by the Lance Oinkers of '''Auros Ruins''', it can also be found in the secret grotto on the western edge of the map. The '''Armourer''' will haggle up to 3,500 Rupees for it.

===Magical Hat===
[[File:FPTRR Magical Hat Sprite.png|left]] "This hat has magical powers, with hundreds of spells sewn into its lining."
The Yin and Yang Illusion Master Oinker Wizard will sometimes drop this hat in '''Auros Ruins'''. [[Chiko's Mum]] will haggle up to 9,800 Rupees for it.

===10-Man Slicer===
[[File:FPTRR 10-Man Slicer Sprite.png|left]] "This finely-crafted sword is clearly superior to other Oinker weaponry."
The Small Oinker Captains in '''Auros Ruins''' will occasionally drop this item. The '''Armourer''' will haggle up to 8,800 Rupees for it.

===999-Man Slicer===
[[File:FPTRR 999-Man Slicer Sprite.png|left]] "Unbelievably sharp. Imagine how many enemies have been on the wrong end of it..."
This sword is dropped by Blue and Red Oinker Captains in '''Auros Ruins'''. The '''Armourer''' will haggle up to 15,000 Rupees for it.

===Lyphos Sword===
[[File:FPTRR Lyphos Sword Sprite.png|left]] "At first glance it looks impressive, but this is a shoddy, broken sword."
Opening the secret passage in the east of '''Auros Ruins''' grants access to this sword lying near a skeleton. It can be sold to the '''Armourer''' for up to 96,000 Rupees.

===Marine Gem===
[[File:FPTRR Marine Gem Sprite.png]] "Glints with the colour of the sea. Its natural, understated beauty shines through."<br/>
This gem is dropped by [[Running Crab]]s in [[Sunshine Seashore]], the Shower Flower in '''Steamy Marsh''' and Embarrassed Statue on [[Mount Desma]]. The [[Jeweller]] will pay 80 Rupees for it.

===Amethyst===
[[File:FPTRR Amethyst Sprite.png]] "Glows with a wonderful purple colour that makes you feel like you're dreaming."<br/>
This gem is dropped by Blue Running Crabs in [[Cape Treasure]] and [[Lon Lon Meadow]]. It can also be found by digging in [[Deku Forest (First Continent)|Deku Forest]] and [[Fairy Garden]]. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 150 Rupees for it.

===Pink Quartz===
[[File:FPTRR Pink Quartz Sprite.png]] "It is the colour of cute pigs. In fact, pigs often carry this gem."<br/>
This gem is dropped by [[Pig (Enemy)#Diver Pig|Diver Pigs]] in [[Steamy Marsh]] and can be found by digging in '''Fairy Garden''' or jumping in the ocean of '''Auros Ruins'''. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 350 Rupees for it.

===Sapphire===
[[File:FPTRR Sapphire Sprite.png]] "This glowing gem is the colour of the limitless sky. It is a colour that calms the heart."<br/>
This gem can be dug up in '''Icy Plain''' and '''Fairy Garden''' and is dropped by the Rumba Statue on [[Mount Desma]]. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 550 Rupees for it.

===Emerald===
[[File:FPTRR Emerald Sprite.png]] "Glows with a gentle green light that recalls the beauty of nature and Mother Earth."<br/>
This gem grows on a tree inside mushroom ring in [[Gooey Swamp]] and is dropped by the Mambo Statue and found inside the blue chest on '''Mount Desma'''. It can also be dug up there and in '''Fairy Garden'''. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 950 Rupees for it.

===Gold Gem===
[[File:FPTRR Gold Gem Sprite.png]] "This golden gem glows with a showy, flashy light. It looks slightly tacky..."<br/>
This gem is rewarded for giving the '''2-Star Sticker''' to [[Dr. Bean]], dropped from the Frill Flower in '''Gooey Swamp''' and can be dug up on '''Mount Desma'''. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 1,400 Rupees for it.

===Ruby===
[[File:FPTRR Ruby Sprite.png]] "A bright red gemstone that glows like fire. This ruby is full of passion."<br/>
'''Dr. Bean''' exchanges this gem for the '''2-Star Sticker''', it's dropped by the Bell Bell Flower and can be found in the blue chests of '''Gooey Swamp''' and '''Fairy Garden'''. It's also found in [[Desma's Labyrinth]] behind a cracked wall in Overground Level 2 and by digging in '''Auros Ruins'''. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 2,800 Rupees for it.

===Topaz===
[[File:FPTRR Topaz Sprite.png]] "Glows like a beautiful sunset and reminds you of your old hometown..."<br/>
This gem is rewarded for giving the '''2-Star Sticker''' to [[Dr. Bean]], found in the blue chest of '''Auros Ruins''' and by digging there. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 3,800 Rupees for it.

===Small Crystal===
[[File:FPTRR Small Crystal Sprite.png]] "This delicate, clear crystal may be beautiful but beware: it's got sharp edges."<br/>
The crystal can be found in the blue chest of [[Sunshine Seashore]], is dropped by the Uneasy Statue on '''Mount Desma''' and can be dug up in [[Fairy Garden]]. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 1,300 Rupees for it.

===Snow Dust===
[[File:FPTRR Snow Dust Sprite.png]] "Very common in snowy areas, these tiny snowflakes are very romantic."<br/>
This item is a common find by digging beyond the frozen lake in [[Icy Plain]] or at the Giant Crystal. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 130 Rupees for it.

===Snow Crystal===
[[File:FPTRR Snow Crystal Sprite.png]] "The very finest gem, this glamorous crystal exudes an icy beauty."<br/>
This rare crystal can be found be digging beyond the frozen lake in '''Icy Plain'''. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 9,500 Rupees for it.

===Ancient Coin===
:''"Ancient Coin" redirects here. For the Ancient Gold Piece in {{ST|-}}, see [[Treasure#Spirit Tracks|here]].
[[File:FPTRR Ancient Coin Sprite.png|left]] "Hidden beneath the waves, it recalls the romance of times past."
This item can be Dug up from the sand of '''Sunshine Seashore''' and [[Fairy Garden]] and is dropped by the Spirited Statue on [[Mount Desma]]. '''Ancient Coins''' can be sold to [[Chiko's Mum]] for 50 Rupees apiece.

===Snatched Pearl===
[[File:FPTRR Snatched Pearl Sprite.png]] "A dazzlingly bright white pearl."<br/>
Tingle can steal these pearls from the [[Oyster]]s of '''Sunshine Seashore'''. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 100 Rupees for them.

===Forest Vitamin===
[[File:FPTRR Forest Vitamin Sprite.png|left]] "Looks like blood vessels are running through it. Unappetizing, but full of goodness."
Feed [[Vitamin Plant]]s scrap broth, and they'll start making one of these. It can be sold to [[Junglo]] for 2,000 Rupees.

===Black Ball===
[[File:FPTRR Black Ball Sprite.png]] "Extremely rare pearl. Plain at first glance, it is prized for its subtle beauty."<br/>
This rare item can be found by jumping into the ocean of [[Auros Ruins]]. The '''Jeweller''' will pay 2,000 Rupees for it.

===Baby Coral===
[[File:FPTRR Baby Coral Sprite.png]] "Small, cute new-born coral. The surface is still smooth to the touch."<br/>
This coral is dropped by the Great Reef in [[Cape Treasure]], by the Saluting Statue on '''Mount Desma''' and sometimes can be found by jumping in the ocean on the [[Second Continent]]. It can be sold to [[Chiko's Mum]] for 70 Rupees.

===Grown-Up Coral===
[[File:FPTRR Grown-Up Coral Sprite.png|left]] "Large coral which has grown over the course of many years. Rough yet still beautiful."
Obtained from giving [[Dr. Bean]] the '''Star Sticker''', this can be sold to '''Chiko's Mum''' for 3000 Rupees. Also obtainable by digging in the middle square of '''Gooey Swamp'''.

===Sharp Shell===
[[File:FPTRR Sharp Shell Sprite.png|left]] "Attractive pure white seashell with a distinctive sharp, spiky surface."
This shell is dropped by the Come-Here Statue on '''Mount Desma''' and can be sold to '''Chiko's Mum''' for 80 Rupees.

===Rainbow Shell===
[[File:FPTRR Rainbow Shell Sprite.png|left]] "The surface of this exquisite shell dazzles with all the colours of the rainbow."
This shell can be found in the blue chest of [[Lon Lon Meadow]] and in '''Icy Plain''' by jumping off the bridge to '''Gooey Swamp'''. It can be sold to '''Chiko's Mum''' for 2,200 Rupees.

===Short Fur===
[[File:FPTRR Short Fur Sprite.png|left]] "A lump of fur taken from a monster with short hair."
'''Short Fur''' is an occasional monster drop worth 35 Rupees when sold to '''Chiko's Mum'''.

===Standing Fur===
[[File:FPTRR Standing Fur Sprite.png|left]] "Unusually, this fur is standing on end and is very hard."
This fur can be dug up on '''Mount Desma''' and is dropped by the Try-Hard Statue there. It's worth 680 Rupees when sold to '''Chiko's Mum'''. 

===Giant Fur===
[[File:FPTRR Giant Fur Sprite.png|left]] "Very large high-quality fur, it is very smooth to the touch..."
The '''Giant Fur''' can be found in the blue chest of '''Cape Treasure''' and is dropped by the Icy Plain Bear and the Desma Bear in '''Icy Plain''' and on '''Mount Desma''' repectivly. It can be sold to '''Chiko's Mum''' for 1,600 Rupees.

===Glossy Fur===
[[File:FPTRR Glossy Fur Sprite.png|left]] "Fur from a monster with very fine, soft hair. You could stroke it forever..."
This fure is dropped by the Thin-Legged Camel in [[Fairy Garden]]. It's worth 2,100 Rupees when sold to '''Chiko's Mum'''.

===Thin Beard===
[[File:FPTRR Thin Beard Sprite.png|left]] "A small, thin, thread-like beard."
Occasional monster drop. Sold to '''Chiko's Mum''' for 40 Rupees.

===Stiff Beard===
[[File:FPTRR Stiff Beard Sprite.png|left]] "A very hard, jagged beard which has grown into a twisted shape."
The '''Stiff Beard''' is dropped by White Bunnies in '''Icy Plain''' and by the Beckoning Statue on '''Mount Desma'''. It can be sold to '''Chiko's Mum''' for 800 Rupees.

===Long Beard===
[[File:FPTRR Long Beard Sprite.png|left]] "This is a very long beard. You don't often see beards like this."
This beard is dropped by the Long-Limb Gibbons in '''Fairy Garden'''. It's worth 2,200 Rupees when sold to '''Chiko's Mum'''.

===Glowing Spore===
[[File:FPTRR Glowing Spore Sprite.png|left]] "Spore from a mysterious toadstool that glows with an eerie light."
This item can be dug up in [[Deku Forest (First Continent)|Deku Forest]] and is dropped by the Leg-Lifting Statue on '''Mount Desma'''. It can be sold to '''Chiko's Mum''' for 240 Rupees.

===Spore Powder===
[[File:FPTRR Spore Powder Sprite.png|left]] "Incredibly fine dry spore powder."
The '''Spore Powder''' is dropped by the Mushroom Men of [[Lon Lon Meadow]], the Mountain Mushroom Mean of '''Mount Desma''' and also the Give-Up Statue there. It can be sold to '''Chiko's Mum''' for 160 Rupees.

===Wind-Cutter Wings===
[[File:FPTRR Wind-Cutter Wings Sprite.png|left]] "Insect wings that are so thin they are see-through, but are very sturdy"
'''Wind-Cutter Wings''' are obtained by defeating the mosquito-like K. A. enemies in [[Gooey Swamp]], dropped by the Greeting Statue on '''Mount Desma''' and can be dug up there too. It's worth 330 Rupees when sold to '''Chiko's Mum'''.

===Downy Feathers===
[[File:FPTRR Downy Feathers Sprite.png|left]] "Fine, downy feathers that feel warm to the touch."
These feathers are dropped by Icy Plain Bird and found by digging around the Giant Crystal in '''Icy Plain'''. They can be sold to '''Chiko's Mum''' for 980 Rupees each.

===Glaring Eyeball===
[[File:FPTRR Glaring Eyeball Sprite.png|left]] "Whichever way you point this eyeball, it will turn to glare at you. Very unnerving."
This item is found by digging in [[Fairy Garden]] and is also dropped by the One-Eye Lizard there. It's worth 2,000 Rupees when sold to '''Chiko's Mum'''.
