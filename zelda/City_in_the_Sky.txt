The {{Term/Store|City in the Sky||Series, TP, TPHD, LCT}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 113 {{Exp Game|TP, TPHD}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|City in the Sky: Defender}}<br/>Aeralfos can fly high or attack from the ground, so watch your radar!|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> It is the seventh dungeon in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
==={{TP|-}}===
====Entrance to the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|display=City}}====
The {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon|link}} can be accessed only after paying {{Term|TPHD|Fyer|link}} 300 {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}} to repair the broken {{Term|TPHD|Sky Cannon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Listen, buddy, I suppose I might be able to fix it for you... I mean, maybe... That is, if you can pay me 300 Rupees...in advance. Whaddaya say?|Fyer|TP}}</ref> This {{Term|TPHD|Sky Cannon|display=Cannon}} must first be {{Term|TPHD|Warping|display=Warped|link}} with {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}}'s help to {{Term|TPHD|Lake Hylia|section=Twilight Princess|link}} after it is discovered beneath the {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village|display=Kakariko|link}} {{Term|TPHD|Sanctuary|link}}.

====Themes and Navigation====
Unlike the previous six {{Plural|TPHD|Dungeon|link}} in the game, the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky}} is not set anywhere in {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}}, but in the heavens above it,<ref>{{Cite|When they created the people of Hylia, they simultaneously created a new capital, a city that floated in the heavens.|Shad|TP}}</ref> where another civilization can be found.<ref>{{Cite|They dwelt there...and some scholars believe that this race lives there still, somewhere in the great sky.|Shad|TP}}</ref> This {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|display=City}} is where the {{Term|TPHD|Oocca|link}} tribe live and is the final {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} where {{Term|TPHD|Ooccoo|link}} and her son, {{Term|TPHD|Ooccoo Jr.|link}}, appear as {{Plural|TPHD|Item|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Phew! Gracious, we made it back! Finally! Welcome, adventurer! This is the city sky of the Oocca. Erm, since you came all this way... I guess I can give you a tour of the city... [...] But you're planning on walking around the city and taking it in, right?! I'm worried about the others and this is an emergency! I can't just stand around waiting! Please take me, too!|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref> It is said the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky}} prospered because of their advanced technology.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It is said the City in the Sky prospered because of their advanced technology.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=52}}</ref>

The {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|display=City}} runs on many ancient and complex machines that are far more advanced than anything found in {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule}}, including spinning propellers that keep the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|display=City}} aloft and fans that generate powerful winds. These machines use the power of the wind to its full extent; it powers every major machine that {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} comes across. The {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|display=City}} also features automatic doors.

As access to the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|display=City}} is only made possible with the {{Term|TPHD|Sky Cannon}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} enters the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} by falling into a pool of water to break his fall. To the {{Exp|GCN version; West in the Wii version|east}} of the pool is a {{Term|TPHD|Oocca Shop|display=Shop|link}} run by an {{Term|TPHD|Oocca}} that sells a few various {{Plural|TPHD|Item}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ooh, goodness... Brave adventurer, you won't believe it! There's a dragon raging outside the city walls... Ooh, I'm terribly worried about everyone... I'm going to check in that shop for survivors!|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref>

The {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} features many high-altitude platforms, including towers and narrow walkways over bottomless drops. Powerful winds outside, as well as fans inside the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|display=City}} structure, attempt to push {{Term|TPHD|Link}} off of these platforms. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can wear the {{Term|TPHD|Iron Boots|link}} or carry the {{Term|TPHD|Ball and Chain|link}} to avoid being pushed off by the wind. After {{Term|TPHD|Link}} obtains the second {{Term|TPHD|Clawshot|link}} after defeating the {{Term|TPHD|Mini-Boss}} {{Term|TPHD|Aeralfos|link}}, forming the Double {{Plural|TPHD|Clawshot}}, exploration is much easier, as the young hero can now climb between walls, as well as hold on to {{Plural|TPHD|Peahat|link}} and moving walls to advance.

The {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Boss}} is the Twilit Dragon, {{Term|TPHD|Argorok}}. After it is defeated, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}} obtain the last of the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror of Twilight|link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Shard|display=Shards}} and a {{Term|TPHD|Heart Container}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{LCT|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
In {{LCT}}, the {{Term|LCT|City in the Sky}} appears in two {{Plural|LCT|Stage|link}}. It first appears as a Target Shooting Mode scenario in {{Term|LCT|Link's Crossbow Training|section=Stage 5|display=Stage 5|link}}. {{Term|LCT|Oocca}} fly across the screen carrying {{Plural|LCT|Target|link}}. {{Term|LCT|Link}} moves to a new view after each 20 second phase of the level, but there is no remarkable difference among them.

The City also appears as a Defender Mode scenario of {{Term|LCT|Link's Crossbow Training|section=Stage 7|display=Stage 7|link}}. It takes place in the same area as the {{Term|TPHD|Argorok}} battle in {{TP|-}}, with many {{Plural|LCT|Aeralfos}} instead of an {{Term|TPHD|Argorok}}. {{Term|LCT|Link}} stays in a fixed position, but must search all 360 degrees, high and low to hunt down the {{Plural|LCT|Aeralfos}}. The {{Plural|LCT|Aeralfos}} can be grounded by shooting their wings off, but their shield is capable of blocking most oncoming shots. Alternatively, {{Plural|LCT|Bomb Arrow|link}} can destroy them in a single hit.
{{Ambig|End}}
