'''Petey Piranha''' is a character from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] and one of [[Mario]]'s foes. He is a giant, mutated [[Piranha Plant]]. Though he has not appeared in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] or its spinoffs, Petey Piranha does appear in a few games of the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], notably being the initial kidnapper of [[Princess Zelda]] in [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]] of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

==In ''Super Smash Bros.'' series==
===''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''===
Petey is the first boss of Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''. After a friendly battle between [[Mario]] and [[Kirby (character)|Kirby]] concludes on the [[Midair Stadium]], Petey kidnaps Zelda, as well as [[Princess Peach|Peach]], into cages, dangling Zelda's cage from his right arm and Peach's cage from his left arm. Petey proceeds to blast Mario away with a cannonball from his mouth. A boss fight between Kirby and Petey Piranha begins.

During his boss fight, Petey attacks by swinging his cages and by jumping into the air then landing, capable of dealing damage from landing. Kirby must attack the cages until one of them breaks open, though he can also hit Petey Piranha directly to break open the two cages; however, Kirby can save only one princess, and the match ends once either of the cages are broken. When this occurs, Petey Piranha burns in flames and is defeated. Various cutscenes in the game are affected depending on whether Peach or Zelda had been rescued, as [[Wario]] changes the other princess into a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|trophy]] and steals it.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Petey Piranha is part of [[Piranha Plant]]'s [[Final Smash]]. He wields the same cages as in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', albeit without the princesses. He can capture opponents into the cages and concludes his attack by breathing fire on them and slamming both cages onto the ground.
