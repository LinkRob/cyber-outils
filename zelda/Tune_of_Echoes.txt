The "{{Term/Store|Tune of Echoes||Series, OoA}},"<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 97}}</ref> also known as the "{{Term/Store|Echo Tune||Series, OoA}},"<ref>{{Cite|It's the {{Color|OoA Red|Echo Tune}}! It will awaken sleeping {{Color|OoA Blue|Time Portals}}!|N/A|OoA}}</ref> is a [[Song]] in {{OoA}}.

==Location and Uses==
It is the first tune that [[Link]] learns to play on the [[Harp of Ages]]. [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]]'s apparition teaches it to him when he takes the harp from her house.<ref>{{Cite|I entrust the harp to you! By playing certain tunes on the Harp of Ages, you can travel through time! Let me teach you the Tune of Echoes!|Nayru (Oracle)|OoA}}</ref>

The "Tune of Echoes" awakens the many [[Time Portals]] scattered across the land of [[Labrynna]].<ref>{{Cite|If you play that tune, you will unlock the powers of the Time Portals found across this land.|Nayru (Oracle)|OoA}}</ref> He must step into an open Time Portal to travel to the opposite age.
