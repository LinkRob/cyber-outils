==Location and Uses==
A {{Term|SSHD|Medium Quiver}} is a supplemental {{Term|SSHD|Quiver|link}} that increases {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}}'s {{Term|SSHD|Arrow|link}}-holding capacity by 10, as long as the {{Term|SSHD|Medium Quiver}} is stored in the {{Term|SSHD|Adventure Pouch|link}}.

A {{Term|SSHD|Medium Quiver}} can be created by [[Upgrading]] a {{Term|SSHD|Small Quiver|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Scrap Shop|link}}, at a cost of three {{Plural|SSHD|Monster Horn|link}}, three {{Plural|SSHD|Dusk Relic|link}}, five {{Plural|SSHD|Amber Relic|link}}, and 50 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}}. They can also be sold at the {{Term|SSHD|Item Check|link}} for 80 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee}}, or upgraded further into {{Plural|SSHD|Large Quiver|link}}.
