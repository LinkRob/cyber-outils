"{{Term/Store|Silent Princess||Series, BotW}}" is the ninth {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Silent Princess}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #9}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory}} can be unlocked by travelling northeast of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Ancient Lab Ruins|link}}, behind {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} are seen in a field together, {{Term|BotW|Link}} watching as {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} takes {{Plural|BotW|Picture|link}} of flowers with the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. She explains to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the flowers in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} are not only beautiful, they are also useful as ingredients.<ref>{{Cite|The flowers we have in Hyrule aren't just beautiful... They're also quite useful as ingredients for a variety of things.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

Something catches {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} eye, and she gasps. With a smile on her face, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} points out a {{Term|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This one here is called the silent princess.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She reveals to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that they are a rare, endangered species, and that there have been difficulties getting them to grow domestically.<ref>{{Cite|It's a rare, endangered species. Despite our efforts, we can't get them to grow domestically yet.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Because of this, she comments that these flowers can only thrive out in the wild.<ref>{{Cite|The princess can only thrive out here in the wild.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She says that all that people can do is hope they will be able to prosper on they're own.<ref>{{Cite|All that we can hope... is that the species will be strong enough to prosper, on its own.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

However, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} once again becomes distracted, reaching over and catching something in her hands.<ref>{{Cite|I don't believe it, but I actually caught one!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She reveals that the delicacy in her hands can have very potent effects under proper circumstances.<ref>{{Cite|This delicacy is known to have very, very potent effects under the proper circumstances.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> With a flourish, she reveals the critter she is holding to be a {{Term|BotW|Hot-Footed Frog|link}}, and she continues to say research shows eating one can augment certain abilities.<ref>{{Cite|Research from the castle shows ingesting one of these can actually augment certain abilities.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> While they are not in a controlled environment, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} notes that {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s level of physical fitness makes him the perfect candidate for the study.<ref>{{Cite|We wouldn't be in a controlled environment out here, and with your level of physical fitness... you'd be a perfect candidate for the study!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> As the {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}} ends, she holds the frog out to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, urging him to taste it.<ref>{{Cite|Go on! Taste it!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>
