{{Term/Store|Spring||Series, MM, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Season|link}} and mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= CE |publisher= Nintendo |page= 123 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|game=OoS|publisher=Nintendo|page=4, 117}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Spring}} goes unnamed in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Stub|MM}}

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{Term|OoS|Spring}}, the {{Term|OoS|Overworld|link}} color palette turns a light green hue. Certain lakes and rivers flood, rising the {{Term|OoS|Water (Terrain)|link}} level to cover obstacles and create whirlpool currents. Flower patches will appear, and normally-impervious flower buds will bloom. Similarly, {{Plural|OoS|Blast Bloom|link}} will open, allowing access to higher areas.

It is the third {{Term|OoS|Season}} required to be controlled with the {{Term|OoS|Rod of Seasons|link}}, and is granted by the {{Term|OoS|Spirit of Spring|link}}.
