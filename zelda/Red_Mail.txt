The {{Term/Store|Red Mail||Series, ALttP, ALBW}},{{Exp Game|ALttP, LANS, ALBW}}<ref name="E 135"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|LANS Red|Powerful red mail}}|N/A|LANS}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Red Clothes||LA, LADX}},{{Exp Game|LADX}}<ref name="E 135"/> are recurring [[Defense]] [[Clothes]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
The Red Mail is the third Mail [[Link]] can wear in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' and is the best defense. It is found in [[Ganon's Tower (A Link to the Past)|Ganon's Tower]] in the [[Dark World]]. When this tunic is worn, it reduces damage taken by half over the [[Blue Mail]], therefore a quarter of normal damage, making the young hero durable.<ref>{{Cite|You found the Red Mail! This provides even better protection than the Blue Mail!|N/A|ALttP}}</ref> Additionally, it turns Link's cap purple.

==={{LA|DX|-}}===
In {{LA|DX}}, the Red Clothes, unlike other appearances, double the damage of Link's [[Sword]] attacks and sends [[enemies]] flying when he hits them,<ref name="E 135"/> acting like a permanent [[Piece of Power]], except that his walking speed remains the same. They are accessible only after completing the special [[Color Dungeon]] hidden in the [[Graveyard]], where the [[Fairy Queen]] will allow Link to choose between the [[Blue Clothes|Blue]] or Red Clothes. Once he selects one of the special tunics, he can switch to the other by speaking to the Fairy Queen again. The Blue Clothes doubles Link's defense.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
The Red Mail in {{ALBW}} is typically found in a [[Big Chest]] hidden on the fourth floor of [[Lorule Castle]]. By extinguishing the torches in the area, an invisible platform leading to the chest will be illuminated. If Link has not yet obtained the Blue Mail, it will instead be in this chest; the Red Mail can then be found in the [[Swamp Palace (A Link Between Worlds)|Swamp Palace]]. As in ''A Link to the Past'', Wearing the Red Mail will reduce the damage Link takes by a further half over the Blue Mail's defense.<ref>{{Cite|You got the red mail! Damage from enemies has been halved again!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* The Red Mail is based on the effects of the [[Red Ring]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]''.
* The [[Goron Tunic]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' strongly resembles the Red Mail/Clothes and it adds resistance from heat and fire-based attacks. However, it doesn't grant [[Link]] actual defense nor increases attack power. 
* If Link wears the Red Clothes in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|Link's Awakening DX]]'' and then acquires a [[Piece of Power]], his attack strength becomes four times the normal amount. When the hero obtains the [[Koholint Sword]], it can inflict 8 times the regular sword's, resulting in very powerful strikes.
* The character [[Gramps]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', despite appearing to be wearing Link's [[Green Clothes]], actually uses the Red Mail in battle, though it's not visible in gameplay.
