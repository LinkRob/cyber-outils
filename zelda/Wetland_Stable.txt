{{Term/Store|Wetland Stable||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|'''Wetland Stable'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} located in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}} along {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}}; it rests north of the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|link}} and northwest of the {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable}} is operated by {{Term|BotW|Lawdon|link}}, who runs both the {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}-tending service and the inn.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to our {{Color|BotW Blue|stable}}. If you've come with a wild horse, you can register it with us here. Or if you want to stay for a {{Color|BotW Red|rest}}, please head to the counter inside.|Lawdon|BotW}}</ref> Just outside the entrance, {{Term|BotW|Yolero|link}} trains with his "Master {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}}," which he claims to be the legendary {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} that is given to the chosen hero.<ref>{{Cite|For I am Yolero, the hero of legend chosen to wield the Master Torch! Well, if you'll excuse me, I need to go save the world.|Yolero|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Ami|link}} sits next to the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} and reluctantly gives directions to the east, west, and north.<ref>{{Cite|My life is a joke. OK, let's get this over with. What do you want to know about the surrounding area?|Ami|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the east, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} replies simply with {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}; if he asks about the west, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} tells him about {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} and the fabled {{Term|BotW|East Passage|link}} that leads directly into the Castle;<ref>{{Cite|There's a hidden escape route from Hyrule Castle called the {{Color|BotW Red|East Passage}}. I hear it leads directly into the castle. It was made for the old royal family though, so who knows if it's still usable.|Ami|BotW}}</ref> if he asks about the north, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} replies with {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You know—{{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}} and some other junk.|Ami|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to leave, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} asks if he would like to know what is to the south. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says yes, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} complains that he would like to know as well, but that he is never allowed to the leave the {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|You want to know what's south of here?|Ami|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|[Yeah.] Me too, but I never get to leave this stable. If you really want to know, you should go ask at {{Color|BotW Blue|Riverside Stable}}.|Ami|BotW}}</ref>

Inside the tent, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a poster depicting a recipe for an {{Term|BotW|Electro Elixir|link}}, as well as the {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill: Volume 5|link}} on a table. Behind the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} sits the {{Term|BotW|Kaya Wan Shrine|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Hestu|link}} may also appear here while on his way back to {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Quince|link}} can be found with his pet {{Term|BotW|Hylian Retriever|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Satty|link}} watching the east; the latter can be befriended to find a metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}} in the woods nearby.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 349}}</ref>

As with all other {{Plural|BotW|Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} can be found sitting just outside {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable}}'s entrance, but he will retreat inside when it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}}. {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} can be found playing his accordion in front, and he mentions the {{Term|BotW|Kaya Wan Shrine|link}} nearby.<ref>{{Cite|Did you see the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} near this stable? I know quite a bit about the {{Color|BotW Red|shrines}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He then asks if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} would like to hear a song about {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} from 10,000 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|I happen to know a song about the ancient hero. It was passed down to me by my teacher. Do you care to hear it?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cambo|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Endai|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kazul|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rik|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Spoone|link}} stop at this {{Term|BotW|Stable}} on their journeys. After {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves {{Term|BotW|Leekah|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Rebonae Bridge|link}}, she will head to the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} for safety.<ref>{{Cite|Saved again... Am I so wrong for just wanting to sit by the river? It's getting so I can't even take a walk anymore. That's life these days, I guess. Whatever. I'm going back to the stable.|Leekah|BotW}}</ref>

One of the stablehands, {{Term|BotW|Izra|link}}, can be found looking into the {{Term|BotW|Water|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hylia River}} next to the remains of an old {{Term|BotW|Wagon|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Izra}} will point out a shape in the {{Term|BotW|Water}} that he believes is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can't believe I'm about to share this with you, but I'm absolutely stumped. Have you seen that strange shape down at the bottom of the river? It's hard to make out, but I'm pretty sure it's a treasure chest.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> He has been trying to figure out how to pull it ashore with little luck,<ref>{{Cite|But I can't figure out how to fish it out of there. I guess I need...some kind of machine or something to get it out.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> and he is so curious about its contents that he has been losing sleep.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so curious about what's inside that chest, I can't even sleep.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> To complete the "{{Term|BotW|Riverbed Reward|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must pull the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Inside, he will find a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Broadsword|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells {{Term|BotW|Izra}} that he found treasure inside, he will be frustrated that he lost his opportunity to become rich.<ref>{{Cite|[It was treasure.] Amazing treasure! Ahh! That's so frustrating! If I had just managed to get that chest, I'd be rich! And the ladies would be tripping over each other to be with me!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} informs {{Term|BotW|Izra}} that it was a sword, he claims he did not want the treasure.<ref>{{Cite|[It was a sword.] What? A cool sword, you say? Whatever. I didn't even want that life-changing treasure anyway...|Izra|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to keep his {{Term|BotW|Knight's Broadsword}} as a reward.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already completed "{{Term|BotW|Find the Fairy Fountain|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Pikango|link}} can be found painting {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Captured Memories|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They say the castle was destroyed 100 years ago. Somehow it looks even more majestic in ruins...|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> He will be happy to see {{Term|BotW|Link}} again and offers him help in identifying {{Plural|BotW|Picture|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! We meet again. Are you out traveling as well? I've traveled all over the world, so I'm happy to tell you about new places, if you want. If you have a picture to show me, let's see it already!|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> In this location, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to show him the twelfth {{Term|BotW|Picture}} in his {{Term|BotW|Album|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} is able to identify as a forest northeast of the {{Term|BotW|Bottomless Swamp|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That forest... I get the feeling I've seen it before. I can't say for certain, but I believe this may depict the forest just south of here. No... Wait... Maybe it's the forest on the other side of the river, northeast of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Bottomless Swamp}}?|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>
