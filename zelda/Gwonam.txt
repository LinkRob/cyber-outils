{{Term/Store|Gwonam||Series, TFoE}} is a character in {{TFoE}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=As you speed towards Koridai on Gwonam's magic carpet, you have only one thought: find Ganon and defeat him.|game=TFoE|page=2}}</ref>

==Biography==
Gwonam is a wizard from the island of {{Term|TFoE|Koridai|link}}. When [[Ganon]] and his evil forces conquer the island, Gwonam flies to [[King Harkinian]]'s palace on his magic carpet to warn the King and [[Link]] of the siege.<ref>{{Cite|Your Majesty, Ganon and his minions have seized the island of Koridai!|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref> Gwonam comes for Link's aid specifically, as a written prophecy claims that Link is the only one who can defeat Ganon.<ref>{{Cite|It is written: only Link can defeat Ganon.|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref> He beseeches that Link hurry and bring only his [[Sword]]. Together they fly back to Koridai as Gwonam explains to Link his task. He instructs that Link must clear the island's "Faces of Evil"; mountains that are being controlled by Ganon's minions as their base.<ref>{{Cite|These are the Faces of Evil. You must conquer each.|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref>

Gwonam accompanies Link throughout his adventure by transporting him around Koridai.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Gwonam is about to drop you off somewhere in Koridai. Where would you like to go?|game=TFoE|page=2}}</ref><ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Tell Gwonam where you'd like to start by moving the cursor over one of the three open areas and Button One.|game=TFoE|page=2}}</ref> He also rescues Link should he fall in battle.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=When you lose all your hearts for the third time, Gwonam picks you up and returns you to the map.|game=TFoE|page=7}}</ref> Gwonam also appears in several cutscenes and points out the bosses and their heinous actions against the islanders. At [[Firestone Lake]], he and Link discover that [[Princess Zelda]] has been kidnapped by Ganon during Link's absence. After defeating {{Term|TFoE|Lupay (Boss)|link}}, Link finds the {{Term|TFoE|Crystal of Vision|link}} and locates the {{Term|TFoE|Hermit Flat|link}}, where Gwonam lives alone.<ref>{{Cite|At last, you have the vision to find my house!|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref> Gwonam gives Link the {{Term|TFoE|Lantern of Vision|link}} to help him see the invisible [[Sancrumi]]es that swarm the way to [[Ganon's Lair]].<ref>{{Cite|Now you will see the Sancrumies that prevent your approach to Ganon! Go, with many blessings!|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref>

After Link defeats Ganon and wakes Zelda from her cursed sleep, Gwonam teleports them outside. He happily states that Koridai's peace has been restored and proclaims Link as the hero of Koridai.<ref>{{Cite|As it is written, you Link, are the hero of Koridai!|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*In the area where Gwonam introduces {{Term|TFoE|Glutko (Boss)|link}}, Gwonam is located two screens from the lava cavern's entrance. In the game's European back cover however, Glutko's introduction is seen with the cave entrance as the background. This suggests that Gwonam may have originally been located at the cave entrance in early versions of the game.
*Gwonam shares similarities with [[Ganondorf]]. They are both wizards with a background inspired by Arab Bedouins, possess a visibly green skin tone, and wear gems on their heads. Given that Gwonam predates Ganondorf's first appearance by a number of years, this is likely a coincidence.
