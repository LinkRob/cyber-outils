"{{Term/Store|Master of the Wind||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Master of the Wind}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Horon Lagoon}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Horon Lagoon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} singing about "tempestuous wind."<ref>{{Cite|Tempestuous wind...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When he approaches, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} is shocked to see someone else in such a remote area, though he realizes he should not be surprised when it comes to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I was sure that I would be alone in so remote a place. Pardon me for not greeting you sooner. Oh ho! It's you! Although I guess I shouldn't be surprised to see you here.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He knows an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" pertaining to the {{Term|BotW|Horon Lagoon|display=Lagoon}}, and he asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} would like to listen.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will begin to sing the song.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's hear it.] Excellent! Without further ado...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics describe an individual breaking rocks above the bay and landing on the an using "wings of cloth and wood".<ref>{{Cite|He breaks the rocks that serve to bind,<br/>Above he tempestuous bay.<br/>On wings of cloth and wood entwined,<br/>He lands on the altar to open the way.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Kass}} believes that the bay is referring to {{Term|BotW|Horon Lagoon}}, he is unsure what the wings of cloth and wood are.<ref>{{Cite|The "tempestuous bay" from the song could be this very bay. But what sort of wings are made of cloth and wood?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He does not know if he is capable of solving this riddle.<ref>{{Cite|It's a juicy riddle, but one I may never puzzle out.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well,<ref>{{Cite|Take care, and may the light illuminate your path.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Horon Lagoon}} is filled with many winds, though their paths are blocked by piles of rocks. Using {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can destroy these piles, which will allow the wind to flow through. After all of the wind is freed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to fly along with the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}, which will allow him to land on the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} nearby. Doing so causes {{Term|BotW|Shai Yota Shrine|link}} to emerge from the ground,<ref>{{Cite|After harnessing the wind and landing atop the dais, a shrine appeared.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
