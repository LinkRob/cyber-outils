{{Term/Store|Face Shrine||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is the sixth {{Term|LADX|Dungeon|link}} in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 148}}</ref> It is the location of the {{Term|LADX|Coral Triangle|link}}. The entrance, located in the {{Term|LADX|Face Shrine|link}} region, is settled on a piece of land next to the cliffs separating it from the {{Term|LADX|Rapids Ride|link}}. It is surrounded by some {{Plural|LADX|Armos|link}}, much like the area with the {{Term|LADX|Southern Face Shrine|link}}, but to a lesser extent. The item hidden in this {{Term|LADX|Dungeon}} is the {{Term|LADX|L-2 Power Bracelet|link}}, while the mini-boss is a beast called [[Rover]]. The {{Term|LADX|Boss|link}} is {{Term|LADX|Facade|link}}.

==Entrance to the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine|display=Shrine}}==
Before going to the {{Term|LADX|Face Shrine}}, {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} is directed to the {{Term|LADX|Southern Face Shrine|link}} by the {{Term|LADX|Owl|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hoot! There are two shrines, one to the north, the other to the south. First, head south, where ancient ruins speak of the Wind Fish... You will learn much there...|Owl|LADX}}</ref>

Inside the {{Term|LADX|Southern Face Shrine}} is a large {{Term|LADX|Armos Knight|link}} that {{Term|LADX|Link}} has to defeat. After obtaining the {{Term|LADX|Face Key|link}} inside and reading the inscriptions on the wall, {{Term|LADX|Link}} travels to the northern {{Term|LADX|Face Shrine}}. In the {{Term|LADX|Water|link}} in the {{Term|LADX|Face Shrine}} region, {{Term|LADX|Link}} finds a much smaller island that he can access. An {{Term|LADX|Armos|link}} is blocking a stairway, which leads to the piece of land where the {{Term|LADX|Face Shrine}} stands. After putting the {{Term|LADX|Face Key}} into the {{Term|LADX|Face Keyhole|link}}, the {{Term|LADX|Face Shrine}} rises from the ground and become accessible.

It is a region on the eastern side of {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}, although prior to {{LA|NS}} it formed the north-western section of a larger region that also encompasses the {{Term|LANS|Shrine Waterfall}} in the east, and the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins}} to the south.

The regions are comprised of two related shrines: the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine}} in the north, and the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins}} in the south that features a relief depicting the origins of the island.<ref name="Owl">{{Cite|Hoot! There are two shrines, one to the north, the other to the south. First, head south, where ancient ruins speak of the Wind Fish... You will learn much there...|Owl|LADX}}</ref> A lone [[Armos]] can be found on a small island in the water, covering stairs that descend into a cave that leads directly to the eponymous shrine.

Initially Link can only pass through a small passage along the southern edge. The rest of the area can only be accessed after navigating a small maze in the [[Shrine Waterfall]] to the east.

==Themes and Navigation==
The interior of the {{Term|LADX|Dungeon|link}} is mostly brown flooring with pink walls. One region is flooded by swallow {{Term|LADX|Water|link}}. An important obstacle in this {{Term|LADX|Dungeon}} is large elephant statues, which are too heavy to be lifted with the {{Term|LADX|Power Bracelet|link}}, but can be lifted with the {{Term|LADX|L-2 Power Bracelet|link}}.

[[Rover]], the mini-boss, attacks exclusively by throwing a large ball at {{Term|LADX|Link|link}}, and is invulnerable to conventional attacks. {{Term|LADX|Link}} must throw the ball at the creature after dodging its attacks. The mini-boss room is located in a secret room that is not on the {{Term|LADX|Dungeon Map|link}}; it is in the "eye" of the "face" that the {{Term|LADX|Dungeon}} forms.<ref>{{Cite|Enter the space where the eyes have walls...|Owl Statue|LADX}}</ref> It can only be accessed by bombing the wall in the room to the south of it. The other eye also hides a room.

Many of the puzzles of the {{Term|LADX|Dungeon}} revolve around {{Plural|LADX|Switch Block|link}} that can be raised and lowered by striking {{Plural|LADX|Crystal Switch|link}}. Another puzzle is just past the mini-boss room, where a normal looking room is located. This room has an exit to the north, however it warps {{Term|LADX|Link}} back to another room to the south of it. The correct exit to this room is a stairway beneath an elephant statue.

The {{Term|LADX|Boss}} of the {{Term|LADX|Dungeon}} is {{Term|LADX|Facade|link}}, a giant face in the floor. {{Plural|LADX|Flying Tile|link}} and {{Plural|LADX|Pot|link}} in the room attack Link, then pits start to appear. Its weak point is its face, which can be hurt using {{Plural|LADX|Bomb|link}} when it appears.

When looked on the {{Term|LADX|Dungeon Map}}, the {{Term|LADX|Dungeon}} is in the shape of a face. There are also a few rooms that have {{Plural|LADX|Block|link}} inside which make up the likeness of a face.

The dungeon's music was redone in the Nintendo Switch remake, now using a dramatic strings section. On every even-numbered loop of the track, the original chiptune melody is incorporated.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In volume 1 of the {{LA|Cagiva}}, after defeating {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Karuna|link}}, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Owl|link}} tells {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} to go to {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Face Shrine}}, much to {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Felicia|link}}'s surprise. In volume 2, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} fights a {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Stalfos|link}} once inside the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Face Shrine|display= Shrine}}. Once defeated, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Stalfos}} comments that if {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}}'s will is imperfect, he will be defeated by his own self. While thinking about what the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Stalfos}} said, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} finds a mural of the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Wind Fish|link}} written in an unknown language. After touching it, he hears a voice which reveals that, when the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Wind Fish}} wakes up, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Koholint Island|link}} will disappear. After feeling lied to by {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Felicia|link}} and the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Owl}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} leaves {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Face Shrine}} to seek answers.
