"{{Term/Store|Playtime with Cottla||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Playtime with Cottla}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Kakariko Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
During the day, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Cottla|link}} running around {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} by herself.<ref>{{Cite|Run, run!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> When he speaks to her, she complains about how bored she is and asks when dinner will be.<ref>{{Cite|When's food?! I'm BORED!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers to play with her, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} will become excited and ask what he wants to play.<ref>{{Cite|[Wanna play?] {{Big|YAAAY!}} What'll it be? What'll it be?|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses a game, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers to play hide-and-seek, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} will offer to hide.<ref>{{Cite|[Hide-and-seek!] {{Big|YAAAAY!}} I'll go hiiiide!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> However, she reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she needs to return to her sister, {{Term|BotW|Koko|link}}, when it is time for dinner.<ref name=
"Dinnertime">{{Cite|But I've gotta go home to Sis when it's dinnertime, OK?|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> Once she hides, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} can be found behind {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link}}.<ref name="Caught">{{Cite|Boo! You found me!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref>

On the other hand, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses tag, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} decides to be the runner.<ref>{{Cite|[Tag!] {{Big|YAAAAY!}} I'll start running!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> In this scenario, she also informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she needs to be with {{Term|BotW|Koko}} for dinner.<ref name="Dinnertime"/> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} catches up to her, she will pout about being caught.<ref name="Caught"/>

Despite losing the game, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} will still be happy that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was willing to play with her.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|THAT WAS SO FUN, THOUGH!}}|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> As a reward for winning, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} will offer {{Term|BotW|Link}} a piece of {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}}, which was formerly her secret treasure. She may also offer a piece of fossilized amber. <ref>{{Cite|This is my secret treasure... But here, you can have it! Crunchy crunchables!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}. The side quest can be completed multiple times to get more salt/amber
