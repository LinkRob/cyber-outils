The '''Look-What-I-Found Kid''' is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR, RTBToL}}

==Biography==
==={{FPTRR|-}}===
The {{Term|FPTRR|Look-What-I-Found Kid}} sells {{Plural|FPTRR|Map|link}} to {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} and can be found near the beginning of every major area in {{FPTRR}}, with the exception of {{Term|FPTRR|Hometown Prairie|link}} and {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Garden|link}}. The Maps he sells to {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} are incomplete, which the latter can use to map the missing landmarks and sell the completed map to the {{Term|FPTRR|Map-Seller|link}}.
