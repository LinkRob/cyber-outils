'''Gloom Monsters''' are an enemy variant in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]. These types of [[Monsters]] are found in the Depths and do [[Gloom]] damage which Link cannot heal unless in sunlight or with sunny Gloom foods. Most monsters have variants of Gloom. These Monsters also have more health and usually drop [[Zonaite]] while bosses and stronger enemies drop crystalized charges.
