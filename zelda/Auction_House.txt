The {{Term/Store|Auction House||Series, TWW, TWWHD}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259}}</ref> also known as the '''Hall of Wealth''',<ref>{{Cite|Word is, they hold an auction every night over there on Windfall Island. Every night the people gather in the Hall of Wealth to amuse themselves by spending a little money.|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> is a location in {{TWW}}.{{Note|[[Tingle]] refers to the Auction House as the '''House of Wealth''' via the [[Tingle Tuner]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite|House of Wealth|Tingle|TWW}}</ref> However, as the Tingle Tuner was removed in {{TWWHD}}, this name is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
At the beginning of the game, the Auction House is owned by [[Mila]] and [[Mila's Father|her father]] on {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}. Mila's Father allows [[Zunari]] to hold a [[Windfall Auction]] on the first floor every night.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, it might interest you to know that I've been renting out the first floor of my home as a so-called auction house.|Mila's Father|TWW}}</ref> However, when he gives the remainder of his money to [[Tetra's Pirate Crew]] and becomes poor, {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie|link}} and her [[Maggie's Father|Father]] become rich from the [[Skull Necklace]]s Maggie received from a [[Moblin]] named [[Moe]], and they begin living in their House. Maggie and her Father live on the second floor, and allow the Auctions to continue on the first. Link can give [[Maggie's Father]] 20 [[Skull Necklace]]s in exchange for [[Treasure Chart 2]]. If Link breaks one of the [[Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase]] on the first floor, he will receive a warning,<ref>{{Cite|You little WEASEL!!! Well, thank you SO much for coming into my home and breaking my high-priced vases! ...If you break any more, I assure you that I shall not let it go lightly! No, sir! Not at all! I shall require compensation!|Mila's Father|TWW}}</ref> but will be forced to pay 10 [[Rupee]]s for every subsequent Vase he breaks.<ref>{{Cite|Do you think you can just break someone's vases and leave without paying for them? I must chortle at your naivete! Ho ho! Those happen to be extremely high-class bone-china vases that run a minimum of 10 Rupees each!|Mila's Father|TWW}}</ref> However, once Maggie and her Father buy the mansion, they will no longer charge this fee.

==Trivia==
*This is one of only two Houses in the game that allow Link to use the Sword indoors, the other being [[Orca]]'s House.
