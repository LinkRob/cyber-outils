The {{Term/Store|Ends of the Earth Station||Series, ST}} is a location in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 290}}</ref>

==Location==
The {{Term|ST|Ends of the Earth Station}} can only be accessed after {{Term|ST|Link|link}} brings five {{Plural|ST|Cucco|link}} to {{Term|ST|Rael|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Sand Sanctuary|link}}. As a sign of gratitude, he will reward the young hero with a {{Term|ST|Force Gem|link}} that reveals the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} to the {{Term|ST|Ends of the Earth Station}}.

==Features and Rules==
In this Station are three caverns featuring intricate block puzzles, similar to ones seen in the {{Term|ST|Sand Temple|link}}, that require aid of the {{Term|ST|Sand Wand|link}}. The levels are called Brainteaser,<ref>{{Cite|Brainteaser Block Puzzle|Sign|ST}}</ref> Braintwister,<ref>{{Cite|Braintwister Block Puzzle|Sign|ST}}</ref> and Brainmasher Block Puzzles,<ref>{{Cite|Brainmasher Block Puzzle|Sign|ST}}</ref> with their names being a sort of "beginner, intermediate, and expert" indicator. Each level contains three puzzles. After completing the levels, {{Term|ST|Link}} will gain access to {{Plural|ST|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a common {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}, a {{Term|ST|Heart Container|link}}, and a rare {{Term|ST|Treasure}}, respectively. {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link}} will then compliment {{Term|ST|Link}}'s puzzle-solving skills.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, you were really amazing, {{Player Name}}! I'm no good at this sort of puzzle, so I'm really impressed with you!|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref> Moreover, the young hero will also find a {{Term|ST|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|ST|Big Green Rupee|link}} on his way out of each cavern.

==Trivia==
In the European version, a small reference to {{ALttP}} is made here: Brainteaser, Braintwister and Brainmasher puzzles each have a small inscription referencing to different swords of {{ALttP|-}}. Brainteaser is said to be for Master Puzzlers ({{Term|ALttP|Master Sword|link}}), Braintwister for Tempered Puzzlers ({{Term|ALttP|Tempered Sword|link}}) and Brainmasher for Golden Puzzlers ({{Term|ALttP|Golden Sword|link}}).
