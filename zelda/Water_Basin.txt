==Location and Uses==
When [[Link]] first meets [[Faron (Dragon)|Faron]] the Water Dragon, she can be found recuperating inside the basin full of sacred water due to the injuries she received from [[Ghirahim]].<ref>{{Cite|The wounds he's given me are stubborn things. I have been forced to recuperate in this basin of {{Color|SS Blue|sacred water}}.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref> However, since the healing properties of the sacred water have been diminishing, Link sets out to obtain more water from one of the [[Skyview Spring|Temple Springs]].<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, the healing properties of the sacred water have diminished, and I am now in need of another dose to freshen up my healing bath.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref>

Later on, the basin is necessary to access the [[Fire Sanctuary (Skyward Sword)|Fire Sanctuary]]. Since the basin is too big for Link to carry, [[Scrapper]] is able to do the job, but he cannot protect himself when bringing the Basin to the entrance of the Fire Sanctuary.<ref>{{Cite|The basin has a handle designed to aid in its transportation. However, because of its size and weight, I do not believe it could be carried by a human.|Fi|SS}}</ref> When reaching the end of the Volcano Summit area, Link will have to protect Scrapper as he is holding the Basin from numerous enemies. At the entrance of the Fire Sanctuary, Scrapper will pour the water in the Basin into the mouth of the mechanical frog head, thus granting access to the Fire Sanctuary.
