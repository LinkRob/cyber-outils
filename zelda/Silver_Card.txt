The {{Term/Store|Silver Card||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|ST|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 138}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Silver Card}} is earned after racking up 200 points in {{Term|ST|Beedle’s Air Shop|link}};<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh! You have 200 points! You're now an official {{Color|ST Light Red|Silver Member}}!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Beedle}} will send {{Term|ST|Link|link}} the {{Term|ST|Silver Card}} through the {{Term|ST|Postman|link|display=mail}}.<ref>{{Cite|I’ll send your Silver Card tomorrow, so be sure to check your mail.|Beedle|ST}}</ref> Alongside the {{Term|ST|Silver Card}}, {{Term|ST|Link}} will also receive a {{Term|ST|Freebie Card|link}} as well as a permanent 10% discount on all future purchases.<ref name=”Rewards”/><ref>{{Cite|Silver Membership Notice! Thank you for continuing to support my stores. (Hi, it's me! Beedle!) Your recent purchase now puts your points at 20. (Yay for you!) Thus, your membership has been upgraded to Silver. (Yay again!) As thanks, please find attached a {{Color|PH Red|Freebie Card}}. (Oooooh, free!) Use it at any of my stores.<br/><br/>-Beedle (Bye!)|Silver Membership|PH}}</ref> The {{Term|ST|Freebie Card}} will allow {{Term|ST|Link}} to choose any {{Term|ST|Item}} in {{Term|ST|Beedle}}’s shop and get it for free. <ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|ST Light Red|Freebie Card}}! Choose any item you want, and you'll get it for free!|N/A|ST}}</ref> After earning the {{Term|ST|Silver Card}}, {{Term|ST|Beedle}} tells {{Term|ST|Link}} to shoot for earning the {{Term|ST|Gold Card|link}} next, which is worth 500 points.<ref>{{Cite|Shoot for the Gold Membership next!|Beedle|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Earn {{Color|ST Light Red|500 points}}, and you’ll get the Gold Card!|Beedle|ST}}</ref>
