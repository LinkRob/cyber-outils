{{Term/Store|Deya Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Deya Lake'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Deya Lake}} is located in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. It sits at the base of the {{Term|BotW|Hills of Baumer|link}}, northwest of {{Term|BotW|Popla Foothills|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins|link}} lie in the center, which are little more than the flooded remains of a few {{Plural|BotW|Ruined House|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found by examining the {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}} that travel along the surface of the Lake at the end of the large, hollowed-out log left behind from the hollow {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} stump on the hill. He gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}} in return for finding him. Another [[Korok]] can be found at the hollowed out [[Tree]] stump on the top of the hill by following/touching all the yellow flowers and the final white flower, which the flowers go from the hollow [[Tree]] stump to the hollowed-out log. The [[Korok]] gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}} in return for finding him.
