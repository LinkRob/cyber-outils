{{Term/Store|West Castle Town||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|West Castle Town|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
West Castle Town is located on the eastern side of the [[Hyrule Castle Town Ruins]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. The landscape is destroyed and covered in deep pools of [[Malice]].

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing a bundle of five [[Bomb Arrow]]s can be found inside the bastion along the southern wall. A second stone Treasure Chest containing a bundle of five [[Fire Arrow]]s can be found along the western wall east of the [[Katah Chuki Shrine]]. A third stone Treasure Chest containing another bundle of five Bomb Arrows can be found inside the bastion along the western wall. Another bundle of five Bomb Arrows can be found in a stone Treasure Chest inside the northwestern bastion. Atop the northernmost wall, Link can find a bundle of five [[Ice Arrow]]s inside a stone Treasure Chest.

A [[Korok]] can be found hiding beneath a [[Rock]] at the southwestern wall along the road to the [[Quarry Ruins]].
