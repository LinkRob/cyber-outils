The {{Term/Store|Rock Spire Shop Ship||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a shop in {{TWW}}.<ref name="Letter">{{Cite|{{Letter|TWW|Rock Spire Shop Ship}}|Letter|TWWHD}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TWWHD|Rock Spire Shop Ship}} is a special instance of {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Shop Ship|link}} that appears only at {{Term|TWWHD|Rock Spire Isle|link}}. It is operated by {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant|link}}, who is in fact just {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle|link}} in a comically obvious disguise. The sides of the ship are draped in purple cloth that cover the paint of {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle}}'s likeness. 

When {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} enters the shop for the first time, {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant}} pretends not to know him from before. He claims to be holding a "clearance sale" on account of the shop's imminent closure in seven days.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! A fine welcome to you, young person
that I've never seen before! You're so lucky to meet me at this time! This shop will close in just {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|seven more days}}, and I'm having a clearance sale.|Beedle's Assistant|TWW}}</ref> This turns out to be a marketing ploy—the day after closing day, reopens the shop and holds a "Grand Reopening Sale" where the prices are no different from before.<ref>{{Cite|OHHHHH! A fine welcome to you! We've remodeled and reopened! Hoo-rah! And right now, we're having a special Grand Reopening Sale! Have a look around!|Beedle's Assistant|TWW}}</ref> He claims to have closed the shop for interior remodeling,<ref>{{Cite|I didn't lie! I closed...and completely redid the inside of my shop. Tah-dah! It's reborn!|Beedle's Assistant|TWW}}</ref> but in reality he merely changed the drapes to the ones seen in other {{Plural|TWWHD|Beedle's Shop Ship}}.

The wares of the {{Term|TWWHD|Rock Spire Shop Ship}} are mounted on {{Plural|TWWHD|Skull|link}}. A hanging {{Term|TWWHD|Lamp|link}} casts gloomy shadows from above. This, some clothes hanging on the back wall, and the drapes (before the remodeling) are the only visual differences from other Shop Ships. The ship itself moves more rapidly than the other Shop Ships.{{Clarify|TWW|reason= How much faster exactly?}}
