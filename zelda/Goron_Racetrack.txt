The {{Term/Store|Goron Racetrack||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 246}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Goron Racetrack is a raceway where the [[Goron Race]] is held every spring. However, the entrance to the Racetrack is blocked by a large boulder, so the [[Powder Keg]] is required to access it.<ref>{{Cite|If you can destroy the boulder that blocks the entrance to the Goron Racetrack near here... using the Powder Keg I'm about to give you, then I'll approve you to carry them.|Goron|MM}}</ref>

The Goron Racetrack has many obstacles placed on the raceway. Trees can be found growing on the track, while several [[Bomb Flower]]s are planted throughout the course. Prior to starting a race, many [[Magic Jar]]s are placed around the track, which must be picked up to prolong [[Goron Link]]'s roll at high speed. The Racetrack crosses itself a few times, with the stone bridges that make up these crossings not having any guard rails. There are many vantage points throughout the track from which spectators can watch a race.

The Racetrack is always open for Link to participate, and, if he wins the race, he will be awarded with [[Gold Dust]].
