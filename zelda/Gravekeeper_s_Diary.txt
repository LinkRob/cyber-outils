The {{Term/Store|''Gravekeeper's Diary''||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an object in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|OoT3D Red|gravekeeper's diary}} is here. Do you want to read it?|N/A|OoT}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The diary is found in [[Dampé]]'s house in the [[Kakariko Village Graveyard#Ocarina of Time|Kakariko Graveyard]]. The text in the diary differs depending on whether it is read by child or adult Link.

During the past, [[Link]] can only read it at night while Dampé is in the graveyard digging. The diary mentions that Dampé found a [[Hookshot|treasure]] while digging a hole.<ref>{{Cite|When I dug a hole, I found a treasure that stretches--BOING! and shrinks--BOING! It's so fun, I'll never give it to anybody!|Gravekeeper's Diary|OoT}}</ref> During the future, after Dampé's death, his shed becomes open for Link to go inside freely. The diary now mentions that whoever reads it must enter Dampé's [[?|grave]], whereupon he will let them have his treasure.<ref>{{Cite|Whoever reads this, please enter my grave. I will let you have my stretching, shrinking keepsake. I'm waiting for you.|Gravekeeper's Diary|OoT}}</ref>
