The {{Term/Store|Trial|Trials|Series, TMC}} is an event in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's up to you to pass this {{Color|TMC Blue|trial}} safely and recover the {{Color|TMC Red|item}} you require.|Librari|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Trial}} is held by {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}, and begins when {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} return the three overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}} and reach him on the second floor {{Term|TMC|Bookshelf|link}}. They are searching for a way to enter the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Librari}} opens a secret panel in the floor of his abode that sends them plummeting down into the sewers of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. Here, they can find the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}} after defeating a pair of {{Plural|TMC|Scissors Beetle|link}}.
