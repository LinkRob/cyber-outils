{{Term/Store|Blue Goo Specter|Blue Goo Specters|Series, LANS|plural}} are enemies in {{LA|DX}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= DAB |publisher= |page= 115}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Blue Goo Specter}} were referred to as {{Term/Store|Blue Camo Goblin|Blue Camo Goblins|LADX}} in {{E}}.<ref name="E 170">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 170}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{LA|NS}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|LANS|Blue Goo Specter}} are only found in the {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon|link}} where they hide in blue colored tiles and pop up to attack {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} when he gets near them.<ref name="E 170"/> They are less common and slightly faster than their {{Plural|LANS|Green Goo Specter|link|display=Green}} and {{Plural|LANS|Red Goo Specter|link|display=Red}} counterparts.
