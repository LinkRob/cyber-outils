==Location and Uses==
Roasted Hearty Bass is the result of [[Hearty Bass]] that has made contact with [[Fire]]. It is said to have crispy skin and just the right amount of fat content to create an exceptionally tasty meal.<ref name="Inventory"/> Occasionally, Blue {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} will drop Roasted Hearty Bass when they are killed. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can restore three {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} by eating a Roasted Hearty Bass.
