'''Doppels''' are animate dolls that appear in {{TFH}}.<ref name="Dolls">{{Cite|Do you see those wooden dolls? Those are no ordinary wooden dolls. They are Doppels. Those sacred dolls serve as a vessel for a hero's soul. This allows you to vacate your body and take over theirs.|Doppel Master|TFH}}</ref> They are used to fulfill the role of the other two [[Links]] in a single-player game.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= You can also play the game solo, using two doll-like Doppels to fill in for the other Links. You'll control each hero at a time to go through dungeons.|retrieved= October 11, 2015|url= http://www.zelda.com/tri-force-heroes/#team-up|title= North American Tri Force Heroes site|type=}}</ref> Control can be switched between the Doppels by tapping the [[Doppel Panel]] on the bottom screen.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= Your player name will be displayed on the panel corresponding to the character you are in control of. Tap a Doppel Panel to switch to that Doppel.|game= TFH|page= 14}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Doppels are wooden dolls that resemble the Links, but differ in that they appear to wear masks that resemble the [[Spooky Mask]]. Doppels act as vessels for a hero's soul, allowing Link to vacate his body and enter theirs.<ref name="Dolls"/> They are used to accompany Link to the [[Drablands]] in a single-player game, wherein two Doppels are provided in place of the Links in [[Multiplayer]] modes, as three heroes are required to activate the [[Triforce Gateway]] to enter the Drablands.<ref>{{Cite|This is a Triforce gateway. Using its power is the only way to travel to the Drablands. Only when three heroes gather will the Triforce's power awaken and allow for travel between the worlds. The way will not open for one hero alone.|Match Master|TFH}}</ref> The Doppels are kept in the [[Hall of the Doppels]] in [[Hytopia Castle]], a room separate from the multiplayer communications lobby.<ref>{{Cite|This is the Hall of the Doppels... It is a place beyond the constraints of the known world. A sacred place for heroes to train in solitude.|Doppel Master|TFH}}</ref> The [[Doppel Master]] keeps watch of the hall. Whenever Link wishes to venture into the Drablands, the Doppel Master will awaken the Doppels and bind them to his will.<ref>{{Cite|To depart for the Drablands in the company of Doppels, speak with me and I will bind them to your will.|Doppel Master|TFH}}</ref>

Though only one Doppel can be possessed by Link at a time, his soul can be transported to another Doppel at any time by tapping the [[Doppel Panel]]. When in a [[Totem]] formation, the top Doppel can be commanded to use a [[Sword]] or an [[Items in Tri Force Heroes|Items]] by the Link at the bottom. Doppels cannot normally be damaged by external influences unless they are presently being possessed by Link. However, if a Doppel falls into a pit, Link will lose a [[Heart]].<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= Doppels cannot move on their own, but they cannot be damaged by enemies either. Your hearts will decrease, however, if they fall into a pit.|game= TFH|page= 14}}</ref> Doppels cannot be dressed in any [[Outfit]]s, and default to the [[Hero's Tunic]]. As the Doppels are commanded by one being, Link and the Doppels also share the same [[Energy Gauge]] despite it normally being separate during multiplayer. Doppels in the Hall of the Doppels cannot exit the lobby, unless the current Doppel is the green-garbed one.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= You cannot exit the lobby as a Doppel. Switch to the green-garbed hero if you want to leave.|game= TFH|page= 14}}</ref>

During single-player while using the Doppels, the option to skip a [[Level]]'s Stage is given. This will use up one of the three [[Fairy|Fairies]] Link is provided with, which will bypass all obstacles and transport Link and the Doppels to the next Stage. Doing this has consequences, however, as skipping Stages will take away all of the Rupees Link has collected thus far. It will also decrease his bonus Rupees at the end of the Level, and one [[Treasure Chest]] will have its [[Material]] be replaced by 20 Rupees for every Fairy used. Also, Levels that are cleared but have had Stages skipped will not be marked as completed on the [[Drablands Challenge#Completion List|Drablands Challenge Completion List]] in the castle lobbies.
