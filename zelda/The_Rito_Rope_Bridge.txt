==Overview==
With the blizzard ended, Gesane the guard of {{Term|TotK|Rito Village|link}} considers rebuilding the rope bridge that was previously destroyed. Juannelle offers to assist. She says that a skilled carpenter in {{Term|TotK|Lookout Landing|link}} might be able to rebuild the bridge, but she couldn't recall who the name of the person was. She only remembered that the person's name ends with ''"-son."''

Overhearing them, {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} heads to Lookout Landing to find a suitable carpenter. There he finds Karson, who tells Link he is out of a job after finishing the {{Term|TotK|Skyview Tower|link}}. Link relayed the tip of the destroyed rope bridge at Rito Village. Dilligent to build things again, Karson embarked to Rito Village.

Back at Rito Village, Karson was discussing with everyone on how to rebuid the bridge. Turned out they do not have any {{Term|TotK|wood|link}} to work with. So Link collected wood for them and gave it to Karson. With the acquired wood, Karson was able to repair the bridge.

As thanks for help, Gesane gave Link a {{Term|TotK|Silver Rupee|link}}.
