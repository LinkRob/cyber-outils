The {{Term/Store|Pure Metal|Pure Metals|Series|plural}} are {{Plural|PH|Item|link}} in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|We will never recognize you as a true brother of the Goron tribe, stranger! Do you really want our {{Color|PH Red|pure metal}}?! Then just try to keep up with me!|Gongoron|PH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Main|Aquanine, Azurine, Crimsonine}}
The three Pure Metals are used to forge the [[Phantom Sword]],<ref>{{Cite|I see...you have gathered the three pure metals. Sail to Zauz the blacksmith and ask him to forge the true sword you must wield.|Astrid|PH}}</ref> the only weapon capable of defeating [[Bellum]].<ref>{{Cite|You will need all of the pure metals to defeat the very darkness itself.|Oshus|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Crimsonine! It's one metal you need to make the sacred sword. Only a sword forged of the three pure metals has the power to defeat Bellum!|N/A|PH}}</ref> The '''Crimsonine''' can be found in the [[Goron Temple]] after defeating [[Dongorongo]]. The '''Azurine''' is located in the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}} after defeating [[Gleeok]] and breaking the curse on the [[Yook]]. The '''Aquanine''', which was a sacred treasure kept in [[King Mutoh]]'s tomb, can be found in [[Mutoh's Temple]] after defeating [[Eox]]. Once all three Pure Metals are found, they can be taken to [[Zauz]] to forge the Phantom Sword.<ref>{{Cite|The Crimsonine, the Azurine, and the Aquanine. I've never seen metal like this. I'll bet I can forge a mighty sword with them.|Zauz|PH}}</ref> The Pure Metals share the same symbol adorned on the face of the [[Temple of the Ocean King]].
