{{Term/Store|Kotts||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kotts|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].''

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kotts}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} child who can be found in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. She is the second-eldest daughter of {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Amali|link}}, the younger sister of {{Term|BotW|Notts|link}}, and the older sister of {{Term|BotW|Genli|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cree|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Kheel|link}}.<ref name="Sisters"/> At night, she sleeps in her home alongside her sisters.<ref>{{Cite|Hrmm...|Kotts|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, he can find {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} in {{Term|BotW|Kaneli's House|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to go visit the elder. Want to come along?|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> She admits that she is scared of the "bird monster" in the sky, and she asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} is also scared.<ref>{{Cite|I...I'm scared of the {{Color|BotW Red|bird monster}}. I hope it doesn't eat me... Did the {{Color|BotW Blue|village elder}} tell you about it? Are you scared of it too?|Kotts|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Aren't you scared of the {{Color|BotW Red|bird monster}}? You don't look very scared.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} claims not to be scared, {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} is shocked and wonders if he could be a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Not scared at all.] Whoa! Really? Are you a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champuon}} or something?|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} that he is scared, she will become hopeless, since he is bigger than her.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm a little scared.] But you're much bigger than me! If you're scared, I'm doomed! What should I do?|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> She gets the idea to feed {{Plural|BotW|Fish|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} until it is too full to eat her.<ref>{{Cite|OH! I've got it! I'll feed it lots of fish until it's too stuffed to eat me!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> However, she becomes concerned that it will try to eat her when she goes to feed it the {{Plural|BotW|Fish}}.<ref>{{Cite|AHHH! But what if when I try to give it the fish, it eats me instead! *gulp*|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} is confused about what she means by "bird monster," {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} tells him to talk to {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Bird monster?] You haven't heard? Then you should go talk to the elder.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> She promises to hold his hand if the story gets too scary for him.<ref>{{Cite|You can always ask me to hold your hand if the story gets too scary.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> When she leaves {{Term|BotW|Kaneli's House}} for the day, {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} remarks that she has to return to her home quickly or else she might be eaten by the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Got to get home quick or the bird monster might eat me!<br/>♪La dee DAAAAAH!♪|Kotts|BotW}}</ref>

After calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kotts}}' sister, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}, at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest|link}}. She complains that {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} and the rest of their sisters promised to rehearse with her, but none of them showed up.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Grrrrrr! My sisters are dumb DUMMIES!}} They promised we'd rehearse together, but NO ONE showed up!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> She asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} and remind her sisters about their rehearsal, as she thinks they will listen to a "scary-looking {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}" over herself.<ref>{{Cite|Can you go tell my sisters in Rito Village to stop ditching rehearsal? They won't listen to me, but they might listen to a scary-looking Hylian like you.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> This starts the "{{Term|BotW|Recital at Warbler's Nest|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} at the ponds in front of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, where she is fishing for {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hiyah! HIYAH!<br/>I'm fishing for {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}}!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reminds her about her rehearsal, she is confused at first.<ref>{{Cite|...Huh? What are you doing here? What? Rehearsal?|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> However, when she realizes what he is talking about, she explains that she forgot due to her focus on catching {{Plural|BotW|Fish}}.<ref>{{Cite|AHHH! I totally forgot!<br/>I was too focused on catching these fish.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> Afraid that {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} will kill her, {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Salmon}} so he can make {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Genli}} in the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kheel's going to kill me! Please take this {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}} and go make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> She hopes that {{Term|BotW|Genli}} will feel like singing after she eats it.<ref>{{Cite|Hopefully she'll feel like singing after she has some.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref>

Before she leaves the pond, {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} wants to try to catch some more {{Plural|BotW|Fish}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to try to catch some more fish before I head out.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> In the meantime, she encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to try and find the rest of her sisters.<ref>{{Cite|Why don't you go find my other siblings in the meantime?|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> She shares that {{Term|BotW|Notts}} like places that are high and {{Term|BotW|Cree}} is at {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|My oldest sister loves {{Color|BotW Red|high}} places, and Cree is probably at the {{Color|BotW Red|Slippery Falcon}}.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has gathered all of the ingredients, she tells him to go to the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}} to make {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière}} in the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Once you get all the ingredients, you can cook the meal in the pot in the {{Color|BotW Red|kitchen}}! ♪Cookie cook cook!♪|Kotts|BotW}}</ref>

Once all of the sisters have been gathered, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}} to find them all singing together.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, thank you so much! All of my sisters are here!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} explains that they are practicing a song passed down in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} that was sung at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}} in ancient times.<ref>{{Cite|Now we can rehearse here at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}}! Want to know what song we're singing? Well...the {{Color|BotW Blue|elder}} told me... It's a song passed down in the Rito Village that was once sung at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}} in ancient times.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kotts}}, she will accidentally mess up the song.<ref>{{Cite|Hiyah! HIYAW! Oh, no... I messed up!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> Promising not to mess it up again, she promises to perform it for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll try not to mess up my song. Do you want to hear it?|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, the sisters will sing for him.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} uncover {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} will excitedly point it out to {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} and her other sisters.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! Hey look, guys!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} notices that it is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} told them about.<ref>{{Cite|No, that's that "shrine" thing that the elder was talking about!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Notts}} says that they should inform their mother,<ref>{{Cite|Let's tell Mom!|Notts|BotW}}</ref> to which {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} declares that the last one to reach {{Term|BotW|Amali}} is an unhatched egg.<ref>{{Cite|Last one to mom is an unhatched egg!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> The five sisters will then fly back to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.

Back in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find the sisters singing together near {{Term|BotW|Akh Va'quot Shrine|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} actually wants to catch {{Plural|BotW|Fish}}, but she is afraid {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} will get mad at her if she does not practice.<ref>{{Cite|I want to go catch some fish, but Kheel will get mad at me if I don't practice my singing.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> She sings about {{Plural|BotW|Fish}}, and she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if there were any inside the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪Fishy fish fish!♪ So how was the inside of that shrine? Were there lots of fish there?|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} says he has not gone inside yet, {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} tells him to bring her back some ancient {{Plural|BotW|Fish}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Haven't gone in yet.] What, you haven't gone yet? Well what are you waiting for? Go and bring me back some ancient fish!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> After they finish singing, {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} hopes she will have time to catch {{Plural|BotW|Fish}} tomorrow.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe I'll have time to catch some fish tomorrow.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Kass}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, he will join his daughters by playing the bandoneon as they sing. {{Term|BotW|Kass}} claims that they refuse to leave him alone for the whole day.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to hear the song again, you'll need to come back at nighttime. That's the only time my kids will leave me alone...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotts}} reveals that her father taught her to catch {{Plural|BotW|Fish}} by dive-bombing them.<ref>{{Cite|Dad taught me how to catch fish. You just dive-bomb them while they're swimming!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> However, she has trouble getting out of the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} afterwards, since she does not know how to {{Term|BotW|Swimming|link|display=Swim}}.<ref>{{Cite|But the hard part is getting back out of the water. I don't know how to swim...|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> She also encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Swimming|display=Swim}} right up to {{Plural|BotW|Fish}} and grab them.<ref>{{Cite|When you spot a fish, swim right up to it and nab it!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref>

In Tears of the Kingdom, Kotts now runs the [[Swallow's Roost|Swallows Roost Inn]].
