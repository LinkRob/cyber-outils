{{Term/Store|King Harkinian||Series, TWoG}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="TWoG Manual">{{Cite manual|quote=Your father, King Harkinian, sailed to assist the Duke months ago and then disappeared.|game=TWoG|page=3}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TLoZ|-}} ''TV Series''===
{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian}} makes an appearance in {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. In the show, he is depicted as a tall, rotund king bearing a snow white beard and a gold crown. Personality-wise, he is a benevolent and charismatic King of the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Hyrule|link}}, wishing to build his citizens a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Royal Water Park|link}} that they can all enjoy.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Eh? What? Oh, yes, hot. Most certainly. Still, my Royal Water Park should be completed soon, then all the kingdom will have some place to cool off and have fun.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=Fairies in the Spring}}</ref> Deeply loved by his daughter, the King seems to have trouble remembering certain things,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Adventurer! Adventurer...didn't we have one of those around someplace?|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=The White Knight}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=You won't get away with this, you know. No, Zelda and um, oh, Link--yes, yes that's his name--will um, uh, rescue me.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=Sing for the Unicorn}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Eh? Oh, yes. I forgot. But she'll be back for the opening of my water park.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=Fairies in the Spring}}</ref> and is somewhat dimwitted. However, he is still loyal to his country and also refuses to willingly give up the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}}, even though he fears the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}} ruler.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=We may not have a chance. Once Ganon discovers the, uh, Triforce isn't here, he'll be mad...|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=That Sinking Feeling}}</ref>

{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian}} often gives {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} tips on wooing his daughter, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}}, such as in {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing for the Unicorn|link}}", where he tells {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} to give {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zelda}} flowers in order to have a better chance of getting a kiss from her.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= No. You need to take flowers, THEN you might succeed. Yes, girls are crazy about flowers. [...] Good luck, my boy!|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=Sing for the Unicorn}}</ref> The king only appears in four episodes out of the twelve: "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|The White Knight|link}}", "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing for the Unicorn|link}}", "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|That Sinking Feeling|link}}", and "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairies in the Spring|link}}." In all of his episode appearances, he only plays a small role, in which two of those he gets kidnapped, prompting {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and {{Term|TLoZ|Princess Zelda}} to rescue him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=We must rescue my father.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=Sing for the Unicorn}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Please, Triforce, my father has been captured, but we don't know who did it or where he is!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=Fairies in the Spring}}</ref>

==={{TLoZ|-}} ''(Valiant Comics)''===
[[File:Valiant Harkinian.png|thumb|right|King Harkinian in the comic]]
In the {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}, {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|King Harkinian}}'s appearance is similar to that of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. He only appears in "[[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 1#Missing in Action|Missing in Action]]" and ''[[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 4#Queen of Hearts|Queen of Hearts]]'', and plays a rather minor role. He lives in the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|North Palace|link}} with his daughter, {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Princess Zelda|link}}, and he seems to be good friends with {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Queen Seline|link|display=Seline}}, the queen of the neighboring kingdom of {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Calatia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Seline...? Seline! It's been...SIXTEEN YEARS! You haven't changed a bit! No...you're even more beautiful!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=3}}</ref>

In ''Missing in Action'', {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|King Harkinian}} is the one that informs {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}} that {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Zelda}} left {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Hyrule|link}} with the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} to keep it safe from {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon|link}}'s clutches, and he urges the young hero to find her and bring her back.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=My daughter Zelda has taken the Triforce of Wisdom and '''left''' Hyrule forever! Zelda said that the only way to keep the Triforce of Wisdom safe from Ganon is for her to take it far away and never return! [...] I could not stop her! [...] You '''must''' find her and convince her to return, Link!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=2 [Missing in Action]}}</ref> In ''Queen of Hearts'', {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Queen Seline}} goes to {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|North Palace}} and meets up with {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|King Harkinian}} after 16 years, where the two of them spend most of the day together catching up.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Oh...can't that wait, darling? I'm spending the day with Seline. It's been sixteen years, you know! So much to catch up on!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=3}}</ref>

==={{TFoE|-}}===
{{Term|TFoE|King Harkinian}} makes a short appearance in {{TFoE}}. He tells {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}} that he should be happier with the kingdom's peacefulness after {{Term|TFoE|Link}} complains of his boredom.<ref>{{Cite|My boy, this peace is what all true warriors strive for!|King Harkinian|TFoE}}</ref> When {{Term|TFoE|Gwonam|link}} arrives to inform the King that the island of {{Term|TFoE|Koridai|link}} has been seized by {{Term|TFoE|Ganon|link}}'s minions, he readily offers their help. However, {{Term|TFoE|Gwonam}} states that only {{Term|TFoE|Link}} can defeat {{Term|TFoE|Ganon}} according to a prophecy.

==={{TWoG|-}}===
{{Term|TWoG|King Harkinian}} plays a large role in {{TWoG}}. He announces that {{Term|TWoG|Gamelon|link}} and its ruler, {{Term|TWoG|Duke Onkled|link}}, are under attack by the evil forces of {{Term|TWoG|Ganon|link}} and he plans to go to aid {{Term|TWoG|Duke Onkled}}.<ref name="TWoG Manual"/> {{Term|TWoG|Zelda|link}} objects out of concern for his safety, but {{Term|TWoG|King Harkinian}} reassures her that he will take the {{Term|TWoG|Triforce of Courage|link}} to protect him. He orders that {{Term|TWoG|Link}} be sent if he does not return in a month. Despite {{Term|TWoG|Impa|link}}'s {{Term|TWoG|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} promising that the King would safely return, he fails to do so, and {{Term|TWoG|Link}} is sent to search for him. After months of no word back from either of them, {{Term|TWoG|Zelda}} decides to go to {{Term|TWoG|Gamelon}} with {{Term|TWoG|Impa}} to find them. It is later revealed that {{Term|TWoG|Duke Onkled}} betrayed {{Term|TWoG|King Harkinian}} and {{Term|TWoG|Ganon}} had him captured.<ref>{{Cite|Duke Onkled betrayed the King!|Lord Kiro|TWoG}}</ref> {{Term|TWoG|Zelda}} manages to free her father after defeating {{Term|TWoG|Ganon}}, and {{Term|TWoG|King Harkinian}} orders that {{Term|TWoG|Duke Onkled}} will scrub all the floors in {{Term|TWoG|Hyrule}} as punishment. The group then decide to have a celebratory feast after {{Term|TWoG|Link}} appears.

==Trivia==
*In a bonus short for [[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 3|{{TLoZ|-}} (Valiant Comics) Issue 3]], the King is named Hyrakian.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Firstborn and only child of Hyrakian, the King of Hyrule, Princess Zelda was raised by her nursemaid Impa and trained at an early age to assume the duties of a princess.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=30 [[The Price]]}}</ref> The king's name would be changed to Harkinian in [[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 4|the next issue]].
*As he only appears in spin-off materials such as the animated series, the Valiant comics, and the CD-i games, his most-likely canon counterpart is the [[King of Hyrule#A Link to the Past|King of Hyrule]] seen in {{ALttP|-}}, as no king is seen in the NES games outside of the backstory of {{TAoL|-}}.
*He is never verbally referred to by name in either of the CD-i games, nor in the animated series.
