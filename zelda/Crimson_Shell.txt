{{Term/Store|Crimson Shell|Crimson Shells|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 120"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Crimson Shell}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Ice Cavern|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Frozen Plateau|link}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Crimson Shell}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Snowball Ravine|link}}''', ''"Adventure in the dark!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Crimson Shell}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Snowball Ravine}}''', ''"Transport the orb quickly!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Crimson Shell}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Silver Shrine|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Crimson Shell}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Silver Shrine}}''', ''"Halved attack and defense!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Crimson Shell}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Ice Temple|link}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Crimson Shell}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Crimson Shell}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make {{Term|TFH|Hammerwear|link}}.<ref name="E 120"/>

==Trivia==
*The description of {{Plural|TFH|Crimson Shell}}, "A mysterious shell that never misses a foe when thrown." is a reference to {{smw|Red Shell}}s from the {{smw|Mario Kart}} series.<ref name="E 120"/>
