The {{Term/Store|Stone Ship||Series, PH}} is an item in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Stone Ship|book= E |publisher= |page= 284}}</ref>{{Note|This term refers only to a specific combination of {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}. In-game, the ship itself is always called the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}}.}}

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|PH|Stone Ship}} is the name given to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} once it has been outfitted with the following {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Stone Ship}}

The {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} can be attached to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} at any time by visiting the {{Term|PH|Shipyard|link}} on {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}. For every three parts of the {{Term|PH|Stone Ship}} used, the durability of the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} is increased by one Heart. When all eight parts are used together, they increase its durability by three Hearts, up to a total of seven Hearts. Aside from this boost in durability, the {{Term|PH|Stone Ship}} is merely a change in appearance to suit the player's personal tastes.
