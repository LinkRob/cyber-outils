The {{Term/Store|Heart Ring L-1||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Heart Ring L-1<br/>Slowly recover lost Hearts|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Heart Ring L-1 is received from the [[Great Fairy]] found behind the waterfall at the [[Temple of Seasons]] after telling her the [[Temple Secret]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee|Rupees]]. When worn, it slowly recovers [[Link]]'s [[Heart|Hearts]] when moving.<ref name="Description"/> The Heart Ring L-1 can be obtained in {{OoA}} by telling the secret received from the Great Fairy to [[Farore]], or by transferring it by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
