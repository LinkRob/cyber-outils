{{Term/Store|Rito Soldier|Rito Soldiers|Series, HWAoC|plural}} are characters and {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
{{Improve}}
{{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Soldier}} first appear as enemies in The Champion Revali, attacking Link and his allies due to mistaking Terrako for Harbinger Ganon. They then appear in many missions, notably Air and Lightning, where they help Link and his allies to save Revali from Windblight Ganon, and All Hyrule United, where they, along with the rest of the allied forces, fight to break through to the castle. They can be equipped with Feathered Blades or Feathered Spears, but they tend to use Bows, being the only Soldier type to use similar size ranged groups to their melee groups. They also form up into columns when they do this. Outside of {{Plural|HWAoC|Scenario|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Soldier|link}} also appear in {{Plural|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} such as {{Term|HWAoC|Fierce Gale of the Rito|link}}.
