The {{Term/Store|Lucky Lobby Ball||Series, TFH}} is an object in {{TFH}}. It is used to relieve boredom while waiting for other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} to join.<ref>{{Cite|Have you ever suffered from feelings of boredom while waiting for friends to join your team? YOU HAVE?! Well, those days are now behind you!|The Lucky Lobby Ball (Book)|TFH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball}} can be found in any of the {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}'s [[Multiplayer]] lobbies by dashing into a wall by holding the L or R buttons.<ref>{{Cite|Simply press L/R to dash into a wall, causing the Lucky Lobby Ball to drop.|The Lucky Lobby Ball (Book)|TFH}}</ref> This occasionally causes the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball}} to drop to the ground from the ceiling; the chances of the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|display=Ball}} appearing are increased while waiting for other {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to join the game. {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} may then hit the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|display=Ball}} with his {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}, causing a [[Mini-Game]] of sorts to commence.<ref>{{Cite|Continuously hit this magical ball of luck and joy with your sword to play a legendary song for your amusement!|The Lucky Lobby Ball (Book)|TFH}}</ref> A random song from {{TLoZ|Series}} plays while {{Term|TFH|Link}} attempts to keep the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|display=Ball}} in the air using his {{Term|TFH|Sword}}, which requires timing and quick reflexes.<ref>{{Cite|Do you have the timing and skill to play through all of the songs? There's only one way to find out!|The Lucky Lobby Ball (Book)|TFH}}</ref> The longer {{Term|TFH|Link}} keeps the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|display=Ball}} in the air, the faster the music plays, which also causes the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|display=Ball}} to fall more quickly. If allowed to touch the ground, the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|display=Ball}} bursts and the music stops, forcing the game to end. As the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|display=Ball}} bursts, a number appears, showing the total number of hits. Depending on this score, a number of {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} may also appear, usually equal to one tenth of the number of hits, rounded up. {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} may occasionally also appear when the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|display=Ball}} first drops from the ceiling.

Multiple {{Plural|TFH|Links}} may also join the game, although only one {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball}} can appear at a time. If a {{Term|TFH|Links}} wearing the {{Term|TFH|Timeless Tunic|link}} strikes the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball}}, an 8-bit version of the song will be heard by all {{Plural|TFH|Links}} present. The song will return to its normal version when a different {{Term|TFH|Links}} hits the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|display=Ball}}. If a {{Term|TFH|Links}} touches the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball}} while wearing the {{Term|TFH|Cacto Clothes|link}}, the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|display=Ball}} will burst instantly.

There are 23 songs in total than can be played by the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball}}.<ref name="Songs">{{Cite|There are five potential songs in the demo version of the game and 23 in the full version.|The Lucky Lobby Ball (Book)|TFH}}</ref>
