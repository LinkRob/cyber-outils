{{Term/Store|Harrow Island||Series, PH}} is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 284}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Harrow Island is hidden north of a triangle of rocks in the northwest of the southeastern sea. Its location is revealed by a [[Yook]] on the [[Isle of Frost]] after [[Link]] has obtained the [[Azurine]].<ref>{{Cite|So you know about Harrow Island, right? It's this small island west of here...|Yook|PH}}</ref> It does not appear on the [[Sea Chart]] until he sails close enough for [[Ciela]] to spot it and mark its location.<ref>{{Cite|Look there! That island isn't marked on our charts, Link! And it looks like we can dock there! There! I've just added the isle to your chart!|Ciela|PH}}</ref>

Harrow Island is home to a [[Digging Game]]. It is separated into two by a wall that spans the width of the Island. The southern half contains a geyser that becomes active once Link pays to play the minigame. This allows him to walk on top of a raised section, where there are a few [[Jar]]s, as well as access to the northern half where the minigame takes place. This area is a large, empty field divided into four parts by stone tiles.

The minigame is run by a [[Gossip Stone]] north of where Link docks the [[SS Linebeck]]. For a fee of 50 [[Rupee]]s, Link can dig up 10 treasures and keep them.<ref>{{Cite|The fee is only 50 Rupees. The only rule: you can dig up just 10 treasures.|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref> The treasures that Link can find are Rupees, [[Treasure Map]]s, and [[Rupoor]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Just between you and me, I can say that a treasure map is buried out there!|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, I almost forgot: among our treasures are unlucky prizes for unlucky souls.|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref> The Gossip Stone will notify Link when he has dug up his tenth treasure.<ref>{{Cite|OK! Thanks for playing! You've dug up 10 treasures and thus end your session! Please return to the entrance!|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref> Link can leave the Island at any time, but his fee will not be refunded.<ref>{{Cite|You can leave without finishing. But I can't refund your fee.|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref> If after 10 treasures have been dug up Link digs two more times, he will be fined 100 Rupees by the Gossip Stone at the entrance.<ref>{{Cite|I warned you so much not to dig, but still you did! So... As a penalty, I will take 100 Rupees from you! Now don't do it again!|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref> If he digs once more, he will be fined all of his current Rupees leaving him with nothing.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, no! You dug one more time! As a penalty, I will collect all the Rupees you got!|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref> If Link digs again, he will be banned from playing.<ref>{{Cite|How many warnings is it going to take?! I'll tell you. NO more warnings! Because you're now banned from playing here!|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref> To play again, he must pay 300 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Then if you're really sorry, we will let you play again for a cost of 300 Rupees. Want to play again and have your ban lifted?|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref>

Occasionally, a spirit named [[Lucky Lee]] will be dug up.<ref>{{Cite|A strange voice rises from deep inside the hole...|N/A|PH}}</ref> If Link pays it 50 Rupees, it will give him a choice to pick between a large or a small [[Treasure Chest]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm {{Color|PH Blue|Lucky Lee}}, the lucky spirit that dwells on this island! I'm here to bring you luck! If you pay me 50 Rupees, you can win big, big, big! Want to try your hand at my game of luck?|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well, then! Let's start! Say there was a {{Color|PH Red|large treasure chest}} and a {{Color|PH Red|small treasure chest}}... Which do you choose?|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref> These Chests may contain a small prize of 40 Rupees,<ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|PH Red|tiny winner}}! And so you win a consolation prize of 40 Rupees. Come again!|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref> an average prize of 150 Rupees,<ref>{{Cite|You're a {{Color|PH Red|so-so winner}}! Yep, sorta lucky, fella! You won 150 Rupees! Come again!|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref> or a large prize of 200 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|You're a {{Color|PH Red|big winner}}! You're one lucky fella! You won 200 Rupees! Come again!|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref> The Treasure Chests may also contain nothing, in which case Link must pay an additional 100 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, no! You lost! You must pay 100 Rupees! Come again!|Lucky Lee|PH}}</ref>
