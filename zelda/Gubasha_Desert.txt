'''Gubasha Desert''' is a location in {{ZA}}. It is an expansive desert in the northeast of [[Tolemac]], consisting of two areas. The '''North Gubasha Desert''' is a coastal plain near the sea, whereas the '''South Gubasha Desert''' is a vast canyon wasteland, and is home to a small desert village. The two regions connect from the north and the south.

==Features and overview==
===North Gubasha Desert===
North Gubasha Desert is a coastal canyon located close to the sea. The region can be entered by taking an alternate route through a tunnel shortcut from [[Ubato Hills]] near the [[Shrine of Destiny]] entrance to the west. It is the location of the [[Shrine of Air]], where a giant, purple condor flies close to some caves and will fly [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] to the Shrine entrance. At the far end of the northern cliff is a woman. However, she apologizes as she has no information to give.

Close to the condor's location is a cabin occupied by its [[House Keeper|keeper]]. He invites Zelda to join him by the fire,<ref>{{Cite|Come here, sit with me! Warm yourself by the fire!|House Keeper|ZA}}</ref> adding that the Desert is known to get very cold.<ref>{{Cite|I know it can get colder than Ganon's heart up here, but I've endured worse...|House Keeper|ZA}}</ref> Near the tunnel entrance is a [[Mystic|Mystic Man]] who offers Zelda a riddle in finding the Shrine of Air.<ref>{{Cite|Something you seek, is not of this world, but above it...|Mystic Man|ZA}}</ref> He then adds that she must look east to find [[Aviana]], the boss of the Shrine of Air.<ref>{{Cite|... Defeat the Woman of the Air, it is to the east you must go.|Mystic Man|ZA}}</ref>

===South Gubasha Desert===
South Gubasha Desert is located north of the East Crossroads of the [[East Forest]]. It is a dry, desert wasteland that sits next to the eastern sea of Tolemac, which can be seen from the coastal cliffs of the region. In the northern half of the desert is a small desert oasis, where a [[Fairy]] lives who will replenish all of Zelda's [[Heart]]s when approached.

The southern half of the desert is home to a small village. The settlement is mostly deserted with only a couple villagers left. Its villagers, however, are not very welcoming to Zelda, and provide her with no advice and instead discourage her. A man named [[Myntoll]] sits in the village square, but refuses to provide Zelda any information to aid her quest.
