{{Term/Store|Rupee Boulder|Rupee Boulders|Series, ALBW|plural}} are {{Plural|ALBW|Object|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALBW|publisher= Prima|page= 108}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|ALBW|Rupee Boulder}} are {{Term|ALBW|Rupee|link}}-laden {{Plural|ALBW|Boulder|link}} that randomly appear in place of regular {{Plural|ALBW|Boulder}}. They are found most commonly on {{Term|ALBW|Death Mountain|link}} where they are seen rolling down narrow pathways inside of {{Plural|ALBW|Cave|link}}, while some can be found planted in the ground. They can be destroyed with the {{Term|ALBW|Hammer|link}} while grounded or rolling, which will cause them to drop several {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}. Occasionally, {{Plural|ALBW|Magma Bomb|link}} erupting from the mountain will also consist of {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, which scatter when they smash into the ground.
