{{Term/Store|Carok Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Carok Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Carok Bridge is a wooden bridge located east of the [[Breach of Demise]] that spans over the [[Regencia River]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. The ruins of an unnamed village can be seen along the eastern side of the Bridge.

Two [[Treasure Chest]]s can be found here in [[Master Mode]]. The first is a wooden Treasure Chest containing a [[Diamond]], situated atop platform suspended by [[Sky Octorok]]s south of Carok Bridge. The second is a metallic Chest containing a Royal Guard's Claymore, also atop a platform north of the Bridge.

A Korok can be found by breaking the [[Pot]] suspended by a chain underneath the Bridge's northern side. A second Korok can be found beneath a [[Rock]] atop a rock formation near the eastern side of the Bridge.
