{{Term/Store|Melari's Mine||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Melari's Mine}} was referred to as '''Melari's Mines''' in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Melari's Mines|N/A|TMC}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Melari's Mine}} is located at the apex of {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}. It is inhabited by {{Term|TMC|Melari|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} blacksmith, and his seven {{Term|TMC|Mountain Minish|link}} apprentices who followed him from the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|These seven students followed Melari from Minish Village to Mount Crenel. Their song is actually a sign that they are full-fledged Mountain Minish now.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> The area is rich in iron and {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}}, for which the {{Term|TMC|Mountain Minish}} dig along the interior and sides of the {{Term|TMC|Melari's Mine|display=Mine}} itself.<ref>{{Cite|Ting along, tong along! Diggin' holes for Kinstones!|Mountain Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Y'know, iron's not the only thing we dig up around here. We've been known to find quite a few Kinstone pieces as well.|Mountain Minish|TMC}}</ref>

The main room of the {{Term|TMC|Melari's Mine|display=Mine}} is {{Term|TMC|Melari}}'s workstation and furnace. It is here where {{Term|TMC|Melari}} and two other {{Term|TMC|Minish}} workers help to reforge the broken {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} after obtaining the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ting along, tong along! Huh, what? Who am I? Why, my lad, I'm the second-best of the Minish metalsmiths! I'm makin' swords with the boss.|Mountain Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hear you want me to reforge the sacred sword and help break a curse. I'll be needin' the old sword, which holds the power of the elements, first. Show me that broken Picori Blade!|Melari|TMC}}</ref> The {{Term|TMC|Melari's Mine|display=Mine}} also features the living rooms of {{Term|TMC|Melari}} and his apprentices.

{{Term|TMC|Melari's Mine}} is the intermediate between the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}} and the rest of {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you head outside from here, you can go see the mine the humans dug. But the boss doesn't let anyone go in there without permission.|Mountain Minish|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must pass through the {{Term|TMC|Melari's Mine|display=Mine}} to continue on his search for the {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link}}. As with every {{Term|TMC|Minish}} area, {{Term|TMC|Link}} will only gain access to {{Term|TMC|Melari's Mine}} if he shrinks down to {{Term|TMC|Minish}} size.
