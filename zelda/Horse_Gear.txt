{{Term/Store|Horse Gear|Horse Gear|Series, BotW}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} that can be equipped on {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 161}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear}} consists of a {{Term|BotW|Bridle|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Saddle|link}}, and can provide various effects.

==Location and Uses==
With the exception of the {{Term|BotW|Stable Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Stable Saddle|link}}, all pieces of {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Key Item|link}}, and can be found under this screen in the {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can equip {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear}} to a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} at a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} by bringing it to the character feeding {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} by the side of the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} and talking with them.<ref>{{Cite|If you're interested, may I help you change your horse's {{Color|BotW Blue|gear}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|mane}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So you'd like me to change the {{Color|BotW Blue|gear}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|mane}} on this little one again, correct?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} must have a maximum bond with his {{Term|BotW|Horse}} for the character to change the {{Term|BotW|Horse}}'s equipment.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! I'll gladly help you out with some {{Color|BotW Blue|gear}}!|Canni|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have a sufficient bond with his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, the worker will refuse.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear}} can be gained through many different means. Some {{Plural|BotW|Horse Gear}} are rewarded as a prize for winning a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}, such as the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Knight's Saddle|link}} for winning the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} at the {{Term|BotW|Horseback Archery Range|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Monster Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Monster Saddle|link}} can be bought from {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}}, while the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ancient Saddle|link}} are found in {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}} available in the {{TCB}} DLC.

With the exception of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Bridle}} and {{Term|BotW|Ancient Saddle}}, all {{Plural|BotW|Horse Gear}} is purely cosmetic and does not provide any effects.
