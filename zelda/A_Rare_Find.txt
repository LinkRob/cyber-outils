"{{Term/Store|A Rare Find||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Rare Find}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Outskirt Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Trott|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} notices that {{Term|BotW|Trott}} appears to be tired, to which {{Term|BotW|Trott}} asks if he is that obvious.<ref>{{Cite|[You sound tired.] Is it that obvious...|Trott|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he got a job at with the {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}} because he liked {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm...into horses...<br/>So I got a job with the stable association, which is great and all, but...|Trott|BotW}}</ref> and though he was dispatched to the middle of nowhere, he can handle it.<ref>{{Cite|They dispatched me here in the middle of nowhere, which I can handle...|Trott|BotW}}</ref> However, because of how often he works, {{Term|BotW|Trott}} has been feeling hazy.<ref>{{Cite|But they have me working around the clock. I feel so hazy all the time...|Trott|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} sympathizes with him, {{Term|BotW|Trott}} wants to eat some meat for energy, but the meals at {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable}} are mostly vegetarian.<ref>{{Cite|[Sounds rough.] If only I could eat some meat for energy. But the stable meals are vegetarian for the most part.|Trott|BotW}}</ref> He wishes for some fresh {{Term|BotW|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, what I wouldn't do for some fresh-caught raw gourmet meat...|Trott|BotW}}</ref> To aid {{Term|BotW|Link}} with his hunt, {{Term|BotW|Trott}} informs him of the migration patterns that occur on {{Term|BotW|Satori Mountain|link}}, indicating the optimal time to hunt for Meat.<ref>{{Cite|[Raw gourmet meat?] Are you into it too? The stuff next to the bone is the BEST! If only I could take a break and go to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Satori Mountain}} area just in front of Hyrule Ridge to go hunting... Usually there aren't a lot of animals out there, but every so often a migration hits and it's {{Color|BotW Red|teeming with animal life}}. But I'm never off the clock, so I can't go myself...|Trott|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns with {{Term|BotW|Raw Gourmet Meat}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Trott}} will recognize its scent and grow excited, offering to purchase it from him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! That meaty aroma! You don't happen to have any raw gourmet meat on you, do you? I'm no freeloader, of course. I'll pay you well if you split it with me.|Trott|BotW}}</ref> If Link accepts, Trott rewards Link with a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} and vows to work harder now that his cravings have been met.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure thing.] For real?! Well, you don't have to tell me twice! I'll take one! Does {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}} work for you?|Trott|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

After completing the Quest, {{Term|BotW|Trott}} will offer to buy additional Raw Gourmet Meat from Link for a Silver Rupee apiece.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure.] Thanks to you, I'm feeling better. If you get ahold of more raw gourmet meat, come see me.|Trott|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Have some meat!] Thanks! Is {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}} still good?|Trott|BotW}}</ref> However, he will only buy three pieces from Link per day.<ref>{{Cite|This is more than enough for today. If you get ahold of more raw gourmet meat, come see me tomorrow.|Trott|BotW}}</ref>
