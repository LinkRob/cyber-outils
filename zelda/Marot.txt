{{Term/Store|Marot||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Marot|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Marot}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} woman who resides in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. She is the proprietor of {{Term|BotW|Marot Mart|link}}, which includes the {{Term|BotW|Coral Reef|link}} {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Hammerhead|link}}.<ref name="Marot Mart">{{Cite|♪Welcome to Marot Mart!♪ This is where you'll find our general store, the Coral Reef. Our workshop, the Hammerhead, is also here.|Marot|BotW}}</ref> The latter of those businesses is operated by her grandfather, {{Term|BotW|Dento|link}}.<ref name="Dento"/>

As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} passes by {{Term|BotW|Marot}}, she will welcome him to {{Term|BotW|Marot Mart}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome!|Marot|BotW}}</ref> When he speaks to her, she will explain the services that {{Term|BotW|Marot Mart}} provides.<ref name="Marot Mart"/> After this introduction, she will ask if there is something else that {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs.<ref>{{Cite|Or...do you need something else?|Marot|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Is there anything else you'd like to ask me?|Marot|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Do you need me for something?|Marot|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Marot Mart}}, {{Term|BotW|Marot}} claims that it refers to the combination of the {{Term|BotW|Coral Reef}} and the {{Term|BotW|Hammerhead}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Marot Mart?] ♪Yep!♪ The general store and the workshop... They're the best shops EVER! And together they're caaaaalled...|Marot|BotW}}</ref> She then proceeds to sing the jingle for {{Term|BotW|Marot Mart}}, which she considers to be cute.<ref>{{Cite|♪Marot Mart!♪ ♪Marot Maaaaaart! La de da de daaa!♪ {{Big|♪LA DEE DAAAAA!♪}} That's my theme song! Isn't it cute?!|Marot|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} ask about {{Term|BotW|Marot Mart}} a second time, she will repeat the theme song for him.<ref>{{Cite|[Marot Mart?] You want me to sing again?! Kee hee... ♪You got it!♪|Marot|BotW}}</ref>

If asked about the {{Term|BotW|Coral Reef}}, {{Term|BotW|Marot}} informs him that is has been in business for more than 100 years.<ref>{{Cite|[The general store?] Our general store has been in business for over 100 years. It has a long and honorable history.|Marot|BotW}}</ref> She describes its proprietor, {{Term|BotW|Cleff|link}}, as a serious individual, but she is amused that his sister {{Term|BotW|Tona|link}} is in love with {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The shopkeep, Cleff, is really serious. But his younger sister, Tona, is head over fins for Prince Sidon! Kee hee!|Marot|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquires about the {{Term|BotW|Hammerhead}}, {{Term|BotW|Marot}} reveals that her grandfather is in charge of it.<ref name="Dento"/> She claims that he was responsible for fixing the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}}, which once belonged to {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|He's the one who fixed Mipha's weapon, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}.|Marot|BotW}}</ref>
