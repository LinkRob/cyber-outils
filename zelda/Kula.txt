{{Term/Store|Kula||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kula|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kula}} is a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} that resides in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}. He can be found wandering around in front of {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree|link}}.

Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Kula}}, he will recognize {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} as the hero and welcome him to {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}, noting that he is much cooler than he thought he would be.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Are you the hero? Could it be? Welcome to Korok forest! You're... much, much cooler looking than I thought you'd be!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> To ensure that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is prepared to be the hero, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} reveals that he has prepared a trial for him.<ref>{{Cite|But to be sure you're up to the task at hand...I prepared a {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} for you!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants to accept his trial.<ref>{{Cite|What do you think?! Will you accept my {{Color|BotW Red|trial}}?|Kula|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to attempt the trial, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} will cheer and ask to be shown a rod with the power of {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll do it!] Hooray! Show me a {{Color|BotW Red|rod with the power of ice}}, please!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> He explains that these rods can be used to freeze monsters and are known to be carried by {{Plural|BotW|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can wave this rod to freeze monsters in their tracks! {{Color|BotW Blue|Ice Wizzrobes}} love to wreak havoc with them.|Kula|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kula}} believes that if {{Term|BotW|Link}} were to wield one of them, he would become both stronger and cooler.<ref>{{Cite|If Mr. Hero were to wield a {{Color|BotW Red|rod with the power of ice}}, he would be even stronger...and cooler!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> When he finishes speaking, the "{{Term|BotW|A Freezing Rod|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} is in possession of an {{Term|BotW|Ice Rod|link}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}} to show it to {{Term|BotW|Kula}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Looks like you've located an {{Color|BotW Red|ice rod}}!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have it equipped, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} instructs him to do so.<ref>{{Cite|But...you're going to have to equip it to impress me!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Kula}} sees the {{Term|BotW|Ice Rod}}, he will be impressed by it.<ref>{{Cite|Shimmering! Sparkling! What a sight to behold!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> As thanks, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Mr. Hero. Your weapon is so amazingly, wonderfully sparkly! I must give you something as thanks!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kula}} will also accept seeing a {{Term|BotW|Blizzard Rod|link}}, for which he will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Is that a blizzard rod?! Incredible shimmer! Unbelievable sparkle!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} will be excited.<ref>{{Cite|Sparkly! Shiny!<br/>You were able to pull out the {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Sword}}!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> He advises {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} is unable to be used, then he could use a rod instead.<ref>{{Cite|If the {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Sword}} isn't feeling well, you could always use a magic rod!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> He says there are more types of rods than {{Plural|BotW|Ice Rod}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Fire Rod|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Meteor Rod|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Lightning Rod|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm Rod|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's more than just ice rods—there are also rods with fire or electric magic too!|Kula|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kula}} with the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} equipped, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} will ask to see it.<ref>{{Cite|Hey hey, hero!<br/>Can you show me the Master Sword? Show me, show me!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} agree to his demands, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} will become excited and shout.<ref>{{Cite|[All right.] YAAAAAAAAAAAY! So exciting!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> After seeing it, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} remarks that it is cool, and he thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for letting him see it.<ref>{{Cite|Schving! Fssssssshew! Wow! So cool! Thanks, hero!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> While he is happy to have seen the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to leave and save {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} with it.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so happy I got to see it! Now please go use that sword to save the princess!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses to show {{Term|BotW|Kula}} the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, he will be disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|[Another time.] Oh... OK.|Kula|BotW}}</ref>
