{{Term/Store|The Great Tripini||Series, TFH}}, also known as '''Trip''' for short, is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Who, me? I'm The Great Tripini. Peeps just call me Trip, though.|The Great Tripini|TFH}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TFH|The Great Tripini}} appears in the north-east of the town of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}, near its bridge leading to {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}. He recommends that {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} should turn on SpotPass for the game, and suggests they should visit the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}} sometime.<ref>{{Cite|Let's kick it in the Drablands together sometime, yeah?|The Great Tripini|TFH}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Tripini is derived from the word trip, to go on a journey or exploration. This refers to his role in announcing new Areas, and suggesting that Link visit them. The Great Tripini is also a play on {{Wp|Harry Houdini}}, a famous illusionist and stunt performer.
