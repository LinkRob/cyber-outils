{{Term/Store|The Fiercest of Foes||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 76 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Fiercest of Foes|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|The Fiercest of Foes}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given fifteen minutes to defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Lynel|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Lynel|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Lynel|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}}. They are found in different areas of the map, some in {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}}, and all surrounded by weaker {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} of the same type. These include many {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, along with a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}. Since both spawn at the same time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} can choose which one to approach first, although the other enemy will begin an assault on them soon afterwards. 

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any four {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Thigh|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
