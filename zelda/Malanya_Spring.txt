{{Term/Store|Malanya Spring||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Malanya Spring'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Malanya Spring is a [[Great Fairy Fountain]] located east of the [[Horse God Bridge]] in the [[Faron Grasslands]]. It is surrounded by the [[Ibara Butte]].

In the "[[EX Ancient Horse Rumors]]" [[Side Quest]], which is exclusive to [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC Pack, a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing the [[Ancient Saddle]] can be found behind Malanya's Spring.
