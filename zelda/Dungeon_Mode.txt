{{Term/Store|Dungeon Mode||Series, CoH}} is a {{Term|CoH|Mode|link}} in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dungeon Mode|Select Mode|CoH}}</ref> It was introduced as part of the {{OO}} [[Downloadable Content]] update.

==Overview==
{{Term|CoH|Dungeon Mode}} replaces the standard map with one continuous dungeon. It is meant to replicate the experience of playing ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.<ref>{{Cite|Replace the overworld with a single large dungeon. For player looking for the classic NecroDancer experience.|Select Mode|CoH}}</ref>
