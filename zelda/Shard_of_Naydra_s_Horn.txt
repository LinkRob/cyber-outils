==Location and Uses==
Shards of Naydra's Horn can be acquired by shooting the dragon [[Naydra]] on one of its horns. Naydra can be found at [[Mount Lanayru]], the [[Lanayru Promenade]], and [[Lanayru Bay]]. The Shard will glow as it falls to the ground.

Meals [[Cooking|cooked]] using a Shard of Naydra's Horn will grant a buff that lasts for thirty minutes when eaten. The Shards can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to create Light Blue [[Dye]].

Shards of Naydra's Horn are used in several [[Armor]] upgrades at {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. Two Shards and three {{Plural|Series|Silent Princess|link}} are needed to upgrade the [[Champion's Tunic]] to its third tier. Two Shards of Naydra's Horn, as well as one [[Star Fragment]], are needed to upgrade the [[Tunic of the Wild]] to its fourth tier. Finally, the [[Barbarian Leg Wraps]] and {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Armor|link}} require one Shard of Naydra's Horn and two [[Lynel Guts]] to be upgraded to their final tiers.
