The {{Term/Store|Compactor|Compactors|Series, OoS}} is a trap in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |publisher= BradyGames |page= 81{{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The {{Term|OoS|Compactor}} is located on the second floor of the {{Term|OoS|Ancient Ruins|link}}. On the ground of its room rests a {{Term|OoS|Big Red Rupee|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} is forced to pick it up to progress, and doing so will activate the {{Term|OoS|Compactor}}. The walls of the narrow room will start closing in, with the only reliable method of escape being to use {{Plural|OoS|Pegasus Seed|link}} to run away. Should {{Term|OoS|Link}} be caught by the {{Term|OoS|Compactor}}, it will result in an instant [[wikipedia:Crushed|Game Over]].
