{{Term/Store|Medoh's Barrier||Series, BotW}} is an object in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Impenetrable">{{Cite|Don't bother aiming for anything covered by {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh's barrier}}. It's impenetrable.|Teba|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Medoh's Barrier is a force-field generated by {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} to deter intruders. Medoh's Barrier is protected by the four [[Cannon]]s that are positioned at Medoh's extremities. In order to enter Vah Medoh, [[Teba]] enlists the aid of [[Link]], aiming to destroy the Cannons that sit just outside the Barrier.<ref name="Impenetrable"/> After destroying all four Cannons, Medoh's Barrier will disappear, allowing Link and Teba to board the [[Divine Beast]].

==Trivia==
*Though unnamed, {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} also bears a similar [[Barrier]], which can only be bypassed after destroying the pink orbs upon its back.
