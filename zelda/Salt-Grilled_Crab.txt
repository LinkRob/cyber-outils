==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Salt-Grilled Crabs by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any crab together with [[Rock Salt]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. There are two ways to make their default version. First, Link must use two different types of crabs. Alternatively, another ingredient can be added to the meal with an effect that differs from the crab used. Eating these dishes will restore some of [[Link|Link's]] {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. A majority of fishermen enjoy crabs most when they're prepared in this way.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on the type of crab used, Link can make the three following variations of Salt-Grilled Crab:

* '''Energizing Salt-Grilled Crab''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Salt-Grilled Crab}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Nine out of ten fishermen agree: crab is best enjoyed grilled and with just a bit of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}. They'll refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} when they're eaten.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Mighty Salt-Grilled Crab''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Salt-Grilled Crab}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Nine out of ten fishermen agree: crab is best enjoyed grilled and with just a bit of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}. When consumed, they will grant Link a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Salt-Grilled Crab''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Salt-Grilled Crab}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Nine out of ten fishermen agree: crab is best enjoyed grilled and with just a bit of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}. Link's defensive abilities will be boosted by eating these meals.<ref name="Tough"/>
