The {{Term/Store|X-Shaped Jewel||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The X-Shaped Jewel is found on an island off the [[Western Coast]]. To reach it, [[Link]] must shoot an [[Ember Seed]] with the [[Slingshot]] to light a [[Torch]], creating a bridge. Then, he must use a [[Mystery Seed]] on a rock found on the island, which reveals a [[Mini-Moldorm]].<ref>{{Cite|The Black Beast rests here.|Sign|OoS}}</ref> After defeating it, a [[Treasure Chest]] holding the X-Shaped Jewel will appear. It is used in conjunction with the other three Jewels to open the door leading to [[Tarm Ruins]].
