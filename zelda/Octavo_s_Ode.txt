{{Term/Store|''Octavo's Ode''||Series, CoH}} is a [[Downloadable Content]] update for {{CoH}}.

==Overview==
{{OO|-}} introduces a new {{Term|CoH|Mode}}, also called {{Term|CoH|Octavo's Ode (Mode)|link}} wherein {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} is added as a playable character. Additionally, {{Term|CoH|Dungeon Mode|link}} allows players to play through single dungeons without an overworld in a similar fashion to the classic ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
