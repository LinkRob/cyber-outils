The {{Term/Store|Ghost Shop||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a location in {{OoT}} and is home to the [[Poe Collector]].<ref>{{Cite|My shop is the only {{Color|OoT3D Red|Ghost Shop}} in Hyrule.|Poe Collector|OoT3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Accessible near the entrance to the [[Market]] where the [[Guard House]] used to be, the Ghost Shop opened after [[Ganondorf]] took over [[Hyrule]] during the seven years that [[Link]] was asleep.<ref>{{Cite|Because of the great Ganondorf, it is a fine time for a business like this...heh heh heh... Oh...I can only hope the world gets even worse!|Poe Collector|OoT3D}}</ref> As such, the Ghost Shop can only be found during Link's adulthood.

Here, the Poe Collector gathers all sorts of [[Poe]]s and offers to pay Link with in exchange for them.<ref>{{Cite|The ghosts, called {{Color|OoT3D Red|Poes}}, are spirits of concentrated hatred that appear in the fields and graveyard. They hate the world! Young man, if you catch a Poe, I will pay a lot of money for it... Heh heh hee!|Poe Collector|OoT3D}}</ref> If Link brings him a normal Poe, the mysterious man will give him 10 [[Rupee]]s for it. However, if Link brings him a [[Big Poe]] from [[Hyrule Field]], the Poe Collector will reward him with 50 Rupees and add 100 Points to his point card.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you brought a Poe today! Hmmmm! Very interesting! This is a {{Color|OoT3D Red|Big Poe}}! I'll buy it for {{Color|OoT3D Red|50 Rupees}}. On top of that, I'll put {{Color|OoT3D Red|100 points}} on your card. If you earn {{Color|OoT3D Red|1,000 points}}, you'll be a happy man! Heh heh.|Poe Collector|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, young man. What's happening today? If you have a {{Color|OoT3D Red|Poe}}, I will buy it. Your card now has {{Color|OoT3D Purple|100}} points. Come back again! Heh heh heh!|Poe Collector|OoT3D}}</ref> By bringing in 10 Big Poes and gathering 1000 points, the Poe Collector will give Link an empty [[Bottle]] as a compensation for increasing his inventory of Big Poes, calling Link a "genuine Ghost Hunter."<ref>{{Cite|Wait a minute! WOW! You have earned {{Color|OoT3D Red|1,000 points}}! Young man, you are a genuine {{Color|OoT3D Red|ghost hunter}}!|Poe Collector|OoT3D}}</ref> After this, the Poe Collector will not buy any more Poes from Link.<ref>{{Cite|Is that what you expected me to say? Heh heh heh! Because of you, I have extra inventory of {{Color|OoT3D Red|Big Poes}}, so this will be the last time I can buy one of these ghosts. You're thinking about what I promised would happen when you earned 1,000 points. Heh heh. Don't worry. I didn't forget. Just take this.|Poe Collector|OoT3D}}</ref>
