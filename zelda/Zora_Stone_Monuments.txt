"{{Term/Store|Zora Stone Monuments||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Zora Stone Monuments}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Zora's Domain}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be able to speak with {{Term|BotW|Jiahto|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. He apologizes for not introducing himself sooner,<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I am sorry I did not introduce myself sooner. I am Jiahto.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> explaining that he is a historian who studies and preserves {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} history.<ref>{{Cite|I am a historian who studies Zora history and preserves it for future generations.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> However, the {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|link}} found in the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber}} is in a deteriorated state,<ref>{{Cite|Even so, this stone monument that King Dorephan supposedly wrote... It's torn to pieces now.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> and he fears the rest are just as bad.<ref>{{Cite|Most likely, the {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} outside of the domain are in the same awful condition.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} wants to compile the contents of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} into a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm thinking of collecting the contents of all of the stone monuments and compiling them into a book.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> but due to his old age, traveling to all 10 of them would be difficult.<ref>{{Cite|But with these old legs and fins, traveling to all {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} will take a great deal of time.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find all of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} and report their contents to him.<ref>{{Cite|That is why... Link... I'd like to ask a favor of you. I'd like you to find all {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} around {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} and tell me what they say.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts this task, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} will be grateful.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Thank you! You truly are a lifesaver! How reassuring that the Champion will search for those monuments for me!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He also shares a rumor that one of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} has the location of a treasure written on it,<ref>{{Cite|I have it on good authority that among the stone monuments, one has the location of some {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}} written on it.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> and should {{Term|BotW|Link}} find it, he would be allowed to keep it.<ref>{{Cite|If you happen to find said {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}}, feel free to keep it. It will most likely be something that a Zora wouldn't need.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the 10 {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monuments}} are located around {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}},<ref>{{Cite|Yes, you'll find the {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} around {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}... I believe in you. In fact, I'm counting on you!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

It would seem you have checked all of the stone monuments for me! Now then, please tell me what was written on them!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He is amazed by the tales inscribed on the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has helped gather for him.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... The history, culture, folklore, and various heroic acts of the proud Zora people... It's all here! Eureka! Thanks to you, I've compiled the precious information from those stone monuments!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link, thank you for taking on such an arduous task! Please, allow me to reward you!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have the {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} space for another {{Term|BotW|Diamond}}, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} will hold onto his reward until he can take it.<ref>{{Cite|I would like to reward you, but it seems you already have quite a collection of {{Color|BotW Blue|diamonds}}.
