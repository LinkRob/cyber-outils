The {{Term/Store|Level Selection||Series, TFH}} screen,{{Exp Game|TFH}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= At the Level Selection screen you'll see information for what Materials can be found and what Sub-Weapons are available.|game= TFH|publisher= Prima|page= 38}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Level Select||LCT}} screen,{{Exp Game|LCT}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|LCT Blue|Level Select}}}}|Level Select|LCT}}</ref> and the '''Level Select Screen''',{{Exp Game|LCT}}<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= {{Big|Level Select Screen}} |game= LCT |page= 5}}</ref> is a recurring menu in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Overview==
==={{TFH|-}}===
The {{Term|TFH|Level Selection}} menus appear when the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} gather on a {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}} inside {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}. It is where they select which {{Term|TFH|Area|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Level|link}} they wish to enter, and optionally, which {{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} to attempt.

The first menu features a graphic of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}} on the top screen, with each locked {{Term|TFH|Area}} obscured by clouds. Small icons of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} raising their {{Plural|TFH|Sword|link}} appear next to the {{Term|TFH|Area}} selected by each. On the touchscreen is a list of {{Plural|TFH|Area}} available for selection; unknown {{Plural|TFH|Area}} appear as "???" and cannot be selected. In the event that {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} has visited a locked {{Term|TFH|Area}} (by way of the other {{Plural|TFH|Link}} selecting it), its name will appear on the list, though it remains unselectable.

When playing online with "unknown heroes", this first menu appears ''before'' the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} gather. This allows players to only be matched with others who are visiting the same {{Term|TFH|Area}}.

The second menu is for selecting a {{Term|TFH|Level|link}}. The top screen features a preview image of the selected {{Term|TFH|Level}}, a list of the {{Plural|TFH|Item|link}} that the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} will use, and a list of the specific {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the {{Term|TFH|Level}}, along with the number of {{Plural|TFH|Material}} already owned. If {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} are available, then the list of {{Plural|TFH|Material}} will show all possible rewards for the various {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|display=Challenges}}.

The third menu is for selecting a {{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge}}, if they are available. It looks and functions the same as the previous menu, except that the list of {{Plural|TFH|Material}} are specific to each {{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|display=Challenge}}. The first option in this menu is always "No challenge". Following this menu is the {{Term|TFH|Pick an Outfit|link}} menu.

When playing a [[Multiplayer]] game, each menu of {{Term|TFH|Level Selection}} includes a 30-second time limit before the highlighted option is automatically selected, as well as an "I can't decide!" option, which defers the selection to the other players in the following manner:

*If one player selects "I can't decide!", their selection will randomly match one of the other players'.
*If two players select "I can't decide!", their selections will both match the third player's.
*If all three players select "I can't decide!", they will each make a random selection.
*If the selections are not unanimous, one will be chosen by a roulette system.<ref>{{Cite|Roulette time!|Level Selection|TFH}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{LCT|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
The {{Term|LCT|Level Select}} menu can be accessed by the player after selecting the {{Term|LCT|Score Attack|link}} option in the {{Term|LCT|Title Screen|link}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= This screen will appear only in Score Attack. |game= LCT |page= 5}}</ref> They will then be shown the {{Term|LCT|Player Select|link}} menu. Once the player chooses their save file, they will be transported to the {{Term|LCT|Level Select}} menu.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Once you’ve chosen a game mode, the Player Select screen will appear. Select a profile to use. If you’re playing a multiplayer match, you will first choose the number of players. When you’re done, you’ll move on to the Level Select screen. |game= LCT |page= 4}}</ref> Upon entering the {{Term|LCT|Level Select}} menu for the first time, only {{Plural|LCT|Level|link}} one through three will be available. More {{Plural|LCT|Level}} will unlock if {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} earns a {{Term|LCT|Medal|link}} on the previous {{Term|LCT|Level}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=  If you complete each of the three stages with a high enough total score, you will clear the level and move on to the next. |game= LCT |page= 7}}</ref> Earning a {{Term|LCT|Medal}} on {{Term|LCT|Level 1|link}} or {{Term|LCT|Level 2|link}} will not unlock any {{Plural|LCT|Level}}. The {{Plural|LCT|Level}} appear as labeled {{Plural|LCT|Target|link}} in two rows in the menu, with {{Plural|LCT|Level}} one through five on the top and six through the {{Term|LCT|Finale|link}} on the bottom. Any {{Plural|LCT|Medal}} {{Term|LCT|Link}} earns on a {{Term|LCT|Level}} will be displayed under the {{Term|LCT|Target}} option for that {{Term|LCT|Level}}. Selecting any {{Term|LCT|Target}} will allow the player to start the {{Term|LCT|Level}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=  To begin playing, point at the level you want to play and press B. A target will appear. Point at it and press B to start. |game= LCT |page= 5}}</ref>  The player can return to the {{Term|LCT|Player Select}} menu by selecting the button in the bottom right of the {{Term|LCT|Level Select}} menu. In the top right corner of the {{Term|LCT|Level Select}} menu, the player can view the {{Term|LCT|Best Scores|link}} menu, where the player can see which player has the highest score on any {{Term|LCT|Level}}.
