The {{Term/Store|Great Butterfly Fairy||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Great Butterfly Fairy}}
The {{Term|TMC|Great Butterfly Fairy}} is found in the northern section of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}, accessible by using the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}} on a hole in the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}}. She mentions she is in need of {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} and asks {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} for all of his.<ref>{{Cite|I have need of many Rupees... Please, throw all of yours into the spring.|Great Butterfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref> If he agrees, she tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} that she does not really need any {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}} and rewards his generosity with a larger {{Term|TMC|Wallet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You are most kind. I do not really need your Rupees. But I must reward such a kind adventurer with some of my power.|Great Butterfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref>
