'''Pompie''' and '''Vera''' are characters in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''''Pompie'''''|book= E |publisher= |page= 258{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''''Vera'''''|book= E |publisher= |page= 258{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TWWHD|Pompie and Vera}} are middle-aged women found together on {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}. They are always spreading idle gossip about the other inhabitants of Windfall, particularly {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Minenco|link}}. If {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} shows them a {{Term|TWWHD|Pictograph|link}} of {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Minenco}} together, they will realize that the two of them are just friends and promise to stop spreading unnecessary rumors about others; moreover, he will be rewarded with a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chart|link}}.
