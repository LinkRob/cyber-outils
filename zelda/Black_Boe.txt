{{Term/Store|Black Boe|Black Boes|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are enemies in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Black Boe}} were referred to as '''Kurobos''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Kurobo}}<br/>Kurobos are small black masses with beady yellow eyes who indiscriminately attack based on floor vibrations.|retrieved= January 22, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160311043311/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/k.jsp#Kurobo|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= It's a {{Color|MM White|Black Boe}}...<br/>Just calm down and attack it! Look, there are just a lot of them, that's all.
}}

{{Plural|MM3D|Black Boe}} are {{Plural|MM3D|Boe|link}} that hide in darkness. They can be found in {{Term|MM3D|Woodfall Temple|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Mountain Village|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Oceanside Spider House|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Beneath the Well|link}}, and in the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|link}}. They can occasionally blend in with their backgrounds, so long as the backgrounds are dark. They can also occasionally be found in {{Plural|MM3D|Jar|link}}.
