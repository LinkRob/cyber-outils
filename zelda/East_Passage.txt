The {{Term/Store|East Passage||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''East Passage'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|East Passage}} is a sprawling cavern situated in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Underground|link}}. Inside the entrance of the {{Term|BotW|East Passage|display=Passage}} are {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}}, which can be broken for supplies. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use a {{Term|BotW|Mine Cart|link}} to traverse through the tunnel. Further along in the {{Term|BotW|East Passage}} is a room containing a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus (Rare)|link}} can be found in the chamber where the rails end, beneath a vertical tunnel shaft.<ref>{{Cite|Hyrule Castle Underground<br/>{{Big|Stone Talus}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref> If it is defeated, an {{Term|BotW|Updraft|link}} will appear where it once rested, allowing easy travel up the shaft. The shaft itself continues into a tunnel leading north, connecting to a break in the southern wall of the first floor of the {{Term|BotW|Library|link}}. However, this gap is filled by {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}} connected to a {{Term|BotW|Glowing Eyeball}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat in order to progress further into {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}.

Near the entrance of the {{Term|BotW|East Passage}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}. A ledge on the southern side of the shaft holds a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} which contains a {{Term|BotW|Shard of Farosh's Fang|link}}. Interspersed through the {{Term|BotW|East Passage|display=Passage}} are {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Rare Ore Deposit|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
If {{Term|BotW|Link}} melts the {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|East Passage}}, he will uncover {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}}. When he interacts with them, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
