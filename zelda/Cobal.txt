==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Cobal}}. He is a member of a highly civilized race of moles called the Mogma. All Mogmas have a deep and abiding love of treasure.
}}

Cobal is a green-haired [[Mogma]] who frequently accompanies [[Ledd]]. He can first be found shortly after [[Link]] arrives at [[Eldin Volcano]] with Ledd. He expresses surprise to Ledd that Link is not a [[Bokoblin]].<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! You're not... Yo, {{Color|SS Blue|Ledd}}, I don't think this is one of those red creeps.|Cobal|SS}}</ref>

He can then be found talking to Ledd outside of the [[Earth Temple (Skyward Sword)|Earth Temple]] about getting the inside to obtain the [[Treasure]] hidden within.<ref>{{Cite|I bet they got something really valuable stashed in there.|Ledd|SS}}</ref> After discussing where the [[Key]] to the [[Temple]] is hidden, Cobal points out that they could just simply dig their way into the Temple.<ref>{{Cite|Hey... Why are we so worried about a key when we can just dig our way in?|Cobal|SS}}</ref>

Link will shortly encounter Cobal again in the Earth Temple regretting his journey after Ledd has gone missing.<ref>{{Cite|My partner's gone missing, and those red guys are everywhere! I never shoulda come here.|Cobal|SS}}</ref> He warns Link that he should go home if he wants to live.<ref>{{Cite|If you wanna live to...live again one day, you should head for home, pal!|Cobal|SS}}</ref>

The last place Link can encounter Cobal is within the {{Plural|SS|Cave|link}} deep beneath Eldin Volcano. He can be found in the room full of [[Pyrup]]s near a bombable wall. When talked to, Cobal will encourage Link to search for [[Rupee]]s in the {{Plural|SS|Cave}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can find all kinds of things in these caves. It's mostly {{Color|SS Gold|Rupees}}, sure, but ya never know. If you get the itch for loot, you should try some cave hunting.|Cobal|SS}}</ref> After he finishes talking, [[Fi]] will unlock Rupee [[Dowsing]] for Link.<ref>{{Cite|Master, the abilities of your sword have increased. It is now possible to {{Color|SS Red|dowse}} for {{Color|SS Gold|Rupees}}.|Fi|SS}}</ref>
