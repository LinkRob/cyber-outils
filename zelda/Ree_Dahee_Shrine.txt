The {{Term/Store|Ree Dahee Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ree Dahee Shrine}}<br/>Timing Is Critical}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Timing Is Critical''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Ree Dahee Shrine is in the valley between the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|link}}, on a small ledge near the bottom of the northern mountain. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ree Dahee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ree Dahee. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Ree Dahee|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Ree Dahee Shrine features a long chamber with multiple floating, tilting and moving platforms with Ree Dahee waiting at the end of the chamber. Initially, all three the moving platforms inside the Shrine are deactivated. To activate each one, Link must use a nearby {{Term|BotW|Switch|link}} to tilt either a single platform or a group of two platforms.

An {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} sits on top of each group of tilting platforms and must be rolled into a socket on a platform close to the tilting platforms. When an Orb rolls into a socket, that socket will then activate a moving platform allowing Link to advance to the next group of platforms.

By approaching Ree Dahee, the Monk will present Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ree Dahee|BotW}}</ref> wishing Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ree Dahee|BotW}}</ref>

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the {{Term|BotW|Climber's Bandanna|link}} is on a small platform above the last tilting platform.
