{{Term/Store|Daruk's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 5 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Daruk's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Daruk's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Ruby|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Rock Salt|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, although a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawns once enough of them are killed. More {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos}} also spawn as {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}} defeats them. Once the objective is reached, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawn and the time limit is taken away. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} are defeated.
