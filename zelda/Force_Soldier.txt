{{Term/Store|Force Soldier|Force Soldiers|Series, FSA}} are {{Plural|FSA|Enemy|link}} in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 178}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|FSA|Force Soldier}} are large {{Plural|FSA|Force Gem|link}} in the form of enemy {{Plural|FSA|Soldier (Enemy)|link}}.

During certain circumstances, usually when playing on the [[Game Boy Advance]], approaching certain large green {{Plural|FSA|Force Gem|link}} causes them to suddenly spring into sentience, growing legs and a sword and shield and attacking any nearby {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}}. They defend and attack as capable swordsmen, much like Sword Soldiers. They can withstand a few hits but upon defeating one, a large green {{Term|FSA|Force Gem}} valued at 100 will appear as a spoil of the battle. However, if the {{Plural|FSA|Links}} do not defeat these foes fast enough, the Force Gem Knights will instead explode, leaving the {{Plural|FSA|Links}} with a single gem.
