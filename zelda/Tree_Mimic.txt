{{Term/Store|Tree Mimic|Tree Mimics|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Tree Mimic|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Tree Mimic}} are enemies that pose as {{Plural|CoH|Tree|link}} in order to ambush {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} in dungeons. When dormant, they appear almost identical to small {{Plural|CoH|Tree}}, but can be identified by their red eye, which occasionally appears to look around for future victims. They are also invulnerable in this form, being immune to damage from all {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} and magic, though can be stunned with the {{Term|CoH|Boomerang|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Hookshot|link}}. They will only transform into their true selves when any of the heroes are standing directly next to them, and will proceed to chase them on every beat until stunned or slain.
