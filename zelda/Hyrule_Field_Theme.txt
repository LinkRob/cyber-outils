"'''Hyrule Field Theme'''" is a recurring {{Term|Series|Song|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|SSBfWU, SSBU}}

==Overview==
There are two versions of "{{Term|Series|Hyrule Field Theme}}" in the {{SSB|-}} series: one is the original music that plays on {{Term|OoT|Hyrule Field|link}} in {{OoT}} and the other one is a remix.

==={{SSBB|-}}===
"{{Term|Series|Hyrule Field Theme}}", a remix of the original music, plays in the {{Term|SSBB|Bridge of Eldin}} {{Term|SSBB|Stage}} in {{SSBB}}.

==={{SSB4|Wii U|-}}===
"{{Term|Series|Hyrule Field Theme}}", the original version, plays in the [[Hyrule Castle]] {{Small|(64)}} {{Term|SSBfWU|Stage|link}} in {{SSB4|Wii U}}, while the arrangement from {{SSBB|-}} plays in the {{Term|SSBfWU|Bridge of Eldin}} {{Term|SSBfWU|Stage}}.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
Both versions of "{{Term|SSBU|Hyrule Field Theme}}" play in any {{Term|SSBU|Stage}} related to {{TLoZ|-}} series in {{SSBU}}.
