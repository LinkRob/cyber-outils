==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can drink Mighty Elixirs to receive a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Inventory"/> This effect works by strengthening one's body and mind, and it applies to all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Mighty Elixirs can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|BotW|Bladed Rhino Beetle|link}}

By adding any of these mighty ingredients, Link can make a Mighty Elixir that will also heal him:

* {{Term|Series|Mighty Bananas|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Razorshroom|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Mighty Thistle|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Mighty Carp|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Mighty Porgy|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Razorclaw Crab|link}}
