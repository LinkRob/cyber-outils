{{Term/Store|Yeko||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|ST|Anouki|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 288}}</ref> He resides in {{Term|ST|Anouki Village|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Snow Realm|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|ST|Yeko}} lives in a house in the northwest portion of {{Term|ST|Anouki Village}}. When {{Term|ST|Link|link}} visits the village for the first time, he is tasked by {{Term|ST|Honcho|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Anouki}} chief, to pair up all of the {{Plural|ST|Anouki}} in order to put together a village watch. {{Term|ST|Yeko}} comments to {{Term|ST|Link}} that he will pair up with anyone except for a certain {{Term|ST|Anouki}} who has "wimpy horns" and dresses in blue.<ref>{{Cite|I'm not gonna name any names, but there's one guy in our village I can't stand! He's got wimpy horns and dresses in blue.|Yeko|ST}}</ref> Due to his preferences, {{Term|ST|Link}} ends up pairing him with an {{Term|ST|Anouki}} named {{Term|ST|Bulu|link}}.
