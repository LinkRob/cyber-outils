'''''The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild — The Complete Official Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Piggyback]] for {{BotW}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The Standard Edition features 356 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{BotW}}, a listing of {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, and collectibles, detailed maps, and concept art.

The Expanded Edition, released in 2018, features 516 pages, and includes content on {{TMT}} and {{TCB}}.
