{{Term/Store|''Mikau's Diary''||Series}} is an object in {{MM}}.{{Name Ref|MM}}

==Uses and Location==
[[File:MM3D Mikau's Diary Notebook Portrait.png|left|A picture of Mika's Diary in the Bombers' Notebook from {{MM3D|-}}]]
[[Mikau]]'s Diary is found in the room he shares with [[Tijo]] in the [[Zora Hall]]. [[Link]] can reach it by using the [[Hookshot]]. Because it is written in [[Zoran Script]], Link can only read it while in his [[Zora Link|Zora form]].<ref>{{Cite|Can you read Zoran script?|Tatl|MM}}</ref>

The Diary contains several entries. In the first, Mikau records the first four notes he plays while having a [[Jam Session]] with [[Japas]].<ref>{{Cite|Mikau. My Diary. Today, Japas and I had a jam session. He's not good with words, but he writes great riffs. Here's what I played: {{Scale|N64|Av>A}}|Mikau's Diary|MM}}</ref> In the next entry, he writes that he had [[Lulu]] listen to their session,<ref>{{Cite|Today, I had Lulu listen to my session with Japas. She seemed to like it a lot. Lulu's a pretty great girl, after all.|Mikau's Diary|MM}}</ref> and in the third entry, he writes the four notes he played at another session.<ref>{{Cite|Today, I had another jam session with Japas. Evan was in a bad mood 'cause the two of us are writin' songs on our own. Here's what I played: {{Scale|N64|v><v}}|Mikau's Diary|MM}}</ref> In the last entry, Mikau wrote that [[Evan]] had asked to speak with him about Lulu, and that his lucky color for the week was green.<ref>{{Cite|Evan called me over this morning. It was something about Lulu. He was in a real rush. I wonder what it could be? By the way, it seems my lucky color this week is green.|Mikau's Diary|MM}}</ref> Upon discovering Lulu's condition, Mikau attempts to confront the [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirates]] and recover her [[Zora Egg|eggs]], which ultimately leads to his death.
