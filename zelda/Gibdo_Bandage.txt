{{Term/Store|Gibdo Bandage|Gibdo Bandages|Series, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 125"/>

==Location and Uses==
==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, {{Plural|TFH|Gibdo Bandage}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Dunes|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Infinity Dunes|link}}''', ''"Guard the orb!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Gibdo Bandage}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Gibdo Bandage}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Gibdo Bandage}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Gibdo Bandage}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum}}''', ''"Avoid falling and quicksand!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Gibdo Bandage}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Desert Temple|link}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Gibdo Bandage}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Gibdo Bandage}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Gust Garb|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Ninja Gi|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 125"/>
