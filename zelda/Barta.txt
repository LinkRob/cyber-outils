{{Term/Store|Barta||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Barta|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Barta}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who protects {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, wielding a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Liana|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Leena|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Kotta|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}} after speaking to {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} for the first time, {{Term|BotW|Liana}} will ask where {{Term|BotW|Barta}} is.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, I haven't seen Barta. Where's she gone off to?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leena}} explains that she went to the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}} two days ago and wonders what happened to her.<ref>{{Cite|She said she was going to scout the thieves' hideout, but then she left before we could say anything.|Leena|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Now that I think about it, she's been gone for two days. I wonder what happened to her...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> Angered, {{Term|BotW|Liana}} asks why she let {{Term|BotW|Barta}} go by herself,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|You— Are you serious?!}} Why did you let her go by herself?! And why did you wait two days to report this?!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Leena}} apologizes for.<ref>{{Cite|I'm— I apologize... She said she just wanted to check it out and would be careful, so I thought...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is upset that they now have to retrieve both the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Barta}}.<ref>{{Cite|Uuugghhh. As if we aren't busy enough searching for the chief's heirloom, now we have to find {{Color|BotW Blue|Barta}} too...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She expresses concern about {{Term|BotW|Teake|link|display=Captain Teake}} finding out about it,<ref>{{Cite|If the captain hears about this fiasco, she'll punish us for sure! Probably 100 laps around Gerudo Desert...again.|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} asks what they should do.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Oh, no. What should we do?|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> This causes {{Term|BotW|Liana}} to yell that she is thinking about it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|I'm thinking about it!}}|Liana|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Barta}} imprisoned in a cell within the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout}}. She is surprised that he has entered the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|display=Hideout}},<ref>{{Cite|Hey, what are you doing here? This is the hideout of those Yiga thieves who stole the {{Color|BotW Red|Thunder Helm}}...|Barta|BotW}}</ref> as they will call in reinforcements if they notice him.<ref>{{Cite|If they spot you, they'll {{Color|BotW Red|call their friends}} for sure. There's no way you can take them all on.|Barta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Barta}} has only ever seen them patrol and eat {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}}, neither of which she can see a weakness in.<ref>{{Cite|If only you had some way of distracting them...but I don't know how. All I ever see them do is patrol and eat {{Color|BotW Red|bananas}}. I haven't seen anything else that you could exploit as a weakness.|Barta|BotW}}</ref> She tries to convince {{Term|BotW|Link}} to leave, as she can take care of herself.<ref>{{Cite|Listen, this place is dangerous. You should get out of here while you can. I can take care of myself.|Barta|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} recovers the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}, {{Term|BotW|Barta}} returns to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} and tells {{Term|BotW|Liana}} how helpful {{Term|BotW|Link}} was.<ref>{{Cite|Barta let me know how helpful you've been.|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Barta}} will recognize {{Term|BotW|Link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout}}, explaining she was able to escape because they spread their forces thin looking for him.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... Aren't you the one who broke into the Yiga Clan's hideout? They spread their forces so thing looking for you that I managed to bust out and return here. I owe you my life!|Barta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} was not wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} when she saw him, she promises not to tell anyone that he is a voe.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Don't worry, I won't tell anyone you're a voe. It's the least I can do.}}|Barta|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Barta}} disappears again after {{Term|BotW|Link}} frees {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}. Speaking to the soldiers, {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is shocked to find {{Term|BotW|Barta}} has not come back.<ref>{{Cite|Whuh... Whah... {{Big|WHAAAAT?!}} Barta didn't come back AGAIN?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} explains that she went to investigate the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid this time she went to investigate the {{Color|BotW Blue|leviathan bones}} in the southwest...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Leena}} claims she tried to stop her this time.<ref>{{Cite|I... I tried to stop her...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Liana}} points out that this obviously did not work,<ref>{{Cite|But obviously it didn't work because she's not here!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leena}} and {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} try to rationalize their decisions, saying everyone knows the area is dangerous and to avoid it.<ref>{{Cite|Well, yeah... But... Everyone knows that area is the domain of a {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga}}, so who would figure she'd even go close to there?|Leena|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes, nobody would dare! And not only that, but it's an {{Color|BotW Blue|extremely hot zone}}, so we wouldn't last long out there in our gear...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> This only angers {{Term|BotW|Liana}} further, who remarks that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} would not realize she was walking right into danger.<ref>{{Cite|You... You {{Big|IDIOTS!}} That scatterbrain wouldn't even realize she was walking into danger! I mean, you know that, right?! Why, it was just the other day she ran off and got captured by the Yiga! Ugh... I guess Barta will be Barta, and you all aren't going to change...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She wonders why {{Term|BotW|Barta}} has to get in trouble all the time.<ref>{{cite|But still, why does she have to go and get in trouble like this all the time...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> Witnessing this conversation activates "{{Term|BotW|The Search for Barta|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Barta}} on the brink of death, kneeling on the ground in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} near the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you...a fairy? An angel? Are you here to guide my passage? ...Everything's going hazy...This might be the end...|Barta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Barta}} wishes she could have eaten a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}} one last time.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh... If only I could've eaten a {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty durian}} one last time...|Barta|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} gives her a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Durian}}, {{Term|BotW|Barta}} will stand up and thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} for saving her life.<ref>{{Cite|Hah... I've returned from the brink of death thanks to you...|Barta|BotW}}</ref> She laments heading out into the heat without the proper gear.<ref>{{Cite|I guess it really was reckless of me to come here without the proper gear...|Barta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}}, she notices he is not wearing the proper gear either and is amazed he made it out so far.<ref>{{Cite|...But you don't seem to have the proper gear, either. It's kind of amazing that you even made it here to be honest.|Barta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not wearing his disguise and {{Term|BotW|Barta}} has not yet seen him without it, she will realize he is a man, mentioning that he seemed muscular for a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Is that you I spoke to before? So you are actually a voe... I thought you were a bit muscular for a Hylian vai.|Barta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Barta}} promises to keep it a secret, especially because {{Term|BotW|Link}} saved her life.<ref>{{Cite|But don't worry, your secret is safe with me. I owe you my life, after all!|Barta|BotW}}</ref> At first, {{Term|BotW|Barta}} is so tired that she wants to rest just a bit longer before she returns to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hahh... Anyway, I'm absolutely beat. Maybe I'll rest just a little longer and then head home.|Barta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hah... Anyway, I'm totally exhausted. Maybe I'll rest just a little longer then head home.|Barta|BotW}}</ref> However, when {{Term|BotW|Link}} informs her that {{Term|BotW|Liana}} was furious, {{Term|BotW|Barta}} realizes she is in trouble and decides to head back.<ref>{{Cite|[Your boss was MAD.] Huh? So...they knew I snuck off... {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|...Oh, I am in such trouble...}}}} Yep, looks like I'd better go on ahead. See you later and sav'orq!|Barta|BotW}}</ref>

Upon returning to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}, {{Term|BotW|Liana}} has heard that {{Term|BotW|Link}} saved {{Term|BotW|Barta}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! What a courageous vai! I heard from Barta! She said you found her!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward,<ref>{{Cite|You must be worn out, right? Here, take this as thanks! Buy yourself something nice in the market!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}. Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Barta}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if he had not come along when he did, she would have died before meeting the voe of her dreams.<ref>{{Cite|If you hadn't come along when you did... I would have had to say my eternal sav'orq before I'd even met the voe of my dreams...|Barta|BotW}}</ref> She wonders how things are going for {{Term|BotW|Perda|link}} and mentions that she should go look for her special someone after everyone forgets how much trouble she is in.<ref>{{Cite|Speaking of which, I wonder how things are going for that voe-crazy Perda... I wonder if she's met the one yet... Maybe once everyone forgets how in trouble I am, I should head out and look for my special someone...|Barta|BotW}}</ref>
