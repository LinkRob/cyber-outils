The {{Term/Store|Twin Juggler|Twin Jugglers|Series, OoT, OoT3D|Plural}} are a recurring group in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 238">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D}}}}</ref> The {{Plural|Series|Twin Jugglers}} go unnamed in {{MM}}.{{Note|Though the twins are only jugglers in {{MM}}, {{E}} uses the term "Twin Juggler" only in the section on {{OoT}} – in the {{MM}} section, they are individually named "Red Juggler" and "Blue Juggler".<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref>}}

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the Blue Juggler is a nervous man who constantly tells the Red Juggler in the [[Market]] various things, only to be mocked by his twin.<ref>{{Cite|Wah hah hah hah hah hah! He must have been seeing things! Wah hah hah hah hah hah!|Red Juggler|OoT}}</ref> The man dressed in blue claims that he attempted to have met [[Princess Zelda]] by sneaking into [[Hyrule Castle]].<ref>{{Cite|I wanted to see Princess Zelda! I avoided the guards... Swam through the moat... I was almost there when they caught me!|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref> After avoiding the [[Soldier]]s, he tried to access the [[Castle Courtyard]] by crawling through the secret passageway; however, the twin became stuck within the passageway and eventually got caught by the castle's guards.<ref>{{Cite|I found a small drain hole on the right side of the castle that I thought I could sneak into, but I got stuck there.|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref> As a result, the guards increased their security, not allowing anyone into the castle.<ref>{{Cite|Wha-ha-ha! What a crazy guy! Can you believe this guy was crazy enough to try to sneak into the castle to see Princess Zelda? All because of this idiot, they've tightened security at the castle. Wha-ha-hah!|Red Juggler|OoT}}</ref>

Later the Blue Juggler is almost kicked by [[Ganondorf's Steed]].<ref>{{Cite|I almost got kicked by that black horse!|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref> The Red Juggler once again mocks him for this.<ref>{{Cite|Look at that guy--he's so scared! What a wimp! Ha ha hah!|Red Juggler|OoT}}</ref>

They flee to [[Kakariko Village#Ocarina of Time|Kakariko Village]] when Link is an adult. The Blue Juggler insists that he saw the ghost of [[Dampé]] descending into his grave, carrying a piece of treasure.<ref>{{Cite|I tell you, I saw him! I saw the ghostly figure of Dampé the gravekeeper sinking into his grave. It looked like he was holding some kind of treasure!|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref> After Link clears the [[Shadow Temple]], the Blue Juggler witnesses Link teleporting out and is shocked.<ref>{{Cite|There he is! It's him! He's the one who dropped from the sky surrounded by a blue light! Now do you believe me?|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref> He also laments that no one has believed him for the last seven years.<ref>{{Cite|Nobody believes what I've been telling them all these past seven years!|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the Twin Jugglers spend their time practicing their juggling skills outside at [[East Clock Town]]. They are always laughing and generally having a good time.<ref>{{Cite|You see, we're entertainers. We must keep people smiling. No matter how grim things get, we must always be optimistic!|Twin Jugglers|MM}}</ref> They like to make fun of each other, with both laughing at their jokes.<ref>{{Cite|Ho! Ho! I hear it's gonna fall harder than the way my clumsy brother is always dropping our juggling balls!|Twin Juggler|MM}}</ref> Both of them are members of the [[Gorman Troupe]].<ref>{{Cite|We're the twin jugglers from the Gorman Troupe!|Twin Jugglers|MM}}</ref> At night, they can be found inside the room that the Gorman Troupe rented in the [[Stock Pot Inn]] playing a game of cards.

==Trivia==
*In {{OoT|-}}, there is another character who is identical to the twins aside from the color of their shirt, the [[:File:OOT Nighttime Man.png|man in white]] looking at the window at night in the Hyrule Castle Town Market. There are also a couple of headswapped models that use the same body as the twin jugglers, including the [[:File:OOT Lookout Man.png|man in purple]] overlooking the Hyrule Castle Town Market and later Kakariko Village's tower, and a [[:File:OOT Young Alley Man.png|bearded man in green]] in the [[Back Alley]] of the Castle Town (can be found in his house at night in the Back Alley). Aside from the twin jugglers, none of these other character models make their appearance in {{MM|-}}.
*In the Japanese version, the brothers are exaggeratedly camp, using feminine speech patterns like the [[Carpenter|carpenters]] in ''Ocarina of Time'' and making a lot of suggestive double entendres, the latter being most prominent [https://youtu.be/0p055n9WvHQ?si=F97ctpHQfS5rVxc5&t=1599 when they play cards at night].
