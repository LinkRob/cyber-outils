==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Warm Safflina}}
Warm Safflinas are found almost entirely in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} (outside of there: there is one at the base of [[Satori Mountain]], one along the tunnel path to [[Death Mountain]]; and one north of & one northwest of the [[Eldin Mountains]]). Many Warm Safflinas can be found near the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} under the [[Gerudo Great Skeleton]]. Meals made in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} using a Warm Safflina will give {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} [[Cold Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Inventory"></ref>  They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Orange [[Dye]].

Three Warm Safflinas, in addition to five {{Plural|BotW|Red Chuchu Jelly|link}}, are needed to upgrade each piece of the [[Snowquill Set]] to its first tier.
