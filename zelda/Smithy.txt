The {{Term/Store|Smithy||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} is a store in the {{Term|HWDE|Bazaar|link}} in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|In the Smithy, you can customize your weapons by fusing new skills onto them or by removing existing skills.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> It becomes available once "{{Term|HWDE|The Sorceress of the Woods|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} has been cleared.<ref>{{Cite|The Smithy is now open for business! You can use it to combine weapon skills by fusing weapons! Press {{Icon|HWDE R}} now to go to the Bazaar.|N/A|HWDE}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
In the {{Term|HWDE|Smithy}}, there are four options that can be chosen to work with {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon|link}}: fusing {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}}, removing {{Plural|HWDE|Skill|link}}, appraising {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Skill|link}}, and selling {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Fuse Weapons}}:<br/>Combine weapons to add skills.<br/>{{Color|HWDE Green|Remove Skills}}:<br/>Remove a skill from a weapon.<br/>{{Color|HWDE Green|Appraise Skills}}:<br/>Check the contents of a sealed skill.<br/>{{Color|HWDE Green|Sell Weapons}}:<br/>Sell a weapon for Rupees.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

{{Term|HWDE|Skill}} on {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} can be transferred to different {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} through fusion.<ref>{{Cite|By fusing weapons, you can transfer {{Color|HWDE Green|skills}} between {{Color|HWDE Green|weapons you own}}.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> In order to fuse {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}}, a {{Term|HWDE|Warrior|link}} must select a base {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} which will receive a {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} and a source {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} which will provide a {{Term|HWDE|Skill}}. Both {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} must be of the same archetype and the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} must have the appropriate amount of {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee|link}}, which are determined by the source {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} and by the type of skills being transferred.<ref name="Fusing Weapons pg2">{{Cite|To transfer a skill, you will need to spend an amount of {{Color|HWDE Green|Rupees}} based on the {{Color|HWDE Green|source weapon}} and the type of skill. The source weapon will also be destroyed in the process.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> Upon fusing two {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}}, the base {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} can only receive one {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} from the source {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}, and the source {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} will be destroyed in the process.<ref>{{Cite|Select weapon to take skill from. (This weapon will be consumed.)|Smithy|HWDE}}</ref><ref name="Fusing Weapons pg2"/> Only {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} with empty {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} slots are permitted to serve as source {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}}, and {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Skill}} cannot be transferred to a different {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} until unlocked.<ref>{{Cite|You can only add skills to weapons with empty skill slots. Overwriting previously activated skills and moving sealed skills won't work.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

In addition to fusion, {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} may be removed from a {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} to provide an empty {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} slot to work with.<ref>{{Cite|By removing a skill, you can change an unlocked skill into an empty skill slot.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> All {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} cost 30,000 {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee}} to remove, and {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} that have been removed cannot be restored.<ref>{{Cite|However, removing a skill requires a lot of Rupees. What's more, removed skills can never be restored, so make sure to think twice before removing one.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Skill}} cannot be removed until they are unlocked.<ref>{{Cite|You cannot select empty slots or locked skills.|Smithy|HWDE}}</ref>

{{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} may also have {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Skill}} on their {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} which cannot be moved off through fusion or removal. {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Skill}} can be appraised, which means to view what the hidden {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} at a rate of 10 {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee}} for each KO required to unlock the {{Term|HWDE|Skill}}.<ref>{{Cite|Check the contents of sealed skills.|Smithy|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|By appraising a sealed weapon skill, you can find out what the sealed skill actually is. The Rupees required to appraise a sealed skill change according to the KO count required to unlock the skill.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> When a {{Term|HWDE|Sealed Skill}} is appraised, the game is saved. This prevents the appraisal process from being abused.

Finally, {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} may also be sold at the {{Term|HWDE|Smithy}}, with their price varying based on the {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} and star rating that the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} has. Empty {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} slots do not contribute to the value of a {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} and {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Skill}} only contribute once they have been unlocked.<ref>{{Cite|When you sell a weapon, you will receive an amount of Rupees dependent on the skills attached to that weapon. The weapons you sell will be lost. Also, sealed skills and empty skill slots do not contribute to the weapon's value, so keep that in mind!|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> The equation for the value of a {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} is dependent upon {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}'s tier, number of stars (between 0 to 5), and the value of a given {{Term|HWDE|Skill}}.
