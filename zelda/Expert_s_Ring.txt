The {{Term/Store|Expert's Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Expert's Ring<br/>Punch when not equipped|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Expert's Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, [[Link]] can punch if no item is equipped.<ref name="Description"/> The Expert's Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
