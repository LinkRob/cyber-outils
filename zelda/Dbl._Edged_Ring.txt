==Location and Uses==
The Dbl. Edged Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it increases [[Sword]] damage while also damaging [[Link]] when a hit is landed.<ref name="Description"/> The Dbl. Edged Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
