{{Term/Store|Stall|Stalls|Series, BotW|plural}} are locations in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 318}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Plural|BotW|Stall}} are the {{Plural|BotW|General Store|link}} located in the center of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. All of the {{Plural|BotW|Stall}} are managed by {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} women. According to {{Term|BotW|Spera|link}}, many of the {{Plural|BotW|Stall}}' proprietors are married and live outside of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, but they return to sell their wares.<ref>{{Cite|[Your husband?] Oh, yes. And a newlywed, too! Many of the vendors who work these stalls are married, in fact. It's pretty typical of married vai to live outside these walls, really. But we come back to sell our wares, of course!|Spera|BotW}}</ref>

==={{Term|BotW|Arrow Specialty Shop}}===
{{Main|Arrow Specialty Shop}}
The {{Term|BotW|Arrow Specialty Shop|link}} is a small {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}-based {{Term|BotW|General Store}} managed by {{Term|BotW|Danda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|As you can see, this is an {{Color|BotW Blue|arrow specialty shop}}.|Danda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Danda}} sells {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}. At night, {{Term|BotW|Danda}}'s granddaughters, {{Term|BotW|Kalani|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Makure|link}}, sleep there.<ref>{{Cite|It's dark out now, so I'm going back to my vaba's booth!|Makure|BotW}}</ref>

==={{Term|BotW|Lorn's Fruit Stand}}===
{{Main|Lorn's Fruit Stand}}
{{Term|BotW|Lorn's Fruit Stand|link}} is a small {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} managed by {{Term|BotW|Lorn|link}}. At night, {{Term|BotW|Lorn}}'s daughter {{Term|BotW|Dalia|link}} sleeps here. {{Term|BotW|Emri|link}}, a fellow fruit merchant at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}}, competes with {{Term|BotW|Lorn}}.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Oh, vasaaq... I've got plenty of {{Color|BotW Blue|hydromelons}}... I bet more than you'll find in {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}. Why not buy a bunch while you're here?|Emri|BotW}}</ref>

==={{Term|BotW|Ardin}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stall}}===
{{Term|BotW|Ardin|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stall}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}} {{Term|BotW|General Store}}. She claims that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} keep their skin beautiful with the {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom}} that she sells.<ref>{{Cite|Just between us... The Gerudo keep their skin so beautiful thanks to the mushrooms that grow here.|Ardin|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys her entire stock, she claims to be selling {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom}} that only the beautiful can see.
