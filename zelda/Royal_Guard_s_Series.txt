==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series}} is a set of equipment made for the {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard|link}}, and can be found at {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series|display=Series}} is built similarly to the {{Term|BotW|Royal Gear|link}}, but instead features hints of {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} design with black metals and highlights of deep red. It has the highest attack power of all the equipment used by the {{Plural|BotW|Soldiers|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref name="CaC"/>

The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series}} was designed and forged by the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} with {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} in preparation of the foretold second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref name="Sword">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Guard's Sword}}<br/>A Sheikah-made replica of the sword that seals the darkness. It was made with ancient technology to oppose the Great Calamity, but its low durability made it inefficient.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Claymore">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Guard's Claymore}}<br/>The Sheikah used the very essence of ancient technology to forge this greatsword. It was designed to oppose the Calamity, but its low durability made it impractical in battle.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Guard's Spear}}<br/>This Sheikah-made spear was created using ancient technology to combat the Calamity. Its attack power is very high, but a critical design flaw left it with poor durability.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Bow">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Guard's Bow}}<br/>This prototype Sheikah-made bow was designed to fight the Great Calamity. Made with ancient technology, it boasts a high rate of fire and firepower but has low durability.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Shield">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Guard's Shield}}<br/>This shield was forged using ancient Sheikah technology. It boasts extremely high stopping power, but its structural weakness made its low durability impractical for combat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} attempted to emulate the power of the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}, resulting in the high power that each item in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series|display=Series}} boasts.<ref name="Sword"/> However, flaws in the designs of these {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} left the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series}} faulted with low durability, which makes them impractical for serious combat.<ref name="Sword"/><ref name="Claymore"/><ref name="Spear"/><ref name="Bow"/><ref name="Shield"/>

Within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} are two of every {{Term|BotW|Item}} in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series}}, which reappear after each {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon|link}}. Surrounding {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} is one of each {{Term|BotW|Item}} inside {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, but they do not respawn.

{{Term|BotW|Parcy|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} traveler who resides at the {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}, often snuck her way into {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} in search of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you gone to the giant castle north of here? That's Hyrule Castle. It's said to have fallen to ruin over a hundred years ago. I used to sneak in there to salvage ancient treasures, but it's too dangerous to go in there now. These days it's surrounded by an eerie mist and savage machine-like creatures.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> However, she was unable to find any of the {{Plural|BotW|Item}} in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series|display=Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Back when it was safer, I went in looking for this ultra-rare equipment that once belonged to the {{Color|BotW Red|royal guard}}. It's legendary among collectors, but I was never able to find any...|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to her, she will task him with "{{Term|BotW|The Royal Guard's Gear|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Though Hyrule Castle was destroyed 100 years ago, somewhere inside it still sleeps rare equipment from those days. It is famed among collectors as the {{Color|BotW Red|royal guard's series}}. Parcy, a traveler at the stable, wants nothing more than to see one of them.<br/><br/>However, the castle is a dangerous place nowadays, and ordinary folk risk their lives getting close...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> As part of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is asked to infiltrate {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} and retrieve any item within the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series}} to show to {{Term|BotW|Parcy}}. If Link succeeds, she will reward him with a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}, completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is...incredible.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you. You have shown me something truly spectacular. I need to go out and find one of these myself.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Here's a token of my appreciation.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref>  At any point after this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can trade one of these {{Plural|BotW|Item}} with {{Term|BotW|Parcy}} in exchange for a {{Term|BotW|Gem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Will you trade it for a topaz?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Will you trade it for a sapphire?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Will you trade it for a ruby?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Will you trade it for a diamond?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref>

All pieces of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series}} is constructed with metal, which means that they will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series}} will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
