The {{Term/Store|Shop Rafters||Series, TMC}} are a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Shop Rafters}} are located above {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. They are only accessible to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} while he is {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, if he climbs up into the {{Term|TMC|Cafe Rafters|link}} and crosses the wooden bridge connecting {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}} to {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop}}. The {{Term|TMC|Shop Rafters}} are inhabited by {{Plural|TMC|Mulldozer|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Pesto|link}}, and have patches of dust that can be cleaned with the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}.

The {{Term|TMC|Shop Rafters}} lead to a staircase behind {{Term|TMC|Stockwell|link}} in his {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|display=Shop}}, allowing {{Term|TMC|Link}} to take the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}} if he promises to feed {{Term|TMC|Fifi|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Dog Food|link}} inside.
