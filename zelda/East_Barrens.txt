The {{Term/Store|East Barrens||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''East Barrens'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The East Barrens are located in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. The [[Gerudo]], [[Pokki]], can be seen collapsed near the entrance to the [[Misae Suma Shrine]] in the East Barrens, inadvertently blocking entry to the [[Ancient Shrine]]. Upon speaking to Pokki, he will be tasked with the [[Shrine Quest]], "[[The Perfect Drink]]".

In ''Tears of the Kingdom'', a [[Molduga]] is found swimming around here.
