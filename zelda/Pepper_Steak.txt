==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Pepper Steaks can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Spicy Pepper]] with any meat in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made by adding additional ingredients, such as {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom|link}} or vegetables, that provide effects that contradict the Spicy Peppers. Eating them will heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The meat in these dishes is cooked in crushed peppers, which suppresses their gamy taste while highlighting their flavor.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Only one variations of Pepper Steak exists:

* '''Spicy Pepper Steak''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Pepper Steak}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A dish made by cooking meat in crushed peppers, suppressing the gamy taste while accentuating its flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using the original recipe. When eaten, these steaks will grant [[Link]] one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>
