{{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Sword}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that are part of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref> They are replicas of the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}} made with {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} to oppose the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, but they were unsuccessful due to their low durability.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, both scattered about and being wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Sword}} is one of several {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series}} that can be given to {{Term|BotW|Parcy|link}} to complete "{{Term|BotW|The Royal Guard's Gear|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you gone to the giant castle north of here? That's Hyrule Castle. It's said to have fallen to ruin over a hundred years ago. I used to sneak in there to salvage ancient treasures, but it's too dangerous to go in there now. These days it's surrounded by an eerie mist and savage machine-like creatures. Back when it was safer, I went in looking for this ultra-rare equipment that once belonged to the {{Color|BotW Red|royal guard}}. It's legendary among collectors, but I was never able to find any...|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wait just a minute! Can you show me that sword you've got there?!|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> As a reward for completing the quest, {{Term|BotW|Parcy}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a token of my appreciation.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, whenever {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Parcy}} while carrying a {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Sword}}, she will ask for it in exchange for a {{Term|BotW|Ruby|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, that sword... Will you trade it for a ruby?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref>

Since they are metallic, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Sword}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they are also capable of conducting {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, which is useful since they will sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Sword}} will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
