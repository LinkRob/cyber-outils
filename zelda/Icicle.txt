{{Term/Store|Icicle|Icicles|Series, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, TotK|plural}} are recurring obstacles in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Exp Game|OoT3D, MM, TotK}}

==Characteristics==
Icicles are generic objects appearing in some games of {{TLoZ|Series}}. They are pointed objects composed of ice that hang from the ceiling and drop, usually when approached. [[Link]] or another character takes damage from an icicle on contact.

==={{MM|-}}===

In {{MM|-}} Icicle is present at the entrance to the [[Snowhead]] region. [[Link]] can knock it down using his [[Hero's Bow]] and [[Arrow]]s. Icicles also appear in [[Snowhead Temple]].

Icicles are mentioned by name in one of [[Tatl]]'s quotes.<ref>{{Cite|What? It's an {{Color|MM Red|icicle}}! It's just like the one we saw outside!|Tatl|MM}}</ref>

==={{TP|-}}===
A piece of Falling Ice is present on the frozen [[Waterfall]] in [[Zora's Domain]], when the land is covered in [[Twilight]]. It falls and nearly hits [[Wolf Link]] as he climbs the Waterfall with [[Midna|Princess Midna]].
