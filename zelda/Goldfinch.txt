The '''Goldfinch''' is an [[Automaton]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Goldfinch|Clockwork bird that knocks [[Rupee|rupees]] out of the enemies it strikes. Goldfinchs are [[Bird]]-like Automatons created by [[Dampé]]. They are obtained as a reward for completing the [[Get Rich Quick!]] [[side quest]], which requires the [[Golden Fan]] and [[Crow]] echo to complete.
