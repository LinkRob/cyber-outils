{{Term/Store|Chilly Elixir|Chilly Elixirs|Series, BotW|plural}},<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Elixir}}<br/>Grants a low/high-level cooling effect, raising your body's resistance to heat. Crucial for long journeys through the desert.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> also known as '''Elixirs That Stave off the Heat''',<ref>{{Cite|I should have made one of those {{Color|BotW Red|elixirs that stave off the heat}} of the desert.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Location and Uses==
Drinking Chilly Elixirs will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They do this by providing a cooling effect, making them important for travelling through the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

When spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Guy|link}} the {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} will complain about the heat at {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But it's so incredibly hot here during the day. It's been tough to work up the will to go any farther.|Guy|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he's heard of an {{Term|BotW|Elixir|link}} that can help with the heat, but it isn't sold at the Bazaar.<ref>{{Cite|I hear there's an {{Color|BotW Red|elixir that can help beat the heat}}. It's made from {{Color|BotW Blue|insects with cooling effects}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|monster parts}}. But they don't seem to be selling the elixir anywhere...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> However, despite knowing that he can find the insects needed in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}, Guy isn't motivated due to the monsters up there.<ref>{{Cite|I've heard {{Color|BotW Blue|insects with these cooling effects}} live in {{Color|BotW Blue|the cold parts of the Gerudo Highlands}}, but... Well, the monsters there are rough, to say the least, so I'm not having a lot of success getting myself motivated...|Guy|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|An Ice Guy|link}}". Link can make a Chilly Elixir for Guy, who will be happy he can cross the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} during the day.<ref>{{Cite|[Here's an elixir.] Thank you! Now I can get across the desert while it's still light out!|Guy|BotW}}</ref> He'll give Link 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Here, let me give you something in return.|Guy|BotW}}</ref>

Chilly Elixirs can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|BotW|Cold Darner|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Winterwing Butterfly|link}}

By adding any of these chilly ingredients, Link can make a Chilly Elixir that will also heal him:

* {{Term|BotW|Hydromelon|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Chillshroom|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}}
