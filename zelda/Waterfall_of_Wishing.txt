The {{Term/Store|Waterfall of Wishing||Series, ALttP}} is a location in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Waterfall Of Wishing is just around the corner.|Sign|ALttP}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Waterfall of Wishing is a large waterfall that can be found in the northeast region of the [[Light World]], partway between the [[Witch's Hut]] and [[Zora's River]].<ref>{{Cite|Whoa... I saw her. A very nice young lady at the Waterfall Of Wishing in the hills where the river begins... Link, you should meet her at least once. I'm sure you will like her.|Hylian|ALttP}}</ref> The Waterfall of Wishing is home to a [[Great Fairy#Waterfall of Wishing Fairy|Great Fairy]] who is known to be a friend of [[Venus]].<ref>{{Cite|I cannot grant any more wishes for you, but a friend of mine might be able to... She lives in the Waterfall Of Wishing near Zora's lake. I will return your Rupees to you. May you be happy...|Venus|ALttP}}</ref> By swimming up the center of the waterfall, [[Link]] can access a secret which contains a small pond known as the [[Mysterious Pond]].<ref>{{Cite| -Mysterious Pond- Won't you throw something in? What will you do? Throw an item/Don't try it|N/A|ALttP}}</ref> Walking up to this pond asks if Link wants to throw something in.  Any item the hero throws in will be returned by the Great Fairy dwelling within the pond, but certain items will be improved upon return if Link answers the Fairy's questions honestly.<ref>{{Cite|Hello there. Did you drop this? I like an honest person. I will give you something better in return.|Great Fairy|ALttP}}</ref> The [[Boomerang]] will be replaced with the {{Term|ALttP|Magical Boomerang|link}}, a [[Fighter's Shield]] will be replaced with a [[Red Shield]], and a {{Term|ALttP|Bottle|link}} will be filled with {{Term|ALttP|Green Potion|link}}. In addition, if any magic-consuming item is thrown, it will be returned with the {{Term|ALttP|Magic Meter|link}} filled up (at least, in the GBA version).
