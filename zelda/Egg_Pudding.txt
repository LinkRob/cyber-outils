==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Egg Puddings are made by combining [[Fresh Milk]], a [[Bird Egg]], and [[Cane Sugar]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they heal {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} for six {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. They also sell for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. They're made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, and they're known to have a soft texture that melts in one's mouth.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can find a recipe for Egg Pudding written in [[Ivee's Diary]].<ref>{{Cite|Today's Recipe:<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Egg Pudding}}|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref> In it, [[Ivee]] lists the ingredients as some Bird Eggs, a few bottles of Fresh Milk, and several servings of Cane Sugar.<ref>{{Cite|Ingredients:<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Bird Eggs}} (a few should do)<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Fresh Milk}} (a few bottles)<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Cane Sugar}} (let's say...several servings)|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref> Though she knows Cane Sugar is difficult to come by, she states that the dish won't work without it.<ref>{{Cite|Note: {{Color|BotW Blue|Cane sugar}} can be tough to come by, but the dish will fail without it.|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

By using additional ingredients, different variations of Egg Pudding can be made:

* '''Hearty Egg Pudding''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Egg Pudding}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, its soft texture melts in your mouth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum number of Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/> Additionally, they sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Energizing Egg Pudding''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Egg Pudding}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, its soft texture melts in your mouth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> When eaten, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/> They also heal him for four Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Enduring Egg Pudding''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Egg Pudding}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, its soft texture melts in your mouth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Not only will these restore Link's Stamina Wheel when eaten, they will also overfill it.<ref name="Enduring"/> They heal Link for five Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Hasty Egg Pudding''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Egg Pudding}}<br/>Grants a mid-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, its soft texture melts in your mouth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> If eaten, these will temporarily increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/> They restore three Hearts to Link's Life Gauge, and they'll sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Spicy Egg Pudding''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Egg Pudding}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, its soft texture melts in your mouth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one will temporarily increase Link's [[Cold Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Spicy"/> They also heal four Hearts in his Life Gauge, and they can be sold for 30 Rupees.

* '''Electro Egg Pudding''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Egg Pudding}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, its soft texture melts in your mouth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when consumed.<ref name="Electro"/> They also heal him for four Hearts, but they sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Egg Pudding''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Egg Pudding}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, its soft texture melts in your mouth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> When eaten, they'll temporarily boost Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/> They also heal for four Hearts, and they can be sold for 40 Rupees.

* '''Tough Egg Pudding''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Egg Pudding}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, its soft texture melts in your mouth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one will temporarily increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/> In addition, they'll heal four Hearts in his Life Gauge, and they sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Sneaky Egg Pudding''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Egg Pudding}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, its soft texture melts in your mouth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> When eaten, they'll grant Link a temporary {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} boost.<ref name="Sneaky"/> They also restore four Hearts to his Life Gauge, and they're worth 30 Rupees when sold.

* '''Chilly Egg Pudding''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Egg Pudding}}<br/>Grants a low-level heat resistance.<br/>Made by cooking eggs and milk in a special mold, its soft texture melts in your mouth.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one increases Link's [[Heat Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Chilly"/> They also heal Link for seven Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.
