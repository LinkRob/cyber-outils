'''Puffers''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Puffers are head-like enemies appearing only in the [[Shrine of Air]]. Their movements appear to consist of either floating above or slide along the ground. Puffers move fairly fast, and can inflict a [[Heart]] of damage on [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] on contact. Zelda can defeat a Puffer by hitting it a few times.
