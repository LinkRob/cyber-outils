The {{Term/Store|Ishto Soh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ishto Soh Shrine}}<br/>Bravery's Grasp}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Bravery's Grasp''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Ishto Soh Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}, west of {{Term|BotW|Oseira Plains|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must climb the western slopes of the {{Term|BotW|Oseira Plains|display=Plains}} and find the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} within piles of bones and rotting trash. As he enters, he will hear the voice of its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ishto Soh|link}}, greeting him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ishto Soh. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Ishto Soh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Ishto Soh Shrine}} features one chamber with a moving platform, a laser, a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}}, and multiple {{Plural|BotW|Launcher|link}}. The {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} will activate when the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} is struck. In order to complete the Trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} on the laser and knock it off of its pillar. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must then place the laser on the moving platform such that the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} will be hit consistently as the platform moves. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can traverse the rudimentary stairs formed by the {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} along the side of the room.

At the top of the stairs, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will meet {{Term|BotW|Ishto Soh}} in their {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Ishto Soh}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ishto Soh|BotW}}</ref> They will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ishto Soh|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} along the north side of the wall contains an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Core|link}}. Another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} behind the laser beam contains a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}}.
