{{Term/Store|Hourglass|Hourglasses|Series, TFH|plural}} are objects in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TFH|publisher= Prima|page=192}}</ref>

==Locations and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Hourglass}} are exclusively found in timed {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}}, granting the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} extra time when collected. Two types of {{Plural|TFH|Hourglass}} exist: small {{Plural|TFH|Hourglass}} and large {{Plural|TFH|Hourglass}}, each granting an additional 5 and 20 seconds respectively. While a {{Term|TFH|Links}} is wearing the {{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform|link}} during the challenge, any {{Plural|TFH|Hourglass}} collected by that {{Term|TFH|Links}} will give a 1.5 multiplier boost to the overall time received. Thus, small {{Plural|TFH|Hourglass}} grant 7.5 seconds while large {{Plural|TFH|Hourglass}} yield 30 seconds each.

Any time gained with {{Plural|TFH|Hourglass}} remains with the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}, even if they faint, allowing for considerably more supplemental time to be obtained at the cost of a {{Term|TFH|Fairy|link}} if done properly.

==Trivia==
*The act of receiving more time while wearing the {{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform}} is a nod to the game in which {{Term|PH|Linebeck|link}} originates, {{PH}}. In said game, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} utilizes the titular {{Term|PH|Phantom Hourglass (Item)|link}} to traverse the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}} with a limited amount of time determined by the {{Term|PH|Sand of Hours|link}}.
*An {{Term|TFH|Hourglass}} icon also appears as a timer when on Multiplayer when each of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are choosing an Area, Challenge, {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} or Arena.
