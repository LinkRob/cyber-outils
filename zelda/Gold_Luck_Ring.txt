The {{Term/Store|Gold Luck Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Gold Luck Ring<br/>1/2 damage from falls|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Gold Luck Ring can be found inside [[Ambi's Palace]] in {{OoA}}. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it reduces damage taken from falls.<ref name="Description"/> The Gold Luck Ring can be transferred to {{OoS}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
