==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Herb Sautés are dishes made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any vegetable, herb, or flower together with [[Goron Spice]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their neutral versions are made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb|link}}, which offer no status effects. When eaten, they'll replenish some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Herb Sautés are fragrant mixtures of herbs and spices that have a unique aroma.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Different variations of Herb Sautés can be made, depending on the type of ingredients used:

* '''Hearty Herb Sauté''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Herb Sauté}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A fragrant mixture of herbs and spices. It's easily recognized by its unique aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}. Eating one will completely heal all Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Enduring Herb Sauté''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Herb Sauté}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A fragrant mixture of herbs and spices. It's easily recognized by its unique aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}. These sautés completely refill and overfill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} when consumed.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Herb Sauté''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Herb Sauté}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A fragrant mixture of herbs and spices. It's easily recognized by its unique aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}}. Link gains a temporary boost to his movement speed when he eats them.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Herb Sauté''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Herb Sauté}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A fragrant mixture of herbs and spices. It's easily recognized by its unique aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}}. When eaten, they grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Herb Sauté''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Herb Sauté}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A fragrant mixture of herbs and spices. It's easily recognized by its unique aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}}. If eaten, [[Link]] will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]] temporarily.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Herb Sauté''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Herb Sauté}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A fragrant mixture of herbs and spices. It's easily recognized by its unique aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}}. They can be eaten to increase the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Herb Sauté''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Herb Sauté}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A fragrant mixture of herbs and spices. It's easily recognized by its unique aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}}. They increase Link's defense if he eats them.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Herb Sauté''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Herb Sauté}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A fragrant mixture of herbs and spices. It's easily recognized by its unique aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}. When eaten, they increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period of time.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Herb Sauté''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Herb Sauté}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A fragrant mixture of herbs and spices. It's easily recognized by its unique aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}}. Eating them grants Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
