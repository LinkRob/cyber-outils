==Location and Uses==
Naydra's Scales can be collected from the dragon, [[Naydra]], who rests atop [[Mount Lanayru]]. After defeating [[Malice Naydra]] and freeing the dragon, [[Link]] will be able to shoot its main body and retrieve the Scale. Link must place a Scale inside the [[Spring of Wisdom]] to complete the Shrine Quest "[[The Spring of Wisdom]]". Doing so will yield the way to [[Jitan Sa'mi Shrine]]. A Scale can also be found in a chest inside [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]].

[[Cooking]] with Naydra's Scales will add 90 seconds to the food's [[effect]] and guarantee a critical dish. They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Light Blue [[Dye]].

Two of Naydra's Scales, as well as ten {{Plural|Series|Acorn|link}}, are needed to upgrade the [[Tunic of the Wild]] to its first tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. Another scale can be used to upgrade the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Armor|link}} to its first tier. Two scales are required to upgrade the [[Snow Boots]] to their fourth tier. 

* Total for '''Fierce Deity Armor''': 1 (1, 0, 0, 0)

* Total for '''Tunic of the Wild''': 2 (2, 0, 0, 0)

* Total for '''Snow Boots''': 2 (0, 0, 0, 2)
