==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Stump Entrance}} are a type of {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}}. They are the most common type of {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|display=Portal}}, and are found across the {{Term|TMC|Overworld|link}}. They allow {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, with {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}}'s help, to reduce his size to that of a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}, and are also used to return {{Term|TMC|Link}} to normal size. There are 16 in total.
