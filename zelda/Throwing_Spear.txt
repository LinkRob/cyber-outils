{{Plural|BotW|Throwing Spear}} are {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}} balanced to be thrown farther than the average spear.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are capable of striking {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} at a great distance.<ref name="Inventory"/> Commonly, they are found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Throwing Spear}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Throwing Spear}} will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
