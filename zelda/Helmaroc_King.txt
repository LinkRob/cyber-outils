The {{Term/Store|Helmaroc King|Helmaroc Kings|Series, TWW, TWWHD, FSA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Boss|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 184 {{Exp Game|TWW, TWWHD, FSA}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's the Helmaroc King. It carries people off! It'll take the town keep!|Tetra|HWDE}} {{Exp Game|HWDE}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWL}}

==Characteristics==
==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, the Helmaroc King, a massive [[Kargarok]], is the secondary antagonist. He resides in the [[Forsaken Fortress]] (as its [[Boss]]), and is a servant of [[Ganondorf]], the human form of [[Ganon]].<ref>{{Cite|I suppose you saw him... The shadow that commands that monstrous bird... His name is...Ganon...|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> He is also the primary minion sent out by Ganondorf to search for [[Princess Zelda]]. He appears early on in the game, flying around [[Outset Island]] with [[Tetra]] in his claws before being shot out of the sky by [[Tetra's Pirate Crew]]. The Helmaroc King then drops Tetra and captures [[Link]]'s younger sister, [[Aryll]], mistaking her for the young pirate captain.<ref>{{Cite|My point is that the bird mistook that poor girl for you, and that's why it grabbed her!|Quill|TWW}}</ref> It is later discovered that the Helmaroc King was bringing young girls with pointed ears to Ganondorf in an effort to find Princess Zelda,<ref>{{Cite|Haven't any of you heard word that young girls have been getting kidnapped lately from all regions of the Great Sea? [...] Young girls with long ears like yours have been getting kidnapped, never to be seen again.|Quill|TWW}}</ref> as Ganondorf wished to find the princess so that he could steal [[Triforce of Wisdom|her piece]] of the [[Triforce]].<ref>{{Cite|Ha! At long last, I have found you... Princess Zelda! [...] Tell me... If you are not Zelda, then why do you have this fragment of the Triforce?|Ganondorf|TWW}}</ref> When Link travels to the Forsaken Fortress to save his sister, the Helmaroc King captures him and throws him into the [[Great Sea]] at Ganondorf's command by simply nudging his head.

Later in the game, Link returns to the Fortress in an attempt to save Aryll once again, now armed with a powerless [[Master Sword]]. He successfully frees his sister and the other kidnapped girls, [[Mila]] and [[Maggie (The Wind Waker)|Maggie]], while Tetra and her pirate crew distract the giant bird.<ref>{{Cite|Do you even realize that we had to draw that monster bird off for you? It was about to come flying in here after you again!|Tetra|TWW}}</ref> Link is then attacked by the Helmaroc King after Tetra and her crew flee.

====Characteristics====
[[File:Helmarocbattle.png|thumb|right|210x210px|The Helmaroc King chasing Link in {{TWW|-}}]]
At the beginning of the boss battle, the tower containing the prison cells is flooded. The Helmaroc King follows Link as he runs along the wall to avoid the rising water. While he does so, Link must avoid the giant bird and the [[Bokoblins]] along the steps of the wall. Once he has reached the top, the bird will swoop down and stand right in his way, and will attack if Link stands too close. The young hero must then use the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer|link}} on his helmet, making the Helmaroc King fall into the water, giving Link the chance to climb out of the flooding tower. At this point, the floor of the tower begins to close and the bird flies up out of the tower in time to challenge the hero. If Link falls off and into the water, he can get back up by using the [[Grappling Hook]] on the torches.

The Helmaroc King performs three attacks. During the first one, he lands on the ground, walks toward Link, and attempts to impale him with his beak. If he misses Link, his beak gets jammed into the floor. This gives Link a chance to use the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer}} and drive it into the Helmaroc King's helmet. This produces a large crack on the bird's helmet. The bird can also fly around the tower trying to run over Link by scratching his talons on the floor, as well as floating in the air and flapping his wings in an attempt to blow the hero into the spikes surrounding the arena. If Link is directly under the monster, the Helmaroc King will attempt to stomp him.

After Link pounds the King's helmet with the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer}} several times, it will crack and fall off, making it vulnerable. Once the evil bird's crest is exposed, he will become more frantic. The strategy is still the same; Link must wait until the bird tries to peck him with his beak. The main difference this time is that the Master Sword can be used to deal more damage. After repeating the pattern enough times, the Helmaroc King is defeated, where it flies up into the air and explodes into black and purple smoke (with all [[Searchlight|Searchlights]] flashing at it), leaving behind a [[Heart Container]].

{{Figurine|TWW|The Monstrous Helmaroc King}}

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, the Helmaroc King is a boss Link must face on [[The Mountain Path]] on [[Death Mountain]]. The Helmaroc King is designated to stop the [[Links]] from entering the [[Tower of Flames]]. This monstrous bird starts the battle by swooping down upon them two times, then attacking by dropping rocks upon the heroes. After that, the King repeats the process. In order to defeat him, the Links are required to daze the bird with their [[Bomb]]s and then destroy the bird's mask. After the mask is destroyed, the Links can defeat him by using their [[Sword]]s.

==={{HW|L|-}}===
In {{HW|L}}, the Helmaroc King appears as a Giant Boss in the [[Legend Mode#Wind Waker|Wind Waker]] [[Legend Mode]] Scenarios, "[[A New Disturbance]]" and "[[The Search for Cia]]". It also appears as a Giant Bosses in certain [[Adventure Mode]] maps that are exclusive to {{HW|L|-}}. Its weakness is the {{Term|HWDE|Hammer|link}}.

In "A New Disturbance", under the name '''Great Bird''', the Helmaroc King leads the [[Great Bird's Forces]], of which it is the only member. The [[Monster Forces]], led by the [[Boss Blin]], manage to drive it from its roost in the [[Forsaken Fortress]], causing it to attack the [[Hyrulean Forces]] led by [[Link]] and [[Tetra]] in [[Windfall]]. Link and Tetra can force it to retreat by attacking it. The Helmaroc King acts as little more than a distraction, and it cannot be defeated as the main objective is to defeat the Boss Blin and the Monster Forces instead. After the defeat of Boss Blin and its Forces, Link spots the Helmaroc King in the sky and notices that it is carrying Tetra in its talons. Link rushes after the bird to save Tetra.

In "The Search for Cia", the Helmaroc King then roosts in the [[Gerudo Desert (Hyrule Warriors)|Gerudo Desert]]. While it is resting, Tetra manages to sneak away and seeks the aid of [[Lana]] and the Hyrulean Forces, who had entered the desert to close the [[Gate of Souls]] that opened there. Eventually, the Helmaroc King awakes and tries to recaptures Tetra. She calls Lana and the recently arrived Link to save her. Together they manage to destroy the Helmaroc King's mask, forcing it to retreat momentarily. However the bird soon returns, only to be killed by the combined might of Lana, Link and Tetra.

When defeated in battle, the Helmaroc King may occasionally drop its [[Helmaroc Plume|Plume]] as a silver [[Material]], and rarely its [[Helmaroc King's Mask|Mask]] as a gold Material.

==Trivia==
*The Helmaroc King and the [[Helmasaur King]] have the same name in Japanese, but they have different English names and official romanizations: the official Japanese website romanizes the Helmaroc King's Japanese name as "'''Zeeklock'''"<ref>{{Cite Web|retrieved=January 3, 2015|url= http://www.nintendo.co.jp/ngc/gzlj/syosai/chara/zeeklock.jpg|title=zeeklock.jpg |site= Nintendo.co.jp|type=}}</ref> whereas the Japanese ending of {{ALttP}} romanizes the Helmasaur King's Japanese name as "Jeakrock".{{Fact|ALttP}}
**This confusion led to the Helmaroc King trophy in the North American version of ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' erroneously stating that he and the Helmasaur King are the same character. The European version fixed this error.
*The Helmaroc King is the second largest flying creature in {{TWW}}, behind [[Valoo]], who is a [[Dragon]]. In the official artwork used for the game's box art, the two can be seen charging at each other.
*This boss is likely the leader of the standard [[Kargarok|Kargaroks]] seen throughout {{TWW|-}}.
*The [[Helmaroc Plume]] [[Treasure]] from {{PH}} is a reference to the Helmaroc King, as the bird itself does not appear in this game due to it being already eliminated.<ref>{{Cite|You got a Helmaroc Plume! It was dropped by the mythical Helmaroc!|N/A|PH}}</ref>
*The Helmaroc King, in the way that he kidnaps young girls with pointed ears, could be a reference to the mythical Thunderbird, a bird that is about the size of the Helmaroc King and who also kidnaps children.
**Coincidentally, the penultimate boss of {{TAoL}} is called the [[Thunderbird]] and has a vaguely similar appearance to the Helmaroc King.
