==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Monster Extracts are sold by [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] during the night. It can also be acquired as a reward for rescuing [[Chabi]], who is searching for the Fang and Bone. Though it can't be eaten, dishes made in a [[Cooking Pot]] with Monster Extract will have their effects randomly affected (increased or decreased).<ref name="Inventory"/> It's also one of the ingredients that can be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Purple [[Dye]].

Using Monster Extract while [[Cooking]] will result in monstrous meals.<ref name="Inventory"/> These include [[Monster Cake]], [[Monster Curry]], [[Monster Rice Balls]], [[Monster Soup]], and [[Monster Stew]].

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
Monster Extracts can now be bought in [[Tarrey Town|'''Tarrey Town''']] for 50 [[Rupee|Rupees]]. You may also find them drop from monsters or creatures such as '''[[Evermean]]'''.

It is implied that '''[[Moza]]''' used Monster Extracts to fix your [[Dubious Food|'''failed dishes''']] and [[Rock-Hard Food|'''rock hard food''']] in her [[The Rikoka Hills Well|Hideaway Restaurant]].
