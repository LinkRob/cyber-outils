{{Term/Store|Zora Village||Series, OoA}} is a location in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 111 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> It is an underwater village found in the [[Zora Seas]] of [[Labrynna]]. The village is inhabited mostly by [[Zora]]s, and is also home to the great [[Lord Jabu-Jabu|Jabu-Jabu]], where another [[Essence of Time]] resides. The Zora Village can only be reached after [[Link]] has obtained the [[Mermaid Suit]] and learned the [[Tune of Ages]].

==History==
In the past during [[Queen Ambi]]'s age, [[King Zora]] had fallen with an illness due to the polluted water coming from the east.<ref>{{Cite|The filthy water that flowed in from the east lately must have made me ill.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> His sickness could only be cured with a [[Magic Potion]],<ref>{{Cite|We believe we need a special medicine called a Magic Potion to cure King Zora's illness.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> however the Zoras were unable to find one, and the King Zora would later pass away. As the King Zora did not leave a successor to the throne, the Zoras would be left without a king in the present time.<ref>{{Cite|King Zora has no successor. If his illness continues and he passes on, we Zoras will lose our last king.|Zora|OoA}}</ref> A Zora in the present says that their last king, the King Zora, had died centuries ago. Link can give the Zora King in the past a Magic Potion to cure his illness. Once cured, the King Zora in the present will grant Link the [[Library Key]] in order to research the causes on why the water of the Zora Seas has been so polluted lately.<ref>{{Cite|This is the key to Eyeglass Isle Library east of here. It is an ancient library, so I do not know if this key still works, but perhaps you can research why the seawater is so filthy. I trust you to save Jabu-Jabu.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> The young hero, with the help of the [[Fairy Queen]], is able rid the sea of its pollution in the past, which grants Link permission to enter inside [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]] during the present time and receive another [[Essences of Time]] in the process.

As Link exits the insides of Jabu-Jabu, a Zora crosses his path and thanks Link for all he has done for the Zora people, granting the young hero a [[Zora Scale]] for his help.<ref>{{Cite|You have done so much for us! Our seas are clean and all is well! I offer you our symbol of heroism--the Zora Scale!|Zora|OoA}}</ref>

A [[Mystical Tree|Gale Tree]] can be found on one of the islands on the surface above the village, providing easy [[Warping|warp]] access to the area. In a [[Linked Game]], the [[Master Diver]]'s senior pupil appears on the shore. He has a [[Linked Game/Secrets#Diver Secret|secret]] for Link to tell to his master in [[Holodrum]].<ref>{{Cite|Say, aren't you a pupil of the master diver in Holodrum's Sunken City? I have a secret I'd like you to tell to your master.|Human|OoA}}</ref>
