{{Compendium/Extract|Bokoblin Arm}}
{{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Arm}} are the arms of {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}} that they drop when they are defeated. Even though they have been removed from their bodies, they continue to move on their own.<ref name="Inventory"/> Due to their old age, they break quickly.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made from bone, {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Arm}} will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}}, making them a good choice to use during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. If they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, they will not burn like a wooden {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} would.
