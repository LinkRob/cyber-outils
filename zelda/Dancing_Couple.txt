{{Term/Store|Dancing Couple||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} are a pair characters in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Dancing Couple first appear in [[Hyrule Castle Town|Castle Town]] when [[Link]] is a child, where they can be seen in the market dancing and complimenting each other with words of affection.<ref>{{Cite|Ohh... You are...so handsome... Just like the King of Hyrule... Hee hee...|Dancing Couple|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ohh... You are...more beautiful... than Princess Zelda... Hee hee...|Dancing Couple|OoT}}</ref> They can later be found at [[Kakariko Village]] behind the [[Windmill]] after [[Ganondorf]] invades [[Hyrule]], where they will call Link annoying for invading their personal space.<ref>{{Cite|Heh.... What an annoying person, interrupting us like this... Don't you agree, my love?|Dancing Couple|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hee hee... Violating our personal space! He must have something better to do... Don't you agree, darling?|Dancing Couple|OoT}}</ref>
