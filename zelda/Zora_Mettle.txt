{{Term/Store|Zora Mettle||HWAoC}} is a level 56 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Zora Mettle|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Zora Mettle}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}. When completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Salmon|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Bass|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to defeat an {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus|link}}. and a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel}} is in the southwest corner of the map, while the {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus}} is in the north and the {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus}} is in the south. There are also two other {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} that do not have to be defeated, but will attempt to capture one of the two Allied Strongholds. Scattered around the map are {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}} in the southeast as well, along with a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} in the center and western area of the map. They stay in their position until the {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus}} is approached, and at that point they advance towards the northern Allied Stronghold. At the same time, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} appears in the southwest and does the same. There is one {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, located between the northern Allied Stronghold and the {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus}}. There are two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} in front of this {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, and when it is captured, it disappears instead of belonging to the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When a {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} enters this {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe}} appears near the southern Allied Stronghold and and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}} near the north, which both advance to capture it alongside {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}} of their element. It is also worth noting that the two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin}} that start in the eastern and central area of the map will not enter the Allied Stronghold until the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}} is defeated, and instead stay right outside of the stronghold until that happens. Some {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin}} are scattered around the map as well, and the general areas of the {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel}} are scattered with {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}} of their element or type. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the three {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
