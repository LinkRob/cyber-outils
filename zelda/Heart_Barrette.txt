The '''Heart Barrette''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Heart Barrette|Fancy barrette with a heart motif. It makes hearts appear more often.<ref name="Might Barrette">{{Cite|Fancy barrette with a heart motif. It makes hearts appear more often.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Heart Barrette is rewarded for completing the [[Beetle Ballyhoo]] [[Side Quest]]. Equipping it grants the [[Heart Appearance Up +]] effect, increasing the drop rate of [[hearts]].
