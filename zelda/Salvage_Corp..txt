The {{Term/Store|Salvage Corp.||Series, TWW, TWWHD}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259}}</ref> also known as '''Salvage Corp''',<ref>{{Cite|Salvage Corp|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> is a group of young treasure hunters in {{TWW}}.

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Salvage Corp}}
The Salvage Corp can be found trying to salvage Treasure around [[Pawprint Isle]], [[Eastern Triangle Island]], and [[Western Fairy Island]].

When [[Link]] first encounters the Corp, they will happily tell him how to use the [[Grappling Hook]] like a [[Salvage Arm]] to raise up treasure from the ocean floor.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ya!!! Bub!}} You collecting treasure, bub? To see where you can find treasure, open your {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Map}}. If you compare the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Island Charts}} on the right with the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Treasure Charts}} on the left, it should be easy to figure out! The secret to hauling up treasure is to go to the spot marked on the map and then use your {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Grappling Hook}} while {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|in your boat}}. Then it just takes patience, bub! A lot of first-time treasure hunters give up pretty quickly because they don't get how it works, but they're missing out! It's really not that hard!|Salvage Corp|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So that's how it works!|Salvage Corp|TWWHD}}</ref> If spoken to near [[Eastern Triangle Island]], they will also give Link the [[Treasure Chart]] 34. If Link asks them how to use the [[Grappling Hook]] again, they will begrudgingly tell him again before shooing him away.<ref>{{Cite|...Bub... ...I don't know how many times I have to explain it to you, but here goes... If you want to search for treasure, first open your {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Map}}, and then compare the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Island Charts}} on the right with the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Treasure Charts}} on the left to figure out where the treasure is! You get that part, right? You'll have to use both your {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Treasure Charts}} and your {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Grappling Hook}} to haul up the treasure. You got that, bub? And do you understand what we're doing now? Good!|Salvage Corp|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So, you understand what treasure hunting is all about now, right, bub?|Salvage Corp|TWWHD}}</ref>

After the [[Tower of the Gods]] rises up from the Sea, the Salvage Corp will give up hunting for minor treasure. They give Link all of [[Rupee]]s that they have found, and focus on finding the [[Triumph Fork]]s instead.{{Fact}}
