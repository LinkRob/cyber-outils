As a long-running and critically acclaimed video game series, ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' has been referenced numerous times within various forms of media and has become a large part of popular culture. This page covers notable homages, parodies and references, not just in other video games but in television and movies. The page does '''not''' include official cameos or crossovers within other [[Nintendo]] games or officially licensed third party appearances, as this covers unlicensed cases. Please visit [[Cameos of The Legend of Zelda in other media]] for examples of these.

==Video Games==
===''{{Wp|3D Dot Game Heroes}}''===
This game is about bringing back the 8-bit nostalgia, and shows many hints from many popular games, especially for {{TLoZ|Series}}. Many of the ''Zelda'' similarities include:
* The overworld is very similar, it has many areas such as towns, dungeons, a lost forest, a castle, and a mountain area where rocks bounce while falling.
* If the chicken gets hit many times, they will get mad and bring back up.
* The enemies resemble [[Armos]], [[Darknuts]], [[Gibdos]], [[Moblin]]s, [[Keese]], [[Lynel]]s, [[Like Like]]s, [[Octorok]]s, [[Stalfos]], [[Tektite]]s, [[Zol]]s, [[Wizzrobe]]s, [[Patra]]s, and [[Ganon]] with extra arms, all in 3D pixels.
* The sword will shoot beams when the health is at its max.
* If a character breaks into a secret cave, he can either be rewarded or fined. An old man even says "It is a secret to everyone".
* A fairy will join the hero's party.
* One of their characters is called Moyomoto, who is the brother to Wigy. This is a reference to the ''Zelda'' creator [[Shigeru Miyamoto]].
* The fight with the Dark Bishop Fuelle is very similar to the boss fight with [[Agunima]] in {{OoS}}.
* The final boss name is Onyx, similar to [[General Onox]]. Onyx is also a dragon, much like Onox's true form.
* Many dungeons and puzzles are the same, but in 3D.

===''{{Wp|Enter the Gungeon}}''===
The Bullet's default weapon is a sword known as Blasphemy. Similar to the [[Master Sword]], it is able to fire sword beams when The Bullet is at full hearts, and it loses this ability after taking damage. The Bullet's past is also a reference to {{ALttP}}. The Bullet starts off in a hallway where he talks to an old man bullet that gives him a de-powered Blasphemy. The Bullet must then go on to kill the "Dark Magician, Agunim". This fight is based on the second round of the fight with [[Agahnim]] in {{ALttP|-}}. After defeating Agunim, a hole opens up in the floor and The Bullet proceeds to fight the "Evil King, Cannon", a fight heavily based on the [[Ganon]] fight in {{ALttP|-}}. The Bullet also has an alternate skin resembling a rabbit, referencing [[Link]]'s appearance in the [[Dark World]].

===''{{Wp|Final Fantasy I and II (compilations)|Final Fantasy I & II Dawn of Souls}}''===
If the main character reads a certain tombstone in ''Final Fantasy I'', it reads "Here Lies [[Link]]." It is in the town of elves, Elfheim, near what could be called the bottom of the map. The original US localization for ''Final Fantasy I'' was done by [[Nintendo of America]] and this tombstone was changed to read "Here lies Erdrick", referring to the legendary hero from the ''Dragon Quest'' series instead. Subsequent ports and remakes changed the reference back to Link.

===''{{Wp|The Binding of Isaac (video game)|The Binding of Isaac}}''===
[[File:I AM ERROR.png|thumb|right|200px|The I AM ERROR character]]
This indie game by Edmund McMillen of Team Meat is an isometric dungeon crawler heavily influenced by the original {{TLoZ}}. The most notable influence graphic-wise is the HUD, which closely mimics the design and style of the one in {{TLoZ|-}}.
:Multiple items in the game are references to items in {{TLoZ|-}}. Among them are Hearts, Bombs, and Keys, but there are also items that function the same and resemble the [[Compass]], [[Map]], and [[Stepladder]].
:Several enemies behave similarly to enemies from {{TLoZ|-}}. Some of these enemies behave the same as [[Rope]]s and [[Darknut]]s. The bosses Chub, C. H. A. D., and the Carrion Queen have the same weakness as [[Dodongo]]. :In some endings of the game, the protagonist will open a treasure chest, and take out an item. he will then hold the item up high, exactly like the {{TLoZ|Series}}, while the "Item Get!" theme will play.<ref>http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cBdupSUQ0Wo</ref>

=== ''[[wikipedia:Tunic_(video_game)|Tunic]]'' ===
''Tunic'' is heavily inspired by {{TLoZ|-}} franchise. The protagonist is a fox who wears a green tunic and uses a sword and a shield that bears resemblance to the [[Hylian Shield]]. The game is in a top-down perspective, and the player explores an overworld to find dungeons to enter and collect keys to free a royal figure from a crystal that they have been trapped in. Various elements from {{TLoZ|-}} also appear, such as the ability to [[Rolling|roll]] to move faster and kneeling in front of a statue to regain your health, similar to praying at a [[Goddess Statue]] in {{BotW|-}}.

===''{{Wp|World of Warcraft}}''===
[[File:WoW Linken-Link Similarity.jpg|thumb|right|150px|Linken in ''World of Warcraft'']]
: A long quest chain is available in the zone Un'Goro Crater which heavily references ''The Legend of Zelda'' series.
: It begins when the player locates a stranded raft (a reference to {{LA}}) in Un'Goro Crater, which starts the quest "It's a Secret to Everybody." The player must then find a bag nearby it, containing a Curled Map Parchment, a Large Compass, a Lion-headed Key, and a Faded Photograph. The photograph shows a picture of a pair of gnomes dressed similarly to Zelda and Link, standing in front of a ruined castle. The player then has to find the gnome dressed as Link in the photo, named Linken, who is located at a camp in the zone, to continue.
: Further down the quest-line, the player is tasked with stealing a plant from a gnoll named Miblon Snarltooth. Miblon will respond with "Grumble grumble" if he is spoken to. To distract Miblon to steal the plant, the player must purchase a Bait item from a nearby dwarf, who will sell it to the main character with the quote, "Buy something, will ya?" Both quotes, and the ability of meat to distract monsters, are a reference to the first ''Zelda''.
: Near the end of the quest-line, the player must summon a water elemental named Aquementas to do a task for them, in reference to the dragon [[Aquamentus]].
: The end of the quest chain, named "It's Dangerous to Go Alone," involves the player slaying a fire elemental and taking the Golden Flame it was guarding back to Linken. The Golden Flame resembles a golden triangle, and Linken says it is "dangerous in the hands of those with an evil heart." The rewards for completing this quest are Linken's Sword of Mastery, Linken's Boomerang, and the Spirit of Aquementas.
: There is a kodo in Un'Goro Crater named Dadanga, and its owner says it "dislikes smoke.", a reference to the first game, when a man says "[[Dodongo|DODONGO]] DISLIKES SMOKE."

===''{{Wp|The Powerpuff Girls}}''===
[[File:TPG Link Navi Cameo.png|thumb|265x265px|Link and Navi in ''The Powerpuff Girls'']]
In the episode "Child Fearing" of Season 3, a reference to ''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'' is seen, with two characters appearing to be Link and [[Navi]]. The former was not officially announced as Link himself, but it has many of his key features, as well as what looks like a [[Fairy]] beside him, which says "Hey!". The character was in a game, controlled by the Mayor of Townsville but has trouble doing so. The Mayor soon gets suddenly smashed by a giant brick square (costing three [[Heart Container|Hearts]]), run into walls, and then makes the character slash and kill the creature which looks like a Fairy (humorously losing another three Hearts). Afterward, the Mayor gets a "start over?", implying a [[Game Over]], in which the screen zooms out of the game, and the viewers can clearly see that the character was indeed playing a ''Zelda'' game based on ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', as well as the Mayor holding what looks like a [[Nintendo 64]] controller. According to series creator Craig McCracken, he stated that when ''Ocarina of Time'' was released, many of the staff had played the game so much that they missed work at times due to the fact the N64 ''Zelda'' title was a massive hit, and so they created this as a comical homage.

===''<span class="plainlinks">[http://www.homestarrunner.com Homestar Runner]</span>''===
A large number of references have been made by ''Homestar Runner'', particularly in the Halloween cartoons. A full list can be found on the [http://www.hrwiki.org/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda ''Homestar Runner'' Wiki]. Some notable references include:

* In the Strong Bad Email "[https://homestarrunner.com/sbemails/57-japanese-cartoon japanese cartoon]", one of the "NES endings" is of [[The Legend of Zelda|''The Legend of Zelda'']]. Additionally, the credits of the hypothetical ''Stinkoman K'' anime were lifted from the game (i.e., "[[Takashi Tezuka|Ten Ten]]", [[Shigeru Miyamoto|S. Miyahon]], and "[[Koji Kondo|Konchan]]").
* In "[https://homestarrunner.com/toons/halloween-potionmajig Halloween Potion-ma-jig]", Homsar is dressed as [[Tingle]].
* In the Strong Bad Email "[https://homestarrunner.com/sbemails/162-the-chair the chair]", attempting to decompile an supposed image of Strong Bad's parents reveals the text "Nice try, [[Dodongo]]!"
** The "Nice try, Dodongo" line would reappear in the teaser for "The Homestar Runner Enters the Spooky Woods".
* In the DVD-exclusive toon "Why Come Only One Girl?", it is claimed the reason a scrapped character, The Hurricane, never appeared in a toon was because the Brothers Chaps (the creators of ''Homestar Runner'') were distracted by the newest Zelda game at the time (presumably [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|''The Wind Waker'']]). Homestar follows this by exclaiming, "Why, [[Ganon|Ganondorf]]? Why?!". He then captures Marzipan in a bottle (similar to how Link captures fairies) and sings the "item get" jingle.
* In "[https://homestarrunner.com/toons/compy-catalog Compy Catalog]", the Poopsmith is seen wearing a hat and bag resembling the [[Postman]]'s, with The Cheat in place of the rabbit on [[Postman's Hat|the hat]].
* In "[https://homestarrunner.com/toons/doomy-tales-of-the-macabre Doomy Tales of the Macabre]", the Poopsmith is dressed as [[Darunia]]. In an Easter egg, Homestar (dressed as Rubin Farr from ''Rubin and Ed'') sings [[Saria's Song]], causing the Poopsmith to dance.
** In the same toon, Strong Bad dresses up as a [[Skull Kid]].
* In "[https://homestarrunner.com/toons/mr-poofers-must-die Mr. Poofers Must Die]", The Cheat is dressed up as a [[Korok]].
* In "[https://homestarrunner.com/toons/halloween-hijinks Halloween Hijinks]", Bubs dresses up as [[Beedle]], in his appearance from [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|''Breath of the Wild'']]. In an Easter egg, Homestar (dressed as Bod from ''Bod'') compliments the costume, while claiming that he is dressed as "70's Bubs giving a piggyback ride to Totoro eating a purple burrito" in the tune to the series' main theme tune.

==Cultural influence==
[[File:Google Maps Street View Link.png|thumb|right|200px|{{Term|Series|Link}}'s appearance on Google Maps]]
*The band {{Wp|Showbread (band)|Showbread}} have expressed devout appreciation for the series in several ways. They [http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b6IxwaJMpM8 came onstage] to {{TWW|-}} intro music during their Reptilian Empire Tour in 2006. Three members of the band can be seen wearing matching [[Triforce]] rings in their music video for "[http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yWU4W_JcUH4 Mouth Like a Magazine]".
*Joe Pleiman created a song parody of the main ''Zelda'' theme. It was recorded by his band "The Rabbit Joint" for their 1998 album of the same name. The song is commonly falsely attributed to System of a Down.
*Boom Bip's song "Roads Must Roll" from the album ''Seed to Sun'' (Lex Records, 2002) contains melodies from the title theme song to {{TAoL}}.
*The late Robin Williams named his daughter "Zelda" because ''Zelda'' was his favorite video game series. When his wife was pregnant with their daughter, they had been playing {{OoT|-}} constantly and both had decided they would name their daughter after [[Princess Zelda]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= Jun 15, 2011|retrieved= |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6oYMtqZk4Y|title= Robin Williams and daughter Zelda talk about their love for Ocarina of Time|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref>
*On the XM channel, 80s on 8, {{TLoZ|-}} theme is occasionally put in the background.
*To commemorate the release of {{TPHD}}, Google Maps added an image of {{Term|Series|Link}} to their app to represent the user's position when using Street View mode.<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.theverge.com/2016/3/4/11160962/google-maps-the-legend-of-zelda |title= Link from Legend of Zelda is now exploring Google Maps |site= The Verge |published= May 4, 2016}}</ref>
*On 18 October, 2023, British power metal band [[wikipedia:DragonForce|DragonForce]] released a song called "[[wikipedia:Power of the Triforce|Power of the Triforce]]" as the second single from their ninth studio album ''[[wikipedia:Warp Speed Warriors|Warp Speed Warriors]]''.
