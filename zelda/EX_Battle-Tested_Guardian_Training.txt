{{Term/Store|EX Battle-Tested Guardian Training||HWAoC}} is a level 57 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}},<ref>{{Cite|EX Battle-Tested Guardian Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> added in the {{PotA}} [[DLC]] pack.
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Battle-Tested Guardian Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}} receives a reward of two {{Plural|HWAoC|Giant Ancient Core|link}}, fifteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}}'s objective is to defeat 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within eight minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}} at first, and more spawn when {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}} reaches 50 K0s. At 100 KOs, a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} also spawns a short distance from {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}} and starts moving towards it. Another one spawns at 200 KOs and does the same as the first, although it spawns further south. When {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}} reaches 300 KOs, a large group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Lizalfos|link}} spawns, along with another {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} that spawns in the southern area of the map and moves toward {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}}. More {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Lizalfos}} spawn at 350 KOs. At 400 KOs, a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} spawns in the eastern side of the map. Once 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, the time limit is taken away and {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}} must then defeat each {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} that spawned and is still alive. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once every {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} is defeated.
