==Overview==
{{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening Collector's Gift Box}} is a merchandise box containing 6 different items all featuring {{LA|NS}}.<ref name="Description"/> These items include a snapback cap, a water bottle, a stationary pouch, a {{Term|LANS|Shield|link}} plush, puffy stickers, and a notebook.<ref name="Description"/> The notebook includes 72 front and back lined pages and dividers placed in different page intervals each featuring a different [[Characters in Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Character]], [[Enemies in Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Enemy]] or [[Items in Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Item]] from {{LA|NS|-}}. The first and last pages include many {{Plural|LANS|Rupee|link}} drawn in a pattern. The snapback cap includes a {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} appliqué on the front of the cap, as well a {{LA|NS|-}} logo appliqué on the side. The puffy stickers include a total of 12 stickers. The stickers each feature a character or item from {{LA|NS|-}},  including {{Term|LANS|Marin|link}}, three different stickers of {{Term|LANS|Link}}, the {{Term|LANS|Wind Marimba|link}}, the {{Term|LANS|Sea Lily's Bell|link}}, the {{Term|LANS|Coral Triangle|link}}, the {{Term|LANS|Full Moon Cello|link}}, the {{Term|LANS|Organ of Evening Calm|link}}, the {{Term|LANS|Surf Harp|link}}, the {{Term|LANS|Conch Horn|link}}, and the {{Term|LANS|Thunder Drum|link}}.
