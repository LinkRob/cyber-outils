==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
The Digging Mitts are received from a [[Mogma]] in the [[Eldin Volcano]] after [[Link]] defeats a horde of [[Bokoblin]]s that invaded the underground cavern. They can be used to dig through special X marked soft dirt spots by pressing the A Button when standing next to it, revealing the contents found underground. This can be used to dig up treasure, or even reveal dungeon puzzle components such as geysers of air used to sail upward with the sailcloth.

The Digging Mitts can be upgraded to the Mogma Mitts. They are received from [[Silva]] after saving him in the [[Fire Sanctuary (Skyward Sword)|Fire Sanctuary]].

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW}}, the Digging Mitts appear as [[Badge]]s that allow Warriors to damage enemies with their guard up. They also appear in [[Adventure Mode]] as an [[Item Card]] used to dig out holes on the map to reveal the Reward.
{{Noncanon|End}}
