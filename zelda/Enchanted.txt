{{Term/Store|Enchanted||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Enchanted|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Enchanted}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor Shop|link}} located in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. It is run by {{Term|BotW|Claree|link}} who stands behind the counter, offering her curated "Claree Collection";<ref>{{Cite|We exclusively stock the special garments of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Claree Collection}}!|Claree|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|Hylian Set|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Stealth Set|link}}. Upon first approaching any of the {{Term|BotW|Stealth Set}}, she will inform {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that the set is exclusive to {{Term|BotW|Enchanted}}.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that lovely? It's a limited-edition Enchanted exclusive!|Claree|BotW}}</ref> She also notifies {{Term|BotW|Link}} of the clothing's aid for sneaking, but warns it has lower defense as a trade-off.<ref>{{Cite|While you wear it, your footsteps are nearly soundless, so it's perfect for hunting and stuff.|Claree|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, but it offers very little defense. Small price for the extra stealth...|Claree|BotW}}</ref> On the counter, {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill: Volume 1|link}} can be read.

Outside the store, {{Term|BotW|Claree|display= Claree's}} sister {{Term|BotW|Lasli|link}} advertises the store to people travelling through {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|We're open rain or shine! {{Color|BotW Blue|Enchanted}}, right this way!|Lasli|BotW}}</ref>
