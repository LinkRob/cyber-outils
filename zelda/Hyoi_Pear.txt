{{Term/Store|Hyoi Pear|Hyoi Pears|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128 {{Exp Game|TWW, TWWHD}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWDE}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
The Hyoi Pear can be stored in the [[Bait Bag]] after being purchased from [[Beedle]] for the price of only 10 [[Rupee]]s.

When positioned on the head, it can be used to attract [[Seagull]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Use this bait to take control of a seagull.|Inventory|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 14}}</ref> A Seagull that then eats the Hyoi Pear will suddenly find itself being controlled by [[Link]] (until pressing R or START or Link or the Seagull is attacked). The controlled Seagull can then be used to collect items or hit switches in normally inaccessible places. It is necessary to use this item to get [[Triforce Chart]] #2. Link also needs the Hyoi Pear at [[Forest Haven]] to get inside the [[Nintendo Gallery]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 155}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|L|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:HWDE Hyoi Pear Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, Hyoi Pears appear as Plant-type [[Food#Hyrule Warriors|Food]]. They are imbued with the Lightning {{Term|HWDE|Element|link}}. When consumed by a [[Companion Fairy]] in the [[Dining Room]] of the [[My Fairy]] mode, Hyoi Pears can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.

Hyoi Pears also appear as [[Item Card]]s in the [[Great Sea Map|Great Sea]] and [[Master Wind Waker Map]]s in [[Adventure Mode]]. Using it on a [[Seagull]] will cause the Seagull to find hidden treasure, unlocking a secret Reward.
{{Noncanon|End}}
