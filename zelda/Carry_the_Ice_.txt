{{Term/Store|Carry the Ice!||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 334}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
During "{{Term|BotW|The Perfect Drink|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with retrieving {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Furosa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Should I get it for you?] Are you serious? You'll get it? That'd be a huge help. Pokki is my best customer, after all. I wish I could get the ice myself.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She explains that {{Term|BotW|Anche|link}}, the manager of the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|display=Icehouse}}, will allow him to take some once he explains the situation to her.<ref>{{Cite|Anche manages it, but I think if you explain the situation, she'll let you have some ice.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> However, since she goes to sleep early, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} during the day.<ref>{{Cite|You should know that she heads to bed quite early. If you plan to go, you'll need to get there while it's bright out.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} believes it would be too much to make him carry it all the way back to {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, young lady... Carrying ice all the way through the desert and then all the way to town? I can't let that stand?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, she promises to meet him at the entrance to the ruins between {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}}, at which point she will take the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} from him.<ref>{{Cite|If you bring the ice to the {{Color|BotW Red|entrance to the ruins}}—between town and the icehouse—I can take it from there.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}}, {{Term|BotW|Anche}} will allow him to take a block of {{Term|BotW|Ice}} once he explains that it is for {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Actually...] I see... Well, if Furosa wants ice, then you should bring her some. You're permitted to take some ice.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> She warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that it will melt if he moves too slowly.<ref>{{Cite|You've taken the heat into account, right? If you move too slowly, the ice will surely melt. Furosa has no use for water...|Anche|BotW}}</ref> After this, he will be given the {{Term|BotW|Ice}}, which he must bring through the ruins. Along the way, he will have to avoid {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Black Bokoblin|link}}, who will attack {{Term|BotW|Link}} if they spot him.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} must keep the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} in the shade in order to prevent it from melting. If the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} melts during the delivery, {{Term|BotW|Anche}} will initially be disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|...You've gotta be kidding me.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> However, she assures {{Term|BotW|Link}} that even the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo|link}} make mistakes and encourages him to try again.<ref>{{Cite|Even the Gerudo make mistakes transporting ice, sometimes. Just grab another block and try again.|Anche|BotW}}</ref>

At the end of the ruins, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} waiting for him.<ref>{{Cite|Over here...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not carrying the block of {{Term|BotW|Ice}} or it is not in range when he speaks to {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}, she will ask him to retrieve it.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry to ask so many time... Could you bring the ice here?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>}}{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} will notice he is a man, but she does not care.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? You're...<br/>Well, whatever... I don't care who you are, as long as I get my ice.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>}} She is grateful that the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} is in good condition,<ref>{{Cite|Whoa. No doubt about it, that's definitely ice {{Color|BotW Red|from the icehouse}}.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Looks like the ice is in good shape. Sarqso.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> and she believes she will be able to make the best {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit|link}} ever.<ref>{{Cite|I'll be able to make a {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}} with this. It may be the best I've ever made...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With this, I'll be able to make the ultimate {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to inform {{Term|BotW|Pokki|link}}, who has collapsed at {{Term|BotW|Misae Suma Shrine|link}}, about the drink she has prepared for her.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, but could you please let {{Color|BotW Blue|Pokki}} know? Tell her that I made the best one ever and I've got it waiting for her...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I've got one more favor to ask. Could you go and let {{Color|BotW Blue|Pokki}} know? Just tell her the best drink ever is waiting for her...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She says that when {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} hears about it, she will come running.<ref>{{Cite|Knowing her, she'll probably come running when she hears that there's an amazing drink with her name on it.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What? No, of course this will work. Knowing her, if she hears a delicious drink is waiting, she'll come running.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

After the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Anche}} will continue to ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to deliver {{Term|BotW|Ice}} to {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} on her behalf.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Talk about great timing! Can you go deliver ice to Furosa again?|Anche|BotW}}</ref> While she would normally do it herself, the monsters congregating in the ruins have made her hesitant to leave her post.<ref>{{Cite|Usually I'm the one who's supposed to deliver it, but... Lately there've been more and more monsters skulking around here, so I can't be away from the icehouse.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> Now, she charges {{Term|BotW|Link}} 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to make a delivery,<ref name="Anche"/> but she promises that {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} will reimburse him.<ref>{{Cite|Once you've delivered it, Furosa should be able to reimburse you for the cost of the ice.|Anche|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}, she will reward him based on how quick his delivery was. If it took {{Term|BotW|Link}} more than two minutes to pass through the ruins, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} will call him a professional.<ref>{{Cite|You did it in {{Color|BotW Red|XX:XX.XX}}?<br/>You're like a professional ice deliverer.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> As payment for his services, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Including the price of the ice... I'm assuming this reward feels ample for services rendered, right?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She also remarks that {{Term|BotW|Anche}} seems happy managing the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}} and that her job suits her.<ref>{{Cite|Sounds like Anche is happy managing the icehouse. Seems to suit her.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes it to {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} in less than two minutes, she will be shocked by his speed.<ref>{{Cite|You've got to be kidding me. Ice delivered in {{Color|BotW Red|XX:XX.XX}}?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he exceeded her expectations and calls him a savant.<ref>{{Cite|That is WAY beyond my expectations! You're like a savant at ice running.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> In this scenario, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Let me reimburse you for the price of the ice...and I'd better dig deep for something extra to reward a run like that!|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Due to how happy the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} seems, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} believes that {{Term|BotW|Anche}} must be happy as well.<ref>{{Cite|Anche must be happy...because the ice sure seems happy!|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

Once she has given {{Term|BotW|Link}} his reward, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} asks him if he would be willing to retrieve more {{Plural|BotW|Ice}} for her, as she needs a lot in order to make cocktails.<ref>{{Cite|If you could, I would love it if you'd make another run... Think you could find it in your heart to go again? You need lots of ice to serve good cocktails, you know?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will have to pay {{Term|BotW|Anche}} 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} for another delivery.<ref>{{Cite|When you get the ice from Anche, make sure you pay {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}} for it.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} informs him that she will be awaiting his arrival.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you... I'll be waiting here, so please bring me that ice!|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} tells him to speak to {{Term|BotW|Anche}} when he would like to help again.<ref>{{Cite|I see... Ah, well. If you feel like helping out, then talk to Anche...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>
