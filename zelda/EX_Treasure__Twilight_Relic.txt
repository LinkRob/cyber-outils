"{{Term/Store|EX Treasure: Twilight Relic||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Treasure: Twilight Relic}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Outpost Ruins}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TMT}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is obtained upon leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with finding {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal|link}}, which is rumored to be in the {{Term|BotW|Outpost Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a journal that places the location of this treasure somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Outpost Ruins}}...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to activate the second stage of the Quest by reading Misko's EX Journal without triggering any of the loading conditions for the Quest's activation.}}

Inside {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal}} are clues to the locations of eight {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, each containing a priceless relic stolen from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Misko|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hid the eight priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> The Journal states the helmet of the [[Midna|Princess of Twilight]] can be found in the ruins of a temple flooded by the {{Term|BotW|Regencia River|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The princess of twilight, whose stories are handed down alongside those of the Hero of Twilight...<br/>Her helmet can be found {{Color|BotW Red|at the temple ruins soaked in the waters of Regencia River}}.|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found buried next to some rubble south of the ruins of the northwestern building in the {{Term|BotW|Sage Temple Ruins|link}}. Using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pull the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} out of the ground and open it. Once he has obtained {{Term|BotW|Midna's Helmet|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.<ref>{{Cite|You found the treasure that was hidden at Sage Temple Ruins and obtained {{Color|BotW Red|Midna's Helmet}}!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
