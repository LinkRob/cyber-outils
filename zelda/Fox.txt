==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Main|Grassland Fox, Snowcoat Fox}}
There are two varieties of {{Plural|BotW|Fox}} in {{BotW}}; {{Plural|BotW|Grassland Fox|link}} that can be found in plains, such as the one north of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Snowcoat Fox|link}} that are found in snowy tundras, such as the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tundra|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Fox}} can be hunted using {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, and drop {{Plural|BotW|Raw Meat|link}} when killed.
