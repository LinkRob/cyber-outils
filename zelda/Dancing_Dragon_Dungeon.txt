The {{Term/Store|Dancing Dragon Dungeon||Series, OoS}} is the fourth {{Term|OoS|Dungeon|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 151}}</ref> It is located near the summit of [[Mt. Cucco]]. Within this dungeon lies the fourth [[Essences of Nature|Essence of Nature]], the [[Soothing Rain]]. To access the dungeon, Link requires the [[Power of Spring]].

==Entrance to the Dungeon==
The entrance to the Dancing Dragon Dungeon is carved into the rock wall of [[Mt. Cucco]]. In order to navigate the mountain range where the dungeon is located, Link must obtain the power of the [[Spirit of Spring]] for the [[Rod of Seasons]]. Link will also need the [[Flippers]] obtained from the [[Master Diver]] in the nearby [[Sunken City]]. Link requires the aid of [[Dimitri]] in order to swim up the waterfall into the secret cave where the Master Diver resides. 

In order to access the dungeon, Link must find the [[Dragon Key]] and use it to stop the flow of the waterfall cascading over the entrance of the dungeon, which is preventing Link from entering. The key is found on [[Goron Mountain]]. However, Link needs the help of [[Moosh]] to cross the pitfalls surrounding the key. To obtain Moosh's aid, Link must use the newly obtained powers of his Rod of Seasons to obtain the [[Bananas|Spring Banana]] and give it to Moosh.

Once the flow of the waterfall is shut off, the entrance of the dungeon can be accessed by climbing the vines that grow only in summer.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|OoS|Dancing Dragon Dungeon}} is a watery {{Term|OoS|Dungeon|link}} littered with {{Plural|OoS|Mine Cart}}. The presence of {{Plural|OoS|Mine Cart}}, {{Plural|OoS|Rail Switch|link}}, the {{Term|OoS|Rail Switch Room|link}}, the {{Term|OoS|Rail Cart Junction|link}}, and the {{Term|OoS|Rail Cart Room|link}} suggests that the {{Term|OoS|Dungeon}} was once a mine used by the inhabitants of {{Term|OoS|Holodrum|link}}. Long abandoned, the mine has become a breeding ground for monsters that Link must slay while progressing through the dungeon. The [[Flippers]] are essential to completing the dungeon: on two occasions, Link must dive underwater to obtain a [[key]] (once for a [[Small Key]] and once for the [[Boss Key]] later on). This dungeon features two floors, a basement floor, and one side-scrolling room.

The mini-boss of the dungeon is [[Agunima]], a wizard resembling [[Agahnim]]. Shortly after defeating him, Link will obtain the [[Mystical Seed|Seed]]-shooting [[Slingshot]], his first projectile weapon of the game.

Within the dungeon resides a strange [[Old Man]] who will help Link locate the Boss Key. The key is located in a submerged room where a raised platform stands. When observed from above, the platform resembles a face, with two watery holes for eyes. Using the Flippers, Link can dive into the easternmost "eye" to retrieve the Boss Key located there.<ref>{{Cite|What you seek is in the eye.|Old Man|OoS}}</ref>

In the basement floor of the dungeon resides [[Gohma]], the boss of Dancing Dragon Dungeon. With the help of the item he acquired within the dungeon, the [[Slingshot]], Link defeats Gohma and obtains the fourth of the [[Essences of Nature]], as well as the obligatory [[Heart Container]].

==Trivia==
*Through {{Wp|sequence breaking}}, it is possible to obtain the [[Power of Autumn]] before the [[Power of Spring]]. Therefore, it is possible to complete the [[Unicorn's Cave]] before this dungeon since the Dancing Dragon Dungeon's main item, the [[Slingshot]], is not needed to complete the dungeon.
