The {{Term/Store|Cozy Parka||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120 {{Exp Game|TFH}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Cozy Parka comes with antislip boots. There's nothing stylish about a broken leg, oui hee hee.}}

The {{Term|TFH|Cozy Parka}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} includes anti-slip boots that improve {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s traction, allowing him to walk on {{Term|TFH|Ice|link}} without sliding.<ref>{{Cite|Prevents skidding on ice. A necessity for trips to colder areas!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Cozy Parka comes with antislip boots. There's nothing stylish about a broken leg, oui hee hee.|Madame Couture|TFH|}}</ref> In addition, the {{Term|TFH|Cozy Parka}} allows the young hero to safely attack {{Term|TFH|Ice}}-based [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]] without taking damage himself. Colliding with Enemies or gusts of {{Term|TFH|Ice|display=icy air}} will still harm {{Term|TFH|Link}} when he wears the {{Term|TFH|Cozy Parka}}, but will not render him frozen in place.

The appearance of the {{Term|TFH|Cozy Parka}} is based on [[MarioWiki:Ice Climbers|Popo and Nana]], the protagonists of ''Ice Climber''.
