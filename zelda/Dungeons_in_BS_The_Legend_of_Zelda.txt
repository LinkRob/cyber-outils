This is a list of all "labyrinths" appearing in {{BSTLoZ}}. It is worth noting that though many of these dungeons are located in specific biomes, the dungeons themselves do not seem to conform to any particular theme or element, just like {{TLoZ}}: the architecture of every dungeon is a simple brick color swap, and aside from the varying enemies from dungeon to dungeon, the overall vibe given off by each dungeon is similar.
