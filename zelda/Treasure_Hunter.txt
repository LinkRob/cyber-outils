The {{Term/Store|Treasure Hunter||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Biography==
The {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter}} of {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} appears inside the various {{Plural|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|link}} hidden throughout the world, where large sums of {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}} can be obtained. Upon speaking to him, he will ponder aloud how he might get to the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|display=Cave}}'s treasure, essentially providing {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} with hints.{{Fact}}

A {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter}} clothed in black and red appears in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}, in a {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave}} near {{Term|ALBW|Misery Mire|link}}. He claims to be a professional who cannot go anywhere without being recognized.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, yes. I'm a professional treasure hunter. I can't go anywhere without being recognized.|Treasure Hunter|ALBW}}</ref> He hints to {{Term|ALBW|Link}} that to complete the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|display=Cave}}'s puzzle, he will need the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod|link}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Having a tough time here, though. If only I could manipulate sand and wind, then I... Well, let's just say I'd be able to secure myself a nice little piece of treasure here... Don't you worry about it, though, kid. Just leave it to the professionals. I'll figure this one out soon enough!|Treasure Hunter|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If only I could control sand and wind... See, my problem is that I can't control either of those things. But if I could...|Treasure Hunter|ALBW}}</ref>

As {{Term|ALBW|Link}} completes the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|display=Treasure Hunter Caves}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter}} begins to suspect that a competitor may be afoot, while failing to realize that it is {{Term|ALBW|Link}} whose abilities surpass his own.{{Fact}} Though the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter}} never manages to claim any treasures, he is able to partially complete the puzzle in a particular {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Hunter Cave|display=Cave}} east of {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Graveyard|link}}.{{Fact}}
