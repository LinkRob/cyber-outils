'''Dondons''' are creatures rumored to be found near [[Lakeside Stable]] in {{TotK}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium|name=Dondon|desc= A new species of four-legged beast found in southern regions. So far only five of these creatures have ever been spotted in Hyrule. Their diet is a mystery. But what is known is that they are easily scared, and their giant horns can be very dangerous.|locs=  Unknown |loot= NA}}
Dondons are ancient creatures and the ancestors of [[Horse|horses]].<ref>{{Cite|These ancient creatures, the ancestors of horses...can make precious gems...out of stones...|Malanya|TotK}}</ref> These friendly beasts have the ability to consume Luminous Stones. After some time, they produce a type of material, ranging from [[Flint]] or [[Amber]], to more rare materials, like [[Ruby|Rubies]] or [[Diamond]]s.

==Trivia==
*A similar creature was found in the 2019 teaser trailer for {{TotK|-}} (at that time simply called the sequel to {{BotW|-}}), where it acted as a steed for Zelda and Link as they journeyed the caves before finding Ganondorf's mummified corpse.
