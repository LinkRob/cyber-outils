'''''The Legend of Zelda'' Trading Cards''' are a set of collectible cards based on five games of {{TLoZ|Series}} released in 2016 by Enterplay.

==Description==
The set consists of 117 cards (85 base, 23 silver foil, and 9 gold foil), 12 decal stickers, and 9 FunTats tattoos featuring characters, bosses, and items from {{OoT}}, {{MM}}, {{TP}}, {{SS}}, and {{ALBW}}. Each pack contains 6 trading cards and 1 sticker or tattoo and features artwork of [[Link]] from {{SS|-}}, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] from {{TP|-}}, or [[Ganondorf]] from {{OoT|-}}. Each pack display box contains 24 packs.

Collector's Fun Boxes were also released on November 11, 2016. Each box contains 4 packs, 1 poster, 1 of 3 pins, and a [[Midna]] gold foil card.
