==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Coliseum: Beginner}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given six minutes to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Stone Talus}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Stone Talus}} spawns first, and after the {{Term|HWAoC|Stone Talus}} is killed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}.
