'''Hylian Tomato''' is an [[Item]] in {{TotK}}.

== Location and uses ==
{{Stub|TotK}}
Hylian Tomatoes are found in a vast number of locations across [[Hyrule]], most notably in grassy areas. They can be eaten raw, cooked individually, or used as an ingredient when cooking [[Food#Tears of the Kingdom|food]].
