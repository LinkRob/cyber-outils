{{Term/Store|General Store|General Stores|Series, BotW|plural}} are locations in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|General Store}}|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Plural|BotW|General Store}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shop|link}} that sell various things ranging from {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|General Store}} can be found at every major settlement in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, though traveling {{Plural|BotW|Merchant|link}} who act as de facto {{Plural|BotW|General Store}} can also be found traveling the roads between towns and {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, all {{Plural|BotW|General Store}} take the form of {{Plural|BotW|Stall|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Spore Store|link}}, located in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, is a {{Term|BotW|Shop}} that functions like a {{Term|BotW|General Store}}, but it is not designated as one on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}.
