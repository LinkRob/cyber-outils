{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword++}} are the final class of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword|link}}, surpassing {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword+|link}} in both strength and durability. They are larger than both previous iterations and are said to easily slice through {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}} use them in "A Major Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, and they can be obtained after their defeat. They also appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn after scanning the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

Since they are made from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword++}} will deal additional damage if they are used to attack {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}. They will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, they do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they will float if they are dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Finally, they can not be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword++}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>
