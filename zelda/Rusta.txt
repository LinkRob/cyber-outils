{{Term/Store|Rusta||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}} that resides in [[Skyloft]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 294}}</ref> He is married to [[Henya]], the school cook at the [[Knight Academy]].

==Biography==

|quote1= <p>His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Rusta}}, and he is an elderly dreamer and husband to {{Color|SS Blue|Henya}}, who works at the Knight Academy.</p><p>He is quite opinionated, and so not many people take this elderly man's opinions seriously.</p>

Rusta can be found at the bird statue near the [[Waterfall Cave]] in [[Skyloft]] before the [[Wing Ceremony]], where he notes to [[Link]] that it is missing an eye.<ref>{{Cite|Look at this thing. Who knows how long this statue has been here? And it only has one eye. Poor thing... You probably know this, but you can press (C) to look around!|Rusta|SS}}</ref> Afterwards, Rusta can be found at the [[Lumpy Pumpkin]]. After [[Kukiel]] goes missing, he informs Link about a [[Batreaux|monster]] in [[Skyloft]],<ref>{{Cite|There's a fiendish demon living in Skyloft! I'm tellin' ya, I came this close to getting eaten by that evil beast! You look like you've gotten a little knight training, but you'd better keep your guard up, or he'll take a bite out of you too!|Rusta|SS}}</ref> something many people don't believe.<ref>{{Cite|You believe me, don't ya?!|Rusta|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Listen to this! The old guy over there says he saw a demon! He's obviously trying to get Kina's attention, but he won't get anywhere using creepy lines like that!|Keet|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey now... What's with you kids today and all your disrespectin' and whatnot! I swear...|Rusta|SS}}</ref> If Link breaks the Lumpy Pumpkin's chandelier, he will scold him for doing so, saying that he scared a year off his life.<ref>{{Cite|What were you thinking? You scared a year off my life! What did you want again? I lost my train of thought...|Rusta|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Rusta's name may be derived from [[Wikipedia:Rooster|rooster]]. Fittingly, his wife's name, [[Henya]], comes from [[Wikipedia:Chicken|hen]].
