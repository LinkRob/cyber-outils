The {{Term/Store|Engineer's Clothes||Series, ST}} are an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 122 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[File:ST LinkEngineer.png|left|80px]]
During the beginning of the game, [[Link]] can be seen wearing the Engineer's Clothes. After he receives the [[Recruit Uniform]] from [[Princess Zelda]], the young hero will don the uniform instead. After adding 15 Stamps to the [[Stamp Book]], the Engineer's Clothes can be worn again after talking to [[Niko]]. Link can then switch between clothes at any time afterwards.

The Engineer's Clothes are used by [[Royal Engineer]]s. They come with a pair of gloves and a red hat which has a dove design on it.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSB4|3DS|-}}===
In {{SSB4|3DS}}, Link wears the Engineer's Clothes while riding the [[Spirit Train]] in the stage of the same name. He also appears with these clothes as a [[Trophies in Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS/Wii U|Trophy]].
