{{Term/Store|Dark Rat|Dark Rats|Series, ALBW|plural}} are enemies in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref> They are the Lorulean Counterparts to {{Plural|ALBW|Rat|link}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ALBW|Dark Rat}} appear mainly in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon|link}} such as the {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Hideout|link}}, but can also be found in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} in the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace|link}}, as well as {{Plural|ALBW|Cave|link}}. They act just like normal {{Plural|ALBW|Rat}}, however, they feature yellow skin and purple eyes. {{Plural|ALBW|Dark Rat}} have twice as much health and can be defeated with any weapon. If {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} is hit by a {{Term|ALBW|Dark Rat}}, he will take double the damage taken from a normal {{Term|ALBW|Rat}}.
