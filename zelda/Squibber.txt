'''Squibbers''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Squibbers are a larger variant of [[Deeler]]s encountered only in the [[Forest of Canvula]]. They can inflict a [[Heart]] and a half of damage on [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], but if she has at least two [[Celestial Sign]]s, this is reduced to just a Heart of damage.
