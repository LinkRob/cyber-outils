The {{Term/Store|To Quomo Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|To Quomo Shrine}}<br/>To Quomo's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''To Quomo's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|To Quomo Shrine}} is located in the north of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, underneath the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Great Skeleton|link}}. To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must break down {{Term|BotW|Giant Stone Door|link}} southeast of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra North Summit|link}} leading to a cave with the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Great Skeleton}} inside it. Large {{Plural|BotW|Snowball|link}} east of the {{Term|BotW|Giant Stone Door|display=Door}} can be rolled downhill into the doors to knock them down. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|To Quomo|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am To Quomo. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|To Quomo|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|To Quomo Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to {{Term|BotW|To Quomo}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Royal Claymore|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|To Quomo}}, he will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|To Quomo|BotW}}</ref> He will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|To Quomo|BotW}}</ref>
