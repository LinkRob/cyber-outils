{{Term/Store|Hudson||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}<ref name=":0" /> and {{TotK}}<ref>{{Cite|Hudson|N/A|TotK}}</ref>.

==Biography==

==={{BotW|-}}===
Hudson is a carpenter who works for [[Bolson Construction]],<ref name=":0">{{Cite|[Who are you?] I'm a carpenter for {{Color|BotW Blue|Bolson Construction}}. Name's Hudson.|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> a company situated in [[Hateno Village]] in [[East Necluda]]. He can initially be found attempting to demolish [[Link's House]] under [[Bolson]]'s orders.<ref name="Yeah">{{Cite|Hey. [What are you doing?] I'm knocking this place down.|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> When [[Link]] asks why he is doing so, Hudson explains that the building has been abandoned since its owner left town, though he does not know the reason they had for leaving.<ref>{{Cite|[Why?] 'Cause no one is living here. [Why?] The owner of this house took off. [Why?] Don't know. Maybe skipped town without paying the bills?|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> Once Hudson confirms that he has answered all of Link's questions, he reveals that [[Hateno Village]] is finally recovering in the wake of the [[Great Calamity]].<ref>{{Cite|[Oh.] Answered all your questions? [Yes.] Good. A lot has happened since the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Calamity}}. Tough to keep track of it all. [Right.] For the first time since then, we're finally getting back on our feet.|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> He wishes both himself and Link luck in the future.<ref>{{Cite|[Good.] So best of luck to both of us. [Yeah.] Yeah.|Hudson|BotW}}</ref>

If Link offers to buy the House, Hudson will direct him to Bolson instead.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll buy this place!] Can't help you. Ask the {{Color|BotW Blue|boss}}.|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> Once Link and Bolson strike a deal on the House, Hudson will take a break underneath a [[Tree]] nearby.<ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] Taking a break. By the way...|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> There, he mentions that Link would be a great hire for Bolson Construction, but retracts the offer upon learning that his name does not end in "-son."<ref>{{Cite|[What?] Are you gonna buy that house? [Yeah.] You're pretty studly, aren't you? [Uh...come again?] You'd be a great hire for our company... What's your name? [I'm Link.] Oh... Well, too bad. Can't hire you. [Why?] 'Cause your name doesn't end in "{{Color|BotW Red|son}}."|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> According to Hudson, Bolson maintains this hiring requirement for team unity.<ref>{{Cite|[Son?] The boss, Bolson, has a strict hiring rule: {{Color|BotW Red|need a name ending in "son"}}. No exceptions. That's too bad. "Builds team unity." he says. Anyway... You got some stuff to do, right? Should get going, then.|Hudson|BotW}}</ref>

After Link pays for his [[Link's House|House]], Bolson mentions that he has plans to send Hudson to [[Akkala]].<ref>{{Cite|So...next-job time, baby! What'd I have lined up again? Oh, yeah, {{Color|BotW Red|that's}} what's next. I was going to send Hudson up {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} way to clear some land!|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> He advises Link to speak to Hudson before he leaves.<ref>{{Cite|Yup... Business expansion, y'know. ==={{TotK|-}}===
Following the demise of [[Calamity Ganon]], Hudson continues to live in Tarrey Town, and now owns his own construction company, [[Hudson Construction]], which is based there. He has also been recruited by Princess Zelda in her efforts to restore Hyrule.

Following the events of {{BotW|-}} but prior to {{TotK|-}}, Hudson and [[Rhondson]] had a Gerudo daughter named [[Mattison]]. Due to Rhondson's status as a [[Gerudo]] it would be next to impossible for her to bear a son, as the only known Gerudo male is [[Ganon|Ganondorf]]. She appears to be 5 or 6 years old by the time of the [[Upheaval]], based on the character model, which coincides with the older appearances of [[Koko]], [[Riju]], [[Tulin]] and [[Kass|Kass's]] daughters, but contradicts the appearance of [[Shamae]], whose character model has not been updated.

A Gerudo law prohibits Gerudo to associate with men until they are of age, but is only enforced once a child gains awareness of the world around them, prompting someone from Gerudo Town to come retrieve them<ref>"''The Gerudo have a law that says they can't associate with men until they come of age, you see. I mean, it's fine when they're young, but when they start to become more... aware of the world around them... someone comes from Gerudo Town to pick them up.''" — [[Hudson]] ({{TotK}})</ref>. Since Mattison has acquired that age an escort from Gerudo Town is sent to retrieve her. During the [[Side Quest]] "[[Mattison's Independence]]" Link helps her get ready to depart Tarrey Town, by providing Hudson with 10 [[Sundelion|sundelions]], for him to dye Madison's balloon yellow.

At various locations throughout Hyrule, various Materials have been laid out. Before each such locations there is a sign that reads: ''"Hyrule Restoration Materials. Use this materials cache for all your building needs. Come find us if you'd like a dream home! -Hudson Construction"''
