{{Term/Store|Gourmet Meat Curry|Gourmet Meat Curries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Gourmet Meat Curries are dishes made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Raw Gourmet Meat]], [[Goron Spice]], and [[Hylian Rice]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. They heal several of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} when eaten. The meat used in these curries is of high quality, which satisfies lovers of both meat and curry.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Additional ingredients can be added to the recipe of Gourmet Meat Curry, which will result in the follow variations:

* '''Hearty Gourmet Meat Curry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating them will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Gourmet Meat Curry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. If eaten, they'll restore a portion of Link's expended {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Gourmet Meat Curry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. These curries completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Gourmet Meat Curry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. When they're eaten, Link's movement speed will be temporarily boosted.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Gourmet Meat Curry''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. Eating these will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Gourmet Meat Curry''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. When eaten, they'll give Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Gourmet Meat Curry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Link's attack power will by increased by eating this curry.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Gourmet Meat Curry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. If eaten, Link's defense will increase.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Gourmet Meat Curry''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Eating these will increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period of time.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Gourmet Meat Curry''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Gourmet Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The high-quality meat used in this prized dish satisfies meat and curry lovers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding cold ingredients. These meals grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] when consumed.<ref name="Chilly"/>
