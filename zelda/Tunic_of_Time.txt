The {{Term/Store|Tunic of Time||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Tunic of Time}}<br/>Legend has it this armor was once worn by a hero who traveled through time. Strangely, its special design is fitted for children and adults alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The '''Tunic of Time''' is a piece of [[Armor]], which can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning ''The Legend of Zelda'' 30th Anniversary '''or''' ''Ocarina of Time'' Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Tunic of Time. It can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Tunic of Time, alongside the [[Cap of Time]] and [[Trousers of Time]], comprise the [[Hero of Time Set]] which resembles Link's default outfit from ''[[Ocarina of Time]]'', which was called the [[Kokiri Tunic]]. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
