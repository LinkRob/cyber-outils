{{Term/Store|Dillie||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dilli|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Dillie}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} traveler who can be found in the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} with his friend, {{Term|BotW|Jora|link}}. The pair can be found being attack by two {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}. He is grateful if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} saves him, and he gives him a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|I owe you my life! I can't thank you enough! This'll have to do...|Dillie|BotW}}</ref>

After being saved, {{Term|BotW|Dillie}} explains that he and {{Term|BotW|Jora}} are going to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We're headed for Gerudo Town, a shimmering oasis in the desert|Dillie|BotW}}</ref> They have heard it is a city of "foxy ladies" and think that the women will treat them like kings.<ref>{{Cite|Someone told us it's a whole city of foxy ladies. I bet they'll treat us two fine studs like kings!|Dillie|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is jealous of their plan and warns him not to copy their idea.<ref>{{Cite|Say... You're not jealous of our plan, are you? Well, too bad! No copying our idea! We had it first!|Dillie|BotW}}</ref> He then questions what woman would be interested in a "skinny twig" like {{Term|BotW|Link}} anyway.<ref>{{Cite|Besides, what woman would be interested in a skinny twig like you?|Dillie|BotW}}</ref> He also wonders what the women in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} do all day and fantasizes about being the only man in the Town.<ref>{{Cite|Only women are allowed in Gerudo Town. What do you think they do all day in there?|Dillie|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Man"/>

Once {{Term|BotW|Dillie}} and {{Term|BotW|Jora}} are saved, they will stand beside each other while scouting out the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|display=Desert}}. {{Term|BotW|Dillie}} wonders to himself whether they are lost.<ref>{{Cite|Are we lost?|Dillie|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Dillie}}, he asks {{Term|BotW|Jora}} if he is alright and reminds him that they are headed to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} in an attempt to cheer him up.<ref>{{Cite|You OK, buddy? C'mon, there's no use crying over spilled milk. We're headed to Gerudo Town!|Dillie|BotW}}</ref> As consolation, he offers to let {{Term|BotW|Jora}} lead the way,<ref>{{Cite|I'll even let you lead the way!|Dillie|BotW}}</ref> shocking {{Term|BotW|Jora}} and prompting him to ask when they decided that fact.<ref name="Leading">{{Cite|I'm leading the way?! What?! When did we decide this?|Jora|BotW}}</ref> Exasperated, {{Term|BotW|Dillie}} hunches over in disappointment and remarks that he has to be kidding.<ref>{{Cite|You've gotta be kidding me...|Dillie|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Jora}}, he explains that {{Term|BotW|Dillie}} knows the way out of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} and to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|We're on our way to Gerudo Town. Course, we need to make it out of this huge desert, first. But Dillie knows the way, so we should be find. Once we get there, we're hitting the big time!|Jora|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Dillie}} reveals that he thought {{Term|BotW|Jora}} knew the way and was following him,<ref>{{Cite|Uh, I thought you knew the way. I was following you, right? Uh-oh...|Dillie|BotW}}</ref> much to {{Term|BotW|Jora}}'s surpise.<ref name="Leading"/>
