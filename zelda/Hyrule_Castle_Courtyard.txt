The {{Term/Store|Hyrule Castle Courtyard||Series, BotW}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 236}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Castle Courtyard||OoT, OoT3D}},{{Exp Game|OoT}}<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Big|Castle Courtyard}}'''|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref> is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:Zelda's Courtyard.png|thumb|306x306px|The Courtyard of Princess Zelda in {{OoT|-}}]]
The Castle Courtyard in {{OoT}} is a small, open enclosure inside [[Hyrule Castle]]. In order to speak to [[Princess Zelda]], [[Link]] must infiltrate the castle, make his way through the [[Courtyard Gardens]], and visit her within the Courtyard. There, upon meeting her in person for the first time, Zelda explains that she had a prophetic dream warning of a dark force attempting to corrupt [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|I had a dream... In the dream, dark storm clouds were billowing over the land of Hyrule... But suddenly, a ray of light shot out of the forest, parted the clouds, and lit up the ground... The light turned into a figure holding a {{Color|OoT3D Green|green and shining stone}}, followed by a {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|fairy}}...|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}</ref> She then shows Link a window where he can spot [[Ganondorf]], who intends to steal the [[Triforce]] from its resting place in the [[Sacred Realm]], the [[Temple of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|I was spying through this window just now... The other element from my dream... the dark clouds... I believe they symbolise...that man in there! Will you look through the window at him?|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Can you see the man with the evil eyes? That is {{Color|OoT3D Red|Ganondorf}}, the leader of the Gerudo. They hail from the desert far to the west. Though he swears allegiance to my father, I am sure he is not sincere. The dark clouds that covered Hyrule in my dream... They must symbolise that man!|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What Ganondorf is after must be nothing less than the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Triforce}} of the Sacred Realm. He must have come to Hyrule to obtain it! And, he wants to conquer Hyrule... No, the entire world!|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}</ref> In order to counter Ganondorf, Princess Zelda devised a plan to keep the tools necessary to enter the Sacred Realm, the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]] and the [[Spiritual Stone]]s, out of his reach. To this end, she tasks Link with the responsibility of retrieving the remaining two Spiritual Stones.<ref>{{Cite|We must not let Ganondorf get the Triforce! I will protect the {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Ocarina of Time}} with all my power! He shall not have it! You go find the other {{Color|OoT3D Red|two Spiritual Stones}}! Let's get the Triforce before Ganondorf does...and then defeat him!|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}</ref> Princess Zelda then gives him a [[Zelda's Letter|Letter]] granting him permission to ascend [[Death Mountain Trail]].<ref>{{Cite|One more thing... Take this {{Color|OoT3D Red|letter}}... I'm sure it will be helpful to you.|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}</ref> Afterwards, she orders [[Impa]] to teach the young [[Hero of Time]] her [[Zelda's Lullaby|Lullaby]] and help escort him out of the Castle.<ref>{{Cite|I am {{Color|OoT3D Red|Impa}} of the Sheikah. I am responsible for protecting Princess Zelda. Everything is exactly as the Princess foretold.|Impa|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My role in the princess's dream was to teach a melody to the one from the forest. This is an ancient {{Color|OoT3D Dark Blue|melody passed down by the Royal Family}}. I have played this song for Princess Zelda as a lullaby ever since she was a baby... There is mysterious power in these notes. Now listen carefully...|Impa|OoT3D}}</ref>

If Link strikes the western window with a [[Deku Seed]] via the [[Slingshot|Fairy Slingshot]], a [[Soldier]] will appear and berate him before throwing a [[Bomb]] in his direction.<ref>{{Cite|Hey you! Don't cause any trouble!|Soldier|OoT3D}}</ref> The eastern window, however, is famously known to feature a [[Cameos in The Legend of Zelda series|cameo]] from the ''[[mariowiki:Mario_(franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise; it contains a scenery from ''[[mariowiki:New Super Mario Bros|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'', but originally showed photographs of [[Yoshi]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Mario]], [[Bowser]], and [[Luigi]] in the initial [[Nintendo 64]] version.

The Castle Courtyard is only accessible during the day, since Soldiers that cannot be bypassed will be stationed just in front of the secret entrance at night. After Princess Zelda flees from the Castle alongside Impa due to Ganondorf's attack, the aforementioned Soldiers will be stationed at the secret entrance at all times, rendering the entire area inaccessible.

At the ending of the game, Link returns to the courtyard to speak to Zelda once again, this time to warn her about Ganondorf's true plans and what the future would unfold had they not intervened correctly, to prevent the game's adventure from ever occurring again. This would lead to the events of {{MM}} and eventually {{TP}}. Due to the young princess' startled reaction upon seeing Link, it is strongly implied her future counterpart sent the hero before he first met her into the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Child Timeline]].

==={{BotW|-}}===
The Hyrule Castle Courtyard composes the majority of the exterior of Hyrule Castle in {{BotW}}. It follows a sprawling and branching road that connects to key locations within the Castle. The primary road leads through three major compounds: the [[First Gatehouse]], the [[Second Gatehouse]], and the [[Sanctum]]. It begins at the [[Hyrule Castle Gate]], which winds around to the entrance to the [[Guards' Chamber]] before passing through the First Gatehouse. After the First Gatehouse, the road splits twice. The first split of the road leads through a garden beneath the tower containing [[Princess Zelda's Room]], continuing on to the [[Dining Hall]] on the southeast side of the Castle before reconnecting with the primary road a short distance after it initially splits. The main road continues through the Second Gatehouse before wrapping around and leading up into the [[Sanctum]]. The road between the First and Second Gatehouse is obstructed by a [[Pool of Malice]], which can be partially dispelled by destroying a [[Glowing Eyeball]] on the eastern side of the [[Malice]].

Prior to the second [[Great Calamity]], the Hyrule Castle Courtyard was used by [[Sheikah]] scientists to research and control the [[Guardian]]s they excavated from beneath Hyrule Castle, as seen in the [[Recovered Memory]], "[[Father and Daughter]]". After [[Calamity Ganon]] escaped its seal beneath Hyrule Castle, it spread its Malice to the Guardians populating the Courtyard, seizing control of them and using them to assail Hyrule Castle, initiating the second Great Calamity that spread to the rest of [[Hyrule|Hyrule Kingdom]].

== Trivia ==
[[File:OoT Triforce of Courage on Link's hand (ending).png|thumb|255x255px|The Triforce of Courage on Link's hand in the ending of {{OoT|-}}]]

* During the post-ending cutscene of {{OoT}} and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|remake]], the [[Triforce of Courage]] can be seen resonating on [[Link|Link']]<nowiki/>s left hand. The piece of the [[Triforce]] itself was used by the [[Hero of Time]] to show his evidence from his adventure in order to truly stop [[Ganondorf]] and gain the [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Family]]'s trust.
* If cheating effects are used to make Link hover in the air and bypass the walls, the Hyrule Castle Courtyard in {{OoT|-}} where he meets Princess Zelda is actually a dome-like map, and the areas where the player can see through the windows do not have proper rooms, including the [[Royal Guard|Royal Guards]] where Ganondorf is seen, who are in fact 2D placements. This is likely to save memory space for the [[Nintendo 64]].
* In the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|{{OoT|-}} manga adaptation]] by [[Akira Himekawa]], its ending in the Courtyard shows Zelda with an emotionally happy reaction when seeing Link, unlike its source material. This possibly suggests that she, like the main protagonist, retained all her memories from the story's events or the actual timeline did not split into two.
** It should be noted that in the original game, the princess' surprised reaction is likely from seeing the Triforce of Courage on Link's hand, or his eventual confession about Ganondorf's true plan according to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]]''.
