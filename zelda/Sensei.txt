{{Name|Japanese|Sensei}}, real name {{Name|Japanese|Florence}}, is one of {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}}'s love interests in {{RTBToL}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|RTBToL|Sensei}} first appears on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 3|link}}, where she was taking care of {{Term|RTBToL|Peter|link}}’s injuries after {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki|link}}’s spaceship crashed on his truck while he was working. When she first meets {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}}, she gets terrified for his odd appearance, and quickly departs.

Later in the game, when {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} returns to {{Term|RTBToL|Page 3}}, she is leaving a note for {{Term|RTBToL|Peter}} on the door of the house, and, after seeing {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}, she climbs up a water tower in fear, and will not calm down until {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} gives her enough gifts in a {{Term|RTBToL|Love Push|link}}. After her second heart is unlocked, {{Term|RTBToL|Sensei}} moves to {{Term|RTBToL|Page 10|link}} where her last {{Term|RTBToL|Love Push}} takes place.

==Nomenclature==
In Japanese, Sensei can mean a teacher, a doctor, a master, etc.
