The {{Term/Store|Bird-Man Research Study||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 332}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The {{Term|BotW|Bird-Man Research Study}} is run by {{Term|BotW|Branli|link}} on top of {{Term|BotW|Ridgeland Tower|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches him, {{Term|BotW|Branli}} assumes he is now also trapped on top of the {{Term|BotW|Ridgeland Tower|display=Tower}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! Now that you've climbed up here, how will you get down without falling?!|Branli|BotW}}</ref> However, when he hears that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is capable of using his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to glide from tall heights,<ref>{{Cite|WHAT?! You can fly?! You can fly like a {{Color|BotW Red|bird-man}}?! Is that true? I thought it impossible for a {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}} to fly like a {{Color|BotW Red|bird-man}}! Ah... It's more like gliding then? That's still a step in the right direction!|Branli|BotW}}</ref> he realizes that he could be useful in his research.<ref>{{Cite|If I can analyze your glide distance and trajectory, it may help us understand the science of {{Color|BotW Red|bird-man}} flight.|Branli|BotW}}</ref> He proposes a study to see how far {{Term|BotW|Link}} can glide,<ref>{{Cite|Would you mind participating in a short research study? I want to see how far you can glide.|Branli|BotW}}</ref> and he promises to compensate him based on how far he can go.<ref>{{Cite|You'll be compensated for your time and travel...depending on how far you fly. So how about it?|Branli|BotW}}</ref> In the event of an accident, {{Term|BotW|Branli}} has {{Term|BotW|Link}} pay a 20 {{Term|BotW|Rupee|link}} liability fee.<ref name="Branli"/>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to participate, {{Term|BotW|Branli}} suggest he keep his landing destination, the {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}} direction, and his {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} in mind during the study.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Landing elevation}}! {{Color|BotW Red|Wind direction}}! {{Color|BotW Red|Stamina usage}}! Keep all these in mind as you consider the {{Color|BotW Red|farthest destination}} you can reach by gliding!|Branli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s distance is tracked the second he jumps off of {{Term|BotW|Ridgeland Tower}}. To go as far as possible, {{Term|BotW|Link}} should jump off the southeast edge of the {{Term|BotW|Ridgeland Tower|display=Tower}}, heading towards the {{Term|BotW|Breach of Demise|link}}. In addition, he can also use {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale|link}} to gain additional height at the beginning of the study.

Depending on how far {{Term|BotW|Link}} manages to glide, {{Term|BotW|Branli}} will have different reactions. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} travels less than 450 meters, {{Term|BotW|Branli}} says he has added nothing to his research and refuses to give him a reward.<ref>{{Cite|This does NOTHING for my research! I'm sorry, but I don't give rewards for paltry performances. I give rewards for poultry performances!|Branli|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to think of himself as a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}}, free from gravity.<ref>{{Cite|Just...try to imagine that you're a {{Color|BotW Red|bird-man}} freeing yourself from the unjust chains of gravity!|Branli|BotW}}</ref> In addition, he suggests {{Term|BotW|Link}} make adjustments to preserve his {{Term|BotW|Stamina}}.<ref>{{Cite|And if you run out of energy while gliding, try making some adjustments that will give you more stamina.|Branli|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} flies between 450 and 600 meters, {{Term|BotW|Branli}} will call it a small step.<ref>{{Cite|Small steps lead to great strides in the long run.|Branli|BotW}}</ref> Initially, he offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} 21 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} as a reward, but he relents and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, right! Your compensation. Isn't the collection of priceless research data compensation enough? No? Fine. Well, a promise is a promise. Here's your reward: 21 rupees! ... Ugh. Fine. I'll make it 50 rupees.|Branli|BotW}}</ref> Finally, when {{Term|BotW|Link}} surpasses 600 meters, {{Term|BotW|Branli}} will be amazed that his unconventional methods worked.<ref>{{Cite|Your methods may have been unconventional—I didn't see you flap your arms once—but they were effective! The field of {{Color|BotW Red|bird-man}} research will benefit greatly from this data.|Branli|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You deserve heavy compensation for your stellar efforts. Please, take this!|Branli|BotW}}</ref>
