== Location and uses ==
=== {{BotW|-}} ===
Goat Butter can be purchased at [[High Spirits Produce]] and [[The Slippery Falcon]] or from the travelling merchant [[Agus]]. Though they can't be eaten, they can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make certain dishes. Goat Butter is one of the ingredients that can make Yellow [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

For the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "[[What's for Dinner?]]", {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must gather Goat Butter and a [[Hearty Blueshell Snail]] for [[Kiana]] so she can make [[Seafood Paella]].<ref>{{Cite|We seem to be a bit short on ingredients though. We'll need {{Color|BotW Red|goat butter}} and a {{Color|BotW Red|hearty blueshell snail}}.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, I know! Could you go and get me the ingredients we're missing?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> After doing so, she will reward [[Link]] with 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} and Seafood Paella. "[[Cooking with Koko]]" also requires Link to retrieve Goat Butter for [[Koko]], and in exchange she will give him a [[Hot Buttered Apple]].<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|OH, NO!!}} Koko is out of {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Mmm! What a yummy smell! And that hint of butter makes all the difference! Oh! Oh! And the apples are superbly sweet too! Here, take some. It's really something else.|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

When [[Cooking]], Goat Butter is required to make stews and meunière.<ref name="Inventory"/> Specifically, it's needed to make [[Apple Pie]], [[Carrot Cake]], an [[Egg Tart]], [[Fish Pie]], [[Carrot Stew]], [[Crab Risotto]], [[Curry Pilaf]], [[Fruit Pie]], [[Gourmet Meat Stew]], [[Gourmet Poultry Pilaf]], [[Hearty Clam Chowder]], [[Hearty Salmon Muenière]], a [[Hot Buttered Apple]], [[Meat Pie]], [[Meat Stew]], [[Monster Cake]], [[Monster Soup]], a [[Mushroom Omelet]], [[Mushroom Risotto]], [[Nutcake]], a [[Plain Crepe]], [[Porgy Meunière]], [[Poultry Pilaf]], [[Prime Meat Stew]], [[Prime Poultry Pilaf]], [[Pumpkin Pie]], [[Pumpkin Stew]], [[Salmon Risotto]], [[Seafood Meunière]], Seafood Paella, a [[Vegetable Omelet]], and [[Vegetable Risotto]].
