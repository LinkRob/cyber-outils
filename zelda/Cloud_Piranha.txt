{{Term/Store|Cloud Piranha|Cloud Piranhas|Series, TMC|plural}} are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 171}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Cloud Piranha}}
Cloud Piranhas only appear in the [[Cloud Tops]]. Whenever [[Link]] is nearby, they will quickly surface and jump towards him in an attempt to damage him. Cloud Piranhas can be forced out of a cloud with the [[Cane of Pacci]], allowing Link can attack them with his [[Sword]].
