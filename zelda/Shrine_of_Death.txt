{{Term/Store|Shrine of Death|Shrines of Death|Series, CoH|plural}} are objects in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|SHRINE OF DEATH!|N/A|CoH}}</ref> They were introduced as part of the {{OO}} [[Downloadable Content]] update.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Death}} appear in {{Plural|CoH|Dungeon|link}} in both {{Term|CoH|Dungeon Mode|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Story Mode — Octavo|link}}. When interacted with, {{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Death}} causes the character and all enemies, including the [[boss]], to be instantly killed by any damage taken. This includes attacks by the character, by other enemies such as [[Moblin]]s and by [[Trap (Cadence of Hyrule)|traps]]. If a character has a {{Term|CoH|Health Potion|link}} or a {{Term|CoH|Fairy}}, any damage taken will automatically consume the item. The effects of {{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Death}} last until the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} is left, the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} {{Term|CoH|Boss|link}} is defeated, or the affected character falls in battle.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, look! A {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! {{Color|CoH Blue|Shrines}} are useful, but they often have a tricky {{Color|CoH Blue|side effect}} that you may not like. Bump into it if you want to take a chance and activate the {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! Shrine effects stay active until you leave the dungeon, defeat the dungeon boss, or are defeated.|Trill|CoH}}</ref>

[[Gohmaracas]] will instead instantly progress to its second phase upon being hit once, and a second hit will defeat the boss. [[Red Puffstool]]s will be instantly killed when hit and will not explode.

When destroyed, the shrine drops a [[Ring of Risk]].
