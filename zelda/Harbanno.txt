==Biography==
Harbanno is the baker of Sakado. He, along with several other residents,<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=A panic is rolling through Sakado! The Mayor and some of the other villagers are missing. Could Ganon be kidnapping more innocent people? You'd better get to work.|game=TWoG|page=10}}</ref> were captured by the [[Iron Knuckle (Boss)|Iron Knuckle]] and imprisoned at [[Tykogi Tower]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh my! It looks like everyone's been taken to Tykogi Tower.|Impa|TWoG}}</ref> [[Impa]]'s [[Triforce of Wisdom]] shows his capture. He is seen either being pulled away, presumably by an enemy to be taken to the Tower, or running away just before being captured. While he is away, his bakery is locked.

[[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] finds Harbanno inside the Tower. He urges her that she save [[Mayor Cravendish]], who has been imprisoned in the top floor. Harbanno then makes his escape, and tells Zelda to visit him in Sakado later, leaving behind a [[Key]] that opens the next door. When Zelda visits his shop afterwards, he supplies her with free [[Bread#The Wand of Gamelon|Bread]] out of gratitude, which can be thrown to distract attacking [[Arpagos]]. Harbanno comments that he seems to know [[Link]], and jokingly states that he could eat ten of his bread loaves.<ref>{{Cite|Your friend Link could eat ten of these! [...] Hah! The Arpagos will die for them.|Harbanno|TWoG}}</ref>

Harbanno seems to be more concerned about his baked goods than of himself, as shown when he first exclaims "My cakes will burn!" when captured, and again when he worries about his cakes as he is escaping Tykogi Tower.
