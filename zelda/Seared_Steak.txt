{{Plural|BotW|Seared Steak}} are the result of [[Raw Meat]] that has made contact with [[Fire]].<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be found around {{Plural|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} being cooked over a [[Campfire]], or dropped by Black {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}}. It's sold by the [[Protein Palace]], [[Estan]] in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, and [[Maike]] in [[Kara Kara Bazaar]]. [[Link]] can eat one to restore one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}.
