{{Term/Store|Linebeck's Uniform||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 130}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform}} is one of two {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}} added in the Ver. 2.0.0 update, released for free on December 2, 2015 in North America.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Ver. 2.0.0 [Released12/02/15] |retrieved=May 25, 2019 |url=https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/15571/~/how-to-update-the-legend-of-zelda%3A-tri-force-heroes |title=How to Update The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes |site=Nintendo Support }}</ref> It is the most expensive {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} in the game and it becomes available for purchase once the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}} is unlocked by the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} completing nine different [[Levels in Tri Force Heroes|Levels]] or {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}}. It is based on the clothing worn by {{Term|PH|Linebeck|link}} in {{PH}} and turns {{Term|TFH|Link|link}}'s {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} into a {{Term|TFH|Telescope|link}}.

Owing to {{Term|PH|Linebeck}}'s profession as a treasure hunter, {{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform}} allows {{Term|TFH|Link}} to see the contents of {{Plural|TFH|Treasure Chest|link}} before opening them.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TFH Blue|Linebeck's Uniform}} lets you peek into unopened treasure chests to behold the goodies within. Simply fabulous!|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> Even if only one {{Term|TFH|Links}} wears the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}, any of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} will be able to see a Chest's contents, as long as the hero wearing the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} is also on-screen. This ability does not work in the {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}} {{Term|TFH|Mini-Game|link}}, as the {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches Owner|link}} has invested in "peek-proof" {{Plural|TFH|Treasure Chest}} to prevent cheating.<ref>{{Cite|They say there's an outfit out there that lets you see inside treasure chests before opening them... What a monstrosity! It's a good thing I invested in these peek-proof chests. Worth every Rupee.|Daily Riches Owner|TFH}}</ref>

Wearing {{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform}} also grants the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} an extra 10 seconds to complete time-based {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}}, multiplied by the number of heroes wearing the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}.<ref>{{Cite|Peek into unopened chests, and enjoy +10 seconds in timed challenges.|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> In addition, picking up an {{Term|TFH|Hourglass|link}} while dressed in {{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform}} will increase the amount of time regained by 50%. These abilities are in reference to the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}}, a dungeon in {{PH|-}} which {{Term|PH|Linebeck}} has {{Term|PH|Link}} explore under a time limit.

Any {{Plural|TFH|Links}} who join the game through [[Download Play]] will be unable to obtain {{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform}}, since the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials}} does not unlock in this mode.
