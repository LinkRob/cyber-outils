The {{Term/Store|Daily Challenge||Series, CoH}} is a {{Term|CoH|Mode|link}} in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Daily Challenge|Select Mode|CoH}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|CoH|Daily Challenge}} is a mode that may be completed daily on {{Term|CoH|Permadeath Mode|link}}. It randomly generates a new overworld and playable character every time. As it is on {{Term|CoH|Permadeath Mode}}, dying will result in failure of the mode and it will not be playable until the next day.<ref>{{Cite|Play in Permadeath Mode with a random character on a new world each day. You only get one chance per day!|Select Item|CoH}}</ref> It is incompatible with {{Term|CoH|Fixed-Beat Mode|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Double-Time Mode|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Mystery Mode|link}}.
