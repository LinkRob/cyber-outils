== Overview ==
The Trial of the Mighty Foe is a side quest that can be unlocked after completing the [[Scenario]], "[[Destroy the Yiga Clan!]]" in [[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|Chapter 4]].

There will be three quests that can make you qualify for [[The Trial of the Ancients]], and this is one of them. It can be found in the [[Faron]] region.

The requirements needed to complete this are 10[[Ethereal Stone|Ethereal Stones]], 1 [[Stone Talus Trophy]], 3 [[Hinox Trophy|Hinox Trophies]], and 3 [[Lynel Trophy|Lynel Trophies]].

The remaining two needed to qualify for The Trial of Ancients are [[The Trial of Monsters]] and [[The Trial of Stones]].
