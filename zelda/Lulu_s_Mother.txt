'''Lulu's Mother''', who was also named '''Lulu''', is an unseen character mentioned in {{MM}}.{{Name Ref|MM}}

==Biography==
Lulu's Mother was the original lead singer of {{Term|MM3D|The Indigo-Go's|link}} and mother of the current lead singer, [[Lulu]].<ref>{{Cite|The one you heard singing must have been the original Lulu, mother of the current lead singer who's also named Lulu.|Toto|MM}}</ref>

Years ago, along with the members of the previous generation of {{Term|MM3D|The Indigo-Go's}}, this Lulu made the iconic song "{{Term|MM3D|Ballad of the Wind Fish|link}}" famous.<ref>{{Cite|It's their old standby... "Ballad of the Wind Fish." That's the song that members of the previous generation of The Indigo-Go's made famous!|Toto|MM}}</ref> It was, in fact, listening to her sing at the {{Term|MM3D|Carnival of Time|link}} that inspired {{Term|MM3D|Gorman|link}} to enter show business, in the hopes that he might someday meet her in person.<ref>{{Cite|I entered show business because I heard this song at the Carnival of Time long ago... By doing performances, I thought maybe I'd get to meet the singers of that song...|Gorman|MM}}</ref> The conversation between [[Toto]] and Gorman explaining about Lulu's mother occurs after Link plays the theme song through all of his individual transformations for the former in the [[Milk Bar]], which grants him the [[Troupe Leader's Mask]] by the latter as he comes to his inner senses.

When her daughter was a child, she would often sing the "{{Term|MM3D|New Wave Bossa Nova|link}}" to her. The {{Term|MM3D|Lulu}} of the current generation explains to Link in his [[Zora Link|Zora]] form that she had forgotten that song from her childhood, and that her {{Plural|MM3D|Zora Egg|display=Eggs|link}} were laid to remind her of it.<ref>{{Cite|But that song you played for me...My mother used to sing it often...long ago... Those eggs were laid to remind me of that. That song was from when I was a very young child, so I had forgotten it...|Lulu|MM}}</ref> If, as {{Term|MM3D|Tijo|link}} speculates, {{Term|MM3D|Lulu}} is the descendant of the {{Term|MM3D|Zora}} protectors of the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple|link}},<ref>{{Cite|There's a rumor that the ocean is getting weird 'cause there's trouble off shore at Great Bay Temple. And they say when that happens, something bad will befall the Zora descendant who's been protecting the temple. Do you catch my drift? And lately, Lulu has been looking kind of weird. Maybe Lulu's the...|Tijo|MM}}</ref> then it is possible that it was through her mother that this bloodline was carried. This would explain why {{Term|MM3D|Lulu}} sang the "{{Term|MM3D|New Wave Bossa Nova}}", which awakens the {{Term|MM3D|Giant Turtle|link}} and opens the way to the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple}}, to her daughter.

It is never mentioned in-game whether Lulu's Mother had died or is somewhere in another land.

== Trivia ==

* [[Lulu]] having an unseen mother is a reference to her [[Hyrulean]] counterpart [[Princess Ruto]], whose her own is also never seen in person in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''.
