{{Term/Store|EX Alert: Akkala Citadel||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 78 {{Term|HWAoC|EX Vicious Monster Battle|link}} in {{HWAoC}} included in the first downloadable content pack, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Alert: Hyrule Field|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} located in different parts of the map. Both are surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Lizalfos|link}}, while {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} are also scattered around the citadel. Once the two are defeated, a gate opens to a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin}} {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} that needs to be captured. Afterwards, a gate opens to an area in the top right corner of the map, in which the vicious monsters spawn. As the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} fights the vicious monsters, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} will appear. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the vicious monsters are defeated, and can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of two {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Endura Carrot|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Tireless Frog|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Claw|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
