The '''Underground Tunnel''' is a location in {{TSP}}.{{Name Ref|TSP}} As its name implies, it is an underground maze-like tunnel filled with {{Term|TSP|Stalfos|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TSP|Link|link}}, {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda|link}} and {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|link}} fall into the {{Term|TSP|Underground Tunnel}} after {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}}'s {{Term|TSP|Sword}} accidentally slices into the roots of a {{Term|TSP|Quaker Tree|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The sword, caught underneath his massive body, sinks into the ground. R-R-R-UMMMMBLE! The earth around Link's feet shakes. Oh, no! Charles's sword must have sliced into the roots of a quaker tree! |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 12}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The next thing knows, he, Zelda and Charles have dropped twenty feet into an underground tunnel. |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 12}}</ref> After the fall, {{Term|TSP|Link}} notices a piece of paper sticking out of a rock and tells {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda}} and {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}} that he found a {{Term|TSP|Map}}, hoping that it is not fake.

If {{Term|TSP|Link}} thinks the {{Term|TSP|Map}} is fake, he will throw it away and tell {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda}} and {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}} to follow him, and although {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda}} tries to stop him, they have no choice but to run after the young hero.<ref>{{Cite Book| quote='This map has to be fake,' Link says. 'Why else would it be in such an easy hiding place? Follow me! I'll get us out of here!' 'But Link!' Zelda cries. 'Maybe it's--' It's too late. Link has already disappeared down the nearest tunnel.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 80}}</ref> With the {{Plural|TSP|Stalfos}} pursuing them, {{Term|TSP|Link}} tries to get the group out of the {{Term|TSP|Underground Tunnel|display=Tunnel}}, but he trips on a {{Term|TSP|Stalfos}} since the {{Term|TSP|Underground Tunnel|display=Tunnel}} is so dark. The page ends with a {{Term|TSP|Game Over}} as {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda}} cries to {{Term|TSP|Link}} saying that he picked the wrong path, claiming that they'll die at the hands of the {{Plural|TSP|Stalfos}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Then Link looks up to see the pale bones of the Stalfos whipping toward them. 'Oh, Link!' Zelda cries. 'You picked the wrong path. We're done for!' |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 81}}</ref>

However, if {{Term|TSP|Link}} decides that the {{Term|TSP|Map}} is real, he will tell the {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda}} and {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}} to follow him after examining the routes just as the {{Plural|TSP|Stalfos}} begin to approach them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link hears the howling of Stalfos rushing toward them. He takes a deep breath. 'Let's hope this map is real,' he says to Zelda and Charles. Quickly, he unfolds it and searches for a route out. |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 48}}</ref> Eventually, they safely exit back out to the woods, leaving the {{Plural|TSP|Stalfos}} howling at the end of the {{Term|TSP|Underground Tunnel|display=Tunnel}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'This way!' he calls. [...] In minutes they're back outside. The skeletons stop at the mouth of the tunnel, howling. |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 48}}</ref>
