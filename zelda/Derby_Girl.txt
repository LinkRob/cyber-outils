The {{Term/Store|Derby Girl||Series, ALBW}} is a character from {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Biography==
The Derby Girl can be found at the playing field in central [[Lorule]]. She runs the [[Octoball Derby]] mini-game with the [[Derby Boy]] and [[Octo]]. When spoken to, she gives [[Link]] instructions on how to aim by changing stances. She is one of several Loruleans to wear a mask, implying that she may be a [[Masked Follower]].

==Trivia==
*The mask she wears bares a resemblance to the transformed [[Bully]] from {{ALttP}}.
