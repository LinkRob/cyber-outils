The {{Term/Store|Elemental Sanctuary||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=There were four elements hidden around the world that Link needed to obtain and then place on the pedestal in the Elemental Sanctuary to restore the sacred power to the blade.|book=HH|page=80}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary}} is the gateway between the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The elemental sanctuary exists between the human world and the Minish world. Here, let me explain with a little example: in a ham sandwich... the elemental sanctuary would be the ham between the two slices of bread!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> The entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary}} is located within the {{Term|TMC|Palace Courtyard|link}} of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} and opens only once every hundred years. Apparently, it is only visible to children, and as such, not many even know of its existence. In the center of the main room stands a pedestal for infusing the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}} into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} to create the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}. A hidden room in the back also contains a set of stained glass windows which tell the story of the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Hero of Men|link}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}}.

After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} defeats {{Term|TMC|Vaati|display=Vaati Transfigured}} at the top of {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, he then has to defeat {{Term|TMC|Vaati|display=Vaati's Wrath}} in a room that can be accessed by the original entrance of the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary}}. After {{Term|TMC|Link}} has defeated {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s final form, the entrance of the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary}} will close.

The {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary}} appears in {{TMC}} and the entrance closes by the end of the game. However, the {{Term|Series|Four Sword Sanctuary|link}}, the altar where {{Term|Series|Vaati}} is sealed in {{FS}} and {{FSA}}, resembles the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary}} and even contains the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements}} on their pedestals.
