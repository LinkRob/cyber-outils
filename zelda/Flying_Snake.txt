{{Name|Fan|Flying Snakes}} are enemies that appear in {{TFoE}}.

==Characteristics==
As their name implies, they are snakes that have gained the ability to fly. As with most enemies, they have a tendency of spawning frequently. Since they are flying enemies, they can be stunned for a brief period of time by ringing the {{Term|TFoE|Bell|section=The Faces of Evil|display=Bell|link}}. Their only means of attack is to fly directly into {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}}.
