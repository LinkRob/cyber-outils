{{Term/Store|King Mutoh||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283}}</ref>

==Biography==
Mutoh is the spirit of the long-dead ruler of the [[Cobble Kingdom]].<ref>{{Cite|My name is Mutoh. I am the king of the great Cobble Kingdom.|Mutoh|PH}}</ref> His coffin can be found within the [[Mutoh's Temple|largest pyramid tomb]] on the [[Isle of Ruins]], guarded by [[Eox]]. After defeating Eox, Mutoh gives [[Link]] the [[Aquanine]] in acknowledgment of Link's strength and cunning.<ref>{{Cite|Our King Mutoh did have the pure metal you seek.|Bremeur|PH}}</ref> He also sends {{Term|PH|Link}} a {{Term|PH|Letter|link}} with a {{Term|PH|Power Gem|link}} enclosed.<ref>{{Cite|This is a letter from King Mutoh! Ahem! Brace yourself... Boy clad in green, I was deeply impressed by your immense strength! I present you with a treasure from my kingdom. I hope it serves you well. His Majesty, King Mutoh|Postman|PH}}</ref>
