{{Term/Store|Din's Flame||Series, SS}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tempered by Din's Flame, this sword now holds a force capable of annihilating evil.|N/A|SS}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|SSHD|Din's Flame}} is one of the three {{Plural|Series|Sacred Flame|link}}, protected by the dragon {{Term|SSHD|Eldin (Dragon)|link}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Surface is divided into three regions. Each region is named after one of the dragons, Faron, Eldin, and Lanayru, who protect the Sacred Flames.|book=HH|page=74}}</ref>, and intended to enhance the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword|link}} with the {{Term|SSHD|Force|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=All the while, Link continued with his task of tempering the Goddess Sword in the Sacred Flames of the Golden Goddesses. Imbued with the immense, sacred power of the Force, the Goddess Sword was transformed into the Master Sword.|book=HH|page=75}}</ref>

In order to awaken the {{Term|SSHD|Gate of Time|link}}, {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} must empower the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword}} with all three {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Flame}};<ref>{{Cite|Ah, sorry to disappoint you, boy, but for now your sword lacks the power necessary to awaken the gate. First, you and your sword must grow together.|Impa|SS}}</ref> in order to obtain them, he must pass the trials found in the {{Plural|SSHD|Silent Realm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The sacred flames are three in number. To obtain them, you must also earn relics known as the three sacred gifts. For each trial you overcome, you shall be blessed with one of the gifts...|Fi|SS}}</ref>

{{Term|SSHD|Din's Flame}} is kept within the {{Term|SSHD|Fire Sanctuary (Dungeon)|link}} in the {{Term|SSHD|Eldin Province|link}}.<ref name=din>{{Cite|The final sacred flame, which you found within the Fire Sanctuary, has purified your sword, allowing it to achieve its true form.|N/A|SS}}</ref> Once {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} defeats {{Term|SSHD|Ghirahim|link}}, he obtains the {{Term|SSHD|Din's Flame|display=Flame}}, which triples the strength of the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword}} and grants it the {{Term|SSHD|Power to Repel Evil|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The sacred red flame has imbued your blade with a divine power that repels evil.|N/A|SS}}</ref> After this final tempering, the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword}} is transformed into the {{Term|SSHD|Master Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Master, now that it has been tempered by the last of the sacred flames, your blade has finally revealed its true form. You now hold the Master Sword.|Fi|SS}}</ref>
