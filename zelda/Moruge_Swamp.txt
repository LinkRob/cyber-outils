{{Term/Store|Moruge Swamp||Series, TAoL}} is a recurring location {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 224 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TCT, TSP}}

==Features and Overview==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
In {{TAoL}}, Moruge Swamp is a swamp situated directly to the north of the [[Water Town of Saria]]. To get to the swamp, Link must first obtain the [[Jump Spell]] from the [[Town of Ruto]], and then pass through the cave south of the town. The swamp borders a mountain range to the north, and a cave blocked by a boulder can be found where it borders said mountain range. By opening the cave using the [[Hammer]] found in [[Death Mountain]], [[Link]] will be able to enter the cave infested with [[Daira]]. It is at the end of the cave that the young hero finds the [[Water of Life]], which he gives to [[Old Woman|the mother]] of a sick girl in the [[Harbor Town of Mido]] to receive the [[Fairy Spell]] from an [[Old Man]]. A 1-Up [[Link Doll]] and a [[Fairy]] can also be found around the swamp.

Link has to cross its motion-inhibiting mire on his way to the Water Town of Saria during his quest to wake [[Princess Zelda]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TCT|-}}===
In {{TCT}}, the Moruge Swamp (misspelled as "Moluge") is briefly mentioned when Princess Zelda reaches the [[Pillar Crossroads]] and is trying to pick which way to go, whether through the [[Desert (The Crystal Trap)|Desert]], [[Swamp (The Crystal Trap)|Swamp]], or the [[Forest (The Crystal Trap)|Forest]]. The Swamp, however, is never confirmed to be the Moruge Swamp.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='And that's a carving of a swamp. But which swamp, Midoro or Moluge?'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=30}}</ref>

==={{TSP|-}}===
Although Moruge Swamp isn't visited in {{TSP}}, it is mentioned by Link as he tries to decide whether he should take the safe route of the [[Ruto Town]] detour and go through Moruge Swamp or if he should go through the short but dangerous [[Midoro Swamp]] path.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Hmm,' he says. 'Maybe I should bypass the swamp and go west, by Ruto Town. Of course, there's still Moruge Swamp to the south of Ruto, but that's safer than Midoro.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=111}}</ref> Even if he does choose to go through Ruto, his passage through the Moruge Swamp is never mentioned in the book.
{{Noncanon|End}}
