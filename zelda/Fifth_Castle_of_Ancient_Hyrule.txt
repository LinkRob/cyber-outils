The '''Fifth Castle of Ancient Hyrule''' is a location mentioned in the book {{TSP}}. It is situated in the southeast corner of [[Hyrule]], past many deserts, rivers, and swamps.<ref name=castle>{{Cite Book|quote='Deserts, rivers, swamps--some of the most treacherous terrain in the land. But it must be done. Ganon and his forces will never get our Triforce there. Never.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 6}}</ref>

After the [[King of Hyrule]] decides that the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] is in danger of being taken by [[Ganon]] from its chamber in [[North Castle]], the king announces that it must be relocated to the Fifth Castle of Ancient Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='I believe Ganon is after our Triforce of Wisdom again. It's in danger here. We must move it.' [...] To the Fifth Castle of Ancient Hyrule.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 6}}</ref> Due to the many dangerous terrains that exist between the Fifth Castle and North Castle, the king considers the Fifth Castle to be a good hiding place for the Triforce since it is unlikely that Ganon and his forces will ever be able to obtain it.<ref name=castle/>  When [[Princess Zelda]] asks who will deliver the Triforce of Wisdom there, the king mentions he has two heroes in mind: Link and [[Charles of Moria|Charles]]. Anxiously, Link awaits the king's decision, but it is soon revealed that Charles will be the one to take the Triforce to the Fifth Castle.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Charles of Moria will carry the Triforce of Wisdom to its new home,' the king announces.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 102}}</ref>
