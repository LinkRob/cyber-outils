{{Term/Store|Fried Egg and Rice|Fried Eggs and Rice|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Fried Egg and Rice}} are meals that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} predominantly {{Plural|BotW|Bird Egg|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}} together. If consumed by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} it will restore his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} by at least six {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. The soft egg yolks of this dish are known to pair well with the fresh rice.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Adding additional ingredients can result in several different variations of Fried Eggs and Rice:

* '''Hearty Fried Egg and Rice''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum number of Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Fried Egg and Rice''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding stamina-related ingredients. If eaten, they'll refill of a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Fried Egg and Rice''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Consuming these dishes will completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Fried Egg and Rice''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding speed-boosting ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's movement speed for a short time.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Fried Egg and Rice''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding warm ingredients. Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when these are eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Fried Egg and Rice''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electricity-related ingredients. When eaten, they'll grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Fried Egg and Rice''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Eating one of these dishes will increase Link's attack power for a short period of time.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Fried Egg and Rice''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding defensive ingredients. If they're eaten, they'll temporarily increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Fried Egg and Rice''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding stealthy ingredients. When these dishes are consumed, they'll provide a temporary boost to Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Fried Egg and Rice''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Fried Egg and Rice}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The soft egg yolk pairs well with the fresh rice in this simple dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding cold ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's [[Heat Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Chilly"/>
