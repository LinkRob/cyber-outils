You can use the paraglider to catch that updraft and ride it high into the sky.|Tips and Tricks|BotW}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Gust Geyser|Gust Geysers|PH|plural}},{{Exp Game|PH}}<ref name="PH Guide 15">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 15}}</ref> are a recurring mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}. {{Plural|Series|Updraft}} go unnamed in {{SS}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|Series|Updraft}} are gusts of wind that push upward, often carrying whatever enters the column of wind with them.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Plural|MM3D|Updraft}} can be found in the [[Stone Tower Temple]] in {{MM}}. They can be used by [[Deku Link]] to increase his altitude while gliding through the air. Deku Link can jump straight into a whirlwind to start flying without the use of a [[Deku Flower]].

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Plural|TWWHD|Updraft}} appear in the {{Term|TWWHD|Wind Temple|link}} and the {{Term|TWWHD|Official Rito Tribe Bird-Man Contest|link}} in {{TWW}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} can use them to gain altitude while gliding with the {{Term|TWWHD|Deku Leaf|link}}.

Although {{Plural|TWWHD|Updraft}} are mostly used as a boost while gliding, they can also be found as obstacles, such as in the {{Term|TWWHD|Wind Temple}}, where various "updrafts" blowing downward will block {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}'s path until deactivated. {{Plural|TWWHD|Updraft}} can also blow {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} away unless he wears the {{Term|TWWHD|Iron Boots|link}}, which must be worn to walk through the wind currents.

Other {{Plural|TWWHD|Updraft}} can be found in the [[Great Sea]]. They are able to move in certain directions and various speeds, similar to a real-life [[wikipedia:Waterspout|waterspout]], and essentially function as miniature [[Cyclone|Cyclones]]. While sailing in the [[King of Red Lions]], these updrafts can catch Link and dramatically change his direction.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Plural|TMC|Updraft}} are small vortexes found throughout {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} in {{TMC}}. {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can jump into them by using the {{Term|TMC|Roc's Cape|link}} or by jumping off a ledge. When jumping into one, {{Term|TMC|Link}} will hold onto {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} like a parachute, letting him glide through the air.

Though they function as a {{Plural|TMC|Tornado|link}}, the {{Plural|TMC|Crimson Cyclone|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}} have the appearance of red {{Plural|TMC|Updraft}}.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Plural|PH|Updraft}} are found on Islands throughout the [[World of the Ocean King]], such as on the [[Isle of Gust]], either buried under soil or appearing naturally on the ground. When Link enters a {{Term|PH|Updraft}}'s column, he will be propelled upwards to a nearby ledge.<ref name="PH Guide 15"/> If {{Plural|PH|Updraft}} are covered by soil, Link must uncover them with the [[Shovel]] in order to use them.<ref name="PH Guide 15"/> Alternatively, Link may purposefully cover {{Plural|PH|Updraft}} with the Shovel to access areas otherwise blocked by their columns of wind. Some Updrafts are activated via [[Switch]]es.<ref name="PH Guide 15"/>

Link can throw [[Bomb]]s into {{Plural|PH|Updraft}}, to make them explode at higher elevations and blast obstacles which obstruct ledges.<ref name="PH Guide 15"/> This tactic is necessary to defeat [[Cyclok]], the boss of the [[Temple of Wind]].

==={{SS|-}}===
{{Stub}}
Updrafts appear throughout [[The Surface]] in {{SS}}, such as in the [[Sealed Grounds]].<ref>{{Cite|I recommend using the air vents to move ahead of the monster instead of chasing it from behind.|Fi|SS}}</ref> By using the [[Sailcloth]] in tandem with these air vents, Link can ascend cliffs and ledges. Much like in {{PH|-}}, these vents of air may be trapped underground. Link can uncover these by using either the [[Digging Mitts]] or the [[Mogma Mitts]].

==={{BotW|-}}===
Updrafts can be caused by a variety of sources in {{BotW}}. Updrafts can occur when fire catches on grass or [[Spicy Pepper]]s, generating lift that [[Link]] can use in tandem with his [[Paraglider]] to soar into the sky.<ref name="Fire"/> [[Dragon]]s also naturally generate Updrafts when they fly through the sky, allowing Link to match their vertical position. Updrafts created by Dragons also fully regenerate Link's [[Stamina Wheel]] whenever he draws his Paraglider while in them. Some Updrafts occur via natural vents in the ground, allowing Link to scale heights with ease.

The Updrafts in the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|link}} are used for training by [[Rito]] warriors.<ref>{{Cite|We Rito warriors use the {{Color|BotW Red|updraft}} in this ravine to hone our aerial {{Color|BotW Red|archery}} skills.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> Except for during the "[[EX Champion Revali's Song]]" [[Main Quest]], the Flight Range's Updrafts will always regenerate Link's Stamina.
