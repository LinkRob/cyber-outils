'''YunoboCo HQ''' is a location in {{TotK}}.

==Features and overview==
If you take the North path out of [[Goron City]] and, when you come to the minecart station, take the left rails and sail down on a minecart. This will take you almost directly to YunoboCo HQ., and the YunoboCo HQ coordinates are (1619, 2820, 0420.) In the shop ripped and shredded They have the chestplate of the [[Flamebreaker Armor]] up for a cheap 700 rupees. There is also a [[Gloom Spawn]] location nearby. Two caves are nearby on rocks, The [[YunoboCo HQ South Cave]] and [[YunoboCo HQ East Cave]].
YunoboCo originated when Yunobo sought to help Goron City recover from the effects of the Calamity by mining gems and other resources from the newly rediscovered caves on Death Mountain, but the company and Yunobo himself changed when [[Phantom Ganon|"Zelda"]] gave Yunobo a evil mask. He started wearing it everywhere and mining strange marbled rocks. The other [[Goron]]s started eating them and not doing work. Their eyes turned pink and purple. The dubbed the food "[[Marbled Rock Roast]]".
Link must save [[Yunobo]] by destroying his mask. Once he has done that Yunobo will inform [[Link]] that he lost his memory when he put on the mask. Link will be given the quest "[[Yunobo of Goron City]]" starting the trek to the [[Fire Temple]] and get [[Vow of Yunobo, Sage of Fire]].

All around YunoboCo HQ are the strange reddish Marbled rock clumps. Once Yunobo reveals his power of fire you can destroy them revealing all manner of treasure.
