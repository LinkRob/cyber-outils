==Biography==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Term|MM3D|Madame Aroma|link}} serves the role of {{Term|MM3D|Postmaster}} in {{MM}}, being referred to by both titles by the {{Term|MM3D|Postman|link}}. After she receives the {{Term|MM3D|Special Delivery to Mama|link}} from the {{Term|MM3D|Postman}} and learns that he refuses to leave his duties in {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Madame Aroma}} orders him to flee.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! Well!!! What shall we do?!? It's from Kafei! [...] Thank you. You flee now. That's an order!|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref>

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Term|PH|Link|link}} receives a {{Term|PH|Letter|link}} from the {{Term|PH|Postmaster}} introducing the new {{Term|PH|Postman|link}} in {{PH}}, asking the hero to treat him nicely.<ref>{{Cite|If you're getting this, I guess that my new postman is working out...so far. So I'd like to make an unusual request. He's as good as a new hire gets! He's as diligent and efficient as they come! I've got no complaints. I want to keep it that way. So be nice to the Postman. He really is a good kid.|Postmaster|PH}}</ref> The {{Term|PH|Postmaster}} never makes a visual appearance, only introducing {{Term|PH|Link}} to the postal system in the first {{Term|PH|Letter}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Term|ST|Link|link}} receives a {{Term|ST|Letter|link}} from the {{Term|ST|Postmaster}} introducing the new {{Term|ST|Postman|link}}, asking the hero to treat him nicely.<ref name="ST Letter"/> The {{Term|ST|Postmaster}} never makes a visual appearance, only introducing {{Term|ST|Link}} to the postal system in the first {{Term|ST|Letter}}.
