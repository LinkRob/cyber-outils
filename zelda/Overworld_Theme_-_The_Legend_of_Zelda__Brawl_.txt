==Overview==
"Overworld Theme - The Legend of Zelda {{Small|(Brawl)}}" is an arrangement from {{SSBB}} of "[[Overworld Theme - The Legend of Zelda]]". The track also contains the title theme from {{TLoZ}}.

==={{SSBB|-}}===

==={{SSB4|Wii U|-}}===
"Main Theme Ver. 2 (The Legend of Zelda)" plays in the [[Bridge of Eldin]] Stage in {{SSB4|Wii U}}.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
"Overworld Theme - The Legend of Zelda {{Small|(Brawl)}}" plays in the any Stage related to {{TLoZ|-}} series in {{SSBU}}.
