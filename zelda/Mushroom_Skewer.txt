{{Term/Store|Mushroom Skewer|Mushroom Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Electro Mushroom Skewer|Electro Mushroom Skewers|Series, HWAoC|plural}},{{Exp Game|HWAoC}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>This simple mushroom packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Mushroom Skewer}} are meals that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. In order to cook the regular version of this dish, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must use either only {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}} or multiple, different {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom}} with varying effects. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} consumes a {{Term|BotW|Mushroom Skewer}} it will restore his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} by at least one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}. 

The skewers are known for their colorful presentation.<ref name="Inventory" />

Using certain Mushrooms when making Mushroom Skewers can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}. Eating these skewers will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}}. By eating them, a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}}. Link can eat them to completely refill and overfill his Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}}. When eaten, they will increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}}. Consuming these skewers will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}}. Eating them will give Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}}. If these skewers are eaten, Link will gain a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}}. Link gains a boost to his defense when he eats them.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}}. Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities will be increased by eating them.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This simple mushroom-packed skewer has its colorful presentation to thank for its appeal.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}}. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
