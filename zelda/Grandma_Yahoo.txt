==Biography==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Term|LANS|Grandma Yahoo}} is an elderly woman who enjoys sweeping with her broom. She lives in {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village|link}} with her husband, {{Term|LADX|Grandpa Ulrira|link}}, and does most of the work outside their house. She later travels to {{Term|LADX|Animal Village|link}}. At one point, she breaks her broom from the amount of sweeping she does.<ref>{{Cite|YAHOO! I worked too hard and now my broom is worn to the handle!|Grandma Ulrira|LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} can give her the {{Term|LADX|Broom|link}} he received from {{Term|LADX|Mr. Write|link}}. If he does, she will reward him with the {{Term|LADX|Fishing Hook|link}} she found while sweeping the river bank.<ref>{{Cite|YAHOO! YAHOO! A new broom?! For me? It is, isn't it?!|Grandma Ulrira|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Okay! In return you can have this fishing hook I found when I swept by the river bank!|Grandma Ulrira|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|YAHOO! A new broom! Superb!|Grandma Ulrira|LADX}}</ref>
