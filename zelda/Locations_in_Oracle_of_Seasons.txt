==Notes==
* Each region of Holodrum has a default season which automatically becomes present when Link enters the area. 
* The seasons in Goron Mountain, Natzu and Samasa Desert are invariable as these areas do not have tree stumps. These locations remain in perpetual winter, spring, and summer, respectively.
* After defeating Onox, the entire land reverts to spring by default, with the exception of Goron Mountain and Samasa Desert.
