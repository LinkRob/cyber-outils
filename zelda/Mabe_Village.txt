{{Term/Store|Mabe Village||Series, LA, LADX, LANS, HWAoC}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Mabe Village}}|N/A|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Mabe Village|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{LA|-}}===
in {{LA}}, {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village}} is a small settlement located in the southwestern area of {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}, and is the first location on the island that {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} visits. It is here that {{Term|LANS|Link}} is nursed back to health by {{Term|LANS|Marin|link}} and his adventure on the island begins.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=The maiden from Mabe village saved your life when she found you washed up on the beach.|game=LA|page=28}}</ref> After waking up in {{Term|LANS|Marin and Tarin's House|link}} and receiving his {{Term|LANS|Shield|link}} from {{Term|LANS|Tarin|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Well, Link, ya finally snapped out of it... Name's {{Color|LANS Pink|Tarin}}. Hope yer feelin' better... What? How did I know your name? You think it's weird, eh? Well, I saw it on back of this shield!|Tarin|LANS}}</ref> {{Term|LANS|Link}} travels to {{Term|LANS|Toronbo Shores|link}} where he finds his {{Term|LANS|Sword|link}}, and begins his quest to wake the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish|link}}. The {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}} is relatively small and contains only four houses—one less than its sister city, {{Term|LANS|Animal Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Did ya know Animal Village and Mabe Village are sister cities? Yes, even though they aren't large enough to be called cities... Anyway, I heard from a very good source that they have a Dream Shrine in Mabe, and that it has something good inside... Is that true?|Rabbit|LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LANS|Marin and Tarin's House}} is located in the middle of the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}}, just south of the {{Term|LANS|Weathercock|link}}. {{Term|LANS|Madam MeowMeow's House|link}} lies to the west of the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}} near the entrance to the {{Term|LANS|Mysterious Forest|link}}. South of {{Term|LANS|Madam MeowMeow's House}} is {{Term|LANS|Old Man Ulrira's House|link}}, where {{Term|LANS|Old Man Ulrira|link}} and {{Term|LANS|Grandma Ulrira|link}} live. In the very north of the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}}, west of the {{Term|LANS|Dream Shrine|link}}, is the {{Term|LANS|Quadruplets House|link}} where the {{Term|LANS|Quadruplet Family|link}} reside.

The {{Term|LANS|Library|link}} is located in the southwest corner of the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}}, which houses many {{Plural|LANS|Book|link}} containing helpful information.<ref>{{Cite|'BRRING! BRRING! Ulrira speaking! You know, there is a library in the village that might have some good information for you! Talk to you later! CLICK!'|Grandpa Ulrira|LADX}}</ref> Just north of the {{Term|LANS|Library}} is a {{Term|LANS|Well|link}} that {{Term|LANS|Link}} can enter to find a {{Term|LANS|Piece of Heart|link}}. South of the {{Term|LANS|Library}} is the way to the {{Term|LANS|South of the Village|link}} region, and north of the {{Term|LANS|Well}} is the entrance to the {{Term|LANS|Mysterious Forest}}. North of {{Term|LANS|Madam Meowmeow's House}} is the {{Term|LANS|Fishing Pond|link}}, where {{Term|LANS|Link}} can spend 10 {{Plural|LANS|Rupee|link}} to go {{Term|LANS|Fishing|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|How about some fishing, little buddy? I'll only charge you 10 Rupees...|Fisherman|LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LANS|Link}} can also obtain a {{Term|LANS|Piece of Heart}} here.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! It's a big one! And it has a Piece of Heart, too! You get a 20 Rupee prize on top of that! Try again?|N/A|LADX}}</ref> In the south of the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}}, east of {{Term|LANS|Old Man Ulrira's House}}, is a {{Term|LANS|Telephone Booth|link}}.

The {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}} also contains two shops. In the northeast of the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}}, west of the entrance to {{Term|LANS|Ukuku Prairie|link}}, is the {{Term|LANS|Town Tool Shop|link}}. Here {{Term|LANS|Link}} can buy helpful items such as {{Plural|LANS|Bomb|link}}, {{Plural|LANS|Heart|link}}, and {{Plural|LANS|Arrow|link}}, as well as major items such as the {{Term|LANS|Shovel|link}} and the {{Term|LANS|Bow|link}}. In the southeast of the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}} is the {{Term|LANS|Trendy Game|link}}, where {{Term|LANS|Link}} can play a crane game for 10 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|TRENDY GAME! One play: 10 rupees.|Trendy Game Shop Owner|LANS}}</ref> {{Term|LANS|Link}} can obtain many items here, most notably the {{Term|LANS|Yoshi Doll|link}}, the first item in the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}. In between the two shops is a large patch of {{Term|LANS|Grass|link}} that {{Term|LANS|Link}} can cut down to find a {{Term|LANS|Secret Seashell|link}}.

In the very center of the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}} is the {{Term|LANS|Weathercock|link}}, where {{Term|LANS|Link}} can learn the "{{Term|LANS|Ballad of the Wind Fish|link}}" from {{Term|LANS|Marin}} once he has obtained the {{Term|LANS|Ocarina|link}} from the {{Term|LANS|Dream Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! That's a nice Ocarina you have there! Will you accompany me as I sing?|Marin|LADX}}</ref> After learning the "{{Term|LANS|Frog's Song of Soul|link}}" from {{Term|LANS|Mamu|link}}, {{Term|LANS|Link}} can revive the {{Term|LANS|Flying Rooster|link}} in the hidden room beneath the {{Term|LANS|Weathercock}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! The Rooster has recovered! He seems very friendly!|N/A|LADX}}</ref> Finally, in the northeastern region of the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}} and east of the {{Term|LANS|Quadruplets House}}, is the {{Term|LANS|Dream Shrine}}. It can only be entered once {{Term|LANS|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|LANS|Power Bracelet|link}}.

In {{LA|DX}}, five {{Plural|LADX|Photograph|link}} of {{Term|LADX|The Travels of Link|link}} can be obtained in {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village}}. The first can be obtained by walking up to the window outside of {{Term|LADX|Old Man Ulrira's House}}. The {{Term|LADX|Photographer|link}} will then appear and take a picture of {{Term|LADX|Old Man Ulrira}} talking on the phone.<ref>{{Cite|I'll call this 'Link Discovers Ulrira's Secret!'|Photographer|LADX}}</ref> The second photo is obtained by walking up to {{Term|LADX|BowWow|link}} after returning him to {{Term|LADX|Madam Meowmeow}}. The {{Term|LADX|Photographer}} will appear and take a picture of {{Term|LADX|BowWow}} and {{Term|LADX|Link}} playing.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, that looks great! I'll call it 'Link Plays With BowWow!' Now get closer to BowWow!|Photographer|LADX}}</ref> The third can be obtained by stealing an item from the {{Term|LADX|Town Tool Shop}}. It will automatically be added to the photo album. The last two {{Plural|LADX|Photograph}} can only be obtained while {{Term|LADX|Link}} is escorting {{Term|LADX|Marin}}. For the first one, {{Term|LADX|Link}} must jump into the {{Term|LADX|Well}} north of the {{Term|LADX|Library}}. The {{Term|LADX|Photographer}} will appear and take a picture of {{Term|LADX|Marin}} landing on {{Term|LADX|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hi! I'm the photographer! What a great photo moment! I'll call this 'Heads Up!'|Photographer|LADX}}</ref> For the second one, {{Term|LADX|Link}} and {{Term|LADX|Marin}} must approach the {{Term|LADX|Weathercock}}. {{Term|LADX|Tarin}} will come along and a picture will be taken of the three of them.<ref>{{Cite|I use this to take pictures. Are you ready? Say 'mushroom!'|Photographer|LADX}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
The name ''Mabe'' is derived from the Japanese version's name for the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}},<span lang="ja">メーベ</span> (''Mēbe''), which may have actually been derived from ''<span lang="de">Möwe</span>'', the German word for "seagull".<ref name="Möwe">[https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/M%C3%B6we#German Wiktionary: Möwe]</ref>  This would be appropriate, given [[Marin]]'s strong association with seagulls.  The {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|display=Village}} is even called ''<span lang="de">Mövendorf</span>'' in the German version, and ''<span lang="fr">Village des Mouettes</span>'' in the French version, which means "village of seagulls" in both languages.
{{Nomenclature}}
