The {{Term/Store|Pirate's Bell||Series, OoS}} is an {{Term|OoS|Item|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 134}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
It is rung to announce departure.<ref>{{Cite|Argh! The sea is truly me home! Me heart's come alive! Aye! Sound the bell!|Captain|OoS}}</ref> It belongs to the [[Cap'n]] of the [[Piratian]]s, who had it as a gift from his lover, [[Queen Ambi]].<ref name="lost" /><ref>{{Cite|Cap'n got a special someone he can't forget. What's more, she's said to be the queen of some great land.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> The bell is lost in [[Samasa Desert]] when the Piratians are shipwrecked on [[Holodrum]] shores and driven into [[Subrosia]].<ref>{{Cite|Our ship got caught in a storm and sank. When we woke, 'ere we were. Where are we? It all looks so weird.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref><ref name="lost">{{Cite|It was so stormy that Cap'n lost the bell he got from a special someone. That's a bit o' trouble...|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> The Captain refuses to budge until his bell is found.<ref name="stuck">{{Cite|With the Cap'n in such a state, we'll never get the ship out!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> When Link finds the {{Term|OoS|Rusty Bell|link}} in the desert, he must bring it to the [[Subrosian Smithy]] to have it polished.<ref name="polish">{{Cite|Oh! Surely this- Ar! This thing's rusted something awful! What are you pullin'? This won't do! Go get it polished!|Captain|OoS}}</ref>

[[File:RustyBell.png|thumb|left|Link finds the Rusty Bell in [[Samasa Desert]]]]
[[File:Polished Bell.png||thumb|right|The bell is polished at the [[Subrosian Smithy]]]]
Throughout most of the game, the Piratians stand idly by at the [[House of Pirates]]. The crew is eager to dig itself out of Subrosia and set sail, but the Captain refuses to give the orders until the bell is found.<ref name="stuck" /> The Captain sends a few members of his crew to [[Samasa Desert]] to search for the bell, telling them not to come back until the bell is found.<ref>{{Cite|I want to get back to all me mates and dig the ship free, but Cap'n said, "Don't come back 'til ya find me bell"! Can't someone take me place..?|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> However, the Captain deems Link to be unfit for this quest until he completes the {{Term|OoS|Ancient Ruins|link}} dungeon.<ref>{{Cite|Ar, har, har! Don't be silly! You ain't an experienced adventurer yet! If you 'aven't at least proven your adventurin' skills by seein' the inside o' some ruins, I've no use for you!|Captain|OoS}}</ref> On the second floor of the House of Pirates, a Piratian teaches Link the combination to open the gate in front of the desert.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 46}}</ref> Once in the desert, Link climbs down into the hold of the ruined ship, where he meets the spirit of a Piratian who got caught in a sandstorm and died in his search for the Pirate's Bell.<ref>{{Cite|Ahoy! When I was searching the desert for the bell, I got caught in a sandstorm. I awoke like this!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> The unfortunate soul is unable to pass onto the afterlife until the bell his found and his quest is complete. He implores Link to find his skull rolling out in the desert and help him find the bell.<ref>{{Cite|I can't go on to the afterlife... If ya pity me, help me find that bell! Me skull is rollin' out in the desert. Find that first.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> Link finds the bristly, talking skull in the desert's oasis and picks it up with the [[Power Bracelet]]. As they roam the desert, the skull will begin to shatter when they near a specific pit of [[quicksand]]. Falling into this pit reveals a cave with the Rusty Bell lying inside.<ref name="Guide 47">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 47}}</ref> His quest complete, the Piratian disappears, but not before noting on the poor state of the bell.<ref>{{Cite|Uh-oh! That's Cap'n's bell, but I hope that'll do. It's awful rusty! ................ Well, at least I can go on to the afterlife now! The rest is up to you!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> In fact, the bell is so badly rusted that the Captain will not accept it and demand that Link have it polished.<ref name="polish" />

{{Clear}}

Arriving at the Subrosian Smithy, the "Master" mentions that Link has a bell of great value, but does not offer to polish it. If Link says "please", they will ignore his request. He must demand that they polish the bell in a firm and direct tone by saying "Do It!"<ref name="Guide 47"/> With the Pirate's Bell restored to its original state, Link returns the bell to the Captain and completes the quest. After a moment of solemn reflection, the Captain gives the order to board the ship and set sail.<ref>{{Cite|Ar! There's no mistakin' it! This bell is full o' memories--but that don't mean they're all good! Sniff! Now I'm all pensive. It's not like me. We're off, mateys! To sea!|Cap'n|OoS}}</ref>
