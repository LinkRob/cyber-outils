The {{Term/Store|Farore's Charm||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
After {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Gorman|link}} will arrive at {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} and search for a young woman to rent out a {{Term|TMC|Vacant House|link}} from him. After this, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with {{Term|TMC|Bremor|link}} to inspire {{Term|TMC|Mutoh|link}} to build a second {{Term|TMC|Vacant House}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} gives either of the {{Plural|TMC|Vacant House|display=Houses}} to {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)}}, she will offer to fill one of his {{Plural|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Farore's Charm}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you have an empty bottle, I can put one of my homemade {{Color|TMC Red|charms}} inside! Just ask anytime!|Farore (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Farore's Charm}} turns {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s tunic purple and increases his attack and defense;<ref>{{Cite|You got {{Color|TMC Red|Farore's Charm}}! Your power and guard will be boosted for a while!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> however, the increase is lesser than those obtained from the {{Term|TMC|Din's Charm|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Nayru's Charm|link}}.
