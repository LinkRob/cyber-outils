The '''Pink Fairy''' is a character featured in {{TCT}}. 

==History==
{{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|link}} and {{Term|TCT|Link|link}} can only meet her if they decide to stay and fight the {{Plural|TCT|Octorok|link}} that ambushed them after leaving the {{Term|TCT|Midoro Palace|link}}. Once Zelda defeats the {{Plural|TCT|Octorok}} with the use of {{Term|TCT|Zelda's Bow|display=her bow|link}} and {{Plural|TCT|Arrow|link}}, a {{Term|TCT|Pink Fairy}} will glow out from the corpse of one of the dead foes.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=But then they see a pink glow rising from the ground next to one of the dead beasts. 'A pink fairy!' Zelda cries with joy.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=69}}</ref> The {{Term|TCT|Fairy}} thanks the heroes for freeing her from the {{Plural|TCT|Octorok}} and rewards them by showing them where to find the weapon that will defeat {{Term|TCT|Ganon|link}}, the {{Term|TCT|Spear|link}}, which is over a hill in the {{Term|TCT|Forest (The Crystal Trap)|display=Forest|link}} they're in.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='You have freed me from the octoroks,' she says, 'and now I'll help you. I can show you where you'll find a weapon that can defeat Ganon.'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=69}}</ref> The heroes, having been healed by the {{Term|TCT|Fairy}}'s powers, rush to the direction that the mystical being pointed to.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Show us!' cries Link, his strength restored by the fairy's power. 'It's over that hill,' the fairy says. They hurry through the dense forest in the direction the sprite has pointed out.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=69}}</ref>
