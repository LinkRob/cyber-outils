'''Hyrule Castle Chasm''' is a location in {{TotK}}.

==Features and overview==
At the beginning of the game, [[Ganondorf]] lifted [[Hyrule Castle]] using [[Gloom]] and created an opening leading down to [[Gloom's Approach]] known as Hyrule Castle Chasm. Above is [[Hyrule Castle]] and its bottom half which the [[Zonai]] survey team and [[Hoz]] are researching the area and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]'s whereabouts.
