The {{Term|HWAoC|Flail}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}} archetype introduced in the {{PotA}} Downloadable Content pack for {{HWAoC}}. {{Plural|HWAoC|Flail}} are wielded by {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}, at the behest of {{Term|HWAoC|Purah|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Robbie|link}}.{{Fact}}

While there are three variants of Flails, unlike other weapons within the game, the Flail variants are not separated by a tiered strength system, nor are they introduced into the weapon drop system. What makes each Flail model unique (aside from the shape of their heads) is having a different action during Link's Strong I (X) button input. Each Flail starts with 35 power, but completing three Quests in the EX Royal Ancient Labs can increase the base power of all Flails by 10 each.

Link's Special Trait with the Flail is to grab a weapon that can be considered a two handed weapon, a one handed weapon, or a spear with the Flails, and make a copy of it to use as a temporary extension of the Flails when used on the ground. The copied weapon gets stashed on Link's back if he takes to the air, performs a Weak Point Smash, or a Special Attack. Attacking with it lowers the [[Durability]] Gauge until it breaks. Once it breaks, the gauge will refill slowly until the Flails are ready to copy again. The Durability Gauge can refill by attacking enemies once Link has completed the Ability Enhancement Quest for the Flails.

The Flails can only be made within the EX {{Term|HWAoC|Ancient Research Institute|link}} by trading in the materials needed to complete their related {{Plural|HWAoC|Side Quest|link|display=Quests}}. As they count as Material Quests, they can completed as many times as possible as long as players have the spare Materials.

The Flail variants are as followed:
*The first variant, the '''Guardian Flail''', is obtained by completing "A Helping Claw". The Guardian Flail's Strong I button input moves Link forward and attack as he makes his hop.
*The second variant, the '''Fortified Flail''', is obtained by completing "Magnificent Mass". The Fortified Flail's Strong I button input conjures a shield in front of Link that can behave exactly like a [[Perfect Guard]] when times right.
*The third and final variant, the '''Ancient Bladed Flail''', is obtained by completing "The Blazing-Blue Blades". The Ancient Bladed Flail's Strong I button input makes Link aim then fire a blast from the heads.
