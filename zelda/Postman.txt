The {{Term/Store|Postman|Postmen|Series, MM, MM3D, OoA, TMC, TP, TPHD, PH}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245,{{Exp Game|MM}} 254,{{Exp Game|OoA}} 270,{{Exp Game|TMC}} 277,{{Exp Game|TP}} 283,{{Exp Game|PH}} & 289{{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|SSBB, SSBU}} He is responsible for {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Delivery|link}} and usually works out of a {{Term|TWWHD|Post Office|link}}.

==Biography==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the Postman can be seen walking around [[Clock Town]] checking [[Mailbox]]es for mail. He takes his job very seriously, as he will not stop to chat when there is mail to be delivered,<ref>{{Cite|I'm currently on the job. If I stop to talk, it will disrupt my schedule.|Postman|MM}}</ref> and is not willing to leave [[Termina]] unless he is given explicit permission.<ref name=flee>{{Cite|Ohhh...I want to flee! B-but, it's not written on the schedule. T-to me, the delivery schedule... It's the h-highest priority.|Postman|MM}}</ref> He spends his free time counting and trying to stop exactly on ten seconds.<ref>{{Cite|4, 5, 6... Whaa! ...You startled me. Do not disrupt my training! In my mind, I am running for exactly 10 seconds without looking at a clock. I was in the middle of mental training!|Postman|MM}}</ref>{{Note|In the Japanese version, Link has to stop at exactly 10 seconds. In the North American version, he has a very small, yet bigger range of time to stop.{{Fact}}}} If [[Link]] manages to do it, the Postman will reward him with a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|You may make fun of me, but this is quite difficult! Will you try?|Postman|MM}}</ref>

The Postman also has a role in the quest to reunite [[Anju]] and [[Kafei]]. He gives Anju a letter from Kafei the [[First Day]], but refuses to tell her exactly where it is from when she asks.{{Ref Cluster|<ref>{{Cite|I have delivered this to you!|Postman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ah! Wait! This letter, wh-where did you?!?|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|From the postbox.|Postman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Th-that's not what I mean! From the postbox where?!?|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|From the postbox somewhere.|Postman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's not what I mean!|Anju|MM}}</ref>}} On the [[Second Day]], he delivers the letter to Kafei.

On the [[Final Day]], Link receives the [[Special Delivery to Mama|Priority Mail]] from the [[Man from Curiosity Shop|Curiosity Shop Man]]; he has two choices for how to use it. One of the choices allows him to free the Postman of his schedule. If Link goes to the [[Post Office#Majora's Mask|Post Office]], he finds him kneeling on the floor, wishing to flee but unable to as he still has jobs for the next day.<ref name=flee/> If Link gives him the Priority Mail, he realizes that the mail is the highest priority and delivers it to [[Madame Aroma]].<ref>{{Cite|Th-this is a priority mail seal! This is the highest of priorities. I shall deliver it!|Postman|MM}}</ref> After receiving the mail, she orders him to abandon his schedule and flee.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you. You flee now. That's an order!|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref> Finally free, the Postman will meet Link outside the [[Milk Bar#Majora's Mask|Milk Bar]] and give him the [[Postman's Hat]] before leaving town.

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, the Postman lives [[Lynna Village]] in the past and participates in the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest|link}}. He gives Link the [[Stationery]] in exchange for the [[Poe Clock]].
{{Clear|left}}

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Quill, Ilari, Koboli}}
The {{Term|TWWHD|Rito|link}} are the postmen of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Quill|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Ilari|link}} in particular bear the title of "Postman".<ref>{{Cite|Quill the Postman|Figurine|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...Oh, you're just some wretched street urchin. I thought you were the {{Color|TWWHD Red|postman}}.|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref> The mail sorter [[Koboli]] resembles the Postman of past games and his figurine implies he is the Postman's descendant.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Postman}}
The {{Term|TMC|Postman}} runs around {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} in {{TMC}} delivering letters.<ref name="Figurine"/>

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} impersonates {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Postman}} will {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Link|link}};<ref>{{Cite|Must deliver the mail... Hey, that's a Kinstone piece!|Postman|TMC}}</ref> a successful {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}} will result in {{Term|TMC|Marcy|link}} appearing at the {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}} and begin selling the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I really wish I could do more to help people... Wait! I've got it! I'll ask {{Color|TMC Green|Swiftblade}} to start up a newsletter for adventurers! That'll be a great way to provide a useful service for everyone... Hm. It's going to be a lot of work, though. I should get {{Color|TMC Green|Marcy}} to help me.|Stamp|TMC}}</ref>

While he runs around {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}, the {{Term|TMC|Postman}} delivers a number of catchphrases.<ref>{{Cite|I am the {{Color|TMC Green|postman}}, crossing town at incredible speed to bring you your mail!|Postman|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Faster than fast delivery! Swift and certain, that is the {{Color|TMC Green|postman}}'s aim!|Postman|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Lightly, lightly, lightly... Lightly treads the carefree {{Color|TMC Green|postman}}!!|Postman|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Dash, jump, delivery!! The {{Color|TMC Green|postman}} cometh!|Postman|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Here before you know it, it's your amazing neighborhood {{Color|TMC Green|postman}}!|Postman|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Known all through Hyrule for his charm and service, it's your friendly {{Color|TMC Green|postman}}!|Postman|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Able to withstand the greatest of delivery challenges! I am the {{Color|TMC Green|postman}} of tomorrow, here today!|Postman|TMC}}</ref>

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the {{Term|TP|Postman}} delivers letters to {{Term|TP|Link|link}} directly, typically when wandering around {{Term|TP|Hyrule Field|link}}. He is much more comically depicted compared to earlier titles. He breathes very oddly when running and hums several bars of one of the victory songs after he finishes delivering a letter. However, like all {{Plural|TP|Postman}}, he still takes his job very seriously. The {{Term|TP|Postman}} is able to recognize {{Term|TP|Link}} while he is in {{Term|TP|Wolf Link|link|display=wolf form}}, though Link will forcibly revert to his Hylian form when the cutscene begins.

When not delivering mail, the {{Term|TP|Postman}} can be seen in various places around {{Term|TP|Hyrule|link}}. He can be found in {{Term|TP|Ordon Ranch|link}} feeding an illegible letter to the {{Plural|TP|Goat|link}},<ref>{{Cite|We can't just let this goat eat the letter!|Postman|TP}}</ref> and in {{Term|TP|Zora's Domain|link}} behind the throne trying to identify who "Mr. Zora" is.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... This letter is addressed to Mr. Zora. Now who could that be?|Postman|TP}}</ref> He is also seen in {{Term|TP|Telma's Bar|link}} deciding what to order,<ref>{{Cite|Hmm - what to order, what to order... I do believe I will start with meat.|Postman|TP}}</ref> and in the {{Term|TP|Elde Inn|link}} drying off a letter he dropped in the {{Term|TP|Hot Spring|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I dropped a letter into the hot springs and now I can't read it. Gotta blow this thing dry!|Postman|TP}}</ref> Finally, he can also be found crouched on a wooden bridge in {{Term|TP|Fruit-Pop-Flight|link}}, and in the forty-ninth floor of the {{Term|TP|Cave of Ordeals|link}} after it has been cleared at least once.<ref>{{Cite|I think you are lost. It's got to be around here somewhere...|Postman|TP}}</ref>
{{Clear|left}}
His red banner bears the symbol of a {{Term|TP|Rabbit|link}}, along with the word "Postman" written in [[Hylian (Language)|Hylian]].

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, the Postman now has wings in order to fly from one island to another, similar to the [[Rito]] from {{TWW}}. He no longer resembles the Postman of ''Majora's Mask.'' Whenever Link finds a wiggling Mailbox, the Postman will appear to deliver his mail. This time around, Link does not receive the letter itself, as it is instead read by the Postman. An exception to this is [[Jolene's Letter]]. He also now features a winged letter as his cap's insignia instead of the bunny from previous games.
{{Clear|left}}

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, the Postman looks very similar to his {{PH|-}} counterpart, but has a different outfit and lacks wings. When meeting with or departing from Link, the Postman does an impression of a train, referencing the game's train theme. Unlike {{PH|-}}, where he reads letters once and then takes off, he now gives Link his letters to read himself, as in {{TP|-}}. He says that while letters used to be read out loud, the system was changed because some people thought it was rude, referencing how the Postman read the letters in {{PH|-}} instead of Link.<ref>{{Cite|I've got a letter for you, so I'll just jump right in and read it aloud. Oh, wait--that's the way we USED to do things. But no more! Apparently, some folks thought our reading their letters out loud was rude. So now our policy is to just hand-deliver the mail unopened.|Postman|ST}}</ref>
{{clear}}
