The {{Term/Store|Sea-Breeze Boomerang|Sea-Breeze Boomerangs|Series, BotW|plural}},<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Sea-Breeze Boomerang}}<br/>A boomerang said to have been used by a hero who traveled the Great Sea. It smells faintly of salt water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> also known as the '''Boomerang''' in {{TWW}},{{Name Ref|TWW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
The Boomerang can be found inside the [[Forbidden Woods]] in {{TWW}}. It now can lock onto up to five different targets,<ref>{{Cite|You got the Boomerang! Set it to and throw it with [Y], [Z], or [X]. Target specific objects by holding the button you set it to and aiming at something until the red targeting cursor changes to a [Target lock mark]. You can target up to five objects at once.|N/A|TWW}}</ref> which is used in various puzzles involving [[Switch]]es or for collecting multiple items at the same time.

The Boomerang primarily stuns enemies, though it can kill [[Boko Baba]]s in one hit.

The {{Term|BotW|Sea-Breeze Boomerang}} is obtained by scanning either the {{SSB|-}} or ''The Legend of Zelda'' series [[Toon Link]] {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. It is the {{Term|BotW|Sea-Breeze Boomerang|display=Boomerang}} used by a hero during the [[Era of the Great Sea]], and it consequently retains the scent of saltwater.<ref name="Inventory"/> Despite this, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain multiple {{Plural|BotW|Sea-Breeze Boomerang}} from repeatedly scanning the Toon Link amiibo.

Since it is made of wood, the {{Term|BotW|Sea-Breeze Boomerang}} will burn and disintegrate if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. It will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} like metallic {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, and it will also float if it is dropped on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. After {{Term|BotW|Link}} throws the {{Term|BotW|Sea-Breeze Boomerang|display=Boomerang}}, it will return to him after a short while. If he does not catch it when it does so, it will hit him and he will take damage.
