The {{Term/Store|Pirate Ship||Series, FPTRR}} is a mini-dungeon in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Entrance to the {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Ship (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|display=Ship}}==
The {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Ship (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)}} is home to the crew of {{Term|FPTRR|Stalfos|link}} {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate|link}}, run by {{Term|FPTRR|Captain Stalfos}} and his {{Term|FPTRR|First Mate|link}}. The {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Ship (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|display=Ship}} sails the waters from island to island, but it also has the ability to fly, which allows it to dock at places such as {{Term|FPTRR|Mount Desma|link}}. Shortly after {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} first meets the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}}, he is taken on the {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Ship (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|display=Ship}} to {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Hideaway|link}}. Later in his journey, he must venture down to the lower decks to help the crew members who are possessed by ghosts. He finds that the {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Ship (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|display=Ship}} is similar to the {{Plural|FPTRR|Dungeon|link}} he has previously explored, and that it is a place filled with numerous dangers.
