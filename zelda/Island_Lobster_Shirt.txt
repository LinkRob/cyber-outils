The Island Lobster Shirt goes unnamed in {{TWW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
The Island Lobster Shirt is part of [[Link]]'s default attire in {{TWW}}. Link switches to wearing the [[Hero's Clothes]] after being gifted them by his [[Link's Grandma|Grandma]].

In the [[Second Quest]], Link is instead gifted the invisible [[Hero's New Clothes]], which he wears on top of the Island Lobster Shirt. This allows Link to instead continue in his adventure with special dialogue addressing Link's blue clothing instead of his standard green. Upon first meeting Link,  [[Medli]] will comment on the color of Link's clothing and the "crawfish" design on them.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! You really DO have {{Color|TWWHD Light Blue|blue clothes}}...with a picture of a crawfish on them!|Medli|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wow! You really DO have {{Color|TWWHD Green|green clothes}} and a {{Color|TWWHD Green|strange-shaped hat}}...|Medli|TWWHD}}</ref>

==={{BotW|-}}===
The Island Lobster Shirt is a piece of [[Armor]] introduced in [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC pack as part of the "[[EX Treasure: Garb of Winds]]" [[Side Quest]]. It is a priceless relic that was once kept safely within the walls of [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]], but was later stolen by [[Misko]]. Misko hints at the whereabouts of the Island Lobster Shirt in [[Misko's EX Journal 2]] in the [[Deya Village Ruins]],<ref>{{Cite|I hid the six priceless relics I stole from {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> claiming that it rests at the mouth of the [[Menoat River]].<ref>{{Cite|The blue garb of the hero who controlled the winds and traveled the ocean is {{Color|BotW Red|where the Menonat River was born}}.|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> The [[Treasure Chest]] housing the Island Lobster Shirt can be found in the water beneath the alcove at [[Cora Lake]]. In order to retrieve it, [[Link]] must use the [[Magnesis]] [[Rune]] to pull it from the water.

The Island Lobster Shirt is the same Shirt that Link wore at the beginning of his journey in {{TWW|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Hero of Winds once wore this shirt on the quaint island he called home. They say he wielded the wind to travel the ocean wide. It's a rather rare find.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> When worn, it grants Link with one level of [[Heat Resistance]]. It also allows Link to move a raft with a korok leaf quicker while equipped. If Link sells the Island Lobster Shirt, he can later purchase it from [[Granté]] at [[Tarrey Town]] for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|L|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Island Outfits are Gold [[Material]]s which are dropped by [[Toon Link]] in {{HW|L}}.

The Outset Shirt is [[Fairy Clothing]] that [[Companion Fairy|Companion Fairies]] can equip in [[My Fairy]]'s [[Salon]].

The Island Lobster Shirt also appears as two unnamed [[Costume]]s in {{HW|L|-}}. The first is an iteration of the Hero's Clothes for Link, found on square A12 of the [[Great Sea Map]]. The second is an iteration of Toon Link's Standard Outfit, found on square G4 of the Great Sea Map.
{{Noncanon|End}}
