{{Term/Store|Lorule Ball and Chain Soldier|Lorule Ball and Chain Soldiers|Series, ALBW|plural}} are {{Plural|ALBW|Sub-Boss}} of {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 190}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The Lorulean variant of the [[Ball and Chain Soldier]], they appear as Sub Bosses in Lorule Castle. Their Ball and Chain is constantly on fire, similar to a Ball and Chain Soldier variant from {{FSA}}. The first one can be defeated if Link is careful, however, the second one is more dangerous due to the Bottomless Pits in its room. One also appears on Floor 39 of the [[Treacherous Tower]] in the Advanced difficulty.
