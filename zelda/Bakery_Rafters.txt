The {{Term/Store|Bakery Rafters||Series, TMC}} are a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Bakery Rafters}} are located above the {{Term|TMC|Bakery|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. They are only accessible to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} when he enters the {{Term|TMC|Bakery}} from its eastern wall while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized.

The {{Term|TMC|Bakery Rafters}} are inhabited by four {{Plural|TMC|Town Minish|link}}. Two, in blue and red, can be accessed through the right entrance; the other two, in green and pink, can be accessed through the left entrance. All four have the potential to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Green Kinstone|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Link}}.

A {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing 100 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} is found in the {{Term|TMC|Bakery Rafters|display=Rafters}} as well, accessible through the left entrance.
