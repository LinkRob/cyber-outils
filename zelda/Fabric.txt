TITLE : Fabric

{{Infobox Item|image=File:Fabric.png|pronunciation=fab-rick|plural=Fabrics|game={{TotK}}|location=Multiple|uses=Remodeling [[Paraglider]]|comp=[[Dye]]}}
'''Fabric'''s are items in {{TotK}}. They are used to remodel your paraglider in the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. <ref> {{Cite|So, which fabric do you have in mind for a paraglider rework today?|Sayge|TotK}} </ref>

Some fabrics come from reference images, and others come from doing tasks. A few can come from [[Amiibo (Rune)|Amiibo]] or even [[Mini-Game]]s.

For Reference Images, [[Sayge]] will make a list of creatures that can be references. The image must be in the [[Album]] to use, and the ability to use references is unlocked after completing the [[Photographing a Chuchu]] [[Side Quest]]. You can obtain fabrics by completing quests, and with [[Pony Point]]s. When you obtain a fabric the person who gives it to you will note that the Dye Shop has started a service for paraglider remodeling. If you ask to remodel the paraglider when you havent been given it by [[Purah]], Sayge will say that it must be a "Pigment of link's imagination".

When obtaining a fabric from [[Amiibo (Rune)|Amiibo]], it will always be in a [[Treasure Chest]]. Some fabrics even come from {{BotW}} Amiibo.

Some fabrics are from mass-collecting items, such as Addison's Fabric or Koltin's Fabric.

The [[Nostalgic Fabric]] remodels the paraglider after the one in {{BotW}}.

Fabrics that come from [[Mini-Game]]s usually have a time limit to complete a course.
