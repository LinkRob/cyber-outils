The {{Term/Store|Rin Oyaa Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Rin Oyaa Shrine}}<br/>Directing the Wind}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Directing the Wind''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Rin Oyaa Shrine}} is located at {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}} in the southwestern part of the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tundra|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rin Oyaa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Rin Oyaa. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Rin Oyaa|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Rin Oyaa Shrine}} features six fans, two metallic blocks, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} that, when activated by the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}}, raises a {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}} up to {{Term|BotW|Rin Oyaa}}. Four of the {{Plural|Series|Wind Switch}} blow the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} from the stone platform towards the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}, but the other two blow the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} into a dead-end. These {{Plural|BotW|Wind Switch}} can be blocked by the metal blocks. In order to complete the Trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must be standing on the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} when the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} activates the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}.

Once this is done, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be raised up, allowing him to approach {{Term|BotW|Rin Oyaa}}'s {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for completing the Trial, and they give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Rin Oyaa|BotW}}</ref> They will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Rin Oyaa|BotW}}</ref>

On the wall opposite the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}} is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Core|link}}. It can be accessed by placing one of the metal blocks on top of the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to jump across the gap.
