The '''Frost Emitter''' is an [[Item]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom.]]<ref>"''Frost Emitter''

''A Zonai device that shoots frost. It's thought to have been originally used as protection from monsters."''</ref>

==Locations and Uses==
{{Manual|TotK|Frost Emitter|A Zonai device that blasts out freezing air, handy for slowing and defeating some monsters. Also, some materials change their properties when exposed to very cold air.}}
Frost Emitters are [[Zonai Devices]] that can be used in battle by attaching them to a [[shield]] using [[Fuse]].
