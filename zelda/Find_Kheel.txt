"{{Term/Store|Find Kheel||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Find Kheel}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Rito Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has stopped the rampage of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}, he can find {{Term|BotW|Amali|link}} alone near {{Term|BotW|Akh Va'quot Shrine|link}}, calling out for her daughter {{Term|BotW|Kheel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kheel!!<br/>Where are you?!|Amali|BotW}}</ref> She explains to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she is unable to find {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}, and she asks where she could be.<ref>{{Cite|I can't find Kheel anywhere! Where could she be?|Amali|BotW}}</ref> Looking out at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Amali}} wonders if she went to practice there.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe she went to {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}} to practice her singing?|Amali|BotW}}</ref> Due to its distance, though, she wonders whether {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} could have made it on her own.<ref>{{Cite|But that's so far away. Could she have made it there on her own? Oh, I'm so worried...|Amali|BotW}}</ref> When she is done talking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins.

At {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} will complain about her sisters to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Grrrrrr! My sisters are dumb DUMMIES!}}|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> They had all promised to rehearse their song for the elder {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}, but {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} was the only one who showed up.<ref>{{Cite|They promised we'd rehearse together, but NO ONE showed up! We have to perform our song for the {{Color|BotW Blue|elder}} at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}}!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> With sadness, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} says they will be laughed off the stage.<ref>{{Cite|We're going to be laughed off the stage...|Kheel|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kheel}} realizes she can use {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you... You look like I can use you... Thaaaat is to say...you look like a nice guy who likes to help people in need.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> asking that he go back to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}} and retrieve her sisters for their rehearsal.<ref>{{Cite|Can you go tell my sisters in Rito Village to stop ditching rehearsal?|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> She knows they will not listen to her, but she think they will listen to a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} like {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They won't listen to me, but they might listen to a scary-looking Hylian like you.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> However, her sister {{Term|BotW|Genli|link}} might be more difficult for him, as she is probably in the kitchen demanding {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Although Genli might be a tough sell. She's probably in the {{Color|BotW Red|kitchen}} yelling, {{Big|"I want some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}!"}} ...or something like that.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> This starts the "{{Term|BotW|Recital at Warbler's Nest|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has confirmed that {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} is fine, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} to tell {{Term|BotW|Amali}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found Kheel?!|Amali|BotW}}</ref> She is relieved to hear that {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} really was at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}.<ref>{{Cite|So she's at {{Color|BotW Blue|Warbler's Nest}} after all. Phew... She really had me worried!|Amali|BotW}}</ref> To say thanks, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for letting me know. Please, take this for your trouble.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Amali}} admits she feels better knowing where {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} is,<ref>{{Cite|I feel much better now that I know where she is. Thank you.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} ends.
