The {{Term/Store|Old Man's Cabin||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 224}}</ref> also known as the '''Woodcutter's House''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 403}}</ref> is a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.

==Features and Overview==

'''''Breath of the Wild'''''

The {{Term|BotW|Old Man's Cabin}} is a small cabin based in the southwestern region of the [[Great Plateau]]. The Old Man's Diary can be found inside, which contains the Recipe for [[Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry]].<ref>{{Cite|On this desolate plateau, the only pleasure that brings me comfort is cooking. And today, I outdid myself! Truly, I created the perfect dish. I call it... spicy meat and seafood fry.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ah, and I will now write down this perfect recipe so that I will never forget it. Place some raw meat, spicy pepper, and hyrule bass in a pot, and then turn up the heat. Voilà!|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} prepares the dish for the [[Old Man]], he will be rewarded with the [[Warm Doublet]].<ref>{{Cite|Sadly, on this lonely plateau, I have only my own knowledge and memory to rely on. Still... If I did find someone who knew the missing ingredient, I would happily reward them with my {{Color|BotW Blue|warm doublet}}.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> If the [[Armor]] is not obtained either by giving the Old Man the dish or by visiting [[Mount Hylia]], a [[Treasure Chest]] containing it will appear inside the Cabin.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, yes, one more thing. I have left the {{Color|BotW Blue|warm doublet}} here as a gift of sorts. I'm sure it will be useful on your journey.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> As Link progresses through the Plateau, the Old Man's Diary will update accordingly.<ref>{{Cite|To my surprise and delight, I did happen upon someone who was able to re-create my spicy meat and seafood fry. I gave him my {{Color|BotW Blue|warm doublet}}, and I was happy to do so. He is a fine young man with great potential, that one.|The Old Man's Diary|BotW}}</ref> A [[Cooking Pot]] can be found outside the Cabin, as well as [[Barrel]]s including a giant Barrel in which Link can hide.

A Korok can be found hiding atop the roof of the {{Term|BotW|Old Man's Cabin}}.

'''''Tears of the Kingdom'''''

After the events of [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild]], the landscape of Hyrule has been altered by some passage of time.  The Old Man's Cabin is now taken over by the [[Yiga]] clan.  Two barricades of spiked-walls surround the cabin to make entry somewhat difficult, with various Yiga headstones and paraphernalia adorn the surrounding area.  Upon approaching the front door, Link can peer through a hole in the middle of the door to see a [[Yiga Footsoldier]] and [[Yiga Blademaster]] waiting inside.  Once answering the door, the two Yiga ninjas are startled by Link's sudden arrival.  Both will teleport outside of the cabin and fight Link.  

A Korok can be found by placing Mighty Bananas in the offering trays of a row of three Yiga-branded frog statues in the area.
