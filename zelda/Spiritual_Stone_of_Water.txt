The {{Term/Store|Spiritual Stone of Water||Series, OoT, OoT3D}},<ref name="E 143">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 143}}</ref> also known as the '''Zora's Engagement Ring''',<ref name="Ruto">{{Cite|My mother gave it to me and said I should give it only to the man who will be my husband. You might call it the Zora's Engagement Ring!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoT3D}} and the '''Zora's Sapphire''',<ref name="E 143"/> is an important item in {{OoT}}.

==Location and uses==
[[File:Zorassapphire.jpg|thumb|left|251x251px|The Spiritual Stone of Water being obtained in {{OoT|-}}]]
The Spiritual Stone of Water is one of the three [[Spiritual Stone]]s, and the last to be obtained. It was given to [[Princess Ruto]] by her unseen mother, who told her to entrust it to the man who will become her husband.<ref name="Ruto"/> After [[Link]] saves Ruto from [[Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly]], the [[Zora]] princess falls in love with the [[Hero of Time]] and gives him the Zora's Sapphire as a symbol of their "engagement."<ref>{{Cite|You! You looked cool...cooler than I thought you would, anyway... Just a little! Well, anyway, you saved me, so I guess I'll reward you. What do you wish? Just tell me... [...] All right! I'll give you my most precious possession: Zora's Sapphire!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref> Despite this proposal, Link is oblivious to the possible marriage with Ruto, as the young hero only knows he need the final Spiritual Stone to complete [[Princess Zelda]]'s request in order to stop [[Ganondorf]] from reaching the [[Sacred Realm]].
