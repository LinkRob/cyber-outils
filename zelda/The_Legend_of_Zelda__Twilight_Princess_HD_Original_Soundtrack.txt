{{Term/Store|''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Original Soundtrack''||Series}} is the original soundtrack for {{TPHD}}. It features three discs, an eight-page booklet, and a double-sided miniposter, all inside a digipak. First prints of the soundtrack also included a 40mm badge.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= |retrieved= June 20, 2016|url= http://symphony-5.com/azaj/|title= The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Original Soundtrack|site= Symphony No.5|type=}}</ref> It is exclusive to Japan.
