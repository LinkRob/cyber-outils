{{Compendium/Extract|Dragonbone Boko Club}}
{{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Club}} are often found being used by {{Term|BotW|Black Bokoblin|link|display=Black}} and {{Plural|BotW|Silver Bokoblin|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, they are also used by {{Plural|BotW|Golden Bokoblin|link}}. They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Being reinforced with fossilized bones has increased the amount of damage that {{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Club}} can deal.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly found in {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Plural|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} and near the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}}. They also have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn when the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Club}} will burn and eventually disintegrate if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. This tactic is often used by enemy {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}, who will set their {{Term|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Club|display=Clubs}} on {{Term|BotW|Fire}} to deal additional damage to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. They also do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, unlike metal {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, and they will float if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Finally, they can not be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
