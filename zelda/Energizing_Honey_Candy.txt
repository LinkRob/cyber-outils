==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Energizing Honey Candies are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} at least one [[Courser Bee Honey]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Since they're composed entirely of Courser Bee Honey, there is no default version of Honey Candies, as all results grant the energizing effect. When eaten, Energizing Honey Candies will instantly refill a portion of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}. They also replenish six {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and sell for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.
