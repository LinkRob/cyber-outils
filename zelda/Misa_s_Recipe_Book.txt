==Location and Uses==
''Misa's Recipe Book'' can be found at {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}}, located in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. It belongs to the shop's proprietor, {{Term|BotW|Misa|link}}, who encourages {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to read it.<ref name="Book"/> Within it, recipes for {{Term|BotW|Apple Pie|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nutcake|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Fried Bananas|link}} are written.
