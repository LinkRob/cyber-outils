==Location and Uses==
Chuchu Trophies are obtained as post-battle rewards after finishing any given Chapter's [[Scenario]] battle or a [[Challenge]], with a Trophy earned for every [[Chuchu]] kill (be they regular, [[Fire Chuchu|Fire]], [[Ice Chuchu|Ice-Breath]], and [[Electric Chuchu|Electric]]) a Warrior obtains.

Chuchu Trophies can be used to either complete [[Side Quest#Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|Quest]]s or be used in the Hyrule Blacksmith Guild workshop to de rust Rusted Weapons or remove Seals on a weapon.
