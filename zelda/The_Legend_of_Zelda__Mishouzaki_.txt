'''''The Legend of Zelda''''' is a [[manga]] following the original {{TLoZ}} storyline. It was drawn and written by [[Yuu Mishouzaki]] and was published in September 1989.

In this manga adaption, {{Term|TLoZ (Mishouzaki)|Link|link}} is an apprentice gardener living in {{Term|TLoZ (Mishouzaki)|Hyrule Castle|link}} who has pointed ears due to him being half-elf. {{Term|TLoZ (Mishouzaki)|Impa|link}} is also drastically younger.
