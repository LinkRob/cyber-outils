The {{Term/Store|Coral Triangle||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a quest item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 120"/>

==Location and Uses==
The Coral Triangle is one of the eight [[Instruments of the Sirens]] needed to awaken the [[Wind Fish]].<ref name="E 120"/> It can be found at the end of [[Face Shrine]] after defeating its boss, [[Facade]].<ref name="E 120"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWL Coral Triangle Sprite.png|left|40px]]
In {{HW|L}}, the Coral Triangle appears as an [[Item Card]] in the [[Koholint Island Map]] of [[Adventure Mode]], which is included in the {{LA|-}} DLC Pack. It increases the amount of [[Food]] earned.
{{Noncanon|End}}
