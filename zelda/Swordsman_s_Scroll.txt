{{Term/Store|Swordsman's Scroll|Swordsman's Scrolls|Series, PH, ST|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140 {{Exp Game|PH, ST}}}}</ref> They are portrayed as scrolls containing ancient wisdom on {{Term|Series|Sword|link}} techniques.

==Appearances==
==={{PH|-}}===
The {{Term|PH|Swordsman's Scroll}} is a collectible in {{PH}}. It is obtained by completing the [[Phantom Hourglass Trading Sequence|trading sequence]], which culminates in {{Term|PH|Link|link}} receiving the {{Term|PH|Swordsman's Scroll|display=Scroll}} from the {{Term|PH|Old Wayfarer|link}} on {{Term|PH|Bannan Island|link}}. Memories of veteran swordsmen flow through the {{Term|PH|Swordsman's Scroll|display=Scroll}}, which enables {{Term|PH|Link}} to execute the [[Great Spin Attack]] by doing three consecutive {{Plural|PH|Spin Attack}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Main|Swordsman's Scroll 1, Swordsman's Scroll 2}}
Two {{Plural|ST|Swordsman's Scroll}} appear in {{ST}}. {{Term|ST|Swordsman's Scroll 1}} can be obtained from {{Term|ST|Bunnio|link}} after {{Term|ST|Link}} manages to capture all 50 {{Plural|ST|Rabbit|link}} and takes them to {{Term|ST|Rabbitland Rescue|link}}. The {{Term|ST|Swordsman's Scroll|display=Scroll}} allows {{Term|ST|Link}} to shoot {{Plural|ST|Sword Beam|link}} out of the {{Term|ST|Lokomo Sword|link}} when he is at full health. {{Term|ST|Swordsman's Scroll 2}} can be obtained by collecting Stamps from all 20 {{Plural|ST|Stamp Station|link}}. When {{Term|ST|Link}} shows the completed {{Term|ST|Stamp Book|link}} to the elderly {{Term|ST|Niko|link}}, he will be awarded the {{Term|ST|Swordsman's Scroll|display=Scroll}}, which enables him to perform the Great Spin Attack.
