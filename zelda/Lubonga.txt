{{Name|German|{{Term/Store|Lubonga||Series, TWoG}}}} is a character in {{TWoG}}.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= {{Exp|Praat met Lubonga om het doodskleed te krijgen.|Talk to Lubonga to get the Shroud.}}|magazine= CD Interactief|volume= 1|issue= 1|date= January 1994|publisher= HUB Uitgevers|page= 36}}</ref> She is a woman living in [[Aru Ainu]], [[Gamelon]].

==Biography==
Lubonga lives in the village of Aru Ainu, where she resides inside one of the village's two huts. She is the last of the settlement's remaining residents, after a [[Gleeok]] chased out the rest of the populace. In spite of this, she says that she is not afraid of the monsters that now plague the village.<ref>{{Cite|Everyone ran when the Gleeok came, but monsters no better than bothering me.|Lubonga|TWoG}}</ref> She spends her time knitting a [[Shroud]] in her hut, which she gives to [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] to defeat [[Gibdo#The Wand of Gamelon|Gibdo]] with, claiming that it will "scare the wraps off (him)."
