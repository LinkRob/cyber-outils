{{Term/Store|Jerrin||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Jerrin|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} researcher who lives in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} with her husband, {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}.<ref name="Robbie"/> The focus of her research is {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref name="Shrines">{{Cite|I'm an amateur scholar of ancient history. To be honest...I've been focusing a lot of my research on {{Color|BotW Blue|shrines}}.|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> She previously worked as a research assistant to {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}, but {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} had her sent to help {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} instead.<ref name="Memoir"/> After arriving at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}, she began to carry the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights|link}}, and she also reworked {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}'s design for the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|She carried the blue flame from Tumela Heights and even reworked and improved my designs for Cherry.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> Not long after, they ended up married.<ref>{{Cite|Her intellect, her spirit... In hindsight, is it any surprise that we eventually ended up married?|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref>

When the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}}'s redesign was completed, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} became jealous of {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}'s interactions with the machine, which {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} referred to as "Cherry" after his first love.<ref>{{Cite|I'd always go personally to fetch more blue flame from Tumlea Heights. I didn't realize our interactions hurt someone... That someone...was Jerrin. Perhaps it was due to her namesake, but my exchanges with Cherry hurt Jerrin.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}...that is, the ancient oven... is a machine I made to create ancient soldier gear. Isn't she adorable? Yes, hm? My first love... She shares the name of my first love. My wife...isn't as crazy about her as I am.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> Though she knew her feelings were illogical, she was in tears when she finally admitted them to {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}.<ref>{{Cite|She was in tears when she told me. She knew it made little sense, and yet she couldn't help how she felt.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> The pair decided to restore Cherry's mechanical way of speaking,<ref>{{Cite|We agreed to restore Cherry to her original, mechanical way of speaking. I took it upon myself to make the changes.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} instead began to refer to it as the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}}.<ref>{{Cite|From then on, I was careful to not refer to her as Cherry. Doing so would anger my wife. Instead, I called her... ancient oven.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes, when I call her {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}, my wife gets angry and throws things. That's why I call her the ancient oven now.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> Some time after this incident, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} gave birth to her and {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}'s son, {{Term|BotW|Granté|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Some time later, Jerrin gave birth to a baby boy, a beautiful young one who bore all the features of his mother. We decided to name him Granté, and together we passed the time as a happy family at Akkala Ancient Tech Lab.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} will stand by as {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} confronts him for trespassing.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you! What are you doing here?|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to talk to {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}}, she calls him suspicious and refuses to speak with him unless he proves that he knows {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm, you're pretty suspicious... If you ARE an acquaintance of Robbie's, you'll have to prove it to him.|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> Due to trade secrets, she is wary of sharing information with {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Who are you?! Unless you're an acquaintance of Robbie's, I can't talk to you. Trade secrets, ya know.|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has confirmed his identity to {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} will excitedly greet him with an imitation of {{Term|BotW|Purah}}.<ref>{{Cite|Aaaaand... Check it out! ...How was that? I figured I'd give an impression of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab}}'s {{Color|BotW Blue|director}}, Purah, a shot.|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> However, she becomes concerned that it was too much for their first meeting.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe it was a bit much for the first time meeting you, though?|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees it was too much, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} assures him that everything is fine.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] Link, don't worry about it! It's all OK!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells that it was not too much, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} is relieved that he is so friendly.<ref>{{Cite|[Not at all.] Aww, Link, I'm really glad you're such a friendly guy!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he needs from her.<ref>{{Cite|So what do you need from me?|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks who she is, she will properly introduce herself.<ref>{{Cite|[Who are you?] Heya! ♪I'm Jerrin!♪ Check it out!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> She explains that she is an amateur scholar with a focus on {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref name="Shrines"/> The topic reminds {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs to go to the left eye of {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link}}, as there is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} there.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh, that reminds me! Link, there's somewhere you need to go. Umm... The {{Color|BotW Red|left eye of Skull Lake}}! I know there's a {{Color|BotW Blue|shrine}} there, but it's a challenge to get to it...|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> She informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the left eye is higher up than the right eye, where {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} sets up the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|left eye}} of {{Color|BotW Red|Skull Lake}} is higher up. The right one is lower down. If you run into {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}, you're on the wrong eye!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> This initiates "{{Term|BotW|The Skull's Eye|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} will be able to tell that he visited {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake}}.<ref>{{Cite|So you went to the left eye of {{Color|BotW Blue|Skull Lake}}?|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} is confused, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} explains she has a magical {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} nose implant that enhances her sense of smell.<ref>{{Cite|... Oh, you don't have to tell me anything. My magical Sheikah nose implant gives me a supernatural sense of smell. Just one sniff tells me everything you did yesterday and what you at for dinner last night.|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s silence, however, she reveals that she is joking.<ref>{{Cite|... Just kidding.|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to be humorless, she surmises that his visit to {{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai Shrine|link}} did not go smoothly.<ref>{{Cite|But judging by your dormant sense of humor, I'm guessing things didn't go as smoothly as you had hoped.|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> She thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his report, but since the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}} is short on funding, she is unable to give him a reward.<ref>{{Cite|In any case, thanks for the report. I wish I could give you a reward, but we're short on funding as it is. That's life in Hyrule for ya!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can also ask {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} about {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}, who she explains that he is a researcher of ancient civilizations who studied {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|[About Robbie...] Robbie is one of the researchers of ancient civilizations. He was studying {{Color|BotW Blue|Guardians}} at {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} 100 years ago.|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> She also reveals that she and {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} are married.<ref name="Robbie"/> This shocks {{Term|BotW|Link}}, so {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} happily shares that they run the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}} and have a son together.<ref>{{Cite|[You're married?] Yes, indeed! Together, we run the {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}. I've got a son, as well!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref>

After hearing about {{Term|BotW|Granté}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} about him.<ref name="Son"/> {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has seen him during his travels.<ref>{{Cite|Have you seen him in your travels?|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that he has, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} will be relieved.<ref>{{Cite|Ooh! Well that's good to hear!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> She stills considers {{Term|BotW|Granté}} to be a work in progress, and once he is done studying {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}, she hopes he will take over her own research.<ref>{{Cite|Granté is still a...work in progress. Once he's done studying armor, I'm hoping to convince him to take over my research.|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that he has not, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} asks him to be nice to him if they ever meet.<ref>{{Cite|[I haven't seen him.] Really... Well, if you see him, be nice to him, OK?|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> In subsequent conversations, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} changes his answer about whether or not he has met {{Term|BotW|Granté}}, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} will notice the discrepency.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|You said, "I saw him," earlier, though...}}}}|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|You said, "I haven't seen him," earlier, though...}}}}|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref>
