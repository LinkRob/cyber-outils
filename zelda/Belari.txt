{{Term/Store|Belari||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 270"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Belari}} is a {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}} that lives in a {{Term|TMC|House|link}} just outside of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}. After completing the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} and obtaining the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}}, he will give {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} a {{Term|TMC|Bomb Bag|link}} with {{Plural|TMC|Bomb|link}} when {{Term|TMC|Link}} speaks to him in his {{Term|TMC|House}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am {{Color|TMC Green|Belari}}, researcher of antiquities. I am also, well, a bit of an inventor. You know, you won't be able to get back to town, due to all the debris... Here, I have something that might come in handy.|Belari|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, he can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Belari}},<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Let's give it a try!|Belari|TMC}}</ref> resulting in a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} appearing in the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|link}} that contains a {{Term|TMC|Big Bomb Bag|link}}.

After another {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion}} is performed with {{Term|TMC|Gentari|link}}, the elder of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can return to {{Term|TMC|Belari}}'s {{Term|TMC|House}} to receive the {{Plural|TMC|Remote Bomb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|At last, it's complete! The {{Color|TMC Red|Remote Bomb}}! It blows up whenever you want!|Belari|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} can speak to {{Term|TMC|Belari}} again at any time to switch back to normal {{Plural|TMC|Bomb}}.<ref>{{Cite|You want to use normal {{Color|TMC Red|bombs}} again? Just trade back those {{Color|TMC Red|Remote Bombs}}.|Belari|TMC}}</ref>
