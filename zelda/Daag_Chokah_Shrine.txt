The {{Term/Store|Daag Chokah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Daag Chokah Shrine}}<br/>Daag Chokah's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Daag Chokah's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Daag Chokah Shrine is located in the northwest corner of the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must first talk to {{Term|BotW|Tasho|link}} at the western entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, who will give Link the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Lost Pilgrimage|link}}", asking Link to follow his little brother {{Term|BotW|Oaki|link}} to the shrine to ensure his safety.<ref>{{Cite|Could you please follow Oaki without being seen? I want to be sure nothing bad happens...|Tasho|BotW}}</ref>

After recieving the Quest, {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} will set off towards the Shrine. While following {{Term|BotW|Oaki}}, Link must avoid being seen by him as {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} will head back and start again if he knows Link is following him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|KYAAH! A ghost!}}...Wait, you're not a ghost! Were you following me? I said I could do this by myself! Ugh, and now I'm not by myself... I'll have to do it all over again.|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> When Oaki reaches the Shrine without noticing Link, he will celebrate completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Yay! I made it! I finished the {{Color|BotW Red|trial}}!}}|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} will then see Link before boasting about his bravery for completing the trial by himself and telling Link that [[The Great Deku Tree]] said there was a Monk's blessing in the Shrine.<ref>{{Cite|Wha? Who's there? Hey! Are you that hero guy? You know—Mr. Hero? Guess what, Mr. Hero! I did the trial all by myself! Aren't I brave? Oh, yeah! You're probably here for the shrine. The Great Deku Tree said that the monk's blessing is waiting in the shrine ahead! Why don't you go take a look?|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> After talking to {{Term|BotW|Oaki}}, the Shrine Quest is completed.

If Link talks to {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} after {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Tasho}}, {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} and {{Term|BotW|Tasho}} will tell Link that he can reach the Shrine again by following the flowers.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to go back to the shrine, just follow the flowers. They won't let you down.|Oaki|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|That's right! If you follow the flowers, you won't get lost!|Tasho|BotW}}</ref>

It is possible to reach the Shrine's location without starting the Shrine Quest but Link will not be able to access the Shrine and will find an empty patch of land instead. However, neaking past {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} after beginning the Shrine Quest will allow Link access the Shrine without completing the Quest.

As Link enters the Shrine, he is greeted by the Shrine's {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Daag Chokah|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the Shrine is proof of Link's worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Daag Chokah. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Daag Chokah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Daag Chokah Shrine features no puzzles, simply a direct path to Daag Chokah. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Core|link}}. As Link approaches Daag Chokah, the Monk will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the Shrine,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Daag Chokah|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Daag Chokah|BotW}}</ref>
