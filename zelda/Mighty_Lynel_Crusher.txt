{{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Crusher}} are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, they are utilized by {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}} instead. They were made by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}} and reinforced to increase their durability and damage.<ref name="Inventory"/> Their origin makes them part of the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|linl}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Their weight is capable of crushing both their opponents and their {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Crusher}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. However, they do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
