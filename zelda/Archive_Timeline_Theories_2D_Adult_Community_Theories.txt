The '''"[[Archive:Timeline Theories/Split Timeline Disciplines#2D Adult Order|2D Adult]]"''' Timeline is a discipline of the Split Timeline that proposes that all of the older (2D) titles are to be placed in the Adult Timeline, along with {{TWW}} and {{PH}}, and that the Child Timeline serves as a separate story apart from the older games and thus completely free to continue into its own chronology. Below is a listing of 2D Adult theories created by the Zelda community at large.

==[[:User:Alter|Alter's]] Trident Timeline Theory==

'''Trident Theory Timeline:''' 
:::::::{{MM}} - {{TP}}

:::::::{{FPTRR}}/{{TWW}}/{{PH}} - {{ST}} - {{FSA}} - {{ALttP}}/{{LA}}/{{OoA}}/{{OoS}} - {{TLoZ}}/{{TAoL}}/[[Valiant Comics|Valiant Comic Series]]

*'''The''' Trident Theory is something I, Alter, came up with myself, and I'm unsure as to whether or not someone else has had any similar ideas. The basic idea of this theory is that [[Ganon]] was once a Human/Gerudo. Once he stole the Trident in FSA, he was transformed into a "demon". From this point on, he was unable to ever return to his human form, and is usually seen with the Trident from then on. 

*Vaati was sealed after the events in TMC, and was released during in FS. He was sealed again, only to be released once again in FSA, where Ganon obtains his Trident.

*The Sealing War is made up of the events in OoT, and the entire OoT arc takes place in between FS and FSA.

*The baby [[Great Deku Tree|Deku Tree]] that [[Tingle]] helped in TRR grows up. The forest then becomes known as [[Forest Haven]]. Link later ventures there during the events of TWW. 

*When Ganon broke out of his seal in TWW's backstory, Hyrule was flooded, explaining why there are islands in TRR, TWW, and PH. The land then drains, likely due to the Deku Tree's success. A new Hyrule is then formed by Link and Tetra in ST. After Ganon obtains the Trident in FSA, he is defeated in ALttP. Link then travels across the now much smaller sea to the lands of Koholint, Holodrum, and Labrynna.

*The game manual for LA states that Link went on a search for enlightenment after defeating Ganon. This makes sense, as ALttP had been its predecessor, and the developers wanted to continue the story. After leaving Koholint Island, and returning to Hyrule, he is warped to the lands of Labrynna or Holodrum, and then sailed to the other land after completing his journey.

:*Note that it is also possible that his "search for enlightenment" took him to Labrynna/Holodrum, and after those adventures, he sailed back to Hyrule, but not before getting shipwrecked on Koholint Island. It's very vague as to what occurred on his quest. However, this idea is not as likely, as it is implied that he set sail (i.e. not warped by the [[Triforce]]), and had just destroyed Ganon (i.e. not the "fake" Ganon).

*Ganon returns to the Kingdom of Hyrule in TLoZ and TAoL. Link and Zelda continue their fight against him throughout the [[Valiant Comics]] as well.

I would love to be able to condense this into a normal two-line theory, but it doesn't work out. We know that TP is along the child line, and TWW along the adult. However, Ganon appears in three distinct forms: fully human in TWW, tainted by twilight (with its powers) in TP, and a half-boar all the time in the 2D games, and each of these forms has a separate ultimate fate. The only way to condense this any further is to say that all the games are just stories, and the one who was telling TWW decided to say that Ganon was petrified instead of just returning to where he came from. I like this interpretation&mdash;([[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|ALttP]]) - [[The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap|TMC]] - ([[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|ALttP]]) - [[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords|FS]] - [[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|FSA]] - ([[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|ALttP]]) - [[The Legend of Zelda|TLoZ]] / [[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|TAoL]] / OoX / [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|LA]] - [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|TWW]] / [[The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass|PH]] - [[The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks|ST]]&mdash; but it definitely messes with how we understand the rules, and completely ignores [[Timeline Glossary#Occam's Razor|Occam's Razor]].


Basically, this theory states that the timeline split once at the end of OoT and once while Ganon was sealed in the Dark World. The child arc is rather straightforward: Link travels to Termina, and one of his descendants kills Ganon after his attempt to use Zant to take over. In the adult arc, though, the split is based on whether or not Ganon is changed by the dark world. If he remains a gerudo, he eventually breaks free on his own and indirectly initiates the flooding of Hyrule and his becoming a statue. Otherwise, he tries to use Vaati, and evidently brings about his own failure; Link is around to send him back to the dark realm, whereas he was largely unopposed just before the flood. The third time he attempts to break free, in TLoZ, he ''is'' killed (we assume that he managed to escape before, as there would otherwise be a huge number of alternate timelines), and the remaining games in the progression he only appears in revival attempts. The Link that stops the schemes in OoX is definitely the one who killed Ganon initially, and so that leads us to the conclusion that he is quite the traveler, thus leading to the conclusion that he is the one "journeying for enlightenment," and therefore the Link in LA.

==Ollathir's "Growing Hyrule" Timeline Theory (ZU)==

Adult: SS - OoT - TAoL BS - TWW/PH - TAoL BS - OoX & TMC BS - TMC - FS/FSA - ALttP/LA -TLoZ/TAoL<br />
Child: SS - OoT/MM - TP

NOTE: This timeline is based on Erimgard's timeline, which I think is improved by moving TLoZ and TAoL to after ALttP and LA. The following points are my explanation of why I believe so.<br />

*In TWW, the Deku Tree says that he wishes to create more land by planting trees and eventually connect the islands on the Great Sea. In FPTRR, we see land growing, and in OoA, there is more land in the present than there is in the past. All these things point to land growing over time.
*More land is seen in TAoL than in any other game. Based on TWW, FPTRR, and OoA, this leads us to assume an end-of-AT placement for TAoL, along with TLoZ.
*In FSA, which was the next game made after TWW, the land to the north of Hyrule on the TAoL map is not there. This directly points to TAoL, and TLoZ, being after FSA, and logically, after ALttP.
*The Triforce being in Hyrule Castle before TLoZ is explained by the "vanishing" of the DW at the end of ALttP. Since the Pyramid of Light was the parallel to Hyrule Castle, the Triforce would end up in Hyrule Castle when the DW vanished.
*Ganon in TLoZ can be explained by a resurrection like in OoX.
*The TAoL BS takes place shortly after the founding of New Hyrule, in accordance with the "first generation Zelda" quote in the4 TAoL manual.
*The TMC BS takes place in Hyrule during OoX. This explains Link's absence from the TMC BS. Also, the darkness in Hyrule during the TMC BS is similar to the darkness in Labrynna and Holodrum during OoX. Lastly, TMC has a lot of connections to OoX, (books in library, Din and Nayru) so it works out well to have the TMC BS taking place during OoX.

SUMMARY: With no sure explanation for Ganon in TLoZ in Erimgard's timeline, it is beneficial to move TLoZ and TAoL to after ALttP and LA, making the explanation for Ganon in FSA available for use to explain TLoZ Ganon. The only thing that is sacrificed in doing so is geography.<br />

NOTE: Ollathir no longer follows this theory because he now believes that either TMC and FSA show a loss of land or that FSA's map is not Canon like Pinceove does.
