"{{Term/Store|Rushroom Rush!||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Rushroom Rush!}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Pirou|link}}, who asks if he has any {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Buddy... Got any {{Color|BotW Blue|rushrooms}}?|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks him what is wrong, {{Term|BotW|Pirou}} explains that he is tired of moving at a normal speed.<ref>{{Cite|[What's the matter?] See, I... I'm tired. Tired of going normal speed, I mean...|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> He started eating {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}} when he was five and has had them every day since.<ref>{{Cite|I first tried {{Color|BotW Blue|rushrooms}} when I was five years old... And come to think of it, I ate them every day since without fail...|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> They used to help him win races,<ref>{{Cite|Back in the day, I'd win every race I ran thanks to those things...|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> but years later, he is unable to get around without them.<ref>{{Cite|Cut to 55 years later... Now I can barely drag these bones off the ground without those {{Color|BotW Blue|rushrooms}}.|Pirou|BotW}}</ref>

Due to his old age, {{Term|BotW|Pirou}} is unable to scale cliffs to pick {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}} for himself.<ref>{{Cite|But no matter how many I eat to fortify myself... I'm just too old to get up the cliffs to pick {{Color|BotW Blue|rushrooms}} where they grow...|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he informs more capable people that he is looking for them.<ref>{{Cite|That's why I'm letting all able-bodied sorts know that I'm in the market for 'em.|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> He offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}} in exchange for 55 {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}} and encourages him to come back and do business.<ref>{{Cite|I'll give a {{Color|BotW Red|diamond}} for {{Color|BotW Blue|55 rushrooms}}. That's worth the effort, right? If you want to do business, just come talk to me. I'm always here.|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}} are most common in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}, though they can be found on the edges of many cliffs throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link|display=Hyrule}}. The small spire in front of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}} grows many {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}} near its top. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} obtains 55 {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Pirou}}.<ref>{{Cite|Heyyy... I thought you'd come back...|Pirou|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Pirou}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has brought the {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}}.<ref>{{Cite|You bring the stuff?|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms that he has and hands them over, {{Term|BotW|Pirou}} will count them to ensure he has the right amount.<ref>{{Cite|[I brought 'em.] {{Small|5... 15... 25... 35... 55...}} Heh, yep, they're all here...|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> Once he confirms there are 55 {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}}, he informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he left the {{Term|BotW|Diamond}} he promised behind the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I left that rock I promised you {{Color|BotW Blue|behind the stable}}.|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> He does not understand why someone would go through so much trouble for a rock,<ref>{{Cite|Beats me why anyone would go to such trouble for a little ol' rock...|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> as he would rather have {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'd rather have {{Color|BotW Blue|rushrooms}} any day.|Pirou|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever has 55 {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom}} in the future, {{Term|BotW|Pirou}} offers to buy them for twice their regular price.<ref>{{Cite|Heh. Come back when you get more. I'm always here. I can't give you any more diamonds, but I CAN offer twice the market value for 'em. Just remember that I won't deal in any volume other than {{Color|BotW Blue|55}} at a time.|Pirou|BotW}}</ref> Once he is done talking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} can collect his reward from the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}.
