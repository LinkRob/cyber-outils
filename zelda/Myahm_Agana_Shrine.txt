The {{Term/Store|Myahm Agana Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Myahm Agana Shrine}}<br/>Myahm Agana Apparatus}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Myahm Agana Apparatus''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Myahm Agana Shrine}} is located within {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Myahm Agana|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Myahm Agana. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Myahm Agana|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Inside {{Term|BotW|Myahm Agana Shrine}}, there is a {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle|link}} that controls a floating platform with a maze. In this maze is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must safely direct through the maze and to the edge of the platform. From here, he will be able to drop it into a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}. Once the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}} has been activated, the portcullis leading to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} will open.

Alternately, the platform can be flipped over, and the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} can be rolled over its flat bottom directly to the target point.<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4_Uw2wv67P4 |site= youtube.com |title= The Legend of Zelda Breath of the Wild Myahm Agana Shrine Cheat!}}</ref> The apparatus can even be skipped entirely through precise use of {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}} to enter the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}'s chamber through its open ceiling.<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZteCNx8e-Y |site= youtube.com |title= Zelda Breath of the Wild Myahm Agana Apparatus Shrine Puzzle Skip}}</ref>

Whatever method {{Term|BotW|Link}} uses to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, {{Term|BotW|Myahm Agana}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Myahm Agana|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Myahm Agana|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Phrenic Bow|link}} sits inside the platform's maze. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can access it by tilting the platform and then {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link|display=Paragliding}} into the maze.
