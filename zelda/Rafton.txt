{{Term/Store|Rafton||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254{{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|OoA|Rafton}} is a raft maker in {{Term|OoA|Labrynna|link}}'s past age. He lives in a small {{Term|OoA|Rafton's House|link|display=House}} south of the [[Black Tower]] in [[Lynna Village]] and builds a [[Raft]] for [[Link]] to sail to [[Crescent Island]].

Rafton tells Link that in order to cross the sea safely, the raft must be bound with a rope that does not decay in water.<ref>{{Cite|If I only had rope that won't decay in water, I could make my raft.|Rafton|OoA}}</ref> [[Cheval]], who is working on such a rope, will not finish until the future.<ref>{{Cite|There's a fellow named Cheval on the east edge of town who is working on such rope, but I don't think he's done yet.|Rafton|OoA}}</ref> As such, Link must first get the [[Cheval Rope]] hidden inside [[Cheval's Tomb]] in the [[Yoll Graveyard]] in the present. Rafton then tells Link that he needs an [[Island Chart]] in order to navigate the sea,<ref>{{Cite|But you'll need a chart if you want to go out to sea. Going without a chart is an invitation to disaster.|Rafton|OoA}}</ref> which can be obtained from [[Tingle]].<ref>{{Cite| As a sign of our friendship, Tingle will give you his chart that shows the way to the secret island!|Tingle|OoA}}</ref>

After he makes a raft for Link, Rafton plans on entering a boating contest, but he needs an oar. As part of the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest|link}}, Link gives him the [[Magic Oar]] in exchange for the [[Sea Ukulele]].<ref>{{Cite|In exchange... I know! How's this? Lazily playing guitar while gazing at the moon is so nice.|Rafton|OoA}}</ref> The oar then enables Rafton to enter himself into the raft race.<ref>{{Cite|I'm thinking of entering a raft race, but I haven't been able to find a decent oar to race with..|Rafton|OoA}}</ref>
