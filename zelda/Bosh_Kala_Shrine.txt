The {{Term/Store|Bosh Kala Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Bosh Kala Shrine}}<br/>The Wind Guides You}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''The Wind Guides You''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Bosh Kala Shrine}} is located between the {{Term|BotW|East Post Ruins|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Proxim Bridge|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters, he is greeted by {{Term|BotW|Bosh Kala|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} who designed the eponymous {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Bosh Kala. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Bosh Kala|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Bosh Kala Shrine}} centers around using the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to glide along {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}} currents, which are generated by the four fans throughout the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. To make use of them, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must stand in front of these currents and activate his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}}. 

{{Term|BotW|Bosh Kala}} awaits on a {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} on a ledge at the far end of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Bosh Kala}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Bosh Kala|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Bosh Kala}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Bosh Kala|BotW}}</ref>

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found on a ledge near the entrance to {{Term|BotW|Bosh Kala Shrine}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can access it by {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|display=Paragliding}} from the fan on his left side, at which point he can open it to obtain an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}}. Another stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} can be found on a ledge across from the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. After landing on the platform that the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}} is located on, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|display=Paraglide}} down to the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, inside which he can find a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Claymore|link}}.
