==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Elevator Platform}} are circular, floating platforms located in {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. They are made using {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} from 10,000 years before {{BotW}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use {{Plural|BotW|Elevator Platform}} to both enter and leave {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.

==Trivia==
* The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah (Language)|link}} text on {{Plural|BotW|Elevator Platform}} reads "MOVING PLATE".
