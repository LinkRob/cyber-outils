The {{Term/Store|Blue Holy Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Blue Holy Ring<br/>No damage from Zora's fire|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Blue Holy Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be obtained by scoring 350 points at the [[Shooting Gallery]] in [[Lynna Village]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it negates all damage from a [[River Zora|Zora]]'s fire.<ref name="Description"/> The Blue Holy Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
