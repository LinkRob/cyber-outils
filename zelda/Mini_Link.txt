{{Name|Japanese|{{Term/Store|Mini Link||Series, TMC}}}} is a transformation of {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|game=TMC |publisher=Shogakukan |page=4 }}</ref> Achieved by using a {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|}} transforms into a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized version of his normal Hylian form.

==Transformation==
{{Term|TMC|Link}} meets {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} within the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}} after being sent by {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}} to repair the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You see, I, too, am on a quest to break a curse of Vaati's. And you say that reforging the {{Color|TMC Red|sacred blade}} can break his curse, eh? Well, then you have found yourself a companion, my boy! My name is {{Color|TMC Green|Ezlo}}. It's a pleasure to make your acquaintance.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> With {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}}'s help, {{Term|TMC|Link}} is able to use {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal}} throughout {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} to shrink into {{Term|TMC|Mini Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Look at that. At first glance, it appears to be a mere stump, yes? No! That stump is a {{Color|TMC Blue|portal}} used by people long ago to adjust their size! With my help, you can use it to {{Color|TMC Blue|shrink down to Minish size}}.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>
