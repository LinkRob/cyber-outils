"{{Term/Store|The Ceremonial Song||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Ceremonial Song}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Zora's Domain}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, he will find {{Term|BotW|Laruta|link}} singing in front of {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square|link|display=Square}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪A light scale...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> She continues to sing once {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her,<ref>{{Cite|♪La dee dooo!♪<br/>♪A gift from the sky...<br/>a {{Color|BotW Red|scale of light}}...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> but she is shocked when she recognizes him as the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who helped {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You're the Hylian who helped our Prince Sidon! You're sooo handsome!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about her singing, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} reveals that she was singing a song from the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival|link}} and asks if he would like to hear it.<ref>{{Cite|[That song?] I just looooove singing! I even remember the song from the Champion Festival! ...Do you want to hear it?|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to listen, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} will sing the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song|link}} for him.<ref>{{Cite|[I want to hear it.] OK! I'm gonna sing it now! The Champion Festival Song!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics describe a scale of light splitting the feet at {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls|link}}, which will reveal a trial.<ref>{{Cite|♪A gift from the sky...<br/>a {{Color|BotW Red|scale of light}}...♪<br/>♪Splits the feet...<br/>of a {{Color|BotW Red|Veiled Falls}} sight...♪<br/>♪Your {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} awaits...<br/>It's glowing bright...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref>

While she does not understand the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song}}, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} plans on singing it until the next {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival}}.<ref>{{Cite|I don't really get what the song's about, but I'm gonna sing it at the next Champion Festival!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> Even though {{Term|BotW|Trello|link}} is in charge of the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival|display=Festival}}, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} has noticed that he has not done anything to prepare yet.<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Trello is supposed to get the festival together, but he hasn't done anything for it yet.|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> She wonders if he forgot that he was supposed to do so.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder what's wrong with him... Did he forget that's his job?|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} begins.

{{Term|BotW|Trello}} can be found pacing on the western bridge out of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}. He reveals that he needs to devise a plan to find the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now then... I must devise a plan to find the {{Color|BotW Red|ceremonial trident}}...|Trello|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks, {{Term|BotW|Trello}} tells him that it is used in the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival}} to honor {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} in place of the actual {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Ceremonial trident?] The {{Color|BotW Red|ceremonial trident}} is used in a festival to honor Champion Mipha... The Champion Festival's {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}... Well, it's a fake.|Trello|BotW}}</ref> On the way to have the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} examined by {{Term|BotW|Dento|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Trello}} accidentally dropped it off the bridge.<ref>{{Cite|I planed to have that {{Color|BotW Red|ceremonial trident}} examined by Dento, but on the way there, I dropped it off the bridge.|Trello|BotW}}</ref> As it is a fake, {{Term|BotW|Trello}} plans on asking {{Term|BotW|Dento}} to make a new one for him.<ref>{{Cite|It doesn't really matter. It's a fake, after all... I'll have to ask Dento to make a new one.|Trello|BotW}}</ref> Should someone find the missing {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}}, {{Term|BotW|Trello}} will allow them to keep it.<ref>{{Cite|If someone happens to pick it up, they can just keep it.|Trello|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} by walking out onto the {{Term|BotW|Water|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}}. Once he is in range, he will be able to lift it out with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. With the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can jump off the top of the {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls}} and strike the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Water}}, causing {{Term|BotW|Dagah Keek Shrine|link}} to rise from the ground nearby,<ref>{{Cite|You thrust the spear that Trello dropped into the pedestal hidden in Veiled Falls, causing an ancient shrine to appear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
