'''Taburi''' is a character in the {{MM|Himekawa}}. They never makes a visual appearance, but are mentioned twice. The first time, Taburi is referred to as belonging to "House Taburi," representative of a larger familial clan under the same name. The second time they are referred to as "Lord Taburi". This suggests that Taburi and their family are ruling lords. In both occasions, their name is used in a context where they mention that they are the reason to why [[Link]] is temporarily tutoring the soldiers there.
