{{Term/Store|Dodoh's High Dive||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a {{Term|SS|Mini-Game|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dodoh's High Dive|Map|SSHD}}</ref>

==Rules==
To begin the {{Term|SS|Mini-Game|link}}, {{Term|SS|Link|link}} must jump into a {{Term|SS|Cannon|link}} which will launch him straight up.<ref>{{Cite|First thing you need to know is you'll be shot out of a CANNON! You'll fly waaaaay up there.|Dodoh|SS}}</ref> At this point, the island begins to rotate and {{Term|SS|Link}} finds himself free-falling. The main objective is to land on a colored space on the rotating board beneath. Each color corresponds to a different {{Term|SS|Rupee}} value, and there is also a {{Term|SS|Loser Space|link}} that will make {{Term|SS|Link}} lose {{Plural|SS|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your goal is to dive onto a cheery, colorful rotating board. Land on a happy, little colored space, and you'll win Rupees based on the color.|Dodoh|SS}}</ref> To increase his profits, {{Term|SS|Link}} can pass through {{Plural|SS|Fortune Ring|link}} while falling. Each ring increases the prize multiplier and slows down the rotating board.<ref>{{Cite|If you can pass through one of those Fortune Rings, you'll increase the awesome Rupee score multiplier! OOOOOoooh! And the more rings you pass through, the slower the wheel will spin, making it easier to land on your target and WIN!|Dodoh|SS}}</ref> At the same time, {{Term|SS|Link}} must avoid giant {{Plural|SS|Dodoh Ball|link}} which reset the multiplier to &times;1.<ref>{{Cite|But hitting one of those Dodoh Balls will reset your multiplier and turn your smile upside down!|Dodoh|SS}}</ref> The multiplier affects all spaces except the gray space. Failing to land on the wheel is treated as if Link landed on a brown space, including if he falls off the platform, however landing on a lighting bulb on the side of the wheel still counts as the space above it.
