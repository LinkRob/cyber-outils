The {{Term/Store|Horon Village Shop||Series}} is a shop located in [[Horon Village]] in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E|page=110}}</ref> Run by [[Stockwell]], the main floor of the shop sells commodities such as [[Bomb]]s and [[Heart]]s. A stairwell behind the counter leads to a basement floor where Stockwell sells special goods and operates the [[Treasure Chest Game]]. This area is reserved exclusively for [[Member's Card|Card]]-holding members.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry, sir. You cannot enter here without a Member's Card. Are you curious? What's back there is a secret! Heh heh heh!|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref>

==Main Floor==
The main floor of the shop sells bombs, hearts, and the highest-grade [[Shield]] Link currently has. This is, in fact, where the young hero can purchase his first shield. However, in a non-[[Linked Game]], the shopkeeper will not sell anything until Link has obtained the [[Wooden Sword]] from the [[Hero's Cave]]. In a Linked Game, the shop will also sell a [[Gasha Seed]], and the shield will only appear after buying it. As usual, Link cannot purchase a shield if he already has one in his [[Inventory]].<ref>{{Cite|You have [a shield]. One more won't help.|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref> In addition, the [[Strange Flute]] can be purchased here after completing [[Snake's Remains]]. Buying it here will lead to the acquisition of [[Moosh's Flute]] later on, henceforth making [[Moosh]] Link's partner.<ref name="Guide 17">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 17}}</ref> The flute disappears from the shop if Link leaves for [[Spool Swamp]] without it or acquires it at the [[Subrosian Dance Hall]] instead.

==Basement Floor==
[[File:Oracle Of Seasons - Horon Village Shop Basement.png|thumb|right|In the basement of Horon Village Shop]]
Once Link obtains the [[Member's Card]] from [[Subrosia Market]], Stockwell grants him access to the shop's basement where special, expensive items are sold.<ref name="Guide 17"/> These include two more Gasha Seeds, a bigger [[Seed Satchel]], and, most importantly, the [[Treasure Map]], which is needed to find the four [[Jewel]]s that open the way to [[Tarm Ruins]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 40}}</ref>

===Treasure Chest Game===
{{Main|Treasure Chest Game}}
Once all the items on the basement floor are bought, Stockwell closes shop.<ref>{{Cite|You've bought it all, so I'm closing shop.|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref> He then decides to enter a new line of business: from then on, he will start to operate the [[Treasure Chest Game]] in the basement instead.<ref>{{Cite|We ran out of goods, so we're in a new line of business now.|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref> In this game, the player pays 10 Rupees to choose between two [[Treasure Chest]]s: one is empty, while the other contains a large Rupee-like gem. Guessing the correct chest three times in a row garners a prize in the form of a [[Magic Ring]].<ref>{{Cite|One out of two chests will have treasure. You'll get a nice gift if you can open the right chest three times in a row! Each try costs 10 Rupees.|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Your gift is a ring, but what kind of ring it is is a secret.|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref> However, the player can choose to guess a fourth time and go "double or nothing" to obtain a better ring.<ref>{{Cite|Congratulations! OK! Double or nothing. If you do it again, you'll get a special gift!|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref> Going for yet another round after that earns the winner an even greater ring.<ref>{{Cite|Jackpot! Congratulations! This is your final chance! If you get this right, you'll get an awesome gift!|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* In order for Link to purchase items, he needs to pick up the item and speak with Stockwell whom is across the counter. If Link tries to leave the shop without paying for his item, Stockwell will run over and push Link away from the doorway.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Don't just take things! Thief! Give that back right now!|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref> After scolding Link, he will return to his spot at the cash register. Link will be stopped from leaving the store with an item in hand every time he approaches the doorway.
