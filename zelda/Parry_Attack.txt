{{Term/Store|Parry Attack||Series, TWWHD}} is a powerful [[Sword]] technique in {{TWW}}.<ref name="Orca">{{Cite|Next is harder! This is a {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|parry attack}}. {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Target}} with {{Icon|TWWHD ZL}} until you see {{Icon|TWWHD Flashing A}}, and then quickly press {{Icon|TWWHD A}} to perform a defensive strike!|Orca|TWWHD}}</ref>

== Uses ==
Parry Attacks can be performed by targeting an enemy and then hitting the [[Controller Buttons|A Button]] when its icon and [[Link]]'s [[Sword]] flashes green as the enemy is about to perform an attack, lasting only a split-second.<ref name="Orca"/> This will make the Hero of Winds perform an automatic counterstrike while evading his foe's attack. They cannot be performed while raising the [[Shield]]. Although usually used for regular [[Enemies in The Wind Waker|enemies]], it is effective on [[Bosses in The Wind Waker|bosses]] as well.
[[File:TWW Parry Attack gif.gif|thumb|262x262px|The Parry Attack in action, as a Darknut attacks Link after dodging his [[Jump Slash|Jump Attack]]]]
Parry Attacks depend on the enemy and the attack they are attempting to use. When evading a vertical strike, Link rolls sideways to reach the enemy's back, he then jumps and slashes with his Sword. When evading a horizontal strike, Link leaps over the enemy while slashing its head. These techniques are particularly effective against [[Darknut]]s, as they can remove their protective armor and expose them to other attacks. When fighting against [[Magtail]]s and [[Floor Master]]s, Link can leap into the air and perform a [[Down Thrust]] at their weak point.  

A special Parry Attack can be performed at the end of the final battle against [[Ganondorf]] for the finishing blow, in which Link swiftly rolls out of the way to avoid the main antagonist's attack (dropping his [[Mirror Shield]] on the process), slices Ganondorf from behind, and then leaps into the air with a [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/WW_New/WW_Link_FinalBlow.wav unique battle cry] to deal a final strike directly to his forehead with a mighty Down Thrust, which would ultimately turn him into stone and killing him. 

== Trivia ==
* The Parry Attack, like the [[Jump Strike]], is an upgraded variant of the [[Jump Slash|Jump Attack]] technique.
* Three of the [[Hidden Skill]]s featured in {{TP}} bear a strong resemblance to Link's Parry Attacks. These are the [[Ending Blow]], the [[Back Slice]], and the [[Helm Splitter]]. Ironically, the Ending Blow is used in a nearly identical manner as the ''The Wind Waker''<nowiki/>'s special Parry Attack on Ganondorf for the final blow, but instead impales him on his gaping wound chest.
* The final blow on Ganondorf is essentially two Parry Attack strikes combined into one; it first uses the jump slice followed by the downward thrust attack.
