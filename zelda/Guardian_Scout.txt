{{Term/Store|Guardian Scout|Guardian Scouts|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Guardian Scout I}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> and {{HWAoC}}{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}} are smaller variants of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}} that can be found within {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}. They come in four different forms. Depending on the form, these can be equipped with different weapons of the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype. They can also use their laser to repeatedly burn the ground surrounding them, sending {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} back if struck. However, this creates an updraft that allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to reach them. Additionally, when near death, they will charge a stronger laser attack that cannot be interrupted. {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}} are susceptible to elemental attacks, becoming stunned when electrified, catching on fire when exposed to it and freezing entirely when attacked with ice.

==={{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I}}===
{{Main|Guardian Scout I}}
The weakest form of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}}, the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I}} walks around and fires lasers, and does not carry any {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}. They can be found in {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} and {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.
====Drops====
A {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I}} drops between 1 and 2 of:
{{BotWDropTable | Ancient Screw | 60 | Ancient Spring | 40 }}

==={{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}}===
{{Main|Guardian Scout II}}
The {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}} fires lasers and carries one {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}. They can be found in Minor Test of Strength {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} and {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}. A unique, translucent blue {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}} appears in the {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg Shrine|link}}.
====Drops====
A {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}} drops exactly 2 of:
{{BotWDropTable | Ancient Screw | 40 | Ancient Spring | 30 | Ancient Gear | 20 | Ancient Shaft | 10 }}

==={{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III}}===
{{Main|Guardian Scout III}}
The {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III}} fires lasers and carries two pieces of {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} equipment, one of which can be a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Shield|link}}. It can be found in Modest Test of Strength shrines.
====Drops====
A {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III}} drops exactly 1 of:
{{BotWDropTable | Ancient Gear | 40 | Ancient Shaft | 40 | Ancient Core | 20 }}
as well as between 3 and 4 of:
{{BotWDropTable | Ancient Screw | 30 | Ancient Spring | 30 | Ancient Gear | 20 | Ancient Shaft | 20 }}

==={{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV}}===
{{Main|Guardian Scout IV}}
The strongest form of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}}, the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV}} fires lasers, carries three pieces of {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} equipment and has the most health out of all the Scouts. It can be found in Major Test of Strength shrines.
====Drops====
A {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV}} drops exactly 1 of:
{{BotWDropTable | Ancient Core | 50 | Ancient Shaft | 30 | Ancient Gear | 20 }}
as well as between 4 and 5 of:
{{BotWDropTable | Ancient Gear | 30 | Ancient Shaft | 30 | Ancient Screw | 20 | Ancient Spring | 20 }}
