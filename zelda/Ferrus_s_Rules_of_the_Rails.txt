TITLE : Ferrus's Rules of the Rails

'''{{Name|Fan|Ferrus's Rules of the Rails}}''' are a set of rules that instruct {{Term|ST|Link|link}} on how to properly drive a {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} while carrying passengers in {{ST}}. These rules are first conveyed orally by {{Term|ST|Ferrus|link}} near the {{Term|ST|Station|link}} at the {{Term|ST|Bridge Worker's Home|link}}, before {{Term|ST|Link}} embarks with his first passenger, {{Term|ST|Kenzo|link}}. These rules can be found written on papers tacked on the walls of {{Term|ST|Ferrus}}'s home at {{Term|ST|Wellspring Station|link}}.

===Rule One of the Rails===
{{Quote|If you see a whistle sign, pull your train whistle before you pass the sign. And put some real feeling into it!|Ferrus}}

===Rule Two of the Rails===
{{Quote|When you see a slow-down sign, check your speed and drive in the lower gear. When you've passed the slow-down sign, shift back into high gear as before. Do this smoothly. It should be like second nature.|Ferrus}}

===Rule Three of the Rails===
{{Quote|When pulling into a station, don't stop beyond the stop zone. You don't want your passengers to panic and think you forgot their stop. That would be inexcusable! Give them a first-class experience.|Ferrus}}

===Rule Four of the Rails===
{{Quote|You'll scare and endanger your passengers if you reverse suddenly. Transport them delicately, as if they were glasses of water filled to the brim... Because what is done cannot be undone.|Ferrus}}

==Locomotive Musings by Ferrus==
[[File:ST Ferrus Spacetime Gates.png|thumb|right|Ferrus's Locomotive Musings on Spacetime Gates]]
The four rules of the rails are accompanied by Ferrus's musings on {{Plural|ST|Spacetime Gate|link}}, which unwittingly provide {{Term|ST|Link}} with information on how to use the {{Term|ST|Warping|link}} devices for himself.
 
{{Quote|You know those archway-gate things you see on the rails sometimes? Who MADE them? WHEN? And WHYYYY? I was up all night pondering these questions, but I worked out a hypothesis. I think you can use them to break the space-time continuum! Cool, huh! There seem to be two types of these space-time gates.<br/>I'm pretty sure you can use both of them to warp to and from places. One type has a triangle at the top of the arch, and the other doesn't. I think they work in pairs and that hitting the triangle does something. I tried throwing rocks at a gate triangle to the south of Anouki Village... But bummer of all bummers, I couldn't reach it. I guess my arm is too weak. That's why I'm starting a new push-up regimen until I can hit the darn thing!|Ferrus}}
