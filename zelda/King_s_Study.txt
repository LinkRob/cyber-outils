The {{Term/Store|King's Study||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''King's Study'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|King's Study}} is a secret room in the {{Term|BotW|Library|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. It is hidden behind a metallic {{Term|BotW|Bookshelf|link}}, which can be moved through the use of the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Within, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can read {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam's Journal|link}}.

Inside the {{Term|BotW|King's Study}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. When it is opened, he will receive a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.
