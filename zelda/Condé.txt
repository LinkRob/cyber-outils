'''Condé''' is a character in {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
Condé is a kind-hearted yeti-like creature who lives in his home on [[Hebra Mountains|Hebra Mountain]], where he works to keep the mountain clear of snow. When Princess Zelda arrives during a blizzard, Condé offers her shelter and guidance toward Holy [[Mount Lanayru]].

Having once maintained the mountain with his father, Condé now waits for his older brother to return from an expedition and is determined to prove himself by dealing with the spreading Rifts. His longing leads him to mistake [[Skorchill]] for his brother during the storm, prompting him to chase after the creature before realizing his mistake.
