"{{Term/Store|Rutela's Theme||Series, TP, TPHD}}" is a {{Term|Series|Song}} in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Track|author= n.a. |track= 9. Rutela's Theme |discography= The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Sound Selection |publisher= Nintendo |year= 2016 |format= CD}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|TPHD|Rutela's Theme}}" is associated with the late {{Term|TPHD|Zora|link}} queen, {{Term|TPHD|Rutela|link}}. Consequently, it plays whenever she appears before {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}. "{{Term|TPHD|Rutela's Theme}}" is a remix of the "{{Term|OoT3D|Serenade of Water|link}}" from {{OoT}}.

"{{Term|TPHD|Rutela's Theme}}" is the ninth track in both {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Sound Selection}} and Disc 2 of {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Original Soundtrack|link}}.
