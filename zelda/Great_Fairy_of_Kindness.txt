==Biography==
The Great Fairy of Kindness is the Great Fairy in the Ikana Canyon. To restore the Great Fairy of Kindness you need to collect all 15 yellow stray fairies from the [[Stone Tower Temple]]. The Great Fairy of Kindness gives you the [[Great Fairy's Sword]].
