==Characteristics==
This Lynel has been fused in by [[Malice]]. It has pale white skin and a black mane, much like a [[Silver Lynel|Silver Lynel]]. Similar to a [[Ice Lynel]], a Malice Lynel wields two [[Savage Lynel Sword]]s. A Malice Lynel uses attacks similar to the dual wielding Lynel fighting style, but with a couple other attacks due to having Malice. It's unique attacks are as follows:

* Launching a malice energy ball
* Causing a malice explosion at the end of its Hover Spin+Sword Slam combo
* Using it's basic combo with the Hover Spin + Sword Slam attack added on
* Causing an explosion of Malice
