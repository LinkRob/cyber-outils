The {{Term/Store|Rod of Seasons||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref> It is a magical tool that can be used to change the current {{Term|OoS|Seasons|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|OoS|Rod of Seasons|You must get all the powers of the seasons to call up any season you wish. Stand on a tree stump and swing the rod to change the seasons.}}
The {{Term|OoS|Rod of Seasons}} is kept within the {{Term|OoS|Temple of Seasons|link}}. After an unfortunate series of events, the {{Plural|OoS|Season Spirit|link}} entrust the Rod to {{Term|OoS|Link|link}}. Four magical gems are encrusted into the Rod, each representing one of the four Seasons.

If the holder of the Rod of Seasons stands on a {{Term|OoS|Tree Stump|link}}, swinging it will cause the {{Plural|OoS|Season}} to change in order. To be able to change each {{Term|OoS|Season}}, however, the appropriate {{Term|OoS|Season Spirit}} must be visited to obtain its {{Term|OoS|Season}} power.

The power of {{Term|OoS|Winter|link}} is the first {{Term|OoS|Season}} power obtained, followed by {{Term|OoS|Summer|link}}. The power of {{Term|OoS|Spring|link}} and {{Term|OoS|Autumn|link}} can be obtained in either order. However, {{Term|OoS|Spring}} is required to access the {{Term|OoS|Dancing Dragon Dungeon|link|display=fourth dungeon}}, and {{Term|OoS|Autumn}} the {{Term|OoS|Unicorn's Cave|link|display=fifth dungeon}}, suggesting that this is the intended order.

Because {{Term|OoS|Link}} swings the {{Term|OoS|Rod of Seasons}} like a sword, it can be used to push enemies and block certain projectiles, like rocks from {{Plural|OoS|Octorok|link}}. It can only directly damage undead foes, including {{Plural|OoS|Stalfos|link}}, {{Plural|OoS|Gibdo|link}}, {{Plural|OoS|Ghini|link}}, and the {{Term|OoS|Poe Sisters|link}}.
