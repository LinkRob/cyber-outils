{{Term/Store|Willi||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259}}</ref>

==Biography==
Willi was originally a carpenter of [[Windfall Island]]. He loved the sky and wanted to fly just like the [[Rito]], so he began dressing up as one of them. He can be found together with his older brother [[Obli]] on the [[Flight Control Platform]], running the [[Bird-Man Contest]].

{{Figurine|TWW|Willi}}

==Nomenclature==
As his Spanish, French, and German names seem to suggest, Willi might be based on Wilbur Wright, one half of the [[Wikipedia:Wright Brothers|Wright Brothers]] who are credited with inventing and building the world's first successful airplane. However, Orville Wright was younger than Wilbur, while Obli is older than Willi.
