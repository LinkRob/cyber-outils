{{Term/Store|Blupee|Blupees|Series, BotW}} are {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Blupee}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Blupee}}
Otherwise resembling {{Plural|BotW|Rabbits|link}}, Blupees are mysterious, luminescent blue beings with a face resembling a barn owl's and golden wreaths in the place of ears.  They flee when noticing {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, eventually disappearing in a flash of light. They drop {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} when struck by an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}}, a thrown {{Term|BotW|Boomerang|link}}, or any melee weapon; though they cannot be killed. {{Plural|BotW|Blupee}} can be found in forests all over {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. Several {{Plural|BotW|Blupee}} gather on {{Term|BotW|Satori Mountain|link}} every time the {{Term|BotW|Lord of the Mountain|link}} appears.  Blupees share many characteristics with the Lord of the Mountain, whose face resembles a pair of Blupees peering out of a voluminous mane.  Like the Lord of the Mountain, Blupees appear to be mystical rather than natural creatures.

During the "{{Term|BotW|Legendary Rabbit Trial|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must take a picture of a {{Term|BotW|Blupee}} for {{Term|BotW|Peeks|link}} in exchange for a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|I just really want to see a {{Color|BotW Red|Blupee}}! Even if it's just a picture of one.|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> Afterward, {{Term|BotW|Peeks}} speculates that perhaps {{Plural|BotW|Blupee}} make all the {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder if {{Color|BotW Blue|Blupees}} sparkle because they're too full of rupees... Where did they get all those rupees anyway? Do they make all the rupees in the world?|Peeks|BotW}}</ref>

==={{TotK}}===
Since the {{Term|TotK|Upheaval|link}}, {{Plural|TotK|Cave|link}} have appeared all over {{Term|TotK|Hyrule|link}} and Blupees have taken a liking to these secluded locations. Nearly every cave has a Blupee nearby. When they're startled, they retreat into the nearest home cave. They have also known to take in a new form called {{Term|TotK|Bubbulfrog|link}}, thanks to the adaptation of the caves.
