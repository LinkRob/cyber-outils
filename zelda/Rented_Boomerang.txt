The {{Term/Store|Rented Boomerang||Series, ALBW}} is an {{Term|ALBW|Item|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rented Boomerang<br/>Returns when you throw it. It can also hit switches and stun enemies on the way!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Rented Boomerang}} is used to stun {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}} and hit {{Plural|ALBW|Shock Switch|link}}. It is rentable from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}} for fifty {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.<br/><br/>Being a rented {{Term|ALBW|Item}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Boomerang}} will be returned to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio}} by {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} falls in battle. It may be permanently obtained as the {{Term|ALBW|Boomerang|link}} for 800 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, and further upgraded into the {{Term|ALBW|Nice Boomerang|link}} by {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}.
