{{Term/Store|Grog||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Grog is the owner of the [[Cucco Shack]] in [[Romani Ranch]]. He loves his [[Cucco]] chicks, but when he hears the rumor that the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] will crash into [[Termina]] in three days from his unseen grandfather, he is filled with sorrow as he realizes he will not see his chicks develop into roosters, understanding that the moon's impact with [[Clock Town]] will reach the ranch as well as everywhere else.<ref>{{Cite|I heard it from my gramps. Says the moon's gonna fall... With somethin' that big, it's sure to take this ranch down with it... Hahhh... Oh, well. My only regret is that I won't get to see these guys in their prime as roosters.|Grog|MM}}</ref>

[[Link]] can help Grog fulfill his wish by parading around the Shack with the [[Bremen Mask]] and getting the Cucco chicks to follow him. Once all 10 of them are following Link, they will immediately begin to transform into full-grown Cuccos, one by one. The young [[Hero of Time]] in turn receives the [[Bunny Hood]] from Grog as thanks for helping him out, saying he does not have any more regrets and is completely satisfied.<ref>{{Cite|I don't really get it, but just seein' these guys with a crest and all... I don't have regrets about anythin' anymore. I'm perfectly satisfied. Here...You can have this from me...|Grog|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, making the Cuccos grow up a second time will lead Grog to give Link a [[Fishing Hole Pass]].<ref>{{Cite|They're all grown up now...How did you do that? Just seein' these guys with a crest and all... I don't have regrets about anythin' anymore. I'm pretty satisfied. Here...You can have this from me...Yeahhhh...These guys are all roosters. Heh, heh.|Grog|MM3D}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* He is the [[Terminan]] counterpart of the [[Master Craftsman's Son|Master Craftman's Son]] from {{OoT}}, with a notable [[wikipedia:Liberty_spikes|liberty spike]]-style hair being added to the character model.
* Grog, alongside the man seen when [[Link]] completes the [[Oceanside Spider House]] at [[Great Bay Coast]], is one of the very few residents of [[Termina]] who appears to be aware of the impending doom by the [[Moon]] on the [[First Day]].
* He shares the same voice as the [[Part-Timer]] employee of the [[Trading Post]] shop.

==Nomenclature==
In common day usage, ''{{wp|grog}}'' is slang for an alcoholic drink, originally composed of rum and water, known for being a popular beverage among pirates. It could also be a play off of "groggy", as Grog is commonly found sleeping.
