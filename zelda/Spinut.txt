{{Term/Store|Spinut|Spinuts|Series, ST|plural}} are {{Plural|ST|Enemy|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=  |book= E |publisher= |page= 206}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
When {{Plural|ST|Spinut}} encounter {{Term|ST|Link|link}}, they will rush toward him, attempting to slam themselves into him. Two variants of {{Term|ST|Spinut}} appear: the defaul weaker type that is brown and yellow; and the rarer, stronger version, the {{Term|ST|Green Spinut|link}}. One or two strikes from {{Term|ST|Link}}'s {{Term|ST|Sword|link}} are usually enough to defeat them.

==Nomenclature==
"Spinut" is derived from a portmanteau of "spine", referring to their points atop their heads, and "nut", indicative of their body shape.
