==Overview==
The {{Term|Series|Inventory}} is viewed on the {{Term|Series|Subscreen|link}}—the two terms are synonymous in games where the {{Term|Series|Inventory}} comprises the entire {{Term|Series|Subscreen}}. It is divided into sections for the different types of items, namely items assigned to buttons, {{Term|Series|Equipment|link}}, quest items, and collectibles. These different types of items may appear in different sections of the same screen, or on different screens entirely: the {{Term|Series|Items|link}} screen (for selected items), the {{Term|Series|Quest Status|link}} screen, the {{Term|Series|Equipment Subscreen|link}}, the {{Term|Series|Collection Screen|link}}, and/or the {{Term|Series|Gear Screen|link}}.

==={{TLoZ|-}}===
[[File:TLoZ Inventory.png|thumb|right|300px|The Inventory screen from {{TLoZ|-}}]]
In {{TLoZ}}, the Subscreen is accessed by pressing the [[Controller Buttons|START]] button. At the top of the screen are icons showing if Link has collected the [[Raft]], the [[Book of Magic]], the [[Blue Ring|Blue]] or [[Red Ring|Red]] rings, the [[Ladder]], the [[Magical Key]], and the [[Power Bracelet]]. Below that is an item box containing, from left to right and top to bottom, the [[Boomerang]] or [[Magical Boomerang]], [[Bombs]], the [[Bow]] and [[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]], the [[Red Candle|Red]] or [[Blue Candle|Blue]] Candle, the [[Whistle]], the [[Food]], the {{Term|TLoZ|Letter|link}}, [[Red Potion]], or [[Blue Potion]], and the [[Magical Rod]]. The number of collected [[Triforce Shard|Triforce Shards]], [[Rupee|Rupees]], [[Small Key|Keys]], [[Bomb|Bombs]], and [[Heart Container|Heart Containers]] can also be seen, as well as the [[Sword]] and item currently equipped to the [[Controller Buttons|B]] button.

==={{LA|-}}===
[[File:Z4 Inventory.png|thumb|right|300px|The Subscreen from {{LA|DX|-}}]]
In {{LA}}, the Subscreen is accessed by pressing the [[Controller Buttons|START]] button. It allows Link to browse his possessions. As such, he can see his collection of items, his current item in the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}, the number of [[Rupee]]s, [[Life Gauge|Heart Container]]s, and [[Secret Seashell]]s he possesses, the [[Instrument of the Sirens|Instruments of the Sirens]] he has so far, which of the Dungeon [[Small Key|Keys]] he is holding onto and whether or not he has obtained the [[Flippers]] and [[Secret Medicine]]. Link can also re-organize the location of his items in the Subscreen. Having a maximum capacity of two items equipped at once, he can decide which items he wants to equip on this screen.<ref>{{Cite|'You can select your favorite item for the A and B Buttons on the Sub-Screen. Using different items, you can fight without a sword! Try many different things to find what's right for you!'|Selecting The Item That's Right For You|LADX}}</ref> Upon holding the [[Controller Buttons|SELECT]] button on the Subscreen, Link can see how many [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]] he is missing until he fills another Heart Container.<ref>{{Cite|You've got a Piece of Heart! Press SELECT on the Subscreen to see.|N/A|LADX}}</ref> In {{LA|DX}}, holding the Select button also lets him know which [[Clothes]] he is wearing and the number of [[Photograph|Photographs]] that have been taken so far.

==={{TMC|-}}===
The {{Term|TMC|Inventory}} is one of the {{Plural|TMC|Subscreen|link}} in {{TMC}}, accessed by pressing [[Controller Buttons|START]]. There are 12 slots for {{Plural|TMC|Item|link}} and four slots for {{Plural|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}}. Each of the 12 {{Term|TMC|Item}} slots are dedicated to a particular {{Term|TMC|Item}}, and unlike the {{Plural|TMC|Empty Bottle}}, the spots do not fill up from left to right; rather, each {{Term|TMC|Item}} obtained is placed in its designated slot.

Whenever an {{Term|TMC|Item}} is exchanged for a variation or an upgrade, the previous version disappears and is replaced with the new version in the {{Term|TMC|Inventory}}.

The {{Plural|TMC|Item}} displayed in the {{Term|TMC|Inventory}} are as follows (left to right, top to bottom):

*{{Term|TMC|Smith's Sword|link}}, {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Boomerang|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Magical Boomerang|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Small Shield|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Mirror Shield|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Remote Bomb|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Roc's Cape|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Bow|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Bow of Light|link}}

==={{TP|-}}===
[[File:Inventory Screen TP 2.jpg|right|250px]]
In {{TP}}, the Inventory appears in a large circle with all of Link's collected items on the border. The player can select the item they wish to use and then select which button they want to use it as. {{Note|X or Y on the [[Gamecube]], and Left, Right, or Down on [[Wii]].}} To use the item, the player must press the button they assigned the item to and Link will pull out the item. The complete order of icons starting from the top going clockwise is, the [[Double Clawshots]], the [[Dominion Rod]], the [[Ball and Chain]], the [[Spinner]], the [[Hero's Bow]], the [[Iron Boots]], the [[Gale Boomerang]], the [[Lantern]], the [[Slingshot]], the [[Fishing Rod]], the [[Hawkeye]], the three [[Bomb Bag]]s, the four [[Bottle]]s, and finally, the [[Horse Call]].{{Note|The Image on the right is missing the fourth bottle, and the image below is missing the Magic Armor.}}
[[File:Inventory Screen TP.jpg|right|250px]]
The second Inventory screen shows the Equipment. In the bottom left corner of the screen, a 2-by-4 array of squares shows icons of, from left to right, the [[Wallet]], the [[Quiver]], the [[Golden Bug]] List, the [[Hidden Skill]] List, the [[Scent]] that [[Wolf Link]] knows at the time, the [[Poe Soul]] Count, the [[Fish Journal]] and then lastly, [[Letters]]. Each of these icons are displayed only once they have been unlocked. When each icon hasn't been found, only a empty box will appear. 
Above these squares, a display of Link shows what equipment Link is using at the time. To the right of this display, 7 boxes display more icons. The top 2 squares display Link's Swords, with the [[Ordon Sword]] next to the Master Sword. Below these two boxes, two shields are displayed with the [[Ordon Shield]] next to the Hylian Shield. Below those boxes, three boxes can be found holding armor, with the [[Hero's Clothes]], the [[Zora Tunic]] and then the [[Magic Armor]] all next to each other. 
Next to these boxes, a display with the [[Pieces of Heart]] that Link has collected shows how many more Link needs to complete a [[Heart Container]]. Next to the Pieces of Heart, the main quest items appear floating around a small area. At first, these are the [[Fused Shadow]]s, but after beating the {{Term|TPHD|Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|link}} Dungeon, they change to the [[Mirror of Twilight]]. And finally, underneath the main quest items and Armor, a description appears depending on which icon is selected at the time.

==Types of Inventories==
===Debut and Split-Screen Inventory===
Debuting in {{TLoZ}}, the Inventory not only displays the different types of items Link receives during his journey, but also keeps track of the number of [[Triforce]] fragments he has obtained throughout the many dungeons of Hyrule thus far. It is in this title originally that, at the press of a button, the Inventory will replace the game screen, stopping time and gameplay, only to retreat upon another pressing of the same button. Later two-dimensional titles such as {{ALttP}}, {{LA}}, and the ''Oracle'' titles carry on this trend, though displaying game-exclusive items and quest specifics.

===Multi-Screen Inventory===
The multi-faceted Inventory is first showcased in {{OoT}}, with multiple layers: the '''Map Subscreen''' lays out the dungeon and overworld maps; the '''Select Item Subscreen''' lists items such as [[Bomb]]s, [[Arrow]]s, [[Empty Bottle]]s; the '''Equipment Subscreen''' lists Link's clothing, such as swords, shields, and tunics; the '''Quest Status Subscreen''' displays [[Medallions]], ocarina songs, [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]], [[Stone of Agony]] and [[Gold Skulltula]] tokens. The multi-faceted Inventory is necessary to combat the increasing complexity of the 3D console ''Zelda'' titles, with many more items to carry, maps to use, and stats to keep track of. A similar trend is carried into {{MM}}, {{TWW}}, {{TMC}}, and even {{TP}}, the latter of which presents a rotating turntable instead of a solid select screen for item selection.

===On the Nintendo DS===
{{PH}} and {{ST}} incorporate the Inventory as a product of the two [[Nintendo DS]] screens; instead of flipping through the Inventory like in {{OoT|-}}, two screens of the Inventory are displayed at once, fully using the capabilities of the two DS screens to the game's advantage. In {{ST|-}} especially, there is a small navigation bar at the bottom of the lower screen that will take the player straight to the Inventory screens, while riding the [[Spirit Train]].
