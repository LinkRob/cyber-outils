The {{Term/Store|Dunewalker Duds||Series, TFH}} are an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Dunewalker Duds have been in vogue for ages, dahling. They prevent you from sinking in quicksand!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Dunewalker Duds}} are an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}. They become available for purchase after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes {{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} features a {{Wp|Nemes|nemes}} and {{Wp|Shendyt|shendyt}} associated with {{Wp|Ancient Egypt|ancient Egyptian}} {{Wp|Pharaoh|pharaohs}}.

The {{Term|TFH|Dunewalker Duds}} allow {{Term|TFH|Link}} to stand on [[Quicksand]] without sinking. This effect is signified by a protective circle rotating around {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s feet. While wearing the {{Term|TFH|Dunewalker Duds}}, {{Term|TFH|Link}} can still be swept away by flowing sand currents, but he will remain standing up, giving him a chance to escape the current.
