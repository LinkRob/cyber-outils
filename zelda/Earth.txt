{{Term/Store|Earth||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Element|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 152/256">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 152 & 256 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Earth}} represents both the planet of {{TLoZ|Series}} and objects like dirt, rocks, and other solid terrain.

==Overview==
{{Incomplete|reason=Gorons, Moldorms, any other enemies/races that live in the ground}}

==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, the {{Term|ALttP|Quake Medallion|link}} uses the {{Term|ALttP|Earth}} {{Term|ALttP|Element|link}} by allowing {{Term|ALttP|Link}} to thrust his {{Term|ALttP|Master Sword|link}} into the ground and cause an earthquake, damaging non-airborne {{Plural|ALttP|Enemy|link}} by either killing them or turning them into {{Plural|ALttP|Zol|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the Quake Medallion!<br/>It's magic causes the ground to shake and defeats nearby enemies!<br/>Watch your Magic Meter!|N/A|ALttP}}</ref><ref name="E 135">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 135}}</ref> The {{Term|ALttP|Quake Medallion}} can shake {{Plural|ALttP|Item|link}} out of {{Plural|ALttP|Tree|link}}, and is used to open the entrance to {{Term|ALttP|Turtle Rock|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 147}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
While {{Term|MM3D|Earth}} {{Term|MM3D|Magic|link}} isn't seen in {{MM}}, many {{Plural|MM3D|Enemy|link}} will use the {{Term|MM3D|Earth}} surrounding them to their advantage. {{Plural|MM3D|Nejiron|link}}, for example, burrow under the ground and wait until {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} walks close to them before quickly rising from the ground and attacking.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 194}}</ref> {{Plural|MM3D|Nejiron}} are made of gunpowder and will explode when attacked or when striking {{Term|MM3D|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|That's a}} {{Color|MM3D White|Nejiron}}{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|.<br/>Don't get too close to it.<br/>Sniff...Sniff...<br/>It smells like gun powder!}}|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref> Because of this, they can quickly be defeated by {{Term|MM3D|Link}}, so when one {{Term|MM3D|Nejiron}} is defeated, another quickly takes its place, hinting at the fact that there are many {{Plural|MM3D|Nejiron}} burrowed under the ground. Similarly, the two {{Plural|MM3D|Twinmold|link}} will burrow under the sand in the arena where they live in order to hide or to attack {{Term|MM3D|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 210}}</ref>

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Earth}} is the primary {{Term|TWWHD|Element|link}} used by the Sage of {{Term|TWWHD|Earth}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Laruto|link}}.<ref name="E 152/256"/> {{Term|TWWHD|Laruto}} would use her power of {{Term|TWWHD|Earth}} to replenish the {{Term|TWWHD|Power to Repel Evil|link}} in the {{Term|TWWHD|Master Sword|link}} by praying inside the {{Term|TWWHD|Earth Temple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am a Zora {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|sage}}. For an age, I offered my prayers here in the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Earth Temple}}, praying that the power to repel evil would ever remain in the Master Sword.|Laruto|TWWHD}}</ref> In order to rid of the power in the {{Term|TWWHD|Master Sword}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Ganondorf|link}} killed {{Term|TWWHD|Laruto}} and stopped the prayers that replenished the power.<ref>{{Cite|He attacked this temple and stole my soul, knowing that he had to remove the power contained in that enchanted blade.|Laruto|TWWHD}}</ref> To regain the power, {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} set out for the {{Term|TWWHD|Sage}} of {{Term|TWWHD|Earth}}'s descendant, {{Term|TWWHD|Medli|link}}, to awaken her as a {{Term|TWWHD|Sage}} and bring her to the {{Term|TWWHD|Earth Temple|link}}.<ref name="E 152/256"/><ref>{{Cite|In order to return the power to repel evil to your sword, you must find another to take my stead in this temple and ask the gods for their assistance. You must find the one who carries on my bloodline... The one who holds this sacred {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|instrument}}...|Laruto|TWWHD}}</ref> After traversing the {{Term|TWWHD|Earth Temple}} and defeating {{Term|TWWHD|Jalhalla|link}}, the pair restored part of the {{Term|TWWHD|Master Sword}}'s power by playing the {{Term|TWWHD|Earth God's Lyric|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The prayers of the Earth Sage have restored the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Master Sword's}} former energy!|N/A|TWWHD}}</ref>

The {{Term|TWWHD|Earth Temple}} and the "{{Term|TWWHD|Earth God's Lyric}}" {{Term|TWWHD|Song|link}} both attribute to and contain great power of {{Term|TWWHD|Earth}}. The {{Term|TWWHD|Earth God's Lyric}} is able to awaken {{Term|TWWHD|Medli}} as a {{Term|TWWHD|Sage}}, as well as restore the {{Term|TWWHD|Power to Repel Evil}} in the {{Term|TWWHD|Master Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|song you just conducted}} is one that will {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|open the eyes}} of the new {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|sage}} and awaken within that sage the melody that will carry out prayers to the gods.|Laruto|TWWHD}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
The element of {{Term|TMC|Earth}} is represented by the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} in {{TMC}}. It is said to be the source of all living things,<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|TMC Red|Earth Element}}! The power of the earth is the source of all living things. The {{Color|TMC Red|Earth Element}} is the embodiment of that power.|N/A|TMC}}</ref> and it is found inside {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TMC Red|Earth Element}} can be found in the {{Color|TMC Green|shrine}} to the north of {{Color|TMC Green|Festari}}'s abbey.|Gentari|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element}}, he is able to infuse it (along with the {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link}}) into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}},<ref>{{Cite|So of the four {{Color|TMC Red|elements}}, we've got Earth and Fire... And now, we have the White Sword to absorb their power!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> empowering the blade.<ref>{{Cite|The powers of the {{Color|TMC Red|Earth}} and {{Color|TMC Red|Fire Elements}} have infused your blade!|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link}} is also able to dig through {{Term|TMC|Earth}} by using the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts|link}}.
