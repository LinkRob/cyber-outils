The {{Term/Store|Stage Select||Series, LCT}} is a menu in {{LCT}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|LCT Blue|Stage Select}}}}|N/A|LCT}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|LCT|Stage Select}} is a menu that appears only when selecting the {{Term|LCT|Multiplayer|link}} or {{Term|LCT|Practice|link}} options on the {{Term|LCT|Title Screen|link}}. Choosing {{Term|LCT|Practice}} will first have the player pick a save file on {{Term|LCT|Player Select|link}} before moving to {{Term|LCT|Stage Select}}. Selecting {{Term|LCT|Multiplayer}} will first have the player determine the number of players on {{Term|LCT|How Many Players?|link}}, and then choose the save files on {{Term|LCT|Player Select}} before moving to {{Term|LCT|Stage Select}}. In {{Term|LCT|Stage Select}}, a full list of every {{Term|LCT|Stage|link}} from each {{Term|LCT|Level|link}} is displayed. Each option displays the {{Term|LCT|Level}} and {{Term|LCT|Stage}} number.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|1-1}}|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> Players can play any of these {{Plural|LCT|Stage}} to practice or fight against opponents in {{Term|LCT|Multiplayer}} without having to complete the entire {{Term|LCT|Level}}. Although every {{Term|LCT|Stage}} is shown, only the {{Plural|LCT|Stage}} that are from {{Plural|LCT|Level}} {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} has unlocked can be accessed to play. {{Plural|LCT|Stage}} that have not been unlocked are dimmed out and not selectable. Because {{Term|LCT|Level 1|link}}, {{Term|LCT|Level 2|link}}, and {{Term|LCT|Level 3|link}} are already unlocked upon starting the game, the {{Plural|LCT|Stage}} from those respective {{Plural|LCT|Level}} are automatically unlocked in {{Term|LCT|Stage Select}}. A button in the bottom left corner of the menu bring take the player back to the {{Term|LCT|Player Select}}.<ref>{{Cite|Back|N/A|LCT}}</ref> A button in the top right corner will take the player to the {{Term|LCT|Best Scores|link}} menu, where players can see which save file holds the highest score for every {{Term|LCT|Stage}} in {{Term|LCT|Practice}}.<ref>{{Cite|Top Scores|N/A|LCT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|LCT Blue|Best Scores}}}}|N/A|LCT}}</ref> In {{Term|LCT|Multiplayer}}, the button to access the {{Term|LCT|Best Scores}} menu does not appear.
