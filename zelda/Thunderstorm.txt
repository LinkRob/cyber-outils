{{Term/Store|Thunderstorm|Thunderstorms|Series, BotW|plural}} are a {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}} mechanic in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|publisher= Piggyback|page= 26}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm}} occur in {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Central Hyrule|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Faron (Region)|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Necluda|link}}. Throughout the duration of a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm}}, {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}} will fall from the sky and {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} will strike at random. {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm}} are also possible during {{Term|BotW|Snow|link}} in places such as {{Term|BotW|Mount Hylia|link}}. Any metallic {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has equipped will begin to spark during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm}}, eventually culminating in {{Term|BotW|Link}} being struck by {{Term|BotW|Lightning}}. In addition to this, metallic {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}}, such as the metal blocks in {{Plural|BotW|Cube Pattern|link}}, will also attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning}}.

Initially, {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Calora Lake|link}} will be permanently under the affects of a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm}}. After solving the "{{Term|BotW|Trial of Thunder|link}}" and "{{Term|BotW|A Song of Storms|link}}" {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} and activating their respective {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, these {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm}} will subside.
