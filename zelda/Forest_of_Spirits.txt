The {{Term/Store|Forest of Spirits||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Forest of Spirits'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits}} is a heavily-wooded area in the north of the [[Great Plateau]] that encircles [[Hopper Pond]]. It is lush with wildlife, inhabited by various {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} as well as a plethora of plant-based {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}. In particular, the forest is home to large numbers of [[Woodland Boar]]s. Various ruins sit within the forest. A stone tower can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits|display=Forest}}'s northwestern reaches. An uninhabited camp containing a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} can be found in the eastern reaches of the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits}}. An enormous hollow log with the top rotted away can be found near the center of the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits}}. North of this log, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}}. A [[Stone Talus]] can be found in the rocky clearing in the eastern portion of the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits|display=Forest}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can find several [[Tree Branch]]es littered across the Forest floor. A [[Traveler's Bow]], bundles of {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}, and [[Torch]]es can be found scattered around the campsite on the eastern portion of the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits}}. A [[Rusty Broadsword]] can be found in the western portion of the clearing in the middle of the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits|display=Forest}}.

Inside the hollow log, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a tall, yellow flower. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to approach this flower, it will vanish, and reappear a short distance away. After it does this several times, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will emerge from it and give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a [[Korok Seed]] in return for finding him. Another {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found by lifting the [[Rock]] on the top of the small, strangely shaped hill. A third {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found underneath a Rock hidden under a pile of leaves under the roots of a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} to the east of the aforementioned camp. To reach the Rock, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must destroy the leaves by cutting them, blowing them away with a [[Korok Leaf]], setting fire to them, or blowing them up with a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}}.

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} cannot access the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} until the enemies surrounding it have been eliminated. A second stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a bundle of 10 {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} can be found atop the tower at the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits|display=Forest}}'s far western edge.
