{{Term/Store|Surf Harp|Surf Harps|Series, LA, LADX, LANS, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 140"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Surf Harp}}: Use this on the search screen to temporarily increase experienced earned.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
The {{Term|LANS|Surf Harp}} is one of the eight {{Plural|LANS|Instrument of the Sirens|link}} needed to awaken the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish|link}}.<ref name="E 140"/> It can be found at the end of {{Term|LANS|Angler's Tunnel|link}} after defeating its {{Term|LANS|Boss|link}}, {{Term|LANS|Angler Fish|link}}.<ref name="E 140"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, after defeating the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Angler Fish|link}} inside {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Angler's Tunnel|link}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} retreives the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Surf Harp}}. After gathering all eight {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Instrument of the Sirens|link}}, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Surf Harp}} plays the "{{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ballad of the Wind Fish|link}}" alongside the other instruments while {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} plays his {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ocarina|link}} in oder to open the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Wind Fish's Egg|link}}.

==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWL Surf Harp Sprite.png|left|40px]]
In {{HW|L}}, the {{Term|HWL|Surf Harp}} appears as an {{Term|HWL|Item Card|link}} in the {{Term|HWL|Koholint Island Map|link}} of {{Term|HWL|Adventure Mode|link}}, which is included in the {{LA|-}} DLC Pack. It increases the amount of {{Term|HWL|Experience|link}} points earned.
