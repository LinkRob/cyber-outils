The {{Term/Store|Gust Garb||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Gust Garb comes highly recommended. It's bold and breezy, and it powers up your Gust Jar too!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Gust Garb}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|TFH|Vintage Linen|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Gibdo Bandage|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Sandy Ribbon|link}} and 1,500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. It becomes available for purchase after completing {{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}.

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Gust Garb}} powers up several aspects of the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}} as well as giving it a fancier appearance. The delay between activating the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar}} and it firing a gust of air is much quicker; while the gust moves at the same speed as without the {{Term|TFH|Gust Garb}}, it travels 50% farther before dissipating, and has a 50% wider radius.
