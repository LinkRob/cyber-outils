The {{Term/Store|Cubus Sisters|Cubus Sisters|Series, PH}} are characters and {{Plural|PH|Boss|link}} in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The {{Term|PH|Cubus Sisters}} initially appear as four young human girls, identical in appearance except for their different colored clothes, who are encountered by [[Link]] while he's exploring the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}. He finds the sister in red cowering in a corner on the first floor while a group of {{Plural|PH|Poe|link}} haunt the area. Once the {{Plural|PH|Poe}} are defeated, she introduces herself to {{Term|PH|Link}} as the youngest daughter of the '''House of Cubus''', having been kidnapped along with her three older sisters and held captive onboard the eerie vessel.<ref>{{Cite|I am the youngest daughter of the great {{Color|PH Blue|house of Cubus}}... My sisters were living in peace, when we were suddenly snatched away! We've been locked up on this boat ever since, waiting to be saved.|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref> She somehow has knowledge of {{Term|PH|Link}}'s name and his quest to rescue {{Term|PH|Tetra|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You... Could you be the legendary [Link]?|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref> and seems relieved that a hero has arrived to save her siblings as well.<ref>{{Cite|But...everything is fine now that you're here to rescue us, [Link]! This is your chance to be a true hero!|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref>

Each of the three remaining {{Plural|PH|Cubus Sisters}} can be found in a cell on the three respective floors of the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}}. {{Term|PH|Link}} must free the sisters and lead them one-by-one to where the youngest sister awaits on the top floor. If {{Term|PH|Link}} gets separated by more than a short distance from the girl he's leading, or if a {{Term|PH|Skulltula|link}} descends into her line of sight, she'll begin to cower in fear and require {{Term|PH|Link}}'s reassurance to continue.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... I'm so sorry. I was frozen with terror! When I am surprised or get left behind, I start to panic! When that happens, please come {{Color|PH Red|close to me and speak to me}}.|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref> When speaking to the {{Plural|PH|Cubus Sisters|display=Sisters}}, they will occasionally show signs of their true, evil nature, either by giving {{Term|PH|Link}} poor advice,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yes, [Link]. A lovely treasure rests here...{{Icon|PH DS}} There are two chests. If you must pick one, definitely pick the {{Color|PH Red|left}}... Left! Yes, yes...the left!|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! There is one thing I have to tell you. The creepy creatures with the hoods and sickles are called {{Color|PH Blue|Reaplings}}. They are evil, but they are even more devilish when they're angry... So no matter what you do, don't {{Color|PH Red|shoot arrows into their backs}}.|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref> or through a simple slip of the tongue.<ref>{{Cite|Most of the abducted have fallen victim to the {{Color|PH Blue|Reaplings}} in the hold. I must say, they are... {{Color|PH Red|Fallen}}! {{Color|PH Red|Fallen}}! They are {{Color|PH Red|the fallen}}! Oh, excuse me! Never mind that. I don't know what came over me...|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref> Once the four girls are reunited, they will gather around and hiss at {{Term|PH|Link}}, sharing disbelief that he survived the {{Plural|PH|Reapling|link}} and the Ghost {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|display=Ship}}'s various traps,<ref>{{Cite|Hssss! The Reaplings should've scared the life from this boy in the hold... Doesn't he know how he's supposed to play along in this little game?|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref> and declaring that it's time to "play extra rough".<ref>{{Cite|Teehee! All it means is we're going to have to play extra rough!|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref> They then transport {{Term|PH|Link}} and themselves to the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|display=Ship}}'s deck and reveal their true forms.

===Battle===
The battle against the {{Term|PH|Cubus Sisters}} takes place across both screens of the [[Nintendo DS]], with the sisters flying around overhead. The youngest sister will launch balls of energy down at {{Term|PH|Link}} in a game of {{Term|PH|Dead Man's Volley|link}}, while the others fire beams of light that {{Term|PH|Link}} must avoid. After outmatching the youngest sister and striking her with her own energy ball twice, she will be defeated and the remaining sisters will continue the battle.

With three or fewer left, the sisters will begin to pass the moving energy ball between themselves several times before returning it to {{Term|PH|Link}}, in an attempt to disorient the hero. The first sister to be struck by the ball twice will be defeated. When two sisters remain, some of the energy balls they launch will be decoys with a different appearance, which will split apart if {{Term|PH|Link}} strikes them with his {{Term|PH|Sword|link}}, likely causing him harm. The final remaining sister will launch three energy balls toward {{Term|PH|Link}} at once; two decoys and one real one. The final sister must be struck three more times before she'll fall, feign congratulations, then disappear, leaving behind the {{Term|PH|Ghost Key|link}} needed to enter the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|display=Ship}}'s hull where {{Term|PH|Tetra}} is trapped.<ref>{{Cite|You really know how to play, [Link]... Gosh, we'd love to reward you, but we don't have any trophies for you. Instead, you can...teehee...drag that precious {{Color|PH Blue|Tetra}} of yours with you. Tee-hee!|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref>

If {{Term|PH|Link}} does not obtain the {{Term|PH|Heart Container|link}} from the {{Term|PH|Treasure Chest|link}} before leaving the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}}, it will be [[Mail Delivery|mailed]] to him by an unknown sender.<ref>{{Cite|It's yours, [{{Color|PH Blue|Link}}], but there's no return address. Who's it from?!|Postman|PH}}</ref> The {{Term|PH|Letter|link}}'s phrasing suggests that it comes from the {{Term|PH|Cubus Sisters}} themselves.<ref>{{Cite|Say, did you leave something important on the ship? Maybe a {{Color|PH Red|Heart Container}}? We grabbed it for you, so please take it. We're closely watching your epic adventure, Oh scatterbrained one... From very high in the sky!|Letter|PH}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese version, the {{Term|PH|Cubus Sisters}} only made sounds when they fired energy balls. They were given a sound for receiving energy balls and dying in the international versions.
*The way in which Link must lead the Cubus Sisters through the dungeon while they're in disguise until they reveal their true forms during the boss battle is very similar to [[Blind the Thief]] from {{aLttP}}.

==Nomenclature==
The name "Cubus" may based off of the word {{wp|Succubus}}, which refers to a female demon which seduces men in order to steal their life force.
