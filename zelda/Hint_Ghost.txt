{{Term/Store|Hint Ghost|Hint Ghosts|Series, ALBW|plural}} are characters in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300}}</ref> They are ghosts that give hints on solving puzzles at the cost of a [[Play Coin]].<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Hint Ghosts will give you hints in exchange for Play Coins.|game= ALBW|page= 10}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Would you like to offer 1 Play Coin? Remember, once you give it up, you can't get it back.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Plural|ALBW|Hint Ghost}} are invisible ghosts that appear in many areas in the {{Term|ALBW|Overworld|link}} and inside of {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon|link}} and [[Treasure Hunter Cave]]s, usually close to a puzzle. Their appearance resembles a blue question mark, wearing glasses and reading a book.<ref name="studying"/> They can only be seen and spoken to by wearing the {{Term|ALBW|Hint Glasses|link}} obtained from the {{Term|ALBW|Fortune-Teller|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= If you use the Hint Glasses, you'll be able to see Hint Ghosts|game= ALBW|page= 10}}</ref> At the cost of one Play Coin, the {{Term|ALBW|Hint Ghost|display=Ghost}} will refer to its book to give {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} a hint on how to solve a nearby puzzle.
