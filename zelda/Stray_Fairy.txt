{{Term/Store|Stray Fairy|Stray Fairies|Series, MM, MM3D, FS|plural}},{{Exp Game|MM, FS}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244{{Exp Game|MM}} & 263{{Exp Game|FS}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Forest Fairy|Forest Fairies|OoA|plural}},{{Exp Game|OoA}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 212 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> are recurring representations of portions of a shattered [[Great Fairy]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}. In a region where a Great Fairy lay at the mercy of her Stray Fairies, they will appear individually and often at hard-to-reach heights or inside [[Treasure Chests]]. The collection of all the Stray Fairies in a region will facilitate the resurrection of said Great Fairy, upon returning them to their respective [[Fairy Fountain]].
 
==Characteristics==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the five [[Great Fairy|Great Fairies]] have been split into pieces by the [[Skull Kid]], who is under the influence of {{MM|Object}}.<ref>{{Cite|Young one! Please hear my plea! I have been broken and shattered to pieces by the masked Skull Kid.|Great Fairy|MM}}</ref> [[Link]] must gather them up and take them to the shrine to heal the Great Fairy, who will aid him in his quest to save [[Termina]].

The first Great Fairy in Termina is found in [[North Clock Town]], and only one Stray Fairy is missing. It is found in the [[Laundry Pool]] in the daytime and in [[East Clock Town]] at night. When healed, she either gives Link the [[Magic Meter]] or the [[Great Fairy Mask]], depending on whether he is [[Deku Link]] or his normal self.

The other [[Great Fairy Fountain]]s are in the four cardinal directions, where each Great Fairy is missing fifteen Stray Fairies that can be found in that area's dungeon.<ref>{{Cite|Scattered about the four temples of this land are Stray Fairies like myself. Please, somehow find the Stray Fairies and return them to their fountains. Surely, they too will add to your strength.|Great Fairy|MM}}</ref> Each time a Great Fairy is healed for the first time, she rewards Link with a helpful item or an upgrade.

==={{OoA|-}}===
[[File:OoA Orange Forest Fairy Sprite.png|right|35px]][[File:ForestFairy Blue.png|left|35px]]
In {{OoA}}, red, blue, and orange friendly but mischievous '''Forest Fairies''' have the power to jumble up the topography of the [[Fairies' Woods]] as they see fit in [[Labrynna]]'s present. Link must locate the three Fairies in a [[Hide-and-Seek]] game to be granted access to the [[Wing Dungeon]].<ref>{{Cite|This is our playground! Won't you play hide 'n' seek? If you can't find the three of us, you can't go on! We'll hide, so try to find us!|Stray Fairy|OoA}}</ref> When Link is successful, the Fairies straighten out the Woods, allowing the young hero to reach the dungeon.

If the original {{OoS}} file is linked to {{OoA|-}}, after Link talks to the Stray Fairy in the rubble of the Wing Dungeon, the [[Heart Ring L-1]] can be found in a secret room behind the waterfall of the [[Temple of Seasons]].
{{Clear}}

==={{FS|-}}===
In {{FS}}, in addition to [[Fairy|Fairy Spirits]], Stray Fairies appear in the [[Chambers of Insight]] at the beginning of the game, and provide the [[Links]] with their advice on how to progress.
