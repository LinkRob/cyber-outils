The {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker}} was the {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} wielded by the {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}}, until his defeat by {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> It is considered part of the {{Term|BotW|Goron Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote =|book = CaC|publisher =|page = 171}}</ref> It is mentioned that {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} made swinging this {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} around look easy, while others who used it would need a large amount of strength.<ref name="Inventory"/> According to {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} used the {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker}} to carve the valley at {{Term|BotW|Gortram Cliff|link}} in one night.<ref>{{Cite|You know the valley at {{Color|BotW Blue|Gortram Cliff}}, southwest of {{Color|BotW Blue|Gorko Tunnel}}? It was made by {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}} with his {{Color|BotW Blue|Boulder Breaker}} in one night!|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> He did so to make it easier for the {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}} to reach their {{Term|BotW|Rock Roast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Why?! Why do ya think? To make it easier for us Gorons to get all that tasty rock roast!|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> In addition, there is a rumor that {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} used to use it to fan himself.<ref>{{Cite|There's even a rumor that Daruk... used it to fan himself sometimes.|Bludo|BotW}}</ref>

After appeasing the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}} will allow {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to take the {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker}} from a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in his {{Term|BotW|Boss's House|display=House|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|There's some {{Color|BotW Blue|treasure}} in my house. Grab it. Keep it. It's yours.|Bludo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's a sharp blade that Daruk himself once wielded. I'm sure you'll be able to make good use of it!|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> Despite {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} describing the {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker}} as a sharp {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}, it does not act as such and cannot cut {{Term|BotW|Grass|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker}} is the strongest variant of the {{Term|BotW|Cobble Crusher|link}}. Just like the other {{Term|BotW|Goron}} crushers, it is a two-handed {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} that can be used for both mining and combat. It also has a hidden property which causes their damage output to be doubled when used on the {{Term|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} atop a {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus|link}}. It is capable of immediately destroying {{Plural|BotW|Stone Pebblit|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} about the {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker}}, he tells him to go to the {{Term|BotW|Goron}} blacksmith, {{Term|BotW|Rohan|link}}, if he ever loses it.<ref>{{Cite|If you ever lose the Boulder Breaker, just go talk to Rohan the blacksmith. He'll be happy to help ya out.|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever misplaces or breaks the {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker}}, {{Term|BotW|Rohan}} will offer to craft him a new one in exchange for a {{Term|BotW|Cobble Crusher}}, five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Looks like ya don't have the {{Color|BotW Blue|Boulder Breaker}}... If ya want a new one, I'm gonna need a {{Color|BotW Blue|cobble crusher}}, {{Color|BotW Red|five pieces}} of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}, and a {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}}.|Rohan|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker}} is made of metal, and it will therefore conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if it is equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. However, it is unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. It sinks if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but it can be picked up and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
