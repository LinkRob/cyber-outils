==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Monster Stews can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any meat, any seafood, and [[Monster Extract]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made either by using seafood without status effects, such as {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}}, or by using seafood with contradictory effects. The Monster Extract used in this recipe causes the amount of {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} healed to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} to be random each time it's made. The same principle applies to the strength and duration of any status effects. These stews are known to be savory, despite the ingredients used.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By using certain types of seafood or adding more ingredients, Link can create several variations of Monster Stew:

* '''Hearty Monster Stew''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Monster Stew}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Meat and seafood simmered in monster extract. A savory dish despite its ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. These stews heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts when eaten.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Monster Stew''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Monster Stew}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Meat and seafood simmered in monster extract. A savory dish despite its ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}, or other energizing ingredients. Eating them will refill a part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Monster Stew''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Monster Stew}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Meat and seafood simmered in monster extract. A savory dish despite its ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using enduring ingredients. When eaten, they will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Monster Stew''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Monster Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Meat and seafood simmered in monster extract. A savory dish despite its ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using hasty ingredients. Link can eat these stews to receive an increased movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Monster Stew''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Monster Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Meat and seafood simmered in monster extract. A savory dish despite its ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} or other spicy ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Monster Stew''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Monster Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Meat and seafood simmered in monster extract. A savory dish despite its ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}} or other electric ingredients. Eating these stews will grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Monster Stew''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Monster Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Meat and seafood simmered in monster extract. A savory dish despite its ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, or other mighty ingredients. Link will gain a boost to his attack power if he eats them.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Monster Stew''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Monster Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Meat and seafood simmered in monster extract. A savory dish despite its ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}, or other tough ingredients. Consuming these stews will increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Monster Stew''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Monster Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Meat and seafood simmered in monster extract. A savory dish despite its ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. By eating them, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} will be increased.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Monster Stew''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Monster Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Meat and seafood simmered in monster extract. A savory dish despite its ingredients.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}} or other chilly ingredients. When eaten, these stews will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
