'''''The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D — Prima Official Game Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{MM3D}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 352 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{MM3D}}, as well as a listing of {{Plural|MM3D|Enemy|link}}, {{Plural|MM3D|Boss|link}}, and collectibles.
