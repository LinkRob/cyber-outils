{{Term/Store|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sand-Seal Rental Shop<br/>Southeast Booth|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth}} is a {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}} operated by {{Term|BotW|Frelly|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. It rents {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} native to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert Gateway|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Sand seals}} are animals native to the Gerudo Desert Gateway.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> Its sister shop, the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth|link}} is operated by {{Term|BotW|Frelly}}'s mother, {{Term|BotW|Kohm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That look... Hmm. You must be the Hylian Frelly was talking about. My name is Kohm—I'm Frelly's mother.|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|display=Southeast Booth}} is marked by a large statue of a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} with the word "rental" written beneath it in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)|link|display=Gerudo}}. It costs 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to rent a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} or 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} does not have a {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to traverse the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} very quickly.

{{Term|BotW|Essa|link}} is an employee at the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|display=Southeast Booth}}. {{Term|BotW|Essa}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that since all of the {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} were trained by {{Term|BotW|Frelly}}, they are faster and will not run away from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}} here were all trained by Frelly. They're faster, and they'll wait for you to return to them.|Essa|BotW}}</ref> A young {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} girl named {{Term|BotW|Pearle|link}} likes to check on the {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal|display=Seals}} in the later hours of the morning. Like {{Term|BotW|Patricia|link}}, all the trained {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} have names. At the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|display=Southeast Booth}}, they are named {{Term|BotW|Sue|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nah|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Zarashi|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sue! Nah! Zarashi! Looks like everyone's doing fine!|Pearle|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sue... Nah... Zarashi... They were all so cute today.|Pearle|BotW}}</ref>
