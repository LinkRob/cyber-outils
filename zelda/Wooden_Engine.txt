The {{Term/Store|Wooden Engine||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wooden Engine|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Wooden Engine}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of two {{Plural|ST|Wood Heart|link}}, two {{Plural|ST|Star Fragment|link}} and one {{Term|ST|Dark Pearl Loop|link}}. Its description reads "The retro style of this train is popular with the younger crowd."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Wooden Engine}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Wooden Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts. Using the {{Term|ST|Wooden Engine}} also changes the {{Term|ST|Whistle|link}} to a higher-pitched sound, similar to that of a model train.

{{Term|ST|Alfonzo|link}}'s train, which {{Term|ST|Link|link}} briefly drives during his {{Term|ST|Royal Engineer|link}}'s exam, also includes a {{Term|ST|Wooden Engine}}.
