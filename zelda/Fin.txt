{{Term/Store|Fin||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fin|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Fin}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who can be found camped out on {{Term|BotW|Trilby Plain|link}}, just off the road. He is a member of the {{Plural|BotW|Shield Breakers|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}} gang.<ref>{{Cite|It's cause we're members of the {{Color|BotW Red|shield-surfing}} gang, the Shield Breakers!|Russ|BotW}}</ref> Initially, he is seen scoping out a hill nearby, where he believes {{Term|BotW|Russ|link}} is slacking off.<ref>{{Cite|I bet he's slacking off...|Fin|BotW}}</ref>

Upon talking to {{Term|BotW|Fin}}, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if he is up for {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, maaan! What's that? You're dyin' for some {{Color|BotW Red|shield-surfing}} action, that it?|Fin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, maaan! Back for some more {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}?|Fin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Fin}} points him to his best friend, {{Term|BotW|Russ}}, under a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} on top of the hill.<ref>{{Cite|My best bud is standin' over there beneath that tree! He can show you the ropes.|Fin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My best bud is beneath that tree over there!|Fin|BotW}}</ref> He then wants to know if {{Term|BotW|Link}} will head up.<ref>{{Cite|So you gonna go up there or not, my man?|Fin|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to head up, Fin is excited and remarks that {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} were made for surfing.<ref>{{Cite|Epic! That's the spirit! After all, shield were made to be surfed on!|Fin|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Fin}} accuses him and cramping his vibe and says to come back after he chills out.<ref>{{Cite|What's your DEAL, man? You're really crampin' my vibe right now! Come back after you chill out.|Fin|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} will be automatically transported to the top of the hill when he agrees. Once there, {{Term|BotW|Russ}} apologizes for {{Term|BotW|Fin}} pressuring him into {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}}, and he offers him {{Plural|BotW|Shield}} to buy.<ref>{{Cite|Heeey, man. Nice of you to stop by! Sorry about {{Color|BotW Blue|Fin}} strong-armin' ya into comin' up here, heh.|Russ|BotW}}</ref> After several rounds of {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}}, {{Term|BotW|Fin}} calls {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}} junkie.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, maaan! So you're a {{Color|BotW Red|shield-surfing}} junkie now, eh?|Fin|BotW}}</ref>
