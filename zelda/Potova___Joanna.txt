'''Joanna & Potova''' is a {{Term|TWWHD|Figurines in The Wind Waker|display=Figurine|link}} and a duo of characters in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Main|Potova, Joanna}}
{{Term|TWWHD|Potova & Joanna}} are two girls who attend the {{Term|TWWHD|School of Joy|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Potova}} is blonde, while {{Term|TWWHD|Joanna}} is brown-haired. They repeatedly claim to be good girls,<ref name="Good"/><ref>{{Cite|...WHAT?! We're GOOD girls! We don't spread rumors!|Potova & Joanna|TWW}}</ref> but spread rumors, much like {{Term|TWWHD|Pompie and Vera|link}}. They only do this "accidentally" and for a small fee of two {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, we're good! No matter how much money you give us, we won't tell you anything! Nope! Nothing! That's right! Not even for 2 Rupees!|Potova & Joanna|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, really? You're gonna give us Rupees?... YIPPEE! We each get 1 whole Rupee!|Potova & Joanna|TWW}}</ref> 

Through rumors, they tell {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}}:
*That {{Plural|TWWHD|Pot|link}} are hidden around the {{Plural|TWWHD|Bush|link}} of the Island.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah! We would NEVER tell you about the jars hidden in the grass clumps around the town!|Potova & Joanna|TWW}}</ref>
*That {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle|link}} is imprisoned in the jail.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah! We would never tell you about that strange guy dressed from head to toe in green who's locked up in the town jail! ...But I wonder if he's related to you...|Potova & Joanna|TWW}}</ref>
*The location of the {{Plural|TWWHD|Traveling Merchant|link}} and of their love for {{Term|TWWHD|Zunari|link}}'s shop.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah! We would never tell you about the merchants with the huge backpacks who are on Bomb Island, Greatfish Isle, and the Mother & Child Isles! ...I hear they all think Zunari's shop is the greatest! Isn't that the weirdest?|Potova & Joanna|TWW}}</ref>
*The location of the {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Firefly|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah! We'd never tell you that there's a rainbow-colored firefly far, far away in the Forest Haven!|Potova & Joanna|TWW}}</ref>
*About {{Term|TWWHD|Mrs. Marie|link}}'s {{Term|TWWHD|Private Oasis|link}} and some schooling they did there.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah! We'd never tell you that our teacher, Mrs. Marie, has a super-nice cabana! ...She only took us there for summer school once.|Potova & Joanna|TWW}}</ref>
*Nothing at all and trick him.<ref>{{Cite|Ha ha! ...We're STILL not going to tell you anything! Not even a teensy-tiny rumor!|Potova & Joanna|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yeah... 'Cause we already told you all the rumors that were flying around town!|Potova & Joanna|TWW}}</ref>
