The {{Term/Store|Sword-Fighting Tournament|Sword-Fighting Tournaments|Series, TMC}} is an event in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Say, wasn't there going to be a {{Color|TMC Blue|sword-fighting tournament}} at the castle? I wonder who won this year...|Princess Zelda|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament}} occurs each year at {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}.<ref name="Location"/> The winner of the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|display=Tournament}} is allowed to touch the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whoever wins the competition wins the honor of touching the sacred blade.|Minister Potho|TMC}}</ref>

During {{TMC}}, {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} wins the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|display=Tournament}} in record time,<ref>{{Cite|The winner this year was a new face. I've been wondering where he's from. He used some incredible techniques and won the whole tourney in record time!|Gepper|TMC}}</ref> using the opportunity of the award ceremony to shatter the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}},<ref>{{Cite|Do not interfere with me... As victor, I've earned the right to approach the {{Color|TMC Red|Picori Blade}}... I've been waiting for this moment!|Vaati|TMC}}</ref> causing the evil it had sealed away in the {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest|link}} to return and plague {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}.

Each of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}} won the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament}},<ref>{{Cite|All of these self trained swordsmen have won the fighting tournament at the Picori Festival before. They see great potential in Link.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> and the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|display=Tournament}}'s only recorded stalemate was between {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Smith|link}}, who participated back in their youth.<ref>{{Cite|The king of Hyrule. Reported to have been a fine swordsman, he appeared in the Picori Festival tournament as a youth and fought to a draw with Smith.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>
