==Biography==
{{Term|ZA|Waldensop}} is a man found outside of the {{Term|ZA|White Steed Lodge|link}}, in the {{Term|ZA|Forest of Torian|link}}. He warns {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda|link}} not to go inside, in fear that she will be robbed.<ref>{{Cite|Good morn, lady. A noble person such as yourself on foot-end? If it's rest you seek, you'll get precious little comfort in this thieves' den, I'll tell you. [...] Be careful if you go inside, milady! Some people in these parts would rob you the same as look you, they would.|Waldensop|ZA}}</ref> He then gives {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda}} {{Term|ZA|Aviana|link}}'s [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Feather|Feather]], as a token of his affection.
