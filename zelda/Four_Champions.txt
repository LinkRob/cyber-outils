The {{Term/Store|Four Champions|Four Champions|Series, CoH|plural}} are a group in {{CoH}}.

==Biography==
The {{Plural|CoH|Four Champions}} are {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}}'s minions. Each {{Term|CoH|Four Champions|display=Champion}} is an enchanted {{Term|CoH|Instrument|link}} that is located deep within one of four dungeons. The {{Plural|CoH|Four Champions}} are composed of the {{Plural|CoH|Bass Guitarmos Knights|link}}, a {{Term|CoH|Bass Guitar|link}} enchanted by {{Term|CoH|Fire|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Power of fire, set my {{Color|CoH Blue|guitar}}'s strings ablaze!|Octavo|CoH}}</ref> {{Term|CoH|Gleeokenspiel|link}}, a {{Term|CoH|Glockenspiel}} enchanted by {{Term|CoH|Lightning|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Power of lightning, electrify my {{Color|CoH Blue|glockenspiel}}'s keys!|Octavo|CoH}}</ref> {{Term|CoH|Gohmaracas|link}}, a pair of {{Plural|CoH|Maraca|link}} enchanted by the power of earth,<ref>{{Cite|Power of earth, fill my {{Color|CoH Blue|maracas}} with shaking soil!|Octavo|CoH}}</ref> and {{Term|CoH|Wizzroboe|link}}, an {{Term|CoH|Oboe|link}} enchanted with the power of water.<ref>{{Cite|Power of water, flow through my {{Color|CoH Blue|oboe}} with an icy cold!|Octavo|CoH}}</ref>

The {{Plural|CoH|Four Champions}} were created by {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} in order to combat {{Term|CoH|Ganon|link}} in anticipation of his return.<ref>{{Cite|I prepared my {{Color|CoH Blue|champions}} to face {{Color|CoH Blue|Ganon}}, but things have taken an unexpected turn. I'll be taking back my instruments now.|Octavo|CoH}}</ref>
