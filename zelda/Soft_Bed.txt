The Soft Bed is an [[Echo]] from {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
{{hatnote|”It's easy to just close your eyes and drift away on this soft, well-maintained bed.” — [[Notebook]]<ref name="Soft Bed">{{Cite|It's easy to just close your eyes and drift away on this soft, well-maintained bed.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Soft Bed is an Echo that can be used to bridge gaps and reach otherwise inaccessible areas. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can regain health by sleeping in the Soft bed, or any other bed found in {{EoW|-}}.
