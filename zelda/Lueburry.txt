Lueburry is a key character in {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
Lueburry, a member of the [[Sheikah]] tribe, is the older brother of [[Impa]] and a renowned inventor. He is credited with crafting the [[Sword of Might]], the weapon that was originally used by [[Link]].

He upgrades the Sword of Might, [[Bow of Might]], and [[Bombs of Might]] in exchange for [[Princess Zelda]] giving him [[Might Crystal]]s.
