{{Term/Store|Fresh Kelp|Fresh Kelps|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 124"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Fresh Kelp}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Secret Fortress|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Fresh Kelp}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Secret Fortress}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Fresh Kelp}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Abyss of Agony|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Fresh Kelp}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 250 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Fresh Kelp}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Torrent Robe|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Zora Costume|link}}.<ref name="E 124"/>
