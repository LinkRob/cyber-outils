The {{Term/Store|Diving Game||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a {{Term|OoT3D|Mini-Game|link}} in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to be a master diver, try the {{Color|OoT3D Red|diving game}} at the top of the waterfall! Have you tried it already?|Zora|OoT3D}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Diving Game}} is run by a {{Term|OoT3D|Zora|link}} located at the top of the waterfall in {{Term|OoT3D|Zora's Domain|link}}. This {{Term|OoT3D|Minigame}} is not optional, as it must be completed in order to get the {{Term|OoT3D|Silver Scale|link}}, which will allow {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} to reach the {{Term|OoT3D|Letter in a Bottle|link}}. The {{Term|OoT3D|Diving Game}} costs 20 {{Plural|OoT3D|Rupee|link}} to play, and the {{Term|OoT3D|Zora}} operator throws {{Plural|OoT3D|Rupee}} over the waterfall for him to retrieve. {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} must jump from the waterfall and dive under the {{Term|OoT3D|Water (Terrain)|link}} to retrieve the {{Plural|OoT3D|Rupee}} in 50 seconds. Once the game is completed, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} must return to the {{Term|OoT3D|Zora}} operator to claim the {{Term|OoT3D|Silver Scale}}. After obtaining the {{Term|OoT3D|Silver Scale}}, the prize will become a {{Term|OoT3D|Purple Rupee|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|OoT3D|Diving Game}} is one of the few mandatory {{Plural|Series|Mini-Game|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}, and arguably the only in {{OoT|-}}.
