{{Term/Store|Babi||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Babi|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Babi}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who wields a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}, and she can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She trains some of the other soldiers.<ref>{{Cite|*pant pant* Babi's training is really tough, but... It's our fault the thieves were successful. We have no room to complain.|Marta|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to her, she warns him that they are on high alert and that outsiders must stay out of their way.<ref>{{Cite|Can't you tell that we're on high alert right now?! And no, you can't help! Outsiders must stay out of it!|Babi|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Babi}} while {{Term|BotW|Malena|link}} is asking her for help, {{Term|BotW|Babi}} is exasperated that {{Term|BotW|Link}} and {{Term|BotW|Malena}} still wander into the barracks, despite her warnings.<ref>{{Cite|Despite my warnings, you and this vai here keep wandering into the barracks!|Babi|BotW}}</ref> She warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that they would be in pieces if they were not busy.<ref>{{Cite|Know that the only reason you're not already sliced into pieces is because we are otherwise occupied!|Babi|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} receives permission from {{Term|BotW|Teake|link|display=Captain Teake}} to hear what happened from the soldiers, {{Term|BotW|Babi}} says that she and her soldiers are busy spear training and that {{Term|BotW|Link}} cannot interfere,<ref>{{Cite|As you can see, we are in the middle of spear training!|Babi|BotW}}</ref> even with {{Term|BotW|Teake}}'s permission.<ref>{{Cite|Teake's permission or no, I would ask that you not interfere!|Babi|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Babi}} after recovering the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}, she proclaims that she must prevent a crime like that from happening again.<ref>{{Cite|Now that the chief's heirloom has been returned, we must prevent such a crime from happening again!|Babi|BotW}}</ref> She believes that her diligence will inspire others.<ref>{{Cite|I must never neglect  my training. My diligence will inspire each and every one of those who strive to be like me!|Babi|BotW}}</ref> Due to her strong appearance, {{Term|BotW|Babi}} claims to feel the gazes of those weaker than her.<ref>{{Cite|Heh... My brave, strong appearance... I can feel the gazes of those weaker than I am.|Babi|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Marta|link}} remarks that once their spears are repaired, she believes {{Term|BotW|Babi}} will have more special training from them.<ref>{{Cite|In theory, anyway. More likely, once we finish repairing our spears, we'll have to get started on more special training. {{Small|*sigh* Babi's always got more special training...}}|Marta|BotW}}</ref> She also claims that {{Term|BotW|Babi}} is working too hard, even though the heirloom has been returned, hoping to never be as intense as she is.<ref>{{Cite|Babi is working way too hard, even though the chief's heirloom is back! I hope I never become as intense as her...|Marta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Smaude|link}} also makes similar comments, wondering if {{Term|BotW|Babi}} thinks training without rest proves her discipline and wishing {{Term|BotW|Babi}} could feel how tired she is.<ref name="Tired"/> She thinks {{Term|BotW|Babi}} would train for days on end if no one stopped her.<ref>{{Cite|Babi... If no one stopped her, I think she would keep training for days and days without taking a break.|Smaude|BotW}}</ref> In addition, {{Term|BotW|Smaude}} tries to avoid eye contact with {{Term|BotW|Babi}} so she does not get dragged into her training.<ref>{{Cite|I've gotta avoid eye contact... I don't want to accidentally get dragged into more training.|Smaude|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* {{Term|Series|Babi}} is one of several characters that will hit {{Term|Series|Link}} back if he attacks them with a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You wanna throw down?!|Babi|BotW}}</ref> These attacks deal no damage.
