{{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} found in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, particularly around {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created and used by the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> Due to this, {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} guards commonly wield this {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Spear}} to keep out intruders. Their center of gravity is in their tips, making them difficult for anyone other than skilled {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} warriors to use.<ref name="Inventory"/>

The following {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}} wield {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Spear}}:
* {{Term|BotW|Babi|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Barta|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Bertri|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Dorrah|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Katta|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Kotta|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Laine|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Lashley|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Leena|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Marta|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Merina|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Ploka|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Reeza|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Ripp|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Smaude|link}}

Being metallic {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Spear}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They are also incapable of burning if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. If they are dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Spear}} will sink, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
