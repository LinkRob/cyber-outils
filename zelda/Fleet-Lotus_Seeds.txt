==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Fleet-Lotus Seeds}}
{{Term|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds}} can be found growing in wetlands across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, such as near the [[Ne'ez Yohma Shrine]] and throughout the [[Lanayru Wetlands]]. They restore half of a {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} for {{Plural|BotW|Link|link}} when eaten. They can also be cooked into meals to increase speed.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> When they're burned by {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}, they turn into {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Lotus Seeds|link}}. At the [[Kochi Dye Shop]], they can be used to make Green [[Dye]].
