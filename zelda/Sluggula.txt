{{Term/Store|Sluggula|Sluggula|Series, ALttP, TMC, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 204 {{Exp Game|ALttP, TMC, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|Series|Sluggula}} are yellow slug-like creatures with blue markings, often appearing in small groups.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Plural|ALttP|Sluggula}} in {{ALttP}} appear in {{Term|ALttP|Misery Mire (Dungeon)|link}}, usually in small groups. They have the ability to lay green {{Plural|ALttP|Bomb|link}} that can damage anything nearby, and will usually drop a large number of these while squirming around the room. As such, they are invulnerable to explosives, so will not be harmed by their own {{Plural|ALttP|Bomb}}; however, they are relatively weak and can be dispatched with most other weapons.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Sluggula}}
{{Plural|TMC|Sluggula}} in {{TMC}} appear as normal slugs. They are {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, and only appear in {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Cave|link}}. They move slowly across the floor, leaving a trail of slime as they do so. Some wait on the ceiling for {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to approach their vicinity, dropping down unexpectedly to deal damage. This attack is telegraphed by the shadow of the falling {{Term|TMC|Sluggula}} on the floor. After falling, they remain on their backs for a short time, exposing a more vulnerable underbelly that will allow {{Term|TMC|Link}} to dispatch them in a single strike from the {{Term|TMC|Sword|link}}.
