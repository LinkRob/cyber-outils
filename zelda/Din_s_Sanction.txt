'''Din's Sanction''' is a [[Key Item]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Din's Sanction|A mystical sanction entrusted to you by the goddess Din, given only to those who have proven their power.<ref name="Din's Sanction">{{Cite|A mystical sanction entrusted to you by the goddess Din, given only to those who have proven their power.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Din’s Sanction is a [[Key Item]] obtained after defeating [[Volvagia]] in the [[Eldin Temple]]. Following the battle, the goddess [[Din]] appears to [[Princess Zelda]], thanking her for purifying [[Eldin Volcano]] and granting her the Sanction as a mark of power. It is one of the three blessings required to open the path to the [[Triforce|Prime Energy]].
