==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Hinox Toenails are {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by all types of {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} when they're killed. They're as thick as a plate of armor and can be [[Cooking|cooked]] with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> Alternatively, they can be sold to [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] for [[Mon]] or used as an ingredient for Brown [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

Each piece of the [[Fierce Deity Set]] requires five Hinox Toenails, as well as a dragon scale, to be upgraded to their first tier by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}.

* Total for '''Fierce Deity Set''': 15 (5, 0, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
