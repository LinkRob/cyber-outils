{{Compendium/Extract|Soldier's Broadsword}}
{{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use to defeat {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, destroy {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Jar|link}}, and cut down {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword}} will usually deal 14, but that increases to 28 damage when they are thrown and broken. They were once brandished by those who once defended {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} prior to the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref name="Inventory" /> They are considered a good and durable {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} to use against {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}}.<ref name="Inventory" /> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find them commonly in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword}} can be found planted in the ground of the cave located behind the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}} that feeds the {{Term|BotW|River of the Dead|link}}.

Scanning the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|SSB|Young Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|SSB|Toon Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ}} Series {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} ({{TLoZ}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|LA|Link|link}} ({{LA}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Toon Link}} ({{TWW}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|TP|Link|link}} ({{TP}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|SS|Link|link}} ({{SS}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Link}} (Rider) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

{{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword}} are made of metal, and they will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. This also means that they will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword}} will not burn near {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which were all utilized by the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}} prior to the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref> A {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword}} is part of an unnamed set of equipment used by {{Term|BotW|Link}} in promotional media, alongside a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Bow|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Shield|link}}.
