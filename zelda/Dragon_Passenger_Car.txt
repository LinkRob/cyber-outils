The {{Term/Store|Dragon Passenger Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dragon Passenger Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Dragon Passenger Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Pirate Necklace|link}}, three {{Plural|ST|Dark Pearl Loop|link}} and eight {{Plural|ST|Demon Fossil|link}}. Its description reads "The strange design of this carriage is appealing, but would you ride in it?"

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Dragon Passenger Car}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Dragonhead Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
