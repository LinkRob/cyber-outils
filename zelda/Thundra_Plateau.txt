{{Term/Store|Thundra Plateau||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|Thundra Plateau|Map|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau}} is located in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}. Aptly named, it is constantly under the effects of a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau}} is filled with pockets of shallow {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} all throughout, with an island in the middle. Large, mushroom-like {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} fill the area, similar to {{Term|BotW|Ludfo's Bog|link}}, which the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau}} is connected to.

The {{Term|BotW|Toh Yahsa Shrine|link}} is available through a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|Trial of Thunder|link}}", that must be completed in {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau}}. Once the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} has been completed, the {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau}} will stop storming.

==Nomenclature==
Thundra comes from thunder.
