The {{Term/Store|Raft Shop||Series, LANS}} is a location in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Raft Shop}}|N/A|LANS}}</ref> It is a small shop which can be reached by using the [[Hookshot]] to the east of [[Tal Tal Heights]], or by using a cave north of the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine|link}}. In the shop, [[Link]] can rent a [[Raft]] for 100 [[Rupees]] and begin the [[Rapids Ride]].<ref>{{Cite|Want to go on a raft ride for a hundred Rupees?|Raft Shop Keeper|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Okay, the raft is ready for you outside! Enjoy! |Raft Shop Keeper|LADX}}</ref>
