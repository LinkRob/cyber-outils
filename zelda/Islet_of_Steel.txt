The {{Term/Store|Islet of Steel||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (E2) is one of the [[Great Sea]] islands in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features==

The Islet of Steel is one of the smallest islands in the Great Sea. [[Link]] can retrieve the first [[Triforce Chart]] inside this islet by shooting down the [[Warship]]s that patrols the front entrance of the steel-covered fortress, encompassing the islet. Once inside the steel-fortress islet, Link will find a small shrine with a Triforce Symbol etched to it, where he must play the [[Wind's Requiem]] to make a [[Treasure Chest]] appear that holds the Triforce Chart within it.

The Islet of Steel is one of the few islands where there are no surfaces for Link to walk on, not counting the interior.  Other such islands include the archipelagos, like [[Star Belt Archipelago]], [[Five-Star Isles]], and [[Seven-Star Isles]].
