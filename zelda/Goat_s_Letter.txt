The {{Term/Store|Goat's Letter||Series, LANS}} is an item in {{LA}}.{{Name Ref|LANS}}{{Note|{{Term|Series|Goat's Letter}} was referred to as the {{Term/Store|Letter||LA, LADX}} in all versions of {{LA}} prior to {{LA|NS}}. However, as this contradicts the name given in {{LA|NS|-}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
[[File:LANS Goat's Letter Text Icon.png|left]]
The Goat's Letter is the ninth item in the [[Trading Quest]] in {{LA}}. It is received from [[Christine]] the [[Goat]] in [[Animal Village]] after giving her the [[Hibiscus]].<ref name="E 129"/> She gives [[Link]] the task of delivering the Goat's Letter to [[Mr. Write]] in the western section of [[Goponga Swamp]].<ref>{{Cite|I would like you to take this letter to a Mr. Write who lives on the border of the Mysterious Forest, please!|Christine|LADX}}</ref> As thanks for his efforts, Mr. Write gives Link his [[Broom]] in exchange.<ref name="E 129"/> The Goat's Letter also includes a [[Photograph]] signed by [[Christine]], which is actually just a Photograph of [[Princess Peach]] from the [[mariowiki:Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]].<ref>{{Cite|You got a Broom as your reward from Mr. Write! But that photo was not of...|N/A|LADX}}</ref>
