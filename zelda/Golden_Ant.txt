==Location and Uses==
[[File:Ant (M).png|left]]
[[File:Ant (F).png|left|47px]]
The male Golden Ant can be found under the [[Tree]] next to the entrance to [[King Zora]]'s tomb in the [[Kakariko Village Graveyard]]. The female Golden Ant is located inside a house in front of the hotel in [[Kakariko Village]]. They can be given to [[Agitha]] in exchange for different items, such as a bigger [[Wallet]], [[Rupee]]s, or her [[Miiverse Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}. If collected a second time, they will drop a [[Heart]].

Golden Ants live next to Hylian homes where they can find food. Females tend to be larger, while males have wings.<ref name= "Golden Ant"/>
