==Location and Uses==
{{Term|MM3D|Rustling Grass}} appears in groups of nine and will animate and jump around several times when {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} cuts a tuft of it. After a few jumps, {{Term|MM3D|Rustling Grass}} will destroy itself, sending stray blades of {{Term|MM3D|Grass|link}} flying about. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} is wearing the {{Term|MM3D|Keaton Mask|link}} while disturbing the {{Term|MM3D|Rustling Grass|display=Grass}}, a {{Term|MM3D|Keaton|link}} will appear and quiz the young hero.<ref>{{Cite|It seems {{Color|MM3D Red|Keatons}}, the ghost foxes, play tricks in the {{Color|MM3D Red|grass}}...|Gossip Stone|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Answer me this... Pick one of the three choices...|Keaton|MM}}</ref>

{{Term|MM3D|Rustling Grass}} always drop one {{Term|MM3D|Green Rupee|link}} when slashed, but they will not drop anything if they destroy themselves. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can destroy all the tufts of {{Term|MM3D|Rustling Grass}} before they disappear, they will drop a single {{Term|MM3D|Red Rupee|link}}.

{{Term|MM3D|Rustling Grass}} can be found only at {{Term|MM3D|Milk Road|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Mountain Village|link}}, in {{Term|MM3D|North Clock Town|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I've seen a real Keaton near the slide!|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|MM}}</ref> and in the grove in the {{Term|MM3D|Portal|link}} at the beginning of the game.
