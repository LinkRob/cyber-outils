'''Sea Island''' is a location in {{ZA}}. It is a small island in the sea of {{Term|ZA|Tolemac|link}}, where the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Water}} is located.{{Name Ref|ZA}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|ZA|Sea Island}} is a small, blue island to the southeast of {{Term|ZA|Tolemac|link}}. Aside from a small river that cuts through the island, it is largely featureless and its surface is completely uninhabited—no people or monsters can be found here. It is, however, home to a cave which leads to the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Water|link}}, where {{Term|ZA|Agwanda|link}} and various other monsters reside.

The island can only be reached by taking a {{Term|ZA|Raft|link}} from the {{Term|ZA|Seacoast Plain|link}} port town. However, as the coast's wind has died down, the seafarer owning the Raft requires that {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda|link}} bring her own in the {{Term|ZA|Items in Zelda's Adventure|section=Vial of Winds|display=Vial of Winds|link}},<ref>{{Cite|If you wish to cross the sea, bring us twenty Rubies and a Vial of Wind. The offshore wind has died down, and you must bring your own breeze.|Rafter|ZA}}</ref> in addition to paying 20 {{Plural|ZA|Rupee|link}}. While crossing the sea to the island, Zelda fights a {{Term|ZA|Sea Monster|link}}, and must defeat it to proceed.
