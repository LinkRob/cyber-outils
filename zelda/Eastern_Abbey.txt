The {{Term/Store|Eastern Abbey||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Eastern Abbey'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Eastern Abbey}} is a ruin surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij Shrine|link}} on the eastern edge of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Eastern Abbey}} contains several broken down {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}, which can be looted for {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}. 3 of these reveal to be [[Guardian|Decayed Guardians]] and will attack Link. The formal entryway to the {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij Shrine}} is blocked by a {{Term|BotW|Cracked Tile|link}} wall.

A pond on the northwest side of the {{Term|BotW|Eastern Abbey}} holds a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that contains an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}}, which can be retrieved with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}.

The wall of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}} on the eastern side of the {{Term|BotW|Eastern Abbey}} has broken apart and given way to a recess that holds an iron door. By moving the door with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can uncover a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}} which hides a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} underneath.

If you've bought DLC Pack 1, a metallic EX chest on top of one of the ruin walls will be available. This chest gives the [[Nintendo Switch Shirt]].
