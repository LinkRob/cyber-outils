{{Term/Store|StreetPass Meadow||Series, ALBW}} is a location in {{ALBW}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Meadow}} is located on the western side of {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}}, where {{Term|ALBW|Gramps|link}} can be found. When {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} arrives at the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Meadow|display=Meadow}}, any newly-collected {{Plural|Series|StreetPass|link|display=StreetPasses}} are displayed in the form of {{Plural|ALBW|Sign|link}}, with blinking lights indicating an unread {{Term|ALBW|Sign}}.<ref>{{Cite|Looks like there's a sign up for a new Shadow Link you tagged via StreetPass. It's blinking green.|Gramps|ALBW}}</ref> Each {{Term|ALBW|Sign}} shows the location, bounty, name, and number of achievements of its corresponding {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}},<ref>{{Cite|It has information about the Shadow Links you tagged, so you should go look at it sometime.|Gramps|ALBW}}</ref> with each {{Term|ALBW|Sign}}'s physical appearance also representing the {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}}'s bounty.

{{Plural|ALBW|Sign}} will only appear for {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link}} waiting in locations that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} has already visited. If there are more {{Plural|Series|StreetPass|display=StreetPasses}} in queue than seen locations, their {{Plural|ALBW|Sign}} will appear once another {{Term|ALBW|Sign}} is removed (by either [[Shadow Link Battle|battling]] or banishing the {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}}) or after {{Term|ALBW|Link}} visits other locations and returns to the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Meadow}}.
