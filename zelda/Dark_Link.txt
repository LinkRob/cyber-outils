{{Term/Store|Dark Link|Dark Links|Series, ALttP, OoT, OoT3D, OoA, ST}}, originally named '''[[Link's Shadow]]''', is a recurring antagonist in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is a mysterious, shadowy monster strongly resembling [[Link]] himself and notoriously known to mirror his every fighting ability (except for [[Item|items]]). This foe makes its first appearance in {{TAoL}}.

==Characteristics==
Dark Link is the evil reflection of [[Link]], the main protagonist, which is shared with [[Shadow Link]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 173 {{Exp Game|ALttP, OoT, OoT3D, OoA, ST}}}}</ref> He is one of the most enigmatic [[enemy]] characters in the {{TLoZ|Series}}, usually appearing with no backstory and no dialogue, although it is implied on numerous occasions to be merely constructed of [[darkness]] [[magic]], usually by [[Ganon]]. One of the most infamous and arguably the most dangerous enemy in the franchise, Dark Link is more than just a mere battle against a monster for Link, but a battle against ''himself'', as the young hero has to face his own strength turned against him.<ref>{{Cite|'''Dark Link'''<br/>Conquer yourself!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> Because of this, he is often the ultimate challenge when encountered, and in [[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|one case]], is even fought as the [[Final Boss]]. Dark Link himself only appears in a few installments where he is directly battled against by his counterpart.

As Link's doppelgänger, Dark Link bears a resemblance to the Link of whichever game he appears in. He wears the same clothing as the hero: a tunic and a long, floppy cap, though the similarities to Link end there. His entire outfit is black or dark gray instead of green, although in the final challenge of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|A Link to the Past & Four Swords]]'', he can be seen in said color, along with red, blue, and violet. Moreover, his hair is usually either a shadowy black or a dark gray. In {{TAoL|-}}, however, he is entirely black. Expectedly, the enemy also wields a [[Sword]] and [[Shield]] (apparently the [[Master Sword]] and [[Hylian Shield]] in most cases) just like the hero. Dark Link tends to be left-handed like Link. However, in some appearances (particularly when multiple copies of him appear), he has been shown to be [[wikipedia:Ambidexterity|ambidextrous]]. Another notable feature is his bright red eyes, which are usually the only prominent feature against his dark visage. Though Dark Link tends to be silent, very few encounters, specifically {{OoT}}, have himself perform grunt and yell vocalizations as Link does.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
[[File:TAoL Final Battle.png|left|thumb|253x253px|The final battle of {{TAoL|-}}, where Link must face and defeat his shadow self in a one-on-one showdown, as the final test to gain the Triforce of Courage.]]

Dark Link, known in-game as [[Link's Shadow]],<ref>{{Cite Book|book=''NES Game Atlas''|publisher=Nintendo of America|page=74}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|book=''The Legend of Zelda: Collector's Edition'' Player's Guide|page=42|publisher=Nintendo of America}}</ref> was introduced in {{TAoL}}. Here, unlike later games, he is the [[Final Boss]], and is fought in the [[Great Palace]] after defeating [[Thunderbird]]. Dark Link appears in the final room where it suddenly turns dark with a violet background and he materializes from Link's body, and then the final battle against his doppelgänger takes place. Dark Link is very agile, as he dodges, blocks, and jumps easily from Link's attacks, while aggressively swinging and thrusting his sword at the hero, rarely dropping his guard. After Link manages to defeat his dark self, Dark Link ultimately falls through the floor and disappears, and Link is awarded the [[Triforce of Courage]] by a [[Sage]] who has been guarding it at the palace. The hero would then use the power of the united [[Triforce]] to help awaken [[Princess Zelda]] from her deep slumber, ending the game. 

There is an infamous strategy in the final battle against Dark Link to make the duel significantly easier, where if Link were to crouch and constantly attack by the left corner, his dark counterpart will continuously jump and run into his attacks, easily landing hits. However, this is actually a flaw in the enemy's A.I. in the international [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] releases only, where it is simplified from the original Japanese version in the [[wikipedia:Famicom_Disk_System|Famicom Disk System]]. Therefore, the trick cannot be done in that release.

==={{OoT|-}}===

[[File:Dark Link OoT.jpg|thumb|287x287px|Link engaging Dark Link in a fierce duel inside the Water Temple in {{OoT|-}}]]
After an 11-year hiatus in the series, Dark Link returns in {{OoT}} as a [[Sub-Boss]] in the [[Water Temple]], where his well-known name is established for the franchise. In a large, mysterious room with no apparent walls and a dead tree in the center, Dark Link materializes from Link's reflection in the water to confront his good counterpart once the hero reaches the other end and then returns to said tree. Dark Link reacts to most of Link's movements and attacks, usually defending himself in the process.<ref name="OoT Prima 83">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT|publisher= Prima|page= 83}}</ref> Dark Link has the same amount of health as Link himself; the number of [[Heart Container]]s the hero has equals the number of hits with the [[Master Sword]] it takes to defeat his doppelgänger. When hit, Dark Link will lean forward in pain, fall through the floor and then respawns afterwards, from behind (reusing his defeat animation from ''The Adventure of Link''). At the beginning of the battle, Dark Link is nearly transparent, but will gradually become opaque as the battle progresses. During this phase, he simply stands in front of Link and will not parry or dodge until the hero makes a move. When the enemy is fully opaque (after 20 seconds), he will become more aggressive and actively attack Link instead of simply reacting to his moves. Dark Link also reacts to Link's every swing of his sword even if he is not facing against his counterpart.

Although Dark Link rarely drops his guard, there are a variety of strategies for defeating him, such as stunning the foe with [[Deku Nut]]s, casting [[Din's Fire]], striking him with the [[Megaton Hammer]], or any of Link's sword attacks, including the [[Biggoron's Sword]], with or without [[Targeting|Z-targeting]]. It is possible to strike Dark Link more easily if Link swings his sword the opposite direction of his shield when targeting at close range. Notoriously, if Link performs a forward thrust, regardless of any [[Sword]] that is currently equipped, Dark Link will simply hop onto the blade for a brief moment (or attacking if aggressive) and Link's movements will freeze in place, leaving the protagonist vulnerable. If the hero attempts to [[Jump Slash|Jump Attack]], his dark self will side-jump to dodge, immediately following with the same attack of his own (when aggressive). Dark Link will also avoid the [[Spin Attack]] with backflips. Once Dark Link is defeated, he falls to the ground and vanishes, and the battlefield slowly reveals itself as a wide enclosed room, suggesting the environment when fighting Dark Link was only an illusion. Link then is able to enter the locked room containing the [[Longshot]], an upgrade of the [[Hookshot]].<ref name="OoT Prima 83"/>

Dark Link shares the [[Hero of Time]]'s voice, but it is slowed down and distorted, giving a demonic feel.

==={{OoA|-}}===
During the second phase of the final battle, [[Veran]] creates four clones of Dark Link that mimic Link's movement in reverse and act as dangerous obstacles. Unlike other appearances of Dark Link, these clones do not attack, and are useful in recovering health, as they are easily destroyed and drop one [[Heart]] each. They are similar in behavior to [[Arm-Mimic]]s and [[Mask-Mimic]]s.

=== {{ALttP&FS|-}} ===
[[File:Dark Links.gif|The four Dark Links in {{ALttP&FS|-}}|left|frameless]]In the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|Game Boy Advance remake of {{ALttP|-}}]], after completing both the main story of {{aLttP}} and the multiplayer ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords|Four Swords]]'' game, the [[Palace of the Four Sword]] is unlocked. The [[Boss|Dungeon Master]] is four Dark Links, each with a unique [[tunic]], corresponding to the colors of the [[Links]] that hold the [[Four Sword]]. Reaching the final room of the dungeon causes the four different colored [[Four Sword]]s to rise into the air, and following this the Dark Links will drop from above. Each Dark Link has slightly different abilities, but, much like in {{TAoL|-}} and {{OoT|-}}, they all aggressively attack with sword swipes and do not open their defenses often, requiring careful timing to land hits. They are quite agile, able to leap backwards out of the way of Link's attacks and can even temporarily vanish to dodge the hero as well (similar to Link's use of the [[Magic Cape]]). They are fought one at a time, and the doppelgängers get progressively more difficult; The green Dark Link uses the [[Pegasus Boots]] to suddenly dash at Link. The red Dark Link uses the [[Great Spin Attack|Hurricane Spin]] and the Pegasus Boots. The blue Dark Link also uses Pegasus Boots and the Hurricane Spin, and he can swiftly leap into the air to perform a [[Down Thrust]] (similar to Link's use of the [[Quake Medallion]]), which can inflict three [[Heart Container|Heart Containers]] of damage and prove difficult to dodge. The purple Dark Link is the final Dark Link, and utilizes the Pegasus Boots, Hurricane Spin, Down Thrust, and the [[Sword Beam]]. Every next Dark Link in order will use the special moves the previous one uses more frequently. Defeating each Dark Link will ultimately break the curse on the Four Sword and return it to normal, completing the game entirely.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Main|Interlopers}}
Dark Link makes a cameo appearance during a cutscene in {{TP}} that tells the tale of how the [[Twili]] race was banished to the [[Twilight Realm]]. Portraying the powerful [[Dark Interlopers]] who sought to control the [[Sacred Realm]], three clones of Dark Link manage to destroy Link (portraying a common citizen) before they are banished to the Twilight Realm by the [[Light Spirits]].

==={{ST|-}}===
Dark Link appears as the final challenge of the [[Take 'Em All On!]] mini-game in [[Hyrule Castle Town|Castle Town]]. In this appearance, he attacks using a blade as dark as himself, that subsequently leaves a fitting black trail, and he can also place [[Bomb]]s and shoot arrows. He can also perform a jump attack like Link, only his is higher and much slower, giving the hero time to dodge. Dark Link also mimics Link's sword attacks when he himself is not attacking, so Link must block his attacks and then counterattack. The music played when fighting him is the same as the track used when Link the Hero of Winds chased the [[Ghost Ship]] during the second phase against [[Bellum]] in {{PH}}. Conversely to his appearance in {{OoT|-}}, he slowly fades from a pure black to a translucent gray as he takes damage, and once defeated, he falls over on his back and disappears, with the dark hero's eyes eerily left open during the whole animation. The room he is fought in is the only one which contains [[Heart]]s, despite [[Joynas]]' earlier warning that there is no way to recover health in the challenge rooms.<ref>{{Cite|However, you'll find nothing to restore your health inside. Nothing at all!|Joynas|ST}}</ref>

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, the [[Dark Series]] is an [[Armor Set]] that gives Link the appearance of Dark Link, as he appeared in {{OoT|-}}. Wearing the full Set grants Link the Night Speed Up [[Set Bonus]].

==={{TotK|-}}===
In {{TotK}}, the Dark Series returns as Armor that can be purchased at Bargainer Statues found throughout [[The Depths]].

==Spinoff appearances==
{{Ambig|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, Dark Link appears as a mini-boss in the "[[A War of Spirit]]" [[Scenario]]. He is summoned by [[Cia]] by channeling the darker side of Link's personality into physical form, appearing as a completely dark version of him with glowing red eyes. After the first one is defeated, several more copies are summoned which attack Link and the [[Hyrulean Forces]]. Link fights them all with the confidence that he can easily overcome them, but quickly becomes overwhelmed, and is saved by his allies who defeat and dispel the Dark Links.

In the ''Master Quest'' DLC pack, Dark Link appears again in the [[Cia's Tale]] Scenario, "[[Darkness Falls]]". When Cia's dark magic begins to steadily unhinge her mind, she begins summoning several Dark Links to defend herself from Ganondorf's Forces, under the delusion that Link will come to protect her. However, by the time Zelda's Forces reach her stronghold, the Dark Links appeared to have all been vanquished in battle.

In {{HW|L}} amd {{HW|DE}}, the Dark Links make one final appearance in the [[Legend Mode#Wind Waker|Wind Waker]] Scenario, "[[Reclaiming the Darkness]]". They are summoned forth by [[Dark Cia]] to capture the real Cia. However, they and Dark Cia are once more vanquished by Link and his allies.

Two of Cia's combo attacks with the [[Scepter]] involve summoning a circle of Dark Links around her to take out nearby enemies.

A Dark Link [[Costume]] is also available to use when playing as Link for players who buy the ''Hero of Hyrule'' DLC season pass, or by purchasing all of the packs included in it. The Costume will also turn Link's Weapons into the same color scheme; [[Epona]] and the [[Great Fairy]] will change color and have glowing red eyes. As Dark Link was spawned from Link's shadow, he himself does not cast one.

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
As in {{BotW|-}}, Link can don the Dark Armor set to take on the guise of the Hero of Time's incarnation of Dark Link, though its pieces are obtained in separate challenge missions. The Dark Hood is obtained in Questionable Escort Mission, the Dark Trousers in Fire Hazards and the Dark Tunic in Unnatural Disaster. Unlike in {{BotW|-}}, the costume is merely cosmetic and does not provide any bonuses when wearing the full set.

==Crossover appearances==

==={{SSB|-}} series===
===={{SSBM|-}}====
[[File:SSBM Dark Link.png|thumb|left|Event Match 18, "Link's Adventure"  in {{SSBM|-}}]]
Dark Link appears in the Event Match "[[Link's Adventure]]", where the player takes control of Link on the [[Hyrule Temple]] stage. In this appearance, Dark Link is designed after his appearance from {{TAoL|-}} but shares the same behavior as Link. Dark Link can be defeated by KO'ing him twice.

===={{SSBB|-}}====
[[File:Dark link3.png|thumb|right|200px|Dark Link, as an alternate costume for Link, in {{SSBB|-}}]]
In {{SSBB}}, a Link and [[Toon Link]] are able to take on the appearance of Dark Link as an alternate costume, something that cannot be done in {{SSBM|-}}. Link's costume mostly resembles the one featured in the {{TP|-}} cutscene, being mostly gray and black with red eyes, while Toon Link's is based on [[Shadow Link]] as he appears in {{FSA|-}}.

Dark Link is fought by Link in the event match "[[Dark Link Duel]]" on the [[Bridge of Eldin]] stage. It is a homage to the Link's Adventure event from {{SSBM|-}}, since the Dark Link here is entirely black, unlike Link's alternate costume based on Dark Link, and is similarly numbered the eighteenth event match. As in {{SSBM|-}}, Link is the only option to fight against Dark Link. However, unlike {{SSBM|-}}, this battle is fought as a Stamina match, making him more like a traditional {{TLoZ|-}} enemy.

Dark Link is also fought during the seventh co-op event, [[Battle of the Dark Sides]], which is similar but the second player takes control of Samus while Dark Link is accompanied by Dark Samus, an entity that, like Dark Link, is completely black, instead of resembling the identically named character of the ''Metroid'' series.

===={{SSB4|-}}====
Link and Toon Link return their Dark Link costumes in {{SSB4}}.

===={{SSBU|-}}====
Link, Toon Link and also [[Young Link]] can take on the appearance of Dark Link as alternate costumes in {{SSBU}}. While Link is based on his {{BotW|-}} appearance, Link's costume does not resemble the [[Dark Series]], instead giving Link dark gray skin and glowing red eyes while wearing a black [[Wild Set]]. Young Link's costume also gives him dark gray skin and glowing red eyes while wearing a black [[Kokiri Tunic]] and black [[Kokiri Boots]].

==Trivia==
*Due to [[Link]] himself being based on the [https://disney.fandom.com/wiki/Peter_Pan_(character) Disney version of Peter Pan] according to [[Shigeru Miyamoto]], Dark Link was presumably modeled after Peter Pan's shadow.
*As he is the dark version of the main protagonist of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]], as well as being one of the most mysterious and difficult opponents, Dark Link is seen as an iconic villainous character among the community, especially for his appearance in {{OoT}}.
*In his appearance in {{OoT|-}}, Dark Link appears under a dead, blackened tree. This may serve as a contrast to the living green trees that surrounded the [[Hero of Time]] in his youth at [[Kokiri Forest]] or the [[Lost Woods]], and to emphasize the light/darkness scenario.
**Upon passing the dead tree, Link's shadow disappears before Dark Link materializes, suggesting that Dark Link is indeed his shadow, much like in {{TAoL}}.
*As seen through ROM hacking in {{OoT|-}}, it is possible for Dark Link to take fall damage with his own voice grunt. However, Link himself will actually take the damage.
*Dark Link in {{OoT|-}} can be made to imitate most of Link's animations. Stunning Dark Link with [[Deku Nut]]s in a specific way will cause him to mirror Link's every move. This can be used to a comical effect. For example, if Link is down to low health and leans over panting, Dark Link will also act fatigued. The dark hero also mimics Link's idle animations, such as checking his sword and tunic. If Link puts on the [[Iron Boots]], Dark Link will start similarly moving as if he were also wearing them.
**In {{OoT3D}}, Dark Link will instead slide towards Link on one leg, frozen in a single frame of his running animation.{{Fact}}<!--Vague statement. How does this occur?-->
**The foe can get hit with the standard stun animation as regular enemies, though this is a rare occurrence.
*Contrary to belief by some fans, Dark Link in ''Ocarina of Time'' does not become more opaque by taking damage at the start of the battle. If one were to stand still and look directly at the enemy in first-person view, it is possible to see his seemingly transparent body slowly become darker until he turns fully opaque and begins to truly fight Link.
*Dark Link's model in {{OoT|-}}, due to its basic textures, appears to be a more simplified version of Link's with some minor differences as he lacks ears, has no facial expression, his tunic is more "skintight", and lacks gauntlets. Additionally, his sword and shield are part of his limbs. This is due to the graphical limitations of the [[Nintendo 64]].
**In {{OoT3D|-}}, because of the [[Nintendo 3DS]] hardware, Dark Link is significantly more properly detailed and textured than in the original N64 release, granting him an appearance similar to the [[Dark Interlopers]] visual aides from {{TP}}. When defeated, he now makes the same sound effect that Link normally makes when knocked to the ground rather than simply screaming.
*Despite being called Link's own shadow, Dark Link in games where he battles against his counterpart is a physical body and indeed has collision detection.
*Due to the enemy's A.I., Dark Link in {{OoT|-}} moves toward Link in the same manner as he is using the [[Targeting|Z-Targeting]] and almost always backflips or rolls when he dodges or if the hero gets too close in his passive state. If Link gets very far away, Dark Link will begin running normally (in a straight line) until he gets close enough to be in range.[[File:OOT Dark Link unused shield block.png|thumb|273x273px|Dark Link's [[Unused Content|unused]] shield behavior in {{OoT|-}}]]
*If Link does not have any [[Shield]] equipped while facing Dark Link in {{OoT|-}}, his dark counterpart will still have his [[Hylian Shield]]-like shield. It is possible to fight and defeat him without such, though this greatly increases the difficulty.
**Oddly, Dark Link himself does not use his own shield to block Link's attacks during the duel, as he relies on his sword to deflect the hero's strikes, similar to [[Ganondorf]] in {{TWW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''. Despite this, however, the enemy AI programming of the dark hero defending himself does exist; If Link were to use [[Nayru's Love]] (not possible under normal circumstances), Dark Link will constantly attempt to shield from his counterpart but remains mostly crouching where he is standing until he gets hit or the spell wears off.
*During his battle in {{OoT|-}}, [[Din's Fire]] as well as the [[Megaton Hammer]] during his virtually transparent stage, will instantly land hits on Dark Link, as he is unable to defend against those attacks, making the duel significantly easier. However, some fans discourage this tactic, as those are viewed as rather cheap methods to defeat him. This is because Dark Link himself is intended to test the player's skills.
**Similarly, the strategy of fighting Dark Link by the west corner in {{TAoL|-}} is also frowned upon for the same reasons.
*In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of {{aLttP}}, the Dark Links have the same sword and shield that Link currently has equipped, but they will always inflict the same amount of damage. If Link does not have any shield, his dark counterparts will have the Fighter's Shield.
*There is a [[Glitches in Ocarina of Time|glitch]] for Dark Link in {{OoT|-}} that if Link were to face away from his dark counterpart and is also far away, and then [[Jump Slash|Jump Attack]] frequently, he will become unable to attack the hero and Link can freely land hits. A telltale sign of this is the enemy will carry his shield in a lowered position and his running animation resembles when Link is unarmed.
*In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', if Link were to grab a Super Mushroom and becomes giant, his voice becomes identical to Dark Link from {{OoT|-}}.
*Strangely, although Link and Dark Link share the same amount of health in {{OoT|-}} based on how many [[Heart Container]]s the former has, the amount of damage they inflict is not. Whenever Dark Link hits Link with his [[Master Sword]]-like weapon, he loses half a Heart. However, as the number of hits with the Master Sword to defeat his counterpart matches the number of Hearts, this suggests the actual damage Link delivers to Dark Link is roughly the same as one ''full'' Heart Container. This is presumably for balance reasons.
*Much like Link himself, in {{TAoL|-}}, the sound of Dark Link [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/AOL/AOL_Hurt.wav getting hit] is taken from [https://castlevania.fandom.com/wiki/Simon_Belmont Simon Belmont] from [[Wikipedia:Konami|Konami]]'s ''[https://castlevania.fandom.com/wiki/Castlevania_(video_game) Castlevania]''.
**Simon himself is a playable fighter in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
*Due to the high level of challenge when facing Dark Link and the fact he mimics Link's every move, many fans find his battles as a way of simulating fighting against other players
**In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', [[Shadow Link]], a similar enemy, can take the place of a second player in the [[StreetPass|Streetpass]] duels against Link.
*Although normally Dark Link in {{OoT|-}} falls through the floor and then suddenly appears directly behind Link whenever he is struck by the hero, if the evil shadow were to stand by a corner of the room where the battle takes place when hit, he will respawn by the dead tree.
*In {{OoT|-}}, as Dark Link's hit points is the same as Link's, if the player were to perform the [[Three Heart Challenge]], the battle becomes very short as it will only take 3 hits to defeat the dangerous enemy (though Link himself is at constant risk of easily getting a [[Game Over]]). On the other hand, having maximum 20 Heart Containers would make the duel become rather an endurance match.
*[[mariowiki:Shadow_Mario|Shadow Mario]] from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' series]] can be seen as a parallel to Dark Link, as this character is an antagonistic doppelgänger to [[Mario]] known to mimic his every move (although it is technically [[mariowiki:Bowser_Jr.|Bowser Jr.]] in disguise).
*In {{BotW}}, a Dark Link [[Armor]] Set is available for purchase at [[Kilton]]'s [[Fang and Bone]] shop after completing all four [[Divine Beast]]s.
*In the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|{{OoT|-}}]] manga, unlike his game appearances where Dark Link doesn't speak, the evil shadow of the hero does talk in this media.
*Perhaps humorously, in the French localization, Dark Link is known as '''Black Link'''.
*Contrary to popular belief, Dark Link, Shadow Link, or even Ganondorf are not the only vulnerable enemies known to mirror Link's fighting skills. A very powerful character in ''A Link Between Worlds'' named [[Gramps]], like the hero's evil shadow, matches his abilities, but more directly, as he in comparison uses all of the possible [[Upgrading|upgraded]] items and gear available to Link including the [[Master Sword Lv3|Master Sword Lv.3]] and maximum 20 Heart Containers, making himself even more deadly. However, unlike Link's shadow, Gramps is not required to be defeated to complete the game, as he is entirely optional.
*He is sometimes referred to as "Evil Link" by some fans of the series.
*[[Fierce Deity Link]] from {{MM}} has a minor similarity to Dark Link in that it is Link himself under a dark form, although he does not wield both a sword and shield.
**Some fans, however, have wished to see Link as his Fierce Deity form battle Dark Link in a future ''Zelda'' game.
*The [[Echo Link]] from {{EoW}} is similar to Dark Link, except the [[Echo]] itself simply resembles Link in a dark aura.
