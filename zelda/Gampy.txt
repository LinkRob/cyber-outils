{{Term/Store|Gampy||Series}} is a character mentioned in {{Term|MM3D|Granny's Diary|link}} in {{MM}} during the {{Term|MM3D|Final Day|link}}.{{Name Ref|MM3D}}

==Biography==
{{Term|MM3D|Gampy}} was {{Term|MM3D|Granny|link}}'s late husband and the father of {{Term|MM3D|Tortus|link}}.{{Fact|MM3D}} {{Term|MM3D|Granny's Diary|link}} mentions that {{Term|MM3D|Granny}}, {{Term|MM3D|Anju|link}}, and {{Term|MM3D|Anju's Mother|link}} will be taking refuge at {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|link}}, and goes on to say that she will take several of {{Term|MM3D|Gampy}}'s favorite books along with her.<ref>{{Cite|We're going to take refuge at Romani Ranch tomorrow. I guess it really will fall. Leaving my lifelong home and fleeing is unbearable, but nothing can be done... I think I'll take several of Gampy's favorite books.|Anju's Grandmother|MM}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
The Japanese text refers to {{Term|MM3D|Gampy}} simply as {{Romanize|おじいさん|ojī-san|grandfather|2}}. {{Term|MM3D|Gampy}} is likewise derived from Grandpa.
