The {{Term/Store|Harp||Series}} is a recurring [[item]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= ZA|page= 4}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HW}}

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{ZA|-}}===
In {{ZA}}, the Harp is found in a tunnel north of the [[Mobilin's Head Inn]], which connects between the [[Plain of Andor]] and the [[Seacoast Plain]]. If [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] plays the Harp in a Shrine, she is warped back to the first room.

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Stub|HW}}
In {{HW}}, the Harp is a class of Weapon used by [[Sheik]]. It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Lightning}}. However, Sheik can play up to five songs that are associated to each Element in {{HW|-}}, which produces unique attacks that correspond to the Element when the Strong Attack button is pressed. These songs are: "[[Serenade of Water]]", which is {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Water}}; the "[[Bolero of Fire]]", which is {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Fire}}; the "[[Song of Storms]]", which is Lightning; the "[[Nocturne of Shadows]]", which is {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Darkness}}; and the "[[Prelude of Light]]", which is {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Light}}. While the Harp is her main Weapon, Sheik also uses {{Wp|kunai}}s as part of her moveset.

In  {{HW|-}} and {{HW|L}}, [[Medli]] uses her own unique Harp as her class of Weapon, referred to as the [[Rito Harp]].

====Variations====
The default Harp is the '''Goddess's Harp''', which is modeled after its [[Goddess's Harp|namesake]]. The second form of the Harp is the '''Typhoon Harp'''. The third form of the Harp is the '''Triforce Harp'''.

The Lv.★ Harp is the '''8-Bit Stepladder''', which is based on the [[Stepladder]] from {{TLoZ}}. It was added to {{HW|-}} with the Master Quest Pack DLC. In {{HW|DE}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Stepladder replaces the Shining Harp when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.

In {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE|-}}, the [[Multi-Element Weapon]] is the '''Shining Harp''', which is associated with both the Elements of Lightning and Light.

=====Weak Point Smash=====
{{Main|Critical Point#Hyrule Warriors}}
Against commander enemies, Sheik rapidly slashes away at the enemies caught in the attack's radius with her kunai, before turning her back at them and finishing the blow with a powerful sound wave strummed from her Harp. Against Giant Bosses, Sheik uppercuts the monster with her kunai before disappearing and reappearing in the air, coming down with a double kunai stab at the foe.

=====Special Attack=====
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Sheik hovers over the ground, while several crystal Harps float in a ring around her. She then strums the Harp, causing a rainbow-colored ring of light to expand and contract, bouncing enemies in and out. The final strum flings enemies away. This attack deals damage in wide circle around Sheik and carries no Element.

=====Focus Spirit=====
{{Main|Focus Spirit}}
Upon activation, Sheik focuses her power in her Harp and then bursts in Lightning energy. The Focus Spirit Attack has Sheik turns her back at the enemies in front of her and play "[[Minuet of Forest]]", causing a small forest of trees to grow beneath the enemies' feet. When the Focus Spirit Gauge is depleted, Sheik spins two kunai in her hands and then unleashes Lightning energy around her.
