'''Beetle Mound''' is an Object  and [[Echo]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Beetle Mound|A strange pillar of soil that produces Beetles endlessly or until it's destroyed.<ref name="Beetle Mound">{{Cite|A strange pillar of soil that produces Beetles endlessly or until it's destroyed.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Beetle Mounds are objects found in caves near [[Gerudo Desert]], which spawn Beetles until destroyed. During the [[Beetle Ballyhoo]] [[Side Quest]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] is asked to destroy these mounds for a [[Gerudo]] guard. Zelda can use the [[Tri Rod]] to learn the Beetle Mound [[Echo]], which costs three Echo energy to summon.
