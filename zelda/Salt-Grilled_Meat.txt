{{Term/Store|Salt-Grilled Meat|Salt-Grilled Meats|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Salt-Grilled Meats by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either {{Term|Series|Raw Meat|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Raw Bird Drumstick|link}} with {{Term|Series|Rock Salt|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. By eating them, some of [[Link|Link's]] {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} can be restored to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These meals are known to be easily made when one is short on ingredients, since preparing them only require the meat to be rubbed in salt.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Additional ingredients can be used when making Salt-Grilled Meats, which results in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Salt-Grilled Meat''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. These meals will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts when they're eaten.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Salt-Grilled Meat''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. When eaten, they will partially refill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Salt-Grilled Meat''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled if he eats them.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Salt-Grilled Meat''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. If they're consumed, Link's movement speed will be increased.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Salt-Grilled Meat''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Salt-Grilled Meat''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. These meats can be eaten for Link to gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Salt-Grilled Meat''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. By eating them, the power of Link's attacks will be increased.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Salt-Grilled Meat''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. Link can eat them to receive a boost to his defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Salt-Grilled Meat''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When they're eaten, they will grant Link a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Salt-Grilled Meat''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Salt-Grilled Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Short on ingredients? Just rub some meat in salt and cook it for a simple, tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
