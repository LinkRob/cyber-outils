The {{Term/Store|Leviathan|Leviathans|Series, BotW|plural}} are an extinct species in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Akrah"/>

==Characteristics==
{{Stub|BotW}}
The {{Plural|BotW|Leviathan}} were a species of large, whale-like creatures that went extinct during a catastrophe of disputed nature. This disaster left several near-complete sets of {{Plural|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Object)|link}} scattered around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link|display=Hyrule}}.

Their remains are the subject of the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Side Quest)|link}}".
