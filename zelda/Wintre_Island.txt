{{Term/Store|Wintre Island||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Wintre Island'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Wintre Island}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Sea|link}} off of {{Term|BotW|Tarm Point|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, valued at 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, can be found embedded in the ground on the northern portion of the {{Term|BotW|Wintre Island|display=Island}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found by following a {{Term|BotW|Flower Trail|link}} from the northern side of the {{Term|BotW|Wintre Island|display=Island}} to its peak. A second {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found by completing a {{Term|BotW|Rock Pattern|link}} on the southern side of the {{Term|BotW|Wintre Island|display=Island}}.
