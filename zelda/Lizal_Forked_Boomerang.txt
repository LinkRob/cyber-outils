{{Compendium/Extract|Lizal Forked Boomerang}}
{{Plural|BotW|Lizal Forked Boomerang}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} favored by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Lizalfos|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> This is common among pieces of {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}}, an equipment archetype that includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created and utilized by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are also used by some {{Term|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}}. They have one more blade than a regular {{Term|BotW|Lizal Boomerang|link}}, but they are still capable of returning when thrown.<ref name="Inventory"/> Therefore, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} should make sure to catch any thrown {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Forked Boomerang}} before they hit him. They are commonly found in {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}.

Since they are made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Forked Boomerang}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} and therefore can be dangerous to equip during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. This also means that they are capable of conducting {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. In addition, {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Forked Boomerang}} can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, but they sink when they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Finally, they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
