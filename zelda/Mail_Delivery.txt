{{Term/Store|Mail Delivery||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a recurring service in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|About Mail Delivery<br/><br/>If I have a letter for you, I will approach you at high speed! Please do not flee!|Postman|TP}}</ref> In {{TWW}}, it is named {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the Island Postal Service! What do you have for delivery?|Postbox|TWW}}</ref> The postal systems in {{TMC}}, {{PH}}, and {{ST}} are unnamed.

The service delivers {{Plural|Series|Letter|link}}, possibly with other items enclosed. It is staffed by the {{Term|Series|Postman|link}} (or {{Term|Series|Rito|link}} postmen), usually in combination with sentient {{Plural|Series|Postbox|link}} and a {{Term|Series|Post Office|link}}. {{Plural|Series|Postbox}} and other postal equipment (such as the {{Term|Series|Postman's Hat|link}}) tends to be colored white on red and bears an insignia, either a rabbit head,{{Exp Game|MM, TMC, TP}} or a winged envelope.{{Exp Game|TWW, PH, ST}}

==Appearances==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}} has talking {{Plural|MM3D|Postbox}} that accept letters.<ref>{{Cite|I cannot accept anything other than letters. Ka-ching!|Postbox|MM}}</ref> The {{Term|MM3D|Postman}} collects the mail in the morning and hand-delivers it in the afternoon. His schedule is set by {{Term|MM3D|Mayor Dotour|link}},<ref>{{Cite|This Week's Schedule<br/>Morning: Collection<br/>Afternoon: Delivery<br/>Evening: Practice<br/>The Week's Motto: Don't slack off—the heavens and the wife are watching.<br/>{{Color|MM3D Red|—Mayor Dotour}}|N/A|MM}}</ref> but it can be overriden by the {{Term|MM3D|Postmaster}},<ref>{{Cite|I have decided to flee. It is an order from the Postmaster. I am now free! I can set my own schedule!|Postman|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Madame Aroma|link}} (the Mayor's wife).

{{Term|MM3D|Kafei|link}} uses the service to deliver an anonymous letter to {{Term|MM3D|Anju|link}} on the {{Term|MM3D|First Day|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Postman}} delivers it but dodges {{Term|MM3D|Anju}}'s question when asked which {{Term|MM3D|Postbox}} it was from. Later on in the {{Term|MM3D|Anju's Anguish|link}} {{Term|MM3D|Event}}, Link mails {{Term|MM3D|Anju}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Letter to Kafei|link}}. This is the only item Link can deposit in the {{Term|MM3D|Postbox}}. If Link attempts to deposit the {{Title|MM3D|Priority Mail|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Postbox}} will direct Link to the {{Term|MM3D|Post Office|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Please take {{Color|MM3D Red|priority mail}} to the Post Office. It will even be accepted late at night. Ka-ching!|Postbox|MM}}</ref>

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Island Postal Service}}
The {{Term|TWW|Island Postal Service|link}} delivers mail to islands of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}. The service is operated by the {{Term|TWW|Rito|link}}, who do mail collection, sorting and delivery out of the {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island|link}}. Sentient {{Plural|TWWHD|Postbox}} serve as the touchpoints for the service. They take outbound mail,<ref>{{Cite|Your parcel has hereby been accepted for delivery. Thank you for using the Island Postal Service. We appreciate your business!|Postbox|TWW}}</ref> give out inbound mail, perform Cash-On-Delivery transactions,<ref>{{Cite|This parcel has been sent {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Cash-On-Delivery}}.|Postbox|TWW}}</ref> and charge postage fees. Postage fees at any given {{Plural|TWWHD|Postbox}} vary according to the distance from {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island}}. {{Plural|TWWHD|Postbox}} wiggle when they have mail for Link.<ref>{{Cite|We cannot do much to help you, but we can at least do this: if we get any letters or parcels for you, we will deliver them to a {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|postbox}} near you. If you see any {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|wiggling}} postboxes, please look inside them.|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref>

{{Term|TWWHD|Link}} uses the service to mail {{Term|TWWHD|Baito|link}}'s {{Term|TWWHD|Note to Mom|link}}, and {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie's Letter|link}} to {{Term|TWWHD|Moe|link}} at the {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}}. The {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island}} {{Term|TWWHD|Postman}} {{Term|TWWHD|Ilari|link}} delivers {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie's Letter}} and returns with {{Term|TWWHD|Moe's Letter|link}}, but is rebuffed by {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie's Father}} when he attempts to deliver the letter to {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can deliver it for him.

{{Term|TWWHD|Garrickson|link}} is the only other character seen depositing mail. He uses the service to send unwanted love letters to an unspecified person.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} has a {{Term|TMC|Postman|link}} and a {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}} in {{TMC}}, although the {{Term|TMC|Postbox}} on its roof is purely decorative – {{Term|TMC|Link}} himself neither receives nor sends mail.{{Note|The development team may have once intended for {{Term|TMC|Link}} to receive the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}} (or other parcels) in the mail. In the European version of {{TMC|-}}, the first issue of the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter}} invites readers to "check [their] mail" for future issues.<ref>{{Cite|That's it for our first issue! We plan to bring you helpful questing hints every week, so check your mail!|Swordsman Newsletter|TMC}}</ref> In the North American version, which was released two months later, the text of the newsletter is in closer alignment with the actual gameplay. It tells readers to "check with [their] local post office for future issues."<ref>{{Cite|That's it for our first issue! We plan to bring you helpful questing hints every week, rain or shine. Be sure to check with your local post office for future issues!|Swordsman Newsletter|TMC}}</ref>}} The {{Term|TMC|Postman}} dashes around {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}, while {{Term|TMC|Stamp|link}} is constantly stamping letters in the {{Term|TMC|Post Office}}. After {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Postman}}, {{Term|TMC|Stamp}} will bring {{Term|TMC|Marcy|link}} to help him in the {{Term|TMC|Post Office}}; {{Term|TMC|Link}} can then buy issues of the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule}} has no physical {{Term|Series|Post Office}} or {{Plural|Series|Postbox}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Postman}} hand-delivers {{Plural|TPHD|Letter}} all over {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule}}. In between deliveries to {{Term|TPHD|Link}}, he can be seen at various locations struggling with some delivery or other.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... This letter is addressed to Mr. Zora. Now who could that be?|Postman|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hmm - what to order, what to order... I do believe I will start with meat.|Postman|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I dropped a letter into the hot springs and now I can't read it. Gotta blow this thing dry!|Postman|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I think you are lost. It's got to be around here somewhere...|Postman|TP}}</ref>

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, the postal system features wiggling {{Plural|Series|Postbox}} as in {{TWW|-}}. When Link approaches one, a winged {{Term|PH|Postman}} appears. Rather than hand over the {{Term|PH|Letter}}, the {{Term|PH|Postman}} will read it aloud and comment on it afterwards.<ref>{{Cite|This is a letter from the Postmaster to you, Link! Ahem! Here's what it says...|Postman|PH}}</ref><ref name="Sign Here">{{Cite|...And that's it. Wow, kinda embarrassing! Not like I wrote it, but it was odd to read out loud! Anyway, sign here, will ya?|Postman|PH}}</ref> The {{Term|PH|Postman}} is a new hire. In fact, the first {{Term|PH|Letter}} Link receives is from the {{Term|PH|Postmaster}} vouching for the new {{Term|PH|Postman}}.<ref>{{Cite|To: Link If you're getting this, I guess that my new postman is working out...so far. So I'd like to make an unusual request. He's as good as a new hire gets! He's as diligent and efficient as they come! I've got no complaints. I want to keep it that way. So be nice to the Postman. He really is a good kid. From: The Postmaster|Letter|PH}}</ref> The {{Term|PH|Postman}} has {{Term|PH|Link}} sign for every delivery.<ref name="Sign Here" />

The {{Term|PH|Drawing Commission|link}} uses the postal system to run sweepstakes. {{Term|PH|Link}} participates by mailing {{Plural|PH|Prize Postcard|link}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, the postal system functions the same way as in {{PH|-}}, only the {{Term|ST|Postman}} hand-delivers {{Plural|ST|Letter}} unopened, and {{Term|ST|Link}} needn't sign for them.<ref>{{Cite|I've got a letter for you, so I'll just jump right in and read it aloud. Oh, wait—that's the way we USED to do things. But no more! Apparently, some folks thought our reading their letters out loud was rude. So now our policy is to just hand-deliver the mail unopened.|Postman|ST}}</ref> The {{Term|ST|Postman}} has the same physical appearance but without wings, and wears a different outfit. He imitates a chugging train when he approaches and leaves a {{Term|ST|Postbox}}. {{Term|ST|Link}}'s first {{Term|ST|Letter}} is again one from the {{Term|ST|Postmaster}} vouching for his new employee. The postal system itself is new as of the day {{Term|ST|Link}} receives the {{Term|ST|Letter}}.<ref name="From the Postmaster">{{Cite|Dear Link, Our new postal system has started running today. If you're reading this letter, it means my new postman is doing his job right. He's as hardworking and efficient as they come! So be nice to him, won't you? We'll be in touch again if anything new comes up. — The Postmaster|From the Postmaster|ST}}</ref> Despite this, the {{Term|ST|Postman}} goes to read the {{Term|ST|Letter}} aloud before remembering that the delivery policy changed due to complaints.<ref name="From the Postmaster"/>

{{Plural|ST|Letter}} concerning {{Term|ST|Beedle's Members' Club|link}} each come with a package consisting of a new {{Term|ST|Beedle's Membership Cards|link|display=membership card}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've got a letter and package for you from Beedle, Link!|Postman|ST}}</ref> {{Plural|ST|Letter}} from the {{Term|ST|Battle Association|link}} come with a prize for having achieved a certain [[Battle Rank]].<ref>{{Cite|Here's a package and a letter from...the Battle Association?|Postman|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You've achieved a battle rank of {{Color|ST Light Red|D}} and have been registered as an {{Color|ST Light Blue|intermediate competitor}}. To celebrate, here is a prize for you. Enjoy!|Battle Rank Notice|ST}}</ref> The {{Term|ST|Letter}} is delivered first, then the package. 

{{Term|ST|Link}} can mail {{Plural|ST|Prize Postcard}} in this game as well.<ref>{{Cite|Hellooooooo there, valued customer! We're here for all your postal needs! Would you like to send any prize postcards?|Postbox|ST}}</ref>
