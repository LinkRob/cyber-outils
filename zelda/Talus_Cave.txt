{{Term/Store|Talus Cave||Series, FS}} is a stage from {{FS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 263}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Talus Cave is an [[Ice|icy]] cavern guarded by the [[Great Fairy of Ice]]. Slippery floors and bottomless pits make for tricky maneuvering. After the [[Links]] have cleared two floors and defeated the boss, the Great Fairy of Ice appears and gives them a Key. The kind of key depends on the number of [[Rupee]]s collected.

The boss of Talus Cave is [[Dera Zol]], and the symbol is the [[Water Element]].

==Nomenclature==
Talus Cave is likely named for a type of cave in real life. {{Wp|Cave#Talus cave|Talus caves}} are openings between rocks found in a pile, often at the base of a cliff, called a ''talus'' or ''scree''.
