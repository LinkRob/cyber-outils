{{Term/Store|Hemaar's Descent||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hemaar's Descent'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Hemaar's Descent is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. The Descent is a pathway leading away from the [[Statue of the Eighth Heroine]] that becomes progressively warmer and grassier as the path continues, eventually leading to a grassy plateau.
