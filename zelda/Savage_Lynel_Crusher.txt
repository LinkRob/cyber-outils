{{Compendium/Extract|Savage Lynel Crusher}}
{{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Crusher}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by high-tier {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}, notably {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Silver Lynel|link}}, as well as {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lynel|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}. They were forged by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} using metal from {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}} and are known to have a powerful downward swing.<ref name="Inventory"/> Due to being made by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}, they are part of the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are commonly used in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Mountains|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.

The {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} who use {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Crusher}} often swing the {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} down to the ground, creating a hemisphere of {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}, or use it to spin and knock out {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. They are wielded with two hands by {{Term|BotW|Link}}, as they are incredibly long and heavy. It is among one of the most powerful {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} in the game, having a base attack damage of 78.

{{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Crusher}} are made of metal, and therefore will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, but they can be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. They will not be burned by {{Term|BotW|Fire}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
