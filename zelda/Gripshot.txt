The {{Term/Store|Gripshot|Gripshots|Series, TFH}} is an {{Term|TFH|Item|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a Gripshot! Press {{Icon|TFH Y}} to grab whatever or whomever you please!|N/A|TFH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} functions similarly to the {{Term|Series|Hookshot|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Clawshot|link}}, in that it fires a gripper that can pull {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}, {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}}, and other {{Plural|TFH|Item}} towards {{Term|TFH|Link|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}}'s main use is to transport the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}; once it attaches to a stake or to another {{Term|TFH|Links}}, the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} quickly retracts, pulling the {{Term|TFH|Links}} who used it towards whatever it grabbed onto. This allows the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to cross gaps that are too wide for them to be thrown over. It can also be used to destroy {{Plural|TFH|Pot|link}} and bushes, stun stronger {{Plural|TFH|Enemy|link}}, and pull weaker {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}}, like {{Plural|TFH|Keese|link}}, {{Plural|TFH|Chasupa|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Water Tektite|link}}, towards the {{Term|TFH|Links}} who used it. Certain foes, like {{Term|TFH|Gyorm|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Arrghus|link}}, require the use of the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} in order to be defeated. In the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}}, wooden rafts can only be moved if a {{Term|TFH|Links}} stands atop one and pulls himself toward a point which the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} can grab onto.

The {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} first appears in the {{Term|TFH|Cove of Transition|link}}, in which all three {{Plural|TFH|Links}} acquire one. The {{Term|TFH|Item}} reappears in the {{Term|TFH|Water Temple|link}} (two {{Plural|TFH|Gripshot}}), {{Term|TFH|Training Ground|link}}, {{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Lone Labyrinth|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Floating Garden|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Deception Castle|link}} (two {{Plural|TFH|Gripshot}}), and {{Term|TFH|Sky Temple|link}} (one {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} per {{Term|TFH|Stage}}). The {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} also appears in the {{Term|TFH|Flooded Zone|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Scorching Zone|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Desert Zone|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Baneful Zone|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}, as well as the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}}'s {{Term|TFH|Riverside}}, {{Term|TFH|Fortress}} and {{Term|TFH|The Ruins}} arenas.

[[File:TFH Nice Gripshot Icon.png|left]]
The effects of the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} is improved while wearing {{Term|TFH|Robowear|link}}. This {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} allows the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} to extend much faster and further, pull larger {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}} toward {{Term|TFH|Link}}, and deal damage to some {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}}.
