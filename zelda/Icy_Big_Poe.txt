'''Icy Big Poes''' are [[Enemies in Hyrule Warriors|enemies]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}}

==Characteristics==
Icy Big Poes are stronger variants of Big Poes. Like Big Poes, they commonly serve the [[Dark Forces]] and resemble their counterparts from {{OoT|-}} and {{MM|-}}, albeit with a dark color scheme. Unlike regular Big Poes, Icy Big Poes rely on Water elemental attacks. Icy Big Poes attack by swinging their [[Lantern]]s and expelling dark elemental magic from it, which may cause their [[Weak Point Gauge]] to be revealed. They have a proclivity for dodging attacks by vanishing and reappearing a short distance away.

When defeated, Icy Big Poes may drop their [[Essence of Icy Big Poe|Essence]] or their [[Icy Big Poe's Talisman|Talisman]] as their Bronze and Silver [[Material]]s, respectively.
