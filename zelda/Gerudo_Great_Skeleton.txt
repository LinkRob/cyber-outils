The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Great Skeleton||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Gerudo Great Skeleton'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Gerudo Great Skeleton is located in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. The Gerudo Great Skeleton is one of the three remaining {{Plural|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Object)|link}} of the now extinct [[Leviathan]]s. Link must take a picture of these remains for the "{{Term|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Side Quest)|link}}" [[Side Quest]]. These bones contain the [[Hawa Koth Shrine]] and the [[Great Fairy Fountain]] belonging to the [[Great Fairy]], [[Tera]].

The skeleton resembles that of a whale. The skeleton has obvious small wing-like protrusions on the back of its body, possibly showing relations to the [[Wind Fish]].

Link can find a [[Korok]] by examining some [[Leaves with Fairy Dust]] on the tip of some bones directly above the Great Fairy Fountain atop the Skeleton.
