{{Term/Store|Lynna Village||Series, OoA}} is a location in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 111 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
On his adventures between time and space, [[Link]] also visits Lynna City centuries in the past, during the reign of [[Queen Ambi]]. The smaller, simpler village is composed of wood houses with thatched roofs. The dreary mood of the hard-working inhabitants is worsened when all the men in the village are sent to work on the [[Black Tower]] during the day by the orders of Queen Ambi,<ref>{{Cite|This is the entry to the tower being built to the heavens by the order of Queen Ambi.|N/A|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|All the men have been hauled off to build the tower. Only us old folk remain.|N/A|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The village has it hard with the construction of Queen Ambi's tower...|N/A|OoA}}</ref> who was influenced and later possessed by [[Veran]], Sorceress of Shadows. Veran, while in [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]]'s body, created a day that never ends, forcing the villagers to work indefinitely on the tower.<ref>{{Cite|Queen Ambi... Now, with my powers, the flow of time is yours to command. For you, I shall create a day that never ends, so the people will never sleep, and work on the tower can continue.|Veran|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There's been no night for many days, as if time has stopped.|N/A|OoA}}</ref>

Lynna Village is the seat of the Labrynnian monarchy, reigned by Queen Ambi, who resides in her [[Ambi's Palace|palace]] which is connected to the village. The palace is so large that it is almost equal in size to the village itself.<ref>{{Cite|My palace is very large--you will not be able to find the way out alone.|Queen Ambi|OoA}}</ref>

Despite there being four hundred years between the past and the present ages,<ref>{{Cite|"When I grow up, I'll marry you!" That's what you said, [Link].♥ Four hundred years I waited. But...|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref> Lynna Village greatly resembles Lynna City. The Maku Tree can still be found northeast of the village (although she is a mere sapling at the time), and an Ember Seed Tree still stands south of the village (although South Lynna is simply part of the [[South Shore]] in the past). New areas appear on the map, such as Northeast Lynna and West Lynna. 

Many of the homes seen in this past age are at the very same location as the homes in the present, although they are inhabited by different people: 

* Vasu Jewelers becomes a duplex where the [[Shooting Gallery#Oracle of Ages|Lynna Village Shooting Gallery]] and the [[Advance Shop]] are found. 
* {{Term|OoA|Mayor Plen's House|link}} becomes the {{Term|OoA|Post Office|link}}.
* {{Term|OoA|Dr. Troy's House|link}} becomes {{Term|OoA|Pippin's House|link}}; {{Term|OoA|Pippin|link}} is a farmer and implied ancestor of [[Bipin]]. 
* {{Term|OoA|Mamamu Yan's House|link}} becomes {{Term|OoA|Ancient Adler's House|link}}; {{Term|OoA|Adlar|link}} is a former advisor to the Queen.<ref>{{Cite|I am Adlar. For fifty years, I was a palace advisor, but I have since been chased from the palace and am now retired.|Adlar|OoA}}</ref>
* The {{Term|OoA|Know-It-All Birds' Hut|link}} is replaced by a {{Term|OoA|Toilet|link}}, inhabited by the [[Hand]].
* The house next to where the comedian and his partner stood becomes {{Term|OoA|Dekadin's House|link}}.

Once Link defeats Veran atop the [[Black Tower]], peace returns to the village and all of Labrynna.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Link. Thanks to you, Lynna City is a peaceful place once again!|Nayru (Oracle)|OoA}}</ref> Afterwards, Link is treated with utmost respect by the inhabitants of Lynna.<ref>{{Cite|You! Kids aren't allowed in... Sir Link! Excuse me!|Guard|OoA}}</ref> Statues of Link are placed within the village,<ref>{{Cite|You look just like the statue outside... Say! Are you Link?!? I never thought I'd actually get to see you!|N/A|OoA}}</ref> which can be seen in the present age as well.
