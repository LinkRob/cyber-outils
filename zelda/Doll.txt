==Location and Uses==
Dolls are odd dolls that seemed to have been crafted to resemble [[Link]].<ref name="Manual">{{Cite manual|quote='''Doll'''<br/>Really looks like Link. Link's LIFE increases by 1 when you get hold of this.|game=TAoL|page=45}}</ref> They increase the number of Link's lives when collected.<ref name="Manual"/> They are well hidden in certain areas of [[Hyrule]]'s overworld. However, once they are collected, they disappear from their locations for the rest of the game and cannot be recollected.

==Trivia==
*The sprite for the Doll was re-used from Link's overworld sprite.
