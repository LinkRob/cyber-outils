{{Term/Store|Volcanic Defense||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Volcanic Defense|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Volcanic Defense}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}}, with {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}} as its pilot. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Fireproof Lizard|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Rushroom|link}}, and 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

Throughout the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}}'s objective is to defeat 4,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. The different {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} include {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Igneo Talus|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Rock Octorok|link}}, and many {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}} scattered around {{Term|HWAoC|Death Mountain|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 4,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
