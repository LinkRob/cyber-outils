{{Term/Store|Neptoona||Series, PH}} is an animal in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 65}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|PH|Neptoona}} is legendary among fishermen in the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}}. It is exceedingly large, with a length of 17 feet and 3 inches, and is considered to be the rarest and most difficult fish to catch in the sea. {{Term|PH|Neptoona}} is similar in appearance to its counterpart, the {{Term|PH|Rusty Swordfish|link}}, but it is much larger and has a blue and light purple coloring. {{Term|PH|Neptoona}} also has a serrated nose. Because of its sheer size, {{Term|PH|Neptoona}} must be lifted out of the water with the {{Term|PH|Salvage Arm|link}} when caught.

The spots where {{Term|PH|Neptoona}} can be found are marked with a black icon in the shape of a swordfish (as opposed to the dark blue marks that yield smaller fish). Although these black icons often yield {{Plural|PH|Rusty Swordfish}}, they are the only icons that may mark {{Term|PH|Neptoona}}. Once {{Term|PH|Link|link}} catches {{Term|PH|Neptoona}}, it cannot be caught again. Subsequent swordfish markings that appear on the map will only yield {{Plural|PH|Rusty Swordfish}}. If {{Term|PH|Link}} takes {{Term|PH|Neptoona}} back to the {{Term|PH|Old Wayfarer|link}}, he rewards {{Term|PH|Link}} with a {{Term|PH|Heart Container|link}}.
