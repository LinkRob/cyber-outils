The {{Term/Store|Poe Scent||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an object in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TPHD Red|Poe}} Scent!|N/A|TPHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TPHD|Poe Scent}} is the third {{Term|TPHD|Scent|link}} learned by {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} and it replaces the {{Term|TPHD|Scent of Ilia|link}}. It is learned in the {{Term|TPHD|Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|link}}, the fourth {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Poe Scent}} is colored purple, and it is picked up from the cloak of a dead {{Term|TPHD|Poe|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Poe Scent}} is used to track down the other three large {{Plural|TPHD|Poe}} within the {{Term|TPHD|Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)}}, as well as help solve certain puzzles.
