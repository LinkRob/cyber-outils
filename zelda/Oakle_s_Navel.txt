{{Term/Store|Oakle's Navel||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Oakle's Navel'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Oakle's Navel}} is a deep basin located near {{Term|BotW|Mount Rozudo|link}} in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Hot-Footed Frog|link}} and various {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} live in the waters alongside a {{Term|BotW|Water Octorok|link}}. Five {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} are also dotted around the edge of the [[Lake|lake]]. A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found by diving into the plant circle from the above cliff.

Slightly above the water, while still remaining in the depression, sits a grassy platform with the {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} that contribute to the site's namesake. It can be accessed via a tunnel connecting to {{Term|BotW|Marblod Plain|link}}.
