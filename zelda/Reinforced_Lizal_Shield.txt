{{Plural|BotW|Reinforced Lizal Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Shield|link}} that have been strengthened with the addition of a different type of metal.<ref name="Inventory"/> Their edges are lined with spikes, which means they must be handled with care.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Blue Lizalfos|link}} commonly use these {{Plural|BotW|Reinforced Lizal Shield|display=Shields}}, though {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}} may also use them. They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created and utilized by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Reinforced Lizal Shield}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. However, they can be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, and they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
