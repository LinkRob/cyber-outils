{{Term/Store|Doof||Series}} is a character featured in the "[[Hitch in the Works]]" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}.{{Fact}} He is [[North Castle|North Castle's]] handyman and is known for his inventions.<ref name=handyman/>

==Biography==
While filling the castle's lamps with oil, Link spies a strange mechanical robot that takes a few steps toward him and promptly shatters, much to the dismay of its builder, Doof. Doof tells Link that the robot was supposed to be his assistant so that it could help him with the chores and is disappointed to see that it failed to work.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, it was supposed to be my assistant. Help with the chores. Didn't work though.|name=Doof|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> Link encourages him to try again so that the young hero will do less work around the castle,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hey, don't give up yet. Any adventure that means doing less work is ok with me. Go on, give 'er another try.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> but Doof's magic only causes the robot to go haywire, using a mop as a pogo stick and making a mess all over the place. While Link and Doof are chasing the robot, some of [[Ganon]]'s [[Moblin]]s infiltrate the castle by using a secret trapdoor under the floor tiles. The Moblins break Doof's robot again,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, darn it! I just fixed that!|name=Doof|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> and during their battle with Link, ensnare him in a net. Doof attempts to help by casting a spell on the robot again, allowing Link enough time to defeat the Moblins while they are distracted by it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, I must help Link! Alakazoo!|name=Doof|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> Unfortunately, a hit from a Moblin's [[Boomerang]] causes Doof's robot to collapse once more, knocking Link unconscious. [[Princess Zelda]] then scolds the young hero for falling asleep on the job, and although Link tries to tell her about the Moblins he defeated, Doof is not there to back him up and Zelda does not believe him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=A likely story. I don't see any Moblins. All I see is a bunch of junk on the floor that needs to be picked up. So get to it, ''hero''. And no more naps, ok?|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> Link then asks Doof if he can conjure up some fake Moblins so he can "rescue" Zelda and get out of doing the chores.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Say, Doof. Could you make up some fake Moblins, that look real, I mean. [...] That way, see, I can save Zelda from the fake Moblins and get out of doing these stupid chores.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref>

Since Zelda overheard Doof and Link's discussion, when real Moblins come and capture her, the young hero is taken off guard since they both assume that they are Doof's fake Moblins.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=If Link thinks his fake Moblins will scare me, he's in for a surprise.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Those Moblins are real!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> Link eventually manages to save the princess and, once they are out of the [[Underworld]], Zelda screams and jumps into Link's arms, when she sees one, which turns out to be Doof's fake Moblin after all.
