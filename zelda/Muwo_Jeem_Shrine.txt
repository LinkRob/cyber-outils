The {{Term/Store|Muwo Jeem Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Muwo Jeem Shrine}}<br/>A Modest Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Modest Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Muwo Jeem Shrine is located on the top of {{Term|BotW|Cape Cales|link}} overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. As Link enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Muwo Jeem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Muwo Jeem. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Muwo Jeem|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Muwo Jeem Shrine features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. As Link approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}} wielding an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe+|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Shield+|link}} will rise from a pit to challenge Link. In order to reach Muwo Jeem, Link must defeat the Guardian Scout III to cause a portcullis separating him from the Monk to rise.

Once Link has done so, Muwo Jeem will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Muwo Jeem|BotW}}</ref> and afterwards will wish him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Muwo Jeem|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the portcullis contains a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bow|link}}.
