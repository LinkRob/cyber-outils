{{Term/Store|Granté||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} <ref>{{Cite|Granté|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Granté}} is a half-{{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}, half-{{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} merchant, who is the son of the researchers {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Jerrin|link}}.<ref name="Diary"/> {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} recalls that {{Term|BotW|Granté}} grew quickly, and he used to train his body by carrying the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Granté grew fast, and it wasn’t long before he towered over me! He loved to carry the blue flame from Tumlea Heights, calling it a chance to train his body.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> Before {{Term|BotW|Granté}} left the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} wrote to {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} to ask if he could study with her at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}, but she refused any guests.<ref>{{Cite|A few days before he left, I thought it might be a good idea to have Granté study with Dr. Purah. I wrote to her to ask what she thought of such an idea, and she sent a letter that simply said… "Don’t send anyone here!"|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref>

Seeing {{Term|BotW|Granté}}’s curiosity, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} encouraged {{Term|BotW|Granté}} to travel.<ref>{{Cite|I could see the curiosity in his eyes, and so I encouraged him to travel the world and see as much as he could.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's why my father told me to go out and see the world!|Granté|BotW}}</ref> On the day his father suggested he should explore the outside world, {{Term|BotW|Granté}} left his home.<ref>{{Cite|He'd always been independent. But I was still surprised when, the very day I told him to travel, he left!|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> During this time, he traveled the world whilst studying ancient civilizations.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Granté, a dashing, novice researcher of ancient civilizations! I've still got a lot to learn, though...|Granté|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Granté}} appears on the balcony of the north-eastern most house in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}} after the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is complete. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first meets {{Term|BotW|Granté}}, he asks if he is also traveling throughout the world.<ref>{{Cite|Are you also making your way through the world?|Granté|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says he is doing something like that, {{Term|BotW|Granté}} is excited to meet someone else like him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You too, huh? I’m beside myself with glee!|Granté|BotW}}</ref>

He sells {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that have no other method of being replaced after {{Term|BotW|Link}} loses them,<ref>{{Cite|Do you want to take a look at my rare-armor collection? It's incredible|Granté|BotW}}</ref> such as Expansion Pass {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set}} or those found within {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I got a complete set of this rare gear. It's {{Color|BotW Red|rubber}}...something-something.|Granté|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A complete set of rare gear, called {{Color|BotW Red|barbarian}}...something or other.|Granté|BotW}}</ref> However, he is unable to sell {{Plural|BotW|Armor|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has not previously obtained.<ref>{{Cite|I'm looking to get a set of rare armor in stock soon! It's called...umm...{{Color|BotW Red|climb}} something or other...|Granté|BotW}}</ref> He will also sell {{Term|BotW|Link}} a new {{Term|BotW|Hylian Shield|link}} for 3,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} if the original is lost or broken.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can also sell {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Granté}}. He asks to see what {{Term|BotW|Link}} has,<ref>{{Cite|How about in your pouch?|Granté|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please show me!|Granté|BotW}}</ref> in addition to warning him not to make a mistake when choosing what to sell.<ref>{{Cite|Don't make a mistake when choosing!|Granté|BotW}}</ref>

===''Tears of the Kingdom''===
At one point, Granté moved to [[Tarrey Town]] and changed his name to Grantéson so he could join [[Hudson Construction]]. He was tasked with the Master overseeing of the "dream home" construction on a lot southeast of the town, one which {{Term|BotW|Link}} could purchase.<ref>{{Cite|I'm here to answer all your home-building questions.|Grantéson|ToTK}}</ref>
