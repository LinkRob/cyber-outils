The {{Term/Store|Postman's Hat||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 134}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's the {{Color|MM Red|Postman's Hat}}, isn't it?</p><p>That is a fine thing. It is filled with the joy of freedom.</p>}}

The Postman's Hat is given to [[Link]] by the [[Postman]] on the [[Final Day]], who is distressed about wanting to flee [[Clock Town]].<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh...I want to flee! B-but, it's not written on the schedule. T-to me, the delivery schedule... It's the h-highest priority.|Postman|MM}}</ref> After Link shows him the [[Special Delivery to Mama|priority mail]], he will pull himself together and quickly deliver [[Kafei]]'s letter to [[Madame Aroma]]. The Postman will then meet up with Link outside the [[Milk Bar]] and give him the Postman's Hat, telling him that he has decided to flee Clock Town.<ref>{{Cite|I have decided to flee. It is an order from the Postmaster. I am now free! I can set my own schedule!|Postman|MM}}</ref> The Postman will then run off, leaving Link to his important deliveries.

Link can use the authority that comes with the mask to check inside [[mailbox]]es. The first time, Link will find a [[Piece of Heart]]. Every subsequent time, he will find a Green [[Rupee]].<ref>{{Cite|You are doing a great job. Ka-ching! Some item other than a letter has been deposited. Ka-ching! Nice work. Ka-ching!|Postbox|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, the Postman's Hat can be used in the [[Swamp Fishing Hole]] to help catch a [[Fish#Postal Salmon|Postal Salmon]], which appears on the First and Second days from 6 AM to 9 PM.
