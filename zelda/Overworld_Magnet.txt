The '''Overworld Magnet''' is an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|TV Series}}.{{Name Ref|TLoZ (TV Series)}}

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld Magnet}} appears in "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|That Sinking Feeling|link}}". Found by a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Goriya|link}} in the furthest cave of the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}}, the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld Magnet|display=Magnet}} has the power to bring whatever is on the surface down into the underworld, including castles.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Goriya search everwhere, Ganon. Overworld Magnet buried in furthest cave of Underworld, just like your magic tell you. |name= Goriya |show= The Legend of Zelda |episode= 5}}</ref> According to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}}, the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld Magnet}} is one of the greatest spells of the ancient wizards.<ref name="Magnet">{{Cite Episode|quote=One of the greatest spells of the ancient wizards, and now, it's mine! But where are Link and Zelda?|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref>

In the episode, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} orders a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Goriya}} to use the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld Magnet|display=Magnet}} to bring {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}} into the underworld,<ref name="Magnet"/> but when that fails, he decides to sink {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|North Castle|link}} down so that he may obtain the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} within it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I have captured the castle, and with it, the Triforce of Wisdom! Now I can conquer Hyrule, destroy that pipsqueak Link, and make Zelda my queen.|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> However, since {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda}} had already taken the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} with her, his plan ends up failing. Eventually {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda}} arrive in {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}'s lair, and when {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} uses a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magic Bracelet|link}} to throw {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} far away, he lands on the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld Magnet}} and knocks it over, causing it to push the castle back up to the surface.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The castle! It's going back up!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> The {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Overworld Magnet|display=Magnet}} ends up overloading and eventually explodes in {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}'s face.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Oh no, it's overloading! It's going to blow! |name= Ganon |show= The Legend of Zelda |episode= 5}}</ref>
