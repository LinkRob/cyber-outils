{{Term/Store|Fire Moblin|Fire Moblin|Series, HWAoC|plural}} are enemies in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
Fire Moblins are red moblins with runic tattoos on them wielding a [[Soldier's Claymore]]. Fire Moblins can shoot beams of flame from a long range, similar to a [[Yiga Blademaster]], when they swing their claymores with both hands, the beams will cause a small explosion when it hits, dealing area of effect damage and also breaking the guard of blocks, so it is best to avoid the beam altogether. Fire Moblins also have a new attack where it slams its weapon on the ground, creating a large explosion. All of its special attacks give the burning effect if they connect.
