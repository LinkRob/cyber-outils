{{Term/Store|Tenne||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tenne|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Tenne}} is a young female {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} stable hand who can be found tending the hay trough at the {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}, within the {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. She is the daughter of the stable's manager, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri|link}}, while the other stable hands {{Term|BotW|Kaifa|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Jana|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Gleema|link}} are her sisters. She is one of four female stable hands that allows {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to style the mane and change the equipment of the registered {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} he has currently withdrawn, though only if they have bonded with him completely. However, like the others, she refuses to do this for {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}.
