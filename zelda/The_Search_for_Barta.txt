"{{Term/Store|The Search for Barta||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Search for Barta}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is one of four that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} needs to complete as part of "{{Term|BotW|The Thunder Helm|link}}". After calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can enter the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}} to see {{Term|BotW|Liana|link}} wondering what has happened to {{Term|BotW|Barta|link}}, one of her soldiers.<ref>{{Cite|What happened to Barta?|Liana|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Liana}}, she expresses her shock that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} has failed to return for a second time.<ref>{{Cite|Whuh... Whah...<br/>{{Big|WHAAAAT?!}}<br/>Barta didn't come back AGAIN?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotta|link}} explains that she went to investigate the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton|link}} southwest of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid this time she went to investigate the {{Color|BotW Blue|leviathan bones}} in the southwest...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Leena|link}} claims she tried to stop her.<ref>{{Cite|I... I tried to stop her...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} states that her attempt oviously did not work, since {{Term|BotW|Barta}} is not present.<ref>{{Cite|But obviously it didn't work because she's not here!|Liana|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Leena}} and {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} say that, since a {{Term|BotW|Molduga|link}} lives in the area and it is very hot, they did not think {{Term|BotW|Barta}} would actually go.<ref>{{Cite|But... Everyone knows that area is the domain of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga}}, so who would figure she'd even go close to there?|Leena|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes, nobody would dare! And not only that, but it's an {{Color|BotW Blue|extremely hot zone}}, so we wouldn't last long there in our gear...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> This only angers {{Term|BotW|Liana}} further,<ref>{{Cite|You... You {{Big|IDIOTS!}}|Liana|BotW}}</ref> as {{Term|BotW|Barta}} would not realize if she were to walk into danger.<ref>{{Cite|That scatterbrain wouldn't even realize she was walking into danger! I mean, you know that, right?!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} reminds them that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} was captured by the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}} not long ago.<ref>{{Cite|Why, it was just the other day she ran off and got captured by the Yiga!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She accepts the fact that her soldiers are not going to change,<ref>{{Cite|Ugh... I guess Barta will be Barta, and you all aren't going to change...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> though she laments how often {{Term|BotW|Barta}} gets into trouble.<ref>{{Cite|But still, why does she have to go and get in trouble like this all the time...|Liana|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes the journey out to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton}}, he can find {{Term|BotW|Barta}} collapsed near its skull. She is delirious at first, wondering if {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Fairy|link}} or an angel to guide her beyond.<ref>{{Cite|Are you...a fairy? An angel? Are you here to guide my passage? ...Everything's going hazy... This might be the end...|Barta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Barta}} wishes that she could have eaten a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}} one last time,<ref>{{Cite|Ahh... If only I could've eaten a {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty durian}} one last time...|Barta|BotW}}</ref> repeating the name of the fruit to herself.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|A hearty durian...<br/>A hearty durian...}}|Barta|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Durian}} for {{Term|BotW|Barta}}, he can return to give it to her. After eating it, she thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for helping her return from the brink of death.<ref>{{Cite|Hah... I've returned from the brink of death thanks to you... Sarqso!|Barta|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has spoken to {{Term|BotW|Barta}} before and is not wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}, she will recognize him and realize he is a man.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Is that you I spoke to before? So you are actually a voe... I thought you were a bit muscular for a Hylian vai.|Barta|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} saved her life, she promises not to tell anybody the truth.<ref>{{Cite|But don't worry, your secret is safe with me. I owe you my life, after all!|Barta|BotW}}</ref>}} She wants to rest for a while before returning to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}},<ref>{{Cite|Hahh... Anyway, I'm absolutely beat. Maybe I'll rest just a little longer and then head home.|Barta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hah... Anyway, I'm totally exhausted. Maybe I'll rest just a little longer then head home.|Barta|BotW}}</ref> but when she hears that {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is angry with her, she realizes that she should leave as soon as possible.<ref>{{Cite|[Your boss was MAD.] Huh? So...they knew I snuck off... {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Oh, I am in such trouble...}}}} Yep, looks like I'd better go on ahead. See you later and sav'orq!|Barta|BotW}}</ref>

Back in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}, {{Term|BotW|Liana}} will praise {{Term|BotW|Link}} for finding {{Term|BotW|Barta}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! What a courageous vai! I heard from Barta! She said you found her! I told her you were as dependable as a Champion! If you were a Gerudo vai, I'd want you in my troop!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She is glad that the situation has been resolved before she could be reprimanded by {{Term|BotW|Teake|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, close one, huh? Looks like I got out of this without getting chewed out by our troop leader!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, saying he should buy something nice in the market.<ref>{{Cite|You must be worn out, right? Here, take this as thanks! Buy yourself something nice in the market!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
