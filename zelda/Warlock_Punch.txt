'''Warlock Punch''' is the neutral special move used by [[Ganondorf]] in all of his playable appearances in the {{SSB|Series}}, beginning with {{SSBM}}.

==Uses==
When using Warlock Punch, Ganondorf charges up dark energy and unleashes it as a punch. The move is similar to [[Captain Falcon]]'s {{ssb|Falcon Punch}}, although the Warlock Punch inflicts more damage and knockback, but at the cost of taking longer to execute. Warlock Punch is more powerful when used in the air, like with Falcon Punch.

As of {{SSBB}}, it is possible for Ganondorf to turn around while charging Warlock Punch, dealing increased damage, but is also slower than the normal Warlock Punch. Additionally, as of {{SSB4|3DS}}, Ganondorf is given "super armor" while using the Warlock Punch, though only while on the ground, so opponents cannot attack Ganondorf while he executes Warlock Punch. In the game's story mode Subspace Emissary, Ganondorf briefly charges up a Warlock Punch while visibly contemplating attacking his nemeses Link and Zelda as they were about to confront Tabuu, but ultimately decided to accompany them instead.

In {{SSB4}}, Warlock Punch has two variations, '''Warlock Blade''' and '''Warlock Thrust'''. For Warlock Blade, Ganondorf pulls out a sword instead to stab opponents with. For Warlock Thrust, Ganondorf can execute an explosive punch, which is done a lot quicker than Warlock Punch, although at the expense of inflicting a lot less damage and knockback.
