{{Plural|BotW|Lynel Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} favored by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}} for both their defensive and offensive capabilities.<ref name="Inventory"/> While they are primarily useful as {{Plural|BotW|Shield}}, their edges are capable of dealing slash attacks while deflecting.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by and for {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Regular {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} predominately use {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Shield}}, however {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}} will use them instead. They can become scarce as {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} tier up, but since the {{Term|BotW|Lynel}} on {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain|link}} will always remain a regular {{Term|BotW|Lynel}}, it will always be a source of {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Shield}}.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Shield}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in water. However, they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, and they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
