{{Term/Store|Slime Eel||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is the [[Boss]] of the [[Catfish's Maw]] in {{LA}}. It is a giant eel-like [[Demon]] that opposes [[Link]] in an attempt to prevent him from waking the [[Wind Fish]].

==Characteristics==
As the battle starts, the Slime Eel will create a large hole in the ground and four smaller holes in the walls of the chamber. Its spiked tail will then spin around continuously from the hole in the ground, which Link must avoid at all costs. Link must wait until its head pops out of one of the walls and use the [[Hookshot]] to pull it out into the open. Once revealed, an exposed heart will appear on its body which Link must strike with his [[Sword|sword]]. The further Link is from the Slime Eel when it is pulled out, the longer it will take for it to retreat back into the hole. Sometimes, Link will pull out a fake eel that will explode. In the Nintendo Switch version of the game, fake eels have black eyes with white pupils, rather than white eyes with black pupils like the real one. This makes it much easier to tell the fake eels from the real Slime Eel. Before the Slime Eel's destruction at the hands of Link, it states that Link does not know what kind of island [[Koholint Island]] is, which appears to amuse it.

Defeating the Slime Eel gets Link the [[Wind Marimba]] as well as a [[Heart Container]].

==Trivia==
*The spiked ball on the end of the Slime Eel's tail is the same design as the spiked ball wielded by the [[Ball & Chain Soldier]]s found in the same game, as well as {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}.
