The {{Term/Store|Giant Wallet||Series, MM, MM3D, TP, TPHD, SS}},{{Exp Game|MM, TP, SS}}<ref name="E 124-125">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124-125 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM, TP, SS}}}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Giant's Wallet||OoT, OoT3D}},{{Exp Game|OoT}}<ref name="E 124-125"/> is a recurring [[item]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and uses==
The Giant Wallet is an upgrade of the [[Wallet]] that can store a lot of [[Rupee]]s. In games that Wallets appear in, they are generally the last or penultimate upgrade.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
The Giant's Wallet in {{OoT}} is the second and final Wallet upgrade, and it allows [[Link]] to carry up to 500 Rupees. It can be obtained by defeating thirty [[Gold Skulltula]]s and delivering the [[Golden Skulltula Spirit|Gold Skulltula Tokens]] to the third child in the [[House of Skulltula]]. As thanks, the child rewards Link with the Giant's Wallet.

In {{OoT|-}}, the Giant Wallet is a brown sack with a yellow hexagon resembling a [[Yellow Rupee]] on the front. In {{OoT3D}}, this was changed to a red hexagon, which resembles a [[Red Rupee]].

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the Giant Wallet retains its maximum Rupee capacity of 500, which is required to purchase the [[All-Night Mask]]. The Giant Wallet is similarly acquired by defeating thirty Gold Skulltulas, this time in the [[Oceanside Spider House]] on the [[First Day]]. By defeating the Gold Skulltulas, Link can talk to [[Mutoh]]'s apprentice at the House's entrance, where he gratefully rewards Link the Giant Wallet. On the [[Second Day]] and [[Final Day]], the man instead rewards Purple Rupees and Red Rupees, respectively.

In {{MM3D}}, Mutoh's apprentice rewards Link with the Giant Wallet on any of the three days. 

==={{TP|-}}===
To obtain the Giant Wallet in {{TP}}, Link has to collect all 24 [[Golden Bug]]s and give them to [[Agitha]], who then rewards him with the Giant Wallet. In the item obtain text, the Giant Wallet is said to be for "distinguished citizens" in the insect kingdom.

In {{TP|-}}, the Giant Wallet is the second and final upgrade, capable of holding up to 1,000 Rupees. In {{TPHD}}, the Giant Wallet is instead the penultimate upgrade, behind the [[Colossal Wallet]], and the maximum carrying capacity of the Giant Wallet is doubled to 2,000 Rupees.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, Giant Wallet is the third and penultimate Wallet upgrade, behind only the [[Tycoon Wallet]]. Link can obtain it by giving fifty [[Gratitude Crystal]]s to [[Batreaux]]. The Giant Wallet can store up to 5,000 Rupees, or up to 5,900 Rupees with the three [[Extra Wallet]]s.
