{{Term/Store|Dr. Bean||Series, FPTRR}} is a character in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} He lives in an underground [[bottle]] found in the [[Steamy Marsh]].

[[Tingle]] needs to meet with Dr. Bean in order to complete several side-quests. From him, he can Obtain several empty jars, and even a [[Rupee Good]]. Dr. Bean has a relatively rude personality, and asks Tingle to leave immediately after speaking with him. He usually speaks as though he has been doing something rather important, although he never gives any details on these "projects" of his.
