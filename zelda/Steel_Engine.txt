The {{Term/Store|Steel Engine||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Steel Engine|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Steel Engine}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of two {{Plural|ST|Pearl Necklace|link}}, one {{Term|ST|Ruto Crown|link}} and one {{Term|ST|Goron Amber|link}}. It features a rigid, black design with red wheels, similar to a classic steam locomotive, and its description reads "This steam train appeals to strong, silent types everywhere."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Steel Engine}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Steel Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts. Using the {{Term|ST|Steel Engine}} also changes the {{Term|ST|Whistle|link}} to a fuller, lower-pitched sound.
