'''Nintendo Power (July/August 1988)''' is a [[:Category:Magazines|magazine]] published by [[Nintendo]].

==Zelda Content==
===Zelda: The Second Quest Begins (p. 26-37)===
The article features a complete walkthrough of the {{Term|TLoZ|Second Quest|link}} of {{TLoZ}} and detailed maps of its {{Term|TLoZ|Level 1|link}} to {{Term|TLoZ|Level 6|link}}. It also provides readers with detailed maps of both {{Plural|TLoZ|Overworld|link}} along with a "Buyer's Guide" revealing the prices of {{Plural|TLoZ|Merchant|link}}.

The article also teases some footage of {{TAoL}} such as its {{Term|TAoL|Overworld}} and {{Term|TAoL|Spell|link}} system.

===Howard & Nester (p. 55)===
The comic features [[Howard Phillips]] and a young boy named Nester. In the comic, Nester is struggling to find {{Term|TLoZ|Level 8|link}} and {{Term|TLoZ|Level 9|link}} in {{TLoZ|-}}. Wanting to know their locations but not admit he does not know them, Nester tricks Howard into telling him.

===Pak Watch (p. 86-87)===
The article announces the release of {{TAoL}} for October 1988.{{Note|name= TAoL October|This release date was not met as {{TAoL|-}} was released on December 1, 1988.}} It also revealed some of its plot and featured {{Term|TAoL|Link|link}} talking: "Hang on Zelda, it won't be long now. Your nightmare will soon be over!".

===NES Journal (p. 92-95)===
The article features an advertisement for {{T&T}}.

It also features information on {{Wp|Kirk Cameron|Kirk}} and {{Wp|Candace Cameron}}, revealing that {{TLoZ}} is one of their favorite games, although Candace had not yet completed it.

===Top 30 (p. 102-104)===
The section features the 30 most popular [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] games according to a poll held by [[Nintendo]]. {{TLoZ|-}} is in first position with 214 points.{{Note|name= Points|The total of points is divided by ten in the final ranking board.}}

The points for {{TLoZ|-}} go as follow:
* '''Player's Picks''': 1496 points {{Small|(Second position)}}
* '''Pro's Picks''': 323 points {{Small|(First position)}}
* '''Dealer's Picks''': 325 points {{Small|(Third position)}}
