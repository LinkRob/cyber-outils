==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Sizzlefin Trout}}
Sizzlefin Trout can be found swimming in [[Hot Springwater]] in the [[Eldin Canyon]] and [[Eldin Mountains]]. They're also sold at the [[Coral Reef]] in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} by [[Cleff]]. It has a special organ that stores heat, which will grant [[Cold Resistance]] to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} when [[Cooking|cooked]] into a dish.<ref name="Inventory"/> Eating one raw restores one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to Link's {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, but it can be exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to make [[Roasted Trout]] or chilled by [[Ice]] to make [[Frozen Trout]].

As is the case with other fish, Sizzlefin Trout can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make a [[Fish Skewer]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], and [[Salt-Grilled Fish]] in a [[Cooking Pot]]. Sizzlefin Trout are also among the seafood that can be used to make [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Meunière]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]].
