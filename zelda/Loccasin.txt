'''Loccasins'''<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote={{Exp|When Zelda defeats the three Loccasins, they will leave behind a magic brown jar.|Als Zelda de drie Loccasins verslaat laten ze een magische bruine kruik achter.}}|magazine= CD Interactief|volume= 3|issue= 12|date= December 1996|publisher=HUB Uitgevers|page=41}} <sup>(Note: The publication makes an error by mistakenly referring to the [[Bow & Arrow]] [[Spell]] as a magic brown jar.)</sup></ref> are enemies in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Loccasins are insect enemies that first appear in the [[Shrine of Air]], and later in the [[Shrine of Fire]]. The Loccasins of the Shrine of Air are large bugs that appear in swarms. They fly in erratic patterns and can be difficult to defeat with the Shrine's slippery floor. They have high endurance, and take four hits of the Wand to defeat. A swarm of three Loccasins inhabiting a giant stone hive near the Shrine's entrance will drop the Bow & Arrow Spell when defeated.

Loccasins have a smaller and weaker variant named [[Firefly (Zelda's Adventure)|Fireflies]], which are encountered in the Shrine of Fire.
