The {{Term/Store|Hero of the Four Sword||Series}} is the given title of a hero who defeated {{Term|Series|Vaati|link}} and who first sealed him into the {{Term|Series|Four Sword|link}} in the backstory to {{FS}} and {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=HH|page=82}}</ref>

==Story==
After the events of {{TMC}}, Vaati had been defeated with the newly forged {{Term|Series|Four Sword}} and had been presumed dead. However, {{Term|Series|Vaati}} once again arose and began terrorizing {{Term|Series|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Long ago, in the kingdom of Hyrule, there appeared a Wind Sorcerer named Vaati. Vaati could bend the wind to his will and used this awful power to terrorize many villages of Hyrule.|Prologue|FS}}</ref> Unlike the deceptive and patient sorcerer he had been in {{TMC|-}}, {{Term|Series|Vaati}} used brute force to defeat the kingdom's knights and kidnap girls from the villages.<ref>{{Cite|In his assaults on the villages, Vaati would kidnap any beautiful girls who caught his fancy. Many knights from the castle and other brave men set out to subdue the sorcerer and rescue the girls, but each one fell in turn to Vaati's awesome power.|Prologue|FS}}</ref> In response, the {{Term|Series|Hero of the Four Sword}} arose to challenge {{Term|Series|Vaati}}'s evil.

The origins of the {{Term|Series|Hero of the Four Sword}} are mysterious, as is his acquisition of the {{Term|Series|Four Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|Just as the people had begun to lose hope, a lone young boy traveling with little but a sword at his side appeared.|Prologue|FS}}</ref> Appearing when {{Term|Series|Vaati}} was already wreaking havoc, the Hero knew how to use the Sword's unique powers.<ref>{{Cite|The young girls told a story of how with just a wave of his sword, the boy's body shattered into four pieces, each of which then formed a complete copy of the boy.|Prologue|FS}}</ref> Whatever his origin, the hero sought to defeat {{Term|Series|Vaati}}, and succeeded in doing so by trapping the sorcerer in the Sword.<ref>{{Cite|When this boy heard what was happening, he said only, "I will defeat this sorcerer." He boldly entered Vaati's palace, mystically trapped the evil sorcerer inside the blade of his sword, and returned the young girls to their villages.|Prologue|FS}}</ref>

With his work finished, the boy vanished into the forest,<ref>{{Cite|The boy then went deep into the forest and disappeared.|Prologue|FS}}</ref> and the story of his victory was subsequently spread by the girls he rescued.<ref>{{Cite|The villagers asked the girls how a boy so young could have saved them all and defeated the sorcerer when no one else could.|Prologue|FS}}</ref>

==Timeline placement==
In the ''[[Hyrule Historia]]'' guidebook, this hero is given the title of the "Hero of the Four Sword" and is not the same hero who appears in {{TMC|-}} or {{FS|-}}, but is instead another hero who appears in between the two games. He is described as someone about whom little is known, but he appears to be the descendant of {{Term|TMC|Link|display=the hero|link}} who previously defeated Vaati in {{TMC|-}}. The {{Term|Series|Hero of the Four Sword}} is the second hero not identified with {{Term|Series|Link}} to wield the {{Term|Series|Picori Blade|link}} (in its original or reforged forms), after the {{Term|TMC|Hero of Men|link}}, who appeared during the {{Term|TMC|Minish Legend|link}}.
