==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>This is {{Color|SS Blue|Parrow}}, a staightforward and honest young man. He exhibits remarkable skill at handling Loftwings, even for a {{Color|SS Blue|Skyloft}} resident.</p><p>He and his younger sister {{Color|SS Blue|Orielle}} are both known for their strong affinity for Loftwings.</p>
}}

Parrow lives with his younger sister [[Orielle]] in [[Skyloft]]. Both he and his sister share a large interest in [[Loftwing]]s, and both have experience in handling them. He can be found hanging around the Plaza during the day. 

Parrow is involved in a sidequest in which he is worried about Orielle, who rode her Loftwing to visit [[Fun Fun Island]] and has not since returned.<ref>{{Cite|So you see, a short while ago my {{Color|SS Red|little sister}} went out for a quick flight, but she hasn't come home yet. It was getting late for her to still be out, so I went looking for her everywhere... But I couldn't find her. I just hope nothing has happened. ...I'm worried sick thinking about it. Hey, Link, would you mind helping me look for her? She said she was curious about that {{Color|SS Red|colorful}} island that has appeared recently, so I suspect she's somewhere in the {{Color|SS Red|south}}western {{Color|SS Red|sky}}. I'm counting on you, Link!|Parrow|SS}}</ref> He asks Link to help him look for her. Link eventually finds Orielle on a small island nursing her injured Loftwing, which had crash landed on the island. When Link returns to Parrow, he gives him a [[Bottle]] of [[Mushroom Spores]] to bring back for her bird to heal. As a result, the siblings are reunited, and Link is allowed to keep the empty Bottle. He also receives ten [[Gratitude Crystal]]s, five from each sibling.

==Nomenclature==
Parrow's name is derived from {{Wp|sparrow}}, a bird.
