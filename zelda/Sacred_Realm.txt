The {{Term/Store|Sacred Realm||Series, OoT, OoT3D}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 10 {{Exp Game|OoT, ALBW}}, 24 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}, 30 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref> also known as the '''Golden Land''',<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT|publisher= Nintendo|page= 6}}</ref> is a holy world connected to the land of [[Hyrule]], said to be the resting place of the [[Triforce]].<ref>{{Cite|This evil man ceaselessly uses his vile, sorcerous powers in his search for the Sacred Realm that is connected to Hyrule...|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Long ago, in the beautiful kingdom of Hyrule surrounded by mountains and forests... legends told of an omnipotent and omniscient Golden Power that resided in a hidden land. Many people aggressively sought to enter the hidden Golden Land...|N/A|ALttP}}</ref> The three [[Golden Goddesses]], [[Din (Goddess)|Din]], [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]], and [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]], respectively, once they finished the creation of the land of Hyrule and its inhabitants, left behind a symbol of their strength, the Triforce, before departing for the heavens. The land where they left behind the golden power was said to be the Sacred Realm.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=After the gods had finished their work, they left the world, but not before creating a symbol of their strength, a golden triangle known as the Triforce.|game=ALttP|page=3}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The three great goddesses, their labors completed, departed for the heavens. And golden sacred triangles remained at the point where the goddesses left the world. Since then, the sacred triangles have become the basis of our world's providence. And, the resting place of the triangles has become the Sacred Realm.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> The realm is said to be of twilight golden skies rather than the blue of Hyrule.<ref name=Quote8>{{Cite|In a realm beyond sight,<br/>The sky shines gold, not blue.<br/>There, the Triforce's might<br/>Makes mortal dreams come true.|Book of Mudora|ALttP}}</ref>

The Golden Goddesses left the omnipotent Triforce under the protection of the [[Hylia|Goddess Hylia]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Upon leaving the world, the goddesses left behind the Triforce: three golden triangles. [...] The golden goddesses departed from the world they created and left the Triforce in care of the goddess Hylia.|book={{HH}}|page=70|publisher=Dark Horse Books}}</ref> During the [[Era of Chaos]], the [[Temple of Light]], located at the center of the Sacred Realm, became the resting place of the Triforce, and was isolated from Hyrule, with the [[Temple of Time]] being the only entrance, as seen in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''.<ref name=page77>{{Cite Book|quote=The Triforce was placed in the Temple of Light, located in the Sacred Realm, which was now isolated from Hyrule.|book={{HH}}|page=77|publisher=Dark Horse Books}}</ref><ref name=Quote6>{{Cite|The Temple of Light, situated in the very center of the Sacred Realm, is the last stronghold against Ganondorf's evil forces.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref>

The Sacred Realm would serve as a mirror to reflect the heart of any who would enter it and come to claim the Triforce as their own.<ref name=Quote13>{{Cite|The resting place of the sacred triangle, the Sacred Realm, is a mirror that reflects what is in the heart...the heart of one who enters it...|Sheik|OoT}}</ref> If that person's heart was pure, the realm would become a paradise and gain the True Force. However, if that person's heart was wicked, the Sacred Realm would become a [[Dark World|world of evil]] and the Triforce itself also splits apart into [[Triforce of Power|three]] [[Triforce of Wisdom|separate]] [[Triforce of Courage|pieces]], making them gain only the part of the Triforce they most believed (Power in all cases so far). It is thought that if [[Link|another]] challenges and defeat the [[Ganon|one who shattered the Triforce]], the Sacred Realm and the golden relic would both return to its former beauty.<ref name=Quote14>{{Cite|If an evil heart, the Realm will become full of evil; if pure, the Realm will become a paradise.|Sheik|OoT}}</ref><ref name=Quote10>{{Cite|But now, you have totally destroyed Ganon. His Dark World will vanish.|Essence of the Triforce|ALttP}}</ref>

The [[Sage]]s and the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]] protect the Sacred Realm from the forces of evil.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book=E |publisher= |page=24 }}</ref>

Due to the aforementioned feats, like the Triforce, the Sacred Realm plays a very important role in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] and it is one of [[Princess Zelda]] and [[Link]]'s main objectives to defend it from evil, specifically their archenemy [[Ganon]].

==Historical Appearances==
The Sacred Realm makes several appearances throughout ''The Legend of Zelda'' series and is mentioned by multiple characters in stories and legends. By in-game appearances, it is first seen in the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]] title ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' as the Golden Land of the Triforce, which was corrupted into the foreboding [[Dark World]] by [[Ganon]].<ref name=Quote18>{{Cite|The place where you now stand was the Golden Land, but evil power turned it into the Dark World.|Sahasrahla|ALttP}}</ref> This story of the Sacred Realm is later expanded upon in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', which explains that the realm had been created by the three Golden Goddesses shortly after Hyrule's creation and serves as the resting place of the Triforce.<ref name=Quote3>{{Cite|The three great goddesses, their labors completed, departed for the heavens. And golden sacred triangles remained at the point where the goddesses left the world. [...] And, the resting place of the triangles has become the Sacred Realm.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> The evil [[Gerudo]] King of Thieves [[Ganondorf]] seeks to enter this mystical plane and take the Triforce for himself, and succeeds with the unwitting assistance of [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]]. As a result, the legend speaking of the Sacred Realm comes true when Ganondorf touches the complete Triforce and shatters it into its three component parts, the [[Triforce of Power]], [[Triforce of Wisdom|Wisdom]], and [[Triforce of Courage|Courage]], resulting in the Sacred Realm's transformation into the Evil Realm and Ganondorf's transformation into his true form, the Demon King Ganon, via the Triforce of Power he stolen.<ref name="Quote4">{{Cite|Seven years ago, Ganondorf, the King of Thieves, used the door you opened in the Temple of Time and entered the Sacred Realm. But when he laid his hands on the Triforce, the legend came true.|Sheik|OoT}}</ref><ref name="Dark Horse Books">{{Cite Book|quote=The Sacred Realm is a mirror that reflects the hearts of those who set foot in it. An evil heart will turn the realm into a living hell. A pure heart will transform it into paradise. Using the Triforce of Power, Ganondorf became the Demon King. The Sacred Realm was distorted into a nightmarish world where demons ran amuck.|book=HH|page=87}}</ref> At the end of the [[Nintendo 64]] game, Ganondorf is sealed within the void of the Sacred Realm, still in its corrupted state, by the [[Sage#Ocarina of Time|Seven Sages]] upon his ultimate defeat by the [[Hero of Time]].<ref name=Quote5>{{Cite|Ancient Creators of Hyrule! Now, open the sealed door and send the Evil Incarnation of Darkness into the void of the Evil Realm!!|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> The Sacred Realm then makes another appearance in the story of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', which speaks of how the ancestors of the [[Twili]], the [[Dark Interlopers]], attempted to break into the Sacred Realm and claim the Triforce for themselves, sparking a [[Interloper War|civil war]] that resulted in much bloodshed over lust for the Triforce's power as well as the banishment of the Dark Interlopers to the [[Twilight Realm]], where they evolved into the Twili race.

=== The Era of Chaos and the Era of Prosperity===
Many years after the events of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'', there was an [[Zelda Timeline#The Era of Chaos|era of chaos]] with wars and fighting over the [[Triforce]] that was located in the Sacred Realm. To put an end to these senseless battles, an ancient sage, [[Rauru]], built over the remains of the [[Sealed Temple]] a new Temple of Time, which contained the only entrance to the Sacred Realm.<ref name="ReferenceA">{{Cite Book|quote=Rauru, the Sage of Light, constructed the Temple of Time, which contained the only existing entrance to the Sacred Realm, where the Triforce was located.|book={{HH}}|page=77|publisher=Dark Horse Books}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Temple of Time: The only entrance to the Sacred Realm's Temple of Light, the Temple of Time shares the same name as the temple in the Lanayru Desert of the Era of the Goddess Hylia. It is thought that Rauru built the Temple of Time directly over the ruins of the Sealed Temple.|book=HH|page=77}}</ref> Using the [[Master Sword]] and the three [[Spiritual Stones]] as keys to the Sacred Realm, this new temple was the sanctified gateway between [[Hyrule]] and the Sacred Realm, and its purpose was to protect that gateway, and thus the Triforce, from evildoers who would seek to misuse it.<ref>{{Cite|The Temple of Time is the entrance through which you can enter the Sacred Realm from our world.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ages ago, we ancient Sages built the Temple of Time to protect the entrance to the Sacred Realm...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So, the ancient Sages built the Temple of Time to protect the Triforce from evil ones.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Inside the Temple of Time is a chamber that is home to the [[Master Sword]] and the [[Pedestal of Time]]; however, the entrance to this chamber is sealed by the Door of Time.<ref>{{Cite|But the entrance is sealed with a stone wall called the Door of Time. And, in order to open the door, it is said that you need to collect three Spiritual Stones.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> When the Master Sword was infused into the Pedestal of Time, it sealed that gateway in the hopes of preventing all but the most worthy to reach the Sacred Realm, where the sacred Triforce lies within the [[Temple of Light]].

==={{OoT|-}}===
The Sacred Realm makes its next appearance (and first under that name) in {{OoT|-}}, which takes place many centuries prior to {{ALttP|-}} according to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|''Hyrule Historia'']]. The game begins at a time when the Sacred Realm has never been successfully opened and the only known gateway to it resides in the [[Temple of Time]]. This game also shows the origins of [[Ganondorf]], the thief spoken of in the backstory of {{ALttP|-}}, where he is depicted fittingly as the leader of a band of thieves, the [[Gerudo]], residing in the desert of Hyrule, namely [[Gerudo Valley]]. Ganondorf somehow learns of the legends of the magical Triforce and lusts for its power. He travels to the land of Hyrule in search of a way into the Sacred Realm so he can steal the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite|A wicked man of the desert cast this dreadful curse upon me... This evil man ceaselessly uses his vile, sorcerous powers in his search for the Sacred Realm that is connected to Hyrule.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref>

The Sacred Realm is first seen in the tale of Hyrule's creation told by the [[Great Deku Tree]] to the game's hero, [[Link]], the legendary Hero of Time, where it is shown as a magical place that serves as the home of the legendary Triforce and has not been visited by any in living memory since the departure of the [[Golden Goddess|Goddesses]] back to the heavens. The Triforce is shown to be shining down upon the Sacred Realm from above in this story as well as a later one told by [[Princess Zelda]] to Link, awaiting the time when one worthy of its power would come to claim it from its hiding place.

====Creation====
[[File:Triforce (Ocarina of Time).png|238x238px|thumb|right|The Triforce, shining down upon the Sacred Realm from above, as seen in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
The Sacred Realm is first seen and mentioned within the game by the [[Great Deku Tree]], who after being relieved of a debilitating curse by Link sees fit to give the young hero information about his coming quest to stop the evil Gerudo King [[Ganondorf]] and relates to him the story of Hyrule's creation as well as the creation of the Sacred Realm itself.<ref>{{Cite|Thou must never suffer that man, with his evil heart, to enter the Sacred Realm of legend...That evil man who cast the death curse upon me and sapped my power...|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> In his story, the Sacred Realm is shown to be a serene place that serves primarily as the home of the Triforce, with the magical relic shining down upon the realm from above.<ref name=Quote3/>

The Deku Tree explains that after the three Golden Goddesses ([[Din (Goddess)|Din]], [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]], and [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]]) breathed their essences into the world of Hyrule, they also created a parallel dimension to Hyrule that was the very place from which they departed the world to return to the heavens. The Goddesses also chose to leave a symbol of their power at that same location. Three golden sacred triangles were left there, joined to form one single larger triangle, each representing the three forces of Power, Wisdom, and Courage.<ref name=Quote3/> This golden sacred triangle came to be known as the Triforce, a very powerful relic that came to be the basis of Hyrule's providence.<ref>{{Cite|Since then, the sacred triangles have become the basis of our world's providence.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> And the lands that served as the home of the Triforce came to be known as the Sacred Realm.
====The Legend of the Triforce====
Princess Zelda is the next to speak of the Sacred Realm within the game, relating to Link the story of the Triforce. She states that the Golden Goddesses left the Triforce in the Sacred Realm at the time of Hyrule's creation and that the ancient relic possesses the power to grant the wish of one who holds the Triforce in his or her hands.<ref>{{Cite|The power to grant the wish of the one who holds the Triforce in his hands.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> A similar image to the one shown by the Deku Tree of the Triforce within the Sacred Realm is shown, revealing the Triforce to be the primary point of interest within the Sacred Realm. She states also that the only known entrance to the Sacred Realm from the world of Hyrule is the [[Temple of Time]], an ancient structure located very close to [[Hyrule Castle Town]]'s marketplace.<ref name=Quote11>{{Cite| The Temple of Time is the entrance through which you can enter the Sacred Realm from our world.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> In addition, in order to open the gateway between the two worlds, one would need the three Spiritual Stones kept by Hyrule's various races as well as the Royal Family's sacred treasure, the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]].<ref name=Quote17>{{Cite|But the entrance is sealed with a stone wall called the Door of Time. And, in order to open the door, it is said that you need to collect three Spiritual Stones. And another thing you need...is the treasure that the Royal Family keeps along with this legend... The Ocarina of Time!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> When one who possesses the three stones stands with the Ocarina of Time and plays the mystical [[Song of Time]] within the Temple of Time, the gateway between the two worlds could then be opened, yielding access to the Sacred Realm.<ref name=Quote12>{{Cite|At least I could leave you the Ocarina and this melody... This song opens the Door of Time...|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref>

====Transformation into the Evil Realm====
[[File:Ganon Sacred Realm.png|thumb|right|260x260px|Ganondorf in the Sacred Realm]]
The seal between Hyrule and the Sacred Realm is finally broken when Link gains possession of the three Spiritual Stones and the Ocarina of Time from Hyrule's differing races and Princess Zelda. He journeys to the [[Temple of Time]] to protect the Triforce from Ganondorf and uses the collection of objects to open the [[Door of Time]], the mighty stone barrier separating the two worlds.<ref>{{Cite|Play this melody in front of the altar in the Temple of Time. You must protect the Triforce!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Beyond the Door of Time rests the [[Pedestal of Time]] and the mythical [[Master Sword]], the lock and key to the portal between the worlds. Link removes the legendary sword from its pedestal, unwittingly opening the portal between Hyrule and the Sacred Realm, but is suddenly put into suspended animation for seven years because he was too young of age to properly wield the Master Sword at the time.<ref name=Quote16>{{Cite|The Master Sword--the evil-destroying sword that you pulled out of the Pedestal of Time--was the final key to the Sacred Realm.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|However, you were too young to be the Hero of Time... Therefore, your spirit was sealed here for seven years.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> Ganondorf is then shown entering the Sacred Realm through the portal Link opened and is later revealed by the Sage of Light [[Rauru]] to have obtained the complete Triforce from the [[Temple of Light]] at the heart of the Sacred Realm, causing his power to radiate from the temples of Hyrule and to transform the Sacred Realm into a [[Dark World|corrupt world]], with only the Temple of Light itself remaining free of his evil power.<ref>{{Cite|As I thought, you held the keys to the Door of Time!You have led me to the gates of the Sacred Realm...Yes, I owe it all to you, kid!|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref><ref name=Quote15>{{Cite|Ganondorf, the Gerudo King of Thieves, used it to enter this forbidden Sacred Realm! He obtained the Triforce from the Temple of Light, and with its power, he became the King of Evil... His evil power radiated from the temples of Hyrule, and in seven short years, it transformed Hyrule into a world of monsters. My power now has only little influence, even in this Sacred Realm...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> Link himself never visits the areas of the Sacred Realm surrounding the Temple of Light within the game, instead being warped to the [[Chamber of Sages]] at the temple's heart each time he succeeds in awakening one of the [[Sage|Six Sages]] - the vast majority turned out to be his childhood friends.

====A Mirror That Reflects What is in the Heart====
Toward the end of the game, Princess Zelda, as [[Sheik]], reveals a final, previously unknown legend of the Triforce that explains what happened to the Sacred Realm after Ganondorf's invasion of it.<ref>{{Cite|And the other, who holds the Triforce of Wisdom...is the seventh Sage, who is destined to be the leader of them all... It is I, the Princess of Hyrule, Zelda.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> According to legend, the resting place of the [[Triforce|Sacred Triangle]], the Sacred Realm, is a mirror that reflects what is in the heart of one who enters it and touches the Triforce with a wish in their heart.<ref name=Quote13/> If that one's heart is pure, the realm will be defined by the purity of his or her heart, transforming the Sacred Realm into a paradise.<ref name=Quote14/> However, if that one's heart is not in balance, the Triforce will separate into its three components, the Triforce of Power, Triforce of Wisdom, and Triforce of Courage, leaving only the part representing the force that one most believes in (that case, Power) and transforming the Sacred Realm into a world of evil that is reflected into the heart of the evil one that shattered the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite|But, if that one's heart is not in balance, the Triforce will separate into three parts: Power, Wisdom and Courage. Only one part will remain for the one who touched the Triforce...the part representing the force that one most believes in.|Sheik|OoT}}</ref> Only when this evil person is utterly defeated can the Sacred Realm then be restored to its former glory.

====The Seal of the Seven Sages====
[[File:chamberofsages.jpg|thumb|right|315x315px|The Six Sages create their seal within the Chamber of Sages deep in the heart of the Temple of Light to seal Ganondorf inside the Evil Realm.]]
In the conclusion of {{OoT|-}}, the Seven Sages, with the assistance of the Hero of Time, are able to throw Ganon back into the Sacred Realm-turned-Evil Realm upon his brutal defeat and seal the portal to prevent him, now as Ganondorf, from further destroying the land of Hyrule.<ref name=Quote5/> Using the [[Power to Repel Evil|power of the Master Sword]], Link is able to near-fatally wound Ganon (with the assistance of Princess Zelda) by a powerful blow and stun him long enough to buy the Sages the precious time they needed to bind the vicious [[demon]] and throw him back into the void of the Evil Realm before creating their seal.<ref>{{Cite|I'm using my power to hold the Evil King! You use your sword and deliver the final blow!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> As he drifts away into the void of the realm, Ganondorf angrily screams that as long as the [[Triforce of Power]] is still in his hand, he will be able to return one day from the Evil Realm and exterminate the descendants of those who defeated him.<ref name=Quote39>{{Cite|Someday... When this seal is broken.... That is when I will exterminate your descendants!! As long as the Triforce of Power is in my hand....|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref> This is supposedly the seal Ganon was attempting to break in {{ALttP|-}}, as it prevented him from being able to return to Hyrule, as well as the one being indeed broken in the prologue of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]''. The game ends with the Triforce remaining separated between its three pre-destined holders (Ganon, Princess Zelda, and Link), and the Evil Realm itself now serves as Ganondorf's prison. Therefore, because Ganon was never outright killed by Link in the [[Nintendo 64|N64]] title, unlike ''A Link to the Past'' or ''Twilight Princess'', the Sacred Realm was not restored.
==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:ALttPGoldenLand.png|thumb|325x325px|A depiction of the Golden Land and the Triforce in the opening of {{ALttP|-}}.]]
The Sacred Realm is first mentioned in the opening story of {{ALttP|-}}, where it is depicted as the mythical "Golden Land" where the wish-granting Triforce is said to reside.<ref name=Quote6/> It is said to have been a beautiful realm where the sky shined gold rather than blue.<ref name="Quote8"/> This magical realm was said to be home to the Temple of Light, where the Triforce would shine down upon the Golden Land from above, beckoning to the outside world for one to come in search of it.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=From its hiding place in the so-called Golden Land where the gods placed it, the Triforce beckoned people from the outside world to seek it in the hope that someone worthy of these titles would find it.|game= ALttP|page=3–4}}</ref>

==== The Imprisoning War and the Spoiling of the Golden Land====
During the [[Downfall Timeline]], Link, the [[Hero of Time]], despite his best efforts, is tragically [[Game Over|defeated]] by Ganon in the final battle. As a result, Ganondorf ultimately steals the remaining Triforce pieces from Link and Princess Zelda, allowing him to complete the Triforce; thus making him unstoppable. In a desperate last resort, the Seven Sages seal Ganon along with the complete Triforce away in the Sacred Realm, which was already transformed into the [[Dark World]], as a reflection of Ganondorf's evil heart, similar to the true ending of ''Ocarina of Time''.<ref name="Dark Horse Books"/><ref>{{Cite|The resting place of the sacred triangle, the Sacred Realm, is a mirror that reflects what is in the heart... the heart of one who enters it... If an evil heart, the Realm will become full of evil; if pure, the Realm will become a paradise.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Hyrule enjoys a brief period of peace, but as time passes, rumors spread of the Golden Power lying in the Sacred Realm, many greedy people traveled to the Sacred Realm seeking it, but never to return. Within the Dark World, they were transformed into monsters by the power of the Triforce and became a part of Ganon's army. From here, he launched an invasion of Hyrule that was repelled by the [[Knight of Hyrule|Knights of Hyrule]] and the [[Sage|Seven Wise Men]].<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=[...]Ganon's evil army swarmed from the tainted Golden Land into Hyrule and attacked the castle. The wise men and the Knights Of Hyrule combined forces to wage war on this evil horde.|game= ALttP|page=5–6}}</ref> The Sages then sealed the gateway to the Golden Land that Ganondorf had discovered, locking him away within the now-tainted Sacred Realm in a conflict that came to be known as the [[Imprisoning War]].<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=However, their lives were not lost in vain, for they bought precious time for the Seven Wise Men to magically seal Ganon in the Golden Land.|game= ALttP|page=6}}</ref>

====Restoration of the Golden Land====
[[File:ALttP Triforce Credits Scene.png|thumb|right|256x256px|Link reclaims the complete Triforce after Ganon's destruction within the Pyramid of Power.]]
The Golden Land remained corrupted under Ganon's rule for many generations as he built up his army in what was now known as the Dark World.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure he's intending to conquer even our Light World after building his power here.|Maiden|ALttP}}</ref> Eventually he enacts a scheme to break the seal between the worlds and free himself from the Dark World.<ref>{{Cite|He is trying to open a larger gate between worlds near the castle using our powers.|Maiden|ALttP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ganon was building up his power here so he could conquer the Light World and make his wish come completely true.|Essence of the Triforce|ALttP}}</ref> A new Hero rises to stop this plot, and the hero, [[Link]], is drawn into the Dark World (formerly the Golden Land) by the Dark Wizard [[Agahnim]], who was serving as a vessel for Ganon's soul in the [[Light World]]. Upon his first visit to the Dark World, he discovers that Ganon has used the Triforce to place a curse over the realm that transforms any not possessing a magical gem known as the [[Moon Pearl]] into a shape reflecting what is in their heart, including Link himself which he will change into a [[Bunny Link|bunny]].<ref name=Quote20>{{Cite|The Golden Power is what changed your shape to reflect what is in your heart and mind. But if you have a ball called the Moon Pearl, you can keep your original shape here.|Bully's Friend|ALttP}}</ref> The young hero scours this Evil Realm and frees the descendants of the Seven Wise Men, the [[Maiden|Seven Maidens]] (one of which is Princess Zelda herself), from Ganon's monsters all across the realm.<ref>{{Cite|You must also rescue the seven maidens who Agahnim sent to the Dark World. As members of the blood-line of the Seven Wise Men, they have power that will surely help you. The maidens are locked in hidden dungeons full of evil creatures and dangerous traps.|Sahasrahla|ALttP}}</ref> Finally, after killing Agahnim, he faces Ganon himself at the heart of the Dark World, the [[Pyramid of Power]], and utterly destorys him with the power of the [[Master Sword]] and [[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]]. Link is then introduced to the [[Essence of the Triforce]], the magical entity that animates the ancient relic. The Essence of the Triforce explains that the Dark World was formerly the Sacred Realm where the Triforce had resided since ancient times, before Ganon found his way into the realm by accident and laid claim to the Triforce, causing the realm's transformation into the Dark World.<ref name=Quote19>{{Cite|Ganon's wish was to conquer the world. That wish changed the Golden Land to the Dark World.|Essence of the Triforce|ALttP}}</ref> It goes on to explain that the Triforce will now await a new master, and that the Dark World will vanish entirely upon the new master's wish before going on to instruct the Hero of Legend to touch the Triforce with a wish in his heart. Link touches the full Triforce and wishes all of Ganon's evil to be undone, which returns peace and prosperity to the land of Hyrule and restores the Golden Land to its former self.<ref name=Quote10/><ref>{{Cite|The Triforce is waiting for a new owner. Its Golden Power is in your hands... Now, touch it with a wish in your heart.|Essence of the Triforce|ALttP}}</ref>
==={{ALBW|-}}===
[[File:ALBW Sacred Realm Triforce.png|250px|thumb|right|The Triforce in Hyrule's Sacred Realm]]
The Sacred Realm makes a brief appearance at the end of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]''.<ref>{{Cite|How rare it is to visit our sacred realm!|Zelda|ALBW}}</ref> Only a tiny portion of the realm (a small outdoor path studded with columns, which may in fact be a part of the Temple of Light) is shown. From this tiny area, it can be seen that the sky is blue, despite its previous restoration in {{ALttP|-}}, which should have turned the skies golden. Several floating structures can also be seen, which coincides with artwork in the former game.

It is also established at this time that [[Lorule]] has its own version of the Sacred Realm.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Lorule's sacred realm.|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref> In a parallel version of the area shown in Hyrule's, there exists a large, cracked tablet that serves to link the two worlds. [[Hilda|Princess Hilda]] utilizes this tablet, along with [[Ravio's Bracelet]], to send Link and Princess Zelda back to Hyrule. This tablet is destroyed once Lorule's Triforce is restored. What this means for the connection between the two worlds is not known.

==={{TP|-}}===
In yet another appearance, the Sacred Realm features into a story told in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'' by the [[Spirits of Light|Light Spirit]] [[Lanayru (Spirit)|Lanayru]]. When Link comes to the [[Lanayru Province]] in search of the final fragment of the [[Fused Shadow]] and light has been restored to the Lanayru Province, the Light Spirit relates to Link the story of the [[Dark Interlopers]], the ancestors of the [[Twili]]. 

====The Story of the Interlopers====
[[File:TP Sacred Realm 2.jpg|356x356px|right|thumb|The Sacred Realm and the Triforce, as shown in Lanayru's tale]]
In this story, Lanayru shows Link images of the creation of Hyrule by the [[Golden Goddess|Golden Goddesses]] as well as the Sacred Realm itself, speaking of its beauty and how the Triforce came into being. The goddesses descended upon the then chaotic world and granted peace and life to the world, and once they granted equal power to all the living beings, they returned to a land in the heavens that came to be known as the Sacred Realm.<ref>{{Cite|When all was chaos, the goddesses descended and gave order and life to the world. They granted power equally to all who dwelt in the light, and then returned to the heavens. The lands where the goddesses descended came to be known as the Sacred Realm.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> For ages, the people lived content in mind and body, but soon word of the Sacred Realm spread across the world, along with that of the wish-granting Triforce it contained.<ref>{{Cite|For ages, the people lived at ease, content in mind and body... But soon, word of the Sacred Realm spread through Hyrule, and a great battle ensued...|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> Tales spoke of how the Triforce could make mortal dreams come true and would grant the wishes of whoever held it as long as they lived. Lust for the Triforce's power caused much bloodshed amongst Hyrule's people until finally a group of powerful sorcerers, the Dark Interlopers, attempted to seize control of the Sacred Realm and take the Triforce for their own (by means of a mighty weapon that was of their own design, the [[Fused Shadow]]), sparking a [[Interloper War|civil war]] that raged across Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|Among those living in the light, interlopers who excelled at magic appeared. Wielding powerful sorcery, they tried to establish dominion over the Sacred Realm.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> As this war dragged on, the four [[Light Spirits]] intervened at the behest of the Golden Goddesses, chasing the Dark Interlopers across Hyrule and into the twilight, locking them away in the [[Twilight Realm]] and shattering the Fused Shadow into four fragments that were separated henceforth to prevent the weapon's misuse.<ref>{{Cite|It was then that the goddesses ordered us three light spirits to intervene. We sealed away the great magic those individuals had mastered.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> As time would go on, the Interlopers would grow into a gentler race in the form of the [[Twili]]. Lanayru finished his tale by warning the Hero of Twilight that those who do not know the dangers of wielding power will soon be ruled by it instead.<ref>{{Cite|Those who do not know the danger of wielding power will, before long, be ruled by it.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref>

==The Temple of Light==
{{main|Temple of Light}}
The [[Temple of Light]] is a mighty temple located at the center of the Sacred Realm, that became the resting place of the Triforce during the [[Era of Chaos]].<ref name=page77/><ref name=Quote6/><ref name=Quote15/> The Temple of Light remained the only place within the realm that was not corrupted by [[Ganondorf]]'s evil influence according to [[Rauru]]. Rauru's personal history remains shrouded in mystery, and it is unknown how he came to reside in the Sacred Realm or when, but he is depicted as the guardian of the Temple of Light and is depicted to be ancient in age. After obtaining the Triforce, Ganondorf's power radiated from the temples of Hyrule and allowed him to transform Hyrule into a world of shadows while the Sacred Realm was reflected into his evil heart and became a world of evil monsters and dark skies. To date, the only confirmed locale visited within the Temple of Light is the [[Chamber of Sages]], where the [[Sage]]s gather to meet in council.<ref>{{Cite|This is the Chamber of Sages, inside the Temple of Light...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref>

== The Temple of Time==
{{main|Temple of Time}}
[[File:TempleofTime.jpg|left|thumb|227x227px|The Temple of Time in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
The [[Temple of Time]] is an ancient structure located in the Kingdom of Hyrule, and is best known for being the sanctified gateway between Hyrule and the Sacred Realm.<ref name=Quote11/> Constructed by the ancient Sage [[Rauru]] during the [[Era of Chaos]], the temple contains the only, sealed entrance to the Sacred Realm, where the Triforce is located.<ref name="ReferenceA"/><ref>{{Cite|So, the ancient Sages built the Temple of Time to protect the Triforce from evil ones.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> The [[Master Sword]], embedded in the Pedestal of Time, serves as the key and lock respectively that can open the portal between the worlds.<ref name=Quote16/> The sacred sword can only be drawn by [[Link|one pure of heart]] that can claim the mantle of "Hero of Time".<ref>{{Cite|Only one worthy of the title of "Hero of Time" can pull it from the Pedestal of Time...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> The Chamber of the Master Sword is in turn protected by a mighty magical barrier known as the [[Door of Time]], which can only be opened by one possessing the three [[Spiritual Stones]] (the [[Kokiri's Emerald]], the [[Goron's Ruby]], and the [[Zora's Sapphire]]) and the magical instrument known as the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]] (as well as knowledge of a mysterious song known only as the "[[Song of Time]]").<ref name=Quote17/><ref>{{Cite|Ye who owns 3 Spiritual Stones / Stand with the Ocarina of Time / And play the Song of Time|N/A|OoT}}</ref> Taken together, this set of items would open the Door of Time and yield access to the Chamber of the Master Sword, and by extension the Sacred Realm itself.
==The Dark World==
{{main|Dark World}}
[[File:Darkworldalttp.png|thumb|right|286x286px|The Dark World, formerly the Golden Land (Sacred Realm), as seen in {{ALttP|-}}.]]
The Sacred Realm has only ever been toured in gameplay in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' within [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], but in the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] installment it appears as the corrupt [[Dark World]] ruled by the demonic [[Ganon]] rather than its standard form. The Dark World was previously the Golden Land that was home to the [[Triforce]] before Ganon's incursion into the realm and the subsequent theft of the Triforce, resulting in the transformation of the realm itself and the creation of the Dark World.<ref name=Quote18/> In this form, the Sacred Realm appeared as a twisted, distorted copy of Hyrule that was meant to serve Ganon's wish to be the ruler of the world.<ref name=Quote19/> It was filled with dangerous evil creatures, and the spirits of the realm and any who ventured into the realm henceforth suffered as they were transformed by the Triforce's magic into a shape reflecting what was in their heart, [[Bunny Link|including Link]] if he does not possess the [[Moon Pearl]], leaving them helpless and to pray for the day when a Legendary Hero would come to challenge Ganon and take back the Golden Power, the Triforce.<ref name=Quote20/> The Dark World was plagued by dark skies and the very air was as poisonous fumes. As a mirror to Hyrule, the Dark World featured several similar locales to its Light World counterparts, though they were typically inverted to reflect the darkness of the world in which they were found. For example, [[Kakariko Village]] of the [[Light World]] (Hyrule) was replaced in the Dark World (the Sacred Realm) by the [[Village of Outcasts]]. And replacing the [[Tower of Hera (A Link to the Past)|Tower of Hera]] of the Light World high atop the Dark World's version of [[Death Mountain]] sat Ganon's stronghold, [[Ganon's Tower (A Link to the Past)|Ganon's Tower]]. The Dark World eventually vanished after Link utterly defeated Ganon and took back the Triforce for himself at the conclusion of {{ALttP|-}}, restoring the Sacred Realm to its former beauty, as it was before the Demon King ever entered it.<ref name=Quote10/>
