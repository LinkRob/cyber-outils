{{Term/Store|Flamola|Flamolas|Series, ALBW|plural}} are fiery relatives of {{Plural|ALBW|Swamola|link}} and {{Plural|ALBW|Lanmola|link}} that appear in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 177}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ALBW|Flamola}} live in {{Term|ALBW|Turtle Rock|link}}, swimming through the magma and jumping over walkways that {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} needs to cross. They can be defeated easily by hitting them with the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Rod|link}} or {{Plural|ALBW|Sword Beam|link}} from the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword|link}} while they are jumping out of the magma. {{Plural|ALBW|Flamola}} are made of magma and exert intense heat, so if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} hits a {{Term|ALBW|Flamola}} with the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword}}, or comes in any direct contact with a {{Term|ALBW|Flamola}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} will be burned.  Killing all of the {{Plural|ALBW|Flamola}} in {{Term|ALBW|Turtle Rock}} will make a {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Chest|link}} appear, which contains a {{Term|ALBW|Silver Rupee|link}}.
