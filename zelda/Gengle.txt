{{Term/Store|Gengle||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/>

==Biography==
Gengle is [[Jovani]]'s [[Cat]]. When his owner got cursed, Gengle became solid gold on top of his head.<ref>{{Cite|I can't move... I can't go see my girlfriend... My pet cat Gengle is frozen on my head... I don't think I could be more miserable...|Jovani|TP}}</ref> If [[Link]] gets 20 [[Poe Soul]]s, Gengle will return to normal. However, Jovani will remain solid gold until Link gets the rest of the Poe Souls.<ref>{{Cite|But...my body is still golden... Does this mean my soul is still not whole? That's strange... Maybe we need the souls of 60 ghosts...|Jovani|TP}}</ref>

Gengle is revealed to be the leader of the cats in the town.<ref>{{Cite|Say, you're the one who fixed my master up, right? I owe you! My name's Gengle. I'm the leader of the cats outside. Well, well... Now that I can move, maybe I should go outside and breathe some fresh air...|Gengle|TP}}</ref> The cats gathering outside Jovani's house say that they are waiting for Gengle to come out and play.<ref name="important"/><ref>{{Cite|We normally hold our meetings in the open space in front of Jovani's house, but... I don't see why, if Boss Gengle isn't coming anyway, we can't have the meeting in front of my house.|Cat|TP}}</ref>

After Link finds all 60 Poe Souls and Jovani recovers, Jovani can be found in Telma's Tavern to progress the quest. Speaking to him allows Gengle to give Link up to 200 [[Rupee]]s from Jovani's Rupee hoard once per visit to Castle Town. In {{TPHD}}, he will also
give Link the Rupee [[Miiverse]] Stamp.
