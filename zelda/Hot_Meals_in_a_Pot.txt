==Overview==
"{{Term|HWAoC|Hot Meals in a Pot}}" is a {{Term|HWAoC|Quest}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} tries cooking after the {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrulean|link}} army's head cook shows them a {{Term|HWAoC|Cooking Pot|link}}. The materials used are a {{Term|HWAoC|Flint}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Raw Meat}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Herb}}.
