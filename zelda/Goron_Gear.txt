The {{Term/Store|Goron Gear|Goron Gear|BotW, Series}} is an archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 171}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Goron Gear}} is a group of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} crafted by the {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}}. Most of these items are powerful two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} made from thick, hard metal; these lack cutting edges, and instead rely on their weight and the force of the swing to crush opponents.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Cobble Crusher}}<br/>A Goron-made two-handed weapon. It's made from thick, hard metal and has no cutting edge, so it relies on its sheer weight to crush all opponents.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Stone Smasher}}<br/>A two-handed weapon forged from a rare metal mined in Goron City. Its center of gravity is at its tip, so it uses centrifugal force and its sheer weight to smash opponents flat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

All pieces of {{Term|BotW|Goron Gear}} are made of metal. This means that they will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They do not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they can be picked up and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
