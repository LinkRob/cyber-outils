{{Term/Store|Zunari||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258}}</ref>

==Biography==
Zunari used to live on a cold island, but he eventually moved to [[Windfall Island]],<ref>{{Cite|I came across cursed seas from a land far away...a land where the blizzards blow violently...heading for this town called Windfall.|Zunari|TWW}}</ref> where he set up a [[Zunari's Shop|stall]] in the hopes of running a prosperous business.<ref>{{Cite|I wish to open a business here in this town, so I am in desperate need of finances!|Zunari|TWW}}</ref> At first, he lacks the necessary funds, with his only stocked item being the [[Sail]] that he used to navigate from his homeland to Windfall.<ref>{{Cite|...Could you not purchase that from me? It is my one and only heirloom from home.|Zunari|TWW}}</ref> However, when [[Link]] helps him by purchasing his Sail,<ref>{{Cite|Yes, yes, yes! Thank you very much, indeed! I have received precisely 80 Rupees for it. I can use these funds to finance my new business! Oh, dear me, what a happy day!|Zunari|TWW}}</ref> he begins to try and sell things for an organization whose goal is putting flowers all over the town. Zunari is also a member of the [[Joyous Volunteer Association]].

Zunari's only possession is the coat that he always wears, crafted by his mother.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, dear me... Sadly, I have nothing else I could possibly sell you. I have naught but my hoodie coat and it is a keepsake from my dear, dear mother!|Zunari|TWW}}</ref> Nonetheless, his safe may contain something of value, as [[Mila]] tries to break into it at night time. During the [[Island Trading Sequence]], if Link makes a [[Merchant's Oath]] with the three Goron [[Wandering Merchants]] situated on [[Mother & Child Isles]], [[Greatfish Isle]], and [[Bomb Island]], Zunari will gift him with the [[Magic Armor]]. Zunari can also be found running the nightly [[Windfall Auction]] at the [[Auction House]].

In {{TWWHD}}, when first spoken to, Zunari will request that Link free [[Tingle]] from the [[Town Jail]] before he will sell him the Sail. He claims that Tingle had originally traveled with him to Windfall but was arrested upon reaching the island for his suspicious looks.<ref>{{Cite|There was one other person with me, yes there was... But, I dare say, he was immediately arrested based on his somewhat suspicious looks and thrown right into jail. Yes, just locked up somewhere on this island.|Zunari|TWWHD}}</ref> Freeing Tingle causes Zunari to lower the price of the Sail from the previously considered 200 [[Rupee]]s to only 80 Rupees,<ref>{{Cite|I'd really like to as 200 Rupees for it, but as a favor for rescuing my friend, perhaps we may strike a deal... How does...80 Rupees sound?|Zunari|TWWHD}}</ref> as in the original game.
