{{Term/Store|EX Utmost Strength||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 87 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Utmost Strength|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It is only available as part of the second wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian of Remembrance|link}}. 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Utmost Strength}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given eleven minutes to defeat three {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}. The first is {{Term|HWAoC|Fireblight Ganon|link}}, and when he only has about a fourth of his health left, {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon|link}} appears. {{Term|HWAoC|Mutated Ganon|link}} appears when they are both defeated, and when killed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of five {{Plural|HWAoC|Sapphire|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Fleet-Lotus Seeds}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
