The {{Term|OoT3D|World's Finest Eyedrops}} are one of the items necessary to assist in the creation of [[Biggoron's Sword]]. They are received from the [[Lake Scientist]] after giving him the [[Eyeball Frog]] from [[King Zora]], which he initially misinterprets as a gift.<ref name="Biggoron's Eyedrops"/> After being told that it is meant to be made into Eyedrops for [[Biggoron]], the Lake Scientist takes the Eyeball Frog and uses it to make the {{Term|OoT3D|World's Finest Eye Drops}}. Returning the Eyedrops to Link, the Lake Scientist urges him to hurry to [[Death Mountain]], as the ingredients will spoil within four minutes.<ref>{{Cite|Here it is. This medicine doesn't have any preservatives in it. So it {{Color|OoT3D Red|won't last long}} at this temperature. Run as fast as you can to Death Mountain. You're young—you can do it!|Lake Scientist|OoT3D}}</ref> If Link tries [[Warping]] while carrying the Eyedrops, it will spoil instantly.

If Link manages to return to Biggoron in time, he will thank Link and use the {{Term|OoT3D|World's Finest Eye Drops}} immediately.<ref>{{Cite|Rrrrreally? You brrrrought the eyedrops? I'm so rrrrrelieved! I'm going to use them rrrrrright now!|Biggoron|OoT3D}}</ref> With his eyes cleared, Biggoron grows excited to return to his work and hands Link a [[Claim Check]] with the promise of finishing his work within a few days.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Red|Wowwwwwwwwwwwwww!}} This is stimulating! Afterrrr a few days...please returrrrrrn... Wait... Just wait patiently...|Biggoron|OoT3D}}</ref>
