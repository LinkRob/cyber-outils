==Spinoff appearances==
==={{ZA|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:ZA Bat Sprite.png|frame|left]]
Bats in {{ZA}} are encountered only in the [[Shrine of Earth]] and [[Shrine of Air]]. They have the appearance of large, purple bats. Bats usually travel around a room in a certain path. They take a few hits from the [[Wand]] to defeat, but become invincible temporarily between each hit.

In the Shrine of Earth, the [[Compass]] can be obtained from defeating every Bat in the room south of the crossroads area.
