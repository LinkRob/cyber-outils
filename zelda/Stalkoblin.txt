{{Term/Store|Stalkoblin|Stalkoblins|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|Enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Stalkoblin}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Stalkoblin}}
Stalkoblins are the skeletal remains of [[Bokoblin]]s which emerge from the earth at night. When they emerge, they may be holding various [[Weapon]]s, however, if they lack any available weapons, they will either swipe at Link if he is nearby, or dig up rocks to throw at him. Some can also be found riding [[Stalhorse]]s.

Stalkoblins are fragile and their bodies will easily collapse if struck, leaving their disembodied skulls to roll around. These skulls have only one health. Shortly afterwards, their bodies will reform and run after their heads to reattach them. If a Stalkoblin's skull is present, the bodies of other Stalkoblins whose skulls have been destroyed will remain. These decapitated skeletons will simply jump and wave at the remaining intact Stalkoblins until their skulls have been detached, at which point they will attempt to take the skull for themselves. Stalkoblins will pick up the detached skulls of other Stalkoblins and throw them at Link to hurt him. If they come into contact with a flame, their entire body will catch on fire, in addition to any wooden implements they carry.

The detached arms of Stalkoblins, known as [[Bokoblin Arm]]s, can be picked up by Link and used as a Weapon. While detached, it will roll towards a common point with the other bones to reform the Stalkoblin's skeleton. If taken, Bokoblin Arms will remain animate when the rest of the skeletons they belong to are destroyed.<ref>{{Cite|A skeletal arm that keeps moving even after it's severed from its body. It's kind of gross to strap it to your back, but it'll do in a pinch. It's old and fragile, so it's quick to break.|N/A|BotW}}</ref> When defeated, they may drop [[Bokoblin Fang]]s. Curiously, the Bokoblin Fangs will not drop from their skulls, but rather from their skeleton bodies.

Link may pick up a detached Stalkoblin head and kick it further away from its body. If it lands in water deep enough to submerge it completely, the Stalkoblin will die.

If Link wears the [[Bokoblin Mask]], [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]], or possesses the [[Disguise]] [[Set Bonus]] by wearing the full [[Radiant Set]] or [[Phantom Ganon Set]], Stalkoblins will not attack Link unless he attacks them.

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Compendium|number=124|name=Stalkoblin|desc=These remains of Bokoblins hate light and only move about in the dark. While they're fragile enough to crumble from a single blow, they have sharp horns. And as long as a skull remains intact, they'll continue to pull themselves back together and go on fighting.|locs=Great Hyrule Forest, Gerudo Desert Depths|loot=Bokoblin Fang, Bokoblin Horn}}
Stalkoblins appear much the same way as their {{BotW|-}} counterparts, except that they have the barbed horns of Red Bokoblins on their skulls. They will perform the charging attack sometimes, but otherwise function identical to {{BotW|-}}.

Notably, only the fangs drop from the body, while the horn drops from the skull.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
Stalkoblins reappear as enemies in {{HWAoC}}, first appearing in [[Freeing Korok Forest]]. Like in ''Breath of the Wild'', they fight with various weapons against Link and his allies. Unlike ''Breath of the Wild'', if a Stalkoblin is defeated, its head flies off and resumes the fight as a [[Cursed Bokoblin]]. Captain Stalkoblins, however, will not turn cursed when defeated.
{{Noncanon|End}}
