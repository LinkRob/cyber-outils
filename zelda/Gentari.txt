{{Term/Store|Gentari||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 270"/>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Gentari}}
{{Term|TMC|Gentari}} is the wise elder of {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}, who explains what {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must do to succeed in his quest: reforge the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} and infuse within it the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}} scattered and hidden across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And you've come here now hoping to have the blade reforged? Hm... Well, if that's what you're after, you'll need four mystic {{Color|TMC Red|elements}}.|Gentari|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Only by {{Color|TMC Blue|infusing the blade}} with these energies can a new blade be forged.|Gentari|TMC}}</ref>

When {{Term|TMC|Link}} reaches {{Term|TMC|Gentari}}'s house, he will mark on {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Map of Hyrule|link|display=Map}} where the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements}} are hidden.<ref>{{Cite|Here, give me your map. I can mark where these {{Color|TMC Red|elements}} can be found.|Gentari|TMC}}</ref> He guides {{Term|TMC|Link}} towards the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} by suggesting that he use the passageway through {{Term|TMC|Festari|link}}'s abbey to reach the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}, where the first {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|display=Element}} is hidden,<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TMC Red|Earth Element}} can be found in the {{Color|TMC Green|shrine}} to the north of {{Color|TMC Green|Festari}}'s abbey. Speak with {{Color|TMC Green|Festari}}. He will show you the path to the {{Color|TMC Green|shrine}}'s entrance.|Gentari|TMC}}</ref> and asks {{Term|TMC|Link}} to report back to him after securing it.<ref>{{Cite|Return to me at once after you have found the {{Color|TMC Red|Earth Element}}.|Gentari|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element}}, {{Term|TMC|Gentari}} instructs him to seek out {{Term|TMC|Melari|link}} on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} to have the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}} reforged,<ref>{{Cite|If your conviction holds strong, head to {{Color|TMC Green|Mount Crenel}}. There, you will find a man named {{Color|TMC Green|Melari}}. Among all the Minish, there is no one more able to repair your sword. If you ask him, I am certain he will reforge your {{Color|TMC Red|broken Picori Blade}}.|Gentari|TMC}}</ref> and opens the curtains in his room, revealing a passageway that leads to the southwestern portion of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods}}.<ref>{{Cite|Leave through this door here. It will take you out of the forest quickly.|Gentari|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, he can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Gentari}},<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Yes, please!|Belari|TMC}}</ref> which inspires {{Term|TMC|Belari|link}} to finish the {{Plural|TMC|Remote Bomb|link}}.
