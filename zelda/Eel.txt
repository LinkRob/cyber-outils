The {{Name|Fan|{{Term/Store|Eel|Eels|Series, TLoZ (TV Series)}}}} is an enemy appearing in the "[[Fairies in the Spring]]" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}.

When [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] go to the [[Royal Water Park]] to investigate the recent sightings of [[Water Monster|water monsters]], they end up going into one of the pools and Zelda is taken deep underwater by an eel, wrapping itself around the princess. Although Link tries to [[zap]] it with his [[Crissword]], all it does is loosen its grip enough to allow Zelda to escape but it then sends an electrified beam at Link, who drops his sword.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Whew! I got a real charge out of that.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> Zelda then gets out a [[Crossbow]] from her pouch and shoots a bolt at the creature, thus defeating it.
