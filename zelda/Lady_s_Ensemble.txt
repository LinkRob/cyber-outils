The {{Term/Store|Lady's Ensemble||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 129}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Lady's Ensemble is something special. Paired with a hero, it can save our dahling princess from her curse!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that resembles the dress and accessories worn by {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link}}. Its primary use is lifting the curse that {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}} has placed on {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}}, in essence completing the game.<ref>{{Cite|The Lady's Ensemble is something special. Paired with a hero, it can save our dahling princess from her curse!|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref>

The {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble}} can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} at no cost, requiring only the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Glasses|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Lady's Collar|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Lady's Parasol|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}}. When {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} brings at least one of the Lady's Materials to {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture|link}}, she reveals her plans to create the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} as well as her personal history with {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}}, in the hopes that {{Term|TFH|Link}} can collect all the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} without battling her.<ref>{{Cite|Now that you have some of {{Color|TFH Blue|The Lady's materials}}, it's time I showed you {{Color|TFH Blue|this}} little piece of work. I call it...<br/>{{Color|TFH Blue|The Lady's Ensemble}}!<br/>If you wear this and visit the princess, you should be able to lift her curse! All without defeating {{Color|TFH Blue|The Lady}}.|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> This turns out to be impossible, as the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} must defeat {{Term|TFH|Margoma|link}}, {{Term|TFH|The Lady's Pets|link}}, and {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}} herself in order to obtain the {{Plural|TFH|Material}}.

Once {{Term|TFH|Link}} dons the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble}} and enters {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}, where {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly|link}} fails to recognize him before complimenting the look,<ref>{{Cite|Did you need something?<br/>Huh?<br/>No?<br/>Maybe?<br/>Wait a dash... Isn't that...<br/>Ah, {{Player Name}}!<br/>Is that the outfit {{Color|TFH Blue|Madame}} spoke of?<br/>You're really pulling it off. Dashing!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> he begins the ritual to remove the {{Term|TFH|Cursed Tights|link}} from {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}} and restore her regular attire. The game's ending sequence then follows.

Functionally, the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble}} combines the abilities of the {{Term|TFH|Jack of Hearts|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Legendary Dress|link}}, increasing the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}' total {{Plural|TFH|Heart Container|link}} by one and also increasing the rate at which {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} appear when breaking {{Plural|TFH|Jar|link}}, cutting grass and defeating [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]].<ref>{{Cite|Lift the curse!<br/>♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦<br/>Like the Legendary Dress, but with +1 Heart Container. Look at you! You big ol' hero, you!|Catalog|TFH}}</ref>

Any {{Plural|TFH|Links}} who join the game through [[Download Play]] will be unable to obtain the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble}}. Since the required {{Plural|TFH|Material}} cannot be  collected in this mode, the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} does not appear in the {{Term|TFH|Catalog|link}}.
