"'''Temple Theme'''", also known as "'''Temple'''", is a recurring [[Song]] in the {{SSBS}}.

==Overview==
"Temple Theme" is an arrangement of the music that plays in the {{Plural|TAoL|Palace|link}} in {{TAoL}}. It plays on the {{Term|Series|Hyrule: Temple|display=Temple|link}} {{Term|Series|Stage|link}} in {{SSBM}}, {{SSBB}} and {{SSB4|Wii U}}, and any {{Term|SSBU|Stage}} related to {{TLoZ|Series}} in {{SSBU}}.
