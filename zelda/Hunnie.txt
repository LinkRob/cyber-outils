{{Term/Store|Hunnie||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hunnie|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} girl who lives with her parents, {{Term|BotW|Hagie|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ruli|link}}. After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} completes the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, she and her family will move to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}} together, as they believe it could improve her health.<ref>{{Cite|Our daughter has been in poor health lately... My husband believed that moving here could help, so he bought a house.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} has started to improve, her parents have had difficulty finding something that she will eat, which leaves her stuck in bed most days.<ref>{{Cite|Despite my husband's determination, I wasn't sure if a move was a good idea... but our daughter is slowly improving. The only thing left is for her appetite to return. I'm sure she'd get well faster if she ate more.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes... Don't worry yourself. I only wish that my daughter would eat more and recover her strength...|Ruli|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} while she is weak, she will excitedly greet him and ask if he is a traveler.<ref>{{Cite|Hi! Are you a traveler?|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms that he is, she then asks if it is fun.<ref>{{Cite|[That's right.] Is it fun?|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells her that it is, {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} reveals that she wants to travel on her own when she grows up.<ref>{{Cite|[It's pretty fun.] I see! When I grow up, I want to go out all by myself!|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref>

After this initial encounter, {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to tell her a story if he approaches her.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Tell me a story!|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Tell me something else!|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks how she is feeling, {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} will respond that she is fine.<ref>{{Cite|[How are you feeling?] I'm fine!|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} what she likes to eat, she reveals that while she hates most {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}},<ref>{{Cite|[Watcha like eating?] I hate everything!|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Watcha like eating?] I hate, hate, HATE it all!|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref> she will eat cake if he offers some to her.<ref>{{Cite|But I'll eat some cake if you have some.|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks if she has heard of the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, she becomes annoyed and calls it boring.<ref>{{Cite|[Hear of the Calamity?] ... What? That's boring!|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} leaves her, she tells him that she does not want him to be there anyway.<ref>{{Cite|[Bye bye.] I don't want you to come here, anyway!|Hunnie|BotW}}</ref>

At night, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can listen in on {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} and {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} discussing {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} through an open window.<ref>{{Cite|Our daughter... The only thing she'll eat is {{Color|BotW Red|cake}}. Can't anything be done?|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} recounts that they have looked all over the world for a cake that {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} could eat, but they have yet to find anything so far.<ref>{{Cite|We've scoured the world, searching for a cake that would please her. None have. Imagine how much money we've spent...|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} declares that she would sell her soul to a monster if it meant finding a cake to cure {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}}.<ref>{{Cite|Even if I have to sell my soul to a {{Color|BotW Red|monster}}, I'll get my hands on a {{Color|BotW Red|cake}} that can cure our daughter!|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> This activates the "{{Term|BotW|A Parent's Love|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

To complete the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs to bring a {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Ruli}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Take this.] What is... Is this a {{Color|BotW Blue|monster cake}}? I should give this to my daughter?|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> She gives the cake to {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} right away,<ref>{{Cite|I'll try to get her to eat it right away.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll wake her up right away and try to get her to eat it.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> after which {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} can be seen running through her front door. {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} reveals that she ate the entire {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake}} and that she is finally healthy again.<ref>{{Cite|That {{Color|BotW Blue|monster cake}}... Our daughter wolfed it down with unbelievable gusto. It seems her health has returned to her!|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I don't even know what to say-I'm just so thankful! Oh, I know. This isn't much, but please take it.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref>

Once she has regained her energy, {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} wakes up at 8:00 AM every day. She immediately runs outside and sits with {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} on the cliff overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Torin Wetland|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going sunabthing with my family. You're in my way-cease to be so.|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> At 8:00 PM, she gets up and slowly walks back to her house with her father, and she goes to sleep at around 9:00 PM.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Hunnie}} may come from {{Wp|Honey}}, a common nickname given to loved ones. It may also be because of her decidedly sweet tooth.
