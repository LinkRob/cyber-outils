{{Term/Store|Snowball Bowling||Series, BotW}}, also known as '''Snowling''', is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Pondo">{{Cite|Can't give you a warm welcome...buuuut how about a game I made up: {{Color|BotW Red|Snowball Bowling}}! Or as I call it, {{Color|BotW Red|Snowling}}!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The {{Term|BotW|Snowball Bowling|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} is run by {{Term|BotW|Pondo|link}} from his {{Term|BotW|Pondo's Lodge|link|display=Lodge}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Pondo}}, he is pleased to meet someone new in such a remote place.<ref>{{Cite|Hail, kiddums! Nice to meet a fresh face in these parts. It's not every day I get company this high in the hills.|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> While he is unable to offer {{Term|BotW|Link}} a warm welcome, he instead allows him to play {{Term|BotW|Snowball Bowling}}, a game that he came up with himself.<ref name="Pondo"/> {{Term|BotW|Snowball Bowling}} is open 24/7 and can be played at any time.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= {{Color|NM Brown|Time: Open 24h}} |magazine= Nintendo Magazine|publisher= |volume= |issue= |date= Winter 2021|page= 17}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks how to play, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} will explain the rules to him.<ref>{{Cite|[How does it work?] What a sweet boy to show an interest! If you don't know the rules, I can explain them in a flurry.|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} will place 10 pins at the foot of the hill.<ref>{{Cite|At the foot of the hill, we have 10, count 'em, 10 pillars or "pins."|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} must push the {{Term|BotW|Snowball|link}} down the hill, aiming to knock over as many of the pins as he can.<ref>{{Cite|Give that snowball a good, hearty toss from the tippy-top of the hill to try and knock over all the pins you can!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> While the path it takes down the hill is rough, it is guaranteed to knock over any pins it hits.<ref>{{Cite|The ball might take a few detours on its ride down the hill, but any pins it hits when it reaches bottom are done for!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} claims that smashing all the pins at once is the coolest feeling and encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to try.<ref>{{Cite|There's no cooler feeling than smashing all the pins at once! But see for yourself! Go on, have a ball!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Pondo}} asks for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}} before he can play the game.<ref name="Rupees"/> Once this has been paid, he will go set up the pins.<ref>{{Cite|Aha! Play bells are ringing! I gotta set up the pins, but I'll be back in a flurry.|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, he presents {{Term|BotW|Link}} with his {{Term|BotW|Snowball}}, explaining he has two rolls down the hill.<ref>{{Cite|This is the snowball you'll use. It might not look like much, but it's snow joke. You get two rolls. Knock 'em flat!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> He explains that knocking all 10 pins over in one roll is called a strike, but only the best bowlers are capable of it.<ref>{{Cite|If you can knock over all 10 pins in one roll, that's a {{Color|BotW Red|strike}}. Only the most snowtorious bowlers can pull that off!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> Next, he shares that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can get a spare by knocking the pins over in two hits, which is still impressive.<ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|BotW Red|spare}} is when it takes both tries to finish the job. Not as good as a strike, but nothing to sneeze at!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> Before the game begins, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} luck.<ref>{{Cite|Well, good luck!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref>

There are two scenarios in which {{Term|BotW|Link}} receives no reward from {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} upon completing his second roll. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not knock over any of the pins, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} advises {{Term|BotW|Link}} to visualize where he is pushing the {{Term|BotW|Snowball}}. Similarly, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} knocks down between one and six pins, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} notes that such a loss must sting and encourages him to try again.<ref>{{Cite|Only {{Color|BotW Red|1}}... That's gotta sting. My advice? Stay frosty and play again!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Pondo}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a reward if his score total more than six. For example, when {{Term|BotW|Link}} knocks down seven or eight pins, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} tells him that he was decent and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}} as a sample.<ref>{{Cite|How many {{Color|BotW Red|pins}}? Eh, {{Color|BotW Red|8}}? That's decent. Tell you what, I'll give you a sampler prize to get you shivering for more!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} knocks over nine pins, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} remarks that he was close and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Ooooh, snow close! A throw like that deserves some kinda prize. Here ya go!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} get a spare, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} will be impressed and gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} for such a feat.<ref>{{Cite|You {{Color|BotW Red|knocked 'em all down}} flat! That was no mean sleet, kiddums. I got you a special prize for pulling that one off!|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets a strike, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} will be pleasantly surprised and initially offers a {{Term|BotW|Blizzard Rod|link}} as a prize, which he refers to as a {{Term|BotW|Snowball}}-making device.<ref>{{Cite|Well, bowl me over, kiddums! A snowing like that deserves an ice prize. How's about this snowball-making doohickey?|Pondo|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} is full, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} will instead give him a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Leastaways, I'd offer it to you if you weren't snowverloaded with stuff. Will this do instead?|Pondo|BotW}}</ref>}} On subsequent strikes, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, whaddaya know, kiddums! Just for that, you get a prize.|Pondo|BotW}}</ref>

After reviewing {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s score, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to try {{Term|BotW|Snowball Bowling}} again.<ref>{{Cite|Soooooooo... What do you think about giving it one more whirl?|Pondo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Pondo}} says he loves the pins too much to leave and will be waiting if {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever wants to return.<ref>{{Cite|[I've lost interest.] No problem. I love the pins too much to ever leave! You bet your snowball I'll be here when you're ready for more.|Pondo|BotW}}</ref>
