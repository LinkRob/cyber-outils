The {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker}} is the most durable and protective variant of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}}, adorned with a jeweled {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} emblem. Before the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, it was beloved by the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> It was designed to be both lightweight and durable, and the gold used to make it was handpicked to ensure so.<ref name="Inventory"/> It belongs to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}'s death, the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker}} and the {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven|link}} became treasures of the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite|These pieces of equipment are priceless treasures of the Gerudo people. They were once worn by {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa}}.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> After defeating {{Term|BotW|Thunderblight Ganon|link}} and calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} will offer {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} both the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker}} and the {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven}}.<ref>{{Cite|I want to offer you these as thanks for your help. I trust you'll accept them.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She believes it's right he should have them, as he was friends with {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}.<ref>{{Cite|Since you and {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa|link}} were friends, it's only right that you have them.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> Both {{Plural|BotW|Item}} can be found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s throne in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.

If either {{Term|BotW|Item}} breaks, {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}} will offer to remake them for {{Term|BotW|Link}}. To make the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker}}, she needs one {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}, five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Daybreaker}}, huh? To make it, I'll need {{Color|BotW Blue|one diamond}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|five}} pieces of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}, and a {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo shield}}.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} will take note of the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} has it equipped when he speaks to her. She will ask if she is dreaming, explaining that she has wanted the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker}} so badly that she has seen it in her dreams.<ref>{{Cite|A Daybreaker! Am I dreaming?! I've wanted that shield so bad that I've seen it in my dreams...|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

Being made of metal, the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if it is equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. It will also sink if it is dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but it can still be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} if it happens. In addition, it will not burn like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Shield}} when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
