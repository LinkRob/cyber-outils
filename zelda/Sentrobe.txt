==Characteristics==

Sentrobes are round, flying robots found mainly in the [[Lanayru Mining Facility]], only becoming active when under the effects of a [[Timeshift Stone]]. They have eyes in the center of their bodies and they fly around using a propeller attached to its inner body. They are separated vertically by their metallic shell, which opens to shoot missiles at [[Link]]. These missiles can be reflected with a Shield Attack or sword strike to damage the Sentrobe. They can only withstand being struck by their missiles twice before they blow up, jettisoning [[Rupee]]s in all directions.
