The {{Term/Store|Whisp Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Whisp Ring<br/>No effect from jinxes|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Whisp Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it prevent [[Jinx]]ing from [[Bubble]]s.<ref name="Description"/> The Whisp Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
