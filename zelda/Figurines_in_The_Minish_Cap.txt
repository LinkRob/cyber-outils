TITLE : Figurines in The Minish Cap

{{DISPLAYTITLE:Figurines in {{TMC|-}}}}
:''You may also wish to see [[Figurines in The Wind Waker|Figurines in {{TWW|-}}]]''
'''Figurines''' are small carved objects depicting people, scenes, and enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|I collect those {{Color|TMC Red|Mysterious Shells}} you've seen scattered all over the place. If you bring those shells to me, I'll trade you these {{Color|TMC Red|figurines}} I have made.|Carlov|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can buy a random {{Term|TMC|Figurine}} at the {{Term|TMC|Figurine Gallery|link}} using {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}}. The more {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|display=Shells}} he puts in increases the chance of getting a new {{Term|TMC|Figurine}} from the {{Term|TMC|Figure-Dispensing Machine|link}}.

There are 130 normal {{Plural|TMC|Figurine}} available to get, and an extra six (referred to as the '''Final Series'''<ref>{{Cite|Hey, I've been waiting for you! I've got something new to show you. It's our {{Color|TMC Blue|final series}}, my friend. Check it out!|Carlov|TMC}}</ref>) are released when {{Term|TMC|Link}} defeats {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}. When {{Term|TMC|Link}} collects the first 130 (or all 136 after defeating {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}), he gains access to the {{Term|TMC|Music House|link}} owned by {{Term|TMC|Herb|link}}, which contains a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Phonograph|link}}.
