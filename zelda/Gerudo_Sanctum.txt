The '''Gerudo Sanctum''' is a [[Dungeon]] in {{EoW}}.

==Entrance to the Sanctum==
The Gerudo Sanctum is unlocked at the end of "The Gerudo Desert Rift" quest line. In order to enter it, players must complete the [[Cryptic Cavern]] mini-dungeon, which will result in the sand burying the sanctum being drained, granting them access to the dungeon.

==Themes and Navigation==
The Gerudo Sanctum is a sacred temple revolving around clearing away sand and using the Bind ability. To beat the sanctum, players are required to collect two echoes to reach the boss door: the Hawk Statue and the Elephant Statue. Placing these two on pedestals outside a door will grant them access to the boss.

Additionally, players are required to weave in and out of the dungeon, finding new entrances from the outside and even finding chests and echoes in the Still World surrounding it.
