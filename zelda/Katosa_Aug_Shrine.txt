The {{Term/Store|Katosa Aug Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Katosa Aug Shrine}}<br/>Katosa Aug Apparatus}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Katosa Aug Apparatus''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Katosa Aug Shrine}} can be found across from {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}} within {{Term|BotW|Deep Akkala|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he will hear its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} {{Term|BotW|Katosa Aug|link}} greeting him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Katosa Aug. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Katosa Aug|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Katosa Aug Shrine}}, he will be able to see the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Katosa Aug}} in front of him, separate by two disconnected platforms. A moving platform in front of the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|display=Pedestal}} is capable of bringing him to the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, but it is inactive.

Interacting with the {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle|link}} nearby will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} control over the giant hammer mechanism. A {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}} will raise an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} in front of the hammer. Using the hammer, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must hit the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} into the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} on the opposite side of the platform. Once this is done, the moving platform will begin to float between {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s platform and the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}.

{{Term|BotW|Katosa Aug}} commends {{Term|BotW|Link}} for overcoming the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero...}}|Katosa Aug|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then grants {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Katosa Aug|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Katosa Aug}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Katosa Aug|BotW}}</ref>

Behind the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find another room with a {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}}. The setup to this {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}} is similar to the first, except for the fact that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must hit the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} at an angle in order to reach the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. When this is done, a nearby moving platform will activate, giving {{Term|BotW|Link}} access to a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Great Frostblade|link}}.
