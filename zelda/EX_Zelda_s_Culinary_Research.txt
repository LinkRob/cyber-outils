{{Term/Store|EX Zelda's Culinary Research||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 59 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Zelda's Culinary Research|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It is only available as part of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian of Remembrance|link}}.

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Zelda's Culinary Research}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given six minutes to collect three monster extracts. A monster extract can be obtained by destroying a box at a targeted position, although there are five of them on the map. The boxes containing monster extract and the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} guarding them are randomized. The position located near the top left and bottom right corner of the map is guarded by an {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}, who appears when a {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} approaches the area. The ones in the east and center of the map are guarded by three {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy}} who do the same, although only one has to be defeated to gain access to the box. The box closest to the bottom left corner of the map is the only one left unguarded, and also the only one to always contain an extract. There are many weaker {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} scattered around the map as well, including {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Keese|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Chuchu|link}} of each element. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when all three monster extracts are obtained. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of 30 {{Plural|HWAoC|Wildberry|link}}, 10 {{Plural|HWAoC|Hydromelon|link}}, 10 {{Plural|HWAoC|Cane Sugar|link}}, 10 {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Princess|link}}, and 10 {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Salmon|link}}.
