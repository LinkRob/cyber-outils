{{Term/Store|Fried Wild Greens|Fried Wild Greens|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fried Wild Greens}}<br/>A basic vegetable dish made by sautéing fresh wild plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Fried Wild Greens are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} at least one herb, flower, or vegetable in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The regular version can be made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb|link}}. Eating one will heal some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These dishes are known to be basic, their recipe being to sauté fresh wild plants.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on the ingredients used, several different variations of Fried Wild Greens can be made:

* '''Hearty Fried Wild Greens''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Fried Wild Greens}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A basic vegetable dish made by sautéing fresh wild plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}. When eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum number of Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Enduring Fried Wild Greens''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Fried Wild Greens}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A basic vegetable dish made by sautéing fresh wild plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}. If these greens are consumed, they'll completely refill and overfill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Fried Wild Greens''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Fried Wild Greens}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A basic vegetable dish made by sautéing fresh wild plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}}. Eating them will increase Link's movement speed temporarily.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Fried Wild Greens''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Fried Wild Greens}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A basic vegetable dish made by sautéing fresh wild plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}}. When eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Fried Wild Greens''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Fried Wild Greens}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A basic vegetable dish made by sautéing fresh wild plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}}. Link will be granted one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when one of these is eaten.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Fried Wild Greens''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Fried Wild Greens}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A basic vegetable dish made by sautéing fresh wild plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}}. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Fried Wild Greens''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Fried Wild Greens}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A basic vegetable dish made by sautéing fresh wild plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}. They temporarily increase Link's defense when consumed.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Fried Wild Greens''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Fried Wild Greens}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A basic vegetable dish made by sautéing fresh wild plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}. When eaten, they'll increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period of time.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Fried Wild Greens''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Fried Wild Greens}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A basic vegetable dish made by sautéing fresh wild plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}}. If they're eaten, they'll increase Link's [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
