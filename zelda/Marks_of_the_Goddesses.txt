The {{Name|Fan|Marks of the Goddesses}} are crests that represent each of the [[Golden Goddesses]]. These marks, known as the '''Mark of Din''', '''Mark of Farore''', and '''Mark of Nayru''', appear in places or items related to the goddesses.<ref>{{Cite|The symbol visible on the floor here is the mark of Din.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The design carved into the floor there is the mark of Farore.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I have identified the design on the floor there as the mark of Nayru.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Although these marks are often associated with the creators of [[Hyrule]], they are also identified with the attributes of the goddesses: Power, Courage, and Wisdom.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Din, Nayru, and Farore each left a crest behind. Each of these symbols—the crests of power, wisdom, and courage—can be found in a location related to its respective goddess.|book= HH|publisher= |page= 70}}</ref>

==Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT3D Spiritual Stone of Water Icon.png|left]]
In {{OoT}}, the [[Spiritual Stones#Zora's Sapphire|Zora's Sapphire]], or the Spiritual Stone of Water, is shaped in likeness to the Mark of Nayru. In actuality, the [[Spiritual Stones]] are shaped in the form of the [[Kokiri]], [[Goron]], and [[Zora]] [[Symbols#Race_Emblems|emblems]]. However, only the Zora's emblem is represented by a Mark of the Goddess (Nayru); the Kokiri and Goron emblems—and thus the Spiritual Stones of Forest and Fire—are not.
{{Clear}}

==={{OoA|-}}===
[[File:OoA Tune of Echoes Sprite.png]][[File:OoA Tune of Currents Sprite.png]][[File:OoA Tune of Ages Sprite.png]]<br>
In {{OoA}}, the marks represent the tunes played in the [[Harp of Ages]]. The "[[Tune of Echoes]]" bears Farore's mark, the "[[Tune of Currents]]" bears Din's mark, and the "[[Tune of Ages]]" bears Nayru's mark.

==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:TWWHD Farore's Pearl Artwork.png|50px]][[File:TWWHD Din's Pearl Artwork.png|50px]][[File:TWWHD Nayru's Pearl Artwork.png|50px]]<br>
In {{TWW}}, it is said that [[Din's Pearl]], [[Farore's Pearl]], and [[Nayru's Pearl]] were handed down to [[Valoo]], the [[Great Deku Tree]], and [[Jabun]] by the goddesses themselves, respectively.<ref>{{Cite|The water spirit Jabun was kind enough to give you this jewel, a treasure of one of the goddesses!|N/A|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You received Din's Pearl! This is the gem the Ritos claim was passed down from the great goddess!|N/A|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You  received Farore's Pearl! The earth spirit, the Deku Tree, has seen fit  to give you this jewel, a treasure of the goddess, Farore.|N/A|TWW}}</ref> These three pearls, known as the [[Goddess Pearls]], are each placed on the three statues resembling the Golden Goddesses located in each of the [[Triangle Islands]] found across the [[Great Sea]] to rise the [[Tower of the Gods]] from beneath the waves.<ref>{{Cite|Wandering traveler who seeks the guidepost of the goddesses... Place the pearl you hold here.|Goddess Statue|TWW}}</ref>

==={{PH|-}}===
[[File:PH Courage Gem Model.png|40px]][[File:PH Power Gem Model.png|40px]][[File:PH Wisdom Gem Model.png|40px]]<br>
The [[Spirit Gems]] from {{PH}} also contain the marks, each representing the attribute of the correspondent Golden Goddess. The Power Gem features the mark of Din, the Courage Gem features the mark of Farore, and the Wisdom Gem features the mark of Nayru. The marks can also be seen on certain doors in the [[Temple of the Ocean King]]. The Door of Power, the Door of Courage and the Door of Wisdom bear the mark of Din, the mark of Farore and the mark of Nayru respectively.

==={{SS|-}}===
[[File:GateofTime8.png|thumb|right|300px|The Gate of Time with the marks of the goddesses]]
The marks of the Goddesses are predominately displayed in {{SS}}. All three marks can be found inside the [[Sky Keep]] in each of the rooms where a [[Triforce]] piece is hidden. Link must strike the Goddess Sword into the mark so that it may transport him to the area containing part of the sacred relic.<ref>{{Cite|I detect the sacred power of the Triforce emanating from its vicinity.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

[[Faron (Dragon)|Faron]], [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]], and [[Eldin (Dragon)|Eldin]] display the mark of Farore, Nayru, and Din, respectively, on their foreheads and robes. The three symbols are also featured on the [[Gate of Time]].

The mark of Farore is seen near the Viewing Platform of the [[Faron Woods]] and also partially on the door leading to [[Lake Floria]]. To access the lake, Link must complete the symbol by drawing the missing circle using the [[Goddess Sword]]. The mark of Nayru is visible in doors inside the [[Sandship]].

Every side of the [[Sealing Spike]] has a different Goddess Mark on it.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
[[File:ALBW Pendant of Courage Model.png|50px]][[File:ALBW Pendant of Wisdom Model.png|50px]][[File:ALBW Pendant of Power Model.png|50px]]<br>
In {{ALBW}}, the [[Pendants of Virtue]] are engraved with each of their respective marks. Likewise, the Pendants of Power and Wisdom are now their traditional colors of red and blue.

==={{BotW|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'', the marks of the Goddesses are displayed at the Sacred Springs; the Crest of Din at the [[Spring of Power]], the Crest of Nayru at the [[Spring of Wisdom]], and the Crest of Farore at the [[Spring of Courage]]. However, the Crest of Farore is heavily eroded.
