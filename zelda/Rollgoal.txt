{{Term/Store|Rollgoal||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a {{Term|TPHD|Mini-Game|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref name="Fun Name">{{Cite|I made that game, y'know! I gave it a really fun name, too...ROLLGOAL!|Hena|TP}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
Rollgoal is a minigame invented by [[Hena]].<ref name="Fun Name"/> It is located inside her hut in the [[Fishing Hole]] in the [[Upper Zora's River]]. In order to play it, [[Link]] must go into first-person perspective and examine the game board located close to the entrance door. It costs 5 [[Rupee]]s per game and awards 10 Rupees for a win.<ref>{{Cite|It costs 5 Rupees to play, but you win 10 Rupees if you make it to the goal!|Hena|TP}}</ref>

Rollgoal involves moving a glass ball over a path all the way to the goal within the time limit without dropping it. The game can be played by tilting the [[Wii]] Remote, the Control Stick in the [[GameCube]] controller, or the [[Wii U]] GamePad.<ref>{{Cite|The rules are simple! Roll the glass ball along the path all the way to the goal within the time limit without dropping the ball!|Hena|TPHD}}</ref> The camera angle can also be changed by tilting the Control Stick in the Nunchuk, the C-Stick in the GameCube controller, or the Right Control Stick in the Wii U GamePad.

Each time Link completes a level, a new one becomes available once he enters the hut again. Once the first eight levels are completed, Link is awarded with the [[Frog Lure]].<ref>{{Cite|Now that you've cleared my Rollgoal game, you've earned the right to use the frog lure!|Hena|TP}}</ref> Each time Link completes the eight levels, he can replay them in the same order; however, each time they will be mirrored and with 20 seconds less in the time limit.<ref>{{Cite|You already cleared all my Rollgoal games, so I don't have any new challenges for you... So I guess I'll just have to start up a harder version of the game with shorter time limits!|Hena|TP}}</ref> If Link completes all 64 levels, Hena will completely fill his [[Wallet]].<ref>{{Cite|Wow! This is really gonna cost me! But I have to fill your wallet!|Hena|TP}}</ref> Link will also be able to choose and play any level with an increased time limit for free;<ref>{{Cite|It's the special-edition Rollgoal game! It's free, and you have lots of time. You can even choose any level you want. What do you say?|Hena|TP}}</ref> however, Hena will not reward him with any Rupees afterwards.<ref>{{Cite|I can't give you any more Rupees after this, but if you want to play my special-edition Rollgoal game, come see me again!|Hena|TP}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*It is possible to cheat by rolling the ball over gaps; however, Hena will catch Link if he attempts to do so, forcing him to pay another 5 Rupees and start the level again.<ref>{{Cite|Come on! You know that's cheating! The rules say you have to roll it on the path! Do over!|Hena|TP}}</ref>
