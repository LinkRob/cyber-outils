The {{Term/Store|Cucco Lady||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref name="E 239"/>{{Note|In {{OoT|-}}, the Gossip Stone that mentions her initially referred to her as the Chicken Lady.<ref>{{Cite|They say that the Chicken Lady goes to the Lakeside Laboratory to study how to breed pocket-sized Cuccos.|Gossip Stone|OoT}}</ref> This was changed to Cucco Lady in {{OoT3D}}.<ref name= "Cucco">{{Cite|They say that the Cucco Lady goes to the Lakeside Laboratory to study how to breed pocket-sized Cuccos.|Gossip Stone|OoT3D}}</ref>}}

==Biography==
The Cucco Lady can be found in [[Kakariko Village]], where she raises and tends to her [[Cucco]]s. The Cucco Lady is allergic to Cuccos, so she has a hard time taking care of them and keeping them inside the pen.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for finding my Cuccos. I have allergies, so I get goose bumps when I touch them.|Cucco Lady|OoT}}</ref> When [[Link]] first meets her, the Cuccos will be running around all over the village. As the Cucco Lady cannot do it herself, she asks the young [[Hero of Time]] to round them up and bring them back to their pen. For Link's help, she will reward him with a [[Bottle]].<ref>{{Cite|For helping me, I will give this to you. It's fine glass, and should be useful. Please take good care of it!|Cucco Lady|OoT}}</ref> Upon bringing back the {{Plural|OoT3D|Cucco}} for a second time, the {{Term|OoT3D|Cucco Lady}} will give him a {{Term|OoT3D|Purple Rupee|link}}.

When Link returns to Kakariko Village as an adult, he can speak to the Cucco Lady to begin the [[Trading Quest]]. She will tell him that she bred a special new type of miniature Cucco that does not give her an allergic reaction.<ref>{{Cite|I bred a new type of miniature Cucco! I call it the {{Color|OoT Red|Pocket Cucco}}! I don't get goose bumps from this baby.|Cucco Lady|OoT}}</ref> She then gives him the [[Pocket Egg]] which he must carry until it hatches. After the egg hatches, Link must use it to wake up [[Talon]], and then return the new Cucco to the Cucco Lady. She will compliment Link's ability to care for Cuccos and entrust him with her brother's blue Cucco, [[Cojiro]].<ref name="Cojiro">{{Cite|Oh, your Cucco looks pretty happy! He must have awakened an extremely lazy guy! You're a good Cucco Keeper! I'll give you a rare, valuable Cucco, if you're interested... Its name is {{Color|OoT Red|Cojiro}}, and it used to be my brother's Cucco. Its blue body is quite charming. It's so cute!|Cucco Lady|OoT}}</ref>

In the ending of the game, the Cucco Lady can be seen juggling several Cuccos in front of [[Medigoron]] at Lon Lon Ranch.

A [[Gossip Stone]] says that the Cucco Lady goes to the [[Lakeside Laboratory]] to study how to breed [[Pocket Cucco|pocket-sized Cuccos]].<ref name= "Cucco"/>

== Trivia ==

* In the Japanese version, the Cucco Lady is known as '''Chicken Lady'''.
* She has a [[Terminan|Terminain]] counterpart in {{MM}} named [[Anju]], who manages the [[Stock Pot Inn]] and is also the lover of [[Kafei]], a male who bears an uncanny resemblance to [[Link]].
