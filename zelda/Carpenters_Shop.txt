The {{Term/Store|Carpenters Shop||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Carpenters Shop}} is located along the western edge of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, between {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Figurine Gallery|link}}. It is a wooden building, and has a waterwheel attached to its eastern side that is powered by the river in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}. The {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter|link}} can be found within it when they are not outside of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|display=Town}}, as well as a {{Term|TMC|Town Minish|link}}. There is also a {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}} that {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can use to explore the western side of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized.
