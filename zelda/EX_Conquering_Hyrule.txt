{{Term/Store|EX Conquering Hyrule||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 76 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Conquering Hyrule|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the first wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.

==Overview==
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}. {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Rito|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Goron|link}} {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} can also be found around the map, especially around the five targets. {{Term|HWAoC|Impa|link}} also appears as another target when ambushing the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When all six are defeated, {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam|link}} appears in front of a closed gate as another target, and must be defeated to open a gate behind him. The gate opens to an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} in the center of the map, with {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda|link}} inside. {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako|link}} appears in the same room when she is damaged, and when both are defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.

Only {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon}}, or {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} can complete the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, although only two out of the three can be used. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} is completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of thirty {{Plural|HWAoC|Wildberry|link}}, twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Prime Meat|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
