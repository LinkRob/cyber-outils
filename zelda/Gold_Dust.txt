{{Term/Store|Gold Dust|Gold Dusts|Series, MM, MM3D, TFH}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 125"/>

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Gold Dust is awarded to the first place contestant of the [[Goron Race|Patriarch's Race]] after completing [[Snowhead Temple]].<ref name="Gossip Stone"/><ref>{{Cite|My son got better immediately. He went to see the Goron Races that celebrate spring.|Goron Elder|MM}}</ref> The Gold Dust comes in a [[Bottle]] [[Link]] can keep the first time he wins it. The Bottle, but not the dust, is brought back through time with him when travelling back to the Dawn of the [[First Day]]. When Gold Dust is won again in future cycles, it is presented to Link on a piece of paper or foil, to be stored in a Bottle he already possesses.

[[Zubora]] and [[Gabora]], blacksmiths of the [[Mountain Smithy]], need this to forge Link's [[Razor Sword]] into the [[Gilded Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey, now that's a mean joke. Your sword has already been reforged into a Razor Sword! Unless... Do you want me to make your sword stronger? To do that, I'll need gold dust. Do you have any?|Zubora|MM}}</ref> Gold Dust can be sold for 200 [[Rupee]]s at the [[Curiosity Shop]], making it one of the most financially profitable items to sell.<ref name="Gossip Stone"/> This is in stark contrast to the abysmal 40 Rupees offered at the [[Mountain Smithy]].<ref>{{Cite|But... If you have gold dust, I'll buy it off you for 40 Rupees. How about it?|Zubora|MM}}</ref>

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Gold Dust}} are one of the rare {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Sword Master Suit|link}}.<ref name="E 125"/> Unlike most {{Plural|TFH|Material}}, they cannot be obtained from [[Levels in Tri Force Heroes|Levels]] in the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}, purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}}, or won in {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}. Instead, {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} can receive one as a reward from {{Term|TFH|King Tuft|link}} for completing all {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} in {{Term|TFH|The Ruins|link}},<ref>{{Cite|As promised, your reward for besting the {{Color|TFH Blue|Drablands challenge}} of The Ruins. Use it well!|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> or as one of the random prizes for winning a {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} battle in the {{Term|TFH|The Ruins}}-based arena.{{Fact}}

Following the Ver. 2.1.0 update, {{Term|TFH|Link}} can also receive {{Term|TFH|Gold Dust}} by speaking to the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}}, who, for every ten {{Plural|TFH|Hero Point|link}} collected, will grant the young hero a {{Term|TFH|Material}} of the lowest value required to craft an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}. The odds of winning one in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum}} have also been increased.{{Fact}}

{{Plural|TFH|Gold Dust}} can also be sold to the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} for 600 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} apiece.
