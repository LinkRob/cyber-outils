==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Plural|OoT3D|Door Mimic}} resemble normal {{Term|OoT3D|Dungeon|link}} doors, but fall down when approached by {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}}, potentially crushing him if he does not move out of the way quickly enough. They can be destroyed with a variety of weapons, including {{Plural|OoT3D|Bomb|link}}.

{{Plural|OoT3D|Door Mimic}} appear in the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo's Training Ground|link}}, and the {{Term|OoT3D|Spirit Temple|link}}. If {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} tries to open them, they will wobble for a moment and fall on him. They can be destroyed using {{Plural|OoT3D|Bomb}} or the {{Term|OoT3D|Megaton Hammer|link}}. Door Mimics can be discerned from regular doors in {{OoT|-}}, as they stick out from the wall and the wall behind the bars in the window appears to be made of bricks.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Door Mimic}}
In {{TMC}}, {{Plural|TMC|Door Mimic}} appear in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}. Those in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt}} are red-orange, while those in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle}} are blue; however, they function identically.
