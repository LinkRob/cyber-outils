{{Term/Store|The Great Bridge: Defender||Series, LCT}},<ref>{{Cite|The Great Bridge: Defender|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> also known as '''Level 3-3'''<ref>{{Cite|Level 3-3|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> and '''Kargarok Assault'''{{Name Ref|LCT}} in the PAL version, is a {{Term|LCT|Stage|link}} in {{LCT}}. It is the third {{Term|LCT|Stage}} in {{Term|LCT|Level 3|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|LCT}}
{{Term|LCT|The Great Bridge: Defender}} takes place close to the {{Term|LCT|Great Hylian Bridge|link}}, where {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} must defend himself from an onslaught of {{Plural|LCT|Kargarok|link}} for one minute. If a {{Term|LCT|Kargarok}} is not shot quickly enough once it gets close to {{Term|LCT|Link}}, it will shoot a spherical, rock-like projectile. Throughout the {{Term|LCT|Stage|link}}, red-colored {{Plural|LCT|Kargarok}} will fly around the {{Term|LCT|Great Hylian Bridge}}.

A giant {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow|link}} can be found on top of the rock facade adjacent to the bridge.
