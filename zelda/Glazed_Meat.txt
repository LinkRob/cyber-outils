{{Term/Store|Glazed Meat|Glazed Meats|Series, BotW|plural}} are [[item]]s in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Glazed Meat}}<br/>The sweetness of the honey permeates the meat, giving it a complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
Glazed Meats are meals that can be made by [[cooking]] predominantly Meat with [[Courser Bee Honey]]. regular version can only be made by adding a third ingredient that differs in effects from the Courser Bee Honey. They heal seven [[heart]]s in [[Link|Link's]] [[Life Gauge]] and sell for 60 [[Rupee]]s. The sweetness of the honey permeates the meat, giving it a complex taste profile.<ref name="Inventory"/>

There is one variation of Glazed Meat:

* '''Energizing Glazed Meat''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Glazed Meat}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The sweetness of honey permeates the meat, giving it a complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by only using Meat and Courser Bee Honey. Eating these will refill a portion of Link's [[Stamina Wheel]].<ref name="Energizing"/> They heal seven of Link's Hearts, and they're worth 50 Rupees when sold.
