"{{Term/Store|Urbosa's Hand||Series, BotW}}" is the sixth [[Recovered Memory]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Urbosa's Hand}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #6}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
[[Link]] recalls the Memory when Lady [[Riju]] puts on the [[Thunder Helm]] during the "[[Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|Divine Beast Vah Naboris]]" [[Main Quest]]. Prior to the events of the Memory, Link is called to the [[Gerudo Desert]] by [[Urbosa]].<ref>{{Cite|Link is called to the Gerudo Desert by Urbosa. There, he sees Zelda, exhausted and asleep. Urbosa opens up about her concern for Zelda and reminds Link that it is a warrior's honor to protect a member of the royal family.|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

During the Memory, Link arrives to the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} to find [[Princess Zelda]] sleeping in Urbosa's arms. The Thunder Helm rests on the floor nearby. Urbosa remarks that Link got there quickly and that she should have expected such from Zelda's own appointed knight.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, well...you certainly got here fast.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I should have expected as much, from the princess's own appointed knight.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> Urbosa then explains that Zelda is exhausted from being out on a survey all day.<ref>{{Cite|She was out on a survey all day today. Still as the sands now...|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> She asks Link if he and Zelda have been getting along and quickly understands that they have not due to Link's response of silence.<ref>{{Cite|So...? Spill it, boy. Have the two of you been getting along all right?|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's OK... I know. Your silence speaks volumes.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref>

Urbosa does not blame Link for their poor relationship and explains that Zelda feels like a failure in her destiny when seeing that Link has succeeded in his.<ref>{{Cite|She gets frustrated every time she looks up and sees you carrying that sword on your back.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It makes her feel like a failure when it comes to her own destiny|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Don't worry, it's not like you carry blame in any of this.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> She goes on to explain that Zelda has put more than enough time and dedication to accessing her [[Triforce|sealing power]], even passing out in freezing waters during one attempt.<ref>{{Cite|It's unfortunate. She's put in more than enough time.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ever since she was a young girl, she's gone through rigorous daily routines to show her dedication...|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|She once passed out in the freezing waters trying to access this sealing power. And she has nothing to show for it...|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> Urbosa sympathizes with Zelda, saying that she would be doing the same thing and that Zelda is quite special.<ref>{{Cite|That's the motivation driving her research. I'd be doing the same thing.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|She really is quite...special.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> Telling Link to protect Zelda with his life, Urbosa turns to smile at Link and tells him that his role as her knight is quite an honor.<ref>{{Cite|You be sure to protect her with your life. It's quite the honor.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref>

With the night getting cold, Urbosa feels that it is time to take Zelda inside, but suddenly a better idea strikes her.<ref>{{Cite|The night brings a chill... It's probably time we take her in.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> Urbosa snaps her fingers to perform [[Urbosa's Fury]], which causes lightning to strike into the Gerudo Desert and startles Zelda awake.<ref>{{Cite|Urbosa! What was that?! Did you feel that?!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Zelda turns to notice Link and questions what he is doing there.<ref>{{Cite|Wait, what—how did you—what are you doing here?!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Urbosa begins to laugh at her confusion as Zelda is still clueless and asks what is funny.<ref>{{Cite|Wh-what's so funny?|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>
