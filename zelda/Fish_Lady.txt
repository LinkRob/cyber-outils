The {{Name|Fan|{{Term/Store|Fish Lady||Series}}}} is a character in {{TFoE}}.{{Name Ref|TFoE}}

==Biography==
The Fish Lady lives in [[Crater Cove]] in a raised house, where she can be seen butchering fish. She had blocked the vent entrance outside to stop the [[Fire Daira]]s from coming out, adding that she used a draconian skull as a latch and hid the cave's [[Key]] inside, and that the Dairas have yet to find it.<ref>{{Cite|Dairas came straight from the crater 'till I boarded the vent. Used a skull for a latch and not one Daira's figured it out!|Fish Lady|TFoE}}</ref> Her words give [[Link]] a clue on how to progress further through the stage.
