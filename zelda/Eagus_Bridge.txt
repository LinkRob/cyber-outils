{{Term/Store|Eagus Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Eagus Bridge}} is a wooden bridge connecting the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. It can be found at the end of a branch of the {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}}, which then flows out into {{Term|BotW|Nabi Lake|link}}. All travelers who move between {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}} will pass over {{Term|BotW|Eagus Bridge}} on their journey. This includes {{Term|BotW|Endai|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Spoone|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Kazul|link}}.

In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, a wooden platform held aloft by {{Plural|BotW|Sky Octorok|link}} can be found on the eastern side of the Bridge. Atop this platform, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find a wooden {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Throwing Spear|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
Just off the western side of {{Term|BotW|Eagus Bridge}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find the wreckage of a cart atop some rocks. On the remains of this cart is a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}}, which serves as a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}} for a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} lifts the {{Term|BotW|Rock}}, the {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
