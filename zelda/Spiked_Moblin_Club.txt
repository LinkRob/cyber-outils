{{Plural|BotW|Spiked Moblin Club}} are upgraded {{Plural|BotW|Moblin Club|link}} that have had {{Term|Series|Animal|link}} bones attached to them to increase their damage.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are wielded most commonly by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Moblin|link}}, particularly in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}.

Since they are made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Moblin Club}} will float when dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They will also burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} is unable to pick them up.
