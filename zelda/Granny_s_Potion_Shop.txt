{{Term/Store|Granny's Potion Shop||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a location in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 240}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|OoT3D|Granny's Potion Shop}} is operated by {{Term|OoT3D|Granny|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}}. However as she is away on an expedition, it is impossible to enter while {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} is a child. Once {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} returns to {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village}} as an adult, he can enter {{Term|OoT3D|Granny's Potion Shop}} and purchase {{Plural|OoT3D|Blue Potion}} from her. Unlike the other {{Term|OoT3D|Potion Shop}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Granny's Potion Shop}} will remain open at night. After receiving the {{Term|OoT3D|Odd Mushroom|link}} from the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Craftsman's Son|link}} in the {{Term|OoT3D|Lost Woods|link}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can take it to {{Term|OoT3D|Granny}} inside her {{Term|OoT3D|Granny's Potion Shop|display=Potion Shop}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cojiro? Why? Normally only a nice guy like me can tame you... Which means... You... You must be a nice guy! Must be! You must be! Please, Mr. Nice Guy! Please! Deliver this to the {{Color|OoT3D Red|old hag in the potion shop}} in Kakariko Village! This will disappear if you take too long, so you gotta hurry!|Master Craftsman's Son|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That fool! He had to go into the forest. ...I see. Hey, give it to me now!|Granny|OoT3D}}</ref> She will then reduce it down to an {{Term|OoT3D|Odd Poultice|link}} for free and urge {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} to return it to him.<ref>{{Cite|If you see that fool, give this to him. It is the strongest medicine I have ever produced. However, this poultice will not work on a monster...|Granny|OoT3D}}</ref> However, she also cautions {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} that the {{Term|OoT3D|Odd Poultice|display=Poultice}} won't work on monsters, implying that it may be too late for it to be of any use to him.

According to one of the {{Plural|OoT3D|Carpenter}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Granny's Potion Shop}} is also the home of the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Craftsman|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I heard that our boss's house is just behind this house. He doesn't want any of us to know, though.|Carpenter|OoT3D}}</ref>
