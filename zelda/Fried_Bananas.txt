==Location and Uses==
Fried Bananas are {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}} items made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}}, [[Tabantha Wheat]], and [[Cane Sugar]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can only be made by adding a fourth ingredient that is a fruit or vegetable without the Mighty effect. Eating them will restore some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Children are known to love this dish, and the trick to make them is to fry them over very high heat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

[[Misa's Recipe Book]], which can be found at [[The Slippery Falcon]] in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, contains a recipe for Fried Bananas. [[Misa]] first instructs the reader to buy Tabantha Wheat and Cane Sugar from her store.<ref>{{Cite|First, you'll want to {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|cane sugar}} here are the {{Color|BotW Blue|Slippery Falcon}}!|Misa's Recipe Book|BotW}}</ref> Next, she says to fry them with Mighty Bananas, though she notes they're rare around Rito Village.<ref>{{Cite|Then, just fry those up alongside some {{Color|BotW Blue|mighty bananas}}! {{Color|BotW Blue|Mighty bananas}} are rare in these parts, but this dish is worth it!|Misa's Recipe Book|BotW}}</ref>

Only one variation of Fried Bananas exist:

* '''Mighty Fried Bananas''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Fried Bananas}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Children love fried mighty bananas. The trick is frying them over very high heat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by only using the base three ingredients. When consumed, they'll temporarily increase the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>
