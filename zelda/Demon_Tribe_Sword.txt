{{Term/Store|Demon Tribe Sword|Demon Tribe Swords|Series, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are recurring [[item]]s and [[object]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and uses==
[[Ghirahim]] uses this blade in battle in {{SS}}. There are different variants of the Demon Tribe Sword such as a broadsword and a greatsword which he both uses when fighting [[Link]]. These blades seem to have an ability to repair themselves and come in different shapes or forms. They also emit a dark aura such as [[Demon]]s.
