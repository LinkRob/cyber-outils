==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hearty Radish}}
Hearty Radishes are found in the ground in [[Hyrule Ridge]] and [[East Necluda]], their location indicated by their stalk. They're also sold by [[Beedle]] at the [[East Akkala Stable]] and the [[General Shoppe]]. They can be eaten raw to restore two and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. If a Hearty Radish is roasted over a [[Campfire]], it becomes a [[Roasted Radish]]. If a Hearty Radish is [[Cooking|Cooked]] in a [[Cooking Pot]], the resulting [[Food]] will temporarily increase [[Link|Link's]] maximum {{Plural|BotW|Heart}}.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> At the [[Kochi Dye Shop]], Hearty Radishes are one of the ingredients that can be used to make Peach [[Dye]].

Making [[Creamy Heart Soup]] in a Cooking Pot requires {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to use either a Hearty Radish or a [[Big Hearty Radish]].
