{{Term/Store|EX Alert: Kakariko Village||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 78 {{Term|HWAoC|EX Vicious Monster Battle|link}} in {{HWAoC}} included in the second [[Downloadable Content]] pack, {{GoR}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Alert: Kakariko Village|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}. They are surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, and when defeated, a gate opens to a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos}} {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}}. After capturing it, a gate opens to an area in which the vicious monsters appear. While the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} fights the vicious monsters, strong {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} such as an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} will appear to fight as well.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the vicious monsters are defeated, and can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of two {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Swift Carrot|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
