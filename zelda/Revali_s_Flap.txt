"{{Term/Store|Revali's Flap||Series, BotW}}" is the second {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Revali's Flap}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #2}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"Revali's Flap" is triggered by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Saki|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. She directs {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Revali's Landing|link}}, explaining that it was named in the memory {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} after the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you take off from {{Color|BotW Blue|Revali's Landing}}, it's a straight shot down to the {{Color|BotW Red|Flight Range}}. Revali's Landing is a memorial for the Rito Champion, Revali... So named in the hope that none might forget the events of that horrible day...|Saki|BotW}}</ref>

The memory starts with Link watching {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} fly overhead, standing on Revali's Landing before it received its name. He's interrupted by a strong gust of wind blowing in from below, which Revali flies up before landing in front of him. He remarks how impressive {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale|link|display=his ability}} is, stating he's made an art of creating updrafts.<ref>{{Cite|Impressive, I know. Very few can achieve a mastery of the sky. Yet I have made an art of creating an updraft that allows me to soar.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> He continues to brag about his skills, calling it a masterpiece of aerial techniques among the {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's considered to be quite the masterpiece of aerial techniques, even among the Rito.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> In addition, Revali believes that with his skills properly applied, {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} could easily be defeated.<ref>{{Cite|With proper utilization of my superior skills, I see no reason why we couldn't easily dispense with Ganon.|Revali|BotW}}</ref>

Revali moves on,<ref>{{Cite|Now then, my ability to explore the firmament is certainly of note...|Revali|BotW}}</ref> and he begins to circle Link on the ground. He expresses his disdain for Link's position as the chosen hero, and he feels his talents are wasted assisting him.<ref>{{Cite|But let's not—pardon me for being so blunt—let's not forget the fact that I am the most skilled archer of all the Rito. Yet despite these truths, it seems that I have been tapped to merely assist you. All because you happen to have that little darkness-sealing sword on your back. I mean, it's just...asinine.|Revali|BotW}}</ref>

As a result, Revali decides to challenge Link to a duel.<ref>{{Cite|Unless...you think you can prove me wrong? Maybe we should just settle this one on one?|Revali|BotW}}</ref> Rhetorically, Revali asks where they should fight, quickly deciding Divine Beast Vah Medoh to be a suitable location.<ref>{{Cite|But where...? Oh, I know! How about up there?!|Revali|BotW}}</ref> He then laughs, reminding Link he has no way of making it up to Vah Medoh on his own.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you must pardon me. I forgot you have no way of making it up to that Divine Beast on your own!|Revali|BotW}}</ref> Once again, Revali uses his Gale, flying up to the Divine Beast and mockingly wishing Link luck sealing the darkness.<ref>{{Cite|Good luck sealing the darkness!|Revali|BotW}}</ref>
