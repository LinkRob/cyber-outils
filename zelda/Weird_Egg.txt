The Weird Egg is given to [[Link]] by [[Malon]] when he speaks to her along the path to [[Hyrule Castle]]. Malon entrusts it to Link in order to wake her father, [[Talon]], who fell asleep near the entrance to the [[Castle Courtyard]].<ref>{{Cite|Are you going to the castle, fairy boy? Would you mind finding my {{Color|OoT3D Red|dad}}? He must have fallen asleep somewhere around the castle. What a thing for an adult to do! Tee hee! Oh, yeah—if you'll look for him, I'll give this to you. I've been incubating this egg very carefully... Tee hee!|Malon|OoT3D}}</ref> After receiving it, the Weird Egg will hatch the next time the sun rises, yielding a [[Cucco]].<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Look! A}} Cucco {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|hatched from the}} egg {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|you were incubating! It's the miracle of life!}}|Navi|OoT3D}}</ref> When presented to Talon, the Cucco will crow and awaken him,<ref>{{Cite|What in tarnation? Can't a person get a little shut-eye around here?|Talon|OoT}}</ref> prompting him to return home.<ref>{{Cite|What?! Malon was looking for me? I'm gonna catch it from her now! I messed up bad, leaving Malon behind to wait for me! She's really gonna let me have it!|Talon|OoT}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWDE Weird Egg Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, Weird Eggs appear as Feast-type [[Food#Hyrule Warriors|Food]]. They are imbued with the Light {{Term|HWDE|Element|link}}. When consumed by a [[Companion Fairy]] in the [[Dining Room]] of the [[My Fairy]] mode, Weird Eggs can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.
{{Noncanon|End}}
