{{Term/Store|Cannon Island||Series, PH}} is a place in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=284 }}</ref> It is a small island situated in the Southwestern Sea, just southwest of Mercay Island.<ref name= "Cannon">{{Cite|They make sea life easier. Or at least louder. Hmmm, that's about all I know. I heard you can get them from a guy named Eddo on an isle to the south.|N/A|PH}}</ref> It is easily distinguishable from the large crane that sits at its peak, which is part of {{Term|PH|Eddo's Garage|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
Before {{Term|PH|Link}} can purchase anything from the local shop's owners, he has to navigate across the {{Term|PH|Cannon Island|display=Island}}, which includes surviving {{Term|PH|Bee|link}} attacks, exploring a cave with block puzzles, and using {{Plural|PH|Bomb Flower|link}} to open new ways across the {{Term|PH|Cannon Island|display=Island}}.<ref>{{Cite|Heh heh...you impress me. You'd brave this dangerous isle for one of my cannons. For a sea shrimp, you've got a good eye...|Eddo|PH}}</ref> After meeting {{Term|PH|Eddo|link}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} can find him anytime more quickly by entering his {{Term|PH|Eddo's Garage|link|display=Garage}} from the original entrance. {{Term|PH|Link}} receives two major items from {{Term|PH|Eddo's Garage}}: the {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}} and the {{Term|PH|Salvage Arm|link}}.<ref name="Cannon"/><ref>{{Cite|You can make a fortune by recovering lost treasure from the bottom of the sea! Gotta get a Salvage Arm, though. I bet old Eddo on that southern isle can help.|N/A|PH}}</ref> {{Term|PH|Eddo|link}} and his assistant, {{Term|PH|Fuzo|link}}, run the shop here that will sell {{Term|PH|Link}} these items throughout his journey.<ref>{{Cite|Hello, there! You've come to Eddo's Garage...that is, Master Eddo's shop! I'm Fuzo, his apprentice!|Fuzo|PH}}</ref> The {{Term|PH|Cannon}} can be purchased for 50 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A cannon bought from Eddo for the high price of 50 Rupees.|N/A|PH}}</ref> The cost of the {{Term|PH|Salvage Arm}} can be dropped from 300 to 200 {{Plural|PH|Rupee}} if {{Term|PH|Link}} follows the instructions carefully.<ref>{{Cite|Want the Salvage Arm?! Then make your voice ring out loud and clear! I will decide your price based on that. OK, go!|Eddo|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Not bad, hmmm... For that scream...well... I'll sell the Salvage Arm for 300 Rupees. Want it?|Eddo|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Now those are some pipes! No complaints here... Well... I'll sell the Salvage Arm for 200 Rupees. Want it?|Eddo|PH}}</ref>

There is a {{Term|PH|Power Gem|link}} hidden on the {{Term|PH|Cannon Island|display=Island}}.{{Where}} To help cover the cost of {{Term|PH|Eddo}}'s wares, {{Term|PH|Link}} can find a {{Term|PH|Big Red Rupee|link}}, valued at 200 {{Plural|PH|Rupee}}, by rolling into a lone {{Term|PH|Tree|link}} east of the {{Term|PH|Bomb Garden|link}}.
