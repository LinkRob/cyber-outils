The {{Term/Store|Kiah Toza Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kiah Toza Shrine}}<br/>Master the Orb}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Master the Orb''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Kiah Toza Shrine}} is found in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Pikida Stonegrove|link}}. It is one of the three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that must be visited as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must quickly {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link|display=Shield Surf}} through a series of {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}}. These start near {{Term|BotW|Hebra Peak|link}} and eventually lead down to the {{Term|BotW|Pikida Stonegrove}}. Each {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|display=Ring}} disappears after a period of time, forcing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to start over from the beginning if he misses one.

After passing through the last {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Kiah Toza Shrine}} will rise up from beneath the snow. As {{Term|BotW|Link}}0 enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kiah Toza|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kiah Toza. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kiah Toza|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Kiah Toza Shrine}} consists of a large structure the continually generates a large {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} that begins to roll down a series of contraptions to the bottom. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must alter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb's}} path to keep it rolling by using his {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Rune|link}}, as well as stepping on {{Plural|BotW|Floor Switch|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Switch|link}} are capable of changing the direction of tracks, as well as activating a {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}} at the end of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}}'s journey. Once the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} has reached the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} at the end, a large portcullis will open that leads to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} at the end of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can also find a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Falcon Bow|link}} at the top of the structure. This can be accessed by using {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} on a stone block at its rear to reach a {{Term|BotW|Ladder|link}}.

Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Kiah Toza}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} rewards {{Term|BotW|Link}} with one of {{Plural|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of the hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Medoh's Emblem.}}|Kiah Toza|BotW}}</ref> If this is the first or second {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that has rewarded {{Term|BotW|Link}} with this {{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|display=Emblem}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will then tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} he must collect three and state how may remain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Kiah Toza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Kiah Toza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Kiah Toza|BotW}}</ref> If it is the third {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} to reward him with {{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will tell him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Medoh...}}|Kiah Toza|BotW}}</ref>
