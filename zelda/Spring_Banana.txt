The {{Term/Store|Spring Banana||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Spring Banana is a fruit that only grows in [[Mt. Cucco]] during the [[Season|spring]].<ref>{{Cite|Delicious Spring Bananas! There's a tree on the top of {{Color|OoS Blue|Mt. Cucco}}, but with the seasons like this, there may be no fruit.|N/A|OoS}}</ref> It is obtained from a single palm tree that grows in the mountain's eastern-most summit,<ref>{{Cite|I surrender! In return, here's a tip! On the summit of the mountain in the east are {{Color|OoS Red|Spring Bananas}}! Many people will want them!|Business Scrub|OoS}}</ref> which can only be reached by flying up with the help of a [[Flying Cucco]] at its base.

[[Moosh]], who can be found at the base of the tree's summit, craves a Spring Banana but cannot reach the fruit.<ref>{{Cite|Sniff! Sniff! There's a  Spring Banana tree up there. But until the Rock Flowers bloom in spring, I can't climb up to it... Can nothing be done?|Moosh|OoS}}</ref> Link may bring him the Spring Banana, and in return Moosh helps him navigate through the remainder of Mt. Cucco.  

==Trivia==
*Among several other items, a [[Subrosian]] can be seen tossing a Spring Banana into a pit of lava in an attempt to make the volcanoes of [[Subrosia]] erupt.
