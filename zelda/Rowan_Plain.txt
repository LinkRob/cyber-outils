{{Term/Store|Rowan Plain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Rowan Plain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Rowan Plain is a flat, grassy field east of [[Aldor Foothills]]. The field contains groups of [[Horse]]s and is loosely surrounded by a ring of [[Tree]]s. In the northeastern, southeastern, and southwestern sections of the Plain are [[Korok]]s, with one in each section. In the northeastern corner, [[Link]] must shoot an acorn into a log to reveal the Korok. In the southeastern corner, Link must climb on top of a destroyed building and subsequently examine the Fairy Lights there. In the southwestern corner, Link must jump three fences in a row while riding a Horse to reveal the Korok.

In a group of Trees in the northwestern section of Rowan Plains rests a [[Stalnox]].
