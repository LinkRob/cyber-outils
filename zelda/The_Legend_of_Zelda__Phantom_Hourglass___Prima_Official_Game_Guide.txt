'''''The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass — Prima Official Game Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{PH}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 336 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{PH}}, a listing of {{Plural|PH|Item|link}}, collectibles, characters, {{Plural|PH|Enemy|link}}, and {{Plural|PH|Boss|link}}, as well as an overview of the game's {{Term|PH|Battle Mode|link}}.
