{{Term/Store|Dye|Dyes|Series, BotW|plural}} are objects in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 97}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Dye}} are used by {{Term|BotW|Sayge|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} to change the color of {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here we go! For 20 rupees total, I'll dye your clothes here to the color of your choosing!|Sayge|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} pays {{Term|BotW|Sayge}} 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} and selects five {{Term|BotW|Material|link}} of the same color, {{Term|BotW|Sayge}} will {{Term|BotW|Dye}} {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s current {{Term|BotW|Armor}} in that color.
