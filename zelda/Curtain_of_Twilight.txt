{{Name|Fan|Curtains of Twilight}} are {{Plural|TPHD|Object|link}} in {{TP}}.{{Name Ref|TPHD}} They are giant walls of {{Term|TPHD|Twilight}} that cascades over {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}} and they serve as the barrier between the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight}} and the {{Term|TPHD|Light World|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TPHD|Curtain of Twilight}} is only penetrable by having someone in the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight Realm|link}} grab {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} and drag him in.<ref name=":0">{{Cite|Last time, a shadow beast pulled you through the curtain of twilight... But if you want to go that way this time, you'll need the cooperation of someone FROM the twilight...like me!|Midna|TP}}</ref> When seen from afar, the entrance appears to be but a black wall with golden outlining, but when observed at a closer distance, strange markings are seen written on it. Any non-{{Term|TPHD|Twili|link}} passing into the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight}} without the protection of the {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword|link}} will be affected by its power and transformed, in most cases, into spirits. Although {{Term|TPHD|Link}}, as the chosen hero with the {{Term|TPHD|Triforce of Courage|link}}, is transformed into a {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|display=blue-eyed wolf|link}} upon passing through it instead.<ref>{{Cite|In the land covered in {{Color|TPHD Red|twilight}}, where people roam as spirits, you were transformed into a blue-eyed {{Color|TPHD Red|beast}}...  That was a sign... It was a sign that the {{Color|TPHD Red|powers of the chosen one}} rest within you...and that they are awakening.|Faron (Spirit)|TPHD}}</ref> When the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight}} is lifted from the province covered by it, the {{Term|TPHD|Curtain of Twilight}} disappears along with it.
