{{Term/Store|Kafei's Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref> It resembles the face of [[Kafei]].

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's {{Color|MM Red|Kafei's Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>The worry of a concerned mother is expressed quite well in that mask.</p>}}

The mask is given to [[Link]] by [[Madame Aroma]], who handmade it herself.<ref>{{Cite|The cute boy's mask seems to have been made by an important man's wife...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> Link must visit the [[Mayor's Official Residence]] on the morning of the [[First Day]] and go into Madame Aroma's office.<ref>{{Cite|The room on your right is the drawing room and it's also Madame Aroma's office.|Receptionist|MM}}</ref> Upon his arrival, Madame Aroma will assume Link is the professional she hired to find [[Kafei]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh dear, are you on a field trip? ...Or are you the expert person-finder I hired? Yes, yes, yes. I say, you have the face of a pro.|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref> She then asks if he will accept the assignment, and if he answers with "Yes," she will give him Kafei's Mask to begin his search.<ref>{{Cite|The person I want you to locate is my son, Kafei. [...] Well? Could you look for him? Oh my, of course. You are an expert. Well, I'm counting on you!|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref>

The mask is used to help gather information about the missing person, Kafei.<ref>{{Cite|You're also looking for Kafei?|Anju|MM}}</ref> Talking to [[Clock Town]]'s residents while wearing the mask will prompt them to comment on Kafei. It plays a vital role in the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest.

==Trivia==
*Kafei's Mask is one of the few duplicated masks in the game, as [[Anju]] also has it. The other masks with this characteristic are the [[Keaton Mask]], which each of the [[Bombers Secret Society of Justice|Bombers]] claim to have, [[Romani's Mask]], which is owned by all customers of [[Latte]], the [[Garo's Mask]], which both [[Gorman Brothers]] own, and the [[Couple's Mask]], which is a symbol of marriage.<ref>{{Cite|My mom and dad have that!|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The symbol of marriage, the Couple's Mask seems to have the power to calm and silence arguments.|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref>
*While wearing Kafei's Mask, the carpenter in the blue vest reacts in a violently angry way. He implies that he has feelings for Anju and wishes for Kafei to remain missing.<ref>{{Cite|Lookee here! Don't be in'erfer'in with my dear Anju!!! You 'fraid o' my fists? Don' go findin' that Kafei!|Carpenter|MM}}</ref>
