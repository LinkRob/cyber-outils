{{Term/Store|Southeastern Sea||Series, PH}} is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 284}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|PH|Southeastern Sea}} is a quadrant in the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}} located in the southeast. 

The {{Term|PH|Southeastern Sea}} contains {{Term|PH|Dee Ess Island|link}}, {{Term|PH|Goron Island|link}}, {{Term|PH|Harrow Island|link}}, {{Term|PH|Isle of Frost|link}}, and the {{Term|PH|Ho Ho Tribe|link}}'s {{Term|PH|Traveler's Ship|link}}.

{{Term|PH|Beedle|link}}'s {{Term|PH|Beedle's Shop Ship|link|display=Shop Ship}} can occasionally be found wandering the waters of the {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea}}, though it will eventually leave for another quadrant of the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King}}. After obtaining the {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate|link}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} can speak to two {{Plural|PH|Golden Frog|link}}, one north of {{Term|PH|Dee Ess Island}} and one northwest of the {{Term|PH|Isle of Frost}}, to learn the corresponding symbols that allow him to {{Term|PH|Warping|link|display=Warp}} to those locations.<ref>{{Cite|Ooof! Nice shot, buddy! The big frog spread the world about you! So, want to know what to draw on the {{Color|PH Red|Cyclone Slate}} to fly right here? Here goes! Pay attention!|Golden Frog|PH}}</ref>

Once {{Term|PH|Jolene|link}} has been encountered in the {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea|link}}, the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} has a chance of running into her in all quadrants of the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King}}, including the {{Term|PH|Southeastern Sea}}.
