{{Term/Store|Fruitcake|Fruitcakes|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fruitcake}}<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Fruitcake}}|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Fruitcake}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Cake|link}} made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either an {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Wildberry|link}}, any fruit, {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Regular versions of {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake}} can be made by only using ingredients that do not grant any effects. They can be eaten to restore some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Fruitcake}} make use of fruits found all over {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, and they are commonly used in celebrations.<ref name="Inventory"/>

The first {{Term|BotW|Castle Library Book|link}} outlines the recipe for {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake}}, noting it to be a secret of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}'s favorite food.<ref>{{Cite|The Royal Family's Secret Recipe #1<br/>The Princess's Favorite: {{Color|BotW Red|Fruitcake}}|Castle Library Book|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} suggests boiling two or more pieces of fruit with {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat}} and {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar}} for a while.<ref>{{Cite|Boil any two (or more) pieces of fruit with Tabantha wheat and cane sugar for a good while.|Castle Library Book|BotW}}</ref> This dish is known to help clear one's head and help with focus, which {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} apparently took advantage of while she researched {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Eating this dish will apparently clear your head and help you focus on things such as ancient technology research.|Castle Library Book|BotW}}</ref>

As part of the "{{Term|BotW|A Royal Recipe|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can give {{Term|BotW|Gotter|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Give him fruitcake.] This... This is the fruitcake said to be eaten by the princess of Hyrule...|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> After eating it, he at first describes it as having a refreshing aroma, reminiscent of galloping across the planes on horseback.<ref>{{Cite|Simply...delicious... How could I possible describe this experience? A refreshing aroma reminiscent of galloping across these majestic planes on horseback...|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> However, he states that undersells the complexity of the dish, saying it fills him with the intensity of being seen by a {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|No, that undersells the dish's complexity. It fills me with that heart-thumping intensity that seizes your very soul when you're spotted by a Guardian!|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Gotter}} calls {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake}} an elegant dish and explains he never thought he would taste something so thrilling.<ref>{{Cite|A truly elegant dish! I never thought I would taste something quite so thrilling!|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I really must thank you for giving me such a beautiful meal! Here, take this. For your trouble.|Gotter|BotW}}</ref>

Several different variations of {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake}} can be made, based on both the kind of fruit used and whether any additional ingredients are added:

* '''Hearty Fruitcake''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Fruitcake}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}} or other hearty ingredients. Eating these cakes will completely heal all of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Fruitcake''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Fruitcake}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using energizing ingredients. When eaten, they will replenish a portion of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Fruitcake''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Fruitcake}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using enduring ingredients. If they are eaten, they will completely refill and overfill {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel}}.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Fruitcake''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Fruitcake}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}} or other hasty ingredients. Eating them will increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s movement speed for a short period of time.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Fruitcake''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Fruitcake}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper|link}} or other spicy ingredients. When consumed, they grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}} temporarily.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Fruitcake''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Fruitcake}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfruit|link}} or other electric ingredients. They grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}} when eaten.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Fruitcake''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Fruitcake}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}} or other mighty ingredients. If eaten, they increase the power of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s attacks for a short time.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Fruitcake''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Fruitcake}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using tough ingredients. Eating one will temporarily boost {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Fruitcake''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Fruitcake}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using sneaky ingredients. When eaten, these {{Plural|BotW|Cake}} will increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period of time.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Fruitcake''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Fruitcake}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Making ample use of fruits found all over Hyrule, this cake is a must for celebrations.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}} or other chilly ingredients. They grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}} temporarily if they are consumed.<ref name="Chilly"/>
