{{Term/Store|Cree||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cree|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].''

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Cree}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} child who lives in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. She is the fourth child of {{Term|BotW|Amali|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}.<ref name="Sisters"/> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first visits {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Cree}} can be found crying in her home.<ref>{{Cite|*sob* *sniff*|Cree|BotW}}</ref> If he asks what is wrong, she explains that she is upset that {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} has been causing so much chaos in the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}} and that she is unable to help.<ref>{{Cite|[What's wrong?] The {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} in the sky is the worst. Molli's dad got hurt...and Tulin's dad disappeared. Mom and Big Sis and my little sis are all sad. And I...can't do anything to help.|Cree|BotW}}</ref> According to {{Term|BotW|Amali}}, {{Term|BotW|Cree}} and her sisters have been getting needier since {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|display=Vah Medoh}} appeared.<ref name="Daughters"/> While she sleeps, {{Term|BotW|Cree}} wishes for her father to return.<ref>{{Cite|If only Dad were here... *sob*|Cree|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Cree}}'s sister, {{Term|BotW|Kheel|link}}, at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} is angry that none of her sisters have showed up to their planned rehearsal.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Grrrrrr! My sisters are dumb DUMMIES!}} They promised we'd rehearse together, but NO ONE showed up!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> Their song is supposed to be performed for {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}}, the elder of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}},<ref>{{Cite|We have to perform our song for the {{Color|BotW Blue|elder}} at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}}!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} is worried that they will be laughed off the stage.<ref>{{Cite|We're going to be laughed off the stage...|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> She asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find her sisters for her, since they would likely listen to a scary {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you... You look like I can use you... Thaaaat is to say...you look like a nice guy who likes to help people in need. Can you go tell my sisters in Rito Village to stop ditching rehearsal? They won't listen to me, but they might listen to a scary-looking Hylian like you.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Recital at Warbler's Nest|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

During this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|display=Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Cree}} can be found in {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}}, where she has bought all of the {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪Yah yah!♪<br/>♪I bought some {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}!♪|Cree|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reminds her of her rehearsal, she admits to forgetting.<ref>{{Cite|...Huh? Rehearsal? Oh yeah! I forgot!|Cree|BotW}}</ref> She gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} her {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter}} on the condition he makes {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Genli|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I give you this {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}, will you make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli? She should be in the {{Color|BotW Red|kitchen}}. She said she won't sing until she's eaten.|Cree|BotW}}</ref> She will also tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Notts|link}} is probably singing somewhere high up and {{Term|BotW|Kotts|link}} went to catch {{Term|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}} at the pond near the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}}'s entrance.<ref>{{Cite|Our older sister Notts is probably practicing her songs somewhere up {{Color|BotW Red|high}}. Kotts left to catch some {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}}. She's probably fishing at the {{Color|BotW Red|pond}} near the entrance to the village.|Cree|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My oldest sister Notts is probably somewhere up high practicing her songs. Kotts, the second oldest, said she was going fishing at the {{Color|BotW Red|pond}} near the village entrance.|Cree|BotW}}</ref>

Once all the sisters are at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}, {{Term|BotW|Cree}} will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} it is fun to sing together.<ref>{{Cite|♪Loo loo loo laa!♪ It's fun singing together!|Cree|BotW}}</ref> However, she admits that she does not know the lyrics to the song.<ref>{{Cite|Even if I don't know the lyrics...|Cree|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} uses their song to unearth {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cree}} and her sisters will be shocked by its sudden appearance. She calls it a weird-looking house and wonders if it was always there.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Was that weird-looking house always there?|Cree|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Notts}}' suggestion, the five sisters fly off to tell their mother.<ref>{{Cite|Let's tell Mom!|Notts|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yeah, let's!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cree}} trips on a rock before taking off with the rest of her sisters.

After they have returned to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Cree}} and her sisters can be found singing together near {{Term|BotW|Akh Va'quot Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|On my way to practice at the shrine!|Cree|BotW}}</ref> Due to {{Term|BotW|Link}} bringing all the sisters together, {{Term|BotW|Cree}} notes that they are all happy and singing together again.<ref>{{Cite|Because of what you did, everyone's happy and singing again! Thank you so much!|Cree|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kass}} joins his daughters upon his return to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, playing his bandoneon along with their songs. {{Term|BotW|Cree}} is so happy to sing his songs with him that she does not hear {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he speaks to her.<ref name="Dad"/> She also expresses that she does not care what they sing, so long as everyone has fun together.<ref>{{Cite|As long as everyone has fun together, I don't care what we sing!|Cree|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} admits that his daughters refuse to leave him alone throughout the whole day.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to hear the song again, you'll need to come back at nighttime. That's the only time my kids will leave me alone...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> At night, she and her sisters run to go to sleep in their home.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to go hit the feathers. *yawn*|Cree|BotW}}</ref>

In Tears of the Kingdom, Cree now runs [[The Slippery Falcon]].
