The '''Magic Scroll''' is an {{Term|TCT|Item|link}} in {{TCT}}.{{Name Ref|TCT}}

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TCT|Magic Scroll}} is in the hands of {{Term|TCT|Ganon|link}} and has the ability to encase the {{Term|TCT|Triforce of Wisdom}} and {{Term|TCT|Triforce of Courage}} in a magic {{Term|TCT|Crystal}}. Although it is not revealed how {{Term|TCT|Ganon}} came about the {{Term|TCT|Magic Scroll}}, he can summon it any time he wants by simply waving his arm, which makes the {{Term|TCT|Magic Scroll|display=Scroll}} appear immediately in his hand.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Ganon waves his arm and the crystal-making scroll appears in his twisted hand. |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 22}}</ref> Since the {{Term|TCT|Triforce of Courage}} is in {{Term|TCT|Link|link}}'s heart, it encases him as well, leading {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|link}} on a quest to break the spell of the {{Term|TCT|Magic Scroll|display=Scroll}} before the young hero gets trapped in the {{Term|TCT|Crystal}} for eternity.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'I already have the Triforce of Power, but I need all three parts of the Triforce to complete my hold on Hyrule. Since I cannot get the Triforces of Courage and Wisdom from you, then you shall not be able to use them, either,' he says. 'With this magic scroll, I have cast a spell to encase them in unbreakable crystal. Of course, since the Triforce of Courage is in Link's heart, I'm afraid the poor boy will have to be shut up in the crystal also.' |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 65}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'There's only one way to break the crystal, and I don't think you'll ever figure it out, or find three things you need. By thes time tomorrow, Link will be trapped in the crystal forever!' |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 65}}</ref>

The only way to break the {{Term|TCT|Magic Scroll}}'s spell is by obtaining the three objects as dictated by the {{Term|TCT|Fairy's Scroll|link}}: the {{Term|TCT|Magnifying Glass|link}}, the {{Term|TCT|Handprint|link}}, and the {{Term|TCT|Magic Honey|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'I'm sorry I can't stay with you, but maybe this will help.' She drops a scroll at Zelda's feet. [...] Zelda picks up the scroll. 'It's a riddle,' she says. I wonder what it means?' 'Look for something hotter than [fire], colder than [ice], and sweeter than [sugar].' |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 19}}</ref> Once the three {{Plural|TCT|Item}} are brought together and used on the {{Term|TCT|Crystal}}, the {{Term|TCT|Crystal}} will open up, freeing {{Term|TCT|Link}}. However, as mentioned by {{Term|TCT|Ganon}}, the evil wizard could simply cast the spell again using the {{Term|TCT|Magic Scroll}} to once again encase {{Term|TCT|Link}} in the {{Term|TCT|Crystal}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'You've done better than I expected. You broke my crystal spell. You fool! Don't you know I'll just cast it again?'. |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 19}}</ref>

Towards the end of the book, {{Term|TCT|Link}} throws the {{Term|TCT|Spear|link}} at {{Term|TCT|Ganon}}, which goes through his hand and nails the {{Term|TCT|Magic Scroll|display=Scroll}} to a nearby {{Term|TCT|Tree}} trunk. Afterward, the spear along with the {{Term|TCT|Magic Scroll}} disappears as they sink into the bark of the {{Term|TCT|Tree}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link hurls the spear. Ganon throws a flaming fireball at it, but the magic weapon can't be stopped. It hits Ganon in the hand and nails the scroll to the tree trunk. [...] As they watch, the spear begins to sink into the bark of the tree, taking the scroll with it. In a few moments, both disappear. |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 119}}</ref>
