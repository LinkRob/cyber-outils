==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Cruisin' for a Bruisin'}}" is an event wherein a greater number of pugilistic [[Spirit]]s randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= There's a parade of premier pugilists on the Spirit Board! Defeat them in battle to earn more gold than usual.<br/><br/>Legend-class spirits Akuma, Giga Mac, and Akira will appear at set times. Don't get punched! |author= Nintendo |published= April 3, 2019 |retrieved= April 5, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/ed38435d/index.html |title= Event: Cruisin' for a Bruisin' - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace Spirits befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
