==Biography==
==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Nayru|As the Oracle of Ages, Nayru wields power over the currents of time in Labrynna. She is a wise and serene young woman who spends her days singing songs and wishing for peace. Little does she know the Sorceress of Shadows covets her powers...}}
Nayru first appears in {{OoA}}, as the eponymous Oracle of Ages. She is a singer with one of the finest voices ever heard in [[Labrynna]].<ref>{{Cite|Nayru's the best singer in Labrynna!|Monkey|OoA}}</ref> However, her true identity as the Oracle of Ages is exposed when [[Veran]] possesses her,<ref>{{Cite|Nayru is no mere diva! She is the Oracle of Ages, who controls time!|Veran|OoA}}</ref><ref name=Possesion>{{Cite|Nayru! Oracle of Ages! I shall make your body my own! Ah hah hah hah!|Veran|OoA}}</ref> in order to take control of Nayru's ability to control the flow of time.<ref>{{Cite|Now the oracle's powers are mine. I can travel through time as I choose!|Veran|OoA}}</ref>

Nayru is a very peaceful person. She enjoys singing, and it is this that has made her popular throughout the land, among [[Human]]s and [[Animals]] alike. She attracts both when she sings. Her personality is somewhat timid, but judging from her actions, she'll do whatever she can to help a friend in need, or [[Link]]. [[Ralph]], her protector whom she has known since they were both children, is a close friend of hers, and never far away.<ref>{{Cite|I've known Nayru was the Oracle of Ages since she was a child. I have always been beside her, protecting her.|Ralph|OoA}}</ref>

Nayru also possesses a mystical instrument known as the [[Harp of Ages]], which allows Link (and presumably whoever holds it) to shift through the folds of time, when the right songs are played.<ref>{{Cite|Harp of Ages: A harp used to navigate time.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> However, Nayru will also use the Harp for simply playing a tune on occasion. As the Oracle of Ages, Nayru has no true need for the Harp of Ages, as she is capable of moving through time at a whim. It is quite likely that Nayru herself created the Harp of Ages.

====Possessed Nayru====
{{Main|Possessed Nayru}}
'''Possessed Nayru''' is a name given to Nayru while she is possessed by [[Veran]]. She was initially possessed in the opening scenes of the game, where the handful of witnesses included [[Impa]], [[Ralph]] and [[Link]].<ref name=Possesion /> Everyone else, aside from the nearly-omniscient [[Maku Tree]], is unaware of the possession, and still believe she is the pure Oracle of Ages. With Nayru serving as her cloak, Veran begins to malevolently advise [[Queen Ambi]]'s decisions in the past,<ref>{{Cite|They said the Oracle of Ages convinced Queen Ambi to build a Black Tower...|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref> which affect both the past age's citizens, and the present age in which Link initially resides.  

One notable influence Veran gave was to seal off [[Ambi's Palace]] from casual visitors, barring both Link and Ralph from attacking Veran directly. Another was to discreetly confiscate all [[Mystery Seed]]s, the only known way to harm a possessing Veran without also hurting the possessed. Early in the game, Link unknowingly offered his seeds to the Queen and her adviser in order to temporarily enter the Palace and meet Veran face-to-face.<ref>{{Cite|Those are the Mystery Seeds Queen Ambi has been demanding! Nice work, kid! I'll take you to the palace!|Castle Guard|OoA}}</ref> He was still able to gain more Mystery Seeds later in the game, however.

After obtaining the [[Lonely Peak]], Queen Ambi leaves her Palace, dropping the guard enough for Link to penetrate the castle's defenses and reach Possessed Nayru.<ref>{{Cite|Link! I have great news!!! Queen Ambi has just left the palace! Now is your chance! This may be your only opportunity to save Nayru!|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref> Once Link is victorious in the ensuing battle, Nayru will be freed from Veran's grasp,<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Link! You've safely pried me from Veran's grip.|Nayru (Oracle)|OoA}}</ref> and teach Link the [[Tune of Ages]].<ref>{{Cite|Now let me teach you the last tune, which will unlock the true power of the Harp of Ages.|Nayru (Oracle)|OoA}}</ref> Veran herself will instead possess Queen Ambi,<ref>{{Cite|Hahh... The body of a queen suits me just fine!|Veran|OoA}}</ref> a possession that will remain until the significantly tougher final boss fight in [[Black Tower]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{OoA|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{OoA|Himekawa}}, after seeing her capture in the future, Nayru placed a barrier around a forest to hide herself. Only a person with the [[Triforce]] mark on their hand could undo it. When Veran possesses Impa, she tricks Link into opening the barrier, entering the forest. As they do so, Nayru senses the presence of light and darkness, becoming disturbed.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Light and darkness approach together...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 24}}</ref> Soon, Link and a possessed Impa enter the glade, where Nayru sits with the [[Harp of Ages]]. She welcomes Link and introduces herself as the Oracle of Ages.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I am the oracle who controls time. I can see the past and, to an extent, the future.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 25}}</ref> Nayru then peers into his future, stating that his story will be told for generations.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Your story is told... generation after generation...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 26}}</ref> Link doesn't believe what she says about him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... But that's not me...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 26}}</ref> Nayru insists her prophecy is true.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= No. I mean you.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 26}}</ref> Possessed Impa then asks if Nayru can see her fate,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I you see the future... then you must know your own fate.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 26}}</ref> the oracle answering that she can, which is why she concealed herself. Although, the oracle accepts that she cannot outwit the [[Goddess of Time]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= But my fate is certain. I cannot outwit the Goddess of Time.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 27}}</ref> With that, Veran reveals her possession of Impa. In vain, the forest animals attack the [[Sheikah]], but Veran switches from Impa's body to Nayru's. She then travels to Labrynna's past, and begins her plan for the [[Twinrova]], with Nayru as her host.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Veran possessed Nayru... and is using her abilities as the Oracle of Ages to move through time.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 31}}</ref> 

After a skirmish with Nayru's childhood friend and guardian, Ralph, Link uses the Harp of Ages to enter Labrynna's past. There, he learns that Veran is using Nayru to manipulate Queen Ambi in an effort to construct a massive [[Black Tower]]. With the aid of [[Sir Raven]] and eventually Ralph, Link battles to overcome Veran and rescue not only Labrynna, but Nayru as well.

When Sir Raven is captured and sentenced to execution, Link, Ralph, and many others come to his rescue. Link tells Queen Ambi that the [[Cap'n]] stills loves her,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= He said that I should tell you... that he will love you for all eternity.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 145}}</ref> revealing that the reason why Ambi had agreed to the tower's construction was because she wanted him to see it and remember her.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I built it so that no matter in the world he was... He might see it... and never forget me.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 145}}</ref> Ambi then proceeds to cancel the tower's construction, upsetting Veran. The latter releases Nayru from her influence, and instead possesses Ambi, commanding her guards to capture Link, Ralph, and Sir Raven.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Stop loafing, guards! Seize them!!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 147}}</ref>  Before any harm can be done, Nayru awakens and teleports the group in Labrynna's present. 

Back in the present, Ralph embraces his friend and starts crying, causing Nayru to laugh and call him a "softy".<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Oh Ralph, you big softy!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 151}}</ref> Nayru tells of Veran's [[Mystery Seed]] weakness and brings them all back to the past, where she knows to find them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= We can still find some Mystery Seeds... If we return to the past.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 152}}</ref> Nayru and her companions find the hidden villagers in a cave. Nayru herself reveals that [[Roperi]]'s plant is actually a young [[Mystery Tree]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link, this is a young mystery tree. It will grow mystery seeds.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 154}}</ref> Nayru begins to play her harp to speed the sprout's growth, yet Ralph leaves the cave, set on killing Veran himself. Alarmed, Nayru tells Link that Ralph is Queen Ambi's descendant and that if she dies he will too.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Ralph is a noble... from Labrynna! Queen Ambi is his direct ancestor.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 156}}</ref> As Ralph confronts a possessed Ambi, Nayru hurriedly speeds up the sprout's growth. Once the tree finally produces a seed, Nayru brings it to Link, who quickly rushes with Sir Raven to Ralph.

Together, Ralph, Link, and Raven defeat Veran but are too late. Sacrificing themselves, the Twinrova resurrect [[Ganon]]. The trio and Queen Ambi's army take down the demonic beast, restoring happiness and light to Labrynna. Queen Ambi thanks and bids farewell to Link, Ralph, and Nayru. Nayru plays her harp, sending them back to present day Labrynna.

Nayru is last seen with Ralph, Impa, and [[Princess Zelda]] at [[Hyrule Castle]], supposedly having a meal together. The princess thanks both Ralph and Nayru for what they have done.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Ralph and Nayru, you've both done well.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 190}}</ref> Nayru says Link was the true hero and he is very brave.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link was the true hero. He's very brave.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 190}}</ref>
