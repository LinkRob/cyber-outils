The {{Term/Store|Ancient Robot|Ancient Robots|Series, SS|plural}} are a race in {{SS}}.<ref name=":0">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 294}}</ref> They are very intelligent mechanical beings who inhabit the [[Lanayru Province]] and are powered by [[Timeshift Stone]]s.<ref>{{Cite|The Timeshift Stones power us, as well as the machines we use, vreep! That's why we have to stay hard at work, zoop!|LD-301|SS}}</ref> 

==History==
{{Stub|SS}}
The Ancient Robots are one of the races that exist on the surface during the [[Zelda Timeline|Era of Hylia]].<ref name=":0" /><ref>[[:File:SkyloftCreation.jpg|File:SkyloftCreation.jpg]]</ref> The Thunder Dragon [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]] controlled the Ancient Robots by way of [[Timeshift Stone|Timeshift Stones]] hidden beneath [[Lanayru Desert]].<ref>The thunder dragon controlled the Ancient Robots  of the era by way of Timeshift Stones hidden beneath the desert that bears his name. — ([[The Legend of Zelda: Encyclopedia|Encyclopedia]], Dark Horse Books, pg. 22)</ref>

A group of these robots crewed the [[Sandship]] which protects [[Sacred Flames#Nayru's Flame|Nayru's Flame]] before pirates took it over and imprisoned them.<ref>"The robot Skipper protected Nayru's flame in his galleon." — ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Encyclopedia|Encyclopedia]]'', Dark Horse Books, pg. 54</ref> [[Skipper]] was the captain of the ship, but when the pirates took over the ship, they threw him into the sea, where he drifted on the currents to the port in Lanayru.<ref>{{Cite|And then those brutes, bzzt... the pirates, suddenly attacked us, brrzt. They were after {{Color|SS Red|Nayru's Flame}}, zzpt. My crew was imprison d...vrrrt... and I was thrown into the sea!I drifted on the current to this port, vrrrrm...|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref>

Thousands of years ago, when the {{Term|Skyward Sword|Lanayru Desert}} was a vast, green landscape, the Ancient Robots performed various mining tasks in the area. However, the land's natural resources were eventually depleted and the land withered, leaving the robots to break down and litter several areas of the desert.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=The Lanayru region was once a lush, green landscape, but due to the irresponsible actions of its past inhabitants, the natural resources of the area have been stripped dry.|url=http://www.zelda.com/skywardsword/#/world/lanayru-sand-sea/|title=The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|site=Zelda.com}}</ref>

Link can bring the Ancient Robots back to life by hitting the {{Term|SS|Timeshift Stones}} scattered throughout the desert. Once saved from a group of [[Technoblin]]s, one of the awakened robots will grant Link the [[Beetle (Item)|Hook Beetle upgrade]], allowing the Beetle to pick up and drop objects from above.

An inactive Ancient Robot can be seen behind the [[Scrap Shop]] in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}}, passed down to [[Gondo]] by his grandfather. Gondo claims that his grandfather used to have a flying robot named [[Scrapper]] that could transport anything.<ref>{{Cite|His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Scrapper}}. He may not be much to look at these days... But he was an amazing robot once! When you called him, he would go anywhere and haul anything!|Gondo|SS}}</ref> Link can reactivate this robot by giving an [[Treasure#Ancient Flower|Ancient Flower]] to Gondo.<ref>{{Cite|But old Gramps did tell me this: you can get him working again with the extract from an {{Color|SS Gold|Ancient Flower}}. It's like oil to this guy.|Gondo|SS}}</ref>

==Types==
[[File:SS LD-002G Scervo Render.png|right|200px]]
Most robots are part of the mass-produced LD-301 Series.<ref>{{Cite|This robot is part of the LD-301 Series of artificial life-forms. As just one of a number of mass-produced robots, it was not assigned a name. These robots usually work to mine Timeshift Stones and provide maintenance.|Fi|SS}}</ref> They do not usually have names, although [[Scrapper]] and [[Skipper]] are exceptions. These robots can be commonly seen around [[Lanayru Desert]], and usually mine Timeshift Stones and perform maintenance. They are powered by the very Timeshift Stones that they mine.<ref>{{Cite|The Timeshift Stones power us, as well as the machines we use, vreep! That's why we have to stay hard at work, zoop!|Ancient Robot|SS}}</ref> The miners are obedient to the dragon [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]], and must ask him for permission to collect and make use of Timeshift Stones.<ref>{{Cite|There are lots of big Timeshift Stones here, zrrt. Did you get permission from {{Color|SS Blue|Master Thunder Dragon}} to come here like we did, vrrm? We were just about to transport this Timeshift Stone to Master Thunder Dragon, vweep.|Ancient Robot|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And we can't do anything with this Timeshift Stone without Master Thunder Dragon's permission, vrrm.|Ancient Robot|SS}}</ref> They worry about their master when he is ill, and try to plant a [[Life Tree Seedling]] to revive him.<ref>{{Cite|The robots were worried about me, so they planted a {{Color|SS Red|seedling from the Tree of Life}}. The fruit of this tree is said to be able to cure any illness... They planted the seedling over there.|Lanayru (Dragon)|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We got permission from our master, the Thunder Dragon, to dig for {{Color|SS Blue|Timeshift Stones}}, vweep. But {{Color|SS Blue|Master Thunder Dragon}} is very ill right now, brrzrrt. I'm worried about him, vrrm. At this rate, our master may cease functioning, vweep...|Ancient Robot|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|SS Blue|Master Thunder Dragon}}'s condition has been bad for a long time, and the soil here is not suitable for growing trees, zrrt. If only we had more time, vrrm...|Ancient Robot|SS}}</ref> The Timeshift Stones that have been mined are inscribed with a [[Crest of the Sheikah]].<ref>[[:File:SS Timeshift Stone Model.png|File:SS Timeshift Stone Model.png]]</ref>

{{Term|SS|LD-002G Scervo|link}} and {{Term|SS|LD-003D Dreadfuse|link}} are part of the LD-002 and LD-003 Series, respectively. They seem to be more aggressive than the LD-301 Series and serve as [[Sub-Boss]]es. {{Term|SS|LD-002G Scervo}} can be found on the {{Term|SS|Sandship}}, but {{Term|SS|LD-003D Dreadfuse}} resides in a grassy area in [[Sky Keep]]. Both robots' tenacity has allowed them to remain alive from the Era of Hylia to the present.<ref>"I can't help but admire the tenacity it has displayed in staying alive and functional all these years." — Fi (Skyward Sword)</ref>

==Trivia==
*Immense statues of the Ancient Robots can be seen from the entrance to the Lanayru Mines.
*A boat schematic poster in the shipyard area has Hylian that translates to "Dogu Ship", a reference to {{Wp|Dogū}}, of which the Ancient Robots closely resemble.
*While the model prefix LD stands for Lanayru Desert, photos within [[Skipper's Retreat]] depict ancient robots operating in the ancient sea.
*The photo "Skipper's Crew" shows an unused variation of the LD-301 robot; it features a significantly larger head crest.<ref>[[:File:SkipperCrewPhotoSS.png|File:SkipperCrewPhotoSS.png]]</ref>
