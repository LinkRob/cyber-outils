}}''{{Name|Japanese|The Adventure of Link}}'' is a [[manga]] by [[Yuu Mishouzaki]] loosely based on the story of {{TAoL}}.

==Overview==
===Plot===
{{Stub|TAoL (Mishouzaki)}}
Three years after the events of {{TLoZ|Mishouzaki|-}}, {{Term|TAoL (Mishouzaki)|Link|link}} plans to travel to the {{Term|TAoL (Mishouzaki)|North Castle|link}}, but along the way he is attacked by demons. Luckily, {{Term|TAoL (Mishouzaki)|Zelda|link}} shows up to save him and she and {{Term|TAoL (Mishouzaki)|Impa|link}} care for him until he recovers. {{Term|TAoL (Mishouzaki)|Zelda}} then chooses accompanies {{Term|TAoL (Mishouzaki)|Link}} to the {{Term|TAoL (Mishouzaki)|North Castle}}. There, they meet {{Term|TAoL|Zelda|link}} from {{TAoL}} who tells the pair her story.
