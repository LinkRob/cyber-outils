{{Term/Store|Hunting Partners||HWAoC}} is a level 58 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hunting Partners|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Hunting Partners}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}}. When completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako Component|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Endura Carrot|link}}.

Throughout the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}}'s objective is to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}} and prevent them from reaching an escape point in the upper right corner of the map. As either {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} chases the {{Term|HWAoC|Bokoblin}}, a gate will close in front of them near the center of the map. A {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} also appears, and must be defeated to reopen it. The same happens again not too long afterwards, although with three {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} instead. The pattern repeats with another {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}}. {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin}}, are scattered around the map as well, while {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}} spawn with the {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} respectively. The final stretch of the chase takes place in a long, winding path that contains ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Bokoblin}} is defeated. 

A {{Term|HWAoC|Korok|link}} can also be found by itself in the bottom right corner of the map, and when talked to, gives the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}}.
