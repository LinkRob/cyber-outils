{{Term/Store|Fish Skewer|Fish Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fish Skewer}}<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Chilly Fish Skewer|Chilly Fish Skewers|Series, HWAoC|plural}},{{Exp Game|HWAoC}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Fish Skewer}}<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Fish Skewers are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} one to three {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Regulars versions of these skewers can only be made by either using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}} or using fish with contradictory effects. They heal {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} when eaten. Fish Skewers are simple dishes made by cooking fresh chunks of Fish on a skewer.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on the type of Fish used, several different variations of Fish Skewers exist:

* '''Hearty Fish Skewer''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Fish Skewer}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, or other heart ingredients. When eaten, they'll replenish all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Fish Skewer''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Fish Skewer}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}} or other energizing ingredients. Eating one of these skewers will refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Hasty Fish Skewer''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Fish Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding speed-boosting ingredients. If they're eaten, Link's movement speed will be boosted temporarily.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Fish Skewer''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Fish Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} other spicy ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Fish Skewer''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Fish Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}} or other electricity-related ingredients. Eating one grants Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Fish Skewer''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Fish Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, or other attack-boosting ingredients. Consuming one of these will increase Link's attack power for a short period of time.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Fish Skewer''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Fish Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, or other defensive ingredients. When eaten, Link's defense will temporarily increase.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Fish Skewer''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Fish Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}} or other {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}-boosting ingredients. Eating one increases Link's Stealth abilities.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Fish Skewer''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Fish Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level heat resistance.<br/>A simple dish made by cooking chunks of fresh fish on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}} or other cold ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
