'''''Scribblenauts Unlimited''''' is a game for the [[Nintendo 3DS]], [[Wii U]], and {{wp|Microsoft Windows}}. Like in its predecessors ''{{wp|Scribblenauts}}'' and ''{{wp|Super Scribblenauts}}'', the core gameplay element is that the player can create any object in the game's dictionary by typing it in Maxwell's notebook. In the [[Wii U]] version only, the player can create characters and objects from the {{smw|Mario (series)|''Mario''}} and {{TLoZ|Series}}.
