The {{Term/Store|Darknut Mask|Darknut Masks|Series, CoH}} is an {{Term|CoH|Item|link}} in {{CoH}}, added with {{SotM}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Darknut Mask}} {{Icon|CoH Skull Kid}}<br/>This mask gives Skull Kid the ability to block damage from the front while standing still. Attacks with a broadsword. Consumes staminia.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|CoH|Darknut Mask}} is obtained after defeating the {{Term|CoH|Black Darknut|link}} in the {{Term|CoH|Kakariko Crypt|link}}. When equipped, {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid|link}} is able to block many forms of damage from the front, including {{Plural|CoH|Bomb|link}}, making it useful in the {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Arena|link}}. However, this consumes {{Term|CoH|Stamina|link}}, so it may not be used continuously; the {{Term|CoH|Mask}} also grants {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid}} the ability to attack with a {{Term|CoH|Broadsword|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Despite the {{Term|CoH|Darknut Mask}} coming at the defeat of a {{Term|CoH|Black Darknut}}, the {{Term|CoH|Mask}} itself is of a regular {{Term|CoH|Darknut|link}}. It is also the only {{Term|CoH|Mask}} that {{Term|Series|Link|link}} does not possess in {{MM}} or {{OoT}}, making it exclusive to {{CoH}}.
