'''Albatrawl''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Albatrawl|A patient hunter of the air, this monster can suddenly dive, even into the water, to catch its prey.<ref name="Albatrawl">{{Cite|A patient hunter of the air, this monster can suddenly dive, even into the water, to catch its prey.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Albatrawl are [[Bird]] enemies that can be found in and around the [[Zora Cove]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Albatrawl [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], and summoning one costs two Echo energy.

==Trivia==
*Albatrawl shares the same model as [[Crow]] and [[Beakon]], but each is distinguished by its colors.
