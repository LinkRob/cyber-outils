{{Term/Store|Growler||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Growler}} is a {{Term|TMC|Dog|link}} that can be seen roaming around in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Growler}} can be found blocking a staircase leading to the southwestern part of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}, right next to {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House|link}}.

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} and can become {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized within {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}, he can cross the short bridge near the {{Term|TMC|Carpenters Shop|link}} and speak to {{Term|TMC|Growler}}, who apologizes for blocking {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s path and moves, giving him access to the southwestern portion of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, sorry... Am I in your way? I just wanted a little attention. Anyway, it was nice of you to say hi. Sorry for the trouble. I'll move now.|Growler|TMC}}</ref>

If spoken to afterwards, he will wonder why no one else ever asked him to move.<ref>{{Cite|Nobody even bothered to ask why I was blocking the way for so long... The people here are so polite, but they lack backbone, you know what I mean?|Growler|TMC}}</ref> He will also comment on the unnamed {{Term|TMC|Hylian|link}} man that is near the {{Term|TMC|Figurine Gallery|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That kid over there in the glasses is such a weirdo... You could probably tell by looking at him. He's all right, really. I mean, it's just hard to hate a guy like that! Hehehe...|Growler|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Growler}} disappears and does not appear for the remainder of the game.

While {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized, {{Term|TMC|Growler}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|Rarf! Arf arf! You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Rarf arf!|Growler|TMC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|TMC|Growler}}'s name is a reference to "growling".
