==Location and Uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, the {{Term|TLoZ|Magical Rod}} allows {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} to shoot projectile beams. The beams are similar to a {{Term|TLoZ|Wizzrobe|link}}'s and travel across the screen. According to the game manual, it is the same wand that {{Plural|TLoZ|Wizzrobe}} use to attack.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Magic wand<br/>This is the wand that Wizzrobe uses. Wave it to let loose magical spells. What's more, if Link picks up the Magic Book and learns some new spells, he can chant some fiery spells and send out flames.|game=TLoZ|page=26}}</ref> In the first quest, the {{Term|TLoZ|Magical Rod}} is found in {{Term|TLoZ|Level 6|link}}. In the {{Term|TLoZ|Second Quest|link}}, it is found in {{Term|TLoZ|Level 8|link}}.

The {{Term|TLoZ|Book of Magic|link}} can be found to increase the power of the {{Term|TLoZ|Magical Rod}}. After obtaining it, the {{Term|TLoZ|Magical Rod}}'s attack leaves a fiery blaze, similarly to the {{Term|TLoZ|Candle|link}}, when it makes contact with an enemy or wall. This also allows the {{Term|TLoZ|Magical Rod}} to be used in lieu of the {{Term|TLoZ|Candle}} to light up pitch dark rooms.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Magic Rod|The holder of this Rod is the master of fire and can blast fireballs to attack his enemies. You can also use the flames from the Rod to light the lanterns in the dungeons. This item can be used an unlimited number of times.}}
In {{LA}}, the Magical Rod is used to expel blasts of magical energy in the form of fireballs.<ref name="E 131"/> It is the main item of {{Term|LANS|Turtle Rock|link}}. It is primarily used as a weapon and for lighting unreachable {{Plural|LANS|Torch}}.<ref name="LA Guide 10"/> The Magical Rod is vital to defeating the {{Term|LANS|Dungeon|link}}'s boss, {{Term|LANS|Hot Head|link}}. The Magical Rod is one of the most powerful weapons in {{LA}}, doing twice as much damage as even the {{Term|LANS|Level 2 Sword|link}}.

There are some enemies the Magical Rod does less damage against, or cannot damage at all, such as {{Plural|LANS|Crow|link}} and {{Plural|LANS|Stalfos|link}}. The Magical Rod is also notable as the only item other than the {{Term|LANS|Magic Powder|link}} that can kill a {{Term|LANS|Cucco|link}} and a {{Term|LANS|Mutt|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= August 5, 2009|retrieved= |url= http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Q6iYSgXJto|title= Link's Awakening - Killing Cuccos|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref>

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Compendium|name=Magic Rod|desc= A small and intensely magic rod, it's said to have been wielded by an ancient magician who awoke maximum latent power in gems.|locs=  Gerudo Highlands, West Necluda| Properties= Atk: 6|}}
The Magical rod returns, now known as the Magic rod. It possesses no magical abilities on its own, but provides a boosted effect when fused with elemental gems like Ruby and Sapphire.

Magic Rods can be purchased at bargainer statues for 100 [[Poe|poes]], along with the Magic Staff and Magic Scepter.

The Magic Rod is a class of {{Term|HWDE|Weapon|link}} used by {{Term|HWDE|Link|link}} in {{HW}}. It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Fire}}. It can shoot wide-ranged projectiles and conjure pillars of flames to juggle enemies. The Rod is also capable of changing shape, such as forming a flamethrower, transforming into a larger staff to emit flame-based energy beams, and transforming into a fiery battleaxe.

An 8-Bit version of the {{Term|TLoZ|Magical Rod}} from {{TLoZ}}, called the {{Term|HWDE|8-Bit Magical Rod?|link}}, appears as the Lv.★ form of {{Term|HWDE|Lana|link}}'s {{Term|HWDE|Spear|link}}. It was added to the game with the {{TP|-}} Pack DLC in {{HW|-}}.

====Variations====
The Lv.1 Magic Rod is the {{Term|HWDE|Fire Rod}}. It resembles the {{Plural|Series|Fire Rod}} of later game appearances like {{ALttP}} and {{FSA}}.

The Lv.2 Magic Rod is the '''{{Term|HWDE|Prism Rod|link}}'''.

The Lv.3 Magic Rod is the '''Magical Rod'''. It more closely resembles the {{Term|TLoZ|Magical Rod}} from {{TLoZ}}.

The Lv.★ Magic Rod is the '''8-Bit Candle''', which is based on the {{Term|TLoZ|Red Candle|link}} from {{TLoZ|-}}. It was added to {{HW|-}} with the Master Quest Pack DLC. In {{HW|DE|-}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Candle replaces the Crackling Rod when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in {{Term|HWDE|Game Settings|link}} within the {{Term|HWDE|Settings|link}} menu.

The Lv.4 Magic Rod is the '''Magical Rod +''', which is available in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE|-}}. It appears identical to the {{Term|HWDE|Magical Rod}} but is a much stronger {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}.

The Lv.4+ Magic Rod is the '''Crackling Rod''', which was added to {{HW|L|-}} with the {{PH|-}} & {{ST|-}} Pack DLC and is unlockable in {{HW|DE|-}}. As a Multi-Element Weapon, the Crackling Rod is also associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Lightning}}. It has the same form as the Magical Rod but with an orange color scheme.

=====Special Attack=====
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Link points the Magic Rod skyward which opens, releasing a fiery dragon that corkscrews ahead for a moderate distance. The attack can hit enemies behind Link as the dragon coils behind him.
