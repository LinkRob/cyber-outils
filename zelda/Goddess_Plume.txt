==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SSHD|Goddess Plume}} are {{Plural|SSHD|Treasure|link}} used to {{Term|SSHD|Upgrading|link|display=Upgrade}} {{Plural|SSHD|Item|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Scrap Shop|link}}­. They rarely appear in place of {{Plural|SSHD|Dusk Relic}} or {{Plural|SSHD|Amber Relic}}, with a slightly higher likelihood of this happening in {{Plural|SSHD|Dungeon|link}}. Additionally, they're available as a random reward from all minigames once the primary reward has been obtained, and are available in numerous chests around {{Term|SSHD|Hyrule|link}} and the {{Term|SSHD|Sky|link}}. {{Term|SSHD|Keese|link}} may drop them in extremely rare circumstances.
