{{Term/Store|Ursore||Series}} is the sixth boss in {{ZA}}.{{Name Ref|ZA}} He is the Shrine Keeper of the [[Shrine of Strength]].<ref>{{Cite|Roar! A fool has entered my realm! Roar!|Ursore|ZA}}</ref>

Ursore is a bear who values strength above all else and values himself as a strong warrior.<ref>{{Cite|So, we meet at last! You are small and weak! You're no more than a bite for a warrior such as me!|Ursore|ZA}}</ref> His servants are the Shrine's three Knights, and he becomes angry when [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] manages to overcome them in battle.<ref>{{Cite|Roar! Who has harmed my Red Knight? I will not forget. Roar!|Ursore|ZA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Something does put my Blue Knight to shame and defeated my Green Knight! This will be punished! Roar!|Ursore|ZA}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Ursore's only means of attack is to move around the room and pounce at his enemy, which will inflict two full [[Heart]]s-worth of damage. Unlike previous bosses, he is significantly slower and it is easier to dodge his attacks.

==Trivia==
*Ursore is the only Shrine Keeper who is not fought again at the [[Vision Henge]] when [[Ganon]] summons the Shrine Keepers to fight Zelda.
*[[Sir Basil]] claims that the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Broadsword|Broadsword]] works well against Ursore.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, Zelda! It's me, Sir Basil. You've fared well so far on your difficult journey. You will shortly be meeting Ursore. Use well your knowledge of the Broadsword.|Sir Basil|ZA}}</ref> In reality, it is rather ineffective.

==Nomenclature==
Ursore is a portmanteau of Ursa, Latin for bear, and sore.
