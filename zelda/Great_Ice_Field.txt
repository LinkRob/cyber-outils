The {{Term/Store|Great Ice Field||Series, PH}}<ref>{{Cite|There was a lot of bad blood between our ancestors. So 100 years ago, we made an agreement. The Anouki live in the west... And those hairy Yook live over in the east on the Great Ice Field. That was the deal, but now some tricky Yook has snuck over here. And he's taken one of my villagers!|Island Chief|PH}}</ref> is a place featured in {{PH}}. It is located on the eastern side of the [[Isle of Frost]] and can be reached through a cave once [[Link]] has talked to the [[Island Chief]] after he has revealed the disguised Yook. The Great Ice Field is the home of the [[Yook]]s and Link must defeat the [[Yook|six guardians of the field]] to gain access to the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}} and get the [[Pure Metal|Azurine]]. Four [[Treasure Chest]]s that contain one [[Spirit Gems|Power Gem]], one [[Rupee|Big Green Rupee]] and two [[Rupee|Red Rupees]] are also located on the field.
