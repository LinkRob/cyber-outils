The {{Term/Store|Tokay Eyeball||Series, OoA}} is a {{Term|OoA|Item|link}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 141}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Tokay Eyeball is obtained from the [[Cap'n]] of the [[Piratian]]s after giving him the [[Zora Scale]].<ref>{{Cite|Take this as a sign o' me thanks! It's the jewel called the Tokay Eyeball!|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref> Once it is inserted into the statue of a [[Tokay]] on [[Crescent Island]], {{Term|OoA|Tokay Cave|link}} will open, which serves as a path to the [[Sea of No Return]] and the [[Ancient Tomb]].<ref>{{Cite|It's said to be the thing that opens the way to the ancient tomb.|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref>
