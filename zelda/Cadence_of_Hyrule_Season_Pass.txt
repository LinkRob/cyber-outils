The {{Term/Store|''Cadence of Hyrule Season Pass''||Series, CoH}} is a downloadable content season pass for {{CoH}}.<ref name="Game Details"/>

==Overview==
The {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Season Pass}} contains all three waves of downloadable content for {{CoH}}: {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Character Pack|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Melody Pack|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Symphony of the Mask|link}}, as well as exclusive bonus costumes for both {{Term|CoH|Link|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} which allow them to don red and blue versions of their ordinary outfits.

The {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Season Pass|display=''Season Pass''}} is included as part of the physical edition of {{CoH}}, which was released on October 23, 2020.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo |published= July 20, 2020 |retrieved= July 20, 2020 |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kNtacp8-GoI |title= Nintendo Direct Mini: Partner Showcase {{!}} July 2020 |site= YouTube |type= video}}</ref>
