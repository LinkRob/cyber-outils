The {{Term/Store|Travel Medallion||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Travel Medallion}}<br/>This mysterious tool was made using forgotten ancient technology. Place it by opening your pouch and registering your current location as a travel gate.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> and [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]<ref name=":0">{{Big|Travel Medallion}}This mysterious tool was built by Robbie. Place it by opening your Inventory and registering your current location as a travel gate." — Inventory ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref>. In {{BotW}} it is exclusive to the [[The Master Trials]] DLC Pack. In {{TotK}} it requires no DLC.

==Location and Uses==

=== Breath of the Wild ===
The Travel Medallion can be found inside a [[Treasure Chest]] in the chamber underneath [[Lomei Labyrinth Island]]. Its location is hinted at in [[Super Rumor Mill EX: Volume 2]] in [[South Akkala Stable]] as part of the "[[EX Teleportation Rumors!]]" [[Side Quest]].<ref>{{Cite|Traysi here! No matter where I go, my love for rumors inevitably follows... Don't you make it a point to dig up rumors while traveling? In any case, today's topic of discussion is... {{Big|The Dream of Teleportation!}} Apparently, there was once an ancient medallion that could instantly teleport you to wherever you placed it... What's more, this wondrous item may still be somewhere out there, just waiting to be scooped up! I must say, it sounds like the perfect item for travelers, adventurers, and rumor-lovers alike! I hear the treasure chest containing this tool is located {{Color|BotW Red|beneath a labyrinth in northeast Akkala}}. Is it true, or just a beautiful dream? I'll let you decide that for yourself, dear reader. Traysi's Recommendation: ★★★★★|Super Rumor Mill V2|BotW}}</ref> The Travel Medallion can be used to register [[Link|Link's]] current position as a fast travel point on the Map. Only one location can be registered at a time.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Somewhere in the world, there is a chest with a Travel Medallion inside. When you use this, you can register your current location as a fast travel point on the map. You can only register one location using the Travel Medallion.|author= |published= |retrieved= May 1, 2017|url= http://zelda.com/breath-of-the-wild/news/expansion-pass-dlc-pack-1-detailed/|title= Expansion Pass DLC Pack 1 Detailed|site= Zelda.com|type=}}</ref>

=== Tears of the Kingdom ===
The Travel Medallion functions identically to how it was used in Breath of the Wild, although Link is given access to three rather than one.

The Travel Medallions in Tears of the Kingdom were created by Robbie<ref name=":0" /> using an ancient [[Zonai]] Travel Medallion (presumably the one from Breath of the Wild's Master Trials DLC Pack) as a blueprint to create its prototype.<ref>''"Travel Medallion Prototype - Robbie reverse engineered a travel Medallion to make this prototypical version, but it's unusable in it's current state because it's missing some important data." — Inventory ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])''</ref>
