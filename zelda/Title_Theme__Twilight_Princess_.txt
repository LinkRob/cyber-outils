'''"Title Theme"''' is a [[Song]] in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
"Title Theme" plays on the title screen of {{TP}}. It is the first track on Disc 1 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Original Soundtrack]]''. 

==Trivia==
* Part of {{Term|TPHD|Midna's Theme|link}} can be heard at the end of the song.{{Fact}}
* This Title Theme also incorporates elements from the original {{TLoZ}} opening theme.{{Fact}}
