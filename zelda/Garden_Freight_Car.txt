The {{Term/Store|Garden Freight Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Garden Freight Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Garden Freight Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Ancient Gold Piece|link}}, two {{Plural|ST|Pearl Necklace|link}} and seven {{Plural|ST|Stalfos Skull|link}}. Its description reads "While very pretty, this mobile garden might not be the best for hauling." However, it transports objects just as well as any other {{Term|ST|Freight Car|link}}.

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Garden Freight Car}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Stagecoach Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
