The {{Term/Store|Castle Town Watchtower||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Castle Town Watchtower'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Castle Town Watchtower is located in the [[Hyrule Castle Town Ruins]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. An unlit [[Campfire]] can be found in the northern part of the wreckage and is accompanied by a red cloth marker.

A metal door covers a [[Rock]] acting as a [[Natural Hiding Spot]] for a [[Korok]] in the eastern part of the ruins. A second Korok can be found by examining the [[Leaves with Fairy Dust]] atop the flagpole in the northeastern section of the ruins.
