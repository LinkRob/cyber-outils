'''Ocarina of Time''' is a ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]''-themed microgame in the ''[[WarioWare]]'' series. It first appears in ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'', as part of 9-Volt and 18-Volt's stage, "The Multiplayer Test," and returns in ''WarioWare Gold'' as one of 5-Volt's microgames. The microgame is based on {{OoT}}, hence the name.

==Gameplay==
[[Young Link]] has to pull the [[Master Sword]] from the [[Pedestal of Time]].

* '''Level 1:''' Easy to pull out, and Young Link turns into [[Adult Link]] after doing so. The two later difficulties each feature a comical exaggeration as a result of Link pulling out the Master Sword. 
* '''Level 2:''' Somewhat hard to pull out, and Young Link turns into an old Link with a long, gray beard after doing so.
* '''Level 3:''' Hard to pull out, and Young Link turns into a [[Cucco]] after doing so.

==Profiles==
*'''Description:''' "Pull the sword out of the stone!"

==Trivia==
*Interestingly, Young Link uses his [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]] model instead of the correct one from Ocarina of Time.
**Likewise, in the original Wii version, [[Navi]] is erroneously colored yellow,  essentially becoming [[Tatl]]. This was likely the result of third party developers taking things from the resources they were given without double-checking.
