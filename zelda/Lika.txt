==Biography==
{{Term|TWoG|Lika}} lives in the house at the far end of {{Term|TWoG|Sakado}}'s main street, and is one of the few remaining inhabitants after most of the town's citizens were captured and taken to {{Term|TWoG|Tykogi Tower|link}}. She can be found sewing cloth in her house. She shows {{Term|TWoG|Princess Zelda|link}} the {{Term|TWoG|Cloak|link}} and gives it to her, offering to make it a {{Term|TWoG|Magic Cloak|link}} if she brings her some {{Term|TWoG|Fairy Dust|link}}. After Zelda brings her the {{Term|TWoG|Fairy Dust}}, she sprinkles it onto the {{Term|TWoG|Cloak}}, giving it the ability to make the wearer temporarily invisible. {{Term|TWoG|Lika}} advises Zelda that can use it to sneak up to her enemies.
