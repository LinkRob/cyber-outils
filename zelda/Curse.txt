{{Term/Store|Curse|Curses|Series, MM3D|plural}},<ref>{{Cite|You've been {{Color|MM3D Red|cursed}}! You can't draw your sword!|N/A|MM3D}}</ref> also known as '''Jinxes''',{{Name Ref}} is a status effect that [[Link]] suffers from in many games after he touches a specific type of [[Bubble]] or [[Fog]]. Once he touches one, he will not be able to draw his Sword until the effect disappears.

==Appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, if Link touches a normal Bubble he becomes unable to use his sword. If Link comes in contact with a [[Red Bubble]], he cannot wield his Sword until he either touches a [[Blue Bubble]], drinks a [[Potion]], or is healed by a [[Fairy]].

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Link is afflicted with a Curse when he makes contact with a Blue Bubble, causing him to flash blue (be colored blue in {{MM3D}}) and forces him to sheath his Sword, also making him unable to draw it; however, the effect is temporary and will wear off after approximately 50 seconds. Link can also immediately remove the effect of a Curse by playing the [[Song of Storms]], or using a healing item. [[Fierce Deity Link]]'s [[Fierce Deity Sword]] and the [[Great Fairy's Sword]] (because it is used on a [[C Button]] rather than the [[B Button]]) are not affected and can still be used.

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, equipping a [[Whisp Ring]] will prevent the Curse from affecting Link.

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, equipping a [[Whisp Ring]] will prevent the Curse from affecting Link.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, Link cannot use his Sword or items if Cursed by a Blue Bubble or by [[Blue Fog]]. Purple smoke escapes from his body and he appears tired, but it does not hinder his movement in any way. The effect will wear off after 5 seconds, but can be removed by stepping in [[Light Beam|light]].

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is unable to use his {{Term|TMC|Sword|link}} if he touches a {{Term|TMC|Wisp|link}} in {{TMC}}. {{Plural|TMC|Wisp}} are found at {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}} and in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, Link cannot use his Sword or items after being hit by a [[Dark Lizalfos]]'s Cursed breath attack, a [[Cursed Spume]]'s projectile, a [[Dark Keese]], or falling in Cursed water.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, "dark" variants of many of the game's enemies appear in the [[Den of Trials]], which will Curse Link for 3 to 5 seconds upon dealing damage.
