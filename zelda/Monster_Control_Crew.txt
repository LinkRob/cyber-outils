The '''Monster Control Crew''' is a military organization in {{TotK}}. They were tasked by [[Purah]] to find [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], and ware later tasked again with the goal of making [[Hyrule]] safer by defeating monsters, sometimes including [[Pirate]]s. The members are a militia of professional and civilian troops divided into three squads.
