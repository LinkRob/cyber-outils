{{Term/Store|Mudoran||Series, ALttP}} is a language in {{ALttP}}.<ref name="Guide 39">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP|publisher= Nintendo|page= 39}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|ALttP|Mudoran}} is the iteration of {{Term|ALttP|Hylian (Language)|link}} text that appears in {{ALttP}}. It is inscribed on various important rocks and pedestals, notably the {{Term|ALttP|Pedestal of the Master Sword|link}}. The {{Term|ALttP|Book of Mudora|link}} is required to translate {{Term|ALttP|Mudoran}} into messages that {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} can understand.<ref name="Guide 39"/>

==Trivia==
* In the Japanese versions of {{ALttP}}, {{Term|ALttP|Mudoran}} characters resemble {{Wp|Egyptian}} {{Wp|hieroglyphs}}. These were changed in subsequent localizations.
* Whereas later {{Term|ALttP|Hylian (Language)}} scripts can be translated to Japanese or English, {{Term|ALttP|Mudoran}} text is unintelligible.
