'''Romravens''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Romravens are purple birds encountered in the [[South Gubasha Desert]] and [[North Gubasha Desert]]. They can inflict [[Heart]] of damage to [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] if she has three or fewer [[Celestial Sign]]s, but this reduced to only half a Heart of damage if Zelda has at least four Celestial Signs.
