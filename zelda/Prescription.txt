==Location and Uses==
{{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} can obtain the {{Term|OoT3D|Prescription|link}} by bringing the {{Term|OoT3D|Broken Goron's Sword|link}} to {{Term|OoT3D|Biggoron|link}} on top of {{Term|OoT3D|Death Mountain|link}}. He will say he would gladly repair the {{Term|OoT3D|Sword|link}}, but is currently unable to because of his irritated eyes.<ref>{{Cite|That broken knife is surely my worrrrrrrrrrk... I really want to repairrrrr it, but...
