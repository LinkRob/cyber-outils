{{Term/Store|Sky Dragon Tail|Sky Dragon Tails|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 138"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Sky Dragon Tail}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Sky Realm|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Floating Garden|link}}''', ''"Win without using a sword!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Sky Dragon Tail}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Floating Garden}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Sky Dragon Tail}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Deception Castle|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Sky Dragon Tail}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Deception Castle}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Sky Dragon Tail}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Dragon Citadel|link}}''', ''"Avoid the volcanic rocks!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Sky Dragon Tail}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Sky Temple|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Sky Dragon Tail}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Sky Dragon Tail}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Robowear|link}} {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 138"/>
