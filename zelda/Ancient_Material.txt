{{Term/Store|Ancient Material|Ancient Materials|Series, BotW|plural}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref name="Purah">{{Cite|If you bring me some {{Color|BotW Red|ancient materials}}, I'll {{Color|BotW Red|power up}} your {{Color|BotW Blue|runes}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Ancient Part|Ancient Parts|HWAoC|plural}}{{Exp Game|BotW, HWAoC}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 201}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|'''Ancient Parts'''|Material|HWAoC}}</ref> and '''Ancient Components''',{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|Purah has offered to help you out in exchange for some {{Color|BotW Red|ancient components}}.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> are a recurring class of {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material}} are {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} that all source from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, like {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, and therefore can be exchanged for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}} and used to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}} {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} defeats {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}, they will drop several {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material}}. A number of {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material}} can be found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}. They are also sold by the {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} {{Term|BotW|Teli|link}}, who travels between {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}.

====Armor Upgrades====
Several pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} require {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material}} in order to be upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
