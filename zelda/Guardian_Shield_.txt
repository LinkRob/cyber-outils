{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield+}} are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}} within "A Moderate Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Shield+}}. They are larger versions of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield|link}} with a boosted output level.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref> In terms of defensive abilities, they are considered to be on par with metal {{Plural|BotW|Shield}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Like other variations of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield+}} are capable of reflecting the beams of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} and other {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}.

As they are made from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Shield+}} will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Unlike metal {{Plural|BotW|Shield}}, they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Shield}}. They also cannot be manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
