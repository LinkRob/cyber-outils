==Location and Uses==
The '''Cap of the Sky''' is a piece of [[Armor]] which can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the Skyward Sword Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Cap of the Sky. It can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Cap of the Sky, alongside the [[Tunic of the Sky]] and [[Trousers of the Sky]], comprise the [[Hero of the Sky Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from [[Skyward Sword]]. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
