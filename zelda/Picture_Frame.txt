The {{Term/Store|Picture Frame||Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Yuga]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with [[Element|Lightning]].

==Variations==
The Level 1 Picture Frame is the '''Wooden Frame'''. This is unlocked with Yuga when the {{ALBW|-}} Pack DLC is purchased in {{HW|-}} and {{HW|L}}. It is a simple dark brown frame with a small golden plaque at the bottom.

The Level 2 Picture Frame is the '''Frame of Sealing'''. In {{HW}}, this is unlocked with Yuga and the other tiers but must be obtained from a Weapon Bag. In {{HW|L|-}}, this is unlocked by getting an A Rank in the [[Lorule Map]] Battle G13 in [[Adventure Mode]]. It is modeled after the frame Yuga used to contain [[Seres]] in {{ALBW}}.

The Level 3 Picture Frame is the '''Demon King's Frame'''.  In {{HW|-}}, this is unlocked with Yuga and the other tiers but must be obtained from a Weapon Bag. In {{HW|L|-}}, this is unlocked by getting an A Rank in the Lorule Map Battle A9. It is an ornate purple frame with spiky vines wrapped around the sides and the bottom of the frame, a red edge around the inside of the frame, spiked corners and the [[Wingcrest#Lorule Wingcrest|Lorule Wingcrest]] on top.

The Level 4 Picture Frame is the  '''Demon King's Frame +''', which can only be found in {{HW|L|-}}. It is unlocked by getting an A Rank in the Lorule Map Battle G14. This is visually identical to the Demon King's Frame but is much stronger.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Yuga waves his staff to summon a giant, purple Picture Frame in the air in front of himself, after which he brings his staff down and the [[King of Evil Trident]] strikes the ground from out of the Frame like a bolt of lightning.
