The {{Term/Store|Great Dragonfly Fairy||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Great Dragonfly Fairy}}
The {{Term|TMC|Great Dragonfly Fairy}} is found near the entrance of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}, behind a wall accessible with {{Plural|TMC|Bomb|link}}. She asks {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to tell her of his adventures, and asks a series of questions regarding several key details of his journey.<ref>{{Cite|Would you tell me of the things you've seen on your adventures?|Great Dragonfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} answers all of her questions correctly, she will reward him with a larger {{Term|TMC|Quiver|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I see. Thank you for telling me about your quest. You remember it all so well. Such a faithful memory should be rewarded with some of my power.|Great Dragonfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} gets any of the questions wrong, she accuses him of lying and takes away all his {{Plural|TMC|Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have lied to me. As punishment for your lie, I will reduce your number of arrows to zero.|Great Dragonfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref>
