{{Term/Store|Electric Hinox|Electric Hinoxes|Series, HWAoC|plural}} are enemies in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
Electric Hinox are green Hinoxes with brown sideburns and bright green runes on their body signifying elemental powers. In addition to having all the moves of the [[Black Hinox]], Electric Hinoxes have original moves of their own such as the ability to produce a large lightning burst whenever one of its strong attack's end. Electric Hinox can spit a ball of lightning which will cause the shock affect like its other attacks. 

Additionally the Electric Hinox's regular attacks now have an area of effect that can give out the shocked status effect to those that get hit.
