==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Hinox Guts are dropped by all three varieties of {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} when defeated. As is the case with other {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, they can be [[Cooking|cooked]] in a [[Cooking Pot]] with critters to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be used to make Yellow [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] or exchanged for [[Mon]] with [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]].

Two [[Armor]] sets require Hinox Guts in order to be upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. The [[Soldier's Set]] needs Hinox Guts and {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos Tail|link}} to reach its third tier, while Hinox Guts and {{Plural|BotW|Smotherwing Butterfly|link}} are needed for the [[Fireproof Armor]] set to reach its fourth tier. Finally, the [[Fierce Deity Set]] needs two Hinox Guts for each piece to be upgraded to its third tier.

* Total for '''Fierce Deity Set''': 6 (0, 0, 2, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Fireproof Armor''': 6 (0, 0, 0, 2) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Soldier's Set''': 4 (0, 0, 1, 0) {{Exp|For chest and legs|x2}} + (0, 0, 2, 0) for helmet
