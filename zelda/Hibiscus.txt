The {{Term/Store|Hibiscus||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 127"/>

==Locations and Uses==
[[File:Hibiscus Screenshot.png|thumb|left|Link receiving the Hibiscus]]
The Hibiscus is the eighth item in the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}} in {{LA}}. It is obtained by bringing the [[Pineapple]] to the famished [[Papahl]] in [[Tal Tal Mountain Range]].<ref name="E 127"/> After eating the pineapple,<ref>{{Cite|This {{Icon|LADX Pineapple}} is so delicious! I'm going to eat the {{Icon|LADX Pineapple}} right now! Bon Appetit!|Papahl|LADX}}</ref> Papahl will give [[Link]] the Hibiscus.<ref>{{Cite|AH! This isn't meant to be a reward... Here, take this {{Icon|LADX Hibiscus}}! It's a hibiscus!|Papahl|LADX}}</ref> If Link takes the Hibiscus to [[Christine]] in [[Animal Village]], she will give Link a [[Letter]] that he must give to [[Mr. Write]].<ref name="E 127"/><ref>{{Cite|I would like you to take this letter to a Mr. Write who lives on the border of the Mysterious Forest, please!|Christine|LADX}}</ref>
