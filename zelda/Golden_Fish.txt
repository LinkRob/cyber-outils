==Location and Uses==
Golden Fish are rare items that can only be acquired by using an {{Term|CoH|Bottle|link}} over any tile of bubbling water found throughout {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}, including bubbling water found inside caves and dungeons. Therefore, they act as an alternative method for recovering {{Plural|CoH|Heart|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Stamina|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This will {{Color|CoH Green|completely refill your hearts and stamina}} when you use it!|N/A|CoH}}</ref>

Unlike bottled {{Plural|CoH|Fairy|link}} and potions, Golden Fish are not automatically consumed when {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} run out of {{Plural|CoH|Heart}} or {{Term|CoH|Stamina}}, and therefore must be manually activated in order to receive their healing benefits.
