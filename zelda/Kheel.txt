{{Term/Store|Kheel||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kheel|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].''

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kheel}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} child who lives in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} initially arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}}, she can be found with her mother, {{Term|BotW|Amali|link}}, on the platform near {{Term|BotW|Akh Va'quot Shrine|link}}. At night, she and her sisters sleep inside their home.

When spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} laments that it is currently too dangerous to leave {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, as she wants to sing with her sisters at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They say it's too dangerous to go outside of the village right now. But all I want to do is go sing with my sisters at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}}.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> Angrily, she calls {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} a jerk, which causes {{Term|BotW|Amali}} to chastise her for using such language.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|That {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} is a big jerk!}}|Kheel|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Kheel! Watch your language!|Amali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} then tries to defend herself.<ref>{{Cite|But, but...|Kheel|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has appeased {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, he can encounter {{Term|BotW|Amali}} searching for {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kheel!!<br/>Where are you?!|Amali|BotW}}</ref> She turns to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and explains that she has been unable to locate her daughter.<ref>{{Cite|I can't find Kheel anywhere! Where could she be?|Amali|BotW}}</ref> She theorizes that she may have gone to {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest|link}} to practice her singing.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe she went to {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}} to practice her singing...|Amali|BotW}}</ref> However, she is worried that {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} may not have been able to travel so far on her own.<ref>{{Cite|But that's so far away. Could she have made it there on her own? Oh, I'm so worried...|Amali|BotW}}</ref> Once she finishes speaking, the "{{Term|BotW|Find Kheel|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

At {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} upset that her sisters did not come to rehearse their song for the elder, {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Grrrrrr! My sisters are dumb DUMMIES!}} They promised we'd rehearse together, but NO ONE showed up! We have to perform our song for the {{Color|BotW Blue|elder}} at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}}!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> She is afraid that they will be laughed off stage if they perform as they are.<ref>{{Cite|We're going to be laughed off the stage...|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a "scary-looking" {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} asks him if he could return to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} to remind her sisters of their rehearsal.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you... You look like I can use you... Thaaaat is to say...you look like a nice guy who likes to help people in need. Can you go tell my sisters in Rito Village to stop ditching rehearsal? They won't listen to me, but they might listen to a scary-looking Hylian like you.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> She warns that it might be tough to convince her sister {{Term|BotW|Genli|link}}, as she is probably in the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)|link}} yelling for {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière|link}} or something similar.<ref>{{Cite|Although Genli might be a tough sell. She's probably in the {{Color|BotW Red|kitchen}} yelling, {{Big|"I want some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}!"}} ...or something like that.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> She complains that {{Term|BotW|Genli}} does not want to sing, which makes her a pain to deal with.<ref>{{Cite|She doesn't want to sing either... She's such a pain!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> When she finishes speaking, the "{{Term|BotW|Recital at Warbler's Nest|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has retrieved all of {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}'s sisters, she thanks him for his help and asks if he wants to know what song they are singing.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, thank you so much! All of my sisters are here! Now we can rehearse at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}}! Want to know what song we're singing?|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> According to {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}}, the song was performed at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}} in the ancient past.<ref>{{Cite|Well...the {{Color|BotW Blue|elder}} told me... It's a song passed down in the Rito Village that was once sung at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}} in ancient times.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} explains that {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}} is the big stone platform with the pillars behind them.<ref>{{Cite|If you didn't know, {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}} is the big stone platform with the pillars behind us.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics to the song do not make sense to her, but the line, "When wind convinces the rocks to sing, open the monk's door will swing," appeals to her.<ref>{{Cite|The lyrics don't make a lot of sense, but there's one line in particular that always catches my ear... "When wind convinces the rocks to sing, open the monk's door will swing."|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he thinks it means and wonders what a {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} is.<ref>{{Cite|What do you think it means? What's a monk?|Kheel|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not already have one, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf|link}} as a reward for helping her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! And I almost forgot! I got you a little something. Just a small thanks for bringing all of my sisters here.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} suggests swinging the {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf}} in the directioin of {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}, as doing so makes a fun noise.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ta-da! A {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Leaf}}!}} Why don't you swing it in the direction of {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}}? It makes a fun noise!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref>

The order of the notes in {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} and her sisters' song correspond to the rocks surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} manages to unearth the {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} turns and shouts for her sisters to look.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! Hey look, guys!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}'s sister {{Term|BotW|Notts|link}} says they should tell their mother about the phenomenon.<ref>{{Cite|Let's tell mom!|Notts|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} agrees and bids {{Term|BotW|Link}} goodbye.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, let's! See ya later!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> She counts her siblings off and they take to the sky.<ref>{{Cite|Ready, set...GO!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref>

Back in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} singing on the platform near {{Term|BotW|Akh Va'quot Shrine}} with her sisters.<ref>{{Cite|We're all going to go sing. You should come listen to us!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> She reveals that {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} was surprised when she told him about {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|When I told the {{Color|BotW Blue|village elder}} that this shrine popped out of the ground, he was so suprised!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> She remarks that {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} must also like playing hide-and-seek.<ref>{{Cite|I guess shrines like playing hide-and-seek too!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, he will join {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} and the rest of his daughters by playing his bandoneon as they sing.<ref>{{Cite|La la laaaa!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} is happy that "Poppy" is back and they are all going to sing together.<ref name="Poppy"/>

In Tears of the Kingdom, she runs the [[Brazen Beak]] clothing shop in Rito Village.
