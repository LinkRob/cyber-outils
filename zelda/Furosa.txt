{{Term/Store|Furosa||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Furosa|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Furosa}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} who manages {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}, she greets him and is surprised to see a "{{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} vai" in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Vasaaq... Oh, are you a Hylian vai? We don't see too many of you...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} comments that {{Term|BotW|Link}} seems too young for the special adult drinks that she mixes.<ref>{{Cite|I can't tell how old you are, but you look a little young to be coming to my shop. You see, we mix special drinks here. Drinks that are definitely just for adults...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the special drinks, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} explains that they make the drinks with the {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, a big part of the drink is the ice we use from the {{Color|BotW Blue|icehouse}}. It makes them especially refreshing.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks for drink recommendations, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} tells him about their featured cocktail, the {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Our featured cocktail is called {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}. Many of our customers come here specifically to order it.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She describes the taste of the {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit}} as a light, sweet and cool sensation and that it is like drinking "distilled motivation."<ref>{{Cite|The light, sweet taste... The cool sensation from each sip... It's like drinking distilled motivation!|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> When asked if they serve {{Term|BotW|Milk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} is appalled by the idea of serving something so childish.<ref>{{Cite|Milk?! We don't carry anything as childish as milk here!|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She then complains, thinking that {{Term|BotW|Greta|link}} sent {{Term|BotW|Link}} to say that as a prank and criticizes the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club|link}} for only drawing young people's attention.<ref>{{Cite|Did Greta tell you to come here and ask that? That store clerk is just playing a prank on you. Good grief, doesn't she have anything better to do with her time? She's always being so secretive about her shop. But hiding something just makes young people more interested...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} ends the conversation, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} comments that she has not seen {{Term|BotW|Pokki|link}} in a while,<ref>{{Cite|That reminds me... I haven't seen Pokki around lately...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> explaining that she used to come to {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}} every day to drink a {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit}}.<ref>{{Cite|She used to come here every day to drink a {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}, too.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} cuts herself off and apologizes after realizing that there is no reason to talk to {{Term|BotW|Link}} about this.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I'm sorry! I don't know why I'm even telling you this.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

During "{{Term|BotW|The Perfect Drink|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can try to order a {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit}} from {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}.<ref>{{Cite|You want to try our signature drink, {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> However, she needs a huge amount of ice to make the drink and she is all out.<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, I need a huge amount of ice to make that drink, and I'm fresh out right now.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> In addition, due to its strength, she refuses to sell it to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Besides, it's a really strong drink. Even if I could make it, I wouldn't sell it to a little vai like you.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} informs her that it is for {{Term|BotW|Pokki}},<ref>{{Cite|What'd you say? Pokki's collapsed?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> she laments that she can not carry the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} from the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's ice at the {{Color|BotW Red|icehouse}}, but my legs aren't in the best shape for hauling ice...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> 

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can offer to retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} on {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}'s behalf.<ref>{{Cite|[Should I get it for you?] Are you serious? You'll get it? That'd be a huge help.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} is her best customer, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} is grateful that {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants to help.<ref>{{Cite|Pokki is my best customer, after all. I wish I could get the ice myself.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if he explains the situation to {{Term|BotW|Anche|link}}, she will allow him to take the {{Term|BotW|Ice}},<ref>{{Cite|Anche manages it, but I think if you explain the situation, she'll let you have some ice.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> but he should get there while the sun is up since {{Term|BotW|Anche}} goes to bed early.<ref>{{Cite|You should know that she heads to bed quite early. If you plan to go, you'll need to get there while it's bright out.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Once she realizes the distance {{Term|BotW|Link}} would have to carry the {{Term|BotW|Ice}}, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} offers to take it from him at the entrance to the ruins between the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}} and {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, young lady... Carrying ice all the way through the desert and then all the way to town? I can't let that stand. If you bring the ice to the {{Color|BotW Red|entrance to the ruins}}—between town and the icehouse—I can take it from there.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

Upon arriving to the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets permission from {{Term|BotW|Anche}} to take some {{Term|BotW|Ice}} once she finds out it is for {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}.<ref name="Anche"/> She warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take the heat into account as he moves, as {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} has no use for water.<ref>{{Cite|You've taken the heat into account, right? If you move too slowly, the ice will surely melt. Furosa has no use for water...|Anche|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Carry the Ice!|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} will begin. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must now carry the block of {{Term|BotW|Ice}} to {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}, who is waiting for {{Term|BotW|Link}} past the ruins south of the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|display=Icehouse}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} realizes that he is a man but decides it does not matter.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? You're...<br/>Well, whatever... I don't care who you are, as long as I get my ice.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She confirms that he has brought her {{Term|BotW|Ice}} from the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}} and that it is in good shape.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa. No doubt about it, that's definitely ice {{Color|BotW Red|from the icehouse}}.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Looks like the ice is in good shape. Sarqso.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She tells him that she may be able to make the best {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit}} she has ever made with the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} he brought.<ref>{{Cite|I'll be able to make a {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}} with this. It may be the best I've ever made...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With this, I'll be able to make the ultimate {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to inform {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} that the "best drink ever" is waiting for her,<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, but could you please let {{Color|BotW Blue|Pokki}} know? Tell her that I made the best one ever and I've got it waiting for her...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I've got one more favor to ask. Could you go and let {{Color|BotW Blue|Pokki}} know? Just tell her the best drink ever is waiting for her...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> and she believes {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} will come running when she hears about it.<ref>{{Cite|Knowing her, she'll probably come running when she hears that there's an amazing drink with her name on it.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What? No, of course this will work. Knowing her, if she hears a delicious drink is waiting, she'll come running.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} has returned to {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}}, she and {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} can be found speaking to each other at the bar. {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} asks if there is any better than the taste of a {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit}}.<ref>{{Cite|I ask you, Furosa, is there anything better than a taste of {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}?|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} is shocked {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} ran all the way from {{Term|BotW|Misae Suma Shrine|link}} when she was at death's door and asks if she has given up investigating the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You were at death's door and you ran all the way here for that! Good grief... You're in incredible shape... Does that mean you don't plan to investigate that shrine anymore?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} calls the work dull,<ref>{{Cite|Definitely. I never dreamed that kind of work would be so dull...|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} reveals that she never thought it would be her thing.<ref>{{Cite|I'm not surprised you found it dull. That sort of thing isn't really "you."|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} declares that her thing is drinking.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, you know what really is "me"? Drinking.|Pokki|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Furosa}} is thankful that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was able to help {{Term|BotW|Pokki}}, claiming that while she heard people outside of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} are cold, {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not seem to be.<ref>{{Cite|Vasaaq, little Hylian vai. I've heard people beyond our walls were cold, but you don't seem to be...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back to {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}} when he is of age, at which point they will celebrate with a {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit}}.<ref>{{Cite|Be sure to come back when you're all grown up. I'll help you celebrate with a {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}!|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} talks to {{Term|BotW|Anche}} again, she will ask him to deliver more {{Term|BotW|Ice}} to {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} for her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Talk about great timing! Can you go deliver ice to Furosa again?|Anche|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Anche}} charges Link 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} for the ice and explains that {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} will reimburse him.<ref>{{Cite|Of course, before you carry the ice, I'll have to ask you to pay the {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}} for the ice in advance, though... Once you've delivered it, Furosa should be able to reimburse you for the cost of the ice.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} is timed during this delivery.

{{Term|BotW|Furosa}} will reward {{Term|BotW|Link}} depending on how quickly he delivered the {{Term|BotW|Ice}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes more than two minutes to reach {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}, she will call him a professional at delivering {{Term|BotW|Ice}}.<ref>{{Cite|You did it in {{Color|BotW Red|XX:XX.XX}}?<br/>You're like a professional ice deliverer.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She considers a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} an ample payment for his services.<ref>{{Cite|Including the price of the ice... I'm assuming this reward feels ample for services rendered, right?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} also remarks that managing the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}} seems to suit {{Term|BotW|Anche}} and makes her happy.<ref>{{Cite|Sounds like Anche is happy managing the icehouse. Seems to suit her.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} in under two minutes, she will be shocked with his time.<ref>{{Cite|You've got to be kidding me. Ice delivered in {{Color|BotW Red|XX:XX.XX}}?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She claims that he has gone beyond her expectations and calls him a savant,<ref>{{Cite|That is WAY beyond my expectations! You're like a savant at ice running.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> rewarding him with a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let me reimburse you for the price of the ice...and I'd better dig deep for something extra to reward a run like that!|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Furosa also states that Anche must be happy , since the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} seems to be happy as well.<ref>{{Cite|Anche must be happy...because the ice sure seems happy!|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

After giving {{Term|BotW|Link}} his reward, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is willing to make another delivery.<ref>{{Cite|If you could, I would love it if you'd make another run... Think you could find it in your heart to go again? You need lots of ice to serve good cocktails, you know?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Once again, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have to pay {{Term|BotW|Anche}} the 50 {{Term|BotW|Rupee}} fee.<ref>{{Cite|When you get the ice from Anche, make sure you pay {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}} for it.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} tells him that she will be waiting for his delivery.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you... I'll be waiting here, so please bring me that ice!|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, she tells him to talk to {{Term|BotW|Anche}} when he feels up to it.<ref>{{Cite|I see... Ah, well. If you feel like helping out, then talk to Anche...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>
