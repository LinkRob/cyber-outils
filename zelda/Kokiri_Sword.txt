{{Term/Store|Kokiri Sword|Kokiri Swords|Series|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=129 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}} }}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoT (Himekawa), SSBM, HWDE, SSBU, CoH}} In its two appearances in {{OoT}} and {{MM}}, it is the most basic [[Sword]] the hero, [[Link]], can obtain.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Link must obtain the Kokiri Sword alongside the [[Deku Shield]] before [[Mido]] allows him to visit the [[Great Deku Tree]]. The young hero can find the Kokiri Sword hidden away in a [[Treasure Chest]] inside the {{Term|OoT3D|Hole of "L"|link}}, which is located in the [[Forest Training Center]] in [[Kokiri Forest]]. This is the only Sword Link can wield as a child and is also the weakest. It is considered a treasure of the [[Kokiri]].<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Red|Kokiri Sword}}<br/>A short blade, suitable for a child. This sword is a treasure of the Kokiri.|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref>

As an adult, the [[Hero of Time]], it is replaced by the [[Master Sword]], which is longer and has twice the strength. Interestingly and rather infamously, the [[Giant's Knife]], which normally has the strength and range of the [[Biggoron's Sword]] (itself double that of the Master Sword), when broken becomes drastically weakened and has the equivalent performance of the Kokiri Sword (but still must be wielded with two hands). 

==={{MM|-}}=== 
In {{MM}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Kokiri Sword}} is the same weapon from the game's prequel (with an updated hilt), and is equipped by default at the beginning of the game.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Kokiri Sword}}<br/>The trusty sword you're familiar with. A treasure from Kokiri Forest.|N/A|MM3D}}</ref> Once again the weakest blade, it can be upgraded to the temporary {{Term|MM3D|Razor Sword|link}} and then again into the permanent {{Term|MM3D|Gilded Sword|link}} by taking it to the {{Term|MM3D|Mountain Smithy|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|Mountain Village|link}}. Although {{Term|MM3D|Link}} begins the game with the {{Term|MM3D|Kokiri Sword}}, he is unable to use it for a time when he is first transformed into a [[Deku Link|Deku Scrub]] by the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}}.

==Trivia==
*Despite being the same weapon in both {{OoT|-}} and {{MM|-}}, the hilt and guard of the {{Term|MM3D|Kokiri Sword}} changes designs between the two [[Nintendo 64]] games.
*This Sword is the signature weapon of the legendary [[Hero of Time]] at his young age in spite of being the first and least powerful blade in both of his main adventures in [[Hyrule]] and [[Termina]].
*The [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] uses the {{OoT|-}} design while ''Hyrule Warriors'' uses the one from its sequel.
*When the [[Razor Sword]] breaks after 100 strikes, the blade becomes dull and will simply revert back to the Kokiri Sword. It also returns to its former shape when Link uses the "[[Song of Time]]" to go back to the [[First Day]] after obtaining this weapon. This does not occur with the more powerful and longer [[Gilded Sword|Glided Sword]].[[File:MM beta Kokiri Sword.png|thumb|228x228px|The beta Kokiri Sword in {{MM|-}}]]
*In the data files of {{MM|-}}, an early version of the Kokiri Sword exists of the blade entirely in wooden textures, resembling the starting Sword from {{TLoZ}}. This is actually a leftover from early development of {{OoT|-}}.
**Interestingly, the [[Master Sword]] in the beta version of the latter game has the Blade of Evil's Bane itself resembling a larger version of the Kokiri Sword and its [[scabbard]] is also fully wooden.
