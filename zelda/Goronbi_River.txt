{{Term/Store|Goronbi River||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Goronbi River|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Goronbi River is a long river of lava in western [[Eldin Canyon]]. It is an extension of the [[Golow River]] north, which itself flows from [[Darunia Lake]]. The Goronbi River flows slightly southeast and eventually ends at [[Goronbi Lake]]. The River is bordered by a mountain range on its western side. On the eastern side is the main road paving the way to [[Goron City]].

A fort camp guarded by monsters sits on a ledge overlooking the Goronbi River at its half point, north of the Southern Mine. The camp is watched by monsters who guard three [[Treasure Chest]]s containing a [[Cobble Crusher]], a [[Knight's Shield]], and a [[Topaz]].

Close to the River's half point is a giant rib cage partially submerged in lava. A [[Korok]] is hidden at the southern point of the rib cage, and will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] for finding it.
