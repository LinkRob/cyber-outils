{{Term/Store|Zora Scale|Zora Scales|Series, OoA, PH, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 143"/>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Zora Scale}} was referred to as the '''Zora Scale Tablet''' in {{Guide|OoA|BradyGames}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA |publisher= BradyGames |page= 95{{Exp Game|OoA}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} They are fragments of the skin of a [[Zora]] which seem to hold the aquatic identity of the species, as a non-Zora in the possession of one gets adapted to water. 

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
====Silver Scale====
{{Main|Silver Scale}}
[[File:OoT3D Silver Scale Icon.png|left]]
In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Silver Scale|link}} enhances {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}}'s {{Term|OoT3D|Diving|link}} capabilities to up to 6 meters.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Silver Scale! Jump into the water and press (A). You can dive deeper than you could before.|N/A|OoT}}</ref> It is required to enter {{Term|OoT3D|Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly|link}}.

====Golden Scale====
{{Main|Golden Scale}}
[[File:OoT3D Golden Scale Icon.png|left]]
The '''Golden Scale''' is an enhanced version of the Silver Scale.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Golden Scale! Jump into the water and press (A). Now you can dive much deeper than you could before!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> The time and depth of a dive that can be achieved with the Golden Scale is up to nine meters. Unlike the Silver Scale, obtaining the Golden Scale is optional, although it is necessary to obtain a [[Piece of Heart]] in the [[Lakeside Laboratory]]. To obtain the Golden Scale, adult Link must catch a [[fish]] that breaks the current record by at least four pounds at the [[Fishing Pond]] in [[Lake Hylia]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, the Zora Scale is given to Link by a Zora after he defeats [[Plasmarine]] and gets the [[Rolling Sea]] essence from [[Jabu-Jabu's Belly]].<ref>{{Cite|You have done so much for us! Our seas are clean and all is well! I offer you our symbol of heroism--the Zora Scale!|Zora|OoA}}</ref> The Zora Scale is required to bypass the Zora guarding the way to the [[Sea of Storms]].<ref>{{Cite|The seas beyond here swallow up all who venture into them--the Sea of Storms! I can't allow a child like you to pass! The Zora Scale? So you're Link! You may pass!|Zora|OoA}}</ref> On the Sea of Storms, Link will find the stranded [[Piratian]]s. The Zora Scale, which is a charm capable of calming the seas,<ref>{{Cite|Har! Ye've got the Zora's Scale sea charm! Aye, that could calm this Sea o' Storms!|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref> must be given to the [[Cap'n]] of the Piratians, so that they can escape the Sea of Storms.<ref>{{Cite|Thank'ee! Now we can escape these seas!|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref> As thanks, Link receives the [[Tokay Eyeball]], which is required to access the [[Ancient Tomb]].<ref>{{Cite|Take this as a sign o' me thanks! It's the jewel called the Tokay Eyeball! It's said to be the thing that opens the way to the ancient tomb.|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref>

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, Zora Scales are [[Treasure]]s that can be sold on [[Mercay Island]] to the [[Teller of Treasures]] for a random amount of [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|It's said that a Zora dropped this rare and sparkling scale!|N/A|PH}}</ref> It is also a highly valued commodity among members of the [[Ho Ho Tribe]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, {{Plural|TFH|Zora Scale}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Secret Fortress|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Zora Scale}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Secret Fortress}}''', ''"Clear with halved energy!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Zora Scale}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Abyss of Agony|link}}''', ''"Halved attack and defense!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Zora Scale}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Cove of Transition|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Zora Scale}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 250 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Zora Scale}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Zora Costume|link}}.<ref name="E 143"/>

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Zora Scale|A charm made via secret Zora methods. Enables the wearer to hold their breath for longer underwater.<ref name="Zora Scale">{{Cite|A charm made via secret Zora methods. Enables the wearer to hold their breath for longer underwater.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Zora Scale is rewarded after completing [[The Zora Child's Fate]] [[Side Quest]]. Equipping them increases Princess Zelda’s diving time.

The Zora Scale is a class of Weapon used by [[Princess Ruto|Ruto]] in {{HW}}. It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Water}}.

====Variations====
The default Zora Scale is the Silver Scale. The second form of the Zora Scale is the Golden Scale, followed by the '''Water Dragon Scale'''.

The Lv.★ form of the Zora Scale is the '''8-Bit Clock''', which is based on the [[Magical Clock]] from {{TLoZ}}. It was added to {{HW|-}} with the {{MM|-}} Pack DLC. In {{HW|DE|-}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Clock replaces the Sun Dragon Scale when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.


In {{HW|L}}, the fourth form of the Zora Scale is the '''Sun Dragon Scale''', which is associated with both the Elements of Water and {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Light}}.

=====Special Attack=====
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Ruto winks to the screen, then forms a pool of water on the ground around her, which quickly turns into a whirlpool as she swims around in it. After a few seconds, the whirlpool shifts into a watery twister, before exploding and throwing enemies outwards. This attack damages enemies in a circular range around Ruto.
