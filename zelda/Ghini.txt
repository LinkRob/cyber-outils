==Characteristics==
Ghinis are one-eyed spirits that are normally found in [[Graveyard]]s, often depicted with their tongues lolling out of their mouths. In nearly all of their appearances, Ghinis fly in sporadic or circular patterns. Ghinis are known to attack in groups, with a seemingly collective life force. In these instances, when the first Ghini is slain, the entire group will be destroyed in the same instant. Later games do not include this behavior, and most Ghinis simply attack alone or in smaller groups. While they can be dispatched with a variety of weapons, Ghinis often require many [[Sword]] swings to defeat.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Ghinis have about the same attacking force as Armos.|game= TLoZ|page= 30}}</ref>

The [;Giant Ghini]] is a notable variation of this enemy, a much larger Ghini that occasionally acts as a [[Sub-Boss]].

==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Ghini|The ghost who lives in the graveyard. There are two types. There are Ghinis who are there from the beginning of the game, and those who appear when Link touches the gravestones. Ghinis have about the same attacking force as Armos.}}
Ghinis inhabit the western [[Graveyard]]s on the [[Overworld]] of [[Hyrule]] in {{TLoZ}}. One Ghini appears haunting each set of tombstones; however, more Ghinis will spawn if [[Link]] disturbs any of the graves by touching them. More Ghinis can appear from the same tombstone if Link continuously touches it. Ghinis that appear from tombstones cannot be harmed by any means, attacks going through them with no effect. Instead, Ghinis all share what appears to be a collective life force, as all Ghinis on the screen will be destroyed if Link slays the first that appears in that area. They do, however, take several hits with the [[Sword]] to defeat. Even after the first is slain, more Ghinis can still appear from tombstones and remain invincible. When both types of Ghinis are on the screen at once, they can be differentiated from each other by their movements; the first type floats slowly and around the graves, whereas the others fly more sporadically. When defeated, Ghinis may drop either a [[Heart]], one [[Rupee]], five Rupees or a [[Clock]].{{Note|If [[Link]] defeats 10 enemies without getting hit or teleporting with the [[Recorder]], then the 10th enemy defeated will drop five Rupees. However, if the 10th enemy is defeated with a [[Bomb]], then it will drop four Bombs. If Link defeats 16 enemies without getting hit or teleporting, then the 16th enemy will drop a [[Fairy|Fairie]]. After the 16th enemy, {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} must get hit and reset the counter to zero in order to achieve this effect again. Defeating [[Armos]]s, [[Like Like]]s, [[Keese]]s, or [[Gel]]s will not be counted.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= n.a. |published= n.d. |retrieved= August 17, 2017 |url= http://redcandle.us/The_Legend_of_Zelda#Forced_drops |title= Forced Drops |site= redcandle.us}}</ref>}}

==={{LA|-}}===
Ghinis appear in the [[Cemetery]] east of the [[Koholint Prairie]] and north of [[Ukuku Prairie]] in {{LA}}. Like those in {{TLoZ}}, one Ghini appears haunting each set of tombstones. Only certain graves can summon Ghinis by touching them, and only one can be summoned to that specific grave. As in {{TLoZ|-}}, slaying the first Ghini will destroy all other Ghinis on the screen. Though the other Ghinis are no longer invincible and can be attacked, defeating them will not destroy the others.

A [[Giant Ghini]] also appears by disturbing one of the Cemetery's tombstones. This larger Ghini takes many more hits to defeat, but like the other Ghinis that spawn from graves, it can be instantly destroyed by defeating the first Ghini in the area.

==={{OoS|-}}===
Ghinis appear similarly to those of {{TLoZ}} and {{LA}} in {{OoS}}. They inhabit the [[Graveyard]] in western [[Holodrum]] close to the [[Western Coast]], and like Ghinis of previous games, a lone Ghini can be found haunting a set of graves, with more appearing when their tombstones are disturbed. Any grave can spawn a Ghini, but only one per grave. All Ghinis that appear can be fought, and destroying the first one of that area will also destroy any on the screen. Despite the Ghinis characteristic resilience, they can be defeated with a single hit of the Rod of Seasons]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
Ghinis vary slightly from their {{OoS}} appearance in {{OoA}}, as they no longer inhabit [[Cemetery]]s on the [[Overworld]], but rather appear inside [[Dungeon]]s. A lone Ghini appears in a single room in the [[Spirit's Grave]], and must be defeated to open a door. Groups of Ghinis appear in several rooms in the [[Ancient Tomb]]. These Ghinis do not appear to share their life force, as defeating any one will not affect the others.

A [[Giant Ghini]] also appears as the [[Sub-Boss]] of the Spirit's Grave. This Ghini bears a distinctive appearance, without a single eye or a lolling tongue, and is surrounded by smaller Ghinis with a similar look.

Friendly Ghinis also play a role in a [[Linked Game]]. Two Ghinis are involved with the [[Linked Game/Secrets#Graveyard Secret|Graveyard Secret]], and a Ghini living in the abandoned house on the Western Coast is involved with the [[Linked Game/Secrets#Library Secret|Library Secret]]. These Ghinis only appear in a Linked Game.

==={{FS|-}}===
Ghinis appear as bright blue ghosts in [[Vaati's Palace]] in {{FS}}. They also appear in {{TLoZ}}-themed stage of the [[Realm of Memories]] and the [[Hero's Trial]] in {{FSAE}}. Ghinis behave differently from previous appearances, appearing in smaller groups and grabbing onto the [[Links]] to drain their health. These Ghinis are not connected to one another, and defeating one will not defeat any others.

==={{FSA|-}}===
Ghinis appear in small groups in {{FSA}}, typically congregating in dark areas. They move in swaying motions and emit blue light as they fly, slightly illuminating the area around them. Ghinis in {{FSA|-}} do not emerge from tombstones, nor do they share a collective life force between each other. They are also much less resilient than previous appearances, and now only take one [[Sword]] strike to defeat. Ghinis will periodically turn invisible, leaving only their shadow behind. They can be found in the cemetery of [[The Swamp]], in the dark castle basement in the [[Infiltration of Hyrule Castle]], and during the blizzard portion of [[Frozen Hyrule (Stage)|Frozen Hyrule]]. Ghinis in The Swamp are susceptible to [[Light]] and can be defeated by lighting certain Torches, while Ghinis in other stages are unaffected by torchlight. Some Ghinis also hide inside [[Jar]]s in the basement of [[Hyrule Castle]]. Ghinis are also summoned by the [[Big Poe (Boss)|Big Poe]] during both of its [[Boss]] battles.

Giant Ghinis also appear in The Swamp. These large ghosts can only be defeated by lighting nearby Torches activated by Switches.

==={{TMC|-}}===
[[File:TMC Ghini Sprite 2.png|50px|right]]
Ghinis in {{TMC}} possess an entirely different set of behaviors from those of previous games. They can be found in the region surrounding the [[Royal Valley]] and within [[Dark Hyrule Castle]], but no longer emerge from graves and appear individually. Ghinis are first seen sleeping, but awaken and pursue [[Link]] once he approaches. They exhibit behaviors similar to Ghinis from {{FS|-}}, as well as [[ReDead]]s of other games; they will constrict Link's body using their tail once they catch him, and lick the life away for as long as they can secure him in their grasp. Link must struggle free to prevent his life from being further drained.

There is also a friendly pink Ghini named [[Gina]], who appears in a grave in the Royal Valley. This Ghini offers to [[Kinstone Fusion|Fuse]] a number of [[Kinstone Piece]]s with Link.

{{Figurine|TMC|Ghini}}

==={{ALBW|-}}===
Ghinis appear in "Light" and "Dark" versions in {{ALBW}}; Light Ghinis resemble the ordinary white-colored Ghinis, whereas [[Dark Ghini]]s are black-colored and have a red eye.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALBW|publisher= Prima|page= 37}}</ref> Both types appear in the pitch-dark corridors of the [[Dark Palace]]. Light Ghinis hide themselves in the darkness of the [[Dungeon]], but are easily seen when the area is lit, either by lighting Torches or using the [[Fire Rod]] or [[Lantern]]. Conversely, Dark Ghinis are entirely visible in darkness but turn invisible when exposed to light. Both Ghinis move in small circular patterns and usually appear alone or in very small groups. They can be attacked regardless if they are visible or not. Both Ghinis also appear in the [[Treacherous Tower]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
Both "Light" and "Dark" Ghini types from {{ALBW}} appear in {{TFH}}, albeit with slightly different behaviors. [[Dark Ghini]] appear in the [[Palace Noir]], inhabiting the dark chamber of the second stage, while Light Ghinis appear in the [[Lone Labyrinth]] and the [[Sky Temple]]. While similar to those from {{ALBW|-}}, neither type is affected by light. Dark Ghinis are able to better conceal themselves in darkness, and remain visible even when exposed to light. Light Ghinis continue to hide in darkness, but only on [[The Ruins]]-themed platform in the Sky Temple.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Ghini|Eternally lonely, this one-eyed ghost has an unfortunate tendency to lick potential friends.<ref name="EoW Ghini">{{Cite|Eternally lonely, this one-eyed ghost has an unfortunate tendency to lick potential friends.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Ghini are one-eyed ghosts primarily encountered within the [[Stilled Northern Sanctuary]]. A stronger variant known as [[Ghini Lv. 2]] also appears. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Ghini [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of one.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
===Philips CD-i Games===
[[File:TFoE Ghini Sprite.png|right]]
In {{TFoE}} and {{TWoG}}, Ghinis resemble ghostly old men that fly through the air. Like other airborne enemies, they repeatedly spawn from certain areas and fly directly at either [[Link]] or [[Princess Zelda]]. They are unable to change direction mid-flight. They appear in white and green-colored varieties, although very little differs between them. Ghinis typically inhabit haunted or crypt-like locations, such as the [[Goronu (Stage)|Goronu]], [[Glutko]] and [[Lupay (Stage)|Lupay]] regions of [[Koridai]], and [[Sakado]]'s crypt and the [[Gobiyan Ship]] of [[Gamelon]]. 
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In the prototype release of {{TLoZ}}, Ghinis were originally depicted with two pairs of legs and an angrier glare. Artwork from [[The Legend of Zelda (Million Publishing)|Million Publishing's game guide]] depicts the Ghini identically to this sprite.<ref>[https://tcrf.net/Proto:The_Legend_of_Zelda#Unused Proto:The Legend of Zelda - The Cutting Room Floor]</ref>
*Although nearly always depicted with a single eye, Ghinis have two eyes in their official artwork from {{TLoZ|-}}, and in their sprites in {{BSTLoZ}}.
*The [[Graveyard]] found in the [[Death Mountain|Death Mountain Area]], which was home to [[Ghini]] in {{TLoZ}}, is instead home to red Moa by the time of {{TAoL|-}}.
