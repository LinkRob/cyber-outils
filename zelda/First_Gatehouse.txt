The {{Term/Store|First Gatehouse||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''First Gatehouse'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|First Gatehouse|link}} is a small tower along the pathway to the {{Term|BotW|Sanctuary|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. Its entrance is flanked by two {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Turret|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|First Gatehouse}}, a {{Term|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} will drop from the ceiling and the entrances will be blocked, trapping him inside.

Defeating the {{Term|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel}} will cause the portcullises to raise. A metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} will then appear, containing 10 {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}. A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found on the upper level of the First Gatehouse, and it holds five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
If {{Term|BotW|Link}} climbs all the way up the spire on top of the {{Term|BotW|First Gatehouse}}, he will find {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}}. When he interacts with them, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
