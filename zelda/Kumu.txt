{{Term/Store|Kumu||Series, PH}} is a character from {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|PH|Kumu}} is an [[Anouki]] who lives in [[Anouki Estates]] on the [[Isle of Frost]] and is one of the Anouki [[Link]] has to question to find a [[Yook]] living in their midst. He tells Link that either [[Aroo]] or [[Mazo]] is lying about being a Yook in disguise.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Kumu. See, either Mazo or Aroo is lying to you... And that's the truth!|Kumu|PH}}</ref>

The only other Anouki to mention Kumu during Link's questioning is the [[Aroo|Yook in disguise]], who accuses Kumu of lying.<ref>{{Cite|My name's Aroo. Between you and me, Kumu is lying. And I'll leave it at that.|Yook|PH}}</ref>
