{{Term/Store|Hot Buttered Apple|Hot Buttered Apples|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Hot Buttered Apples are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} an {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}} and [[Goat Butter]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they'll heal several of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The sweetness of the Apple has been enhanced by smothering it with Goat Butter and baking it.<ref name="Inventory"/>

For her second meal, [[Koko]] decides to make Hot Buttered Apples, since her mother used to make them and her sister [[Cottla]] loves them.<ref>{{Cite|What should Koko make now... {{Big|OH!}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Hot buttered apple}} sounds good! Mother used to make it all the time! Cottla loves it too! Let's see... The ingredients are... some {{Color|BotW Blue|apples}} and...{{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}?|Koko|BotW}}</ref> However, she realizes that she's run out of Goat Butter, and therefore can't make the meal.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|OH, NO!!}} Koko is out of {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> When [[Link]] brings her Goat Butter, Koko will be excited, thanking him and making sure he means it.<ref>{{Cite|Is this... {{Big|goat butter?!}} Are you sure about this? Really? Thank you so much! Koko means it! Stay right there for a second!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> She then makes a Hot Buttered Apple, which she gives to Link as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Mmm! What a yummy smell! And that hint of butter makes all the difference! Oh! Oh! And the apples are superbly sweet too! Here, take some. It's really something else.|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

Additional ingredients can be added when making Hot Buttered Apples, which can result in one of the following variations:

* '''Hearty Hot Buttered Apple''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating them will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Hot Buttered Apple''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. If eaten, they'll replenish a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Hot Buttered Apple''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. They completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Hot Buttered Apple''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. When eaten, they temporarily increase Link's speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Hot Buttered Apple''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. Eating these apples will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Hot Buttered Apple''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Hot Buttered Apple''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Link gains a boost to the power of his attacks when he eats these apples.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Hot Buttered Apple''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's defense for a short period of time.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Hot Buttered Apple''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When eaten, they grant Link a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} temporarily.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Hot Buttered Apple''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Hot Buttered Apple}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The apple's sweetness has been enhanced by smothering it with butter and baking it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding cold ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
