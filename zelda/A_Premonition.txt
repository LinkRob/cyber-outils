"{{Term/Store|A Premonition||Series, BotW}}" is the eight {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Premonition}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #8}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|A Premonition}}" is unlocked along the path northeast of {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}, leading to {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}. It opens up on the bodies of {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}}, panning up to show {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} sitting together. {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} is tending to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s wounds, telling him that while his cut does not look too bad, he should be more careful.<ref>{{Cite|That cut doesn't look too bad, actually. You're fine for now. But you know, there's a fine line between courage and recklessness.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She reminds him that, though he is brave, he is still mortal.<ref>{{Cite|As brave as you are, that does not make you immortal.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

With {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s safety assured, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} notes that not only have [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monster]] attacks been increasing, so have the scale of enemies they have faced.<ref>{{Cite|It seems that, not only is the frequency of these types of attacks on the rise... but the scale of beasts we are facing is intensifying as well.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She expresses to {{Term|BotW|Link}} her fear that this could be a sign of the return of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I fear that— I fear that this is an omen which portends the return of Calamity Ganon.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> With this, she stands and brushes herself off, telling {{Term|BotW|Link}} she is ready to expect the worst and that they need to make preparations as soon as possible.<ref>{{Cite|And, if that's the case, I'm ready to expect the worst. We'll need to make preparations as soon as possible.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} stands to join her, and the two head down the slope together.
