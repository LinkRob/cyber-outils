"{{Term/Store|The Three Giant Brothers||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Three Giant Brothers}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Mount Taran}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
On top of {{Term|BotW|Mount Taran|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|link}} surrounded by three {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}. Inscribed upon the {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|display=Tablet}} is a message, which states that the three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} that are guarded by "giants" will lead to an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|ancient orbs}} guarded by the giants of Mount Taran lead to the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> Reading the {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet}} will begin the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

The giants referred to on the {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet}} are three {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} tied around their necks that are sleeping in different places on {{Term|BotW|Mount Taran}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orbs}} by either sneaking past or defeating the {{Plural|BotW|Hinox}}. The {{Term|BotW|Hinox (Youngest Kin)|link}} is a regular {{Term|BotW|Hinox}} that rests on an island in {{Term|BotW|Hanu Pond|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Hinox (Middle Kin)|link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} that sleeps in {{Term|BotW|Uten Marsh|link}}. Finally, the {{Term|BotW|Hinox (Oldest Kin)|link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Black Hinox|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Rabella Wetlands|link}}. This {{Term|BotW|Hinox}} is surrounded by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}}, which will attack {{Term|BotW|Link}} if they notice him.

Each {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} must be placed in the {{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot}} of one of the {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal}} at the top of {{Term|BotW|Mount Taran}}. After all three {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal}} have been activated, {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn Shrine|link}} will rise from the ground between them.<ref>{{Cite|Offering these orbs to the matching pedestals caused an ancient shrine to appear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
