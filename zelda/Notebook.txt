The notebook provides descriptions of [[Echo|echoes]] in {{EoW}}.
==Overview==
The Notebook serves as a log of the Echoes acquired by [[Princess Zelda]]. After completing ‘[[The Prime Energy and Null]]’ [[quest]], all unacquired Echoes are also revealed in the Notebook. She can then revisit the [[The Great Deku Tree|Deku Tree]] to inquire about Echoes she has yet to find.
