==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Mountain Crow}}
Mountain Crows are a species of [[Bird]] noted for their intelligence and claws.<ref>{{Cite|This bird is known for its cleverness and for its distinct claws.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Despite their name, they can also be found in forests and villagers.<ref>{{Cite|They make their homes in mountains, forests, and villages alike.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They're often hated, due to their feathers being seen as ill omens and the fact that they steal crops from farms.<ref>{{Cite|Not only are their jet-black feathers seen as an ill omen, but they'll steal the crops right out of a farmer's hands if given the chance. Because of this, they're often met with enmity.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> If Mountain Crows are killed, they may drop a [[Raw Bird Drumstick]].
