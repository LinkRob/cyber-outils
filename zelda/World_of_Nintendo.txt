'''World of Nintendo''' is a line of figures and plushes produced and distributed by Jakks Pacific. It primarily features characters from the ''[[mariowiki:Mario (franchise)|Super Mario]]'' series, but also covers other Nintendo properties such as ''[[Metroidwiki:Metroid (series)|Metroid]]'', ''[[mariowiki:donkey kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'', and ''[[The Legend of Zelda (Series)|The Legend of Zelda]]''.
