==Biography==
The {{Term|MM|Ikana Royal Family}} once governed the [[Ikana]] region from the [[Ancient Castle of Ikana]]. The King of Ikana and head of the Ikana monarchy, [[Igos du Ikana]], is the only known member of that family. If he had a spouse and children, they undoubtedly made up the other members of that monarchy. In addition, the family probably included the parents, siblings, or extended family of Igos du Ikana. As [[Sakon]] says that there are "swarms" of ghosts appearing in Ikana that belong to the Royal Family, the family may have been very large.<ref name="Ghosts"/>

The servants of the Ikana Royal Family felt such devotion towards it that they continued to serve the Kingdom even after their death. Notable servants of the Royal Family include the [[Composer Brothers]], Sharp and Flat, who composed the songs connected to that Royal Family,<ref name="Flat"/> the Royal Soldier [[Captain Keeta|Skull Keeta]], who protected the [[Ikana Graveyard]] and the Royal Family's treasure,<ref name="Royal Soldier">{{Cite|Did you see the huge skeleton at the back of this graveyard? My father once said that was all that was left of the former Royal Soldier who once guarded this graveyard. Even though he is dead, his remains still block the way. In a sense, he is still guarding the Royal Family's treasure. He must have been a fine soldier, indeed.|Dampé|MM}}</ref> and the King's [[Igos du Ikana|body guards]] and [[Stalchild|armies]].<ref>{{Cite|It's a Stalchild. They were the cursed soldiers of the Kingdom of Ikana...What are you gonna do?|Tatl|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And convey my words to my men, who, even in death, remain loyal to me. Tell them the war has ended...|Captain Keeta|MM}}</ref> Apparently, Sharp was even willing to sell his soul to the devil in an attempt to revive the Royal Family.<ref>{{Cite|Forgive your foolish brother who dreamt of the revival of the Royal Family...|Sharp|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh...Sharp, my dear brother. He sold his soul to the devil and was the one who locked me in here...|Flat|MM}}</ref>

The Ikana Graveyard was said to be used exclusively for the burial of members of the Royal Family, which would explain the elaborateness of the tombs.<ref>{{Cite|All the graves here belong to the family members of the King of Ikana Castle, a place that lies beyond the mountains. Even nowadays, the ghosts come out at night. It is a frightening graveyard.|Dampé|MM}}</ref> As Igos du Ikana is a skilled swordsman, and inscriptions of two of the graves indicate that a soldier rests within, Ikana Royal Family members may have also served in Ikana's army.<ref>{{Cite|The soul of a brave soldier from Ikana rests here.|Tombstone|MM}}</ref> This graveyard was also the resting place of the Royal Family's legendary treasures, perhaps being buried with them for the afterlife as practiced by the {{wp|Ancient Egyptian burial customs|Ancient Egyptians}}.<ref>{{Cite|I came looking for the Royal Family's legendary treasure, but my torch has gone out.|Dampé|MM}}</ref><ref name="Royal Soldier"/>

When [[Link]] arrives in [[Termina]], long after the fall of Ikana Kingdom, he can interact with the King, his servants, and acquire some of the Royal Family's treasures. After overthrowing Igos du Ikana, Link learns the [[Elegy of Emptiness]] from him and uses the song to scale [[Stone Tower]]. He also acquires a [[Piece of Heart]], the [[Song of Storms]], and an [[Empty Bottle]] from the graves in Ikana Graveyard.
