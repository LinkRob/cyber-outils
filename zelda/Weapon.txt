==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}. {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} are {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s main way of defeating {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} by dealing damage to them directly. {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} have a set amount of durability and will break when this value reaches zero, dealing twice as much damage as they do so. Many of these follow a simple series of archetypes that often coincide with the names of {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}}. For instance, {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Claymore|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Spear|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}} compose the {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear|link}} archetype, alongside {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Bow|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Shield|link}}. Each archetype possesses its own traits, such as boasting high offense at the cost of {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}} as is the case with the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Gear|link}}, or possessing special properties that react to {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}, such as {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} which can reflect lasers from {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} or easily pierce through their defenses.

Separately from these archetypes, {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} also follow behavioral patterns. There are several main types of weapons, with very few exceptions leaving these groups: 
*One-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Sword|link}} or simple clubs, which have a medium attack rate and damage stat, can be charged at the cost of {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} to release a spinning charge attack and can be used in tandem with {{Plural|BotW|Shield}}. One-handed weapons also include two subtypes:
**{{Plural|BotW|Boomerang|link}}, which will fly in a straight line and then return to {{Term|BotW|Link}} after flying a set distance whenever they're thrown, dealing extra damage. They otherwise perform as regular one-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pick up the {{Term|BotW|Boomerang}} on its way back, but if he fails to catch it or if it crashes into something, the {{Term|BotW|Boomerang}} will fall to the floor normally.
**Rods, which do very little melee damage but are instead mostly used at range. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} performs an attack or throw with them, an elemental effect will happen in a straight line. They come in three varieties, fire, ice and electric. The {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf|link}} has very similar behavior to {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} in this category and uses wind, but is strangely considered a two-handed {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} instead. Charge attacks become a bigger elemental blast instead of a spin.
*Spears, which boast very high attack speeds and low damage but are mostly notable for their good range, and do a flurry of rapid stabs when {{Term|BotW|Link}} performs a charge attack.

Apart from a weapon's type, bladed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} can cut through {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} while spears and blunt {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} will not. Heavy two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} can disrupt an opponent's balance if they brandish a {{Term|BotW|Shield}}, allowing for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to knock their {{Term|BotW|Shield}} out of their hand and bypass their defenses.

Apart from their melee uses, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can throw his {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} at his enemies. When thrown, if a {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} makes direct contact with an enemy, it will immediately break, guaranteeing that it will deal double damage due to a critical hit. Some {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} types and exceptions do not follow these rules.

Additionally, all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} are made of different materials such as wood, bone, stone, metal, and {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}. Wood can be burned when exposed to open flames and allows for the user to light it on fire and use it. Wood also burns in {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Temperature|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} made of wood, bone, or {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}} will float in water. {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} made from stone or metal will sink in water and the latter can be manipulated with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} and will conduct electricity, drawing {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is electrocuted while wielding any {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} except for the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} or the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}}, it will fly from his hands and land on the ground nearby.

Initially, Link can carry up to eight {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} at a time in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}; however, he can obtain additional slots by giving {{Plural|BotW|Korok Seed|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Hestu|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Hestu}} will upgrade {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s Weapon Stash up to 11 times.<ref>{{Cite|Shakalo... I can expand your {{Color|BotW Blue|weapon stash}} no further.|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s Weapon Stash will also be upgraded once upon pulling the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}} from its {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|link|display=Pedestal}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to carry a maximum of 20 {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes another {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} while his {{Term|BotW|Inventory}} is full, he will drop the {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} or put it back if it came from a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. Although he does not actually take the {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}, it will still be identified if it was the first time {{Term|BotW|Link}} had taken that {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}. Each {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}, including duplicates, occupies its own slot in the {{Term|BotW|Inventory}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} can also randomly have modifiers when obtained from {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} or dropped by enemies, with descriptors such as "Durability Up" and "Long Throw" which denote effects such as increased durability or throwing distance. These {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} exhibit a shimmering sound and have a golden sparkle when laying on the ground.

In {{HW|-}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} are used by all {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} to defeat {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy|link}}. {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} can be found in {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon Bag|link}} dropped by {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}} while in-battle, or in {{Plural|HWDE|Treasure Chest|link}}. A {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} can find new {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} to use in {{Plural|HWDE|Treasure Chest}} after capturing {{Plural|HWDE|Keep|link}}. Upon finding a new {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}, it will be known as a {{Term|HWDE|Sealed Weapon|link}}, which denotes that the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} has never found a {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} of that kind before.<ref>{{Cite|You got a sealed weapon!<br/>It might be a completely new weapon,<br/>but you'll have to check later!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> If a {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} has already obtained that kind of {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Sealed Weapon}} will appear as a normal {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}. Once a {{Term|HWDE|Weapon Bag}} is dropped by an {{Term|HWDE|Enemy}}, it can be picked up by {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} and used in later battles or sold for {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Smithy (Hyrule Warriors)|link}}. {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} that {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} have picked up will only be kept if the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} wins the battle.<ref>{{Cite|If you pick up a weapon midbattle, you will be able to use that weapon once you have claimed victory in that battle.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

===={{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} Information====
Each {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} has a certain tier that can determine the range of Attack Strength, skill limit, and element the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} holds. Most {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} and their higher tiers can be unlocked in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}, mainly after obtaining A Rank in certain battles.<ref name="Prima">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 8}}</ref> The higher the tier of {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}, the higher base attack strength the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} will have, the higher the amount of {{Plural|HWDE|Skill|link}} the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} can hold, and the higher amount of {{Plural|HWDE|Element|link}} the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} attains.<ref name="Prima"/>

{{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} will also have a Star Rating associated with them. This star rating can range from 1 to 5 stars, with 5 being the rarest and 1 being the most common. The star rating determines how high or low the Attack Strength of the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} is boosted.<ref name="Prima"/> Each star rating will boost the {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}}'s base Attack Power by 10 percent.<ref name="Prima"/> The tier determines the general range of Attack Strength, while the star rating determines the exact value.<ref name="Attack Strength">{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Attack Strength}}:<br/>The higher the value, the more damage you deal to enemies. The value is decided based on {{Color|HWDE Green|basic weapon strength}} and the weapon's {{Color|HWDE Green|star rating}}.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> The chance of obtaining higher star rated {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} can be raised by both using a {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} with the skill "Stars+" or by making a "Weapon Star 1", "Weapon Star 2", or "Weapon Star 3" {{Term|HWDE|Potion|link}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Apothecary|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mixture Effect: Weapon Star 1<br/>Weak boost to dropped-weapon stars.|Apothecary|HWDE}}</ref><ref name="Prima"/>

All {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} will have an Attack Strength based on the weapons star rating and tier. This value will never change, but can be added on to by adding {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} and leveling up the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Training Dojo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Leveling up {{Color|HWDE Green|increases a warrior's basic attack strength and maximum health}}.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> The higher the value of the attack strength, the more damage the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} will be able to inflict.<ref name="Attack Strength"></ref>

Each {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} a {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} obtains can hold from 1 to 6 {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} to help the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} in battle. {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} can boost the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} with Attack Strength, increase {{Term|HWDE|Enemy}} Drops, increase [[Items in Hyrule Warriors|Item]] power-up duration, and more. Some {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} on {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} are sealed, or locked, and can only be unlocked after defeating a certain number of {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}} with said {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Skill}}:<br/>Skills attached to weapons give them various added effects. If a skill is sealed, you must defeat a certain number of foes to unlock it.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> Sealed {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} are hidden and will only be revealed after either unlocking the {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} or appraising it in the {{Term|HWDE|Smithy (Hyrule Warriors)}}. Sealed {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} can be appraised to reveal the {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} effect at the cost of {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee}}. The price to appraise the {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} will scale depending on how many KO's needed to unlock the {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} at 10 {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee}} per KO.<ref>{{Cite|By appraising a sealed weapon skill, you can find out what the sealed skill actually is. The Rupees required to appraise a sealed skill change according to the KO count required to unlock the skill.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> Some {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} will have a slot for a {{Term|HWDE|Skill}}, but no {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} will be inside. This open slot can be filled by fusing {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Smithy (Hyrule Warriors)}}. If one {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} has a {{Term|HWDE|Skill}} while another does not, the {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} can be transferred from one {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} to another. Doing so will destroy the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} that originally had the {{Term|HWDE|Skill}}, and fill in the open slot on the other.<ref>{{Cite|By fusing weapons, you can transfer {{Color|HWDE Green|skills}} between {{Color|HWDE Green|weapons you own}}. To transfer a skill, you will need to spend an amount of {{Color|HWDE Green|Rupees}} based on the {{Color|HWDE Green|source weapon}} and the type of skill. The source weapon will also be destroyed in the process. You can only add skills to weapons with empty skill slots. Overwriting previously activated skills and moving sealed skills won't work.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

All {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} will also have an {{Term|HWDE|Element}} assigned to them. Depending on the tier, {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} may have more than one {{Term|HWDE|Element}} which the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} will automatically switch between depending on which {{Term|HWDE|Element}} has a greater effect.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Multi-element weapons}} are weapons that have {{Color|HWDE Green|an extra elemental attribute}} in addition to their usual attribute. Only one attribute will be active at a time. This active attribute is determined by whichever will be more beneficial based on the current enemy or situation.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}s' {{Term|HWDE|Element}} can cause the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} to have different effects when used on the right enemy, or right battle.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Element}}:<br/>Equipping the right element for the right enemy or battle can make all the difference.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|An elemental effect can be added to certain attacks from specific weapons. The effect will change depending on whether the weapon has fire, water, lightning, light, or darkness attributes.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> If the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}s' {{Term|HWDE|Element}} is Fire and used in the correct situation, hitting an {{Term|HWDE|Enemy}} will cause it to explode when it hits the ground, dealing damage to {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}} around it.<ref>{{Cite|Hitting an enemy with a fire elemental attack will cause the enemy to blow up when it hits the ground, causing additional damage to enemies caught in the blast.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> If the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}s' {{Term|HWDE|Element}} is Water and used in the correct situation, an {{Term|HWDE|Enemy}} will continue to take damage for a short amount of time after being struck.<ref>{{Cite|Hitting an enemy with a water elemental attack will cause the enemy to continue taking damage over a set period of time.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> If the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}s' {{Term|HWDE|Element}} is Lightning and used in the correct situation, {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}} will take more damage when attacked in the air.<ref>{{Cite|Enemies sent flying by lightning elemental attacks will take extra damage from your attacls while in the air.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> If the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}s' {{Term|HWDE|Element}} is Light and used in the correct situation, the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}s' Attack Strength will become stronger when the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} consecutively hits a group of {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}} but will return to base attack when the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} stops attacking.<ref>{{Cite|Light elemental attacks gradually become stronger as you make consecutive attacks on groups of enemies.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> If the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}s' {{Term|HWDE|Element}} is Darkness and used in the correct situation, the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}s' Attack Strength will become stronger when the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} consecutively hits a single {{Term|HWDE|Enemy}} but will return to base attack when the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} stops attacking.<ref>{{Cite|Darkness elemental attacks gradually become stronger as you make consecutive attacks on a single enemy.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

===={{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} Usage====
{{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} can be used to defeat {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}}, cut {{Term|HWDE|Grass|link}}, and destroy {{Plural|HWDE|Jar|link}}. {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} can be used by {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} in different combinations of moves to inflict damage upon {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}}. Depending on what buttons the player presses, the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} will do a different set of moves. {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} will use regular attacks if the player presses the Y button, (Or the B button if the player chose the Zelda Style controls) or will use Combo Attacks if the player presses the X button. (Or the Y button if the player chose the Zelda Style controls)<ref name= "Basic Controls Tutorial">{{Cite|Y: Attack enemies<br/>X: Use more powerful attacks<br/>B: Evade enemy attacks<br/>A: Use Special Attacks to overwhelm foes|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> If the player presses the A button (Or the X button if the player chose the Zelda Style controls) the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} will use a Special Attack.<ref name= "Basic Controls Tutorial"></ref> Special Attacks are powerful attacks that {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} can use once their Special Attack Gauge is filled. {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} can raise their Special Attack Gauge by defeating many {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeating enemies fills your {{Color|HWDE Green|Special Attack gauge}}. Press A to use up your Special Attack gauge and perform a powerful {{Color|HWDE Green|Special Attack}}.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} can use different combinations after the player presses the Combo Attack button after pressing the Regular Attack button any number of times. If the player presses X alone, the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} will use a Strong Attack. Some combinations for {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}}' {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} can only be unlocked after crafting a {{Term|HWDE|Badge|link}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Badge Market|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Creating badges for your warriors at the Badge Market will help improve their battle abilities.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|New Combo Attack for Hylian Sword:<br/>Y Y Y Y Y Y Y|Badge Market|HWDE}}</ref> Some {{Plural|HWDE|Skill}} on {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}} will also improve the Attack Strength of certain combinations of moves.<ref>{{Cite|Increases X attack damage.|Smithy|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Increases Y X attack damage.|Smithy|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Increases Y Y X attack damage.|Smithy|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Increases Y Y Y X attack damage.|Smithy|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Increases Y Y Y Y X attack damage.|Smithy|HWDE}}</ref>

Throughout battles, {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} can find patches of {{Term|HWDE|Grass}} growing in many places. A {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} can cut the {{Term|HWDE|Grass}} by using any attack, which can reveal {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee|link}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Force Fragment|link}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Magic Jar|link}} and {{Plural|HWDE|Heart|link}}. Each {{Term|HWDE|Grass}} cluster will only give the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} one {{Term|HWDE|Green Rupee|link}}, and the {{Plural|HWDE|Force Fragment}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Magic Jar}} and {{Plural|HWDE|Heart}} will be at their smallest form, with the {{Plural|HWDE|Heart}} only refilling one of the {{Plural|HWDE|Heart}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}}s' {{Term|HWDE|Life Gauge|link}}, the {{Plural|HWDE|Force Fragment}} only filling 33 percent of the Special Attack Gauge, and the {{Plural|HWDE|Magic Jar}} only filling 20 percent of the {{Term|HWDE|Magic Gauge|link}}.

In {{Plural|HWDE|Keep}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} can find multiple {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} lining the walls of the {{Term|HWDE|Keep}} which the {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} can destroy with their {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon}}. Tan colored {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} will reveal {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee}} or {{Plural|HWDE|Power-Up|link}} for the {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} {{Plural|HWDE|Item|link}} when destroyed. Yellow colored {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} will reveal {{Plural|HWDE|Force Fragment}} when destroyed. Green colored {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} will reveal {{Plural|HWDE|Magic Jar}} when destroyed, and finally, red colored {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} will reveal {{Plural|HWDE|Heart}} when destroyed.

==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} can use a variety of {{Plural|CoH|Weapon}} in {{CoH|-}}. {{Plural|CoH|Broadsword|link}}, {{Plural|CoH|Spear|link}}, and {{Plural|CoH|Flail|link}} can be used by any of the three. {{Plural|CoH|Dagger|link}} are only used by {{Term|CoH|Cadence}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}}, though {{Term|CoH|Link}} can use {{Term|CoH|Short Sword|link}}, which are functionally identical. In addition, each character except for {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} has at least one special {{Term|CoH|Weapon}} that only they can use.

Most {{Plural|CoH|Weapon}} can be infused by a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy|link}} in exchange for {{Plural|CoH|Diamond|link}}. Glass and legendary {{Plural|CoH|Weapon}} cannot be infused. When a {{Term|CoH|Weapon}} is infused, it will gain a new attribute.

Glass {{Plural|CoH|Weapon}} boast 3 points of damage, but will shatter and disappear if the owner takes damage while wielding them. Legendary {{Plural|CoH|Weapon}} have powerful properties such as multiple infusion effects, different special moves, or even armor-piercing capabilities. There is at least one legendary {{Term|CoH|Weapon}} for each type of {{Term|CoH|Weapon}}.
