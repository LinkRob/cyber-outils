'''Vanish''' is the up special move of [[Sheik]] throughout the {{SSB|Series}}. The move first appears in {{SSBM}} and returns in {{SSBB}}, {{SSB4}}, and {{SSBU}}.

==Overview==
When using Vanish, Sheik throws a [[Deku Nut]] onto the ground and vanishes, a behavior originating from {{OoT}}. Shortly afterward, Sheik reappears elsewhere on the stage, the location being dependent on which direction is held on the Control Stick. If Sheik uses Vanish in the air, she leaps up a little before vanishing. As of {{SSB4|-}}, opponents can be damaged from the Deku Nut explosion and Sheik reappearing.

Vanish has two custom variants in {{SSB4|-}} only: '''Gale''', which increases the speed of the warp but removes the explosion, making the move incapable of damaging, and '''Abyss''', which knocks any opponents hit into the air, {{ssb|meteor smash}}ing them, although Abyss is slower than Vanish.
