The {{Term/Store|Dining Hall||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Dining Hall'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall}} is a spacious room that previously hosted guests of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 246}}</ref> The entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall}} can be found on a break in the main road to the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}} just before the {{Term|BotW|Second Gatehouse|link}}. On the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall}}'s northwestern wall stand three wooden doorways, which lead into the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors|link}}

A {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} and an unlit {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} can be found on the southern end of the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall}}. Numerous {{Plural|BotW|Pot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}} can be found along the walls of the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall}}.
