{{Term/Store|Robred Dropoff||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Robred Dropoff'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Robred Dropoff}} is located in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Robred Dropoff}} is a large cliff that overlooks the {{Term|BotW|Ovli Plain|link}}. 

On the side of {{Term|BotW|Robred Dropoff}}, a {{Term|BotW|Rare Ore Deposit|link}} and several {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} grow out of the cliff.
