{{Term/Store|All-Purpose Bait|All-Purpose Bait|Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 115 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> It can be bought from [[Beedle's Shop Ship]]s once [[Link]] has bought the [[Bait Bag]]. It can also be bought from [[Rat]]s and from [[Tingle]] using the [[Tingle Tuner]].

All-Purpose Bait comes in three-use bundles and is delectable for many [[Animal]]s, including [[Pig]]s, [[Rat]]s, and [[Fishman]]. All-Purpose Bait can be used to feed it to the [[Fishman]] so they can draw parts of Link's [[Sea Chart]] while giving him information about the islands. It can also be used to persuade the large Pig, [[Link (Pig)|Link]], on [[Outset Island]] to dig in black spots of soil to reveal a [[Piece of Heart]]. All-Purpose Bait can also be used to lure Rats from their [[Rat Hole]] and convince them to sell things to Link, including [[Arrow]]s, [[Bomb]]s, [[Potion]]s, [[Hyoi Pear]]s, and even more All-Purpose Bait. All-Purpose Bait can also be used in the fight against [[Puppet Ganon]] in [[Ganon's Tower]].

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{HW|L|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
