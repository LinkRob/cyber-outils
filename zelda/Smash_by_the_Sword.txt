==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Smash by the Sword}}" is an event wherein a greater number of sword-wielding {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Sword-wielding spirits are ready to cross blades on the Spirit Board! |author= Nintendo |published= October 29, 2019 |retrieved= July 26, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/e432a816/index.html |title= Event: Smash by the Sword |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
