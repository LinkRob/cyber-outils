The {{Term/Store|Ancient Columns||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Ancient Columns'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Columns}} are located on the {{Term|BotW|Rayne Highlands|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. Dozens of ruined stone columns litter the area, some of which have fallen over. There is a small pond in the center of the area, which contains {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Columns|display=Columns}} are also home to {{Term|BotW|Tena Ko'sah Shrine|link}}.

Prior to the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} attempted to investigate the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} using the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, but she was unable to gain access.<ref>{{Cite|Nothing. Just as I thought. Hmm... It appears that this structure was designed to be exclusively accessed by the sword's chosen one. But designs can always be worked around, at least I hope. How do I get inside...? I need to activate it somehow.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approached her, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} reiterated her annoyance at having an escort and ordered him to stop following her.<ref>{{Cite|I thought I made it clear that I am not in need of an escort. It seems I'm the only one with a mind of my own. I, the person in question, am fine, regardless of the king's orders. Return to the castle. And tell that to my father, please. And stop following me!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> During the "{{Term|BotW|Captured Memories|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can claim the "{{Term|BotW|Zelda's Resentment|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} outside of {{Term|BotW|Tena Ko'sah Shrine}}, which depicts these events.

Two {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found among the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Columns}}. One can be revealed by diving into the {{Term|BotW|Circle|link}} of {{Plural|BotW|Lily Pad|link}} in the pond. A second can be found at the end of a {{Term|BotW|Race|link}} on the west side of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Columns|display=Columns}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} uncovers these {{Plural|BotW|Korok}}, they will each give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
