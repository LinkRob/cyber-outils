{{Term/Store|Blue Flame|Blue Flames|Series, BotW|plural}},{{Exp Game|BotW}} also known as {{Term/Store|Blue Fire|Blue Fires|OoT, OoT3D}},{{Exp Game|OoT}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 117 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref> are recurring items and objects in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Blue Fire}}<br/>This blue fire... It doesn't seem natural. Maybe you can use it for something. But how could you carry it?
}}

{{Term|OoT3D|Blue Fire}} can be found in the {{Term|OoT3D|Ice Cavern|link}} and [[Ganon's Castle]] in {{OoT}}. It can also be bought at the [[Medicine Shop]] for 300 [[Rupee]]s. In {{MQ}}, it can also be found in the [[Gerudo's Training Ground]].

{{Term|OoT3D|Blue Fire}} is used to melt [[Red Ice]]. {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} must first scoop it from a pedestal by using a {{Term|OoT3D|Bottle|link}} or buying it from the Medicine Shop. {{Term|OoT3D|Blue Fire}} is required to progress through the Ice Cavern and obtain the [[Iron Boots]] and a [[Piece of Heart]]. It is also needed to get the [[Zora Tunic]] by melting the Red Ice in which [[King Zora]] is encased. Alternatively, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can melt the Red Ice blocking the entrance to the [[Zora Shop]] in order to buy the Tunic.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} is a type of [[Fire]] that never burns out while in a [[Torch]] in {{BotW}}. Certain Torches can only be lit with {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame}}, such as inside the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}, within {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, and the [[Ancient Furnace]]s outside of the [[Akkala Ancient Tech Lab]] and the [[Hateno Ancient Tech Lab]]. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must bring {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} to both Ancient Tech Labs from a nearby Blue Fire furnace in order to activate certain features of the Tech Lab. Link must use a wooden {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, such as a [[Torch]] or a [[Tree Branch]], to transport {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}. At the Hateno Ancient Tech Lab, this will allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to upgrade his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. At the Akkala Ancient Tech Lab, this will allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to exchange [[Ancient Material]]s for many types of [[Ancient Soldier Gear]] and equipment. If {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame}} are used to set flammable objects on fire, they will behave the same way as normal Fire.

The Blue Flame blows up [[Remote Bomb|Remote Bombs]] and shatters [[Cryonis]] blocks on contact.
