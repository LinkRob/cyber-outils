{{Term/Store|Gust Jar|Gust Jars|Series, TMC, TFH|plural}},{{Exp Game|TMC, TFH}}<ref name="E 126">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126 {{Exp Game|TMC, SS, TFH}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Gust Bellows|Gust Bellows|SS|plural}},{{Exp Game|SS}}<ref name="E 126"/> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TMC|-}}===
The {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar}} can be found in the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}. It can be used to suck in enemies and shoot them out, throw certain enemies off balance, absorb fire attacks, and remove fire scorching the ground. It can also pull hard-to-reach items towards {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, such as {{Plural|TMC|Mushroom|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar}} changes its color to indicate its power. Turning yellow indicates medium charge, while turning red indicates that it is at max power and air capacity.

The {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar}} is also used for navigational purposes. While {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can jump onto {{Plural|TMC|Lily Pad|link}} and use the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar}} to shoot bursts of {{Term|TMC|Wind|link}} to the rear of the {{Term|TMC|Lily Pad}} to cross bodies of water. The {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar}} is effective against air-based enemies, such as {{Plural|TMC|Lakitu|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Peahat|link}}. It can also be used to defeat {{Plural|TMC|Puffstool|link}}. It is the key item needed to defeat the {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine}} and the {{Term|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}. It can also be use to save {{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}} from the ghost in the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
[[File:Gust Bellows Icon.png|left]]
The Gust Bellows can found in the [[Lanayru Mining Facility]] in a large, sand-filled warehouse patrolled by [[Froak]]s. It can create a gust of wind used to reveal objects underneath piles of sand or the remains of non-hardened magma.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|SS Gold|Gust Bellows}}! It is an ancient and mystical device capable of blowing an endless gust of wind.|N/A|SS}}</ref> It can also be used to blow away small enemies, making them vulnerable for a short period of time, and to expose enemy's weak points, such as the [[Armos]]. This depiction of the Gust Bellows matches the art style of the {{Plural|SS|Ancient Robot|link}} and is used in conjunction with their technology.

After completing the Lanayru Mining Facility, the Gust Bellows can be used in a sidequest for [[Mallara]], [[Pipit]]'s mother, who asks [[Link]] to blow away all of the dust infesting the interior of her home.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh, Link. Welcome. So here's the thing, Link. My house is terribly dirty... Pipit is always telling me to do some dusting around here, but I just can't bring myself to do it. That's why I wanted to ask you if you wouldn't mind cleaning it for me. I'll even pay you, Link.|Mallara|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You will?! Great! I knew you wouldn't leave me to sit in this dusty house, Link. What's that? ... A broom? You know, I don't even remember where we keep the crazy thing... ...Or if we ever owned one... Most of the time I just wait for a good strong wind to come along and {{Color|SS Red|blow the dust away}}. So look, I don't really care how you get the job done--I just want you to {{Color|SS Red|blow it all away for me}}. So I'll just leave you to it, then!|Mallara|SS}}</ref> Upon doing so, he will receive a red [[Rupee]] and 5 [[Gratitude Crystal]]s as a reward.

==={{TFH|-}}===
[[File:TFH Nice Gust Jar Icon.png|left]]
{{Plural|TFH|Gust Jar}} in {{TFH}} can be used to propel other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} across gaps too wide to be thrown across, as well as blow items and enemies away. They are necessary for many puzzles involving teamwork, such as when a {{Term|TFH|Link}} needs to propel another's {{Term|TFH|Bomb|link}} across a gap to hit {{Plural|TFH|Switch|link}} or enemies. {{Plural|TFH|Gust Jar}} can also blow out fires, stun enemies, and push certain foes off of elevated platforms.

{{Plural|TFH|Gust Jar}} first appear in the {{Term|TFH|Den of Flames|link}}, in which two of the three {{Plural|TFH|Links}} acquire them. They reappear in the {{Term|TFH|Fire Temple|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Sealed Gateway|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Bomb Storage|link}} (two {{Plural|TFH|Gust Jar}}), {{Term|TFH|Training Ground|link}}, {{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Infinity Dunes|link}} (two {{Plural|TFH|Gust Jar}}), {{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Palace Noir|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Lone Labyrinth|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Deception Castle|link}}, and {{Term|TFH|Sky Temple|link}} (one {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar}} per Stage). They also appear in the {{Term|TFH|Scorching Zone|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Fortified Zone|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Desert Zone|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Baneful Zone|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}, as well as the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}}'s Volcano, Fortress and The Ruins arenas.

The effects of {{Plural|TFH|Gust Jar}} can be improved while {{Term|TFH|Link}} is wearing the {{Term|TFH|Gust Garb|link}}. This {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} increases the size and range of a {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar}}'s puff of air.
