"{{Term/Store|Tools of the Trade||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Tools of the Trade}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is one of four that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} needs to complete as part of "{{Term|BotW|The Thunder Helm|link}}", which will grant him the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Isha|link}} pacing around the market in front of {{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories|link}}. Though she greets {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Sav'aaq...|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Vasaaq...|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sav'saaba...|Isha|BotW}}</ref> she remarks that she does not have a reason to be doing so.<ref>{{Cite|Not that I have any reason to be greeting people...|Isha|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not speak to {{Term|BotW|Isha}} until after completing the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, she claims not to have the time to greet people since she is expecting a big rush of customers.<ref>{{Cite|I haven't really got the time to be greeting people, honestly... Now that the Divine Beast has finally calmed down, there should be a rush of customers any second...|Isha|BotW}}</ref> She laments her misfortune ocurring when it did.<ref>{{Cite|So why did something like this have to happen to me now, of all times?|Isha|BotW}}</ref>}}

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what is wrong, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} explains that she manages a {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}} specializing in accessories, but she has run out of the {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}} she needs to make them.<ref>{{Cite|[What's the matter?] I manage a shop that specializes in handmade accessories, but I've run out of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}. I need it to make my items.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> She says that if someone would just give her 10 {{Term|BotW|Flint}}, she would offer a nice reward.<ref>{{Cite|If only I had {{Color|BotW Red|10}}... Just {{Color|BotW Red|10}}... If some kind vai would give me {{Color|BotW Red|10 pieces of flint}}, I could offer a nice reward...|Isha|BotW}}</ref> However, she realizes that nothing is that easy in the real worlds, and her dreams will change nothing.<ref>{{Cite|I can dream all I want, though... Nothing goes that smoothly in the real world.|Isha|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s inquiry, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} reveals that she makes her accessories using {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Accessories?] I use {{Color|BotW Blue|rubies}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|sapphires}}, and other gems to make {{Color|BotW Blue|accessories}}.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> By wearing {{Plural|BotW|Gem}}, one can have their powers transferred to them.<ref>{{Cite|Each gem type has its own power that it can transfer to whoever wears it.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> However, she once again brings up her lack of {{Term|BotW|Flint}},<ref>{{Cite|There's nothing I can do about not having any {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}, though...|Isha|BotW}}</ref> which officially begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}. Before {{Term|BotW|Link}} leaves, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} apologizes for having nothing to give him after visiting.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry that you came all the way to see me for nothing.|Isha|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has 10 pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint}} for {{Term|BotW|Isha}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to give them to her.{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to give {{Term|BotW|Isha}} less than 10 pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint}}, she appreciates the gesture, but she claims that she is unable to start without a total of 10 pieces.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm. Well, I do appreciate the gesture... But unless I have {{Color|BotW Red|10 pieces of}} {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}} to get started with, there's no point...|Isha|BotW}}</ref>}} {{Term|BotW|Isha}} is unsure how to react at first,<ref>{{Cite|[Here's some flint.] Uh...|Isha|BotW}}</ref> but she quickly becomes shocked by {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s generosity.<ref>{{Cite|What?! You're just...giving these to me?|Isha|BotW}}</ref> She thanks him, though she realizes she has to do more to repay him.<ref>{{Cite|Sarqso! But...I can't take this all from you with just a sarqso. We hardly know each other, and you've been so nice...|Isha|BotW}}</ref> However, she does not have any products ready to give to him.<ref>{{Cite|The shop's been closed so long, I hardly have any inventory...|Isha|BotW}}</ref>

Suddenly, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} realizes she can make an accessory for {{Term|BotW|Link}} now that she has {{Term|BotW|Flint}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, of course! Now that I have some {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}, I could make a jeweled accessory for you if you're interested in something.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> She offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} a choice between a {{Term|BotW|Ruby Circlet|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Sapphire Circlet|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Topaz Earrings|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Rubies}} have the power to grant cold resistance. So you'd like a {{Color|BotW Blue|ruby circlet}}?|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Sapphires}} have the power to grant heat resistance. So you think you'd like a {{Color|BotW Blue|sapphire circlet}}?|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Topaz}} has the power to grant electric resistance. So you think you'd like {{Color|BotW Blue|topaz earrings}}?|Isha|BotW}}</ref> Whichever one {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} reveals that she had an inkling he might pick it, before stepping away for a moment.<ref>{{Cite|Teehee, I thought you might pick that one. Please give me a moment...|Isha|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Isha}} presents {{Term|BotW|Link}} with his new accessory, noting that it turned out well.<ref>{{Cite|Brand-new, especially for you! I think it turned out really well!|Isha|BotW}}</ref> If he ever needs another accessory, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} invites him back to {{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories}} for a discounted price.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, I got the shop running again, so if you ever need an accessory, please come by for a special discount.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> However, she asks him not to tell any other customers about his special deal.<ref>{{Cite|Just...don't tell my other customers, {{Small|OK?}}|Isha|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
