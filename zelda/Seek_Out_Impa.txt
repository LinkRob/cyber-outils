"{{Term/Store|Seek Out Impa||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Seek Out Impa}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Great Plateau}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} receives this {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} from {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} after being informed of the events 100 years ago. {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to meet with {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I suggest...that you make your way east, out to one of the villages in the wilderness. Follow the road out to Kakariko Village. There you will find the elder, Impa.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> as he can learn about his journey from her.<ref>{{Cite|She will tell you more about the path that lies ahead.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use the {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} for the exact location of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Consult the map on your Sheikah Slate for the precise location of Kakariko Village.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He also gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} directions, saying to go past the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|link}} and to follow the road north.<ref>{{Cite|Make your way past the twin summits of the Dueling Peaks. From there, follow the road as it proceeds north.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> The Quest begins alongside "{{Term|BotW|Destroy Ganon|link}}" once {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} fades away.

At {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Impa}} in {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link|display=her home}}. He is intially stopped by her guards, {{Term|BotW|Cado|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Dorian|link}},<ref>{{Cite|You there! Who are you?! How dare you trespass upon {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Impa}}'s abode!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> but they calm down once they notice he is carrying a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Is that...a {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}?|Cado|BotW}}</ref> Upon realizing who he is,<ref>{{Cite|But that would mean you are... No, it's not possible. Can it be?|Cado|BotW}}</ref> the pair apologize and allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} through.<ref>{{Cite|Please forgive us for behaving so rudely. Of course we have heard the legends from Lady Impa herself. Please, friend... Go ahead and step inside.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Impa's House}}, she is pleased to see that he is awake,<ref>{{Cite|...So you're finally awake.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> and despite how long it has been, she asks if he remembers her.<ref>{{Cite|It has been quite a long time...Link. I am much older now, but... you remember me, don't you?|Impa|BotW}}</ref> However, she quickly notices that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has no recollection of her,<ref>{{Cite|What is the matter? You are looking at me as though I am a stranger to you. Those eyes... They lack the light of familiarity.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> and she asks if he at least recognizes her name.<ref>{{Cite|It is I, courageous one. Impa. Surely you must at least remember the name Impa?|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Seeing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has lost his memory,<ref>{{Cite|I see... So you have lost your memory.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> she decides that it may be a blessing for the time being.<ref>{{Cite|Well, it matters not. In fact, that may actually be a blessing in disguise for the time being.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Impa}} beckons {{Term|BotW|Link}} closer,<ref>{{Cite|Dearest Link... Please come a bit closer.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> where she begins to tell him the story of 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|A hundred years ago... Yes... A hundred years ago, the kingdom of Hyrule was destroyed.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> After he fell, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} wished for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be placed into a sacred slumber.<ref>{{Cite|After you fell, Princess Zelda's final wish was to place you in a sacred slumber.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} went on her own to fight {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And then...all alone... Alone she went to face {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}}.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She entrusted {{Term|BotW|Impa}} with message for {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Before Princess Zelda went to nobly meet her fate...she entrusted me with {{Color|BotW Red|some words}} she wished to say to you.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> which she has held onto since then.<ref>{{Cite|I have been waiting 100 years to deliver the princess's message.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants to hear this message, though, he must be prepared to risk his own life.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|However!}} These words, which the princess risked her life to leave you... Well, if you are to hear them... You must be prepared to risk your life as well.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She is fearful that {{Term|BotW|Link}} would be burdened by it when he lacks his memories,<ref>{{Cite|But I am afraid that burden may be too much to bear while you are still without your memories.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> so she tells him to return when he wants to hear it.<ref>{{Cite|I leave the choice to you. When you feel you are ready to receive the princess's message, return to me.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} reiterates that she can not pass {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} message to someone who lacks conviction.<ref>{{Cite|The words that the princess risked her life to impart to you...I cannot pass them on to someone who lacks conviction. Are you prepared to risk your life for the greater good?|Impa|BotW}}</ref> When he agrees to listen, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} notes that he is still as courageous as he was before he lost his memories.<ref>{{Cite|Ha! Not a memory to your name, yet you are as intent as ever to charge forward with only courage and justice on your side. You have not changed a bit. Once a hero, always a hero.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} has no memory, she decides to recount what happened for him.<ref>{{Cite|Very well. Since you have lost your memory, I will recount for you all that has happened...|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Impa}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} the history of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} and how it is tied to the history of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The history of the royal family of Hyrule is also the history of Calamity Ganon, a primal evil that has endured over the ages.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Whenever the {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Calamity}} has arisen, it has been turned back by a [[Link#The_Hero_10.2C000_Years_Prior_to_Breath_of_the_Wild|warrior with the soul of a hero]] and a [[Princess_Zelda#Ten_Thousands_Years_prior_to_Breath_of_the_Wild|princess with the blood of the Goddess]].<ref>{{Cite|This evil has been turned back time and time again by a warrior wielding the soul of a hero, and a princess who carries the blood of the Goddess.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Every conflict eventually faded into legend,<ref>{{Cite|With the passage of time, each conflict with Ganon faded into legend.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> so {{Term|BotW|Impa}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} of the first {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, ten thousand years ago.<ref>{{Cite|So listen closely as I tell you this "legend" that occurred 10,000 years ago.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

Prior to the first {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}, {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} was a flourishing and advanced civilization.<ref>{{Cite|Hyrule was then blossoming as a highly advanced civilization. Even the most powerful of monsters posed little threat to the denizens of the realm.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Due to their technological prowess, the people decided to create defenses against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} in the event of his return.<ref>{{Cite|The people thought it wise to utilize their technological prowess to ensure the safety of the land should Calamity Ganon ever return.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> They constructed the four {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}},<ref>{{Cite|They constructed four mechanical wonders that came to be known as the Divine Beasts.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> in addition to an army of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They also built a legion of autonomous weapons called Guardians.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Four individuals, all of whom had exceptional skill, were selected to pilot the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} as part of the plan to defeat {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Divine Beasts were piloted by four individuals of exceptional skill from across the land. And thus, the plan to neutralize Ganon was forged.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} returned to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|display=Hyrule}}, the princess, hero, and {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} fought together against him.<ref>{{Cite|Upon Ganon's inevitable return to Hyrule, the princess and the hero fought alongside these four Champions against this ancient evil.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> The {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} protected the hero while the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} attacked {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Guardians were tasked with protecting the hero as the Divine Beasts unleased a furious attack upon their terrible foe.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Using the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}, the hero dealt a final blow that allowed the princess to seal {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} away.<ref>{{Cite|And when the hero wielding the sword that seals the darkness delivered his final blow... The princess used her sacred power to seal away Calamity Ganon.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

100 years ago, the people of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|display=Hyrule}} tried to follow in their footsteps.<ref>{{Cite|A hundred years ago... in preparation for the foretold revival of Ganon, we strove to follow the lead of our ancestors 10,000 years prior.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> However, they underestimated {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}'s power.<ref>{{Cite|But...in the end... Despite our best efforts and careful planning, we underestimated his power.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> To avoid this happening again, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} left her message for {{Term|BotW|Link}} with {{Term|BotW|Impa}}.<ref>{{Cite|In order to avoid making that grave mistake again, the princess left you these words...|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Her message told {{Term|BotW|Link}} to free the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Free the four Divine Beasts}}. That is what she said.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Impa}} explains that the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} are ancient {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} weapons wielded by the defeated {{Plural|BotW|Champions}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|four Divine Beasts}} are the ancient Sheikah weapons wielded by the four Champions who Ganon defeated.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> They are {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}}, controlled by {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}} of the Gorons.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, controlled by {{Color|BotW Blue|Revali}} of the Rito.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, controlled by {{Color|BotW Blue|Mipha}} of the Zora.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And the {{Color|BotW Red|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}, controlled by {{Color|BotW Blue|Urbosa}} of the Gerudo.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Impa}} discourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} from attempting to fight {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} without the power of the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|It would be extremely ill-advised to face {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}} without the power of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beasts}} to help you.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She tells him to infiltrate the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} and bring them back to their side.<ref>{{Cite|You must infiltrate the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beasts}} that were stolen away by {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}} 100 years ago and bring them back to our side.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> For more information, he can locate the four races across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}}.<ref>{{Cite|More information about these {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beasts}} can be found by locating the {{Color|BotW Red|four races}} scattered across Hyrule.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} opens, displaying new markers on the map as Impa instructs him to meet the leader of each race.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} will guide you on your way. You must go where it tells you and meet with each {{Color|BotW Red|leader}} there.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> This begins the "{{Term|BotW|Free the Divine Beasts|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}.

During this, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} notices that the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} is not functioning completely.<ref>{{Cite|...It seems to me that your {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} is not yet complete.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Since it is supposed to be his guide,<ref>{{Cite|The device Princess Zelda left you is your guide and also your memory.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> she directs him to the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now, let's see... Someone at the {{Color|BotW Red|research lab in Hateno Village}} might be able to help you.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> A new marker appears on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}, which {{Term|BotW|Impa}} explains is the location of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}},<ref>{{Cite|The location shining on the eastern edge is {{Color|BotW Red|Hateno Village}}.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> which is one of the few places to avoid significant damage during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}.<ref>{{Cite|It is a small village... One of the very few places that avoided suffering significant damage during the Great Calamity.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> This begins the "{{Term|BotW|Locked Mementos|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}.

{{Term|BotW|Impa}} asserts that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is both {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|display=Hyrule's}} only hope.<ref>{{Cite|You are Princess Zelda's only hope... and Hyrule's, as well...|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She encourages him to follow his heart and seize his destiny.<ref>{{Cite|You cannot turn back now. Follow your heart and seize your destiny!|Impa|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} will be completed.
