{{Term/Store|Kosi||Series, TotK}} is a character in {{TotK}}.

==Biography==
Kosi is a weapons repairman who lives in [[Lookout Landing]]. He can be found inside the Royal Family Emergency Shelter attempting to fix up weapons that have decayed as a result of the Upheaval.
