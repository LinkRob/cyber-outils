{{Term/Store|Morth|Morths|Series, TWW, TWWHD, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 194 {{Exp Game|TWW, TWWHD, TFH}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Morth}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= Eeek! Morths! Fan them away with the Deku Leaf before they can stick to you!<br/>
If they stick to you, use your rolling attack to shake them loose.
}}

{{Plural|TWW|Morth}} mainly appear in wooded areas, most commonly in the {{Term|TWW|Forbidden Woods|link}} in {{TWW}}. They will close in on {{Term|TWW|Link|link}} whenever he moves close. If several {{Plural|TWW|Morth}} latch on, they will hamper his movements. {{Term|TWW|Link}} can easily free himself by performing a {{Term|TWW|Spin Attack|link}} or just by rolling. They are easily dispatched with the {{Term|TWW|Sword|link}} and the {{Term|TWW|Boomerang|link}} if they are not latched on to {{Term|TWW|Link}}. They can also be sent flying away by using the {{Term|TWW|Deku Leaf|link}}. {{Plural|TWW|Morth}} are also occasionally released by {{Term|TWW|Mothula|link}} when injured. Certain [[Wizzrobe#The Wind Waker|Wizzrobe]]s can also summon small groups of Morths.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Morth}} appear in certain Levels of [[The Dunes]] area and in the [[Desert Zone]] of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}} in {{TFH}}. They always appear in groups of three and will immediately slow down the {{Term|TFH|Link}} they manage to attach to. When attached, {{Plural|TFH|Morth}} prevent the {{Term|TFH|Link}} from dashing. {{Plural|TFH|Morth}} cannot be removed by the {{Term|TFH|Link}} they are latched onto, instead, another {{Term|TFH|Link}} can free him by attacking them with his {{Term|TFH|Sword}} or with another item. {{Plural|TFH|Morth}} often appear in sandy areas, potentially making the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} susceptible to sinking in quicksand. {{Plural|TFH|Morth}} will also respawn shortly after they are defeated. They often appear in Stages where all enemies have to be defeated, however, {{Plural|TFH|Morth}} are not counted. They will disappear completely after clearing these Stages.

{{Plural|TFH|Morth}} will die if a {{Term|TFH|Link}} steps onto an active {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}}. {{Plural|TFH|Morth}} that attempt to attach onto a {{Term|TFH|Link}} wearing the {{Term|TFH|Cacto Clothes|link}} will die immediately.
