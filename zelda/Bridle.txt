==Location and Uses==
Bridles are a type of [[Horse Gear]] that are equipped to a [[Horse|Horse's]] head. They can be obtained by registering Horses, completing [[Mini-Game]]s, or in [[Chest]]s across [[Hyrule Kingdom]]. With the exception of the [[Ancient Bridle]], all Bridles are cosmetic and offer no changes to horseback riding. The [[Stable Bridle]] is also the only Bridle that isn't a [[Key Item]] in [[Link|Link's]] [[Inventory]].

Only four [[Stable]]s have a character who can change the Bridles of Link's Horses, and they will only do so when Link has the highest Bond possible with his Horse. However, they won't change the Bridles of the [[Giant Horse]] or [[Epona]]. The Stables are as follows:
