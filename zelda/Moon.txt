The {{Term/Store|Moon|Moons|Series, MM, MM3D}}[https://www.google.com/url?sa=t&source=web&rct=j&opi=89978449&url=https://aminoapps.com/c/zelda/page/blog/the-moon-trials-majoras-mask/vdMt_nu6Y14YrZ81m8Nez2wbDKM4nxz&ved=2ahUKEwir85aZ_beMAxWbKkQIHb0lHVE4ChAWegQIJBAB&usg=AOvVaw2v_L7kGq3bij2eEG1fu0nb ¹] the rock-like ominous object that constantly threatens to destroy [[Clock Town]] in {{MM}}.<ref name="consume">{{Cite|I... I shall consume. Consume... Consume everything.|Moon|MM}}</ref> By the evil power of [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]], it's destined to crash into Clock Town, which will [[Terminan Apocalypse|obliterate]] the town along with the rest of [[Termina]], including [[Link]]. It is also known for having an angry, snarling face.

==Appearances==
==={{MM|-}}===
Before the events of {{MM|-}}, the [[Skull Kid]] ambushed the [[Happy Mask Salesman]] and took one of his precious masks, the Majora's Mask. Even though the Skull Kid was unaware of it, Majora's Mask gradually began to fully possess him, turning his harmless pranks into acts with malicious intents behind them. Eventually, the mask completely embraced the Skull Kid's mind, and, using him as a puppet, Majora's Mask set the Moon on a collision course with Clock Town. Once Link, the [[Hero of Time]], arrives in Termina, he discovers that he has just [[Time|72 hours]] (three days) to prevent the Moon from extinguishing all of Termina, including himself.

Along with his [[Fairy]] companion [[Tatl]], Link sets out to find the [[Four Giants]] in each of the [[temple]]s found throughout Termina. After they free the Giants from the Skull Kid's curse, the young hero and his Fairy climb atop the [[Clock Tower]] on the [[Final Day]] to call upon the Giants using the [[Oath to Order]]. One by one, the Four Giants appear and stop the Moon's descent, knocking the Skull Kid unconscious in the process.

At this point, Majora's Mask realizes that the Skull Kid has become useless, so it abandons the imp's body and transports itself inside the Moon through its mouth.<ref name="puppet">{{Cite|A puppet that can no longer be used is mere garbage. This puppet's role has just ended...|Majora|MM}}</ref> With the Moon itself now being possessed by Majora's Mask, it becomes too strong for the Giants to hold back and it threatens to continue with its plan of obliterating Termina.<ref name="consume">{{Cite|I... I shall consume. Consume... Consume everything.|Moon|MM}}</ref> While the Four Giants use all of their might to restrain the evil menace, Link and Tatl follow Majora's Mask into the Moon.<ref>{{Cite|Well? You're going in, aren't you? ...We can't do anything down here. Let's go!|Tatl|MM}}</ref> Inside, Link defeats Majora in rough battle and the Moon disintegrates, returning peace to Termina.

====Dungeon====
{{Main|Moon Dungeons}}
Once Link successfully summon the [[Four Giants]] to halt the descent of the Moon, Majora's Mask discards the Skull Kid's unconscious body and enters the Moon's open mouth,<ref name="puppet"/> once more driving it down toward Termina.<ref name="consume"/> To stop this, Link and Tatl follow the cursed Mask to the Moon.

Link is transported to a serene, hilled, paradise-like landscape known as the [[Inside the Moon|Grassy Playfield]]. Several [[Moon Child]]ren appear in this field, wearing the remains of the four previous [[Bosses in Majora's Mask|bosses]] as masks. If Link decides to play with any of these Moon Children at the cost of giving them a few of his masks, they will transport him to a special [[dungeon]]. If Link decides to play with the Moon Child wearing Majora's Mask sitting under the tree in the center of the field, he will initiate the final battle with the actual Majora's Mask. In addition, if the young hero were to talk to the same Child after trading all 20 of the non-transforming Masks including the [[Giant's Mask]], he will earn the [[Fierce Deity Mask|Fierce Deity's Mask]] as a reward.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSBM|-}}===
In {{SSBM}}, the Moon appears in the background of the [[Great Bay]] Stage. As the match progresses, the Moon slowly descends until the Four Giants appear and push it back up to the sky. This cycle repeats over time.

Compared to its original appearance in {{MM}}, the Moon in the Great Bay stage is noticeably smaller in size, especially compared to the Giants, it's faced in a different position, and its eyes are always glowing, which they originally didn't until it tried to "consume everything" when Majora's Mask entered it. Because the stage, unlike ''Majora's Mask'', lacks the 'three day-and-night cycles' element, the Moon moves much faster than it originally did.

The Moon also appears as a [[Trophies in Super Smash Bros. Melee|Trophy]].

==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, the Moon appears in "[[The Shadow King]]", where it is constructed by [[Fairy|Fairies]] and summoned by the [[Great Fairy]] to knock [[Argorok]] out of the sky. The Moon can also be summoned when using the [[Clawshot]] into the air, pulling a smaller Moon down to crush enemies. Link can also summon the Moon when using the [[Great Fairy (Item)|Great Fairy]] weapon as a combo attack. [[Young Link]] wearing the Fierce Deity's Mask can also summon the Moon, which is pulled by the Skull Kid and then sliced in half by the Fierce Deity. Skull Kid as a playable Warrior kicks a miniature Moon at his enemies to attack.

An 8-bit version of the Moon also appears on the [[Termina Map]] in [[Adventure Mode]], which will crash and destroy Termina if the map is not cleared in time. The Moon will crash in 72 moves, similarly to the 72 hours in {{MM|-}}. However, if the Four Giants [[Item Card]] is used and the hidden battle is completed on the Moon itself, the Moon will not fall down. The Moon can be prevented from crashing by using the Song of Time Item Card, however this also resets the map.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
The Moon appears as an [[Assist Trophy]] in {{SSBU}}. When summoned, the Moon will hurtle towards the Stage, becoming fiery and throwing debris from the Stage as it becomes closer. The Moon will then collide with the Stage, damaging anything in its path, before exploding in a flash and launching any Fighters that were close to the Moon.

The Moon also appears as a [[Spirit]]. It reprises its role in the background of the Great Bay Stage as well.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*During development of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' and in some early artwork, the Moon had no face on it.
*The Moon itself, due to its appearance, constant threat of destroying the land of [[Termina]] and is almost impossible to stop during gameplay, is often considered one of the most haunting or antagonistic objects in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], not unlike the [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]].
*According to [[Yoshiaki Koizumi]], the idea of the Moon crashing to Termina comes from when he was daydreaming of what would happen if the [[wikipedia:Moon|real-world moon]] were to impact Earth, after creating the three-day time cycle for the game.
**Perhaps unsurprisingly, the scale of Termina's Moon is incredibly small compared to the real-life moon or [[Hyrule]]'s, as it appears to be only as large as a medium-sized meteorite. In ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'', it can be even smaller, as seen when used by [[Skull Kid]] and the [[Great Sky Fairy]].
**If the Earth's moon were to crash onto the planet from its perspective (if not breaking apart entirely), although indeed everything would be wiped out like its ''Zelda'' counterpart, the destruction would be far more catastrophic in comparison, as first the atmosphere is heavily disrupted, tides would cease and cause incredible flooding, and upon impact massive worldwide earthquakes and volcanic eruptions would occur alongside fiery rain of lunar fragments.
*In the original [[Nintendo 64]] version of the game, the Moon's eyes are similar to that of Majora's Mask's.
*Even when inside the Moon, in the Grassy Playfield, the dungeons, or the [[Final Boss]] arena against Majora's Mask, the earthquakes during the [[Final Day]] still occur, unless glitches or cheats are used to battle Majora at a prior day.
*Under normal circumstances, when inside the Moon, if the [[Bombers' Notebook]] were to be checked, the time will display as 7:45 AM, seemingly back on the dawn of the Final Day, though it is technically [[New Day]].
*In the promotional artwork of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'', the Moon, with its face facing to the east, has one of its eyes looking directly toward the viewer. This doesn't happen in any of its in-game appearances - mainline or spin-offs, as they remain completely still and facing forward.
*When Majora's Mask enters and possesses the Moon after detaching itself from the Skull Kid, its own eyes begin to eerily glow.
*Two separate models of the Moon exist in the original N64 version of ''Majora's Mask''. One is used for [[Termina Field]] and the [[Clock Tower]] with a slightly simplified face, while the other, a larger model with an even angrier face, is used for the rest of Termina, including [[Clock Town]].
*According the [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (Himekawa)|''Majora's Mask'' (Himekawa)]] manga, the inside of the Moon is the world of the Majora's soul, giving the likely reason why the Moon itself breaks apart when Majora's Mask is defeated in the game's climax.
*Due to the limitations of the Nintendo 64 hardware, the Moon's model simply changes sizes between the [[First Day|First]] and [[Second Day]] while remaining in the same position - half and full, to give the impression of it getting closer, likely because of the limited draw distance. But on the Final Day, however, it begins visibly and very slowly descending toward Clock Town in real time. This remains mostly the same in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] remake.
*If one uses cheating effects to make [[Link]] hover directly up the Moon, its model has no hit detection and he will simply go through it.[[File:HW Fierce Deity Link slicing Moon in half.png|thumb|301x301px|The Moon being utterly slashed down by Link in his Fierce Deity form as seen in ''Hyrule Warriors'']]
*Although this does not occur in ''Majora's Mask'' or its remake, in ''Hyrule Warriors'', [[Fierce Deity Link]] is capable of destroying the seemingly indestructible Moon entirely into two pieces with one extremely powerful vertical slice, when using [[Young Link]]'s Focus Slash, after being sent down by Skull Kid at a rather fast pace.
**Because the Fierce Deity is stated to be canon and Majora itself was defeated by this very transformation according to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]]'', some fans had wished this aforementioned special move be the ultimate outcome for the Moon in the mainline installment's ending.
*When Majora's Mask is defeated at the final battle, the Moon disintegrates into ashes much like Majora itself during the ending cutscene in ''Majora's Mask 3D''. However, in the original N64 version, due to limitations (though its top is indeed breaking apart), the Moon itself appears to simply disappear into the sky, seemingly returning to its proper orbit.
*The Moon's position and sometimes size in the sky when looked closely at every map across Termina is not consistent, due to graphical limitations.[[File:MM 4th Day Glitch Moon.png|thumb|268x268px|The Moon during the 4th Day glitch, believed to be the planned starting distance of when it begins its original 7-day course early in development]]
*If one performs the "[[Glitches in Majora's Mask|4th Day glitch]]" to enter the New Day and then Link looks directly up to the sky, the Moon will appear extremely far away (in reality, its model has simply become tiny). Since ''Majora's Mask'' was originally planned to have a seven-day cycle (168 hours) rather than three, it's rumored that the glitch itself brings Link to the very first day of the week's leftover data, with the Moon being in the original distance of when it begins its collision course by Majora's Mask's evil powers during the game's development, and thus the First, Second, and Final Days in the final release actually being days 5, 6, and 7, respectively, based on the scale of the Moon.
**If the aforementioned hover hack was used during this glitch, the heavily downscaled Moon is only slightly larger than Link in size.
**The "0th Day glitch", which makes the time stuck at 5:59AM just before the First Day and functions like the above counterpart, shows the Moon's model in a slightly smaller scale compared to said day, suggesting this was the Day 4 size of the original week planned in development.
**Interestingly and rather oddly, if Link were to enter the Moon to face Majora's Mask while the glitch is active, the sky and time of day in the Grassy Playfield will match as seen in Termina.

* The Moon's model in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' is very similar to that of ''Hyrule Warriors''.
* Contrary to popular belief, ''Majora's Mask'' is '''not''' the first piece of [[Nintendo]] media to depict an ominous moon with a face. The original ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_(game)|Star Fox]]'' of the [[starfox:Star_Fox_(series)|''Star Fox'' series]] released on the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] in 1993 has a hidden level known as "[[starfox:Out_of_This_Dimension|Out of This Dimension]]" where multiple moons with faces are seen in the background. Ironically, like the ''Zelda'' title when the Moon crashes, encountering this stage is the game's Bad Ending, as it is impossible to escape it.
