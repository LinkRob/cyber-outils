{{Term/Store|Cyclone|Cyclones|Series, TWW, TWWHD, PH|plural}},{{Exp Game|TWW, PH}}<ref>{{Cite|As you go forward, it would be wise to try not to get sucked up by any {{Color|TWWHD Red|cyclones}} you see upon the seas.|King of Red Lions|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH |publisher= Prima |page= 268}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Whirlwind|Whirlwinds|TLoZ|plural}}{{Exp Game|TLoZ}}<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= Whirlwind |game= TLoZ |page= 26}}</ref> and '''Waterspouts''',{{Exp Game|ZA}} are recurring [[object]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, whenever [[Link]] plays the [[Recorder]], a Cyclone will appear and [[Warping|Warp]] him to any [[Underworld]] [[dungeon]] he has completed.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, the wind deity [[Cyclos]] harnesses the power of cyclones. After his monument in [[Dragon Roost Island]] was destroyed, he dedicated his life using his control over cyclones to wreak havoc on all who encountered him, as explained by his older brother [[Zephos]].<ref>{{Cite|You want an example of it being a bad thing, then you should see my brother. It saddens me to say that my brother, Cyclos, is miffed about his monument here being broken, and now he spends his time creating cyclones to torment people with.|Zephos|TWW}}</ref> Link can find Cyclos in a giant cyclone throughout various islands of the [[Great Sea]]. The Cyclones themselves, unlike most [[Updraft|Updrafts]], are stationary but are more powerful and strongly resemble real-life tornadic [[wikipedia:Waterspout|waterspouts]]. If he sails close to it, Cyclos will draw him in, giving the Hero of Winds a slim chance to defeat him with his [[Bow]] before being blown away into another part of the Great Sea. If Link succeeds in shooting three [[Arrow]]s at Cyclos, he will calm down and be impressed with his skills, and teach him the "[[Ballad of Gales]]," allowing Link to use the deity's cyclones (in smaller scale) to [[warp]] to different islands.<ref>{{Cite|That's a heck of an arm you've got there! And what an eye! No one's ever been able to spot me in there before now! You obviously have mastery of the air. And now that we've established that you're quite the Wind Waker... Let's see if you can handle this!|Cyclos|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Waaaaa-hoooooooooooooo! In the hands of a Wind Waker like you, my adorable little cyclones will be as happy as can be! If you need to move like the wind over the ocean deeps, just call on my cyclones! Well, I guess I'll head for home now! See you on down the road!|Cyclos|TWW}}</ref>

==={{PH|-}}=== 
[[File:Big Cyclone PH.png|thumb|right|A larger Cyclone blocking the way to the Isle of Ruins, as seen in {{PH|-}}|236x236px]]
In {{PH}}, the smaller cyclones hurt Link's ship if he sails into them. They can be destroyed if Link shoots them with his [[cannon]] two times.

The bigger cyclones are rarer to find and mostly serve as obstacles to impede Link from accessing certain islands. To make the cyclones disappear, Link has to complete certain dungeons or obtain specific items, such as obtaining the [[Regal Necklace]] to eradicate the cyclone preventing entrance to the [[Isle of Ruins]]. If Link obtains the [[Cyclone Slate]] and has the necessary symbols from the [[Golden Frog]]s, the hero can ride a cyclone to warp to a different sector of the [[World of the Ocean King]] by drawing the corresponding symbol.

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{ZA|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:ZA Waterspout Sprite.png|frame|left]]
Waterspouts are obstacles in {{ZA}}. They form on the surface of [[Lake Boruna]], where they block [[Faust]]'s canoe from accessing [[Mystery Island]] and send it back to the dock near his hut. Zelda can only make the Waterspouts disappear if she has the [[Calm]] [[Spell]].
{{Noncanon|End}}

== Trivia ==

* If [[Link]] sails to a Cyclone without the [[Bow]] in {{TWW}}, he will be pulled into the center in about 5 seconds. When equipped with the Bow to shoot at [[Cyclos]], however, the amount of time is greatly increased, as the speed of the [[King of Red Lions]] being drawn in is slower.
* The Cyclone from the [[Flute|Recorder]] in the original {{TLoZ}} would be reused for ''[[mariowiki:Super Mario Bros. 3|Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' series]] to allow [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to warp between worlds.
* It is possible, especially with cheats from a device to allow infinite air, to make Link swim directly into a Cyclone in {{TWW}}. However, it does not suck the hero into the powerful vortex or send him flying to the air, unlike the King of Red Lions.
