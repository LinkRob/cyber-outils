{{Compendium/Extract|Vicious Sickle}}
{{Plural|BotW|Vicious Sickle}} are the primary {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}, who either randomly attack {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} on his journey or pose as {{Plural|BotW|Traveler|link}} to lure him in. Their crescent shape allows the wielder to quickly deliver injuries to their target, but they have a low durability.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} used by the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 171}}</ref>

Since they are made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Vicious Sickle}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and can be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
