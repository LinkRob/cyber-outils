{{Term/Store|Ternio Trail||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Ternio Trail'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Ternio Trail is a mountain path that paves through southeastern [[Eldin Canyon]], between [[Eldin (Region)|Eldin]] proper, [[Akkala]], and northern [[Lanayru (Region)|Lanayru]]. The Trail begins with a fork in the road that splits between Eldin to the north, and Akkala further east. The Trail is characterized by its fir trees, which gradually turn more scarce the further the path goes into central Eldin. The Eldin split leads to the [[Foothill Stable]], before continuing as a winding road that eventually leads to [[Goron City]] and ultimately [[Death Mountain]].

The travelers {{Term|BotW|Nat|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Meghyn|link}} can often be found on the Eldin split hunting for [[Hearty Truffle]]s.<ref>{{Cite|We're traveling all over to find hearty truffles. I know, I know—it's dangerous for two folks to travel on their own these days. You don't have to tell me twice! But when I think about how wonderful those truffles taste, all the worries and warnings melt away.|Nat|BotW}}</ref> They are sometimes attacked by [[Bokoblin]]s in the area.
