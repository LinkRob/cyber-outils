{{Manual|EoW|Snake Statue|A foreboding statue with a raised head and a coiled tail. Its cold eyes stare into the distance.<ref name="Snake Statue">{{Cite|A foreboding statue with a raised head and a coiled tail. Its cold eyes stare into the distance.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Snake Statues are large statues found in the [[Gerudo Sanctum]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]]. They cost one [[Echo]] energy to summon.
