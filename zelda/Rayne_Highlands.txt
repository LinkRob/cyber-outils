The {{Term/Store|Rayne Highlands||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Rayne Highlands'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Rayne Highlands}} are located in the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} on the western border of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. A small canyon cuts through the ridge. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Columns|link}} are nearby to the southeast.
