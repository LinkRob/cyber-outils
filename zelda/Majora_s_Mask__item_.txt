{{Term/Store|Majora's Mask|Majora's Masks|Series, MM, MM3D, BotW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}}, {{Term|Series|Object|link}}, and {{Term|Series|Boss|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 191 {{Exp Game|MM3D}}}}</ref><ref name="BotW Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Majora's Mask}},An eerie mask passed down from ancient times. It is the titular ancient spiky [[Mask]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' originally used by an ancient tribe that contains great dark powers similar to that of [[Ganondorf]] and various other villains in the franchise, although in later games it appears to be weakened or even powerless.

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
Majora's Mask serves as the main antagonist that [[Link]] must stop and defeat within the [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|game of the same name]], rather than [[Ganon]]. It is first introduced after the {{Term|MM3D|Happy Mask Salesman|link}} removes the curse that was placed on Link by {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Happy Mask Salesman}} explains that Majora's Mask is an ancient, powerful artifact that was used by an ancient tribe for its bewitching rituals, but the Mask's wicked power became so great that it was sealed away to prevent a great catastrophe. However, since the ancient tribe vanished, no one accurately knows the true nature of its power. The {{Term|MM3D|Happy Mask Salesman}} fears the great [[Terminan Apocalypse|devastation]] that could befall [[Termina]] if Majora's Mask is not quickly taken back from the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}} within three days.<ref>{{Cite|The mask that was stolen from me... It is called {{Color|MM3D Red|Majora's Mask}}. It is an accursed item from legend that is said to have been used by an ancient tribe in its hexing rituals. It is said that an evil and wicked power is bestowed upon the one who wears that mask. According to legend... the troubles caused by {{Color|MM3D Red|Majora's Mask}} were so great... the ancient ones, fearing such catastrophe, sealed the mask in shadow forever, preventing its misuse. But now, that tribe from the legend has vanished, so no one really knows the true nature of the mask's power... ...But I feel it. I went to great lengths to get that legendary mask.<br/>When I finally had it... I could sense the doom of a dark omen brewing. It was that unwelcome feeling that makes your hair stand on end. And now...<br/>that {{Color|MM3D Red|imp}} has it... I am begging you!<br/>You must get that mask back quickly or something horrible will happen!|Happy Mask Salesman|MM3D}}</ref> 

When the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}} donned the Majora's Mask, it transformed a world into the land of {{Term|MM3D|Termina}}, out of a combination of the evil within the {{Term|MM3D|Mask}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}}'s memories and delusions.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 36}}</ref>

For the vast majority of the game, the Majora's Mask itself is worn by {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}} on the top of the {{Term|MM3D|Clock Tower|link}}, drawing the {{Term|MM3D|Moon|link}} to {{Term|MM3D|Termina}}, eager to cause total destruction of the world.<ref>{{Cite|What could that troublemaker be doing atop the clock tower? I just hope he's not plotting another one of his no-good tricks...|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref> Although the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}} is unaware, Majora's Mask is gradually beginning to take complete control of his mind the moment he first wore it after stealing it from the Happy Mask Salesman, with his once harmless pranks becoming malicious acts of pure evil, therefore using him as a puppet.<ref>{{Cite|The power of the mask made him do it. It was too much for the Skull Kid to handle.|Tael|MM}}</ref> Prior to the game's events, {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}} used the great power of the Majora's Mask to prank and curse others around him, such as {{Term|MM3D|Kafei|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I was turned into this by a strange imp wearing a mask...|Kafei|MM}}</ref> In the [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (Himekawa)|manga]], it is also shown that Majora's Mask has the power to control other beings, as Skull Kid uses it to hypnotize [[Epona]].
[[File:Majora's Wrath 2.png|thumb|311x311px|Majora, the evil being behind the Mask]]
After the {{Plural|MM3D|Guardian Deity|link}} are eventually called once Link completes the world's four [[Dungeons in Majora's Mask|Dungeons]], Majora's Mask abandons {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}} and enters the {{Term|MM3D|Moon}} via its mouth so that it can push it through the {{Plural|MM3D|Guardian Deity}}' blockade and complete its destructive path.<ref>{{Cite|A puppet that can no longer be used is mere garbage. This puppet's role has just ended...|Majora's Mask (Item)|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I...I shall consume. Consume...Consume everything.|Moon|MM}}</ref> Link then proceeds to follow the Majora's Mask into the {{Term|MM3D|Moon}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well? You're going in, aren't you? ...We can't do anything down here. Let's go!|Tatl|MM}}</ref> Once [[Inside the Moon|inside]], he finds four {{Term|MM3D|Moon Children|link}} wearing the [[Boss]] remains and one additional lonely child under a large tree wearing Majora's Mask itself. If the hero gives all 20 of his non-transformation [[Mask|Masks]] to the four children (including the [[Giant's Mask]]), the child wearing the evil Mask will give him the [[Fierce Deity Mask|Fierce Deity's Mask]] as a special reward. He will then take Link to a room where the final battle takes place against the Mask. It comes in three increasingly more powerful forms. Its final form, Majora's Wrath, is the actual entity behind Majora's Mask's evil powers, called '''Majora'''.

After the young [[Hero of Time]], with the aid of {{Term|MM3D|Tatl|link}}, defeats Majora in a clashing battle, he returns to the land of {{Term|MM3D|Termina}} where the {{Term|MM3D|Happy Mask Salesman}} informs them that the evil inside the {{Term|MM3D|Mask}} has disappeared for good.<ref>{{Cite|Oh...So the evil has left the mask after all...|Happy Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref>Majora's Mask is last seen as the {{Term|MM3D|Happy Mask Salesman}} departs with it in his hands, with itself now nothing more than an ordinary mask.

According to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Encyclopedia|''Zelda Encyclopedia'']], although Link saved the day, as Termina was created by the combinations of the burdens of Skull Kid's heart and the evil powers of Majora's Mask, when the former became free and Majora itself being ultimately destroyed by Link, the world ceases to exist when the Hero of Time leaves the land to seemingly return back to [[Hyrule]] while searching for his missing [[Fairy]] friend, [[Navi]]. It's unclear whether this is canon due to the Encyclopedia being riddled with several errors.

[[File:Majora's Mask.png|thumb|right|304x304px|Majora's Mask (base form)]]
When the [[Final Boss]] battle of ''Majora's Mask'' begins, Majora's Mask, as its standard appearance, comes to life and grows hair-like tentacles. To damage and defeat it, [[Link]] must first stun it with any projectile by hitting it in the back. This will make it collapse to the ground, allowing Link to strike it with his [[Sword]]. Alternatively, Link can transform into {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link|link}} and use his {{Term|MM3D|Boomerang|link}} fins. When [[Targeting|Z-Targeting]] the {{Term|MM3D|Mask}}, the fins will fly behind it, hitting its back. Additionally, {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link}} can burrow into the flower designs on the ground, protecting him from any attack. This also allows {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link}} to hit the {{Term|MM3D|Mask}} as it flies over him. After Majora's Mask has taken enough damage, the four {{Term|MM3D|Boss}} Remains will begin to assist the Mask to attack he hero as well, shooting energy balls at him periodically, causing one [[Heart Container|Heart]] of damage if hit. These enemies can be defeated by shooting any type of {{Plural|MM3D|Arrow|link}} at them. Majora's Mask will also start shooting fire beams at Link, which can be reflected back into it or the Boss Remains with the {{Term|MM3D|Mirror Shield|link}}. Majora's Mask also uses its tentacles to perform a whirling attack. The attack can be blocked, but it can also be countered with a well-timed [[Spin Attack]], causing the Mask to become stunned, allowing the hero to strike it down.

[[File:Majora's Incarnation.png|thumb|right|295x295px|Majora's Incarnation (second form)]]
After the Mask form is defeated, it catches on fire and loses its tentacles, and then grows a set of arms and legs, as well as a tiny, one-eyed head with horns, becoming a humanoid shape. It also gains a young, feminine voice as well. This form of Majora's Mask will run very quickly around the room, pausing every now and then to perform a variety of dances, including the {{wp|Moonwalk (dance)|Moonwalk}}, {{wp|Kozachok}}, pirouettes, and {{wp|jumping jacks}} or to make taunting gestures at Link. Despite its seemingly mindless act, Majora's Incarnation's one and only attack is a sudden rapid-fire barrage of energy balls it fires from its hands at random times, which can be very difficult to block or dodge, and are the same as the Boss Remains'. To defeat this form, Link must strike the monster with either his Sword or [[Bow|Hero's Bow]] when it stops running and performs its dances. When hit, it will fall to the ground on its face and flail about. If the foe recovers without being hit, it will get up and taunt at Link. After being struck again with the sword while it is down, it will get up again in pain. During this part of the battle, {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Incarnation}} makes humorous clucking and whooping noises as it runs and dances, and emits a high-pitched screech whenever struck.

[[File:Majora's Wrath.png|thumb|right|307x307px|Majora's Wrath (true form)]]
After the final painful scream from {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Incarnation}} is heard, it stands up to assume its final form. With a loud heartbeat being heard, the muscles of its limbs begin to throb and inflate into an even more humanoid shape. A new demonic head then bursts forth from its neck, and whip-like tentacles shoot out from his hands, becoming {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Wrath}}, which is none other than the actual [[demon]] behind Majora's Mask's evil, known as Majora. To defeat {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Wrath}}, Link must use a projectile weapon such as [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]] to stun it while it is immobile. He can then strike it with his Sword. It often jumps from one side of the arena to the other to dodge attacks, wickedly laughing at Link. Its attacks are fast-hitting, but slow to wind up, giving the hero a window of opportunity to attack. {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Wrath}} attacks by whipping its tentacles in various ways, throwing dangerous giant spinning tops lined with spikes (when its health is low), grabbing Link and tossing him across the arena, and using a furious whipping attack with an accompanying shriek. Most of {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Wrath}}'s attacks, although not highly damaging, have a very long reach which allows it to attack Link numerous times in succession and can be difficult to dodge. When finally defeated, Majora unleashes one final scream in agony and the villain ultimately disintegrates into ashes in a blinding light as the room breaks apart, killing it, therefore winning Link the final battle and completing the game. In the ending cutscene leading to the credits, the Moon also disintegrates due to Majora's defeat, rescuing Termina altogether.

==={{MM|-}} (Himekawa)===
[[File:Majoramanga.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Majora in the manga]]
In an extra chapter of the {{MM|Himekawa}} it is explained that before the time setting of {{MM|-}}, there was an evil dragonlike creature who guarded an empty, timeless land -- a land neither living nor dead -- which fed on the desires of humans who had come to try and claim its armor and be granted their wishes. A traveler then came forward to the place where the monster lived and started talking with the creature, where he found that the monster developed a wish of its own; for time to pass, that it may rest at last and end its loneliness. The man then proceeded to pull out his bongo and started playing. The monster went into a trance and started dancing furiously. The traveler played for three days straight, and as a result, time was born. The beast dropped dead on the fourth day, as do all creatures for whom time passes. The traveler then took its magical armor, but instead of wishing upon it, he carved it into a Mask in an attempt to seal the beast's power inside the Mask forever. The Mask was named Majora after the beast. The Mask had a spirit because Majora's evil power lingered on inside the Mask seeking a host, not resting until its wish was granted.

==Trivia==
*During the battle with {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Incarnation}}, it can be heard "singing" part of the [[Level 9|Level-9]] ([[Death Mountain]]) theme from ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' when spinning around.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= KERO|published= July 22, 2012|retrieved= |url= http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DGzlVe8eSVs|title= A Little Fact About Majora's Incarnation|site= Youtube|type=}}</ref>[[File:MM Fierce Deity's Mask Model.png|thumb|210x210px|The Fierce Deity's Mask is another notorious mask known to rival the Majora's Mask]]

In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'', its eyes also glow in dark areas just like its counterpart does.
**Although not required to complete ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', the Fierce Deity Link form, due to its extreme raw power, makes the final battle against Majora's Mask significantly easier, and therefore is commonly used by many players if obtained. However, despite its usage by the [[Hero of Time]] is stated to be canon according to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]]'', some fans discouraged this particular [[transformation]] to defeat the game's main antagonist, due to it being capable of ending [[Bosses in Majora's Mask|boss]] fights with virtually no effort (primarily due to its [[Sword Beam]] ability).

*Majora in its Incarnation and Wrath forms is on-par in size with that of [[Ganon]] as seen in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', as well as [[Odolwa]]''.''[[File:MM3D Nendoroid Link 6.jpg|thumb|206x206px|Official ''Majora's Mask 3D'' merchandise of Link wearing the Majora's Mask]]
*Though Link himself never wears the titular mask in ''Majora's Mask'', promotional merchandise of its [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Nintendo 3DS remake]] exists of the main protagonist wearing the evil mask.
*According to the manga adaptation of ''Majora's Mask'', everything in Termina, its actions of controlling [[Skull Kid]], and destroying the world using the Moon is all just a game to Majora, making the evil demon under the mask nothing less than a psychopath (and possibly more inherently evil than not only Ganon, but even [[Demise]]), as can be observed in-game. It also depicts the inside of the Moon as the world of its soul, giving a reason why the Moon disintegrates upon its defeat in the [[Nintendo 64]] game and especially ''Majora's Mask 3D''.
*Majora is one of the few [[Final Boss|Final Bosses]] of the franchise to have multiple forms that is not Ganon.
*The clucking sounds of Majora's Incarnation as its runs around during the final battle are actually edited sounds of [[Cucco|Cuccos]] clucking.
*Although commonly believed by fans that only [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]] are effective on Majora's Wrath, any other type of [[Arrow]], including regular ones, can also stun the villain as it attacks. [[Sword]] blows would stun any of Majora's forms as well before taking damage from subsequent hits.
*Interestingly, the shape of the Majora's Mask is that of a heart with spikes, referencing its corruption to users' minds upon wearing it, specifically the Skull Kid.
*The [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_MajorasMask_Presence.wav roaring sound of Majora's Mask] is a slowed down and distorted version of a [https://soundeffects.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Ideas,_ANIMAL,_SCREAM_-_T-REX:_SCREAM,_DINOSAUR_01 stock sound effect] from Jurassic Dinosaurs Sound Effects Library. This would later be reused in the [[metroidwiki:Metroid_(series)|''Metroid'' series]] for the [[metroidwiki:Hunter Ing|Hunter Ing]] when they are destroyed in ''[[metroidwiki:Metroid_Prime_2:_Echoes|Metroid Prime 2: Echoes]]''.

==Nomenclature==
''Mujura'', the Japanese name for Majora, was created by Takaya Imamura, designer of the {{Term|Series|Mask}} and art director for the original game. He created the word by combining Imamura, his last name, with the title of the 1995 film ''[[wikipedia:Jumanji|Jumanji]]'', according to [[Eiji Aonuma]].<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= Imamura, who made the design for Majora’s Mask, invented the word by combining characters from the movie title Jumanji and from his name Imamura. Takaya Imamura was the art director for the N64 version of Majora’s Mask.|name= Eiji Aonuma|url= http://nintendoeverything.com/aonuma-talks-majoras-mask-majora-and-termina-name-origins-lots-more/|title= Aonuma talks Majora’s Mask – Majora and Termina name origins, lots more}}</ref>
