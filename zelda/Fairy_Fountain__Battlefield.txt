==Overview==
Fairy Fountain: Battlefield is the last side quest needed in order to unlock the [[Characters in Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|character]] [[Great Fairy|Great Fairies]]. This can be found in the [[Akkala]] region after completing the previous three side quests, [[Fairy Fountain: Grassy Plains]], [[Fairy Fountain: Deep Snows]], and [[Fairy Fountain: Desert Sands]].

Unlike the previous Fairy Fountain: Grassy Plains and Fairy Fountain: Desert Sands, this side quest has a series of time conditions, depending on which monster the [[Characters in Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|character]] is fighting. The time conditions and the order they go in are below:

# Defeat the [[Blue Moblin]](2:00)
# Gather [[Rupee|rupees]](Timer continues)
# Defeat the Blue Moblin(1:30)
# Gather rupees(Timer continues)
# Defeat the [[Black Moblin]](1:00)
# Gather rupees(Timer continues)
# Defeat the [[Ice Moblin]](1:00)
# Gather rupees(Timer continues)
# Defeat the [[Electric Moblin]] and key enemies(precisely the Black Moblin;1:30)
# Gather rupees(Timer continues)
# Head to the Targeted Position(No timer)
# Defeat [[Mija]](No timer)

Once all the minibosses are defeated, the character will fight [[Mija]]. Once Mija is defeated, the side quest will be complete, along with obtaining the Great Fairies. If this was completed on the first try, the player will get an additional: 1 [[Ethereal Stone]], 5 [[Fairy|Fairies]], and 5 [[Hearty Bass]].
