==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Farosh's Claw}} originate from {{Term|BotW|Farosh|link}}, no longer containing its {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} powers.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Term|BotW|Farosh}} can be found flying through {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Lake Floria|link}}, or the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} shoots at {{Term|BotW|Farosh}}'s hands with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Farosh's Claw|display=Claw}} will be surrounded by light and fall to the ground onearby.

When used in {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Farosh's Claw}} will always result in {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}. However, if they are {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cooked}} with critters, the result will be an {{Term|BotW|Elixir|link}}. They can also be used at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} as an ingredient for {{Term|BotW|Light Yellow Dye|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Farosh's Claw}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}:
