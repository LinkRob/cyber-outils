{{Term/Store|Funday School||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Funday School}} was referred to as the '''Schoolhouse''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 91}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given both in-game and in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Funday School}} is a school run by two teachers, {{Term|TMC|Tina|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Dina|link}}.

Once {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} has obtained the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}} and seen the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, he can go inside the {{Term|TMC|Funday School|display=School}}, where he can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Berry|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Leila|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Jim|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Joel|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Erik|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Harrison|link}}.

In the schoolyard, {{Term|TMC|Mini Link|link}} can enter a pathway that has a large {{Term|TMC|Rock|link}} at its end. This {{Term|TMC|Rock}} requires the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}} to be moved, and behind it are three {{Plural|TMC|Chest|link}} containing red {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}, as well as a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}.

==Trivia==
*{{TWW}} features a similar school, the {{Term|TWW|School of Joy|link}}, run by {{Term|TWW|Mrs. Marie|link}}. She shares a similar hairstyle to {{Term|TMC|Tina}} and {{Term|TMC|Dina}}'s.

==Nomenclature==
The name "Funday School" is a {{Wp|portmanteau}} of fun and {{Wp|Sunday school}}.
