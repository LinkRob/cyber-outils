The {{Term/Store|Kah Yah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kah Yah Shrine}}<br/>Quick Thinking}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Quick Thinking''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Kah Yah Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Palmorae Ruins|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. Initially, it is buried underground and must be uncovered by completing the "{{Term|BotW|A Fragmented Monument|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. To enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must find all the scattered {{Plural|BotW|Monument Shard|link}} near the {{Term|BotW|Palmorae Ruins}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you really want to help, help me find the missing fragments, will you? They'll be scattered all around. I think the fragments had the same kind of script carved into them... If you find a fragment, please draw a {{Color|BotW Red|picture}} of it and bring it to me. I'll be here finishing my examination of this stone monument!|Garini|BotW}}</ref> Upon finding them, he must capture a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of them with the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} and return to {{Term|BotW|Garini|link}}, who is capable of reading the instructions detailed upon the full monument.<ref>{{Cite|If you find a fragment, please draw a {{Color|BotW Red|picture}} of it and bring it to me. I'll be here finishing my examination of this stone monument!|Garini|BotW}}</ref>

After reading the instructions, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that two people are meant to bow in reverence upon the two nearby {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! Thanks to you, I've deciphered the entire passage! "When the two find their place and kneel in reverence, the shrine will reveal itself." ...Ah! I...haven't the foggiest what that's supposed to mean.|Garini|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} and {{Term|BotW|Garini}} kneel on the {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal}}, {{Term|BotW|Kah Yah Shrine}} will rise from the ground, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} entry. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kah Yah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kah Yah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kah Yah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Two long corridors flank the interior of {{Term|BotW|Kah Yah Shrine}}, inside each of which a floating platform can be seen moving back and forth along the corridors' empty centers. {{Plural|BotW|Floor Switch|link}} can be found at the end of each corridor, which control portcullises at opposite sides of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Floor Switch}} can be held down either by {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} or by one of the many {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}} placed in the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.

The left corridor's {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}} connects to an antechamber with a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}}. Using the {{Term|BotW|Small Key}} to bypass a locked door, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can approach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Kah Yah}}, who will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Kah Yah|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Kah Yah}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Kah Yah|BotW}}</ref>

A second stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Claymore|link}} can be accessed by holding down the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}} on the right side of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.
