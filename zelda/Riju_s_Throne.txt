{{Term/Store|Riju's Throne||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 320}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace's Throne Room|link}}, which makes up the first floor of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. As seen in the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} sat on this {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|display=Throne}} 100 years ago. Since then, a set of steps have been added to the front, which {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} uses in order to sit upon her {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|display=Throne}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first meets {{Term|BotW|Riju}}, she will be seated upon her {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|display=Throne}}. She sits on her {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|display=Throne}} during the afternoon, where she is guarded by {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}}.
