{{Term/Store|Glass Torch|Glass Torches|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.

==Location and Uses==
Glass Torches are a type of {{Term|CoH|Torch|link}} that help {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} navigate through dungeons by lighting their way through the dark and clearing away stumps. However, they offer the brightest light out of all the torches and can reveal the location of enemies and NPCs, along with pointing the way out to the dungeon's next floor.<ref>{{Cite|Provides amazing light and reveals the location of enemies and NPCs. Destroyed when you take damage. Can burn stumps and light up braziers.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> Like all glass items, Glass Torches will never wear out but they will break immediately upon getting hit.

They can be found either from a red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} for 40 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}}.
