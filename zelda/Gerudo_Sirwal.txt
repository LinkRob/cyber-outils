==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Sirwal}} can be acquired from {{Term|BotW|Vilia|link}} on top of the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}}, who sells the sirwal along with the rest of the set for 600 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} as part of the "{{Term|BotW|Forbidden City Entry|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. It can also be purchased by itself from {{Term|BotW|Saula|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} for 180 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}. It provides {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with 3 defense, and if the entire {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} is worn, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have level 1 {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance}}, even though the pieces of the set cannot be upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
