The {{Term/Store|Scale|Scales|Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 137"/>

==Location and Uses==
The Scale is the thirteenth item in the game's {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You returned the {{Icon|LANS Necklace}}{{Color|LANS Pink|necklace}} and got a {{Icon|LANS Scale}}{{Color|LANS Pink|scale}} of the mermaid's tail. How will you use this?|N/A|LANS}}</ref> It is obtained from [[Martha]] the Mermaid, who promises Link one of her Scales from her tail in return of her missing [[Necklace]].<ref name="E 137"/> The Scale is pivotal to complete "[[The Mourning Mermaid]]", an unfinished Statue that [[Schule Donavitch]] sculpted in [[Martha's Bay]].<ref>{{Cite|Iz zat zee Mermaid scale? I can't use it now. I have to werk on zis drawing. You should go finish zee mermaid statue for me.|Schule Donavitch|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You put the missing scale in the mermaid statue!|N/A|LADX}}</ref> After placing the Scale onto the statue, it will move aside and reveal a cave where Link finds the final item in the Trading Sequence, the [[Magnifying Lens]].<ref>{{Cite|An artist once asked me to pose for him, and he wanted a scale, too... Can the legend of the Magnifying Lens be true...? |Martha|LADX}}</ref>
