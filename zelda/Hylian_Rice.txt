==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Hylian Rice can be found by cutting grass in [[East Necluda]]. It's also sold by establishments like the [[Coral Reef]], the [[East Wind]], the [[General Shoppe]], and [[The Slippery Falcon]] and the travelling merchant [[Yammo]]. Eating it restores one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. After completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "[[Curry for What Ails You]]", Link can give [[Lester]] [[Goron Spice]] at any time to receive Hylian Rice. At the [[Kochi Dye Shop]], Hylian Rice can be used to make White [[Dye]].

When [[Cooking]] in a [[Cooking Pot]], Hylian Rice is required to make [[Crab Risotto]], a [[Crab Omelet with Rice]], [[Curry Pilaf]], [[Curry Rice]], [[Fried Egg and Rice]], a [[Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl]], [[Gourmet Meat Curry]], [[Gourmet Poultry Curry]], [[Gourmet Poultry Pilaf]], a [[Meat and Rice Bowl]], [[Meat Curry]], [[Meaty Rice Balls]], [[Monster Curry]], [[Monster Rice Balls]], [[Mushroom Rice Balls]], [[Mushroom Risotto]], [[Poultry Curry]], [[Poultry Pilaf]], a [[Prime Meat and Rice Bowl]], [[Prime Meat Curry]], [[Prime Poultry Curry]], [[Prime Poultry Pilaf]], [[Salmon Risotto]], [[Seafood Curry]], [[Seafood Fried Rice]], [[Seafood Paella]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], [[Vegetable Curry]], [[Vegetable Risotto]], and [[Veggie Rice Balls]].

It can be created into many things

Rice + Meat = meaty rice balls 5-26 hp

Rice + Bird Egg = Fried egg and rice 4-10 hp

Rice + fish = Seafood Rice Balls 4-10 hp

Rice + herb = Veggie Rice balls 2-full recovery+20hp (only if big hearty radish x 4 is used) health is temporary

Rice + mushroom= Mushroom rice balls 3-full recovery+16hp (only if big hearty truffle x 4 is used) health is temporary

Rice + fruit = simmered fruit 3-full recovery+16hp (only if hearty durian x 4 is used) health is temporary

Rice + snail = seafood skewer  4-full recovery+12hp (only if hearty blue shell snail x 4 is used) health is temporary

for any other inquiries, go to https://www.guideofthewild.com/cook
