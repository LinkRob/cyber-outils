{{Term/Store|Graceful Goron|Graceful Gorons|Series, OoA|plural}} are characters in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Graceful Gorons are professional dancers in [[Labrynna]] who exist in both the Past and the Present. The first generation [[Goron]] has red skin while the fifth generation has blue skin. They refer to themselves as "Graceful Dancers" and they both hold the [[Goron Dance]] minigame in a cave at [[Rolling Ridge]].<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! I am the first generation of Graceful Dancers! This is my dance hall.|Graceful Goron|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Welcome! I am the fifth generation of Graceful Dancers! This is my dance hall.|Graceful Goron|OoA}}</ref>

[[Link]] must dance to win the [[Brother Emblem]] from the Graceful Goron in either the Present or the Past to gain access to the rest of Rolling Ridge, and later on the [[Mermaid Key]] from the Graceful Goron in the Past once he shows him the [[Letter of Introduction]].<ref>{{Cite|One must know our dance if he is to be called a Goron. If you dance well, I'll give you a Symbol of Brotherhood!|Graceful Goron|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Graceful Goron downstairs was bragging about how he found a beautiful key in a waterfall grotto. I'm so jealous.|Goron|OoA}}</ref>
