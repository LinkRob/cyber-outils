{{Term/Store|Nayru's Pearl||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TWWHD|Nayru's Pearl}}, named after {{Term|TWWHD|Nayru (Goddess)|link}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Golden Goddess|link}}, is the third and final of three {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}} that {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} obtains. It is initially kept by {{Term|TWWHD|Jabun|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Greatfish Isle|link}}; however, when {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} travels there, he finds that it has been destroyed by {{Term|TWWHD|Ganondorf|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A great water spirit named Jabun once lived here, but no sign of him remains...|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> Fortunately, {{Term|TWWHD|Jabun}} had sensed him coming and fled to {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But fear not! Jabun was able to flee this island before it was attacked. He is in a safer abode now.|Quill|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} and the {{Term|TWWHD|King of Red Lions|link}} travel to his cave after the stone door is broken down by {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} using the {{Term|TWWHD|Cannon|link}} and {{Plural|TWWHD|Bomb|link}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Jabun}}, after some convincing from the {{Term|TWWHD|King of Red Lions}}, then gives {{Term|TWWHD|Nayru's Pearl}} to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The one I have brought with me has no connection to the legendary one. And yet I sense great promise in the courage that this one possesses.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I give to you the guidepost to the gods.|Jabun|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} later places the {{Term|TWWHD|Nayru's Pearl|display=Pearl}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Southern Triangle Island|link}} to make the {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}} rise from the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}.
