The {{Term/Store|View Warriors||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} menu is an interface in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|View Warriors|Gallery|HW}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|View Warriors}} menu is where the player can display all unlocked {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}}.<ref name="Gallery"/> The player can view the {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} while wearing different {{Plural|HWDE|Costume|link}}, as well as wielding different {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon|link}}. The player can rotate or zoom in/out on the selected {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} using the right stick. The plus button can be pressed to reset the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} to their original position. The {{Term|HWDE|Costume}} worn by the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} can be changed by pressing the X button. The player can change the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} wielded by the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} by pressing the A button. The {{Term|HWDE|View Warriors}} can be accessed from the {{Term|HWDE|Gallery|link}}.
