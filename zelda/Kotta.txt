{{Term/Store|Kotta||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kotta|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kotta}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She carries a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}.

The first time {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kotta}}, she expresses disbelief that the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}} was stolen.<ref>{{Cite|I can't believe the {{Color|BotW Blue|Thunder Helm}} was stolen...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> Before she can continue, she is interrupted by {{Term|BotW|Liana|link}}, who yells at her for speaking of their problems to outsiders.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Kotta! Stop blabbing to outsiders about our problems!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> Upset that she is in trouble again, {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} blames {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* I'm in trouble again... Th-this is all your fault, you know!|Kotta|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has started the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} is afraid she will have to go searching on a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} again, as she gets sick while riding them.<ref>{{Cite|I'll have to go out with a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} and search again, but... Ugh... It makes me feel so sick to surf around with one.|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Liana}}, he will hear her asking {{Term|BotW|Leena|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} if they have seen {{Term|BotW|Barta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, I haven't seen Barta. Where's she gone off to?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leena}} reveals that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} told them two days ago that she was going to investigate the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}}, and she has not returned since then.<ref>{{Cite|She said she was going to scout the thieves' hideout, but then she left before we could say anything. Now that I think about it, she's been gone for two days. I wonder what happened to her...|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Liana}} is afraid that {{Term|BotW|Teake|link}} will make them run 100 laps around the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} once she hears that they lost {{Term|BotW|Barta}}.<ref>{{Cite|Uuugghhh. As if we aren't busy enough searching for the chief's heirloom, now we have to find {{Color|BotW Blue|Barta}} too... If the captain hears about this fiasco, she'll punish us for sure! Probably 100 laps around the Gerudo Desert...again.|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} asks what they should do about this,<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Oh, no. What should we do?|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> to which {{Term|BotW|Liana}} snaps that she is thinking about it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|I'm thinking about it!}}|Liana|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}} to find that {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} is anxious about {{Term|BotW|Barta}} being in trouble again.<ref>{{Cite|Ahhh... What do I do... Poor Barta is in trouble again...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> When he approaches {{Term|BotW|Liana}}, she will be shocked to hear that {{Term|BotW|Barta}} has yet again failed to return to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whuh... Whah...<br/>{{Big|WHAAAAT?!}}<br/>Barta didn't come back AGAIN?|Liana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} explains that she went to investigate the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton|link}} in the southwest of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid this time she went to investigate the {{Color|BotW Blue|leviathan bones}} in the southwest...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leena}} reminds {{Term|BotW|Liana}} that a {{Term|BotW|Molduga|link}} lives in that area, so she did not think {{Term|BotW|Barta}} would even approach.<ref>{{Cite|But... Everyone knows that area is the domain of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga}}, so who would figure she'd even go close to there?|Leena|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} agrees, adding on that the {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}} in that part of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|display=Desert}} would make it difficult for {{Term|BotW|Barta}} to last with their gear.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, nobody would dare! And not only that, but it's an {{Color|BotW Blue|extremely hot zone}}, so we wouldn't last long there in our gear...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> This only angers {{Term|BotW|Liana}} further, as she believes they let {{Term|BotW|Barta}} walk into a dangerous situation.<ref>{{Cite|You... You {{Big|IDIOTS!}} That scatterbrain wouldn't even realize she was walking into danger! I mean, you know that, right?!|Liana|BotW}}</ref> She resigns herself to the fact that {{Term|BotW|Barta}}, {{Term|BotW|Leena}}, and {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} are never going to change.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh... I guess Barta will be Barta, and you all aren't going to change.|Liana|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, "{{Term|BotW|The Search for Barta|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} while the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is active, she shares a rumor about a {{Term|BotW|Tera|link|display=fairy}} living in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I guess I can't sit on this any longer... There's a rumor that a fairy lives in the {{Color|BotW Blue|leviathan bones}} southwest of town...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> She thought {{Term|BotW|Barta}} would be interested in this, but since a {{Term|BotW|Molduga}} lives in the area, she never thought she would actually investigate it alone.<ref>{{Cite|I thought it was something that Barta might be interested in... But that area is also known as the home of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Molduga}}... Going there alone would be unthinkable...|Kotta|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Barta}} has returned to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}, {{Term|BotW|Kotta}} notes that she seems to feel guilty about the mess she caused.<ref>{{Cite|Well, it looks like Barta at least feels bad about what happened this time... What a mess, though.|Kotta|BotW}}</ref> However, she is jealous that someone who is all over the place like {{Term|BotW|Barta}} is still popular with men.<ref>{{Cite|And you know the worst part? Even a vai like Barta, who's all over the place, is popular with the voe. I don't get it.|Kotta|BotW}}</ref>
