{{Term/Store|Elrora||Series}} is the tribal leader of the [[Watarara]] tribe that appears in the bonus chapter ''Rouru of the Watara'' in the {{OoT|Himekawa}}.{{Fact}} She bears the form of a dove, and is a strict, but caring leader. She has a son named [[Rouru]], who is the heir to the tribe.

==Biography==
Elrora is seen a few times in the manga searching for her lost son, [[Rouru]]. It is seen that she loves him deeply and believes in his return. She appears above [[Lake Hylia]] staring into the depths of the lake and looking for her missing son.<ref>{{Cite|Guufo... I've been standing here to see if I could see him...|Elrora|OoT (Himekawa)}}</ref> After speaking with Lord Guufo, Elrora decides that even though her heart is breaking, she can't let a single [[Watarara]] put the tribe in danger, accepting that they may need to leave Rouru behind.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= We may have to leave him behind.|book = OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |volume= 2|page= 173}}</ref> <ref>{{Cite Book|quote= As a mother, my heart is breaking... but as chieftess, I cannot let any single Watarara put the whole tribe in danger.|book = OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |volume= 2|page= 173}}</ref> Despite this, [[Guufo]] promises to find Elrora's son, even if he must forfeit his own life.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ...I'll find Rouru... or die trying!|book = OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |volume= 2|page= 173}}</ref> As he flies away, Elrora wonders where her son is, and begs him to answer if he can hear her voice.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Rouru... where are you? If you can hear your mother's voice... please, give me an answer...|book=  OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |volume= 2|page= 174}}</ref> Later on, she sees [[Link]] and [[Rouru]] falling out of the sky after defeating a pair of [[Flare Dancer]]s, and rushes to catch them as they fall. She finally reunites with her son and thanks Link for his safe return.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... Thank you so much for caring for my son.|book = OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |volume= 2|page= 137}}</ref>
