The {{Term/Store|Stamina Scroll||Series, ALBW}} is an item in {{ALBW}}.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Stamina Scroll}} is an item that increases the size of {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}}'s [[Energy Gauge]], allowing him to use more items at a time, and stay [[Wall Merging|Wall Merged]] longer. It is found as the main item in the [[Ice Ruins]] on the floor 3B after going through the dungeon and pulling a lever revealing the {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Chest|link}}.

One of the {{Plural|ALBW|Treasure Chest}} within the Ice Ruins containing a {{Term|ALBW|Silver Rupee|link}} can only be reached by Wall Merging with the extended Energy Gauge. This is the only treasure that requires the {{Term|ALBW|Stamina Scroll}} to reach, so it is otherwise an optional item.

The {{Term|ALBW|Stamina Scroll}} can also be used by [[Shadow Link]] during a [[Shadow Link Battle]].
