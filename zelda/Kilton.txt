{{Term/Store|Kilton||Series, BotW}} is a recurring character in the ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|Legend of Zelda]]'' series of games, debuting as a monster-crazy vendor in a colourful hot air balloon who sells monster-related items and buys monster parts for outrageous prices in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kilton|N/A|BotW}}</ref> 

In {{TotK}}, Kilton decides to settle in [[Tarrey Town]] and create a display diorama of monster sculptures to educate people about the better sides of monsters, and it is revealed that he has a younger brother, [[Koltin]], who wishes to become a [[Satori]]. {{Cite|Kilton|N/A/|TotK}}{{Cite|Koltin|N/A|TotK}}

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kilton}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Monster|link}} fanatic who owns the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} traveling shop where he sells {{Term|BotW|Monster}}-related wares. He can only be encountered during the night, when the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}} appears. Initially, the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}} can only be found at {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link}}.

When first spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} will be startled by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s sudden appearance, though he becomes disappointed when he realizes he is simply a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|HOOOOOOOYAG!}} ... Hmm? Oh. It's just a {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}}.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks who he is, {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} introduces himself, and asks if Link came because he heard rumors of a {{Term|BotW|Monster}} {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Who are you?] Me? My name is {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}. I suppose you're here because you heard I was opening a monster shop, and you wanted to see it for yourself.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> Kilton further explains that he is still preparing for the grand opening of the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Monster shop?] Yes. I'm opening a monster shop called {{Color|BotW Blue|Fang and Bone}}. But I'm still preparing for my grand opening.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> To prove his point, he provides {{Term|BotW|Link}} with his business card, which advertises the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's my business card...|Kilton|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|- Fang and Bone -<br/>Come see ol' Kilton for the most {{Color|BotW Blue|monstrous}} deals in all of Hyrule!|N/A|BotW}}</ref> However, he requests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} return the card to him because it is the only one he has.<ref>{{Cite|I'm...going to need that card back. It's the only one I have.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kilton}} then hesitantly asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is interested in {{Plural|BotW|Monster}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway... Do you, uh... Are you into monsters?|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} affirms that he likes them, {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} then asks how much he likes them.<ref>{{Cite|[I like them.] How, uh... How much do you like them?|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} claims to adore them, {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} interjects and states that he loves {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} more than {{Term|BotW|Link}} does.<ref>{{Cite|[I ADORE monsters!] {{Big|I LOVE MONSTERS MORE THAN YOU DO!}}|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> After taking a moment to calm himself, {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} apologizes to {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his outburst.<ref>{{Cite|... I...uh... Sorry! I did it again.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he loves researching {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} so much that he decided to open the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}}, which is dedicated to them.<ref>{{Cite|I just love the field of monster research so much that I decided to open a shop all about monsters! I call it...{{Color|BotW Blue|Fang and Bone}}!|Kilton|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kilton}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he travels all over {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, though he is unsure where he should visit next.<ref>{{Cite|I travel all around Hyrule in search of rare and exciting monster parts. But I'm not sure which village I should visit next.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> During his musings, he discusses the benefits of visiting {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Kakakriko Village}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|Hateno Village}}? I'm off!|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> Before he leaves, he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he is sure they will meet again in a village during the night.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure we'll meet again in some {{Color|BotW Red|village}} during the {{Color|BotW Red|night}}. Fare thee well!|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kilton}}, along with the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}}, will then disappear.

Following this encounter, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} on the outskirts of all the villages he mentioned starting at 9:00 PM. The spots where {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} appears are marked by a {{Term|BotW|Sign|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fang and Bone<br/>(only open at night)|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

During their second meeting, {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} will once again be surprised to see {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|HOOOOOOOYAG!}} Ahhh...it's just you. {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} explains that if {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings him {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}}, he will give him {{Plural|BotW|Mon}} in exchange.<ref>{{Cite|Here's the deal: if you bring me {{Color|BotW Blue|monster parts}}, I'll exchange them for {{Color|BotW Red|mon}}.|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}} is the only business that accepts {{Plural|BotW|Mon}}, which {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} considers to be exclusive.<ref>{{Cite|I'm the {{Color|BotW Blue|only business}} in Hyrule that accepts {{Color|BotW Red|mon}} as payment!
