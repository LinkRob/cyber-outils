{{Term/Store|Winder|Winders|Series, ALttP, TMC, PH, ST, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 212}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Winder}} were referred to as '''Firewinders''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 61}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}{{Note|Winders are referred to as '''Moldolas''' in {{Guide|ST|Prima}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ST |publisher= Prima |page= 33}}</ref> However, because Winder's name was officially stated in {{E}}, this name is no longer considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Plural|ALttP|Winder}} appear in various {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} {{Plural|ALttP|Dungeon|link}} in {{ALttP}}. Their movement is unpredictable, as they change directions randomly whenever they hit an object.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Plural|TMC|Winder}} appear in various {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}} in {{TMC}}. They can set fire to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} if touched.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Plural|PH|Winder}} are electrically charged and travel along walls in {{PH}}. Touching one will shock and damage {{Term|PH|Link|link}}. 

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Plural|ST|Winder}} can be defeated by firing an {{Term|ST|Arrow|link}} at their heads in {{ST}}. They can also damage {{Plural|ST|Heatoise|link}}, as they are weak against electricity.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Plural|ALBW|Winder}} go unnamed in {{ALBW}}. They appear in a single room of the {{Term|ALBW|Dark Palace|link}} alongside several fire-breathing {{Plural|ALBW|Red Kodongo|link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} must avoid the {{Plural|ALBW|Winder}} while finding his way through the labyrinthine room, where the walls are only visible while shrouded in darkness.
