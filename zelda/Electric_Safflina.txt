==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Electric Safflina}}
{{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina}} can be located on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} and in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. They can be found growing near the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} under the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} uses {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina}} in a meal made in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, the resulting dish will grant him {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}} when eaten.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> In addition, {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina}} can be used to create {{Term|BotW|Light Yellow Dye|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}.

As flowers, {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina}} can be used to make the following {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}}:
