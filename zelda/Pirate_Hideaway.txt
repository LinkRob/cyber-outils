The {{Term/Store|Pirate Hideaway||Series}} is the second dungeon in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Entrance to the Hideaway==
It requires 500 [[Rupee]]s to open the entrance.

==Themes and Navigation==
In the dungeon, [[Tingle]] must collect three pirate items as a test before he can see the Captain. Several pirates and [[Barkle]] are encountered here. This dungeon's boss is [[Captain Stalfos]].
