==Location and Uses==
Naboris's Emblems are exclusive to the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} from {{TCB}} DLC Pack. They can be obtained by completing {{Term|BotW|Keive Tala Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri Shrine|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Kihiro Moh Shrine|link}} and speaking to their respective {{Plural|BotW|Monk|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Naboris's Emblem.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> They're commemorative symbol of one's completion of a lightning trial.<ref name="Inventory"/>

At the end of the final Shrine Link completes, the Monk will tell him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Naboris...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> When he arrives, Link will hear the voice of a Monk. In exchange for Naboris's Emblems, he will be given a chance to fight a manifestation of his fear in a realm of memories, the [[Illusory Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the one who approaches this Divine Beast... In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial. In exchange for Naboris's Emblems... you will be granted the chance to tread through the realm of memories. Those who lack determination will find this trial unforgiving. Do not take this place lightly, nor dismiss it as merely a world within your mind. The truth is much deeper than you know.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> He will then find himself inside Vah Naboris and will face off against {{Term|BotW|Thunderblight Ganon|link}} once more. After completing the trial, Link can fight Thunderblight Ganon at any time with no cost.
