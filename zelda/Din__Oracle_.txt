==Biography==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Din|Disguised as a dancer, the strong-willed Oracle of Seasons quietly assures peace in Holodrum, but an hour of evil draws near...}}
Din appears in {{OoS}}, where she is a talented and famous dancer in the land of [[Holodrum]].<ref>{{Cite|Din is a popular dancer. Just seeing her dance raises one's spirits!|Hylian|OoS}}</ref> As the Oracle of Seasons, Din is responsible for regulating the seasons of Holodrum. Although she has the power to change the seasons at any time or place, she refrains from doing so, allowing them to flow naturally. She travels with a [[Troupe of Performers]] throughout the land as a means of evading [[Onox|General Onox]], who intends to imprison her in order to throw the natural order into chaos.<ref>{{Cite|Hya, ha, ha! I've found you, Din, Oracle of Seasons! You hid yourself well but you cannot escape Onox, General of Darkness!|Onox|OoS}}</ref> Din is also aware of the location of a mystical tool known as the [[Rod of Seasons]], which allows [[Link]] to change the seasons at will. 

Din's personality is an energetic one, and she loves to have people dance with her. At the start of {{OoS|-}}, Din has Link dance with her.<ref>{{Cite|Come! Won't you dance with me?|Din (Oracle)|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Don't be shy! Dancing will be fun!|Din (Oracle)|OoS}}</ref> She is also known to be a caring individual who helps others.<ref>{{Cite|Din has been caring for you since she found you. She's strong-willed but very kindhearted.|Hylian|OoS}}</ref> After Onox's downfall, Din will quite often suggest that she and Link dance together again. She also does so in {{OoA}}, and in a [[Linked Game]] after [[Ganon]] has been defeated.

{{Term|TMC|Din (Oracle)}} is said to be a famous dancer from {{Term|TMC|Holodrum|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|She's looking for a house in Hyrule to move into. She is a famous dancer from the land of Holodrum.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} impersonates {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, she appears alongside the other {{Plural|TMC|Oracle|link}} in a room on the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}, where she will tell {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} that they are looking for somewhere to live.<ref>{{Cite|We need to find a nice, quiet place where we can settle down.|Din (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref>

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Gorman|link}} arrives in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} seeking to rent out his {{Term|TMC|Vacant House|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow... I just had the best idea! I can rent out my house for some extra cash!|Gorman|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} speaks with {{Term|TMC|Gorman}}, he will ask {{Term|TMC|Link}} to let him know if he finds a woman willing to rent the {{Term|TMC|Vacant House|display=House}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm trying my hand at real estate. Turns out, it's a total pain. Like right now, I'm looking for a tenant, you see? But the thing is, I can only rent to a woman, single occupancy. ... If you find anyone who can rent this place, would you let me know?|Gorman|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} has the option to show any of the three {{Plural|TMC|Oracle}} the {{Term|TMC|Vacant House}}; if he chooses {{Term|TMC|Nayru (Oracle)|link}} or {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)}}, {{Term|TMC|Din (Oracle)}} remarks that things are different with only two of them remaining.<ref>{{Cite|Things just aren't the same around here now that it's just the two of us.|Din (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref> If the second {{Term|TMC|Vacant House}} is built and given to another {{Term|TMC|Oracle}}, {{Term|TMC|Din (Oracle)}} is left as the only one of the trio without a home, and will remark that she is lonely.<ref>{{Cite|Being all alone is...well, lonely. I'm going to find myself a nice home, too.|Din (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref>

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} shows either of the {{Plural|TMC|Vacant House}} to {{Term|TMC|Din (Oracle)}}, she will move in; he can visit her afterwards, whereupon she will thank him and offer to give him {{Term|TMC|Din's Charm|link}} whenever he has an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}! Thanks for coming! I love this place, and I owe it all to you! If you ever need a charm, I'll give you another, so just ask!|Din (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref> When consumed, the {{Term|TMC|Charm (The Minish Cap)|link}} makes {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s attacks stronger and turns his tunic (and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}}) red for the duration of the effect.<ref>{{Cite|You got {{Color|TMC Red|Din's Charm}}! Your strength will be boosted for a while!|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can also {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with {{Term|TMC|Din (Oracle)}},<ref>{{Cite|You would like to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Gladly!|Din (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref> resulting in a {{Term|TMC|Joy Butterfly|link}} appearing in the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|link}}; when it is collected, {{Term|TMC|Link}} is able to shoot {{Plural|TMC|Arrow|link}} more quickly.<ref>{{Cite|You caught a {{Color|TMC Red|Joy Butterfly}}! You can now shoot arrows faster than before.|N/A|TMC}}</ref>
