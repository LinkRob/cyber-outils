{{Term/Store|Ashei||Series, TP, TPHD}} is character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 276 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref> She is a member of the {{Term|TPHD|Resistance|link}}.<ref name="TP Guide 13"/> She is a strong young woman with raven-black hair and impressive knowledge of the mountains. She is the one who aids {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} in investigating the evil at {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|TPHD|Ashei}} was raised in the mountains by her knight father, who taught her the art of war.<ref>{{Cite|I grew up in the mountains with my father, who was a knight in his own right. He taught me the arts of war as though I was his son.|Ashei|TP}}</ref> She is seen carrying a {{Term|TPHD|Sword|link}} and later a {{Term|TPHD|Bow|link}} and a {{Term|TPHD|Quiver|link}} of {{Plural|TPHD|Arrow|link}}. Due to her rather masculine upbringing, she took on a somewhat blunt personality and a rough way of speaking.<ref>{{Cite|Of course, lessons in common courtesy were not part of the regimen, so forgive me if I come off as rude, yeah?|Ashei|TP}}</ref> As she has a derogatory view of the {{Plural|TPHD|Soldier|link}} of {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}},<ref>{{Cite|And here I was, thinking Hyrule was empty of men of valor...|Ashei|TP}}</ref> she is rude to {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} at first.<ref>{{Cite|Listen, sorry I was so nasty the other day. I thought you were like those sorry excuses for Hyrule soldiers...but I thought wrong.|Ashei|TP}}</ref>

She is interested in finding out why evil seems to control the mountain of {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Snowpeak, the mountain to the north of Hyrule... It's unlike any mountain I've ever known. I don't know much yet, but things happen there that happen on no other mountain I know of. ...Evil controls it... As soon as I know something more, I'll tell you. Until then, you should stay away, yeah?|Ashei|TP}}</ref> and later tries to figure out the reason for the beast sightings around {{Term|TPHD|Zora's Domain|link}}.<ref name="north"/> When {{Term|TPHD|Link}} goes to meet her there, she gives him {{Term|TPHD|Ashei's Sketch|link}}, which depicts a {{Term|TPHD|Yeti|link}} holding a {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The only clues we have right now are the stories of the Zoras and my sketch. It's not much, I know.|Ashei|TP}}</ref> After {{Term|TPHD|Fishing|link}} and catching a {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} is able to follow the fish's strong scent through the blizzard encompassing {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak}}.

When {{Term|TPHD|Link}} enters {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle|link}}, just as he is about to get overwhelmed by enemies, {{Term|TPHD|Ashei}} and the rest of the {{Term|TPHD|Resistance|link}} arrive and clear a path, allowing {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to proceed to the last few floors of {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle}} and eventually reach {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf|link}}.

After {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule}} returns to peace, {{Term|TPHD|Ashei}}, {{Term|TPHD|Shad|link}}, and {{Term|TPHD|Auru|link}} can be seen at the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Grove|link}} exploring its ruins.
