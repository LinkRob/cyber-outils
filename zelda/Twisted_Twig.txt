{{Term/Store|Twisted Twig|Twisted Twigs|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 142"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Twisted Twig}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Ruins|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Illusory Mansion|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Twisted Twig}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Palace Noir|link}}''', ''"Halved attack and defense!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Twisted Twig}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Lone Labyrinth|link}}''', ''"Don't drop the pot!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Twisted Twig}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Lone Labyrinth}}''', ''"Adventure in the dark!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Twisted Twig}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Twisted Twig}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Grim Temple}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Twisted Twig}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Twisted Twig}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Fire Blazer|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Serpent's Toga|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 142"/>
