The '''Flux Construct III''' is a [[Sub-Boss]] in {{TotK}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium|name= Flux Construct III|number=162|desc= A construct composed of interconnected blocks, made by the Zonai with advanced techniques and magic. This model has increased defenses to protect its vulnerable core. Can change shape in order to block attacks. To provide sufficient energy to power its gigantic form, this construct contains a massive store of Zonai charge.|locs= North Akkala Sky Archipelago, Central Hyrule Depths|loot= [[Zonai Charge]], [[Large Zonai Charge]], [[Big Battery]]}}
Flux Constructs III are [[Construct|Constructs]] made of floating cubes held together by [[Zonai]] magic. They can change forms, including a humanoid shape, a giant cube, and a flying form that can be [[Ascend|Ascended]] through.

Flux Constructs III attack similarly to [[Flux Construct II|Flux Constructs II]] but rearrange their cubes to make removing their weak point with [[Ultrahand]] more difficult. When defeated, they drop [[Zonai Capsule|Zonai Capsules]], [[Zonai Charge|Zonai Charges]], and [[Flux Construct III Core|Flux Construct III Cores]].
