{{Term/Store|Gerudo||Series, BotW}} is the written and spoken language of the {{Plural|Series|Gerudo|link}}.<ref name="Sarqso">{{Cite|By the way, in Gerudo, "thank you" is "sarqso." SARQSO!|Guy|BotW}}</ref> It was first confirmed as such in the {{HH}},<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author=Johnson, David|published= |retrieved=March 8, 2017|url=http://www.zeldauniverse.net/zelda-news/ocarina-of-times-gerudo-font-in-hyrule-historia-available-for-download/ |title=Another Zelda alphabet cracked, Gerudo font made available - Zelda Universe|site=Zelda Universe |type=}}</ref> though it first appeared in {{OoT}}. Consisting of 26 different characters, it is directly based on the modern Latin alphabet. It appears at the [[Gerudo's Training Ground]] and [[Spirit Temple]] (where it appears written in order and its entirety on the stone to the {{exp|Master Quest: right|left}} for the stairs at the entrance) in {{OoT|-}}, sparsely at the [[Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|Arbiter's Grounds]] in {{TP}}, a stairway in [[Ganon's Castle]] in {{OoT3D}} and throughout [[Gerudo (Region)|Gerudo]] encampments and [[Gerudo Town]] in {{BotW}} & [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]. 

The accompanying spoken language itself was first introduced in {{BotW|-}}, where it is used throughout the Gerudo Region by both the Gerudo and travelers of the region.

==Trivia==
*The "y" in the Gerudo Typography bears resemblance to the "y" found in the medieval runic alphabet, or {{Wp|Medieval runes|fuþark}}.
*The usage of only two single numbers (1 and 5) suggests that the Gerudo numerical system is a simplified variation of {{Wp|Roman numerals}}. It is possible that counting would consist of the two available numbers being used consecutively to reach the desired number. For example, 6 would be written "5 1", ten would be written "5 5", and fifty five would thus be written with eleven consecutive 5's.
*In the original Japanese version of {{HH}}, the transcription table identifies the top and bottom parts of the {{Term|Series|Gerudo (Language)}} semi-colon as a period and comma, respectively.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= {{HH}}|publisher= Shogakukan |page= 90}}</ref>
*The title at the top of the Gerudo Alphabet says "Geldo's Typography" in Gerudo symbols. Geldo may have been the original intention for the name's translation.
