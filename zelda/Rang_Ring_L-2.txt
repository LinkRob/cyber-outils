The {{Term/Store|Rang Ring L-2||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Rang Ring L-2<br/>Boomerang damage {{Icon|OoS Up Arrow}}{{Icon|OoS Up Arrow}}|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Rang Ring L-2 can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it increases [[Boomerang]] damage.<ref name="Description"/> The Rang Ring L-2 can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
