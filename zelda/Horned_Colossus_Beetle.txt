The {{Term/Store|Horned Colossus Beetle||Series, SS}} is [[Beedle]]'s pet beetle in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 116}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
After [[Strich]] settles in the [[Bug Rock]], [[Link]] can talk to Beedle at [[Beedle's Island|his island]] during nighttime to discover that his Horned Colossus Beetle has gone missing.<ref>{{Cite|But I would be remiss if I didn't let you know of the weight on my heart... My precious {{Color|SS Blue|Horned Colossus Beetle}} has gone missing, along with his cage... It's a tragedy that shatters a man to the core... 'Twas such a rare insect, and so very...friendly to boot.|Beedle|SS}}</ref> If Link flies to Bug Island, he will learn that Strich has obtained the bug.<ref>{{Cite|Well... Let's just say I managed to land a legendary {{Color|SS Blue|Horned Colossus Beetle}}. It's pretty much a miracle that I found it! I can't stop laughing or smiling. I'm so happy, my face hurts!|Strich|SS}}</ref> However, he will only let Link have it after playing a game in which he must catch 10 [[Bug]]s in under three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|Well then, go get 10 insects of my choosing! I'll be timing you. You've got to get 10 insects within {{Color|SS Red|three minutes}}. If you somehow do it, I'll give the {{Color|SS Blue|Horned Colossus Beetle}} back.|Strich|SS}}</ref> Once Link succeeds, he must return the Horned Colossus Beetle to his owner to receive five [[Gratitude Crystal]]s. Moreover, Beedle will allow Link to buy any item from his [[Beedle's Airshop|airshop]] at half price.<ref>{{Cite|To thank you, you can choose any single item here and I will sell it to you at half price.|Beedle|SS}}</ref>
