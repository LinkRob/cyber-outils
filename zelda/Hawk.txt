{{Term/Store|Hawk|Hawks|Series|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=63 {{Exp Game|TP}} }}</ref>{{Name Ref|BotW}}

==Characteristics==
==={{TP|-}}===
{{Plural|TPHD|Hawk}} can be summoned in {{TP}} by blowing into {{Term|TPHD|Hawk Grass|link}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= There are areas of the land where special whistle grass grows. If you are near the grass, press A to pick up and whistle with the grass. There are two types of special grass, one for horses and one for hawks.|game= TP|page= 21}}</ref> They can be used to fetch items and attack enemies. {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} can talk to {{Plural|TPHD|Hawk}}, who will tell him if there are any secrets in the area.

{{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} is first taught how to use a {{Term|TPHD|Hawk}} by {{Term|TPHD|Jaggle|link}} while living in {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}}, before going on to use one to knock down a nest of {{Plural|TPHD|Hylian Hornet|link}} so that {{Term|TPHD|Hanch|link}} can get some {{Term|TPHD|Bee Larva|link}} for his wife {{Term|TPHD|Pergie|link}}, and to retrieve {{Term|TPHD|Uli|link}}'s stolen cradle from a {{Term|TPHD|Monkey|link}}.

When {{Term|TPHD|Link}} later returns to {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village}} as {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}}, he will be spotted by {{Term|TPHD|Hanch}}, who mistakes him for a monster and summons a {{Term|TPHD|Hawk}} to attack him. {{Term|TPHD|Hanch}} continues to do this whenever he spots {{Term|TPHD|Link}}, until {{Term|TPHD|Link}} manages to sneak up behind him and scare him off. If {{Term|TPHD|Link}} then summons a {{Term|TPHD|Hawk}} via howling, it will apologize for attacking him.

{{Term|TPHD|Rusl|link}} later summons a {{Term|TPHD|Hawk}} when he comes to {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s aid inside {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle|link}}, getting it to retrieve an {{Term|TPHD|Arrow|link}} that had been shot by a {{Term|TPHD|Bokoblin|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Main|Islander Hawk}}
A sub-species of {{Plural|BotW|Hawk}} called {{Plural|BotW|Islander Hawk|link}} appear in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|These carnivorous birds live in plains or highlands. They use their sharp talons and beaks to catch and eat small animals and other birds. They soar calmly through the sky and search for prey from above.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Like most {{Plural|BotW|Bird|link}} they can be hunted by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} for food and will drop {{Plural|BotW|Raw Bird Thigh|link}} when killed.
