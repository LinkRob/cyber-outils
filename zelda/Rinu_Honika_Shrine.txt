The {{Term/Store|Rinu Honika Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Rinu Honika Shrine}}<br/>Block the Blaze}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Block the Blaze''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika Shrine}} is found in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}} to the south of {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond|link}}. It is one of three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} accessed as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers|link}} preparing to train at {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond}}, just like {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} did 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|Do ya know why {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}} chose this place to train, brother? ...Neither do we! But us Goron Blood Brothers are gonna find out by completing the training ourselves!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Heehl|link}} points out the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} on top of the {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond|display=Pond}},<ref>{{Cite|You see that {{Color|BotW Red|glowing ring}} in the lava? Think of it as a target, and...|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> sharing their plan to stand on the {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} at that spot.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|stand on the lava, brother}}!}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must safely fall through the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}. This can be accomplished by using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} to lift a nearby steel crate on the shore and drop it in the center of the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|display=Ring}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to glide into it without touching the {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}}. Alternatively, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the metal crates to push around the rocks near the shore, creating a path to the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}. After doing so, the {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika Shrine}} will rise from beneath the ground nearby. Upon entering the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is offered the trial by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Rinu Honika. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Rinu Honika|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika Shrine}} features a winding walkway over {{Term|BotW|Lava}} that leads to the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika}}, at the very end. Along the walkway are columns that continuously release streams of {{Term|BotW|Fire}}, requiring {{Term|BotW|Link}} to maneuver around them or to otherwise partly eliminate the columns that the streams are mounted on. One column can be broken down by bombing the cracked block beneath its base, and another column by removing its steel block support by using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}}. This steel block can then be used to block the streams of fire ahead. All of these obstacles can also be avoided by wearing the complete {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Set|link}}, provided all of its pieces have been upgraded at least twice.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can also use the steel blocks to then cross a pool of {{Term|BotW|Lava}} and reach a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Stone Smasher|link}}. Following the columns is a gateway barricaded by more fire streams. A second steel block that lies in the lava nearby can be used to stack with the first steel block, thereby creating a safe passage for Link to slip by unharmed.

At the final stretch of the walkway is a platform obstructed by multiple {{Term|BotW|Fire}} streams, serving as the final obstacle before the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. The platform can be manipulated using the nearby gyroscopic device. By tilting the platform's side upward, it is possible to use the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to glide over to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} meets the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika}}, he will be commended his resourcefulness and rewarded with one of {{Plural|BotW|Rudania's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of the hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Rudania's Emblem.}}|Rinu Honika|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to collect all three of {{Plural|BotW|Rudania's Emblem}} to reveal a new challenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Rinu Honika|BotW}}</ref>

If this is the first {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} visits during "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song}}", {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika}} tells him that two of {{Plural|BotW|Rudania's Emblem}} remain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Rinu Honika|BotW}}</ref> If it is the second {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} he visits, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will tell him that one remains.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Rinu Honika|BotW}}</ref> If it is the third and final {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} he visits, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will tell him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Rudania...}}|Rinu Honika|BotW}}</ref>
