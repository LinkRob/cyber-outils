==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} was written by {{Term|BotW|Finley|link}}. Before {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} appeases {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, she keeps the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} in the {{Term|BotW|Seabed Inn|link}}. While she wants to deliver it at the {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes|link}}, her mother {{Term|BotW|Kodah|link}} has banned her from going.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh! I'm so mad at her! I need to go to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Bank of Wishes}} to send a letter...but Mother won't let me go because of that water monster.|Finley|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Finley}} can be found with the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} on the {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes}}, where she asks it to carry her thoughts.<ref>{{Cite|Carry my thoughts...to him...|Finley|BotW}}</ref> When she finds out {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a traveler,<ref>{{Cite|If you're a traveler, that's lucky for me!|Finley|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Finley}} requests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} follow the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter|display=Letter}} to see who picks it up.<ref name="Love Letter"/> Once this is done, she wants him to return to her and tell her about the person.<ref>{{Cite|Once you do that, please report back to me so I can find out what kind of person he is...|Finley|BotW}}</ref> Agreeing to do so begins the "{{Term|BotW|Special Delivery|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Finley}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is ready before she throws the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}}.<ref>{{Cite|Excellent! OK, I'm going to throw the letter in now! Are you ready?|Finley|BotW}}</ref> If he says he is, she warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the container that holds the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter|display=Letter}} breaks easily,<ref>{{Cite|These letter containers {{Color|BotW Red|break easily}}, so please be careful.|Finley|BotW}}</ref> at which point she casts it down the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}}.

As the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} drifts down {{Term|BotW|Zora River}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must break obstacles that have appeared in its path. It can also get stuck, at which point he must correct its course. If the container is broken or {{Term|BotW|Link}} loses sight of it, he must return to the {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes}} to inform {{Term|BotW|Finley}}.<ref>{{Cite|You lost sight of my {{Color|BotW Red|love letter}}, didn't you?|Finley|BotW}}</ref> Having predicted this, she made a copy of her {{Term|BotW|Love Letter|display=Letter}}.<ref>{{Cite|Luckily, I thought something like that might happen, so I made an extra.|Finley|BotW}}</ref>

Eventually, the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} will arrive at the camp of {{Term|BotW|Sasan|link}}. He was previously traveling to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, but he stopped when he noticed the first of {{Term|BotW|Finley}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Love Letter}}.<ref>{{Cite|I was on my way to Zora's Domain... but a {{Color|BotW Red|love letter}} in this inlet caught my eye.|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that he has been corresponding with {{Term|BotW|Finley}} by giving his replies to travelers heading to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|The writer seems to be a lovely Zora named Finley. I've been corresponding with her by giving my replies to travelers heading to {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}.|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s encouragement, {{Term|BotW|Sasan}} decides to go to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}}, where the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter|display=Letter}} says {{Term|BotW|Finley}} is waiting.<ref>{{Cite|She's waiting in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}. That's what her letter says, anyway. So I'll achieve two goals at the same time and train up my muscles on the way there! I'll get super beefy! Yeah, that's the ticket! I'll run all the way to the domain!|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Sasan}} leaves, the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} disappears with him.
