The '''Force Field Ring''' is an item featured in the episode "[[Doppelganger]]" of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. As its name suggests, when the ring is worn, it creates a barrier that protects its wearer from any attacks. [[Link]] obtains this item after he defeats a [[Moblin]] that was wearing it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Huh, left a souvenir. A force field ring! Just what I always wanted for Christmas.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref>

Later on in the episode, when Link and [[Princess Zelda]] are pursuing [[Zelda's Clone|Zelda's evil clone]], the young hero uses the Force Field Ring after he jumps off a ledge to protect themselves from the [[Octorok|Octoroks']] attacks while Link [[zap]]s them with his [[Crissword|sword]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Uh-oh, Octoroks! Time for a force field. Hang on, princess!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> After Link and Zelda land safely, the force field disappears.
