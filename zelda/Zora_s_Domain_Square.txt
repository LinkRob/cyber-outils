{{Term/Store|Zora's Domain Square||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Find Muzu in {{Color|BotW Red|Zora's Domain square}} to find out where to get them.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Zora's Domain Square}} was referred to as the '''Central Plaza''' in {{CaC}}. However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher=  |page= 277}}</ref>}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square}} is located in the center of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. It features {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|link}}, a memorial for the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is a statue of the former Zora Champion. Her name was Lady Mipha.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> There are several paths that branch off from the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square}} to other parts of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|}}. Two staircases at the front of the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square|display=Square}} lead up to the second level. It also features an entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Seabed Inn|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Marot Mart|link}}, a combination of the {{Term|BotW|Coral Reef|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Hammerhead|link}}.

Several {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} pass through the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square}} throughout their routines. {{Term|BotW|Marot|link}} is in the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square|display=Square}} at all hours, where she will attempt to beckon customers into {{Term|BotW|Marot Mart}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪Welcome to Marot Mart!♪|Marot|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Keye|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tumbo|link}} spend part of their day running around the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square|display=Square}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} will be in the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square|Square}} inspecting the damage to {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}}.<ref>{{Cite|The damage is minimal...|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} has been appeased, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} will also visit {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}} at night. During this time, {{Term|BotW|Laruta|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square|display=Square}}, where she will give "{{Term|BotW|The Ceremonial Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.
