The {{Term/Store|Spin Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
The Spin Ring is received from the [[Mayor Plen]] at [[Lynna City]] after telling him the [[Plen Secret]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee|Rupees]]. When worn, it allows [[Link]] to perform two [[Spin Attack|Spin Attacks]] in a row.<ref name="Description"/> The Spin Ring can be obtained in {{OoS}} by telling the secret received from the [[Great Fairy]], or by transferring it by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
