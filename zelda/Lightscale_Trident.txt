The {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} is a variant of the {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear|link}} used by the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} before her death during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> It belongs to the {{Term|BotW|Zora Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a group of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref> Even though {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} preferred to use her healing abilities, it is said that she was talented with this {{Term|BotW|Spear}} as well.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} had the story of the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} written on the {{Term|BotW|History of the Zora, Part Four|link}}, one of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument|link}} found throughout the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} was made as a gift for {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} by {{Term|BotW|Dento|link}} to celebrate her birth.<ref>{{Cite|To celebrate her birth, the smithy Dento presented Mipha with a gift: a mighty spear called the Lightscale Trident.|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Seggin|link}} was the one who taught {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} her skill with the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|display=Trident}}.<ref>{{Cite|Under Seggin's instruction, Mipha honed her skills, and her radiance grew alongside her skill with the Lightscale Trident.|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} did not return after the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}, the people of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} mourned her. They tried to send the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} down {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}} in an attempt to put her {{Term|BotW|Spirit}} to rest.<ref>{{Cite|As a way of offering her soul repose, they tried to send the Lightscale Trident drifting down the Zora River|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref> However, the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|display=Trident}} began to glow, and {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}'s voice was heard.<ref>{{Cite|But when they did, the trident began to glow, and Mipha's voice rang loud and clear for all Zora to hear:|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref> She told the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} that she was one with the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}}, and they should forget their grief and focus on remembering her.<ref>{{Cite|"The Lightscale Trident and I are one... Abandon your grief and know joy once again. Do not cry...just remember."|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref> As a result, the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} celebrate the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival|link}} and honor the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} on the anniversary of the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}.<ref>{{Cite|And so, keeping to her request, one the day of the Great Calamity...the day that Mipha passed from this world...the Zora venerate the Lightscale Trident and remember their brave princess. Such is the origin of the Champion Festival.|History of the Zora, Part Four|BotW}}</ref>

However, if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks with {{Term|BotW|Trello|link}} while "{{Term|BotW|The Ceremonial Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} is active, he will admit that the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival}} uses a replica of the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} instead of the real one.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|ceremonial trident}} is used in a festival to honor Champion Mipha...|Trello|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Champion Festival's {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}... Well, it's a fake.|Trello|BotW}}</ref> This replica, the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident|link}}, is nearly identical to the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}}, but it is not as strong or durable.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ceremonial Trident}}<br/>A spear modeled after the Lightscale Trident wielded by the Zora Champion Mipha. They may be identical in appearance, but this spear's strength and durability are inferior.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} after taming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} and saving {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} from flooding. {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} will gift the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|display=Trident}} to him as a reward and symbol of their friendship.<ref>{{Cite|Link! I must reward your efforts! Now then! I implore you to collect the treasure inside that chest over there. It was cherished by Mipha. A memento of sorts. I would like you to have it as a token of our friendship.|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref> After the cutscene, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can collect the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} from inside a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}}. If the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} breaks, {{Term|BotW|Dento}} will remake it if {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches him at the {{Term|BotW|Hammerhead|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}... I somehow felt it had disappeared from this world. Shall I remake it for you?...|Dento|BotW}}</ref> To do so, he requires a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}, and five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|In that case, I will need a {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora spear}}, {{Color|BotW Red|one}} {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|five pieces}} of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}.|Dento|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} is made of metal, and therefore it can conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if it is equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. It will also sink if it is dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but it can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. Finally, it will not burn if it is exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
In ''The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom'', the Lightscale Trident can be obtains by completing the [[Glory of the Zora]] side quest.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20260215101345/https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Glory_of_the_Zora</ref> To complete that quest, a Zora Spear (with nothing fused to it) and other items are needed.
