{{Term/Store|Magtail|Magtails|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are enemies in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Magtail|Figurine|TWW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Tingle
|quote1= Oh!  A '''Magtail'''!  His weakness is water.  Attack him when he's curled up into a ball!
}}

Magtails slither around rooms waiting for [[Link]] to come close. Upon recognizing him, they stretch out their mandibles, trying to slice Link down. When charging its attack, its eye becomes vulnerable to attacks from the Sword or ranged items, such as the [[Grappling Hook]].<ref>{{Cite|Their eyes are their only weakness.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> Touching its mandibles when it attacks deals half a [[heart]] of damage. Touching any other part of a Magtail will also hurt Link. Once attacked, the Magtail curls into a ball that can be either destroyed by continuing attacks, or lifted. Once rolled, Link can carry the Magtail around and use it to press switches.<ref>{{Cite|Magtails can be picked up and carried around when they've rolled into a defensive position.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> [[Pot#Water Pot|Water Pots]] can also be used to stun the Magtail. The same effect can be achieved by shooting it with an [[Ice Arrow]]. Link can also defeat a Magtail in one hit by using a [[Parry]] attack.

{{Figurine|TWW|Magtail}}

==Trivia==
*Magtails bear a resemblance to [[Gohma#The Wind Waker|Gohma]], the boss of the [[Dragon Roost Cavern]], although they are much smaller. Magtails share the large mandibles, hard exoskeleton, physical appearance, coloration and lone vulnerable eye with the gargantuan boss.
*Magtails seem to be fiery relatives of [[Moldorm]]s, which are known as "Tail" in Japan.
