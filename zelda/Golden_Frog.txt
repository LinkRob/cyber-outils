{{Term/Store|Golden Frog|Golden Frogs|Series, PH|plural}} are animals in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 54}}</ref> 

==Overview==
{{Plural|PH|Golden Frog}} are placed in several locations throughout the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|display=sea|link}} in {{PH}}. There are six in total, and finding all of them will make sea travel much faster. They jump in front of the ship, and {{Term|PH|Link|link}} can fire the {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}} at them.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page=13}}</ref> If the shot hits, the {{Term|PH|Frog}} will, provided {{Term|PH|Link}} is carrying the {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate|link}}, show hoist a sign with a {{Term|PH|Symbols|section=Cyclone Slate Symbols|display=Symbol|link}}. This symbol can then be drawn onto the {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate}} and {{Term|PH|Link}} will then be {{Term|PH|Warping|display=warped|link}} via cyclone to the location the symbol was learned. To gain this ability, one must find {{Term|PH|Golden Chief Cylos|link}} on the {{Term|PH|Uncharted Island|link}}.

==Golden Frog Locations==
[[File:goldenfroglocations.gif|left|thumb|The locations of all six Golden Frogs.]]
*'''Northwestern Sea''' has one {{Term|PH|Golden Frog}} between {{Term|PH|Bannan Island|link}} and {{Term|PH|Isle of Gust|link}}.
*'''Northeastern Sea''' has one {{Term|PH|Golden Frog}} southwards of the {{Term|PH|Isle of the Dead|link}}.
*'''Southwestern Sea''' has one {{Term|PH|Golden Frog}} south of {{Term|PH|Mercay Island|link}} and another north of {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}.
*'''Southeastern Sea''' has one {{Term|PH|Golden Frog}} north of {{Term|PH|Dee Ess Island|link}} and another northwest of {{Term|PH|Isle of Frost|link}}.
