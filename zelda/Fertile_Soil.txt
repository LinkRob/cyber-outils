The {{Term/Store|Fertile Soil||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The [[Fertile Soil]] is the reward for completing the [[Gnarled Root Dungeon]], and is guarded by [[Aquamentus]]. It is the first of the eight [[Essence of Nature|Essences of Nature]] collected by Link. Its description text reads: "Seeds scattered across bountiful lands are nourished in this Fertile Soil!"<ref>"Seeds scattered across bountiful lands are nourished in this [[Fertile Soil]]!" – N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]])</ref>
