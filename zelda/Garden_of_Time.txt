TITLE : Garden of Time

== Features And Overview ==
The Garden Of Time covers most of the surface of the [[Great Sky Island]]. [[King Rauru]] says it was once the site of rites and ceremonies conducted by [[Hyrule Kingdom]]. It was originally on the [[Surface]] but thanks to an unknown event, it was raised into the sky along with the Great Sky Island.
