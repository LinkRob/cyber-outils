The {{Term/Store|Swamp Fishing Hole Man||Series, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM3D}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Fishing Hole Man}} runs the {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Fishing Hole|link}} located next to the {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Shooting Gallery|link}}. He will share secrets about {{Term|MM3D|Fishing|link}} with {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} if talked to. He will also mention that certain {{Term|MM3D|Fish|link}} can be caught on that day. The {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Fishing Hole Man}} will sometimes mention his younger brother, the {{Term|MM3D|Fishing Hole Man|link}}, and vise versa.<ref name="Bro"/><ref>{{Cite|Huh? Oh. Yeah. My big bro mans the Swamp Fishing Hole. Haven't seen 'im in years, though. Hey. If ya see 'im, tell 'im I said...hey.|Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref>

Just as in {{OoT|-}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Fishing Hole Man}}'s headgear can be hooked with the {{Term|MM3D|Fishing Rod|link}}, revealing that he is wearing a toupée. By doing so, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must give him every {{Term|MM3D|Rupee|link}} currently in his {{Term|MM3D|Wallet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bwaaahh?! Ya scared the livin' daylights outta me! How dare you!|Swamp Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That tears it. I ain't lettin' ya off the hook that easy. I demand...what's the word...oh yeah! Reparation! Gimme all the Rupees in your wallet!|Swamp Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref> If he has no {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Fishing Hole Man}} will grudgingly forgive the offense.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh. Awkward. Didn't know ya were hard up. Penniless. Broke. Figures. I'll let it go, but just this once. Mind yer manners from now on!|Swamp Fishing Hole Man|MM3D}}</ref>

The {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Fishing Hole Man|link}} bears resemblance to the {{Term|MM3D|Man from Trading Post|link}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Man from Curiosity Shop|link}}. When {{Term|MM3D|Link}} mentions this, however, he will deny it, saying that he has no idea who {{Term|MM3D|Link}} is talking about.
