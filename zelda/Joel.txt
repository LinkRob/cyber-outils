{{Term/Store|Joel||Series, TWW, TWWHD, TMC}} is a recurring character in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258 {{Exp Game|TWW}} & 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
Joel is the older brother of [[Zill]] and the son of [[Abe]] and [[Rose]]. He likes to jump a lot and admires [[Link]] for his jumping abilities. He waves around a stick, and at the start of the game  he can be found on the rocks near the walkway between the two halves of [[Outset Island]]. Later in the game, if Link had given Rose more than one pet pig, her two sons will admit to having eating them, saying they were delicious. Rose tries to deny it, and hushes her sons hurriedly.<br/>

{{Figurine|TWW|Joel}}

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Joel}} is a child that lives in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} in {{TMC}}. He is first seen listening to {{Term|TMC|Marshall|link}}'s story during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, where he asks if he will see the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} if he is on his best behavior.<ref>{{Cite|Do you think I'll really see the Picori if I'm on my best behavior all the time?|Joel|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Joel}} can be seen playing with other children outside of {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}}. The children go back inside after {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} impersonates {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, but {{Term|TMC|Funday School}} becomes available for {{Term|TMC|Link}} to access after he sees the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|Joel}} continues playing outside {{Term|TMC|Funday School}}, where he mentions how food falls out of his mouth when he runs,<ref>{{Cite|When I run, the thing that I'm eating always falls out of my mouth.|Joel|TMC}}</ref> until after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the golden {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} at the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}, whereupon {{Term|TMC|Joel}} falls asleep inside the classroom.<ref>{{Cite|Mmmmmnumnumnum... Zzzzzzzzzzzzz...|Joel|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Joel}} moves to the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}, where he explains that {{Term|TMC|Funday School}} let the children out early;<ref>{{Cite|School let out early today! Lucky!♪|Joel|TMC}}</ref> he remains at the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|display=Inn}} for the remainder of the game.

{{Term|TMC|Joel}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.
