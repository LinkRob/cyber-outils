{{Term/Store|Strato||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Strato}} lives in his {{Term|TMC|Stranger's House|link|display=House}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. Although he does not reveal it at first, it is eventually revealed that he is a member of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}} who recently moved to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|I come from a faraway land. You can tell by my clothing, can't you? [...] I'm thinking about calling my family over soon. Living with your feet on the ground is not so bad, I've decided.|Strato|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with him, a {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)|link}} will appear in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} near {{Term|TMC|Link's House|link}}, which allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to teleport to a limited section of the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}. Here, {{Term|TMC|Link}} has the opportunity to save {{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Ghost|link}}, which will reward him with the {{Term|TMC|Light Arrow|link}} after he arrives at the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} via the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{TMC|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Term|TMC|Strato}} appears in the {{TMC|Himekawa}}, where he encounters {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} and requests to see his {{Term|TMC|Map|link}}. He explains to {{Term|TMC|Link}} that he is searching for {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}}'s tomb and requests that he visit {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}} in his place due to being unaccustomed to walking on the ground. He gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}} in exchange for going and explains their purpose before disappearing into the wind.

Later, when {{Term|TMC|Link}} is in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}, he is attacked by the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}}, who stops shortly after seeing a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone|link}} fall out of {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s bag. {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} recognizes them as a sign from the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} and directs {{Term|TMC|Link}} to the {{Term|TMC|Source of the Flow|link}}, leading to the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
