{{Term/Store|Ring of Blasting|Rings of Blasting|Series, CoH}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Ring of Blasting}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Ring of Blasting}} are equipment that protect their wearers from all explosive damage, and make bombs detonate immediately upon use whenever the {{Plural|CoH|Ring of Blasting|display=Rings}} are equipped.<ref>{{Cite|Makes you immune to all explosive damage, and makes your bombs explode immediately.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or slain minibosses.
