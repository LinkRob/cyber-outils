The {{Name|Fan|{{Term/Store|Man in Blind's Hideout||Series}}}} is a character in {{ALttP}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography==
The man is found in the former hideout of [[Blind the Thief|Blind's]] gang of [[Thief|thieves]] in [[Kakariko Village]]. He tells [[Link]] that Blind, the leader of the gang, once lived in Kakariko Village, where his home served as the hideout for his mischievous companions. He also gives Link a clue about Blind's weakness and hatred of [[Light Beam|bright light]].<ref>{{Cite|Yo link! This house used to be a hideout for a gang of thieves! What was their leaders name... Oh yeah, his name was Blind and he hated bright light a lot.|Man in Blind's Hideout|ALttP}}</ref>
