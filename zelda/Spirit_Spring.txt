'''Spirit Springs''' are locations in {{TP}}.{{Name Ref|TPHD}} They serve as the home of the four [[Spirit of Light|Spirits of Light]], who each protect a province of [[Hyrule]]. 

There are four Spirit Springs located throughout [[Hyrule]], situated in the four major provinces: [[Ordon Spring]], [[Faron Spring]], [[Eldin Spring]], and [[Spring of the Spirit Lanayru]], all named after its respective province and Spirit of Light. 

During the events of the game, the Spirit Springs of [[Faron (Spirit)|Faron]], [[Eldin (Spirit)|Eldin]], and [[Lanayru (Spirit)|Lanayru]] are all invaded by [[Shadow Beast]]s, who attack the Light Spirits and promptly steal their power, causing [[Twilight]] to cover the area. With the exception of the Ordon Spring, which is where [[Link]] learns of Hyrule's fate, the Spirit Springs are where Link receives the [[Vessel of Light|Vessels of Light]] from the Light Spirit guarding that area. Here, he is tasked to retrieve all of the [[Tear of Light|Tears of Light]] located within its respective province.<ref>{{Cite|...Blue-eyed beast. Look...for my light... Retrieve the light stolen by the dark beasts...and keep it...in this vessel.|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> Once Link returns to the Spirit Spring with the complete Vessel of Light, the province's light will be restored, allowing the young hero to return to his [[Hylian]] form. After this, Link can walk into any of the Spirit Springs to have his health slowly recovered merely by stepping into the water.

In the [[Cave of Ordeals]], after every ten floors, the [[Great Fairy]] appears and grants the power of [[Fairy|Fairies]] to each Spirit Spring, serving as the game's [[Fairy Fountain]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, Hero, to the Cave of Ordeals. I am the Great Fairy. I reign over all fairies of this realm as their queen. In praise of your efforts thus far, I will release fairies to Ordon Spring.|Great Fairy|TP}}</ref> Reaching the last floor of the cave will allow Link to talk to the Great Fairy in each spring and receive unlimited [[Great Fairy's Tears]], provided that he does not already have a [[Bottle]] full of them.<ref>{{Cite|If ever you need my assistance again, visit any spring.|Great Fairy|TP}}</ref>
