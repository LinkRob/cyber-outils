==Location and Uses==
Bokoblin Trophies are obtained as post-battle rewards after finishing any given Chapter's [[Scenario]] battle or a [[Challenge]], with a Trophy earned for every [[Bokoblin]] kill (be they regular, [[Blue Bokoblin|Blue]], [[Black Bokoblin|Black]], and [[Silver Bokoblin|Silver]]) a Warrior obtains.

Bokoblin Trophies can be used to either complete [[Side Quest#Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|Quest]]s or be used in the Hyrule Blacksmith Guild workshop to de rust Rusted Weapons or remove Seals on a weapon.
