==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star}} are streaks of light which appear in [[Hyrule]]'s night sky. Whenever they appear, {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star}} fly towards the ground at a fast pace, usually crashing wherever they land. After landing, {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star}} emit a beacon which extends upward, allowing onlookers to easily locate their crash sites. At the base of the beacon, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find various treasures based on the type of {{Term|BotW|Shooting Star}} that fell.<ref>{{Cite|If you're lucky, you might be able to see a shooting star when gazing at the night sky. If you go to where a shooting star lands, you'll find treasure there! ...Or so I've heard. I would love to see a shooting star at least once in my life!|Glendo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you go to where a shooting star lands, apparently you'll find treasure there. A shooting star... Oh, how I'd love to see one with my own eyes.|Glendo|BotW}}</ref> The most common {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star}} are yellow, which yield {{Plural|BotW|Star Fragment|link}}.

Occasionally, {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star}} can also land on top of water, where they will sit above the water until {{Term|BotW|Link}} draws near. If Link uses {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} on the spot where the {{Term|BotW|Shooting Star}} has landed before its beacon has disappeared, the {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} block will raise the {{Term|BotW|Shooting Star}} and its beacon, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to safely retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Shooting Star|link}}'s treasure without it sinking into the water. {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star}} are covered in [[Traysi]]'s ''[[Rumor Mill: Volume 4]]''. In her book, she explains the rumors surrounding {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star}} and rates her recommendation with a scale of four stars.<ref name="Falling Stars"/>

=== Red Shooting Stars ===
In Breath of the Wild, after obtaining the "{{Term|BotW|Xenoblade Chronicles 2|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, red {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star}} will start appearing over certain locations while {{Term|BotW|Link}} is standing in specific spots. Red {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star}} yield special metallic {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} which each hold a piece of Salvager gear.
