The {{Term/Store|Super Lamp||Series, ALBW}} is an item in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Super Lamp is obtained after [[Link]] has acquired the [[Lantern]] and successfully completes the [[Treacherous Tower]]'s advanced level for the first time. Like the normal Lantern, it can be used to illuminate areas and produce a small flame to light torches or ignite [[Enemies in A Link Between Worlds|enemies]]; however, it is brighter and deals eight times the damage.
