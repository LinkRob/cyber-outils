The '''Curious Charm''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Curious Charm|An unusual memento. The spells on it greatly decrease damage for its wearer.<ref name="Curious Charm">{{Cite|An unusual memento. The spells on it greatly decrease damage for its wearer.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Curious Charm is an [[Accessory]] obtained in [[Scrubton]] after completing the [[Cotton-Candy Hunt]] [[Side Quest]], where a [[Deku Scrub]] upgrades [[Princess Zelda]]’s [[Ancient Charm]] into the Curious Charm. Equipping it grants the [[Damage Reduced +]] effect.
