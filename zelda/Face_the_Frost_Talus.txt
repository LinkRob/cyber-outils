"{{Term/Store|Face the Frost Talus||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Face the Frost Talus}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Rito Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}, he can speak to {{Term|BotW|Gesane|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} who guards the entrance to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. He shares with {{Term|BotW|Link}} that, while he was out earlier, he saw a {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Never mind that. Please listen to this! I was out for a walk earlier, and I saw... a {{Color|BotW Blue|Frost Talus}}!|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquires more, {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} explains it is a monster made out of enchanted ice,<ref>{{Cite|[Frost Talus?] Yes! It's a giant monster made of enchanted ice!|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> and the one he saw was "incredibly terrifying."<ref>{{Cite|I was out running an errand when I saw it... It was incredible...and terrifying. It was incredibly terrifying.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus}} was located in {{Term|BotW|Coldsnap Hollow|link}}, though {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} fled before he could be sure.<ref>{{Cite|I believe I saw it over in {{Color|BotW Blue|Coldsnap Hollow}} before my adrenaline kicked in and I fled like the wind.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> Even though it was big, he believes a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} could easily take it down.<ref>{{Cite|In any case, it was enormous. But I bet a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} could have taken it down no problem.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> Once he is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has defeated the {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} to share the news. He is shocked to hear that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was capable of doing so, as he is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I defeated it.] WHAT?! You defeated the {{Color|BotW Blue|Frost Talus}} in {{Color|BotW Blue|Coldsnap Hollow}}?! I never would have thought that a {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}} could take down something that big. No offense.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, he is relieved, as the {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus}} could have been dangerous if it had made its way to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, that's a relief. If that thind had wandered this way, the entire village would've been in danger!|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's not much, but I'd like to reward you for your trouble.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
