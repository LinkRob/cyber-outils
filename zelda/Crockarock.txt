'''Crockarocks''' are [[enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Crockarocks attack by shooting rocks at [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], which inflict little damage to her, but their physical attacks can inflict two [[Heart]]s of damage. Zelda can defeat a Crockarock by hitting it with her [[Wand]] three times.

Four Crockarocks appear in the [[Shrine of Fire]], and defeating them is required to obtain the [[Dungeon Map]].
