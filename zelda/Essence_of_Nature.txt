The {{Term/Store|Essence of Nature|Essences of Nature|Series, OoS|plural}} are {{Plural|OoS|Item|link}} in {{OoS}}.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Plural|OoS|Essence of Nature}} are the eight relics that {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} must obtain to help the {{Term|OoS|Maku Tree|link}} and save {{Term|OoS|Din (Oracle)|link}} in {{OoS}}. Each one is hidden in one of the enemy- and puzzle-filled {{Plural|OoS|Dungeon|link}} spread across {{Term|OoS|Holodrum|link}}. When {{Term|OoS|Link}} obtains all eight {{Plural|OoS|Essence of Nature|display=Essences}}, the {{Term|OoS|Maku Tree}} gives him a special {{Term|OoS|Huge Maku Seed|display=Seed}} called the {{Term|OoS|Huge Maku Seed|link}} needed to enter the {{Term|OoS|Northern Peak|link}} and defeat the evil {{Term|OoS|Onox|link}}.
