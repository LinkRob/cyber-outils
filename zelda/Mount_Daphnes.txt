{{Term/Store|Mount Daphnes||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Mount Daphnes'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Mount Daphnes is located in [[Hyrule Field]]. Mount Daphnes encircles a nameless lake inside which grows the [[Ancient Tree Stump]]. A wooden bridge connects the Ancient Tree Stump to Mount Daphnes. At the base of the bridge, [[Link]] can find three [[Barrel]]s, containing [[Apple]]s, [[Hylian Shroom]]s, and a [[Chuchu]].

A [[Korok]] can be found by dropping an [[Apple]] as an Offering to the statue on the eastern peak of the mountain.
