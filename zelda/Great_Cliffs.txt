The {{Term/Store|Great Cliffs||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Great Cliffs'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Great Cliffs are located north of the [[Kara Kara Bazaar]] in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. [[Link]] can complete the [[Shrine Quest]], "[[The Eye of the Sandstorm]]", by finding the [[Dako Tah Shrine]] among the Great Cliffs. A large sandstorm plagues the area, making it difficult to navigate and interfering with the [[Sheikah Slate]]'s [[Sheikah Slate#Map|Map]]. However, the sandstorm clears up during the night between 9:00 PM and 4:00 AM. The sandstorm begins to return at 3:00 AM, but is not fully blown until 4:00 AM.

A [[Korok]] resides on the southern edge of the cliffs.
