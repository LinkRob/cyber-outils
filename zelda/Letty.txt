{{Term/Store|Letty||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Letty|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Letty}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who travels the {{Term|BotW|Faron Highroad|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}. She carries a {{Term|BotW|Hunter's Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}}. During her journey, she rests at the table inside {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable}} and at the {{Term|BotW|Fishing Resort|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Letty}} has been known to suffer from mood swings, which has led others to describe her as "temperamental".<ref>{{Cite|People often tell me that I can be a bit temperamental. That...doesn't mean I'm getting sick or anything...|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey! Do you think I'm...temperamental? You do? You don't? Do you?! You think I've got random mood swings, huh?!|Letty|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to her, she may greet him differently depending on her mood.<ref>{{Cite|Heya! Wheeee! I'm feeling SO darn good today!|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What now? Whaddya want? *grouse* I don't have any food for your belly, but I do have some food for thought.|Letty|BotW}}</ref> She also has difficulty discerning and managing her emotions.<ref>{{Cite|I've gotta cheer up...|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This feeling...|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I feel very...something.|Letty|BotW}}</ref> If she notices that she is treating {{Term|BotW|Link}} poorly, she will become embarrassed and apologize.<ref>{{Cite|Er, sorry... Here, I'll share some nice info I came across as my form of apology.|Letty|BotW}}</ref>

As she travels, {{Term|BotW|Letty}} shows an interest in {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A famous place where love can become reality, huh... Oh, sorry! I was talking to myself there. I was just thinking about a place called {{Color|BotW Blue|Lover's Pond}}...|Letty|BotW}}</ref> She has heard that it is south of their location in {{Term|BotW|Atun Valley|link}}, recalling that she saw it on a {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} once.<ref>{{Cite|They say it's to the south, and I think I saw it on a map once...|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Lover's Pond}}, which they say is to the south...|Letty|BotW}}</ref> Though she is surprised that it is shaped like a heart, she does not know what else she would have expected.<ref>{{Cite|It's literally heart shaped, which is surprising to me...but I don't know what I expected.|Letty|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Letty}} has considered visiting {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond}} to find a life partner.<ref>{{Cite|I think I might head south of here to Lover's Pond and try to find myself a partner, yessir!|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I think I might head south of here and find Lover's Pond! Maybe fortune will smile on me and I'll find a partner!|Letty|BotW}}</ref>

Aside from {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond}}, {{Term|BotW|Letty}} will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} about other geographical features. While she is in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village}}, she shares that he can take a {{Term|BotW|Raft|link}} from their current location to {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you take a boat and head east of Lurelin Village, you can get all the way out to Eventide Island! Ha! Such a sudden revelation. I'd expect nothing less than for you to make that face!|Letty|BotW}}</ref> She has also heard of the {{Term|BotW|Palmorae Ruins|link}} near {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village}}, but she does not know much about them.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently, there are ruins on the coast near Lurelin Village! That's all! Did you get all that? It was pretty short, after all.|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What? You want to hear the helpful info I gave you again? The really short info? OK... Apparently, there are ruins not too far from the coast near Lurelin Village! That's it!|Letty|BotW}}</ref> She suggests he check out {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, west of {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable}}, but she warns him that he is likely to encounter {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} on his way there.<ref>{{Cite|What's up? Feeling bored? Looking for adventure? Why don't you travel west of the Lakeside Stable, then? There's a place out there called Highland Stable. But be careful—there's a bunch of mosters on the way.|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What about trying to head further west of Lakeside Stable? There's a place called Highland Stable, but you're bound to run into a bunch of monsters on your way there...|Letty|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Letty}} will often talk about {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}. Her reason for visiting {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village}} is to try the {{Plural|BotW|Fish|link}} and fruit there, which she has heard are delicious.<ref>{{Cite|What is it? Oh, just thought you'd come and talk to me, then? Well, I'm on my way to {{Color|BotW Blue|Lurelin Village}}. They say the fish and fruit there are really fantastic, so I thought I'd go there and add them to my diet!|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm on my way to {{Color|BotW Blue|Lurelin Village}}... They say the fish and fruit there are really delicious, so I thought I'd try and add them to my diet!|Letty|BotW}}</ref> Due to how fatty the {{Plural|BotW|Fish}} are, she believes that grilling them would enhance their flavor.<ref>{{Cite|The fish in Lurelin Village are pretty fatty. As a result, I hear they're incredibly delicious when you grill them!|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The fish around Lurelin Village are pretty fatty... That means they're unbelievably delicious if you grill them! Are you glad I was here to give you such a great tip? Of course you are!|Letty|BotW}}</ref> She also informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that, while meat dishes can recover {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fish}} dishes can grant special effects to those that eat them.<ref>{{Cite|What's that? Did you want some information from me? Let's see... Meat dishes are well suited for refilling your health, and you can expect some special effects from dishes with fishes. Hehe. Is that the sort of thing you're curious about?|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Meat dishes are really great for helping you recover your health. Fish dishes can contain pretty-special effects, though!|Letty|BotW}}</ref> Finally, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has has success at {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Fish}} and fruit, or if he has ended up with {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, hey! Hey! Have you got a second? I wanted to ask... Are you good at cooking with fish and fruit? You've had a lot of success combining those ingredients, right?|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Have you had a lot of success cooking fish or fruit dishes? You haven't ended up with dubious meals while you're learning new recipes, have you?|Letty|BotW}}</ref>

If she encounters any foes during her travels, {{Term|BotW|Letty}} will draw her {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword}} to protect herself.<ref>{{Cite|Is this the end?|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ugh, I'm really tired of this... Seriously...|Letty|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, geez... How'd I get myself into this mess...|Letty|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves her, she may remarks that she thought she was a goner.<ref>{{Cite|I thought I was a goner for sure!|Letty|BotW}}</ref> She can also comment on her bad luck.<ref>{{Cite|Uh—I think I've got really bad luck!|Letty|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've got to give you a reward, huh...|Letty|BotW}}</ref>
