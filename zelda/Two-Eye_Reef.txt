{{Term/Store|Two-Eye Reef||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (G4) is a location in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==

The {{Term|TWWHD|Two-Eye Reef}} features two round reefs in the center. If all the enemies are destroyed, a [[Treasure Chart]] that leads to the [[Secret Cave Chart]] will appear. The coordinates of the {{Term|TWWHD|Two-Eye Reef}} are G4.

A {{Term|TWWHD|Great Fairy|link}} can be found on the outskirts of the {{Term|TWWHD|Two-Eye Reef|display=Reef}}. A projectile is used to kill the [[Big Octo]]. Once the Octo is vanquished, the Great Fairy will appear, thanking Link by doubling his [[Magic Meter]].

Once Link has the proper [[Triforce Chart]], he will be able to retrieve a [[Triforce Shard]] from the bottom of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}.
