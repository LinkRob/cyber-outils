{{Term/Store|Kairo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kairo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kairo}} is a {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}}  who travels the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|link}} and the end of the {{Term|BotW|Maw of Death Mountain|link}}. At the end of his trail, he stops to search for {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} before heading back.<ref>{{Cite|Let's find some ore to sell.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> He used to work at the {{Term|BotW|Goron Hot Springs|link}}, but he grew bored there and decided to travel the world as a {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I got plain sick and tired of working the Goron Hot Springs in {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}, so I decided to just up and leave.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> He carries a {{Term|BotW|Traveling Pack|link}} on his journeys.

{{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}} is {{Term|BotW|Kairo}}'s hometown, and the reason he is so far away is to sell the {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} he has mined from {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|My hometown of {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}} is the fifth stop up the trail. I'm just out here selling the ore I dug up from {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The chunks of ore I dug up from {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}} are pretty high quality. Take a look and you'll dig 'em too!|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> While he admits that the area can be dangerous at times, he claims there is an abundance of {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} to mine.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}} is the fifth stop on this road. It can be a pretty dangerous place, but there's a lot of ore to be mined.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> He also mentions that he is friends with {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}}, and he wonders how he is doing while he is away.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder how my old buddy {{Color|BotW Red|Yunobo}} back in {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}} is doing. Nice guy, that {{Color|BotW Red|Yunobo}}. Always a pleasure to chomp the ore with.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kairo}} considers himself to be more suited for the life of traveling {{Term|BotW|Merchant}}, as he enjoys the scenery he sees along the way.<ref>{{Cite|I am more suited to the life of a traveling merchant for sure. The scenery is much nicer than the {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron Hot Springs}}.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm digging this greenery.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What great scenery.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> He wants to see parts of the world he otherwise would not be able to in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, such as cold locations, green {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}, and hills.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to see the world! I want to see somewhere cold, I want to see green trees and hills!|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> He is interested in the [[Weather]] further away from {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain}}, as there are no {{Plural|BotW|Magma Bomb|link}} falling from the sky.<ref>{{Cite|Is it normal for magma bombs to not rain down nonstop? I just don't understand the weather around here.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref>

Since {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kairo}} warns him that he will need a way to protect himself from the {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} if he continues past the {{Term|BotW|Maw of Death Mountain}}.<ref>{{Cite|You do not look to be made of rock, so here's some advice: you'll need some fire protection if you go any farther.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|mountain road}} is pretty dangerous because of how hot it gets. If you're taking a trip to {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}, be sure to bring some fire protection.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> He recommends the use of a {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}}, but he is unable to remember any of the ingredients required beyond a {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Lizard|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I recommend a {{Color|BotW Red|fireproof elixir}}. You can make it by cooking a {{Color|BotW Red|fireproof lizard}} with a... A, um... Huh, I forget what else.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|BotW Red|fireproof elixir}} will help a little Hylian like you stand up to this heat. Just cook a {{Color|BotW Red|fireproof lizard}} with a... With a, um... Huh. I just can't remember.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> He also warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} about {{Plural|BotW|Magma Bomb}}, as getting hit by them would even hurt a {{Term|BotW|Goron}}.<ref>{{Cite|Getting hit by a {{Color|BotW Blue|magma bomb}} even hurts us Gorons! If you're thinking about going for a climb on {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}, you had better be careful.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you're heading to {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}'s famed {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron Hot Springs}}, watch out for magma bombs on the way. I haven't had to worry about them since leaving to travel the world, and I do NOT miss dodging those things.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref>

Along his route, {{Term|BotW|Kairo}} may be attacked by {{Plural|BotW|Fire Chuchu|link}} or other [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monsters]]. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves him, he may share his relief to live another day.<ref>{{Cite|Looks like I'll live to sell another day!|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> He also has a chance to remark that he finds monsters to be scary.<ref>{{Cite|Those monsters are pretty scary.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Hasty Steamed Mushrooms|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Sneaky Elixir|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I survived? All right! Here, take this as a thank you, brother.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kairo}} sells a limited selection of {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}} at a fairly low price.<ref>{{Cite|I have some rare ore burning a hole in my pack! Take your time looking it over, and let me know what you want.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|All right, let's get down to brass tacks. Feel free to browse my wares!|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> During {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, Kairo will add rare {{Plural|BotW|Gem}}, such as {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}, to his lineup.<ref>{{Cite|This weather has me in a rare mood to sell rare ore. How about a {{Color|BotW Red|diamond}}? You interested in rare items?|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys from {{Term|BotW|Kairo}}, he shows his appreciation for his business.<ref name="Appreciate">{{Cite|I appreciate your business, brother!|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Good doing business with you!|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you for stopping by, brother!|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's the last of it. I'm all sold out! Thanks for your business, brother!|Kairo|BotW}}</ref>

As with all other {{Plural|BotW|Merchant}}, {{Term|BotW|Kairo}} will buy {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}} as well.<ref>{{Cite|I can buy some things from you.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll buy whatever it is you're selling.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll buy anything. Just try me!|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you have anything you don't need, I'll be glad to buy it off you.|Kairo|BotW}}</ref> He is grateful for any wares he is able to purchase from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for your business, brother!|Kairo|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Appreciate"/><ref>{{Cite|It's been a pleasure! Need anything else?|Kairo|BotW}}</ref>
