{{Term/Store|Golden Ship|Golden Ships|Series, PH, HWL, HWDE}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 284">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 284}}</ref><ref name="HWDE Tutorial"/>{{Note|In {{PH}}, this term refers only to a specific combination of {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}. In-game, the ship itself is always called the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}}.}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, the {{Term|PH|Golden Ship}} is the name given to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} once it has been outfitted with the following {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Golden Ship}}

The {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} can be attached to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} at any time by visiting the {{Term|PH|Shipyard|link}} on {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}. For every two parts of the {{Term|PH|Golden Ship}} used, the durability of the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} is increased by one Heart; the entire {{Term|PH|Golden Ship}} therefore has a durability of eight Hearts. Aside from this boost in durability, the {{Term|PH|Golden Ship}} is merely a change in appearance to suit the player's personal tastes.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|L|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW|L}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Golden Ship}} appear as an {{Term|HWDE|Item Card|link}} on the {{Term|HWDE|Grand Travels Map|link}} in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}. The {{Term|HWDE|Grand Travels Map}} was not included with the {{Term|HW|Phantom Hourglass & Spirit Tracks Pack|link}} for the original {{HW}}, so {{Plural|HWDE|Golden Ship}} weren't introduced until the {{Term|HWL|Phantom Hourglass & Spirit Tracks Pack}} released for {{HW|L|-}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Phantom Hourglass & Spirit Tracks Pack}}<br/>This pack includes:<br/>New playable character: Toon Zelda<br/>New weapon: Sand Wand for Toon Link|author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= November 30, 2021|url= https://www.zelda.com/hyrule-warriors/wiiu/dlc/|title= Expand your adventure with dlc packs|site= Zelda.com|type= }}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= A new map for Adventure Mode|author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= November 30, 2021|url= http://web.archive.org/web/20160904032504/http://www.zelda.com/hyrule-warriors-legends/#buynow/1|title= Individual Packs|site= Zelda.com|type= web archive}}</ref> Because {{HW|DE}} came with all previous DLC packs included, {{Plural|HWDE|Golden Ship}} are available from the moment {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode}} is unlocked.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This new version brings every map, character, and mission from both the Wii U game and the Nintendo 3DS game together, including all the original premium downloadable content.|author= Nintendo|published= January 11, 2018|retrieved= January 11, 2018|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_u7g5BlfiY|title= Nintendo Direct Mini 1.11.2018|site= Youtube.com|type= video}}</ref>

{{Plural|HWDE|Golden Ship}} can be obtained either by purchasing them from the {{Term|HWDE|Item Card Shop|link}} for 20,000 {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee|link}}, or by getting one as a battle reward. Because the {{Term|HWDE|Grand Travels Map}} is split between {{ST}} and {{PH}}-based sections, {{Plural|HWDE|Golden Ship}} only appear as victory rewards of battles to the right on the pink dividing line. When a {{Term|HWDE|Golden Ship}} is used, it activates a {{Term|HWDE|Captain Power|link}} that causes every battle across the {{Term|HWDE|Grand Travels Map}} to allow for a higher amount of damage to be taken before lowering the battle rank, which makes it easier for {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} to achieve higher ranks while still taking more damage.<ref name="HWDE Tutorial"/><ref>{{Cite|An {{Color|HWDE Green|Engine Power}} is affecting the entire Grand Travels Map!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Captain Power|link}} provided by {{Plural|HWDE|Golden Ship}} runs for three battles and cannot be overridden by another Captain or {{Term|HWDE|Engine Power|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Captain and Engine Powers will last for a limited amount of time. The powers' duration counters will decrease by one after each battle. When a counter reaches zero, the power will fade. Even if the battle you're fighting doesn't exactly benefit from the current power, the counter will still decrease, so use the items wisely! Captain and Engine Powers cannot override other Captain or Engine Powers. Once the power takes effect, it will remain in effect until the duration counter reaches zero.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

{{Plural|HWDE|Golden Ship}} can be traded for a random delicious-type {{Term|HWDE|Food|link}} at the {{Term|HWDE|Linebeck Trading Company|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|On map squares with the Linebeck Trading Company, you can {{Color|HWDE Green|swap one Item Card for food}}.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> While searching a map space with the {{Term|HWDE|Linebeck Trading Company}}, any {{Term|HWDE|Item Card}} can be traded by selecting it.<ref>{{Cite|We have a deal!<br/>I'll trade you some food for that Item Card!|Linebeck Trading Company|HWDE}}</ref> After a single trade, the company will disappear from the space.<ref>{{Cite|The Linebeck Trading Company disappears from the square after just a single trade but will pop up again after a certain number of battles.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> During a {{Term|HWDE|Two-for-One Sale|link}}, two delicious {{Plural|HWDE|Food}} will be given instead of just one.<ref>{{Cite|The Linebeck Trading Company will occasionally hold a {{Color|HWDE Green|Two-for-One Sale}}. During these promotions, trading an Item Card will get you double the usual amount of food in return.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}
