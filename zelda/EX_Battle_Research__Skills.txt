{{Term/Store|EX Battle Research: Skills||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 83 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Battle Research: Skills|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Battle Research: Skills}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must fight {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} according to {{Term|HWAoC|Robbie|link}}'s instructions. The first {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Hinox|link}}, and first, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must {{Term|HWAoC|Perfect Dodging|display=Perfect Dodge|link}} its attacks twice. Afterwards, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat it with a {{Term|HWAoC|Weak Point Smash|link}}. When it is defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|White-Maned Lynel|link}} spawns, which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must {{Term|HWAoC|Perfect Dodging|display=Perfect Dodge}} five times. Afterwards, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat it with a {{Term|HWAoC|Special Attack|link}}. If either of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated at the wrong time or in the wrong way, they will simply reappear, giving the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} another chance to reach their objective. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} except {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of a {{Term|HWAoC|High-Yield Ancient Furnace|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Machine Lubricant|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
