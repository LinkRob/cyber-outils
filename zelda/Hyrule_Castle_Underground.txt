The {{Term/Store|Hyrule Castle Underground||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Talus">{{Cite|Hyrule Castle Underground<br/>{{Big|Stone Talus}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Underground}} consists of the portion of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} that is located below that main structure. 

===East Passage===
{{Main|East Passage}}
The {{Term|BotW|East Passage}} is located on the eastern side of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Underground}}. It houses a {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus (Rare)|link}}.<ref name="Talus"/> It hosted mining operations for {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}, which have ceased since the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.

===Lockup===
{{Main|Lockup}}
{{Term|BotW|Lockup}} is a series of cells located on the northwest side of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Underground}}. Since {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} overtook {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}, it has become overrun by {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Stalnox|link}} can be found in its back room.<ref>{{Cite|Hyrule Castle Underground<br/>{{Big|Stalnox}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

===West Passage===
{{Main|West Passage}}
The {{Term|BotW|West Passage}} is an abandoned tunnel on the west end of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Underground}}. Like the {{Term|BotW|East Passage}}, it was utilized in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}'s mining operations, and has since been abandoned.
