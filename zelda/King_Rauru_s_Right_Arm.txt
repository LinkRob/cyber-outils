'''King Rauru's Right Arm''' {{Exp Game|TotK}}, also simply known as the '''Right Arm''', is an item in {{TotK}}. It was originally used by [[King Rauru]] to seal [[Ganondorf]] beneath [[Hyrule Castle]] during the [[Imprisoning War (TotK)|Imprisoning War]], long before the events of {{BotW}}.

== Overview ==
Rauru's Right Arm functionally acts as the conduit for using powers, similar to how the [[Sheikah Slate]] used [[Rune]]s.

When Link and [[Princess Zelda]] descended into the [[Imprisoning Chamber]], they found the right arm sealing away Ganondorf's emaciated body. Upon getting closer, the arm fell off, alongside with Rauru's [[Secret Stone]], which Zelda picks up. After the reawakened Ganondorf destroyed the [[Master Sword]] and crippled Link's right arm with a stream of [[Gloom]], the Demon King caused the room to collapse, and when Link tried to jump after a falling Zelda and failing to catch her, Rauru's right arm grabbed Link to prevent him from falling into the abyss.

With Link's right arm crippled beyond recovery by the Gloom, Rauru's spirit replaced it with his own right arm. Once Link defeated Ganondorf, the spirits of Rauru and [[Queen Sonia]] restored [[Princess Zelda]] from her [[Light Dragon|draconified]] form back to human via channeling their power through the right arm. Once that is complete, the arm is gone, and Link's own right arm is fully restored.
