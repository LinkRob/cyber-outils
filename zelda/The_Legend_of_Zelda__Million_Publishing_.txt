'''The Legend of Zelda''' is an unofficial {{TLoZ}} guide book published in Japan.

==Overview==
[[File:Million Publishing Logo.png|left]]
This guide book was published exclusively in Japan in 1986. It provides information for the {{Term|TLoZ|Overworld|link}} and {{Plural|TLoZ|Dungeon|link}} of the First Quest of {{TLoZ}}. It does not cover {{Term|TLoZ|Level 7|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Level 8|link}}, and {{Term|TLoZ|Level-9|link}} with as much detail as the first six {{Plural|TLoZ|Dungeon}}. The book's {{Term|TLoZ|Overworld}} guide displays information for every screen on the map. The book also contains unofficial artwork for all of the {{Plural|TLoZ|Item|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Enemy|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Boss|link}}, and characters in {{TLoZ}}.
