The Triforce consists of three separate triangles: the [[Triforce of Power]] is always depicted as the top piece, with the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] represented by the lower-left piece, and the [[Triforce of Courage]]<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 383}}</ref> represented by the lower-right piece.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 76}}</ref> Although the Triforce was in fact created by gods, it can never be used by such,<ref>{{Cite|For while the supreme power of the Triforce was created by gods, all of its power can never be wielded by one. Knowing this power was her last and only hope, the goddess gave up her divine powers and her immortal form.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> possibly the goddesses' way of giving hope to all the mortal beings in Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|Somehow, I think that may have been their way of giving hope to all the mortal beings of the land.|Zelda|SS}}</ref>

The Triforce is the ultimate source of power in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], and it serves as the balance that weighs the three forces: Power, Wisdom, and Courage.<ref>{{Cite|The Triforce...the sacred triangle...it is a balance that weighs the three forces: Power, Wisdom and Courage.|Sheik|OoT}}</ref> It has the ability to grant any wish of those who obtain it, regardless of if the person's intentions are "good" or "evil".<ref name="wish">{{Cite|The Triforce is waiting for a new owner. Its Golden Power is in your hands...Now, touch it with a wish in your heart.|Essence of the Triforce|ALttP}}</ref><ref name="Quote13" /><ref name="E 70" /> It is said that the wish will hold true until the user of the Triforce dies.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 13}}</ref> Some known wishes are [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|King Daphnes']] wish in {{TWW}} to give hope to "these children", Link and Zelda, and to wash away the old Hyrule, Ganondorf's to burn Hyrule down and conquer it (though he was unsuccessful), as well as Link and Zelda's wish in {{ALBW}} to give [[Lorule]] a new Triforce to replace the one they destroyed. The Triforce can also change the appearance of the [[Sacred Realm]] to reflect the heart of the person who enters it, as seen in {{ALttP|-}}.<ref name="sheik">{{Cite|If you would seek the sacred triangle, listen well... The resting place of the sacred triangle, the Sacred Realm, is a mirror that reflects what is in the heart...the heart of one who enters it... If an evil heart, the Realm will become full of evil; if pure, the Realm will become a paradise.|Sheik|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book=E |publisher= |page=29 }}</ref> Because the Triforce is the basis of Hyrule's providence,<ref name="providence" /> a crest bearing the image of the Triforce serves as the [[Royal Crest|official crest]] of the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]].

The Triforce was believed to have been initially left in the Sacred Realm at the point where the Golden Goddesses departed the newly created world for the heavens.<ref>{{Cite|The three great goddesses, their labors completed, departed for the heavens. And golden sacred triangles remained at the point where the goddesses left the world. Since then, the sacred triangles have become the basis of our world's providence. And, the resting place of the triangles has become the Sacred Realm.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> During the [[Era of the Goddess Hylia]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'', [[Demise]] and his hordes launched an attack to the Surface in an attempt to get their hands on the omnipotent relic.<ref name="Demise">{{Cite|In his thirst to make the world his own, Demise readied a massive army of monsters for war. He sought to take the Triforce for himself by force.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> It is unknown if the Triforce still rested in the Sacred Realm at that point, though by the end of the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline#Sky Era|Sky Era]] the individual pieces of the Triforce were located in isolated areas of the [[Silent Realm]] that were only accessible through [[Trial Gate|Trial Gates]] hidden within the [[Sky Keep]]. During the [[Era of Chaos]], the Triforce again resided in the Sacred Realm and the ancient Sage [[Rauru]] constructed the [[Temple of Time]], which contained the only, sealed entrance to the Sacred Realm.<ref name="E 77">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 77}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 86}}</ref> The Triforce was placed in the [[Temple of Light]], at the center of the Sacred Realm, where it remained undisturbed for many ages until the events of {{OoT}}.<ref name="E 77" /><ref>{{Cite|He obtained the Triforce from the Temple of Light, and with its power, he became the King of Evil...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref>

==Legends of the Triforce==
[[File:GoddessesDepartOoT.png|left|thumb|221x221px|The Golden Goddesses, [[Din (Goddess)|Din]] (right), [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]] (center), and [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]] (left) are the creators of the sacred Triforce, as seen in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
The Goddesses ordained the Triforce as Hyrule’s providence, the ‘True Force’ to govern all and the ultimate source of power.<ref name="providence">{{Cite|And golden sacred triangles remained at the point where the goddesses left the world. Since then, the sacred triangles have become the basis of our world's providence.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref><ref name="ALttP Manual 3" /> Of all the powerful mystical forces in the realm of Hyrule, none compare to the Triforce; thus, whoever claims the Triforce gains virtual omnipotence, their power, wisdom or courage dwarfed only by the Goddesses themselves.<ref name="ALttP Manual 3" /> The Triforce ultimately determines Hyrule's destiny, such that if a person with a righteous heart claims the Triforce, Hyrule will enter into a golden age of peace and prosperity. But, if a person with a wicked heart captures the Triforce, the kingdom will inevitably fall into an age of darkness. Nonetheless, it is prophesied that if [[Ganon|one with an evil heart]] were to claim the Triforce, a [[Link|Hero]] is destined to stand against him.<ref>{{Cite|When evil rules all, an awakening voice from the Sacred Realm will call those destined to be Sages, who dwell in the five temples. [...] Together with the Hero of Time, the awakened ones will bind the evil and return the light of peace to the world...|Sheik|OoT}}</ref>[[File:ALttP Triforce Sacred Realm Artwork.png|thumb|right|The Triforce within its resting place in the Sacred Realm, as seen in an artwork of ''A Link to the Past''|250x250px]]The Triforce contains the essence of the [[Golden Goddess|Goddesses]]. Unlike the Goddesses themselves, however, it does not discriminate between those who are good or evil. Therefore, the powerful golden relic is completely neutral: It grants the wishes of whoever obtains it, regardless of their motives.<ref name="ALttP Manual 3" /> The stronger the wish, the more the wish comes true since the Triforce will express it more powerfully.<ref>{{Cite|The stronger the wish, the more powerful the Triforce's expression of that wish.|Essence of the Triforce|ALttP}}</ref> The resting place of the Triforce—the Sacred Realm—is a mirror that will reflect the heart of the one who enters it and lays claim to the so-called "Sacred Triangle". This being said, one of two things can happen when this event occurs. If the heart of the one that holds the Sacred Triangle in his or her hands is pure and balanced, they will gain the True Force to govern all and the Sacred Realm will become a paradise. On the other hand, if the heart of the one that holds the Sacred Triangle does not contain an equal balance of Power, Wisdom and Courage, the Triforce will separate into its three component parts, leaving only the part representing the force that one most believes in ([[Triforce of Power|Power]] in all cases) and transforming the Sacred Realm into a [[Dark World|world of evil]] as well.<ref name="Quote9">{{Cite|If the heart of the one who holds the sacred triangle has all three forces in balance, that one will gain the True Force to govern all. But, if that one's heart is not in balance, the Triforce will separate into three parts: Power, Wisdom and Courage. Only one part will remain for the one who touched the Triforce...the part representing the force that one most believes in.|Sheik|OoT}}</ref> To gain the full power of the Triforce, the tainted one who touched the Triforce must seek the two remaining parts, which will be held by two special individuals chosen by destiny who will bear the [[mark of the Triforce]] on the backs of their hands like the former. The tainted one must defeat them in order to obtain their respective pieces.<ref name="Quote10">{{Cite|If that one seeks the True Force, that one must acquire the two lost parts. Those two parts will be held within others chosen by destiny, who will bear the Triforce mark on the backs of their hands.|Sheik|OoT}}</ref>

==Pieces of the Triforce==
[[File:Triforce Logo.png|right|178x178px|The complete Triforce]]
As mentioned in the [[Sheikah/Sheikah Legends#The Legend of the Sacred Triangle|Sheikah legends]], when someone with an unbalanced heart touches the Triforce, the sacred relic will split up into three separate pieces: Power, Wisdom and Courage.<ref name="Quote9" /> As shown in {{TLoZ|-}} and {{TWW|-}}, however, a Triforce piece can be split up even further into many small fragments known as {{Plural|Series|Triforce Shard|link}}, where reassembling the shattered parts will fully restore that piece. When all three Triforce pieces are in close contact, they will begin to resonate as they attempt to combine into one again.<ref name="resonate" /> This is shown towards the end of {{OoT|-}} and most prominently in {{TWW|-}}, where the Triforce ends up becoming whole again.<ref>{{Cite|It can only be called fate. ...That here, I would again gather the three with the crests.  ...That I should lay my hand on that which grants the wishes of the beholder... That when power, wisdom, and courage come together, the gods would have no choice but to come down...|Ganondorf|TWW}}</ref>

It is possible for a Triforce piece to be inactive inside its bearer's body and under certain circumstances for it to activate. An example of this scenario is Link's transformation into a [[Wolf Link|wolf]] in {{TP}}, when he was first covered by the [[Twilight|twilight]].<ref>{{Cite|In the land covered in twilight, where people roam as spirits, you were transformed into a blue-eyed beast... That was a sign... It was a sign that the powers of the chosen one rest within you...and that they are awakening.|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> Another case, from the same game, is Ganon's revival by the Triforce of Power when the [[Sage]]s attempted to execute him by impaling him with a [[Sword of the Sages|sword]].<ref name="sage" />

===Triforce of Power===
{{main|Triforce of Power}}
[[File:TLoZ Series Triforce of Power Artwork.png|The Triforce of Power resonating|left|166x166px]]
The Triforce of Power embodies the essence of the Goddess of Power, [[Din (Goddess)|Din]], the creator of Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|Din, the goddess of power... [...]With her strong flaming arms, she cultivated the land and created the red earth.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> Whoever holds the Triforce of Power is granted "true power".<ref name="ALttP Manual 3"/> This power is absolute, making the recipient of Din's blessing a god-like entity, vastly more powerful than any mortal, and affording its wielder near-invulnerability,<ref name=sage/> vast strength, and an unlimited source of mystical power. As such, due to his sheer desire for ruling the world with an iron fist, [[Ganon]] is always the bearer of the Triforce of Power, but in rare cases it has been taken by others including Link himself. It represents the top piece of the Triforce.

===Triforce of Wisdom===
{{main|Triforce of Wisdom}}
[[File:TLoZ Series Triforce of Wisdom Artwork.png|The Triforce of Wisdom resonating|left|169x169px]]
The Triforce of Wisdom embodies the essence of the Goddess of Wisdom, [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]], the author of law.<ref>{{Cite|Nayru, the goddess of wisdom... [...] Poured her wisdom onto the earth and gave the spirit of law to the world.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> It imparts divine wisdom upon its holder and grants them untold mystical abilities, such as the ability to heal others<ref name=heal/> and, presumably, the ability to communicate telepathically. The Triforce of Wisdom leads its holder to make the right decisions,<ref>{{Cite|It is time for you to choose: surrender or die. Oh yes, a question for all the land and people of Hyrule... Life? Or death?!|Zant|TP}}</ref> making them wiser than any mortal.<ref name="ALttP Manual 3"/> The essence of Nayru grants [[Princess Zelda]], the Triforce of Wisdom's bearer, myriad mystical abilities as well as divine wisdom. In {{TLoZ}}, Zelda purposely splits up the Triforce of Wisdom into [[Triforce Shard|eight fragments]] and hides them throughout the land, fearing [[Ganon]]'s rule after he steals the Triforce of Power.<ref>{{Cite|LONG AGO, {{Color|TLoZ Red|GANON}}, PRINCE OF DARKNESS, STOLE THE TRIFORCE OF POWER. {{Color|TLoZ Blue|PRINCESS ZELDA}} OF HYRULE BROKE THE TRIFORCE OF WISDOM INTO EIGHT PIECES AND HID THEM FROM {{Color|TLoZ Red|GANON}} BEFORE SHE WAS KIDNAPPED BY {{Color|TLoZ Red|GANON'S}} MINIONS. {{Color|TLoZ Green|LINK}}, YOU MUST FIND THE PIECES AND SAVE {{Color|TLoZ Blue|ZELDA}}.|N/A|TLoZ}}</ref> Link must fully reassemble the Triforce of Wisdom if he hopes to enter [[Death Mountain]], destroy Ganon (taking the Triforce of Power for himself), and save the princess of Hyrule. It represents the lower-left piece of the Triforce.

===Triforce of Courage===
{{main|Triforce of Courage}}
[[File:TLoZ Series Triforce of Courage Artwork.png|The Triforce of Courage resonating|left|174x174px]]
The Triforce of Courage embodies the essence of the Goddess of Courage, [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]], the source of all life. <ref>{{Cite|Farore, the goddess of courage... [...] With her rich soul, produced all life forms who would uphold the law.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> [[Link]] is usually the bearer of the Triforce of Courage. The Triforce of Courage itself is more than just a mere symbol of Link's exemplary courage,<ref>{{Cite|My only mistake was to slightly underestimate the power of this kid... No... It was not the kid's power I misjudged, it was the power of the Triforce of Courage!|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref> however. Although Link himself is not as skilled at magic as Ganon or Princess Zelda, as his main method of combat is swordplay, the Triforce of Courage, like the other sacred triangles, grants him untold mystical abilities, including protection against dark magic, as seen in ''Twilight Princess''. It is also likely this piece of the Triforce increases Link's courage even further. In {{TWW|-}}, like the Triforce of Wisdom in {{TLoZ|-}}, this piece of the Triforce was split into eight fragments hidden deep within the [[Great Sea]] due to the [[Hero of Time]] departing from Hyrule during the events of {{MM|-}} (or being send back to his childhood by Princess Zelda in ''Ocarina of Time'' according to ''Hyrule Historia''). It represents the lower-right piece of the Triforce.

=== ''The Legend of Zelda'' ===
[[File:TLoZ Triforce Shard Sprite.png|thumb]]
In the original {{TLoZ}}, the [[Triforce of Power]] and [[Triforce of Wisdom]] component parts of the Triforce are introduced, and described as "golden triangles possessing mystical powers" that grant their holders great power.<ref name="TLoZ Manual 3">{{Cite Manual|quote=|game=TLoZ|page=3}}</ref> Prior to the events of the game, [[Ganon]] attacked the [[Hyrule|kingdom of Hyrule]] and stole the Triforce of Power.<ref name="TLoZ Manual 3" /> Fearing Ganon's wicked rule, the [[Princess Zelda]] split the Triforce of Wisdom into [[Triforce Shard|eight shards]] and hid them in the dungeons beneath Hyrule.<ref name="TLoZ Manual 3" /><ref name="TLoZ Manual 4">{{Cite manual|quote=|game=TLoZ|page=4}}</ref> [[Link]] must fight his way through the eight dungeons to recover all the pieces of the Triforce of Wisdom to reassemble it and then battle his way through Ganon's stronghold in [[Death Mountain]] ([[Level 9|Level-9]]) to defeat him and recover the Triforce of Power.<ref name="TLoZ Manual 4" /> Link and Zelda then use the two pieces to restore peace to Hyrule. The [[Triforce of Courage]] was not yet introduced in the very first game of the franchise, making it the only case the complete relic is never featured in installments the Triforce itself appears.

=== ''The Adventure of Link'' ===
[[File:TAoL Triforce.png|thumb|Link using the power of the united Triforce to awaken Princess Zelda, as seen in {{TAoL|-}}|221x221px|left]]
The Triforce of Courage makes its first appearance in {{TAoL}}, debuting the full essence. In the prologue of the game, it is mentioned that a historic [[King of Hyrule]] used the Triforce to keep peace in Hyrule. He eventually passed away, however, and, fearing someone would misuse the powers of the Triforce, hid the Triforce of Courage in the [[Great Palace]]. Here, it could only be obtained by one who could fight his way through six other palaces to break the magical seal left by the king.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=|game=TAoL|page=9-10}}</ref><ref name="TAoL Manual 11">{{Cite manual|quote=|game=TAoL|page=11}}</ref> Guarded by a wizened [[Sage]], the Triforce could only be recovered by a hero capable of defeating not only the many soldiers and monsters set to guard the Great Palace, but even his [[Link's Shadow|very shadow]]. Link overcame these obstacles to ultimately achieve the third Triforce (as he already obtained the Wisdom and Power pieces in the [[The Legend of Zelda|prequel]]), and used the power of the united relic to wake the slumbering Princess Zelda.<ref name="TAoL Manual 11" /> The completed Triforce can also be seen at the end of the credits.

=== ''A Link to the Past'' ===
[[File:ALttP Triforce Sprite.png|left]]
[[File:ALttP Triforce Credits Scene.png|thumb|right|Link obtains the complete Triforce after defeating Ganon, as seen in {{ALttP|-}}|245x245px]]
The Triforce in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' plays a major role for the first time in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]. It is first revealed as a divine manifestation of the [[Golden Goddess|Goddesses]]' power. Bestowed upon Hyrule upon their departure, it lay hidden in the fabled [[Golden Land]], where it beckoned the people of Hyrule to seek it in hopes that someone worthy of its power would find it.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=|game=ALttP|page=3-4}}</ref> Although many tried to find it, none knew of its exact location, not even the Sages, because that knowledge had been lost with time.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=|game=ALttP|page=4}}</ref> Eventually, the search for the Triforce precipitated a great war, which ended when the evil thief [[Ganondorf]] and his minions broke the seal into the hidden land.<ref name="ALttP Manual 3" /><ref name="ALttP Manual 5" /> Upon murdering his companions and claiming the Triforce for himself, used its power to become Ganon and turn the Golden Land into the [[Dark World]] with his vile wish of conquering Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|Ganon's wish was to conquer the world. That wish changed the Golden Land to the Dark World.|Essence of the Triforce|ALttP}}</ref> Raising an army of monsters to assault the land of Hyrule (the [[Light World]]), Ganon was nearly successful in spreading his evil over all the land, but was stopped when the [[Knights of Hyrule]] and the [[Sage|Seven Wise Men]] sealed him in the Dark World.<ref name="ALttP Manual 5-6" /> Still in possession of the Triforce, Ganon used [[Agahnim]] to crack the seal between worlds by teleporting the seven [[Maiden|Maidens,]] including Princess Zelda, who were descended from the Wise Men, one by one into the Dark World, sealing them in [[Crystal|crystals]] and creating a portal between the worlds.<ref>{{Cite|Agahnim has seized control of the castle and is now trying to open the seven wise men's seal.|Princess Zelda|ALttP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|He is trying to open a larger gate between worlds near the castle using our powers.|Maiden|ALttP}}</ref> 

After obtaining the [[Master Sword]], the [[Silver Arrow]]s, and rescuing the seven maidens, Link destroys Ganon inside the [[Pyramid of Power]] and recovers the complete Triforce for himself. Instructed by the [[essence of the Triforce]] to take it with a wish in his heart, Link used its power to cleanse the evil that had been done by Ganon: healing those who had been wounded by his evil, restoring the Dark World back to the Golden Land, and restoring peace to Hyrule entirely.<ref name="wish" /> The Triforce once again rested in the hands of Hyrule’s [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Family]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book=HH|publisher=|page=97}}</ref> The in-game Triforce, however, is mostly seen only in the title screen and credits, though with its three separate parts unconnected.

=== ''Ocarina of Time'' ===
[[File:Triforce (Ocarina of Time).png|thumb|left|The Triforce as seen in {{OoT|-}}|255x255px]]
The history of the Triforce is further elaborated upon in {{OoT}}. Its nature of splitting into three pieces when someone with an unbalanced heart touches it makes its debut, and is revealed to be one of the many [[Sheikah/Sheikah Legends#The Legend of the Sacred Triangle|Sheikah legends]] spoken of in the game. It is also the first time all three central characters of the series have their signature component parts of the Triforce that would become standard for later games and other media.

[[Ganondorf]], the sole male [[Gerudo]] from the [[Gerudo Desert|desert]] in the west of [[Hyrule]], is in search of the Triforce to use it to conquer Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|What Ganondorf is after must be nothing less than the Triforce of the Sacred Realm. He must have come to Hyrule to obtain it! And, he wants to conquer Hyrule... no, the entire world!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Although Princess Zelda and Link tried to protect the Triforce and the entrance to the Sacred Realm to the best of their ability, the latter retrieves the [[Master Sword]] from the [[Pedestal of Time]] and unknowingly opens the gates to the [[Sacred Realm]], allowing Ganondorf to enter the realm uncontested.<ref>{{Cite|Geh heh heh! Excellent work! As I thought, you held the keys to the Door of Time! You have led me to the gates of the Sacred Realm... Yes, I owe it all to you, kid!|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref> In that instant, Ganondorf seized his opportunity and attempted to lay claim to the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite|Ganondorf, the Gerudo King of Thieves, used it to enter this forbidden Sacred Realm! He obtained the Triforce from the Temple of Light, and with its power, he became the King of Evil... His evil power radiated from the temples of Hyrule, and in seven short years, it transformed Hyrule into a world of monsters.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> Nonetheless, there was another tale told about the Triforce: If a person whose Power, Wisdom, and Courage were out of balance with each other should lay their hand upon it, the Triforce would shatter into its three separate pieces. The person would instead keep the piece of Triforce that represents the thing one most believes in, and the other two pieces would seek out a hiding place in the body of those chosen by destiny.<ref name="Quote9" /> When Ganondorf laid his hand upon the Triforce, this prophecy came true: The Triforce indeed shattered, with Ganondorf keeping the Triforce of Power, as [[Din (Goddess)|Din]]'s blessings is what he believes are the most important, while the Triforce of Wisdom going to [[Princess Zelda]], and the Triforce of Courage going to [[Link]], respectively.<ref name="Quote10" /> Ganondorf used the Triforce of Power to rule over Hyrule for seven years, ushering the kingdom into a dark age.

Eventually, Link, now proclaimed as the [[Hero of Time]], with the power of the Triforce of Courage, ultimately defeats [[Ganon]], allowing Princess Zelda and the Six Sages to send the King of Evil into the void of the [[Evil Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|Ancient Creators of Hyrule! Now, open the sealed door and send the Evil Incarnation of Darkness into the void of the Evil Realm!!|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> The princess then uses her Triforce of Wisdom and the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]] to send Link back to his original time (which [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|erases his adult history of saving Hyrule]]),<ref>{{Cite|Link, give the Ocarina to me... As a Sage, I can return you to your original time with it.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> while Ganondorf, still with the Triforce of Power in his hand, furiously swears to "exterminate" Link's, Zelda's, and the Sages' descendants once the seal is broken, foreshadowing the events of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Someday... When this seal is broken.... That is when I will exterminate your descendants!!  As long as the Triforce of Power is in my hand....|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref>

If Link were to be [[Game Over|tragically defeated]] by Ganon, the archvillian steals his Triforce of Courage and Princess Zelda's Triforce of Wisdom, gaining the Full Force at last. But the Seven Sages refuse to back down in spite of losing their beloved hero and friend, so they resort to seal Ganon away into the Evil Realm similarly to the game's true ending, taking the Triforce with him and leading to the Downfall Timeline.
[[File:OoT Triforce Concept.png|thumb|Link encountering the full Triforce in the beta version of the game]]
Out of all current games in the core series, {{OoT|-}} has been the basis of the most rumors on receiving the Triforce in any ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' game to date. Although it is well known that it is actually not an item that can be received in the [[Nintendo 64]] game, many stories and rumors from fans claiming they have obtained the Triforce by doctored photos  have come up. These rumors were mostly due to an engraving of the Triforce on the Quest Status menu, centered between all of the [[Medallions]], although it is only there for decoration purposes. In the beta screenshots and footage, however, the completed Triforce can be seen inside the [[Temple of Time]] where Link can actually pick it up.

=== ''Twilight Princess'' ===
:''See also: [[Sacred Realm#The Story of the Interlopers|The Story of the Interlopers]]''
[[File:Zelda's Triforce.png|thumb|left|Princess Zelda revealing she is the holder of the Triforce of Wisdom|228x228px]]
The word "Triforce" is not used at any point during {{TP}}. Nonetheless, the Triforce is obviously well-known because markings of it exist on the [[Hylian Shield]] and on various walls around [[Castle Town]] and [[Hyrule Castle]]. Its power is also spoken of by the ancient Sages of Hyrule who guarded the [[Mirror of Twilight]], and by the [[Light Spirit]] [[Lanayru (Spirit)|Lanayru]] when she tells Link the story of the interlopers who tried to take over the Sacred Realm and, with it, the Triforce.<ref name="sage" /> Unlike in {{OoT|-}} and {{TWW|-}}, Ganondorf makes no attempt to retrieve the other Triforce parts, despite being in the same position of power as he was in the former: the "ruler" of Hyrule but lacking omnipotence.

In this game, the wielders of the three parts of the Triforce (Power, Wisdom, and Courage) are obviously Ganondorf, Princess Zelda, and Link, the same as in {{OoT|-}} (and most other installments). Each of the Triforce bearers can be seen using their piece of the Triforce at various points in the game, such as when Link is protected by the Triforce of Courage's power when he first enters into the [[Twilight]]. Interestingly, the wielders of the Triforce are said to be "blessed by the goddesses".<ref>{{Cite|Link... Hero sent by the goddesses... Like you, I have been granted special powers by the goddesses...|Princess Zelda|TP}}</ref> Though it is not directly stated, it appears that Zelda transfers her piece of the Triforce to [[Midna]] to save her life when Link brings the dying [[Twili]] imp to the princess.<ref name="heal" /> The princess herself tries to use her Triforce of Wisdom to revive Link from his [[Wolf Link|wolf form]] due to Ganondorf's Triforce of Power, but it proved ineffective. Ganondorf survived his [[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|execution]] thanks to the Triforce of Power dwelling inside him before he was cast into the [[Twilight Realm]] by the Sages.

Link, Princess Zelda, and possibly Ganondorf are not turned to spirits by the [[Twilight]], just as Midna, once she receives Zelda's piece of the Triforce, does not have to transform into a shadow in the Light World, signifying that their Triforce pieces protect them from evil magic.<ref name="sign" /> It is worth noting that when Link thrusts the Master Sword into Ganondorf's gaping wound in his chest at the end of the final battle, the villain's Triforce of Power stops glowing on his hand, causing him to slowly die from the mortal blow.

=== ''Phantom Hourglass'' ===
{{images|PH}}
The Triforce is not mentioned at all in {{PH}} except for an instance in the [[Temple of the Ocean King]]. Link is told that in order to pass through a door in the Temple, the "sacred crest" must be drawn on it.<ref>{{Cite|In a single stroke, draw the sacred crest to reveal a new path.|N/A|PH}}</ref>  There are two valid symbols that open this door, and the one [[Zauz]] shows Link is the Triforce. In addition, the three triangular pedestals that Link must place [[Force Gems]] on to continue in various rooms are in the shape of the Triforce, albeit each piece not touching.

It should be noted that while the Triforce, which is considered to be the famous emblem of Hyrule and its [[Hyrule Royal Family]], is nowhere to be seen, the[[Spirits of Good#Symbol of the Spirits| symbol of the Spirits]] appears throughout many places of the "[[new Hyrule]]."

=== ''Skyward Sword'' ===
[[File:SS Triforce Assembled.png|thumb|right|300px|The Triforce assembles atop the Statue of the Goddess, as seen in {{SS|-}}]]
In {{SS}}, the Triforce's origins are revealed. It is first referred to during the game's introduction. When the [[Golden Goddesses|gods of old]] departed from the world, they trusted their ultimate power, the Triforce, in the hands of the goddess [[Hylia]] to protect it.<ref>{{Cite|Handed down by the gods of old, this power gave its holder the means to make any desire a reality. Such was the might of the ultimate power that the old ones placed it in the care of the goddess.|N/A|SS}}</ref> [[Demise]], wanting to make the world his own, gathered an army of monsters and together launched an attack against the people of the surface, murdering them and causing misfortunes all just to get their hands on the power guarded by Hylia.<ref name="Ultimate Power" /><ref name="Demise" /> To prevent the great power from falling into the hands of the demonic creatures—and specifically their leader, Demise—Her Grace gathered the surviving humans on a piece of land and sent it to the heavens, beyond the clouds and out of reach from the evil plaguing the land.<ref>{{Cite|To prevent this great power from falling into the hands of the evil swarming the lands... The goddess gathered the surviving humans on an outcropping of earth. She sent it skyward, beyond the reach of the demonic hordes. Beyond even the clouds.|N/A|SS}}</ref> She then joined forces with the five tribes of the land and defeated Demise, sealing him away in the [[Sealed Grounds]], where he took the new form of "[[The Imprisoned]]".<ref>{{Cite|With the humans safe, the goddess joined forces with the land dwellers and fought the evil forces, sealing them away. At last, peace was restored to the surface.|N/A|SS}}</ref>

Unbeknownst to anyone, Hylia also hid the entrances to the land where the Triforce lay on this aerial outcropping, in a secret structure called the [[Sky Keep]].<ref>{{Cite|For the safety of all things, she hid the Triforce somewhere within the rock you call Skyloft. However, its location has been kept secret. Even I do not know where it rests now.|Levias|SS}}</ref> Inside this keep were three separate gates that led to another realm, each gate leading to a different piece of the Triforce. Before departing, Hylia also entrusted the guardians of the surface as well as [[Levias]] to teach the chosen hero their part of the [[Song of the Hero]], because, should the hero succeed in conquering the last trial, the path to the Sky Keep would open to him.<ref>{{Cite|Now that you know the song, I trust you know what to do with it. That old song opens a door to a great trial. Should you succeed in conquering the challenge awaiting you there, I'm certain the path to the Triforce will be revealed to you.|Levias|SS}}</ref> The Sky Keep was sealed for an age below [[Skyloft]], and generations later, when it becomes clear that there is no other way to prevent The Imprisoned from eventually resurrecting himself, [[Princess Zelda]] sends [[Link]] to learn the [[Song of the Hero]],<ref>{{Cite|When Demise is finally gone, there will be no more need for the seal that binds him, and then I'll be able to wake up.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> which would unlock the [[Silent Realm|final trial]] to receive the [[Stone of Trials]] needed to open the path to the Sky Keep. Link succeeds in his task and, with the Triforce in hand, the young hero goes up onto the [[Statue of the Goddess]] and makes his wish: the destruction of Demise. The Triforce's power causes the entire island containing the Goddess Statue to break free from Skyloft and plummet down to the surface, thus returning to its original location at the Sealed Grounds and destroying the Imprisoned just as it attempts to break out once more with the Sky Keep itself.<ref>{{Cite|Furthermore, I have detected the successful eradication of Demise. Your prayers to the Triforce have been heard.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Nonetheless, subsequent events made it necessary to travel back to the past and defeat Demise there as well. When this is accomplished, [[Impa]] reminds Zelda that she must stay in the past to watch over the Triforce because its might was too powerful to leave in "the grasp of man". She also mentions that once the Triforce has served its purpose, it will be secreted away once again, its existence kept hidden from everyone.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda, I shall watch over the Triforce. Its power is too great to leave in the grasp of man. Dependence on its might is an invitation to disaster.When it has served its purpose, it must be secreted away to lay dormant once again...the knowledge of its existence hidden from mortal history. These are the words the goddess spoke to me long ago. I remember them well. As do you, I'm sure.|Impa|SS}}</ref> Princess Zelda assents and returns to her own time with Link and resolves to stay below the clouds to continue guarding the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite|Look around us! As a child, I always dreamed of a world below. I wanted to see the surface with my own eyes and feel the land's warm breeze on my skin. I... I think I want to live here. I always want to feel solid ground beneath my feet, see the clouds above my head|Zelda|SS}}</ref>

Many years after the events of {{SS|-}}, there was an [[Zelda Timeline#The Era of Chaos|era of chaos]] with wars and fighting over the Triforce, which was located in the [[Sacred Realm]]. To put an end to these wars, an ancient sage, [[Rauru]], built over the remains of the [[Sealed Temple]] a new Temple of Time, which contained the only entrance to the Sacred Realm.<ref name="E 77" /> Using the [[Master Sword]] and the three [[Spiritual Stones]] as keys to the Sacred Realm, this new temple was the sanctified gateway between Hyrule and the Sacred Realm, and its purpose was to protect that gateway, and thus the Triforce, from evildoers who would seek to misuse it.<ref>{{Cite|The Temple of Time is the entrance through which you can enter the Sacred Realm from our world.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ages ago, we ancient Sages built the Temple of Time to protect the entrance to the Sacred Realm...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So, the ancient Sages built the Temple of Time to protect the Triforce from evil ones.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Inside the Temple of Time is a chamber that is home to the Master Sword and the [[Pedestal of Time]]; however, the entrance to this chamber is sealed by the Door of Time.<ref>{{Cite|But the entrance is sealed with a stone wall called the Door of Time. And, in order to open the door, it is said that you need to collect three Spiritual Stones.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> When the Master Sword was infused into the Pedestal of Time, it sealed that gateway in the hopes of preventing all but the most worthy to reach the Sacred Realm, where the sacred Triforce lies within the [[Temple of Light]].

=== ''A Link Between Worlds'' ===
The Triforce, still split into three pieces after the events of {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}, appears again in {{ALBW}}. Ganon claimed the Triforce of Power whilst he was still sealed; the Triforce of Wisdom remained with the [[Princess Zelda|Royal Family]]; and the Triforce of Courage merged into the heart of the [[Link|Hero Eternal]], where it slumbers.<ref>{{Cite Web|retrieved=November 30, 2013|url=http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ve1MG5Xee64|title=Opening Legend of "A Link Between Worlds"|site=YouTube}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Triforce was split into three pieces, separated forever. One piece remains with the royal family. Another piece has fallen into the hands of Ganon, sealed with him. The third piece of the Triforce has vanished, though legend says it is hidden in the spirit of a true hero. It slumbers now somewhere in Hyrule--waiting for the time when the world needs a new hero.|Painting|ALBW}}</ref> To obtain the Triforces of Power and Wisdom, [[Yuga]] captures the seven Sages and Princess Zelda, turning them all into [[Painting|paintings]] that he then uses to free Ganon himself. He then [[Yuga Ganon|merges with Ganon]], thus gaining the Triforce of Power, and also gains the Triforce of Wisdom through [[Princess Hilda]] after she steals it from Zelda, making his power almost complete. Once Link has rescued all of the Sages, they work together to summon the Triforce of Courage, which then enters into Link's possession.

=====Lorulean Triforce=====
[[File:LorulebackstoryALBW.png|left|thumb|Lorule's Triforce being destroyed|242x242px]]
Once Link storms [[Lorule Castle]] to rescue Zelda, Princess Hilda reveals that, at one time, [[Lorule]] had its own parallel counterpart of the Triforce, which bears the same name and visually resembles an inverted version of Hyrule's, appearing upside-down. Hilda also implies that Lorule's Triforce is ''not'' based on the well-known traits of Power, Wisdom, and Courage.<ref>{{Cite|We NEED a Triforce. So imagine my surprise when I learned of the existence of another one. Yours. A Triforce based on such virtues as Power, Wisdom, and Courage.|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref> As was the case in Hyrule, wars were fought over this Triforce, prompting Hilda's ancestors to destroy it to end the bloodshed.<ref>{{Cite|My ancestors got rid of the Triforce to stop the war—by destroying it.|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref> Unfortunately, destroying this Triforce threw Lorule out of balance, causing the kingdom to begin crumbling. To save her kingdom, Princess Hilda sent Yuga to Hyrule to steal the Triforce, which would thus restore balance to Lorule, though at the same time condemn Hyrule to the same collapse.<ref>{{Cite|I wish the best for our kingdom. But by ruining Hyrule...? You'd bring the absolute worst in Lorule.|Ravio|ALBW}}</ref> Hilda then takes the Triforce of Wisdom for herself, ordering Yuga (as [[Yuga Ganon]]) to attack Link to obtain the final Triforce. Link succeeds in defeating Yuga, however, prompting an angered Hilda to demand Yuga's Triforce of Power; in response, he instead reveals that he never cared for Lorule's fate, merely desiring the Triforce for himself.<ref>{{Cite|My dear, sweet, deluded Hilda...! What care have I to save your crumbling kingdom? It's no better than that hideous Hyrule. When the Triforce is mine, I plan to remake Lorule in MY image.|Yuga|ALBW}}</ref>[[File:ALBW Lorule Sacred Realm Triforce.png|right|thumb|The restored inverted Triforce within Lorule's Sacred Realm|250x250px]] He then turns Princess Hilda into a painting and merges with her, gaining her Triforce of Wisdom. With help from Princess Zelda, however, Link manages to ultimately defeat Yuga Ganon, freeing both princesses. After [[Ravio]] manages to persuade Hilda of the evil nature of her actions, Hilda agrees to send Link and Zelda back to Hyrule, though seemingly dooming her kingdom in the process. Once back in Hyrule, however, Link and Princess Zelda wish together on Hyrule's Triforce to restore its Lorulean counterpart. As it reappears, Lorule's skies begin to lighten as Ravio looks on in shock and Hilda expresses tearful gratitude, seemingly indicating that Lorule is once more in balance.

=== ''Tri Force Heroes'' ===
Although the true Triforce does not appear in {{TFH}}, Triforce-shaped portals called '''Triforce Gateways''' appear in [[Hytopia Castle]] and throughout the [[Drablands]]. The Triforce Gateways are the only known entrances to the Drablands. The power of these gateways will only open when three heroes gather, allowing for travel between the worlds.<ref>{{Cite|This is a Triforce gateway. Using its power is the only way to travel to the Drablands. Only when three heroes gather will the Triforce's power awaken and allow for travel between the worlds. The way will not open for one hero alone.|Match Master|TFH}}</ref> Once three heroes arrive, the [[Match Master]] will open the Triforce Gateway and permit them entry. In the [[Hall of the Doppels]], if Link is without allies, then he can still enter the Drablands, but with the presence of two [[Doppel]]s.

In each Level of the Drablands are four Stages, with a Triforce Gateway at the end of each one. These gateways will only appear and activate once the [[Links]] have fulfilled certain requirements in each Stage, such as opening a pathway, defeating all enemies, or picking up the available items. Stepping on these gateways will advance the Links to the next Stage.

===''Breath of the Wild''===
Like in {{TMC}} and {{TP}}, the word "Triforce" never appears in {{BotW}} but its design can be seen on architecture, clothing, and weapons. {{Term|BotW|Zonai|link}} ruins have statues of dragons, owls, and boars which represent Courage, Wisdom, and Power respectively.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book=CaC|publisher=|page=342}}</ref> When Princess Zelda awakens her power during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, the Triforce symbol appears on the back of her hand, and when she deals the final blow to {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, a glowing image of the Triforce appears before her being using it to seal away the monster.

==={{TotK|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', in addition to the various design appearances returning from ''Breath of the Wild,'' the Triforce symbol also appears as a tattoo on {{Term|TotK|Queen Sonia|link}}'s arm and as a stylized stained glass window in {{Term|TotK|King Rauru|link}} and Sonia's castle. When Link retrieves the Master Sword, the Triforce can be seen glowing on the back of Princess Zelda's hand.

=== ''Echoes of Wisdom'' ===
[[File:EoW Triforce.jpg|left|230px|thumb|Link and Princess Zelda obtaining the completed “Prime Energy”]]
The Triforce in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]'' is never called by its common name from past iterations but rather as the "Prime Energy", despite sharing its prefix "Tri" with the goddesses-made guardian species. The Triforce in this game is very similar to its prior appearances, splitting itself in late game when [[Tekom]] tried to seize it, only able to keep the Triforce of Power, sending the two other pieces to the chosen ones, Princess Zelda and Link. Tekom then fused with [[Null]], granting it enough energy to plunge most of Hyrule into the [[Still World]] on an even larger scale than before. The Triforce of Power could still be physically recovered from Null by Zelda using the [[Tri-Rod|Tri Rod]] pulling ability, causing its demise. After reuniting the Triforce to its complete state, Princess Zelda and Link both touched it, wishing to save Hyrule from the Still World, returning the kingdom to safety and peace

==Other Appearances==
{{Main|Triforce/Other Appearances}}
The Triforce has made several other appearances, including {{TLoZ|-}} comics, mangas, animated shows, and the {{ssb|Super Smash Bros. (series)|{{SSB|-}} series}}. Although its manifestation varies, it still holds the role as being a sacred, powerful relic in Hyrule.

== Day of the Triforce ==
''Main article: [[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 5|Day of the Triforce]]''

The Day of the Triforce is an event that happens once every ten years where the Triforce completely vanishes from the world, from dawn until dusk, for an unexplained reason. This becomes significant in the Valiant Comics story "Day of the Triforce" where Ganon assures Shadow Link that Link will be weakened during the upcoming duel over the throne of Calatia, with Link representing Seline, the rightful queen overthrown by Shadow Link. Unfortunately for Shadow Link, the real Link proves just as capable without the Triforce of Courage as he is with it, and he is forced to surrender.

==Trivia==
*The Triforce is the trademark symbol of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], followed by the [[Master Sword]].
*The Triforce, in development, did not start with three pieces. As seen in the [[The Legend of Zelda|very first game]], it only featured the [[Triforce of Power]] and [[Triforce of Wisdom|Wisdom]]. The [[Triforce of Courage]] was added to the group in its [[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|sequel]]. This implies that the name "Triforce" initially referred to the shape of the pieces, instead of the number of pieces as is commonly believed.
* The Japanese name for the Triumph Forks is ''Tarai to Hosu'', which means "Bucket and Hose", whilst Triforce is ''Toraifōsu'', so the name is a pun in both languages (Japanese and English).
** The Triumph Forks are mentioned again as an [[Cameos in The Legend of Zelda|Easter Egg]] in {{TMC|-}}, appearing as titles of books in the library (the titles appear in [[Hylian Language|Hylian]], translated as Ta Ra I To Ho Su). The books bearing the Triumph Forks titles appear next to other books referencing the original titles of the ''Oracle'' series.
* The mainline games in the series where the Triforce is used in its complete form so far are ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Oracle of Ages]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]''.
** Many fans believe the completed Triforce appears in {{BotW|-}}, due to the symbol appearing when Zelda's powers awaken, but there is no evidence, in-game or in {{CaC|-}}, to suggest that this is the case.
[[File:Hōjō clan Triforce.svg|thumb|The original Hōjō clan symbol, upon which the Triforce's appearance is directly based.]]
* According to [[Shigeru Miyamoto]], during the development of ''The Legend of Zelda'', the Triforce's fragments were originally planned to be electronic chips, as the in-game setting was set to be in the past and future before it was scrapped. The symbol of the golden relic itself was inspired by the emblem of the [[wikipedia:Hōjō_clan|Hōjō clan]], a samurai family who took control of Japan in the 13th century, and their own symbol is historically identical.
** The Triforce also resembles the initial state of the Sierpinski Triangle, a famous fractal, a shape that can continuously replicate inside of itself.
* [[Link]] is the only character in franchise's core series to obtain all three of the Triforce pieces individually, based on the events of {{TLoZ|-}} and its sequel, {{TAoL|-}}. He manages to fully reassemble the shattered Triforce of Wisdom, which normally belongs to [[Princess Zelda]]. When he destroys [[Ganon]] with a [[Silver Arrow]], he recovers the Triforce of Power as well. The former game is also the only instance in the franchise where the Triforce of Power is taken away from Ganon entirely. Later, after defeating [[Dark Link#The Adventure of Link|Dark Link]], he gains the Triforce of Courage, which itself would become his standard piece for all future games starting in ''Ocarina of Time''.
** ''Skyward Sword'' is currently the only game where Link obtains all three pieces of the Triforce in a single game. At the end of {{TLoZ|-}} he relinquishes at least the Triforce of Wisdom back to Zelda, while the location of the Triforce of Power between then and Link making his wish at the end of {{TAoL|-}} remains unknown.
* ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords|Four Swords]]'', and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]'' are the only games so far where the Triforce makes no appearance, not even as a symbol.
*Both instances of the Triforce of Wisdom and Courage shattering into smaller pieces in {{TLoZ|-}} and {{TWW|-}} respectively have the same number of fragments - 8.
*The Master Sword, another essential item of the series, as seen from some games, often required Link to test great trials of his power, wisdom, and courage to obtain the legendary blade, coincidentally the same forces as the Triforce.[[File:MM unused Triforce screen transition.png|thumb|228x228px|The unused Triforce screen transition as seen in ''Majora's Mask'']]
*In ''Ocarina of Time'', there is an [[Unused Content|unused]] screen transition effect using the Triforce symbol as its silhouette. Although unavailable under normal circumstances, it can be accessed by hacking. This also applies for ''Majora's Mask'', as its engine was reused. It is unknown why it was left out for their final releases.
*Despite its explicit importance in the franchise, specifically the mainline games' storyline, the Triforce does not appear to have any significant role in terms of gameplay unlike the Master Sword, as in the case of Link, the Triforce of Courage and/or other pieces are usually simply given to him late in his quest and cannot be used as an item.
*Although the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series do not have its own singular equivalent to the Triforce, few media within its franchise contain references to it. For example, in ''[[mariowiki:Mario_&_Luigi:_Superstar_Saga|Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', a small, yellow triangular object known as the [[mariowiki:Great_Force|Great Force]] bares a resemblance to ''Zelda''<nowiki/>'s Triforce. In addition, in the 1986 Japan-only animated film ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros.:_Peach-hime_Kyūshutsu_Dai_Sakusen!|Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]'', [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] journeyed far to collect the "Triforce" (which its power consists of a [[mariowiki:Super_Mushroom|Super Mushroom]], [[mariowiki:Fire_Flower|Fire Flower]] and [[mariowiki:Super_Star|Super Star]]) in order to save [[Princess Peach]], defeat [[Bowser]] and restore peace in the [[mariowiki:Mushroom_Kingdom|Mushroom Kingdom]].
*In the beta version of ''The Legend of Zelda'', the Triforce was originally going to be called '''Power Triangle'''.{{Fact}}
*As seen in a few games such as ''Ocarina of Time'' and ''Twilight Princess'', the three Triforce pieces inherently share certain mystical abilities such as healing, surviving otherwise fatal blows, and increased raw power for their hosts' own techniques, but they do each possess unique powers corresponding to their roles.[[File:OoT Ganondorf calling Triforce "toys".png|thumb|255x255px|A [[Iconic Quotes from The Legend of Zelda|memetic quote]] in ''Ocarina of Time'' exists where Ganondorf is dismissive of the Triforce's fragments Zelda and Link respectively own]]
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', its [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|remake]], as well as its [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|manga adaptation by Akira Himekawa]], when [[Ganondorf]] demands Link and Princess Zelda to give their respective Triforce pieces to him before the final battle, the Evil King can be humorously heard calling the Wisdom and Courage parts "toys", as he believes they are too much for the young heroes to handle and have no proper use for them, unlike how the villain intended with the Power piece.
*The [[Golden Goddesses#Physical Appearance|three colors]] that represent each [[Golden Goddesses]], red (Din), blue (Nayru), and green (Farore), are interestingly the three primary colors of visible light. When these colors are combined, a white light results. When the three deities combined their essences and left, the yellow (or golden) Triforce resulted.
*The size of the Triforce appears to be inconsistent throughout the series. Although the complete form of the relic is normally large, its component parts and shards are often rather small, but they have the ability to change its scale.
*In ''The Legend of Zelda'', the Triforce, specifically the Triforce of Power, can be seen in the title logo and credits. In the same game, this piece is also shown upside-down unlike the Triforce of Wisdom. An inverted Triforce would not be seen in the mainline ''Zelda'' games until ''A Link Between Worlds'', released nearly three decades later. The complete Triforce on the title screen would be used for ''A Link to the Past'' and ''A Link Between Worlds,'' as well as in the box artwork of the Japanese version of ''The Adventure of Link''.
*In [[The Legend of Zelda (television series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' TV series]], the Triforce of Power and Wisdom have the power of speech, which does not occur in the games.
**Despite this, in ''A Link to the Past'', the [[Essence of the Triforce]] indeed talks to Link when encountered during the game's ending.
*''The Wind Waker'' is the only game in the series so far where more than one pieces of the Triforce were broken into smaller fragments, which are the Wisdom and Courage component parts.
*The [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] cartridges of ''The Legend of Zelda'' and ''The Adventure of Link'' being golden is likely a reference to the Triforce, due to itself being usually golden in color.
*There is a large misconception in ''A Link to the Past'' among ''The Legend of Zelda'' series' community that Ganon wields and fully possesses the power of the complete Triforce during the [[Final Boss]] battle (and therefore controversially considering the Hero of Legend to be the ultimate Link in the franchise). Contrary to popular belief, Ganon, although very powerful, does '''not''' harness any fragment of the Triforce within him during this encounter, including his usual piece the Triforce of Power, as the villain himself, although he did indeed wished to rule the [[Sacred Realm|Golden Land]] prior to the game's events and plans to do it again for the [[Light World]] (Hyrule), he was sealed in the [[Dark World]] by the [[Sage|Seven Wise Men]] and cannot escape with his own power. Consequently, he requires [[Agahnim]] to weaken his seal with the seven [[Maiden]]s, explained in-game, the instruction manual, and even the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past — Nintendo Player's Guide|official strategy guide]]. The erroneous belief is likely based on the fact that the full Triforce does appear following Ganon's ultimate defeat. The golden relic is seen as its component parts from its own pedestal and not absorbed by anyone before Link makes his peaceful wish with it. This would technically be similar to what was seen later in the ''Wind Waker'' when Ganondorf separates the pieces from their respective hosts to reform the Triforce in order to make a wish but fails to do so due to [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|King Daphnes]]' interference.
**Ironically, in ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'', Ganon does possess the full Triforce, as he manages to steal all pieces and it can be seen appearing out of his body upon his ultimate defeat in its main campaign.
*The color of the Triforce is not consistent throughout the games and other media within the franchise. Although normally seen as yellow or golden, there are some cases of the relic or its component parts shown in orange, red, blue, cyan, and even black.[[File:Triforce (SSBB).jpg|thumb|266x266px|Link with the full Triforce resonating on his hand in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
*In spite of being a non-canon spinoff appearance, in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', Link (who appears as his ''Twilight Princess'' incarnation) seemingly possesses the completed Triforce, as in his introduction cutscene of the [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary|Subspace Emissary]] mode, when the hero pulls the Master Sword from its pedestal, the [[mark of the Triforce]] appears in his hand with all three sacred triangles resonating.
*Although there is no official name of the complete Triforce, rather than its split pieces in the series other than "Triforce", some fans claim the full essence as "Triforce of Faith", due to its ability to grant any wish.
*Unlike Ganon or Princess Zelda, whom their respective Triforce pieces are marked on their right hands, Link is the only character to have his on his left hand. This suggests the mark of the Triforce appears on the user's dominant hand.
**In ''A Link Between Worlds'', however, when [[Yuga]] merges himself with Ganon to become [[Yuga Ganon]], the Triforce of Power is seen on the villain's left hand rather than the right.
*In the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] starting in ''Brawl'', for any version of Link's [[Final Smash]], he would use the Triforce of Courage to trap an opponent into a giant void strongly resembling the whole Triforce, before unleashing a series of rapid slashes then hitting them with a powerful blow, shattering it and send the foe flying. This special attack is known as the [[Triforce Slash]].
*Although {{TAoL|-}} is the game in the franchise to introduce the full essence of the Triforce, its complete form was not used properly until the following installment ''A Link to the Past''. In the Japanese box art of the former title, the full golden relic can be seen behind Link. It's also seen at the ending message of the credits.
* The Triforce of Power is the only piece that has never been split into smaller fragments anywhere in the series, although the planned but cut [[The Legend of Zelda: Mystical Seed of Courage|third entry to the ''Oracle'' games]] had Ganondorf ironically splitting it into multiple parts as a means to safeguard it from Link and Princess Zelda, similar to {{TLoZ|-}} for the Triforce of Wisdom.{{Fact}}
* During the final battle with Ganondorf in {{TWW}}, Ganondorf and Zelda oddly retained their Triforce marks on their hands despite the Triforce itself already being used. This aspect was retained in its [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD|HD remaster]] as well.
* There is minor [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda Series|controversy]] among some fans that the Triforce is similar the [[wikipedia:Trinity|Holy Trinity]] from real-life Christianity religious beliefs, as each of its three equally powerful pieces represents the same divine source, and contains omnipotent mystical abilities when complete, much like how the Trinity represents [[wikipedia:God_in_Christianity|God]] (Father), specifically in connection with [[wikipedia:Jesus_in_Christianity|Jesus]] (Son) and the [[wikipedia:Holy_Spirit_in_Christianity|Holy Spirit]]. The Triforce pieces themselves are also positioned identically to the Trinity symbol, as Power, Wisdom, and Courage respectively match the Father, Son, and the Holy Spirit.
* In the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series, the Triforce was used as the logo representing ''The Legend of Zelda'' franchise for its respective characters, specifically Link ([[Young Link|Young]] and Adult), Princess Zelda, [[Sheik]], Ganondorf, and [[Toon Link]].
* Dummied dialogue from Ganondorf just before his death in {{TP|-}} indicated that those who wielded the Triforce pieces would be reborn and then obligated to confront each other in every age, implying such a fate had been preordained by the Golden Goddesses themselves.<ref>{{Cite Web|retrieved= April 6, 2022|url= https://tcrf.net/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Twilight_Princess/Unused_Text#Ganondorf|title= The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess/Unused Text|site= The Cutting Room Floor}}</ref>
* In the [[mariowiki:Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]] of the ''Super Mario'' franchise, specifically ''[[mariowiki:Mario Kart 8|Mario Kart 8]]'', the Triforce was used as the emblem for the Triforce Cup, the second cup of the game's ''[[The Legend of Zelda × Mario Kart 8|The Legend of Zelda x Mario Kart 8]]'' [[Downloadable Content|DLC]].
