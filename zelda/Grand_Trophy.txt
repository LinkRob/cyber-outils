The {{Term/Store|Grand Trophy||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Shabonne">{{Cite|This is the {{Color|BotW Blue|grand trophy}} that's been passed down by ten thousand years of sand-seal-racing champions.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> also referred to as a '''Jewel''',<ref>{{Cite|As proof of your victory, you've receive a {{Color|BotW Red|jewel}}! What happens when you place this {{Color|BotW Red|jewel}} on the pedestal?|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}} is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} that is found at the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rally|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. It is displayed in front of a {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|link}}. For 10,000 years, the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}} has been given to {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|link|display=Sand-Seal Racing}} champions.<ref name="Shabonne"/> They have passed down a legend about the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy|display=Trophy}},<ref>{{Cite|This trophy is something special, you know. Our champions have passed down a legend about it for ten thousand years.|Tali|BotW}}</ref> which states that the chosen one will gain a new power when the "holy jewel" is offered to a pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|The legend goes... "When the holy jewel is offered unto the pedestal, the chosen one will be granted a new power."|Tali|BotW}}</ref> However, no racers have been able to obtain this power.<ref>{{Cite|But that power has never been granted to anyone. Not even to me, obviously...|Tali|BotW}}</ref>

Initially, when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tries to take the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}}, {{Term|BotW|Shabonne|link}} will stop him and say only winners of the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race}} can touch it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hey, hey! Hey, hey, hey! Hey!!!}} That item is only for those who have proven themselves in the {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal races}}! Hands off!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy|display=Trophy}} by beating {{Term|BotW|Tali|link|display=Tali's}} record, which is 1 minute and 30 seconds.<ref>{{Cite|Tali's record is {{Color|BotW Red|1}}:{{Color|BotW Red|30}}! If you can beat that record, that {{Color|BotW Blue|trophy}} is yours.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> Agreeing to try and beat the record begins "{{Term|BotW|The Undefeated Champ|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} successfully beats {{Term|BotW|Tali}}'s time, {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} will be shocked.<ref>{{Cite|It's a... A new record?!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Tali}} will graciously hand over the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|I believe this belongs to you...|Tali|BotW}}</ref> She informs him of the legend surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy|display=Trophy}}, but since she senses a great power from him, she encourages him to give it a shot.<ref>{{Cite|And yet... I sense a power emanating from you... The likes of which I have never felt in anyone else. You should give it a shot.|Tali|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} places the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}} into the {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket}}, it will disappear and activate the {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|display=Socket}}. This causes {{Term|BotW|Raqa Zunzo Shrine|link}} to emerge from the sand, which completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
