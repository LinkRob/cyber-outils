The {{Term/Store|Piratian|Piratians|Series, OoS|plural}} are a recurring group of skeletal pirates in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250{{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Piratian}} were referred to as '''Stalfos Pirates''' and '''Skeletons''' in {{Guide|OoS|BradyGames}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |guide= BradyGames |page= 85{{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref> However, as these contradict the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:Samasa Piratian.png|left]]
In {{OoS}}, the Piratians' ship is stuck midways through the {{Term|OoS|Subrosia Cemetery|link}} and the [[Samasa Desert]].<ref>{{Cite|'Ave you seen our ship? It's over by the graveyard. We 'aven't fixed it, so it's in terrible shape.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> While the Piratians have made their residence in the [[House of Pirates]],<ref>{{Cite|Some weird guys called Piratians have settled down south.|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|How long do they plan on staying here!?!|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> Link must find the [[Rusty Bell]] in the Samasa Desert after the [[Cap'n]] of the Piratians deems him worthy of the task. Once given the task of locating and bringing back the bell, Link is able to relieve the Piratian who has been unsuccessful in his mission to find the missing bell, which will enable Link the ability to open the desert gate with the combination learned from a crew mate earlier. Upon entering the front end of the ship found in the desert, Link will find the ghost of a Piratian who can not move onto the afterlife until he finds the Captains missing Bell.<ref>{{Cite|Ahoy! When I was searching the desert for the bell, I got caught in a sandstorm. I awoke like this! I can't go on to the afterlife...|Piratian|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= Go inside the half-submergence Pirate Ship. Inside is a Pirate Skeleton ghost, who will tell you he was searching for his bell, but can't rest until it's found.|game= OoS|guide= Versus|page= 54}}</ref> The pirate asks Link to help find the bell, suggesting that he start by finding his skull rolling out in the desert.<ref>{{Cite|If ya pity me, help me find that bell! Me skull is rollin' out in the desert. Find that first.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> The skull can be found in the small northeastern oasis, and will inform Link that he can help by being carried around the desert.<ref>{{Cite|Carry me around. I'll tell ya if I think we're close.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> It can be picked up with the [[Power Bracelet]], and will shout out if dropped,<ref>{{Cite|Pthh! I got sand in me mouth!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Snf! I got water up my nose!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> and even yell out more if lost in the [[quicksand]].<ref>{{Cite|Argh! Ya dropped me!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> The skull will always reappear in the oasis, regardless of how it was lost. After finding the Rusty Bell, the Piratian's skull will disappear after claiming that it can finally rest.<ref>{{Cite|Well, at least I can go on to the afterlife now! The rest is up to you!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref>
Upon showing the Captain the Rusty Bell, he will tell Link of its unacceptable condition,<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Surely this- Ar! This thing's rusted something awful! What are you pullin'? This won't do! Go get it polished!|Captain|OoS}}</ref> forcing Link to bring it to the [[Subrosian Smithy]] for polishing. Receiving the polished bell brings the Piratian Captain around, where he then decides to move the ship, allowing Link to reach the [[Explorer's Crypt]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, [[Link]] must bring to the Piratians the [[Zora Scale]], which can dissipate the [[whirlpool]]s, allowing them to sail on the [[Sea of Storms]]. In return, they give Link the [[Tokay Eyeball]], necessary to pass the [[Sea of No Return]]. In a [[Linked Game]], the Piratians only appear in the present.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{OoA|-}} (Himekawa)===
The Piratians make an appearance in the {{OoA|Himekawa}}. A piratian enters the Captain's cabin and informs him that two castaways were fished from the sea.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Captain... We fished out a couple castaways. What should we-?|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 104}}</ref> The Captain asks him to describe the two.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Describe 'em.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 104}}</ref> The piratian says the castaways are two little brats.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Two little brats.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 104}}</ref> The Captain says to toss them below deck for use later, before changing his mind and asking for them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= They can at least swab the deck or man the lookout. Toss 'em below decks... No, wait. First...Bring 'em to my cabin.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 104}}</ref> A piratian escorts the castaways, Link and [[Ralph]], to the cabin as another piratian leaves. 

When the Captain sets the ship on course for [[Labrynna]],<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Set a course for Labrynna!!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 109}}</ref> a piratian says it is no use, they will sink.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= It's no use, Captain!! We'll sink!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 109}}</ref> He tells the piratian to stop wailing and the others begin to work.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Stop yer wailing!!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 109}}</ref> Link and Ralph try to escape the ship but are confronted by the Captain and the piratians. As piratians attack him, Ralph says he cannot die for he has to find [[Nayru]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I can't die now! I have to find Nayru!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 111}}</ref> Link promises the Captain that if they leave, they can fix everything for him, his crew, and his [[Queen Ambi|lady]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Captain, we have to leave! You're stuck here, but if we go...We can go fix everything. For your crew and you...And your lady!|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 112}}</ref> A piratian expresses concern for the Captain as he staggers to one knee.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Captain?|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 112}}</ref> The Captain tells the piratians to give Ralph and Link a boat.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Ye have my leave to go. Give 'em a boat.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 112}}</ref> The piratians watch as their Captain gives Link a locket and message for his lady.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= If y'see this lady on your journeys, lad, tell her...The knight who set sail...Will love her for all eternity.|book= OoA (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 112}}</ref> They then disappear on the ship as the storm rages on.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Triva==
