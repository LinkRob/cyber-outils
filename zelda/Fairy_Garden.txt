The '''Fairy Garden''' is a location in {{FPTRR}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Garden}} was once a beautiful place, but after the five {{Plural|FPTRR|Fairy|link}} that protected it went to sleep, it became a dried-up desert. When {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} visits the garden, he restores power to each of the five fairy fountains, thus waking the {{Plural|FPTRR|Fairy}}. In turn, the {{Term|FPTRR|Grand Fairy}} tells him the truth about {{Term|FPTRR|Uncle Rupee|link}}. Once he realizes that he is being used by {{Term|FPTRR|Uncle Rupee}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} decides to stop him. After {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} departs, the {{Plural|FPTRR|Fairy}} begin the long process of restoring the garden. Later, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} discovers that {{Term|FPTRR|Pinkle|link}}, his fairy assistant, is the {{Term|FPTRR|Grand Fairy}}'s daughter, and is being held captive by {{Term|FPTRR|Uncle Rupee}}.

The {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Garden}} has its own transit system. {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can rent a vehicle with space for two for the price of 2,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}. If the vehicle runs out of gas, it will disappear, leaving him stranded. However, the vehicle can be refueled at each of the five fairy fountains once they have been restored to their full power.
