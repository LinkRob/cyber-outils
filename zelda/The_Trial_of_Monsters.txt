The Trial of Monsters is a [[Side Quest|side quest]] in [[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity]].

{{Infobox Quest|name=The Trial of Monsters|reward=Qualify for the Trial of Monk Maz Koshia|location=Gerudo Desert|image=File:HWAoC Trial of Monsters.png}}

== Overview ==
The Trial of Monsters is a side quest that can be unlocked after completing the [[Scenario]], "[[Destroy the Yiga Clan!]]" in [[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|Chapter 4]]. 

There will be three quests that can make you qualify for [[The Trial of the Ancients]], and this is one of them. It can be found in the [[Gerudo Desert]].

The requirements needed to complete this are 5 [[Ethereal Stone|Ethereal Stones]], 350 [[Bokoblin Trophy|Bokoblin Trophies]], 100 [[Lizalfos Trophy|Lizalfos Trophies]], 3 [[Wizzrobe Trophy|Wizzrobe Trophies]], and 20 [[Moblin Trophy|Moblin Trophies]].

The remaining two needed to qualify for The Trial of Ancients are [[The Trial of the Mighty Foe]] and [[The Trial of Stones]].
