The {{Term/Store|Oasis Flight||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a service in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Red|Oasis flight}}...right?|Fyer|TPHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TPHD|Oasis Flight}} is an amusement ride that {{Term|TPHD|Fyer|link}} offers to {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} in {{Term|TPHD|Lake Hylia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hrrrm... From old Auru, huh... Ah well, I guess I oughta do what the old coot says... {{Color|TPHD Red|Oasis flight}}...right? So here you go, one of our secret options not available to the general public...|Fyer|TPHD}}</ref> It houses a {{Term|TPHD|Cannon|link}} under its roof that can launch {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to various places. Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} enters it, the door shuts behind him and {{Term|TPHD|Fyer}} operates a crank, setting the direction that {{Term|TPHD|Link}} will be shot.

Until {{Term|TPHD|Link}} presents {{Term|TPHD|Auru's Memo|link}} to {{Term|TPHD|Fyer}},<ref>{{Cite|You see {{Color|TPHD Red|Lake Hylia}} below us? A man named {{Color|TPHD Red|Fyer}} runs an amusement ride of sorts there. Give him this. See, long ago, I saved this man {{Color|TPHD Red|Fyer's}} life, and now he cannot refuse me if I ask a favor.|Auru|TPHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Just give my note to Fyer, the man who runs the amusement ride in Lake Hylia. If you leave the rest to Fyer, all should go well.|Auru|TP}}</ref> the {{Term|TPHD|Cannon}} only launches him to the {{Term|TPHD|Flight-by-Fowl|link}} game area, which also counts as the only way back to the mainland from {{Term|TPHD|Lake Hylia}} besides transportation via the {{Term|TPHD|Kargarok|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Plumm|link}}'s mini-game, and {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Warping|link}} ability. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} receives his initial ride for free,<ref>{{Cite|All righty then... But remember, next time, you gotta pay!|Fyer|TPHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yeah, this time is free just 'cause I don't need to be getting an earful from the old fella later on. So then... One for the oasis flight!|Fyer|TP}}</ref> but it will cost him 10 {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}} for every ride after that. The free ride launches {{Term|TPHD|Link}} into the southern edge of the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Desert|link}}.

This method of traveling to the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Desert}} becomes unnecessary once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} opens up a {{Term|TPHD|Portal (Object)|link}} in the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Mesa|link}}; he will not have to pay to access the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Desert}} from that point forward.

==Trivia==
*The crank that {{Term|TPHD|Fyer}} operates to launch {{Term|TPHD|Link}} rotates cut-outs of {{Plural|TPHD|Oocca|link}} when in use.
