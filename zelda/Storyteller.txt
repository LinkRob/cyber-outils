{{Name|Fan|{{Term/Store|Storyteller|Storytellers|Series|plural}}}} is the name given to a group of storytelling characters in {{ALttP}}.

==Biography==
{{Main|Storytelling Bird, Storytelling Insect, Storytelling Octopus, Storytelling Tree}}
Found in the [[Dark World]], the Storytellers give [[Link]] tips for 20 [[Rupee]]s while also refilling his [[Heart]]s and [[Magic Meter]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= Storytellers are similar to fortune-tellers, but they sell Hyrule's myths and legends. Pay storytellers 20 Rupees to hear their helpful bit of lore—they'll also refill your life and magic for free.|game= ALttP&FS|guide= Nintendo|page= 103}}</ref> The octopus is found in a cave where the [[Sanctuary (A Link to the Past)|Sanctuary]] is in the [[Light World]], and talks about the [[Great Fairy#Fat Fairy|cursed Great Fairy]] within the [[Pyramid of Power]].<ref>{{Cite|Hah! Thank you. To tell you the truth, I found incredible beauty inside the pyramid, but someone sealed the door. You can't do anything with a standard bomb, they say...|Storyteller|ALttP}}</ref> The plant-like creature, found near the [[Palace of Darkness]] (where [[Sahasrahla]] hides in the Light World), tells a story about [[Ganon]]'s curse over the [[Sacred Realm|Golden Land]] for free.<ref>{{Cite|You're new here, aren't you? Did you come here looking for the Power of Gold? Well, you're too late. It will obey only the first person who touches it. The man who last claimed the Power of Gold wished for this world. It reflects his heart. Yes, I came here out of greed for the Golden Power, and look what happened to me...To restore the Golden Land, a person worthy of the Golden Power must defeat the man who created this place...Until that time, I am stuck in this bizarre shape. But what a mischievous thing to leave lying around...The Power of Gold...Triforce...|Storyteller|ALttP}}</ref> The bird-like creature, in a cave south of the [[Palace of Darkness]], talks about the circle of stones in [[Lake of Ill Omen]].<ref>{{Cite|Ha! Thank you. They say there is a tiny circle of rocks in the lake at the source of the river. I don't know what will happen, but it might be fun to throw something into it...|Storyteller|ALttP}}</ref> The insect-like creature is found in a cave in the [[Swamp of Evil]] (where [[Aginah]] is in the Light World) and tells Link to use the [[Ether Medallion]] to clear the rain in the mire.<ref>{{Cite|Heh heh. Thank you. As a matter of fact, monster magic is making it rain in the swamp. If you can move the air with more force than the monsters, the rain may stop.|Storyteller|ALttP}}</ref> The hand, found in a cave located where the [[Ice Rod]] is in the Light World, is a former [[thief]] and talks about [[Average Middle-Aged Man|another former thief]] specialized in the picking of [[lock]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Heh heh. Thank you. To tell you the truth, I used to be a thief in the Light World... some of my fellow thieves went into hiding because they were afraid of being caught. One of them was a master locksmith, but now he is hiding the fact that he was a thief... by pretending to be a strange middle-aged guy! Ha ha ha...|Storyteller|ALttP}}</ref>
