==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, the Dog Food is the third item in the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}. [[YipYip]] will give [[Link]] this can of Dog Food in return for a [[Ribbon]].<ref>{{Cite|Make-up! Jewels! Dresses! I want it all! Sigh... And some new accessories would be nice... Oh! That Ribbon! I need it! Will you trade for my dog food?|YipYip|LADX}}</ref> The can of Dog Food can then be given to the hungry [[Sale]] on [[Toronbo Shores]],<ref>{{Cite|What's that you have?! It's canned food! For heaven's sake, man, give that {{Icon|LADX Dog Food}} to ME!! PLEASE! He's hysterical! What do you do?|Sale|LADX}}</ref> who then gives Link a bunch of [[Bananas]] as thanks.<ref name="E 121"/>

==={{TMC|-}}===
A {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link|display=Bottle}} of {{Term|TMC|Dog Food}} appears behind {{Term|TMC|Stockwell|link}} in his {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|link|display=Shop}} in {{TMC}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} sneaks behind the counter in {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop}} while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can pick up the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|display=Bottle}} of {{Term|TMC|Dog Food}}. {{Term|TMC|Stockwell|link}} will tell {{Term|TMC|Link}} that he can keep the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle}} if {{Term|TMC|Link}} feeds his {{Term|TMC|Dog|link}} {{Term|TMC|Fifi|link}}, as he is too busy to do it himself.<ref>{{Cite|If you feed Fifi, then I guess {{Color|TMC Blue|you can have the bottle}}.|Stockwell|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Stockwell complains that he's been too busy lately to see his dog, Fifi.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Fifi}} can be found inside {{Term|TMC|Stockwell}}'s home at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, where he can be fed the {{Term|TMC|Dog Food}} when {{Term|TMC|Link}} places it in the {{Term|TMC|Doggie Dish|link}}.
