'''Gloom''' is a deadly substance that drains the life of its victims. It appears in {{TotK}}, and was unleashed when [[Ganondorf]] was resurrected underneath [[Hyrule Castle]], nearly killing [[Link]] while attempting to protect [[Princess Zelda]] from hitting her, striking both the [[Master Sword]] and his right arm, and drastically reducing his [[Life Gauge]] of 30 [[Heart Container|Heart Containers]] down into just three - the amount shown in the beginning of all previous games. Following the [[The Upheaval|Upheaval]], it was found in vast quantities in the [[The Depths|Depths]] and around chasms on the surface.

Gloom has a similar viscous, ooze-like appearance to [[Malice]] - but whereas Malice was purple-black, Gloom was red-black. Gloom is also more potent and dangerous than Malice, as damage done by it can be permanent as shown by Ganondorf's attack on Link. Less severe exposure to Gloom was reversible, however only under certain conditions: activating or traveling to a [[Lightroot]], eating something with the "sunny" effect such as food or elixirs cooked using a Sundelion or Sun Pumpkin, or remaining outside the Depths for long enough provided that Link is not being chased by [[Gloom Spawn]] or [[Phantom Ganon]], where the sky turns red similar to a [[Blood Moon]], will restore lost Hearts at a rate of one Heart per second.

The Gloom has also affected many weapons on the surface, coating most [[Sword]]s, [[Spear]]s and [[Long Sword]]s, causing them to decay and weaken in both [[durability]] and [[Weapon Strength|strength]]. However, Gloom seems to be unable to affect [[Zonaite]] weapons.

==Enemies==
===''Gloom Spawn''===
[[Gloom Spawn]] are arm-like tendrils that are made of gloom, and travel around with Gloom following them. Defeating the Gloom Spawn summons a [[Phantom Ganon]]. Moving a certain distance away from certain Gloom Spawn will cause it to de-spawn and leave [[Dark Clump]]s, without spawning Phantom Ganon.

===''Phantom Ganon''===
Phantom Ganon in {{TotK|-}} is a Gloom infused creation of [[Ganondorf]] created from his own flesh and blood. Phantom Ganon can wield a variety of weapons including: [[Gloom Sword]], [[Gloom Club]], and [[Gloom Spear]]. Phantom Ganon will deal Gloom damage and will have a small pool of Gloom around him. Phantom Ganon serves as the boss of the fifth dungeon in the game but can be summoned upon defeating Gloom Spawn. Upon defeat, Phantom Ganon can drop two of four weapons; Gloom Sword, Gloom Club, Gloom Spear, and [[Demon King's Bow]]. The three melee weapons, when used by Link, will cause one heart of Gloom damage whenever an enemy is hit. the Demon King's Bow's damage will vary based on maximum number of hearts.

===''Depths Monsters''===
With the exception of [[Frox]]es and [[Flux Construct]]s, all monsters that appear in the Depths will be Gloom infused and deal Gloom damage. 

===Ganondorf/Demon Dragon===
When [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] first found [[Ganondorf]] at the Depths and awakened him, Ganondorf unleashed a stream of Gloom that not only crippled Link's right arm and [[Decayed Master Sword|shattered]] the [[Master Sword]], but also permanently destroyed twenty-seven of Link's thirty Heart Containers. Link would have to collect [[Light of Blessing|Lights of Blessing]] to exchange for new Heart Containers.

During the final battle with Ganondorf the Demon King, all of his attacks are Gloom infused and will deal Gloom damage. However, he can also unleash unique Gloom attacks, which will, if it hits Link, destroy a single Heart Container, which will be irretrievable. Ironically, said Gloom attacks can also be [[Dead Man's Volley|redirected against Ganondorf]] and supply massive damage on him if parried just right. Upon becoming the [[Demon Dragon]], his entire body is coated with Gloom, which will indeed cause Link to suffer Gloom damage so long as he walks on the scales outside of the four weak spots. The Dragon also releases Gloom blasts from his mouth that will once again permanently destroy a Heart Container if it connects. However, the game cannot be saved during the battle, so any permanently lost Heart Containers will be restored upon ultimately defeating Ganondorf and completing the game.

==Notes==
*Sunlight, Lightroots, and Gloom healing foods will only restore the "maximum" hearts and does not recover the current amount of health. 
*When Gloom Spawn de-spawns, this will count as being killed/defeated and will not spawn until a Blood Moon occurs.

==Trivia==
[[File:BotW Dark Beast Ganon Blood Moon.png|thumb|244x244px|A Blood Moon in {{BotW|-}}]]
Gloom could possibly be a form of blood magic, which is often considered a dark art. This would make sense due to the connection of blood to [[Ganon]] in few past media.
*In the [[Nintendo Switch]] games, when Ganon is at the peak of his power, a Blood Moon occurs and resurrects those loyal to him.
*In {{TAoL}}, after Ganon was destroyed, the minions of the archvillain sought Link's blood to resurrect him in the event he is [[Game Over|defeated]], further linking Ganon to the concept of Blood Magic.
