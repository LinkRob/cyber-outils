==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hearty Lizard}}
{{Plural|BotW|Hearty Lizard}} can be found resting on {{Plural|BotW|Palm Tree|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} and on the shores of the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}}. They can be {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}} with {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Elixir|link}}, which will temporarily increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} maximum {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} when consumed.<ref name="Inventory"/> However, {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} them with other ingredients will result in {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}, but will yield more hearts than normal. The {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} also accepts {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Lizard}} as an ingredient to make {{Term|BotW|Blue Dye|link}}.
