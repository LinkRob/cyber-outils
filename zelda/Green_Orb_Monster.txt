{{Term/Store|Green Orb Monster|Green Orb Monsters|Series, LADX|plural}} are enemies in {{LA|DX}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 196}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|LADX|Green Orb Monster}} are found exclusively in the {{Term|LADX|Color Dungeon|link}}. After being hit with a {{Term|LADX|Sword|link}}, they retreat into their orbs and can be picked up by using the {{Term|LADX|Power Bracelet|link}}. {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} must throw the {{Plural|LADX|Green Orb Monster}} into their corresponding green holes. When all the other {{Term|LADX|Orb Monster|link}} are placed correctly, they vanish thus spawning either a {{Term|LADX|Treasure Chest|link}} or a {{Term|LADX|Small Key|link}}.

To assist with red-green {{Wp|color blindness}}, {{Plural|LADX|Green Orb Monster}} are replaced with yellow {{Plural|LANS|Orb Monster}} in {{LA|NS}}.
