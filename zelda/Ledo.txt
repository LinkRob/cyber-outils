{{Term/Store|Ledo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ledo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Ledo}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} who lives in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. He an apprentice of {{Term|BotW|Dento|link}} who has been tasked with repairing the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}}.<ref name="Fronk">{{Cite|[Fronk?] Fronk and I are Master Dento's faithful apprentices.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Master always tells us, "Repairing the domain is part of your training." So that's what we're doing now.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Ledo}} can first be encountered near the {{Term|BotW|Moor Garrison Ruins|link}}, where he will call out to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You there!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to where he is swimming in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Over here! I'm over here!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches him, he apologizes for disturbing him.<ref>{{Cite|I apologize for calling on you so suddenly!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} introduces himself, and he explains that he had no choice but to speak to {{Term|BotW|Link}} once he realized that he was a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am Ledo, a proud member of the distinguished Zora. As you are a real {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}}, I had no choice but to call upon you!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take a moment to listen to what he has to say.<ref>{{Cite|Will you please spare me a moment of your time?|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to hear him out, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} thanks him.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure thing.] Thank you so very much!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He points out {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Tower|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, asking that he follow the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}} towards it until he reaches {{Term|BotW|Inogo Bridge|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Do you see that tower...the one atop that mountain yonder? I need you to go upstream of {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora River}}, which runs along the north side of that tower...to {{Color|BotW Blue|Inogo Bridge}}.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> Noticing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears confused, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} promises that it will be worth it if {{Term|BotW|Link}} does so.<ref>{{Cite|... I can see it in your eyes. You're asking yourself, "Why me?" A reasonable question for a stranger accosted by a passing Zora in such a manner. But I must assure you, this venture will be very profitable for you as well!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He explains that prince of the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}, {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}}, is searching for a strong {{Term|BotW|Hylian}}.<ref>{{Cite|You see, {{Color|BotW Red|Prince Sidon}} of the Zora is in desperate search of a strong Hylian.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> Since this request is from a member of the {{Term|BotW|Zora}} royal family, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} reasons that that there must be a reward for fulfilling it.<ref>{{Cite|And as this is a royal request from the {{Color|BotW Red|prince}} himself, it's safe to assume a generous reward is in the cards...|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to meet {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} at {{Term|BotW|Inogo Bridge}}.<ref>{{Cite|That is why I am asking you to meet with {{Color|BotW Red|Prince Sidon}} at {{Color|BotW Blue|Inogo Bridge}}, along the {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora River}} just upstream of here.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} arrives at {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} can be found conducting repairs on the entrance. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} did not meet him at the {{Term|BotW|Moor Garrison Ruins}}, he will introduce himself.<ref>{{Cite|Oy! Hylian! You must be exhausted after your long journey! Oh, and as for me, I am called Ledo.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> If they have already met, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} will recognize {{Term|BotW|Link}} and thank him for coming.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! It's you! We met at {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora River}}... It's me, Ledo! Thank you for coming so quickly!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to meet with {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But enough about me... Could I possibly ask you...to go and meet with {{Color|BotW Red|King Dorephan}}? He's in the throne room. Please, go ahead!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has spoken to {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}}, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} will reiterate that {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} has been causing trouble for the citizens of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|You listened to what King Dorephan has to say! Yes, so that is the cause of our suffering in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}. It is all because of that beast, {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}}.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> Due to this, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} and {{Term|BotW|Fronk|link}} have been tasked with repairing the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}}, but they have been running out of supplies.<ref>{{Cite|That's why my senior apprentice, Fronk, and I are repairing the domain. However, our materials are a bit depleted...|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about this, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} explains that the{{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}}'s upkeep requires a large amount of of {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Materials?] Repairs require a large number of {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}}.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that many of them can be found in {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can find plenty of them around {{Color|BotW Blue|Upland Zorana}}.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} could bring him 10 {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone}}, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} claims that it would aid his efforts.<ref>{{Cite|Have you, by chance, already stumbled across some {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}} during your travels? For instance, if you have {{Color|BotW Red|10 of them}}, that would be a big help...|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> Once he finishes speaking, the "{{Term|BotW|Luminous Stone Gathering|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} has collected 10 {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} to give them to {{Term|BotW|Ledo}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Here are the stones.] Thank you! You're really giving me {{Color|BotW Red|10}} {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}}?!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He then proceeds to count out all 10 of them.<ref>{{Cite|In that case, please excuse me while I count them! {{Big|And a oooone! And a twooooo! And a threeeeee! Foooooour! Fiiiiiive! Siiiiiiix! Seveeeeeeen! Eeeeeeeeeeight! Eeeeeeeeeeight! Niiiiiiiiine! Aaaand...TEN!}} Oh, my! You really brought all 10! Thank you! Yes, I will happil take those {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}} from you|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} two {{Plural|BotW|Diamond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that's right... I must reward you!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And what's more, I'll give you {{Color|BotW Red|one}} extra as a bonus!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He calls {{Term|BotW|Link}} a lifesaver, promising to trade give one {{Term|BotW|Diamond}} if he ever brings him 10 {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone}} again.<ref>{{Cite|You're a lifesaver! Truly! If you ever find yourself in possession of {{Color|BotW Red|10}} {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}} in the future, I hope you will bring them to me. But if that happens, know that I'll only be able to give you {{Color|BotW Red|one}} {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}} for them. I would be honored if you would accept it when the time comes!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} can be seen among the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} gathered to celebrate in the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}}. When spoken to later, he will express his gratitude towards {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have appeased {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}}! I cannot express how grateful I am!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has not received "{{Term|BotW|Luminous Stone Gathering}}" yet, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} remarks that {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} is still suffering from {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}'s rampage.<ref>{{Cite|However, those of us in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} are still feeling the ramifications of the chaos that {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} wrought...|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to ask {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} questions while he speaks to him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, if it isn't the Hylian! Did you need something from me?|Ledo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Do you have any more questions?|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquires about {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone}}, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} explains that they were one of the many {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} used to construct {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Luminous stones?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Luminous stones}} were part of the raw materials used to build {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> Supposedly, the reason {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} was built in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}} was because {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana}} is a reliable source of {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone}}.<ref>{{Cite|In the past, {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}} were often mined from {{Color|BotW Blue|Upland Zorana}}...which is why {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} was built here. That's the story, anyway.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Fronk}}, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} reveals that they are both {{Term|BotW|Dento}}'s apprentices.<ref name="Fronk"/> He claims that they both work hard to better their craft, and part of their training is the repair of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fronk and I are always striving to better ourselves in the workshop. Master always tells us, "Repairing the domain is part of your training." So that's what we're doing now.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} ends their conversation, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} will return to his work.<ref>{{Cite|[Good-bye.] OK then! Back to work I go!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>
