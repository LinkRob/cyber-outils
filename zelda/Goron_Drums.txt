The {{Term/Store|Goron Drums|Goron Drums|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125}}</ref> also known as the '''Drums of Sleep''',<ref>{{Cite|It seems the face of the slightly larger, strange stone changes when the {{Color|MM3D Red|drums of sleep}} are played...|Gossip Stone|MM3D}}</ref> are an item in {{MM}}.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|MM3D|Goron Drums}} are obtained alongside the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Mask|link}} after {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} heals {{Term|MM3D|Darmani III|link}}'s soul. It replaces the {{Term|MM3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} when {{Term|MM3D|Link}} is transformed into a {{Term|MM3D|Goron Link|link|display=Goron}}. In this form, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must use them to learn the "{{Term|MM3D|Goron Lullaby|link}}" from the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Elder|link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Elder's Son|link|display=his son}} in order to put the latter to sleep. By playing the song on the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Drums}} in {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can put {{Term|MM3D|Biggoron|link}} to sleep, allowing passage to {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead Temple|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|MM|Goron Drums}} existed as an independent [[C-Button]] item in the original {{MM|-}}. Their icon was coded into the game but was not implemented in the final release.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= darkeye14|published= August 25, 2010|retrieved= |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KYdj1DiCgug|title= Japanese Majora's Mask: Exclusive Beta Items|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref> It can be seen in certain [[Unused Content|pre-release]] screenshots. The item was later given its own icon in {{MM3D}}.
