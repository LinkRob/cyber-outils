==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|MM3D|Link-Eating Plant}} are large, carnivorous flowers that appear exclusively in the {{Term|MM3D|Woodfall Temple|link}}. If {{Term|MM3D|Hylian|link}} {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} stands on one, he will be eaten. However, if he stands on one in the form of {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link}}, he will remain safe and may use it as a platform.
