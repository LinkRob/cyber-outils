==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon}} is a {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} located in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. It is operated by {{Term|BotW|Misa|link}}. Inside the {{Term|BotW|Shop}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Misa's Recipe Book|link}}, which teaches {{Plural|BotW|Recipe|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Apple Pie|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nutcake|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Fried Bananas|link}}.
