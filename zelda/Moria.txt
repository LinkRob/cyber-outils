'''Moria''' is a kingdom mentioned in the book {{TSP}}. [[Charles of Moria|Charles]], one of the main characters of the book, claims to have traveled from the land of {{Term|TSP|Moria}} to {{Term|TSP|Hyrule|link}} as part of a custom that requires him to do good deeds in order for him to become the King of {{Term|TSP|Moria}}.<ref name=moria>{{Cite Book| quote= Ignoring Link, the man smiles at Zelda. "Charles of Moria, at your service. [...] "Oh, it's far away," Charles says. "I've been travelling for months, doing good deeds as part of Morian custom. When I've done enough I'll go home and become King Charles"|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 37}}</ref> Not many details are given about where {{Term|TSP|Moria}} is located except that it's "far away."<ref name=moria/> However, later on it is discovered that Charles is actually {{Term|TSP|Ganon|link}} in disguise, which heavily implies that {{Term|TSP|Moria}} is a non-existant kingdom, especially since {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda|link}} had mentioned she had never heard of it.<ref>{{Cite Book| quote= "Moria? [...] I've never heard of it."|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 37}}</ref> 

==Morian Customs==
Charles speaks of the many customs and other traditions that {{Term|TSP|Moria}} has, although considering that {{Term|TSP|Moria}} most likely does not exist, they were probably all made up by Charles to convince others of his good nature. Such customs include:

*The king-to-be cannot take off their necklace until they become the king.<ref>{{Cite Book| quote="Begging your highness's pardon, I'd rather you didn't. According to Morian lore, I'm not supposed to take it off until I'm king. You see, it's an ancient legend..."|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 93}}</ref> 
*Holding a scarf for good luck.<ref>{{Cite Book| quote="Zelda?" he asks. "Will you hold my scarf for luck? It's a Morian custom."|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 82}}</ref> 
*He who wishes to be the king of {{Term|TSP|Moria}} must walk through the night and ask the night {{Plural|TSP|Fairy|link}} to tell him the secrets of kingship.<ref>{{Cite Book| quote= "In Moria we have an ancient custom. He who is to be king must walk through the night and ask the night fairies to tell him the secrets of kingship," says Charles.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 105}}</ref> 
*Exchanging gifts.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote="Take this as a token of friendship. It's an ancient Morian custom to exchange gifts. Do you have something to give me?"|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 27}}</ref> 

==Trivia==
*{{Term|TSP|Moria}} may be named after the Dwarven realm of the same name from {{Wp|J.R.R. Tolkien}}'s {{Wp|Middle-earth}} legendarium. It may also be an anagram of [[Mario|another famous Nintendo character's name]].
