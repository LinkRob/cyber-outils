{{Term/Store|Glutko||Series, TFoE}} (pronounced {{IPA|ˈɡlʌtkoʊ}} {{Respell|GLUT|koh}})<ref name=onkled>{{Cite|Through the eye of Glutko lies the shrine of Koridai.|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref> is the fourth boss in {{TFoE}}.{{Name Ref}} He is located in the eponymous Stage, {{Term|TFoE|Glutko|link}}.

==Biography==
Glutko is a gluttonous cyclops who is a minion of [[Ganon]]. He is encountered at his Face of Evil, the {{Term|TFoE|Glutko|link}} area. There he consumes [[Koridian]]s (and [[Daira]]) alive with no remorse. Following the advice of [[Droolik]], Link defeats Glutko by throwing a [[Bomb]] into his mouth, causing the cyclops to explode. He is encountered again at the [[Shrine of Koridai]] on the summit, where he guards the [[Book of Koridai]]. He is fought and defeated the same way.

==Trivia==
*Glutko is similar to {{TWoG}} boss [[Omfak]]. Both are plot-significant bosses who guard items at a Shrine that are necessary to defeat Ganon, and are known for their gluttonous behavior.

==Nomenclature==
Glutko's name is derived from the word glutton or gluttonous, referring to his voracious and greedy appetite and behavior.
