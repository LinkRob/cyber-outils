The '''Gizmol''' is an [[Automaton]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Gizmol|Filling the world with its sparkling songs, this clockwork cutie breaks hearts and enemies.<ref name="Gizmol">{{Cite|Filling the world with its sparkling songs, this clockwork cutie breaks hearts and enemies.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Gizmols are [[Zol]]-like Automatons created by [[Dampé]]. They are obtained as a reward for completing the [[Performance Artist!]] [[side quest]], which requires the [[Prismatic Music Box]] and any [[Zol]] echo to complete.
