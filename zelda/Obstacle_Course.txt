The {{Term/Store|Obstacle Course||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Minigame|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 332}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course}} is operated by {{Term|BotW|Blynne|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}. It can be played by speaking to him outside the {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|display=Stable}} while riding a tame {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} and accepting his challenge to see who is the better rider.<ref>{{Cite|I grew up taming horses 'round these parts. I'm confident there's not a rider in Hyrule better'n me.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And I got just the way to prove it—a li'l game to test which fella's better in the saddle. One I almost never lose.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What do you think? You got the grit to step up and challenge the master?|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} pays the 20 {{Term|BotW|Rupee|link}} fee to play, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will set up the obstacles for the {{Term|BotW|Minigame|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Blynne}} explains that the goal of the {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course}} is to navigate all ten {{Plural|BotW|Obstacle|link}} as quick as possible.<ref>{{Cite|It's an obstacle course, but on horseback! There are 10 obstacles to navigate. Best time wins.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> He guarantees that it will teach {{Term|BotW|Link}} how to ride his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} better.<ref>{{Cite|If you're not an ace in the saddle when the game starts, I guarantee you'll be one by the time it's through!|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> The difficult part is being able to control one's {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, instead of riding as fast as possible.<ref>{{Cite|The difficult part about this is the fact that you can't just try to recklessly ride as fast as you can. In order to clear the obstacles on the path, you have to be able to control your horse very well.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> He offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear|link}} if he can beat his time.<ref>{{Cite|If you can beat my personal best time, I'll give you some mighty fine {{Color|BotW Red|horse gear}}.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will refuse to start the {{Term|BotW|Minigame}} if the necessary conditions are not met. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to speak to him without being atop a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} to mount one and come back.<ref>{{Cite|Well shoot, fella. You can't enter a horse race without a horse to ride! Go mount up and come back to me when you're serious.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will reject {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is riding atop an unregistered {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, telling {{Term|BotW|Link}} to handle the paperwork at a {{Term|BotW|Stable}} first.<ref>{{Cite|That horse you're riding isn't properly {{Color|BotW Red|registered}} yet. Go take care of the paperwork at the stable counter before you come to challenge my best time.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> Additionally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to challenge {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} while riding atop anything other than a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, Blynne will emphasize that the {{Term|BotW|Minigame}} is exclusively meant for {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|See here, fella. This is a HORSE race. If you want in, you gotta be on a horse. Not...whatever's happening here.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> If it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}}, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will not be in the mood to run the {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course|display=Course}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm in no mood to run the course when it's raining. If you want to beat my time, come back when the sun's out.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> However, if it starts {{Term|BotW|Rain|display=Raining}} while {{Term|BotW|Link}} is attempting the {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course|display=Course}}, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will still ask if he wants to try again.

Before {{Term|BotW|Link}} starts the {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course}}, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} asks if he wants to hear the rules.<ref>{{Cite|You need a rundown of the rules?|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> He explains that {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs to ride his {{Term|BotW|Horse}} around {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain}} while jumping over all of the {{Plural|BotW|Obstacle}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ride your horse around this grassy field while jumping the obstacles.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not allowed to cross the finish line until he has jumped over all 10.<ref>{{Cite|There are {{Color|BotW Red|10 obstacles}}, all told. You don't get to cross the finish line 'til you've jumped them all. When you clear the last one, ride back here. Simple, right?|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> Additionally, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be disqualified if he dismounts his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Don't dismount your horse, or you're out of the running. Not that you need telling, right?|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} informs him that his best time is 1 minute and 15 seconds,<ref>{{Cite|My best time's {{Color|BotW Red|1 minute 15 seconds}}. Think you can beat that?|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> though he suggests trying to complete the {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course|display=Course}} in 1 minute and 30 seconds.<ref>{{Cite|Why don't you aim for {{Color|BotW Red|1 minute and 30 seconds}} first?|Blynne|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will only reward {{Term|BotW|Link}} with encouragement if he completes the {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course}} with any time over 2 minutes.<ref>{{Cite|Your time was {{Color|BotW Red|02:00.00}}, huh... Keep riding until you've got some hearty saddle sores. Lotta work ahead of you! Your horse may balk when you spur it on right in front of an obstacle. I would too!|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes the {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course|display=Course}} between 1 minute and 30 seconds and 2 minutes, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nice riding, fella! You didn't beat my time, but I'm impressed all the same. Here, this is for your hardworking friend.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has at least one {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will instead give him a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}, valued at 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.}} When {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course}} between 1 minute and 15 seconds and 1 minute and 30 seconds, Blynne rewards him with the {{Term|BotW|Extravagant Bridle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's that {{Color|BotW Red|horse gear}} I promised you. Keep at it, and maybe someday you'll be on my level.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} already owns the {{Term|BotW|Extravagant Bridle}} but does not have any {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory}}, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will give an {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot}} instead. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} also has at least one {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot}}, he will receive a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}, valued at 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.}} If {{Term|BotW|Link}} beats {{Term|BotW|Blynne}}'s time, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will give him the {{Term|BotW|Extravagant Saddle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Holy smoke, would you look at that? Never thought I'd see the day.. But a man's gotta abide by his word, so here's that {{Color|BotW Red|horse gear}} I promised.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} already owns the {{Term|BotW|Extravagant Saddle}}, {{Term|BotW|Blynne}} will give the {{Term|BotW|Extravagant Bridle}} instead. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} also has the {{Term|BotW|Extravagant Bridle}}, he will receive a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, valued at 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.}}
