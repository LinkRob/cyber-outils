"{{Term/Store|Forbidden City Entry||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Forbidden City Entry}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Gerudo Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tries to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, the guards will stop him, as men are not allowed inside.<ref>{{Cite|Freeze! Where do you think you're going?! Not into town—that's for sure! No voe allowed. Off with you!|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> However, he can find a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} man named {{Term|BotW|Benja|link}} nearby, where he is watching the main entrance. When asked if he is spying, {{Term|BotW|Benja}} will become flustered and refute the claim.<ref>{{Cite|[Are you spying?] What?! N-no! I'm not a spy!|Benja|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he is the leader of a {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} caravan that has been stalled at {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm a merchant! I may not look like it, but I'm actually the leader of a caravan! I've traveled all the way here, but this town doesn't allow men to go in. So...that's why I...had to...um...|Benja|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks once again if he is spying, {{Term|BotW|Benja}} insists that he is conducting research.<ref>{{Cite|[Are you spying?] It's research! RESEARCH!|Benja|BotW}}</ref> Through his research, {{Term|BotW|Benja}} has found information on sneaking into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|And think what you want, but I've finally found some key info on how to sneak into {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}!|Benja|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about this information, {{Term|BotW|Benja}} will gladly share his tale.<ref>{{Cite|[What info?] Heheheh... Well, allow me to share with you...my heroic tale!|Benja|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Benja}} has been attempted to access {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} for almost a week with no success,<ref>{{Cite|It's been close to a week now... The guards have kicked me out more times than I can count... I almost gave up hope...|Benja|BotW}}</ref> but then he heard about a man who managed to slip inside.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|HOWEVER!!}} I have learned only recently of a {{Color|BotW Red|man that slipped into}} this well-guarded town!|Benja|BotW}}</ref> He has heard rumors that this man travels between {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}} and {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|I heard that man is frequently traveling between {{Color|BotW Blue|Kara Kara Bazaar}} and Gerudo Town.|Benja|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he has been waiting at the entrance to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to try and spot him.<ref>{{Cite|I've been waiting here, trying to spot him as he travels!|Benja|BotW}}</ref> Once he is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} will begin.

To find the rumored man, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must go to the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}} and ask around about a suspicious male {{Term|BotW|Merchant}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Maike|link}} about the man, she suggests that he asks one of the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} merchants at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|display=Bazaar}} instead.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, there are Hylian merchants here, too. Why don't you go ask one of them?|Maike|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Emri|link}} about the man, she tells him that she can recall a female {{Term|BotW|Merchant}} who frequents the {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Still... Yeah, I can think of someone... That one vai merchant in full Gerudo garb... She frequents the {{Color|BotW Red|general store}} a lot.|Emri|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Robsten|link}} about the man, he also recalls seeing a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} girl at the {{Term|BotW|General Store}}.<ref>{{Cite|There was that pretty Hylian gal at the {{Color|BotW Red|general store}}. It's unusual to see a Hylian girl dressed like a Gerudo...|Robsten|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Ripp|link}} about the man, she recalls a particular female {{Term|BotW|Merchant}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn}} who wore {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} clothes.<ref>{{Cite|Although... Now that you mention it, one merchant in particular did stand out to me.|Ripp|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I saw a {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian vai}} merchant at the inn before. She was wearing traditional Gerudo clothes, which caught my eye.|Ripp|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ripp}} reasons that the {{Term|BotW|Merchant}} is probably still at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|display=Bazaar}} because {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} is scaring people from traveling.<ref>{{Cite|She's probably still around town; that {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} is scaring a lot of people out of traveling.|Ripp|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} goes to the {{Term|BotW|General Store}} and asks {{Term|BotW|Shaillu|link}} about a sneaky guy, she mentions a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} girl who likes to relax on the roof.<ref>{{Cite|There's a {{Color|BotW Red|Hylian vai}} who likes to relax on the roof. Though in the afternoon, it's probably too hot up there.|Shaillu|BotW}}</ref>

On the roof of the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar Inn}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find the mysterious {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} woman, who turns out to be a {{Term|BotW|Merchant}} named {{Term|BotW|Vilia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my. What a lovely young lad you are. Do you need something from me?|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> She claims not to know anyting about a man sneaking into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} and apologizes for not being able to help.<ref>{{Cite|What's that? You've heard about {{Color|BotW Red|a man}} who snuck into {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}? Oh, I don't know anything about him... Sorry I couldn't be more help.|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} investigates her more closely, she becomes embarrassed.<ref>{{Cite|Heehee... You're making my cheeks warm looking at me like that.|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} compliments her beauty, {{Term|BotW|Vilia}} says that her clothing only enhances her looks.<ref>{{Cite|[You're very beautiful!] Oh, such a nice thing to say! And I'm sure you'd agree that these clothes only help my looks, right?|Vilia|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accuses {{Term|BotW|Vilia}} of being a man, she will dismiss his claims and send him away.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! You sure say the funniest things, young man. Anyway, if you don't need anything, you should be off, then.|Vilia|BotW}}</ref>}} She offers to sell {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} for 600 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, which she promises will make people see him differently.<ref>{{Cite|Now that I think about it, this style would look quite fetching on you...  I'd have to charge you, of course, but I bet people would see you completely differently if you wore them! What do you think? Do you want to buy them for...{{Color|BotW Red|600 rupees}}?|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> After buying it, {{Term|BotW|Link}} changes into the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} clothing as {{Term|BotW|Vilia}} looks away.<ref>{{Cite|Heehee... I have the perfect outfit for you! Go ahead and change into it right now. Don't worry. I'll look away...|Vilia|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Vilia}} sees {{Term|BotW|Link}} in his new clothes, she says that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a "good-lookin' gal" and doubts that anyone would suspect that he is a man.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|♪Eeeeeeeee!♪ You look adorable!!}} Ahhh heeheehee... Just as I thought! You make for quite a {{Color|BotW Red|good-lookin' gal}}. That's a traditional Gerudo outfit. It's such a striking look around here, I doubt anyone would even suspect that you're a man!|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Vilia}} also explains that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}} helps with the scorching {{Plural|BotW|Temperature|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}},<ref>{{Cite|On top of that, it breathes so well that it's sure to help you deal with the desert heat... At least a little!|Vilia|BotW}}</ref> referring to its heat-resistant {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link}}. While wearing the full {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|display=Outfit}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to be female to everyone and can walk into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} undisturbed. After walking into the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}}, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} will be completed.
