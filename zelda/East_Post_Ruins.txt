The {{Term/Store|East Post Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''East Post Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The East Post Ruins are the remains of an outpost located between the [[Outpost Ruins]] and [[Proxim Bridge]] in [[Hyrule Field#Breath of the Wild|Hyrule Field]]. Various pieces of furniture can be found among the wreckage of the buildings, and a wagon can be found along the road that runs through the Ruins. This wagon can be searched for [[Material#Breath of the Wild|Material]]s. A [[Yiga Footsoldier]] may occasionally pass through the Ruins disguised as a [[Traveler]].

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing an [[Opal]] can be found in the northeasternmost building by moving aside two metal doors with [[Magnesis]]. A second stone Chest holding a [[Traveler's Bow]] is located in a corner of a ruined structure near the center. A third stone Chest lies in the south of the westernmost building and contains 10 [[Arrow]]s. A wooden platform held aloft by [[Sky Octorok]]s appears due west of the Proxim Bridge in [[Master Mode]], bearing a metal Treasure Chest which contains an [[Iron Sledgehammer]].

Link can find a [[Korok]] in a cloud of leaves at the top of a flagpole toward the northern foot of the hill just northwest of Proxim Bridge. A second Korok can be revealed at the northern end of the Ruins by shooting two targets usually obscured by a wall while facing south from a stump with a [[Pinwheel]].
