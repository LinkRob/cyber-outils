==Entrance to the Grotto==
The {{Term|CoH|Frozen Grotto}} is located in the middle of {{Term|CoH|Lake Hylia|link}}, where it is surrounded by water. A {{Term|CoH|Sheikah Stone|link}} sits just outside its entrance.
