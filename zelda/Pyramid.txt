The {{Term/Store|Pyramid||FSA}} is a level that appears in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 154}}</ref>

It is the final level in the {{Term|FSA|Desert of Doubt}}, coming after the {{Term|FSA|Desert of Doubt}} and the {{Term|FSA|Desert Temple (Four Swords Adventures)|display=Desert Temple|link}}. This large monolith reminiscent of the {{Wp|Ancient Egyptian}}s is said to have been constructed by the ancestors of the {{Term|FSA|Zuna|link}}.

This pyramid is filled with slow-moving, yet dangerous {{Plural|FSA|Gibdo|link}} and {{Plural|FSA|Puzzle|link}} which the {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} must complete to reach the {{Term|FSA|Big Moldorm}} that lurks deep within this ancient tomb. {{Term|FSA|Shadow Link|link}} also appears to place {{Plural|FSA|Big Bomb|link}} that instantly defeat the {{Plural|FSA|Links}} if hit. After the {{Term|FSA|Big Moldorm}} is defeated, the {{Plural|FSA|Links}} rescue the {{Term|FSA|Purple Shrine Maiden|link}}. With the inclusion of the Purple Maiden, all six {{Plural|FSA|Maiden|link}} call {{Term|FSA|Princess Zelda|link}} to determine her location. However, before the {{Plural|FSA|Links}} rescue the Maiden, they realize that the {{Term|FSA|Trident|link}} is missing from its resting place in the {{Term|FSA|Pyramid}}. The maidens believe the one behind its robbery is {{Term|FSA|Ganon|link}}.

{{Plural|FSA|Force Gem|link}} are rather abundant in this area, with the 2000 needed to charge the {{Term|FSA|Four Sword|link}} obtained quickly. The {{Term|FSA|Big Key|link}} unlocks a {{Term|FSA|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a rare {{Term|FSA|Force Gem}} worth 1000.
