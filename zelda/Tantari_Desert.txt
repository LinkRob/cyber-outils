The {{Term/Store|Tantari Desert||Series, TAoL}} is a location in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 224}}</ref>

==Features==
The desert is a small, sandy plain north of [[North Castle]], known for its single, isolated cave. The Tantari Desert is plot-significant; hidden within its depths is a [[Trophy]] stolen from a woman in the [[Town of Ruto]]. After defeating the very [[Goriya]] that stole the trophy,<ref>{{Cite|GORIYA OF TANTARI STOLE OUR TROPHY.|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref> Link returns the trophy to the woman, and in return he receives the [[Spells of The Adventure of Link#Jump Spell|Jump spell]] from her [[Old Man|uncle]] in her house.<ref>{{Cite|YOU SAVED THE TROPHY COME SEE MY UNCLE.|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Tan in Japanese means "smooth", "flat", "quiet", and "pale",<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.behindthename.com/name/tan/submitted |site= behindthename.com |title= Given Name TAN}}</ref>  whereas Tari is the name of an Ijaw tribe in west Africa.<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.behindthename.com/name/tari/submitted |site= behindthename.com |title= Given Name TARI}}</ref>
