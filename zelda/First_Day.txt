The {{Term/Store|First Day||Series, MM, MM3D}} is the first of the three total days that [[Link]] has before the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] crashes into [[Clock Town]] and destroys all of [[Termina]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 246}}</ref> From the moment that Link exits the [[Clock Tower]], the young hero and [[Tatl]] count with 72 whole hours to complete different tasks. Link always starts his First Day in front of the entrance of the Clock Tower in [[South Clock Town]] regardless of where he was prior to playing the [[Song of Time]], since that is the location that the melody always takes him to.

==Overview==
[[File:MM Clock Town.png|thumb|245x245px|The Clock Town as it appears during the daytime of the First Day]]
During the First Day, the state of Termina is generally peaceful, as the citizens of [[Clock Town]] seem at ease, making their preparations for the [[Carnival of Time]] while the weather is clear and sunny for both day and night (though becomes cloudy at 3:00AM). A few of the townsfolk seem a bit concerned about the size of the [[Moon]] and make some comments about it; however, this is the least of their concerns since they have other business to take care of. Their attitude is reflected by the cheerful and bright theme of Clock Town.

== Trivia ==

* Although virtually no people in [[Clock Town]] pay any attention to the [[Moon]] during the First Day, the man standing on top of the tower in construction in the middle of the [[South Clock Town|South]] section, whom is the same person [[Link]] meets if he completes the [[Oceanside Spider House]] sidequest, appears to be aware of the impending doom right from the start. A hint can be seen if he speaks to him during the nighttime, where he confirms the Moon is getting larger (though this does not occur in gameplay until the [[Second Day]]). [[Grog]] in [[Romani Ranch]] is also aware at the beginning of the cycle as well.
* Anytime the First Day begins and Link walks out of the [[Clock Tower]]'s entrance, the time is set at 6:01 AM. The only occurrence where it would be exactly 6:00 is when the young hero resumes from a lost three-day cycle if the Moon [[Terminan Apocalypse|crashes into Termina]], inside the Clock Tower itself.
* In the [[Bombers' Notebook]] as well as the time meter in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'', the First Day is marked with a blue theme.
* If debugs are used to reveal the originally planned seven-day cycle, the First Day was supposed to be "'''Monday'''".
