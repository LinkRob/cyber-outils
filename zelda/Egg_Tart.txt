==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Egg Tarts can be made in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} by combining [[Tabantha Wheat]], a [[Bird Egg]], [[Cane Sugar]], and [[Goat Butter]]. When eaten, they'll restore four of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, though they can also be sold for 30 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. It's said that one knows when this dish is done baking due to its delightful smell.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding a fifth ingredient, different variations of Egg Tarts can be made:
* '''Hearty Egg Tart''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Egg Tart}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum heart.<br/>You'll know this simple dessert is done baking when it smells just delightful.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one will completely restore Link's missing Hearts, in addition to temporarily increasing his maximum hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/> They can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* '''Energizing Egg Tart''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Egg Tart}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>You'll know this simple dessert is done baking when it smells just delightful.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> If eaten, these will restore a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/> They'll also heal him for five Hearts, but they're worth 50 Rupees when sold.

* '''Enduring Egg Tart''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Egg Tart}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>You'll know this simple dessert is done baking when it smells just delightful.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one of these dishes will completely refill Link's Stamina Wheel and temporarily increase his maximum {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}}.<ref name="Enduring"/> They also restore six Hearts to his Life Gauge and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Hasty Egg Tart''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Egg Tart}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>You'll know this simple dessert is done baking when it smells just delightful.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> These tarts temporarily boosts Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/> They'll also heal him for five Hearts, and they can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* '''Spicy Egg Tart''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Egg Tart}}<br/>Grants a low-level cold resistance.<br/>You'll know this simple dessert is done baking when it smells just delightful.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> When eaten, they will temporarily increase Link's [[Cold Resistance]] for one level.<ref name="Spicy"/> They'll also heal him for five Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Electro Egg Tart''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Egg Tart}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>You'll know this simple dessert is done baking when it smells just delightful.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> If they're eaten, they'll temporarily increase Link's [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/> They also heal him for five Hearts, and they sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Egg Tart''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Egg Tart}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>You'll know this simple dessert is done baking when it smells just delightful.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one will temporarily boost Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/> They'll also heal five of his Hearts, and they sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Tough Egg Tart''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Egg Tart}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>You'll know this simple dessert is done baking when it smells just delightful.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They grant a temporary defense boost when eaten.<ref name="Tough"/> They restore five of Link's Hearts to his Life Gauge and are worth 50 Rupees when sold.

* '''Sneaky Egg Tart''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Egg Tart}}<br/>Grants a mid-level stealth boost.<br/>You'll know this simple dessert is done baking when it smells just delightful.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating them will temporarily increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/> They also heal him for five Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Chilly Egg Tart''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Egg Tart}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>You'll know this simple dessert is done baking when it smells just delightful.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> If they're eaten, these tarts will temporarily increase Link's [[Heat Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Chilly"/> They'll also heal four of his Hearts, but they sell for 50 Rupees.
