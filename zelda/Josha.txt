{{Term/Store|Josha||Series, TotK}} is a character in {{TotK}}.<ref name="Name"/> She is a young {{Term|TotK|Sheikah|link}} girl who is [[Purah|Purah's]] assistant and the head of {{Term|TotK|Depths|link}} Research in the {{Term|TotK|Zonai Survey Team}}.

==Biography==
{{Stub|TotK}}

==Nomenclature==
Josha's name is the name "Joshua" in Japanese. This was altered to "Josha" for most other languages.
