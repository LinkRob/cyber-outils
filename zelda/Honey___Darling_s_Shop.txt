{{Term/Store|Honey & Darling's Shop||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Honey & Darling's Shop|Map|MM3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Honey and Darling's Shop is located near the [[Shooting Gallery]] in East Clock Town. It is open from 6 AM to 10 PM.<ref>{{Cite|Target Range / Honey & Darling's Shop / 6 a.m. to 10 p.m.|N/A|MM}}</ref> The shop is a gaming center where [[Link]] can play three types of {{Plural|MM3D|Mini-Game|link}}, with the game changing daily.<ref>{{Cite|Honey & Darling's Shop<br/>A Gaming Cender<br/>Changes Daily!|Sign|MM3D}}</ref> It costs 10 [[Rupee]]s to play one of the shop's games.

===Mini-Games===
The game run by the couple changes daily. The shop has three {{Plural|MM3D|Mini-Game|link}}, including [[Bombchu]] Gallery on the [[First Day]], Basket [[Bomb]] on the [[Second Day]], and Target Shooting on the [[Final Day]].

All three {{Plural|MM3D|Mini-Game}} involve hitting the targets around the platform in the given time before the music Honey and Darling dances to finishes. The timed games take place on a rotating and raising platform, making the aiming necessary in these games difficult. If Link falls off the platform, the game is over. As with other Mini-Games in {{MM|-}}, Link is given infinite ammunition for all three of the {{Plural|MM3D|Mini-Game}}.

In Bombchu Gallery, Link must hit the targets with Bombchu, which will climb up the walls. The targets can be destroyed either by the Bombchu directly colliding with it or by the Bombchu exploding near it.

In Bomb Basket, Link must throw Bombs into the baskets. The baskets are at different heights, requiring Link to aim with the raising platform. The Bombs will still go into the baskets even if the aim is slightly off.

In Target Shooting, Link must shoot the targets on the wall with either a [[Bow]] or with [[Deku Link]]'s bubbles.<ref>{{Cite|But it looks like he can shoot bubbles, so let's let him play, Darling.|Honey|MM}}</ref> There are fewer targets when playing as Deku Link, to make up for bubbles' slow firing and travel speed. Deku Link will also be given infinite [[Magic Power|Magic]] for the duration of this {{Term|MM3D|Mini-Game}}.

The prize for getting a perfect score on any given day is a Purple Rupee worth 50. However, if Link manages to get a perfect score for all three challenges during the same three-day cycle, he will earn a [[Piece of Heart]] on the Final Day.<ref>{{Cite|H-H-Honey!!! He...He got a perfect score three days in a row. This is all we have left to give him, Honey.|Darling|MM}}</ref> In {{MM3D}}, Link receives [[Fishing Hole Pass]]es instead of Purple Rupees.

==Trivia==
*If Link hits Honey & Darling during any of the {{Plural|MM3D|Mini-Game}}, they will tell him to stop and exclaim how much it hurts.<ref>{{Cite|That hurts!|Honey & Darling|MM}}</ref> While they do this, the timer is temporarily paused.
*Shooting the two targets outside the shop causes Rupees to fall out of the targets.
