{{Term/Store|Noko||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|ST|Anouki|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 288 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref> He resides in {{Term|ST|Anouki Village|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Snow Realm|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|ST|Noko}} lives in a house in the southern portion of {{Term|ST|Anouki Village}}. When {{Term|ST|Link|link}} visits the village for the first time, he is tasked by {{Term|ST|Honcho|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Anouki}} chief, to pair up all of the {{Plural|ST|Anouki}} in order to put together a village watch for all the monsters that have begun to appear after the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} vanished. {{Term|ST|Noko}} comments to {{Term|ST|Link}} that he dislikes {{Plural|ST|Anouki}} who wear yellow clothes.<ref>{{Cite|Me? My biggest gripe is {{Color|ST Light Red|guys wearin' yellow}}. I mean...blech! As far as I'm concerned, outside of bananas, nothing looks good in yellow!|Noko|ST}}</ref> Due to his preferences, {{Term|ST|Link}} ends pairing him up with an {{Term|ST|Anouki}} named {{Term|ST|Kofu|link}}.

{{Term|ST|Noko}} also works at the village's spring, creating {{Term|ST|Mega Ice|link}}, an ice so cold it can freeze lava. However, when {{Term|ST|Link}} needs to acquire some {{Term|ST|Mega Ice}}, he learns that an {{Term|ST|Ice ChuChu|link}} fell into the village's spring, ruining the water.<ref>{{Cite|Ya know how we sell Mega Ica here? Yeah, it's our village's major export. Well, a little while ago, an Ice Chu Chu fell in the water... And ever since, there's been a gooey film floatin' on the surface... It's revoltin'!|Noko|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Link}} must bring {{Term|ST|Noko}} aboard the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} and take him to the {{Term|ST|Wellspring Station|link}}, where he can resume making {{Term|ST|Mega Ice}}.<ref>{{Cite|Booya! Is this a prime spot for makin' Mega Ice or what?! Yep, this is it. I'm gonna start up the business right here, guy!|Noko|ST}}</ref>
