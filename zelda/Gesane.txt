{{Term/Store|Gesane||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Gesane|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Gesane}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} guard who patrols the lowest entrance of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. He carries a {{Term|BotW|Feathered Spear|link}} and continuously paces the bridge closest to {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Gesane}}, he greets him and asks if he needs something.<ref>{{Cite|Good day. Did you need something?|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks who he is, so {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} introduces himself.<ref>{{Cite|[Who are you?] I am Gesane of the Rito.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Plural|BotW|Rito}}, {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} recites a saying about the {{Plural|BotW|Rito}} and is surprised {{Term|BotW|Link}} has never heard it.<ref>{{Cite|[The Rito?] Yes... "The Rito of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Totori}}. Where the men are fine archers and the women better singers." You being a {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}}, I'm surprised you haven't heard that before.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to visit {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, should he have a chance.<ref>{{Cite|You should visit the {{Color|BotW Blue|village}} if you get a chance.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has obtained the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, he can ask {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} about {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}}'s whereabouts. {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} reveals that {{Term|BotW|Teba}} left for the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|link}} to prepare to avenge {{Term|BotW|Harth|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Where's Teba?] He stormed off to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Flight Range}} with an angry look on his face. Bit of a loose cannon that one. I'm guessing he went to get revenge for Harth by himslef.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> He explains that, since the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range}} is where {{Term|BotW|Rito}} warriors train, {{Term|BotW|Teba}} went there to stock up on {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Flight Range}} is where Rito warriors train for battle. He probably went ther eto stock up on {{Color|BotW Blue|bows}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|bomb arrows}}.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> If asked about the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range}}, {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} directs him to {{Term|BotW|Dronoc's Pass|link}} on the other side of {{Term|BotW|Lake Totori|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Flight Range?] The {{Color|BotW Blue|Flight Range}} is beyond {{Color|BotW Blue|Dronoc's Pass}} on the other side of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Totori}}.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> Due to the difficulty of traveling around {{Term|BotW|Lake Totori}}, {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} suggests {{Term|BotW|Link}} take the western path.<ref>{{Cite|If you're going around {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Totori}} to get there, there's a big difference between the east and west sides. For a {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}} like yourself... I'd suggest the west side. It's flatter.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} will note that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has come at a good time now that the trouble is gone.<ref>{{Cite|You've come at a good time... {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} was kicking up a storm just a while ago, but he's calmed down now.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> He asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs something,<ref>{{Cite|Hmm? Did you need something?|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Link}} replies to by asking how {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} is. He has nothing to complain about in his life,<ref>{{Cite|How am I? Can't complain, I suppose...|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> but then he reveals that he saw a {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus|link}} when he went on a walk earlier.<ref>{{Cite|Never mind that. Please listen to this! I was out for a walk earlier, and I saw... a {{Color|BotW Blue|Frost Talus}}!|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> He clarifies that it is a giant monster made of enchanted {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Yes! It's a giant monster made of enchanted ice!|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> and he thought it was both incredible and terrifying.<ref>{{Cite|I was out running an errand when I saw it... It was incredible...and terrifying. It was incredibly terrifying.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> He thinks he saw it in {{Term|BotW|Coldsnap Hollow|link}},  but he ended up fleeing very quickly.<ref>{{Cite|I believe I saw it over in {{Color|BotW Blue|Coldsnap Hollow}} before my adrenaline kicked in and I fled like the wind.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> Despite its size, {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} believes a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} could take it down easily.<ref>{{Cite|In any case, it was enormous. But I bet a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} could have taken it down no problem.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> This begins the "{{Term|BotW|Face the Frost Talus|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} again, he can ask about the {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus}}. {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} explains that it is made of enchanted {{Term|BotW|Ice}},<ref>{{Cite|[Frost Talus?] A {{Color|BotW Blue|Frost Talus}} is a giant made entirely of enchanted ice.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> and he says that {{Term|BotW|Coldsnap Hollow}} should be avoided because of it.<ref>{{Cite|I saw one over by {{Color|BotW Blue|Coldsnap Hollow}}. If you want to keep breathing, I suggest you stay far away from there.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> Though he is good at fighting, {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} would not want to face a {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm pretty good in a fight, but I wouldn't want to bump into that thing in a dark valley.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has defeated the {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} to share the news with {{Term|BotW|Gesane}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I defeated it.] WHAT?! You defeated the {{Color|BotW Blue|Frost Talus}} in {{Color|BotW Blue|Coldsnap Hollow}}?!|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> He would not have thought that a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} could take down something of that size.<ref>{{Cite|I never would have thought a {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}} could take down something that big. No offense.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> Ultimately, {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} is relieved, since {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} could have been in danger if it had wandered towards the town.<ref>{{Cite|Well, that's a relief. If that thing had wandered this way, the entire village would've been in danger!|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gifts {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's not much, but I'd like to reward you for your trouble.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref>

When spoken to afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Gesane}} is still very shocked that {{Term|BotW|Link}} managed to defeat the {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus}}.<ref>{{Cite|To think you felled the {{Color|BotW Blue|Frost Talus}} in {{Color|BotW Blue|Coldsnap Hollow}}. It boggles the mind.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref> However, the world is still full of other dangerous creatures, so he remains vigilant.<ref>{{Cite|But this world's chock-full of savage and terrifying beasts. I must remain ever vigilant.|Gesane|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
