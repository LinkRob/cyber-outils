"{{Term/Store|EX The Champions' Ballad||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX The Champions' Ballad}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} is only accessible after calming all four {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}. It can be received after reloading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, loading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. After this is done, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|... Link...}} Link...|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> who reveals she has been watching him on his journey.<ref>{{Cite|I have witnessed your journey. You have overcome every challenge laid before you.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If the DLC Pack is installed after calming all of the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will hear {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} as soon as the game is opened.}} Now that the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} are free, <ref>{{Cite|Ruta. Rudania. Medoh. Naboris. All of the Divine Beasts are now free.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> there is another challenge that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} believes {{Term|BotW|Link}} can overcome.<ref>{{Cite|With the strength you have attained, I am certain you can unlock the powerful force hidden within.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She instructs him to return to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}} and place the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} into the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Go to the Shrine of Resurrection and return the Sheikah Slate to its terminal.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> When she finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|display=Quest}} will begin.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}} and returns the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}, the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal}} will verify him as a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} and activate the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate and Champion verification complete. Activating the Divine Beast Tamer's Trial.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} will then open, revealing four new glowing points around the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} associated with the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial|display=Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Associated locations have been marked on the map.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} is returned to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, at which point a pedestal bearing the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator|link}} appears in the chamber. He is directed to take the {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} to begin the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial|display=Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Take hold of the provided weapon to begin the trial.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}}, his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} will be reduced to a quarter of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}. He hears the voice of {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|link}} speaking to him,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I am Maz Koshia. I now address the one who wishes to master a Divine Beast...}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> who warns him that he must be dedicated to undertake the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To undertake this task requires unwavering dedication.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> He explains that, while the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} is capable of defeating {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} in a single hit, anyone who wields it can also be defeated in a similar way.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|That weapon defeats foes with one hit. However, the reverse is also true, as its wielder will also fall to a single strike.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> It is only usable twice in a given period, which is indicated by whether or not it is glowing.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You can use this weapon's ability only twice within a set period of time... only when it is glowing. When enough time passes, the weapon will regain its sheen along with its power.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to prove his power by defeating the monsters found at the four locations marked on the {{Term|BotW|Map}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|At the four marked locations within the Great Plateau... defeat all monsters present to prove your power.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} wish to abandon the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial}}, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} tells him to leave the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}, which will cause the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} to return to its pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|If you are not up to the challenge, leave this plateau. The weapon will return here, and the trial will end.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>

After each {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} is cleared, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} will rise from the ground. These are {{Term|BotW|Etsu Korima Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ruvo Korbah Shrine|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Yowaka Ita Shrine|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} leaves {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah Shrine}}, he will find {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} performing a song nearby.<ref>{{Cite|What a doleful sky...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches him, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} is surprised to see another traveler on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? How unexpected to see a traveler on this solitary plateau.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} recognizes {{Term|BotW|Link}}, but he realizes that he is unwell.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, we meet again! Hm? Not to be rude, but you don't look so well. Much like this plateau, you're looking a mite dreary.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} apologizes if he overstepped, assuming that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is simply tired from traveling.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... I apologize. Perhaps it was not my place to say as much. I understand more than most the burden of travel.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kass}} reveals that he came to the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}} in search of an ancient song.<ref>{{Cite|As for me, I came to this spot to pursue an {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient song}} about a hero at this very plateau.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He believes that he and {{Term|BotW|Link}} were destined to meet at this location,<ref>{{Cite|I can't shake the feeling that we were destined to meet here.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> so he asks if he would like to hear the song.<ref>{{Cite|In any case, would you like to hear my song?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to listen, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will thank him as ask that he open his heart to the song.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes, please!] Thank you, kind traveler! Now then... please open your ears and your heart to my song...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics descrive a beast separate from the four {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} waiting for a hero, which is capable of making a sound that could rile {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪The flames crawl, the waters rise,<br/>the lightning strikes, the wind sighs.<br/>♪A beast beyond the divine four<br/>awaits a hero forevermore<br/>♪Let not the sound leave horses riled.<br/>Breathe in the breath of the wild.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} remarks how odd the song is and explains that he found it among the notes of his teacher, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Curious, indeed. As it were, I perused my teacher's old notes...and found that song there.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He wonders what kind of sound would be capable of scaring a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm, what sort of sound could alarm a horse so effectively? Kabam? Kapow? Rumble, rumble? Honestly, I haven't a clue.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He plans on visiting other locations mentioned in the notes to help open his mind.<ref>{{Cite|It certainly helps to visit the locations that inspired the song. Yes, it helps to open my mind to inspiration. Those notes had other interesting songs too... I plan to visit those locations as well.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

Once all four {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} have been completed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will hear {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} praising him for completing the trials.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I am Maz Koshia. You have done well to conquer the trials laid before you.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Link}} has done well so far, there are more trials that the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} will lead him to.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You are Hyrule's beacon of hope. But your trials have only just begun. The divine weapon shall lead you...}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} draws the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}}, it splits into four orbs, which fly into the sky before scattering across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}}. Where they land, {{Plural|BotW|Monument|link}} rise from the ground. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go to the four {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} to continue his trials, the locations of which appear on his {{Term|BotW|Map}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have proven your power, a new journey awaits. Go to the four locations that will be revealed to you, and reach for greater heights than ever before.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> After this, all of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} are restored.

To continue, {{Term|BotW|Link}} needs to complete the following {{Plural|BotW|Main Quest}}:
* "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}"
* "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}"
* "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song|link}}"
* "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song|link}}"

After all four {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|display=Quests}} are completed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} commending him for his efforts in the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I am Maz Koshia. I must commend you for conquering the trial thus far.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will now be able to access the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|The door to the final trial is now open at the Shrine of Resurrection. Go, and face your destiny.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> A glowing marker appears on the {{Term|BotW|Map}} on top of the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|display=Shrine}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} will speak to him again.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the brave soul who wishes to master a Divine Beast... You have done well to return to this place of beginnings.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> He must face one {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have overcome many challenges thus far. Do so, and the final passageway will reveal itself...}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|display=Pedestal}}, he will once again be instructed to return the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Place the Sheikah Slate in the pedestal.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref> Once it has been placed in the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal}}, it will be confirmed.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate confirmed.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref> The chamber's door will close as it begins to descend into the ground.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Descending...<br/>Please beware of tremors.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref>

When the doors open, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to see an overview of the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}}. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} welcomes him to the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|display=Trial}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Welcome, hero. You have overcome many trials to reach me. Now it is time to face the final trial.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> instructing him to retrieve a {{Term|BotW|Map}} from the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}} ahead.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|This trial is a complex labyrinth. To start, you will need a map. You may obtain map information from the Guidance Stone.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> Once he does so, he will receive a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} {{Term|BotW|Map}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling map of Divine Beast.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> which grants him some control over his environment.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Divine Beast Controls have been enabled.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

Inside the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must traverse four chambers, each based around a specific element. These are {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Water|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}}. In each chamber, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must activate a {{Term|BotW|Terminal|link}}, which will authenticate his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref> Once this is done, one of the locks on the door in the back of the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial}} will be deactivated.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Deactivated one lock of the door.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref> He will then be informed of how many {{Plural|BotW|Terminal}} have yet to be activated.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|There are three terminals remaining.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|There are two terminals remaining.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|There is one terminal remaining.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref> After activating the final {{Term|BotW|Terminal}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to open the door and access the last part of the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|display=Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|All terminal locks have been deactivated. You can now access the deepest area.}}|Terminal|BotW}}</ref>

Past the door, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}. When he approaches the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|display=Maz Koshia}} remarks that he has the heart of a hero.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have returned. Your heart is truly that of a hero.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> Slowly, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} stands, at which point he offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} one last trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of the Goddess Hylia... I offer this final trial!}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} is {{Term|BotW|Warping|display=Warped}} away to a platform above the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}. {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} {{Term|BotW|Warping|display=Warps}} into the center of the platform, and he begins {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s last test.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Let us begin.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>

After defeating {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}}, he congratulates {{Term|BotW|Link}} for proving himself as a hero.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You faced the challenge with great courage. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} places the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} under the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}}, it distills the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} into a {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling rune...}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} reiterates that this {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} is destined for a hero.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|That which you have obtained... is a masterpiece of innovation, created for the one true hero.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> He wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before he is {{Term|BotW|Warping|display=Warped}} away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Hero of Hyrule... May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} appears outside of the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}. {{Term|BotW|Kass}} stands nearby, overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits|link}} and the rest of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}}. When he notices {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hm? As {{Term|BotW|Link}} looks upon {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}, he suddenly remembers "{{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad (Memory)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.

After the {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}} concludes, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} explains that this was the day that cemented the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}}' roles.<ref>{{Cite|After this ceremony, they would all forever be known as Champions.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He recalls his teacher telling him to write songs that could transport listeners to the past,<ref>{{Cite|My teacher always advised me to write songs that transport the listener to the moment in time you're singing of.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and he now understands the power of music he was speaking of.<ref>{{Cite|Now...I finally feel I understand what he meant...and the true power of music.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Among his teacher's notes, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} found an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} that he would like {{Term|BotW|Link}} to keep.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, I found this when I was looking through my teacher's notes.
