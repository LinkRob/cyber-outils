The Star Belt Archipelago is located in the northeastern region of the [[Great Sea]], southeast of [[Dragon Roost Island]]. It is made up of a group of rocks sticking out of the water in the formation of the Orion constellation. When the moon is in its first quarter stage in the night sky, the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship|link}} will appear in these waters.
