The '''Battle Axe''' is a weapon featured in {{TCT}}. Belonging to {{Term|TCT|Ganon|link}}, {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}} and {{Term|TCT|Link}} are tricked by a {{Term|TCT|Goriya|link}} who tells them that the {{Term|TCT|Battle Axe}} is the only weapon that can truly defeat {{Term|TCT|Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Spare me and I'll show you a powerful weapon,' the monster pleads. 'A battle axe. It's the only thing that can defeat Ganon, and I know where it is.'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=13}}</ref> 

If the princess and the young hero decide to follow the {{Term|TCT|Goriya}}, he will lead them into a large room in the depths of the mountain, where at the far end of the room, is a throne holding the massive battle axe.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=They enter a huge room carved into the depths of the mountain, big enough to hold an army. Together they cross the bare stone floor to a throne set on a platform in the center. There on the throne lies a massive battle-axe.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=93}}</ref> The {{Term|TCT|Goriya}} disappears as {{Term|TCT|Link}} grabs the axe, following Zelda out of the palace and into the forest. Just then, {{Term|TCT|Ganon}} appears from behind a lone tree.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='I don't know why,' Zelda says. 'But I have a feeling this is the place we're going to meet Ganon.' 'You're so right, little princess,' says a cold, menacing voice. From behind the tree steps a dark, hooded figure. 'Ganon!' Zelda whispers.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=94}}</ref> {{Term|TCT|Link}}, thinking that he has the right weapon to defeat the evil wizard, will throw the axe at the {{Term|TCT|Magic Scroll|link}} in {{Term|TCT|Ganon}}'s hand, but it stops in midair.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link hurls the axe with all his might at the scroll in Ganon's hand. It turns end over end as it flies through the air. Then it stops! It hangs in mid-air for a second, then drops to the ground with a thud.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=94}}</ref> The King of Evil then informs the pair that the {{Term|TCT|Battle Axe}} belongs to him, and that he told the {{Term|TCT|Goriya}} to lead {{Term|TCT|Link}} and {{Term|TCT|Zelda}} to it so that they'd stop looking for the real weapon that could defeat him: the {{Term|TCT|Spear}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='My axe, you mean,' says Ganon. 'I told that goriya to lead you to it. I knew if you had the axe you'd stop looking for the real weapon that can defeat me.'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=95}}</ref> The book ends with a {{Term|TCT|Game Over|link}} as {{Term|TCT|Ganon}} prepares to cast the {{Term|TCT|Magic Scroll}} spell again to encase {{Term|TCT|Link}} in the {{Term|TCT|Crystal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Laughing, Ganon raises the magic scroll in his gnarled claw. 'Oh no!' cries Zelda. 'He's going to cast the crystal spell again!'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=95}}</ref>
