==Features and Overview==
Misery Mire is [[Lorule]]'s equivalent of [[Hyrule]]'s desert. Upon entering it, [[Link]] hears the voice of [[Hilda]] saying she can sense a {{Term|ALBW|Sage|link}} nearby. Link needs to go through Misery Mire in order to reach the {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace|link}} in Hyrule by using [[Fissure]]s.
