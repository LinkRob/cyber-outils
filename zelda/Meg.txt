'''Meg''' is a recurring [[Sub-Boss]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}. She is the oldest of the four [[Poe Sisters]].

==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Meg - one of the Poe sisters}}<br/>If she splits into multiple images, you need to figure out which one is the real one. Is it this one?
}}
Meg appears along with her Sisters in the [[Forest Temple]] in {{OoT}}. She appears after all of her sisters have been slain and their respective torches lit, and uses the Temple's main room as her battleground to fight [[Link]].

Meg's battle technique is vastly different from her Sisters; she replicates herself into three other clones and begins to spin around Link. Mistaking the true Meg for an illusion will cause the fake to disappear, giving Meg a chance to inflict damage. Although her technique is intimidating, her true form is easily recognizable; the real Meg will be the first to spin around once the four "Megs" have surrounded Link and again when she attacks. Meg can be struck by Link's [[Master Sword]], hit by [[Arrow]]s, as well as pierced by the [[Hookshot]]. Four strikes on the true Meg is enough to dispel her and light the final, purple torch in the center of the Temple. Meg's death and the lighting of the four torches will enable Link to access the Temple's basement, where the Temple's [[boss]], [[Phantom Ganon]], awaits.

Meg appears along with her three Sisters at the [[Spirit House]] in [[Ikana Canyon]] in {{MM}}. Link can battle all four of the Poe Sisters to earn a [[Piece of Heart]] at the cost of 30 [[Rupee]]s. Her battle is identical to in ''Ocarina of Time''.

==Trivia==
*Before Meg's battle in {{OoT|-}} starts, she appears to be [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_PoeSister_Lurk3.wav crying]. This is likely because her sisters were all defeated, hence she reveals herself after they have perished by Link.
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'', [[Tatl]] claims Meg is the youngest of the Poe Sisters. However, this is a developer oversight. In the original [[Nintendo 64]] version, she is stated to be the oldest - as per Meg March from ''Little Women'', the character she is named after.
