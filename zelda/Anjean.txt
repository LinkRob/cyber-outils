{{Term/Store|Anjean||Series, ST}} (pronounced {{IPA|ænˈdʒiːn}}, {{Respell|an|JEEN}}){{Fact}} is a [[:Category:Characters|character]] in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 289 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref> She is a member of the {{Term|ST|Lokomo|link}} people and the protector of the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits|link}}, the central hub of the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}}, whose goal is to prevent the evil {{Term|ST|Malladus|link}} from breaking free of his imprisonment.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I wouldn't put it exactly that way. But I do watch over the Tower of Spirits.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Anjean}} provides the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}}, which {{Term|ST|Link|link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link}} use along their quest. {{Term|ST|Anjean}} was also a very close friend with {{Term|ST|Tetra|link}} after she met her shortly after the feisty pirate arrived to the land that would become the next {{Term|ST|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Feisty AND bright, just like she was! The fruit doesn't fall far from the tree, does it? Yes, I knew one of your ancestors. I met her when she had just arrived here. She and I were friends for a very long time.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> As a result of their strong friendship, {{Term|ST|Anjean}} gave {{Term|ST|Tetra}} the sacred instrument known as the {{Term|ST|Spirit Flute|link}}, but only under the condition that she and her descendants would use it to protect the land.<ref>{{Cite|Yes! And it was I who gave it to your ancestors! But only under the condition that they protect the land for all time. Of course, back then, I didn't think things would take such a perilous turn. I'm glad that after all these years it has found its way into your hands.|Anjean|ST}}</ref>

As the guardian of the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits}}, {{Term|ST|Anjean}} plays a major role, providing both {{Term|ST|Link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} with helpful advice on how to help restore the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)}} and destroy {{Term|ST|Malladus}}, once and for all. She also supplies most of the background information about the {{Term|ST|Lokomo}}, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Flute}}, and the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)}}. One of {{Term|ST|Anjean}}'s most notable abilities is her skill to telepathically communicate with others, which she does frequently to aid the young heroes in their quest.

==Biography==
==={{ST|-}}===
{{Manual|ST|Anjean|Guardian of the Tower of Spirits, which controls the kingdom’s Spirit Tracks. She gives Link the Spirit Train.}}
{{Term|ST|Anjean}} is first introduced after {{Term|ST|Link|link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link}} arrive to the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits|link}} for the first time. Here, she elaborates to the young duo how {{Term|ST|Malladus|link}} is being imprisoned by the use of the energy that the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} provide between the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits}} and the four {{Plural|ST|Temple|link}}, keeping {{Term|ST|Malladus}}' prison intact.<ref>{{Cite|It's not chains or bars that keep him imprisoned, but an energy that flows between the tower and the four temples that surround it.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> Moreover, she reveals that in order for {{Term|ST|Malladus}} to truly resurrect, he needs {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}}'s physical body to act as a vessel.<ref>{{Cite|Of course, in order to resurrect Malladus, they need one other key ingredient... A vessel from the royal line of ancient Hyrule, with sacred power coursing through its veins. Yes, Malladus requires YOU in order to return. But...only your shell, my dear.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Anjean}} tasks {{Term|ST|Link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} with restoring the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)}} to the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits}} to fortify {{Term|ST|Malladus}}' prison, thus prevent his resurrection.<ref>{{Cite|To prevent him from possessing your body, you must fortify his prison. To do this, restore the energy between the temples and this tower. In other words... Restore the tracks between here and the temples.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> After {{Term|ST|Link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} successfully retrieve the first {{Term|ST|Rail Map|link}}, {{Term|ST|Anjean}} explains that they must travel to the different {{Plural|ST|Realm|link}} of {{Term|ST|Hyrule|link}} and ask for the {{Plural|ST|Lokomo|link}}' help by playing their sacred {{Plural|ST|Instrument|link}} and thus reveal the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)}} leading to the {{Plural|ST|Temple}}.<ref>{{Cite|Not yet. But there is a way to restore energy to the rail maps. You can ask my people to help you.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> She then lends to {{Term|ST|Link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} the {{Term|ST|Spirit Flute|link}}, one of the sacred {{Plural|ST|Instrument}} needed to restore the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)}}, as well as the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} to help them travel easily between {{Plural|ST|Realm}}.<ref>{{Cite|No, you two will have need of it. Please take it with you! For now... You must go find the other Lokomos and restore the energy of the rail maps. Once you do, the Spirit Tracks will reappear.|Anjean|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This train is a symbol of the spirits. People don't ordinarily ride around in it. But these are no ordinary circumstances. I'm sure the spirits wouldn't mind lending it to you.|Anjean|ST}}</ref>

[[File:ST Anjean Speech.png|thumb|left|{{Term|ST|Anjean}}'s first appearance in {{ST|-}}]]
After {{Term|ST|Link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} retrieve the {{Term|ST|Fire Rail Map|link}} from the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits}}, they are confronted by {{Term|ST|Byrne|link}}, and just as he is about to attack them, {{Term|ST|Anjean}} shows up and stops him.<ref>{{Cite|Stop, Byrne.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> As {{Term|ST|Anjean}} affronts {{Term|ST|Byrne}}, it is revealed that he used to be her apprentice, but left her teachings once he found out that the {{Plural|ST|Spirit of Good|link}} would never provide him with any more power, thus pushing him towards joining in the resurrection of {{Term|ST|Malladus}}. {{Term|ST|Anjean}}, ashamed of his recent actions since the {{Plural|ST|Spirit of Good}} sent them to protect the land, calls him an "unworthy apprentice," for no matter how many times she corrected his behavior, he would never listen to her.<ref>{{Cite|I always suspected this day would come. Indeed, I had a premonition the day you left the tower. No matter how many times I corrected your behavior, you never were very good at listening, were you? You were an unworthy apprentice. And now look at you. How can I face the spirits of good? The spirits sent us here to continue their work! That you would try to resurrect the Demon King... It's unfathomable to me.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> After {{Term|ST|Byrne}} explains his reasoning behind his actions, {{Term|ST|Anjean}} gives up hope in convincing him otherwise and urges {{Term|ST|Link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} to hurry to the {{Term|ST|Fire Temple|link}}, warping them to the entrance of the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits}} while she ensues in combat against {{Term|ST|Byrne}}.<ref>{{Cite|...Your thoughts are twisted. It's obvious that the time for saving you is past. My words would only be lost on you. Hurry to the Fire Temple, Your Highness. Quickly! You too, Link! Restore the Spirit Tracks! But, Anjean-- Go! I will handle this situation. Now then... Shall we begin?|Anjean|ST}}</ref>

Once {{Term|ST|Malladus}} is resurrected and escapes with {{Term|ST|Cole|link}} to the {{Term|ST|Dark Realm|link}} using the {{Term|ST|Demon Train|link}}, {{Term|ST|Anjean}} appears again, seemingly unharmed from her battle with {{Term|ST|Byrne}}, although she mentions she was not able to best him since he had gotten stronger.<ref>{{Cite|I...I couldn't best Byrne. I have to admit, he has grown very strong. Of course, he's had a century to train. And he's always had the drive to succeed.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} then asks her if there is a way to force {{Term|ST|Malladus}} out of her body, and {{Term|ST|Anjean}} mentions that the {{Term|ST|Bow of Light|link}}, which was used in the {{Term|ST|War|link}} between the {{Plural|ST|Spirit of Good}} and {{Term|ST|Malladus}}, could drive his spirit out from {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}}'s body.<ref>{{Cite|In the battle between the Demon King and the spirits, there was a weapon called the Bow of Light... Perhaps it can drive his spirit from your body.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> To reach the {{Term|ST|Sand Temple|link}}, the location of the {{Term|ST|Bow of Light}}, she gives them a {{Term|ST|Force Gem|link}}, revealing the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)}} to the {{Term|ST|Sand Sanctuary|link}}, sending them to their next task.

[[File:ST Anjean Giving the Lokomo Sword to Link.png|thumb|right|{{Term|ST|Anjean}} entrusting the {{Term|ST|Lokomo Sword}} to {{Term|ST|Link}}]]
With the {{Term|ST|Bow of Light}} in hand, {{Term|ST|Link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} return to {{Term|ST|Anjean}}, and although {{Term|ST|Anjean}} tried to find where {{Term|ST|Malladus}} and {{Term|ST|Cole}} had gone to, she could not pinpoint their exact location.<ref>{{Cite|While you two were busy obtaining the Bow of Light, I have been searching for the Demon Train. That's the train that Malladus used to escape. But he and Cole have hidden it too well...|Anjean|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Byrne}}, who had been left in the care of {{Term|ST|Anjean}} after he was betrayed and injured by {{Term|ST|Malladus}} and {{Term|ST|Cole}}, tells them to find the {{Term|ST|Compass of Light|link}} beneath the altar in the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits}}, which reveals the location of the {{Term|ST|Demon Train}} within the {{Term|ST|Dark Realm}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Demon Train comes from the Dark Realm. But even Cole didn't know its exact location at first. He had me build a compass using the spirits' power to find the Demon Train in the Dark Realm.|Byrne|ST}}</ref> Before {{Term|ST|Link}} departs to find the {{Term|ST|Compass of Light}}, {{Term|ST|Anjean}} gives him the {{Term|ST|Lokomo Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This Lokomo Sword was a gift from one of the spirits. As such, it is filled with sacred energy. It has felled many evil creatures in its lifetime. Until now, only the spirits have wielded this blade...|Anjean|ST}}</ref> Although only {{Plural|ST|Spirit|link}} had wielded it before, she entrusts it to him, saying that he is the one meant to wield it.<ref>{{Cite|Before you go, please take this, Link. I think you're the one who is meant to use it.|Anjean|ST}}</ref>

After the defeat of the {{Term|ST|Demon Train}}, {{Term|ST|Anjean}} wishes to accompany {{Term|ST|Link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} on their battle against {{Term|ST|Malladus}}.<ref>{{Cite|I shall accompany you from here on out. It's too dangerous for just the two of you.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> However, since her wounds from her battle against {{Term|ST|Byrne}} are still fresh, {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} urges her to rest, for she and {{Term|ST|Link}} will face {{Term|ST|Malladus}} alone.<ref>{{Cite|But your wounds from our battle are still fresh. And what's to come will be very demanding. It's my turn now, Master. It's...the least I can do.|Byrne|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes, Anjean, it's time for you to rest. [Link] and I can take care of them. After all, it's just Malladus and Cole. It'll be a piece of cake.|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref> At the least, {{Term|ST|Anjean}} provides {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} a {{Term|ST|Phantom|link}} to aid {{Term|ST|Link}} in their upcoming battle. She then blesses them and tells them to go forward with caution.<ref>{{Cite|As you wish, my dear. It's up to you two, then, Link and Zelda. May the spirits be with you. I can do no more. You must go forth on your own.|Anjean|ST}}</ref>

{{Term|ST|Anjean}} is last seen once {{Term|ST|Link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} defeat {{Term|ST|Malladus}}: seeing how their protection is no longer needed, {{Term|ST|Anjean}} and the rest of the {{Plural|ST|Lokomo}} depart to the heavens, entrusting {{Term|ST|Link}} and {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} to watch over the land in their stead.<ref>{{Cite|You see, as Lokomos... We weren't only meant to protect the Spirit Tracks. We were also meant to watch over mankind. But our protection is no longer needed. Even without the spirits' guidance, you will do well. So I think I will return to the heavens... In the company of my old friend Byrne. Please watch over this land, my dear. And, Link...You must help her.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Anjean}}'s vehicle is last seen in {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}}'s office during the ending sequence.

==Trivia==
*It is implied that {{Term|ST|Anjean}}'s sacred {{Term|ST|Instrument|link}} is the {{Term|ST|Spirit Flute|link}}. However, she no longer owns it as she gave it to the current {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link}}'s great-great grandmother, {{Term|ST|Tetra|link}}, who was a very close friend to {{Term|ST|Anjean}}.
*{{Term|ST|Anjean}} has a hairstyle very similar to {{Term|ST|Tetra}}, the incarnation of {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} one hundred years prior to {{ST|-}}. She is also usually seen with a winking expression, a habit she shares with {{Term|ST|Tetra}}.
