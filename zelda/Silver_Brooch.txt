The '''Silver Brooch''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Silver Brooch|Jewelry of high quality. Wearing it makes rupees appear a little more often when breaking objects or defeating monsters.<ref name="Silver Brooch">{{Cite|Jewelry of high quality. The Silver Brooch is an [[Accessory]] found on the west side of [[Jabul Waters]], behind a bombable wall. Equipping it grants the [[Rupee Appearance Up]] effect, increasing the drop rate of [[Rupee|Rupees]].
