{{Term/Store|Malice Naydra||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book= CaC|publisher= |page=158}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Stub|BotW}}
{{Term|BotW|Malice Naydra}} is {{Term|Series|Naydra|link}}, who has been corrupted by {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}} and is suffering as a result. She has glowing eyes covering her body, which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must shoot at while she is in flight to dispel the {{Term|BotW|Malice}}.

At the beginning of the fight, {{Term|BotW|Naydra}} curls up at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must shoot one of the large eyes to send {{Term|BotW|Naydra}} flying into the air around {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}. A second shot will cause {{Term|BotW|Malice Naydra}} to fly down the mountain, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pursue her and attack the remaining eyes to destroy the {{Term|BotW|Malice}}.  This will cure {{Term|BotW|Naydra}} and allow her to roam freely across Hyrule, much like {{Term|BotW|Dinraal}} and {{Term|BotW|Farosh}}.

==Trivia==
*During the fight with {{Term|BotW|Malice Naydra}}, a corrupted version of the {{Plural|BotW|Dragon|link}}' theme plays.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Peaches Lamb|published= March 6th, 2017|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0zLDAHkPxpc|title= Corrupted Dragon (The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild OST)|site= YouTube|type= video}}</ref>
*During the fight, Naydra makes no effort to attack Link directly. This may mean that she still has some control over her body and mind.
*The fight is reminiscent of the fight against the possessed [[Levias]] where Link is also in the air and has to destroy [[Bilocyte|Bilocyte's]] eyes.
