==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|History of the Zora, Part Five}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|link}} that can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Zodobon Highlands|link}}. It needs to be read to complete the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}".

This {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monument}} details the story of {{Term|BotW|Princess Ruto|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Long, long ago... In a past more distant than even the Great Calamity or the creation of the Divine Beast Vah Ruta... There was a Zora princess named Ruto.|History of the Zora, Part Five|BotW}}</ref> She was an attendant to the {{Term|OoT|Lord Jabu-Jabu|link|display=patron deity}} of the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}}, and she was a fair and lively girl beloved by all.<ref>{{Cite|We know that she was an attendant to the Zora patron deity and that she was a fair and lively girl, beloved to all.|History of the Zora, Part Five|BotW}}</ref> When an {{Term|OoT|Ganondorf|link|display=evil man}} threatened {{Term|OoT|Zora's Domain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Ruto|display=Ruto}} awoke as a {{Term|OoT|Sage|link}} and faced him alongside the {{Term|OoT|Princess Zelda|link|display=princess}} and the {{Term|OoT|Link|link|display=hero}}.<ref>{{Cite|Around that same time, an evil man with designs on ruling the world appeared, bringing disaster upon Zora's Domain. It is said that Ruto then awoke as a sage, facing this foe alongside the princess of Hyrule and the hero of legend.|History of the Zora, Part Five|BotW}}</ref> Ages later, {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} was named in her honor.<ref>{{Cite|The Divine Beast Vah Ruta, built ages later to face off against Calamity Ganon, was named in honor of Ruto.|History of the Zora, Part Five|BotW}}</ref>
