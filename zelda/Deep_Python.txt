{{Term/Store|Deep Python|Deep Pythons|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are enemies in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Deep Python}} were referred to as '''Sea Snakes''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Sea Snake}}<br/>Sea Snakes are long, green eels who aggressively hunt their prey in the waters of Termina.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160311084700/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/s.jsp#SeaSnake|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= It's a {{Color|MM White|Deep Python}}. Get close to it and stun it. If you fail, it'll getcha!
}}

Deep Pythons are giant sea snakes that infest the [[Great Bay]],<ref>{{Cite|It's a Deep Python. Get close to it and stun it. If you fail, it'll getcha!|Tatl|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please do this for me. Please defeat all the vicious sea snakes and save my friend.|Seahorse|MM}}</ref><ref name=Quote1>{{Cite|Yes, b-but Aveil... The sea is strangely murky where we were attacked by the sea snakes...|Gerudo|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I heard that the pirates were attacked by sea snakes near the creatures' lair.|Zora|MM}}</ref> lurking in the depths of [[Pinnacle Rock]]. The Deep Pythons are guarding some of [[Lulu]]'s missing [[Zora Egg|Egg]]s, which the thieving [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirates]] lost to when they were attacked,<ref name=Quote1/> as well as a [[Seahorse]].

When [[Link]] draws near, they shoot out of their burrows to grab him in their mouths and damage him before spitting him out. To defeat them, [[Zora Link]] must swim close to lure them out of their burrow, and stun and destroy them with his Electrical Shield or [[Boomerang]] Fins.

==Trivia==
*Although consistently described as sea snakes, they may in fact be based on {{wp|eel|eel}}s as they do not need to breathe air.
*According to [[Aveil]], Deep Pythons eat Zora Eggs.<ref>{{Cite|Hurry! Go find the other three eggs before those sea snakes eat them!|Aveil|MM}}</ref>
*In ''{{Animal Crossing|Animal Crossing (Series)|Animal Crossing}}'', {{Animal Crossing|Gulliver}} makes a reference to the Deep Pythons and Pinnacle Rock when he tells a story where he states, "When I fought the sea snakes at Pinnacle Rock! Wait a minute... That wasn't me!"
