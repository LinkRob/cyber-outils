{{Plural|BotW|Iron Sledgehammer}} are {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}}-made {{Plural|BotW|Hammer|link}} that can be found in all the major populated settlements of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.{{Fact}} They are reasonably durable, and good for breaking open {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}}. Despite being crafted by {{Term|BotW|Goron}} blacksmiths, they appear to be favored most by {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}}, such as {{Term|BotW|Bolson|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Karson|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Hudson|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Iron Sledgehammer}} are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, meaning they can not be used in conjunction with a {{Term|BotW|Shield}}. They were originally used for mining,<ref name="Inventory"/> but the {{Plural|BotW|Goron}} of the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|link}} have found more use in {{Plural|BotW|Pickaxe|link}}.{{Fact}}

Befitting their original purpose as a mining tool, the {{Term|BotW|Iron Sledgehammer}} has a hidden property that increases its damage dealt by four times when used against the {{Term|BotW|Ore Deposit}} atop a {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus|link}}. It is also capable of immediately destroying {{Plural|BotW|Stone Pebblit|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Iron Sledgehammer}} have a metal head and a wooden pole, but they are considered metal {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}. This means they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} can move them, and they will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
