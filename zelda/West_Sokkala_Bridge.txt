{{Term/Store|West Sokkala Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|West Sokkala Bridge|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|West Sokkala Bridge}} is a bridge found in {{Term|BotW|Deep Akkala|link}} where it can be used to cross {{Term|BotW|South Lake Akkala|link}}.

A metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found in the waters nearby, close to the waterfall that connects {{Term|BotW|South Lake Akkala}} to {{Term|BotW|Toto Lake|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can also find a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} hidden under a rock beneath the bridge.
