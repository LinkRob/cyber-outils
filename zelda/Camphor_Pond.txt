{{Term/Store|Camphor Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Camphor Pond'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Camphor Pond is a small body of water east of the [[Equestrian Riding Course]] in [[East Necluda]].

In the water is a submerged [[Treasure Chest]], which must be lifted out with [[Magnesis]]. This Treasure Chest contains 10 [[Arrow]]s.

A ring of lily pads rests in the Pond, which Link can jump into from a nearby rock to find a [[Korok]], who will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] in return for finding him.
