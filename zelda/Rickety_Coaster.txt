The {{Term/Store|Rickety Coaster||SS}} is the name of a {{Term|SSHD|Mini-Game|link}} in {{SS}}. It is run by a {{Term|SSHD|Goron|link}} by the name of {{Term|SSHD|Gortram|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|His name is Gortram. He is a colorful entrepreneur of the Goron race--beings made of living stone. He has started various ventures and currently owns and operates the Rickety Coaster.|Fi|SS}}</ref>{{Name Ref|SSHD}}

==Location and Rules==
Running around multiple sharp turns, the {{Term|SSHD|Rickety Coaster}} is a mine-cart track that connects large, ancient buildings that were part of a town where the {{Term|SSHD|Ancient Robot|display=citizens|link}} would help build the ships located in {{Term|SSHD|Lanayru Shipyard|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We've arrived, vrrm! This is the island where we used to make our ships, bzzt. You'll find the Shipyard here, as well at the town where the workers lived vweep!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SSHD|Gortram}} would spend his day riding the tracks due to being alone for years.<ref>{{Cite|I have been here alone for...well, who knows how long. Years. But I get to ride these carts every day, and I never get bored of it!|Gortram|SS}}</ref> It is first used as an alternative when the entrance to the Shipyard leading to more clues about the {{Term|SSHD|Sandship|link}} is found to be locked.<ref>{{Cite|That building is the construction bay, vrrm! But the entrance is closed, vrrrm. Looks like you can't get in. Each location on the island is linked to the others via a mine-cart track, vrrm! You might be able to get to the back door of the construction bay if you can get around to the other side of it using a mine cart, brrzrrt.|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SSHD|Gortram}} allows {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} to use the mine cart after explaining how to ride it safely. After riding through twists and turns {{Term|SSHD|Link}} reaches the Construction Bay where nothing but a {{Term|SSHD|Moldarach|link}} and sand is found.

After defeating the {{Term|SSHD|Moldarach}}, {{Term|SSHD|Link}} can return to the {{Term|SSHD|Rickety Coaster}} to find that {{Term|SSHD|Gortram}} has set up a minigame for the price of 20 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}} with two track choices: the "Heart Stopping!" or the "Scary!" track.<ref>{{Cite|What do you say? Have you got the guts to ride the Rickety Coaster? Just 20 Rupees a ride.|Gortram|SS}}</ref> "The Heart Stopping!" requires that {{Term|SSHD|Link}} beats {{Term|SSHD|Gortram}}'s incredible time of 1:05 in order to receive a {{Term|SSHD|Piece of Heart|link}}. Provided that {{Term|SSHD|Link}}'s time is under 1:10, he is awarded with various {{Plural|SSHD|Treasure|link}}. The "Scary!" track's rewards are 50 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee}} for a time of under 40 seconds, and 100 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee}} for beating {{Term|SSHD|Gortram}}'s record of 30 seconds.
