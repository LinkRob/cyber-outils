The '''Yamatami''' are a tribe that resides on [[Mount Desma]] in {{FPTRR}}.

==Characteristics==
They are relatively primitive, however they certainly have a "[[Rupee]] tooth."{{Fact}} Although visitors seldom show up, their entire town is set up as a tourist attraction. But if visitors are not careful, they will gouge them.

==History==
When [[Tingle]] visits them, he finds out that [[Uncle Rupee]] had corrupted the [[Tribal King|Tribe's chieftain]].<ref>{{Cite|Eh?! King say he no want Nidorian! King changed! Mamba sad!|Mamba|FPTRR}}</ref> He then decides to venture in to the bowels of the mountain. In the process, he destroys the monster that lives there, and cures the chieftain. Afterwards, the Yamatami Tribe names Tingle as one of their gods, and a statue is erected in his honor.
