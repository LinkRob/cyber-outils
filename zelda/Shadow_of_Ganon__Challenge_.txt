{{Term/Store|Shadow of Ganon||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 60 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Shadow of Ganon|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Shadow of Ganon}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given ten minutes to defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}} in the southern part of the map either is defeated or {{Term|HWAoC|Weak Point Smash|link}}ed, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Guardian|link}} spawn. Three {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} spawn as well when the same is done to the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}} in the east. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when all seven of these {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} are defeated. From the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, there are also many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}} that can be found in between the two {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Guardian}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using only both {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Terrako Component|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hydromelon}}.
