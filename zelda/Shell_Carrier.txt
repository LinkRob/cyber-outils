'''Shell Carriers''' are enemies in {{FPTRR}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|FPTRR|Shell Carrier}} appear in {{Term|FPTRR|Cape Treasure}} and the {{Term|FPTRR|Gooey Swamp}}. They are aggressive and will leap out from under their shells to angrily attack {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} if they spot him in its territory. They tend to drop {{Plural|FPTRR|Large Shell|link}} when defeated, however {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can only obtain this item by attacking it while it remains in its shell. As such, he should sneak up on them while they are asleep.
