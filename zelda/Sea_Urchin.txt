{{Term/Store|Sea Urchin|Sea Urchins|Series, LA, LADX, FSA|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|Enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 202 {{Exp Game|LA, FSA}}}}</ref>{{Note|This enemy is also referred to as '''Spiny Urchins''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref name="LA Guide 21">{{Cite Guide|game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 21}}</ref> However, as {{E}} supersedes the guide, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, they appear on [[Toronbo Shores]]. While they can not move and are not actively aggressive, they will damage [[Link]] if he touches them. He must push them out of the way using his [[Shield]] in order to reach his washed up [[Sword]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 95}}</ref> He can then use his Sword to destroy them with a single hit.

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, the four [[Links]] cannot push or destroy them with any weapons. Instead, they are forced to either simply avoid them, or defeat other enemies in the area in order to make them disappear.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Sea Urchin|An unmoving aquatic monster with hard spines that can pierce metal.<ref name="EoW Sea Urchin">{{Cite|An unmoving aquatic monster with hard spines that can pierce metal.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Sea Urchins are spiked enemies that deal damage on contact and are commonly encountered in and around water. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Sea Urchin [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of one.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
[[File:LA (Cagiva) Sea Urchin Artwork.png|thumb|left|A Sea Urchin from the manga]]
A single Sea Urchin is seen on [[Toronbo Shores]] blocking the access to [[Link]]'s [[Sword]]. Being chased by [[Octorok]]s, Link dashes and bounces off the Sea Urchin using his [[Shield]] to reach his Sword.

==={{SMM|-}} and {{SMM2|-}}===
Sea Urchins are seen in {{SMM}} and its sequel where they can be placed in the ''Super Mario World'' underwater level style, replacing the Spike Traps. Their names are only shown in {{SMM2}}.
