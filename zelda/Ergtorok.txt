{{Term/Store|Ergtorok|Ergtoroks|Series, ST|plural}} are {{Plural|ST|Enemy|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 176}}</ref> They are a form of {{Term|ST|Octive|link}} that live in the {{Term|ST|Sand Temple|link}}.

==Characteristics==
Ergtoroks attack in the same manner as [[Octive]]s but are more intelligent when doing so. When Link attempts to reflect their attacks back at them, the strategy used to great success with their water-bore relatives, they retreat under the sand before the projectile makes contact with their body. To defeat them, [[Link]] must use the [[Sand Wand]] in their appropriate area once they begin to startle the [[Sand]] above them. The Sand Wand will create hard sand blocks which trap the Ergtoroks, making them dizzy and vulnerable to attack. In order to attack them, however, Link himself must get on top of the sand blocks created by the Sand Wand to reach the Ergtoroks.

==Nomenclature==
The name "Ergtorok" is possibly a portmanteau of ''{{Wp|Erg (landform)|erg}}'' and ''[[Octorok]]''.
