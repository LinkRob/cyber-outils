'''Lesser Hyrule''' is a location in {{TLoZ}}. It is the damaged version of [[Hyrule]] after the attack by [[Ganon]], with [[Monsters]] now infesting the land. Travelers and [[merchant]]s are forced to live in caves hidden around the [[overworld]], which [[Link]] can find to buy items or play games to make [[Rupee]]s. Ganon's Lair is located near [[Death Mountain]], while the [[Lost Woods]] is to the east.

Also, [[Hyrule Castle]] is nowhere to be seen or plotted on the map. Link can find different [[Old Man|Old Men]] who offer information and sometimes even [[Sword]]s for him to use on his journey. The connection with the [[Zora]] has also been severed, as they are now aggressive and called [[Zora (Enemy)|River Zora]].

== Second Quest Changes ==
[[Second Quest]] introduces many changes to the world of Lesser Hyrule. The secret [[Red Moblin]] cave near the [[Old Woman]]'s hint at the southwest corner of the map has been removed. A money making cave can be uncovered with the [[Flute]] just south of the [[Graveyard]]. Another secret Red Moblin cave is removed slightly east of the looping forest of maze which previously gave 100 Rupees. Link can use the Flute and open up a secret cave that forces him to pay the old man for the door when entering, located a little east from the south west corner. The 100 Rupee secret was moved to a tree that required burning at a place a screen south from the [[Fairy]] pond. [[Level 3]] has been moved elsewhere and a shop replaces it.

The secret Red Moblin cave found just north from Link's starting location is not there anymore. It was instead moved 1 screen east and 1 screen north to a tree uncovered by playing the Flute. The [[Life Heart]] hidden 4 screens east from the starting location has been moved elsewhere, and the secret Red Moblin cave that paid 100 Rupees a screen above was removed. A screen east from that lies the hidden entrance to [[Level 7]], opened by burning the tree. The [[Level 8]] entrance a screen east from there has been moved elsewhere, and another screen east reveals a secret Red Moblin cave after playing the Flute. Going north, the [[Level 2]] temple entryway was changed to a Level 3 pond flushed out with the Flute. Going 3 screens east and 4 screens north from the starting location resides a hidden Life Heart opened with the Flute in the desert.

In both quests, the [[Level 1]] location is the same, but another Life Heart that was previously just south of it was moved. The [[Level 4]] dungeon's previous entrance was substituted for the [[Level 5]] dungeon, and the Level 7 entrance located in a pond was moved to an aforementioned region. In the Graveyard, the [[Magical Sword]] has been moved to a different place on the map, Another Life Heart can be uncovered in the top left corner of the cemetery, and a screen below that is the [[Level 6]] dungeon in another grave. The previous location for the Level 6 dungeon is now changed to a Red Moblin cave. There lies a shop that sells the [[Blue Ring]] adjacent to the pond east of the Graveyard, but it has been changed to the new Level 2 entryway. For the [[Power Bracelet]], it stays in the same spot in both quests, but the potion shop slightly north from the Level 1 entrance is moved 2 screens east to the desert uncovered by the Flute. Another 2 screens east in the desert is a Red Moblin cave opened up the same way. The Life Heart just east from there was moved, but the two Life Hearts (one requiring the [[Stepladder]] and the other needing the [[Raft]]) at the far east side of the map are unchanged.

At the northeast corner of the map, the secret Red Moblin temple has been changed to the shop that sells the Blue Ring. The location of the [[Letter]] required to buy potions was also changed to another shop. On the looping mountain screen (slightly east from the waterfall) lies the new Level 4 location found by pushing one of the boulders. The Level 5 temple was removed and is now a pond with a Fairy. Location for the [[White Sword]] is the same, but the Magical Sword is now a screen west of it (resembling a giant arrow), uncovered by pushing a boulder. Furthermore, getting to the Magical Sword requires a completely different path from the White Sword despite the two being close in proximity. 1 screen west of the waterfall is the new Level 8 entrance, requiring a Stepladder and [[Bomb]]. Another screen west is a hidden potion shop needing the same equipment. 3 screens west from the Magical Sword is a Life Heart uncovered by the Flute, and another screen west used to house the entrance to the final dungeon in the game, [[Level 9]], but it has been moved elsewhere; a screen south from there is a new hidden shop. The final top left corner of the map resides a few more changes from quest 1. East from the corner is another hidden potion shop. The Letter can also be retrieved after pushing the rock just southeast from the corner. The last change is in the very northwest screen of the overworld, where Level 9 can be uncovered with a Bomb.
