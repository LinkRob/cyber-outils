The {{Term/Store|Brother Goriyas|Brother Goriyas|Series, OoS|plural}} are the {{Plural|OoS|Sub-Boss}} of the {{Term|OoS|Gnarled Root Dungeon|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The {{Plural|OoS|Brother Goriyas}} attack {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} by circling him and tossing a {{Term|OoS|Boomerang|link}} back and forth between one another. While they move quickly, they briefly stand still when trading the {{Term|OoS|Boomerang}}. The {{Plural|OoS|Brother Goriyas}} can be defeated by simply striking them enough times with the {{Term|OoS|Sword|link}}. When one is defeated, both will disappear; therefore, the most effective strategy is to attack a single {{Term|OoS|Brother Goriyas|display=Goriya}} repeatedly. Defeating the {{Plural|OoS|Brother Goriyas}} grants {{Term|OoS|Link}} access to the room containing the {{Term|OoS|Seed Satchel|link}} already equipped with {{Plural|OoS|Ember Seed|link}}.

==Trivia==
*In issue 144 of [[Nintendo Power]], the {{Plural|OoS|Brother Goriyas}} are referred to as {{Plural|OoS|Moblin|link}}.{{Fact|OoS}}
*The Brother Goriyas are based on the Goriya Bros from [[The Legend of Zelda (Mishouzaki)|The Legend of Zelda manga by Mishouzaki]], the Bros being created 12 years before ''Oracle of Seasons''.
