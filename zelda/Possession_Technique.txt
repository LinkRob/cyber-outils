{{Term/Store|Possession Technique|Possession Techniques|Series, TMC|plural}} are abilities used exclusively by the [[Blade Brothers]] in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the {{Color|TMC Blue|Swiftblade Possession technique}} of training!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}} use {{Plural|TMC|Possession Technique}} to teach {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} various abilities by taking possession of his body and showing him how the move is performed.<ref>{{Cite|But one must FEEL the technique, not just hear about it! That's why I will now possess your body so as to demonstrate the technique!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref> After this, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must repeat the action he was just shown in order to earn the {{Term|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}} for the ability. Five of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers}} all use a variation of the {{Term|TMC|Possession Technique}}, though they each claim they came up with it and that theirs is better.
