{{Term/Store|Vasu Jewelers||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book= E|page= 110 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Vasu Jewelers|Map|OoA}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Vasu Jewelers}} is referred to as the '''Ring Shop''' in {{Guide|OoS|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 60 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Stub|OoS}}
{{Term|OoS|Vasu Jewelers}} is found within {{Term|OoS|Horon Village|link}}. The roof of the building will change color depending on the current {{Term|OoS|Season|link}}. It is light green during {{Term|OoS|Spring|link|display=Spring}}, dark green during {{Term|OoS|Season|link|display=Summer}}, orange during {{Term|OoS|Season|link|display=Autumn}}, and blue during {{Term|OoS|Season|link|display=Winter}}.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Stub|OoA}}
{{Term|OoA|Vasu Jewelers}} is found within {{Term|OoA|Lynna City|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The ring sign in front of the shop changes color between games, being red in {{OoS}} and blue in {{OoA}}. This is consistent with the games' respective logo colors.
