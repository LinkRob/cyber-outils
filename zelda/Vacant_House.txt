{{Term/Store|Vacant House|Vacant Houses|Series, TMC, ALBW|plural}} are recurring locations in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Vacant House|Map|ALBW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{TMC|-}}===
After completing the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} a red {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)|link}}, one of the three {{Plural|TMC|Oracle|link}} at the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}, who is looking for a house to move into.<ref>{{Cite|We need to find a nice, quiet place where we can settle down.|Din (Oracle)|TMC}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|TMC|Gorman|link}} will be renting a home in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, at which point {{Term|TMC|Link}} can talk to one of the three {{Plural|TMC|Oracle}} so that she may move in to the new house.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= When you first fuse Kinstones with Farore inside the inn, Gorman will appear in town with the remarkable idea to rent out some of the empty houses. Speak to him, then return to the women in the inn to inform one of them of the vacancy|game= TMC|guide= Nintendo|page= 102}}</ref> By speaking to the {{Term|TMC|Oracle}} in the house, {{Term|TMC|Link}} will be able to obtain one of the {{Plural|TMC|Charm (The Minish Cap)|link}}.

To make the next house available, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must then fuse a red {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with {{Term|TMC|Bremor|link}}, the carpenter by the {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Mutoh|link}} then becomes motivated to build a second house for {{Term|TMC|Gorman}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= After you fuse Kinstones with Bremor, Mutoh will assist Gorman in building another house.|game= TMC|guide= Nintendo|page= 102}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} can talk to one of the two remaining {{Plural|TMC|Oracle}} to obtain a second {{Term|TMC|Charm (The Minish Cap)}} once the house is completed.

A third house never becomes available for the remaining {{Term|TMC|Oracle}}. Thus, its charm [[Permanently Missable Items|cannot be obtained]]. In the European version of the game, {{Term|TMC|Gorman}} considers building a third house south of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure I could build a new place here if I could get rid of these cats... Moohahaha!|Gorman|TMC}}</ref> but he never does. This comment was replaced in the American version, released two months later.<ref>{{Cite|I've lined up a sizable pile of cash from my rental properties... Maybe I should start breeding pets next. Moohahaha!|Gorman|TMC}}</ref>

==={{ALBW|-}}===
The {{Term|ALBW|Vacant House}} is the {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} counterpart to {{Term|ALBW|Link's House|link}}. A {{Term|ALBW|Bottle|link}} can be found inside it after using a {{Term|ALBW|Bomb|link}} on the back wall. A {{Term|ALBW|Weather Vane|link}} can be found outside. A {{Term|ALBW|Fissure|link}} can also be found on the front.

In {{Term|ALBW|Hero Mode|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Ravio|link}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Diary|link}} can be found inside, suggesting that the {{Term|ALBW|Vacant House}} belongs to him.
