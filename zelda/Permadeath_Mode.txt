{{Term/Store|Permadeath Mode||Series, CoH}} is a toggleable option when creating a new save file in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Permadeath Mode|Select Mode|CoH}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|CoH|Permadeath Mode}} is a game mode that makes it so that when the player experiences a {{Term|CoH|Game Over|link}}, they will have to restart the game from the beginning or start a completely new save file. Toggling it also increases the game's difficulty, with stronger enemies appearing throughout {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}} and dungeons now sporting two minibosses within their respective lobbies.

The four elemental bosses appear as their stronger red-and-blue counterparts regardless of the order of dungeons completed, and also gain the addition of {{Plural|CoH|Sarcophagus|link}} to their battlefields, which spawn undead enemies to attack the player during battle. The only bosses to not have {{Plural|CoH|Sarcophagus|link}} in their boss fights are {{Term|CoH|Bass Guitarmos Knights|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}}.

Toggling it also adds further randomization to a save file by randomizing the placement of key items, including those given out by sleeping characters, which can be found either by opening {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}}, by mini-bosses, or have them given out by a different character entirely. The only exceptions to this rule are the {{Term|CoH|Goron Locket|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Snorkel|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Tingle's Dowser|link}}, {{Plural|CoH|Bottle|link}}.

Additionally, the pricing of items are also doubled at all shops, including potions sold by the {{Plural|CoH|Fate|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}} upgrades sold by the {{Plural|CoH|Great Fairy|link}}. Wearing the {{Term|CoH|Ring of Charisma|link}} inside a shop temporarily returns the prices to their original state, but only as long as the ring is equipped.
