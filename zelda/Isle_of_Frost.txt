The {{Term/Store|Isle of Frost||Series, PH}} is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 113 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref> It is an island found in the southeastern quadrant of the ocean.

==Features==
When [[Link]] first arrives, the isle is barricaded by massive, icy walls that he must use his [[cannon]] to destroy before he can dock.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! What's the deal with all this ice? We can't dock, Link! But I'm having one of my brilliant ideas! No, really! Do you see it already? Chart a course around the icy isle! We'll obliterate it all with the cannon!|Linebeck|PH}}</ref> The Island is home to two races: the [[Anouki]] and the [[Yook]]. Despite bad blood between them,<ref>{{Cite|We Anouki and the Yook have been rivals for I don't know how long. I gotta be honest... I don't even know how it all started. But maybe that's how these tribal rivalries go, you know?|Anouki|PH}}</ref> both were at peace with each other for 100 years,<ref>{{Cite|Nobody thought the trouble between the Anouki and Yook was a good thing. So we Anouki and those hairy Yook got together 100 years ago... and entered into a little "peace agreement."|Anouki|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's a peace treaty, you know... Peace... Peace is like............ I think it's like a piece of cake.|Anouki|PH}}</ref> living on opposite sides of the island,<ref>{{Cite|We Anouki have got one enemy on this island. They're the Yook. There was a lot of bad blood between our ancestors. So 100 years ago, we made an agreement. The Anouki live in the west... And those hairy Yook live over in the east on the Great Ice Field.|Island Chief|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Was it fair? Yeah, it was fair. Yeah...|Anouki|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Speakin' metaphorically, we got our moist, frosted hunk of cake to live on. And we generously gave the Yook the dry, unfrosted morsel of cake to the east.|Anouki|PH}}</ref> until a Yook sneaked into the Anouki village, kidnapped a [[Aroo|resident]] of the [[Anouki Estates]] and disguised himself as as the victim.<ref>{{Cite|You, guy! Did you hear the big news? One of those hairy Yook has sneaked over here!|Anouki|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That was the deal, but now some tricky Yook has snuck over here. And he's taken one of my villagers! Now he's pretending to be him and living with us! ...All comfortable-like!|Island Chief|PH}}</ref> Link must resolve this conflict through a series of interrogations,<ref>{{Cite|Find the Yook who's impersonating a villager, and kick him OUT!|Island Chief|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...Hey! How'd you figure it out? And I thought I had an amazing costume.|Yook|PH}}</ref> and then get the [[Azurine]] from the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That Azurine you're looking for should be in the Temple of Ice.|Island Chief|PH}}</ref>

After he clears the dungeon, it's revealed that the Yook were being mind-controlled by an evil force, and that they are actually quite friendly.<ref>{{Cite|OoooooOof... My head hurts. It's like my brain was being controlled by some evil force.. Now that I'm back to normal, I feel pretty guilty. This is sort of awkward... but, uh...sorry if I tried to eat you.|Yook|PH}}</ref>
