{{Term/Store|Elastic Mushroom|Elastic Mushrooms|Series, TMC|plural}} are [[object]]s in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 21}}</ref> They appear in parts of the [[overworld]] and [[dungeon]]s throughout [[Hyrule]], including [[Deepwood Shrine]], the [[Palace of Winds]], and the caves of [[Mount Crenel]] and [[Veil Falls]]. [[Link]] can grab an Elastic Mushroom from any of the four sides and then pull the Elastic Mushroom, stretching it back. When Link stops moving, if he is still holding onto the Elastic Mushroom, it contracts, and he is sent flying forward. He can also use the [[Gust Jar]] to draw an Elastic Mushroom toward him across gaps.
