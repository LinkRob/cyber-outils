The {{Term/Store|Command||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} menu is an interface in {{HW}}.<ref name="Tutorial">{{Cite|You can {{Color|HWDE Green|command}} playable warriors to march to given destinations, even when you aren't directly controlling them. Open the Info screen and choose {{Color|HWDE Green|Command}}, then choose a warrior. Confirm their destination to set them on their way.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|Command}} menu allows the player to move all playable {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} without directly controlling them.<ref name="Tutorial"/> On the left side of the {{Term|HWDE|Command}} menu, a list of all playable {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} is displayed. Each {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} in the list points a colored line to the right side of the {{Term|HWDE|Command}} menu, where a map can be found. The arrows point to where each playable {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} is located on the {{Term|HWDE|Stage|link}}. Selecting a {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} will bring the cursor to the map, where the player can move on a grid system to choose where to send the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}}. If a location has any points of interest, for example, a {{Term|HWDE|Hylian Captain|link}}, an option to send the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} to the {{Term|HWDE|Hylian Captain}} or only to move the selected location will appear.<ref>{{Cite|To this location.|Command|HWDE}}</ref> Points of interest able to be selected include {{Plural|HWDE|Keep|link}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy|link}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} and allied {{Plural|HWDE|Captain (Hyrule Warriors)|link}}. Below the list of {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}}, the victory conditions, defeat conditions, and mission are displayed in a list which can be cycled through using L and R.<ref>{{Cite|L Victory Conditions R|Command|HWDE}}</ref> Above the map also displays the amount of time spent in battle, and if playing in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}} or {{Term|HWDE|Challenge Mode|link}}, will also display the amount of damage taken.<ref>{{Cite|Elapsed Time|Command|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Damage Taken|Command|HWDE}}</ref>
