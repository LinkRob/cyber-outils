==Overview==
In "{{Term|HWAoC|Dyeing to Make Contact}}", the {{Term|HWAoC|Kochi Dye Shop}} is unable to get hold of a dye merchant. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} find someone that will help, and after paying them a {{Term|HWAoC|Ruby}}, that person is revealed to be the dye merchant himself. As a result, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} gain access to the dye shop.
