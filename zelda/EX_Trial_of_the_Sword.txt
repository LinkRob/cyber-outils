"{{Term/Store|EX Trial of the Sword||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Trial of the Sword}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Korok Forest}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TMT}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
To activate this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must have already pulled the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}} from its {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|link|display=Pedestal}}. The overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} must also be reloaded, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. Once this is done, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will hear the voice of a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} call out to him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Chosen one... The hero called Link...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If the DLC Pack is installed after drawing the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will hear the voice as soon as the game is opened.}} The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} tells him that the time has come to test his courage,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|The time has come. You are now ready to test the limits of your courage.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> and he is instructed to return to the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|At the place where you reunited with the Master Sword, the sacred Korok Forest...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Once he is there, he should listen to the words of {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree|link}}, at which point he will receive a trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Go there and heed the Great Deku Tree's words. Do so, and a glorious trail will be bestowed upon you.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

Upon approaching the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} will speak to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and remark that the time has come.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? What's this? A voice beckoned you here? I see. So the time has come.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> In sharing the details of the upcoming trial, he reveals that the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} has not yet realized its true power.<ref>{{Cite|I shall now tell you all I know about the arduous trial that awaits you. That sword you have reunited with... the Master Sword...it has yet to realize its true splendor.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> Through the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can prove his worthiness of such power, though it will be a challenge unlike any he has faced before.<ref>{{Cite|This trial shall test your courage to ensure you are worthy of this splendor. It is called the Trial of the Sword. It will challenge you as you have never been challenged before.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} begins the trial, all of his {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Armor|link}} will be taken away, and he will be forced to fight [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|enemies]] without them.<ref>{{Cite|All of your weapons and armor will be sealed away. You must face your enemies without the tools you've come to rely on.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> After proving himself, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to call the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} his own.<ref>{{Cite|Oncr you have tempered your soul and proven your wisdom and courage by completing the Trial of the Sword... the Master Sword shall truly be yours!|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref>

Based on {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s appearance, {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} believes he is ready to take on the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|...Hm. Unless my eyes deceive me, you seem quite willing to take on this trial. A hero through and through!|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> He instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to place the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} back into its {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|display=Pedestal}} to begin the trial.<ref>{{Cite|Now, Link... Place the Master Sword in its pedestal.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}} is divided into three levels: the Beginning Trials, the Middle Trials, and the Final Trials. All three are separate from each other. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters one of the sets, he is greeted by a {{Term|BotW|Monk}} who presents him with the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the blade's chosen hero... In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer the Trial of the Sword.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> During the Beginning Trials, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} tells him he is not powerful enough to wield the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}'s true splendor.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You presently lack the power necessary to wield the true splendor of the Master Sword...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> To gain it, he must eliminate all obstacles in this trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You must fortify your mind, body, and soul by eradicating all obstacles that appear in this realm.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Anything he obtains within this realm will be lost once the trial is compelte.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In this illusory realm of sacred mystery, anything can happen... All that you obtain here will be lost upon your return to the reality you know.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Finally, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} encourages him to overcome the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Chosen hero of the Master Sword... Overcome the Trial of the Sword and claim the blade's true splendor!}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

Once the Beginning Trials are complete, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is transported to a room with several {{Plural|BotW|Monk}}. They commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his efforts,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the one chosen by the sacred blade... I commend your efforts in this trial.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> allowing him to take back the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} and wield more of its strength.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You are now strong enough to wield more of the Master Sword's power than ever before. Courageous hero... chosen by the blade... Take hold of the Master Sword.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, he is returned to the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}. After the Beginning and Middle Trials, {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} takes note of both {{Term|BotW|Link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, saying they look stronger than before.<ref>{{Cite|Ah. You have finally returned. You look stronger than when I last saw you... The Master Sword you wield is now more powerful than before.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to place the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} back in its {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|display=Pedestal}} if he wishes for more power.<ref>{{Cite|If you desire still more power and wish to retry to blade's test... once again place the Master Sword into its pedestal.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref>

At the start of the Middle Trials, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} greets {{Term|BotW|Link}} the same, but they tell him that he still needs more power to wield the true splendor of the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You must acquire still more power to wield the true splendor of the Master Sword...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> All other dialogue, including that of the {{Plural|BotW|Monk}} at the end and {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}}, remains the same.

The Final Trials also start the same, but this time the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} he has almost proven his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You are nearly worthy of wielding the true splendor of the Master Sword...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> At the end of the trials, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to approach all seven {{Plural|BotW|Monk}}, who tell him that he has done well.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well... The depth of your heroism is beyond question.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> They share that they were the ones who prepared the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}} after receiving a vision from {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|We are the ones who prepared this trial, following a revelation from the Goddess Hylia.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> By overcoming their trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} has proven himself worthy of the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the one who has overcome the Trial of the Sword...you have proven your hand worthy of the legendary Master Sword.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Now that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has the power to defeat {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, their duty has been fulfilled.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that the hero has gained the power necessary to combat the Calamity, our sacred duty has been filfilled.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> As they fade away, the {{Plural|BotW|Monk}} tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} to protect {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}} with the power of the awakened {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Chosen hero... with the awakened Master Sword, now boasting its true splendor... protect the kingdom of Hyrule... for now and for always...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

After drawing the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is once again transported in front of {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} holds out the sword, it [[Fi|resonates]] with him. {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} notices his return and recognizes that he is now capable of wielding the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}'s full potential.<ref>{{Cite|Ah. So you have finally returned. Your mind, body, and soul are now ready to wield the Master Sword at its full potential. You never fail to impress!|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he is now one with the sword and that he will continue to watch over his journey.<ref>{{Cite|Now you are truly one with that sword of yours. I must say, its sacred glow suits you well. As ever, I shall watch over your journey from here...|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> He believes that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} would be pleased with {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s accomplishments.<ref>{{Cite|The princess... I am certain Hyrule's princess is pleased with your accomplishment as well...|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed.
