The {{Term/Store|Mozo Shenno Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Mozo Shenno Shrine}}<br/>A Major Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Major Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The Shrine is located below the {{Term|BotW|Biron Snowshelf|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. It's accessed as part of the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|Series|The Bird in the Mountains|link}}".

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can start the Quest by talking to {{Term|BotW|Molli|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, where she'll ask him if he wants to hear a story.<ref>{{Cite|Want me to tell you a story?|Molli|BotW}}</ref> She tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} a story about her grandfather, who stopped at a big tree while climbing below him and looked northwest.<ref>{{Cite|Once upon a time... My grandpa stopped at a {{Color|BotW Red|big tree}} while he was climbing a big mountain and looked below him to the northwest.|Molli|BotW}}</ref> Suddenly, he saw a huge, snow-white bird with its wings spread.<ref>{{Cite|When suddenly he saw {{Color|BotW Red|a HUGE snow-white birdie}} with its wings spread wide!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> He flew after the bird, noticing something important in its stomach as he approached.<ref>{{Cite| My grandpa took off {{Color|BotW Red|flying after the snow-white birdie}}. And as he got a little closer to the white birdie, he saw something SUPER important inside its belly!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> Molli starts to share what it was,<ref>{{Cite|The birdie had swallowed—!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> but since she gets sleepy around this part of the story, she doesn't remember.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|...I always get sleepy around that part of the story...so I don't remember...}}|Molli|BotW}}</ref>

However, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} does know where the big tree is.<ref>{{Cite|OH! I know where the {{Color|BotW Red|big tree}} is, though!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> She points {{Term|BotW|Link}} towards a cedar tree atop {{Term|BotW|Talonto Peak|link}}, claiming it's the tree from her grandfather's story.<ref>{{Cite|It's over there! You see it? Grandpa said he saw the {{Color|BotW Red|snow-white birdie}} from THAT tree!|Molli|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} glides northwest with the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} from the same cedar tree, he can see the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} hidden in a small cave under the Biron Snowshelf, which resembles a white bird with its wings spread. Activating the Shrine will complete the Quest. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the Shrine, he is greeted by the Shrine's {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mozo Shenno|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Mozo Shenno. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Mozo Shenno|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Mozo Shenno Shrine|link}} features a large central chamber which acts as an arena. As Link approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}}, wielding an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe++|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword++|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear++|link}}, will rise from a pit to challenge Link. There are metal blocks embedded into the floor of the chamber, which can be lifted with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} to protect {{Term|BotW|Link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV}} to cause a portcullis separating him from the Monk to rise.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has done so, {{Term|BotW|Mozo Shenno}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Mozo Shenno|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Mozo Shenno|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the portcullis contains a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.
