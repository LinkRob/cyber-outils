==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Making a Splash}}" is an event wherein a greater number of aquatic {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Aquatic spirits are spilling onto the Spirit Board! Defeat them in battle to earn more gold than usual.<br/><br/>Legend-class spirit Kaptain K. Rool, along with Ace-class spirits Volcanion, Nia, and Esna, will also appear at set times. Water you waiting for? |author= Nintendo |published= June 5, 2018 |retrieved= June 5, 2018 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/f0109b5e/index.html |title= Event: Making a Splash - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
