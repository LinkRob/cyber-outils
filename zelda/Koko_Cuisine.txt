"{{Term/Store|Koko Cuisine||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Koko Cuisine}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Kakariko Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} help with {{Term|BotW|Koko|link|display=Koko's}} last {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|Cooking with Koko|link}}" opens up this next problem. Once he speaks to her at the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} decides to make her father {{Term|BotW|Dorian|link|display=Dorian's}} favorite dish, {{Term|BotW|Tough Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What should Koko make now... {{Big|OH!}} Koko will make Father's favorite! {{Color|BotW Blue|Tough meat-stuffed pumpkin}}!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> However, when she goes over her ingredients, she realizes she is missing {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}}. <ref>{{Cite|Let's see... The ingredients are... {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkin}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|raw meat}}. Hrm? ...{{Color|BotW Blue|Raw meat}}? {{Big|OH, NO!!}} Koko is out of {{Color|BotW Blue|raw meat}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Though using a different type of meat is an option, her mother's recipe specifically calls for {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat}}.<ref>{{Cite|Koko could use a different kind of meat, but...without {{Color|BotW Blue|raw meat}}, Koko can't re-create Mother's recipe...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} has been working so much lately, he has not been able to hunt for {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat}} for his family.<ref>{{Cite|Father used to go hunting to make sure we always had {{Color|BotW Blue|raw meat}}. But he's at work right now...|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Koko}} becomes sad, saying she is a bad daughter who can not do anything right and can never be like her mother.<ref>{{Cite|Koko is a bad daughter who can't do anything right. Koko will never be like Mother...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Should Link comfort her instead of offering assistance, Koko will sink further into a self-imposed depression.<ref>{{Cite|[Cheer up!] Without {{Color|BotW Blue|raw meat}}... Koko feels so empty...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers her {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat}} without any his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} will be able to tell he is lying.<ref>{{Cite|[Voilà! Raw meat!] Koko knows when someone is lying. Father isn't good at lying either...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> When she finishes talking to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} begins.

{{Term|BotW|Raw Meat}} is dropped by three different types animals, those being {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mountain Goat|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Grassland Fox|link}}. There is also a merchant who travels near the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mezer|link}}, who sells {{Plural|BotW|Raw Meat}}.

When spoken to again, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} is busy beating herself up for forgetting another important ingredient.<ref>{{Cite|ARGH! Koko wants to cook {{Color|BotW Blue|tough meat-stuffed pumpkin}} but forgot the {{Color|BotW Blue|raw meat}}?!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers her {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat}}, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} makes sure he is alright with her taking it before starting to cook before asking him to wait.<ref>{{Cite|Is this...{{Big|raw meat?!}} Are you sure about this? Really? Thank you so much! Koko means it! Stay right there for a second!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Koko}} is pleased with the result and offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} some of the meal as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Mmm! What a scrumptious smell! Oh, and that savory meat pairs SO well with the sweetness of the pumpkin! This is just how Mother made it! Here, have some! It's so good!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Koko}} declares that she will continue to improve, ending the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|Koko will work hard to get better and better at cooking!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>
