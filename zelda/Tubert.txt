==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>That is {{Color|SS Blue|Mr. Tubert}}. He is a crafty entrepreneur of the subterranean Mogma race. He runs the Thrill Digger game.</p><p>A unique Mogma, he believes there is more money to be made in serving treasure hunters rather than being a treasure hunter himself.</p>

Tubert is an enormous, bald [[Mogma]]. He is a cunning entrepreneur who runs the [[Thrill Digger]] mini-game at the [[Eldin Volcano]], where customers can dig for [[Rupee]]s at the cost of paying Rupees. He believes that serving treasure hunters is more profitable than hunting for treasure himself.<ref>{{Cite|A unique Mogma, he believes there is more money to be made in serving treasure hunters rather than being a treasure hunter himself.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

Tubert is found inside a cave near a [[Bokoblin]] village, however, the entrance to the cave is blocked by a large piece of rubble that can only be destroyed with a [[Bomb]]. Once inside, Tubert pops out of the ground and explains the Thrill Digger game rules to Link. He also tells Link that the [[Rupee Ore]] found along the walls can be shot with a projectile to yield Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|You see the shining stone sticking out of the wall? That's pure {{Color|SS Red|Rupee Ore}}! It's also why there are so many Rupees to dig up here. If you sling something at it, it'll {{Color|SS Red|pop}} right out. Go on and give it a try!|Tubert|SS}}</ref>

Tubert claims to want his customers to admire his "best features". Because of this, he stands on his tail so people can admire his paws as well as his mustache.<ref>{{Cite|I don't need to tell you that my paws are my best feature. I like to give all my customers a chance to admire them. That's why I stand on my tail--it does wonders for sales!|Tubert|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And I'm plenty proud of my fine {{Color|SS Red|mustache}} too! Cute and curly, all the way. I groom it every day! Wait. That rhymed, didn't it?|Tubert|SS}}</ref> He also prides himself in his glasses, claiming they make him look fashionable.<ref>{{Cite|You mean my glasses? Looking dapper, eh? The round frames make me look even more fashionable!|Tubert|SS}}</ref>
