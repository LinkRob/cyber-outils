The {{Term/Store|Great Moblin||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring [[Sub-Boss]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 183 {{Exp Game|OoS, OoA}}}}</ref> He is depicted as a large [[Moblin]] that leads a troop of smaller ones.

==Biography==
{{Manual|OoS|Great Moblin|The Great Moblin lives in his proud keep and tries to swindle a toll from all who pass by.}}
{{Manual|OoA|Great Moblin|The Great Moblin has begun to harass the Gorons with the hope of getting a monopoly on their prized Bomb Flowers.}}
The Great Moblin is the boss of a gang of Moblins who terrorize the populace of [[Holodrum]] and [[Labrynna]]. In whichever game is played first, [[Link]] drives him out of that land. If the two games are linked, he goes into the other land, where Link can further his plot. If the games are not linked, he will not recognize Link in that second land.

In Labrynna, he builds a [[Moblin's Keep|keep]] in the mountain, and prevents the [[Goron]]s from growing [[Bomb Flower]]s so that he can sell his hand-made [[Bomb]]s. 

In Holodrum, he builds another keep, as more of a fortress with cannons in the walls, shelling anyone who comes near.<ref>{{Cite|You! You, boy! This is the Great Moblin's realm! If you wish to get by alive, pay my toll! Hah, heh!|Great Moblin|OoS}}</ref>

If Link is in Holodrum while playing a [[Linked Game]], the Great Moblin kidnaps [[Princess Zelda]], hoping to make a profit.<ref>{{Cite|Such fine clothes... You must be a high-class girl! Rupees, jewels, everything! Give me your valuables!|Great Moblin|OoS}}</ref> After a brief skirmish he flees back to the Moblin's Keep. Later, Link can find his minions surrounding [[Dimitri]] in [[Spool Swamp]], intending to sell him.<ref>{{Cite|Bwee! I found somethin' weird! If we catch it and sell it, we'll be rich!!! And the Great Moblin will be very pleased!!!|Moblin|OoS}}</ref> To rescue Dimitri, Link must pay 80 [[Rupee]]s.

After Link destroys the Moblin's Keep in Holodrum, the Great Moblin will move into an abandoned house in the south-eastern corner of [[Sunken City]]. There, he and his minions make Bombs, restoring their funds to rebuild the Keep once more. If Link drops a Bomb or an [[Ember Seed]] on their pile of Bombs and escapes, the house explodes, making the Great Moblin and his henchmen flee. They soon return and rebuild the house, and continue making Bombs once more. Link can blow up this second house too, making them flee again. If Link tries to ignite their pile of Bombs a third time, the Great Moblin will discover his actions.<ref>{{Cite|Bwee-hee! So it was you! You'll pay!!! An eye for an eye!|Great Moblin|OoS}}</ref> They will then throw Link into the house and proceed to run away as it explodes, resulting in a [[Game Over]].
