==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Stub|OoT3D}}
In {{OoT}}, {{Plural|OoT3D|Target}} appear in the {{Term|OoT3D|Horseback Archery Range|link}} in the {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo Valley|link}}. They are stationary targets that can be shot with the {{Term|OoT3D|Fairy Bow|link}} to gain points.

{{Plural|OoT3D|Target}} also appear as objects that {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} can latch onto with the {{Term|OoT3D|Hookshot|link}}.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, {{Plural|PH|Target}} appear in the {{Term|PH|Cannon Minigame|link}} as the source of points. Two types of {{Plural|PH|Target}} appear: red {{Plural|PH|Target}} can be shot multiple times for 20 points each; blue {{Plural|PH|Target}} can be shot once before breaking and yield 100 points each.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Target}} are used during the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range (Mini-Game)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}, which is located at the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|link}}. During the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}} will task {{Term|BotW|Link}} with destroying five {{Plural|BotW|Target}} within three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|Now let's see what you're capable of. You'll have to maneuver through the air and destroy {{Color|BotW Red|five of the targets}} set up in this valley {{Color|BotW Red|in three minutes}}.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> Once he does so, {{Term|BotW|Teba}} will allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to accompany him to face {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if you really want to help me bring down {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}, it's now or never.|Teba|BotW}}</ref>

After the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to speak to {{Term|BotW|Tulin|link}} to activate the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}}. This time around, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to destroy as many {{Plural|BotW|Target}} as he can in 30 seconds.<ref name="Tulin"/> {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} promises a prize for {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he completes this task.<ref>{{Cite|When you break a bunch, I'll give you a prize!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref>
