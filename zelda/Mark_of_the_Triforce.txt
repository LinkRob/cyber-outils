You see, the {{Color|SS Gold|mark of the Triforce}} on your hand is a symbol of the greatest power in this world.|Princess Zelda|SS}}</ref> also known as the '''Crest of Courage''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 68}}</ref> is a recurring {{Term|Series|Symbol|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|TAoL}}

==Characteristics==
[[File:OoT3D Himekawa Promotional Manga 14.png|left|thumb|264x264px|The three main characters with their respective Marks of the Triforce in a ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'' promotion manga]]
The Mark of the Triforce is a sign of a character possessing the complete [[Triforce]] or at least a fragment of it. The crest normally appears whenever they use its divine power or standing in close proximity to another bearer, in which the mark itself begins to resonate and glow brightly on one's hand. Usually but not always, it's seen on [[Link]], [[Princess Zelda]], and [[Ganondorf]]'s hands throughout the series, having the [[Triforce of Courage]], [[Triforce of Wisdom]], and [[Triforce of Power]], respectively.

== Mainline appearances ==

==={{TAoL|-}}===
In ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'', the Mark of the Triforce appears on [[Link]]'s hand on his sixteenth birthday, identifying him as the one who must place six {{Plural|TAoL|Crystal|link}} in the {{Plural|TAoL|Palace|link}} around [[Hyrule]], dispelling the barrier placed on the [[Great Palace]] by the then-soon-to-die [[King of Hyrule (The Adventure of Link)|ancient King of Hyrule]] to protect the [[Triforce of Courage]] from those who would misuse its powers, such as [[Prince of Hyrule|his own son]].

When Link shows it to {{Term|TAoL|Impa|link}}, she presses his hand against a door in [[North Castle]] that has never been opened in many years, revealing the slumbering {{Term|TAoL|Princess Zelda|link}}. Impa then explains the Tragedy of Zelda I to Link, while giving him the crystals and a scroll that reveals to the hero his new objective.

=== ''Ocarina of Time'' ===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', the Mark of the Triforce appears in Link's, Princess Zelda's, and [[Ganondorf]]'s respective hands when the [[Hero of Time]] reaches the top of [[Ganon's Castle]], due to the Triforce of Courage, [[Triforce of Wisdom|Wisdom]], and [[Triforce of Power|Power]] [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_HandTriforce.wav resonating]. It appears again on Ganondorf's hand when he transforms into his true form [[Ganon]] during the final battle and in the post-credits scene on Link when he meets Zelda after being sent to his childhood.

==={{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, Link possesses the Mark of the Triforce on his hand, but it is unknown if he had it prior to his first summons by the Triforce at the beginning of the game played first, or if he was branded with it by the relic when sent on his journey.

After saving the second Oracle, Link's mark glows, fully restoring his vitality and, together with both [[Din (Oracle)|Din]] and [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]], Link appears at the [[Room of Rites]] to stop {{Term|OoS|Twinrova|link}}'s plan to resurrect Ganon.

===={{OoS|-}}====
{{Stub|OoS}}
[[Din (Oracle)|Din]] notices Link's mark and comments that the bearer is destined to meet a special fate. After which, she nearly tells Link something, but thinks better of it and leads him to another dance before [[Onox]] interrupts and attacks.

In the non-linked ending, some monsters close in on an unsuspecting Link and Din, but flee when Link's mark glows.

===={{OoA|-}}====
{{Stub|OoA}}
When [[Veran]] attempted to infiltrate the {{Term|OoS|Forest of Time|link}} in [[Labrynna]], she was obstructed by a barrier, one she couldn't slip past even when possessing {{Term|OoA|Impa|link}}, who was granted access in advance. Only Link is able to push the stone aside, unknowingly allowing Veran to get to Nayru. Veran had noticed that the triangle on the stone was a sign, and deduced that Link, bearing the Mark of the Triforce, would be able to bypass it for her.

=== ''The Wind Waker'' ===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', the Mark of the Triforce is first seen when Link and [[Tetra]] attempted to fight Ganondorf, and the Triforce of Power resonated on the villain's hand while he grabs her throat, due to her necklace being a [[Triforce Shard|fragment]] of the Triforce of Wisdom (which itself also resonated). This caused him to deduce Tetra's true identity. When Tetra becomes Princess Zelda, its Mark dwells within her hand. Link also gains his mark when he restores the shattered Triforce of Courage formerly owned by the Hero of Time and became known as the Hero of Winds. The Marks appear one final time when Ganondorf forces Zelda and Link to expose their respective Triforce pieces so he could gain access to the complete Triforce, only for [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]] to touch the golden relic, first.

In both the original and [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD|HD]] versions of the game, both Zelda and Ganondorf retain their respective Triforce marks during the final battle against the latter even though the Triforce had been used up by Daphne Nohansen Hyrule.

==={{TP|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', Link is shown to have the Mark of the Triforce on his left hand, likely showing his possession of the Triforce of Courage. It also appears on {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}'s front left paw (front right in the Wii version), visible when he is dragged away following his first transformation in {{Term|TPHD|Faron Woods|link}}.

{{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf|link}} unveils his Triforce mark shortly after surviving his [[Failed Execution of Ganondorf|attempted execution]]. He later unveiled it to Link and Midna while boasting about how the power of the Goddesses made him a king in his own right. It lastly shines during his final moments when he warns Link and Princess Zelda that their conflict is far from over, only to fade from him as he dies.

{{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda|link}} was shown to have the mark on her hand when attempting to alleviate the [[Wolf Link|curse]] on Link after Zant's ambush on him and Midna, causing her to deduce that the curse was the result of [[Ganondorf|an evil power]], and also when revealing that she was [[Triforce of Wisdom|blessed]] by the Goddesses similar to Link.

==={{ALBW}}===
After {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} becomes {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon|link}} by merging with {{Term|ALBW|Ganon|link}}, he promptly shows off his {{Term|ALBW|Mark of the Triforce}} for the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Power|link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|link}} unveils the {{Term|ALBW|Mark of the Triforce}} for the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} that she had earlier stolen from {{Term|ALBW|Zelda|link}} as part of her agenda of restoring {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} after revealing her intention of forcibly confiscating the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Courage|link}} from {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}}, immediately before summoning Yuga Ganon to deal with him. Later, after the antagonist betrays and consumes {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda}} for her {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Wisdom}}, he shows off the {{Term|ALBW|Mark of the Triforce}}, this time showing both Power and Wisdom. Link also briefly shows off his Triforce of Courage shortly before the final battle.

== Trivia ==

* In the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' games since ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl|Brawl]]'', the Mark of Triforce has been weaponized into a [[Final Smash]] by [[Link]]. He uses his mark of the [[Triforce of Courage]] to trap an opponent into a powerful barrier closely resembling the full Triforce to perform rapid slashes before knocking them into the air with one mighty blow, shattering it. It is known as the [[Triforce Slash]].
