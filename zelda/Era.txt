TITLE : Era

{{Term/Store|Era|Eras|Series|plural}} are time periods in the chronology of {{TLoZ|Series}}. An Era may refer to the lifetime of a single incarnation of Link,<ref name="E 10">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 10}}</ref> to periods in between {{TLoZ|-}} titles,<ref name="HH 69">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH|publisher= |page= 69}}</ref> or even to entire branches of the [[Zelda Timeline]].<ref name="HH 69"/>
