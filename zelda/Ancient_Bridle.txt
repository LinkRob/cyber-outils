The {{Term/Store|Ancient Bridle||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Bridle}}<br/>A mysterious bridle made with forgotten ancient technology. Equipping a horse with this will maximize their abilities, increasing the number of spurs associated with them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Bridle}} is a {{Term|BotW|Bridle|link}} exclusive to {{TCB}} DLC Pack. When the DLC is active, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will receive the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|Series|EX Ancient Horse Rumors}}", which will require him to read the {{Term|Series|Super Rumor Mill V3|link}}, which has information about this Bridle and the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Saddle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor has it there's a bridle that enhances a horse's skill and a saddle that can summon your horse right to you. Does this supposed ancient horse gear really exist?<br/><br/>Apparently {{Color|BotW Red|Highland Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare horse gear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> According to the {{Term|BotW|Book|link}}, the Bridle can be found south of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}, beneath a cherry blossom tree on a mountain where creatures gather.<ref>{{Cite|The bridle is south of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Ridge}}, {{Color|BotW Red|beneath a cherry blossom tree at a mountain where creatures gather}}.|Super Rumor Mill V3|BotW}}</ref> After reading this, Link can find the Ancient Bridle under the cherry tree on top of {{Term|BotW|Satori Mountain|link}}.

These Bridles can be equipped at either {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Change to {{Color|BotW Red|Ancient Bridle}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> Ancient Bridles are made of forgotten {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> When worn by a Horse, they'll maximize the Horse's abilities, increasing their number of spurs by two.<ref name="Inventory"/> As with other Bridles, workers will refuse to equip them to {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}.
