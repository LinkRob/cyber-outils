==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Scroll of Enchant Weapon}} are items that temporarily increases a {{Term|CoH|Weapon}}'s attack power by 1, allowing its user to deal more damage than normal against enemies. Collecting multiple {{Plural|CoH|Scroll of Enchant Weapon}} will not stack, meaning that collecting multiple scrolls of the same type will not add more scrolls to the hero's inventory, forcing the heroes to be carefully manage how they use them and when they should pick up extras if there are any present.

They can be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}}, or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or as a random reward after destroying a stack of books with an explosive.
