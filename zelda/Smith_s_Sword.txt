The {{Term/Store|Smith's Sword||Series, TMC}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref> also known as '''Smith's Blade''' and the '''Grandfather's Sword''',<ref>{{Cite|Smith's Blade|Quest Status|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can put away your {{Color|TMC Red|grandfather's sword}} now.|N/A|TMC}}</ref> is a {{Term|Series|Sword|link}} in {{TMC}}.

==Locations and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Smith's Sword}} is created by {{Term|TMC|Smith|link}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Then, with the broken Picori Blade and the Smith's Sword in hand, Link set off in search of their land.|book=HH|page=79}}</ref> the finest blacksmith in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. It is given to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} by {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}} at the start of his quest. Though it was originally intended for the winner of the sword-fighting competition at the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}},<ref>{{Cite|This is the blade that will be presented to the winner of the competition. Don't lose it. And while the two of you may be childhood friends, remember...Zelda is the princess of Hyrule. You watch over her. Don't let anything bad happen to her.|Smith|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}} gives it to {{Term|TMC|Link}} so he can venture into the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}, seeking out the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} in order to save {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}}.

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the reforged {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Melari|link}}, called the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Smith's Sword}} disappears from his inventory.
