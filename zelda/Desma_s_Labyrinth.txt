{{Term/Store|Desma's Labyrinth||Series, FPTRR}} is the fifth dungeon found in {{FPTRR}}.

==Entrance to the {{Term|FPTRR|Desma's Labyrinth|display=Labyrinth}}==
It is located within the bowels {{Term|FPTRR|Mount Desma}} itself.

==Themes and Navigation==
This dungeon contains a "fire" theme, which {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} will quickly notice as there is magma flowing through the numerous chambers. 

The {{Term|FPTRR|Boss|link}} of this level is {{Term|FPTRR|Dora Dora}}. After he has been destroyed, his body will yield 60,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got 60,000 rupees!|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref>
