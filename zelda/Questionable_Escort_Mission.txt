{{Term/Store|Questionable Escort Mission||HWAoC}} is a level 45 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Questionable Escort Mission|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Questionable Escort Mission}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Dark Hood|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to escort a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Soldier|link}} to a Targeted Position at the end of a long path in the upper left corner of the map. Almost immediately after moving forward, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} spawn, along with a large group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat them for the soldier to move forward. The soldier decides to rest after a short while, and the same happens again, except that the {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} are replaced with a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} and an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}. The pattern repeats once more, and in this case, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Hinox|link}} appears alongside a smaller bunch of {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}}. When the soldier is extremely close to the Targeted Position, a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}} spawns alone and must also be killed by the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When it is defeated, the solder is revealed to be from the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga|link}} Clan. Three {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}} appear, accompanied by a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} similar in size to that of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the three {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} are defeated.
