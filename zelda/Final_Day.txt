The {{Term/Store|Final Day||Series, MM, MM3D}} is the last of the three total days that [[Link]] has before the [[Moon]] crashes into [[Clock Town]] and destroys all of [[Termina]] in {{MM}}. From the dawn of this day, the young hero and [[Tatl]] only have 24 hours left to complete whatever tasks they have yet to resolve.

==Overview==
Many of the citizens of {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}} are now aware of the impending doom that is about to befall them, and the environment and theme of {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town}} once again reflect the desperate state of {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}: [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_Moon_Rumble_Stereo.wav slight earthquakes] begun to unravel throughout the entire land at random times, and the {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town}} music has taken a darker tone along with a frantic rhythm. The majority of the townsfolk already have their plans of evacuation ready, and the [[Soldier|soldiers]], who are being forced to stay at their guard, are urging the remaining citizens to evacuate as soon as possible. However, some people such as {{Term|MM3D|Mutoh|link}} openly ignore this command and call the townspeople cowards.
[[File:MM Final Day (Japanese).png|thumb|267x267px|Link at [[Termina Field]] during the daytime of the Final Day (Japanese version). Note the green sky.]]
The daytime sky highly contrasts that of the [[First Day]] (which is clear and sunny), for it is now turned cloudy with a sickly green coloration (In {{MM3D}}, the sky is mostly clear with a few purple highlights, and only the horizon turns green), similar to that of a real-world severe thunderstorm containing [[wikipedia:Hail|hail]] and/or a [[wikipedia:Tornado|tornado]], illustrating the depressing and somber fate of {{Term|MM3D|Termina}}. Once night arrives with only 12 hours left, where the weather becomes a starry sky with a purplish horizon (blood red in the remake), almost all of the shops will be closed, and the majority of the citizens have evacuated out of town, a few to {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|link}} in hopes of staying safe from the deadly threat of the now dangerously close {{Term|MM3D|Moon}}, turning {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town}} from a lively, cheerful place to a bleak, desolate ghost town (although the few residents of the ranch soon realize it is not any safer there).<ref>{{Cite|Actually... I know... We're not safe here, either... I'm no expert on these things, but that's the feeling I get.|Cremia|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I heard it from my gramps. Says the moon's gonna fall... With somethin' that big, it's sure to take this ranch down with it... Hahhh...Oh, well.|Grog|MM}}</ref> The remaining citizens who have not fled town either refuse to believe the {{Term|MM3D|Moon}} is falling, have given up hope, or are not aware of the situation.<ref>{{Cite|The only ones left are those who don't believe the rumors or aren't aware of them.|Guard|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Those who remain in town either don't believe the rumors or have given up all hope.|Guard|MM}}</ref>

At midnight, the Eve of the [[Carnival of Time]] begins and a continuous dark theme plays all over {{Term|MM3D|Termina}} (except in [[Dungeons in Majora's Mask|Dungeons]]), with the earthquakes becoming more fierce and frequent over time. At this stage, the [[Clock Tower]]'s entrance for the final battle against [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]] will be open, and its bell will also toll frequently and progressively faster as the final six hours tick down. From 12:00AM, it tolls every 10 Terminan minutes, until 5AM, where it starts tolling every 5 Terminan minutes. Upon 5:30 AM, it tolls every 3 Terminan minutes until time is up and the Moon [[Terminan Apocalypse|crashes into Termina]], warning the player they should use the [[Song of Time]] or defeat Majora before it's too late and ultimately result a [[Game Over]].

Link cannot use the {{Term|MM3D|Song of Double Time|link}}, dance with {{Term|MM3D|Pierre|link}}, or listen to [[Granny]]'s stories to advance to the next day. In ''Majora's Mask 3D,'' he can advance until 5AM, the final hour,  when using the {{Term|MM3D|Song of Double Time}}.

==Trivia==
*The [[Moon]] begins visibly descending in real-time during the Final Day, albeit very slowly.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Alan Flamer|published= May 3, 2012|retrieved= [[Special:Diff/408211/413010|August 11, 2014]]|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sSEWmat2crY|title= Majora Mask - 3rd day Moon's Falling|site= YouTube|type= video}}</ref>
*In the original [[Nintendo 64]] version of {{MM|-}}, the night sky changes to dawn as [[Termina]]'s final hours tick down. However, in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|remake]] of the game, the sky stays eerily red throughout the entire night of the Final Day, even if [[Link]] calls the [[Guardian Deity|Four Giants]] and halts the Moon's descent, as well as in the resulting scenes.
*In the Japanese version of the game, the Final Day is called "'''The Last Day'''", and the HUD clock reads "Last" rather than "Final". This change for the international release is possibly because "Final" seems more ominous than simply "Last".
*If Link is in [[South Clock Town]] at 12:00AM, a cutscene will play, showing the [[Clock Tower]] change to its alternate position and its door opening. If he is in any other area in Termina, including the [[West Clock Town|West]], [[North Clock Town|North]], and [[East Clock Town|East]] sides of the town, the game will continue in real-time.
**In the initial three-day cycle, if Link is at [[Termina Field]] by the [[Astral Observatory]], a unique cutscene occurs where the camera will zoom toward the Clock Tower and the animation of itself changing alongside fireworks will be seen.
*In the [[Bombers' Notebook]] as well as the time meter in ''Majora's Mask 3D'', the Final Day is marked with a magenta theme.
*If debugs are used to reveal the originally planned seven-day cycle, the Final Day was going to be called "'''Wednesday'''".
*Although this requires precise timing, it's possible to pause the game the exact moment the time cycle ends and the [[New Day]] is reached. When viewed at the Bomber's Notebook, the time will return back to the beginning of the Final Day due to limitations.
*The Final Day in {{MM|-}}, especially during nighttime, is often described by fans as one of the most harrowing experiences in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]], due to the overall dark atmosphere of impending doom by the Moon going to crash.
