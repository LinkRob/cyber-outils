{{Term/Store|Bird Statue|Bird Statues|Series, SS|plural}} are {{Plural|SSHD|Object|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Just face the {{Color|SSHD Red|bird statue}} over there, and say a short prayer to {{Color|SSHD Red|save}} your progress.|Jakamar|SSHD}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Bird Statue|plural}} were referred to as '''Loftwing Statues''' on the official Zelda website.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Loftwing Statues located throughout the world act as save points for Link to record his progress on his adventure. He will always return to the last place he saved, whether he is defeated, or chooses to take a break. These statues are even located inside dungeons, so Link can easily try again without too much backtracking if he is defeated therein.|title=Exciting New Save Features|site=Skyward Sword Official Website|url=http://zelda.com/skywardsword/#/about/exciting-new-save-features}}</ref> However, because this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Bird Statue}} were referred to as '''Save Points''' in {{Guide|SS|Prima}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS |publisher= Prima |page= 256}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} They are stone effigies of [[Loftwing]]s that function similarly to the [[Owl Statue]]s in {{MM}}. They can be found both in the overworld and inside dungeons. All of the Bird Statues on the surface are activated when Link examines the special statue near the [[Sealed Temple]].<ref>{{Cite|Supposedly this statue is special, as it is said to have the ability to activate all the other statues.|Gorko|SS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Bird Statues primarily serve both as the save feature of {{SS|-}} and as a gateway to [[The Sky]].<ref>{{Cite|I highly recommend that you save your progress whenever you encounter a bird statue.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This bird statue contains significant sacred power. If you offer prayers through it, you can make a record of your quest progress.|Fi|SS}}</ref> When in a dungeon, they cannot be used to return to the sky; instead, they are used to teleport Link out of the dungeon, similar to the [[Magic Mirror]] from {{ALttP}} or [[Ooccoo]] and [[Ooccoo Jr.]] from {{TP}}. If Link dies in his journey, he can continue it at these statues, saving him the trouble of exploring the land or dungeon again. Even if Link does not want to save a record of his quest, it is recommended to visit these statues so that he may mark their position on the [[map]] and dive to them later in the future.<ref>{{Cite|When you find a bird statue, you should always examine it more closely, even if you have no intention of using it to save your progress. Examining the bird statue will record it on your map, allowing you to check its position later with [+].|Fi|SS}}</ref>
