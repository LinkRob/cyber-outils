The {{Term/Store|Knight Academy||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|'''Knight Academy'''}}|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>

==Overview==
The Knight Academy is located in [[Skyloft]]. It is there where young students live and train to become knights once they graduate.<ref>{{Cite Web|url= http://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/#/wii/zelda-skyward-sword/4/1|title=Producer Trap|site= Iwata Asks}}</ref> Next to the academy is the [[Sparring Hall]], where students can go to improve their [[Sword]] skills. [[Gaepora]] is the headmaster of the academy, while [[Owlan]] and [[Horwell]] are instructors. The residents of the academy include the students [[Link]], [[Groose]], [[Cawlin]], [[Strich]], [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]], and [[Karane]], and the graduated knight [[Pipit]]. The lunch lady [[Henya]] can also be found in the kitchen.

The main building consists of two floors, with the kitchen, restroom, classroom, and male {{Term|SSHD|Dormitory|link}} on the bottom level; {{Term|SSHD|Gaepora's Room|link}}, the bathroom, the female {{Term|SSHD|Dormitory}}, and both {{Term|SSHD|Owlan's Room|link}} and {{Term|SSHD|Horwell's Room|link}} are all on the second level. There is an exit on each floor, though the entrance on the first floor is locked at night, forcing Link to use the upper entrance.

Most of the rooms in the Knight Academy are accessible, with the exception of the bathroom in the upper floor at night due to the headmaster taking a bath, Headmaster {{Term|SSHD|Gaepora's Room}} on occasions, and {{Term|SSHD|Zelda's Room|link}}, although {{Term|SSHD|Zelda's Room}} can be entered through the chimney outside after acquiring the [[Clawshot]]s.

==Trivia==
*During the events of {{SS|-}}, the Knight Academy celebrates its 25th anniversary.<ref>{{Cite|Today is a special day for many reasons, but it is also the 25th anniversary of our fine institution.|Owlan|SS}}</ref> This is a reference to [[The Legend of Zelda 25th Anniversary|{{TLoZ|-}} 25th Anniversary]], the time in which the game was released.
*It is implied by Gaepora that the color of the knights' tunics changes every year,<ref>{{Cite|That green uniform is what our knights will be wearing this year. To be honest, I've had my doubts about the color.|Gaepora|SS}}</ref> suggesting Link's traditional [[Tunic]] may have been selected green at random.
*Hitting the bell atop the academy or blowing on it with the [[Gust Bellows]] will cause a single Rupee to fall out.
