The {{Term/Store|Ancient Tree Stump||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Ancient Tree Stump'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Tree Stump}} is located in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. It is the remains of a giant, ancient {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} at the northern base of {{Term|BotW|Mount Daphnes|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= {{Big|Ancient Tree Stump}}<br/>This giant stump is located directly to the south of Hyrule Castle Town. In this past, this was a massive tree with resplendent branches. The stump that remains demonstrates just how big it was. Now it is surrounded by a lake, and a simple bridge spans the water to the top of the stump.|book= CaC |publisher= |page= 255}}</ref> A small village was situated at the roots of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Tree Stump}}, but it has since been submerged in an unnamed lake. A {{Term|BotW|Garbage Dump|link}} now sits at the top of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Tree Stump|display=Stump}}, which is connected to {{Term|BotW|Mount Daphnes}} by a simple bridge.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can by found by diving into the {{Term|BotW|Circle|link}} of {{Plural|BotW|Lily Pad|link}} north of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Tree Stump}}. When this {{Term|BotW|Korok}} is uncovered, it will give {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
