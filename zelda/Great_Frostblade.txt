==Location and Uses==
{{Compendium/Extract|Great Frostblade}}
{{Plural|BotW|Great Frostblade}} are a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} forged with ore found in the permafrost of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> As a result, this is where they are commonly found, as well as in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. Their elemental nature is a hallmark of the {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear|link}} equipment archetype, to which {{Plural|BotW|Great Frostblade}} belong.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are especially common around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}.

When {{Plural|BotW|Great Frostblade}} glow blue, they have the ability to freeze any {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} that they are used against. They can also be equipped in cold areas to give {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}. When they are used to attack {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}-related {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Fire Wizzrobe|link}}, they will be defeated in a single swing.

{{Plural|BotW|Great Frostblade}} are made of metal, which means they will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They are also unable to be burned by {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, but they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. To rectify this, they can be picked up and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.
