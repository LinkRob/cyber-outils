The {{Term/Store|Hylian Crest|Hylian Crests|Series, TP, TPHD}} is a recurring symbol in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|A shield with a Hylian crest. Defend against attacks with [Z]. It's metal and will never burn.|Inventory|TP}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoT, ST, SS, ALBW, BotW}} It is the mark of the Goddess [[Hylia]] and a crest of the {{Term|Series|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Mark of Hylia">{{Cite Book|quote=|book= E |publisher= |page= 84}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Romanize|ハイラル王家の紋章が刻まれた盾[L]で構えて防御鋼鉄製なので炎に触れてもなくならない|Hairaru ōke no monshō ga kizama reta tate [L] de kamaete bōgyo kōtetsu-seinanode honō ni furete mo nakunaranai|Shield with the crest of the Hyrulean Royal Family. Hold with [L] to defend. It’s made of steel, so it’s not lost even if you touch flames.|2}}|Inventory|TP|Japanese version}}</ref> The {{Term|Series|Hylian Crest}} is commonly used on {{Term|Series|Hylian|link}} weapons, armour, and architecture; most notably it appears on the {{Term|Series|Hylian Shield|link}}, where it represents the {{Term|SS|Crimson Loftwing|link}} that was ridden by {{Term|SS|Link|link|display=the hero}} during the {{Term|Series|Sky Era|link}}.<ref name="Mark of Hylia" /> It is similar to the {{Term|Series|Royal Crest|link|display=Wingcrest}} of the {{Term|Series|Hyrulean Royal Family}}, both being derived from the {{Term|Series|Goddess Crest|link}} used in the ancient {{Term|Series|Era of Hylia|link}}.

==Overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:Red Shield.png|left|thumb|100px|A winged figure on the {{Term|ALttP|Red Shield}}]]
Depictions of avian figures similar to the {{Term|ALttP|Hylian Crest}} appeared in {{ALttP}}, where artwork of the {{Term|BotW|Small_Shield#A_Link_to_the_Past|link|display=Fighter's Shield}} and {{Term|ALttP|Red Shield|link}} both show a stylized bird below the {{Term|ALttP|Triforce|link}}. Another winged figure is shown above the doorway of the {{Term|ALttP|Sanctuary (A Link to the Past)|link|display=Sanctuary}}.
[[File:OoT Hylian Shield Render.png|thumb|125px|The {{Term|OoT|Hylian Shield}} as it appears in {{OoT|-}}, with a {{Term|OoT|Hylian Crest}} in red]]

==={{OoT|-}}===
The current design of the {{Term|OoT|Hylian Crest}} first appears in {{OoT}}, shown in multiple locations within the game. The {{Term|OoT|Hylian Shield}} of the {{Term|OoT|Knights of Hyrule}} has the {{Term|OoT|Hylian Crest|display=Crest}} with the {{Term|OoT|Triforce}} above it and an inverted triangle below it.<ref name="Mark of Hylia" /> The clothing and jewelry worn by {{Term|OoT|Princess Zelda|link}} has designs including the {{Term|OoT|Hylian Crest}} with other symbols such as the {{Term|OoT|Triforce}} and the {{Term|OoT|Sheikah Eye|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book=A&A|publisher=|page=164, 165}}</ref>

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}} the {{Term|TWW|Hylian Crest}} can be seen in the background of [[:File:TWW Portrait.jpg|a painting]] in {{Term|TWW|Hyrule Castle#The Wind Waker|link|display=Hyrule Castle}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
Within {{TP}}, the {{Term|TP|Hylian Crest}} is displayed on the {{Term|TP|Hylian Shield}}, on {{Term|TP|Zelda|display=Zelda}}'s dress, and on suits of armour in {{Term|TP|Hyrule Castle (Twilight Princess)|link|display=Hyrule Castle}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, when {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}}'s spirit assumes control of a {{Term|ST|Phantom|link}}, a glowing pink {{Term|Series|Hylian Crest}} appears on its back.

==={{SS|-}}===
The {{Term|SS|Hylian Crest}} is in multiple locations in {{SS}}, including on the {{Term|SS|Hylian Shield}}, in the {{Term|SS|Temple of Time|link}}, and on an awakened {{Term|SS|Gate of Time|link}}. The Hylian Crest also appears on the game's logo. {{SS|-}} also features its likely precursor, the {{Term|SS|Goddess Crest}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}} the {{Term|ALBW|Hylian Crest|display=Crest}} is shown on the {{Term|ALBW|Hylian Shield}}.<ref name="Mark of Hylia" />
[[File:BotW Royal Guard Set Concept Artwork.png|thumb|x150px|The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Set}} from {{BotW}} includes multiple {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Crest}}]]

==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Hylian Crest}} is in many locations in {{BotW}}. It is displayed throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link|display=Hyrule Castle}} on banners, in sculptures, on the floor of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)#Sanctum|link|display=Sanctum}}, among other locations.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book=CaC|publisher=|page=231–253}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Hylian Crest|display=Crest}} is displayed on {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} used by {{Term|Series|Hyrule#Military|link|display=Hyrule's Military}}, such as the {{Term|BotW|Hylian Shield}}, {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Set|Set}}, the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Set|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Shield|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book=CaC|publisher=|page=168, 184, 190}}</ref>
