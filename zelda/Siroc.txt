{{Term/Store|Siroc||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Siroc}} is the leader of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}}, and resides in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}} along with the rest of her {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|display=Tribe}}. She can be found on the uppermost floor of the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}}.

When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} meets her, {{Term|TMC|Siroc}} explains to him the story of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} and how they came to the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}}, as well as their ability to see all that goes on in the land of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, welcome. Long, long ago, and then an age before that... Our tribe abandoned the place you call the {{Color|TMC Green|Wind Ruins}}... With our magic, we moved our palace up into the skies. We are the {{Color|TMC Green|Wind Tribe}}. We have been watching, and we know all that happens on your surface world. We know a small boy collects {{Color|TMC Red|elements}} to lift the curse from a princess. We know of {{Color|TMC Green|Vaati}} and his evil acts... The winds tell us these things.|Siroc|TMC}}</ref> Since she knows of {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s quest to collect the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Siroc}} tells him that the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}} he seeks is located in the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TMC Red|Wind Element}} that you seek lies deep in the {{Color|TMC Green|Palace of Winds}} above me.|Siroc|TMC}}</ref> Normally, surface dwellers are not allowed to access the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|display=Palace}}, but knowing that {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s goal is sincere, she permits him to access the roof that leads him to the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds}}.<ref>{{Cite|Surface dwellers are not permitted to enter our palace, but you are special. You may continue to the roof.|Siroc|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Siroc}} twice;<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? OK, but let's be careful about it!|Siroc|TMC}}</ref> both times will yield a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a red {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} located in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}.
