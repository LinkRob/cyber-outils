{{Plural|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe}} are great tools for cutting down {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}},<ref name="Inventory"/> as they have a hidden property which multiplies their damage sevenfold when used against one. However, their weight makes them slow and inefficient {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> The {{Term|BotW|Old Man|link}} owns one, and he suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} take it to defend himself with.<ref>{{Cite|If you need a weapon, you can find something better. Many beasts roam the land past this point. You can take my axe with you, if you please. Assuming you can find it.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> They are very common on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}, as well as in groves of {{Plural|BotW|Tree}}.

Since they are made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, and they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
