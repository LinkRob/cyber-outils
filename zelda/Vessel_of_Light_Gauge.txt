The {{Term/Store|Vessel of Light Gauge|Vessel of Light Gauges|Series, TP, TPHD}} is a mechanic in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= |game= TPHD |page= 8}}</ref> The {{Term|Series|Vessel of Light Gauge}} goes unnamed in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
In the process of freeing {{Term|TPHD|Faron Province|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Eldin Province|link}}, and {{Term|TPHD|Lanayru Province|link}} from the eternal {{Term|TPHD|Twilight|link}} cast by {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} must gather a certain number of {{Plural|TPHD|Tear of Light|link}} from each Province.<ref>{{Cite|When the vessel is full of tears, you can dispel the twilight and return the land to its light-filled state.|N/A|TP}}</ref> These are collected in the {{Term|TPHD|Vessel of Light|link}}, represented on-screen as the {{Term|TPHD|Vessel of Light Gauge}}.

In the original {{TP}} for the [[Wii]] and [[GameCube]], there are 16 {{Plural|TP|Tear of Light|display=Tears}} to collect in each Province. In {{TPHD}}, there are instead only 12 {{Plural|TPHD|Tear of Light}} to collect in each. The {{Term|TPHD|Vessel of Light Gauge}} was altered to reflect this.
