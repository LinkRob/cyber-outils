{{Term/Store|Star|Stars|Series, LA, LADX|plural}} are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{LA}}.{{Name Ref|LA}}

==Characteristics==
Stars consists of a spinning creature which appears in [[Dungeon]]s. Its name is due to the star-like shape of its body. It can be defeated with a single sword blow, which will destroy them. However, Stars can be very fast at times, making it harder to strike. Its behavior is similar to [[Bubble]]s, in that it moves diagonally and bounces off of walls.
