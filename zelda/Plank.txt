The '''Plank''' is an item in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Plank is located in the [[Shrine of Water]], in the screen after the second wall spike room. The Plank can repair the gap of a broken bridge in the Shrine of Water, allowing [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] to cross over.
