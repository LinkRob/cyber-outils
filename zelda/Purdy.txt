{{Term/Store|Purdy||Series, TP}} is [[Hena]]'s pet cockatiel in {{TP}}.<ref name="Name"/>

==Biography==
Purdy is found inside [[Hena]]'s hut in the [[Fishing Hole]], living inside a round cage on the counter next to the [[fish]] tank. Despite Hena's love for the bird, who constantly says how cute she is whenever she is examined by [[Link]],<ref name="Name"/> Purdy constantly insults Hena by calling her old.<ref>{{Cite|Hena's OLD! Very OLD!|Purdy|TP}}</ref> Whenever Link rolls into the cage, Purdy gets even ruder and tells Link to leave whenever he comes close.<ref>{{Cite|Scra-AAM!|Purdy|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Go ho-OME!|Purdy|TP}}</ref> However, if Link has not bothered Purdy, she will occasionally warmly greet Link.<ref>{{Cite|Go-OOD moor-NING!|Purdy|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Go-OOD eve-NING!|Purdy|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|How-DEE!|Purdy|TP}}</ref> Purdy will also call herself pretty.<ref>{{Cite|Pur-DEE's pur-DEE!|Purdy|TP}}</ref>
