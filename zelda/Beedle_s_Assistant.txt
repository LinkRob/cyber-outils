{{Term/Store|Beedle's Assistant||Series, PH}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="PH 106">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 106}}</ref> He is {{Term|Series|Beedle|link}}'s alter ego. He goes unnamed in {{TWW}} aside from the title with which he signs a {{Term|TWWHD|Letter|link}}, '''Asst. Manager, Rock Spire Shop Ship'''.<ref name="TWW Letter" />

==Biography==
{{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant}} manages a special version of {{Term|Series|Beedle's Shop Ship|link}} that sells expensive, unique items such as {{Plural|Series|Piece of Heart|link}}.<ref name="TWWHD 171">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD|publisher= Prima|page= 171}}</ref> The first time {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} enters the shop, {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant}} insists they had never met before. He claims to be closing the shop in seven days and is holding a "liquidation" or "clearance" sale.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! A fine welcome to you, young person
that I've never seen before! You're so lucky to meet me at this time! This shop will close in just {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|seven more days}}, and I'm having a clearance sale.|Beedle's Assistant|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OHHHHHH! Welcome! I don't think we've met before! Nope! But I know all about you! I close up shop in {{Color|PH Red|seven days}}! Liquidation sale!|Beedle's Assistant|PH}}</ref> Each day, {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant}} counts down the remaining days until closing day.{{Clarify|TWW, TWWHD|reason= See talk page}} This is revealed to be a marketing ploy the day after "closing day", when {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant}} holds a "grand reopening" sale with the prices the same as before. He claims to have closed the shop for interior remodeling,<ref>{{Cite|OHHHHH! A fine welcome to you! We've remodeled and reopened! Hoo-rah! And right now, we're having a special Grand Reopening Sale! Have a look around!|Beedle's Assistant|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I didn't lie! I closed...and completely redid the inside of my shop. Tah-dah! It's reborn!|Beedle's Assistant|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OHHHHHHH! Welcome! I've remodeled! Welcome to our grand reopening sale! Take a look around! ...I didn't lie! I closed, redid the interior, and the shop is reborn!|Beedle's Assistant|PH}}</ref> but in reality makes only token changes to the shops's appearance.{{Verify|PH}}

{{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant}} wears a bronze{{Exp Game|TWW}} or gold{{Exp Game|PH}} centurion helmet as a disguise—his clothing and mannerisms are otherwise the same.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD|publisher= Prima|page= 275}}</ref><ref name="PH 106"/>

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Rock Spire Shop Ship}}
In {{TWW}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant}} runs the {{Term|TWWHD|Rock Spire Shop Ship|link}} near {{Term|TWWHD|Rock Spire Isle|link}}. When {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} gets the first upgrade to the {{Term|TWWHD|Wallet|link}}, he becomes able to afford the {{Term|TWWHD|Empty Bottle|link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chart|link}}, and {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart|link}} for sale at the {{Term|TWWHD|Rock Spire Shop Ship}}. At that point {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} receives a {{Term|TWWHD|Letter|link}} from {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant}} advertising for the shop.<ref name="TWW Letter" />

{{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant}} refuses to buy {{Plural|TWWHD|Spoil}} the way {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle|link}} normally does. If presented with some, he claims not to know what it is and denies being {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle}}.<ref>{{Cite|What's that? Why would I know what that is? I'm not Beedle! Or whatever his name is! Don't show me that!|Beedle's Assistant|TWWHD}}</ref> This despite the fact that he appears on {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Chart|link}}. Furthermore, if {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} gives {{Term|TWWHD|Carlov|link}} a {{Term|TWWHD|Pictograph|link}} of {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Assistant}}, the sculptor will make {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle}}'s {{Term|TWWHD|Figurine|link}}.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Main|Masked Ship}}
In {{PH}}, {{Term|PH|Beedle's Assistant}} and his {{Term|PH|Masked Ship|link}} appear at random locations on the ocean from 10pm to midnight on weekdays, and 10am to noon on weekends (according to the [[Nintendo DS]] clock). He sells a single {{Term|PH|Piece of Heart|link}} and a "Good Thing" (a {{Term|PH|Courage Gem|link}}),<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|PH Red|Good Thing}}: 500 Rupees<br/>Collect these for a good reward...and some change!|Beedle's Assistant|PH}}</ref> as well as {{Plural|PH|Potion|link}}, {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}} and {{Term|PH|Treasure|link}}.
