==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Weapon Mount}} are wall decorations that can be hung up in {{Term|BotW|Link's House|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches one with a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} equipped, he will have the option to display that {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} on the {{Term|BotW|Weapon Mount}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} places a {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} on the {{Term|BotW|Weapon Mount|display=Mount}}, he can retrieve it at any time, so long as he has room in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} purchases his {{Term|BotW|Link's House|display=House}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Hylian Homeowner|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bolson|link}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Weapon Mount}} as a housewarming gift. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} to install two additional {{Plural|BotW|Weapon Mount}} for 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} each.<ref>{{Cite|[Weapon mount.] That'll be {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}}, OK? You can shell that out?|Bolson|BotW}}</ref>
