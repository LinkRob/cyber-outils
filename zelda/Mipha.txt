== Physical appearance ==
Mipha, like all members of her race, is a humanoid dolphin being half-human and half-fish. Her skin is scaly and of different shades of red and white and she has a fish fin on her head as hair. Mipha is shorter than the average Zora. She has a serene face, with thin red lips and yellow eyes.

Originally she only wore jewelry and ornaments of Zora royalty, but after she officially became a [[Champions|Champion]] she began to wear a robe with the emblem of Hyrule.

==Personality==
Although usually demure and introverted, Mipha can be courageous when needed and does whatever she can to assist [[Link]] and her allies in any way she can, especially in their battle to stop the [[Calamity Ganon]]'s return.

Mipha is known to be extremely kind, sweet, treating both her people and strangers with equal kindness. Her cutscene in [[the Champions' Ballad]] shows that she was a nurturing elder sister to [[Sidon]].

Mipha is also known to be in love with Link, promising to heal him whenever and no matter how bad the wound may be. She shyly tells the hero that after the Great Calamity was dealt with, she wants to spend time with him, like they did in their younger days.

== Abilities ==
As the [[Champions|Champion]] of the [[Zoras]], Mipha is one of the most powerful elite warriors in [[Zora's Domain]] across Hyrule. She was a great fighter who is skilled with her [[Lightscale Trident]] that was gifted from birth, receive training and tutelage from the sergeant royal guard of the Zora Seggin and the elder Zora council Muzu as her spearmanship skills was a class of her own.

Mipha was born with the unique power called [[Mipha's Grace]]: an ability to heal people from fatal injuries, a power that was passed to Link after defeating [[Waterblight Ganon]]. Aside from her magical healing abilities, Mipha can breathe underwater and swim at great speed like any Zora and she also display some control over the flow of water.

As a playable {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} wields a spear in combat and attacks with fast, multi-hitting combo attacks mixed with {{Term|HWAoC|Water (Terrain)|link}} magic. Her Unique Action allows her to form a geyser and warp to it while jumping in the air, giving her an aerial advantage and hitting her opponent's {{Term|HWAoC|Weak Point|link}}. She can also warp to {{Term|HWAoC|Water (Terrain)}} constructs created by her Strong Attacks. Her Enhanced Ability grants her a shockwave whenever she emerges from a geyser or construct. Her Special Attack uniquely heals herself as well as nearby allies, making her especially useful in missions where healing items are disabled or cannot drop.
