{{Term/Store|Eryck||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Eryck|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Eryck}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} traveler in search of self-discovery.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Eryck. I'm traveling the world on a journey of self-discovery.|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> He can be found beside a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Rabia Plain|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Eryck}}, he asks if he heard him humming.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... So you heard me humming, eh?|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Eryck}} explains that he was intending to travel to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, but he became lost and almost gave up.<ref>{{Cite|I'm heading for {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} now...but I'm lost. I was just about to give up.|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> Before this could happen, however, he heard a {{Term|BotW|Gruve|link|display=voice}} coming from the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Tower|link}}, reigniting his interest.<ref>{{Cite|But then, like, I heard a voice...coming from the {{Color|BotW Red|north tower}}...|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, he is unable to cross {{Term|BotW|Rutala River}}.<ref>{{Cite|Crossing the {{Color|BotW Blue|Rutala River}} would be next to impossible, though...|Eryck|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to ask {{Term|BotW|Eryck}} about the locations on his journey.<ref>{{Cite|Is there something else you'd like to ask me?|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> When asked about {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, he shares that it is a town in {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)|link}}, but he is unable to reach it due to needing to cross the {{Term|BotW|Rutala River}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Zora's Domain?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} is home of the Zora. It's a town in {{Color|BotW Blue|Lanayru}}. But crossing the {{Color|BotW Blue|Rutala River}} is impossible.|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> Due to how hard reaching {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} is, he decided to give up on visiting it.<ref>{{Cite|It's a hard place to get to, so I just gave up altogether.|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Tower}}, {{Term|BotW|Eryck}} explains that if one crosses {{Term|BotW|Rutala River}}, they will be able to see it on the opposite bank.<ref>{{Cite|[North tower?] If you continue north from here, on the other side of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Rutala River}}, you can see a huge tower on the opposite bank.|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> He claims that he heard a voice coming from the top of it, and he wonders if someone is up there.<ref>{{Cite|I thought I heard a voice coming from the top of that tower. I wonder if someone's up there.|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Rutala River}}, {{Term|BotW|Eryck}} reveals that it is the widest river in {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Rutala River?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Rutala River}} is the widest river in {{Color|BotW Blue|Lanayru}}.|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> Since it is dangerous to swim across it, {{Term|BotW|Eryck}} suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} pass it by way of {{Term|BotW|Linebeck Island|link}}.<ref name="Linebeck Island">{{Cite|You'd be risking your life if you tried to swim across it... If you want to go north of it, you'll have to walk patiently to {{Color|BotW Blue|Linebeck Island}} in the east so you can circumvent it.|Eryck|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* When {{Term|BotW|Eryck}} offers directions to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he erroneously states that {{Term|BotW|Linebeck Island}} is located to the east.<ref name="Linebeck Island"/> In reality, it is northwest of his campsite on {{Term|BotW|Rabia Plain}}.
