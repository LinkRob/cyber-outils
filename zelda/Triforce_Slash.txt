The '''Triforce Slash''' is [[Link]]'s, [[Toon Link]]'s and [[Young Link]]'s [[Final Smash]] in the {{SSB|Series}}, appearing in {{SSBB}}, {{SSB4}}, and {{SSBU}}.

==Overview==
When [[Link]], [[Toon Link]] or [[Young Link]] pick up the Smash Ball, they gain the ability to unleash a [[Final Smash]]. When activated, the [[Triforce of Courage]] on the back of Link's hand glows, and if the beam hits an opponent, it traps them inside a void strongly resembling the [[Triforce]] as Link repeatedly slashes him or her with his [[Sword]]. Each strike deals 4% or 5% of damage depending on the range. During the fifteenth strike, all the opponents trapped in the attack but the primary target are ejected. The final seventeenth strike causes 18% of damage with a high knockback. The Triforce Slash can cause a total of 83% of damage in {{SSBB}} and 69% in {{SSB4}}.

Although both Final Smashes deal the same amount of damage, the range and knockback of Toon Link's attack is shorter than Link's.

In {{SSBU}}, Link received a different Final Smash, the [[Ancient Bow and Arrow]], while Toon Link and Young Link retain Triforce Slash as their Final Smash.
