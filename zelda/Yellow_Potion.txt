{{Term/Store|Yellow Potion|Yellow Potions|Series, PH, ST, ALBW|plural}} are recurring [[Potion]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 143 {{Exp Game|PH, ST, ALBW}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWDE}}

==Location and uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
[[File:ST Yellow Potion Icon.png|right|Yellow Potion sprite from both {{PH|-}} and {{ST|-}}]]
In {{PH}}, Yellow Potions are the most powerful Potions available, as they restore all of Link's [[Heart]]s. Yellow Potions can only be purchased from the [[Beedle's Shop Ship|Masked Ship]] for 200 [[Rupee]]s. This shop ship only appears between 10 AM and noon according to the internal [[Nintendo DS]] clock.

==={{ST|-}}===
Yellow Potions in {{ST}} function identically as in {{PH}}. They can be bought at the [[Goron Country Store]] and the [[Papuchia Shop]] for 200 Rupees. Like other Potions in these games, the Yellow Potion comes in its own flask and restores all of Link's [[Heart]]s.<ref>{{Cite|That's a yellow potion! It restores all your hearts! It's only 200 Rupees!|Shopkeeper|ST}}</ref>

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, the Yellow Potion turns Link invincible for a short period of time when drank. More specifically, it turns Link invincible for as long as his energy gague is not empty, but it drains said gague at a rate of about 1 notch every 3 seconds. Link can buy a dose of the Yellow Potion from the [[Witch's House]] for 100 Rupees, and by supplying the [[Witch]] with 10 [[Monster Horn]]s. An empty [[Bottle]] is also required to store it in.

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:HW Yellow Potion Icon.png|x100px|right]]
In {{HW}}, Yellow Potions appear as the level 5 equivalent and last of the Bottled Potion items that Warriors take with them into battle, following the [[Green Potion]]. It is unlocked after obtaining 50 [[Illustration Piece]]s (90 in the Definitive Edition) by slaying [[Gold Skulltula]]s. It restores 15 Hearts and one and a half of the [[Special Attack]] Gauges. Like other Potions brought into battle, it is automatically refilled for the next battle.
{{Noncanon|End}}
