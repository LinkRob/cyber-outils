The '''Forest of Findo''' is a location in {{ZA}}. It is located close to central {{Term|ZA|Tolemac|link}}, between the {{Term|ZA|Plain of Andor|link}} and the {{Term|ZA|Meadow of Skotness}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|ZA|Forest of Findo}} is a small forest that is situated between the {{Term|ZA|Plain of Andor}} to the south, and the {{Term|ZA|Meadow of Skotness}} to the north. It is {{Term|ZA|Tolemac}}'s smallest forest, consisting of only three screens. The forest can only be accessed once {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda|link}} has obtained the {{Term|ZA|Items in Zelda's Adventure|section=Red Boots|display=Red Boots|link}} in the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Earth|link}}, which are required to cross the river that borders between the southern plain and the forest. At the end of the forest path is a fork in the road; the north exit leads to the {{Term|ZA|Meadow of Skotness}}, while the eastern path leads to the {{Term|ZA|Great Dry Canyon|link}}.

The forest's lone inhabitant is a strange Talking Mushroom with a humanoid face. It provides Zelda with no useful information, and only says that {{Plural|ZA|Moblin|link}} are "slow and stupid."
