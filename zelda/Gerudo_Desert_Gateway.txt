{{Term/Store|Gerudo Desert Gateway||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Gerudo Desert Gateway'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Gerudo Desert Gateway is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. The Gerudo Desert Gateway is a large, stone gate which marks the main entrance to [[Gerudo Desert]]. It is found near the [[Gerudo Canyon Stable]]. A road passes through the Gerudo Desert Gateway from the Gerudo Canyon Stable to the [[Kara Kara Bazaar]]. Consequently, travelers such as [[Calisa]], [[Garini]], [[Lukan]], and [[Maypin]] can be seen passing through the Gerudo Desert Gateway.
