The {{Term/Store|Lost Hills|Lost Hills|Series, TLoZ}} are a location in {{TLoZ}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= LOST HILLS|game= TLoZ|page= }}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Lost Hills are a mysterious series of hills located on [[Death Mountain]]. In order to reach {{Term|TLoZ|Level 5|link}}, [[Link]] must go up the mountain four times in a row.<ref>{{Cite|GO UP,UP, THE MOUNTAIN AHEAD|Old Woman|TLoZ}}</ref> If Link goes up, right or down, he will end up lost in the same screen. Link can only exit the Lost Hills by going left.

==Trivia==
*Although the Lost Hills made no reappearance in later ''Zelda'' games, a similar mechanic to the Lost Hills was used for [[Onox's Castle]] in ''Oracle of Seasons'' where the player has to continue going forward to reach Onox's boss room, and entering either the left or right rooms will force the player to start at the beginning.
