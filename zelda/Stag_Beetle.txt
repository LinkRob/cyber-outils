{{Term/Store|Stag Beetle|Stag Beetles|Series, PH|plural}} are enemies in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 206}}</ref>

==Characteristics== 
Stag Beetles appear in the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}}. They have strong masks that protect them from frontal attacks, similar to [[Helmasaur]]s. In order to damage them, [[Link]] can either attack them from behind or remove their mask with the [[Grappling Hook]]. With their mask gone, Stag Beetles attempts to flee. A single [[Sword]] strike is usually enough to defeat them.
