{{Term/Store|Zanc||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 295}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Zanc}}. He is a member of a highly civilized race of moles called the Mogma. All Mogmas have a deep and abiding love of treasure.
}}

Zanc is a brown-haired [[Mogma]] who is initially found right before the retracted bridge where [[Impa]] is briefly seen. He proclaims in shock when [[Link]] arrives after just having seen another [[Human]] dressed in black.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Now there's a green one! One of your people just came charging through here. {{Color|SS Red|Dressed all in black}}! That a friend of yours?|Zanc|SS}}</ref> 

Zanc can also be found in the same {{Term|SS|Cave|link}} as the [[Thrill Digger]] minigame. For a fee of 20 [[Rupee]]s, he will give Link "top-secret info."<ref>{{Cite|Pssst. You want some {{Color|SS Red|top-secret info}}? It's guaranteed to make you rich! R-I-C-H. [...] I've got a super-discount price just for you, buddy. How does {{Color|SS Red|20 Rupees}} sound?|Zanc|SS}}</ref> After payment, Zanc will give Link advice on playing the minigame. He says that there are no bad holes around a Green Rupee, one or two bad holes around a Blue Rupee, three or four bad holes around a Red Rupee, five of six bad holes around a Silver Rupee, and seven of eight bad holes around a Gold Rupee.<ref>{{Cite|You listening? You've gotta remember this. There are {{Color|SS Red|no bad holes}} around a {{Color|SS Red|green Rupee}}. A {{Color|SS Red|blue Rupee}} will have {{Color|SS Red|one or two bad holes}} around it, and a {{Color|SS Red|red Rupee}} will have {{Color|SS Red|three or four}}. A {{Color|SS Red|silver Rupee}} will be surrounded by {{Color|SS Red|five or six bad holes}} and a {{Color|SS Red|gold Rupee}} by {{Color|SS Red|seven or eight}}. Those are the rules!|Zanc|SS}}</ref> Despite the claim that this information is "top-secret," it can be found on the wall near the entrance of the {{Term|SS|Cave}}.

==Nomenclature==
Zanc's name is derived from the mineral {{Wp|zinc}}. In other languages, his name is derived from {{Wp|crystal}}.
