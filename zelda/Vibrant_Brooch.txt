{{Term/Store|Vibrant Brooch|Vibrant Brooches|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 142"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Vibrant Brooch}} are rare {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Fortress|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Training Ground|link}}''', ''"Don't drop the pot!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Vibrant Brooch}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Vibrant Brooch}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair}}''', ''"Only Bombs-no swords!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Vibrant Brooch}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 2,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Vibrant Brooch}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Showstopper|link}} {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 142"/>
