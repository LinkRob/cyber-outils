{{Term/Store|Minish||Series, TMC}} is an ancient tongue spoken only by the {{Term|TMC|Picori|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{TMC}}, [[King Daltus]] sends Link on a quest to find the ''Picori'' in order to learn how to reforge the broken blade known as the [[Picori Blade]], the only means possible to break [[Vaati]]'s curse and save [[Princess Zelda]]. However, when [[Link]] first encounters the Minish in [[Minish Village]], he cannot understand their language and has to visit [[Festari]]<ref>{{Cite|You... seem to be having some trouble with our language, don’t you?|Festari|TMC}}</ref> in the sanctuary in the north of the village. Festari explains that [[Link]] has to obtain and eat the [[Jabber Nut]]<ref>{{Cite|You could use a Jabber Nut. It will allow you to understand our tongue.|Festari|TMC}}</ref> located in the Barrel House in the eastern part of the Village. From this point forward, Link can freely speak to all Minish.

==Trivia==
*It is almost certain that the name "Picori" for the Minish and [[Picori Blade|their sword]] comes from humans misunderstanding what the Minish called themselves. Oddly enough, as [[Ezlo]] points out, the [[Minish Woods]] use their real name, so this discrepancy is odd.<ref>{{Cite|Eh? Who are the Minish? Ah, yes! Silly me! Allow me to explain... You humans call them "Picori," but they refer to themselves as the Minish! Strange how, in the world of humans, only this forest has kept that name...|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>
*It would seem that there are several dialects of the Minish Language, as Ezlo (who is later revealed to be a Minish) admits that even he couldn't understand most of what the Forest Minish said when they encountered Link.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, yes. That was the language of the Minish! I'm afraid it's a little different from the dialect I am most familiar with. I'm afraid I didn't catch most of what they said myself.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> While not outright stated, it can be inferred that the dialects differ according to region, as in human society. 
*The Japanese, German, French, and Italian version of the game use actual words for Minish, just written backwards. For example, in the German version, one of the Minish Link meets when first arriving at the village says: ''"Hcsnem nie!"'', which is just ''"Ein Mensch!" (A human!)'' written backwards. However, Link still needs to obtain the Jabber Nut in order to progress in the game.
