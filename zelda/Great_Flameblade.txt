==Location and Uses==
Great Flameblades are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} made by {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} smiths in an ancient age.<ref name="Inventory"/> Due to their elemental nature, they belong to the {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They appear in the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, and they have a base attack damage of 34. Their additional elemental bonus adds 10 to the damage in a single hit if the they are charged.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Great Flameblades appear in the following locations:
* {{Term|BotW|Ancient Tree Stump|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Coliseum Ruins|link}} (only if all four {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} have been calmed)
* The {{Term|BotW|Docks|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Eldin Great Skeleton|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Lomei Labyrinth Island|link}}

Great Flameblades are among one of the most useful {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} in non-combat scenarios. Upon being swung, a Great Flameblade can turn {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}} into {{Plural|BotW|Campfire|link}}, at which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can rest. Carrying a Great Flameblade also grants {{Term|BotW|Link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}. They will one-shot {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}-related {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Ice Wizzrobe|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, when used against them.

Being made from metal, a Great Flameblade will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if it is equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. They also do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
