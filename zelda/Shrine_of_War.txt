{{Term/Store|Shrine of War|Shrines of War|Series, CoH|plural}} are objects in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|SHRINE OF WAR!|N/A|CoH}}</ref> They were introduced as part of the {{OO}} [[Downloadable Content]] update.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Shrine of War}} appear in {{Plural|CoH|Dungeon|link}} in both {{Term|CoH|Dungeon Mode|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Story Mode — Octavo|link}}. Before it is activated, the shrine will occasionally spawn enemies around it. While active, some enemies are replaced with tougher versions, and the shrine drops [[Boots of Power]] and a [[Ring of Protection]] when activated. The effects of {{Plural|CoH|Shrine of War}} last until the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} is left, the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} {{Term|CoH|Boss|link}} is defeated, or the affected character falls in battle.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, look! A {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! {{Color|CoH Blue|Shrines}} are useful, but they often have a tricky {{Color|CoH Blue|side effect}} that you may not like. Bump into it if you want to take a chance and activate the {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! Shrine effects stay active until you leave the dungeon, defeat the dungeon boss, or are defeated.|Trill|CoH}}</ref>

When destroyed, the shrine drops a lit [[Bomb]], which will explode in 4 beats.
