{{Term|BotW|Frostblade}} are magical swords that were forged in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} with elemental properties.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} struck by their blade while they are glowing blue will be frozen.<ref name="Inventory"/> Equipping a {{Term|BotW|Frostblade}} will lower {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s temperature by the equivalent of one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}, but they are ineffective against {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Climate|linkl}}. {{Plural|BotW|Frostblade}} can commonly be found in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Frostblade}} will one-shot {{Term|BotW|Fire}} elemental {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Fire Wizzrobe|link}}.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Frostblade}} can attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. If they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate}}, they will not burn like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} do.

The reason for the setting is unknown in Tears of the Kingdom, but it was deleted due to [[Fuse]] as a factor outside of the setting
