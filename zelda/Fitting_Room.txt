The {{Term/Store|Fitting Room||Series, TFH}} is the location and interface in {{TFH}} where {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} changes his {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What in fashion's name are you doing, my misguided dahling?! That door leads to le {{Color|TFH Blue|fitting room}}!|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TFH|Fitting Room}} is to the left of the front counter inside {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}, and is occupied by the {{Term|TFH|Stylish Woman|link}} until {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} purchases an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|EEP! This room is occupied! OCC-U-PIED!!|Stylish Woman|TFH}}</ref> From that point onward, {{Term|TFH|Link}} can enter the room by pressing the A button near its curtain. {{Term|TFH|Link}} is not controlled inside the {{Term|TFH|Fitting Room}}; entering it brings up an interface similar to the {{Term|TFH|Catalog|link}} which lets the player select one of the {{Plural|TFH|Outfit}} {{Term|TFH|Link}} owns. Once an {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} is selected, {{Term|TFH|Link}} is shown standing inside the {{Term|TFH|Fitting Room}} as the curtains draw closed. Moments later, the curtains reopen and {{Term|TFH|Link}} strikes a pose in his new {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}.

Whenever {{Term|TFH|Link}} emerges from the {{Term|TFH|Fitting Room}}, the {{Term|TFH|Stylish Woman}} makes a flirtatious comment about his {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}, usually referring to him as some type of food item.<ref>{{Cite|Oh my, what a cute little Kokiri boy! But where's your fairy? ♥|Stylish Woman|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That outfit is simply azorable, my little sushi roll! ♥|Stylish Woman|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's a hot look, lava cake! I'm feeling a bit flush now... ♥|Stylish Woman|TFH}}</ref> When a new {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} is purchased, {{Term|TFH|Link}} will automatically enter the {{Term|TFH|Fitting Room}} and change into it.
