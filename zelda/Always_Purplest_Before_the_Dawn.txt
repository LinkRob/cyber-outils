"{{Term/Store|Always Purplest Before the Dawn||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: Always Purplest Before the Dawn}}<br/>03/16/2021 |author= Nintendo |published= March 16, 2021 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/a5133715/index.html |title= Event: Always Purplest Before the Dawn |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Always Purplest Before the Dawn}}" is an event wherein a greater number of purple {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits of purple-colored characters will be blotting out the Spirit Board!|author= Nintendo |published= March 16, 2021 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/a5133715/index.html |title= Event: Always Purplest Before the Dawn |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
