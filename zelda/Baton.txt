The {{Term/Store|Baton|Batons|Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with [[Element|Lightning]].

==Variations==
The default Baton is the '''Wind Waker''', which is directly based on its [[Wind Waker (Item)|namesake]]. The second form of the Baton is the '''Sacred Baton''', followed by the '''Glorious Baton'''.

The Lv.★ form of the Baton is the '''8-Bit Recorder''', which is modeled after the [[Recorder]] from {{TLoZ}}. It was added to {{HW|-}} with the {{MM|-}} Pack DLC. In {{HW|DE}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Recorder replaces the Liquid Glorious Baton when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Zelda summons eight tornadoes, which she conducts using her Baton and sends toward her enemies ahead of her, closing in on them. With a final flick of the Baton, she creates an updraft that knocks her enemies away. This attack damages enemies in a circular range ahead of Zelda.

==Trivia==
*When Zelda uses her Special Attack, "[[Saria's Song]]" and the "[[Song of Time]]" can be heard in the background.
