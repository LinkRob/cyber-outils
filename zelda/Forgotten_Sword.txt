The {{Term/Store|Forgotten Sword||Series, ALBW}} are recurring {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} and {{Plural|ALBW|Item|link}} in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Forgotten Sword|Camera|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses ==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
After the {{Term|ALBW|Captain|link}} forgets his {{Term|ALBW|Forgotten Sword|display=Sword}} at the {{Term|ALBW|Blacksmith's Forge|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} is tasked with finding him and returning the {{Term|ALBW|Forgotten Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lucky you. Instead of getting an earful, you've got an errand, Link. Take that sword and hurry after the {{Color|ALBW Blue|captain}}.|Blacksmith|ALBW}}</ref> This takes {{Term|ALBW|Link}} to the {{Term|ALBW|Sanctuary|link}}, where he witnesses the {{Term|ALBW|Captain}} locked inside.<ref>{{Cite|Th-th-the doors are shut tight. Can't get them open!|Dampé|ALBW}}</ref> With help from {{Term|ALBW|Dampé|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} takes the {{Term|ALBW|Secret Passage|link}} under the {{Term|ALBW|Graveyard|link}} so he can make it into the {{Term|ALBW|Sanctuary}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dampé once heard the {{Color|ALBW Blue|priest}} talking about a secret way into the {{Color|ALBW Blue|Sanctuary}}, hidden right in my {{Color|ALBW Blue|Graveyard}}.|Dampé|ALBW}}</ref> Once inside, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} discovers that {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} has transformed the {{Term|ALBW|Captain}} into a {{Term|ALBW|Painting|link}}, allowing him to keep the {{Term|ALBW|Forgotten Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a sword! Sort of, anyway. The captain won't mind if you borrow it. Swing it with {{Icon|ALBW B}}|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

The {{Term|ALBW|Forgotten Sword}} does half a {{Term|ALBW|Heart|link}} of damage.{{Fact|ALBW}} It can also be used to cut and break objects such as {{Plural|ALBW|Grass|link}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Jar|link}}, and {{Plural|ALBW|Sign|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, the Forgotten Sword is a massive sword sculpture originally part of the [[Statue of the Eighth Heroine]].<ref>{{Cite|You found the eighth heroine's sword at the summit of the Gerudo Highlands.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is located on [[Gerudo Summit]] between the statue and [[East Gerudo Ruins]], a moderate distance away from the rest of the statue. A [[Lynel]] can be found in the vicinity. A [[Golden Claymore]] is found at the base of the blade.

A photo of the Sword is required to be shown to [[Bozai]] as part of "[[The Forgotten Sword]]" [[Side Quest]].

==={{TotK|-}}===
In {{TotK}}, the Forgotten Sword is still present at Gerudo Summit, and a [[Korok]] can be found on the sword's pommel. However, a [[Frost Gleeok]] lurks in the vicinity, and can easily be provoked by Link if he tries to reach the Korok.
