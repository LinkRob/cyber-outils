==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos Tail}} are {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} that can be dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Lizalfos|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Black Lizalfos|link}}. They are highly valued as ingredients in {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}} due to their ability to continue wriggling after being severed from their body.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos Tail}} can be exchanged for 12 {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} or sold for 28 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. They can also be used to make {{Term|BotW|Green Dye|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} can use {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos Tail}} to enhance the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Set|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Zora Set|link}}.

* Total for '''{{Term|BotW|Soldier's Set}}''': 9 (0, 0, 3, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''{{Term|BotW|Zora Set}}''': 54 (3, 0, 5, 10) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
