{{Term/Store|Harlow||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Harlow|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Harlow}} is a worker at the {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tundra|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to {{Color|BotW Blue|Snowfield Stable}}!|Harlow|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to her, she suggests that he rest in one of their {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}} before journeying into the snow.<ref>{{Cite|If you're planning to head deeper into the snow, I recommend resting in one of our soft beds first.|Harlow|BotW}}</ref> At night, {{Term|BotW|Harlow}} will scrub the {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|display=Stable}} floor, as she can only clean when it is dark out.<ref>{{Cite|I've got to clean while it's still dark... Must clean... |Harlow|BotW}}</ref> During the day, she will sit beside {{Term|BotW|Danton|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} next to the {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|display=Stable}}. However, she will retreat inside if it starts to snow.

{{Term|BotW|Harlow}} is annoyed by the pursuit of power and thinks that there is more to being a hero than having the {{Term|BotW|Sword that Seals the Darkness|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Ugh, why do some people equate the unnecessary pursuit of power with some childish dream of adventure? That sword may be good for slaying monsters, sure, but it's not like a sword alone makes a hero. Ya know?|Harlow|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Danton}} is fascinated by. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} agrees with her, she says that she hopes a kind swordsman like him will be the one to wield the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, if it exists.<ref>{{Cite|[I guess.] If the {{Color|BotW Red|sword that seals the darkness}} or whatever it's called really exists... I hope a kind swordsman like you will be the one to wield it.|Harlow|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells her that he loves adventure, she notes that he willingly ventured all the way to {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Oh, I guess that you did venture all the way out to a stable in the mountains, didn't you...|Harlow|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Harlow}} overhears {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he shows the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} to {{Term|BotW|Danton}}, which she will later admit to.<ref>{{Cite|I overheard what you and Danton were taking about.|Harlow|BotW}}</ref> While she can not do much to help, she promises to keep {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}} clean if {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever needs a place to rest.<ref>{{Cite|I can't do much to help, but...I'll keep this stable clean and tidy, so please come rest here when you need to.|Harlow|BotW}}</ref>
