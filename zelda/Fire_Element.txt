The {{Term/Store|Fire Element||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Fire Element}} is the second of the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}} obtained by {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, found within the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}} on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}. After defeating {{Term|TMC|Gleerok|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|display=Element}} and travels to the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} to infuse it and the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} into the reforged {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}}, turning it into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword (Two Elements)|link}} and granting him the ability to split himself in two.
