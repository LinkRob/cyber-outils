'''Calm''' is a Spell in {{ZA}}.

==Overview==
Calm fires a spread of three small whirlwinds in the direction that [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] is facing, which spread out as they travel. The Spell has the power to calm the elements, and is particularly effective against the [[Cyclone|Waterspouts]] in the [[Shrine of Air]]. It costs 1 [[Rupee]] to use.

The Calm Spell can be purchased from [[Lothar]] at the [[Mobilin's Head Inn]] for 100 Rupees. The Calm Spell is required to access [[Mystery Island]] and its [[Heart Container|Crystal Heart]], as there are Waterspouts blocking the path along the way.
