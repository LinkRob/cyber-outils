{{Plural|BotW|Double Axe}} are {{Plural|BotW|Axe|link}} that have been designed with fighting in mind, though they require practice to use efficiently.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be found either abandoned or being used by {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. As with the {{Term|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Double Axe}} has a damage multiplier when used against {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}, allowing them to be felled in one swing. 

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Double Axe}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. However, they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

Tears of the Kingdom reason for the setup is unknown, but a factor outside the setup was deleted due to [[Fuse]]
