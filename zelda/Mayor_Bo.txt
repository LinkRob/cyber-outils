{{Term/Store|Mayor Bo||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/>

==Biography==
Mayor Bo is the Mayor of [[Ordon Village]] and father of [[Ilia]].<ref>{{Cite|FATHER! How can you be so easy on him? You're the MAYOR!|Ilia|TP}}</ref> He's also friends with Renado, a shaman from  [[Kakariko Village#Twligth%20Princess|Kakariko Village]]. He is first seen in front of the mayor's house by the road that leads up to [[Ordon Ranch]]. If [[Link]] speaks to him on the second or third day, a sequence where an [[Ordon Goat]] comes rushing down the road will be triggered.  

[[File:Bograblink222.jpg|thumb|Mayor Bo wrestling Link]]
When Link fails to reach [[Death Mountain]] due to the [[Goron]]s' strength,<ref>{{Cite|Are you trying to reach the Gorons of Death Mountain? It is far too dangerous, Link! They recognize only strength! A normal person could never persuade them...|Renado|TP}}</ref> he returns to Ordon, where Mayor Bo manages to battle Link in the art of [[Sumo|sumo wrestling]] in his home, as long as Link keeps it a secret. As the two undress and wrestle, Link gets defeated a few times by Bo's massive weight and sheer strength, but eventually Link barely manages to beat Mayor Bo. He tells Link that he defeated the Goron patriarch [[Gor Coron]] using the [[Iron Boots]].<ref>{{Cite|Naw, the secret to beatin' the Gorons...is locked away in that chest.|Mayor Bo|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can probably tell, those boots are made of iron, lad. Whoever wears 'em won't easily be pushed around...even by a Goron. If you're fixin' to fight a Goron, be sure to wear those boots.|Mayor Bo|TP}}</ref> He then gives the boots to Link, which ultimately helps the young hero defeat Gor Coron in a sumo wrestling match, allowing him entrance to the [[Goron Mines]]. Later, when Link speaks to Gor Coron, he will wryly say that it is fortunate that Ilia looks more like her mother instead of Bo.<ref>{{Cite|I must confess, young warrior, I am surprised a lug like Bo could sire such a cute daughter. It is fortunate she resembles her mother so much!|Gor Coron|TP}}</ref>

During the end credits, Bo is seen reuniting with his daughter after she and the children are brought back to the village.

==Nomenclature==
Bo is based on the Norse nickname Bua, which means "to live".<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.behindthename.com/name/bo-1 |site= behindthename.com |title= Give Name BO (1)}}</ref>
