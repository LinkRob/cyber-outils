{{Term/Store|Gepper||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 106}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Gepper}} can be seen in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}. He enters the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|link}} each year, though he has never won.<ref>{{Cite|Every year, I enter the tournament trying to win that sword of his. I haven't won yet, and maybe I never will, but the tournament was awesome!|Gepper|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} is turned to stone and the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}} ends, {{Term|TMC|Gepper}} disappears and is not seen for the remainder of the game.
