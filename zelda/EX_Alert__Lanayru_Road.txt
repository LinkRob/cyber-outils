{{Term/Store|EX Alert: Lanayru Road||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 78 {{Term|HWAoC|EX Vicious Monster Battle|link}} in {{HWAoC}} included in the second [[Downloadable Content]] pack, {{GoR}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Alert: Lanayru Road|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}. It is surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and when defeated, a gate opens to a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos}} {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}}. After capturing it, a gate opens to an area in which the vicious monsters appear. While the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} fights the vicious monsters, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} will appear to fight as well.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the vicious monsters are defeated, and can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of two {{Plural|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Cane Sugar|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Goron Spice|link}}, 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
