{{Term/Store|Mayor Hagen's House||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's House}} is located along the eastern side of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, just south of {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}} and near the exit to {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen|link}} lives within it, as well as two {{Plural|TMC|Town Minish|link}} that have a small home behind the {{Plural|TMC|Mask|link}} that decorate the {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's House|display=House}}'s walls. If {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} dashes into the northern wall of the {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's House|display=House}} with the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}}, the {{Plural|TMC|Mask}} will be knocked loose and break, occasionally yielding {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.

There is also a {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}} inside, and the eastern doorway leads outside to a ladder that descends into the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Underground|link}}.
