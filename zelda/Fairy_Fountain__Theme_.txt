{{Term/Store|Fairy Fountain||Series}} is a recurring song in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref}}

==Overview==
The Fairy Fountain theme first appeared in {{ALttP}}, played on the file selection screen and when near [[Fairy Fountain]]s/[[Great Fairy Fountain]]s. Since then, the song has appeared in every installment of the series, with the exception of {{LA}}, {{OoS}}, {{OoA}}, {{BotW}}, and {{EoW}}. The theme is inspired by the map theme for World 3, {{smw|Water Land}}, from ''{{smw|Super Mario Bros 3}}'' of the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C382wc4StOw Super Mario Bros. 3 - World Map 3: Water Land Theme]</ref> It has become one of the most iconic theme songs in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]], alongside the [[The Legend of Zelda (Theme)|main theme]], [[Princess Zelda]]'s, and [[Ganon]]'s own themes.

==Musical Elements==
This theme is ethereal, with soft, repetitive harmonies. The piece is composed of two main elements: a staccato melody that winds slowly up and down both diatonically and chromatically (due to voice-leading), and a fuller harmony that shifts down a diatonic step after every note of the melody where every eight bars the harmony progression returns back from the top. The form of the theme is simple, being a repeating loop of 'A to A' over and over where 'A' could be seen as the antecedent and 'A' could be seen as the consequent.

== Trivia ==

* The Fairy Fountain theme, composed by [[Koji Kondo]], is rumored to have been influenced by the 1982 Japanese hit song "[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r5PH4RjofGE ''Morning Glory'']" by [[wikipedia:Tatsuro_Yamashita|Tatsuro Yamashita]], from his ''[[wikipedia:For_You_(Tatsuro_Yamashita_album)|For You]]'' album.
