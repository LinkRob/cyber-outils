{{Term/Store|Escorting the Injured||HWAoC}} is a level 44 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Escorting the Injured|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Escorting the Injured}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Bass|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Winterwing Butterfly|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Cold Darner|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}'s objective is to escort a {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} to a Targeted Position on the other side of a long path. At the bottom of the map where {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} and the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} spawn at a fork in the road, only a small group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}} is in their way. The {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} then turns right and approaches a gate, and when he does, more {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}} spawn alongside a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} on the other side. There is also another gate in that area, which closes if {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} enters the area and opens when the {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} is defeated. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} passes through that gate after defeating the {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} spawns.  The {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} suggests taking the other path in the fork in the road and heads in that direction. {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} is then given a notification to avoid conflict with the {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel}}, but can defeat it anyways. The gate in the alternate route, originally closed, opens up, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel}} will not chase the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} or {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} if they leave his vicinity. In the same area as the {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel}}, there is also a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} and a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}}, along with a closed gate that prevents {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} from advancing on that path. {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Lizalfos|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}} are scattered along the alternate route, with a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Lizalfos}} appearing at the start of the path and at another area when {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} reaches it. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} reaches a certain area in the path, a {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox|link}} and a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}} spawn, and a gate behind the {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox}} closes. The gate opens when the {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox}} is defeated. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} reaches a certain area in the path, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel}} spawns that prevents him from moving further. As they fight the {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}} and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} appear at the Targeted Position, and move to help the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel}} from the original route also advances towards the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} if not already defeated. If the first {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel}} was already defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the second one is. If it was not defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} reaches {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}.
