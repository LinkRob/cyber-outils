The {{Term/Store|Great Swords||Series, HW}} are a class of Weapon used by [[Ganondorf]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Darkness}}.

==Variations==
The default form of the Great Swords are the '''Swords of Despair''', which are gigantic, mismatched blades with sinister designs.

The second form of the Great Swords are the '''Swords of Darkness''', which are purple in design and feature more spikes than the Swords of Despair. In {{HW|-}}, they can be obtained from getting an A-Rank on [[Adventure Mode]]'s Adventure Map square F-15 after using an [[Ice Arrow]] [[Item Card]] to find them.

The third form are the '''Swords of Demise''', which are black and red swords with gold hilts and pommels, now bearing several red, horn-like spikes emerging from each blade. In {{HW|-}}, they can be obtained from getting an A-Rank on the Adventure Map square B-8, after using a [[Digging Mitts]] Item Card to find them.

The Lv.★ Great Swords are the '''8-Bit Magical Key''', which are based on the [[Magical Key]] and a [[Key]] from {{TLoZ}}. They were added to {{HW|-}} with the {{TP|-}} Pack DLC and are obtained as a Reward for A-Rank Victory on [[Twilight Map/Palace of Twilight (Hyrule Warriors)#Battle H5|Battle H5]] on the [[Twilight Map]], after using a [[Bomb]] Item Card to find them. In {{HW|DE}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Magical Key replaces the Swords of Renewal when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Ganondorf raises the sword in his right-hand skyward, summoning a bolt of lightning from the sky that electrifies the blade. Several more lightning bolts strike in a line ahead of him which crash into the enemies ahead. He then slashes his sword horizontally, firing the collected lightning ahead in a final, devastating blow.
