{{Term|TWWHD|Cyclops Reef}} is a minor island located west of the {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}}, at D3. It has one round reef in the center, giving it its name. After the {{Term|TWWHD|Helmaroc King|link}} is defeated in {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Warship|link}} and wall-mounted {{Plural|TWWHD|Cannon (Enemy)|link}} will appear at the island. When {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} destroys all of them, a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest|link}} appears containing a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chart|link}} that gives the location of the {{Term|TWWHD|Light Ring Chart|link}}.
