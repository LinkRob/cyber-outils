The {{Term/Store|100th Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|100th Ring<br/>100 rings appraised|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoS|100th Ring}} is received from {{Term|OoS|Vasu|link}} after appraising 100 {{Plural|OoS|Magic Ring|link}}. It can be appraised at {{Term|OoS|Vasu Jewelers|link}} for 20 {{Plural|OoS|Rupee}}. The Ring has no effect when worn. The {{Term|OoS|100th Ring}} can be transferred between games by talking to either the {{Term|OoS|Blue Snake|link}} or the {{Term|OoS|Red Snake|link}}.
