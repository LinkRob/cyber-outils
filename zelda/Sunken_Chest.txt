The {{Term/Store|Sunken Chest|Sunken Chests|Series, TWW}} are recurring [[object]]s in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Sunken Chests Recovered |game= TWW |page= 14}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
Sunken Chests are [[Treasure Chest]]s located on the ocean floor, and they can only be salvaged using an extendable crane. The precise locations of Sunken Chests are nearly impossible to find without the aid of a certain map. Sunken Chests contain [[Sunken Treasure]], which can include [[Rupee]]s, [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]], [[Special Chart]]s, [[Ship Part]]s, and the [[Sand of Hours]].

==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:TWW Sunken Chest recovery.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Link recovering a Sunken Chest with the Grappling Hook in {{TWW|-}}]]
With the [[Grappling Hook]], Sunken Chests can be salvaged from the ocean floor in various corners of the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}}. The precise location of Sunken Chests can be found using [[Treasure Chart]]s. There are eight Sunken Chests that Link is required to unearth, since each of them contains a [[Triforce Shard]], and the location of each Sunken Chest is revealed from its corresponding [[Triforce Chart]].
{{clear|left}}
==={{PH|-}}===
[[File:Salvaged Chest PH.png|thumb|{{PH|-}} model]]
Sunken Chests are located all around the [[World of the Ocean King]] in {{PH}}. They are instead found using [[Treasure Map]]s and salvaged using the [[Salvage Arm]] of the [[SS Linebeck]].
