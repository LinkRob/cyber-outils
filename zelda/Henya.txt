==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= That is {{Color|SS Blue|Henya}}. She is spirited and quick to offer opinions. She cares for the students at the Knight Academy, where she has worked for 25 years.
}}

Henya is an old woman who works in the [[Knight Academy]] as its cook, and has worked there for 25 years. She is married to [[Rusta]].<ref>{{Cite|His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Rusta}}, and he is an elderly dreamer and husband to {{Color|SS Blue|Henya}}, who works at the Knight Academy.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

If Link speaks to [[Fledge]] before the [[Wing Ceremony]], he can help him deliver barrels into the Knight Academy kitchen, and Henya will thank him for doing Fledge's job.<ref>{{Cite|What's all this? I thought I told {{Color|SS Blue|Fledge}} to carry that! Bah, it's probably for the best... That skinny boy would probably have gotten flattened. Say...don't you have some important ceremony or some such today? Hmph! Well, thanks for the help.|Henya|SS}}</ref> If Link breaks any of the [[Pot|pottery]] near her, she will get angry at him and demand he cleans the mess, eventually threatening him if he continues to break them.<ref>{{Cite|What do you think you're doing?! You'd better clean that up!|Henya|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Enough already! You little brat! Do that again, and you'll be mopping soup stains for a week!|Henya|SS}}</ref> If the young hero breaks even more objects near her, she will threaten that she will get him expelled from the Knight Academy.<ref>{{Cite|I've had it! I'm going to have a word with the headmaster about having you expelled!|Henya|SS}}</ref> However, she never follows through on this threat, no matter how many times Link breaks her Pots. 

==Trivia==
*If Link takes a seat at one of the cafeteria tables near her, she will tell him that he missed breakfast and if he wants food, he will have to come back later when lunch is ready.<ref>{{Cite|You're too late for breakfast. We stopped serving hours ago. You'll have to wait for lunch!|Henya|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Henya's name is derived from hen, a female {{Wp|chicken}}. This is further supported by the fact that her husband, Rusta, is named after the {{Wp|rooster}}.
