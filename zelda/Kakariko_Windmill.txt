The {{Term/Store|Kakariko Windmill||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a location in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT|publisher= Nintendo|page= 84}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill}} is used to draw up water from the nearby well.<ref>{{Cite|The windmill draws water up from the depth of the well.|Hylian|OoT}}</ref> To reach the upper level from the outside, {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} must use the {{Term|OoT3D|Longshot|link}} to grapple to a small wooden post located in an opening on the left of the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill|display= Windmill}}. Once inside, he can go up some stairs to find a lone {{Term|OoT3D|Cucco|link}}.

Inside the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can find the {{Term|OoT3D|Phonogram Man|link}} who is trying to come up with a musical theme inspired by it.<ref>{{Cite|I'm a music man who loves to go around and around! Go around and around!!! I'm trying to come up with a musical theme inspired by this windmill...going around and around and around!!!|Phonogram Man|OoT}}</ref> As an adult, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can go inside the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill}} and speak with the {{Term|OoT3D|Phonogram Man}}, who recalls that seven years ago, a kid came and played a strange melody, causing the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill|display= Windmill}} to speed up drastically.<ref>{{Cite|Grrrrrrrrr! I'll never forget what happened on that day, seven years ago! Grrrrrrrrr! It's all that Ocarina kid's fault! Next time he comes around here, I'm gonna mess him up!|Phonogram Man|OoT}}</ref> If {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} shows him the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Phonogram Man}} will teach him the "{{Term|OoT3D|Song of Storms|link}}", which, paradoxically, is the song {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} played for him seven years earlier.<ref>{{Cite|That reminds me of that time, seven years ago! Back then a mean kid came here and played a strange song. It messed up this windmill!|Phonogram Man|OoT}}</ref> {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can then go back to the past and play that same melody inside the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill}}, which will drain the well, allowing entry to its {{Term|OoT3D|Bottom of the Well|link|display= bottom}}.

If {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} enters the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill}} from {{Term|OoT3D|Dampé|link}}'s {{Term|OoT3D|?|link|display= grave}}, he can reach the  {{Term|OoT3D|Piece of Heart|link}} on an upper ledge by jumping on the moving platforms. Alternatively, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can also get it by using the {{Term|OoT3D|Boomerang|link}}.
