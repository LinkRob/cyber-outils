{{Term/Store|Skeleton|Skeletons|Series, OoT, LCT|plural}}, also known as {{Term/Store|Fallen Adventurer|Fallen Adventurers|Series, PH|plural}}<ref>{{Cite|A fallen adventurer... Look Link!|Ciela|PH}}</ref>, are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|OoT, PH, LCT}}

==Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, {{Term|OoT|Link|link}} finds piles of bones and {{Plural|OoT|Skeleton}} in various places. Most are found in graves in the {{Term|OoT|Graveyard|link}}, outside {{Term|OoT|Ganon's Castle|link}}, in the {{Term|OoT|Shadow Temple|link}} and at the {{Term|OoT|Bottom of the Well|link}}. They sometimes give valuable information, and also makes {{Plural|OoT|Fairy|link}} appear if {{Term|OoT|Link}} plays the {{Term|OoT|Song of Storms|link}} next to them.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Plural|PH|Skeleton}} are the piles of bones located mainly in the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}} in {{PH}}. They give {{Term|PH|Link}} hints or information about the floor he is on. The three {{Plural|PH|Skeleton}} in the entrance give {{Term|PH|Link}} hints about the entire {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}}, for a {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}. Many {{Plural|PH|Skeleton}} tell how they died, so that {{Term|PH|Link}} can avoid a similar fate. On a few occasions, {{Term|PH|Ciela|link}} will call {{Term|PH|Link}} over to a {{Term|PH|Skeleton}}, which will give him vital information. These {{Plural|PH|Skeleton}} are identical to the collapsed bodies of {{Term|PH|Stalfos|link}}.

==Other Appearances==

==={{LCT|-}}===
In {{LCT}}, {{Plural|LCT|Skeleton}} appear in the {{Term|LCT|Arbiter's Grounds: Ranger|link}} stage and the {{Term|LCT|Skulltula Forest: Ranger|link}} stage. {{Term|LCT|Link}} can shoot the {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}} with the {{Term|LCT|Crossbow|link}} for one point per hit up to six shots. Upon the sixth shot, the {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}} will break and {{Term|LCT|Link}} will be given 5 points instead of 1, with a total of 10 points. If {{Term|LCT|Link}} shoots the {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}} with a {{Term|LCT|Bomb Arrow|link}}, he will obtain 6 points and destroy the {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}} instantly.

In the {{Term|LCT|Arbiter's Grounds: Ranger}} stage, two {{Plural|LCT|Skeleton}} lie at the back of the path taken by {{Term|LCT|Link}} behind a roasting {{Term|LCT|Bullbo|link}} and a watchtower used by a {{Term|LCT|Bulblin|link}}. In the {{Term|LCT|Skulltula Forest: Ranger}} stage, one {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}} lies behind a {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow|link}} in the back of the forest, while another rests along the left wall of the starting area. The {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}} along the left wall will also make an {{Term|LCT|Orange Rupee|link}} appear when destroyed.
