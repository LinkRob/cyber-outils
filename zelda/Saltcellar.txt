'''Saltcellar''' is an [[item]] in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Saltcellar is an item sold by a [[Shrine Merchant]] in the [[Shrine of Strength]] for 10 [[Rupee]]s. Like the [[Bouquet]], which is sold in the same location, the Saltcellar has no purpose.
