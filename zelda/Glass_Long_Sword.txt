==Location and Uses==
Glass Long Swords are strong yet brittle {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} that can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, dishing out 3 damage to any enemies that get hit by it.<ref name="Obtention"/> As {{Plural|CoH|Long Sword|link}}, they can either be used like a {{Term|CoH|Spear|link}} to damage up to two enemies standing in front of Link, or held out and swung to hit enemies adjacent to Link.  As glass weapons, they cannot be created or infused with any materials, and therefore can only be found as loot from {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or slain enemies.
