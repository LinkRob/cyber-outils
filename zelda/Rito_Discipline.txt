{{Term/Store|Rito Discipline||HWAoC}} is a level 56 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rito Discipline|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Rito Discipline}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}. They are also surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} and a some {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Soldier|link}}, and must complete their task while keeping a {{Term|HWAoC|Rito Captain|link}} safe at the same time. After the {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} are defeated, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} spawn, which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat as well. When one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} is killed, two more spawn and are added to the list of targeted {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. When three are defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian|link}} appear, which have to be defeated as well. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} is defeated, more {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin}} spawn. After 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are killed, a gate opens and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawn. The time limit is also taken away, so the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} are defeated.
