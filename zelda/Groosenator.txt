The {{Term/Store|Groosenator||Series, SS, HW, HWL, HWDE}} is an recurring object in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|I call it...the {{Color|SS Red|Groosenator}}!|Groose|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|To halt The Imprisoned's advance, you need to use {{Color|HWDE Green|The Groosenator}}|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
Created by {{Term|SS|Groose|link}}, the {{Term|SS|Groosenator}} is a railed weapon equipped with a catapult that fires {{Plural|SS|Bomb Flower|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Duh huh huh! Try not to drool on your shirt as you stare at this amazing superweapon I've been working on! I call it...the {{Color|SS Red|Groosenator}}! [...] I don't care how beefy you are--you're bound to stumble for a few moments after taking one of my big bombs to the body!|Groose|SS}}</ref> The {{Term|SS|Groosenator}} is accompanied by tracks that circle the {{Term|SS|Sealed Grounds|link}}, allowing it to freely move upon and aim anywhere. {{Term|SS|Groose}} unveils it to {{Term|SS|Link|link}} when {{Term|SS|The Imprisoned|link}} breaks free for the second time. {{Term|SS|Link}} and {{Term|SS|Groose}} must use it to temporarily stun {{Term|SS|The Imprisoned}}, allowing {{Term|SS|Link}} to land on it and strike the spike on its head. The third time {{Term|SS|The Imprisoned}} breaks free, the {{Term|SS|Groosenator}} will stop it from flying. However, the road to {{Term|SS|Groose}}'s {{Plural|SS|Bomb Flower}} will become blocked by rubble, so {{Term|SS|Link}} must launch himself onto {{Term|SS|The Imprisoned}}'s head to attack the spike and seal it for the final time.<ref>{{Cite|Not good! The road crumbled, and I can't get to the rest of my bomb stash! What do I do?! Link! Come up to the temple, and make your way over here. Hustle up!|Groose|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Link, we're in trouble! I can't get to my stash of bombs! All right... Link! We've got no choice! You've got to get up onto that ugly thing's head! Quick now!|Groose|SS}}</ref>

Whenever {{Term|SS|Link}} needs to use the {{Term|SS|Groosenator}}, the Up Button on the Control Pad must be pressed to set up the shot.<ref>{{Cite|The Groosenator is ready for action! Just press [[File:SS D-Pad Up.png|22px]] to set up the shot!|Groose|SS}}</ref> The machine's position can then be adjusted with the Control Stick while the target is aimed at with the Wii Remote. The A Button is used to fire the {{Term|SS|Bomb Flower}}.<ref>{{Cite|Adjust the machine's position with [[File:SS Control Neutral.png|20px]], and aim at your target with the Wii Remote. Once your shot's set, let that scaly creep have it!|Groose|SS}}</ref> Once {{Term|SS|Link}} fires the {{Term|SS|Groosenator}}, he must wait until {{Term|SS|Groose}} shouts at him to fire once again.<ref>{{Cite|Soon as I'm ready to fire, you'll see my signal appear above [[File:SS D-Pad Up.png|22px]]. Till then, it's up to you and that sword of yours to keep that thing away!|Groose|SS}}</ref>

After {{Term|SS|Link}} has obtained the three parts of the {{Term|SS|Song of the Hero|link}} from the three regions on the {{Term|SS|Surface|link}}, {{Term|SS|Groose}} will offer to transport him to different {{Plural|SS|Mini-Game|link}} found around the {{Term|SS|Surface}} or the {{Term|SS|Sky|link}} using the {{Term|SS|Groosenator}}, which can also serve as a faster alternative to flying on {{Term|SS|Link}}'s {{Term|SS|Loftwing|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This machine of mine can sling you all over the land if you're interested in flying without a Loftwing.|Groose|SS}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Groosenator}} appears in several {{Plural|HWDE|Keep|link}} throughout the {{Term|HWDE|Sealed Grounds (Hyrule Warriors)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Various keeps have weapons useful against The Imprisoned but only if it is immobile.|Fi|HWDE}}</ref> It is used to repel {{Term|HWDE|The Imprisoned|link}} whenever it attempts to destroy an allied {{Term|HWDE|Keep}}.<ref>{{Cite|Master, the enemy is healing itself by magical means. We must attack it with the Groosenator.|Fi|HWDE}}</ref> Unlike in {{SS|-}}, it is stationary and cannot be moved. The {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} are required to travel to the targeted {{Term|HWDE|Keep}} and use the {{Term|HWDE|Groosenator}} to stun {{Term|HWDE|The Imprisoned}}, causing it to fall, leaving it vulnerable to attacks once again.
{{Noncanon|End}}
