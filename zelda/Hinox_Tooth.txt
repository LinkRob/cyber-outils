==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Hinox Teeth are {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Stalnox|link}} upon their defeats. Like other Monster Parts, they can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be used as an ingredient for Brown [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] or exchanged for [[Mon]] at the [[Fang and Bone]].

The [[Fierce Deity Set]] requires Hinox Teeth in order to be upgraded to its second tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
