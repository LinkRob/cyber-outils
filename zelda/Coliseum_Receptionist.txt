The {{Term/Store|Coliseum Receptionist||Series, TFH}},{{Exp Game|TFH}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 305 {{Exp Game|TFH}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Captain||Series, ALBW}},{{Exp Game|ALBW}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref> is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Biography==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
====Hyrule====
The Captain first appears in the [[Blacksmith's Forge]] to collect his new [[Sword]] and [[Shield]] from the [[Blacksmith (A Link Between Worlds)|Blacksmith]] in {{ALBW}}. In the process of examining the Shield, he uncharacteristically forgets about his Sword and leaves the shop without bringing it with him.<ref>{{Cite|The captain left without taking his new sword.|Blacksmith's Wife|ALBW}}</ref><ref name= "Forgot"/> [[Link]] is sent on an errand to return the Sword to the Captain, eventually finding him at the [[Sanctuary (A Link Between Worlds)|Sanctuary]]. [[Dampé]] mentions that the Captain often visits the Sanctuary to talk with the [[Priest]], Seres' father, but is aware that he only visits to have an excuse to see Seres.<ref>{{Cite|Coming and going! Going and coming! The captain stops by here more than anyone... You think he wants to yak with the priest? No, sir - just makin' excuses to see Seres there.|Dampé|ALBW}}</ref> As [[Seres]] leaves to fetch the Captain, her scream is heard and the doors to the Sanctuary are locked. After Link finds another way into the Sanctuary through the [[Secret Passage]], he learns that the Captain and Seres have been turned into [[Painting]]s by [[Yuga]].<ref>{{Cite|Not even the captain stood a chance against him!|Priest|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Not even that captain of yours could stand against my might. For all his spit and polish, he made but a crude doodle on the wall.|Yuga|ALBW}}</ref>

During the ending credits, it is shown that the Captain has been returned to normal following Yuga's defeat, and that Link has returned his Sword to him.

====Lorule====
The Lorulean Captain is found drinking at the [[Milk Bar#A Link Between Worlds|Milk Bar]] in {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}. Like his counterpart, he works for the Royal Family, and seems to be stationed in Thieves' Town. However, with the condition of the land and its people, he stays at the Bar only to complain about the poor state of their kingdom and blame those responsible.<ref>{{Cite|Here I am working for the royal family. As if it weren't their fault things have fallen apart! Look around, kiddo. It's all thieves and creepy masked folks in this village. There's nothin' royal about this place.|Captain|ALBW}}</ref>

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, the Captain gets transferred to [[Hytopia]],<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 42}}</ref> where he is known as the Coliseum Receptionist. At the entrance to the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}}, he appears in the basement hall, where he tells [[Link]] how the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)}} works when he first enters.<ref>{{Cite|A new hero? Excellent! Welcome to the Coliseum! This is where heroes come to clash blades and sharpen their skills. You can have a one-on-one match or a free-for-all with three heroes. You'll use wireless communication to find opponents. Once you're ready, head to the room in the back!|Coliseum Receptionist|TFH}}</ref> When talked to afterward, he will tell Link more about the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)}} and give him battling tips.<ref>{{Cite|If there's anything you want to know about the Coliseum, feel free to talk to me anytime.|Coliseum Receptionist|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Aggressive, aren't you? Very good! Now, which of my three tips would you like to hear?|Coliseum Receptionist|TFH}}</ref> The Coliseum Receptionist also keeps Link's score of how many times he has won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)}}, and evaluates him based on this total.
