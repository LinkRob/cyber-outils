{{Term/Store|Keaton|Keatons|Series, MM, MM3D}} are a recurring race of fox-like creatures in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Anatomy==
Keatons are three-tailed, fox-like creatures whose characteristics are derived from the mythological Japanese fox creatures, {{Wp|kitsune}}, which are nine-tailed foxes. They have yellow fur and their three tails bear black fur on the tips. Keatons are said to recognize one another by the sheen of their tails,<ref>{{Cite|Hee-hee-ho! Hello, child. If you are attempting to fool me, it is impossible. We Keatons can recognize our own by the sheen of our tails.|Keaton|MM}}</ref> indicating subtle differences between each member.

==Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT Keaton Mask Render.png|left|60px]]
{{Main|Keaton Mask}}
No Keatons appear in {{OoT}}, though a [[Keaton Mask]] can be obtained as part of a trading quest. It is the first [[Mask]] obtained from the shop in [[Hyrule Castle Town]], and can be sold to the guard at the far end of [[Kakariko Village]] as a gift for his son. Keaton is said to be a popular character.<ref>{{Cite|Is that a "Kee...something..." character mask? I heard he's very popular recently? He's my boy's favorite. That "Kee...something..." mask... If you don't mind... Will you sell it to me?|Guard|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Keaton's Quiz}}
A single Keaton appears in several locations in {{MM}}, hiding within [[Moving Grass]] and playing tricks on people.<ref>{{Cite|It seems Keaton, the ghost fox, plays tricks in the grass...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> In order to draw out the Keaton, Link must cut at least one clump of Moving Grass wearing the Keaton Mask a before all of the clumps have been destroyed. The Keaton will then appear and offer the [[Keaton's Quiz]] minigame, asking the young [[Hero of Time]] five questions about people and locations in {{MM|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|Answer me this... Pick one of the three choices...|Keaton|MM}}</ref> The Keaton appears in all locations in Termina that contain Moving Grass, such as [[North Clock Town]], [[Mountain Village]] or along [[Milk Road]], excluding those in the beginning [[Portal]]. If Link attempts to remove the Keaton Mask while a Keaton appears, it will vanish.

== Trivia ==

* Though not outright stated by [[Nintendo]], the Keaton appears to be a cross between [[pokemon:Pikachu|Pikachu]] and a [[pokemon:Ninetales|Ninetales]] from the [[pokemon:Pokémon_Wiki|''Pokémon'']] franchise, the former being its mascot. It also resembles [[starfox:Fox_McCloud|Fox McCloud]] from the ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_(series)|Star Fox]]'' series as well.
* As seen in {{MM}}, the Keaton appears to be one of the wisest characters of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]], among the likes of the [[The Great Deku Tree|Great Deku Tree]].
* Although Keatons are normally friendly, a hostile enemy variant exists in {{TMC}} where they are known as [[Yellow Keaton|Yellow]] and [[Purple Keaton|Purple Keatons]]. Unlike in the [[Nintendo 64]] games, however, these are bipedal and do not have multiple tails.

==Nomenclature==
Keaton seems to be based on the Japanese word, {{Romanize|狐|{{Wp|Kitsune}}}}. It is possible that the Japanese name, {{Romanize|キータン|Kītan}}, is a combination of the first letter in Kitsune, "Ki" (''き''), and "-tan" (''たん'') a childish suffix often used for mascot characters, as would fit with the Keaton Mask.
