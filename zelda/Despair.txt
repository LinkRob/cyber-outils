"{{Term/Store|Despair||Series, BotW}}" is the sixteenth {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Despair}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #16}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Memory}} can be unlocked in a small stretch of woods northeast of the {{Term|BotW|Bottomless Swamp|link}}. It opens up with {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} and [[Princess Zelda]] running through the woods and away from [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]], Link holding Zelda's wrist. It is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}}, and the two of them are covered in mud. {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} suddenly slips, falling from {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s grasp.

Kneeling on the ground, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} asks how it came to this.<ref>{{Cite|How... How did it come to this?|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She reveals that {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} has turned all of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, including the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} against them.<ref>{{Cite|The Divine Beasts... The Guardians... They've all turned against us... It was... Calamity Ganon. It turned them all against us!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Because of this, the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} have become trapped in their {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|And everyone—Mipha, Urbosa, Revali and Daruk... They're all trapped inside those things|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} believes it is all her fault, since she was unable unlock her [[Triforce|sealing power]].<ref>{{Cite|It's all my fault! Our only hope for defeating Ganon is lost all because I couldn't harness this cursed power!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Crying, she states that everything she has done up until now has all been for nothing, and she calls herself a failure.<ref>{{Cite|Everything—everything I've done up until now... It was all for nothing... So I really am just a failure!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} laments that she has failed her friends, {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link|display=Hyrule}}, and {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link|display=her father}}, leaving them all to die.<ref>{{Cite|All my friends... the entire kingdom... my father most of all... I tried, and I failed them all... I've left them...all to die.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

With another sob, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} falls into {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s arms. He holds her, and the {{Term|BotW|Memory}} ends with the sounds of her cries.
