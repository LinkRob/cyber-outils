{{DISPLAYTITLE:Great Sky Island}}

The Great Sky Island is a [[Sky Islands|Sky Island]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].

[[File:F09F58FC-EDFA-485F-B032-51822B5510B9.png|thumb]]

== Characteristics ==
The Great Sky Island is the largest Sky Island in [[Hyrule]]. Its only inhabitants are the [[Construct|Constructs]], [[Chuchu|Chuchus]], the spirit of [[King Rauru|Rauru]], and the [[Light Dragon]]. It is the starting location after Link was transported from the imprisoning chamber at the beginning of the game.
