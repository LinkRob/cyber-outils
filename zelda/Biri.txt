{{Term/Store|Biri|Biri|Series, ALttP, OoT, OoT3D, CoH|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|Enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name= "E 164"/>{{Name Ref|AST, CoH}} Biris, in some games, are split into smaller variants.

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, Red Biris split from [[Red Bari]]s when attacked. They pulse with electricity, so [[Link]] must wait for them to cease in order to attack them and avoid getting shocked. Biri can be easily defeated from afar by using the [[Hookshot]] or by throwing objects, such as [[Pot]]s, at them.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Biri}}<br/>If you touch it, you will be electrocuted!
}}

In {{OoT}}, Biri can be found [[Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly]]. They float around by themselves or by attacking a Bari, which causes it to split into three Biri. They can electrocute Link on contact, so he must use a ranged weapon such as the [[Boomerang]] to attack them. Alternatively, Link can stun them by using a [[Deku Nut]] before slashing them with the Sword.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Stub|OoA}}

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Stub|ALBW}}
In {{ALBW}}, Biri are split into three variants; [[Red Biri]], [[Yellow Biri]] and [[Purple Biri]]. They appear similarly to their appearance in {{ALttP|-}}, splitting from [[Bari]] of their respective color whenever one is killed.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Biri|Electrically charged, this floating creature zaps an area around itself. Its field reaches further in water.<ref name="EoW Biri">{{Cite|Electrically charged, this floating creature zaps an area around itself. Its field reaches further in water.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}} 
Biri are Jellyfish like monsters with the ability to shock enemies in and out of water. Biri can be summoned as Echoes to assist you in combat or with electricity puzzles. An Extra big Biri, Known as a [[Bori]] can be found in the [[Wrecked Ship]] as part of “[[The Zappy Shipwreck]]” quest, with the reward being a [[Fairy Bottle|Fairy bottle]].

The Biri also comes in a [[Dark Echo|Dark]] variant apon coming in contact with a [[Tecuum]].

==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Stub|CoH}}
Biri appear as enemies in {{CoH}}, where they can be found in various caves and dungeons. They move to the beat of the music in a specific pattern, where they can only move diagonally in the shape of a diamond unless blocked by an object, in which they will either stay in one place or continue to move between their given set of tiles. They can electrify their bodies for a total of four beats, stunning and damaging [[Cadence]], [[Link]], [[Yves]], or [[Princess Zelda]] when they make contact with them, and lose their electricity for another four beats before repeating the pattern. 
{{Ambig|End}}
