==Location and Uses==
'''Guardian Potions +''' are an [[Upgrading|upgraded]] version of the regular [[Guardian Potion|Guardian Potions]]. They can be infused from a regular Guardian Potion for a fee of 40 [[Rupee#Skyward%20Sword|Rupees]] at the [[Infusion Shop#Skyward%20Sword|Infusion Shop]] and require the following [[Bug#Skyward%20Sword|Bugs]]: one [[Faron Grasshopper]], two [[Eldin Roller|Eldin Rollers]], three [[Blessed Butterfly|Blessed Butterflies]] and three [[Starry Firefly|Starry Fireflies]].

When consumed, an Guardian Potion + prevents its user from sustaining any damage for 3 minutes.<ref name="Obtain" />
