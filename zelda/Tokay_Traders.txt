The {{Term/Store|Tokay Traders|Tokay Traders|Series, OoA}} is a location in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tokay Traders|Map|OoA}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|OoA|Tokay Traders}} is a trading post found in past-age {{Term|OoA|Labrynna|link}}, run by the {{Term|OoA|Tokay Trader|link}}. The shop is located on [[Crescent Island]] to the northeast, on a small islet connected to the mainland by a bridge. The proprietor of the trading post is a [[Tokay]]. The only two items the shop sells are, in fact, [[Link]]'s [[Power Bracelet]] and [[Roc's Feather]], which were stolen from him by the Tokay when he washed up on the shores of the island.<ref>{{Cite|That's the hoop I found. I'll trade it for 10 Scent Seeds.|Tokay Trader|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I found that feather. You can have it for 10 Mystery Seeds.|Tokay Trader|OoA}}</ref>

Unlike conventional shops, items at Tokay Traders are bought with [[Mystical Seed]]s instead of [[Rupee]]s. If Link does not have Mystical Seeds at the time, he can trade his [[Shovel]] for either the Power Bracelet or the Roc's Feather.<ref>{{Cite|You don't have [Mystical Seeds]? Then I'll trade you for your Shovel.|Tokay Trader|OoA}}</ref> When needed, Link can return the item, take back his shovel, and trade it for the other item as many times as is necessary.<ref>{{Cite|I'll give you your Shovel back if you give me my hoop back.|Tokay Trader|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll give you your Shovel back if you give me my feather back.|Tokay Trader|OoA}}</ref> Doing so allows him to explore the island and collect his items.

Once Link has bought back both items, his inventory will once again be complete. Afterwards, the only item on sale at the shop will be a [[Shield]].<ref>{{Cite|I found this strange board by the coast. 10 Scent Seeds|Tokay Trader|OoA}}</ref>
