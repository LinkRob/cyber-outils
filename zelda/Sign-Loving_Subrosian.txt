The {{Term/Store|Sign-Loving Subrosian||Series, OoS}} is a character from {{OoS}}.<ref name="Sign">{{Cite|Sign-Loving Subrosian's Home|Sign|OoS}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Sign-Loving Subrosian, as the name implies, is a [[Subrosian]] who resides at the eastern part of [[Subrosia]] in a house called "Sign-Loving Subrosian's Home".<ref name="Sign"/> The Subrosian has an obsession with [[Sign]]s and will give [[Link]] the [[Magic Ring#Sign Ring|Sign Ring]] once he has broken 100 Signs to remind him of his "mistake."<ref>{{Cite|Simply divine! Impeccably fine! Gorgeous design! Notably kind! Signs!!! Have you broken any signs! Of course not! Who could? They're so lovely! You and I see eye to eye!|Sign-Loving Subrosian|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I warned you, but now..! How could you be so cold! Prepare yourself! You'll pay! Surprised? You've learned your lesson now! You won't break any more signs. Take this ring to remind you of your mistake! Are you treating signs properly?|Sign-Loving Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> The Ring does not give Link any abilities; it only shows that Link has destroyed 100 Signs.

==Trivia==
*To teach Link his lesson for breaking 100 signs, they flash the start-up screen, giving the impression that the game has been restarted or erased. Afterwards, the game will return to the Sign-Loving Subrosian's home, at which point they will give Link the Sign Ring.
