"{{Term/Store|A Song of Storms||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Song of Storms}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Calora Lake}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Calora Lake|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} singing about {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lightning from the sky...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He is impressed that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was able to make it up so high in spite of the {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Very impressive. Not many could make it up here in such rain.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> though he recognizes that such a feat is feasible for a traveler like {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'd expect no less from a well-worn traveler like yourself.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Even though it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|display=Raining|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}}'s feathers are able to repel the wetness, and his bandonean is designed to withstand moisture.<ref>{{Cite|Me? I stay nice and dry. My feathers repel the rain rather well. And my instrument was designed to withstand extreme moisture.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> His livelihood collecting "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" has prepared him for such a scenario.<ref>{{Cite|When your livelihood revolves around ancient songs, you've got to be prepared for a few raindrops.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

On that topic, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} reveals that he knows an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song}}" pertaining to the area,<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to hear it.<ref>{{Cite|Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will then sing it for him.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's hear it.] Excellent! Without further ado...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The song details a lost hero calling {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} from the sky, at which point a {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} will respond from a {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When a lost hero calls down {{Color|BotW Red|lightning}} from the sky / The monk responds from a {{Color|BotW Red|giant mound}} on high.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Kass}} realizes that there is a puzzle within the lyrics, he thinks it could apply to many things.<ref>{{Cite|There's a puzzle in this somewhere, but as far as I can tell, it could apply to a great many things.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He bids {{Term|BotW|Link}} farewell,<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, may the light shine ever on your path.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which initiates the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must attract the {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm}}'s {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} to the {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound}} with a metal {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}. When it is struck, the {{Term|BotW|Giant Mound|display=Mound}} will shatter, revealing {{Term|BotW|Qukah Nata Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|By attracting a lightning strike, you shattered the large rock and revealed an ancient shrine concealed within.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} activates the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
