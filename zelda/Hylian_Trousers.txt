==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Hylian Trousers are pieces of [[Armor]] that can be obtained from a [[Treasure Chest]] found in the ruins of a building in front of the [[Temple of Time#Breath of the Wild|Temple of Time]] on the [[Great Plateau]]. They can also be purchased from {{Term|BotW|Enchanted|link}} in [[Kakariko Village]] and at the [[Ventest Clothing Boutique]] in [[Hateno Village]]. Hylian Trousers can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and they can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Hylian Trousers, alongside the [[Hylian Hood]] and [[Hylian Tunic]], compose the [[Hylian Set]]. However, the Hylian Trousers consistently appear matched with the [[Champion's Tunic]] in promotional media, in each of [[Link]]'s [[Recovered Memory|Recovered Memories]] and at the end of the game if Link has recovered all of his lost Memories. Similarly, the two aformentioned pieces of Armor are also paired with the Hylian Hood when Link is seen on horseback in promotional media.
