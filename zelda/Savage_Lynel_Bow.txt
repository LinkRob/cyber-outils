{{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} found in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Lynel|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lynel|link}} all use them when fighting. These {{Plural|BotW|Bow}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a group of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are forged with a special steel found at the peak of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}, and they have the strength to pierce thick {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Bow}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Bow}} will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

==Appearances==
The Savage Lynel Bow first appeared in ''The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild''.

The Savage Lynel Bow later appeared in ''The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom''. In {{TotK}}, this item sometimes can be found in treasure chests.
