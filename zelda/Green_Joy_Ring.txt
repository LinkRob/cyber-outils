The {{Term/Store|Green Joy Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Green Joy Ring<br/>Find double Ore Chunks|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]]'', the Green Joy Ring can be found in a cave on [[Mt. Cucco]], just below the [[Spring Banana]] Tree. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it doubles the value of picked up [[Ore]] Chunks.<ref name="Description"/> The Green Joy Ring can be transferred to {{OoA}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
