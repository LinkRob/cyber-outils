{{Term/Store|Warm Milk|Warm Milks|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Warm Milk}}<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Warm Milk}} are a kind of {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Fresh Milk|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Drinking them will heal Link's {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} of some of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. Having one before sleeping will ensure a good night's rest.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can add extra ingredients to Warm Milk, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Milk''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Milk}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. If consumed, all of Link's Hearts will be healed, and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Milk''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Milk}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. When drunk, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be partly refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Milk''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Milk}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. They can be drunk to completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Milk''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Milk}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. These drinks will provide a boost to Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Milk''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Milk}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. One level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}} will be given to Link if he drinks them.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Milk''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Milk}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. If drunk, they will grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Milk''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Milk}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Drinking them will grant Link a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Milk''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Milk}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. Link's defense will be increased when he drinks them.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Milk''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Milk}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When drunk, they'll provide a boost to Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Milk''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Milk}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Make this by heating up some milk. Drink it before bed to ensure a good night's sleep.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. By drinking them, Link will receive one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Chilly"/>
