{{Term/Store|Blin Bling|Blin Blings|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 117"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Blin Bling}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Deku Forest|link}}''', ''"Guard the orb!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Blin Bling}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Buzz Blob Cave|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Blin Bling}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Moblin Base|link}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Blin Bling}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Moblin Base}}''', ''"Transport the orb quickly!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Blin Bling}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Blin Bling}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Forest Temple}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Blin Bling}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Blin Bling}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Loungewear|link}}.<ref name="E 117"/>

==Nomenclature==
"Blin Bling" is a play on the phrase "bling-bling" that also implies they are worn by {{Plural|Series|Moblin|link}}.
