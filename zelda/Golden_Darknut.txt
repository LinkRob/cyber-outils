The {{Term/Store|Golden Darknut|Golden Darknuts|Series, OoS}} is an enemy in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The Golden Darknut, along with three other [[Golden Monsters]], appears around various regions of [[Holodrum]] after talking to a golden robed [[Old Man]] in the water receded cave around the [[Gnarled Root Dungeon]] while it is [[Seasons|Summer]]. The Golden Darknut can be found in the [[Western Coast]] while it is Spring. After defeating it, along with the other Golden Monsters, [[Link]] will receive a [[Magic Ring]] from the Old Man which, when [[Vasu#Appraisal|appraised]], becomes the [[Red Ring]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 17}}</ref>
