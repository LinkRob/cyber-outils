{{Term/Store|Kalle Demos||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is the [[Boss]] of the [[Forbidden Woods]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 186}}</ref> It swallowed [[Makar]] whole after he ventured too far from the [[Forest Haven]] in preparation for the [[Korok Ceremony]] and fell into the Forbidden Woods. The plant-based monster is a giant [[Boko Baba]].

==Characteristics==
Kalle Demos is made up of two integral parts: a vulnerable flower-like head and the multi-armed hanging bulb that not only poses a threat to [[Link]], but shields the flower inside from damage. The bulb has two different types of tentacles: the ones that anchor the creature to the ceiling and those that act as electrified barbs. The latter motivates Link to stay mobile throughout the entire battle. If Link stays in one position for too long, the arms can strike and damage him. 

Link must first snip the tentacles that anchor the boss to the ceiling with the [[Boomerang]], causing it to fall to the ground. The beast then fully outstretches and blooms wide open, exposing the flower inside. Link must run in with his [[Sword]] and hack at the flower, taking full advantage of the boss' [[Critical Point]]. If Link spends too much time attacking the boss during this state, the bulb will close around him and chew him before spitting him out, causing a full [[Heart]] worth of damage. Alternatively, pouring [[Forest Water]] on the exposed flower will defeat Kalle Demos instantly.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author=Cirquet |published= July 11, 2022|retrieved= July 11, 2022|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q32dTKjv8F4|title= Secret Way To Defeat Kalle Demos (Wind Waker)|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author=Fish_waffle64 |published= October 2, 2016|retrieved= October 5, 2016|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaksmndynT8|title= Forest Water kills Kalle Demos (WWHD)|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref>

After Kalle Demos is defeated, Makar thanks Link the Hero of Winds for his heroic service, and the latter yields a [[Heart Container]] before returning to the [[Great Deku Tree]]. 

Kalle Demos is found again in [[Ganon's Tower (The Wind Waker)|Ganon's Tower]] later in the game, as with most other bosses. It must be defeated for a second time in the same fashion to break the seal to the door leading to [[Ganondorf]].
