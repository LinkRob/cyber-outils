{{Term/Store|Doyle||Series, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Doyle}} goes unnamed in {{MM}}.

==Biography==
==={{MM|-}}===
Doyle is seen in the [[Mayor's Residence]] supporting [[Mutoh]] in his argument against Viscen to continue the [[Carnival of Time]].
{{Stub|MM3D}}

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|The Carpenters}}
{{Term|TMC|Doyle}} is a {{Term|TMC|Carpenter|link}} that first appears alongside the other {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter}} repairing the northern entrance to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} is turned to stone by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}. The {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter}} are later found repairing an area in the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}. He mentions the backlog of repair work the {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter}} need to complete due to {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s attack.<ref>{{Cite|That sorcerer was something else! He really tore up the town, so we’ve got a serious backlog of work to do.|Doyle|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Doyle}} can be found in the {{Term|TMC|Carpenters Shop|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} for the remainder of the game. He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? I hope they fit!|Doyle|TMC}}</ref>
