'''Chompfin''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Chompfin|This jagged-toothed bully rules the water, chomping and spinning with a slap of its tail.<ref name="Chompfin">{{Cite|This jagged-toothed bully rules the water, chomping and spinning with a slap of its tail.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Chompfin are [[shark]]-like enemies found in many bodies of water. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Chompfin [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of four.
