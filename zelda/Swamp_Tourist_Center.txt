The {{Term/Store|Swamp Tourist Center||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Please feel free to take our fresh spring water home with you. - Swamp Tourist Center|Sign|MM}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Swamp Tourist Center}} is referred to as the '''Tourist Info Center''' in {{Guide|MM|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Versus |page= 23}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{MM}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
The Swamp Tourist Center is located at the entrance of the [[Southern Swamp]]. [[Koume]] works in the center as the [[Boat Cruise]] attendant, where [[Link]] can make a reservation to ride a Boat Cruise around the Swamp.<ref>{{Cite|Boat cruise reservations are handled at that window over there. If you're interested, go for a ride.|Swamp Tourist Center Guide|MM}}</ref> However, when Link arrives, he finds that Koume has gone missing in the [[Woods of Mystery]].<ref>{{Cite|Have you seen an old hag named Koume who looks just like me? She hasn't come back from gathering medicinal herbs...|Kotake|MM}}</ref>

Once Koume is rescued, she returns to operate the tour. She then gives Link a free ride on the boat for saving her in the Woods. Moreover, she also hands him the [[Pictograph Box]] as part of a promotion the Tourist Center has going on.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Thanks for what you did back there! In a special deal just for you, I'll let you take this cruise for free.|Koume|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ah, yes, we've got a special going on right now, so we're giving this out for free!|Koume|MM}}</ref> While riding on the boat, Link is able to pass the [[Big Octo]]s that block certain areas in the Swamp, allowing access to the [[Deku Palace]]. Once the curse has been lifted from Southern Swamp and the water is no longer poisoned, Koume opens a target shooting minigame.<ref>{{Cite|How about a little game? I'll fly around on my broom, and you try to hit my target?|Koume|MM}}</ref> In it, Link can win a [[Piece of Heart]] by hitting twenty or more targets with his [[Bow]] as Koume flies around the Swamp, although he will fail the minigame if he hits Koume more than five times. In {{MM3D}}, the Piece of Heart is replaced with an empty [[Bottle]].

The Swamp Pictograph Contest also operates from the Tourist Center, but only until the curse on the Woodfall Temple is lifted. The contest is for humans only,<ref>{{Cite|Oh...The pictograph contest we're holding is only for humans. Sorry.|Swamp Tourist Center Guide|MM}}</ref> and only [[Pictograph]]s taken inside the Swamp are accepted.<ref>{{Cite|You can't enter the contest with pictographs that weren't taken in the swamp. It is a nice shot, though...|Swamp Tourist Center Guide|MM}}</ref> If Link shows a picture of either the [[Deku King]] or [[Tingle]] to the [[Swamp Tourist Center Guide]] holding the contest,<ref>{{Cite|The Swamp Tourist Center will not accept pictographs taken outside the swamp. But if you bring a pictograph of the guide's son, he'll give you something nice...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> he will receive a Piece of Heart. Another Piece of Heart can be found on the roof of the Tourist Center. It can only be reached by using the [[Deku Flower]] next to it as [[Deku Link]] after trading the [[Town Title Deed]] for the [[Swamp Title Deed]] with the [[Business Scrub]] outside.
