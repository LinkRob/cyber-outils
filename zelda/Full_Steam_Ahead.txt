"'''Full Steam Ahead'''",{{Exp Game|SSBU}}{{Name Ref|SSBU}} also known as "'''Full Steam Ahead (Spirit Tracks)'''",{{Exp Game|SSBfN3DS, SSBfWU}}{{Name Ref|SSBfN3DS, SSBfWU}} is a [[Song]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Overview==
"Full Steam Ahead" is an arrangement of the music that plays when traversing the land on the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} in {{ST}}.

"Full Steam Ahead" appears as track 9 in disc 1 of ''{{Ssb|Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U ♪—A Smashing Soundtrack—|Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS/Wii U: A Smashing Soundtrack}}''.

==={{SSB4|-}}===
"Full Steam Ahead (Spirit Tracks)" plays in the Spirit Train and Hyrule: Temple Stages in {{SSB4|3DS}} and {{SSB4|Wii U}}, respectively.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
"Full Steam Ahead" plays in any Stage related to {{TLoZ|-}} series in {{SSBU}}.
