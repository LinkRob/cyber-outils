The {{Term/Store|Magic Hags' Potion Shop||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.{{Fact}}{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Magic Hags' Potion Shop}} is referred to as '''Kotake's Potion Shop''' in {{Guide|MM|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Versus |page= 23}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{MM}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
Owned by the sorceress sisters [[Koume]] and [[Kotake]], it is located in the [[Southern Swamp]]. It opens from 6 AM to 6 PM,<ref>{{Cite|Magic Hags' Potion Shop / Preparing for business. / Open 6 a.m. to 6 p.m.|N/A|MM}}</ref> or all day in {{MM3D}}. At first, Kotake will only have the [[Red Potion|Red]] and [[Green Potion]]s for sale, but if [[Link]] brings her a [[Mushroom]], she will be able to make and start selling the [[Blue Potion]].<ref>{{Cite|What's that? You want a Blue Potion, do you? Well, you gave me a mushroom, so I'll give you one for free.|Kotake|MM}}</ref>

The shop itself is shaped like a giant kettle, painted red and suspended on a high platform above a small lake. Kotake generally serves customers in the shop while Koume gathers ingredients, such as Mushrooms, in other areas of the swamp.<ref>{{Cite|Have you seen an old hag named Koume who looks just like me? She hasn't come back from gathering medicinal herbs...|Kotake|MM}}</ref> During the three days that Link repeats in [[Termina]], Koume is attacked by the [[Skull Kid#Majora's Mask|Skull Kid]] and is immobilized in the [[Woods of Mystery]].<ref>{{Cite|I was just busy minding my own business, picking mushrooms, when... BAM! I got hit from behind! That pesky Skull Kid! Did he think an old hag wouldn't recognize him if he hid his face? Oh, ow! To think he's that powerful...and now I can't even move!|Koume|MM}}</ref> If Link encounters her, he can report her status to Kotake in return for a [[Red Potion]],<ref>{{Cite|What's that?!? The Skull Kid got to Koume?!? That's ridiculous... If it's just the Skull Kid, then what harm could he possibly do? Oh!! Well, if that's true, then take this potion to her... This isn't good...|Kotake|MM}}</ref> which he then can use to revitalize Koume. However, if Link does not rescue the hag before a certain time, Kotake will enter the woods on her broomstick in order to locate her missing sister, and Link will be unable to purchase Potions from the shop.
