{{Term/Store|Crystal Skull|Crystal Skulls|Series, TFH|plural}} are {{Plural|TFH|Item|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 120"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Crystal Skull}} are rare {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Dunes|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum|link}}''', ''"Only Bombs-no swords!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Crystal Skull}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Desert Temple|link}}''', ''"Don't pop any balloons!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Crystal Skull}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Desert Temple}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Crystal Skull}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 2,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Crystal Skull}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Spin Attack Attire|link}}.<ref name="E 120"/>
