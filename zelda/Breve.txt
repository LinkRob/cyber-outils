{{Term/Store|Breve||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Breve}} can be seen pacing inside of {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}}. She advises {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} about moving objects, such as bookcases.<ref>{{Cite|Let me tell you something that might come in handy someday. You can move bookcases and stuff. Try to {{Color|TMC Blue|push and pull different things}}... After all, you're young! You should be trying all kinds of new things! Ha!|Breve|TMC}}</ref> She expresses her skepticism about the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}},<ref>{{Cite|It seems the {{Color|TMC Green|king}} has commanded all of the guards to search for magic power. Folks say that this "{{Color|TMC Red|light force}}" will grant any wish, but I don't buy it... I know all kinds of stuff.|Breve|TMC}}</ref> notices the change in {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}'s temperament,<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TMC Green|king}} was always such a nice man, but lately, he's been such a brute... I wonder what happened to him? Hohoho...|Breve|TMC}}</ref> and even posits that a monster has taken over his body.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe a monster crawled in his skin and is parading around as the real {{Color|TMC Green|king}}! Hohohoho!|Breve|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Breve}} also tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} of a secret entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}} in {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}}, warning him that the place is haunted.<ref>{{Cite|You're going to the {{Color|TMC Green|Royal Valley}}? I hear that place is haunted... But there's a secret entrance that will get you there from {{Color|TMC Green|Hyrule Field}}. I mean, that's what I've heard.|Breve|TMC}}</ref> She also hints at the true nature of the {{Term|TMC|Source of the Flow|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TMC Green|source of the flow}}? Well, that's easy. It's talking about water! The water flowing into {{Color|TMC Green|town}} and into the {{Color|TMC Green|lake}} all comes from the same place. Hohohoh...|Breve|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, she despairs the darkness that now covers it, wondering if the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} will save them again.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Green|Hyrule Castle}} is covered in darkness. This is the end. I never imagined something like this would happen in my lifetime. I wonder if the {{Color|TMC Green|Picori}} will show up and help us once again...|Breve|TMC}}</ref>

She is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? OK, but let's be careful about it!|Breve|TMC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Breve}}'s name likely comes from {{Wp|caffè breve}}, a {{Wp|latte}}. {{Term|Series|Breve}}'s Japanese name, {{Romanize|キリマン|Kiriman}}, is another blend of coffee.
