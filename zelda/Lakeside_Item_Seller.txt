The {{Term/Store|Lakeside Item Seller|Lakeside Item Sellers|Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=300 }}</ref> It is the name of two characters in [[Hyrule]] and [[Lorule]], both who run shops near their respective worlds' major lakes.

==Biography==
===Hyrule===
The Lakeside Item Seller in Hyrule owns a shop on the lake front of [[Lake Hylia]]. He is not very social, and does not have much to say if [[Link]] speaks with him. If spoken to again, he expresses dislike being in the area, and finds the "[[Swimmer|sporty fellow]]" and "[[Mother Maiamai|weird creature]] in the cave" outside to be particularly bothersome.<ref>{{Cite|That sporty fellow out there, that weird creature in the cave! Not sure I'm cut out for this line of work.|Lakeside Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref>

===Lorule===
The Lakeside Item Seller in Lorule owns a shop on the lake front of the {{Term|ALBW|Turtle Rock|link}} area. Unlike his Hyrulean counterpart, he is much more sociable and welcomes Link to his store. He has a habit of saying "Ay lliw nihtemos yub", which he believes to be an incantation that will save him and transport him out of Lorule.<ref>{{Cite|Some believe that chanting the worlds “Ay lliw nihtemos yub” is the key to being saved. Saved from what, I ask? High prices?! Still, it’s a catchy incantation. I sort of hope if I say it enough, it’ll transport me out of this world.|Lakeside Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref> He knows about the [[Maiamai]]s, and that they are able to travel between worlds.<ref>{{Cite|Speaking of, have you heard of the Maiamais? They say they can travel between worlds.|Lakeside Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref> He hopes to find one so that he can leave Lorule.<ref>{{Cite|But until I can hitch a ride with one of them weird squidlings...? Ay lliw nihtemos yub... Ay lliw nihtemos yub...|Lakeside Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref>

He is one of several Loruleans to wear a mask, implying that he may be a [[Masked Follower]].

==Trivia==
*The Item Sellers closely resemble the [[Merchant]]s in {{TLoZ}}. The Lorulean Item Seller's incantation, "Ay lliw nihtemos yub", is also "Buy somethin will ya" in reverse, which is a phrase that the Merchants in {{TLoZ|-}} would say.
*The Lorulean Item Seller's mask resembles the [[Shooting Gallery#A Link to the Past|Shooting Gallery]] Keeper from {{ALttP}}.
