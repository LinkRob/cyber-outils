{{Term/Store|Ice Block|Ice Blocks|Series, OoS}}'''s''' are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|OoT3D, MM3D, TPHD}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |publisher= Nintendo |page= 56}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Ice Block}} go unnamed in {{TMC}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Plural|OoT3D|Ice Block}} appear in the {{Term|OoT3D|Ice Cavern|link}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon's Castle|link}} in {{OoT}}. They can be pushed around and climbed on in order to reach elevated areas. {{Plural|OoT3D|Ice Block}} cannot be destroyed, though they can be pushed into bottomless pits.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Plural|MM3D|Blue Chuchu|link}} can be frozen with {{Plural|MM3D|Ice Arrow|link}} to form {{Plural|MM3D|Ice Block}} in {{MM}}. These {{Plural|MM3D|Ice Block}} can then be climbed in order to reach elevated areas. After a period of time, the {{Plural|MM3D|Ice Block}} will defrost and return to being {{Plural|MM3D|Blue Chuchu}}.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Stub|OoS}}
{{Plural|OoS|Ice Block}} appear in the {{Term|OoS|Sword & Shield Maze|link}} in {{OoS}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Stub|TPHD}}
{{Plural|TPHD|Ice Block}} appear in caves and in the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins|link}} in {{TP}}. Unlike other iterations, these {{Plural|TPHD|Ice Block}} contain actual {{Plural|TPHD|Block}} within them. They cannot be melted.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Plural|TMC|Ice Block}} appear in the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}} in {{TMC}}. They can be pushed, sliding along {{Term|TMC|Ice|link}} until they strike another surface; the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets}} utilizes this mechanic in several puzzles where {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must use {{Plural|TMC|Ice Block}} to press down {{Plural|TMC|Floor Switch|link}} or push them out of the way to clear a path. Special {{Plural|TMC|Ice Block}} known as {{Plural|TMC|Key-sicle|link}} contain {{Plural|TMC|Small Key|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Big Key|link}} for the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets}}; they can be melted with {{Plural|TMC|Light Beam|link}}.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Ice Block|Like most ice, this block slides when pushed and floats in water. It also melts in the heat, again like most ice.<ref name="EoW Ice Block">{{Cite|Like most ice, this block slides when pushed and floats in water. It also melts in the heat, again like most ice.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Ice Blocks are frozen blocks that [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can summon after learning their [[Echo]]. They cost one Echo energy to create, slide when pushed, float on water, and melt near heat or fire.
