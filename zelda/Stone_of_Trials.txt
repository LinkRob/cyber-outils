The {{Term/Store|Stone of Trials||Series, SS}} is an item in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Stone of Trials is obtained after completing the fourth and final [[Silent Realm]] located in [[Skyloft]].<ref>{{Cite|Stone of Trials - Proof that you've overcome all the trials set before you. Opens the way to the Triforce.|Inventory|SS}}</ref> The Stone of Trials was created to lead the chosen hero to the [[Sealed Temple|Temple of Hylia]].<ref>"You got the Stone of Trials!

This engraved object was created to lead the chosen hero to the hidden location of the Triforce!"— N/A (''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'')</ref> It is one of a pair,<ref>{{Cite|Congratulations, Master. You have passed all of the trials. The Stone of Trials you just obtained is actually one of a pair, Master. Another similar object with a mark just like the one you hold exists on this island. Combining the two should open the way to the Triforce. I suggest you search for the matching stone.|Fi|SS}}</ref> and when the two are reunited, the way to the [[Sky Keep]] opens beneath the [[Statue of the Goddess]]. The identical stone is lodged in an one-eyed bird statue located on Skyloft near the entrance to the [[Waterfall Cave]]. When [[Link]] inserts the Stone of Trials into the empty eye socket, the bird statue turns around, opening its beak and firing a cannon ball at the Isle of the Goddess. Eventually, the isle begins to shake and the bottom of the island breaks apart, unveiling the Sky Keep. The bird statue then spits out a number of orbs with [[Clawshot]] targets which Link can use to reach the Sky Keep. The statue that holds the other Stone of Trials turns 180 degrees at night, rendering it impossible to open the Sky Keep unless it is daytime.
