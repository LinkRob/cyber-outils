{{Plural|BotW|Fisherman's Shield}} are semi-rare {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}}, similar to the {{Term|BotW|Hunter's Shield|link}}. They are often carried by fishermen for their {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} design, which is said to represent hope for a great catch.<ref name="Inventory"/> This is aided by their light wooden construction, which makes them easier to take onto a boat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

There are five areas where a {{Term|BotW|Fisherman's Shield}} can be found. Two are in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}. One is near {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn Shrine|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Rabella Wetlands|link}}. Another is in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in an enemy camp north of {{Term|BotW|Parache Plains|link}}. There is also in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} on a cliff near {{Term|BotW|Brynna Plain|link}} on the shores of {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Bay|link}}. Finally, {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} gives one out as a reward for completing the {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)|link}} between 2:45 and 3:00.<ref>{{Cite|A shiny new record deserves a shiny new shield. Here y'go!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Fisherman's Shield}} will burn to a crisp if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} like metal {{Plural|BotW|Shield}} would. Finally, they cannot be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
