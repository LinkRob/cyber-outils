{{Term/Store|EX Alert: Hyrule Field||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 78 {{Term|HWAoC|EX Vicious Monster Battle|link}} in {{HWAoC}} included in the first downloadable content pack, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Alert: Hyrule Field|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must capture an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}. Afterwards, a gate in the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} opens to an area with two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} and more {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin}}. They must be defeated to open another gate, which leads to another area in which the vicious monsters spawn. These monsters are a {{Term|HWAoC|Meteo Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Thunder Wizzrobe|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Blizzrobe|link}}. While the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} fights them, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}} also spawn and attack them. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the three vicious monsters are defeated, and can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of two {{Plural|HWAoC|Sapphire|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
