The {{Term/Store|Cucco Girl||Series}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Fact}} She runs the [[Cucco Ranch]] mini-game.

==Biography==
The Cucco Girl runs the Cucco Ranch mini-game south of [[Kakariko Village]] in [[Hyrule]], where contestants must dodge her [[Cucco]]s in a fenced-in area for as long as possible. She will charge [[Link]] a different amount of [[Rupee]]s depending on the difficulty level he chooses; 20 Rupees for the 'Egg' difficulty, 30 Rupees for the 'Chick' difficulty, 50 Rupees for the 'Rooster' difficulty, and 10 Rupees for the 'Endless' difficulty. The Cucco Girl rewards Link with large sums of Rupees each time he successfully completes a difficulty level.<ref>{{Cite|You're no fledgling, that's for sure! The Cuccos are hopping happy! Here's your prize—60 Rupees!|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> After Link finishes the 'Rooster' level, she rewards him with 150 Rupees and a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|That was amazing! I was on the edge of my seat all the way! Oh yeah, the Cuccos asked me to thank you. Here's a gift—150 Rupees!|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref>

In 'Endless' difficulty, the Cucco Girl will reward Link with 100 Rupees if he is able to dodge the Cuccos for at least 100 seconds.<ref>{{Cite|Whooie! That was some spectacular footwork! By the looks of it, you can take on bigger challenges than that. Your prize is 100 Rupees!|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> However if Link manages to reach the 999.99 second limit mark, the Cucco Girl will stop the game and congratulate Link on dodging every Cucco, and gives him the title of Cuccomaster and rewards him with 3,000 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, oh, wowie-wow! You dodged every single Cucco? You know what that makes you? The Cuccomaster! Well, this prize might feel like an insult to someone as incredible as you, but still... Here... 3,00- Rupees!|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> She thanks Link for taking all this time to play with her Cuccos.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for taking all this time to play with my Cuccos! They like you too, I can tell.|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> Shortly after Link receives the title, a Giant Cucco appears outside the ranch, which the Cucco Girl comments that it only appears once in a blue moon.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, look! The Giant Cucco came to meet you as well! He usually doesn't like strangers... The Giant Cucco only comes out once in a blue moon, you know. Go talk to him, why don't you?|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref>
