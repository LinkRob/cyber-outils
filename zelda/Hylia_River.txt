{{Term/Store|Hylia River||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hylia River'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Hylia River is located between [[Hyrule Field]], the [[Lanayru Wetlands]], and [[West Necluda]].

==Nomenclature==
Hylia River is named for [[Hylia]], the Goddess who protected the [[Triforce]].
