The {{Term/Store|Ancient Ruins|Ancient Ruins|Series, LANS, OoS}} are a recurring dungeon in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Ruins}}|Map|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 151 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Ancient Ruins}} were referred to as the {{Term/Store|Southern Face Shrine||LA, LADX}} in all versions of {{LA}} prior to {{LA|NS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 234}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{LA|NS|-}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Overview==
==={{LA|-}}===
====Entrance to the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins|display=Ruins}}====
The eastern side of {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}} houses two related shrines: the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine|link}} in the north, which serves as the sixth {{Term|LANS|Dungeon|link}}, and the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins}} in the south that features a relief depicting the origins of {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island}}. As {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} travels to the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine}} he encounters the {{Term|LANS|Owl|link}}, who suggests he first venture to the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hoot! There are two shrines, one to the north, the other to the south. First, head {{Color|LANS Blue|south}}, where {{Color|LANS Pink|ancient ruins}} speak of the {{Color|LANS Pink|Wind Fish}}... You will learn much there...|Owl|LANS}}</ref>

The region features sandy terrain, with the sand covering a decorated stone path on the ground. Cracked stone walls and broken columns make navigating the area complicated as the path cannot be followed. Many {{Plural|LANS|Armos|link}} statues are situated among the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins|display=Ruins}}, though approaching some of the statues reveal them to in fact be living enemies. {{Plural|LANS|Gel|link}} and {{Plural|LANS|Zol|link}} are also found in the area.

The region can only be accessed from the {{Term|LANS|Shrine Waterfall|link}} region to the north-east. To the south of the region is {{Term|LANS|Animal Village|link}} and the {{Term|LANS|Yarna Desert|link}}, but they are blocked off by cliffs and cannot be accessed from the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins}}.

A set of stairs leading underground can be found by moving one of the {{Term|LANS|Armos}} statues. A {{Term|LANS|Secret Seashell|link}} can be found here, which is replaced with 20 {{Plural|LANS|Rupee|link}} if the {{Term|LANS|Koholint Sword|link}} has already been obtained.

====Themes and Navigation====
[[File:LANS Writing on the Wall Artwork.png|left|thumb|160px|The {{Term|LANS|Writing on the Wall}} in the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins}}]]
The {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins}} is a short, linear {{Term|LANS|Dungeon}} that is guarded by an {{Term|LANS|Armos Knight|link}}. Defeating it will reward {{Term|LANS|Link}} with the {{Term|LANS|Face Key}}, which unlocks the entrance to the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine}}.

At the end of the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins}}, {{Term|LANS|Link}} comes to a room with a relief known as the {{Term|LANS|Writing on the Wall|link}}. The relief is written in a riddle-like tone and reveals the secret of {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island}} and its guardian {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish|link}}. It is seemingly addressed to {{Term|LANS|Link}} directly, and explains that {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island}} is nothing but an illusion dreamed by the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}, all of which will completely vanish as soon as the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}} is awoken.<ref>{{Cite|TO THE FINDER... THE ISLE OF KOHOLINT, IS BUT AN ILLUSION... HUMAN, MONSTER, SEA, SKY... A SCENE ON THE LID OF A SLEEPER'S EYE... AWAKE THE DREAMER, AND KOHOLINT WILL VANISH MUCH LIKE A BUBBLE ON A NEEDLE... CASTAWAY, YOU SHOULD KNOW THE TRUTH! ... ... ... ... What? Illusion?|Writing on the Wall|LADX}}</ref>

The {{Term|LANS|Owl}} appears outside of the {{Term|LANS|Ancient Ruins|display=Ruins}} after {{Term|LANS|Link}} learns of {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island}}'s secret and informs him that only the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}} knows for certain if it is true.

==={{OoS|-}}===
====Entrance to the {{Term|OoS|Ancient Ruins|display=Ruins}}====
The {{Term|OoS|Dungeon|link}} is located in the {{Term|OoS|Tarm Ruins|link}} and holds the {{Term|OoS|Blowing Wind|link}}, the sixth {{Term|OoS|Essence of Nature|link}}. After retrieving the {{Term|OoS|Nurturing Warmth|link}} from the {{Term|OoS|Unicorn's Cave|link}}, {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} receives a telepathic message from the {{Term|OoS|Maku Tree|link}}, who will direct him towards the {{Term|OoS|Tarm Ruins}} in the search of the next {{Term|OoS|Essence of Nature}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nurturing Warmth has brought me strength! I saw a dream of Link inside vast ruins. Perhaps an essence is hidden in some.|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref> If in a {{Term|OoS|Linked Game|link}}, shortly after, a cutscene shows {{Term|OoS|Twinrova|link}} within the {{Term|OoS|Room of Rites|link}}. As the resurrection of {{Term|OoS|Ganon|link}} draws near, the {{Term|OoS|Flame of Destruction|link}} is lit by the chaos and destruction reverberating from {{Term|OoS|Holodrum|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Eee hee hee hee! Despair, Link... Your struggles are fruitless! Decay, seasons! Steal the fruits of the ravaged lands of Holodrum... Raze the earth! Oh ho ho ho! The lands rot, and the Flame of Destruction burns!|Twinrova|OoS}}</ref> At this point, only the {{Term|OoS|Flame of Despair|link}} remains unlit. This turn of events inspires a new sense of urgency to collect the remaining {{Plural|OoS|Essence of Nature}}.

The entrance to the {{Term|OoS|Ancient Ruins}} is found atop {{Term|OoS|Tarm Ruins}}, at the north-westernmost peak. The {{Term|OoS|Dungeon}} itself is a tall tower, and is the highest point in all of {{Term|OoS|Holodrum}}. In order to unlock the gate barring {{Term|OoS|Link}} from the {{Term|OoS|Ancient Ruins}}, he must gather the four {{Plural|OoS|Jewel|link}} scattered across {{Term|OoS|Holodrum}}. Navigating the {{Term|OoS|Tarm Ruins}} requires extensive use of the {{Term|OoS|Rod of Seasons|link}}.

====Themes and Navigation====
The {{Term|OoS|Ancient Ruins}} are five stories high, the most of any {{Term|OoS|Dungeon}} in {{Term|OoS|Holodrum}}. This often necessitates the use of {{Plural|OoS|Trampoline|link}} to go between floors. It features sand-colored floors and walls, creating an {{Wp|Ancient Egypt}}-style sense of design. Near the entrance is an often-revisited room containing a {{Term|OoS|Dial|link}}. There are many sections of flooring throughout the {{Term|OoS|Dungeon}} that crumbles under {{Term|OoS|Link}}'s weight, causing him to plummet to his death if he stands in one spot for too long. There is also a section where a {{Term|OoS|Compactor|link}} begins to close the walls in when {{Term|OoS|Link}} enters, which will result in an instant [[wikipedia:Crushed|Game Over]] should {{Term|OoS|Link}} be caught.

There are many bombable walls throughout the {{Term|OoS|Dungeon}}. One of these walls leads to an unmarked room on the map where a total of 100 {{Plural|OoS|Rupee|link}} are strewn across the floor. Progressing through the {{Term|OoS|Dungeon}} requires extensive use of the {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather|link}}, {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Glove|link}}, and the {{Term|OoS|Magical Boomerang|link}}. The {{Term|OoS|Mini-Boss|link}} of the {{Term|OoS|Dungeon}} is {{Term|OoS|Vire|link}}, {{Term|OoS|General Onox|link}}'s loyal henchman. Using the {{Term|OoS|Boss Key|link}} found within the {{Term|OoS|Dungeon}}, {{Term|OoS|Link}} faces {{Term|OoS|Manhandla|link}} in the {{Term|OoS|Boss Room|link}} on the top floor of the {{Term|OoS|Dungeon}}. After defeating {{Term|OoS|Manhandla}} and obtaining the {{Term|OoS|Heart Container|link}}, a set of {{Plural|OoS|Stair|link}} to the very top of the tower appears. It is here, at the highest point in all of {{Term|OoS|Holodrum}}, that {{Term|OoS|Link}} obtains his sixth {{Term|OoS|Essence of Nature|link}}.
