{{Term/Store|Din's Pearl||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TWWHD|Din's Pearl}}, named after {{Term|TWWHD|Din (Goddess)|link}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Golden Goddess|link}}, is the first of three {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}} that {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} obtains. It is a precious possession of {{Term|TWWHD|Komali|link}}, as it was given to him by his deceased grandmother.<ref>{{Cite|It's so pretty, isn't it? It's called Din's Pearl. My grandma gave it to me.|Prince Komali|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Komali}} wanted to visit {{Term|TWWHD|Valoo|link}} and get the scale needed to grow his wings; however, {{Term|TWWHD|Valoo}} was acting up at the time, upsetting {{Term|TWWHD|Komali}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll tell you what: If you can find me someone who can get past all the obstacles on Dragon Roost to get to Valoo... THEN I'll listen to anything you say!|Prince Komali|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's strange... Holding this calms me down. I forget all the bad things.|Prince Komali|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}, with assistance from {{Term|TWWHD|Medli|link}}, manages to defeat {{Term|TWWHD|Gohma|link}} and calm {{Term|TWWHD|Valoo}} down. {{Term|TWWHD|Komali}} feels that giving the {{Term|TWWHD|Din's Pearl|display=Pearl}} to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} would give him courage, and thus decides to give it to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Giving you the thing I value most will give me the courage I need to stand up to bad things!|Prince Komali|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} later places the {{Term|TWWHD|Din's Pearl|display=Pearl}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Northern Triangle Island|link}} to make the {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}} rise from the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}.
