{{Term/Store|Fruit Pie|Fruit Pies|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fruit Pie}}<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Fruit Pies are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Cane Sugar]], [[Goat Butter]], and any fruit together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. However, {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}} are unable to be used in this recipe, as the resulting dish will be [[Apple Pie]]. Regular Fruit Pie can be made by using either {{Plural|BotW|Palm Fruit|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry|link}}. Eating these pies will restore some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Fruit Pies are used commonly in celebrations in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Different variations of Fruit Pie can be made by using specific fruits or by adding additional ingredients. These variations are:

* '''Hearty Fruit Pie''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Fruit Pie}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}} or other hearty ingredients. When eaten, these pies will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Fruit Pie''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Fruit Pie}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using energizing ingredients. Eating these will refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Fruit Pie''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Fruit Pie}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using enduring ingredients. If eaten, they'll completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Fruit Pie''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Fruit Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}} or other hasty ingredients. Consuming these pies will temporarily boost Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Fruit Pie''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Fruit Pie}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper|link}} or other spicy ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] if they're eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Fruit Pie''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Fruit Pie}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfruit|link}} or other electric ingredients. If consumed, they grant Link one temporary level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Fruit Pie''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Fruit Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}} or other mighty ingredients. These pies temporarily increase Link's attack power when he eats them.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Fruit Pie''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Fruit Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using tough ingredients. Eating these dishes will boost Link's defense for a short period of time.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Fruit Pie''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Fruit Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using sneaky ingredients. When eaten, they'll increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} temporarily.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Fruit Pie''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Fruit Pie}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A celebration isn't a celebration until this fruit-filled crust hits the table!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}} or other chilly ingredients. Eating them will increase Link's [[Heat Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Chilly"/>
