{{Term/Store|Sancrumi|Sancrumies|Series, TFoE|plural}} (pronounced {{IPA|sænˈkɹuːmiː}} {{Respell|san|KROO|mee}})<ref>{{Cite|Now you will see the Sancrumies that prevent your approach to Ganon!|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref> are enemies in {{TFoE}}.

==Characteristics==
Sancrumies are flying, bat-like enemies that swarm the road leading to [[Ganon's Lair]]. Initially Sancrumies are completely invisible, but once [[Link]] has acquired the {{Term|TFoE|Lantern of Vision|link}} from {{Term|TFoE|Gwonam|link}}, they become visible. Their only means of attack is to fly directly at Link. As with most flying enemies in {{TFoE|-}}, they can respawn repeatedly from certain areas.

Sancrumies are vulnerable to the {{Term|Series|Sword|link}} and most other weapons.
