{{Term/Store|Zill||Series, TWW, TWWHD, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 258"/><ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Zill}}
In {{TWW}}, Zill is the younger son of [[Abe]] and [[Rose]] on [[Outset Island]]. The inquisitive boy lets his nose run on purpose, and follows his older brother, [[Joel]], and [[Link]] around persistently. Interestingly, Zill's nose will continue to run even if he is in the water. If Link revisits Outset Island and inquires the status of the other pigs, Zill will obliviously end up exposing his mother's lie about the other pigs fleeing during the Endless Night by claiming something (heavily implied to be the aforementioned pigs) was delicious.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Zill and Friends}}
{{Term|TMC|Zill}} appears in {{TMC}} as a young child in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. He is first seen north of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains a {{Term|TMC|Small Shield|link}}, where he claims that he has been good all year, and is waiting for the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} to come play with him.<ref>{{Cite|So, so, so... I've been good all year? Like, all year long? And you know how the Picori only show themselves if you've been good? And so, so, so I'm just waiting for the Picori to come and play with me.|Zill|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} and returns to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}, {{Term|TMC|Zill}} accompanies the {{Term|TMC|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|link}}, surrounding {{Term|TMC|Link}} until he {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Hurdy-Gurdy Man}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wanna {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}?|Zill|TMC}}</ref> Afterwards, he can be found standing in front of {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}}, wishing he could attend.<ref>{{Cite|Aw... It looks like they're having fun in there. I wish I could play with them... I can't wait until I'm big enough to go to school and have fun ALL the time!|Zill|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} impersonates {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Zill}} and his friends move to the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}, where they run around.<ref>{{Cite|Yaaay! Yaaay!|Zill|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}}, he moves to stand outside {{Term|TMC|Simon's Simulations|link}}, telling {{Term|TMC|Link}} that it is open.<ref>{{Cite|It looks like the simulation arcade is open for business. Simon's here now.|Zill|TMC}}</ref>

When {{Term|TMC|Link}} has encountered the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Zill}} begins wandering north of the {{Term|TMC|Fountain|link}}, claiming that a {{Term|TMC|Ghost|link}} is inside {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Well|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a ghost in the well...|Zill|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the golden {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}}'s spirit in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Zill}} moves to the roof of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}, where he is impressed with the view.<ref>{{Cite|You can see everything from up here...|Zill|TMC}}</ref> Finally, once {{Term|TMC|Link}} acquires the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Zill}} returns to the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn}}, where he runs around with his friends for the remainder of the game.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Wait up! Wait for me!|Zill|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} three times with {{Term|TMC|Zill}},<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Let's do it!|Zill|TMC}}</ref> although he is unique in that the three {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusions}} associated with him can also be performed with a {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}. Each {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}} results in a {{Term|TMC|Lily Pad|link}} appearing in {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}; each one leads to a new area while {{Term|TMC|Link}} is {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized.
