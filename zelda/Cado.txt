{{Term/Store|Cado||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cado|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Cado}} is a member of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link|display=Sheikah Tribe}} who lives in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} with his beloved {{Plural|BotW|Cucco|link}}. He is separated from his ex-wife, {{Term|BotW|Rola|link}},<ref name="Wife"/> but both remain in the {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|display=Village}}. He is a talented archer who normally wears his {{Term|BotW|Phrenic Bow|link}} on his back in addition to the {{Term|BotW|Eightfold Blade|link}} he wears on his hip.

In the {{Term|BotW|Journal of Various Worries|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} recounts the end of his relationship with {{Term|BotW|Rola}}. She asked him to choose between herself and his {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}}, and he chose the {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}}.<ref name="Journal"/> Within, he signs his name as "The Bow Master".

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} and his fellow guard {{Term|BotW|Dorian|link}} will take a defensive stance and demand he identify himself.<ref>{{Cite|You there! Who are you?! How dare you trespass upon {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Impa}}'s abode!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> However, he then notices the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s belt and realizes who the young hero must be.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Is that...a {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}? But that would mean you are... No, it's not possible. Can it be?|Cado|BotW}}</ref> The two relax their stances and {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} apologizes,<ref>{{Cite|Please forgive us for behaving so rudely.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> inviting the young hero inside to see {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Of course we have heard he legends from Lady Impa herself. Please, friend... Go ahead and step inside.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cado}} and {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} will step aside to allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} passage, and {{Term|BotW|Cado}} will again apologize.<ref>{{Cite|Though it was simple ignorance, I must apologize again for my actions.|Cado|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Cado}} admits they are on high alert due to the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link|display=Yiga Clan}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lately the {{Color|BotW Blue|Yiga Clan}} has been stirring up trouble. We are all on high alert. Still... That is no excuse for my behavior.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask about them to learn the history of both the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} and the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}}. {{Term|BotW|Cado}} will describe how their {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} was once used against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}},<ref>{{Cite|We of the Sheikah tribe have long been heralded as a people of great wisdom. Our technology became the key to sealing {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}} away during the Great Calamity, some ten thousand years ago.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> but then was considered too dangerous and forced the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} into exile.<ref>{{Cite|At one point, our technology was praised as the power of the gods...but eventually the people turned on it. Turned on us. Our creations came to be viewed as a threat to the kingdom. The Sheikah became outcasts, forced into exile.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cado}} explains that while some were able to move on and lead normal lives,<ref>{{Cite|Some, like us, chose to cast off our technological advances and strove to live normal lives.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> others were embittered and formed the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=Yiga Clan}}.<ref>{{Cite|Others formed a hatred toward the kingdom that shunned them. These sad souls swore their allegiance to {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}}. They now call themselves the {{Color|BotW Blue|Yiga Clan}}.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He declares that he and {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} are stationed to protect {{Term|BotW|Impa}} against a possible attack from them.<ref>{{Cite|Recently, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Yiga Clan}} has been acting suspiciously... It is our duty to protect Lady Impa.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cado}} asks that {{Term|BotW|Link}} take caution in his travels, as the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}} are likely to wish him harm.<ref>{{Cite|Their sole mission is to eliminate all who stand against {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}}. Please, dear hero...be careful out there.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He reminds the hero not to underestimate them as an opponent.<ref>{{Cite|To the {{Color|BotW Blue|Yiga Clan}}, anyone who so much as feels bitterness toward {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}} is an enemy. Do not underestimate them.|Cado|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has had his first meeting with {{Term|BotW|Impa}}, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} and {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} will begin to take shifts guarding her home. At noon, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} will head home to his {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}}, whom he refers to very affectionately.<ref>{{Cite|I must be going soon. They'll be waiting for me, my little ones...|Cado|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} is utterly shocked and horrified to find that most of the birds have flown away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Sir... Sir Link... My Cuccos...}} {{Big|My precious Cuccos... They haven't come home yet!}}|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He is so dismayed by their absence that he breaks down into tears, explaining that his wife has left him and the {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} are all he has.<ref>{{Cite|Now that my wife wants nothing to do with me, my Cuccos are my only emotional support...|Cado|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cado}} has difficulty holding himself together, startling {{Term|BotW|Link}} with his sudden sobbing.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Waaaah... What do I doooo...}} {{Big|AAAARRRRGH!!}}|Cado|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can try to reassure him that they will return, but he replies that he has been waiting for his wife to return as well.<ref name="Wife"/> Speaking to him again has him despairing over the idea that his {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} have decided to leave him the way his wife did.<ref>{{Cite|Waaah... Sir Link... Could it be that my little Cuccos have stopped loving me just like my wife? My Cuccos... It can't be so... Where are you, my sweet Cuccos... {{Small|My poor Cuccos...}}|Cado|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cado}} breaks down into sobs again, but {{Term|BotW|Link}} can volunteer to search for them to cheer him up.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll go find them!] Really? {{Big|Thank you so much!!}}|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he has 10 {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} in total,<ref>{{Cite|I have {{Color|BotW Red|10}} Cuccos total. Hopefully you'll be able to find them all...|Cado|BotW}}</ref> at which point the "{{Term|BotW|Flown the Coop|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns all of the {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}}, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} will excitedly inform him that they have all returned.<ref>{{Cite|Link!! My Cuccos... My sweet little baby Cuccos... They're all back!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his help and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you so much! Really, I could never thank you enough! This isn't much, but I insist you take it!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> With his {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} back at his side, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} asserts he is ready to face the world again.<ref>{{Cite|I'm ready to get out there again!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> However, he still wishes that {{Term|BotW|Rola}} would come back to him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Though I do wish my wife would come back to me, just like my Cuccos did...}}|Cado|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

After the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} will continue to watch over his {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} until late into the night.<ref>{{Cite|As I was watching my Cuccos... the day just passed me by. Now it's late!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He admits that while he is nervous about leaving them alone, he knows he needs his sleep.<ref>{{Cite|Ever since what happened, I'm afraid to leave them. But I have some work to do tonight, so I'd better go get some rest.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> Even is his sleep, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} mutters to his {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}}, begging them not to leave.<ref>{{Cite|Don't leave me... Little Cucco... *sniffle*|Cado|BotW}}</ref>

Any other time {{Term|BotW|Link}} comes by to see him, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} is okay with his {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} hopping the fence and exploring, mentioning that they can be used to fly.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Link! Because of you, my Cuccos are out there playing happily. So, what do you think? Aren't they special? And to think, you can fly them anywhere!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask him about the fact, and {{Term|BotW|Cado}} will proudly declare that it is true.<ref>{{Cite|Of course! Didn't you know that? A Cucco that can't fly would be...just a glorified chicken! How boring!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He happily describes the feeling of flying with a {{Term|BotW|Cucco}} as the greatest joy in his life.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, the moment when you take hold of a precious Cucco and leap off an edge... Oh, how your stomach flutters! It's truly my greatest joy in life.|Cado|BotW}}</ref>

At the outset of "{{Term|BotW|The Stolen Heirloom|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} explains to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that something awful happened, despite the fact that he and {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} were standing guard the whole time.<ref>{{Cite|Link. We were standing guard the whole time, and yet this awful thing still happened.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He is shocked that they allowed someone to sneak past them.<ref>{{Cite|How could we have let someone sneak past us?|Cado|BotW}}</ref> During the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} is angry that the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom|link}} was stolen and wonders how the thief made it past him and {{Term|BotW|Dorian}}.<ref>{{Cite|That filthy thief! Me and {{Color|BotW Blue|Dorian}} were standing guard the entire time!
