==Features and overview==
The entrance to the {{Term|OoT3D|Hole of "L"}} is located within the [[Forest Training Center]], near the [[Know-It-All Brother]]s' house. It is a small hole in the forest boundary that [[Link]] may crawl through, and leads to a small maze containing a [[Boulder]]. If Link makes his way past the rolling {{Term|OoT3D|Boulder}} and to the end of the area, he will find the [[Kokiri Sword]] inside a [[Treasure Chest]] atop a tree stump.

[[Mido]] requires that Link possess the Kokiri Sword and [[Deku Shield]] to enter the [[Great Deku Tree's Meadow]],<ref>{{Cite|Hey you! "Mr. No Fairy!" What's your business with the Great Deku Tree?
