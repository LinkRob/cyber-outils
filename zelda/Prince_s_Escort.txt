{{Term/Store|Prince's Escort||HWAoC}} is a Level 44 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Prince's Escort|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Prince's Escort}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} except for {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Prime Meat|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to escort {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} to a Targeted Position within eight minutes. {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Keese|link}} will spawn to prevent the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} from succeeding. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} reaches a certain point, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe}} will spawn. When they are all defeated, a gate opens to the Targeted Position, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel|link}} appears. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once it is defeated and {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} reaches the Targeted Position.
