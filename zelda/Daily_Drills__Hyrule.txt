{{Term/Store|Daily Drills: Hyrule||HWAoC}} is a Level 26 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Daily Drills: Hyrule|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Daily Drills: Hyrule}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} except for {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Drumstick|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

Throughout the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat six training opponents within six minutes. Initially, there is only one {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}} on the field, but two more spawn when he is killed. Another one spawns when one of those two are killed, followed by another after one of them is KO’d again. After defeating all five of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}} is defeated.
