{{Term/Store|Fugo||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Fugo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Fugo}} is a {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} who lives in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}. He can be found outside of the blacksmith {{Term|BotW|Rohan|link|display=Rohan's}} house. {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} is training under {{Term|BotW|Rohan}} as his apprentice so that he may one day be his successor.<ref name="Rohan"/>

Even when he is tired, {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} will answer any questions.<ref>{{Cite|It's time I got some sleep...but if ya really need somethin', I don't mind.|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> According to {{Term|BotW|Fugo}}, {{Term|BotW|Rohan}} is a blacksmith who keeps the ancient arts alive.<ref>{{Cite|Master Rohan is a godly blacksmith. He's responsible for keepin' the ancient arts alive!|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> However, while {{Term|BotW|Rohan}} is working, he warns {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to approach him carefully.<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid Master Rohan is hard at work. If you wanna talk to him, be sure to approach him carefully.|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Rohan}} is sleeping, {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} come back the next day to speak to him.<ref>{{Cite|Well, {{Color|BotW Blue|Master}} Rohan is resting now... You should get some sleep at the {{Color|BotW Blue|inn}} or kill some time and come back tomorrow.|Fugo|BotW}}</ref>

As part of his training, Rohan tasked {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} with defeating every {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus|link}} around {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|He's really strict with everyone—me included. In my case, he told me to beat every {{Color|BotW Blue|Igneo Talus}} around Goron City as part of my training.|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> He later explains that Rohan fought every {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus}} to grow stronger when he was young, and he hopes to also reach that same goal.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Master}} fought the Igneo Talus when he was young, and he got stronger that way. Some say he fought every {{Color|BotW Blue|Igneo Talus}} that exists in the world and beat 'em all! Hrm... I hope I'm that amazing one day|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} laments that he cannot get to the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus}} at {{Term|BotW|Darunia Lake|link}},<ref>{{Cite|But I can't get to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Igneo Talus}} at {{Color|BotW Blue|Darunia Lake}}. *siiiigh*|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> and openly wishes that someone else could go in his stead.<ref>{{Cite|If only someone would go in my place...|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> This starts "{{Term|BotW|The Road to Respect|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

Upon defeating the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus}} and returning to {{Term|BotW|Fugo}}, he is surprised to learn that {{Term|BotW|Link}} defeated the monster. {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} then decides to offer Link 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} in exchange for the achievement, and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}. in return.<ref>{{Cite|Whaaaa—?! You say you beat the {{Color|BotW Blue|Igneo Talus}} at {{Color|BotW Blue|Darunia Lake}}?! Hmm...I have an idea. How about ya sell me that achievement for a cool {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}}?|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Fugo}} eagerly plans to tell {{Term|BotW|Rohan}} about his false success.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks! Thanks so much! I can't wait to tell Master about the {{Color|BotW Blue|Igneo Talus}}!|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> However, he cannot work up the courage to tell him nor confide his secret.<ref>{{Cite|I still haven't been able to tell him I defeated the {{Color|BotW Blue|Igneo Talus}} at {{Color|BotW Blue|Darunia Lake}}.|Fugo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I haven't worked up the nerve to tell him yet since I technically didn't defeat it myself.|Fugo|BotW}}</ref> He realizes that he has a long way to go before he learns {{Term|BotW|Rohan}}'s secrets.<ref>{{Cite|I've got a long way to go before he trusts me enough to teach me his real secrets...|Fugo|BotW}}</ref>
