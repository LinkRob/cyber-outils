{{Term/Store|Freezlord||Series, TFH}} is the {{Term|TFH|Boss}} of {{Term|TFH|Snowball Ravine|link}}, which serves as the {{Term|TFH|Sub-Boss}} of {{Term|TFH|Ice Cavern|link}}, in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 178}}</ref> It resembles a giant {{Term|TFH|Freezard|link}}.

==Characteristics==
During the first phase of the battle, {{Term|TFH|Freezlord}} stands in a frozen pond in the center of the Stage, and is covered in icy armor. It will rapidly spin around, before stopping to face a random direction and breathe frigid air, which will hurt and freeze any of the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} who are in its path. The frozen {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can be quickly thawed out by having another {{Term|TFH|Links}} use fire-based {{Plural|TFH|Item}}, such as the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves|link}}, on them. While {{Term|TFH|Freezlord}} spins at full speed, it will be invincible to all attacks. Once it stops spinning, it can be attacked and its armor stripped away by any fire-based attack, such as the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}} or by throwing the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang|link}} into the surrounding {{Plural|TFH|Torch|link}} to create a Fire {{Term|TFH|Boomerang}}. {{Term|TFH|Freezlord}} will hold its position for a few seconds before spinning again, allowing the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to strike it several times. During this time, it will release rolling balls of ice from its mouth which will home in on the nearest {{Term|TFH|Links}}, though if {{Term|TFH|Freezlord}} is hit directly in the mouth by a fire-based attack, it will be immobilized for several seconds. Eventually, it will start to blow cold air around it as it spins.

Once all of its armor has been destroyed, the pond will drain as {{Term|TFH|Freezlord}} enters its second phase. During this phase, it will jump after the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}, smashing the ground and leaving behind pools of water as it lands. These ponds will eventually freeze over and disappear. It will do this three times, before blowing a stream of cold air that pushes it back and across the Stage. It then repeats this attack. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can damage it whenever it lands, again by using the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}} or {{Plural|TFH|Boomerang}} thrown though {{Plural|TFH|Torch}}. As more damage is inflicted, it will start jumping and landing much more quickly until it is finally defeated.
