{{Term/Store|Carben's Letter||Series, ST}}, also called '''From Carben''',<ref>{{Cite|From Carben|Letters|ST}}</ref> is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got {{Color|ST Light Red|Carben's Letter}}!|N/A|ST}}</ref> As its name suggests, it is a {{Term|ST|Letter|link}} from {{Term|ST|Carben|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
Unlike most {{Plural|ST|Letter}} in {{ST}}, which are delivered to {{Term|ST|Link|link}} by the {{Term|ST|Postman|link}}, {{Term|ST|Carben's Letter}} is given directly to 
{{Term|ST|Link}} from {{Term|ST|Carben}} after the two play the {{Term|ST|Lokomo Song|link}} to restore the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} leading to the {{Term|ST|Ocean Temple|link}}. {{Term|ST|Carben}} forgets how to access the {{Term|ST|Ocean Floor|link}} where the Temple awaits,<ref>{{Cite|The way to get down there is... Blast! I can't remember where the entrance is.|Carben|ST}}</ref> but had fortunately written the {{Term|ST|Letter}} to himself as a reminder.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yes, now I remember. I knew I might need this information later... That's why I wrote it down for myself, just in case. Here. Take this with you.|Carben|ST}}</ref> Attached to the {{Term|ST|Letter}} is a map of the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm|link}} with three numbers written at the locations of the statues holding glowing red orbs.

The {{Term|ST|Letter}} reads,
<blockquote>
''Dear Self,''

''I know you'll forget, so I'm leaving you instructions to get to the ocean floor.''

''-- Carben''</blockquote>

While driving the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} toward the statues, {{Term|ST|Link}} passes {{Term|ST|Ferrus|link}} taking photographs of the newly-restored {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)}}.<ref>{{Cite|I heard the ocean Spirit Tracks had returned, so I came to see for myself.|Ferrus|ST}}</ref> If the young hero stops to talk, he will learn from {{Term|ST|Ferrus}} that the statues respond to "big noises", hinting that he must sound the Train's {{Term|ST|Whistle|link}} while driving past them.<ref>{{Cite|They say there are sculptures that react to big noises out there too!|Ferrus|ST}}</ref> Doing so in the order shown in {{Term|ST|Carben's Letter}} opens the pathway to the {{Term|ST|Ocean Floor}}.
