{{Term/Store|Rutimala Hill||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rutimala Hill|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Rutimala Hill is a flat area covered in snow on the western side of [[Gerudo Highlands]] and directly South of [[Risoka Snowfield]]. Multiple [[Treasure Chest]]s are buried beneath the ground. 

There is an incomplete ring of [[Rock]]s in Rutimala Hill, with a complementary Rock nearby. Once Link places a Rock in the missing space, a [[Korok]] will appear and give Link a [[Korok Seed]].<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
