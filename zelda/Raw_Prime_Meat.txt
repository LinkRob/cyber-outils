==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Raw Prime Meat}} can be obtained by killing {{Plural|BotW|Red-Tusked Boar|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mountain Buck|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mountain Doe|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Water Buffalo|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Snowcoat Fox|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Maraudo Wolf|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Wasteland Coyote|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Cold-Footed Wolf|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Tabantha Moose|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Great-Horned Rhinoceros|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Honeyvore Bear|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Grizzlemaw Bear|link}}. Alternatively, it can be purchased from the travelling merchant {{Term|BotW|Mezer|link}} near {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}}. When eaten, {{Term|BotW|Raw Prime Meat}} heals one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Raw Prime Meat}} can be burned by {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to create {{Term|BotW|Seared Prime Steak|link}} or frozen by {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} to create {{Term|BotW|Icy Prime Meat|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Raw Prime Meat}} is needed to cook {{Term|BotW|Prime Meat Curry|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Prime Meat and Rice Bowl|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Prime Spiced Meat Skewer|link}}. Either {{Term|BotW|Raw Prime Meat}} or a {{Term|BotW|Raw Bird Thigh|link}} is required to make {{Term|BotW|Prime Meat Stew|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Salt-Grilled Prime Meat|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Prime Meat and Seafood Fry|link}}. Among other meats, they can also be used to make {{Term|BotW|Creamy Meat Soup|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Glazed Meat|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Meat and Mushroom Skewer|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Meat Pie|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Meat Skewer|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Copious Meat Skewers|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Meaty Rice Balls|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Monster Stew|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pepper Steak|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Steamed Meat|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin|link}}.
