{{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Aba||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a young girl that appears in {{FPTRR}}. She is the only recognizable [[Hylian]] in the game and is an excellent fighter. She is good friends with the other children in [[Port Town]]: [[Missy (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|Missy]], [[Chiko]], [[Dave]], "Big Bro", and [[Paul]].

==Biography==
[[Tingle]] meets Aba early in his quest in [[Hometown Prairie]] after leaving [[Port Town]]. Aba warns him that he is going to die,<ref>{{Cite|Hey you! Do you know something? Just now, when I sensed you coming... I got a really bad feeling... My intuition is never wrong... I have to warn you... You are going to... ...DIE!|Aba|FPTRR}}</ref> though she immediately retracts this as a joke, and offers to teach Tingle how to fight.<ref>{{Cite|Don't look so scared! I was kidding! All I meant was this is a dangerous place to be strolling round... You really should enroll in "Aba's Battle Class."|Aba|FPTRR}}</ref> If Tingle accepts, she has him fight against a rubber toy for practice.<ref>{{Cite|Let's star you off with a rubber toy! Let battle commence!|Aba|FPTRR}}</ref> When Tingle wins, she instructs him to fight against [[Shiyou Rat]]s and [[Pig (Enemy)#Eyelid Pig|Eyelid Pig]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Good job! It was only a rubber toy but that's the idea. Okay, what's next? Quick! Over here!|Aba|FPTRR}}</ref> After Tingle defeats those, she tasks him with fighting two enemies at once.<ref>{{Cite|Next up you have to battle 2 monsters at the same time! The important thing is footwork! To battle monsters at the same time you need to use the +Control Pad. While you have a smoke cloud around you, move to battle other monsters. You will lose more power but you'll get other things... Don't worry now about what those are... Here come two opponents! Move fast and get them both involved in the battle.|Aba|FPTRR}}</ref>

Aba later appears again in [[Sunshine Seashore]], where she teaches him more advanced battling, and in [[Lon Lon Meadow]], where she informs him about the whereabouts of [[Missy (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|Missy]].

Aba appears her again in the [[Deku Forest (First Continent)|Deku Forest]], where it is revealed that she is the daughter of [[Junglo]], the old forest hero. After saving the [[Great Deku Tree#Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|Deku Tree Sprout]], Junglo tells Tingle that Aba is not actually his daughter, but that he found her as a baby in the arms of her dead mother. Because he did not know what else to do, he raised her as his own. Aba overhears this conversation, however, and becomes furious at Junglo for lying about her past and decides to run away. 

Fortunately, she had thrown away the dagger in her anger first and Tingle finds it just outside of Junglo's house. Tingle brings it to the only person who knows anything about weapons, the [[Armourer]]. When he sees the dagger, however, he immediately insists that Tingle tells him where he got the dagger and, after hearing the answer, runs off to the Deku Forest. 

The Armourer and Aba meet near the [[Cherry Tree Family]] and he reveals that he was, in fact, her father. Years ago, his wife was fed up with his obsession with weapons and fighting. Taking Aba with her, she left him and went to the Deku Forest.

Aba argues with him and the two quickly begin fighting. However, they are evenly matched, and each is impressed at the other's strength. They decide to return to Port Town together. As they leave, Junglo comes to say goodbye to Aba, to which she replies with "Bye, Forest Dad!"
