The {{Term/Store|Lake of Ill Omen||Series, ALttP}} is a location in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 229}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The lake is located in the northeast region of the [[Dark World]], exactly where [[Zora's Lake]] is in the [[Hyrule|Light World]].

[[File:BigCatfish.png|left]]
The lake features a ring of stones where the [[Catfish]] resides. Near the ring, a [[sign]] reads: "Curses to anyone who throws something into my circle of stones." If [[Link]] ignores the sign and throws something into the ring of stones, the Catfish appears and gives him the [[Quake Medallion]].<ref>{{Cite|Was it you who disturbed my peaceful nap? I will give this to you if you go away!|Catfish|ALttP}}</ref> Throwing another item into the ring of stones results in a fireball or a [[Bomb]] being thrown at Link.
