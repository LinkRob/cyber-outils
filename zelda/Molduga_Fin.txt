==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Molduga Fins are {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} that are obtained by defeating {{Plural|Series|Molduga|link}}. They can be used with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be exchanged for [[Mon]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] or used as an ingredient for Gray [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The [[Sand Boots]] require Molduga Fins in order to be upgraded to their first and second tiers at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Sand Boots''': 15 (5, 10, 0, 0)
