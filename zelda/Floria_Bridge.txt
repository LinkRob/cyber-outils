{{Term/Store|Floria Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Floria Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Floria Bridge is located in [[West Necluda]]. On either end are remains of masonry laid down by the ancient {{Term|BotW|Zonai|link}}. However, the span in between these two points, which crosses the breadth of [[Lake Floria]], is made of wooden planks kept together by the boughs of several large trees growing up from the lake's surface. From a platform about midway, one can take in the sight of all ten of the [[Floria Falls]].
