{{Term/Store|Skytail|Skytails|Series, SS}} are enemies in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Skytail final version|book=HH|page= 39}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Skytails are giant, winged monsters that live inside large thunderclouds such as the [[Thunderhead]]. They fly in winding, twirling motions, usually in small groups. When they spot [[Link]] flying his [[Crimson Loftwing]], they will congregate on his location and charge at him and his bird, aiming to bite them with their large pincers. Their movements are meandering and unpredictable, making them difficult to avoid and damage. Their weak point is the tip of their tails, which must be hit with a Charge or [[Spiral Charge]] from Link's Loftwing to destroy them.

They are one of the primary dangers during the battle with [[Levias]].

==Nomenclature==
The Skytail's Japanese name, {{Romanize|スカイテール|Sukaitēru}}, contains the word {{Romanize|テール|Tēru}}, which is the Japanese name for [[Moldorm]]s in most games.
