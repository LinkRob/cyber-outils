The {{Term/Store|Goron Stone Memorial||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 400}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Goron Stone Memorial}} is a statue overlooking {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}. It depicts four {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}}: {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} at the top, {{Term|Series|Darmani III|link}} carrying the {{Term|Series|Elder's Son|link}}, and {{Term|Series|Gor Coron|link}}. Originally, it only depicted the latter three, but {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} was added in memorium of his sacrifice during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref name="CaC"/>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} returns to the {{Term|BotW|Boss's House|link}} after rescuing {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}} mentions that, while he means no disrespect to {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}'s legacy, he is eager to send {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} away from {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|I mean no disrespect to {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}}'s legacy, but if I'm not there to give that {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}} a good walloping...|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} expresses confusion over who {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} is,<ref>{{Cite|[Daruk?] Are ya tellin' me ya don't know about {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}}? The Goron {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}}?|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> which causes {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} to tell him to look up at the {{Term|BotW|Goron Stone Memorial}}.<ref>{{Cite|See that statue up there? That's {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}}!|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> Seeing the statue causes {{Term|BotW|Link}} to remember the "{{Term|BotW|Daruk's Mettle|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.

Three {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} are hiding around the {{Term|BotW|Goron Stone Memorial}}. One is found by inspecting the {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}} inside {{Term|Series|Gor Coron}}'s mouth. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can {{Term|BotW|Race (Object)|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}'s head to his outstretched hand in order to find a second {{Term|BotW|Korok}}. The third {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}} by lifting a rock on the path below {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} and {{Term|Series|Darmani III}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} locates these {{Plural|BotW|Korok}}, they will all give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
