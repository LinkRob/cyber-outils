{{Term/Store|Guru-Guru||Series, MM, MM3D, OoS}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM}} & 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{MM|-}}===
Guru-Guru is the [[Gorman Troupe]]'s musician in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Guru-Guru / Musician in the Gorman Troupe|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> Guru-Guru is seen at night in the [[Laundry Pool]] area of [[Clock Town]] playing his Phonograph since he has been kicked out of his room for practicing too loud.<ref>{{Cite|They said I was much too loud when I practiced in my room. They got mad. Sigh...Now I'm sad I'll just stay here all night and away from their pad.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> He is usually a happy person, and long ago was a member of an animal troupe. However, he was jealous of the [[dog]] being the leader of the troupe, who could always get any animal to follow his command.<ref>{{Cite|Dear guest...Long ago, I was in an animal troupe, with dogs and donkeys and such.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Why was the dog the leader?!? Was it 'cause something was wrong with me, sir??? Oh, that dog was an amazing leader! He always had a stellar troupe no matter what animals he had to work with...|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> As such, Guru-Guru stole the dog's mask, called the [[Bremen Mask]].<ref>{{Cite|That's why I stole it... The dog's mask. I stole it.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> He gives Link the mask as a reward for listening to his story and to get rid of his guilt.<ref>{{Cite|But I no longer need it... I give it to my guest.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It seems Guru-Guru, the traveling musician, had the Bremen Mask...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> 

He also appears during the day in the [[Stock Pot Inn]] in the room at the end of the hall.

==={{OoS|-}}===
Guru-Guru is located in the [[Eastern Suburbs]] in {{OoS}}. He can be found standing by his windmill playing his Phonograph, wanting to oil the windmill to make it go faster.<ref>{{Cite|Go around, go around! If I could just oil it, it would go around more quickly...|Guru-Guru|OoS}}</ref> Link can give him the [[Engine Grease]] in exchange for his [[Phonograph#Oracle of Seasons|Phonograph]].<ref>{{Cite|Engine Grease? Will that make it go quickly? Really? Take my Phonograph!|Guru-Guru|OoS}}</ref> This will make the windmill go faster.<ref>{{Cite|Go around, go around! Is it going too fast? |Guru-Guru|OoS}}</ref> After giving Link the Phonograph, Guru-Guru will reveal he had a spare one and resume his normal animation playing it.

==Nomenclature==
His name is a Japanese onomatopoeia for "turning round and round, going around in circles," which also happens to be the {{Term|Series|Phonogram Man}}'s favorite expression in {{OoT|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|Go around! Go around and around and around! What fun! I'm so happy!|Phonogram Man|OoT}}</ref>
