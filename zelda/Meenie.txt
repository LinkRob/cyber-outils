==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Eenie & Meenie}} 
{{Term|TMC|Meenie}} is a farmer that tends to land within the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}} of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. He and {{Term|TMC|Eenie|link}} give their crops to {{Term|TMC|Brocco|link}} to sell in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Howdy, howdy! Come get your fresh veggies, grown by Eenie and Meenie!|Brocco|TMC}}</ref> and occasionally he digs up {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rock and roll! I dug up another {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone piece}} from my field! That rocks!|Meenie|TMC}}</ref>
