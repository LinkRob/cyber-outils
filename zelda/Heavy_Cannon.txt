The {{Term/Store|Heavy Cannon||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Heavy Cannon|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Heavy Cannon}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of two {{Plural|ST|Pearl Necklace|link}}, two {{Plural|ST|Dragon Scale|link}} and one {{Term|ST|Mystic Jade|link}}. Its description reads "This steel cannon has unmatched accuracy. Its only hurdle is its weight."; however, it performs identically to all other {{Plural|ST|Cannon|link}}.

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Heavy Cannon}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Steel Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
