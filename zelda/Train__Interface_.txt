{{Term/Store|Train||Series, ST}} is an interface in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Train|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|ST|Train (Interface)}} screen is a submenu of the {{Term|ST|Collection|link}} screen. It displays the {{Plural|ST|Train Car|link}} in {{Term|ST|Link|link}}'s possession.

The {{Term|ST|Train (Interface)}} submenu is not accessible until {{Term|ST|Link}} obtains the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} from {{Term|ST|Anjean|link}}. When first opened, the bottom screen displays icons for the {{Plural|ST|Engine|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Cannon|link}} (if {{Term|ST|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|ST|Practical Cannon|link}}), {{Plural|ST|Passenger Car|link}}, and {{Plural|ST|Freight Car|link}} (if {{Term|ST|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|ST|Trusty Freight Car|link}}). Tapping an icon brings up the list of owned {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} corresponding to the specified type. Tapping on an item in the list brings up a rotating view of its model accompanied by a three-line description. The currently installed {{Term|ST|Train Car}} is selected by default and is indicated by a swirl on the list item. The list shows 3 {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} at a time and is scrolled using a draggable scrollbar on the right-hand side. The arrows above and below the scrollbar can also be tapped to move the scrollbar up or down one position.

==Trivia==
* When {{Term|ST|Link}} obtains the {{Term|ST|Freight Car}}, the four {{Term|ST|Train Car}} icons overlap their containing border.
