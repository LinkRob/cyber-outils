{{Term/Store|Eye Guard|Eye Guards|Series, LA|plural}} are enemies in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 101}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Eye Guards appear inside [[Dungeon]]s. They spit fireballs at [[Link]] until every other enemy in the room is defeated. An Eye Guard statue also appears as part of a puzzle in {{Term|LANS|Turtle Rock|link}}, in which an [[Arrow]] must be shot into its eye to drop a [[Small Key]].

==Trivia==
*Eyegores are also called Eye Guards in the official {{ALttP&FS}} strategy guide.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP&FS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 25}}</ref>
