{{Term|TWWHD|Eastern Triangle Island}} is an {{Term|TWWHD|Island|link}} of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}, located on square D6. A {{Term|TWWHD|Sign|link}} on this {{Term|TWWHD|Island}} provides directions to {{Term|TWWHD|Bomb Island|link}} to the south.<ref>{{Cite|To reach {{Color|TWWHD Red|Bomb Island}}, head {{Color|TWWHD Red|south}} from here.|Sign|TWWHD}}</ref>  It is part of a trio of triangle-shaped {{Plural|TWWHD|Island}} that in turn form the inner part of a {{Term|TWWHD|Triforce|link}} when lines are drawn on a map connecting each in conjunction with the {{Plural|TWWHD|Island}} that house the {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}}.

The location of {{Term|TWWHD|Northern Triangle Island}} is strategic as a {{Term|TWWHD|Statue|link}} depicting the {{Term|TWWHD|Golden Goddess}} {{Term|TWWHD|Farore (Goddess)|link}} awaits the placement of {{Term|TWWHD|Farore's Pearl}}. When {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} approaches the {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}} while holding {{Term|TWWHD|Farore's Pearl}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}}'s eyes glows a greenish hue and it prompts him to place the {{Term|TWWHD|Farore's Pearl|display=Pearl}} in its hands.<ref>{{Cite|Wandering traveler who seeks the guidepost of the goddesses... Place the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|pearl}} you hold here.|Statue|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You placed {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Farore's Pearl}} in the ancient statue's hands.|N/A|TWWHD}}</ref> Afterwards, it will continue to glow until all three {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl}} have been placed in their respective {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}}'s hands, at which point they will come to life and emit beacons toward the other {{Plural|TWWHD|Triangle Island|link}}. This raises the {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}} from the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* Before all three of the {{Plural|TWWHD|Statue|link}} are activated by their respective {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}}, they are shown with unique characteristics that their counterparts lack. The {{Term|TWWHD|Statue}} of {{Term|TWWHD|Farore (Goddess)|link}} possesses ears, whereas the {{Plural|TWWHD|Statue}} of {{Term|TWWHD|Nayru (Goddess)|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Din (Goddess)|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Southern Triangle Island|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Northern Triangle Island|link}} possess a mouth and nose, respectively. These features correspond to three kinds of Pikmin from the [[pk:Pikmin series|''Pikmin'' series]], as each Pikmin has a feature that the others do not: [[pk:Yellow Pikmin|Yellow Pikmin]] have noses, [[pk:Blue Pikmin|Blue Pikmin]] have mouths, and [[pk:Red Pikmin|Red Pikmin]] have noses.
