The {{Term/Store|Monya Toma Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Monya Toma Shrine}}<br/>Drawing Parabolas}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Drawing Parabolas''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Monya Toma Shrine}} is located east of the {{Term|BotW|Hilltop High Road|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Salari Hill|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is greeted by the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link|display=Ancient Shrine's}} {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Monya Toma|link}}, as he enters.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Monya Toma. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Monya Toma|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Monya Toma Shrine}} consists of one large room. Directly in front of the entrance is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}, and its corresponding {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} is located behind a closed portcullis. The rest of the room consists of three slanted {{Plural|BotW|Launcher|link}}. The third {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} has a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}} at its side, which can only be accessed by following the paths of the first and second {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}}. Hitting the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} will cause the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}'s structure to move.

The second {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} can be aimed towards the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}, though the path is blocked by four {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|display=Boxes}} must be destroyed in order to progress. After correctly aligning the {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can place the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} on the first {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}, which will sent it into the second and third {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}}. From there, it will be sent into the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. Past the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|display=Slot}} is the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Monya Toma}}.

When interacted with, {{Term|BotW|Monya Toma}} will commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for overcoming the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero...}}|Monya Toma|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} grants {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Monya Toma|BotW}}</ref> After giving him the {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb}}, {{Term|BotW|Monya Toma}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Monya Toma|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found on the structure connected to the third {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}. It can be accessed by turning the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} towards the second {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} using the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}}, at which point {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the second {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} to be sent towards it. Inside the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Thunderblade|link}}.
