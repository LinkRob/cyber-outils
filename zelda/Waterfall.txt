==={{TMC|-}}===
Five Waterfalls across [[Hyrule]] have large rocks hidden within them that extend out after [[Link]] [[Kinstone Fusion|Fuses]] [[Kinstone Piece]]s with certain individuals ([[Gale]], [[Gina]], [[Grayblade]], [[Grimblade]], and [[Waveblade]]). The rocks break the flow of water, revealing hidden caves behind the Waterfalls themselves. Three of these caves contain the dojos of three [[Blade Brothers]]; [[Greatblade]], [[Scarblade]], and [[Splitblade]].

The source of all the water in Hyrule is a large Waterfall at [[Veil Falls]].<ref>{{Cite|Well, this waterfall certainly is the source of all Hyrule's water.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>
