{{Term/Store|Steamed Mushrooms|Steamed Mushrooms|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Steamed Mushrooms by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}} with any vegetable, herb, or flower in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. To make their default version, Link can either use {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb|link}}, which offer no status effect, or use ingredients that have contradicting effects. Eating them will heal some of Link's {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These healthy dishes are made by steaming Mushrooms in the leaves of plants.<ref name="Inventory"/>

* '''Hearty Steamed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. If eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Steamed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}} or other energizing ingredients. They can be eaten to refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Steamed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely replenished and overfilled if he eats them.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Steamed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}}, or other hasty ingredients. Consuming them will increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Steamed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}}, or other spicy ingredients. These mushrooms will grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}} when eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Steamed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>rants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}}, or other electric ingredients. Link can eat them to gain one level of {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Steamed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}}, or other mighty ingredients. Eating them will provide a boost to Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Steamed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}}, or other tough ingredients. Link's defense will be increased when he eats them.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Steamed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. By eating them, Link will gain a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Steamed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Steamed Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A healthy vegetable dish achieved by steaming mushrooms in plant leaves.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}}, or other chilly ingredients. They grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}} when he eats them.<ref name="Chilly"/>
