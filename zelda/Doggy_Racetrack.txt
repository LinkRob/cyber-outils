The {{Term/Store|Doggy Racetrack||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Doggy Racetrack|Map|MM3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Doggy Racetrack can be found south of [[Romani Ranch]]. It is run by [[Mamamu Yan]] and is open from 6 AM to 8 PM.<ref>{{Cite|Mamamu Yan's / Doggy Racetrack / 6 a.m. to 8 p.m.|N/A|MM}}</ref> [[Link]] can enter here to watch and bet on [[Dog]] races, earning [[Rupee]] rewards if he bids successfully. A [[Treasure Chest]] containing 50 Rupees can be found on the top of the awning. It can be reached with the [[Hookshot]], planting a [[Magic Bean]] in the soft [[soil]] spot, or by climbing the fence as [[Zora Link]]. The track itself houses 14 Dogs in various fur colors - four white, three beige and gray, two brown, and a single gold and blue.

===Mini-Game Rules===
After accepting to play, Link is instructed by Mamamu Yan to choose any dog from the corral; that is the dog Link will bet for before the race starts. The dog choice can be made significantly easier if Link wears the [[Mask of Truth]] while lifting a dog. This will allow him to read the dog's mind to determine how the dog feels about the day's race. If the dog he chooses places first, he will win triple his bet. If the dog places second, he will win double the bet. For third to fifth places, he will break even. Any place lower wins nothing, and Link loses the Rupees he bet.<ref>{{Cite|If the dog you brought me takes 1st place, you get triple your bet. 2nd place gets double. If the dog you've chosen places 3rd through 5th, you'll break even and get back however much you bet. If your dog does any worse than that, you'll lose whatever you bet.|Mamamu Yan|MM}}</ref>  Winning more than 150 Rupees in one or more bets, without losing a bet in between, will win Link a [[Piece of Heart]].
[[File:MM Dog Yellow Model.png|left|thumb|The yellow/golden Dog is known to win races more easily than average|196x196px]]
The dogs' thoughts start with either "Ruff!" (positive) "Rrr-Ruff!" (neutral), or "Hoo-whine." (negative), and vary each time Link enters the Doggy Racetrack or finishes a race. These determine the dogs' general placement when they race. Dogs thinking something starting with "Ruff" generally finish near the front, those with "Rrr-Ruff!" near the middle, and those with "Hoo-whine." near the end. The precise order the dogs will finish is random, with the exception of two certain dogs - the ones with the unique gold and blue coat.<ref>Explanation https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y1l6Xj4PLEk & Proof https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MXrU5gBagYE</ref>
[[File:MM Dog Blue Model.png|thumb|The blue Dog is known to fail races more easily than average]]
If the golden dog is thinking anything starting with "Ruff," it will almost always win between first to third place (with the exception of "''Ruff, I should do pretty good today.''"), but it is possible, although rare, for it to place sixth or worse, so caution should be exercised in betting even on the golden dog. Once this dog has won after having a "Ruff" thought, it will tend to keep having "Ruff" thoughts until Link leaves the area. The blue dog, on the other hand, will almost always lose very badly no matter what it is thinking — often trailing far behind the others and in rare cases even being lapped by them (resulting it infamous among fans). The other dogs are rather typical and will usually win only if they are thinking a very positive thought (eg. "''Ruff! Oh, yeah! I feel unstoppable! There's no way I'll lose!''").

Mamamu Yan will caution the young [[Hero of Time]] against participating if he is wearing any of the [[Transformation#Majora's Mask|transformation]] [[Mask|Masks]]. She warns that the dogs would be too afraid of [[Goron Link]],<ref>{{Cite|Oh, well, actually, I don't think this is right for you... If the doggies see a face like yours, I think they'd all roll over and play dead!|Mamamu Yan|MM}}</ref> that they would like [[Zora Link]] a bit too much,<ref>{{Cite| Oh...Actually I don't think I can let you play... If the doggies smell you, I don't think I'd be able to pull them away!|Mamamu Yan|MM}}</ref> and that they would have less than friendly ideas for [[Deku Link]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh, well, actually I don't think this is a good idea... I mean, look at you... You know what doggies do to trees, don't you?|Mamamu Yan|MM}}</ref> In each respective case, all the dogs will react to Link's presence accordingly. For Goron Link they will all growl until approached where they run away in fear, for Zora Link they will all follow him lovingly, and for Deku Link the dogs will all attack him (but do not cause damage). If Link attempts to enter as [[Fierce Deity Link]], the dogs do not react to him, like when he is normal.

==Trivia==
*The gambling [[Dog]] races by [[Mamamu Yan]] is similar to real-world [[wikipedia:Horse_racing|horse racing]].
**There are actual professional dog races in reality, though in that case, specific breeds are often used for [[wikipedia:Greyhound_racing|Greyhound]] and [[wikipedia:Sled_dog_racing|sled racing]].
*The arch at the entrance is the same arch from [[Lon Lon Ranch]] from {{OoT}}. The Doggy Racetrack itself even resembles the ranch.
*According to the sign in the track, the race is called '''Dog Race 500'''. Presumably, this means the oval is 500 meters (1640 feet) long in distance.[[File:MM blue dog winning race.png|thumb|313x313px|The blue coated Dog winning first place in {{MM|-}}, something which is one of the rarest occurrences in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]]]]
*Contrary to popular belief, it is ''not'' impossible for the blue Dog to legitimately win any races in the Doggy Racetrack, although the chances are astronomically low. As of 2026, there are only two recorded wins for this particular canine; one done through an extremely rare early-finish glitch and the other done through intentional mix-maxing. Both of those known cases were achieved by a YouTuber known as [https://www.youtube.com/@falkush falkush] in 2024 - 24 years after the original release of {{MM|-}}.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y1l6Xj4PLEk</ref>
*The highest possible bet win is 270 [[Rupee|Rupees]], achieved by getting first place while betting 90 Rupees.
*If Link wears the [[Bremen Mask]] and plays the march song with the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]] to make a Dog follow him, only one does so, and it will be the canine nearest to him.
