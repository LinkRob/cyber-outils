{{Term/Store|Dmitri||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dmitri|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} and the owner of the {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}.<ref name="Welcome">{{Cite|Welcome to my {{Color|BotW Blue|stable}}. We deal in all things horse related. We also double as an inn.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> He can be found at the front of {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}}. {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} is also the father of {{Term|BotW|Kaifa|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Jana|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Gleema|link}}.<ref name="Father"/><ref name="Gleema"/>

If {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}} is the first {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has visited, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} will welcome him and introduce him to the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}.<ref name="Welcome"/> Since he does not recognize {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he asks if he knows how the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} system works.<ref>{{Cite|Huh... I haven't seen your face before. Do you know about the {{Color|BotW Blue|stable}} system?|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says he does not, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} explains that he can board {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} and summon them to any {{Term|BotW|Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|Then allow me to explain. You can board your horse companions with us and then summon them as you please from our network of stables.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> He also tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he can tame a wild {{Term|BotW|Horse}} and register it to make it his companion.<ref>{{Cite|You may have come across {{Color|BotW Red|wild horses}} during your travels... If you catch one and ride it here to register it, it will become your horse companion.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> While each person is limited to five {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, they can be swapped in and out.<ref>{{Cite|One person can board up to five horses, and you're able to swap horses in and out as you please.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he knows how to catch a wild {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Do you...know how to catch a wild horse?|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} instructs him to sneak behind a wild {{Term|BotW|Horse}} and hop on.<ref>{{Cite|[Catching horses?] OK. To catch a wild horse, all you have to do is approach it from behind—slowly, so it doesn't notice you—and then hop on.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> When the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} starts panicking, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} soothe it.<ref>{{Cite|But these are wild animals we're talking about, so if the horse you're riding starts to act up, try soothing it.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> Once it has settled down, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to bring it to {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} to register it.<ref>{{Cite|It will eventually settle down, and when that happens, bring it to me.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> Any wild {{Term|BotW|Horse}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} catches will take time to get used to being ridden, so {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to gently redirect and calm his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} when they veer off course.<ref>{{Cite|It may take a while for wild horses to get used to being ridden. They may change directions or slow down on their own until you earn their trust and respect. If your horse disobeys your direction, you'll want to gently redirect it and then calm it.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} continues interacting with is {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} in this way, his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} will trust him and become more obedient.<ref>{{Cite|And if you continue interacting that way, your horse will naturally begin to trust you and become more obedient.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref>

When spoken to at the front of the {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} will welcome {{Term|BotW|Link}} and ask if he wants to register or take out a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! Welcome to our fine {{Color|BotW Blue|stable}}! Do you want to register a horse? Take one out for a ride?|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} out, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} thanks him for choosing their {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And there you go! Thanks for choosing us for all your horsey needs.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to register a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} without one present, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} will call him a comedian, saying to "quit horsing around" and to find a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... I see we have a comedian in our midst. Quit horsing around, and get around to finding a horse!|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} with a wild {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} asks if he wants to register it or rest for a while.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to our {{Color|BotW Blue|stable}}. If you've come with a wild horse, you can register it with us here. Or if you want to stay for a {{Color|BotW Red|rest}}, please head to the counter inside.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> He explains that {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}} charges a registration fee of 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We charge a registration fee to help support our conservation efforts. The fee also gets you a locally made {{Color|BotW Blue|saddle}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|bridle}} for your horse. The fee is {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}. Is that OK?|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> Once the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} has been registered, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants to board it or take it with him.<ref>{{Cite|Your registration is complete. Would you like to take your new horse with you?|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref>

When attempting to register a {{Term|BotW|Deer|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Bear|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} will scold {{Term|BotW|Link}} and say they only register {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Not a problem. Let me just— Oh, for the love of Hylia... That's not a horse! We only board horses here, stranger. No exceptions.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} insists that {{Plural|BotW|Stalhorse|link}} are monsters, and he will not board it in fear of it eating the other {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|What in Hylia's name?! That's a monster, not a horse! Sorry, but we can't board that...thing here. It might eat the other horses.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to register the {{Term|BotW|Lord of the Mountain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} refuses, believing they will all by cursed if he does so.<ref>{{Cite|Understoo— Uh... Wh-what is that? Can that...THING you're riding be the fabled Lord of the Mountain?! Why in Hylia's name would you bring something like that here? We'll all be cursed!|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} registers {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} starts to ask him to enter a name for his new {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|OK, please enter a name for your new hors—|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> However, he recognizes {{Term|BotW|Epona}} as a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} of legend,<ref>{{Cite|...Wait a minute! Isn't this {{Color|BotW Red|Epona}}, the horse of legend?|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> and he states that there is probably a law against renaming her.<ref>{{Cite|What are you thinking?!<br/>You can't rename a horse of legend! Pretty sure there's a law against that.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> He forces {{Term|BotW|Link}} to name her {{Term|BotW|Epona}}.<ref>{{Cite|So {{Color|BotW Red|Epona}} it is?|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} several tips for taking care of {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, such as instructions on how to tame a wild {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|To catch a wild horse, slowly approach it from behind and hop on—got it?|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} instinctively follow the road, which is great for viewing the scenery.<ref>{{Cite|If you're riding on a road, your horse will just naturally follow the path, as is its instinct. Travel by road if you want to leave the steering up to your steed and just enjoy the scenery!|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> He also tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if he is separated from his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, whistling will cause it to come running, but it will not be able to hear him if he is too far away.<ref>{{Cite|If you get separated from your horse, you can whistle and it will come running! But your horse isn't magic. It can't hear you if you're too far away, so do keep that in mind!|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> In addition, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} gives instructions as to how to navigate a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} that is scared of its surroundings and refusing to move.<ref>{{Cite|If a horse gets spooked or doesn't like where it's at, it might shut down and refuse to move at all. If that happens, you can {{Color|BotW Red|target}} while mounted and then force the horse to {{Color|BotW Red|move step by step}}. Just be sure you don't put your horse in a dangerous situation.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref>

Inside {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} once again welcomes {{Term|BotW|Link}} and asks if he will be staying with them.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome!<br/>Will you be staying with us?|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> He offers regular {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}} for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} and a soft {{Term|BotW|Bed}} for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|A regular bed will cost you {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}. A soft bed is {{Color|BotW Red|40}}.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> When asked, he explains that the soft {{Term|BotW|Bed}} a special bed offered by {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}}, and anyone who uses it will wake up feeling more energized.<ref>{{Cite|The soft bed is a special bed we're proud to offer here at the stable. It costs more than the regular bed, but you'll wake up more energized.|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref>

Before {{Term|BotW|Link}} rests, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} tells him to sleep well.<ref>{{Cite|Sleep well!|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OK. Rest well!|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} wakes up, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri}} wishes him a good morning, day, or evening and hopes he enjoyed his stay.<ref>{{Cite|Good morning.<br/>I hope you enjoyed your stay!|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hope you enjoyed your stay. Have a good day!|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hope you enjoyed your stay. Have a good evening!|Dmitri|BotW}}</ref>
