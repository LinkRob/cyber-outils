The {{Term/Store|Royal Guard Boots|Royal Guard Boots|Series, BotW}} are a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|lik}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Guard Boots}}<br/>These boots are part of the uniform for Hyrule's Royal Guard. They're a fine blend of style and function and a rather rare find.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Guard Boots}}<br/>These boots are part of the uniform for Hyrule's Royal Guard. The bright base of the design exudes nobility. A fine blend of style and function.|Allies|HWAoC}}</ref> They are exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Boots}} can be found as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Royal Guard Rumors|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. As part of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is required to read the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V4|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor has it a Royal Guard uniform can still be found at Hyrule Castle.<br/><br/>Apparently {{Color|BotW Red|Riverside Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare outfit.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> In this {{Term|BotW|Book|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} claims that pieces of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Set|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall|link}}, near the {{Term|BotW|Guard's Chamber|link}}, and on the second floor.<ref>{{Cite|Amongst {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule's soldiers}}, only the best of the best had the honor of guarding the royal family. Apparently, the equipment they wore can still be found somewhere in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. They say a former maid spoke of treasure near the {{Color|BotW Red|Dining Hall}}, in the {{Color|BotW Red|Guards' Chamber}}, and on {{Color|BotW Red|the second floor}}.|Super Rumor Mill V4|BotW}}</ref>

They {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Boots}} are located in an EX {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|ling}}. After they have been found, {{Term|BotW|Granté|link}} will begin to sell them in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}.{{Note|Prior to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild#Updates|Version 1.3.3 Update]], {{Term|BotW|Granté}} would not sell any {{Term|BotW|Armor}} that is exclusive to the {{Term|BotW|Breath of the Wild Expansion Pass|link}}. This meant that the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Boots}} could not be replaced if sold.}} They {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Boots}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Set}}, along with the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Cap|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Uniform|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 190}}</ref> Wearing the whole set will decrease the amount of {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} required to perform a Charge Attack. The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Boots}} cannot be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}, nor can they be {{Term|BotW|Dye|link|display=Dyed}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}.
