{{Term/Store|Offering Spot|Offering Spots|Series, BotW|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 335}}</ref> also known as '''Food Offerings''',<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 331 |edition= Expanded Edition |year= 2018}}</ref> are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Offering Spot}} are a type of puzzle that conceals a hidden {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}}. They are often marked by {{Plural|BotW|Stone Statue|link}}, but in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Karusa Valley|link}}, these are changed to {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Guardian Deity|link}}. To solve them, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must place the {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} present in other nearby {{Plural|BotW|Offering Spot}} spots into an empty one. Doing so will cause a {{Term|BotW|Korok}} to appear and give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
