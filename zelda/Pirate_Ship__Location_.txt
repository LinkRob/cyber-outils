The {{Term/Store|Pirate Ship||Series, OoS, OoA, OoA (Himekawa)}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |guide= BradyGames |page= 86{{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA |guide= BradyGames |page= 76{{Exp Game|OoA}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoA (Himekawa)}} It is staffed by the {{Term|OoS|Cap'n|link}} and his crew of {{Plural|OoS|Piratian|link}}. 

==Overview==
==={{OoS|-}}===
The well-being of the {{Term|OoS|Pirate Ship (Location)}} directly influences {{Term|OoS|Link}}'s passage to the {{Term|OoS|Graveyard|link}} and the {{Term|OoS|Explorer's Crypt|link}}, the penultimate dungeon in {{OoS|-}}. While sailing to {{Term|OoS|Holodrum}}, a large storm strands the bow of the ship in the {{Term|OoS|Samasa Desert|link}} and the stern in {{Term|OoS|Subrosia|link}}, near the {{Term|OoS|Subrosia Seaside|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Our ship got caught in a storm and sank. When we woke, 'ere we were. Where are we? It all looks so weird.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|'Ave you seen our ship? It's over by the graveyard.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> The {{Term|OoS|Cap'n}} does not give the orders to fix the damaged ship until {{Term|OoS|Link}} finds and polishes the {{Term|OoS|Rusty Bell|link}},<ref>{{Cite| I want to get back to all me mates and dig the ship free, but Cap'n said, "Don't come back 'til ya find me bell"!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! Surely this- Ar! This thing's rusted something awful! What are you pullin'? This won't do! Go get it polished!|Cap'n|OoS}}</ref> a relic from the {{Term|OoS|Cap'n}}'s love, {{Term|OoS|Queen Ambi|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Queen Ambi had a boyfriend--a seafaring man. But he set sail one day and never came back. She's building a tall tower so she can search the lands for any sign of his return.|N/A|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ambi??? Nay, 'tis I that must be dreamin'!!! I never thought we'd meet again after so long! Even now, when I think of our parting, it pains my heart. Though many years 'ave gone by since then, you are as beautiful as ever...|Cap'n|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That bell he got from 'is special someone was so rusty. Aye... I'd be mad, too.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> that was lost in the storm.<ref>{{Cite|It was so stormy that Cap'n lost the bell he got from a special someone. That's a bit o' trouble...|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> {{Term|OoS|Link}} finds the rusted bell in the {{Term|OoS|Samasa Desert}}, has it polished by the {{Term|OoS|Subrosian Smithy|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the Subrosian Smithy! We do the finest work in the land, but we take on only jobs that interest us! He says you hold a bell of great value. He hasn't offered to polish it, but should he?|Subrosian Smithy|OoS}}</ref> and returns it the {{Term|OoS|Cap'n}}. The {{Term|OoS|Cap'n}} motivates his crew to fix the ship's damage<ref>{{Cite|Now reporting, Cap'n! The ship's all fixed! We can set sail anytime!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> and drives the ship out of {{Term|OoS|Subrosia}} towards the Western Coast of {{Term|OoS|Holodrum}}. The ship docks at the {{Term|OoS|Western Coast|link}}, allowing {{Term|OoS|Link}} passage to the {{Term|OoS|Graveyard}} and the {{Term|OoS|Explorer's Crypt}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |guide= Nintendo |page= 47}}</ref>

==={{OoA|-}}===
The unforgiving {{Term|OoA|Sea of Storms|link}}<ref>{{Cite|The seas beyond here swallow up all who venture into them--the Sea of Storms! I can't allow a child like you to pass!|Zora|OoA}}</ref> stalls the {{Term|OoA|Pirate Ship (Location)}} abreast a ring of whirlpools in the past era of {{OoA|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|We got stuck in this Sea o' Storms and can't get out!|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref> The {{Term|OoA|Cap'n|link}} tells {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} that the {{Term|OoA|Sea of Storms}} disrupted his return voyage from {{Term|OoA|Holodrum|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Ahoy! It's been a long time! I didn't expect to meet you 'ere! We've come all the way back from 'Olodrum...|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref> and that the {{Term|OoA|Zora Scale|link}} is a powerful charm capable of calming these seas.<ref>{{Cite|Har! Ye've got the Zora's Scale sea charm! Aye, that could calm this Sea o' Storms! 'Ow about you give me that Zora's Scale!|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref> After {{Term|OoA|Link}} gives the {{Term|OoA|Cap'n}} the {{Term|OoA|Zora Scale}}, the {{Term|OoA|Cap'n}} declares he and his crew are off to {{Term|OoA|Holodrum}} once again.<ref>{{Cite|We're off to 'Olodrum, the land o' seasons!|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref> The {{Term|OoA|Cap'n}} presents {{Term|OoA|Link}} the {{Term|OoA|Tokay Eyeball|link}} as a token of gratitude, before departing.<ref>{{Cite|Take this as a sign o' me thanks! It's the jewel called the Tokay Eyeball!|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{OoA|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Most of the [[manga]]'s Chapter 7, ''The Pirate Captain'', takes place on the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Pirate Ship (Location)}}. It is first shown as {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link|link}} and {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Ralph|link}} cling to their raft during a storm. The two are rescued and brought aboard.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= We fished out a couple castaways...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 104}}</ref> It is here that {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} and {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Ralph}} meet the Captain and learn about his past. The Ship itself is not shown again until the Captain sets course for {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Labrynna|link}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Set a course for Labrynna!!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 109}}</ref> making the vessel fight against the storm it has been trapped in for years.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= They've been stuck in this storm forever!...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 110}}</ref> After a battle on the deck, the Captain allows {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Link}} and {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Ralph}} to leave the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Pirate Ship (Location)}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Ye have my leave to go. Give 'em a boat.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 112}}</ref> It is last seen disappearing into the storm.
{{Noncanon|End}}
