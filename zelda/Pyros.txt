'''Pyros''' is a spell in {{ZA}}.

== Overview ==
Pyros creates a wall of several flames that appear before [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], and it costs four [[Rupee]]s for her to use. Pyros is dropped by a [[Snake]] west of [[Great Wimbich]].
