{{Term/Store|Cow Figurine|Cow Figurines|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are objects in {{MM}}.<ref name="Keaton's Quiz">{{Cite|How many tiny {{Color|MM3D Red|cow figurines}} are there in {{Color|MM3D Red|Clock Town}}?|Keaton|MM3D}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
There are a series of 10 {{Plural|MM3D|Cow Figurine}} scattered throughout {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}}. They function like bobblehead toys and move whenever the {{Term|MM3D|Moon|link}} shakes the earth. {{Plural|MM3D|Cow Figurine}} can appear as the subject of one of the questions in the {{Term|MM3D|Keaton's Quiz|link}}, in which the {{Term|MM3D|Keaton|link}} asks {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} how many there are within {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town}}.<ref name="Keaton's Quiz"/>
