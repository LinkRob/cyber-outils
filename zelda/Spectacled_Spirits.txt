==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Spectacled Spirits}}" is an event wherein a greater number of {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} of spectacled characters randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= There's an influx of characters with glasses and sunglasses on the Spirit Board! Defeat them to earn extra gold!<br/><br/>Legend-class spirits James McCloud and Jeff, and Ace-class spirits Brittany and Brewster will each appear for a fixed time.<br/><br/>Don't miss these spectacular spirits! |author= Nintendo |published= December 12, 2018 |retrieved= December 16, 2018 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/92f2956b/index.html |title= Event: Spectacled Spirits - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
