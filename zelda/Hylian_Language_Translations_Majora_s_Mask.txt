<noinclude>{{MM}} shares the same [[Hylian Language|Hylian alphabet]] as {{OoT}}, which is based on Japanese. Most of the text in {{MM|-}} appears to be nonsensical random symbols, with many of the shop signs being mirror images of the symbols. Those that are not mirror-imaged are either random sequences of symbols that do not translate into anything, or graphic elements; e.g. the square signs and the Romani Ranch sign, being borrowed directly from {{OoT|-}} without any change.
