The {{Term/Store|Gorman Track Rear Gate||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Gorman Track Rear Gate}}<br/>Please inquire at the main gate to the left.<br/>The Gorman Bros.|Sign|MM3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Track Rear Gate}} is situated at the end of the {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Track|link}} where it connects to the main {{Term|MM3D|Milk Road|link}}. It is normally inaccessible as an additional gate stands between it and the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Road}}, though it is possible to visit on the {{Term|MM3D|Final Day|link}} as the aforementioned gate is removed.
