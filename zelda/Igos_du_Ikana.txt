==Biography==
Igos du Ikana is the long dead king of the [[Ikana Kingdom]]. Once [[Link]] defeats him, he explains the source of Ikana's curse.<ref>{{Cite|It all happened after somebody thrust open the doors of that Stone Tower.|Igos du Ikana|MM}}</ref> He will then proceed to teach him the "[[Elegy of Emptiness]]," which is necessary to return true light to the land of Ikana.<ref>{{Cite|To return true light to this land, you must seal the doors of Stone Tower where the winds of darkness blow through. But Stone Tower is an impenetrable stronghold. Hundreds of soldiers from my kingdom would not even be able to topple it. It is far too reckless for one to take on such a challenge. ...And so... I grant to you a soldier who has no heart. One who will not falter in the darkness.|Igos du Ikana|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The spellbinding that had been cast upon us was broken by that light which you carry.|Igos du Ikana|MM}}</ref> Igos du Ikana is last seen during the ending credits.

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= Stick with using the basic target methods while defending.<br/> ...He's a different rank than those other two lackeys, though...
}}

Igos du Ikana will watch the battle against his [[King's Lackeys|Lackeys]] from his throne, deflecting any attacks thrown his direction. If Link ventures near him, he will be kicked back into the midst of battle. 

When fighting the King himself, he can take off his head and chase Link while the rest of his body keeps attacking with the Sword. Link must watch out for the head when it comes off, as there is no way to counter it. If the flying head attaches to Link, it will latch on to his face, taking [[Heart]]s away. Igos du Ikana can also breathe purple flames as a mid-range attack. When he falls down on the ground, Link must shine the light on him using his [[Mirror Shield]] to defeat him.
