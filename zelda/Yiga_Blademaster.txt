{{Term/Store|Yiga Blademaster|Yiga Blademasters|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|enemies]] in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Yiga Blademaster}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Yiga Blademaster}}
Yiga Blademasters are the elite soldiers of the [[Yiga Clan]].<ref>{{Cite|These are the elite soldiers of the Yiga Clan. They favor the windcleaver blade and are extremely agile despite their bulky build. At this Yiga Clan rank, they have mastered a technique that allows them to manipulate the very earth. By striking the ground, they can create stone pillars and blasts of air.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They can be found patrolling the [[Yiga Clan Hideout]], and if they spot [[Link]] in the hideout, they will close all doors and exits while calling in back up. During this quest, Yiga Blademasters can be distracted by dropping items, which they will then approach and pocket. When encountered, a [[Gerudo]] captive named [[Barta]] mentions that she had only ever seen them patrol and eat [[Mighty Bananas]]s, a hint to use the numerous fruits scattered throughout the base to bypass them.<ref>{{Cite|If only you had some way of distracting them...but I don't know how. All I ever see them do is patrol and eat {{Color|BotW Red|bananas}}. I haven't seen anything else that you could exploit as a weakness.|Barta|BotW}}</ref> If detected, the Yiga Blademasters guarding the Clan Hideout can unleash a one hit-kill attack at close range that ignores defensive stats, armor as well as recovery items and magic like [[Fairy|Fairies]] or [[Mipha's Grace]]. After [[Master Kohga]] is defeated, they will also start appearing randomly to attack Link.

Yiga Blademasters attack with [[Windcleaver]]s that send blasts of air at long distances. They can also create fissures with their Earthwake technique that produce updrafts that Link can use to glide before a stone pillar emerges from them. A shot to the head with the [[Bow]] can stun Yiga Blademasters, opening them for attacks. Yiga Blademasters cannot be ragdolled and will instead catch themselves with their hand when knocked back before resuming their fighting stance. If Link gets too far away during an encounter with a Yiga Blademaster, they may teleport closer to his location. When defeated, they flee while leaving behind their Windcleaver and Mighty Bananas. After defeating [[Master Kohga]], Yiga Blademasters will randomly appear all around [[Hyrule]] attempting to assassinate Link.

During the [[Side Quest]], "[[The Stolen Heirloom]]", Link must defeat a Yiga Blademaster in order to retrieve the heirloom that grants access to the [[Lakna Rokee Shrine]].<ref>{{Cite|It turned out that the heirloom thieves who struck Impa's manor belonged to the Yiga Clan. The incident was revenge for Dorian attempting to leave the guild of assassins. After you solved the problem, you returned the heirloom and discovered a new ancient shrine.|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==={{TotK|-}}===
Yiga Blademasters appear as enemies in {{TotK|-}}, acting the same as in {{BotW|-}}, but wield [[Eightfold Longblade]]s, instead of their Windcleavers. A Yiga Blademaster can unwittingly teach Link their Earthwake technique when the Hero of Hyrule is disguised as a Yiga Footsoldier after he defeats 3 Yiga Footsoldiers in under 1 minute, with the [[Thunder Helm]] itself being the grand prize for defeating 9 Yiga Footsoldiers in under 1 minute.

The Blademaster in charge of the Blademaster Headquarters, oddly, does not sound an alarm when Link reveals himself. This is, presumably, to keep players from entering a fight as soon as they warped in. If Link knocks on the door before equipping the Yiga set, the Blademaster will simply refuse entry without doing anything else.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|start}}
Yiga Blademasters appear in {{HWAoC}} as [[Captain (Hyrule Warriors)|Captain]] ranked enemies. They fight the same way as in {{BotW}}, though their [[Weak Point Gauge]]s can be exploited with the [[Rune]]s. They also speak, similar to the Yiga Footsoldiers, in instances such as when they are defeated in battle.<ref>{{Cite|Master Kohga...I failed.|Yiga Blademaster|HWAoC}}</ref> After [[Astor]] uses the souls of some Yiga Footsoldiers to revive the four Blight Ganons, [[Master Kohga]] and [[Sooga]] turn against him. The rest of the Yiga then follow suit, using the [[Forces|Hyrulean Army]] to exact vengeance on Astor.
{{Noncanon|end}}

==Trivia==
[[File:Yiga blademaster steals master sword.jpeg|thumb|A Yiga Blademaster stealing the Master Sword.]]
*Due to a programming oversight, Yiga Blademasters can be alerted by whistling outside the back entrance of the Clan Hideout and then attacking with a [[Korok Leaf]] during the [[Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|Divine Beast Vah Naboris quest]], allowing players to coax them into following Link in the open world. This has been exploited to drag them halfway across the map to the [[Korok Forest]], where their coding allows them to steal the [[Master Sword]] directly from its pedestal. Disposing of them afterwards will only yield the normal spoils of Mighty Bananas and weapon, not the seized holy relic, but a glitched invisible blade can still be retrieved from its resting place. These Yiga Blademasters retain the ability to defeat Link with a single strike in the overworld, become increasingly aggressive once in the [[Lost Woods]] and are passive in safe zones. They can be killed by [[lightning]], which is attracted by the Windcleaver in stormy conditions.
