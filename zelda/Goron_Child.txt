The {{Term/Store|Goron Child||Series, ST}} is a young {{Term|ST|Goron|link}} who resides in the {{Term|ST|Goron Village|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 289 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref> He is the  grandson of the [[Goron Elder#Spirit Tracks|Goron Elder]].<ref>{{Cite|I hope Gramps is OK... He is the oldest Goron in our whole village! Everyone calls him the Goron Elder.|Goron Child|ST}}</ref>

==Biography==
Upon first visiting the {{Term|ST|Goron Village}}, the {{Term|ST|Goron Child}} informs {{Term|ST|Link|link}} that the entrance to the village is blocked by fire geysers. After {{Term|ST|Link}} brings {{Term|ST|Mega Ice|link}} and extinguishes the flames blocking his way to the village, he is granted access to the {{Term|ST|Fire Sanctuary|link}} by the {{Term|ST|Goron Elder|link}} who first notes that {{Term|ST|Link}} must prove himself, only to be swayed moments later when his grandson runs into the room, noting that {{Term|ST|Link}} was the one whose efforts put out the geysers.<ref>{{Cite|Gramps! Gramps! This boy fixed the path to the village, Gramps! That is how I could make it back home!|Goron Child|ST}}</ref>

After {{Term|ST|Link}} defeats {{Term|ST|Cragma|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|ST|Fire Temple|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Goron Child}} tells him that he wants to meet a [[Princess Zelda|Princess]] and asks him to be brought to a big city.<ref>{{Cite|This may seem a little random, but I have made up my mind. I am tired of living in the sticks, so I am moving to the big city.|Goron Child|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The city is where the castle is. And the castle is where the princess lives. See what I am getting at?|Goron Child|ST}}</ref> The {{Term|ST|Goron Elder}} thinks that a trip to {{Term|ST|Hyrule Castle Town|link}} will help his grandson realize that his village is paradise, something that the his grandson does not understand.<ref name="young"/> When {{Term|ST|Link}} brings him to {{Term|ST|Hyrule}}'s capital city, the young {{Term|ST|Goron}} mistakes {{Term|ST|Lucia|link}} for a princess before rewarding {{Term|ST|Link}} with a {{Term|ST|Force Gem|link}}. Later, he tells {{Term|ST|Link}} that he feels short-changed and homesick;<ref>{{Cite|Oh, let me tell you, friend... It is hard times living in the city, especially for a Goron like me. I go to the castle, and the guards tell me no rocks are allowed inside. Most folks think that I look like a boulder and that I should be treated as one. And do not get me started on the prices. One Cucco costs an arm and a leg! I am broke. Just the other day, I used the last of the money Gramps gave me. This place is so different from Goron Village... And I am so very tired.|Goron Child|ST}}</ref> if he cheers him up, the {{Term|ST|Goron Child}} gives {{Term|ST|Link}} a {{Term|ST|Goron Amber|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for the words of encouragement. You are right. I will not let this city beat me! Here, let me give you something for those nice words of yours!|Goron Child|ST}}</ref>
