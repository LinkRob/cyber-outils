{{Term/Store|Loone||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Loone|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Loone}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman who resides at the camp on {{Term|BotW|Puffer Beach|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first encounters her, she will be sitting at the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} next to an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Loone}}, he will overhear her telling the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} how smooth and gorgeous its skin is, referring to it by the name {{Term|BotW|Roscoe|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your skin is so smooth and gorgeous, my little Roscoe...|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Noticing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is watching them, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} teases {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}} for being shy.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Someone's watching us? Oh, my sweet Roscoe! You're so shy, aren't you? YES, YOU ARE!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> She encourages {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}} to show himself off.<ref>{{Cite|Why not proudly show off your beaming beauty, my darling? You really MUST come out of your shell.|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Realizing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to want something, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} becomes fearful that he is after {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? The person watching looks like he wants something? Maybe he's after... *GASP* Is he after you?!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> She assures {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}} that she will never let someone take it, claiming to be in love with it.<ref>{{Cite|Don't you worry, my sweet Roscoe! I would never ever EVER let you go! I'm head over heels for my special friend who's been around since ancient times! You're so ancient and special, Roscoe! YES, YOU ARE!!|Loone|BotW}}</ref>

Quietly, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} admits that she lied to {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Sorry, my sweet Roscoe, but I fibbed a little... The truth is...}}|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Much more loudly, she proclaims that she wants to see {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|I wanna see Guardians!}}|Loone|BotW}}</ref> In particular, she wants to see {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Flying ones}}! {{Color|BotW Red|Small ones}}! {{Color|BotW Red|Ones that walk around}}! I wanna see all the Guardians in all the world!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> She specifies that she wants to see active ones, not like the ones on {{Term|BotW|Puffer Beach}} that have ceased functioning.<ref>{{Cite|Not the ones around here that have rusted away, either. I wanna see {{Big|fresh and lively Guardians!}}|Loone|BotW}}</ref> She admits that if she were to ever see them, she may abandon {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I ever do get to see them... I may just have to leave you, my friend.|Loone|BotW}}</ref> However, she soothes {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}}'s worries, saying that getting close to a {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} would be too dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, but don't you worry! Getting near a Guardian is too dangerous for me. {{Color|BotW Red|Flying ones}}, {{Color|BotW Red|small ones}}, {{Color|BotW Red|ones that walk}}...|Loone|BotW}}</ref> She decides that seeing them in person is simply a dream.<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid seeing Guardians like that is nothing more than a dream within a dream for me...|Loone|BotW}}</ref> After this, the "{{Term|BotW|Guardian Slideshow|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Loone}} again, he can hear her repeating her desire to see {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} to {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}} while she comforts him.<ref>{{Cite|My sweet Roscoe... Don't you worry, my special friend! I would never ever, EVER let you go. ...Unless, of course, I find a way to see a {{Color|BotW Red|flying}} Guardian and a {{Color|BotW Red|small}} one and one {{Color|BotW Red|that walks around}}.|Loone|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has taken {{Plural|BotW|Picture|link}} of all three {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}, he can show them to {{Term|BotW|Loone}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll show them to you.] Hm? Someone's speaking? You'll...show me? A Guardian?|Loone|BotW}}</ref> She begins to tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} to not bother her and {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}}, but she stops once she sees the {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you! Could you please not bother my Roscoe and me? It's not like you can show me a Guardian or anything...|Loone|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Loone}} is shocked to see the {{Term|BotW|Picture}}, stating that the beauty of the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher}} makes her feel alive with terror.<ref>{{Cite|That image... That's a flying Guardian! Wow, I feel alive with terror just from gazing at its beautiful form!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> When she sees a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout}}, she calls it adorable, noting that it appears vicious for its small size.<ref>{{Cite|A small Guardian! Awww, it looks so vicious for its tiny size! Adorable!!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Now that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has shown her two types, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} begins to realize that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must have a third {{Term|BotW|Picture}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hold on... If you've been able to show me two types, then that must mean...|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Seeing the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker}} {{Term|BotW|Picture}}, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} has trouble believing it, and she exclaims that it is "exhilaratingly scary."<ref>{{Cite|A walking Guardian! I can't believe it! Nothing could be more dangerous! What an exhilaratingly scary sight!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> After seeing all three {{Plural|BotW|Picture}}, she remarks that it was a joy.<ref>{{Cite|Phew... What a joy.|Loone|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Loone}} demands the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}}, as she wants to look at the {{Plural|BotW|Picture}} everyday.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you! Give me that box, OK?! I wanna look at those images every single day!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} will be disappointed, but she resolves herself to burn the {{Plural|BotW|Picture}} into her mind.<ref>{{Cite|[I can't do that.] You can't? I see... How unfortunate. I guess I'll just have to burn those images into my imagination.|Loone|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks if he can take {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}}, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} allows it, claiming to be over it.<ref>{{Cite|[Can I have that orb?] Hm? Oh, you mean Roscoe? Sure, that weird sphere thing is all yours. Really, I'm over it.|Loone|BotW}}</ref> After seeing the {{Plural|BotW|Picture}}, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} is now obsessed with {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}, and they are all she can see when she closes her eyes.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, I'm all about Guardians now! If I close my eyes, I can see them in my imagination! It's glorious!|Loone|BotW}}</ref>

Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Loone}} will stand by the broken down {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker}} near her camp.<ref>{{Cite|And this... Is that...?|Loone|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her, she states that he has inspired her imagination.<ref>{{Cite|You are the inspiration to my imagination! You really are!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> Since she saw {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} {{Plural|BotW|Picture}}, she has spent her time daydreaming.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, I've been daydreaming while recalling those wonderful Guardians you showed me!|Loone|BotW}}</ref> She also claims to not remember {{Term|BotW|Roscoe}}.<ref>{{Cite|Roscoe? Who in the world is that? I can't remember!|Loone|BotW}}</ref>
