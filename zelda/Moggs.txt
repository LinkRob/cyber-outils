{{Term/Store|Moggs||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Moggs|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Moggs}} and his wife {{Term|BotW|Monari|link}} are {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} who reside in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}. They move to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}} after {{Term|BotW|Fyson|link}} opens the {{Term|BotW|Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)|link}}, and they live in the {{Term|BotW|House|link}} behind {{Term|BotW|Ore and More|link}}. He owns two {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Retriever|link}}, which can be found behind his home.<ref name="Pets">{{Cite|I just had to move here. And I'm glad I did! My pets seem much happier here, too.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> During the day, he walks throughout {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|display=Town}}. At night, he sleeps in one of the {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}} in his {{Term|BotW|House}}.<ref>{{Cite|*snore*... *snore*...|Moggs|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Moggs}}, he is pleased to see someone so young visiting {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, what do we have here? Looks like we have another young'n in town.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks who he is, he introduces himself.<ref>{{Cite|[Who are you?] I'm called Moggs.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he is retired, and he came to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}} after hearing a rumor about it.<ref name="Retired"/> He is glad he did so, and he has even noticed that his {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Retriever}} seem happier.<ref name="Pets"/> He invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to speak with him at any time.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, come back and chat with me again sometime.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} again, he asks him to stay and chat with him.<ref name="Old Man"/><ref>{{Cite|Ask me anything.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about his family, {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} explains that his son who has left to travel the world.<ref name="Family"/> While their home feels empty without him, {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} believes it has brought him closer to {{Term|BotW|Monari}}.<ref>{{Cite|The house feels a little empty without him around, but at least it's helped me and my wife get closer together.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> Before {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed the "{{Term|BotW|A Parent's Love|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} states that he hears {{Term|BotW|Hagie|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ruli|link}} fighting during the night.<ref>{{Cite|The people living across from us fight {{Color|BotW Red|night after night}}...|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> He has considered listening in from behind their {{Term|BotW|House}}, but he does not want to be nosy.<ref>{{Cite|I sometimes think about sneaking around back and listening, but I also don't want to be so nosy...|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> After the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} claims that he has not heard {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} and {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} arguing lately.<ref>{{Cite|I haven't heard the people across from us argue in quite some time...|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> He states that with every obstacle they face together, their relationship grows stronger.<ref>{{Cite|I believe that every time they tackle an obstacle together, their relationship grows stronger.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref>

Following this conversation, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} about {{Term|BotW|Monari}}, who he claims is likely wandering around {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Your wife?] My wife? She's probably wandering around town right now, I'd imagine.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> He claims that she is great at {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}, and he especially likes her {{Term|BotW|Mushroom Skewer|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|She's a really gifted chef. Her mushroom skewer is out of this world!|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> He suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} speak with her if he has the time.<ref>{{Cite|If you've got some time, you should definitely go talk with her.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about his pets, {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} claims to be talking about the {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Retriever}} behind his {{Term|BotW|House}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Your pets?] Oh, I'm talking about the animals that live behind my house. My wife and I both love animals.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> He has noticed that they enjoy the clean air of {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}, which makes him glad that they moved.<ref>{{Cite|The air feels really clean out here, and they seem to love it. I'm glad we moved out here.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} after 9:00 PM, he explains that he is going to {{Term|BotW|Bed}} and asks to be excused.<ref>{{Cite|I'm heading to bed... Please excuse me.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> He may also invite {{Term|BotW|Link}} to talk to him the next day.<ref>{{Cite|I think I'm going to head to bed soon. Come back and chat with me again tomorrow.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref>
