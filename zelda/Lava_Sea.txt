The {{Term/Store|Lava Sea||Series, OoS}} is a location in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |guide= BradyGames |page= 32{{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|OoS}}
The {{Term|OoS|Lava Sea}} is a sea of lava that borders the majority of the southern edge of {{Term|OoS|Subrosia|link}}. It directly borders both the {{Term|OoS|Subrosia Seaside|link}} and {{Term|OoS|Treasure Grove|link}}.

==Trivia==
* The {{Term|OoS|Map|link}} of {{Term|OoS|Subrosia|link}} depicts it as an island within the {{Term|OoS|Lava Sea}}. It is unconfirmed whether this is truly representative of the area.
