"{{Term/Store|Stranded on Eventide||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Stranded on Eventide}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Eventide Island}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Upon stepping onto the shores of {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who has traveled to this island...}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} has been relying on his {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} during his journey,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I present you with a challenge. In your travels, you've relied on the equipment you've found along the way.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} has created a challenge during which he must utilize whatever he can scavenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Here, you must cast this equipment aside and face this trial with only your wits and whatever you can scavenge.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> All of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Item}}, save for the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} and other {{Plural|BotW|Key Item|link}}, are then taken from him. He must insert {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} to the three {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} in order to have his belongings returned.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Offer up the orbs to the three altars on this island. Only then will I acknowledge your skill and return your items.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> Once the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Link}}'s equipment will only be returned to him if he completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} or leaves {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} cannot {{Term|BotW|Warping|display=Warp}} during the trial and must {{Term|BotW|Swimming|link|display=Swim}} away from the {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|display=Island}} to leave. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to the {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|display=Island}} after leaving, he must start the trial over from the beginning.

To complete the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must find the three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} located on {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}} and place them in their corresponding {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal}}. One {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} is located on top of a {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} can be found nearby on the northern tip of the {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|display=Island}}. Another {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} is attached to the necklace of a {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} on the eastern side of the central jungle, and it can be placed in a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} on a rock formation. The final {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} can be found on {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock|link}}, within another {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}. A {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} can also be found here under a big slab that must be moved with {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} or by scavenging throughout {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}}. He can use {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} to defeat weaker {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, which conserve {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}}. He is also able to use {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury|link}} to inflict high damage to an entire camp of {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}}.

When all three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} have been placed in {{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot}}, {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground on top of {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock}}.<ref>{{Cite|Once you offered precious orbs to each of the three altars on the island, an ancient shrine appeared.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh}} will return {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s belongings,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You've done well to complete the trial. I will now return your things, as I promised.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> at which point the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} invites him to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Come, enter the shrine.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
