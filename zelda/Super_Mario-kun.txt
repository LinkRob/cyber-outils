'''''Super Mario-kun''''' is a manga series of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] originally from Japan. Certain issues both reference and include content originally from {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Relevance to ''The Legend of Zelda''==
In chapter 4 of volume 4 of the long-running manga, {{Smw|Mario}} and {{Smw|Yoshi}} find themselves in [[Hyrule]]. Mario is armed with a Mushroom Sword (which later is powered-up into the Master Mushroom Sword), while Yoshi becomes his shield. They encounter a {{Term|ALttP|Spear Throwing Soldier|link}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Rupee|link}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Octorok|link}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Zora (Enemy)|link}}, an {{Term|ALttP|Armos|link}}, and finally {{Term|ALttP|Agahnim|link}}, as well as a {{Term|ALttP|Fairy|link}} and the [[Cursed Fairy]] with the same face as {{Smw|Bowser}}'s.

In chapter 7, still of volume 4, while approaching Bowser's Castle, Mario pulls out the [[Pegasus Boots]] that he stole from his adventure in Hyrule and uses them to reach quickly the castle. Meanwhile, behind the page, [[Link]] is looking desperately for them.

The Master Mushroom Sword later reappears in chapter 3 of volume 13.
