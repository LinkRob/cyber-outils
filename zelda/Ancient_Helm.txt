{{Term/Store|Ancient Helm|Ancient Helms|Series, BotW, SSBU|plural}} are items in {{BotW}} and {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Helm}}<br/>This headgear is the result of Robbie's research. It uses Guardian parts to decrease damage from ancient energy attacks.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Ancient Helms are pieces of [[Armor]] that can be purchased from [[Cherry]] at the [[Akkala Ancient Tech Lab]]. When worn, they provide one level of Guardian Resist. Ancient Helms can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Ancient Helm, alongside the [[Ancient Cuirass]] and [[Ancient Greaves]], compose the [[Ancient Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides an Ancient Proficiency [[Set Bonus]].
