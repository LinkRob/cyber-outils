The {{Term/Store|Cliffs of Ruvara||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Cliffs of Ruvara'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Cliffs of Ruvara are the northernmost section of [[Gerudo Highlands]] and serve as the transition between Gerudo Highlands and the [[Tabantha Frontier]]. The Cliffs are sections of mesa that become less vertical as the altitude increases. Along the sides of the cliffs are multiple scaffolds and evidence of architectural involvement. The Cliffs are where the ancient [[Sheikah]] technology was uncovered, including the [[Divine Beast]]s and the [[Guardian]]s. There are also multiple geothermal vents which cause [[Updraft]]s that allow [[Link]] to [[Paraglider|Paraglide]] up the sides of the Cliffs.

[[Link]] can find multiple [[Korok]] puzzles along the cliffs, including a puzzle where Link must follow a flower that teleports away when he is near it. Once Link follows the flower for long enough, a Korok will appear and give Link a [[Korok Seed]].<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
