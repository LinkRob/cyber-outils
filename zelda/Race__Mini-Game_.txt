'''Races''' are a type of [[Mini-Game]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}

==Location and rules==
Racing games are a type of minigame where Link either participates with the goal of getting in first place (if there are one or more rival contestants), or simply follows a character or a designated route through a course.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, a race is held by two children in the [[Village of the Blue Maiden]], in the Dark World. The four [[Links]] have twenty seconds to reach a goal in order to obtain the [[Pegasus Boots]]. With Bombs, the Links can reach the end in less than three seconds.
