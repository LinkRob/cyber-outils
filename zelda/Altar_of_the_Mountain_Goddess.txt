The {{Term/Store|Altar of the Mountain Goddess||Series, ST}} is an object in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Path to the Altar of the Mountain Goddess|Sign|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Altar of the Mountain Goddess}} is a special shrine and altar built to honor the {{Term|ST|Mountain Goddess|link}}, the guardian deity of the {{Plural|ST|Goron|link}} that inhabit the mountain. It is located just to the northwest of {{Term|ST|Goron Village|link}}, through several passes filled with rolling boulders and across a lake of lava. It is here that {{Term|ST|Kagoron|link}} comes to pay homage to the {{Term|ST|Mountain Goddess}} and ask for her aid in times of distress. The {{Term|ST|Altar of the Mountain Goddess}} is a sanctified place for the {{Plural|ST|Goron}}, and only few are permitted to visit it.{{Fact}} Though an exception is made for {{Term|ST|Link|link}} when he comes to {{Term|ST|Goron Village}} seeking information on the {{Term|ST|Fire Sanctuary|link}} and the mountain suddenly becomes violent and unstable. Due to the recent eruptions that have blocked off the part of {{Term|ST|Goron Village}} where the elder lives, the {{Plural|ST|Goron}} tell {{Term|ST|Link}} to visit {{Term|ST|Kagoron}} at the {{Term|ST|Altar of the Mountain Goddess}} to seek his advice on how to proceed. {{Term|ST|Link}} passes through the many dangers to reach the {{Term|ST|Altar of the Mountain Goddess}} and meets with {{Term|ST|Kagoron}}, who gives him a {{Term|ST|Trusty Freight Car|link}} in order to bring {{Term|ST|Mega Ice|link}} to {{Term|ST|Goron Village}} to stem the {{Term|ST|Lava|link}} flows that have blocked off {{Term|ST|Goron Village}} to some of its residents.
