{{Term/Store|Koukot Plateau||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Koukot Plateau'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Koukot Plateau is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. A series of wooden platforms can be found along the walls of the Plateau, which [[Link]] must traverse in pursuit of [[Sesami]]'s missing friends during the "[[Missing in Action]]" [[Side Quest]].

==Nomenclature==
Koukot Plateau derives its name from [[Koume]] and [[Kotake]] from {{OoT}}.
