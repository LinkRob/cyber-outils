The {{Term/Store|Rented Ice Rod||Series, ALBW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rented Ice Rod<br/>If you wave it, you can freeze all sorts of things.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HW}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Ice Rod}} is used to create and drop blocks of {{Term|ALBW|Ice|link}} on {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}} and fiery hazards. It is rentable from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}} for 100 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.<br/><br/>Being a rented {{Term|ALBW|Item}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Ice Rod}} will be returned to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio}} by {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} falls in battle. It may be permanently obtained as the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Rod|link}} for 1200 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, and further upgraded into the {{Term|ALBW|Nice Ice Rod|link}} by {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}.
