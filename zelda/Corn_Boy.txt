{{Name|Japanese|{{Term/Store|Corn Boy||Series}}}} is a character found in {{RTBToL}}.{{Fact}} Corn Boy is found on [[Page 3]], and lives in a small farmhouse with [[Peter]] and his daughter, [[Sensei]]. 

Corn Boy is a talking doll. He is in possession of the [[Slingshot#Ripened Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love|Slingshot]], an item which [[Tingle]] requires to advance to [[Page 4]]. He freely gives the item to Tingle when asked.
