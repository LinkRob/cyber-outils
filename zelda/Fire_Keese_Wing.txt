==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Fire Keese Wing}} are {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} that are dropped by {{Term|BotW|Fire Keese|link}}, which can be found flying in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} regions. They can be can be exchanged for 6 {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} or sold for 6 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. Like other {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Fire Keese Wing}} can be combined with critters in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Fire Keese Wing}} can also be used to produce {{Term|BotW|Red Dye|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Fire Keese Wing}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}:
