{{Plural|BotW|Boko Bow}} are basic wooden {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} that are used by regular {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. They are made by tying a string to either end of a tree branch, so they are not considered to be very effective in combat.<ref name="Inventory"/> Being made by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}, they belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype as a result.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> If scanned using the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Term|BotW|Amiibo|link}} has a chance of spawning a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Boko Bow}}, along with other {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}}.

Being made from wood, {{Plural|BotW|Boko Bow}} will burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they are unable to be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Boko Bow}} do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, nor do they attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}.
