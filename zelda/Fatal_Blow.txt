The {{Term/Store|Fatal Blow|Fatal Blows|Series, SS, SSHD}} is a sword technique in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS|publisher= Prima|page= 7}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So... Why don't you try a {{Color|SSHD Green|fatal blow}} to close out our session?|Eagus|SSHD}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|SS|Fatal Blow}} is one of [[Link]]'s [[Link's Special Sword Strike|Special Skyward Strikes]], and is essentially a powerful [[Down Thrust]]. It is executed by shaking the [[Wii Remote]] and [[Nunchuk]] vertically while targeting a downed enemy. In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword HD|Skyward Sword HD]]'', it is performed by shaking both [[Joy-Con|Joy-Cons]] vertically at the same time while targeting a downed enemy or, in button-only controls, quickly flicking the right stick up, down and then up again while targeting a downed enemy. If the Hero of the Sky successfully performs a {{Term|SS|Spin Attack|link}} while training with the {{Term|SS|Practice Sword|link}}, {{Term|SS|Eagus|link}} will teach him to perform a {{Term|SS|Fatal Blow}}.<ref>{{Cite|All right! When your enemy is down, target it with {{Icon|SS Z}} and wave the Wii Remote and Nunchuk at the same time to land a fatal blow. Do a side slash on that log. When it's down, try finishing it off with this technique!|Eagus|SS}}</ref>

The {{Term|SS|Fatal Blow}} can be executed on nearly any bipedal foe, and is used during the battle against {{Term|SS|Ghirahim|link}} once he has fallen to a lower battle platform. It is later vital to ultimately defeat [[Demise]] in the [[Final Boss]] battle, finishing him, then sealing his remains inside the {{Term|SS|Master Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The last remnants of Demise are decaying slowly within the sword.|Impa|SS}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* The Fatal Blow is very similar to the finishing attacks via the [[Parry Attack]] and [[Ending Blow]] used in {{TWW}} and {{TP}} to defeat [[Ganondorf]]. Like in this game, Ganondorf himself also dies as a result.
