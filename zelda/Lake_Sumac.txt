{{Term/Store|Lake Sumac||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lake Sumac'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Lake Sumac}} is located north of the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}, and south of {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Lake Sumac}} is a small lake with an island in the center, and on the small island, a large {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} grows tall. On the south-eastern edge of the lake, a {{Term|BotW|Raft|link}} floats tied to a pole in the water. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can cut the rope and use the raft to float across the lake.

On the top of the giant {{Term|BotW|Tree}} that grows on the island, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found hiding underneath a rock. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} picks up the rock, the {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will appear and gift {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
