{{Term/Store|Dorrah||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dorrah|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who guards the front entrance of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} with her fellow guard, {{Term|BotW|Merina|link}}. She wields a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tries to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} without a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link|display=disguise}}, the guards will stop him. {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} will tell him that no "voe" are allowed in Town.<ref>{{Cite|Freeze! Where do you think you're going?! Not into town—that's for sure! No voe allowed. Off with you!|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> She apologizes if {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her and says that it is a strict tradition that "voe" are not permitted into the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}},<ref>{{Cite|Sav'otta. Sorry, but in Gerudo Town it is tradition that voe are not permitted entry. We honor that tradition very strictly.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> suggesting that {{Term|BotW|Link}} instead go to the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}} if he needs to prepare for a journey.<ref>{{Cite|If you need to prepare for a journey, you should head to the oasis near the desert's entrance, {{Color|BotW Blue|Kara Kara Bazaar}}.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sorry, but {{Color|BotW Blue|voe}} are not allowed in town. If you need to prepare for your journey, you can do so elsewhere. Try {{Color|BotW Blue|Kara Kara Bazaar}}, the oasis at the entrance of the desert. {{Color|BotW Blue|Voe}} are welcome there.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} mishears "voe" as "boe," {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} corrects him, explaining that "voe" means "man" in the {{Term|BotW|Hylian (Language)|link}} language.<ref>{{Cite|Not "boe"! {{Color|BotW Blue|Voe}}! Bite your bottom lip as you say it. It translates as "man" in your language.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} also explains that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)|link}} word for "woman" is "vai".<ref>{{Cite|So long as we're on the subject, {{Color|BotW Blue|vai}} is our word for "woman." What do you think? Pretty easy to remember?|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes speaking to {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}}, she tells him that "Good-bye" is "sav'orq" in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)}}.<ref>{{Cite|Since you're here, it might be worth it to learn some Gerudo words, right? "Good-bye" is {{Color|BotW Blue|sav'orq}}.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to sneak into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} without being disguised or removes any piece of his disguise while inside, {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} will yell that a man has been detected and commands the soldiers to capture him at once.<ref>{{Cite|Sound the alarm! A voe has been detected! Capture him at once!|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} is then thrown out to the front entrance of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, where {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} points her {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear}} at him and tells him that men are forbidden by {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} law.<ref>{{Cite|Voe are forbidden from entering this town. This is Gerudo law. Do you understand? Never come back!|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} obtains the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}}, he can speak to {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} again. She explains that {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} is {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}'s largest trade hub and that only "vai" are permitted to enter.<ref>{{Cite|This is {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}, Hyrule's largest trade hub.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Only {{Color|BotW Blue|vai}} are permitted entry within the walls, and we're constantly on alert for anyone who looks suspicious.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This place is famous for its bustling trade, but also for our unusual rule allowing only {{Color|BotW Blue|vai}} into the town. We're constantly on the lookout for anyone suspicious, so you can rest easy here.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} mishears "vai" as "bai," {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} corrects him and explains that "vai" means "woman" in {{Term|BotW|Hylian (Language)}}.<ref>{{Cite|Not "vai"! {{Color|BotW Blue|Vai}}! Bite your bottom lip as you say it. It translates to "woman" in your language.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> She also explains that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)}} word for "man" is "voe."<ref>{{Cite|So long as we're on the subject, {{Color|BotW Blue|voe}} is our word for "man."|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} recommends learning the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)}} language if {{Term|BotW|Link}} plans to stay in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you plan to stay awhile, you'd do well to learn the language.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Dorrah}} says that {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} has calmed the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} and, if he is wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can now relax in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|The chief has calmed the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}. The town can finally rest easy with that threat handled...|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Now you can truly relax in town. Feel free to come inside.|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref> She expects there to be more traffic in and out of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, which means she will have to be more diligent to keep men from entering.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure we'll see more business traffic going in and out than we have in a long while, too. Which means I have to be extra diligent in keeping voe from slipping into town with the crowds!|Dorrah|BotW}}</ref>
