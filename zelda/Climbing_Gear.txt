==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Climbing Gear is a piece of [[Armor]] located in [[Chaas Qeta Shrine]] on [[Tenoko Island]]. After obtaining it, [[Granté]] will begin selling the piece of Armor for 4,000 [[Rupee]]s. According to its description, it is infused with ancient technology.<ref name= "Inventory"/> When worn, it provides one level of Climb Speed Up. The Climbing Gear can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Climbing Gear, alongside the [[Climber's Bandanna]] and the [[Climbing Boots]], compose the [[Climbing Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a Climbing Jump Stamina Up [[Set Bonus]].
