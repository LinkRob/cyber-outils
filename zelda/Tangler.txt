'''Tangler''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Tangler|Nearly spherical and always scowling, this angry fish charges directly at any foe it sees.<ref name="Tangler">{{Cite|Nearly spherical and always scowling, this angry fish charges directly at any foe it sees.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Tanglers are [[fish]] enemies commonly found near most bodies of water. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Tangler [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of one.
