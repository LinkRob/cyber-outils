{{Plural|BotW|Moonlight Scimitar}} are sharp {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}}-style engravings that were previously used for ceremonial purposes.<ref name="Inventory"/> These {{Plural|BotW|Moonlight Scimitar|display=Scimitar}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which consists of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> They can be found throughout the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}, particularly within and around {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.

The following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Moonlight Scimitar}} when they are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}:
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} (Rider) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

As metallic {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, {{Plural|BotW|Moonlight Scimitar}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. They sink when dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
