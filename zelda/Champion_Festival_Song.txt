The {{Term/Store|Champion Festival Song||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Name">{{Cite|[I want to hear it.] OK! I'm going to sing it now! The Champion Festival Song!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> also known as the '''Ceremonial Song''',<ref>{{Cite|'''The Ceremonial Song'''|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> is a {{Term|BotW|Song|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Learning Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song}} is taught to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Laruta|link}}. She is seen singing it by {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}'s statue.<ref>{{Cite|♪La dee dooo!♪ ♪A gift from the sky... a {{Color|BotW Red|scale of light}}...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|♪La dee dooo!♪ ♪Splits the feet... of a {{Color|BotW Red|Veiled Falls}} sight...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} allows her, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} will sing the entire {{Term|BotW|Song|link}} to him.<ref name="Name"/> When the conversation ends, the [[Shrine Quest]] "[[The Ceremonial Song]]" is unlocked.

The {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song}} was used at the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival|link}}.<ref name="Name"/>

==Lyrics==
The lyrics hint how the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} can be completed: the "scale of light" refers to the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident|link}}, "Splits the feet of a Veiled Falls sight" indicates that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must jump from the top of {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls|link}} and plunge the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} into the pedestal of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} below it, represented by "Your trial awaits, it's glowing bright."
