{{Term/Store|Dungeon Map|Dungeon Maps|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, OoS, OoA, TWW, TWWHD, TMC, TP, TPHD, SS, SSHD|plural}},{{Exp Game|OoT, MM, OoS, OoA, TWW, TMC, TP, SS}}<ref name="E 122"/><ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|SSHD Gold|Dungeon Map}}!|N/A|SSHD}}</ref> also simply known as {{Term/Store|Map|Maps|TLoZ, ALttP, LA, LADX|plural}},{{Exp Game|TLoZ, ALttP, LA}}<ref name="E 122"/> are recurring [[item]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}} ===
{{Stub|TLoZ}}
In {{TLoZ}}, collecting the Dungeon Map reveals the layout of the current [[dungeon]]. However, any hidden rooms that can occasionally appear in later dungeons are not shown.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Stub|ALttP}}
{{Manual|ALttP|Map|The dungeon map shows the layout of all the rooms in the dungeon and your current position.}}
{{Manual|ALttP&FS|Map|Even rooms you have not visited appear on the map.}}
Since {{ALttP}}, Dungeon Maps have appeared in [[Treasure Chest]]s. They have the property to indicate which rooms [[Link]] has already visited, by displaying them in a brighter color than any rooms not yet visited.<ref name="E 122"/> Additionally, the player can navigate between the various floors of the [[dungeon]] while viewing the Dungeon Map.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Stub|LA}}
{{Manual|LA|Map and Compass|The Map will show you all the rooms in the dungeon. The Compass will indicate the location of the dungeon's Nightmare and the position of treasure chests you have not opened yet.}}
Dungeon Maps in {{LA}} function the same as in {{ALttP}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Stub|OoT}}
Dungeon Maps in {{OoT}} function relatively the same as in previous installments. Due to the complex nature of the 3D environment, individual rooms can span multiple floors.

The model for the Dungeon Map features a drawn layout of the second dungeon, [[Dodongo's Cavern]]. This was changed in {{OoT3D}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Stub|MM}}
Dungeon Map in {{MM}} function as the same as in previous installments. Their model is reused from {{OoT}}, so the map still shows the layout of [[Dodongo's Cavern]].

==={{OoS|-}} / {{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Dungeon Map|The Dungeon Map shows the layout of all the rooms in the dungeon. The darkened rooms on the Map Screen are ones you have not entered yet.}}
{{Stub|OoS, OoA}}

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Stub|TWW}}

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Manual|TMC|Dungeon Map|When you find this item, you will be able to see all the rooms on every floor of the dungeon. Rooms you’ve visited will be lighter than rooms you haven’t visited yet.}}
Each of the six [[dungeon]]s in {{TMC}} have a Dungeon Map, with each one contained in a large blue [[Treasure Chest]]. They are displayed on the [[Map Screen]] in place of the [[Map of Hyrule]] whenever [[Link]] is inside the relevant dungeon. Rooms that Link has visited are a lighter shade of blue, while rooms he has not visited are darker blue; the room he is currently in blinks on the Dungeon Map.<ref>{{Cite|You found a {{Color|TMC Red|dungeon map}}! Check it on your {{Color|TMC Blue|Map screen}}. Lighter rooms are ones you've visited. The blinking room is your location. Press Up or Down on {{Icon|TMC Direction Pad}} to check different floors.|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

==Spinoff appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{ZG&W|-}}===
{{Stub|ZG&W}}
{{Manual|ZG&W|Map|This shows you how the labyrinth is arranged. This will be helpful for choosing the correct stairwell to go up. (Link's present position will be displayed flickering whether he has a map or not.)}}

==={{ZA|-}}===
[[File:ZA Maps.png|thumb|left]]
In {{ZA}}, each of the seven shrines have a corresponding Map revealing the full layout of the location. The Maps are obtained using the following methods:

#The Map of the [[Shrine of Earth]] appears after [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] defeats every [[Tektite]] in the crossroads area of the Shrine.
#The Map of the [[Shrine of Illusion]] appears after Zelda defeats the [[Vire]] in the mirror and red curtains room of the Shrine.
#The Map of the [[Shrine of Air]] appears after defeating every [[Tornado]] in the entrance area of the Shrine.
#The Map of the [[Shrine of Destiny]], unlike other Maps, does not appear in its corresponding dungeon. Instead, the Map is in the [[Barachno Marsh]], where it is given to Zelda by [[Yelena]].
#The Map of the [[Shrine of Water]] appears after defeating the [[Zol]]s in the second spike wall area of the Shrine.
#The Map of the [[Shrine of Strength]] appears after pushing the left [[block]] to the right in the block [[puzzle]] east of the Shrine's entrance area.
#The Map of the [[Shrine of Fire]] appears after defeating the [[Crockarock]]s in the area after the second [[Blacksmith (Enemy)|Blacksmith]] screen of the Shrine.
{{Noncanon|End}}
