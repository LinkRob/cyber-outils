==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Hard 'n' Heavy Metal}}" is an event wherein a greater number of metallic {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Get ready for some metallic mayhem—spirits with metal bodies have crashed on to the Spirit Board. Defeat them in battle to earn more gold than usual.<br/><br/>Legend-class spirits Mecha-Fiora and Ness's Father, along with Ace-class spirit Metal Mario will also appear at set times. Take a look! |author= Nintendo |published= February 26, 2019 |retrieved= March 1, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/66649363/index.html |title= Event: Hard 'n' Heavy Metal - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace Spirits befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
