{{Term/Store|The Noble Canteen||Series, BotW}} is a bar in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Noble Canteen|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Noble Canteen is a bar in [[Gerudo Town]] managed by [[Furosa]]. Furosa serves [[Noble Pursuit]], a drink that [[Pokki]] needs during the [[Side Quest]], "[[The Perfect Drink]]". Pokki will be found inside here talking to Furosa about the drink after the quest.<ref>{{Cite|I ask you, Furosa, is there anything better than a taste of {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}?|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> [[Pyra]], [[Sumati]] and [[Yaido]] can also be found inside here, talking about a password.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I was going to tell you... I finally got my hands on the {{Color|BotW Blue|password}} for you-know-where.|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> They stop talking about it as soon as they notice that [[Link]] is listening.<ref>{{Cite|You!! Did  you really think we wouldn't see you standing there, listening to our conversation?!|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> Link can eavesdrop on them from [[Rotana]]'s house nearby to find out the password, which will allow him entry to the [[Gerudo Secret Club]]. [[Deltan]] can be found sitting in the Canteen, hunched over and seemingly intoxicated. [[Nellie]], [[Pritana]] and [[Jules]] also visit the Canteen sometimes. A poster on the wall of The Noble Canteen shows how to make [[Chilly Mushroom Risotto]] by cooking [[Rock Salt]], [[Chillshroom]], [[Goat Butter]] and [[Hylian Rice]].
