{{Term/Store|Tadtone|Tadtones|Series, SS|plural}} are items in {{SS}}.<ref name="Name">{{Cite|Some Tadtones hang out in groups. You have to catch those similar Tadtones {{Color|SS Red|one right after the other}}, or they'll swim out of your score.|Parella|SS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
In order to get rid of monsters tainting her realm, [[Faron (Dragon)|Faron]] floods the [[Faron Woods]]. [[Link]] must then collect the Tadtones in order to prove that he is worthy to receive Faron's portion of the "[[Song of the Hero]]."<ref>{{Cite|As it happens, almost every inch of greenery in the area is submerged. So it's here, in this flooded land, that I will test you. If you're up to completing the task I ask of you, I see no reason why I shouldn't reward you with the melody you seek. As you just saw, I have split the melody you seek among my many Tadtones and I have hidden them around the forest. If you haven't already guessed, Link, here's your task: take this score and {{Color|SS Red|collect}} all my little musical friends out there. You can use that score to figure out how many Tadtones are in each group. Find them, collect them, and bring them back to me. If you can do that, well, then I'll just have to see you as someone worthy of learning the {{Color|SS Blue|Song of the Hero}}.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref>

Tadtones appear in six colors: red, yellow, green, cyan, violet, and magenta, denoting their membership in groups of several Tadtones each. Each same color group of Tadtones corresponds to a sequence of as many notes in the musical score and its members must be collected consecutively before other Tadtones are collected, otherwise they will leave Link's inventory and must be collected again.<ref name="Name"/> Members of a given group of Tadtones are generally found close together. This behaviour is useful for catching multiple Tadtones at a time by using the spin maneuver to pull them towards Link.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to catch a bunch at once, use a {{Color|SS Red|spin maneuver}} to {{Color|SS Red|draw in}} nearby Tadtones. That's your best bet.|Parella|SS}}</ref>

Tadtones replenish air when collected, however, purple bubbles of poison gas that rapidly deplete Link's [[Oxygen Gauge]] also appear. In some cases, violet Tadtones are camouflaged near the poison bubbles. Link must also avoid [[Blue Chuchu]]s, [[Froak]]s, and [[Cranioc]]s while collecting the Tadtones.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Link! Some nasty fish seem to have swam their way into the flooded woods. I'd hate to bump into those things underwater, kewww!|Bucha|SS}}</ref> Tadtones are said to get along with everyone, meaning they can be found where other fish swim.<ref>{{Cite|Tadtones get along with everybody. You can usually find them where {{Color|SS Red|other fish are swimming}}.|Parella|SS}}</ref>

It is possible to [[Dowsing|dowse]] for Tadtones, however, this option only becomes available partway through the challenge after Link has collected a certain number of Tadtones. The ability is obtained by talking to [[Bucha]].<ref>{{Cite|There was a technique you used when searching for the others of my tribe remember?  That!  Use that thing you do, kewww, and you'll have a much easier time getting those Tadtones!|Bucha|SS}}</ref> Link must stand on the floating vegetation or the top of the [[Great Tree]] in order to dowse.<ref>{{Cite|However, please note that you {{Color|SS Red|cannot use your dowsing ability underwater}}.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Tadtones resemble the [[Zora]] hatchlings from {{MM}}, which are also used to learn a [[New Wave Bossa Nova|new song]].

==Nomenclature==
Tadtones and the Zora hatchlings are based on a pun, as the Japanese word for "musical note" is the same as the word for "tadpole."
