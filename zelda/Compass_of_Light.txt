The {{Term/Store|Compass of Light||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Compass of Light is found beneath the altar of the [[Tower of Spirits]]. It is a special golden [[compass]] that uses the power of the [[Spirits of Good]] and has the ability to track the location of the [[Demon Train]]. Since [[Chancellor Cole]] didn't know where to find the Demon Train, he tricked [[Byrne]] into making this special compass to find the Demon Train in the [[Dark Realm]] and thus be able to free [[Malladus]] from his imprisonment.<ref>{{Cite|The Demon Train comes from the Dark Realm. But even Cole didn't know its exact location at first. He had me build a compass using the spirits' power to find the Demon Train in the Dark Realm.|Byrne|ST}}</ref>

When [[Anjean]] reveals she does not know where Malladus could be after his escape with Cole to the Dark Realm via the Demon Train, Byrne reveals that there is a special set of tracks located beneath the altar of the Tower of Spirits that leads to the Compass of Light.<ref>{{Cite|The area where Malladus was resurrected... Below that altar is a hidden section of tracks. Somewhere down there is the Compass of Light. Only it can lead you to the Demon Train.|Byrne|ST}}</ref> As it uses the power of the spirits, neither Cole nor Malladus can touch the Compass of Light.<ref>{{Cite|Since the compass uses the power of the spirits, Cole and Malladus can't touch it. That's why it should still be where we left it.|Byrne|ST}}</ref>

Once obtained, the Compass of Light reveals the tracks leading into the Dark Realm, which are located just west of [[Aboda Village]] in the [[Forest Realm]]. This enables [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] to go after Cole and Malladus for a final confrontation to restore Zelda to her body and destroy the villains.
