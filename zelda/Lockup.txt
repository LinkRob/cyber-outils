{{Term/Store|Lockup||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''East Passage'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Lockup}} is a series of dungeon cells found under the northeastern side of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Underground|link}}. The entrance is guarded by a {{Term|BotW|Decayed Guardian|link}} and marked {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone Deposit|link}}. The portcullis is placed over a small pool of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to lift it with {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} to gain entry into the {{Term|BotW|Lockup}}. It is also possible for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to enter from the side of the cliff bearing the {{Term|BotW|Lockup}}, placing him inside a cell opposite a {{Term|BotW|Glowing Eyeball}}. If defeated, the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Eyeball}} will release its control on a wooden {{Term|BotW|Lever|link}} placed between the two cells, consequently opening the parallel cells.

As the {{Term|BotW|Lever}} is broken, the third cell on the left-hand side can only be accessed through a cracked tile wall between the second and third cells. A second {{Term|BotW|Glowing Eyeball}} connected to a mass of {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}} occupies the switch between the fourth row of cells. The left-hand side hosts a cracked tile wall that connects to the {{Term|BotW|Water Pump Room|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|West Passage|link}}.

A spacious side chamber at the far end of the {{Term|BotW|Lockup}} was initially used for the Test of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard|link}}, a combat trial to determine those worthy of becoming royal knights.<ref>{{Cite|- Test of the Royal Guard -<br/>Strike down the giant foe to become recognized as a knight of the kingdom.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> Following the destruction of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}, it stands occupied by a lone {{Term|BotW|Stalnox|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hyrule Castle Underground<br/>{{Big|Stalnox}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the chamber, the portcullis at the entrance will close behind him, forcing him to face the {{Term|BotW|Stalnox}}. Once it has been defeated, the portcullis will lift back up.

Inside the {{Term|BotW|Lockup}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Box|link}}, which can be broken open for supplies. The end of the waterway beyond the cells bears a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}}. Inside the third cell rests a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} that contains a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}. After defeating the {{Term|BotW|Stalnox}}, a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} will appear, and a {{Term|BotW|Hylian Shield|link}} will be inside. Embedded within the {{Term|BotW|Stalnox}}'s bones are a {{Term|BotW|Royal Halberd|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Great Thunderblade|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Royal Broadsword|link}}.
