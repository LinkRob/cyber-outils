The {{Term/Store|Flute Boy||Series, ALttP}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 388 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, the Flute Boy's [[Man in Tavern|father]] can be found in the tavern in [[Kakariko Village]]. He tells [[Link]] that his son went off in search of the [[Triforce|Golden Power]] and never returned.<ref>{{Cite|My son really liked to play the flute, but he went to search for the Golden Power and never returned.|Man in Tavern|ALttP}}</ref> When Link visits the Kakariko Village Inn, he is told by the [[Innkeeper]] how the Flute Boy had a [[Flute Boy's Bird|pet bird]] who flew with him everywhere and how they went to [[Death Mountain#A Link to the Past|Death Mountain]] but never returned.<ref>{{Cite|He had a pet bird that went with him everywhere, but he went to the mountain and never returned.|Innkeeper|ALttP}}</ref> Link sees an apparition of the boy playing the [[Flute]] for the woodland [[animals]] of the [[Haunted Grove]] in the [[Light World]], but the apparition disappears as Link approaches. This happens every time the young hero enters the Haunted Grove prior to finding the Flute.

[[File:Flute Boy (Dark World).gif|left|35px]]
Later on, when Link enters the [[Dark World]] equivalent of the [[Haunted Grove]], he finds the Flute Boy transformed into a strange creature as a result of the [[Triforce|Golden Power]]'s effects by [[Ganon]].<ref>{{Cite|After wandering into this world I turned into this shape.|Flute Boy|ALttP}}</ref> The Flute Boy tells Link that he buried his [[Flute#A Link to the Past|Flute]] with some flower seeds in the Light World's Haunted Grove, asking him to search for it. When Link agrees to his request, the Flute Boy then gives him the [[Shovel]] so Link can dig it up. After Link finds the Flute and brings it back to him in the Dark World, the Flute Boy says he cannot play it anymore.<ref>{{Cite|But it looks like I can't play my flute any more. Please take it.|Flute Boy|ALttP}}</ref> He then asks Link to give it to his father.<ref>{{Cite|If by chance you go to the village I lived in, please give it to a tired old man.|Flute Boy|ALttP}}</ref> He finally requests Link to play the Flute so he can hear it one final time.<ref>{{Cite|Please let me hear the sound of the flute one last time...|Flute Boy|ALttP}}</ref> After Link [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/LTTP/LTTP_Flute_Extended.wav plays the Flute at its full theme], the boy petrifies into a [[tree]] and his apparitions cease to exist in the Light World, implying death.

[[File:LttP fluteboytree.gif|right]]
When Link brings the Flute to the Flute Boy's father, the man understands what has happened to his son and asks Link to keep it. He asks Link to play the Flute in front of the [[Weathercock]] in the center of Kakariko Village. When Link proceeds to comply with his request, the Flute Boy's pet bird breaks out of the Weathercock and flies off. Whenever Link plays the Flute in the Light World overworld from that point forward, the pet duck can [[Warping|transport]] him to one of eight locations. The Flute is required to reach [[Misery Mire (A Link to the Past)|Misery Mire]]. 

During the game's ending, the Flute Boy is shown back in his normal form reunited with his father and playing again for the forest creatures in the Haunted Grove.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, the Flute Boy is a resident of the Kakariko Village [[Milk Bar#A Link Between Worlds|Milk Bar]]. He refuses to speak to strangers,<ref name="strangers"/> but is willing to play various [[Milk Bar#Songs|songs]] for Link with his flute for a fee of 10 [[Rupees]].

==Trivia==
*In {{aLttP|-}}, the flute that the Flute Boy plays is actually an [[Ocarina]]. Ironically, in the original Japanese version, he is also named '''Ocarina Boy'''.
*[[Gulley]]'s visual design is based on the Flute Boy from {{ALttP|-}}.
*The Flute Boy in {{aLttP|-}} bares some similarities to [[Link]], as he is a lad who plays music, has long pointy ears and even wears green clothing with a floppy hat. Despite this, official artwork depicts his clothes as red.
**His appearance in {{aLBW|-}} is completely different, however, as the Flute Boy now looks more like a stereotypical nerd rather than an elf.
