The Energy Belt is an [[Accessory]] found in the [[Hebra Mountains]], where it is guarded by a [[Sword Moblin Lv. 3]]. Equipping it grants the [[Extra Energy Appearance Up +]] effect, increasing the drop rate of [[Energy]].
