==Location and uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
The Stepladder is found in [[Level 4]] in the First Quest and in [[Level 6]] in the [[Second Quest]] in {{TLoZ}}. It is used to traverse small spans of [[Water (Terrain)|Water]], [[Pit|Holes]], or [[Lava]], which would otherwise block [[Link]]'s progress. It is used automatically when Link needs to cross any of these obstacles.

==={{TMC|-}}===
Ladders are found across [[Hyrule]] in {{TMC}}. They can be found inside [[Tree]]s, [[Dig Cavern|Secret Caves]], [[Minish]]-sized cracks in the [[Overworld]], and within other cave systems throughout the land. They often lead down to rooms that contain [[Treasure Chest|Chests]] or the homes of [[Forest Picori]].

All the Ladders in the game already exist in place with the exception of one; after [[Link]] has [[Kinstone Fusion|Fused]] [[Kinstone Piece]]s with all four of the [[Tingle Siblings?]], a Ladder extends down from the center of [[North Hyrule Field]], leading to a large Chest containing the [[Magical Boomerang]].

==={{ZA|-}}===
In {{ZA}}, the Ladder can be found next to a tree in the [[Forest of Ogbam]]. Its only use is to bridge and cross the giant chasm near the entrance of the [[Shrine of Earth]].

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Stub|HWDE}}
In {{HW}}, Ladders appear as [[Item Card]]s in [[Adventure Mode]]. Additionally, in the Master Quest DLC Pack, the [[8-bit Ladder]] is the 8-bit [[Harp]] weapon used by [[Sheik]], and it has the same sprite as the Ladder in {{TLoZ|-}}.
