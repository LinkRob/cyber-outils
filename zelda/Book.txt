==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Library Book, Swordsman Newsletter}}
{{Plural|TMC|Book}} play a prominent role in obtaining the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}} in {{TMC}}. There are three overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}} that {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must retrieve in order to reach {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}: {{Term|TMC|A Hyrulean Bestiary|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|A History of Masks|link}}. When these three {{Plural|TMC|Book}} are found and returned, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can shrink down to {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} size and climb their spines to reach {{Term|TMC|Librari}}, who will let {{Term|TMC|Link}} to face his {{Term|TMC|Trial|link}} and reward him with the {{Term|TMC|Flippers}}.

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Postman|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Marcy|link}} appears in the {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}} and begins selling volumes of the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}} for 200 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} each. There are a total of eight volumes, and a new volume becomes available each time {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains a {{Term|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}}.
