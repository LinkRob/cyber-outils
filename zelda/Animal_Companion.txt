The {{Term/Store|Animal Companion|Animal Companions|Series, OoA, OoS|plural}} are three characters in {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}: {{Term|OoS|Ricky|link}}, {{Term|OoS|Moosh|link}}, and {{Term|OoS|Dimitri|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250}}</ref> They become {{Term|OoS|Link|link}}'s {{Plural|OoS|Mount|link}} when he needs their special abilities to traverse certain terrain.

==Biography==
{{Lacking Sources}}
{{Term|OoS|Link|link}} gains the ability to {{Term|OoS|Call|link}} only one of the {{Plural|OoS|Animal Companion}} by obtaining either {{Term|OoS|Ricky's Flute|link}}, {{Term|OoS|Moosh's Flute|link}}, or {{Term|OoS|Dimitri's Flute|link}}. Which Flute he receives is determined by whether and how he obtains the {{Term|OoS|Strange Flute|link}}. In a [[Linked Game]], Link partners with the same {{Term|OoS|Animal Companion}} as before.

The terrain of the {{Term|OoA|Nuun Highlands|link}} and the {{Term|OoS|Natzu|link}} region vary to suit the chosen {{Term|OoS|Animal Companion}}'s abilities. {{Term|OoA|Ricky|link}} can leap up cliffs and over holes, {{Term|OoA|Moosh|link}} can fly, and {{Term|OoA|Dimitri|link}} can swim against currents and up waterfalls.

In {{OoA}}, Link mounts only one of the {{Plural|OoS|Animal Companion}}, to traverse {{Term|OoA|Nuun Highlands}}. In {{OoS}}, he mounts all three. In addition to {{Term|OoS|Natzu}}, Link needs the Animal Companions in {{Term|OoS|Holodrum Plain|link}} ({{Term|OoS|Ricky}}), {{Term|OoS|Mt. Cucco|link}} ({{Term|OoS|Moosh}}), {{Term|OoS|Sunken City|link}} ({{Term|OoS|Dimitri}}).
