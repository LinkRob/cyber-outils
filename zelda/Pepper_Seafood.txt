{{Term/Store|Pepper Seafood|Pepper Seafoods|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Pepper Seafood}}<br/>The pepper seeds grilled with this seafood draw out its taste and pleasant aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Pepper Seafood by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Spicy Pepper]] with any type of seafood in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The default can only be made by using seafood that has contradictory effects to Spicy Peppers. When eaten, they will heal some of [[Link|Link's]] {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The seeds from the Spicy Peppers draw out these taste of these dish, in addition to providing a pleasant aroma.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Only one variation of Pepper Seafood can be made:

* '''Spicy Pepper Seafood''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Pepper Seafood}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The pepper seeds grilled with this seafood draw out its taste and pleasant aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}}. Eating these meals will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>
