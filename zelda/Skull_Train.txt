The {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} can be attached to the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}} upon purchase from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}}, or at any time thereafter at {{Term|ST|Alfonzo|link}}'s house in {{Term|ST|Aboda Village|link}}. Used together, they give the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}} a durability of 6 Hearts, up from its default of 4 Hearts. Aside from this boost in durability, the {{Term|ST|Skull Train}} is merely a change in appearance to suit the player's personal tastes.
