{{Term/Store|Ronny||Series}} is a highly-trained bodyguard that appears in {{FPTRR}}.{{Fact}} [[Tingle]] will not encounter him until he is well into his journey.

Ronny lives in the eastern-most part of [[Gooey Swamp]], in a small area that is difficult to find. He comes at a very high price, as he is the most skilled of the bodyguards. It is said that he is an old friend of [[Yamori]].
