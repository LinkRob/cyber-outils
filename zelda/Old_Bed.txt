The Old Bed is an [[Echo]] from {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
{{hatnote|”This bed is a bit worn out, but you'll still get sleepy if you lie down on it.” — [[Notebook]]<ref name="Old Bed">{{Cite|This bed is a bit worn out, but you'll still get sleepy if you lie down on it.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Old Bed is an Echo that can be used to bridge gaps and reach otherwise inaccessible areas. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can regain health by sleeping in the old bed, or any other bed found in {{EoW|-}}. They also float on water making them ideal to get Zelda to dry land.
==Trivia==

* Beds have become an infamous meme, primarily the old bed due to it's lowest cost, due to how useful they are outside of their basic function, being the best bridges for Zelda before the water block and clouds but even after combining them with tornados she could literally use beds to fly to get above the world's boundary or even boundary break to Clonde's area.
