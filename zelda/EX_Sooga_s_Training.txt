{{Term/Store|EX Sooga's Training||HWAoC}} is a level 53 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Sooga's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the second wave of the game’s {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian of Remembrance|link}}.
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Sooga's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} receives a reward of ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Energetic Rhino Beetle|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Voltfin Trout|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Cold Darner|link}}, 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

For the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within six minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}, aside from a {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}}. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} reaches 30 KOs, more {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} spawn. At 100 KOs, {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} spawn again alongside another {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}}. More {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} spawn again at 150, 200, and 250 KOs, with a {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} spawning yet again at 200. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} reaches his objective, a certain number of {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} spawn in order for there to be a total of three in the  Once all of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} are defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.
