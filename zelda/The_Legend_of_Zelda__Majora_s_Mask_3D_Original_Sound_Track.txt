{{Term/Store|''The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D Original Sound Track''||Series, MM3D}} (ゼルダの伝説　ムジュラの仮面」オリジナル・サウンドトラック) is the original soundtrack for {{MM3D}}. It features two discs, a miniposter, and a rubber keychain.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= |retrieved= June 20, 2016|url= http://www.otomart.jp/SHOP/TSCM-0029.html|title= The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D Original Soundtrack|site= Otomart|type=}}</ref> It is exclusive to Japan.

Much of the soundtrack is identical to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask Official Soundtrack|original soundtrack]], but has been remixed for the 3DS' sound system, and with the addition of the 3DS end credits theme "Staff Roll 2" arranged by Naoto Kubo. There is a [https://github.com/DeathWrench/Majora-s-Mask-N64-OST-to-3DS mod] that replaces the 3DS soundtrack with the original N64 soundtrack.[https://github.com/DeathWrench/Majora-s-Mask-N64-OST-to-3DS]
