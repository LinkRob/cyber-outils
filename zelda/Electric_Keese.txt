==Characteristics==
==={{SS|-}}===
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Target lock: {{Color|SS Blue|Thunder Keese}}</p><p>Found in desert habitats, these winged monsters are attracted to dark places, such as caves. Their internal organs generate electricity for attacking prey. </p><p>They often gather in colonies, are active at night, and sleep in the day. Thunder Keese are similar to the fiery variant of [[Fire Keese]], but differ as they have electrified bodies as their main form of defense. They inhabit the [[Lanayru Desert]] area, and like other electrified enemies in that region, they produce small bursts of electricity that will stun and harm [[Link]] if he strikes one while it is shrouded in electricity. Though Thunder Keese are weak, they can be dangerous in large groups as they may constantly electrocute Link, giving him little opportunity to defend himself. A safe and effective way to eliminate them is to use the [[Shield Bash]] technique, knocking them to the ground, then finishing them with the [[Fatal Blow]].

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Electric Keese}}
{{Term|BotW|Electric Keese}} appear in {{BotW}}. They can be found in a variety of places such as [[East Necluda]], [[Lanayru Great Spring]] and the [[Gerudo Desert]]. When attacking Link, they have the ability to shock him, which will stun him and cause him to drop any metal weapons he may be holding. Upon their defeat, Electric Keese may drop [[Keese Eyeball]]s or [[Electric Keese Wing]]s.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Electric Keese|Wreathed in electricity, this flying monster shocks and paralyzes any foe it touches.<ref name="EoW Electric Keese">{{Cite|Wreathed in electricity, this flying monster shocks and paralyzes any foe it touches.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Electric Keese are a variant of keese capable of electricity-based attacks. They are found throughout [[Faron Temple]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Electric Keese [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], and summoning one costs four Echo energy.
