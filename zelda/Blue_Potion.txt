{{Term/Store|Blue Potion|Blue Potions|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD, TMC, TP, TPHD, ALBW}},{{Exp Game|OoT, MM, TWW, TMC, TP, ALBW}}<ref name="E 117">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 117 {{Exp Game|ALttP, OoT, MM, TWW, TMC, TP, ALBW}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Medicine of Life and Magic|Medicines of Life and Magic|ALttP}},{{Exp Game|ALttP}}<ref name="E 117"/> and '''Cure-All Medecine''',{{Exp Game|ALttP}}<ref>{{Cite|CURE-ALL MEDECINE|Sub Screen|ALttP}}</ref>{{Note|In the original version of {{ALttP|-}} for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]], the name is stylized '''Cure·All Medecine''' on the [[Sub Screen]], in all caps.'''}} is a recurring [[Potion]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}, commonly used to instantly restore both [[Link]]'s [[Life Gauge]] and [[Magic Meter]]. Typically, it is known as a "cure-all" Potion that is effective in recovering many to all of Link's [[Heart]]s in addition to restoring his Magic when present in the game.<ref>{{Cite|I just brewed up this cure-all!|Kotake|MM}}</ref> Furthermore, the Potion has been noted to be easy for Link to consume.<ref>{{Cite|It goes down easy and gets right to work.|Kotake|MM}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
===Inception and Original Usage===
Prior to the Potions, the earlier games in the series had introduced [[Medicine]]. In {{TLoZ}}, the blue Medicine could be purchased from various [[Old Woman]]s after receiving the [[Letter]] from an [[Old Man]] in a cave in northeast [[Hyrule]], and then showing it to them. Once the Letter is delivered, the blue and red Medicine will become available for Link to purchase, costing 40 and 68 [[Rupee]]s, respectively. However, unlike later versions of the Blue Potion that could replenish both Magic and health, it was merely a one time use health Potion, due to Magic not existing in the game. Potions debuted in {{ALttP}}, which was also the first game where the Blue Potion was used to restore both health and Magic. Just like [[Red Potion|Red]] and [[Green Potion]]s, Link needs an [[Empty Bottle]] to store the Potion.

In [[OoT]] a [[Odd Mushroom]] can be recieved from the [[Master Craftman's Son]], this is part of the [[Biggoron's Sword Trading Quest]]. Once [[Granny]] has recieved the [[Odd Mushroom]] she will begin to sell [[Blue Potion]]s. They will be sold for 100 [[Rupees]]. It will fully heal all hearts and refill your [[Magic Meter]].

In {{MM}}, Link can either purchase a Bottle of Blue Potion from the [[Ikana Canyon]] [[Business Scrub]] for 100 Rupees, or give a [[Magic Mushroom]] to [[Kotake]] at the [[Magic Hags' Potion Shop]], who will then use it to create Blue Potions and sell them for 60 Rupees each. They also cure the [[Curse]] that [[Blue Bubble]]s inflict.

In {{TWW}}, there are three ways to obtain the Blue Potion. The first is to collect four [[Boko Baba Seed]]s from defeating Boko Baba]]s and then giving them to [[Hollo]], who will mix a Blue Potion for Link. The second way is to collect 15 [[Blue Chu Jelly]]s from [[Blue ChuChu]]s and trade them to [[Doc Bandam]] on [[Windfall Island]]. He will use them to make a Blue Potion and give Link the first sample for free, and from then on will have it for sale at 60 Rupees. The third is to throw [[All-Purpose Bait]] in front of a [[Rat]] home (the hole on the wall that they run out of). The Rat may then sell you a Blue Potion for 80 Rupees.

===As a Health Restorer Only===
Blue Potions in {{TMC}} restore up to eight Hearts only.

In {{TP}}, Blue Potions can be obtained by defeating a [[Blue Chuchu]] and scooping its [[Chu Jelly]] into an Empty Bottle. The blue jelly of a Blue Chuchu acts like a Red Potion, restoring all Hearts. Blue Potions can also be bought from the [[Goron]]'s son in [[Kakariko Village]], the shop in the [[City in the Sky]], or from [[Malo Mart]] in [[Hyrule Castle]]. Blue Potions only restore Hearts, as the Magic Meter does not appear in {{TP|-}}.

In {{ALBW}}, the Blue Potion completely restores all of Link's Hearts, making it the most powerful health-restoring Potion in the game. It can be bought from the [[Witch's House]] for 200 [[Rupee]]s and ten [[Monster Tail]]s.
