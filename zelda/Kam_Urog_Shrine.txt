The {{Term/Store|Kam Urog Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kam Urog Shrine}}<br/>Trial of Passing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Trial of Passing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The Kam Urog Shrine Shrine is located in a site northeast of {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|link}}, initially buried beneath the ground. In order to access it, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must complete the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Cursed Statue|link}}". The Quest can be accessed by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Calip|link}} and referring to him as "doctor",<ref>{{Cite|[Of course, Dr. Calip.] {{Color|BotW Gray|{{Small|Doctor...}}}} Did I...just hear you call me doctor? As in, not merely Calip, but DOCTOR Calip? It rolled off your tongue so naturally. It was downright salivary! Herm... This changes things...|Calip|BotW}}</ref> at which point he will feel charitable and share with Link some of his research.<ref>{{Cite|Very well then! I'm feeling charitable, so I'll let a few things spill. This is just between us, understand. "When a dark light resides in the cursed statue's eyes, pierce its gaze to purge the seal from the shrine."|Calip|BotW}}</ref> It can be accessed again at any time be reading {{Term|BotW|An Ancient Text|link}}, which is found in Calip's cabin.

If Link approaches the statues at night, the eyes of one will begin to glow purple. Shooting it with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} will cause Kam Urog Shrine to emerge from the ground, allowing Link to access it. As Link enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the Shrine's {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kam Urog|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kam Urog. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kam Urog|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The Kam Urog Shrine Shrine contains a wheel-like chamber in which gears rotate the inner section. These gears propel the interior platforms which rotate along the cylindrical body, providing Link a way to ascend the chamber. The gears can temporarily be stopped using the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, freezing the corresponding platforms.

Near the top of the cylindrical chamber are two flights of stairs, which must be connected with either of the floating platforms that hold stairs. This leads to the pathway to Kam Urog.

Upon greeting him, he will present Link with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Kam Urog|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Kam Urog|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}} can be found in an aperture along the wall. A second Treasure Chest connected to a rotating platform inside the cylindrical chamber contains a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Spear|link}}.
