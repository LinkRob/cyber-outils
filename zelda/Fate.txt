The {{Term/Store|Fate|Fates|Series, CoH|plural}} are characters in {{CoH}}.<ref name="Sheikah Stone">{{Cite|Hey, look! A {{Color|CoH Yellow|Sheikah Stone}}! Did you know that if you are defeated, the Fates will return you to the world at any {{Color|CoH Yellow|Sheikah Stone}} you've activated?|Trill|CoH}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Plural|CoH|Fate}} are elderly {{Plural|CoH|Fortune Teller|link}} who weave {{Term|CoH|Triforce|link}}-shaped quilts in {{CoH}}. There are four {{Plural|CoH|Fate}} throughout {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}. Three {{Plural|CoH|Fate}} can be found across {{Term|CoH|Hyrule}} inside huts marked with the {{Term|CoH|Crest of the Sheikah|link}} while one can be found upon a {{Term|CoH|Game Over}} and in the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}}. Each {{Term|CoH|Fate}} operates a shop where they offer to read fortunes for 10 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, fate's chosen one. I've been expecting you. Would you like me to read your future for 10 rupees?|Fate|CoH}}</ref> In addition, the {{Plural|CoH|Fate}} found in the overworld as well as the {{Term|CoH|Fate}} in the {{Term|CoH|Future World}} will sell {{Plural|CoH|Health Potion|link}} and {{Plural|CoH|Stamina Potion|link}} for {{Plural|CoH|Rupee}} and {{Plural|CoH|Diamond|link}}, respectively, provided the heroes have a {{Term|CoH|Bottle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe you'd like to buy a {{Color|CoH Yellow|potion}}? You'll need an {{Color|CoH Yellow|empty bottle}} to put it in first!|Fate|CoH}}</ref> Whenever a hero has fallen, they are taken to a {{Term|CoH|Fate}} who will return them to {{Term|CoH|Hyrule}}.<ref name="Sheikah Stone"/> This {{Term|CoH|Fate}} will also sell various equipment in exchange for {{Plural|CoH|Diamond}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hello, dear. This is where adventurers with unfinished business end up. It looks like the Fates aren't done with you yet! Why not take something along to help you on your quest? You never know what might come in handy in the future!|Fate|CoH}}</ref>

Whenever the {{Plural|CoH|Fate}} read a hero's fortune, a waypoint will be added to the {{Term|CoH|Map|link}}, pointing the heroes in the direction of their destiny. The power of the {{Plural|CoH|Fate}} has been waning since {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} stole their {{Term|CoH|Thread of Wisdom|link|display=Threads of Wisdom}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry I can't tell you more. The future is cloudy... My visions have been limited since {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}} stole my {{Color|CoH Yellow|Thread of Wisdom}}.|Fate|CoH}}</ref> {{Term|CoH|Thread of Courage|link|display=Courage}},<ref>{{Cite|My {{Color|CoH Yellow|Thread of Courage}} was stolen by {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}}. He used it to construct a lute. What? Of course I knew it was going to happen! But I couldn't do anything about it. It's not easy to change one's fate, you know.|Fate|CoH}}</ref> and {{Term|CoH|Thread of Power|link|display=Power}} to construct his {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I recently foretold {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}}'s future. I told him he would meet his demise at {{Color|CoH Blue|Ganon}}'s hand in the future. He didn't like it, but that's what I saw! Serves him right for stealing my {{Color|CoH Yellow|Thread of Power}}...|Fate|CoH}}</ref> Consequently, the {{Plural|CoH|Fate}} have lost sense of the past and future and have been forced to move in a rhythm by the power of the {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you wondering what's causing our movement to be controlled? It all starts in the {{Color|CoH Blue|future}}...or was it in the past?|Fate|CoH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In any case, it's the power of the {{Color|CoH Yellow|Golden Lute}}. The magician {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}} can use it to compel people to follow his orders! He must really like dancing...|Fate|CoH}}</ref> Though their ability to see through time has been limited, one of the {{Plural|CoH|Fate}} is able to advise {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} to travel 25 years into the future to stop {{Term|CoH|Ganon|link}} in the {{Term|CoH|Future World}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Blue|Cadence}}! You need to get yourself to the future immediately! ... FURTHER to the future that is. 25 years, to be exact. That's not so far, in the grand scheme of things.|Fate|CoH}}</ref> dismissing the span of time as insignificant. As they have had a significant amount of time to weave, they are capable of doing so with their eyes closed.<ref>{{Cite|It's not easy to weave with your eyes closed. Luckily, I've had a lot of time to practice.|Fate|CoH}}</ref>
