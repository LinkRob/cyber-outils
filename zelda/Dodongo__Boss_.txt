{{Term/Store|Dodongo|Dodongos|Series, TLoZ|plural}} are a recurring [[Boss]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 175 {{Exp Game|TLoZ}}}}</ref> They are saurian monsters that resemble a {{Wp|Ceratopsidae|ceratopsid dinosaur}} or a {{Wp|rhinoceros}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= A giant rhinoceros.|game= TLoZ|page= 37}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Dodongo|A giant rhinoceros. It hasn't got so much attacking power, but it bounces off attacks with its thick hide.}}
In {{TLoZ}}, a single Dodongo appears as the [[Boss]] of {{Term|TLoZ|Level 2|link}} of the First Quest. In addition, Dodongos appear in a group of three as the Boss of {{Term|TLoZ|Level 3|link}} and {{Term|TLoZ|Level 8|link}} of the [[Second Quest]]. Each group guards a fragment of the [[Triforce of Wisdom]]. Dodongos also appear in {{Term|TLoZ|Level 5|link}} and {{Term|TLoZ|Level 7|link}} of the First Quest as well as {{Term|TLoZ|Level 1|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Level 4|link}}, and Level-8 of the Second Quest. [[Link]] must use [[Bomb]]s to defeat Dodongos, either by placing two Bombs in their mouth or by Bombing their side and hitting them once with his {{Term|TLoZ|Sword|link}}. If they are defeated with a Sword, Link is rewarded with four Bombs. When defeated with Bombs, Dodongos may drop either a [[Heart]], a [[Fairy]], or one [[Rupee]].{{Note|If {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} defeats 10 enemies without getting hit or teleporting with the {{Term|TLoZ|Recorder|link}}, then the 10th enemy defeated will drop five {{Plural|TLoZ|Rupee|link}}. However, if the 10th enemy is defeated with a {{Term|TLoZ|Bomb|link}}, then it will drop four {{Plural|TLoZ|Bomb}}. If {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} defeats 16 enemies without getting hit or teleporting, then the 16th enemy will drop a {{Term|TLoZ|Fairy|link}}. After the 16th enemy, {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} must get hit and reset the counter to zero in order to achieve this effect again. Defeating {{Plural|TLoZ|Armos|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Like Like|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Keese|link}}, or {{Plural|TLoZ|Gel|link}} will not be counted.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= n.a. |published= n.d. |retrieved= August 17, 2017 |url= http://redcandle.us/The_Legend_of_Zelda#Forced_drops |title= Forced Drops |site= redcandle.us}}</ref>}} After defeating the whole group, Link is rewarded with a [[Heart Container]].

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, Dodongo appears as the Boss of [[Snake's Remains]]. Link must equip the [[Power Bracelet]] to pick up and throw Dodongo into [[Floor Spikes]] after Bombing it.

==={{FSA|-}}===
Dodongos appear as a Boss in {{FSA}} in the [[Tower of Flames]]. They attack by emitting streams of [[Fire]] from their mouths. Later in the game, they are also found in the [[Realm of the Heavens (Stage)|Realm of the Heavens]].
