The {{Term/Store|Royal Palace's Throne Room||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 320}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace's Throne Room}} is a part of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. It consists of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|display=Palace}}'s first floor, and it contains {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|link}} and the pedestal for the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}. During the day, {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} will sit on her {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|display=Throne}}, which is constantly guarded by {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}}. The guards {{Term|BotW|Bertri|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Katta|link}} guard the interior of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace's Throne Room|display=Throne Roome}}.

{{Term|BotW|Riju}} greets {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he first enters the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace's Throne Room}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yet another traveler... How did you get in here?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> However, she takes note of his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, calling it something interesting.<ref>{{Cite|It seems you have something rather interesting there...|Riju|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches, but once he reaches the steps to {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne}}, {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} demands he stop and declare his business.<ref>{{Cite|You stand before {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Riju}}, chief of the Gerudo! Declare your business, but come no closer!|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> Believing that he is more than the average traveler, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} halts {{Term|BotW|Buliara}}'s questioning and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s name.<ref>{{Cite|Hold on Buliara... This one appears to be more than a common traveler. You there—what is your name?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She then asks what his purpose his coming so far to meet her.<ref>{{Cite|Link... And what is it you've come all the way here to tell me, Link?|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

At first, {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} does not believe that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is capable of calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I can calm Naboris.] You think you have what it takes to subdue something so powerful as a Divine Beast?|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> as all of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} that were capable of controling it died 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|The only ones who could ever control them were Champions like Lady Urbosa. And all of the Champions died in the {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity}} 100 years ago.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} remembers her mother telling her a story of a fallen swordsman that the {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link|display=princess}} put into a deep sleep during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Buliara, a memory just jumped into my head—something my mother spoke of. When the Calamity happened, the princess of Hyrule placed a fallen swordsman into a deep sleep.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> Though the story always seemed more like a legend than history, the swordsman she heard of was also named {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That swordsman, much like our new friend here, was named Link...though it always seemed more legend than fact.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Riju}} points out that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is carrying a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} relic,<ref>{{Cite|What of the device on this one's hip? It appears to be a precious relic of the Sheikah.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> which means he must be more than a simple wanderer to possess it.<ref>{{Cite|I can't imagine they would give something so valuable to a simple drifter—can you?|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} can not recall a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman among the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}},<ref>{{Cite|...I don't remember ever hearing of a Hylian vai among the Champions.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> she quickly realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is actually a man.<ref>{{Cite|Wait a moment... You're a voe!|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Champions}} and is there to help with {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} refuses to throw him out.<ref>{{Cite|A voe within our walls is a great crime. But a voe who is a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}}... Well, we'd never mistreat a fried of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa}}. And if you're here to help us with {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}, then we are allies.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> Though the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} is dangerous,<ref>{{Cite|You saw it on your way to our town. {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}, cloaked in a massive sanstorm, hurling lightning at any who dare approach.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} have been unable to find a way to calm it on their own.<ref>{{Cite|We have to do something to stop it, but we have yet to find any way to appease the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} on our own.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She believes that {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s role as a {{Term|BotW|Champions}} will allow him to enter and tame {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you truly are a Champion, perhaps you will be able to enter {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} and calm its anger.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Buliara}} interrupts, urging {{Term|BotW|Riju}} to reconsider entrusting such a task to a stranger.<ref>{{Cite|Lady Riju! I don't see how you can trust a complete stranger with something as important as this task.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> Instead, she suggests he prove himself by retrieving the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}.<ref>{{Cite|Perhaps if this person were first to prove his worth by recovering your stolen Thunder Helm...|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Riju}} likes this suggestion,<ref>{{Cite|Ah, yes... An interesting suggestion.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> explaining that the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} is the only thing that can resist the lightning of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}.<ref>{{Cite|You see, there is only one thing in all of Hyrule that can withstand the {{Color|BotW Blue|lightning}} from {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}. The {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}, a family heirloom and relic of the Gerudo. But as Buliara says, it was stolen from us.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} sends {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Teake|link|display=Captain Teake}} in the barracks to learn more about the thieves.<ref>{{Cite|Our soldiers can tell you all you need to know about the thieves who stole Lady Riju's heirloom. Head through this arch. It will lead you to the barracks. Then, seek out {{Color|BotW Blue|Captain Teake}}, and ask for a full report.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> Without the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will never be able to approach {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|display=Naboris}}.<ref>{{Cite|Without my heirloom, you'll never be able to approach {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> However, she sees {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s appearance as proof that {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} is looking out for them.<ref>{{Cite|Heh, you know... I take heart in having such a rare visitor as yourself. {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa}} must be looking out for us.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace's Throne Room}} to speak to {{Term|BotW|Riju}} after he has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you're back?!|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She is glad to see that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is alright, and while she is still worried about the greater threat to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, she is relieved that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} will no longer be in danger from the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm glad you're OK... {{Small|Truly, I am...}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} is no longer a threat to the town... I'm still worried about the larger threat to Hyrule, but at least my people won't be trouble by {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} anymore.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> Behind {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne}} is two {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, the contents of which {{Term|BotW|Riju}} offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} to show her appreciation.<ref>{{Cite|I want to offer you these as thanks for your help. I trust you'll accept them.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> Inside are the {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker|link}}, which are treasured by the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} as belongings of {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}.<ref>{{Cite|These pieces of equipment are priceless treasures of the Gerudo people. They were once worn by {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa}}.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} was friends with {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} believes it is right for him to have them.<ref>{{Cite|Since you and {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa}} were friends, it's only right that you have them.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

After completing "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)}}", {{Term|BotW|Link}} can attempt to take the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} from the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace's Throne Room}}. {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} stops him, as his status as a hero does not give him permission to touch it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Halt! That is the treausre of the Gerudo, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Thunder Helm}}!}} You may be held in high esteem, but this does not give you the freedom to lay your hands on the pride of our people.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Riju}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} to calm down and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he desires the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|display=Helm}}.<ref>{{Cite|Becalm yourself, Buliara. You...desire this helm?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> Despite the deeds {{Term|BotW|Link}} has done for the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} is unable to give it to him,<ref>{{Cite|I see... And yet, despite the great debt we owe you, I cannot just give such a precious thing to you.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> but since {{Term|BotW|Link}} is fighting the enemy of {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}, she offers to lend it to him.<ref>{{Cite|...But you ARE up against the enemy of Lady Urbosa... And if you truly desire it, then perhaps I can lend it to you...|Riju|BotW}}</ref> In order to earn the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must gain the trust of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well then, let's see how serious you are. This helm is a cultural treasure. To earn it, you must gain the trust of our people.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> By solving the worries of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will prove himself worthy.<ref>{{Cite|I want you to address their concerns and bring a resolute peace to our town.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> This begins "{{Term|BotW|The Thunder Helm|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed all of the {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} shares that she has heard of what he has done and offers her thanks.<ref>{{Cite|I have heard of your recent actions. You have returned smiles to the faces of my people... On behalf of all Gerudo, I must offer you my heartfelt thanks. Sarqso.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She declares {{Term|BotW|Link}} a true friend of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} and allows him to take the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}.<ref>{{Cite|You are, from this moment on, a true friend of the Gerudo! And if it can help a true friend in need, we will gladly lend you this sacred treasure.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> More than anyone else, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} believes {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to use it to its fullest potential.<ref>{{Cite|I know that you, more than any other, will be able to use this {{Color|BotW Blue|Thunder Helm}} to its fullest potential... I believe you can unlock the power of Urbosa that lies within.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

When {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack is active, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to obtain the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}. This {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}} begins inside the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace's Throne Room}}, with {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} and her {{Term|BotW|Attendant|link}} standing in front of {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}, who is sitting upon the {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|display=Throne}}. {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} accepts {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} request for her to be a {{Term|BotW|Champions}},<ref>{{Cite|Princess of Hyrule, I accept your proposition without hesitation.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> explaining that their enemies, the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}}, have been planning to steal their {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} along with resurrecting {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Not only are they planning to resurrect Calamity Ganon, it seems the Yiga Clan is also after our treasured heirloom.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> She vows not to rest until the {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Calamity}} falls.<ref>{{Cite|You have my word. I won't rest until the Calamity falls.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> On behalf of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gerudo chief Urbosa... On behalf of Hyrule and its king, I thank you.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>
