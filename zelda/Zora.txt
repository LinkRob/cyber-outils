The {{Term/Store|Zora|Zora|Series, BotW|plural}},<ref name= "Encyclopedia"/> also known as the '''Zora Tribe''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 175 {{Exp Game|OoT3D}}, 214 {{Exp Game|OoA}} & 285 {{Exp Game|TPHD}}}}</ref> are a race in {{TLoZ|Series}}. The Zora are an aquatic folk who possess fish and amphibian-like traits, including fins, webbed feet, and gills, which allow them to swim and thrive underwater. Zora can come in many shapes and sizes, but are most commonly depicted as humanoids with long tail fins on their heads and finned appendages on their arms and legs. They are protectors of water.<ref name= "Encyclopedia"/>

Prior to their debut as a friendly race in {{OoT}}, Zoras were present in the earliest titles as a [[River Zora|hostile enemy]] where they attack [[Link]]. These Zora are radically different from their friendly counterpart, appearing more reptilian and more fish-like than a human. Both kinds of Zora have appeared throughout the series following their debuts, with the non-hostile Zora race appearing almost exclusively in the 3D console games. In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Oracle of Ages]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]'', the only games where both friendly and antagonistic Zora coexist, the enemy Zora are referred to as "River Zora" to distinguish them from the peaceful race.<ref name="Sea Zora">{{Cite|Don't think us noble Sea Zoras the same as those savage, vulgar River Zoras!|Zora|OoA}}</ref> However, these "River Zora" are not hostile enemies in the latter game.

==Characteristics==
[[File:TLoZ Series Crest of the Zora Symbol.png|thumb|213x213px|The Crest of the Zora|left]]
Zora are normally found in or near large bodies of water, but very rarely stray far away from them. due to their aquatic nature. In [[Hyrule]], Zora live in the [[Zora's Domain]], a large cavernous fountain that is connected to the [[Zora River]], which flows directly to [[Lake Hylia]]; it is common to see Zora within these areas as well. Zora, however, do not solely live in fresh water, but can also thrive in salt water, such as the [[Great Bay]] Zora of {{MM}} and the sea Zora of {{OoA|-}}. In contrast to the enemy Zora in the early 2D titles, the peaceful Zora appear more distinctively humanoid than monstrous. Most individuals (but not all) have fins attached to their limbs and a tail at the back of their head, making their head alone resemble a fish. However, Zora also came in various shapes and sizes, with some lacking the head fin and others, like [[King Zora]], having a rotund appearance. Some Zora possess fins that greatly resemble the folds of clothing, such as the case with [[Rutela]]. Their skin color ranges between white, blue and gray, but few have also appeared bright red or brown as a seemingly rarer variant.
[[File:MM Baby Zoras Model.png|thumb|180px|Newly-hatched Zora babies in {{MM|-}}]]
Zora lay [[Zora Egg|Eggs]]<ref>{{Cite|Isn't this... a Zora egg?|Tatl|MM}}</ref> which hatch into tadpole-like young. Their Eggs hatch one to three days after they are laid,<ref>{{Cite|It takes about one to three days from the time Zora Eggs are laid for them to hatch.|Professor|MM}}</ref> provided that the water they lie in is of a proper temperature,<ref>{{Cite|Zora eggs are very sensitive to changes in the temperature.|Professor|MM}}</ref> and that the Eggs that were laid at the same time are together.<ref>{{Cite|Zora eggs won't hatch unless all eggs that were laid at the same time are brought together.|Professor|MM}}</ref> While they resemble tadpoles when they first hatch{{Fact}}, they become humanoid even before reaching adulthood as seen with both [[Ruto|Princess Ruto]] in {{OoT|-}} and multiple Zora children in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]''.

Zora are known to have long lifespans, with [[Sidon]] being a child 100 years before {{BotW|-}} and a young adult in the game's present, which indicates a de-accelerated aging process compared to [[Hylian|Hylains]], in contrast to the [[Gerudo]]. The King Zora in ''Oracle of Ages'' is the same individual in the game's past and present times, 400 years apart. Zora are considered young until they reach triple digits.<ref>{{Cite|She is a practically a child still, not even in the triple digits, yet look what she has accomplished. I am so proud!|Rivan|TotK}}</ref> Zora children age in such a way where even as young adults they can still appear to be children before reaching a growth spurt.<ref>{{Cite|It's really terrible to be at that awkward Zora age where I'm a young woman but I haven't had my growth spurt yet.|Finley|BotW}}</ref> As Zora age, they hold themselves to higher standards of speaking as a matter of pride.<ref>{{Cite|Yep! It's a Zora thing. As we age, we hold ourselves to higher speaking standards. It's a matter of pride.|Tona|BotW}}</ref>

Zora have a strong sense of duty for history and order. Because they do not travel far from sources of water and are protective of Zora's Domain, they have limited interaction with other races.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Zora take pride in a strong sense of duty and respect for the history, laws, and leadership of their people. They do not often stray far from water, so interaction with other races tends to be limited. This is especially true of Zora's Domain. Typically, the only Hylians allowed in this central hub are official messengers of the Hyrulean royal family. |book= E |publisher= |page= 48}}</ref>

Zora primarily subsist on [[fish]] or other marine life, and many are skilled fishers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Zora primarily subsist on fish and other marine life, and many are skilled fishers.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=48}}</ref> They can swim against the current and even up waterfalls.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Zora can swim against the current and even up waterfalls.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=48}}</ref>

The Zora have maintained positive relations with the Hylians since ancient times. However, in the timeline of Hyrule's decline that bond gradually fades.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Zora have maintained positive relations with their Hylian counterparts since ancient times. However, in the timeline of Hyrule's decline, that bond gradually fades.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=48}}</ref>

The Zora, along with Hylians and [[Goron|Gorons]], have often been called to watch over Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Zora, along with Hylians and Gorons, have often be called to watch over Hyrule.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=48}}</ref>

Women of their royal family have been given the crucial role of sages in times of need. In addition, when the sacred realm was sealed, the Zora were entrusted with guarding the Spiritual Stone of Water.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Women of their royal family have been given the crucial role of sages in times of need. And when the sacred realm was sealed, the Zora were entrusted with guarding the Spiritual Stone of Water.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=48}}</ref>

Zora do not have individual homes, instead sleeping in large communal pools within Zora's Domain.<ref name="CaC 117">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 117}}</ref> This seems to only apply to Hyrule's Zora, as the Zora of [[Labrynna]] and [[Termina]] do have private dwellings.

The [[Lanayru Great Spring]] in the era of {{BotW|-}} contains a wealth of ore, and so the Zora have become crafters of beautiful stone buildings and metal jewelry and equipment.<ref name="CaC 117"/>

All Zora possess the ability to manipulate water.<ref>{{Cite|We each, to varying extents, possess the ability to actually manipulate water. We use this gift for many purposes.|Learnings of the Zora|TotK}}</ref>

After the [[Imprisoning War]] prior to {{ALttP}}, Hyrule fell into decline. By the era of ''A Link to the Past'' the Zora's once close relationship with the Hylians had soured. They grew hostile to outsiders, and their physical appearance changed to reflect this, turning them into [[River Zora|enemy Zora]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=By the era of A Link to the Past, the kingdom of Hyrule is in decline, and relations with the Zora have soured greatly. Their bodies changed from blue to green and they have become increasingly aggressive toward outsiders, going so far as to spit fireballs at anyone who dares tread near their waters.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=49}}</ref> In this same era, friendly blue Zora still exist beyond Hyrule in the neighboring kingdom of Labrynna. Several generations later in the time of {{ALBW}}, Queen [[Oren]] attempted to return peace between the Zora and Hylians, but they split, with half pledging loyalty to her and half rebelling in violent fashion.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=In A Link Between Worlds, Queen Oren attempts to rule over the Zora, but they split, with half pledging loyalty to her and half rebelling in violent fashion.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=49}}</ref> Despite the queen's attempts to return the Zora to their former glory, this hostile faction of Zora grow stronger over time, and by the era of {{TAoL}}, the Zora are outright monsters.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Despite the queen's attempts to return the Zora to their former glory, this hostile faction of Zora grow stronger over time, and by the era of The Adventure of Link, the Zora are outright monsters.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=49}}</ref>

==History==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the Zora all live in the [[Zora's Domain]], located in northeastern Hyrule at the end of [[Zora's River]], where their domain is guarded by the impassable [[Sleepless Waterfall]].<ref name="Allies">{{Cite|The Zoras come from Zora's Domain in northeast Hyrule. An aquatic race, they are longtime allies of Hyrule's Royal Family.|Kaepora Gaebora|OoT}}</ref> The Zora folk are responsible for guarding the {{Term|OoT3D|Water Temple|link}} in [[Lake Hylia]], which the Zora River flows into, as well as the [[Zora's Fountain]], the source of the River's water, on behalf of the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|Beyond this point lies Lake Hylia. It is full of deep, pure water. The Zoras, who live all the way upstream, guard the sunken temple at the lake's bottom, as well as Zora's Fountain.|Kaepora Gaebora|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Just ahead lies Zora's Domain. The Zoras serve Hyrule's Royal Family by protecting this water source. Their door will not open for anyone except those who have some connection with the Royal Family.|Kaepora Gaebora|OoT}}</ref> Due to their close servitude, the Hyrulean Zora have been allies to the Royal Family for years.<ref name="Allies"/> [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]], who resides in the Fountain, serves as their patron deity.<ref>{{Cite|Lord Jabu-Jabu is the patron deity of the Zoras. Princess Ruto is in charge of preparing his meals, morning and night.|Zora|OoT}}</ref> [[King Zora]] leads the Zora as their monarch ruler. The latter has a young, elegant daughter, [[Princess Ruto]], who is left in charge of preparing Jabu-Jabu's meals.
[[File:Rutoadult.png|thumb|317x317px|Ruto, a notable Zora and the princess of the race during the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Era of the Hero of Time]]]]
As a child, [[Link]] visits the Domain in search of the [[Zora's Sapphire]], the [[Spiritual Stone]] that was passed down by the Zora, as well as hearing that Ruto was in danger.<ref>{{Cite|You obtained Zora's Sapphire! This is the Spiritual Stone of Water passed down by the Zoras!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> Link saves Princess Ruto from [[Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly]], after she is swallowed by Jabu-Jabu due to being cursed by [[Ganondorf]]. As a reward, she entrusts the young hero with the Zora's Sapphire and announces her [[wikipedia:Engagement|engagement]] to him.<ref>{{Cite|You mean the Spiritual Stone of Water, Zora's Sapphire, don't you? My mother gave it to me and said I should give it only to the man who will be my husband. You might call it the Zora's Engagement Ring! All right! I'll give you my most precious possession: Zora's Sapphire! Don't tell my father...|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref>

Seven years later, after Ganondorf came into power as the Evil King, [[Morpha]] had caused the Zora's Domain and all of its residents to become frozen in ice.<ref>{{Cite|This ice is created by an evil curse... The monster in the Water Temple is the source of the curse. Unless you shut off the source, this ice will never melt....|Sheik|OoT}}</ref> [[Sheik]] had managed to rescue Princess Ruto,<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure you've already seen it! Zora's Domain--totally frozen! A young man named Sheik saved me from under the ice... But my father and the other Zoras have not...yet...|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref> who had then went to the Water Temple to save her people,<ref>{{Cite|We meet again, Link... If you came here to meet the Zoras, you wasted your time... This is all there is... With one exception, the Zoras are all now sealed under this thick ice sheet... I managed to rescue the Zora princess from under the ice, but... she left to head for the Water Temple...|Sheik|OoT}}</ref> but it is the legendary [[Hero of Time]] who ultimately destroys the evil inside the Temple and thus saves Zora's Domain. His efforts also awakens Princess Ruto as the [[Sage of Water]] (which forces her engagement with Link into an indefinite hold). Although Link had defeated Morpha, Zora's Domain never recovers in-game, as Ruto explains it will take time to thaw.<ref>{{Cite|Zora's Domain and its people will eventually return to their original state.|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref> Only during the ending are the Zora found to be thawed. Princess Ruto as one of the [[Sage|Seven Sages]], aids her fiancé in the final battle against [[Ganon]]. However, when [[Hyrule]]'s princess, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], permanently sends Link to the past, [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|splitting the timeline into two]] and into the Child Timeline, Ruto, who is in the Adult Timeline, despite finally completing her duty as a Sage, was sadly unable to marry the Hero of Time.

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM3D Zora Notebook Portrait.png|left|A Zora's picture in the Bombers' Notebook from {{MM3D|-}}]]
[[File:MM Indigo-Go's Artwork.png|thumb|343x343px|The Indigo-Go's Band (left to right: [[Toto]], [[Japas]], [[Tijo]], [[Lulu]], [[Mikau]], [[Evan]])]]
In {{MM}}, the Zora live in the [[Great Bay]] region of [[Termina]], where they congregate in the [[Zora Hall]] as their home. The Zora Hall is also the home to [[The Indigo-Go's]], a famous Zora band.<ref>{{Cite|You came to see the Zora band, The Indigo-Go's, didn't you?|Zora|MM}}</ref> The band was planned to play at the [[Carnival of Time]] in [[Clock Town]], but suddenly had to cancel when [[Lulu]], the band's lead singer, lost her voice when she laid seven [[Zora Egg|Egg]]s. However, the Eggs were shortly stolen by the [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirate]]s. [[Mikau]], the band's guitarist and a Zora hero, had tried to recover the Eggs, but was beaten by the Pirates and left gravely injured in the [[Great Bay Coast|coast]]. Link eventually finds Mikau and pushes him to shore, but he unfortunately passes away, and after being [[Song of Healing|healed]] by the young Hero of Time, his soul is encased into the [[Zora Mask]].
[[File:MM Zora Link Artwork.png|left|thumb|280x280px|Link as a Zora]]
Wearing the Zora Mask allows Link to transform into [[Zora Link]] in the resemblance of Mikau.<ref>{{Cite|Wear the mask with (C) to inhabit the body of a Zora. Press (C) again to return to normal.|N/A|MM}}</ref> Under the guise of this form, Link retrieves the scattered Zora Eggs, allowing Lulu to recover her voice and to subsequently open the way to the [[Great Bay Temple]].

The Great Bay Temple is implied to be an important location to the Zora folk. It is said that should anything happen to the Temple, a disaster will befall the Bay and the Zora descendant of the Temple's protector.<ref>{{Cite|There's a rumor that the ocean is getting weird 'cause there's trouble off shore at Great Bay Temple. And they say when that happens, something bad will befall the Zora descendant who's been protecting the temple.|Tijo|MM}}</ref> The Zora also have their own [[Zoran Script|writing system]].<ref>{{Cite|Can you read Zoran script?|Tatl|MM}}</ref>

==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:OoS Zora Sprite.png|44px|left]]
Although Zora do not live in [[Holodrom]] in {{OoS}}, one Zora appears during a [[Linked Game]]. The Zora can be found inside the [[Hero's Cave]], searching for a place to build a new Domain under King Zora's orders, but cannot find any place that exceeds Labrynna's seas.<ref>{{Cite|I am traveling the lands under King Zora's orders to find a new domain, but no place exceeds Labrynna's seas.|Zora|OoS}}</ref> They will request that Link reports back to King Zora their findings by telling him a [[Secret]], which will prompt King Zora to upgrade Link's [[Sword]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{OoA}} features both the Zora race and enemy Zora, and distinguishes between the two as sea and river Zora, respectively.<ref name="Sea Zora"/> The peaceful sea Zora live in a [[Zora Village|Village]] in the southern [[Zora Seas]] of [[Labrynna]]. One sea Zora in the Village refers to the river Zora as "savage and vulgar", making it clear that the two groups have no social connections to each other.<ref name="Sea Zora"/> As in {{OoT|-}}, the sea Zora's guardian is Lord Jabu-Jabu.<ref>{{Cite|My permission is needed to enter Jabu-Jabu, but I can't permit just anyone to see our guardian.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref>

In the past, the Zora Seas were polluted due to a curse inflicted by [[Veran]]. The pollution had made the Zora population fall ill and disrupted their way of life.<ref>{{Cite|The seas are filthy--koff! Koff! I can't swim in peace.|Zora|OoA}}</ref> The pollution had also made the Zora King fall sick, becoming deceased in the present. His death left the Zora with no ruler ever since.<ref>{{Cite|This was the palace of our ruler, King Zora, but long ago the king fell ill. Ever since, we've continued on with no king.|Zora|OoA}}</ref> This becomes an issue in Link's quest, as Link cannot enter Lord Jabu-Jabu and find his [[Essence of Time]] without the King's consent. Link purifies the ocean in the past by rescuing the [[Fairy Queen]] and cures the King's illness with a [[Magic Potion]], allowing the Zora King to become well and survive into the present.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the Zora live in Zora's Domain, upstream from Lake Hylia. Many can be found down along the Zora River as well as in Lake Hylia proper, caring for and patrolling the Lake.<ref>{{Cite|I am a member of the Zora tribe, the proud people who care for Lake Hylia.|Zora|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The other Zoras are all on guard patrol at the bottom of the lake.|Zora|TP}}</ref> These Zora guards wear fish-shaped helmets over their faces and carry halberds with them. The bottom of the Lake is sacred to the Zora folk,<ref>{{Cite|The bottom of this lake is a sacred place for us Zoras.|Zora|TP}}</ref> where the [[Lakebed Temple]] lies at its depths. The Zora serve as the protectors of this Temple.<ref>{{Cite|The Zoras are usually the protectors of Lakebed Temple, so I think they should help us...|Midna|TP}}</ref>

[[File:TP Zora 2.png|244x244px|left]]
When [[Wolf Link]] arrives to [[Telma]]'s [[Telma's Bar|Bar]] in [[Hyrule Castle Town]], he finds [[Ilia]] with a [[Ralis|Zora boy]] who is facing death. Link travels to Lake Hylia to investigate a possibly related incident,<ref>{{Cite|But this is strange... A child of the Zoras... I wonder if this is at all related to the incident the soldiers were talking about in back...|Telma|TP}}</ref> and upon arriving in Zora's Domain, finds that it is frozen with all the Zora encased in ice, similar to the events of ''Ocarina of Time''.<ref>{{Cite|Those... Those are the Zoras! Wow... This is really strange... What in the world happened? Hey... What do you want to do? We can't just leave them like this... I think all we need to do is thaw them out...|Midna|TP}}</ref> Using the [[Twilight Portal]] that opened above the Domain, Wolf Link and [[Midna]] warp a [[Molten Shard]] that fell in [[Death Mountain]] into the Domain, destroying the ice and thawing the Zora trapped inside. Shortly afterwards they are approached by the spirit of Zora [[Rutela]]. She explains that their Domain was raided by [[Zant]]'s minions and herself executed as an example to her people.<ref>{{Cite|The dark ones... They raided this village and, as a message to my people, executed me before them.|Rutela|TP}}</ref> The absence of a ruler throws the Zora into chaos.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=When Queen Rutela is killed during the invasion from the Twilight Realm, the absence of a ruler throws the Zora into chaos.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=49}}</ref> Link is told that if he can help her save her son, Prince Ralis, and bring him back to the Domain, she will aid him in his quest.<ref>{{Cite|Please... Would you save my dearest, Prince Ralis? If you do this thing, I will bestow upon you the protection of water.|Rutela|TP}}</ref>

After restoring light to the [[Lanayru Province]], Link as a [[Hylian]] helps Telma and Ilia take the Zora prince to [[Kakariko Village]], where [[Renado]] heals him. After Ralis is saved, Queen Rutela makes a final appearance as she guides Link to her late [[King Zora|husband]]'s grave. She commends the Hero of Twilight for bringing her son to Kakariko, as the village is sacred to her people and where they are laid to rest.<ref>{{Cite|You were right to bring him here. Kakariko Village is a sacred place for the Zora people. It is where we take our eternal rest. My husband in life, King Zora, also rests his spirit here.|Rutela|TP}}</ref> There she bestows him the [[Zora Armor]], allowing him to swim underwater and reach the Lakebed Temple.

After completing the Lakebed Temple, Zora can also be found in Death Mountain's hot springs.

==={{BotW|-}}===
[[File:BotW Mipha Artwork.png|thumb|259x259px|Mipha, a Champion and the princess of the Zoras in the [[Era of the Wilds]]]]
In {{BotW}}, the Zora live in Zora's Domain, located in the [[Lanayru]] region of Hyrule. One hundred years ago, the Zora were spared the damage caused by the [[Great Calamity]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book=CaC|publisher= |page=399}}</ref> While crossing [[Inogo Bridge]], Link meets [[Sidon]], the prince of the Zora. He claims that they are in need of a Hylian and leads Link to the Domain. Once there, it is revealed that the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} is responsible for the heavy rainfall, having been reactivated sometime before Link's arrival.

Interestingly, the Zora seen in {{BotW|-}} have a range of physical features, resembling a variety of marine life. Sidon himself resembles a hammerhead shark, and the elderly [[Muzu]] resembles a manta ray. And as well, [[King Dorephan]]'s appearance seems to be based on that of a blue whale. They can also be found in a variety of colors, unlike in previous games. Due to the appearance of both Zora and Rito, It is unknown if the Rito are descendants of the Zora in this game, as they are in {{TWW|-}}. Similar to ''Ocarina of Time'', the Zora has a princess in this era, named [[Mipha]], one of the [[Champions]], and, like her ancestor Ruto, has a crush on Link the Hero of the Wild.

Ten thousand years ago the Zora with help from Hyrule built the Rutala Dam to create the East Reservoir Lake. Thanks to this construction, Hyrule was no longer plagued by devastating floods<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 364}}</ref>, though due to the downpour Vah Ruta was generating, the reservoir filled to the point where the dam was on the verge of bursting, which would cause a flood.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Like in {{BotW|-}}, the Zora in {{TotK|-}} live in Zora's Domain. Once Link arrives, [[Chroma|three]] [[Khira|of]] [[Yona|the]] Zora explain to him that their homeland has been covered in an unknown sludge. The statue also has been covered in the sludge, which is very important to the people. One of the Zora NPCs, [[Torfeau]], suggests to Link that he should see King Dorephan, but because he is busy investigating the sludge, he should speak to Yona, who is at the infirmary, which has a pool of water. Yona has difficulty getting some sludge off of [[Tona]]. 

[[Tula]] claims to "protect" the throne room, where [[Prince Sidon|Sidon]] is. Because of the sludge around the Domain, the Zora are unable to swim in the river.

==={{EoW|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]'', both Sea Zoras and [[River Zora|River Zoras]] make an appearance in the game, with one point of the game requiring [[Princess Zelda]] and Tri to act as mediators for a negotiation between the two parties. This marks the first time both varieties of Zora appear together since {{OoA|-}}.

==Trivia==
*The Zora hatchlings are likely a visual pun based on the Japanese word {{Romanize|おたまじゃくし|Otamajakushi}}, which means "tadpole", but can also mean "musical note".
*While Zora do not appear in {{PH}} and {{ST}}, several [[Treasure]]s make reference to them, including the [[Treasure|Ruto Crown]] and the [[Zora Scale]], the latter of which only appears in ''Phantom Hourglass''. [[Geozard]]s also appear similar to the monstrous or [[River Zora|River Zora]] of the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Downfall Timeline]].
*[[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]], the [[Golden Goddess]] who establishes the physical and meta-physical laws of [[Hyrulean]] existence, as well as being the designer of space-time itself, is important among the Zora and their descendants, the [[Rito]].
*Female Zora are rare throughout the franchise, as the vast majority of the race are male. [[Princess Ruto]] is the first individual seen of such gender, followed by [[Lulu]], [[Laruto]], [[Rutela|Queen Rutela]], and [[Mipha]].
*In an interview with Nintendo of Europe, [[Eiji Aonuma]] said that he would be a Zora if he could choose to be a [[Goron]], a [[Deku Scrub]], or a Zora.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= '''NoE: If you could choose to be one, would you be a Goron, Zora or Deku Scrub?'''<br/><br/>'''EA:''' Zora!|author= |published= February 23, 2017|retrieved= |url= https://www.nintendo.co.uk/News/2017/February/Find-out-some-of-Eiji-Aonuma-s-favourite-things-from-The-Legend-of-Zelda-series-in-our-interview--1198478.html|title= Find out some of Eiji Aonuma’s favourite things from The Legend of Zelda series in our interview!|site= Nintendo UK|type= }}</ref>
*''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'' is the only 3D game in the series to not have any Zoras present as NPCs. Instead, the [[Parella]] serve as the "Aquatic" race in this title.
*According to ''[[The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia]]'' formerly hosted on [[Community:Zelda.com|Zelda.com]], the {{Plural|Series|Zora}} were highly evolved from fish that swam in {{Term|Series|Zora's Fountain|link}}, which was blessed by the {{Plural|Series|Golden Goddess|link}} during the {{Term|Series|Creation of the World|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= When the Goddesses created Hyrule, they blessed Zora's Fountain and eventually the fish which swam in its water evolved into the Zoras we know today.|author= |published= |retrieved= April 15, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20050313070812/http://www.zelda.com/universe/pedia/z.jsp|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia|site= Zelda.com|type= }}</ref>[[File:LinkRuto.jpg|thumb|257x257px|Young Princess Ruto attracted to Link as seen in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
*Princess Ruto's engagement with the [[Hero of Time]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' marks the first time in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] a Zora (or any race in general) falls in love with a member of a species other than theirs, which in this case, a [[Hylian]]. This would repeat with Mipha in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]''. In both of those cases, the individual chosen is [[Link]].
**The [[Nintendo 64]] title is also the first case in the core games the main protagonist explicitly has a love interest.
**Although this never occurred in any official media based on ''Ocarina of Time'', if Link the Hero of Time were to actually marry Ruto, due to her title, it is likely he himself would become the Zora race's prince.
*In the Japanese version of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', Zora's trophy states that their other appearance is ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]''. For unknown reasons, it was changed to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' in Western releases of the game.
