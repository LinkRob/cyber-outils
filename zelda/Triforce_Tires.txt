The {{Term/Store|Triforce Tires|Triforce Tires|Series, MK8, MK8D}} are an {{Term|MK8D|Item|link}} in {{MK8}}.

==Location and Uses==
The Triforce Tires are the [[mariowiki:Tire|Tires]] that accompany the {{Term|MK8D|Master Cycle|link}} and the {{Term|MK8D|Hylian Kite|link}} in {{MK8}}. They are Tires which bear the {{Term|Series|Triforce|link}} symbol on the hubcaps. In {{MK8|-}}, they bear the same stats as the [[mariowiki:Slim (tire)|Slim]] and [[mariowiki:Crimson Slim|Crimson Slim]] tires. However in {{MK8|D}}, they were changed to be identical to the [[mariowiki:Off-Road (tire)|Off-Road]] and [[mariowiki:Retro Off-Road|Retro Off-Road]] Tires instead.

The Triforce Tires are available as part of the [[The Legend of Zelda × Mario Kart 8|''The Legend of Zelda'' × {{MK8|-}}]] [[DLC]] pack for {{MK8|-}}. In {{MK8|D|-}}, they can be obtained randomly by collecting enough [[mariowiki:Coin|Coins]].
