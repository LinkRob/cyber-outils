{{Term/Store|Ash Swamp||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Ash Swamp'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Ash Swamp is located in [[West Necluda]]. Like the adjacent [[Blatchery Plain]], Ash Swamp is composed of generally flat land with tall [[Grass]] and ruined buildings.

During the second [[Great Calamity]], [[Guardian#Breath of the Wild|Guardian]]s made their way through Ash Swamp on their way to [[Fort Hateno]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 385}}</ref> After Link fell in battle to a Guardian in Ash Swamp, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]'s powers awoke and she destroyed most of the surrounding Guardians.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 384}}</ref> Consequently, the remains of several Guardians litter the Plain and can be searched for [[Ancient Material]]s. The events of the Great Calamity can be retrieved in the final [[Recovered Memory]], "[[Zelda's Awakening]]", near the southern part of the Swamp.

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing an [[Emblazoned Shield]] can be found beneath a pile of bombable [[Rock]]s in the northern section of the Swamp. A second stone Treasure Chest containing a bundle of five [[Fire Arrow]]s can be found at the center of the Swamp. A third stone Treasure Chest containing a [[Double Axe]] can be found at the southeastern section of the Swamp.
