{{Name|Japanese|Edelweiss}} is a character in {{RTBToL}}. 

==Biography==
{{Term|RTBToL|Edelweiss}} is the flower shop owner's dog. She appears on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 6|link}} and {{Term|RTBToL|Page 7|link}} The flower shop owner asks {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} to take {{Term|RTBToL|Edelweiss}} for a walk, and will pay him for the favor. During this section, she will get in a fight with {{Term|RTBToL|Masaru|link}}, causing the monkey to drop the dentures it stole from a local fisherman.

If {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} talks to {{Term|RTBToL|Iona|link}}'s Mother and enters the turnip minigame while walking {{Term|RTBToL|Edelweiss}}, she becomes playable in the minigame.

==Nomenclature==
{{Wp|Edelweiss (disambiguation)|Edelweiss}} is the German name of the "Leontopodium alpinum," a mountain flower that {{Term|RTBToL|Edelweiss}}'s tail resembles.
