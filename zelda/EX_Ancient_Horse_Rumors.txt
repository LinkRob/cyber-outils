"{{Term/Store|EX Ancient Horse Rumors||Series, BotW}}" is a [[Side Quest]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Ancient Horse Rumors}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Highland Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is obtained upon reaching exiting the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with finding a copy of the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill EX|link}}, which is located at {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently {{Color|BotW Red|Highland Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare horse gear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to activate the second stage of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} by reading the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V3}} without triggering any of the loading conditions for its activation.}}

At the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V3|link}} at the table inside. Within, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} discusses ancient {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient horse gear is real, and it's FABULOUS!}}|Super Rumor Mill V3|BotW}}</ref> Originating from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|display=Hyrule's}} distant past, this {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear}} includes a {{Term|BotW|Bridle|link}} that increases a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link|display=Horse's}} stats and a {{Term|BotW|Saddle|link}} that can summon a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} to its owner's side.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently, there's some incredible horse gear from Hyrule's distant past. I'm talking the long, long ago, folks! A bridle that increases your horse's skill and a saddle that can instantly summon your horse to your side.|Super Rumor Mill V3|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Bridle}} is rumored to be south of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}, under a cherry blossom tree on a mountain where creatures gather.<ref>{{Cite|The bridle is south of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Ridge}}, {{Color|BotW Red|beneath a cherry blossom tree at a mountain where creatures gather}}.|Super Rumor Mill V3|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Saddle}} is rumored to be near the spring of a Horse spirit.<ref>{{Cite|The saddle is {{Color|BotW Red|at a mysterious spring where a horse spirit resides}}.|Super Rumor Mill V3|BotW}}</ref>

Following these tales, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Bridle|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} buried under the cherry blossom tree on {{Term|BotW|Satori Mountain|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Saddle|link}} is in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} buried behind {{Term|BotW|Malanya Spring|link}}. Each {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be dislodged from the ground using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to acquire the {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear}} within. The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed once both pieces of ancient {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear}} have been obtained.<ref>{{Cite|You found the {{Color|BotW Red|ancient horse gear}} at Satori Mountain and Malanya Spring!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
