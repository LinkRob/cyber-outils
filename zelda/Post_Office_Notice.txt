The {{Term/Store|Post Office Notice||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an {{Term|TPHD|Letter|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Post Office Notice|Letters|TPHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[Postman|The Postman]] will deliver this letter to [[Link]] in [[Faron Province|Faron]] [[Hyrule Field]] just before he enters the [[Twilight]] to [[Kakariko Gorge]].

The letter is designed to teach players how to use the in-game letter system.
