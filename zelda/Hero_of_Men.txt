The {{Term/Store|Hero of Men||Series, TMC}} is the name associated with a great hero spoken of in the legends of the {{Term|Series|Picori Blade|link}} and the mythical {{Term|Series|Light Force|link}} in the land of {{Term|Series|Hyrule|link}} during the {{Term|Series|Force Era|link}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 83}}</ref>. He was the first owner of those two special gifts given to him by the {{Term|Series|Minish|link}} to assist him in his efforts to battle the many evil creatures plaguing the land of {{Term|Series|Hyrule}} long in its past.

==History==
:''See also: {{Term|TMC|Minish Legend|link}}''
Long ago in the land of {{Term|Series|Hyrule|link}}, during its early development after the {{Term|Series|Era of Prosperity|link}}, the day came when many powerful beasts suddenly appeared and began to lay siege to the kingdom. Just when {{Term|Series|Hyrule}} was on the verge of destruction,<ref>{{Cite|A long, long time ago...when the world was on the verge of being swallowed by shadow...|N/A|TMC}}</ref> the {{Term|Series|Minish|link}} came from the sky and gave the {{Term|Series|Hero of Men}} the {{Term|Series|Picori Blade|link}} and the {{Term|Series|Light Force|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The tiny Picori appeared from the sky, bringing the hero of men a sword and a golden light.|N/A|TMC}}</ref> Using these tools, the {{Term|Series|Hero of Men}} successfully defeated the monsters and drove the darkness away from {{Term|Series|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|With wisdom and courage, the hero drove out the darkness.|N/A|TMC}}</ref> The {{Term|Series|Hero of Men|display=Hero}} then locked all the realm's evil away into the {{Term|Series|Bound Chest|link}}, sealing it with the {{Term|Series|Picori Blade}} itself and bringing peace to the kingdom.
