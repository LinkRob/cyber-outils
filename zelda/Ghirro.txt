'''Ghirro''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Ghirro|This airborne pest hinders foes with its powerful breath. The gale is strong enough to shove a person around.<ref name="Ghirro">{{Cite|This airborne pest hinders foes with its powerful breath. The gale is strong enough to shove a person around.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Ghirros are flying [[mushroom]] creatures known for blowing gusts of [[wind]] at its enemies. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Ghirro [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of two.
