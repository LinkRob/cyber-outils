{{Term/Store|Fireblight Ganon||Series, BotW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Boss|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Intro"/>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
{{Compendium/Extract}}
In {{BotW}}, {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon}} is one of the four {{Plural|BotW|Blight Ganon|link}}, phantoms of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} that took control of the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This phantom of Ganon attacked the Divine Beast Vah Rudania and was responsible for the demise of the Champion Daruk.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> It is found either inside the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} or in the center of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} if it is not already defeated. {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon}} is responsible for the demise of {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That ugly pain in the crag is Ganon's handiwork. Thing got the best of me a hundred years ago.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> It is particularly weak to {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} attacks.

==== First Phase ====
When Link tries to activate the Main Control Unit, a pool of [[Malice]] will spiral and Fireblight Ganon will appear. Then when the battle begins, Daruk says, "Watch out for the sword." If the player plays dirty like [[Windblight Ganon]] and keeps their distance, Fireblight Ganon will throw multiple tiny fireballs. When close in contact, he'll try to hit Link with its sword. If Link dodges it perfectly, he can do a Flurry Rush. If he gets enough hits on him, Fireblight Ganon will be stunned for a bit. Once Link removes half of his health, Fireblight Ganon will teleport to the Main Control Unit with a flaming sword and a flaming shield, beginning the second phase.


=== Second Phase ===
During the Second phase of the fight, Fireblight Ganon will infuse its sword with fire, giving it an orange-like color and he will form a flaming ball around him similar to Daruk's Protection. Daruk warns Link by saying, "That flaming ball is dangerous." This flaming ball can shield off every attack except for a [[Remote Bomb]]. However, this can only happen if Fireblight is creating a spiral wind pattern toward him. If successful, Fireblight Ganon will be stunned for short period of time allowing Link a good amount of hits in. In addition, Daruk will say, "Now's your chance." Once Fireblight gets up, he acts exactly like he did in the first phase.

When {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon}} is defeated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will receive a {{Term|BotW|Heart Container|link}} as a reward. {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} will appear and thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} for freeing his spirit.<ref>{{Cite|I owe you big for this. Because of you, my spirit is finally free. Can't thank you enough!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> He will apologize to {{Term|BotW|Link}} for failing to defeat {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon}} on his own.<ref>{{Cite|I feel like I should apologize.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I was doing all I could to protect Hyrule when that thing got the best of me.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sorry that me resting with the rubble caused such a mess.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} [[Daruk's Protection]].<ref>{{Cite|I want to give you something. It's a special power of mine called Daruk's Protection.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref>
