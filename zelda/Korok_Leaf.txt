<!--A single swing of this giant, sturdy leaf can create a gust of wind strong enough to blow away light objects. These will sometimes fall off trees as they're chopped down.-->
{{Plural|BotW|Korok Leaf}} can occasionally be found lying on the ground near {{Plural|BotW|Raft|link}}, though they can also be found from felling {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}. Though they are naturally occurring, they belong to the {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear|link}} archetype of equipment.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> When swung, {{Plural|BotW|Korok Leaf}} produce a strong gust of {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}} that can blow back light {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|linl}},<ref name="Inventory"/> such as {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}. This gust is also useful against {{Term|BotW|Wind}}-sensitive objects such as windmills and {{Plural|BotW|Raft}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to sail independently from chance {{Plural|BotW|Wind|link}}. The gust from {{Plural|BotW|Korok Leaf}} can also be used to fan flames, quickly spreading {{Plural|BotW|Fire|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Korok Leaf}} are flammable and can be used to transfer flames. However, they will disintegrate if left ablaze for too long. Swinging a {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf}} without hitting a solid object will not damage its durability.

As {{Plural|BotW|Korok Leaf}} are not designed to be used as {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|lik}}, they are not optimal for direct melee combat. Nonetheless, they are still capable of dealing minor damage. {{Plural|BotW|Korok Leaf}} cannot be thrown, as {{Term|BotW|Link}} will merely blow a gust of {{Term|BotW|Wind}} if the [[R Button]] is held and released. Charging an attack with a {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf}} will cause {{Term|BotW|Link}} to forcefully thrust it forward from overhead, generating a longer-lasting gust of {{Term|BotW|Wind}}. Gusts from a {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf}} can upset {{Plural|BotW|Cucco|link}}, prompting them to attack if sufficiently agitated.

During the "{{Term|BotW|Recital at Warbler's Nest|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kheel|link}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf}} as a reward if he does not already have one.<ref>{{Cite|Oh!
