The {{Term/Store|Book of Sorcery||Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Lana]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Lightning}}.

==Variations==
The Lv.1 Book of Sorcery is the {{Term/Store|Spirit's Tome|Spirit's Tomes|HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}}, which is a simple, maroon grimoire with gold designs on the cover.

The Lv.2 Book of Sorcery is the {{Term/Store|Sealing Tome|Sealing Tomes|HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}}. It is a black book with more intricate, silver designs on the cover with small studded jewels and a silvery tassel bookmark. It is obtained from opening the [[Treasure Chest]] that appears after capturing the Lower Level East [[Keep]] in "[[The Shadow King]]" while playing as Lana.

The Lv.3 Book of Sorcery is the {{Term/Store|Sorceress Tome|Sorceress Tomes|HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}}, which is a blue book featuring fanciful gold designs with large gemstones and golden feathers decorated upon it. In {{HW|-}}, it is obtained from earning an A-Rank on the [[Adventure Mode]] battle A-11, after using the [[Item Card#Digging Mitts|Digging Mitts]] to find it.

The Lv.★ Book of Sorcery is the '''8-Bit Book of Magic''', which is only available in {{HW|-}} and {{HW|DE}}. Its appearance is based on the [[Book of Magic]] from {{TLoZ}}. It is obtained by getting an A-Rank on the [[Master Quest Map]] battle H-12, after using a [[Item Card#Water Bomb|Water Bomb]] to find it. In {{HW|DE|-}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Book of Magic replaces the Tome of the Night when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.

The Lv.4 Book of Sorcery is the '''Sorceress Tome +''', which is only available in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE|-}}. It appears identical to the Sorceress Tome but is a much stronger Weapon.

The Lv.4+ Book of Sorcery is the '''Tome of the Night''', which only appears in {{HW|L|-}} and {{HW|DE|-}}. As a Multi-Element Weapon, the Tome of the Night is also associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Darkness}}. The Tome has features the same fanciful design as the Sorceress Tome but with a purple color scheme instead of gold.

===Weak Point Smash===
{{Main|Critical Point#Hyrule Warriors}}
Against Captains and Warriors, Lana summons a magic wall behind the enemy and then slams them into it, causing the wall to break and unleash Lightning energy around them. Against most Giant Bosses, Lana summons a magic wall to uppercut the monster. While midair, Lana faces the enemy as she charges magic to her feet and comes down with a double thrust kick. Against [[Argorok]], Lana jumps over the dragon and lands on its back while slamming her book against it.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Lana creates six magic walls, then wall-jumps between them, damaging enemies along the way. When she reaches the end, she turns to face the walls and destroys them, damaging the enemies caught inside and nearby. This attack damages enemies in a rectangular area a short distance ahead of Lana.

===Focus Spirit===
{{Main|Focus Spirit}}
Upon activation, Lana channels her power in her book and unleashes Lightning energy. The Focus Spirit Attack has Lana summons three magic plates she casts at her enemies in front of her. When the Focus Spirit gauge is depleted, Lana charges the rest of the energy in a magic ball in her hand and then slams it at the ground. She gives a short victory pose to the screen as she lands before turning back to the battle.
