The {{Term/Store|Central Square||Series, BotW}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|Central Square|Map|BotW}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Castle Town Central Square||TP}},{{Exp Game|TP}}<ref>{{Cite|Castle Town Central Square|N/A|TP}}</ref> is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{TP|-}}===
The Castle Town Central Square is the busiest section of [[Hyrule Castle Town]] in {{TP}}. A fountain bearing the [[Hylian Crest]] rests in the middle, while a cafe bar can be found in the {{exp|east in the Wii version|west}} portion of the Square. Near the cafe is an entrance to the viewing platform, where Link can view the entire Central Square as well as buy a variety of wares from [[Goron]]s.<ref>{{Cite|The entrance to the viewing platform is on the side of this cafe. I heard that Gorons have been selling their wares there lately. If you want to get to the bar, use the southern thoroughfare. Swordsmen like yourself tend to gather there.|Hylian|TP}}</ref> Some musicians are situated near the entrance to Hyrule Castle, and farther back is a young [[Hylian]], [[Soal]], who will polish Link's shoes for just 10 [[Rupee]]s, thus allowing the young hero to enter [[Chudley's Fine Goods and Fancy Trinkets Emporium]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you! Your shoes are grimy! I'll polish 'em for you for 10 Rupees.|Soal|TP}}</ref>

The cafe bar is one of Castle Town's oldest shops, so most of its customers are regulars.<ref name="Gossip">{{Cite|This is one of the older shops in Castle Town, so most of our customers are regulars... Though most of them are gossips...|Hylian|TP}}</ref> The cafe bar does not play an important role in Link's journey, but he can visit the cafe bar to hear the latest gossip from its customer.<ref name="Gossip"/> The customers at the cafe will also advise Link on which places to visit, such as the viewing platform to the side of the cafe or [[Fanadi's Palace]].<ref>{{Cite|Through the door on the side of this cafe is the viewing platform. Have you seen it already?|Hylian|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You know the fortune-teller's place on the southern thoroughfare? She's amazingly accurate!|Hylian|TP}}</ref>

==={{BotW|-}}===
The Central Square is located at the center of the [[Hyrule Castle Town Ruins]] in [[Hyrule Field]] in {{BotW}}. It was once a place for rest, but became the starting grounds of the [[Guardian]]s' rampage across [[Hyrule]] at the advent of the second [[Great Calamity]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 394}}</ref> The ruins of a large stone fountain featuring three large birds sits in the middle, marking the center of the Square and the surrounding Town Ruins.

A [[Korok Seed]] is given to [[Link]] for finishing a race that begins along the southern wall of the Hyrule Castle Town Ruins and ends just above the decimated fountain.
