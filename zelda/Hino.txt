{{Term/Store|Hino||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|Hino|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Hino}} is an inhabitant of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} who resides at {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}}. He is a researcher focused on the phenomenon of the {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've been doing rigorous research day and night to figure out the mystery of that blood moon.|Hino|BotW}}</ref> Each night, he stays up until 3:00 AM in order to observe the moon, at which point he decides to sleep.<ref>{{Cite|What is it? I was just about to get some sleep so I can be rested for researching the moon tonight...|Hino|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What is it? I was just sleeping so I'd be well rested for researching the moon tonight...|Hino|BotW}}</ref>

Should {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} reveal that he knows nothing about the {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon}}, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} will tell him that in some nights, the moon rises blood-red and, at midnight, revives all monsters that have been defeated thus far.<ref>{{Cite|[Blood Moon?] Whenever the clock strikes midnight on unlucky nights, the sky turns blood red. That's the {{Color|BotW Red|blood moon}}. When that happens, {{Color|BotW Blue|monsters}} come back to life, no matter how many times you've defeated them.|Hino|BotW}}</ref> Due to it being a common occurrence, there is not much research into why it happens, but this does not dissuade {{Term|BotW|Hino}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's happened for so long now that no one really pays it any mind, but I have no plans to give up on my research.|Hino|BotW}}</ref> However, he admits that he has no idea why this is happening.<ref>{{Cite|Why do the {{Color|BotW Blue|monsters}} return to life? Why does it only happen when the sky turns red? No one really has the answers to those questions. It's a mysterious phenomenon...|Hino|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to tells him if he finds any information on what exactly causes the {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon}} to rise.<ref>{{Cite|If you learn anything about the {{Color|BotW Red|blood moon}} as you travel, be sure to come back and tell me.|Hino|BotW}}</ref>

While he is watching for the moon, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} will make comments on where it is in the sky if {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches him. During the day, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is interested in his research.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you again...<br/>Are you interested in my research?|Hino|BotW}}</ref> Before the moon rises, he will be awaiting its arrival.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you again.<br/>The moon is about to come out...|Hino|BotW}}</ref> While the moon is up, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} remarks that it is beautiful.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you again.<br/>A beautiful moon tonight, isn't it?|Hino|BotW}}</ref> After the moon sets for the night, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} will be disappointed that it is gone.<ref>{{Cite|Oh...<br/>It looks like the moon has already disappeared today...|Hino|BotW}}</ref>

In addition to information on the {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon}}, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} is able to tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} what phase the moon will be in that coming night. On the night of a new moon, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} says that even though it can't be seen, he is happy to know the moon is there.<ref>{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] Tonight is a new moon...<br/>Even though we can't see it, isn't it great just knowing it's there?|Hino|BotW}}</ref> He comments that the shape of the crescent moon makes him hungry.<ref>{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] Tonight is a crescent moon...<br/>That shape makes me hungry for some reason...|Hino|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Hino}} compares the seven-day moon to a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} aiming towards the land, which he thinks is beautiful.<ref>{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] Tonight is a seven-day moon...<br/>Like a bow aiming for the land below, it really is quite beautiful.|Hino|BotW}}</ref> On the 13th-day moon, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} anticipates it expanding in the coming nights.<ref>{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] Tonight is the 13th-day moon...<br/>Now it will slowly expand. So beautiful...|Hino|BotW}}</ref> When the full moon rises, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} says it looks as though it could fall at any minute, and he has a hard time taking his eyes off of it.<ref name="Full Moon">{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] Tonight is a full moon... Looks like it could come falling down any minute. I can't take my eyes off of it.|Hino|BotW}}</ref> The day after the full moon, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} remarks that though it is smaller, it is nice in its own way.<ref>{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] Tonight's moon will be a little smaller than yesterday's full moon. It's nice in its own way, really...|Hino|BotW}}</ref> Similar to the seven-day moon, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} compares the third-quarter moon to a {{Term|BotW|Bow}} aiming towards the heavens.<ref>{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] Tonight is the third-quarter moon. Like a bow aiming for the heavens above.|Hino|BotW}}</ref> The 26th-day moon has a sloping line that {{Term|BotW|Hino}} considers to be wonderful.<ref>{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] Tonight is the 26th-day moon... Its sloping line is really wonderful.|Hino|BotW}}</ref> Finally, leading up to the {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon}}, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} will reveal that he has a feeling something is going to happen.<ref>{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] I'm getting that feeling again... Something is going to happen tonight. I can taste it!|Hino|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] Tonight is the {{Color|BotW Red|blood moon}}, at last...|Hino|BotW}}</ref>

When it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}}, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} will flee inside {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rain...|Hino|BotW}}</ref> He complains that he is unable to see the moon during this weather,<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Can't see the moon like this.|Hino|BotW}}</ref> a point which he reiterates if {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what phase it will be in that night.<ref>{{Cite|[Tonight's moon?] Can't see anything in this rain!|Hino|BotW}}</ref>

During a {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon}}, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} will grow agitated and begin to run around {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}} screaming.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Here...it...COMES!}}|Hino|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|BLOOD!}}|Hino|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} talks to him during this time, he may witness him growling,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|{{Big|Gr...gr...gr...}}}}|Hino|BotW}}</ref> basking in the color of the moon,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|{{Big|It's...so...RED! So gloriously RED!}}}}|Hino|BotW}}</ref> claiming that his blood is boiling,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|{{Big|My blood...it's boiling!}}}}|Hino|BotW}}</ref> or reveling in the revival of the monsters.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|{{Big|Arise, monsters!}}}}|Hino|BotW}}</ref> He calms down at 12:30 AM, and when {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him during this time, he asks if he was also observing the {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... You were researching the {{Color|BotW Red|blood moon}}, as well?|Hino|BotW}}</ref> Now that the {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon}} has passed, {{Term|BotW|Hino}} states that it is time to wait for the next one.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|blood moon}} has come and gone. Time to start waiting for the next one...|Hino|BotW}}</ref>

In {{TotK}}, Hino can be found as a captive in various monster camps as a result of his attempts at observing them. Upon being freed, he will reward Link with some food and elixirs. As in {{BotW|-}}, Link can speak to Hino to find out what the upcoming moon phase will be, as well as whether or not a Blood Moon will occur during the following night.
