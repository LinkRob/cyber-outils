'''{{PAGENAME}} is a [[Main Quest]] in {{TotK}}.

== Overview ==
[[Purah]] will task you with seeing what’s up around the allied cities in [[Hyrule]]: [[Rito Village]], [[Zora’s Domain]], [[Goron City]], and [[Gerudo Town]]. For those who played {{BotW}}, you’ll recognize these as the homes to the unique races around Hyrule, as well as the companions and [[Divine Beast]]s from the last games.

You’ll have to go to each individual one to gain new abilities, but which area should you go to first? Though there is no correct order, but there are some areas that are easier to beat than others, making them more early-game friendly.

There’s no right or wrong way to go and if you have trouble at one, you can always pause and go back later. And, once you’re all done, then you’ll be off to finish the “[[Crisis at Hyrule Castle]]” quest.
