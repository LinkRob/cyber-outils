==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Grandpa}} is the deceased grandfather of the {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} child {{Term|BotW|Molli|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can't hear the story from Grandpa anymore...<br/>*sniff*|Molli|BotW}}</ref> By the time of {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s arrival in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, he has already passed away.

One day, {{Term|BotW|Grandpa}} was {{Term|BotW|Climbing|link}} up {{Term|BotW|Talonto Peak|link}}, but he decided to stop at the {{Term|BotW|Cedar Tree|link}} at its top and looked to the northwest.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* Once upon a time... My grandpa stopped at a {{Color|BotW Red|big tree}} while he was climbing a big mountain and looked below him to the northwest.|Molli|BotW}}</ref> When he did, he saw the {{Term|BotW|Biron Snowshelf|link}}, so he began to fly towards it.<ref>{{Cite|When suddenly he saw {{Color|BotW Red|a HUGE snow-white birdie}} with its wings spread wide! My grandpa took of {{Color|BotW Red|flying after the snow-white birdie}}.|Molli|BotW}}</ref> As he got closer, he noticed that there was something within the {{Term|BotW|Biron Snowshelf|display=Snowshelf}}.<ref name="Birdie"/> Once he arrived, he realized that it was the {{Term|BotW|Mozo Shenno Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It was... A {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}!|Molli|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Grandpa}} would tell this story to {{Term|BotW|Molli}}, but she would always become tired before hearing about the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Well? What was it?!] {{Small|...I always get sleepy around that part of the story...so I don't remember...}}|Molli|BotW}}</ref> He referred to the {{Term|BotW|Biron Snowshelf}} as a {{Term|BotW|Bird|link}} in his story, which {{Term|BotW|Molli}} attributes to his nature as a prankster.<ref>{{Cite|[Well...] A...{{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}? The snow-white bird wasn't actually a bird? So the white birdie was actually Hebra Mountain! Oh, Grandpa! You always were such a prankster!|Molli|BotW}}</ref>
