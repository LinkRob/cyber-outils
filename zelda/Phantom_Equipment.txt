The {{Term/Store|Phantom Equipment||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor Set|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|"Armor worn by fierce phantoms, feared even by heroes, is hidden in the ruins of Hyrule Field. Find these pieces at a battleground where brave souls tested their skill...at ceremonial grounds where royal guards are honored...and at the ruins where the soldiers of Hyrule gathered."<br/><br/>You found the treasure that was hidden at Coliseum Ruins, Sacred Grounds Ruins, and Hyrule Garrison Ruins. You obtained the {{Color|BotW Red|Phantom Equipment}}!|EX Treasure: Phantasma|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Phantom Equipment}} was referred to as the '''Phantom Set''' in {{CaC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 188}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It's exclusive to [[The Master Trials]] DLC Pack.

==Location and Uses==
The Phantom Equipment is [[Armor]] composed of the [[Phantom Helmet]], [[Phantom Armor]], and [[Phantom Greaves]]. They're available through the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "[[EX Treasure: Phantasma]]", which can be obtained by reading [[Misko's EX Journal]] at the [[Outpost Ruins]].<ref>{{Cite|Armor worn by fierce phantoms, feared even by heroes, is hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in the ruins of Hyrule Field}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at a battleground where brave souls tested their skill}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at ceremonial grounds where royal guards are honored}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where the soldiers of Hyrule gathered}}.|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> They can be found at the following locations:
* The [[Coliseum Ruins]]
* The [[Sacred Ground Ruins]]
* The [[Hyrule Garrison Ruins]]

Wearing the Phantom Equipment does not provide a [[Set Bonus]], though wearing the full set will "scare" villagers link encounters. Each individual piece will increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} attack power. After each piece is obtained, [[Granté]] will begin to sell them in [[Tarrey Town]]. They can't be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] nor can they be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}. Their design is based off the {{Plural|Series|Phantom|link}} from {{PH}} and {{ST}}.
