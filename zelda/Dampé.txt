{{Term/Store|Dampé|Dampés|Series, LANS, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dampé|N/A|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239,{{Exp Game|OoT}} 245,{{Exp Game|MM}} & 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Dampé}} was referred to as '''Dampe''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 105}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} He is known for being a large friendly man with a frightening face. He makes his first appearance in {{OoT}}.

==Biography==
==={{LA|NS|-}}===
Although he does not exist in the original {{LA}}, {{Term|LANS|Dampé}} lives in his {{Term|LANS|Dampé's Shack|link|display=Shack}} in {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Heights|link}} in [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|{{LA|-}} for the Nintendo Switch]]. In his {{Term|LANS|Dampé's Shack|display=Shack}}, he crafts {{Plural|LANS|Chamber|link}} based on the dungeons around {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}} which can be arranged into {{Plural|LANS|Chamber Dungeon|link}}.<ref name="Ruins">{{Cite|Hey, there you are. I wanna show you somethin' interesting. You've gone pokin' into some of the ruins on this island. I can tell. I make little {{Color|LANS Pink|chambers}} based on those dungeons. Put some chambers into the slab back there and it turns into a real dungeon. With a few chambers, you can make basic dungeons. With a bunch, you can make complicated ones. Though you're not done once you finish arranging a dungeon. You've gotta adventure through it too.|Dampé|LANS}}</ref>

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Dampé's Ghost}}<br/>That's the ghost of the dead gravekeeper! If you chase him, he might give you his keepsake...
}}

In {{OoT}}, Dampé lives in his {{Term|OoT3D|Dampé's Shack|link|display=Shack}} in the [[Kakariko Village Graveyard]]. He presides over the graveyard,<ref>{{Cite|Hey kid! Don't mess around with the graves! I'm Dampé the Gravekeeper!|Dampé|OoT}}</ref> sleeping during the day and pacing it at night.<ref>{{Cite|Have you seen Mr. Dampé, who lives in the graveyard up ahead? It's [TIME] right now, so he's probably asleep in his hut.|Saburo|OoT}}</ref> Dampé's [[Heart-Pounding Gravedigging Tour]] is his famed nightly romp: he unearths any plot of dirt along his route through the graveyard when asked, either revealing a [[Piece of Heart]], [[Rupee]]s, or nothing at all.<ref>{{Cite|Let me give you an advertisement for my side business... Dampé the Gravekeeper's Heart-Pounding Gravedigging Tour! What's gonna come out?! What's gonna come out?! When I start digging, we'll find out!|Dampé|OoT}}</ref> A [[Graveyard Boy|young boy]] walks the graveyard during the day, mimicking Dampé's walk and chastising [[Link]] anytime he tries to pull on a gravestone, threatening to tell Dampé.<ref>{{Cite|Don't cause trouble! I'll tell Dampé!|Graveyard Boy|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Little kids can't go on the "Heart-Pounding Gravedigging Tour" described on the sign. Since I can't do that, I'm just imitating Dampé the gravekeeper all day.|Graveyard Boy|OoT}}</ref> 

Dampé died during Link's seven-year disappearance, however, his ghost known as '''Dampé's Ghost''' can be found in his [[?|grave]].<ref name= "Treasure"/> He challenges Link to a race and rewards him with the [[Hookshot]] as promised in his {{Term|OoT3D|Gravekeeper's Diary|link|display=Diary}} that he left posthumously inside his shack.<ref>{{Cite|Whoever reads this, please enter my grave. I will let you have my stretching, shrinking keepsake. I'm waiting for you. --Dampé|Gravekeeper's Diary|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You were very quick to be able to keep up with me! Hehehe! As a reward, I'm going to give you my treasure. It's called the Hookshot!|Dampé|OoT}}</ref> Finishing the race in under a minute also warrants a Piece of Heart. If Link does not finish in under a minute after obtaining the Hookshot, he will receive a Red Rupee instead. After obtaining the Hookshot, it can be used on a torch to cut a few seconds off the clock in the race, allowing the [[Hero of Time]] to beat it more easily in less than a minute.

==={{MM|-}}===
Dampé appears as the gravekeeper for the [[Ikana Graveyard]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am Dampe, the grave keeper, who watches over this graveyard. My face is frightening, but I am not a bad person!|Dampé|MM}}</ref> Though terrified of ghosts, he keeps a haunted graveyard.<ref>{{Cite|Soon, a whole lot of frightful ghosts will appear. [...] Aaaieeee!!! Th-they're out!|Dampé|MM}}</ref> Little is known about Dampé in-game, but it is insinuated that he used to visit the graveyard with his father.<ref name="Father"/> Apparently, he also has a wife.<ref name="Wife"/>

Humorously, if Link talks to Dampé while wearing the [[Captain's Hat]], he, originally believing ghosts only come out at night, will become terrified and run around the graveyard before running back to his house (actually the path he walks throughout the day) and locking the door.

On the night of the [[Final Day]], Dampé can be found underground beneath a gravestone searching for the treasure of Ikana's Royal Family at the basement of his house.<ref>{{Cite|I came looking for the Royal Family's legendary treasure, but my torch has gone out.|Dampé|MM}}</ref> However, his torch has gone out, so he asks Link to guide him with Tatl's light.<ref>{{Cite|...A fairy? Oh, it was the fairy's light. In that case, it's just perfect. [...] I'm sorry, but could you walk in front of me and light the way?|Dampé|MM}}</ref> By [[Targeting System|Z-targeting]] him, Link can lead Dampé to mounds of dirt where he will sometimes dig up a blue fire (care must be taken as a [[Wallmaster]] is present). Once three such fires are found, they come together and reveal a [[Big Poe]],<ref>{{Cite|Waah!!! It's the leader of the ghosts!|Dampé|MM}}</ref> causing Dampé to run away and claims it is the leader of the ghosts in the graveyard. Once Link defeats this enemy and optionally capturing its [[Poe Soul]], a [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Bottle]] reveals itself. In {{MM3D}}, this was changed to a [[Piece of Heart]] instead. If Link leaves the area, he will find himself inside Dampé's house, with Dampé himself hiding underneath his bed. If spoken to, he will deny about seeing the Big Poe.

==={{FSA|-}}===
Dampé lives in the graveyard found to the far north in [[The Swamp]] in {{FSA}}. He is the caretaker of the graveyard, but since monsters roam the place, he cannot do his work.<ref>{{Cite|My name's Dampé. I'm supposed to be the caretaker at this here graveyard. Only I can't take care of a single thing right now. I'm helpless!!!|Dampé|FSA}}</ref> He is also tormented by [[Shadow Link]] whom he warns the [[Links]] about, surprised at how similar they are to him.<ref>{{Cite|This shadowy guy has been stirring up trouble lately. Actually, he looks like you!|Dampé|FSA}}</ref> Dampé then tells the four Links about a mirror, in which a dark tribe was sealed in a long time ago, informing them that it is hidden somewhere in the Forest of Light.<ref>{{Cite|Legends speak of a mirror in which a dark tribe was sealed away long ago. I understand it's supposed to be hidden now somewhere in the Forest of Light.|Dampé|FSA}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Dampé}}
{{Term|TMC|Dampé}} lives in his {{Term|TMC|Dampé's Shack|link|display=Shack}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}} and tends to the graveyard in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|You want to go to the tomb of {{Color|TMC Green|King Gustaf of Hyrule}}? Then you'd probably better talk to {{Color|TMC Green|Dampé}}. He's the gravedigger at the cemetery. Nobody knows the {{Color|TMC Green|Royal Crypt}} better!|Hylian|TMC}}</ref> Upon hearing that {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} was called to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}} by the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}}, he gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} the {{Term|TMC|Graveyard Key|link|display=Key}} to the graveyard.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if the king has called you, I can't see any reason not to let you in... Here, take this {{Color|TMC Red|graveyard key}}.|Dampé|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link}} recovers the {{Term|TMC|Graveyard Key}} from the {{Term|TMC|Takkuri|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Dampé}} opens the gate for him.<ref>{{Cite|I'll open the gate for you. Be careful in there.|Dampé|TMC}}</ref>

When spoken to after obtaining the gold {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} from within the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|display=Crypt}}, he will state a secret path in the woods of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley}} that leads to 50 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Blue|Left}}, {{Color|TMC Blue|left}}, {{Color|TMC Blue|left}}... {{Color|TMC Blue|Up}}, {{Color|TMC Blue|up}}, {{Color|TMC Blue|up}}... Dang! What's that last turn?|Dampé|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with {{Term|TMC|Dampé}} twice;<ref>{{Cite|What, you want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}?|Dampé|TMC}}</ref> the first successful {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}} results in the tomb of {{Term|TMC|Gina|link}} opening nearby. The second successful {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}} causes a crack to appear in the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|link}}; a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a red {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} is found inside.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
Dampé appears again as a gravekeeper in {{ALBW}}. He initially appears to be dull-witted, as he refers to himself in the third person, but he also claims to understand that the [[Captain]] only visits the [[Sanctuary (A Link Between Worlds)|Sanctuary]] to see [[Seres]].<ref>{{Cite|Coming and going! Going and coming! The captain stops by here more than anyone... You think he wants to yak with the priest? No, sir--just makin' excuses to see Seres there. She hasn't got a clue, either. But I s'pose that's what's so lovely about Seres. Carefree as a bird... But ol' Dampé sees more'n people think. Like those paintings on the castle walls this morning. There's trouble brew--|Dampé|ALBW}}</ref> While Link is searching for the Captain, he encounters Seres and Dampé speaking. After Seres becomes trapped within the Sanctuary, Dampé remembers that the [[Priest]] spoke of a secret passage into the Sanctuary and prompts Link to use the [[Forgotten Sword]] to make his way through it.<ref>{{Cite|Wh-what's happening in there? Th-th-the doors are shut tight. Can't get them open! Wh-what can we do? That was Seres crying out! Dampé doesn't have a key! Dampé just digs the graves around—! Wait! Dampé remembers! Dampé once heard the priest talking about a secret way into the Sanctuary, hidden right in my Graveyard. Bad luck, Dampé thinks, messing around with graves. Worse luck, going under the ground... But YOU! You do it. If you get scared, just swing that sword around!|Dampé|ALBW}}</ref>

Dampé also has a {{Term|ALBW|Lorulean|link}} counterpart named {{Term|ALBW|Dampé?|link}}.
