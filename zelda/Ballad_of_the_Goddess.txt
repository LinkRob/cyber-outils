The "{{Term/Store|Ballad of the Goddess||Series, SS, SSBfN3DS, SSBfWU, SSBU}}" is a recurring song in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 97 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref> The "{{Term|Series|Ballad of the Goddess}}" goes unnamed in {{TFH}}.

==Overview==
==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, the "Ballad of the Goddess" has been passed down from ancient times.<ref>{{Cite|You learned the Ballad of the Goddess! It's a mysterious melody that has been passed down from ancient times.|N/A|SS}}</ref> The song is first heard sung by [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] on the day of the [[Wing Ceremony]], performed on the [[Goddess]] Harp as well as sung with Hylian lyrics.<ref>{{Cite|The "Ballad of the Goddess." Based on my projections, this is likely the song Zelda sang on the day of the Wing Ceremony.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

[[Link]] learns the song from the [[Impa|old woman]] in the [[Sealed Temple]] after obtaining the Goddess's Harp. The woman tells him that the lyrics of the song can be used to locate the [[Sacred Flames]], which Link must find to strengthen his blade.<ref>{{Cite|Clues to finding the sacred flames have been woven into the lyrics of a song precious to your people, the Ballad of the Goddess. These clues are your best hope of finding your way to the flames.|Old Woman|SS}}</ref> Link can learn the lyrics from [[Gaepora]], which reveals that the two large windmills in [[Skyloft]] must face the [[Light Tower]]. Link must then search for the remaining sail to activate the windmills. Once done, Link must play the ballad on top of the tower to reveal a path into the [[Thunderhead]] and the [[Isle of Songs]] contained inside.

After the end credits, Link plays the "Ballad of the Goddess" atop the [[Statue of the Goddess]] with the Goddess's Harp.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, the "Ballad of the Goddess" is one of the songs that can be played by the musicians in the [[Milk Bar]] in [[Kakariko Village]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, the "Ballad of the Goddess" is one of the many tunes the [[Lucky Lobby Ball]] plays when hit with the [[Sword]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSB4|Wii U|-}}===
In {{SSB4|Wii U}}, the "Ballad of the Goddess" appears as one of several tracks played in the Skyloft and [[Tetra's Ship#Super Smash Bros. Series|Pirate Ship]] stages. A remix of the song also appears as part of a medley, titled "[[Ballad of the Goddess / Ghirahim's Theme]]".

==={{SSBU|-}}===
The "Ballad of the Goddess" and the arrangement from the medley, "Ballad of the Goddess / Ghirahim's Theme" reappear as tracks in {{SSBU}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The core melody of the "Ballad of the Goddess" is "[[Zelda's Lullaby]]" played backwards.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Vidvandre|published= May 4, 2011|retrieved= September 9, 2016|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rZwbchA4Hw4|title= Legend of Zelda Skyward Sword Trailer Played Backwards - Hidden theme|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref> This could be because Zelda's Lullaby is a song for Zelda to sleep in [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]], whereas the Ballad of the Goddess is tied to Zelda's metaphorical Awakening to her divine origin.
