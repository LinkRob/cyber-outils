The {{Term/Store|''Chief's Diary''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Chief's Diary|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|Chief's Diary}} is the personal journal of {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}, located in her {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom|link|display=Bedroom}} in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. It details her rise to chiefhood after her mother's death, the rampage of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, the theft of the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} arrival in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.

===Transcription===
Page 8 only appears once Link has initiated the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Thunder Helm|link}}".
