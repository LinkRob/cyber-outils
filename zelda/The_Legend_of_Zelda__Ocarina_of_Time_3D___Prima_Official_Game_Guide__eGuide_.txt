'''''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D — Prima Official Game Guide (eGuide)''''' is a digital strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{OoT3D}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The 224-page eBook has the same contents as "[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D — Prima Official Game Guide]]," containing a complete walkthrough of {{OoT3D}}, detailed maps, and a listing of {{Plural|OoT3D|Item|link}} and collectibles. It was offered as a digital download from Prima and features 21 sections with hypertext-enabled subsections.

The guide had a free preview containing partial snippets of various chapters. These include an overview of the game's introduction story, an explanation of controls and menus, a partial walkthrough of {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Forest|link}}, and {{Term|OoT3D|Item}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Minigame|link}}, and {{Term|OoT3D|Gold Skulltula|link}} lists.<ref>{{Cite Web|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110627170336/http://www.primagames.com/catalog/promo_image/9780307891549_1708.pdf|site=primagames.com (archived)|title=The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D (eGuide) - Free Preview|type=PDF|author=Prima Games|retrieved=October 1, 2022}}</ref>
