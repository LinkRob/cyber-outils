The {{Term/Store|Bee Badge||Series, ALBW}} is an item found in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 116 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Bee Badge is obtained after Link presents a [[Golden Bee]] to the {{Term|ALBW|Bee Guy|link}} in [[Kakariko Village]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh, bee still my beating heart! Is THIS--? Oh my, it IS! It's a golden bee! Thank you! She's very bee-witching, don't you think? That golden glow? That sweet note of honey that lingers in the air? And so regal--a queen of bees! She's perfect! You have outdone yourself. So please take this as thanks. Buzz.|Bee Guy|ALBW}}</ref> When obtained, the Bee Badge protects Link from any [[Bees]] and [[Golden Bee]]s that he might stumble upon while cutting grass in [[Hyrule]] or [[Lorule]].<ref>{{Cite|You got the Bee Badge! Now those pesky bees won't pester you anymore!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's the Bee Badge. As long as you have that with you, bees will be friendly. No more stings. Of course, you know what that means. You can find me even MORE bees. It should be a breeze! Keep up the good work--buzz, buzz!|Bee Guy|ALBW}}</ref> Instead of attacking Link, the Bee will instantly become friendly towards Link and follow the young hero,<ref>{{Cite|No more stings! This badge makes you the friend of every bee.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> attacking any other enemies that Link approaches. As the friendly Bees will simply hover around Link, it also makes collecting Bees a much easier task.
