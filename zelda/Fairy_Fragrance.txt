The '''Fairy Fragrance''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Fairy Fragrance|Cologne made of fairy flowers. Makes fairies appear a bit more often when cutting grass or other plants.<ref name="Fairy Fragrance">{{Cite|Cologne made of fairy flowers. Makes fairies appear a bit more often when cutting grass or other plants.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Fairy Fragrance is obtained from [[Buzzu]] after completing the [[Looking for Bempu]] [[Side Quest]]. Equipping it grants the [[Fairy Appearance Up +]] effect, which increases the likelihood of [[Fairy|Fairies]] appearing.
