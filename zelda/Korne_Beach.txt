{{Term/Store|Korne Beach||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Korne Beach'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Korne Beach}} is located along the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. It stretches south along the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}} before shifting west, turning into {{Term|BotW|Clarnet Coast|link}}. There are many small patches of {{Plural|BotW|Palm Tree|link}} along the beach which sport {{Term|BotW|Palm Fruit|link}}.

Around the middle of the beach, there is a spot with many rocks. Underwater near these rocks is a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can raise the chest out of the water with his {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} Rune. South of this chest, there is another chest underwater that holds an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}}. Farther out in the water, there are 2 chests that have sunk below the water. One of these chests holds a bundle of 5 {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}} while the other holds an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}}. Another chest can be found behind the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} underwater. This chest encloses a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}. East of this chest in the water is another chest. This chest contains a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}}. Farther north of this chest is another chest. This chest holds a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee}}. The final chest can be found right before {{Term|BotW|Korne Beach}} turns west into {{Term|BotW|Clarnet Coast}}. The chest is buried in the sand near a cluster of {{Plural|BotW|Palm Tree}}. This chest holds an {{Term|BotW|Amber}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found hiding in a Rock Circle a small distance from the water. There are 3 gaps that need to be filled with rocks. The rocks can be found in their own smaller circle formations around the bigger circle.

===Fang and Bone===
{{Main|Fang and Bone}}
{{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}} is a shop run by {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}}, who uses his own currency called {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mon is a currency I invented to disabilize the market and fight the establishment!|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> Kilton opens his shop in many different locations throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Korne Beach}} is one of them. {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} only opens his shop at Night so {{Term|BotW|Link}} can only find him then. During the Day, only a sign appears without {{Term|BotW|Kilton}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fang and Bone (only open at night)|Sign|BotW}}</ref>
