{{Term/Store|Extra Wallet|Extra Wallets|Series, SS|plural}} are items in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 122}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
Extra Wallets are yellow [[Wallet]]s that can each carry up to 300 Rupees, like the stands [[Small Wallet]]. There are only three Extra Wallets, each appearing in [[Beedle's Airshop]], where they can be bought for 100 Rupees apiece. Each Extra Wallet increases the maximum number of Rupees that Link can carry by 300, so having all three Extra Wallets increases the maximum capacity by 900 Rupees.
