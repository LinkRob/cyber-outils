The {{Term/Store|Benevolent Spring|Benevolent Spring|Series, HWAoC|plural}} is a {{Term|Series|Weapon|link}} used by {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Benevolent Spring}}<br/>|Weapons|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
In order to get the benevolent spring you need to first finish the main story line, then you will need to find 50 pieces of [[Terrako]] in order to unlock him. The you can play as him to be able to get it as a [[Weapons in Hyrule Warriors|weapon]] drop.
