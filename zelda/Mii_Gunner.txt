'''Mii Gunners''' are recurring characters in the {{SSB|Series}}.

==Biography==
==={{SSB4|-}}===
Mii Gunners are one of three types of customizable fighters introduced in {{SSB4}}. Their appearances are based on Mii characters created by the player, and are further customized with a variety of unlockable [[Mii Outfit]]s and [[Mii Headgear]]. Mii Gunners are unable to wear the Link Outfit (the only available Mii Outfit based on {{TLoZ|Series}}), but can wear all available Mii Headgear, including the Link Cap, [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]], and others.

One of the Mii Gunner's possible Side special moves, the Stealth Burst, functions similarly to [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]'s special move, [[Din's Fire]]; the Mii fires a projectile which gains speed and passes through opponents, exploding once the special move button is released (or after a certain length of time). The Mii Gunner's Down smash move has the same unique property as [[Toon Link]]'s where the forward first hit "pulls" the opponent into the rearward second hit, except that the move is performed with short-range blasts from the Mii's firearm instead of with slashes of the [[Master Sword]].
