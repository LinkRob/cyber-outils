==Other Appearances==

A compassionate [[Gerudo]] warrior once loyal to [[Ganondorf#Pre-Breath of the Wild|Ganondorf]], she is elected as their new chief and the later becomes the first '''[[Sage|Sage of Lightning]]''' after his ascension as Demon King.<ref>{{HWAoI|-}} -- In-game description: The compassionate chief of the Gerudo. Though she was once bound by Gerudo law to serve Ganondorf, she has since been chosen to lead her people in wake of the Demon King's ascension.</ref><ref name=":0">Tears of the Kingdom Masterworks: 初代ハイラル国王ラウルの 時代、封印戦争に力を貸し 秘石を受け取ったゲルド族。 族長 として一族を率いたと 思われ、ゲルド王家に連な る人物なのかもしれない (Translation: In the time of Rauru, the first King of Hyrule, the Gerudo tribe leant their power for the sealing war and received a secret stone.
