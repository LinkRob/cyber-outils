==Characteristics==
Fire Lynels, like the name suggests, are [[fire]]-elemental Lynels, with fiery runes wreathed around them.

Fire Lynels are equipped with a [[Savage Lynel Crusher]] and [[Fire Arrow]]s, but can be quickly be taken out of action with smart usage of the [[Ice Rod]].

Like all Lynels, Fire Lynels have choreographed attacks that can be countered by [[Runes]], but are quite quick to initiate them.

It's unique attacks are as follows

* Sending a fire wave forwards by slamming it's weapon
* Jumping back and firing a Fire Arrow
