{{Name|Japanese|Lion}} is a character in {{RTBToL}}.

==Biography==
Lion is a muscular humanoid lion. He is one of {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}}'s partners in the picture book world, beneath {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi|link}} and {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki|link}}. Despite Lion's appearance and strength, he is a cowardly character. His strength allows him to perform actions {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} and his other two friends cannot, such as removing certain obstacles.

==Trivia==
*Lion is based on the {{wp|Cowardly Lion}} from the ''Oz'' books.
