==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Creamy Seafood Soups are made in {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any seafood, [[Fresh Milk]], [[Rock Salt]], and any vegetable, herb, or flower together. However, if {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}} are used, the resulting dish will be [[Veggie Cream Soup]]. Regular Creamy Seafood Soup can be made by using either a {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}} in addition to ingredients lacking any effects. When eaten, Creamy Seafood Soups heal five {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and sell for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. The thick-cut chunks of seafood in these soups are known to provide a savory flavor.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on the ingredients used, the final meal may be one of the following varieties:

* '''Hearty Creamy Seafood Soup''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Creamy Seafood Soup}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Thick-cut chunks of seafood and stock provides a satisfying savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. Eating one of these soups will completely fill Link's Life Gauge and grant him additional hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/> They sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Energizing Creamy Seafood Soup''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Creamy Seafood Soup}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Thick-cut chunks of seafood and stock provides a satisfying savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}, or other stamina-boosting ingredients. When eaten, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be partially refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/> They heal eight of Link's Hearts, but they're worth 70 Rupees when sold.

* '''Hasty Creamy Seafood Soup''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Creamy Seafood Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Thick-cut chunks of seafood and stock provides a satisfying savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using speed-boosting ingredients. Eating these soups will temporarily increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/> They heal three Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Spicy Creamy Seafood Soup''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Creamy Seafood Soup}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Thick-cut chunks of seafood and stock provides a satisfying savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} or other spicy ingredients. When these are eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/> They also restore five Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Electro Creamy Seafood Soup''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Creamy Seafood Soup}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Thick-cut chunks of seafood and stock provides a satisfying savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}} or other electricity-related ingredients. When consumed, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]] for a short period of time.<ref name="Electro"/> They'll heal eight of Link's Hearts, but they can also be sold for 40 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Creamy Seafood Soup''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Creamy Seafood Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Thick-cut chunks of seafood and stock provides a satisfying savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, or other attack-boosting ingredients. Eating one will increase the damage of Link's attacks for a time.<ref name="Mighty"/> They replenish five Hearts in Link's Life Gauge, and they sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Tough Creamy Seafood Soup''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Creamy Seafood Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Thick-cut chunks of seafood and stock provides a satisfying savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}, or other defensive ingredients. If eaten, Link's defense will be temporarily increased.<ref name="Tough"/> They heal eight of Link's Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Sneaky Creamy Seafood Soup''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Creamy Seafood Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Thick-cut chunks of seafood and stock provides a satisfying savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. Eating one of these soups will increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities.<ref name="Sneaky"/> They heal five Hearts, and they're worth 50 Rupees when sold.

* '''Chilly Creamy Seafood Soup''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Creamy Seafood Soup}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Thick-cut chunks of seafood and stock provides a satisfying savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}} or other chilly ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/> They restore five Hearts to Link's Life Gauge, and they can be sold for 40 Rupees.
