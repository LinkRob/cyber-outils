The '''Sword of Might Lv. 3''' is the upgraded version of the [[Sword of Might]].

==Overview==
The [[Sword of Might Lv. 2]] can be upgraded to Lv. 3 at [[Lueburry|Lueburry’s]] House, by using 20 [[Might Crystal|Might Crystals]], enhancing its damage even more and unlocking the [[Sword Beam]] attack, which activates when the wielder is at full health.

==Trivia==
*The Sword of Might Lv. 3 bears a strong resemblance to the unpowered Master Sword from {{TWW}} and {{SS}}.
