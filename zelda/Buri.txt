{{Term/Store|Buri||Series, OoA (Himekawa)}} is a character in the {{OoA|Himekawa}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography== 
Buri is first shown fighting [[Link]] in an arena match in [[Lynna]], [[Labrynna]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Lynna, a city in Labrynna.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 5}}</ref> A member of the audience cheers for him,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Finish 'em off, Buri!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 5}}</ref> while the others bet [[Rupee]]s on Link winning.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The boy's going to win!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 5}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I bet 60 Rupees on that boy!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 5}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Okay! I'm still taking bets here.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 5}}</ref> Buri calls Link a pipsqueak and asks if he is ready to die.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Y'ready to die, Pipsqueak?|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 6}}</ref> He swings his axe at the hero, who in turn dodges the weapon and sends Buri crashing to the ground. When he gets back on his feet, Buri looks for Link, unaware that the boy is beneath him. Link says Buri made a big mistake by dropping his guard because of the hero's size.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You dropped your guard 'cause I'm small... BIIIG MISTAKE!!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 7}}</ref> Link pokes Buri in the behind with the handle of his sword and cuts the axe in half. Buri storms away angrily as Link receives the match's prize.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The winner is... Link! His prize...200 Rupees and one cow!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 8}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Rats!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 8}}</ref>
