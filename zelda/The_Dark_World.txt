{{Term/Store|The Dark World||Series, FSA}} is the fifth {{Term|FSA|Level|link}} of {{FSA}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|FSA|The Dark World}} appears as an area separate from the {{Term|FSA|Dark World|link}} accessed via {{Plural|FSA|Moon Gate|link}}. It is located in northwestern {{Term|FSA|Hyrule|link}}, and serves as the fifth {{Term|FSA|Level|link}}, consisting of three stages: the {{Term|FSA|Lost Woods (Four Swords Adventures)|link}}, {{Term|FSA|Kakariko Village (Four Swords Adventures)|link}}, and the {{Term|FSA|Temple of Darkness|link}}. As in other {{Plural|FSA|Level}}, {{Term|FSA|Tingle's Tower|link}} appears in multiplayer mode.

In search of the {{Term|FSA|Dark Mirror|link}}, the {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} venture here, where a cloud of darkness has covered {{Term|FSA|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|My forest has lost its light.
