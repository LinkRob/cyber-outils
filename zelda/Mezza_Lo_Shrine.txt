The {{Term/Store|Mezza Lo Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Mezza Lo Shrine}}<br/>Ancient Trifecta}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Ancient Trifecta''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Mezza Lo Shrine}} is initially buried beneath the ground of {{Term|BotW|Rabia Plain|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives in the area, he will find {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}, who will share that he knows an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" pertaining to their location.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The song describes mounting a beast with a bone crown atop its throne in order to reveal an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A beast that wears a crown of bone,<br/>Prancing through the lush green.<br/>Mount the beast upon its throne,<br/>For only then the shrine is seen.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} believes that "the lush green" refers to the plain, but he is unsure what animal wears a "crown of bone".<ref>{{Cite|I suppose "the lush green" could refer to this place. But what sort of beast wears a "crown of bone"?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Listening to him initiates "{{Term|BotW|The Crowned Beast|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

To complete the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must ride a {{Term|BotW|Mountain Buck|link}}, which can be found at the pond nearby, onto the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}. Mounting these {{Term|BotW|Deer|link}} requires a high level of {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}, as they will run away if they spot {{Term|BotW|Link}} nearby. Once this is done, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|The beast wearing a crown of bone was a mountain buck.<br/><br/>As the song instructed, being atop a mountain buck while it stood on the pedestal caused the shrine to appear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Mezza Lo Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mezza Lo|link}}, who introduces the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Mezza Lo. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Mezza Lo|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Mezza Lo Shrine}} is comprised of one large room. The {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} can be seen at a distance, though it is higher up than {{Term|BotW|Link}} and blocked by a portcullis. To the right of the entrance is a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}} located up a ramp. When the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} is struck, the pillar in the center of the room rotates clockwise through four positions. Past this ramp is a metal box, which is blocking a laser that would otherwise strike the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} must first move the pillar so that it located at the end of the ramp. A {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} must also be left beside the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}}. Then, after using the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the laser, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can move the metal box with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} while standing on the pillar. When {{Term|BotW|Stasis}} wears off, the laser will strike the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}}, moving both the pillar and {{Term|BotW|Link}} to its next position. While here, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Thunderblade|link}}.

While in this position, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} on the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} to move it to the pillar's next position. Alternatively, he can do the same thing to the metal box. He must then shoot the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}, moving the pillar to this position. Here, he can place the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} or metal box on a {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}}, which will open the portcullis blocking the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}.

By detonating the {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}}, the pillar will move {{Term|BotW|Link}} to its final position. From here, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to approach {{Term|BotW|Mezza Lo}}. The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} commends {{Term|BotW|Link}} for overcoming the Trial, giving him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Mezza Lo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mezza Lo}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Mezza Lo|BotW}}</ref>
