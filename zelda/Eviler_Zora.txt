{{Term/Store|Eviler Zora|Eviler Zora|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Eviler Zora|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Eviler Zora}} are stronger variants of the {{Term|CoH|Evil Zora|link}} enemy, appearing in both shallow and deep water, firing fiery projectiles at {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Zelda|link}} as they pass by. While stationary enemies, their behavior is linked to the beat of the music, firing their projectile only on the fourth beat, and then remaining exposed until returning back to the water on the eighth beat. Then, they will still stay hidden underwater for four more beats before reappearing on the fifth in order to repeat the pattern. They only stay above water for a total of seven beats.
