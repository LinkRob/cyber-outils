{{Term/Store|Laissa||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Laissa|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Laissa}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} who lives in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. She spends her days pacing around the home that she shares with her sister, {{Term|BotW|Bedoli|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} initially speaks to her, {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} asks what he is doing in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} and calls him crazy.<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing here? You adventurers are crazy.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} expresses confusion, she points out {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}, which is circling the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|[What?] What do you mean, "what"? You see {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} flying around up there?|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> She explains that it has put {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} is disarray.<ref>{{Cite|The village is in disarray thanks to that thing.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} is so angry about it that she would try to bring it down herself, but she restrains herself since she is not a soldier.<ref>{{Cite|If I were a properly trained soldier, I'd go straight at the beast and bring it down...|Laissa|BotW}}</ref>

During "{{Term|BotW|The Ancient Rito Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bedoli}} directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} to learn the second half of the song.<ref name="Bedoli"/> When she hears that {{Term|BotW|Bedoli}} forgot the rest of it, {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} remarks that her memory is the worst.<ref>{{Cite|So Bedoli told you I would sing you the last half of the ancient Rito song? Her memory really is the worst...|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} agrees to help {{Term|BotW|Link}}, but only if he can recite the half that {{Term|BotW|Bedoli}} taught him.<ref>{{Cite|I can help you...but there is a catch! Recite the first half from memory, and I will teach you the seocnd half.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to accurately repeat the first half of the song, {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} will honor her promise to teach him the second half.<ref>{{Cite|Aha! You got every word right! Well, a promise is a promise. I'll teach you the last half of the song.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics describe a heart shining upon an incomplete path, but a warm {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} is able to stir its soul.<ref>{{Cite|The heart shines upon a path not whole,<br/>but a warming flame can stir its soul.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> She remarks that the second half is profound.<ref>{{Cite|That's the second half of the ancient Rito song. Profound, isn't it?|Laissa|BotW}}</ref>

Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to ask {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} to sing the entire song for him at any time.<ref>{{Cite|...Don't tell me you came all the way here just to hear an ancient Rito song.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You want to hear the ancient Rito song?|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to listen, {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} remarks that being asked to sing on the spot makes her want to do so less, but she agrees nonetheless.<ref>{{Cite|[OK.] Nothing makes me want to not do something more than being asked to do it on the spot... But sure, why not.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} then proceeds to sing the whole song.<ref>{{Cite|The pride of the Rito, pillar in the sky,<br/>its heart lights up when the sun is high.<br/>The heart shines upon a path not whole,<br/>but a warming flame can stir its soul.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> She comments that the lyrics are deep and make one think.<ref>{{Cite|That's the whole song. Pretty deep, huh? Makes you think.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref>
