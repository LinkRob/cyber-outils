==Characteristics==
{{Stub|HWDE}}
They appear as red-colored Aeralfos with blue wings, although their attack strategy and weakness are identical to their common counterparts. When defeated, they may drop [[Fiery Aeralfos Leather]] as Bronze Material, and occasionally [[Fiery Aeralfos Wing]]s as Silver Material.
