The '''{{Romanize|Chamber of Giants|きょじんのま|Kyojin no ma|3}}''' is a mystical environment {{Term|MM3D|Link}} enters in {{MM}}.{{Name Ref|MM3D}} Each time he defeats a {{Term|MM3D|Boss|link}} dwelling in one of {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}'s four {{Plural|MM3D|Temple|link}} and acquires its Remains, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} frees one of the Four Giants and meets it here. The true nature of this chamber is something of a mystery, and whether it is actually a physical location or something that exists on an incorporeal plane is not revealed.

Each time {{Term|MM3D|Link}} visits this chamber, the surroundings change slightly to reflect the nature of the {{Term|MM3D|Temple}} he has just cleansed. When he meets the first Giant, the sky and clouds of the room are green, to reflect the {{Term|MM3D|Woodfall Temple|link}}; when he meets the second Giant, they are white, to reflect the {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead Temple|link}}; the third, blue, to reflect the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple|link}}; the fourth, yellow, to reflect the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|link}}. Alternately, as {{Term|MM3D|Link}} never meets more than one Giant at a time in this chamber, each of these locations could be a different place. {{Term|MM3D|Tatl|link}} has to interpret for {{Term|MM3D|Link}} on these occasions, as he does not understand the Giant's wailing language.

The first time {{Term|MM3D|Link}} meets a giant here after defeating the {{Term|MM3D|Woodfall Temple}}, he learns the {{Term|MM3D|Oath to Order|link}}, a mystical melody he can use to call the Giants once they are all free.<ref>{{Cite|Wait...Listen... It seems to be saying something... Could that crying be its way of teaching us some sort of melody? Don't just stand there... Get your instrument... "Call us." ...That's what it's saying.|Tatl|MM}}</ref> During his second visit, the Giant reveals their nature as protective {{Term|MM3D|Deity|display=gods|link}} of the people.<ref>{{Cite|W-What exactly are all of you? "Guardians." Guardians? So...You're protective gods? That's why you're in the temples... But...why are the protective gods...? No, wait! Are you listening to me? Hey! Wait...|Tatl|MM}}</ref> On the third visit, {{Term|MM3D|Tatl}} implores them to intervene on behalf of the world on the condition that she and {{Term|MM3D|Link}} free the last Giant.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, listen. We want you to lend us your power! If you just leave things as they are,  something terrible will happen to this world! Surely, you're the only ones who can stop it! That's what Tael was trying to tell us! "Help our friend." I get it. We have to help the last one! Then promise us this... You'll cooperate with us...|Tatl|MM}}</ref> Finally, on the last visit, the Giant instructs {{Term|MM3D|Link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Tatl}} to call them, but also tells them to forgive {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|display=their friend|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, we've helped all of you. ==Trivia==
*Regardless of the order in which {{Term|MM3D|Link}} clears the dungeons of {{Term|MM3D|Termina}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Chamber of Giants}} will always have the same order of color schemes, meaning green will always come first, followed by white, and then blue and finally yellow. This would seem to imply that the color of the chamber has no relation to the region {{Term|MM3D|Link}} had just cleared, or else that it is simply an oversight on the part of the developers.
