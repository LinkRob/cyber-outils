{{Term/Store|Jini||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Jini|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Jini}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who resides at the {{Term|BotW|Mounted Archery Camp|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}. Alongside {{Term|BotW|Straia|link}}, he is conducting an ecological study of the area.<ref>{{Cite|Hey there! It's not every day that I see someone all the way out here! We're doing an ecological study on the wild animals in the area.|Straia|BotW}}</ref> They were drawn to the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands}} when they heard of a {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}} living there that could not be found anywhere else.<ref>{{Cite|We'd heard that there was a {{Color|BotW Red|giant horse}} living in this region. Apparently it can't be found anywhere else... That was all we needed to hear! We headed this way to investigate right away!|Straia|BotW}}</ref> Due to monsters in the area, however, they have been unable to get close to it.<ref>{{Cite|However, with all the monsters roaming around, we simply can't go any farther. To be so close to discovery...|Straia|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Jini}}, he recognizes him as a trained warrior.<ref>{{Cite|Hello there, young man. I can tell just by looking that you're a trained warrior.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} how confident he is fighting while riding a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But tell me this: How confident are you in your ability to fight on horseback?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} claims to be confident, he asks if he would like to prove his skills.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah? You got the skills to back that confidence up? Nothing's more dangerous than a man who thinks he can handle the worst, only to fall apart when the time comes. What do you think? Are you confident enough to {{Color|BotW Red|prove you got the right stuff}}?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not confident, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will be concerned and asks if he wants to run some drills.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... I don't like the sound of that. The monsters out this way are strong critters. You're sure you don't want to {{Color|BotW Red|drill}} with me some?|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask how he can prove himself, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} introduces him to {{Term|BotW|Horseback Archery|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Prove it how?] Ever heard of {{Color|BotW Red|horseback archery}}? They invented it way out east somewhere.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> The goal is to shoot targets with {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} while riding a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|It takes a lot of coordination to shoot targets with a bow and arrow from the back of a galloping horse.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> With these drills, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} claims that {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be prepared to face off against mounted [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|enemies]].<ref>{{Cite|I expect you'll run into mounted varmints now and again, so this'll be a good way to get some practice in.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to attempt a drill, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} remarks that such impulsiveness may save his life one day.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure, I'm game.] They say youth breeds impulsiveness, but it's that kind of quick certainty that'll save your hide. Maybe someday soon.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} with him, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} tells him to find one before they can begin.<ref>{{Cite|Now...for a young man interested in horseback archery, you seem to be in want of a horse. That's no way to {{Color|BotW Red|drill}}.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return to the nearby {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}} to retrieve his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, if he owns one.<ref>{{Cite|Head down that path there, and you ought to come across a {{Color|BotW Red|stable}}. Fetch your steed from them if you got one. Get a horse, mount up, then come talk to me {{Color|BotW Red|while you're riding high in the saddle}}.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Jini}} asks for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} in order to run a {{Term|BotW|Horseback Archery}} drill,<ref>{{Cite|Now...I'm loath to ask, but would you mind forking over {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} to cover some of my costs?|Jini|BotW}}|</ref> which he guarantees is worth twice as much.<ref>{{Cite|Much obliged, young man. I guarantee my {{Color|BotW Red|drills}} are worth twice what you pay.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> While he lis happy running drills, he requires payment in order to meet his own needs.<ref>{{Cite|This here's a service I'm happy to provide, but I can't do it for free. Don't judge a man for his needs.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

After completing a drill, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will call {{Term|BotW|Link}} back to the {{Term|BotW|Mounted Archery Camp}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hold it! Head on back here.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} hit less than 11 {{Plural|BotW|Balloon|link}}, he suggests trying to shoot while the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} is trotting instead of galloping.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... You only hit {{Color|BotW Red|[X]}}... Nothing to write home about. Might want to shoot from {{Color|BotW Red|a trot}} some more before you jump straight into firing at a gallop.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> There is no reward. Between 12 and 19 {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}}, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} is not convinced of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s skill and reminds him to keep his eye on the targets.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... Well, you hit {{Color|BotW Red|[X]}}... Not bad, but I'm not rightly convinced you're ready to raise the stakes. Hitting your mark at a gallop is no small potatoes. Make sure to {{Color|BotW Red|keep your eye}} on the target.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} 15 {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a reward for seeing it through. Keep drilling and you'll get it.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} pops between 20 and 22 {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}}, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} says he has the potential to be among the best archers and gives him the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bridle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm...{{Color|BotW Red|[X] targets}}... You've got some fire in you, young man. The best archers say {{Color|BotW Red|targets seem to freeze when they concentrate on them}}. I think you could reach that level too. Here's a gift to help you on your travels. Hope you find a good use for it.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} already has the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bridle}}, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will instead give him 10 {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}.}} Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} hits between 23 and 25 targets, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will say he has not seen someone so young with such talent in a long time.<ref>{{Cite|Hm...{{Color|BotW Red|[X] targets}}... It's been a dog's age since I've seen such talent in someone so young.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He does not believe that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is actually over 100 years old and gives him the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Saddle|link}}, as he no longer needs it.<ref>{{Cite|What do you mean you're not that young? Over 100 years old? Go ahead, pull the other one. Anyway, this gift is my thanks for putting on a good show. I'm past needing it, and I think you should have it.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} already has the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Saddle}}, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will instead give him 20 {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow}}.}}

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has received his reward, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} asks if he would like to run another drill.<ref>{{Cite|Well? You want to {{Color|BotW Red|drill}} some more?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} reminds him not to be a stranger and to come back.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm in a hurry...] Don't be a stranger, young man. I'll be waiting here for you, come wind or storm.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} says he made the right call and asks for 20 more {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sounds like the right call to me. The price hasn't gone down any—{{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}, same as ever. That fine with you?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has paid, he needs to fix the {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}} that were destroyed on {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s previous run.<ref>{{Cite|I'll go fix up the targets. Shouldn't be long.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> After fixing up the {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}}, he is ready for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go again.<ref>{{Cite|All done now. Let's get drilling again.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>

Before running any drills, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will offer to sell {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} or buy from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Can't drill without a {{Color|BotW Red|bow and arrows}}. I'd think that's just common sense. If you need some, I can sell you a few of mine. Or I'm open for any sort of business! What do you say?|Jini|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take his time browsing.<ref>{{Cite|I have an impressive collection here! Take your time!|Jini|BotW}}</ref> During {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, he will include {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}} in his stock.<ref>{{Cite|I've got a special rainy-day deal for you! I'll sell you the best item I've got!|Jini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Jini}} will continue with the drill.<ref>{{Cite|Suit yourself. We'll see how well you and your horse act as a team on the field.|Jini|BotW}}</ref>
