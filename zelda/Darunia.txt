He is the leader of the [[Hyrule]]an [[Goron|Gorons]] and the Sworn Brother of the [[Royal Family of Hyrule#Ocarina of Time|King of Hyrule]].<ref name=Quote3>{{Cite|What the heck? Who are you?! ==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
Living in [[Goron City]] within [[Death Mountain]], Darunia is the patriarch of the Gorons in {{OoT}}. He is a strict leader who sees the greater cause behind initial problems and solutions. During [[Link]]'s childhood, [[Ganondorf]], the king of the [[Gerudo]] tribe (and human form of [[Ganon]]), visited the Gorons shortly before the young hero did, and demanded that they give him the [[Spiritual Stone of Fire]], also known as the Goron's Ruby, itself one of the three [[Spiritual Stone|Spiritual Stones]] Link must obtain for [[Princess Zelda]]. If the Gorons would not hand over the sacred gem, he would keep the entrance to [[Dodongo's Cavern]], their main source of food, closed.<ref name=Quote5>{{Cite|It's me, Darunia! Well done! Thanks to you, we can once again eat the delicious rocks from the Dodongo's Cavern until our stomachs burst! What a wild adventure! It will make an incredible story... I can't believe that the Dodongos suddenly appeared in such great numbers! And that big rock blocking the cave... All this trouble must have been caused by that Gerudo thief, Ganondorf! He said, "Give me the Spiritual Stone! Only then will I open the cave for you!" You, on the other hand, risked your life for us... Kid, I like you! How's about you and I become Sworn Brothers?! No, there's no big ceremony involved! Just take this as a token of our friendship! Brother! You'll keep brushing up on your skills as you travel, won't you? You should go see the Great Fairy on top of Death Mountain! She will power you up! Hey, everybody! Let's see off our Brother!|Darunia|OoT}}</ref> Despite that this would mean that the Gorons would starve to death, Darunia did not give the stone to Ganondorf, knowing that it was too important. However, because the Goron species' main diet is rocks, the Goron's Ruby also became more and more delicious-looking to the now-hungry Gorons. To protect the pink gem entrusted to the Goron race, Darunia locked himself up in his room with the Spiritual Stone, stating that he would wait there for the messenger of the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey! It's dangerous for a little kid like you to come out here. You might fall down! If I'm not mistaken, you came out here to eat the red stone! Well, too bad! It's not here! What? That's not why you're here? You're looking for a "Spiritual Stone?" You must mean that delicious-looking stone that was once displayed here! I was so hungry that I thought it would be OK to just give it one tiny little lick...so I snuck out here. But, it was already gone! I think Big Brother took it away. He always says that everyone is after that red stone! Big Brother has shut himself up in his room saying, "I will wait in here for the
Royal Family's messenger!"|Goron|OoT}}</ref>

When Link arrived, he was informed of this by the other Gorons. He then went to Darunia's room and played [[Zelda's Lullaby]]. Darunia recognized this sign and opened the door, only to be enraged by the fact that the Royal Family's messenger was a mere boy, and refused to talk with him about the Goron's Ruby.<ref name="Quote3" /> Link was tipped by one of the other Gorons in the city that the way to Darunia's good side was through music and dance.<ref name="Quote4" /> From the words of several more Gorons, Link understood that the song most likely to get Darunia in a good mood was none other than [[Saria's Song]], which itself can be heard by the entrance leading to the [[Lost Woods]].<ref>{{Cite|Well, that's not quite what I was looking for... I wanted to hear... a tune... something... um... from outside the mountains... something... green... natural... That's the kind of music I want to hear...|Darunia|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I remember Big Brother used to always listen to the music that comes from the forest... Ah yes, the good old days... That music makes me feel nostalgic, too...|Goron|OoT}}</ref> The young hero played the song for Darunia, causing the latter to lose his depression and break out in a fit of dancing.<ref>{{Cite|Heeey!! What a nice tune! Just like that, my depression is all gone! Something just came over me! I suddenly wanted to dance like crazy! I am Darunia! I'm the big boss of the Gorons! Was there something you wanted to ask me about? What? You want the Spiritual Stone of Fire, too? The Spiritual Stone of Fire, also known as the Goron's Ruby, is our race's hidden treasure... But hold on--I'm not going to give it to you that easily. If you want it so badly... Why don't you go destroy the monsters inside of the Dodongo's Cavern and prove you're a real man? That way, everybody will be happy again! If you do it, I will give you anything you want, even the Spiritual Stone! I have something for you. I'm not really giving you this in return for anything, but take it anyway. If you wear this, even a little fella like you can pick up a Bomb Flower. Destroy the monsters in the Dodongo's Cavern and become a real man! Then, we can talk about the Spiritual Stone!|Darunia|OoT}}</ref> Despite this, Darunia was still unwilling to give up the Goron's Ruby and challenged Link to prove his worth by destroying the monsters inside of Dodongo's Cavern. As a small bit of help, he gave Link the [[Goron Bracelet]], which allows him to lift [[Bomb Flower|Bomb Flowers]].

Link, with his heroic deeds, passed the test and defeated [[King Dodongo]]. When he left the cavern, Darunia was waiting for him. He happily thanked and congratulated Link for what he had done for himself and his people (and comically patting the hero hard enough to knock him to the ground). He then asked Link to become his Sworn Brother, symbolized by giving him the Spiritual Stone of Fire.<ref name="Quote5" />

Some time in Link's 7-year absence, Darunia had a son, who he named after his friend and the Gorons' hero: [[Link (Goron)|Link]]. Darunia made sure to tell his son as much as possible about the "legendary [[Dodongo]] Buster" who had helped them so much. Shortly before Link returned as the [[Hero of Time]], Ganondorf also returned to Goron City once more to cause even more trouble than before. The Evil King revived the fire dragon [[Volvagia]], and imprisoned the Gorons in the [[Fire Temple]] with the intention of having them eaten by Volvagia, as an example to the [[:Category:Races|other races]] on who would oppose the Gerudo warlock.<ref>{{Cite|Everybody was taken to the Fire Temple... While my dad was out... Ganondorf's followers came and took them all away! All of them will be eaten by Volvagia! Dad said that Ganondorf has revived Volvagia... As a warning to those who might oppose him, Ganondorf is going to feed them all to Volvagia! Dad went to the Fire Temple all by himself to try to save everyone... Please help, Link! I'll give you this heat-resistant tunic!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> Both Darunia and his son managed to escape this fate, but Darunia himself left for the Fire Temple anyway in an attempt to save his people. Before he left, he instructed his son, who stayed in Goron City, to let no one follow him.<ref>{{Cite|What? Your name is also Link? Then you must be the legendary Dodongo Buster and Hero, Link! My dad is Darunia... Do you remember him? Dad named me Link after you, because you're so brave! It's a cool name! I really like it! Link, you're a hero to us Gorons! I'm so glad to meet you! Please give me your autograph! Sign it: "To my friend, Link of the Gorons" Oh... I guess it's not a good time to ask you for this... Please help everyone! My dad, Darunia, went to the Fire Temple. A dragon is inside! If we don't hurry up, even my dad will be eaten by the dragon!! B-b-b-boooo hooooo!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> However, when the latter met with the hero he had heard so much about from his father, he was convinced that he should let him help and told Link about the secret entrance to [[Death Mountain Crater]].<ref>{{Cite|Dad told me not to let anybody follow him to the temple, but... Only you, Link, can save everyone! I'm sure that the shop owner, who is hiding somewhere right now, will also help you! Now, I'll tell you about the secret passage to the Fire Temple! Try to move the statue inside Dad's room!|Link (Goron)|OoT}}</ref> 

Link soon met up with Darunia in the Fire Temple. The Goron leader was very happy to see his friend again and is also proud to see Link grow up into a young man, but also realized that this was not the time for talk. After informing him of Volvagia's revival, Darunia asked Link to free his people while he attempts to stop the dragon (but feels concerned that he does not possess the [[Megaton Hammer]], which his ancestor used to to destroy the monster in the past), who had been locked up in prison cells throughout the temple.<ref>{{Cite|Who's there? Is that you, Link...? Oh, it really is Link! You've grown so big since I last saw you! I want to have a man-to-man talk with you, but now's not the time. Ganondorf is causing trouble on Death Mountain again! He has revived the evil, ancient dragon Volvagia! On top of that, he is going to feed my people to that evil dragon as a warning to other races that might resist him... If that fire-breathing dragon escapes from the mountain, all of Hyrule will become a burning wasteland! I will go on ahead to try to seal up the evil dragon... I'm concerned, though, because I don't have the legendary hammer... But I have no choice. Link...I'm asking you to do this as my Sworn Brother... While I'm trying to deal with the dragon, please save my people! The prisoners' cells are in the opposite direction. I'm counting on you, Link!|Darunia|OoT}}</ref> After eventually fulfilling Darunia's request, Link encountered and defeated Volvagia using the Megaton Hammer himself. He was then teleported to the [[Chamber of Sages]] to meet the new [[Sage|Sage of Fire]], who turned out to be none other than Darunia.<ref name="Quote2">{{Cite|Thank you, Brother! I really appreciate what you did. I thank you on behalf of the entire Goron race! You turned out to be a real man, just as I thought you would! By the way, I, the wild Darunia, turned out to be the great Sage of Fire... Isn't that funny, Brother? Well, this must be what they call destiny. Nothing has made me happier than helping you seal the evil here! Hey, Brother, take this! This is a Medallion that contains the power of the fire spirits--and my friendship.|Darunia|OoT}}</ref> Darunia, filled with heartwarming gratitude, thanked the Hero of Time for helping him save his people a second time and gave him the [[Fire Medallion]],<ref>{{Cite|You received the Fire Medallion! Darunia awakens as a Sage and adds his power to yours!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> reminding him that they were true brothers.<ref>{{Cite|Don't forget... Now you and I are true brothers!|Darunia|OoT}}</ref>

When Link ultimately defeated Ganon, Darunia and the other five Sages combined their powers to seal away the [[demon]] into the [[Dark World|Evil Realm]]. During the ending, he was seen overlooking a peaceful [[Hyrule]] on top of [[Death Mountain Trail]] with the fellow Sages [[Saria]], [[Princess Ruto]], [[Impa]], and [[Nabooru]]. Saria can be seen riding on Darunia's shoulders, whom he proudly looks at above him.

== Minor References ==

=== {{MM|-}} ===
[[File:MM Darmani III Model.png|thumb|Darmani III, the Terminan counterpart of Darunia]]
Although Darunia himself does not appear in {{MM}}, he has a [[Termina|Terminan]] counterpart, named [[Darmani III]]. He is a renowned Goron hero who was considered the greatest warrior of his tribe in the [[Goron Village]] and a possible heir to Goron throne. However, he tragically died when he was blown into the valley of [[Snowhead Region|Snowhead]], ironically by [[Biggoron]] (who was under the [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]]'s influence) in an attempt to destroy [[Goht]], the mechanical monster responsible for the suffering. Link would soon gain his powers through the [[Goron Mask]] upon [[Song of Healing|healing]] his spirit. Darmani being a hero of the Gorons and helping Link are likely a reference to Darunia being the leader of the race as well as the Hero of Time being his Sworn Brother.

=== {{TWW|-}} ===
[[File:TWW Darunia Stained Glass.png|thumb|The stained glass of Darunia in {{TWW|-}}]]
While Darunia did not appear in {{TWW}}, like the other Sages who helped the Hero of Time seal Ganondorf within the corrupted [[Sacred Realm]], he is memorialized within one of the stained glass windows in [[Hyrule Castle]]'s basement, where the [[Master Sword]] rests in the [[Pedestal of Time]]. The [[Goron Emblem]] is depicted directly above him. His likelihood in the stained glass window is one of several examples of how famous the events of {{OoT|-}} became in the history of Hyrule within the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Adult Timeline]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
Darunia appears as a playable Warrior in {{HW}}. He is a very straightforward power character, forgoing finesse and agility for brute force. His weapon type of choice is the [[Hammer]], which he is able to swing around with ease despite its immense size. Darunia uses the Hammer to pound away and swing at enemies, as well as to guard. Although while his main weapon is the Hammer, Darunia also uses his brute, physical strength in battle as well. His strength is so great that he can crack the earth, causing geysers of fire and lava to shoot forth and can catch flaming boulders from the sky that he powerfully throws. He can also roll to dodge attacks as well as to perform a Special Attack where he grows to enormous size temporarily and smashes surrounding enemies. The Hammer is of the Fire element.

Darunia first appears in the [[Death Mountain (Hyrule Warriors)|Death Mountain]] stage, after [[Cia]]'s power merges lands from other eras into present-day Hyrule. [[Impa]] and [[Sheik]] arrive to close the nearby [[Gate of Souls]], but are then approached by a Fairy ([[Navi]] in the North American release) who tells them that [[Princess Ruto|Ruto]] was kidnapped and is being held captive by Darunia and his troops.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Listen! Princess Ruto is in trouble! [...] She was abducted by the Gorons!|Navi|HW}}</ref> Darunia, normally a gentle person, had mysteriously changed ever since Zelda had appeared.<ref>{{Cite|Their leader Darunia used to be such a gentle soul. Someone named Zelda showed up and... He hasn't been the same since!|Navi|HW}}</ref> The [[Hyrulean Forces]] leave to investigate the matter, and are attacked by Darunia and his Gorons. However, Impa and Sheik manage to power through his troops and defeat Darunia, snapping him out of his senses<ref>{{Cite|There she subdued the Goron chieftain, Darunia, who had temporarily lost his senses.|N/A|HW}}</ref> and releasing Ruto from his hold. The two Sheikah learn that the cause for the Gorons' and the Zoras' conflict was the Gate of Souls open in the {{Term|OoT3D|Water Temple|link}} of [[Lake Hylia (Hyrule Warriors)|Lake Hylia]], apparently being controlled by Zelda herself.<ref>{{Cite|Upon freeing Princess Ruto, however, Impa was shocked to learn the reason for the conflict between the Zora and Goron people. The ancient Water Temple was teeming with monsters that poured from the Gate of Souls. What's more, the monsters were apparently led by Princess Zelda herself!|N/A|HW}}</ref> Darunia then joins the Hyrulean Forces alongside Ruto to do battle with Cia's forces and to close the Gate, and later accompany Impa and Sheik back into present-day Hyrule to join the Allies in fighting Cia's forces.

Darunia accompanies many of Link's battles, including the battle at the [[Temple of Souls]], when he arrives to help defend Link from Cia's [[Dark Link]]s. After Link defeats Cia at the [[Valley of Seers]], [[Lana]] sends Darunia back to his time. However when Ganondorf's forces attack Hyrule, she summons him again and the other Allies in [[Gerudo Desert (Hyrule Warriors)|Gerudo Desert]]. After Ganon is defeated, Darunia fades away with the others, presumingly back to his time again.

==Trivia==
*Darunia, like the other [[Sage|Sages]] from {{OoT}}, shares his name with a [[Mountain Town of Darunia|town]] from {{TAoL}}.
*If [[Link]] attempts to play a different song rather than "[[Saria's Song]]" to Darunia, he can be seen seemingly trying to dance for a brief moment before smirking and telling the young hero that it doesn't have the beat they he knows that would make him happy.
*Darunia is the only character in ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'' to have his own theme song played on his victory sequence. The theme is a remixed version of "Saria's Song" with him dancing in the same way as in {{OoT|-}}.
*As the scene can occur at anytime, Darunia's humorous dance in {{OoT|-}} is a popular meme among the ''Zelda'' fanbase.
*While Darunia does not appear in {{BotW}}, the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} is named after him as an anagram.
*Considering his physical build, it's likely Darunia is one of the strongest [[Goron|Gorons]] in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]].
*Like Link the [[Hero of Time]], Darunia normally appears serious, but is good in heart.
