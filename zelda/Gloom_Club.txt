The '''Gloom Club''' is a [[Weapons in Tears of the Kingdom|weapon]] introduced in {{TotK}}.

== Location and uses ==
Gloom Clubs can be wielded by [[Link#Tears of the Kingdom|Link]], though the [[Gloom]] will gradually wear down his [[Life Gauge|health]], making it a double-edged weapon like the [[Gloom Sword]] and [[Gloom Spear]]. When [[fuse]]d with other weapons, however, it gives them a massive boost, allowing Link to use it without suffering the Gloom depletion. As with the other Gloom weapons, the Club cannot be upgraded nor repaired by [[Rock Octorok]]s. One Gloom Club is wielded by a [[Gerudo]] during the ''[[The Gerudo Assault]]'' [[Memory]] back when they were fighting [[Hyrule]], while it is most notably used by [[Ganondorf]] himself, as well as [[Phantom Ganon]] during the game itself.

== Trivia ==
*The Gloom Club is a [[wikipedia:Kanabō|Kanabō]], a type of spiked club that's often associated with Oni, which is the partial basis for Ganondorf's Demon King form in the game.
