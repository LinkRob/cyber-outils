The {{Term/Store|Goron Garb||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Goron Garb lets you swim in lava. Is that not what fashionless young heroes do in their spare time?}}

The {{Term|TFH|Goron Garb}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} dresses {{Term|TFH|Link}} as a {{Term|TFH|Goron|link}}, with body paint on his flanks, a loincloth, and a mane similar to {{Term|OoT|Darunia|link}}'s. Notably missing are the [[Goron Emblem]]s on the arms of the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}.

The {{Term|TFH|Goron Garb}} grants {{Term|TFH|Link}} protection from {{Term|TFH|Fire|link}} of all forms, allowing him to swim freely in [[Lava]] and attack {{Term|TFH|Fire}}-based enemies—such as {{Plural|TFH|Fire Keese|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Fire Gimos|link}}—without being burned.<ref>{{Cite|Receive no damage from lava. Burns, begone!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> {{Term|TFH|Link}} is also unaffected by {{Term|TFH|Fire}} projectiles and traps while wearing the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}.
