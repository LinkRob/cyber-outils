==Overview==
{{Term|TotK|Sidon|link}} gives {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} a tip of a "secret treasure" while he was at the archives. In short, it read of a "secret treasure beneath the bridge that leads to the great statue, behind two waterfalls." He feels intrigued if there really is a treasure behind this riddle, but leaves it up to Link to pursue it or not.

Transcribing the riddle, Link sees the large fish monument of the {{Term|TotK|Zora's Domain|link}} from the distance at the Great Zora Bridge. Noting the bridge and the waterfall under it, Link dove into the ravine and swam behind the waterfall. Inside, he finds a secret cave, and another waterfall ahead of it. Inside the second waterfall is a treasure chest, containing {{Term|TotK|Vah Ruta Divine Helm|link}}.
