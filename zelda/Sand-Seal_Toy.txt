==Locations and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Sand-Seal Toy}} are plush versions of {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} that are found in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. A vast majority of them are owned by {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} and located within {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}}. Another can be found in the room off of {{Term|BotW|Patricia|link|display=Patricia's}} courtyard.

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom}} at night, he may catch her playing with her {{Plural|BotW|Sand-Seal Toy}}.<ref>{{Cite|What should we play?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She becomes embarrassed when {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her and tells him to ignore the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Toy|display=Toys}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wh-what?! The sand-seal stuffed animals? Don't worry about them! Just... Just ignore them, OK?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> Despite saving {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} still thinks he is rude for entering her room so late.<ref>{{Cite|Besides, town hero or no, you shouldn't just come wandering into someone's room at this time of night!|Riju|BotW}}</ref> When she sleeps, she curls up next to the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Toy}} on her bed.
