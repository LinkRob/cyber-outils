==Biography==
{{Term|PH|Dobo}} is an {{Term|PH|Anouki}} from the {{Term|PH|Isle of Frost}}, living in the {{Term|PH|Anouki Estates|link}}. It is among those {{Term|PH|Link|link}} questions while he is searching for a {{Term|PH|Yook|link}} who has abducted and impersonated one of the villagers. {{Term|PH|Dobo}} tells {{Term|PH|Link}} that {{Term|PH|Mazo|link}} is honest, and therefore can not be the Yook.<ref>{{Cite|They call me Dobo. Mazo's real honest. The guy never lies!|Dobo|PH}}</ref> Mazo, in turn, tells Link that Dobo is also telling the truth.<ref>{{Cite|They call me Mazo. I don't care if you believe me, but me and Dobo only tell the truth. Just thought I'd say that.|Mazo|PH}}</ref>

As it turns out, neither {{Term|PH|Dobo}} or {{Term|PH|Mazo}} is being impersonated.
