==Location and Uses==
{{Lacking Sources|BotW}}
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Cuirass}} are pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|BotW|Cherry|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. When worn, it provides one level of Guardian Resist. {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Cuirass}} can be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} and can be {{Term|BotW|Dye|link}}d at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Cuirass}} joins the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Helm|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ancient Greaves|link}} to compose the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Set|link}}. When each piece of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Set}} has been enhanced at least two times by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy}} and the full {{Term|BotW|Ancient Set}} is worn, it provides the "Ancient Proficiency" {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link}}.
