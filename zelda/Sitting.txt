{{Term/Store|Sitting||Series, SS, BotW}} is a recurring ability in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|SS, BotW}}

==Overview==
==={{SS|-}}===
{{Stub|SS}}
In {{SS}}, {{Term|SS|Sitting}} allows {{Term|SS|Link|link}} to recover {{Plural|SS|Heart|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
In {{BotW}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can {{Term|BotW|Sitting|display= Sit}} near a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} to pass time.  {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}}'s can be either lit bundles of wood, natrually spawned in certain enemy camps and traveller camps/settlements (Identical to the lit bundles) or cooking pots that are found around stables and major settelments.

When standing nearby and facing a lit {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} a prompt to "sit" will be given. If the player presses (A) they will be prompted with a list of 4 choices, each of which will fast forward the player to a specific time.

* Morning. (''5:00 AM'')
* Noon. (''12:00 PM'')
* Night. (''9:00 PM'')
* Never mind. (''Leave the Campfire'')
