The {{Term/Store|Gold Card||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|ST|Link|link}} unlocks the {{Term|ST|Gold Card}} after he accumulates 500 points from {{Term|ST|Beedle's Air Shop|link}}, at which point he reaches Gold Membership and is given a {{Term|ST|Heart Container|link}} by {{Term|ST|Beedle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh! You have 520 points! You're now an official {{Color|ST Light Red|Gold Member}}! That means you get {{Color|ST Light Red|20% off all merchandise}} AND a {{Color|ST Light Red|lovely prize}}!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> 

{{Term|ST|Beedle}} promises to send Link his {{Term|ST|Gold Card}} the next day,<ref>{{Cite|I'll send your Gold Card tomorrow, so be sure to check your mail. Keep earning those points, because the next level is Platinum!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> however regardless of the span of time that has passed, Link will be able to receive the {{Term|ST|Gold Card}} at any {{Term|ST|Postbox|link}}. When {{Term|ST|Link}} has the {{Term|ST|Gold Card}}, all products at {{Term|BotW|Beedle's Air Shop}} will be 20% off.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|ST Light Red|Gold Card}}! Now everything at Beedle's shop is 20 percent off!|N/A|ST}}</ref>
