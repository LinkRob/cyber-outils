The {{Term/Store|Power to Repel Evil||Series, TWW}} is a powerful recurring [[Magic]] ability in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name=":0">{{Cite|You cannot defeat me with a {{Color|TWW Red|blade that does not sparkle}} with the {{Color|TWW Red|power to repel evil}}! What you hold is useless.|Ganondorf|TWW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|FSA, TPHD, SSHD, BotW}} Two primary [[Sword|Swords]] of the franchise have this ability: the {{Term|Series|Master Sword|link}} (which is called the "Blade of Evil's Bane" for this very reason), and the {{Term|Series|Four Sword|link}}. A telltale sign of the aforementioned weapons having the sacred power is by their blades glowing with bright holy energy (most likely [[light]]). Much like the swords themselves, it plays a very important role in the franchise, as the ability is required for [[Link]] in order to defeat [[Ganon]] or other lead antagonists in most games and other media.

==Uses==
The Power to Repel Evil is the legendary ability to banish pure evil. It is most often used to defeat the series' primary antagonist, Ganon, usually as [[Ganondorf]], though it has been shown to have other uses in some games. For instance, in {{ALttP}}, Link used this power to reflect [[Agahnim]]'s fireball spell in the [[Dead Man's Volley]] duel.

In {{TWW}}, the Hero of Winds uses the Master Sword infused with the power to repel evil to destroy the dark barrier surrounding [[Hyrule Castle]], thus giving him free roam of [[Hyrule|the path]] to [[Ganon's Tower (The Wind Waker)|Ganon's Tower]]. The Power to Repel Evil is infused in the Master Sword with the help of the two [[Sage|Sages]] of the Master Sword, resulting in its trademark magical glow. Similarly to the [[Light Arrow]]s, the power to repel evil is the only thing that can harm Ganondorf in-game.

In {{FSA}}, at the end of every level there are dark magic barriers blocking entrance to the next level. The [[Links]] can only destroy them if they collect at least 2000 [[Force Gem]]s to give the [[Four Sword]] the power to repel evil.

==Appearances==

=== ''A Link to the Past'' ===
[[File:ALttP Master Sword power to repel evil.png|thumb|The Master Sword flowing with the power to repel evil in ''A Link to the Past'']]
In ''A Link to the Past'', the Master Sword can be seen glowing with the power to repel evil when it is pulled from its [[Pedestal of the Master Sword|pedestal]] in the [[Lost Woods]]. This is the first explicit appearance of the ability overall.

=== ''Ocarina of Time'' ===
[[File:OoT Master Sword Power to Repel Evil.png|thumb|195x195px|The Master Sword in full power, in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', during the final battle against Ganon, the Master Sword can be seen glowing with the power to repel evil, just before the [[Hero of Time]] must deliver the final blow onto the beast.

==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:Halfpower.png|right|thumb|200px|Link and [[Medli]] restoring some of the power to repel evil in {{TWW|-}}]]
In {{TWW|-}}, it is revealed that at some unknown point, the power to repel evil became dependent on the prayers of the [[Sage#The Wind Waker|Earth and Wind Sages]], which continue to keep the Goddess' power within the Master Sword.<ref>{{Cite|I had been praying here so that the power to repel evil would continue to be contained within the blade of the Master Sword...|Fado (The Wind Waker)|TWW}}</ref> These prayers are silenced when [[Laruto|these]] [[Fado (The Wind Waker)|sages]] are killed by Ganondorf,<ref>{{Cite|The fact that the Master Sword lost the power to repel evil suggests to me that something has happened to the sages who infused the blade with the gods’ power.|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref> forcing Link to seek out [[Medli|their]] [[Makar|successors]] to restore the power.<ref>{{Cite|In that case, look for the one who has the same instrument that I hold, and teach him the song you just played. Once you do, I’m certain he holder of the instrument will feel the blood of the sages awaken within his veins.|Fado (The Wind Waker)|TWW}}</ref> After awakening the new Wind Sage, Makar, the power to repel evil is restored.<ref>{{Cite|The Master Sword has returned! Its blade once again houses the power to repel evil! The sword of the one true hero is revived at last!|N/A|TWW}}</ref>

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{TMC}} and {{FS}}, the [[Four Sword]] is shown to repel evil influences, but it is not until {{FSA|-}} that this power is called such.<ref>{{Cite|Your swords are filled with the power to repel evil!|N/A|FSA}}</ref> In this same game, it is revealed that the sword initially lost this power due to containing [[Vaati]] and his evil magic for so long; it is also revealed that this power comes from [[Force Gems]]. In each level, the Links must gather 2000 Force Gems to restore the power to repel evil. This power allows the Links to kill Vaati and the various enemies throughout the game, as well as to defeat Ganon.

==={{TP|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', [[Zant]] casts a spell on Link, keeping him trapped in his [[Wolf Link|wolf form]].<ref>{{Cite|What binds him is a different magic than what transformed him when he first passed the curtain of twilight. It is an evil power.|Princess Zelda|TP}}</ref> [[Princess Zelda]] is quick to realize that only the Master Sword can break through Zant's curse<ref>{{Cite|The Master Sword is a sacred blade that evil can never touch. Evil cloaks you like a dark veil… and that blade is the only thing that can cleave it.|Princess Zelda|TP}}</ref> and return Link to normal, and instructs Link to find it. Once he does so, the power to repel evil pours out of the blade, driving out the curse and condensing it into a [[Shadow Crystal]].<ref>{{Cite|This thing is the embodiment of the evil magic that Zant cast on you.|Midna|TP}}</ref> [[Midna]] remarks that the item is different compared to the usual shadow magic of the [[Twili]],<ref>{{Cite|It’s definitely different from our tribe’s shadow magic…|Midna|TP}}</ref> before deciding to keep it. By using the alternating effects of the evil magic and the Master Sword's power to repel it, Link gains the ability to transform and warp at will.<ref>{{Cite|But on the other hand… if we kept it, you’d be able to transform into a beast any time you wanted…|Midna|TP}}</ref> During Link's journey through the [[Twilight Realm]], the Master Sword is imbued with the energy from the [[Sol]] orbs.

==={{SS|-}}===
The Power to Repel Evil beings was first displayed during the creation of the Master Sword, which occurred during the events of {{SS|-}}. Originally known as the [[Goddess Sword]],<ref>{{Cite|You got the Goddess Sword! This mysterious sword is bathed in divine light.|N/A|SS}}</ref> the weapon possessed a minor version of this power, and was used by the goddess [[Hylia]] in her fight with the Demon King, [[Demise]]. However, when Link later finds himself in need of a [[Gate of Time]], his Sword is unable to activate it until he forges it in the three [[Sacred Flames]]. The flames of [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]] and [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]] improve the physical strength of the blade and its [[Dowsing]] capabilities, respectively, but it is [[Din (Goddess)|Din]]'s flame that imbues his sword with "a sacred white light that demons revile,"<ref>{{Cite|The flames of Din have imbued your blade with a sacred white light that demons revile.|N/A|SS}}</ref> a form of the power to repel evil, and at the same time transforms it into the legendary Master Sword.<ref>{{Cite|Master, now that it has been tempered by the last of the sacred flames, your blade has finally revealed its true form. You now hold the Master Sword. With this sword’s great power, you can awaken the Gate of Time within the Sealed Temple.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

Shortly thereafter, Link uses his new blade to activate the Gate of Time and travels to the past. There, after explaining her history and plan, [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Princess Zelda]] blesses the sword, turning it into the [[True Master Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|The goddess has blessed your blade, and now the Master Sword has at last achieved its ultimate form! The sword is now imbued with the mythical power to drive back demons, and only Link may wield it!|N/A|SS}}</ref> This transformation augments the power granted to the blade by [[Din's Flame]], causing it to actually shine with that power.

It is also revealed that the Master Sword also wields the power to ''absorb'' evil. Upon the defeat of [[Demise]], the blade's mystical qualities not only slay him, but he is completely dissolved. His residual consciousness is absorbed into the blade, where the power keeps him trapped long enough for him to decay within the sword.<ref>{{Cite|I have confirmed the eradication of the demon king. His residual consciousness has been absorbed into the Master Sword… and is now sealed away.|Fi|SS}}</ref> However, this is only so far seen in this game.
==Other Sources==
Although the [[Master Sword|Master]] and [[Four Sword]] are the clearest, most versatile examples of the power to repel evil, this power seems to be displayed by other aspects of the ''Zelda'' universe as well, such as:
*[[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' and ''A Link to the Past'' are able to obliterate [[Ganon]] and instantly destroy wicked creatures in the latter, therefore clearly having the power to repel evil arguably surpassing that of the Master/Four Sword.
*The [[Magical Sword]] in ''The Legend of Zelda'' and ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'' may have the power to repel evil, as Ganon was stunned by this weapon after only a few strikes in the former and [[Dark Link]] was defeated by it in the latter.
*The [[Kokiri Forest]], and in particular the [[Great Deku Tree]] and [[Great Deku Tree#Deku Tree Sprout|his child]] who preside over it, are shown to keep out evil creatures and influences. The forest had virtually no monsters originally and served as a safe home for the [[Kokiri]] prior to the death of the Great Deku Tree; some time after his death, it became filled with monsters; and upon the growth of the Deku Tree Sprout, they disappeared once more.
*The Six Sages in ''Ocarina of Time'' appear to have a limited form of this power, being able to keep [[Ganondorf]]'s influence out of the [[Chamber of Sages]] despite his takeover of the [[Sacred Realm]], and later being able to bind his power and seal him away upon his defeat. The [[Maiden]]s in ''A Link to the Past'' descended from them also seem to have a limited form of this power, being able break the barrier on [[Ganon's Tower (A Link to the Past)|Ganon's Tower]] (in the Downfall Timeline) and seal Ganon within the [[Four Sword]] (in the Child Timeline).
*The [[Sacred Shield]] and its upgrades in {{SS|-}} are shown to be able to repel certain curses and dark influences, such as [[Cursed Bokoblin]]s.
*[[Light Arrow]]s are able to actually pierce evil itself and burn the essences of those they pierce.
*The [[Mirror Shield]] may have the power to repel evil, as it is shown to be able to repel attacks by [[Twinrova]] (in its {{OoT|-}} incarnation) and nullify certain attacks by Ganondorf and others; although, this may be due to its reflective properties as a mirror, rather than the actual power to repel evil.
*The [[Great Fairy's Sword]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' is suggested to possess some form of the power to repel evil, as it is known to be blessed by the [[Great Fairy]]. 
*The [[Phantom Sword|Phantom]] and [[Lokomo Sword|Lokomo]] swords are both imbued with sacred energy and are the only weapons (outside of the [[Bow of Light]] and weapons enhanced by [[Tears of Light]]) than can kill and stun [[Phantom]]s, respectively.
*The [[Sol]] orbs seen in {{TP|-}} seem to have some form of this power, as they are able to stave off and reverse the corrupting influences of the Twilight, making them essential for the resident [[Twili]].

==Trivia==
*Due to the Power to Repel Evil's important role in most games within [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] and is highly prominent on the [[Master Sword]] during cutscenes, it has led some fans to believe that the Blade of Evil's Bane is [[Link]]'s absolute strongest weapon. However, this is not technically true, as in gameplay, certain weapons in multiple installments and even ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', [[Weapon Strength|surpass the Master Sword in raw strength]] in spite of lacking the special ability. The most famous examples of this are the [[Biggoron's Sword]], [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]], [[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]], etc., and the sacred blade itself in [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|rare]] [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|cases]] can be [[Upgrading|upgraded]] to be [[Master Sword Lv2|more]] [[Master Sword Lv3|potent]] than it usually is.
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', the Master Sword's power to repel evil was only infused into the blade when the [[Medli|Earth Sage]] and the [[Makar|Wind Sage]] pray to the gods for its continuous power by playing the [[Earth God's Lyric]] and [[Wind God's Aria]], respectively. This does not apply to other games.
*It is unknown whether the [[Triforce]]'s power, which plays an integral role in creating the [[True Master Sword]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'', holds any bearing on the sword's power to repel evil. As is constantly shown, evil beings have no difficulties in using the Triforce, and the golden relic's connection to the Gods is shown to be completely impartial to those who use and wish upon it.[[File:TWW Ganondorf warning Link about the Power to Repel Evil.png|thumb|259x259px|Link warned by Ganondorf that he is unable to defeat his archenemy without the Power to Repel Evil in ''The Wind Waker'']]
*[[Ganon]], as seen in a few games, is shown to be aware of his critical weakness against the power to repel evil. As [[Ganondorf]], in ''The Wind Waker'', he warns Link the Hero of Winds that it is impossible to defeat him with an unawakened Master Sword (or any other regular blade),<ref name=":0" /> while in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', he expressed genuine surprise when his deadly [[Gloom]] [[Decayed Master Sword|shatters the legendary blade]] during Link's attempt to protect [[Princess Zelda]].
*In ''The Wind Waker'', the double damage ability from the [[Elixir Soup]] resembles the power to repel evil, as Link's [[Sword]] gains an orange glow. This occurs even if the Master Sword already restores its power.
*Although unrelated to ''The Legend of Zelda'' franchise or other [[Nintendo]] games, in ''SoulCalibur II'', when defeating Inferno, Link encounters a unique sword known as [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Calibur Soul Calibur], the titular weapon. Much like the Master Sword and Four Sword, this blade technically has the power to repel evil, as only heroes with a pure heart can wield it and the weapon has battled and defeated the evil [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Edge Soul Edge] many times over ages. Strangely, Link himself doesn't have an unlockable weapon based on it.
*Due to the blades glowing with powerful energy, some fans have claimed the Four Sword and especially Master Sword being similar to the famous [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber Lightsaber] weapon from the ''[https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars Star Wars]'' franchise when they explicitly possess the power to repel evil.
**Coincidentally, in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], an item called the Beam Sword has a striking resemblance to the Lightsaber.
