{{Term/Store|Watcher|Watchers|Series, SS|plural}} are a variety of {{Term|SSHD|Enemy|link}} in {{SS}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Watcher final version|book=HH|page=58}}</ref> consisting of {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher|link}} and {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher|link}}. Along with the {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Guardian|link}}, they protect and patrol the {{Term|SSHD|Silent Realm|link}}. 

==Characteristics==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>{{Color|SS Red|Two types of Watchers}} patrol the realm, looking for intruders. I highly suggest you take caution.</p><p>So long as you do not step into their light, you will remain undetected by {{Color|SS Red|flying Sky Watchers}}.</p><p>However, the {{Color|SS Red|Earth Watchers}}, who {{Color|SS Red|hover near the ground}}, will give chase if you come too close to them, so {{Color|SS Red|stay alert}} and maintain a safe distance.</p>
}}

{{Plural|SSHD|Watcher}}, along with {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Guardian|link}}, patrol the {{Term|SSHD|Silent Realm|link}} from outside intruders. Unlike the {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Guardian}}, the {{Plural|SSHD|Watcher}} will not actively hunt {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} and are not affected by the {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear|link}}, and thus will not be stunned by its power. The {{Plural|SSHD|Watcher}} patrol the Realm in a set path by shining a beam of light produced by the lanterns they carry. If {{Term|SSHD|Link}} passes through this light, the {{Term|SSHD|Watcher}} will emit a loud shriek that will awaken and alert the {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Guardian}} to {{Term|SSHD|Link}}'s location, canceling the effects of the {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear}} no matter how much time {{Term|SSHD|Link}} has remaining. 

{{Plural|SSHD|Watcher}} come in two similar variations: {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher|link}} and {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher|link}}. {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher}} patrol the air with a light focused on the ground in a fixed route. They are relatively easy to avoid, though they can be difficult in narrow areas or when in large groups, such as in the {{Term|SSHD|Pumpkin|link}} patch of {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft|link}}'s {{Term|SSHD|Silent Realm}}, where four of them encircle a solitary {{Term|SSHD|Sacred Tear}}.

{{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} hover near the ground and will chase {{Term|SSHD|Link}} if he comes too close in range to them; however, the young hero can outrun them by dashing. {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} give away their position in ways that give {{Term|SSHD|Link}} warning so he is not caught off guard. A visual cue is that the {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} have dimmer, larger light radii, and it is safe for Link to be caught in this light, as long as their brighter direct light does not touch him. They also have an audible cue, as {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} will give off a jingling sound upon noticing {{Term|SSHD|Link}}.
