{{Term/Store|Anti-Flame Training: II||HWAoC}} is a level 26 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Flame Training: II|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Flame Training: II}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Spicy Pepper|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Warm Safflina|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Warm Darner|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat three {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} within five minutes, although only one is seen at first. After the first one is killed, the other two spawn. When all three are defeated, the timer resets to three minutes, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Hinox}} spawns, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}} spawn along the path leading to the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Hinox}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Hinox}} is defeated.
