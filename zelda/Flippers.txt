The {{Term/Store|Flippers||Series, LA, LADX, LANS, TMC}},{{Exp Game|LA, TMC}}<ref name="E 123"/>{{Name Ref|LANS}} also known as the {{Term/Store|Zora's Flippers|Zora's Flippers|ALttP, OoS, OoA, ALBW, CoH}},{{Exp Game|ALttP, OoS, OoA, ALBW, CoH}}<ref name="E 123"/><ref name= "CoH Obtain">{{Cite|You got the {{Color|CoH Yellow|Zora's Flippers}}! These will let you swim through deep water, like in {{Color|CoH Blue|Lake Hylia}}!|N/A|CoH}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Zora's Flippers|These Flippers belong to Zora the man-fish, whose domain is the wet blue yonder. They are a necessity — you cannot swim without them (If you try swimming without the Flippers you will be returned to land). To get through this adventure, you must get your feet wet, so find the Flippers as soon as possible.}}
{{Manual|ALttP&FS|Zora's Flippers|The flippers allow the wearer to swim in the water.}}
[[File:ALttP Zora's Flippers Icon.png|36px]]
The Zora's Flippers can be bought from [[King Zora (A Link to the Past)|King Zora]] for 500 [[Rupee]]s at the [[Zora's Waterfall]] in {{ALttP}}. They allow [[Link]] to swim in deep water and to use [[Whirlpool Waterway]]s to warp between bodies of water. In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version, Link is able to [[Diving|dive]] underwater while swimming. This can be used to avoid enemy attacks and certain traps.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Flippers|If you acquire the Flippers, you'll be able to swim! When in the water, press the A Button to swim and the B Button to dive.}}
The Flippers are obtained in the [[Angler's Tunnel]] in {{LA}}. They allow Link to swim and dive underwater by pressing the B button,<ref>{{Cite|You've got the Flippers! If you press the B Button while you swim, you can dive underwater!|N/A|LADX}}</ref> which is required to enter the [[Catfish's Maw]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 58}}</ref> By diving underwater, Link can avoid attacks from enemies while swimming.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Zora's Flippers|You'll be able to swim after you get the Zora's Flipers. Use the + Control Pad to swim and the B Button to dive underwater. Press the A Button repeatedly {{Typo|able|to be able}} to swim faster.}}
The Zora's Flippers are received from the [[Master Diver]] at the [[Sunken City]] if Link retrieves the [[Master's Plaque]] for him in {{OoS}}. With the Zora's Flippers, Link can dive for a short period of time. If the [[Zora Ring]] is worn, he can dive for an infinite length of time.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Zora's Flippers|You'll be able to swim after you get the Zora's Flipers. Use the + Control Pad to swim and the B Button to dive underwater. Press the A Button repeatedly to swim faster.}}
The Zora's Flippers are found in [[Cheval's Tomb]] in the [[Yoll Graveyard]] in {{OoA|-}}. They are used to pass [[Cheval]]'s tests in order to get the [[Cheval Rope]]. Link can later find the [[Mermaid Suit]], an upgrade to the Flippers. With the Zora's Flippers, Link can dive for a short period of time. If the [[Zora Ring]] is worn, he can dive for an infinite length of time.

==={{TMC|-}}===
The {{Term|TMC|Flippers}} can be found in a sewer underneath the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}} after returning all the missing books in {{TMC}}. With the {{Term|TMC|Flippers}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} is able to gain entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
Link receives the Zora's Flippers from [[Oren]] at [[Zora's Domain]] after returning the [[Smooth Gem]] stolen by the [[Shady Guy]] in {{ALBW}}. They allow Link to swim and dive in deep water.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Flippers|Flippers that resemble Zora fins. You can swim faster while wearing them.<ref name="Flippers">{{Cite|Flippers that resemble Zora fins. You can swim faster while wearing them.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Flippers can be bought at the [[River Zora Shop]] for 350 [[Rupee|Rupees]]. Equipping them increases [[Princess Zelda]]’s swim speed.

==Other Appearances==
{{Ambig|Start}}
==={{AST|-}}===
In {{AST}}, the Zora's Flippers can be bought from King Zora for 300 Rupees. If the [[Hero of Light]] talks to King Zora during a raining period, he will sell the Flippers for 150 Rupees instead. They allow the hero to swim in deep water, which is required to enter Level 2.

==={{CoH|-}}===
In {{CoH}}, the Zora's Flippers are received from the {{Term|CoH|Prince|link}} after waking him up.<ref>{{Cite|Here, take these {{Color|CoH Yellow|flippers}} as thanks!|Prince|CoH}}</ref> They allow the hero to swim in deep water like {{Term|CoH|Lake Hylia|link}}, allowing easier access to the {{Term|CoH|Frozen Grotto|link}} and general mobility around the map.<ref name="CoH Obtain"/>
