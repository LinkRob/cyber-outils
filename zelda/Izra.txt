{{Term/Store|Izra||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Izra|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Izra}} is a worker at {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}}. At night, he dozes near the center of the {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|display=Stable}}, though he denies falling asleep.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm? Ah... I wasn't sleeping. I wasn't...|Izra|BotW}}</ref> During the {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, he flees inside the {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|display=Stable}}, and he laments that the men visiting will track mud all over.<ref>{{Cite|I hope the sun comes out soon... I bet all the men coming into the stable will track dirt in, making a filthy place even worse...|Izra|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives at {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable}}, he will find {{Term|BotW|Izra}} investigating the banks of {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... How can I...|Izra|BotW}}</ref> Since he is afraid of deep {{Term|BotW|Water|link}}, he mutters to himself that he is unsure what to do.<ref>{{Cite|What should I do? I'm terrified of deep water...|Izra|BotW}}</ref> However, when he notices {{Term|BotW|Link}} listening to him, he apologizes for being lost in thought.<ref>{{Cite|...Hmm?|Izra|BotW}}<br>{{Cite|Oh... Sorry. I was lost in thought.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks, {{Term|BotW|Izra}} explains that he can see a shape at the bottom of the {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|display=River}} that he believes to be a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}.<ref name="Lost">{{Cite|[Lost in thought?] I can't believe I'm about to share this with you, but I'm absolutely stumped. Have you seen that strange shape down at the bottom of the river? It's hard to make out, but I'm pretty sure it's a treasure chest. But I can't figure out how to fish it out of there. I guess I need...some kind of machine or something to get it out.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, he has no way to reach the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}},<ref name="Lost"/> but he is so curious about it that he has been unable to sleep.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so curious about what's inside that chest, I can't even sleep.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> After he finishes speaking, the "{{Term|BotW|Riverbed Reward|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can pull the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} out of {{Term|BotW|Hylia River}} using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, much to {{Term|BotW|Izra}}'s surprise.<ref>{{Cite|What the heck?!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him before opening {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}, {{Term|BotW|Izra}} will be amazed and urges him to open it.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! The treasure chest!|Izra|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|INCREDIBLE! I can't believe you managed to get that treasure chest out of there! Hurry up and open it already!|Izra|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whoa, buddy! I don't know who you are or what kind of power you have...but it's awesome! Like...REALLY AWESOME! Now hurry up and open that chest!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} does so, he asks what he found.<ref>{{Cite|And...yeah! What was inside the treasure chest?|Izra|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells him that it was treasure, {{Term|BotW|Izra}} will be disappointed, as he could have become rich and attracted many women.<ref>{{Cite|[It was treasure.] Amazing treasure?! Ahh! That's so frustrating!|Izra|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|If I had just managed to get that chest, I'd be rich! And the ladies would be tripping over each other to be with me!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells him it was a {{Term|BotW|Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Izra}} will brush it off, since it was not what he wanted.<ref>{{Cite|[It was a sword.] What? A cool sword, you say?|Izra|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Whatever. I didn't even want that life-changing treasure anyway...|Izra|BotW}}</ref> Ultimately, {{Term|BotW|Izra}} will be able to relax now that he knows the contents of the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}},<ref>{{Cite|Well, I suppose you did all of the hard work with that chest. At least I can sleep now.|Izra|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well, at least I can relax now that I know what was inside. Thanks.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

During the day, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Izra}} searching the {{Plural|BotW|Wagon|link}} and boxes at {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable}} for anything useful that has been left out.<ref>{{Cite|Don't get the wrong idea! I'm not slacking off. I'm just checking to see if anything useful is lying around!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> He suggests that one could investigate the destroyed {{Plural|BotW|Wagon}} on the roads to find something useful.<ref>{{Cite|There could be something useful just... abandoned in busted wagons on the main roads, even.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> He also tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to ask {{Term|BotW|Ami|link}} for information about the area, remarking that he enjoys having a younger brother who can pull his weight.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to learn about the area, you should go talk to Ami. It's so nice to have a little brother who can really pull his weight.|Izra|BotW}}</ref>
