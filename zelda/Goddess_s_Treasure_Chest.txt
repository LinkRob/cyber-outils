{{Term/Store|Goddess's Treasure Chest|Goddess's Treasure Chests|Series, SS|plural}} are objects in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS |publisher= Prima |edition= Collector's Edition |year= 2013 |page= 26}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
Goddess's Treasure Chests are special [[Treasure Chest]]s found only on various islands in the [[Sky]]. Unlike regular Treasure Chests, these are violet in color when activated; before activation, Goddess's Treasure Chests appear gray and cannot be opened.

Each Goddess's Treasure Chest has its own corresponding [[Goddess Cube]] found on the [[Surface]]. A Goddess's Treasure Chest cannot be opened until its corresponding Goddess Cube has been struck with a [[Skyward Strike]]. Upon doing so, the Goddess Cube will send a beam of light to the Sky, indicating that its Goddess's Treasure Chest has been activated. When [[Link]] returns to the Sky, he can check his [[Map]] to see that [[Fi]] has marked the location of the activated Goddess's Treasure Chest on his Map. Link can then fly to the [[Island]] that holds the Goddess's Treasure Chest and take claim of the contents.

Goddess's Treasure Chests contain various rare treasures such as [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]], [[Medal]]s, [[Adventure Pouch]] slots, [[Gold Rupee]]s and [[Silver Rupee]]s, [[Quiver]]s, [[Bomb Bag]]s, and [[Seed Satchel]]s.
