'''Constructs''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] and characters in {{TotK}}.

==Characteristics==
Constructs are mechanical creatures created by the [[Zonai]] that resurfaced following the [[The Upheaval|Upheaval]], an event that shook Hyrule. These constructs can be found in various locations, including the [[Sky Islands]], the surface of [[Hyrule]], and the [[The Depths|Depths]].
