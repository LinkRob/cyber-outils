The {{Name|Fan|Dragon}} is an enemy appearing in "[[The Ringer]]" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}.{{Name Ref}}

When an amateur magician makes a lizard appear on Zelda's hand during the [[Magician's Contest]], [[Ganon]] uses his magic to turn it into a huge dragon that promptly turns towards the princess, causing her to scream in fear. [[Link]] immediately rushes down from his room to help Zelda, and although the young hero tries to defend himself from the dragon's fire by using his [[Magical Shield]], the flame is too strong for him to be able to [[Sword Beam|zap]] the dragon.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Whoa! Toasty! [...] Can't...get a clear zap!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> Zelda, who had managed to run away, then throws a nearby plate in the direction of Link,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Link! Get ready!|name=Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> and the young hero fires several [[Sword Beam|sword beams]] at the plate which causes them to bounce back at the dragon, promptly defeating the monster. However, Zelda and Link soon learn that the dragon was just a diversion since Ganon had managed to steal the [[Triforce of Wisdom]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hah! The Triforce of Wisdom! Mine, at last!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref>
