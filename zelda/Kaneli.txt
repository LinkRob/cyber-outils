{{Term/Store|Kaneli||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kaneli|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} is the chief of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. He once was a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} warrior and {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}}'s mentor.<ref name="CaC"/> He resides in his {{Term|BotW|Kaneli's House|link|display=House}} at the top of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}}, he will take note of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} that he is carrying.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, a visitor. Welcome to... ... Oh. OH! That object on your hip... Could it be...|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> He introduces himself to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and asks if he truly has a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|Forgive me. Where are my manners? I am Kaneli, elder of Rito Village. Now then...is that not a {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} dangling from your hip?|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms it is, {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} will reason that he must be a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} like {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}}, and therefore capable of boarding {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[That's right.] Oh, my! So it is true! That means you must be a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} like Master Revali—one of the few able to board {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}!|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> However, realizing that it has been 100 years since the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} died, he surmises that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must instead be one of their descendants.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|No... What am I thinking? The}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Champions}} {{Color|BotW Gray|have all been dead for 100 years. This boy must be a mere descendant...an inheritor of the}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}{{Color|BotW Gray|.}}}}|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, he still asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to listen to his request.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, forgive me... {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} descendant... If you would listen to this old man's request, you would have my eternal gratitude.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to hear him out, {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} will be grateful.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll listen.] Thank you. Truly.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} has the blood of a {{Term|BotW|Champions}}, {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} asks him to deal with their issues with {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now that I know you have the blood of a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}}, there is something I must ask of you. How shall I put this... I would like you to deal with {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}. The giant beast circling above us.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> Only {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} are capable of stopping the rampage of a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Only a chosen one, a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}}, can stop a {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}. You must enter the beast and bend it to your will.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> This information was lost on {{Term|BotW|Teba}} and {{Term|BotW|Harth|link}}, who went to confront {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|display=Medoh}} on their own and were defeated.<ref>{{Cite|I tried explaining this to the more headstrong Rito warriors, but they wouldn't listen to reason. {{Color|BotW Blue|Teba}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Harth}} tried to confront {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}, but it did not go well, and {{Color|BotW Blue|Harth}} was injured.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Teba}} is unharmed, {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} fears that he is planning another attack on the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|Teba escaped unscathed, but I fear he now plans to face {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}} alone.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to seek out {{Term|BotW|Teba}} and try to stop {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} together.<ref>{{Cite|As a descendant of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champions}}, perhaps you can help us. Please. Find Teba. If you work together, you may be able to stop {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> After this conversation, the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} will begin.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Revali}}, {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} explains that he was a warrior from {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} who died 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|[Who's Master Revali?] Master Revali was a fierce warrior from Rito Village who died 100 years ago.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> Stories claim that {{Term|BotW|Revali}} was able to fly as though he were the {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}}, and he was capable of wielding a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} twice his size.<ref>{{Cite|According to the stories, he flew like the wind itself and wielded a bow twice his own size with ease.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} recognized {{Term|BotW|Revali}}'s skill and appointed him pilot of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.<ref>{{Cite|His skill was recognized by {{Color|BotW Blue|King Rhoam}}, and he was entrusted to control {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, {{Term|BotW|Revali}} fell during the fight against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Master Revali lost his life while piloting the Divine Beast during the battle with {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}}.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} wonders what could have been capable of killing such a strong warrior.<ref>{{Cite|What could have happened inside {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} 100 years ago to kill such a celebrated warrior?|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref>

Finally, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} about {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}. He will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he noticed the giant bird in the sky, and he explains that this is {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Medoh?] Did you perchance see that {{Color|BotW Blue|giant bird}} up there? I must reward you properly!|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> He implores {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take the contents of the nearby {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} for himself, proclaiming he will get more use out of it.<ref>{{Cite|Feel free to take what you find in that treasure chest.
