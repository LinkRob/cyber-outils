{{Plural|Series|Flameblade}} are magical swords that were forged in the lava of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} with elemental properties.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> When they are glowing red, they will emit {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} when swung.<ref name="Inventory"/> Equipping one will provide {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}. They are often used by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. When used against {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}-related {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ice Keese|link}}, they will be defeated with a single strike.

Though their strength comes from their {{Term|BotW|Fire}} damage, {{Plural|BotW|Flameblade}} are still made of metal. Therefore, they can attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. However, they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. They also sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

A single Flameblade may be obtained especially-early by locating the trove of [[Misko, the Great Bandit]]; it's buried within a rock pile, behind all their other treasures. Flameblades are also a potential drop of [[Stalnox|Stalnoxes]], like other Magic Gear.

The reason for the setting is unknown in Tears of the Kingdom, but it was deleted due to [[Fuse]] as a factor outside of the setting
