'''Ignizol''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Ignizol|A white Zol with a flame on top of it. Anything it touches is set ablaze.<ref name="EoW Ignizol">{{Cite|A white Zol with a flame on top of it. Anything it touches is set ablaze.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Ignizols are white variants of [[Zol|Zols]] that have a flame on top, giving them a candle-like appearance. They are known for their ability to spread fire wherever they go. Ignizols have a cost of two when summoned as a [[Echo]].

They have counterparts in the [[Hydrozol]], and to a lesser extent the [[Buzz Blob]]. A [[Dark Echo|Dark variant]] can be encountered after coming in contact with a [[Tecuum]].
