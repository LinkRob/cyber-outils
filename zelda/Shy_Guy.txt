{{Term/Store|Shy Guy|Shy Guys|Series, LANS|plural}}, also named {{Term/Store|Mask-Mimic|Mask-Mimics|LA, LADX|plural}} and '''Mask Mimics''', are enemies in {{LA}}. They are the same species as the [[mariowiki:Shy Guy|Shy Guys]] from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]].

==Characteristics==
Shy Guys are short creature that always wear a mask obscuring their face. In ''Mario'' games, Shy Guys wears robes of varying colors, usually red, which is also the color used by them in the reissues {{LA|DX}} and {{LA|NS}}. 

In {{LA|-}} only, Shy Guys are the behavioral counterparts of [[Arm-Mimic]]s, in that they mirror [[Link]]'s movement in the opposite direction; when Link moves up, they move down, and when Link moves right, they move left, and vice versa. Shy Guys are invulnerable to [[Sword]] attacks because of the mask covering their face, but their backsides are still vulnerable to attack. Link cannot face their backside as a result of the enemy mirroring his every movement, they can only be attacked by charging and releasing a [[Spin Attack]] when their backs are turned against each other. Alternately, they can also be defeated by either throwing [[Bomb]]s, shooting two [[Arrow]]s at them, the [[Magic Rod]], or the [[Boomerang]]. While Shy Guys can be defeated by dashing into them with the Sword and [[Pegasus Boots]].

==Mainline appearances==
==={{LA|-}}===
Shy Guys are encountered by Link in two [[dungeon]]s, [[Bottle Grotto]] and [[Face Shrine]].
