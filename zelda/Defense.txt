It is the measurement of the amount of damage [[Link]] takes directly from [[Enemy|enemies]]. The various ways to acquire enhanced defense utilize differing methods in each game. Although obtaining defensive [[Upgrading|upgrades]], much like having optional stronger [[Sword|Swords]] or [[Shield|Shields]], are not technically required to complete a game, it is rather advised, as the hero often faces dangerous foes late in his quest that can inflict high damage otherwise, especially by [[Boss|bosses]] including [[Ganon]].

==Appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, Link can acquire two rings that enhance his defensive abilities:
<br/>[[File:TLoZ Blue Ring Sprite.png|14px]] The [[Blue Ring]] reduces damage Link takes in half and turns his tunic into a whitish blue. Link can find it beneath an [[Armos]] in the overworld and buy it from the [[Merchant]] for 250 [[Rupee]]s. In the [[Second Quest]], this item is found at the northeastern portion of [[Hyrule]].
<br/>[[File:TLoZ Red Ring Sprite.png|14px]] The [[Red Ring]] reduces damage to a quarter of its original amount and turns Link's tunic red. It is found in [[Level-9]] on both Quests. 

Notably, when obtaining the Blue or Red Ring, due to the graphical limitations of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]], certain characters, items, and even enemies that uses the same color palette as the [[Green Clothes]] will also change correspondingly. 

==={{TAoL|-}}===
[[File:TAoL Link Shield Sprite.png|20px|left]] In {{TAoL}}, Link can use the [[Shield Spell]] to enhance his defense, reducing the damage he takes by half. Link's tunic turns red, and the effect of the spell will wear off as soon as he leaves to another area. It can be found in [[Rauru Town]]. In addition, every time Link's life level goes up via experience points, it will decrease the damage he takes permanently.
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, Link's defense is enhanced by his tunics. 

[[File:BlueMail Sprite.png|left]]
The [[Blue Mail]] reduces the damage taken from enemies by 1/2. It is found in the [[Ice Palace]].

[[File:RedMail Sprite.png|left]]
The [[Red Mail]] reduces the damage taken from enemies by 1/4.  It is found in [[Ganon's Tower (A Link to the Past)|Ganon's Tower]].
==={{LA|-}}===
[[File:LA Guardian Acorn Sprite.png|left|21x21px|Guardian Acorn]]
In {{LA}}, once Link defeats twelve enemies without being hit, the twelfth will drop a [[Guardian Acorn]], which temporarily doubles Link's defense.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 89}}</ref>
==={{LA|DX|-}}===
[[File:LADX Blue Clothes Sprite.png|left]]
In {{LA|DX}}, after completing the [[Color Dungeon]], Link can choose either the [[Blue Clothes]], or the [[Red Clothes]] as a reward. The Blue Tunic doubles his defense, while the Red Tunic, though not a defense upgrade, doubles Link's attack power. These abilities can be further increased - though only temporarily - by obtaining a Guardian Acorn or [[Piece of Power]], respectively.
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, enhanced defense is awarded to Link by the [[Great Fairy#Great Fairy of Courage|Great Fairy of Courage]] who lives in a hidden cave by [[Ganon's Castle]] in when he becomes the [[Hero of Time]]. The entrance is blocked by a large granite rock pillar which can only be removed by the use of the [[Golden Gauntlets]]. The outline of his [[Life Gauge]] becomes white and damage is reduced to half.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, if Link is able to collect all 15 [[Stray Fairy|Stray Fairies]] in the [[Great Bay Temple]] and return them to the [[Great Fairy Fountain]], he is rewarded with enhanced defense, which functions the same as in {{OoT|-}}.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, if Link collects 10 or 20 [[Spirit Gems|Wisdom Gems]], he can use them to power up the [[Neri|Spirit of Wisdom]] which when equipped increases his defense.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, Link can purchase [[Guardian Potion]]s from the [[Potion Shop#Skyward Sword|Potion Shop]] at the [[Bazaar]]. This Potion, when drunk, halves the damage dealt to Link by half for three minutes. By upgrading it to the [[Guardian Potion +]], the Potion will make Link completely invincible for three minutes.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, like in {{ALttP|-}}, Link's defense is enhanced by his tunics. The Blue Mail is found in the [[Swamp Palace (A Link Between Worlds)|Swamp Palace]]. The Red Mail is found in [[Lorule Castle]].

== Trivia ==

* Defense upgrades are essential for [[100% Completion]].
* The Guardian Acorn from {{LA|-}} remains the only case in the core series where [[Link]] gains increased defense while wearing his traditional [[Green Clothes|Green Tunic]].
* Despite the [[Goron Tunic]] in {{OoT|-}} adds resistance from fire-based damage or walking on poison water for Link, it does not affect physical damage from enemies. The [[Zora Armor|Zora Tunic]] also simply allows him to breathe underwater, and therefore does not grant any extra defense.
* Defense upgrades in the 2D games do not affect fixed damage from certain hazards, traps, or even enemies/bosses such as the [[Blade Trap]], lasers from a [[Beamos]], [[Flying Tile|Flying Tiles]], [[Chain Chomp|Chain Chomps]], etc.
* The character [[Gramps]] in {{aLBW}}, despite appearing to be wearing Link's standard outfit, uses the Red Mail in his optional battle against the hero, but it is not visible in gameplay.[[File:SCII Link in Goron Tunic.webp|thumb|239x239px|Link wearing the Red Mail/Goron Tunic costume in ''SoulCalibur II'']]
* In the crossover fighting games ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' and ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', although Link can wear tunics that would technically give him increased resistance or special protection against certain hazards in the core series, they serve no such purpose, as they are instead used as alternate costumes.
* The [[Links]] in the ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords|Four]] [[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Sword]]'' games, despite most of them wearing non-green tunics, they do not offer any improved defense in gameplay.
* The [[Armor]] mechanic in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'' serves a similar purpose as Defense.
