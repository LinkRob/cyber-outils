==Biography==
{{Term|ALBW|Tallo}} is a baby {{Term|ALBW|Turtle|link}} who resides in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake|link}} with his {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle|link}} and his brothers, {{Term|ALBW|Jillo|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Sabro|link}}. He is the largest of the three brothers, and has a tendency to get stuck in strange places.<ref name="stuck"/>

When {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} first arrives at {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake}}, he can encounter {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle}}, who pleads for his help in finding her lost babies.<ref>{{Cite|Oh no! I got separated from my little turtles. You have to help me look for them—you just have to!|Mama Turtle|ALBW}}</ref> {{Term|ALBW|Tallo}} can be found stuck to a stone face high above the north end of the lake. To rescue him, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must [[Wall Merging|Wall Merge]] from the nearby land, position himself under {{Term|ALBW|Tallo}}'s belly and then emerge, propelling him off the wall and into the lake. {{Term|ALBW|Tallo}} thanks {{Term|ALBW|Link}} for the help and swims off to rejoin his mother.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, thank you, thank you, thank you! That wall was so sticky! It wouldn't let me go!|Tallo|ALBW}}</ref> Once all three baby {{Plural|ALBW|Turtle}} have been rescued, they all climb onto {{Term|ALBW|Mama Turtle}}'s back, forming a stack that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can stand on to receive a ride around {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake}}, including access to {{Term|ALBW|Turtle Rock|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found all of my babies! Thank you so much! I'm sorry I don't have anything I can offer you as a reward. If you need a ride somewhere, you can go ahead and hop on, though!|Mama Turtle|ALBW}}</ref>
