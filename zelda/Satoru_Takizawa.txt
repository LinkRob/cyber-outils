'''Satoru Takizawa''' is an art director for [[Nintendo]].

==Biography==
{{Stub}}
Takizawa joined Nintendo in [[1995]]. In his early years at the company, worked as character and enemy designer on {{OoT}} and {{MM}}, respectively. Since {{TWW}}, he has directed the entire art style for a number of {{TLoZ|-}} series.
