The {{Term/Store|GBA Time Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|GBA Time Ring<br/>Life Advanced!|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoA}}

==Location and Uses==
The GBA Time Ring can be bought for 100 [[Rupee]]s at the [[Advance Shop]] in {{OoA}} only when playing on a [[Game Boy Advance]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. It has no effect when worn. The GBA Time Ring can be transferred to {{OoS}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
