"{{Term/Store|Curry for What Ails You||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Curry for What Ails You}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Rito Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Lester|link}}, who is talking about his need for {{Term|BotW|Curry Rice|link}} to himself.<ref>{{Cite|Need curry rice...|Lester|BotW}}</ref> When he speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Lester}} shares that he can not get enough spice,<ref>{{Cite|The thing about me is...I just can't get enough spice in my life.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> specifically requiring {{Term|BotW|Curry Rice}}.<ref>{{Cite|Spicyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy... I need that burny tingle... I need {{Color|BotW Blue|curry rice}}!|Lester|BotW}}</ref> While he is in possession of the {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}} needed to make the meal, he does not have any {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now, I have {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}} on hand, which I need to make {{Color|BotW Blue|curry rice}}, but I'm fresh out of {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}!|Lester|BotW}}</ref>

Unable to leave the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} on a whim,<ref>{{Cite|I can't just run off and leave the stable.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring him some {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}} if he ever has any.<ref>{{Cite|If you happen to get your hands on some {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}, can you give it to me?|Lester|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to help, {{Term|BotW|Lester}} thanks him.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure thing!] Thank you kindly, sonny boy.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> He shares that {{Term|BotW|Curry Rice}} warms him up when he eats it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Curry rice}} always warms me right up.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}} is sold at the {{Term|BotW|Goron Gusto Shop|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} {{Term|BotW|Yammo|link}} also sells {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has obtained some, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable}}. {{Term|BotW|Lester}} notices if it is in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} and asks to see it.<ref>{{Cite|What's that? You got your hands on some {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}?<br/>Can you show it to me?|Lester|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Lester}} confirms that it is indeed {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}} and asks to purchase it in exchange for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} all right! Hey... Could I ask you to part with that {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} for, say, {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}}?|Lester|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Lester}} remarks that he will now be able to make {{Term|BotW|Curry Rice}}.<ref>{{Cite|[It's all yours!] Thank you! With {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}}, I'll be able to make some {{Color|BotW Blue|curry rice}} to warm my soul.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> He gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} as payment.<ref>{{Cite|Here—your {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}}!|Lester|BotW}}</ref>

Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can continue to give {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}} to {{Term|BotW|Lester}} in exchange for {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you come across any more {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}, please bring it to me. Next time, I'll trade you some {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}} for it.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> He also tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he can make other types of curry by adding extra ingredients.<ref>{{Cite|If you add other ingredients to {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}}, you can make all kinds of different curry rice dishes. Try it next time you have some spare ingredients.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> Once he is done talking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
