{{Term/Store|Boneyard Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Boneyard Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Boneyard Bridge is located in eastern [[Hyrule Field]] where the [[Hyrule Castle Moat]] flows into the [[Hylia River]].

In [[Master Mode]], a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Royal Guard's Shield]] can be found atop a platform held aloft by [[Sky Octorok]]s beside the Bridge.

A [[Korok]] can be found by throwing a [[Rock]] into the circle of Rocks just east of the Bridge.
