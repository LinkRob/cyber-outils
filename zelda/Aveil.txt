{{Term/Store|Aveil||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a [[Gerudo]] character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> She is the [[Terminan]] counterpart of [[Gerudo Warrior|Nabooru's second-in-command]] from {{OoT}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|MM3D|Aveil}} is the leader of the [[Gerudo|Gerudo Pirates]]. She has a room deep inside the [[Pirates' Fortress|Pirate's Fortress]] that is filled with all kinds of treasures and luxuries that the {{Plural|MM3D|Gerudo Pirate|display=Pirates}} have stolen, including four out of the seven [[Zora Egg|Zora Eggs]] and the {{Term|MM3D|Hookshot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|See, it was long ago when I used to use this thing called a Hookshot to catch fish off the coast... But I was attacked by these pirates, and they took it away with them. They say... it's a legendary treasure.. And I just happened to swipe it from the bottom of the sea, so I hold no grudge against 'em.|Fisherman|MM}}</ref>

After infiltrating the {{Term|MM3D|Pirates' Fortress}}, [[Link]] overhears {{Term|MM3D|Aveil}} speaking about the {{Plural|MM3D|Zora Egg}} which they stole from {{Term|MM3D|Lulu|link}}, the lead singer of the popular [[The Indigo-Go's]] Zora band; where they are and why they stole them in the first place, as he learned from [[Mikau]].<ref>{{Cite|Silence! That's why the Zoras can't send for any help! Now that the eggs are gone, the Zoras should be frantically searching for them. If we don't hurry, the Zoras will get to them before we do! There are four eggs here now. Hurry! Go find the other three eggs before those sea snakes eat them!|Aveil|MM}}</ref> Apparently, the [[Skull Kid]] told them that the {{Plural|MM3D|Zora Egg}} were key elements in obtaining a luxurious treasure that should be lying in the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wait!!! The Zora eggs are the only clue we have about that dragon cloud floating over the bay... If what that strange, masked one says is true... And if we can get our hands on the treasure that lies sleeping in the temple in that dragon cloud... Then we can spend the rest of our lives living the good life! So get a move on and go find them! Now!!!|Aveil|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Aveil}} wants the {{Plural|MM3D|Zora Egg}} for their value as treasure, but also fears the harm done to their reputation if people would find out that they could not hold on to their loot.<ref>{{Cite|What are you trying to pull here?!? If people hear the great pirates have lost the treasure they stole, we'll become the laughing stock!|Aveil|MM}}</ref> In order to collect the {{Term|MM3D|Zora Egg|display=Eggs}}, the young [[Hero of Time]] must shoot a {{Term|MM3D|Beehive|link}} hanging on the ceiling of her room, causing the {{Plural|MM3D|Bee|link}} to come out and chase away {{Term|MM3D|Aveil}} and the other {{Plural|MM3D|Gerudo Pirate}}. She cannot be seen again after fleeing from the {{Plural|MM3D|Bee}}.

The [[Stone Mask]] is ineffective against {{Term|MM3D|Aveil}}, and she will alert her guards immediately upon spotting Link.<ref>{{Cite|Halt! Everyone! A rat wearing a strange mask has snuck in!|Aveil|MM}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*{{Term|MM3D|Aveil}} from {{MM|-}} and the {{Plural|FSA|Mage|link}} from {{FSA}} shares the same laugh SFX.
*Although technically a bug, if [[Link]] were to wear the [[Goron Tunic]] while meeting the [[Gerudo Warrior]] at [[Gerudo's Fortress]] in {{OoT}}, Aveil's [[Hyrulean]] counterpart will appear identical to herself. This is because her clothes share the same color palette as [[Green Clothes|Link's tunic]]. ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'', however, fixed this issue.

==Nomenclature==
In Japanese, the name Abēru (Aveil) is supposed to be reminiscent of Nabōru ([[Nabooru]]). This was lost in the localized names.
