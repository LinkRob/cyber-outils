The {{Term/Store|Giant's Forest||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Giant's Forest'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Giant's Forest is located in [[Hyrule Field]]. A [[Road]] cuts through the east of the Forest, running north towards [[Mount Gustaf]] and south between the [[Central Tower]] and [[Mount Daphnes]].

A [[Black Hinox]] sleeps in a clearing at the center of the Giant's Forest.

A [[Korok]] can be found by following the [[Flower Trail]] that starts on top of a [[Tree]] stump in the south of the Forest.
