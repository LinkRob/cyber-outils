{{Term/Store|Ice Gimos|Ice Gimos|Series, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 185 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Term|ALBW|Ice Gimos}} are primarily found on {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Death Mountain|link}} in {{ALBW}}. They are stronger than standard {{Plural|ALBW|Gimos}} but are weak to the {{Term|ALBW|Fire Rod|link}}, which will melt them instantly. Inactive {{Term|ALBW|Ice Gimos}} can be found blocking various paths and entrances and must be melted to progress. They can be distinguished from living {{Term|ALBW|Ice Gimos}} by their lack of visible breaths.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Term|TFH|Ice Gimos}} appear in the Frozen and Fortified Zones of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}} in {{TFH}}. They can be defeated with several slashes of the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} or melted instantly with a single fireball of the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves|link}}.
