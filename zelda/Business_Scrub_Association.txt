The {{Term/Store|Business Scrub Association||Series, MM}} is a trade-regulating organization of merchant [[Business Scrub]]s in {{MM}}.<ref name="Regulation">{{Cite|I'm sorry, but I can't do business with you if you're not a Goron. It's a regulation of the Business Scrub Association.|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref>

==Members==
There is one member of the organization in each of the five regions of Termina. The Business Scrubs in [[Southern Swamp]], [[Goron Village]], and [[Zora Hall]] sell their wares only to the native population (i.e. [[Deku Link]], [[Goron Link]], and [[Zora Link]], respectively) as per a regulation of the association.<ref name="Regulation"/><ref>{{Cite|Sorry, but if you're not a Zora, I can't do business with you. It's a regulation of the Business Scrub Association.|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref> They work the exclusivity into their sales pitch.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for stopping by! I'm doing business with a focus on a Deku Scrub clientele... Do you know what magic beans are, sir? I'll sell you one for 10 Rupees.|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm doing business with a focus on a Goron clientele. And that, sir, makes you a customer.|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm selling Green Potions, but I'm focusing my marketing efforts on Zoras.|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref>

The Scrub who owns the yellow [[Deku Flower]] in [[South Clock Town]] has already sold out his wares ahead of the [[Carnival of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|I've already sold out of my wares,
and the carnival hasn't even begun.|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref> He will buy a [[Moon's Tear]] in exchange for the [[Town Title Deed]], which starts the {{Term|MM3D|Trading Quest|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Trading Quest|link}} consists of exchanging one Title Deed for another with each of the other four Business Scrubs, fulfilling their dreams of moving to different regions.<ref>{{Cite|That's a... Oh, that's a Town Title Deed. The town is too loud to hear the sound of waves. That's a... Oh, that's a Swamp Title Deed. Big waves don't reach the swamp. That's a... Oh, that's a Mountain Title Deed. Sea breezes don't blow on tall mountains. Oh! If that isn't an Ocean Title Deed. Let me have that! Please. If I have that, my dreams will come true!|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref>

* The Scrub in [[Southern Swamp]] outside the [[Swamp Tourist Center]] wants the [[Town Title Deed]] so it can move to Clock Town.{{Fact}}
* The Scrub in [[Goron Village]] wants the [[Swamp Title Deed]] so it can move back home to the swamp.{{Fact}}
* The Scrub in [[Zora Hall]] in [[Lulu]]'s room wants the [[Mountain Title Deed]] so it can move to the mountains, where the climate is cooler and the air is cleaner.{{Fact}}
* The Scrub in [[Ikana Canyon]] wants the [[Ocean Title Deed]] so it can do business by the sea. It is also eager to see the [[Gerudo]] renowned for their beauty.{{Fact}}

When a Business Scrub vacates their [[Deku Flower]], Link can use it to reach a [[Piece of Heart]] (one for each flower). 

After moving, each Business Scrub is in some way disappointed by its new surroundings.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, I've been able to do business in the place I've always dreamed of. But there aren't many places to plant beans around here...|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well, technically it is the ocean... But I'm doing business inside somebody's room at the ocean. Well, I don't care whose room this is. I'm running a business here.|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref> The one who moves to the mountains is especially unhappy with the cold climate.<ref>{{Cite|...Thanks to you, I'm doing business on a mountain so cold I could die! ...What a swindle.|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref> However, the Scrub that moves from the mountains to the swamp is unreservedly happy to be home.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, I was able to come back home and do business. I am really happy!|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref>
