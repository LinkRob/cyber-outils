The Trial of Stones is a [[Side Quest|side quest]] in [[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity]].

{{Infobox Quest|name=The Trial of Stones|reward=Qualify for Monk Maz Koshia's Trial|image=File:HWAoC The Trial of Stones.png|location=Faron|caption=The Trial of Stones description}}

== Overview ==
The Trial of Stones is a side quest that can be unlocked after completing the [[Scenario]], "[[Destroy the Yiga Clan!]]" in [[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|Chapter 4]].

There will be three quests that can make you qualify for [[The Trial of the Ancients]], and this is one of them. It can be found in the [[Faron]] region.

The requirements needed to complete this are 7[[Ethereal Stone|Ethereal Stones]], 10 [[Amber|Ambers]], 5 [[Topaz]], 5 [[Ruby|Rubies]], and 2 [[Diamond|Diamonds]].

The remaining two needed to qualify for The Trial of Ancients are [[The Trial of Monsters]] and [[The Trial of the Mighty Foe]].
