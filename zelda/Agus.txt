{{Term/Store|Agus||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Agus|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Agus}} is a {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} who lives in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}, where he grows vegetables to sell at {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}} in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}.<ref name="Agus">{{Cite|I grow vegetables in Hateno Village, sell 'em at Dueling Peaks Stable...then I use the money to buy meat and head back. So I guess my job is to make sure folks in Hateno Village have full bellies?|Agus|BotW}}</ref> As such, he can be found traveling the roads between these locations with his {{Term|BotW|Donkey|link}}. He uses the {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} from his vegetable sales to purchase meat at the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}}, which he exchanges to the people of his home town.<ref name="Agus"/> He will offer to sell his stock to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} or purchase items from him, as well.<ref>{{Cite|By the by...how's your belly? I'd love it if you bought something, but, I mean, I'm happy just to show you what I've got for sale. The rest is up to you!|Agus|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What do you want to sell?|Agus|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Agus}} arrives in the {{Term|BotW|Midla Woods|link}}, he will rest below a cliff near the edge of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}. He laments the fact that he is unable relax in his hometown, since he has to gather fresh vegetables to bring back to {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, it's been a good, long while since I was back in my hometown... I'd love to just kick back, but I can't.|Agus|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I gotta load up my buddy with vegetables an' get 'em over to Dueling Peaks Stable, so I can sell 'em while they're fresh.|Agus|BotW}}</ref> After a while, he will get up and start to head back towards the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}}.

{{Term|BotW|Agus}} prefers {{Plural|BotW|Donkey}} to {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}}, claiming they are more his style.<ref>{{Cite|Feeling a little donkey envy, huh? Horses are faster, sure, but donkeys are more my style.|Agus|BotW}}</ref> He claims that, while people talk about horsepower, they tend to forget about "donkeypower", which is the power to be friends.<ref>{{Cite|People talk about horsepower, but they forget about donkeypower. That's the power to stay chill and be good buds.|Agus|BotW}}</ref> He calls his {{Term|BotW|Donkey}} his buddy,<ref>{{Cite|I'm hoping to do a lot of business at {{Color|BotW Blue|Dueling Peaks Stable}}... Me and my buddy came all the way from {{Color|BotW Blue|Hateno Village}}!|Agus|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Link}} is apparently confused by.<ref>{{Cite|...Huh? Whaddya mean, "what buddy?" You got donkey blindness or something? He's right here!|Agus|BotW}}</ref>

On his journey, {{Term|BotW|Agus}} will pass by {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, explaining that he has not had luck selling his wares there.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, I just can't seem to sell anything in {{Color|BotW Blue|Kakariko Village}}. So...yeah.|Agus|BotW}}</ref> While passing {{Term|BotW|Lake Siela|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Agus}} will grow nervous, being frightened if {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches him.<ref>{{Cite|Woah there!|Agus|BotW}}</ref> He will be relieved so see Link is a regular person and apologize.<ref>{{Cite|Oh...it's just a normal person... What a relief! Sorry for freaking out like that.|Agus|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Agus}} explains that a while ago, he was scared by a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} ghost on the other side of the {{Term|BotW|Lake Siela|display=Lake}}, so he ends up being a bit jumpy in this area.<ref>{{Cite|A little while back, just on the other side of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Siela}}, I got spooked bad by this weird tree ghost, so I'm a little jumpy.|Agus|BotW}}</ref> He keeps an eye on this {{Term|BotW|Tree}}, asking {{Term|BotW|Link}} if it is actually moving or if his eyes are playing tricks on him.<ref>{{Cite|Take a look at that tree on the other side of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Siela}}... Did it just move, or are my eyes playing tricks on me?|Agus|BotW}}</ref>

To make something delicious, {{Term|BotW|Agus}} suggests {{Term|BotW|Cooking}} {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}} together.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to make something really delicious, whip up some {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}}!|Agus|BotW}}</ref> There is apparently a third item that should be used, but he is unable to remember what it was.<ref>{{Cite|...This is just something I heard, but... If you take {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}...and {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}} and... {{Small|Wait, what was the other thing...}} Anyway, you combine all that stuff, and apparently it comes out real, real yummy.|Agus|BotW}}</ref> This recipe lines up with {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Porgy Meunière|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Seafood Meunière|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves {{Term|BotW|Agus}} from an [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|enemy]] attack, he might ask if {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a famous swordsman and say he should be one.<ref>{{Cite|Say, are you some famous swordsman or something? Because if you're not, you should be!|Agus|BotW}}</ref> He may also wonder what would have happened to him if {{Term|BotW|Link}} had not come along, but soon dismisses this thought as something he does not want to worry about.<ref>{{Cite|What would have even happened to me if you hadn't come along? I don't want to think about it...|Agus|BotW}}</ref> He can also comment that he thought {{Term|BotW|Link}} was strong the first time he saw him, and he ended up being proven right.<ref>{{Cite|When I first got a look at you I was like, I bet he's strong... And I was right!|Agus|BotW}}</ref> In all scenarios, he will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Sneaky Elixir|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|You deserve a reward. Here, take this.|Agus|BotW}}</ref> On subsequent rescues, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} ingredients.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys from him while it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}}, {{Term|BotW|Agus}} will note that that many {{Plural|BotW|Merchant}} sell their best wares or cut deals when the {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}} is bad.<ref>{{Cite|Lots of folks sell their best stuff or cut you a deal when the weather's bad... Have you noticed that?|Agus|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Agus}} himself has no special offers during such scenarios. He reveals that he does not mind the rain, since it gives one a good excuse to stay put for a while.<ref>{{Cite|I don't mind the rain, really. It gives you a good excuse to stay put for a while.|Agus|BotW}}</ref>
