{{Term/Store|Tiny Pesto|Tiny Pestos|Series, TMC|plural}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 210}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TMC|Tiny Pesto}} are found on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} when {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is still at his regular size, as they hover around where beanstalks grow. They are easily dispatched with the {{Term|TMC|Sword|link}}, and signify that a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized area is nearby, where they appear as {{Plural|TMC|Red Pesto|link}} while {{Term|TMC|Link}} is {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized.
