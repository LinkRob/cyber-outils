{{Term/Store|Hebra North Summit||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hebra North Summit'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Hebra North Summit is located in the [[Hebra Mountains]] overlooking the [[Sturnida Basin]] between the [[Hebra West Summit]] and the [[Hebra East Summit]]. The Hebra North Summit is a steep incline up to a small flat peak.

At the top of the Summit, a [[Korok Pinwheel]] can be found sitting at the top. If [[Link]] stands near this pinwheel, a [[Korok Balloon]] will appear and fly around the peak, stopping every few seconds. If Link shoots the balloon with an [[Arrow]], the Korok will appear and grant Link a [[Korok Seed]] for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
