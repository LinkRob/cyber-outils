==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
The {{Term|OoS|Member's Card}} can be bought at the {{Term|OoS|Subrosia Market|link}} for five {{Plural|OoS|Ore Chunk|link}}. The {{Term|OoS|Member's Card|display=Card}} is required to access the basement of the {{Term|OoS|Horon Village Shop|link}}, which is reserved exclusively for {{Term|OoS|Member's Card|display=Card}}-holding members.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry, sir. You cannot enter here without a Member's Card. Are you curious? What's back there is a secret! Heh heh heh!|Stockwell|OoS}}</ref>

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Term|TWWHD|Beedle|link}} creates the {{Term|TWWHD|Member's Card}} after {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} makes one purchase at his {{Term|TWWHD|Shop Ship}}, and he uses it to keep track of {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}'s points in his {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle Member's Club|link}} program.<ref name="Greatest"/> {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle}} will assign a single point to the {{Term|TWWHD|Member's Card|display=Card}} for each individual purchase after it is made. It is never seen or given directly to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}.

Depending on the amount of points accumulated on the {{Term|TWWHD|Member's Card}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can attain higher levels of membership in the {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle Member's Club|display=Member's Club}}, which allow {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} to receive gifts that can be redeemed at any of {{Plural|TWWHD|Beedle's Shop Ship}}.<ref>{{Cite|Notice to Silver Membership Holders: Thank you for your frequent patronage of Beedle Shop Ships. Since you have earned 30 points, you have been granted Silver Membership. All Silver Membership holders receive a special gift of {{Color|TWW Red|one Complimentary ID}}. It is valid at all {{Color|TWW Red|Beedle Shop Ships}}, so bring it to a shop ship near you. I look forward to your future patronage.<br/>Beedle|Beedle|TWWHD}}</ref>

==={{PH|-}}===
After {{Term|PH|Link|link}} makes his first purchase from {{Term|PH|Beedle's Shop Ship|link}}, {{Term|PH|Beedle|link}} will prepare a {{Term|PH|Member's Card}} for him.<ref name="Prep"/> It is never seen or given directly to {{Term|PH|Link}}. {{Term|PH|Beedle}} will assign a single point to the {{Term|PH|Member's Card|display=Card}} for every 100 {{Plural|PH|Rupee}} spent.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH |publisher= Prima| page= 17}}</ref>

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Term|ST|Beedle}} offers {{Term|ST|Link}} a {{Term|ST|Beedle's Member's Club|display=Membership}} with his business that details a reward system if he continues to provide {{Term|ST|Beedle}} with his patronage. To accept the {{Term|ST|Beedle's Member's Club|display=Membership}}, {{Term|ST|Link}} must pay a fee of 100 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}}. 

For every 10 {{Plural|ST|Rupee}} spent, {{Term|ST|Link}} gains one point towards the next Membership rank. As long as {{Term|ST|Link}} buys items in {{Term|ST|Beedle's Air Shop}}, he will continue to accumulate points, but after he has obtained the final Membership available, the point system will cease, as nothing further can be acquired through it.

The purpose of the Membership is to benefit both parties: as {{Term|ST|Beedle}} sees an increase in the purchasing of his products, {{Term|ST|Link}} gets price discounts, {{Plural|ST|Item|link}} that can be redeemed in {{Term|ST|Beedle}}'s {{Term|ST|Beedle's Air Shop|display=Shop}}, and even {{Plural|ST|Treasure}} in return for his valued patronage. Each Membership comes with a different perk, and as the memberships increase in value, so do the value and significance of the bonuses that come in conjunction with the Memberships.
