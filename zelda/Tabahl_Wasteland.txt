{{Term/Store|Tabahl Wasteland||Series, LA}} is an area in {{LA}} located directly north of the Cemetery on {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tabahl Wasteland|Sign|LA}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|LANS|Tabahl Wasteland}} is a deserted portion of the island, used mainly to bridge the Cemetery and the northern {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Heights|link}} region. In the north-west section, there is a road west to {{Term|LANS|Crazy Tracy|link}}'s house on {{Term|LANS|Koholint Prairie|link}}. To the east, across the moat, is {{Term|LANS|Kanalet Castle|link}}, which is inaccessible from this area. The area is full of dead or dying trees with {{Plural|LANS|Crow|link}} in the branches, and fully deserves the title of wasteland.
