{{Term/Store|Cliff Plateau Isles||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (F7) is an island of the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Fishman|Hey, small fry, do you know about the [[Nintendo Gallery]]? The only way in there is to hit a switch that's way up high on the [[Forest Haven]]. But the only way to do THAT would be to bribe one of the [[seagull|gulls]] by putting a little [[Hyoi Pear|bait]] on your head!}}

The island consists of two bodies of land that are connected to each other through a unique [[Secret Cave]] accessible from a hole found in the lower body. Said cave incorporates the use of the [[Deku Leaf]] as well as [[Boko Stick]]s and [[torch]]es (or the [[Fire Arrow]]s) to navigate the labyrinth inside. After successfully completing the labyrinth within, [[Link]] will find himself upon the largest island. The [[Korok]] [[Aldo (The Wind Waker)|Aldo]] and a wilted [[tree]] can be found there. A [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Treasure Chart]] can also be found there. A [[Triforce Shard]] lurks beneath the waves in close proximity to the island.
