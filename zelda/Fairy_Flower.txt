The '''Fairy Flower''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Fairy Flower|Lovely and fragrant flower. Wearing one makes fairies appear occasionally when you cut grass or other plants.<ref name="Fairy Flower">{{Cite|Lovely and fragrant flower. Wearing one makes fairies appear occasionally when you cut grass or other plants.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Fairy Flower is an [[Accessory]] found in a cave on the southwest side of [[Eldin Volcano]]. Equipping it grants the [[Fairy Appearance Up]] effect, increasing the drop rate of [[Fairy|Fairies]].
