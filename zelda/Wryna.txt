{{Term/Store|Wryna||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}} who lives in [[Skyloft]]. She is the wife of [[Jakamar]] and the mother of [[Kukiel]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 294}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>That is {{Color|SS Blue|Wryna}}. She is the creative wife of {{Color|SS Blue|Jakamar}}, the resident repairman of {{Color|SS Blue|Skyloft}}.</p><p>She is said to have a limitless imagination and is often rumored to be the inspiration behind her husband's work.</p>
}}

When Link first encounters Wryna, she is standing in her house. If Link speaks to her, she asks the young hero if he has seen her daughter,<ref>{{Cite|Pardon me, Link. You haven't seen Kukiel, have you? She's always running off without a word to anyone. I don't know what I'm going to do with her. I guess she'll never learn unless she runs into trouble one day.|Wryna|SS}}</ref> who happens to be right outside her house. After winning the [[Wing Ceremony]], Wryna can be found outside her house, still looking for Kukiel. She comments on Kukiel's ability to disappear like a magician.<ref>{{Cite|Hi, Link. Have you seen Kukiel anywhere? I swear that child can disappear in the blink of an eye! She's quite a magician.|Wryna|SS}}</ref>

Once Link places the [[Ruby Tablet]] inside the [[Chamber of the Sword]], he finds a distressed Wryna looking for Kukiel, who hasn't been seen since the start of the Wing Ceremony.<ref>{{Cite|Kukiel! Kukiel! Where could that child have gone?! Oh, Kukiel... Where did you go? What will I do if she's been...kidnapped?!|Wryna|SS}}</ref> She asks for Link's help to find her daughter, to which he agrees.<ref>{{Cite|Please, Link, won't you help me look for her? I've been looking everywhere, but I can't find her! [...] Thank you! The Knight Academy should be proud to have a student like you. Please, find my daughter quickly!|Wryna|SS}}</ref> After Link eventually finds Kukiel in the company of the friendly demon [[Batreaux]], the little girl returns safely to her parents, who show their happiness and appreciation towards Link by giving him five [[Gratitude Crystal]]s each.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Link! Kukiel is safely back at home, and it's all because of you! I don't know how to express my gratitude. I can finally smile and laugh again! Thank you ever so much.|Wryna|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Wryna's name is derived from the [[wikipedia:wren|wren]], a bird.

{{Nomenclature}}

==Trivia==

* Wryna herself does not appear in [[Hyrule Warriors]], but her clothes can be unlocked and worn by Companion Fairies in ''[[Hyrule Warriors Legends|Legends]]'' and ''[[Hyrule Warriors: Definitive Edition|Definitive Edition]]'' under the name "Skyloft shirt" and "Skyloft slacks".
