The {{Term/Store|Ho Ho Tribe||Series, PH}} are a group of seven treasure-seekers appearing in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Ho Ho Tribe can be found sailing in the Southeastern quadrant of the [[World of the Ocean King]], but they originate from the land of [[Wayaway]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm a member of the {{Color|PH Blue|Ho Ho Tribe}}. Yes, indeed! I came from the {{Color|PH Blue|country of Wayaway}}, across the ocean, looking for treasure. Certain items are treasure to the people of {{Color|PH Blue|Wayaway}}, and I'll buy them from you! I'll buy them for more than any of the general stores around here, too.|Ho Ho Tribe|PH}}</ref>

They buy a specific [[Treasure]] from [[Link]] that changes from time to time, for more {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}} than the [[Teller of Treasures]] found on [[Mercay Island]]. The amount they offer and the item they seek changes at midnight on the internal clock of the [[Nintendo DS]].

During the [[Phantom Hourglass Trading Sequence|{{PH|-}} trading sequence]], [[Hoiger Howgendoogen]], who is the only member of the tribe who doesn't have a [[Telescope]], will trade [[Link]] the [[Guard Notebook]] for a [[Kaleidoscope]].
