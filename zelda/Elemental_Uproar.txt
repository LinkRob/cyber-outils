{{Term/Store|Elemental Uproar||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 47 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Elemental Uproar|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Elemental Uproar}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must defeat elementally infused monsters. Each {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} is separated from the other two, and can not reach them. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} in the lower-left hand corner of the map fights against ice monsters, the one in the north fights against fire monsters, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} in the lower-right hand corner fights against electric monsters. At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, each of them must capture the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} they spawned next to. After this objective is reached, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} of each element spawn, along with an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus|link}}, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Hinox|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when all nine of these monsters are killed. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any three {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, fifteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Durian|link}}, 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

Two {{Plural|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}} can also be found around the map.
