"{{Term/Store|The Secret Club's Secret||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Secret Club's Secret}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Through the doorway behind {{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Armor Shop|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, is a curtain that conceals an entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} attempts to enter, {{Term|BotW|Saula|link}} stops him, telling him that the doorway is not for customers.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, um, that doorway isn't for customers, actually...|Saula|BotW}}</ref> She then whispers to use the door on the side of the building if he is a member.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|If you're a member, please head around to the door on the side.}}|Saula|BotW}}</ref> There, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a closed, wooden door. If he attempts to open it, he will hear {{Term|BotW|Greta|link}} from the other side of the door asking for the password.<ref>{{Cite|...What's the password?|Greta|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to enter the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club}} before beginning the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} by either guessing the password or learning it beforehand. The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} will begin when {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the shop.}}

The password contains three letters and a symbol, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must give to {{Term|BotW|Greta}} one at a time at her prompting.<ref>{{Cite|And what comes next?|Greta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And after that?|Greta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And what comes last?|Greta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} is only able to guess the password once per day.<ref>{{Cite|If you get our password wrong, you have to wait until the next day to try again.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets it wrong, {{Term|BotW|Greta}} will tell him to go drink milk at {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Go drink milk in the bar or something!|Greta|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

At {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will encounter three {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} women drinking: {{Term|BotW|Pyra|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sumati|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Yaido|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} mentions that she recently learned the password for a secret location.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I was going to tell you... I finally got my hands on the {{Color|BotW Blue|password}} for you-know-where.|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} calls the place crazy,<ref>{{Cite|Seriously? Wow... That place is a little crazy, isn't it?|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pyra}} is interested in it.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe, but I'm really interested...|Pyra|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} begins to share the password,<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, the password is...|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} notices {{Term|BotW|Link}} listening to their conversation.<ref>{{Cite|Wait a second! You!! Did  you really think we wouldn't see you standing there, listening to our conversation?!|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> She calls him rude and tells him to leave.<ref>{{Cite|So rude! This is a private conversation! Go away!|Sumati|BotW}}</ref>

Next door to {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}} is the home of {{Term|BotW|Rotana|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Pearle|link}}. Every night, {{Term|BotW|Pearle}} hears the voices from the patrons of {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}} through her window,<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* Night after night, the voices of the people in the cant-eenter come right in my window. They're super loud...|Pearle|BotW}}</ref> and if someone were to stand under it, they would hear everything said inside.<ref>{{Cite|If you stand under the window, you can hear everything they're saying...|Pearle|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can stand under the window to eavedrop on the women's conversation.{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} eavesdrops on the women here before ever speaking to them in {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}}, he will hear {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} asking the others if they have heard the rumor about {{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, have you heard the {{Color|BotW Red|rumor about Fashion Passion}}?|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} replies that she has heard the rumor,<ref>{{Cite|I have!|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> and Yaido responds by saying that she had asked {{Term|BotW|Saula}} about it, but she would not tell her anything.<ref>{{Cite|I tried asking Saula about it indirectly, but she wouldn't tell me anything.|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pyra}} then mentions how suspicious {{Term|BotW|Saula}} is.<ref>{{Cite|Doesn't Saula work at Fashion Passion? Geez, that's extremely suspicious...|Pyra|BotW}}</ref>}} {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} complains about the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman who spoke to them,<ref>{{Cite|What was up with that little Hylian vai earlier, anyway?|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Pyra}} dismisses it and urges {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} to share the password.<ref>{{Cite|Forget her! Just hurry up and tell us the password, Yaido!|Pyra|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} reveals she overheard {{Term|BotW|Saula}} using the password, "GSC♦".<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that's right, the password! It's {{Color|BotW Red|GSC♦}}. I overheard Saula talking about it, so I'm sure that's right!|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} remarks that the "GSC" must stand for {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club}}, but she wonders what the diamond stands for.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|GSC♦}}? What could that... Oh, I guet it. Gerudo Secret Club—GSC. But what's the {{Color|BotW Red|♦}} at the end mean?|Sumati|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has the password, he can return to the wooden door to share it with {{Term|BotW|Greta}}. After {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets it right, {{Term|BotW|Greta}} will allow him into the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club}}.<ref>{{Cite|All right... You can come in.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} is inside, she realizes that she has never seen him around before.<ref>{{Cite|Hey... I haven't seen your face around here before...|Greta|BotW}}</ref> She calls {{Term|BotW|Link}} a rare visitor,<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Heh heh heh... Looks like we've got ourselves a rare visitor.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> promising not to ask any questions if he does not.<ref>{{Cite|I guess I have a suspicious mind... So maybe you don't ask too many questions, and I won't either.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Greta}} then accepts {{Term|BotW|Link}} as a member of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club}},<ref>{{Cite|You're a member here now, so thanks for your patronage.|Greta|BotW}}</ref> which completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
