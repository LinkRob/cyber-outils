'''Nao Ueda''' is a programmer for [[Nintendo]].

==Biography==
After graduating from his university program, Ueda would work for [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capcom Capcom] and [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlatinumGames PlatinumGames], working as a programmer for game series such as [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayonetta Bayonetta], [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil Resident Evil], and [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devil_May_Cry Devil May Cry] before joining Nintendo.
