{{Term/Store|Deku Elementary||Series, MM}} is a location mentioned in {{MM}}.<ref name="Madame Aroma">{{Cite|Oh dear, are you a student of Deku Elementary? Where's your teacher? You shouldn't be here. Field trips go next door. Tell your teacher that.|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Deku Elementary is never shown in-game, but if [[Link]] speaks to [[Madame Aroma]] in the [[Mayor's Official Residence]] while in his [[Deku Link|Deku form]], she will assume he is a student of Deku Elementary visiting on a field trip.<ref name="Madame Aroma"/>

Since there are few [[Deku Scrub|Deku]] residents in [[Clock Town]], it is more likely that Deku Elementary is located somewhere in the [[Southern Swamp]].
