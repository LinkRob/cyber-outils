{{Term/Store|Bremor||Series, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{MM|-}}===
Bremor works on the festival tower for the [[Carnival of Time]] in [[Clock Town]] with the other carpenters. When spoken to, he wonders if [[Brac]] is working in the evening and if his tasks will be finished. By the third day, he is atop the festival tower until the evening, when he evacuates with the remaining townspeople. If Link successfully defeats the evil spirit within Majora's Mask, Bremor is seen cheering in Termina Field with other townspeople.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|The Carpenters}}
{{Term|TMC|Bremor}} is a {{Term|TMC|Carpenter|link}} that first appears alongside the other {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter}} repairing the northern entrance to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} is turned to stone by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}. The {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter}} are later found repairing an area in the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}.

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Bremor}} can be found standing next to the {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, where he expresses his desire to cheer up {{Term|TMC|Mutoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lately, Mutoh’s been grumbling about how there’s no work with any punch. I wish I could do something about that, but I don’t even know what he means!|Bremor|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Gorman|link}} has filled the blue-roofed {{Term|TMC|Vacant House|link}} with a tenant, {{Term|TMC|Bremor}} will {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? I hope they fit!|Bremor|TMC}}</ref> resulting in {{Term|TMC|Mutoh}} being inspired to build a brand-new {{Term|TMC|Vacant House}} that can then be occupied by a second tenant.

After a successful {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}}, he will express his gratitude that {{Term|TMC|Mutoh}} has gone back to normal.<ref>{{Cite|I’m so glad the boss is back to his usual self. It just makes you grateful the good times, you know?|Bremor|TMC}}</ref> After this, {{Term|TMC|Bremor}} disappears from {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} and does not make another appearance for the remainder of the game.
