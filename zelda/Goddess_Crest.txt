The {{Term/Store|Goddess Crest||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a recurring symbol in {{SS}} and {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 15}}</ref>{{Name Ref|SSHD}} It represents the goddess [[Hylia]], appearing throughout many [[Temple]]s from the [[Zelda Timeline#The Era of the Goddess Hylia|era in which she was in power]]. The predecessor to the [[Wingcrest]] and the [[Hylian Crest]], it has multiple functions, all of which are activated by a [[Skyward Strike]]. Other, more bird-like variations of the crest can be found throughout [[Skyloft]], including on [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]]'s clothing.

==Location and Uses==
=== Temples and the Ancient Tablet ===
Glowing, levitating Goddess Crests are found on the [[Statue of the Goddess]] in [[Skyloft]], at[[Skyview Spring]] in [[Skyview Temple]], at the [[Earth Spring]] in the [[Earth Temple (Skyward Sword)|Earth Temple]], and inside the [[Isle of Songs]]. Triggering the first with a Skyward Strike raises the altar on which the Ancient Tablet is to be assembled, while the second and third grant recorded messages from Hylia and access to the [[Ruby Tablet|Ruby]] and [[Amber Tablet]]s. The fourth is used in the quest for the [[Sacred Flames]].

===Goddess Cubes===
{{Main|Goddess Cube}}
Goddess Cubes are cubes of an unknown metal with the Goddess Crest embossed on them. A Skyward Strike converts them to a beam of light that shoots into the sky and activates a corresponding [[Goddess Cube Chest]].

===Goddess Wall===
{{Main|Goddess Wall}}
The Goddess Crest also sometimes appears on the walls of [[Dungeon]]s. Charging up a Skyward Strike and then drawing the form of a consumable [[Item]] (e.g. [[Heart]]s, [[Rupee]]s, [[Bomb]]s, [[Arrow]]s) where the symbol is will lead to that Item appearing. Drawing the [[Triforce]] will produce [[Fairy|Fairies]].

===Spirit Orb===
{{Main|Spirit Orb}}
In {{BotW}}, whenever the trial of an [[Ancient Shrine]] is completed, that shrine's [[Monk]] will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a Spirit Orb, which contains the Goddess Crest in the center, before fading away, his duty to the Goddess fulfilled.

== Trivia ==

* The Goddess Crest can be seen at both the [[Spring of Power]] and [[Spring of Wisdom]] in {{BotW}}, but cannot be seen at the [[Spring of Courage|Spring of Courage.]]
