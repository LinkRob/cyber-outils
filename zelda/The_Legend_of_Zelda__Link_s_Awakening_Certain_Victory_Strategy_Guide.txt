'''''The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening Certain Victory Strategy Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Futabasha]] for {{LA}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 112 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{LA}}, a listing of {{Plural|LA|Enemy|link}}, {{Plural|LA|Boss|link}}, and collectibles, and maps of all the locations and {{Plural|LA|Dungeon|link}} in the game.
