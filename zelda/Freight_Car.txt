{{Term/Store|Freight Car|Freight Cars|Series, ST|plural}} are items in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Trusty Freight Car|N/A|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|ST|Freight Car}} are used to transport goods on the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}}. The first {{Term|ST|Freight Car}} that {{Term|ST|Link|link}} obtains in his adventure is the {{Term|ST|Trusty Freight Car}}, given to him by {{Term|ST|Kagoron|link}} in {{Term|ST|Goron Village|link}}, initially to transport {{Term|ST|Mega Ice|link}} back to the {{Term|ST|Goron Village|display=Village}} in order to put out the fires caused by an erupting volcano. {{Term|ST|Link}} is allowed to keep the {{Term|ST|Freight Car}} so that he can make deliveries throughout {{Term|ST|Hyrule|link}}, usually in exchange for {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}} or {{Plural|ST|Force Gem|link}}.

After the first one is obtained, a variety of {{Plural|ST|Freight Car}} can be purchased at {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}}.
