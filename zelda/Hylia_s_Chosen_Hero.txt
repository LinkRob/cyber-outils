'''Hylia's Chosen Hero'''<ref name="Hylia's Chosen Hero">{{Cite|You know, they say that the goddess gave the Sailcloth to her chosen hero long ago.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> is another epithet assigned to the '''[[Link#Skyward_Sword|Hero of the Sky]]'''.<ref name=":1">{{Cite|Come, Link. You must take up this sword. As the one chosen by my creator, it is your destiny.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>"Ah, I see it now. Link, you are the goddess' chosen hero. How interesting..." — [[Levias]] ({{SS}})</ref><ref>"You are the chosen hero, and I, Zelda... I am the goddess reborn as a mortal." — [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] ({{SS}})</ref><ref name=":0">{{Cite|{{Color|SS Gold|The youth who draws forth the guiding sword shall be known as the goddess's chosen hero, and it is he who possesses an unbreakable spirit.}}|Fi|SS}}</ref>

In the [[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword (Himekawa)|Akira Himekawa manga adaptation of the game]], he is the earliest-known incarnation of the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]]' main protagonist, with his successor in {{SS|-}}<nowiki/>'s events being his reincarnation.

=={{SS|-}}==

When telling Link about the {{Term|SS|Wing Ceremony|link}}, [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]] says that [[The Goddess Hylia|the Goddess]] gave her chosen hero her {{Term|SS|Sailcloth|link}} in the event that the ceremony is intended to re-enact.<ref name="Hylia's Chosen Hero" /> Thus, {{Term|SS|Zelda}} gives {{Term|SS|Link}} a {{Term|SS|Sailcloth}} that she made herself, unaware that, in fact, this was the event being referenced, as Zelda is the Goddess reborn, and Link is her chosen hero.<ref>"You are the chosen hero, and I, Zelda... I am the goddess reborn as a mortal." — [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] ({{SS}})</ref>

According to [[Fi]], the spirit that Hylia created for the sole purpose of aiding her chosen hero,<ref>{{Cite|First, she created {{Color|SS Blue|Fi}}. She made the spirit that resides in your sword to serve a single purpose: to assist her {{Color|SS Red|chosen hero}} on his mission.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> said hero is the youth who draws the {{Term|SS|Goddess Sword|link}} from its pedestal, possessing an unbreakable spirit<ref name=":0" />.

Fi later states that the [[Silent Realm]] can only be entered by Hylia's chosen hero,<ref>"The Silent Realm is a spirit world that only the Goddess' chosen hero is allowed to enter." — [[Fi]] ({{SS}})</ref> not even she can enter with him.<ref>"As expected, I cannot follow you into this realm, for this trial calls out to your mind alone." — [[Fi]] ({{SS}})</ref>

Finally, the {{Term|SS|Stone of Trials|link}} is stated to have been created to lead the chosen hero to the {{Term|SS|Sky Keep|link}}, the hidden location of the {{Term|Series|Triforce|link}}.<ref>"You got the Stone of Trials! This engraved object was created to lead the chosen hero to the hidden location of the Triforce!"— N/A ({{SS}})</ref>

'''Link''' is a character who appeared in the era after the creation of the world, thousands of years prior to the events of {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=And so… thousands of years later…|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=030}}</ref> Although only briefly mentioned in the game,<ref name="Hylia's Chosen Hero" /> Link plays a significant role as the main protagonist in the {{SS|Himekawa}}, where he is depicted as the earliest incarnation of {{Term|Series|Link|link}}, though this has not been canonically confirmed. In the manga, {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Link}} wears his signature green tunic and hat, as well as a red cape and golden pauldrons. He is also depicted as a young adult, likely his early-to-mid twenties, rather than his late teens.

===History===
Little is known about Link's history except that he used to live in the [[Hylia's Realm|Land of Hylia]] during the [[Era of the Goddess Hylia]],<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Far beneath Skyloft, the city in the sky where the young Link now stands, under the thick sea of clouds... people once lived on the world below.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=004}}</ref> thousands of years before the events of {{SS|-}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=And so… thousands of years later…|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=030}}</ref>

[[File:009.jpg|thumb|left|150px|The Children of Hylia join forces.]]
{{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Link}} was a knight of the Land of Hylia, respected by the Hylian citizens and regarded as a hero.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I am Link, a knight of the land of Hylia...!|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=011}}</ref> However, [[Lord Dagianis]] frames him for some crime and he is imprisoned.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Back when it was still called "the land of Hylia"... a dark age was brewing shrouded in fear and betrayal...|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=004}}</ref>

Four years later, after Lord Dagianis' death at the hands of {{Term|SS|Demise|link}}, the people decide to free Link from his prison.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Lord Dagianis, the man who framed you, has been killed by the Demon King. Please come out into the light of day. The Demon King's army will soon be upon us.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=006}}</ref> They realize that he is the only one capable of saving the land from {{Term|SS (Himekawa)|Demise}}'s army of monsters, however Link has been weakened after four years of imprisonment.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It was you––my own people––who said you had no need for a powerful hero. And now that it's convenient for you, you want me to fight? What's more, my sword is broken. I can't even run anymore.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=006}}</ref> Still believing that Link can save them, they give him his repaired sword and his [[Tunic|clothes]], and freed him from his prison.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Our little misunderstanding has been resolved. Your premonition of danger was correct. We all understand that now. You are the only one who drive off the Demon King and protect Hylia!|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=006}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Here is your weapon. Orville has tended to it these past four years.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=006}}</ref>

Once more a Knight of Hylia, Link appears atop the castle, facing a crowd cheering for him. Gazing at the sky, he says to himself that he will fight for the Goddess Hylia forever as long as she needs him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=My dear land of Hylia! Though people's hearts may change with time, this land's beauty, pride and purity never change. If you have need of me... Then I will fight forever to defend you.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=008}}</ref> 

===The Goddess And Her Hero Unite===
Ready to fight with his allies against Demise and his army, he sees the Goddess Hylia suddenly appear in person, riding on an old Loftwing. The articulate Loftwing expresses his low opinion of the [[Hylian]]s, saying that they are useless and didn't know the differences of deities and monsters, given the people's fearful reaction. He could not believe that the hero who would ride him to face Demise could be among the people of the Surface.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Hmph! Our search is in vain, Hylia. They are so fearful that they cannot tell gods from demons! That is how useless these humans are. The one I seek will not be found here in this lower world.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=010}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=...Someone able to fight the Demon King with me! Someone worthy to be rider!!|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=011}}</ref>

Link reveals himself as the Knight of Hylia and the hero whom they sought. He claims that, although humans may seem small in the eyes of the gods, there are those among them who possess great courage.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Oh? And who might you be?|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=011}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link, a knight of the land of Hylia. Humans may be small in the eyes of a god... But there are those among us who have the courage to fight!|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=011}}</ref> The old Crimson Loftwing gains interest in Link, and orders him to show him how worthy he truly is while he departs.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= If this is so, then show me how much a human is worth!|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=012}}</ref> Meanwhile, the Goddess Hylia, sensing the Demon King's upcoming arrival to the Surface, convince to her people to escape to the sky with the Loftwing guiding them there.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The fearsome Demon King will soon raze the surface world with fires of destruction. I cannot stand aside while my land and people fall to ruin. My people, you can escape to the sky. The Loftwing will show you the way.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=012}}</ref> Hylia acknowledges her knight's unmatched valiance and honor and proclaims him again her chosen hero. She then allows him to wield her legendary weapon brought by the gods, the {{Term|Series|Master Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Master Sword, A blade that repels evil, will sunder the earth and act as a pillar to support the land as it floats up to the sky. And yet, it was created by the gods, meant for our use alone. In order to expert its power on the surface world, it must be reforged by human hands. The hands of the most honorable hero in the land.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=013}}</ref>

However, Link sorrowfully claims that he cannot touch a holy sword, thinking he is impure because of his long imprisonment.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Goddess, I was imprisoned for a long time, and my honor tarnished. I couldn't possibly lay hands on this sacred blade.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=013}}</ref> Despite this, Hylia says that the Master Sword will decide whether he is impure or not, and asks him for his choice; to seek revenge on the surface's people who imprisoned him or to save the world from its terrible fate.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link. This blade knows whether or not you are tarnished.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=014}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Do you intend to take revenge on the kingdom that held you in contempt? Or will you save it from its doom?|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=014}}</ref> Hylia's chosen hero decides that he will always stand with his friends and accepts the Master Sword from the goddess.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Regardless of whether you are humans or gods... All of you seek my answer. Truly... you use me whenever it suits you. But right now, it goes without saying--My spirit will always stand with my friends!|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=014}}</ref>
[[File:Hylia link master sword.png|thumb|right|150px|[[Hylia]] allows Link to wield the [[Master Sword]]]]
In order to properly wield the Master Sword, Link must break the blade and reforge it.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Stoke the fires! With the Goddess' strength, we shall take to the sky!|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=015}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Master Sword was broken by the hands of the hero, melted down in the searing flames... beaten... and reborn from suffering.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=015}}</ref> Suddenly, Demise eventually came with his army of monsters.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=This time, I will rule everything... Once the legacy of the gods is mine!|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=015}}</ref>

===The Battle Against Demise===
[[File:020.jpg|left|thumb|150px|Link, Hylia's chosen hero with the three legendary dragons giving him their blessing.]]
Thus the war began, the Goddess Hylia and her chosen hero's forces, with support from the world's other tribes, had been fighting Demise and his army of monsters for a week.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Wicked desire assaulted the world's surface, staining it the color of obsidian and the mountains, the plains, and all that flowed pure on earth were ruthlessly scoured by fire.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=016}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Over the course of seven days and seven nights, the Goddess's army fought valiantly against the army of the Demon King. Eventually, Link faces Demise on his own.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Your prayers from afar won't vanquish the demons! Creatures of the world. Servants of the Goddess. The humans are at war. As his strength wanes, the Goddess's Loftwing appears, allowing him to ride on his back and fight together. The hero asks for the Loftwing's guidance to show his people to the skies, to which the Loftwing agrees to as long as the hero continues to fly with him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Loftwing, will you show our people to the skies? I ask your guidance.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=020}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=As long as you fly with me henceforth.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=020}}</ref> At that moment, the three legendary dragons, [[Faron (Dragon)|Faron]], [[Eldin (Dragon)|Eldin]], and [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]], appear, offering their blessing to the Hero's [[Master Sword]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=At that moment, as though offering a blessing, three dragons appeared and circled the two.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=020}}</ref> They reveal the legacy of the gods dwells within the Master Sword.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The legacy of the gods dwells within the Master Sword. Link, mortally wounded, stays behind and watches the land floating through the skies before passing away.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=May you thrive, my comrades. === The Chosen Hero Reborn===
Thousands of years later,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=And so… thousands of years later…|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=030}}</ref> the hero's reincarnation, also named [[Link]], goes outside and encounters his [[Crimson Loftwing]], who is actually the reincarnation of the Goddess' Loftwing from that bygone age.

The bird's eyes tells a message to the hero's spirit, reborn as his own successor. Link then seems to understand that the Loftwing had chosen him to be his rider.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote="Hello. I have long awaited... this moment". For some reason... that's what I felt the red Loftwing's eyes were saying that to me.|book= {{SS|Himekawa|-}} |publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=032}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Allow me to guide you through this tale. It was written on the heart of a young boy, as pure as fresh parchment… a story that weaves destinies and spans eternity. ==Legacy==
Starting with his successor, the Hero of the Sky, Link's soul would be reborn alongside Hylia's across the {{Term|Series|Hyrule|link}}'s long history. Though not all of them would take up the {{Term|Series|Master Sword}}, these future heroes would show remarkable courage and be hailed as heroes in their own right, saving Hyrule from many dangers, not just {{Term|Series|Ganon|link}}, the incarnation of {{Term|SS|Demise}}'s hatred, but many others as well.

As both of them are known to be the Goddess' chosen knights, most of Link's reincarnations carry the bloodline of the [[Knights of Hyrule]], who protects the [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Family]],<ref>{{Cite|Only a person of the Knights Of Hyrule, who protected the royalty of Hylia, can become the Hero... You are of their blood-line, aren't you? Then you must rescue Zelda without fail.|Maiden|ALttP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You are perhaps the last one to carry on the blood-line of the Knights... It is ironic that the last one in the line has the potential to become the Hero of legend.|Maiden|ALttP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Knights of Hyrule are a clan that protect the Royal Family, their members descended from the Hero who governed the Crest of Courage. Legend has it that the Hero will one day reappear within their ranks.|book=HH|page=93}}</ref>

Regardless of whether they were born into this knightly bloodline, those future heroes would bear his name for generations.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The heroes of these chronicles all share the name Link. These Links might have been the same person, a series of familial descendants, or a numbers of heroes with different names entirely. The Links of certain eras may also have been named after the legendary hero.|book=HH|page=68}}</ref>
