{{Term/Store|Innkeeper's Request||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 25 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Innkeeper's Request|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Innkeeper's Request}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must capture three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} in 12 minutes. The first {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with only {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}}, along with a few at the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}'s exit. An {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}} is stationed nearby, to the side of the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}. There is a seemingly empty part of the map behind the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe}} although a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok|link}} is located there and will reward the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} with a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}} if found.

The second {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos|link}}, with a few in front of the entrance, and to capture it the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat the {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} inside. There are also a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}} in this {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}. An {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}} and more {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos}} are stationed outside the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}'s exit.

The third {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}}, a few of them being right in front of the entrance. To capture this {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat the {{Term|HWAoC|Lynel|link}} inside. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured. 

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, and 600 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.
