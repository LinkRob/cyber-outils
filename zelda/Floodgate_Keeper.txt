The {{Term/Store|Floodgate Keeper||Series, OoS}} is a man in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref> He resides in the [[Spool Swamp]], and is usually in possession of the [[Poison Moth's Lair]]'s [[Floodgate Key]]. When [[Link]] first encounters him, he has unfortunately misplaced the key.<ref>{{Cite|You want in the swamp? Well... Actually, I lost the key to open the floodgates!|Floodgate Keeper|OoS}}</ref> When Link finds it for him, he allows Link to keep it.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! That's where I left it! That should open the floodgates.|Floodgate Keeper|OoS}}</ref> His house is located next to the underground maze that leads to the key hole.<ref>{{Cite|You can get to the keyhole from that puddle outside.|Floodgate Keeper|OoS}}</ref>

In a [[Linked Game]], the mother of [[Mamamu Yan]] appears in the Floodgate Keeper's home after completing [[Poison Moth's Lair]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm the mother of Mamamu Yan, Labrynna's top dog breeder.|Mamamu Yan's Mother|OoS}}</ref> She will wish to tell Link a secret to her daughter in [[Labrynna]].<ref>{{Cite|Can you tell this secret to my daughter, Mamamu Yan, in Labrynna?|Mamamu Yan's Mother|OoS}}</ref> The old woman mentions that the Floodgate Keeper is also from [[Labrynna]], and that they met "by chance".<ref>{{Cite|This fellow's from Labrynna, too, but we met here by chance.|Mamamu Yan's Mother|OoS}}</ref>
