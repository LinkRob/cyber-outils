{{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Claymore}} are two-handed swords that were designed to be used in combat.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are heavy, and therefore difficult to use, but have a decent quality and durability.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly used by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}, though they can also be found abandoned.

Scanning the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} have a chance to drop a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Claymore}}
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Young Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|SSB|Ganondorf|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|LA|Link|link}} ({{LA}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}} 
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|OoT|Link|link}} ({{OoT}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Young Link}} ({{MM}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Due to being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Claymore}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, and they will sink if dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. If they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, they will not burn like a wooden {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} would.

{{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Claymore}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which were designed for the soldiers of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>
