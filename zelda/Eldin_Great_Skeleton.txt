The {{Term/Store|Eldin Great Skeleton||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Eldin Great Skeleton|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Eldin Great Skeleton is a giant {{Term|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Object)|link|display=skeletal landmark}} in the [[Eldin Mountains]]. The skeletal remains are of the extinct [[Leviathan]].<ref>{{Cite|Leviathan (Volcano)|Camera|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But we do have a general idea of where such remains might be found... The {{Color|BotW Blue|Eldin Mountains}} in the northeast, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra region}} in the northwest, and the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo region}} in the southwest.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref> The remains consist of a flat skull and a ribcage that are partially buried underground. A small camp of monsters live beneath the skeleton. The Eldin Great Skeleton becomes a point of interest during the [[Side Quest]] "{{Term|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Side Quest)|link}}", where Link is sent by [[Akrah]] and his brothers to take pictures of the three Leviathan skeletal remains around [[Hyrule]]. Akrah requests a photo of the Eldin Great Skeleton.<ref>{{Cite|I really need to see the {{Color|BotW Red|leviathan bones}} from the {{Color|BotW Blue|Eldin region}}.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref>

A [[Korok]] can be found by lifting up a rock buried under a pile of leaves that is located within the skeleton's eyehole.
