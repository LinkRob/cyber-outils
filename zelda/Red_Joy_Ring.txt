The {{Term/Store|Red Joy Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Red Joy Ring<br/>Beasts drop double Rupees|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Red Joy Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it doubles the value of picked up [[Rupee]]s.<ref name="Description"/> The Red Joy Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
