The {{Term/Store|Victory Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Victory Ring<br/>The Evil King Ganon defeated|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Victory Ring can be found in [[Link]]'s inventory after starting a new game using the [[Hero's Secret]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. It has no effect when worn. The Victory Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
