{{Term/Store|Charlo||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 276}}</ref>

==Biography==
Charlo is a beggar who stands near the [[Goat]] pens on the {{Exp|east in the Wii version|west}} road of [[Castle Town]]. He accepts donations of 30 and 50 [[Rupee]]s for the sake of peace in Hyrule,<ref>{{Cite|If you would... For the sake of peace in Hyrule... Would you please make a donation?|Charlo|TP}}</ref> and assures [[Link]] that he will one day be "surrounded with love."<ref>{{Cite|May the spirit of love descend upon you... If you continue to pray for peace, someday you will be surrounded with love...|Charlo|TP}}</ref> If Link attempts to donate with less than 30 Rupees in his wallet, Charlo will call him a "callous youth" and tell him to "work before praying for peace."<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Callous youth... Work before praying for peace...|Charlo|TP}}</ref> If Link gives him a total of 1,000 Rupees, Charlo will reward him with a [[Piece of Heart]], which descends upon him.<ref>{{Cite|May the spirit of love descend upon you...|Charlo|TP}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Charlo's name may come from the world charlatan, as there are those who believe he is running some sort of scam.
