==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the Happy Mask Shop Owner opens the [[Happy Mask Shop]] in the [[Market]] after Link delivers [[Zelda's Letter]] to the [[Death Mountain Trail Gatekeeper]] in [[Kakariko Village]].<ref>{{Cite|By the way, Mr Hero... If you're going to climb Death Mountain, you should equip a proper shield! It is an active volcano, after all! If you go back to Hyrule Castle Town Market, you should check out the Bazaar. They sell the shield you need there. Tell 'em I sent you, and they should give you a special discount! If you think you're good to go already, don't worry about it. I'd like to ask a favour of you. No, I don't expect you to do it just because of the great tip I just gave you! I'm just asking! Have you been to the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Happy Mask Shop}} that just opened in the Hyrule Castle Town Market? Everyone is talking about it! My little boy pesters me for a popular mask, but I don't have time to go there... So could you go and get the mask for me next time you are in the Market? If you don't feel like it, that's OK, but... Well, I have no choice - this is my job... ...Sigh...|Death Mountain Trail Gatekeeper|OoT3D}}</ref> Once the shop is open, [[Link]] can speak to him to become a "happiness salesman." This starts a [[Mask Trading Sequence]] where Link can borrow each of the Shop's Masks and sell them to people.<ref>{{Cite|Hiyeee! Welcome to the Happy Mask Shop!
We deal in masks that bring happiness to everyone! How would you like to be a happiness salesman? I'll lend you a mask. You sell the mask and bring the money back here. If you want to read the fine print, take a look at the sign right over there. After you've sold all the masks, you will become happy yourself! Have faith...|Happy Mask Salesman|OoT3D}}</ref> After selling all four Masks, Link receives the [[Mask of Truth]], which allows him to understand what [[Gossip Stone]]s say.<ref>{{Cite|As a reward...I will lend you this special mask. This is the Mask of Truth. It is a mysterious mask passed down by the Sheikah. With this mask you can see into other people's minds... It's useful, but scary! Why is it scary? You may find out as you grow older and wiser in the ways of the world... Ho ho ho!|Happy Mask Salesman|OoT3D}}</ref> Along with this, he can return to the Happy Mask Shop to borrow all of the Masks he has previously sold as well as four new Masks.

Throughout the Trading Sequence, the Happy Mask Shop Owner is usually in a cheerful mood, encouraging Link as he is selling the Masks.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You haven't sold the newest mask yet.<br/>Just have faith... Keep trying a little longer.|Happy Mask Salesman|OoT3D}}</ref> However, if Link does not return with enough [[Rupee]]s for the sold Mask, the Happy Mask Shop Owner will become angry and threaten the young hero.<ref>{{Cite|What?! You don't have my money?! How dare you! You'd better bring me my money...or else!|Happy Mask Salesman|OoT3D}}</ref> If Link returns to the Happy Mask Shop later, he will no longer be upset.

The Happy Mask Shop Owner disappears after [[Ganondorf]] takes control of [[Hyrule Castle]] and forces the residents of Hyrule Castle Town to flee, when Link becomes the [[Hero of Time]]. He is last seen during the ending credits celebrating at [[Lon Lon Ranch]].

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM Happy Mask Salesman Chuckling.png|left|frameless]]
The Happy Mask Salesman plays a major role in {{MM}}. Prior to the events of the game but after {{OoT|-}}, he was traveling through the woods in search for new Masks.<ref>{{Cite|I own the Happy Mask Shop. I travel far and wide in search of masks...|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> However, the man was ambushed and knocked out by the [[Skull Kid#Majora's Mask|Skull Kid]] and his two [[Fairy]] friends, [[Tatl]] and [[Tael]]. While the Happy Mask Salesman was unconscious, the imp stole the notorious {{MM|Object}} from his bag and became possessed by its evil powers as he wears it.

When Link arrives in [[Termina]] while searching for [[Navi]], he himself is also assaulted by the Skull Kid, who steals his {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} and rides away with [[Epona]]. Link chases after the Skull Kid, who turns him into a [[Deku Link|Deku Scrub]]. Tatl, who was left behind by the Skull Kid and Tael, decides to follow Link. The two of them make their way to the [[Clock Tower]], where they meet the Happy Mask Salesman. He reveals that he has been following Link, and that he knows of a way to return him to his former self.<ref>{{Cite|You've met with a terrible fate, haven't you? Now don't think me rude, but I have been following you... For I know of a way to return you to your former self.|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> However, he first needs to reclaim his Ocarina from the Skull Kid.<ref>{{Cite|If you can get back the precious item that was stolen from you, I will return you to normal.|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> In exchange, the Salesman asks Link to return the "precious mask" that was stolen from him within three days, before he has to depart from Termina.<ref>{{Cite|In exchange... All I ask is that you also get back my precious mask that the imp stole from me. Except... The one thing is... I'm a very busy fellow... And I must leave this place in three days. How grateful I would be if you could bring it back to me before my time here is up...|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref>

Just a few hours before the [[Moon]] falls, Link and Tatl confront the Skull Kid at the top of the Clock Tower. The hero retrieves his Ocarina and plays the [[Song of Time]], which transports him back to the beginning of the three day cycle. He then returns to the Happy Mask Salesman with his Ocarina, who teaches him the [[Song of Healing]], a melody that heals troubled spirits and turns them into Masks.<ref>{{Cite|Then listen to me. Please play this song that I am about to perform, and remember it well... This is a melody that heals evil magic and troubled spirits, turning them into masks. I am sure it will be of assistance to you in the future.|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> After the song is played, the curse's magic is encased in the [[Deku Mask]] and Link returns to his [[Hylian]] form.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, yes. I give you this mask in commemoration of this day.|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> Having fulfilled his promise, the Happy Mask Salesman asks for his end of the deal. After learning that Link failed to retrieve Majora's Mask, he becomes distressed, and picks up and violently shakes Link out of frustration.<ref>{{Cite|Now, I have fulfilled my promise to you... So, please, give me that which you promised me... Don't tell me... My mask... You did... get it back... Didn't you? What have you done to me!!!|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> He tells Link of the great lengths that he went through to obtain the Mask, as well as its true nature. He explains that if the Majora's Mask is not immediately taken back from the Skull Kid, something [[Terminan Apocalypse|horrible]] will happen.<ref>{{Cite|I went to great lengths to get that legendary mask. When I finally had it... I could sense the doom of a dark omen brewing. It was that unwelcome feeling that makes your hair stand on end. And now... that imp has it... I am begging you! You must get that mask back quickly or something horrible will happen!|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> He begs Link to recover the Mask for him, once again stressing the fact that he must leave in just three days, which is the day of the [[Carnival of Time]], as well as the time remaining before the Moon ultimately crashes into Clock Town. As Link and Tatl set off to recover Majora's Mask, the Happy Mask Salesman waits patiently inside the Clock Tower for their return.

Once Link successfully defeats the evil inside Majora's Mask, the Moon vanishes, thus saving the land of Termina from destruction. The Happy Mask Salesman finally reclaims his Mask, which has seemingly lost its evil power for good.<ref>{{Cite|Oh...So the evil has left the mask after all... Well, now...I finally have it back.|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> As he is in the middle of his travels, he bids farewell and implies that Link should return to [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|Since I am in the midst of my travels... I must bid you farewell. Shouldn't you be returning home? Whenever there's a meeting a parting is sure to follow. However, the parting need not last forever...|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> After commenting on the number of people who Link made happy, he walks away and disappears.<ref>{{Cite|...But, my, you sure have managed to make quite a number of people happy. The masks you have are filled with happiness. This is truly a good happiness.|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref>

Despite {{MM|-}} technically taking place in the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Child Timeline]], as he had already met the Hero of Time before in {{OoT|-}}, the Happy Mask Salesman seems to know a lot about Link and his previous adventures, mentioning that retrieving Majora's Mask should be of no difficulty to someone like him.<ref>{{Cite|What? Is it not a simple task? Why, to someone like you, it should by no means be a difficult task.|Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> Moreover, if Link wears any mask, including the Deku, [[Goron Mask|Goron]], and [[Zora Mask|Zora]] Masks, and speaks to him with it on, he will say something about that mask: what emotion it is filled with, its rarity, or something special about it. If the young hero talks to the Happy Mask Salesman without wearing a Mask, he will become angry at Link for not obtaining the Majora's Mask as demanded, and then tells how many hours remaining before the Moon crashes into Termina.

In {{MM3D}}, the Happy Mask Salesman also gives Link the [[Bombers' Notebook]] after they first meet.

==={{OoA|-}}===
[[File:OoA Happy Mask Salesman Sprite 2.png|30px|left]]
In {{OoA}}, the Happy Mask Salesman owns the [[Mask Shop]] on the outskirts of [[Lynna City]]. He is starving and will give Link the [[Doggie Mask]] in exchange for something to eat, namely the [[Tasty Meat]] from the [[Tokay]] cook.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so hungry my stomach hurts. AHH! I'M HUNGRY! Pardon me. I just thought you could help.|Happy Mask Salesman|OoA}}</ref> His appearance is almost identical to previous games, the one notable difference being his ears which are rounded and not pointed.

==={{MM|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{MM|Himekawa}}, the Happy Mask Salesman's role appears the same as in the game. Towards the final battle with Majora's Mask, he is shown far more sinister than previously.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Ho ho... They're off to confront that little demon. Now for the real show. If I can only get that mask back, I'll no longer have a need for this world. I just move on. Ho... Ho ho ho...|book= MM (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 144}}</ref> After the battle, the Happy Mask Salesman appears before Link still wearing the [[Fierce Deity's Mask]] to take his mask. Link strikes out at him with his Sword and tells him to leave, slicing various masks in half, possibly in an attempt to strike Majora's Mask. He misses, however, and the mysterious man picks up the mask and fades away laughing.

==Trivia==
*The Happy Mask Salesman has nine masks attached to his bag in {{MM}}. One of them features the face found on the [[Mirror Shield]] in-game and another one is based on [[Mario]] from the ''[[mariowiki:Mario_(franchise)|Super Mario]]'' franchise.
[[File:OoT Angry Happy Mask Salesman.png|thumb|314x314px|The notorious angry expression of the Happy Mask Salesman, as seen in {{OoT|-}}]]
*The Happy Mask Salesman's angry face in {{OoT}} and {{MM|-}} has become an infamous meme in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] community, as many call it one of the most frightening expressions ever seen in any of the franchise's many NPC characters, and some even claiming he has the look of a psychopath.
**Ironically, it is strongly implied he could be mentally insane, based on his behavior in the latter game and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (Himekawa)|Akira Himekawa manga adaptation]].
**His mad smile is comparable to that of the supervillian [https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Joker Joker] from the [[wikipedia:DC_Comics|DC Comics]] ''Batman'' series.
*The [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_HMS_Laugh1.wav giggling laugh] of the Happy Mask Salesman is similar to that of the late pop superstar [[wikipedia:Michael_Jackson|Michael Jackson]].
*The scene of when the Happy Mask Saleman teaches [[Link]] the "[[Song of Healing]]" in {{MM|-}} is seen by fans as one of the most comical in the series, as despite the [[song]] itself requiring only three notes, he plays it on a massive piano with three long rows.
**The unusual instrument itself possibly implies the Happy Mask Salesman is also a musician.
[[File:MM Moon Child Maskless Model.png|thumb|172x172px|The Moon Children in {{MM|-}} have very similar heads to the Happy Mask Saleman (original version has no facial texture)]]
*Although never confirmed by [[Nintendo]] to be himself as a child, hacking into the files of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'' reveals that the face models used for the [[Moon Children]] is that of the Happy Mask Salesman.<ref>{{Cite Web|author= NewSuperMoiWii|published= February 18, 2015 |retrieved= March 15 2015|url= https://tcrf.net/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Majora%27s_Mask_3D#Moon_Children.27s_Faces|title= The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D Unused Models|site= The Cutting Room Floor}}</ref> In the original [[Nintendo 64]] game, however, their faces had no texture whatsoever.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Shesez|published= June 30, 2016|retrieved= July 26, 2016|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-U07Dpe9_yU&t=4m59s|title= Boundary Break: Majora's Mask|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref>
*The Happy Mask Salesman's "''You've met with a terrible fate, haven't you?''" line in {{MM|-}} is one of the most [[Iconic quotes in The Legend of Zelda series|iconic quotes]] in the series.
*[[Vaati]], the main antagonist of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords|Four Swords]]'' and {{TMC}}, shares the laugh to the Happy Mask Salesman.
*The Happy Mask Salesman appeared as a guest in a Miiting released to promote ''Majora's Mask 3D''.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This is the third time we've held a Miiting, so we thought we would try something a little different - my guest today is not a developer, but a certain important character from The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D.|author= MariChan|published= March 13, 2015|retrieved= July 25, 2017|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20170721071837/https://miiverse.nintendo.net/posts/AYMHAAACAAADVHjGDkmRww|title= Majora's Mask 3D Miiting|site= Miiverse|type= Archive}}</ref>
**Viewing his [[mariowiki:Mii|Mii]] data file, his creator name is zeroed out to "<code>?</code>やべ", likely meant to be "Ayabe", implying the Mii was made by Naoko Ayabe, who is credited as an illustrator on the game. His birthday is also set to December 31st, which could not be a placeholder, and must be intentional. As the Happy Mask Salesman is associated with "the end", and December 31st is the end of the year, this makes sense.
*It's heavily implied the Happy Mask Salesman is the one who "saved" Link during the [[Terminan Apocalypse]] by reversing the time from the [[New Day]] to the start of the [[First Day]], though not without taunting the hero with his aforementioned quote.
*There is a debate among players whether or not the Happy Mask Salesman in ''Majora's Mask'' and the manga adaptation is secretly an antagonistic character or rather neutral. Despite this, he has helped Link on a few occasions. However, like [[Tatl]] (in the former), he did not give the young [[Hero of Time]] a proper thanks at the ending.
