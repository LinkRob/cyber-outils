{{Term/Store|The Battle of Hyrule Field||Series, HWAoC}} is the first {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Battle of Hyrule Field|Chapters|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
{{Term|HWAoC|The Battle of Hyrule Field}} starts off with {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam|link}} sending the {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrulean Army|link}}, which includes {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}}, to stop the army of {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}} headed for {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Castle|link}}. A {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} appears in the northwest of the battlefield at first, only to be taken out by {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}}.

During the battle, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} finds a small [[Terrako|Guardian]], in a dormant state. Before he can examine it, {{Term|HWAoC|Impa|link}} arrives with the {{Term|HWAoC|Sheikah Slate|link}} in her hands, which activates the Guardian and a {{Term|HWAoC|Sheikah Tower|link}}. The small {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} then follows {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} around.

Impa joins the battle with Link, and the two then takes out 3 enemy outposts.

In the end, 3 {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} appear in the southeastern battlefield. Link and Impa take the Moblins out and {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam|link}} compliments Link for successfully finishing his job.

{{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Shroom|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Hyrule Herb|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Hyrule Bass|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Meat|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Flint|link}} can be found in the "{{Term|HWAoC|The Battle of Hyrule Field}}".
