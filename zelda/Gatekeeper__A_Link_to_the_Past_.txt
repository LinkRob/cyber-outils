The {{Term/Store|Gatekeeper||Series, ALttP}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 228 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref> also knows as '''Friendly Pikku''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 388 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref> is a character in the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|Game Boy Advance version of {{ALttP|-}}]].

==Biography==
The Gatekeeper appears inside the [[Palace of the Four Sword]] blocking the path forward.<ref>{{Cite|Actually, only true heroes can enter this palace.|Gatekeeper|ALttP}}</ref> When talked to, it asks for 20 [[Rupee]]s in exchange for a hint.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! I'll tell you a profitable story if you pay me 20 Rupees. How about it?|Gatekeeper|ALttP}}</ref> After defeating [[Ganon]] and completing {{FS}}, the Gatekeeper will allow [[Link]] to continue through the Palace after paying the 20 Rupee fee.<ref>{{Cite|Heh heh! Thanks! Actually, this palace is here precisely for heroes like you. I bet you can get through it. ...But it will take everything you've got!|Gatekeeper|ALttP}}</ref> The Gatekeeper vanishes afterwards.
