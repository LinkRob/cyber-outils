{{Term/Store|Ancient Orb|Ancient Orbs|Series, BotW|plural}},<ref name="Stone Tablet">{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|ancient orbs}} guarded by the giants of Mount Taran lead to the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> also known as '''Precious Orbs''',<ref>{{Cite|If you offer {{Color|BotW Red|precious orbs}} to each of the three altars on the island, the voice has promised to return your things.|Stranded on Eventide|BotW}}</ref> are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} are spherical pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} constructed by the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} 10,000 years ago. They come in two sizes. The first size is smaller, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} is capable of moving them by picking them up. In addition, they come in a larger size which can be pushed, but usually must be moved by alternative means. Placing {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} inside {{Plural|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|link}} will activate {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, such as revealing {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} or opening doors.

Several {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} are given specific names. These {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orbs}} are involved in {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} throughout the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. They are as follows:
* The {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy|link}} is used in "{{Term|BotW|The Undefeated Champ|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
* The {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb|link}} is used in the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, which is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.
* The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom|link}} is used in "{{Term|BotW|The Stolen Heirloom|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
* {{Term|BotW|Roscoe|link}} is used in the "{{Term|BotW|Guardian Slideshow|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.

In addition, there are unnamed {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} that are also involved in {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
* During the "{{Term|BotW|Into the Vortex|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must bring an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} from the base of {{Term|BotW|Rist Peninsula|link}} all the way to its tip, where there is a {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket}}.<ref>{{Cite|Only a hero bearing this {{Color|BotW Red|ancient orb}} shall be granted the right of {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} on Rist Peninsula.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> When this is done, {{Term|BotW|Ritaag Zumo Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground.
* During the "{{Term|BotW|Stranded on Eventide|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must retrieve three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|link}} and place them in {{Plural|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Offer up the orbs to the three altars on this island. Only then will I acknowledge your skill and return your items.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> This will cause {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh Shrine|link}} to emerge from the top of {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Once you offered precious orbs to each of the three altars on the island, an ancient shrine appeared.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
* During "{{Term|BotW|The Three Giant Brothers|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must collect three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} from three sleeping {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Mount Taran|link}}.<ref name="Stone Tablet"/> They are located in {{Term|BotW|Hanu Pond|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Uten Marsh|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Rabella Wetlands|link}}. Once all of these {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} are placed in {{Plural|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket}}, {{Term|BotW|Tawa Jinn Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|Offering these orbs to the matching pedestals caused an ancient shrine to appear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Ancient Orb|Enigmatic and extremely old, this is an artifact of the Eternal Forest. Ancient Orbs are mysterious orbs found in the [[Eternal Forest]] during [[The Prime Energy and Null]] [[quest]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]]. They cost one Echo energy to summon and are used to activate the pedestals that open the path to the [[Great Deku Tree]].
