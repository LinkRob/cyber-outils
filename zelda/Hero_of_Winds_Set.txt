The {{Term/Store|Hero of Winds Set||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor Set|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 193}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Hero of Winds Set}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor Set|link}} consisting of the {{Term|BotW|Cap of the Wind|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tunic of the Wind|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Trousers of the Wind|link}}. All three pieces can be obtained using the {{Term|BotW|amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} while scanning the {{TWW|-}} series Link [[amiibo]]. Each time the figure is scanned, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} will appear that has a small chance of containing one of the pieces. As the amiibo can only be scanned once per real-world day, the set must be collected over three or more real-world days. 

The set can be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} but cannot be {{Term|BotW|Dye|link|display=Dyed}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}. When each piece has been enhanced by two levels, the Master Sword Beam Up {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link}} takes effect when the full set is worn. It resembles {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}}'s outfit from {{TWW}}.
