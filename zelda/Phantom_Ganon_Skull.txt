The {{Term/Store|Phantom Ganon Skull||Series, BotW}} is an item in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Phantom Ganon Skull}}<br/>Some believe this eerie headgear is inspirited by a dark entity resembling the Great King of Evil himself. It's a rather rare find.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It is a piece of [[Armor]] exclusive to [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC Pack.

==Location and Uses==
Obtaining the Phantom Ganon Skull is one part of the objective of the "[[EX Treasure: Dark Armor]]" [[Side Quest]]. The Skull was stolen from [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] by [[Misko]] and a clue to its location can be found in [[Misko's EX Journal 2]] at the [[Deya Village Ruins]].<ref>{{Cite|I hid the six priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The evil spirit's armor set is hidden within {{Color|BotW Red|Faron's sea of trees}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at the highest of three waterfalls north of Lake Floria}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at the bridge between small waterfalls at Floria River}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the broken stone bird of Ebara Forest}}.|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> The metallic [[Treasure Chest]] that hides the Phantom Ganon Skull can be found submerged under the highest of three waterfalls flowing south from [[Corta Lake]] to [[Floria Lake]]. Link must use the [[Magnesis]] [[Rune]] to pull the Chest from the waterfall to retrieve it.

When worn, the Skull provides one level of Stealth Up. The Phantom Ganon Skull cannot be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] nor can it be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Phantom Ganon Skull, alongside the [[Phantom Ganon Armor]] and [[Phantom Ganon Greaves]], compose the [[Phantom Ganon Set]].

According to its description, it is believed that the Phantom Ganon Skull is infused with a [[Phantom Ganon|dark entity]] that resembles the [[Great King of Evil]].<ref name="Inventory"/> After obtaining the Phantom Ganon Skull, [[Granté]] in [[Tarrey Town]] will begin selling it for 50 [[Rupee]]s.
