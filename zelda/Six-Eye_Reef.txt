{{Term|TWWHD|Six-Eye Reef}} is a minor island located in the center of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}} and east of {{Term|TWWHD|Cyclops Reef|link}}, at D4. It has one large square reef and six smaller round reefs in the center, giving it its name. After the {{Term|TWWHD|Helmaroc King|link}} is defeated in {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Warship|link}} and wall-mounted {{Plural|TWWHD|Cannon (Enemy)|link}} will appear at the island. When {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} destroys all of them, a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest|link}} appears containing a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chart|link}} that leads to the {{Term|TWWHD|Octo Chart|link}}.

In the waters nearby, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can board a {{Term|TWWHD|Submarine|link}}, defeat the three {{Plural|TWWHD|Moblin|link}} inside, and obtain a {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart|link}}.
