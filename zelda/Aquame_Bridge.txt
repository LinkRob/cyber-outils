{{Term/Store|Aquame Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Aquame Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Aquame Bridge spans over [[Aquame Lake]] in [[Hyrule Field]], connecting the [[Coliseum Ruins]] to the main portion of Hyrule Field.

In [[Master Mode]], a wooden [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Sapphire]] can be found east of the Bridge atop a platform held aloft by [[Sky Octorok]]s. A metallic Treasure Chest containing a [[Knight's Shield]] can be found also atop a platform suspended by Sky Octoroks west of the Bridge.

Hanging just under the bridge lies an archery [[Puzzle]], disguised as a {{Term|BotW|Vase|link}} shaped like an [[Acorn]] dangling from a chain. If [[Link]] breaks the {{Term|BotW|Vase}}, the [[Korok]] will reveal itself and reward him with a [[Korok Seed]].
