{{Term/Store|Traveler|Travelers|Series, BotW|plural}} are characters in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.

==Biography==
Travelers are members of the [[Yiga|Yiga Clan]] in disguise who can be found scattered along roadsides throughout [[Hyrule]] after one of two possible conditions are met: either by completing the "[[Seek Out Impa]]" [[Main Quest]], or by entering the [[Yiga Clan Hideout]]. They assume the appearance of both Hylian men or women who travel the roads of Hyrule and call to [[Link]] whenever he is nearby to engage in conversation. If Link speaks to them, they will direct the discussion towards Link's demise before dropping their disguise and attacking as [[Yiga Footsoldier]]s.

If Link speaks to travelers after defeating [[Master Kohga]], they will reference the fact that Kohga was slain by him and assail him in their late leader's name.<!-- Gerudo Canyon Pass, road east of Wetland Stable --><ref>{{Cite|You can't avoid us forever. Will you join the Yiga Clan?|Traveler|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|[Yiga Clan?] A group of brave warriors, founded by Master Kohga—who YOU defeated—set on defeating a hero thought long dead! {{Big|I'll send you to join Master Kohga!}}|Traveler|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can't avoid us forever. Will you join the Yiga Clan?|Traveler|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|[I'll pass.] What?! You're refusing? Here I thought you could replace our master and lead us to victory against the hero... You're just a fool, though... {{Big|I'll send you to join Master Kohga!}}|Traveler|BotW}}</ref>

Travelers cannot spawn during rain and thunderstorms, but if they do end up spawning before a storm starts, they will not seek shelter during that rainstorm, though they will run from other enemies. They do not reward Link if he rescues them, only instead opting to resume their plan to assassinate him.

Some travelers will attempt to sell Link their [[Mighty Bananas]],<!-- Atun Valley, road east of Zelo Pond --><ref>{{Cite|Come on down, young man! Looking to buy bananas this time? You can! Just take a look at these fine bananas! Buy them up quick before you've missed your chance!|Traveler|BotW}}</ref> a fruit which all Yiga are obsessed with. If Link initially denies the offer to look at their wares, they will insist that he buys from them.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll pass.] Pass? Pass?! It would be a mistake to pass on these amazing, wonderful, fantastic bananas! Listen, here's how it works: I sell; you buy! Stop holding up the show, and do your part!|Traveler|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|[I'll buy!] These bananas are my pride and joy!|Traveler|BotW}}</ref> Rejecting the offer a second time will anger the traveler, prompting him to attack.<ref>{{Cite|...What—do you hate bananas? {{Big|For the bana—I mean, for the boss!}}|Traveler|BotW}}</ref> These travelers carry 99 Mighty Bananas at 99 [[Rupee]]s per bundle. If Link purchases all of the Mighty Bananas, the traveler will refer to him as a "banana fan" and announce that he has nothing left to sell.<ref>{{Cite|All the bananas?! I don't know what to say! ==Trivia==
*The act of Yiga Footsoldiers disguising themselves as travelers is rooted in tales of the {{Wp|Ninja}}, whom the Yiga are based on, donning the appearance of simple travelers for various purposes such as for espionage or assassination.
*Even though some Yiga Footsoldiers disguise themselves as Hylian female travelers, they are all males as the Yiga Clan is an all-male Sheikah splinter group.
