The {{Term/Store|Temple of Storms||Series, CoH}} is a dungeon in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Temple of Storms}}|N/A|CoH}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Temple==
The {{Term|CoH|Temple of Storms}} is situated atop {{Term|CoH|Death Mountain|link}} and can only be accessed by successfully navigating the {{Term|CoH|Death Mountain|display=Mountain}}. A {{Term|CoH|Sheikah Stone|link}} sits just outside its entrance.
