{{Term/Store|Ivee||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Ivee|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Ivee}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman who lives in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. She can typically found sweeping the ground outside of the {{Term|BotW|East Wind|link}} with her {{Term|BotW|Broom|link}}. When it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}} outside, {{Term|BotW|Ivee}} will move from her usual spot to sweep underneath the canopy instead. {{Term|BotW|Ivee}} stays outside until 9:00 PM, when she decides to turn in.<ref>{{Cite|I gotta grab some sleep! We can talk later if we need to.|Ivee|BotW}}</ref> At night, she sleeps on the second floor above the {{Term|BotW|East Wind}}, along with her brother {{Term|BotW|Azu|link}} and her mother {{Term|BotW|Amira|link}}.

Whenever {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} passes by the {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}}, she will call him over with a wave and a greeting.<ref>{{Cite|Young man! Over here!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hullo!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref> If it is their first meeting, she will bring up the nice weather to him.<ref>{{Cite|What great weather!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref> She then mention that the {{Term|BotW|East Wind}} is open for business and urges {{Term|BotW|Link}} to check the stock inside.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|East Wind}} general store is open... and it's just as lively inside there as it is out here! Go take a look!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref>

After their initial encounter, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Ivee}} a couple of questions. Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquire where he is, she will happily identify the {{Term|BotW|East Wind}} again, assuring {{Term|BotW|Link}} of the quality of their products.<ref>{{Cite|[What's this place?] This is the general store, {{Color|BotW Blue|East Wind}}! We're open and we are PEPPY! We carry all sorts of goods—from the freshest produce to my father's handmade arrows! We have lots of things in stock, so pop your head in there and take a look!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to ask {{Term|BotW|Ivee}} who she is, she will be shocked by the question before bringing herself to properly answer.<ref>{{Cite|[Who are you?] M-me? I'm...Ivee. My name is Ivee!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref> She will continue to stumble over a formal greeting before going with an informal one.<ref>{{Cite|Pleased to meet your... No, wait. Pleased to MAKE your...acquaintance? Anyway, nice to meet you!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref> The next time he asks, she assures him that she will introduce herself as many times as he likes.<ref>{{Cite|[Who are you?] Oh, OK, sure! I'll introduce myself as many times as it takes!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Ivee}} is finally able to get out the formal greeting she was trying to find before, she will be pleased pleased with herself.<ref>{{Cite|Pleased... Uh, I am very pleased to make your acquaintance! Yesss! I said it right!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} leaves the conversation, she responds by asking him to return to the shop later.<ref>{{Cite|Stop by again sometime!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Ivee}} is heading to her bed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will not be able to ask her any questions. If he speaks to her during this time, she mentions that even though the hour is late, the {{Term|BotW|East Wind}} is still open.<ref>{{Cite|I'm off to bed, but the shop's still open if you wanna take a look around.|Ivee|BotW}}</ref> Even while asleep, she dreams of beckoning patrons into the {{Term|BotW|General Store}}.<ref>{{Cite|<small>The store... Open... Zzz...</small>|Ivee|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|<small>Welcommmrgh... Zzz...</small>|Ivee|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Ivee}} has a {{Term|BotW|Ivee's Diary|link|display= Diary}} sitting on a side table next to her bed. Within, she has written down recipes for three different meals.<ref>{{Cite|Ivee's Recipe Book|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

Like other residents of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Ivee}} has a unique reaction when {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her while carrying the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}. She recognizes the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|display=Flame}} as something {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} used to carry around.<ref>{{Cite|That's the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}}! Grandma Purah used to carry that around all the time!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I know that {{Color|BotW Blue|flame}}! {{Color|BotW Blue|Grandma Purah}} used to carry it around everywhere!|Ivee|BotW}}</ref>
