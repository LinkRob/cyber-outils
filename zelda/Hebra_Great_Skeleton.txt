The {{Term/Store|Hebra Great Skeleton||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hebra Great Skeleton'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Hebra Great Skeleton is located in the [[Hebra Mountains]]. It is one of the three remaining {{Term|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Object)|link|display=skeletons}} of the now-extinct [[Leviathan]]s. Link must take a picture of the remains for the "{{Term|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Side Quest)|link}}" [[Side Quest]].
