[[wikipedia:Dog|'''Dogs''']],<ref>{{Cite|Arrrgh! That is not my dog! How could you mix up my puppy with a mutt like that?!|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=62 {{Exp Game|Series}} }}</ref>{{Name Ref|LANS, BotW}} also known as {{Term/Store|Canine|Canines|TMC|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC |publisher= Nintendo |page= 39}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They usually appear in towns or other populated areas. They make their first appearance in {{LA}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Main|Mutt}}
[[File:LA Mutt Sprite.png|left|frameless]]
Dogs appear as [[Mutt]]s who roam [[Mabe Village]] in their first appearance in {{LA|-}}. Although normally harmless, treated as enemies, they will retaliate at [[Link]] if he attacks them. Mutts can be defeated by using [[Magic Powder]] or the [[Magic Rod]]. [[Chain Chomp]]s, black round animals with dog-like behavior (and also actually enemies from the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Super Mario]]'' franchise), also appear for the first time in this game as pets of [[Madame MeowMeow]], though they technically debuted in {{aLttP}} for the ''Zelda'' series (and ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros._3|Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', overall).

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, dogs are commonly found running around the [[Hyrule Castle Town|Castle Town Market]] and throughout [[Castle Town]]'s back alleys. These dogs are smaller and have shaggy fur, resembling [[wikipedia:Terrier|Terriers]]. One particular dog, [[Richard (Ocarina of Time)|Richard]], is owned by [[Mamamu Yan]], who holds her dog in very high esteem compared to the other mutts of the town.<ref>{{Cite|My precious little puppy's fur coat is very special compared to the other mutts around here!|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My precious puppy can run faster than any of the other mutts around here!|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref> At night, when all the dogs roam the town, Mamamu Yan is unable to find hers, and asks Link to go look for him.<ref>{{Cite|I can't find my puppy anywhere! I need you to look for it! Please!|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref> Richard can be found behind a stall near the entrance to the market. As thanks for returning Richard, Mamamu Yan rewards Link with a [[Piece of Heart]]. 

After awakening seven years later as an adult in the [[Temple of Time]], Hyrule Castle Town is destroyed by [[Ganondorf]] and the dogs are nowhere to be found. Mamamu Yan will have also moved to [[Kakariko Village]] along with the rest of the [[Hylian]]s. After the [[Hero of Time]] successfully gains [[Epona]] and [[Talon]] moves back to [[Lon Lon Ranch]], she will mention that Talon reminds her of her dog, Richard.<ref>{{Cite|That lazy man, he kind of reminded me of my little Richard...|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref> Since Richard is nowhere to be found, and she refers to him in the past tense,<ref>{{Cite|What's that? You say you've never heard of my little Richard? He was such a famous puppy! You seriously haven't heard of him?|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref> it's implied that Richard died in the seven year period.

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM Dog Model 2.png|thumb|A brown dog from {{OoT|-}}/{{MM|-}}]]
The Dogs of [[Termina]] are mostly found at the [[Doggy Racetrack]] near [[Romani Ranch]], operated by Mamamu Yan in {{MM}}, which houses fourteen of the canines. In this [[Mini-Game|mini-game]], Link chooses a certain dog from the kennel that he will bet on in a race. By picking up a dog while using the [[Mask of Truth]], Link can read the thoughts of the animal.<ref>{{Cite|The mask that can see into people's hearts and minds also seems to work on animals as well...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> This is especially useful while choosing a dog for the races, as each one have unique reactions at random. In addition to the white and brown colorations from {{OoT|-}}, dog coats in {{MM|-}} may also be gray, yellow, and even blue. Other dogs include the dog of the [[Cursed Rich Man]] found inside the [[Swamp Spider House]]. If Link reads its mind, it will say his master's eyes are always filled with greed.<ref>{{Cite|Rrr-ruff! Stupid master...His eyes are always filled with greed and desire...|Dog|MM}}</ref> There is also the dog roaming [[South Clock Town]], who constantly thinks of [[Deku Link]].<ref>{{Cite|Ruff-ruff! Where'd that Deku Scrub go? When I see his face, I get all riled up!|Dog|MM}}</ref>

Dogs react differently to each of Link's [[Mask]] [[Transformation|transformations]]. When Link is in Deku form, Dogs will act hostile towards him, growling and barking, and even knocking him over (but will not cause damage). When Link is in [[Goron Link|Goron form]], Dogs will growl at him while stepping back and then run away frightened if approached. When Link is in [[Zora Link|Zora form]], Dogs will give affectionate barks as they chase him, and whine at his feet. However, they pay no mind to the [[Fierce Deity Link|Fierce Deity]], much like in his normal self. If Link performs the march while wearing the [[Bremen Mask]], Dogs will fall in line behind him and march along with him to the music, until he stops playing. If he does this at the Doggy Racetrack, the dog nearest to the young hero will follow him.

It is also mentioned by [[Guru-Guru]] that a dog was the leader of the animal troupe,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that dog was an amazing leader! He always had a stellar troupe no matter what animals he had to work with...|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> and wore the Bremen Mask, which Guru-Guru stole.<ref>{{Cite|That's why I...That's why I... That's why I stole it... The dog's mask. I stole it. I wanted it because it was the leader's mask...|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref>

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Main|Flash}}
A dog named Flash appears in eastern [[Horon Village]] in {{OoS}}. He can be seen fetching a red ball thrown by his master.

==={{OoA|-}}===
One unnamed dog appears as the pet of Mamamu Yan in [[Lynna City]] in {{OoA}}. Mamamu Yan still believes her pet to be much cuter than the rest,<ref>{{Cite|I'm Mamamu Yan, the top breeder in town. My dog is much cuter than that dog! Much cuter!|Mamamu Yan|OoA}}</ref> despite the fact that there are no other dogs in all of [[Labrynna]]. In the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest|link}}, Mamamu Yan will take the [[Doggie Mask]] to hide her dog's face, whom she claims is very shy.<ref>{{Cite|That [[File:OoA Doggie Mask Sprite.png|15px]] mask!!! It fits my dog perfectly! Let me have that [[File:OoA Doggie Mask Sprite.png|15px]] mask!|Mamamu Yan|OoA}}</ref> In return, she gives Link the [[Dumbbell]] that the dog played with.<ref>{{Cite|You deserve the toy that my dog played with!|Mamamu Yan|OoA}}</ref> After receiving a secret from Mamamu Yan's mother in a [[Linked Game]] of {{OoS}}, and telling this secret to Mamamu Yan, she will mention that she has lost her dog somewhere in town and will ask Link to go find it.<ref>{{Cite|My precious dog has gotten lost. He's somewhere in town, so I'd like you to get him. He's so delicate. You carry him properly. There is a huge reward if you find him!|Mamamu Yan|OoA}}</ref> The dog will appear randomly in Lynna City. After finding and returning the dog (who can be lifted using the [[Power Bracelet]]), Mamamu Yan rewards Link with the [[Snowshoe Ring]].<ref>{{Cite|My precious pup! Where were you? Thank you for bringing him home safely! Here is your reward! Don't forget to take it to the jeweler for
appraisal!!!|Mamamu Yan|OoA}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Fifi, Growler, Rolf}}
Several {{Plural|TMC|Dog}} appear in {{TMC}}. {{Term|TMC|Fifi|link}} is a {{Term|TMC|Dog}} owned by {{Term|TMC|Stockwell|link}} and can be found at {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's House|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Stockwell}} claims he is too busy to visit his {{Term|TMC|Dog}},<ref>{{Cite|Stockwell complains that he's been too busy lately to see his dog, Fifi.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> and as such he becomes very lonely.<ref>{{Cite|My master, Stockwell, has been coming home far too late to play with me... I'm so lonely.|Fifi|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can bring him a {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link|display=Bottle}} of {{Term|TMC|Dog Food|link}} as a favor to {{Term|TMC|Stockwell}}, who will let {{Term|TMC|Link}} keep the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle}} afterwards.<ref>{{Cite|If you feed Fifi, then I guess you can have the bottle.|Stockwell|TMC}}</ref>

Other, more mutt-like {{Plural|TMC|Dog}} appear in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. One is {{Term|TMC|Rolf|link}}, a dark-furred {{Term|TMC|Dog}} belonging to {{Term|TMC|Romio|link}}. The other is {{Term|TMC|Growler|link}}, a light-brown {{Term|TMC|Dog}}, who, in the beginning of the game, blocks a staircase leading to the southwestern part of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|display=Town}}.

While in {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can speak to {{Plural|TMC|Dog}} and even {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Fifi}} and {{Term|TMC|Rolf}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
[[File:TP Dog Model.png|right|frameless]]
Dogs can be found in {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle Town|link}} in {{TP}}. Unlike previous games where dogs resemble Terriers, the canines are now based on [[wikipedia:Labrador_Retriever|Labrador Retrievers]]. Dogs can be picked up and played with. Link can even play fetch with them using bones found lying around, much to the dog's enjoyment.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TP Animal Green|I like...to pick things up!}}|Dog|TP}}</ref> If the bone is thrown into the patch of {{Term|TPHD|Grass|link}} next to the {{Term|TPHD|Medical Clinic|link}} or near a {{Term|TPHD|Tree|link}}, the dog may bring back a {{Term|TPHD|Green Rupee|link}} or a {{Term|TPHD|Heart|link}} instead of the bone.

Like [[Cat|cats]], dogs can be spoken to while in {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link|display= Wolf form}}, and will often give {{Term|TPHD|Link}} helpful advice on his quest. One particular dog, owned by {{Term|TPHD|Jaggle|link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Pergie|link}}'s family, gives {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} helpful information as to how to dig and use his senses when he returns to {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}} for the first time in wolf form.

{{Main|Stalhound}}
{{Plural|TPHD|Stalhound|link}}, skeletal dog-like beasts, appear roaming {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Field|link}} at night.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Animal Green|Those}} {{Color|TPHD Red|fiends}} {{Color|TPHD Animal Green|lurk just outside the}} {{Color|TPHD Red|southern gate}}{{Color|TPHD Animal Green|... Unfortunately, they only}} {{Color|TPHD Red|come out}} {{Color|TPHD Animal Green|at}} {{Color|TPHD Red|night}}{{Color|TPHD Animal Green|.}}|Louise|TPHD}}</ref> Link must defeat a pack of {{Plural|TPHD|Stalhound}} in order to recover the {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Statue|link}}, the key to recovering {{Term|TPHD|Ilia|link}}'s memory.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Animal Green|You're looking for the}} {{Color|TPHD Red|wooden statue}} {{Color|TPHD Animal Green|that}} {{Color|TPHD Red|Ilia}} {{Color|TPHD Animal Green|had, aren't you, dear? Think it's a coincidence that I happen to know it got stolen from the doctor's place? No... Yes, I'll admit it...I took it. But then I was attacked by some sort of skeletal}} {{Color|TPHD Red|dog beasts}} {{Color|TPHD Animal Green|who took it from me.}}|Louise|TPHD}}</ref> {{Plural|TPHD|Stalhound}} also appear within the {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Ordeals|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Main|Hylian Retriever, Satty}}
{{Compendium/Extract|Hylian Retriever}}
{{Plural|BotW|Hylian Retriever|link}} are a breed of dog in {{BotW}}. According to the [[Hyrule Compendium]], it is said that all Hylian Retrievers are descendants of the dog once owned by the [[King of Hyrule (Disambiguation)|King of Hyrule]]. These dogs have a unique appearance, as they are not entirely based on a real-world breed.
