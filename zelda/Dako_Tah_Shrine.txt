The {{Term/Store|Dako Tah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Dako Tah Shrine}}<br/>Electric Path}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Electric Path''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
At {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nobiro|link}} will tell {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} a rumor that an ancient civilization hid their treasure in the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm|link}} over the {{Term|BotW|Great Cliffs|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor has it {{Color|BotW Red|an ancient civilization hid a treasure}} inside that sandstorm.|Nobiro|BotW}}</ref> However, despite constantly watching it during the day, he has never seen the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} subside.<ref>{{Cite|I've been here for days waiting for the storm to settle down so I can nab it! I just hope it wasn't a bad lead...|Nobiro|BotW}}</ref> Listening to him initiates "{{Term|BotW|The Eye of the Sandstorm|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

Between 9:00 PM and 4:00 AM, the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} will subside, making it easier for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to navigate the {{Term|BotW|Great Cliffs}}. There, he will find {{Term|BotW|Dako Tah Shrine}}, completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|The treasure sleeping in the sandstorm visible from the Kara Kara Bazaar turned out to be an ancient shrine.<br/><br/>All that is left now is to overcome the hero's trial within.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} {{Term|BotW|Dako Tah|link}} can be heard presenting the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine...I am Dako Tah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Dako Tah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
In {{Term|BotW|Dako Tah Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a long hallway containing a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}} that leads to a room with a big gap. An {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link|display=Electric}} current runs from across the gap to a switch near {{Term|BotW|Link}}, which powers a moving platform that will take him across the gap. On the other side of the gap, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a metal cube that is powering the platform switch.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} to power the switch from across the gap to get across with the cube. He must then carry the cube to the other switch in the room, which will raise the platform that the switch is on. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Moonlight Scimitar|link}} by stopping the platform before it reaches the top. 

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has reached the top, he will find a new room containing two {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout I}} and another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} with an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Core|link}} inside. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must push the big metal cube in the wall with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} to continue forward. Hitting a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}} in the back of the room will activate a moving platform, which serves as a shortcut to and from the bottom floor. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} looks under this platform after it moves up, he will find a small area with a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.

After, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must carry the cube up another moving platform, where he must place the cube on a circular slot. This will activate a moving platform that will take {{Term|BotW|Link}} across the room. On the way there, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must push a big metal cube forward and out of the way of the platform. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain another {{Term|BotW|Treasure}} Chest containing a {{Term|BotW|Radiant Shield|link}} by moving the big metal cube backwards after the platform has passed it and jumping to its location.

Once the platform has reached the end, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Dako Tah}} waiting for him at the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. As a reward for {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s resourcefulness, {{Term|BotW|Dako Tah}} gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Dako Tah|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} luck before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Dako Tah|BotW}}</ref>

After completing {{Term|BotW|Dako Tah Shrine}}, the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} over the {{Term|BotW|Great Cliffs}} will disappear.
