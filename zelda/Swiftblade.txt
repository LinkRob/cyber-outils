{{Term/Store|Swiftblade||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Swiftblade}} is the second eldest of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}. His dojo, the {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade School|link}}, is located in the southwest corner of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. Unlike the other {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|display=Brothers}}, he teaches four separate {{Plural|TMC|Sword Technique|link}}: the {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack|link}},<ref>{{Cite|We shall start with the most basic of all techniques! I shall teach you...the {{Color|TMC Red|Spin Attack}}!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Rock Breaker|link}},<ref>{{Cite|You will now learn a new technique, the {{Color|TMC Red|Dash Attack}}!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Dash Attack|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I will now teach you a technique called the Rock Breaker, so pay attention!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref> and {{Term|TMC|Down Thrust|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now I will teach you a new technique, the {{Color|TMC Red|Down Thrust}}!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref> In order to learn the latter three {{Plural|TMC|Sword Technique|display=Techniques}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must obtain the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Come again when you have {{Color|TMC Red|a new sword}} in hand!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Come again when you learn to {{Color|TMC Blue|run like the wind}}!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref> and {{Term|TMC|Roc's Cape|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Come again when {{Color|TMC Blue|you can jump farther}} than any other!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref> respectively.

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Postman|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade}} will begin writing the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I know! I should start a newsletter! I can reach a lot more people that way.|Stamp|TMC}}</ref> each issue of which is sold by {{Term|TMC|Marcy|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}} for 200 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.
