{{Term/Store|Leef||Series, ALBW}} is the name of one of the 20 possible {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} that can appear in {{ALBW}} without being met by {{Term|Series|StreetPass|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's Leef!<br/>This {{Color|ALBW Blue|Shadow Link}} has come via {{Color|ALBW Red|StreetPass}} and awaits battle!|Shadow Link|ALBW}}</ref>{{Note|All Shadow Links claim to have come via StreetPass, even when they are pre-generated.}}

==Overview==
Once {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} has spoken to {{Term|ALBW|Gramps|link}} and enabled {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link Battle|link}}, a new signpost signifying the arrival of a {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} will appear at the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Meadow|link}} after every five hours of gameplay, regardless of whether or not the player has received a {{Term|Series|StreetPass|link}} tag. {{Term|ALBW|Leef}} is the most powerful of the pre-generated {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link}} who can be met by this method.

{{Term|ALBW|Leef}} wears the {{Term|ALBW|Red Mail|link}} and wields the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword Lv3|link}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Hylian Shield|link}}, and is the only pre-generated {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}} to have learned the {{Term|ALBW|Great Spin|link}}. He is tied with {{Term|ALBW|Slatt|link}} for having the largest {{Term|ALBW|Life Gauge|link}} (20 {{Plural|ALBW|Heart}}), has the most Medals won (49), possesses the {{Term|ALBW|Pegasus Boots|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Bee Badge|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Stamina Scroll|link}}, and has the highest overall bounty at the maximum 999 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}. He carries a {{Term|ALBW|Nice Hookshot|link}} and a {{Term|ALBW|Nice Hammer|link}} into battle.
