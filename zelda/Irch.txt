{{Term/Store|Irch||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''''Irch'''''|book= E |publisher= |page= 259{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Irch}}
Irch is a [[Korok]] that wears a long leaf mask. His unique mask consists of a triangle mouth, oval right eye and crescent moon under a triangle left eye.

Irch lives in the [[Forest Haven]]. Before the Forest Ceremony, Irch can be found standing on a bough of the Great Deku Tree along the Baba Bud path. After the Forest Ceremony, Irch goes to the [[Shark Island]] to plant new [[Tree]]s.

==Trivia==
*Several unnamed Koroks resembling him, and one named [[Daz]] with a design similar to Irch, appear in {{BotW}}.
