The {{Term/Store|Pineapple||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an {{Term|LANS|Item|link}} in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 134}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|LANS|Pineapple}} is the seventh item in the game's {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You exchanged {{Icon|LADX Honeycomb}} for {{Icon|LADX Pineapple}}! It's not as sweet, but it is delicious! |N/A|LADX}}</ref> It is received from {{Term|LANS|Chef Bear|link}} in {{Term|LANS|Animal Village|link}} after giving her the {{Term|LANS|Honeycomb|link}} for her dish.<ref>{{Cite|Hi ho! Hey you! Is that possibly a {{Icon|LADX Honeycomb}} you have? I just ran out! Will you swap it for a pineapple? |Chef Bear|LADX}}</ref>

The {{Term|LANS|Pineapple}} is later given to {{Term|LANS|Papahl|link}} in {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Heights|link}} when he asks {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} to give him something to eat.<ref>{{Cite|Yep, Papahl got lost, just like he said! Now, I am so famished I can't move! Can you give me some vittles? |Papahl|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This {{Icon|LADX Pineapple}} is so delicious! I'm going to eat the {{Icon|LADX Pineapple}} right now! Bon Appetit! |Papahl|LADX}}</ref> As thanks, {{Term|LANS|Papahl}} gives him the {{Term|LANS|Hibiscus|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|AH! This isn't meant to be a reward... Here, take this {{Icon|LADX Hibiscus}}! It's a hibiscus! |Papahl|LADX}}</ref>
