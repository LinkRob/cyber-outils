==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|ALBW|Rented Bomb}} are used to blow up cracked walls, as well as damage {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}} and {{Plural|ALBW|Shock Switch|link}}. They are rentable from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}} for fifty {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.<br/><br/>Being rented {{Plural|ALBW|Item}}, the {{Plural|ALBW|Rented Bomb}} will be returned to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio}} by {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} falls in battle. They may be permanently obtained as {{Plural|ALBW|Bomb|link}} for 800 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, and further upgraded into {{Plural|ALBW|Nice Bomb|link}} by {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}.
