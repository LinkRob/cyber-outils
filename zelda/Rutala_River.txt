{{Term/Store|Rutala River||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Rutala River'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Rutala River is a river that begins at the [[Rutala Dam]] and spans through the [[Lanayru Great Spring]] to the [[Lanayru Wetlands]].

==Nomenclature==
Rutala River derives its name from [[Rutela]], the late Zora queen from {{TP}}.
