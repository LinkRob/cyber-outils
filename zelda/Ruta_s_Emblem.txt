{{Term/Store|Ruta's Emblem|Ruta's Emblems|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Ruta's Emblem}}<br/>The commemorative symbol of a water trial's completion by one who wishes to control a Divine Beast. Collecting three emblems will lead to a new challenge.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They are exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can only obtain {{Plural|BotW|Ruta's Emblem}} as part of the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song|link}}". The {{Plural|BotW|Monk|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sato Koda Shrine|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Mah Eliya Shrine|link}} grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} these {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|display=Emblem}} once he completes their trials.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Ruta's Embelm.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> They are commemorative symbols of the completion of a water trial.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has collected all three of {{Plural|BotW|Ruta's Emblem}}, the last {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will instruct him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Ruta...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> There, an unknown {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will take the {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|display=Emblems}} so {{Term|BotW|Link}} can face a challenge in the {{Term|BotW|Illusory Realm|link}} composed of his own memories.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the one who approaches this Divine Beast... In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial. In exchange for Ruta's Emblems... you will be granted the chance to tread through the realm of memories. Those who lack determination will find this trial unforgiving. Do not take this place lightly nor dismiss it as merely a world within your mind. The truth is much deeper than you know.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find himself inside {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, where he will have to face {{Term|BotW|Waterblight Ganon|link}} once more. After doing so, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be allowed to challenge it again at any time.
