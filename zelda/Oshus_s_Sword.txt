{{Term/Store|Oshus's Sword||Series, PH}} is an item in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133}}</ref>{{Note|This item was also formatted as '''Oshus Sword''' in the Prima Games guide.<ref name="Prima">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page=10}}</ref> However, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It is the first Sword obtained by Link.<ref name="Prima"/>

==Location and Uses==
Oshus's Sword is found inside a [[Treasure Chest]] in [[Oshus]]' storehouse, which can be opened using the correct password. [[Ciela]] tells [[Link]] that the password she has seen Oshus use is the number of palm [[tree]]s found on the beach of [[Mercay Island]]. The Sword is nearly identical to the [[Hero's Sword]] from {{TWW}}, except with a blue pommel and a slightly longer blade.

Oshus's Sword is somewhat weak, like all first Swords acquired in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series. However, it has power enough to get Link through most of his adventure. The young hero can power up this Sword more by collecting [[Power Gem]]s and then sailing to [[Spirit Island]] to put them to use on the power [[Fairy]].
