{{Term/Store|Remnants of the Calamity||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 68 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Remnants of the Calamity|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Remnants of the Calamity}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Waterblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Fireblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Thunderblight Ganon|link}}. One of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} spawns next to {{Term|HWAoC|Waterblight Ganon}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon}}, and the other {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} spawns next to the other two. Although, there isn’t anything separating the two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, so they can easily interact with each other. It can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Star Fragment|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Courser Bee Honey|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
