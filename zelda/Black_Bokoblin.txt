{{Term/Store|Black Bokoblin|Black Bokoblins|Series, BotW, CoH}} are [[Enemy|enemies]] in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Black Bokoblin}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Black Bokoblin|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TotK, HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Black Bokoblin}}
Black Bokoblins are a stronger variant of [[Blue Bokoblin]]s. They are much more resilient and are usually armed with stronger Weapon than their common counterparts, including [[Dragonbone Boko Club]]s and [[Dragonbone Boko Bat]]s. Black Bokoblins may be found in monster camps and occasionally on roads where they may attack passing travelers. Black Bokoblins may also sometimes be found going on hunts while riding [[Horse]]s.

Black Bokoblins share an attack pattern with other Bokoblin counterparts. On the ground, Black Bokoblins mainly attack by swinging and slamming their held Weapon. In the event that their Weapon is lost or stolen, they will search for any other available Weapons nearby and use them instead. In some monster camps, Black Bokoblins may also be found standing on top watch towers, where they may alert other monsters to Link's presence by blowing a horn. These Black Bokoblins may also be equipped with [[Bow]]s that they use to snipe intruders with [[Arrow]]s.

When defeated, Black Bokoblins may drop [[Bokoblin Fang]]s, [[Bokoblin Horn]]s, or [[Bokoblin Guts]].

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Stub|TotK}}
