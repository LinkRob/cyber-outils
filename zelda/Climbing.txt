{{Term/Store|Climbing||Series, BotW}} is an ability in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Climbing Gear}}<br/>The ancient technology in this gear will make you a better climber. The special no-slip gloves help you use your energy more efficiently to facilitate nimble climbing.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Stub|BotW}}
In {{BotW}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can scale up most landscapes while climbing. While Climbing, in most scenarios, Link will have his {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} meter to be used up continuously if he climbs, at a linear rate. Link does not use up his stamina while climbing ladders, however, jumping still uses up his stamina, and unlike climbing other surfaces, Link can still climb slowly when his stamina is drained completely. 

Link cannot climb on {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} technology such as {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.
