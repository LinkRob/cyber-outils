==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Stub|ALttP}}

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Blue Bow Soldier}} are normally found in and around {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule Castle|link}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Graveyard|link}}, with some also appearing in the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower|link}}. When one notices {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} nearby, they will ready their {{Term|ALBW|Bow|link}} shoot {{Plural|ALBW|Arrow|link}} at him. {{Plural|ALBW|Blue Bow Soldier}} can be defeated by {{Term|ALBW|Link}} easily, as only a couple of hits from his {{Term|ALBW|Sword|link}} will cause them to fall. Any of {{Term|ALBW|Link}}'s {{Plural|ALBW|Item|link}} will also prove to be effective against the {{Plural|ALBW|Blue Bow Soldier}}.

In the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower}}, seven {{Plural|ALBW|Blue Bow Soldier}} appear on the Seventh Floor of the Intermediate Stage. Four {{Plural|ALBW|Blue Bow Soldier}} also appear on the 27th Floor of the Advanced Stage along with four {{Plural|ALBW|Blue Sword Soldier|link}}. In each of these floors, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must defeat the {{Plural|ALBW|Soldier}} in order to progress to the next floor.
