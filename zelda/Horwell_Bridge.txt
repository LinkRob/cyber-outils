{{Term/Store|Horwell Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Horwell Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Horwell Bridge}} is a wooden bridge that spans the {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Leekah|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} traveler who can be found being attacked by {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}} here, will always appear on this Bridge until the primary {{Term|BotW|Enemy}} terrorizing her have been killed. Other travelers such as {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Endai|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kazul|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Spoone|link}} will also occasionally cross the Bridge.

In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, a metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Serpentine Spear|link}} can be found atop a platform held aloft by {{Plural|BotW|Sky Octorok|link}} south of the Bridge.

Northeast of the bridge is the {{Term|BotW|Floret Sandbar|link}}, where {{Term|BotW|Magda|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|Watch Out for the Flowers|link}}", can be completed for access to the {{Term|BotW|Hila Rao Shrine|link}}.
