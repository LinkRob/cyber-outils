{{Term/Store|EX Champion Revali's Song||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Champion Revali's Song}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Strock Lake}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
After finishing the trials on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator|link}} will split into four glowing orbs and scatter to different areas of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, revealing four ancient structures called {{Plural|BotW|Monument|link}}. One of the {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} appears near {{Term|BotW|Strock Lake|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Cuho Mountain|link}}, south of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Monument}}, a voice belonging to an unspecified {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} speaks to him and instructs him to complete the trials revealed to him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I applaud your courage in coming here. The locations that will be revealed to you are of trials that will enhance the power sleeping within. Do not let your guard down...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Three maps then appear on the {{Term|BotW|Monument}}'s pillars, each revealing the location of a trial that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must undergo.

{{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}, who is standing nearby, makes a remark on either the {{Term|BotW|Monument}} or the flash of light, depending on how many {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} have already been visited.<ref>{{Cite|What was that...flash of light? Perhaps it was my imagination playing tricks on me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Did that {{Color|BotW Blue|stone monument}} just start glowing? I suppose that proves I didn't imagine it the first time.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This {{Color|BotW Blue|stone monument}} has survived for 10,000 years. What secrets does it hold...|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That light... Is it a reaction to someone nearby? Is it me...or is it...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Though he did not initially notice {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hm? How long have you been here? I didn't even notice your arrival!|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} is still happy to see him again.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, we meet again! And it seems you caught me...well, talking to myself.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He points out the {{Term|BotW|Monument}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, explaining that it was mentioned in his teacher's {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}}.<ref>{{Cite|Look at that! There's one here too! One of the {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} mentioned in my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If this is the first {{Term|BotW|Monument}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has visited, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will explain that it was mentioned in an {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}} written by his teacher.<ref>{{Cite|This {{Color|BotW Blue|monument}}... I must admit it has taken me by surprise! My teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}} mentions such {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}}, and now one has appeared before my eyes...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s confusion, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} explains that it is missing its key verses.<ref>{{Cite|[Unfinished song?] Yes. The song is mostly complete. However... My teacher left this world without finishing the {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He is now on a journey to finish the song.<ref>{{Cite|I wish the weave the missing pieces together and finish the song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>}} He thinks it is glorious that he is able to sing it in front of a {{Term|BotW|Monument}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, sweet joy! How glorious to sing this song in front of the real {{Color|BotW Blue|monument}}...|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kass}} adds that there is a verse in the song that mentions the trials that {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} overcame,<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Revali}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which he offers to sing it for {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Allow me to sing it for you...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics provide instructions for the three trials {{Term|BotW|Link}} will need to face.<ref>{{Cite|♪Wind's ally soars the land of cloud.<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}'s Champion, swift and proud.♪<br/>♪The hero's power shall grow.<br/>Seek trials {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} show.♪<br/>♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|shoot the flame dragon's horn}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|race down a peak rings adorn}}.♪<br/>♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|shoot four targets to win}}.<br/>Champion, the trials begin!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When he is finished singing, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} was moved by his performance.<ref>{{Cite|...So? Did it move you?|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...So? Did the {{Color|BotW Blue|verse}} move you? It was written in honor of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} challenging the trial.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Learning about the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} in their homes have helped {{Term|BotW|Kass}} with completing the song's key verses.<ref>{{Cite|By asking around about the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champions}} of each region, I'm starting to piece together the {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}} of the song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He is inspired by the confidence it would take to master a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The confidence it must have taken to master a {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}... How inspiring.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}} weighs heavily on him, and so {{Term|BotW|Kass}} has resolved to learn more about the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}}.<ref>{{Cite|My teacher's dream is now mine to fulfill. An {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}} weighs heavily on a bard's soul... I won't allow it. But in order to see this dream to fruition, I must learn more about the Champions of long ago...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}} have been helpful in {{Term|BotW|Kass}}'s search for knowledge,<ref>{{Cite|Thankfully, {{Color|BotW Blue|Teba}} and the {{Color|BotW Blue|Rito Village}} elder, {{Color|BotW Blue|Kaneli}}, told me some wonderful stories of the Rito Champion.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> but he still has much work to do on his journey.<ref>{{Cite|Yet this unfinished song still pulls at my heart. And so, my hometown is but a stopping point on this journey of mine.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When he is finished speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} may complete the trials in any order. The first trial tasks Link with shooting {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link|display=Dinraal's}} horn as it flies over {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}}, revealing {{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar Shrine|link}}. The second trial involves {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}} through the {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} starting near {{Term|BotW|Hebra Peak|link}} and leading down to the {{Term|BotW|Pikida Stonegrove|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes through the final {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Kiah Toza Shrine|link}} will be unearthed. The third trial has {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoot four targets in quick succession at the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|link}}, which causes {{Term|BotW|Noe Rajee Shrine|link}} to rise up at the bottom of the chasm.

Each of these {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} rewards {{Term|BotW|Link}} with one of {{Plural|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|link}} upon their completion.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Medoh's Embelm.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that collecting all three {{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|display=Emblems}} will unlock a new trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> They will then tell him how many {{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|display=Emblems}} he still has to collect.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> After completing all three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, the third {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will instruct {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Medoh...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}, he is spoken to again by an unspecified {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, who gives him a trial in exchange for the three {{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|display=Emblems}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the one who approaches this Divine Beast... In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial. In exchange for Medoh's Emblems... you will be granted the chance to tread through the realm of memories. Those who lack determination will find this trial unforgiving.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} not to dismiss it as only a world in his mind, as there is a deeper truth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Do not take this place lightly nor dismiss it as merely a world within your mind. The truth is much deeper than you know.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} is then transported to the {{Term|BotW|Illusory Realm|link}}, a realm created by his memories, where he must face a version of {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon|link}} made from his own fear.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|This illusory realm was created from the depths of your memories... The enemy you shall face is a product of the fear that dwells within. This battle is a trial of the soul. You must emerge victorious using only the tools provided.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}}'s equipment, {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} will be changed to the following:

* {{Term|BotW|Feathered Edge|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Falcon Bow|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Duplex Bow|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Phrenic Bow|link}}
* 100 {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}
* Five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Wildberry|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}}

After defeating the phantasm, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is transported back and finds {{Term|BotW|Kass}} nearby. He believes that meeting {{Term|BotW|Link}} in this location was not a coincidence,<ref>{{Cite|Perhaps meeting you here was no coincidence.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> as he considers it a perfect place to get lost in thought and write songs.<ref>{{Cite|This is a perfect spot for you to get lost in your thoughts, as well as a perfect spot for me to write a song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Learning about {{Term|BotW|Revali}} has inspired {{Term|BotW|Kass}} to write a song to honor the {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions}} in his own way.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Wandering around here and listening to anecdotes about {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Revali}} has truly invigorated me. In fact, it has inspired me to honor him in my own way, separate from my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}. That is why I felt inclined to roost here, near the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, which {{Color|BotW Blue|Revali}} himself mastered. I've written a song...to try and capture the essence of {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Revali}}...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He sings {{Term|BotW|Champion Revali's Song (Song)|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|♪Recall {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}'s Champion of flight,<br/>before he fell to his fateful plight.♪<br/>♪{{Color|BotW Blue|Revali}}'s feat was a wonder to see,<br/>so the princess made her plea.♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which allows him to visualize the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Revali's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} from 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|Kass's composition, entitled {{Color|BotW Red|Champion Revali's Song}}, was so true and moving that it allowed you to visualize a memory from 100 years ago.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

After the {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} notes that {{Term|BotW|Revali}} did not like sharing his feelings with others.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... It seems the {{Color|BotW Blue|Rito Champion}} was not one to share his feelings.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he thought of the {{Term|BotW|Champion Revali's Song (Song)|display=Song}},<ref>{{Cite|So? What did you think of {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Revali's Song}}?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> explaining that writing it has helped him feel closer to his teacher.<ref>{{Cite|By writing a song that highlights the hidden feelings of Champion Revali, I feel closer than ever to my dear teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} feels confident that he will be able to finish the key verses of the {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}}.<ref>{{Cite|I feel much more confident that I can do those missing lyrics justice.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Revali}} then speaks to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and expresses his shock that he was able to complete the trials, and by doing so, he will be able to use {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale|link}} more often.<ref>{{Cite|I truly never imagined you would do so well. Regardless, Revali's Gale should be more efficient than ever.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> The ability is upgraded to {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale +|link}}, which reduces its recharge time to two minutes. As he is, {{Term|BotW|Revali}} believes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} could theoretically defeat {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|As you are, I guess it's theoretically possible for you to best Ganon.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} will be completed.
