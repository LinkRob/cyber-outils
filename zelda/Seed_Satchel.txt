==Location and uses==
{{Manual|OoS|Seed Satchel|The Seed Satchel holds your Mystical Seeds. Set the Seed Satchel to the A or B Button to use the seeds.}}
{{Manual|OoA|Seed Satchel|The Seed Satchel holds your Mystical Seeds. Set the Seed Satchel to the A or B Button to use the seeds.}}
In {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}, the Seed Satchel carries the [[Mystical Seed]]s found throughout [[Holodrum]] and [[Labrynna]]. In {{OoS|-}}, it is the dungeon item of [[Gnarled Root Dungeon]], while in {{OoA|-}}, the [[Maku Tree]] gives it to Link in the present after he rescues her in the past. In both cases, the bag is filled with [[Ember Seed]]s upon obtaining it. The four other kinds of Seeds ([[Mystery Seed|Mystery]], [[Scent Seed|Scent]], [[Pegasus Seed|Pegasus]], and [[Gale Seed|Gale]]) can be found later in both games.

The Seed Satchel starts with the capability of holding 20 Seeds of each type. In both games, the Seed Satchel can be upgraded twice, once by doing something within the game itself and once via a [[Linking Secret|Secret]]. The two upgrades can be performed in either order; upgrading the Satchel once allows it to hold 50 Seeds of each type, and upgrading it a second time allows it to hold 99 of each. In {{OoS|-}}, the Satchel can be upgraded by purchasing an upgrade in the basement of [[Horon Village Shop]]. In {{OoA|-}}, the Satchel can be upgraded by talking to [[Tingle]] again some time after he gives Link the [[Island Chart]]. If Link has not chosen [[Ricky]] as his animal companion, he will need the [[Seed Shooter]] to access Tingle a second time.

The two secrets that upgrade the Seed Satchel are the [[Linking Secret#Tingle Secret|Tingle Secret]] in a linked {{OoS|-}} and the [[Linking Secret#Deku Secret|Deku Secret]] in a linked {{OoA|-}}. In either case, once the password has been used in the non-linked game a return password can be used in the linked game, thus upgrading the Seed Satchel in both games.
