The {{Term/Store|Soothing Rain||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The '''[[Soothing Rain]]''' is the reward for completing the [[Dancing Dragon Dungeon]], and is guarded by [[Gohma]]. It is the fourth of the eight [[Essence of Nature|Essences of Nature]] collected by Link. Its description text reads: "Bathed in drops of Soothing Rain, shoots grow into saplings!"<ref>"Bathed in drops of [[Soothing Rain]], shoots grow into saplings!" – N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]])</ref>
