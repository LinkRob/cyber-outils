{{Term/Store|Mountain Doe|Mountain Does|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Mountain Doe}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Mountain Doe}}
A Mountain Doe is a large female [[deer]] covered in shaggy brown fur speckled with light spots along the back. It is a quadruped that stands on thin, muscular legs, each one ending in a cloven hoof. The Doe’s face has drab fur running down its snout and thick brows of the same color above its small, beady eyes. Its rear and tail are covered in fluffy white fur that appear prominent when the Doe is running.

Mountain Doe are female counterparts to {{Plural|BotW|Mountain Buck|link}}. They can be distinguished from them by their lack of antlers and drab-colored chests. Doe can be found throughout deciduous areas in [[Hyrule]], usually in the presence of a Buck, suggesting they are maintained in harems. The [[Hyrule Compendium]] notes [[Hyrule Field]] and the [[Lanayru Great Spring]] as areas where Mountain Doe are common, but in actuality they are native to most regions, including [[Faron Province|Faron]] and [[Akkala]]. Despite their size, Mountain Doe are skittish animals with very sensitive ears: the slightest detection of sound causes them to freeze in place before abruptly running away. When idle, Doe can be seen grazing and may even lie down to rest. They have a preference for [[apple]]s and tend to drop their guard when eating them.<ref>{{Cite|They're very timid animals by nature, but they tend to let their guard down when eating apples, a favorite food.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

If [[Link]] is stealthy enough, he may be able to mount and ride a Mountain Doe as if it were a [[Horse|wild horse]]. Though fast, their utility is limited and they cannot be registered at the [[stable]]s. A successful hunt on a Mountain Doe supplies Link with [[Raw Prime Meat]], but their skittishness makes this difficult. The challenge is made most apparent in the [[Deer Hunting]] {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} maintained by {{Term|BotW|Dantz|link}}, found south of [[Hateno Village]]. When Link completes this {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} will award him with [[Rupee]]s.

==Trivia==
* The Mountain Doe is one of nine ridable animals in {{BotW|-}}, with the other eight being the [[Horse]], the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|White Horse|link}}, the [[Lord of the Mountain]], the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Buck|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse|link}}, the [[Honeyvore Bear]], and the [[Grizzlemaw Bear]].
** Of these mounts, the Mountain Doe is one of six that cannot be registered at the stables.
* The Mountain Doe and the Mountain Buck are the only creatures to have gender-specific entries in the [[Hyrule Compendium]].
* The Mountain Doe strikingly resembles and is likely based on the female {{wp|sika deer}}, a large Japanese forest-dwelling deer with spots on its back.
