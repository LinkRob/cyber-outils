The {{Term/Store|Golden Freight Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Golden Freight Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Golden Freight Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Regal Ring|link}}, four {{Plural|ST|Pearl Necklace|link}} and one {{Term|ST|Mystic Jade|link}}. Its design features embossed gold panels with wooden trim, and its description reads "This freight car can double as an extravagant bathtub."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Golden Freight Car}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Golden Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to eight Hearts.
