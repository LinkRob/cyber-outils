{{Term/Store|EX Alert: Goponga Village||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 78 {{Term|HWAoC|EX Vicious Monster Battle|link}} in {{HWAoC}} included in the second [[Downloadable Content]] pack, {{GoR}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Alert: Goponga Village|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
To complete the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} defeat the vicious monsters that appear as soon as it starts. {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} can also be found around the map, while three {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} appear in different spots as a {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} approaches the area. While the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} fights the vicious monsters, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, and two {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} will appear as well and attack them.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the vicious monsters are defeated, and can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of two {{Plural|HWAoC|Luminous Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Goat Butter|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Fresh Milk|link}}, 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
