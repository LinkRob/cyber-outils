==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TPHD|The Way of Sumo, Part II}} is found within the basement of {{Term|TPHD|Mayor Bo|link|display=Mayor Bo's}} house. Subtitled ''Grasping Victory: Alpha Rule'', it is the second in a series of three instructional posters on {{Term|TPHD|Sumo Wrestlin'|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|The Way of Sumo, Part II|display=''Part II''}} details the "rock-paper-scissors" structure of {{Term|TPHD|Sumo Wrestlin'}}–grabbing, sidestepping, and slapping.

The poster is attached to a rough plank board near the Sumo ring.
