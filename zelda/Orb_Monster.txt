{{Term/Store|Orb Monster|Orb Monsters|Series, LADX|plural}} are enemies in {{LA|DX}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 196}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Orb Monster}} were referred to as '''Karakoro''' in {{Guide|LADX|Prima}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LADX |publisher= Prima |page= 98}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|LANS|Orb Monster}} are found exclusively in the {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon|link}}. After being hit with a {{Term|LANS|Sword|link}}, they can be picked up by using the {{Term|LANS|Power Bracelet|link}}. {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} must throw the {{Plural|LANS|Orb Monster}} into holes matching their respective colors and shapes.{{Note|In {{LA|NS}}, both {{Plural|LANS|Orb Monster}} and their corresponding holes bear geometric shapes to assist with {{Wp|color blindness}}. This feature was not present in {{LA|DX}}. Yellow-colored {{Plural|LANS|Orb Monster}} also replaced {{Plural|LADX|Green Orb Monster}} to assist with red-green color blindness.}} When all of them are placed correctly, they vanish while spawning either a {{Term|LADX|Treasure Chest|link}} or a {{Term|LADX|Small Key|link}}.
