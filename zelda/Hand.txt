The {{Term/Store|Hand||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Hand appears in the toilet of an outhouse in [[Lynna Village]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 67}}</ref> The Hand is in need of paper and asks [[Link]] for some. Link can give the Hand the [[Stationery]] as part of the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest|link}}. In exchange, the Hand will give him the [[Stink Bag]], the next item in the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest}}.

Link can throw various items into the toilet, including the [[Pot]] next to it, to cause different reactions from the Hand.<ref>{{Cite|Wh-What?!?|Hand|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ooo... hot!|Hand|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ewwww!|Hand|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My head spins!|Hand|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Koff!|Hand|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Nooooo!|Hand|OoA}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The concept of a hand in the toilet is based on Japanese ghost stories where a hand grabs from beneath the toilet whenever someone sits in the bathroom.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= It's actually something from a Japanese ghost story. Not a specific ghost story. There are some ghost stories in Japan where — when you are sitting in the bathroom in the traditional style of the Japanese toilet — a hand is actually starting to grab you from beneath.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= https://ew.com/article/2012/02/10/shigeru-miyamoto-zelda/|title= Shigeru Miyamoto talks 'Legend of Zelda',' 'Mariokart'}}</ref>
