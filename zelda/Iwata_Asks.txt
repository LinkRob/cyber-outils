'''Iwata Asks''' (社長が訊く ''Shachō ga kiku'', lit. ‘The President Asks’) is an interview series conducted by [[Satoru Iwata]], the fourth president and CEO of [[Nintendo]], where he would interview various designers, artists, programmers, and other game developers about the creation process of many Nintendo games from 2006 to 2015.<ref>https://www.nintendo.co.jp/corporate/links/index.html</ref><ref>https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/</ref>

The interview series was transcribed on Nintendo's corporate website. It is organized chronologically and categorized by game platform including [[Nintendo DS]], [[Wii]], [[Nintendo 3DS]], and [[Wii U]] titles. There are eleven interviews about [[The Legend of Zelda (Series)|Zelda]] games including {{TP}} (2006), {{ST}} (2009), {{SS}} (2010), {{OoT3D}} (2011), {{TWWHD}} (2013), {{ALBW}} (2013), and {{MM3D}} (2015).

==Interviews==

In each interview, Iwata asks the directors about their experience and challenges facing development of various Zelda games spanning between 2006 and 2015. Typically, the subject matter would be the creation and development process of the game itself, usually done around the time of the game's release.

===''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess'' (2006)===
The first Zelda-related interview of ''Iwata Asks'' about {{TP|nolink}} from 2006 features [[Yoshiyuki Oyama]], [[Keisuke Nishimori]], [[Koji Kitagawa]], and [[Atsushi Miyagi]].

===''The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks'' (2009)===
This 2009 interview about {{ST|nolink}} features [[Eiji Aonuma]], and Iwamoto.

===''The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword'' (2011)===
This 2011 interview about {{SS|nolink}} features Eiji Aonuma, [[Hidemaro Fujibayashi]], [[Ryuji Kobayashi]], and [[Ryo Tanaka]].

===''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D'' (2011)===
There are five interviews about {{OoT3D}}. They feature [[Koji Kondo]] and [[Mahito Yokota]]; [[Toshio Iwasaki]], Eiji Aonuma, [[Takumi Kawagoe]], [[Yoshiaki Koizumi]], and [[Toru Osawa]]; [[Kazuaki Morita]], [[Makoto Miyanaga]], [[Satoru Takizawa]], [[Yoshihiki Haruhana]], and Eiji Aonuma; Eiji Aonuma, [[Takao Shimizu]], [[Nagako Ikuta]], [[Shun Moriya]], [[Takashi Tonooka]], and [[Koichi Ishii]]; and [[Shigeru Miyamoto]].

===''The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD'' (2013)===
This 2013 interview about {{TWWHD}} features [[Masanao Arimoto]], [[Takuhiro Dohta]], [[Satoru Takizawa]], [[Daiki Iwamoto]], and Eiji Aonuma.

===''The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds'' (2013)===
This 2013 interview about {{ALBW}} features Koji Takahashi, [[Kentaro Tominaga]], [[Shiro Mouri]], [[Hiromasa Shikata]], and Eiji Aonuma.

===''The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D'' (2015)===
The final Zelda-related interview from 2015 about {{MM3D}} features [[Tomomi Sano]], [[Tomohiro Yamamura]], Eiji Aonuma, and [[Mikiharu Ooiwa]].
