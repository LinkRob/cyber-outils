'''Nintendo Magazine (Winter 2021)''' is a [[:Category:Magazines|magazine]] published by [[Nintendo]]. It is the fourth issue in the {{NM}} series and is the first issue to include excerpts translated to English alongside the original Japanese version.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo |published= December 14, 2021 |retrieved= March 10, 2022 |url= https://www.nintendo.com/sg/news/article/NgYngT6SokRkbg1ny3S7Q |title= Excerpts from the Japanese version of the Nintendo Magazine 2021 Winter (Digital edition) is available in English |site= Nintendo |type= }}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Excerpts from the Japanese version of the Nintendo Magazine 2021 Winter (Digital edition) is now available in English. |author= Nintendo |published= December 9, 2021 |retrieved= March 10, 2022 |url= https://twitter.com/nintendo/status/1469138070612365315 |title= |site= Twitter |type= }}</ref> The translated English excerpts only include 24 pages out of the 100 pages in the Japanese release.
