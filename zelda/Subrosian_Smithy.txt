The {{Term/Store|Subrosian Smithy||Series}} is a location in {{OoS}}.{{Name Ref|OoS}} It is the only metalworking place in [[Subrosia]], or even [[Holodrum]].

==Features and Overview==
While at the metalwork shop, [[Link]] can get the workers to combine the [[Hard Ore]] and [[Wooden Shield]] in order to create the [[Iron Shield]], and later, to get the [[Rusty Bell]] polished so that it will become the [[Pirate's Bell]]. If Link already has the Iron Shield, the shield the Subrosians create will be the [[Mirror Shield]]. This can only occur in a [[Linked Game]]. 

The smiths are somewhat idiosyncratic—when Link asks them to fix the bell, they refuse the request unless he is forceful, but when he asks them to fix the shield, they refuse the request unless he is lackadaisical.
