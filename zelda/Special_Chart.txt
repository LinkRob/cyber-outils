{{Term/Store|Special Chart|Special Charts|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are items in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= '''Special Charts'''<br/>Sometimes you'll pull up a chart that doesn't point the way to treasure. Some of them show you how  many items you need to find, some are essential to completing the game. and one is a bizarre crayon drawing by everyone's favorite oddball — Tingle|game= TWW|guide= Nintendo|page= 114}}</ref> These 12 Special Charts give specific information to Link as to where to find the locations of the [[Great Fairy|Great Fairies]], the [[Big Octo]]s, and so on. Each of the Special Charts are listed below with their description as well as where to find each one.

The chart allows Link to track the movements of the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship|link}}; it shows what island the Ghost Ship is at during each phase of the moon. With this, he will be able to enter the Ghost Ship to obtain a [[Triforce Chart]]. Curiously, the man who made the map mysteriously died as soon he had finished it.<ref>{{Cite|The rumors I've heard tell of a man who researched the reaches of the sea where the Ghost Ship faded in and out of sight, and that he drew a map of its movements. ==Trivia==
*The Island Hearts Chart [[Mistakes in The Legend of Zelda Series|erroneously]] indicates that there are nine [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]] in [[Windfall Island]]. In actuality, there are only ''eight'' of them. The accidentally-added piece is actually found in [[Greatfish Isle]], as a result of concluding the [[Island Trading Sequence]] (by giving the [[Decorations|Shop Guru Statue]] to the [[Goron#The Wind Waker|Goron Merchant]]). The addition of this piece in Windfall (and not in Greatfish) is probably due to the sequence actually starting there.
