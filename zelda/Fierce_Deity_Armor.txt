==Location and Uses==
==={{TFH|-}}===
The {{Term|TFH|Fierce Deity Armor}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that was added in the Ver. 2.0.0 update of {{TFH}}. It is obtained by completing the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}} for the first time. When worn, the {{Term|TFH|Fierce Deity Armor}} will double the power of {{Term|TFH|Link|link}}'s {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} and also allow {{Term|TFH|Link}} to shoot four {{Plural|TFH|Sword Beam|link}} in a cross shape when the {{Term|TFH|Sword}} is swung, provided his {{Term|TFH|Heart Meter|link}} is full. The {{Plural|TFH|Sword Beam}} can be alternatively be fired from the {{Term|TFH|Sword}} when performing a {{Term|TFH|Spin Attack|link}}, even when the {{Term|TFH|Heart Meter}} is not full. Additionally, {{Term|TFH|Link}} will remain steadfast at all times and won't get knocked back from [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]] while wearing the {{Term|TFH|Fierce Deity Armor}}.

Any {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} who join the game through [[Download Play]] will be unable to obtain the {{Term|TFH|Fierce Deity Armor}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Armor}} is a piece of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is obtained by scanning the {{MM|-}} {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} [[amiibo]] with the {{Term|BotW|amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. According to its description, the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Armor}} is the same armor as the one worn by {{Term|MM3D|Fierce Deity Link|link}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Era of the Hero of Time|link}}.<ref name="BotW Inventory"/> When worn, the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Armor}} provides one level of Attack Up. The {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Armor}} can be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}, but it cannot be {{Term|BotW|Dye|link}}d at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Armor}}, alongside the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Boots|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Mask|link}}, compose the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Set|link}}.
