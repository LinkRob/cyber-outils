The {{Term/Store|Mask of Scents||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a [[mask]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 131}}</ref> It resembles the head of a [[pig]], and is used to increase [[Link]]'s sense of smell.

==Location and Uses==
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That is the {{Color|MM Red|Mask of Scents}}, isn't it?</p><p>You've done well to get that mask. Looking at its condition, I can tell that its previous owner was neat and tidy.</p>}}

The Mask of Scents is given to [[Link]] by the [[Deku Butler]] after finishing his race. After clearing the [[Woodfall Temple]] and bringing the [[Deku Princess]] back to the [[Deku Palace]], Link can talk to the Deku Butler, who is waiting to meet the hero in the [[Deku Shrine]] outside of the palace.<ref>{{Cite|It seems our butler has taken somewhat of a liking to you, Mr. Link. I believe you'll find him waiting in the shrine that will be to your right when you leave the palace.|Deku Princess|MM}}</ref> Once there, he will ask the young [[Hero of Time]] to race him. If Link manages to keep up, the Deku Butler will say how it reminds him of racing with his [[Deku Butler's Son|son]], and then gives him the Mask of Scents in return.<ref>{{Cite|Actually, when I see you, I am reminded of my son who left home long ago... Somehow, I feel as if I am once again racing with my son...|Deku Butler|MM}}</ref> The mask was also originally owned by his son.<ref>{{Cite|My son often used that mask to find mushrooms in the forest. Oh, how I long for those days.|Deku Butler|MM}}</ref>

When worn, the mask imbues Link with the ability to sniff for the [[Scents|scent]] of [[Mushroom#Majora's Mask|Magic Mushrooms]], which can be given to [[Kotake]] to create [[Blue Potion]]s.<ref>{{Cite|What's that? You want a Blue Potion, do you? Well, you gave me a mushroom, so I'll give you one for free.|Kotake|MM}}</ref> Magic Mushrooms appear as clouds of purple mist and can be scooped into a [[Bottle]].<ref>{{Cite|The power of this mask allows you to see what you can smell...|Tatl|MM}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Like the [[Transformation|transformation masks]], [[Link]]'s voice changes when he wears the Mask of Scents. Humorously, he snorts and grunts similarly to a porcine or [[Moblin]] when the hero rolls, attacks with his [[Sword]], taking damage (except for being attacked by [[ReDead|ReDeads]] or [[Gibdo|Gibdos]]), or falling from a great height.[[File:Star Fox Zero Pigma.webp|thumb|The Mask of Scents shares a resemblance to [[starfox:Star_Wolf|Star Wolf]] member Pigma Dengar from the ''Star Fox'' series, as seen in ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_Zero|Star Fox Zero]]'']]
*Coincidentally, the second row of the [[Inventory|Mask Subscreen]], the [[Keaton Mask]], [[Bremen Mask]], [[Bunny Hood]], [[Don Gero's Mask]], and Mask of Scents are said to represent ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_(series)|Star Fox]]'' characters, specifically [[starfox:Fox_McCloud|Fox McCloud]], [[starfox:Falco_Lombardi|Falco Lombardi]], [[starfox:Peppy_Hare|Peppy Hare]], [[starfox:Slippy_Toad|Slippy Toad]], and [[starfox:Pigma_Dengar|Pigma Dengar]], respectively.
*The mask's design is based on specially-trained real-world pigs that are used to sniff for {{wp|truffles}}, a rare fungus.
