==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Stub|OoT}}

==={{MM|-}}===
The Great Fairy of Magic is situated in [[West Clock Town]] in a cave next to the Deku Playground. Unlike the other Great Fairies, There is only one [[Stray Fairy]] that you need to find. During the day, the stray fairy can be found floating over the water by the [[Laundry Pool]]. During the night, the stray fairy can be found flying over the main part of [[East Clock Town]].
