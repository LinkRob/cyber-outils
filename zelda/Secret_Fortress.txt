{{Term/Store|Secret Fortress||Series, TFH}} is a Level in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=159 }}</ref> It is the first Level in the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}} area.

==Themes and Navigation==
The Level takes place in a large, open lake that has a fortress built within. The Level introduces the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|link}}, which is essential to crossing the Level's waters.

In '''{{Term|TFH|Stage|link}} 1''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} enter the Level from the base a large hill next to a rushing waterfall, and climb up several ledges to the top. Three {{Plural|TFH|Water Rod}} provided on the ledges make climbing easy work, as the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can use it to create pillars of water to propel them up. At the top of the hill are three lookout posts with three {{Plural|TFH|Switch|link}}, watched by a pair of {{Plural|TFH|Moblin|link}}. Using the {{Plural|TFH|Water Rod}}, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} press the {{Plural|TFH|Switch}} to activate a bridge to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}}.

In '''{{Term|TFH|Stage}} 2''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} appear at the lakeside with the fortress on the other side. Its gate must be opened by hitting the two {{Plural|TFH|Switch}} on its sides. These {{Plural|TFH|Switch}} can be reached by creating bridges of water with the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}}. One {{Term|TFH|Switch}} is over the water to the right of the fortress gate, and another is on a grassy island inhabited by a {{Term|TFH|Moblin}}. Striking both {{Plural|TFH|Switch}} lowers the fortress gate, which the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can then enter by creating more water bridges. Inside the fortress walls, streams of fire blast through cannons and prevent the {{Term|TFH|Links}}' approach. These flames can be temporarily extinguished by creating a water pillar in their path. Another stream of fire lies in the path of one of two switches. This must also be doused with water in order for the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to press the {{Plural|TFH|Switch}} to lower another gate. A final stream of fire blocks the waterway outside, which has a pair of spinning {{Plural|TFH|Guruguru Bar|link}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must cross this water way with the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}} and avoid the flames to get to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}}.

'''{{Term|TFH|Stage}} 3''' puts the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} at the beginning of a river bridged by docks. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can cross the river to these docks using the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}}. One of the docks has {{Plural|TFH|Bomb Flower|link}} growing, which must be thrown at a pair of opening and closing {{Plural|TFH|Switch}} ahead. This opens a gate to two giant, cracked blocks barricading the passage. Using the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}}, at least two {{Plural|TFH|Link|link}} must work together to carry a {{Term|TFH|Bomb Flower}} to demolish the blocks. Destroying them opens the way to a {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}}. Alternately, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can simply {{Term|TFH|Bomb|link}} one block and cross over the other using the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}}.

In '''{{Term|TFH|Stage}} 4''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} appear on a dock over a rushing waterfall beneath them. The fast-moving water will push the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} off should any of them fall in. The dock leads to a switch in the center of several small platforms. Pressing it with all three {{Plural|TFH|Links}} summons a horde of {{Plural|TFH|Moblin}} and {{Plural|TFH|Keeleon|link}}, and the dock will break apart. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must defeat all the {{Plural|TFH|Enemy|link}}, by using the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}} to reach them on the platforms they stand on. The {{Plural|TFH|Keeleon}} can likewise also be stunned with the rod. Narrow pathways will emerge from the depths once most of the {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}} are defeated. After all {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}} are defeated, a platform with the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} will rise.

==={{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge}}===
Secret Fortress contains the following {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}}: "{{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|section=Pop all balloons!|display=Pop all balloons!|link}}", "{{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|section=Clear with halved energy!|display=Clear with halved energy|link}}", and "{{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|section=Evade the Wallmaster!|display=Evade the Wallmaster!|link}}".

In "Evade the Wallmaster!", a {{Term|TFH|Wallmaster|link}} will continuously follow the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} throughout the entire Level and must be avoided. If it slams onto one of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}, it will instantly KO them. It can be defeated, but will soon respawn.

==={{Plural|TFH|Material}}===
At the end of the Level, three {{Term|TFH|Treasure Chest|display=chests|link}} can be opened. Two contain the same {{Term|TFH|Material|link}}, and one contains another.
