{{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos Arm}} are the severed arms of {{Plural|BotW|Stalizalfos|link}}, and they can only be acquired by defeating them. When thrown, they will act as {{Plural|BotW|Boomerang|link}}, and they will eventually loop back around to return to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. They continue to move and struggle, even after being disconnected, and they are very brittle.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made of bone, {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos Arm}} will float if they are dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They also do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, nor will they burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
