{{Term/Store|Juney||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Juney|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Juney}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman on her honeymoon in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}.<ref name="Honeymoon"/> She spends her days sitting on the ledge between {{Term|BotW|Kaneli's House|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}}'s home. During the night, she sleeps at the {{Term|BotW|Swallow's Roost|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|*SNOOOOOOORE*|Juney|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Juney}}, she will be shocked to see another {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} in such a remote location, and she asks if he is wandering around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} in order to find himself.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa... I just realized that you're {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}}! You just wandering around Hyrule? Trying to find yourself?|Juney|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Juney}} explains that she is visiting {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} for her honeymoon, but she is not pleased with it.<ref>{{Cite|I'm here on my honeymoon. But this town is seriously a hole.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> She believes that {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} would be more interesting, as {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} only makes her want to return home.<ref>{{Cite|In Gerudo Town, they've got jewelry to buy. Zora's Domain is always a hoot with all of the weird, spiritual fish people... But here...I just want to go home already.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Juney}} is angry at her husband, {{Term|BotW|Jogo|link}}, for choosing this as their honeymoon destination.<ref>{{Cite|[Oh, really...] Give me a break already! I can't believe my husband brought me here for our honeymoon...|Juney|BotW}}</ref> Though he has tried to apologize, {{Term|BotW|Juney}} threatened to divorce him if he did not bring her a {{Term|BotW|Baked Apple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|He already apologized, but if he doesn't want a divorce, I told him he'd better bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apple}}.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> After this, "{{Term|BotW|The Apple of My Eye|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

To complete the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must bring a {{Term|BotW|Baked Apple}} to {{Term|BotW|Juney}}.<ref>{{Cite|Distracting her from her troubles with a {{Color|BotW Red|baked apple}}, her favorite snack, is one way to lighten the mood.<br/><br/>Bring Juney a {{Color|BotW Red|baked apple}} and save her marriage!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her with a {{Term|BotW|Baked Apple}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Juney}} will be able to smell it on him.<ref>{{Cite|*sniff sniff*<br/>I know that smell!<br/>You've got a {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apple}}!|Juney|BotW}}</ref> She asks if she can have one, promising {{Term|BotW|Link}} a present in return.<ref name="One">{{Cite|Can I have {{Color|BotW Blue|one}}? I'll give you a present!|Juney|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Juney}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure thing.] You serious? Cheers! Just hold out your hand.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Juney}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Jogo}} would bring her {{Plural|BotW|Baked Apple}} more often, and she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring her more in the future.<ref>{{Cite|Now if I could only get my husband to cook me a {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apple}} every one in a while. I've got a second stomach and a hollow leg for {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apples}}. Bring me as many as you possibly can.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

After completing "{{Term|BotW|The Apple of My Eye}}", {{Term|BotW|Link}} can sell {{Plural|BotW|Baked Apple}} to {{Term|BotW|Juney}}. She buys them in groups of one,<ref name="One"/> five,<ref>{{Cite|Can I have {{Color|BotW Blue|five}}? I'll give you a present!|Juney|BotW}}</ref> 10,<ref>{{Cite|Can I have {{Color|BotW Blue|10}}? I'll give you a present!|Juney|BotW}}</ref> 30,<ref>{{Cite|Can I have {{Color|BotW Blue|30}}? I'll give you a present!|Juney|BotW}}</ref> 50,<ref>{{Cite|Can I have {{Color|BotW Blue|50}}? I'll give you a present!|Juney|BotW}}</ref> and 100.<ref>{{Cite|Can I have {{Color|BotW Blue|100}}? I'll give you a present!|Juney|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to sell to {{Term|BotW|Juney}}, she promises that she will pay higher prices if he brings her more.<ref>{{Cite|The more you bring me, the more I'll pay you for each one.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Juney}} will call him a cheapskate.<ref>{{Cite|What a cheapskate.|Juney|BotW}}</ref>

In Tears of the Kingdom, Juney can be found directly East of the Riverside Stable at the second river crossing you find. She has a horse and cart full of toys she is planning on giving to children. When you approach,  she and her horse are startled, sending the toys flying. This starts a mini game wherein you pay 20 rupees to her in order to collect all of the ten toys and return them safely to the horse cart under a time limit. On success, she will reward you with a silver rupee worth 100 rupees. The quest can be repeated with a shorter time limit indefinitely after completion.  This is an excellent early game infinite rupee generator. The only skill you need to worry about using is rewind. Start with the closest toy to you and rewind it. Use the brief time after activating rewind to target the next toy. Once you hear the audible indication that the first toy is in the cart, rewind the second one. While the second toy is moving back to the cart, swim to the small rock in the river. From there, rewind each toy in a counter-clockwise fashion, adjusting your targeting for the next toy. Once you hear the tone indicating that the toy has landed in the cart, cancel out of rewind and send the next one. Make sure to not miss the one directly adjacent to the rock you are standing on (it is below you on the opposite side of the rock outcropping from the cart). Once all ten hit the cart safely, collect your reward and repeat the quest as many times as you want.
