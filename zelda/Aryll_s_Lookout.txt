==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TWWHD|Aryll's Lookout}} is a tall, stationary crow's nest that was most likely built to spot oncoming ships on {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}}. Because of Outset's distant location from many other inhabited islands, however, it is mainly used by Link's little sister to play with the gulls.<ref name="Lookout"/> She often uses her telescope from the Lookout to watch over the island.
