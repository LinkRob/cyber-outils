{{Term/Store|Hintobo||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283}}</ref>

==Biography==
Hintobo is an [[Anouki]] who lives on the [[Isle of Frost]]. It lurks around in the [[Anouki Estates]] and will give [[Link]] a hint that will help him discover the disguised [[Yook]] for 100 [[Rupee]]s. It will help Link to exclude three of the six Anouki by telling him that he should focus on the ones accusing others of lying, which are [[Aroo]], [[Gumo]] or [[Kumu]]. The Yook does later on turn out to be [[Aroo]].<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, I'm Hintobo. Whatcha want? Looking for the liar, huh? I know how to spot one. Gimme 100 Rupees. Maybe I'll talk.|Hintobo|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OK then, listen up. Don't worry your head about guys who say that other guys don't lie. The guys who say that others are honest won't lie to you. Why, you ask? Because then there would be two liars. Why again, you ask? There is only one liar. So, knowing that... Look at the ones who accuse others of lying, and you'll find your liar! Hah hah! Brilliant, right? No?|Hintobo|PH}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Hintobo's name derives from the word "hint".
