The {{Term/Store|Fire Blazer||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Fire Blazer gives your Fire Gloves a little extra oomph! It's my hottest design, dahling.}}

The {{Term|TFH|Fire Blazer}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|TFH|Mystery Extract|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Twisted Twig|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Lava Drop|link}} and 1,500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. It becomes available for purchase after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes {{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} features a black bodysuit and mask with flame motifs in the style of a superhero costume.

When {{Term|TFH|Link}} wears the {{Term|TFH|Fire Blazer}}, he will be able to throw three fireballs at once with the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves|link}}; one larger fireball with two smaller ones trailing behind it. This does not consume any more of the {{Term|TFH|Energy Gauge|link}} than a regular fireball.

==Nomenclature==
The term "blazer" refers to both a large fire and a type of formal jacket.
