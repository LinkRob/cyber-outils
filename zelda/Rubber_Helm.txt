The {{Term/Store|Rubber Helm||Series, BotW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Rubber Helm}}<br/>A helmet made from ancient techniques and electricity-resistant material. ==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Rubber Helm, along with the [[Rubber Armor]] and [[Rubber Tights]], comprise the {{Term|BotW|Rubber Armor (Set)|link}} set. The Helm can be acquired by completing the "[[Thunder Magnet]]" [[Side Quest]] at [[Lakeside Stable]]. It provides [[Link]] with 3 defense and level 1 shock resistance. After completing the quest, it will be available to purchase from [[Granté]] in [[Tarrey Town]].
