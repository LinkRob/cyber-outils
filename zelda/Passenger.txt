{{Term/Store|Passenger|Passengers|Series, ST|plural}} are a mechanic in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ST |publisher= Prima |page= 16}}</ref> It refers to {{Term|ST|Link}}'s ability to transport certain people from {{Term|ST|Station|link}} to {{Term|ST|Station}} in the {{Term|ST|Passenger Car|link}} of the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}}. 

==Overview==
{{Stub|ST}}
{{Term|ST|Carben|link}} is the only mandatory passenger on the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}. Other passengers can be taken on at later times for sidequests and rewards, such as [[Rupee|Rupees]] and [[Force Gem|Force Gems]] 

== Game Over Dialogue ==
Should the Spirit Train collide with a [[Dark Train]]/[[Armored Train]] or run out of hearts without the passenger getting dissatisfied and disembarking, they will be the one to cry out/make a remark as the train combusts instead of [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]].

* "YeeeeeeeeeOOOOW!" - [[Dovok]]
* "Whoooooooooa!" - [[Wood (Character)|Wood]]
* "All I wanted was to meet that special sooooomeone!" - [[Yamahiko]]
* "All I wanted to do was get marriiiiied!" - [[Morris]]
* "All I wanted to do was meet a nice laaaaaady!" - [[Mash]]
* "Wh-whoa there!" - [[Mona]]
* "Pr-Princess! Oh, wait... no." - [[Teacher]]
* "Waaaah! I want my mom!" - [[Joe]]
* "You drive like a maniac! A maniac, I tell you!" - [[Noko]]
* "Bwah! Watch it, you knucklehead!" - [[Kenzo]]
* "Whoooaa!" - [[Ferrus]]
* "Gaaaah!" - [[Goron]] bound for [[Anouki Village]]
* "Ayiii!" - [[Goron Child]]
* "Are you TRYING to crash this train?" - [[Kofu]]
* "Aaaaaaaaaaugh!" - [[Wadatsumi]]
== Trivia ==

* Kofu's line upon crashing seems unfitting for the context, as it implies that Link is driving recklessly, yet has not yet crashed the train. His line for when his satisfaction decreases by a level due to reckless driving ("AUGH! This is just awful!") seems more fitting for Link crashing, meaning the two lines may have been written for the opposite scenarios, but erroneously swapped in development.
