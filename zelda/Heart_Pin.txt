The '''Heart Pin''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Heart Pin|Hairpin with a distinctive heart design. It makes hearts appear a little more often.<ref name="Might Bell">{{Cite|Hairpin with a distinctive heart design. It makes hearts appear a little more often.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The '''Heart Pin''' is found in a [[Treasure Chest]] within the [[Suthorn Ruins]]. Equipping it grants the [[Heart Appearance Up]] effect, increasing the drop rate of [[hearts]].
