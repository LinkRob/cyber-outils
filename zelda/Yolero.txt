{{Term/Store|Yolero||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yolero|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Yolero}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} that can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}}. During the daytime, he practices swinging a {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}} at a wooden dummy outside the {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|display=Stable}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to speak to him while he is practicing, {{Term|BotW|Yolero}} will stop and ask if he is a wanderer.<ref>{{Cite|Ha...hooooo... Are you some kind of wanderer?|Yolero|BotW}}</ref> Regardless of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s response, {{Term|BotW|Yolero}} excitedly shares that he possesses the legendary {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} known as the Master {{Term|BotW|Torch}},<ref>{{Cite|I don't care who you are! Just lend me an ear! I've finally got it in my hands—the legendary weapon!|Yolero|BotW}}</ref> a {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} that can only be wielded by the chosen hero.<ref>{{Cite|[Amazing!] Ha! Prepare to be jealous! I'm sure you've heard of it... You know... The legendary weapon that only the chosen hero can wield?|Yolero|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Really?] Heh... Prepare to be blown away. Are you ready?|Yolero|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's called... the {{Big|MASTER TORCH!}} I'm...pretty sure that's what it's called, anyway. Whatever... Leave me be, commoner. I'm trying to practice here!|Yolero|BotW}}</ref>

Upon revealing the name of his legendary {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}, {{Term|BotW|Yolero}} dismisses {{Term|BotW|Link}} so that he can practice. Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Yolero}} will muse on his {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}'s magical properties, reciting a corrupted version of the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}'s alias, the Sword That Seals the Darkness, as the "flame that lights the darkness".<ref>{{Cite|The flame that lights the darkness... The MASTER TORCH!|Yolero|BotW}}</ref> He will also threaten to burn {{Term|BotW|Link}} with the sacred light from his {{Term|BotW|Torch}} for bothering him.<ref>{{Cite|Scram, foul wanderer! Lest I burn you with the sacred light of my Master Torch!|Yolero|BotW}}</ref> Despite {{Term|BotW|Yolero}}'s claims, his {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} appears to be a normal {{Term|BotW|Torch}}. His younger brother, {{Term|BotW|Ami|link}}, refers to his practice sessions as simply playing.<ref>{{Cite|My older brothers Izra and Yolero play all day long, but I have to stay here and give directions.|Ami|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Yolero}} after obtaining the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Yolero}} asks what the {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} is called, remarking that it probably could not sustain a fire.<ref>{{Cite|Nice baby sword, pal. I bet it can't even keep a flame going. What's its name?|Yolero|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds truthfully, {{Term|BotW|Yolero}} remarks that it's an "off-brand knockoff" of his own {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wha—? Master Sword? Ha ha! Must be some kind of off-brand knockoff of my legendary Master Torch! For I am Yolero, the hero of legend chosen to wield the Master Torch!|Yolero|BotW}}</ref>
