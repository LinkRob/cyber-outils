The {{Term/Store|Oceanside Spider House||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location and a mini-dungeon in {{MM}}.{{Fact}} It is located by the left of the [[Fisherman's Hut]] at the [[Great Bay Coast]]. Like the [[Swamp Spider House]] in [[Southern Swamp|Southren Swamp]], it is full of [[Gold Skulltula]]s that have cast a curse upon the house (though no residents live inside). In order to get to the inner part of the Spider House, the entrance must be blown up using a [[Bomb]] (or [[Blast Mask]]). The [[Hookshot]] and [[Fire Arrow|Fire Arrows]] are also required to proceed further.

==Themes and Navigation==
The Oceanside Spider House bears a greater resemblance to an actual house than its Swamp counterpart, albeit an evidently abandoned one, full of storage boxes and cobwebs. When [[Link]] first enters the house, he finds himself in an empty room. However, by blowing up the cracked wall before him, an underground passage is revealed that leads to a hidden basement with another entrance to the rest of the house. The first room after the basement entrance is a corridor with a stairway down and three rooms. A great library is located on the first basement floor, while the second basement floor holds a storage room and a dining room. The latter is inhabited by four [[Stalchild]]ren sitting by a large table, while another two can also be found in the library. By speaking to them using the [[Captain's Hat]], it is revealed that they are under orders to investigate the secrets of the place.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Captain, sir! It's been a long time since we've seen you! As you have ordered, sir, we are investigating the secrets of this place.|Stalchild|MM}}</ref> In addition, each of the six Stalchildren will reveal a piece of information that is required to solve a [[puzzle]] in the dining room. When Link solves this puzzle, a gateway will open within the dining room's fireplace, revealing a secret chamber where a [[Treasure Chest]] with a [[Piece of Heart]] inside can be found.

The most intriguing mystery surrounding the Oceanside Spider House is the presence of pictures of the [[Skull Kid#Majora's Mask|Skull Kid]] not wearing [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]] within the house. It is never elaborated on as to why such pictures are in there, nor even referenced by anyone within the house, even [[Tatl]]. The only purpose they serve is to be shot down to potentially reveal a hidden Gold Skulltula.[[File:SkulltulaPerson.png|thumb|282x282px|The man who is looking for shelter]]Once the 30 Gold Skulltulas are slain and their [[Gold Skulltula Token|Spirits]] collected, a man will appear at the entrance, curiously looking around. He claims he has heard loud noises from Link's activity and then asks the young [[Hero of Time]] if he can have the house for himself but rewards him for his efforts. If Link collects all the tokens during the [[First Day]], he will be rewarded with the [[Wallet|Giant Wallet]], or a Silver [[Rupee]] for completing it again on the First Day in subsequent cycles. On the [[Second Day]], he will receive a Purple Rupee, and on the [[Final Day]], a Red Rupee. The man says the Rupees are his life fortune and wants to use the Oceanside Spider House as a shelter from the falling [[Moon (Object)|Moon]].<ref>{{Cite|This place... Is this some sort of underground shelter? [...] I beg you! Let me have this place! Not for free, of course. I hope this will do...|Hylian|MM}}</ref> If Link re-enters the house, the man will now be sitting crouched up on the basement floor, praying for the [[Goddess of Time]] to save him.<ref>{{Cite|Only a little time left... Oh, Goddess of Time, please save me...|Hylian|MM}}</ref> He cannot enter deep inside as intended, as the large gate-like barrier requiring the Hookshot prevents the man from doing so, leaving the main basement areas empty.

In {{MM3D}}, the colored masks in the dining room are redesigned to feature a different card suit symbol on each one. The hints the Stalchildren give also refer to the mask's symbol instead of the color. Link can also now get the Giant Wallet during any of the three days, and defeating all 30 Gold Skulltulas a second time will award him a [[Fishing Hole Pass]].

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese version, the mini-dungeon is called '''Ocean Spider Mansion'''.
*The man seeking shelter is the same person who stands on the top of the [[Carnival of Time|Carnival Tower]] at [[South Clock Town]] on the [[First Day]]. If [[Link]] collects all of the Gold Skulltula Spirits, the man disappears from [[Clock Town]].
**The man himself is one of the very few residents in [[Termina]] who is aware of the impending doom by the [[Moon]] from the start of the three-day cycle, alongside [[Grog]].
*The puzzle order explained from the [[Stalchild|Stalchildren]] to open the dining room's fireplace is different per save file.
*It is possible to exit the house's entrance passage with the [[Bunny Hood]].[[File:MM Skull Kid Japanese version.png|thumb|240x240px|Skull Kid's pictures in this mini-dungeon match how he appears in the Japanese version]]
*The paintings of [[Skull Kid]] within the house has the character with black skin with a beak as his mouth, which is identical to how he appears in the Japanese release of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]''. In the international version, his face was given a wooden texture due to [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda series|controversy]] but the picture itself remains unchanged. In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'', however, they are corrected for all regions.
*The man is the [[Terminan]] counterpart of the children from the [[House of Skulltula|Skulltula Family]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''.
*Despite the Oceanside Spider House being indeed an underground shelter, as it is two basement floors deep, like [[Pinnacle Rock]]'s lowest depths, it does not offer any protection for Link in the event the Moon [[Terminan Apocalypse|crashes into Termina]].
**Realistically, some fans claim that if Link did survive the inital fiery shockwave by the Moon's collision course, almost certainly the shelter itself would collapse entirely on top of the hero.
*In {{SSBM}}, the house itself can be seen, alongside the [[Fisherman's Hut]], in the background of the [[Great Bay#Super Smash Bros. Melee|Great Bay]] stage.
