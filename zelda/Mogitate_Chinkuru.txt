'''Mogitate Chinkuru''' is one of [[mariowiki:18-Volt|18-Volt]]'s microgames, appearing only in the Japanese version of ''[[w:c:mario:WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase|WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. It is one of the {{TLoZ|Series}}-themed microgames in the ''[[WarioWare]]'' series, Mogitate Chinkuru is unique in that it is based on ''[[Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland]]''.

The object is for [[Tingle]] to defeat an [[Eyelid Pig]]. If done on time, the player wins, but if not, they lose, and Tingle lets out a scream.
