{{Term/Store|East of the Bay||Series}} is a location in {{LA}}.{{Fact}} It is located east of [[Martha's Bay]], hence its name. It is also south of [[Animal Village]],  west of [[Yarna Desert]] and along the island's southern shore. This area is where [[Marin]] sings to the [[walrus]] in order to unblock the path to Yarna Desert. Entering a passageway in the north end of the area is the only way to reach a cave near Animal Village that contains a piece of heart. It also features a [[warping|warp hole]], but little else.
