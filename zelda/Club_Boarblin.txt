'''Club Boarblin''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Club Boarblin|A brute of the northern Gerudo Desert, this creature bounds directly at foes and lays into them with its club.<ref name="EoW Club Boarblin">{{Cite|A brute of the northern Gerudo Desert, this creature bounds directly at foes and lays into them with its club.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Club Boarblins are a variant of [[Boarblin]] known for attacking with wooden clubs, often found across the [[Gerudo Desert]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Club Boarblin [[Echo]], which costs two Echo energy to summon.
