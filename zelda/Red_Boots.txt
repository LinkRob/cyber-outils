The '''Red Boots''' are an [[item]] in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Red Boots are found near the end of the cave maze in the [[Shrine of Earth]]. With the Red Boots, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can cross certain parts of [[Tolemac]]'s river to reach the northern region. The Red Boots do not have to be selected from the [[inventory]] to use.
