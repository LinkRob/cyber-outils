The '''Construct Factory''' is a dungeon in {{TotK}}.

== Entrance to the Factory ==
{{Stub|TotK}}

== Themes and navigation ==
{{Stub|TotK}}
The Construct Factory is made up of a large, centralized, tower-like structure and four adjacent depots in [[The Depths]], located near [[Tobio's Hollow]]. Link must visit this area to assist [[Mineru]] in creating a new body for her using the same [[Zonai]] technology that is used to assemble the [[Construct]]s. Each depot is used to create one of the Construct's new limbs, which must then be brought to the central chamber for final assembly. The Construct Factory does not house a [[Boss]]. Instead, Link and Mineru must travel to the [[Spirit Temple#Tears of the Kingdom|Spirit Temple]] in order to find her [[Secret Stone]] and defeat the [[Seized Construct]] protecting it.
