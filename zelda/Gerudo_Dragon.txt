'''Gerudo Dragon''' is [[Ganondorf]]'s side special move appearing only in {{SSBM}}. Since {{SSBB}}, the move has been removed by [[Flame Choke]].

==Uses==
Gerudo Dragon involves Ganondorf charging forward and inflicting an uppercut to anyone in his path.
