'''{{TLoZ|Colon}} {{SS|-}} Save Data Update Channel''', known on the [[Wii]] Menu's Title Screen as '''''Save Data Update Channel For The Legend of Zelda Skyward Sword''''', '''''Save Data Update''''' by the Wii Message Board, and '''''Zelda Data Restoration Channel''''' in Japan, is a channel for the Wii system, made available on the Wii Shop Channel for free in December 2011. This channel is the first available on the Wii Shop Channel to feature a screen reading "the download of this software is only advised to those who own a copy of (specified game)".

This channel came into fruition after [[Nintendo]] became aware of the infamous [[Glitches in Skyward_Sword#Song of the Hero Game Ending Error|Song of the Hero glitch]] in {{SS}}, a bug that can potentially prevent completion of the game. This channel was first announced when Nintendo confirmed this glitch on their official website, where they then stated, "We will be releasing an online patch in order to fix this bug. Those who do not have access to the internet will need to send their copies of The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword, their SD Card with Skyward Sword save data on it, or their Wii system to our Customer Service."<ref>{{Cite Person| quote=We will be releasing an online patch in order to fix this bug. Those who do not have access to the internet will need to send their copies of The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword, their SD Card with Skyward Sword save data on it, or their Nintendo Wii system to our Customer Service. | name= Nintendo's Official Website}}</ref>

The channel features an original soundbyte on the Wii Menu Title Screen, but the software within contains an explanation of the Song of Heroes glitch, and the option to accept or decline an online update for {{SS|-}} that will fix this bug. The channel then informs the user that it is safe to delete ''The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword Save Update Channel'' from the Wii's system memory after having performed the update.

== Trivial ==

* The Save Data Update Channel took many of its code and design from ''Animal Crossing: City Folk / Let's Go to the City''. 
** The channel melody is the same as ''City Folk''.
** The pointer is an asset taken straight from ''City Folk''.
** Internally, the channel includes leftover data from ''City Folk'', some of which would be used in the sequel, ''Animal Crossing: New Leaf''.
* The Save Data Update Channel is the only channel available on the Wii Shop Channel after the shop's discontinuation January 30, 2019, along with the Wii U Transfer Tool.
