==Appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, the Old Woman makes multiple appearances. She can be found adjacent to the river just left of the starting area, recommending [[Link]] to "MEET THE OLD MAN AT THE GRAVE", which is where the [[Magical Sword]] is located.

She is present near the [[Lost Woods]] and will offer Link a deal. If he pays [[Rupees]], she will grant him [[Fortune Teller|useful information]] in return, stating  "PAY ME AND I'LL TALK". Link has the option to pay her 10, 30, or 50 Rupees. Choosing to spend only 10 will make her respond with "THIS AIN'T ENOUGH TO TALK". Spending 50 on the deal will cause the Old Woman to say "BOY, YOU'RE RICH!". It is only by buying the information for 30 Rupees that she will reply with "GO NORTH, WEST, SOUTH, WEST TO THE FOREST OF MAZE". The hint provided guides Link into getting past the looping screen that blocks the [[Graveyard]].

She also appears in the hidden waterfall of the [[Death Mountain]] area, with the option to buy her advice for 5, 10, and 20 Rupees. Spending 5 or 10 will both make her respond with "THIS AIN'T ENOUGH TO TALK". Only by giving her all 20 [[Rupee]]s requested will she tell him to "GO UP, UP, THE MOUNTAIN AHEAD". Similar to the other hint, this one is integrated into getting past the looping screen that blocks the Level 5 Dungeon entrance (In the Second Quest it simply leads to a pond with a [[Fairy]]).

[[Link]] can receive a [[Letter]] from the [[Letter-Entrusting Old Man]] in a northeast cave of Hyrule. The Old Man tells Link to show the Letter to the Old Woman which is a childhood friend of his.<ref>{{Cite|SHOW THIS TO THE OLD WOMAN.|Old Man|TLoZ}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Childhood Friends|book= E |publisher= |page= 219{{Exp Game|TLoZ}}}}</ref> After Link shows the Letter to the Old Woman, the various other Old Women around the Overworld will sell him [[Life Potion]]s and [[2nd Potion]]s.

==={{TAoL|-}}===
[[File:TAoL Old Woman Orange Sprite.png|left|40px|Magic Restoring Lady]]
Old Women that restore [[Magic Meter|magic]] are found in nearly every town in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite|I CAN GIVE YOU MAGIC. COME BACK ANYTIME.|Old Woman|TAoL}}</ref> They are recognized by their orange dresses and offer Link to rest in their houses.<ref>{{Cite|STOP AND REST HERE.|Old Woman|TAoL}}</ref>

Two Old Women, one in [[Harbor Town of Mido]], who claims to need [[Water of Life]] for her sick daughter, and one in [[Mountain Town of Darunia]], who has a [[Child|Kidnapped Child]], allow Link to meet the [[Old Man|Wise Men]] teaching [[Spells of The Adventure of Link|spells]] in their house if the young hero helps them. An Old Woman in the [[Kasuto|Hidden Town of Kasuto]] invites Link inside her house to claim the fourth and last [[Magic Jar|Magic Container]] if he has already three out of the four containers.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TSP|-}}===
The Old Woman is a character featured in {{TSP}}. Link can only meet her if he opens the gates of the [[Water Town of Saria]] using the key given to him by an [[Old Man]] on his way to [[Town of Ruto|Ruto Town]]. Once the young hero enters the town square of Saria, the Old Woman will waddle towards him, asking if he is Link.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He is in a busy town square! People bustle by without looking at him. Then a hunched old woman waddles toward him. A draped shawl hides her face. 'Might you be Link?' she rasps.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=57}}</ref> Link confirms this, and just then he notices that the Old Woman has eyes "clear as glass."<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=She looks up. Link has never seen a face so wrinkled. But her eyes are clear as glass.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=57}}</ref> When Link remarks about her eyes, the Old Woman rewards his observation by advising him to retrieve the fruit of an apple tree located in the middle of the square. Doing so, she says, will make Link's eyes as clear as hers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='You're an observant youngster!' she says. 'I'm going to give you some advice, clever lad.' She points to a gnarled tree in the middle of the square. 'See that apple tree? If you can get to it, its fruit will make your eyes as clear as mine.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=57, 58}}</ref> After reaching the tree in the [[Wiseapple Farm]], Link notices a small mirror with a thin wooden frame in one of the brances, realizing that the Old Woman was actually referring to the [[Mirror of Truth]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=As Link is reaching for his own snack, he sees something shining in a forked branch. 'Hmmm...' He reaches up and pulls down a small mirror with a thin wooden frame. Link grins. 'So that's what the old lady meant about clear eyes,' he says.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=43}}</ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}
