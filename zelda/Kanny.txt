{{Term/Store|Kanny||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kanny|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kanny}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} who travels on {{Term|BotW|Horse|link|display=Horseback}}. He follows a portion of the {{Term|BotW|Hilltop High Road|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}}, at which point he continues on to {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable|link}}. He wears a {{Term|BotW|Snowquill Tunic|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Snowquill Trousers|link}}, and he carries an {{Term|BotW|Emblazoned Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}}.

On his way to {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Kanny}} will invite {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to accompany him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Rito Stable}} is just up ahead. Want to go there with me?|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> Once there, he plans on selling off the last of his stock.<ref>{{Cite|I'm hoping to sell the rest of my stock of frozen mushrooms at this stable.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Rito Stable}} is just up ahead. I'm hoping to sell the rest of my stock of specialty mushrooms there.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm off to {{Color|BotW Blue|Rito Village}} to sell my specialty mushrooms from the land of ice and snow.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> He also demonstrates a familiarity with {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable}}, referring to it as his stomping grounds and explaining it is where he finds the best {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to my stomping grounds, {{Color|BotW Blue|Serenne Stable}}. You won't find better shrooms anywhere.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This area is known for its mushrooms. We've got warm ones, cool ones, and stamina ones. Lots of varieties.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> Some of his {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom}} are also sourced from {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's about time to head back to {{Color|BotW Blue|Snowfield Stable}} to refresh my stock.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Kanny}} is appreciates the {{Term|BotW|Snow|link}}, he dislikes the cold.<ref>{{Cite|I like the snow and all, but I wish it wasn't so cold.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> Even though the road between {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable}} and {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}} is cold, {{Term|BotW|Kanny}} relies on it to keep his products fresh.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, the road from {{Color|BotW Blue|Rito Stable}} to {{Color|BotW Blue|Snowfield Stable}} is a cold one. But that cold is what makes their specialty products so good.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The road to {{Color|BotW Blue|Snowfield Stable}} is cold, but the cold helps the shrooms stay fresh.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> After he crosses into the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tundra|link}}, he remarks that the wind that comes from the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} is cold enough to freeze him to his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Brrrrrrr... So cold. This breeze coming off of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}} is likely to freeze me to my horse.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This cold wind blowing off of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}}... Hahhhhh... It's likely to freeze me to my horse.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kanny}} also harbors a dislike for the [[Weather]] when it is {{Term|BotW|Snow|display=Snowing}}, as he believes it would be easy to become lost in such conditions.<ref>{{Cite|I don't like to venture out on days like this. With the snow swirling like that, you're bound to get lost.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> However, he is unable to sit idly by while people need to buy from him.<ref>{{Cite|All the same, I can't sit here on my duff with people waiting for my mushrooms.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> He congratulates {{Term|BotW|Link}} for making to the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}, and he explains that he will need protection from the cold if he goes any further north.<ref>{{Cite|Good work making it this far. If you're going north of here, don't forget to bring warm clothes.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kanny}} believes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to be good with a sword, but he wonders how good he is with a {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You look like you know your way around a sword, but how good are you with a shield?|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> He suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} check out the {{Term|BotW|Selmie's Spot|link|display=cabin}} on the {{Term|BotW|Hebra East Summit|link}} if he is interested in {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You should consider heading over to the small cabin on {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra East Summit}} to experience the highs of {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you're interested in {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}, visit the cabin on {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra East Summit}}.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you ever happen to be in the area, you should visit the small cabin on {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra East Summit}}.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> He remarks that many young people are into {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}}, which he relates to as a way to forget about the cold.<ref>{{Cite|You young folk are crazy about {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}, and I don't blame you. It's a great way to forget about the cold.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref>

As a {{Term|BotW|Merchant}}, {{Term|BotW|Kanny}} specializes in selling {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom}}.<ref name="Mushroom"/><ref>{{Cite|Would you like to browse my selection of specialty mushrooms?|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> When it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}} or {{Term|BotW|Snow|display=Snowing}}, {{Term|BotW|Kanny}} offers rare {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm running a little bad-weather special today. Check out my rare mushrooms.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to buy from {{Term|BotW|Kanny}}, he urges him to take his time as he browses.<ref>{{Cite|I'm quite proud of my assortment of shrooms. Take your time looking.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> He thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for every purchase he makes.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks!|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you so much.|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That was the last of it. Thanks for being such a great customer!|Kanny|BotW}}</ref>

Like other {{Plural|BotW|Merchant}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can sell his own belongings to {{Term|BotW|Kanny}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll buy something off of you!|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You'll sell me something?|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll buy anything!|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What do you want to sell?|Kanny|BotW}}</ref> He will thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} each time he sells to him.<ref>{{Cite|Thanky, thanky!|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you so much!|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks!|Kanny|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks! Did you need something else?|Kanny|BotW}}</ref>
