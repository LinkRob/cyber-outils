==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Her name is {{Color|SS Blue|Goselle}}, and she is a bit of a demanding lady. She is the mother of the gear peddler, {{Color|SS Blue|Rupin}}.</p><p>She hates housecleaning but adores antiques. I recommend keeping a safe distance from her.</p>
}}

Goselle is a resident of [[Skyloft]]. She lives with her son [[Rupin]]. Goselle is fond of antiques which she proudly displays in her home. She appears kind and happy when [[Link]] first talks to her. Yet if Link rolls into the walls and knocks down her antiques, she will become enraged, demanding compensation for her broken possessions. After Link pays her the amount of [[Rupee]]s required, she calms down. She will also get angry with Link if he sits down on her couch, but calms down and says that it is okay if he sits there, so long as he does not make it dirty.<ref>{{Cite|HEY! Oh... I guess it's OK as long as you're going to just sit. But listen up! THAT is an expensive chair, and if you get it dirty... Well, I can't be held responsible for what happens.|Goselle|SS}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Goselle demanding compensation for her broken antiques is similar to when Link breaks a [[Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase|vase]] in the [[Auction House]] in {{TWW}}, where [[Mila's Father]] will make Link pay.

==Nomenclature==
Goselle's name is derived from gosling, a baby {{Wp|goose}}. It may also be a combination of gosling and the Italian name "Roselle".

Goselle's Japanese name, Otaka, likely comes from the Japanese word {{Romanize|御高い|Otakai}}, meaning "self-importance" or "haughty."
