{{Term/Store|Hyrule Outpost||Series, HWAoC}} is a location in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Improve}}
Hyrule Outpost is a town nearby the [[Great Plateau]]. As a map, it is one of the tightest in the game, filled with compact corridors, interspersed with Keep areas that are larger. However, at the right edge of the map is a wide open grassy area and at the top of it is a coliseum-like area. During the mission it first appears in, it is overrun with Yiga and varying monsters.
