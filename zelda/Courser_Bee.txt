{{Term/Store|Courser Bee|Courser Bees|Series, BotW|plural}} are animals in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book=CaC|publisher=|page=165}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Courser Bee}} can be found in swarms, with each swarm guarding one piece of {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee Honey|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches, they attack, dealing minimal damage. They can be targeted and take two hits to scare off, but don't officially appear in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}}. They appear on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} during {{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad|link}}, where they are a major threat, as they are ferocious and the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator|link}} forces {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be stuck on a quarter of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}.
