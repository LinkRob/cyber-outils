The {{Term/Store|Rattle||Series, SS}} is an {{Term|SS|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 135}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
If {{Term|SS|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|SS|Bertie|link}} in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}}, he will tell him to come to his house at night.<ref>{{Cite|You'll have to forgive me. I'm a little too busy right now. Do you think...you could come by my house later? I'll be there {{Color|SS Red|tonight}}. Sorry for the inconvenience... Hope to see you later.|Bertie|SS}}</ref> If {{Term|SS|Link}} speaks to him again at night, he tells him that a bird has stolen his {{Term|SS|Bertie's Baby|link|display=Baby}}'s {{Term|SS|Rattle}},<ref>{{Cite|If we had the you know what, then the baby would settle down. But we lost it the other day. {{Color|SS Red|A bird picked it up}} in its beak and {{Color|SS Red|flew off with it}}. Maybe the bird thought it was food. I don't know...|Bertie|SS}}</ref> and wishes that someone would find it.<ref>{{Cite|Sigh... It sure would be nice if someone would find it for me.|Bertie|SS}}</ref>

To retrieve it, {{Term|SS|Link}} must {{Term|SS|Clawshot|link}} his way onto the floating islands near the {{Term|SS|Waterfall Cave|link}} until he reaches one with a large pool of {{Term|SS|Water (Terrain)|link}}. From there, he must dive down onto the bird's nest on a small windmill and use the {{Term|SS|Gust Bellows|link}} to uncover the {{Term|SS|Rattle}}. Once {{Term|SS|Link}} returns the {{Term|SS|Rattle}}, {{Term|SS|Bertie}} will reward him with five {{Plural|SS|Gratitude Crystal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whew. The baby seems to have settled down nicely. I bet the baby will sleep well tonight. Oh! That means I'll finally get a good night's sleep too! Thank you so much! I won't forget what you did for me.|Bertie|SS}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*It is possible to retrieve the {{Term|SS|Rattle}} without the {{Plural|SS|Clawshot|link}}. If {{Term|SS|Link|link}} stops {{Term|SS|Beedle's Airshop|link}} close to the {{Term|SS|Rattle}}, he can climb up and retrieve it using the {{Term|SS|Gust Bellows|link}} and {{Term|SS|Hook Beetle|link}}. {{Term|SS|Link}} must first use the {{Term|SS|Hook Beetle}} to collect the {{Plural|SS|Rupee|link}}, allowing him to use the {{Term|SS|Gust Bellows}} to blow the dust off the {{Term|SS|Rattle}} without the {{Plural|SS|Rupee}} obstructing him. He can then use the {{Term|SS|Hook Beetle}} once more to retrieve the exposed {{Term|SS|Rattle}}. However, because the {{Term|SS|Side Quest|link}} would not be available at this time, {{Term|SS|Link}} cannot hand the {{Term|SS|Rattle}} to {{Term|SS|Bertie}} until after obtaining the {{Plural|SS|Clawshot}}.
