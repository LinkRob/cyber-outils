==Overview==
"Forest Temple" is the background music for the eponymous dungeon, the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}}.

"Forest Temple" is the 59th track of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Original Sound Track]]''.
[[File:OoT Forest Temple Theme.mp3|center|thumb]]

== Trivia ==

* The theme of the Forest Temple is known among fans as one of the most eerie songs of {{OoT|-}}, if not the series as a whole. This is because alongside the interior of the dungeon itself bare a resemblance to an abandoned mansion or castle similarly to [[Ganon's Castle]], the music uses a mix of haunting sounds and a slow, gentle melody driven by wind instruments.
* If one listens closely, sped-up, high-pitched grunts of [[Link]] can be heard at certain parts of the theme song.
