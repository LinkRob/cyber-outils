'''Gerudo Sandals''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Gerudo Sandals|Traditional desert sandals. They prevent the wearer from sinking in quicksand.<ref name="Gerudo Sandals">{{Cite|Traditional desert sandals. They prevent the wearer from sinking in quicksand.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Gerudo Sandals are an [[Accessory]] that can be purchased at the [[Gerudo Shop]] for 400 [[Rupee|Rupees]]. Equipping them grants the [[Quicksand-Proof]] effect, making [[Princess Zelda]] immune to quicksand.
