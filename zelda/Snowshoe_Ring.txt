The {{Term/Store|Snowshoe Ring||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Snowshoe Ring<br/>No sliding on ice|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Snowshoe Ring is received from [[Mamamu Yan]] at [[Lynna City]] after telling her the [[Mamamu Secret]] and finding her dog. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it prevents [[Link]] from sliding on ice.<ref name="Description"/> The Snowshoe Ring can be obtained in {{OoS}} by  transferring it by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
