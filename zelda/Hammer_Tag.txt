{{Term/Store|Hammer Tag||Series, FSA}} is a {{Term|FSA|Mini-Game|link}} in {{FSA}}.{{Name Ref|FSA}}

==Rules==
To play {{Term|FSA|Hammer Tag}}, {{Term|FSA|Link|link}} must visit {{Term|FSA|Tingle's Tower|link}}. {{Term|FSA|Hammer Tag}} starts off with a villager sitting by a {{Term|FSA|Tree|link}}. To start the {{Term|FSA|Mini-Game|link}}, someone must speak with the villager. After a short conversation, he selects a player at random. This person holds the {{Term|FSA|Hammer|link}}, and is dizzy for a few seconds, while the other players get a chance to run away. The player with the {{Term|FSA|Hammer}} must then chase the players and try to hit them with the {{Term|FSA|Hammer}}. On occasion, a portal to the {{Term|FSA|Dark World|link}} will appear, allowing players to escape. The people who do not carry the {{Term|FSA|Hammer}} run much slower than usual, and are unable to roll. Meanwhile, the person with the hammer runs at normal speed, and can roll.

The first person to spend a minute in total time with the {{Term|FSA|Hammer}} loses, while everyone else wins.
