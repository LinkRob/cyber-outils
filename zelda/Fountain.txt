The {{Term/Store|Fountain||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Fountain}} is located northwest of the main square of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. It appears as an ordinary fountain, though to the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}, it is the resting place of a powerful treasure &ndash; the {{Plural|TMC|Power Bracelet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The treasure sleeps on the other side of the secret entrance there... They say it's a magical tool that allows even little people to push big things! According to the legend, you're supposed to start from this house!|Minish|TMC}}</ref> It is full of enemies that {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must defeat to retrieve the {{Plural|TMC|Power Bracelet}}.

The {{Term|TMC|Fountain}} is first seen during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}} with no water inside it. When {{Term|TMC|Link}} returns to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} after obtaining the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}, water has begun to pour out into it, and a small vine has grown down from the upper ledge.

Since the {{Term|TMC|Fountain}} can only be entered while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must make his way to the {{Term|TMC|Fountain}} from {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House|link}}. From there, he must climb the chimney, cross the river, and eventually make it past the {{Plural|TMC|Cat|link}} and inside the {{Term|TMC|Fountain}}. The {{Plural|TMC|Power Bracelet}} found within are needed in order to obtain all of the overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}}, and to be able to meet the elder {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}} to obtain the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}.
