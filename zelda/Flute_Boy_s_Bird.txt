The {{Name|Fan|Flute Boy's Bird}} is a {{Term|ALttP|Duck}} who belonged to the {{Term|ALttP|Flute Boy}} in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite|He had a pet bird that went with him everywhere, but he went to the mountain and never returned.|Innkeeper|ALttP}}</ref> The {{Term|ALttP|Bird|link}} can be summoned by the {{Term|ALttP|Flute Boy}}'s {{Term|ALttP|Flute|link}} found in the {{Term|ALttP|Haunted Grove|link}}.

When {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} plays the {{Term|ALttP|Flute}} near the {{Term|ALttP|Weathercock|link}} in the center of {{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village|link}}, the {{Term|ALttP|Flute Boy's Bird}} appears from inside the statue. If {{Term|ALttP|Link}} plays the {{Term|ALttP|Flute}} again, the duck appears and swoops down on him, picking {{Term|ALttP|Link}} up in order to {{Term|ALttP|Warping|display=transport|link}} him to any of eight different locations in the {{Term|ALttP|Light World|link}}:

#On the western side of {{Term|ALttP|Death Mountain|link}}
#In front of the {{Term|ALttP|Witch's Hut|link}}
#{{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village|link}}
#{{Term|ALttP|Link's House|link}}
#South of the {{Term|ALttP|Eastern Palace|link}}
#{{Term|ALttP|Desert of Mystery|link}}
#{{Term|ALttP|Great Swamp|link}}
#{{Term|ALttP|Lake Hylia|link}}
#On the eastern side of {{Term|ALttP|Death Mountain|link}} ([[Game Boy Advance]] only)

The use of the pet bird and the {{Term|ALttP|Flute}} greatly cuts walking time between the regions of {{Term|ALttP|Hyrule|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The use of {{Term|TMC|Zeffa}} and the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind}} in {{TMC}} is similar to the use of the {{Term|ALttP|Flute Boy's Bird}} and the {{Term|ALttP|Flute}}.
*{{Term|ALttP|Link}} can avoid being warped by the duck if he runs out of the way of the bird's dive after he calls him using the flute.
*Even though the {{Term|ALttP|Flute Boy's Bird}} cannot normally be summoned in the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}}, {{Term|ALttP|Link}} summons it to take him to the {{Term|ALttP|Pyramid of Power|link}} upon the defeat of {{Term|ALttP|Agahnim|link}}.
