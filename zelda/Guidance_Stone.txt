{{Term/Store|Guidance Stone|Guidance Stones|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|series}}.<ref>{{Cite|This sweet little {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} will start working once you do that, allowing us to restore your missing {{Color|BotW Red|runes}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}} {{Plural|Series|Guidance Stone}} go unnamed in {{SSBU}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Guidance Stone}} are pieces of {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} that are capable of distilling information to a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. They are outfitted with {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|link}}, which keep the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} in place. Their power is derived from ancient energy.

According to {{Term|BotW|Robbie's Memoirs|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} transported the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But not before loading a cart with the Guidance Stone Dr. Purah had been using at Hyrule Castle.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} returned to {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} to retrieve their second {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} and began his journey to {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I returned to Kakariko Village to collect the remaining Guidance Stone, then I immediately set out for Akkala.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> Eventually, he settled in a lighthouse, which he turned into the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|In time, I reached the lighthouse in Akkala. What a perfect place to keep an eye on the monsters out there. I unloaded my Guidance Stone and settled into my new home.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref>

The first {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} encounters will be the one belonging to the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|link}}. After the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}} have been activated, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to approach {{Plural|BotW|Guidance Stone}}, which will cause it to distill local information.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Distilling local information...}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> A drop of ancient energy will drop onto the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}, granting {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Region|link|display=regional}} {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Regional map extracted.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

The four {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} all contain {{Plural|BotW|Guidance Stone}}. All of them will distill a {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} for the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling rune...}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Rune extracted}}.|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Oman Au Shrine|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Owa Daim Shrine|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Keh Namut Shrine|link}}. In {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} can also be distilled after completing the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab}} during "{{Term|BotW|Locked Mementos|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Purah}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to light the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|display=Lab's}} {{Term|BotW|Furnace|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Let me explain what this errand details. You know the unlit {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}} on the wall just outside this laboratory? Oh, and also use it to light our {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}}?|Purah|BotW}}</ref> as doing this will reactivate their {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}}.<ref>{{Cite|This sweet little {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} will start working once you do that, allowing us to restore your missing {{Color|BotW Red|runes}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> Once he has restored the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}, the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} will begin to work again,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you! Linky, thank you so much! Now then, take a look over there! The {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} must be starting to react! There's nothing mysterious about it—this is pure science!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Purah}} will remove the lock on the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} to fix his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|display=Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hokay then, {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} repair... underway! {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} lock removed! Now...let's do this thing!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} places the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} under the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}}, it will recognize the missing functions and initiate a repair.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Camera, Hyrule Compendium, and album missing files confirmed. Starting repair.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> This restores the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Album|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|A rune has been added.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Repair complete.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

After this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will receive the "{{Term|BotW|Slated for Upgrades|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. With the appropriate {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}, he will be able to upgrade his {{Plural|BotW|Rune}} using the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab}}'s {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you bring me some {{Color|BotW Red|ancient materials}}, I'll {{Color|BotW Red|power up}} you {{Color|BotW Blue|runes}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can upgrade the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Enhancing Remote Bomb rune.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Stasis}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Enhancing Stasis rune.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> Additionally, once {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes "{{Term|BotW|Sunshroom Sensing|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Symin|link}} will be able to unlock {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}} entries for {{Term|BotW|Link}} using the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}}.<ref>{{Cite|As I was conducting my research, I found that there were still some {{Color|BotW Red|pictures}} of the past left on the {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}}! If you add those {{Color|BotW Red|pictures}} to your {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Compendium}}, you can search for their subjects with the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Sensor +}}.|Symin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated.<br/>Adding picture to Hyrule Compendium.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Hyrule Compendium updated.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

At the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} has converted his {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} into the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}}, which he initially named {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|display=Cherry}}.<ref>{{Cite|It took some significant work, but I was able to successfully remodel the Guidance Stone. Thanks to my effort, it can refine any ancient material inserted into it and offer something in return. I named this machine Cherry, after the first girl I ever loved...|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}} is able to refine {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material}} into {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Cherry?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}... that is, the ancient oven... is a machine I made to create ancient soldier gear.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My research didn't end with the creation of Cherry, not at all! Anyone could make ancient equipment!|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> This can only be done after restoring power to it after "{{Term|BotW|Robbie's Research|link}}".

Each {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} also contains one {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}}, which the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} will direct {{Term|BotW|Link}} towards.<ref>{{Cite|You're gonna need a map that shows the guts of this place. ==Trivia==
* In {{BotW|-}}, the theme that plays while information is being distilled is a rendition of the music that plays when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} opens a {{Term|Series|Treasure Chest|link}} in every 3D game of {{TLoZ|Series}}.
* When transliterated into English, the [[Sheikah (Language)|Sheikah runes]] on the crystal's surface read "Master using it and you can have this." This line is spoken by the {{Term|TLoZ|White Sword-Giving Old Man|link}} protecting the {{Term|TLoZ|White Sword|link}} in {{TLoZ}}.
** In a pre-release demo for {{BotW|-}} shown as part of E3 2016, the Sheikah runes read "Now loading", "Do not turn off", and "All your base are"; the latter being a reference to an early Internet meme.<ref>{{Cite Web|published=June 15, 2016 |retrieved= Nov. 5, 2021|url=https://youtu.be/H59M89AKefs?t=864 |title=The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild - Shrine of Trials Gameplay Part 3/4 - Nintendo E3 2016 |site=YouTube}}</ref>
