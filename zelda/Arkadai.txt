==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TWoG|Arkadai}} are crocodile-like {{Plural|TWoG|Enemy|link}} that inhabit lakes and rivers in the island of {{Term|TWoG|Gamelon|link}}. They swim back and forth repeatedly in a set path. They are for the most part harmless, and are actually helpful as {{Term|TWoG|Zelda|link}} can ride on their backs to reach other places. Although while they do not directly attack {{Term|TWoG|Zelda}}, the {{Plural|TWoG|Arkadai}} in the last section of {{Term|TWoG|Washubi Swamp|link}} will submerge into the water periodically, which will make {{Term|TWoG|Zelda}} fall into the water should she stand on one while it dives. They cannot be attacked or defeated by any means.
