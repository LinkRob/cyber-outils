The '''''Too Much Tingle Pack''''' is a [[Tingle]] DSiWare app.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= {{Color|E Green|'''TOO MUCH TINGLE PACK'''}}<br/>For players who couldn't get enough of Tingle, there was the {{TMTP|-}}: a small collection of applications including a fortuneteller, a calculator, a timer, and weirder things all said to be made by Tingle himself.|book= E |publisher= |page= 312}}</ref> It includes several applications like a calculator, a dancing Tingle image, the [[Tingle Reader]], a timer, and a coin-flipping minigame. The game can be downloaded for 500 Nintendo Points. It is currently only available in Japan, as DSiWare is region-locked.
