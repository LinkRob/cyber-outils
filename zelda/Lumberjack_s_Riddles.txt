The {{Term/Store|Lumberjack's Riddles|Lumberjack's Riddles|Series, ALttP|plural}} are a {{Term|ALttP|Side Quest|link}} in {{ALttP&FS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP&FS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 128}}</ref>

==Procedure==
The Lumberjack's Riddles can only be started if [[Link]] has gathered at least 10 [[Medal of Courage|Medals of Courage]] in {{FS}} on the same game file.

To begin the quest, Link must first talk to [[Q. Bumpkin]] inside the Lumberjack's house, located north of the [[Death Mountain]] entrance.<ref>{{Cite|If you can solve some of my riddles, I'll make something special for you!|Q. Bumpkin|ALttP}}</ref> He will give Link the [[Basket]] and a riddle, and send him off to catch the creature or item with the [[Bug Catching Net]] that solves it.<ref>{{Cite|Take this basket. Put whatever you think the right item is in it and bring it to me.|Q. Bumpkin|ALttP}}</ref> After solving all 10 riddles, Link will receive the [[Hurricane Spin]].<ref>{{Cite|Nooo! This can't be! I'm no match for you! I guess I'll teach you my family's secret sword technique... Charge up your sword and press the B Button many times. Then your charge will last longer.|Q. Bumpkin|ALttP}}</ref> He will also receive three [[Carver's Statue]]s after solving the first, fourth, and seventh riddles.<ref>{{Cite|This time I'll give you the carver's statue I made. I'll send it to your house.|Q. Bumpkin|ALttP}}</ref>
