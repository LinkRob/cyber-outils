The {{Term/Store|Grip Ring||Series, TMC}} is an item in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Grip Ring}} is found halfway up {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}, inside a {{Term|TMC|Cave|link}} that needs to be opened with a {{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}}, south of the door that leads to {{Term|TMC|Grayblade|link}}'s dojo. Upon deflecting the {{Term|TMC|Business Scrub|link}}'s nut, it will offer the {{Term|TMC|Grip Ring}} for 40 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|OK, ya got me! Let me make it up to ya! This fabulous {{Color|TMC Red|Grip Ring}} is just perfect for a young mountaineer like yourself! Climb in style for only 40 Rupees! What a bargain! So, what do you say, huh?|Business Scrub|TMC}}</ref> With the {{Term|TMC|Grip Ring}} in his inventory, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is able to climb rocky walls, which allows him access the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}} and other areas he could not reach before.
