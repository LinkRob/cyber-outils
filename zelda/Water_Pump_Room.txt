The {{Term/Store|Water Pump Room||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 240}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Water Pump Room}} is located below {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}} runs down its northern wall. Partway down the {{Term|BotW|Water Pump Room|display=Room}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find a small side-room with a {{Term|BotW|Lever|link}}. Pulling this {{Term|BotW|Lever}} causes an {{Term|BotW|Updraft|link}} to rise from the floor. A passageway in the {{Term|BotW|Water Pump Room}} leads to the {{Term|BotW|West Passage|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lockup|link}}.

100 years ago, the {{Term|BotW|Water Pump Room}} was used to supply {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}.<ref name="CaC"/> Since the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, however, it has fallen into disrepair.<ref name="CaC"/>

On the wooden support beams at the top of the {{Term|BotW|Water Pump Room}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can discover a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Frostblade|link}}. There is a bombable wall in the side-room. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} destroys it, he will find a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Shard of Dinraal's Fang|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}} on a ledge high up in the {{Term|BotW|Water Pump Room}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} lifts the {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
