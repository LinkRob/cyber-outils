The {{Term/Store|Dah Kaso Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Dah Kaso Shrine}}<br/>A Minor Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Minor Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Dah Kaso Shrine}} is located in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} underneath the {{Term|BotW|Digdogg Suspension Bridge|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dah Kaso|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Dah Kaso. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Dah Kaso|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Dah Kaso Shrine}} features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}} wielding a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear|link}} will rise from a pit to challenge him.

In order to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}}. Doing this will cause the portcullis in the back of the chamber to rise. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Dah Kaso}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for overcoming the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Dah Kaso|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Dah Kaso}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Dah Kaso|BotW}}</ref>

There is a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the portcullis. Inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can recover an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Core|link}}.
