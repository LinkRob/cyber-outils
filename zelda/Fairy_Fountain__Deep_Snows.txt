==Overview==
Fairy Fountain: Deep Snows is a side quest located in [[Tabantha Frontier]] after completing the side quest, [[Offering Help on the Road]].

Unlike the other side quests unlocked, [[Fairy Fountain: Grassy Plains]] and [[Fairy Fountain: Desert Sands]], it does not have a time limit. Instead, the player has to protect the [[Rito Captain]] from [[Blue Moblin|Blue Moblins]], while defeating 5 [[Ice Moblin|Ice Moblins]] in order to progress through the challenge. Once all the Ice Moblins are defeated, 5 [[Treasure Chest|treasure chests]], each containing 300 [[Rupee|rupees]]. After heading to the targeted position, the character will need to fight [[Kaysa]]. Once defeated, the side quest will be completed. If this was completed on his/her first try, the rewards will be 1 [[Ethereal Stone]], 3 [[Fairy|Fairies]], 10 [[Hearty Salmon]], and a random weapon.
