==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Wildberry Crepes can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Wildberry|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fresh Milk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Bird Egg|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. By eating them, some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} will be restored to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The wildberries in this meal are noted to be sweet and tart, while the dough is thin and springy.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Since Wildberry Crepes require five ingredients to be made, all of which lack any status effects, no variations can be made.
