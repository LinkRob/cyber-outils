{{Term/Store|Maiamai|Maiamai|Series, ALBW|plural}} are a jointly snail and squid-like species that appear in {{ALBW}}. The '''Lost Maiamai''' are the children of {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}, scattered across the lands of {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}.

==Biography==
When {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} first meets {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai}} in a cave next to {{Term|ALBW|Lake Hylia|link}}, she pleads for the hero to find and collect her missing children and return them,<ref>{{Cite|Could you find all of them? You'll hear my children crying—calling for their Mother Maiamai!|Mother Maiamai|ALBW}}</ref> promising rewards in exchange. For every ten Lost {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai}} {{Term|ALBW|Link}} finds, {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai}} will upgrade one of his items into a "Nice" weapon (provided he owns the weapon and is not merely renting it from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio|link}}). To aid him in his search, {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai}} also gives {{Term|ALBW|Link}} the {{Term|ALBW|Maiamai Map|link}}, which tracks the number of Lost {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai}} remaining in each of the world's divided sections.

100 Lost {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai}} in total can be found in the overworlds of {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}, each of them crying for their mother. Their squeaking will be audible if the {{Term|ALBW|Maiamai}} is within the camera's view. If {{Term|ALBW|Link}} stands near a Lost {{Term|ALBW|Maiamai}}, he will turn his head and stare towards its direction, providing a hint towards its exact location if it is hidden. This also works when {{Term|ALBW|Link}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Maiamai}} are at different elevations. Many {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai}} are well hidden, and require certain abilities or items to rescue. Some {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai}} may be found under rocks or in grass. {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai}} hidden in trees can be jostled out by running into the tree with the {{Term|ALBW|Pegasus Boots|link}}. {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai}} stuck on walls can be pushed off by {{Term|ALBW|Link}} {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|link}} into the wall, then separating himself from the wall when he is beneath the {{Term|ALBW|Maiamai}}. Once {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can use the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod|link}}, he can use it to unearth {{Term|ALBW|Maiamai}} from sand as well.

After all {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai}} have been found, {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai}} will upgrade {{Term|ALBW|Link}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Spin Attack|link}} ability to the [[Great Spin]], and {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai}} will leave her cave with her children to a different dimension.<ref>{{Cite|It's a bit sudden, but we must bid you good-bye. I'm off to a different dimension with my Maiamais!|Mother Maiamai|ALBW}}</ref> After this, a single female {{Term|ALBW|Maiamai}} will stay behind in the cave, telling {{Term|ALBW|Link}} that she is the 72nd that he saved,<ref>{{Cite|Hello there, {{Player Name}}! I'm the 72nd Maiamai you saved! Thanks for all your help. Mom and the others have gone off. But I—? Well, I stayed behind. Time for me to be my own Maiamai.|Maiamai|ALBW}}</ref> and asking the young hero to wish her luck on being as good of a mother as {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hopefully I'll learn to be as good of a mother as my Mother Maiamai. Wish me luck, {{Player Name}}!|Maiamai|ALBW}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
"Maiamai" comes from the Japanese word マイマイ maimai, meaning "snail". The name is also a play on the phrase "My, oh, my!"
