{{Term/Store|Antique Coin|Antique Coins|Series, TFH|plural}},{{Exp Game|TFH}}<ref name="E 115"/> also known as {{Term/Store|Ancient Gold Piece|Ancient Gold Pieces|ST|plural}},{{Exp Game|ST}}<ref name="E 115"/> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, Ancient Gold Pieces are [[Treasure]]s. They were issued by the [[Bank of Hyrule]] during an unknown era.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 95}}</ref> A [[Triforce]] and the [[Hylian Language|Hylian]] text for "Hyrule Bank" have been molded on the Coins.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Antique Coin}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Fortress|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Sealed Gateway|link}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Antique Coin}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Bomb Storage|link}}''', ''"Win without using a sword!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Antique Coin}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Bomb Storage}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Antique Coin}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Training Ground|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Antique Coin}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Training Ground}}''', ''"Halved attack and defense!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Antique Coin}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Antique Coin}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Antique Coin}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make {{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Rupee Regalia|link}}.<ref name="E 115"/>
