{{Term/Store|Good-Sized Horse||Series, BotW}} is a [[Side Quest]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Good-Sized Horse}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Canyon}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can begin this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Zyle|link}}, who can be found standing near a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Zyle}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he was attacked by monsters,<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, so yeah, I'm a traveler like you... And I was attacked by monsters in this very canyon...|Zyle|BotW}}</ref> causing his {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} to run away and leaving him stranded.<ref>{{Cite|That's when my partner, that's to say, my {{Color|BotW Red|horse}}, ran off. And hey, I've been stranded here ever since...|Zyle|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Zyle}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to sell him a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} for 300 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} so that he may continue his travels.<ref>{{Cite|If, on your travels, you see a horse that would suit me, would you sell it to me for 100, no, {{Color|BotW Red|300 rupees}}?|Zyle|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, yeah, if I just had a {{Color|BotW Red|horse}}, I could continue my travels, and you're a fellow traveler, so I have to ask, right?|Zyle|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to find him a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Zyle}}'s only request is that the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} is not too big.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, thanks! But don't bring me {{Color|BotW Red|too big a horse}}... Won't be able to ride it! No, I need a {{Color|BotW Red|suitably sized horse}}!|Zyle|BotW}}</ref>

To get a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} for {{Term|BotW|Zyle}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must ride any [[Stable|unregistered Horse]] to his location and speak to him. A group of {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Pass|link}}. Upon returning with a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Zyle}} is excited that the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} is just like his old one and asks if he could have it for 300 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ohh! HEY! That {{Color|BotW Red|horse}}... It's just like my old partner! Talk about a fine {{Color|BotW Red|horse}}...|Zyle|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Think I could have this {{Color|BotW Red|horse}}? I'll pay you {{Color|BotW Red|300 rupees}} for it, what do you say, hey?|Zyle|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to sell the {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Zyle}} pays him 300 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} as promised.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, yeah thanks so much! Here's the {{Color|BotW Red|300 rupees}} I promised you as fair payment for this {{Color|BotW Red|horse}}!|Zyle|BotW}}</ref> Before departing, {{Term|BotW|Zyle}} expresses excitement about his new partner and the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! I'm making this {{Color|BotW Red|horse}} I bought from you my new partner! My new best buddy and traveling attendant!|Zyle|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So off we go, me and my partner! May we all have good travels!|Zyle|BotW}}</ref>

* There are three {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} that {{Term|BotW|Zyle}} will reject. ** If {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings {{Term|BotW|Zyle}} the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}, he will refuse to take it due to its size.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! You brought a {{Color|BotW Red|horse}}! But it's a little too big for me to ride...|Zyle|BotW}}</ref>
** If {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings {{Term|BotW|Zyle}} a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse|link}}, he will refuse to take it due to it being scary.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! You brought a {{Color|BotW Red|horse}}! But it's kind of really scary...|Zyle|BotW}}</ref>
** If {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings {{Term|BotW|Zyle}} the {{Term|BotW|Lord of the Mountain|link}}, he will refuse to take it since it is not a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey!
