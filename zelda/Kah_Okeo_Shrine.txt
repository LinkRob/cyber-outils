The {{Term/Store|Kah Okeo Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kah Okeo Shrine}}<br/>Wind Guide}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Wind Guide''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Kah Okeo Shrine}} is found in a hole in the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}, to the west of the {{Term|BotW|Rayne Highlands|link}}. To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Octo Balloon|link}} to remove the stone slab blocking the cave entrance. As he enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he will be greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kah Okeo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kah Okeo. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kah Okeo|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Kah Okeo Shrine}}, he will find a blocked portcullis and a {{Term|Series|Wind Switch|link}}. In a nearby {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf|link}}, which can be used to activate the {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}} and open the portcullis. In the following hallway is a {{Term|BotW|Bomb Barrel|link}} suspended in the air by three {{Plural|BotW|Balloon|link}}.

At the end of the hallway, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will come upon a tall room. At the bottom of the room are two {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword|link}}. By {{Term|BotW|Climbing|link}} to the top of the room, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find another {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}}. Activating this switch will spawn a platform suspended by four {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}}, which can be moved using the {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf}}. Reactivating the {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}} will respawn the platform.

The platform can be use to access the other side of the second room, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} can move further into {{Term|BotW|Kah Okeo Shrine}}. There is a locked portcullis in this room, beyond which the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} can be seen. Past this wall is a large room with a {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}} next to a {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}} embedded in the ground, which will send {{Term|BotW|Link}} into the air when it is activated. From here, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to reach another platform, also bearing a {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}} and a {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}. Though {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use this {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} to reach the next structure, he can also access it through a series of platforms and {{Plural|BotW|Ladder|link}}.

The next structure has an indentation that can be accessed via {{Term|BotW|Ladder}}. The bottom of this indentation has a cracked wall, which can be destroyed with a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}}. When this is done, an {{Term|BotW|Updraft|link}} will begin to flow through the structure's hollow center. After ascending the {{Term|BotW|Updraft}} with his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can access a higher series of platforms.

At the end of the first set of platforms is a fourth {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}}, which spawns another platform suspended by {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}} when it is activated. To proceed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will need to use his {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf}} to navigate the platform through a group of {{Plural|BotW|Floor Spikes|link}} that have been turned into walls. Past this maze, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can activate another {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}} that will activate a {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} below his feet, which will bring him to a third level.

On the third level, the top of the structure used on the first level can be seen to be blocked by bombable walls. In a side room, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can activate a {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}} to spawn {{Term|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} suspended by {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}}. Following this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must push the {{Term|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} out of the room with the {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf}}. Using either the {{Term|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} or {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can destroy the walls, allowing the {{Term|BotW|Updraft}} from before to continue upwards. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} activates the {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}} next to a {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} here, he will gain enough height to be able to reach the {{Term|BotW|Updraft}} with his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}}. At the top, there is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}}.

From the top of the structure, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to drift to a platform that leads to the locked portcullis. With the {{Term|BotW|Small Key}}, he will be able to unlock the portcullis, giving him access to {{Term|BotW|Kah Okeo}}. When approached, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for being able to complete the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero...}}|Kah Okeo|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, they grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Kah Okeo|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has received the {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|display=Orb}}, {{Term|BotW|Kah Okeo}} will wish him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Kah Okeo|BotW}}</ref>

After obtaining the floating platform in the second room, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use it to access a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} in the corner. This {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}. Past this door, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will encounter a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} in front of a cracked wall. The wall can be destroyed with either {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} or the nearby {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Barrel}}. A second {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be obtained by gliding to it after {{Term|BotW|Link}} uses the first {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}, and it contains a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Sword|link}}. Before attempting to navigate the {{Plural|BotW|Floor Spikes}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can move his platform to access a third {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, containing a {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}. Finally, as {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns from collecting the {{Term|BotW|Small Key}}, he can drift down to a platform above the locked portcullis. On this platform is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} with a {{Term|BotW|Thunderspear|link}} inside.
