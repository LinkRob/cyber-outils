{{Term/Store|Fruit and Mushroom Mix|Fruit and Mushroom Mixes|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Fruit and Mushroom mixes are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}} and any fruit together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. They're regular version is made by using either ingredients without status or ingredients with differing effects. Ingredients lacking statuses that can be used are {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Palm Fruit|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}}. Eating them will restore some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The sweetness of fruit in this dish is said to contrast with the savoriness of the {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Depending on the ingredients used, several different versions of Fruit and Mushroom Mix can be made:

* '''Hearty Fruit and Mushroom Mix''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}. Eating one will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Fruit and Mushroom Mix''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}}. When eaten, they'll replenish a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Fruit and Mushroom Mix''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}}. Consuming these mixes completely refills and overfills Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel}}.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Fruit and Mushroom Mix''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}}. If eaten, they'll temporarily boost Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Fruit and Mushroom Mix''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}}. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] for a short period of time.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Fruit and Mushroom Mix''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfruit|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}}. They give Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Fruit and Mushroom Mix''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}}. If eaten, they increase the power of Link's attacks for a time.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Fruit and Mushroom Mix''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}}. Eating them increases Link's defense temporarily.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Fruit and Mushroom Mix''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}}. Eating these mixes will increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Fruit and Mushroom Mix''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Fruit and Mushroom Mix}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This dish contrasts the sweetness of fruit with the savoriness of mushrooms.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}}. When eaten, they grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] temporarily.<ref name="Chilly"/>
