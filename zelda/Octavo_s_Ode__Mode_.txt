{{Term/Store|Octavo's Ode||Series, CoH}},<ref>{{Cite|Octavo's Ode|Select Mode|CoH}}</ref> known as '''Story Mode — Octavo''' prior to an update,<ref>{{Cite|Story Mode — Octavo|Select Mode|CoH}}</ref> is a {{Term|CoH|Mode|link}} in {{CoH}}. It was introduced as part of the {{OO}} [[Downloadable Content]] update and can only be played after {{Term|CoH|Story Mode|link}} has been completed.

==Overview==
{{Term|CoH|Octavo's Ode (Mode)}} is an additional story {{Term|CoH|Mode|link}} wherein {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} is the playable character. It details an alternate future of {{Term|CoH|Octavo}}'s journey after stealing the {{Term|CoH|Thread of Courage|link}} from a {{Term|CoH|Fate|link}}. In this version of events, many of {{Term|CoH|Octavo}}'s machinations have already been carried out, such as his meddling with the {{Term|CoH|Kakariko Crypt|link}} and the {{Term|CoH|Frozen Grotto|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I saw a strange man with a {{Color|CoH Yellow|lute}} doing something suspicious over near the crypt. Wait... It was you, wasn't it!? I'm watching you!|N/A|CoH}}</ref><ref name="Swimming">{{Cite|Have you tried swimming in the lake yet? Some musician made the grotto too chill for my liking.|Zora|CoH}}</ref>{{Note|Despite knowing of {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} and his deeds surrounding {{Term|CoH|Lake Hylia|link}}, the {{Plural|CoH|Zora|link}} and their {{Term|CoH|Prince|link}} do not seem to recognize {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} himself.<ref name="Swimming"/><ref>{{Cite|Some wizard guy came by and messed everything up. He's in the {{Color|CoH Blue|Frozen Grotto}} under the lake if you want to help. No pressure though.|Prince|CoH}}</ref> Prior to {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} finding the {{Term|CoH|Oboe|link}} within, they direct him to the {{Term|CoH|Frozen Grotto}}, claiming that is where he is located. Given that other characters such as {{Term|CoH|Barriara|link}} recognize {{Term|CoH|Octavo}},<ref>{{Cite|It's you! You're the one who put me to sleep! Prepare for a pounding! ... ... ... But wait... Why did you wake me up? Perhaps you're not as evil as you seem. If I give you this item, will you promise to help take care of the mischief in the ruins?|Barriara|CoH}}</ref> this is likely an oversight.}} He is accompanied by [[Trill (Cadence of Hyrule)|Trill]], who is simultaneously watching his actions and searching for someone who can stop him,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you're the guy who put my friends to sleep! I saw how you attack using that {{Color|CoH Yellow|Golden Lute}}. Whenever you move into a square adjacent to an enemy, that enemy will take damage. You can also use the lute to attack enemies directly in front of you. Now that I've seen your attacks, I just need to find someone who can stop you!|Trill|CoH}}</ref> indicating that the {{Term|CoH|Fairy|link}} has not yet met {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}. As in the primary story, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} put both {{Term|CoH|Link|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} to sleep. They can be found in {{Term|CoH|Link's House|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Kakariko Village|link}} respectively, though neither can be awoken.

Using the {{Term|CoH|Thread of Courage}}, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} transforms his {{Term|CoH|Lute|link}} into a {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute|link}}. He then travels to four dungeons in search of a Champion who can defeat {{Term|CoH|Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Finally, I'm free of that wretched crone's babbling! Now then, I'll need to find a {{Color|CoH Yellow|champion}} capable of defeating {{Color|CoH Blue|Ganon}}. This {{Color|CoH Yellow|Golden Lute}} should allow me to defend myself as I search.|Octavo|CoH}}</ref> Upon turning each {{Term|CoH|Instrument|link}} into a beast with the power of his {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute}}, they turn monstrous and attack him, forcing him to defeat them.<ref>{{Cite|A musical instrument! I sense power emanating from it. Interesting... With the magic in my lute, perhaps I can create a powerful beast with this! A champion to defeat {{Color|CoH Blue|Ganon}} for me!|Octavo|CoH}}</ref> With nobody to stop {{Term|CoH|Octavo}}, he collects all four {{Plural|CoH|Instrument}} and returns to {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle (Cadence of Hyrule)|link}} and prepares to travel to the future to battle {{Term|CoH|Ganon}} himself.<ref>{{Cite|With the {{Color|CoH Yellow|Threads of Fate}} and the {{Color|CoH Yellow|Golden Lute}}, my power has grown! Nothing in {{Color|CoH Yellow|Hyrule}} remains to challenge me. I can feel it... I have come powerful enough to defeat {{Color|CoH Blue|Ganon}} on my own! I must hurry to the future.|Octavo|CoH}}</ref>

However once {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} arrives in the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}}, he finds his future self, now known as {{Term|CoH|The NecroDancer|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What? You're not {{Color|CoH Blue|Ganon}}! Who are you?|Octavo|CoH}}</ref> {{Term|CoH|The NecroDancer}} explains that he defeated {{Term|CoH|Ganondorf|link}} before he could rise to power and proceeded to conquer {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}} for his own gain.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, how quaint. It's my past self! Don't worry, dear {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}}. Your troubles are over. I've silenced the fortune-tellers, broken the bonds of fate, and defeated {{Color|CoH Blue|Ganondorf}} before he could take power! And now I will rule over {{Color|CoH Blue|Hyrule}} at last!|The NecroDancer|CoH}}</ref> {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} rejects his future self's plan to rule {{Term|CoH|Hyrule}} and initiates battle with him.<ref>{{Cite|Rule over {{Color|CoH Blue|Hyrule}}? That was never what I wanted! I just wanted to prepare for the inevitable... You've become a monster, like the one I wanted to stop! I'll put an end to you!|Octavo|CoH}}</ref> After defeating his future self, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} uses the power of their two {{Plural|CoH|Golden Lute}} to return himself to a point before he acted to change the fate of {{Term|CoH|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now it is time to atone for what I have done.|Octavo|CoH}}</ref> He then affirms to leave his own fate in the hands of wiser people.<ref>{{Cite|(gasp)|Octavo|CoH}}<br/>{{Cite|Ah! I see that you're back. Are you still dying to know what fate has in store for you?|Fate|CoH}}<br/>{{Cite|No! No. I will leave that for those who are much wiser than I.|Octavo|CoH}}</ref>
