The {{Term/Store|Armor Ring L-1||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Armor Ring L-1<br/>Damage taken {{Icon|OoA Down Arrow}}<br/>Sword damage {{Icon|OoA Down Arrow}}|Quest Status Screen|OoA}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Armor Ring L-1 can be found in the [[Moblin's Keep]] in {{OoA}}. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee|Rupees]]. When worn, it decreases damage taken by [[Link]] while also decreasing [[Sword]] damage.<ref name="Description"/> The Armor Ring L-1 can be transferred to {{OoS}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
