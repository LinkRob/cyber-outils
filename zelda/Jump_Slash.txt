==Overview==
Introduced in {{OoT}} as one of [[Link]]'s signature special [[Sword]] techniques in the franchise, he performs the Jump Slash by [[Targeting]] an enemy, followed by leaping forward into the air, then aggressively bringing down his sword towards the foe. He can also use this attack if Link were to leap from heights (but it does not prevent damage from great falls). In earlier cases of the Jump Attack's appearances, it deals [[Weapon Strength|twice the damage]] of a regular sword strike like the [[Spin Attack]], making it useful against stronger enemies, though since {{TP}}, it now only inflicts 1.5 times the damage.<ref>{{Cite|If you have your sword ready while (Z) Targeting, you can do a jump attack by pressing (A)! A jump attack does double damage!|Kokiri|OoT}}</ref><ref name="OoT">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT |publisher= Nintendo |page= 10}}</ref><ref name="MM">{{Cite manual|quote= The jump attack does twice as much damage as a normal swing, but it will take a moment to move again afterwards. |game= MM |page= 23}}</ref> However, much like the aforementioned Spin Attack, the Jump Attack, though iconic among fans, is in fact a basic move; A few games have upgraded variants of the technique, such as the [[Jump Strike]] in {{TP|-}}, or a [[Parry Attack]] (when enemies perform a horizontal attack) in {{TWW}}.

This move is effective against most enemies, but some foes, such as [[Darknut]]s in {{TP|-}} or certain [[Boss|bosses]] in various games are able to dodge or counter it if not timed correctly. Moreover, for a short while after Link performs a Jump Slash, he is vulnerable to enemy attacks.<ref name="MM"/><ref>{{Cite manual|quote= While holding the sword, press Z + A. Offensive power will double. |game= OoT |page= 20}}</ref>

Certain enemies in various games such as Darknuts, [[Stalfos]], [[Bokoblin|Bokoblins]], and even [[Dark Link]] or [[Ganondorf]] can also perform a Jump Attack to counter Link.

To perform this attack in any 3D ''Zelda'' game, [[Link]] must have his [[Sword]] drawn, and he must be targeting the [[enemy]].<ref name="OoT"/><ref>{{Cite|And finally, the jump attack. Press (A) after drawing your sword and Z-Targeting with (Z). Begin.|Swordsman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Nintendo |page= 9}}</ref> When both conditions are met, a simple press of the action button will unleash the Jump Attack. Some titles, however, may require a slightly different approach in order to execute it due to controls. The Jump Attack can also be performed when Link falls from a height to strike enemies or objects as he comes down.

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, the Jump Attack can be performed by "tapping" the control stick of a [[Nintendo GameCube|GameCube]] controller, or D-Pad of a connected [[Game Boy Advance]], and simultaneously pressing the sword button. It is much harder to pull off than in other incarnations, but serves less of a purpose now, as the [[Roll Attack]] is better at dealing damage from a distance, and multiple regular strikes up close are comparatively equal in dealing damage up close.

==={{TP|-}}===
Link performs the Jump Attack in {{TP}} with only one hand instead of two, and this time around less enemies are affected by it. While in mid-air, if the player moves the Control Stick in a circle, Link will do a [[Spin Attack]] upon landing.

==={{PH|-}}===
The {{Term|PH|Jump Slash}} can be performed by tapping on an enemy with the [[Stylus]], but only while Link is distant from his target.<ref name="PH"/> At closer range, the hero will do a more modest Sword thrust instead.

==={{ST|-}}===
The {{Term|ST|Jump Slash}} can be performed by tapping on an enemy with the Stylus, but only while Link is distant from his target.<ref name="ST"/> At closer range, Link will do a more modest Sword thrust instead. Like in {{OoT|-}}, [[Dark Link]] is also able to perform this move; however, he jumps much higher and more slowly than his good counterpart does, giving him time to dodge.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, {{Plural|SS|Jump Attack}} are one of Link's [[Link's Special Sword Strike|Special Sword Strikes]]. They can be performed by Z-Targeting, jumping forward and then shaking the [[Wii Remote]]. The Jump Attack in {{SS|-}} does the same amount of damage than a normal slash.

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, Link must jump manually with the {{Icon|BotW X}} button and then attack with the {{Icon|BotW Y}} button to execute a Jump Slash. Its execution varies slightly depending on the weapon used, but it always deals double the amount of damage and creates a small shock-wave upon impact.

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', the procedure of the Jump Slash is the same as its prequel. [[Ganondorf]] can perform this attack against Link during the final battle.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SCII|-}}===
The Jump Attack, called Running Hack in-game, is one of Link's moves in {{SCII}}. The young hero can perform it by running up to the enemy and then pressing the vertical attack button. It can deal up to 42 HP worth of damage and is considered to be a mid-attack.

==={{SSB4|-}}===
Link can perform a Jump Attack in {{SSB4}}. It serves as his dash attack, which is executed by running and pressing the attack button.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
The Jump Attack returns as Link's dash attack in {{SSBU}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

== Trivia ==
[[File:OoT Jump Attack.gif|thumb|281x281px|Despite its usefulness, the frequent usage of the Jump Slash in the early 3D games against bosses such as [[Phantom Ganon]] in {{OoT|-}} as the main attack by many players has led to criticism among others who prefer to battle enemies fairly]]
* Similar to the [[Spin Attack]], the Jump Attack in older 3D ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|Zelda]]'' games, specifically ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]],'' {{MM}} and {{TWW}}, due to its high damage output and easy to perform, is a common tactic among experienced players or the [[speedrun]] community to defeat tougher enemies or [[Boss|bosses]] quickly and is even suggested by their official strategy guides. However, because of this, the technique by [[Link]] is often used as their sole attack move and therefore arguably the most abused; Using the Jump Attack repetitively, especially with stronger [[Sword|Swords]] ([[Biggoron's Sword]], [[Great Fairy's Sword|Great Fairy Sword]], etc.), is frowned upon by some fans against such foes, as in those cases it is viewed as a cheap shot and often takes away the challenge to the point it even caused a [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda series|controversial debate]] that this move breaks the difficulty of certain installments. In addition, unlike the Spin Attack, due to the requirement of [[targeting]] the enemy as well as leaping forward when executed, the Jump Attack can prove dangerous or rather reckless to use against fast-moving opponents or incoming attacks, especially if Link misses.
* In {{TWW|-}}, if Link were to be hit from a [[Darknut]]'s Jump Slash, which can occur after it dodges the hero's own, he will lose two [[Heart Container|Hearts]] in damage and be sent flying a great distance, not unlike when it mimics the Spin Attack.
** Link will also take the same amount of damage if he were to be struck by a [[Stalfos]]' Jump Slash in {{OoT|-}}.
* In ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', there are two versions of the Jump Attack, one where Link runs at his opponent, and another if he backflips and then performs it in an identical manner seen in the core series.
