"{{Term/Store|The Skull's Eye||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Skull's Eye}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Jerrin|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. When spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} explains that she us a scholar of ancient history who specializes in studying {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm an amateur scholar of ancient history. To be honest...I've been focusing a lot of my research on {{Color|BotW Blue|shrines}}.|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> She then remembers that there is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} atop {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link|display=Skull Lake's}} left eye, though it is challenging to reach.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh, that reminds me! Link, there's somewhere you need to go. Umm... The {{Color|BotW Red|left eye of Skull Lake}}! I know there's a {{Color|BotW Blue|shrine}} there, but it's a challenge to get to it...|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> While instructing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to visit the proper eye of the {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|display=Lake}}, she warns him that accessing the {{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai Shrine|display=Shrine}} will prove a challenge.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|left eye}} of {{Color|BotW Red|Skull Lake}} is higher up. The right one is lower down. If you run into {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}, you're on the wrong eye!|Jerrin|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.{{Note|It is possible to immediately complete this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} by activating {{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai Shrine}} before speaking to {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}}.}}

The {{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai Shrine|link}} is located at the top of a rock spire in {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake}}. To access it, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must either climb the rock formation, or glide to it with the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|display=Quest}} is completed when {{Term|BotW|Link}} opens the {{Term|BotW|Zuna Kai Shrine|display=Shrine}}.
