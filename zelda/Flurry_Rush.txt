A {{Term/Store|Flurry Rush|Flurry Rushes|Series, BotW}} is a recurring ability in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Flurry Rush|N/A|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
A flurry rush is an ability in {{BotW}}  after {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} successfully executing a {{Term|BotW|Perfect Dodging|link|display=Perfect Dodge}}. A perfect dodge is executed by performing a Backflip or a Side-Hop right before an enemy attack hits you. This causes time to slow down, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to perform multiple hits in quick succession with his {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in a short period of time. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not hit the enemy within a certain period of time after initiating the {{Term|BotW|Flurry Rush}}, it will cancel, and the flow of time will normalize. This assault can bypass certain defenses, such as shields or invincibility. If the weapon being used during a flurry rush breaks, or if Link gets knocked back from damage, flurry rush will end. It's possible to jump during the final hit of a Flurry Rush to cancel it early, but it can't be activated again immediately by doing this. 

=== {{TotK|-}} ===
A Flurry Rush works the exact same way as it does in {{BotW|-}}, although the animation for it is sped up slightly. [[Royal Gear|Royal Weapons]] now have a special modifier to them, which increases the power of flurry rush during perfect dodge. This doubles their damage output during a Flurry Rush.{{Fact|TotK}} [[Ganondorf#Tears of the Kingdom|Ganondorf]] is also able to perform a Flurry Rush in the final boss fight. During the third phase, he even does this after Link Flurry Rushes. Unlike Link's Flurry Rush, Ganondorf's can be dodged or blocked.
