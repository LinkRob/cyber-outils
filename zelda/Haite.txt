{{Term/Store|Haite||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Haite|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Haite}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} child who lives at the {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, which is located on the southwestern edge of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. She runs around the {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|display=Stable}} during the day. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to her during this time, she will politely respond.<ref>{{Cite|Yes?|Haite|BotW}}</ref> When he inquires about what she is up to, {{Term|BotW|Haite}} will remark that she is playing tag and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants to join.<ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] Playing tag! You want to play?|Haite|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses to end the conversation instead of asking her a question, {{Term|BotW|Haite}} will be annoyed a blow a raspberry at him.<ref>{{Cite|[Nothing.] Thbbb!|Haite|BotW}}</ref>

If it begins to {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}} while {{Term|BotW|Haite}} is out, she will run indoors to get out of the bad {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Raaaain!|Haite|BotW}}</ref> She will then sit on the floor, hoping for the {{Term|BotW|Rain}} to stop.<ref>{{Cite|I wish the rain would stop.|Haite|BotW}}</ref> As soon as the {{Term|BotW|Rain}} lets up, she will bolt outside and continue her game of tag.

In the evening, {{Term|BotW|Haite}} joins {{Term|BotW|Myti|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} for dinner.<ref>{{Cite|Eat up now.|Myti|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Din din!|Haite|BotW}}</ref> She will explain to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she eats her vegetables so that she can grow big and strong.<ref>{{Cite|I eat vegetables 'cause I wanna be big and strong!|Haite|BotW}}</ref> At night, {{Term|BotW|Haite}} sleeps in the {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}} that is second from the right.<ref>{{Cite|Gonna sleep!|Haite|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Zzz... Zzz...|Haite|BotW}}</ref> While asleep, she mutters about being full and tired.<ref>{{Cite|So full... So sweepy...|Haite|BotW}}</ref>
