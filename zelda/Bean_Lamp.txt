{{Term/Store|Bean Lamp|Bean Lamps|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Maca">{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|bean lamps}} act as landmarks. You'll want to familiarize yourself with them to make it easier to find your way around.|Maca|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Bean Lamp}} are a source of light found in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Maca|link}}, about the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|display=Forest}}, they will explain that {{Plural|BotW|Bean Lamp}} serve as landmarks to aid in navigation.<ref name="Maca"/> They suggest that {{Term|BotW|Link}} familiarize himself with the {{Term|BotW|Bean Lamp|display=Lamps}} to help find his way around.<ref name="Maca"/>

The path leading into the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}} is lined with {{Plural|BotW|Bean Lamp}}, in addition to the two paths leading around {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree|link}}. They are also found on top of {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}}, where {{Term|BotW|Walton|link}} gives the "{{Term|BotW|Riddles of Hyrule|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. In addition, {{Plural|BotW|Bean Lamp}} lead to the three {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial|link}} found at the edges of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|display=Forest}}.
