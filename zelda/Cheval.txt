{{Term/Store|Cheval||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Cheval was an old inventor who lived south of [[Lynna Village]] in the past. He is met once by [[Link]] on his journey, when Link was searching for the [[Cheval Rope]]. At the time, Cheval had not finished his rope, and was optimistic that he would finish it before his death. His ultimate goal was to create a rope that would not deteriorate when wet.<ref>{{Cite|Before I die, I want to finish my work on flippers for swimming and rope that won't decay in water.|Cheval|OoA}}</ref>

Cheval was successful, and did manage to finish it before his death, as well as a swimming aid, the [[Flippers|Zora's Flippers]]. When Cheval died, a pair of Zora's Flippers and a coil of Cheval Rope were buried with him in [[Cheval's Grave|his Grave]]. With these inventions, Cheval was a huge help to Link, who could not swim without the flippers, or reach [[Crescent Island]] without the rope.

==Nomenclature==
''Cheval'' means ''horse'' in French.
