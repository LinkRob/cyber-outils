The {{Term/Store|Masked Follower|Masked Followers|Series, ALBW|plural}} are a group of characters found in [[Lorule]] in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Biography==
The Masked Followers are a group of [[Lorule#Population|Loruleans]] that have begun to wear masks in the images of monsters. They are a cult led by the [[Masked Elder]], who preaches to his followers and visitors to resist the corruption of thievery and to don the masks of monsters,<ref>{{Cite|Though we have seen the world crumble before us, we must not give in to the corruption of thievery!|Masked Elder|ALBW}}</ref> which he states will bring them salvation from Lorule's deteriorating world.<ref>{{Cite|Monsters can keep you strong! They are your only salvation, my son! You must don the mask! Don the mask!|Masked Elder|ALBW}}</ref><ref name="Mumbo1">{{Cite|We are corrupt. CORRUPT! Do not be deceived. Only monsters will save us now! Mumbo jumbo, mumbo jumbo...|Masked Elder|ALBW}}</ref> These beliefs are shared by the members.<ref name="Mumbo2">{{Cite|The only way to get saved is to become a monster! C'mon! Get saved! Mumbo jumob, mumbo jumbo...|Masked Follower|ALBW}}</ref> The Masked Elder can be found standing on a pedestal in {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}, preaching to three of his followers. While only this group state themselves to be Masked Followers, other people wearing masks can also be found throughout Lorule, implying a connection to the main group. The [[Captain]] in the Thieves' Town [[Milk Bar]] also suggests there are more followers.<ref>{{Cite|Look around, kiddo. It's all thieves and creepy masked folks in this village.|Captain|ALBW}}</ref>

Two characters are referred to as Masked Follower by name. The male Masked Follower can be found with the Elder, and mentions to Link how the statues to the north of town are entrances to a hideout,<ref>{{Cite|Those statues at the north edge of town are entrances to some sort of hideout.|Masked Follower|ALBW}}</ref> hinting the location of the [[Thieves' Hideout (A Link Between Worlds)|Thieves' Hideout]]. Although frightened, he had tried to knock on the door, but was told to leave. The female Masked Follower complains how she cannot see through her mask's eye, but implores that Link also wear a mask and be saved.<ref name="Mumbo2"/> Another follower, the '''Masked Granny''', states she is the Elder's housekeeper and was made to join as part of the job.<ref>{{Cite|I'm the guru's housekeeper. If the guru says that this is part of the job, then who am I to complain?|Masked Granny|ALBW}}</ref>

The Masked Followers have a tendency of saying "Mumbo jumbo" at the end of their conversations.<ref name="Mumbo1"/><ref name="Mumbo2"/> Given that the Masked Granny was made to join, it may be that some followers may have been forced into the group.

==Trivia==
*Several of the Masked Followers wear masks resembling characters from {{ALttP}}.
**The male Masked Follower's mask resembles the [[Bomb Shopkeeper]].
**The female Masked Follower's mask resembles a [[Hinox]].
**The Masked Granny's mask resembles the [[Digging Game]] keeper.
**The [[Derby Girl]]'s mask resembles the transformed [[Bully]].
**The [[Lakeside Item Seller]]'s mask resembles the [[Shooting Gallery#A Link to the Past|Shooting Gallery]] keeper.
**The [[Bird-Masked Man]]'s mask bares a slight resemblance to the [[Storyteller|storytelling bird]].
**The [[Thief Girl]]'s mask looks like a [[Pikku]], which is appropriate as they are both thieves.
