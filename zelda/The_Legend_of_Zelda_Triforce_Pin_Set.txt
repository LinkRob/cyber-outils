'''''The Legend of Zelda'' Triforce Pin Set''' is a set of pins manufactured by [[Penny Arcade]].

==Overview==
''The Legend of Zelda'' Triforce Pin Set is a set of four decorative pins based on [[:File:TLoZ Series Triforce Artwork.png|general artwork of the {{Term|Series|Triforce}} from {{TLoZ|-}} series]]. It is part of the [[Penny Arcade]] series of products released by [[Penny Arcade]]. Three of the pins feature silhouettes of {{Term|Series|Link|link}}, {{Term|Series|Princess Zelda|link}}, and {{Term|Series|Ganondorf|link}} alongside {{Term|Series|Hylian Language|display=Hylian|link}} text of their names and the corresponding crests of {{Term|Series|Farore|link}}, {{Term|Series|Nayru|link}}, or {{Term|Series|Din|link}}. Both {{Term|Series|Link}} and {{Term|Series|Ganondorf}}'s pins are painted their associated colors of green and red, respectively. However, {{Term|Series|Princess Zelda}}'s pin is painted fuchsia instead of the blue often associated with the {{Term|Series|Triforce|display=Triforce of Wisdom|link}}. The uncolored center pin depicts the {{Term|Series|Royal Crest|link|display=Hyrule Crest}}. The packaging for the Pin Set also features [[:File:TLoZ Series Hyrule Map Artwork.png|artwork of a map of the {{Term|Series|Hyrule}}]].
