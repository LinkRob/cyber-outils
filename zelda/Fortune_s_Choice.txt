{{Term/Store|Fortune's Choice||Series, ALBW}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Overview==
{{Term|ALBW|Fortune's Choice}} appears in corresponding locations in both {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}, inside a house in {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}, respectively. Each {{Term|ALBW|Mini-Game|link}} is run by its respective world's {{Term|ALBW|Fortune's Choice Guy|link}}.

==Rules==
Both {{Plural|ALBW|Mini-Game}} task {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} with simply opening his choice of {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Chest|link}}(s) from a group of available Chests. He wins whichever prizes have been randomly assigned to the Chest(s) he opens. The available prizes are pre-determined.

===Hyrule===
In {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}, {{Term|ALBW|Fortune's Choice}} costs 50 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}} and allows {{Term|ALBW|Link}} to open one of two {{Plural|ALBW|Treasure Chest}}.<br/>

==Trivia==
*Statistically, the average winnings in {{Term|ALBW|Fortune's Choice}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}} is 50.5 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}—only 1% greater than the cost to play—meaning that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} would just barely break even by playing the {{Term|ALBW|Mini-Game}} repeatedly.
*The average winnings in {{Term|ALBW|Fortune's Choice}} in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}, after the {{Term|ALBW|Piece of Heart}} is collected, is 252.6 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}; over 26% greater than the cost to play. This makes {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}'s version of the {{Term|ALBW|Mini-Game}} more profitable to {{Term|ALBW|Link}}.
