{{Term/Store|Ingredient|Ingredients|Series|plural}} are items used to craft [[Recipes]] in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Location and Uses==
Most ingredients are dropped after defeating an enemy, though some ingredients can be obtained through interacting with the overworld, such as rustling trees or falling into bodies of water, albeit at the cost of 150 [[Rupee]]s each time Tingle falls in
