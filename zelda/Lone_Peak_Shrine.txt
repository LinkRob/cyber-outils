The {{Term/Store|Lone Peak Shrine||Series, MM}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lone Peak Shrine ahead. Watch your step! It's a long way down!|Sign|MM}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Lone Peak Shrine is located across an abyss in [[Goron Village]]. In order to reach the entrance, [[Link]] must follow [[Kaepora Gaebora]] as it drops feathers on invisible platforms leading to it. The [[Lens of Truth]] can be found in the Lone Peak Shrine inside a [[Treasure Chest]]. Two other Chests can also be found hidden behind [[Boulder]]s that can be destroyed with [[Bomb]]s. One of the Chests contains a Purple [[Rupee]], while an invisible Chest guarded by an invisible [[Skulltula]] contains a Red Rupee.

Once spring arrives to the Goron Village, the floating platforms leading to the Lone Peak Shrine will become visible.
