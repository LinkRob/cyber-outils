{{Term/Store|Minshi Woods||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Minshi Woods'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Minshi Woods is a tree-covered path in the [[Great Hyrule Forest]]. The path leads directly into the [[Lost Woods]], and fairy particle effects appear on screen near the transition from Minshi Woods to the Forest.

==Nomenclature==
Minshi comes from [[Minish]].
