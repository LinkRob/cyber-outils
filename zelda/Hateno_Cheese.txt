==Location and Uses==
Hateno Cheese can be available after completing the {{Term|TotK|Side Quest|link}} {{Term|TotK|A Letter to Koyin|link}}. Once done, it can be purchased regularly at {{Term|TotK|Hateno Village|link}} from two stores.<ref>{{Cite|Friend, this is your lucky day! We just started stocking Hateno Cheese!|Pruce|TotK}}</ref> It is also for sale at {{Term|TotK|Lookout Landing|link}} or from certain traveling merchants. It can be eaten raw to restore one heart. They can be used in a {{Plural|TotK|Cooking Pot|link}} to make certain dishes.

For the Side Quest "{{Term|TotK|A New Signature Food|link}}", {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} needs to bring a Hateno Cheese to help Reede jog his memory of the sample of the food he once tasted.<ref>{{Cite|Way back when, my grandfather worked with <font color{{=}}"Crimson">someone from the village</font> to create a special kind of food. It was too ahead of its time to catch on, so it never went further than the first trial run, but I never forgot it. Even today, I can still recall the <font color{{=}}"Crimson">gooey mouthfeel</font> of the one bite my grandfather shared with me. In the back of my mind, I've always wanted to make it a <font color{{=}}"Crimson">signature staple</font> of this village. If I could accomplish that, then I'd have something that could stand up to Cece!|Reede|TotK}}</ref>

When [[Cooking]], Hateno Cheese is used for creating dishes with melty cream toppings. Specifically, it's needed to make these following dishes:
