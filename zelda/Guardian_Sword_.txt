{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword+}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}} in "A Moderate Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. They can be obtained by defeating the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} that use them. In addition to this, they have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn after scanning the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword+}} are more powerful than regular {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword|link}}, and they slightly more durability as well.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword+}} will deal additional damage when used again {{Term|BotW|Guardian}}-type {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. They will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. Finally, the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} will not be able to pick them up, and they do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword+}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} used by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}} in {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>
