The {{Term/Store|Ocean Sanctuary||Series, ST}} is a location in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 290}}</ref> It is found within the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|ST|Ocean Sanctuary|display=Sanctuary}} is situated on a small island just beyond {{Term|ST|Papuchia Village|link}} and primarily serves as the abode of the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm|display=Realm}}'s {{Term|ST|Lokomo|link}} guardian, {{Term|ST|Carben|link}}. The outside of the {{Term|ST|Ocean Sanctuary|display=Sanctuary}}, which is divided in two sections, is the biggest {{Term|ST|Ocean Sanctuary|display=Sanctuary}} in all of {{Term|ST|New Hyrule|link}}.

{{Term|ST|Link}} comes to the {{Term|ST|Ocean Sanctuary|display=Sanctuary}} to meet with {{Term|ST|Carben}} after restoring just enough of the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm|display=Realm}}'s lost {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} to reach it. In order to access the chamber of the {{Term|ST|Ocean Sanctuary|display=Sanctuary}} in which {{Term|ST|Carben}} is located, {{Term|ST|Link}} must trace the paths of the {{Plural|ST|Tribal Head Statue|link|display=Stone Statues}}' gazes that are scattered throughout the {{Term|ST|Ocean Sanctuary|display=Sanctuary}}.<ref>{{Cite|Follow the gazes of the statues. Trace their paths to open the door.|Sign|ST}}</ref> Once he draws the correct figure on a panel, the door to the chamber will open only to reveal an empty room with a sign left by {{Term|ST|Carben}}, indicating that he is in {{Term|ST|Papuchia Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm at Papuchia Village visiting my sky friends. -Carben|Carben|ST}}</ref> After getting {{Term|ST|Carben}}'s attention through the use of the {{Term|ST|Song of Birds|link}}, {{Term|ST|Link}} takes {{Term|ST|Carben}} aboard the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} and returns with him to the {{Term|ST|Ocean Sanctuary|display=Sanctuary}}, fighting off invading {{Plural|ST|Pirate Ship (Enemy)|link|display=Pirates}} along the way.<ref>{{Cite|Hmph. Let me guess--you want me to restore the Spirit Tracks, don't you? I was hoping to relax a little longer, but fine. Take me to the Ocean Sanctuary on your train.|Carben|ST}}</ref> At the {{Term|ST|Ocean Sanctuary|display=Sanctuary}}, the pair, with the help of {{Term|ST|Link}}'s {{Term|ST|Spirit Flute|link}} and {{Term|ST|Carben}}'s [[wikipedia:flute|flute]], play the required {{Term|ST|Lokomo Song|link}} to restore the lost {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)}} that lead to the entrance of the {{Term|ST|Ocean Floor|link}} and, eventually, to the {{Term|ST|Ocean Temple|link}}.
