{{Term/Store|Toon Zelda||Series, HW}}<ref name="name and title"></ref> is a playable Warrior in {{HW}}, appearing in the {{PH|-}} & {{ST|-}} DLC pack. Her design is based on [[Princess Zelda#Spirit Tracks|Zelda]]'s appearance in {{ST}}, with an added airbrush shading as opposed to her normal {{Wp|cel shading}}.

==Biography==
Toon Zelda is a ghostly spirit who attacks using a [[Phantom Zelda|Phantom]] [[Phantom Arms|vessel]]. Apart from her [[Sword]] attacks, Toon Zelda can curl into an iron ball like a Wrecker Phantom and warp over enemies like a Warp Phantom. She can also come out of the Phantom's body and use light energy attacks. Toon Zelda is cowardly afraid of [[Rat]]s, which she uses to perform attacks as well.

Toon Zelda does not drop any unique [[Material]]s when defeated in battle. She instead drops [[Tetra's Sandals]] as a Silver Material and [[Tetra's Bandana]] as a Gold Material.
