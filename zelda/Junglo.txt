'''Junglo''' the Jungle Hero is a character in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} In his youth {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo|link}} was a muscular Tarzan-like hero, but over the years he has become old and frail. He lives in the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Forest|link}} where he raised his adopted daughter {{Term|FPTRR|Aba|link}}. He can be found in the area Deku Forest.

== Story ==
[[File:FPTRR Female Baboon Sprite.png|left|Junglo's Baboon]]
{{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}} is first encountered near the waterfall in the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Forest|link}}. When {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} first meets up with {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}}, he is being attacked by insects. After defeating the insects, {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}} asks {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} to escort him home. By offering to escort him, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} loses his current {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard|link}}. After escorting {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}} home, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can obtain several {{Plural|FPTRR|Item|link}} such as an {{Term|FPTRR|Bottle|display=empty jar|link}} and the {{Term|FPTRR|Recipe|link}} for Sleepy Spray.
[[File:Junglophoto.gif|left|frameless|200px]]
There are two {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}} Bells located in {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Forest}}. The first one is near where {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} first arrives. When {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} rings the bell {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}} comes to his assistance and has a large female baboon form a bridge over the gap. The second bell is located in front of the {{Term|FPTRR|Lost Woods|link}}. {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}} will adventure into the woods and set up signs to point {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} in the right direction.

{{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} soon learns that {{Term|FPTRR|Aba|link}} is not {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}}'s real daughter. Overhearing this, {{Term|FPTRR|Aba}} grows angry with {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}} and leaves. Later, it is revealed that {{Term|FPTRR|Aba}} is the {{Term|FPTRR|Armourer|link}}'s daughter. After {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} finds her father, {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}} will reward him.
