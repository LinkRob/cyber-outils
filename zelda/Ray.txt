'''Rays''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Rays are dark purple stingrays encountered only in a single screen area of [[Shrine of Water]]. They take several hits to defeat, even if [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] has six [[Celestial Sign]]s. If Zelda touches a Ray, she loses a [[Heart]] of her health.
