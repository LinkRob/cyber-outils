The {{Term/Store|Quick Beetle||Series, SS, SSHD}} is an {{Term|SSHD|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 135 }}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|SSHD|Quick Beetle}} is an optional upgrade for the {{Term|SSHD|Beetle|link}} available at the {{Term|SSHD|Scrap Shop|link}} in the {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft Bazaar|link}}. It allows the {{Term|SSHD|Beetle}} to fly faster.<ref>"''You've upgraded to the Quick Beetle! It flies faster than the Beetle. Watch where you're flying, speedy!''" — N/A (''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword HD|Skyward Sword HD]]'')</ref>

==Trivia==
*{{Plural|SSHD|Bokoblin|link}} are afraid of the {{Term|SSHD|Quick Beetle}} and will run away from it. {{Plural|SSHD|Red Bokoblin (Archer)|link|display= Archer Bokoblins}} will attempt to shoot it at first sight.
*The {{Term|SSHD|Quick Beetle}} cannot be used during the {{Term|SSHD|Horde Battle|link}}.
