{{Name|Japanese|{{Term/Store|Karuna||Series, LA (Cagiva)}}}} is a powerful pig-like warrior that appears in the manga for {{LA}}.{{Name Ref}}

==Biography==
Karuna is the self-proclaimed master of [[Ukuku Prairie]]. He bears resemblance to, and likely is, a [[Moblin]]. After [[Link]] has broken his sword in [[Angler's Tunnel]], Karuna soon engages him in battle. He proves to be a strong adversary, besting Link and almost being the death of him. However, [[Ghost (Link's Awakening)|Nakura]]'s help allows Link to both survive and gain a new sword. When Link and Karuna meet for a second time, Karuna is quickly struck down and killed.

== Nomenclature ==

Karuna's Japanese name is spelled as カルナ.
