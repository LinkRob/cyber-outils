The {{Term/Store|Royal Bridle||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Bridle}}<br/>This bridle is well known for being a favorite of the Hyrulean royal family. Its gold ornamentations leave an impression, but its true value lies in its solid craftsmanship.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Royal Bridle is a [[Bridle]] that can be obtained from [[Toffa]] by completing the [[Side Quest]] "[[The Royal White Stallion]]". Prior to the second [[Great Calamity]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] equipped her {{Term|BotW|White Horse|link}} with the custom-made Royal Bridle and its counterpart, the [[Royal Saddle]], as part of an attempt to bond with her [[Horse]].<ref>{{Cite|At first, I wasn't sure if I should outfit him with all of the royal gear. I thought maybe he should have to earn it first. But it works! He wears it like a true natural.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm trying to be a bit more empathetic. Benefit of the doubt, you know?|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Following the Great Calamity, Toffa's grandfather inherited the Royal Bridle and passed it down to Toffa.<ref>{{Cite|What? You brought the royal family's {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}? Well I'll be... Grandpa wasn't lying! Son, you just made my life. Staying alive in this godforsaken land may just have been worth it after all.|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> By registering the White Horse at a Stable and showing it to Toffa, the elderly man will gift Link the Royal Bridle and equip it onto the Horse.<ref>{{Cite|I've got a surprise for you. My grandpa handed this down to me, and now I'm handing it down to you. I present to you...the royal family's saddle and bridle! Custom fitted for the white royal horse!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref>

The Bridle can be equipped to any other Horse, except for [[Epona]] and the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}, after Link maxes out the Bond with his Horse and speaking to [[Canni]] at the [[Outskirt Stable]], {{Term|BotW|Phanna|link}} at the [[Highland Stable]], [[Breen]] at the [[Woodland Stable]], and {{Term|BotW|Tenne|link}} at the [[South Akkala Stable]]. It's well-known for its craftsmanship and how much the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} favored it.<ref name="Inventory"/> These Bridles, like others, can't be used by {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}.

The Royal Bridle can be seen equipped to Zelda's White Horse during the special cutscene that plays after Link completes the "[[Captured Memories]]" and "[[Destroy Ganon]]" [[Main Quest]]s.
