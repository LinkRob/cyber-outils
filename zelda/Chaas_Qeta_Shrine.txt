The {{Term/Store|Chaas Qeta Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Chaas Qeta Shrine}}<br/>A Major Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Major Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Chaas Qeta Shrine is located on {{Term|BotW|Tenoko Island|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea}}. As Link enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the Shrine's {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Chaas Qeta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Chaas Qeta. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Chaas Qeta|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Chaas Qeta Shrine features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. As Link approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}} wielding an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe++|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword++|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear++|link}} will rise from a pit to challenge Link.

In order to reach Chaas Qeta, Link must defeat the Guardian Scout IV to cause a portcullis separating him from the Monk to rise. Once Link has done so, Chaas Qeta will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Chaas Qeta|BotW}}</ref> and afterwards will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Chaas Qeta|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the portcullis contains the {{Term|BotW|Climbing Gear|link}}.
