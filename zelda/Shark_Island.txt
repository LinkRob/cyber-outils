{{Term/Store|Shark Island||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (F3) is one of the islands of the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Fishman|Tell me, small fry, have you ever been caught in one of those cyclones? The wind deity, [[Cyclos]], uses those cyclones to fly across the sea instantaneously, or so I've heard. Could be just a rumor.  Boy, if you had that [[Ballad of the Gales|power]], you wouldn't have to spend so much time sailing back and forth across the sea all the time. Wouldn't that be nice, fry?}}

The island looks like a shark (hence, the name) and it contains a secret cave surrounded by fire.<ref name="TWW Guide 135">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 135}}</ref> To extinguish this fire, [[Link]] must press four switches in any order. The first switch to be activated is a floor switch at the "head" of the shark. The second one is located on the "bottom flipper," but it must be hit with the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer|link}}. Next, on the "top flipper," there is a crystal switch that can be activated by just slashing it with the sword. The final switch is at the "tail" and needs to be pressed down with the [[Iron Boots]].<ref name="TWW Guide 135"/> Once all these switches have been pressed, the fire will go away. In the cave, there are hordes of [[Bokoblin]]s and [[Miniblin]]s, as well as [[Moblin]]s, [[Darknut]]s, and two [[Wizzrobe]]s. A Treasure Chest containing 200 [[Rupee]]s will appear once all these enemies are defeated.<ref name="TWW Guide 135"/>

As in [[Triangle Islands#Northern Triangle Island|Northern Triangle Island]] and the [[Mother & Child Isles]], [[Cyclos]] can be found near the island, thus it has been known to have raging cyclones. It is also one of the 22 islands in the game that has a [[Chuchu#Blue Chuchu|Blue ChuChu]] on it; however, this island's is placed in a very high spot, so Link will need the power of a nearby mini-cyclone (which appears only after the island's four switches were pressed) to reach there.

This island is the location the [[Korok]] [[Korok#Irch|Irch]] chose to plant one of the [[Great Deku Tree|Great Deku Tree's]] seeds.
