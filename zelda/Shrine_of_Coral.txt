{{Term/Store|Shrine of Coral|Shrines of Coral|Series, CoH|plural}} are objects in {{CoH}}. They were introduced as part of the {{OO}} [[Downloadable Content]] update.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Coral}} appear in {{Plural|CoH|Dungeon|link}} in both {{Term|CoH|Dungeon Mode|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Story Mode — Octavo|link}}. While active, certain unique terrain features of dungeons such as lava and pits are replaced with deep water, and falling into deep water beyond the ability to swim causes the character to take damage. The effects of shrines last until the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} is left, the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} {{Term|CoH|Boss|link}} is defeated, or the affected character falls in battle.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, look! A {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! {{Color|CoH Blue|Shrines}} are useful, but they often have a tricky {{Color|CoH Blue|side effect}} that you may not like. Bump into it if you want to take a chance and activate the {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! Shrine effects stay active until you leave the dungeon, defeat the dungeon boss, or are defeated.|Trill|CoH}}</ref>

When destroyed, the shrine drops {{Term|CoH|Iron Boots|link}}.

==Trivia==

* The appearance of the shrine may be a reference to [https://crypt-of-the-necrodancer.fandom.com/wiki/Coral_Riff Coral Riff], a boss from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
