{{Term/Store|Sapphia's Table||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sapphia's Table|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Sapphia's Table is a mesa in the southeastern corner of the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. The table is an elevated, snowy area containing trees, an enemy camp, and multiple [[Treasure Chest]]s buried in the snow.

[[Link]] can find multiple [[Korok]] puzzles along the cliffs, including one where he must fill in a circle of rocks that is missing one. Once Link drops a [[rock]] in the gap of the circle, a Korok will appear and give him a [[Korok Seed]].
