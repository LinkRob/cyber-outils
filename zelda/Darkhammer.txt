{{Term|TPHD|Darkhammer}} will continuously guard his body with his massive {{Term|TPHD|Ball and Chain|link}}. In order to defeat him, {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can latch onto a target behind {{Term|TPHD|Darkhammer}} with the {{Term|TPHD|Clawshot|link}} as soon as {{Term|TPHD|Darkhammer}} starts swinging his weapon. {{Term|TPHD|Darkhammer}} will then pause to retrieve his weapon, giving {{Term|TPHD|Link}} the opportunity to attack his exposed tail. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can also roll behind {{Term|TPHD|Darkhammer}} while he is swinging the {{Term|TPHD|Ball and Chain}} or after his attack lands. {{Term|TPHD|Darkhammer}} can be defeated in two hits with {{Plural|TPHD|Bomb Arrow|link}}, or a single hit with the {{Term|TPHD|Mortal Draw|link}}.

When defeated, {{Term|TPHD|Darkhammer}} drops his {{Term|TPHD|Ball and Chain}}, which can picked up by {{Term|TPHD|Link}}.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|TPHD|Darkhammer}}'s armor resembles the suits of armor that line the halls and rooms of the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins|link}}.
*The room in which he is fought had an alternative setup as a long hallway with two {{Term|TPHD|Clawshot|link}} targets, a {{Term|TPHD|Switch|link}}, and five suits of armor. This can be found in the game's files with the ID of Room 49 for {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins}}. The final game uses the ID of Room 51.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= GlitterBerri |published= March 14, 2016 |retrieved= June 7, 2019 |url= https://tcrf.net/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Twilight_Princess/Unused_Rooms#D_MN11B_-_Snowpeak_Ruins_Miniboss |title= The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess/Unused Rooms - The Cutting Room Floor |site= The Cutting Room Floor |type= }}</ref>
