{{Term/Store|Goponga Flower|Goponga Flowers|Series, LA, LADX, LANS, OoS, OoA|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|Enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 182 {{Exp Game|LA, OoS, OoA}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, they block the entrance to [[Bottle Grotto]] in [[Goponga Swamp]]. They are [[BowWow]]'s favorite food,<ref>{{Cite|'BRRING! BRRING! Yes, this is Ulrira. The Indigestible Flowers of Goponga Swamp... Those flowers are BowWow's favorite. Why don't you take him for a walk there? CLICK!'|Grandpa Ulrira|LADX}}</ref> and letting him eat them gets rid of them. They can later be burned using the [[Magical Rod]].

[[Giant Goponga Flower]]s are larger variations of these plants which.

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, Goponga Flowers inhabit [[Spool Swamp]] and can generate and fire flame-like spherical projectiles from their core when prey or a threat has entered their domain. They can be defeated by using [[Ember Seed|Ember Seeds]],  the [[Magical Boomerang|Magic Boomerang]] or the [[Master Sword]]. The can also be eaten by [[Dimitri]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, Goponga Flowers inhabit [[Spool Swamp]] and can generate and fire flame-like spherical projectiles from their core when prey or a threat has entered their domain. They can be defeated by using Ember Seeds, the [[Switch Hook|Switch]]/[[Long Hook]], or the Master Sword. The can also be eaten by Dimitri.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, Goponga Flowers block Link's path when he first enters Goponga Swamp. After returning with BowWow, the [[Chain Chomp]] proceeds to eat the Flowers.
{{Noncanon|End}}
