'''"Midna's Theme"''' is a [[Song]] in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
"Midna's Theme" often plays during cutscenes involving {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}}. Parts of "{{Term|TPHD|Midna's Theme}}" can also be heard in other songs such as {{Term|TPHD|Title Theme|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Boss Defeated|link}}, and {{Term|TPHD|Midna's Lament|link}}.{{Fact}} It is the 13th track in Disc 1 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Original Soundtrack]]''.
