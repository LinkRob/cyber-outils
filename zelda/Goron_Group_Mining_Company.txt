The {{Term/Store|Goron Group Mining Company||Series, BotW}} is a group in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Bludo"/>

==Biography==
The {{Term|BotW|Goron Group Mining Company}} was founded by [[Bludo]] after noticing that gemstones found around [[Death Mountain]] could be traded with other races for a high price. It is the largest enterprise among the Gorons and drives the economy of [[Goron City]]. Many of the Gorons in the City are employees of the {{Term|BotW|Goron Group Mining Company|display=Company}}.<ref name="CaC"/> [[Dorill]] is the head of the {{Term|BotW|Goron Group Mining Company}},<ref name="Dorill"/> working under direct orders from the boss, Bludo.<ref>{{Cite|We're diggin' this site out under direct orders from the Boss of Goron City. No time to chat! Gotta dig!|Dorill|BotW}}</ref>
