==Location and Uses==
Toasty Hylian Shrooms are the result of a [[Hylian Shroom]] that's been burnt by fire. They can purchased from the [[Protein Palace]] in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}. It has an amplified taste and fragrance compared to regular Hylian Shrooms, restoring three-quarters of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"></ref>
