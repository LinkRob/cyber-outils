{{Plural|BotW|Knight's Broadsword}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use to defeat {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, destroy {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Jar|link}}, and cut down {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Knight's Broadsword}} will deal 26 damage to {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}}, and 52 damage when thrown and broken on an {{Term|BotW|Enemy}}. They were once used by the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}} before the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> In the present, they are commonly used by adventurers due to their ease of use and high attack power.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Scanning the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Broadsword}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|SSB|Young Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|SSB|Toon Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ}} Series {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} ({{TLoZ}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|LA|Link|link}} ({{LA}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Young Link}} ({{MM}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Toon Link}} ({{TWW}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|TP|Link|link}} ({{TP}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|SS|Link|link}} ({{SS}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Link}} (Rider) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

{{Plural|BotW|Knight's Broadsword}} will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Being magnetic, however, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use his {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} on them. However, this also means that they will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Knight's Broadsword}} belong to the equipment archetype known as the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Gear|link}}, which was primarily used by the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>
