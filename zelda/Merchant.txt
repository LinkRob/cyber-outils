{{Term/Store|Merchant|Merchants|Series, TLoZ, BotW, FPTRR|plural}} are recurring characters in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 219 {{Exp Game|TLoZ}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 99}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Merchant|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
[[File:TLoZ Shop.png|thumb|left|x120px|A Merchant from {{TLoZ|-}}]]
Merchants can be found either in [[cave]]s or underground. Their shops are located in 12 areas in the First Quest and in 15 areas in the [[Second Quest]].  The items they sell to [[Link]] and their prices vary from a merchant to another.<ref>{{Cite|BOY, THIS IS REALLY EXPENSIVE!|Merchant|TLoZ}}</ref> There are four types of merchants: the ones who sell [[Magical Shield]]s, [[Key]]s, and [[Candle|Blue Candles]]; Merchants who sell Magical Shields, [[Bomb]]s and, [[Arrow]]s; the ones who sell Magical Shields, [[Food]], and [[Heart]]s; and only one Merchant in each quest who sells Keys, Food, and the [[Blue Ring]].

==={{aLttP|-}}===
A [[Street Merchant]] can be found in Kakariko Town selling a Magic bottle for 100 rupees.

==={{tWW|-}}===
Three [[Wandering Merchant|Wandering Merchants]] can be found throughout the [[Great Sea|Great sea]].

==={{tMC|-}}===
[[Goron Merchant]] makes a appearance.

==={{aLBW|-}}===
A Street Merchant with a striking resemblance to the one from Link to the past can be found in Kakariko Village.

==={{TFH|-}}===
Making his way from hyrule, the street merchant from A Link between World’s can be found once again on Hytopia.

==={{BotW|-}}===
Merchants can be found travelling to and from different [[Stable]]s, as well as on the paths between Stables and towns. When it's raining, most merchants will change out their stock, selling better items than they do during regular weather.

==={{TWoG|-}}===
[[File:TWoG Merchant Cutscene Sprite.png|right|200px]]
In {{TWoG}}, the Merchant is the owner of the General Store in [[Sakado]].<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=The merchant sells a pack of three bombs for 20 rubies.|game=TWoG|page=3}}</ref> He sells [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] [[Rope (Object)|Rope]], [[Lantern Oil]], and [[Bomb]]s. His mannerisms suggest that he is quite sinister and greedy, and is more interested in making a profit.<ref>{{Cite|'Course I'm on your side. But I still have to sell the stuff. Just pick what you want, I'll handle the Rubies.|Merchant|TWoG}}</ref> If Zelda does not have enough [[Rupee#The Wand of Gamelon|Rubies]] to afford something, he suggests that she fight a few [[Goriya]]s.

==Trivia==  
*Depending on which defensive ring Link wears, the clothing of the Merchants changes to match the color of Link's [[Tunic]].
* In the Merchant's cutscene in {{TWoG}}, a Bomb is marked with a price tag of 20.00, erroneously suggesting that an unstated decimal currency is used as part of [[Gamelon]]'s currency. The Bombs are actually priced at 20 Rubies for three.
