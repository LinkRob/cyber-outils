{{Term/Store|Concave Slot|Concave Slots|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 31}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Improve}}
Usually found within {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot}} are large, glowing, bowl-shaped divots in the floor with a small or large slot at the center, serving as a receptacle for an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}, one of which is usually somewhere nearby. They are covered in {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} iconography, and act as {{Plural|BotW|Switch|link}} to activate various mechanisms, such as doors in {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrines}}. Because of the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}'s bowl shape, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} can easily roll down into it when tossed from a distance.

{{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot}} are sometimes found in the {{Term|BotW|Overworld|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot|display=Slots}} are embedded into {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal|link}}, and by inserting an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} into them, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground nearby.
