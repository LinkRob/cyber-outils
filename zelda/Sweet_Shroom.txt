{{Term/Store|Sweet Shroom|Sweet Shrooms|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 140"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Sweet Shroom}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Deku Forest|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Sweet Shroom}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Buzz Blob Cave|link}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Sweet Shroom}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Buzz Blob Cave}}''', ''"Clear using only Bombs!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Sweet Shroom}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Moblin Base|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Sweet Shroom}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Moblin Base}}''', ''"Defeat all foes sans sword!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Sweet Shroom}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}''', ''"Adventure in the dark!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Sweet Shroom}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Sweet Shroom}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Legendary Dress|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Loungewear|link}}.<ref name="E 140"/>
