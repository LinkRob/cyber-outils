The '''Bombs of Might''' is an item from {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
{{Manual|EoW|Bombs of Might|Mysterious bombs able to blast through the monsters of the rifts.<ref name="Bombs of Might">{{Cite|Mysterious bombs able to blast through the monsters of the rifts.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Bombs of Might can be upgraded to [[Bombs of Might Lv. 2|Lv. 2]] by using 10 [[Might Crystal|Might Crystals]] at [[Lueburry|Lueburry’s]] House, significantly increasing its damage. It can be further upgraded to [[Bombs of Might Lv. 3|LV. 3]] with an additional 20 Might Crystals, enhancing its damage even more.
