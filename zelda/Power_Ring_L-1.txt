==Location and Uses==
The Power Ring L-1 can be found in the [[Explorer's Crypt]] in {{OoS}} and the [[Spirit's Grave]] in {{OoA}}. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it increases [[Sword]] damage while also increasing damage taken by [[Link]].<ref name="Description"/> The Power Ring L-1 can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
