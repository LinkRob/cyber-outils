==Overview==
Link enters the {{Term|TotK|Deku Tree|link}}'s mouth and finds Oaki, next to a large bed. He says he plan on decorating the secret room but needs a few materials to put the finishing touches. He asks for some {{Plural|TotK|blue nightshade|link}} and some {{Plural|TotK|Brightbloom Seed|link}}, but {{Term|TotK|Giant Brightbloom Seed|link|display=bigger}} than the one in the General Shoppe.

Link gathered all the required plants for Oaki. He then planted them in the room, glowing bright in the evening's glow. Oaki gave Link a {{Term|TotK|Korok Fabric|link}} as a reward for helping him style his secret room.
