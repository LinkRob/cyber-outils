"{{Term/Store|EX Vicious Monster Battle|EX Vicious Monster Battles|Series|plural}}" are a type of {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
{{Improve}}
EX Vicious Monster Battles are temporary missions that appear around the map, as the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} gain access to more locations. Each one will have one to three vicious monsters in it. Vicious Monster Battles appear in {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Field|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Breach of Demise|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Outpost|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Crenel Peak|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Kakariko Village|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Goponga Village|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Lanayru Road|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Mount Daphnes|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Forgotten Temple|link}}.

Possible Vicious Monsters include a {{Term|HWAoC|Chuchu|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Chuchu|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Meteo Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Blizzrobe|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Thunder Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Stone Talus|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Black Hinox|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian|link}}, and pairs of {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} (which may include {{Plural|HWAoC|Bomb Barrel Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Octo Balloon Moblin|link}}).

Interestingly, while Vicious Monster {{Plural|HWAoC|Challenge}} initially have varying levels based on when they were unlocked, when the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} beat the {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}} in {{Term|HWAoC|EX Alert: Forgotten Temple|link}}, all Vicious Monster Battles gain a level recommendation of 78.
