==Description==
{{Term|TFH|Hytopia}} is a small city settlement which is situated directly in front of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}. It serves as the main hub world when the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} are not in the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}. It is aligned with several town houses, as well as numerous trees and a couple shops: most notably {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}, where people go to get {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}} tailored by {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture|link|display=Couture}}, as well as the {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}} shop and the {{Term|TFH|Miiverse Gallery|link}}. Once {{Term|TFH|Link}} has cleared several {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} in the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}}, the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} will also arrive and set up a small stall, where he will sell and buy various {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Didja know? Didja?! Ma says a Street Merchant is comin' to town! Mimi's gonna be the first in line, teehee!|Mimi|TFH}}</ref> Between the castle and the town's main square is a small waterway with a bridge. {{Term|TFH|Link}} can dive into the water by climbing down a nearby fleet of stairs. Green {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} can be found hidden beneath the bridge.

In the center of town is a stone statue fountain, known as the {{Term|TFH|Tri Force Hero Statue|link}}.<ref name="Thanks">{{Cite|However!! I've been watching you. So I know you haven't given thanks to the Tri Force Hero statue yet. Not once! The heroes are Hytopia's guardians! They will surely keep record of all your doings, be they good or bad.|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> This statue, which depicts three heroes forming a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} and pointing a {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} skyward, is a monument to the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero|link}}, the heroes of legend in the kingdom's prophecy. It is considered customary to give thanks to this statue.<ref name="Thanks"/><ref>{{Cite|Now then, boy! Go press {{Icon|TFH A}} by the statue and give thanks. Raise your hands to the sky and go with the flow!|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> Doing so allows {{Term|TFH|Link}} to [[Saving|save]] his game's progress anytime.

As a small settlement, {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}}'s town is home to only a handful of civilians. As the center place of high fashion, its residents regularly enjoy wearing stylish and unique clothes.<ref name="Fashion"/> After {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}} is cursed to wear an irremovable, ugly outfit by {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link}}, a resulting panic sweeps through the town as its residents turn to wearing ordinary and unusual {{Plural|TFH|Outfit}} out of fear of being cursed as well.<ref>{{Cite|The worst part is that, ever since Princess Styla became cursed, Hytopia and its people have fallen into despair. Everyone is afraid of fashion now, lest they become cursed as well. Hytopia has never looked so drab...|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|They say the witch is going after fashionable people. Obviously, I have much to fear. That's why I've cut my stylishness in half. It's the most I can do.|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> As a result of the panic, {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} begins a search for qualified heroes to hunt down the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}} witch. A sign near {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's}} seeks out wanted heroes, and promises a great reward to anyone who manages to defeat the witch.<ref>{{Cite|WANTED: TRI FORCE HEROES!! Do you have pointed ears, truly epic sideburns, and side-parted hair? If so, YOU may be a Tri Force Hero! Our kingdom needs such heroes to defeat the evil witch and lift the curse placed on Princess Styla. Whoever succeeds in this most noble of tasks will be rewarded to the fullest extent of my extensive power. So sayeth King Tuft, ruler of Hytopia!|Sign|TFH}}</ref>

{{Term|TFH|Link}} can leave the town by taking the southern gate, where a [[Soldier|Guard]] keeps watch to ensure that no suspicious people leave. {{Term|TFH|Link}} can speak with him whenever he wishes to leave town; doing so will save the game's progress and return to the title screen.

=== Merger with Hyrule ===
Sometime following the lifting of {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla}}'s curse, {{Term|Series|Hytopia}} ceased its autonomy, and underwent a merger with {{Term|Series|Hyrule}}, becoming the kingdom's northern region. Although {{Term|Series|Hytopia}} as its own settlement was no more, [[Hyrule/Appearances by Game#The Adventure of Link|the incarnation of Hyrule]] that existed during The Era of Decline's [[Zelda Timeline#The Hero.27s Quest to Reunite the Triforce|Quest to Reunite the Triforce]] was influenced by its legacy. {{Term|Series|Hytopia}}'s powerful magic users led to many people who could use magic to exist during this generation, and the residents of settlements to the northernmost region of {{Term|Series|Hyrule}} were particularly fashionable.<ref name="E 61" />

==Trivia==
*In the north-east corner of town, there is a broken, forgotten {{Term|TFH|Doppel|link}} that resembles {{Term|TFH|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's a broken, forgotten doll. Hmm... It kinda looks like YOU! You gently wipe away the dust.|N/A|TFH}}</ref>
**Following the 2.1.0 update, the {{Term|TFH|Doppel}} begins to appear tidier and more dignified as {{Term|TFH|Link}} collects {{Plural|TFH|Hero Point|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The doll was once old and broken. Now it has a dignified air about it. There's not a speck of dust on it.|N/A|TFH}}</ref> Eventually it becomes a symbol of heroism, attracting visitors from afar.<ref>{{Cite|The doll was once old and broken. Now it's seen as a symbol of heroism. Heroes come from far away to see it.|N/A|TFH}}</ref>
*A coded message can be found on the southern wall of the east side of the waterway in town. It can only be read and decoded while wearing the {{Term|TFH|Tri Suit|link}} or {{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform|link}}, and reads "[[Iconic Quotes from The Legend of Zelda#"It's a secret to everybody"|IT'S A SECRET TO EVERYBODY]]".<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= GameXplain|published= October 23, 2015|retrieved= |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y2ecergnLWI|title= Mysterious Message CRACKED in Zelda: Tri Force Heroes!|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref>
*Throughout the game, {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}} can occasionally be found hiding around town. The townspeople mistake her for a "black ghost", and hope to take a picture of her.
*Many pink and yellow birds are seen around {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}}, but very rarely, a blue one can be found. The little girl, {{Term|TFH|Mimi|link}}, hopes to take a picture of this bird, believing it to bring good luck.
*The tree to the southeast of town, when examined, is described to have many cicada shells on it. No other trees share this trait, and the {{Term|TFH|Bearded Baron|link}} claims spotting this tree is an omen of good things to come.<ref>{{Cite|I found a tree covered in cicada shells! Most certainly an omen that something good will happen today.|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref>
**Following the 2.1.0 update, the number of cicada shells that appear reflects the number of {{Plural|TFH|Hero Point}} {{Term|TFH|Link}} has.

==Nomenclature==
Hytopia may be a portmanteau of "Hy" from Hyrule, in reference to its connection with it, and "utopia", an ideal or perfect place.
