{{Term/Store|Zauz||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 282{{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Zauz is the blacksmith who helps [[Link]] by forging the blade of the [[Phantom Sword]] used to defeat [[Bellum]]. The {{Term|PH|Sword Blade|link}} is made of three [[Pure Metal]]s,<ref>{{Cite|The Phantom Sword is no ordinary sword... When forged, normal metal can never handle that kind of sacred power! Aquanine... Azurine... And Crimsonine... These three pure metals must be forged together to create the Phantom Sword.|Zauz|PH}}</ref> which Link must collect by befriending the island natives and delving through their respective temples. Zauz tells Link to return in time, as the forging of the {{Term|PH|Sword Blade}} is a lengthy process.<ref>{{Cite|The Crimsonine, the Azurine, and the Aquanine. I've never seen metal like this. I'll bet I can forge a mighty sword with them. But it will take some time.|Zauz|PH}}</ref> After Link defeats [[Jolene]] one last time, Zauz will have finished the {{Term|PH|Sword Blade}} upon returning to [[Zauz's Island|his island]].

Zauz provides Link a secret that proves to help him navigate further into the [[Temple of the Ocean King]]. By drawing an [[Triforce|ancient symbol]] on a particular red door on one of the temple's many levels, he will be able to access an entirely different temple floor,<ref>{{Cite|There was a door that you could open by drawing a symbol, was there not? If you draw this symbol on that door, another way should open to you!|Zauz|PH}}</ref> and by extension, the remaining two [[Sea Chart]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Go to the Temple of the Ocean King and find the last two sea charts! Draw a new shape on the door, and a new path should open for you.|Zauz|PH}}</ref> 

When Zauz first speaks to [[Ciela]], he indicates that he is not human; later dialog suggests he is of the [[Cobble]] race, which defended the [[Oshus|Ocean King]].<ref>{{Cite|You see, for generations, our ancestors lived to serve the Ocean King. They defended the Ocean King in an age when great evil lurked in the sea. My people forged our own weapons and fought in epic battles. You seek one of those great weapons. An artifact called the Phantom Sword.|Zauz|PH}}</ref> He shares dark skin and a large, oddly-colored nose with [[King Mutoh]] and his Knights.
