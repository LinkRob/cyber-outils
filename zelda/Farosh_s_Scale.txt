{{Term/Store|Farosh's Scale|Farosh's Scales|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Farosh's Scale}}<br/>This precious scale fell from the golden spirit Farosh. ==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Farosh's Scale}} can be collected from the {{Term|BotW|Dragon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Farosh|link}},<ref name="Inventory"/> who typically flies around {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Lake Floria|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must shoot anywhere along the main body of {{Term|BotW|Farosh}} to receive a {{Term|BotW|Farosh's Scale|display=Scale}}, except the horns, teeth, or claws. It will subsequently fall somewhere on the land in the form of a light.

{{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Farosh's Scale}} will always result in {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}. However, when critters are used in the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, the result will be an {{Term|BotW|Elixir|link}}. At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Farosh's Scale}} can be used as an ingredient to create {{Term|BotW|Light Yellow Dye|link}}.

During "{{Term|BotW|The Serpent's Jaws|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will come upon the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage|link}}. When he prays to the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} there, {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}} will instruct him to offer one of {{Plural|BotW|Farosh's Scale}} to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage|display=Spring}}.<ref>{{Cite|Offer {{Color|BotW Red|Farosh's scale}}, received from the golden spirit, to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Courage}}. I, Goddess Hylia, will guide you.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> Doing this will open the way to {{Term|BotW|Shae Katha Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Just as the Goddess instructed, you threw {{Color|BotW Red|Farosh's scale}} into the Spring of Courage. A shrine appeared as a result!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
