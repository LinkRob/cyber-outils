The {{Term/Store|Automatic Crossbow||Series, LCT}} is an ability used by the {{Term|LCT|Crossbow|link}} in {{LCT}}.<ref name="Manual 7"/>

==Overview==
{{Manual|LCT|Automatic Crossbow|If you defeat an enemy that's flashing green, your weapon will turn into a rapid-fire crossbow, able to fire many shots in rapid succession. Once you run out of automatic arrows, your crossbow will revert to normal.}}
The {{Term|LCT|Automatic Crossbow}} is an ability that {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} gains temporarily after killing a green, flashing {{Term|LCT|Enemy|link}}.<ref name="Manual 7"/> The {{Term|LCT|Automatic Crossbow}} gives {{Term|LCT|Link}} 100 {{Plural|LCT|Arrow}} to use before it reverts back to the normal {{Term|LCT|Crossbow}}; the {{Term|LCT|Automatic Crossbow}} has no time limit.<ref name="Manual 7"/> While using the {{Term|LCT|Automatic Crossbow}}, the player only needs to hold down the {{Term|LCT|B Button|link}} in order to rapidly shoot {{Plural|LCT|Arrow}} instead of having to press it for each {{Term|LCT|Arrow}}.
