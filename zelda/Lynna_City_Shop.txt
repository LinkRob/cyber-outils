The {{Term/Store|Lynna City Shop||Series}} is a location in {{OoA}}.{{Name Ref|OoA}} Found in present-age [[Lynna City]], it is run by [[Stockwell]]. The shop consists of two floors, the second of which can only be reached by travelling between the past and present ages, using the [[Harp of Ages]].

==Features and Overview==
===First Floor===
The first floor, which is the main floor of the Lynna City Shop, is available at the very beginning of the game. However, the shopkeeper will only start selling [[Bomb]]s after the player has obtained them from [[Queen Ambi]]. In a [[Linked Game]], the Shop will also sell a [[Gasha Seed]], and the [[Shield]] will only appear after buying it. In addition, the [[Strange Flute]] can be purchased here after completing [[Moonlit Grotto]]. Buying it here will lead to the acquisition of [[Dimitri's Flute]] later on, henceforth making [[Dimitri]] Link's partner. The flute disappears from the shop if Link leaves for [[Symmetry City]] without it or acquires it at the [[Shooting Gallery]] instead.

The Shop always sells the most recent [[Shield]] that Link has obtained. As usual, Link cannot buy a Shield if he already has one.<ref>{{Cite|You have [[File:OoA Wooden Shield Sprite.png|link=]]. One more won't help.|Stockwell|OoA}}</ref>

===Second Floor===
The second floor is a hidden section of the Shop that can only be accessed by using the [[Tune of Currents]] or the [[Tune of Ages]] to teleport from the past to the present into the hidden area. This section of the store originally sells the L-2 [[Ring Box]], a [[Gasha Seed]], and a [[Piece of Heart]]. After all these items are bought, the store will restock with a second, more expensive Gasha Seed and a [[Magic Ring]]. After all these items are purchased, [[Stockwell]] will renovate the second floor to host the [[Treasure Chest Game]].<ref>{{Cite|We ran out of goods, so we're in a new line of business now.|Stockwell|OoA}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Stockwell, the proprietor of the shop, also runs the [[Advance Shop]] in [[Lynna Village]] in the past. This would seem to be impossible, as there are several centuries between the Past and the Present.
*If Link attempts to exit the shop with an item that he hasn't paid for, Stockwell will rush towards Link and push him away from the door to prevent him from leaving.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Don't just take things! Thief! Give that back right now!|Stockwell|OoA}}</ref>
