The {{Term/Store|Master Sword Lv3||Series, ALttP, ALBW}} (originally named '''Golden Sword''') is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 131 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Master Sword Lv3}} was referred to as the '''Golden Sword''' in {{Guide|ALttP|Nintendo}} and on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP |publisher=  Nintendo|page= 142}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Golden Sword}}<br/>The Golden Sword is a magical blade created by a benevolent fairy in the Mysterious Pond. When a normal sword is thrown into the water, the fairy uses her ancient magic to create this powerful weapon with a gold-toned blade.|retrieved= January 22, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160308195651/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/g.jsp#GoldenSword|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It is the ultimate [[Upgrading|upgrade]] to the {{Term|Series|Master Sword|link}} and thus the strongest [[Sword]] in the games it appears in.<ref>{{Cite|The Master Sword, made ultimate! Now it's the fiercest blade imaginable!|Gear|ALBW}}</ref> The {{Term|Series|Master Sword Lv3}} goes unnamed in {{AST}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', this weapon was called the Golden Sword. In order to obtain this upgrade, {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} must throw the [[Master Sword Lv2|Tempered Sword]] into the {{Term|ALttP|Mysterious Pond|link}} of the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} within the {{Term|ALttP|Pyramid of Power|link}}. As a result, the {{Term|ALttP|Cursed Fairy|link}} will transform it into the Golden Sword. This version of the Master Sword has a golden blade, green hilt, and is an extremely powerful sword, as it twice as strong as the Tempered Sword (making the weapon four times as powerful as the regular Master Sword, and therefore eight times the {{Term|ALttP|Fighter's Sword|link}}'s strength), making it able to destroy most common enemies in only one or two slashes, and has an [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/LTTP/LTTP_Sword4.wav even deeper swing sound]. However, its great power is still outclassed by the [[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]], which can kill ''any'' enemy (including some [[Bosses in A Link to the Past|bosses]]) with a single hit. In the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|Game Boy Advance remake of the game]], it also has the ability to break {{Plural|ALttP|Jar|link}} and {{Plural|ALttP|Skull|link}}.

Like the Tempered Sword, the Golden Sword is able to harm [[Ganon]] without requiring the use of the [[Spin Attack]] unlike the normal Master Sword, making the final battle significantly easier and it deals more damage in comparison.

In ''A Link to the Past & Four Swords'', during the final challenge in the [[Palace of the Four Sword]], if Link has obtained the Golden Sword, the four [[Dark Link|Dark Links]] will also wield this weapon as well.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
Renamed the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword Lv3}}, much like in ''A Link to the Past'', this weapon is the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword}} at its maximum strength in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Master Sword is now at its maximum strength! This is a true hero's weapon!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> It is obtained similarly to the Master Sword Lv2, requiring two pieces of {{Term|ALBW|Master Ore|link}}, but they must be taken to the {{Term|ALBW|Blacksmith|link}} at the {{Term|ALBW|Blacksmith's Forge|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}.

[[Gramps]] also wields this sword during the optional [[StreetPass]] duel against Link even if the latter already obtained the blade.

== Trivia ==

* Although this highly powerful upgrade of the [[Master Sword]] is commonly used by players in ''A Link to the Past'' and ''A Link Between Worlds'' as it greatly reduces the difficulty of the [[Final Boss]] battle against [[Ganon]] or [[Yuga Ganon]] (or to accomplish [[100% Completion|100% completion]]), respectively, like the [[Master Sword Lv2|Tempered Sword]], it is not required to complete the games, as the main antagonists of either title can be defeated with the Blade of Evil's Bane only at its standard power.
* Despite only existing in two games of the core series like the previous upgrade and some titles instead have the Master Sword being weakened such as ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'' or ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', the Master Sword Lv3 is arguably the most powerful [[Sword]] in the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] alongside the [[Fierce Deity Sword]], as it inflicts four times the damage of the traditional version of the Blade of Evil's Bane.
* Unlike ''A Link to the Past'', as with the Master Sword Lv2, the Master Sword Lv3 does not make a very deep swinging sound when [[Link]] attacks in ''A Link Between Worlds''.
* In spite being derived from the Master Sword, the Golden Sword's official artwork has a radically different design to it, namely the ricasso being less apparent in the blade, the [[Triforce]] mark being closer to the hilt and is inverted, and the hilt itself, aside from being colored green instead of blue, is sleeker and straight-edged rather than thick and curved, as well as lacking the central jewel.
* The Master Sword Lv3 in ''A Link Between Worlds'', unlike its appearance in the game's indirect prequel, looks identical to the usual Master Sword aside from having a golden blade, similar to the Master Sword Lv2.
* Much like its Lv2 counterpart, the Master Sword Lv3, due to the distinctive yellow color of its sacred blade, is often compared to the famous [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber Lightsaber] weapon from the ''[https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars Star Wars]'' franchise by the ''Zelda'' community, specifically the [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_pike ones used by Jedi Temple Guards] as well as [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rey_Skywalker Rey Skywalker]'s [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rey%27s_lightsaber own lightsaber].
