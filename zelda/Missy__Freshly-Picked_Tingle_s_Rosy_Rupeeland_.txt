'''Missy''' is a character that appeared in {{FPTRR}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|FPTRR|Missy}} is a very intelligent girl who appears early in the game. {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} must bring her {{Term|FPTRR|Ground Shell|link}} and {{Term|FPTRR|Minced Meat|link}}. After {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} succeeds in doing this, {{Term|FPTRR|Missy}} will give him the recipe for {{Term|FPTRR|Tingly|link}}, a medicine which he can then bring to the Guard to help his sick wife. The medicine can also be made to give to {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}'s {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard|link}}.

Later in the game, {{Term|FPTRR|Missy}} is lost in {{Term|FPTRR|Lon Lon Meadow|link}}. {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} has to find her glasses in order to save her. {{Term|FPTRR|Missy}}'s father, {{Term|FPTRR|Jeweller|link}}, will then reward {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} for {{Term|FPTRR|Missy}}'s rescue. For the rest of the game, she will be in the most eastern part of {{Term|FPTRR|Port Town|link}}. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} speaks with her after he has reached the {{Term|FPTRR|Second Continent|link}}, she will tell him about a medic in the {{Term|FPTRR|Gooey Swamp|link}} who has invented a new medicine, {{Term|FPTRR|Recipe|section=Tinglene|display=Tinglene|link}}. Once he has reached the {{Term|FPTRR|Third Continent}}, she will tell him about the Shaman of {{Term|FPTRR|Mount Desma|link}} who has invented another new medicine, {{Term|FPTRR|Mega-Tingly|link}}.

{{Term|FPTRR|Madame Scratchcard|link}}, a character that appears later in the game is probably her mother because of the fact that she stands next to the {{Term|FPTRR|Jeweller}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Missy}}'s father, and also lives in the same house.
