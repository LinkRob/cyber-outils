The {{Term/Store|Lakna Rokee Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Lakna Rokee Shrine}}<br/>Lakna Rokee's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Lakna Rokee's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Lakna Rokee Shrine}} is in a forest high up on a mountain east of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is hidden underground next to a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}.

To reveal the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must place the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom|link}}, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} kept in {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link}}, into the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}'s {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}. However, before "{{Term|BotW|The Stolen Heirloom|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} begins, {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} will not let {{Term|BotW|Link}} touch the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom|display=Heirloom}}.<ref>{{Cite|That {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah heirloom}} has been guarded by our tribe for generations. I must request that you not touch it... Even for an esteemed guest such as yourself, there are certain rules that must be respected.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

"{{Term|BotW|The Stolen Heirloom}}" can be initiated by completing all {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}. Once this has been done, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can enter {{Term|BotW|Impa's House}} to find {{Term|BotW|Paya|link}} crying. She reveals to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}} has been stolen.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah heirloom}}... {{Big|The {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} has been stolen!}}|Paya|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Impa}} then shares a legend about the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}}, revealing that {{Term|BotW|Paya}} believes it referred to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|"The hero, as chosen by the Sheikah heirloom, will be gifted the blessing of antiquity."}} Paya thinks "{{Color|BotW Red|The hero, as chosen by the Sheikah heirloom}} is you, so she stayed up all night researching the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}}|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Eventually, her research showed that the legend may have been a way to unearth an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} using the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}}.<ref>{{Cite|She can to the conclusion that "{{Color|BotW Red|the blessing of antiquity}}" refers to one of those {{Color|BotW Red|ancient shrines}}. And that the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} is the {{Color|BotW Red|key that opens the shrine}}.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Paya}} wonders whether it was someone in the {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|display=Village}} who stole the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom|display=Heirloom}}.<ref>{{Cite|And no one from the village would do such a thing! Would they?|Paya|BotW}}</ref>

Due to {{Term|BotW|Paya}}'s fears, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is asked to spend a day by her side.<ref>{{Cite|Link... Paya is afraid that the thief may still be nearby. Would you please stay by her side today?|Impa|BotW}}</ref> After doing so, {{Term|BotW|Paya}} calms down, saying that {{Term|BotW|Dorian|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Cado|link}} saw no strangers around.<ref>{{Cite|I see now that crying a feeling scared won't help us get the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} back. I checked with Dorian and Cado, but they said they didn't see anyone other than villagers around.|Paya|BotW}}</ref> She warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that whoever the thief is, they might still be nearby.<ref>{{Cite|I'll try again to remember all I can about the night the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} was stolen, but the thief may still be hiding nearby.|Paya|BotW}}</ref> If he sees anyone acting suspiciously at night, she tells him not to approach.<ref>{{Cite|I have a bad feeling. If you spot any suspicious movements at night, {{Color|BotW Red|don't approach}}...|Paya|BotW}}</ref>

At night, {{Term|BotW|Impa}}'s guard {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} can be seen abandoning his post at 10:00 PM. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can follow him out of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}} and into the nearby woods, all the way to the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}. {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} will shout at an unknown entity,<ref>{{Cite|I know you're there! Show yourself!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> causing a {{Term|BotW|Yiga Blademaster|link}} to appear on the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}.

{{Term|BotW|Dorian}} accuses the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}} of stealing the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}}.<ref>{{Cite|So you're the ones who stole the heirloom!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} inquires about the theft,<ref>{{Cite|Why would you steal it?|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Blademaster|display=Blademaster}} tells him not to play dumb.<ref>{{Cite|Don't play dumb, Dorian. You knew what you were getting yourself into when you decided to leave the organization. You forfeited your life the moment you left.|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref> He then reveals that {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} was the one who told them about the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom|display=Heirloom}}, but now he is useless to the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}} and must be ended.<ref>{{Cite|You did well to tell us about the heirloom. Unfortunately, your usefulness has come to an end, as must you. But first...|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref> At this point, the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Blademaster}} notices {{Term|BotW|Link}} watching and proceeds to attack him.<ref>{{Cite|It appears we have an audience!|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref>

Once the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Blademaster}} has been defeated, {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} admits that everything was his fault.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure you've figured it out, but all of this is my fault...|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He then reveals to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he was previously a member of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga }}.<ref>{{Cite|I am...a member of the Yiga Clan. Or I used to be, anyway.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He left when he met an incredible woman, the mother of his daughters.<ref>{{Cite|But then I met the most incredible woman who blessed me with two amazing children. And so I tried to change my ways. Tried to leave the clan.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> However, his wife was killed as punishment.<ref>{{Cite|But the Yiga... They took my wife's life as punishment.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> Fearful for the lives of {{Term|BotW|Koko|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Cottla|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} continued to gather information for the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=Yiga}}.<ref>{{Cite|I couldn't let them hurt my children, so I used my position as a gatekeeper to gather information for the Yiga. I told them about you and about the village in exchange for safety.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} again tried to separate from the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=Yiga}}, the lives of {{Term|BotW|Impa}} and {{Term|BotW|Paya}} were threatened.<ref>{{Cite|But my grief over losing my wife consumed me, and I finally decided to cut ties with the Yiga completely! I decided I could protect my children from the Yiga. I wasn't afraid of them. But the Yiga are pure evil. They told me they would kill both Impa and Paya if I didn't play along!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Dorian}} says that the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah|link}} tried to use the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}} to unlock the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, but they could not make it work.<ref>{{Cite|Paya figured out that this {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} is the {{Color|BotW Red|key to opening an ancient shrine}}. We weren't able to make it work, but Paya thinks that maybe you can.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He then asserts that he and the rest of the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah}} are devoted to helping {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Paya...and all of us are devoted to helping you in your quest.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to place the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}} in the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now, please restore this {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} {{Color|BotW Red|to its pedestal}}.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

After placing the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}} into the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}, the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} will activate, and {{Term|BotW|Lakna Rokee Shrine}} will rise from beneath the ground. {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} apologizes to {{Term|BotW|Link}} for everything, asking for what really happened to be kept a secret.<ref>{{Cite|Master Link, I... I'm very sorry for all of this. I know it's a selfish request, but do you think you could keep what really happened a secret?|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> Though he does not believe he deserves mercy, he does not wish for {{Term|BotW|Koko}} and {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} to suffer more.<ref>{{Cite|I truly don't deserve mercy, but I don't want my children, Koko and Cottla, to suffer anymore.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He pledges to protect {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}} and his family from now on.<ref>{{Cite|I know it doesn't make up for what I've done, but I pledge to protect the village and my family from now on. No matter the cost.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Lakna Rokee Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Lakna Rokee|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Lakna Rokee. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Lakna Rokee|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Lakna Rokee Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Lakna Rokee}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Edge of Duality|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Lakna Rokee}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will commend him for his efforts, giving him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Lakna Rokee|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lakna Rokee}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Lakna Rokee|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
Carrying the Ancient Orb intended for the [[Ritaag Zumo Shrine]] and placing it into the [[Concave Slot]] of the Pedestal of this shine (Lakna Rokee Shrine) does nothing. It does not activate anything or make this shrine rise up from the ground.
