==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Gohma Egg}}<br/>When this egg hatches, a Gohma Larva will be born.
}}

Gohma Eggs appear [[Inside the Deku Tree]] in {{OoT}}. They can be found hanging on the ceiling, falling when [[Link]] gets close enough, damaging him on contact. After hatching, a [[Gohma Larva]] will be born from it. Link can prevent this by destroying the Egg with the [[Fairy Slingshot]] while still on the ceiling. During the dungeon's boss fight, [[Gohma]] will hatch more Eggs to distract Link.

Gohma Eggs also appear in [[Dodongo's Cavern]] in {{MQ}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
Gohma Eggs appear during the fight against [[Armogohma]] in {{TP}}. While crawling on the ceiling, Armogohma will hatch Eggs from which [[Baby Gohma]]s are born.
