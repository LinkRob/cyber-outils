'''Evermean''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] that appear in {{TotK}}.

== Characteristics ==
{{Compendium|name=Evermean|desc=  A monster that mimics a tree, found throughout the forests of Hyrule. These impostors are hard to differentiate from the genuine article, making it easy for them to team up and pounce on unsuspecting passersby. Because the species shares characteristics with trees, it can be chopped down in much the same way.|locs=  Great Hyrule Forest, Hyrule Field|loot= Energetic Rhino Beetle, Bladed Rhino Beetle, Rugged Rhino Beetle, Acorn, Hightail Lizard, Sturdy Thick Stick, Chickaloo Tree Nut, Monster Extract, Korok Frond, Golden Apple}}
Evermean are found across many forests and plains in [[Hyrule]], hiding amongst regular trees in order to catch [[Link]] by surprise. They are nearly indistinguishable from regular trees, save for a subtle wiggling motion they occasionally exhibit. When Link gets too close, they will spring out of the ground and attempt to smash him by slamming their trunks down at him. Evermean are immune to several forms of damage, including sources of [[Ice]] and [[Lightning]] [[magic]]. Due to their resemblance to trees, however, they are susceptible to sharp weapons such as [[Sword]]s and axes. They are also weak to [[Fire]].

Evermean can also be encountered in [[The Depths]], where they mimic large fungi and ferns. They are often coated in [[Gloom]].

==Trivia==
*The name Evermean is a pun based on "evergreen", a type of plant that possesses foliage all year round.
*Compared to normal trees, Evermean Logs will drop notably more wood when destroyed.
*Evermean may have been inspired by the [https://www.mariowiki.com/Walking_Tree Walking Trees] in the [https://www.mariowiki.com/DS_Luigi%27s_Mansion Luigi's Mansion] course from ''[https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Kart_DS Mario Kart DS]''.
