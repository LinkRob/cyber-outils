{{Term/Store|Kalani||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Kalani|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kalani}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} child who lives in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She and her sister, {{Term|BotW|Makure|link}}, reside at the {{Term|BotW|Arrow Specialty Shop|link}} {{Term|BotW|Stall|link}} that their grandmother {{Term|BotW|Danda|link}} manages.

During the day, {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} will stand in front of the {{Term|BotW|Arrow Specialty Shop}}, where she encourages customers to buy from her grandmother.<ref>{{Cite|Vasaaq! Hey, lady! Be sure to buy a LOT of stuff, OK?|Kalani|BotW}}</ref> Even as she sleeps, {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} will attempt to attract customers.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Vasaaq. Buy a lot...OK?}}|Kalani|BotW}}</ref> She recommends each type of elemental {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}, saying that {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}} go "BOOOOOM!!!", {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}} go "BZZBZZT!!!", {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}} go "CRAAAAACK!!!" and {{Plural|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}} go "BOOSH!!!".<ref>{{Cite|I really recommend...{{Color|BotW Blue|bomb arrows}}! If you shoot them at something... BOOOOOM!!! It's amazing!|Kalani|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I really recommend...{{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}! If you shoot them at something... BZZBZZT!!! They zap!|Kalani|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I really recommend...{{Color|BotW Blue|ice arrows}}! If you shoot them at something... CRAAAAACK!!! Frozen solid!|Kalani|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I really recommend...{{Color|BotW Blue|fire arrows}}! If you shoot them at something... BOOSH!!! Flames everywhere!|Kalani|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} buys all of the {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} at the {{Term|BotW|Arrow Specialty Shop|display=Shop}}, {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} asks if he is a good person, since her grandmother told her that people who spend a lot of {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} are good people.<ref>{{Cite|You're a good person, right, lady? My vaba always says that people who spend lots of money are good people!|Kalani|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} running around {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she does not have time to greet people and that the {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Frita|link}}, will be gone if she does not hurry.<ref>{{Cite|Wait... I don't have time to hang around here greeting people like this! If I don't hurry, the vure will go away!|Kalani|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} while she is staring at {{Term|BotW|Frita}}, she comments about what a "big vure" she is.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa... That's a really big vure...|Kalani|BotW}}</ref>

As the sun sets, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} and the other three children in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} speaking to each other as a group.<ref>{{Cite|I've got to hurry up and meet the others!|Kalani|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dalia|link}} will be late joining the group. During this time, {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} asks the others if they have heard what happened to her crops.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard about Dalia's crops?|Kalani|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Makure}} comments that {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} has not stopped crying.<ref>{{Cite|Dalia... She won't stop crying...|Makure|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pearle|link}} declares she will never forgive the person who destroyed {{Term|BotW|Dalia}}'s crops.<ref>{{Cite|Who would do something like that? I'll never forgive you!|Pearle|BotW}}</ref>

Once all of the children are gathered together, {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} asks them if they are in love.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you guys! Are you in love?|Kalani|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Makure}} then responds that she wants to attend {{Term|BotW|Ashai|link}}'s class when she grows up so she can get married.<ref>{{Cite|When I'm all growed up, I will be! I'll go to Miss Ashai's class, and I'll become in love and I'll get married!|Makure|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pearle}} then comments that {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} is unmarried, but she does not know what that word means.<ref>{{Cite|Mom says it's really strange that Miss Ashai is unmarried but teaches her class on voe. I wonder what unmarried means...|Pearle|BotW}}</ref> Lastly, {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} says that she heard being in love is the sweetest thing in the world, but she does not think she can handle something sweeter than berries.<ref>{{Cite|I heard that being in love and being married is the sweetest thing in the world! But I really like berries, and those are really sweet. I don't know if I can handle something sweeter than that!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref>

At the end of the day, {{Term|BotW|Kalani}} and {{Term|BotW|Makure}} return to the {{Term|BotW|Arrow Specialty Shop}} to sleep.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'saaba.<br/>Today was another busy day, so I think I'll go to bed soon.|Kalani|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's dark out now, so I'm going back to my vaba's booth. Sav'orq!|Makure|BotW}}</ref>
