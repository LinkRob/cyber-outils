{{Term/Store|Gooey Swamp||Series, FPTRR}} is an area in the Second Continent in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Dungeon==
The dungeon in this area is called [[Insect Cavern]]. In this dungeon are dark rooms and rooms filled with poisonous gas. The dungeon's boss is a reappearance of [[Death Bug]].

==Local Speciality==
The Gooey Swamp's local specialty is the [[Recipes|Gooey Tartare]]. The recipe for Gooey Tartare is as follows:
* [[Bitter Newt]] X 7
* [[Soft Egg]] X 8
This recipe can be found in the [[Insect Cavern]].

==Other information==
* In this area, [[Tingle]] can use his [[Bone Ocarina]] to summon the pirates. To progress on [[Mount Desma]], Tingle will have to return to Gooey Swamp to help the pirates who are under attack by Death Bugs.
* Tingle can earn many Rupees by finding and catching the butterflies in this area. There are two butterflies in Gooey Swamp, and one in [[Steamy Marsh]].
* An empty jar can be bought from the Explorer's Assistant for 1050 Rupees.
