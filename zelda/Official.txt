The {{Term/Store|Official||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Biography==
When [[Link]] arrives at [[Hyrule Castle]], the Official guides him through the castle's art gallery, which consists of paintings depicting the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|ancient battles]] [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|against Ganon]] fought by Link, [[Princess Zelda]], the [[Sage#A Link to the Past|Seven Sages]], and their [[Maidens#A Link to the Past|descendants]]. During the tour, he mentions that [[Impa]] is part of the Sage's illustrious lineage.<ref>{{Cite|The paintings here are spectacular. But they also tell of our legendary past - and of the Seven Sages.|Official|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The descendants of the Seven Sages live among us today. It's no secret that Lady Impa is one of them!|Official|ALBW}}</ref> He is absent after [[Yuga]] lays siege to the castle, presumably slain or having fled with the castle's other denizens.
