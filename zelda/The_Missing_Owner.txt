==Overview==
Cara is worried for Isha, the owner of the {{Term|TotK|Gerudo Town|link}} accessory shop. She went east of {{Term|TotK|Gerudo Desert|link}} and into Toruma Dunes to collect some materials,<ref>{{Cite|She was in such high spirts, too, gushing about all the treasures she'd find around the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Toruma Dunes</font>.|Cara|TotK}}</ref> but hasn't came back. She forewarned that it is {{Term|TotK|Molduga|link}} territory<ref>{{Cite|That's a dangerous place, though. <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Molduga</font> territory. I hope she didn't run into trouble...|Cara|TotK}}</ref> and fear the worst she may have been caught up by one.

{{Term|TotK|Link|link}} heads over to Toruma Dunes to find Isha. There he found Isha, as well as a Molduga roaming around. Because of the Molduga in the area, Isha is stuck on a rock and cowering in fear.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Go away, Molduga. Go away. Please...}}|Isha|TotK}}</ref> To save her, Link defeats the Molduga.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The— The Molduga... It's gone?!}}|Isha|TotK}}</ref>

Upon discovering the Molduga is gone, Isha is amazed at Link that he defeated it himself. She thanked Link for saving her. She wishes to repay him his heroism and asked him to come to her accessory shop. Returning to Gerudo Town, he finds Cara relieved to see Isha okay. Cara thanked Link for rescuing her and gave him a {{Term|TotK|Diamond|link}} as thanks.
