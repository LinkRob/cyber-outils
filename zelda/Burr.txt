{{Term/Store|Burr|Burrs|Series, SS|plural}} are traps in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS |publisher= Prima |edition= Collector's Edition |year= 2013 |page= 63}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|SS|Burr}} are usually found attached to {{Plural|SS|Rope (Object)|link}}, but can also be found on the ground hidden among {{Term|SS|Grass|link}}. If they come in contact with {{Term|SS|Link|link}}, they will attach to him, flashing red with an increasing frequency before finally exploding. Though their explosions deal little damage, if {{Term|SS|Link}} is on a {{Term|SS|Rope (Object)}} he can be knocked off. {{Plural|SS|Burr}} stuck to {{Term|SS|Link}} can shaken off, for example by swinging his {{Term|SS|Sword|link}}. {{Plural|SS|Burr}} can be detached from {{Plural|SS|Rope (Object)}} by shaking the {{Term|SS|Wii Remote|link}} to make {{Term|SS|Link}} jostle.

{{Plural|SS|Burr}} can be found in the {{Term|SS|Faron Woods Entry|link}}, {{Term|SS|In the Woods|link}}, in the {{Term|SS|Deep Woods|link}}, at the {{Term|SS|Forest Temple|link}}, and within the {{Term|SS|Skyview Temple|link}}.
