'''''The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword''''' is a manga loosely based on the [[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|eponymous game]] drawn by [[Akira Himekawa]]. All 32 pages of the manga were included in the artbook, {{HH}}.

==Overview==
{{Stub|SS}}
The manga serves as a prequel to the story of {{SS|-}}, detailing the origin of the relationship between [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]] and [[Link]], [[Hylia's Chosen Hero|Link]]'s relationship to the goddess, [[Hylia]], and the tale of how [[Skyloft]] rose into the sky.  The book introduces [[:en:Link#Skyward_Sword|Link]] from his past life before his ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'' incarnation. The manga also introduces the [[The Goddess Hylia|goddess Hylia]] herself. In the end of the manga it shows [[Link]] and the [[The Goddess Hylia|Goddess Hylia]] being reincarnated into the next [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]].
