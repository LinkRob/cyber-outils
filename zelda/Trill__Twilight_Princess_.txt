{{Term/Store|Trill||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 276 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TPHD|Trill (Twilight Princess)}} is an intelligent {{Wp|Toucan}} who operates his [[Trill's Shop|Shop]] near the {{Term|TPHD|Forest Temple|link}}, and he is also [[Coro|Coro's]] pet [[Bird]].<ref name="TPHD Guide 50"/> If {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} takes an item and leaves without paying {{Term|TPHD|Trill (Twilight Princess)}}, he will peck {{Term|TPHD|Link}} who will take damage until he pays or escapes. If {{Term|TPHD|Link}} pays less than the cost of the item, {{Term|TPHD|Trill (Twilight Princess)}} calls Link a cheapskate,<ref>{{Cite|...Pay like you're supposed to next time, you cheapskate...|Trill|TP}}</ref> while paying more than the amount required causes {{Term|TPHD|Trill (Twilight Princess)}} to call him a generous young man.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! What a generous young man!|Trill|TP}}</ref> He will call {{Term|TPHD|Link}} stingy every time {{Term|TPHD|Link}} returns if he pays less than he is supposed to for an item.<ref>{{Cite|Pfft! Stingy! Cheap!|Trill|TP}}</ref> If {{Term|TPHD|Link}} steals from his store and talks to {{Term|TPHD|Trill (Twilight Princess)}} as {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}, he reveals that his master got mad at him for it. As a result, he puts water in the Lantern Oil urn out of frustration.<ref>{{Cite|The other day my master got mad at me for letting a thief steal stuff... I was so frustrated that I put water in the oil urn!|Trill|TP}}</ref> This does not effect the potency of the Oil.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|TPHD|Trill (Twilight Princess)}} will attack {{Plural|TPHD|Bokoblin|link}} if they come too close to him and will thank {{Term|TPHD|Link}} if he defeats them for him.<ref>{{Cite|GRRRARR! FWEEEEEEEEEEET! Hey! Punish the evildoers!|Trill|TP}}</ref>
*The feathers on top of Trill's head resemble Coro's hair.
