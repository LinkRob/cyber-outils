{{Term/Store|Mouse|Mice|Series|plural}} are [[animals]] that appear in {{LA}}.{{Name Ref|LA}}

==Characteristics==
Blue Mice appear in the [[Witch's Hut]] and [[Crazy Tracy's Health Spa]]. One Mouse runs amok in the Witch's Hut, while two Mice are found in the Health Spa. The Mice simply run about the houses and cannot be interacted with.

{{LA|DX}} also introduces the [[Photographer]], an anthropomorphic purple Mouse that resides in the [[Camera Shop]] in [[Tal Tal Heights]]. This Mouse can be visited to have him take [[Link]]'s picture, after which the Photographer will begin to appear at certain moments to take more pictures of Link's travels.
