{{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Recipe|Recipes|Series, FPTRR|plural}}}} are items in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Recipes|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Recipes allow Tingle to create certain items, such as [[bomb]]s, soups, and healing potions from specific ingredients. [[Tingle]] can cook the same items free-hand and without the recipes, but cannot produce more than one batch at a time and must select each ingredient individually. There are a total of 31 recipes in all, with requirements ranging from three to fifteen ingredients.

Recipes can be found in many different places; some are located in [[Treasure Chest]]s, while others can be found in [[dungeon]]s, or generally exploring the map. Most, however, must be purchased or received - often in exchange for aid or assistance - from people in [[Port Town]] or elsewhere. All potions can be sold somewhere, and a few have no further purpose than raising [[Rupee]]s in this way.

The Super Chef Hat [[Rupee Good]] can be obtained by speaking to the Port Town chef after [[Tingle]] has collected all 31 recipes.
