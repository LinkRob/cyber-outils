{{Term/Store|Black Stalfos Mage|Black Stalfos Mages|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Black Stalfos Mage|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==

{{Plural|CoH|Black Stalfos Mage}} are the strongest variety of {{Term|CoH|Stalfos Mage|link}}. They move on every other beat, and if {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} stands two spaces ahead of them, they will use their magic to create a gust of wind that pulls the heroes towards them before attacking.

==Trivia==
They behave identically to the Black Skeleton Mage from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
