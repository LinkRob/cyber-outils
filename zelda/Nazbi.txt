{{Term/Store|Nazbi||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nazbi|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who travels the {{Term|BotW|Hilltop High Road|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}} with his {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dauntless|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Slumber awaits me at {{Color|BotW Blue|Serenne Stable}}, and then it's on to {{Color|BotW Blue|Snowfield Stable}}. Heigh-*huff*, Dauntless, my noble steed!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> The pair stop at {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable|link}} along their journey. {{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} carries a {{Term|BotW|Fisherman's Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} is a treasure hunter and adventurer.<ref>{{Cite|My name is Nazbi, treasure hunter and adventurer extraordinaire!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My name is Nazbi, treasure hunter and adventurer extraordinaire! Ahh, what a wonderful title to claim. Why I just may introduce myself again! My name is Nazbi— What's that? You really think once was enough?|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> His purpose in tracing the {{Term|BotW|Hilltop High Road}} is to search of the {{Term|BotW|Dragon's Road|link}}, which is said to be near the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm travelling {{Color|BotW Blue|Hilltop High Road}} in search of the {{Color|BotW Red|dragon's road}}. They say it's not far from {{Color|BotW Blue|Tanagar Canyon}}.|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My never-ending pursuit of treasure and adventure has led me to {{Color|BotW Blue|Hilltop High Road}} in search of the {{Color|BotW Red|dragon's road}}. They say it's not too far from {{Color|BotW Blue|Tanagar Canyon}}.|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|They say the {{Color|BotW Red|dragon's road}} is not too far from {{Color|BotW Blue|Tanagar Canyon}}. It's my duty as an adventurer extraordinaire to find it!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I long for adventure as a weary traveler longs for a warm bed. Right now, I'm in pursuit of the legend of the dragon!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> Since the {{Term|BotW|Hilltop High Road}} is long and winds like a {{Term|BotW|Dragon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} wonders if it could be the {{Term|BotW|Dragon's Road}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm, now that I think about it... The {{Color|BotW Blue|Hilltop High Road}} is long and winding, very much like a dragon itself!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Could it be that this very {{Color|BotW Blue|Hilltop High Road}} is the selfsame {{Color|BotW Red|dragon's road}}?|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> As he travels, he often wonders about the {{Term|BotW|Dragon's Road|display=Road}} aloud to himself.<ref>{{Cite|Where could it be?|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The dragon's road is...|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Is this the dragon's road?|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> Even though he may encounter danger, {{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} is determined to find the {{Term|BotW|Dragon's Road}}.<ref>{{Cite|The road to {{Color|BotW Blue|Serenne Stable}} is wrought with danger, but I shall gallop onward until I find the {{Color|BotW Red|dragon's road}}!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The road to {{Color|BotW Blue|Serenne Stable}} is wrought with danger, but we shall gallop onward until we find the {{Color|BotW Red|dragon's road}}!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} pledges to continue his search for {{Term|BotW|Dragon's Road}} as long as {{Term|BotW|Dauntless}} is strong.<ref>{{Cite|As long as my noble steed, Dauntless, is strong, I, Nazbi, treasure hunter and adventurer extraordinaire, shan't relent!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> He thanks {{Term|BotW|Dauntless}} for this, declaring that they are equal partners.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, my noble Dauntless! We are equal partners even though you do all the work! Now, heigh-ho!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref>

In addition to the mystery of {{Term|BotW|Dragon's Road}}, {{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} wants to solve the riddle pertaining to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, which is supposed to be west of the snow fields.<ref>{{Cite|I dream of solving the riddle of Rito Village, west beyond the snow fields.|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> However, considering how long his bucket list is, he does not think he will be able to get to it.<ref>{{Cite|I dream of solving the riddle of Rito Village, west beyond the snow, but my adventuring bucket list is long enough.|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Then again, my adventuring bucket list is long enough. Heigh-ho, Dauntless, my noble steed! Onward to parts unknown!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref>

Despite being an adventurer, {{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} has difficulty coping with cold {{Plural|BotW|Temperature|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've conquered many an adventure in my time, but overcoming this cold has proven ever an insurmountable feat. The road to {{Color|BotW Blue|Snowfield Stable}} is just plain freezing! {{Small|Heigh-ho, Dauntless... Heigh...cooooold...}}|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> He attempts to work himself in order to keep warm, but this strategy has little success.<ref>{{Cite|My name is Nazbi, treasure hunter and adventurer extraordinaire! Adventure lurks around every corner! Heigh-ho! {{Small|I hoped getting myself worked up like that would warm me up, but...the road to {{Color|BotW Blue|Snowfield Stable}} is just too cold! Brrrrr...}}|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> He will still encourage {{Term|BotW|Dauntless}} to face the cold.<ref>{{Cite|Heigh-ho, Dauntless, my noble steed! This chill is but another obstacle that stands in the way of our adventure!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> When he stops at {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} is unable to stop his teeth from chattering due to the cold.<ref>{{Cite|When it gets this cold, *clack-chatter* I just can't stop my *chatter-chatter* teeth from chattering. Heigh...*clack-clack* ho, Dauntless!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref>

When he is caught in the {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} will be disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, why must it rain so heavy upon my adventuring soul...|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> After finding shelter, he wonders when the {{Term|BotW|Rain}} will end.<ref>{{Cite|The only recourse in the rain is to keep dry until it passes. But when, oh when, will the rain cease?|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} will occasionally share knowledge of the area with {{Term|BotW|Link}}. For example, he has heard of a "forest where the master lives" south of {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, in that case, allow me to simply offer a fellow adventurer some top-tier treasure-hunter info. Rumors speak of a "forest where the master lives" somewhere to the south of here.|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I don't have the exact details, but rumors tell of a "forest where the master lives" to the south of here.|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> He is also interested in the {{Term|BotW|Footrace|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Upland Lindor|link}}, and he suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} try it out.<ref>{{Cite|There's a person at {{Color|BotW Blue|Upland Lindor}} who challenges travelers to {{Color|BotW Red|footraces}}. Give it a shot if you think you're quick enough.|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Though, I am rather curious about the person at {{Color|BotW Blue|Upland Lindor}} who challenges travelers to {{Color|BotW Red|footraces}}...|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} initially claims that he is too busy adventuring to try it himself, he will then admit he has become reliant on {{Term|BotW|Dauntless}} for transportation.<ref>{{Cite|Why don't I give it a shot? Because a renowned adventurer such as myself is far too busy adventuring! ...OK, you got me. After riding Dauntless here for so long, I can only run a few steps before I'm totally winded.|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref>
