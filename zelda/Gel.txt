{{Term/Store|Gel|Gels|Series, TLoZ, OoS, FS, PH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name= "Encyclopedia"/>

==Characteristics==
When attacked with a weak weapon, [[Zol]]s have the ability to split into two Gels, which are smaller versions of Zols. Gels are circular, often green or red blobs that tend to bounce aimlessly around wherever they are found. They appear within Labyrinth, as seen in {{TLoZ}}, or across the overworld, as seen in {{LA}}. Sometimes, Gels can appear without the presence of Zols. In {{TLoZ|-}}, Gels never drop any items when defeated. Gels in {{LA|-}}, {{OoS}}, {{OoA}}, and {{PH}} do not cause damage, but can attach themselves to [[Link]]'s body.
