The {{Term/Store|House of Pirates||Series, OoS}} is a location in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite|House of Pirates|Map Screen|OoS}}</ref> As its name suggests, it is where the Piratians take residence. 

==Features==
===Overview===
Surrounded by the {{Term|OoS|Subrosia Cemetery|link}}, the House of Pirates is the place where the [[Piratian]]s take up residence when their ship crash-lands in the [[Samasa Desert]], driving them all the way into the hidden underground world of Subrosia.<ref>{{Cite|Our ship got caught in a storm and sank. When we woke, 'ere we were. Where are we? It all looks so weird.|Piratian|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Some weird guys called Piratians have settled down south.|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> The Piratians live in the House of Pirates until Link helps them escape by helping the [[Cap'n]] find his [[Pirate's Bell|bell]] after completing the {{Term|OoS|Ancient Ruins|link}} dungeon.

The House of Pirates is two stories tall. On the second story, there is a Piratian who will teach Link the correct pattern for opening the gate to [[Samasa Desert]] using a set of four drawers. On the roof of the Pirate House is a [[Portal (Object)|Portal]] that warps Link to a cave beneath [[Horon Village]] to the south, providing easy access to the area. 

===Unlucky Sailor===
{{Main|Unlucky Sailor}}
After the Piratians leave Subrosia, a few of them get left behind. In a [[Linked Game]], one of the Pirates, named the Unlucky Sailor, who seems to believe strongly in luck, believes that the ship would never have crashed (and thus he would have never been left behind) if he knew the [[Linked Game/Secrets#Pirate Secret|secret to being lucky]].

==Trivia==
*Although the building is currently named the "House of Pirates" due its residents, the Subrosian within indicates that it is actually his house and that the pirates are merely crashing there. Once they leave, he reacts angrily to Link's return, believing the Hylian intends to crash there as well.{{Fact}}
