{{Term/Store|Dauntless||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Noble"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Dauntless}} is a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} belonging to the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} treasure hunter {{Term|BotW|Nazbi|link}}.<ref name="Noble"/> Together they can be found traveling along the {{Term|BotW|Hilltop High Road|link}}, making stops at {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Dauntless}} is equipped with a {{Term|BotW|Stable Saddle|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Stable Bridle|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} claims to have become dependent upon {{Term|BotW|Dauntless}}, being unable to run a few steps on his own without getting winded.<ref>{{Cite|After riding Dauntless here for so long, I can only run a few steps before I'm totally winded.|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> He shows a great deal of affection toward {{Term|BotW|Dauntless}}, who he describes as his "beloved Dauntless," "dear horse,"<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my beloved Dauntless... My dear horse is every bit an adventurer as I!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> "noble steed,"<ref name="Noble"/> and "equal partner."<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, my noble Dauntless! We are equal partners even though you do all the work! Now, heigh-ho!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> Even in the face of cold weather, {{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} encourages {{Term|BotW|Dauntless}} to continue forward.<ref>{{Cite|Heigh-ho, Dauntless, my noble steed! This chill is but another obstacle that stands in the way of our adventure!|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref>
