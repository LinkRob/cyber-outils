{{Term/Store|The So-Called Knight||HWAoC}} is a level 45 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The So-Called Knight|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|The So-Called Knight}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Rubber Armor|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}'s objective is to capture two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} within three minutes. Both {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are occupied by a large number of {{Plural|HWAoC|Zora Soldier|link}} and one {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain|link}}. One of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} is in the north, and the other in the south. When {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} approaches the northern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}} appears and must be defeated to capture it, along with the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain}}. Once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured, the timer resets to three minutes and {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} is given the objective to capture the remaining two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}}, located in the center and eastern side of the map. The central {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Soldier|link}}, and when entered, a {{Term|HWAoC|Rito Captain|link}} spawns, which must be defeated to capture it. There is a {{Term|HWAoC|Rito Captain}} in the eastern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} as well, along with more {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Soldier}}, although {{Term|HWAoC|Teba|link}} appears as well and must be defeated to capture the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}. Once the objective is reached, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} spawn, and the timer is reset once again to three minutes. A group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Soldier}} and another group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Zora Soldier}} also spawn on a path in between {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} and the two {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} are defeated.
