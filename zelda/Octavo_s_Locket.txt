{{Term/Store|Octavo's Locket||Series, CoH}} is an item in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Octavo's Locket}}<br/>Gives immunity to wind and electrical damage. A prized heirloom, this locket was passed down through Octavo's family for generations.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> It was introduced as part of the {{OO}} [[Downloadable Content]] update.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|CoH|Octavo's Locket}} can only be found in [[Octavo's Ode (Mode)|Octavo’s Ode]]. When found, {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} recognizes it and reasons that he must have lost it.<ref>{{Cite|Oh. There's my locket. I must have misplaced it. But how did it end up here?|Octavo|CoH}}</ref> {{Term|CoH|Octavo's Locket}} grants immunity to wind and electrical damage.<ref>{{Cite|You got {{Color|CoH Yellow|Octavo's Locket}}! This passive item grants immunity from wind effects and electrical damage.|N/A|CoH}}</ref>
