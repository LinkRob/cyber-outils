{{Term/Store|Club Moblin|Club Moblins|Series, OoT, OoT3D|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 171}}</ref> also known as '''Great Moblins''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 177}}</ref> are enemies in {{OoT}}.

==Characteristics==
A Club Moblin stands as the final obstacle between Link and the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}}. It easily surpasses the height of its [[Sacred Forest Meadow]] counterparts, and with that, it also boasts new techniques that enable it to protect the Forest Temple from entry. It wields an enormous club, which creates shock-waves upon striking the ground. In {{OoT3D}}, a brief scene shows upon encountering the Club Moblin for the first time, showing it laughing at Link. In {{MQ}}, another Club Moblin appears in the [[Spirit Temple]].

Before obtaining the [[Bow]], which is the most efficient method of defeating them, Link can release [[Bombchu]] at the Club Moblin to attack from a distance. If Link has none, he must run towards the Great Moblin, dodging the shockwaves. After being struck, the Club Moblin takes some time to recover, allowing Link to sneak behind it. Once surpassed, Link can slash it with the [[Sword]] from behind. Because it is immobile, the Club Moblin is unable to turn around and face Link.

Upon death, Club Moblins will always drop a single Red [[Rupee]], or three Blue Rupees.

==Trivia==
*As with other [[Moblin]]s in the game, Club Moblins cannot be targeted so [[Navi]] has no comment about them.
*Its Japanese name is shared with the [[Moblin Chief]] and [[Big Blin]].
