{{Term/Store|Shooting Gallery Owner||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Shooting Gallery Owner, as his name suggests, is the Owner of the [[Shooting Gallery]] located in the [[Hyrule Castle Town Market]]. He runs the Shooting Gallery when [[Link]] is a child, but then moves to [[Kakariko Village]] after seven years.
