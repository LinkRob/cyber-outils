"{{Term/Store|The Sheep Rustlers||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Sheep Rustlers}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Hateno Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Hateno Pasture|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Koyin|link}}, who says that she will make someone pay.<ref>{{Cite|They'll pay! They'll all pay!|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> She rudely asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he wants,<ref>{{Cite|What do YOU want?|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> badgering him for acting clueless.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, don't act so clueless.|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> However, when she realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is harmless, she apologizes for her behavior and explains that she has been on edge lately.<ref>{{Cite|Although... Fine. You seem harmless enough. Forgive me, I've just been on edge with all these monsters running around.|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> Monsters have been coming to the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Pasture|display=Pasture}} and stealing the {{Term|BotW|Highland Sheep|link}}, depleting their numbers.<ref>{{Cite|They come up from {{Color|BotW Blue|Hateno Beach}} to steap my sheep. If someone doesn't do something soon, I won't have any left!|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins.

At {{Term|BotW|Hateno Beach}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will encounter a {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} consisting of seven monsters,<ref>{{Cite|I was mad enough to spit, but maybe not mad enough to take on {{Color|BotW Red|seven monsters}} at once.|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> six {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} and a single {{Term|BotW|Moblin|link}}. In addition, there are two {{Plural|BotW|Highland Sheep}} that are kept in a makeshift pen. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat all of the monsters for {{Term|BotW|Koyin}}.<ref>{{Cite|According to her, the monsters in question congregate at {{Color|BotW Red|Hateno Beach}}. She won't breathe easy until {{Color|BotW Red|every last}} monster there is defeated.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to open their {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Ruby|link}}.

Back at {{Term|BotW|Hateno Pasture}}, {{Term|BotW|Koyin}} notes that the {{Plural|BotW|Highland Sheep}} have calmed down now that they are no longer in danger.<ref>{{Cite|The sheep have calmed down. I think they get that they're not about to be snatched at any moment.|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> She asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he was the one who handled the monsters,<ref>{{Cite|Hey... Sorry if I'm off the mark in asking this, but are you the one who took care of those monsters?|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> revealing that she had a suspicion.<ref>{{Cite|[That's right.] I had a feeling. When the sheep all calmed down, I thought, "Maybe he did it..."|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} 10 {{Plural|BotW|Fresh Milk|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So it seems I owe you some thanks. This isn't much of a reward, but hey, it's light. That counts for something.|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Koyin}} remarks it will be nice to sleep at nice without worrying about the flock,<ref>{{Cite|It'll be nice to have a night's sleep without worrying about my flock.|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> and she sheepishly invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back and speak to her at any time.<ref>{{Cite|And, er... If you want...you can talk to me anytime. O-or not!|Koyin|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
