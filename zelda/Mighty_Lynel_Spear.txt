{{Compendium/Extract|Mighty Lynel Spear}}
{{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, though, they are used by {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}}. Both the weight and cutting edge of these {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Spear|display=Spears}} have been enhanced by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Though they are heavy for a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} are strong enough to cleave through rocks with them.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Due to their metallic nature, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Spear}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} is able to pick them up. They are unable to burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
