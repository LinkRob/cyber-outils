{{Term/Store|The Legend of Zelda Chess Set||Series}} is a Zelda-themed {{Wp|Chess}} set manufactured by [https://theop.games/ USAopoly].<ref name="Info"/>

==Overview==
The Legend of Zelda Chess Set is a chess set themed around {{TLoZ|Series}}. The chess set includes 32 figures of characters that serve as the chess pieces, a game board, and an instruction booklet.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= 32 Custom Legend of Zelda Figurine Chess Pieces made out of Vinyl|author= |published= |retrieved= May 29, 2020|url= https://theop.games/products/game/the-legend-of-zelda-chess-set/|title= The Legend of Zelda™ Chess Set|site= theop.games|type= }}</ref> The fold out chess board includes two {{Plural|Series|Royal Crest|link}} around the main playing area. The four page instruction booklet gives detailed instructions on how to play chess as well as information on the chess pieces. Of the 32 pieces, {{Term|Series|Link|link}} and {{Term|Series|Ganondorf|link}} are the kings, {{Term|Series|Zelda|link}} and {{Term|Series|Twinrova|link}} are the queens, {{Term|Series|Impa|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Iron Knuckle|link}} are the bishops, {{Term|Series|Epona|link}} and {{Term|Series|Phantom Ganon|link}}'s {{Term|Series|Horse}} are the knights, {{Term|Series|Darunia|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Armos|link}} are rooks, and lastly, {{Term|Series|Navi|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Deku Scrub|link}} are the pawns.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= '''{{Big|Contents:}}<br/>One Chess board<br/>16 The Legend of Zelda Hero Gold Chess pieces:'''<br/>1 King - Link • 1 Queen - Zelda • 2 Bishops - Impa<br/>2 Knights - Epona • 2 Rooks - Darunia • 8 Pawns - Navi<br/>'''16 The Legend of Zelda Villain Black Chess Pieces:'''<br/>1 King - Ganondorf • 1 Queen - Twinrova<br/>2 Bishops - Iron Knuckle • 2 Knights - Phantom<br/>2 Rooks - Armos • 8 Pawns - Deku Scrub |product= The Legend of Zelda Chess Set|page= 1}}</ref>
