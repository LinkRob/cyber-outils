{{Term/Store|Kamaro||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=  |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref>

==Biography==
Kamaro is a ghost who died in [[Termina Field]].<ref>{{Cite|The spirit of a charismatic dancer who died in Termina Field dances there night after night.|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> In life, he was a dancer, but now he is filled with regret because he was not able to pass on his dance moves to the world while he was alive.<ref>{{Cite|I am no longer part of the living...My sadness to the moon... I haven't left my dance to the world...I am filled with regret.|Kamaro|MM}}</ref> He planned to bring the world together with his choreography, but he inexplicably passed away and now lingers the world wishing he had taught his routine to someone.<ref>{{Cite|I am disappointed, oh moon. I have died! Oh, I planned to bring the world together and stir it into a giant melting pot with my dance! If only I had taught my new dance to someone...|Kamaro|MM}}</ref>

The ghost dancer can be found from midnight until 6 AM in the giant mushroom-shaped stones near the entrance to [[Snowhead]]. When [[Link]] eases his soul by playing the "[[Song of Healing]]," his dancing ability becomes encased in the [[Kamaro's Mask]], granting it to the young hero and trusting him to make his routine into a "popular dance craze."<ref>{{Cite|Spread my dance across the world... Train its followers... (Translation) I have taught it to you, now make it into a popular dance craze!|Kamaro|MM}}</ref> With this, Kamaro's dance can be taught to the [[Rosa Sisters]] in [[West Clock Town]] to gain a [[Piece of Heart]].

==Trivia==
*A modified version of his theme can be heard in [[West Clock Town]] at night even if Link has not taught the [[Rosa Sisters]] his dance. This is a hint that the player is supposed to use Kamaro's Mask right in front of them.
