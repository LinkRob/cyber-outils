{{Term/Store|Swinging Platform|Swinging Platforms|Series, SS|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Crossing the Swinging Platform|Sheikah Stone|SS}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Swinging Platform}} go unnamed in {{TP}}, {{TFH}}, and {{BotW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
Metallic {{Plural|TPHD|Swinging Platform}} can be found within the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins|link}} in {{TP}}. They are suspended by metal braces and can be moved by being struck by the {{Term|TPHD|Ball and Chain|link}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
Wooden {{Plural|SS|Swinging Platform}} can be found {{Term|SS|Inside the Great Tree|link}} and in the {{Term|SS|Sky Keep|link}} in {{SS}}. They can be moved with the {{Term|SS|Gust Bellows|link}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
Metallic {{Plural|TFH|Swinging Platform}} can be found in the {{Term|TFH|Den of Flames|link}} in {{TFH}}. They are suspended by metal braces and pivot from side to side automatically. The {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} can change the direction and speed of a {{Term|TFH|Swinging Platform}} by firing the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}} while riding on one.

==={{BotW|-}}===
Metallic {{Plural|BotW|Swinging Platform}} can be found inside {{Term|BotW|Mogg Latan Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. They can be moved with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
