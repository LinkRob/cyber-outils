{{Term/Store|Farore's Flame||Series, SS}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tempered by Farore's Flame, this blade's edge is exquisitely sharp.|N/A|SS}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|SSHD|Farore's Flame}} is one of the three {{Plural|Series|Sacred Flame|link}}, protected by the dragon {{Term|SSHD|Faron (Dragon)|link}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Surface is divided into three regions. Each region is named after one of the dragons, Faron, Eldin, and Lanayru, who protect the Sacred Flames.|book=HH|page=74}}</ref>, and intended to enhance the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword|link}} with the {{Term|SSHD|Force|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=All the while, Link continued with his task of tempering the Goddess Sword in the Sacred Flames of the Golden Goddesses. Imbued with the immense, sacred power of the Force, the Goddess Sword was transformed into the Master Sword.|book=HH|page=75}}</ref>

In order to awaken the {{Term|SSHD|Gate of Time|link}}, {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} must empower the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword}} with all three {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Flame}};<ref>{{Cite|Ah, sorry to disappoint you, boy, but for now your sword lacks the power necessary to awaken the gate. First, you and your sword must grow together.|Impa|SS}}</ref> in order to obtain them, he must pass the trials found in the {{Plural|SSHD|Silent Realm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The sacred flames are three in number. To obtain them, you must also earn relics known as the three sacred gifts. For each trial you overcome, you shall be blessed with one of the gifts...|Fi|SS}}</ref>

{{Term|SSHD|Farore's Flame}} is kept within the {{Term|SSHD|Ancient Cistern|link}} in the {{Term|SSHD|Faron Province|link}}. It doubles the damage dealt by the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|The flames of Farore have improved your sword, making it longer and sharp enough to do twice as much damage!|N/A|SS}}</ref> Once {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} acquires it, the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword}} is transformed into the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Longsword|link}}.
