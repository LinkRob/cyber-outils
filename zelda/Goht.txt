{{Term/Store|Goht||Series, MM, MM3D}} is the [[Boss]] of [[Snowhead Temple]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 182}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Goht}} is also referred to as '''Ghot''' in the {{Guide|MM|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Versus |page= 51}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{MM}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It is a giant mechanical creature with resemblance to a goat or bull. It is responsible for keeping the second of the [[Four Giants]] prisoner, and cast a curse that is causing the abnormally long and cold winter at [[Snowhead]], which threatens the lives of the native [[Goron]]s living there.<ref>{{Cite|It's this cold snap brought on by the disturbances at Snowhead that has crippled the village and frozen it in an icy grip...|Goron Elder|MM}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= Hurry! Chase after it. Even if it means throwing your own body into it, do it so it'll stop!
}}

When [[Link]] reaches the boss room, he finds Goht frozen. He must use a [[Fire Arrow]] to melt the ice block. The monster will then thaw, come back to life while breaking out of the rocks surrounding it, and start charging around the course in an attempt to tackle Link, knocking him down.

To defeat Goht, Link must use the [[Goron Mask]] to chase the beast down by rolling after it.<ref>{{Cite|Hurry! Chase after it. Even if it means throwing your own body into it, do it so it'll stop!|Tatl|MM}}</ref> There are many [[Magic Jar]]s around the room to replenish Link's [[Magic Meter]], depleted by the rolling. Goht can be harmed by being rolled into with [[Goron Link]]'s spikes. To prevent this attack, Goht will launch a homing beam at the young hero. This attack streaks forward and then doubles back to strike Link. It is possible to dodge this attack by moving to the side quickly as it approaches. If Goron Link managed to hit Goht's back, it will be temporarily knocked down, leaving him open to attacks. Link can also attack Goht by using [[Bomb]]s, the [[Bow]], or [[Fierce Deity Link]]'s [[Sword Beam|Sword Beams]] if obtained. However, the timing required makes this method impractical. 

Goht will become gradually harder to follow as the battle goes on. It starts by kicking up rocks, which will damage Link and stop him from rolling. After taking more damage, stalactites will also begin to fall from the ceiling. These only damage Link if they fall directly on him, stopping him if he hits them while rolling. Near the end of the fight, Goht will begin dropping Bombs in addition to the rocks and stalactites. It is possible to approximate Goht's damage level by looking at how much black smoke is coming out of it.

If Link falls too far behind, Goht will stop and wait. When Link enters Goht's field of view, the boss will fire a lightning bolt at Link. This can be easily dodged by swerving side to side. Goht will continue the assault until Link reaches it, at which point it will resume its stampede. If Link falls behind for too long, the boss will charge toward him. If Goht reaches him, it will throw the young [[Hero of Time]] over itself with its head and continue onward, causing a [[Heart Container|Heart]] in damage. 

Once Goht is defeated, it will malfunction and careen around the room, then crashing into a wall while causing a large number of [[boulder]]s to fall on top of it, destroying the mechanical monster. Link will then receive a [[Heart Container]], as well as [[Goht's Remains]], releasing the second of the [[Four Giants]].<ref>{{Cite|You've seized Goht's Remains! You have just freed the innocent spirit that this dark mask had kept imprisoned within the body of evil Goht.|N/A|MM}}</ref> Afterward, the icy blizzard coming from the Snowhead Temple will be sealed off,<ref>{{Cite|I had heard that you, the one whom I once thought to be dead, had sealed off the blizzard that was blowing in from Snowhead.|Goron Elder|MM}}</ref> and the spring will return to Snowhead.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, spring has come to Goron Village.|Goron Elder|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM|publisher= Nintendo|page= 60}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, an eye was added to Goht's back, which acts as its weak point when stunned. The eye can be shot with [[Arrow]]s if Goht falls on its belly once Goron Link falls on top of it while rolling. The eye can also be attacked with the [[Sword]] or punches should Goht fall on its side by hitting its legs repeatedly. However, Goht can still be defeated purely by rolling into it while is still running.

==Nomenclature==
"Goht" is pronounced like "got".  This is a play on the word "goat", which Goht is similar in appearance to.

== Trivia ==
[[File:MM3D Majora's Evil leaving Boss' Corpse.jpg|thumb|251x251px|Majora's Mask's evil leaving Goht in ''Majora's Mask 3D'']]

* In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'', after Goht crashes into the wall upon its defeat, the soul of [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]] can be seen coming out of the boulders.
* Compared to the other three main bosses in {{MM}} ([[Odolwa]], [[Gyorg (Boss)|Gyrog]], and [[Twinmold]]), as Goht has high health and resorts to running away from [[Link]], fans often claim he takes the longest time to defeat. A known strategy to take him down quickly as often showcased by [[Speedrun|speedruns]] is to knock him down as [[Goron Link]], return to normal or as [[Fierce Deity Link]], and then repeatedly [[Jump Slash|Jump Attack]] using the [[Great Fairy's Sword|Great Fairy Sword]] or [[Fierce Deity Sword|Fierce Deity's Sword]]. It takes up to 4 hits and every powerful blow has black smoke appearing from its rear.
