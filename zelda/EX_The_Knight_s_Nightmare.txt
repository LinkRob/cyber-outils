{{Term/Store|EX The Knight's Nightmare||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 59 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX The Knight's Nightmare|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the first wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX The Knight's Nightmare}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must rescue {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} before he is defeated. At first, they must defeat five {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. The group of five consists of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Guardian|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}, with the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} located right next to {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} can only access the {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Guardian}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian}} at first, but when they are both defeated, the gates behind them will open, allowing both of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} to reach the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian}} is approached, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}} spawn very close to it, and are added to the group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} that must be defeated. When all three are defeated, a gate opens leading to {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} and the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}}. Until this point, the area is with {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}, but changes to {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} on the path to the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}}, along with the rest of the map. When both {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} are defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} spawns in the southern area of the map, in front of an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}}. There are two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} in the southern area of the map, and the gates open to both when all seven of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} from the previous objective are defeated. There is a stationary {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} in the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} farther north, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} in the lower {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}. When the gates open, the newly spawned {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} and the {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} from the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} farther south move toward the {{Plural|HWAoC|Link}}. After a certain amount of time, the exact same {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} reappear in the same location and move in the same direction if the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} they spawn in front of is not captured. After another certain amount of time, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}} spawns in front of the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} farther north and do the same. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} are also given the objective to capture both {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}}. When they are captured, the gate to the west of {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} opens, and from there the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} must escort him to a Targeted Position in the southwest corner of the map. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} cannot take a southern route to this position, as there is a gate that blocks them off. Two sets of {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} stand {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}'s way, both in front of closed gates. The first set contains two flying {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} and the second set contains a stationary {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian}}. When either set is defeated, the gate behind them opens. There are also a few small groups of {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Keese|link}} scattered around the path up until the first gate, a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}} behind the first set of {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}}, and two small groups {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Keese|link}} behind the second set. As {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} and the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} approach the Targeted Position, a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}} spawn. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when both are defeated.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Swift Carrot|link}}, twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Warm Safflina|link}}, twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Cool Safflina|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
