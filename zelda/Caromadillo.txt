'''Caromadillo''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Caromadillo|This monster lives in caves or on plains, recklessly charging everything it sees. It holds its tail to roll, bouncing off walls.<ref name="Caromadillo">{{Cite|This monster lives in caves or on plains, recklessly charging everything it sees. It holds its tail to roll, bouncing off walls.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Caromadillos are armadillo-like enemies that roll toward their targets, with a stronger [[Caromadillo Lv. 2|Lv. 2]] variant that boasts higher health and damage also exists. Zelda can learn the Caromadillo [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], and summoning one costs two Echo energy.
