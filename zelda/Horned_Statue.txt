The {{Term/Store|Horned Statue||Series, BotW}}, also known as a '''Demon Statue'''<ref>''"A demon... STATUE was in there, you say?"  —'' [[Jerrin]] ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref> and formerly the '''Horned God''',<ref name=":0" /> is a character in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Gray|Horned Statue}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> and [[Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']].<ref name=":1">''"Then that must mean the horned statue is an entity who deals in life and power"'' — [[Jerrin]] ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref>

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} is found along the path to {{Term|BotW|Firly Pond|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Teebo|link}} to be guided to the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}}'s exact location,<ref>{{Cite|'Kay! Follow me!|Teebo|BotW}}</ref> though {{Term|BotW|Teebo}} has no knowledge of what the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue|display=Statue}} is meant for.<ref>{{Cite|It looks kinda like the {{Color|BotW Blue|statue}} at the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}}'s house... What do you think it is?|Teebo|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} prays to the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}}, he will note that there are still people who pay him homage.<ref>{{Cite|Ahhh... So there are some who still pay me homage.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reacts with shock, the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} is surprised that he is able to be heard.<ref>{{Cite|[You can talk?!] You don't say... You can hear my voice?|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} denies hearing his voice, the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} says he makes bargains with those who can hear him.<ref>{{Cite|[No.] No, eh? Fa ha ha... If you say so. Just know that I'm willing to strike a bargain with anyone who can hear my voice.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he was praying for a long life or wealth, promising that he can grant these requests.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] Now, that is a surprise. What were you praying for? Long life? Wealth, like so many of your kind? Whichever it was, you came to the right place.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} explains that he once made life-for-money bargains. {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}}, disapproved of his heinous dealings and smote him by trapping him inside the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}}.<ref>{{Cite|A dealer in life and power... That was me, once. Alas, my life-for-money bargains didn't sit well with the {{Color|BotW Blue|Goddess Hylia}}. She trapped me in this stone statue long, long ago.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> Since then, he has been neglected by the residents of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}.<ref name=":2">{{Cite|The villagers here call me the Horned Statue and don't even bother to wipe away the bird droppings.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, the spirit of the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} remained headstrong and chose to bide his time waiting for another person to make a deal with him.<ref>{{Cite|One would think years upon years of such treatment would have made me mend my ways... One would think wrongly. I have only been biding my time for the moment someone who can hear me appears. Someone such as you. But I've said enough.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to strike a bargain.<ref>{{Cite|Now then, shall we strike a bargain? Then stay that way. No further action on your part is required.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> Without waiting for an answer, he steals either a {{Term|BotW|Heart Container|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Stamina Vessel|link}} from him.<ref>{{Cite|Fa ha ha... My touch is as subtle as ever... Note how I took that {{Color|BotW Red|essence}} from you without leaving a mark.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> This initiates "{{Term|BotW|The Statue's Bargain|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, tasking Link with the responsibility of recovering his stolen essence.<ref name="Quest" />

In speaking to the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} once again, he at first pretends not to understand why {{Term|BotW|Link}} is upset.<ref>{{Cite|What's wrong, then? Have your prayers gone unanswered?|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} demands his stolen essence back, the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} laughs at {{Term|BotW|Link}} for believing he was deceived.<ref>{{Cite|[Give it back.] Fa ha ha... Did you think I'd deceived you?|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> He hopes that they will have a long and profitable relationship, and he explains that for a price, he will give Link either a {{Term|BotW|Heart Container}} or a {{Term|BotW|Stamina Vessel}}.<ref>{{Cite|Perish the thought. I hope that ours will be a long and profitable relationship. So which will it be? As I may have mentioned once or twice, I'm a dealing in life and power. I can provide a valuable service to you. I take {{Color|BotW Red|Heart Containers}} or {{Color|BotW Red|Stamina Vessels}} from you...and then, for a price, return whichever of the two you like.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses which one to receive, the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} tells him to stay still as he returns his essence to him.<ref>{{Cite|A wise choice. Now, remain still.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref>

Afterwards, the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} notices that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is sceptical, and he explains that he sees money and life as the same thing.<ref>{{Cite|You look skeptical. Not to worry. Money and life are all as one to me.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> He explains that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can sell either of his essences to him for 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} and buy it back for 120 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're ready to begin, then first you must sell me either type of {{Color|BotW Red|essence}} for {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}}. If you want to buy an essence back, the price rises to {{Color|BotW Red|120 rupees}}.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> However, he allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to choose whether he receives a {{Term|BotW|Heart Container}} or a {{Term|BotW|Stamina Vessel}}.<ref>{{Cite|Before you cry foul, know that you may choose which one I return to you: a {{Color|BotW Red|Heart Container}} or a {{Color|BotW Red|Stamina Vessel}}.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can begin this process by praying to the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} again.<ref>{{Cite|I'm eager to help tamper with your essence anytime you want to return and pray here.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}}, he asks if he has come to tamper with his essences.<ref>{{Cite|You... Come to tamper with your {{Color|BotW Blue|essences}}, yes?|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> If the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} is not currently holding any essences, he will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is willing to sell them.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes, please.] I'm currently without any of your {{Color|BotW Blue|essences}}. Are you in the mood to sell?|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} will only take the essence that {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks him to.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] Which type of {{Color|BotW Blue|essence}} will you surrender to me?|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> Through this process, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to transfer the upgrades that he has received from {{Plural|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} from Heart Containers to Stamina Vessels, or vice versa.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to sell his {{Plural|BotW|Heart Container}} or {{Plural|BotW|Stamina Vessel}} beyond their default limitations (3 Hearts and 1 full Stamina Wheel), the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} will reject his request.<ref>{{Cite|I don't see how you can reduce your {{Color|BotW Blue|Stamina Vessels}} any further.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I don't see how you can reduce your {{Color|BotW Blue|Heart Containers}} any further.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> Additionally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to speak to the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} before he has received any blessings from {{Term|BotW|Hylia}}, he will be turned away and instructed to return with something to trade.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? You haven't received even one more {{Color|BotW Red|Heart Container}} or {{Color|BotW Red|Stamina Vessel}}? Such a pity... I was eager to help you with a minor {{Color|BotW Red|transaction}}. But not if you have nothing to trade. Consult a {{Color|BotW Red|Goddess Statue}} to improve yourself, and then return here to pray.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref>

===''Tears of the Kingdom''===
After the Calamity, the statue mentions once again how he was turned into a statue long, long ago.<ref>''"She turned me into this statue long, long ago."  —'' the horned statue ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref> He then recounts how he continued his bargains in a village (presumably [[Hateno Village|Hateno village]]).<ref>''"Even then, I continued my bargains as a statue in a village."  —'' the horned statue ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref> He was then moved from the main street to the outskirts of town, scorned and neglected.<ref>''"I was banished from the main street to the outskirts of town, scorned and neglected."  —'' the horned statue ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref> This is corroborated by an earlier quote in Breath of the Wild, in which he says the villagers don't bother to clean bird droppings from him.<ref name=":2" /> Due to the Goddess' displeasure he was relocated to a pond and then ultimately here.<ref>''"Again, I was sent away. I spent time trapped in a pond, with only fish for company. And finally... here."  —'' the horned statue ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref> He currently sits in the Royal Hidden Passage.

[[Jerrin]] breaks open the entrace to the Royal Hidden Passage in the Emergency Shelter at [[Lookout Landing]], and by speaking to her, this starts the ''Who Goes There?'' quest. She claims to hear a demon from behind the opening. By investigating the opening, the Horned Statue is found off to the side of the main passage, in a room blocked by cracked rock, which can be broken with a weapon or [[Key Items in Tears of the Kingdom|Yunobo's Vow]]. It does not recognize Link, but offers the same service as in {{BotW|-}}. After telling Jerrin, she claims to know a bit about demon statues and quotes ''"A dealer in life and power touched by the wrath of the Goddess, fallen to ruin."'' from an unknown source. Jerrin argues that the dais was the place it was enshrined back when the horned statue was still a [[Deity|god]].<ref>''"Which would make the dais the place where it was enshrined, back when it was still a god."  —'' [[Jerrin]] ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref> She goes on to claim that the horned statue is the opposite of the goddess of light<ref name=":0" /> (Goddess Hylia), that both entities cannot exist without the other and that their power manifests through the other's existence<ref>''"Like light and dark, one cannot exist without the other—their power manifests through the other's existence."  —'' [[Jerrin]] ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref>.
