'''Dolomites''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Dolomites are humanoids made of rocks, only ever seen by the upper half of their body. Dolomites attack by throwing rocks in a single direction. They are one of the more common enemies, as [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] encounters them in the [[Forest of Torian]], the [[Great Dry Canyon]], the [[South Gubasha Desert]], and the [[Seacoast Plain]]. Dolomites take a few hits from the [[Wand]] to defeat, even as Zelda obtains more [[Celestial Sign]]s. If Zelda either touches a Dolomite or is hit by their rock, she takes a [[Heart]] of damage.
