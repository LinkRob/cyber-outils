{{Term/Store|Elma||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Elma}}
Elma is a [[Korok]] who resides in the [[Forest Haven]]. Before the Forest Ceremony, Elma can be seen standing on the cliff outside of Forest Haven on the other side of the door that Rown is standing in front of, on the path to the Forbidden Forest. After the Forest Ceremony, Elma goes to [[Needle Rock Isle]] to plant new [[Tree]]s.
