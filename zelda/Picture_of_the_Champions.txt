The {{Term/Store|Picture of the Champions||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Key Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Picture of the Champions}}<br/>A picture of Zelda and the five Champions, taken after the inauguration ceremony at Hyrule Castle. Kass gifted you this precious item after completing The Champions' Ballad.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Picture of the Champions}} is a {{Term|BotW|Key Item}} in the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. It is given to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} after the latter sings "{{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad (Song)|link}}".<ref name="Inventory"/>

The {{Term|BotW|Picture of the Champions}} was taken with the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} after the inauguration ceremony for the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}. After hearing of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}'s ability to capture realistic photographs from {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} suggests that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} and the five {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} have their picture taken to commemorate the event.<ref>{{Cite|The princess showed me something strange recently. Somehow, it can create true-to-life images.|Urbosa|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Oh, wow. I would love to see it. Um, Princess... May I ask a special favor of you?|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> After some preparation, {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} snaps a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of the group.<ref>{{Cite|All right! This spot should work nicely. Keep your eyes on the Sheikah Slate, everyone! Daruk, can you crouch down a bit? You're as big as Death Mountain. What's with the glum face, Princess? Gimme a big smile! Revali, move your tail closer to the group.|Purah|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Ugh, fine.|Revali|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Mipha, you look so tense. Deep breaths, OK?|Purah|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Right!|Mipha|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Stay just like that! Here we go! Smiiiile... Click, snap! ♪|Purah|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} forcefully pulls the group together at the last moment, immortalizing a comical scene.

At some point, the {{Term|BotW|Picture of the Champions}} was printed and fell into the hands of {{Term|BotW|Kass}}'s teacher, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet|link}}, who kept it safe. {{Term|BotW|Kass}} later discovered it while attempting to finish "{{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad (Song)}}".<ref>{{Cite|By the way, I found this when I was looking through my teacher's notes. I was thinking...you should be the one to keep it. Your courageous heart reminds me of the indomitable spirit of the Champions. I know you'll treasure it.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He gifts it to {{Term|BotW|Link}} atop the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} after performing his late teacher's completed song with the understanding that {{Term|BotW|Link}} would treasure it most.

If the "{{Term|BotW|Hylian Homeowner|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can hang the {{Term|BotW|Picture of the Champions}} on the wall next to his {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}} at his {{Term|BotW|Link's House|display=House}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}.

The picture returns in the same location in ''Tears of The Kingdom'' if a save file from ''Breath of the Wild'' with the picture exists on the same Nintendo account.
