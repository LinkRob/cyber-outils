'''Trophies''' are collectible items in {{SSB4}}. There are 707 Trophies (685 before DLC) in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] version, 58 of which are {{TLoZ|Series|-}}-related, and 743 Trophies (716 before DLC) in the [[Wii U]] version, 49 of which are {{TLoZ|-}}-related.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' alone features 685 trophies in the vanilla game and 707 trophies with DLC, while ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'' features 716 trophies in the vanilla game and 743 trophies with DLC.|url= https://www.ssbwiki.com/Trophy#In_Super_Smash_Bros._4|title= Trophy| site=SmashWiki}}</ref>
