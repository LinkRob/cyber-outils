==Appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Main|Dwarven Swordsmith}}
In {{ALttP}}, the missing {{Term|ALttP|Dwarven Swordsmith|link}} can be found in the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} transformed in a {{Term|ALttP|Frog}}-like creature due to the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World}}'s magic.

==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, there is a {{Term|LANS|Frog}} choir lead by {{Term|LANS|Mamu|link}} that teaches {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} the "{{Term|LANS|Frog's Song of Soul|link}}" after paying 300 {{Plural|LANS|Rupee|link}}. There is also a {{Term|LANS|Frog}} troupe inside {{Term|LANS|Richard's Villa|link}} as a reference to the game {{Term|LANS|Prince Richard|link}} originated from, {{Wp|Kaeru no Tame ni Kane wa Naru|The Frog For Whom the Bell Tolls}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Main|Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors}}
In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors}} is a group of singing {{Plural|OoT3D|Frog}} found in the {{Term|OoT3D|Zora's River|link}}. They reward {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} with {{Plural|OoT3D|Rupee|link}} and {{Plural|OoT3D|Piece of Heart|link}} for playing the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina|link}} for them. During the [[Trading Quest]], Link receives the [[Eyeball Frog]] from [[King Zora]] after giving him the Prescription. This time-sensitive item must be delivered to the Lakeside Laboratory within three minutes, or it will spoil. If successfully delivered, the Eyeball Frog is transformed into the World's Finest Eye Drops.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Frog Choir}}
In {{MM}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Frog Choir}} is a group of singing {{Plural|MM3D|Frog}} found all around {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}. {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} must gather all five of them in the {{Term|MM3D|Mountain Village|link}} in order to conduct them using {{Term|MM3D|Don Gero's Mask|link}}. They will then reward him with a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}}.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Cyclos, Zephos}}
In {{TWW}}, two {{Term|TWWHD|Frog}}-like deities named {{Term|TWWHD|Cyclos|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Zephos|link}} teach {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} the "{{Term|TWWHD|Wind's Requiem|link}}" and the "{{Term|TWWHD|Ballad of Gales|link}}". These songs let {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} control the direction of the wind and {{Plural|TWWHD|Cyclone|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, three frogs can be found by the stream near [[Rusl]] and [[Uli]]’s house in [[Ordon Village]]. [[Wolf Link]] can speak to the frogs, and the first one tells him about sparkly patches on the ground. The second frog tells him to dig around for buried treasure or clues. The third frog just ribbits.<ref name=":0">''[https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Prima_Games_Twilight_Princess_Premiere_Edition_Strategy_Guide_Wii_Version Strategy Guide: Wii Version, Premier Edition]'' (Prima Games) pg. 60, 246</ref> Wolf Link encounters another frog while carrying the wounded [[Midna]] to [[Princess Zelda]]. This frog directs Link to [[Telma's Bar]], through which he can enter the [[Castle Town Sewers|Castle Waterways]].<ref name=":0" />

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Main|Golden Frog}}
In {{PH}}, six {{Plural|PH|Golden Frog|link}} appear in several locations throughout the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}}. If {{Term|PH|Link|link}} hit them with a shot from the {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}}, they will show him a {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate Symbol|link}} that can be drawn onto the {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate|link}} to {{Term|PH|Warping|link|display= Warp}} via {{Term|PH|Cyclone|link}} to the location the symbol was learned.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, the {{Term|SS|Fire Sanctuary|link}} and the path leading up to it have ornate carvings of open-mouthed {{Plural|SS|Frog}} positioned over fire barriers. Their mouths must be filled with water to turn off the flames. Outside the {{Term|SS|Fire Sanctuary|link}}, the two small {{Plural|SS|Frog}}' thirst can be quenched with a {{Term|SS|Bottle|link}} filled with water from a nearby pool, while the large frog requires a whole cauldron of water transported from {{Term|SS|Lake Floria|link}}. Inside the {{Term|SS|Fire Sanctuary|link}}, the water is derived from {{Term|SS|Water Fruit|link}}.
