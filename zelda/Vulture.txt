==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Stub|ALttP}}
In {{ALttP}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Vulture}} are usually found resting atop cactus found in the {{Term|ALttP|Desert of Mystery|link}}, or high ledges. Once they spot {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}}, they will take off and circle him. They will slowly close in on {{Term|ALttP|Link}} while following him, and will eventually move close enough to attack directly.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Stub|FSA}}
In {{FSA}}, {{Plural|FSA|Vulture}} are more aggressive, actively diving at the four {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} after circling them for a short while.
