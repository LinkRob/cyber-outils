The {{Term/Store|Woodland Tower||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Woodland Tower'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Woodland Tower is a [[Sheikah Tower]] in the [[Great Hyrule Forest]]. The Tower sits in the middle of the [[Military Training Camp]] amid broken wooden towers and swamps. To get to the top of the Woodland Tower, Link must fight to the top of a smaller tower that is nearby and glide from that tower to the Woodland Tower and then grab onto the walls of the Woodland Tower and climb up to the top. Once [[Link]] reaches the top of the Woodland Tower, he can activate the Tower,<ref>{{Cite|Sheikah Tower activated.|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> filling in the [[Sheikah Slate]]'s [[Sheikah Slate#Map|Map]] of the area surrounding the Great Hyrule Forest, the {{Term|BotW|Woodland Tower Region|link}}.

Unique to Woodland Tower, there is a large stone skull on top of the Tower. A [[Royal Claymore]] can be found embedded in its top.
