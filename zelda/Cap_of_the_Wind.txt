The {{Term/Store|Cap of the Wind||Series, BotW}} is an item in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Cap of the Wind}}<br/>According to legend, this cap was treasured by a hero who traveled the Great Sea. It flaps quite serenely in a soft sea breeze.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The '''Cap of the Wind''' is a piece of [[Armor]] which can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the Super Smash Bros. Toon Link Amiibo '''or''' Legend of Zelda 30th Anniversary Toon Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Cap of the Wind. It can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Cap of the Wind, alongside the [[Tunic of the Wind]] and [[Trousers of the Wind]], comprise the [[Hero of the Wind Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from [[Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
