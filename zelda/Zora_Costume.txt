The {{Term/Store|Zora Costume||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 143}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Zora Costume lets you swim much faster. They say cold water halts aging. Perhaps I should... oh, nothing.}}

The {{Term|TFH|Zora Costume}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple|link}}, at a cost of one {{Term|TFH|Fresh Kelp|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Zora Scale|link}} and 400 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} resembles the traditional [[River Zora|enemy Zoras]] previously seen in {{ALBW}}, as opposed to the friendly race of [[Zora]]s.

The {{Term|TFH|Zora Costume}} improves {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s swimming abilities by allowing him to swim faster and with less inertia. In addition, he will be unaffected by currents, and can deal damage to [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]] when pressing the A button to Boost.<ref>{{Cite|Swim like a champ!<br/>♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦<br/>Brave the currents. Swim faster with {{Icon|TFH A}}. Rock that hip Zora look and be one with the water.|Catalog|TFH}}</ref> Boosting with the {{Term|TFH|Zora Costume}} also allows {{Term|TFH|Link}} to destroy {{Plural|TFH|Spike|link}} without being harmed.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|TFH|Zora Costume}}'s description on the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} selection screen erroneously states that it allows {{Term|TFH|Link}} to {{Term|TFH|Spin Attack|link}} in water.<ref>{{Cite|Swim like a pro, even in swift currents. Also lets you Spin Attack in water!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> This may be referring to the corkscrew motion {{Term|TFH|Link}} usually makes when pressing the A button to Boost, but this would be wrong nonetheless, since wearing the {{Term|TFH|Zora Costume}} changes {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s Boost motion to a slight forward arc.
