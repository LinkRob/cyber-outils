'''Doppelganger''' is the seventh episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. It first aired in the United States and Canada on October 20, 1989.

==Plot==
:''See also: [[Doppelganger/Episode transcript|Episode transcript]]''
[[File:Zelda Clone (Doppelganger).png|thumb|left|Zelda's evil clone appearing on the Mirror]]
A {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin|link}} tries to steal the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce of Wisdom|link}}, but {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} easily defeats it with his {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Crissword|display=sword|link}} and sends it back to the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Evil Jar|link}}, with it dropping a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Force Field Ring|link}} that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} picks up.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Huh, left a souvenir. A force field ring! Just what I always wanted for Christmas.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}}, tired of always failing, decides to use a new magic spell which plants a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Mirror (Object)|link}} in {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}}'s room.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=That dreaded Link is seriously starting to annoy me.  But he does have a weakness. He'll do anything for Zelda! I do believe it's time to try this special bit of magic I've been saving. Off to the chambers of Princess Zelda you go! And maybe a bad reflection on her...|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> Later on, when Zelda is brushing her hair in front of the Mirror, nightlight strikes it and copies her reflection in an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zelda's Clone|display=evil clone of her|link}}. The clone orders the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} that accompany her to tie her up and take her into the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}} via the Mirror.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Get her out of here.|name=Zelda Clone|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}, hearing Zelda's screams, rushes up to her room and defeats several of the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}}, but one of the foes accidentally throws a vase at the Mirror, breaking it and impeding Zelda's clone from taking the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce of Wisdom}} to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} via the Mirror.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Drat. I'll need to find another way to get the Triforce back to Ganon. And I think I know just the way...|name=Zelda Clone|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> After {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} zaps all of the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}}, the clone kisses him unexpectedly and tells the young hero that they, along with the Triforce, are going to attack {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hm, good. Because you, me and the Triforce are going to go uh...um...attack Ganon. Right now!|name=Zelda Clone|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> However, as the duo are making their way to an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}} entrance, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} realizes the truth about the evil clone as she has no reflection but decides to follow her anyway to rescue the real Zelda.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=She doesn't have a reflection... I knew that smooch was too good to be true. That's NOT Zelda...! So, you're not really Zelda, eh? That means she's been captured, and this is all a trick. [...] Sure, I'll follow you...right to Zelda! Actually, this could be fun!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref>

In the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}}, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} leaves Zelda unattended as he summons monsters to welcome {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}},<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I must choose some of my pets to welcome him.|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> and the princess uses this time to break herself free from the ropes tying her by using a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Boomerang|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=We'll see about that. Good thing I kept this.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> Meanwhile, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} is now also in the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}} following Zelda's clone when suddenly {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} and {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos|link}} attack them. The young hero easily disposes of them, and when the clone rewards {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} with a kiss, the real Zelda appears. The evil clone takes the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce of Wisdom}} and runs to deliver it to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}, with the princess and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} in pursuit.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, boy, here we go. Time for me to deliver this to Ganon.|name=Zelda Clone|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> Eventually, they catch up to the clone, but not before defeating three {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Octorok|link}} while using the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Force Field Ring}} that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} found earlier.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Uh-oh, Octoroks! Time for a force field. Hang on, princess!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> During the struggle, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda}} and her clone fall into muddy water, which makes both their clothes the same color and leaving {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} unable to tell who is the real Zelda. The young hero decides to determine who the real one is by a "kissing content,"<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I'm afraid there's only one way to tell you two apart. A kissing contest!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> and when one Zelda slaps him and refuses to kiss him, he knows that she is the real one because the evil clone had willingly kissed him several times before.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ow! That's my Zelda alright.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> Quickly, Zelda picks up {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}'s sword and zaps her clone,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Here's your sword. Now let's get out of here before--|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> but before they can escape, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} appears.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Before I show up? Prepare to meet your doom you pitiful creatures!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} fires a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam|link}} towards {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} but it hits the ceiling, causing rocks to collapse on top of the evil wizard and falling into the mud. Taking this opportunity, the duo exit to the surface using the Triforce's power.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=There's our exit. Quick, grab the Triforce!|name=Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=7}}</ref>
