The {{Term/Store|Daqa Koh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Daqa Koh Shrine}}<br/>Stalled Flight}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Stalled Flight''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Daqa Koh Shrine}} is located off the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}}, shortly before the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Eldin|link}}. It is hidden between a pair of rockfaces. As Link enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Daqa Koh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Daqa Koh. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Daqa Koh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Daqa Koh Shrine}} features a massive stone block in the center of the room, upon which is an electric generator. This stone block is thrown into the air at stalled intervals by a series of glowing blocks beneath it that push it upward. On the ceiling is an electric connector that, when connected with the generator, will open the bars to a raised cell where the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} awaits. However, the connection will be shortly cut as the stone block falls.

To reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must climb atop the stone block and use {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} on it just when the generator connects, thus keeping the cell open. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can then glide into the cell using the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}. When approached, {{Term|BotW|Daqa Koh}} will commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for overcoming the Trial and give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Daqa Koh|BotW}}</ref>Afterwards, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fade away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Daqa Koh|BotW}}</ref>

A single {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} rests on a suspended platform in the corner of the room, which can be be accessed by gliding from the raised stone block. It contains a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.
