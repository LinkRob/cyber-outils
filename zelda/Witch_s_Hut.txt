==Features and Overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
The {{Term|ALttP|Magic Shop}} is located in the northeast region of the [[Light World]] in {{ALttP}}. It is the home of an unnamed witch who brews [[Potion]]s outside, and a shopkeeper who maintains the shop inside. They brew and sell Magic Potions in three varieties: [[Red Potion]], [[Green Potion]] and [[Blue Potion]]. If [[Link]] brings the witch a [[Mushroom]] and waits for a while, she will produce [[Magic Powder]] and leave it for him in the shop. The shopkeeper will offer a Link a free sample of the Red Potion, refilling his hearts on the spot.

In {{ALttP&FS}}, the generic shopkeeper inside was replaced by [[Maple]].

==={{LA|-}}===
The {{Term|LADX|Witch's Hut}} also appears east of the [[Mysterious Woods]] on [[Koholint Island]] in {{LA}}. Provided Link brought the witch a [[Mushroom|Sleepy Toadstool]], she gives him [[Magic Powder]].

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, {{Term|OoS|Syrup's Potion Shop}} is located in the middle of the [[Sunken City]] and can only be accessed in winter, when the water has frozen. Syrup do not sell anything here until Link trades her a [[Mushroom]] for the [[Wooden Bird]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, {{Term|OoS|Syrup's Potion Shop}} is located in the north-eastern corner of the [[Yoll Graveyard]], in the present. Here, Syrup sells [[Magic Potion]]s, [[Gasha Seed]]s, and one [[Piece of Heart]]. During his quest, Link will have to buy a Magic Potion here to cure the [[King Zora]]'s illness.

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, {{Term|TMC|Witch's Hut}} is located in the northeastern section of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Link}} must buy a {{Term|TMC|Wake-Up Mushroom|link}} from the hut for 60 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} and use it to wake up {{Term|TMC|Rem|link}} in order to receive the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Syrup}} also sells {{Term|TMC|Blue Potion|link}} for the same amount of {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
The {{Term|ALBW|Witch's House}} is the home of the [[Witch]] and her granddaughter [[Irene]]. Inside, Link can either buy Potions or sell [[Treasure#A Link Between Worlds|spoils]]. In exchange for spoils and Rupees, the Witch can brew [[Blue Potion|Blue]], [[Yellow Potion|Yellow]] and [[Purple Potion]]s. The [[Red Potion]] can be bought for only 50 Rupees. By talking to the Witch, Link can receive a free sample of the Red Potion, instantly recovering 8 [[Heart]]s.
