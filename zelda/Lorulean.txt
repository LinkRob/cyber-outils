{{Term/Store|Lorulean|Loruleans|Series, ALBW}} is the demonym applied to all people that live in {{Term|Series|Lorule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 299}}</ref>

==Overview==
Many Loruleans appear as enemies. There are several [[Lorule Soldier]]s that guard the entrance to the [[Dark Ruins]] as sentries. Many soldiers are fought directly in the final dungeon, [[Lorule Castle]]. [[Lorule Ball and Chain Soldier]]s serve as mini-bosses throughout the dungeon that must be defeated to progress.

There are also civilians who appear in the ruined residences of Lorule. There are a group of Loruleans wearing monster masks known as the [[Masked Follower]]s which include the [[Bird-Masked Man]], [[Masked Elder]], [[Derby Girl]], and a few others.
