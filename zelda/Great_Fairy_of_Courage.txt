==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Great Fairy of Courage}} is one of the six {{Plural|OoT3D|Great Fairy|link}} and grants the power of {{Term|OoT3D|Defensive Power|link}}. She resides in a {{Term|OoT3D|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} near {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon's Castle|link}} while {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} is an adult. 

==={{MM|-}}===
The {{Term|MM3D|Great Fairy of Courage}} is one of the five {{Plural|MM3D|Great Fairy|link}} in {{MM}}. She was split into 15 violet-colored {{Plural|MM3D|Stray Fairy|link}} by the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}} and scattered around the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Young one! Please hear my plea! I have been broken and shattered to pieces by the masked Skull Kid.|Great Fairy|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} can restore the {{Term|MM3D|Great Fairy of Courage}} by returning the {{Plural|MM3D|Stray Fairy}} to the {{Term|MM3D|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} on the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Coast|link}}. As thanks for this, she grants the power of {{Term|MM3D|Defensive Power|link}}.
