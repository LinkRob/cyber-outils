The {{Term/Store|True Goddess Blade||Series}} is an item in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}}

==Location and Uses==
The True Goddess Blade serves as a weapon for [[Fi]], serving as her Tier 3 weapon. 

Introduced in {{HW|L}}, there is an enhanced version of the True Goddess Blade, called the '''True Goddess Blade+''', which serves as her Tier 4 weapon, though it doesn’t have any distinct differences from the Tier 3 version beyond its base power.

Additionally, there is a Tier 4+ version of the True Goddess Blade called the '''Liquid Goddess Blade''', a recolored version of the True Goddess Blade that’s infused with the Water element, in addition to its innate Light element.
