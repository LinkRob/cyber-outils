{{Term/Store|Fyson||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fyson|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Fyson}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} who is initially found in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to him, he asks if he's noticed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, what's up? So, have you seen it? {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}?|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> Even though everyone in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} is concerned about it, {{Term|BotW|Fyson}} believes he has bigger problems.<ref>{{Cite|Everyone in the village is freaking out because of it, but I have bigger problems...|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> He helps his mother, {{Term|BotW|Misa|link}}, with her shop, {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}}, which she wants him to take over some day.<ref name="Mom"/><ref>{{Cite|My mom said she wants me to take over the family store someday...<br/>Can you believe that?!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> However, this is not what he wants, as Fyson's dream is to open his own {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I don't...want to just "help." I want to open my OWN store and sell whatever I want!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> He does not want to compete with his mother's shop, so he is found searching for a new place to open it.<ref>{{Cite|But I don't want to compete with Mom. I wish there was a place I could set up my own shop...|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> Even after {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} has been calmed, he is still plagued by his sorrows.<ref>{{Cite|I'm glad {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}} has settled down, but I have bigger problems.|Fyson|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, after asking for 30 pieces of {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}} to build more homes, {{Term|BotW|Hudson|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find someone who would like to open a {{Term|BotW|General Store}} in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's finally starting to look like a town around here. Which is exactly why we need to set up some distribution. I'd like to get a general store up and running, but there's no one with the connections and qualifications to run it. Do you think you could keep an eye out for someone who knows about {{Color|BotW Red|distribution}} and {{Color|BotW Red|can run a general store}}?|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks if he should look for a traveling trader, {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} suggests looking for a {{Term|BotW|Rito}}, as their flight could help with transporting supplies.<ref>{{Cite|[So a traveling trader?] Oh, that's a good thought.... {{Color|BotW Red|Rito}} can fly around, so they should be able to retrieve and deliver supplies.|Hudson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can head to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} to find {{Term|BotW|Fyson}}, who is intrigued by the prospect of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Heard of Tarrey Town?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}}? Tell me more.|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> While he is excited to hear they need a {{Term|BotW|General Store}}, he is worried about the distance {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} is from {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! So they're in need of a general store? That's perfect! But the {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala region}}? That's pretty far out there.|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> However, he reasons that he needs to leave the nest and this is the only way to open his own {{Term|BotW|General Store|display=Store}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|But I guess I've got to leave the nest someday. And the have my own store...}}}}|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> In the end, {{Term|BotW|Fyson}} decides to head to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}, planning to gather {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} on the way that he will sell there.<ref>{{Cite|OK.... I made up my mind! I'm going! I know! I'll gather some items on the way to {{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}} and then sell them at my new shop...|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> He thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for helping him and says to come visit him.<ref>{{Cite|Thank for the tip! And hey, don't be a stranger. Come visit me at my new home sometime!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref>

On {{Term|BotW|Fyson}}'s journey, a group of bandits attempted to mug him, but he was able to fly away.<ref>{{Cite|After I left Rito Village, some bandits tried to mug me. Lucky for me, they didn't have wings!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> Once he reached {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}, he established the {{Term|BotW|Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)|link}}. He finds the {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|display=Town}} to be great, appreciating the {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}} and that his mother can no longer annoy him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}} is really great!<br/>Nice weather. No moms to annoy me...|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> He is glad to be able to run his {{Term|BotW|General Store}} and sell what he wants to.<ref>{{Cite|It's really great being able to run my own shop and sell the things I want to sell!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to shop as much as he wants.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, I'm open for business, so shop to your heart's content!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Fyson}} can be seen in the crowd gathered for {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} and {{Term|BotW|Rhondson|link}}'s wedding. Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Fyson}} is happy that all went well.<ref>{{Cite|So glad the wedding went well!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> He comments on {{Term|BotW|Rhondson}}'s beauty and hopes to find a wife as pretty as her one day.<ref>{{Cite|Rhondson looked stunning... I hope to find a bride as pretty as her someday... Hehehe!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> Due to his responsibility, he decides to go back to manning his {{Term|BotW|General Store}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I should get back to the shop. Responsibility and all that.|Fyson|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Fyson}} asks how he is and claims to be doing great.<ref>{{Cite|How's it going? I'm great as always!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} is an adventurer, {{Term|BotW|Fyson}} realizes that he need to carry a lot of supplies with him.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, so... You're an adventurer, right? That probably means you need to carry a lot of supplies with you at all times.|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> He encourages him to buy anything he needs at the {{Term|BotW|Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if there's anything you need, come get it at my shop!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} stands on {{Term|BotW|Fyson}}'s table, he asks him not to do so.<ref>{{Cite|Please, get down from there...|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Can I ask you not to stand on the shelves, please? Those are expensive.|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is a place of business, not a gym. Please get down.|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I don't care if you are a paying customer, I can't let you climb up there.|Fyson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Fyson}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} whenever he makes a purchase.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for your business!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys the entirety of one product, {{Term|BotW|Fyson}} claims he will restock soon.<ref>{{Cite|I'll make sure to stock up, then!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, I guess we're out... I'll find some more.|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> He will also purchase {{Plural|BotW|Item}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|OK.<br/>What do you want to sell me?|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Show me what you got!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What do you want to offload?|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You're selling? I'm buying.|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm in the mood to buy!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to keep selling to him, continuously asking if he has anything else.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks! Wanna sell anything else?|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks! Anything else to sell?|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank ya! Want me to buy anything else?|Fyson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Alrighty! Do you have anything else for sale?|Fyson|BotW}}</ref>
