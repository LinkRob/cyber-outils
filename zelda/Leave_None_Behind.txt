{{Term/Store|Leave None Behind||HWAoC}} is a level 12 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Leave None Behind|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Leave None Behind}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Voltfruit|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Thigh|link}}, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Desert Voe Headband|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is aid/rescue three captains. One is a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}}, and is being attacked by a {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}. He can be saved by defeating the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}}. Another is located inside an enclosed area, which can be opened by defeating the {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}} that stand outside it. The {{Term|HWAoC|Rito Captain|link}} inside is being attacked by {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}}, and is saved when the {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin}} is defeated. The third captain is a {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain|link}} found in the eastern-most section of the map and is fighting two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}}. She can be saved by defeating the {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin}}. When all three allies are saved, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.
