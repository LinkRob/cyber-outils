The {{Term/Store|Goddess Longsword|Goddess Longswords|Series, SS}} is a recurring item in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWDE}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
{{Stub|SSHD}}
The {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Longsword}} is the enhanced form of the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword|link}}. The {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword}} is enhanced after {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} finds {{Term|SSHD|Farore's Flame|link}} in the {{Term|SSHD|Ancient Cistern|link}}, doubling its attack power and increasing its reach.<ref>{{Cite|The flames of Farore have improved your sword, making it longer and sharp enough to do twice as much damage!|N/A|SS}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}

It is unlocked as the A Rank Victory reward from a map in {{Term|HW|Adventure Mode|link}} as a {{Term|HW|Sealed Weapon|link}}, in square {{Exp|F being the 6th row descending, 5 being in the fifth column heading right|F-5}}.
