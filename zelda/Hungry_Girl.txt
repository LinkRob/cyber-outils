The {{Name|Fan|{{Term/Store|Hungry Girl||Series}}}} is a character in {{TWoG}}.{{Name Ref|TWoG}} She is a young woman living in [[Kobitan]], [[Gamelon]].

==Biography==
The Hungry Girl lives in a locked house in Kobitan. She is the town's lone inhabitant, presumably because the [[Moblin]]s have chased out the rest of the population. She complains that the Moblins have eaten everything in town, and desires to have an [[Arpagos Egg]].<ref>{{Cite|Those darned Moblin have eaten everything. I'd give anything for an Arpagos Egg.|Hungry Girl|TWoG}}</ref> [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] brings her one from the tower behind [[Mayor Cravendish]]'s house in [[Sakado]], which the Hungry Girl cracks over a frying pan, stating that it would make a great omelette. She gives Zelda the [[Flute]] in return.
