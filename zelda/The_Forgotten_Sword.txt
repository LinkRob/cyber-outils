"{{Term/Store|The Forgotten Sword||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Forgotten Sword}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Desert}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
To begin this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must be wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} so that {{Term|BotW|Bozai|link}} will think he is a girl. At the end of "{{Term|BotW|The Eighth Heroine|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to join him under the tent outside of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I think I'll just park myself under the tent in front of Gerudo Town and take five. Care to join me later?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} next speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} will be surprised.<ref>{{Cite|You! You came! I mean, hey, yeah, of course. Figured you'd swing by. Yup. Do the ol' swing-by.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} asks him if he wants to "hang out,"<ref>{{Cite|We didn't get a chance to spend much time together earlier, did we? Whoosh! Total whirlwind! So...wanna hang out?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} immediately asks for his {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I want snow boots.] What? You want my snow boots, too?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He mocks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his "boot thirst that cannot be slaked."<ref>{{Cite|Ha! Well aren't we greedy! Or should I say insatiable for boots. A boot thirst that cannot be slaked!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} does not like the idea of being stuck without footwear,<ref>{{Cite|Adorable. But listen, if I give you these, then I'll have nothing for my feet!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> he admits that he cannot resist a request from {{Term|BotW|Link}} in an attempt to win him over.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Oh, man,what should I do? THINK, Bozai! Can't put her in a bad mood... OK... Do NOT blow this. Just relax and work a little verbal magic...}}}} How could I resist a request from you?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Bozai}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will give him the {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}} if he is able to surprise him again.<ref>{{Cite|No lie, you really gave me a surprise earlier...and I love surprises. Surprise me again like that... and I'll give you these snow boots as a surprise present!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He then explains that the {{Term|BotW|Statue of the Eighth Heroine|link}} is not holding a sword like the other {{Term|BotW|Seven Heroines|link}} are.<ref>{{Cite|So let me ask you this: Did you notice that the statue of the eighth heroine...isn't actually holding a sword? Pretty weird, right? Seven other heroines, they all have their swords! Suspicious, y'know?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} believes that the {{Term|BotW|Statue of the Eighth Heroine|display=Statue}} once had a sword and feels that evidence of its existence would be a great surprise.<ref>{{Cite|Which leads me to deduce that the eighth heroine must have had a sword as well. Must have! To see that such a sword actually exists, that my deduction is right... There could be no greater surprise!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to go look for it,<ref>{{Cite|[I'll go look for it.] What? You're really going?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} says that it could not have been carried off too far.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, all right, then. The sword would be pretty big, so I doubt it could have been carried off too far.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> It is likely residing on the steeps of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm betting it's probably somewhere in the steeps of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Highlands}}.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> Before he finishes speaking, {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} apologizes that he cannot lend {{Term|BotW|Link}} his {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}},<ref>{{Cite|And the thing is, I can't lend you the {{Color|BotW Blue|snow boots}} this time. Just can't do it! I'm really sorry.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> muttering to himself that he could never find it and will probably come crawling back.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|No way a normal person can make it through the Gerudo Highlands without good snow boots... She'll probably give up halfway through and come crawling back...}}}}|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> Finally, he comments on how headstrong {{Term|BotW|Link}} is and invites him back for anything besides footwear.<ref>{{Cite|Fine, you win. So headstrong! I love it. I'll just be waiting here, OK? If you ever need me for anything above and beyond footwear, you just come find ol' Bozai anytime!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref>

In the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Sword|link}} southwest of the {{Term|BotW|Statue of the Eighth Heroine}}. It is located on the southeastern section of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Summit|link}}. After taking a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Sword|display=Sword}} with his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} at the tent.

{{Term|BotW|Bozai}} once again became worried for {{Term|BotW|Link}} after a traveler swore he saw him in the Gerudo Highlands as he did not think {{Term|BotW|Link}} would really go without {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}}.<ref>{{Cite|Y-you! A traveler swore he saw you in the Gerudo Highlands, so I've been gnawing on my fingernails with worry! I mean, all that talk about the sword was just chitchat... I didn't think you'd go up there without snow boots!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> To {{Term|BotW|Bozai}}'s shock, {{Term|BotW|Link}} shows him the picture of the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Feast your eyes.] WHAAAAAT?! It really exists?!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He is surprised that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was able to traverse the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands}} without {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}} and wonders just who he is.<ref>{{Cite|And you went searching for it without a decent pair of snow boots... I mean, the Gerudo Highlands are unforgiving. I doubt I could survive there, and I'm burly. Who ARE you?!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref>

Stating that {{Term|BotW|Link}} risked his life for the {{Term|BotW|Picture}},<ref>{{Cite|Yeah... You did risk your life to fulfill your promise, after all...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} admits that it would be uncouth of him to break his promise and gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} his {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}}.<ref>{{Cite|It'd be pretty uncouth of me to drop my end of the bargain. Just a second. I'll take them off...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> Although stuck barefoot,<ref>{{Cite|Well, now I'm barefoot...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} is happy that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is happy.<ref>{{Cite|But if you're happy, then I'm happy!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> Before Link ends the conversation, {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} confesses that all of this has endeared him to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And what's more, this whole episode has endeared you even more to me! Your cutely greedy side. Your sassy, playfully rude side... Your unexpectedly tough side...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He starts to ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go out with him,<ref>{{Cite|Um... Would you... ...g-g-go out... ...w-w-w-with...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Link}} rejects him.<ref>{{Cite|[Not a chance.] Urk... I kind of knew you'd say that... but would it have killed you to let me finish the question?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete.
