The {{Term/Store|Ribbon|Ribbons|Series, LA, LADX, LANS, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 136"/><ref name="LANS Obtention"/>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Ribbon}} is also referred to as the '''Bow''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 24}}</ref> However, because it contradicts the term given in {{E}}, this term is not considered [[Zelda Wiki:Canon Policy|Canon]].}}

==Locations and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
The {{Term|LANS|Ribbon}} in {{LA}} is the second item in the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}.<ref name="LANS Obtention"/> It is received from {{Term|LANS|Mamasha|link}} in the {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|link}} in exchange for the {{Term|LANS|Yoshi Doll|link}}.<ref name="LANS Obtention"/> The {{Term|LANS|Ribbon}} can then given to {{Term|LANS|YipYip|link}}. In return for the {{Term|LANS|Ribbon}}, {{Term|LANS|Link}} is given a can of {{Term|LANS|Dog Food|link}}.<ref name="E 136"/>

==={{OoS|-}}===
The {{Term|OoS|Ribbon}} in {{OoS}} is a quest item that belongs to {{Term|OoS|Rosa|link}}, who had lost it to the sea.<ref>{{Cite|My name's {{Color|OoS Blue|Rosa}}. My favorite {{Color|OoS Red|Ribbon}} floated out to sea... I, like, wanted to go explore the "temple," but I can't go out like this!|Rosa|OoS}}</ref> The {{Term|OoS|Ribbon}} is found at the {{Term|OoS|Subrosia Market|link}}, and is traded for a {{Term|OoS|Star-Shaped Ore|link}}. After returning the {{Term|OoS|Ribbon}} to {{Term|OoS|Rosa}}, she agrees to accompany {{Term|OoS|Link}} to the {{Term|OoS|Temple of Seasons|link}} on a date.<ref>{{Cite|Since you're so nice, I'll go on a date with you. Let's go look in that "temple."|Rosa|OoS}}</ref>
