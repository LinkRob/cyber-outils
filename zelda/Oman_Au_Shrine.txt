The {{Term/Store|Oman Au Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Oman Au Shrine}}<br/>Magnesis Trial}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Magnesis Trial''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. 

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Oman Au Shrine}} is located on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. It is one of four {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must complete during "{{Term|BotW|The Isolated Plateau|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Old Man explained that there are a total of four Shrines on the plateau. He said that he will give you the Paraglider once you have acquired the Spirit Orb from all the of the Shrines.<br/>He told you to survey the plateau from the top of the tower to find the remaining shrines. He also pointed out that you can use your Sheikah Slate if you need to travel quickly.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} activates the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}} during the first {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}, "{{Term|BotW|Follow the Sheikah Slate|link}}," the {{Term|BotW|Old Man|link}} approaches him at the base of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} points him to towards {{Term|BotW|Oman Au Shrine}} across a small lake nearby, and asks Link to go there and bring him any treasure he finds inside.<ref>{{Cite|Do you see that structure there? The one shining with a strange light?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I want you to go there and fetch me any treasure you find inside.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, it is not required to complete {{Term|BotW|Oman Au Shrine}} first. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Oman Au|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Oman Au. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Oman Au|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first enters the {{Term|BotW|Oman Au Shrine}}, he will find a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}} above it in the corner of the room. When Link places his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} into the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|display=Pedestal}}, the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} will authenticate it and distill a {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} for him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling rune...}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> A drop of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy|link}} will then fall onto the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}, unlocking the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Rune extracted.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> Using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to lift the two metallic slabs in the center of the chamber, revealing a hidden passageway with a  {{Term|BotW|Ladder|link}}.

After going through the passageway, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will come up in another room with a wall of cubes blocking the way to the next room. One of the cubes is metallic, allowing  {{Term|BotW|Link}} to remove it with  {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} and use it to topple the wall. Past this wall is a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}}, which will shoot lasers at {{Term|BotW|Link}}. It must be defeated in order to progress.

Past the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout I}} are three separate platforms in a large pool of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. The first two platforms are bridged together by a metal slab. The first platform also has a {{Term|BotW|Ladder}} so that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can climb back up if he falls into the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}. After crossing to the second platform, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} to move the metal slab to connect the final two platforms. At the end of the room, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a pair large metallic doors that he can pull open with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}}, revealing the final room of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.

At the end of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Oman Au}} is found waiting for Link on the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. If this is the first {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed, {{Term|BotW|Oman Au}} explains that they are a {{Term|BotW|Monk}} of {{Term|BotW|The Goddess Hylia|link}} dedicated to helping defeat {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have proven to possess the resolve of a true hero. I am Oman Au, the creator of this trial. I am a humble monk, blessed with the sight of Goddess Hylia and dedicated to helping those who seek to defeat Ganon.}}|Oman Au|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s arrival fulfills {{Term|BotW|Oman Au}}'s duty, and the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|With your arrival, my duty is now fulfilled. In the name of Goddess Hylia, allow me to bestow this gift upon you... Please accept this Spirit Orb.}}|Oman Au|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Oman Au|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Oman Au}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Oman Au|BotW}}</ref>

Before entering the final room, a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found on a ledge above {{Term|BotW|Link}}. Using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can bring the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} down to his level. Inside, he will find a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Bow|link}}.
