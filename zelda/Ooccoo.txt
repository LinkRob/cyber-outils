==Biography==
A member of the [[Oocca]] tribe, Ooccoo tries to find her way back to the [[City in the Sky]] when [[Link]] discovers her stuck in a [[pot]] in the {{Term|TPHD|Forest Temple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You see, I'm, erm... I'm an adventurer too. I'm looking for something...|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref>  She is found in every dungeon up until the [[Palace of Twilight]] looking for the [[Dominion Rod]], a piece of technology invented by her people.<ref>{{Cite|We can't return home until we find that thing. Oh, gracious no!|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref> This rod has the power to help Ooccoo get back to her home. It is not until Ooccoo journeys to the [[Temple of Time (Dungeon)|Temple of Time]] that she finds the Dominion Rod.<ref>{{Cite|The ancient technology of our people sleeps in this place. We've searched all over...and now we're so close, I can SMELL it! Once we find that thing, we can go home at last! Oh, gracious, yes!|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref> By using the rod, Link finds the  broken [[Sky Cannon]], which [[Fyer]] fixes for 300 [[Rupee]]s. Just before Link uses the cannon to reach the City in the Sky, Ooccoo jumps into the cannon and, along with Link, is launched to the heavens. However, she and Link soon find out that the Oocca are being terrorized by a giant dragon named [[Argorok]]. After Argorok is defeated, Ooccoo is found in the [[Oocca Shop]] in the City in the Sky.

==Location and Uses==
With the help of her son, [[Ooccoo Jr.]], Link can use her to [[Warping|warp]] out dungeons and return later to the room that he warped from.<ref>{{Cite|Ooh! Did you want to leave? I can warp you right out!|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Let me introduce you! This is Ooccoo Jr.! Anytime you want to warp back to this spot from outside, ask him.|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref> This enables Link to save his progress within a dungeon rather than having to start again from the beginning. However, if Link re-enters the dungeon from the entrance door, Ooccoo will meet him there and rejoin him.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to meet me at the entrance rather than warping here, just go there and I'll meet you!|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref> If Link exits a dungeon before its boss is defeated without using Ooccoo, she is replaced in Link's inventory with Ooccoo's Note.<ref>{{Cite|Ooccoo has things to do, so she will remain here. If you return let's get together!|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref>

Ooccoo can not be used on red Warp Portals. Warp Portals are red until the battle square with the Warp Portal or its partner are completed, at which point both portals turn green.
