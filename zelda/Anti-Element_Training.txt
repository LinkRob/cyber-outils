{{Term/Store|Anti-Element Training||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 50 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Element Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Element Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given ten minutes to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Hinox|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Lynel|link}}, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel|link}}. Each one of them is surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Keese|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Chuchu|link}} of their own element, and when the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel}} is approached, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}} spawn. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when all three of the monsters are killed. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Barbarian Helm|link}} and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
