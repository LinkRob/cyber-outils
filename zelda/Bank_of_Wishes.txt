The {{Term/Store|Bank of Wishes||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Bank of Wishes'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes}} is located along the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}. According to legend, if someone sends a letter down the {{Term|BotW|Zora River}} from the {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes}}, the person who replies will be theirs forever.<ref>{{Cite|If you send a {{Color|BotW Red|letter}} down the River from the {{Color|BotW Blue|Bank of Wishes}} and get a reply... that person will be yours forever!|Finley|BotW}}</ref> These wishes are supposedly granted by a {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Fairy|link}}.<ref name="Fairy">{{Cite|Hm? Do you have a wish for the water fairy too? Oh... I suppose not. If you did, you'd have a letter to send.|Finley|BotW}}</ref>

Prior to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} arriving in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Finley|link}} would go to the {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes}} to communicate with her pen pal, {{Term|BotW|Sasan|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I have a pen pal! When I put a {{Color|BotW Red|letter}} in a bottle and send it down {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora River}}, I always get a reply, without fail. It's so fun and mysterious!|Finley|BotW}}</ref> However, once {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} started its rampage started, her mother {{Term|BotW|Kodah|link}} forbade her from visiting.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh! I'm so mad at her! I need to go to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Bank of Wishes}} to send a letter...but Mother won't let me go because of that water monster.|Finley|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, he can find {{Term|BotW|Finley}} at the {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes}} with her {{Term|BotW|Love Letter|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her, she asks if he also has a wish for the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} {{Term|BotW|Fairy}}, but she dismisses this idea when she notices he does not have a letter.<ref name="Fairy"/> She then wonders if he is a servant to the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} {{Term|BotW|Fairy}}.<ref>{{Cite|But wait, does that mean you're... a servant of the water fairy?!|Finley|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Finley}} realizes that he is a traveler, she asks him if he could follow her {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} down the {{Term|BotW|Zora River}} to see who picks it up.<ref>{{Cite|If you're a traveler, that's lucky for me! OH, WOW! If that's the case, I have an important request for you. Once I release it, I'd like you to follow my {{Color|BotW Red|love letter}} and get to know whoever picks it up. Once you do that, please report back to me so I can find out what kind of person he is...|Finley|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Finley}} herself is unable to, as she promised her mother that she would not stray far from {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|I know, I know. You're probably thinking, "Why not just go yourself, lazy girl?!" I'm right, aren't I? The thing is, I promised my mother I wouldn't venture out too far.|Finley|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to her request, {{Term|BotW|Finley}} will thank him before preparing to throw the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} into the {{Term|BotW|Zora River}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Fine...] Tee hee! Thank you! Excellent! OK, I'm going to throw the letter in now! Are you ready?|Finley|BotW}}</ref> Once she does, the "{{Term|BotW|Special Delivery|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

If the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} is broken or lost, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to the {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes}} to inform {{Term|BotW|Finley}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I can't see the letter.] You lost sight of my {{Color|BotW Red|love letter}}, didn't you?|Finley|BotW}}</ref> Having predicted this would happen, she made an extra one.<ref>{{Cite|Luckily, I thought something like that might happen, so I made an extra.|Finley|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Finley}} will also push this {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} into the {{Term|BotW|Zora River}}, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} will still have to follow it.
