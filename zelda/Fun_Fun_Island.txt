{{Term/Store|Fun Fun Island||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a recurring place in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fun Fun Island|Map|SSHD}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Fun Fun Island}} goes unnamed in {{HW}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{SS|-}}===
{{Term|SS|Fun Fun Island}} is located in the {{Term|SS|Sky|link}} southwest of {{Term|SS|Skyloft|link}}. The entire island is used for a {{Term|SS|Mini-Game|link}}, but {{Term|SS|Link|link}} cannot access this {{Term|SS|Mini-Game}} until he has helped {{Term|SS|Dodoh|link}}. A {{Term|SS|Goddess Cube Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|SS|Gold Rupee|link}} can be found beneath the island.

When {{Term|SS|Link}} first lands on the island, he learns that {{Term|SS|Dodoh}}'s {{Term|SS|Party Wheel|link}} fell off the island to the {{Term|SS|Surface|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You see, the party wheel that I pinned to my back flew off the island and fell down somewhere below the clouds. Without my party wheel, there's just no way I can make this island a place where people come to have fun!|Dodoh|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SS|Dodoh}} is overcome by sadness and loses hope for recovering the wheel.<ref>{{Cite|Without that, there's just no way... The dream of fun is dead. I can't bring myself to do anything without it. The laughter has turned to tears.|Dodoh|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|How am I supposed to go get something that fell below the clouds? That's impossible!|Dodoh|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SS|Link}} can raise {{Term|SS|Dodoh}}'s spirits by locating the wheel in {{Term|SS|Lanayru Desert|link}} and using {{Term|SS|LD-301S Scrapper|link}} to carry it to the {{Term|SS|Sky}}. {{Term|SS|Dodoh}} will thank {{Term|SS|Link}} by giving him a free play of {{Term|SS|Dodoh's High Dive|link}}. All subsequent plays require 20 {{Plural|SS|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Normally I'd charge 20 Rupees to play, but since I owe you, I'll let you play your first game for free! FREE is so much more fun than PAY!|Dodoh|SS}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW}}, {{Term|HWDE|Fun Fun Island}} appears on the {{Term|HWDE|Skyloft (Stage)|link|display=Skyloft}} {{Term|HWDE|Stage|link}}, northwest of the {{Term|HWDE|Fairy Fountain|link}} {{Term|HWDE|Keep|link}} and north of the Statue {{Term|HWDE|Keep}}. The {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} cannot walk on {{Term|HWDE|Fun Fun Island}}, but they can spot it from a distance. The island appears tilted and destroyed, with many small pieces of the island floating away from the main piece. Most of the Rupee tiles are visible, with some breaking off from the main island. The small platform that used to hang at the bottom of {{Term|HWDE|Fun Fun Island}} is missing, replaced with a fabric wrapping around the whole island. The {{Term|HWDE|Fun Fun Island}} slowly ascends and descends in place, otherwise unmoving from its location.
{{Noncanon|End}}
