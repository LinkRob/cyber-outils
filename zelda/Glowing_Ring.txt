{{Term/Store|Glowing Ring|Glowing Rings|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Heehl">{{Cite|You see that {{Color|BotW Red|glowing ring}} in the lava? Think of it as a target, and... {{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|stand on the lava, brother}}!}}|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> They are exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}} appear during the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}", "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song|link}}", "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song|link}}", and "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Plural|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. They are referred to in the {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} sings, where they are part of the trials that the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} undertook 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|♪Crawling flame on a mighty peak.<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}}'s Champion, never weak.♪<br/>♪The hero's power shall grow.<br/>Seek trials {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} show.♪<br/>♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|stop a titan of molten stone}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|follow rings of light alone}}.♪<br/>♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|survive lava's fiery fate}}.<br/>Champion, trials await!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|♪Reaching skyward from waters blue.<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}}'s Champion a heart true.♪<br/>♪The hero's power shall grow.<br/>Seek trials {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} show.♪<br/>♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|find what the light's path shows}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|conquer ancient foes}}.♪<br/>♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|chase rings of the waterfall}}.<br/>Champion, the trials call!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|♪Wind's ally soars the land of cloud.<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}'s Champion, swift and proud.♪<br/>♪The hero's power shall grow.<br/>Seek trials {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} show.♪<br/>♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|shoot the flame dragon's horn}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|race down a peak rings adorn}}.♪<br/>♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|shoot four targets to win}}.<br/>Champion, the trials begin!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|♪Lightning king of the desert.<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}'s Champion, the stalwart.♪<br/>♪The hero's power shall grow.<br/>Seek trials {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} show.♪<br/>♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|fight the brute of the sand}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|chase rings upon the land}}.♪<br/>♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|throw the orb underground}}.<br/>Champion, trials abound!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}} appear in two forms. One is stationary, requiring {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to solve a puzzle in order to reach them. The second type appears during races, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have a limited time to reach them before they disappear. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} solves these puzzles or completes these races, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} will appear.

Two of the trials during "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song}}" involve {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}}. On top of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find the first in a series of {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}} that he must follow by both gliding with his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} or climbing. When he reaches the final {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|display=Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Sharo Lun Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground. The second trial occurs at {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond|link}}, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} finds the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers|link}} preparing to train where {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} used to.<ref>{{Cite|Do ya know why {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}} chose this place to train, brother? ...Neither do we! But us Goron Blood Brothers are gonna find out by completing the training ourselves!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> They point out the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}} on top of the {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond|display=Pond}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, explaining it is their target.<ref name="Heehl"/> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can reach this {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|display=Ring}} by pushing the rocks in the {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} with metal boxes. Once he touches it, {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground.

An additional two trials from "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song}}" make use of {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}}. For the first trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Muzu|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tottika|link}} overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Sea|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Muzu}} recounts a phrase from {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link|display=Mipha's}} notes that describes one of the trials she undertook.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Princess Mipha's}} notes had this phrase... "While the {{Color|BotW Red|morning sun}} is newly born, follow the {{Color|BotW Red|path of light}} to the trial."|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> At sunrise, a {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}} will appear in the water between {{Term|BotW|Ankel Island|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tingel Island|link}}. Passing through it will cause {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia Shrine|link}} to emerge from the {{Term|BotW|Water|link}}. The next trial starts at {{Term|BotW|Mikau Lake|link}}, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a series of {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}} leading up the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}} all the way to {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} swims through the final {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|display=Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Mah Eliya Shrine|link}} will rise from the ground.

"{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song}}" has one trial that includes {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}}. Near {{Term|BotW|Hebra Peak|link}}, he will find the beginning of a trail of {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}} that he must {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link|display=Shield Surf}} through. The {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|display=Rings}} lead down into the {{Term|BotW|Pikida Stonegrove|link}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} surfs through the final {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Kiah Toza Shrine|link}} will emerge from the snow.

Finally, one trial during "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song}}" involves {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}}. A series of {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}} will appear in the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} to pass through all of the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|display=Rings}}. He can rent one from the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|link}}, which are trained to be faster and wait if {{Term|BotW|Link}} leaves them.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|sand seals}} in town were all trained by Frelly. They're faster, and they'll wait for you to return to them.|Essa|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, {{Term|BotW|Essa|link}} will appear outside of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} during this {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}, where she is attempting to tame wild {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}}.<ref>{{Cite|Frelly asked me to go recruit some wild {{Color|BotW Red|sand seals}}.|Essa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can take one of her {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}}, but they are not trained like the ones in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|By comparison, the ones outside who haven't been trained will just run off the moment you dismount.|Essa|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes through the final {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri Shrine|link}} will rise from the sand.
