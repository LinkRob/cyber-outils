The {{Term/Store|Battle Log||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} is an interface in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Battle Log|Info|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|Battle Log}} is a list submenu found in the {{Term|HWDE|Info|link}} menu. On the right side of the {{Term|HWDE|Battle Log}}, a blank map of the current {{Term|HWDE|Stage|link}} appears. On the left side, the {{Term|HWDE|Battle Log}} displays all messages, missions, and other events that have occurred throughout the battle. When selected, an icon will appear on the map where the event occurred, message was spoken, or where the mission objective is located. Above the map also displays the amount of time spent in battle, and if playing in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}} or {{Term|HWDE|Challenge Mode|link}}, will also display the amount of damage taken.<ref>{{Cite|Elapsed Time|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Damage Taken|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref>
