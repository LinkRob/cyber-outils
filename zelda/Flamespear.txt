{{Compendium/Extract|Flamespear}}
{{Plural|BotW|Flamespear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. They are also found within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. They were forged in the magma of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} with elemental properties.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Their attacks deal {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} damage when they are used while they glow.<ref name="Inventory"/> Equipping a {{Term|BotW|Flamespear}} in a {{Term|BotW|Freezing Climate|link}} will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}. Finally, they will one-shot any {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}-type {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} they are used against.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Flamespear}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will also sink when they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. If they are exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Fire}} or {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, they will not burn like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} do.
