The {{Name|Fan|{{Term/Store|Innkeeper||Series}}}} is a character in {{ALttP}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography==
The Innkeeper works in the Inn in [[Kakariko Village#A Link to the Past|Kakariko Village]], directly east of the [[Bug-Catching Kid]]'s house. He is unnamed, but is familiar with and knows [[Link]]'s name. He tells Link about [[Zora's Lake]] and refers to the [[Flippers|Zora's Flippers]] and their ability to make Link swim.{{Fact|ALttP}} Later on, if Link visits him again, the man talks about the [[Flute Boy]], the Flute Boy's [[Flute Boy's Bird|pet bird]], and his disappearance after going to [[Death Mountain#A Link to the Past|Death Mountain]].<ref>{{Cite|He had a pet bird that went with him everywhere, but he went to the mountain and never returned.|Innkeeper|ALttP}}</ref>

The Innkeeper is seen with the Bug-Catching Kid and the [[Informant Woman|Informant Woman]] wearing a yellow dress during the ending sequence.

==Trivia==
* The Innkeeper bears some similarities to [[Link's Uncle]].
