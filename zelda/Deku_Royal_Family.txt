The {{Term/Store|Deku Royal Family||Series, MM, MM3D}} is the presiding body of the [[Deku Kingdom]] that appears in {{MM}}.<ref name=Sonata>{{Cite|Everyone, did you hear that? This melody, which only the Deku Royal Family knows...|Deku King|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Our swamps have become poisoned...This may be the end of the Deku Kingdom!|Deku Butler|MM}}</ref>

The [[Deku King]] and his daughter, the [[Deku Princess]], make up the ruling monarchs, and are served faithfully by the [[Deku Butler]] and the palace guards. While he was alive, the [[Deku Butler's Son]] undoubtedly served at the palace as well. They inhabit the [[Deku Palace]] of the [[Southern Swamp]], the southernmost region of [[Termina]].

The Deku Royalty apparently possess absolute judiciary power in their Kingdom, as demonstrated by the Deku King's merciless punishment of the [[Monkey#Majora's Mask|Monkey]] accused of kidnapping the princess.<ref>{{Cite|The monkey shall suffer and suffer 'til he can suffer no more! You shall know the wrath of a king whose darling princess was taken away from him!!!|Deku King|MM}}</ref> The palace guards are equally quick to obey the commands of the princess.<ref>{{Cite|WHAT are you doing?!? Let that monkey go this instant!!|Deku Princess|MM}}</ref> The Deku Royal Family also appear to be the kingdom's spiritual leaders: they are the only Deku allowed inside the hidden [[Woodfall Temple|Temple]] at [[Woodfall]].<ref>{{Cite|Only royal family members can enter the sunken temple, so it's obvious that the monkey was using her!|Deku Scrub|MM}}</ref> According to the Deku King, only the Deku Royal Family may learn the "[[Sonata of Awakening]]" needed to reveal the Temple's entrance.<ref name=Sonata/> This is undoubtedly why the Deku Princess had taken up learning the [[Deku Pipes]].<ref>{{Cite|The tiny princess only recently learned to play the Deku Pipes... His Royal Highness was so proud of her...|Deku Scrub|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! You have them! You do! Just like the princess... Deku Pipes! Those will work great!!!|Monkey|MM}}</ref>
