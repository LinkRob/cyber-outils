'''Bazaar''' is a character from ''[[w:c:donkeykong:Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!|Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''. He has minimal connections to {{TLoZ|Series}}, though he has talked to [[Link]] on at least one occasion.

==Biography==
[[File:Bazaar mentions Link.png|thumb|left|Link is mentioned by Bazaar in ''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!'']]
Bazaar runs a [[w:c:donkeykong:Bazaar's General Store|general store]] on the main map of the [[w:c:donkeykong:Northern Kremisphere|Northern Kremisphere]]. [[w:c:donkeykong:Dixie Kong|Dixie Kong]] and [[w:c:donkeykong:Kiddy Kong|Kiddy Kong]] can go here to purchase a few items that are necessary to give to certain Brothers Bears, akin to the trading item sequences in {{TLoZ|Series}}. He is the first Brothers Bear whom the [[w:c:donkeykong:Kong|Kongs]] meet.

Bazaar also sells some information to Dixie and Kiddy to help them on their journey. Dixie and Kiddy can pay two Bear Coins to Bazaar to learn about the castle, Kastle Kaos, to which Bazaar mentions that Link had asked him the same question about a week ago. Though Link did not have any Bear Coins to purchase the information, Bazaar accepted his payment of Rupees instead. Link left afterwards, murmuring about the [[w:c:donkeykong:Shell|Shells]] being the wrong shape, a likely reference to his side-quest of searching for [[Secret Seashell]]s in {{LA}}.

Bazaar's dialogue was changed for the [[Game Boy Advance]] version, where he no longer mentions Link, retroactively disconnecting not only Bazaar but also ''Donkey Kong Country 3'' from {{TLoZ|-}} series entirely. Bazaar talks about what he had witnessed at the castle instead.

Though Bazaar does not appear in ''[[w:c:donkeykong:Donkey Kong Land III|Donkey Kong Land III]]'', there are a series of [[w:c:donkeykong:Bear|Bears]] throughout the game who strongly resemble Bazaar and even appear to wear his white apron shirt. Unlike Bazaar, the Bears do not mention Link or anything from {{TLoZ|-}} series at all.

This section gives a basic overview of some of the aforementioned subjects in ''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!''.

*{{anchor|Brothers Bear}} '''Brothers Bear:''' A group of bears with a variety of personalities whom Dixie and Kiddy encounter during their adventure of searching for Donkey Kong and Diddy Kong. Some Brothers Bears are helpful toward the Kongs directly, while others ask favors of them.
*{{anchor|Kastle Kaos}} '''Kastle Kaos:''' A castle in the penultimate world, [[w:c:donkeykong:Kaos Kore|Kaos Kore]], where the final bosses [[w:c:donkeykong:KAOS|KAOS]] and [[w:c:donkeykong:Baron K. Roolenstein|Baron K. Roolenstein]] are fought.
*{{anchor|Northern Kremisphere}} '''Northern Kremisphere:''' The main setting of ''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!'' and a neighboring land of [[w:c:donkeykong:Donkey Kong Island|Donkey Kong Island]]. It is the game's hub area.
*'''Shell:''' One of the items for sale at Bazaar's General Store. Link muttered about it being the wrong shape.
