'''Dark Soups''' are [[Item|Items]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]. <ref><q>''"A gooey soup built on stewed dark clump. One could get lost in its swirls and flavors.''</q> " - [[inventory]] [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|(Tears of the Kingdom)]]</ref>
