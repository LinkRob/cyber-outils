The {{Term/Store|Face Key||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 122"/>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|LANS|Face Key}} is dropped by the {{Term|LANS|Armos Knight|link}} located in the {{Term|LANS|Southern Face Shrine|link}}.<ref name="Location"/>

The {{Term|LANS|Face Key}} allows {{Term|LANS|Link|link}}, by inserting it into the {{Term|LANS|Face Keyhole|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hunh? A keyhole here? It says, 'Face Keyhole'|N/A|LADX}}</ref> to unlock access to the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine|link}}.<ref name="E 122"/>
