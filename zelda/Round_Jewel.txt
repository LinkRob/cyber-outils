The {{Term/Store|Round Jewel||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Round Jewel is given to [[Link]] by an [[Old Man]] after obtaining the first five [[Essences of Nature]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh keeper of the essences! I have awaited your arrival! I give you this!|Old Man|OoS}}</ref> It is used in conjunction with the other three Jewels to open the door leading to [[Tarm Ruins]].
