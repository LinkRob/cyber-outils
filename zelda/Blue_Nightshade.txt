==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Blue Nightshade}}
{{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} can be found growing in the {{Plural|BotW|Region|link}} of {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. Many {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} grow near the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} outside of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. These flowers glow at night, making them easier to find.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> When {{Term|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} is {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}} into a meal in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, it will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} increased {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} when eaten.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} to make {{Term|BotW|Blue Dye|link}}.

As part of the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|A Gift of Nightshade|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Link}} must give {{Term|BotW|Wabbin|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} that he can gift to {{Term|BotW|Perda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I had a {{Color|BotW Red|blue nightshade}}, I'd head straight over to that woman...|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> However, once he obtains one, {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} will become nervous and ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to deliver the {{Term|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} on his behalf.<ref>{{Cite|Ah... I just... Ugh.<br/>When I look at her, my legs freeze up. My throat goes dry. I... Please, could you take {{Color|BotW Red|my gift}} to her?|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> After he does this, {{Term|BotW|Perda}} will confront {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}}, asking how he knew that {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} were her favorite flower.<ref>{{Cite|Why would you say that? This is my favorite kind of {{Color|BotW Red|flower}}! How did you know that anyway? Are you a Yiga spy?!|Perda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} reveals that {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} are his favorite flowers as well, and he decided he would give them to his true love.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Blue nightshade}} just happens to be my favorite {{Color|BotW Red|flower}}! Long ago, I decided to give my true love this flower...|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> Once the two of them confess their feelings, {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.
