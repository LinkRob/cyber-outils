The {{Term/Store|Flying Rooster||Series, LA, LADX}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref name="Ulrira"/>{{Name Ref|LANS}}

==Biography==
[[File:LADX Flying Rooster Sprite 2.png|left|48px]]
The {{Term|LANS|Flying Rooster}} is initially a deceased corpse resting underneath the {{Term|LANS|Weathercock|link}} of {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here Sleeps The Flying Rooster|Weathercock|LADX|}}</ref> By playing the "{{Term|LANS|Frog's Song of Soul|link}}" next to him, {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} can resurrect him. The {{Term|LANS|Flying Rooster}} will then follow {{Term|LANS|Link}} around on the {{Term|LANS|Overworld|link}}. By holding him above his head, {{Term|LANS|Link}} is able to fly, allowing him to reach the {{Term|LANS|Bird Key|link}} in {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Heights|link}}. The {{Term|LANS|Flying Rooster}} can also be used to an optional {{Term|LANS|Secret Seashell|link}} near the bridge of {{Term|LANS|Kanalet Castle|link}}.

Once {{Term|LANS|Link}} has completed {{Term|LANS|Eagle's Tower|link}}, the {{Term|LANS|Flying Rooster}} will leave and make his new home in the {{Term|LANS|Chicken Coop|link}} with the {{Term|LANS|Cucco Keeper|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wooo! Finally! This flying rooster is the greatest!|Cucco Keeper|LADX}}</ref> The {{Term|LANS|Flying Rooster}} is seen giving the man some flights in his house, and {{Term|LANS|Link}} will no longer be able to use it.<ref name="Keeper"/>
