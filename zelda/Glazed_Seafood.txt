{{Term/Store|Glazed Seafood|Glazed Seafoods|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Glazed Seafood}}<br/>A seafood dish that you can actually wolf down whole!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Glazed Seafood is a dish that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any seafood together with [[Courser Bee Honey]]. The default variation can be made by using seafood that has a different effect than the Courser Bee Honey. When eaten, they'll some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Glazed Seafood is known to be a meal that can be eaten in one sitting.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Only one variation of Glazed Seafood is available:

* '''Energizing Glazed Seafood''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Glazed Seafood}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A seafood dish you can actually wolf down whole!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}. Eating these meals will refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>
