'''"The Ringer"''' is the first episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. It was broadcast in the United States and Canada on September 8, 1989.

==Plot==
:''See also: [[The Ringer/Episode transcript|Episode transcript]]''
{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} reluctantly gets out of bed, complaining about his "boring" life in {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|North Castle|link}} and reminiscing about the days when he would roam the world and constantly fight monsters.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Yuck, another beautiful day in the magical kingdom of Hyrule. Boring place. I used to roam the world, fighting monsters and sleeping in mud. A hero's life! Now look at me, living in a castle, sleeping in a bed...aren't I sweet? Yuck!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1|time=x}}</ref> He looks out the window and sees {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}} in a nearby balcony and whistles at her. Annoyed, Zelda leaves and shortly after, a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin|link}} sneaks behind {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and attacks him while more {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} come into his room. One of the minions tries to steal the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce|link}} of Wisdom in {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}'s room, but the young hero manages to defeat them and sends them back to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}}'s {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Evil Jar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hey it's been fun, but the other customers are getting impatient." Guys, it's been a blast, but you're going home. And tell Ganon that if he really wants this triforce, he'll have to get it himself. Ok, that's better!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1|time=x}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} then notices that one of the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} dropped a Magic {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Bow|link}}, and stores it in his pocket before Zelda knocks on his door. After the young hero tries to kiss her, Zelda greets him with a smack to his cheek for having whistled at her before.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Don't you ever whistle at me again!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} then tells her of the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} that attacked him earlier,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=About ten seconds ago, about 15 of them. Armed to the teeth! Naturally, I fought them off, risking my life to save the Triforce and your kingdom.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> and the princess notices that this was the third attack by {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} in the same month.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Anyway, this is the third attack by Ganon this month. We'll have to be on our guard.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> Much to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}'s dismay,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Aw, princess, it's too nice a day!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> Zelda orders {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} to stay in his room and guard the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} while she goes off to judge the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magician's Contest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=By "we", of course I mean you. Stay here today and guard the Triforce. [...] Don't argue. The Triforce must be guarded. And I'm already late to judge the Magician's Contest.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref>
[[File:Link Dragon (The Ringer).png|thumb|right|Link fighting the Dragon]]
Meanwhile, in the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} punishes the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin}} and concludes that if he wants the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom, he will have to get it himself. He begins his plans to spoil the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magician's Contest}} by appearing ''in cognito'': since his teleportation powers are limited in the "upper world," he disguises four {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos|link}} in robes and makes them carry him on a platform.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Obviously, if I want the Triforce of Wisdom, I'm going to have to get it myself. There is a contests of amateur magicians in Hyrule today. I shall enter that contest! [...] Since my teleportation powers are limited in the upper world, I'll have to travel in style.|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} eventually arrives to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|North Castle}} and asks to be let in the contest.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=My name is not important. I merely wish to enter this contest.|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> When Zelda asks him if he is an amateur magician, the evil wizard answers by making a bird appear out of his hands, which the princess approves of but asks that he wait his turn.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Fine, wait your turn.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> Zelda continues to judge the contest while {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} makes the bird turn into an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ache|link}} and orders it to go see who is guarding the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Quickly, fly to the tower if anyone guards the Triforce of Wisdom.|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> The {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ache}}, having seen {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte|link}} next to the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}}, later comes back to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} and informs him that the young hero is guarding the sacred relic.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=It's guarded by Link.|name=Ache|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> When one of the magicians makes a lizard appear on Zelda's hand, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} uses his magic to turn the lizard into a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Dragon (Animated Series)|display=fire-breathing dragon|link}} that promptly turns towards the princess, causing her to scream in fear. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} hears Zelda's scream and rushes down from his room to help the princess. Although the young hero tries to defend himself from the dragon's fire using his {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Magical Shield|link}}, the flame is too strong.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Whoa! Toasty! [...] Can't...get a clear zap!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> In order to make an opening for {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}'s attacks, Zelda throws a nearby plate in his direction,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Link! Get ready!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> and the young hero fires several {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam|link}} at it which reflect back at the dragon, promptly defeating it. However, Zelda and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} soon learn that the dragon was just a diversion and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} had managed to steal the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hah! The Triforce of Wisdom! Mine, at last!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref>

[[File:Link Zelda 4 (The Ringer).png|left|thumb|200px|Link and Zelda defending against the Stalfos]]
{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda}} quickly grabs a seed that was sitting on a nearby table and asks one of the magicians from the contest to use their growing spell, causing the seed to immediately grow into a giant tree.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Quick, your growing spell!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> The princess then ties a rope around the tip of the tree, and after {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda climb on top of it, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} snaps the rope with his sword so that the two are launched out of the castle. Zelda falls on top of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}, who tries to get her to kiss him, but Zelda pushes him away and warns him that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} is coming.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ta-da! Saved your life again. Kiss me.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Look out! It's Ganon!|name=Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> Although {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} is quickly approaching a secret {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}} entrance, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} zaps one of the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos}} carrying the evil wizard, resulting in {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} falling over, who commands his minions to attack the young duo while he calls for reinforcements.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Get them now while I call for reinforcements!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} gives Zelda the Magic {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Bow}} that he found earlier,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= A measly three Stalfos? Hah! Take this, princess.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> and when he sees that more {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos}} are coming from the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}} entrance, he takes his belt off and ties it around both him and Zelda to fight back-to-back.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Back-to-back, princess! Fast!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> The pair easily defeats the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos}} together, and when there is only one {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos}} left, it takes out a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Bomb|link}} from underneath its chest and throws it at {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda; however, the young hero blocks it with his {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword|link}} and zaps the foe. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} attempts to make a quick getaway with the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom in hand,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=But I have the Triforce! You have lost! Hyrule is mine!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> but {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} uses the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Bomb}} that the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos}} threw at him and knocks it back at {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}}, causing the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} to be knocked from his hands.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Not yet, Ganon!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref>

With the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce}} of Wisdom now safe, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} tells Zelda that he will not get the belt off around them until she kisses him. Just as they are about to kiss, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte}} comes in and interrupts them, using her magic to get the belt off.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Hey! Knock it off, you two! [...] Oh, I see. The belt's stuck. Here, I'll fix that.|name=Spryte|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> The episode ends with {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} inside the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Evil Jar}} threatening that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda will pay for what they have done.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Wait until I get out of here, Zelda and Link. You'll pay for this!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref>

*The cartoon series establishes that [[Link]]'s entire inventory is actually shrunk inside his magic pouch. When {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} is fighting the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Dragon|link}}, he pulls a miniaturized shield from the pouch, which then grows to normal size.
*This episode marks the only time the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce|link}} of Power speaks.
