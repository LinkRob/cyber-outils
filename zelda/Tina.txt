{{Term/Store|Tina||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Tina}} is a teacher at {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, along with their twin sister {{Term|TMC|Dina|link}}.<ref name="Figurine"/> They hold the philosophy that for children, playing is equally as important as schoolwork.<ref>{{Cite|The children are on their lunch recess. Playing is as important as studying! After all, a healthy body leads to a healthy mind!|Tina|TMC}}</ref> They remain inside {{Term|TMC|Funday School}} for the entirety of the game, and thus can only be spoken to once {{Term|TMC|Link}} has encountered the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}, they urge the children to go to the safety of their homes.<ref>{{Cite|You kids run along now, you hear?|Tina|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element}}, he can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Tina}},<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Very well.|Tina|TMC}}</ref> causing a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a red {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} to appear in {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}}.
