The {{Term/Store|West Deplian Badlands||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''West Deplian Badlands'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The West Deplian Badlands are a series of barren landscapes in the [[Eldin Mountains]] range. They are located south of the [[Deplian Badlands]] and northeast of the [[Drenan Highlands]]. The West Deplian Badlands offer very little vegetation, though more plant life can be found along the slope to the West Deplian Badlands' east. At the top of this slope, there is a large monster camp. Two metallic [[Treasure Chest]]s can be found at the top of this camp, containing a [[Ruby]] and a [[Sapphire]].

A Korok can be found underneath a Rock atop a spire of land at the base of West Deplian Badlands' northern slope.
