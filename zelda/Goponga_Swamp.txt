==Features and Overview==
==={{LA|-}}===
Goponga Swamp is a swamp located in the northwest section of [[Koholint Island]]. It is at the base of the [[Tal Tal Mountain Range]]. It is west of [[Tal Tal Heights]], northwest of [[Koholint Prairie]], and north of the [[Mysterious Woods]]. The eastern half of what is called Goponga Swamp is the swamp proper, while the western half is normal terrain.

The second dungeon, [[Bottle Grotto]], can be found here.  To access the dungeon's entrance, Link must bring along [[Chain Chomp#Link's Awakening|BowWow]] to eat through several [[Goponga Flower]]s guarding the entrance. There is only one non-monster resident of Goponga Swamp, [[Mr. Write]], who lives in the less swampy western half. There is also a phone booth and a cave.
