The '''Wand''' is a [[weapon]] in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Wand is a weapon used by [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] throughout her journey in {{ZA|-}}. She finds it on a rock east of the starting point at the [[Vision Henge]].

The Wand is used similarly to a [[Sword]]. Its status of being Zelda's main weapon is comparable to a [[Sword]] being [[Link]]'s main weapon in many of his appearances. Zelda can also use the Wand in conjunction with the [[Spell]]s that she learns along the way to throw magical projectiles for added offense, but at the cost of a certain number of [[Rupee]]s per Spell use. The attacking power of the Wand also increases as Zelda acquires more [[Celestial Sign]]s.
