The '''Hinox''' is a character in {{ALBW}}. It is a lone, friendly [[Hinox]] who lives inside a cave behind a waterfall in eastern [[Lorule]].

==Biography==
The Hinox lives in a cave behind a waterfall in Lorule. When found and spoken to, it will bribe [[Link]] with [[Rupee]]s to leave it alone. The Hinox will initially start with five Rupees, increasing the amount for every time Link demands more. However, if Link asks for too much, it will become enraged and demand its Rupees back, attacking Link with charges that inflict a huge amount of damage. Link will be able to attempt again later if he saves and quits, although returning immediately will cause the Hinox to resume its attacks. The Hinox cannot be harmed or stopped once it becomes hostile. The maximum amount of Rupees the Hinox will offer before becoming angry is set at random each time, and will rise to a maximum of 300 Rupees.

If Link speaks to the Hinox after agreeing to leave it alone, it will tell Link to [[Iconic Quotes from The Legend of Zelda#"It's a secret to everybody"|keep it a secret to everybody]], as it has a reputation to protect.<ref>{{Cite|Please don't tell anyone I paid you off. I have a reputation to protect. Let's keep this a secret to everybody.|Hinox|ALBW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The Hinox is likely a reference to the [[Moblin]]s in {{TLoZ}} that could be found hidden around the overworld, who also give Link Rupees and tell him "it's a secret to everybody."
