{{Term/Store|amiibo||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} ability in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|amiibo|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}. The {{Term|BotW|Rune}} enables compatible [[amiibo]] figures to be scanned into the game.

==Location and Uses==

=== Breath of the Wild ===
Unlike other {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}s, amiibo support is activated via the Options menu of the game and available after obtaining the first Rune on [[the Great Plateau]].<ref>{{Cite|You can now use the amiibo rune with the Sheikah Slate. You can change this setting on the Options menu at any time.|N/A|BotW}}</ref> The support can also be disabled at any time.

==Ability==
When activated, the amiibo {{Term|BotW|Rune}} allows for all available amiibo figures to be scanned into the game. A cursor appears in front of Link which can be moved to aim where the amiibo's effects will generate. At certain locations, the amiibo {{Term|BotW|Rune}} will be deactivated, such as when the landing point is obstructed by something above, in {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, during the [[Eventide Island]] trial, or during the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword|link}}. Each amiibo figure can only be scanned once per day, with the exception of {{Term|BotW|Wolf Link|link}}, which can be summoned indefinitely as long as it still has hearts remaining.

==Scanning Results==
===Overview===
The majority of available amiibo will grant random items and {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}. Most ''Zelda''-related amiibo will drop both a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} that drop from the sky, while some may also drop [[Crate]]s and [[Barrel]]s. The Treasure Chests may contain rare Gems, weapons and Armor, some of which are not otherwise available. Additionally, the {{SSB|-}} series {{Term|SSB|Link}} amiibo and {{TLoZ|-}} series {{TP|-}}-style {{Term|TP|Link}} amiibo can summon {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}}, while the {{TLoZ|-}} series {{Term|BotW|Wolf Link}} amiibo will exclusively summon {{Term|BotW|Wolf Link}} into the game.

Some items, including exclusives, are only available depending on the game's progression and previous drops. Scanning one amiibo figure every day for 4 days and receiving drops from the "Normal chest" category each day will cause the scan to yield a drop from the "Rare chest" category on the 5th day.<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://pastebin.com/he80NGqB|title= BotW datamined amiibo loot tables|site= Pastebin}}</ref> A few of the weaker weapons are also only available before leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} for the first time.<ref name="Reddit amiibo Guide">{{Cite Web|url= https://www.reddit.com/r/zelda/comments/5y3r8o/spoilers_updated_breath_of_the_wild_amiibo_item/|title= {{BotW|-}} amiibo Item Guide|site= Reddit}}</ref> Items and probabilities under "A" will only happen before obtaining the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}. Items and probabilities under "B" will happen after obtaining the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}} but before freeing the first {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}. Items and probabilities under "C" will happen any time after freeing the first {{Term|BotW|Champions}}.
