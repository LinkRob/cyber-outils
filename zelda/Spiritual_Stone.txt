The {{Term/Store|Spiritual Stone|Spiritual Stones|Series, OoT, OoT3D|plural}} are important items in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref> They are large, colorful gems containing mystical powers.

==Uses and Location==
As explained by [[Princess Zelda]], the Spiritual Stones are required to open the [[Door of Time]], which has sealed away the [[Master Sword]] in the [[Temple of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|That's right... The Temple of Time is the entrance through which you can enter the Sacred Realm from our world. But the entrance is sealed with a stone wall called the Door of Time. And, in order to open the door, it is said that you need to collect three Spiritual Stones.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> When [[Link]] travels to [[Hyrule]] and meets Princess Zelda for the first time, she reveals that she had a dream that someone from the [[Kokiri Forest|Forest]] in possession of the [[Spiritual Stone of the Forest|Kokiri's Emerald]] would come to meet her.<ref>{{Cite|...You wouldn't happen to have... the Spiritual Stone of the Forest, would you?! That green and shining stone...|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The light turned into a figure holding a green and shining stone, followed by a fairy... I know this is a prophecy that someone would come from the forest... Yes, I thought you might be the one...|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> She also reveals that the [[races]] who protect these stones ([[Kokiri]], [[Goron|Gorons]], and [[Zora|Zoras]]) will only open their minds to those who know the [[Zelda's Lullaby|melody of the Royal Family]].<ref>{{Cite|The Spiritual Stone... You finally found it! It is said that the races that hold the Spiritual Stones open their minds only to one who knows the melody of the Royal Family. But you already know that, don't you?|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref>

During his quest to unlock the [[Sacred Realm]] and steal the [[Triforce]], {{Term|OoT3D|Ganondorf|link}} was denied the Spiritual Stones by each of their respective guardians.<ref>{{Cite|All this trouble must have been caused by that Gerudo thief, Ganondorf! He said, "Give me the Spiritual Stone! Only then will I open the cave for you!"|Darunia|OoT}}</ref> In response, the [[Gerudo]] king cursed them all. In order to obtain the Spiritual Stones, Link, the [[Hero of Time]], is required to free the guardians from Ganondorf's malevolence. Once all the Spiritual Stones are collected, Link must place them in the pedestal facing the Door of Time inside the Temple of Time. He must then play the "[[Song of Time]]" in order to open the door leading to the Master Sword resting on its own pedestal.

===Kokiri's Emerald===
{{Main|Kokiri's Emerald}}
[[File:OoT3D Spiritual Stone of the Forest Icon.png|left]]
The '''Kokiri's Emerald''', the Spiritual Stone of the Forest, is protected by the [[Great Deku Tree]] within [[Kokiri Forest]] and is a treasure hidden by the [[Kokiri]] race. In order to obtain it, Link must destroy the parasite that had infested the Great Deku Tree, [[Gohma|Queen Gohma]]. However, the Great Deku Tree unfortunately dies afterwards.
===Goron's Ruby===
{{Main|Goron's Ruby}}
[[File:OoT3D Spiritual Stone of Fire Icon.png|left]]
The '''Goron's Ruby''', the Spiritual Stone of Fire, is a treasure hidden by the [[Goron]] race. After Link defeats the evil creatures inside [[Dodongo's Cavern]], specifically [[King Dodongo]], the Gorons' source of food, he is given the stone by [[Darunia]], the patriarch of the Gorons, which he calls Link his 'Sworn Brother' afterwards.
===Zora's Sapphire===
{{Main|Zora's Sapphire}}
[[File:OoT3D Spiritual Stone of Water Icon.png|left]]
The '''Zora's Sapphire''', the Spiritual Stone of Water, is a hidden treasure by the [[Zora]] race. It is obtained by saving [[Princess Ruto]] from [[Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly]], but it can be seen prematurely within the Dungeon. After defeating [[Barinade]] which revives [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]] from the curse, the Zora princess falls in love with Link and gives him the Zora's Sapphire as a symbol of their engagement (although the hero is oblivious of Ruto's proposal), as it also serves as the so-called "Zora's Engagement Ring".

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese version of the game, the Spiritual Stones are called '''Spiritual Jewel'''.
*The three Spiritual Stones are based on the [[wikipedia:Emerald|emerald]]. [[wikipedia:Ruby|ruby]], and [[wikipedia:Sapphire|sapphire]] precious gemstones, hence their names.
*During development of {{OoT|-}}, the Spiritual Stones would be inserted inside the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]] in order to enter the [[Temple of Time]].<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= Just as the subtitle of the game suggests, this is important for going to the "Tower of Time." If you go inside, Ocarina must have the three soul stones inserted.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= http://www.ign.com/articles/1997/12/02/the-z-files-3|title= The Z-Files}}</ref>
*The Kokiri's Emerald was called "Kokiri's Jade" in early screenshots of the game,{{Fact|OoT}} which is a direct translation from its Japanese name.
*The Spiritual Stones when given to [[Link]] by the [[The Great Deku Tree|Great Deku Tree]], [[Darunia|Daruina]], or [[Princess Ruto]] appear in a bright flash of colorful light before revealing themselves, giving the background a shade of its hue.[[File:OoT weakened Spiritual Stones (rescaled).jpg|thumb|211x211px|Powerless Spiritual Stones by the Door of Time as seen in an earlier build of ''Ocarina of Time'']]
*In the beta version of ''Ocarina of Time'', it was once planned during development that the Spiritual Stones would lose their power when Link becomes the Hero of Time, making them appear gray. In order for them to regain their energy, Link would've had to complete the [[Forest Temple|Forest]], [[Fire Temple|Fire]] and [[Water Temple]] before going back to Child Link. Although this was scrapped in the final release to allow Adult Link to go back in time after only getting the [[Forest Medallion]], remnants of this still exist in the game's code through unused cutscenes.
*The Spiritual Stones have a [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_SpiritualStone.wav distinctive glittering sound] as they appear on-screen.
*Coincidentally, the Spiritual Stones are each shaped in the form of the emblems of the respective races who own them, as well as the Mark of [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]] for the Zora's Sapphire.
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'', the model of the Zora's Sapphire was changed to have its appearance based on its sprite rather than the official artwork.
*The Spiritual Stones are similar to the [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Emerald Chaos Emeralds] from Sega's ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_series Sonic the Hedgehog]'' franchise in that they are large, important gemstones containing powerful energy and it is possible for them to lose it. However, they do not grant Link any special abilities when all are obtained.
