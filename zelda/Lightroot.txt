'''Lightroots''' are structures that are located in [[The Depths]], and are situated directly below [[Shrine of Light|Shrines]] on the surface (with the exception of Turakawak Shrine, as the Kawakarut Lightroot is slightly off). They act as mini-[[Skyview Tower]]s, revealing a small portion of the map while also creating a Fast Travel point when activated. They also create a large light source when activated, reducing the need to use [[Brightbloom Seed]]s when in the surrounding radius. Being in the radius of these structures when activated will also restore the potential to replenish hearts that have been sapped by [[Gloom]]. Names of Lightroots and Shrines are anadromes of each other (the name of a Lightroot is the name of its' corresponding Shrine spelled backwards).

Lightroots are appended by their respective (x, y, z) coordinates (exact location of travel gate).
