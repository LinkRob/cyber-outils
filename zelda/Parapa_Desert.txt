{{Term/Store|Parapa Desert||Series, TAoL}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 224 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref>

==Features==
The place is a wide desert peninsula in the northern region of [[Western Hyrule]] famous for its singular landmark, the [[Parapa Palace]]. A [[Heart Container]] lies deep within the southernmost area of the desert. [[Link]] has to trek through this area on his quest to wake [[Princess Zelda]].

The Parapa Cave is located at the southwest portion of the Parapa Desert. It connects the forest northeast of [[Rauru Town]] with the Parapa Desert. The first time through the cave, it will be dark inside since Link has yet to acquire the [[Candle]].

==Non-canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{CN|-}}===
The Parapa Desert appears in the "[[Quest for the Potion of Power]]" episode of {{CN}}. Interestingly, rocks fly in course with the air all throughout the desert, making the area dangerous to any unprepared visitors. [[Link]], [[Princess Zelda]], and Kevin must journey through the desert during their search to obtain the [[Potion of Power]]. As they are making their way to the [[Parapa Palace]], they encounter a giant [[Geldarm]], which Kevin easily defeats with his Zapper.
{{Noncanon|End}}
