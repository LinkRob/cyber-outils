The {{Term/Store|Knight's Gear|Knight's Gear|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Knight's Gear}} was equipment issued to the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}} who guarded {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. It is known for its reliability and {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}}, qualities for which it still sees use in the wake of the the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Knight's Broadsword}}<br/>Knights of Hyrule once carried this sword. These days it's the weapon of choice for seasoned adventurers thanks to its ease of use and high attack power.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Knight's Claymore}}<br/>Only the most confident of Hyrule Castle's knights carried this two-handed sword. Its cutting edge is finely honed.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Knight's Bow}}<br/>The sturdy metal construction of this bow offers superior durability, while its lack of firing quirks makes it quite reliable. Once favored by the knights at Hyrule Castle.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Knight's Shield}}<br/>A shield favored by the knights who served the Hyrulean royal family. Its sturdy metal construction makes it quite durable, but its weight requires decent skill to wield.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

All pieces of {{Term|BotW|Knight's Gear}} is made of metal, which means they will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. Finally, they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
