{{Term/Store|Jump Strike||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an ability in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Very well. My sixth hidden skill is... The {{Color|TPHD Red|jump strike}}! Let it be hewn into your mind!|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TPHD|Jump Strike}} is the sixth {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill|link}} learned by {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can learn this {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}} by first finding a {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone|link}} to awaken the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Search for the statues that howl with the {{Color|TPHD Red|sound of the wind}}. Seek the sound that calls the spirit of the beast to awaken me again.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref> The {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone}} needed to learn the {{Term|TPHD|Jump Strike}} can be found high on a ledge while climbing up {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak|link}}. After howling with the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}}, the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} instructs {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to find him while in human form.<ref>{{Cite|Let teachings of old pass to you... Take sword in hand and find me...|White Wolf|TPHD}}</ref> The {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} also marks the location of where he can be found on {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Map|link}}. Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} returns to human form, he can find the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} waiting in the back of the {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village Graveyard|link}}, just in front of the hole leading to the grave of {{Term|TPHD|King Zora|link}}. Approaching the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} will return {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to the clouded area where the {{Term|TPHD|Hero's Spirit}} can teach {{Term|TPHD|Link}} the {{Term|TPHD|Jump Strike}}.

This {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}} is an upgraded version of {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s already devastating {{Term|TPHD|Jump Attack|link}}, giving him a greater advantage against a group of enemies. Instead of tapping the Action Button while locked on to an enemy, holding the button will charge the {{Term|TPHD|Jump Attack}} to the upgraded {{Term|TPHD|Jump Strike}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lock on with {{Icon|TPHD ZL}}, then press and hold {{Icon|TPHD A}}. Wait for just the right time to release your strength!|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref> When the A Button is released, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} will jump into the air, swing his {{Term|TPHD|Sword|link}} left and right to knock any enemies in his path, and then slam his {{Term|TPHD|Sword}} into the ground, creating a shockwave that will knock down all enemies in range.<ref>{{Cite|Carefully consider the distance of your enemies, and then focus your power with {{Icon|TP A}}. Then release it!|Hero's Spirit|TP}}</ref>
