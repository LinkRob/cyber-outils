{{Term/Store|Big Skulltula|Big Skulltulas|Series, OoT, OoT3D}} are enemies in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 167}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Big Skulltula}}<br/>Its soft belly is its weak point!
}}

A larger variant of the [[Skulltula]], the [[Big Skulltula]] is found in many places around [[Hyrule]]. Its size makes its attacks have a wider range, it has very similar behavior to that of its smaller counterpart. They can be defeated in several ways however using the [[Hookshot]]  or the [[Fairy Bow]] is the most effective way of killing them.
