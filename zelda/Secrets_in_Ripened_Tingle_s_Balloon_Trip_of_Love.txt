'''Secrets''' are special awards given to [[Tingle]] when he performs certain actions. There are a number of them throughout ''[[Ripened Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love]]'', and most of them require Tingle to revisit previous pages with new items.

#Obtained by giving medicine obtained on [[Page 12]] to Tingle's [[Tingle's Mother|Mother]] and [[Tingle's Father|Father]].
#Obtained by performing a [[Love Push]] on Tingle's Mother, then using the clock key (found on Page 12) on the clock in Tingle's room.

==[[Page 2]]==
#Obtained by returning to the page and speaking with the Fairy Girl.
#Obtained by giving the bag obtained on Page 12 to the red ladybug.

==[[Page 3]]==
#Obtained by giving an ear of roasted corn to the statue at the entrance to [[Peter|Peter's]] farm.
