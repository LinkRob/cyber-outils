{{Term/Store|Animal Village||Series, LA, LADX}} is a location in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 109 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref> It is a small village of [[Animal]]s.

==Overview==
The Animal Village is the home of many anthropomorphic [[animal]]s that live together peacefully. It is located in the southeastern corner of [[Koholint Island]]. The inhabitants consist of [[Rabbit]], [[Christine]], [[Chef Bear]], [[Schule Donavitch]], the [[Hippo Model]], and a [[Zora]].

One of the Rabbits mentions that the Animal Village is a sister city to [[Mabe Village]]. Its inhabitants are unable to go visit Mabe Village because of the uprising in monster sightings and attacks that spanned the area connecting the two [[village]]s together.

All of the talking animals in the village seem to be enamored with the singing voice of [[Marin]], who goes with [[Link]] to sing the [[Ballad of the Wind Fish]] to the inhabitants. When Marin is not there, the animals constantly remind Link that Marin must return someday to sing them another [[song]]. Marin is later finally able to travel to the Animal Village with the young hero, in order to wake the [[Walrus]] blocking a path that Link is required to access in order to continue his adventure.

There is a [[Warp Hole]] in the small pond south of the village.

== Treasure ==
* A [[Piece of Heart]] can be found in a cave outside of the fence in the north-eastern corner of Animal Village. Both [[Bomb]]s and the [[Hookshot]] are required to obtain it.
* The [[Honeycomb]] can be traded for a [[Pineapple]] in the [[Chef Bear]]'s house.
* The [[Hibiscus]] can be traded for a [[Letter]] in [[Christine]]'s house.

==Other appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
[[File:LA (Cagiva) Animal Village.jpg|thumb|Marin at Animal Village from the {{LA|-}} manga by Ataru Cagiva]]
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, after learning the "[[Ballad of the Wind Fish]]" from [[Marin]], [[Link]] decides to help her get to Animal Village so she can sing with her animal friends. Before reaching the village, Link and Marin get attacked by a group of [[Moblin]]s and [[Boarblin]]s While escaping, they find that the bridge leading to Animal Village is missing. Marin then gets attacked by a club, so Link pushes her and gets hit instead, making him fall from the cliff. As the enemy group drew closer, Link asks Marin to jump, catching her within his arms. Shortly after, both arrive to Animal Village.

After gathering all eight [[Instrument of the Sirens|Instruments of the Sirens]], Link decides to stop by at Animal Village to play some music for the animals alongside Marin before going to [[Mt. Tamaranch]].
{{Noncanon|End}}
