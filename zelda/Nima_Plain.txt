{{Term/Store|Nima Plain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Nima Plain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Nima Plain is an expansive, empty meadow located at the base of [[Mount Satori]] in the [[Hyrule Ridge]]. The only notable landmarks present are a dirt road passing through it, and a few nearby [[Tree]]s.

A [[Korok]] can be found by breaking the [[Acorn]]-shaped {{Term|BotW|Vase|link}} in a nook of one of the two Trees in the Plain.
