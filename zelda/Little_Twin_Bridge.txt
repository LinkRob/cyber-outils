{{Term/Store|Little Twin Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Little Twin Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Little Twin Bridge}} is one of two bridges that cross [[Squabble River]] to the east of [[Dueling Peaks]] in [[West Necluda]]. The alternate bridge is the [[Big Twin Bridge]]. This Bridge is smaller than its twin and leads to [[Hickaly Woods]] to the southeast. The Bridge is hinted at in the "{{Term|BotW|Misko, the Great Bandit|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, which can be activated by speaking with {{Term|BotW|Domidak|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Prissen|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The little twin steps over the little river. My cave rests above that river's source.|Domidak|BotW}}</ref>

In [[Master Mode]], there is a sky platform on the southern side of the Bridge being held up by [[Sky Octorok]]s that holds a [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Soldier's Claymore]].
