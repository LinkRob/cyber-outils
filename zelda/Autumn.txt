{{Term/Store|Autumn||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Season|link}} and mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|game=OoS|publisher=Nintendo|page=4, 25}}</ref> {{Term|Series|Autumn}} goes unnamed in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{Term|OoS|Autumn}}, the {{Term|OoS|Overworld|link}} color palette turns an orange hue. Piles of leaves cover {{Plural|OoS|Hole|link}} and function similarly to {{Plural|OoS|Cracked Floor Tile|link}}, allowing access to previously-unreachable areas. {{Plural|OoS|Rock Mushroom|link}} will also become ripe, permitting them to be picked up with the {{Term|OoS|Power Bracelet|link}} or otherwise removed.

It is the fourth {{Term|OoS|Season}} required to be controlled with the {{Term|OoS|Rod of Seasons|link}}, and is granted by the {{Term|OoS|Spirit of Autumn|link}}.
