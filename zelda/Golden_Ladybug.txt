==Location and Uses==
[[File:Ladybug (M).png|left]]
[[File:Ladybug (F).png|left|47px]]
The male Ladybug can be found in the flowers below a platform south of {{Term|TP|Hyrule Castle Town|link}}. The female Ladybug can be found among the trees on the stairs leading into Hyrule Castle Town. They can be given to [[Agitha]] in exchange for different items, such as a bigger [[Wallet]], [[Rupee]]s, or her [[Miiverse Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}. If collected a second time, they will drop a [[Heart]].
