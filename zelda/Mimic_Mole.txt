The '''{{Name|Fan|Mimic Mole}}''' is an enemy in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Mimic Moles appear in the [[Forest of Torian]]. A Mimic Mole pops out the ground, moves around quickly, and then hides itself after two seconds. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can only hit a Mimic Mole while it is outside of the ground. Mimic Moles do not inflict much damage.
