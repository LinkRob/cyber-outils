{{Term/Store|Geldman|Geldmen|Series, ALttP, FSA, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 179 {{Exp Game|ALttP, FSA, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Plural|TLoZ|Geldman}} do not appear in {{TLoZ}}. However, {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} is seen battling one in an artwork referring to {{TLoZ|-}} in {{Guide|ALttP|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP|publisher= Nintendo|page= 5}}</ref>

==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Geldman|This sand-man lives beneath the shifting desert sands. He will jump out at you from the sand and strike at you unexpectedly.}}
{{Stub|ALttP}}

Hiding just beneath the shifting sands, {{Plural|ALttP|Geldman}} in {{ALttP}} quickly surface and pursue when a foe approaches, only to retreat back under the sand. They are surprisingly fast, and can withstand a couple of blows from {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}}'s {{Term|ALttP|Sword|link}}.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Stub|FSA}}
In {{FSA}}, {{Plural|FSA|Geldman}} can get caught in quicksand, where they will then sit in the center of the sinkhole and cause damage to {{Term|FSA|Link}} if he gets pulled in. On the other hand, they can also be destroyed by {{Plural|FSA|Devalant|link}} living in these sinkholes.

They also appear in {{Term|FSA|Tingle's Tower|link}} during the Mole Melee game. Playing the role of the moles, {{Plural|FSA|Geldman}} appear in a field and must be hit with {{Plural|FSA|Hammer|link}}. Certain {{Plural|FSA|Geldman}} will appear in green, red, blue or purple colors and will give the respective {{Term|FSA|Link}} three points for hitting them. Black {{Plural|FSA|Geldman}} will also appear who will explode when hit, stunning {{Term|FSA|Link}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Stub|ALBW}}
In {{ALBW}}, when the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod|link}} is used on {{Plural|ALBW|Geldman}}, it launches them from the sand, revealing legs and stripping them naked of their sand aside from red thong briefs. They then proceed to run away from {{Term|ALBW|Link}}, and can be struck while attempting to escape and will drop {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.
