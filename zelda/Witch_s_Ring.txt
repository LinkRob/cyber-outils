The {{Name|Fan|Witch's Ring}} is an item featured in {{TCT}}. Obtained from the {{Term|TCT|Tree Witch|link}}, the ring is required to open the door that leads to the room guarding the {{Term|TCT|Magnifying Glass}}, which is one of the items necessary to free {{Term|TCT|Link|link}} from the {{Term|TCT|Crystal|link}}.

{{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|link}} can only obtain the {{Term|TCT|Witch's Ring}} if she chooses to stay and eat with the {{Term|TCT|Tree Witch}}. For having had the courage to try the witch's stew, the old woman will reward her with the ring.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='I told you!' the witch laughs. 'Course, not many folks have the courage to try some tree witch stew, but them that do deserve a reward. So here's yours.' [...] 'A ring!' Zelda says. It's a thing band of gold, with an oldy shaped pink crystal set in it. Zelda slides it on her finger. Suddenly she doesn't feel tired at all.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=111}}</ref> Once the princess reaches the {{Term|TCT|Cavern (The Crystal Trap)|display=Cavern|link}}, she will come upon a door that will not open with the {{Term|TCT|Large Brass Key|link}}, and the only way to open it is by inserting the {{Term|TCT|Witch's Ring}} on the keyhole.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='The ring,' Zelda says. 'The one the tree witch gave me!' [...] 'It's the same shape as the keyhole!' [...] The crystal fits smoothly into the keyhole and the door springs open.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=23}}</ref> This will cause the locked door to swing open, allowing the young princess to retrieve the {{Term|TCT|Magnifying Glass}} inside the chamber.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=A thin shaft of light falls from the ceiling onto the tabletop. Something shining there catches Zelda's eye. 'A magnifying glass!' Zelda says, surprised.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=15}}</ref>
