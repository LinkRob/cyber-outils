{{Term/Store|Naydra||Series, BotW}} is a [[dragon]] in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Naydra}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Naydra}}
{{Term|BotW|Naydra}} is one of three roaming {{Plural|BotW|Dragon|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, the others being {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Farosh|link}}. While she cannot be defeated, she does not actively try to harm {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets too close, he will be frozen by the icy aura surrounding her body. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can prevent being frozen by wearing either a full {{Term|BotW|Snowquill Set|link}} that has been enhanced twice by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Zant's Helmet|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Naydra}} will drop different {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots her with a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}. Depending on what part of {{Term|BotW|Naydra}} is hit, she will drop either a {{Term|BotW|Shard of Naydra's Horn|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shard of Naydra's Fang|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Naydra's Claw|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Naydra's Scale|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Naydra's Scale}} can be offered at the Spring of Wisdom to reveal the {{Term|BotW|Jitan Sa'mi Shrine|link}}.

===Location===
{{Term|BotW|Naydra}}, unlike the other dragons, does not roam Hyrule at first. She is first encountered as {{Term|BotW|Malice Naydra|link}} at the [[Spring of Wisdom]]. After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has purified {{Term|BotW|Naydra}} of the {{Term|BotW|Malice}}, {{Term|BotW|Naydra}} roams the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)|link}} and {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}} Regions. However, {{Term|BotW|Naydra}} will not appear if {{Term|BotW|Link}} is too close to her spawn point.
