{{Term/Store|Courage Gem|Courage Gems|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref name="E 120"/>

==Location and Uses==
By collecting 10 {{Plural|PH|Courage Gem}}, the {{Term|PH|Ciela|link|display= Spirit of Courage}} allows {{Term|PH|Link|link}} to shoot {{Plural|PH|Sword Beam|link}}. Getting all 20 doubles the width of the {{Plural|PH|Sword Beam}}. This has no known effect on the Phantoms or Reaplings.
