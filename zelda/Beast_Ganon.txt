'''Beast Ganon''' is a [[Final Smash]] in the {{SSB|Series}}.

==Overview==
[[File:Beast Ganon SSBB charging.png|thumb|left|250px|Ganon charging into [[Captain Falcon]] during his Beast Ganon attack in {{SSBB|-}}]]
Beast Ganon is the Final Smash of [[Ganondorf]] in {{SSBB}}, {{SSB4|3DS}}, and {{SSB4|Wii U}}. It involves Ganondorf transforming into his beast form, Ganon. When the move is activated, Beast Ganon stomps and then charges across the stage. Once this is complete, Ganon transforms back into Ganondorf. If any opponents are charged into, they suffer heavy damage and knockback, and anyone stomped at the start of the Final Smash is stunned temporarily.

A [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|Trophy]] of Beast Ganon appears in {{SSBB|-}} and {{SSB4|Wii U|-}} each.

In {{SSBU}}, the Final Smash is replaced with [[Ganon, The Demon King]], which instead features an iteration of Ganon based on his appearance in {{OoT}}.
