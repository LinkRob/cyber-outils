==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Frozen Trout by freezing [[Chillfin Trout]], [[Sizzlefin Trout]], [[Voltfin Trout]], or [[Stealthfin Trout]]. Their taste is condensed due to being frozen.<ref name="Inventory"/> Eating one restores one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to Link's {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and grants temporary [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"/>
