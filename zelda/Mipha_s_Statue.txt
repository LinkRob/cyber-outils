{{Term/Store|Mipha's Statue||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 399}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
===The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild===
{{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}} is a statue located in the center of the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square|link}}. It depicts {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}, the princess of the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}}, carrying her {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}}. She is standing upon a pedestal, with rocks forming outwards from the tip of her spear.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Laflat|link}} can be found inspecting {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}} for damage.<ref>{{Cite|The damage is minimal...|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her, she asks if he is curious about the {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|display=Statue}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you curious about this statue of Lady Mipha?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> She explains that {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} was formerly the {{Term|BotW|Zora}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}, and she was known to be kind and beautiful.<ref>{{Cite|This is a statue of the former Zora Champion. Her name was Lady Mipha. She was as kind as she was beautiful...|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} had the {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Grace|link|display=power}} to heal wounded soldiers,<ref>{{Cite|They say her ability to heal wounded soldiers was beyong compare.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> and though they are older now, they still remember and love her.<ref>{{Cite|Those soldiers she healed...they are old and decrepit now. But they remember. For this reason, the elders of the domain all love Lady Mipha dearly.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} suggests speaking to the elders to learn more about {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you would like to know more, I suggest you try talking to one of them.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>

During {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s meeting with {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|display=King}} will find out about {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s missing memories.<ref>{{Cite|[I've been sleeping.] Come again? Sleeping, you say... Hm. Is that why you do not seem to remember me?|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[I lost my memory.] Come again? You say you have lost your memory?|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref> Once he finds out this also applies to {{Term|BotW|Mipha}},<ref>{{Cite|I cannot believe it... Have you truly forgotten my dear Mipha as well? You and Mipha were so close... Yet you do not remember her?|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} suggests {{Term|BotW|Link}} look out at her {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|display=Statue}} to try and jog his memory.<ref>{{Cite|Young hero... Please look upon the beauty of Zora's Domain. Do you see that statue? Does gazing upon Mipha's immortalized form not jog your memory?|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref> When this does not work, {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} decides to let {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s memory to come back with time.<ref>{{Cite|Well, perhaps your memory will return in time. I dearly hope so.|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Muzu|link}} storms out of the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}}, he and {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}} can be found arguing with each other in front of {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}}.<ref>{{Cite|Muzu... Please listen...|Sidon|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I don't care what you say!|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} reveals that {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} was in love with {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|He who stands here...the man called Link...is the one whom my sister, Mipha, had feelings for.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Muzu}} refuses to believe it.<ref>{{Cite|You cannot fool me with such a fanciful lie. Not this Zora!|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> He cites {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s missing memories of {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} as proof against this, noting how he does not recognize her when he looks at the {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|display=Statue}}.<ref>{{Cite|The facts are clear. He remembers nothing. Even when he looks upon Princess Mipha's statue...|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> This prompts {{Term|BotW|Link}} to look up at it, which triggers the "{{Term|BotW|Mipha's Touch|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} will periodically stand in front of {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him during this time, he will apologize for being caught in a moment of weakness.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, Link... I am sorry you had to see me in such a moment of weakness.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Laruta|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Keye|link}} will also begin to stand near {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}}, where {{Term|BotW|Keye}} will practice fighting with his {{Term|BotW|Tree Branch|link}} while {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} sings the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪A light scale...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|♪La dee dooo!♪<br/>♪A gift from the sky...<br/>a {{Color|BotW Red|scale of light}}...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the song, she explains that it originates from the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival|link}} and asks if he wants to hear it.<ref>{{Cite|[That song?] I just looooove singing! I even remember the song from the Champion Festival! ...Do you want to hear it?|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to listen, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} will share the full song.<ref>{{Cite|[I want to hear it.] OK! I'm gonna sing it now! The Champion Festival Song!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> The song details a "scale of light" splitting the feet of a sight at {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls|link}}, which will reveal a trial.<ref>{{Cite|♪A gift from the sky...<br/>a {{Color|BotW Red|scale of light}}...♪<br/>♪Splits the feet...<br/>of a {{Color|BotW Red|Veiled Falls}} sight...♪<br/>♪Your {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} awaits...<br/>It's glowing bright...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> This initiates "{{Term|BotW|The Ceremonial Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Keye}}, he laments not being able to jump high enough to read the {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|display=Statue's}} pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|But I can't even jump high enough to reach Lady Mipha's {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}}.|Keye|BotW}}</ref> He remarks that it seems familiar to him and wonders if there was a similar one at {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls}}.<ref>{{Cite|Urgh, that {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}}! I feel like I've seen it somewhere before... Was there a pedestal at {{Color|BotW Blue|Veiled Falls}}?|Keye|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Keye}} wishes to jump high enough to be able to pierce it.<ref>{{Cite|I want to be able to jump high enough to {{Color|BotW Red|pierce}} that {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}}...|Keye|BotW}}</ref>

===The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom===
Mipha's Statue is no longer found in the plaza as it has been replaced by a statue of Sidon and Link. It has been relocated to Ploymus Rock where it is named Mipha Court.
