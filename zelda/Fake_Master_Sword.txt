{{Term/Store|Fake Master Sword|Fake Master Swords|Series, ALttP|plural}} are objects in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP&FS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 128}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Several Fake Master Swords can be found in the [[Lost Woods]]. They are decoys placed in there to prevent {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} from finding the real {{Term|ALttP|Master Sword|link}}. Although these blades are stuck into a pedestal, they can be distinguished in that they resemble the [[Sword|Fighter's Sword]] and are slightly smaller than the Blade of Evil's Bane. They can be lifted and thrown at enemies much like when throwing a {{Term|ALttP|Pot|link}}.

In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of {{ALttP|-}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Fake Master Sword}} are used to complete the [[Lumberjack's Riddles]]; however, they disappear if picked up and thrown, so destroying them all makes impossible to complete the Lumberjack's Riddles.{{Verify|ALttP}}
