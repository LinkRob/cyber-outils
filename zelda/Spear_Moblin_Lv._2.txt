'''Spear Moblin Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Spear Moblin Lv. 2|This Moblin throws spears relentlessly, keeping foes at bay. Its arcing throws can reach even distant enemies.<ref name="EoW Spear Moblin Lv. 2">{{Cite|This Moblin throws spears relentlessly, keeping foes at bay. Its arcing throws can reach even distant enemies.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Spear Moblin Lv. 2 are stronger, blue-skinned versions of regular [[Spear Moblin|Spear Moblins]]. They throw spears from greater distances and often appear in Monster Camps beside normal ones. When summoned as an Echo, they cost four [[Echo]] energy to summon.
