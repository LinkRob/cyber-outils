{{Term/Store|Magic Ring|Magic Rings|Series, OoS, OoA|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 130 {{Exp Game|OoS, OoA}}}}</ref> also known as '''Magical Rings''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 87 {{Exp Game|OoS, OoA}}}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
Magical Rings can be found all around [[Holodrum]] and [[Labrynna]]. They are made from magical seeds.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= |game= OoS|page= 8}}</ref> Some of them can be obtained randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], through a Ring Fortune, bought in shops, or won in minigames. Magical Rings must be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s before they can be worn.  The rest of them will be stored by Vasu and if a duplicated Ring is obtained, [[Vasu]] will buy it for 30 Rupees. [[Link]] can initially hold up a single Magical Ring in his [[Ring Box]], which can be upgraded to hold up to five of them. Magical Rings can be transferred between [[Linked Game]]s by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].

When worn, Magical Rings grant Link different abilities, such as increased attack or defense. Some Rings are simply collectables and have no effect when worn. Only a single Ring can be worn at a time. A total of 64 different Magical Rings are available in both games.
