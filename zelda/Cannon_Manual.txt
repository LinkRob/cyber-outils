The {{Term/Store|Cannon Manual||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an {{Term|TPHD|Object|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TP Green|--Cannon Manual--}}<br/>Insert.....iron....continue.....put in.......explosion......launch.|Cannon Manual|TP}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TPHD|Cannon Manual}} is a poster hanging on the wall of a room in the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins|link}}. It is found near a {{Term|TPHD|Cannon|link}}, a {{Term|TPHD|Cannonball|link}}, several blocks of {{Term|TPHD|Ice|link}}, and many {{Plural|TPHD|Mini Freezard|link}}. It is meant to provide instructions on how to operate a {{Term|TPHD|Cannon}}, but it is rendered difficult to read in its ruined state. Only a few key {{Term|TPHD|Hylian (Language)|link}} words remain, just enough to clue {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} into the basics of {{Term|TPHD|Cannon}} operation. On the {{Term|TPHD|Cannon Manual}} is a drawing depicting a firing {{Term|TPHD|Cannon}}.
