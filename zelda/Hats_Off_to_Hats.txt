"{{Term/Store|Hats Off to Hats||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: Hats Off to Hats}}<br/>7/12/2022 |author= Nintendo |published= July 12, 2022 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/6e6b296f/index.html |title= Event: Hats Off to Hats |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Hats Off to Hats}}" is an event wherein a greater number of hatted {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits with snazzy hats are stylin' their way onto the Spirit Board in this event!|author= Nintendo |published= July 12, 2022 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/6e6b296f/index.html |title= Event: Hats Off to Hats |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
