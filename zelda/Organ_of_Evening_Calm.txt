The {{Term/Store|Organ of Evening Calm||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a quest item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 133"/>

==Location and Uses==
The Organ of Evening Calm is one of the eight [[Instruments of the Sirens]] needed to awaken the [[Wind Fish]].<ref name="E 133"/> It can be found at the end of [[Eagle's Tower]] after defeating its boss, [[Evil Eagle]].<ref name="E 133"/>
