The {{Term/Store|Green Clothes|Green Clothes|Series, ALttP, LA, LADX}},{{Exp Game|ALttP, LA}}<ref name="E 126">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126{{Exp Game|ALttP, LA, ALBW}}}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Green Tunic||ALBW}},{{Exp Game|ALBW}}<ref name="E 126"/> is [[Link]]'s trademark outfit and therefore the default [[Clothes|clothing]] in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] and other media, with the exception of {{BotW}} and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|sequel]], as in those games he instead wears the [[Champion's Tunic]]. Because of this, it has become an important symbol of the hero who saved [[Hyrule]] from evil, similar to the [[Master Sword]] and [[Hylian Shield]].<ref>{{Cite|Look at your awakened form... The green tunic that is your garb once belonged to the ancient hero chosen by the gods... His power is yours. His is the true power that slept within you. Your name is [Link]. You are the hero chosen by the gods.|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|On a certain island, it became customary to garb boys in green when they came of age. Clothed in the green of fields, they aspired to find heroic blades and cast down evil. The elders wished only for the youths to know courage like the hero of legend...|N/A|TWW}}</ref> Despite the Green Tunic's heroic role in the franchise's story, like the standard boots and being his regular outfit, it is rather basic in gameplay. Therefore, unlike the other types of clothes ([[Blue Mail]], [[Goron Tunic]], etc.), it does not grant Link any special abilities. In some games, such as {{TP}} and {{SS}}, he wore a chain mail underneath his tunic to further protect himself.

Due to this garb's transcending generations, the Green Clothes are known by different names, including '''Green Jerkin''', '''[[Kokiri Tunic]]''', '''[[Hero's Clothes]]''', '''[[Recruit Uniform]]''', '''[[Knight's Uniform]]''', and '''[[Hero's Tunic]]'''.

===Wild Set===
{{Main|Wild Set}}
The Green Clothes in {{BotW}} appear as the Wild Set. It is comprised of the [[Tunic of the Wild]], [[Trousers of the Wild]] and [[Cap of the Wild]]. This Set is a reward for completing all 120 [[Ancient Shrine]]s. The Tunic retains the stitching from the original design of Link. These Green Clothes are unique in that they are not obtained near the beginning of Link's adventure.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, at the end of [[The Armies of Ruin]], Link discovers that Zelda is missing after [[Wizzro]]'s attack on the castle and the rampaging of [[King Dodongo]]. Running back to the entrance of the castle, before he was ambushed by [[Bokoblin]]s, Impa returns the favor after Link saves her from [[Volga]]'s wrath by stopping the ambush and then hands him the Hero's Clothes showing complete worth and trust in him to be the Hero Hyrule needs.

==Trivia==
*[[Link]]'s green tunic/hat, much like [[Mario]]'s signature [[mariowiki:Mario's_Cap|red cap]] and overalls from the ''[[mariowiki:Mario_(franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise, is one of the most widely recognized character outfits in the video game industry.
*According to [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'' series]]' creator [[Shigeru Miyamoto]], alongside primarily giving Link his [[wikipedia:Elves_in_fiction|elf]]-like appearance, the design of the iconic garb is based on [https://disney.fandom.com/wiki/Peter_Pan_(character) Disney's version of Peter Pan], as he himself loved the [https://disney.fandom.com/wiki/Peter_Pan_(film) 1953 animated film].
*Like the other related tunics (red, blue, etc.), the shade of green on Link's traditional outfit is inconsistent across the franchise or other media, especially in 2D games. In some installments, the grab is at a darker or lighter color than pure green.[[File:OoT Child and Adult Link together.png|thumb|242x242px|Link's Green Clothes seen on both his child and adult stages in {{OoT|-}}]]
*The Green Clothes are implied to be one-size-fits-all. In {{OoT}}, when Link awakens from his deep seven-year slumber to become the legendary [[Hero of Time]] after pulling the [[Master Sword]], he is already shown wearing it on-screen at a seemingly larger size to match his age. However, similar to the [[Hylian Shield]] in-game, this is likely just a gameplay mechanic of itself being available by default, as there is no such description anywhere in the series.
*The [[Kokiri]] are known to wear similar clothing as Link's main tunic.
*As the standard Green Tunic does not grant Link any [[Defense]], in spite of its heroic history, it proves dangerous to wear when fighting against stronger [[Enemy|enemies]] and [[Boss|bosses]] in certain games, including [[Ganon]], who can inflict as many as eight [[Heart Container|Heart Containers]] in {{aLttP}}, for example.
**An exception exists in {{LA}}, where acquiring the [[Guardian Acorn]] doubles Link's defense, although only temporarily.
*Due to the hardware limitations of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]], in {{TLoZ}}, some characters, items, and even enemies share the same color palette as Link's Green Clothes. These include the hilt of the [[Sword]], [[Arrow|Arrows]], [[Princess Zelda]]'s dress, [[Merchant]]s, the whiskers of the [[Pols Voice]], and even Link's own eyes. When the hero obtains the [[Blue Ring|Blue]] and [[Red Ring]], they will also change correspondingly.
**Likewise, in {{OoT|-}}, the clothing of the [[Gerudo Warrior]] at [[Gerudo's Fortress]] also shares the same palette as Link's outfit and therefore will change to match the [[Goron Tunic|Goron]] and [[Zora Armor|Zora]] Tunics. In the original v1.0/v1.1 NTSC release, due to a bug, whenever Link uses a [[Bomb]] or [[Bombchu]] while she is on-screen, her own outfit flashes correspondingly until it explodes. This is fixed in the PAL and subsequent versions.
*Although Link technically wears the Green Tunic in {{LA|-}}, due to the graphical limitations of the [[Game Boy]], it is shown as black and white.
*The character [[Gramps]] from {{aLBW}} is known to wear the Green Clothes (although actually the [[Red Mail]], but not shown in gameplay).
*The Green Tunic is the only outfit that cannot be stolen from a [[Like Like]].
*Though the Green Tunic in {{OoT|-}} and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|remake]], known in-game as Kokiri Tunic, does have a model, it is impossible to see it under normal circumstances, unlike the Goron and Zora Tunics.[[File:TWW Medli reaction to Green Clothes.png|thumb|238x238px|Medli reacting to Link's Green Clothes in {{TWW}}]]
*With Link's tunic and floppy hat being distinctively green identical to an elf's, some NPC characters in various games express interest in his outfit such as [[Kafei]], [[Medli]], [[The Great Deku Tree|Great Deku Tree]], and more.
**[[Romani]] in {{MM}} is arguably the most famous example of this case, as she gives Link the nickname "Grasshopper". 
**Even fans sometimes call Link an "elf-boy" due to his appearance.
*The [[Player|BS-X avatar characters]] who are the protagonists of the Japan-exclusive [[Satellaview]] games, ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda]]'' and ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|Ancient Stone Tablets]]'', share Link's tunic but not his hat.
*In the [[Wii U]] exclusive [[The Legend of Zelda Zone]] DLC for ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_Lost_World Sonic Lost World]'' of Sega's ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_series Sonic the Hedgehog]'' franchise, its [[Sonic the Hedgehog|titular main protagonist]] can be seen dressed entirely like Link excluding his weapons.
*Most of Link's [[Transformation|transformations]] in the series retain this clothing, sometimes only the hat and shorts.[[File:MLP FiM Link Pony Screenshot.png|thumb|215x215px|An example of a Green Tunic-like clothing seen in pop culture, taken from ''[https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/My_Little_Pony_Friendship_is_Magic My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic]''. Note the the Pony character resembles Link and is pulling [[Rupee|Rupees]]]]
*Link's standard tunic, similarly to the character himself and the Master Sword, due to its popularity, is often referenced in [[The Legend of Zelda in Popular Culture|popular culture]].
