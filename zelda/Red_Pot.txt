The {{Term/Store|Red Pot|Red Pots|Series, PH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Break a {{Color|PH Red|red pot}} to spill its magical contents on the floor. Stand in the puddle to conceal yourself...|Skeleton|PH}}</ref> {{Plural|ST|Red Pot}} go unnamed in {{ST}}.{{Verify}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
{{Plural|PH|Red Pot}} are found throughout the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}}. They contain small puddles of {{Plural|PH|Safe Zone|link}} that cover the ground where they are thrown. If {{Term|PH|Link|link}} exits the room and returns after breaking one, the {{Term|PH|Safe Zone}} created will be gone and the {{Term|PH|Red Pot}} returns to its original position.

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Plural|ST|Red Pot}} are found throughout the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits|link}} and the {{Term|ST|Lost at Sea Station|link}}. They contain small puddles of {{Plural|ST|Safe Zone}} that cover the ground where they are thrown. If {{Term|ST|Link}} exits the room and returns after breaking one, the {{Term|ST|Safe Zone}} created will be gone and the {{Term|ST|Red Pot}} returns to its original position.
