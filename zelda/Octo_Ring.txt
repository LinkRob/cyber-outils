The {{Term/Store|Octo Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Octo Ring<br/>Become an Octorok|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Octo Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. In {{OoS}}, the Ring can also be found inside a cave on the water in [[Holodrum Plain]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be obtained by scoring 350 points at the [[Shooting Gallery]] in [[Lynna Village]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it transforms [[Link]] into an [[Octorok]].<ref name="Description"/> While in this shape, Link cannot use items nor can be eaten by [[Like Like]]s. The Octo Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
