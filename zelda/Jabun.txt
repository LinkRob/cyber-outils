{{Term/Store|Jabun||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a major character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> He is thought to be a descendant of [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 127}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Jabun}}
Jabun is a giant fish known as the Water Spirit who used to live at [[Greatfish Isle]]. [[Ganondorf]] went to his island to destroy it and presumably kill Jabun, but Jabun himself sensed the evil [[Gerudo]] warlock coming and fled to a cave hidden in [[Outset Island]].<ref name="Abode"/> Like [[Valoo]], he can only speak [[Hylian (Language)|Ancient Hylian]]. Knowing of his whereabouts and seeking the third pearl, the [[King of Red Lions]] and [[Link]], the Hero of Winds, sailed to his new home. Although Jabun at first hesitated to hand over [[Nayru's Pearl]] to one who had no connection to the legendary [[Hero of Time]], the Water Spirit relented and gave the final pearl to Link, leaving it up to the gods to test the true courage of the young hero.<ref>{{Cite|You got Nayru's Pearl! The water spirit Jabun was kind enough to give you this jewel, a treasure of one of the goddesses!|N/A|TWW}}</ref> <ref>{{Cite|Promise? You suggest that I leave the fate of Hyrule up to mere chance?|Jabun|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I see. Then I suppose it is up to the gods to deem whether his courage is true.|Jabun|TWW}}</ref>

Since Jabun only speaks Ancient Hylian, Link is not able to understand him (though the player can in the [[Second Quest]] as it becomes translated). Jabun even calls the King of Red Lions by his title of King [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]], but Link himself is unaware of this statement.<ref>{{Cite|Well met, Hyrule King!|Jabun|TWW}}</ref> He also was aware of [[Princess Zelda]] (from {{OoT}})'s [[Tetra|descendant's]] existence, though not necessarily her true identity, as he asked the King of Red Lions if he has found her<ref>{{Cite|Tell me, Hyrule King... Have you learned the whereabouts of the one who carries the bloodline of the princess, Zelda?|Jabun|TWW}}</ref> and he confirmed it<ref>{{Cite|...I believe I have.|The King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> with Jabun requesting to protect her from Ganondorf before leaving,<ref>{{Cite|That is well. You must protect Zelda. She cannot be permitted to fall into the hands of Ganon.|Jabun|TWW}}</ref> though Link himself didn't understand the conversation between them.

==Trivia==
*Jabun's theme is a remix of the music played [[Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly]] in {{OoT}}.
*Despite having lived on Greatfish Isle, his [[Figurine]] is placed in the room of Outset Island characters, as he moves and stays there during the rest of the game.
*{{Guide|TWW|Prima}} states that Jabun was once known as [[Lord Jabu-Jabu|Jabu-Jabu]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page=26}}</ref> Given {{HH}}<nowiki/>'s claim that Jabun is more likely a descendant and not actually the character from {{OoT|-}}, this is not thought to be canon.
*Jabun has slight resemblance to the [[Angler Fish]] boss from {{LA}}.
*Inside the files of {{TWW|-}} are two audio clips of a [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/WW_New/WW_Jabun_Distress1.wav screaming] [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/WW_New/WW_Jabun_Distress2.wav noise] Jabun was originally intended to make, implying himself in agonizing distress. While they are never used in connection to him in the final game, a [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MC/MC_BoundChest_Spirits_Scream.wav sped-up version of the latter] is instead heard after obtaining the [[Triforce Chart]] onboard the [[Ghost Ship]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Strangely a sped-up version of the sound effect is actually used in-game when Link collects the Triforce Chart from the Ghost Ship.|author= Beta64|published= March 18, 2013|retrieved= March 26, 2014|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBToXMXJh1o&t=260s|title= Beta64 - Wind Waker|site= YouTube|type= video}}</ref>
**The sped-up eerie scream is also used for {{TMC}} when [[Vaati]], its main antagonist, opens the [[Bound Chest]].
*Within the code of {{TMC|-}}, three unused panes of [[Stained Glass]] exist; one of them depicts Jabun.
