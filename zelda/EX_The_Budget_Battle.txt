{{Term/Store|EX The Budget Battle||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 70 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX The Budget Battle|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the second wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian of Remembrance|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX The Budget Battle}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} must present their research findings to {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam|link}} by defeating {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} under certain conditions. Their first task is to attack a {{Term|HWAoC|Meteo Wizzrobe|link}} with ice three times. An {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}} is also present at the start of the mission, and can be defeated to drop energy for the {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Rod|link}}. After three ice attacks are used, the objectives becomes to defeat the {{Term|HWAoC|Meteo Wizzrobe}}. When it is defeated, a large {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}} spawns, and the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' goal is to defeat it with a {{Term|HWAoC|Weak Point Smash|link}}. The next two objectives are to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} using a {{Term|HWAoC|Special Attack|link}} and to defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Guardian|link}} using {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}}. Their final goal is to defeat {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}}, and after doing so, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. 

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be only cleared using {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Truffle|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, fifteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Sanke Carp|link}}, 5,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
