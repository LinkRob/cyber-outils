The {{Term/Store|Drenan Highlands||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Drenan Highlands'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Drenan Highlands are a stretch of land along the western range of the [[Eldin Mountains]]. They lie at the northern flank of [[Hyrule]] at the Mountains' base. The Highlands may be entered through a passage in the western Mountains, which lies at the northern road between the [[Hebra Mountains]] and [[Great Hyrule Forest]].

The Drenan Highlands shares its landscape with the [[Deplian Badlands]] further east, in that it is largely barren and has rugged terrain. Very little vegetation grow here aside for a few patches of grass, flowers, and shrubs; the latter of which can be cut down to produce [[Tree Branch]]es. In spite of its scarce landscape, the Highlands, along with the entire Eldin Mountains stretch, is home to numerous [[Creature]]s.

Most of the Highlands' greenery is condensed in its core, where a small meadow lies. To the east of the meadow is a dirt path lined by several stone monuments. The path leads to the entrance of the [[Typhlo Ruins]] in the Great Hyrule Forest. A small wood south of this pathway bears a fallen log, which hides a nut at one end. Destroying the nut will reveal a [[Korok]]. Along the cliffside east of the [[Lynel]] prowling the Highlands, Link can find an orange [[Pinwheel]]. Upon approaching the Pinwheel, three [[Balloon]]s will spawn and fly around. Destroying the Balloons will reveal a Korok, who will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
