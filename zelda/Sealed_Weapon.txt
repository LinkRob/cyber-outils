{{Term/Store|Sealed Weapon|Sealed Weapons|Series, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are {{Plural|HWDE|Item|link}} found in {{HW}}.<ref name="Prima">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 8}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Obtaining a {{Term|HWDE|Sealed Weapon}} will unlock a new {{Term|HWDE|Weapon|link}} category or {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}} level for the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior|link}} it belongs to, after finishing the {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} it is found in. {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Weapon}} appear as translucent, shimmering crystals containing small swords. {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Weapon}} are found throughout {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode|link}} and {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}, within {{Plural|HWDE|Treasure Chest|link}} found within {{Plural|HWDE|Scenario}} in {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode}}, and within {{Plural|HWDE|Treasure Chest}} given as rewards for an A-Rank Victory, or a Battle Victory if the {{Term|HWDE|Sealed Weapon}} unlocks a Level 1 {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}, in certain battles in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode}}.<ref name="Prima"/> These {{Plural|HWDE|Treasure Chest}} can be opened by any {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} but often battles which contain them are restricted to the {{Term|HWDE|Weapon}}'s future owner.
