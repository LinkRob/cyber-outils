{{Term/Store|Mini-Ghini|Mini-Ghinis|Series, OoA|plural}} are {{Plural|OoA|Enemy|link}} in {{OoA}}.<ref name="BradyGames 8"/>

==Characteristics==
As their name implies, {{Plural|OoA|Mini-Ghini}} are a miniature form of the standard {{Term|OoA|Ghini|link}}. {{Plural|OoA|Mini-Ghini}} are only present during the battle with the {{Term|OoA|Mini-Boss|link}} {{Term|OoA|Giant Ghini|link}}, in the {{Term|OoA|Spirit's Grave|link}}. They act as a defensive shield for the larger {{Term|OoA|Ghini}} by taking blows from {{Term|OoA|Link|link}}'s {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}} for it. They can be easily dispatched by {{Term|OoA|Link}}, but reappear shortly afterward. Should they come into contact with him, they will slow {{Term|OoA|Link}} down in the same manner a {{Term|OoA|Gel|link}} would. They are unable to directly attack or harm {{Term|OoA|Link}}.
