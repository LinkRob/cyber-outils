{{Term/Store|Nayru's Flame||Series, SS}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nayru's Flame has strengthened this blade, increasing its dowsing capabilities.|N/A|SS}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|SSHD|Nayru's Flame}} is one of the three {{Plural|Series|Sacred Flame|link}}, protected by the dragon {{Term|SSHD|Lanayru (Dragon)|link}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Surface is divided into three regions. Each region is named after one of the dragons, Faron, Eldin, and Lanayru, who protect the Sacred Flames.|book=HH|page=74}}</ref>, and intended to enhance the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword|link}} with the {{Term|SSHD|Force|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=All the while, Link continued with his task of tempering the Goddess Sword in the Sacred Flames of the Golden Goddesses. Imbued with the immense, sacred power of the Force, the Goddess Sword was transformed into the Master Sword.|book=HH|page=75}}</ref>

In order to awaken the {{Term|SSHD|Gate of Time|link}}, {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} must empower the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword}} with all three {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Flame}};<ref>{{Cite|Ah, sorry to disappoint you, boy, but for now your sword lacks the power necessary to awaken the gate. First, you and your sword must grow together.|Impa|SS}}</ref> in order to obtain them, he must pass the trials found in the {{Plural|SSHD|Silent Realm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The sacred flames are three in number. To obtain them, you must also earn relics known as the three sacred gifts. For each trial you overcome, you shall be blessed with one of the gifts...|Fi|SS}}</ref>

{{Term|SSHD|Nayru's Flame}} is kept within the {{Term|SSHD|Sandship|link}} in the {{Term|SSHD|Lanayru Province|link}},<ref>{{Cite|This is Nayru's Flame, vrrm! Just looking at it makes my circuits hum, vweeeeep. In fact, the protection of the flame is what allowed us to fix the ship so quickly, brrzrrt!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> which was once protected and helmed by {{Term|SSHD|LD-301N Skipper|link}} before pirates stole it.<ref>{{Cite|It was the day of the storm, phoo-weep... My crew and I were navigating the seas as usual, vrrm... And then those brutes, bzzt... the pirates, suddenly attacked us, brrzt. They were after Nayru's Flame, zzpt. My crew was imprisoned...vrrrt... and I was thrown into the sea!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> It enhances the {{Term|SSHD|Dowsing|link}} abilities of the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword}}, allowing {{Term|SSHD|Fi|link}} to search for {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}}, {{Plural|SSHD|Treasure|link}}, {{Plural|SSHD|Gratitude Crystal|link}}, and {{Plural|SSHD|Goddess Cube|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The flames of Nayru have transformed your blade, expanding Fi's powers. She can now help you use your dowsing ability to search for more objects!|N/A|SS}}</ref> Once {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} acquires it, the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Longsword|link}} is transformed into the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess White Sword|link}}.
