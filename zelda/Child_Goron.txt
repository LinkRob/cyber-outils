==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, {{Plural|PH|Child Goron}} reside on {{Term|PH|Goron Island|link}}, where they are almost as numerous as their adult counterparts. {{Plural|PH|Child Goron}} exhibit the same tribal pride and manners of speaking as grown-up {{Plural|PH|Goron}},<ref>{{Cite|You have put your foot on {{Color|PH Blue|Goron Island}}, outsider. Make yourself known to all. We Gorons are proud, and we do not play with strangers.|Child Goron|PH}}</ref> but they have a predilection toward playing as well as a variety of personality traits.<ref>{{Cite|So {{Color|PH Blue|Gongoron}} got away, [{{Color|PH Blue|Goro-Link}}]? I saw him head east! Catch him quick so you can play with me next!|Child Goron|PH}}</ref> One {{Term|PH|Child Goron}} seems annoyed by his father's obsession with with {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link|display=Linebeck's Ship}},<ref>{{Cite|Your face is new to me, outsider. Have you come from the ship? My father is staring at it. That bothers me. If only someone would tell him...|Child Goron|PH}}</ref> and another one rewards {{Term|PH|Link|link}} with a {{Term|PH|Treasure Chart|link}} for defeating a group of {{Plural|PH|Yellow Chuchu|link}} which have appeared on a nearby hill.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, outsider! I am relieved that those strange creatures are gone! You are a good stranger, outsider. So please accept this token of my thanks!|Child Goron|PH}}</ref> Some readily welcome {{Term|PH|Link}} to their homeland,<ref>{{Cite|You want to come over here, outsider? I will put the bridge out for you. Now you can cross anytime you want. No toll, of course!|Child Goron|PH}}</ref> with several even willing to call the young hero "Brother" before he has officially joined the tribe,<ref>{{Cite|You there! I have some useful information for you, Brother! Give a {{Color|PH Green|shout}} to the guy nearby when crossing right here.{{Icon|PH DS}} Remember to let your voice bellow, Brother. Shout to him like you mean it!|Child Goron|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Busy, busy, so busy! I have no idea where I should even start, Brother!|Child Goron|PH}}</ref> while others are more hesitant to trust him,<ref>{{Cite|Make yourself known to each and every Goron first. We do not play otherwise.|Child Goron|PH}}</ref> including the {{Term|PH|Biggoron|link|display=island chief}}'s son, {{Term|PH|Gongoron|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We will never recognize you as a true brother of the Goron tribe, stranger! Do you really want our {{Color|PH Red|pure metal}}?! Then just try to keep up with me!|Gongoron|PH}}</ref> {{Plural|PH|Child Goron}} also appear on {{Term|PH|Dee Ess Island|link}}, where they help to set up and manage the {{Term|PH|Goron Game|link}}.

While the average maturation period of a {{Term|PH|Child Goron}} is unknown, one particular {{Term|PH|Child Goron}} is shown to grow into a full-sized {{Term|PH|Goron}} by the time {{Term|PH|Link}} has completed the {{Term|PH|Goron Temple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|An outsider! Have you ever heard of a wood heart? I love them so much! We Gorons eat the small wood hearts in order to grow into big Gorons!|Child Goron|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hear that you are leaving, [Goro-Link]. Well, I plan on eating many wood hearts while you are away. I will grow so big!|Goron|PH}}</ref>

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Main|Goron Child}}
Several {{Plural|ST|Child Goron}} appear in {{ST}}. One is the {{Term|ST|Goron Elder|link}}'s grandson, who is known simply as the {{Term|ST|Goron Child|link}}. After {{Term|ST|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|ST|Fire Temple|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Goron Child}} asks {{Term|ST|Link}} to take him to {{Term|ST|Castle Town|link}}, as he would like to experience life in the "big city" and hopefully meet a {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link|display=Princess}} in-person.<ref>{{Cite|This may seem a little random, but I have made up my mind. I am tired of living in the sticks, so I am moving to the big city.|Goron Child|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The city is where the castle is. And the castle is where the princess lives. See what I am getting at?|Goron Child|ST}}</ref>

A different {{Term|ST|Child Goron}} manages the {{Term|ST|Goron Target Range|link}} just west of {{Term|ST|Goron Village|link}}. Another is held captive at the {{Term|ST|Pirate Hideout|link}}, while his friend waits outside the Hideout's entrance to request help from {{Term|ST|Link}}; these two {{Plural|ST|Child Goron}} appear to perpetually swap places whenever the captive one is rescued.

==={{BotW|-}}===
Several {{Plural|BotW|Child Goron}} appear in {{BotW}}, including {{Term|BotW|Axyl|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dugby|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Offrak|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pelison|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Slergo|link}}.
