The {{Term/Store|Exchange Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Exchange Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Exchange Ruins are located in [[Hyrule Field]].

Atop a keep in the southwest portion of the Ruins, Link can find a stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Soldier's Claymore]]. A metallic Treasure Chest containing a [[Purple Rupee]] can be found embedded in the pond east of the aforementioned keep. Link can find a second stone Treasure Chest containing a bundle of 10 [[Arrow]]s inside the ruined building in the northern portion of the Ruins. In the "[[EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes]]" Side Quest exclusive to [[The Master Trials]] DLC Pack, [[Tingle's Hood]] can be found inside a metallic Treasure Chest stuck in the ground at the center of the Ruins.

A [[Korok]] can be seen disguised as [[Leaves with Fairy Dust]] running around the Ruins. If [[Link]] is able to catch him, he will be rewarded with a [[Korok Seed]].
