The Trial of the Ancients is the final [[Side Quest|side quest]] that needs to be completed in order to unlock the [[Characters in Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|character]] [[Monk Maz Koshia]].

This can be unlocked once the side quests, [[The Trial of Stones]], [[The Trial of the Mighty Foe]], and [[The Trial of Monsters]] are completed. Once those are completed, this side quest will appear in the [[Gerudo Desert]].

The Trial of Ancients represents a [[stage]] similar to a [[Ancient Shrine|Shrine]]. The player will need to defeat Monk Maz Koshia in within 3 minutes.

If completed on the first try, the character will get 1 [[Ethereal Stone]], 2 [[Star Fragment|Star Fragments]], 1 [[Diamond]], and a random [[Weapons in Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|weapon]], along with unlocking Monk Maz Koshia. If completed with more than one try, the character will still earn Monk Maz Koshia as a character.
