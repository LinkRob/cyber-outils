The {{Term/Store|Zora Mask||Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, CoH}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 143 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|CoH Yellow|Zora Mask}}!<br/>This mask can be worn by {{Icon|CoH Skull Kid}}. It resembles the face of a Zora.|N/A|CoH}}</ref> It resembles the face of a [[Zora]].

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
The {{Term|OoT3D|Zora Mask}} can be borrowed from the [[Happy Mask Shop]] after completing the [[Mask Trading Sequence]] in {{OoT}}. It has no function other than to change peoples' reactions when [[Link]] talks to them. When shown to the princess of the Zoras, [[Princess Ruto|Ruto]], she will express her happiness that the young [[Hero of Time]] is ready to become a Zora himself and then asks would he like to marry her now (no option would appear, however).<ref>{{Cite|All right! You actually decided to become a Zora! Are you actually ready to marry me now?|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
The Zora Mask in {{MM}} is one of five [[mask]]s used for [[transformation]].<ref>{{Cite|When you wear it, you will transform into the shape you just were.|Happy Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> When worn, it transforms Link into [[Zora Link]]. The mask contains the spirit of the fallen Zoran hero [[Mikau]], and when the [[Hylian]] hero dons the Mask, he inhabits the Zora guitarist's body, becoming [[Zora Link]]. With the transformation comes all the special abilities of that species, including a new fighting style, the ability to swiftly swim underwater, two boomerang-like fins to attack, and an electric barrier shield.

Link acquires the mask while visiting [[Great Bay]], where he notices a Zora near death floating in the water surrounded by [[seagull]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, what do you think that is? Out there in the bay, beneath where the gulls are flying... Can't you see it?|Tatl|MM}}</ref> Once Link helps the Zora to shore, he tells him that he is Mikau, guitarist for the famous {{Term|MM3D|Zora}} band {{Term|MM3D|The Indigo-Go's|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Uuungh...I am Mikau of the Zora people......guitarist in the Zora band... I think this is it for me... My final message...|Mikau|MM}}</ref> He sings a song explaining that he was trying to help [[Lulu]], the [[Terminan]] counterpart of Princess Ruto and lead singer of the band, recover her seven [[Zora Egg|Zora Eggs]] that were stolen from her by [[Gerudo Pirate]]s, but was fatally injured in the process.<ref>{{Cite|But that girl, our vocalist... She laid some strange eggs. And she's lost her voice, You can't hear what she says. Whoa-oh! In Great Bay now somethin' is a-happenin'. Is it now? Oh! Baby, listen to me. I don't wanna beg. Gerudo Pirates! They stole that girl's eggs. I went to stop the Gerudo Pirates, then pow and bam! I got knocked down, and here I am!|Mikau|MM}}</ref> Full of regret, he cannot die in peace, and asks someone to heal his soul.<ref>{{Cite|Baby! If I die like this... Even if I die... It won't be in peace... That's for suuure! Somebody, please rescue her eggs before the pirates take their toll. Oh, somebody, somebody, please heal my soul.|Mikau|MM}}</ref> By playing the "[[Song of Healing]]," Link can rest Mikau's soul, leaving behind the Zora Mask.

==Other appearances==

==={{ALttP|-}} (Ishinomori)===
[[File:Zora's Mask (comic).png|thumb|right|90px|Zora's Mask in the comic]]
In the {{ALttP|Ishinomori}}, {{Term|ALttP|Zora}} grants Link the Zora's Mask when he first meets the hero. After Link mentions that he is going to the [[Palace of Darkness]] to rescue the imprisoned [[Maiden]]s, Zora takes one of his scales and places it on Link's face, calling it Zora's Mask, which is meant to disguise the hero's face and not look so conspicuous as he is sneaking around the palace.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= If you mussst go, take my Epheremelda for your ggguide... Also, your gggrotesssque face isss too conssspicuousss... You musst wear Zzzora's Masssk!!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page= 6}}</ref> Although Link is at first able to sneak around with the Zora's Mask without being noticed,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Now I know why Zora put this thing on my face!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page= 9}}</ref> he is eventually discovered by a [[Rocklops]], who sees right through Link's disguise.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I can see another face behind the mask! The face of an intruder! Death to the interloper!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page= 9}}</ref>

==Crossover appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
The Zora Mask appears as an [[item]] for [[Skull Kid]] in the {{SotM}} [[DLC]] pack, found within the [[Frozen Grotto]]. When equipped, Skull Kid is able to use a [[Rapier]] and gains the ability to swim in deep water and shoot balls of ice.<ref>{{Cite|This mask gives Skull Kid the ability to move freely through water. Attacks with a rapier.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> While using the Zora Mask, it is possible to entirely bypass some of the puzzles in the [[Temple of Brainstorms]].
{{Ambig|End}}

==Trivia==
*In January 2001, [[Nintendo Power]] held a contest where the winners of the second prize would receive a copy of {{MM|-}} and one of five ceramic replicas of the [[mask]]s used in the game. These include the Zora Mask and [[Deku Mask]].
*If Link attempts to talk to [[King Zora]] while wearing the Zora Mask in ''Ocarina of Time'', he will react in disgust, saying the hero is mocking him, if not the Zora race altogether.
