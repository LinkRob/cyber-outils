{{Term/Store|Dark Stalfos|Series, FSA, TFH}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{FSA|-}}===
Dark Stalfos make their first appearance in {{FSA|-}}, first appearing in the Temple of Darkness and appear in various Dark Worlds afterwards. They attack similarly to their [[Big Dark Stalfos]] counterparts, appearing as invincible dark shadows wielding swords and charging at the Links. Only by entering the Dark World can their true form be seen and fought.

==={{TFH|-}}===
Dark Stalfos reappear in {{TFH|-}}, being found in the [[Drablands]]' Den of Trails via the [[Desert Zone]], [[Shadow Zone]] and [[Baneful Zone]]. They appear as normal Stalfos in this game and attack by throwing balls of dark energy, which will briefly [[Curse]] the Links to prevent them from using their Sword or other [[Items in Tri Force Heroes|Items]] if they are hit. Some of them also ride Dark [[Totem Armos]] to give them an edge in battle.
