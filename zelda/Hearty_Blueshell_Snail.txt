==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hearty Blueshell Snail}}
Hearty Blueshell Snails can be found on the shores of the [[Lanayru Sea]] and the [[Necluda Sea]]. They can become {{Plural|Series|Icy Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}} when frozen by [[Ice]] and [[Blueshell Escargot]] when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. Eating one restores three {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. When Hearty Blueshell Snails are [[Cooking|cooked]] into a meal, the resulting dish will grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} temporary Hearts.<ref name="Inventory"/>

As part of the "[[What's for Dinner?]]" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, Link must gather a Hearty Blushell Snail and [[Goat Butter]] for [[Kiana]] in [[Lurelin Village]].<ref>{{Cite|We seem to be a bit short on ingredients though. We'll need {{Color|BotW Red|goat butter}} and a {{Color|BotW Red|hearty blueshell snail}}.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, I know! Could you go and get me the ingredients we're missing?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> He will be rewarded with fifty {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} and [[Seafood Paella]] for doing so.

When cooking in a [[Cooking Pot]], a Hearty Blueshell Snail is required to make [[Hearty Clam Chowder]] and Seafood Paella. Either a Hearty Blueshell Snail, [[Mighty Porgy]], or [[Armored Porgy]] are needed to make [[Seafood Curry]] and [[Seafood Fried Rice]]. A Hearty Blueshell Snail, a [[Sneaky River Snail]], or any {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} can be used to make [[Salt-Grilled Fish]]. To make a [[Seafood Skewer]], either a snail, a [[Bright-Eyed Crab]], an [[Ironshell Crab]], or a [[Razorclaw Crab]] must be used. Hearty Blueshell Snails are among the seafood that can be used to make [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Meunière]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]].
