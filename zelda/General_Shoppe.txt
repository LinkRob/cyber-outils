==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|General Shoppe}} is a {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} located inside {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}. The store is managed by {{Term|BotW|Daz}}, who has collected items for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've prepared some arrow and food that I thought may prove useful to you, Mr. Hero!|Daz|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Daz}} also offers to buy items from {{Term|BotW|Link}}. <ref>{{Cite|If there's anything you don't need, please let me see it. I love seeing things from outside the forest.|Daz|BotW}}</ref>
