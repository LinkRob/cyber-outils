The {{Term/Store|Cabana Deed||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an item in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[Mrs. Marie]] gives the item to [[Link]] in exchange for 20 [[Joy Pendant]]s.<ref>{{Cite|My dear! Did you bring me {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Joy Pendants}} yet again? How many {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Joy Pendants}} do you have right now? 20...? Why, to think a child like you would pay such a large tribute to me... Oh, your teacher is very impressed by your efforts, little dear! My whole body is numb with the thrill of excitement! ...So this is what pure joy feels like! I'll take precisely {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|20}} of them, thank you. That will do just fine! My heart will be all aflutter! I don't know if you know, but {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|20}} is my {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|lucky number}}! And just as I promised, I will reward you for giong out and collecting all these for me. I'm going to give you... A wonderfully splendid {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|thing}}! Close your eyes, dear...|Mrs. Marie|TWW}}</ref> This deed makes Link the owner of the [[Private Oasis]], which was previously owned by Mrs. Marie. To gain entrance to the resort for the first time, Link must show this document to [[Oasis Butler|the cabana's butler]]. The Cabana Deed is a crucial item in obtaining all [[Triforce#Triforce Shards|Triforce Shards]], for there is a [[Triforce Chart]] located in a [[Secret Cave]] inside the cabana.
