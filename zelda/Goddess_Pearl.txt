The {{Term/Store|Goddess Pearl|Goddess Pearls|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are three major [[:Category:Quest Items|Quest Items]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl}} each bear their respective [[Marks of the Goddesses]], and are used to open up a passage to the {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}}, which lies underwater in sunken {{Term|TWWHD|Hyrule|link}}. Once collected, the {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|display=Pearls}} are placed on the three statues resembling the {{Plural|TWWHD|Golden Goddess|link}} located on each of the {{Plural|TWWHD|Triangle Island|link}} found across the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}, causing the temple to rise above the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|display=Sea}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wandering traveler who seeks the guidepost of the goddesses... Place the pearl you hold here.|Goddess Statue|TWW}}</ref> The {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl}} were named after the {{Plural|TWWHD|Golden Goddess}}: {{Term|TWWHD|Din (Goddess)|link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Farore (Goddess)|link}}, and {{Term|TWWHD|Nayru (Goddess)|link}}.

==List of {{Plural|Series|Goddess Pearl}}==
==={{Term|Series|Din's Pearl}}===
{{Main|Din's Pearl}}
[[File:TWWHD Din's Pearl Artwork.png|100px|right]]
{{Term|TWWHD|Din's Pearl|link}} is the first of three {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}} that {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} obtains. It is given to him by {{Term|TWWHD|Komali|link}} after he defeats {{Term|TWWHD|Gohma|link}} and calms {{Term|TWWHD|Valoo|link}}.
{{clear}}

==={{Term|Series|Farore's Pearl}}===
{{Main|Farore's Pearl}}
[[File:TWWHD Farore's Pearl Artwork.png|100px|right]]
{{Term|TWWHD|Farore's Pearl|link}} is the second of three {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}} that {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} obtains. It is given to him by the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Deku Tree|link}} after he rescues {{Term|TWWHD|Makar|link}} from the {{Term|TWWHD|Forbidden Woods|link}}.
{{clear}}

==={{Term|Series|Nayru's Pearl}}===
{{Main|Nayru's Pearl}}
[[File:TWWHD Nayru's Pearl Artwork.png|100px|right]]
{{Term|TWWHD|Nayru's Pearl|link}} is the third and final of three {{Plural|TWWHD|Goddess Pearl|link}} that {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} obtains. It is given to him by {{Term|TWWHD|Jabun|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}}.
{{clear}}
