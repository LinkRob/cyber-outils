{{Term/Store|Pumpkin Head||Series, OoA}} is the {{Term|OoA|Boss}} of {{Term|OoA|Spirit's Grave|link}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''''Pumpkin Head'''''|book= E |publisher= |page= 199}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
[[File:Pumpkin Head Spirit.png|left]]
{{Term|OoA|Pumpkin Head}} has an invulnerable jack-o'-lantern as a head and will pace around the room aimlessly, shooting groups of three fireballs when {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} crosses his path. {{Term|OoA|Link}} must attack {{Term|OoA|Pumpkin Head}}'s torso and legs with his {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}}. This eventually causes his body to disappear, leaving only the jack-o'-lantern on the ground. {{Term|OoA|Link}} can then pick up the head using his {{Term|OoA|Power Bracelet|link}} and toss it aside to reveal a small spirit, which can be momentarily damaged with the {{Term|OoA|Sword}} before it dashes back into the head, regenerating {{Term|OoA|Pumpkin Head}}'s body. After several more hits to the exposed spirit, {{Term|OoA|Pumpkin Head}} will be defeated, leaving behind a {{Term|OoA|Heart Container|link}} and opening the door to the [[Eternal Spirit]].
