The {{Term/Store|Tho Kayu Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Tho Kayu Shrine}}<br/>Tho Kayu's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Tho Kayu's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Tho Kayu Shrine is located in the {{Term|BotW|Toruma Dunes|link}}, though it's initially buried beneath the sand. To reveal the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must light the four {{Plural|BotW|Torch|link}} on the surrounding rocks. The area is inhabited by a {{Term|BotW|Molduga|link}}, which must be either avoided or defeated when lighting the Torches.

Once all four Torches have been lit, Tho Kayu Shrine will rise from the sand. As Link enters the Shrine, he's greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tho Kayu|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Tho Kayu. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Tho Kayu|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The Tho Kayu Shrine is one large room with no puzzles or enemies. There is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in the center of the room containing a {{Term|BotW|Golden Bow|link}}. As a reward for arriving to this Shrine, Tho Kayu gives Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Tho Kayu|BotW}}</ref> He then wishes Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Tho Kayu|BotW}}</ref>
