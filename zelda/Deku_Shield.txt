{{Term/Store|Deku Shield|Deku Shields|Series, OoT, OoT3D|plural}} are items in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref> It is the first [[Shield]] usable by [[Link]].

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT Deku Shield.png|thumb|Link defending with the Deku Shield in {{OoT|-}}]]
The Deku Shield is a simple wooden shield crafted from the bark of the [[Great Deku Tree]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT|publisher= Nintendo|page= }}</ref> The [[Symbols#Race Emblems|Kokiri Symbol]] painted on its surface resembles the [[Kokiri]]'s [[Spiritual Stones|Spiritual Stone]], the [[Kokiri's Emerald]]. Link must first purchase the shield in the [[Kokiri Shop]] found in the [[Kokiri Forest]] for 40 Rupees, alongside the [[Kokiri Sword]], to convince [[Mido]] that he is worthy of his council with the Great Deku Tree.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Red|Deku Shield: 40 Rupees}}<br/>Once equipped, you can defend with {{Icon|OoT3D R}}. If set on fire, it will burn!|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Eh, what's that?! Oh, you have a {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Deku Shield}}… And what's THAT?! Is that the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Kokiri Sword}}?! GOOD GRIEF!|Mido|OoT3D}}</ref> After passing Mido, Link can purchase another shield from [[Business Scrub]]s in either the [[Lost Woods]], [[Inside the Deku Tree]], or within [[Dodongo's Cavern]] for 50 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|I give up! If you let me go,I will sell you a Deku Shield! It's 50 Rupees!|Business Scrub|OoT}}</ref>

The Deku Shield is fairly functional, but its durability may be compromised by certain flame attacks, as it instantly burns up if a [[Fire Keese]] touches Link or a [[Dodongo]]'s breath harms him.<ref>{{Cite|Fire Keese-Destroy it before it flies into you! If you don't, its flames will burn up your Deku Shield!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> As the shield itself is relatively small, the Deku Shield can only be equipped while the [[Hero of Time]] is young.<ref>{{Cite|Hi, mister! You can't use a Deku Shield! It's only for kids!|Kokiri|OoT}}</ref> Although the more powerful [[Hylian Shield]] is available to Child Link, it is far too heavy and it hinders his movement into a sort of "turtle shield" technique. Because of this, the Deku Shield provides a more mobile defense and is well utilized by the [[Targeting System|Z-Targeting system]]. Like the Hylian Shield, the Deku Shield can also be stolen and eaten if Link is swallowed by a [[Like Like]].<ref>{{Cite|Like Like<br/>An enemy that eats shields and certain clothes. Beat it quickly to get your gear back!|Navi|OoT}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:HW - Deku Shield.png|right|thumb|130px|Lana wielding the Deku Shield in {{HW|-}}]]
==={{OoT|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{OoT|Himekawa}}, once Link and [[Mido]] had lifted the curse of [[Queen Gohma]] and relieved her from inside the Great Deku Tree, he requests Link to carve a shield, the Deku Shield, out of his remains once he passes away so it may protect him from evil on his coming journey outside the forest. The shield is later destroyed when it catches fire while Link duels a [[Stalfos]] during [[Ganondorf]]'s assault on Hyrule Castle.

==={{SSBM|-}}===
In {{SSBM}}, [[Young Link]] carries the Deku Shield. He can use it to block incoming projectiles while crouching. Despite the Deku Shield's vulnerability to flames in {{OoT|-}}, in {{SSBM|-}} it can block fire projectiles.

==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}} when using the Spear weapon, [[Lana]] is capable of having it transform into various different forest themed weapons, one of them being the Deku Shield. It transforms into the shield while guarding, and appears on her left arm while attacking with the spear in the other during her Focus Spirit Special. Noticeably, the Deku Shield in-game is as large as the Hylian Shield.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
The Deku Shield is wielded by Young Link in {{SSBU}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The Deku Shield shares the same material as [[Deku Stick|Deku Sticks]].
*One should be careful of not letting [[Link]] die and receive a [[Game Over]] while he is within areas requiring the use of the [[Goron Tunic]] ([[Death Mountain Crater]], etc.) or walking on lava tiles, as the young hero will instantly catch on fire and potentially lose the Deku Shield. This can be avoided by simply equipping the [[Hylian Shield]].
*Although the Deku Shield is not technically lost during the ending of {{OoT}}, in {{MM}}, it is replaced by the [[Hero's Shield]]. This suggests that like the [[Kokiri Sword]] which had an updated appearance, at some point between the games' events while the [[Hero of Time]] is at [[Hyrule Castle]], Link was given more adequate gear by the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]] (which is implied in the flashback with [[Princess Zelda]]).
*The doors inside the [[Forbidden Woods]] in {{TWW}} are similar to the Deku Shield, as the red Kokiri symbol appears on the brown doors, forming a Deku Shield-like shape and look. This characteristic is also shared with the doors of the {{Term|TPHD|Forest Temple|link}} from {{TP}}.[[File:MM Deku Shield.jpg|thumb|The Deku Shield in the beta version of {{MM|-}}]]
*The Deku Shield can be seen in very early screenshots of {{MM|-}}, as the game used Link's model from {{OoT|-}} as a placeholder.
*During early development of {{OoT|-}}, the Deku Shield would use Link's family crest.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= First of all, it's made of wood. In the center is Link's family crest.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= http://www.ign.com/articles/1997/12/02/the-z-files-3|title= The Z-Files}}</ref> This could mean that the Kokiri symbol was meant to be Link's family crest, or that the [[Shield]] originally used a different symbol.
**Also during this stage, the Kokiri Sword was completely wooden in textures like the Deku Shield, closely resembling the first [[Sword]] from {{TLoZ}}.
