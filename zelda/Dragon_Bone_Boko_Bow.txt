{{Plural|BotW|Dragon Bone Boko Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} used by high-tier {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} and in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. They are {{Plural|BotW|Boko Bow|link}} that have been reinforced with fossils that were handpicked by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Because of this, they are known to have a respectable firepower.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Due to being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Dragon Bone Boko Bow}} will burn and eventually disintegrate if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water|link}}. Unlike metal {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, they do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, nor do they conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}.

If scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Term|BotW|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Dragon Bone Boko Bow}}, along with other {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}}.
