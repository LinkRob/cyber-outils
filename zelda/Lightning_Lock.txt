{{Term/Store|Lightning Lock|Lightning Locks|Series, ALttP|plural}} are recurring magical barriers in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|ALttP, AST}}

==Appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
The Lightning Lock is placed by [[Agahnim]] on the [[Hyrule Castle Tower]], blocking anyone from entering. Link can witness the Lightning Lock early in the game but cannot destroy it. As it is created by Agahnim's evil magic, it cannot be destroyed with anything less than the [[Master Sword]]. Striking it will simply cause Link to take damage.

===''Ancient Stone Tablets''===
{{Ambig|start}}
In {{AST}} the Lighting Lock is placed on [[Ganon's Tower]], blocking the hero from entering and challenging [[Ganon]] for the final battle. Even with the Level 4 Sword he cannot destroy this barrier. The hero will have to travel to the [[Lost Woods]] to obtain the [[Master Sword]] if he wishes to break through and enter the tower.
{{Ambig|end}}

==Trivia==
*Similar electrified barriers appear in {{TWW}} and {{FSA}}, also at Hyrule Castle in each game. In {{TWW|-}} the barriers also appear in Hyrule Castle, and can only be removed by destroying all the enemies in the area. In {{FSA|-}}, only the [[Fairy Queen]] can remove the barrier blocking the way to the tower of Hyrule Castle.
