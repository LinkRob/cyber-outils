{{Term/Store|EX Request from the Lab||HWAoC}} is a level 57 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Request from the Lab|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the first wave of the game’s {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Request from the Lab}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} except {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Claw|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|High-Yield Ancient Furnace|link}}, 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

For the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat five {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} according to {{Term|HWAoC|Purah|link}}'s instructions. The first group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} consists of a large {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}} and eight normal {{Plural|HWAoC|Chuchu|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} has to kill all of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Chuchu}} without defeating the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu}}, and if he or she does, they fail the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}. When the first objective is complete, a gate opens in the center of the map to a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}}. This time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin}} while avoiding the {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}}. After doing so, a gate opens again to reveal an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Meteo Wizzrobe|link}}, with the {{Term|HWAoC|Meteo Wizzrobe}} as the one to be kept alive. Although, when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} does, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} appears and must be defeated under the same circumstances. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends upon the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}}'s defeat.
