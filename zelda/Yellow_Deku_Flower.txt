{{Term/Store|Yellow Deku Flower|Yellow Deku Flowers|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are {{Plural|MM3D|Object|link}} in {{MM}}.<ref name="Nintendo CE 151"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|MM3D|Yellow Deku Flower}} are similar to regular {{Plural|MM3D|Deku Flower|link}}, but launch {{Plural|MM3D|Deku Scrub|link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link}} higher than the normal variant and are entirely yellow in color. One appears in {{Term|MM3D|South Clock Town|link}}, which {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} may purchase in exchange for a {{Term|MM3D|Moon's Tear|link}}, and one is present in the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Dungeon|link}} as part of a {{Term|MM3D|Puzzle|link}}.
