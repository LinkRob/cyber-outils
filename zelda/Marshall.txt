{{Term/Store|Marshall||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Marshall}} first appears in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, where he tells the story of the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} to {{Term|TMC|Berry|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Joel|link}} while {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} listens.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Green|Link}}, there's a storyteller here! We should stop and listen to--Oh! I wonder what's over there...|Princess Zelda|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Long, long ago, and then even longer before that... Evil creatures appeared in the world of humans. They were powerful, frightening beasts, and they created such trouble! But then the {{Color|TMC Green|Picori}} came from the sky, bringing humans powerful magic. With the magic light force and the Picori Blade, the beasts were repelled. And the world of humans once more knew peace, thanks to the Picori.|Marshall|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}} is turned to stone, {{Term|TMC|Marshall}} can be found near the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's House|link}}, where he will ask {{Term|TMC|Link}} about the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|display=Inn}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, kid! Did you stay at the inn? Well? And did you get your gift?|Marshall|TMC}}</ref> He also approves of {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I think the mayor is doing a fine job, no matter what people say!|Marshall|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Marshall}} disappears from {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} and does not appear for the remainder of the game.

He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure, why not?|Marshall|TMC}}</ref>
