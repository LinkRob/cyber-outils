==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Swift Violet}}
Swift Violets grow on cliffsides in the [[Gerudo Highlands]] and [[Hebra Mountains]]. They're also available to purchase at the [[Coral Reef]] in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. When Swift Violets are cooked into a meal using a [[Cooking Pot]], the resulting dish will temporarily increase speed when eaten.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Purple [[Dye]].

Fifteen Swift Violets, along with five {{Plural|BotW|Fire Keese Wing|link}}, are needed to upgrade each piece of the [[Climbing Set]] to its highest tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. Both the [[Sand Boots]] and [[Snow Boots]] require fifteen Swift Violets to be upgraded to their fourth tier.

* Total for '''Climbing Set''': 45 (0, 0, 0, 15) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Sand Boots''': 15 (0, 0, 0, 15)

* Total for '''Snow Boots''': 15 (0, 0, 0, 15)
