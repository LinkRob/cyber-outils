{{Term/Store|Dampa||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Dampa is a sailor who stands near the [[Bomb Shop]] on [[Windfall Island]], and is the son of [[Missy]]. He owns the island's [[Pig]]s and charges [[Skull Necklace]]s for a pig-finding game. In {{TWWHD}}, a [[Treasure Chart]] can be obtained by beating his mini-game.
