{{Term/Store|Hasty Elixir|Hasty Elixirs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Elixir}}<br/>Grants a low/mid/high-level haste effect, which boosts your movement speed while running, swimming, or climbing.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can drink Hasty Elixirs to receive a boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Inventory"/> This haste effect will influence his running, swimming, and climbing speed.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Hasty Elixirs can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|Series|Hightail Lizard|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Hot-Footed Frog|link}}

By adding any of these hasty ingredients, Link can make a Hasty Elixir that will also heal him:

* {{Term|Series|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Rushroom|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Swift Violet|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Swift Carrot|link}}
