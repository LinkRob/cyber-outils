The {{Term/Store|Guchini Plain Barrows||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Guchini Plain Barrows'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Guchini Plain Barrows are an eastern subsection inside the [[Guchini Plain]] located in the [[Faron Grasslands]]. The barrows are a lush, plain with many small hills, rocks and trees. Throughout the Guchini Plain Barrows, {{Term|BotW|Totsuna|link}} can be found walking through the plain.

In the eastern Barrows, a large flat rock sits between two short pillars. If [[Link]] moves this rock out of the way, a Stone [[Chest]] holding a [[Royal Halberd]] can be found sitting underneath the rock. In an area near [[Herin Lake]], there is a tall stone pillar that extends into the sky. If Link climbs up this stone pillar, he can find a stone chest containing a [[Boomerang]] at the top.

The stone pillars and the structures around their bases are typical architecture of {{Term|BotW|Zonai|link}} origin.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |page= 413}}</ref>
