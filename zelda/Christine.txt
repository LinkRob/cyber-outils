{{Term/Store|Christine||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Christine is a goat who lives in the [[Animal Village]]. She loves eating paper and corresponds with [[Mr. Write]] from [[Mabe Village]].<ref>{{Cite|You know, sometimes I can't help eating a delicious piece of paper, even if it's a letter to my darling Mr. Write... How embarrassing!|Christine|LADX}}</ref> When [[Link]] talks to her for the first time, Christine says to him that the proper etiquette is to give flowers when first meeting a lady.<ref>{{Cite|You don't know the proper etiquette when dealing with a lady, do you? You should have brought flowers or something, then I might be more inclined to talk with you... Oh yes, in my case, hibiscus are best...|Christine|LADX}}</ref> During the game's {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}, in exchange for a [[Hibiscus]] flower, Christine gives Link a [[Goat's Letter|Letter]] that must be delivered to Mr. Write.<ref>{{Cite|I would like you to take this letter to a Mr. Write who lives on the border of the Mysterious Forest, please!|Christine|LADX}}</ref> When the Letter is delivered, it is revealed that it is apparently a love letter, and that Christine has enclosed a photograph.<ref>{{Cite|What's this?! A letter for me?! I'm so happy! ...And look! The letter came with a photograph!|Mr. Write|LADX}}</ref> Although Mr. Write believes the photo to be of Christine herself, it is actually an image of [[Princess Peach]] from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Super'' ''Mario'' franchise]].<ref>{{Cite|You got a Broom as your reward from Mr. Write! But that photo was not of...|N/A|LADX}}</ref>

Christine owns a green [[Bird]] while Mr. Write owns an orange bird.
