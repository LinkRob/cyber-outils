The {{Term/Store|Zora Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Zora Ring<br/>Dive without breathing|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Zora Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it allows [[Link]] to dive without breathing.<ref name="Description"/> The Zora Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
