{{Term/Store|Sealed Statue|Sealed Statues|Series, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are objects in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|All {{Color|HWDE Green|sealed statues}} have been activated!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
There are three {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Statue}} located in different {{Plural|HWDE|Keep|link}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Temple of the Sacred Sword|link}}, respectively the {{Term|HWDE|Statue of Wave and Flame}} in the south square, the {{Term|HWDE|Statue of Twilight}} in the north square, and the {{Term|HWDE|Statue of Servitude}} in the town entrance.<ref>{{Cite|You discovered a {{Color|HWDE Green|sealed statue}} at the {{Color|HWDE Green|North Sqaure}}!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You discovered a {{Color|HWDE Green|sealed statue}} at the {{Color|HWDE Green|South Square}}!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You discovered a {{Color|HWDE Green|sealed statue}} at the {{Color|HWDE Green|Town Entrance}}!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> These {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Statue}} include the {{Term|HWDE|Statue of Servitude|link}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Statue of Twilight|link}}, and the {{Term|HWDE|Statue of Wave and Flame|link}}. The {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} first discover the {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Statue}} while trying to find the {{Term|HWDE|Master Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Master Sword is up ahead. We must work together to retrieve it!|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref> Upon reaching the {{Term|HWDE|Temple of the Sacred Sword}}, they find that the doors are locked and can only be opened by awakening the three {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Statue}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|HWDE Green|Temple of the Sacred Sword}} is closed!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|First we must awaken the three sealed statues.|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref> While searching for the {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Statue}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Ghost Soldier|link}} and {{Plural|HWDE|Ghost Summoner|link}} appear to stop the {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} from opening the {{Term|HWDE|Temple of the Sacred Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Yellow|Ghost Forces}} appeared throughout the battlefield!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Those who would threaten the temple... will be punished!|Ghost Soldier|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Impa|link}}, {{Term|HWDE|Darunia|link}}, and {{Term|HWDE|Midna|link}} must each activate their respective {{Term|HWDE|Sealed Statue}}.<ref>{{Cite|It will take all three of your abilities to awaken the sealed statues.|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref> Once each {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} reaches their respective statue, they will begin to activate.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Blue|Midna}} has reached a {{Color|HWDE Green|sealed statue}}, causing it to activate!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Blue|Impa}} has reached a {{Color|HWDE Green|sealed statue}}, causing it to activate!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Blue|Darunia}} has reached a {{Color|HWDE Green|sealed statue}}, causing it to activate!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Statue}} don't activate immediately, and while the {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} are waiting on the statues, more {{Plural|HWDE|Ghost Soldier}} appear.<ref>{{Cite|More {{Color|HWDE Yellow|Ghost Forces}} appeared!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> {{Plural|HWDE|Ghost Soldier}} appear inside the {{Term|HWDE|Temple of the Sacred Sword}}, which makes {{Term|HWDE|Zelda|link}} realize that they are getting manipulated.<ref>{{Cite|Inside the temple? That is unusual... Someone must be manipulating them!|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} search for the commander of the {{Plural|HWDE|Ghost Soldier}} and soon find a fake {{Term|HWDE|Lana|link}} controlling the {{Plural|HWDE|Ghost Soldier}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found the person controlling the {{Color|HWDE Yellow|Ghost Forces}}!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's not Lana! Link, take her out!|Proxi|HWDE}}</ref> After defeating the fake {{Term|HWDE|Lana}}, the {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Statue}} awaken and open the {{Term|HWDE|Temple of the Sacred Sword}}, simultaneously freeing the {{Plural|HWDE|Ghost Soldier}}.<ref>{{Cite|The three {{Color|HWDE Green|sealed statues}} have awoken!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|HWDE Green|Main Temple gate}} is open!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You defeated the fake {{Color|HWDE Red|Lana}}! The {{Color|HWDE Yellow|Ghost Forces}} are free!|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Plural|HWDE|Ghost Soldier}} see the temple opened and realize that {{Term|HWDE|Link|link}} has appeared, causing them to retreat and apologize for attacking.<ref>{{Cite|The temple door is open! He who shall wield the sacred sword has appeared!|Ghost Soldier|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please excuse our trespasses...|Ghost Soldier|HWDE}}</ref>

The {{Plural|HWDE|Sealed Statue}} can be found during battles in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}, but they serve no purpose during those battles.
