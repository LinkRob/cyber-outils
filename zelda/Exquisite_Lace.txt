{{Term/Store|Exquisite Lace|Exquisite Laces|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 122"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Exquisite Lace}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Fortress|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Bomb Storage|link}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Exquisite Lace}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Training Ground|link}}''', ''"Halved attack and defense!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Exquisite Lace}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Exquisite Lace}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair}}''', ''"Only Bombs-no swords!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Exquisite Lace}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Exquisite Lace}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Queen of Hearts|link}} {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 122"/>
