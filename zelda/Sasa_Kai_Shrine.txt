The {{Term/Store|Sasa Kai Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Sasa Kai Shrine}}<br/>A Modest Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Modest Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Sasa Kai Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}, directly next to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Tower|link}}. It is originally buried beneath the ground, with a pedestal nearby.

On top of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Tower}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}, who reveals he knows of an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" from the area.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When asked, he will sing the song for {{Term|BotW|Link}}, which describes shooting an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} into the sky from the shadow of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Tower}}.<ref>{{Cite|As light shines from the northwest skies<br/>From the tower’s shadow an arrow flies<br/>Pierce heaven’s light to reveal {{Color|BotW Red|the prize}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Sign of the Shadow|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

At three in the afternoon, when the sun casts {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Tower}}'s shadow onto the nearby {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must shoot at the sun with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow}}. Doing so will cause the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} to reveal itself.<ref>{{Cite|When the light was in the northwest, you saw Gerudo Tower cast a shadow upon a pedestal. After firing an arrow toward the light from the top of that pedestal, a shrine appeared!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Sasa Kai Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sasa Kai|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Sasa Kai. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Sasa Kai|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Sasa Kai Shrine}} features a large primary chamber, in which a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}} rises from a pit once {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets near. In order to complete the Trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat this {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}.

Once the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III}} is defeated, a portcullis leading to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} will rise, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to pass through. {{Term|BotW|Sasa Kai}} will commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for completing the Trial, giving him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Sasa Kai|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Sasa Kai|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the portcullis contains a {{Term|BotW|Frostblade|link}}.
