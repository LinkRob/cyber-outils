The {{Term/Store|Blue Luck Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Blue Luck Ring<br/>1/2 damage from beams|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Blue Luck Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be found inside a cave located above the [[Zora Village]] during the [[Lynna City|Present]], requiring the [[Power Glove]] to reach it. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it reduces damage taken from beams.<ref name="Description"/> The Blue Luck Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
