{{Term/Store|Golden Bug|Golden Bugs|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} and objects in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Bug}} glow and make a shimmering sound. They can be attracted with the {{Term|TPHD|Lantern|link}}. Some {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Bug}} are out of reach, so {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} must use the {{Term|TPHD|Clawshot|link}} or {{Term|TPHD|Gale Boomerang|link}} to collect them. There are 12 types of {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Bug}}, including a male and female of each type, for a total of 24. Both male and female variations of a single {{Term|TPHD|Golden Bug}} can be found in the same general area.<ref>{{Cite|I've invited the 24 golden bugs of the world to the ball, but... I don't know if they got lost on the way or what...but they're not here, that's for sure. [...] Oh, boy, if you have a bug with you now, please give it to me. The ball must begin!|Agitha|TP}}</ref>

After giving {{Term|TPHD|Agitha|link}} the first {{Term|TPHD|Golden Bug}}, she will reward {{Term|TPHD|Link}} with the {{Term|TPHD|Big Wallet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|...You actually went bug hunting for me, didn't you? To be honest, I doubted you would... I guess some might consider me a poor- mannered princess...From now on, if you bring me insects, I'll share my happiness with you. You can keep it in this!|Agitha|TP}}</ref> From then on, giving {{Term|TPHD|Agitha}} a {{Term|TPHD|Golden Bug}} will reward {{Term|TPHD|Link}} with a {{Term|TPHD|Purple Rupee|link}} (50). Giving her a pair of the same {{Term|TPHD|Golden Bug}} will reward him with an {{Term|TPHD|Orange Rupee|link}} (100).<ref>{{Cite|If any bug becomes part of a pair, I think I'll be able to share GREAT happiness with you!|Agitha|TP}}</ref> Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} gives a {{Term|TPHD|Golden Bug}} to {{Term|TPHD|Agitha}}, it will be marked on the collection screen with a butterfly. After giving all 24 {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Bug}} to {{Term|TPHD|Agitha}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} will be rewarded with the {{Term|TPHD|Giant's Wallet|link}}.

In {{TPHD}}, {{Term|TPHD|Agitha}} will also give {{Term|TPHD|Link}} a {{Term|TPHD|Miiverse Stamp|link}} of herself upon delivering half of the {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Bug}}.

==Non-Canon Appearancess==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Stub|HWDE}}
In {{HW}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Golden Bug}} appear as part of {{Term|HWDE|Agitha|link}}'s attacks. She can summon swarms of {{Plural|HWDE|Golden Bug}} as well as giant ones. She specifically uses female {{Plural|HWDE|Golden Butterfly|link}} and male {{Plural|HWDE|Golden Beetle|link}} for her insect related attacks.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*After capturing a {{Term|TPHD|Golden Bug}} in {{TP|-}}, it does not disappear; rather, another one takes its place even though {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can not bring back more than one, as it will be released after collecting it again. After {{Term|TPHD|Link}} releases a {{Term|TPHD|Golden Bug}} he already captured, it will release a {{Term|TPHD|Heart|link}}.
*If {{Term|TPHD|Link}} has {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Bug}} in his possession that he has not given to {{Term|TPHD|Agitha}} and tries to leave her castle, she will angrily mutter to his back.<ref>{{Cite|I know you have bugs...|Agitha|TP}}</ref>
*The menu description for the male and female {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Snail|link}} mention that possibility that they might actually be the opposite sex.<ref>{{Cite|A golden-bodied ♂ snail. It was found in the Sacred Grove. (...It might actually be a ♀.)|Inventory|TP}}</ref> This is a reference to the fact that snails are actually hermaphrodites, so there are no male or female snails. In the French version of the game, the description refers to the snail as "{{Exp|Un escargot♂♀au corps doré.|A golden-bodied ♂♀ snail.}}"
