{{Term/Store|''Purah's Diary''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Purah's Diary|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|Series|Purah's Diary}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} found in {{Term|BotW|Purah's Room|link}} at the top of the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. This {{Term|BotW|Purah's Diary|display=''Diary''}} was used to detail {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}}'s anti-aging experiments,<ref>{{Cite|Research diary for anti-aging rune. CLASSIFIED! For Purah's eyes only!|Purah's Diary|BotW}}</ref> as well as {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} emergence from the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Purah}} will mention her {{Term|BotW|Purah's Diary|display=''Diary''}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he first meets her, but she insists he not read it due to its embarrassing nature.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I say "failed," but...in some ways, it was a success. I documented the full affair in my {{Color|BotW Blue|diary upstairs}}. BUT! The whole thing is embarrassing, so I insist you refrain from reading it!|Purah|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} reads the {{Term|BotW|Purah's Diary|display=''Diary''}}, {{Term|BotW|Purah}} will ask if he did so the next time he speaks to her.<ref>{{Cite|You read it...didn't you?|Purah|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} be honest with her, she praises his honesty but scolds him for going through a young lady's belongings.<ref>{{Cite|[I read it.] Well, I commend your honesty. Still, it is never acceptable to rummage through a young lady's private musings! Got it?!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> Due to this, she decides to punish him by deleting all of the {{Plural|BotW|Rune|link}} on his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Naturally, you shall be punished for that. Switch ON!|Purah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|All Sheikah Slate runes have now been deleted.}}|Purah|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Purah}} encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take a look,<ref>{{Cite|I completely deleted the data on your {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}. Go on, take a look!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> which reveals that the {{Plural|BotW|Rune}} are still in place. She then laughs at {{Term|BotW|Link}} and asks if he liked her impression of a {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}}'s voice.<ref>{{Cite|GOTCHYA! HA! Did you like my {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} impression? Superb, right?|Purah|BotW}}</ref> Quietly, she wonders if young girls do mean impressions for fun.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Don't young girls do mean impressions for fun? Maybe not? Oh, well!}}}}|Purah|BotW}}</ref>
