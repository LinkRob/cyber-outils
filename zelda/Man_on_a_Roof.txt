The {{Term/Store|Man on a Roof||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 239}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Man on a Roof}} can be found sitting on the roof of the {{Term|OoT3D|Granny's Potion Shop|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}}. He spends all day staring at {{Term|OoT3D|Death Mountain|link}}. The {{Term|OoT3D|Man on a Roof}} can be reached by using the {{Term|OoT3D|Hookshot|link}}, in which he will reward {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} a {{Term|OoT3D|Piece of Heart|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Good to see you again! I'll give you this as a memento.|Man on a Roof|OoT}}</ref>
