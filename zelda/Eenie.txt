{{Term/Store|Eenie||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Eenie & Meenie}} 
{{Term|TMC|Eenie}} is a farmer that tends to land within the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}}. He and {{Term|TMC|Meenie|link}} give their crops to {{Term|TMC|Brocco|link}} to sell in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Howdy, howdy! Come get your fresh veggies, grown by Eenie and Meenie!|Brocco|TMC}}</ref> and occasionally he digs up {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I love working in my garden. It makes me feel so close to nature... And sometimes, just sometimes, I dig up a {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone}} from the ground!|Eenie|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Eenie}},<ref>{{Cite|What's up there, bud? You sayin' you wanna match {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone pieces}}?|Eenie|TMC}}</ref> resulting in a {{Term|TMC|Goron Digger|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}} breaking open the entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I feel so much stronger now! Here we go!!!|Goron Digger|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} has already {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fused}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with a {{Term|TMC|Mysterious Wall|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave}} will already be open, and another {{Term|TMC|Goron Digger}} will join the others instead.

==Trivia==
*In the PAL version of {{TMC}}, the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Eenie}} is [[Permanently Missable Items|permanently missable]], resulting in the loss of one {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}}, an appearance by {{Term|TMC|Biggoron|link}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Mirror Shield|link}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} begins to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Eenie}}, but then quits and leaves the area, {{Term|TMC|Eenie}} will never give the option to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} again.
