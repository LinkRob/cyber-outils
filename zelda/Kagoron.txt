{{Term/Store|Kagoron||Series, ST}} is a character in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 289}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|ST|Kagoron}} is the {{Term|ST|Mountain Goddess|link}}'s messenger. He is found on the north mountain in {{Term|ST|Goron Village|link}}, praying for the nearby volcano to calm.<ref name="Praying"/>

Upon {{Term|ST|Link}} arriving at the {{Term|ST|Mountain Goddess}}'s altar, {{Term|ST|Kagoron}} will commend the young hero for his bravery, but will also inform him that the altar is a sacred place for {{Plural|ST|Goron}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmm? Who is this strange face before me? This is a sacred place of the Gorons! You cannot just come here on a whim! Hmm... I see you are still a child. Tell me, did you travel here by yourself?... I must salute your bravery, young stranger.|Kagoron|ST}}</ref> After {{Term|ST|Link}} asks him how to access the {{Term|ST|Fire Sanctuary|link}},<ref>{{Cite|You wish to hear about the Fire Sanctuary? Unfortunately, the way there is hidden to us. Only the elder would be able to tell you that secret. Do you wish to meet him?|Kagoron|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Kagoron}} notices that {{Term|ST|Link}} is a {{Term|ST|Royal Engineer|link}},<ref>{{Cite|But first... You are an engineer, are you not? Those who drive trains have a special aura about them. I can see it on you!|Kagoron|ST}}</ref> and asks the young hero to descend the mountain with him.<ref>{{Cite|...But moving on! Your train may come in handy. But let us descend the mountain so we can speak in peace. I have some work to do first, so I will meet you down there. You go ahead and get your train ready!|Kagoron|ST}}</ref> Upon returning to {{Term|ST|Goron Village}}, the {{Term|ST|Mountain Goddess}}'s messenger gives {{Term|ST|Link}} a {{Term|ST|Freight Car|link}} to help him haul {{Term|ST|Mega Ice|link}} from the {{Term|ST|Anouki Village|link}} in order to put out the fires at {{Term|ST|Goron Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the favor I require of you, young stranger. Use the freight car to bring back something cold enough to cool the lava. Go talk to the merchants in the various villages. They may be able to help you.|Kagoron|ST}}</ref> Once {{Term|ST|Link}} has access to {{Term|ST|Dark Ore Mine|link}}, {{Term|ST|Kagoron}} will send him a {{Term|ST|Letter|link}} with a map leading to a {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Dark Ore Mine|display=Mine}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hi, little engineer! How has your freight car been holding up? Things here have been peaceful, and the mountain is silent. Thanks for all your work! Here is a map I drew as a young Goron. Use it to find some treasure I hid in the Dark Ore Mine as a young one. I cannot remember where I hid it. The hints on the map might help you find it though. Hope to see you soon!|Kagoron|ST}}</ref> 

==Nomenclature==
Kagoron's name is likely a portmanteau of the Japanese word, {{Romanize|鍵|Kagi|Key}}, and Goron. This is reflected in his appearance, and that of his storage house containing the {{Term|ST|Freight Car}}, which are both covered in markings resembling keys.
