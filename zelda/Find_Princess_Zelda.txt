After the Ganondorf and the Demon Dragon disappeared, Link's right arm glowed and flashed in bright white light. He was transported in an otherworldly realm, along with the Light Dragon. His right arm partially took control where it releases a ray of light onto the Light Dragon. He is joined by two ghosts: {{Term|TotK|King Rauru|link}} and {{Term|TotK|Queen Sonia|link}}. Together, they amplified the power through Link's right arm, creating a shroud of light that enveloped around them. When it dissipates, Link found Zelda back to normal, and his right arm, fully restored. The two ghosts later disappears into the afterlife, and the world around Link and Zelda vanishes before their eyes.

Link woke up, now falling high in the sky. As he looked around, he found Zelda down below, falling and unconscious. He dived in fast to reach her and was able to grab her just in time before they fell into the lake below.

Link carries Zelda to safety. When she woke up, she saw the Master Sword with Link, who communed that the two have defeated Ganondorf, putting her in total relief. As she and Link view the risen Hyrule castle, Zelda couldn't remember what happened while she was a dragon. But she remembered a warm, feeling embrace shortly before waking. Zelda still remembered the memories and people she had while in Ancient Hyrule — but in the end, she was more than happy to be reunited with Link.--><ref>{{Cite|Link, I met such wonderful people.
