'''Din''' is the name of two different people in the ''Zelda'' universe

*'''[[Din (Goddess)]]''', the goddess of power who helped create the land of Hyrule, as revealed in {{OoT}}.
*'''[[Din (Oracle)]]''', the Oracle of Seasons from the land of [[Holodrum]], who has power over the seasons and commands the [[Rod of Seasons]]. She appears in {{OoS}} on the [[Game Boy Color]]. She also makes a cameo appearance in {{TMC}}.
