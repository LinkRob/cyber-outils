The {{Term/Store|Sato Koda Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Sato Koda Shrine}}<br/>Support and Guidance}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Support and Guidance''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Sato Koda Shrine}} is located on top of {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana|link}} within the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}, though it is initially buried beneath the ground. It is one of three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that must be accessed as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana}} from the direction of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, he will come across {{Term|BotW|Bazz|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Seggin|link}}. When spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Bazz}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} of the blue-eyed {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} that have appeared in the area.<ref>{{Cite|While you are here, I should warn you... {{Color|BotW Red|Blue-eyed Guardians}} have appeared in {{Color|BotW Blue|Upland Zorana}}.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> Both he and {{Term|BotW|Seggin}} reveal that they appeared last during {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}'s sacred Trial.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently, those things last appeared when {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Mipha}} challenged her sacred trial.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The same creatures that appeared when {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Mipha}} challenged her sacred trial have returned to {{Color|BotW Blue|Upland Zorana}}.|Seggin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Seggin}} wonders if the Trial has been activated for someone else.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Mipha}} defeated them with her usual grace and skill. I wonder...is the trial calling to someone new?|Seggin|BotW}}</ref>

In {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find three {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher|link}} and one {{Term|BotW|Guardian Turret|link}}. Though they are uncorrupted by {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}, they must be defeated in order to reveal the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. Once the last {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} has been defeated, {{Term|BotW|Sato Koda Shrine}} will rise from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|When those {{Color|BotW Blue|blue-eyed Guardians}} left, a {{Color|BotW Red|strange shrine}} appeared. I knew those {{Color|BotW Blue|Guardians}} were related to the trial!|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sato Koda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Sato Koda. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Sato Koda|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Sato Koda Shrine}} features a large contraption set upon an {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}}. At its beginning, a giant {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} consistently spawns, but it is unable to reach the end before falling into the void. Its end is a large {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}, into which the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} must be inserted.

Using the {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can create pathways for the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}, preventing it from falling. He can also use the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} to freeze the stone slabs in place, preventing them from titling to one side when the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} falls on top of them. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} will eventually fall near the bottom of the contraption, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}} that periodically juts upwards. The {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} will launch the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} into the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}, opening the portcullis blocking the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Sato Koda}}, they will commend him for completing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero.}}|Sato Koda|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} one of {{Plural|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Ruta's Emblem.}}|Sato Koda|BotW}}</ref> After giving {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|display=Emblem}}, {{Term|BotW|Sato Koda}} will reveal that collecting all three of them will unlock a new trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Sato Koda|BotW}}</ref> They then state how many are left to obtain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Sato Koda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Sato Koda|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Sato Koda Shrine}} is the last {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} visits during "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song}}," {{Term|BotW|Sato Koda}} will tell him to return to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Ruta...}}|Sato Koda|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} sits on a platform above the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can access it by building a staircase with the {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. Inside, he will recover a {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}.
