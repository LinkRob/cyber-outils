The '''Firecorn''' is an explosive material featured in the {{ALttP|Ishinomori}}. It is only found in the [[Palace of Darkness]].

==Location and Uses==
When {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Zora (Ishinomori)|link}} tells {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link|link}} that one of the {{Plural|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Maiden|link}} is imprisoned in the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Palace of Darkness|link}}, he warns the young hero of the dangerous {{Plural|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Firecorn}} that are kept in there.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Yesss,   and one is imprisoned in Crystal in the palace nearby...but you   shouldn't go there! In legendsss that place is known as the Palace of   Darkness... Ganon's servantss have ssstockpiled the dangerousss,   explosssive firecorn there...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=5,6}}</ref> Despite his warnings, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} goes to the palace anyway and eventually finds the storeroom where {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Ganon|link}}'s minions keep the {{Plural|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Firecorn}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The storeroom of the palace! If this went off, they wouldn't call it the Palace of Darkness!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=8}}</ref> {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} is eventually caught by a {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Rocklops|link}} and warns him of what could happen if he set off the {{Plural|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Firecorn}} in the palace,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=You fool! you know what will happen if you use the firecorn here?!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref> but the young hero disregards his warning and takes a {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Hinox|link}}'s bomb belt that was wrapped around it.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=We'll all go up in smoke! But if you guys are going to get me...we'll all go together!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref> Giving Ephemerelda ones of the bombs from the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Hinox}}'s belt, the bomb eventually detonates the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Firecorn}} stockpile and destroys the entire palace, freeing the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Maiden}} in the process.
