{{Term/Store|Harlequin||Series, TFoE}} is the second boss in {{TFoE}}.<ref>{{Cite|See how Harlequin captures Koridians?|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref> He is located at the {{Term|TFoE|Harlequin Bazaar}}, and is encountered again at the {{Term|TFoE|Lupay (Stage)|link}} area.

==Biography==
{{Term|TFoE|Harlequin}} is an anthropomorphic pig who is a minion of {{Term|TFoE|Ganon|link}}. He is located at his Face of Evil, the {{Term|TFoE|Harlequin Bazaar}}. There he enslaves {{Plural|TFoE|Koridian|link}} by turning them into {{Term|TFoE|Goriya|link}} minions after they lose all of their {{Plural|TFoE|Rupee|link}} while gambling at his casino.<ref>{{Cite|Do you know what it means when you lose your last Ruby? Now you work for me. Take him away!|Harlequin|TFoE}}</ref> He also has {{Term|TFoE|Odranoel|link}} the astronomer imprisoned at the end of his Bazaar. The source of {{Term|TFoE|Harlequin}}'s magic seems to come from the heart-shaped insignia on his sleeve, which releases a yellow beam. He also demonstrates the ability to disappear and reappear in puffs of smoke, and uses this to taunt {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}}. Yet {{Term|TFoE|Link}} prevails and defeats {{Term|TFoE|Harlequin}}, who deflates like a balloon and drops the {{Term|TFoE|Key|link}} to the astronomer's room.

{{Term|TFoE|Harlequin}} is later encountered again at the {{Term|TFoE|Lupay (Stage)|link}} area, and is defeated the same way.

===Characteristics===
{{Term|TFoE|Harlequin}} has a simple pattern where he moves back and forth in the room he is fought in. He relentlessly throws projectiles at {{Term|TFoE|Link}} when he is within his line of vision. However, these can be blocked by {{Term|TFoE|Link}}'s {{Term|TFoE|Shield|link}}. {{Term|TFoE|Harlequin}} is defeated with just the {{Term|TFoE|Sword}} and takes several strikes. Although while he seems to possess the ability to vanish in his cutscenes, he remains visible all through his battles.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|TFoE|Harlequin}} is similar to {{TWoG}} boss {{Term|TFoE|Hektan|link}}. Both bosses demonstrate the ability to disappear and reappear in their cutscenes, yet do not actually possess this ability in their boss battles.
