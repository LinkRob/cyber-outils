==Location and Uses==
Glass Short Swords are powerful yet fragile {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} that can be found throughout {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}. Being a variety of {{Term|CoH|Short Sword|link}}, only {{Term|CoH|Link|link}} can wield them, dealing 3 damage to enemies but breaking as soon as he takes damage.

They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Glass Dagger|link}}.
