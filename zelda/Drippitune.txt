'''Drippitune''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Drippitune|This amphibious monster's croaking song can call rain from a cloudless sky.<ref name="Drippitune">{{Cite|This amphibious monster's croaking song can call rain from a cloudless sky.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Drippitune are [[frog]]-like monsters whose singing causes rain. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Ribbitune [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], and it costs three Echo energy to summon.
