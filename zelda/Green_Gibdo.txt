==Other Appearances==

{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Plural|CoH|Green Gibdo}} are the stronger counterpart to the {{Term|CoH|Gibdo|link}}, only appearing in caves and dungeons. Unlike their weaker counterparts, they move every three beats, crouching on the second beat before attacking {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} on the third beat. If struck by a {{Term|CoH|Fire Arrow|link}} or by a fire projectile cast by a {{Term|CoH|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} or the {{Term|CoH|Fire Rod|link}}, their bandages will burn off and they will turn into a {{Term|CoH|ReDead|link}}.
