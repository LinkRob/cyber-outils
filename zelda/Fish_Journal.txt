The {{Term/Store|Fish Journal||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref> It is used to document the amount and type of [[fish]] [[Link]] catches while [[fishing]], as well as the size record of each.<ref name="inv desc">{{Cite|A bobber-fishing journal. Press {{Icon|TPHD A}} to open it and view the largest size and number of fish Link's caught.|N/A|TPHD}}</ref>

==Appearances==
==={{TP|-}}===
The Fish Journal in {{TP}} is obtained after catching a fish with the [[Fishing Rod]] obtained from {{Term|TPHD|Uli|link}}, [[Colin|Colin's]] mother. It keeps a record of all the fish that Link has caught while [[Bobber Fishing]]; that is, any fish that are caught while [[Lure Fishing|lure fishing]] in [[Fishing Hole#Twilight Princess|Hena's Fishing Hole]] will not be recorded in the journal. Moreover, the journal does not record any [[Skullfish]] or [[Bombfish]] caught since they are considered illegal.<ref>{{Cite|You caught a skullfish! It looks stronger than it is... This fish is forbidden, so it won't be added to your fish journal.|N/A|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You caught a bombfish! This handy fish can be used as a bomb! Actually, catching this fish is forbidden, so it won't be added to your fish journal.|N/A|TP}}</ref>

The Fish Journal is located in the Start menu, and it shows a picture of the type of fish, how many have been caught, and the biggest size of the fish caught thus far.<ref name="inv desc"/>

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, the Fish Journal is only accessible after receiving the Fishing Rod from the [[Old Wayfarer]]. It can be viewed from the left portion inside the Collection menu. Like in {{TP|-}}, the Fish Journal records a picture of each fish, how many of each have been caught as well as the size of the largest one caught thus far, and the total number of fish caught.
