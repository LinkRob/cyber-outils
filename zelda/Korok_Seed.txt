== Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Korok Seeds are given by [[Korok]]s when they are found throughout [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh, and my friends are hiding in lots of different places too! Don't be shy about poking your nose into suspicious places!|Korok|BotW}}</ref> They possess a distinct smell.<ref name="Inventory" /> When [[Link]] receives his first Seed, he is asked to return it to the Korok [[Hestu]].<ref>{{Cite|Well, if you run into Hestu, please return this to him.|Korok|BotW}}</ref> Korok Seeds are actually the beads used in [[Hestu's Maracas]]. When first found, Hestu asks Link to retrieve his Maracas from a group of [[Bokoblin]]s nearby. After Link returns them, Hestu realizes the Seeds were taken by the other Koroks as part of a prank.<ref>{{Cite|But wait! There's something wrong with my maracas! The {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seeds}} inside are gone! How am I supposed to dance now? Shoko...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The children of the forest must have taken the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seeds}} from my beloved maracas. I never thought I would be the target of one of their pranks. Shoko...|Hestu|BotW}}</ref>

In exchange for Korok Seeds, Hestu will add one slot to Link's {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, [[Bow#Breath of the Wild|Bow]], or [[Shields in Breath of the Wild|Shield]] stashes.<ref>{{Cite|If I had just {{Color|BotW Red|one Korok seed}}, I could sing, dance and use my {{Color|BotW Blue|powers}} to help you. You see, I have the power of {{Color|BotW Blue|inventory}} expansion! So let me know if you find some {{Color|BotW Red|Korok seeds}}!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref> Link can fully upgrade his inventory with only 441 Korok Seeds. If all 900 Seeds are returned to Hestu, he will reward Link with a "[[Hestu's Gift|gift of friendship]]."<ref>{{Cite|All of the forest children returned to Korok Forest! [...] You've maxed all of your stashes! So let me give you this!|Hestu|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A gift of friendship given to you by Hestu. It smells pretty bad.|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Korok Seeds can be obtained in Chapter Quests and certain Challenge Quests by uncovering hidden Koroks, and can additionally be received as rewards for completing Challenge Quests centered around Hestu's involvement. Once a Warrior locates a Korok, it will give them a Korok Seed. Koroks who Warriors find will remain out in the open as a way to help players keep track which Koroks they have found.  

There is a total of 175 Korok Seeds found in the game, and are divided as such:

*122 Korok Seeds are hidden within the 20 Story missions.
*12 Korok Seeds are hidden with 11 specific Challenge missions.
*And 41 Korok Seeds are obtained as rewards, which are further divided:
**26 of these Korok Seeds are obtained as part of the First Clear Rewards for completing specific battles.
**The outlier 15 Korok Seeds are obtained by completing the Hestu-specific Side Quest "Helping with Heights".


Once Hestu is unlocked as a character after completing the [[Scenario]], "[[Freeing Korok Forest]]", Link will be able to complete side quests that will be able to give everyone extra inventory, just like in {{BotW|-}}. These side quests, found under the Battle Support tab in the Quest search bar, have [[Hestu's Maracas]] as their icons.

After completing the "A Most Peculiar Korok" [[Side Quest]], Korok Seeds can be seen on the Battle Info screen of a Scenario that has them, with collected Korok Seeds being colored in white and uncollected Korok Seeds being left in a very dull dark gray.
{{Noncanon|End}}
