==Location and Uses==
[[File:ALttP Link shooting Silver Arrow at Ganon.png|left|thumb|Link shooting a Silver Arrow towards a stunned Ganon in ''A Link to the Past'']]
Silver Arrows are legendary [[Arrow]]s with incredible holy powers, specifically used to defeat [[Ganon]] in {{TLoZ}} and {{ALttP}}. In the only two early games they appeared in the core series, even though the [[Master Sword]] is present in the latter, they are the only weapon that is capable of eliminating the Demon King, who will recover from any [[Sword]] wounds if not immediately pierced by a Silver Arrow.<ref name="Faerie" /> Therefore, they are necessary to complete these titles. 

In later installments of the series since ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', these special Arrows are replaced by the [[Light Arrow]]s and performed a similar role, although in comparison they actually function the complete opposite, in that they merely stun Ganon or his human form [[Ganondorf]], not to outright destroy him (although in some games they can instantly kill regular enemies like Silver Arrows did in ''A Link to the Past''); The Master Sword is usually used to deal the final blow instead. Aside from one occasion in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]]''/''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Ages]]''<nowiki/>' [[Linked Game|linked game]] where in its final battle the King of Evil was not fully revived, the Silver Arrows are the ''only'' weapon known in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] to completely destroy Ganon in his demonic beast form, unlike the Blade of Evil's Bane. Silver Arrows also do not consume [[Magic]], although in the original game, due to the lack of ammunition mechanic, as with normal Arrows, one [[Rupee]] is required for [[Link]] to shoot them each time.

==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Bow, and wooden and silver arrows|Link can use them only if he has both the bow and an arrow. For each arrow Link shoots, he loses one ruby. Oh, by the way, there are some enemies that can be disposed of using only the bow and arrow. So be alert!}}
[[File:TLoZ Link firing Silver Arrow.png|left|thumb|224x224px|Link firing a Silver Arrow in ''The Legend of Zelda'']]
In {{TLoZ}}, the Silver Arrow is found in [[Level-9]] of both the main quest and the [[Second Quest]], as the second key item alongside the [[Red Ring]]. In the final battle, after hitting Ganon 15 times with the [[Sword]], 8 times with the [[White Sword]] or only 4 times with the [[Magical Sword]], the beast becomes stunned and [[Link]] must use a single Silver Arrow to finish him off, causing the Dark Beast to ultimately explode into dust and dropping the [[Triforce of Power]] afterwards.<ref>{{Cite manual| quote= Link's no match for Ganon and his might. Whatever attack Link uses, Ganon just repels him. But if Link could only use the magical sword and some other object, he'll manage to topple the mighty Ganon. ''Other object''? What's that?| game= TLoZ| page= 39}}</ref> These special Arrows also deal double the damage normal Arrows does to enemies, equal in power to the Magical Sword and [[Bomb|Bombs]]. Due to graphical limitations of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]], rather than silver, the weapon appears blue. In addition, as with regular arrows, a [[Rupee]] is subtracted every time Link shoots a Silver Arrow and therefore cannot use them if his amount of currency reaches zero.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, Silver Arrows are given to Link by the [[Fat Fairy]] inside the [[Pyramid of Power]], once the way has been opened with a [[Super Bomb]].<ref name="Faerie"/> Significantly stronger than in their previous appearance, they are much more powerful versions of the standard Arrows Link obtains and are the strongest weapon in the game, outclassing the [[Master Sword Lv3|Golden Sword]] in raw power. When used as weapons in the field, they can kill ''any'' enemy with a single shot, including even some [[Bosses in A Link to the Past|bosses]]. Keeping in tradition with the original ''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'' game, the Silver Arrows are required to kill Ganon, although it takes four shots to finally defeat him rather than one, once again making the main antagonist explode to his death. They are to be used when Ganon is stunned after being struck by the [[Master Sword]] (or any upgraded form) in the late part of the [[Final Boss]] battle.<ref name="Faerie"/>

==Trivia==
*The Silver Arrows' function as [[Ganon]]'s only weakness in the earliest ''Zelda'' titles prior to the existence of [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' is similar to more modern {{Wp|werewolf}} legends, in which a [[wikipedia:Silver_bullet|silver bullet]] is the only weapon that can kill a werewolf. This may be because the precious metal [[wikipedia:Silver|silver]] was considered a holy element in some religious cultures, especially Judaism.
*After ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda|BS The Legend of Zelda (Map 2)]]'' and ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|Ancient Stone Tablets]]'', both released in 1997 on the Japan-only [[Satellaview]] for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super Famicom]], the Silver Arrows would not appear again in any game of the franchise until 17 years later, with ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'', in 2014. Despite this, they remain absent in the main series since ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', which was released in 1991.
*An artwork from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]''<nowiki/>'s [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening — Nintendo Player's Guide|official player's guide]] referring to ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' exists of [[Link]] about to fire the Silver Arrow at a ghostly Ganon.
*As this weapon is specifically designed to destroy Ganon entirely and is even greater in damage than the strongest [[Master Sword]] variant - the [[Master Sword Lv3|Golden Sword]], in ''A Link to the Past'' (and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'' in spite of not appearing in-game), the Silver Arrows are arguably the most powerful weapon in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]].
**Although not outright confirmed, it's likely the Silver Arrows possess the [[Power to Repel Evil|power to repel evil]] to the highest degree.
