The {{Term/Store|Ocean Title Deed||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
<!-- {{Color|MM3D Red|Ocean Title Deed}}<br/>The title deed for the Deku Flower in Zora Hall. You traded your Mountain Title Deed for this. -->
The Ocean Title Deed is a claim to the [[Deku Flower]] located inside [[Lulu]]'s room at [[Zora Hall]]. If Link approaches the Deku Flower, a [[Business Scrub]] will pop out from it. If Link speaks to the Business Scrub as a [[Goron Link|Goron]], the Scrub will inform him that he is interested in moving to [[Goron Village]], where the air is clean.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for stopping by! Oh! You're from the mountains? I'm selling {{Color|MM3D Green|Green Potions}}, but I'm focusing my marketing efforts on Zoras. Actually, I'd like to do business someplace where it's cooler and the air is clean. But to open a business in another place... You need to go through the proper channels first. Do you know what I mean?|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> If Link presents the [[Mountain Title Deed]], the Business Scrub will offer his own Deku Flower in return.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that's a {{Color|MM3D Red|Mountain Title Deed}}! Thank you! I'll give you this land in exchange!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> If Link accepts, the Business Scrub will give Link the Ocean Title Deed and leave for his new Deku Flower in Goron Village.<ref>{{Cite|You traded your Mountain Title Deed for an {{Color|MM3D Red|Ocean Title Deed}}!|N/A|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm going right back to the swamp!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>

With the Ocean Title Deed in Link's possession, he can freely use the Deku Flower in Lulu's room to fly to a ledge in her room. Link can alternatively repurpose the Ocean Title Deed as toilet paper by giving it to {{Term|MM3D|??? (Majora's Mask)|link}} in the [[Stock Pot Inn]]'s bathroom after midnight each night.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|Crisis in the toilet!}}<br/>You helped the mysterious hand in the restroom. It's no good to be out of {{Color|MM3D Red|paper}} in the toilet!<br/>Stock Pot Inn - restroom|Bombers' Notebook|MM3D}}</ref> Link can also trade the Ocean Title Deed to the Business Scrub outside [[Sakon's Hideout]] in [[Ikana Canyon]] in exchange for a [[Huge Rupee]] and the Deku Flower he has claimed.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for stopping by! You're from the ocean, aren't you? I'm here to sell {{Color|MM3D Dark Blue|Blue Potion}}. Actually, I want to do business in the sea breeze while listening to the sound of the waves. But to open a business in another place... You need to go through the proper channels first. Do you know what I mean?|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! If that isn't an {{Color|MM3D Red|Ocean Title Deed}}. Let me have that! Please. If I have that, my dreams will come true! I'm not asking for it for free. I'll give you this!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> However, if he does so, the Business Scrub will move to the Deku Flower in Zora Hall and Link will not be able to access it.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yeeeeah! The nut-brown beauties are waiting!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>
