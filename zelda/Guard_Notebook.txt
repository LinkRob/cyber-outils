The {{Term/Store|Guard Notebook||Series, PH}} is an item in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
If [[Link]] returns the [[Kaleidoscope]] to Hoiger Howgendoogen, a member of the [[Ho Ho Tribe]] and its rightful owner,<ref>{{Cite|Regardless, this appears to belong to me. Thanks for delivering it!|Hoiger Howgendoogen|PH}}</ref> he will give him the guard notebook in return, which he found floating in the ocean.<ref>{{Cite|I saw it floating among the waves and picked it up.|Hoiger Howgendoogen|PH}}</ref> Link must take it to [[Nyave]] aboard the [[Traveler's Ship]] in the Southwestern Sea, who had been looking for it after he had lost it.<ref>{{Cite|And me... A wanderer of the seas, always hunting for my guard notebook!|Nyave|PH}}</ref> In return, Link receives the [[Wood Heart]], which should be taken to the [[Old Wayfarer]].

==Trivia==
*The symbol on the front of the Guard Notebook slightly resembles the [[Eye Symbol|Sheikah Eye Symbol]].
