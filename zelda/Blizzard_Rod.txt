{{Compendium/Extract|Blizzard Rod}}
{{Plural|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} are one-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}. They are superior counterparts to {{Plural|BotW|Ice Rod|link}}, crafted from refined {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} found at the summit of {{Term|BotW|Hebra Peak|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} are most commonly wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Blizzrobe|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} are imbued with {{Term|BotW|Ice}} elemental energy, generating three flurries of {{Term|BotW|Ice}} when swung. However, {{Plural|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} are fragile and will break easily if they make direct contact with {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} will also break simply from extended use.<ref name="Inventory"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} executes a {{Term|BotW|Spin Attack|link}} while wielding a {{Term|BotW|Blizzard Rod}}, he will loose eight blasts at once in different directions. They also provide one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}, though they have no effect in {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Climate}}. When thrown with the [[R Button]], {{Plural|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} will not leave {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s hand, and they will instead produce three flurries of {{Term|BotW|Ice}} that will advance in the direction they are thrown. {{Plural|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} will temporarily lose their magical properties if used excessively, requiring a brief cooldown period before they can be used again.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kula|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, he will ask to be shown a "rod with the power of ice," which he describes as having the power to freeze {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll do it!] Hooray! Show me a {{Color|BotW Red|rod with the power of ice}}, please! You can wave this rod to freeze monsters in their tracks! {{Color|BotW Blue|Ice Wizzrobes}} love to wreak havoc with them.|Kula|BotW}}</ref> After listening to him, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will receive the "{{Term|BotW|A Freezing Rod|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings a {{Term|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} to {{Term|BotW|Kula}}, he will be amazed by how it sparkles.<ref>{{Cite|Is that a {{Color|BotW Blue|blizzard rod}}?! Incredible shimmer! Unbelievable sparkle!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Mr. Hero. Your weapon is so amazingly, wonderfully sparkly! I must give you something as thanks!|Kula|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} are not made of flammable materials and thus can safely be used around open {{Term|BotW|Fire|link|display=Flames}} or {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} are also not made of magnetic materials and cannot be manipulated with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Blizzard Rod}} do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they will float in sufficiently deep bodies of {{Term|BotW|Water|link}}.
