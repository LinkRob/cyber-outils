{{Term/Store|Konba||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Konba|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Konba}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} man who can be found camped at the {{Term|BotW|Footrace Check-In|link}}. He operates the {{Term|BotW|Footrace|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}.

When approached, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} notices that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} looks tough and challenges him to a friendly race.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hey, friend!}} You look like a tough boy. But how tough are you? Tough enough to challenge me to a {{Color|BotW Red|race}}?|Konba|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} declines his offer, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will be disappointed, as he finally thought that he found a worthy opponent.<ref>{{Cite|"[I don't feel like it.] {{Big|Noooo!}} What a letdown... I thought I'd finally met a tough boy with some real potential. {{Small|Guess I thought wrong.}}|Konba|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will introduce stakes to their race.<ref>{{Cite|[You're on.] {{Big|Yahoo!}} Now we're talking! This is fantastic! But every challenge has to have some stakes.|Konba|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can refuse {{Term|BotW|Konba}}'s stakes, which causes him to say he will never get anywhere in life with such an attitude.<ref>{{Cite|[You lost me.] {{Big|What?!}} You're giving up before we even start? {{Small|Yikes...}} You'll never get anywhere in life with that attitude!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to go along with it, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will leave to set up the {{Term|BotW|Footrace}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's get started.] {{Big|Yesss!}} Now you're getting into the spirit of things! I'll go set things up. Don't go anywhere!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Konba}} returns, he explains that the objective of the {{Term|BotW|Footrace}} is the pair of {{Plural|BotW|Big Flag|link}} on top of {{Term|BotW|Mount Rhoam|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|OK!}} Thanks for waiting! I'm so ready I could burst, and I bet you are too! So let's begin...the {{Color|BotW Red|race}}! The goal is the {{Color|BotW Red|flags on top of the mountain}}! There's only two of us, so the race ends when someone reaches them!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> However, he reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that it is a {{Term|BotW|Footrace}}, not a "hoof race," and he will be disqualified if he rides a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|One more thing!}} You could probably guess this already, but this is a {{Color|BotW Red|foot race}}, not a {{Color|BotW Red|hoof race}}. If you ride your horse or run away from the goal instead of toward it, you'll be {{Color|BotW Red|disqualified}}!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> After explaining this, he will begin the {{Term|BotW|Footrace}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|And now...it's time! Get ready... Go!}}|Konba|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the {{Plural|BotW|Big Flag}} before {{Term|BotW|Konba}} does, he will be shocked to see that he lost.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Done!}} Wait... You're done too? You were done...first? Hrm... {{Small|That means I lose...}}|Konba|BotW}}</ref> Back at the {{Term|BotW|Footrace Check-In}}, he reluctantly rewards {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I wasn't REALLY trying my best... but a bet is a bet! Here's your {{Color|BotW Red|reward}} for a race well run!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Konba}} finishes the {{Term|BotW|Footrace}} before {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he will gloat and cheer for his victory.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Wheeee! I did it!}} I won! You gotta give those flat feet some exercise if you ever wanna beat me, friend!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> When they return to the {{Term|BotW|Footrace Check-In}}, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he did well, but he will have to try harder if he wants to beat him.<ref>{{Cite|You did OK, but if you're ever gonna beat me, you've gotta try harder than that!|Konba|BotW}}</ref>

After finishing a {{Term|BotW|Footrace}}, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants to start another one.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Whew!}} So...wanna race again?|Konba|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will become excited and explains that the rules are the same as before.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Woooo-hooo! I love this guy! All right!}} Same rules as before. Whoever reaches the goal first wins!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Konba}} will be disappointed, but he encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use his traveling as a chance to build his leg muscles.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Aww.}} That's...{{Small|not the answer I hoped for...}} Use all that traveling you do as a chance to build up your leg muscles!|Konba|BotW}}</ref> He also invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back to the {{Term|BotW|Footrace Check-In}} if he ever wants to race again.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Whew!}} If you ever want to go again, I'd jump at the chance! See you around!|Konba|BotW}}</ref>
