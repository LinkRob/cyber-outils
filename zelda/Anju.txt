{{Term/Store|Anju||Series, MM, MM3D, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM}} & 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> She makes her first appearance in {{MM}}.

==Biography==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Anju is the inn keeper at the [[Stock Pot Inn]] and best friend of [[Cremia]], the owner of [[Romani Ranch]].<ref>{{Cite|Cremia will take us in. She is your best friend, right?|Anju's Mother|MM}}</ref> She is engaged to [[Kafei]], [[Mayor Dotour]]'s son, and they planned to have their wedding on the day of the [[Carnival of Time]]. However, certain circumstances have caused Anju to start doubting her fiancé.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder if Kafei is really at Cremia's place...|Anju|MM}}</ref> Although her father, [[Tortus]], has passed away, her [[Anju's Mother|mother]] and [[Granny|grandmother]] live with her in the inn.<ref name="Tortus"/> She is one of the characters in the game with an entry in the [[Bombers' Notebook]]. 

Anju is portrayed as apologizing quite often and appears somewhat nervous. She is not very good at cooking and, according to [[Kafei's Diary]], tends to leave things until the last minute.<ref>{{Cite|What is Anju, the innkeeper, bad at doing? Cooking|Keaton|MM}}</ref><ref name="Mask"/> She also seems to have trouble remembering customers' faces, which can cause her to deliver reservations to the wrong person, where [[Link]] can use this as an advantage to [[Room Key|gain such]] that was meant for a [[Link (Goron)|Goron sharing his name]].<ref>{{Cite|My mother is always telling me to remember our customer's faces, but I tend to forget them...|Anju|MM}}</ref> 

====Reuniting Kafei and Anju====
{{Main|Reuniting Kafei and Anju}}
[[File:Anju Sweeping.png|left|thumb|Anju sweeping with her broom in {{MM|-}}]]
The famous sidequest involving Anju and Kafei is the only sidequest in the Bombers' Notebook that takes an entire 3-day cycle. When they were children, Anju and Kafei themselves promised to marry each other on the day of the Carnival of Time, exchanging the [[Sun's Mask|Sun's]] and [[Moon's Mask]]s during the event.<ref>{{Cite|We promised each other when we were children that we'd marry on the day of the Carnival of Time.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> However, Kafei disappeared with his wedding ceremony mask one month before the wedding. After he was turned into a child by the [[Skull Kid]],<ref>{{Cite|I was turned into this by a strange imp wearing a mask...|Kafei|MM}}</ref> his Sun Mask was stolen by [[Sakon]] as he was on his way to see the [[Great Fairy]] about his desperate situation.<ref>{{Cite|When I was turned into this, I went to see the Great Fairy in the shrine near the North Gate... But on the way, my precious mask--a wedding ceremony mask--was stolen from me by some prancing man with a grinning face!|Kafei|MM}}</ref> Despite Kafei's terrible fate, Anju does not know what has become of him or why he disappeared. She is afraid to meet her fiance and hear the reason as to why he escaped, for she fears his disappearance might have been because of her.<ref>{{Cite|I'm...actually...afraid to meet him...and to hear the reason why he wanted to disappear... It might be because of... me...|Anju|MM}}</ref>

Anju's Mother puts ideas into Anju's head that her fiancé might have run off with her best friend [[Cremia]], who actually has a crush on Kafei (told by [[Romani]]).<ref>{{Cite|Romani knows... My sister, Cremia, has someone in town she likes... But that person is supposed to get married the day of the carnival. It's hard for my sister... Going into town...|Romani|MM}}</ref> Since Cremia needs strength from a partner and business support from Kafei's mother [[Madame Aroma]] to run Romani Ranch, Kafei running off with the ranch owner would allow her to have the stability she needs.<ref>{{Cite|Besides, think about Cremia. She needs strength from a partner and business support from Madame Aroma. If Kafei really has run off with Cremia, she'll get both.|Anju's Mother|MM}}</ref>

When [[Link]] intervenes to help reunite Kafei and Anju, the young hero meets with the inn keeper in the kitchen at her request.<ref>{{Cite|Tonight at 11:30, please come to our kitchen. We'll talk then...|Anju|MM}}</ref> She then asks him to deliver a [[Letter to Kafei|letter]] to the [[Mailbox]] in her stead, hoping to get a description of her fiancé if Link is to meet with him.<ref>{{Cite|Here is my letter. Please put this in a postbox. When this is delivered...When he receives my letter... You should be able to meet him!|Anju|MM}}</ref> Anju is too afraid to do this on her own, so she tells Link to pass on the message that Anju is waiting for him.<ref>{{Cite|Please tell him that Anju is waiting for him. And please, after you've seen him... Tell me how he looked. I'm scared...I...I can't go.|Anju|MM}}</ref>

Link meets Kafei, who explains his situation and hands over [[Pendant of Memories|his pendant]] to him.<ref>{{Cite|This pendant... Give it to Anju.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> Once Link delivers the pendant to Anju, she will once again believe in Kafei and decides to wait for his return despite her mother's wishes and the fact that everyone else will be taking refuge in Romani Ranch.<ref>{{Cite|I have decided to wait for him. I've made my promise... I'm fine with this. I believe him.|Anju|MM}}</ref>[[File:MM Anju Wearing a Wedding Dress Model.png|thumb|217x217px|Anju on her wedding day in {{MM|-}}]]On the night of the [[Final Day]], with just a few hours left until the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] falls, Anju is desperately waiting for Kafei in her room. The two lovers at last reunite and exchange their masks and oaths when Kafei returns from having retrieved the Sun Mask that was stolen from him.<ref>{{Cite|Let us exchange the promised masks. We have exchanged our oaths and have become a couple.|Anju|MM}}</ref> In return for witnessing this event, they grant Link the [[Couple's Mask]].<ref>{{Cite|You are our witnesses. Please accept this mask.|Anju|MM}}</ref> Even though the Moon is just a couple of hours from [[Terminan Apocalypse|destroying]] Termina, they urge the young [[Hero of Time]] to take refuge, for they will stay and greet the morning together.<ref>{{Cite|Please take refuge. We are fine here. We shall greet the morning... together.|Anju|MM}}</ref>

During the [[New Day|ending credits]], Anju can be seen outside [[Clock Town]] having her [[Anju and Kafei's Wedding|wedding ceremony]].

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Anju}}
{{Term|TMC|Anju}} can always be found standing outside of her {{Term|TMC|Anju's Henhouse|link|display= Henhouse}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} in {{TMC}}. If spoken to, she will ask {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to help her catch her escaped {{Plural|TMC|Cucco|link}}, much like the Cucco Lady from {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, no! This is horrible! My cuccos have gotten out! Can you catch them for me?|Anju|TMC}}</ref>

Each time, Link must collect at least a certain number of {{Plural|TMC|Cucco}} within a certain time limit. They are scattered across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}, and must be picked up and thrown into the pen. Each time the hero completes the challenge, {{Term|TMC|Anju}} rewards him with a number of {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} depending on what kinds of {{Plural|TMC|Cucco}} he retrieved; each white {{Term|TMC|Cucco}} is worth 10 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}}, and each gold {{Term|TMC|Cucco}} is worth 50 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}}. {{Term|TMC|Anju}} will also either reward him with {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}} or a red {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} depending on which stage of the challenge he is on; in addition, once the tenth challenge is complete, {{Term|TMC|Anju}} will give Link a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks! Here's your reward.|Anju|TMC}}</ref>

The challenge becomes more difficult each time, with the number of {{Plural|TMC|Cucco}} needed, their distance from the pen, and the time available to catch them all changing. After a certain point, they become impossible to complete without the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}}, and the last few rounds require the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}} to complete.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:AnjuMangaMM.png|thumb|right|100px|Anju atop the Clock Tower]]
==={{MM|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{MM|Himekawa}}, Anju plays a different role than she does in the game. {{Term|MM|Link|link}} first meets her after he is transformed into [[Deku Link]] by Skull Kid. She finds him and brings him home to care for him. She also informs Link that he is no longer in [[Hyrule]]. Soon, Skull Kid attacks her and chases Link throughout Clock Town. She is also called by [[Tatl]] while Link is fighting Skull Kid with Kafei atop the [[Clock Tower]] on the Final Day.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= That fairy told me to come.|book= MM (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= }}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{TMC|Himekawa}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} first meets {{Term|TMC|Anju}} when he is transformed into a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} and accidentally traps himself in her chicken coop. The chicks attempt to eat {{Term|TMC|Link}} while he is {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized, as they mistake him for a bug.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Wha-? A bug? I love bugs! And I'm hungry!|book= TMC (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= }}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Anju}} then comes in time to save him by distracting the baby chicks by feeding them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Here, cucco chicks! It's dinner time!|book= TMC (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= }}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} tries to look up her skirt as a {{Term|TMC|Minish}}, but instead nearly gets stepped on.

== Trivia ==

* She is the [[Terminan]] counterpart of the [[Cucco Lady]] from {{OoT}}. Anju can be distinguished by her not smiling as often.
* Similarly to [[Kafei]], Anju is one of the most popular [[Side Quest|sidequest]] characters in {{MM}}, if not the series as a whole, due to her romantic relationship with him and both never giving up on achieving their goals with the help of [[Link]].[[File:Kafeianjureunited.jpg|thumb|228x228px|If one looks closely, Anju is not seen smiling when she is hugging her lover Kafei in the original version of {{MM|-}}]]
* When Anju and Kafei are finally reunited by Link and the two are hugging each other, oddly, her expression is not reacting with happiness, likely a developer oversight. This is fixed in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]''.
