{{Term/Store|Militron||Series, TFoE}} (pronounced {{IPA|ˈmɪlɪtɹɒn}} {{Respell|MI|li-tron}})<ref>{{Cite|Look! How Militron makes his warriors.|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref> is a location in {{TFoE}}. It is unlocked after completing [[Harlequin Bazaar]].

==Features and Overview==
Militron is the name of the area that surrounds [[Militron (Boss)|Militron]]'s Face of Evil. Is it located in the northeast of [[Koridai]], represented by a mountain peak carved in the resemblance of Militron's likeness. A waterfall cascades down this mountain from the Face's mouth, which forms the [[Spearfish Falls]]. An armored military base is constructed inside the Face, and is under Militron's control.

[[Link]] enters the area from the mountain side and waterfall, where he meets [[Gwonam]] who informs him that Militron is turning [[Koridian]]s into warriors. Link climbs the mountain side and eventually reaches the Face of Militron. A boulder blocks an opening in the Face's teeth, which is destroyed with either 10 [[Bomb]]s or the [[Power Glove#The Faces of Evil|Power Glove]]. Inside is the military base, where [[Armos]] patrol the area. At the end of the first section is a [[Bomb]]-able wall, and beyond it a pair of wooden gates leading into the next area. Link fights [[Darknut]]s in the next room, eventually making his way to a faced doorway.

Link confronts Militron in the next chamber. After he is defeated, the [[Winged Helmet]] is left behind. A [[Triforce Map]] then appears. After completing the stage, {{Term|TFoE|Glutko|link}} is unlocked.
