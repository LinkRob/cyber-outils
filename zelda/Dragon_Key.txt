The {{Term/Store|Dragon Key||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref> 

==Location and Uses==
The Dragon Key is found on [[Goron Mountain]], past a number of pits. The only way to get it is after obtaining [[Moosh]]. [[Link]] must use Moosh to fly over the pits and retrieve the key. It is needed to stop the waterfall flowing in front of the [[Dancing Dragon Dungeon]].
