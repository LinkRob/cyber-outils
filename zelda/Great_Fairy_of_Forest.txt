The {{Term/Store|Great Fairy of Forest||Series, FS}} is a character in {{FS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 263 {{Exp Game|FS}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Great Fairy of Forest is the guardian of the [[Sea of Trees]].<ref name="Guardian">{{Cite|I am the Great Fairy of Forest, guardian of the Sea of Trees. You have done well to gather so much treasure. I dub you...little eggs, waiting to hatch into heroes. I grant you each a {{Color|FS Orange|Silver Key}}.|Great Fairy of Forest|FS}}</ref> She appears from her [[Fairy Fountain|Fountain]] at the end of the Sea of Trees after the [[Links]] have defeated [[Big Manhandla]]. There, she bestows the Links a Key alongside a title befitting their achievements. The type of Key that she distributes is based on the amount of [[Rupee]]s the Links have collected as well as whether they have defeated [[Vaati]] with a given set of Keys. If the Links have collected at least 1,000 Rupees, the Great Fairy of Forest will gift the Links a [[Silver Key]] and dub them "little hero eggs".<ref name="Guardian"/> If the Links have collected at least 3,000 Rupees and have defeated Vaati after collecting all of the Silver Keys, she will dub them "brave heroes" and bestow upon them [[Golden Key]]s.<ref>{{Cite|I am the Great Fairy of Forest, guardian of the Sea of Trees. Collecting this many Rupees is an incredible feat! I dub you...brave heroes and grant you each a {{Color|FS Orange|Golden Key}}.|Great Fairy of Forest|FS}}</ref> Defeating Vaati and returning to the Great Fairy of Forest's Fountain with at least 5,000 Rupees will prompt her to declare the Links the "greatest of heroes" and grant them [[Hero's Key]]s.<ref>{{Cite|I am the Great Fairy of Forest, guardian of the Sea of Trees. There is nothing left to be said. I dub you...the greatest of heroes and grant you each a {{Color|FS Orange|Hero's Key}}.|Great Fairy of Forest|FS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I am the Great Fairy of Forest, guardian of the Sea of Trees. You have done well, young adventurers. This time, you have managed to collect {{Icon|FS Rupee}} [#] Rupees. There is nothing left to be said. I dub you... The greatest of heroes, and grant you this {{Color|FS Red|Hero's Key}}.|Great Fairy of Forest|FS}}</ref>

The Great Fairy of Forest then cautions the heroes that they must have collected a complete set of Keys in order to open the way to [[Vaati's Palace]].<ref>{{Cite|If all of you collect the three {{Color|FS Orange|Silver Keys}} of the Great Fairies of Forest, Ice, and Flame, the path to Vaati's Palace shall open. You can then make your way there to rescue Princess Zelda. Onward, little hero eggs!|Great Fairy of Forest|FS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If all of you collect the three {{Color|FS Orange|Golden Keys}} of the Great Fairies of Forest, Ice, and Flame... ...a path to Vaati's Palace shall open! Onward, heroes!|Great Fairy of Forest|FS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If all of you gather the {{Color|FS Red|Hero Keys}} of the Great Fairies of Forest, Ice, and Flame, a path to Vaati's Palace shall open. Onward, great heroes!|Great Fairy of Forest|FS}}</ref>

If the Links have defeated Vaati after collecting all of the Hero's Keys in {{FSAE}}, the Great Fairy of Forest's appearance and personality will change to resemble the {{Term|MM|Great Fairy of Power|link}} from {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! You have done well to get this far. I am the Great Fairy of Forest! Throughout the ages I have listened to the whispering of these woods as the guardian of the Sea of Trees.|Great Fairy of Forest|FSAE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I see you already have the three {{Color|FS Orange|Hero Keys}}! I wonder if you know about the path that opened to {{Color|FS Blue|Vaati's Palace}}. It has {{Color|FS Orange|12 floors}}... Do you need an explanation? ...No? Then off you go, brave heroes!|Great Fairy of Forest|FSAE}}</ref>
