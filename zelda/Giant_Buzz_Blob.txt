The {{Term/Store|Giant Buzz Blob||Series, LADX, LANS}} is a [[Sub-Boss]] of the [[Color Dungeon]] in {{LA|DX}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 181}}</ref>

This Boss is comparable to a regular [[Buzz Blob]] but is much larger and more formidable, and blue instead of green. Upon his defeat, the door to the [[Nightmare Key]] will open.

==Characteristics==
[[File:LADX Giant Buzz Blob Sprite 2.png|left|The Giant Buzz Blob once it's been sprinkled with Magic Powder]]
To defeat the Giant Buzz Blob, [[Link]] must sprinkle [[Magic Powder]] upon it. Then, he must strike it with the [[Sword]], although in Link's Awakening DX only, the battle can be won without the need of the Sword by sprinkling it with Magic Powder a total of 6 times.  Throughout the battle, Giant Buzz Blob will shoot lightning bolts which the young hero must avoid. When first encountering it, Giant Buzz Blob will gloat that it's not a weakling and that Link's sword is no match for it.  In the event Link runs out of Magic Powder before defeating the Giant Buzz Blob, a [[Fairy]] will appear to provide him with more.

In {{LA|NS}}, the Giant Buzz Blob is redesigned to be colored green in its Buzz Blob state, although it is still colored blue in its weakened state.

==Trivia==
*The Magic Powder turns the Giant Buzz Blob into a form that resembles the [[Boss Bot]] from {{TAoL}}.
*The Giant Buzz Blob shares some of its characteristics with the [[Big Blue Chuchu]] from {{TMC}}.
