==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos Horn}} are dropped by all varieties of {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} as well as [[Stalizalfos]] when they're defeated. They're too hard to make [[Food]] with, but they can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] with critters to make {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}} with an additional minute and ten seconds to their duration.<ref name="Inventory"/> The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] also accepts Lizalfos Horns as an ingredient for Gray [[Dye]]. Finally, [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] will take Lizalfos Horns in exchange for [[Mon]].

Three Lizalfos Horns are used to upgrade each piece of the [[Zora Set]] to its first tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}

* Total for '''Zora Set''': 9 (3, 0, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
