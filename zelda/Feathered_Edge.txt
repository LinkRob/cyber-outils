{{Plural|BotW|Feathered Edge}} are lightweight, double-edged swords forged by {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} craftsmen, designed so that {{Term|BotW|Rito}} warriors can soar into battle unaffected by their weight.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Rito Gear|link}} equipment archetype,  a grouping of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Rito}} origins.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Feathered Edge}} are primarily found on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Feathered Edge}} can be found inside {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Rota Ooh Shrine|link}} and at {{Term|BotW|Dragon Bone Mire|link}}. In addition, two {{Plural|BotW|Blue Lizalfos|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier}} equip a {{Term|BotW|Feathered Edge}}. One can be found just south of {{Term|BotW|Kolami Bridge|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}}, while the other is hiding in a path north of {{Term|BotW|Kolami Bridge}}. The {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} and the swords reappear every {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found containing a {{Term|BotW|Feathered Edge}} at the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hebra Plunge|link}}.<!--How are the Chests obtained?-->

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Feathered Edge}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they are capable of conducting {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, but they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They can be picked up and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.
