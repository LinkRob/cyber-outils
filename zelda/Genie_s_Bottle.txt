==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|LADX|Genie|link}} lives inside the {{Term|LADX|Genie's Bottle}}. {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} has to lift it up with the {{Term|LADX|Power Bracelet}} and throw it to damage it. Once broken, the {{Term|LADX|Genie}} will be damageable.
