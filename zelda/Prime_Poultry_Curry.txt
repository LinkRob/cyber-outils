{{Term/Store|Prime Poultry Curry|Prime Poultry Curries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Prime Poultry Curry is made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Raw Bird Thigh]], [[Goron Spice]], and [[Hylian Rice]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Eating them will heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The flavor of these curries comes from adding the spices after they've been taken off the heat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can use additional ingredients when making these curries, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Prime Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. When eaten, they heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Prime Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. Link can eat these curries to have his {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} partially refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Prime Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Consuming them will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Prime Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Prime Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. Eating them will grant [[Link]] one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Prime Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Prime Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Prime Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. Link will gain a boost to his defense by eating these curries.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Prime Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Eating these meals will increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Prime Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Prime Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The secret to this curry's flavor is taking it off the heat while you add the spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. These curries will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Chilly"/>
