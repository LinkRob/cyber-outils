{{Term/Store|Winter||Series, MM, OoS}} is a recurring [[season]] and mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= CE |publisher= Nintendo |page= 123 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|game=OoS|publisher=Nintendo|page=4, 41}}</ref>

==Overview==
Winter is a season both in real life and in {{TLoZ|Series}}. Winter is the season during both the end and start of a calendar year, although not in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series. Winter is linked to [[snow]]fall, which often occurs during the season, so snowy areas in ''The Legend of Zelda'' games are implicitly winter.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the [[Snowhead Region]] undergoes constant winter.

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, during the winter, the [[Overworld]] color palette turns a blueish hue. [[Lake]]s and ponds freeze, with portions turning into [[ice]] or cracked ice. Large snowdrifts and ramps will also appear, alongside smaller piles of [[snow]]. Certain [[Tree]]s will lose their leaves and turn into bare trunks, allowing passage through otherwise-blocked areas.

It is the first season that can be controlled with the [[Rod of Seasons]], as granted by the [[Spirit of Winter]].

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, certain [[fish]] show up at [[Hena's Fishing Hole]] during the winter, like with the other seasons.
