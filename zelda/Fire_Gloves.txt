The {{Term/Store|Fire Gloves||Series, TFH}} are an incendiary {{Term|TFH|Item}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got Fire Gloves! Press {{Icon|TFH Y}} to throw a fireball. Hold it to make a fire shield. So hot!|N/A|TFH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
When used, the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}} create a fireball that can either be thrown forward, or held in front of the [[Links]] as a fire shield, which gradually drains his {{Term|TFH|Energy Gauge|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}}' fireballs bounce along the ground and ricochet off of walls. They are used to light {{Plural|TFH|Torchlight|link}}, burn bushes, and damage enemies. Objects and enemies of the ice element, such as ice crystals and {{Plural|TFH|Freezard|link}}, can only be melted using the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}}, while others, like {{Plural|TFH|Snowball|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}, are highly susceptible to the {{Term|TFH|Item}}, but can still be destroyed by other means. {{Term|TFH|Freezlord|link}}, the boss of {{Term|TFH|Snowball Ravine|link}}, can only be damaged with fire, making the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}} invaluable during the fight against it.

The {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}} first appear in the {{Term|TFH|Frozen Plateau|link}}, in which all three Links acquire them. The {{Term|TFH|Item}} reappears in {{Term|TFH|Snowball Ravine}} (two pairs), {{Term|TFH|Ice Temple|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Palace Noir|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Lone Labyrinth|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}, and {{Term|TFH|Sky Temple|link}} (one pair per Stage). The {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}} also appear in the {{Term|TFH|Forest Zone|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Frozen Zone|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Shadow Zone|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Baneful Zone|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}, as well as the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}}'s Ice Cavern, Fortress and The Ruins arenas.

[[File:TFH Nice Fire Gloves Icon.png|left]]
The effects of the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}} are improved while wearing the {{Term|TFH|Fire Blazer|link}}. This {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} allows the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to throw three fireballs at once instead of one, the first being larger than the following two.

==Trivia==
*An [[Cameos in The Legend of Zelda|Easter Egg]] to the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], the Fire Gloves produce the exact same sound effect that [[smw:Fire Mario|Fire Mario]] does while casting fireballs with the [[smw:Fire Flower|Fire Flower]] in ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]''.
