The {{Term/Store|Evil Eagle||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is the eponymous [[Boss]] of the [[Eagle's Tower]] and the minion of the [[Grim Creeper]], the mini-boss of the same dungeon, in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 176 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref>

==Background==
At the top of the tower, [[Link]] is met with Grim Creeper who had earlier vowed revenge on Link for killing his beloved [[Battle Bat]]s.<ref>{{Cite|You dirty rat! You k-k-k...beat my brothers! You'll pay!! I'll never forget you!|Grim Creeper|LADX}}</ref> The Grim Creeper, who threatens Link with his life, sends away his remaining bats and plays his flute to summon Evil Eagle.<ref>{{Cite|BAH! I'm not going to hold back! I'm going to make you wish you were never born!!|Grim Creeper|LADX}}</ref> Shortly after the Grim Creeper's jeering, the Eagle flies in and the boss battle begins.

==Characteristics==
[[File:LADX Evil Eagle.png|200px|thumb|The Grim Creeper, two [[Keese#Size|Big Keese]], and the Evil Eagle]] 
Link can block most of the Evil Eagle's attacks using his [[Mirror Shield]]. Evil Eagle will fly back and forth across the screen at varying altitudes until Link hits him in the beak three times with any of the following weapons: the [[Sword]], the [[Hookshot]], the [[Boomerang]], the [[Bow]], or the [[Magic Rod]]. After three hits, Evil Eagle will hover to the side of the tower and either blow wind at Link and shoot little feathers (to combat this, Link should keep his shield up and walk towards the wind), or he will dive at him. If Link falls off the tower, he has to restart the entire battle. The Eagle is defeated with six hits from the Bow or L-2 Sword, and eight from the Hookshot or Boomerang.

It is possible to charge the L-2 Sword with a [[Piece of Power]] before the boss fight, which will allow Link to defeat Evil Eagle in three hits. It is also possible to use the [[Magic Rod]] to fight Evil Eagle, by skipping the boss battle and heading straight to {{Term|LANS|Turtle Rock|link}}, as all that is needed to enter the dungeon is the Mirror Shield and the [[Frog's Song of Soul]]. Evil Eagle will go down in three hits to the Magic Rod, and will not attack directly if it is used. 

Once defeated, Grim Creeper will warn Link that he is also in the [[Wind Fish]]'s dream and will disappear if the Wind Fish is ever awakened.<ref>{{Cite|My energy... gone...I...lost! But you will be lost too, if the Wind Fish wakes! Same as me...you ...are...in... his...dream...|Grim Creeper|LADX}}</ref> Following this, both the Evil Eagle and Grim Creeper will perish and Link will receive a [[Heart Container]] and the [[Organ of Evening Calm]] as a reward.

==Trivia==
*As the Grim Creeper speaks for it, the Evil Eagle is the only dungeon boss in {{LA|-}} that does not speak.
*Beak and neck of the Evil Eagle resemble more a vulture than an eagle. However, for a vulture, the wings would be too small.
