The {{Term/Store|Fairy Ocarina||Series}} is a recurring [[item]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 122 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
''"A treasured keepsake from Saria. Its gentle tones create soothing melodies.''- ''Ocarina of Time 3D'' description

The Fairy Ocarina is given to [[Link]] by [[Saria]] when he first leaves [[Kokiri Forest]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you're leaving... I always knew that one day, this day would come. I've always known we're so different, and I'm not sad, because you have a lot ahead of you. I want you to take this. Don't forget me.|Saria|OoT}}</ref> Link uses this item, as well as the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]] later on, to solve various puzzles and get past obstacles in the game with the usage of [[music]]. The Fairy Ocarina is only used for the duration of the initial child stage of the game. It is later replaced by the Ocarina of Time when [[Princess Zelda]] is seen fleeing [[Hyrule Castle]] with [[Impa]] after Link collects all three [[Spiritual Stones]]. Its musical range is the same as the Ocarina of Time's range. Also  Upon receiving the new ocarina, if Saria is spoken to immediately, through [[Saria's Song]], she will note that the Ocarina of Time has a slightly different sound compared to the Fairy Ocarina.<ref>{{Cite|What? Your ocarina sounds...different somehow... Have you been practicing a lot, Link?|Saria|OoT}}</ref>

==In other media==
==={{OoT|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In the {{OoT|Himekawa}}, after the attack on Hyrule Castle, Link loses the Fairy Ocarina and [[Ganondorf]] takes it, mistaking it for the Ocarina of Time. After realizing his mistake later, he crushes it in his hand.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW}} and {{HW|L}}, the Fairy Ocarina appears as the Lv.1 tier of the [[Ocarina]], [[Skull Kid]]'s signature weapon.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In pre-release screenshots of {{OoT|-}}, Link can be seen with the Fairy Ocarina equipped as an adult. The Fairy Ocarina can also be seen in early screenshots of {{MM}} and still exists in the game's code. It can be placed into Link's inventory and when used, it acts like when using the [[Pictograph Box]].
