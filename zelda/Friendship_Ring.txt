The {{Term/Store|Friendship Ring||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
The Friendship Ring is received from [[Vasu]] the first time [[Link]] talks to him.<ref>{{Cite|Take this {{Color|OoS Red|ring}} as a sign of our friendship.|Vasu|OoS}}</ref> The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. It has no effect when worn. The Friendship Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
