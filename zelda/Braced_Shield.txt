==Location and Uses==
{{Lacking Sources|SSHD}}
{{Plural|SSHD|Braced Shield}} are the third and final upgrade that can be done on a {{Plural|SSHD|Wooden Shield|link}}. They are the upgraded version of {{Plural|SSHD|Banded Shield|link}}. {{Plural|SSHD|Braced Shield}} are twice as strong as {{Plural|SSHD|Wooden Shield}} and 1.5 times as strong as {{Plural|SSHD|Banded Shield}}. {{Plural|SSHD|Braced Shield}}, like the lower two upgrades, are weak to {{Term|SSHD|Fire|link}} and will burn if exposed to {{Term|SSHD|Fire}} attacks for too long, or if {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} falls into {{Term|SSHD|Lava|link}} while using it.
