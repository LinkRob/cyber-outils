{{Term/Store|EX Battle Research: Items||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 83 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Battle Research: Items|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Battle Research: Items}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must fight {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} according to {{Term|HWAoC|Purah|link}}'s instructions. The first {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy}} is an extremely large {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Chuchu|link}}, and first, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must attack it with fire three times. There is also a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} that spawns in the corner, and when defeated, reappears after a certain amount of time. Afterwards, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat the {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Chuchu}} with a {{Term|HWAoC|Remote Bomb|link}}. When it is defeated, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} spawn, which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must counter with {{Plural|HWAoC|Rune|link}} three times. Also, while the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}} from before does not disappear, it will also not reappear when defeated. Afterwards, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Lynel|link}} spawns, which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat with a {{Term|HWAoC|Remote Bomb}} as well. Also, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} from before will no longer respawn when defeated. If any {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated at the wrong time or in the wrong way, they will simply reappear, giving the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} another chance to reach his or her objective. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} except {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Maintenance Oil|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Machine Lubricant|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Curing Agent|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
