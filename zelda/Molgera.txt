{{Term/Store|Molgera||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is the [[Boss]] of the [[Wind Temple]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 194}}</ref> It is a giant sand worm with a wide mouth.

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TWW|Molgera, Protector of the Seal}}
When Molgera emerges from the sand, [[Link]] must be careful not to get too close to its gaping mouth, as he is slowly drawn toward it; otherwise, the monster might devour him whole and cause two [[Heart Container|Hearts]] worth of damage before regurgitating him. Link must [[Targeting|target]] Molgera's tongue with the [[Hookshot]] so he can attack it with any weapon, including the [[Sword]].

After enough damage is dealt, Molgera alters its attack strategy by releasing three of [[Molgera Larva|its larvae]] to distract and attack Link, which will inflict one Heart if struck. He can use the Hookshot to latch onto and dispatch them, rewarding him with [[Heart|Recovery Heart]]s. In addition, Link must look out for Molgera whenever the monster emerges from the sand and flies through the air, attempting to crush him when it dives back in.

Upon its vanquishment, Molgera painfully emerges and explodes into a shower of sand. This yields a [[Heart Container]] for Link as a reward and restores the [[Wind Temple]]'s seal, allowing [[Makar]] to take up his role as the [[Sage of Wind|Wind Sage]] and restoring the second half of the [[Master Sword]]'s [[Power to Repel Evil|lost power]].

Like most other bosses in the game, Molgera is found again in [[Ganon's Tower (The Wind Waker)|Ganon's Tower]] later in the game. It must be defeated for a second time in the same fashion to break the seal to the door leading to [[Ganondorf]].

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese release, Molgera is called '''Morudo Geira'''.
*In {{A&A}}, Molgera got its name swapped with [[Jalhalla]].
*Molgera's battle and defeat is similar to that of [[Volvagia]] and [[Twinmold]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', respectively.
*The battle theme song of Molgera is a popular favorite among fans who played ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'' and its [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD|HD remaster]], due to its more intense beat compared to most other [[Bosses in The Wind Waker|bosses]] and is similar to the [[Sub-Boss]] theme (Wind Temple battle only).
**It is also used as one of the possible themes of the [[Tetra's Ship|Pirate Ship]] stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

==Nomenclature==
Molgera's name is a play on ''Mogura'', the Japanese word for mole.
