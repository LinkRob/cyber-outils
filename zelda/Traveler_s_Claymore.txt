{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Claymore}} are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} commonly used by aspiring adventurers.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are hefty enough to knock the {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}} out of {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}' hands.<ref name="Inventory"/> These {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Claymore|display=Claymores}} can be found abandoned in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. They are also carried by the {{Plural|BotW|Swordswoman Statue|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.

When scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Claymore}}:
* {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|SSB|Young Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|SSB|Ganondorf|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|LA|Link|link}} ({{LA}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|OoT|Link|link}} ({{OoT}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Young Link}} ({{MM}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Claymore}}, since they are made of metal, will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. However, they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, and they will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Claymore}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear|link}} archetype of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, which is used for defense by the travelers of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>
