{{Term/Store|Chudley||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 276}}</ref>{{Note|This character was also referred to as '''Malver''' in {{Guide|TP|Prima}},<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 15}}</ref> However, because Chudley's name was officially stated in {{A&A}}, any other term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
Chudley is a man who runs [[Chudley's Fine Goods and Fancy Trinkets Emporium]], an extravagant shop in [[Castle Town]] which charges a large amounts of [[Rupee]]s for common items. He is very snobbish and will not let anyone into his store unless they shine their shoes.<ref>{{Cite|Well, having a customer in the store with shoes as filthy as yours can make our other clients uncomfortable. We hope that you will come back once you have found some more-polished footwear.|Chudley|TP}}</ref>

If enough Rupees are donated at [[Malo Mart]] in [[Kakariko Village]], [[Malo]] will buy the store and convert it into the [[Malo Mart Castle Branch]], drastically reducing the prices of his wares.<ref>{{Cite|The hero of discounts, Malo Mart, will be a legend in Castle Town!|Malver|TP}}</ref> Chudley remains in charge of the shop after this, but he drastically changes his demeanor. He now wears extravagant colorful clothing, constantly dances, acts cheerful, and is completely devoted to satisfying his customers.

==Trivia==
*Chudley is one of the few characters in the series who ever speaks in actual words. After buying the [[Magic Armor]], he says "Thank you."
*According to {{Guide|TP|Prima}}, Chudley is about 30 years old.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 13}}</ref>
