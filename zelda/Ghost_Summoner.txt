'''Ghost Summoners''' are enemies in {{HW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Improve}}
A Ghost Summoner in Hyrule Warriors is a [[Hylian Summoner|summoner]] for the [[Ghost Forces]]. They, like the rest of the [[Ghost Forces]], look like a translucent, light blue variant of the [[Hyrule Forces]] variant and drop only [[Rupee|Rupees]]. They only appear in [[The Sacred Sword]], where Wizzro brainwashes them and the rest of the Ghost Forces into attacking the Hyrule Forces. Throughout the mission, they appear in waves while they keep the [[Temple of the Sacred Sword|Temple Of The Sacred Sword]] doors closed. When the player defeats [[Lana|Lana?]], they, along with the remaining Ghost Forces, recognize Link as the hero they were waiting for and open the doors, before disappearing.
