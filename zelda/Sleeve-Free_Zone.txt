==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Sleeve-Free Zone}}" is an event wherein a greater number of sleeveless {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits with sleeveless shirts are flashing their biceps on the Spirit Board!|author= Nintendo |published= May 31, 2022 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/c4a51dbb/index.html |title= Event: Sleeve-Free Zone |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
