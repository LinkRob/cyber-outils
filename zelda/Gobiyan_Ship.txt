{{Term/Store|Gobiyan Ship||Series, TWoG}} is a location in {{TWoG}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gobiyan Ship}}|Map|TWoG}}</ref> It is unlocked after completing the [[Fairy Pool]].

==Features and Overview==
The Gobiyan Ship is an old shipwreck off the northern coast of [[Gamelon]], close to the shores near [[Aru Ainu]]. It is a haunted by the undead, including [[Ghini]]s and [[Stalfos]], and inhabited by [[Arkadai]]s and [[Zola]]s that swim around the Ship's waters.

The first section of the area is a sand bank that reaches out to the Ship's bow. Arkadais patrol these waters in large numbers, and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] must ride on their backs to reach the Ship. A large hole in the Ship's hull opens to the rest of the Ship, where the undead infest the corridors of the vessel. A small door in the Ship's mast leads to the upper levels of the mast, supported by many ropes tying the structure together. Zelda climbs these ropes further up the mast, all the while being pursued by Ghinis. At the very top of the mast is an [[Arpagos Feather]] in a nest as well as a [[Key]] that opens a door to a cabin back on the Ship. Inside this cabin is the [[Reflecting Shield]], as well as a [[Triforce Map]] that takes Zelda back to the map screen. After the stage is completed, the [[Shrine of Gamelon]] is unlocked.
