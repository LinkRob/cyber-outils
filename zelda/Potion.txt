{{Term/Store|Potion|Potions|Series, ALttP|plural}} are medicinal drinks with magical properties in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 393 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref> They can heal [[Link]], refill his [[Magic Meter]], or both, as well as other unique effects. Potions are often contained in [[Bottle]]s, in which case Link cannot buy or obtain a Potion unless he has an empty Bottle. In games without Bottles as items, Potions are standalone items.

{{OTOC}}
{{clear|left}}

==Common Potions==
These Potions, the '''[[Red Potion|Red]]''', '''[[Green Potion|Green]]''', and '''[[Blue Potion]]s''' are the most common in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series, as they perform the most basic and relevant behaviors to Link: that of health replenishment, [[Magic Power|magical]] restoration, and for the most part, a combination of the two, respectively. They first appear in {{ALttP}}, where they were called '''Medicines''', and have been seen in the series numerous times since.

===Red Potion===
{{Main|Red Potion}}
[[File:TWWHD Red Potion Artwork.png|left|50px]]
The '''Red Potion''' is the most prevalent concoction found in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series, seen often between the release of {{ALttP|-}}, in which it was called '''Medicine of Life''', to that of {{ALBW}}. In all of its incarnations, it operates as a health Potion. It can be found in {{OoT}}, {{MM}}, {{TWW}}, {{PH}}, and most other games. The replenishment rate of this Potion differs and, notably, has recently declined in functionality in the past few games. From {{ALttP|-}} to {{TMC}}, the Red Potion restores all of Link's [[heart]]s. However, with the release of {{TP}} and eventually {{ALBW|-}}, this Potion has fallen to restoring only eight hearts,<ref>{{Cite|You got some red potion! Drink it with Y or X to replenish 8 hearts.|N/A|TP}}</ref> and had came to an all-time-low of a six heart restoration in {{PH|-}} and {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Red Potion: 100 Rupees. Restores up to six hearts. What a deal!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> The DS games eliminated the need for Link to carry Red Potions in Bottles as their containers are provided. As seen in {{TWW|-}} and {{TP|-}}, it appears that [[Chu Jelly|Red Chu Jelly]] shares similar properties to the Red Potion.

===Green Potion===
{{Main|Green Potion}}
[[File:TWWHD Green Potion Artwork.png|left|50px]]
The '''Green Potion''', first introduced in {{ALttP|-}} as '''Medicine of Magic''', is the chemistry concoction that usually responds exclusively to the Magic Meter, restoring the entire magic bar, regardless of the meter's size. Often, this Potion can be bought in Potion shops alongside the Red Potion at a cheaper cost. Out of the three common Potions, Green Potion costs the least in terms of [[Rupee]]s. In games that don not feature the magic bar, however, the Green Potion does not exist. In 3D console games, it appears in {{OoT|-}}, {{MM}}, and {{TWW|-}}. In {{TWW|-}}, it can be obtained once 15 spoils of Green Chu Jelly are given to [[Doc Bandam]] at the [[Chu Jelly Juice Shop]], and can be bought for 10 Rupees after that.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry, but could you bring these [Green Chu Jelly globs] to me in groups of...oh, say, about fifteen? You see, I can't make any potion without a specific minimum amount of Chu Jelly...and I can't accept any until you've got enough for me. Sorry. Really, I am.|Doc Bandam|TWW}}</ref> A similarly green-colored Potion in {{SS}}, called the Stamina Potion, completely replenishes Link's [[Stamina]] and slows its consumption rate. It can also be upgraded to completely prevent Stamina loss for a limited time.

===Blue Potion===
{{Main|Blue Potion}}
[[File:TWWHD Blue Potion Artwork.png|left|50px]]
The '''Blue Potion''' is the "jack-of-all-trades" of its kind; in its most common form, it fully replenishes the Magic Meter and the majority, if not all, of Link's hearts. Blue Potions are often the most expensive purchase at a Potion shop, and rightly so, considering its dual replenishment qualities. It debuted in {{ALttP|-}} as '''Medicine of Life and Magic''' or '''Cure-All Medicine''', where it is available at the [[Witch's Hut|Magic Shop]]. Link can obtain it at the [[Granny|Potion Shop]] as an adult in {{OoT|-}}. {{MM|-}} requires Link to give [[Kotake]] a [[Mushroom]] before she will sell Blue Potions at the [[Magic Hags' Potion Shop]]. Similarly in {{TWW|-}}, Link must give 15 spoils of Blue Chu Jelly to Doc Bandam before he will begin selling Blue Potion at the Chu Jelly Juice Shop. Every time Link collects 4 [[Boko Baba Seed]]s, he can hand them over to [[Hollo]] of the [[Korok]]s and he will make a Blue Potion.

While the Blue Potion is usually associated with restoring both health and magic, it is seen in games with the absence of a Magic Meter. This Potion appears in {{TMC|-}}, where it is the first Potion available from [[Syrup]]'s hut. It has only a healing value of ten hearts. It appears again in {{TP|-}}, where it heals all of Link's hearts.<ref>{{Cite|You got some blue potion! Set it to and use it with Y or X to replenish all of your hearts.|N/A|TP}}</ref> This Potion requires Link to have his own Bottle to be carried.

There are many other Potions which appear in the series in many forms, but most work in the same fashion as Red Potion, providing Link health. These Potions are usually red, but they do come in a variety of colors, especially in recent ''Zelda'' titles. The amount of life these Potions restore to Link also varies, as well as their names. While most Potions are used manually, some act as some [[Fairy|Fairies]] do, refilling Link's health when he is devoid of hearts. Most of these Potions do not require a Bottle to be obtained.

===Life Potion===
{{Main|Life Potion}}
[[File:TLoZ Life Potion Sprite.png|16px|left]]
[[File:TLoZ 2nd Potion Sprite.png|16px|left]]
Life Potion appears in {{TLoZ}} and is a blue drink that [[Link]] can purchase for 40 [[Rupee]]s from any [[Old Woman]] in a {{Term|TLoZ|Cave|link}} after he has shown one of them the {{Term|TLoZ|Letter|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=Letter to little old lady / When you choose this letter at the sub menu and press button B, Link can show the letter to the little old lady who lives inside one of the many caves.|game= TLoZ|page=23}}</ref> If Link uses a Life Potion, he will drink it, restoring all of his [[Heart]]s.<ref name="name_use">{{Cite Manual|quote=Water of Life / Drink this and all of Link's LIFE hearts will turn red. Red water turns blue when Link sips it. Drink the blue water and that's the last of the water.|game= TLoZ|page=25}}</ref> Life Potions only have one use while blue, but can be used twice if Link purchases it as the red variety known as [[2nd Potion]].

===Water of Life===
{{Main|Water of Life}}
[[File:TAoL Water of Life Sprite.png|16px|left]]
The Water of Life is a potion in {{TAoL}} that is hidden in a {{Term|TAoL|Cave|link}} just west of the [[Midoro Palace]] and must be given to an Old Woman in the [[Harbor Town of Mido]] who asks Link for his help in saving her sick daughter.<ref>{{Cite|PLEASE. MY DAUGHTER IS SICK. HELP HER!|Old Woman|TAoL}}</ref> To enter the {{Term|TAoL|Cave}}, Link must have the [[Hammer]] to break the [[Boulder]] sealing it. The Water of Life itself is guarded by three [[Daira]]s. After returning the Medicine to the elderly woman, she rewards Link by allowing him inside her house,<ref>{{Cite|THE WATER OF LIFE! QUICK COME WITH ME.|Old Woman|TAoL}}</ref> where he meets an [[Old Man]] who teaches him the [[Magic of The Adventure of Link#Fairy|Fairy]] magic.<ref>{{Cite|THIS MAGIC WORD WILL GIVE YOU POWER.|Old Man|TAoL}}</ref>

===Secret Medicine===
{{Main|Secret Medicine}}
[[File:LADX Secret Medicine Sprite.png|left]]
In {{LA}}, Link can purchase Secret Medicine from [[Crazy Tracy]]. This is the first Potion to work automatically, as it will activate when Link loses all of his hearts, completely refilling them, and then it will disappear from his inventory.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= |game= LA|page= 19}}</ref>
{{Clear|left}}

===Magic Potion===
{{Manual|OoS|Magic Potion|If you have a Magic Potion when your life energy reaches zero, your energy will be replenished. Magic Potions can be used only once, and you can carry only one of them at a time.}}
{{Manual|OoA|Magic Potion|If you have a Magic Potion when your life energy reaches zero, your energy will be replenished. Magic Potions can be used only once, and you can carry only one of them at a time.}}
[[File:OoS Magic Potion Sprite.png|left]]
The '''Magic Potion''' is identical to effect of the Secret Medicine. This red-colored Potion can be found in {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}. In these games, it can be obtained through purchase from [[Syrup]]'s shop, by taking it if [[Maple]] drops one, or as contents of a [[Gasha Seed|Gasha Nut]]. In either game, only one of these Potions can be held at once. The Magic Potion is consumed automatically when Link would otherwise die, filling all of his lost hearts instantly.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote=Magic Potion / If you have a Magic Potion, your life energy will be completely replenished the instant it reaches zero. A Magic Potion is very useful, but it can be used only once, and you can carry only one at a time. [...] It is rumored that a witch makes and sells Magic Potions in her shop in some far-off corner of the world.|game= OoA|page=43}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's Syrup's magic ointment. Works great when your hearts run out.|Syrup|OoS}}</ref> In {{OoA}}, a Magic Potion is also used to cure [[King Zora]] of his deathly illness.<ref>{{Cite|We believe we need a special medicine called a Magic Potion to cure King Zora's illness.|Zora|OoA}}</ref>
{{clear|left}}

===Tings===
'''Tings''' are Potions in {{TWW|-}} that are accessible only when using the [[Tingle Tuner]]. Link can purchase three types of Tings from [[Tingle]], which correspond to the usual effects of the Red, Green, and Blue Potions for 20, 30, or 60 Rupees, respectively. Once bought, Tingle will deliver the Ting to Link by balloon, as seen on the [[Game Boy Advance]] screen, which will instantly replenish all his hearts, his magic, or both depending on the color bought. Further participation in Tingle's sidequests will cause Tingle to cut his prices.

===Chu Jelly===
{{Main|Chu Jelly}}
[[File:TP Purple Chu Jelly Render.png|left|40px]]
While '''Chu Jelly''' is used to make Potions in {{TWW|-}}, it can be used directly after defeating [[Chuchu]]s in {{TP|-}}. Link merely has to scoop the Jelly up with a Bottle before using it. Depending on the color, Chu Jelly will have the same effects as Potions of the same in color in the game. Purple Chu Jelly has random effects, which will either replenish or decrease Link's health. Yellow Chu Jelly cannot be drunk, and is instead used as a substitute to [[Lantern Oil]]. Rare Chu Jelly shares the same effects as [[Great Fairy's Tears]], refilling all of Link's health and boosting his sword's strength until he receives damage.
{{clear}}

===Purple Potion===
{{Main|Purple Potion}}
[[File:ALBW Purple Potion Icon.png|left|50px|Purple Potion sprite from {{ALBW|-}}]]
'''Purple Potions''' are an uncommon type of Potion that appear in {{PH|-}}, {{ST|-}} and {{ALBW|-}}. Their usage varies between games. In {{PH|-}} and {{ST|-}}, they replenish eight Hearts, and also revive Link when he collapses in battle, fulfilling a similar role to the Secret Medicine and Magic Potion, as well as [[Fairy|Fairies]].<ref>{{Cite|Purple Potion: 150 Rupees. Restores eight hearts when you fall!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> In {{ALBW|-}}, it instead unleashes a powerful attack that damages all enemies in the immediate area.<ref>{{Cite|You got some purple potion! Use it to unleash an attack that will deal big damage to surrounding foes!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> While all Potions in {{PH|-}} and {{ST|-}} come in their own flasks, the Purple Potion in {{ALBW|-}} must be kept in a separate Bottle.
{{clear}}

===Yellow Potion===
{{Main|Yellow Potion}}
[[File:ALBW Yellow Potion Icon.png|left|50px|Yellow Potion sprite from {{ALBW|-}}]]
'''Yellow Potions''' are another uncommon type of Potion that appear in {{PH|-}}, {{ST|-}} and {{ALBW|-}}. They are among the most powerful Potions in the series, as they restore all Hearts in {{PH|-}} and {{ST|-}},<ref>{{Cite|That's a yellow potion! It restores all your hearts! It's only 200 Rupees!|Shopkeeper|ST}}</ref> while in {{ALBW|-}} they turn Link invincible to all enemy attacks for a short period of time. Like the Purple Potion in the former two games, it comes in its own flask, but must be kept in a Bottle in {{ALBW|-}}.

===Potions in {{SS|-}}===
{{Main|Potion Shop}}
{{SS|-}}'s Potion collection is unique in having mostly new Potions added to the collection. These can be bought from [[Luv]] at her [[Potion Shop]] at the [[Bazaar]], and can be upgraded by giving [[Bertie]] the proper [[Bug|Insect]]s to infuse into the Potion at his [[Infusion Shop]], powering up their effects.

If Link has a [[Potion Medal]] equipped in his [[Adventure Pouch]], the effects of a Potion that runs on a timer will last longer.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Potion Medal! Carry this with you to prolong the effects of a potion. If you've got this, remember to pack lots of potions!|N/A|SS}}</ref> The effects of one of each type of these Potions can be taken for concurrent bonuses: for example, taking the Stamina and Guardian Potions before starting [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]]'s [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|Lightning Round]] can make it much easier to complete.

===Energy Potion===
[[File:ALBW Energy Potion Model.png|left|40px]]
{{Main|Energy Potion}}
In {{ALBW|-}} and {{TFH|-}}, the Energy Potion is used to quickly refill the [[Energy Gauge]]. Unlike other Potions in the game, it is not a Potion that is purchased and stored in a Bottle, but can instead be found in areas that require large usage of the Energy Gauge, such as in dungeons or [[Treasure Hunter]] rooms. It immediately refills the Energy Gauge when picked up, making it more similar to [[Magic Jar]]s than typical Potions. In dungeons, these Potions are usually found inside purple-colored [[pot]]s or skulls.

==Potion Shops==
There have been several Potion brewers throughout ''The Legend of Zelda'' series, and they use different ingredients to create their Potions. [[Syrup]], a witch who has appeared in several games, apparently uses [[Mushroom]]s to create her Potions, and so do [[Twinrova|Kotake and Koume]] in {{MM|-}} and possibly [[Granny]] from {{OoT|-}}. In {{TWW|-}}, Doc Bandam from [[Windfall Island]] uses Chu Jelly obtained from ChuChus to create different Potions, while Hollo the Korok uses the magic inside Boko Baba Seeds to make Blue Potions.

In the games in which these characters don not appear, the origin of Potions is unknown, although in {{TP|-}}, it is possible that at least some of them are made with Chu Jelly as in {{TWW|-}}. Evidence for this is that the remains of a defeated ChuChu leaves a blob that when scooped into a Bottle will act as a Potion. However, [[Trill]] states that Red Potion is made from a mix of mushrooms and herbs.

===''The Legend of Zelda'' (Valiant Comics)===
The Water of Life appears in the {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}. In "''[[Trust Me]]''", after the townsfolk of the [[Water Town of Saria]] build a dam following [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]'s advice since their land was infertile, the dam unfortunately does not work and the princess comes to the conclusion that the land is cursed. ==={{TFoE|-}}===
[[File:TFoE Water of Life Sprite.gif|32px|left]]
In {{TFoE}}, Water of Life is used to recover Link's health and is the only form of recovery during a stage. Link can carry Water of Life inside of [[Canteen]]s he receives from certain characters. The Canteen can be refilled by [[Hamsha]] and the [[Fairy|Fairies]] of [[Spearfish Falls]], as well as by bottles of Water of Life found in [[Fortress Centrum]], {{Term|TFoE|Glutko|link}}, {{Term|TFoE|Lupay (Stage)|link}} and [[Serigon Caves]]. If Link does not have a Canteen, then the Water of Life they give will simply restore his health.

==={{TWoG|-}}===
Water of Life is used in {{TWoG}} to recover Zelda's health. It is the only way to recover health during a stage. Zelda can carry Water of Life inside of Canteens she receives from certain characters. The Canteen can be refilled by the Fairies of the [[Fairy Pool]]. If Zelda does not have a Canteen, then the Water of Life they give will simply restore her health.

==={{ZA|-}}===
[[File:ZA Life Potion Sprite.png|32px|left]]
In {{ZA}}, Zelda can purchase Life Potions from a number of Merchants around [[Tolemac]] for 200 Rupees. They fully restore Zelda's health when consumed. She may only carry one Potion at a time.

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Main|Apothecary}}
Potions in {{HW}} appear as both usable items and as special Mixtures that can be crafted in the {{Term|HW|Bazaar|link}}'s Apothecary which will give temporary boosts to certain attributes of a Warrior for the duration of the next battle.

As items, Potions can be used in battle to recover a Warrior's health. These cannot be used unless a Warrior has a Bottle [[Badge]] crafted, and multiple Bottle Badges can be crafted to allow a Warrior more Potion uses. The type and quality of Potions is increased by the number of [[Illustration|Illustration Piece]]s collected, with more pieces resulting in higher quality Potions. Higher quality Potions will restore more hearts, as well as restore the Special Attack gauge. Red, Purple, Blue, Green, and Yellow Potions exist, and are unlocked in that order.

Mixtures crafted in the Apothecary provide Warriors with a specified boost that lasts for the duration of the next battle. These can range from increased rates in weapon or [[Material]] drops, a boost in a Warrior's attack power, allowing a Warrior to break an enemy's guard, and many others. Mixtures require Rupees and a certain amount of Materials to be crafted. As more Illustration Pieces are acquired, more Mixtures are unlocked.
