The {{Term/Store|Lokomo Sword||Series, ST}} is a weapon in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 130 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
According to [[Anjean]], the Lokomo Sword was given to the [[Lokomo]] as a gift. It was once used by the [[Spirits of Good]], who used it to defeat many evil creatures during their lifetime.<ref name= "Sacred">{{Cite|This Lokomo Sword was a gift from one of the spirits. As such, it is filled with sacred energy. It has felled many evil creatures in its lifetime. Until now, only the spirits have wielded this blade...|Anjean|ST}}</ref> The Lokomo Sword is given to [[Link]] by Anjean inside the [[Spirit Train]] after he and [[Princess Zelda]] retrieve the [[Bow of Light]] from the [[Sand Temple]]. Although only spirits had wielded the Sword before, Anjean trusts the Lokomo Sword to Link, saying that he is the one who is meant to wield it.<ref>{{Cite|Before you go, please take this, Link. I think you're the one who is meant to use it.|Anjean|ST}}</ref>

The Lokomo Sword can stun [[Phantom]]s without the need of [[Tears of Light]], since it is already infused with sacred energy.<ref name= "Sacred"/> Unlike the [[Phantom Sword]] from {{PH}}, it does not defeat them, as it instead stuns the Phantoms long enough for Princess Zelda to possess their bodies. In the final battle against [[Malladus]], Link, with the help of Princess Zelda, thrusts the Lokomo Sword into Malladus's forehead, defeating the Demon King.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}} and {{HW|L}}, the Lokomo Sword appears as the third tier of [[Toon Link]]'s [[Light Sword]] Weapon paired with the [[Mirror Shield]] from {{TWW}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The Lokomo Sword's hilt is shaped like the [[Spirits of Good#Symbol of the Spirits|symbol of the Spirits of Good]], while the pattern on the blade is reminiscent of the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}}.
*It's unknown what became of the Lokomo Sword following the ending of the game, as Link is shown using the [[Recruit's Sword]] once again.
