{{Term/Store|Leekah||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Leekah|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Leekah}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman who can be found either at {{Term|BotW|Rebonae Bridge|link}}, near {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}, or near {{Term|BotW|Horwell Bridge|link}}. While there, she likes to watch the {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}}.<ref name="Leekah">{{Cite|I like watching the river flow by. Is that such a crime?|Leekah|BotW}}</ref> Whenever {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} encounters her, she will be under attack by a {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Somebody, please!|Leekah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh nooooo!|Leekah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yikes!|Leekah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yikes! Help!|Leekah|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves {{Term|BotW|Leekah}}, she gives him {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}} or ingredients as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Gosh... Thanks for the save. I ought to reward you, huh? Will this do?|Leekah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whew. I was nervous for a minute there! Thanks for the help!|Leekah|BotW}}</ref> When gifting it to him, she may remark that he deserves it more than the guy she planned to give it to.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for saving my hide! You deserve this more than the guy I was taking it to.|Leekah|BotW}}</ref> She might also claim that she is scared of what would have happened to her if {{Term|BotW|Link}} did not show up.<ref>{{Cite|I don't even want to think about where I'd be if you hadn't shown up! This is the least I can do in return.|Leekah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wow, thanks. I might've been that thing's stew without you. Hmm, what do I have to give you in return? How's this?|Leekah|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves {{Term|BotW|Leekah}} for the first time, she informs him that she likes watching the {{Term|BotW|Hylia River}} flow by.<ref name="Leekah"/> When she watches the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, she finds it hard to believe that the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}} ever happened.<ref>{{Cite|Seeing the water stream so peacefully makes it hard to believe the Great Calamity really happened.|Leekah|BotW}}</ref> When she is inevitably attacked by {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}}, she is reminded to return to the nearest {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Then monsters attack out of nowhere, and I think—oh, right. Time to head back to the stables! Ugh... Whatever.|Leekah|BotW}}</ref>

On subsequent rescues, {{Term|BotW|Leekah}} expresses disdain that she is unable to take a walk and watch the {{Term|BotW|Hylia River}} without needing to be saved.<ref>{{Cite|Saved again... Am I so wrong for just wanting to sit by the river? It's getting so I can't even take a walk anymore.|Leekah|BotW}}</ref> She then decides to return to either {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable}}, or {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's life these days, I guess. Whatever. I'm going back to the stable.|Leekah|BotW}}</ref> She may also remark that it is typical that her day out would be ruined.<ref>{{Cite|Typical. Just typical. I try to have a day out, just watching the river go by, and now... Ugh. I'm going back to the stable.|Leekah|BotW}}</ref>

Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} fail to save {{Term|BotW|Leekah}}, she will eventually pick herself up off the ground.<ref>{{Cite|That was close...|Leekah|BotW}}</ref> When she does so, she claims that her head hurts, and she wonders why she thought it would be a good idea to take this road.<ref>{{Cite|Ughhh, my aching head... Why did I think it was a good idea to take this road?|Leekah|BotW}}</ref> She may also be angry, accusing {{Term|BotW|Link}} of standing by while she got beat up.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, look who's here! The guy who stood around and watched me get beat up! A little help next time maybe?!|Leekah|BotW}}</ref>

When she arrives at a {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Leekah}} will stand at its entryway. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} previously saved her, she claims to feel less anxious when he is around.<ref>{{Cite|I feel less anxious with you around. Thanks.|Leekah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Leekah}} has heard that ladies 100 years ago were able to walk alone without feeling threatened, but she is unable to imagine that.<ref name="Lady"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} did not save her, {{Term|BotW|Leekah}} will be irritated and wonder what she should do now.<ref>{{Cite|Stupid monsters... Now what do I do?|Leekah|BotW}}</ref>
