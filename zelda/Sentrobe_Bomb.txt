{{Term/Store|Sentrobe Bomb|Sentrobe Bombs|Series, SS|plural}} are enemies in {{SS}}.{{Name Ref|SS}}

==Characteristics==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Target lock: {{Color|SS Blue|Sentrobe Bomb}}</p><p>This is a bomb projectile fired from a {{Color|SS Blue|Sentrobe}}. It will follow its target anywhere, although a timed fuse ensures it will explode after a set time.</p>
}}

{{clear|left}}
After a {{Term|SS|Sentrobe}}'s missile is deflected back at it, it will deploy {{Plural|SS|Sentrobe Bomb}}, which are smaller, more easily destructible versions of the {{Plural|SS|Sentrobe}}. When deployed, {{Plural|SS|Sentrobe Bomb}} follow {{Term|SS|Link|link}} around and explode after a set time limit. {{Term|SS|Link}} can safely destroy them by slicing them alongside their opening, either vertically or horizontally. Unlike their larger counterparts, they cannot fire missiles at {{Term|SS|Link}}. When {{Plural|SS|Sentrobe Bomb}} are following {{Term|SS|Link}}, they are accompanied by a high-pitch, mechanical theme.
