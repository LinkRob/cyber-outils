[[File:DS Lite stylus.jpg|thumb|Nintendo DS Lite stylus]]
The '''stylus''' is a pen-like accessory used to operate the touch screen on the [[Nintendo DS]], [[Nintendo 3DS]], and the [[Wii U]].

The stylus is commonly used to make menu selections, which the player can accomplish by bringing the stylus in contact with their selection on the touch screen. The stylus is often helpful or required in gameplay, such as moving [[Link]] around in {{PH}}.

On the Nintendo DS, the stylus slot is located on the back, and is inserted downwards, relative to the Nintendo DS itself. For the [[Nintendo DS Lite]], it is on the right side, where one inserts it from right to left, relative to the DS Lite. For the Nintendo 3DS and [[Wii U GamePad]], the stylus slot is on the back, similar to the Nintendo DS, but is located on the side for the [[Nintendo 3DS XL]], similar to the Nintendo DS Lite.

The stylus for the DS is noticeably thinner than the stylus for the DS Lite. A Nintendo DS generally comes with two styluses, and extra styli have been released separately.

==Special edition styli==
==={{PH|-}} styli===
[[File:PH Sylus Set.jpg|thumb|The {{PH|-}} stylus un-boxed]]
A {{PH|-}} stylus set was released by Yujin in 2007. There are a total of seven different styli in the set, each with a different item top. Each stylus has a body that is either green or orange.

===''Feather Pen Stylus''===
Around the launch of {{PH}}, [[Nintendo]] of America ran a giveaway of {{PH|-}}-themed styli. To obtain the stylus, the customer had to register {{PH|-}} on the Nintendo website and take the subsequent survey.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Register your The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass game and fill out a quick survey to get a free Nintendo DS Feather Pen Stylus.|published= |retrieved=February 12, 2016|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20080206232811/http://my.nintendo.com/consumer/opr_promos.jsp|title= |site= My.Nintendo.com|type=}}</ref>

At E3 2009, the stylus was given to players who tried the demo for {{ST|-}}.

In Japan, the same stylus was released as an early buyer bonus for {{ST}}. It came inside a green Rupee case.
