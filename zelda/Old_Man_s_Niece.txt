The {{Term/Store|Old Man's Niece||Series, TAoL}} is a character in {{TAoL}}.<ref name="E 223"/>

==Biography==
The [[Old Man]]'s Niece lives in [[Ruto Town]]. She informs [[Link]] that their [[Trophy]] was stolen.<ref>{{Cite|GORIYA OF TANTARI STOLE OUR TROPHY|Old Man's Niece|TAoL}}</ref> The Trophy, which is located in a {{Term|TAoL|Cave|link}} in the [[Tantari Desert]], is being guarded by a [[Goriya]]. As a reward for recovering the Trophy, she allows Link to meet [[Old Man|her uncle]], who will teach the young hero the [[Jump]] spell.<ref>{{Cite|YOU SAVED THE TROPHY COME SEE MY UNCLE.|Old Man's Niece|TAoL}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|WITH THIS YOU CAN JUMP VERY HIGH.|Old Man|TAoL}}</ref>
