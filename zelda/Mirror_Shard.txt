{{Term/Store|Mirror Shard|Mirror Shards|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are objects in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 131}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
A {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Shard}} is a piece of the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror of Twilight|link}}, which {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}} broke so that {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}} could not reach the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight Realm|link}}. Only one {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Shard}} is intact in the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror of Twilight}} when {{Term|TPHD|Link}} first discovers it in the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Chamber|link}}. The {{Plural|TPHD|Sage|link}} reveal to {{Term|TPHD|Link}} the locations of the remaining {{Plural|TPHD|Mirror Shard}}: one is "in the mountains", which is where the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins|link}} are; another is in an "ancient grove" which is actually the {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time|link}} in the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Grove|link}}. The last shard is "in the heavens", meaning the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The mirror shards lie in deep mountain snows, within an ancient wood, and somewhere in the sky...|Midna|TP}}</ref> {{Term|TPHD|Link}} must retrieve all three missing {{Plural|TPHD|Mirror Shard}} and head back to the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Chamber}} to reassemble it.  When the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror of Twilight}} is repaired, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} is able to enter the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight Realm}} and confront {{Term|TPHD|Zant}}.

The {{Plural|TPHD|Mirror Shard}} can cause misfortune to those who happen upon them. {{Term|TPHD|Yeta|link}} of {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak|link}} mentions that ever since {{Term|TPHD|Yeto|link}} found a {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Shard}}, monsters have started to appear around the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins}} and she has fallen sick.<ref>{{Cite|But...since I get mirror, I get sick, and then bad monsters appear... So many bad things happen since mirror...|Yeta|TP}}</ref> The power of the {{Plural|TPHD|Mirror Shard}} is enough to completely transform the others around them, much like how the {{Plural|TPHD|Fused Shadow|link}} drastically changed {{Term|TPHD|Darbus|link}} into {{Term|TPHD|Fyrus|link}}. This is shown when {{Term|TPHD|Yeta}} shows {{Term|TPHD|Link}} the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Shard}} that {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} found: when she looks at the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Shard}}, she mentions how beautiful it is, but shortly after its powers cause for {{Term|TPHD|Yeta}} to transform into a monster known as {{Term|TPHD|Blizzeta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Still... I feel bad about the way we treated that girl. To think the Mirror of Twilight has the power to change people like that...|Midna|TP}}</ref>
