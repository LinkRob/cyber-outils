The {{Term/Store|Toruma Dunes||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Toruma Dunes'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Toruma Dunes are located in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. A set of four [[Torch]]es can be found in the Toruma Dunes which, when lit, reveal the [[Tho Kayu Shrine]].

A [[Molduga]] can be seen swimming around the sands of the Toruma Dunes.

==Nomenclature==
The Toruma Dunes derive their name from ''{{Wp|tourmaline}}'', a type of gemstone.
