The {{Term/Store|Pendant of Memories||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.{{Fact}}{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Pendant of Memories}} is referred to as the '''Memorial Pendant''' in {{Guide|MM|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Versus |page= 55}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
[[File:MM3D Pendant of Memories Icon.png|left]]
<!-- Obtention:
You accepted the {{Color|MM3D Red|Pendant of Memories}}! Though you may not understand these grown-up matters, you should probably take the pendant to {{Color|MM3D Red|Anju}}, anyway.

Inventory:
{{Color|MM3D Red|Pendant of Memories}}<br/>Kafei entrusted you with this cherished memento. See to it that it's safely delivered to Anju! -->
The Pendant of Memories is a small charm that [[Kafei]] wears around his neck. When [[Link]] meets Kafei for the first time in the [[Curiosity Shop Back Room]] during the [[Second Day]], Kafei gives Link the Pendant after explaining what happened to him and asks that he give it to [[Anju]].<ref>{{Cite|I know Anju is worried...but I can't go out yet. I made a promise to her that I would bring the wedding mask and greet her. This {{Color|MM3D Red|pendant}}... Give it to Anju.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> Upon receiving it, Anju will promise to wait for Kafei until he returns, heavily implying the sentimental value of the Pendant.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! ...It's from Kafei. Th...th...thank you... Very much!|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I have decided to wait for him. I've made my promise... I'm fine with this. I believe him.|Anju|MM}}</ref>

If Link fails to deliver the Pendant of Memories to Anju but continues to help Kafei retrieve the [[Sun's Mask]] from [[Sakon's Hideout]], Kafei will arrive at the [[Stock Pot Inn]] without Anju being there, blaming himself for not being able to keep his promise.<ref>{{Cite|...But my promise... I couldn't keep it.|Kafei|MM}}</ref>
