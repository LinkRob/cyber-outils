{{Term/Store|Blizzagia||Series, TFH}} is the {{Term|TFH|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Ice Temple|link}} in {{TFH}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}} is a snake-like creature covered in {{Term|TFH|Ice|link}} crystals and wearing a rocky mask. It is battled in a large pit with five cave entrances at its rear.

In the first battle phase, {{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}} emerges from one of the five caves at random and targets one of the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}, as indicated by a colored flash from its eyes. It will then make a powerful lunge toward that {{Term|TFH|Links}} in an attempt to bite him. If the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are able to dodge {{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}}, they will have a brief moment to strike its head with a {{Term|TFH|Hammer|link}} before it retreats back to the cave. After four hits from the {{Term|TFH|Hammer}}, a large crack appears in {{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}}'s mask, followed by the creature roaring loudly and using its cold breath to cover the ground with a layer of {{Term|TFH|Ice}}.

{{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}} then begins to slither around the pit in a circular pattern, gnashing its teeth when the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} get near to continue attacking its mask with their {{Plural|TFH|Hammer}}. The {{Term|TFH|Ice}} crystals along its back can be destroyed with the {{Term|TFH|Hammer}} or melted with the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}} to produce {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Energy Potion|link}}, and hitting {{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}}'s body with the {{Term|TFH|Hammer}} will also stop its movement for about one second. {{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}}'s icy tail will harm and freeze the {{Term|TFH|Links}} on contact, and can only be temporarily melted with the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}}; it will regrow after five seconds. Occasionally, {{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}} will slither into one of the caves and re-emerge with its {{Term|TFH|Ice}} crystals fully restored.

After taking several more hits, {{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}}'s mask will shatter, revealing a glowing jewel on the creature's head. {{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}} will begin to move more erratically, frequently raising and lowering its head to avoid the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}' attacks, and targeting the nearest {{Term|TFH|Links}} by slithering in tight coils around him, making it difficult to escape unharmed. However, in this final phase, {{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}}'s head is vulnerable to all weapons including the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}, and after enough hits the {{Term|TFH|Boss}} will be defeated.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|TFH|Blizzagia}}'s name is a portmanteau of "Blizzard" and "{{Term|Series|Volvagia|link}}".
