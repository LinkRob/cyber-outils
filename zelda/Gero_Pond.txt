{{Term/Store|Gero Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gero Pond|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Gero Pond is a small, shallow [[Hot Spring]] in northern [[Eldin Canyon]]. It is located right next to the road that paves northeast through [[Eldin (Region)|Eldin]], sitting right outside the tunnel's southern entrance. The Pond is guarded by a [[Guardian Stalker]] that patrols the general area. As a Hot Spring, the water is warm and will gradually recover Link's health as he stands in it.

In the Pond's water is a ring of jutting rocks and several [[Rock]]s nearby. Throwing one of the Rocks into the ring causes a [[Korok]] to appear, who will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] for finding him.
