{{Term/Store|Tick Tock||Series, OoS}} is a characters in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250}}</ref> He is a clockmaker who lives in [[Horon Village]].

==Biography==
Tick Tock is always working on improving clocks in his [[Clock Shop]]. During the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}, Tick Tock trades [[Link]] the [[Engine Grease]] for a [[Wooden Bird]] he needs to complete a clock.<ref>{{Cite|I want to make a Cuckoo Clock better than any before, but I can't find a Wooden Bird that I like... One that's just breathtaking...|Tick Tock|OoS}}</ref>

In a [[Linked Game]], a secret staircase behind Tick Tock's Shop can be unearthed with the [[Shovel]] leading to a secret room. The room is inhabited by an [[Old Man]], who is the recipient of a secret obtained in {{OoA}}.
