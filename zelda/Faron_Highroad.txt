==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Faron Highroad}} is an expansive road that begins around the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}, continues into the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}, then ends in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. It passes through the {{Term|BotW|Taobab Grassland|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Oseira Plains|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nautelle Wetlands|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Darybon Plains|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mounted Archery Camp|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Harfin Valley|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Faron Woods|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pagos Woods|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Floria River|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sarjon Bridge|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sarjon Woods|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Floria Bridge|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ebara Forest|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Atun Valley|link}} before ending at {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} travelers, {{Term|BotW|Chumin|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Letty|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Spinch (Hylian)|link}}, can be found traveling the {{Term|BotW|Faron Highroad}}.
