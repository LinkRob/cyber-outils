==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Bird Egg}} are {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} that can be found in birds' nests atop {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are also sold at the {{Term|BotW|East Wind|link}} and {{Term|BotW|High Spirits Produce|link}}, as well as by the travelling {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} {{Term|BotW|Agus|link}}. They can be eaten raw to restore a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} or used as a cooking ingredient to make various {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Bird Egg}} become {{Plural|Series|Campfire Egg|link}} when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Hard-Boiled Egg|link}} when dropped in {{Plural|BotW|Hot Spring|link}}. At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bird Egg}} can be used to make {{Term|BotW|Yellow Dye|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bird Egg}} are necessary to make the following {{Plural|BotW|Food}}:
