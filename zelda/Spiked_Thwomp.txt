{{Term/Store|Spiked Thwomp|Spiked Thwomps|Series, LA, LADX, OoS, OoA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 205 {{Exp Game|LA, LADX, OoS, OoA}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Spiked Thwomp}} were referred to as '''Thwomp Blocks''' in {{Guide|OoA|BradyGames}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA |publisher= BradyGames |page= 16 |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} They are a variant of {{Smw|Thwomp}}s from the ''{{Smw|Mario (series)|Mario}}'' series, but only appear in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series.

Spiked Thwomps possess a single eye and spikes on their lower side, hence their name. Like other Thwomps, they drop down when [[Link]] approaches, attempting to crush him from above. Their top surface is flat however, and can be used as a platform to reach higher areas.

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA|-}}, Spiked Thwomps appear in side-scrolling areas of dungeons. They quickly drop down from a great height to crush any intruders below. This can be predicted when the Thwomp glares down below it, just before falling. After slamming down, they slowly rise up back into the air before falling again. Link can safely by-pass them by teasing the Spiked Thwomp by inching forward to initiate it to drop, then stepping back and walk by as it is slowly rises into the air. Alternately, it can also be quickly evaded by equipping and using the [[Pegasus Boots]] to run past. Unlike the normal [[Thwomp]]s, only its bottom side is covered in spikes, and its top surface is flat. Link can jump on top of one as it lands by using the [[Roc's Feather]]. This becomes necessary to reach higher ledges in certain areas. Even when Link is standing on top of one, it will continue to slam down and rise, thus requiring Link to time his jumps to reach higher ledges. The enemies are completely invincible to all attacks and cannot be destroyed or stunned. They appear in [[Angler's Tunnel]] and [[Catfish's Maw]].

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}, Spiked Thwomps appear identically to those in {{LA|-}}, but appear blue. They appear in side-scrolling areas, and drop down in an attempt to slam onto Link. As before, their top surfaces are flat and can be used to reach higher places. As the Pegasus Boots are absent, the [[Pegasus Seed]]s can be used instead to quickly run past the falling Thwomps. They appear in the [[Wing Dungeon]] in {{OoA}}, and [[Snake's Remains]] in {{OoS}}.

==Trivia==
*In one of the side-scrolling passages in the Wing Dungeon of {{OoA|-}}, if Link uses the Roc's Feather to jump onto the Spiked Thwomp at just the right moment, he can reach a secret tunnel with 30 [[Rupee]]s otherwise hidden.
