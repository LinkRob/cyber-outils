'''Tekom''' is a secondary antagonist in {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
A [[Tecuum]] created in the image of [[Princess Zelda#Echoes of Wisdom|Princess Zelda]] by Null to trick [[The Great Deku Tree#Echoes of Wisdom|the Great Deku Tree]] into granting her access to the [[Ancient Ruins#Echoes of Wisdom|Ancient Ruins]] below, where the "[[Triforce#Echoes of Wisdom|Prime Energy]]" is stored.

Once Zelda manages to catch up to Tekom in the [[Stilled Ancient Ruins]], the latter is forced to engage her in battle, and upon defeat, she flees into [[Null's Body]]. After she wins, Zelda reunites with [[Link#Echoes of Wisdom|Link]], returns to him the [[Sword of Might|Weapons]] [[Bow of Might|of]] [[Bombs of Might|Might]], and the two chase Tekom into Null's Body, where Tekom surrenders her piece of the Prime Energy to Null and the final battle begins.

==Characteristics==
Tekom is cold and dispassionate, only caring about her mission and serving as an indirect mouthpiece for Null, explaining its motives and desires in its stead.

===Physical Appearance===
Tekom resembles Zelda in her [[Customary Attire]] and carrying the [[Tri Rod]], but with red eyes and often frowning. At low health, she gains a purple hue.

==History==
Tekom is a [[Tecuum]], one of Null's minions. She assumed Zelda's form to obtain the "Prime Energy" on behalf of her master, who seeks to restore the state of emptiness that preceded the [[Creation of the World]]. At the Eternal Forest, Tekom neutralizes [[General Wright]] and his soldiers, and tricks the Great Deku Tree into revealing the path to the Ancient Ruins, allowing her access to the Prime Energy.

However, due to her unbalanced heart, as both she and her master wish only for the Power to destroy existence, Tekom is only left with [[Triforce of Power|one piece]] of the Prime Energy. Meanwhile, Zelda gains [[Triforce of Wisdom|another piece]], while the [[Triforce of Courage|third piece]] opens a small [[Rift]] and enters it. Choosing to leave Zelda for later, Tekom chases the third piece into the [[Still World]], where she sees the piece enter Link's possession, but Zelda chases after her and defeats her in combat. Weakened and desperate, Tekom flees into Null's Body, where she gives her piece to her master's core, empowering it and allowing herself to be erased.

==Strategy==
The overall battle against Tekom is basically a mirror battle, as Tekom uses the same moves and abilities as Zelda's normal form, but her [[Dark Echo]]es will be stronger then Zelda's own Echoes. Thoughout the battle, Tekom will fly around and summon Dark Echoes to fight Zelda. In each phase, Tekom will summon diffrent types of Echoes:

Phase 1: Small Echoes like Freeze Slugs and Darknuts. 

Phase 2: Stronger Echoes like ReDeads and Tweelus. 

Phase 3: Tougher Echoes such as Lizalfos Lv. 3, Boarblins and Arurodas.

{{Clarify}}{{Exp|This needs a more exact list of what Dark Echoes she can summon per phase.}}

Damaging Tekom is difficult, even with flying Echoes, due to her flying around. Using the [[Bow of Might]] has better chances of hitting, but can cost too much [[Energy]] if you don't have at least the Final Mastery equipped. Meanwhile, you should equip strong Echoes that are capable of both crowd control to keep her Dark Echoes off of you while you take aim at her, and dealing additional damage when she is stunned.

When you manage to stun Tekom, get in close and attack with the [[Sword of Might]] and your strongest ground Echoes, like the [[Lynel#Echoes of Wisdom|Lynel]] or the [[Sword Moblin Lv. 3]].

==Nomenclature==
Tekom's name resembles the Hebrew word ''t'hóm'' (written תְּהוֹם) which means "abyss" or "void", reflecting how she and her master wish to erase existence.

==Trivia==
* Tekom's name comes from the game's internal files concerning her. In-game, she is only ever called "Zelda" when speaking, and is just known as the "echo of Zelda" when discussed in dialogue.
* Tekom is the last story boss in {{EoW|-}} to be fought by Zelda's Swordfighter Form, as Zelda will give the Sword and Shield back to Link before entering Null's Body, and both [[Smog#Echoes of Wisdom|Smog]] and the [[Volcanic Talus]] are optional.
