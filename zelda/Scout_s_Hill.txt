{{Term/Store|Scout's Hill||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Scout's Hill'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Scout's Hill is located in [[Hyrule Field]], along the western bank of the [[Hylia River]] just before it empties into [[Lake Hylia]]. [[Bosh Kala Shrine]] can be found at the Hill's northern base.

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Sapphire]] can be found atop a stone pillar on the Hill's northern section. At the base of the pillar is a wooden crate holding [[Arrow]]s. Some [[Ore Deposit]]s are present near the Shrine, just to the west.

A [[Korok]] can be found by lifting a [[Rock]] on top of the ruins at the Hill's peak.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref> Another can be found by pushing a [[Boulder]] down into a hole on the northern half of the Hill.
