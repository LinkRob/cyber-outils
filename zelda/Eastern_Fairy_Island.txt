{{Term|TWWHD|Eastern Fairy Island}} is an island located on the Great Sea. The entrance to the fairy shell on the island is blocked by a giant boulder, so [[bomb]]s or [[Power Bracelets]] are necessary to get inside. The Great Fairy that resides within will grant Link an upgrade to his [[Bomb Bag]]. Interestingly, despite its name, the island is actually in a portion of the Great Sea's northeastern region.

This island is home to a rare [[Blue ChuChu]], and it's also where the [[Korok]] [[Olivio]] and one of the withered {{Plural|TWWHD|Forest Tree|link}} can be found.
