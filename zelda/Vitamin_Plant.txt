'''Vitamin Plants''' are large [[wikipedia:Venus Flytrap|venus flytrap-like]] creatures that appear on the {{Term|FPTRR|First Continent|link}} in {{FPTRR}} in the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Forest (First Continent)|display=Deku Forest}}. {{Plural|FPTRR|Vitamin Plant}}, are nearly identical in behavior and appearance to their oceanic counterparts the {{Plural|FPTRR|Oyster|link}}, although {{Plural|FPTRR|Vitamin Plant}} are green. Instead of pearls, {{Plural|FPTRR|Vitamin Plant}} hold {{Plural|FPTRR|Recipes|link}} and even people. If fed scrap broth, {{Plural|FPTRR|Vitamin Plant}} turn them into {{Plural|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Forest Vitamin|display=Forest Vitamin|link}} after a certain amount of time. {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} must be cautious not to have them clamp down on him, causing him to lose 15 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}. {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can be freed from the Plant's grip by tapping it with the stylus.
