==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Giant Ancient Core}} are a rare drop from {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}. They can also be obtained from {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. Like all {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}, they were made by the ancient {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah|link}} 10,000 years before {{BotW|-}}. When placed on the ground, the inner ring will spin horizontally and the core will spin diagonally.

The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} requires {{Plural|BotW|Giant Ancient Core}} to make several pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}, such as the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Bow|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Giant Ancient Core}}, as {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, can also be combined with critters in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}. However, {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} them with anything else will result in {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} will accept them as an ingredient for {{Term|BotW|Gray Dye|link}}, but they can also be sold to {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Giant Ancient Core}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
