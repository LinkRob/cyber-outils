'''Mega Cheep-Cheeps''' (spelled '''Mega Cheep Cheep''' or '''Big Cheep Cheep''' in later ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' media) are [[enemy|enemies]] in {{FSA}}. They are a larger variant of [[Cheep Cheep]]. Mega Cheep-Cheeps are from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], although {{FSA|-}} is the first game to feature them as enemies in gneeral.

Mega Cheep-Cheeps are encountered in [[Lake Hylia (Four Swords Adventures)|Lake Hylia]], the [[Desert of Doubt (Stage)|Desert of Doubt]], and [[Frozen Hyrule (Stage)|Frozen Hyrule]], where they attack one of the [[Links]] by charging at him.
