==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Copious Fried Wild Greens are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} four different vegetables, herbs, or flowers together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they'll restore several {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. They're known to be healthy meals made by cooking mixed greens over a strong flame.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Due to the variety and number of ingredients needed to make Copious Fried Wild Greens, only their default version is available. It's impossible to add any effects to this dish.
