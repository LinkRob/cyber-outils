The {{Term/Store|Shrine of Koridai||Series, TFoE}} is a location in {{TFoE}}.<ref>{{Cite|Through the "eye of Glutko" lies the Shrine of Koridai.|Gwonam|TFoE}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TFoE|Shrine of Koridai}} is a small structure that appears at the end of the {{Term|TFoE|Glutko|link}} {{Term|TFoE|Stage}}. It houses the sacred {{Term|TFoE|Book of Koridai|link}}. {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}} encounters {{Term|TFoE|Glutko (Boss)|link}} here a second time, who is guarding the {{Term|TFoE|Book of Koridai}} and relinquishes it after he is defeated again. A {{Term|TFoE|Triforce Map|link}} also appears after {{Term|TFoE|Glutko (Boss)}} is defeated, which exits and clears the {{Term|TFoE|Stage}}.
