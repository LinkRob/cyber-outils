The {{Term/Store|Temple of Courage||Series, PH}} is the third {{Term|PH|Dungeon|link}} in {{PH}}, situated on {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}. It protects a clone of {{Term|PH|Ciela|link}}, which contains some of her lost powers and memories.<ref>{{Cite|I understand how you feel. You lost your memory... The spirit you just freed holds the powers you lost.|Oshus|PH}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Temple==
The {{Term|PH|Dungeon|link}} is located north of {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}. The path to the {{Term|PH|Dungeon}} is blocked by a large door within the {{Term|PH|Old Wayfarer|link}}'s secret cave that can only be opened by using the {{Term|PH|Sun Key|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Temple of Courage will open to the holder of the Sun Key.|N/A|PH}}</ref> In order to gain entrance to the temple itself, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} must explore the dense northern forest and find three {{Plural|PH|Tribal Head Statue|link}} to make them shine intersecting beams on the outer gate.<ref>{{Cite|Only statues can urge the temple door open, so focus their light gaze on it!|N/A|PH}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
[[File:Arrow Orb Activated.png|thumb|left|Link activating an Arrow Orb]]
The {{Term|PH|Temple of Courage}} consists of four floors. Thematically, the {{Term|PH|Dungeon|link}}'s puzzles test the courage and faith of the venturing visitor. For example, one huge room with pillars has an invisible passage over a cliff, while another room has a cliff that must be traversed using a controllable platform. The {{Term|PH|Dungeon}}'s {{Term|PH|Item|link}} is the {{Term|PH|Bow|link}} and {{Plural|PH|Arrow|link}}, useful to press switches and making use of {{Plural|PH|Arrow Orb|link}}. On the last floor is the {{Term|PH|Boss|link}}, {{Term|PH|Crayk|link}}. Upon defeating it, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} will free the last servant {{Term|PH|Spirit|link}} (who actually turns out to be part of {{Term|PH|Ciela|link}}) as well as earn another {{Term|PH|Heart Container|link}} and more {{Term|PH|Sand of Hours|link}}.
