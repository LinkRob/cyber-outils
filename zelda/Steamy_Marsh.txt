The Steamy Marsh is a location in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|FPTRR|Steamy Marsh}} is an area in the {{Term|FPTRR|Second Continent|link}}. When the Steamy Marsh is first entered, the air is filthy. This is caused by a strange hole on a hilltop in the south-eastern part of the area. The area is also home to a bee hive, where the Bee Queen lives. The polluted air has damaged the Queen's health, and to advance to the {{Term|FPTRR|Insect Cavern|link}} in {{Term|FPTRR|Gooey Swamp|link}} she must have first regained her health. To clear the air, the strange hole in the south-eastern part of the Marsh must be destroyed using {{Plural|FPTRR|Bomb|link}}.

In this area, several archaeologists and a man who claims to have invented empty jars are also encountered. This area's local specialty is {{Term|FPTRR|Steamy Oil|link}}.
