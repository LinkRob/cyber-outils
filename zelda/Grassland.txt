{{Term/Store|Grassland||Series, CoH}} is a location in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Grassland|Map|CoH}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|CoH|Grassland|link}} is a region that consists of a temperate, grassy biome with puddles of shallow water and mounds of dirt scattered across it. Dungeons are often hidden throughout its landscape, either under bushes or {{Plural|CoH|Tree Stump|link}} marked with blue {{Plural|CoH|Butterfly}} on them, or behind cracked walls that must be broken with a {{Term|CoH|Bomb|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Bombchu|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Din's Fire|link}}.

Though its placement in the world is random, it always appears directly between {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Lake Hylia|link}}, while remaining scattered everywhere else.
