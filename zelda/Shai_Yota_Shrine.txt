The {{Term/Store|Shai Yota Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Shai Yota Shrine}}<br/>Shai Yota's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Shai Yota's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Shai Yota Shrine}} is located on an island in {{Term|BotW|Horon Lagoon|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Sea|link}}. Initially, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is buried beneath the ground.

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Horon Lagoon}}, who will ask him if he wants to hear an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" passed down in the area.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses to listen, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} sings a song about how one must land on the altar to open the way.<ref>{{Cite|He breaks the rocks that serve to bind,<br/>Above the tempestuous bay.<br/>On wings of cloth and wood entwined,<br/>He lands on the altar to open the way.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> This begins the "{{Term|BotW|Master of the Wind|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, several cracked rocks have to be destroyed using explosives. After destroying each cracked rock, {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}} will begin blowing through where the rock used to be. When all are destroyed, a large gust of {{Term|BotW|Wind}} will blow from a large island in the {{Term|BotW|Horon Lagoon}} to a smaller island with a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} uses the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to glide from the large island onto the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}, {{Term|BotW|Shai Yota Shrine}} will rise from beneath the ground on another small island in the {{Term|BotW|Horon Lagoon|display=Lagoon}}, completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|The "wings of cloth and wood entwined" turned out to refer to the paraglider.<br/><br/>After harnessing the wind and landing atop the dais, a shrine appeared.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shai Yota|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Shai Yota. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Shai Yota|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Shai Yota Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Shai Yota}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Great Flameblade|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Shai Yota}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Shai Yota|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Shai Yota}} will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Shai Yota|BotW}}</ref>
