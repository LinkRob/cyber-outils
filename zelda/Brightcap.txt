'''{{PAGENAME}}''' are [[items]] in {{TotK}}. This mushroom can be found in dark environments like caves. It grants weapons bouncy properties, causing enemies to be launched back when struck. 15 Brightcaps are required to upgrade [[Miner's Mask]], [[Miner's Top]] and [[Miner's Trousers]] at a Great Fairy Fountain. They are sold at the [[Spore Store]] in the Korok Forest.
