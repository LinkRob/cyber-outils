==Location and Uses==
Seared Gourmet Steak can be made by roasting a [[Raw Gourmet Meat]] with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. It can also be purchased from [[Estan]] in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} and [[Chef Cooke|Cooke]] at [[Bedrock Bistro]]. Black {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} may also drop Seared Gourmet Steak when defeated. It can be eaten by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to restore four and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}.
