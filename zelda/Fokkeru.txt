==Characteristics==
Fokkeru are enormous red birds resembling hawks and other raptors. They sport a pair of bare human breasts, similar to mythical sirens or harpies.

{{Plural|TAoL|Fokkeru}} only appear in the {{Term|TAoL|Great Palace|link}}. They they hop up and down while moving back and forth, spitting fireballs in an upward arc. These fireballs sit and burn on the ground for short periods of time, and even move along the ground towards {{Term|TAoL|Link|link}} when they land. To defeat a {{Term|TAoL|Fokkeru}}, {{Term|TAoL|Link}} must get underneath the fireball arc and strike at it with his {{Term|TAoL|Sword|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The Futabasha guide artwork, which was not created by [[Nintendo]], appears to be a complete misinterpretation of the sprite. Rather than appearing as a bird, it gives it a bizarre alien-like appearance.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|TAoL|Fokkeru}} (pronounced {{IPA|'ɸok.keɽu͍̥}} {{Respell|PHOHK|kel}}) is likely based on the German word "Vogel" (pronounced {{IPA|ˈfoːɡəl}} {{Respell|FO|gəl}}), which means "bird".
