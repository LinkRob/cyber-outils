{{Term/Store|Dr. Borville||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 276 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref> He plays a role in the restoration of {{Term|TPHD|Ilia|link}}'s memory.

==Biography==
{{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville}} is the sole doctor in {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town|link}}, where he runs the {{Term|TPHD|Medical Clinic|link}}. A proud and avaricious man, he charges high fees for his services and has no interest in helping those who cannot afford them.<ref>{{Cite|Just as I wondered who it might be, I see it's the admirable young lad who was willing to help the Zora boy for nary a single Rupee...|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Just what is it you want, lad? A physical? You've some money, don't you?|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll be honest, lad, it'll cost you a fair bit for an examination.|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you don't have any money, then you're just a bother! A bother! And I'm too busy for bothers!|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref> Dr. Borville boasts about people calling his hands "The Golden Mitts" and claims to have no trouble making money.<ref>{{Cite|It's not that I'm having trouble making money... Not at all! Why, I'm so skilled, they call my hands "The Golden Mitts"!|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref> He changes his tune when shown his astronomical running tab at {{Term|TPHD|Telma's Bar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the invoice! This invoice is for the town doctor's running tab, which is an astronomical sum.|N/A|TP}}</ref>{{Note|The running tab is at 76,480,000 {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee}} according to {{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville}}'s [[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess Trading Cards|Trading Card]].<ref>{{Cite|He is the only doctor in Castle Town and spends much time at Telma's Bar, where he has a whooping 76,480,000 Rupee tab.|Dr. Borville|[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess Trading Cards]]}}</ref> The number on the {{Term|TPHD|Invoice|link}} texture reads 75,480,000.}} He then claims not to have any money.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh! I can't get rid of that medicine stench, I don't have any money... When it rains misfortune, it pours! Remember that, lad!|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref>

When {{Term|TPHD|Prince Ralis|link}} falls ill on the way to {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town}}, {{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville}} refuses to treat the {{Term|TPHD|Zora|link}} child, stating it is beyond his expertise to do so.<ref>{{Cite|That is a Zora child! This is beyond my expertise! Hmmmph!|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref> 

{{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville}} is the first to bring {{Term|TPHD|Ilia|link}} to {{Term|TPHD|Telma's Bar}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmm, what else do I know... The first one to bring Ilia to this shop was that doctor...|Telma|TP}}</ref> He somehow obtains the {{Term|TP|Wooden Statue|link}} from {{Term|TPHD|Ilia}} with the intention of selling it.<ref name="Sell Stolen">{{Cite|Look, lad, if I'd been able to sell the wooden statue that young lady had, I would've been able to get the money...|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref> {{Term|TPHD|Louise|link}} steals it from him when he leaves it out to dry after spilling medicine on it.<ref>{{Cite|But see, I accidentally spilled medicine all over that little carving, which made it stink awfully. I put it outside to dry...but then... Well, it was stolen.|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes, I'll admit it...I took it. But then I was attacked by some sort of skeletal dog beasts who took it from me.|Louise|TP}}</ref> When {{Term|TPHD|Link}} visits the {{Term|TPHD|Medical Clinic}} after these events, {{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville}} anxiously asks him in a low voice whether Ilia has said anything about him.<ref>{{Cite|...By the way, that girl Ilia didn't mention anything about me, did she?|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...Did she really not say anything at all about me?|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref> {{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville}} denies having ever seen the {{Term|TP|Wooden Statue}}.{{When|TP}}<!-- This is from the text dump but I don't know how to trigger the dialogue. --><ref>{{Cite|I haven't seen any statue that belongs to an Ilia, or whatever her name was!|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref> When threatened with the {{Term|TPHD|Invoice|link}}, he admits the {{Term|TP|Wooden Statue}} was stolen from him.<ref name="Sell Stolen"/> If {{Term|TPHD|Link}} shows {{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville}} the retrieved {{Term|TP|Wooden Statue}}, he will be startled and claim not to know what it is.<ref>{{Cite|AIIIGH!!! That... That statue... Er... Hmm... Hmmmmph! I haven't the foggiest notion what that thing is!|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref>{{Verify|TP}}<!-- Inferring from the text dump, please check in-game --> He immediately contradicts himself by telling {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to return the {{Term|TP|Wooden Statue}} to {{Term|TPHD|Ilia}}.<ref>{{Cite|Take that thing and go return it to that girl Ilia, or whatever her name is!|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref> If {{Term|TPHD|Link}} brings news of {{Term|TPHD|Ilia}}'s recovery later on, he is startled by that too, but then claims to have predicted it from the beginning.<ref>{{Cite|Eh?! The young lady's memory... returned?! Er... Hmmm... Well... I diagnosed from the beginning that it would come back at some point! Yes, I most certainly did!|Dr. Borville|TP}}</ref>

Unlike the other residents of {{Term|TPHD|Castle Town}}, {{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville}} does not react to {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}. This may be due to his poor eyesight.
