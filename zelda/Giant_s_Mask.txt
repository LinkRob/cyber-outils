The {{Term/Store|Giant's Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's the {{Color|MM Red|Giant's Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>That truly is a rare mask...</p>}}

In {{MM}}, the {{Term|MM|Giant's Mask}} is obtained in the {{Term|MM|Stone Tower Temple|link}} after defeating the second {{Term|MM|Eyegore|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A mask that contains gigantic power seems to be resting in the temple of the accursed land...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> In the [[Nintendo 3DS|3DS]] remake {{MM3D|-}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Giant's Mask}} is instead obtained during the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|display=Temple}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Boss|link}} battle, after defeating the blue {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold|link}}.

When {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} wears the {{Term|MM3D|Mask}}, he becomes a giant.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Giant's Mask! Within this mask lies the might of a giant, but can you use its power anywhere?|N/A|MM}}</ref> It can only be used in {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold|link}}'s chamber at the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple}}. The Giant's Mask is not actually required to defeat the {{Term|MM3D|Boss}} as other items can defeat it, but it is recommended, to make the battle easier. Wearing the {{Term|MM3D|Mask}} will drain {{Term|MM3D|Link}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Magic Meter|link}}, and if depleted, he will return to normal size.

In {{MM3D}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Mask}} has an entirely different function from the original [[Nintendo 64|N64]] game. With his [[Sword]] and [[Shield]] seemingly vanished, it increases {{Term|MM3D|Link}}'s muscle mass and allows him to attack with just his bare hands, performing punches, uppercuts, and double-hand smashes, similar to [[Goron Link]]. Once the red {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold}} has been knocked down by several of these attacks, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} will be able to squeeze the {{Term|MM3D|Boss}}'s neck or swing it around by its tail before slamming it to the ground to finish it off, a more violent method in comparison to Mask's original purpose.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, the Giant's Mask appears as a costume for [[Volga]] in the {{MM|-}} DLC pack.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*When this mask is worn, Link's voice becomes slightly lower pitched.
*Although technically a transforming mask, the Giant's Mask is required to give to the [[Moon Child|Moon Children]] in order to obtain the [[Fierce Deity Mask|Fierce Deity's Mask]].
*By using ROM hacking cheats to wear the Giant's Mask inside the [[Clock Tower]], the [[Happy Mask Salesman]] will comment "That truly is a rare mask..."<ref>{{Cite|That's the Giant's Mask, isn't it? That truly is a rare mask...|Happy Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref>
