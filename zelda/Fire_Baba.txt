{{Term/Store|Fire Baba|Fire Babas|Series, ST|plural}} are enemies in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 177}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ST|Fire Baba}} look like simple bulbs, but when approached, they sprout out of their bulbs. {{Plural|ST|Fire Baba}}, when in flower form, can spit a fire ball that does a quarter of a {{Term|ST|Heart}} damage. However, they will try to eat {{Term|ST|Link}} if he gets too close. If they swallow the young hero and if the stylus is not rubbed back and forth, they will cause about one and three quarters {{Plural|ST|Heart}} of damage. When {{Plural|ST|Fire Baba}} are sliced once, their bulb parts will fly off and they will lose their ability to spew fire. In this state, they inflict greater damage when they eat {{Term|ST|Link}}.

Using the {{Term|ST|Whip|link}} on a {{Term|ST|Fire Baba}} will risk pulling the bulb toward {{Term|ST|Link}}, potentially causing {{Term|ST|Link}} get swallowed in the process. Without their stems, {{Plural|ST|Fire Baba}} are more versatile, and cannot be stunned by the {{Term|ST|Boomerang|link}}. The {{Term|ST|Boomerang}} is only effective when the {{Plural|ST|Fire Baba}} still have stems. {{Plural|ST|Bomb|link}} and {{Plural|ST|Arrow|link}} are very effective.
