The {{Term/Store|Color Dungeon||Series, LADX, LANS}} is a [[dungeon]] exclusively for {{LA|DX}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Color Dungeon}}|N/A|LANS}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Color Dungeon}} was referred to as the '''Secret Dungeon''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Secret Dungeon}}<br/>The Secret Dungeon is found beneath the Cemetery on Koholint Island. Link explored this dungeon during this quest to wake the Wind Fish, and was forced to solve many puzzles based solely on color. When he defeated the dungeon guardian, the Great Fairy rewarded him with the choice to accept either the Red or Blue Tunic.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160311084700/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/s.jsp#SecretDungeon|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It was initially added to the deluxe release of {{LA}} to showcase the use of the then-new [[Game Boy Color]]'s screen to enhance gameplay. [[Link]] can enter the {{Term|LANS|Dungeon}} as soon as he returns {{Term|LANS|BowWow|link}} to {{Term|LANS|Madam MeowMeow|link}}.

==Entrance to the {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon|display=Dungeon}}==
In the {{Term|LANS|Village Library|link}}, an unreachable {{Term|LANS|Book|link}} high on a bookshelf gives directions as to locating and revealing the dungeon's entrance.<ref>{{Cite|New world of color under the 5 gravestones.<br/>3{{Icon|LADX Up Arrow}} 4{{Icon|LADX Right Arrow}} 5{{Icon|LADX Up Arrow}}<br/>2{{Icon|LADX Left Arrow}} 1{{Icon|LADX Down Arrow}}<br/>Try with all your might. Open a new path! Whoever is worthy receives the power of color. I wonder what the world of color is?|Book|LADX}}</ref> By {{Term|LANS|Dash|link|display=Dashing}} into the {{Plural|LANS|Bookshelf|link}} with the {{Term|LANS|Pegasus Boots|link}}, [[Link]] is able to knock the book on the floor. The book instructs him to push the {{Plural|LANS|Tombstone|link}} in the southeast corner of the {{Term|LANS|Cemetery|link}} in a specific order; by following the book's instructions, the entrance reveals itself under the last pushed {{Term|LANS|Tombstone}}. However, before Link can go further, {{Term|LANS|Dion|link}} and {{Term|LANS|Gar|link}} stand in his way. They caution Link that he must have [[Magic Powder]] in order to advance and offer to sell him some if he has none.{{Note|In {{LA|DX}}, he must describe to them what color of tunic each is wearing.<ref>{{Cite|Our colors are never the same! If I am red, he is blue! If he is red, I am blue! What color is my cloth?|Gar|LADX}}</ref>}}

The {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon}} can be accessed without reading the {{Term|LANS|Book}} in the {{Term|LANS|Village Library}}, as the {{Plural|LANS|Tombstone}} can be pushed once Link has obtained the [[Power Bracelet]]. However, the final {{Term|LANS|Tombstone}} can not be moved if Link is accompanied by another character.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon}} incorporates many color-based [[Puzzle|puzzles]]. In some rooms, Link must hit {{Plural|LANS|Switch|link}} to harmonize their colors, or throw {{Plural|LANS|Orb Monster|link}} into holes matching their color to progress. The [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Nintendo Switch remake of {{LA|-}}]] adds distinctive shapes to all of the color puzzle elements so that they can be distinguished and solved by colorblind players.

Inside the {{Term|LANS|Dungeon}}, the Power Bracelet is required to complete some puzzles, and the Magic Powder is a vital item to defeat the [[Sub-Boss|Mini-Boss]] [[Giant Buzz Blob]].<ref>{{Cite|Do you have the powder? If not, you must go back.|Gar|LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LANS|Stone Hinox|link}} exhibits a more mechanical appearance in comparison to regular {{Plural|LANS|Hinox|link}}; they can make {{Plural|LANS|Boulder|link}} fall from the ceiling.

Once its [[Boss]], [[Hardhit Beetle|Hardhit Battle]], is defeated, Link meets a {{Term|LANS|Great Fairy|link}} who offers him "the power of color" in the form of either the {{Term|LANS|Blue Clothes|link}} or the {{Term|LANS|Red Clothes|link}}, which either increases his [[defense]] or [[Weapon Strength|attack power]], respectively, gaining an advantage in battle never found in the original {{LA|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, Link . I admire you for coming this far. I will give you the power of color. If you want offense, choose red. If you want defense choose blue. Which power do you want?|Great Fairy|LADX}}</ref> Link can clear the {{Term|LANS|Dungeon}} again to switch between the two special tunics. In ''Link's Awakening DX'', Link can never revert back to his standard [[Green Clothes]], but the [[Nintendo Switch]] remake allows him to switch between all three tunics.

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese version, this dungeon is known as the '''Clothing Dungeon''', in reference to the [[Red Mail|Red]] and [[Blue Mail]] awarded by the [[Great Fairy]].
*The theme played inside the {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon}} is a remixed version of the {{Term|LANS|Dungeon}} theme from {{TLoZ}}.
*The {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon}} is the only location where [[Rupee|Green Rupees]] are found in ''Link's Awakening DX''.
*As this dungeon does not exist in the original {{LA}}, the {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon}} is referred to as "Level 0" on the [[Dungeon Map]] in ''Link's Awakening DX''. This designation is not present in the [[Nintendo Switch]] remake of {{LA|-}}, however.
*The Color Dungeon is one of the primary reasons some fans rather played ''Link's Awakening DX'' over the original game, as unlocking the exclusive tunics upon completing it makes [[Link]]'s adventure slightly easier.
*It is possible to enter this dungeon while playing the original [[Game Boy]] when Link starts at the entrance with the [[Game Boy Color]] and then the player switch consoles. However, due to the dungeon itself emphasizing the use of color, it is extremely difficult otherwise to solve most puzzles and battling [[Hardhit Beetle]].
