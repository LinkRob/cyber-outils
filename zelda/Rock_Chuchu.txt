{{Term/Store|Rock Chuchu|Rock Chuchus|Series, TMC, PH|plural}},{{Exp Game|TMC, PH}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 200 {{Exp Game|TMC, PH}}}}</ref> also known as '''Rock ChuChus''',{{Exp Game|PH}}{{Name Ref|PH}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Rock Chuchu}}
{{Plural|TMC|Rock Chuchu}} in {{TMC}} appear on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}. They are {{Plural|TMC|Red Chuchu|link}} that wear rocky helmets over their faces, protecting them from most of {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}'s attacks. They do not jump, and their helmets can be destroyed by various attacks, including the {{Term|TMC|Rock Breaker|link}} technique, turning them into regular {{Plural|TMC|Red Chuchu}}. A {{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}} can also be used to defeat them instantly.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Stub|PH}}
{{Plural|PH|Rock Chuchu}} reappear in {{PH}}, though their rock helmets are now designed to look like regular rocks. They act the same as in {{TMC|-}} and are defeated by the same methods. The {{Term|PH|Hammer|link}} and {{Term|PH|Grappling Hook|link}} items are able to defeat {{Plural|PH|Rock Chuchu}} as well.
