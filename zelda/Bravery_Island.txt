== Features & Overview ==
Bravery Island is a towering Sky Island that is part of the [[North Hyrule Sky Archipelago]] in the [[Great Hyrule Forest Sky]]. It was the site of a Dive Ceremony for young [[Zonai]] to test their courage in ancient times. Zonai would prove themselves in this rite of passage by diving through a set of rings leading to a pool at the bottom of the Island. Link can participate in the Dive Ceremony by speaking to the facilitator of the Dive Ceremony, a lone [[Steward Construct]], at the bottom of Bravery Island. Before Link can undergo the Ceremony, he must pass a trial run by passing through the rings without a time limit.
When Link completes the trial run, the [[Simosiwak Shrine]] will appear at the bottom of the Island and he will be allowed to attempt the Dive Ceremony for the price of one [[Zonai Charge]]. In order to complete the Dive Ceremony, Link must pass through all of the rings in 35 seconds or less. If Link successfully does so, he will be given the [[Glide Tights]] as a reward. By beating the current record in the Dive Ceremony, Link will also receive a Large [[Zonai Charge]] and be registered as the new record holder.
