{{Term/Store|Purry||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Purry}} is a {{Term|TMC|Cat|link}} that can be found on the bridge over the southern entrance to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. While {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, she will crouch down and wiggle her haunches, swiping at him if he gets too close.

Despite this, while {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized, {{Term|TMC|Purry}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|Rowr! Would you like to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Mrow...|Purry|TMC}}</ref>

When {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Purry}} disappears and does not reappear for the remainder of the game.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Purry}}'s name is a corruption of "purr".
