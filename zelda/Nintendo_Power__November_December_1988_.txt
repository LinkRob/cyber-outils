'''Nintendo Power (November/December 1988)''' is a [[:Category:Magazines|magazine]] published by [[Nintendo]].

==Zelda Content==
===Powerful Gift Ideas (p. 38)===
The section features multiple [[:Category:Merchandise|merchandise]] as suggestions of gifts for the holidays.
* Nintendo Beach Towels {{Small|([[A La Carte/Saydah]])}}
* Action Figures {{Small|([[Hasbro Industries]])}}
* Nintendo Baseball Caps {{Small|([[Universal Industries]])}}
* Nintendo Back Packs {{Small|([[Imaginings 3]])}}
* Nintendo Stickers {{Small|([[Imperial Toy]])}}
* Nintendo Shirts {{Small|([[Shift Shed]])}}

===NES Advantage & NES Max (p. 56-57)===
The section is an advertisement for the [[NES Advantage]] and [[NES Max]] [[:Category:Controllers|controllers]]. It lists games where the different features of the controllers are the most useful. {{TLoZ}} is listed as number three under the "Turbo Function" of both controllers.

===Pak Watch (p. 81)===
The article mentions that {{TAoL}} is going to be released in December of 1988, but in "very limited quantities".

===Captain Nintendo (p. 82-84)===
This article features the Part I of the Captain Nintendo's adventures. Near the end of it, {{Term|TLoZ|Princess Zelda|link}} and {{Term|TLoZ|Ganon|link}} are mentioned. The teaser for the Part II mentions the arrival of {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} and some {{Plural|TLoZ|Darknut|link}}.

===Giant Giveaway (p. 85-91)===
This section features a giveaway of [[NES]] games and accessories for people participating in the next "Player's Poll".

Among the prizes, there are 10 sets containing the following:
* A copy of Super Mario Bros. 2
* A NES Advantage
* A copy of {{TAoL|-}}

===Dr. Power's Ten Telling Questions (p. 93)===
This section features ten questions with answers to choose from. The calculated score determines whether the reader is a "Power Player", "Video Rookie" or "Couch Crusader".

Among the questions, some featured Zelda-related answers:

;"2. Of the following, who do you admire most?"
:A. {{Wp|Jay Leno}}
:B. [[Gleeok]]
:C. [[Howard Phillips]]

;"6. What is the greatest challenge you've ever had?"
:A. Having to wait for "The {{Typo|Adventures|Adventure}} of Link."
:B. Learning to pronounce "Kid Icarus."
:C. Starting a Local Fun Club.

===Player's Forum (p. 94)===
The article mentions that Jay Leno called Nintendo Power to get help on how to beat {{Term|TLoZ|Level 7|link}} of {{TLoZ|-}}. It also specifies that Jay Leno did end up beating both the {{Term|TLoZ|First Quest|link}} and the {{Term|TLoZ|Second Quest|link}}.

===TOP 30 (p. 102-104)===
The section features the 30 most popular [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] games according to polls held by [[Nintendo]]. {{TLoZ|-}} is in first position with 8476 points. {{TAoL|-}} is in eighth position with 4204 points.{{Note|name= TAoL TOP 30|The game has received votes despite not being released yet.}}

The points for {{TLoZ|-}} go as follow:
* '''Player's Picks''': 2015 points {{Small|(Fifth position)}}
* '''Pro's Picks''': 3857 points {{Small|(First position)}}
* '''Dealer's Picks''': 2607 points {{Small|(Third position)}}

The points for {{TAoL|-}} go as follow:
* '''Player's Picks''': 1194 points {{Small|(Sixth position)}}
* '''Pro's Picks''': 1045 points {{Small|(11th position)}}
* '''Dealer's Picks''': 1165 points {{Small|(12th position)}}
