{{Term/Store|Tingle's Hood||Series, BotW}} is an item in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Tingle's Hood}}<br/>There was once a man who claimed to be the very reincarnation of a fairy. He treasured this hood, and so will you. After all, it's a rather rare find. Kooloo-Limpah!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It's only available when [[The Master Trials]] DLC pack is active.

==Location and Uses==
Tingle's Hood can only be located after obtaining the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "[[EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes]]". It can be found in a chest stuck in the ground of the [[Exchange Ruins]]. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must use [[Magnesis]] to retrieve the chest. It's only available while "[[The Master Trials]]" DLC Pack is active. After obtaining Tingle's Hood, [[Granté]] will begin selling it in [[Tarrey Town]]. Wearing the full [[Tingle's Set]] will increase Link's speed at night. Tingle's Hood can't be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] or upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
