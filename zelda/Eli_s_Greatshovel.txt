==Location and Uses==
{{Term|CoH|Eli's Greatshovel}} is a legendary {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}} that can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}. It is a type of {{Term|CoH|Greatshovel|link}} that deals 2 damage to three enemies standing in front of her along with granting a +1 attack bonus after each dig.<ref name="Obtention"/>  It can be found within a blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} located in a room accessible by the leftmost door found within the ruined {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle|link}} courtyard in the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|CoH|Eli's Greatshovel}} is named after {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}'s uncle and caretaker, Eli, from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
