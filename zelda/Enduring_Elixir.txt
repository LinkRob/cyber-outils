{{Term/Store|Enduring Elixir|Enduring Elixirs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Elixir}}<br/>Restores stamina and temporarily extends your Stamina Wheel. The additional stamina will disappear as it's used.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Enduring Elixirs will completely restore and overfill {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} when he drinks them.<ref name="Inventory"/> The excess stamina granted to Link will disappear as he uses it.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Enduring Elixirs can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|BotW|Tireless Frog|link}}

By adding any of these enduring ingredients, Link can make an Enduring Elixir that will also heal him:

* {{Term|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}}

* {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}

== Trivia ==
There are no "Enduring" ingredients that don't also restore Link's lost Hearts, since the Tireless Frog also happens to restore 3 lost Hearts when used in Elixirs. In the menu, the Hearts restored are shown instead of the full Stamina recovery effect.
