==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SSBU|Fighter Spirit}} are the {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} of {{Plural|SSBU|Fighter|link}}. They can be obtained by clearing a {{Term|SSBU|Fighter}}'s [[Classic Mode#Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|Classic Mode]] Route or by purchasing them from the [[Shop#Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|Shop]] for G 300. {{Plural|SSBU|Fighter Spirit}} serve no purpose other than for viewing in the Spirit List.
