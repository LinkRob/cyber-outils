The {{Term/Store|Goron Island Shop||Series, PH}} is a shop in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 200}}</ref> Located in [[Goron Island]], it offers a wide variety of common items for sale, such as [[Bombchu]] and [[arrow]]s. Once [[Link]] obtains Bombchu from the [[Goron Temple]], the Goron Island Shop will put up a Bombchu Bag upgrade for sale at 1000 [[Rupee]]s.

Like all the other shops, the Goron Island Shop also offers a random [[treasure]]. Only one treasure is available at any time, and the one it offers is random. After Link buys one treasure from the shop, it will take a day for the shop to restock with a new, random treasure.
