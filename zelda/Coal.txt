'''Coal''' is an item in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Coal is received from the [[Quarry Miner]] in the [[East Forest]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] cannot use the Coal herself, but she can give it to the [[Blacksmith]] in [[Great Wimbich]] for him to create the [[Diamond]].
