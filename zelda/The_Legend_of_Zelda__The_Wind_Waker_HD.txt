'''''The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD''''' is a [[Wii U]] enhanced remaster of the 2002 [[Nintendo GameCube]] title, {{TWW}}. It was officially announced via a Nintendo Direct webcast on January 23, 2013. Further information on the game was revealed at [[E3|E3 2013]] and in subsequent Nintendo Directs. The Japanese version is named '''''The Legend of Zelda: The Baton of Wind HD'''''.<br><br>{{TWWHD|-}} first became available on September 20, 2013 as a digital download from the North American Nintendo eShop.<ref name="NA Release"/> Two [[#Limited Editions|limited edition bundles]] of the game—one a special edition Wii U Deluxe Set, the other including an exclusive [[Merchandise/Figures and Plushies|figurine]] of [[Ganon|Ganondorf]]—were also released in North America and Europe.<br><br>The remaster has more detailed graphics than the original, namely rendering at a higher resolution, a new lighting engine and higher resolution textures. Some changes to gameplay were also made, the most significant of which include new items—namely the [[Swift Sail]], the [[Tingle Bottle]], and an improved [[Picto Box]]—as well as the addition of a [[Hero Mode]].

== Changes and Additions ==
=== Graphics ===
[[File:TWWHD Web 1.jpg|thumb|left|An official screenshot displaying the enhanced graphics of the remaster|273x273px]]

Being a game for the [[Wii U]], {{TWWHD|-}} runs at 1080p and 16:9 widescreen, compared to the [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|original game]]'s 480p and 4:3 for the [[Nintendo GameCube|GameCube]]. It addition, it also runs at 60 frames per second. The remaster uses an enhanced version of ''The Wind Waker''<nowiki/>'s cel-shading, the soft-shading. It utilizes a full-fledged lighting and shadowing system that allows for more realistic and fuller lighting compared to the original.<ref>{{Cite Web|url= http://www.youtube.com/watch?v= IkVt1s_ZFbw&t =01m02s |title= Wind Waker HD E3 2013 Trailer }}</ref> The remaster also features bloom lighting to give a sense of "surreal realism".<ref>{{Cite Web|url = http://www.youtube.com/watch?v= DZRTakeKTbw&t =01m04s |title=Wii U Developer Direct - The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD @E3 2013}}</ref> In low-light areas, the cel-shading gives way to a softer, more realistic shading system. The lighting from torches and other point light sources is now much softer and more realistic, and objects now subtly reflect the color of nearby light sources. Other shaders are used to increase the expressivity of characters. The textures used are actually the original high-resolution ones created during development of the original ''The Wind Waker'', which its release used scaled-down versions of the textures to match the GameCube's hardware capabilities due to limitations. 2D elements such as icons and the HUD are completely redrawn to match the style of the game's promotional artwork.<br><br>Heat wave effects in [[Dragon Roost Cavern]] now only appears when the magma fountains are spouting. When [[Link]] uses a [[Warp Pot]], the transition effect is a shrinking/growing circle instead of the fade-out in the original game.<br><br>The shallow water in the boss room for [[Puppet Ganon]], which in the original game did not reflect Link and some other objects, now reflects everything within the large room.<br><br>Though all other graphical elements were updated for high-definition display, the main 3D models and character animations of the original game were left untouched.

When Link obtains an important item (such as the shield or the sword) on the GameCube, the text box displays an image of the item itself. The HD version removes the image entirely and changes the text box's color from purple to blue.

=== Audio ===
Music in {{TWWHD|-}} uses higher-quality instrument samples than the original. The updated instruments are most noticeable in fanfares and some boss themes. Some music includes new instrumental tracks, creating a fuller sound. However, most sound effects are unchanged from the originals.<br><br>While the original game used Dolby (Surround) Pro Logic II, which encodes surround sound using analog stereo audio, the remaster uses true digital surround sound encoded for and sent through HDMI.

=== Gameplay Elements ===
[[File:TWWHD Web 2.jpg|thumb|left|The Swift Sail increases sailing speed, a major improvement over the original version|266x266px]]

While the main storyline is virtually unchanged, the gameplay in {{TWWHD|-}} has been tuned up from the original to enhance the player experience. The infamous [[Triforce Shard]] Quest has been shortened; five of the eight shards are now found directly without having to go through the process of decrypting a [[Triforce Chart]] by [[Tingle]], therefore requiring less [[Rupee|Rupees]] to complete. Certain item usage cutscenes, such as the ones that play upon using the [[Grappling Hook]] to swing and salvage, have also been shortened.<br><br>Some items, such as the [[Picto Box]], have new and improved functions. The Picto Box can now store 12 pictographs as opposed to 3.<ref name= "WWHDChangeList">{{Cite Web|published = 2013-10-06 |retrieved= 2015-05-16 |url= http://www.zeldauniverse.net/2013/10/06/the-wind-waker-hd-changes-list/ |title = The Wind Waker HD changes list |site= Zelda Universe}}</ref> Link is now able to take self-portraits while making any of nine facial expressions.<ref name =WWHDChangeList/> Upon activating the [[Nintendo Gallery]] side quest, a mark appears in the upper left corner of pictographs that are eligible for figurine-making. In addition, [[Carlov]] can now sculpt up to 12 figurines per day, as opposed to only one. The gallery can thus be completed much more rapidly and efficiently. All pictographs can be sent to other players in the [[Tingle Bottle]]. The receiving players can add the pictographs to their own Picto Boxes and use them to complete their Nintendo Gallery. Additionally, he no longer needs a [[Forest Firefly]] for the Deluxe Pictobox.<br><br>Sailing has also been greatly improved with an upgrade to the [[sail]], called the [[Swift Sail]]. The Swift Sail enables the [[King of Red Lions]] to sail faster by pressing the A button. In addition, it automatically changes the direction of the wind to the boat's heading (therefore making playthroughs significantly faster compared to the original version). This replaces the function of the A button in the original game, which was used to stop the boat from sailing. The changed functions came about as a result of technical limitations in the original; the Nintendo GameCube version fully loaded only a single "chunk" of ocean as Link traveled, requiring the slower sailing speed to mask loading times,<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://youtu.be/MbSwqFIZRhw?t= 170 |title =Developer Direct - The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD @E3 2013 (Wii U)}}</ref> while the more advanced Wii U is able to load the entire Great Sea at once. This does, however, result in occasional framerate issues, particularly during rainfall. In fact, on Gamecube, if you travel across the sea at high speeds with Superswim, you can observe a low-poly version of the islands without textures, which load shortly after Link arrives. In the HD version, this is never possible to observe due to the greater amount of rendering and resolution.

The [[Tingle Bottle]], which allowed players to randomly send messages to other players, replaces the [[Tingle Tuner]] in the original game. The Tingle Bottle was used to send messages in bottles through Miiverse, which would subsequently appear on the shores of other players' islands (this no longer functions as Nintendo Network was shut down in 2017).<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Players will also be able to post messages to Miiverse, which exist within the game world as notes in a bottle. Notes will then wash ashore in other players' games. |author = Andrew Goldfarb |published= 2013-06-11 |retrieved= 2013-06-11 |url = http://www.ign.com/articles/2013/06/11/e3-2013-zelda-wind-waker-hd-release-window-announced/|title= E3 2013: Zelda Wind Waker HD Release Window Announced|site= IGN}}</ref> The [[Tingle Statue]]s, previously only accessible via the Tingle Tuner's [[Tingle Bomb]]s, can now be obtained with regular Bombs. [[Knuckle]] now appears on [[Tingle Island]] after all five statues have been found. Interestingly, Tingle urges Link to find the treasure in the cave where he was imprisoned, unlike in the Gamecube version, where he only spoke of his island.<br><br>Link's actions have been altered slightly. He is now capable of changing direction while swinging on a rope, without having to stop first. He can also freely move while in first-person view while not using an item or aiming the Grappling Hook, [[Boomerang]], [[Bow|Hero's Bow]], and [[Hookshot]]. However, the shield button is no longer the "grab" button for draggable objects, and instead both the "grab" and "climb" commands are part of the primary action button, making block controls act like older games.<br><br>As ''The Wind Waker'' is known for its low difficulty, the remaster includes a tougher [[Hero Mode]], but unlike other incarnations it has no unlocking requirements, and can be toggled on or off when starting a new game or when a save game is resumed.<ref name= WWHDChangeList/> [[Recovery Heart|Recovery Hearts]] do not appear in Hero Mode, forcing Link to use [[Potion|Potions]], [[Fairy|Fairies]], or [[Elixir Soup]] to regain health.<ref name =WWHDChangeList/> In addition, enemies deal double the amount of damage as in normal gameplay, similar to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]''<nowiki/>'s ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Master Quest|Master Quest]]''.<ref>{{Cite Web|url= http://www.youtube.com/watch?v= NxLJCC1r1VI&t =17s |title=The Legend of Zelda The Wind Waker HD Hero Mode Trailer}}</ref><br><br>The game supports GamePad-only play, allowing for play without the need of a television, as well as control with the Wii U Pro Controller, for those who prefer a traditional control scheme. During use of the Pro Controller, the GamePad can still be used as a map.

Other altered gameplay elements include:<ref name=WWHDChangeList/>
* The starting [[Wallet]] holds 500 [[Rupee]]s instead of 200. As a result, obtaining a wallet upgrade is no longer mandatory to pay Tingle's fees and thus complete the game.
* There are 5 additional [[Treasure Chart]]s that replace the removed [[Triforce Chart]]s.
* The [[Hero's Charm]] is no longer obtained from [[Mrs. Marie]]. It is found at the bottom of the [[Savage Labyrinth]]. The [[Piece of Heart]] that was there in the original game was moved to an underwater chest, found via the new Treasure Chart #46.
* Link does not get knocked out of the King of Red Lions when he takes damage at sea (aside from some exceptions, such as explosive barrels).
* Enemies have less invincibility after being damaged, making it possible for certain [[sword]] swings to hit twice.
* Conducting with the [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|Wind Waker]] does not begin until a direction is input.
* After being learned the first time, the preanimated version of Wind Waker melodies is only cued the first time the song is played since loading the game, with the main exception being during the critically important parts (using the [[Wind's Requiem]] on the symbols to make a [[Treasure Chest]] appear, or removing the stone tablets in the [[Earth Temple]] and [[Wind Temple]] by conducting [[Earth God's Lyric]] or [[Wind God's Aria]] when [[Medli]] or [[Makar]] are with [[Link]]).
* While gliding, [[Deku Leaf]] shows a [[Tingle Tuner]]-like glowing symbol below Link's feet where he will land.
* When using the [[Grappling Hook]], you can now alter grapple swing direction while swinging.
* While using the cannon, the projectile's trajectory is displayed on the screen, thereby making aiming the cannon much easier.
* Once obtained the first time, a [[Fishman]]'s advice can be read from the [[Sea Chart]].
* The Fishmen no longer change their location after feeding them [[All-Purpose Bait|bait]].
* Pots which previously contained 3 of an item now only contain 2.
* Items dropped by enemies defeated at sea are automatically drawn towards Link.
* [[Forest Water]] lasts for 30 minutes instead of 20.
* The [[Figurines in The Wind Waker|figurine]] of [[Knuckle]] is required to complete the [[Nintendo Gallery]].
* When a [[Big Octo]]'s eye is destroyed, it glows blue instead of turning black.
* [[Lenzo]]'s [[Legendary Pictograph]]s cost 150 [[Rupee]]s instead of 50.
* The chest that [[Treasure Chart]] 18 leads to contains 50 Rupees instead of one.
* The [[Forest Firefly]] is no longer needed to get the [[Deluxe Picto Box]] from Lenzo. He gives Link a [[Joy Pendant]] for bringing him one.
* [[Jump Attack]]ing on top of the chest in the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship|link}} no longer freezes the game.
* There is now a northwest wind in [[Hyrule]], as opposed to no wind.
* The [[Boomerang]] no longer hits [[Kalle Demos]] through the bulb when it comes back to Link.
* [[Molgera]]'s tongue no longer extends and his tongue cannot be latched onto while he is flying towards Link.
* The [[Octorok]] figurine no longer states that Octoroks are the "winners of the perfect attendance award", since they were absent from {{TP}}.
* The [[Goddess Pearls]] symbols now are always visible regardless of which direction they are facing.
* Medli and Makar will follow Link through doors in the Earth and Wind Temples, instead of having to be carried through them.
* A circle of light no longer appears around Link when he enters a dark area.
* All-Purpose Bait now costs 30 Rupees when purchased from [[Beedle]], as opposed to 10 Rupees.
* Seagulls can no longer fly through the small tree saplings that are found throughout many different islands.
* After clearing the [[Forbidden Woods]], the [[Octorok]]s near the entrance to the [[Forest Haven]] will disappear.
* Certain character animations that were repeated in the original game are now only used once.
* In the HD version, Link will always jump when performing a successful roll clip. In the original version, he doesn't, so he'll have to use the Deku Leaf for most jumps.
* If Link falls and uses the Wind Waker frames before falling, the screen of the baton no longer remains in the air; instead, it is canceled. This was done to avoid canceling the animation in the final frames and to patch the Storage Glitch.
* In Wind Waker HD, Link can no longer use items inside houses by pressing a button and pausing simultaneously to change the item (for example, using bombs indoors). Therefore, the Easter egg of the piglets behind the bomb shop on Windfall Island remains exclusive to the GameCube version.
* It is no longer possible to restart the game with a button combination in Windwaker HD, unlike on Gamecube.
* On GameCube, it's possible to buy the Sail without rescuing Tingle from his prison. On Wii U, Zunari asks Link to rescue him and then offers him the Sail.
** In fact, on Gamecube, Zunari always says the Sail costs 80 rupees. On Wii U, after rescuing Tingle, he mentions that he was going to charge Link 200 rupees but lets him have it for 80 rupees, 60% less.

=== Interface Changes ===
Because {{TWWHD|-}} was developed for control with the Wii U GamePad, additional control methods have been added. The [[Inventory]] has been made accessible through the GamePad's touchscreen, with no need to pause the game. It is now possible to slide an item icon on the GamePad to one of the three slot icons at the top of the screen, allowing players to switch items quickly. The older style of item selection by pausing and using the d-pad and buttons must be used when playing the game with the Wii U Pro Controller, and may also still be used with the Wii U GamePad.<br><br>Some items, such as the [[Wind Waker (Item)|Wind Waker]], are permanently mapped to certain buttons as opposed to being equipped to the three item slots. This allows players to quickly and conveniently access these items, as they are key to playing the game.<br><br>First-person aiming of items is achieved through either the right analog stick or the GamePad's gyroscope, though there is an option to disable the gyroscope. Link can also move in first-person mode, though item usage is limited to ranged weapons such as the [[Hero's Bow]].<ref name=WWHDChangeList/>

In the original GameCube version, a map is always visible in the bottom right corner, similar to Ocarina of Time. However, in the HD version, the minimap was removed, and only a reduced version of the islands is visible on the nautical chart or the dungeon map when paused. Interestingly, Twilight Princess HD retained the minimap from the GameCube and Wii versions, albeit in a smaller size.

In the HD version, Orca's teachings were changed to accommodate the new controls. For example, one lesson focuses on playing without a television (something impossible on the GameCube) thanks to the GamePad, in addition to updated controls for the Wii U version.

In HD, menu items are displayed in diamond shapes, and dungeon items are displayed in a hexagon that can hold all 9 items. On Gamecube, they were distributed across 1x3 and 1x4 rectangles. Due to this change, all items obtained in dungeons or that are important for progressing (such as the Deku Leaf, the Magic Aura, and the Iron Boots) are grouped together in the same sub-section. On GameCube, they were scattered.

In Wind Waker HD you can hide the controls from view, creating a more immersive experience. This isn't possible in the original version.

=== Other Changes ===
Some cutscenes where Link makes a facial expression silently have voices added to match the expressions. The ending cutscene and staff credits, which were prerendered in the original game, are now fully real-time cutscenes in the remaster. However, the smooth transition effects from the original cutscenes (the first part of the ending cutscene transitions with a fade effect to the credits, which then fade to the second part of the cutscene) have been replaced by fade-to-white effects, presumably due to the difficulty of implementing the original cutscenes' transitions in real-time rendering.<br><br>The intro cutscene, which plays upon the start of a new file, is skippable, but no other cutscenes are.

In the Spanish version (and presumably others that have accents on the words), words like "Este" are no longer accented, following the current grammar rule.

When Link can perform a surprise attack (ZL + A), the action is indicated at the bottom of the screen. On GameCube, this could only be seen by looking the A button on the HUD.

== Development ==
[[File:TWW HD 7.jpg|left|thumb|251x251px|Tetra and Link in a promotional image of the game, showcasing the improved graphics]]
The game was officially announced on January 23, 2013, in which only a couple of screenshots featuring [[Windfall Island]], [[Link]], [[Tetra]], and [[Sue-Belle]] were shown. The decisions to remaster the game came after the team decided to attempt to upscale the game's graphics to the Wii U's HD capabilities, after [[Zelda HD Experience|doing the same to the graphics of {{TP|-}}]] in E3 2011. The game was also designed to decrease the gap in time between the release for ''Skyward Sword'' and {{BotW}} (which were released in 2011 and 2017, respectively).<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= After the Zelda team showed the Wii U Zelda demo that we called the Zelda HD Experience at E3 2011, we continued to experiment with converting past Zelda styles into HD as we continued to consider graphical styles for the new game. |name =Eiji Aonuma |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v =fIyU8-XTTLc&t= 30m26s |title =Wii U Direct }}</ref>

Nintendo showed the first trailer for the game during its E3 Direct livestream on June 11, 2013, revealing the game in motion as well as some of the new features of the game.

=== Fan Reception ===
Like the original version, ''The Wind Waker HD'' is critically acclaimed among the ''Zelda'' fandom. It is often considered to be the definitive version due to the improvements of gameplay.

== Trivia ==

* Unlike most other remakes of ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' games, this enhanced release of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'' for the [[Wii U]] has overshadowed the [[Nintendo GameCube]] original significantly among the fanbase in recent years, to the point many players think of the HD remaster whenever they hear "''The Wind Waker''". This is especially true on social media where videos show this version much more often than the original. When the [[wikipedia:Nintendo_Switch_2|Nintendo Switch 2]] was announced to have the initial version for its Nintendo Classics service rather than ''The Wind Waker HD'', it was even met with criticism. The primary reason is due to the addition of the [[Sail|Swift Sail]] and shortening the [[Triforce Shard]] quest, where in the original ''The Wind Waker'' is known to be rather tedious among some fans.
** An often overlooked advantage the initial ''The Wind Waker'' has over its HD counterpart in original hardware is cheating devices such as Action Replay can be used to make [[Link]] easily bypass almost all major obstacles and therefore complete the game quickly.

== Glitches ==
These glitches are exclusive to the HD version; they are not found in the GameCube version, or their presence is unknown. They are new due to the new features in the Wii U version.

=== Item Slide ===

* '''Effect:''' Going at high speeds and even swimming very fast
* '''Prerequisites:''' Bow, Grappling Hook, Hookshot or Boomerang
* '''How To:''' With one of these four items, take one out and, while aiming, move and pause the game (without stopping moving). While paused, move the stick in the opposite direction Link was moving and direct it to the dead spot (known as ESS Position in speedruns). Unpause, and with luck, you'll see Link slide at high speeds until he reaches a wall. This is due to the addition in the HD version of the ability to move while aiming. With this glitch, you can swim at high speeds (if you manage to direct Link into the water if there's no ledge to jump from), pass through walls (if the glitch is performed on a slope), and skip many sections of the game.

=== Pause Storage Glitch ===

* '''Effect:''' Pause in cutscenes and the ability to skip them.
* '''Prerequisites:''' Wii U Pro Controller and Wii U Gamepad
* '''How To:''' If the game is paused at the same time as entering a loading screen or cutscene, the game will glitch. It is possible to switch to playing with the Gamepad and enable saving and quitting during cutscenes.

=== Zelda's Text Storage ===

* '''Effect:''' Play with a Zelda text box on screen
* '''Prerequisites:''' Reaching the final battle, Wind Waker
* '''How To:'''  A few seconds after the final battle against Ganon begins, Zelda will speak to Link in a text box that appears automatically. Link must use the Wind Waker on the same frame the text appears. If done correctly, Link will use the Wind Waker while the text box remains visible. Canceling the text box before releasing the Wind Waker and quickly jumping and using the Deku Leaf will cause the save effect to continue. Using the Wind Waker again will allow Link to move with the text box repeated, and if the Wind Waker is used again, the camera will position itself in front of Link, making it more difficult to move. This is very similar to the save error in the GameCube version of The Wind Waker.

=== Ending Glitch ===

* '''Effect:''' Make the game impossible to finish
* '''Prerequisites:''' Reach Ganon, have a heart (optional before the fight, it's recommended to have several and let Ganon take them away)
* '''How To:'''  When Link and Zelda combine their powers to defeat Ganon (when Zelda asks Link to use his shield to reflect the Light Arrows), have Zelda and Link deflect the arrow back at Ganon and deliver the final blow at the exact moment he takes your last heart. After Link stabs Ganondorf with his sword, he will die. Save the game and defeat Ganon this time. Instead of the final cutscene, Link will appear on the battlefield but will be able to move. Zelda and Ganondorf will not move (Zelda only rotates her eyes, and Ganon strikes his T-pose as his swords disappear and reappear).
