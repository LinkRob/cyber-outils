{{Term/Store|Chef Bear||Series, LA, LADX}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 233"/>

==Biography==
Chef Bear resides in a house located in the southeastern corner of [[Animal Village]]. She can be found cooking and talking about opening a branch in [[Mabe Village]].<ref>{{Cite|My ultimate plan is to open a branch in Mabe Village!|Chef Bear|LADX}}</ref> She is very amiable to [[Link]] and offers him advice on how he can get past the [[Walrus]] that blocks [[Yarna Desert]].<ref>{{Cite|Hi ho! Yeah, I know, that tub of goo is asleep right in the way to Yarna Desert! Once he's asleep he won't budge for a loooooonng time. But hey! Take Little Marin and wake him up with her song! That slob would wake up with a jump if he heard her sing, for sure! Heh heh heh!|Chef Bear|LADX}}</ref> She is very fond of [[Marin]], and like other residents she refers to her as "Little Marin".<ref>{{Cite|HI HO! Little Marin! Welcome! ... ... ... ... Oh, shucks! You are here too... Sorry...|Chef Bear|LADX}}</ref>

She is also essential for completing the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}},<ref name="Trading Sequence"/> as she has run out of ingredients.<ref>{{Cite|Rik'm rak'm! I ran out of ingredients! If I had honey, I could make this fit for a king!|Chef Bear|LADX}}</ref> She asks [[Link]] to give her the [[Honeycomb]] he obtained from [[Tarin]].<ref>{{Cite|Hi ho! Hey you! Is that possibly a {{Icon|LADX Honeycomb}} you have? I just ran out! Will you swap it for a pineapple?|Chef Bear|LADX}}</ref> In exchange, she gives Link a [[Pineapple]]. She believes Honeycombs to be a rare delicacy.<ref>{{Cite|That's a crying shame, but I realize those are a rare delicacy!|Chef Bear|LADX}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* Chef Bear is one of the few residents of [[Animal Village]] that does not leave her home when [[Marin]] visits.
