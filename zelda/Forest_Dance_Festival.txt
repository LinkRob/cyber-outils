{{Term/Store|Forest Dance Festival||HWAoC}} is a Level 42 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Forest Dance Festival|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Forest Dance Festival}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} besides {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}} and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}} announces the start of the dance competition and all of the competitors (except for the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} and himself) are revealed. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s first objective is to defeat two dancers, which are a {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}}. When both are defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Goron Captain|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Rito Captain|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Captain|link}} spawn, which also must be defeated. When all of them are KO'd, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}} spawns. After {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} is defeated, {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} defeats {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}}.
