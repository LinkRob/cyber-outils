==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hyrule Herb}}
{{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb}} can be found growing in the grasslands of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and the [[Akkala Highlands]]. When eaten raw, they will heal {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} of one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}.
