{{Term/Store|Lord Jabu-Jabu||Series, OoT, OoT3D, OoA}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238{{Exp Game|OoT}} & 254{{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Lord Jabu-Jabu is a large whale-like creature that serves as the guardian of the [[Zora]].

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, he resides upon an altar in [[Zora's Fountain]],<ref>{{Cite|Of course, you'll go find Ruto. You can pass through here to the altar of Lord Jabu-Jabu.|King Zora|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Zora's Fountain is the source of the river. Lord Jabu-Jabu lives in the fountain.|Zora|OoT}}</ref> a sacred lake behind [[Zora's Domain]], where he is worshiped by the [[Zora]]s and acts as a guardian deity similar to the [[Great Deku Tree]].<ref name="Deity"/> The royal family are tasked with Jabu-Jabu's care.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The royal family are tasked with Jabu-Jabu's care.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=48}}</ref> According to [[Legend of Zora|Zora legend]], offering a fish to Jabu-Jabu is supposed to bring the bearer happiness.<ref>{{Cite|According to the "Legend of Zora," the act of offering a fish to Lord Jabu-Jabu will make you happy.|Zora|OoT}}</ref> Lord Jabu-Jabu does not appear in the adult timeline.

Lord Jabu-Jabu is apparently very benevolent,<ref>{{Cite|She's inside Lord Jabu-Jabu? That's not possible! Our guardian god, Lord Jabu-Jabu, would never eat my dear Princess Ruto!|King Zora|OoT}}</ref> and the Zora encourage all visitors to see him.<ref>{{Cite|Have you seen Lord Jabu-Jabu? Oh, that's not good. Everybody who comes around here should see Lord Jabu-Jabu at least once! Zora's Fountain is just beyond King Zora's throne. That is where Lord Jabu-Jabu swims.|Zora|OoT}}</ref> After [[Ganondorf]] encountered him, however, Lord Jabu-Jabu began displaying outward symptoms of the curse cast on him; as [[King Zora]] cites Ganondorf's interference as a reason the whale may have swallowed Ruto, these possibly include changes in his behavior.<ref>{{Cite|But since that stranger, Ganondorf, came here, Lord Jabu-Jabu has been a little green around the gills...|King Zora|OoT}}</ref>

Jabu-Jabu is very fond of [[Fish]], and Young [[Link]] must feed him one in order to gain entrance to his [[Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly|Belly]] to kill the [[Barinade|parasite]] which ails him and save [[Princess Ruto]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, Jabu-Jabu lives in [[Jabu-Jabu's Shrine]], once again near the Zoras in [[Zora Village]], though the exact relationship between this incarnation and the one that appears in {{OoT|-}} is unknown. He appears in both the past and present ages of [[Labrynna]], but in the former, he is too small to enter.<ref>{{Cite|You wish to enter Jabu-Jabu? I wish I could tell you to do as you please, but Jabu-Jabu is still young. He's too small to go inside him.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> As part of his quest, Link must venture inside [[Jabu-Jabu's Belly]] in the present to obtain the Rolling Sea, the seventh [[Essences of Time|Essence of Time]]. However, King Zora will initially prevent the young hero from visiting the deity,<ref>{{Cite|My permission is needed to enter Jabu-Jabu, but I can't permit just anyone to see our guardian.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> and in order to gain his permission, Link must first find a way to save Jabu-Jabu's life by cleansing the polluted [[Zora Seas]].<ref>{{Cite|The filthy seawater has left Jabu-Jabu near death. If you say you must, then find a way to get rid of this filthy seawater and save Jabu-Jabu.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> To do this, he must rescue the [[Fairy Queen]], allowing her to purify the filthy seawater.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, young one! Now I can cleanse the seas of evil!|Fairy Queen|OoA}}</ref>

==={{EoW|-}}===
In {{EoW}}, Lord Jabu-Jabu resides in Jabul Waters inside of his den.<ref name=":0">"He's been running wild all over the place since this rift spread in his den" - Unknown River Zora ({{EoW|-}})</ref> When [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] starts the Zora questline, she learns that he has been running wild throughout Jabul Waters ever since a rift appeared in his den.<ref name=":0" /> Zelda also learns that the chiefs of both the [[River Zora|River Clan]] and Sea Clans of the [[Zora]] must play music together to open up Jabu-Jabu's den.<ref>"To open the door, the chiefs of the river clan and the sea clan must play music here together." - Unknown River Zora ({{EoW|-}})</ref> While Dradd and Kushara argue, and try to prove who plays better music, Lord Jabu-Jabu appears at Crossflows Plaza. He is apparently angered by their playing, and sucks up both Rogma and Inawa into his mouth, before swimming away.<ref>"Gulped Down! The both of them!" - Dradd ({{EoW|-}})</ref> The only way to resolve the Zora's conflict so they can play together, and rescue both Inawa and Rogma, is to mend the various [[Rift|Rifts]] across Jabul Waters. Once they are completed, Zelda will be directed to Jabu-Jabu's den, where Kushara and Dradd play to open the door forward. Once you reach where Jabu-Jabu is located, [[Tri]] realizes that the Jabu-Jabu causing a rampage is an imposter [[Echo]].<ref>"The way that big fish (Jabu-Jabu) looks, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]... It's got to be another imposter from a rift!" -[[Tri]] ({{EoW|-}})</ref> Afterwards, you will commence a short boss battle against Echo Jabu-Jabu, where you must throw a [[Bombfish]] into his mouth to stun him, then attack him while he is stunned. Once completed, the echo of Jabu-Jabu Will disappear, releasing Rogma and Inawa from its belly. The path forward to the rift is cleared allowing Zelda access to the [[Jabul Ruins]] dungeon. 

Once the dungeon is complete, and the rift restored, the real Jabu-Jabu will return to Crossflows Plaza, where both Zora chiefs will play their song as Jabu-Jabu moves along to the music.

==Related Appearances==
Jabu-Jabu has been compared to the [[Wind Fish]] from {{LA}}, as both are god-like whales or whale-like creatures. He also shares strong similarities with [[Jabun]] in {{TWW}}; not only are they both large round fish, but Jabun's theme music is a remix of the dungeon theme for Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly, and according to {{HH}} Jabun is implied to be Jabu-Jabu's descendant. Both of these deity-like whales also resemble the [[Oshus|Ocean King]] from {{PH}} on a lesser scale.

In the [[Lakebed Temple]] of {{TP}}, sculptures that resemble Jabu-Jabu can be seen. Some of the Zora guards also wear masks that resemble Jabu-Jabu as he appeared in {{OoT|-}}.

In {{MM3D}}, Link can catch a fish called [[Lord Chapu-Chapu]] in the [[Swamp Fishing Hole]]. It resembles a smaller version of Lord Jabu-Jabu. The owner of the Swamp Fishing Hole calls it "the king o' the swamp."<ref>{{Cite|Jumpin' jellyfish! I don't believe it! That there fishy is the one they call the king o' the swamp! Good work, pal! Congrats and all that!|Fisherman|MM3D}}</ref>

The large fish statue at Zora's Domain in {{BotW}} represents Lord Jabu-Jabu.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 274}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*An emblem of the Zora's Sapphire can be seen across Jabu-Jabu's crown.
*"Jabu Jabu" is the Japanese onomatopoeia for splashing water.
*In {{BotW}}, there is a ridge southeast of [[East Reservoir Lake]] named Ja'Abu Ridge, referencing Jabu-Jabu.
