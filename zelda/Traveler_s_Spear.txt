{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Spear}} are {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}} utilized by travelers to defend themselves from wild {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are noted to be easy to hold and simple to use.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}.

When scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Spear}}:
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|LA|Link|link}} ({{LA}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Spear}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, though they will not burn when exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. Finally, they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Spear}} belong to the equipment archetype known as {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear|link}}, which is utilized for self-defense by travelers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>
