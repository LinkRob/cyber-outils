''{{Term/Store|Nintendo Official Guidebook—The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past Vol. 2||Series, ALttP}}'', along with '''[[Nintendo Official Guidebook—The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past Vol. 1|Vol. 1]]''', is Nintendo's guidebook for the Japanese version of {{ALttP}}. It was published under Shogakukan's ''Wonder Life Special'' label. There are two volumes in total. 

==Features==
The walkthrough covers from Skull Woods to the end of the game. The guidebook is filled with both illustrations and real-life photoes.
