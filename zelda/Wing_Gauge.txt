The {{Term/Store|Wing Gauge||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a mechanic in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= |game= SS |page= 9}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|SSHD|Wing Gauge}} shows how many times the {{Term|SSHD|Crimson Loftwing|link}} can boost through the air, or use a {{Term|SSHD|Spiral Charge|link}}, before it must rest. There are three wing icons that each represent a boost or {{Term|SSHD|Spiral Charge}}. One is used up each time a boost is used, and they regenerate over time.
