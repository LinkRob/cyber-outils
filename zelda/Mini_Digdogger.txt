{{Term/Store|Mini Digdogger|Mini Digdoggers|Series, OoS|plural}} are {{Plural|OoS|Enemy|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''''Mini Digdogger'''''|book= E |publisher= |page= 192}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
During the {{Term|OoS|Boss|link}} fight in {{Term|OoS|Unicorn's Cave|link}}, by using the {{Term|OoS|Magnetic Gloves|link}} at north polarity, {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} can cause the spiked ball that lays in the boss area to smash into {{Term|OoS|Digdogger|link}}. After a few smashes from the spiked ball, the {{Term|OoS|Digdogger}} will split into {{Plural|OoS|Mini Digdogger}}. {{Term|OoS|Link}} must then hit each of them with the spiked ball or his {{Term|OoS|Sword|link}} to defeat them. If he takes long enough, the {{Plural|OoS|Mini Digdogger}} will merge again into the {{Term|OoS|Digdogger}}.
