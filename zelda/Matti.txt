{{Term/Store|Matti||Series, ALBW}} is the name of one of the 20 possible {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} that can appear in {{ALBW}} without being met by {{Term|Series|StreetPass|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's Matti!<br/>This {{Color|ALBW Blue|Shadow Link}} has come via {{Color|ALBW Red|StreetPass}} and awaits battle!|Shadow Link|ALBW}}</ref>{{Note|All Shadow Links claim to have come via StreetPass, even when they are pre-generated.}}

==Overview==
Once {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} has spoken to {{Term|ALBW|Gramps|link}} and enabled {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link Battle|link}}, a new signpost signifying the arrival of a {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} will appear at the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Meadow|link}} after every five hours of gameplay, regardless of whether or not the player has received a {{Term|Series|StreetPass|link}} tag. {{Term|ALBW|Matti}} is one of the pre-generated {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link}} who can be met by this method, and usually appears in the {{Term|ALBW|Desert of Mystery|link}}, unless another {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}} already awaits there.

{{Term|ALBW|Matti}} wears a {{Term|ALBW|Green Tunic|link}} and wields the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword|link}} and a {{Term|ALBW|Shield|link}}. He has a {{Term|ALBW|Life Gauge|link}} of ten {{Plural|ALBW|Heart}}, has 15 Medals won, and possesses the {{Term|ALBW|Pegasus Boots|link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Matti}} carries a {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|link}} and a {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod|link}} into battle, and has an overall bounty of 350 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.
