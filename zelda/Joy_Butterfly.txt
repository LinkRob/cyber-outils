==Location and Uses==
{{Figurine|TMC|Joy Butterfly}} 
{{Plural|TMC|Joy Butterfly}} appear after {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Farore (Oracle)|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Nayru (Oracle)|link}}, or {{Term|TMC|Din (Oracle)|link}}. They can be found in {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|link}}. Catching a {{Term|TMC|Joy Butterfly}} will increase {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}'s speed while digging,<ref>{{Cite|You caught a Joy Butterfly!
