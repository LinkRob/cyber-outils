The '''Knights''' are the mini-bosses of the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Strength|link}} in {{ZA}}. They are the '''Red Knight''', the '''Blue Knight''', and the '''Green Knight'''. They are {{Term|ZA|Ursore|link}}'s knights who challenge {{Term|ZA|Zelda|link}} to battles of strength.<ref>{{Cite|Roar! Who has harmed my Red Knight? I will not forget! Roar!|Ursore|ZA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Something does put my Blue Knight to shame and defeated my Green Knight! This will be punished! Roar!|Ursore|ZA}}</ref> {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda|link}} must purchase the {{Term|ZA|Items in Zelda's Adventure#Ticket|display=Ticket|link}} prior to facing them in order to challenge them. They appear to be related.<ref>{{Cite|One as small as thee put aflight my brother!? This I must test!|Green Knight|ZA}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|ZA|Zelda}} first faces the {{Term|ZA|Red Knight}} in the Jousting Field.<ref>{{Cite|Hello there, little one! Step up and buy a ticket to the jousting field where the bravest and strongest knights in Tolemac test their skills! Who knows, beautiful, you may get a chance yourself!|Ticket Seller|ZA}}</ref> He will only appear if {{Term|ZA|Zelda}} bought the {{Term|ZA|Items in Zelda's Adventure|section=Red Bow|display=Red Bow|link}} from a merchant earlier in the Shrine, which once belonged to the {{Term|ZA|Red Knight}} who will then accuse {{Term|ZA|Zelda}} of stealing it.<ref>{{Cite|Hold tight, little enemy! You have something stolen from me and I am strong!|Red Knight|ZA}}</ref> The Red Knight can only be defeated with the Joust {{Term|ZA|Spells of Zelda's Adventure|display=Spell|link}}, and requires four hits of the Spell to defeat.

The Blue and Green Knights are later encountered in the Field of the Broadsword.<ref>{{Cite|What have we here? A princess, wandering the field of honor on her own. Come over here, and I'll sell you a ticket to the Field of the Broadsword. Watch yourself inside; the swords are heavier than you are.|Ticket Seller|ZA}}</ref> {{Term|ZA|Zelda}} faces them individually in separate halls. They are fought much the same way, except they can only be defeated with the Broadsword Spell. They both take four hits of the Spell to defeat.

{{Term|ZA|Zelda}} must defeat all three to face {{Term|ZA|Ursore}}.
