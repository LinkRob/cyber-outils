{{Term/Store|Hoz||Series, BotW}} is a recurring character in the {{TLoZ|Series}}. He first appears in {{BotW}} and returns in {{TotK}}.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW|-}}, Hoz is a soldier who works to fend off monsters from [[East Akkala Stable]] in the [[Akkala Highlands]].<ref>{{Cite|Hello! I've been on patrol duty lately. The name's Hoz! Geez, it's gotten dangerous around here lately!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> He carries a [[Soldier's Spear]] and wears a [[Soldier's Helm]]. He patrols the [[Stable]] throughout the day and night.<ref>{{Cite|No issues to report!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> If it begins to rain, he will flee inside.<ref>{{Cite|Raining already, eh...|Hoz|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Raining into the night, it seems...|Hoz|BotW}}</ref>

If [[Link]] agrees that it has become dangerous in the area, Hoz shares a rumor he has heard of a suspicious individual known as [[Kilton]].<ref>{{Cite|[It has.] Yes! Recently I've been hearing about a suspicious guy named {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}...|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> The rumors say that he runs a shop called [[Fang and Bone]], which he operates during the night.<ref>{{Cite|He runs a shop called {{Color|BotW Blue|Fang and Bone}}. They say he goes around pushing some kind of cart {{Color|BotW Red|night after night}}.|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> Kilton's behavior has reportedly been making people uncomfortable.<ref>{{Cite|He's been making people around here uncomfortable with his suspicious and otherworldly activity...|Hoz|BotW}}</ref>

Hoz asks Link if he knows Kilton.<ref>{{Cite|Do you know...{{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}?|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> If Link replies that he does not, Hoz will be disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|[No, I don't.] I see...|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if Link says that he does, Hoz is shocked that he is aware of him.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes, I do.] Whaaa? You've heard something?!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> Despite Link's answer, Hoz asks him for a detailed description of any suspicious people that he encounters, especially if he meets Kilton.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if you see any suspicious people, please tell me {{Color|BotW Red|what they look like in detail}}! Really! If you see this {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}} character, please tell me all the {{Color|BotW Red|juicy details}}. I'm counting on you!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> This begins the [[Side Quest]] "[[A Shady Customer]]."

Once Link has taken a [[Picture]] of Kilton, he can return to the East Akkala Stable to show it to Hoz.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? You've got information about {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}? Allow me to take a look...|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> Though he thinks the Picture is strange,<ref>{{Cite|Heheheh. I see... How strange.|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> Hoz believes it will help him track Kilton down.<ref>{{Cite|Seriously, I can't thank you enough! This will surely help me hunt him down. Really, I must thank you somehow!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gives Link a [[Silver Rupee]]. He bids Link farewell so that he can return to his mission.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I must return to my mission now. Farewell!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref>

After completing the Side Quest, Link can continue to speak to Kilton. Now that he has seen Kilton, he wonders what he sells at the Fang and Bone.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}... I've finally seen you! I wonder what kind of stuff {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}'s {{Color|BotW Blue|Fang and Bone}} shop sells...|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> He believes it could be fangs, bones, or "evil things", but thinking about it creeps him out.<ref>{{Cite|Fangs? Bones? EVIL THINGS FOR EVIL? The more I think about it, the more it gives me the creeps!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref>

Hoz is one of several characters who will hit Link back if he attacks them with a [[Weapon]].<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing?!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Don't cross me!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You want to fight?!|Hoz|BotW}}</ref> These attacks deal no damage.

==={{TotK|-}}===
In {{TotK|-}}, he works as the captain of [[Monster Control Crew Squad 1]]. His bosses are head of the Monster Control Crew [[Gralens]] and strategist [[Atmus]]. He wears a Soldier's Helm and wields the flag of the [[Monster Control Crew]]. Riding his horse, he commands his squad. If Link helps him and his squad fight monsters in [[Hyrule Field]], he explains how having him will greatly improve morale. After securing victory in Hyrule Field, he thanks Link for helping and moves onto [[Fort Hateno]] in [[Necluda]]. After securing victory there, he again thanks Link for participating. After a [[Blood Moon|blood moon]], he goes back to Hyrule Field and then Fort Hateno to defeat the monsters again. Together with [[Toren]] of [[Squad 2]] and [[Flaxel]] of [[Squad 3]], they command small groups of militia troops of varying training and professionalism to defeat monsters and make [[Hyrule]] at least a bit safer.
