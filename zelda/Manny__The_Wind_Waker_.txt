{{Term/Store|Manny||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Manny}}
Manny is a freckled, blonde-haired boy about {{Term|TWW|Link}}'s height who is interested in the {{Term|TWW|Nintendo Gallery|link}}. He wears a blue shirt and can be seen standing outside the entrance to the gallery before {{Term|TWW|Link}} gets in, and loiters near {{Term|TWW|Carlov|link}}'s counter or looks at the figurines once inside. If {{Term|TWW|Link}} takes his picture, {{Term|TWW|Carlov}} will make a {{Term|TWW|Figurines in The Wind Waker|link|display=Figurine}} of him and say he is quite handsome.<ref>{{Cite|Wow... That golden, freshly brushed hair... That stylish, yet unassuming bod... ...Why, this is a mighty handsome man!|Carlov|TWW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The bag that Manny carries has a picture of an {{Term|TLoZ|Octorok}} from {{TLoZ}}.
*In ''The Wind Waker'', Manny's path in the Nintendo Gallery is unfinished in the Japanese and North American versions, and he will simply stop and stand in place once he reaches the end of his path. This was fixed in the European version, where the path was finished to create a closed loop. The bug returns in ''The Wind Waker HD'', which is based on the North American version.

==Nomenclature==
Manny's Japanese name, ''Mani A.'', is likely a joke about his maniacal obsession with {{Term|TWW|Figurines in The Wind Waker|display=Figurines}}.
