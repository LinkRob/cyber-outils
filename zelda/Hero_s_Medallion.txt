==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|CoH|Hero's Medallion}} is obtainable after completing the {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Arena|link}} by entering on the ground floor and speaking to the character on the right. The medallion grants immunity from {{Term|CoH|Poison|link}}, making it very useful in the fight against {{Term|CoH|Ganon|link}} and his {{Plural|CoH|Poison Wizzrobe|link}}.
