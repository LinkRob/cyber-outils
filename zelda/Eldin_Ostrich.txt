==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Eldin Ostrich}}
Eldin Ostriches are {{Plural|BotW|Bird|link}} that can be found in the [[Eldin (Region)|Eldin region]] of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, particularly the area surrounding {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This large bird makes its home in the volcanic region of Eldin.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They have heat resistant feathers that allow them to live comfortably in the region.<ref>{{Cite|Their heat-resistant feathers and skin are evolutionary traits produced by the harsh environment.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Though they're unable to fly, Eldin Ostriches have the ability to run fast.<ref>{{Cite|Their wings are too small to facilitate flight, but their long legs make them pretty speedy runners.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When killed, they drop either a [[Raw Bird Thigh]] or a [[Raw Whole Bird]].
