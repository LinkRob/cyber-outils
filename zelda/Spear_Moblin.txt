{{Term/Store|Spear Moblin|Spear Moblins|Series, FS, TMC, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Spear Moblin|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>{{Name Ref|FS, TFH}} They are a named variant of the [[Moblin]] that uses spears to attack. Depending on the game, they may either charge at [[Link]] with their spears or throw them as javelins.

Most Moblins in the series use spears or javelins as their main choice of weapon, such as those in {{TLoZ}}, {{TAoL}}, {{ALttP}} and {{LA}}, but are necessarily called a [[Boarblin|Boarblin.]]

==Characteristics==
==={{FS|-}}===
Making their official ''Zelda'' game debut in {{FS}}, Spear Moblins carry large spears with them they use to attack. They greatly resemble the Moblins of the successor game {{TWW}}, but differ as they charge at the [[Links]] with their spears rather than engage in full combat. Whenever they see one of the Links, they will sprint to gain momentum and then rush straight ahead with their spears pointed forwards. Their spears are able to parry the Links' [[Sword]]s, so it is advised that they be attacked from behind. They can also be stunned with the [[Boomerang]]. Spear Moblins often appear in groups, and appear in the [[Sea of Trees]] and [[Vaati's Palace]]. In {{FSAE}}, they also appear in the [[Realm of Memories]] and the [[Hero's Trial]].

[[Bow Moblin]]s also appear in {{FS|-}}, which otherwise look identical but carry [[Bow]]s instead.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Spear Moblin}}
{{Plural|TMC|Spear Moblin}} will charge at {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} with their spears when they spot him in {{TMC}}. They appear in the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}}, and occasionally in {{Term|TMC|Simon's Simulations|link}}. Although their {{Term|TMC|Figurine|link}} claims that they can be found in {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}, they do not actually appear there.

Dark blue {{Plural|TMC|Spear Moblin}} appear in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}. They are functionally identical as the ordinary brown variant. A pair of dark blue {{Plural|TMC|Spear Moblin}} also appear in {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}}; they are summoned by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} infuses the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link|display=Earth}} and {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link|display=Fire Elements}} and exits {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}}.

According to the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}}, defeating them is a good source of income, however, this is not necessarily true.<ref>{{Cite|Those pig-faced Moblins... You see them around the Minish Woods, right? They're big and dumb? Well, they're also rich! Beat one up for some quick cash!|Swordsman Newsletter 5|TMC}}</ref>

As with {{FS|-}}, Bow Moblins also appear in {{TMC|-}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, '''Spear Throwing Moblins''' appear identically to the spear-wielding Moblins of {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote=SPEAR THROWING MOBLIN These fellas are a bit of a pain. They like to keep a good distance away from you and yours while they unleash a volley of spears at you.|game= TFH|publisher= Prima|page=12}}</ref> As with their previous incarnation, they carry with them spears that they throw at the Links as javelins. Spear Throwing Moblins will back away and try to maintain a distance from the Links as they aim their spears at them. They are prolific to the [[Woodlands]] region of the [[Drablands]], appearing in the [[Deku Forest (Level)|Deku Forest]] and [[Moblin Base]]. They also appear in the [[Secret Fortress]] and the [[Den of Trials]]. In the Moblin Base and Den of Trials, Spear Throwing Moblins can be found riding [[Totem Armos]]. In these instances, the Links will need to form a [[Totem]] to reach the Moblin's height to knock it down, causing the Totem Armos to self-destruct in the process.

In the [[Den of Trials]], a "dark" variant of the Spear Throwing Moblin appears. These Moblins throw shadowy javelins that will [[jinx]] a Link it hits, preventing him from using his Sword.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Spear Moblin|A brutish monster found all over Hyrule. Its hurled spears can reach even high-up targets.<ref name="EoW Spear Moblin">{{Cite|A brutish monster found all over Hyrule. Its hurled spears can reach even high-up targets.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Spear Moblins are a variant of Moblin known for throwing spears. A stronger version, [[Spear Moblin Lv. 2]], also exists. They can be summoned with an Echo cost of 2 when acquired.

A [[Dark Echo|Dark variant]] can be encountered after coming in contact with a [[Tecuum]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
===Philips CD-i Games===
[[File:TFoE Moblin Sprite.png|right]]
In {{TFoE}} and {{TWoG}}, Moblins are called '''Moblin Spearthrowers'''.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Koridai is crawling with Dairas, Moblin Spearthrowers and other soldiers of Ganon.|game=TFoE |page=4}}</ref> These Moblins carry spears with them as they patrol the area. Most Moblins will not actively attack Link or [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], however, some Moblins crouch down and throw a barrage of spears at either hero. Their thrown spears can be blocked with the Shield. They are among the most common soldiers of [[Ganon]] in the lands of [[Koridai]] and [[Gamelon]], appearing in numerous Stages. The Moblins that inhabit [[Nortinka]] in {{TFoE|-}} drop [[Snowball]]s after being defeated.

In {{TFoE|-}}, the minion [[Lupay (Boss)|Lupay]] enslaves [[Koridians]] by replacing their souls with [[Rupee|Rubies]], turning them into Moblin Spearthrowers.
{{Noncanon|End}}
