The "{{Term/Store|Wind God's Aria||Series, TWW, TWWHD}}" is a song featured in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 97}}</ref>

==Learning Location==
It is learned from a {{Term|TWWHD|Stone Tablet|link}} inside the cave of [[Gale Isle]]. Upon learning this melody, the [[Kokiri]] sage known as [[Fado (The Wind Waker)|Fado]] makes an appearance in front of [[Link]]. Fado was the Sage of Wind until [[Ganondorf]]'s magic destroyed him inside the [[Wind Temple]].<ref>{{Cite|I had been praying here so that the power to repel evil would continue to be contained within the blade of the Master Sword... But I was attacked by the evil Ganondorf... and this is what happened to me.|Fado|TWW}}</ref> His spirit remained in the hopes that the new Sage of Wind could be awakened.

==Uses==
The "Wind God's Aria" must be played in front of [[Makar]] in order to awaken him as the new Sage of Wind.<ref>{{Cite|In that case, look for the one who has the same instrument that I hold, and teach him the song you just played.|Fado|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This sacred song that you've conducted has awakened me... I know myself now! I am the sage of the Wind Temple...|Makar|TWW}}</ref> Makar, with the help of Link, plays this song on his violin to open the entrance to the [[Wind Temple]].

This melody must be played numerous times inside the Wind Temple in order to break down several {{Plural|TWWHD|Stone Tablet}} and thus be able to progress through the dungeon. After defeating [[Molgera]], Link conducts the Wind God's Aria for Makar and Fado so that the power to repel evil is fully restored to the Master Sword.

==Trivia==
*This is the second part of {{TWW|-}} theme.
*If Link cuts up a wooden sign, the "Wind God's Aria" can be played to repair it, similar to "[[Zelda's Lullaby]]" and the "[[Song of Healing]]".
*The first few notes of the Wind God's Aria can be heard during the ending of {{PH}}.
