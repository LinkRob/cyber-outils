{{Term/Store|Darpa||Series}} is a character in the {{TP|Himekawa}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography==

[[File:Boys sparring.png|500px]]

Before the events of the [[manga]] took place, Darpa lived in a city that guarded the border of a [[Gerudo Desert|desert]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= My hometown was close to a desert-A city that guarded the border.|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 139}}</ref> He and the other boys would practice sword fighting so they could one day defend [[Hyrule]]'s borders.<ref name="Boy"/><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= When we grow up, we'll defend Hyrule's borders!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>

Darpa confronts [[Link]] after he tells [[Zeu]] and [[Rioma]] that he will drive away the [[Gerudo|desert thieves]] left handed.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I'll show you I can drive away...All the desert thieves left handed!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 140}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You say you'll drive them all away?!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 140}}</ref> Darpa says one of the thieves is a [[Ganon|demon thief]] and tells Link not to get cocky with his sword skills.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= One of them is a demon thief! Can you really defend Hyrule with just a sword? Don't get cocky just cause you've got some skill!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 140}}</ref> Rioma asks what Darpa plans to do.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= What about you then, Darpa?|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 140}}</ref> The boy says he has a plan to get a sword stronger than magic.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I have a plan. I'm gonna get a sword that's stronger than magic!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 140}}</ref> This sword turns out to be the [[Gaurof Sword]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You don't mean the Gaurof Sword, do you?|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 141}}</ref> Darpa, followed by Link, Zeu, and Rioma, enter the building where the Gaurof Sword is kept. Rioma reminds Darpa that they are not allowed near the weapon.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Let's not do this, Darpa. We've been told not to go near that since we were kids. <small>By parents and teachers...</small>|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 141}}</ref> Darpa says if he is scared of getting in trouble to go back.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= If you're scared of getting in trouble, go back! |book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 141}}</ref> Link tells Darpa that thousands have tried to pull the sword from its stone but failed.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= That sword is stuck in a stone. thousands have tried to pull it out, but failed.|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 141}}</ref> Darpa says he heard it has the power to drive off a god and it is the right sword for him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I hear it has the power to drive off a god. It's the right sword for me!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 141}}</ref> Insults are thrown at Darpa as he tries to pull out the sword but fails.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Umph! Umph! Rrgh! I can't pull it out!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page=142}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Give it up!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 142}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You suck!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 142}}</ref> He tells Link to give it a try.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Tsk! Hey, Link! Give it a try!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 142}}</ref> When Link hesitates, Darpa says that if he pulls it out, he will be a hero.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= If you pull it out, you'll be a hero!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 142}}</ref> Link steps forward and successfully draws out the blade.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I did it...|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 144}}</ref> However, the stone sucks the boys and the entire city into it, with Link as the only survivor.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... Everyone...Got sucked into the darkness. Why did I alone survive? I don't know!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 149}}</ref>

In the manga's second volume, [[Wolf Link]] has a flashback showing Darpa with Rioma and Zeu, feeling despair that they were unable to protect [[Princess Zelda]] like they had trained to do.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= "In the future, we'll defend Hyrule's borders!"|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 79}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Back home...I went through tough training so I'd be able to fight for my country in a crisis... We all...Believed that. But now the threat is real. I was supposed to protect her! But our home has disappeared... Our very lives have disappeared...And I stand before Princess Zelda not as a person...But as a beast!|book= TP (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 79}}</ref>
