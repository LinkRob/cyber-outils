'''South Lomei Castle Top Floor''' is a location and mini - dungeon in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].'' It is found directly above [[South Lomei Labyrinth]] in at the foot of [[Daval Peak]] in the and is only accessible after the completion of the South Lomei Labyrinth as well. Though this labyrinth has no [[enemies]] and traps, it does include a sprawling maze which serves as the large puzzle in finding your way to each terminal. The labyrinth also rewards [[Link]] with the [[Evil Spirit Mask]] which is located in the [[South Lomei Depths Labyrinth]] after clearing the castle and it is part of the [[Evil Spirit set]] and based off of [[Phantom Ganon]] in appearance.
