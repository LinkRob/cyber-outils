The {{Term/Store|Haunted Wasteland||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a location in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 240}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
The {{Term|OoT3D|Haunted Wasteland}} is a massive desert between {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo's Fortress|link}} and the {{Term|OoT3D|Desert Colossus|link}}, far outside the land of {{Term|OoT3D|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. As well as being extremely difficult to navigate, with a lack of landmarks and constant {{Plural|Series|Sandstorm|link}}, it is also said to be haunted. Due to this, the entrance to the {{Term|OoT3D|Haunted Wasteland}} remains closed until {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} shows his {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo Token|link}} to the {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo Guard|link}} nearby. However, before opening up the gate to the young hero, the guard mentions to Link that he will not be able reach the Desert Colossus unless he passes a set of two trials: the River of Sand and the Phantom Guide.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, rookie! Are you going into the desert? I'll open this gate for you, but... You can't cross the desert unless you pass the {{Color|OoT Red|two trials}}.|Gerudo Guard|OoT3D}}</ref> At the entrance to the {{Term|OoT3D|Haunted Wasteland}} is a {{Term|OoT3D|Sign|link}}, which warns travelers that they will be swallowed by the desert if they do not choose the correct path.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT Yellow|Haunted Wasteland}}<br/>If you chase a mirage, the desert will swallow you. Only one path is true!|Sign|OoT3D}}</ref>

====Trials====
=====River of Sand=====
The first trial, known as the '''River of Sand''', consists of crossing the large river of quicksand that cuts through the {{Term|OoT3D|Haunted Wasteland}}.<ref>{{Cite|The first trial is...the {{Color|OoT Red|River of Sand}}! You can't walk across this river! After you cross it, follow the flags we placed there.|Gerudo Guard|OoT3D}}</ref> In order for {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} to pass this trial, he will need the assistance of either the {{Term|OoT3D|Hover Boots|link}} or the {{Term|OoT3D|Longshot|link}}. Equipping the {{Term|OoT3D|Hover Boots}} allows the young hero to float over the River of Sand. Likewise, he can also cross the valley of quicksand by using the {{Term|OoT3D|Longshot}} to grapple to one of the boxes on the other side of the river. Once {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} has successfully completed the first trial, he must follow the series of flags that have been placed throughout the desert. Straying too far from these flags will cause him to wind up back at the entrance of the {{Term|OoT3D|Haunted Wasteland}}. 

While following the flag posts, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} will encounter a sign advertising a {{Term|OoT3D|Shop|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|All those reckless enough to venture into the desert—please drop by our shop. —{{Color|OoT Yellow|Carpet Merchant}}|Sign|OoT3D}}</ref> He can follow the direction in which the sign is pointing to find the {{Term|OoT3D|Carpet Merchant|link}}, who is ready to sell {{Plural|OoT3D|Bombchu|link}} to {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} for 200 {{Plural|OoT3D|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well Come! I am selling stuff, strange and rare, from all over the world to everybody. Today's special is... A dangerous, running object! Terrifying! I won't tell you what it is until I see the money.... How about {{Color|OoT Red|200 Rupees}}?|Carpet Merchant|OoT3D}}</ref> It is necessary to use the {{Term|OoT3D|Hover Boots}} to reach the {{Term|OoT3D|Carpet Merchant|display=Merchant}}, since his flying carpet is far off a ledge.

At the end of the flag posts is a stone monument that houses the second trial: the Phantom Guide.<ref name="Phantom Guide">{{Cite|The second trial is...the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Phantom Guide}}!|Gerudo Guard|OoT3D}}</ref>

=====Phantom Guide=====
Once {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} climbs the stone structure, there will be a small plaque mentioning that one with the "eye of truth" will be guided to the {{Term|OoT3D|Spirit Temple|link}} by a ghost.<ref>{{Cite|One with the {{Color|OoT Red|eye of truth}} shall be guided to the Spirit Temple by an inviting ghost.|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref> Here, {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} must equip the {{Term|OoT3D|Lens of Truth|link}} to see a {{Term|OoT3D|Poe|link}} known as the '''Phantom Guide'''.<ref name="Phantom Guide"/> This {{Term|OoT3D|Poe}} will be his guide on the way to the {{Term|OoT3D|Desert Colossus}}. However, the Phantom Guide will not do the same for the young hero on his way back to {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo's Fortress}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll be your guide on your way, but coming back, I won't play! I'll show you the only way to go, so follow me and don't be slow!|Poe|OoT3D}}</ref> {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} has to follow the Guide closely, since deviating too far from the Phantom's path will result in him getting lost in the {{Term|Series|Sandstorm}} and returning to the {{Term|OoT3D|Haunted Wasteland}} entrance. While following the {{Term|OoT3D|Poe}}, various {{Plural|OoT3D|Leever|link}} will appear in an attempt to distract {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} from following the Phantom's path.

Successfully completing these two trials will lead {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} to the {{Term|OoT3D|Desert Colossus}}, a vast, secluded desert that is home to the {{Term|OoT3D|Spirit Temple}}.
