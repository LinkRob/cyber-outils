The {{Term/Store|Helm Splitter||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an ability in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Very well. My fourth hidden skill is... The {{Color|TPHD Red|helm splitter}}! Let it be hewn into your mind!|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TPHD|Helm Splitter}} is the fourth of the seven {{Plural|TPHD|Hidden Skill|link}} taught by the [[Hero's Spirit]] (who himself is the former [[Hero of Time]]). [[Link]] can learn this {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}} by first finding a {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone|link}} to awaken the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Search for the statues that howl with the {{Color|TPHD Red|sound of the wind}}. Seek the sound that calls the spirit of the beast to awaken me again.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref> The {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone}} needed to learn the {{Term|TPHD|Helm Splitter}} can be found outside of the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Grove|link}}. After howling with the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}}, the wolf itself instructs Link to find him while in human form.<ref>{{Cite|Let teachings of old pass to you... Take sword in hand and find me...|White Wolf|TPHD}}</ref> The {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} also marks the location of where he can be found on Link's {{Term|TPHD|Map|link}}. Once the hero returns to human form, he can find the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} waiting at the southern edge of {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle Town|link}}, {{Exp|East on the Wii Version|west}} of the stairs. Approaching the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} will return Link to the clouded area where the {{Term|TPHD|Hero's Spirit}} can teach the Hero of Twilight the {{Term|TPHD|Helm Splitter}}, which, like the [[Mortal Draw]], is a lethal attack.

This skill proves useful when the [[enemy]] is too fast to successfully land a [[Back Slice]] on. The move is initiated by a [[Parry|Shield Attack]] to stun the enemy, then by attacking again, Link will jump in the air above the foe and slice its head in a vertical flipping motion.<ref>{{Cite|Do not miss your chance to land a {{Color|TPHD Red|helm splitter}} with {{Icon|TPHD A}} after your shield attack!|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref> After performing the {{Term|TPHD|Helm Splitter}}, Link will land behind his opponent, giving him a chance to continue attacking the foe from behind. This skill is most effective against enemies with helmets, such as {{Plural|TPHD|Darknut|link}}.
