'''Gold Necklace''' is a spell in {{ZA}}.

==Overview==
Whenever [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] uses the Gold Necklace, this fires a spread of gold sparkles that spread out while traveling. The spell costs 2 [[Rupee]]s to use.

The Spell is received from a [[Thirsty Child]] standing by a well in the [[Shrine of Strength]] after Zelda draws him water from the well. The Gold Necklace is the only Spell that can defeat [[Maxus]] in the [[Shrine of Fire]].
