{{Term/Store|Bodyguard|Bodyguards|Series|plural}} are characters in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} Since {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} is not a fighter himself, early on he is forced to hire and team up with one of the many {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} around if he wants to continue his journey. Each {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} has their own set of traits that subtly alters the way {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} moves through the many islands. {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}, apart from Drifter {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}, are hired in {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard Salons|link}}.

==Function==
{{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}, once hired, accompany {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} throughout the continent and aid in battles with the local wildlife. They can fight independently or with {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}. When battling together, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} will take less damage.<ref>{{Cite|"Battling Together" - Want to hear about this? Bodyguards are great allies. They will defend you in battles so they lose power instead of you... By "together", I mean being part of the same smoke cloud in a battle. If you get into a battle... ...either call your Bodyguard, or move to where they are...But of course, you'll still lose some energy... You can't get 100% protection. But it's much better than trying to take on monsters by yourself. Give it a try!|Salona|FPTRR}}</ref> {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can restore a salon-hired {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}'s health with {{Plural|FPTRR|Potion|link}} so long as they still have some energy left. If a {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}'s health completely depletes and they collapse due to their wounds, they will not be affected by {{Plural|FPTRR|Potion}}.<ref>{{Cite|"Watch The Hearts" - You have to hear this! Make your Bodyguard work too hard and they'll collapse. You could be in real trouble if it's a dangerous place. So before things come to that... ...as the employer, you should always have a supply of power-up potions... if you throw it at your Bodyguard, they'll recover... You can throw it so it hits them, or get them to walk over it... Basically, as long a they touch the potion, they will feel its effects... Of course... You can also pay rupees to get your Bodyguard to recover their energy... But if you don't want to lose rupees, make sure you always carry power-up potions. But remember, once they have collapsed, it's too late to give them any power-up potion... Make sure you get it to them before that!|Salona|FPTRR}}</ref> Instead, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} will have to hire them again for their services. Some time is given for this, but if they are not paid, they will go back to the {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard Salon}} without warning.<ref>{{Cite|"I Give Up!" - You need to hear this. If your Bodyguard collapses, you're in trouble... But even if you go far away from where they are... They will stay lying on the ground, waiting for you... If you give them rupees, they'll regain energy. But... Bodyguards have limits to their patience... If you leave a Bodyguard who has collapsed and decide... ...to hire a new Bodyguard... Your old Bodyguard will get up and go home. Also, if you leave your Bodyguard lying on the ground... ...and go off exploring on your own... ...they might get fed up and wander back to the Bodyguard Salon. The one thing you don't have to worry about is that because they've gone... ...they'll hold a grudge against you next time you hire them... That won't happen. These guys are pros.|Salona|FPTRR}}</ref> Hiring a new {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} at a salon or in the overworld will terminate any existing {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} contract.

The three main traits of a {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} are related to their continent of work, their size class and their behaviour. Salon-hired {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} cannot travel between continents and are instead bound to the continent in which they are hired. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} travels to another continent, his {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} will not meet him there and he may decide to hire one of the local {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} for help - at the cost of the contract on the other continent. If no other {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} is hired though, the old one will loyally await their employer's return to their continent.<ref>{{Cite|"Hello and Goodbye" - This is top info... You've got a balloon, haven't you? But your Bodyguard doesn't... So when you use your balloon... You're saying goodbye to your Bodyguard... But don't worry! The contract will continue even after that... Your Bodyguard will be waiting for you when you next go somewhere... Then you can be reunited and give them a cheerful "hello!" As long as you are on the same continent, they will always be waiting... But I have one more thing to tell you! This is the First Continent, right? I've never left it, so I don't really know but... If you say goodbye and go to another continent... You won't find your Bodyguard waiting there... Ask me again anytime!|Salona|FPTRR}}</ref> Each continent has the same set of nine {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} types, with power levels appropriate for that continent. The nine types are composed of three behavior patterns among three size classes. The size class's immediate use is the ease with which {{Plural|FPTRR|Enemy|link}} are destroyed. Small-sized {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} need more healing and take longer to destroy an enemy whereas large {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} can handle nearly any battle with ease. The size classes come with a secondary function, a so-called "special ability". In {{Plural|FPTRR|Dungeon|link}}, some {{Plural|FPTRR|Treasure Chest|link}} are out of reach because they lie deep in a small hole, or are located behind a locked door or are obstructed by a large boulder. Small {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} can crawl into holes to fetch the treasure, medium {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} are skilled lock-pickers and large {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} have the strength required to lift the boulder out of the way.<ref>{{Cite|"Special Abilities" - This is just great! I must apologise... I don't actually know that much about this... Every individual Bodyguard... ...has their own skills, and not just when it comes to battling... Small Bodyguards have certain advantages... Big Bodyguards pack a lot of muscle... And the medium-sized ones are good with their hands... I'm not lying! This is all true! Sorry I can't give you more details... And here's something else! When you're battling monsters, touch the smoke cloud... Keep touching it as fast as you can! Do that and you and your Bodyguard will both give it your best. This means that the power of your attacks will increase... I'm not kidding! Give it a try!|Salona|FPTRR}}</ref> Behavior is what sets the members of one size class apart. There are passive {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} who will not engage in battle alone unless forced to,<ref>{{Cite|When you have a Bodyguard, try touching a monster on the screen... If you get it right, the Bodyguard will attack that monster... As long as the Bodyguard is a smart one, they'll know what to do... But remember that Bodyguards are only human... Things will go better if you help them out in battle... If you keep touching the smoke cloud during battle, they'll be grateful... Well, the best way to learn is to actually try things out with a Bodyguard... |Salona|FPTRR}}</ref> often ignoring a direct command to attack. Aggressive {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} in turn attack everything in sight, whether they can defeat it or not and whether it ruins the chances of getting multiple enemies in one fight or not. Generally the most useful, and therefore most expensive {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} in a given size class, are the assertive ones, who only engage in battle if ordered or if their employer is in trouble.

===Drifter {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}===
There are three Drifter {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can employ in {{FPTRR|-}}:<ref>{{Cite|According to some very reliable sources of mine... There are a total of 3 Drifter Bodyguards in the world...|Salona|FPTRR}}</ref> {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo|link}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Yamori|link}} and {{Term|FPTRR|Ronny|link}}. They work very differently from the normal {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}, being easier to handle in some regards and more difficult in others. They work without a license, so they cannot be hired in the {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard Salon}}. Instead, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} must stumble upon them by chance, at first to win their respect, then later to actually hire them. They are significantly more powerful than regular {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} and are capable of trademark moves during battle.<ref>{{Cite|If you touch the smoke cloud round Drifter Bodyguards you'll see their true power! If you're lucky enough to hire one, you should really try it during a battle!|Salona|FPTRR}}</ref> Not being bound to a Salon also means they can travel with their employer across continents.<ref>{{Cite|Drifter Bodyguards are free to travel around the world... I wish I was that free! But no, I'm stuck here every day... You never know where you're going to meet those Drifter Bodyguards...|Salona|FPTRR}}</ref> However, Drifter {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} do not take {{Plural|FPTRR|Potion}} when injured and refuse to re-sign a contract when they have collapsed, leaving the length of their use limited.<ref>{{Cite|I heard a rumor the other day... Apparently, there are non-Salon Bodyguards you can hire... They operate without a license... I heard that they are exceedingly powerful but... They won't drink power-up potions, they don't negotiate twice and worse than that... I heard that they are incredibly expensive to hire. Are they worth it? Personally, I think you're better off sticking with the Bodyguard Salon...|Salona|FPTRR}}</ref> To re-hire them, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} must seek them out once more.

===Unofficial Bodyguards===
Throughout the game, there are several compulsory contracts that need to be made in order for the storyline progress. These contracts are always temporary, and dissolve once a particular task has been achieved. These {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}, with the exception of {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo}}, are unofficial {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} and will not be added to the {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} list in {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}'s notebook. They cannot be taken far from where they were first engaged.

[[File:FPTRR Teddy 1 Portrait Sprite.png|Left‎]]'''Teddy Todo'''<br/>
Health - ''5 hearts''<br/>
Behaviour - ''Aggressive''<br/>
Location - {{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine|link}}<br/>
Before becoming a full-powered Drifter {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo}} is found in the {{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine}}, where he can be hired for a minimum of 20 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}. This transaction is essential to progress any further into the dungeon. The contract terminates after completing the dungeon. {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo}} is also found in the {{Term|FPTRR|Insect Cavern|link}}. Despite fighting alongside {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} in the {{Term|FPTRR|Boss|link}} battle, there is no formal {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} contract and {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo}} will not follow {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} around.

[[File:FPTRR Junglo Portrait Sprite.png|Left‎]]'''Junglo'''<br/>
Health - ''1 heart''<br/>
Behaviour - ''Passive''<br/>
Location - Western {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Forest (First Continent)|display=Deku Forest|link}}, near the waterfall<br/>
Once a fearless jungle hero, {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo|link}} has become weak in old age. He has a lower health than any other {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} in the game, and requires {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} to escort him to his home after being rescued from three insects. If {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}}'s health is depleted he will retreat to the waterfall. The contract is terminated on arrival at {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo}}'s house.

[[File:Townguardphoto.png|Left‎]]'''Town Guard'''<br/>
Health - ''10 hearts''<br/>
Behaviour - ''Aggressive''<br/>
Location - {{Term|FPTRR|Icy Plain|link}} cemetery<br/>
The {{Term|FPTRR|Town Guard|link}} travels to {{Term|FPTRR|Icy Plain}} after discovering his wife is being plagued by ghosts. He will offer to join {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} as a {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} to defeat the Ghost Ringleader. The contract terminates when the Ghost Ringleader initially defeats the guard.

[[File:Townguardphoto.png|Left‎]]'''Town Guard'''<br/>
Health - ''20 hearts''<br/>
Behaviour - ''Aggressive''<br/>
Location - {{Term|FPTRR|Icy Plain}} cemetery<br/>
Once revived, the guard's health reaches 20, making him comparable to the Drifter {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}. His contract with {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} will renew instantly and terminates with the defeat of the Ghost Ringleader.

==Trivia==
*Since the {{Term|FPTRR|Stalfos|link}} pirates are deathly afraid of dogs, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can not go to {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Hideaway|display=their hideout|link}} when one of the dog {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} tags along.
*It seems {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}'s sidekicks from ''{{Term|FPTRR|Ripened Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love|link}}'', are based on the three {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} types. {{Term|FPTRR|Kakashi|link}}'s smaller size, {{Term|FPTRR|Buriki|link}}'s ability to solve puzzles and {{Term|FPTRR|Lion (Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love)|display=Lion|link}}'s strength are alike to the small, medium and large size classes' special abilities.
