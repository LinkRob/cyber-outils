{{Term/Store|Tower of Flames||Series, FSA}} is the third {{Term|FSA|Stage|link}} of {{Term|FSA|Death Mountain|link}} in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 153}}</ref> It is where the {{Term|FSA|Green Shrine Maiden|link}} is being held hostage. Although few {{Plural|FSA|Enemy|link}} are encountered in this {{Term|FSA|Dungeon|link}}, there are many hazards and fire traps such as rotating flame pillars and fire shooting statues. Large parts of the {{Term|FSA|Dungeon}} are also covered by lava pits. 

In addition to {{Term|FSA|Dark Link|display=Shadow Link|link}}, the four {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} will also encounter a {{Term|FSA|Ball and Chain Soldier|link}} as a {{Term|FSA|Sub-Boss|link}}, whose ball happens to ignite fire.

==Themes and Navigation==
Near the end of the {{Term|FSA|Dungeon|link}}, the four {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} meet a lone, but talkative {{Term|FSA|Goron|link}}. Apparently he entered the tower in order to vanquish the monster around, but accidentally dropped the {{Plural|FSA|Bomb|link}} he brought with him into the lava pits.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, this isn't good. I brought my bombs all this way, and then I dropped 'em. They went right into the lava, too. And I thought I had a good plan... I was gonna use them on the monsters around here. You should watch out for them.|Goron|FSA}}</ref> He then advises the four {{Plural|FSA|Links}} on how to defeat the {{Plural|FSA|Enemy|link}}, namely a group of {{Plural|FSA|Dodongo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Even with my strength, it's a waste to hit them. I can't even make a dent! But then it came to me! They may be tough on the outside but weak on the INSIDE! So right when they open their mouths to breather fire, gulp! And then... BLAMMO! What a great idea! ...But then I dropped my bombs in the lava! Ugh! I can't believe it!|Goron|FSA}}</ref> After acquiring {{Plural|FSA|Bomb}} the four {{Plural|FSA|Link}} defeat the {{Plural|FSA|Dodongo}}, they head out to a terrace were they once again meet {{Term|FSA|Kaepora Gaebora|link}}, who warn them of the evil that as struck {{Term|FSA|Hyrule Castle (Four Swords Adventures)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hoo hoot! Link! Danger has struck Hyrule Castle once again! ... Where there is great evil, though there may also be an imprisoned shrine maiden. You must tell the maiden of this tower! We cannot abandon Hyrule Castle!|Kaepora Gaebora|FSA}}</ref> They then proceed to rescue the {{Term|FSA|Green Shrine Maiden|link}}.
