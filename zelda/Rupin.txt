==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>This is {{Color|SS Blue|Rupin}}, a crafty salesman. During the day, he sells a variety of items at his Gear Shop in the bazaar.</p><p>During the night, he buys treasures, but only the treasures he has decided to buy on that night.</p>
}}

Rupin runs the [[Gear Shop]] in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}}, where he sells [[Link]] a variety of items, including [[Shield]]s, [[Deku Seed]]s, [[Bomb]]s, [[Seed Satchel]]s, [[Bomb Bag]]s, [[Quiver]]s, and [[Arrow]]s. When first confronted, he will greet Link, referring to him as "friend" thereafter.<ref>{{Cite|Ahaa! A customer! Hello there, friend.|Rupin|SS}}</ref> Rupin states that his shop has the finest merchandise,<ref>{{Cite|My shop has the finest products you'll find anywhere!|Rupin|SS}}</ref> and invites Link to savor his products, as he has supposedly prepared it all just for the hero.<ref>{{Cite|Please, take a moment for your senses to savor the wonderful treasures I have prepared just for you, friend!|Rupin|SS}}</ref> He then tells Link not to hesitate if he has any questions.<ref>{{Cite|If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to ask.|Rupin|SS}}</ref> Although, if Link attempts to purchase something and doesn't have the appropriate bag to carry it in, or the weapon needed to use the product, Rupin will refuse.<ref>{{Cite|My friend... you don't appear to have a slingshot. I simply couldn't sell you these in good conscience if you do not have a slingshot. Perhaps I can interest you in some of my other wares...|Rupin|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ahaa! Very sorry, but I can't sell you that unless you have a Bomb Bag. Again, my apologies.|Rupin|SS}}</ref> Rupin will follow Link around the shop, and if the hero walks away he will slump forward and sigh. If Link views an item and doesn't buy it, Rupin will scowl before quickly regaining his usual expression, telling Link it is alright and to continue his time in the shop.<ref>{{Cite|... That's quite alright, friend! I've quite a few other fine items in stock, so do take your time and look around!|Rupin|SS}}</ref>

At night, Rupin can be seen in his home in the residential district on the south-eastern part of Skyloft. In contrast to his assumed personality during the day, Rupin behaves rudely towards Link at night.<ref>{{Cite|What? You think I'm being gruff? You think I'm totally different during the day? Well, I get tired of being a smiley shopkeeper all day, all right? Just don't think that this is the real me.|Rupin|SS}}</ref> He will also buy randomly selected [[Treasure]]s at his night market.<ref>{{Cite|... You're here to make some money at my {{Color|SS Red|night market}}, am I right?... Night is when I do my buying. See, {{Color|SS Red|I buy treasure}}, but that's not to say I'll buy just any old thing. I only deal in {{Color|SS Red|specific types of treasure}}. |Rupin|SS}}</ref>
