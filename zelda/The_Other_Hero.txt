"{{Term/Store|The Other Hero||Series, HWL, HWDE}}" is the fifth {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} of the {{Term|HWDE|Linkle's Tale|link}} arc of {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode|link}} in {{HW|L}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|The Other Hero}}|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> It takes place in {{Term|HWDE|Ganon's Tower (Stage)|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Stub|HWL, HWDE}}
"The Other Hero" takes place after "[[Liberation of the Triforce]]". [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] have recently departed to return the [[Master Sword]] to its pedestal. However, in their absence, the remains of Ganondorf's army have regrouped and are again attacking [[Hyrule Castle]]. Linkle, having finally arrived at Hyrule Castle, immediately takes charge of the situation and fights alongside Impa to finally quell Ganondorf's army.
