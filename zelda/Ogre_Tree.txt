==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Ogre Tree}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} located within the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods|link}}. Their knots and bark resemble faces,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 255}}</ref> several of which contain {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in their mouths. Near the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find two stone {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} in the mouths of {{Plural|BotW|Ogre Tree}}, containing a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Spear|link}} and an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}}.

To exit the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must enter the mouth of an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}}, which can be found by following a {{Term|BotW|Sign|link}} east of the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|link}}.<ref name="Maca"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|BotW Arrow Left}} The Test of Wood<br/>Forest Exit Shortcut {{Icon|BotW Arrow Right}}|Sign|BotW}}</ref> A nearby {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|link}}, written by {{Term|BotW|Maca|link}}, assures its readers that they will not be eaten by the {{Term|BotW|Tree}}.<ref>{{Cite|A Note from Maca:<br/>Just climb into this tree's mouth to leave the forest. I promise it won't eat you.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|Trial of Second Sight|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to spot the metal {{Plural|BotW|Boulder|link}} in the mouths of {{Plural|BotW|Ogre Tree}}, which will lead him to an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|"The hungry trees will show you the way. Spy their {{Color|BotW Red|iron}} to win the day."|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> Along this path, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet}} in front of an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}}. It asks to be fed a {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield|link}}, promising a reward from its brother.<ref>{{Cite|Feed me the aged shield guarded by monsters eternal. My brother will repay you in kind.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> When a {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield}} is placed in its mouth, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}} will fall out of the mouth of an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}} nearby. In the middle of {{Term|BotW|Lake Saria|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find another {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet}} in front of an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}}. It asks for the return of the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, explaining that it was stolen by the brother.<ref>{{Cite|Please place my chest in my mouth.<br/>{{Small|The shield eater's brother stole it from me.}}|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> Once the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} is placed in its mouth, {{Term|BotW|Kuhn Sidajj Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground across the {{Term|BotW|Lake Saria|display=Lake}}.<ref>{{Cite|"The hungry trees will show you the way.<br/>Spy their iron to win the day."<br/><br/>You discovered an ancient shrine just beyond the trees with iron orbs in their mouths.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

As part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Strange Mask Rumors|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, which is exclusive to {{TMT}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must read the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V1|link}}. It describes the {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask|link}} as being hidden in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} in a {{Term|BotW|Tree}} hollow in a forest that makes people lose their way.<ref>{{Cite|Some say the treasure chest containing this mask is hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in a tree hollow in a forest that makes people lose their way}}. Hmm, sounds like a tough place to find something! Luckily, word has it there's a convenient trick for finding your way...|Super Rumor Mill V1|BotW}}</ref> South of the pair of {{Plural|BotW|Torch|link}} at the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find an EX {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} in the mouth of an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}} containing the {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Mask}} in the spooky tree's hollow within the Lost woods!<br/><br/>The Korok Mask will inform you when a Korok is hiding nearby.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
