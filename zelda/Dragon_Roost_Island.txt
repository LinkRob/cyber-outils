{{Term/Store|Dragon Roost Island||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (B6) is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 111 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>{{Fact}} It is located in the northeast of the [[Great Sea]], and is home to the [[Rito]] tribe and the Spirit of the Sky, [[Valoo]], who sits on the very top of the mountain. The game's second dungeon, [[Dragon Roost Cavern]], is situated on this island. After successfully completing it, [[Link]] will receive the first of the three Goddess Pearls, [[Din's Pearl]]. It is remarkable for its catchy and vigorous music.

Dragon Roost Island can be seen from a great distance, as it primarily consists of a huge mountain. There are many [[Bomb Flower|explosive fruits]] scattered across the island, and many [[Kargaroc]]s have made it their home. The planted bombs are necessary to blow up some rocks that prevent access to the inner area of the island. After the obstacles are cleared, it is a matter of walking through a wooden passage that leads to the shrine of the Rito tribe.

Around Dragon Roost Island are three secret [[Treasure Chest]]s to be discovered. The first is in a secret {{Term|TWWHD|Cave|link}} blocked by a boulder near the [[Wind Shrine]]. Defeating all the enemies in the {{Term|TWWHD|Cave}} unlocks a door that leads to a chest containing a [[Purple Rupee]]. The second is a {{Term|TWWHD|Cave}} behind the foot of the island that can be accessed by hovering across the steep rock formations with the help of the [[Deku Leaf]] and the [[Wind Waker (Item)|Wind Waker]]. Link must be careful when dealing with [[Kargaroc]]s while hovering to the {{Term|TWWHD|Cave}}. Inside is a single Treasure Chest containing an [[Orange Rupee]]. The final Treasure Chest is found at the southwestern end of the island, atop a boulder high up against the wall of Dragon Roost. Growing near the boulder are several [[Bomb Flower]]s. Detonating one of the Bomb Flowers by piercing it with an [[Arrow]] or using a [[Hyoi Pear]] to bring a [[Seagull]] up to touch it engages a chain reaction; when the Bomb Flower nearest to the boulder explodes, the boulder will crumble and the Treasure Chest on top of it will fall to the ground. Inside this chest is a [[Silver Rupee]].

===Dragon Roost Pond===
{{Main|Dragon Roost Pond}}
The [[Dragon Roost Pond]] is located in between the [[Mail Center]] and [[Dragon Roost Cavern]].

===Mail Center===
{{Main|Mail Center}}
The {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center|link}} is a small cavern close to the island base where the Rito make their home.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD|publisher= Prima|page= 87}}</ref> It doubles as the headquarters of the [[Island Postal Service]].

===Wind Shrine===
{{Main|Wind Shrine}}
Dragon Roost Island is home to the Wind Shrine, which can be found on the eastern side of the island. This Shrine has two stone monuments which honor the two Wind Gods, [[Cyclos]] and [[Zephos]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
In {{HW|L}}, Dragon Roost Island appears conjoined with [[Windfall Island]] and the [[Forsaken Fortress]] as a stage. The stage it self named soly for the Fortress, while Windfall Island and Dragon Roost Island are the names of the primary keeps in their respective sections of the stage.

==Trivia==
*In both [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker Original Sound Tracks|{{TWW|-}} official soundtrack]] and {{SSBB}}, a bassline was added to Dragon Roost Island's theme and the general audio quality of the instruments was improved. It's the only song on the soundtrack, as well as one of the few songs in ''Brawl'' with the "Original" label, that is not a fully accurate representation of the in-game music.
*The background music heard in [[Rito Village]] in {{BotW}} contains references to the theme of Dragon Roost Island.  
**In {{TotK|-}}, a somber version of the Dragon Roost Island theme is heard in the Rito Village. The boss theme for [[Colgera]] also contains a more dramatic take on the theme. Finally, after completing the [[Wind Temple#Tears of the Kingdom|Wind Temple]], the original version of the song from {{BotW|-}} resumes playing in the village.
*Before the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Flood|link}} in {{TWW}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island}} was {{Term|Series|Zora's Domain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 59}}</ref> However, Dragon Roost visually resembles [[Death Mountain Trail|Death Mountain]].  Both locations are large volcanoes with dragons and rings of clouds encircling the peak.
*With the exception of sequence-breaking glitches, {{TWW|-}}'s in-game clock remains static during Link's first trip to the island. Time does not start progressing naturally until he completes Dragon Roost Cavern and sails away from the island.
