{{Term/Store|Bipin's & Blossom's House||Series, OoA}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bipin's & Blossom's House|Map|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoA Map Present|Bipin's & Blossom's House}}|Map|OoA}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Bipin's & Blossom's House can be found in northeastern [[Lynna City]] in {{OoA}} and in southwestern [[Horon Village]] in {{OoS}}. The House is identical in both appearances, appearing as a wide building with two entrances. The interior of the House is mostly sparse; the left side of the House holds two beds, a table, and some shelves for the family to use. The right side of the House has a patch of soil and a small pool of water, where Bipin may be found planting crops.

The House is where [[Link]] may come to interact with the family, especially with [[Bipin]]'s and [[Blossom]]'s son, [[Bipsom]], whose personality and eventual choice of career is dependent on Link's decisions.

After Link defeats either [[Veran]] in {{OoA|-}} or [[General Onox]] in {{OoS|-}}, the family will move away to the other land and the house will become vacant, with a sign outside announcing their departure.<ref>{{Cite|Off to Labrynna! Bipin & Blossom|Sign|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Off to Holodrum! Bipin & Blossom|Sign|OoA}}</ref> Link may meet the family again in the other land in a [[Linked Game]], where Bipsom will eventually grow into an adult. Link will receive different rewards from Bipsom depending on the child's career and the decisions Link made regarding him.
