{{Term/Store|Golden Bomb|Golden Bombs|Series, OoA, TMC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="OoA">{{Cite|Did you drop a {{Color|OoA Red|Golden Bomb}}? Or a {{Color|OoA Red|Silver Bomb}}?|Fairy|OoA}}</ref><ref name="TMC">{{Cite|Did you throw the {{Color|TMC Red|Golden Bomb}} into the spring? Or the {{Color|TMC Red|Silver Bomb}}?|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA|-}}, {{Term|OoA|Talus Peaks|link}} has a natural fountain with a {{Term|OoA|Sign|link}} next to it warning people not to throw anything into the water.<ref>{{Cite|Do not throw anything into the water.|Sign|OoA}}</ref> If {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} throws in a {{Term|OoA|Bomb|link}}, a red {{Term|OoA|Fairy|link}} appears. She materializes a {{Term|OoA|Golden Bomb}} and a {{Term|OoA|Silver Bomb|link}} and asks {{Term|OoA|Link}} which one he dropped.<ref name="OoA"/> If he answers honestly, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} rewards him by increasing the amount of {{Plural|OoA|Bomb}} he can carry by 20.<ref>{{Cite|You are an honest person. I'll increase the number of {{Color|OoA Red|Bombs}} you can carry to reward you. Hee-yah!|Fairy|OoA}}</ref> If {{Term|OoA|Link}} responds by saying he dropped the {{Term|OoA|Golden Bomb}}, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} asks him to confirm,<ref>{{Cite|...Really? A {{Color|OoA Red|Golden Bomb}}?|Fairy|OoA}}</ref> twice.<ref>{{Cite|Really and truly a {{Color|OoA Red|Golden Bomb}}???|Fairy|OoA}}</ref> If he affirms the lie both times, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} teaches him a lesson by depleting his {{Term|OoA|Life Energy|link}} to a single {{Term|OoA|Heart|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Liar! Greed begets sorrow!|Fairy|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hope you've learned not to lie.{{Icon|OoA Heart}}|Fairy|OoA}}</ref> To obtain the upgrade {{Term|OoA|Link}} must leave the area,  then return and throw in another {{Term|OoA|Bomb}} to restart the whole interaction.

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} throws a {{Term|TMC|Bomb}} into the {{Term|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy|link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Great Fairy Fountain|link|display=Fountain}}, she will ask him whether he threw a {{Term|TMC|Golden Bomb}}, a {{Term|TMC|Silver Bomb}}, or neither.<ref name="TMC"/> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} gives the honest answer of "Neither", she rewards him with a {{Term|TMC|Big Bomb Bag|link}};<ref>{{Cite|You are honest. I must reward such an honest adventurer with some of my power.|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref> however, if he gives either of the other incorrect answers, she accuses him of lying and takes away all of his {{Plural|TMC|Bomb}}.<ref>{{Cite|You're lying, aren't you? As punishment for your lie, I will reduce your number of bombs to zero.|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref>

{{Plural|TMC|Golden Bomb}} are not seen in-game, only mentioned.
