{{Term/Store|Stone Watcher Island||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (E3) is an island found in the [[Great Sea]] of {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features==
{{Fishman|So tell me, small fry, have you heard about the golden Triumph Forks? There are such things... And there's a chart that'll show you where to find them on that island there. If you want it, try asking that huge, grouchy-looking stone watcher! I doubt you could even budge the thing, though... You've got skinny arms, fry.}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= <p>Stone Watcher Island</p><p>The water is really rough! Mr. Fairy! If you feel seasick, hold your wrist! That should make you feel better.</p>
}}

On the island, there is a giant [[Stone Watcher]] looking out to sea. This stone can be moved once [[Link]] has the [[Power Bracelet]], which will reveal a secret cave full of enemies. Once all the enemies are defeated, a Treasure Chest will appear containing [[Triforce Chart]] #7. In {{TWWHD}}, Link will instead receive the [[Triforce#Triforce Shards|Triforce Shard]] itself.

A [[Piece of Heart]] can also be retrieved here by heading to a platform due east of the Stone Watcher Island, but the [[Bokoblin]] and the wall-mounted cannons must be taken out first. Furthermore, once Link has the Triforce Chart from [[Bird's Peak Rock]], he will be able to find another Triforce Shard at the bottom of the Great Sea.
