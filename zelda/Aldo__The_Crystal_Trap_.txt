{{Term/Store|Aldo||Series, TCT}} is a character in {{TCT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= TCT|publisher= |page= 6}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TCT|Aldo|link}} serves as the gatekeeper of the [[Ruto Town|Town of Ruto]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book= TCT|publisher= |page= 55}}</ref>

[[Princess Zelda]] meets the gatekeeper when she exits the [[Cavern (The Crystal Trap)|Cavern]] and finds herself outside the gates of the Town of Ruto. When she approaches the gates, Aldo recognizes her and greets her, taking her into the gatehouse and bringing her food and water.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Aldo leads Zelda into the gatehouse and brings her fresh bread and cheese and cool water.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=56}}</ref> The princess informs Aldo of her quest, and when Zelda asks him if he has seen anything or anyone unusual, he tells her of a beekeeper known as [[Conly]] who, according to townsfolk, has [[Magic Bee|bees]] that can make [[Magic Honey]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Between bites, Zelda tells him about her quest. 'Have you seen anything or anyone unusual in town?' she asks. 'In town?' The gatekeeper shakes his head. 'No,' he says. 'But just out of town there's a strange newcomer--a beekeeper.' [...] 'Name of Conly,' Aldo replies. 'Very strange. He lives just out of town, back along the road a bit. Folks say his bees are magic.'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=56}}</ref> As Zelda is listening to Aldo, she realizes that the last part of the riddle of the [[Fairy's Scroll]] is Honey.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Hmm,' Zelda says softly.' How did the riddle go? What's sweeter than sugar? I think I know the answer!'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=56}}</ref>

Although Zelda eagerly wants to visit the beekeeper and get some Magic Honey from him, Aldo begs her to not go since Conly is known to be unfriendly. As such, he advices the princess to not visit him until she has learned how to make a deal with Conly.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Take my advise and don't go see Conly until you've learned how to make a deal with him.'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=56}}</ref> If Zelda chooses to follow Aldo's advise, she will eventually receive [[Fairy Flower Sap]], which is what she gives to the beekeeper in order to obtain the Magic Honey from him. However, if she ignores the gatekeeper's advice, Zelda will be attacked by Conly's magic bees and eventually lead to her death.
