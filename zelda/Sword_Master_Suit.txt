The {{Term/Store|Sword Master Suit||Series, TFH}} is an {{Term|TFH|Item|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Sword Master Suit is the mark of a truly gifted sword wielder. Wearing it is considered an honor, my pet!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Sword Master Suit}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}. It becomes available for purchase after the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} complete the {{Term|TFH|Desert Temple|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}.

The {{Term|TFH|Sword Master Suit}} is similar to the {{Term|TFH|Sword Suit|link}} in that it doubles the damage that {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} deals, along with giving his {{Term|TFH|Sword}} the ability to fire {{Plural|TFH|Sword Beam|link}} while {{Term|TFH|Link}} is at full Health. The {{Term|TFH|Sword Master Suit}} also extends the length of {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s {{Term|TFH|Sword}}, nearly doubling its reach.<ref>{{Cite|The best duds a sword wielder can wear. Increases sword power and range.|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref>

Owning the {{Term|TFH|Sword Master Suit}} is considered an honor and a sign of expertise.<ref>{{Cite|The Sword Master Suit is the mark of a truly gifted sword wielder. Wearing it is considered an honor, my pet!|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> If {{Term|TFH|Link}} visits the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}} in possession of the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} and claims to be a "budding hero", the {{Term|TFH|Match Master|link}} will doubt him and only match him with other players who selected "expert hero".<ref>{{Cite|You can't be serious!<br/>One who wears the Sword Master Suit must certainly be experienced! I admire your humility, young hero, but this is the Coliseum... It's where one goes to sharpen their skills.<br/>Going there is only worthwhile if you battle heroes of comparable strength.<br/>I shall consider you an expert hero and find suitable opponents for you. Relish the title, for you have earned it.|Match Master|TFH}}</ref>

Any {{Plural|TFH|Links}} who join the game through [[Download Play]] will be unable to obtain the {{Term|TFH|Sword Master Suit}}. Though the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} can appear in the {{Term|TFH|Catalog|link}}, it is impossible to collect the required {{Plural|TFH|Material}} in this mode.

==Trivia==
*The crossguard on the {{Term|TFH|Sword}} used with the {{Term|TFH|Sword Master Suit}} features a sun's face identical to the one carved into stone above {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}'s entrance. This could indicate that the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} is a symbol of [[Hytopia]]n royalty.
