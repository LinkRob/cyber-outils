'''Bouncing Fish''' is the down special move of [[Sheik]] in the {{SSB|Series}}, appearing in {{SSB4}} and {{SSBU}}.

==Overview==
Upon using the move, Sheik leaps forward with a kick, capable of inflicting a lot of knockback on whoever it hits. If Sheik kicks an opponent, she bounces backward in a high arc. It is possible for Sheik to inflict a second kick on an opponent after kicking them once.

Bouncing Fish replaces the [[Transform]] move that she and Zelda had in {{SSBM}} and {{SSBB}}, since the two characters were made entirely separate fighters in later installments.
