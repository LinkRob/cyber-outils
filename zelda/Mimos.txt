{{Term/Store|Mimos||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mimos|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Mimos}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who can be encountered on {{Term|BotW|Ebon Mountain|link}}. He constantly wanders around the broken-hearted pond at its peak.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches to {{Term|BotW|Mimos}}, he asks if he is also searching for his soulmate.<ref>{{Cite|You here to meet your soul mate too?|Mimos|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds that he is, {{Term|BotW|Mimos}} reveals that he shares the same goal.<ref>{{Cite|[That's right.] Yeah, same. There's just one problem...|Mimos|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} acts confused, {{Term|BotW|Mimos}} tells him not to be shy, since this is the only reason anyone would climb {{Term|BotW|Ebon Mountain}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Huh?] Hey, don't be shy. I'm here for the same reason, after all. Why else would anyone come all the way out here?|Mimos|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mimos}} himself has been waiting on the {{Term|BotW|Ebon Mountain|display=Mountain}} for a while.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, though, I've been waiting for a pretty long time, and...well...|Mimos|BotW}}</ref> Despite how {{Term|BotW|Link}} answers, {{Term|BotW|Mimos}} reveals that nobody else has shown up yet.<ref>{{Cite|Nobody's shown up yet.|Mimos|BotW}}</ref> He believes he is at {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond|link}}, but he wonders if he could be at the wrong place.<ref>{{Cite|I thought this was the "sweetheart's pond" everyone talks about, but maybe I'm in the wrong place?|Mimos|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mimos}} again, he asks where his soulmate is, and he theorizes that his loneliness may be a sign to put himself out there more.<ref>{{Cite|Where's that special someone I've been waiting for? Is this a sign? Should I put myself out there more?|Mimos|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} tell {{Term|BotW|Mimos}} to believe in himself, {{Term|BotW|Mimos}} thinks that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is trying to cheer him up.<ref>{{Cite|[Believe in yourself!] Are you trying to cheer me up?|Mimos|BotW}}</ref> He says that {{Term|BotW|Link}} should not worry about him, as his mother always said that he could do anything he puts his mind to.<ref name="Mom"/> He then asks about {{Term|BotW|Link}}, stating that putting all of one's faith in a legend will not attract women.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, what about you? Putting your faith in some silly legend won't make the ladies come calling!|Mimos|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells {{Term|BotW|Mimos}} to try another pond, {{Term|BotW|Mimos}} asks if he truly thinks that will help.<ref>{{Cite|[Try another pond.] Hmm. Hmmmmm... You really think that'll help?|Mimos|BotW}}</ref>

While it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimos}} will remark that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must be desperate for love, since he came out in such bad {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You must really be hungry for love, coming out here in this weather.|Mimos|BotW}}</ref>
