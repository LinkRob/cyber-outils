The '''Golden Fan''' is an item from {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Golden Fan|A Steel Trap with sharp, pointy teeth. It doesn't work anymore and is too dangerous to use.<ref name="Golden Fan">{{Cite|A Steel Trap with sharp, pointy teeth. It doesn't work anymore and is too dangerous to use.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Golden Fan is obtained from [[Tourma]] in the [[Tough Mango Plant Lab]] after collecting at least 50 [[Tough Mangoe|Tough Mangoes]] in the [[Mango Rush]] [[Mini-Game]] on Vibrant Seeds difficulty. It is one of the items required to make the [[Goldfinch]] [[Automaton]] and to complete the [[Get Rich Quick!]] [[Side Quest]].
