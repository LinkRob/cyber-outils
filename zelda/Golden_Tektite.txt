{{Term/Store|Golden Tektite|Golden Tektites|Series, TMC|plural}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 209}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Up to three {{Plural|TMC|Golden Tektite}} will appear, as well as the other {{Plural|TMC|Golden Monster|link}}, after certain {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} in {{TMC}}. Like the other {{Plural|TMC|Golden Monster}}, {{Plural|TMC|Golden Tektite}} move much faster, take much more damage to defeat, and deal much more damage to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} than standard {{Plural|TMC|Tektite|link}}. They can be found on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} and at {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}. When defeated, they drop a large sum of {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.
