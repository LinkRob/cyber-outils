Several types of flowers appear in Breath of the Wild as both {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} and ambient plant life in Hyrule.
The [[Armoranth]], [[Blue Nightshade]], [[Mighty Thistle]], [[Silent Princess]], and [[Swift Violet]], as well as the [[Cool Safflina|Cool]], [[Electric Safflina|Electric]], and [[Warm Safflina]]s are all flowers usable in [[Cooking]] and as dyes at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. Additionally the locations of [[Hearty Radish]]s and [[Big Hearty Radish]]s are revealed by their distinct pink flowers.

A flower garden is featured prominently in the Shrine quest "[[Watch Out for the Flowers]]".
