'''Piranha Plants''' are enemies in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Is this a stand for something? It says, "{{Color|LANS Pink|Piranha Plant}}"...|N/A|LANS}}</ref> Piranha Plants are from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] and made their overall debut in ''[[mariowiki:Super Mario Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]'' for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]].

==Characteristics==
Piranha Plants are immobile {{Wp|Venus Flytrap}}-like flowers that emerge from pipes at regular intervals and try to bite [[Link]] (or [[Mario]] in the [[mariowiki:Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]) as he passes by.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{LA|-}}===
Piranha Plants appear in side-scrolling areas of dungeons, particularly in the [[Bottle Grotto]] and [[Key Cavern]]. Link can simply wait for these plants to return to their pipes to safely pass or jump over them with the [[Roc's Feather]], or he can use his [[Sword]] to defeat them.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{MK8|-}} / {{MK8|D|-}}===
Piranaha Plants appear as obstacles in {{MK8}} and [[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]. Unlike in the ''[[mariowiki:Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' platforming games and {{LA|-}}, these Piranha Plants are not in pipes, but instead on the field in certain courses, lunging at any approaching racers. If a racer is hit by a Piranha Plant, their kart topples over.

In [[Hyrule Circuit]] course, Piranha Plants are replaced with their {{TLoZ|Series}} equivalent, [[Deku Baba]]s, which behave identically to them.

==Trivia==
*[[Manhandla]] in {{OoS}} is depicted as a [[Buzz Blob]]-like creature with four Piranha Plant heads on its sides. These plants spit fireballs at Link, similarly to how some Piranha Plants in the ''Super Mario Bros.'' series breathe fire. In the Japanese manual, Manhandlas are said to be "jumbo-sized" Piranha Plants.
