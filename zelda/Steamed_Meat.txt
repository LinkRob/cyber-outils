{{Term/Store|Steamed Meat|Steamed Meats|Series, BotW, TotK, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Steamed Meat}}<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Steamed Meat}}<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Steamed Meats can be {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=cooked}} by combining a piece of meat with any vegetable, herb, or flower, with the exception of {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. To make the default version, {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb|link}} must be used, as they don't provide any status effects. Eating these meals will heal several of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The method of preparation used on these meats preserves their moisture.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Depending on the vegetables, herbs, or flowers used, as well as the presence of extra ingredients, [[Link]] can make the following variations of Steamed Meat:

* '''Hearty Steamed Meat''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Steamed Meat}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When eaten, they'll restore all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Steamed Meat''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Steamed Meat}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using energizing ingredients. They can be eaten to replenish a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Steamed Meat''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Steamed Meat}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}} or other enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled when he eats them.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Steamed Meat''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Steamed Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}}, or other hasty ingredients. By eating them, Link's movement speed will be increased.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Steamed Meat''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Steamed Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}} or other spicy ingredients. They'll grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}} if they're eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Steamed Meat''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Steamed Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}} or other electric ingredients. If consumed, Link will gain one level of {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Steamed Meat''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Steamed Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}} or other mighty ingredients. Eating them will provide Link with a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Steamed Meat''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Steamed Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}} or other tough ingredients. Link can eat these meats to receive a boost to his defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Steamed Meat''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Steamed Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. By eating them, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} will be increased.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Steamed Meat''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Steamed Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This meat dish has been wrapped in fragrant leaves and steamed to preserve its moisture.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}} or other chilly ingredients. These meats will grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}} when he eats them.<ref name="Chilly"/>
