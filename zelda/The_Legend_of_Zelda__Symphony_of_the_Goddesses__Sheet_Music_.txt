'''''The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses''''' is a book of sheet music released by Alfred Publishing. It is based on the concert of the same name, and arranges the music from the show into solo piano pieces.<ref>[https://www.alfred.com/the-legend-of-zelda-symphony-of-the-goddesses/p/00-44550/ - The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses]</ref> It contains 80 pages and 49 titles. It is split into four movements: The [[Ocarina of Time]], [[The Wind Waker]], The [[Twilight Princess]], and Time of the Falling Rain.
