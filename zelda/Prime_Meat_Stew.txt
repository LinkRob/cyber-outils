{{Term/Store|Prime Meat Stew|Prime Meat Stews|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Prime Meat Stew by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either [[Raw Prime Meat]] or a [[Raw Bird Thigh]] with [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Goat Butter]], or [[Fresh Milk]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Eating them will restore some of Link's {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The savoriness of these meals comes from letting the meat simmer.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding a fifth ingredient, several different variations of Prime Meat Stew can be made:

* '''Hearty Prime Meat Stew''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hearty ingredient. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed and his maximum Hearts will be temporarily increased.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Prime Meat Stew''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an energizing ingredient. Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} can be refilled partially by eating these stews.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Prime Meat Stew''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an enduring ingredient. These stews will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Prime Meat Stew''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hasty ingredient. Consuming them will increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Prime Meat Stew''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a spicy ingredient. By eating them, Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Prime Meat Stew''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an electric ingredient. If they're eaten, they will grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Prime Meat Stew''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a mighty ingredient. Eating these will provide a boost to the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Prime Meat Stew''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a tough ingredient. Link will receive a boost to his defense by eating these.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Prime Meat Stew''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a sneaky ingredient. When these stews are eaten, they'll increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Prime Meat Stew''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Prime Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Letting the large portions of choice cuts of meat simmer brought out their savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a chilly ingredient. They will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Chilly"/>
