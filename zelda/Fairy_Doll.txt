The {{Term/Store|Fairy Doll|Fairy Dolls|Series, FPTRR}} is an item in {{FPTRR}}.<ref name="Fairies">{{Cite|My daughter Chiko just loves fairies... It's all she talks about: Fairies, fairies, fairies, fairies, fairies... In the end, I gave in and made her a Fairy Doll. She loves it! She takes that doll everywhere with her.|Chiko's Mum|FPTRR}}</ref> It is owned by [[Chiko]]. [[Chiko's Mum|Her mother]] made it for her a long time ago.<ref>{{Cite|This doll has seen better days, but the wear and tear shows that somebody loves it.|Inventory|FPTRR}}</ref><ref name="Fairies"/>

==Location and Uses==
When [[Tingle]] encounters Chiko, he learns that she has lost her precious doll to a fish.<ref>{{Cite|Boo hoo! A fish ate my fairy!|Chiko|FPTRR}}</ref> [[Chiko's Mum]] tells Tingle that Chiko was talking about a fish, saying that her daughter must have been referring to [[Hometown Prairie#Fish Rock|Fish Rock]].<ref>{{Cite|Today I think she said something about fish... She must mean that Fish Rock, north-west of Hometown Prairie...|Chiko's Mum|FPTRR}}</ref> Tingle can retrieve the Fairy Doll for her by interacting with Fish Rock, which will spit the doll out of its mouth. Once he has found the doll and given it back to Chiko, the girl will reveal the path to the first dungeon of the game, the [[Hero's Shrine]].<ref>{{Cite|Yippee!! My fairy! My fairy! You found my fairy! Thank you!! I'll show you something! Come to the town hero's statue! Come on! I'll go there first! I'll wait for you there!|Chiko|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|GREETINGS! I AM THIS TOWN'S LEGENDARY HERO... I'm going to give you a reward for finding my fairy!|Chiko|FPTRR}}</ref> Chiko tells Tingle that she found it while playing with the doll and that she was told that there is something good beneath the statue.<ref>{{Cite|Hello! I was playing with my fairy here the other day... She got a bit carried away and kicked the town hero... This came loose! Go through here and you can go under the ground! The Armourer said that there was something good under the ground... What can it be?! See you later!|Chiko|FPTRR}}</ref>

After Tingle awakens the fairy that lives in [[Port Town]], Chiko will give him the Fairy Doll as a gift.

Once Tingle returns the Fairy Doll to Chiko, Chiko's Mum will thank Tingle and reward him with some Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Can I have a word? It seems you were kind enough to find Chiko's beloved fairy doll... Thanks to you, I didn't have to make a new doll! He he! Don't worry! I know that it's not words that count... I'll give you a reward! You only get 1 chance! If you're too greedy I'm going to change my mind! How much do you want?|Chiko's Mum|FPTRR}}</ref> She will change her mind if Tingle asks for too many Rupees, however.<ref>{{Cite|[Number] rupees?! You're asking a lot! I've no time for this! Such a shame...|Chiko's Mum|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Because of her obsession with fairies, Chiko mistakes her doll for being a real fairy.<ref>{{Cite|Boo hoo! I thought my fairy was nearby... Sob! But I must have imagined it...|Chiko|FPTRR}}</ref>
