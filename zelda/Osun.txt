'''Osun''' is a character who appears in the {{MM|Himekawa}}. He makes an appearance prior to when {{Term|MM (Himekawa)|Link|link}} meets {{Term|MM (Himekawa)|Skull Kid|link}}. {{Term|MM (Himekawa)|Link}} makes a stop at a building with attributes similar to a castle, where he is supposed to give out lessons to a group of soldiers. {{Term|MM (Himekawa)|Osun}} has the grade of a master and is more calm and less naive than the other ones. {{Term|MM (Himekawa)|Link}} manages to defeat all of the soldiers during practice, while it doesn't get specified whether he defeats {{Term|MM (Himekawa)|Osun}} or not.

One of his apprentices is named {{Term|MM (Himekawa)|Vicente|link}}, who is the most stubborn and temperamental of the soldiers.
