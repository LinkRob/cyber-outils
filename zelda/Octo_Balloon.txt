==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Octo Balloon}} are {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Octorok|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, while using a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, can cook {{Plural|BotW|Octo Balloon}} with critters such as {{Plural|BotW|Lizard|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Frog|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Insect|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Fairy|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}. They can also be purchased from {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} at the [[Dueling Peaks Stable]] and [[Highland Stable]] or in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}} at the Fish Market. They can be sold to merchants or exchanged at the [[Fang and Bone]] for [[Mon]]. Octo Balloons can also be used as an ingredient for Purple [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

{{Plural|BotW|Octo Balloon}} can be dropped and used to lift items such as a {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}} or stone slabs.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can thus be used to solve simple puzzles or lift non-metallic objects. During the "{{Term|BotW|Balloon Flight|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, a young girl named {{Term|BotW|Shamae|link}} living at the {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}} requires {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use at least two {{Plural|BotW|Octo Balloon}} to lift a {{Term|BotW|Barrel}} into the sky. {{Plural|BotW|Sky Octorok|link}} use this property to lift wooden platforms, creating aerial fortresses for other enemies.
