{{Term/Store|Weather||Series, BotW}} is a mechanic in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 12}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Weather}} is indicated by a forecast in the bottom right corner of the screen. When opened to the {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} can also display the current {{Term|BotW|Weather}}. Every four in-game hours, the {{Term|BotW|Weather}} of a given area will change.

===Heat===
{{Main|Heat}}
Extreme {{Term|BotW|Heat}} is a {{Term|BotW|Temperature|link}} level. During high levels of {{Term|BotW|Heat}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will overheat and start to take damage. Its effects can be mitigated with {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}} that provide {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.

===Rain===
{{Main|Rain}}
While it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will slip when climbing, consuming {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Rain}} also puts out any exposed {{Plural|BotW|Fire|link}}.

===Sandstorm===
{{Main|Sandstorm}}
{{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm|link}} are {{Term|BotW|Weather}} events exclusive to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.

===Scorching Climate===
{{Main|Scorching Climate}}
The only {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}} and the areas surrounding {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}. In a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate}}, wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} will burn. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can avoid taking damage by drinking {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}} or wearing pieces of {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Armor|link}}.

===Snow===
{{Main|Snow}}
{{Term|BotW|Snow|link}} occurs at high elevations, such as {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.

===Temperature===
{{Main|Temperature}}
{{Term|BotW|Temperature|link}} is a fluctuating type of {{Term|BotW|Weather}} that includes {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Freezing Climate|link}}. It can be monitored from the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.

===Thunderstorm===
{{Main|Thunderstorm}}
{{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} include the effects of {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, in addition to many natural {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} strikes. These {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} strikes are attracted to equipment and {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} made of metal.

===Wind===
{{Main|Wind}}
{{Term|BotW|Wind|link}} is a gust of air that travels in a random direction. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can take advantage of {{Term|BotW|Wind}} by gliding with his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}.
