'''Birth of the Demon King''' is a [[memory]] in {{TotK}}.

== Overview ==
Picking up immediately from where the [[Sonia Is Caught by Treachery|previous memory]] left off, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] is still scrambling to wake [[Queen Sonia]]. [[Ganondorf]] then turns away and walks to the balcony, relishing at he now finally managed to secure a [[Secret Stone]] for himself. He then lifted the stone in the air, and, using his magic, artificially enlarges the stone before turning it into the Secret Stone of Darkness. The stone then levitates in the air before dropping onto Ganondorf's head. A head brooch like container then forms around Ganondorf's forehead and then the Gerudo chief gets enveloped in an eerie red aura, with Ganondorf stating his strength is surging as Zelda watches in horror.

The night sky then [[Blood Moon|turns blood red]] while Ganondorf is growling over the surging power within himself. With a final monstrous yell, Ganondorf is then consumed by a red vortex that emitted from him as Zelda watches. Once the vortex dissipates, Ganondorf has changed to have long flowing red hair and a very demonic visage. The newly-christened Demon King then turns to face Zelda and then, with a smirk, sends a wave of energy to the moon, causing various monsters to be formed throughout the kingdom. At this moment, [[King Rauru|Rauru]] enters the area and sees Sonia's body, horrified to see what became of his wife. Ganondorf then taunts him about how Rauru was too late to stop him while claiming that the king took for granted the divine power in his hands, while inferring the demonic landscape was the true potential of the Secret Stones that Rauru squandered. He then stated that Sonia's condition was a consequence of Rauru's arrogance in thinking he could control Ganondorf, with the Demon King vowing that he will die knowing that he failed. Rauru attempts to charge at Ganondorf in both grief and fury before Zelda dissuaded him, telling him that Sonia needed him right now. Ganondorf then send demonic energy towards the three, with the Zonai barely holding it back with his powers. The magical field ultimately was overwhelmed, although not before Zelda successfully used the [[Purah Pad]] to teleport all three of them out. Ganondorf was ultimately undaunted by his quarry escaping, and instead walks off while cackling heinously over his overall success.
