The {{Term/Store|River Man||Series}} is a character from {{TAoL}}.<ref name= "Bagu">{{Cite|BAGU IS MY NAME. SHOW MY NOTE TO RIVER MAN.|Bagu|TAoL}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
The River Man lives in the [[Water Town of Saria]], residing in the western part of the town and guarding the bridge to the other side of the town. He only allows inhabitants of the town through.<ref>{{Cite|ONLY TOWN FOLK MAY CROSS THIS RIVER!|River Man|TAoL}}</ref> [[Link]] has to get a note from [[Bagu]], who lives in the [[Moruge Forest]], to get permission to cross the bridge.<ref name= "Bagu"/>  Upon showing the note to the River Man, he will reveal the bridge to the young hero for him to cross.<ref>{{Cite|YOU KNOW BAGU? THEN I CAN HELP YOU CROSS.|River Man|TAoL}}</ref>

Another River Man can be seen on the other side of the bridge.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TLoZ|-}} (Valiant Comics)===
[[File:River Man (VC).png|thumb|right|150px|The River Man in the comics]]
The River Man appears in two issues of {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}} published by Valiant Comics. Here, he is the husband of [[Cassiopia]] and the father of [[Rus]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Cassiopia: '''Rus'''! My son!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5 [Day of the Triforce]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=River Man: Rus is my '''son'' as well, princess.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6 [Day of the Triforce]}}</ref>

When [[Ganon]] comes to the Water Town of Saria to gain the trust of the townsfolk, he speaks to the River Man's son, Rus, in particular, acting as a father figure. The River Man believes [[Ganon]]'s words, and thus when Link and [[Princess Zelda]] visit the town, he does not trust them.<ref>''Troublemakers! We don't want your kind!'' - River Man (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> When Ganon attacks Link, and Rus grabs the [[Magical Sword]] and refuses to give it to Ganon, the evil wizard tries to attack Rus, which makes the River Man realize his mistake.<ref>''How can we thank you for all you've done for us?'' - River Man (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref>

In a later edition, after Cassiopia returns from the [[North Palace]] where she informed Zelda that [[Lynel]]s had attacked the town, the River Man reveals to her that Rus has been gravely wounded by a [[Rope|Rope Snake's]] bite.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=This is what I tried to tell you! The child was bitten by a rope snake while trying to escape the Lynels!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5 [The Day of the Triforce]}}</ref> Desperate, Cassiopia confesses that Rus is beyond her healing powers and that he will not last beyond sundown,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He is beyond by healing powers! He will not last beyond sundown!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6 [The Day of the Triforce]}}</ref> but just then Zelda tells her that she will go to the Magic Spring and bring a fairy to cure the boy.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I  can go to the Magic Spring and bring one of the fairies back with me to  cure him! Their power to heal is even greater than yours, Cassiopia!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6 [The Day of the Triforce]}}</ref> Although Cassiopia wants her to go,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Oh, yes! Please|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6 [The Day of the Triforce]}}</ref> the River Man insists that Zelda go and destroy Ganon instead, for who  knows how many other children will die if the Prince of Darkness is not  stopped once and for all.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=NO! Today Ganon is at his weakest--!  Today you MUST destroy him! [...] Rus is my son as well, princess.  But...how many OTHER children will perish if Ganon is not stopped once  and for all?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6 [The Day of the Triforce]}}</ref> When Zelda changes her mind and chooses to get out of Death Mountain to save the River Man's son, she is ambushed by Ganon's minions, but the River Man and soon all of the villagers from Saria, who had been hiding out in a nearby cliff, begin to help Zelda defeat the monsters.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Attack, Saria people! Zelda does not fight alone this day!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=9 [The Day of the Triforce]}}</ref> Back at the town, Zelda asks [[Miff]] to cure Rus, and the River Man thanks the princess for all her help.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I...thank you for saving my son's life, princess.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=10 [The Day of the Triforce]}}</ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}
