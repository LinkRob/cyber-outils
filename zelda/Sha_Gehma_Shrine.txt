The {{Term/Store|Sha Gehma Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Sha Gehma Shrine}}<br/>Shift and Lock}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Shift and Lock''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
She Gehma Shrine is located close to the northern edge of the {{Term|BotW|N. Tabantha Snowfield|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tundra|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sha Gehma|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Sha Gehma. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Sha Gehma|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Sha Gehma Shrine features a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} trapped under a metallic block on a rail, a walkway that spirals up around the rail and two moving platforms that pass near the rail. In order to complete the Trial, Link must retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}} inside the trapped Treasure Chest.

With the Small Key, Link can open the portcullis to Sha Gehma. He will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Sha Gehma|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Sha Gehma|BotW}}</ref>

A Treasure Chest underneath the top of the spiraling walkway contains a {{Term|BotW|Royal Broadsword|link}}.
