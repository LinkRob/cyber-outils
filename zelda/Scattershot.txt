The {{Term/Store|Scattershot||Series, SS}} is an item in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 137}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Slingshot can be upgraded to the Scattershot at the [[Scrap Shop]]. This upgrade can fire several shots at once, widening the area that it can hit. Despite firing multiple projectiles, it only consumes one seed per use, as it breaks the seed into several pieces.
