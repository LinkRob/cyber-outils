The '''Bombs of Might Lv. 3''' is an item from {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
{{hatnote|”Perfected bombs optimized for fighting off the monsters of the rifts.” — [[Key Items in Echoes of Wisdom|Key Items]]<ref name="Bombs of Might Lv. 3">{{Cite|Perfected bombs optimized for fighting off the monsters of the rifts.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The [[Bombs of Might Lv. 2]] can be upgraded to Lv. 3 by using 20 [[Might Crystal|Might Crystals]] at [[Lueburry|Lueburry’s]] House, significantly increasing its damage, and giving it a larger explosive radius.
