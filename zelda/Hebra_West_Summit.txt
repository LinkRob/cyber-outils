{{Term/Store|Hebra West Summit||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hebra West Summit'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Hebra West Summit is located in the [[Hebra Mountains]] overlooking the [[Sturnida Basin]]. The Hebra West Summit is a very steep slope leading up to a flat surface at the top of the peak.

At the top of the Hebra West Summit, a [[Korok]] can be found hiding under a rock. If [[Link]] lifts this rock up, the Korok will appear to him and award him a [[Korok Seed]] in return for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
