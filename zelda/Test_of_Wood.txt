The {{Term/Store|Test of Wood||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Damia">{{Cite|But do you really think you're ready for the {{Color|BotW Red|Test of Wood}}?! Some say it's the hardest of the Korok Trials.|Damia|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood}} is referred to as the '''Test of Wood — Revisited''' in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild — The Complete Official Guide]]''.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 338}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Rules==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Damia|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}}, on the eastern edge of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}. When he speaks to {{Term|BotW|Damia}}, he reveals that he has been awaiting his arrival.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hero guy! I was told you'd be coming.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> However, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is serious about attempting the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood}}, as it is supposedly the hardest of the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial|link}}.<ref name="Damia"/> He explains that the goal of the game is to reach the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} at the back of the area while equipped with the {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But it's not complicated—all you need to do is equip this {{Color|BotW Red|Korok weapon set}} and reach the shrine at the back of the area.|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Damia}} will be excited if {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to try.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm ready.] All right. Go get 'em!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>  He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if his {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear}} breaks or is unequipped, he will be disqualified.<ref>{{Cite|You have the keep the {{Color|BotW Red|sword}}, {{Color|BotW Red|bow}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|shield}} equipped the whole time. If they break or you unequip them, you're out!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have the {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} space for all of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear}}, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will tell him that he needs to make room before they can begin.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Your inventory is full! You need to make some space for a sword, a shield, and a bow before we can get started.|Damia|BotW}}</ref>}} He will then give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Sword|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Shield|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Bow|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Damia}} reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he must reach the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} before sending him on his way.<ref>{{Cite|All you have to do is reach the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} in the back of the area. Off you go!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> This initiates "{{Term|BotW|The Test of Wood|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

While making his way through the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will face {{Plural|BotW|Keese|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Fire Keese|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Electric Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Fire Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Water Octorok|link}}. He must either avoid them with {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} or defeat them. {{Plural|BotW|Bottomless Bog|link}} are also found within and around the course. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} falls into one, he will be brought back to the start. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes through {{Term|BotW|Mido Swamp|link}} and reaches {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will end the game.<ref name="Goal">{{Cite|{{Big|Gooooooooal!}}|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Damia}} will fly up to {{Term|BotW|Link}} to congratulate him for finishing the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood}}.<ref>{{Cite|CONGRATULATIONS!<br/>You've passed the {{Color|BotW Red|Test of Wood}}!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> He then allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to continue on and enter {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well done, hero man!<br/>Happiness awaits you at the shrine.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> Before leaving, he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come find him if he wants to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood}} again.<ref>{{Cite|Oh yeah! If you ever want to do the trial again, just let me know. See ya!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> Once he flies away, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Damia}}, he offers to let him attempt the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood}} again, but this time he will be timed.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if you thought that was fun, how about taking a crack at a trial that'll really turn up the heat? Don't worry, Mr. Hero, you'll be doing the same thing as before! The only different is how long you have to do it.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} can reach the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} in under five minutes, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} promises him a reward.<ref>{{Cite|If you can make it to the goal within {{Color|BotW Red|five minutes}}, you'll get a prize!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> This time, however, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} requires a security deposit of 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} for the {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear}}.<ref name="Rupees"/> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} pays, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will prepare the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} for him.<ref>{{Cite|I'll get everything ready for you!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} fails to complete the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} in less than five minutes, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will immediately end his run.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|That's it!}} Sorry, Mr. Hero, but you didn't finish within five minutes, so you're done. Try harder next time!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> Back at the entrance, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will console {{Term|BotW|Link}} about his failure.<ref>{{Cite|Too bad, Mr. Hero. We all fail from time to time. Don't be too hard on yourself.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, when {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} within the time limit, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will once again stop him when he reaches the goal.<ref name="Goal"/> He will fly down to present {{Term|BotW|Link}} with his prize, which is a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|All right! You made it here {{Color|BotW Red|within five minutes}}, so you get a prize!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Damia}} congratulates {{Term|BotW|Link}} and expresses his hope that he likes his prize.<ref>{{Cite|Ta-daaa! Congrats, Mr. Hero! Hope you like it!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} broke his previous record, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} says that he knew he would be good at the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, a new record! Congrats, Mr. Hero! I knew you'd be good at this!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to let him know if he ever wants to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood|display=Test}} again.<ref>{{Cite|Let me know if you wanna try to break your record!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} did not beat his previous record, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will be disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|Gosh, that's too bad. You didn't beat the current record.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> He withholds {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s prize until he can beat it.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I'll hold on to the prize for now. You'll beat it next time!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

After going over {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s results, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} asks if he wants to try again, explaining that he will have to pay another 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|So, do you wanna give it another go? I'll need {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} to cover the gear.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} says that he will see him next time.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, no problem.<br/>See you next time, Mr. Swordsman!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>
