{{Term/Store|Red Picolyte|Red Picolyte|Series, TMC|plural}} are items in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 135}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TMC|Red Picolyte}} is made from {{Plural|TMC|Pico Bloom|link}} by a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We're doing cutting-edge research on Pico Blooms here.|Minish|TMC}}</ref>

In {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, if {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} cleans the dust on the carpet by using the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Beedle|link}} will eventually start selling {{Term|TMC|Picolyte|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Red Picolyte}} is immediately available once the shop opens.

Once bought, the {{Term|TMC|Red Picolyte}} is stored in an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}}; however, only one {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|display=Bottle}} of it can be carried at a time.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I'm sorry! You already have some of that kind of Picolyte!|Beedle|TMC}}</ref> When consumed, {{Term|TMC|Red Picolyte}} increases the rate of finding {{Plural|TMC|Heart|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got some {{Color|TMC Red|Red Picolyte}}! If you use it, you might find lots of hearts!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> The effects of {{Term|TMC|Red Picolyte}} wear off after approximately 30 seconds.
