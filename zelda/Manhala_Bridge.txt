{{Term/Store|Manhala Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Manhala Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Manhala Bridge is a bridge that spans the [[Regencia River]] between [[Hyrule Field]] and [[Hyrule Ridge]]. The [[Hylian]] traveler, [[Glendo]], can occasionally be seen crossing the Bridge.

In [[Master Mode]], a wooden [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Radiant Shield]] can be found atop a platform suspended by [[Sky Octorok]]s south of the Bridge. A metal Treasure Chest containing a [[Ruby]] can be found atop a platform suspended by another set of Sky Octoroks north of the Bridge.
