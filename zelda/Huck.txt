{{Term/Store|Huck||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Huck|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Huck}} can first be found talking with {{Term|BotW|Verla|link}} about {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} outside {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Howdy! ...I mean, hey. We were just talking about that {{Color|BotW Blue|beast in the sky}}.|Huck|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Rito}}, once tamed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|display=Vah Medoh}} and used it to protect {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Right. A long time ago, the Champion of Rito Village, Revali, tamed {{Color|BotW Blue|that beast}} and used it to protect the village. ...According to the elder, at least. And I believe him!|Huck|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Long ago, the village Champion, Revali, tamed that {{Color|BotW Blue|thing}} and used it to protect the village.|Huck|BotW}}</ref> He continues, saying that all males from the {{Term|BotW|Rito}} tribe are encouraged to be formidable warriors like {{Term|BotW|Revali}},<ref>{{Cite|Hm? You need something else? [Who's Revali?] Not that I was there to see it, but... Revali was one of the warriors who died in battle 100 years ago during the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Calamity}}. All Rito males are urged from birth to become honorable warriors just like Revali.|Huck|BotW}}</ref> though he considers this expectation is nearly impossible.<ref>{{Cite|No pressure, right? I mean, he was basically the greatest warrior to ever live.|Huck|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Huck}} can later be found outside of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}'s {{Term|BotW|Armor Shop|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Brazen Beak|link}}, advertising the products inside.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, sport. On your way to the mountains? If you plan to go {{Color|BotW Blue|someplace cold}}, you should consider buying some of our warm clothes.|Huck|BotW}}</ref> He advises {{Term|BotW|Link}} to purchase the {{Term|BotW|Snowquill Set|link}} if he wishes to head to the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} or go to any elevation higher than {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Someplace cold?] Go up in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}} or anywhere {{Color|BotW Blue|higher than here}}, and even experienced climbers freeze to death! But bundle up with some of our warm {{Color|BotW Blue|clothes}} and even an amateur can make it to the summit! So come on! Check out our wares!|Huck|BotW}}</ref> When asked about the {{Term|BotW|Brazen Beak}}, he pitches their gear, which is made from {{Term|BotW|Rito}} plumage.<ref>{{Cite|[Where am I?] Welcome to {{Color|BotW Blue|Brazen Beak}}, armor shop extraordinaire. There is no need to be shy, sport! We have incredible gear made from the very pride of the Rito—our plumage!|Huck|BotW}}</ref> He quickly adds that the feathers used in the {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} are not simply taken from {{Plural|BotW|Rito}} and glued to shirts, rather they are crafted from the soft, molted feathers from baby {{Plural|BotW|Rito}} as they mature.<ref>{{Cite|But it isn't as if I just tore some of my feathers and glued them to a shirt. No, we weave together only the softest, thickest baby feathers that are naturally molted with the changing of the seasons. You will not find {{Color|BotW Blue|clothes}} of this quality anywhere else! With my clothing, you'll stay comfy in the coldest of weather!|Huck|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to buy his clothing, {{Term|BotW|Huck}} becomes excited and invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to enter the store.<ref>{{Cite|Great! Come in and shop your little heart out!|Huck|BotW}}</ref>
