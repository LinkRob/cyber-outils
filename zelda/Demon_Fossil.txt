{{Term/Store|Demon Fossil|Demon Fossils|Series, ST, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 121"/>

==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
{{Plural|ST|Demon Fossil}} are a common of {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}. They can be found inside {{Plural|ST|Jar|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Treasure Chest|link}}, or under piles of leaves. As with most treasure {{Term|ST|Link|link}} can also buy them from shops, or to win them with a {{Term|ST|Prize Postcard|link}}.

{{Term|ST|Link}} can sell {{Plural|ST|Demon Fossil}} are for 50 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}} each, or trade them with {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}} as part of the cost for the {{Term|ST|Wooden Freight Car|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Skull Cannon|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Dragonhead Engine|link}} and the {{Term|ST|Dragon Passenger Car|link}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, {{Plural|TFH|Demon Fossil}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Volcano|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Blazing Trail|link}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Demon Fossil}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Blazing Trail}}''', ''"Transport the orb quickly!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Demon Fossil}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Hinox Mine|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Demon Fossil}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Hinox Mine}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Demon Fossil}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Den of Flames|link}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Demon Fossil}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Fire Temple|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Demon Fossil}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 250 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Demon Fossil}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Boomeranger|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Energy Gear|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 121"/>
