==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|White Horse}}

=== Breath of the Wild ===
White Horses are a special breed of {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} that possess pure-white coats with no mottling.<ref name="Mottling">{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} on {{Color|BotW Blue|Safula Hill}} supposedly has a beautiful pure-white coat without any mottling... They say it roams by itself, apart from other groups of horses. If you end up finding it and {{Color|BotW Blue|registering it}} at a stable, I'd really love to see it. Thanks.|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> At least two White Horses exist in {{BotW}}. The first White Horse was owned by [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] prior to the second [[Great Calamity]]. In the [[Recovered Memory]] "[[To Mount Lanayru]]", Link counseled Zelda to soothe her Horse in order to strengthen her [[Bond]] with it.<ref>{{Cite|"Be sure to take the time to soothe your mount... That's the only way it will know how you truly feel." Your advice was quite helpful—thank you. This little one and I are getting along quite well now.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Heeding his advice, Zelda equipped her White Horse with the [[Royal Bridle]] and [[Royal Saddle]] and rode with Link to the [[Sanidin Park Ruins]] atop [[Safula Hill]] to speak with him about her plans to visit the [[Spring of Wisdom]].<ref name="Gear"/> While she was visiting the Park, Toffa's grandfather saw her atop her White Horse and remembered the moment as indescribably beautiful.<ref name="Beauty">{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} that {{Color|BotW Blue|Princess Zelda}} rode. The one my grandfather told me about when I was little... The sight of the princess riding atop the {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} was supposed to be a beauty beyond words... There's no doubt about it, this {{Color|BotW Red|horse}} is the grandchild of that horse of long ago. So beautiful... So pure...|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> Following the Great Calamity, Toffa's grandfather came into possession of the Royal Bridle and Saddle, which he passed on to his grandson along with word of the White Horse that once wore them.<ref name="Pure-White"/>

At some point, Zelda's White Horse bore a foal which in turn bore a second White Horse.<ref name="Beauty"/> Following Link's [[Slumber of Restoration]], a traveler spotted the second White Horse isolated atop Safula Hill and passed word of it to Toffa at the [[Outskirt Stable]].<ref name="Pure-White"/> Wishing to see the White Horse for himself, Toffa asks Link to tame the Horse and return to him after registering it at a [[Stable]],<ref name="Pure-White"/> beginning the [[Side Quest]] "[[The Royal White Stallion]]". If Link tames the Horse and speaks to Toffa before registering it, Toffa will prompt him to register it and return immediately.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You— That {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}! You can {{Color|BotW Blue|register that beauty}} at this stable's reception desk. Go {{Color|BotW Blue|register}} the {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} and then come right back!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can {{Color|BotW Blue|register your horse}} here at the stable's reception desk! Talk to me again after you've {{Color|BotW Blue|registered}} your {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref>

Upon successfully showing Toffa the White Horse after registering it, Toffa will give Link the Royal Bridle and Saddle that its grandparent once wore as thanks for completing his request.<ref>{{Cite|What? You brought the royal family's {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}? Well I'll be... Grandpa wasn't lying! Son, you just made my life. Staying alive in this godforsaken land may just have been worth it after all. I've got a surprise for you. My grandpa handed this down to me, and now I'm handing it down to you. I present to you...the royal family's saddle and bridle! Custom fitted for the white royal horse!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> This completes "The Royal White Stallion" as Toffa automatically equips the [[Horse Gear]] to Link's Horse regardless of its Bond with its owner. After completing the Quest, Toffa will inform Link of his fantasy of riding the White Horse with a woman like Zelda.<ref>{{Cite|If I was a bit younger, I would go off on an adventure. I'd ride the {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} with a gal just like the {{Color|BotW Blue|princess}}!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref>

If the White Horse perishes before Link can register it at a Stable, it will reappear on Safula Hill the following day. Once registered, the White Horse will no longer reappear on Safula Hill if it perishes and can only be brought back by [[Malanya]] at his [[Malanya Spring|Spring]]. Additionally, after registering the White Horse at a Stable, no White Horses will subsequently appear on Safula Hill until Link releases it from a Stable.

During the special cutscene that plays after Link completes the "[[Captured Memories]]" and "[[Destroy Ganon]]" [[Main Quest]]s, Zelda has a White Horse. During the cutscene, she makes plans to ride her White Horse to [[Zora's Domain]] to investigate the source of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}'s malfunction and to bring closure with [[King Dorephan]] over his late daughter.<ref>{{Cite|We'll make our way to Zora's Domain.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Divine Beast Vah Ruta...looks like it stopped working. Let's investigate the situation.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Mipha's father... I believe he would like to hear more about her. The least we can do is visit him and offer him some closure.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> It is unknown if this is a different White Horse to the White Horse that Link can find after his Slumber of Restoration, if Link transfered ownership of the White Horse he tamed to Zelda, or if it is her original White Horse that was resurrected by the Horse God.

In addition to the Royal Bridle and Royal Saddle, Zelda's original White Horse has its mane French-Braided. According to [[Baddek]], girls often talk dreamily about princes on White Horses though he could care less about some stuffy prince as he'd prefer to have the White Horse.<ref>{{Cite|Girls often talk dreamily about princes on white horses or some odd nonsense... But I couldn't care less about some stuffy prince. Give me the white horse! Pinto, chestnut, black... Horse coats come in many different colors, but white horses take my breath away.|Baddek|BotW}}</ref>

== Other Zelda’s horse’s ==

=== Tears of the Kingdom ===
The golden horse ([[Princess Zelda|Zelda’s]] [[horse]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|TotK]]) is a one of a kind horse found while doing the Zelda’s Golden House [[Side Quest|side quest]].

=== Echoes of Wisdom ===
[[White Horse|Zelda's Horse]] is a [[horse]] that [[Impa]] has been secretly raising, intending to present it as a gift to [[Princess Zelda]]. it has a white coat and orange mane. The player is denied access to it while Zelda is wanted and wearing her disguise but once her name is cleared and the accompanying quest is completed, Zelda can summon it to her side by creating a [[Carrot]] echo or pulling out a carrot from the ground where they are available. 

Zelda's Horse is required to participate in the ranch's obstacle course, however unlike Epona and the loaner horses from Ocarina of Time there are no performances differences between Zelda's Horse and the rental ones, other than needing to use it for the obstacle courses. 

==Trivia==
*In {{OoT}}, [[Impa]] and [[Princess Zelda]] ride upon a white horse when they flee from [[Ganondorf]] after his attack on [[Hyrule Castle]]. Presumably the White Horses that appear in {{BotW|-}} are a reference to the white horse from {{OoT|-}} given their association to incarnations of [[Princess Zelda]] and the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]] who ride white horses as a symbol of their divine right.
*In Echoes of Wisdom Zelda has a white horse figure in her room that resembles the horse she eventually gains access to.
