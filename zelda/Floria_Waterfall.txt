{{Term/Store|Floria Waterfall||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Floria Waterfall|Map|SSHD}}</ref> It protects the entrance to the [[Ancient Cistern]].

==Features and Overview==
The Floria Waterfall is found outside the Water Dragon [[Faron (Dragon)|Faron]]'s hall near [[Lake Floria]]. It is first visited by [[Link]] as a shortcut to [[Faron Woods]] after Faron asks him to retrieve some [[Sacred Water]] from the [[Skyview Spring]] to heal the wounds she received from [[Ghirahim]] in an earlier battle. After Link collects the Sacred Water and heals her wounds, Faron leads him outside to Floria Waterfall to reveal the location of [[Sacred Flames#Farore's Flame|Farore's Sacred Flame]]. Faron uses her power to halt the flow of the waterfall, clearing a path to the [[Ancient Cistern]],<ref>{{Cite|Go. The sacred flame you seek lies within this place. While the waterfall has barred entrance to this place for many years, monsters have still found a way in...and inside they flourish.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref> which holds Farore's Flame.
