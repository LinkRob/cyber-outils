{{Term/Store|Great Wimbich||Series}} is a location in {{ZA}}.{{Name Ref|ZA}} It is a bustling town located in central [[Tolemac]]. It is home to many shops that sell a variety of items and [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure|Spell]]s.

== Features and Overview ==
Great Wimbich is a small town located in central Tolemac. It is situated close to the [[Meadow of Skotness]], and sits between the Skotness River and the river of the [[Forest of Torian]] to the north. As there is no proper bridge crossing the rivers, the town can only be accessed by crossing a fallen tree log near one of the houses. The town consists of six bungalows lined with hedges, of which only four can be entered. In the center is the town square, where a small market stall has been set up by the [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Town Merchant|Town Merchant]]. She sells [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] a [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Candle|Candle]] for 100 [[Rupee]]s and a [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Life Potion|Life Potion]] for 200 Rupees. She and her husband once ran a locksmith forge for [[Warbane]] the dragon, until Warbane had joined [[Ganon]].<ref>{{Cite|For thirty suns my husband and I ran the great locksmith forge for Warbane. Then Ganon came and pressed Warbane into service for his own evil plan.|Merchant|ZA}}</ref> With the forge closed, the couple were forced to become merchants in town.<ref>{{Cite|The forged was closed! Warbane built a fortified retreat of his own, somewhere off in the Forest of Canvula to the north. We were left with nothing but these trinkets to sell. Perhaps they will be of use to you in the underground. I can not give them up cheap.|Merchant|ZA}}</ref>

In the north is a street called Upper Street,<ref>{{Cite|Hello, little one! I hope I can be of help to you. Did you see my dog out on upper street?|Shopkeeper|ZA}}</ref> where three additional shops can be found. The red shop is the town's general store, and is run by the [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Shopkeeper and Knave|Shopkeeper and the Knave]]. The shopkeeper's dog and a bored, [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Lounger|lounging man]] can be found outside of the shop. The shop only sells one item, the [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Bone|Bone]], which can be given to the dog outside for the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Short Axe|Short Axe]] Spell. The white shop covered in vines is home to [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Madame Kriggle|Madame Kriggle]]'s magic shop, where she sells Zelda a variety of magic spells, including the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Rings of Fire|Rings of Fire]], [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Turquoise Ring|Turquoise Ring]], and [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Jade Amulet|Jade Amulet]] Spells. Lastly, the blue shop is the town smithy. The [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Blacksmith|Blacksmith]] inside complains that the town steals from him, despite living an honest life.<ref>{{Cite|Who goes there!? Oh, it's a young girl. Don't touch anything! People steal me blind; I know you all hate me! What did I ever do to any of you!? I give an honest bargain and I grind the coal into Rubies. I stay open late and work my fingers to the bone. Do any of you appreciate it? No!|Blacksmith|ZA}}</ref> Zelda can bring him a piece of [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Coal|Coal]] to produce the [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Diamond|Diamond]], which gives Zelda 999 Rupees. The Blacksmith also sells a Candle 100 Rupees.

A yellow house in the east is home to [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Eric and Ian|Eric and Ian]] the twins and their father. Standing next to their house is [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Yvonne|Yvonne]], a music-loving woman who gives Zelda a [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Flute|Flute]] to help her. The Flute can be played to a [[Rope|snake]] outside of town near a pond to make it combust, subsequently dropping the [[Spells of Zelda's Adventure#Pyros|Pyros]] Spell.
