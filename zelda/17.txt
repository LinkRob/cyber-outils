==Overview==
"{{Term|TMC|17}}" first plays when {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}} reveals the existence of the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} is turned to stone. After this scene, it plays at {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} and in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden|link}}. It appears as the seventeenth track on the {{Term|TMC|Phonograph|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Music House|link}}.
