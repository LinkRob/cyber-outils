{{Term/Store|Wheat Bread|Wheat Breads|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Wheat Bread}}<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Wheat Bread}}|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Wheat Breads by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Eating them will restore some of [[Link|Link's]] {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The scent of these soft, springy breads is known to be heavenly.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Adding extra ingredients to Wheat Bread can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Wheat Bread''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Wheat Bread}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. If eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Wheat Bread''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Wheat Bread}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using energizing ingredients. Consuming them will partially refill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Wheat Bread''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Wheat Bread}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled when he eats these breads.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Wheat Bread''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Wheat Bread}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using hasty ingredients. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Wheat Bread''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Wheat Bread}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using spicy ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Wheat Bread''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Wheat Bread}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using electric ingredients. One level of {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}} will be given to Link when he eats them.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Wheat Bread''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Wheat Bread}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using mighty ingredients. By eating these breads, Link's attack power will be boosted.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Wheat Bread''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Wheat Bread}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using tough ingredients. If consumed, they'll increase Link's defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Wheat Bread''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Wheat Bread}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using sneaky ingredients. Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} will receive a boost if he eats them.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Wheat Bread''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Wheat Bread}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Made with wheat from the Tabantha region, this soft, springy bread smells just heavenly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using chilly ingredients. Eating these breads will grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Chilly"/>
