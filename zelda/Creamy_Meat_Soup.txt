==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Creamy Meat Soups are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any meat, [[Fresh Milk]], [[Rock Salt]], and any vegetable, herb, or flower. Regular Creamy Meat Soups are made by using ingredients without effects or with contradicting effects. However, [[Endura Carrot]]s, [[Swift Carrot]]s, and [[Fortified Pumpkin]]s can't be used, as they will result in [[Veggie Cream Soup]]. They heal six {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, and they sell for 60 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. These soups are noted to be nutritious, containing a good amount of lightly-braised meat and vegetables.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Several different variations of Creamy Meat Soup can be made by using different ingredients.

* '''Hearty Creamy Meat Soup''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Creamy Meat Soup}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When consumed, they fully heal all of the {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and grant him additional Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/> They're worth 70 Rupees when sold.

* '''Hasty Creamy Meat Soup''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Creamy Meat Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}} or other speed-boosting ingredients. Eating one will temporarily boost Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/> They heal four of his Hearts and sell for 80 Rupees.

* '''Spicy Creamy Meat Soup''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Creamy Meat Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level cold resistance.<br/>This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}} or other spicy ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/> They restore four Hearts to Link's Life Gauge, and they sell for 60 Rupees.

* '''Electro Creamy Meat Soup''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Creamy Meat Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}} or other electricity-related ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]] for a short period of time.<ref name="Electro"/> They heal four of his Hearts, but they can also be sold for 60 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Creamy Meat Soup''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Creamy Meat Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}} or other attack-boosting ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/> They also heal four Hearts, and they sell for 60 Rupees.

* '''Tough Creamy Meat Soup''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Creamy Meat Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}} or other defensive ingredients. When consumed, these soups increase Link's defense for a time.<ref name="Tough"/> They heal four Hearts and sell for 60 Rupees.

* '''Sneaky Creamy Meat Soup''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Creamy Meat Soup}}<br/>Grants a mid-level stealth boost.<br/>This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. When eaten, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} will be boosted for a short while.<ref name="Sneaky"/> They heal four of Link's Hearts, but they also can be sold for 60 Rupees.

* '''Chilly Creamy Meat Soup''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Creamy Meat Soup}}<br/>Grants mid-level heat resistance.<br/>This nutritious soup contains serious portions of lightly-braised meat and many vegetables.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}} or other chilly ingredients. Eating these soups grants Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/> They heal four Hearts, and they sell for 60 Rupees.
