==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, Apples are a health-restoring item that can only be obtained by dashing into [[Tree]]s with the [[Pegasus Shoes]]. Apples are also the answer to one of the [[Lumberjack]]'s [[Lumberjack's Riddles|riddles]] in {{ALttP&FS}}.

==={{LA|NS|-}}===
In {{LA|NS}}, {{Plural|LANS|Apple}} are found growing on certain {{Plural|LANS|Tree}} scattered on the {{Term|LANS|Overworld|link}} of {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}. {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} can perform a {{Term|LANS|Dash Attack|link}} into these {{Plural|LANS|Tree}} to make them fall on the ground. He can then pick them up and eat them to restore some {{Plural|LANS|Heart|link}}.{{How Many}}

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, Apples are found in the stalls of [[Hyrule Castle Town#South Road|Hyrule Castle Town's South Road]]. However, [[Link]] cannot actually obtain or use them.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, Apples can recover one [[Heart]]. They can also be stored in [[Bottle]]s by catching them with the [[Bug Net|Net]]. Next to [[Link's House#A Link Between Worlds|Link's House]], an Apple tree grows Apples with every [[StreetPass]] encounter, starting with three and reaching a maximum of 20.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Apple}}
In {{BotW}}, Apples are {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} that can be used in {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}. This red fruit can be found growing on various trees throughout [[Hyrule]].<ref name="Inventory"/> They're also sold by the {{Term|BotW|General Shoppe|link}} and the travelling {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} {{Term|BotW|Gartan|link}} near {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. Regular {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} also drop {{Plural|BotW|Apple}} when defeated.

{{Plural|BotW|Apple}} can be eaten fresh to heal half of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}. They can also be made into {{Plural|BotW|Baked Apple|link}} by exposing them to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}},<ref name="Inventory"/> which heal three-quarters of a {{Term|BotW|Heart}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Baked Apple}}<br/>Direct heat has softened and sweetened this apple. Eat it to restore three quarters of a heart.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Simple foods such as apples are fine to eat raw, but roasting them on a fire makes them tastier and more nutritious.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Apple}} can be used to create {{Term|BotW|Red Dye|link}}.

During the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Riddles of Hyrule|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Walton|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to bring him something small, red, round, and sweet.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Small}}, {{Color|BotW Red|red}}, {{Color|BotW Red|round}}, {{Color|BotW Red|and sweet}}! Bring me one of this simple treat!|Walton|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} drops an {{Term|BotW|Apple}} on the leaf in front of {{Term|BotW|Walton}}, he will cheer before moving on to the next riddle.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Apple}}! It's an {{Color|BotW Red|apple}}! That's it! THAT'S IT! You got it!}}|Walton|BotW}}</ref>

== Other Appearances ==
==={{MNPTP|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:MNPTP Apple Sprite.png|left|50px]]
The fifth puzzle in the ''How to Play Picross'' series depicts an {{Term|MNPTP|Apple}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity, apples do not appear physically. However, there is the icon of an apple, which can be used to heal several hearts. Although the character can only hold three apples at first, as the character progresses, he will be able to hold 4 apples. There are also baked apples, which can be obtained after burning the character. For example, if the character gets burned by a [[Bomb Barrel]], the apples will become baked, and heal more hearts.
{{Noncanon|End}}
