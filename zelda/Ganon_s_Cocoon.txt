{{Term/Store|Ganon's Cocoon|Ganon's Cocoons|Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 77}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}} at the top of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}. During the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} sealed {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} in this state before he could completely be revived.<ref name="CaC"/> Since then, he has been dormant, though he remains on the cusp of a revival.<ref name="CaC"/>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}}, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}, who apologizes for not being able to hold {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} back any longer.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|... Link...}} Link... I'm sorry...but my power isn't strong enough... I can't hold him.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> A laser will erupt from {{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon}}, tearing through the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}}. {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} emerges from inside, breaking the weakened floor beneath himself and {{Term|BotW|Link}}.
