{{Term/Store|Keeleon|Keeleons|Series, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 187 {{Exp Game|ALBW, TFH}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Keeleon}} float in the air and attack by spitting {{Plural|ALBW|Bomb|link}} at {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}}. They cannot be defeated with the {{Term|ALBW|Sword|link}}, as they float too high for it to reach. Instead, {{Plural|ALBW|Keeleon}} must be defeated by either using the {{Term|ALBW|Fire Rod|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Ice Rod|link}}, or by throwing {{Plural|ALBW|Bomb}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, {{Plural|TFH|Keeleon}} can be attacked with the {{Term|TFH|Sword}} by having the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} form a {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}} to reach them. This may either require a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} of two or three depending on how high a {{Term|TFH|Keeleon}} is floating. They can also be reached with the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|link}}, which will stun them as well. {{Plural|TFH|Keeleon}} mainly appear in the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Ice Cavern|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Area}} of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}.

Several variants appear in the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}. In addition to {{Plural|TFH|Fire Keeleon|link}} in the {{Term|TFH|Scorching Zone|display=Scorching|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Frozen Zone|link}}, dark {{Plural|ALBW|Keeleon}} appear in the {{Term|TFH|Fortified Zone|display=Fortified|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Baneful Zone|link}}, which attack with blasts of dark magic that harm and {{Term|TFH|Curse|link}} the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}. A regular-looking {{Term|ALBW|Keeleon}} in the {{Term|TFH|Flooded Zone|link}} attacks by spitting {{Plural|TFH|Spike|link}} instead of {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}}.
