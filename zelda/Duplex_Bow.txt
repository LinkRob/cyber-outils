{{Plural|BotW|Duplex Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} used by skilled archers of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan|display=Yiga}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 171}}</ref> They can be found by only two methods. The first is to infiltrate the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}, where they are both abandoned and in use by several {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}. The second is to defeat the random {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier}} assassins that appear and take their {{Term|BotW|Duplex Bow}} from them. Unlike other {{Plural|BotW|Bow}}, {{Plural|BotW|Duplex Bow}} have been engineered to fire two {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} in order to ensure targets are stunned.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Term|BotW|Nebb|link}}, as part of the "{{Term|BotW|The Weapon Connoisseur|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, will ask {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to show him a {{Term|BotW|Duplex Bow}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|duplex bow}}? Grandpa said you can't find those around here. If you get ahold of one, let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings one back to {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} for him, {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} will be excited and thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that the {{Color|BotW Blue|duplex bow}}? You remembered our promise! Thanks! Let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you! Take this as a token of my thanks!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Duplex Bow}} will burn if they touch {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or are exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} like metal {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} do. Finally, they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
