Tauro is a character in {{TotK}}.

In-game description:
An explorer whose passion for field research is unrivaled. He lives to make big discoveries, and he spent a long time traveling solo, investigating ruins. He is also an expert in reading Zonai writing. [[Princess Zelda]] found him to be the perfect choice in leading the [[Zonai Survey Team]].
