The {{Term/Store|Great Hylian Bridge||Series, TP, TPHD}}, also known as Great Bridge of Hylia, is recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|GREAT HYLIAN BRIDGE / This large bridge spanning Lake Hylia is the largest in Hyrule and offers excellent lake views.|Sign|TP}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TPHD, LCT}}

==Features and Overview==
==={{TP|-}}===
{{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} first journeys over the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge}} in his {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|display=Wolf|link}} form, in an attempt to reach {{Term|TPHD|Lake Hylia|link}} to gather {{Term|TPHD|Tears of Light|link}} for the {{Term|TPHD|Light Spirit|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Lanayru (Spirit)|link}}. However, a {{Term|TPHD|Bulblin|link}} grunt sets the oil-coated {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}} on fire, hindering {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s progression, and he is forced to jump off of the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}} into the depths of {{Term|TPHD|Lake Hylia}} below to continue his search for the {{Term|TPHD|Tears of Light}}. Failure to jump off the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}} in time will result in an instant {{Term|TPHD|Game Over|link}}.

{{Term|TPHD|Link}} later crosses the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}} again in his human form while escorting a wagon, carrying {{Term|TPHD|Ilia|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Telma|link}}, and a mortally sick {{Term|TPHD|Ralis|link}} to {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village|link}} for medical care, as the {{Term|TPHD|Bridge of Eldin|link}} has been destroyed by {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So you say the bridge to the east has been destroyed, huh? Then our only option is to cross the bridge.|Telma|TP}}</ref> En route to the village, {{Term|TPHD|King Bulblin|link}} stands in {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s path on the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}} and challenges him to a jousting match of sorts, to redeem his defeat at the {{Term|TPHD|Bridge of Eldin}} earlier in the game. By defeating the {{Term|TPHD|Bulblin}} King the second time, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} is able to cross the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}} and continue to {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village}}.

The {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge}} is also home to both male and female species of {{Plural|TPHD|Golden Mantis|link}}. The female {{Term|TPHD|Golden Mantis|display=Mantis}} is located south of the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}} against the rock façade immediately after the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}}, while the male counterpart flies around the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}} overhang near its north side. There is also a hidden {{Term|TPHD|Treasure Chest}} with an orange {{Term|TPHD|Rupee|link}} on the northern part of the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}} which {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can reach through using the {{Term|TPHD|Clawshot|link}} on nearby {{Plural|TPHD|Vine|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
=== {{LCT|-}} ===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{LCT}}, the {{Term|LCT|Great Hylian Bridge}} appears in a Defender Mode scenario in {{Term|LCT|Stages in Link's Crossbow Training|section=Stage 3|display=Stage 3|link}}. The camera angle is fixed with a view away from the actual {{Term|LCT|Great Hylian Bridge|display=Bridge}} towards the surrounding countryside. {{Plural|LCT|Kargarok|link}} will continuously fly towards {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} and attack by spitting rocks. As with other Defender Mode stages, few additional point-giving {{Plural|LCT|Object}} can be found.
{{Noncanon|End}}
