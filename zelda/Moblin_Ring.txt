The {{Term/Store|Moblin Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Moblin Ring<br/>Become a Moblin|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Moblin Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. In {{OoS}}, it can also be found inside a cave during [[Autumn]] at the [[Woods of Winter]], just next to [[Snake's Remains]]. The [[Magnetic Glove]] is required to reach it. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it transforms [[Link]] into an [[Moblin]].<ref name="Description"/> While in this shape, Link cannot use items nor can be eaten by [[Like Like]]s. The Moblin Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
