The {{Term/Store|Tu Ka'loh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Tu Ka'loh Shrine}}<br/>Tu Ka'loh's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Tu Ka'loh's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Tu Ka'loh Shrine}} is located in a chamber at the top of the {{Term|BotW|Lomei Labyrinth Island|link}} in the northern part of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Sea|link}}. Upon reaching the {{Term|BotW|Lomei Labyrinth Island|display=Labyrinth}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is greeted by a mysterious voice that informs him that he will receive a blessing for navigating the maze.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You who have reached this point... proceed onward through the maze, and you will receive a reward.}}|Tu Ka'loh|BotW}}</ref> After this, the "{{Term|BotW|Trial of the Labyrinth|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

After successfully navigating the {{Term|BotW|Lomei Labyrinth Island}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a spiraling path leading up to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tu Ka'loh|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Tu Ka'loh. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Tu Ka'loh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Tu Ka'loh Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the {{Term|BotW|Barbarian Helm|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Tu Ka'loh}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Tu Ka'loh|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Tu Ka'loh}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Tu Ka'loh|BotW}}</ref>
