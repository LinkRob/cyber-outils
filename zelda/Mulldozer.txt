{{Term/Store|Mulldozer|Mulldozers|Series, TMC|plural}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 194">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 194}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Mulldozer}}
{{Main|Blue Mulldozer, Red Mulldozer}}
{{Plural|TMC|Mulldozer}} are bug-like monsters that harbor a skull pattern and spikes on their shell. There are two variants: {{Plural|TMC|Blue Mulldozer|link}}, which inhabit wet areas, and {{Plural|TMC|Red Mulldozer|link}}, which inhabit areas rich with plant life. {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can only encounter them while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized. They move quickly and erratically in circles after spotting a target, but can be halted by hitting them with a {{Term|TMC|Shield|link}}, leaving them vulnerable to attacks.<ref name="E 194"/>
