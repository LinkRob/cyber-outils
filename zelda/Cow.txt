'''[[wikipedia:Cattle|Cows]]''' are a recurring [[Animal]] species in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 62 {{Exp Game|Series}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|How many {{Color|MM3D Red|cows}} are there at {{Color|MM3D Red|Romani Ranch}}?|Keaton|MM3D}}</ref> They are [[Hyrule]]'s primary source of [[Milk]], and are kept in the land's various farms. A few Cows seem to strangely possess the ability to talk, such as speaking to [[Link]].

==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Cows can be found all over Hyrule. Many of these Animals can be found in various holes and caverns, but a few are also kept at [[Lon Lon Ranch]], and tended to by [[Talon]] and [[Malon]]. In this game, a free [[Empty Bottle|Bottle]] of [[Milk|Lon Lon Milk]] can be obtained by playing "[[Epona's Song]]" in front of a Cow. They will appreciate the song, and give [[Link]] milk for it, which can prove useful, as they contain two helpings and recovers five [[Heart Container|Heart Containers]].<ref>{{Cite|Moooooooooo! What a nice song... It reminds me of the pasture... That song makes me feel so good, I can produce a lot of milk! Have some of my refreshing and nutritious milk!|Cow|OoT}}</ref> In the {{MQ}} version, several additional Cows can be found [[Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly]]. These Cows serve a rather odd function of being a [[switch]], unlocking doors and dropping [[Treasure Chest|Treasure Chests]] or items when hit by the [[Slingshot]].

Link can win his own Cow by beating the [[Obstacle Course]] record at Lon Lon Ranch. The Cow will be delivered to [[Link's House|his house]] and can be found in his room.

==={{MM|-}}===
Cows are also featured in {{MM}}. They are not as commonly found around [[Termina]] as they are in Hyrule, but there are several special "Romani-Bred" Cows at [[Romani Ranch]], from which one can milk [[Chateau Romani]], a highly luxurious type of Milk.<ref>{{Cite|The special Romani-bred cows are the source of Chateau Romani, the most desired of forbidden milk! It is a mystical milk that fills you with Magic Power...|Cremia|MM}}</ref> As in {{OoT|-}}, "Epona's Song" can be played to a Cow, and they will produce regular Milk to give to Link for free.<ref>{{Cite|Moo! Nice moooosic. It reminds me of the pasture. That song just makes me want to let go and put out a lot of milk! I'll give some to you. Milk will make you very healthy.|Cow|MM}}</ref> The [[Ghost (Majora's Mask)|Ghost]]s come on the [[First Day]] to abduct the Cows from the Ranch, and the young [[Hero of Time]] is hired by [[Romani]] to fend them off.<ref>{{Cite|My older sister won't believe me... But Romani must protect the cows! Hey, Grasshopper, I'm recruiting for an assistant right now! You're a boy, won't you try?|Romani|MM}}</ref> If Link fails, the Cows are taken, and the girl with them, in which only the latter will return on the [[Second Day]] but unfortunately becomes permanently disoriented. It is unknown why the Ghosts tend to steal the Cows.

Cow figurines are also found on the counters of many shops in [[Clock Town]], and they are the subject of one of [[Keaton]]'s questions.<ref>{{Cite|Answer me this... Pick one of the three choices... How many tiny cow figurines are there in Clock Town?|Keaton|MM}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
Cows also appear in {{TMC}}, although they appear slightly differently than their past incarnations. The majority of these Cows appear in {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}} under the care of Talon and Malon, where they produce [[Milk|Lon Lon Milk]] that {{Term|TMC|Malon}} brings to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, via {{Term|TMC|Epona|link}} and cart, to be sold. Cows also serve as occasional road blocks and will only move once Link has progressed further on his quest.

{{Term|TMC|Elsie|link}}, one of the Cows at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}, can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Link}} if he is {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized. A Cow also appears in the "At Lon Lon Ranch" {{Term|TMC|Figurine|link}}.

==Trivia==

* The term "cow", by technical means, are mature female cattle. Therefore, the [[Hateno Cow|Hateno Cows]] or [[Water Buffalo]] seen in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'' are bulls, which are mature male cattle.
* In the Japanese version of {{OoT}} and {{MM}} as well as their remakes, Cows are seen with large [[wikipedia:Nose_ring_(animal)|nose rings]]. In real-life, they are used to help wean cattle to prevent suckling. It is unknown why they are removed in the international release, but such rings remain on products based on Cows.
* Despite Cows are normally heard by [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Cow.wav mooing], in {{MM|-}}, when one is speaking to Link after playing "[[Epona's Song]]", Honey's [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_Honey_Laugh1.wav laugh] from the [[Honey and Darling]] duo can be heard.
* Similar to [[Horse|horses]], the ''Zelda'' cows in the [[Nintendo 64]] games appear to be slightly larger than their real-world counterparts, as their shoulder height stands about as high as Adult Link, who, according to the corrected graph in the pool in the [[Lakeside Laboratory]] via the [[Iron Boots]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'', is 1.7m (5'7") tall.
** Although real cows are generally 4.5 to 5 feet at the shoulder, some exceptional cases exist. The tallest cow on record, named Blossom, recognized in 2014 by Guinness World Records, stood a staggering 190cm (6.2ft) in height.
