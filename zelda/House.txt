{{Term/Store|House|Houses|Series|plural}} are recurring objects in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Overview==
{{Plural|Series|House}} appear in most games in {{TLoZ|Series}}, particularly in towns and villages. While most are unremarkable and go unnamed, there are some notable exceptions throughout the series.

==Appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Main|Link's House}}
In {{ALttP}}, both {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} and his {{Term|ALttP|Link's Uncle|link|display=Uncle}} live in {{Term|ALttP|Link's House|link}}.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Main|Sale's House o' Bananas}}
In {{LA}}, {{Term|LANS|Sale|link}} runs a small store at {{Term|LANS|Toronbo Shores|link}} called {{Term|LANS|Sale's House o' Bananas|link}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Main|House of Skulltula, Link's House}}
In {{OoT}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Link's House|link}} is located in {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Forest|link}}, and the {{Term|OoT3D|House of Skulltula|link}} is located in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Music Box House, Oceanside Spider House, Spirit House, Swamp Spider House}}
In {{MM}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Music Box House|link}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Spirit House|link}} are located in {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Canyon|link}}. Additionally, there are two mini-{{Plural|MM3D|Dungeon|link}} that are {{Plural|MM3D|House}} &mdash; the {{Term|MM3D|Oceanside Spider House|link}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Coast|link}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Swamp Spider House|link}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Southern Swamp|link}}.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Auction House, Link's House}}
In {{TWWHD}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link's House|link}} is located on {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}}, and the {{Term|TWWHD|Auction House|link}} is located on {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Main|Moving House}}
In {{FSA}}, the {{Term|FSA|Moving House|link}} is found in the {{Term|FSA|Death Mountain Foothills|link}}, where it is used to defeat the {{Plural|FSA|Deadrock|link}} in the area.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Barrel House, Hyrule Town, Link's House, Percy's House, Stockwell's House, Talon and Malon's House}}
In {{TMC}}, a handful of {{Plural|TMC|House}} are found outside {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}; {{Term|TMC|Link's House|link}} is in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Percy's House|link}} is in the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's House|link}} is at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|link}} is at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Eenie|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Meenie|link}} also have a {{Term|TMC|House}} in the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}}, and there are a number of small mushrooms converted into {{Plural|TMC|House}} by the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}, concentrated in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}. Other {{Plural|TMC|House}} in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|display=Village}} are formed from objects such as {{Plural|TMC|Barrel|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Jar|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} is the location of several {{Plural|TMC|House}}, including:
*{{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Girl's House|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Guy's House|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's House|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Music House|link}}
*{{Term|TMC|Stranger's House|link}}

There are also a {{Term|TMC|Vacant House|link}} (and a second one if {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Bremor|link}}) that can be leased to the {{Plural|TMC|Oracle|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Main|Jovani's House, Link's House, Rusl, Uli & Colin's House}}
In {{TP}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link's House|link}} is just outside the main area of {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}}, where {{Term|TPHD|Rusl, Uli & Colin's House|link}} is located. {{Term|TPHD|Jovani's House|link}} is located in {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle Town|link}}, and is only accessible while {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} is transformed into {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Main|Link and Niko's House}}
In {{ST}}, {{Term|ST|Link and Niko's House|link}} is located in {{Term|ST|Aboda Village|link}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Main|House of Gales, Link's House, Vacant House}}
In {{ALBW}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link's House|link}} is located in the same location as it is in {{ALttP}}. {{Term|ALBW|Ravio|link}} moves in and sets up his shop after {{Term|ALBW|Link}} initially fails to defeat {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} equivalent of {{Term|ALBW|Link's House}} is the {{Term|ALBW|Vacant House|link}}.

Additionally, the {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales|link}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}, accessible after {{Term|ALBW|Link}} completes the {{Term|ALBW|Eastern Palace|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Main|Link's House}}
In {{BotW}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can purchase a {{Term|BotW|House}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} that is being demolished by the {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction Company|link}}. This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Hylian Homeowner|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to display gear and purchase installations for his home.
