'''Takashi Nagasako''' is a Japanese voice actor who is affiliated with [[Arts Vision]]. Nagasako is the most recurring voice actor for [[Ganon|Ganon(dorf)]].

==Biography==
Outside the ''Zelda'' series, Nagasako provided the voice of Donkey Kong from the ''Donkey Kong'' and ''Mario'' series since 2004.
