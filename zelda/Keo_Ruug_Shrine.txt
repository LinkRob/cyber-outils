The {{Term/Store|Keo Ruug Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Keo Ruug Shrine}}<br/>Fateful Stars}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Fateful Stars''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
[[File:BotW Keo Ruug Shrine Exterior.png|The exterior of the {{Term|BotW|Keo Ruug Shrine}}|thumb|left]]
The {{Term|BotW|Keo Ruug Shrine}} is located in the middle of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}} on the east side of the {{Term|BotW|Great Deku Tree|link}}, northeast of the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|link}}.<ref name="Piggyback 199">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|page= 199|publisher= Piggyback|edition= Expanded Edition|year= 2018}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Keo Ruug Shrine}} is available as soon as {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Keo Ruug|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Keo Ruug. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Keo Ruug|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Inside the {{Term|BotW|Keo Ruug Shrine}}, there are a series of five {{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} in four rows, each associated with a constellation shape. On the far wall opposite the entrance, there is a large mural depicting these constellations multiple times. A {{Term|BotW|Sign|link}} near the entrance provides a hint for {{Term|BotW|Link}}, instructing him to look to the stars for guidance.<ref>{{Cite|Look to the stars for guidance. The constellations are the key.|Sign|BotW}}</ref> This is a riddle indicating that each time a constellation appears, a corresponding hole inside one of the five pits must be filled with an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}.

Each {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket}} is associated with a number of constellations, with tall blue lights on each side of the sockets indicating the amount of constellations that appear. Thus, a constellation appearing only once must be placed in the socket with a single blue light adjacent to it. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has properly placed each {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}, a large portcullis separating him from {{Term|BotW|Keo Ruug}} will permanently rise, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to access the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}.

When Link speaks to Keo Ruug, he will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Keo Ruug|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, Keo Ruug will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Keo Ruug|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} is hidden in a chamber on the back side of the wall separating the front and back portions of the Shrine. A mural above it depicts another set of constellations with an alternate order that must be followed in order to release the portcullis protecting the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}. This {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Claymore|link}}.
