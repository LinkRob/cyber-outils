The {{Term/Store|Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors||Series, OoT}} are a group of singing [[frog]]s in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|Look at us, we're all huge frogs!! We are the world famous Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors!! Rrrribbit!!|Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors|OoT}}</ref>

==Biography==
During the past, [[Link]] can find the five frogs swimming near a log in [[Zora's River]].<ref>{{Cite|Some frogs are looking at you from underwater...|N/A|OoT}}</ref> When the hero stands on the log, the frogs hop out of the water and onto it. When Link plays his [[Ocarina]], the frogs hop to the corresponding note. The frogs will award the hero 50 [[Rupee]]s for every song he plays for them. If Link plays "[[Zelda's Lullaby]]," "[[Epona's Song]]," "[[Saria's Song]]," the "[[Sun's Song]]," or the "[[Song of Time]]," one of the frogs will grow in size.<ref>{{Cite|Young lad, you play the Ocarina well... Mmmmm...! That melody is so fine...ribbit! We all should practice it, ribbit! Take Rupees as a souvenir! If you come up with another nice melody, please drop by and play it...Ribbit ribbit!|Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors|OoT}}</ref> If Link plays the "[[Song of Storms]]," the frogs will instead reward him with a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|Wow, that melody is so cool! Ribbit! Siiiiingin' in the raaaaaain, Oh what a feeling! Rrrrrribbbit!! Please take this as a token of our froggish gratitude, ribbit! All right. See you, ribbit!|Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors|OoT}}</ref>

After all the frogs have fully grown, they will ask Link to help them eat butterflies by playing notes on his Ocarina.<ref>{{Cite|Make us jump so we can eat the bugs flying above us, ribbit! OK? Then, ready, set, go!|Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors|OoT}}</ref> When the game begins, a butterfly will appear over one of the frogs. Link must then play the note on his Ocarina which corresponds to that frog in order to make it jump and eat the butterfly. If Link plays a wrong note or waits too long, he will have to restart the song. If Link manages to complete it, they will reward him with another Piece of Heart.<ref>{{Cite|Young boy, you did grrreat! Perrrrrfect! We are stuffed, ribbit! Would you take this token of our gratitude?|Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors|OoT}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*In {{OoT3D}}, the frogs' reference to "{{wp|Singin' in the Rain (song)|Singin' in the Rain}}" was changed to "singin' in the pond,"<ref>{{Cite|Wow, that melody is so cool! Ribbit! Siiiiingin' in the pooooond! Oh, what a feeling! Rrrrrribbbit!|Fabulous Five Froggish Tenors|OoT3D}}</ref> possibly to avoid copyright infringement.
