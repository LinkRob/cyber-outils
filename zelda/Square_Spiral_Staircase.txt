The {{Term/Store|Square Spiral Staircase||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 238}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Square Spiral Staircase}} is located within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. It connects two different levels of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors|link}}. Its lower exit leads towards the {{Term|BotW|Library|link}}, while following its upper exit will bring {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Reception Room|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard|link}} stored {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Square Spiral Staircase}} in case of fights breaking out.<ref name="CaC"/> In the years since the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Square Spiral Staircase|display=Staircase}} has partially collapsed.<ref name="CaC"/> 

Several {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Square Spiral Staircase}}. By breaking them, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to find certain {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} and other supplies.
