'''Magic''', also known as '''Spells''',{{Fact|TAoL}} are abilities in {{TAoL}}. In most cases, the effect of the magic will wear off as soon as Link leaves to another room or the scenery changes.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Magic wears off if the display changes to another.|game= TAoL|page= 43}}</ref> Each [[Magic Container]] holds 16 Magic Points, and each magic uses a different amount of Magic Points. Link will learn these eight magical powers from [[Old Man|old men]] in different towns, typically as a reward for a specific task.

==Description and effect==
===Shield===
{{Main|Shield (Magic)}}
[[File:TAoL Shield (Magic) Artwork.png|75px|left]]
The '''Shield''' magic is an ability that increases Link's defensive abilities, reducing the damage he takes by half.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Raises Link's life level temporarily. Reduces damage inflicted by the enemy by half.|game= TAoL|page= 43}}</ref>
{{Clear}}

===Jump===
{{Main|Jump}}
[[File:TAoL Jump Artwork.png|75px|left]]The '''Jump''' magic makes Link [[jump]] twice as high as before, allowing access to areas that would be otherwise unreachable.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Using this magic increases Link's jumping power. He can even climb to heights he could not reach with a normal jump.|game= TAoL|page= 43}}</ref> He can learn it by claiming the stolen [[Trophy]] in the [[Tantari Desert]] and taking it to a woman in the [[Town of Ruto]]. This magic is required to get to [[Midoro Palace]], and will surely help the young hero get to other places as well. The magic also increases the width span of Link's jumps, which assists in jumping over deadly pits.
{{Clear}}

===Life===
{{Main|Life}}
[[File:TAoL Life Artwork 2.png|75px|left]]
The '''Life''' magic recovers some of Link's life energy, and is one of the spells that uses the most Magic Points.
{{Clear}}

===Fairy===
{{Main|Fairy (Magic)}}
[[File:TAoL Fairy Artwork.png|75px|left]]
The '''Fairy''' magic is a unique ability that temporarily turns Link into a [[Fairy]], allowing him to fly and get into high places, cross dangerous pits of water or lava, and get through small holes.
{{Clear}}

===Fire===
{{Main|Fire (Magic)}}
[[File:TAoL Fire Artwork.png|75px|left]]
The '''Fire''' allows [[Link]] to shoot fireballs out of his [[Sword]] from a distance.
{{Clear}}

===Reflect===
{{Main|Reflect}}
[[File:TAoL Reflect Artwork.png|75px|left]]
The '''Reflect''' magic increases the strength of Link's shield.
{{Clear}}

===Spell===
{{Main|Spell}}
[[File:TAoL Spell Artwork.png|75px|left]]
The Spell is used to open up a building at the edge of {{Term|TAoL|Kasuto|link}} and turn several enemies into [[Bot]]s.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= It is also said that it'll make the enemy tremble in fear.|game= TAoL|page= 44}}</ref>
{{clear}}

===Thunder===
{{Main|Thunder}}
[[File:TAoL Thunder Artwork.png|75px|left]]
The '''Thunder''' magic is used to cause [[Thunderbird]]'s face to appear, which is its weak spot.
