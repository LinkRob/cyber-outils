==Location and Uses==
The Broken Sword becomes the [[Noble Sword]] when Link goes with [[Patch]] to repair it at the [[Restoration Wall]]. If Link has already received the Noble Sword via a [[secret]], it will become the [[:en:Master_Sword#Oracle_of_Ages|Master Sword]] instead. Link receives it from the [[Old Zora]] in a cave in the [[Coast of No Return]] in exchange for the [[Sea Ukulele]].<ref>{{Cite|That Ukulele has the spirit of the sea in it! That smell! That sound!
