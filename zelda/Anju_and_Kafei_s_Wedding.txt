The '''Wedding''' of {{Term|MM3D|Anju}} and {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}} is an event that takes place during the {{Term|MM3D|New Day|display=ending credits|link}} in {{MM}}. It acts as the finale to the chain of events in which {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} becomes involved in an attempt to {{Term|MM3D|Reuniting Kafei and Anju|display=reunite|link}} the estranged lovers. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can only interact with this scene if the player uses cheating devices.

{{Term|MM3D|Anju}} and {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}} made their promise to marry when they were children, long before the impending {{Term|MM3D|Terminan Apocalypse|display=apocalypse|link}} that overshadows their planned wedding.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't her bridal dress lovely? We promised each other when we were children that we'd marry on the day of the Carnival of Time.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> They vowed to exchange masks based on the {{Term|MM3D|Sun's Mask|display=sun|link}} and the {{Term|MM3D|Moon's Mask|display=moon|link}} at the {{Term|MM3D|Carnival of Time|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What a familiar scent. Long, long ago...Yes. We were still young. We made a promise... Didn't we? The masks of the sun and the moon... We were to exchange them on the day of the Carnival of Time...|Anju|MM}}</ref> That promise is threatened when the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}}, wearing {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Mask (Item)|display=Majora's Mask|link}}, turns {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}} into a child. When {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}} is on his way to the {{Term|MM3D|Great Fairy|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|North Clock Town|link}} to implore her for aid, {{Term|MM3D|Sakon|link}} steals his wedding mask and {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}} goes into hiding in the back room of the {{Term|MM3D|Curiosity Shop|link}} until he can recover it.<ref>{{Cite|I was turned into this by a strange imp wearing a mask... But I'm not hiding because I look this way. When I was turned into this, I went to see the Great Fairy in the shrine near the North Gate... But on the way, my precious mask--a wedding ceremony mask--was stolen from me by some prancing man with a grinning face! [...] I know Anju is worried...but I can't
go out yet. I made a promise to her that I would bring the wedding mask and greet her.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Link}} acts as a liaison between {{Term|MM3D|Anju}} and her fiance and helps {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}} recover his mask in {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Canyon|link}}, so that they can finally be reunited just before the dawn of the fourth day.

In fact, their act of exchanging masks in the employee's only room of the {{Term|MM3D|Stock Pot Inn|link}} with {{Term|MM3D|Link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Tatl|link}} as their witnesses, creating the {{Term|MM3D|Couple's Mask|link}}, is when they exchange their oaths and officially become a couple.<ref>{{Cite|Let us exchange the promised masks. We have exchanged our oaths and have become a couple. You are our witnesses. Please accept this mask.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> The following wedding, therefore, appears to be purely ceremonial in nature, and not necessarily a binding event. Whether the exchanging of masks is supposed to take place during the wedding, and did not only because of their extenuating circumstances, is uncertain.

The wedding takes place in western {{Term|MM3D|Termina Field}}, facing the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay|display=ocean|link}}. {{Term|MM3D|Anju's Mother|link}}, her {{Term|MM3D|Anju's Grandmother|display=grandmother|link}}, and the {{Term|MM3D|display=Man from Curiosity Shop|Man from the Curiosity Shop|link}} stand on {{Term|MM3D|Anju}}'s right during the ceremony. {{Term|MM3D|Mayor Dotour|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Madame Aroma|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Mutoh|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Cremia|link}}, and {{Term|MM3D|Captain Viscen|link}} stand on her left. The final guest, {{Term|MM3D|Tingle|link}}, watches the ceremony from his balloon and throws confetti as they wed.

As the cutscene ensues, {{Term|MM3D|Anju}} walks down the aisle created by the guests approaching the camera, which is presumably showing {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}}'s perspective. She offers her left hand to him, and the camera pans away to {{Term|MM3D|Tingle}}, followed by a distant shot of the wedding.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|MM3D|Kafei}} is never shown in his adult form, presumably because the developers decided not to create a model for him. Similarly, {{Term|MM3D|Madame Aroma}} is seen floating above the ground in a sitting position has she is never shown standing otherwise. This is also probably the reason why {{Term|MM3D|Mayor Dotour}} is shown sitting in his office chair.
