The '''Death Mountain Shop''' is a {{Term|ALttP|Light World|link}} shop in {{ALttP}}.{{Name Ref|ALttP}} It is identical in appearance to the {{Term|ALttP|Lake Hylia Shop}}, consisting of a small cave-like enclosure. Like its {{Term|ALttP|Death Mountain}} counterpart, it sells the same wares as the {{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village Shop}} and all three of them are run by identical {{Plural|ALttP|Hooded Shopkeeper}}.

The shop is one of the more obscure locations in the game, and like the other shops in the {{Term|ALttP|Light World}}, it need never be visited in order to complete the game. In order to reach it, {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} must possess the {{Term|ALttP|Magic Hammer|link}} and travel due East from the {{Term|ALttP|Tower of Hera|link}}. After crossing the bridge and pounding down the pegs on the other side, {{Term|ALttP|Link}} will be able to explore a previously inaccessible portion of the mountain. To the East lies a {{Term|ALttP|Magical Warp Tile|link}} that will transport him to the {{Term|ALttP|Turtle Rock|link}} area of the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}}. However, if he instead goes South, he will find a series of caves. All the caves at the base of this section of the mountain are obstructed by large stones which require the {{Term|ALttP|Titan's Mitt|link}} to lift, except for one. If {{Term|ALttP|Link}} enters that cave and bears left, then he will come across a cracked wall which if bombed will lead him to the {{Term|ALttP|Death Mountain Shop}}.
