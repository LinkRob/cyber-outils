{{Term/Store|Defend the Sands!||HWAoC}} is a level 34 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defend the Sands!|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Defend the Sands!}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} except {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, and 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} while protecting an Allied Stronghold. A few {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} are scattered around the map, but more spawn as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} moves throughout the map. There is a pair of one {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} to the right and left of the Allied Stronghold's entrance, with the {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} assaulting the Stronghold almost immediately. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}} stay in the same spot, surrounded by a small group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} approaches {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} spawn around the map in addition to the large group around him, and two more {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} spawn on the sides of the Allied Stronghold. All of these {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} advance toward the Allied Stronghold unless interrupted by the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and can spawn again in the same way if the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} leaves {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} and confronts him again. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} is defeated.
