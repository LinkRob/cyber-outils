The {{Term/Store|Golden Scale||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Golden Scale}} was referred to as the '''Gold Scale''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Gold Scale}}<br/>The power of the Gold Scale allows its owner to dive just as deeply as any Zora.|retrieved= January 22, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160308195651/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/g.jsp#GoldScale|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In Ocarina of Time, if Adult Link catches a fish at least four pounds greater than the initial record in the Fishing Hole, he will be rewarded with the Golden Scale. This item enables Link to dive even further than the [[Silver Scale]] allowed.
