The {{Term/Store|Sahasra Slope Skyview Tower||Series, TotK}} is location in {{TotK}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TotK|Sahasra Slope Skyview Tower}} is a {{Term|TotK|Skyview Tower|link}} located in {{Term|TotK|Sahasra Slope|link}}. It is positioned on top of a hill, overlooking above {{Term|TotK|Nabi Lake|link}}.

When {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} first approaches the Skyview Tower, he finds {{Term|TotK|Billson|link}} at the door. Upon talking to him, he says that he needs to repair the Skyview Tower but the door won't budge open.<ref>{{Cite|I heard that the terminal at this Skyview Tower needed repairs, so I came to fix it. But... The door won't open. I have no clue why. The door isn't locked, but it won't budge, no matter how hard I push or pull.|Billson|TotK}}</ref> He then rambled on going mushroom picking, to which he pointed out to a {{Term|TotK|cave|link}} being below the tower.<ref>{{Cite|Yep. There are <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">caves at the bottom of the hill</font>.|Billson|TotK}}</ref> True to Billson's word, Link is unable to open the doors.

To open the door to the tower, Link will have to follow Billson's cave tip; finding an entrance around the ridges leading to the Sahasra Slope Cave. There will be a distinguishable flathead surface in the ceiling that can be {{Term|TotK|Ascend|link|display=ascended}} into, which will take Link directly inside the tower. Even when inside, when Link tries to open the door, it still won't budge open; Billson will then see Link, much to his surprise.{{Fact}}<!--Triggered after trying to open the door from the inside--> Seeing that Link's already inside, he suggests him to find out why the doors are shut tight.{{Fact}}<!--Triggered after trying to open the door from the inside, *twice*--> Looking around, there are two {{Plural|TotK|Long Stick|link}} apparently jamming the doors closed. Once they're removed, Link is able to open the door for Billson.

After Billson gains entry, he manages to fix the terminal,<ref>{{Cite|It wasn't a big problem, so a little oil cleared it right up.|Billson|TotK}}</ref> allowing the tower to be used. Once Link uses the tower to scan the surroundings, he gains {{Term|TotK|map|link}} information for {{Term|TotK|North Necluda|link}} in both the surface and the {{Term|TotK|sky|link}}. The tower can also be used to reach the {{Term|TotK|North Necluda Sky Archipelago|link}}.
