The {{Term/Store|Library Key||Series, OoA}} is a quest item found in {{OoA}} that serves to open [[Eyeglass Island Library]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 129}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoA|Library Key}} is found in {{Term|OoA|King Zora's Palace|link}}. After curing [[King Zora]]'s deathly illness with the [[Magic Potion]] in the past, the king gives [[Link]] the Library key in the present. King Zora hopes that Link can research the cause of the filthy seawater at the Eyeglass Library,<ref>{{Cite|...Perhaps you can research why the seawater is so filthy.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> which has left [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]] near death.<ref>{{Cite|Anyhow, the filthy seawater has left Jabu-Jabu near death.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> 

Upon entering the library, Link discovers that, long ago, [[Fairy Powder]] was kept in the back of the library, but it had since been taken away.<ref>{{Cite|Long ago, Fairy Powder was kept in this very library. However, at some point in the past, someone took it away, and now none remains.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> In the back of the library, Link finds an [[Old Man]] who gives Link the [[Book of Seals]].<ref>{{Cite|I give to you the Book of Seals!|Old Man|OoA}}</ref> Link returns to the library in the past and gets the Fairy Powder from the back of the library. To get to the Fairy Powder, Link must solve a puzzle using the Book of Seals from the present. 

With the Fairy Powder, Link lifts the curse on the [[Queen Fairy]],<ref>{{Cite|You brought me Fairy Powder! Now I can return to normal!|Queen Fairy|OoA}}</ref> who will then cleanse the seas.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, young one! Now I can cleanse the seas of evil!|Queen Fairy|OoA}}</ref> This allows Link to enter [[Jabu-Jabu's Belly]], the seventh dungeon of the game.
