The {{Term/Store|Yiga Clan Gear|Yiga Clan Gear|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 171}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Gear}} is a set of weaponry created and used by the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Vicious Sickle}}<br/>A grim weapon favored by the Yiga. The half-moon shape of the blade allows for the rapid delivery of fatal wounds and serves as a symbol of their terror. Its durability is low.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Demon Carver}}<br/>This lethal weapon is forged by the Yiga. Its unique shape facilitates the sound dispatching of any target and strikes fear into the hearts of all who see it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Windcleaver">{{Cite|{{Big|Windcleaver}}<br/>This sword is favored by high-ranking members of the Yiga. When wielded by a proficient fighter, its unique shape cleaves the very wind and creates a vacuum.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Duplex Bow">{{Cite|{{Big|Duplex Bow}}<br/>A bow favored by the skilled archers of the Yiga Clan. It's been engineered to fire two arrows at once to ensure your target comes to a swift and none-too-pleasant halt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They can be obtained by defeating members of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=Clan}}, both the assassins sent to kill {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} and the fighters within the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} like the {{Term|BotW|Windcleaver|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Duplex Bow|link}} are given to high-ranking or talented members.<ref name="Windcleaver"/><ref name="Duplex Bow"/>

Aside from the {{Term|BotW|Duplex Bow}}, all {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Gear}} is made out of metal. This makes them dangerous to equip during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, as they will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}}, and they can also conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They are able to be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, will sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
