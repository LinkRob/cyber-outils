{{Term/Store|Fool's Ore||Series, OoS}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref> also known as '''Traded Ore''',<ref>{{Cite|Traded Ore  Not as useful as a feather.|Subscreen|OoS}}</ref> is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{OoS}}. It is one of several types of {{Term|OoS|Ore|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
When {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} first enters {{Term|OoS|Subrosia|link}} through the {{Term|OoS|Portal|link}} on {{Term|OoS|Mt. Cucco|link}}, the {{Plural|OoS|Strange Brother|link}} take {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather|link}} from him and "trade" him the {{Term|OoS|Fool's Ore|link}},<ref>{{Cite|What's that? It's so weird. I'll trade you this ore for it! Thanks!|Strange Brother|OoS|link}}</ref> which replaces the {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather}} in the {{Term|OoS|Inventory|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got {{Color|OoS Red|Fool's Ore!}} But that's no good! Get your feather back!|N/A|OoS}}</ref> Link uses {{Term|OoS|Stealth|link}} to follow the brothers to spot in the {{Term|OoS|Treasure Grove|link}} where they bury {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather}}. Link uses his {{Term|OoS|Shovel|link}} to retrieve {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather}}, at which point the {{Term|OoS|Fool's Ore}} is permanently lost from the {{Term|OoS|Inventory|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got your feather back! Now return the ore.|N/A|OoS}}</ref>

{{Term|OoS|Link}} can swing the {{Term|OoS|Fool's Ore}} like a {{Term|OoS|Sword|link}}. It deals more damage than even the {{Term|OoS|Master Sword|link}} or {{Term|OoS|Biggoron's Sword|link}}.<ref>https://youtu.be/Dd9y7P8U5co</ref>
