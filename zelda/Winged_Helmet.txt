The '''Winged Helmet''' is an item in {{TFoE}}.{{Name Ref|TFoE}} It is used to jump higher and longer distances.

==Location and Uses==
The Winged Helmet is obtained in the {{Term|TFoE|Militron (Stage)|link}} area after defeating {{Term|TFoE|Militron (Boss)|link}}. It is used to enable [[Link]] to jump higher and longer distances, at the cost of 5 {{Plural|TFoE|Rupee|link}} per use. The helmet's use makes it helpful in jumping over large gaps difficult to cross. It is notably required to jump over a deep chasm in [[Shipwreck Cliff]], as the gap is too wide for regular jumps to cross.

The item's name comes from the word "wing" meaning appendages that let you jump higher and longer distances, most likely in reference to how the item lets you jump higher and longer distances.
