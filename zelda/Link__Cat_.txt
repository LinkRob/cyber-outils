{{Term/Store|Link||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a [[Cat]] character in {{TP}}.{{Name Ref|TP}}{{Note|In reality, the {{Term|TPHD|Cat}}'s name matches whatever the player chooses as the hero {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s name.}}

==Biography==
Link is the pet [[Cat]] of [[Sera]], the owner of [[Sera's Sundries]], the only shop in [[Ordon Village]]. The Cat himself is named after the hero [[Link]].

After being scolded for stealing the [[Fish]] that was going to be the family's dinner, Link runs off causing Sera to become so worried that she's unable to run the shop<ref>{{Cite|Ohhhh... I'm terribly sorry...but my mind is so filled with thoughts of my little cat that I simply can't deal with costumers right now... Ohhh... If I had known this was going to happen, I would have let my little kitty eat fish to his heart's content... Link, m'dear... I'm sorry, but would you leave me be? I'll serve you milk later.|Sera|TP}}</ref>. Link the hero can find Link the Cat behind [[Jaggle]]'s house, attempting to catch fish from the water. However Link is unable to catch the pet animal as he runs away whenever the hero approaches.

Once Link has caught two [[fish]], Link the Cat will run over and steal one of them, and then run home with it. Inside he is greeted by half a {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link|display=bottle}} of [[milk]] and an overjoyed Sera, who is so relieved by his return that she gives the hero the other half of the milk on the house.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh, just look... Isn't he so cute, the way he laps up that milk? Say, m'dear, why don't you have some, too! I'm in a good mood, so it's on the house today! Oh, don't worry, you don't have to return the empty bottle, m'dear. You can reuse it--you know, put something else in it. Recycling is a very important thing, m'dear!|Sera|TP}}</ref>

When the Hero of Twilight later returns to Ordon Village in his [[Wolf Link|wolf form]], he can talk to Link, who will tell him how to sneak up behind [[Hanch]] and surprise him, thereby avoiding being attacked by his [[Hawk]].

For the rest of the game, whenever the player enters Sera's shop, Link the Cat will always be on the counter, eating or lapping up something.

== Trivia ==

* Link the Cat is one of few creatures in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] who shares the name of the [[Link|main protagonist]]. The others are the [[Link (Goron)|Goron named Link]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', and [[Abe]] and [[Rose]]'s [[Link (Pig)|pet Pig]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]''.
* Unlike the [[Goron]] and [[Pig]] Links, it is not known in-game why Sera named her pet Cat after the hero.
* Ironically, in ''The Wind Waker'', during the [[Side Quest|sidequest]] in [[Windfall Island]] where the hero Link quietly follows [[Mila]], whenever she says "''...Who is that?''", he [http://noproblo.dayjo.org/ZeldaSounds/WW_New/WW_Link_Meow.wav meows like a Cat], technically mimicking the pet counterpart.
