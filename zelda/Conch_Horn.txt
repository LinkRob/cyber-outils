{{Term/Store|Conch Horn|Conch Horns|Series, LA, LADX, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 120"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Conch Horn}}: Use this on the search screen to temporarily reduce damage taken.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
The Conch Horn is one of the eight [[Instruments of the Sirens]] needed to awaken the [[Wind Fish]].<ref name="E 120"/> It can be found at the end of [[Bottle Grotto]] after defeating its boss, [[Genie]].<ref name="E 120"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, after defeating a {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Hinox|link}} inside {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Bottle Grotto|link}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} exits the dungeon with the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Conch Horn}} in hand. After gathering all eight {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Instrument of the Sirens|link}}, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Conch Horn}} plays the "{{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ballad of the Wind Fish|link}}" alongside the other instruments while {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} plays his {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ocarina|link}} in oder to open the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Wind Fish's Egg|link}}.

==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWL Conch Horn Sprite.png|left|40px]]
In {{HW|L}}, the Conch Horn appears as an [[Item Card]] in the [[Koholint Island Map]] of [[Adventure Mode]], which is included in the {{LA|-}} DLC Pack. It reduces the damage the Warrior takes.
{{Noncanon|End}}
