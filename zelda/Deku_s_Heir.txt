{{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Deku's Heir||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a character in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Deku's Heir is waiting for you!|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref> He is the heir of the {{Term|FPTRR|Great Deku Tree|link}}.<ref name="Polluted"/>

==Biography==
When {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} first meets {{Term|FPTRR|Deku's Heir}}, the {{Term|FPTRR|Great Deku Tree|link}} reveals that his Heir has grown sickly because of the recent pollution caused by {{Plural|FPTRR|Human|link}}.<ref name="Polluted"/> In order to save his {{Term|FPTRR|Deku's Heir|display=Heir}}, the {{Term|FPTRR|Great Deku Tree}} requests that {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} enters the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Temple|link}}, located at the base of the {{Term|FPTRR|Great Deku Tree}}'s trunk, and retrieve the {{Term|FPTRR|Life Dew|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I have a request. Beneath the earth there is a special water called Life Dew. My heir desperately needs it. Will you help us?|Deku Tree|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I am grateful to you. Without further ado, I will open the entrance to the Deku Temple.|Deku Tree|FPTRR}}</ref>

Upon clearing {{Term|FPTRR|Bana Bana|link}} from the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Temple}} and retrieving the {{Term|FPTRR|Life Dew}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can use it to heal {{Term|FPTRR|Deku's Heir}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've recovered! I feel great! I must protect the forest!|Deku's Heir|FPTRR}}</ref> As thanks for saving his {{Term|FPTRR|Deku's Heir|display=Heir}}, the {{Term|FPTRR|Great Deku Tree}} will reward {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} with up to 6,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! That is a relief! I am very grateful to you! Now... The time has arrived... Thank...you!|Deku Tree|FPTRR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh no! This won't do! I almost forgot! Don't look so sad! I will reward you properly!|Deku Tree|FPTRR}}</ref> After rewarding {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}, the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Tree}} will wither and die, leaving {{Term|FPTRR|Deku's Heir}} to protect the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Forest|link}} in his place.<ref>{{Cite|So, once again I will wither... Farewell!|Deku Tree|FPTRR}}</ref> Despite inheriting the responsibility, {{Term|FPTRR|Deku's Heir}} will be unsure how to carry out his duties.<ref>{{Cite|I must protect the forest! But how?|Deku's Heir|FPTRR}}</ref>
