The {{Term/Store|Charge Ring||Series, OoA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Charge Ring<br/>Spin Attack charges quickly|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Charge Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it allows [[Link]] to charge [[Spin Attack]]s faster.<ref name="Description"/> The Charge Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
