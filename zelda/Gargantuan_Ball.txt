{{Term/Store|Gargantuan Ball|Gargantuan Balls|Series, ALttP, FSA, ALBW, TFH, BotW|plural}} are recurring traps in {{TLoZ|Series}}. {{Name Ref|ALttP, FSA, ALBW, TFH, BotW}} {{Plural|Series|Gargantuan Ball}} also have a smaller variant, called {{Plural|Series|Ball|link}}, that also make an appearance in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Stub|ALttP}}

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, {{Plural|FSA|Gargantuan Ball}} will sometimes fall down from above when certain {{Plural|FSA|Switch|link}} are hit. They will then chase the {{Plural|FSA|Link|link}} and constantly hound them even down to lower floors. The only way to escape the giant metal traps is to pass through a small passageway and return to a higher floor.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Gargantuan Ball}} appear similarly as they did in {{ALttP|-}}. They are found rolling around in {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon|link}} and will cause damage to {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} whenever he touches one.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, {{Plural|TFH|Gargantuan Ball}} appear solely in the third stage of {{Term|TFH|Training Ground|link}}, where they roll down a narrow pathway surrounded by {{Term|TFH|Lava|link}}, preventing the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} from crossing. One {{Term|TFH|Link}} must ride a {{Term|TFH|Totem Armos|link}} against the flow of {{Plural|TFH|Gargantuan Ball}}, allowing the other {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to follow safely behind.
