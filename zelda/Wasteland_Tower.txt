The {{Term/Store|Wasteland Tower||Series, BotW}} is a [[Sheikah Tower]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wasteland Tower|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Wasteland Tower is located in the east [[Gerudo Highlands]]. It sits in the middle of a [[Bottomless Bog]] on the northernmost peak of [[Spectacle Rock]]. To begin climbing the Tower, Link must avoid sinking in the swamp surrounding it by finding a way across. He can do so by [[Paraglider|Paragliding]] from above or using [[Cryonis]]. Activating the Tower will update Link's [[Sheikah Slate]] with a [[Sheikah Slate#Map|Map]] of the {{Term|BotW|Wasteland Tower Region|link}}, including the eastern section of the Gerudo Highlands and all accessible parts of the [[Gerudo Desert]].
