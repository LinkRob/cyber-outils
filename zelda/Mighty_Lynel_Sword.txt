{{Compendium/Extract|Mighty Lynel Sword}}
{{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Sword}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that are used by {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, though, they are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}}. Because most {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}} scale up as {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} defeats {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Sword}} may become more scarce as the game progresses. However, the {{Term|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel}} in the {{Term|BotW|First Gatehouse|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} does not scale up, making it a permanent source of {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Sword}}. Skilled {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} are able to draw this {{Term|BotW|Mighty Lynel Sword|display=Sword}} in passing and still damage their foes.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a collection of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that are created by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

As metallic {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Sword}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. However, they can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, which is useful since they sink if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Sword}} will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
