{{Term/Store|Cremia||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}} and the [[Termina|Terminan]] adult counterpart of [[Malon]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} is the look-alike older sister of {{Term|MM3D|Romani|link}}. She manages {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|link}} in place of her deceased father. She is a close friend of the {{Term|MM3D|Stock Pot Inn|link}} keeper, {{Term|MM3D|Anju|link}},<ref>{{Cite|In town...I have a friend. Her name's Anju...|Cremia|MM}}</ref> and it is revealed by {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} that she has a crush on {{Term|MM3D|Kafei|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Anju}}'s fiancée.<ref name="Crush">{{Cite|Romani knows... My sister, Cremia, has someone in town she likes... But that person is supposed to get married the day of the carnival. It's hard for my sister... Going into town...|Romani|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref> She treats {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} like a child, a fact that annoys her younger sister. Since the death of her father, however, everything seems to have been going wrong for {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} around the {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|display=Ranch}}, likely due to the {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers|link}}, who has a rival business of producing [[Milk]] (though Cremia's quality is [[Chateau Romani|far superior]]).<ref name="Father" /> {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} is known to be a kindhearted and caring older sister, and is almost constantly working, trying to provide for herself and {{Term|MM3D|Romani}}. Despite this, she does not believe {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} about the {{Plural|MM3D|Ghost (Majora's Mask)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Romani was practicing... For tonight...Tonight... They are coming... They... They come at night every year when the carnival gets closer. They come riding in a bright, shining ball, and many of them come down...to the barn... My older sister won't believe me... But Romani must protect the cows!|Romani|MM}}</ref>

If [[Link]] helped {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} save the {{Plural|MM3D|Cow}} from the {{Plural|MM3D|Ghost (Majora's Mask)}}, {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} will invite the young [[Hero of Time]] to accompany her on the ride to deliver the Milk to the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar|link}} in the night of the [[Second Day]], and claims to be lonely, as she welcomes company. As the {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers}} have blocked {{Term|MM3D|Milk Road}}, they ride down the {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Track|link}} instead. As the {{Plural|MM3D|Gorman Brothers|display=Brothers}} intend to steal the special beverage, Link must prevent them from doing so. After they deliver the {{Term|MM3D|Milk}}, {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} will reward him the {{Term|MM3D|Romani's Mask|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|By doing one good deed, a child becomes an adult. That mask is only given to a limited number of adult customers. It's proof of membership. I now acknowledge you as being an adult!|Cremia|MM}}</ref> If the quest is completed again after obtaining this {{Term|MM3D|Romani's Mask|display=Mask}}, {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} will give Link either a {{Term|MM3D|Gold Rupee|link}} or an affectionate hug (something very hardly seen in the main series and is popular among fans).<ref>{{Cite|You did it! You helped Cremia! {{Typo|You feel all warm and fuzzy! inside!|You feel all warm and fuzzy inside!}} Sigh... You could get used to this!|N/A|MM}}</ref> The decision amounts to a random choice with a 50/50 probability for either outcome.<ref>{{Cite Web|author= punk7890 |published= September 20, 2018 |retrieved= July 5, 2019 |url= https://twitter.com/punk_7890/status/1043023261846519810 |site= Twitter}}</ref> If Link fails to defend her Milk, she will dejectedly return home, lamenting that she is a failure of a rancher. 

If Link fails to prevent the Ghosts from abducting Romani and their Cows in the [[First Day]], Cremia will become heartbroken, due to not only her business being destroyed from the loss of her specially-bred Cows but also her younger sister being in an unhealthy, disoriented state, along with regret for not listening to her about the dangerous spirits.

Even though some of the citizens of {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}} take refuge at Romani Ranch on the night of the {{Term|MM3D|Final Day|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} reveals that she knows even they are not safe from the {{Term|MM3D|Moon|link}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|display=Ranch}} (as with the rest of Termina), and sadly accepts the [[Terminan Apocalypse|fate that awaits all of the residents]].<ref>{{Cite|Actually... I know... We're not safe here, either... That's how life goes, I guess. There are some things in life that you can't change no matter how hard you try.|Cremia|MM}}</ref> Knowing that they likely will not survive, {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} grants her younger sister, who does not know of the fate that will befall them, the wish to drink {{Term|MM3D|Chateau Romani|link}} for the first time, something she had been denying {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} since she was not an adult yet.<ref>{{Cite|It's "Chateau Romani." It's the first time I get to drink it! Until now, my sister always said "Wait until you're an adult." But why now?|Romani|MM}}</ref> Furthermore, she acknowledges {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} as an adult and even promises to make her a {{Term|MM3D|Mask}} to officially signify her younger sister's status as a grown-up.<ref>{{Cite|You've become an adult now, Romani...I see it in you. I'm acknowledging it. [...] Well, yes, I'll make one for you...|Cremia|MM}}</ref> Before heading back to their home, seemingly for one final time, {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} asks her sister to sleep with her in her {{Term|MM3D|Bed}}, to which {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} agrees.<ref>{{Cite|Sleep with me in my bed tonight, OK, Romani?|Cremia|MM}}</ref>

During the ending sequence, {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} can be seen cheering on her younger sister, who is practicing her {{Term|MM3D|Bow|link}}. {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} is later seen witnessing {{Term|MM3D|Anju}}'s wedding with {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}}.

==Trivia==
*There is a typo in the original [[Nintendo 64]] version of {{MM}} when {{Term|MM|Cremia}} gives [[Link]] a hug.<ref>{{Cite|You feel all warm and fuzzy! inside!|N/A|MM}}</ref>
*In the demo version {{MM|-}}, when {{Term|MM|Link}} shows {{Term|MM|Kafei's Mask|link}} to {{Term|MM|Cremia}}, she mentions {{Term|MM|Madame Aroma|link}} is her aunt.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Ah... You. Did my aunt put you to work? Kafei isn't here... |author= Liliana |published= February 26, 2011 |retrieved= April 12, 2015 |url= https://tcrf.net/Proto:The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Majora%27s_Mask/Preview_Demo/Text_differences |title= Proto:The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask/Preview Demo/Text differences|site= The Cutting Room Floor |type=}}</ref> This would have made her cousins with her crush, [[Kafei]].<ref name="Crush"/> The final release has {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} refer to "some middle-aged woman" instead,<ref>{{Cite|Ah... You. Did some middle-aged woman put you to work? Kafei isn't here...|Cremia|MM}}</ref> albeit in a context where "some middle-aged woman" could not possibly be taken to mean anyone other than {{Term|MM3D|Madame Aroma}}.
**The change was likely made in order to fix a mistranslation. In Japan, as well as other East Asian cultures, the word for "aunt" can be used to refer to any middle-aged woman even if they are not related to the speaker. 
*Like her [[Hyrulean]] counterpart [[Malon]] (as well as [[Talon]]), Cremia alongside her sister Romani wear [[Bowser]] brooches, whose character is the main antagonist of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. This is due to their models being reused.[[File:MM Cremia hugging Link (modified angle).gif|thumb|257x257px|Modified gameplay of {{MM|-}} showing a clear angle of Link being hugged by Cremia]]
*Cremia is the only character to outright give Link a display of affection in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] other than [[Princess Zelda]].
**Cremia's heartwarming hug towards Link in ''Majora's Mask,'' surprisingly, was not originally planned for the game. According to [[Yoshiaki Koizumi|Yoshiaki Kolzumi]], he stated "''That was born out of a mistake. When we were setting up Link's position, we put him too close so his face got lodged into Cremia. When we saw that, we immediately wrote up a way to include it in the game. The script director and I thought this would be any boy's dream.''" 
**As genuine affection are extremely rare in the franchise (or most [[Nintendo]] games in general), Cremia's hug for Link is the second instance in the core series since {{TAoL}} where the main protagonist is embraced, by Zelda. Another would not appear again until {{ST}} before and after the final battle against [[Malladus]] (though the princess can be seen giving a him kiss on his cheek at the ending of the [[Linked Game|linked ''Oracle'']] game).
**Cremia's hug could also possibly be a reference to Malon being one of the [[Hero of Time]]'s several love interests, alongside Princess Zelda, [[Princess Ruto]], and [[Saria]].
*Though the {{Term|MM3D|Triforce|link}} itself is not worshiped in [[Termina]], {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} has a belt buckle with an emblem of the golden relic in the center, due to her model being reused from adult Malon from {{OoT}}.
*{{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} and her younger sister Romani were supposed to appear in the {{MM|Himekawa}} but were cut as she could not fit them in the story. This omission is acknowledged in a side note at the end of the M Mollymanga.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= We couldn't work them into the story no matter how we tried, so we're putting them here... Cremia and Romani.|book= MM (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= }}</ref>
