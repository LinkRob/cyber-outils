{{Plural|BotW|Golden Bow}} are {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}-made {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} popular for their ornamentation.<ref name="Inventory" /> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} uses these {{Plural|BotW|Bow}}, the camera zooms in much more than normal to provide extra range, making it easier to hit far away targets.<ref name="Inventory" /> They are commonly found in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, particularly around {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, as well as in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Golden Bow}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. In addition, they will not burn if they touch {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or if they are exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
