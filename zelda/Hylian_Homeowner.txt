"{{Term/Store|Hylian Homeowner||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hylian Homeowner}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Hateno Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Behind the {{Term|BotW|Link's House|link|display=abandoned house}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Bolson|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Need something from me?|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} asks what he is doing, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} explains that he is demolishing the building, as nobody wants to live in it.<ref>{{Cite|[What're you doing?] In case it's not obvious, I'm demolishing a vacant house. Time are rough. Not a lot of buyers, y'know? So the villagers decided it was best to just tear it down.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can offer to buy the house, though {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} is suspicious of his ability to do so.<ref>{{Cite|[I can buy it!] {{Small|Whoa, get a load of young moneybags... Just wants to "buy" it, huh?}}|Bolson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Bolson}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the house has been vacant for years,<ref>{{Cite|Okey-doo... Let me lay it out for you. See this house? Been vacant for years.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> and the people of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}} decided to pay {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction|link}} 50,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to take it down.<ref>{{Cite|So the villagers had a meeting, argued, voted, and decided to tear it down. The demolition costs, with associated fees, and other such details...come in at {{Color|BotW Red|50,000 rupees}}.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, to buy the house, {{Term|BotW|Link}} would need to pay that amount.<ref>{{Cite|So when you say you want to "buy" it, you're talking about {{Color|BotW Red|50,000 rupees}}... You got that much on you?|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> However, he decides to lower the cost to 3,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} and 30 bundles of {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|... ...Alright. All right, look. Here's the deal! Just for you! I'll cut you a special price of {{Color|BotW Red|3,000 rupees}}. {{Big|BUT IN RETURN!}} I'm going to need you to bring me {{Color|BotW Red|30 wood bundles}}. Building materials... You know how it is.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to pay the 50,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} will be surprised and remarks that he reminds him of himself in the past.<ref>{{Cite|Well! Who would have thought?! ...Little go-getter, aren't you? Heh. Reminds me of me back in the day.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> However, he still offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} the deal.}} Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} is aware of the terms,<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, you understand the terms? Then get to it. And show me what you're built out of. ...That's a construction joke.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} stops {{Term|BotW|Hudson|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Karson|link|display=Karson's}} demolition work.<ref>{{Cite|Hudson! Karson! Stop what you're doing!|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has gathered the {{Term|BotW|Wood}} bundles, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}} to deliver them.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll pay your price!] ♪Okey-doo.♪ I'll grab them from you.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> He realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is serious about the house and agrees to accept the 3,000 {{Term|BotW|Rupee}} payment.<ref>{{Cite|All I needed to see... Good-faith wood. You're obviously serious about this, and now I know it. So, as promised, I'll sell you this old house for the low price of {{Color|BotW Red|3,000 rupees}}. Once you've got the money together, come grab me.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to pay the 3,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} is at first shocked to hear that he is not lying.<ref>{{Cite|Seriously?! You're really gonna give me {{Color|BotW Red|3,000 rupees}}? Like, with three zeroes? You aren't just posturing?|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asserts his willingness, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} will accept the payment.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll shell out!] ♪Now way!♪ Talk about studly! ♪I'll just take that off your hands.♪|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> He thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his investment and compliments his ability to spend so much money at his age.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for the sweet investment. Spending so much money on ANYTHING at your age... It's just so...studly!|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> As a housewarming gift, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to keep the {{Term|BotW|Weapon Mount|link}} inside that never sold.<ref>{{Cite|There's a {{Color|BotW Blue|weapon mount}} in there that never sold... Consider it a housewarming gift. Hope you get some use out of it.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Bolson}} remarks that he has a {{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link|display=job}} lined up in {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Hudson|link}},<ref>{{Cite|So...next-job time, baby! What'd I have lined up again? Oh, yeah, {{Color|BotW Red|that's}} what's next. I was going to send Hudson up {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} way to clear some land!|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> which involves clearing some land.<ref>{{Cite|I asked Hudson to clear some land in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} region.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants to say anything to {{Term|BotW|Hudson}}, he should speak to him before he leaves.<ref>{{Cite|Yup... Business expansion, y'know. I'll probably have him head out soon. If you want to say anything to Hudson, better catch him before he leaves.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} has left, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} suggests that it is time for the two of them to relax.<ref>{{Cite|So the both of us sort of hit a personal milestone, huh? Time to kick back and get LEISURELY, baby!|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> However, from {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s face, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} realizes that he thinks they should continue to work on the house,<ref>{{Cite|...Though you look a bit judgemental for such a fine moment. You're looking at me like we should both keep working at it.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> specifically addressing the fact that the house is empty.<ref>{{Cite|Heh! You don't even have to say it. "Oh, wah, I just bought a house and it's totally empty and depressing!" Oh, sure, that's you to a T. I see it in your eyes. Yup, I was just like you, about a lifetime ago.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> He offers to take care of additions for 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|So tell you what, lemme help by taking care of furniture, or the exteriror, or anything really, for {{Color|BotW Red|5,000 rupees}}. Actually, no, scratch that. For you? Let's start at {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}}.|Bolson|BotW}}</ref>
