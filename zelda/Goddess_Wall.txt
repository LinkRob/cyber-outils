{{Term/Store|Goddess Wall|Goddess Walls|Series, SS, SSHD|plural}} are objects in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Drawing on Goddess Walls|Sheikah Stone|SS}}</ref> They were created by the goddess Hylia for the hero, Link.<ref>{{Cite|There is no mistaking it! This symbol must mark one of the {{Color|SS Red|goddess walls}} left for the hero by the goddess!|Gorko|SS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SS|Goddess Wall}} are sacred murals that are used to generate various [[Item]]s. {{Plural|SS|Goddess Wall}} are initially invisible, and can only be found by playing the [[Goddess's Harp]] near a wall where three {{Plural|SS|Blessed Butterfly}} congregate. Once revealed, {{Plural|SS|Goddess Wall}} appear as ornately carved indigo rectangles, with the [[Goddess Crest]] in its center.

After charging a [[Skyward Strike]], [[Link]] is able to draw on the {{Term|SS|Goddess Wall}} with his [[Sword]]. Depending on the shape Link draws, {{Plural|SS|Goddess Wall}} will release a different reward. Once used, a {{Term|SS|Goddess Wall}} goes inactive until restarting the game.

After obtaining [[Farore's Courage]], [[Gorko]] will ask Link to draw shapes on a {{Term|SS|Goddess Wall}} in the [[Sealed Grounds]]. If Link does this successfully, he will be rewarded with a [[Piece of Heart]]. Once he draws all the shapes, he will receive a {{Term|SS|Gold Rupee|link}}. In all other cases, Gorko will give Link a {{Term|SS|Red Rupee|link}} or a [[Treasure]].
