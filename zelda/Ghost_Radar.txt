The {{Term/Store|Ghost Radar||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an object mentioned in {{MM}}.<ref name="Father">{{Cite|Oh! Hmmmm! My Ghost Radar is reacting fiercely.|Pamela's Father|MM}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Ghost Radar is a device used by [[Pamela's Father]] in his studies of the supernatural in [[Ikana Canyon]].<ref>{{Cite|I am the famed academic who researches fairies, ghosts and similar so-called supernatural phenomena.|Pamela's Father|MM}}</ref> He only ever mentions the Radar going off if [[Link]] wears any of his [[Transformation#Majora's Mask|Transformation Masks]] in his presence.<ref name="Father"/> The Radar's response is different than usual when detecting Link, however, reading him as both similar to a ghost and different from one at the same time.<ref>{{Cite|But... This is minutely different from its usual response. This is a verrrry intriguing response! Hmmm...You seem to be both similar to a ghost and different from one at the same time...|Pamela's Father|MM}}</ref> This response leads Pamela's Father to ask Link if he can study him.<ref>{{Cite|This is verrry intriguing. Could you remain here a while and permit me to research you?|Pamela's Father|MM}}</ref>

The strange response that occurs as a result of Link's presence in his [[Deku Link|Deku]], [[Goron Link|Goron]], or [[Zora Link|Zora]] forms is undoubtedly a result of him being alive, and yet wearing a mask containing a spirit at the same time.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Goron Mask! This mask contains the spirit of a proud Goron hero!|N/A|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Zora Mask! This mask contains the spirit of a legendary guitarist!|N/A|MM}}</ref>
