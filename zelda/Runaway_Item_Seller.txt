The {{Term/Store|Runaway Item Seller||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Biography==
The Runaway Item Seller ran the Item Shop in [[Kakariko Village]], until he spent almost every [[Rupee]] he had to buy an [[Apple]] from the [[Street Merchant]].<ref>{{Cite|I bought an apple from the street merchant outside my shop. It looked so tasty, I gave him almost every Rupee I had.|Runaway Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And it was the best apple EVER. Worth every Rupee for sure.|Runaway Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref> After that, his [[Item Seller|wife]] got mad at him,<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, my wife didn't see things that way. She really let me have it. And wow, how my wife can yell when she thinks she's right.|Runaway Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref> so he decided to run away and hide in a cave.<ref>{{Cite|[...] When I took a lunch break, I just sort of didn't come back.|Runaway Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref> He can be found north of the swamp and south of [[Link's House]], left of the northern entrance of the area. The entrance to his cave is completely concealed, and can only be accessed by placing a [[Bomb]] between the two rocks that sit in front of it. Unlike most caves of this type, there is no crack or boulder indicating its location. He is surprised that Link managed to get inside.<ref>{{Cite|I sealed up the opening nice and tight. There wasn't even a crack, so how'd you get in here?|Runaway Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref>

He requests that [[Link]] bring him a [[Scoot Fruit]] so he can escape to a [[Dungeon]].<ref>{{Cite|I've been thinking that I've got to hide myself away until I really sort it all out. Maybe in some secluded dungeon! But I know those places are dangerous without the right gear. Like a Scoot Fruit! Yeah, I need one of those before I can go. Please get me one!|Runaway Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref> If Link brings him the fruit, he will be rewarded with a Silver Rupee.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you so much! Here-take this as thanks.|Runaway Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref>
