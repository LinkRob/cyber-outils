{{Term/Store|Black Lynel|Black Lynels|Series, ALBW, CoH|plural}} are recurring enemies and {{Plural|Series|Sub-Boss|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 190 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Black Lynel|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Plural|ALBW|Black Lynel}} appear only in the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower|link}} in {{ALBW}}, on the 49th floor of the Advanced level. They have the same amount of health and deal the same damage as regular {{Plural|ALBW|Lynel}}, but have the ability to spit single fireballs in addition to breathing streams of fire.

==Other Appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
[[File:CoH Black Lynel Sprite.png|left]]
{{Plural|CoH|Black Lynel}} appear as sub-bosses in {{CoH}}, where they act as the strongest variety of {{Term|CoH|Lynel|link}}. Like their weaker counterparts, they move to the beat of the music on every second beat, always crouching before hopping onto an adjacent tile, and drawing their bows when {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} walks within their line of sight. Once their bows drawn, they will fire {{Plural|CoH|Arrow|link}} on the following beat, dealing damage to any heroes or enemies that may be in the way. Afterwards, they will reposition themselves until they are able to take aim again.  These {{Plural|CoH|Arrow}} can pass through {{Plural|CoH|Torch|link}} and catch fire.

They can be found in caves, dungeons, outside the entrance of the {{Term|CoH|Lost Swamp|link}}, and within the {{Term|CoH|Grassland|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Forest|link}} biomes across {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}.
{{Ambig|End}}
