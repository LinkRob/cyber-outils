==Characteristics==
==={{ST|-}}===
Stagnox is a giant green and brown stag beetle with a purple abdomen. It is first fought in the {{Term|ST|Forest Temple|link}}, and is fought again in the [[Hyrule Castle Town#Spirit Tracks|Castle Town]] mini-game [[Take 'Em All On!]].

At the start of the fight, Stagnox will be on the ground and will try to ram [[Link]] with its horn. He must run to its backside and use the [[Whirlwind]] to remove the gas which will allow him to attack its [[Critical Point|vulnerable backside]]. Eventually, it will take flight and release [[Blastworm]]s, creatures that act as [[Bomb]]s when attacked. Link must hit them with his [[Sword]] and use the Whirlwind to blow one of the Blastworms at Stagnox when it charges at him to flip it over. This will leave it once again vulnerable to attack.

==Nomenclature==
Stagnox's name is derived from "stag", as in a stag beetle, upon which it is based. The "nox"-part may be derived from noxious, meaning toxic, which may refer to the poison gas Stagnox's rear end is emitting. The name "Kornules" used in some localizations comes from "Corno" (horn) and Hercules Beetle.
