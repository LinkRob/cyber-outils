==Features and Overview==
The Coral Reef is a [[General Store]] operated by [[Cleff]] that is located inside [[Marot Mart]] in [[Zora's Domain]].<ref>{{Cite|[The general store?] Our general store has been in business for over 100 years. It has a long and honorable history. The shopkeep, Cleff, is really serious. But his younger sister, Tona, is head over fins for Prince Sidon! Kee hee!|Marot|BotW}}</ref>
