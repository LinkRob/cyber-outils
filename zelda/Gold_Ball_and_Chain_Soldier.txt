==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
Gold Ball and Chain Troopers appear as [[Enemy|enemies]] in [[Hyrule Castle Tower]]. Unlike the [[Grey Ball and Chain Soldier|grey version]], they are only regular [[Enemy|enemies]], and not [[Sub-Boss|Sub-Bosses]], so therefore, it is not mandatory to kill them. They appear in duos, and other than that, act exactly like the [[Grey Ball and Chain Soldier|grey version]].{{Stub|ALttP}}

==={{ALBW|-}}===
Gold Ball and Chain Soldiers appear as enemies in the fourth dungeon, [[Hyrule Castle#A Link Between Worlds|Hyrule Castle]].
