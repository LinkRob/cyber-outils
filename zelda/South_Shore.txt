The {{Term/Store|South Shore||Series, OoA}} is a location in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 255}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The South Shore is a beach region of [[Labrynna]], home to only two inhabitants: [[Rafton]], a sailing enthusiast,<ref>{{Cite|I am Rafton. My dream is to make fine rafts that can sail the seas!|Rafton|OoA}}</ref> and [[Cheval]], an inventor of water-based items,<ref>{{Cite|I am Cheval. I am researching items for use in water. Before I die, I want to finish my work on flippers for swimming and rope that won't decay in water.|Cheval|OoA}}</ref> in the past. The South Shore is located south of the [[Black Tower]] and east of [[Fairy Woods]]. In both time periods, the South Shore is mainly a transitional region, that exists primarily to connect the lanscapes of [[Lynna Village]] in the past, [[Lynna City]] in the present (both to the north), and the southern sea regions during both time periods. In the present, South Shore's landscape and region splits in two regions, the lower half taking the same name, and the upper half going by '''South Lynna'''.

The main sidequest of {{OoA}} takes Link deep into this region: Rafton hands Link the [[Sea Ukulele]]<ref>{{Cite|In exchange... I know! How's this? Lazily playing guitar while gazing at the moon is so nice.|Rafton|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Sea Ukulele! It has a sweet, sea scent!|N/A|OoA}}</ref> after trading over the [[Magic Oar]] Link had received from [[Maple]], [[Syrup]]'s assistant, to Rafton's surprise: he had hinted at needing an oar if he was ever to enter himself in a raft race, earlier in the game.<ref>{{Cite|I'm thinking of entering a raft race, but I haven't been able to find a decent oar to race with..|Rafton|OoA}}</ref> These two items prolong and continue the very sidequest that ends at the [[Noble Sword]], and like this region as a whole, only serve as transitional pieces.

In a [[Linked Game]], a woman appears in South Lynna next to the [[Ember Tree]]. She will gives Link the [[Linked Game/Secrets#Clock Shop Secret|Clock Shop Secret]]. In the past, a little girl with the [[Linked Game/Secrets#Pirate Secret|Pirate Secret]] appears south of the [[Black Tower]].
