The {{Term/Store|Traveler's Gear|Traveler's Gear|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear}} is equipment used by travelers passing through the wilderness of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. It is not especially strong or durable, and is designed to fend off wild {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}} and weak {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Traveler's Sword}}<br/>A very common sword often kept by travelers to fend off small beasts. It's fairly durable, but a bit unreliable against monsters.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Traveler's Spear}}<br/>A spear used mainly by travelers to fend off wolves and other beasts. It's easy to hold and simple to use.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Traveler's Shield}}<br/>A sturdy shield loved by {{Typo|many an adventurer}}. It is made of animal hide and sturdy wood and is best suited to defending against weak monsters or animals.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

Pieces of {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear}} are composed of both metal and wood. Wooden {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear}} will burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, can float on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, cannot conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and are unable to be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. The inverse is true of metallic {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear}}.
