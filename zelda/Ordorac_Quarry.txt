{{Term/Store|Ordorac Quarry||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ordorac Quarry|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Ordorac Quarry}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. This valley, true to its name, contains a number of {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}}. It is, however, patrolled by three {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher|link}} requiring caution. Two {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found along the quarry's outer walls, each appearing after solving a {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} puzzle. A tunnel in the west side leads to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|BotW|Ordorac Quarry}} derives its name from the Japanese name for {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}}.
