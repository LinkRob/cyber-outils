{{Term/Store|Kakariko Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Kakariko Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Kakariko Bridge is a large, damaged stone brick bridge in [[West Necluda]] that spans [[Lake Siela]]. An oversized boulder is stuck in the eastern side of the Bridge, and many [[Rock]]s cover its surface. The Bridge serves as one of the means to enter [[Kakariko Village]].<ref>{{Cite|Kakariko Village is the village where the Sheikah clan lives. It's said that, some time ago, they had advanced techniques that were more powerful than anything anyone had seen. But that power put them at odds with the king at the time, and they were scattered across the land... Anyway, if you leave here and follow the road straight north, you'll find a big bridge. Cross that and keep following the path, and you'll come to the Kakariko Village gate.|Rensa|BotW}}</ref>The [[Hylian]] travelers {{Term|BotW|Brokka|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Bugut|link}} can be seen crossing it on their way to and from Kakariko Village. After [[Link]] has either completed the "[[Seek Out Impa]]" [[Main Quest]] or entered the [[Yiga Clan Hideout]], a [[Traveler]] will appear at the northern entrance to the Bridge, waiting to ambush the Hylian [[Champion]]. A small patch of land beneath the Kakariko Bridge hosts a campsite. In this campsite, Link can find a lit [[Cooking Pot]], which is flanked by a [[Roasted Bass]] and metal boxes that contain [[Arrow]]s, [[Rupee]]s, and cooked [[Food]]. A metallic crate sits atop some boulders in the waters east of the Kakariko Bridge.

A metallic [[Treasure Chest]] hides behind two metallic crates in a small alcove north of the Cooking Pot and contains five [[Ice Arrow]]s. A second metallic Treasure Chest containing an [[Opal]] can be found in the water beside these boulders. A wooden platform held aloft by [[Sky Octorok]]s appears due east of the Kakariko Bridge in [[Master Mode]], bearing a metal Treasure Chest which contains a [[Boomerang]].

In the water on the western side of the Kakariko Bridge is a circle of rocky pillars. If Link throws a Rock into this circle, a [[Korok]] will appear.
