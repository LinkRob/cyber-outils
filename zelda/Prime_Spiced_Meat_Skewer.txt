{{Term/Store|Prime Spiced Meat Skewer|Prime Spiced Meat Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Prime Spiced Meat Skewers are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Raw Prime Meat]] and [[Goron Spice]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. By eating them, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can have some of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} restored to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Though the preparation of these skewers is simple, they're known to have a complex taste profile.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can add more ingredients when making Prime Spiced Meat Skewers, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Prime Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. When eaten, they will heal all of [[Link|Link's]] Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Prime Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. They can be eaten to refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Prime Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Eating these skewers will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Prime Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. Link can eat them to have his movement speed increased.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Prime Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when he eats them.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Prime Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Prime Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Consuming them will grant Link a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Prime Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. When these skewers are eaten, Link will gain a boost to his defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Prime Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. By eating them, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities will receive a boost.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Prime Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Prime Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The simple preparation of this steak dish belies its complex taste profile.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]] when he eats these skewers.<ref name="Chilly"/>
