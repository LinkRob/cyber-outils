==Overview==
The {{Term|Series|Game Screen}} refers to the main interface where gameplay occurs, as opposed to selection and viewing screens such as the {{Term|Series|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|Series|Map Screen|link}}, {{Term|Series|Title Screen|link}}, and so on. It includes the head-up display which usually consists of the {{Term|Series|Life Gauge|link}}, a {{Term|Series|Rupee|link}} counter, and the currently selected items, among other game-specific elements.
