The {{Term/Store|Nice Tornado Rod||Series, ALBW}} is an item in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Once Link acquires the three [[Pendants of Virtue]], he can buy the [[Tornado Rod]] from Ravio for 800 Rupees. Once he owns the rod, Link can then find and bring 10 [[Lost Maiamai|Lost Maiamais]] to [[Mother Maiamai]] to get her to upgrade the [[Tornado Rod]] into the [[Nice Tornado Rod]].

The [[Nice Tornado Rod]] is much the same as the [[Tornado Rod]], except that it is enhanced to stun enemies over a larger area.
