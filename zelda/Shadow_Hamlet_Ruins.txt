The {{Term/Store|Shadow Hamlet Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Shadow Hamlet Ruins|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|BotW}}
The Shadow Hamlet Ruins are a ruined settlement in [[Eldin Canyon]] that lie on the mountain range between the eastern side of [[Death Mountain]]'s lava moat, and the [[Shadow Pass]] of [[Akkala]]. It is home to a Meteo Wizzrobe, which is possibly the one who destroyed this small settlement. It only has three small, burned houses to its name. There is a Zapshroom on the outskirts of the hamlet, and a few ore deposits, including one rare deposit, scatter the cliffside near and above the village. Several trees can be found in the town, which can be broken for tree branches and Korok Leaves. It is possible to find Fireproof Lizards here.
