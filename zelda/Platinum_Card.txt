The {{Term/Store|Platinum Card||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 134}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Platinum Card}} is one of {{Plural|ST|Beedle's Membership Card|link}}, and it can be obtained by accumulating 1,000 points at {{Term|ST|Beedle's Airshop|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have [x] points, valued customer. Rack up more points for a Platinum Card and even better rewards!|Beedle|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Your point total has climbed to [x]! Rack up {{Color|ST Light Red|1,000 points}} to earn yourself a Platinum Card!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> Once {{Term|ST|Link|link}} has reached this threshold, {{Term|ST|Beedle|link}} will announce that he qualifies for a prize.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh! You have [x] points! You're now an official {{Color|ST Light Red|Platinum Member}}! That means you get {{Color|ST Light Red|30% off all merchandise}} AND a {{Color|ST Light Red|lovely prize}}!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> As a result, he gives {{Term|ST|Link}} the {{Term|ST|Quintuple Points Card|link}}, which multiplies the number of points he receives when he makes a purchase by five.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|ST Light Red|Quintuple Points Card}}! Any item you buy earns five times the points!|N/A|ST}}</ref>

{{Term|ST|Beedle}} promises to send {{Term|ST|Link}} his {{Term|ST|Platinum Card}} the next day.<ref>{{Cite|I'll send your Platinum Card tomorrow, so be sure to check your mail. Keep earning those points, because the next level is Diamond!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> After one day has passed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will receive the {{Term|ST|Platinum Membership|link}} letter from {{Term|ST|Beedle}}, at which point he receives the {{Term|ST|Platinum Card}}. With this card, {{Term|ST|Link}} will receive 30% off the price of {{Plural|ST|Item|link}} at {{Term|ST|Beedle's Airshop}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|ST Light Red|Platinum Card}}! Now everything at Beedle's shop is 30% off!|N/A|ST}}</ref>
