The {{Term/Store|Viewing Platform||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Viewing Platform|Map|SSHD}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|SS|Viewing Platform}} is located within the {{Term|SS|Faron Woods|link}} in the {{Term|SS|Faron Province|link}}. It is a stone structure which offers a view of the {{Term|SS|Great Tree|link}} and its surrounding area. The rear of the {{Term|SS|Viewing Platform}} leads into the {{Term|SS|Deep Woods|link}}, which can only be accessed by uncurling a {{Term|SS|Spiral Ivy|link}} with the {{Term|SS|Slingshot|link}}.

Several giant {{Plural|SS|Mushroom|link}} and one glowing {{Term|SS|Mushroom}} can be found near the base of the {{Term|SS|Viewing Platform}}. They can be struck to retrieve {{Plural|SS|Mushroom Spores|link}} and {{Term|SS|Glittering Spores|link}}, respectively.
