'''Bouquet''' is an [[item]] in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Bouquet is a bouquet of flowers that [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can purchase from a [[Shrine Merchant]] in the [[Shrine of Strength]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. It has no purpose, meaning that Zelda is scammed if she purchases the item.
