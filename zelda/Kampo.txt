{{Term/Store|Kampo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kampo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kampo}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} worker at {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable|link}}. For most of the day, he tends to the {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} in the stall next to the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}. At midnight, he goes inside to sleep in one of the beds.<ref>{{Cite|Zzz...zzz...|Kampo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kampo}} is fascinated by the {{Term|BotW|Floria Falls|link}}, considering them to be perfect.<ref>{{Cite|Don't you think the waterfalls flowing down from {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Floria}} is the very picture of perfection?|Kampo|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that he has noticed something strange at the top of the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's something strange at the {{Color|BotW Red|top of that waterfall}}, though...|Kampo|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to climb {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable}}'s watchtower so he can get a better look at it.<ref>{{Cite|Climb the ladder on the watchtower to take a peek. You'll probably need a telescope or something for a good look.|Kampo|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed the "{{Term|BotW|A Song of Storms|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kampo}} notes that the thing at the top of the {{Term|BotW|Floria Falls}} has shrunken, though a faint glow can still be seen.<ref>{{Cite|And even though it has shrunken over time, you can still see a faint glow.|Kampo|BotW}}</ref> However, wonders if it has always been like this and he has been imagining things.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe I'm just imagining things... Was it like that the whole time?|Kampo|BotW}}</ref>
