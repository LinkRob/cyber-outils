{{Compendium/Extract|Dragonbone Boko Spear}}
{{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by {{Plural|BotW|Black Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Bokoblin|link}}, as well as {{Plural|BotW|Black Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Moblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Moblin|link}}. They are most commonly found near the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}} and on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Spear}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are {{Plural|BotW|Boko Spear|link}} that have been enhanced with fossilized bones, positioned so the fangs will bite opponents.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Spear}} will float if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. If exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, they will start to burn. Also, unlike metal {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, they do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}.

If scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} {{Term|BotW|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Spear}}.
