==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Hylian Hood is a piece of [[Armor]] that can be purchased at the [[Ventest Clothing Boutique]] in [[Hateno Village]] or [[Enchanted]] in [[Kakariko Village]]. According to its description, it is made from a sturdy material to protect travelers from the harsh sun and weather.<ref name="Inventory"/> It can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Hylian Hood, alongside the [[Hylian Tunic]] and [[Hylian Trousers]], compose the [[Hylian Set]].
