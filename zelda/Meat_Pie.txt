==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Meat Pies are meals made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any meat, [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Goat Butter]], and [[Rock Salt]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The resulting dish, when eaten, will restore some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These pies are juicy and contain perfectly baked minced meat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding other ingredients, several different variations of Meat Pie can be made:

* '''Hearty Meat Pie''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Meat Pie}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these pies will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Meat Pie''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Meat Pie}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. When eaten, they'll refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Meat Pie''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Meat Pie}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. If they're eaten, they'll completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Meat Pie''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Meat Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. They can be consumed to provide a boost to Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Meat Pie''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Meat Pie}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. When eaten, these pies grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Meat Pie''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Meat Pie}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. Link gains one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when he eats them.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Meat Pie''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Meat Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Eating them temporarily boosts the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Meat Pie''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Meat Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. When eaten, Link's defense will be temporarily increased.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Meat Pie''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Meat Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Link can eat these pies to gain a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Meat Pie''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Meat Pie}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>You'll need an extra napkin to deal with this juicy pie of perfectly baked minced meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. If eaten, they grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
