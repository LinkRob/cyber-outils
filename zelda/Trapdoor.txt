{{Term/Store|Trapdoor|Trapdoors|Series, FSA|plural}} are traps in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|game=FSA |publisher=Nintendo |page=107 }}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|FSA|Trapdoor}} are indistinguishable from normal ground until activated, at which point they become {{Plural|FSA|Pit|link}} leading to lower levels.

The variant found in the {{Term|FSA|Cave of No Return|link}} activates when a {{Term|FSA|Links|link}} steps within its area, while the variant in {{Term|FSA|The Field|link}} {{Term|FSA|Shadow Battle|link}} arena is activated using a {{Term|FSA|Star Tile|link}}.
