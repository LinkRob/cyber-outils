The {{Term/Store|Great Fairy of Flame||Series, FS}} is a character in {{FS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 263 {{Exp Game|FS}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Flame}} is the guardian of {{Term|FS|Death Mountain (Four Swords)|link}}.<ref name="Guardian">{{Cite|I am the Great Fairy of Flame, guardian of Death Mountain. You have done well to gather so much treasure. I dub you...little eggs, waiting to hatch into heroes. I grant you each a {{Color|FS Orange|Silver Key}}.|Great Fairy of Flame|FS}}</ref> She appears from her {{Term|FS|Fairy Fountain|display=Fountain|link}} at the end of {{Term|FS|Death Mountain (Four Swords)}} after the {{Plural|FS|Links|link}} have defeated {{Term|FS|Gouen|link}}. There, she bestows the {{Plural|FS|Links}} a {{Term|FS|Key|link}} alongside a title befitting their achievements. The type of {{Term|FS|Key}} that she distributes is based on the amount of {{Plural|FS|Rupee|link}} the {{Plural|FS|Links}} have collected as well as whether they have defeated {{Term|FS|Vaati|link}} with a given set of {{Plural|FS|Key}}. If the {{Plural|FS|Links}} have collected at least 1,000 {{Plural|FS|Rupee}}, the {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Flame}} will gift the {{Plural|FS|Links}} a {{Term|FS|Silver Key|link}} and dub them "little hero eggs".<ref name="Guardian"/> If the {{Plural|FS|Links}} have collected at least 3,000 {{Plural|FS|Rupee}} and have defeated {{Term|FS|Vaati}} after collecting all of the {{Plural|FS|Silver Key}}, she will dub them "brave heroes" and bestow upon them {{Plural|FS|Golden Key|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am the Great Fairy of Flame, guardian of Death Mountain. Collecting this many Rupees is an incredible feat! I dub you...brave heroes and grant you each a {{Color|FS Orange|Golden Key}}.|Great Fairy of Flame|FS}}</ref> Defeating {{Term|FS|Vaati}} and returning to the {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Flame}}'s Fountain with at least 5,000 {{Plural|FS|Rupee}} will prompt her to declare the {{Plural|FS|Links}} the "greatest of heroes" and grant them {{Plural|FS|Hero's Key|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am the Great Fairy of Flame, guardian of Death Mountain. There is nothing left to be said. I dub you...the greatest of heroes and grant you each a {{Color|FS Orange|Hero's Key}}.|Great Fairy of Flame|FS}}</ref>

The {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Flame}} then cautions the heroes that they must have collected a complete set of {{Plural|FS|Key}} in order to open the way to {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You all seem to have collected the three {{Color|FS Orange|Silver Keys}}. Vaati's palace of winds is a great castle that floats in the sky. Now that you have gathered the keys of the Fairies of Forest, Ice, and Flame, the palace shall appear. Onward, young heroes! You must save Princess Zelda!|Great Fairy of Flame|FS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|At last all of you have collected the three {{Color|FS Orange|Golden Keys}}. You can now choose the path to Vaati's Palace. That path will likely be much more dangerous than any you've seen yet. And yet onward you must go, great heroes!|Great Fairy of Flame|FS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|At long last all of you have gathered the three {{Color|FS Orange|Hero's Keys}}. You should now be able to choose the path to Vaati's Palace. It is a path of 12 levels, all more dangerous than those you've seen. Yet onward you must go, great heroes!|Great Fairy of Flame|FS}}</ref>

If the {{Plural|FSAE|Links}} have defeated {{Term|FSAE|Vaati}} after collecting all of the {{Plural|FSAE|Hero's Key}} in {{FSAE}}, the {{Term|FSAE|Great Fairy of Flame}}'s appearance and personality will change to resemble the {{Term|MM3D|Great Fairy of Magic|link}} from {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! You have done well to get this far. I am the Great Fairy of Flame! Throughout the ages I have conversed with the flames as the guardian of Death Mountain.|Great Fairy of Flame|FSAE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I see you already have the three {{Color|FS Orange|Hero Keys}}! I wonder if you know about the path that opened to {{Color|FS Blue|Vaati's Palace}}. It has {{Color|FS Orange|12 floors}}... Do you need an explanation? ...No? Then off you go, brave heroes!|Great Fairy of Flame|FSAE}}</ref>
