{{Term/Store|Bananas|Bananas|Series, LA, LADX, LANS|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 116"/>

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
The Bananas are the fourth item in the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}} of {{LA}}.<ref name="E 116"/> Bananas are available at [[Sale's House O' Bananas]] on [[Toronbo Shores]].<ref>{{Cite|I was hungry somethin' fierce so I went and got bananas at the beach... Link, if you want some, you should go and get some!|Tarin|LADX}}</ref> Although [[Link]] can't purchase them, he may receive a bunch of Bananas by giving [[Sale]] his can of [[Dog Food]],<ref name="E 116"/> who in turn gives him the bunch as thanks.<ref>{{Cite|MUNCH MUNCH!! ... ... ... ... That was great! I know it's not a fair trade, but here's some bananas! YUM...|Sale|LADX}}</ref>

The Bananas can then be given to [[Kiki]], a hungry [[Monkey]] found near the moat of [[Kanalet Castle]].<ref name="Chiquita">{{Cite|Chi-kiita! Chikiita! Kiki the monkey! Hungry! Kiki the monkey!|Kiki|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|LADX Bananas}}!{{Icon|LADX Bananas}}! Oooh! Oooh! Give to Kiki!?|Kiki|LADX}}</ref> He will repay Link, with the help of his Monkey brethren, by building a bridge across the water moat giving Link access to Kanalet Castle.<ref name="E 116"/> Upon leaving, the Monkeys leave behind the [[Stick]],<ref name="E 116"/> the next item in the Trading Sequence.<ref>{{Cite|You found a stick a monkey left behind... You take it!|N/A|LADX}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{RTBToL|-}}===
{{Plural|RTBToL|Banana}} are used to lure down the {{Term|RTBToL|Monkey|link}} {{Term|RTBToL|Masaru|link}} from {{Plural|RTBToL|Tree|link}}. They cost 30 {{Plural|RTBToL|Rupee|link}} apiece, and can be bought from {{Plural|RTBToL|Shop|link}} located on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 6|link}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Page 7|link}}, and {{Term|RTBToL|Page 8|link}}. However, the {{Term|RTBToL|Shop|link}} will only sell them to {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} while he is on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 6}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
