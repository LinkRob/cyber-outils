==Mainline Appearances==
The weapon empowers [[arrow]]s with sacred [[light]] that can easily pierce and destroy evil itself.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Light Arrow! Set it to (C) and your arrows will be powered up! The light of justice will smite evil!|N/A|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Light Arrow! Set it to (C) to power up your arrows. The light of justice shall target evil!|N/A|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Light Arrow! When you take out your bow, use [R] to change arrowheads, then fire away. The sacred light of these arrows can pierce pure evil itself!|N/A|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Light Arrow! Now, you can fire arrows filled with sacred light!|N/A|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Spirits of the light! Wielders of the great power that shines far and wide upon the lands of our world... In my hour of need, grant me the light to banish evil!|Princess Zelda|TP}}</ref> Due to their immense power, they usually can destroy any [[enemy]] with just one hit, as seen in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'' (though not the case in prior games). The {{Term|Series|Light Arrow}}, unlike the {{Term|Series|Fire Arrow|link|display=Fire}} and {{Plural|Series|Ice Arrow|link}}, consumes more of [[Link]]'s [[Magic Meter]] than they do, with the exception of {{TMC}} and {{TP}}, due to the lack of the gameplay mechanic. 
[[File:BotW Link aiming Light Arrow.png|thumb|280x280px|Link aiming his [[Bow]] with a Light Arrow in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'']]
It is commonly assumed that these sacred arrows are based on the legendary [[Silver Arrow]] from the earliest games predating ''Ocarina of Time''. Although both are necessary to complete the main storyline and are undoubtedly powerful weapons capable of [[Weapon Strength|surpassing the Master Sword in raw strength]], in reality, the Light Arrows are fundamentally different in that they only weaken [[Ganon]]'s defenses and stun him so Link can freely strike his archnemesis with the [[Master Sword|Blade of Evil's Bane]]. Behaving in the exact opposite manner, the Silver Arrows in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' are used to ''damage'' Ganon directly while the [[Sword]] stuns him. With the exception of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]]/[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Ages]]'' [[Linked Game|linked game]] where Ganon himself was not properly revived, the Silver Arrows are the '''only''' weapon in the long-running franchise shown to be capable of truly destroying him in his actual beast form. With this in mind, it's suggested that the Light Arrows are weaker than the Silver counterpart in comparison. 

Since ''The Wind Waker'', the Light Arrows had become [[Princess Zelda]]'s signature weapon of choice in the same manner as Link has for the Master Sword. 

==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT3D Fairy Bow Light Arrow Icon.png|left]]
In its debut in {{OoT}}, Princess Zelda entrusts Link with the Light Arrows in the {{Term|OoT|Temple of Time|link}} after all of the {{Plural|OoT|Sage|link}} have been awakened.<ref>{{Cite|The six Sages will open the sealed door and lure Ganondorf back into the Sacred Realm. I will then seal the door to the Sacred Realm from this world. Thus, Ganondorf the Evil King will vanish from Hyrule. Link... In order to do this, I need your courage again. Please protect me while I do my part. And here is a weapon that can penetrate the Evil King's defenses... The power given to the chosen ones... The sacred {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Light Arrow}}!|Princess Zelda|OoT3D}}</ref> Although the Light Arrows don't do as much damage than regular {{Plural|OoT|arrow}} to normal enemies, they are required to defeat the King of Evil, Ganondorf, and are useful in stunning his beast form - the [[Final Boss]], Ganon. They are also required to dispel the magical barrier that blocks access to the upper levels of {{Term|OoT|Ganon's Castle|link}}. An added bonus is the almost-guaranteed side effect that an enemy killed with a {{Term|OoT|Light Arrow}} drops 50 {{Plural|OoT|Rupee|link}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM3D Hero's Bow Light Arrow Icon.png|left]]
In {{MM}}, the Light Arrows are found in the first half of the [[Stone Tower Temple]] and are used to invert the temple in order to continue through the [[dungeon]]s.<ref>{{Cite|If you shoot that which releases the sacred golden light into the blood-stained, red emblem outside the temple... it shall rearrange things, in which the earth is born in the heavens and the moon is born on the earth.|Garo Master|MM}}</ref> Moreover, they are useful for defeating the temple's boss, [[Twinmold]], and are also helpful during the Final Boss, [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]].

Similarly to {{OoT|-}}, if Link defeats an enemy with a Light Arrow, a Purple Rupee is automatically rewarded. They are identical from the game's prequel, but are more effective against traditional enemies, and are even able to damage fully armored [[Iron Knuckle (Boss)|Iron Knuckles]].

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, Link obtains the Light Arrows in [[Ganon's Castle|Ganon's Tower]] by completing the Illusionary Room [[puzzle]] and defeating [[Phantom Ganon]] the second time. They are used to destroy Phantom Ganon, [[Puppet Ganon]], and [[Ganondorf]].<ref>{{Cite|Phantom Ganon, Habitat: Forsaken Fortress, Effective Weapons: Light Arrows, Master Sword|N/A|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Puppet Ganon, Habitat: Ganon's Castle, Effective Weapon: Light Arrows, This is a gigantic marionette created by Ganon himself. It transforms into three different shapes, but all are vulnerable to rays of light.|N/A|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ganondorf, Base of Operations: Ganon's Castle, Effective Weapons: Light Arrows, Master Sword|N/A|TWW}}</ref> In the final battle against Ganondorf, Princess Zelda ([[Tetra]]) borrows Link's Bow and Light Arrows to fire them at the King of Evil and to stun him in order to assist the hero (a first for the series).<ref>{{Cite|I'll use this bow to cover you! Attack him as best you can!|Princess Zelda|TWW}}</ref> If Zelda were to accidentally strike Link with a Light Arrow, he gets an electric shock in consequence.

The Light Arrows are the ultimate weapon in the game, inflicting a one-hit kill on ''any'' enemy except for [[Boss|bosses]], even if it's wearing armor, as is the case with [[Darknut]]s, and can pierce through multiple targets with a well-placed shot. However, like in {{OoT|-}} and {{MM|-}}, they consume [[Magic]] and must be used with care.

==={{TP|-}}===

[[File:Zelda LightArrow.jpg|thumb|241x241px|Princess Zelda assisting Link at shooting Ganondorf with a Light Arrow in ''Twilight Princess'']]
In {{TP}}, Link cannot actually obtain the Light Arrows; they are instead wielded by Princess Zelda. They are granted to her by the four [[Spirit of Light|Spirits of Light]], who have actually turned into the Light Arrows themselves, after she calls for their aid to banish evil.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Zelda's Bow / The weapon used by Zelda during the final battle with Ganondorf. Her arrows were created from the transformed Light Spirits.|book=HH|page= 196}}</ref> Along with the Light Arrows, Princess Zelda is given a bow with which to wield them. In the battle on horseback with Ganondorf, Princess Zelda will fire {{Plural|TP|Light Arrow}} at him to slow him down, allowing Link to attack the King of Evil with the Master Sword.

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, the Light Arrows are obtained late in the game. After defeating [[Byrne]] with the help of Princess Zelda, and meeting [[Chancellor Cole|Cole]] and [[Malladus]] at the altar at the top of the [[Tower of Spirits]], Link is told that he must get a weapon from the [[Sand Temple]] in order to defeat Malladus. After clearing the Sand Temple, Link claims the Bow of Light. After this, Link's Arrows become Light Arrows, as the Bow of Light replaces his normal Bow. During the final battle, Link must fire a Light Arrow at Princess Zelda's body to free her from Malladus. Shortly after, Princess Zelda fires Light Arrows at Malladus' weak point on his back while Link distracts the Demon King.
==={{ALBW|-}}===
The [[Bow of Light]] is given to Link by Princess Zelda during the final battle with [[Yuga Ganon]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]''. When Yuga Ganon merges into the wall and becomes invulnerable, Link must use the Bow of Light to shoot Light Arrows at his back. This stuns Yuga Ganon, forcing him out of the wall and making him vulnerable to sword attacks. Repeat this process three times to defeat him.
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Link}} receives the Bow of Light from {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} during the fight with [[Dark Beast Ganon]] in {{BotW}}. By scanning the {{SSBS}} Zelda [[amiibo]], Link can also receive the [[Twilight Bow]]. Both Bows can shoot an unlimited number of Light Arrows. They must be used to attack Ganon's glowing points in order to damage him.

==Spinoff Appearances==
===''Hyrule Warriors'' series===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
===={{HW|-}}====
The Light Arrows appear after [[Ganondorf]] transforms into [[Ganon]] in the "[[Liberation of the Triforce]]" [[Scenario]] in {{HW}}. [[Princess Zelda|Princess Zelda]] blesses Link's Bow and Arrow set with this upgraded form so that he may attack and stun Ganon with them. She will do the same for other player Warriors as well. Should Zelda or Sheik be the player Warrior, [[Princess Ruto|Princess Ruto]] will grant them instead. They are only effective when the gem on Ganon's forehead glows, when he is about to charge. Otherwise they behave identically to the upgraded version of the standard Bow.

Light Arrows also appear in some of Zelda's combo attacks as part of her [[Rapier]] moveset. These do not have the same effect as the Light Arrows in "Liberation of the Triforce", which must be received separately.

In {{HW|-}} and {{HW|L}}, Tetra is capable of summoning Light Arrows based on the ones from {{TWW|-}}. During her [[Focus Spirit Attack]], the Light Arrow bursts a giant floating bubble of water, damaging all enemies in range.

In the {{ALBW|-}} DLC Pack for {{HW|-}} and {{HW|L|-}}, [[Ravio]] fires Light Arrows from the upgraded Bow as part of both the Focus Spirit Attack and the [[Special Attack]] of his [[Rental Hammer]] Weapon. During the Special Attack, the Light Arrows have [[Bomb]]s attached to them like certain appearances of [[Bomb Arrow]]s.

==Trivia==
*In the French instruction booklet of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', Light Arrows were mistranslated as "Flèches légères", meaning that these arrows are not heavy.
*The Light Arrows' one-hit kill ability in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'' and some other games mirrors how the [[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]] functioned in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]''.
*[[Link]] being electrocuted and not outright destroyed by Light Arrows if he is accidentally hit by [[Princess Zelda]] during the final battle against [[Ganondorf]] in ''The Wind Waker'' is a result due to these [[Arrow|arrows]] being specifically designed to wipe out evil, similar to the [[Master Sword]], but Link himself is a heroic character of pure heart, and therefore does not contain any evil.
*If hacking from cheating devices are used to make Link retain his [[Bow]] and Light Arrows during the battle against Ganondorf in ''The Wind Waker'', the young hero can assist Princess Zelda into shooting such arrows at him, stunning the main antagonist more often than usual, and can prove useful for the phases when she is knocked out by the [[Gerudo]] warlock and the final blow via [[Parry Attack]].
