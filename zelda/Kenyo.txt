{{Term/Store|Kenyo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kenyo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kenyo}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} child who resides at {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}. During the day, he runs around the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|display=Stable}}. At night, he sleeps in one of the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|display=Stable's}} {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}}. During the {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, he and {{Term|BotW|Ena|link}} sit together inside.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kenyo}}, he will shout "BOOM BOOM! BAM BAM!" at him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|BOOM BOOM! BAM BAM!}}|Kenyo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds in kind, {{Term|BotW|Kenyo}} will yell "BOOM-BAM!"<ref>{{Cite|[BOOM BAM!] {{Big|BOOM-BAM! BOOM-BAM!}}|Kenyo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} expresses confusion, {{Term|BotW|Kenyo}} informs him that there is a {{Term|BotW|Modar|link|display=ghost}} under that {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Great Bridge|link}} that makes loud noises.<ref>{{Cite|[Uh... What?] Buddy, you don't know?! The BOOM-BAM GHOST of {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha Great Bridge}}! Under the bridge, at the bottom of the valley, there's a ghost that makes weird noises like "BOOM-BAM BOOM-BAM!"|Kenyo|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to listen for the noises, only to realize that they have stopped.<ref>{{Cite|Just listen and you'll hear it. ...Huh? It stopped...|Kenyo|BotW}}</ref> He then theorizes that the Boom-Bam Ghost is taking a nap.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe the Boom-Bam Ghost is taking a nap.|Kenyo|BotW}}</ref>

While it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|display=Raining}}, {{Term|BotW|Kenyo}} wonders if the Boom-Bam Ghost likes such weather.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder if the Boom-Bam Ghost of {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha Great Bridge}} likes the rain...|Kenyo|BotW}}</ref> Even in his sleep, he mutters about the ghost.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Boom... Bam... Zzz...}}|Kenyo|BotW}}</ref>
