{{Term/Store|Silver Bomb|Silver Bombs|Series, OoA, TMC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="OoA">{{Cite|Did you drop a {{Color|OoA Red|Golden Bomb}}? Or a {{Color|OoA Red|Silver Bomb}}?|Fairy|OoA}}</ref><ref name="TMC">{{Cite|Did you throw the {{Color|TMC Red|Golden Bomb}} into the spring? Or the {{Color|TMC Red|Silver Bomb}}?|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA|-}}, {{Term|OoA|Talus Peaks|link}} has a natural fountain with a {{Term|OoA|Sign|link}} next to it warning people not to throw anything into the water.<ref>{{Cite|Do not throw anything into the water.|Sign|OoA}}</ref> If {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} throws in a {{Term|OoA|Bomb|link}}, a red {{Term|OoA|Fairy|link}} appears. She materializes a {{Term|OoA|Golden Bomb|link}} and a {{Term|OoA|Silver Bomb}} and asks {{Term|OoA|Link}} which one he dropped.<ref name="OoA"/> If he answers honestly, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} rewards him by increasing the amount of {{Plural|OoA|Bomb}} he can carry by 20.<ref>{{Cite|You are an honest person. I'll increase the number of {{Color|OoA Red|Bombs}} you can carry to reward you. Hee-yah!|Fairy|OoA}}</ref> If {{Term|OoA|Link}} responds by saying he dropped the {{Term|OoA|Silver Bomb}}, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} asks him to confirm.<ref>{{Cite|...Really? A {{Color|OoA Red|Silver Bomb}}?|Fairy|OoA}}</ref> If he affirms the lie, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} confiscates all his {{Plural|OoA|Bomb}}. To obtain the upgrade {{Term|OoA|Link}} must leave the area, acquire more {{Plural|OoA|Bomb}}, then return and throw in another {{Term|OoA|Bomb}} to restart the whole interaction.

==={{TMC|-}}===
If {{Term|TMC|Link}} throws a {{Term|TMC|Bomb}} into the {{Term|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy|link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Great Fairy Fountain|link|display=Fountain}} in {{TMC}}, she will ask him whether he threw a {{Term|TMC|Golden Bomb|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Silver Bomb}}, or neither.<ref name="TMC"/> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} gives the honest answer of "Neither", she rewards him with a {{Term|TMC|Big Bomb Bag|link}};<ref>{{Cite|You are honest. I must reward such an honest adventurer with some of my power.|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref> however, if he gives either of the other incorrect answers, she accuses him of lying and takes away all of his {{Plural|TMC|Bomb}}.<ref>{{Cite|You're lying, aren't you? As punishment for your lie, I will reduce your number of bombs to zero.|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref>

{{Plural|TMC|Silver Bomb}} are not seen in-game, only mentioned.
