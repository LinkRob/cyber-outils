'''Rus''' is a young child who appears in the {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}} published by Valiant Comics. He is the son of {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Cassiopia}} and the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|River Man}} and lives in the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Water Town of Saria|link}}.<ref name=quote>''Our boy Rus, here, came through in the end!'' - Link (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Cassiopia: '''Rus'''! My son!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5 [Day of the Triforce]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=River Man: Rus is my '''son'' as well, princess.|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6 [Day of the Triforce]}}</ref> He is featured in the ''[[Trust Me]]'' and ''[[Day of the Triforce]]'' stories of the comic.

==Biography==
{{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} is first introduced in ''Trust Me''. When {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon|link}} visits {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Saria Town|link}}, claiming to come in peace,<ref>''Wait! I come in peace!'' - Ganon (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> he picks up {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} as the rest of the villagers flee. When {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon}} asks {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} why he is afraid of him,<ref>''Why do you fear me, boy?'' - Ganon (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} replies that {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon}} is the evil one.<ref>''You're the '''Evil''' one! My daddy said so!'' - Rus (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> The Prince of Darkness explains to him him that those are simply lies that were spread by {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link|link}}, whom he refers to as a man-monster.<ref>''Lies, my dear innocent child! All lies! '' - Ganon (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref><ref>''I know why you fear me--! Because you believe the venomous '''lies''' spread by one who is truly evil! A man-monster called... '''Link!''''' - Ganon (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> 

The villagers befriend {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon}}, but {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Princess Zelda|link}} come to Saria for a visit. When {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon}} is about to kill {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}}, he asks {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} to give him {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}}'s {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Sword|link}}.<ref>''Ah! Link's Magic Sword! His greatest weapon! You, boy! Give it to me!'' - Ganon (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} refuses, asking what will happen to {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}}.<ref>''W-what are you going to do to them?'' - Rus (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> When {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon}} demands {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} to give him the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Sword}},<ref>''Do not question me! Give me the sword! Now!'' - Ganon (''Valiant Comic Issue #2'')</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} attempts to run away. {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon}} then tries to punish him for his disobedience with a {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Magic|link}} {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Spell|link}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=There is only '''one punishment''' for disobedience!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=7 [Trust Me]}}</ref> but fails when {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Princess Zelda}} dives in front of the boy, taking the blast. After {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}} manages to defeat {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon}}, {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} is seen safe and sound, with {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}} thanking him for his help.<ref name=quote/>

In ''Day of the Triforce'', after {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Cassiopia|link}} goes to {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|North Palace|link}} to ask for {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Princess Zelda}}'s help in ridding her town free of {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon}}'s minions, the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|River Man}} reveals to his wife that {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} was bitten by a {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rope Snake|link}} as he was trying to run away from the {{Plural|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Lynel|link}} that were attacking him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=This is what I tried to tell you! The child was bitten by a rope snake while trying to escape the Lynels!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> Desperate, {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Cassiopia}} confesses that {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} is beyond her healing powers and that he will not last beyond sundown,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He is beyond by healing powers! He will not last beyond sundown!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> but just then Zelda tells her that she will go to the Magic Spring and bring a {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Fairy|link}} to cure the boy.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I   can go to the Magic Spring and bring one of the fairies back with me  to  cure him! Their power to heal is even greater than yours, Cassiopia!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> Although {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Cassiopia}} wants her to go,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Oh, yes! Please|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> her husband insists that Zelda go and destroy {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon}} instead, for who   knows how many other children will die if the Prince of Darkness is not   stopped once and for all.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=NO! Today Ganon is at his weakest--!   Today you MUST destroy him! [...] Rus is my son as well, princess.   But...how many OTHER children will perish if Ganon is not stopped once   and for all?|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> Eventually, {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Princess Zelda}} makes up her mind and chooses to save {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Rus}} instead, bringing {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Miff|link}} along to easily cure the boy's injuries and thus saving his life.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Of course, princess! If I can restore health to a lug like Link, a cute kid like this will be easy!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=10}}</ref>
