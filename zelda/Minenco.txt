{{Term/Store|Minenco||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258}}</ref>

==Biography==
Minenco is an elderly woman living on [[Windfall Island]], and has a keen interest in [[Pictograph]]y. She can be often found discussing the topic with [[Lenzo]]. According to her, she started to look for true beauty in pictographs ten years ago, and was named Miss Windfall Island in her youth. If [[Link]] shows [[Pompie and Vera]] a pictograph of her and Lenzo together, they will discard their thoughts of the two of them possibly being lovers and reward him with a [[Treasure Chart]].

She will realize how beautiful she looks once Link takes a colored pictograph of her and shows it to the former Miss Windfall Island, and in return, Minenco will in return give him a Treasure Chart.
