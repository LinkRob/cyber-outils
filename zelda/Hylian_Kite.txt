The {{Term/Store|Hylian Kite|Hylian Kites|Series, MK8, MK8D}} is an {{Term|MK8D|Item|link}} in {{MK8}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hylian Kite|N/A|MK8D}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|MK8D|Hylian Kite}} is a {{smw|Glider}} that accompanies the {{Term|MK8D|Master Cycle|link}} and the {{Term|MK8D|Triforce Tires|link}} in {{MK8}}. It is a Glider that bears a stylized red {{Term|Series|Wingcrest|link}} and draws its color scheme from the {{Term|Series|Hylian Shield|link}}. The {{Term|MK8D|Hylian Kite}} is available as part of the [[The Legend of Zelda × Mario Kart 8|{{TLoZ|-}} × {{MK8|-}}]] [[DLC]] pack for {{MK8|-}}. In {{MK8|D|-}}, it can be obtained randomly by collecting enough {{smw|Coin}}s.
