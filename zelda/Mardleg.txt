'''Mardlegs''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Mardlegs are purple centipede-like creatures and relatives of [[Lanmola]]s. They have two large pincers, red eyes, and a thin body. Mardlegs move around aimlessly but snap their pincers repeatedly. If Zelda has anywhere below two [[Celestial Sign]]s, Mardlegs inflict two [[Heart]]s of damage, but if she has two or more Celestial signs, a Mardleg instead inflicts a Heart and a half of damage.

Mardlegs are encountered in the [[Great Dry Canyon]], the [[Seacoast Plain]], and the [[Plain of Hobel]].
