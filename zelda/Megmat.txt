{{Term/Store|Megmat|Megmats|Series, TAoL|plural}} are enemies in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 192}}</ref> They appear to be of marsupial and vermin origin.

==Characteristics==
Megmats attempt to lunge into prey by hopping along the ground at a fast pace. They are seen by Link in {{TAoL}} when he journeys [[Hyrule]] in his quest to wake the slumbering [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]. Megmats have light blue pelts and tails, and can be found in a variety of climates, including but not limited to: grasslands, caves, and deserts. Due to their small form and lack of any natural armor, Megmats can easily be killed with weapons, such as a Sword.
