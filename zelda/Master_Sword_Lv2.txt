The {{Term/Store|Master Sword Lv2||Series, ALttP, ALBW}} (originally named '''Tempered Sword''') is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 131 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Master Sword Lv2}} is referred to as the '''Tempered Sword''' in {{Guide|ALttP|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP |publisher= Nintendo |page= 142}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It is an upgrade to the {{Term|Series|Master Sword|link}} preceding the {{Term|Series|Master Sword Lv3|link}} and is the second strongest [[Sword]] in both ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' and {{ALBW}}. The Master Sword Lv2 goes unnamed in {{AST}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In ''A Link to the Past'', this weapon was called the Tempered Sword. [[Link]] can obtain this version of the Master Sword by taking the {{Term|ALttP|Dwarven Swordsmith|link}} trapped in the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} back to his brother in the {{Term|ALttP|Light World|link}} to the {{Term|ALttP|Smithery|link}} east of {{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village|link}}. As a token of gratitude, the brothers will upgrade the young hero's sacred blade. However, the forging process takes time and Link is forced to leave his sword with them and wait (this can be quickly done by moving one screen and then returns back). During this time, Link can perform the action of swinging a blade without one in his possession, resembling a punch, but he cannot deal damage to enemies this way. Link must use items if he wishes to defeat enemies before retrieving the Tempered Sword.

Tempering the {{Term|ALttP|Master Sword}} makes the Blade of Evil's Bane much stronger, gives the blade a reddish-orange glow, and causes it to make a [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/LTTP/LTTP_Sword3.wav deeper swing sound] as opposed to the usual slash sound. It is twice as strong as its normal state (making the Tempered Sword four times as strong as the {{Term|ALttP|Fighter's Sword|link}}), but the {{Term|ALttP|Sword Beam|link}} still retains its original power and continues to do so even as the Master Sword is upgraded again into the [[Master Sword Lv3|Golden Sword]] via throwing it to the [[Mysterious Pond]] of the [[Cursed Fairy]] inside the [[Pyramid of Power]] in the [[Dark World]], which causes the sacred blade to have double its raw strength once more.

Like the Golden Sword, the Tempered Sword is able to harm [[Ganon]] without the need of a [[Spin Attack]] in contrast to the regular Master Sword, making the final battle much easier. However, it deals less damage in comparison.

In the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|Game Boy Advance remake of the game]], during the final challenge of the [[Palace of the Four Sword]], if Link has obtained the Tempered Sword, the four [[Dark Link|Dark Links]] will also use this sword as well.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In ''A Link Between Worlds'', the weapon is renamed the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword Lv2}}. It is obtained by bringing two pieces of [[Master Ore]] to {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Blacksmith|link}} at the {{Term|ALBW|Blacksmith's Forge|link}}. Similar to ''A Link to the Past'', it is three times more powerful than the {{Term|ALBW|Forgotten Sword|link}}, and its {{Plural|ALBW|Sword Beam}} are 1.5 times more powerful. It can be further upgraded to the Master Sword Lv3 when Link brings the remaining two Master Ore pieces.

== Trivia ==

* Although useful, the Master Sword Lv2 or its [[Master Sword Lv3|superior upgrade]] are not required to complete ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' or ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', as [[Ganon]] or [[Yuga Ganon]] can be defeated with the [[Master Sword|standard Master Sword]].
* The Master Sword Lv2 does not make a deeper swing sound in ''A Link Between Worlds'', unlike its indirect prequel.
* As the Master Sword Lv2 is roughly double the power of its normal state, this makes the weapon's strength comparable to the [[Biggoron's Sword]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''.
* The alternate official artwork of the Tempered Sword in ''A Link to the Past'' shows the sword without its red-orange blade, with only its hilt being that color.
* In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', when Link drinks the [[Elixir Soup]], his [[Sword]] gains an orange glow until he takes damage. Coincidentally, the Master Sword when this ability is active becomes like this upgrade of the scared blade, as its strength is also doubled.
* The Master Sword Lv2 in ''A Link Between Worlds'', unlike its appearance in ''A Link to the Past'', looks identical to the traditional Master Sword aside from having a red blade, similar to the Master Sword Lv3.
* In ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|BS Ancient Stone Tablets]]'', for unknown reasons, the Tempered Sword (in-game called [[LV3 Sword]]) has is blade being pink, but retains its original color on the [[Player|BS-X hero]]'s hand.
** It also appears pink in its inventory icon in ''A Link Between Worlds''.
* Due to the distinctive reddish color of the sacred blade, the Master Sword Lv2 is often compared to the popular [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber Lightsaber] weapon from the ''[https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars Star Wars]'' franchise by fans, specifically [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_lightsaber ones used by Sith Lords]. This also applies for its Lv3 counterpart.
