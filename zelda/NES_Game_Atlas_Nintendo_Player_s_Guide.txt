'''''NES Game Atlas Nintendo Player's Guide''''' is a strategy guide authored and published by [[Nintendo of America]] in 1991. It includes coverage on select [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] games, and the second chapter, titled "Amazing Zelda," specifically has strategies for {{TLoZ}} and {{TAoL}}.
