'''Tattoos''' have been becoming more and more evident in {{TLoZ|Series}} as character art and models have increased in detail. The first mention of a body marking was the [[Triforce]] crest appearing on [[Link]]'s hand in ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link]]'' and likewise throughout the series, although  this wasn't meant to be interpreted as a tattoo.

The Bomb Shop Owner from {{OoT|-}} and {{MM|-}} has tattoos on his head and on his arms. The symbols on his arms are similar to the symbols on the Treasure Chest Game Owner/Fisherman's shoulders.
{{clear}}

==[[Goron]]s==
[[File:TP Goron Render.png|right|160px]]
All Gorons proudly wear the [[Symbols#Race Emblems|Goron Emblem]] on their biceps. The design has a basic tribal appearance of presumably a pawprint, made of isoceles triangles and a central diamond shape. This tattoo also matches the shape of the [[Goron's Ruby]]. Gorons in {{TP|-}} and {{ST}} have many more body markings that have not been confirmed to be either tattoos nor body paint. The war paint speculation has credence given the state of alert the Gorons are in during the events of Link's quest.
{{clear}}

==[[Mikau]]==
[[File:MM Mikau Artwork.png|left|137px]]
Mikau, the guitarist of [[The Indigo-Go's]] from {{MM|-}}, has a large tattoo on his right arm. The tattoo can also be seen on [[Zora Link]]'s replica created by the "[[Elegy of Emptiness]]". He also appears to have one on his head and neck as well.
{{clear}}

==[[Baito]]==
[[File:Baito Figurine Back.png|right|80px]]
Baito, [[Koboli]]'s assistant at the [[Island Postal Service]] found on [[Dragon Roost Island]] in {{TWW}}, has tribal tattoos on his body, and notably an [[Eye Symbol]] on his back.
{{clear}}

==[[Zauz]]==
[[File:PH Zauz Tattoo.png|thumb|left|256px|The visible tattoo]]
Zauz, the blacksmith who helps Link by forging the [[Phantom Sword]] in {{PH}}, has a large purple tattoo running over his shoulder similar to that of Baito.
{{clear}}

==[[Madame Fanadi]]==
[[File:Fortuneteller.png‎|right|160px|The eye symbol on Madame Fanadi's forehead in addition to her red eyes have led many to believe she is a Sheikah.]]
Madame Fanadi, the [[Fortune Teller|fortune teller]] from {{TP}}, has the [[Eye Symbol|Sheikah symbol]] on her forehead. The eye symbol on her forehead in addition to her red eyes have led many to believe she is a [[Sheikah]].
{{clear}}

==[[Monkey]]==
