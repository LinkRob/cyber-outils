{{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Bella||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a character in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bella|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref> She lives and owns a cafe in [[Port Town]]. Bella buys [[Ingredient]]s from [[Tingle]], but later in the game, she sells [[recipe]]s for  juices to Tingle to help heal the [[Journalist]]. Tingle can purchase several such recipes from her.

==Nomenclature==
Bella's Japanese name, ''Komachi'', literally means "town beauty," much like her English name means "beautiful" in Italian.
