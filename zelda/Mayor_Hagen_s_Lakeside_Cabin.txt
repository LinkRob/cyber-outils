{{Term/Store|Mayor Hagen's Lakeside Cabin||Series, TMC}},<ref>{{Cite|This way to Mayor Hagen's Lakeside Cabin|Sign|TMC}}</ref> also known as the '''Cabin by the Lake''',<ref>{{Cite|The mayor took off for his {{Color|TMC Green|cabin by the lake}} clutching a {{Color|TMC Red|book}}. We saw him go...|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> is a location in {{TMC}}. Located on the southeastern portion of {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, it is owned by {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen|link}}, and it is here where he comes to enjoy his hobby of making masks.<ref>{{Cite|I hope I don't sound like I'm bragging, but I have a cabin at Lake Hylia. It's a gorgeous little place where I go to enjoy my hobby, mask making.|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must travel to the {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's Lakeside Cabin|display=Lakeside Cabin}} to retrieve one of the three {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}}, ''{{Term|TMC|A History of Masks|link}}''.<ref>{{Cite|The mayor took off for his cabin by the lake clutching a book. We saw him go... But when he came back, he didn't have the book anymore. I'm sure he just forgot it at the lake cabin.|Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm sure he just left it at his {{Color|TMC Green|lakeside cabin}}. He's pretty forgetful like that.|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> The book must be knocked down from the shelf using the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}}. Once done, the book must be taken back to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}, where {{Term|TMC|Link}} can then meet the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} elder {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}} and receive the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}.
