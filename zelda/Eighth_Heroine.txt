The {{Term/Store|Eighth Heroine||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography==
The {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine}} is a rumored eighth member of the {{Plural|BotW|Seven Heroines|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Everyone's heard the legend in the Gerudo region about the seven heroines, but...some tell of an {{Color|BotW Red|eighth heroine}}...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> The Heroines are known as the divine protectors of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} people.<ref>{{Cite|It's dedicated to the {{Color|BotW Blue|seven giant statues of the heroines}}, said to be the divine protectors of the Gerudo.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine}} is still worshiped by those faithful to her.<ref>{{Cite|There's a legend about seven heroines in the Gerudo region, but it turns out that an {{Color|BotW Red|eighth heroine}} existed, and she is worshiped to this day by her faithful in the {{Color|BotW Red|Gerudo Highlands}}. Show a picture of the {{Color|BotW Red|eighth statue's torso}} to Bozai to get the sand boots. You've heard that there's someone well acquainted with the seven heroines' legend in Gerudo Town.|The Eighth Heroine|BotW}}</ref> Although massive statues of the {{Plural|BotW|Seven Heroines}} can be found at the {{Term|BotW|East Gerudo Ruins|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Statue of the Eighth Heroine|link}} is found in the northern {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. This Statue plays a role in the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Eighth Heroine|link}}", given by {{Term|BotW|Bozai|link}}. Though the {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine}} was wiped from history and is not proven to exist,<ref>{{Cite|Wiped clean from history, and no one knows why or how...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} sends {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} in search of its Statue.<ref>{{Cite|Find her! Show me the {{Color|BotW Red|eighth heroine}}, and I'll give you these {{Color|BotW Blue|sand boots}}. What do you say?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} shows {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} a picture of the Statue, he can receive another {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} from {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} titled "{{Term|BotW|The Forgotten Sword|link}}", where {{Term|BotW|Link}} must find the Statue's missing Sword on the southeastern section of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Summit|link}}.

During the {{Term|TotK|Side Quest}} "{{Term|TotK|The Heroines' Secret|link}}", it is revealed the {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine}} was actually an ancient Hylian who roused the {{Plural|BotW|Seven Heroines}} against a great evil and saved the Gerudo people, but afterwards was forbidden from entering Gerudo Town due to the law prohibiting a man's entry. The Gerudo would later regret this fact, and as penance, subsumed the Hylian into the {{Plural|BotW|Seven Heroines}}, with the truth eventually becoming lost to time.
