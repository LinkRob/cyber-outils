"{{Term/Store|The Seven Heroines||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Seven Heroines}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Gerudo Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Rotana|link}} studying in her home next to {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}}. When she notices {{Term|BotW|Link}}, she wonders what a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} is doing there.<ref>{{Cite|Mmm... Huh? And what is a Hylian vai like yourself doing here?|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} instead asks what she is doing, {{Term|BotW|Rotana}} explains that she is an archaeologist and that she was reviewing some ancient texts she discovered recently.<ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] I'm an archaeologist...of sorts. I was just poring over some ancient texts that I discovered the other day...|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Rotana}} was able to partially decode the text, though she thinks she is close to completely understanding it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|And I've finally decoded it!}} Well...part of it, at least. ...But the rest is not far off.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> She believes that this discovery will allow her to make a name for herself in the field of archaeology.<ref>{{Cite|With this discovery, I'll finally be able to make a name for myself as an archaeologist!|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> Stating that her meeting with {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a work of fate,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|OF COURSE!}} You and I meeting here is no coincidence. I've never been one to question fate.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> she decides to share her research with him in confidentiality.<ref>{{Cite|I can't explain why...but I trust you for some reason. I'll tell you the details of my research if you can keep a secret.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} promises to keep what he hears a secret, she explains that there is a temple near the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} dedicated to the statues of the {{Plural|BotW|Seven Heroines|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[My lips are sealed.] There's a temple east of here near the entrance to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Desert}}. It's dedicated to the {{Color|BotW Blue|seven giant statues of the heroines}}|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> At this temple, {{Term|BotW|Rotana}} found text that describes a "blessed path" being revealed when the seven symbols are in the rightful places.<ref>{{Cite|It was at this temple that I originally discovered the ancient scripture. It reads: "When the symbols of the seven find their place, the blessed path will be revealed." ...That's all I've been able to decipher so far.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> In the path, people used to travel from all over the world to find the blessing of the {{Plural|BotW|Seven Heroines}}, but none ever found it.<ref>{{Cite|It's believed that people once came from around the world in search of the heroines' blessing. But as far as I can tell, it doesn't appear that anyone has found it yet...|Rotana|BotW}}</ref>

Though she does not know what this blessing is, {{Term|BotW|Rotana}} thinks she will find out after deciphering the rest of the text.<ref>{{Cite|[Blessing?] I'm not sure what exactly it means, but maybe I'll find out once I've deciphered more of the text.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> In addition, each {{Term|BotW|Seven Heroines|display=Heroine}} held a different power: skill, spirit, endurance, knowledge, flight, motion, and gentleness.<ref>{{Cite|I'm not sure if this is related, but it's said that the heroines held powers that were part of a bigger whole. Each heroine had a different power: skill, spirit, endurance, knowledge, flight, motion, and gentleness. But they all look the same to me.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Rotana}} believes that she will soon understand the entire text.<ref>{{Cite|Before long, I'll understand the entire text!|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> With {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} calmed, {{Term|BotW|Rotana}} can now conduct her research in peace.{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has not calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} yet, {{Term|BotW|Rotana}} says that understanding the text is only the beginning of her problems.<ref>{{Cite|I should understand the entire script before long, but that's only the beginning of my problems.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} has made it difficult for her to continue her research.<ref>{{Cite|That {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} has been moving around the area, making it harder for me to conduct my research. That thing has no respect for academia!|Rotana|BotW}}</ref>}} When she finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

At the {{Term|BotW|East Gerudo Ruins|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find seven metal orbs scattered about. The symbols on these orbs correspond to symbols located on one of the {{Term|BotW|Seven Heroines}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must place the orbs into the socket in front of their corresponding statue, which will cause {{Term|BotW|Korsh O'hu Shrine|link}} to rise from the sand.<ref>{{Cite|The ancient shrine appeared after you matched the symbols on the orbs with the symbols of the seven heroines.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
