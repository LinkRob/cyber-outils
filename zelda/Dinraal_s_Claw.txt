==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Dinraal's Claw}} can be collected from the {{Term|BotW|Dragon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}}, who typically flies around the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Mountains|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must shoot its hands with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Dinraal's Claw|display=Claws}} in question will fall onto the land in the form of light.

{{Plural|BotW|Dinraal's Claw}} that are used in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} will make {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}. However, when used with critters, the result will be {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}. At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Dinraal's Claw}} are one of the ingredients that can be used to make {{Term|BotW|Crimson Dye|link}}. They also count as {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, which means they can be exchanged for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Dinraal's Claw}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}:
