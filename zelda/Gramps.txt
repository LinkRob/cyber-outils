{{Term/Store|Gramps||Series, ALBW}} is a character that appears in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>{{Note|This character is also known as '''Old Man StreetPass''' on [[Zelda.com]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= |retrieved= April 24, 2020|url= http://zelda.com/link-between-worlds/heroic/|title= |site= Official A Link Between Worlds Website|type= }}</ref> However, because {{E}} takes precedence, it is not considered [[Zelda Wiki:Canon Policy|canon]].}} He is an elderly citizen in charge of the game's [[StreetPass]] functionality, {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link Battle|link}}, and stands in the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Meadow|link}} on the western side of {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}}. 

Far stronger than [[Yuga Ganon]], who is the [[Final Boss]], Gramps also acts as the game's [[wikipedia:Boss_(video_games)#:~:text=A%20superboss%20is%20a%20type,all%20optional%20bosses%20are%20superbosses.|Superboss]]. Once all 50 Challenge Medals have been collected, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} can be challenged to a duel in a similar fashion to fighting a [[Shadow Link]]. He himself wields upgraded versions of all the items and gear available to [[Link|Link.]] He is, however, entirely optional and therefore not required to be beaten in order to complete the game.

==Biography==
Upon first speaking to {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}}, he will explain how StreetPass works and help Link create his {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}}. Once a {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}} has been created for the first time, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} will plant the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree|link}} just west of {{Term|ALBW|Link's House|link}}. The {{Plural|ALBW|Apple|link}} on the Tree will increase with the number of challenges that are completed.{{Fact|ALBW}} {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} can be spoken to at any time to make changes to the {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}}'s inventory, look at the list of Challenges and earned Medals, or have him repeat an explanation of how StreetPass works. Occasionally when speaking to him, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} will ask Link if he has visited the Apple Tree. If Link says that he hasn't, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} will wonder aloud that the young hero must prefer oranges to apples.{{Fact|ALBW}} However, if Link claims that he has visited the Tree when he really hasn't, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} will catch him in the lie.{{Fact|ALBW}}

Speaking to {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} after obtaining all 50 Challenge Medals prompts him to challenge Link to a duel.<ref name="Shadow Link"/> Defeating him rewards the hero with 5 [[Rupee|Rupees]], and allows Link to rematch him at any time.<ref>{{Cite|Now, putting that aside... Would you like to battle again? [No] I see... That's a shame. If you ever feel like fighting, come talk to me again.|Gramps|ALBW}}</ref> {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} explains that his bounty is so low because he pays for all the {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}} bounties out of his own pocket, and also reveals that he established the Shadow Link Battles so that he could meet someone who is stronger than him.<ref>{{Cite|I lost... But I had a hard time deciding how many Rupees to give to fighters who defeated me... Ha! I'm not so bold as to put a high price on my head. That's why the reward is so meager.|Gramps|ALBW}}</ref>

{{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} is also shown during the [[credits]] sequence, following scenes of the {{Plural|ALBW|Sage|link}} returning home and the [[Hyrulean]]s celebrating. Unlike the other characters, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} himself is not seen to be celebrating, but simply standing in the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Meadow}}.

{{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} bears numerous notable similarities to Link himself: he wears the [[Green Tunic]], wields a vast arsenal of items, and even has the capability to wield the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword|link}}. ==In battle==
[[File:ALBW Gramps Battle.png|thumb|left|248x248px|{{Term|ALBW|Link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} preparing to face off against each other in the Sky Arena]]
Going by the player name of "'''Legendary'''", {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} has 20 [[Heart Container|Heart Containers]], all 50 Challenge Medals and fully upgraded gear; the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword Lv3|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Hylian Shield|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Red Mail|link}} (although it is not visible on him), {{Term|ALBW|Pegasus Boots|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Stamina Scroll|link}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Bee Badge|link}}. He will use two of any item in its upgraded form and may also use {{Term|ALBW|Blue Potion|link|display=Blue}}, {{Term|ALBW|Yellow Potion|link|display=Yellow}} or {{Term|ALBW|Purple Potion|link}}. In addition, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} also knows the [[Great Spin]]. Essentially, he is copying Link with all possible [[Upgrading|upgrades]], making himself a deadly opponent and will prove a challenge for an unprepared player.

The battle against {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} will take place in a randomly selected arena. However, the Desert Arena is usually only chosen if he has equipped the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod|link}}. He is extremely agile, and will frequently dodge Link's attacks or defend himself with his {{Term|ALBW|Shield}}.

===Characteristics===
The battle will differ somewhat depending on the arena and the items that {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} brings to the fight. In particular, the Purple Potion can be very difficult for Link to combat against if {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} is given the opportunity to use it, as it gives him the ability of double damage; It will deal up to 8 Hearts of damage, except in [[Hero Mode]] where it can instantly deal up to a devastating '''32''' Hearts, always resulting in a 1-hit KO. It is not recommended to bring {{Plural|ALBW|Bee|link}}, which are completely ineffective on {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} as he carries the {{Term|ALBW|Bee Badge}}. It is best to circle around {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} and continually stun him with the {{Term|ALBW|Boomerang|link}}, opening a chance to safely attack him and preventing him from using his items.

==Trivia==
*Due to Gramps being able to mirror [[Link]] in every way in skill and abilities even more so than [[Dark Link]], along with wearing the [[Green Clothes]] and wielding the [[Master Sword]]/[[Hylian Shield]], making him arguably the most dangerous opponent in the entire franchise, some fans believe this character to be a former incarnation of the main protagonist, specifically the Hero of Legend from {{aLttP}}/{{LA}}. Despite this, it is not confirmed by [[Nintendo]].
*{{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} is tied with [[Shadow Link]] for the highest potential damage output of any vulnerable enemy in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]], able to deal 32 Hearts of damage with a single use of the {{Term|ALBW|Purple Potion}} in [[Hero Mode]].
*{{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} is the only [[Hylian]] [[Boss]] and one of the few "friendly bosses" of the series. Along with Dark Link in {{ST}}, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} is also one of two secret bosses in the games. 
*Due to the one-on-one duel being a StreetPass battle, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} is the only boss who is unable to actually kill Link if he defeats him, and therefore will not result a [[Game Over]].
*{{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} musing that Link prefers oranges is a reference to the idiom of comparing [[wikipedia:Apples and oranges|apples and oranges]].
*Gramps is one of the very few characters known to wield the Blade of Evil's Bane in the series, with the others being Link and [[Princess Zelda]]. He is also the only person besides the hero to wield any upgraded form of the Master Sword (excluding the [[Player|BS-X avatars]] in ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|BS Ancient Stone Tablets]]'').
*The optional duel with Gramps is one of the rare cases in the series or other media where more than one Master Sword is in existence.
