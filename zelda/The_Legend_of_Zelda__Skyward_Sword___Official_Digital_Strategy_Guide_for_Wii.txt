'''''The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword — Official Digital Strategy Guide for Wii''''' is a digital strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{SS}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The guide has the same contents as "[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword — Prima Official Game Guide]]" 2011 edition, containing a complete walkthrough of {{SS}}, collectibles and secrets such as {{Plural|SS|Gratitude Crystal|link}}, and listings for equipment upgrades, {{Plural|SS|Treasure|link}}, {{Plural|SS|Insect|link}}, and {{Term|SS|Potion|link}} recipes.

It is served using Prima's in-browser digital guide service and, unlike most other Prima eGuides, doesn't have sample chapters.<ref>{{Cite Web|author= Prima Games|url=https://eguides.primagames.com/games/legend-zelda-skyward-sword/guides/legend-of-zelda-skyward-sword-eguide|site=eguides.primagames.com|title=The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword Official Digital Strategy Guide for Wii|retrieved=October 1, 2022}}</ref> Prior to October 1, 2019, the guide was available for 20 gold points as a [[My Nintendo Rewards|My Nintendo Reward]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= October 1, 2022|url= https://my.nintendo.com/rewards/db7335444dc17f9e|title= Online Access - The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword Prima Official Game Guide|site= my.nintendo.com|type= }}</ref>
