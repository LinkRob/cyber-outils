The {{Term/Store|Dragon Freight Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dragon Freight Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Dragon Freight Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of two {{Plural|ST|Pirate Necklace|link}}, two {{Plural|ST|Ruto Crown|link}} and six {{Plural|ST|Bee Larvae|link}}. Its design is based on the tail of a Chinese dragon seen in {{Wp|Dragon dance}} parades, and its description reads "Mythical beasts are great--especially when they haul your stuff."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Dragon Freight Car}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Dragonhead Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
