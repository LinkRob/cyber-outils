'''Yoshiyuki Oyama''' is a character designer and senior lead artist for [[Nintendo]].

==Biography==
{{Stub}}
Oyama joined Nintendo in 1999 and has been involved in {{TLoZ|-}} series as a key staff member in character design since {{MM}}. He is also deeply involved as a design director in the ''{{Nw|Wii Fit}}'' series.
