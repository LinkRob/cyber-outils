'''Might Crystals''' are items used for upgrading in {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
{{hatnote|”A crystal infused with a strange power. It can be used for upgrades at Lueburry's.” — [[Key Items in Echoes of Wisdom|Key Items]]<ref name="Might Crystal">{{Cite|A crystal infused with a strange power. It can be used for upgrades at Lueburry's.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Might Crystals are items used by [[Lueburry]] to upgrade the [[Sword of Might]], [[Bow of Might]], [[Bombs of Might]], and [[Princess Zelda]]’s [[Energy]] [[Energy Gauge|Gauge]]. A total of 150 exist, obtained by repairing Rifts, completing quests or mini-games, and scattered across Hyrule.
