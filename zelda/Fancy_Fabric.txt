{{Term/Store|Fancy Fabric|Fancy Fabrics|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 123"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Fancy Fabric}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Fortress|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Sealed Gateway|link}}''', ''"Halved attack and defense!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Fancy Fabric}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Bomb Storage|link}}''', ''"Transport the orb quickly!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Fancy Fabric}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Training Ground|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Fancy Fabric}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Training Ground}}''', ''"Don't drop the pot!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Fancy Fabric}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Fancy Fabric}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Fancy Fabric}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Fancy Fabric}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Rupee Regalia|link}}.<ref name="E 123"/>
