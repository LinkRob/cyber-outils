==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Horns! Horns for Days!}}" is an event wherein a greater number of horned {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Horned spirits are stampeding their way to the Spirit Board!|author= Nintendo |published= June 16, 2020 |retrieved= August 13, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/fd05d654/index.html |title= Event: Horns! Horns for Days! |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
