The {{Term/Store|Book of Seals||Series, OoA}}, is an item in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 118 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Book of Seals is found in [[Eyeglass Island Library]] in the Present. It is needed to solve the Invisible Floor Puzzle in the Library in the Past. Without the Book of Seals, [[Link]] cannot get the [[Fairy Powder]].<ref>{{Cite|Fairy Powder is locked away in back, but without another Book of Seals, no one can reach the powder.|N/A|OoA}}</ref>
