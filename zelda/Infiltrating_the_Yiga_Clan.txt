==Overview==
At the {{Term|TotK|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} sees a man named Mimos trying to gain access to the hideout. He is denied entrance by the doorkeeper, unless he finds three branches and acquire their {{Term|TotK|Yiga Clan Armor Set|link|display=clan attire}}.<ref>{{Cite|Find out <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">three branches</font>, and collect the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">clan attire</font>. Earn your place among us and the door will open to you.|N/A|TotK}}</ref> Disappointed, he leaves past Link to figure out the "three branch" places.

Before Mimos leaves, he left a tip to Link that the a {{Term|TotK|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link|display=lab}} in the {{Term|TotK|Akkala|link}} region may have been occupied by the Yiga.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|<font color{{=}}"SlateGrey">I guess there was a rumor the Yiga Clan took over the what's-its-name lab way up northeast in Akkala... Maybe that's something.</font>}}|Mimos|TotK}}</ref> Upon visiting one of these branches, Link encounters the Yiga Clan at these locations.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|You're Link!}} Come to free our tailor, have you? You'll have to get through us first!|Yiga Blademaster|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Who's there? Wait, you— You're LINK! Thought you'd ambush us, did you?! Well, you're the one getting ambushed today!|N/A|TotK}}</ref> Upon defeating the guards, he finds hostages from {{Term|TotK|Hateno Village|link}}, who were forced to make attires for the Yiga Clan.<ref>{{Cite|The name's Konba. I've got a knack for style and the nimble digits I need to spin my ideas into reality. Alas, that pure talent is why the Yiga Clan kidnapped me and forced me to make their outfits for them.|Konba|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Me? I've been making outfits for the Yiga Clan since they kidnapped me. I mean, I like fashion, but I couldn't even sew! Did they listen? No, of course not.|Aliza|TotK}}</ref> After freeing them, they give him pieces of the Yiga Clan attire, often telling him to do whatever he wants with them.<ref>{{Cite|In fact, you should take the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Yiga armor</font> I just finished. Burn it, toss it, do what you want. Just get it away from me.|Konba|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This isn't my size, but it might fit you. Here! Take it so I can keep it out of their grimy mitts.|Aliza|TotK}}</ref>

Eventually, Link acquires a complete set of the Yiga clan attire. Wearing it, he is able to enter the Yiga Clan headquarters through the door.<ref>{{Cite|... All right. Come in.|N/A|TotK}}</ref>
