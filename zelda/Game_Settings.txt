The {{Term/Store|Game Settings||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} menu is an interface in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Game Settings|Settings|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|Game Settings}} are a submenu in the {{Term|HWDE|Settings|link}} menu. The {{Term|HWDE|Game Settings}} menu allows the player to change the settings of the gameplay to their preferences.<ref name="Settings"/> The first setting is the difficulty level, where the player can choose how much of a challenge they want to give themselves.<ref>{{Cite|Difficulty Level|Game Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Set difficulty level.|Game Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The second setting is the health gauge, which allows the player to see every health gauge of every {{Term|HWDE|Enemy|link}} while in battle.<ref>{{Cite|Health Gauge|Game Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Turn enemy grunt health gauges on or off.|Game Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The third setting is the midbattle cinematics, which determines whether cutscenes like {{Plural|HWDE|Giant Boss|link}} and enemy {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} appearing play while in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Midbattle Cinematics|Game Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Set whether cinematics play in Adventure Mode.|Game Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The fourth and final setting in the 8-bit weapons setting.<ref>{{Cite|8-Bit Weapons|Game Settings|HWDE}}</ref> This setting allows the player to change the appearance of some multi-element weapons to instead be 8-bit versions of references to other items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Choose whether or not you want certain multi-element weapons to instead be 8-bit weapons.|Game Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The default version of these settings are the difficulty level set to normal, the health gauge off, the midbattle cinematics on, and finally the 8-bit weapons off. The player can return to these default settings quickly by pressing the return to default button.<ref>{{Cite|Return to Default|Game Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Reset current state and return settings to default?|Game Settings|HWDE}}</ref>
