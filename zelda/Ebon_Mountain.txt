{{Term/Store|Ebon Mountain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Ebon Mountain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Ebon Mountain}} is located in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}, southwest of [[Hateno Village]]. At the top of the mountain, there is a pond shaped like a broken heart. This pond is commonly mistaken as the Lovers' Pond, also in East Necluda west of [[Lurelin Village]].

==Nomenclature==
Ebon Mountain derives its name from ''ebony''.
