{{Term/Store|Ordon Pumpkin|Ordon Pumpkins|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are recurring [[object]]s and [[item]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|LCT}} However, they appear mainly in {{TP}}.

==Location and uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
Ordon Pumpkins are crops that are grown in [[Ordon Village]].<ref>{{Cite|You got the Ordon pumpkin! This pumpkin was grown in your hometown of Ordon.|N/A|TP}}</ref> Several Ordon Pumpkins can be found growing in pumpkin patches in the village, which will drop [[Pumpkin Seed]]s when destroyed. These seeds can be used as [[Slingshot]] ammunition. [[Link]] can also pour water on smaller pumpkins in order to make them grow.

An Ordon Pumpkin can also be found in the [[Snowpeak Ruins]] inside a [[Treasure Chest]] while Link searches for the ruins' [[Bedroom Key]]. [[Yeta]] mistakenly directs Link to its location as a result of her forgetting where the Key is.<ref>{{Cite|You find it, uh? ...What? Pumpkin? But why there? No good, uh... Where I leave it?|Yeta|TP}}</ref> This becomes her first mistake, while the [[Ordon Goat Cheese]] becomes her second when she leads Link to its location.

When the Ordon Pumpkin is brought to [[Yeto]], he will knock Link to the floor and take the Pumpkin to use in his Soup.<ref>{{Cite|What flavor... Uh... PUMPKIN! You have pumpkin, uh? Pumpkin, pumpkin, uh! Thanks, uh! You taste if you want!|Yeto|TP}}</ref> It will upgrade his Simple Soup into Good Soup. This new Soup will replenish four [[Heart]]s instead of the Simple Soup's two.<ref>{{Cite|You put good soup in your bottle! It smells sweet, like pumpkins. Set it to B on the - screen, and drink it with B to replenish 4 hearts.|N/A|TP}}</ref>
