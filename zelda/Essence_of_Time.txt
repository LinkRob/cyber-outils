The {{Term/Store|Essence of Time|Essences of Time|Series, OoA|plural}} are [[Item]]s in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= Dark Horse Books|page= 252}}</ref> 

==Location and Uses==
The Essence of Times are the eight relics that [[Link]] must obtain to help the [[Maku Tree]] and save [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]]. Each one is hidden in one of the puzzle- and enemy-filled [[Dungeon]]s spread across [[Labrynna]]. When Link obtains all eight [[Essence of Time|Essences]], the Maku Tree gives him a special [[Huge Maku Seed|Seed]] called the [[Huge Maku Seed]] needed to enter [[Black Tower]] and defeat the evil [[Veran]].
