The {{Term/Store|Secret Shrine||Series, MM, MM3D}},<ref>{{Cite|Secret Shrine|Map|MM3D}}</ref> also known as the '''Secret Shrine behind Waterfall''',<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Secret Shrine behind Waterfall}}<br/>Now awaiting the challenges of bold visitors sure of their might.<br/>Hee-hee-hee!|Sign|MM3D}}</ref> is a location in {{MM}}.

==Features and Overview==
[[File:MM3D Secret Shrine Notebook Portrait.png|left|The picture used for the Secret Shrine in the Bombers' Notebook from {{MM3D|-}}]]
The Secret Shrine can be found in [[Ikana Canyon]], behind the waterfall of the [[Ikana River]]. It is accessible only after [[Link]] has obtained the [[Light Arrow]]s, since the door can only be unlocked by shooting a Light Arrow at the [[Sun Pattern]] on top of the entrance.

Once inside, Link once again meets the [[Poe Collector]], who informs him that the Shrine is full of creatures that are eagerly awaiting a rematch with him.<ref>{{Cite|The ones in this room want to meet you again and have been waiting here for quite a while. Go see them if you feel like it...|Poe Collector|MM}}</ref> The Shrine contains four rooms, with each containing a different mini-boss that Link had encountered before. However, Link must have at least a certain number of [[Heart Container]]s to battle them.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to meet the ones in this room, then extend your life energy more.|Poe Collector|MM}}</ref>

A [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Piece of Heart]] is revealed at the center of the Shrine once Link successfully defeats all the enemies within.

In {{MM3D}}, upon beating all the enemies a second time, the Treasure Chest holds a [[Fishing Hole Pass]] instead.
