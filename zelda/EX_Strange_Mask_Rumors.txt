"{{Term/Store|EX Strange Mask Rumors||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Strange Mask Rumors}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Woodland Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TMT}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is obtained upon leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with finding a copy of the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill EX|link}}, which can be found at {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently the {{Color|BotW Red|Woodland Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare mask.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to activate the second stage of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} by reading the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V1}} without triggering any of the loading conditions for its activation.}}

Inside {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable}}, the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V3|link}} can be found on a table. In the {{Term|BotW|Book|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} discusses rumors of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Today I've got a hot rumor fresh from the mill that is...wait for it...an EXCLUSIVE! And that special topic is... {{Big|the mysterious Korok Mask!}}|Super Rumor Mill V1|BotW}}</ref> She reports on a rumor that it is inhabited by a spirit,<ref>{{Cite|They say if you pour enough love into an object, a spirit will inhabit it. Something that creepy's just gotta be true! And apparently, this Korok Mask really is inhabited by a spirit!|Super Rumor Mill V1|BotW}}</ref> and it to shakes when it is worn and {{Term|BotW|Korok|link|display=friends}} are hidden nearby.<ref>{{Cite|If you wear it, it supposedly shakes and makes adorably weird noises every now and then. Maybe it does that to tell you when friends are nearby and you can't see them? Who knows!|Super Rumor Mill V1|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask}} is rumored to be in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} hidden a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} hollow in forest that causes people to become lost.<ref>{{Cite|Some say the treasure chest containing this mask is hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in a tree hollow in a forest that makes people lose their way}}.|Super Rumor Mill V1|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods|link}} south of a pair of {{Plural|BotW|Torch|link}}. It is inside the mouth of an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree|link}}. Once the {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask}} has been retrieved, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.<ref>{{Cite|You found the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Mask}} in the spooky tree's hollow within the Lost woods!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
