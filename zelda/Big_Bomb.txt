{{Term/Store|Big Bomb|Big Bombs|Series, TFH|plural}} are recurring traps and {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 90 {{Exp Game|TFH}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{FSA|-}}===
Big Bombs are a dangerous type of [[Bomb]] in {{FSA}}. Big Bombs are dropped by [[Shadow Link]] and found as traps within Treasure Chests.<ref>{{Cite|You got a Big Bomb! This will blow everything to smithereens! Uh-oh...RUN!!|N/A|FSA}}</ref> Big Bombs explode after 10 seconds and blast the entire area. The timer can explode faster if a Big Bomb is slashed with the Four Sword, with each slash reducing the timer by one second. If the Links are hit by its blast, they will be defeated instantly, regardless of the number of hearts they have at that moment.<ref>{{Cite|A giant bomb! Run away!|N/A|FSA}}</ref> In order to avoid its blast, the Links can escape into a hole,<ref>{{Cite|A giant bomb! Escape to your Game Boy Advance!|N/A|FSA}}</ref> walk into a cave, or by diving underwater.<ref>{{Cite|A giant bomb! Quickly! Dive underwater!|N/A|FSA}}</ref>

==={{TFH|-}}===
When one of the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} wears the {{Term|TFH|Big Bomb Outfit|link}}, all {{Plural|TFH|Bomb|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Bomb Flower|link}} that {{Term|TFH|Link}} uses turn into {{Plural|TFH|Big Bomb}}, which have an increased blast radius and inflict double damage. {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}} have a blast radius that reaches one level above or below, while {{Plural|TFH|Big Bomb}} reach two levels above or below.

During the {{Term|TFH|Boss|link}} battle in {{Term|TFH|Bomb Storage|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Hinox Brothers|link|display=Li'l Hinox}} throws an even larger {{Term|TFH|Big Bomb}} at the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}. This {{Term|TFH|Big Bomb|display=Bomb}} bears a {{Term|TFH|Hinox|link}} marking and has triple the blast radius and damage output of a regular {{Term|TFH|Bomb}}. It is too heavy for any of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to lift and will only move a short distance when blown with the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}}, but with precise timing it can be fired back at {{Term|TFH|Hinox Brothers|display=Li'l Hinox}} to damage him.

== Trivia ==

* The icon for bombs in the Spaceworld 2001 footage of [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|{{TWW|-}}]] was similar to the sprite of the Big Bomb in [[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|''Four Swords Adventures'']] and may have been the basis for that sprite.
