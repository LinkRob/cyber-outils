The '''Rug''' is an [[item]] in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Rug is a blue rug that, when equipped, makes [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] slide around less on slippery surfaces. However, a drawback is that she cannot attack with her [[Wand]] while using it. The Rug is dropped by the second [[Vapora]] in the [[Shrine of Air]].
