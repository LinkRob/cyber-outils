The {{Term/Store|Symmetry City Ruins||Series, OoA}} are a location in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Those lazy, worthless fools! just as we're buildin' this bridge to the {{Color|OoA Blue|Symmetry City Ruins}}, they go off on break! I wait and I wait, but they don't come back!|Mutoh|OoA}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|OoA|Link|link}} first enters {{Term|OoA|Symmetry City|link}} in its ruined state and overtaken by {{Plural|OoA|Podoboo Tower|link}}, the {{Term|OoA|Symmetry City Ruins}}. By traveling to the past to {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village|link}}, {{Term|OoA|Link}} finds the cause of the City's ruin is the broken {{Term|OoA|Tuni Nut|link}}, which retains balance in the Village. {{Term|OoA|Link}}, with the aid of {{Term|OoA|Patch's Crazy Cart|link}} on the {{Term|OoA|Restoration Wall|link}}, repairs the {{Term|OoA|Tuni Nut}} and returns it to the Village. When he returns to the present, the {{Term|OoA|Symmetry City Ruins}} have been saved and became the peaceful {{Term|OoA|Symmetry City}}, revealing the path to the {{Term|OoA|Skull Dungeon|link}}.
