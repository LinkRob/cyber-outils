{{Term/Store|Master Spirit|Master Spirits|Series, SSBU|plural}} are items in {{SSBU}}.

==Location and Uses==
Master Spirits are [[Spirit]]s that operate various facilities in the [[World of Light]] and the Collection sub-menu in the Spirits menu. In order for a Master Spirit to operate a facility, it must be freed in a [[Spirit Battle]] in the World of Light. Master Spirits cannot be used in battles.
