The {{Term/Store|Freebie Card||Series, PH, ST}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{PH}} and {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|PH, ST}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
When {{Term|PH|Link|link}} obtains 20 points on his {{Term|PH|Beedle's Membership Card|link|display=Membership Card}}, {{Term|PH|Beedle|link}} will write him a {{Term|PH|Letter|link}} to inform him of his new {{Term|PH|Silver Card|link}} and rewards him with a {{Term|PH|Freebie Card}}.<ref>{{Cite|Silver Membership Notice! Thank you for continuing to support my stores. (Hi, it's me! Beedle!) Your recent purchase now puts your points at 20. (Yay for you!) Thus, your membership has been upgraded to Silver. (Yay again!) As thanks, please find attached a {{Color|PH Red|Freebie Card}}. (Oooooh, free!) Use it at any of my stores.<br/><br/>-Beedle (Bye!)|Beedle|PH}}</ref> When {{Term|PH|Link}} uses the {{Term|PH|Freebie Card}} at {{Term|PH|Beedle's Shop Ship|link}}, he will be able to obtain a free {{Term|PH|Item|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|PH Red|Freebie Card}}! Use it at Beedle's shop to shop for free!|N/A|PH}}</ref>

If {{Term|PH|Link}} boards {{Term|PH|Beedle's Shop Ship}} with a {{Term|PH|Freebie Card}} in his {{Term|PH|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|PH|Beedle}} asks if he would like to use it.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhhh, a {{Color|PH Red|Freebie Card}}? Do you want to use it?|Beedle|PH}}</ref> When {{Term|PH|Link}} agrees to use it, {{Term|PH|Beedle}} will take the {{Term|PH|Freebie Card|display=Card}} from him, at which point {{Term|PH|Link}} is able to take one {{Term|PH|Item}} from his display for free.<ref>{{Cite|Right away! One {{Color|PH Red|Freebie Card}}! Oh, happy day!|Beedle|PH}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|PH|Beedle}} thanks him for using his {{Term|PH|Freebie Card}}.<ref>{{Cite|Done! Thank you so much for using your {{Color|PH Red|Freebie Card}}!|Beedle|PH}}</ref>

==={{ST|-}}===
After joining the Beedle Member's Club, {{Term|ST|Link|link}} can obtain the {{Term|ST|Freebie Card}} when he becomes a Silver Member.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh! You have 200 points! You're now an official {{Color|ST Light Red|Silver Member}}! That means you get {{Color|ST Light Red|10% off all merchandise}} AND a {{Color|ST Light Red|lovely prize}}!|Beedle|ST}}</ref>
The {{Term|ST|Freebie Card}} allows {{Term|ST|Link}} to redeem it in exchange for one free {{Term|ST|Item|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|ST Light Red|Freebie Card}}! Choose any item you want, and you'll get it for free!|N/A|ST}}</ref>
