The {{Term/Store|Second Day||Series, MM, MM3D}} is the second of the three total days that [[Link]] has before the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] crashes into [[Clock Town]] and destroys all of [[Termina]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 246}}</ref> From the dawn of this day, the young hero and [[Tatl]] have 48 hours left to complete different tasks. 

==Overview==
[[File:Scarescrow(MM).jpg|thumb|252x252px|Link next to [[Pierre]] the [[Scarecrow]] during the nighttime of the Second Day]]
By now, more townsfolk are starting to become aware of the impending fate that could befall them, as the Moon now appears much closer, and some of them have even begun to make plans to flee out of the town. The environment and the theme of Clock Town reflect their restlessness, for the weather across [[Termina]] is unpleasant with a constant thunderstorm during the daytime and the Clock Town music has taken a slightly faster pace, with a few less instruments compared to [[First Day]]. At nighttime, the weather stops raining and becomes partly cloudy.

== Trivia ==

* In the Japanese version of the game, the Second Day is called " '''The Next Day'''". Despite this, the HUD's clock still reads "2nd".
* In the [[Bombers' Notebook]] as well as the time meter in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'', the Second Day is marked with a yellow colored theme.
* If debugs are used to reveal the originally planned seven-day cycle, the Second Day was going to be called "'''Tuesday'''".
