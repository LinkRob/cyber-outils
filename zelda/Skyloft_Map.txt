==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SS|Skyloft Map}} are {{Plural|SS|Map|link}} of {{Term|SS|Skyloft|link}} that can be found in the {{Term|SS|Knight Academy|link}}. {{Plural|SS|Skyloft Map}} cover the general locations of {{Term|SS|Statue of the Goddess|link}}, the {{Term|SS|Residential Quarter|link}}, the {{Term|SS|Plaza|link}}, and the {{Term|SS|Knight Academy}} in relation to {{Term|SS|Skyloft}}. Three {{Plural|SS|Skyloft Map|display=Maps}} can be found in total: one on the second floor by the exit, one in the classroom, and one in the dining hall.
