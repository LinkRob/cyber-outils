The {{Term/Store|Emerald Tablet||Series, SS, SSHD}} is an item in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 122 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|SSHD|Emerald Tablet}} is found {{Term|SSHD|Inside the Statue of the Goddess|link}} in {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft|link}}. When {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} first enters and receives the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Sword|link}}, {{Term|SSHD|Fi|link}} materializes the tablet to give to {{Term|SSHD|Link}} and instructs him to hit the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Crest|link}} with a {{Term|SSHD|Skyward Strike|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Master, the first thing you must do is hit the crest sitting in this room with a Skyward Strike. These blasts are formed of pure energy that charges within your blade when you lift it skyward. Once you have charged your blade, face the crest and swing your sword to send out a powerful Skyward Strike.|Fi|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SSHD|Link}} does this, raising an altar in which the {{Term|SSHD|Emerald Tablet}} can be inserted. When he inserts the tablet, a break in the clouds appears in the southeastern part of the {{Term|SSHD|Sky|link}} and a pillar of green light radiates from it.<ref>{{Cite|Master Link, it is done. Until now, a cloud barrier created by the goddess has separated the world you know from the one below. The tablet you placed in the altar has opened a small rift in the cloud barrier. You can use it to travel through the clouds to the realm below. I have recognized you as my master, and so it is my duty to follow you wherever you may go.|Fi|SS}}</ref> This marks the way to {{Term|SSHD|Faron Woods|link}}.
