{{Term/Store|Skullrope|Skullropes|Series, ALttP, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 203 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW, TFH}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|Series|Skullrope}} are a variety of {{Plural|Series|Rope|link}} that appear as worm-like monsters with skulls for heads.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Plural|ALttP|Skullrope}} in {{ALttP}} appear in {{Term|ALttP|Thieves' Town (Dungeon)|link}}. If {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} pulls one particular {{Term|ALttP|Lever|link}} in the {{Term|ALttP|Dungeon|link}},{{Which}} a group of them will fall from above. Aside from their appearance, they are identical to normal {{Plural|ALttP|Rope|link}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Stub|ALBW}}

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Skullrope}} in {{TFH}} are encountered more frequently than normal {{Plural|TFH|Rope|link}}, and require more hits to be defeated. They attack by jumping above the {{Plural|TFH|Link|link}}' heads and dropping down onto them. The internal file names in {{TFH|-}} refer to {{Plural|TFH|Skullrope}} as "Bubble Ropes".
