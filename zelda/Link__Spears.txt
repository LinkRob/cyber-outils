{{Term/Store|Link: Spears||HWAoC}} is a Level 10 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link: Spears|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Link: Spears}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}, and with only a spear as his {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Endura Shroom|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hightail Lizard|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}}. Once 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos}} are killed, more spawn alongside a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}}. When another 100 are killed, a large amount of {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos}} spawn again. Once the objective is reached, a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawns and the time limit is taken away. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} is defeated.
