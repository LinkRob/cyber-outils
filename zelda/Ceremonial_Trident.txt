The {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} is a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} modeled after the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}} that belonged to {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> It belongs to the {{Term|BotW|Zora Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a collection of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by the {{Plural|BotW|Zora|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref> Though it is visibly identical, the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} is weaker and less durable than the original.<ref name="Inventory"/> However, it does have the same power.<ref>{{Cite|That {{Color|BotW Red|ceremonial trident}} you're holding... In addition to its combat use, it has the same power as the {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}.|Trello|BotW}}</ref> It is used in the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival|link}}, which was started to honor {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|ceremonial trident}} is used in a festival to honor Champion Mipha... The Champion Festival's {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}... Well, it's a fake.|Trello|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} is required to complete "{{Term|BotW|The Ceremonial Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Trello|link}} while the quest is active, he will reveal that he dropped the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} off the west bridge of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|I planned to have that {{Color|BotW Red|ceremonial trident}} examined by Dento. but on the way there, I dropped it off the bridge.|Trello|BotW}}</ref> He decides to abandon it in favor of asking {{Term|BotW|Dento|link}} to make a new one, leaving it for someone else to use.<ref>{{Cite|It doesn't really matter. It's a fake, after all... I'll have to ask Dento to make a new one. If someone happens to pick it up, they can just keep it.|Trello|BotW}}</ref> Under the bridge, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Then, if he jumps off the top of the {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls|link}} and strikes the pedestal at the {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall|link}}' base, {{Term|BotW|Dagah Keek Shrine|link}} will appear.<ref>{{Cite|You thrust the spear that Trello dropped into the pedestal hidden in Veiled Falls, causing an ancient shrine to appear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can visit {{Term|BotW|Dento}} at the {{Term|BotW|Hammerhead|link}} to replace the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} if it is ever broken.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|ceremonial trident}}... I somehow felt it had disappeared from this world. Shall I remake it for you?|Dento|BotW}}</ref> He requires a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear|link}} and five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}} in order to craft a new one.<ref>{{Cite|In that case, I will need a {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora spear}} and {{Color|BotW Red|five pieces}} of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}...|Dento|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} is made of metal. This means that it will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. It sinks if it is dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. However, it can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, and it is unable to be burned by {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Ceremonial Trident}} is primarily used by {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon|link}}. Unlike {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}}, who wields a single {{Term|HWAoC|Lightscale Trident|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} uses two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ceremonial Trident}} in combat. When he arrives in the past, he immediately uses them to protect {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} from {{Term|HWAoC|Waterblight Ganon|link}}'s {{Term|HWAoC|Ice|link}} flail and {{Term|HWAoC|Cryonis|link}} formations.
