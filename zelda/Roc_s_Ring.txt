{{Term/Store|Roc's Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Roc's Ring<br/>Cracked floors don't crumble|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Roc's Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it prevents cracked floors from crumbling <ref name="Description"/>, however, even if Link has the Roc's Ring equipped, thin ice will still crumble under his weight if he stands on it too long, with the resulting water tile damaging him if he doesn't yet have the [[Flippers]]. Roc's Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
