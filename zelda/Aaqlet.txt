Aaqlet is a character in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.

== Biography ==
Aaqlet is a [[Gerudo]] child who lives in [[Gerudo Town|Gerudo Town.]] She is the daughter of the [[Gerudo]] [[Perda]] and the [[Hylian]] [[Wabbin]]. The former works in the [[Gerudo Secret Club]] and the latter sits within the Gerudo shelter jail. 

[[Link]] can find her looking at her secret map in the shelter's inn which shows the location of the [[Gerudo Secret Club]]. If Link has completed the side adventure "[[Mattison's Independence]]", Aaqlet and [[Mattison]] can be seen spending time together around [[Gerudo Town|Gerudo town]] during the day.
