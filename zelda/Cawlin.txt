Cawlin is a student at the [[Knight Academy]]. He is a friend and henchman to Link's personal rival, [[Groose]]. He, Groose, and [[Strich]] all have a reputation for being bullies. He rides a brown [[Loftwing]].

Cawlin is first seen talking with Groose and Strich about how they caught Link's [[Crimson Loftwing]] in their trap. Link overhears them, and Groose proceeds to taunt and berate him until [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] arrives, distracting Groose as he becomes infatuated with her. Cawlin is also one of the four participants in the [[Wing Ceremony]].

Cawlin has a crush on [[Karane]] and eventually asks Link to deliver a love [[Cawlin's Letter|Letter]] he wrote to her.<ref>{{Cite|You fool! How dare you! This is no ordinary piece of paper! It's a {{Color|SS Red|letter}}! A very special one at that! I put my heart into every word... I guess you could say it's sort of like a... All right, fine! It's a love letter!|Cawlin|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So listen up! The person you must give it to is a girl... An upperclassman... Her name is {{Color|SS Blue|Karane}}. You know who I mean, right? And don't you dare...not even by mistake...even think of giving it to that {{Color|SS Red|weirdo in the restroom}}! Swear it!|Cawlin|SS}}</ref> However, Link has a choice between delivering the Letter to Karane or to [[Phoeni]], who appears in the Knight Academy's restroom at night. Doing either will result in Link receiving five [[Gratitude Crystal]]s.

If Link chooses to deliver the Letter to Phoeni,<ref>{{Cite|This is the hand of a ghost. The ghost's original name is unknown. ==Trivia==
*There is an inscription in the lower right hand corner of the chalkboard in the Knight Academy classroom that reads "Karane + Caw...". Although the source of the inscription is unknown, it was likely to have been written by Cawlin.

==Nomenclature==
Cawlin's name is likely derived from "caw", onomatopoeia of the sound crows make.
