{{Term/Store|Purple Keaton|Purple Keatons|Series, TMC}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 187}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Keaton}}
{{Plural|TMC|Purple Keaton}} only appear in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}. They bear a purple color palette in accordance with {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}'s corruption, though they retain the same amount of health and deal no more damage than {{Plural|TMC|Yellow Keaton|link}}. Like {{Plural|TMC|Yellow Keaton}}, they will charge forward at {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} with their daggers; if he is struck, he will lose {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}, though they can be recovered. {{Plural|TMC|Purple Keaton}} often drop {{Plural|TMC|Blue Rupee|link}} when defeated.

==Trivia==
*{{Plural|Series|Keaton}} are derived from the mythological Japanese fox creature, [[Wikipedia:Kitsune|kitsune]], notable for their trickery.{{Fact}}
