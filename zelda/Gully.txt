==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= That is {{Color|SS Blue|Gully}}, a highly mischievous boy. He is the son of {{Color|SS Blue|Piper}}, a food researcher, and is known to have an interest in insects.
}}

Gully is the mischievous son of [[Piper]]. He has an interest in [[Bug|insects]] and the [[Bug Net|Nets]] used to catch them. When Link first encounters Gully, the child asks him to roll into a tree so that he may retrieve the [[Sky Stag Beetle]] that is resting on it.<ref>{{Cite|Hold up, Link! Hey! Hey, Link! Could you help me with something? All you have to do is use {{Icon|SS A}}  to {{Color|SS Green|dash}} and {{Color|SS Green|roll}} into the tree. Just like I've been doing. Dash, then {{Color|SS Red|shake the Nunchuk}} when you're in front of the tree to--BAM! Do a {{Color|SS Green|roll}} right into it! So come on... Pleeeeease?|Gully|SS}}</ref> After Link does so, the Sky Stag Beetle will fly away and Gully will give chase to it.<ref>{{Cite|Whooooooooa! You did it! A {{Color|SS Blue|Sky Stag Beetle}}! Wait up!|Gully|SS}}</ref> After the bug outruns him, he becomes discouraged and mentions that he would need a Bug Net from [[Beedle's Air Shop|Beedle's Airshop]] to catch it in the first place.<ref name="Quote1">{{Cite|Awwww! My {{Color|SS Blue|Sky Stag Beetle}}... So close... Oh well. To catch it, I would need a Bug Net, anyway. I wish I hadn't thrown out the one I bought at {{Color|SS Red|Beedle's Airshop}}. Awwww...|Gully|SS}}</ref>

After Link obtains the [[Goddess Sword]], Gully can be found wandering the [[Skyloft]] Pier. He calls attention to Beedle's Airshop, which houses a Bug Net, but mentions that Link must use a projectile to get Beedle's Airshop to stop.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, look up there! It's {{Color|SS Blue|Beedle's Airshop}}! It's here! He sells {{Color|SS Red|Bug Nets}} there! I want one sooooo bad! There's a bell hanging down from the shop, and I hear if you ring it, Beedle will stop for you and pick you up! But that bell is up off the ground... You'd have to {{Color|SS Red|launch}} something at it to have any hope of ringing it!|Gully|SS}}</ref> If Link purchases a Bug Net, Gully will become jealous,<ref>{{Cite|Hi, Link! WHAT? You went to Beedle's Airshop? Without me?! That's so unfair!|Gully|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ahhh, the wait is killing me! I've got to have a Bug Net! How can I get up to Beedle's Airshop?!|Gully|SS}}</ref> saying that he needs to figure out a way to get up to Beedle's Airshop so that he can purchase a Bug Net and upgrade it into a [[Bug Net#Skyward Sword|Big Bug Net]].<ref>{{Cite|I have to figure out a way to get up to Beedle's Airshop soon so I can get a Bug Net too! Then I'm going straight to the Scrap Shop to get Gondo to upgrade it and make it even bigger!|Gully|SS}}</ref>

Gully's house contains many bug-themed paraphernalia. Aside from the many drawings of bugs, Gully also keeps framed insects beside the dining table. He also has two Bug Nets kept in a barrel with other tools. Gully has many mannerisms of a typical kid, such as wiping his nose with the sleeve of his shirt.

==Trivia==
*Although Gully says that he needs a Bug Net from Beedle's Airshop to catch the Sky Stag Beetle,<ref name="Quote1"/> he already has two Bug Nets in his house.
*Gully was shown as a test character when experimenting with a miniature race like the [[Minish]] in pre-release screenshots for {{BotW}}.
