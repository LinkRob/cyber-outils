{{Term/Store|Chork||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Chork|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Chork}} is a worker at the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}. He spends his mornings drinking at the table inside.<ref>{{Cite|Oooh, I don't have time to be drinkin' right now...|Chork|BotW}}</ref> In the afternoons, he takes care of the {{Plural|BotW|White Goat|link}} outside the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oooh, I don't have time to be lookin' after mountain goats right now...|Chork|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to Chork, he mentions the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff|link}}, south of the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You stayin' here at our stable? That's nice, but check this out. Go way out south of here, and you'll find a flat hill called {{Color|BotW Blue|Washa's Bluff}}. On that hill, there's this mysterious-lookin' platform thingie...|Chork|BotW}}</ref> Previously, he investigated the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} with {{Term|BotW|Geggle|link}}, but neither of them could figure it out.<ref>{{Cite|Me and Geggle tried all day to figure out what it does, but we're stumped...|Chork|BotW}}</ref> If spoken to again, he will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} that a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} {{Term|BotW|Kass|link|display=Bard}} flew to {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff}} to observe it himself.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to see the platform thingie, go south toward {{Color|BotW Blue|Washa's Bluff}}. There was this one...{{Color|BotW Blue|Rito}} guy that went flying to the south after I told him about it. He was a big ol' bard with some fancy instrument thingie. He probably wanted to see the altar thingie himself.|Chork|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Chork}} after completing the "{{Term|BotW|Under a Red Moon|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Chork}} is surprised to hear an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} was hidden by the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}.<ref>{{Cite|Huh?! An {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient shrine}} thingie appeared? Who'da thunk that platform thingie was hidin' a mystery like that!|Chork|BotW}}</ref> He decides that his new goal is to solve the mystery of {{Term|BotW|Mijah Rokee Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I guess that means I have to solve the mystery of the shrine thingie now.|Chork|BotW}}</ref> He mutters to himself about how the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} only deepens the mystery.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Ancient shrine}}...<br/>The mystery deepens...|Chork|BotW}}</ref>
