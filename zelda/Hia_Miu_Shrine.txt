The {{Term/Store|Hia Miu Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Hia Miu Shrine}}<br/>A Major Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Major Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Hia Miu Shrine is located on the {{Term|BotW|Icefall Foothills|link}} in the northwestern corner of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the Shrine's {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hia Miu|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Hia Miu. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Hia Miu|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Hia Miu Shrine features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. As Link approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}} wielding an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe++|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear++|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword++|link}} will rise from a pit to challenge Link. There are metal blocks embedded into the floor of the chamber, which can be lifted with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} to protect Link from the Guardian Scout IV.

Link must defeat the Guardian Scout IV to cause a portcullis separating him from the Monk to rise. Once Link has done so, Hia Miu will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Hia Miu|BotW}}</ref> and afterwards will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Hia Miu|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the portcullis contains a {{Term|BotW|Sapphire|link}}.
