{{Term/Store|Ikana Village||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref name="E 246">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 246}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|MM3D|Ikana Village}} is what remains of the civilization that lived in the {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Kingdom|link}}, situated atop {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Hill|link}} above the {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Canyon|link}}. Following the collapse of the {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Kingdom|display=Kingdom}}, its inhabitants fled.<ref name="E 246"/> By the time {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} arrives in {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}, only {{Term|MM3D|Pamela|link}} and her {{Term|MM3D|Pamela's Father|link|display=Father}} live in the ruins of the {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Village|display=Village}} to research the lingering ghosts of the former {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Kingdom|display=Kingdom}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, that's a nice {{Color|MM3D Red|sword}} you have...
