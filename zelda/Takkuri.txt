The {{Term|MM3D|Takkuri}} in {{MM}} is a large bird that flies around the entrance to {{Term|MM3D|Milk Road|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|Termina Field|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's a Takkuri. Run! This bird steals your items! C'mon! What are you doing just standing around?!|Tatl|MM}}</ref> As it spots [[Link]], it swoops down and attacks him like other flying enemies. If the {{Term|MM3D|Takkuri}} makes a successful hit, it will make Link drop various [[Item|items]] such as {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee|link}}, {{Plural|MM3D|Bomb|link}}, and {{Plural|MM3D|Arrow|link}}. After the young [[Hero of Time]] obtains the {{Term|MM3D|Hero's Bow|link}}, there is a chance the {{Term|MM3D|Takkuri}} can steal other important items such as the Bow itself, an empty {{Term|MM3D|Bottle|link}} or even his [[Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing?!? Didn't your bottle just get stolen?!?|Tatl|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What are you doing?!? Didn't your sword just get stolen?!?|Tatl|MM}}</ref> The bird will then quickly fly away to {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}} with the item. If Link attempts to shoot the bird down with his Bow in this critical situation, the Takkuri will easily dodge, making it impossible to stop. The {{Term|MM3D|Takkuri}} works with the {{Term|MM3D|Man from Curiosity Shop|link}}, and as such, stolen items such as a {{Term|MM3D|Bottle}} and the Sword can later be bought back at the {{Term|MM3D|Curiosity Shop|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cool Bottle: 20 Rupees Isn't it nice? Put anything in it. Brand new, too.|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Nice Sword: 100 Rupees Just got this sword in. It's a deal. I kid you not.|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Good Sword: 50 Rupees You don't have a sword, buddy. At this price, this one's a steal. Heh-heh...|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You bought back the Great Fairy's Sword that was stolen from you! From now on, be more aware of birds when you're strolling down the road...|N/A|MM}}</ref> Because of this unusual hazard, the player must be careful when avoiding or defeating this enemy.

The {{Term|MM3D|Takkuri}} has 24 points of health, taking about 12 regular arrows or six [[Magic]] arrows of any kind to defeat it. Link can also destroy the enemy with four [[Spin Attack|Spin]] or [[Jump Slash|Jump Attack]] strikes if he has obtained the [[Gilded Sword]] or three with the [[Great Fairy's Sword]] (though using the latter move puts the hero at considerable risk of getting hit). If he is successful in killing the bird, he will receive a [[Rupee|Huge Rupee]], which worth 200 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee}}. Defeating the Takkuri is made easier on {{Term|MM3D|Epona|link}}, where it cannot hurt Link. Wearing the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Mask|link}} makes himself undetectable by the {{Term|MM3D|Takkuri}}, and prevents it from stealing his items. If he plays the "{{Term|MM3D|Song of Time|link}}" to reset back to the dawn of the {{Term|MM3D|First Day|link}}, any items lost or stolen by the {{Term|MM3D|Takkuri}} will be recovered.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Crow & Takkuri}}
{{Plural|TMC|Takkuri}} in {{TMC}} are nearly identical to {{Plural|TMC|Crow|link}}, but are red rather than black. They try to steal Link's {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} and can be found in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}. One {{Term|TMC|Takkuri}} will swoop down and steal the {{Term|TMC|Graveyard Key|link}} from Link after he obtains it from {{Term|TMC|Dampé|link}}. In order to retrieve the {{Term|TMC|Graveyard Key|display=Key}} and progress to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}, the young hero must use the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}} and dash into the {{Term|TMC|Tree|link}} where the {{Term|TMC|Takkuri}} is roosting.
