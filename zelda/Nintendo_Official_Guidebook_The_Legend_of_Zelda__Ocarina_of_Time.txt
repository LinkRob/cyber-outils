''{{Term/Store|Nintendo Official Guidebook—The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time||Series, OoT, OoT3D}}'' is [[Nintendo]]'s guidebook for the Japanese version of {{OoT}}. It was published under Shogakukan's ''Wonder Life Special'' label.

==Features==
Contains: a complete walkthrough; aerial maps taken in-game; drawn maps; a list of characters, monsters, items, and melodies; checklists for {{Plural|Series|Piece of Heart|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Gold Skulltula|link}}; basic gameplay help; and tips and secrets.
