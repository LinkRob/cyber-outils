{{Term/Store|Great Fairy's Tears|Great Fairy's Tears|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWDE}} They are presumably full of tears that the {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy|link}} has cried while praying.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Great Fairy's Tears! This sacred water contains the Great Fairy's prayers.|N/A|TP}}</ref>

==Uses and Location==
==={{TP|-}}===
{{Plural|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears}} are kept in an {{Term|TPHD|Empty Bottle|link}}. When used, they completely refill {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Life Gauge|link}} and temporarily double {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s strength. Their function is similar to the {{Term|TWWHD|Elixir Soup|link}} from {{TWW}}, but unlike the soup, a bottle of these tears only holds one serving and wears off after a short time (16 seconds) or if {{Term|TPHD|Link}} takes damage before then. Additionally, drinking the Great Fairy's Tears will turn Link's tunic a golden color.

One bottle of {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears}} is given to {{Term|TPHD|Link}} by {{Term|TPHD|Jovani|link}} after capturing 20 {{Plural|TPHD|Poe Soul|link}}. After this, {{Term|TPHD|Jovani}} will reveal that more can be found in the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Desert|link}}. This refers to the {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Ordeals|link}}, where {{Plural|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears}} are the reward from the {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy|link}} after completing the cave. Clearing the cave also allows {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to receive a new bottle of {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears}} from the {{Plural|TPHD|Spirit of Light|link}} in their springs around {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}}.

{{Term|TPHD|Link}} can only acquire {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears}} from spirit springs when he has none in his inventory. Completing the {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Ordeals}} again will fill another empty {{Term|TPHD|Bottle}} with {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears}} even if {{Term|TPHD|Link}} already has a bottle of it.

==Other Appearances==

==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWDE Great Fairy's Tears Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, {{Term|HWDE|Great Fairy's Tears}} appears as a drink-type {{Term|HWDE|Food|link}}. It is imbued with the light {{Term|HWDE|Element|link}}. When consumed by a {{Term|HWDE|Companion Fairy|link}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Dining Room|link}} of the {{Term|HWDE|My Fairy|link}} mode, {{Term|HWDE|Great Fairy's Tears}} can increase the Fairy's trust level and personality traits.
