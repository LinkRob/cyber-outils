{{Term/Store|Fairy Dust|Fairy Dusts|Series, TFH, TWoG}},{{Exp Game|TFH, TWoG}}<ref name="E 122"/>{{Fact}} also known as {{Term/Store|Fairy Powder|Fairy Powder|Series, OoA}},{{Exp Game|OoA}}<ref name="E 122"/> is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, the [[Fairy Queen]], who has been turned into an [[Octorok]] by a curse set by [[Veran]],<ref>{{Cite|You may not believe this, but I am the Queen Fairy, guardian of this sea! The curse of an evil one named Veran has made me appear so!|Fairy Queen|OoA}}</ref> can be cured with the Powder.<ref>{{Cite|The magic of Fairy Powder can turn me back to normal.|Fairy Queen|OoA}}</ref> The Fairy Powder is kept in the nearby [[Eyeglass Island Library]].<ref>{{Cite|This is the Eyeglass Isle Library. Fairy Powder is said to be stored in back, but I've yet to see it.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> Using the [[Harp of Ages]] to travel between the past and present ages, [[Link]] is able to obtain the [[Book of Seals]] and use it to procure the Powder from the library,<ref>{{Cite|Fairy Powder is  locked away in back, but without another Book of Seals, no one can reach the Powder.|Old Man|OoA}}</ref> and subsequently use it to cure the fairy.  The Fairy Queen then cleanses the seas with her magic, curing [[Jabu-Jabu]] of his illness.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, our seawater is clean again, and Jabu-Jabu's health has returned. All is well.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> Link is dubbed "Zora Hero" by the [[King Zora]], and is permitted to enter [[Jabu-Jabu's Belly]],<ref>{{Cite|I dub you Zora Hero and permit you to enter Jabu-Jabu.|King Zora|OoA}}</ref> the seventh dungeon of the game.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Fairy Dust}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Abyss of Agony|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Fairy Dust}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Cove of Transition|link}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Fairy Dust}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Water Temple|link}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Fairy Dust}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Water Temple}}''', ''"Transport the orb quickly!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Fairy Dust}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Fairy Dust}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Jack of Hearts|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Queen of Hearts|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 122"/>
