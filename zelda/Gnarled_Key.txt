The {{Term/Store|Gnarled Key||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Gnarled Key is given to [[Link]] by the [[Maku Tree]] after the hero initially wakes him up with the sword he found in [[Hero's Cave]]. This Key opens up the first dungeon of {{OoS|-}}: [[Gnarled Root Dungeon]].
