{{Term/Store|Cove of Transition||Series, TFH}} is a {{Term|TFH|Level|link}} in {{TFH}}. It is the third {{Term|TFH|Level}} of the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}} area.

==Themes and Navigation==
In '''Stage 1''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} enter a wide cliff side next to rushing {{Plural|TFH|Waterfall}}, and are initially separated from each other. {{Plural|TFH|Gripshot|link}} are provided nearby, which can be used on the wooden posts to pull the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} forward and reunite them. A broken bridge can be crossed by gripping onto the three posts across. Another ledge prevents them from going forward, however the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can form a {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}} and throw the top {{Term|TFH|Links}} onto a wooden platform with {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} nearby that drops down to the other side. The other {{Plural|TFH|Links}} then simply have to grip onto him with their {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} to cross over the gap. Once reunited, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can continue east until they reach another gap, which can be crossed by throwing two {{Plural|TFH|Links}} over and having the third {{Term|TFH|Links}} grip onto them with the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}}. On the other side, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must form another Totem so that the top {{Term|TFH|Links}} can grip onto a post on a wooden platform on the other side. Once he is on the other side, the other {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must then use the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} on that {{Term|TFH|Links}} again, and finally reach the {{Term|TFH|Stage}}'s {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}}.

In '''Stage 2''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} reappear on a stone platform in the middle of a lake. Immediately the dams of a stone wall open as water pours in, and {{Plural|TFH|Tektite|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Water Tektite|link}} emerge. The rushing water will alternate between flooding the lake and draining it, changing the water's depth. This causes the stone platform to go under as the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are forced to swim, but also makes the {{Term|TFH|Stage}}'s {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} on an overhead platform reachable. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must defeat all of the enemies in the area, by swimming onto land attacking the {{Plural|TFH|Tektite}} as they approach. The {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} will then open after all {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}} have been defeated.

'''Stage 3''' places the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} in another lake, who immediately notice a {{Term|TFH|Switch|link}} on top of a tall dam. A {{Term|TFH|Raft|link}} nearby can be moved by having the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} stand on it, and move it forward by using the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} on a post. By moving the {{Term|TFH|Raft}}, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} reach a square platform where one must {{Term|TFH|Links}} must stand, so as to use the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} on him to continue moving the {{Term|TFH|Raft}}. After moving the {{Term|TFH|Raft}} to the dam's wall, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can then form a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} to have the top {{Term|TFH|Links}} reach its switch. Pressing it opens the wall's dam as the entire area floods. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can then swim over to a platform where the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} is.

In '''Stage 4''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} enter the courtyard of a temple with shallow water. In the center is a {{Term|TFH|Gyorm|link}} that must be defeated, by using the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot}} to pull it out of its shell whenever it emerges and having another {{Term|TFH|Links}} slash at it. Three more will emerge after each one is defeated, after which the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} will open. {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} can be found on top of platforms, reachable by forming a {{Term|TFH|Totem}}.
