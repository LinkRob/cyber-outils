The {{Term/Store|''Cadence of Hyrule Melody Pack''||Series, CoH}} is the second downloadable content pack for {{CoH}}.<ref name="Game Details"/> It is available as a standalone DLC pack or as part of the {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Season Pass|link}}.

==Overview==
The {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Melody Pack}} introduces 39 {{Plural|CoH|Song|link}} which can be manually played as the background music through the game's settings.<ref name="Game Details"/> The {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Melody Pack|display=''Melody Pack''}} features remixes of previous music composed and arranged by [[A_Rival]], [[Chipzel]], and [[FamilyJules]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo |published= July 20, 2020 |retrieved= July 20, 2020 |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kNtacp8-GoI |title= Nintendo Direct Mini: Partner Showcase {{!}} July 2020 |site= YouTube |type= video}}</ref>
