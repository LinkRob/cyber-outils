The {{Term/Store|Source of the Flow||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Source of the Flow|Kinstone Fusion|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Source of the Flow}} is a a large stone slab located at the end of a bridge at {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}. After going through the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is instructed to find it by the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Blue|Seek the}} {{Color|TMC Green|source of the flow}} {{Color|TMC Blue|in Hyrule... Only then will your path open...}}|Gustaf|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} also gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} a golden {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} that he can use to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Source of the Flow}}. Doing so opens the stone slab and reveals a passageway that takes {{Term|TMC|Link}} upwards through {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls}} and to its summit, where {{Term|TMC|Veil Springs|link}} is located. Here, a large {{Term|TMC|Tornado|link}} will transport {{Term|TMC|Link}} to the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}.
