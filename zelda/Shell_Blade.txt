Shell Blades are aquatic, clam-like enemies that inhabit submerged areas.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TPHD|publisher= Prima|page= 219}}</ref> They will open their mouths when [[Link]] approaches and expose their vulnerable muscle ligaments, which he can attack with the [[Hookshot]] in {{OoT}}, as [[Zora Link]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', and with the [[Clawshot]] or [[Sword]] <ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TPHD|publisher= Prima|page= 19}}</ref> in {{TP}}. If Link takes too long, they will close their mouths before turning around and lunging backwards at him in an attempt to harm the hero with the sharp spines on their backs. When they do this, Link must block them with his [[Shield]] or move out of the way. Shell Blades in {{TP|-}} have no spines and will simply try to chomp on Link if he gets too close. In {{MM|-}}, Shell Blades guard four of the seven stolen [[Zora Egg]]s in the [[Pirates' Fortress]] and also appear in the [[Great Bay Temple]]. In {{MM3D}}, they are no longer found in the Pirates' Fortress.

It should be noted that when encountered out of water in {{OoT|-}}, although they move faster when attacking Link, these enemies can be defeated with a larger spectrum of weapons. The [[Megaton Hammer]] is useful as it may shatter a Shell Blade in a single blow, as will [[Bomb]]s and the [[Spin Attack]] if timed correctly.

==Trivia==

* In ''Ocarina of Time'', like the [[Spike]], when a Shell Blade is defeated underwater, bubbles of red blood can be seen in the original v1.0/v1.1 NTSC release of the [[Nintendo 64]] game. In the v1.2, PAL, and subsequent ports it was censored to green due to [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda series|controversy]]. However, in ''Majora's Mask'', the initial red color is retained. They are also known to bleed in ''Twilight Princess'' as well.
