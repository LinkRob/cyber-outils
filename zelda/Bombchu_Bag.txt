{{Term/Store|Bombchu Bag|Bombchu Bags|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 118 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The first {{Term|PH|Bombchu Bag}} is obtained when {{Term|PH|Link|link}} finds the {{Plural|PH|Bombchu|link}} inside the {{Term|PH|Goron Temple|link}}. It allows him to carry up to 10 {{Plural|PH|Bombchu}}. This {{Term|PH|Bombchu Bag}} does not appear in {{Term|PH|Link}}'s inventory and is instead represented by an icon of a {{Term|PH|Bombchu}}; nonetheless it is required for purchasing or collecting {{Plural|PH|Bombchu}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can't sell you Bombchus if you don't have a Bombchu bag. Sorry!|Mi|PH}}</ref> An upgraded {{Term|PH|Bombchu Bag}} can be bought at the {{Term|PH|Goron Island Shop|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|PH|Rupee}}. Another upgraded {{Term|PH|Bombchu Bag}} can be won by completing the {{Term|PH|Goron Game|link}} at {{Term|PH|Dee Ess Island|link}} in 35 seconds or less. Each upgraded {{Term|PH|Bombchu Bag|display=Bag}} increased the capacity by 10 {{Plural|PH|Bombchu}} and can be obtained in any order.
