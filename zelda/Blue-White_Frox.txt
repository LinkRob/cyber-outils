The '''Blue-White Frox''' is a large frog-like monster and a [[Frox]] encountered in [[The Depths]] in {{TotK}}.

Blue-White Froxes have 2100 HP, and are even stronger than the [[Obsidian Frox]]. There body is silver-like, or blue-white, that has a red eye, instead of the green eye on an Obsidian Frox, and a different fang. Blue-White Froxes are pretty common in the depths if you've progressed A lot into the game, they are mostly found in the depths of the [[Tabantha Frontier]], the [[Hebra Mountains]], and the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. They have a lot of attacks, they can swallow you whole, slam their hand on the ground, belly flop on you three times, running towards you with their mouths open, and a lot more.

With that said, you can still use their inhaling against them.

The Blue-White Frox has various spoils, one of which is the [[Blue-White Frox Fang]].
