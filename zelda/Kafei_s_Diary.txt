==Location and Uses==
{{Term|MM3D|Kafei's Diary}} is found in the bedroom just off {{Term|MM3D|Madame Aroma|link}}'s office in the {{Term|MM3D|Mayor's Official Residence|link}}.

In the {{Term|MM3D|Kafei's Diary|display=''Diary''}}'s single entry, {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}} writes about his upcoming wedding to {{Term|MM3D|Anju|link}}. He had just completed his {{Term|MM3D|Sun Mask|link|display=Wedding Ceremony Mask}}, and planned to show it off and talk about his future bride with some friends at the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar|link}} that evening.<ref name="Wedding"/> As this is this the last entry, this trip to the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar}} is undoubtedly when {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}}'s fateful meeting with the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}} who turned him into a child took place.<ref>{{Cite|I was turned into this by a strange imp wearing a mask... But I'm not hiding because I look this way.|Kafei|MM3D}}</ref> This event leads {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}} to search out the help of the {{Term|MM3D|Great Fairy|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|North Clock Town|link}}, where his {{Term|MM3D|Sun Mask}} was stolen by {{Term|MM3D|Sakon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When I was turned into this, I went to see the Great Fairy at her shrine near the {{Color|MM3D Red|North Gate}}... But on my way there, a man hanging around the gate--a prancing man in a white shirt--he stole my precious {{Color|MM3D Red|wedding ceremony mask}}!|Kafei|MM3D}}</ref>
