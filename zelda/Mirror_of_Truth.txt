The {{Term/Store|Mirror of Truth||Series, TSP}} is an {{Term|TSP|Item}} in {{TSP}}.<ref name="True"/> It is used to reveal people's true identities and plays an important role in the plot.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth}} is first introduced after {{Term|TSP|Link|link}} informs {{Term|TSP|Impa|link}} that the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule|link}} has trusted {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|link}} with the {{Term|TSP|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} and has dubbed him a Knight of Triforce.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= With a great effort, Link manages to hold his tongue. He hurries off to find Impa and tell her the terrible news. 'The king is trusting that weasel Charles with the safety of the Triforce!' |book= TSP | publisher= Mammoth |page= 88}}</ref> With no other option, {{Term|TSP|Impa}} tells {{Term|TSP|Link}} that he must hold {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}} up to the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth}} to see his true self.<ref name="True"/> Although she does not know where it is located, she says to the young hero that the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth|display=Mirror}} was last seen in the {{Term|TSP|Water Town of Saria|link}} and urges him to start looking there.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'Well, thing do get lost.' Impa sounds a little touchy. 'I hear it was last seen in Saria Town. You might start looking there.' |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 88}}</ref> 

Once {{Term|TSP|Link}} arrives to {{Term|TSP|Saria Town}} and opens its gates using the {{Term|TSP|Key|link}} that was given to him by the {{Term|TSP|Old Man|link}} near {{Term|TSP|Ruto Town|link}}, he will bump into an {{Term|TSP|Old Woman|link}} with eyes clear as glass.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= She looks up. Link has never seen a face so wrinkled. But her eyes are clear as glass. Link can't quite believe them. |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 57}}</ref> The woman tells {{Term|TSP|Link}} that if he wants to have eyes as clear as her, he must get to a nearby {{Term|TSP|Tree}} and eat its fruit.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'I'm going to give you some advice, clever lad.' She points to a gnarled tree in the middle of the square. 'See that apple tree? If you can get to it, its fruit will make your eyes as clear as mine.' |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 57, 58}}</ref> After reaching the {{Term|TSP|Tree}}, he notices a small {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth|display=Mirror}} with a thin wooden frame in one of the branches, realizing that the old woman was actually referring to the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= As Link is reaching for his own snack, he sees something shining in a forked branch. 'Hmmm...' He reaches up and pulls down a small mirror with a thin wooden frame. Link grins. 'So that's what the old lady meant about clear eyes,' he says. |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 43}}</ref>

After the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule}} announces that {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}}, and not {{Term|TSP|Link}}, will be the one to take the {{Term|TSP|Triforce of Wisdom}} to the {{Term|TSP|Fifth Castle of Ancient Hyrule|link}}, {{Term|TSP|Link}} furiously reminds the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule|display=King}} of all the brave deeds he has done and claims that {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}} cannot be trusted because he is evil. The {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule|display=King}} raises his hand and silences {{Term|TSP|Link}}, saying he better have proof to back up the serious charge he has raised against {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'And anyway, Charles can't be trusted. He is evil!' The room is in an uproar. The king raises his hand. 'Silence! Link, you've made some very serious charges against Sir Charles. You'd better have proof to back up your words, young man!' |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 103}}</ref> {{Term|TSP|Link}} immediately takes out the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth}} to prove his point, but the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule|display=King}} warns him that if the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth|display=Mirror}} does not show something important, {{Term|TSP|Link}} will be punished.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'If it's the Mirror of Truth, use it,' the king says. 'But let me warn you, Link. If this mirror doesn't show something important, I'm going to be very angry with you!' |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 14}}</ref> If the young hero promptly uses the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth|display=Mirror}}, it will reveal that {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}} is actually {{Term|TSP|Ganon|link}} in disguise and will allow the reader to continue the story.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Zelda sees the reflection first. Instead of Charles's face, a horrible, hooded, skull-like mask with burning white eyes fills the mirror. 'It's Ganon himself!' Zelda cries. |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 64}}</ref> However, if {{Term|TSP|Link}} hesitates to use it, {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}} will knock the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth|display=Mirror}} off his hands and shatter it, leading to {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}} stealing the {{Term|TSP|Triforce of Wisdom}} as everyone passes out from the blue smoke that filled the room.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The, before he knows what's happening, Charles is at his side. The Morian knight knocks the mirror out of his hands. Kuh-rash! The mirror shatters against the floor. [...] Thick blue smoke fills the room. The king, Link, Zelda and all the counselors begin to cough and fall to the ground. |book= TSP |publisher= Mammoth |page= 9}}</ref>
