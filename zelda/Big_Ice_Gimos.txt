{{Term/Store|Big Ice Gimos|Big Ice Gimos|Series, ALBW|plural}} are enemies in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 166}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ALBW|Big Ice Gimos}} are a giant variety of {{Term|ALBW|Gimos}}. One appears near the western summit of {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Death Mountain|link}}, while another appears in the cave to the east. They attack by leaping high above {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} to slam down upon him, often sending him off the edge of a platform. {{Plural|ALBW|Big Ice Gimos}} can withstand many hits but are easily destroyed with the {{Term|ALBW|Fire Rod|link}}.
