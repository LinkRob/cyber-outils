{{Term/Store|Meat Stew|Meat Stews|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Meat Stew}}<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Meat Stews are meals that are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either [[Raw Meat]] or a [[Raw Bird Drumstick]] with [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Goat Butter]], and [[Fresh Milk]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they'll heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These stews are mainstay dishes, containing hearty meats that leaves people satisfied throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding a fifth ingredient to Meat Stew, Link can make one of the following variations:

* '''Hearty Meat Stew''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Meat Stew}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hearty ingredient. Eating them will heal all of Link's Hearts and increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Meat Stew''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Meat Stew}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an energizing ingredient. If one of these stews are eaten, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be partially refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Meat Stew''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Meat Stew}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an enduring ingredient. Consuming them will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Meat Stew''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hasty ingredient. When eaten, they'll boost Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Meat Stew''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a spicy ingredient. Eating these stews will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Meat Stew''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. Link gains one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when he eats them.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Meat Stew''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. If they're eaten, they'll increase the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Meat Stew''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. Eating these stews will increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Meat Stew''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain a temporary boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Meat Stew''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Meat Stew}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The hearty meat in this mainstay dish leaves bellies satisfied all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. Link can eat these stews to gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
