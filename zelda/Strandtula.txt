'''Strandtula''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Strandtula|A spidery monster that spins thread to dangle from ceilings. The thread is strong but quite flammable.<ref name="Strandtula">{{Cite|A spidery monster that spins thread to dangle from ceilings. The thread is strong but quite flammable.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Strandtulas are [[spider]]-like creatures that hang from ceilings by long strands, which [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can climb to reach otherwise inaccessible areas. After defeating one, Zelda can learn the Strandtula [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of two.
