{{Plural|BotW|Hunter's Shield}} are wooden {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} similar to the {{Term|BotW|Emblazoned Shield|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Fisherman's Shield|link}}. They are favored by hunters for their rabbit designs, which are said to bring luck.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Hunter's Shield}}, like {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Shield|link}}, have low defensive power, but they are known to be easy to use.<ref name="Inventory"/>

There are three places to find {{Plural|BotW|Hunter's Shield}}. One is in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} near a lake below {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Tower|link}}. There is another in the east side of the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth|link}}, and it will respawn after some time. Finally, {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} gives one out as a reward for completing the {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in between 3:15 and 3:30.<ref>{{Cite|A shiny new record deserves a shiny new shield. Here y'go!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> Female {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} travelers are known to wield them as well, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to take a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of them for the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Hunter's Shield}} are made of wood, and they will therefore burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} like metal {{Plural|BotW|Shield}} do. Finally, they cannot be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
