'''Moragia''' {{Exp Game|TotK}} is a [[Boss]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']], that appears from [[Death Mountain]] and blocks your way to the [[Fire Temple]].

== Characteristics ==
{{Stub}}

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
{{Compendium|name=Moragia|desc=An enormous monster dwelling in Death Mountain.
Its body is composed of boiling lava and hard rocks that are infused with gloom. To keep its body held together, the creature draws heat and gloom power from the crater.|locs=Death Mountain|loot=None}}
When Link and [[Yunobo]] search Death Mountain for [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] (actually [[Phantom Ganon]] in disguise), they eventually catch up with her on the summit. Before they can approach her, however, she enters the [[Death Mountain Crater|crater]] and Moragia rises up from within to block the duo's progress. Moragia is formed of orange and black lava rock, with three dragonlike heads made of marbled rock. It attacks by shooting sets of fireballs from each head. There is a Zonai vehicle nearby, a wing with four fans, a steering stick, and a battery attached to the top, and a cart attached to the bottom. Link must pilot it to destroy Moragia. When Link takes off in the vehicle, Yunobo will jump on the front and can be fired off using his charge ability. Aiming the vehicle at one of Moragia's heads and launching Yunobo at it will destroy the head and reduce Moragia's health by one third. When all three heads are destroyed, Moragia's body will crumble and you can dive into the chasm in Death Mountain's crater and reach Lost Gorondia, the Fire Temple.
