==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Flint}} can be found after breaking {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone Deposit|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Rare Ore Deposit|link}}. Striking pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint}} with metal {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} cause the former to ignite.<ref name="Inventory"/> This can be used to create {{Plural|BotW|Campfire|link}} by dropping a piece near some {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}} before striking it.<ref name="Inventory"/> It can be used to relight {{Plural|BotW|Campfire}} as well, including those under {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} also accepts {{Term|BotW|Flint}} as an ingredient for {{Term|BotW|Black Dye|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Champions|link|display=Champions'}} {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}}, if broken, can be reforged by special craftsmen, with {{Term|BotW|Flint}} as a requirement.

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
Flint can be obtained in the same way as in [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|{{BotW|-}}]]. However, it is now revealed (via the [[Side Quest|side quest]] [[Cash In On Ripened Flint]]) that there is a delicacy known as ripened flint that is indistinguishable from normal flint, until [[Gomo]] the [[Goron]] gourmand eats it.
