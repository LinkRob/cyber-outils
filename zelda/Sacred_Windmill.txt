{{Term/Store|Sacred Windmill|Sacred Windmills|Series, PH|plural}} are objects in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 119}}</ref>

==Locations and Uses==
Of the eight windmills in total scattered in the northern section of the {{Term|PH|Isle of Gust|link}}, three are designated as {{Plural|PH|Sacred Windmill}} by a map found in the northwestern corner of the island. In order for {{Term|PH|Link|link}} to gain access to the {{Term|PH|Temple of Wind|link}}, the young hero must blow on the three {{Plural|PH|Sacred Windmill|display=Windmills}} to start them spinning.<ref>{{Cite|The hero must blow on the three sacred windmills to open the path forward.|Stone Tablet|PH}}</ref> However, he must do so while looking out for the {{Plural|PH|Sandworm|link}} that are situated in the area, who will swallow up {{Term|PH|Link}} if he makes any noise while walking.

Once {{Plural|PH|Link}} finally activates all three {{Plural|PH|Sacred Windmill}}, the doors to the temple will open, and the gust statues impeding the path to the entrance will stop blowing.

Although the other windmills on the island are not referred to as {{Plural|PH|Sacred Windmill}}, they share the same appearance. Several more windmills are placed throughout the {{Term|PH|Temple of Wind}}. {{Term|PH|Link}} must make them spin by blowing in their direction to open doors within the Temple.
