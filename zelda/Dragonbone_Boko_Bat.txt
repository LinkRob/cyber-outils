{{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Bat}} are {{Plural|BotW|Boko Bat|link}} that have been reinforced with fossilized bone.<ref name="Inventory"/> As a result, they are stronger and more durable than before.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created and used by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are often wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Black Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Bokoblin|link}}, as well as {{Plural|BotW|Black Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Moblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Moblin|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Bat}} are common on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} and along the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}}. The {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Bat}} when it is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Boko Bat}} will burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float when dropped on the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They are unable to be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.
