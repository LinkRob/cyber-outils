The {{Term/Store|Ring Box||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 137 {{Exp Game|OoS, OoA}}}}</ref>

==Uses and Locations==
[[File:OoS Ring Box L-1 Sprite.png|left]]
[[File:OoS Ring Box L-2 Sprite.png|left]]
[[File:OoS Ring Box L-3 Sprite.png|left]]
A Ring Box allows [[Link]] to carry [[Magical Ring]]s with him on his journey through [[Holodrum]] and [[Labrynna]]. The L-1 Ring Box, which holds a single Ring, is obtained for free from [[Vasu]], along with the [[Friendship Ring]]. In {{OoA|-}}, the L-2 Ring Box, which holds up to three Rings, can be bought for 300 [[Rupee]]s in the second floor of the [[Lynna City Shop]]. In {{OoS|-}}, it is received from a [[Goron]] inside a cave in [[Goron Mountain]]. The L-3 Ring Box, which holds up to five Rings, can only be obtained through a [[Linked Game]]. In {{OoA|-}}, it is obtained from the [[Linked Game/Secrets#Ruul Secret|Ruul Secret]], while in {{OoS|-}}, from the [[Linked Game/Secrets#Symmetry Secret|Symmetry Secret]].
