{{Term/Store|Ploka||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ploka|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Ploka}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} soldier who guards the front entrance to the palace in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} at all times, alongside {{Term|BotW|Reeza|link}}. Before {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} in the palace, the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} reporter, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}}, is seen speaking to {{Term|BotW|Ploka}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Ploka}}, she mentions that between him and {{Term|BotW|Traysi}}, many {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} have come by the palace.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'aaq. There sure are a lot of Hylians coming by today.|Ploka|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|First that vai, Traysi, and now you... There sure are a lot of Hylians coming around today.|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ploka|link}} then explains that she guards the palace and that it is the home of {{Term|BotW|Riju}}, Chief of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the home of the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} of the Gerudo. It is my duty to make sure that no one suspicious enters.|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> She also warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} not to distract her from her duty.<ref>{{Cite|Do not distract me from my job!|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that he is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}, {{Term|BotW|Ploka}} finds the notion ridiculous and yells that he is too short, his hair is not red and he does not have abs.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|You're short! Your hair is the wrong color!! And I can't even see your abs!!!}}|Ploka|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It is absolutely ridiculous for you to claim that you're Gerudo. All evidence says otherwise.|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks for entry to the palace, {{Term|BotW|Ploka}} yells that they are busy with an emergency,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|We're busy! It's an emergency!! Authorized personnel only!!!}}|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> but quickly explains that {{Term|BotW|Riju}} is in fact allowing visitors.<ref>{{Cite|...That's what I would say if I had my way, but the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} is a kind person. She's told me to allow visitors.|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ploka}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if he says anything rude to {{Term|BotW|Riju}},<ref>{{Cite|If you want to go inside, be my guest. But if you say even one rude thing to the chief...|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> she will attack him with her {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|display=Spear|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You and my spear will have a very personal conversation!|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Ploka}} after speaking to {{Term|BotW|Riju}} for the first time, she comments that the stolen {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}} is a failure shared by every {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} soldier.<ref>{{Cite|Allowing the {{Color|BotW Blue|Thunder Helm}} to be stolen was a failure shared by everyone tasked with guarding this town.|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> She feels that only {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} should be allowed into the palace at the moment.<ref>{{Cite|If I'm being honest, I'd prefer to ban all but Gerudo from the palace for the moment...|Ploka|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} calms Naboris, Ploka comments that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} is back to normal now.<ref>{{Cite|Now that {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} has calmed, it seems like the desert is different... I suppose it is, actually. It's back to normal now.|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> She also says that she thought {{Plural|BotW|Hylian}} were weak people, but admits that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not so bad.<ref>{{Cite|I thought that Hylians were a small, weak little people, but you're not so bad...|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> Also, as opposed to before, {{Term|BotW|Ploka}} will enthusiastically welcome {{Term|BotW|Link}} into the palace, as she considers {{Term|BotW|Link}} one of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite|<big>Of course! Anytime!! You're welcome!!!</big> Naturally, you are already one of us! You are welcome here anytime!|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} bids farewell by saying "Sav'ork", {{Term|BotW|Ploka}} corrects him and tells him to say "sav'orq" instead, as he is a friend to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's not "sav'ork"! You're a friend to the Gerudo now, and it's "sav'orq" among friends!|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> She also mentions that he must work on his pronunciation.<ref>{{Cite|You just need to work on you pronunciation a little bit!|Ploka|BotW}}</ref>
