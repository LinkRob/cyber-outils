'''Sonia Is Caught by Treachery''' is a memory in {{TotK}}. It is unlocked from a [[Geoglyph]] depicting a [[Gerudo Scimitar]].

== Overview ==
One night, after [[Ganondorf]], the Gerudo Chief, made his suspicious show of loyalty to the royal family of [[Hyrule]] and was granted access to the kingdom, [[Zelda]] asked to speak to [[Queen Sonia|Sonia]] alone. Sonia accepted and they went to a balcony, away from the castle. Upon being asked what she wanted to talk about with her back turned to Zelda, the Princess produces a Gerudo Scimitar from behind her, sneering that Sonia is far too trusting, throwing it at her and intending to kill. 

Unexpectedly, [[Recall]] is used on the scimitar, which confuses Zelda. Sonia slowly turns around, feigning surprise at hearing Zelda say such a thing, calling out this [[Phantom Ganon|Zelda]] as a puppet of Ganondorf.

The real Zelda then emerges from behind a pillar, still effecting the scimitar with Recall, and asks the Fake Zelda if she thought that they hadn't realized her deceit. Using Recall, Zelda sends the dagger back to the fake Zelda but stops it short, causing the fake Zelda to vanish in a cloud of [[Gloom]], laughing ominously. Suddenly, after some seconds of silence, Sonia is punched hard in the back by Ganondorf, who had been hiding, snapping her spine and ending the queen's life. It is then that he snatches her [[Secret Stone]] as it flies into the air. Zelda, horrified by the sight, tries pointlessly to wake Queen Sonia up as Ganondorf cackles psychotically at her suffering and his acquisition of Sonia's Secret Stone.
