'''Quino''' is a character in {{HWAoI}}. He is an ancient Hylian noble whose innate sense of justice led him to becoming a elite soldier in service to the kingdom of Hyrule.<ref name=":0">''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment'' -- "Controlling Quino": An elite Hylian soldier who swings his giant two-handed sword with ease,and will do anything to protect his beloved Hyrule.Some of his strong attacks will unleash cutting waves that can strike distant enemies.</ref><ref name=":1">''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment'' -- "Characters: Quino"; A young man whose innate sense of justice drove him to serve his kingdom as a soldier. Raised a well-bred noble, he is optimistic and diligent, although his youthful inexperience occassionally shows through.</ref>
==Biography==
Born as a nobleman in Hyrule,<ref name=":1" /> Quino joined the ranks of the Hylian soldier as an elite due to his skills despite his inexperience.<ref name=":0" /> He is present beside Typhan when King Rauru and Queen Sonia arrive with a mysterious woman on their side and when asked who she was, Sonia would state she is a friend and all will be explained later. While moving back to Hyrule Castle, they're attacked by shades and Quino is saved by Zelda's light power.<ref>''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment'' -- Chapter 1: to the Great Plateau</ref> The ordeal privately gave him a fear of shades.<ref>''Hryrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment'' -- Quest: Overcoming His Fears</ref>

Some time after Zelda takes residence in ancient Hyrule Castle and trains under Rauru and Sonia's tutelage, Quino is among the Hylian attendants for the ceremony honoring the Goron's acceptance in King Rauru's alliance. Shortly after, he helps Rauru by acting as an opponent for Rauru's mock training but is among those defeat by Zelda.<ref>''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment'' -- Chapter 1: Art of the Blade</ref> 
{{Stub}}
==Physical Appearance==
Qunio appears as a purple-haired, young Hylian male with a eye-like symbol (resembling the Zonaite Helm's symbol). He wears a variant of the [[Zonaite Set]], having a navy-blue dress waistguard, shingards, and guards on his elbows.

==Personality==
Quino is described as typically being an optimist and digilent individual with a strong sense of justice.<ref name=":1" />

==Abilities==
Quino is considered an elite soldier and swordsman despite his young age and inexperience.<ref name=":0" /><ref name=":1" /> However, after the completion of numerous character Quests in which details instances of training throughout the [[Imprisoning War (Tears of the Kingdom)|Imprisoning War]], the "Wandering Warrior" challenge focusing on Quino has Typhan state that none matches his skill with a sword, making him among the most skilled Hylian swordsman and soldier during the timeframe.<ref>''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment'' -- Wandering Warriors: "Typhan: I was thinking just the same thing about you, Quino. None could ever match your skill with a sword -- or your love for Hyrule!"</ref>

==Trivia==

Back where I'm from, the knight who served as my personal guard was around the same age as the two of us are now." --'' Zelda ''(''Helpin' Quino Aside Quest'')''</ref>
