The {{Term/Store|Instrument of the Sirens|Instruments of the Sirens|Series, LA, LADX|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Plural|Series|Instrument of the Sirens}} are referred to as the '''Sirens' Musical Instruments''' in the manual for {{LA|DX|-}},<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Rumor has it that the Sirens' Musical Instruments are hidden in dungeons.|game=LADX|page=15}}</ref> and the '''Nightmares' Instruments''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 14}}</ref> However, as these terms contradict the name given in {{E}}, they are not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
The whole set contains eight Instruments. Each of the eight {{Plural|LANS|Instrument of the Sirens}} can be found at the end of a {{Term|LANS|Dungeon|link}} after defeating its respective {{Term|LANS|Boss|link}}. When combined, they allow {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} to awaken the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, according to the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Owl|link}}, once the power of the eight {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Instrument of the Sirens|link}} is combined, the mystery of the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Wind Fish|link}} will be solved. Therefore, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} must find the scattered {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Instrument of the Sirens|display= Instruments}} throughout {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Koholint Island|link}} in order to leave. Once collected, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} plays the "{{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ballad of the Wind Fish|link}}" on the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ocarina|link}} in order to activate their power, causing the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Wind Fish's Egg|link}} to crack open.

==={{HW|-}}===
[[File:HWS Sea Lily Bell Icon.png|right|80px]]
In {{HW}}, the Sea Lily Bell appears as the Lv. 1 tier of [[Marin]]'s [[Bell#Hyrule Warriors|Bell]] Weapon.

In {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}}, the eight Instruments of the Sirens also appear as [[Item Card]]s in the [[Koholint Island Map]] of [[Adventure Mode]], which is included in the {{LA|-}} Pack DLC in {{HW|L|-}}. Each Instrument can dispel [[Nightmare]]s, increase the Egg Counter by one and activate a [[Siren Power]] that assists [[Warrior]]s. The Full Moon Cello gives Warriors a maxed out [[Special Attack]] Gauge. The Conch Horn reduces the damage taken by Warriors. The Sea Lily Bell grants Warriors a maxed [[Magic Gauge]]. The Surf Harp increases the amount of [[Experience]] points earned. The Wind Marimba increases the amount of [[Rupee]]s earned. The Coral Triangle increases the amount of [[Food]] earned. The Organ of Evening Calm increases [[Material]] drops. The Thunder Drum increases [[Weapons in Hyrule Warriors|Weapon]] drops.
{{Noncanon|End}}
