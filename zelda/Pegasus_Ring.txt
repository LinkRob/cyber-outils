The {{Term/Store|Pegasus Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Pegasus Ring<br/>Lengthen Pegasus Seed effect|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Pegasus Ring can be found in a cave at the [[Sea of Storms]] during the past in {{OoA}}. In a [[Linked Game]], the Ring can be found during the present. The [[Zora Scale]] is required to obtain it. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it lengthens the effect of [[Pegasus Seed]]s.<ref name="Description"/> The Pegasus Ring can be transferred to {{OoS}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
