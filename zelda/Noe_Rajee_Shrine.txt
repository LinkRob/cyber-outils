{{Term/Store|Noe Rajee Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Noe Rajee Shrine}}<br/>The Four Winds}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''The Four Winds''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} DLC Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Noe Rajee Shrine}} is found on the very bottom of the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|link}}. It's one of three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} unlocked as part of the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song|link}}".

To access the Shrine, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must shoot four targets at the Flight Range with a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} in quick succession. After the fourth target is shot, {{Term|BotW|Noe Rajee Shrine}} will rise up from beneath the ground at the very bottom of the chasm. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the Shrine, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Noe Rajee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Noe Rajee. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Noe Rajee|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Noe Rajee Shrine}} requires {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link|display=glide}} around an open area using four main wind gusts surrounding a spinning pillar that contains several small compartments and platforms. Hidden within the pillar are four {{Plural|BotW|Switch|link}} that must be hit with a weapon or {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} to activate. Each {{Term|BotW|Switch|link}} that is activated turns on an additional wind gust on the spinning pillar.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} activates all four {{Plural|BotW|Switch}}, he is able to use the wind gusts to glide to the top of the pillar. From here, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can glide to a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Shield of the Mind's Eye|link}}. He can glide to the end of the Shrine, where {{Term|BotW|Noe Rajee}} awaits.

Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Noe Rajee}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} rewards {{Term|BotW|Link}} with one of {{Plural|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of the hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Medoh's Emblem.}}|Noe Rajee|BotW}}</ref> If this is the first or second Shrine that has rewarded {{Term|BotW|Link}} with this Emblem, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will then tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} he must collect three and state how may remain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Noe Rajee|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Noe Rajee|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Noe Rajee|BotW}}</ref> If it is the third Shrine to reward him with{{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will tell him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Medoh...}}|Noe Rajee|BotW}}</ref>
