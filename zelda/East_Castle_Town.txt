{{Term/Store|East Castle Town||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''East Castle Town'''|Map|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Cathedral|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Castle Town Watchtower|link}} are within this location.

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|East Castle Town}} is located on the eastern side of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town Ruins|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. It is an area that features many ruined buildings, dead {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}, and is covered in {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}. Near the center of the {{Term|BotW|East Castle Town|display=Town}} ruins, an unlit {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} can be found sitting near a wall. Deactivated {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} can be found scattered through the ruins and can be searched for {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}.

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bow|link}} can be found in the southern bastion. A second stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}} can be found sitting in a small puddle, covered by a large stone slab. A third stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Shield|link}} can be found inside the bastion along the eastern wall. The bastion along the northern tip of the wall contains a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} with a bundle of five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}. A fifth stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} is hidden in the remains of a small decayed tower to the west of the {{Term|BotW|East Castle Town|display=Town}}, surrounded by {{Plural|BotW|Pool of Malice|link}}. Within the walls of a {{Term|BotW|Ruined House|link}} near the southern part of the {{Term|BotW|East Castle Town|display=Town}} ruins, a sixth stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}} can be found.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} {{Term|BotW|Pinwheel|link}} can be found on the southwestern wall, and if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} stands near it, five {{Plural|BotW|Balloon|link}} will appear and spin in a spiral, each a farther distance from {{Term|BotW|Link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots these five {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will appear and gift him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
