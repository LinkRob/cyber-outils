{{Term/Store|Kinov||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kinov|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kinov}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} child who lives in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}. He spends his day by his mother {{Term|BotW|Kiana|link}}'s side, following her around as goes about her day. At night, he sleeps in his home with his brother, {{Term|BotW|Zuta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Zzz... Zzz...|Kinov|BotW}}</ref>

The first time {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kiana}}, she will turn to {{Term|BotW|Kinov}} to ask him what he wants for dinner.<ref>{{Cite|What should I cook for tonight's dinner?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kinov}} responds that he wants {{Term|BotW|Seafood Paella|link}}, since he likes how she makes it.<ref>{{Cite|Make your {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}}! It's so good and yummy and great!|Kinov|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} decides to go with his suggestion,<ref>{{Cite|That settles it!<br/>I'll make some {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}}.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kinov}} celebrates.<ref>{{Cite|Yaaaaay!|Kinov|BotW}}</ref>

During the day, {{Term|BotW|Kinov}} will express his desire for his mother to make {{Term|BotW|Seafood Paella}} for dinner.<ref>{{Cite|I hope dinner is {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}}! It's sooooo yummy!|Kinov|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hope mom cooks {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}} tonight!|Kinov|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} is preparing their dinner, {{Term|BotW|Kinov}} complains that he is hungry.<ref>{{Cite|Mommy, I'm hungry!<br/>Can you cook some more food?|Kinov|BotW}}</ref> When his entire family is gathered to eat, {{Term|BotW|Kinov}} proclaims that {{Term|BotW|Kiana}}'s food is the best.<ref>{{Cite|My mommy's food is the best!|Kinov|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to speak to him while he is heading into his home for the night, {{Term|BotW|Kinov}} informs him that he has to sleep.<ref>{{Cite|I gotta go to sleep... Good night.|Kinov|BotW}}</ref>
