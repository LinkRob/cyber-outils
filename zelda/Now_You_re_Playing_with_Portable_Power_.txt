"{{Term/Store|Now You're Playing with Portable Power!||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: Now You're Playing with Portable Power!}}<br/>04/19/2022 |author= Nintendo |published= April 19, 2022 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/a63bb418/index.html |title= Event: Now You're Playing with Portable Power! |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Now You're Playing with Portable Power!}}" is an event wherein a greater number of {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} that debuted on the [[Game Boy]] or [[Game Boy Color]] randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits that made their debut on Game Boy or Game Boy Color are coming to the Spirit Board!|author= Nintendo |published= April 19, 2022 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/a63bb418/index.html |title= Event: Now You're Playing with Portable Power! |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
