{{Term/Store|Julietta||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Young Couple}}
{{Term|TMC|Julietta}} lives on the eastern side of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, south of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}, in a blue {{Term|TMC|Girl's House|link|display=House}}. Her house is connected with {{Term|TMC|Romio|link}}'s by a small board that {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} can walk across.

She is first seen with {{Term|TMC|Romio}} during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, standing near {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square|link}}; she complains about {{Term|TMC|Romio}}'s interest in the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}, believing them to be a myth.<ref>{{Cite|Day and night, he's going on and on about Picori this and Picori that! Doesn't he know they're nothing but a silly fairy tale to make kids behave?|Julietta|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Julietta}} can be found inside her {{Term|TMC|Girl's House|display=House}}, where she writes a letter to {{Term|TMC|Romio}} and brushes off any attempts at conversation.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, hello. My brother's at school. I'm writing a letter right now, so I don't have any time to play with you. Sorry.|Julietta|TMC}}</ref>

While attempting to locate {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is tasked with retrieving overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}}, one of which is in the possession of {{Term|TMC|Julietta}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} enters her {{Term|TMC|Girl's House|display=House}} during this time, there will be a note on her table addressed to {{Term|TMC|Verona|link}}, her mother, stating that she is currently at {{Term|TMC|Romio}}'s {{Term|TMC|Guy's House|link|display=House}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dear Mom. I'll be next door if you need me.|Julietta|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} walks to the center of her house, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} will point out the {{Term|TMC|Library Book|display=Book}} on top of her bookshelves and state that the {{Term|TMC|Library Book|display=Book}} cannot be knocked down by ramming into it.<ref>{{Cite|Hmn?! Say, look at that, Link! There's a book up on top of the bookcase! But how are we going to get it? We can't reach it, and we have no ladder. And I don't think just shaking the bookcase will make it fall, either. This presents an interesting problem, wouldn't you say?|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} speaks to {{Term|TMC|Julietta}} directly, she will commend {{Term|TMC|Link}} for being a dedicated librarian but will ask him to retrieve it in her stead.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? A book from the library? A Hyrulean Bestiary, is that the one? Yeah, I checked it out a while ago. You came all this way to get it? Wow! You're one dedicated librarian! But... I don't know how to tell you this, but it's back at my house. Would you mind going there to get it? I just can't leave right now, see...|Julietta|TMC}}</ref>

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} speaks to {{Term|TMC|Rolf|link}} while {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized, he will complain about the heat produced by the fireplace and the fact that there are more people in his house, adding to the heat.<ref>{{Cite|It's too hot! Arf... The fireplace is too hot... Now there are even more people in here, and it's getting too hot to bear! What will it take to cool this place down!? Pant, pant, pant!|Rolf|TMC}}</ref> In order to reach {{Term|TMC|Julietta}}'s house while {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must douse the flames in the fireplace with {{Term|TMC|Water|link}} from an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}},<ref name="Miffed"/> revealing a pathway to the roof where {{Term|TMC|Link}} can walk across the beam connecting the two houses.

From inside {{Term|TMC|Julietta}}'s house, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must make his way up onto the top of the bookshelf while avoiding {{Term|TMC|Scratcher|link}}, her {{Term|TMC|Cat|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} warns {{Term|TMC|Link}} of the {{Term|TMC|Cat}}, claiming that it looked at him as if it wanted to eat him.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Did you see that cat looking at me?! I know it wants a piece of me!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} makes it past {{Term|TMC|Scratcher}}, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} will exclaim in relief.<ref>{{Cite|Waaah! That really startled me! Everything's scary when you're small!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> To retrieve the {{Term|TMC|Library Book|display=Book}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must climb the small staircase leading to the top of the bookshelves, push the {{Term|TMC|Library Book|display=Book}} off, and return as while regularly sized to claim it.<ref>{{Cite|You got a library book called A Hyrulean Bestiary!|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

If {{Term|TMC|Julietta}} is spoken to after returning {{Term|TMC|A Hyrulean Bestiary}}, she will thank {{Term|TMC|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You returned my book? Thanks!|Julietta|TMC}}</ref> As the game progresses, she notes the strange behavior of {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, but believes it irrelevant to her and {{Term|TMC|Romio}}.<ref>{{Cite|They say the king's been weird lately. But that doesn't really have anything to do with our love...|Julietta|TMC}}</ref> She remains in {{Term|TMC|Romio}}'s {{Term|TMC|Guy's House|display=House}} until {{Term|TMC|Link}} sees the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, whereupon she returns to her own home.

{{Term|TMC|Julietta}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}, don't you? Well, OK! Let's give it a try.|Julietta|TMC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|TMC|Julietta}}, along with her boyfriend {{Term|TMC|Romio|link}}, are a reference to the famous {{Wp|William Shakespeare}} play ''{{Wp|Romeo and Juliet}}''.
