{{Term/Store|Green Chuchu|Green Chuchus|Series, MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD, TMC, PH, SS|plural}},{{Exp Game|MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD, TMC, PH, SS}}<ref name="E 171">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 171 {{Exp Game|MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD, TMC, TP, TPHD, PH, SS}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Green Chu|Green Chus|TP, TPHD|plural}},{{Exp Game|TP}}<ref name="E 171"/> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= That's a {{Color|MM White|Green Chuchu}}.<br/> It's nothing to be afraid of...It usually has something in its stomach that's of use.
}}

{{Plural|MM3D|Green Chuchu}} are commonplace to {{Term|MM3D|Termina Field|link}}, and carry {{Plural|MM3D|Magic Jar|link}} in their bodies.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Tingle
|quote1= A '''Green ChuChu'''!  Pulverize it and collect some green Chu Jelly!
}}

{{Plural|TWWHD|Green Chuchu}} can be found on {{Term|TWWHD|Pawprint Isle|link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Haven|link}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Forbidden Woods|link}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Earth Temple|link}}, and the {{Term|TWWHD|Wind Temple|link}} in {{TWW}}. They behave like and are often found with {{Plural|TWWHD|Red ChuChu|link}}. They, however, possess the ability to shrink into a puddle to evade {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}}'s attacks. When defeated, they drop {{Term|TWWHD|Green Chu Jelly|link}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can give {{Term|TWWHD|Doc Bandam}} 15 {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Chu Jelly}} to craft {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Potion|link}}, making them available for purchase.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Plural|TMC|Green Chuchu}} are common to the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}} in {{TMC}}. They attack simply by jumping.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Plural|TPHD|Green Chu}} are created when a {{Term|TPHD|Blue Chu|link}} merges with a {{Term|TPHD|Yellow Chu|link}} in the [[Wii]] version of {{TP}} and in {{TPHD}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= SuperZambezi|published= January 19, 2019|retrieved= January 31, 2020|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ILM5DkuARNE|title= Green Chu Jelly - Zelda: Twilight Princess Wii + HD|site= YouTube|type= video}}</ref> In the [[Nintendo GameCube]] version, the merge will result in a {{Term|TPHD|Purple Chu|link}} instead.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= WhiteWolfos14|published= n.d.|retrieved= January 31, 2020|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ETChA6zIGKs|title= TP: Green Chu Jelly not obtainable on GC|site= YouTube|type= video}}</ref> When defeated, they will leave {{Term|TPHD|Green Chu Jelly|link}} behind, which can be scooped up with an {{Term|TPHD|Empty Bottle|link}}.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Stub|PH}}
{{Plural|PH|Green Chuchu}} can be found in the {{Term|PH|Temple of Courage|link}} in {{PH}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
{{Stub|SS}}
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Target lock: {{Color|SS Blue|Green Chuchu}}</p><p>These creatures prefer exceptionally damp environments. They will encompass and digest anything they can absorb into their gelatinous bodies.</p><p>As they have no genders, they are believed to increase their population by dividing themselves into several smaller-sized Chuchus.</p>
}}

{{Plural|SS|Green Chuchu}} are found on land in {{SS}}, in [[Faron Woods]], [[Skyloft]], and other places. They are more complex to defeat than in previous Zelda games, having to be split into smaller variants, which should then be killed, otherwise they would merge back. They are now the weakest Chuchu, since [[Red Chuchu]]s are on fire. If they make contact with Link, they will hold him until he wiggles out.
