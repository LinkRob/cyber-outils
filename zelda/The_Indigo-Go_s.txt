{{Term/Store|The Indigo-Go's||Series, MM, MM3D}},<ref name="Band">{{Cite|This is the room of Evan, the leader of the Zora Band, The Indigo-Go's.|Zora|MM3D}}</ref> also known as the '''Zora Band''',<ref name="Band"/> is a group in {{MM}}. Famous across [[Termina]], it is a [[Zora]] music band consisting of five members. The band is based in [[Zora Hall]] of [[Great Bay]].<ref>{{Cite|The Indigo-Go's is a wildly popular band right now.|Zora|MM}}</ref> One of their most popular hits is "[[Ballad of the Wind Fish#Majora's Mask|Ballad of the Wind Fish]]," which was made famous by the previous generation of The Indigo-Go's.<ref>{{Cite|The Indigo-Go's is the popular group that we Zora are so proud of. They've put out a lot of songs. One of their hits is "Ballad of the Wind Fish." You know that song, don't you?|Toto|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's their old standby... "Ballad of the Wind Fish." That's the song that members of the previous generation of The Indigo-Go's made famous!|Gorman|MM}}</ref>

==Members==
The members include:
*[[Lulu]], the lead singer.<ref>{{Cite|The one you heard singing must have been the original Lulu, mother of the current lead singer who's also named Lulu.|Toto|MM}}</ref>
*[[Evan]], pianist, songwriter and leader of the band.<ref>{{Cite|This is the room of Evan, the leader of the Zora Band, The Indigo-Go's.|Zora|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|They've got Evan, the keyboard king...|Zora|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Isn't songwriting the job of the bandleader? What, you're not happy with my songs?|Evan|MM}}</ref>
*[[Mikau]], the guitarist.<ref>{{Cite|But above all, Mikau's weeping guitar and the shimmering voice of Lulu make the best songs!|Zora|MM}}</ref>
*[[Japas]], the bassist.<ref>{{Cite|This is the room of Japas, bassist for the Zora band, The Indigo-Go's.|Zora|MM}}</ref>
*[[Tijo]], the drummer.<ref>{{Cite|This is the room of Mikau and Tijo, the guitarist and drummer for the Zora band, The Indigo-Go's.|Zora|MM}}</ref>
*[[Toto]], the manager of the band.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, forgive me. I am Toto, manager of the band, The Indigo-Go's.|Toto|MM}}</ref>

Each of the members have their own room in Zora Hall, with the exception of Mikau and Tijo, who share a room. The entrance to their rooms are guarded by a Zora who will not allow entry to anyone who is not a band member.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry, but he's busy practicing for the concert, so I can't let you see him. If you're not a band member, you can't go in...Even if you're an acquaintance.|Zora|MM}}</ref>

==Biography==
[[File:MM The Indigo-Go's Performing.png|thumb|right|300px|The Indigo-Go's performing at the [[Milk Bar]]]]
The Indigo-Go's had planned a performance on the day of the [[Carnival of Time]];<ref>{{Cite|The Indigo-Go's, the Zora Band that's revived the deep-sea sound with its famous song, will be playing at the Carnival of Time!!! Contact Madame Aroma regarding performances and details.|N/A|MM}}</ref> however, when Lulu laid seven [[Zora Egg|Eggs]], she lost her voice. To make matters worse, they were soon stolen by the [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirates]], forcing their manager to cancel the show.<ref>{{Cite|But that girl, our vocalist... She laid some strange eggs. And she's lost her voice, You can't hear what she says.|Mikau|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Actually, there's been an unusual accident in Great Bay... And due to this unusual accident, Lulu, the diva in The Indigo-Go's, has lost her voice.|Toto|MM}}</ref> Evan, keeping Lulu's problem a secret from the rest of the band, charged Mikau with the task of retrieving the Eggs from the Pirates in the hopes that the Eggs were the key to getting Lulu's voice back.<ref>{{Cite|...Mikau! We need those eggs safe so Lulu can get her voice back.|Evan|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm still keeping Lulu's problem a secret from the other band members.|Evan|MM}}</ref> Unfortunately, Mikau was gravely injured by the thieving women and was left in the waters of the [[Great Bay Coast]].

When [[Link]] travels to Great Bay, he finds Mikau floating offshore, nearly dead, and drags him back to land in an attempt to save him. In spite of his efforts, Mikau passes away after Link plays the "[[Song of Healing]]", but not before the guitarist plays a final song in which he pleads that the young [[Hero of Time]] help get Lulu's voice back by retrieving her Eggs.<ref>{{Cite|Baby! If I die like this... Even if I die... It won't be in peace... That's for suuure! Somebody, please rescue her eggs before the pirates take their toll. Oh, somebody, somebody, please heal my soul. [...] Carve my farewell song on my grave... I'm depending on you to help that singer girl...|Mikau|MM}}</ref> Mikau leaves the [[Zora Mask]] behind when he dies, allowing Link to transform into a [[Zora Link|Zora form]] resembling the guitarist himself and successfully regain the Zora Eggs. After he takes the Eggs to the {{Term|MM3D|Marine Research Lab|link}}, the Eggs hatch and the newborns teach Link the "[[New Wave Bossa Nova]]".<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... If these Zoras were born to teach this song, then hurry! You must play this song for the Zora who laid these eggs!|Professor|MM}}</ref> When he plays the song in front of Lulu, she recalls that her [[Lulu's Mother|mother]], the original diva of the band,<ref>{{Cite|The one you heard singing must have been the original Lulu, mother of the current lead singer who's also named Lulu.|Toto|MM}}</ref> used to sing it to her, and her voice is restored.<ref>{{Cite|But that song you played for me...My mother used to sing it often...long ago... Those eggs were laid to remind me of that.|Lulu|MM}}</ref>

Once Link defeats [[Gyorg (Boss)|Gyorg]] from the [[Great Bay Temple]], The Indigo-Go's have a much-anticipated [[Band Rehearsal|rehearsal]] on the [[Zora Hall]] stage.<ref>{{Cite|Well, the town's concert is very soon. Let's start our rehearsal!|Lulu|MM}}</ref> During the ending credits, the band, along with Link as a Zora, can be seen performing a longer version of their rehearsal song in the [[Milk Bar]].

== Trivia ==

* In the Japanese version of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', The Indigo-Go's are called '''The Zora-Band: Dull Blue'''.
* Lulu and Toto, respectively, are the Terminan counterparts of [[Princess Ruto]] and [[King Zora]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''.
* Several members of the band, when entering their respective rooms, can be heard playing music from past games of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], as [[Cameos in The Legend of Zelda|Easter Eggs]]. Japas and Evan play the [[dungeon]] and [[Game Over]] theme from ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', respectively, while Tijo plays the [[cave]] theme from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]''.
