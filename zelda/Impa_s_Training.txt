{{Term/Store|Impa's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 14 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Impa's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Impa's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Endura Carrot|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Sunset Firefly|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}}'s objective is to defeat 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are  Hylian {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Soldier|link}}, and more spawn as {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}} defeats them. Once {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}} reaches 90 KOs, a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} also spawns. Once 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} spawn and the time limit is taken away. All three of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} must be defeated for the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} to end.
