{{Term/Store|Rudania's Emblem|Rudania's Emblems|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Rudania's Emblem}}<br/>The commemorative symbol of a fire trial's completion by one who wishes to control a Divine Beast. Collecting three emblems will lead to a new challenge.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Rudania's Emblems are collected as part of the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}", which is exclusive to {{TCB}} DLC Pack. They're given to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} by the {{Plural|BotW|Monk|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sharo Lun Shrine|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika Shrine|link}} after he completes them.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Rudania's Emblem.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> After completing the third Shrine, a Monk will direct Link to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Rudania.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

Once he arrives, Link will hear the voice of a Monk offer him a trial in the {{Term|BotW|Illusory Realm|link}} in exchange for all of his Emblems.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the one who approaches this Divine Beast... In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial. In exchange for Rudania's Emblems... you will be granted the chance to tread through the realm of memories. Those who lack determination will find this trial unforgiving. Do not take this place lightly nor dismiss it as merely a world within your mind. The truth is much deeper than you know.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> He'll be transported inside Rudania, where he will once again fight {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon|link}}. After defeating it, however, Link may challenge it again at any time.
