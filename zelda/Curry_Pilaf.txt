{{Term/Store|Curry Pilaf|Curry Pilafs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Curry Pilaf}}<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Curry Pilaf is made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Hylian Rice]], a [[Bird Egg]], [[Goat Butter]], and [[Goron Spice]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}.They restore four {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} when eaten, but they can also be sold for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. The Goron Spice used to make these pilafs gives them a rich, spicy aroma.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding a fifth ingredient, several different variations of Curry Pilaf can be made:

* '''Hearty Curry Pilaf''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Curry Pilaf}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating this will completely restore all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/> They're also worth 60 Rupees when sold.

* '''Energizing Curry Pilaf''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Curry Pilaf}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. When eaten, these pilafs will replenish a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/> They heal four of Link's Hearts, and they sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Enduring Curry Pilaf''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Curry Pilaf}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding either {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}}. Consuming these meals will completely replenish and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/> They restore nine Hearts in Link's Life Gauge, but they can also sell for 60 Rupees.

* '''Hasty Curry Pilaf''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Curry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding speed-related ingredients. Eating one will temporarily boost Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/> They heal for five Hearts, and they can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* '''Spicy Curry Pilaf''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Curry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. When eaten, they'll grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] for a short period of time.<ref name="Spicy"/> They heal eight Hearts in his Life Gauge and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Electro Curry Pilaf''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Curry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electricity-related ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/> They heal eight of Link's Hearts, but they can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Curry Pilaf''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Curry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding attack-boosting ingredients. Eating one of these pilafs will temporarily boost Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/> They restore five Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Tough Curry Pilaf''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Curry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding defensive ingredients. When consumed, Link's defense will be temporarily increased.<ref name="Tough"/> They replenish five Hearts in Link's Life Gauge, and they sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Sneaky Curry Pilaf''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Curry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}-boosting ingredients. Eating one will increase Link's Stealth abilities for a short time.<ref name="Sneaky"/> They heal five of Link's Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Chilly Curry Pilaf''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Curry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The Goron spice used in this pilaf has given it a rich, spicy aroma.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using cold ingredients. When eaten, Link will temporarily gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/> They restore five Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.
