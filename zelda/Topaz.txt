==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Topazes can be found by destroying [[Ore Deposit]]s and [[Rare Ore Deposit]]s, as well as by defeating {{Plural|Series|Stone Talus (Rare)|link}}. The travelling merchant [[Kairo]] and the store [[Ore and More]] both sell Topazes for differing amounts. They're a precious gem that are said to have the power of electricity.<ref name="Inventory"/>

The jewelry shop [[Starlight Memories]] requires five Topazes as payment for the [[Topaz Earrings]]. In addition, the merchant [[Ramella]] in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}} will occasionally ask for Topazes, paying more than the regular price.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, do you have {{Color|BotW Red|10 topazes}}?|Ramella|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You'll get 1,800 rupees if you sell them at a store...or you can trade them to me for {{Color|BotW Red|2,000 rupees}}.|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] also accepts Topazes as an ingredient for Yellow [[Dye]].

Several {{Plural|Series|Armor|link}} require Topazes to be upgraded at a {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. The Topaz Earrings and the [[Hero of Twilight Set]] both needs Topazes to be upgraded to the first, second, third, and fourth tiers. Each piece of the {{Term|BotW|Rubber Armor (Set)|link}} set also require Topaz to be upgraded to their fourth tier.

* Total for '''Topaz Earrings''': 22 (2, 4, 6, 10)

* Total for '''Hero of Twilight Set''':  57 (1, 3, 5, 10) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Rubber Armor''':  30 (0, 0, 0, 10) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

{{Noncanon|Start}}
In Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity, Topazes have their appearance in Breath of the Wild. They can be bought at the store in the Gerudo Desert, or be found in gray treasure chests in main quests, as well as be obtained through first-clear rewards. They can be sold to most Merchants for 180 Rupees, but Dayfah will buy them for 450 Rupees.
