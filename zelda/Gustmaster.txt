Gustmaster is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Gustmaster|Entirely immobile, this unusual creature intermittently blows gusts of air strong enough to lift a person off their feet.<ref name="Gustmaster">{{Cite|Entirely immobile, this unusual creature intermittently blows gusts of air strong enough to lift a person off their feet.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Gustmasters are immobile enemies that create bursts of wind to shove foes away, often knocking them off ledges. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Gustmaster [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of three.
