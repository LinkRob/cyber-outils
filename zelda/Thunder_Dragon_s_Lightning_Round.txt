The {{Term/Store|Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round||Series, SS}} is a {{Term|SS|Mini-Game|link}} in {{SS}},{{Name Ref|SS}} which becomes available after giving [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]] the [[Life Tree Fruit]].

==Features==
The Thunder Dragon's Lighting Round is, as its name implies, a challenge that [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]] presents to [[Link]]. The objective of the challenge is to either complete all 9 (12 in [[Second Quest#Hero Mode|Hero Mode]]) boss battles or all of the [[Silent Realm]] challenges. In normal mode, {{Plural|SSHD|Boss}} cannot be fought if they haven't been fought already, and {{Term|SSHD|Demise|link}} can never be fought in normal mode; in {{Term|SSHD|Hero Mode|link}}, all 12 {{Plural|SSHD|Boss}} are available as soon as the {{Term|SSHD|Minigame}} is unlocked, and {{Term|SSHD|Demise}} is also available to fight. However, he will always be fought as the player's final battle in the round, unless he is picked manually at the start. However, the [[Horde Battle]] and [[Ghirahim|Ghirahim's]] third fight can occur at any point.

===Boss Battles===
If Link chooses to replay Boss battles, he must face all of the Bosses in succession of each other without the use of bottled items. The only items Link will be able to use are the items he had at the time he fought each boss and whichever [[Shield]] he is currently equipped with, though the effects of the [[Stamina Potion]], [[Guardian Potion]], and [[Air Potion]] will persist through the fights, if any of these are consumed before starting the Lightning Round. The potions' bonuses will last until their timer runs out. The effect of the potions can also be extended from 3 to 9 minutes if the [[Potion Medal]] is equipped. At the start of the round, the young hero will be able to choose the first Boss battle. Then the Thunder Dragon will choose the next battle depending on how well Link performed in the battle.

The first time Link defeats a given Boss through the Lightning Round, and each time Link beats his record time thereafter, Lanayru will automatically reward him with 50 [[Rupee]]s. If Link dies, he will only receive the Rupees he has earned by beating his best times.
