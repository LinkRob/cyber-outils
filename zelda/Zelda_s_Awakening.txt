"{{Term/Store|Zelda's Awakening||Series, BotW}}" is the seventeenth {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Zelda's Awakening}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #17}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Once {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has unlocked all twelve {{Plural|BotW|Memory|link}} from the {{Plural|BotW|Picture|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} to learn the location of the final memory.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Then I suppose it's time. I will reveal the location of the final memory.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Hopping down for her pillow, she reveals that {{Term|BotW|Zelda|link}} instructed her to wait to show something to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lady Zelda gave me specific instructions. She asks me to wait to show you this.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She stands in front of the {{Term|BotW|Final Picture|link}}, which is hanging on her wall, asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if it looks familiar, and states he should be able to get there in a half a day.<ref>{{Cite|This is the final picture. Does it look familiar? From this village, you should be able to get there in a half-day's time.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She sends {{Term|BotW|Link}} off, telling him to seek out the location.<ref>{{Cite|Now go, child. Seek out what Lady Zelda has shown you.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

The memory is unlocked in {{Term|BotW|Ash Swamp|link}} near {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|link}}. Several {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}}, corrupted by {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}, are seen scouring the area. {{Term|BotW|Link}} is visibly weakened, but he continues to protect {{Term|BotW|Zelda}} despite her protests.<ref>{{Cite|Link, save yourself! Go! I'll be fine! Don't worry about me! Run!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> He stumbles to his feet again, just as a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker}} finds and locks onto them.

With a shout,<ref>{{Cite|NO!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Zelda}} pushes {{Term|BotW|Link}} out of its crosshairs and holds her hand up. A glowing light forms, and the symbol of the {{Term|BotW|Triforce|link}} appears on the back of her hand. The light expands, deactivating all nearby {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}. Looking at her hand, {{Term|BotW|Zelda}} is in shock and wonders if that was her sealing power.<ref>{{Cite|Was... Was that...? The power...|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

Behind {{Term|BotW|Zelda}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} finally collapses. {{Term|BotW|Zelda}} rushes to his side, shaking him and begging him to get up.<ref>{{Cite|No, no... Link! Get up!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> He coughs as she holds him up, and she tells him he will be fine.<ref>{{Cite|You're going to be just fine...|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Link}} goes limp in her arms, and {{Term|BotW|Zelda}} begins to cry. Suddenly, a noise can be heard from the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}. Zelda hears {{Term|BotW|Fi|link|display=a voice}} within the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|display=Sword}} that tells her of a way to save {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The sword...? So he can... He can still be saved?|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

Two {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} guards arrive, kneeling by the princess's side. {{Term|BotW|Zelda}} commands them to take {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}, stating that it is the only way to save his life.<ref>{{Cite|Take Link to the Shrine of Resurrection. If you don't get him there immediately, we are going to lose him forever! Is that clear?!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She tells them to go, reminding them that {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s life is in their hands.<ref>{{Cite|So make haste and go! His life is now in your hands!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* During the game, {{Term|BotW|Garill|link}} can be found at {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno}}, where he recounts the events of this memory. However, he mistakenly attributes the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}' defeat to {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Almost hard to believe they managed to hold back a whole horde of Guardians here. If not for {{Color|BotW Blue|Fort Hateno}} and the warrior, there'd be no such place as {{Color|BotW Blue|Hateno Village}} anymore.|Garill|BotW}}</ref> when in reality {{Term|BotW|Zelda}} was the one who defeated them.
* When the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} "speaks" to {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}, {{Term|Series|Fi's Theme|link}} can be heard playing as part of the background music, and her signature sound effect can be heard.
* If the player takes a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of the {{Term|BotW|Final Picture}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will take out the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} to look at it before recalling the memory, as he does with other {{Plural|BotW|Memory}}.
