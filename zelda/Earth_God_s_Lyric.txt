The "{{Term/Store|Earth God's Lyric||Series, TWW, TWWHD}}" is a recurring [[Song]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 97 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Learning Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
It is conducted using the [[Wind Waker (Item)|Wind Waker]] and is learned from a {{Term|TWWHD|Stone Tablet|link}} inside the cave of [[Headstone Island]]. Upon learning this melody, the [[Zora]] sage known as [[Laruto]] appears in front of [[Link]]. Laruto was the [[Laruto|Sage of the Earth]] until [[Ganondorf]] stole her soul inside the [[Earth Temple (The Wind Waker)|Earth Temple]].<ref>{{Cite|He attacked this temple and stole my soul, knowing that he had to remove the power contained in that enchanted blade.|Laruto|TWW}}</ref>  Her spirit remained in the hopes that the new Sage of the Earth could be awakened. 

The "Earth God's Lyric" must be played in front of [[Medli]] in order to awaken her as the Sage of Earth.<ref>{{Cite|The song you just conducted is one which will open the eyes of the new sage and awaken within that sage the melody that will carry our prayers to the gods.|Laruto|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, I've been awakened to the knowledge that I'm a sage of the Earth Temple.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> Medli, with the help of Link, plays this song on her [[Medli's Harp|harp]] to open the entrance to the Earth Temple.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|door}} that blocks your way will only open when {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|the sage plays that song}}.|Laruto|TWW}}</ref>

This melody must be played numerous times inside the Earth Temple in order to break down several {{Plural|TWWHD|Stone Tablet}} and thus be able to progress through the dungeon. After defeating [[Jalhalla]], Link conducts the Earth God's Lyric for Medli and Laruto so that the power to repel evil is restored to the Master Sword.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
Medli plays the "Earth God's Lyric" on her [[Rito Harp]] in {{HW}}. She plays this Song to create a [[Crest of Din]] on the ground as one of her attack combos and as one of her attacks while under the effects of [[Focus Spirit]] and it ends. She also plays the Song during her victory animation.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The "Earth God's Lyric" is the first part of {{TWW|-}} introduction theme.
