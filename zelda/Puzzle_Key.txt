{{Term/Store|Puzzle Key|Puzzle Keys|Series, SS|plural}} are items in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS|publisher= Prima|page=28}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Puzzle Keys are found in [[Treasure Chest]]s inside all dungeons aside from the [[Sky Keep]]. They are used to unlock the door leading to the dungeon's [[Boss]]. In order to unlock a door, Puzzle Keys must be rotated into an orientation that will allow it to fit into the shape of the key slot by using the Wii Remote. Doing so is often a small puzzle, requiring multiple rotations to find the correct placement.
