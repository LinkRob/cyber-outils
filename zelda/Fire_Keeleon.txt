{{Term/Store|Fire Keeleon|Fire Keeleons|Series, TFH|plural}} are {{Plural|TFH|Enemy|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TFH|publisher= Prima|page= 15}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TFH|Fire Keeleon}} release {{Plural|TFH|Fireball|link}} instead of {{Plural|TFH|Bomb|link}}. When fighting {{Plural|TFH|Fire Keeleon}}, the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} are unable to strike them with their {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}, as they will get burned if they do. {{Plural|TFH|Fire Keeleon}} can be defeated by first stunning them with the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}}, which will douse their flames. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can also freely attack them while wearing the {{Term|TFH|Goron Garb|link}} {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}, which will protect them from their flames.
