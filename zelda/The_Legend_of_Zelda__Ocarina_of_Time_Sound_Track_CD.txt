'''''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Sound Track CD''''' is an {{OoT}} soundtrack released in America. It was released through the [[Nintendo Power]] magazine in November 1998. The album contains fewer songs than the [[The Legend of Zelda: Original Sound Track|Japanese album]], as well as combining six of the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} melodies onto a single track. The remaining six Ocarina {{Term|OoT|Warping|link}} songs are not listed on the album case.
