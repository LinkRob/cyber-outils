The {{Term/Store|Tah Muhl Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Tah Muhl Shrine}}<br/>Passing the Flame}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Passing the Flame''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Tah Muhl Shrine}} is located in a crag in {{Term|BotW|Trilby Valley|link}} within the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Mayro|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|link}}, who is admiring the painting on the wall.<ref>{{Cite|Have you come to look at this picture too? It's so beautiful... I could spend hours just looking at it.|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> He believes that the painting depicts the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} from a southern direction.<ref>{{Cite|I think the {{Color|BotW Red|stable was painted from a southern direction}}... The composition is just so impressive...|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|A Landscape of a Stable|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} can be found to the southwest of {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}}. It can be accessed at any time, even if the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} has not been activated. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tah Muhl|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Tah Muhl. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Tah Muhl|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The first room of the {{Term|BotW|Tah Muhl Shrine}} is a curving passage, with a pair of stationary {{Plural|BotW|Torch|link}} at the end. The walls in the main room are covered in leafy vines, which can be burned away by using the {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} from the nearby {{Plural|BotW|Torch}}, either by lighting a wooden {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} or an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}. Alternatively, the vines can be ignited with {{Plural|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}} or a piece of {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear|link}} based around {{Term|BotW|Fire}}. Burning one of the vine patches reveals a small passage into the next room blocked by crates, which are also burned in the process by the vines' {{Term|BotW|Fire}}.

The second chamber contains a barred cell locked by a door. The {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} sits on an elevated platform opposite to the cell, but is too high to reach. A ramp leading to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|display=Pedestal}} is suspended by four stacked crates. As with the previous room, these crates can be burned away using the {{Term|BotW|Fire}} provided by the chamber's two {{Plural|BotW|Torch}} or with a {{Term|BotW|Fire}}-based {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}, thereby lowering the ramp. Once approached, {{Term|BotW|Tah Muhl}} will present {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Tah Muhl|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Tah Muhl|BotW}}</ref>

Four {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} are also found in the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}'s chamber. The first is concealed in an alcove by vines behind a pair of crates to the right of the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, and it can be uncovered by burning them. This {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Cobble Crusher|link}}. The second {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} sits atop three crates in the corner of the room. Burning these crates will also burn the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}, revealing an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}} inside.

On the left side of the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}} is a cell with a locked door. A third {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} sits on a wooden platform inside the cell, containing a {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}} to the door. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} can be burned open by burning the vines on the other side that lead to it, thus dropping the {{Term|BotW|Small Key}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can then carry the {{Term|BotW|Small Key}} over the bars using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. The last {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} inside the cell contains a {{Term|BotW|Ruby|link}}.
