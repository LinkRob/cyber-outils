'''Crafter Constructs''' are a [[Zonai]] machines from {{TotK}}

==Overview==
Crafter Constructs are a unique type of [[Construct]] found in the [[The Depths|Depths]]. Significantly larger and more durable than standard Constructs, they possess enhanced combat capabilities that set them apart. With her original body long gone, [[Mineru]], the Sage of Spirit, required a new vessel to aid [[Link]] in his quest. To accomplish this, Link helped construct a Crafter Construct to serve as a host for Mineru’s spirit, allowing her to fight alongside Link and provide invaluable aid.

During [[Ganondorf|Ganondorfs]] return to power, the Demon Kings dark magic corrupted a Crafter Construct transforming it into the [[Seized Construct]], and tasked it with guarding the [[Spirit Temple]], which housed Mineru’s [[Secret Stone]].

[[Master Kohga]] constructs the [[Ultimate Weapon]] intending to gift it to Ganondorf believing this will help bring about the world’s end. However, before his plan can succeed, Link confronts him, destroying the Ultimate Weapon and thwarting Kohga’s ambitions.
