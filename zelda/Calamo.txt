'''Calamo''' is a character in {{HWAoI}}.

==Characteristics==
Calamo, is a [[Korok]] Warrior who is looking for a place to start a forest. He wields a [[Forest Dweller's Sword]] and throws an assortment of three of Fruit as his Special Attack, which can be any combination of [[Bomb Flower|Bomb Flowers]], [[Fire Fruit|Fire Fruits]], [[Ice Fruit]]s, and [[Shock Fruit]]s. He fights alongside the [[Knight Construct|Mysterious Construct]]. His head protrusions and the leaf connected to them form a shape that resembles a bycocket. At some point, he wears a scarf reminiscent of [[Mineru]], [[King Rauru|Rauru]], and the Mysterious Construct's attire.
