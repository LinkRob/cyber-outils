{{Term/Store|Helmhead Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Helmhead Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Helmhead Bridge}} is a stone bridge that spans the {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}} between the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}.

A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} can be found on the ground underneath the {{Term|BotW|Helmhead Bridge}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, a second metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow|link}} can be found atop a platform held aloft by {{Plural|BotW|Sky Octorok|link}}.
