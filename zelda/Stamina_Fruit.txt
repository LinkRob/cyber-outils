==Location and uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
Stamina Fruits grow in many places across [[Hyrule|The Surface]] and [[The Sky]]. They can usually be found in places that require [[Link]] to exert energy to pass, such as on vines Link has to climb or beneath ledges Link has to cross. Upon obtaining a Stamina Fruit, Link's [[Stamina Gauge]] is fully restored. Stamina Fruit cannot be picked up and carried by Link, and are prone to bursting by most of Link's weapons, with the exception of the [[Beetle]] and the [[Bug Net]].

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:HW Stamina Fruit Badge Icon.png|left|64px]]
In {{HW}}, Stamina Fruit appears as an Attack [[Badge]]. It allows Warriors to deplete [[Weak Point Gauge]]s faster. The speed of the gauge depletion can be increased by crafting its enhanced Badges

In {{HW|L}}, Stamina Fruit also appears as a Plant-type [[Food#Hyrule Warriors|Food]]. It is imbued with the Fire [[Element]] When consumed by a [[Companion Fairy]] in the [[Dining Room]] of the [[My Fairy]] mode, Stamina Fruit can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.
{{Noncanon|End}}
