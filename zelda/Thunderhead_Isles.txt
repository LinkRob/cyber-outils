==Overview==

{{Term|TotK|Thunderhead Isles}} are sky islands below {{Term|TotK|Dragonhead Island|link}} in {{Term|Botw|Faron(Region)|link}}. The islands ressemble a dragon and are covered in a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} that {Term|TotK|Link|link}} The Thunderhead Isles are {{Term|ToTK|Sky Islands|link}} that are covered in a {{Term|BotW|thunderstorm|link}}, making traversal difficult if {{Term|TotK|Link}} visits, as anything metallic that he has equipped will have him struck by lightning and climbing in the {{Term|BotW|rain|link}} will cause him to slip. By completing {{Term|TotK|Secret of the Ring Ruins|link}}, the weather will clear. The {{Term|TotK|Joku-Usin Shrine|link}} and a {{Term|TotK|Sage’s Blessing|link}} can be found here.
