{{Term/Store|Shrine of Mystery|Shrines of Mystery|Series, CoH|plural}} are objects in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|SHRINE OF MYSTERY!|N/A|CoH}}</ref> They were introduced as part of the {{OO}} [[Downloadable Content]] update.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Mystery}} appear in {{Plural|CoH|Dungeon|link}} in both {{Term|CoH|Dungeon Mode|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Story Mode — Octavo|link}}. While active, {{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Mystery}} will obfuscate all enemies and items and turns them into question marks. When activated, it drops a [[Ring of Piercing]] and [[Torch of Wisdom]]. The effects of {{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Mystery}} last until the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} is left, the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} {{Term|CoH|Boss|link}} is defeated, or the affected character falls in battle.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, look! A {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! {{Color|CoH Blue|Shrines}} are useful, but they often have a tricky {{Color|CoH Blue|side effect}} that you may not like. Bump into it if you want to take a chance and activate the {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! Shrine effects stay active until you leave the dungeon, defeat the dungeon boss, or are defeated.|Trill|CoH}}</ref>

When destroyed, the shrine drops a [[Scroll of Confusion]].
