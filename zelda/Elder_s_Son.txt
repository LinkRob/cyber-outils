The {{Term/Store|Elder's Son||Series, MM, MM3D}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> also known as the '''Goron's Elder's Son''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 197 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> is a character in {{MM}}.{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Goron Elder's Son}} is referred to as the '''Baby Goron''' in {{Guide|MM|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Versus |page= 41}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
The {{Term|MM3D|Elder's Son}} is a baby {{Term|MM3D|Goron}} who has a rocky back, a large strand of straw-colored hair, large pink lips, and a cloth diaper. He even has the [[Goron Emblem]] on his arms, and, being young, he only has one tooth. His in-game model depicts him with 5 toes, but his artwork depicts him with only one toe.

The {{Term|MM3D|Elder's Son|display=Goron Elder's Son}} lives in the {{Term|MM|Goron Village|link}} and can be found crying inside the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Shrine|link}}. He wails so loudly that he extinguishes the {{Plural|MM3D|Torch|link}} outside of his room.<ref>{{Cite|To think that his crying can put out a torch... That's the Goron Elder's son for you.|Goron|MM}}</ref> The {{Plural|MM3D|Goron}} inside the shrine hold their heads to try and muffle the shrill, sharp sound of his crying. He is crying because his father, the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Elder|link}}, has gone out and has not come back.<ref>{{Cite|...Hyunh, hunh... Ahh, Darmi, where's my daddy? Where's my daddy?|Goron Elder's Son|MM}}</ref> 

When {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} melts the {{Term|MM3D|Ice|link}} entrapping the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Elder}}, he teaches him the introduction to the "{{Term|MM3D|Goron Lullaby|link}}", called the "Lullaby Intro."<ref>{{Cite|It's no good! I can remember only the beginning!|Goron Elder|MM}}</ref> To learn the rest, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must play the Intro in front of his son, who will sing the rest of the notes.<ref>{{Cite|That song...That's the song daddy always plays for me before I go to sleep... I'll sing the next part.|Elder's Son|MM}}</ref> After {{Term|MM3D|Link}} learns the full song, the {{Term|MM3D|Elder's Son}} will have a vision of his dad carrying him, become calm and fall asleep, as will several of the surrounding {{Plural|MM3D|Goron}}.<ref>{{Cite|Aha! So the Gorons fall asleep if they hear the Goron's Lullaby! That's convenient...|Tatl|MM}}</ref>

After {{Term|MM3D|Link}} defeats {{Term|MM3D|Goht|link}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead Temple|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Elder's Son}} will be waiting for him outside the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Racetrack|link}}. If spoken to, he will begin to cry, as the entrance to the Racetrack is blocked by a {{Term|MM3D|Boulder|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But this rock is in he way, so I can't get in to see the race... And spring's just started, too... I was so excited...but there's nothing I can do... I hate it! I hate it! I wanna see it! I wanna see it! I wanna see the race!|Elder's Son|MM}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can open the entrance as {{Term|MM3D|Goron Link|link}} by blowing up the {{Term|MM3D|Boulder}} with a {{Term|MM3D|Powder Keg|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you can destroy the boulder that blocks the entrance to the Goron Racetrack near here using the Powder Keg I'm about to give you, then I'll approve you to carry them.|Medigoron|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Way to go, Darmi! That was cooool!|Elder's Son|MM}}</ref> The {{Term|MM3D|Elder's Son}} will then root for {{Term|MM3D|Link}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Goron Races|link}}<ref>{{Cite|You have to! I want to show everyone how Darmi races!|Goron Elder's Son|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...You're just a little stiff because winter was so long. You're not really like this. Remember how good you really are, then enter the races again.|Elder's Son|MM}}</ref>, giving him a bottle of [[Gold Dust]] if he wins.
