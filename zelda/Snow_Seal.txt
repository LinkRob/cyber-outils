The {{Term/Store|Snow Seal||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|ST|Snow Seal}} is never named as such in-game. In Nintendo of America's English localization, the {{Term|ST|Collection Screen|link}} icon is captioned '''Snow Temple Clear!'''. In Nintendo of Europe's English localization, it is captioned '''Snow Source'''.}}

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Snow Seal}} appears on the {{Term|ST|Collection Screen|link}} and {{Term|ST|Select File|link}} screens once {{Term|ST|Link|link}} defeats {{Term|ST|Fraaz|link}} at the {{Term|ST|Snow Temple|link}} and restores the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} to the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits|link}}.

The symbol of the {{Term|ST|Snow Seal}} appears on the spot of the boss room floor where the {{Term|ST|Snow Temple}}'s {{Term|ST|Force Gem|link}} emerges.
