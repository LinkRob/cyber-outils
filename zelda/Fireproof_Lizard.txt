==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Fireproof Lizard}}
{{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}} can be found in the {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, particularly {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}} and the nearby {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. They can be combined with {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make a {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}}, which will provide {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with temporary {{Term|BotW|Flame Guard|link}} when consumed.<ref name="Inventory"/> However, if they are {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}} with other ingredients, the result will be {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} also accepts {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}} as an ingredient for {{Term|BotW|Gray Dye|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kima|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Southern Mine|link}}, he explains that he is returning from {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}} and forgot to buy a souvenir.<ref>{{Cite|I'm on my way back from {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}} but forgot to buy souvenirs.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> He says that he wants some {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}} and offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Armor|link}} if he can catch 10.<ref>{{Cite|I wanted to capture some of those {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}} that live here on Death Mountain, but those suckers are quick. Say, you look pretty speedy. I bet you could catch a ton! If you bring me {{Color|BotW Red|10}}, I'll even give you some heat-resistant armor.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> This begins the "{{Term|BotW|Fireproof Lizard Roundup|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings the {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}}, {{Term|BotW|Kima}} will reward him with {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Armor|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, a deal's a deal. Hand them over and I'll give you this heat-resistant armor.|Kima|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks, buddy! Here, take this.|Kima|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can deliver three {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Lizard}} to {{Term|BotW|Kima}} for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So if you come across any more {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}}, I'll buy them off you at {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} for {{Color|BotW Red|three}}.|Kima|BotW}}</ref>
