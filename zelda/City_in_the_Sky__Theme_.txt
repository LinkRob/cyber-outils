==Overview==
"City in the Sky" is associated with its eponymous location, the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|link}}.

"City in the Sky" is the 16th track in Disc 3 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Original Soundtrack]]''.
