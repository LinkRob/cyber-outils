==Biography==
{{Stub|BotW}}
The {{Plural|BotW|Shield Breakers}} are a gang of {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}} enthusiasts, consisting only of the members {{Term|BotW|Fin|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Russ|link}}. They run a {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} at a hill in {{Term|BotW|Trilby Plain|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
The name "{{Plural|BotW|Shield Breakers}}" is most likely a reference to the fact that {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} will lose {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}} and eventually break when {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}} on terrain other than {{Term|BotW|Sand|link}}, snow, and marked paths.
