The {{Term/Store|Poe Sisters|Poe Sisters|Series, MM3D|plural}} are recurring characters in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|You defeated the four Poe Sisters!<br/>This was added to your {{Color|MM3D Red|notebook}}!|N/A|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Poe Sisters<br/>The Poe Sisters were four ghostly siblings named Amy, Beth, Joelle and Meg. They haunted the Forest Temple for many years until Link was able to put their souls to rest.<br/>Ocarina of Time|published= n.d.|retrieved= December 12, 2015|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20151209140017/http://www.zelda.com:80/universe/pedia/p.jsp|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia|site= Zelda.com|type= Archive}}</ref> They are four unique [[Poe]] siblings that haunt and torment their respective residences, due to the unrelenting restlessness of their souls. They only make their appearance in the [[Nintendo 64]] installments and their remakes.

==Background==
In life, the Poe Sisters were four priestesses who worshiped at the [[Forest Temple]] in the distant past.<ref>https://archive.org/details/64dream-April-1999-ozidual/64Dream%201999%2004%20%28J%20OCR%29/page/n89/mode/2up</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the Poe Sisters were four ghostly siblings named [[Joelle]],<ref>{{Cite|Joelle – one of the Poe sisters {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Aim for her when she appears!}}|Navi|OoT3D}}</ref> [[Beth (Poe)|Beth]],<ref>{{Cite|Beth – one of the Poe sisters {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Aim for her when she appears!}}|Navi|OoT3D}}</ref> [[Amy]],<ref>{{Cite|Amy – one of the Poe sisters {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|Aim for her when she appears!}}|Navi|OoT3D}}</ref> and [[Meg]].<ref>{{Cite|Meg - one of the Poe sisters {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|If she splits into multiple images, you need..to figure out which one is the}} real one{{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|. Is it this one?}}|Navi|OoT3D}}</ref> They haunted the Forest Temple for many years until [[Link]] was able to put their souls to rest. While most of the sisters are fought as regular enemies similar to ordinary Poes, Meg on the other hand serves as a [[Sub-Boss]]. The first three sisters require the [[Hero of Time]] to solve a puzzle within the Temple before facing them. When defeated, each yielded a different colored flame that illuminated a torch at the center of the temple.<ref>{{Cite|A torch is lit! That's because you beat a ghost, isn't it?!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> When all four torches were lit, Link was granted access to the temple's basement by an elevator, where the dungeon's boss, [[Phantom Ganon]], awaited Link's arrival.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the sisters return, and haunt the [[Spirit House]] of [[Ikana Canyon|Ikana]]. Unlike ''Ocarina of Time'', they do not have major roles for the game's storyline, as the sisters serve as four optional mini-bosses, and can be fought by paying the [[Poe Collector]] thirty [[Rupee]]s. Although the fights against them are identical to the prequel (though Joelle and Beth team up together), Link only has three minutes to defeat them, and his life cannot drop below three [[Heart]]s or else the Poe Collector stops the match.<ref>{{Cite|Defeat the ladies within the time limit and rest their souls in peace. But if your life energy drops below three hearts, I will stop the match. Mercy is the least I can offer.|Poe Collector|MM}}</ref> The reward for defeating them is a [[Piece of Heart]] or a [[Rupee|Purple Rupee]] if already completed.<ref>{{Cite|It seems their souls have been healed. I give to you the earthly possession which the ladies kept. Take it, Link.|Poe Collector|MM}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The Poe Sisters are the only type of [[Poe|Poes]] in the [[Nintendo 64]] games to not have a [[Poe Soul]], as they instead leave the flames of their torches when defeated.
*It is possible to take the Poe Sisters out of their invisibility (even when they perform the lantern twirl) by using a [[Deku Nut]] in {{OoT|-}}. However, there is only a short time before they try to do the lantern twirl again.
*According to [[Tatl]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'', Meg is the youngest of the Poe Sisters while Joelle is the oldest. This, however, is likely a developer oversight, as in the original N64 version, Amy is said to be the youngest while the next in order to oldest are Joelle, Beth, and Meg. Amy's status as the youngest and Meg's as the oldest correspond to the ages of their corresponding March Sisters in ''Little Women.''
**In addition, Joelle is also referred to as "Jo".
*Like regular Poes, the [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_PoeSister_LowLaugh.wav Poe Sisters' laugh] is actually a sped up version of [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Ganondorf_Laugh.wav Ganondorf's laugh] in ''Ocarina of Time'',<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= In Ocarina of Time, Ganondorf's laughter is sped up to create the laughter of the Poe Sisters in the Forest Temple.|author= Gaming Humor & News|published= December 9, 2017|retrieved= April 25, 2018|url= https://twitter.com/VGFGamers/status/939589342930010112|title= Gaming Humor & News on Twitter: "In Ocarina of Time, Ganondorf's laughter is sped up to create the laughter of the Poe Sisters in the Forest Temple.… "|site= Twitter|type= }}</ref> which is also shared by the original [[Garo Master]] in ''Majora's Mask''.
*They bear some resemblance to the four [[Links]] created after Link pulls the [[Four Sword]] from its pedestal, as they share similar colors, albeit with slight differences in tone. This is most likely unintentional.
*The sound of the Poe Sisters [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_PoeSister_Die.wav screaming] when they are defeated is the same as when a normal Poe is captured in an [[Empty Bottle]] in ''Ocarina of Time''.
**If one listens closely, the screech itself is actually a two-tone noise of a high-pitch shriek and a wicked laugh.
*In ''Majora's Mask'', if the Poe Sisters are defeated and Link returns back to the Spirit House, they will reappear behind the barrier, despite the Poe Collector claiming their souls are healed. They also cannot be battled again, as the Poe Collector himself had left the hut. They will also still appear had Link completed the [[Stone Tower Temple]] but cannot be fought.

==Nomenclature==
The names of the four Poe Sisters in {{OoT|-}} and {{MM|-}} are the names of the sisters in the 1868 novel ''{{wp|Little Women}}''.
