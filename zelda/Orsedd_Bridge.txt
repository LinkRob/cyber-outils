{{Term/Store|Orsedd Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Orsedd Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Orsedd Bridge is a decayed wooden bridge that spanning the [[Hylia River]] in [[Hyrule Field]].

A metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing an [[Opal]] can be found in the waters of the Hylia River just south of the Bridge. In [[Master Mode]], a second metallic Treasure Chest containing a [[Royal Guard's Spear]] can be found atop a platform held aloft by [[Sky Octorok]]s beside the Bridge.

A [[Korok]] can be found by breaking the [[Pot]] dangling from a chain underneath the Bridge.
