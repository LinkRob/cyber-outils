{{Term/Store|Hearty Elixir|Hearty Elixirs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Elixir}}<br/>Restores you to full health and increases your maximum hearts. The additional hearts are lost as you take damage.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Drinking {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Elixir}} will restore all of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}, in addition to granting him additional Hearts.<ref name="Inventory"/> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes damage, these extra {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} are lost.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Hearty Elixir}} can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|Series|Hearty Lizard|link}}

By adding any of these hearty ingredients, Link can increase the number of temporary Hearts he receives:

* {{Term|Series|Hearty Durian|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Hearty Truffle|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Hearty Radish|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Big Hearty Radish|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Hearty Salmon|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Hearty Bass|link}}
