"{{Term/Store|EX Champion Mipha's Song||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Champion Mipha's Song}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Ja'Abu Ridge}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
Once {{Term|Series|Link}} has finished the trials on the {{Term|Series|Great Plateau|link}} during "{{Term|Series|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}", the {{Term|Series|One-Hit Obliterator|link}} will split into four glowing orbs and scatter to different areas of {{Term|Series|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, revealing four ancient structures called {{Plural|BotW|Monument|link}}. One of the {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} appears on {{Term|Series|Ja'Abu Ridge|link}} overhanging the south side of the {{Term|Series|East Reservoir Lake|link}}. As {{Term|Series|Link}} approaches the {{Term|Series|Monument}}, a voice belonging to an unspecified {{Term|Series|Monk|link}} speaks to him and instructs him to complete the trials that will appear to him, as they will enhance his power.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I applaud your courage in coming here. The locations that will be revealed to you are of trials that will enhance the power sleeping within. Do not let your guard down...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Three maps then appear on the {{Term|Series|Monument}}'s pillars, each revealing the location of a trial that {{Term|Series|Link}} must undergo.

{{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}, who is standing nearby, remarks on either the {{Term|BotW|Monument}} or the flash of light, depending on how many {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already visited.<ref>{{Cite|What was that...flash of light? Perhaps it was my imagination playing tricks on me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Did that {{Color|BotW Blue|stone monument}} just start glowing? I suppose that proves I didn't imagine it the first time.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This {{Color|BotW Blue|stone monument}} has survived for 10,000 years. What secrets does it hold...|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That light... Is it a reaction to someone nearby? Is it me...or is it...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Noticing {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} asks why he has come to such a remote area.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Fellow traveler... What brings you here? I suppose everyone has their own reasons for traveling.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He directs {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s attention to the {{Term|BotW|Monument}}, as it was mentioned in his teacher's {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Look at that! There's one here too! One of the {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} mentioned in my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If this is the first {{Term|BotW|Monument}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has visited, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will explain that it was mentioned in an {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}} written by his teacher.<ref>{{Cite|This {{Color|BotW Blue|monument}}... I must admit it has taken me by surprise! My teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}} mentions such {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}}, and now one has appeared before my eyes...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s confusion, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} explains that it is missing its key verses.<ref>{{Cite|[Unfinished song?] Yes. The song is mostly complete. However... My teacher left this world without finishing the {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He is now on a journey to finish the song.<ref>{{Cite|I wish the weave the missing pieces together and finish the song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>}} He reveals that there is a verse in the song that mentions the trials that the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}, overcame,<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Mipha}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kass}} sings the verse for {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Allow me to sing it for you...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which provides instructions for the trials he will have to face.<ref>{{Cite|♪Reaching skyward from waters blue.<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}}'s Champion a heart true.♪<br/>♪The hero's power shall grow.<br/>Seek trials {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} show.♪<br/>♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|find what the light's path shows}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|conquer ancient foes}}.♪<br/>♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|chase rings of the waterfall}}.<br/>Champion, the trials call!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After the singing the verse, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he was moved by it.<ref>{{Cite|...So? Did it move you?|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...So? Did the {{Color|BotW Blue|verse}} move you? It was written in honor of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} challenging the trial.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Through learning about the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} believes his is closer to finishing the key verses.<ref>{{Cite|By steeping myself in legends of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champions}}, I'm beginning to form a deeper understanding of them. This will prove invaluable in writing the {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}} of the song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He feels as though his teacher sang the verse in this place before him,<ref>{{Cite|I can feel it... My teacher surely sang this very verse from this very spot...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and he once again affirms his desire to complete the {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}}.<ref>{{Cite|I wish nothing more than to carry on my teacher's dream to complete and perform this special song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After visiting {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} was able to hear stories of {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}} and {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|To do so, I  must learn all there is to know about the Champions of old... Thankfully, {{Color|BotW Blue|the Zora king and prince}} shared memories of their Champion with me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> His ultimate goal is to sing the finished song under the sky, which was beloved by his teacher.<ref>{{Cite|To sing the completed song under this sky that my teacher so loved...that is the end goal of this long journey.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When he is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} may complete the three trials in any order. The marker for the first trial guides {{Term|BotW|Link}} to a cliff in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}, overlooking {{Term|BotW|Knuckel Island|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Ankel Island|link}}. Here Link finds {{Term|BotW|Muzu|link}} who reveals that {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}'s notes mention that the path of light should be followed when the sun is rising.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Princess Mipha}}'s notes contained a phrase related to the trial... "While the {{Color|BotW Red|morning sun}} is newly born, follow the {{Color|BotW Red|Path of light}} to the trial."|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> During the sunrise, a path of light, the sun's reflection on the water, leads to a {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Sea|link}}. Going through this {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring|display=Ring}} causes {{Term|BotW|Kee Dafunia Shrine|link}} to rise from beneath the water. The second trial involves defeating the {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Eyed Guardian|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently, those {{Color|BotW Red|blue-eyed Guardians}} last appeared when {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Mipha}} challenged her sacred trial.|Bazz|BotW}}</ref> After defeating the three {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher|link}} and one {{Term|BotW|Guardian Turret|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sato Koda Shrine|link}} will emerge fromt the ground. The third trial tasks {{Term|BotW|Link}} with following a path of {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}} from the western shore of {{Term|BotW|Mikau Lake|link}}, up both of its {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall|link}}, across to {{Term|BotW|Lulu Lake|link}}, up its {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}}, and finally to {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain|link}}, revealing {{Term|BotW|Mah Eliya Shrine|link}}.

Each of these {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} rewards {{Term|BotW|Link}} with one of {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|link}} upon their completion.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Ruta's Embelm.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} will then inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} that collecting all three {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|display=Emblems}} will unlock a new challenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> They will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} how many more trials he has to complete before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> After completing all three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, the third {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will instruct {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Ruta...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}, he is spoken to again by an unspecified {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, who gives him a trial in exchange for the three {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|display=Emblem}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the one who approaches this Divine Beast... In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial. In exchange for Ruta's Emblems... you will be granted the chance to tread through the realm of memories. Those who lack determination will find this trial unforgiving.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} warns him not to dismiss it as a figment of his mind, as there is a deeper truth to it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Do not take this place lightly nor dismiss it as merely a world within your mind. The truth is much deeper than you know.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} is then transported to the {{Term|BotW|Illusory Realm|link}}, a realm created by his memories, where he once again battles {{Term|BotW|Waterblight Ganon|link}} inside {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|This illusory realm was created from the depths of your memories... The enemy you shall face is a product of the fear that dwells within. This battle is a trial of the soul. You must emerge victorious using only the tools provided.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> This {{Term|BotW|Waterblight Ganon}} is a phantasm born from {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s memories and fears.<ref>{{Cite|You defeated the phantasm of Waterblight Ganon, which was born from your own memories and fears.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}}'s equipment, {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} will be changed to the following:
* {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}}
* Two {{Plural|BotW|Ceremonial Trident|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Silver Bow|link}}
* 10 {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Zora Set|link}} (upgraded twice)
* {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}}

After defeating the phantasm, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is transported back outside of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, where {{Term|BotW|Kass}} has now landed. Noticing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is no longer lost in thought,<ref>{{Cite|Oh? My, my. For some, the best part of traveling alone is the freedom to get lost in thought with only the wind to distract you.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} informs him that learning about {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} has inspired him to write a song in her honor.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Wandering around here and listening to anecdotes about {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Mipha}} has truly invigorated me. In fact, it has inspired me to honor her in my own way, separate from my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}. That is why I felt inclined to roost here, near the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, which {{Color|BotW Blue|Mipha}} herself mastered. I've written a song...to try and capture the essence of {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Mipha}}...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When he sings the song to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, at allows him to visualize the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Mipha's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪Recall the Champion of {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}}'s wrath,<br/>before she walked her chosen path.♪<br/>♪{{Color|BotW Blue|Mipha}}'s kindness was known to all,<br/>which is why the princess came to call.♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Kass's composition, entitled {{Color|BotW Red|Champion Mipha's Song}}, was so true and moving that it allowed you to visualize a memory from 100 years ago.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

After singing, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} notes that, while {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} was kind, she could also be strict.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Though she was known for her kindness, it seems the Zora princess could be quite strict when necessary.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he thought of "{{Term|BotW|Champion Mipha's Song (Song)|link}}",<ref>{{Cite|So? What did you think of {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Mipha's Song}}?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which he reveals makes him feel closer to his teacher.<ref>{{Cite|By writing a song that captures the determination of Champion Mipha, I feel closer than ever to my dear teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} has realized that he is the only one who can complete his teacher's {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}}.<ref>{{Cite|I was struck by a realization...that the missing parts of the song can only be finished by my teacher's pupil. By me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} then hears the voice of {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}, who notices he is now stronger and allows him to use {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Grace|link}} more often.<ref>{{Cite|You're so strong...so much stronger than before. The power of Mipha's Grace will now recharge much faster.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> With his power, {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} believes he is capable of saving both {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}} and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|As powerful as you are, I am certain that you can save Hyrule...as well as the princess who awaits you.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> He then receives {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Grace +|link}}, which reduces the ability's recharge time to eight minutes. After this, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} is completed.
