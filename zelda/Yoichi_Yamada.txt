'''Yoichi Yamada''' is a video game designer at [[Nintendo]].

==Biography==
{{Stub}}
Yamada is one of the veteran game designers at Nintendo, who has been working on systems such as 3DCG and polygons since the early 1990s. In particular, he has long been a core game designer in {{TLoZ|-}} series to date.
