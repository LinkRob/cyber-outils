"{{Term/Store|Riverbed Reward||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Riverbed Reward}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Wetland Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the edge of {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}} near {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Izra|link}} staring into the water.<ref>{{Cite|You noticed Izra at the Wetland Stable staring keenly into Hylia River.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> He wonders what he should do, mentioning his fear of deep water.<ref>{{Cite|What should I do? I'm terrified of deep water...|Izra|BotW}}</ref> However, he notices {{Term|BotW|Link}} listening,<ref>{{Cite|...Hmm?|Izra|BotW}}</ref> he apologizes as he was lost in thought.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Sorry. I was lost in thought.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} shows an interest, {{Term|BotW|Izra}} asks if he sees the shape at the bottom of the {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|display=River}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Lost in thought?] I can't believe I'm about to share this with you, but I'm absolutely stumped. Have you seen that strange shape down at the bottom of the river?|Izra|BotW}}</ref> He thinks it might be a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}},<ref>{{Cite|It's hard to make out, but I'm pretty sure it's a treasure chest.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> though he is unsure how to pull it out of the water without some kind of machine.<ref>{{Cite|But I can't figure out how to fish it out of there. I guess I need...some kind of machine or something to get it out.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Izra}} has been unable to sleep due to his curiosity about its contents.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so curious about what's inside that chest, I can't even sleep.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> When he is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins.

Using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pull the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} from {{Term|BotW|Hylia River}}. {{Term|BotW|Izra}} is shocked by this action,<ref>{{Cite|What the heck?!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> though it turns to awe once he sees that it works.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! The treasure chest! INCREDIBLE! I can't believe you managed to get that treasure chest out of there!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> He urges {{Term|BotW|Link}} to open the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hurry up and open it already!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> Within, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Broadsword|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Izra}}, he commends him for the "wizardry" he used to pull out {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} and asks what was inside.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know what kind of wizardry you used, but that's incredible! And...yeah! What was inside the treasure chest?|Izra|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells him that it was a treasure,<ref>{{Cite|[It was treasure.] Amazing treasure!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Izra}} will become frustrated.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh! That's so frustrating!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> He could have become rich with the treasure inside, and therefore would have attracted women.<ref>{{Cite|If I had just managed to get that chest, I'd be rich! And the ladies would be tripping over each other to be with me!|Izra|BotW}}</ref> However, now that he knows the contents of the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}, he can sleep at night.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I suppose you did all of the hard work with that chest. At least I can sleep now.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells him it was a sword,<ref>{{Cite|[It was a sword.] What? A cool sword, you say?|Izra|BotW}}</ref> he brushes it off as he did not want the treasure anyway.<ref>{{Cite|Whatever. I didn't even want that life-changing treasure anyway...|Izra|BotW}}</ref> This time, he still says that he will be able to sleep now that he knows what was inside.<ref>{{Cite|Well, at least I can relax now that I know what was inside. Thanks.|Izra|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
