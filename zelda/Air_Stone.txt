==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
When {{Term|ST|Link|link}} approaches an {{Term|ST|Air Stone}}, colored orbs of light float from the hole, matching the colors on the {{Term|ST|Spirit Flute|link}}'s pipes. If {{Term|ST|Link}} plays the notes corresponding to these colors, he will learn a new song for the Flute. The {{Term|ST|Air Stone}} will then take off into the sky.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
Song Stones appear in the [[Grand Travels Map]] of [[Adventure Mode]], which is included in the {{PH|-}} & {{ST|-}} DLC Pack. When a Warrior uses an [[Item Card]] on a Song Stone, the Song Stone will take off into the sky, revealing a [[Treasure Chest]] with a duplicate of the Item Card used.<ref name= "Song Stone"/> Each Song Stone will disappear once used but will later reappear after multiple Battles.
{{Noncanon|End}}
