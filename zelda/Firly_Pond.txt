{{Term/Store|Firly Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Firly Pond'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Firly Pond is located in [[Hateno Village]], situated just below [[Link's House]]. Along the path between Firly Pond and Hateno Village rests the [[Horned Statue]], a cursed statue that can exchange Link's [[Heart Container]]s and [[Stamina Vessel]]s.<ref>{{Cite|You look skeptical. Not to worry. Money and life are all as one to me. If you're ready to begin, then first you must sell me either type of {{Color|BotW Red|essence}} for {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}}. If you want to buy an essence back, the price rises to {{Color|BotW Red|120 rupees}}. Before you cry foul, know that you may choose which one I return to you: a {{Color|BotW Red|Heart Container}} or a {{Color|BotW Red|Stamina Vessel}}. I'm eager to help tamper with your essence anytime you want to return and pray here.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref>

A Korok can be found hiding underneath a [[Rock]] on the eastern part of the Pond. He will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] in return for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>  However, a price of rupees will need to be paid in order to exchange these.
