==Biography==
Renado lives in [[Kakariko Village]] with his daughter {{Term|TPHD|Luda|link}}, and it is he who finds the children from [[Ordon Village]] and shelters them when the Village is covered in [[Twilight]] and overrun by [[Zant]]'s minions.<ref>{{Cite|The beasts took us and left us to die...but Mr. Renado found us.|Colin|TP}}</ref> After [[Ilia]] and [[Ralis]] come to the Village, he takes care of both of them as well. [[Telma]] apparently has a romantic attraction to Renado,<ref>{{Cite|If you're going to Kakariko Village, be sure to give my regards to that adorable shaman, OK, honey?|Telma|TP}}</ref><ref name="rude"/> although these feelings are unrequited. When he gives [[Link]] his [[Renado's Letter|Letter]] for Telma, he specifically says he does not wish to see her.<ref>{{Cite|Please take this to Telma! It shames me to say it, but to be honest, I cannot abide Telma's company...|Renado|TP}}</ref> He also mentions that he was friends with [[Mayor Bo]] and sends Link back to Ordon Village to find him and ask him how to best the [[Gorons]] of [[Death Mountain]].<ref>{{Cite|I do know one person who was able to best them and earn their trust... His name is Bo. You may know him as the mayor of your hometown, Ordon. Please, go to him. And while you are there, please let him know that the children are safe.|Renado|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It is a blessing to see Ilia return to good health. The return of her memory is the fruit of your efforts. As for me... Nothing could bring me greater joy than to aid the daughter of an old friend. I look upon her and see her father's smile.|Renado|TP}}</ref>
