{{Term/Store|Rebonae Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rebonae Bridge|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Rebonae Bridge is a bridge that spans the [[Hylia River]] in [[Hyrule Field#Breath of the Wild|Hyrule Field]] just northwest of the [[Wetland Stable]]. The [[Hylian]] traveler, [[Leekah]], can be found on the Bridge being attacked by a [[Bokoblin]], offering Link [[Food]] for saving her.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, thanks. I might've been that thing's stew without you. Hmm, what do I have to give you in return? How's this?|Leekah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I don't even want to think about where I'd be if you hadn't shown up! This is the least I can do in return.|Leekah|BotW}}</ref> She will also make a remark to Link if he rescues her after she is knocked out by the Bokoblin.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, look who's here! The guy who stood around and watched me get beat up! A little help next time, maybe?!|Leekah|BotW}}</ref>

In [[Master Mode]], a wooden platform can be found suspended by [[Sky Octorok]]s. Atop this platform, Link can find a metal [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Double Axe]].

A lone [[Rock]] can be found underneath the west side of the Bridge, a [[Natural Hiding Spot]] for a [[Korok]].
