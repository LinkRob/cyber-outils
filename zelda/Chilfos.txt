{{Term/Store|Chilfos|Chilfos|Series, TP, TPHD, LCT|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 170 {{Exp Game|TP, TPHD}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Snowpeak Ruins: Ranger}}<br/>Move using the Control Stick, and defeat the 25 Chilfos.|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> They are warriors that appear to consist of animated bones made of ice.

==Characteristics==
Chilfos are found in the [[Snowpeak Ruins]] and in the [[Cave of Ordeals]]. They carry large, spear-like icicles that they use as two-handed weapons against [[Link]] in hand-to-hand combat. Chilfos also throw the icicles as javelins to attack Link from a distance. They can be taken out in two hits with [[Bomb Arrow]]s or with a single swing of the [[Ball and Chain]].

A useful strategy in defeating a Chilfos is for Link to keep his distance away from it, where it will attempt to throw its javelin icicle at him. Once it breaks against Link's [[Shield]], the Chilfos will take a few moments to reconstruct a new icicle, at which point Link will be able to successfully slice at the Chilfos with his [[Sword]]. A Chilfos can also be killed instantly by performing a [[Helm Splitter]], [[Jump Strike]], or a [[Back Slice]] upon it.

==Other Appearances==

==={{LCT|-}}===
[[File:LCT Snowpeak Ruins: Ranger Sprite.png|thumb|right|Chilfos in {{LCT|-}}]]
In {{LCT}}, Chilfos appear in Level 6: Snowpeak Ruins: Ranger. They appear identically to their {{TP|-}} appearance, as ice warriors who throw their javelin icicles at Link when he is within sight, and who swing their spears at him if he is at close range. As before, they are able to respawn their spears after they are thrown or destroyed. Chilfos may use their spears to defend themselves from Link's [[Crossbow]] shots, which will shatter their spears. They can be defeated in one shot of a charged Bomb Arrow.
