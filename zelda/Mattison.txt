== Biography ==

=== Tears of the Kingdom ===
Mattison is a Gerudo who was born between the events of Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom. During the events of Tears of the Kingdom, Mattison has grown old enough to be taken to Gerudo Town as per Gerudo Tradition.
