The '''Lovely Pendant''' is an item from {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Lovely Pendant|A pendant made especially for the Great Fairy. Like her, it's gorgeous, sparkling, and one of a kind.<ref name="Lovely Pendant">{{Cite|A pendant made especially for the Great Fairy. Like her, it's gorgeous, sparkling, and one of a kind.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
After handing in the [[Floral Seashell]] and [[Magma Stone]] to the [[Gerudo Artisan]], Zelda receives the Lovely Pendant, which is required to complete [[The Great Fairy’s Request]] [[Side Quest|side quest]].
