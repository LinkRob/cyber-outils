The {{Term/Store|Younger Racing Bro||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300}}</ref>{{Note|Appears as "Racing Bro" in-game.}}

==Biography==
The {{Term|ALBW|Younger Racing Bro}} and [[Older Racing Bro|older brother]] run {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule Hotfoot|link}}, a racing {{Term|ALBW|Minigame|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Younger Racing Bro}} is found atop a hill in the area east of {{Term|ALBW|Lake Hylia|link}}, and acts as the start of the race. By paying 20 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The entry fee is 20 Rupees. If you make it to the finish, you'll get an excellent prize.|Younger Racing Bro|ALBW}}</ref> {{Term|ALBW|Link}} will have the chance to run to the {{Term|ALBW|Older Racing Bro}} at the end goal, north of {{Term|ALBW|Rosso|link}}'s house.<ref>{{Cite|The finish is behind the miner's place. I'll mark it on your map with a flag. My brother is standing at the finish, so talk to him when you get there.|Younger Racing Bro|ALBW}}</ref> If {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} talks to the {{Term|ALBW|Older Racing Bro}} again after completing the race, he will ask {{Term|ALBW|Link}} if he would like to be brought back to the {{Term|ALBW|Younger Racing Bro}}.<ref>{{Cite|Check in with my little brother at the starting point. What, unless it's too much trouble to hightail it all the way back there?|Older Racing Bro|ALBW}}</ref> Accepting will transport {{Term|ALBW|Link}} back to the starting point.
