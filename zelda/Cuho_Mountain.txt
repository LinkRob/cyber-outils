{{Term/Store|Cuho Mountain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cuho Mountain|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Cuho Mountain is located in the western portion of [[Tabantha Frontier]], where it overlooks [[Gisa Crater]] to the south, [[Passer Hill]] to the northwest and [[Strock Lake]] to the east. Cuho Mountain bears little vegetation, growing only [[Cool Safflina]]s along the flat portions of the Mountain and [[Rushroom]]s and [[Swift Violet]]s along its walls. [[Ore Deposit]]s and [[Rare Ore Deposit]]s can be found along the slopes of Cuho Mountain. A [[Great Thunderblade]] sits in the middle of Cuho Mountain's three highest peaks.

The northern base of Cuho Mountain features a series of ruins, housing a [[Black Hinox]] and [[Bareeda Naag Shrine]]. A [[Bokoblin]] encampment sits east of Bareeda Naag Shrine. A magnetic [[Korok]] puzzle can be found at the far eastern base of Cuho Mountain, near Strock Lake.
