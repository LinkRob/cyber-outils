{{Term/Store|Fishing Rod|Fishing Rods|Series, LA, LADX, OoT, OoT3D, MM3D, TP, TPHD, PH, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM, TP, PH}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Fishing Rod}}: Use this to catch fish in bodies of water.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoA (Himekawa)}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, Link can use a Fishing Rod in the [[Fishing]] minigame located north of [[Madam MeowMeow]]'s house in [[Mabe Village]].

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Link can rent a Fishing Rod as a child and adult in the [[Fishing Pond]] located in [[Lake Hylia]]. For 20 [[Rupee]]s, he can use it for as long as he wants.<ref>{{Cite|Well, do you want to go fishing for 20 Rupees? [...] I'm so generous, I'll let you fish for as long as you want.|Fisherman|OoT}}</ref> However, it can only be used inside the pond, as Link must return it before leaving.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Hey! You can't take the rod out of here! When you quit fishing, return the rod to the counter. I'm serious!|Fishing Hole Man|OoT}}</ref>

The default [[Lure]] of the Fishing Rod can only float. However, Link can find the [[Sinking Lure]] around the pond, making the lure sink to the bottom of it. Although, the use of a Sinking Lure is against the rules.<ref>{{Cite|Rules and Regulations / 1. Don't use sinking lures. / 2. Don't litter. / 3. Walk quietly. / 4. Don't cast at anyone.|Sign|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That sinking lure is in violation of the rules!|Fisherman|OoT}}</ref> Besides fishing, adult Link can use the Rod to steal the [[Fisherman]]'s hat.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, two types of Fishing Rod appears: the Bobber Fishing Rod and the Lure Fishing Rod. The Bobber Fishing Rod is obtained from {{Term|TPHD|Uli|link}} after saving her cradle from a [[Monkey]].<ref>{{Cite|There is something I would like to give to you, so can you carry that cradle and come with me? [...] All right... Here you go! It is a little...unpolished. My son, Colin, made this under the instruction of his father. You can use it if you like.|Uli|TP}}</ref> Link can use it to fish almost anywhere a body of water is located. Any [[fish]] caught with the Bobber Fishing Rod are kept in the [[Fish Journal]]. Link can also add [[Bait]], such as worms and bee larvae, to attract fishes more easily. The Rod is first needed to catch a fish for {{Term|TPHD|Sera|link}}'s cat so Link can get the [[Slingshot]]. Afterwards, Link must use the Rod again to catch a [[Reekfish]] in [[Zora's Domain]] in order to follow [[Yeto]]'s trail. However, to catch a Reekfish, Link must first obtain the [[Coral Earring]] from [[Ralis]].<ref>{{Cite|It must be a reekfish. That is a rare specimen found only in the Zora village. It feeds on a valuable type of coral, and without that bait, even our best fishers cannot catch one. Look closely, Link... My earring is made of this coral. I would like to give it to you.|Prince Ralis|TP}}</ref> The earring serves as an upgrade to the Rod, as it also increases the fish bitting rate. Link can also use the Bobber Fishing Rod to catch an empty [[Bottle]] and the Sinking Lure in [[Hena]]'s Fishing Hole.

The Lure Fishing Rod can be rented in Hena's Fishing Hole for 20 or 100 Rupees. The Lure Fishing Rod is controlled differently from the Bobber Fishing Rod and can only be used while riding a canoe inside the pond. It also allows Link to use a wider variety of lures. Besides fishing, Link can also use the Rod to catch a [[Piece of Heart]] in the middle of the Fishing Hole.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, the Fishing Rod can be obtained from the [[Old Wayfarer]] on [[Bannan Island]] by introducing [[Joanne]] to him.<ref>{{Cite|Haha! I finally met the mermaid of my dreams! Never would I have dreamed the mermaid would come to ME! You had a hand in this, young man, and so I want to give you this! I had that fishing rod made so that I could hook the mythical mermaid, but… I obviously don't need it anymore…so you can have it!|Old Wayfarer|PH}}</ref> Link can only use the Rod while sailing the [[World of the Ocean King]] on board the [[SS Linebeck]]. When the ship is above a fish shadow, Link can cast the Rod and try to catch the fish by tapping the touch screen.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 13}}</ref>

==={{MM3D|-}}===
In {{MM3D}}, Link can rent a Fishing Rod in either of the Fishing Ponds located next to the [[Swamp Shooting Gallery]] or in the [[Great Bay]]. Link can rent the rod for 50 Rupees or for free if he has a [[Fishing Hole Pass]]. This time, he can freely use the Sinking Lure whenever he wants.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{OoA|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{OoA|Himekawa}}, a Fishing Rod is used by [[Sir Raven]]. Link finds the knight fishing near a river in [[Labrynna]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Why's he just sitting there fishing?|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 65}}</ref> Raven continues fishing until a fish bites on the line.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I got a bite!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 66}}</ref> He pulls it out of the water and unhooks it from the Rod. After a struggle between Link and the knight, Raven asks the hero to come with him and picks up the Fishing Rod before leaving.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Come with me.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 68}}</ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In {{TP|-}}, it is possible to distract [[Ganondorf]] using the Fishing Rod during the final battle. Ganondorf will watch the hook of the rod, leaving him open to attacks.
