{{Term/Store|Mushroom|Mushrooms|Series, ALttP|plural}} are recurring [[item]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's a simple dish that you can make if you have some {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|mushrooms}}.|Nellie|BotW}}</ref>

==Mainline appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:Mushroom obtained ALttP.png|thumb|Link obtaining the Mushroom in {{ALttP|-}}.]]
[[File:ALttP Mushroom Sprite.png|frame|left]]
[[File:ALttP Mushroom Inventory Icon.png|frame|left]]
In {{ALttP}}, the Mushroom can be found within the [[Lost Woods]]. Upon obtaining the Mushroom, [[Link]] can offer it to the [[Syrup|Witch]] in exchange for the [[Magic Powder]].
{{clear|left}}
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Main|Sleepy Toadstool}}
[[File:Sleepy Toadstool.png|The location of the Sleepy Toadstool in {{LA|DX|-}}|thumb|left]]
In {{LA}}, the Sleepy Toadstool is found in the [[Mysterious Woods]]. It can be given to [[Syrup]] to be made into Magic Powder.
{{Clear|left}}

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Main|Odd Mushroom}}
In {{OoT}}, the Odd Mushroom is part of the [[Trading Quest]]. [[Grog]] gives it to [[Link]] in exchange for [[Cojiro]].

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Magic Mushroom}}
In {{MM}}, a Magic Mushroom can be sold to the [[Magic Hags' Potion Shop]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s each; at least one must be delivered during that cycle before [[Blue Potion]]s can be purchased there. They can also be sold to the [[Curiosity Shop]] for 5 Rupees each.

==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:OoS Mushroom Sprite.png|thumb|left]]
[[File:OoS Link Obtaining the Mushroom.png|thumb|Link receiving the Mushroom in {{OoS|-}}.]]
In {{OoS}}, a Mushroom appears as an item in the Trading Quest. Before he can obtain the Mushroom, Link has to first use the [[Megaphone]] to wake up [[Talon]] in a cave atop [[Mt. Cucco]]. In exchange for being awoken, Talon offers Link a Mushroom, which Link can in turn give to [[Syrup]] in [[Sunken City]] in exchange for the [[Wooden Bird]].<ref>{{Cite|Sniff, sniff!!! That scent!!! Ya've got a Mushroom! I'll trade my Wooden Bird!|Syrup|OoS}}</ref>

There is also the [[Rock Mushroom]], which is a field obstacle that obstructs Link's movement. They ripen in [[Autumn]], allowing them to be picked up and thrown.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Elastic Mushroom, Wake-Up Mushroom}}
[[File:TMC Elastic Mushroom Sprite 2.png|left]]
In {{TMC}}, the Wake-Up Mushroom is required for Link to obtain the [[Pegasus Boots]].

Elastic Mushrooms can also be found in certain areas while Link is shrunk to [[Minish]]-sized. The [[Gust Jar]] can be used to suck and pull back the mushroom caps, which will then throw Link once released.

==={{SS|-}}===
{{Main|Mushroom Spores}}
[[File:Fungal Spores.png|Link obtaining Mushroom Spores in {{SS|-}}|thumb]]
In {{SS}}, mushrooms can be found in the [[Faron Woods]], [[Deep Woods]], [[Skyview Temple]], and on certain islands in [[The Sky]]. Link can slash them with his [[Sword]] to produce [[Mushroom Spores]], which can be stored in Bottles and used to heal [[Loftwing]]s. Glittering mushrooms can also be found, which produce [[Glittering Spores]] than can also be stored in Bottles. They produce different kind of effects when used on items and enemies.

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, mushrooms are a type of [[Material]] that Link can collect for cooking [[Food]] or in some cases upgrading [[armor]]. Mushrooms are typically found on the ground growing next to trees or cliffs. Available mushrooms are [[Hylian Shroom]]s, [[Endura Shroom]]s, [[Stamella Shroom]]s, [[Hearty Truffle]]s, [[Big Hearty Truffle]]s, [[Chillshroom]]s, [[Sunshroom]]s, [[Zapshroom]]s, [[Rushroom]]s, [[Razorshroom]]s, [[Ironshroom]]s, and [[Silent Shroom]]s.

==Spinoff appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWDE Odd Mushroom Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, Odd Mushrooms appear as Plant-type [[Food#Hyrule Warriors|Food]]. They are imbued with the Darkness [[Element]]. When consumed by a [[Companion Fairy]] in the [[Dining Room]] of the [[My Fairy]] mode, Odd Mushrooms can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In {{LA|-}}, the Mushroom is one of many ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' references. When Tarin (who bears resemblance to [[Mario]], and also shares his love of mushrooms) eats one, he turns into a raccoon, which may be a reference to Mario's [[mariowiki:Raccoon Mario|Raccoon]] or [[mariowiki:Tanooki Mario|Tanooki]] transformations.
