==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Lizalfos Infestation}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given five minutes to defeat 300 {{Term|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}}. It can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Durian|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Energetic Rhino Beetle|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hyrule Herb|link}}.
