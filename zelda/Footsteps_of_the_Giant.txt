==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Footsteps of the Giant}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given four minutes to defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}. Only a {{Term|HWAoC|Stalnox}} is seen at first, but a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox}} spawns once either 30 seconds pass or the {{Term|HWAoC|Stalnox}} is defeated. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Durian|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Wildberry|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hydromelon|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
