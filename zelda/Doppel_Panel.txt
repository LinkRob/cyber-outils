{{Term/Store|Doppel Panel|Doppel Panels|Series, TFH|plural}} are an interface in {{TFH}}.<ref name="Desc">{{Cite|The shining icons on the lower screen are your {{Color|TFH Blue|Doppel Panels}}.|Mr. Tudor|TFH}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Plural|TFH|Doppel Panel}} are a set of three panels that appear on the bottom screen of the [[Nintendo 3DS]] once {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} has been bound to the two {{Plural|TFH|Doppel|link}} to set out for the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}.<ref name="Desc"/> Each {{Term|TFH|Doppel Panel}} is color-coded to represent one of the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link|display=three heroes}} and features an icon of that {{Term|TFH|Links}}'s face, the item he carries, and a shortened name for the {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} he wears (eg. the {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic|link}} is called "Hero"). The Panel representing the active {{Term|TFH|Link}} also displays his chosen name, while the others feature the {{Plural|TFH|Doppel}}' stone masks over their {{Plural|TFH|Links}}' faces.

Tapping any of the {{Plural|TFH|Doppel Panel}} will transport {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s soul to the respective {{Term|TFH|Doppel}}, giving the player control over it.<ref>{{Cite|By tapping a {{Color|TFH Blue|Doppel Panel}}, you can transport your soul from one {{Color|TFH Blue|Doppel}} to another.|Mr. Tudor|TFH}}</ref> This is represented by a glowing orb that quickly beams from the active {{Term|TFH|Link}} to the {{Term|TFH|Doppel}}, followed by a puff of colored smoke around the {{Term|TFH|Doppel}} as it becomes the "new" {{Term|TFH|Link}}.
