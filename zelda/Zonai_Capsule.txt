A '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a convenient means to carry portable [[Zonai Device|Zonai devices]]. Once a device is taken out, it cannot be put back in its capsule, and is intended to be disposable. If you talk to one of the [[Mining Construct]]s in the [[Mining Cave]], he will give you three containing a [[fan]] out of pity.{{Cite Item|{{PAGENAME}}|TotK}}

The capsules are created by device dispensers, which produces them by converting e.g. [[Construct Horn]]s and [[Zonai Charge]]s.<ref>{{Big|Maker Construct}} on [[Great Sky Island]]<br>This is a {{Color|BotW Red|device dispenser}}. It produces Zonai capsules by converting certain resources. The resources it accepts are things such as {{Color|BotW Red|construct horns}} and {{Color|BotW Red|Zonai charges}}.</ref>
