==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Aldo}}
{{Term|TWWHD|Aldo}} is a {{Term|TWWHD|Korok|link}} who was born in the {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Haven|link}} where he lives. Before the {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Ceremony|link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Aldo}} is standing to the left of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Deku Tree|link}}, near the {{Term|TWWHD|Lily Pad|link}}. After the {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Ceremony}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Aldo}} goes to the {{Term|TWWHD|Cliff Plateau Isles|link}} to plant new {{Plural|TWWHD|Forest Tree|link}}.

==Trivia==
