{{Term/Store|Woodland Boar|Woodland Boars|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Woodland Boar}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Woodland Boar}}
{{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}} are stocky species of {{Term|BotW|Boar|link}} with hunched backs and rotund abdomens. They are quadrupeds that walk on muscular legs, each one ending in a cloven hoof. Their bodies are covered in brown fur, with the abdomens and heads being a lighter shade than the muddier legs, haunches, tails, and ears. Spots appear down the back of the same muddy color mane rises from the {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}}' shoulders and furry ruffs extend from their cheeks. Their eyes are small and beady. {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}}' snouts are long and narrow, and are used to dig through the ground when grazing. Short ivory tusks poke out of their mouths, which are used to defend themselves when charging at potential threats. Their tails are short and end in tufts of hair. Their tails stand up when they are startled.

{{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}} are herbivorous swine that inhabit grassy and forested areas throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, particularly {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and the [[Lanayru Great Spring]]. When slain, {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}} produce between one and two pieces of {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}}, but they are often quite skittish to approach and will flee once detected. Though uncommon, startled {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}} may charge at {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} in defense if he is detected. {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}} will not charge at {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is on horseback. {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} chase after {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}} that wander near their camps, occasionally killing and eating them. {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}} are notably the only large animals found on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}, being most populous in the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits|link}}.

==Trivia==
* Both {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}} and {{Plural|BotW|Red-Tusked Boar|link}} closely resemble {{wp|wild boar}}s, a species of large, tusked pigs native to the forests of {{wp|Eurasia}} and parts of {{wp|North Africa}}.
