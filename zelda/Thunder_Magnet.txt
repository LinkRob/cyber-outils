"{{Term/Store|Thunder Magnet||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Thunder Magnet}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Lakeside Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can meet {{Term|BotW|Cima|link}}, who works at the location. She complains that {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} strikes the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} so often that she now becomes nervous whenever it starts to {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh dear, oh dear... {{Color|BotW Red|Lightning}} strike this stable so often, I get nervous whenever it rains even a little.|Cima|BotW}}</ref> Since she does not understand why this happens, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to figure out the source of the issue for her.<ref>{{Cite|I just don't understand why it strikes so often... Hey, it's a long shot, but if you have some time, could you find out why?|Cima|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Cima}} will tell him that she is counting on him,<ref>{{Cite|You will?! Really? I'm so happy! I'm counting on you.|Cima|BotW}}</ref> which begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

On top of {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It turns out the {{Color|BotW Red|lightning}} was drawn to Lakeside Stable because of a metal weapon stuck on top of the building!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> As it is made of metal, it attracts {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} during thunderstorms. After removing the {{Term|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe|display=Axe}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must then inform {{Term|BotW|Cima}} of his findings.<ref>{{Cite|Head back to Cima, and let her know what was going on.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

Back on the ground, {{Term|BotW|Cima}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he found out what was attracting {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} to the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|What? You found out what was drawing the {{Color|BotW Red|lightning}} here?|Cima|BotW}}</ref> When she hears about the {{Term|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe}}, she remarks that it makes sense that it would be the cause.<ref>{{Cite|[It was a metal axe.] Aha! Yeah, that's as good as a lightning rod, isn't it?|Cima|BotW}}</ref> She also wonders if it was put on top of the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} on purpose.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder if someone put it up there on purpose. How strange...|Cima|BotW}}</ref> To thank {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cima}} gives him the {{Term|BotW|Rubber Helm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for looking into it for me! Here's something for your trouble.|Cima|BotW}}</ref> Now that {{Term|BotW|Cima}} can relax,<ref>{{Cite|I can finally relax!|Cima|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
