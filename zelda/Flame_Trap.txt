{{Term/Store|Flame Trap|Flame Traps|OoS, OoA|plural}}{{Name Ref|OoS, OoA}} are recurring traps in {{TLoZ|series}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}===
{{Plural|OoS|Flame Trap}} appear in side-scrolling areas, often near the same areas that contain {{Term|OoS|Podoboo|link}} and near {{Term|OoS|Lava|link}}. Unlike {{Term|OoS|Podoboo}}, these fireballs fly through the air and spin in huge circles, similar to how {{Plural|OoS|Blade Trap|link}} act. They move extremely fast and through all obstacles, and cannot be stopped. {{Term|OoS|Link}} is forced to simply wait and time his jumps between platforms very carefully to avoid the swooping balls of fire.
