'''Gerudo Pot''' is an Object and [[Echo]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Gerudo Pot|A fine pot with vibrant colors to catch the eye. It is typical in Gerudo culture and has been for ages.<ref name="Hyrule Castle Pot">{{Cite|A fine pot with vibrant colors to catch the eye. It is typical in Gerudo culture and has been for ages.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Gerudo Pots are objects primarily located within [[Gerudo Town]] and the [[Gerudo Desert]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the [[Echo]] after using the [[Tri Rod]] on one, allowing her to summon pots as needed. She can hide inside them to sneak past enemies or throw them to create distractions.
