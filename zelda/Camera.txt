{{Term/Store|Camera|Cameras|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Camera}}|Sheikah Slate|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, the {{Term|TFH|Camera}} is given to {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} by the {{Term|TFH|Photo Bro|link}} at the {{Term|TFH|Miiverse Gallery|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Camera}} is used to take screenshots of {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}} by pressing the 'X' button, which is then saved to the {{Term|TFH|Miiverse Gallery}}'s photo album after the {{Term|TFH|Level}}'s completion. Up to 99 photos can be saved to the photo album; the photos can be posted onto [[Miiverse]] from the {{Term|TFH|Miiverse Gallery}},{{Note|The Miiverse service was discontinued on November 7, 2017.}} and unwanted photos can be deleted from the photo album. The {{Term|TFH|Camera}} cannot be used outside of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}} or in [[Download Play]].<ref>{{Cite|In the full version, photos can be freely taken within levels.|N/A|TFH}}</ref>

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, the Camera appears as a [[Rune]] on the [[Sheikah Slate]]. It is an ancient piece of [[Sheikah]] technology.<ref>{{Cite|A Camera is a type of ancient Sheikah technology that allows you to capture the image of a person or object.|Camera Manual|BotW}}</ref> It is obtained in the [[Hateno Ancient Tech Lab]] from [[Purah]] after bringing [[Blue Flame]] to the [[Ancient Furnace]] outside of the Lab. Link must place the Sheikah Slate into the [[Guidance Stone]] in the Lab to repair the Slate and restore missing files. Doing so will add the Camera Rune, the Album, and the [[Hyrule Compendium]] to the Sheikah Slate.<ref>{{Cite|Camera, Hyrule Compendium, and album missing files confirmed. Starting repair.|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> With the Hyrule Compendium, Link also receives the [[Sheikah Sensor +]]. After obtaining the Camera Rune, Purah asks Link to try it out by taking a Picture of her.<ref>{{Cite|Take a {{Color|BotW Red|picture}} of me with the {{Color|BotW Red|Camera}}! Oh, and don't forget to lemme see it!|Purah|BotW}}</ref>

The Camera Rune allows [[Link]] to take a Picture by pressing the 'A' button, which is saved to the Album.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Pictures}} you take with the {{Color|BotW Blue|Camera}} are stored in the {{Color|BotW Blue|album}}.|Symin|BotW}}</ref> Link can aim the Camera with either the right analog stick or the gyroscope. Link can zoom in and out by pressing up and down on the D-pad. Link can also quickly access the Album by pressing the minus button and take a Self-Portrait by pressing the 'X' button. While taking a Self-Portrait, Link 
can position himself in four different poses by using the left analog stick. 

If Link takes a Picture of certain creatures, monsters, [[Material]]s, [[Treasure Chest]]s, Ore Deposits, or pieces of equipment, it will be logged in the Hyrule Compendium.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Pictures}} of animals and objects will be automatically registered in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Compendium}}.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> After being logged, Link can view information about the subject in the Compendium or track it with the Sheikah Sensor +.

The Camera's Album comes with twelve Old Landscape Photos that were taken by [[Princess Zelda]] 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, there are also some {{Color|BotW Red|old landscape photos}} stored in the {{Color|BotW Blue|album}}. I knew Princess Zelda had made frequent use of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Camera}} feature, but this...|Purah|BotW}}</ref> After obtaining the Camera Rune and speaking to [[Impa]], she will instruct Link to go to each location in all of Zelda's Pictures to recover his [[Recovered Memory|Memories]].<ref>{{Cite|Princess Zelda's memories are your memories as well. Visit the locations chronicled by the {{Color|BotW Red|Camera}}, and you may be able to recover bits of your memory.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> By visiting these locations, Link will experience a flashback of a Memory with Zelda where the Picture was taken.

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
The Camera Rune is featured as a crucial part of Zelda's Special Attack when she is using her starting weapon class, which is Sheikah Runes.
{{Noncanon|End}}
