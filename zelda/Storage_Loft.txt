The {{Term/Store|Storage Loft||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Uh, yeah, it should be in our {{Color|TPHD Red|storage loft}}.|Jaggle|TPHD}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TPHD|Storage Loft}} is located in {{Term|TPHD|Jaggle|link}}'s home in {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}}. After the children of the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|display=Village}} are kidnapped by {{Term|TPHD|King Bulblin|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Jaggle}} keeps the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Shield|link}} inside. With {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}}'s help, {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} is able to get inside the house and knock the {{Term|TPHD|Shield|link}} loose by dashing into the wall.
