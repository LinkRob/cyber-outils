'''Farore''' is the name of two different people in the ''Zelda'' universe:

*'''[[Farore (Goddess)]]''', the goddess of courage who helped create the land of Hyrule, as revealed in {{OoT}}.
*'''[[Farore (Oracle)]]''', the Oracle of Secrets who appeared in both {{OoA}} and {{OoS}} on the [[Game Boy Color]]. She also makes a cameo appearance in {{TMC}}.
