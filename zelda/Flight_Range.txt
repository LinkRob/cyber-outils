The {{Term/Store|Flight Range||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|'''Flight Range'''|Map|BotW}}</ref> also known as the '''Rito Flight Range''',<ref>{{Cite|Rito Flight Range {{Icon|BotW Arrow Right}}|Sign|BotW}}</ref> is a location in {{BotW}}.

==Features and Overview==
The Flight Range is an area at the end of [[Dronoc's Pass]] in the [[Hebra Mountains]]. [[Rito]] warriors use the Flight Range to prepare for aerial battles due to its constant [[Updraft]].<ref name="Prepare">{{Cite|[Where did he go?] Well... My husband headed to a place called the {{Color|BotW Red|Flight Range}}. It's in {{Color|BotW Blue|Dronoc's Pass}} at the base of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}}. It's a place where Rito warriors prepare for aerial combat... I imagine he's gone there to gather weapons for another run at {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}. As it happens, he made his way to the {{Color|BotW Red|Flight Range}} on foot this time.|Saki|BotW}}</ref> As such, [[Link]] can find a number of [[Target]]s throughout the Flight Range and weaponry in the small hut on the eastern side of the Range.

According to [[The Diary of Revali]], when the previous elder asked Revali what he wanted for a prize in honor of his accomplishments, Revali requested an archery training ground to be constructed so that not only can he further his skills to new heights,<ref>{{Cite|The elder asked me what I would like as a prize for my achievements. I told him I wish for an archery training ground.|The Diary of Revali|BotW}}</ref> but he can have the time to invent a new move that would take it up to a new level.<ref>{{Cite|If I am to keep this up, I need somewhere to train at various elevations. I'm hoping to master a new move I've dreamed up...|The Diary of Revali|BotW}}</ref> When the Rito children expressed interest in training there too,<ref>{{Cite|The Rito children look up to me. I hear they wish to start training at the Flight Range too.|The Diary of Revali|BotW}}</ref> Revali eventually permits the public to use it as the times aren't always safe.<ref>{{Cite|Perhaps it's not a bad idea to allow everyone to use my training space. These are dangerous times, after all.|The Diary of Revali|BotW}}</ref> In short, Revali was the reason why the Flight Range existed.

Prior to the second [[Great Calamity]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] visited the Flight Range in search of [[Revali]], seeking to appoint him as the Rito [[Champion]] and pilot of [[Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Dungeon)|Divine Beast Vah Medoh]]. Revali was using the Flight Range to practice and master his special ability, [[Revali's Gale]]. Link can receive a vision of this in the form of a [[Memory]], "[[Champion Revali's Song (Memory)|Champion Revali's Song]]", by listening to [[Kass]]'s eponymous [[Champion Revali's Song (Song)|Song]] in [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC Pack.

The Rito warrior, [[Teba]], retreats to the Flight Range after being wounded by {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}. There, he prepares a counteroffensive against the [[Divine Beast]]. After speaking to [[Kaneli]] and [[Saki]], Teba can be found altering a [[Falcon Bow]] on the landing connected to the Flight Range's small hut. If Link speaks to him and offers to help him quell Vah Medoh, Teba will force Link to undergo a test to see his aerial combat skills before agreeing to join forces. If Link accepts, he must destroy five Targets within three minutes. Upon successfully completing Teba's test, Teba will agree to let Link accompany him on his next attack on Vah Medoh.<ref>{{Cite|You're...a skilled archer. It was as if time stopped with every arrow you let fly. I've got to tell you, Link... When you first showed up, I thought someone was pulling a prank on me. But after seeing you handle that bow, I can tell you're the real deal. You must have seen a battle or two.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> To help Link, Teba directs him to a stone [[Treasure Chest]] on the landing, which contains the Falcon Bow that Teba was modifying moments before.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if you really want to help me bring down {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}, it's now or never. I'll get you into position so you can give its {{Color|BotW Red|cannons}} all you've got. Inside that treasure chest is a {{Color|BotW Blue|bow}} I've modified. Take it, and let me know when you're ready.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> Teba also gifts Link 20 [[Bomb Arrow]]s to destroy Vah Medoh's [[Cannon]]s and dispel [[Medoh's Barrier]].<ref>{{Cite|OK, it's time to face {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}. Normal arrows won't put a dent in those {{Color|BotW Red|cannons}}. You'll need to hit them with {{Color|BotW Blue|bomb arrows}}. These are a precious commodity, so don't expect me to do this again, but...take these {{Color|BotW Blue|bomb arrows}}.|Teba|BotW}}</ref>

The Flight Range is the location that Teba takes Link if he chooses to retreat during their combined assault on Vah Medoh's Cannons. Link is also [[Warping|Warp]]ed to the Flight Range when exiting Vah Medoh. After successfully destroying Vah Medoh's Barrier, Teba will recuperate near the Cooking Pot in the Flight Range, cursing his inability to finish Vah Medoh himself.<ref>{{Cite|You... I can't believe it got me... My leg is in worse shape than I thought. And I was so close to stopping {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}... Dammit!|Teba|BotW}}</ref>  

After saving Vah Medoh from [[Windblight Ganon]], Link can speak to Teba in [[Rito Village]] to discover that Teba plans on taking his son, [[Tulin]], to the Flight Range now that his wounds have healed.<ref>{{Cite|My wounds are just about healed, so I guess it's time to take Tulin to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Flight Range}}. I'm hoping Tulin grows up to be a distinguished warrior like Master Revali.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> At the Flight Range, Teba will ask Link to show Tulin how he handles a [[Bow#Breath of the Wild|Bow]].<ref>{{Cite|It's you... Haven't seen you in a while. Now that {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}'s calmed down, I've started training Tulin again. If you have the time, would you mind showing Tulin how you handle a bow?|Teba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Show Tulin how you handle a bow. It could set a good example for him.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> If Link speaks to Tulin at the Flight Range, Tulin will ask Link to show his skill.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Hey, Link! [Hey, Tulin!] You're Link, right? My dad told me about you! Also, he said you're REAL good with a bow! Is that true?|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> If Link agrees, Tulin will ask Link to break as many Targets as he can in the time limit,<ref>{{Cite|[Of course!] Wow! That's so neat! You gotta show me!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[I'm all right.] Nuh-uh! Dad said you were great, and Dad never lies, so it must be true! Come on, show me!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll count down, and you'll break all the {{Color|BotW Red|targets}} before time's up! When you break a bunch, I'll give you a prize!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> beginning the [[Flight Range (Mini-Game)|Flight Range]] Mini-Game.

During the "[[EX Champion Revali's Song]]" Main Quest, the Targets in the Flight Range become modified and bear light blue rings with a [[Sheikah Eye]] symbol. In order to complete the Quest, Link must destroy four of them within a small timeframe to make the [[Noe Rajee Shrine]] appear at the bottom of the Flight Range.<ref>{{Cite|The scattered One-Hit Obliterator led you to a location where a stone monument with a map of trials appeared. Kass's song seems related...<br/><br/>One, {{Color|BotW Red|shoot the flame dragon's horn}},<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|race down a peak rings adorn}},<br/>Three, {{Color|BotW Red|shoot four targets to win}},<br/>Champion, the trials begin!<br/><br/>You still have {{Color|BotW Red|three}} trials to complete.|EX Champion Revali's Song|BotW}}</ref>

A [[Korok]] can be found by climbing to the top of the hut and examining the shimmering leaves. He gives Link a [[Korok Seed]] for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref> There are two metallic Treasure Chests at the base of the Flight Range, both of which can be pulled from underwater with [[Magnesis]]. One contains a [[Knight's Broadsword]], and a [[Gold Rupee]] can be found in the other.
