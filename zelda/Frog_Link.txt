{{Term/Store|Frog Link||Series}} is one of [[Link]]'s transformations, featured in the "[[Kiss'n Tell]]" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}.{{Fact}} After Link defeats a [[Gleeok]] that was harrassing a maiden, the girl rewards Link with a kiss; however, as their lips meet, Link turns into a frog and the maiden turns out to be a [[Gibdo]] sent by [[Ganon]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=What's the matter, Link? You don't like my charms anymore? You fell for it! Ganon's plan worked perfectly!|name=Gibdo|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> As Link is about to reach for his sword, he realizes that his hands have turned frog-like and is too distracted to see that the Gibdo is about to attack him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=My hands! They're all weird!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Fortunately, Zelda defeats the foe and then takes Link to look into a pond for him to realize his frog form. He starts to cry and Zelda hugs him, saying she will be his friend no matter what.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I've seen worse! Hey, you still have me. I'll always be your friend.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> He is then attacked by Ganon, who kidnaps the princess and takes her into the [[Underworld]] while Link is unconscious.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Of course not! You, take care of him! And you, my dear, are coming with me!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref>

When Link wakes up, he goes to the castle and asks the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] for advice on how to return to his human form. The Triforce then tells him to look for the [[Witch of Walls]] in an endless wall by an ion tree.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=When one's in need of sage advice, one must seek out the one who's wise. The witch of walls will help you best. To find her you must make a quest. Seek the wall that has no end, beneath the bow that cannot bend.|name=Triforce of Wisdom|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Eventually, he and [[Spryte]] find the witch, who tells the young hero that a princess must kiss him by her own free will in order for him to be human again.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The cure is simple. You must be kissed by a princess of her own free will.|name=Witch of Walls|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Following the witch's advice, Link goes to the Underworld and finds Zelda, telling her about the cure that will free him of his frog self.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=You have to kiss me. Please? A princess's kiss is the only cure.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref>  Just when Zelda is about to kiss him, Ganon zaps Link and knocks him unconscious again, and Spryte, feeling sorry for the young hero, gives him a kiss on the cheek.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, Link, you poor thing. You've had such a hard day.|name=Spryte|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> Spryte's kiss causes Link to transform back into a human, and it is later explained that since Spryte is the daughter of the [[King Oberon|King of Fairies]], that naturally makes her a princess.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Naturally. My father was the king of the fairies, you know. That makes ME the princess.|name=Spryte|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref> 

As a frog, Link has a tendency to immediately eat any insect that is near him, much to Spryte's dismay.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ah! Stop that! Making me nervous.|name=Spryte|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=4}}</ref>
