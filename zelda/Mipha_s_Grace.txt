{{Term/Store|Mipha's Grace||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Mipha's Grace}}<br/>A restorative power born from the spirit of the Champion Mipha. When your hearts run out, you'll automatically be resurrected with full health plus temporary bonus hearts.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> also known as the '''Power of Mipha''',<ref>{{Cite|So, you've gained the {{Color|BotW Red|Power of Mipha}}.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> and '''Mipha's Power''',<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Mipha's power}} is that of healing. It will aid you when your strength has been exhausted.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> is a Key Item and an ability in {{BotW}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} receives Mipha's Grace from the spirit of the [[Zora]] {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} after defeating [[Waterblight Ganon]] and calming the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}. Mipha's Grace protects {{Term|BotW|Link}} from death and recovers his health, activating only when he loses all of his hearts similarly to using a fairy. The blessing will heal him back to full health and also grant him five extra temporary hearts. It takes priority over any fairies that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has in his inventory.
[[File:BotW Mipha's Grace.png|left|thumb]]
Mipha's Grace can only be used once before recharging and cannot be used again for 24 real-time minutes. While in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, Mipha's Grace instead takes eight minutes to recharge. After being upgraded to Mipha's Grace +, its normal recharge time is reduced to eight minutes and its recharge time inside {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} is reduced to two minutes and forty seconds. Like all {{Term|BotW|Champions}} blessings in {{BotW|-}}, it can be disabled from the Key Items inventory.

Mipha is seen using this ability in Link's tenth [[Recovered Memory]] "[[Mipha's Touch]]" where she is sitting beside Link, tending to a wound on his arm that he received in one of his battles.
