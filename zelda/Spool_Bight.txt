==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Spool Bight|link}} is a shallow bay located in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Sea|link}}.

==Trivia==
* In geography a bight refers to a bend in the coastline<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= In geography, a bight is a bend or curve in a coastline, river, or other geographical feature |url= https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bight_(geography) |title=Bight |site= Wikipedia}}</ref>. The {{Term|BotW|Spool Bight}} resembles the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Australian_Bight Great Australian Bight] because of its sheer cliff-face.
==Nomenclature==
Spool Bight derives its name from {{Term|OoS|Spool Swamp|link}}.
