The {{Term/Store|Isle of Ember||Series, PH}} is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 113}}</ref>

==Features==
The {{Term|PH|Isle of Ember}} is the {{Term|PH|Island|link}} where the first {{Term|PH|Temple}}, {{Term|PH|Temple of Fire|link}}, is located. It also has a large volcano, which erupts when {{Term|PH|Link|link}} is traveling up the mountain to the {{Term|PH|Temple of Fire|display=Temple}}.

The {{Term|PH|Island}} is the home of the {{Term|PH|Fortune Teller|link}}, {{Term|PH|Astrid|link}}. {{Term|PH|Astrid}}, upon seeing the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}} dock at the {{Term|PH|Island}}, took refuge in the basement of her home and became trapped inside. {{Term|PH|Link|link}} must shout through the door to find out that it can be only unlocked by finding the whereabouts of the three {{Plural|PH|Torch|link}} located around the {{Term|PH|Island}}. Only her assistant, {{Term|PH|Kayo|link}}, knows where they are. {{Term|PH|Kayo}} is unfortunately dead, but, once his grave is found, he will tell the hero the locations of the {{Plural|PH|Torch}}.

After access to the {{Term|PH|Dungeon}} is revealed, {{Term|PH|Link}} must navigate around the volcano and walk close to its crater. While at the same time, he must dodge the native {{Plural|PH|Tektite}} and the {{Plural|PH|Magma Bomb|link}} that fall from the sky after being expulsed by the volcanic eruptions. At the end, he finds the {{Term|PH|Temple of Fire}}, which holds the {{Term|PH|Boomerang|link}} and the {{Term|PH|Leaf|display=Spirit of Power}} known as {{Term|PH|Leaf|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Look, Link! It's the Spirit of Power, Leaf! This must all be what Astrid was talking about.|Ciela|PH}}</ref>

{{Term|PH|Ojibe|link}} of {{Term|PH|Mercay Island|link}} claims that the {{Term|PH|Isle of Ember}} is revered as a place of prophetic power.<ref>{{Cite|You have? Impressive! You know, that island she's said to live on... We all revere it as a place of prophetic power! I'd sail there too, but not with so many monsters lurking on the seas!|Ojibe|PH}}</ref>
