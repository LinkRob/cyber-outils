The '''Golden Boots''' are an item in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Golden Boots are found in the [[Vendoss]] swamp. They are used to enter [[Sirram Bew]]'s domed house, although the player does not have to select the Golden Boots from the [[inventory]] to use them.
