The {{Term/Store|Gift of Time||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The [[Gift of Time]] is the reward for completing the [[Snake's Remains]], and is guarded by a [[Dodongo]]. It is the second of the eight [[Essence of Nature|Essences of Nature]] collected by Link. Its description text reads: "Seeds sprout as seasons change with the Gift of Time!"<ref>"Seeds sprout as seasons change with the [[Gift of Time]]!" – N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]])</ref>
