The Long Stick is a weapon in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom''.]] It appears to be a variation of the [[stick]], just with a wider range.

Outside of settings, it's a weapon that replaces [[Boko Spear]] and [[Moblin Spear]] and it's divided into a version that can be obtained from the ground and a version that can be obtained from [[Sky Islands]], but only in color and no difference in performance

==Appearances==

===''Tears of the Kingdom''===
The Long Stick made its debut in this game, it can evidently be obtained by chopping down trees, same as the [[stick]]. It can be [[Fuse|fused]] with other [[Weapons in Tears of the Kingdom|weapons]] to give it a wider range and better attack power.
