"{{Term/Store|The Hero's Sword||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Hero's Sword}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Korok Forest}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After traversing the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will arrive in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}. There he will find a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|link|display=pedestal}}, upon which rests the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}. If he attempts to pull the sword, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} saying he is the final hope of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link|display=Hyrule}}, overlaid with flashbacks from several {{Plural|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link... You are our final hope. The fate of Hyrule rests with you.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} draws back from the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, which awakens {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Who is that...? Did I doze off again?|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> He recognizes {{Term|BotW|Link}} and admonishes him for taking so long to return.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm? Well, well...it's you. You finally decided to return. Better late than never.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> After a century, his patience has been tested by waiting for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|After 100 years, I'd nearly given up hope on seeing you again. Even my patience has limits, you know...|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not remember him.<ref>{{Cite|That look on your face tells me that you have no recollection of me, however.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he has watched over {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}} for a very long time,<ref>{{Cite|I have watched over Hyrule since time immemorial. Many have referred to me over the ages as the Deku Tree.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> and that the {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in front of him is the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, crafted by the {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link|display=Goddess Hylia}} that only the chosen knight can wield against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That is the weapon created by the ancient Goddess. The sword that seals the darkness—that only the chosen knight can wield against the Calamity Ganon.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> 100 years ago, {{Term|BotW|Link}} himself carried that {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|display=Sword}},<ref>{{Cite|Believe it or not...it was actually you who wielded that sword 100 years ago.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> though in order to wield it again, the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} will test him.<ref>{{Cite|But I must warn you to take extreme caution. The sword stands as a test to anyone who would dare attempt to possess it.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref>

In {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s current state, {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} is unsure whether or not he is worthy of the blade,<ref>{{Cite|As you are now, I cannot say whether you are worthy or not...|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> and if he were to try to draw it in a weakened state, he would lose his life.<ref>{{Cite|If you sought to free the sword in any sort of weakened state, you would surely lose your life where you stand.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> He wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} luck.<ref>{{Cite|Best of luck, young one...|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> To pull the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} says {{Term|BotW|Link}} will need to use his true strength,<ref>{{Cite|If you hope to pull the sword from its resting place, you must use your true strength.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> and so he challenges {{Term|BotW|Link}} to test his worth.<ref>{{Cite|Let us see whether or not you currently possess said power.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} begins.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have enough {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to pull the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} will stop him once he only has a quarter of a {{Term|BotW|Heart}} left,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Enough.}}|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> as continuing any further would have killed him.<ref>{{Cite|You would have lost your life if you hadn't released your grip on the sword...|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to prepare before his next attempt, since he will not have him stop next time.<ref>{{Cite|Prepare before you try again...for I will not stay your hand partway through next time.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> True to his word, the next time {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to pull the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} without enough {{Plural|BotW|Heart}}, he will receive a {{Term|BotW|Game Over|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} needs at least 13 {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} in order to successfuly draw the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}. Once he does so, he raises it towards the sky, which begins "{{Term|BotW|The Master Sword|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory}}. After {{Term|BotW|Link}} practices swinging the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|display=Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} explains that the {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}} he witnessed ocurred in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}} 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|What you just saw happened where you stand 100 years ago...|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} decided to bring the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} to the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|display=Forest}} to keep it safe after {{Term|BotW|Link}} collapsed.<ref>{{Cite|After you were separated from the sword, the princess thought to bring it here, where she knew that it would be safe under my watch.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> Now, she is trapped fighting {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} inside {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}},<ref>{{Cite|She continues to fight, trapped deep within the confines of Hyrule Castle.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> hoping for {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s return.<ref>{{Cite|Her heart cascades with faith that you will return.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} likens her smile to the sun, and he would like to see it once again.<ref>{{Cite|She has a smile like the sun... I would do much to feel its warmth upon me once again.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} explains that the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} will glow with a holy light when used against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} or his {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That blade has a name... It is the {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Sword}}. When used against {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}} or those tainted by his Malice, it will become suffused with holy light...|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> Though this is the true power of the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|display=Sword}}, it can become exhausted from extended use.<ref>{{Cite|At such times, the true power of the sword will manifest...but be warned. Do not rely too much on its power. If you wield it without need, its power will be exhausted and it will no longer be of use to you.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> In this scenario, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have to wait for the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} to recover.<ref>{{Cite|...Should this happen, you must wait for the sword to recover.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} with care and wield it bravely,<ref>{{Cite|It was your partner a century ago... Use it with care... And wield it bravely for the one who waits for you...|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> which completes the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}.
