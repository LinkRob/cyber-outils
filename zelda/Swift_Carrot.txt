==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Swift Carrots are one of three foodstuffs favored by the Sheikah race, with the others being [[Fortified Pumpkin]]s and [[Mighty Bananas]]. They are primarily and exclusively cultivated by the [[Sheikah]] farmer [[Steen]], who grows 14 in his field in [[Kakariko Village]], and has 12 stocked at his wife {{Term|BotW|Trissa|link}}'s [[High Spirits Produce|shop]], suggesting he keeps two of each batch to eat himself. They may also be found at the foot of [[Satori Mountain]], or behind the {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} at the [[Highland Stable]]. [[Beedle]] sells them at the [[East Akkala Stable]], as will the [[Merchant]] [[Giro]]. Some may also be purchased from [[Cambo]] as he leaves Kakariko Village, implying he bought some from Trissa, however this does not reduce her stock.

Eating Swift Carrots raw will heal {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} for half of a {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. Swift Carrots can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to create Orange [[Dye]]. Burning a Swift Carrot with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} will turn it into a {{Term|BotW|Roasted Swift Carrot|link}}. When cooked in a dish, the resulting food provides Link with temporarily superior speed.<ref name="Inventory"></ref>

The [[Side Quest]] "[[Koko's Kitchen]]" requires Link to gather a Swift Carrot for [[Koko]] so she can make hasty [[Veggie Cream Soup]].<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|...OH, NO!}} Koko is all out of {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrots}}! But...Koko promised to make Cottla some {{Color|BotW Blue|hasty veggie cream soup}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[I'll bring you some.] Thank you! So much!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> As a reward for delivery, Koko will give Link some of the soup she makes.<ref>{{Cite|Ooooh, it came out perfect! Here, please have some! Koko's treat!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

When cooking in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, either a Swift Carrot, [[Endura Carrot]], or a [[Fortified Pumpkin]] is needed to make Veggie Cream Soup, [[Vegetable Risotto]], and [[Vegetable Curry]].

According to {{Term|BotW|Steen}}'s grandfather, {{Term|BotW|Link}} exclusively ate and trained with {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot}}.<ref>{{Cite|You see, the thing about that swordsman is... He trained with {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrots}} instead of a blade! He strengthened his body with a diet made entirely of {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrots}}! And he tempered his soul with the sweet aroma of {{Color|BotW Blue|swift carrots}} too! That's what my grandfather always used to say, anyway...|Steen|BotW}}</ref>

Ten Swift Carrots are needed to upgrade both the [[Sand Boots]] and [[Snow Boots]] to their second tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Sand Boots''': 10 (0, 10, 0, 0)

* Total for '''Snow Boots''': 10 (0, 10, 0, 0)
