{{Term/Store|Ha Dahamar Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ha Dahamar Shrine}}<br/>The Water Guides}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''The Water Guides''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Ha Dahamar Shrine}} is located in a small pool beside the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}} at the east end of the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by the {{Term|BotW|Ha Dahamar Shrine|display=Shrine}}'s {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ha Dahamar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ha Dahamar. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Ha Dahamar|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Ha Dahamar Shrine}} features several waterfalls, which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} to bypass. Further inside, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} rolls along a wall connected to a {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}}. In order to reach {{Term|BotW|Ha Dahamar}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} to direct the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} along the features of the wall, similar to a pinball machine, into a {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|link}}. This releases the portcullis separating him from {{Term|BotW|Ha Dahamar}}.

When Link reaches the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, {{Term|BotW|Ha Dahamar}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ha Dahamar|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Ha Dahamar}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ha Dahamar|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} located on a ledge between the first two {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}, valued at 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.
