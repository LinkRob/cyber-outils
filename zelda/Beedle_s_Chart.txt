{{Term/Store|Beedle's Chart||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an item in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 116}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub}}
To unlock Beedle's Chart, progress through the story until Bombs have been obtained from Windfall Island (specifically the Pirate Ship sequence). As soon as you have them, Beedle will send a letter in the mail with his chart attached. The map marks all the locations of his shop shipsallowing allows {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} to locate him on the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}.
