==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Raw Whole Bird}} are dropped when {{Plural|BotW|White Pigeon|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Eldin Ostrich|link}} are killed. Eating a Raw Whole Bird will heal three {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. Raw Whole Birds can be burned with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Whole Bird|link}} or chilled with [[Ice]] to make {{Plural|BotW|Frozen Whole Bird|link}}.

A Raw Whole Bird is required to make [[Gourmet Poultry Curry]] and [[Gourmet Poultry Pilaf]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Either a Raw Whole Bird or a [[Raw Gourmet Meat]] is needed to make [[Gourmet Meat Stew]], [[Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl]], [[Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat]], and [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]]. They're one of the types of meat that can be used to make [[Creamy Meat Soup]], [[Glazed Meat]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Meat Pie]], a [[Meat Skewer]], [[Copious Meat Skewers]], [[Meaty Rice Balls]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Steak]], [[Steamed Meat]], and a [[Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin]].
