{{Term/Store|Mia||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a beige [[Remlit]] belonging to [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]] and [[Gaepora]] in {{SS}}.

==Biography==
Mia has a habit of scampering off. [[Link]] can help [[Horwell]] find Mia at the beginning of the game.<ref name="scamp"/><ref>{{Cite|Will you help me out and go retrieve Mia for me?|Horwell|SS}}</ref> During the night, when Link chases [[Fi]], she also becomes aggressive.<ref>{{Cite|Even the headmaster's darling Mia turns quite feral when night falls...|Owlan|SS}}</ref> Horwell really enjoys watching Mia and keeps a scratching post set in his room for Mia to play with while he is watching her.<ref>{{Cite|She's so adorable that I'm starting to think I need one of my own!|Horwell|SS}}</ref>
