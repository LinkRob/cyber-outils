{{Term/Store|Swiftblade I||Series, TMC}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref> also known as '''Swiftblade the First''',<ref>{{Cite|There are nine of us, not counting the ghost of Swiftblade the First.|Swordsman Newsletter 3|TMC}}</ref> is a character in {{TMC}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Swiftblade I}} is the eldest of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}, and "lives" under his grave on the southwest side of {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}, which {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can push upwards to access. Despite being a member, he is not, in fact, a brother to any of the others. He teaches the {{Term|TMC|Great Spin Attack|link}}, which he claims only he has mastered.<ref>{{Cite|This is a technique that only I have mastered... Only one who holds the sacred blade can perform...the {{Color|TMC Red|Great Spin Attack!}}|Swiftblade I|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade I}} will not train {{Term|TMC|Link}} until he obtains the first seven {{Plural|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It seems you are not yet ready to train with me! Come back when you have {{Color|TMC Red|all 7 Tiger Scrolls}}!|Swiftblade I|TMC}}</ref> There is a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} inside his dojo.

According to {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade I}} spends his free time battling {{Plural|TMC|Cow|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I went to {{Color|TMC Green|Lon Lon Ranch}} the other day. I saw the ghost of Swiftblade the First defeat a cow with his bare hands! It was kind of sad, really...|Swordsman Newsletter 4|TMC}}</ref>
