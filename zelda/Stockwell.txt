{{Term/Store|Stockwell||Series, OoS, OoA, TMC}} is a recurring character in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}} & 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 207 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> He is a shopkeeper and proprietor.

==Biography==
==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, Stockwell owns a shop the [[Lynna City Shop]] in [[Labrynna]] and the [[Horon Village Shop]] in [[Holodrum]]. He also operates the [[Advance Shop]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, Stockwell owns a shop the [[Lynna City Shop]] in [[Labrynna]] and the [[Horon Village Shop]] in [[Holodrum]]. He also operates the [[Advance Shop]].
