'''Bollas''' are an enemy in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Bollas are orange, monkey-like creatures. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] only ever encounters two of them, both in a room of the [[Shrine of Fire]]. Bollas attack by throwing [[fireball]]s. Zelda has to defeat both Bollas in order to progress onward to [[Maxus]].
