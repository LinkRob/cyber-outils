{{Term/Store|Bird's Peak Rock||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (E7) is an island located on the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features==
{{Fishman|Tell me, small fry, have you ever heard of the golden Triumph Forks? They're there, on that island... Or, at least, a chart leading to them is there. Where's it hidden, you ask? Well, for that answer you'll have to ask a gull.}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= <p>Bird's Peak Rock</p><p>That place is famous for the many birds that live there. [[Aryll]] would like it. She was always surrounded by [[seagull]]s, wasn't she?</p>
}}

Bird's Peak Rock is named after the large number of [[Kargaroc]]s and [[seagull]]s that nest at its peaks. It is comprised of two parts; the first is a small grassy area with a secret cave on it, while the second island is impossible to walk on, but there are switches on the nests at its peaks. By using a [[Hyoi Pear]] to control a seagull, the seagull can hit the switches, allowing [[Link]] to receive a [[Charts|chart]] to one of the [[Triforce#Triforce Shards|Triforce Shards]].

A [[Blue ChuChu]] can also be found on the smaller of the two islands.
