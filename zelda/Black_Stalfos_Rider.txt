{{Term/Store|Black Stalfos Rider|Black Stalfos Riders|Series, CoH}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Black Stalfos Rider|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Black Stalfos Rider}} are a special variety of {{Plural|CoH|Black Stalfos|link}} that pursue {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} while riding on top of skeletal beings. Their steeds allow them to move on every beat of the music, making them faster than a normal {{Term|CoH|Black Stalfos}} until their mount is slain, which will ground the rider and turn him into a normal Black Stalfos. They only appear in caves and dungeons, and are one of the few enemies that can be summoned by a {{Term|CoH|Black Sarcophagus|link}}.

==Trivia==
They behave identically to the Black Skeleton Knight enemies from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
