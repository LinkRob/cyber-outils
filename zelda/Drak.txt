{{Term/Store|Drak||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Drak|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Drak}} is a {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} miner who can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. He carries a {{Term|BotW|Cobble Crusher|link}}, and he noticeably speaks with a slur where his vowels extend.<ref>{{Cite|They call meeeee...{{Color|BotW Blue|Draaaaak}}!|Drak|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Drak}} helps {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} by explaining the area to him. He tells him that the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine}} used to be active,<ref>{{Cite|This is the {{Color|BotW Blue|Abandoned North Mine}}. Oh, and this is the little ditty we all used to siiiiing as we worked: {{Color|BotW Red|Ammo}} goes BOOM 'til {{Color|BotW Blue|ore}} fills the ROOM! Gather said {{Color|BotW Blue|ORE}} for rupees GALORE! Dig all the DAY to get the good PAY!|Drak|BotW}}</ref> until it grew too hot and dangerous due to the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} and the infestation of monsters.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, this is a famous mine, my maaaan. Or it was...until {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}} showed up. Now it's waaaay too hot! There's tons more lava and all kiiiiiinds of monsters out and about. It's so dangerous, we can't dig here anymore!|Drak|BotW}}</ref> Because of that, {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}} had appointed him to keep watch of the area.<ref>{{Cite|This place is daaangerous, so Boss told me not to let anyone through... That means you tooooo, my maaaan.|Drak|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Drak}}, he wonders why so many people are out and about.<ref>{{Cite|Yeesh! What's goin' on today?! So many peeeople out and about!|Drak|BotW}}</ref> He warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} gets hotter past him and there are more monsters.<ref>{{Cite|You should know it gets much hotter past here, and there are also looots of monsters. It's reeeally dangerous!|Drak|BotW}}</ref>

At the beginning of the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Drak}} about {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Drak}} is at first suspicious and asks why a stranger is asking about {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Do you know Yunobo?] Why's a stranger like yoooou askin' about Yunobo?|Drak|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} explains that {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} asked, {{Term|BotW|Drak}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} went into the {{Term|BotW|Vault|link}} to retrieve painkillers, but he has yet to return.<ref>{{Cite|[Boss wants to know.] {{Big|Huuuuh? {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} does?!}} Well... Then I guess I better tell you wheeeeere Yunobo is! Yunobo...went to the {{Color|BotW Blue|vault}} to fetch some painkillers. But now that ya mention it, I suppooooose he never came back.|Drak|BotW}}</ref> Due to its danger, {{Term|BotW|Drak}} suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not go after him.<ref>{{Cite|This place is daaaaangerous, so ya probably shouldn't go lookin' for him.|Drak|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Cannon|link}} closest to them, {{Term|BotW|Drak}} warns him not to touch it.<ref>{{Cite|HEY! Stooop that! That's {{Color|BotW Red|Boss's cannon}}! Don't you daaaare touch that lever!|Drak|BotW}}</ref> He discourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} from putting any explosives inside.<ref>{{Cite|HEEEY! YOOOOU!! Don't mess with {{Color|BotW Red|Boss's cannon}}! You dooon't wanna send a cannonball flyyyin', so don't even thiiiiink about puttin' any explosives inside!|Drak|BotW}}</ref>

Throughout "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania (Quest)}}", {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to keep asking {{Term|BotW|Drak}} where {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} is. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has rescued him from the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine}}, {{Term|BotW|Drak}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} went back to {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yunobo? He went rushin' off to Goron City... I think. Preeeetty sure. All cuz of {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}}... Maaaaybe he went to talk with {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}}?|Drak|BotW}}</ref> He will also inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} of {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}}'s location after he departs for the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Eldin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yunobo went to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Bridge of Eldin}}... I thiiiiink. Probably. To get to {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain Summit}}, just keep going uuuuup this hill aaaaall the way to the top!|Drak|BotW}}</ref>
