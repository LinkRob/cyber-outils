The {{Term/Store|Item Check||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Item Check|Map|SSHD}}</ref> It is one of the many shops in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}} and is run by {{Term|SSHD|Peatrice|link}}.

==Overview==
[[Link]] can store items here that he does not need in his [[Pouch#Skyward Sword|Adventure Pouch]] or otherwise can not carry in the pouch due to its limited space.<ref>{{Cite|Hello...and welcome to the Item Check. Want to put stuff from your pouch into storage? OK. Want to take something out of storage? That's fine. So is there anything in your pouch or in storage that you want to move?|Peatrice|SS}}</ref> The player can store items in whichever of the 60 slots Peatrice offers for storage, or can sort the items already in the storage automatically.<ref>{{Cite|So you want to get your junk in order, is that right? The option you picked lets you clean up and sort the stuff you have in storage.|Peatrice|SS}}</ref>

If Link finds an Adventure Pouch item out in the field, that item will get sent to the Item Check automatically<!--I haven't tested what happens when both the Adventure Pouch and Item Check are full-->. On the other hand, if Link's Adventure Pouch is full when trying to buy a pouch item over at the [[Gear Shop]], [[Rupin]] will not let him; the player will have to store items at the Item Check before being able to buy a pouch item from Rupin in this instance.<ref>{{Cite|It appears your pouch is full. May I suggest you stow some of your belongings at the Item Check?|Rupin|SS}}</ref>

However, the Item Check's storage is also limited; Link can only store 60 items with Peatrice at any one time.<ref>{{Cite|Listen, I'd love to take some things off your hands, but it looks like I'm all full here. Just trade some items or do something to make some room, OK?|Peatrice|SS}}</ref> To free up room, Link will have to sell items out of either the pouch or the storage to Peatrice. Each type Adventure Pouch item is either given a value that it can be sold for, typically at a net loss, or otherwise made unsellable.
