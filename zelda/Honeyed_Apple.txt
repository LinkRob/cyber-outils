{{Term/Store|Honeyed Apple|Honeyed Apples|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Honeyed Apple}}<br/>A juicy sweet-and-sour dish combining newly ripened apples with honey.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Energizing Honeyed Apple}} is a meal that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee Honey|link}} together with {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}}. Their regular version can only be made by adding a fruit or vegetable to the recipe that has a differing effect than the Courser Bee Honey. They heal six {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, but they can also be sold for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. These dishes are noted to be a juicy combination and sweet and sour flavors, since they combine newly ripened Apples with Honey.<ref name="Inventory"/>

In {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, after completing the first three of {{Term|BotW|Koko|link|display=Koko's}} {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, she will decide to make Honeyed Apples for dessert.<ref>{{Cite|What should Koko make for dessert? {{Big|OH!}} Koko will cook a {{Color|BotW Blue|honeyed apple}}!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Her mother loved them, and she notes how they melt in one's mouth.<ref>{{Cite|Mother loved those! It melts in your mouth and is sooo delicious!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> However, when going over the ingredients, she realizes she's missing Courser Bee Honey.<ref>{{Cite|Let's see... the ingredients are... an {{Color|BotW Blue|apple}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|courser bee honey}}... {{Big|Hrm? ...{{Color|BotW Blue|Courser bee honey}}? OH, NO!!}} Koko is all out of {{Color|BotW Blue|courser bee honey}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> This activates the Side Quest "[[Koko's Specialty]]". If Link returns with Courser Bee Honey for Koko, she will be ecstatic and thank Link, asking him to wait while she makes dessert.<ref>{{Cite|Wait... Is this... {{Big|courser bee honey!!}} Are you sure about this? Really? Thank you so much! Koko means it! Just stay right there a second!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> When the Honeyed Apple is made, Koko becomes emotional after remembering how her mother used to always make it for her.<ref>{{Cite|This... This is... Koko...remembers... When Koko had a fever, Mother would always cook this just for Koko. When Mother made her special dessert, she always put some of this in it.|Koko|BotW}}</ref> She thanks Link, rewarding him with a Honeyed Apple.<ref>{{Cite|It's all thanks to you! Please, take this!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

There is one variation of Honeyed Apples:
* '''Energizing Honeyed Apple''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Honeyed Apple}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A juicy sweet-and-sour dish combining newly ripened apples with honey.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using only Apples and Courser Bee Honey. When eaten, they'll instantly refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/> They heal five Hearts and sell for 30 Rupees.
