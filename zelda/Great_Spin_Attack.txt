==Overview==
[[File:TWWHD Great Spin Attack.gif|thumb|280x280px|The Great Spin Attack in action, performed by Link onto Orca in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD|The Wind Waker HD]]'']]
When {{Term|Series|Link|link}} performs a {{Term|Series|Great Spin Attack}}, he spins around 360 degrees with his [[Sword]] many times in rapid motion for five seconds, prolonging the use of a regular Spin Attack and causing significantly more damage onto almost anything in his path. It also leaves {{Term|Series|Link}} invincible to most attacks. However, he is not stationary during this technique and it causes him to become dizzy and vulnerable to enemy attacks for a few seconds after being used, unless he collides with an obstacle during the attack. In {{TWW}} and the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|Game Boy Advance version of {{aLttP|-}}]], this powerful maneuver consumes {{Term|Series|Magic Meter|link|display=Magic}}. A version of this move is first displayed by {{Term|OoA|Ralph|link}} when he is practicing sword fighting with {{Term|OoA|Link}} in the ending credits of {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|author=ZeldaAdventures |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_JgJSkLxSk&t=63 |title=The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages Ending Credits |site=YouTube}}</ref>

A similar attack is also utilized by {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}} when he is fought in the {{Term|TPHD|Palace of Twilight|link}} in {{TP}}; he is also left stunned after prolonged use,<ref>{{Cite Web|author=Hazey A |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pDkuU6lp6fs&t=577 |title=Usurper King ZANT Boss Fight - The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD |site=YouTube}}</ref> although when nearing death he will sometimes randomly alternate between this attack and his double vertical slice attacks.<ref>{{Cite Web|author=Hazey A |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pDkuU6lp6fs&t=633 |title=Usurper King ZANT Boss Fight - The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD |site=YouTube}}</ref> The attack is also performed by {{Term|ALBW|Stalblind|link}} in {{ALBW}}, during the second phase of the battle.<ref>{{Cite Web|author=BossBattleChannel |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8_oBiIp5mac&t=59 |title=The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds Boss 8 - Stalblind |site=YouTube}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* True to its alternate names "Hurricane Spin" or "Hurricane Spinning Attack", in {{TWW}}, the glowing yellowish waves from [[Link]]'s Sword as he performs the upgraded [[Spin Attack]] technique resembles that of a [[wikipedia:Tropical_cyclone|tropical cyclone]], specifically ones in the Southern Hemisphere (hurricanes technically rotate counterclockwise).
* The Great Spin Attack is considered by many among [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] fanbase as arguably Link's most devastating attack move.
* As this move in {{TWW|-}} requires 10 [[Knight's Crest|Knight's Crests]] to learn it, this highly suggests the Great Spin Attack is an attack used by the [[Knight of Hyrule|Knights of Hyrule]].
