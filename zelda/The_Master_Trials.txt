{{Term/Store|''The Master Trials''|''The Master Trials''|Series, BotW}} is the first downloadable content pack for {{BotW}}, released on June 30, 2017. It is available as part of the game's Expansion Pass.

==Overview==
After purchasing the DLC, [[Master Mode]] can be accessed from the Title Screen and Link can also review his journey using [[Hero's Path Mode]] by pressing {{Icon|BotW X}} while the [[Sheikah Slate]]'s Map is open. Ten metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing [[Armor]] appear around [[Hyrule]]. [[Misko's EX Journal]], [[Super Rumor Mill V1]], and [[Super Rumor Mill V2]] also appear at the [[Outpost Ruins]], [[Woodland Stable]], and [[South Akkala Stable]], respectively. Link can unlock six new [[Side Quest]]s after leaving the [[Great Plateau]] and one more is unlocked after obtaining the [[Master Sword]].

===Side Quests===
* [[EX Trial of the Sword]] allows Link to increase the [[Master Sword]]'s power; after the [[Trial of the Sword]], the Sword will permanently retain its powered-up state with 60 Attack and 188 Durability.
* [[EX Strange Mask Rumors]] rewards Link with the [[Korok Mask]].
* [[EX Teleportation Rumors!]] rewards Link with the [[Travel Medallion]].
* [[EX Treasure: Ancient Mask]] rewards Link with {{Term|BotW|Majora's Mask (Item)|link}}.
* [[EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes]] rewards Link with {{Term|BotW|Tingle's Set|link}}.
* [[EX Treasure: Twilight Relic]] rewards Link with [[Midna's Helmet]].
* [[EX Treasure: Phantasma]] rewards Link with the [[Phantom Equipment]].
