{{Term/Store|Colossal Wallet||Series, TP}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119 {{Exp Game|TPHD}}}}</ref> is a [[Wallet]] upgrade appearing only in {{TPHD}}. It is a dark purple bag ornately decorated with a few [[Rupee]]s circling around the center area.

The Colossal Wallet is the third and final Wallet upgrade in {{TPHD|-}}. It is obtained after completing the [[Cave of Shadows]], provided that [[Link]] already has the Giant Wallet. The Colossal Wallet can hold up to 9,999 [[Rupee]]s, the most out of any Wallet upgrade in {{TLoZ|Series}}, including the [[Tycoon Wallet]] from {{SS}}.
