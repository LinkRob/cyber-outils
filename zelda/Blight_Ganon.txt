The {{Term/Store|Blight Ganon|Blight Ganons|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are the secondary villains in {{BotW}}. They are a group of [[Malice]] and [[Ancient Technology]] hybrids created by [[Calamity Ganon]] as a result of taking over the [[Divine Beast]]s,<ref>{{Cite|Awaken, Blight Ganons! Consign that wretched princess to oblivion!|Astor|HWAoC}}</ref>{{Name Ref|BotW}}. They are responsible for the death of the [[Champions]] and are servants to Calamity Ganon. Because of the [[Sheikah]] technology embeded deep inside them, they can teleport and float.

==Description==
Blight Ganons appear as maroon humanoids. They share their colour with the [[Malice]] that comprises part of them with a pair of arms and no legs, making them appear ghostly. Each of them wields a weapon linked to the champion who faced them and died to them. [[Mipha|Mipha’s]] weapon of choice was a spear, and when you fight [[Waterblight Ganon]], it's weapon is a spear. [[Daruk]] held his giant boulder breaker. [[Fireblight Ganon]] holds an axe. [[Revali]] used his bow. [[Windblight Ganon]] uses a laser, like the [[Guardian|guardians]]. 
Windblight and [[Thunderblight Ganon|Thunderblight]] are the only ones whose only weapon are lasers, and since it can fire from a distance, it’s the Sheikah equivalent to a bow. Thunderblight Ganon also has a shield and a blight version of a scimitar.
