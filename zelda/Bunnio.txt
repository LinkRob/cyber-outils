{{Term/Store|Bunnio||Series, ST}} is a character in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 288 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Bunnio resides at [[Rabbitland Rescue]], a [[Train Station]] in the [[Forest Realm]]. As the only human inhabitant of the region and owner of the [[Rabbit]] haven, he rewards [[Link]] with [[Treasure]] whenever he brings him a certain number, and sometimes pattern, of lost Rabbits from the overworld of [[Hyrule]]. He ultimately grants Link the [[Swordsman's Scroll]] after retrieving and safeguarding all 50 Rabbits, 10 from each of the five realms. 

Later on, [[Mona]], Bunnio's wife who once lived with him but now lives alone in [[Castle Town]], will question his whereabouts as he has been pursuing his Rabbit-rescuing dream halfway across the realm for some time. Reuniting Mona with her husband at Rabbitland Rescue will yield Link a [[Force Gem]] that illuminates extra connective [[Spirit Tracks (Object)|Spirit Tracks]] in the Forest Realm.
