The Old '''Mermaid Key''' is an item in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Oracle of Ages]]''. It is the key to [[Mermaid's Cave#Oracle%20of%20Ages|Mermaid's Cave]] and exists in both time periods, as the [[Mermaid Key]] for the past and the Old Mermaid Key for the present. Both keys are obtained in a similar fashion.

==Location and Uses==
To obtain the {{Term|OoA|Old Mermaid Key}}, {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} must first obtain the {{Term|OoA|Brother Emblem|link}} from the {{Term|OoA|Graceful Goron|link}} by successfully completing the {{Term|OoA|Goron Dance|link}} in either the present or the past.<ref>{{Cite|If you dance well, I'll give you a Brotherhoood Emblem!|Graceful Goron|OoA}}</ref> Obtaining the {{Term|OoA|Brother Emblem}} makes {{Term|OoA|Link}} an honorary {{Term|OoA|Goron|link}},<ref>{{Cite|You got the Brotherhood Emblem! Now you are a Goron, too!|N/A|OoA}}</ref> allowing him to bypass the {{Term|OoA|Goron}} guarding the stairs to the upper reaches of {{Term|OoA|Rolling Ridge|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|For generations, my ancestors have sat here guarding the peace on Rolling Ridge.|Goron|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Huh?!? That's a Brotherhood Emblem! You understand the passion of a Goron's heart! You may pass!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Next, {{Term|OoA|Link}} must complete the {{Term|OoA|Target Carts|link}} {{Term|OoA|Mini-Game|link}} with a perfect score in order to receive the {{Term|OoA|Rock Brisket|link}}. This item is given to the very same {{Term|OoA|Goron}} guard, who happens to be very hungry.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so hungry I could starve. I'd love some Rock Brisket...|Goron|OoA}}</ref> In return, he will give {{Term|OoA|Link}} the {{Term|OoA|Goron Vase|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Please, let me have that Rock Brisket! I'll trade my ancestors' Goron Vase for it!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Back in the past, the {{Term|OoA|Goron}} guard (who is the ancestor of the one in the present), ironically, would like the vase to use as an heirloom to teach his descendants the value of life.<ref>{{Cite|I need an heirloom that will teach my descendants to value life...|Goron|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yeah... A vase would be great!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> In return, {{Term|OoA|Link}} will be given the {{Term|OoA|Goronade|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What a fine Goron Vase! Please! Let me have that vase! I'll trade you some Goronade for it!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Finally, after giving the {{Term|OoA|Goronade}} to the {{Term|OoA|Goron}} in charge of the {{Term|OoA|Big Bang Game|link}}, {{Term|OoA|Link}} will gain the opportunity to play for the {{Term|OoA|Old Mermaid Key}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm wide awake now! Thanks! I'm gonna open up shop! Want to give my game a try?|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Once he wins, the key is his.
