The {{Term/Store|School of Joy||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a location in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258}}</ref>

==Features==
The school is run by [[Mrs. Marie]], [[Windfall Island]]'s teacher. When [[Link]] first talks to Mrs. Marie, she explains of the problem she has with a group of students known as the [[Killer Bees]]. They never listen to a word she says and they are always skipping school, causing trouble around the island.<ref>{{Cite|There's this delinquent gang of four young boys who never listen to a word I say. Even though I wait patiently for those boys to show up, they never come. It's as if they don't want joy in their hearts!|Mrs. Marie|TWW}}</ref> Link can help Mrs. Marie by playing [[Hide-and-Seek]] with them. After finding them all, they will promise to stop misbehaving and start attending school.

Since Mrs. Marie loves jewelry, Link can give her 20 [[Joy Pendant]]s to receive the [[Cabana Deed]], which gives him ownership of a [[Private Oasis]] located to the south.<ref>{{Cite|You see, although I am but a mere teacher, I own my very own cabana in a tropical oasis down south. But lately I haven't made use of it at all, so you can have it!|Mrs. Marie|TWW}}</ref> Furthermore, by giving her another set of 20 Joy Pendants, for a total of 40, she will reward Link with the [[Hero's Charm]], <ref>{{Cite|You got the Hero's Charm! This mystical trinket gives you the ability to see the life forces of your foes!|Mrs. Marie|TWW}}</ref> or [[Treasure Chart|Treasure Chart #45]] in {{TWWHD}}.

[[Joanna]] and [[Potova]] can also be found inside the school from time to time.
