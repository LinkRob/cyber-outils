==Overview==
For the January 2001 issue of [[Nintendo Power]], [[Nintendo]] offered a giveaway of a copy of {{MM}} and the original {{Term|Series|Special Edition Fish Bone Guitar}}.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= One lucky Grand Prize Winner will learn scales on a {{Web Color|Orange|SPECIAL EDITION}} {{Web Color|Yellow|FISH BONE GUITAR!}} The custom-made instrument looks just like the one that belonged to the late, great Indigo-Gos front man, Mikau. The winner will also score a copy of The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask, as well.|magazine= Nintendo Power|publisher= |volume= |issue= 140|date= January 2001|page= 99}}</ref> It was hand-carved and painted to resemble the {{Term|MM|Zora Guitar|link}} by luthier Dan Lawrence.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This ultra-rare Jackson is a replica of the Zora guitar, hand carved and painted by Jackson Custom Shop luthier Dan Lawrence.|author= Walter Bryant|published= November 19, 2015|retrieved= October 9, 2021|url= https://www.themusiczoo.com/blogs/news/out-of-the-case-jackson-custom-shop-legend-of-zelda-zoraxe|title= Out Of The Case: Jackson Custom Shop Legend of Zelda “Zoraxe”|site= The Music Zoo|type= }}</ref> Six other {{Plural|Series|Special Edition Fish Bone Guitar}} were created for sale outside of the contest, for a total of seven.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= The original instrument was commissioned by Nintendo Power magazine for a 2001 Player’s Poll giveaway with another six instruments produced for display and sale, making this instrument an absolute must-have for any Zelda fanatic!|author= Walter Bryant|published= November 19, 2015|retrieved= October 9, 2021|url= https://www.themusiczoo.com/blogs/news/out-of-the-case-jackson-custom-shop-legend-of-zelda-zoraxe|title= Out Of The Case: Jackson Custom Shop Legend of Zelda “Zoraxe”|site= The Music Zoo|type= }}</ref> They are electric guitars with seven strings.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Used 2003 Jackson Custom Shop Legend of Zelda �Zoraxe� 7-String Electric Guitar|author= |published= |retrieved= October 9, 2021|url= https://reverb.com/item/1405166-used-2003-jackson-custom-shop-legend-of-zelda-zoraxe-7-string-electric-guitar|title= Used 2003 Jackson Custom Shop Legend of Zelda �Zoraxe� 7-String Electric Guitar|site= Reverb|type= }}</ref>
