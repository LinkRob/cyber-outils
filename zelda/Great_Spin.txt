Let it be hewn into your mind!|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref><ref name="ALBW">{{Cite|Incredible! You've mastered the Great Spin! Build power by holding {{Icon|ALBW B}}, and then let loose!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TFH}}

==Overview==
==={{TP|-}}===
The Great Spin is the seventh and final [[Hidden Skill]] that [[Link]] learns in {{TP}} by the [[Hero's Spirit]], the restless soul of the legendary [[Hero of Time]]. Link can learn this Hidden Skill by first finding a [[Howling Stone]] to awaken the [[White Wolf]].<ref>{{Cite|Search for the statues that howl with the {{Color|TPHD Red|sound of the wind}}. Seek the sound that calls the spirit of the beast to awaken me again.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref> The Howling Stone needed to learn the Great Spin can be found in the [[Hidden Village]] near the [[Cucco Leader]]. After howling with the White Wolf, he instructs Link to find him while in human form.<ref>{{Cite|Let teachings of old pass to you... Take sword in hand and find me...|White Wolf|TPHD}}</ref> The White Wolf also marks the location of where he can be found on Link's [[Map]]. Once Link returns to human form, he can find the White Wolf waiting in front of [[Hyrule Castle]]. Approaching the White Wolf will return Link to the clouded area where the Hero's Spirit can teach Link the Great Spin.

This Hidden Skill is a much stronger version of Link's normal [[Spin Attack]]. At full health,<ref name="Skill Info"/> he uses a wide-range Spin Attack with a powerful wave for an extended radius.<ref>{{Cite|You learned the final hidden skill, the great spin! With full life energy, unleash a spin attack for even more damage.|N/A|TP}}</ref> The difference between the Great Spin and the standard Spin Attack can be noticed by how the Great Spin's attack radius is red instead of the Spin Attack's light green.

In the [[Twilight Realm]], if Link is at full health and has absorbed the [[Sol]] into the [[Master Sword]], the Spin Attack will reach its maximum radius, as the Spin Attack has the same reach as the Great Spin, and combined with the Great Spin's radius makes it much wider (approximately 1.5 the radius of the Great Spin).

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Stub|ALBW}}
The Great Spin returns in {{ALBW}} as the reward for returning all 100 [[Maiamai]] to [[Mother Maiamai]].<ref name="ALBW"/> It works as an increase in power and radius over the [[Spin Attack]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
Link can perform the Great Spin in {{TFH}} only by purchasing and donning the [[Spin Attack Attire]].<ref>{{Cite|Increases Spin Attack range and power. Hold and release {{Icon|TFH B}} to unleash the fury!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> This version of the Great Spin has double the range, power, and duration of the normal Spin Attack, and grants Link invincibility while performing the attack, allowing him to strike any type of {{Term|TFH|Enemy|link}} without being harmed and causing traps such as [[Fire Bar]]s to pass through him.
