{{Term/Store|Malanya||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Malanya|N/A|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Malanya}} is the god who watches over the {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} of the world.<ref name="Malanya">{{Cite|My name is {{Color|BotW Blue|Malanya}}. I am the god who watches over the {{Color|BotW Red|horses}} of this world.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He makes his home in the {{Term|BotW|Faron (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Malanya Spring|link}}, near the {{Term|BotW|Lake of the Horse God|link}}. While he has a strange sense of humor about reviving {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, he will not harm those who truly care for them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book= CaC |publisher= |page= 141}}</ref>

When first visited, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} is found trapped inside the bud of his {{Term|BotW|Malanya Spring|display=Spring}}. He begs {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to listen to his story.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Boy...}} Sweet boy... Please... Listen to my story...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} introduces himself and asks if he has ever lost one of his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure.] I am Malanya... Hast thou... I mean... Have you ever felt the pain of losing a horse companion?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} claims that if his power is restored, he will be able to help {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If my power is returned to me, I may be able to help you out in some way...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> In order to regain his power, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} requires 1,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So please... I beg of you... Help me regain the power I've lost... All I need is {{Color|BotW Red|1,000 rupees}}... When my power has been restored, I should be able to be of use to you, who loves horses so.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> His hand will appear from inside the bud, and he will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to hand over his {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now... You have rupees for me, right? Hand then over. Quickly! In order for me to regain my power, I need {{Color|BotW Red|1,000 rupees}}.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to the payment, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will thank him.<ref>{{Cite|[Here you go.] Thank you. You are too kind.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malanya Spring}} will then open, and {{Term|BotW|Malanya}}'s power will be restored.<ref>{{Cite|Nwaaaaaagh... I...am... Revived!!|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} himself then emerges from the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and he remarks on how free he feels.<ref>{{Cite|Ho ho ho... At last! Free of that tiny bud after so long! If my arms were corporeal, they'd have cramped for sure!|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He recognizes {{Term|BotW|Link}} as the one who set him free.<ref>{{Cite|And you're the one who set me free, are you not?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} then properly introduces himself to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, explaining he presides over the {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} of the world.<ref name="Malanya"/> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} helped, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} believes he should grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} a reward.<ref>{{Cite|I suppose you deserve a {{Color|BotW Red|reward}} for setting me free... What shall I do...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} considers eating him for a meal, which causes {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take on a defensive stance.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Shall I make you my meal?!}}|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} reveals that he is joking.<ref>{{Cite|Hahaha, I only jest...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Malanya}} explains that if one of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} is killed, he will be able to revive it.<ref>{{Cite|If a horse you love passes away from some sort of tragic, fatal accident, it is within my power to revive that horse...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> This only works on {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} that have been registered at a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}. He warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if he believes the {{Term|BotW|Horse}}'s death was by his own hands, he will be subject to his wrath.<ref>{{Cite|However... If I detect vile intent behind the death of your horse... {{Big|You will suffer my wrath!}}|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> Claiming to be joking once again, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} takes a look at {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|I only jest... Let me see...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

On subsequent visits to {{Term|BotW|Malanya Spring}}, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will recognize {{Term|BotW|Link}} when he emerges from the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's you again...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} why he has returned, and he takes a look at {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} to ensure their wellbeing.<ref>{{Cite|Why have you sought me out? Surely no ill has befallen your beloved, faithful steeds... Let me see|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} not have any dead {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} claims that all seems well.<ref>{{Cite|...Hrm! I see you're good with horses. All seems to be well.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has a high bond with one of his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} notes that they have an extraordinary friendship.<ref>{{Cite|The friendship you established with a horse really is extraordinary.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} care for him, just as he does for them.<ref>{{Cite|Remember that such a friendship is a two-way road. Horses care for you as much as you do for them.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have any {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} registered when he speaks to {{Term|BotW|Malanya}}, he will be shocked.<ref>{{Cite|...Whoa. Do you really not own a single horse?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} cannot imagine going through life without a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, but he supposes this is how {{Term|BotW|Link}} lives his life.<ref>{{Cite|I can't imagine going through the world without a faithful steed to keep me company... To each their own, I suppose.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He is unable to do anything to help those who have decided to avoid {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|In that case, I, the god of horses, can not do much for you who avoids horses for some reason.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever does befriend a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} that later dies, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} tells him to come see him.<ref>{{Cite|If you ever do bond with a horse and then tragedy strikes that proud friend, you should come see me.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

If one of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} has died, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will be able to tell.<ref>{{Cite|...Neigh! A horse, loyal and true, has fallen during your adventures!|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> In anger, he threatens to end {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|I could end you, right here and now!}}|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He quickly calms down, revealing this to be another joke, and he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} to be revived.<ref>{{Cite|I jest, of course... Shall I bring a companion back to travel with you once more?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will ask him to choose among his deceased {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Select a loyal friend to bring back...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> Depending on how {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Horse}} died, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will have differing reactions to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s request.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Horse}} died to a {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will be moved by how well it served its master.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... You want to revive this horse? It seems this brave animal spent its last moments in a fight with a Guardian. It served its master well. I'm so moved...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} becomes angry that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was not able to control his {{Term|BotW|Horse}} well enough to save its life.<ref>{{Cite|Alas, if you had managed to control this loyal beast with more skill... {{Big|I wouldn't have to revive it!}}|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} then claims to be joking, and he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is certain he wants to revive this {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|I jest... This must have been one brave friend. Would you like to revive it and return it to your side?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Horse}} died due to an explosion, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will be saddened by its manner of death.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... Would you like to revive this horse? Oh, goodness! It was caught in an explosion? Poor soul...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> While he does not want to cast unwarranted doubt on {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he wonders if he actually killed his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Not to cast suspicion where none is warranted, but...you didn't kill the horse, did you?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} decides this could not be, and he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} again if he wants to revive the {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|It couldn't be the case. I'll give you the benefit of the doubt. Would you like to revive this horse?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Horse}} was killed by him, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will be shocked.<ref>{{Cite|So... You would like to revive this horse? This one, specifically? I can do that, of course! But... What is this...|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he only wants to revive his {{Term|BotW|Horse}} so he can kill it again, and he accuses {{Term|BotW|Link}} of thinking of {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} only as tools that can be discarded.<ref>{{Cite|This horse says that YOU killed it! You! How could you?! Do you plan to revive it just to kill it again? Are horses only a tool to you? Do you treat their noble lives so casually that you'd use them and throw them away?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} claims that if he were a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, he would kick {{Term|BotW|Link}} in the chest.<ref>{{Cite|If I were a horse right now... {{Big|I'd kick you in the chest!}}|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> After claiming to be joking again, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} asserts that {{Term|BotW|Link}} likely struck his {{Term|BotW|Horse}} on accident, and he asks for confirmation that {{Term|BotW|Link}} would like to revive his "temporarily betrayed" friend.<ref>{{Cite|I...jest. You accidentally struck the horse, I'm sure. Would you like to revive this loyal, temporarily betrayed friend?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Horse}} died in any other manner, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will call it an accident. He assures {{Term|BotW|Link}} that such things happen sometimes.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm. I can tell that this horse died due to an unexpected accident. Worry not, this happens from time to time.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He claims that the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} knows that its death was not {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s intention, and he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is certain that this is the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} he wants to revive.<ref>{{Cite|The horse knows that you did not mean for this to happen. Would you like for me to revive this horse?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} once again confirms his decision to revive his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will then attempt to bring it back.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, understood! Let me bring back this horse!|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He will then wave his hands in the air and spin his head, after which the selected {{Term|BotW|Horse}} will appear nearby. After this, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} proclaims that he was successful in reviving the {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|I was successfully able to bring back the horse!|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> In the future, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take good care of his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, as they are supposed to be partners.<ref>{{Cite|Next time, don't let the horse die! Horses are your partners. Don't forget that.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will regret it if he is careless with them.<ref>{{Cite|If you're careless with their lives, you may regret it... {{Big|UNDERSTAND?}}|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} claims that he is only joking for now.<ref>{{Cite|I jest... For now!|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> At the end of their conversation, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will collapse and fall back into his {{Term|BotW|Malanya Spring|display=Spring}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes another visit to {{Term|BotW|Malanya Spring}}, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will be shocked that he has come back to revive another {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you again... You didn't come here to revive a horse again, did you?|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he only helped {{Term|BotW|Link}} for free the first time due to the fact that he freed him, but he is too busy to always be available to him.<ref>{{Cite|I felt I was in your debt before since you freed me from that bud and all...but you didn't think I'd always help out, right? {{Big|I'm very busy!}}|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} reveals that if {{Term|BotW|Link}} were to bring him something, he would continue to revive his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|I jest. I feel that there is respect in your heart... If you bring me something, I would consider helping you once more.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s confusion, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} reveals that he needs {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}, which he has only eaten once.<ref>{{Cite|[Some...thing?] I may not look the part, but I am the {{Color|BotW Red|god of horses}}. I need that uh...that thing... A kind of carrot... One I've only eaten once in all my many years... An {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|endura carrot}}!}} That is what I ask of you. Fulfill this request and I will consider helping you.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} hands over an {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot}}, {{Term|BotW|Malanya}} will agree to revive one of his {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I have one now!] I see you have an {{Color|BotW Blue|endura carrot}}. Allow me to revive your horse.|Malanya|BotW}}</ref>

==={{TotK|-}}===
Malanya returns in {{TotK}} with the same role he had in {{BotW}}. He can now upgrade the stats of Link's Horses in exchange for certain dishes that Link cooked.
