{{Term/Store|Big Pengator|Big Pengators|Series, ALBW}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Sub-Boss}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 166}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
A {{Term|ALBW|Big Pengator}} is the {{Term|ALBW|Sub-Boss}} of the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Ruins|link}}, where it is accompanied by four regular {{Plural|ALBW|Pengator|link}}. It shares the same characteristics, attacks and weaknesses as a regular {{Term|ALBW|Pengator}}, differentiating only in size and health.

When {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|ALBW|Ice|link}}-covered room where the {{Term|ALBW|Sub-Boss}} is encountered, the doors will lock behind him and the north wall will open up, revealing the {{Term|ALBW|Big Pengator}} and the smaller {{Plural|ALBW|Pengator}} on a plateau, ready to dive into battle. After {{Term|ALBW|Link}} defeats them, the doors will reopen and a {{Term|ALBW|Warp Point|link}} will appear in the center of the room, which will transport {{Term|ALBW|Link}} to the entrance of the {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}} and back.

Two {{Plural|ALBW|Big Pengator}} can also be found on floor 18 of the advanced stage in the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower|link}}, which {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must fight in order to proceed.
