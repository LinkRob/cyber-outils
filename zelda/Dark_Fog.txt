{{Term/Store|Dark Fog|Dark Fog|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} is a trap in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 155}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|TPHD|Dark Fog}} is an element that is found only in the {{Term|TPHD|Palace of Twilight|link}}, the eighth {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon|link}} in {{TP}}. This mysterious haze-like element is composed of {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Crystal|link}} created by {{Term|TPHD|Zant|link}}.<ref name="Midna Dark Fog">{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Blue|This black fog is made up of shadow crystals created by Zant. They blot out light... He's responsible for stealing the light from the spirits and turning your world into twilight... But he's not all-powerful. We're prepared too. I can return you to your normal state whenever you need me, so don't worry, OK?}}|Midna|TPHD}}</ref> It is scattered across various parts of the {{Term|TPHD|Palace of Twilight}}, and if {{Term|TPHD|Link}} comes in contact with the mysterious element, he will automatically transform into {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}, and will be unable to have {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}} transform him back into his human shape until he leaves the {{Term|TPHD|Dark Fog}} or clears it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Blue|No, Link! The darkness is too powerful in fog like this. Remain in wolf form!}}|Midna|TPHD}}</ref> Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} obtains the light-infused {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword|link}}, he will be able to clear away {{Term|TPHD|Dark Fog}} using the {{Term|TPHD|Spin Attack|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Shadow crystals flow like waterfalls here... There's no way we can get rid of all of them. Charge your sword up and release the power of the Sol all at once.|Midna|TP}}</ref> {{Term|TPHD|Midna}} mentions that the {{Term|TPHD|Dark Fog}} is what caused {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to become a wolf for the first time.<ref name="Midna Dark Fog"/><ref>{{Cite|This black fog is the light-absorbing shadow crystals that Zant magically produced… It's responsible for stealing the light from the spirits and turning Hyrule into Twilight. If you want to return to your normal state, tell me so after slipping out of the fog.|Midna|TP|[http://forums.legendsalliance.com/topic/14202-translation-of-japanese-game-texts/page__st__750__p__398496#entry398496 Japanese version]}}</ref>
