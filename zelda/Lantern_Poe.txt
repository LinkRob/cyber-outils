The {{Term/Store|Lantern Poe|Lantern Poes|Series, TFH}} is an {{Term|TFH|Enemy}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 189}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
A single {{Term|TFH|Lantern Poe}} appears in the first {{Term|TFH|Stage}} of {{Term|TFH|Palace Noir|link}}. It is a yellow {{Term|TFH|Poe}} that slowly flies around the room carrying a {{Term|TFH|Lantern}}. It cannot be harmed directly by the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}, and the only means of defeating it is to light all the {{Plural|TFH|Torchlight|link}} in the room.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TFH |publisher= Prima |page= 18}}</ref> Once it is defeated, the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}} to the next {{Term|TFH|Stage}} will open.
