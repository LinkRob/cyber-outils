{{Term/Store|Vaati Transfigured||Series, TMC}} is a {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Vaati Transfigured|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
After enough damage is dealt to {{Term|TMC|Vaati Reborn|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms into his second form, {{Term|TMC|Vaati Transfigured}}. He floats around the room, occasionally causing rocks to fall down, and conjuring purple spikes around the battlefield, or electrifying himself before shooting energy balls at {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}. Four of the eight orbs that surround {{Term|TMC|Vaati Transfigured}} must be struck with {{Plural|TMC|Arrow|link}} to reveal which ones are vulnerable to the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}; vulnerable orbs turn into red eyes, while invulnerable orbs turn blue. After all four weak spots are uncovered, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must clone himself with the duplication tiles on the battlefield to strike all of {{Term|TMC|Vaati Transfigured}}'s vulnerable eyes at the same time. This will stun {{Term|TMC|Vaati Transfigured}}, rendering him weak to continued {{Term|TMC|Sword|link}} strikes. {{Term|TMC|Vaati Transfigured}}'s attacks become more intense as he takes damage, and he also moves faster, making it more difficult for {{Term|TMC|Link}} to line up his own attacks.

When enough damage is dealt to {{Term|TMC|Vaati Transfigured}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} will be able to break the curse on {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} and attempt to escape {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}} before fighting {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s third and final form, {{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath|link}}.
