Pins are used on the [[Map]] to add waypoints. There are five available types of Pins, each depicted with a unique color and pattern. Contrarily to [[Stamp (Object)|Stamps]], only once instance of each Pin can be placed at once, making the maximum number of Pins set at once a total of five.
