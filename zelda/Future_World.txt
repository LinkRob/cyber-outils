TITLE : Future World

{{Ambig}}The {{Term/Store|Future World||Series, CoH}} is  a location in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite Internal|quote= Future World |game= CoH |system= Nintendo Switch |version= 1.0.0 |country= United States of America}}</ref>
==Entrance to the {{Term|CoH|Future World}}==
The Future World can be accessed as early as the beginning of the game through a portal in the [[Lost Woods]]. After arriving through the portal, Link will end up in Deku Village, one of the last surviving settlements in Hyrule at the time. 

== Lost Woods ==

=== Deku Village ===
The [[Deku Village|Deku village]] is one of the two remaining settlements in [[Hyrule]] after [[Ganon]] used the [[Golden Lute]] to take over [[Hyrule]]. A [[Deku Scrub]] tells Link that it is the largest Deku settlement in all of Hyrule, implying there to be more than one. The village is composed of a couple houses and one large palace-like building housing the [[Deku Royal Family]]. One of the houses is the [[Deku Shooting Gallery]] and the other is the house that has the [[Time Portal|time portal]] inside. In the centre of the village is a totem pole-like structure with a [[Deku Scrub]] carved into the top. To the north of the village is a hollowed out log leading to the [[The Great Deku Tree|Deku Tree]], whom the [[Deku Scrub|Deku Scrubs]] refer to as “grandfather”. The [[The Great Deku Tree|Deku Tree]] perishes, however, if visited in [[Story Mode]]. If visited in any other mode he continues to live. 

== Gerudo Desert ==

=== Gerudo Village ===
[[Gerudo Village]] has mostly been destroyed by the time of [[Ganon]]’s reign and is now home to the [[Gerudo Arena]] where people fight through waves of enemies and eventually, [[King Dobongo]]. The [[Gerudo Arena]] is also one of the main dungeons in the game. The village has one other building that sells [[Potion|potions]]. At the time of [[Ganon]]’s rule, [[Barriara]] is no longer the chief of the [[Gerudo]] as [[Ganondorf]] came of age during Link’s absence.

=== Gerudo Ruins ===
The [[Gerudo Ruins]] are a mini dungeon and also the location of the [[Goron Mask]]. If visited in the present, it is one of the main dungeons.
