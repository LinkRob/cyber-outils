{{Term/Store|Drona||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Drona}}
{{Stub|TWW}}
Drona is a [[Korok]] who lives in the [[Forest Haven]]. Before the Forest Ceremony, Drona can be found flying low to the ground around the base of the Great Deku Tree. After the Forest Ceremony, Drona goes to the [[Private Oasis]] to plant new [[Tree]]s.

==Trivia==
*Many Koroks resembling Drona, including a Korok known as [[Walton]], appear in {{BotW}}.
