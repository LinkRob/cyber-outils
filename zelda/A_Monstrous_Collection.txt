==Overview==
{{Term|TotK|Kilton|link}}, a fan of monsters, dreams of creating a monster collection stage of sculptures. He is working with {{Term|TotK|Hudson|link}} of {{Term|TotK|Hudson Construction|link}} to help him build sculptures of monsters and place them in {{Term|TotK|Tarrey Town|link}}. Unfortunately, Hudson has trouble trying to visualize Kilton's descriptions. He would need a picture for a more precise reference of the monsters. To help them, {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} shows them a picture of the monsters on his {{Term|TotK|Purah Pad|link}}. This allows Hudson to get a better idea, and he was able to build a near-perfect replica of the monsters in the pictures.<ref>For Part 1 (and all the other parts), the monster sculpture is in the pose it was pictured in, and the variant is also depicted. For example, a picture of a Red Bokoblin vs. a picture of a Silver Bokoblin (both work for completing Part 1); as seen here: https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/A_Monstrous_Collection_I for red and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yepuqv3_yc8 for silver. However, the weapon seen in the sculpture does not always match to the weapon seen in the picture.</ref>

From this point on, Link may help with Kilton and Hudson to create a stage of monster sculptures by showing them any pictures of monsters using his Camera. During time to time, Kilton may ask for specific monsters and Link may look for these monsters to fulfill these specific requests. For every request, Kilton gives Link rewards for his troubles, particularly {{Plural|TotK|Monster Extract|link}} and some other item with them.
