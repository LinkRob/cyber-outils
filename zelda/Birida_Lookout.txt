{{Term/Store|Birida Lookout||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Birida Lookout'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Birida Lookout is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. Birida Lookout is a small collection of ruins that overlook the valley that contains [[Gerudo Tower]]. It has been abandoned and contains mechanisms like pulleys that Link can manipulate using [[Magnesis]]. Metal boxes are littered throughout the ruins, which contain [[Rupee]]s and [[Apple]]s.

==Nomenclature==
Birida Lookout derives its name from ''viridine'', a type of green-colored {{Wp|andalusite}}.
