The {{Term/Store|Sage Temple||Series, HWAoC}} is a location in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Soldiers gather in a room within the Sage Temple to meditate upon the discipline of soldiering. Bring them something to help guide their military mindfulness.|Gallery|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|HWAoC|Sage Temple}} is located on the west side of {{Term|HWAoC|Kingdom of Hyrule|link|display=Hyrule}}, near a shallow pond off of the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylia River|link}}. It is a spot where {{Plural|HWAoC|Soldier|link}} go to meditate. In "{{Term|HWAoC|Relief for Soldier Stress|link}}", the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} assist the {{Plural|HWAoC|Soldier}} in their meditation by providing three {{Plural|HWAoC|Hyrule Herb|link}} to burn as incense. This unlocks {{Term|HWAoC|Meditative Training|link}} for use.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Sage Temple}} has become the {{Term|HWAoC|Sage Temple Ruins|link}} by the time of {{BotW}}.
