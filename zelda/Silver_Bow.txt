{{Plural|BotW|Silver Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} that were originally made for fishing, and because of this, they do not bear high attack power.<ref name="Inventory"/> However, they do have a higher durability due to the special metal they're crafted from.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Zora Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by the {{Plural|BotW|Zora|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref> Commonly, they are found in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}, particularly around {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, as well as in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. Many {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} in these areas utilize them.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Bow}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Bow}} will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
