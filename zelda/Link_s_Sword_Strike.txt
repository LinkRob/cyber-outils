{{Term/Store|Link's Sword Strike|Link's Sword Strikes|Series, SS|plural}} are abilities in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS|publisher= Prima|page= 6}}</ref>

==Overview==
There is a total of four {{Plural|SS|Link's Sword Strike}}. {{Term|SS|Link|link}} can execute them by swinging his {{Term|SS|Sword|link}} in a specific manner.

===Diagonal Slash===
{{Main|Diagonal Slash}}
{{Term|SS|Link}} can execute a {{Term|SS|Diagonal Slash|link}} by swinging his {{Term|SS|Sword}} diagonally.

===Side Slash===
{{Main|Side Slash}}
{{Term|SS|Link}} can execute a {{Term|SS|Horizontal Slash|link}} by swinging his {{Term|SS|Sword}} horizontally.

===Thrust===
{{Main|Thrust}}
{{Term|SS|Link}} can execute a {{Term|SS|Thrust|link}} by thrusting his {{Term|SS|Sword}} forwards.

===Vertical Slash===
{{Main|Vertical Slash}}
{{Term|SS|Link}} can execute a {{Term|SS|Vertical Slash|link}} by swinging his {{Term|SS|Sword}} vertically.
