==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}} was a female {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} who lived at {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}} with her mate {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Monkton|link}} when he was a child.<ref name="Monkton"/> {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} was an adventurous child and, as there were no other children around for him to play with, he befriended {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}} and {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} and took them with him on his adventures.<ref name="Playing"/>

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} about {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}}, he will explain that, in the nearby {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}}, there are a set of ruins called the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Temple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Tell me about Mary.] Maybe you've seen the big canyon to the southeast. There are some secret ruins down at the base of that valley.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> While he does not know who made the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Temple|display=Temple}}, the adults would always tell him that it was too dangerous to investigate.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know who made them or when. The adults always said not to go near. They said it was too dangerous there.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> Ignoring this advice, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} took {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}} to the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon}} to search for the ruins.<ref>{{Cite|One day {{Color|BotW Blue|Mary}} and I went out there to explore and search for the ruins.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}} lost her footing and slipped into the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|display=Canyon}}, never to be seen again.<ref name="Mary"/>

{{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} and {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}}'s descendants currently live in the stalls next to {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}}, where they are looked after by an elderly {{Term|BotW|Monkton}}.<ref name="Monkton"/> Though he has been haunted by the memory of losing {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}},<ref>{{Cite|That memory will haunt me until my dying day...|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} has been able to reconcile with his grief.<ref>{{Cite|As I grew older, I learned to overcome my grief over losing them. You can't change the past, only the future.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref>
