The "{{Term/Store|Song of Soaring||Series, MM, MM3D}}" is a [[Song]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96}}</ref>

==Learning Location and Uses==
[[File:MM3D Song of Soaring Icon.png|left]]
The "Song of Soaring" allows [[Link]] to warp to all the different [[Owl Statues]] throughout [[Termina]]. However, he can only warp to an Owl Statue he has activated.<ref>{{Cite|If you have left proof of our encounter on any of those stone statues... Then the song carved at my feet will certainly be of some assistance...|Kaepora Gaebora|MM}}</ref> Link can also warp to the entrance of a dungeon by playing the song while inside one. Link can learn the song from a stone monument in the [[Southern Swamp]], just next to the entrance to [[Woodfall]]. In {{MM3D}}, he learns the song at the entrance of the Southern Swamp.
