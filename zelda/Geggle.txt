{{Term/Store|Geggle||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Geggle|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Geggle}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} worker at {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}. Sometime prior to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} arrival at the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}, he and {{Term|BotW|Chork|link}} went to investigate the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff|link}}, but they were unable figure out its secrets.<ref>{{Cite|Go way out south of here, and you'll find a flat hill called {{Color|BotW Blue|Washa's Bluff}}. On that hill, there's this mysterious-lookin' platform thingie... Me and Geggle tried all day to figure out what it does, but we're stumped...|Chork|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Geggle}} on the cliff behind the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|display=Stable}}, staring up at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first speaks to him, he asks what he needs.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm?<br/>What is it, my smart man?|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants to know what he is looking at,<ref>{{Cite|[Whatcha looking at?] Hmm... What could it be?|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Geggle}} points out a section of the cliff face that appears to be man-made.<ref>{{Cite|I happened to notice that a section of the cliff face there in the distance looks almost man-made.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> He asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} can see it too, and while he would check it out if he could, he can not make it there on his own.<ref>{{Cite|My smart man, tell me... You can see it too, yes? I would take a closer look if I could get there on my own.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Cliffside Etchings|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

When spoken to again after this, {{Term|BotW|Geggle}} recognizes {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, well if it isn't my smart man.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> Once more, he points out the strange section of the cliff,<ref>{{Cite|That section of cliff, off in the distance... It almost looks man-made, no?|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> claiming he would investigate if he had the energy of an adventurer.<ref>{{Cite|If I was as chock-full of energy as an adventurer, I would climb that cliff and take a closer look in a heartbeat.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Geggle}} returns to the cliff even after {{Term|BotW|Link}} has finished the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, where he looks for something to pique his curiosity.<ref>{{Cite|I'm in the market for a little something to pique my curiosity, so I spend my time gazing into the horizon.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> However, he is disappointed at the end of the day when nothing has happened,<ref>{{Cite|Another day come and gone. No excitement to speak of.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> and he admits that he is simply trying to pass the time.<ref>{{Cite|Just passing the time...looking off into the distance.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref>

Throughout the night, {{Term|BotW|Geggle}} sits inside the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable}}. He wonders when he got so old,<ref>{{Cite|When did I get so old?|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> and he claims if he would be off exploring if he was still young.<ref>{{Cite|If I still had my youth, I'd be off in a flash, in search of something... fascinating.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref> He feels trapped by his age, as the only thing he can do is stare off into the distance.<ref>{{Cite|But this prison of old bones has condemned me to a short future of staring off into the unknown.|Geggle|BotW}}</ref>
