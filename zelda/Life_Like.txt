{{Term/Store|Life Like|Life Likes|Series, TFH|plural}} are enemies in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TFH|publisher= Prima|page=17}}</ref> They are a smaller variant of the {{Term|Series|Like Like|link}}, and can be compared to the {{Term|Series|Rupee Like|link}}.

==Characteristics==
Life Likes are found in the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}. They hide themselves in the ground, appearing as a seemingly harmless {{Term|TFH|Heart|link}}, but pop up when a {{Term|TFH|Links|link}} comes near. Their fake {{Plural|TFH|Heart}} can be distinguished by a tiny shaking they make. They will then begin moving towards any nearby {{Plural|TFH|Links}}. If allowed to get close enough, they will lunge and capture {{Plural|TFH|Links}} in their mouth. The {{Term|TFH|Link}} will begin to lose {{Plural|TFH|Heart}} at a rapid rate. Eventually, it will release them, and they must quickly run away to avoid being inhaled again. The imprisoned {{Term|TFH|Links}}'s teammates may also free him to save {{Plural|TFH|Heart}}. When defeated, {{Plural|TFH|Life Like}} drop a single {{Term|TFH|Heart}}.

There are various methods to destroy them, but one of the most effective is to throw a {{Term|TFH|Bomb|link}} into their mouth. This will kill them instantly. They can also be defeated with {{Plural|TFH|Arrow|link}}. This is a safe way to kill them from a distance. Lastly, they can be damaged with the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}, however, this is perhaps the least effective strategy, as it takes many hits to kill it this way, and the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} put themselves within easy swallowing distance while attacking.
