{{Term/Store|Flare Dancer|Flare Dancers|Series, OoT, OoT3D|plural}} are {{Plural|OoT3D|Sub-Boss|link}} in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 177}}</ref> They appear twice in the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}}. They are tall flaming humanoid beings with a black core stored within their chest, and as their name suggests, they dance while attacking their targets.

==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Flare Dancer}}<br/>Extinguish its flaming clothes first...
}}

The {{Term|OoT3D|Flare Dancer}} will frequently pirouette during its battle with {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}}, and the young hero will sustain damage if he comes in contact with it while it is ignited, or if he treads on the fire trail that is left as the {{Term|OoT3D|Sub-Boss}} circles the room. A {{Term|OoT3D|Flare Dancer}} is defeated by removing the core from its 'body' composed of fire, which can be done with the {{Term|OoT3D|Hookshot|link}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Megaton Hammer}}, or by throwing {{Plural|OoT3D|Bomb|link}}. Once the core is exposed, it will attempt to avoid {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} by running around the room; when blocked by {{Term|OoT3D|Link}}, it will quickly skid to a halt, and begin to run in the opposite direction. At this time the core may be damaged by any {{Term|OoT3D|Sword}} strike, though jump attacks are difficult to land due to the agility and mobility of the core. 

After {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} damages the core or does not attack it for a set amount of time, it will jump back into the flaming platform to restore its flaming body (accompanied by an eerie laugh), each time changing color from yellow/orange to blue/purple then finally to two shades of green. When a {{Term|OoT3D|Flare Dancer}} is defeated, the core rapidly expands and explodes, dropping {{Plural|OoT3D|Bomb}}. This explosion can damage {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} if he is close enough.

{{Term|OoT3D|Link}}'s first encounter with a {{Term|OoT3D|Flare Dancer}} activates a volcanic lift which leads to a new area of the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}} where the {{Term|OoT3D|Megaton Hammer|link}} is enshrined. The second encounter leads to a room where one of the imprisoned {{Plural|OoT3D|Goron|link}} and the {{Term|OoT3D|Big Key|link}} are found.

As revealed by an imprisoned {{Term|OoT3D|Goron|link}}, {{Plural|OoT3D|Flare Dancer}} are invulnerable to arrows while they are aflame but are vulnerable to {{Plural|OoT3D|Bomb}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a tip for rescuing me! Somewhere in this temple, you're sure to meet up with some creatures that dance as they attack. Arrows won't hurt them! Looks like you might need some of the Goron "special crop!" That's all I have to tell you!|Goron|OoT}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Plural|OoT (Himekawa)|Flare Dancer}} also make an appearance in the bonus story "''Rouru of the Watarara''" of the {{OoT|Himekawa}}. Two twin {{Plural|OoT (Himekawa)|Flare Dancer}} create a destructive tornado on {{Term|OoT (Himekawa)|Lake Hylia|link}}. With the help of {{Term|OoT (Himekawa)|Navi|link}}, {{Term|OoT (Himekawa)|Link}} flies into the tornado and defeats the two {{Plural|OoT3D|Flare Dancer}}. The tornado dissipates and {{Term|OoT (Himekawa)|Link}}, stuck in midair, begins to fall. When {{Term|OoT (Himekawa)|Navi}} and {{Term|OoT (Himekawa)|Rouru|link}}'s attempt to rescue {{Term|OoT (Himekawa)|Link}} fails, his mother, {{Term|OoT (Himekawa)|Elrora|link}}, intervenes and helps {{Term|OoT (Himekawa)|Rouru}} fly Link to safety.
{{Noncanon|End}}
