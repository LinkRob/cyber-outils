==Location and Uses==
A {{Term|FS|Hero's Key}} from the {{Plural|FS|Great Fairy|link}} of {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Forest|link|display=Forest}}, {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Ice|link|display=Ice}}, and {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Flame|link|display=Flame}} are required to enter the hero's door of {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace|link}}.<ref name= "Hero's Key"/>

They are obtained after first finishing the {{Term|FS|Golden Key|link}} door of {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace}}, then completing the {{Term|FS|Sea of Trees|link}}, {{Term|FS|Talus Cave|link}}, and {{Term|FS|Death Mountain|link}} {{Plural|FS|Stage|link}} each with a minimum of 5000 {{Plural|FS|Rupee|link}}.<ref name= "Hero's Key"/>

==Trivia==
*In {{FSAE|-}}, {{Term|FS|Rupee Fever|link}} isn't present within single-player mode. As only a limited number of sources exist on each randomly-generated map, obtaining enough {{Plural|FS|Rupee}} for a {{Term|FS|Hero's Key}} often requires repeatedly defeating respawning enemies.
