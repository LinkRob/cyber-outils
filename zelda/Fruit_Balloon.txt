{{Term/Store|Fruit Balloon|Fruit Balloons|Series, LCT|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|LCT}} {{Plural|Series|Fruit Balloon}} go unnamed in {{TP}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
{{Plural|TP|Fruit Balloon}} are balloons of varying sizes that [[Link]] must hit and pop in order to get points. [[Link]] can play [[Plumm]]'s {{Term|TPHD|Mini-Game|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Fruit-Pop-Flight|link}}, as a [[Wolf Link|wolf]] and score points for them. Link can also hit fruit balloons of the same type in a row to score more points than normal.

Hitting balloons of the same size in a row results in a score multiplier that doubles with each successive hit, but if Link interrupts his combo by hitting a balloon of a different size, his score multiplier will reset and balloons will go back to their original value.
