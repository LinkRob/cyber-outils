{{Term/Store|Jaggle||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/>

==Biography==
Jaggle is the father of [[Malo]] and [[Talo]] and the husband of [[Pergie]].<ref>{{Cite|I suppose I should be apologizing to you for the trouble Talo and Malo caused you yesterday... They're a mischievous pair...but they'll always be a pair of angels to their father and me.|Pergie|TP}}</ref> He is usually found on a rock formation overlooking the river in his hometown, [[Ordon Village]],<ref name="hubby"/> where he resides in his and {{Term|TPHD|Pergie}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Jaggle & Pergie's House|link|display=House}}. Jaggle teaches Link about [[Targeting System|targeting]],<ref>{{Cite|Yo, Link! Try talking to me from there with A while Z-targeting!|Jaggle|TP}}</ref> climbing [[vine]]s, and [[Hawk Grass]].<ref>{{Cite|See that grass growing there on the edge of that rock? Haven't I seen you whistling with that stuff? I figured it was pretty rare to see it growing in a place like that, so I thought I'd let you know.|Jaggle|TP}}</ref>

Jaggle does not allow his children to go into [[Faron Woods]] because it is dangerous; however, the two end up disobeying to chase after a [[monkey]].<ref>{{Cite|Um, Link... You're not gonna mention this to my dad, are you? He's always telling me to never, EVER go into the forest, 'cause he says it's dangerous...|Talo|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Talo and Malo went chasing like crazy people after that monkey. I can't keep up with those two...|Beth|TP}}</ref> After Talo is captured by the [[Bokoblin]]s and is then saved by Link, [[Colin]] tells [[Rusl]] what happened, so when Jaggle hears of it, he scolds the two of them.<ref>{{Cite|Link! My son told me Talo disappeared into the woods and had not returned. I came as soon as I heard...|Rusl|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! Colin! Way to spoil everything by telling your dad. Nice job.|Talo|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yeah, thanks a lot! We got scolded by our dad something awful. I thought it was never gonna end...|Malo|TP}}</ref>
