The {{Term/Store|Korgu Chideh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Korgu Chideh Shrine}}<br/>Korgu Chideh's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Korgu Chideh's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an [[Ancient Shrine]] in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh Shrine}} is located on top of {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|link}}, though it is initially buried beneath the ground. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first steps foot onto {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}}, he will hear the voice of the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh|link}}, who tells him he has relied on his equipment through his journey.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who has traveled to this island... I present you with a challenge. In your travels, you've relied on the equipment you've found along the way.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh}} says {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have to abandon his equipment,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Here, you must cast this equipment aside and face this trial with only your wits and whatever you can scavenge.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> and all of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} will disappear from his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Upon arriving on Eventide Island, a voice spoke of a challenge and then took all of your armor and weapons away.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> In order to access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must offer up the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} to the {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|display=Island}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Offer up the orbs to the three altars on this island. Only then will I acknowledge your skill and return your items.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Stranded on Eventide|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

There are three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} in total on {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}}. One {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} along the eastern edge of the {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|display=Island}}. A second {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} is tied around the neck of the {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} sleeping at the base of {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock}}, requiring {{Term|BotW|Link}} to either sneak it away or to defeat the {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}}. The third and final {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} is found on a platform in the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}} on {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock}}.

Three {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal}} with {{Plural|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} can also be found around {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}}. One is located off the northern edge of {{Term|BotW|Toronbo Beach|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} to bring an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} to it. Another {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} is located within the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}} on the southwestern part of the {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|display=Island}}. Finally, the last {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} is found in the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}} on {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock}}.

Once all three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} have been socketed, {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh Shrine}} will emerge. Since the Trial is complete, {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh}} will return all of Link's belongings.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You've done well to complete the trial. I will now return your things, as I promised.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Come, enter the shrine.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} steps into the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he is greeted by {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh}}, who informs him that he has already proven his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Korgu Chideh. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
There is no Trial within {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh Shrine}}, only a straight path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches, {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh}} will grant him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for arriving at the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Korgu Chideh|BotW}}</ref>
