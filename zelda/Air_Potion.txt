==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SSHD|Air Potion}} can be bought at the {{Term|SSHD|Potion Shop|link}} for 200 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}}. They can be stored in empty {{Plural|SSHD|Bottle|link}} and reduce the depletion time of the {{Term|SSHD|Oxygen Gauge|link}} for three minutes when drank.<ref name="SSHD Obtention"/>

{{Plural|SSHD|Air Potion}} can be upgraded into {{Plural|SSHD|Air Potion +|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Potion Shop|link}} by infusing them with various {{Plural|SSHD|Insect|link}}, avoiding completely the depletion of the {{Term|SSHD|Oxygen Gauge}} for three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|You got an {{Color|SSHD Gold|Air Potion +}}! This wonder potion keeps the oxygen gauge from depleting at all for three {{Color|SSHD Red|minutes}}!|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>
