The {{Term/Store|Ginner Woods||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Ginner Woods'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Ginner Woods are located to the west of [[Hateno Village]] in [[East Necluda]]. Inside Ginner Woods, [[Link]] can find {{Term|BotW|Meghyn|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Nat|link}}, a pair of sisters on the search for [[Hearty Truffle]]s.<ref>{{Cite|We're traveling all over to find hearty truffles. I know, I know—it's dangerous for two folks to travel on their own these days. You don't have to tell me twice! But when I think about how wonderful those truffles taste, all the worries and warnings melt away.|Nat|BotW}}</ref> However, the two are often being attacked by the monsters that lurk inside the Woods, and require Link's aid.

The Ginner Woods are separated from [[Midla Woods]] by a road from the south that leads to Hateno Village.

==Nomenclature==
The Ginner Woods derives their name from ''be'''ginner'''''. Its name is the first in a progression of names for the wooded areas surrounding [[Hateno Village]]; the second being [[Midla Woods]] (''middle'') and the third being [[Retsam Forest]] (''master'').
