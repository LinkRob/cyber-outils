{{Term/Store|Cima||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cima|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Cima}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} worker at {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable|link}} in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. She tends to the {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} that reside at the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} during the day. At night, she sleeps in one of the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}'s beds.<ref>{{Cite|Zzz...|Cima|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Cima}}, she complains about how nervous {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} makes her when it strikes the {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable|display=Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh dear, oh dear... {{Color|BotW Red|Lightning}} strikes this stable so often, I get nervous whenever it rains even a little.|Cima|BotW}}</ref> Since she can not figure out the reason, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to figure out why it is struck so often.<ref>{{Cite|I just don't understand why it strikes so often... Hey, it's a long shot, but if you have some time, could you find out why?|Cima|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cima}} will be happy if he accepts,<ref>{{Cite|You will?! Really? I'm so happy! I'm counting on you.|Cima|BotW}}</ref> which initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Thunder Magnet|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

On top of {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe|link}}. After taking it, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Cima}} to inform her of his discovery.<ref>{{Cite|What? You found out what was drawing the {{Color|BotW Red|lightning}} here?|Cima|BotW}}</ref> She realizes that the {{Term|BotW|Woodcutter's Axe|display=Axe}} must have been drawing the {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} to the {{Term|BotW|Lakeside Stable|display=Stable}} and wonders if it was put up there on purpose.<ref>{{Cite|Aha! Yeah, that's as good as a lightning rod, isn't it? I wonder if someone put it up there on purpose. How strange...|Cima|BotW}}</ref> She thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his help, giving him a {{Term|BotW|Rubber Helm|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for looking into it for me! Here's something for your trouble.|Cima|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cima}} is relieved to finally be able to relax.<ref>{{Cite|I can finally relax!|Cima|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

Afterwards, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Cima}}, she will ask if he has seen the {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall|link}} leading into {{Term|BotW|Lake Floria|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Did you check out the falls at Lake Floria? There are 10 different falls there, big and small!|Cima|BotW}}</ref> She claims each one is unique and worth seeing.<ref>{{Cite|Each one is unique and absolutely worth a look.|Cima|BotW}}</ref> While she is going to sleep, she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to wait for whatever he needs until tomorrow.<ref>{{Cite|Did you need something? Well, I'm going to sleep now. See you tomorrow.|Cima|BotW}}</ref>
