The {{Term/Store|Goron Gusto Shop||Series, BotW}} is a General Store in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|How's it goin'? This is the general store. Everyone here calls it the {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron Gusto Shop}}.|Tanko|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Goron Gusto Shop is a small General Store located in [[Goron City]]. It is run by the [[Goron]], [[Tanko]]. Their motto is "From the kitchen to the battlefield, we've got the right tool for you to wield!".<ref>{{Cite|Our motto is, "From the kitchen to the battlefield, we've got the right tool for you to wield!"|Tanko|BotW}}</ref> The Shop sells a small variety of items, including [[Material]]s and elemental [[Arrow]]s. 

As with all stores, Link can also sell his items to Tanko as well.
