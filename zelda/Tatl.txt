{{Term/Store|Tatl||Series, MM, MM3D}} is [[Link]]'s [[Link's Partners|Fairy companion]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244{{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> Much like [[Navi]] from {{OoT}}, Tatl assists Link by allowing him to interact with the environment through [[Targeting]] certain objects, people, and enemies. Unlike Navi, however, Tatl expresses herself with [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_Tatl_Listen4.wav bell-like noises] of varying repetition, whereas Navi shouts several catch phrases such as "''Hey!''", "''Listen!''", or "''Watch out!''". She is also the older sister of [[Tael]].

==Biography==
[[File:MM Link Tatl Artwork.png|left|184x184px]]
Prior to the events of {{MM|-}}, Tatl and her brother Tael were flying around [[Termina Field]] looking for shelter on a stormy day, when suddenly they find a lonely [[Skull Kid]] under a log, shivering. Tatl and her brother soon become close friends with him, and when the Skull Kid began to show his mischievous side, the two [[Fairy|fairies]] decided to follow through it: at the beginning of {{MM|-}}, they accompany the Skull Kid, now possessed by the [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]], to the [[Lost Woods]] seemingly in the outskirts of [[Hyrule]], where Tatl and Tael startle Link's horse, [[Epona]], causing the young hero to fall off unconscious, while the Skull Kid steals Link's [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]]. When Tael asks the Skull Kid to try to play the Ocarina himself, Tatl quickly scolds him, saying he will drop and break it due to his tiny size. When Link wakes up, Tatl escapes with Tael and the Skull Kid as they ride away on Epona, heading towards Termina. After the young [[Hero of Time]] is transformed into a [[Deku Link|Deku Scrub]], Tatl gets in his way and knocks him down to impede him from going after the Skull Kid (even [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MM/MM_Tatl_Raspberry.wav blowing raspberries] at him), which causes Tatl to get separated from both the Skull Kid and her brother. Although at first she blames Link for the turn of events,<ref name="blame">{{Cite|You! If I wasn't dealing with you, I wouldn't have gotten separated from my brother! Well, don't just sit there, Deku Boy! Do something!!! ...Why are you looking at me like that? What, is there something stuck on my face?" Will you stop staring and just open that door for me?!? Please!!! C'mon, a helpless, little girl is asking you... So hurry up!|Tatl|MM}}</ref> she decides to become Link's partner in order to be reunited with her brother and prevent the Skull Kid from destroying [[Termina]].<ref name="partner" />

Eventually, Link and Tatl find the Skull Kid atop the [[Clock Tower]], where Tael gives them a clue about how to stop the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]].<ref>{{Cite|Swamp. Mountain. Ocean. Canyon. Hurry...The four who are there... Bring them here...|Tael|MM}}</ref> The Skull Kid, however, suddenly and violently smacks Tael for speaking out of line, angering Tatl for harming her brother and betraying their friendship.<ref>{{Cite|Nooo!! What are you doing to my brother? Skull Kid, do you still think you're our friend after that?!!?|Tatl|MM}}</ref> When Link retrieves the Ocarina of Time from the Skull Kid and remembers about [[Princess Zelda]], Tatl snaps Link out of his mind and scolds the young hero for losing himself in memories in the heap of the Moon about to crash into Termina. But he plays the "[[Song of Time]]" to take him and his fairy sidekick back to the [[First Day]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite|Snap out of it! What are you doing lost in memories?!? Get yourself together! Getting that old ocarina back isn't gonna help us!!! Somebody! Anybody!!! Goddess of Time, help us please! We need more time!|Tatl|MM}}</ref> From then on, Tatl accompanies Link for the rest of his adventure and his subsequent confrontations with the Skull Kid, and also plays a part in preventing Termina from its impending [[Terminan Apocalypse|apocalypse]]. Once the duo manage to free the [[Four Giants]], Link and Tatl go up against the Skull Kid one last time, successfully halting the Moon from its path of destruction by calling the almighty deities. Although Tael tries to explain to his sister that the power of {{MM|Object}} was too great for the Skull Kid to handle, Tatl accounts it on him having a "weak will and no strength of heart," on top of being a "fool."<ref name=":1">{{Cite|It's 'cause he doesn't know his place! On top of having a weak will and no strength of heart...he's a fool!|Tatl|MM}}</ref> Shortly after, Majora's Mask detaches itself from the Skull Kid (breaking its control) and travels to the [[Moon (Object)#Inside the Moon|inside of the moon]] to possess it and consume Termina, prompting Link to go after it. Tatl refuses to follow him since she wants to stay with her brother,<ref>{{Cite|I won't do it! You go by yourself! I'm staying here with Tael.|Tatl|MM}}</ref> but when Tael says that he will go,<ref>{{Cite|I'll go!!!|Tael|MM}}</ref> Tatl changes her mind and decides to accompany Link to the moon.<ref name=":2">{{Cite|I understand. I'll go! After all this time, Tael, you've gotten really stubborn... Just like someone else I know...|Tatl|MM}}</ref> Together, the young hero and the fairy ultimately defeat Majora, freeing the Skull Kid from the mask's evil power.

Tatl is last seen during the [[New Day|ending sequence]] of the game, when, just when she rudely tells Link to leave and go on his business, as soon as the Hero of Time departs from Termina to search for Navi and part ways from her, Tael and the Skull Kid, Tatl suddenly becomes emotional and thanks him for helping her in their adventure,<ref>{{Cite|Link! ...Thank you.|Tatl|MM}}</ref> causing the tearful fairy to be comforted by her younger brother. As with Tael himself and the Skull Kid, it is unknown what happened to Tatl later into the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Child Timeline]].

==Personality==
[[File:MM Tatl apologizing to Link.gif|thumb|252x252px|Tatl apparently apologizing to Link at [[Woodfall Temple]] in ''Majora's Mask'']]
In contrast to [[Navi]], Tatl is much more tomboyish, frequently expresses her opinions and partakes an active role in several cutscenes, specifically when Link confronts the [[Skull Kid]] atop the [[Clock Tower]]. She is also relatively more informal than Link's previous [[Fairy]] companion, often speaking to him in a more straightforward manner.<ref name="partner">{{Cite|Good! So then it's settled! Now then, I'll be your partner... or at least until we catch that Skull Kid... My name is Tatl. So, uh, it's nice to meet you or whatever. Now that we've got that all straightened out, can we stop messing around and get moving?|Tatl|MM}}</ref> At the beginning of the game, Tatl is initially aggressive, blunt, and even hostile in her treatment of Link, blaming him for the fact that she got separated from Tael.<ref name="blame" /> She is also bossy toward her younger brother as well, behaving similarly to [[Princess Ruto]] (as a child) from ''Ocarina of Time'' and [[Tetra]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]].'' However, as ''Majora's Mask'' progresses, Tatl gradually modifies as she grows to tolerate his company, up to the point where she apologizes for what happened to his horse and even admits that she admires Link for his acts of bravery at the end of the game.<ref>{{Cite|All that stuff I did to you... ...your horse... I...I apologize. ...Sorry. There! I apologized! So don't hold it against me, got it?|Tatl|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You never want to give up. You always want to try. I admire you!|Tatl|MM}}</ref> Although Tatl provides less information than Navi when facing [[Enemy|enemies]], the details she does provide is generally more direct and less formal than Navi's. When questioned about an enemy from ''Ocarina of Time'' (such as a [[Skulltula]], [[Dodongo]], etc.), she tends to react with surprise, then criticizes Link for his ignorance,<ref>{{Cite|What?!  Don't you know about the Dodongo? It moves slowly, so you can circle around to its backside. Got that?|Tatl|MM}}</ref> though this trait was removed in {{MM3D}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's a '''Dodongo'''! It moves slowly, so you should be able to circle around to its backside. Got that?|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*If [[Link]] decides to stop the [[Moon]]'s descent again after completing the game, Tatl stops [[Tael]] in the middle of his speech about [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]] controlling [[Skull Kid]] saying she already knows, breaking the 4th wall.<ref>{{Cite|"Yeah, yeah. I know... The Skull Kid was lonely 'cause nobody would be his friend. Blah, blah, blah... And the power of the mask made him do it 'cause it was too much for him to handle..."|Tatl|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|"How? ...How did you know that, sis?"|Tael|MM}}</ref>
*Tatl's tomboyish or stern personality has received criticism among fans, as some view her as self-centered or mean compared to [[Navi]], the [[Hero of Time]]'s previous [[Fairy]] [[Link's Partners|partner]].<ref name="blame" /><ref name="partner" /><ref name=":0" /><ref name=":1" /><ref name=":2" />
*Tatl and her brother also seem to be well known around [[Clock Town]], at least with the [[Bombers' Notebook|Bombers]]. After Link receives the [[Bombers' Notebook]] from [[Jim]], he directly addresses Tatl, telling her to inform the Skull Kid that he is no longer a member of the Bombers.<ref>{{Cite|Tatl, you tell that Skull Kid he's outta here! He's been actin' like a real jerk lately!|Jim|MM}}</ref> Also, if Link addresses a Bomber as a [[Goron Link|Goron]] or a [[Zora Link|Zora]], he asks, "''Why is Tatl with you?''"<ref>{{Cite|Oh! A Goron! Do you live in the mountains? Why is Tatl with you? Why? Huh? Why?|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! A Zora! Do you live in the ocean? What's it like? Why is Tatl with you? Why? Huh? Why?|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|MM}}</ref>[[File:MM Tatl attacking Sakon.gif|thumb|264x264px|Tatl in a red color, attacking Sakon in ''Majora's Mask'']]
*If Link talks to [[Sakon]] in [[Ikana Canyon]], who attempts to steal his [[Sword]], Tatl will turn red and stop the thief from doing so, making angry bell sounds.
**Interestingly, this was originally an idea planned for Navi in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' when she sees an [[enemy]] or something dangerous otherwise but was scrapped in the final release, as the Fairy instead turns yellow in a similar color to Tatl's.
*Although her behavior in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (Himekawa)|Akira Himekawa manga adaptation of ''Majora's Mask'']] is identical to the original game, in the ending when Link parts ways with her, Tael, and Skull Kid, she is shown to be much friendlier to Link, eagerly saying thank you and wishing she would see him again.
*In the ''Majora's Mask'' Himekawa manga, Tatl is afraid of the [[Fierce Deity Link]] transformation, due to its tremendous raw power and fearsome appearance. Despite this, she still intends to be Link's partner no matter what form he is in.

==Nomenclature==
Along with her younger brother, [[Tael]], her name stems from the term "Tattletale" pertaining to her nature as an informant, particularly of the enemies encountered in the game.
