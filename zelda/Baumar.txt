{{Term/Store|Baumar||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Baumar|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Baumar}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who travels with his {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bamboo|link}},<ref name="Horse"/><ref name="Bamboo">{{Cite|Just hit the northern passage from here, man, and you'll find {{Color|BotW Blue|Serenne Stable}}. Let's kick it, Bamboo!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> while carrying a {{Term|BotW|Fisherman's Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}}. The pair travel a portion of the {{Term|BotW|Hilltop High Road|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Baumar}} was a child, he almost got lost up in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, man, I just, like...had a total flashback! When I was just a kid, I almost got lost up on the snowy mountains...|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> While a {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link|display=shield surfer}} saved him, {{Term|BotW|Baumar}} has trouble recall his name.<ref>{{Cite|But I was saved by this totally wicked {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfer}}! His name? It was Fa—Falma... Oh man... I totally forgot.|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Falmark|link}} was the shield surfer, and he claims that surfing down the mountain with a child on his back was difficult.<ref>{{Cite|When I was about your age, I saved a child lost in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}}. I tell ya what, fella. {{Color|BotW Red|Shield surfing}} down the mountain with that kid on my back was tougher than I expected!|Falmark|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Baumar}} shows a lot of appreciation for {{Term|BotW|Bamboo}}. He refers to his {{Term|BotW|Horse}} with a lot of affectionate terms, such as "totally gnarly", "radical", and "most excellent".<ref>{{Cite|All right, Bamboo, my totally gnarly and radical horse! We're finally ready to hit the mountain for some {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Snowfield">{{Cite|All right, Bamboo, my most excellent horse! First to {{Color|BotW Blue|Snowfield Stable}}, and then to the mountain for {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}! Heeeeyaahh!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> He also offers directions as the two of them travel.<ref name="Bamboo"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} overhears him talking to {{Term|BotW|Bamboo}}, he will explain that he is not good at conversing with people.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Oh, sorry, man. I was talking to my horse, Bamboo. I'm not, like, all that great at conversations with people.|Baumar|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Baumar}} enjoys {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}}, finding it difficult to go long times without doing so.<ref>{{Cite|This is such a drag, dude... I just wanna get back to {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}. I miss that fresh powder!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, man, I miss the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}} something fierce.<br/>The {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}} there is just radical!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> His plan for his journey is to stop at {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}}, at which point he will head to the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains}} to {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|display=Shield Surf}}.<ref name="Snowfield"/> Ocassionaly, he will say that he has no time to chat with {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|First, we gotta hit up {{Color|BotW Blue|Snowfield Stable}}. So let's get a move on! Heeeyaah— Huh? Oh, sorry, dude. No time to chat!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> He claims to be talented, though he describes his process as climbing the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|display=Mountains}} and {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|display=Surfing}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm pretty wicked at {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}, man. Actually I'm, like, totally wicked at it. I just climb up {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}} and surf.|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> When he makes it to {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Baumar}} is excited to head up the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains}} and finally go {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}}.<ref>{{Cite|I finally made it to {{Color|BotW Blue|Snowfield Stable}}! {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}}, I'm comin' for you, dude! You shall taste the flat of my shield and my gnarly {{Color|BotW Red|shield-surfing}} skills!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref>

On the way to the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains}}, {{Term|BotW|Baumar}} mentally prepares himself for {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}}, and he becomes annoyed if {{Term|BotW|Link}} interrupts him.<ref>{{Cite|Not cool, dude. You interrupted my mental preparations! If I wanna be the best {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfer}}, I need to plan ahead!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> The legendary {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|display=Shield Surfer}}, {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}}, supposedly had all of her routes memorized.<ref>{{Cite|They say the legendary shield surfer {{Color|BotW Blue|Selmie}} has all the routes memorized. She's intense, man!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> There is a rumor {{Term|BotW|Baumar}} has heard that {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} is waiting on the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains}} for a worthy opponent.<ref>{{Cite|Dude, apparently she's just waiting up on the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}} for a worthy {{Color|BotW Red|shield-surfing}} opponent!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Dude, I heard the legendary {{Color|BotW Red|shield-surfer}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Selmie}} is just waiting on the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}} for a worthy opponent!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref>

If he is caught in the rain, {{Term|BotW|Baumar}} will be disappointed disappointed that he can not shield surf.<ref>{{Cite|This weather is totally bogus, dude. I just want it to clear up so I can get back to {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> While it is storming or snowing, {{Term|BotW|Baumar}} wonders if professional shield surfers head out in this type of weather, confessing he would break his neck if he wiped out.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, man, do professional shield surfers surf on days like this? If I wiped out, I'd break my neck! Bogus, dude...|Baumar|BotW}}</ref>

Occasionally, {{Term|BotW|Baumar}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}} tip. For example, depending on the type of {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}} used, one's surfing will be different.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, man, check it. Did you know that depending on the shield, your surfing can be totally different? What am I talking about? I'm talking about {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}, man! Get with it, dude!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> He knows of a {{Term|BotW|Shield}} that is supposed to be good for {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}}, which is the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Shield|link}}, but he can not remember what it is called.<ref>{{Cite|Speaking of, I heard about a shield that's supposed to be wicked for surfing. It's called a kni— A knight's... Oh man. I totally forgot. What a drag, dude.|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> Additionally, he has heard of the {{Term|BotW|Hylian Shield|link}} within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, which is supposed to be durable.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently, there's a totally durable shield called a {{Color|BotW Red|Hylian shield}} somewhere in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. Sounds gnarly, dude!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref>
