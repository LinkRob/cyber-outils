The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Gear|Gerudo Gear|Series, BotW}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear}} is weaponry crafted by the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo|link}} and used by both travelers and soldiers.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gerudo Shield}}<br/>The design of this metal shield has changed over time to match the Gerudo's sword-and- shield fighting style. It's favored by soldiers and travelers alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It is characterized by its highly ornate designs.<ref name="Moonlight Scimitar">{{Cite|{{Big|Moonlight Scimitar}}<br/>Delicate Gerudo carvings decorate this curved sword. The engraved blade is extremely sharp. Apparently it once served ceremonial purposes in festivals.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Golden Bow}}<br/>This Gerudo-made bow is popular for the fine ornamentations along its limbs. Designed for hunting and warfare alike, this bow was engineered to strike distant targets.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Radiant Shield}}<br/>This extravagant shield is presented to Gerudo warriors who rise to the rank of captain. Its apparent opulence is rivaled only by its combat capabilities.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}} swords are distinguished by their distinctive curved blades.<ref name="Moonlight Scimitar"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gerudo Scimitar}}<br/>This common sword is often carried by Gerudo women for self-defense. Its short, curved blade is easily recognized.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

All forms of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear}} are constructed with metal, meaning they will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, pieces of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear}} will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
