The {{Term/Store|Special Delivery to Mama||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Special Delivery to Mama}} was referred to as the '''Express Mail to Mama''' in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|You were given {{Color|MM Red|express mail to Mama}}. This is priority mail, so hurry up and deliver it!|N/A|MM}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
Although it is used only in the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest, the Special Delivery to Mama enables [[Link]] to obtain two items within the game.

During the [[Final Day]], the Curiosity Shop Back Room will unlock at 1 PM. When Link enters, the [[Man from Curiosity Shop|Man from the Curiosity Shop]] reveals that [[Kafei]] went to [[Sakon's Hideout]] to get the [[Couple's Mask#Sun's Mask|Sun Mask]] back,<ref>{{Cite|A customer came to my shop last night... Now Kafei sees him, and Kafei's color just changes and he goes runnin' after the guy. The guy's a regular... A greedy thief named Sakon. I think he's from Ikana Village...|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM}}</ref> and he had left a letter to [[Madame Aroma]], his mother.<ref>{{Cite|Now Kafei... He says he wants you to take this to his mother.|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM}}</ref> The letter can either be delivered to Madame Aroma personally for a [[Bottle]] filled with [[Chateau Romani]],<ref>{{Cite|Oh dear...Priority mail? This!!! It's from Kafei! Correct? Correct? Wonderful! You really are an expert! Yes, yes, I'm sorry. My thanks. Yes, yes, it is your job, after all. I'm sorry. At this point in time, I can give you only something like this.|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Chateau Romani/Milk Bar/Thanks for delivering letter to mom|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> or be given to the [[Postman]] to receive the [[Postman's Hat]].<ref>{{Cite|I have decided to flee. It is an order from the Postmaster. I am now free! I can set my own schedule!|Postman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Postman's Hat/Town: Near Milk Bar/Thanks for enabling him to flee|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> The letter can also be given to {{Term|MM3D|??? (Majora's Mask)|link}} in the [[Stock Pot Inn]] when he asks for paper, thus earning Link a [[Piece of Heart]], though other letters can be used as well.

In {{MM3D}}, giving the mail directly to Madame Aroma a second time will earn Link a [[Fishing Hole Pass]].
