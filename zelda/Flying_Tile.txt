{{Term/Store|Flying Tile|Flying Tiles|Series, LA, LADX|plural}},{{Exp Game|LA}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 101}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Flying Floor|Flying Floors|FS|plural}},{{Exp Game|FS}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|game= ALttP&FS|publisher= Prima|page= 176}}</ref> and {{Term/Store|Crazy Floor Tile|Crazy Floor Tiles|OoT|plural}},{{Exp Game|OoT}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|game= CE|publisher= Nintendo|page= 76 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref> are recurring traps in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|ALttP, OoA, OoS, FSA, ALBW}} {{Plural|Series|Flying Tile}} go unnamed in {{OoT3D}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|Series|Flying Tile}} commonly appear as possessed floor tiles that rise up from the ground and hurl themselves at {{Term|Series|Link|link}}, causing damage on impact. They fly forward in a straight line and break upon impact with anything.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Stub|ALttP}}
In {{ALttP}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Flying Tile}} appear in {{Plural|ALttP|Dungeon|link}} with {{Plural|ALttP|Door|link}} that only open once every {{Term|ALttP|Flying Tile}} is destroyed. To protect against {{Plural|ALttP|Flying Tile}}, {{Term|ALttP|Link}} can use the {{Term|ALttP|Cane of Somaria|link}} to put up temporary barriers to block against the traps.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Stub|LA}}
In {{LA}}, {{Plural|LANS|Flying Tile}} commonly appear in the {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine|link}} and are the primary attack method of {{Term|LANS|Facade|link}}.

{{Manual|EoW|Flying Tile|This magical floor tile flies at any who come near. If you stand on top of it, you can briefly fly with it.<ref name="EoW Flying Tile">{{Cite|This magical floor tile flies at any who come near. If you stand on top of it, you can briefly fly with it.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Flying Tiles are enemy tiles found in the [[Gerudo Desert]] and [[Gerudo Sanctum]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]] after scanning one before it breaks. When summoned, they cost three Echo energy. They are required to complete [[The Flying Tile]] [[Side Quest]].
