{{Term/Store|Molida Island||Series, PH, BotW}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 113 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|'''Molida Island'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
==={{PH|-}}===
{{Term|PH|Molida Island}} is an {{Term|PH|Island|link}} in the {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea|link}} in {{PH}}. The southern portion of the {{Term|PH|Molida Island|display=Island}} is populated, whereas the northern territory is a wild grove. The {{Term|PH|Wayfarer's Words|link}} can be found scattered throughout the {{Term|PH|Molida Island|display=Island}}, which many of the inhabitants find to be a nuisance.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, just look at this stone tablet! The guy who lives nearby, {{Color|PH Blue|Romanos}}, had a father who scattered these here. They're left as a testament to his trip to the {{Color|PH Blue|Isle of Gust}}, but they're a big nuisance!|Potato|PH}}</ref> The {{Term|PH|Shovel|link}} can be found in a cave on the {{Term|PH|Molida Island|display=Island}}, which is required for finding a safe route through the foggy passage in the {{Term|PH|Northwestern Sea|link}}. {{Term|PH|Molida Island}} also bears the {{Term|PH|Temple of Courage|link}}, which can be found on the back of the isle. To access it, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} needs a {{Term|PH|Salvage Arm|link}} from {{Term|PH|Eddo|link}} on {{Term|PH|Cannon Island|link}}, as {{Term|PH|Link}} needs the {{Term|PH|Sun Key|link}}, which is hidden underwater. Before {{Term|PH|Link}} can return to {{Term|PH|Molida Island}} with the {{Term|PH|Salvage Arm}}, a {{Term|PH|Giant Eye Plant|link}} will appear and guard the {{Term|PH|Molida Island|display=Island}}.

{{Term|PH|Molida Island}} is also where {{Term|PH|Link}} will find the {{Term|PH|Archery Game|link}}, which is unlocked after clearing the {{Term|PH|Temple of Courage}}. In addition, three small portions of land east of the {{Term|PH|Molida Island|display=Island}} can be accessed by blowing up a {{Term|PH|Wooden Box|link}}, or by making a ropebridge between the wooden box and the stone sign, retrieving the {{Term|PH|Cucco|link}} within with the {{Term|PH|Grappling Hook|link}}, then using the {{Term|PH|Cucco}} to fly there. With the help of the {{Term|PH|Shovel}}, a {{Term|PH|Treasure Map|link}} can be unveiled from the ground at the roots of a tree on the second small island. On the smaller island to the left of the treasure map, there is a smaller island that has a Big Red Rupee which can be obtained by using the shovel in the middle of the island.

Two {{Plural|PH|Big Green Rupee|link}} can be found in patches of dirt inside the {{Term|PH|Old Wayfarer}}'s hideaway. A third {{Term|PH|Big Green Rupee}} can be found by digging just under the {{Term|PH|Tree}} south of {{Term|PH|Romanos}} and {{Term|PH|Maronie|link}}'s house.
