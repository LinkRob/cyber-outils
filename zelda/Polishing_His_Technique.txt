{{Term/Store|Polishing His Technique||HWAoC}} is a level 53 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Polishing His Technique|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Polishing His Technique}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can only be completed by {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Flint|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within six minutes. Most of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} scattered around the map are {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, who also defend an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} near the center of the map. Other {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} found around the map include {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Pebblit|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|limk}}. Once 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, a gate opens at the bottom of the map to a pair of {{Plural|HWAoC|Igneo Talus|link}}. {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}} is given three minutes to defeat them, and when he approaches them, {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}} appears to help him.
