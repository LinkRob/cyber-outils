The {{Term/Store|Talus Peaks||Series, OoA}} are a mountain range found in northwestern [[Labrynna]] in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 255}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
[[File:OoA ChangingTalusFlow.gif|thumb|left|{{Term|OoA|Link}} changing the flow of water on {{Term|OoA|Talus Peaks}}]]
The {{Term|OoA|Talus Peaks}} are the largest geographical area in all of Labrynna. Although present in both time eras of the story, Talus Peaks is shown to be receded in the present age; much of the eastern face of past era's Talus Peaks is simply [[Nuun Highlands]] in the present era. Traversing the mountain range requires constant time-warping even after short distances, since the landscape shuffles inaccessible points between the two time periods.<ref>{{Cite|Time's flow can change the land.|Owl Statue|OoA}}</ref> [[Symmetry Village]], home to the [[Skull Dungeon]], is nestled into the Talus Peaks. Talus Peaks can be accessed from the foothills west of [[Lynna City]], or by crossing the bridge from the [[Nuun Highlands]] once it has been built. The [[Restoration Wall]], home to [[Patch]], is also found within the mountain range. [[Darknut]]s, [[Leever]]s, [[River Zora]]s, [[Moblin]]s and [[Tektite]]s are all enemies that can be found along the mountain range.

The {{Term|OoA|Talus Lake|link}}, found at the very top of the mountains, is the area's only notable landmark. Water can be seen cascading from the Talus Lake, forming pools at various points further down the mountain, finally flowing into one larger pool near the base of the peaks. In the past, there exists a device partway down the mountain that allows [[Link]] to direct the flow of the water towards the left or the right, allowing him to navigate the peaks. 

The only {{Term|OoA|Human|link}} inhabitant of Talus Peaks is [[Tokkey]], a man who is researching a tune that moves time forward.<ref>{{Cite|I am Tokkey. I research time. After many years of research, I'm one step away from perfecting a tune that lets one move forward in time, but it seems I've hit a roadblock. "Echoes produce waves..." What could it mean?|Tokkey|OoA}}</ref> His home is found inside a secret grotto, which Link can access by diving into the Talus Lake and swimming under a rock wall underwater.

===Better Bomb Bag===
An unnamed red {{Term|OoA|Fairy|link}} resides in a pool in the southwestern corner of {{Term|OoA|Talus Peaks}} in the present. To meet the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}}, {{Term|OoA|Link}} must set the flow of water eastward and push the {{Term|OoA|Vine|link}} bulb to the western side in the past. Using the {{Term|OoA|Tune of Currents|link}} or {{Term|OoA|Tune of Ages|link}} to return to the present, he can then climb the {{Plural|OoA|Vine}} up to the pool. A {{Term|OoA|Sign|link}} next to it warns not to throw anything into the water.<ref>{{Cite|Do not throw anything into the water.|Sign|OoA}}</ref> If {{Term|OoA|Link}} throws in a {{Term|OoA|Bomb|link}} and approaches the pool between the two statues, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} appears and asks him whether he threw in a {{Term|OoA|Golden Bomb|link}} or a {{Term|OoA|Silver Bomb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Did you drop a {{Color|OoA Red|Golden Bomb}}? Or a {{Color|OoA Red|Silver Bomb}}?|Fairy|OoA}}</ref>

* If {{Term|OoA|Link}} answers <code>Golden</code>, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} gives him two opportunities to change his answer.<ref>{{Cite|...Really? A {{Color|OoA Red|Golden Bomb}}?|Fairy|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Really and truly a {{Color|OoA Red|Golden Bomb}}???|Fairy|OoA}}</ref> If he re-affirms his lie, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} teaches him a lesson by depleting his {{Term|OoA|Life Energy|link}} to a single {{Term|OoA|Heart|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Liar! Greed begets sorrow!|Fairy|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hope you've learned not to lie.{{Icon|OoA Heart}}|Fairy|OoA}}</ref>
* If {{Term|OoA|Link}} answers <code>Silver</code>, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} asks for confirmation only once.<ref>{{Cite|...Really? A {{Color|OoA Red|Silver Bomb}}?|Fairy|OoA}}</ref> It will punish {{Term|OoA|Link}} for lying by confiscating all his {{Plural|OoA|Bomb}}.<ref>{{Cite|Liar! I'm confiscating your {{Color|OoA Red|Bombs}}!|Fairy|OoA}}</ref>
* If {{Term|OoA|Link}} answers <code>A regular one</code>, the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} rewards his honesty by increasing his {{Term|OoA|Bomb}}-carrying capacity by 20.<ref>{{Cite|You are an honest person. I'll increase the number of {{Color|OoA Red|Bombs}} you can carry to reward you. Hee-yah!|Fairy|OoA}}</ref>

If {{Term|OoA|Link}} is punished by the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} for lying, he must leave the area then return and throw in another {{Term|OoA|Bomb}} to restart the interaction. The {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} disappears permanently once it has granted the upgrade. 

==Trivia==
*The interaction with the {{Term|OoA|Fairy}} at {{Term|OoA|Talus Peaks}} was replicated in {{TMC}} with the {{Term|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Did you throw the {{Color|TMC Red|Golden Bomb}} into the spring? Or the {{Color|TMC Red|Silver Bomb}}?|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You're lying, aren't you? As punishment for your lie, I will reduce your number of bombs to zero.|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You are honest. I must reward such an honest adventurer with some of my power.|Great Mayfly Fairy|TMC}}</ref> {{ALttP}} first introduced the concept of throwing an item into a {{Term|ALttP|Great Fairy|link}}'s fountain and receiving an upgrade for answering honestly.<ref>{{Cite|You are an honest person. I like you. I will give you something important... These are the Silver Arrows. To deal the final stroke to Ganon, you definitely need them! I know I don't quite have the figure of a fairy... Ganon's cruel power is to blame! You must defeat Ganon!|Cursed Fairy|ALttP|GBA version}}</ref>
*In the past, a citizen of [[Symmetry Village]] reports hearing music flowing down from the mountaintops,<ref>{{Cite|Sometimes I hear music flowing down from the mountaintop, but I wonder why.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> which in fact comes from Tokkey.
*The choice between a golden, silver or common item lost in water is a reference to Aesop's fable "The Honest Woodcutter" who has a similar interaction with the god Hermes after losing his axe in a stream and is rewarded him for his honesty in the same way.
