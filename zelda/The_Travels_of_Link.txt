{{Term/Store|The Travels of Link||Series, LADX}} is an object in {{LA|DX}}.<ref>{{Cite|'The Travels of Link ' Do you want to look at your album?|N/A|LADX}}</ref> It does not appear in {{LA|NS}}.

==Location and Uses==
The album is a compilation of the photos taken by the [[Photographer]] throughout Link's quest, nearly half of which are taken in [[Mabe Village]]. The album is found in the [[Camera Shop]] after the photographer takes the first picture of Link in the shop.<ref>{{Cite|What's your name, young man? Link ? Well here's your album, Link . Give it a look before you leave! |Photographer|LADX}}</ref> Afterwards, Link may encounter various events in game where the photographer returns to take another photograph.<ref>{{Cite|Let's see if we can fill that album! |Photographer|LADX}}</ref> The album can be obtained at anytime except when [[Marin]], [[Madam MeowMeow]]'s [[BowWow]], or the [[Flying Rooster]] is following him.<ref>{{Cite|See me later, when you're alone! |Photographer|LADX}}</ref> There are several photographs that can only be taken at certain times, and will be [[Permanently Missable Items|impossible to obtain if Link misses them]], whereas the others are available at anytime.

If Link does not get his first photograph taken at the shop, any moments where he would normally encounter a photo event would be ignored until Link gets his album. The exception to this is the photo of Link [[Thievery|stealing]] from the [[Town Tool Shop]]; if Link steals an item from the shop before going to the Camera Shop, the picture automatically appears in the album when it is made. 

There are a total of 13 photographs, but only 12 can be taken. Link's first photograph also has an alternate version, but only one or the other can be obtained. Photos 2, 3, and 4 are only available when Marin is accompanying Link. Photo 10 is only available before Link has collected all 5 [[Golden Leaves]] and opened [[Kanalet Castle]] gate. Afterwards, these photos can no longer be obtained. The following is a table containing all 13 photos and how to obtain them.

==Trivia==
* Cutscenes occur before the photographs 2, 4, 6, 10, and 11 are taken. They are viewed in first person from the Photographer's perspective. If Link is wearing the [[Red Clothes|red]] or [[Blue Clothes|blue clothes]], the color changes accordingly in these cutscenes.
* The photograph of Link shoplifting from the Town Tool Shop depicts readable Japanese text. The sign of the left reads "万引防止" ("''manbiki boushi''"), which means "shoplifting prevention"; and the sign on the right reads "万引は犯罪です" ("''manbiki wa hanzai desu''"), which means, "Shoplifting is a crime."
