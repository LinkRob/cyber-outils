{{Term/Store|Hestu's Training||HWAoC}} is a level 22 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hestu's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Hestu's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Acorn|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within eight minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Soldier|link|display=Hylian Soldiers}}, except for a single {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}}. When {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}} reaches 50 and 90 KOs, a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} will spawn alongside more {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Soldier|display=Hylian Soldiers}}. Many {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Soldier|display=Hylian Soldiers}} also spawn as {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}} gets closer to reaching the objective. After 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are killed, {{Term|HWAoC|Impa|link}} spawns. The time limit is also taken away, so the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}} is defeated.
