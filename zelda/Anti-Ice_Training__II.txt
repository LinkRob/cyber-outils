{{Term/Store|Anti-Ice Training: II||HWAoC}} is a level 25 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Ice Training: II|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Ice Training: II}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Chillshroom|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Cool Safflina|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Chillfin Trout|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat four {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} within five minutes, although only one is present at first. After the first one is defeated, the others spawn. After they are all defeated, the gate behind them opens, the timer resets to three minutes, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus}} spawns alongside nine {{Plural|HWAoC|Frost Pebblit|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus}} is defeated.

A {{Term|HWAoC|Korok|link}} can also be found at the south end of the map, who awards the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} with a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}} when found.
