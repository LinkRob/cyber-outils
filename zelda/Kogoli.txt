{{Term/Store|Kogoli||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Kogoli}}
{{Term|TWWHD|Kogoli}} was born on {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island|link}}. He can be found atop the wooden platforms on one of the higher levels of {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Island}} during the day, while he moves inside the {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center|link}} after nightfall. When spoken to, he comments on the damage that was done to the {{Term|TWWHD|Wind Shrine|link}} on the island, as well as the wind god {{Term|TWWHD|Cyclos|link}}' resulting anger and its destruction.

Strangely, {{Term|TWWHD|Kogoli}} disappears without explanation after {{Term|TWWHD|Medli|link}} is awakened as the {{Term|TWWHD|Sage of Earth|link}}, never to be seen again.
