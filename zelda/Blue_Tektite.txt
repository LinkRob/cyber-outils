==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
Blue Tektites are common {{Plural|FSA|Enemy|link}} found throughout {{TLoZ}}. They hop around annoyingly and the most efficient way of killing them is by using the {{Term|FSA|Boomerang|link}} to stun them and to stab them using any of the three types of {{Plural|FSA|Sword|link}} in the game. They can hop around anywhere, meaning they can jump on walls that are impassable to {{Term|FSA|Link|link}} and cannot be interacted with until it jumps off the wall again. When defeated, they can drop either a Heart, one {{Term|FSA|Rupee|link}}, five {{Plural|FSA|Rupee|link}} or a {{Term|FSA|Clock|link}}. They have a collision damage of half a heart.

==Other Appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Plural|CoH|Blue Tektite}} appear as enemies in {{CoH}} where they move to the beat of the music. They are a stronger variant of {{Term|CoH|Tektite|link}}, and possess a unique attack pattern as they move every two beats, crouching for one beat before jumping on the next beat. However, they can only move diagonally and possess a short jump, being only able to hop onto short cliffs and other objects one block at a time.
