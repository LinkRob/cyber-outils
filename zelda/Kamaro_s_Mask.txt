{{Term/Store|Kamaro's Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref> It resembles [[Kamaro]].

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's {{Color|MM Red|Kamaro's Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>That is a fine mask. It is filled with the joy a teacher has in finding a good student.</p>}}

Kamaro's Mask is given to [[Link]] by the spirit of [[Kamaro]]. Kamaro appears in [[Termina Field]] near the [[Snowhead]] area on top of a mushroom-like tree. He appears between midnight and 4 AM on the [[First Day|First]] or [[Second Day]], where some music can be heard.<ref>{{Cite|The spirit of a charismatic dancer who died in Termina Field dances there night after night.|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> Kamaro is afraid that his dance moves will never be passed down to the new generation, and asks that Link rest his soul.<ref>{{Cite|I am no longer part of the living...My sadness to the moon... I haven't left my dance to the world...I am filled with regret. (Translation) I am disappointed, oh moon. I have died! Oh, I planned to bring the world together and stir it into a giant melting pot with my dance! If only I had taught my new dance to someone...|Kamaro|MM}}</ref> By playing the "[[Song of Healing]]", his musical talent will be encased inside the Mask. He entrusts the hero with his final request, to spread his dance across the world.<ref>{{Cite|Spread my dance across the world... Train its followers... (Translation) I have taught it to you, now make it into a popular dance craze! I am counting on you. (Translation) I am counting on you.|Kamaro|MM}}</ref>

The Mask, when worn, allows Link to perform Kamaro's dance. While in [[West Clock Town]] at night, Link can find the [[Rosa Sisters]] trying to come up with a dance for the [[Carnival of Time]]. If Link stands in front of them and performs the dance, they will learn it and thank him, calling Link "master" and giving him a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for teaching us those steps. You are our master! Master! You're so cool!|Rosa Sisters|MM}}</ref>
