"{{Term/Store|Flown the Coop||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Flown the Coop}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Kakariko Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has spoken to {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} for the first time, {{Term|BotW|Cado|link}} will begin his regular schedule. During the day, he can be found standing in front of his fenced in yard, wondering how something could have happened.<ref>{{Cite|How could this happen...|Cado|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} reveals that several of his {{Plural|BotW|Cucco|link}} have managed to escape their pen.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Sir... Sir Link... My Cuccos...}} {{Big|My precious Cuccos... They haven't come home yet!}}|Cado|BotW}}</ref> His {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} are his only emotional support after his wife {{Term|BotW|Rola|link}} left him.<ref>{{Cite|Now that my wife wants nothing to do with me, my Cuccos are my only emotional support...|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He cries about what he should do.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Waaaah... What do I doooo...}} {{Big|AAAARRRRGH!!}}|Cado|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can offer to retrieve the {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} for {{Term|BotW|Cado}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll go find them!] Really? {{Big|Thank you so much!!}}|Cado|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cado}} notes that there are ten {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} altogether and hopes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find them.<ref>{{Cite|I have {{Color|BotW Red|10}} Cuccos total. Hopefully you'll be able to find them all...|Cado|BotW}}</ref> Any time {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Cado}} without all of the {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} in their pen, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} will remind him of how many he needs and tells him how many have returned.<ref>{{Cite|I should have {{Color|BotW Red|10}} Cuccos total. Even just one unaccounted for won't do! Sadly, only 3 have returned to me.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He stresses over the thought of any of his {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} being gone and begs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to do his best in finding them.<ref>{{Cite|To think that somewhere out there, even one of my poor Cuccos could be lost and lonely... Please! Hurry up and bring them back to me! {{Small|For their sake...and for mine, as well...}}|Cado|BotW}}</ref> Once he is done talking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

All of the {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} can be found in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, with three of them remaining in the pen where they belong. One has flown up onto the hill and is loitering near {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg Shrine|link}}, just above {{Term|BotW|Cado}}'s home. Another is standing beside the {{Term|BotW|Common Cooking Area|link}}. One {{Term|BotW|Cucco}} has roosted on the roof of {{Term|BotW|Enchanted|link}}, across the road from {{Term|BotW|Cado}}'s house. Yet another is perched on the roof of a wooden structure in front of {{Term|BotW|Nanna|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lasli|link|display=Lasli's}} house. The eighth is trotting about the {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} in front of the village's {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}}. Number nine is situated in another of these structures next to {{Term|BotW|Steen|link|display=Steen's}} {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}} patch. Finally, the last {{Term|BotW|Cucco}} is pecking around in {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|link}}. This last {{Term|BotW|Cucco}} is tricky to obtain, as {{Term|BotW|Mellie|link}} will immediately scold anyone for trespassing in her garden.<ref>{{Cite|So long as I'm around, there's no way I'll let you mess with my plum garden!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> It can only be grabbed when she goes home for the night.

Once all of the {{Plural|BotW|Cucco}} are accounted for, {{Term|BotW|Cado}} will be surprised and overjoyed with {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s success. He thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} repeatedly for his work and rewards him with a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}, valued at 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you so much! Really, I could never thank you enough! This isn't much, but I insist you take it!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> This ends the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}. {{Term|BotW|Cado}} declares that he is ready to face the world again, although he wishes his wife would return as well.<ref>{{Cite|I'm ready to get out there again! {{Small|Though I do wish my wife would come back to me, just like my Cuccos did...}}|Cado|BotW}}</ref>
