The '''Blue-White Frox Fang''' is one of the most powerful fuses in {{TotK}}. It adds 35 attack power to your weapon. They are not sharp, but are good for smashing ores and rocks, they look like a long wavy white spike with mini brown spikes around and on top of it. In the strongest weapons list, the Blue-White Frox Fang is the 9th strongest material to fuse with your weapon. You could also cook it with any critter to create an [[Elixir]].
