"{{Term/Store|EX Champion Daruk's Song||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Champion Daruk's Song}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Eldin Mountains}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] pack.

==Overview==
After finishing the trials of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} during "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}", the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator|link}} will split into four glowing orbs and scatter to different areas of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. When they land, they reveal four ancient structures called {{Plural|BotW|Monument|link}}. One of the {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} appears in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}} on a ledge overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Golow River|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Monument}}, a voice belonging to an unspecified {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} speaks to him and instructs him to complete the trials at the locations shown to him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I applaud your courage in coming here. The locations that will be revealed to you are of trials that will enhance the power sleeping within. Do not let your guard down...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Three maps then appear on the {{Term|BotW|Monument}}'s pillars, each revealing the location of a trial that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must undergo within the {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}, who is standing nearby, will make a remark on either the flashing light or the {{Term|BotW|Monument}} itself, depending on how many {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already visited.<ref>{{Cite|What was that...flash of light? Perhaps it was my imagination playing tricks on me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Did that {{Color|BotW Blue|stone monument}} just start glowing? I suppose that proves I didn't imagine it the first time.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This {{Color|BotW Blue|stone monument}} has survived for 10,000 years. What secrets does it hold...|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That light... Is it a reaction to someone nearby? Is it me...or is it...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Noticing {{Term|BotW|Link}} nearby, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} is concerned about his ability to withstand the heat.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? A traveler in the land of flames? Are you truly able to withstand the heat? I know it's not my business, but I'm rather concerned.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he also drank many {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It must be...that you applied an ample amount of {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixir}}, just as I did. Is that so?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms this, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will remark that he is in a similar situation and would fry in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[It is!] Yes, I knew it! I'm in the same boat, friend. If I don't use {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixir}}, I go from a bard to a baked bird!|Kass|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} refutes this, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} comments that he must have natural immunity to heat.<ref>{{Cite|[Nope.] How about that! You must have a natural ability to withstand intense heat, just like the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gorons}}!|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

Dismissing himself, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} points out the nearby {{Term|BotW|Monument}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, explaining it was mentioned in his teacher's {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, never mind my musings. Look! I'm sure you already know, but it's one of the {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} mentioned in my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If this is the first {{Term|BotW|Monument}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has visited, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will explain that it was mentioned in an {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}} written by his teacher.<ref>{{Cite|This {{Color|BotW Blue|monument}}... I must admit it has taken me by surprise! My teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}} mentions such {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}}, and now one has appeared before my eyes...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s confusion, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} explains that it is missing its key verses.<ref>{{Cite|[Unfinished song?] Yes. The song is mostly complete. However... My teacher left this world without finishing the {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He is now on a journey to finish the song.<ref>{{Cite|I wish the weave the missing pieces together and finish the song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>}} He wonders if his teacher worked on this {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song|display=Song}} at this spot.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* I wonder if my teacher ever contemplated this very song at this very spot...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that there is a verse in the song that mentions the trials that {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} overcame, which he will sing for him.<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}} overcame. Allow me to sing it for you...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The verse provides {{Term|BotW|Link}} with instructions for completing all three of {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}'s trials.<ref>{{Cite|♪Crawling flame on a mighty peak.<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}}'s Champion, never weak.♪<br/>♪The hero's power shall grow.<br/>Seek trials {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} show.♪<br/>♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|stop a titan of molten stone}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|follow rings of light alone}}.♪<br/>♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|survive lava's fiery fate}}.<br/>Champion, trials await!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> 

After playing for {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he was moved.<ref>{{Cite|...So? Did it move you?|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...So? Did the {{Color|BotW Blue|verse}} move you? It was written in honor of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} challenging the trial.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> His journey has allowed him understand the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}}, which he hopes will help him with finishing the key verses of the {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}}.<ref>{{Cite|By understanding the true natures of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champions}}, I'm closer than ever to completing those {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}}. I can imagine it vividly...the Champion's vigilance from 100 years ago.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He recalls that his teacher advised him to travel and meet people in order to write good songs, which has inspired his current way of living.<ref>{{Cite|My teacher always advised that, to write good songs, one must travel the world and meet lots of people. That is why I spend my days afoot, talking to anyone who will listen.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Recently, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} was able to meet with {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}}, who told him much about {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}.<ref>{{Cite|I was lucky enough to meet with {{Color|BotW Blue|Yunobo}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Bludo}} in {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}}, both of whom knew much about the Goron Champion. They regaled me with some rather interesting stories!|Kass|BotW}}</ref> With their stories, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} believes he will be able to finish the key verses.<ref>{{Cite|I'm certain the stories they shared will help me greatly in my efforts to accurately write the song's {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} may complete the three trials in any order. The first trial involves defeating the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan|link}} on the shore of {{Term|BotW|Lake Darman|link}}, which reveals the {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna Shrine|link}}. The second trial has {{Term|BotW|Link}} follow the {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} from the top of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}, and in doing so, unearths the {{Term|BotW|Sharo Lun Shrine|link}}. Finally, the third trial tasks Link with safely falling into the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}} over {{Term|BotW|Darb Pond|link|display=Darb Pond's}} lava, thereby revealing {{Term|BotW|Rinu Honika Shrine|link}}.

Each of these {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} rewards {{Term|BotW|Link}} with one of {{Plural|BotW|Rudania's Emblem|link}} upon their completion.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Rudania's Emblem.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} will then inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} that collecting all three {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|display=Emblems}} will unlock a new challenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> They will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} how many more trials he has to complete before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> After completing all three {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrines}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} of the third will instruct him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Rudania.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}, he is spoken to again by an unspecified {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, who offers him a trial in his memories in exchange for {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|display=Rudania}}'s three {{Term|BotW|Rudania's Emblem|display=Emblems}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the one who approaches this Divine Beast... In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial. In exchange for Rudania's Emblems... you will be granted the chance to tread through the realm of memories.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} says the trial will be unforgiving without determination and reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} not to dismiss it as simple memories.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Those who lack determination will find this trial unforgiving. Do not take this place lightly nor dismiss it as merely a world within your mind. The truth is much deeper than you know.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} is then transported to the {{Term|BotW|Illusory Realm|link}}, a realm created by his memories, where he once again battles {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon|link}} on top of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|This illusory realm was created from the depths of your memories... The enemy you shall face is a product of the fear that dwells within. This battle is a trial of the soul. You must emerge victorious using only the tools provided.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>  This Fireblight Ganon is a phantom born from Link's memories and fears.<ref>{{Cite|You defeated the phantom of Fireblight Ganon, which was born from your own memories and fears.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}, equipment, and {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} will be changed to the following:
* {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Set|link}} (upgraded twice)
* {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bow|link}}
* Five {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Seared Steak|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Toasty Ironshroom|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Baked Apple|link}}

After defeating {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon}} for the second time, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is transported back outside of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}}. He will find {{Term|BotW|Kass}} nearby, who notices that he appears to still be lost in thought.<ref>{{Cite|Are you still adrift in thought? You seem lost to the whims of your mind. Oh... Pardon me. I suppose it's none of my business.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that learning about {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}'s tales has inspired him to write his own song in his honor.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Wandering around here and listening to anecdotes about {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}} has truly invigorated me. In fact, it has inspired me to honor him in my own way, separate from my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}. That is why I felt inclined to roost here, near the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}}, which {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}} himself mastered. I've written a song...to try and capture the essence of {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}}...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He will sing the song to {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|♪Recall {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}}'s Champion of flame,<br/>before his fated moment finally came.♪<br/>♪This is the tale of Great {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}}'s task,<br/>and the princess who came to ask.♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to visualize the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Daruk's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} from 100 years ago,<ref>{{Cite|Kass's composition, entitled {{Color|BotW Red|Champion Daruk's Song}}, was so true and moving that it allowed you to visualize a memory from 100 years ago.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

After the song, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} notes that even {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} are capable of fear.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... I suppose even Champions are not immune to fear.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he thought of {{Term|BotW|Champion Daruk's Song (Song)|link}},<ref>{{Cite|So? What do you think of {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk's Song}}?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and he explains that writing it helped him feel closer to his teacher.<ref>{{Cite|By writing a song that celebrates the softer side of Champion Daruk, I feel closer than ever to my dear teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Content with his composition, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} then remarks how {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} would make a suitable setting for his teacher's song.<ref>{{Cite|The missing {{Color|BotW Blue|verses}} of an ancient song... I think the perfect setting would be {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} in the distant past.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} then hears the voice of {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}, who congratulates him and says he can now use {{Term|BotW|Daruk's Protection|link}} more often.<ref>{{Cite|I knew ya could do it, little guy! Now you can use Daruk's Protection more than ever.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever sees {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} asks that he tell him good riddance for him.<ref>{{Cite|If ya see that Ganon jerk, give him a message for me... Good riddance, bacon-breath!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} receives {{Term|BotW|Daruk's Protection +|link}}, which reduces the ability's recharge time to six minutes. After receiving the upgrade, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} is completed.
