{{Plural|BotW|Kite Shield}} are mid-grade {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} made by the {{Plural|BotW|Rito|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Kite Shield}} bear a {{Term|BotW|Rito}} emblem on its face, similar to the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}. They are highly regarded among {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} warriors for their effectiveness and ease of use in aerial combat.<ref name="Inventory"/> These {{Plural|BotW|Shield}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Rito Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created and used by the {{Plural|BotW|Rito}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref>

There are three locations where {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain a {{Term|BotW|Kite Shield}}. The first is in a metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in the ground in an enemy giant skull at {{Term|BotW|N. Tabantha Snowfield|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} to pull out of the ground. The second {{Term|BotW|Kite Shield}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} in an ice formation at {{Term|BotW|Hebra Headspring|link}}. A third {{Term|BotW|Kite Shield}} can be found mounted on the wall in {{Term|BotW|Selmie's Spot|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can win it as a reward from {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} by taking on her {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)|link}} Advanced Course and reaching the goal between 2:05 and 2:20.<ref>{{Cite|You clocked in at {{Color|BotW Red|[X]}}... That's not too far off my own record. That takes some serious skills! I'll admit, you've impressed me. Take this shield and use it the next time you hit the slopes.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, it will disappear from its mount on her wall, and she will offer {{Term|BotW|Link}} 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} instead each subsequent time he meets that window of time.<ref>{{Cite|Whoops! This is the same shield I gave you last time, huh? Uh...how about some rupees as a consolation prize?|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

Due to their wooden construction, {{Plural|BotW|Kite Shield}} will burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. Finally, they cannot be manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

==Trivia==
*{{Plural|BotW|Kite Shield}} are one of the [[Permanently Missable Items]] in {{BotW}}. If both {{Plural|BotW|Kite Shield}} in the {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} are destroyed, and the {{Term|BotW|Kite Shield}} won from {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} is also destroyed, no more {{Plural|BotW|Kite Shield}} can be obtained.

==Nomenclature==
The {{Term|BotW|Kite Shield}} receives its name from its resemblance to a kite, as well as its aesthetic relationship to the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}}. Despite its name, it does not resemble an actual {{Wp|kite shield}}, instead more closely resembling an inverted {{Wp|heater shield}} with a concave chief.
