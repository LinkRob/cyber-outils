The {{Term/Store|Digdogg Suspension Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Digdogg Suspension Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Digdogg Suspension Bridge}} is a bridge spanning the {{Term|BotW|Regencia River|link}}, connecting {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Pass|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. Beneath the {{Term|BotW|Digdogg Suspension Bridge|display=Bridge}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find the {{Term|BotW|Dah Kaso Shrine|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Raft|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Traveler|link}} can occasionally be seen walking along the {{Term|BotW|Digdogg Suspension Bridge}}. If spoken to, they will reveal themselves as a {{Term|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} and attack {{Term|BotW|Link}}. Additionally, a {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} sleeps at the center of the {{Term|BotW|Digdogg Suspension Bridge}}.<ref>{{Cite|Digdogg Suspension Bridge<br/>{{Big|Hinox}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref> It wears a single wooden brace around one of its ankles, but this can be burned away with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, it is replaced with a {{Term|BotW|Black Hinox|link}}.

In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode}}, four wooden platforms held aloft by {{Plural|BotW|Sky Octorok|link}} appear along the sides of the Bridge: three at the northern end and one the Bridge's southern end. Atop two of the northern platforms, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find metal {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} both containing {{Plural|BotW|Diamond|link}}. Atop the southern platform, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a third metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
A {{Term|BotW|Circle|link}} of {{Plural|BotW|Lily Pad|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} along the southwestern side of the {{Term|BotW|Digdogg Suspension Bridge}}'s central pillar. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} jumps into the {{Term|BotW|Circle}}, he will uncover a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}}, who will give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
