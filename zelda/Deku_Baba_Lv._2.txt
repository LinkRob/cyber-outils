'''Deku Baba Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Deku Baba Lv. 2|A ferocious plant that uses its large mouth to snap at and swallow enemies. Its hard head protects it from attacks.<ref name="Deku Baba Lv. 2">{{Cite|A ferocious plant that uses its large mouth to snap at and swallow enemies. Its hard head protects it from attacks.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Deku Baba Lv. 2 is a tougher variant of the standard [[Deku Baba]], with higher health and damage. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Deku Baba Lv. 2 [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of three.

==Trivia==
*Deku Baba Lv. 2 bears a stronger resemblance to the traditional Deku Babas seen in earlier Zelda titles compared to Deku Baba Lv. 1.
