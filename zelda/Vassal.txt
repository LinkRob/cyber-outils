{{Term/Store|Vassal|Vassals|Series, TMC|plural}} are servants of {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|These vassals serve the king of Hyrule. They are loyal and diligent. Like the king himself, they are courteous yet frank.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Vassals}}
{{Plural|TMC|Vassal}} mostly stay within {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} or in its {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden|link|display=Garden}}, though some scout in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} turns {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} to stone at the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}. The {{Plural|TMC|Vassal}} with blue stripes guard {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle}}, while the {{Plural|TMC|Vassal}} with green stripes patrol {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}. When {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} impersonates {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, he orders the {{Plural|TMC|Vassal}} to search for the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}} and not allow anyone past {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle}}'s gates.<ref>{{Cite|Waste no time! Go forth at once, and bring me the {{Color|TMC Red|light force}}! Fling anyone who refuses into the dungeon! Him and his family, too! I will tolerate no disobedience!|Vaati|TMC}}</ref>

The {{Plural|TMC|Vassal}} follow these orders, with those stationed in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden}} keeping {{Term|TMC|Link}} away from the path that leads to the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|By the order of the king, none shall pass!! Now! Begone with you!!|Vassal|TMC}}</ref> The {{Plural|TMC|Vassal}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} search for the {{Term|TMC|Light Force}}, though their numbers dwindle and their morale lowers over the course of the game.<ref>{{Cite|You know what? I don’t even feel like looking for that {{Color|TMC Red|light force}} anymore.|Vassal|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Only a few guards are still out there looking for that {{Color|TMC Red|light force}} stuff.|Vassal|TMC}}</ref> Many of them suspect something is amiss with {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}}, as the denizens of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} do.<ref>{{Cite|I’ve never seen the {{Color|TMC Green|king}} like this! He’s so angry, so impatient! I hear he’s been just horrible to the guards, too.|Vassal|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, the {{Plural|TMC|Vassal}} that were outside commit themselves to defending {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Green|Hyrule Castle}} has become nothing more than a giant nest for monsters! Those of us who are left must protect the town from those beasts!|Vassal|TMC}}</ref> while those that were inside are turned to stone.<ref>{{Cite|One by one, he turned us all to stone. Everyone, cursed by his evil magic... Then, the monsters arrived. Even paralyzed, I saw it all...|Vassal|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link}} restores them with the power of the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}, they promise they will help seek out the true {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}}.<ref>{{Cite|The real {{Color|TMC Green|king of Hyrule}} must be locked up somewhere in the castle... Please find him. As soon as we've recovered, we'll start looking, too.|Vassal|TMC}}</ref> The remaining {{Plural|TMC|Vassal}} that were turned to stone are shown to be restored after {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} is defeated and {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} uses the {{Term|TMC|Mage's Cap|link}} to undo the damage he had done to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}.
