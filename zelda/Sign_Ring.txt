The {{Term/Store|Sign Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sign Ring<br/>100 signs broken|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Sign Ring is received from the [[Sign-Loving Subrosian]] after breaking 100 [[Sign]]s. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. It has no effect when worn. The Sign Ring can be transferred to {{OoA}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
