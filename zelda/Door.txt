TITLE : Door

'''Door''' may refer to any of the following:

*'''[[North Castle|Door That Does Not Open]]''', an object mentioned in {{TAoL}}.
*'''[[Temple of Time#Door of Time|Door of Time]]''', an object featured in {{OoT}} and {{TP}}.
**The [[Door of Time]] is not to be confused with a '''[[Gate of Time]]''', an object from {{SS}}.
*'''[[Door Mimic]]''', an enemy that looks like a door, but that really is a trap which will fall down on whoever gets too close. It appears in {{OoT}} and {{TMC}}.
*'''[[Minish Door]]''', the magical gateway between the realm of [[Hyrule]] and the [[Minish Realm]] in {{TMC}}.
*'''[[Fire Sanctuary (Spirit Tracks)#Door of Friendship|Door of Friendship]]''', an object featured in {{ST}} that blocks the entrance to the [[Fire Sanctuary (Spirit Tracks)|Fire Sanctuary]].
