{{Term/Store|Two Chiefs||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Two Chiefs|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Two Chiefs}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} with {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Riju|link}} as its pilots. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Meat|link}}, 20 {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Drumstick|link}}, and 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

At the start of {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to defeat each of the 10 {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} on the map within five minutes. Many {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} can be found attacking Vah Naboris as well. Five more {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} also appear when five, ten, fifteen, and twenty-five are killed. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once all 30 of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}} are defeated.
