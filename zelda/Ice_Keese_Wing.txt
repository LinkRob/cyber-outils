==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Ice Keese Wings are dropped by [[Ice Keese]], which can be found in cold regions such as the [[Hebra Mountains]] and [[Mount Lanayru]]. They are {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} and can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] to create [[Elixir]]s. They can also be exchanged for [[Mon]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] or used to make Blue [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

At a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, Ice Keese Wings can be used to upgrade pieces of [[Armor]]. The [[Climbing Set]] requires Ice Keese Wings for its third upgrade, while the [[Desert Voe Set]] needs them for its second and third upgrades.

* Total for '''Climbing Set''': 15 (0, 0, 5, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Desert Voe Set''': 33 (0, 3, 8, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
