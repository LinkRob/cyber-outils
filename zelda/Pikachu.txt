'''Pikachu''' is an Electric-type Pokémon and the mascot of the ''[[w:c:pokemon:Pokémon (franchise)|Pokémon]]'' franchise. He resembles a yellow mouse with a jagged tail and red cheeks. He attacks both physically and with electric power it stores in his cheeks.

While it has not appeared in any of ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' games, the [[Keaton Mask]] does have a vague resemblance to Pikachu (namely the primarily yellow color with black-tipped ears), with the [[Death Mountain Trail Gatekeeper]] alluding to Keaton being popular with kids as an indirect reference to Pikachu's popularity.

In addition, Pikachu does appear as a playable character in every game of the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. Starting as one of the twelve playable characters in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'', Pikachu returns in every sequel henceforth: ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
