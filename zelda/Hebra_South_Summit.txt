{{Term/Store|Hebra South Summit||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hebra South Summit'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Hebra South Summit is located in the [[Hebra Mountains]] between [[Talonto Peak]] and [[Lake Kilsie]]. It is a large flat terrain with a cliff on one side and the bottom of another cliff on the other.

Near the western cliff on the summit, a large flagpole extends into the air. At the foot of this flag sits a Wooden [[Chest]] containing a bundle of 5 [[Fire Arrow]]s.
