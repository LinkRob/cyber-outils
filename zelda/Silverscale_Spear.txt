{{Plural|BotW|Silverscale Spear}} are spears used by the {{Plural|BotW|Zora|link}} with a fish-tail design.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Zora Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which consists of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created and used by the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref> Their strength is known to be impressive, being able to pierce the toughest scales.<ref name="Inventory/> The male guards of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, such as {{Term|BotW|Bazz|link}}, use the {{Term|BotW|Silverscale Spear}}. However, {{Term|BotW|Link|Link}} cannot pick them up, and attacking the guards will result in retaliation.

An unoccupied {{Term|BotW|Silverscale Spear}} can be found under the flowing {{Plural|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}. They are also used by {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} in the surrounding {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}, as well as in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Silverscale Spear}} is wielded by a {{Term|BotW|Black Moblin|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. More {{Plural|BotW|Silverscale Spear}} can be obtained in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} dropped when the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Silverscale Spear}} are made of metal, which means they will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if wielded during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, but they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Silverscale Spear|display=Spears}} are also unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
