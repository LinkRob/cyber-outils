{{Term/Store|''Legend of the Picori''||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 115">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 115}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori}} is a {{Term|TMC|Library Book|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}.<ref name="E 115"/> It was borrowed by {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left|link}}, who is studying the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}.

After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} finds and returns {{Term|TMC|A Hyrulean Bestiary|link}}, he is told by {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|link}} that {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left}} had checked out {{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori}}.<ref>{{Cite|The next overdue book is... Let's see... Ah, yes! Of course...{{Color|TMC Red|Legend of the Picori}}! It's been out forever! It was checked out by a somewhat {{Color|TMC Blue|absentminded scholar}}, I believe...|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} speaks to {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left}} about the book, he claims not to know where it is.<ref>{{Cite|My name is {{Color|TMC Blue|Dr. Left}}. You're here about that book, {{Color|TMC Red|Legend of the Picori}}, aren't you? Well, I hate to say this, but I haven't seen it in several days. Perhaps a mischievous little mouse has taken it? Well, I'm sure it's in this house somewhere. I just don't know where.|Dr. Left|TMC}}</ref> To push the {{Plural|TMC|Bookshelf|link}} in his {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House|link|display=House}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must shrink to {{Term|TMC|Minish}} size and enter the {{Term|TMC|Fountain|link}} to obtain the {{Plural|TMC|Power Bracelet|link}}. Afterwards, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can align the {{Plural|TMC|Bookshelf}} in a way that allows him to go into the rafters. {{Term|TMC|Link}} will find {{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori}} here, but he must first help a {{Term|TMC|Town Minish}} push it down below.<ref>{{Cite|Ho! Ha! Hey!! Phew... I want to return this book by dropping it down below. It's impossible without some help pushing... But my brother is so busy with a book of his own that he won't help me out!|Minish|TMC}}</ref> Once this is done, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can retrieve the {{Term|TMC|Library Book|display=Book}} and give it back to the {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)}}.
