{{Term/Store|Daily Use Goods|Daily Use Goods|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} are a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Daily Use Goods}} are a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that were initially made as tools for domestic life.<ref name="Soup Ladle">{{Cite|{{Big|Soup Ladle}}<br/>A kitchen implement often used for serving delicious soups. It was carved from the wood of a sturdy tree, so it actually packs quite the wallop.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Farming Hoe}}<br/>A farming tool primarily used for tilling fields. Its fine craftsmanship is sturdy enough to withstand backbreaking fieldwork, but its battle applications are untested.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Boat Oar}}<br/>Made for paddling boats, but it was made sturdy enough to fight strong currents. Maybe it's useful for self-defense in a pinch.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Wooden Mop">{{Cite|{{Big|Wooden Mop}}<br/>Just a mop to the untrained eye, it excels at tidying up the place. But it owes its sturdy construction to a true craftsman, so it actually has some combat merit.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> However, due to their composition, many of them can find use in combat situations.<ref name="Soup Ladle"/><ref name="Wooden Mop"/>

With the except of {{Plural|BotW|Farming Hoe|link}}, all {{Term|BotW|Daily Use Goods}} are considered wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}. This means that they will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
