==Features and Overview==

=== Breath of the wild ===
The Rikoka Hills are located in the [[Lanayru Wetlands]].

==== Tears of the Kingdom ====
The Rikoka Hills Well now become '''[[Moza]]'s [[The Rikoka Hills Well|Hideaway Restaurant]]'''.
