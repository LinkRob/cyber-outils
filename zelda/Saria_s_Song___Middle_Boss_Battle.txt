"'''Saria's Song / Middle Boss Battle'''" is a recurring [[Song]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Overview==
"Saria's Song / Middle Boss Battle", as the title indicates, combines two tracks from {{OoT}}: "{{Term|OoT3D|Saria's Song}}" and the middle boss battle theme. It also incorporates the fanfare that plays when {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} defeats a {{Term|OoT3D|Boss|link}}.

==={{SSB4|Wii U|-}}===
"Saria's Song / Middle Boss Battle" plays in the {{Term|SSBfWU|Skyloft}} Stage in {{SSB4|Wii U}}.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
"Saria's Song / Middle Boss Battle" plays in any Stage related to {{TLoZ|Series}} in {{SSBU}}.
