==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Hilltop High Road}} is a winding path that runs from {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tundra|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}, passing through the western edge of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. It starts at {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}} and follows the edge of {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}} to meet {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable|link}}. The road continues south through the {{Term|BotW|Breach of Demise|link}}, along the southern side of the {{Term|BotW|Seres Scablands|link}}, and ends at {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}.

The travelers {{Term|BotW|Baddek|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nazbi|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Savelle|link}} can be found following the {{Term|BotW|Hilltop High Road}}. The ingredient merchant {{Term|BotW|Yammo|link}} also wanders the route, and {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} may be found along the road near {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}}.
