{{Term/Store|Real Bombchu|Real Bombchu|Series, MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD, FSA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 200 {{Exp Game|MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD, FSA}}}}</ref> They are named after the [[Bombchu]], a type of [[Bomb]].

==Characteristics==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= That's a {{Color|MM White|Real Bombchu}}!<br/> It runs at you with its pack of explosives, so protect yourself or else!
}}

In {{MM}}, Real Bombchu are a dangerous living version of the [[Bombchu|Bombchu Bomb]] [[item]] with a wide, grinning smile. In-universe, they may be the source of inspiration for the mechanical counterpart. These creatures run around with [[Bomb|Bombs]] hanging from their tails, waiting to blow themselves up in order to damage their enemies. They can run across water. If they sight [[Link]], they will run after the young [[Hero of Time]] and explode either upon contact or after a short while. They will reappear from the ground to attack him again after a short time.

An [[Arrow]] from the [[Hero's Bow]] will make the Real Bombchus explode immediately; however, if Link uses the [[Hookshot]], the Real Bombchu will just be reeled in towards him and explode upon contact, making this tactic hazardous. In addition, if Link raises his [[Shield]] when facing a Real Bombchu, it will bounce off and fall backwards, and then explode, which can prove useful if aimed correctly. Like most enemies, Real Bombchu cannot hurt Link if he is riding on [[Epona]]. Wearing the [[Stone Mask]] will cause them to completely ignore the hero.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, Bombchus are simply [[Rat]]s holding Bombs and have reddish fur rather than blue<ref>{{Cite|These creatures attack spontaneously and attempt to steal Rupees. The ones that carry bombs around are apparently known as Bombchus.|Figurines in The Wind Waker|TWW}}</ref>. Their main habitat is the [[Forsaken Fortress]], but they can also be found in a submarine near [[Headstone Island]]. The Real Bombchu can be seen emerging from Rat holes, which they will leave to throw a Bomb at Link and then quickly retreat to get another Bomb.

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, Bombchus appear in the {{Term|FSA|Temple of Ice|link}} and the [[Tower of Winds]] stages. They swarm the room, exploding upon impact with any of the [[Links]]. They spawn from holes in the ground and will only stop appearing when the holes are closed upon activating a [[switch]].

==Trivia==
*Despite being rodents, the chatter noise of the Real Bombchu in ''Majora's Mask'' is an edited version of a [https://soundeffects.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Ideas,_SQUIRREL_-_CHATTERING,_ANIMAL,_RODENT_(Universal_Studios) stock sound effect] of squirrels squeaking, taken from Universal Studios Sound Effects Library.
*If a Real Bombchu is chasing Link and then he puts on the Stone Mask, the enemy can be seen continuing to run in a straight line until its explodes by running into a wall if its fuse ran out of time.
*In the Japanese version, the enemies are called '''Genuine Bomchuu'''.
