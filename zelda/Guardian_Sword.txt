{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} sometimes wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}} and, in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}}, which can be found in "A Minor Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword|display=Swords}} can be retrieved when the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} drop them after being defeated. They also have a chance to appear inside {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that are spawned by scanning the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword}} use {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} to produce their blue energy blade, though they are not very durable.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Due to their composition, {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword}} deal additional damage against {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}. They will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they are unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword}} also do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. Finally, the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} is incapable of picking them up.

{{Plural|BotW|Guardian Sword}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} used by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>
