The "{{Term/Store|Minuet of Forest||Series, OoT, OoT3D}}" is a recurring Song in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT3D Minuet of Forest Icon.png|left]]
The "Minuet of Forest" is a [[Warping]] song taught by [[Sheik]] when [[Link]] first enters the [[Sacred Forest Meadow]] as an adult. When played on the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}}, it warps the Link to the Sacred Forest Meadow right before the entrance to the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}}.

Even though the song warps to the Sacred Forest Meadow, the message appearing after playing it asks if Link wishes to warp to the [[Lost Woods]].<ref>{{Cite|Warp to the Lost Woods?|N/A|OoT}}</ref> This was corrected in {{OoT3D}}.<ref>{{Cite|Warp to the Sacred Forest Meadow?|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the "Minuet of Forest"'s notes sometimes appear on a wall in the northern part of [[Termina Field]], where Link can play a song for [[Rupee]]s.
