"{{Term/Store|Slated for Upgrades||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Slated for Upgrades}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
During the "{{Term|BotW|Locked Mementos|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to gather some {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}} for her after he sees {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You don't have to worry about this until after you see Impa, but I want you to bring me back some {{Color|BotW Red|ancient materials}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> She says he can deliver them to either her or {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} and promises him "something good" in return.<ref>{{Cite|Bring them either to {{Color|BotW Red|me}} or to {{Color|BotW Red|Robbie}} at the {{Color|BotW Red|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}. As if my joy weren't enough, if you bring us {{Color|BotW Red|ancient materials}}, we'll give you {{Color|BotW Red|something good}} as a reward.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.
