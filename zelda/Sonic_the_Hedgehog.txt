'''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog Sonic the Hedgehog]''', also known as simply '''Sonic''', is the eponymous main protagonist of the [[w:c:sonic:Sonic the Hedgehog (series)|''Sonic the Hedgehog'' franchise]] created by {{Wp|Sega}}, and is the company's current mascot since June 1991, replacing [https://alex-kidd.fandom.com/wiki/Alex_Kidd Alex Kidd] before him. Although the titular first installment of his franchise released on the [[wikipedia:Sega_Genesis|Sega Genesis/Mega Drive]] that year, ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_(1991) Sonic the Hedgehog]'', was Sonic's breakthrough title, he actually makes his debut appearance in the arcade racing game ''[[wikipedia:Rad_Mobile|Rad Mobile]]'' in 1990 as a cameo. A blue anthropomorphic hedgehog, as his name suggests and nicknamed '''The Blue Blur''', Sonic is best-known for his unmatched super speed capable of running faster than the [[wikipedia:Speed_of_sound|speed of sound]] (which creates a sonic boom), big love for adventure, and flashy character. His objective in most of his mainline games is to protect the world from the clutches of his arch-nemesis [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Doctor_Eggman Dr. Eggman] and other antagonists. Much like [[Mario]], [[Nintendo]]'s mascot, as well as [[Link]], Sonic is known to have many friends and enemies, and has transformation abilities. In general, he is one of the most widely recognized video game characters of all-time and a cultural icon.

In the 1990s, he himself had an infamous competition with Mario, when Nintendo and Sega faced a heated business rivalry that made the ''Sonic'' series directly compete with the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], mainly in North America during the [[wikipedia:Fourth_generation_of_video_game_consoles|16-bit era]] of the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] against the Genesis, leading to a near-stalemate in sales and what fans in the region called the "Console Wars" (though the SNES ultimately became the best-seller). Eventually, by the release of the [[Nintendo GameCube]] and [[Game Boy Advance]] in the early 2000s after Sega retired being a 1st-party video game developer and shifted into 3rd-party due to commercial failures of the [[wikipedia:Sega_Saturn|Sega Saturn]] and [[wikipedia:Dreamcast|Dreamcast]], Sonic's games began to appear in those consoles as the rivalry is settled, and even made crossover appearances since 2007 beginning with ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Mario_%26_Sonic_at_the_Olympic_Games Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]'' for the [[Wii|Nintendo Wii]], which became a significant surprise worldwide among both fandoms. Current media have depicted the two mascots and their cast as friends.

Though Sonic the Hedgehog is a non-Nintendo character and makes no appearance whatsoever in the core series of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'']], he is playable in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] starting in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl|Brawl]]'', and would receive an official crossover in ''[[The Legend of Zelda Zone]]'', a DLC package for the [[Wii U]] version of ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_Lost_World Sonic Lost World]''. However, it's no longer available due to [[Nintendo eShop]]'s closure in 2023.

== ''The Legend of Zelda'' appearances ==
{{Noncanon}}

=== ''Sonic Lost World'' ===
[[File:Sonic in The Legend of Zelda Zone DLC.webp|left|thumb|245x245px|Gameplay screenshot of Sonic in the [[Green Clothes|Green Tunic]] as seen in ''Sonic Lost World''<nowiki/>'s The Legend of Zelda Zone DLC]]
In ''Sonic Lost World'' of the ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' series, exclusively for the [[Wii U]] release, Sonic the Hedgehog makes a crossover appearance in the ''Zelda'' universe in The Legend of Zelda Zone DLC. Here, he is in a [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda_Zone unique Zone] based on ''The Legend of Zelda'' series, with elements taken from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]''. Sonic is dressed as [[Link]] (but is not armed with a [[Sword]] and [[Shield]]) and Rings are replaced with [[Rupee|Rupees]], which turn into saved animals after being collected similarly to defeating Badniks. Like in the ''Zelda'' games, Sonic himself has a [[Life Gauge]] and loses Hearts when hit rather than using the traditional Ring gameplay mechanic. If all the Heart Containers are fully depleted, he loses a life. Sonic must find new ones in [[Treasure Chest|Treasure Chests]] that add to his total.

The first part of the Zone takes place in [[Hyrule Field]] with [[Hyrule Castle]] seen in the background; the [[Enemy|enemies]] found in this part are [[Stalchild|Stalchildren]] and [[Deku Scrub|Deku Scrubs]] from the [[Nintendo 64]] games (''Ocarina of Time'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]''). The second part of the Zone takes place in a [[dungeon]], in the same style of ''The Legend of Zelda''. In addition to Stalchildren as well as [[Keese]], Sonic must also avoid [[Goron|Gorons]], who will rise from the ground and attempt to roll over him. At the end of the Zone, Sonic finds the [[Triforce]].

Link himself also appears in the Zone (appearing as his ''Skyward Sword'' iteration), where he occasionally assists Sonic as he rides on a [[Loftwing]].

== Trivia ==

* His catchphrase is "''Gotta go fast!''", reflecting Sonic's super speed. It would also become the theme song of his 2003 anime adaptation ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_X Sonic X]''.
* Sonic's blue fur is based on the [[wikipedia:Sega|Sega]] logo.[[File:Sonic and Tails fist bump.webp|thumb|226x226px|Sonic sharing a fist bump with his best friend, Tails, as seen in ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_Colors Sonic Colors]'']]
* Like [[Mario]], Sonic has a sidekick or partner named [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Miles_%22Tails%22_Prower Miles "Tails" Prowler], whom he is best friends with and debuted in the [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_2_(8-bit) Sega Master System version of ''Sonic the Hedgehog 2''], making him a mirror to [[Luigi]].
* In the ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' series lore, Sonic himself is afraid of water and cannot swim. This is due to a misconception by the Sonic Team (the developers of most mainline ''Sonic'' games) believing that [[wikipedia:Hedgehog|real-life hedgehogs]] do not swim in water and wanted to give him a weakness. However, such mammals actually do swim. Ironically enough, though, Sonic enjoys running ''on'' water.
* In the 2D games of his series, if Sonic were to stand still for a certain amount of time, he will look directly at the player and stomps his feet in disgust, due to the fact he dislikes being still. In ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_CD Sonic the Hedgehog CD]'', however, this is put to a rather humorous level; If he remains completely motionless for three minutes, he will say "''I'm outer here!''" then jumps out of the screen by himself losing his patience entirely, resulting in an immediate [[Game Over]]. Some other older games made by Sega share this reference by having playable characters becoming impatient upon remaining still.
* Sonic's [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic%27s_shoes trademark red shoes] were based on the boots worn by the late pop superstar [[wikipedia:Michael_Jackson|Michael Jackson]] on the cover of the 1987 album ''Bad'' and the colors were inspired by Santa Claus. Sega considered it fitting as Sonic's legs spun as he runs at fast speeds.
** Jackson himself actually was a fan of the [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_series ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' series], as a photograph of him playing ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_2 Sonic the Hedgehog 2]'' exists and also was heavily involved in the music production of ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_3 Sonic the Hedgehog 3]''.
** Early Western instruction manuals claim that the shoes themselves are responsible for Sonic's iconic high-speed ability. However, most other official sources stated that he was born with them.
** Even Sonic himself does not know the actual source of his speed, as he confessed to [https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Flash_(Barry_Allen) The Flash] in a crossover DC comic after the latter asks him where his powers came from.
** In terms of gameplay design, according to Sonic's co-creator [[wikipedia:Yuji_Naka|Yuji Naka]], he stated that Sonic's running speed, ironically, was inspired by ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]'', by him playing the first level, [[mariowiki:World_1-1_(Super_Mario_Bros.)|World 1-1]], as fast as possible, similarly to a [[speedrun]]. Naka himself confessed that he enjoyed the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' title during the development of ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_(1991) Sonic the Hedgehog]''.
** The shoes make a [[Cameos of The Legend of Zelda in other media|cameo appearance]] in one [[Nintendo]] game for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]], ''[https://donkeykong.fandom.com/wiki/Donkey_Kong_Country_2:_Diddy%27s_Kong_Quest Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]'', as part of the "No Hopers" inside Cranky's Video Game Heroes room next to a trash can alongside [https://earthwormjim.fandom.com/wiki/Earthworm_Jim Earthworm Jim]'s [https://earthwormjim.fandom.com/wiki/Plasma_Blaster Plasma Blaster]. This is a reference to the rivalry between Sega and Nintendo at the time. [[Link]] is present in the same room along with Mario and [[Yoshi]], standing at third, first, and second, respectively.

* Sonic's canonical birthday is June 23rd, the same date as the North American release of ''Sonic the Hedgehog''.
* His favorite food is chili hot dogs.
* Although confirmed to be a teenager, Sonic is generally known to be 15 years of age according to some sources. However, in 2022, Sega has removed his and all other of his friends' age sections on the Sonic Channel website, suggesting that they no longer have a specific age. Despite this, fans still frequently claim this information on social media and even the Sonic Wiki continues to display them.[[File:Super Sonic.webp|thumb|267x267px|Sonic as Super Sonic, his Super Transformation state]]
* Sonic's famous [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Super_transformation power up transformation] via obtaining the seven [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Emerald Chaos Emeralds] alongside 50 Rings, [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Sonic Super Sonic], which he uses in [[Final Boss]] battles (though allowed on regular Zones in certain games), due his appearance and general powers, is widely seen as a homage to the ''[https://dragonball.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Ball_(franchise) Dragon Ball]'' series, specifically the [https://dragonball.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Saiyan Super Saiyan] transformation which he himself clearly resembles.
** In the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros''.]] series, this highly powerful state serves as Sonic's [[Final Smash]].
** According to Yuji Naka, who himself is a fan of the anime series, he has never received any responses by late ''Dragon Ball'' creator [[wikipedia:Akira_Toriyama|Akira Toriyama]] regarding the concept. He also stated that some aspects of ''Sonic'' including Super Transformation were indeed influenced by it.
** Super Sonic's golden fur color is a coincidental similarity to the [[Triforce]], [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]], and [[Master Sword Lv3]].
** Although only existing in very few games, known enhanced versions of the transformation include [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Hyper_Sonic Hyper Sonic] and [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Sonic_2 Super Sonic 2].
** The [[Spiritual Stone|Spiritual Stones]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' share similarities with the Chaos Emeralds in that they are large gemstones containing mystical powers. Unlike the ''Sonic'' counterpart, however, they are not based on brilliant cut diamonds and do not grant [[Link]] any special abilities when all are obtained.
* Since the release of ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_Generations Sonic Generations]'' in 2011, Sonic and his older friends and foes have been divided between their original Genesis-era and present incarnations, known as "Classic" and "Modern".
* In [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], although Link does not naturally possess the level of superhuman speed Sonic himself does, in the PAL version of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', his [[Dash Attack]] depicts the hero able to sprint at a very fast rate of speed similarly to that of the blue hedgehog's in his own games.
* Two major rivals of Sonic, [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Metal_Sonic Metal Sonic] and [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_the_Hedgehog Shadow the Hedgehog], are known to be equal to him in every ability. However, there are cases where they can outclass Sonic, making them dangerous opponents. Ironically enough, these two characters are also his doppelgängers.
* In spite of himself capable of running supersonic speeds, Sonic owns several sporty convertible cars and proves to be a skillful racer in various racing games such as ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_Drift Sonic Drift]'' and ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_Racing:_CrossWorlds Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds]''.
* Being a hedgehog, Sonic is capable of spiking the quills on the back of his head like the real animal, as seen when he gets startled or performing an up-throw against an opponent in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]]. When as Super Sonic, they usually stand upright.[[File:Modern Sonic parade balloon.jpg|thumb|Sonic as a balloon in the 2011 Thanksgiving Day Parade]]
* Sonic himself is the first video game character ever to appear as a [https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Macy%27s_Thanksgiving_Day_Parade_Sonic_Balloon balloon] in the annual [[wikipedia:Macy's_Thanksgiving_Day_Parade|Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade]], in 1993, and flew until 1997. He reappeared in 2011 as the lead of the parade, with his balloon receiving a redesign to match his modern appearance, commemorating his 20th anniversary. Sonic continued to be in the parade for the following two years. In 2021, his balloon returned to commemorate his 30th anniversary.
** His original "Classic" balloon met a tragic accident three times. At its very first parade in '93, strong gusts of wind pushed it into a lamppost at Columbus Circle. The lamppost punctured and popped the Sonic balloon and the top of the post broke off while inside the balloon itself, dragging it down to the ground, injuring a child and an off-duty police officer in the process. Next, two years later in 1995, it collided with another lamppost and was forced to be removed when it lost too much helium. Finally, after another two years, Sonic's head suddenly ripped open as the parade was beginning to march. Therefore, the balloon was retired by Sega due to damages until the "Modern" one made its comeback over a decade later.
*** The second balloon nearly got destroyed itself in 2013 when Sonic's left arm was caught on trees but did not suffer damage when cut free.

* A [[The Legend of Zelda in Popular Culture|pop culture]] reference to the ''Zelda'' series can be found in a ''Sonic'' comic where a character speaks to Sonic and says "''It's a secret to everyone.''", based on the [[Iconic quotes in The Legend of Zelda series|famous quote]] by friendly [[Moblin|Moblins]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]''.
* Despite being officially stated as only 3'3" in height, Sonic's size is inconsistent across various media, as some cases have him significantly larger or smaller, which also applies to his friends. This is likely due to his cartoonish physical body proportions.
* Excluding his franchise and Nintendo games, other characters known to run at high speeds like Sonic the Hedgehog other than The Flash in popular culture include [https://looneytunes.fandom.com/wiki/Road_Runner Road Runner], [https://looneytunes.fandom.com/wiki/Speedy_Gonzales Speedy Gonzales], [https://powerpuffgirls.fandom.com/wiki/The_Powerpuff_Girls_(characters) The Powerpuff Girls], [https://the-incredibles.fandom.com/wiki/Dash_Parr Dash Parr], [https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Pietro_Maximoff_(Earth-616) Quicksilver], [https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Superman_(Clark_Kent) Superman], and more.
** One character from the ''[https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/My_Little_Pony_Friendship_is_Magic My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic]'' animated TV series, [https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/Rainbow_Dash Rainbow Dash], is often compared to Sonic by fans, as she is capable of producing rainbow-colored sonic booms through flight (known as a "[https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_rainboom_(event) sonic rainboom]") and shares a similar personality. Her fur also appears to be blue, though it's actually light cerulean.
