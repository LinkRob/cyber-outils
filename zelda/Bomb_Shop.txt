{{Term/Store|Bomb Shop|Bomb Shops|Series, MM, MM3D, TP, TPHD}} is a recurring shop that specializes in explosives in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 90 {{Exp Game|MM, TP}}}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Bomb Shops cater to customers that use Bombs and other Bomb-related goods. Their wares generally include Bombs, [[Bomb Bag]]s of different sizes and other specialized varieties of explosive, such as the [[Bombling]] and [[Water Bomb]]. They usually sell bushels of Bombs in different quantities. They are commonly located in towns and villages across the overworld.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, the Bomb Shop is located in the [[Dark World]]. It is situated exactly where [[Link's House]] appears in the [[Light World]]. The Shop is kept by the [[Bomb Shopkeeper]]. At first, he sells only regular Bombs, but after [[Link]] completes the [[Ice Palace]], [[Misery Mire (A Link to the Past)|Misery Mire]], and reunites the [[Dwarven Swordsmiths]], the Shopkeeper will sell a special [[Super Bomb]] that allows [[Link]] to access the [[Mysterious Pond]] at the base of the [[Pyramid of Power]], where the [[Silver Arrow]]s and [[Golden Sword]] can be acquired.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{See also|Goron Powder Keg Shop}}
[[File:MM Goron Powder Keg Shop.png|thumb|right|200px|The Goron Powder Keg Shop from {{MM|-}}]]
In {{MM}}, the Bomb Shop is located in [[West Clock Town]]. It is run by the [[Bomb Shop Owner]] and his [[Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|mother]], who originally ran it with her late husband.<ref>{{Cite|But it's been our lifelong dream to sell Big Bomb Bags since back in your Daddy's day.|Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|MM}}</ref> The Shop sells Bombs as well as Bombchu. After saving the Old Lady in [[North Clock Town]] from being robbed by [[Sakon|a thief]], the Shop will sell a [[Big Bomb Bag]].<ref>{{Cite|A nice fella helped me out, so we can finally sell Big Bomb Bags!|Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|MM}}</ref> There is a second Bomb Shop located in the [[Goron Village]], known as the Goron Powder Keg Shop, that sells [[Powder Keg]]s for 100 Rupees each. Link must pass the certification test before he may purchase any Powder Kegs, however. A [[Goron]] in the Bomb Shop in [[Clock Town]] also sells Kegs, at half the price the Powder Keg Shop sells them for.<ref>{{Cite|You're a Goron, so you should be able to carry a Powder Keg, right? So, won't ya buy a Powder Keg for 50 Rupees?|Goron|MM}}</ref> However, Link must have already passed the test to be able to purchase Powder Kegs there.

Unique decorations inside the Bomb Shop include a large model rocket, a giant model Bomb, and notes and schematics for a planned trip to the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] using Bomb-powered flight.<ref>{{Cite|Planned Moon Trip/Experiment Memo: Try using bomb-powered flight|N/A|MM}}</ref> The owner often recommends the fireworks show at the [[Carnival of Time]], which may indicate that the Bomb Shop provides them.<ref>{{Cite|There'll be a fireworks show on the night before the carnival. You should go see it.|Bomb Shop Owner|MM}}</ref>

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, the Bomb Shop is located on the southern part of [[Windfall Island]]. It is run by [[Bomb-Master Cannon]], the only Bomb maker left in the entire [[Great Sea]].<ref>{{Cite|My ancestors been making bombs for generations. Uh-huh. Now, seems like I'm the only person left in the world who still works in the bomb industry...|Bomb-Master Cannon|TWW}}</ref> At first, Master Cannon's Bombs are far too expensive for Link to afford, and he cannot obtain them in the early part of the game.<ref>{{Cite|10 Bombs 10,000 Rupees / Come on, now! How's a kid like you going to pay for these? You're dreaming!|Bomb-Master Cannon|TWW}}</ref> However, after [[Tetra]] and [[Tetra's Pirate Crew|her crew]] come in and steal his Bombs, he lowers the prices.<ref>{{Cite|Now, I don't like to brag, but aren't these just the finest bombs you've ever seen in your entire life? Real fine, and real cheap!|Bomb-Master Cannon|TWW}}</ref>

There is a total of 50 Rupees stored in the five [[barrel]]s behind the counter—10 Green Rupees in each. Link can reach them and break them while Bomb-Master Cannon is still tied up, i.e. while [[Tetra's Ship]] is anchored at Windfall Island.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Main|Bomb Flower Store}}
In {{ALBW}}, the Bomb Flower Store is located in [[Lorule]], south of {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}. It is owned by the [[Bomb-Shop Man]]. The store's only ware is the [[Big Bomb Flower]], which grows in a garden outside of the store. Link can purchase the Big Bomb Flower for 200 Rupees in order to use it to destroy several large [[boulder]]s around Lorule, including the boulder barricading the entrance to the [[Swamp Palace (A Link Between Worlds)|Swamp Palace]].
