The {{Term/Store|Racing Bro|Racing Bros|Series, ALBW|plural}} are a pair of characters in {{ALBW}}.

==Biography==
The {{Plural|ALBW|Racing Bro}} run {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule Hotfoot|link}}, a racing {{Term|ALBW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Younger Racing Bro|link}} is found next to the {{Term|ALBW|Fairy Fountain|link}} east of the {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales|link}}, and acts as the start of the race. By paying 20 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The entry fee is 20 Rupees. If you make it to the finish, you'll get an excellent prize.|Younger Racing Bro|ALBW}}</ref> the {{Term|ALBW|Older Racing Bro|link}} appears as the end goal of the race, just north of [[Miner's House|Rosso's house]].<ref>{{Cite|The finish is behind the miner's place. I'll mark it on your map with a flag. My brother is standing at the finish, so talk to him when you get there.|Younger Racing Bro|ALBW}}</ref> If {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} talks to the {{Term|ALBW|Older Racing Bro}} again after completing the race, he will ask the hero if he would like to be transported back to his younger brother.<ref>{{Cite|Check in with my little brother at the starting point. What, unless it's too much trouble to hightail it all the way back there?|Older Racing Bro|ALBW}}</ref> Accepting will [[Warping|warp]] {{Term|ALBW|Link}} back to the starting point, allowing him to quickly retry the race, if he desires.
