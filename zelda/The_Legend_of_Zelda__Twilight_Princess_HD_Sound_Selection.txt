{{Term/Store|''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Sound Selection]]''||Series, TPHD}} is an original soundtrack for {{TPHD}}. The soundtrack is included in the game's [[amiibo]] bundle in Europe and Japan. In North America, it is a pre-order bonus exclusive to GameStop retailers. It features a limited selection of remastered tracks from {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= We were really happy to remaster the soundtrack so that everyone can appreciate all the distinctive qualities of each track. |author= |published= February 18, 2016|retrieved= February 20, 2016 |url= https://www.facebook.com/LegendofZelda/posts/1024497747611355:0 |title= |site= ''The Legend of Zelda'' official Facebook|type=}}</ref>
