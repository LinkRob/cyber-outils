'''Wizard's Foot''' is a move used by [[Ganondorf]] exclusively in the {{SSB|Series}}.

==Uses==
Wizard's Foot is similar to [[Captain Falcon]]'s {{ssb|Falcon Kick}}. Upon using the move, Ganondorf slides across the ground with his leg forward, and any opponents whom he kicks receive knockback and take damage. It is possible for Ganondorf to use Wizard's Foot while in midair, in which case he kicks at a diagonal angle.
