{{Term/Store|Drill Prow|Drill Prows|Series, PH, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Drill Prow|Shipyard|PH}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Drill Prow}} go unnamed in {{HW|L}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
{{Plural|PH|Drill Prow}} are {{Plural|PH|Prow|link}} belonging to the {{Term|PH|Iron Ship|link}} set of {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}. They are broken and merely decorative.<ref>{{Cite|Broken, and nobody in the world has the parts to fix it.|Shipyard|PH}}</ref> Just like most {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}}, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} can collect multiple {{Plural|PH|Drill Prow}}. They can be obtained through various methods:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Methods}}

Attaching three, six, or all eight {{Term|PH|Iron Ship}} parts to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} will increase its stamina to five, six, or seven {{Plural|PH|Heart}}, respectively. {{Plural|PH|Drill Prow}} can be sold to the {{Term|PH|Treasure Teller|link}} for either 50, 150, or 800 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}. Their value is determined randomly when the [[Saving|Save]] file is created, and is the same for all {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} from the {{Term|PH|Iron Ship}} set.
