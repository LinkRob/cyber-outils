{{Term/Store|Illusory Mansion||Series, TFH}} is a {{Term|TFH|Levels in Tri Force Heroes|display=Level|link}} in {{TFH}}. It is the first Level of {{Term|TFH|The Ruins}} area.

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|TFH|Illusory Mansion}} takes place in a haunted, garden courtyard outside of an abandoned palace. The Level notably features several floating, colored platforms which only the {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} whose color matches it can stand on. If the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} walk onto a platform that does not match, they will fall through. The matching {{Term|TFH|Link}} can pick up the other {{Plural|TFH|Links}} as he walks across, allowing them to cross over these platforms as well.

In '''Stage 1''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} appear on a ledge over a great pit. Three sets of colored platforms lie ahead of them, which the matching {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must use to cross over to the other side.  The next ledge has two {{Plural|TFH|Poe|link}} and three {{Plural|TFH|Boomerang|link}}. Immediately after the second ledge is a bridge of green platforms, leading to blue-colored ones. A gap lies between the blue platforms and the next ledge. To cross this gap, the blue {{Term|TFH|Link}} must first pick up the red {{Term|TFH|Link}} as a {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}}. The green {{Term|TFH|Link}} then has to pick up the blue {{Term|TFH|Link}}, and cross the green walkway to throw him to the next set. The blue {{Term|TFH|Link}} then simply has to throw the red {{Term|TFH|Link}} over the gap, who can then bring the others back using the Boomerang. On the next ledge, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} encounter three more {{Plural|TFH|Poe}} and a locked gate barricading the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}}. Its {{Term|TFH|Key|link}} rests inside a {{Term|TFH|Treasure Chest|link}} atop a small tower. A pair of blue and red platforms lead to its staircase, requiring that blue {{Term|TFH|Link}} throws red {{Term|TFH|Link}} over. Red {{Term|TFH|Link}} can then toss the {{Term|TFH|Key}} over to blue {{Term|TFH|Link}}, or throw it over the tower onto the ground for one of the other {{Plural|TFH|Link}} to collect. Blue {{Term|TFH|Link}} will have to retrieve red {{Term|TFH|Link}} from his platform using the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang}}.

In '''Stage 2''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} enter the mansion's hedge garden. They soon encounter a set of three unlit {{Plural|TFH|Torch|link}}, which spawns colored {{Plural|TFH|Poe}}. These {{Plural|TFH|Poe}} can only be attacked by the {{Term|TFH|Link}} whose tunic matches with their color. Their defeat will light the {{Plural|TFH|Torch}} and drop the {{Term|TFH|Key}} that unlocks the next gate. As the {{Plural|TFH|Link}} approach the gate, two white {{Plural|TFH|Poe}} appear. The gate opens to the next garden with several stone podiums. Six more colored {{Plural|TFH|Poe}} arrive to fight the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}. All of them are floating beyond their reach, and require the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to form a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} to fight them. After they are defeated, the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} appears. On the right side of the area is a {{Term|TFH|Switch|link}} in some tall grass that can be pressed down to lower a wall revealing a {{Term|TFH|Treasure Chest}}, which contains a silver {{Term|TFH|Rupee|link}}.

In '''Stage 3''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} appear in a fenced enclosure next to a ledge. A cracked block to the left blocks the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}' passage. The northern ledge has a small blue platform, as well as a {{Term|TFH|Chasupa|link}} that will attack if the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} get close. A {{Term|TFH|Bomb Flower|link}} sits on a pillar, which is used to demolish the block after being grabbed with the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang}}. The block reveals a heavy {{Term|TFH|Switch}} which, after being pressed with all three {{Plural|TFH|Links}}, reveals more colored platforms to the north side and spawns more {{Plural|TFH|Chasupa}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must cross these platforms to the other side, while throwing each other onto the matching platforms and using the {{Plural|TFH|Boomerang}} to carry each other over. On the other side are a pair of cracked blocks which must be demolished. This can be achieved by having red {{Term|TFH|Link}} pick up the {{Term|TFH|Bomb Flower}} from his platforms and throwing it over to the ledge, allowing another {{Term|TFH|Link}} to pick it up and throw to the blocks.

The next ledge features more colored platforms to the right side, as well as a {{Term|TFH|Life Like|link}} hidden in the north-west among some grass. The blue and red platforms continuously move, requiring the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to time their throws. The red platform moves close to a ledge above, which can only be reached with a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} of three. This is made possible by having blue {{Term|TFH|Link}} carry green {{Term|TFH|Link}} while standing close to the edge of the blue platform, then having red {{Term|TFH|Link}} pick them up when his platform moves close. The top {{Term|TFH|Link}} can then be thrown up to the ledge, where the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} and a {{Term|TFH|Switch}} lie. Pressing the switch rises a drawbridge, allowing the other two {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to walk to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}}.

In '''Stage 4''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} appear on another ledge with more colored platforms, which also move. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must again use a combination of throwing each other across and using the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang}} to get to the other side. As the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} approach the staircase, {{Plural|TFH|Poe}} suddenly appear, three of which are colored. Defeating them reveals a Chest containing three {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} then arrive to a circular platform with a pit in its center, and three {{Plural|TFH|Switch}} to be pressed. The {{Plural|TFH|Switch}} will lower the bridge leading to the platform, and a group of {{Plural|TFH|Prankster Poe|link}} will appear. These {{Plural|TFH|Enemy|link}} will attack the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} by sneaking up to them, then grabbing them to throw off the ledge. Once they are defeated, the bridge to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} appears.
