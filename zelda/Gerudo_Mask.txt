The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Mask||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an item in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Gerudo Mask is one of four special Masks that [[Link]] may borrow after selling all of the Masks that he was asked to sell from the [[Happy Mask Salesman]].<ref>{{Cite|You borrowed the Gerudo Mask. Wear it with (C) to show it off! This mask will make you look like... a girl?|N/A|OoT}}</ref> It serves no significant purpose, merely getting odd reactions from the civilians of [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|YEEEAH! What's your problem?!|Mamamu Yan|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|AAAAAAAAAAAH! A GERUDO!!|Hylian|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wha-? A mask? Why do you have a mask like that?!|Hylian|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, no! You're scary! Don't come any closer!|Hylian|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's kind of funny...it makes me laugh! Hee hee!|Hylian|OoT}}</ref>

While wearing the Mask, it makes people think that Link is a woman and causes [[Gerudo]] thieves to be a bit annoyed.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, kid, you've got guts coming around here wearing that! Still, you can't fool us that easily!|Gerudo|OoT}}</ref> [[Darunia]], the "big boss of the [[Goron]]s", will respond by saying that he hates Gerudos,<ref>{{Cite|I hate Gerudos!!|Darunia|OoT}}</ref> most likely because the Gerudo Leader, [[Ganondorf]], had sealed [[Dodongo's Cavern]]. [[Nabooru]] reacts to the Mask by commenting that it looks like her, but retracts her opinion not long after.<ref>{{Cite|That's odd... it kind of looks like me... No... not really. Not at all, if you look at it closely.|Nabooru|OoT}}</ref> If [[King Zora]] is spoken to with the Gerudo Mask, he responds that it gives him the chills.<ref>{{Cite|That face... it gives me the chills...|King Zora|OoT}}</ref> Although, King Zora's daughter [[Princess Ruto]] will tell Link that he is a "funny guy" when wearing the Mask.<ref>{{Cite|You're a funny guy!|Princess Ruto|OoT}}</ref> [[Talon]] will comment that the Mask reminds him of his wife, but will quickly take back his words.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, that reminds me of my wife... On second thought, it doesn't... It doesn't look like her at all!|Talon|OoT}}</ref> If the {{Term|OoT3D|Dancing Couple|link}} is confronted while the Mask if equipped, the man apologizes, as he does not want to look at any other woman.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry. I don't even want to look at any other woman!|Dancing Couple|OoT}}</ref> While speaking with [[Saria]], she will remark that it looks "cool", but is also frightening.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, cool mask... but a little scary, don't you think?|Saria|OoT}}</ref>
