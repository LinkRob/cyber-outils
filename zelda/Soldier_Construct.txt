The '''Soldier [[Construct]]''' is an Enemy foud in {{TotK}} and [[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment|''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment'']]. They are known for guarding ancient Zonai Structures. 
==Characteristics==
Soldier Constructs are Constructs created for combat by the [[Zonai]]. In ''Tears of the Kingdom'', they can be found in [[Shrine of Light|shrines]], near or in mines in [[The Depths]], and in small camps near fallen chunks of the [[Sky Islands]] on the ground. In ''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment'', most hostile Soldier Constructs are a result of being possessed by the [[Demon King]]. These Constructs are commonly found all around [[Hyrule]]. Other hostile Soldier Constructs in ''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment'' are found in The Depths and Sky Islands, defending the ancient Zonai Structures left before the events of the game. The Constructs vary between 4 levels, getting progressively stronger as you go on.

== Methods of Attacking ==
Soldier Constructs usually appear in groups wielding a variety of  [[Zonaite]] weapons. In ''Tears of the Kingdom'', they can also be found, wielding [[Old Wooden Bows]], [[Old Wooden Shields]], Rusty weapons, [[Wooden Stick|Wooden Sticks]], and even [[Tree Branch|Tree Branches.]] Soldier Constructs can also use the blade on top of their heads as a weapon. Before a Construct attacks, their eye glows, signalling the player to either dodge or [[Perfect Guard|parry]] the attack. In ''Tears of the Kingdom'', if a Soldier Construct does not have a weapon, they will hit [[Link]] instead or throw rocks at him from afar.

=== In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'' ===
These Constructs appear in small camps, usually accompanied with a [[Captain Construct]] of around the same level. Unlike other Enemies in ''Tears of the Kingdom'', Soldier Constructs only attack Link because they see him as a trespasser. If a Construct notices Link, they will make an alarm, alerting all nearby Constructs. If a Construct find an Enemy, they will attack it instead. When a Construct is defeated, they drop a [[Zonai Charge]] and their [[Soldier Construct Horn|horn]].

=== In ''[[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment]]'' ===
The Constructs appear in large groups. Depending on the mission, they will either be possessed by the Demon King or simply attacking to protect the location they are guarding. There is no difference between a construct being possessed or not. Similarly, a Soldier Construct drops their [[Soldier Construct Horn|horn]], which can be used for [[Fuse Attacks.]]
