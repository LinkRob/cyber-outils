==Biography==
McNey was an esteemed explorer who spent much of his lifetime trying to discover the legendary treasure held dearly by the [[Cobble]] Kingdom: the [[Aquanine]]. Much of his findings were left written in an article for the "[[Explorer's Compass]]".

Eventually, McNey ended up in the [[Isle of the Dead]] and stumbled upon the [[Phantom Corridor]] and prematurely entered it before solving the corridor's puzzle. As a result, he ended up wandering its halls until his death.<ref name="Introduction"/> When [[Link]] arrives to the isle and makes his way to the Phantom Corridor, McNey advises him that, if he still has not solved the corridor's puzzle, he must find the entrance as soon as he can before he meets the same fate as the deceased explorer.<ref>{{Cite|If I keep going down, I should be able to reach the entrance... If you haven't solved the puzzle yet, find the entrance while you can. Otherwise, you'll end up like me and run out of energy... Before you're able to find the exit, cursed to remain here forever.|McNey|PH}}</ref>
