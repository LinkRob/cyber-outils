{{Term/Store|Cake|Cakes|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Our daughter... The only thing she'll eat is {{Color|BotW Red|cake}}. Can't anything be done?|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Cake}} go unnamed in {{TMC}} and {{HWAoC}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Slice of Cake}}
{{Plural|TMC|Slice of Cake}} are sold at the {{Term|TMC|Bakery|link}} for 60 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}. Eating them will heal one of {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}'s {{Plural|TMC|Heart}} and has a chance to give him a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 138}}</ref>

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is able {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}} a variety of {{Plural|BotW|Cake}}. All {{Plural|BotW|Cake}} require both {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar|link}} in their creation, while all but {{Plural|BotW|Fruitcake|link}} also utilize {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} eats these {{Plural|BotW|Cake}}, several of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} will be healed. The exact number is dependent on the type of {{Term|BotW|Cake}} he eats and what ingredients were used to make it.
