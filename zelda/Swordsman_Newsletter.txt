The {{Term/Store|''Swordsman Newsletter''|''Swordsman Newsletters''|Series, TMC|plural}} are issues written by {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm selling issues of a helpful adventurer's guide... It's called the {{Color|TMC Red|Swordsman Newsletter}}.|Marcy|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
After {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Postman|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can buy an issue from {{Term|TMC|Marcy|link}} at the {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}} for 200 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}. Every time {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains a new {{Term|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}}, a new issue is made available, for a total of 8 newsletters.
