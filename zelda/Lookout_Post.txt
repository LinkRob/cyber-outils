The {{Term/Store|Lookout Post||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let's meet up at the {{Color|BotW Red|lookout post}} {{Color|BotW Blue|south of town}}.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Lookout Post}} was referred to as the '''Observation Post''' in {{CaC}}. However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher=  |page= 322 }}</ref>}}

==Features and Overview==
The Lookout Post is a small post south of [[Gerudo Town]] in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. The Lookout Post is where the [[Gerudo]] soldier, [[Sudrey]], monitors the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|My orders from the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} are to monitor the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} from here.|Sudrey|BotW}}</ref> Naboris is always near the Post, walking around and kicking up a massive sandstorm. The [[Divine Beast]] cannot harm [[Link]] with its lightning attack while he is inside the Lookout Post. A ladder outside of the Lookout Post leads up to a small wooden platform. There is a bed inside the Lookout Post that Link can sleep on.

After Vah Naboris has been cleared of [[Thunderblight Ganon]]'s influence, Sudrey will remain here to monitor the monsters that prowl the Gerudo Desert.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} has calmed down, but the monsters around here haven't. I'll patrol the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Desert}} for a while more and keep an eye on things here.|Sudrey|BotW}}</ref>

A [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Golden Bow]] resides inside the Lookout Post.
