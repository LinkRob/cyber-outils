{{Name|Japanese|{{Term/Store|Masaru||Series}}}} is a mischievous [[monkey]] in {{RTBToL}}.{{Name Ref|RTBToL}} He lives in [[Page 6]] and in [[Page 7]]. When [[Tingle]] first encounters Masaru, he is mocked by the monkey, whom he cannot reach, as he is up in a tree. Masaru has a fear of dogs, and when Tingle lures him to the ground with a [[Bananas|Banana]], the dog will attack Masaru. During the fight, Masaru will drop the dentures that he stole from a fisherman. 

Later on, Tingle has the opportunity to have Masaru temporarily join his party, if he allows [[Kakashi]] to remain behind. Masaru can help out by climbing trees, and bringing down fruits and even a hat at times. He can also perform several poses which have no effect. After [[Characters in Ripened Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love#Iona|Iona]] unlocks the gate, Masaru will captain the boat that takes Tingle to an island. 

==Nomenclature==
Masaru's name is derived from the Japanese word, {{Romanize|猿|Saru}}, meaning monkey. Masaru is also a common name meaning "victory".
