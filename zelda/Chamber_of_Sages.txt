The {{Term/Store|Chamber of Sages||Series, OoT, OoT3D, ALBW}} is a location in {{OoT}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 25}}</ref> and {{ALBW}}.

==Features and Overview==
The Chamber of Sages is where the [[Sage|Sages]] meet. Each Sage stands on a dais along the edge of the central platform, in the middle of which is a triangular space where {{Term|Series|Link|link}} stands when meeting with the Sages.

In {{OoT|-}}, the chamber is filled with fountains, up from each of which a beam of light shines, except for the large one in the middle, which depicts the {{Term|Series|Triforce|link}} surrounded by the six {{Term|OoT|Sage Medallion|link}}s. Link stands in the empty space of the Triforce image while each Sage stands on the image of the Medallion they give to him.

In {{ALBW|-}}, the chamber has been redesigned into an all-stone platform in a void illuminated by various auroras. Gone are the fountains in the background, and both the flowing water around the central platform, and the Triforce image in the middle, have been replaced with an ornate design with a lone triangle in the middle. It also features a seventh Sage's dais to the six in {{OoT|-}}. Each dais contains a letter from the word "COURAGE" instead of a symbol of the Sage's element.

=={{OoT}}==
After Link withdraws the Master Sword from the Pedestal of Time, his spirit is sealed away in the Chamber of Sages due to him being too young to bear the title of the "Hero of Time".<ref name="hero">{{Cite|The Master Sword is a sacred blade which evil ones may never touch... Only one worthy of the title of "Hero of Time" can pull it from the Pedestal of Time.... However, you were too young to be the Hero of Time... Therefore, your spirit was sealed here for seven years. And now that you are old enough, the time has come for you to awaken as the Hero of Time!|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> Since Link's action opened the gate to the Sacred Realm, [[Ganondorf]] took this opportunity to gain access to the Sacred Realm, eventually reaching the Temple of Light and taking the Triforce for himself.<ref>{{Cite|But, remember... Though you opened the Door of Time in the name of peace... Ganondorf, the Gerudo King of Thieves, used it to enter this forbidden Sacred Realm! He obtained the Triforce from the Temple of Light, and with its power, he became the King of Evil...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> While the Sacred Realm transformed into the [[Dark World]],<ref>{{Cite|The resting place of the sacred triangle, the Sacred Realm, is a mirror that reflects what is in the heart... the heart of one who enters it... If an evil heart, the Realm will become full of evil; if pure, the Realm will become a paradise.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Rauru used his powers to keep the Chamber of Sages intact.<ref>{{Cite|My power now has only little influence, even in this Sacred Realm... Namely, this Chamber of Sages.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref>

Seven years pass, and [[Rauru]], the Sage of Light, awakens Link in the Chamber of Sages now that he is old enough to be considered the Hero of Time.<ref name="hero" /> Rauru explains Link's fate and grants him the [[Light Medallion]] to add to his power, sending off the young hero to awaken the other five Sages so that they may add their might to Link and aid him on his quest to save Hyrule from Ganondorf's rule.<ref>{{Cite|I, Rauru, am one of the Sages... And... Your power to fight together with the Sages makes you the Hero of Time! The Hero of Time, chosen by the Master Sword! Keep my spirit with you... And, find the power of the other Sages and add their might to your own!|Rauru|OoT}}</ref>

Though Link is not able to visit the Temple of Light on his own, he is always warped to the Chamber of Sages every time he awakens a Sage. Here, the Sage bestows their powers to Link in form of a [[Medallions|Medallion]] before returning to Hyrule again.

At the end of the game, after Link gives the final strike to Ganon and [[Princess Zelda]] calls upon the power of the Sages to seal Ganondorf away, Rauru and the other five Sages appear inside the Chamber of Sages to send the King of Evil into the void of the Evil Realm.<ref>{{Cite|Ancient Creators of Hyrule! Now, open the sealed door and send the Evil Incarnation of Darkness into the void of the Evil Realm!!|Rauru|OoT}}</ref>

=={{ALBW}}==
The Chamber of Sages returns in {{ALBW|-}}, albeit unnamed. As with {{OoT|-}} before it, Link arrives here after rescuing a Sage within a dungeon, watches the rescued Sage emerge from a crystal, shares a short conversation with them, and promptly takes his leave to rescue the next one.

After all seven Sages are rescued, each one's dais raises a set height, staircases of light appear between them, and at the top, on a ninth platform directly above where Link stands, the {{Term|Series|Triforce of Courage|link}} manifests for Link to claim, having proven his courage by braving so many dungeons and rescuing the Sages.
