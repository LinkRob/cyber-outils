The '''Jungle of Favors''' is a location mentioned in the book {{TSP}}.

After the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule|link}} calls a Royal Council informing about the latest sightings of monsters appearing in strange places, he mentions that an entire mob of {{Plural|TSP|Darknut|link}} were spotted in the {{Term|TSP|Jungle of Favors}} the night before.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Word came last night that an entire gang of Darknuts were spotted in the Jungle of Favors.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 5}}</ref> Since {{Plural|TSP|Darknut}} don't usually appear outside of a cave or a castle, the king considers the spotting of the {{Plural|TSP|Darknut}} in a jungle to be alarming and decides that the {{Term|TSP|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} should be immediately delivered to the {{Term|TSP|Fifth Castle of Ancient Hyrule|link}} to keep it safe from {{Term|TSP|Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='I believe Ganon is after our Triforce of Wisdom again. It's in danger here. We must move it.' [...] To the Fifth Castle of Ancient Hyrule.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 6}}</ref>
