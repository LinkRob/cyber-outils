The {{Term/Store|Maritta Exchange Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Maritta Exchange Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Maritta Exchange Ruins are located in northwestern [[Central Hyrule]]. The [[Serenne Stable]] is nearby to the east. This exchange was the hub of trade between inhabitants of the [[Hebra]] and [[Tabantha Frontier|Tabantha]] regions to the west, and those of Hyrule to the east. <ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 396}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
The Maritta Exchange Ruins share their Japanese name, {{Romanize|マリッタ|Maritta}}, with [[Salari Hill]], [[Salari Plain]], and the [[Serenne Stable]].
