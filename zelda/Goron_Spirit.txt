{{Term/Store|Goron Spirit||HWAoC}} is a level 57 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Goron Spirit|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Goron Spirit}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Warm Safflina|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Goron Spice|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}}'s objective is to capture three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} while defending an Allied Stronghold. An {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Lynel|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}} is at the head of one of the target {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}}. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} to be defended against consist of two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} that attack the Allied Stronghold at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, along with an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} that spawn when a target {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is captured. When another one is captured, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} spawn with the same goal. When all three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.
