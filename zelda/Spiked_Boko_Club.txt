{{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Club}} are common {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. Sharpened bones have been added to them to increase their damage.<ref name="Inventory"/> These {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Club|display=Clubs}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} may first see one in a {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} near the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|link}}, where it is being wielded by a {{Term|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}}. After that, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find them in various {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Plural|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} on his adventure, particularly in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. They can also appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn after the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} has been scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

Due to their wooden composition, {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Club}} will burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. Enemy {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} will often ignite them at a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} before attacking to deal additional {{Term|BotW|Fire}} damage. They also do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} like metallic {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} do, and they will float on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} if dropped. However, the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} is unable to pick them up.
