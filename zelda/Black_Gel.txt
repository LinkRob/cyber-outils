{{Term/Store|Black Gel|Black Gels|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Black Gel|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Black Gel}} are slime-like enemies that only appear after a {{Term|CoH|Black Zol|link}} is defeated. Unlike their {{Term|CoH|Zol|link}} parent, they move on every second music beat towards any adjacent tile, trying to chase and attack {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}.
