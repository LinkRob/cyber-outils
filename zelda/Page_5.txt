==Features and Overview==
Page 5 is a deserted train station and a junkyard. The only inhabitants are living machine parts which must be collected in order to make the [[Tinliner|train]] run. When it's repaired, Tingle rides it to [[Page 6]]. After this page, the train becomes Tingle's main method of travel. [[Segāre]] makes his second appearance here shortly after Tingle's departure.
