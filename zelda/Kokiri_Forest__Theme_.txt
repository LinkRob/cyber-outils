"'''Kokiri Forest (Theme)'''" is a [[Song]] in {{OoT}}.

==Overview==
The track is associated with the eponymous location, {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Forest|link}}.

The track appears as Track 6 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Original Sound Track]]'' and Track 2 of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Hyrule Symphony]]''.

==Trivia==
*In {{TWW}}, the music that plays in {{Term|TWW|Outset Island|link}} contains a remix of part of "Kokiri Forest." After {{Term|TWW|Aryll|link}} gets kidnapped by {{Term|TWW|Helmaroc King|link}} and [[Link]] returns to Outset Island, another remix of part of "Kokiri Forest" is incorporated into the music. A much slower remix of the music also plays for the exterior of the {{Term|TWW|Forest Haven|link}}.
