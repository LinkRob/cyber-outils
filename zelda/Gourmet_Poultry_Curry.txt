{{Term/Store|Gourmet Poultry Curry|Gourmet Poultry Curries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Gourmet Poultry Curries are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Raw Whole Bird]], [[Goron Spice]], and [[Hylian Rice]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they'll heal several of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Prior to the second [[Great Calamity]], these meals were served in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, and the poultry used in making it is of high quality.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding more ingredients, different variations of Gourmet Poultry Curry can be made:

* '''Hearty Gourmet Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these curries will completely heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Gourmet Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. If eaten, they'll replenish a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Gourmet Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. When consumed, they'll completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Gourmet Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. Link's movement speed is boosted by eating these curries.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Gourmet Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Gourmet Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. When eaten, [[Link]] will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Gourmet Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. If eaten, they'll boost the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Gourmet Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. They increase Link's defense if consumed.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Gourmet Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When Link eats these, he'll receive an increase to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period of time.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Gourmet Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Gourmet Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Once served in Hyrule Castle, the poultry used in this dish is of immensely high quality.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding cold ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] temporarily.<ref name="Chilly"/>
