The {{Term/Store|Invoice||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an item in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Invoice}} was referred to as '''Doctor's Invoice''' in {{Guide|TP|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Nintendo|page= 192}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
The Invoice is a document which details the debt of [[Borville]], the town's doctor, in [[Telma's Bar]]. Seeing as he is an uncooperative being, [[Telma]] gives [[Link]] the Invoice and suggests showing it to Borville in order to learn the location of the [[Wooden Statue]], as it will assist Link in recovering [[Ilia]]'s [[Retrieval of Ilia's Memory|memory]].

==Trivia==
*The Hylian written on the Invoice is mostly gibberish. However, the number 175480000 can be seen as well, which might be the number of [[Rupee]]s Borville owes to Telma.
