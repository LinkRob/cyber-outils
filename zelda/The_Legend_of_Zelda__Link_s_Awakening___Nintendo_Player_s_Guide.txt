'''''The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening — Nintendo Player's Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Nintendo]] for {{LA}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The book features 112 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{LA}}, lists of collectibles such as {{Plural|LA|Piece of Heart|link}} and {{Plural|LA|Secret Seashell|link}}, a detailed enemy listing, and other tips and secrets.
