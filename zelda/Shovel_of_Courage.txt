{{Term/Store|Shovel of Courage|Shovels of Courage|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Shovel of Courage}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Shovel of Courage}} are items that allow {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} to dig through walls and mounds of dirt, but grants the heroes the ability to automatically dash into the space they just dug, allowing them to speedily dig their way through tight situations or quickly maneuver through dungeons.<ref>{{Cite|Allows you to dig through walls. You dash forward into the space you just dug.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> Like other {{Plural|CoH|Shovel|link}}, using them to dig costs durability, and using up a {{Term|CoH|Shovel}}'s durability completely will cause it to wear out and disappear from the hero's inventory unless the {{Term|CoH|Glass Ring of Preservation|link}} is equipped.

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}}.
