The {{Term/Store|Akh Va'quot Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Akh Va'quot Shrine}}<br/>Windmills}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Windmills''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The Akh Va'quot Shrine is located in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, southwest of the {{Term|BotW|Brazen Beak|link}}. As Link enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the Shrine's {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Akh Va'quot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Akh Va'quot. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Akh Va'quot|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Akh Va'quot Shrine features a central chamber containing six fans and 14 windmills. In order to complete the Trial, Link must strike the {{Plural|BotW|Switch|link}} accompanying the fans to rotate them in an order that hits all windmills at once. Once this is done, a portcullis leading to Akh Va'quot will rise allowing Link to speak to him. He will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Akh Va'quot|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Akh Va'quot|BotW}}</ref>

A room behind the elevator features a long pool of water and a floating platform holding a Switch and a fan that will release a portcullis protecting a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Core|link}}. Two niches along the wall of the pool contain two Treasure Chests, containing a {{Term|BotW|Sapphire|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Feathered Spear|link}} respectively.

==Trivia==
* It is possible for Link to lock himself in the section of the Shrine with Akh Va'quot by striking one of the Switches that releases the portcullis holding the Monk in.
