{{Term/Store|Dekadin||Series, OoA}} is a character from {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Dekadin is a sad, humorless boy who lives in his {{Term|OoA|Dekadin's House|link|display=House}} in [[Lynna Village]] in the past, where the [[Comedian]] is found in the present. Dekadin's house is very dark and gloomy, which suits the boy's mood. The cause of Dekadin's depression is implied to be due to be partially due to a recent book, he read.

Dekadin plays a role in the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest|link}}. Link can tell Dekadin the [[Funny Joke]] in an attempt to make Dekadin laugh. As thanks, Dekadin will let Link take a book from his bookshelf.<ref>{{Cite|Oh...That's so funny, I forgot to laugh. Thanks... Thanks to you, I can be as depressed as I want to be... You can take any book you want from that bookshelf...|Dekadin|OoA}}</ref> This book happens to be the [[Touching Book]], the next item in the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest}}.
