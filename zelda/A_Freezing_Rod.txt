"{{Term/Store|A Freezing Rod||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Freezing Rod}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Great Hyrule Forest}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter the {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} {{Term|BotW|Kula|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Kula}} welcomes him to the forest,<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Are you the hero? Could it be? Welcome to Korok Forest!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> remarking that he is much cooler than he thought.<ref>{{Cite|You're...much, much cooler looking than I thought you'd be!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that he prepared a trial for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to ensure that he is up to his task as a hero.<ref>{{Cite|But to be sure you're up to the task at hand...I prepared a {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} for you!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is willing to accept his trial.<ref>{{Cite|What do you think?! Will you accept my {{Color|BotW Red|trial}}?|Kula|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts {{Term|BotW|Kula}}'s trial, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} requests that he show him a {{Term|BotW|Rod|link}} with the power of {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll do it!] Hooray! Show me a {{Color|BotW Red|rod with the power of ice}}, please!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> He goes on to explain that these {{Plural|BotW|Rod}} can freeze {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, and they are commonly wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can wave this rod to freeze monsters in their tracks! {{Color|BotW Blue|Ice Wizzrobes}} love to wreak havoc with them.|Kula|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kula}} says that {{Term|BotW|Link}} would be stronger and cooler if he had one.<ref>{{Cite|If Mr. Hero were to wield a {{Color|BotW Red|rod with the power of ice}}, he would be even stronger...and cooler!|Kula|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Kula}} once he obtains an {{Term|BotW|Ice Rod|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Looks like you've located an {{Color|BotW Red|ice rod}}!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have the {{Term|BotW|Rod}} equipped, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} tells him to do so.<ref>{{Cite|But...you're going to have to equip it to impress me!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kula}} enjoys how sparkly the {{Term|BotW|Ice Rod}} is.<ref>{{Cite|Shimmering! Sparkling! What a sigh to behold!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> He thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}}, giving him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Mr. Hero. Your weapon is so amazingly, wonderfully sparkly! I must give you something as thanks!|Kula|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kula}} will also accept a {{Term|BotW|Blizzard Rod|link}} for this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|Is that a {{Color|BotW Blue|blizzard rod}}?! Incredible shimmer! Unbelievable sparkle!|Kula|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, {{Term|BotW|Kula}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.
