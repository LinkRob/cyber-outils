'''Master Kohga''' is a recurring character and Boss in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Master Kohga}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>
==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Master Kohga}}
Master Kohga is the leader of the [[Yiga]], an opposing Clan to the [[Sheikah]] who hunt [[Link]] on a ceaseless mission.<ref name="Compendium"/> They are also known for their loyalty to [[Calamity Ganon]]. Master Kohga is a respected figure of authority, but he spends most of his time napping and loafing around.<ref name="Compendium"/> He loves [[Mighty Bananas]] the most.<ref>{{Cite|You know what the leader of the Yiga Clan loves the most? You'll never guess. Bananas!|Endai|BotW}}</ref> Master Kohga is known to possess many secrets about the Sheikah.<ref>{{Cite|He's the leader of the Yiga Clan, master of countless Sheikah secrets and sworn enemy of a hero thought long dead!|Traveler|BotW}}</ref>
Link meets Master Kohga during the "[[Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)]]" Main Quest outside of the exit of the [[Yiga Clan Hideout]], in an open area with a large pit in the center. Initially, he berates Link for invading his napping spot.<ref>{{Cite|Who the heck are you? And what are you doing in my napping spot?!|Master Kohga|BotW}}</ref> However, Master Kohga will then recognize the [[Sheikah Slate]] he is carrying, which causes him to realize that Link is the hero he has been looking for.<ref>{{Cite|That thing on your hip... Is that... Could it be a Sheikah Slate? If it's a Sheikah Slate... Then that means... Yeah, IT'S YOU! You're that Link guy I've been looking for!|Master Kohga|BotW}}</ref> He comments on his luck, as Link wandered right into the [[Yiga Clan Hideout]] while all of his scouts are looking for him.<ref>{{Cite|What luck! My scouts are out in the field looking high and low, but you just wander into my hideout!|Master Kohga|BotW}}</ref> Master Kohga introduces himself to Link, and he tells him to prepare himself before he attacks.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, excuse me. I haven't introduced myself yet. I am the leader... Of the Yiga Clan! The strong! The burly!! The one!! The only!!! MASTER KOHGA!!! Now, prepare yourself!|Master Kohga|BotW}}</ref>
The fight against Master Kohga consists of three phases, each of which utilizes his control over magnetism and levitation. In the first phase, Master Kohga conjures up a rock above him to hurl at Link. Striking Master Kohga from afar as he levitates in the air, such as with a [[Bow]], [[Remote Bomb]] or thrown [[Weapon]], will break his focus and cause the rock to fall on his head. This temporarily stuns Master Kohga, partially burying him in the ground and allowing Link to attack him unopposed. In the second phase, he levitates above the large pit nearby and conjures up two rocks that spin around him. Link must hit Master Kohga when one of the rocks is directly above his head, causing it to once again fall on his head and stun him. In the third and final phase, he conjures up a fortified boulder to attempt to crush Link. Link must use [[Magnesis]] to gain control of the boulder and push it back towards Kohga. This will stun Kohga once again and give Link the opportunity to finish the fight. In all phases, he is protected by a blue shield which disappears when he goes offensive in the first two stages but remains permanently during the third. [[Ancient Arrow]]s act like normal arrows when Master Kohga is hit with one, and he and his boulders cannot be stopped with [[Stasis]] at any time. He is the only notable Sheikah to display this, as even [[Maz Koshia]] can be placed under [[Stasis]].
After defeating Master Kohga, he will use his last bit of energy to perform his "secret technique", which backfires and causes him to fall down the large pit in the center of the area.<ref>{{Cite|I need to bust out my serious moves... A secret technique taught by my father's mother's father! It will...destroy you!|Master Kohga|BotW}}</ref> While falling down the pit, Master Kohga is heard yelling that he shall be remembered and the Yiga will track Link to the ends of [[Hyrule Kingdom]].<ref>{{Cite|Coward! I shall be remembered! The Yiga Clan will track you to the ends of Hyruuuule!|Master Kohga|BotW}}</ref> As a reward for defeating him, Link will receive a [[Treasure Chest]] containing the [[Thunder Helm]].

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Compendium|number=Master Kohga|locs=Unknown|loot=None|desc=The leader of the Yiga Clan, who worship the Demon King. His larger-than-life presence makes him popular and deeply respected. After the Upheaval, Kohga latched on to Zonai devices as a way to increase his power. He had his underlings use the technology to make a variety of bizarre and deadly contractions to kill you with.}}
Kohga is revealed to have survived his fall, and now seeks the power of the [[Zonai]] so he can construct a weapon to win Ganondorf's favor. He is fought on four different occasions, using a different vehicle in each battle. The first battle is fought at the Great Abandoned Central Mine, after the player attains the [[Autobuild]] ability. Master Kohga appears, and then recognizes Link, challenging him to a battle. Master Kohga uses a giant truck with spikes attached to the front to ram Link. The player must dodge the vehicle and either board it to melee attack him or shoot Master Kohga with arrows to stun him. After being defeated, Master Kohga retreats in a Fanplane, leaving Link with a Huge Crystallized Charge.
He is next encountered at the Abandoned Gerudo Mine, futiley trying to activate a Steward Construct before noticing Link behind him, then brings out a Fanplane complete with Cannons. To defeat Master Kohga this time, Link must shoot him with an arrow to knock him out of the air, then attack him. Having been beaten again, Master Kohga gives up on trying to acquire the power Link possesses and flies on to the Abandoned Lanayru Mine, leaving behind another Huge Crystallized Charge.
During the fight at the Abandoned Lanayru Mine, Master Kohga conjures a boat and summons two Yiga Footsoldiers armed with Duplex Bows to assist him. Link must fuse a Rocket to one of his shields to fly high into the air, then shoot Kohga from above, or try to land on the boat and attack him. Kohga and his lackeys then disappear and reappear on another boat; during this phase, Master Kohga summons boulders to hurl up at Link. At the end of the battle, Master Kohga reveals that his troops have found Ganondorf, and that he has sufficient Crystallized Charges for his weapon, then heads for the Abandoned Hebra Mine, forking over yet another Huge Crystallized Charge.
Upon reaching the Abandoned Hebra Mine, accessible from a chasm beneath [[Rito Village]], Link finds Master Kohga sitting in the center of the mine, and, after some taunting, he brings out his Zonai Construct, vowing to take Link down for good this time. This battle will be easier if the player has already awakened [[Mineru]] and has her construct at the ready: Master Kohga's construct fights fairly similarly to the [[Seized Construct]]. Link must use Mineru's construct to punch Kohga into the wires surrounding the arena, stunning him, and then attack him. Once half of his health is depleted, Master Kohga starts attacking with huge spiked metal balls, that can be sent back at him via Recall. Upon defeat, Master Kohga laments on the destruction of the weapon he worked so hard on, then attempts to destroy Link with his brand new Kohga Rocket, which backfires again, sending him flying out of the depths to the sky while vowing to get revenge.

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
Master Kohga retroactively debuts in {{HWAoC}}, first disguising himself as [[Urbosa]] to try and turn the [[Gerudo]] against Princess Zelda, but he is zapped by the real Urbosa. He is later defeated, but [[Sooga]] saves him at the last minute.<ref>{{Cite|Sooga, you're late! I nearly got peeled like a banana!|Master Kohga|HWAoC}}</ref> He is later shown working alongside [[Astor]] as he explains Calamity Ganon's [[Harbinger form]], and the role the Demon King will play in due time.
Later, the Hyrulean Army raids his hideout and defeats them, despite his best efforts. Master Kohga berates Astor for missing the show and wants Sooga to kill him,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, prophecy man! You missed the part where we get walloped!|Master Kohga|HWAoC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I am afraid we cannot overlook this failure, seer.|Sooga|HWAoC}}</ref> though they relent when the prophet ominously explains that Kohga and the [[Yiga]] would become enemies of Calamity Ganon by harming him.<ref>{{Cite|Hm. Fate decides all...even this defeat. As you well know, if you decide to turn against me... ...you would make an enemy of Calamity Ganon.|Astor|HWAoC}}</ref>
Later, after Calamity Ganon awakens on Princess Zelda's 17th birthday and the [[Blight Ganon]]s are stopped from killing the [[Champions]] like they were supposed to in {{BotW}}, Master Kohga objects to Astor harvesting souls from his men to revive the Blight Ganon. He is almost killed himself by a [[Hollow]] of [[Link]], but Sooga intervenes and tells him to run, stating that he would give his life to protect him. Kohga ultimately chooses to stay and fight alongside him. The outcome of the battle is revealed in "[[EX The Yiga Clan's Retreat]]" [[Scenario]], where they fight through many of their former allies and even the four Blight Ganon once again. A flashback after the Scenario is completed reveals that Kohga gave a young Sooga a [[Mighty Banana]] to help him, despite two [[Yiga Footsoldier]]s attacking him. Master Kohga and Sooga manage to escape to [[Crenel Peak]], where the Blight Ganons strike once again. Sooga manages to freeze the Blights so Master Kohga can escape. It is unknown what happens to Sooga. After Princess Zelda has awakened her power and rallied the [[Hylian]]s, Master Kohga sheds his Hyrulean Soldier disguise and bows before her, joining the Hyrulean forces in order to avenge his betrayed clan.
At a unknown point, Master Kohga appears during the "[[EX Liberate the Royal Ancient Lab]]" Scenario, where he and the [[Yiga|Yiga Clan]] try to stop Urbosa and [[Riju]] from helping the Hyrulean Forces. He is defeated yet again, but he takes the [[Thunder Helm]] from Riju and boasts that Urbosa is unable to hit him with her [[Lightning]] this time.<ref>{{cite|As long as I...have...THIS...Your silly lightning can't hurt me one little-|Master Kohga|HWAoC}}</ref> However, she manages to strike him again anyway. Master Kohga vows to steal the Thunder Helm again and properly master it, even if it takes him a 100 years to do so, referencing the successful theft of the [[Thunder Helm|Helm]] in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Even if it does take a hundred years, it will be mine! Just you wait!|Master Kohga|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
The name Kohga is inherited by leaders of the [[Yiga|Yiga Clan]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 369}}</ref> Together, the Yiga and Kohga reference the rival [[Wp:Ninja|Ninja]] clans of ''Iga'' and ''Kōga''; this is mirrored by the [[Yiga|Yiga Clan]] having a rival clan, the Sheikah.
{{Nomenclature}}

==Trivia==
* Master Kohga is the only antagonist in {{BotW}} to have any dialogue.
** Master Kohga is also the only boss in the base game's main campaign that is not a manifestation of [[Malice]] bearing the title of [[Ganon]].
** The reason why Calamity Ganon couldn't revive Master Kohga in {{BotW}} was because he survived his fall down the [[Yiga Clan Hideout Chasm]] into [[the Depths]] before reappearing alive in {{TotK}}.
