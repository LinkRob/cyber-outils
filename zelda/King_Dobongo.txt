==Characteristics==
{{Improve|February 2021}}
{{Term|CoH|King Dobongo}} will pace around its arena, stopping and performing a spin attack each time it reaches a corner. It can shoot fireballs out of its mouth, but it must take a short time to charge first. While charging, if a {{Term|CoH|Bomb|link}} is placed in front of it, the {{Term|CoH|Bomb}} will be inhaled, damaging {{Term|CoH|King Dobongo}}.
