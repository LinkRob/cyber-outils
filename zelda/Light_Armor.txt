The {{Term/Store|Light Armor||Series, TFH}} is an {{Term|TFH|Item|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 129}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Light Armor is a must when heading somewhere dark. Banish those unflattering lighting conditions!}}

[[File:TFH Forest Temple Light Armor.png|right|thumb|Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Light Armor}} in the {{Term|TFH|Forest Temple}}]]
The {{Term|TFH|Light Armor}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}, at a cost of one {{Term|TFH|Carrumpkin|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Poe Soul|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Spider Silk Lace|link}} and 1,200 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. It becomes available for purchase after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes {{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}.

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Light Armor}} illuminates the area around {{Term|TFH|Link}} with a radius of six to seven floor tiles. This effect is most useful for the {{Term|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} "Adventure in the dark!", as very few {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} ordinarily take place in the darkness. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} has the bonus effect of allowing {{Term|TFH|Link}} to peek into the tent at the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}}'s stall to find Vol. 7 of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands Diary|link}}.

In the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}}, the {{Term|TFH|Light Armor}} will burn any opposing {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} who come into contact or attempt to {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}} the wearer, inflicting one {{Term|TFH|Heart}} of damage and sending them running frantically.
