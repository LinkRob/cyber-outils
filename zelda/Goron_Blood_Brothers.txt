The {{Term/Store|Goron Blood Brother|Goron Blood Brothers|Series, BotW|plural}} are a trio of {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} siblings in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is a special platform! Sacred even! Only real Goron men, as decided by us three Goron Blood Brothers, are allowed.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} consist of {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} the eldest,<ref>{{Cite|If ya cleared my older bro's training... you must be pretty impressive!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Heehl}}, and {{Term|BotW|Kabetta}} the youngest.<ref>{{Cite|Besides, my littlest bro is in the middle of trainin' right now. A brother does NOT interrupt a brother's trainin', brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> The three brothers are each highly engrossed in difficult training exercises of strength and endurance.<ref>{{Cite|PUMP IT UP, BROTHER! Ya did a good job stayin' strong during my {{Color|BotW Red|contest of ultimate endurance}}! In fact, I devised the perfect training program to help take your strength to the next level! I'll be waitin' for ya to take the {{Color|BotW Red|Gut Check Challenge}}. You'll find it to the north of {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}, brother.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|FEEL THE BURN, BROTHER! You are a Goron among Hylians, as recognized by the three Goron Blood Brothers! But I'm continuing my endurance training every day to make sure I never lose to you again!|Heehl|BotW}}</ref> Their home is located in southern {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}, however it is abandoned and left with a sign saying they have left to train.<ref>{{Cite|We are currently training at Gut Check Rock north of Death Mountain. Come watch us train at Gut Check Rock!<br/>- Bayge, Heehl, and Kabetta|Signpost|BotW}}</ref> This is because they were struggling to cope with the heat of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We're Gorons, but we cant' really stand this extreme heat either... That's why we came here to train! After this, we'll be able to handle the extra heat in Goron City, no problem.|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's too hot to handle in Goron City...so we came here to train ourselves...|Kabetta|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} are involved in two {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}: the "{{Term|BotW|Test of Will|link}}" and "{{Term|BotW|The Gut Check Challenge|link}}". In the former, the three brothers have traveled to {{Term|BotW|Mount Nabooru}} to train in heat endurance, and challenge {{Term|BotW|Link}} to join them. Beating both of their contests reveals the {{Term|BotW|Joloo Nah Shrine|link}}. In "{{Term|BotW|The Gut Check Challenge}}", {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} challenges {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} where he must climb {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}} while collecting enough {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. Beating the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} permits {{Term|BotW|Link}} to approach the {{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr Shrine|link}}.

Upon first clearing the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} becomes so impressed with {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he appoints him as the honorary fourth {{Term|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}}.<ref>{{Cite|UNBELIEVABLE!! You've passed our training with the grit of a true Goron, my brother! In fact...you've earned the right to call yourself...the fourth Goron Blood Brother!! As promised, you are now allowed to stand on this sacred platform of Goron manliness. A real honor, I know. You're officially one of us now! Our true Goron brother, brother! Welcome to the family!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>
