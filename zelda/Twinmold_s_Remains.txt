{{Term/Store|Twinmold's Remains|Twinmold's Remains|Series, MM, MM3D}} are an item in {{MM}}.<ref name="E 142">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|MM3D|Twinmold|link}} are the giant masked insect {{Plural|MM3D|Boss|link}} of {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|link}}.<ref name="E 142"/> When {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} cleanses the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple}} for the first time and acquires {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold's Remains}}, the final {{Term|MM3D|Guardian Deity|link}} is freed and meets {{Term|MM3D|Link}} in the [[Chamber of Giants]],<ref>{{Cite|You seized {{Color|MM3D Red|Twinmold's Remains}}! In doing so, you've freed the innocent spirit that was imprisoned within this dark mask.|N/A|MM3D}}</ref> where the {{Plural|MM3D|Guardian Deity|display=Giants}} agree to help {{Term|MM3D|Link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Tatl|link}} but ask them to forgive {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|Well, we've helped all of you. That's all we can do. "Call us." That's what they're saying. Without being told, we understand. So now you'll help us out in exchange! We'll call you from atop the {{Color|MM3D Red|Clock Tower}}, so do something about him, all right? What? Their voices sound sad... You don't want to? "Forgive your friend." Forgive our friend? What do you mean by forgive? Huh? What friend?}}|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref> The {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold's Remains|display=Remains}} allow {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to {{Term|MM3D|Warping|link|display=Warp}} directly to the {{Term|MM3D|Boss}} room from the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|display=Temple}}'s entrance.<ref>{{Cite|Ye who hold my remains... Return to the appointed place to face me...|N/A|MM}}</ref> In {{MM3D}}, {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold's Remains}} are not required for this; simply challenging {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold}} once will activate the {{Term|MM3D|Portal (Object)}} so {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can try again without having to go back through the {{Term|MM3D|Dungeon}}.

{{Term|MM3D|Twinmold's Remains}} are worn by one of the {{Plural|MM3D|Moon Child|link}}. They can also be seen hanging on one of the walls of the {{Term|MM3D|Boss}} chamber inside the {{Term|MM3D|Moon|link}} during the battle against {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Mask (Item)|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold's Remains|display=Remains}} eventually detach from the wall and attack {{Term|MM3D|Link}} by shooting energy at him. They can be destroyed with any weapon.

==Trivia==
*The icon for {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold's Remains}} in {{MM3D|-}} uses {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold}}'s red-eyed appearance from that game; however, the smaller icon seen on the file selection screen retains its green-eyed appearance from {{MM|-}}.
