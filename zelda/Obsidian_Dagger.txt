{{Term/Store|Obsidian Dagger|Obsidian Daggers|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Obsidian Dagger}} {{Icon|CoH Zelda}}{{Icon|CoH Cadence}}<br/>Deals up to {{Color|CoH Red|3 damage}} depending on your beat combo to a single enemy in front of you.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>


==Location and Uses==
Obsidian Daggers are a type of {{Term|CoH|Dagger|link}} whose damage increases as {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}'s beat combo rises, starting from 1 and increasing to 2 and finally 3, then resetting back to 1 when the combo is lost. They can only be wielded by Cadence and Zelda, and can only be acquired either after they infuse the Dagger with {{Term|CoH|Obsidian|link}} at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, or if either them or {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} opens the blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an already-made one in {{Term|CoH|Death Mountain|link}}.

They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Obsidian Short Sword|link}}.
