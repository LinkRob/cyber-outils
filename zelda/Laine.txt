{{Term/Store|Laine||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Laine|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Laine}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who guards the {{Term|BotW|Northwest Gate|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Greetings, Hylian. This is the {{Color|BotW Blue|northwest gate}} of Gerudo Town. Feel free to admire it from the outside.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She stands at her post all day carrying a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tries to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} without wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} will stop him from passing by.<ref>{{Cite|I cannot allow a voe to go any farther. I must ask you to leave at once.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> While it is safe inside, she is unable to let a man enter due to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} law.<ref>{{Cite|It's safe within the walls of the city, but I can't let a voe like you inside.|Laine|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But don't attempt to cross its threshold. Town law states voe like yourself are not allowed within these walls.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laine}} suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} goes back to the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}} if he seeks shelter.<ref>{{Cite|If it's shelter you seek, you'll have to head back to {{Color|BotW Blue|Kara Kara Bazaar}}.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Laine}} is impressed that {{Term|BotW|Link}} approached {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} from the northwest, and she asks if he passed through the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm|link}} over the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Though I must admit I'm impressed you got here from the northwest. Did you truly pass through that {{Color|BotW Red|sandstorm}} to get here?|Laine|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} claims that he did, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} will deny it and say that it is impossible.<ref>{{Cite|No, that would be impossible...|Laine|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says he did not, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} does not find that surprising.<ref>{{Cite|Of course you didn't.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She goes on to explain that a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} has the "temperament of a raging stallion."<ref>{{Cite|A Gerudo sandstorm has the temperament of a raging stallion.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She goes on to explain that the ancients used these {{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm}} to their advantage and hid treasure in them.<ref>{{Cite|And that's precisely why ancient cultures hid all manner of treasure in the Gerudo Desert.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Laine}} while wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}}, she warns him to avoid {{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm}}.<ref>{{Cite|We caution all travelers to avoid the sandstorms.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She explains that it is very easy for one to lose their sense of direction, and even {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}} get lost in such circumstances.<ref>{{Cite|If you wander into one, you'll lose all sense of direction. Even us Gerudo are in a bind if one seizes upon us.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She remarks that the {{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm}} have made the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} a perfect hiding place for bandits and treasures.<ref>{{Cite|The danger of the sandstorms makes the desert the perfect hiding place for thieving bandits and priceless treasures.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the treasure, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} admits that she does not know all the details.<ref>{{Cite|[Treasure?] Having said that, I don't really know the details either.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> However, she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} about the {{Plural|BotW|Swordswoman Statue|link}}, which are scattered across the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There are {{Color|BotW Red|statues of swordswomen}} scattered across Gerudo Desert. They're remnants of ages long past.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She has heard rumors that they point to the hiding place of an ancient treasure, though {{Term|BotW|Laine}} herself dismisses this as superstition.<ref>{{Cite|People talk about the statues pointing to the hiding place of ancient treasure...but that's just superstition.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> Even if it was true, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} considers the threat of the dangerous {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} in the {{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm}} mean it is not worth the risk.<ref>{{Cite|Even if it was true, there are dangerous monsters in the sandstorms. I wouldn't risk it.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Laine}} is finished speaking, {{Term|BotW|Link}} receives "{{Term|BotW|The Silent Swordswomen|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

During the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to ask {{Term|BotW|Laine}} about {{Term|BotW|Karusa Valley|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Karusa Valley?] So you're the Hylian that Captain Teake was talking about? The thieves' hideout is in {{Color|BotW Red|Karusa Valley}}.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She points him past the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens}}, and she claims that even though he is unable to see it, {{Term|BotW|Karusa Valley}} is in that direction.<ref>{{Cite|You can't see {{Color|BotW Red|Karusa Valley}} from here, but it's in that direction.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> Due to the length of the {{Term|BotW|Karusa Valley|display=Valley}}, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} suggests that he take a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Karusa Valley}} runs deep into the highlands and is longer than you'd expect. If you intend to go, you should take a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}}.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} will comment that the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|link|display=Sand-Seal Races}} will be starting up again.<ref>{{Cite|The Divine Beast is gone, so the {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal races}} should be starting up again.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She remarks that it has been too long since she has seen the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|display=Sand-Seal Racing}} champion, {{Term|BotW|Tali|link}}, show her talent.<ref>{{Cite|It's been too long since I've seen the champion strut her stuff.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She considers going to the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rally|link}} once she finishes her shift.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe I'll head over there after work...|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|display=Sand-Seal Races}}, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} tells him that he can find the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rally}} by looking for a gate with red fabric past the other side of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Sand-seal races?] If you go around Gerudo Town along this wall from the back, you'll see a gate with red fabric on it.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> Since it is located outside of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}}, both men and women are able to participate.<ref>{{Cite|That's the {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal race}} course. It's outside the town, so vai and voe can participate|Laine|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laine}} explains that the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race}} is traditional {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} competition.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal race}} is a traditional Gerudo competition. The champion may even be there right now!|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

Even with the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} gone, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that there are still {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} and {{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm}} to be careful of.<ref>{{Cite|I'm glad that the Divine Beast is gone, but we still have monsters and sandstorms to deal with.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> Due to the danger, she reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} to tread lightly as he travels the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|Make no mistake—the desert still hides peril between every dune. Tread lightly.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>
