{{Term/Store|Obsidian Rapier|Obsidian Rapiers|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.{{Fact}}

==Location and Uses==
Obsidian Rapiers are a unique variant of the {{Term|CoH|Rapier|link}} that deal damage based on {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}'s beat combo, and can only be acquired after she infuses a Rapier with {{Term|CoH|Obsidian|link}} at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. Like all {{Plural|CoH|Rapier}}, they can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}}.
