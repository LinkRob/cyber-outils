==Characteristics==
{{Improve}}
Rito Captains first appear in The Champion Revali, where they attack Link and his allies, mistaking Terrako for Harbinger Ganon. They also appear in Air and Lightning, All Hyrule United and The Final Battle. They don't wield Two Handed Weapons, but to make up for it, they do wield Bows, as well as Spears and Swords.
