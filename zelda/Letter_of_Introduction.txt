The {{Term/Store|Letter of Introduction||Series, OoA}} is an item in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 129}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Letter of Introduction is given to [[Link]] by the [[Graceful Goron]]'s friend in exchange for the [[Lava Juice]] when Link shows him the [[Old Mermaid Key]].<ref>{{Cite|I don't know if it will work, but if you give me your Lava Juice, I'll introduce you to the Graceful Goron!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> The letter must be shown to the Graceful Goron, who will give Link his [[Mermaid Key]], providing he can successfully complete the [[Goron Dance]].<ref>{{Cite|Huh? That's an introduction from a brother? What's this..? Give me your Mermaid Key? How did he know I have it? Oh well... I can't turn down a brother's request. But I can't just give you this! Dance me for it!|Graceful Goron|OoA}}</ref> The Mermaid Key will allow Link to enter [[Mermaid's Cave]], the sixth dungeon of the game. However, to complete the dungeon, Link will also need the [[Mermaid Key#Old Mermaid Key|Old Mermaid Key]], which is obtained in a similar fashion to the Mermaid Key.
