==Biography==
==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Goron Merchant}}
A {{Term|TMC|Goron Merchant}} appears in {{TMC}} after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} has {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fused}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with a {{Term|TMC|Goron|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Goron Cave|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}. This [[Merchant]] appears wandering in from {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}} and immediately decides to open a shop in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's been a long time since the last time I visited {{Color|TMC Green|Hyrule}}... I think I'll set up a little place where I can sell my Kinstones.|Goron Merchant|TMC}}</ref> The {{Term|TMC|Goron Merchant}} will then be found in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square|link}} selling three differently colored {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}}, although their shapes are random when bought. Green {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} are priced at 50 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}},<ref>{{Cite|This Kinstone costs 50 Rupees.|Goron Merchant|TMC}}</ref> blue {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} at 200,<ref>{{Cite|This Kinstone costs 200 Rupees.|Goron Merchant|TMC}}</ref> and red {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} at 300.<ref>{{Cite|This Kinstone costs 300 Rupees.|Goron Merchant|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} purchases all three of the {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} available, the {{Term|TMC|Goron Merchant}} will restock his wares after a short time,<ref>{{Cite|No more, no more, but come back again. I promise to find more, goro!|Goron Merchant|TMC}}</ref> although if enough pieces are bought, he eventually runs out.<ref>{{Cite|I've sold all my pieces! Yeah! I KNEW coming to Hyrule was a good idea!|Goron Merchant|TMC}}</ref>
