'''Dark Four-Legged Spiders''' are {{Plural|FPTRR|Enemy}} in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} They appear in the {{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine}}.

==Characteristics and Varieties==
==={{Term|FPTRR|Dark Four-Legged Spider}}===
{{Plural|FPTRR|Dark Four-Legged Spider}} appear deep into the {{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine|link}}. They appear as a group of three as if from nowhere in a similar manner to {{Plural|FPTRR|Dark Nyuudou|link}} and close in on {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} and {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo|link}}. Although a large group, they should not pose too much of a threat to both of them together. Like other {{Term|FPTRR|Dungeon}} {{Plural|FPTRR|Enemy}}, they drop {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} when defeated. The enemies do not appear again and once defeated will not return.

===Strong Dark Four-Legged Spider===
[[File:FPTRR Strong Dark Four-Legged Spider Sprite.png|35px]]
A single {{Term|FPTRR|Strong Dark Four-Legged Spider|link}} appears guarding the door that leads to the huge {{Term|FPTRR|Treasure Chest|display=chest|link}} where the boss, {{Term|FPTRR|Death Bug|link}} is fought. Unlike the red variety, this blue monster will not immediately attack {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}, but must be defeated in battle before the door will open. Although it is stronger than the normal variety, it is defeated fairly easily if {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} and {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo}} attack together.

==Trivia==
*{{Plural|FPTRR|Dark Four-Legged Spider}}, along with {{Plural|FPTRR|Dark Bat|link}} and {{Plural|FPTRR|Dark Nyuudou}} have a sketchy appearance, as if they were an animated drawing. They appear from nowhere similar to monsters appearing from clouds of smoke in the original {{TLoZ}} game.
