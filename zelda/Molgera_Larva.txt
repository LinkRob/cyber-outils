==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TWWHD|Molgera Larva}} appear during [[Molgera]]'s fight in the [[Wind Temple]]. When damaged for the first time, Molgera will alter its attack strategy by releasing three of the larvae to distract {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}}. The hero can use the [[Hookshot]] to latch onto and dispatch them, rewarding him with [[Heart]]s.
