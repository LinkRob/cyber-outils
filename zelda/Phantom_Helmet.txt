The {{Term/Store|Phantom Helmet||Series, BotW}} is an item in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Phantom Helmet}}<br/>Myths abound about armored phantoms that terrorized brave heroes. This helmet looks just like the ones they supposedly wore. It's a rather rare find.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It is a piece of {{Term|BotW|Armor}} exclusive to {{Term|BotW|The Master Trials|link}} DLC Pack. 
==Location and Uses==
Obtaining the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Helmet}} is one part of the objective of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Phantasma|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. The Helmet was stolen from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|display=Hyrule Castle|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Misko|link}} and a clue to its location can be found in {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Outpost Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hid the eight priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Armor worn by fierce phantoms, feared even by heroes, is hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in the ruins of Hyrule Field}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at a battleground where brave souls tested their skill}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at ceremonial grounds where royal guards are honored}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where the soldiers of Hyrule gathered}}.|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that hides the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Helmet}} can be found buried near a wall on the first floor of the {{Term|BotW|Coliseum Ruins|link}}. After obtaining the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Helmet}}, {{Term|BotW|Granté|link}} will begin selling it for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.{{Note|Prior to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild#Updates|Version 1.3.3 Update]], Granté would not sell any Armor that is exclusive to the Expansion Pass or The Master Trials DLC Pack. This meant that the Phantom Helmet could not be replaced if sold, which was noted in the description of the Helmet.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Phantom Helmet}}<br/>Myths abound about armored phantoms that terrorized brave heroes. This helmet looks just like the ones they supposedly wore. If sold, this rare find can't be replaced.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Phantom Helmet}}'s description was changed in the Version 1.3.3 Update as the Helmet could now be replaced.<ref name="Inventory"/>}} According to its description, the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Helmet}} looks just like the {{Plural|BotW|Helmet}} supposedly worn by {{Plural|BotW|Phantom|link}} that tormented heroes.<ref name="Inventory"/> When worn, the Helmet provides one level of Attack Up. The {{Term|BotW|Phantom Helmet}} cannot be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} nor can it be {{Term|BotW|Dye|display=Dyed|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Phantom Helmet}}, alongside the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Armor|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Phantom Greaves|link}}, compose the {{Term|BotW|Phantom Set|link}}.
