{{Term/Store|Lakitu|Lakitus|Series, TMC|plural}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 188}}</ref> They are directly based on the {{Smw|Lakitu}} enemies from the ''{{Smw|Mario (series)|Mario}}'' series.

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TMC|Lakitu}} appear in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}. They can throw lighting bolts at {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} when he is near them. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} is too close to the {{Term|TMC|Lakitu}}, it will hide in its cloud until he walks farther away. {{Term|TMC|Link}} must use ranged weapons to defeat them, including the {{Term|TMC|Boomerang|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Bow|link}}, or the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Link}} can also use the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}} to dispel their clouds.
