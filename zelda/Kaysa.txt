{{Term/Store|Kaysa||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kaysa|N/A|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} is a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} who resides in the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Piper Ridge|link}}, which is located on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. Like the other {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy}}, {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} will upgrade {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} in exchange for {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} is the subject of the "{{Term|BotW|A Gift for the Great Fairy|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. At the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Toren|link}}, who is determined to locate {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}}'s {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain}} and meet her.<ref name="Toren"/> He has heard that the shadow of the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tower|link}} points towards the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|display=Fountain}} in the afternoon.<ref>{{Cite|There's a tower just beyond the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha Great Bridge}}. They say that if you go to the top and look at the ground sometime in the afternoon... {{Color|BotW Red|The tower's shadow will point}} to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}}'s fountain!|Toren|BotW}}</ref> However, he is unable to reach the top of the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tower|display=Tower}} on his own, so he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to make an offering to {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} for him on his behalf.<ref>{{Cite|After hearing that legend, I decided to find out for myself. But now I'm stuck here at this stable because... Well... No matter how hard I try, I just know I'll never be able to climb that tower. And to think, I even saved up all my rupees as an offering for the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}}. But you... You look like you could climb that tower without breaking a sweat! WOuld you be willing to make an offering of rupees to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}} on my behalf?|Toren|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Toren}} will give him 500 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to give to {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll do it!] Oh, thank you! Here, take the {{Color|BotW Red|500 rupees}} I saved up. It's my life savings! {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|So please don't just go running off with it, OK?}}}}|Toren|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain}}, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} inside, asking him to listen to her story.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Boy...}} Sweet boy... Please... Listen to my story...|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to listen, she introduces herself and explains that there was once a spring in this location.<ref name="Kaysa"/> Over time, the number of travelers visiting to offer {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} to {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} dwindled, and thus her power weakened.<ref>{{Cite|But as time passed, fewer and fewer travelers arrived to offer me rupees. As a result, my power has abandoned me.|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> Without her power, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to give her {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm nearly powerless now, so I beg your help. I need rupees to become whole again.|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> Depending on how many {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already given offerings to, {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} will ask for either 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}},<ref>{{Cite|All I need is {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}}...|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> 500 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}},<ref>{{Cite|All I need is {{Color|BotW Red|500 rupees}}...|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> 1,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}},<ref>{{Cite|All I need is {{Color|BotW Red|1,000 rupees}}...|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> or 10,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|All I need is {{Color|BotW Red|10,000 rupees}}...|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> She promises to help him after her power has been restored.<ref>{{Cite|Once my power has been restored, I can help you do great things, boy.|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> Her hand will then emerge from the bud, and she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to hand over his {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now... If you have rupees, hand them over to me. Quickly!|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to give her an offering, {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} will thank him for his kindness.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you. You are too kind.|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> Her power returns to her, causing her {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain}} to bloom once again.<ref>{{Cite|Aah... The power... {{Big|It's overflowing!!}}|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> She then emerges from the pool.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ah-HAAAAAAAAAAA!!!}}|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> Finally able to see {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} notes that she was correct in assuming that he is handsome.<ref>{{Cite|Mmm... So you're the one who brought me back from the brink. Oh, my... I see now that my first impression of you was correct. You most definitely are pleasant to look at.|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> She jokingly asks him if he would like to live at the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain}} with her.<ref>{{Cite|How would you like to live here with me, hmm?|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> Revealing that she was kidding, {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} remarks that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is cute when he blushes.<ref>{{Cite|Hah! I'm kidding, of course. But you certainly are cute when you're blushing.|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref>

To thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his assistance, she offers to enhance his {{Term|BotW|Armor}} if he brings her the correct {{Plural|BotW|Material}}.<ref>{{Cite|I really can't thank you enough, boy, but that won't stop me from trying. If you have the {{Color|BotW Red|materials}}needed to enhance a piece of clothing, I will {{Color|BotW Red|enhance}} your {{Color|BotW Red|clothes}}. So why don't you let me take a peek in that little pouch of yours?|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> The degree to which she will be able to enhance {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Armor}} depends on how many other {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy}} have received offerings.<ref>{{Cite|With the power available to me... I should be able to enhance your clothing {{Color|BotW Red|a little bit}}.|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With the power available to me... I can enhance your clothing {{Color|BotW Red|a fair amount}}.|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With the power available to me... I can {{Color|BotW Red|greatly}} enhance your clothing.|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With the power available to me... I can enhance your clothing {{Color|BotW Red|to the max}}!|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} selects a piece of {{Term|BotW|Armor}} to upgrade, {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} will tell him to close his eyes as she upgrades it.<ref>{{Cite|OK. Got it! Now then... ♪Close your eyes!♪|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} lack the materials for a requested upgrade, {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}} will playfully chide him for being impatient<ref>{{Cite|You don't have the necessary materials... ♪All in good time, boy.♪|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref>, whereas declining an available upgrade has her accuse him of attempting to tease her with the prospect.<ref>{{Cite|Teasing me? You little meanie...|Kaysa|BotW}}</ref>
