{{Term/Store|Illumeni Plateau||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Illumeni Plateau'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Illumeni Plateau is located in the [[Tabantha Frontier]] between [[Satori Mountain]] and [[Tanagar Canyon]]. Illumeni Plateau is a large, flat, oval-shaped raised piece of land with all rocky terrain and scattered dead plants.

On the southern side of the plateau, a [[Stone Talus]] sits underground by a clump of bushes.
