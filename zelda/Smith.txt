{{Term/Store|Smith||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 270"/>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Smith}}
{{Term|TMC|Smith}} is {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}'s grandfather.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|TMC Red|Smith's Sword}}! It's a sharp blade made by your grandfather.|N/A|TMC}}</ref><ref name="E 270"/> He creates the {{Term|TMC|Smith's Sword|link}} for the winner of the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|link}} at the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Then, with the broken Picori Blade and the Smith's Sword in hand, Link set off in search of their land.|book=HH|page=79}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is the blade that will be presented to the winner of the competition. Don't lose it. And while the two of you may be childhood friends, remember...Zelda is the princess of Hyrule. You watch over her. Don't let anything bad happen to her.|Smith|TMC}}</ref> He was once a great swordsman and now is a renowned blacksmith. He is close friends with {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The finest swordsmith in Hyrule. As a young man, he was a great swordsman. He and King Daltus are friends and enjoyed a pleasant rivalry as youths.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> and even fought with him to a draw in a previous {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament}}.<ref>{{Cite|The king of Hyrule. Reported to have been a fine swordsman, he appeared in the Picori Festival tournament as a youth and fought to a draw with Smith.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> Throughout the game, {{Term|TMC|Smith}} can be found in {{Term|TMC|Link's House|link}} in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} making {{Plural|TMC|Sword|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm busy making swords for the guards to keep the monsters at bay.|Smith|TMC}}</ref> and only leaves the {{Term|TMC|Link's House|display=House}} to go to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} is turned to stone by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}. Here, {{Term|TMC|Smith}} suggests that {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}} send {{Term|TMC|Link}} to seek out the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I see... If that's the case, then why not send {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}?|Smith|TMC}}</ref> and gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} a {{Term|TMC|Map of Hyrule|link}} to find his way to the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Take this {{Color|TMC Red|map}} with you. If you get lost on your way to the forest, simply check your map.|Smith|TMC}}</ref>

Throughout the game, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can return to his {{Term|TMC|Link's House|display=House}} and speak with {{Term|TMC|Smith}}, who offers insight on where {{Term|TMC|Link}} should go next on his journey. While attempting to cross {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}, he suggests visiting {{Term|TMC|Rem's Shoe Store|link}};<ref>{{Cite|Say, have you been to {{Color|TMC Green|Rem's Shoe Shop}}? His shoes really are the best. I'm sure they could help you on your quest. Why don't you give him a visit?|Smith|TMC}}</ref> after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}}, he suggests visiting the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}};<ref>{{Cite|Say, have you been to the {{Color|TMC Green|library}} yet? I've heard some kids say that they've seen Picori there... I thought maybe that would help you on this quest of yours.|Smith|TMC}}</ref> after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}}, he suggests going to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}};<ref>{{Cite|Say... Have you been to the {{Color|TMC Blue|Royal Valley}}, west of Hyrule Castle? You used to be able to get there easily from {{Color|TMC Green|Trilby Highlands}}. Nowadays, however, you have to take a secret path to get there. I'm sure it was to the {{Color|TMC Blue|west of the northern part of Hyrule Field}}...|Smith|TMC}}</ref> after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains a gold {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} from the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}, he suggests going to {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But say... Have you been to {{Color|TMC Green|Veil Falls}}? I hear no one has ever found the source of water that feeds the falls.|Smith|TMC}}</ref>

When Link finds the [[Great Dragonfly Fairy]], she asks him "Your grandfather's name is Smee, isn't it?" to see if Link will agree to a lie.

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Smith}} twice;<ref>{{Cite|You want to fuse Kinstones? OK! Let's do this!|Smith|TMC}}</ref> the first time results in a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}} appearing in the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}}, and the second time results in a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}} containing a red {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} appearing in {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}}.
