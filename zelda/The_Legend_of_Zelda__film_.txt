'''''The Legend of Zelda''''' is an upcoming [[wikipedia:Live_action|live-action]] film based on [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The'' ''Legend of Zelda'' series]]. It will be jointly produced by [[Nintendo]] and Sony.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=It was easy to forget this even existed, but the Legend of Zelda live-action movie from Sony and Nintendo is still happening |author=Paul Tassi |published=March 28, 2025 |retrieved=March 29, 2025 |url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2025/03/28/nintendos-risky-the-legend-of-zelda-movie-gets-a-release-date/ |title=Nintendo’s Risky ‘The Legend Of Zelda’ Movie Gets A Release Date |site=Forbes}}</ref> It is planned to be released in April 30, 2027.

==Development==
===Production===
Historically, [[Nintendo]] has been wary of allowing its popular IPs to be adapted into films; a trepidation partially stemming from the failure of the 1993 ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros._(1993_film)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=After decades of wariness when it came to adapting its video game franchises, partially stemming from the failure of 1993’s live-action “Super Mario Bros.,” Nintendo reached a box office high score with 2023’s animated “Mario,” starring Chris Pratt and produced with Chris Meledandri’s Illumination. The movie jumped to more than $1.36 billion worldwide at the box office. |published=November 7, 2023 |retrieved=March 29, 2025 |url=https://variety.com/2023/film/news/zelda-movie-nintendo-wes-ball-1235783308/ |title=‘Legend of Zelda’ Live-Action Film in Development From Nintendo and ‘Maze Runner’ Director Wes Ball |site=Variety}}</ref> Wes Ball had floated the idea of a ''The'' ''Legend of Zelda'' movie since at least 2010, believing it should be "the next big mo-cap [motion capture] movie after 2009's ''[https://james-camerons-avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Avatar_(film) Avatar]''.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Since I could never even hope to have the chance to direct it... the next big mo-cap Avatar-like movie should be... THE LEGEND OF ZELDA. |author=James Whitbrook |published=November 8, 2023 |retrieved=March 29, 2025 |url=https://gizmodo.com/wes-ball-zelda-movie-old-tweets-mo-cap-nintendo-sony-1851004003 |title=Wes Ball’s Old Tweets About a Zelda Movie Are Fascinating Hindsight |site=i09}}</ref> Work on the film began "many years" prior to 2023.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=This is Miyamoto. I have been working on the live-action film of The Legend of Zelda for many years now with Avi Arad-san, who has produced many mega hit films. |published=November 7, 2023 |retrieved=March 29, 2025 |url=https://variety.com/2023/film/news/zelda-movie-nintendo-wes-ball-1235783308/ |title=‘Legend of Zelda’ Live-Action Film in Development From Nintendo and ‘Maze Runner’ Director Wes Ball |site=Variety}}</ref> The existence of the film was first announced in November, 2023.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=It’s the kind of news one wants to be excited about, until one gets to the details. Yesterday, Nintendo announced that a Legend of Zelda film—a live-action film, no less—is officially in development. |author=Molly Templeton |published=November 8, 2023 |retrieved=March 29, 2025 |url=https://reactormag.com/nintendo-is-officially-developing-a-live-action-legend-of-zelda-movie/ |title=Nintendo Is Officially Developing a Live-Action Legend of Zelda Movie |site=Reactor}}</ref> The film will be produced by [[Shigeru Miyamoto]]<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Nintendo’s Shigeru Miyamoto will produce the film along with Avi Arad, who is also known for his many blockbusters (including countless Spider-Man films, and also Morbius). |author=Molly Templeton |published=November 8, 2023 |retrieved=March 29, 2025 |url=https://reactormag.com/nintendo-is-officially-developing-a-live-action-legend-of-zelda-movie/ |title=Nintendo Is Officially Developing a Live-Action Legend of Zelda Movie |site=Reactor}}</ref> and [[Avi Arad]], with [[Wes Ball]] directing.<ref name = "WesBall">{{Cite Web|quote=Yes: Inexplicably, Hyrule’s fate is in the hands of Wes Ball, whose entire available feature film directorial output to date is the three films in the Maze Runner series. |author=Molly Templeton |published=November 8, 2023 |retrieved=March 29, 2025 |url=https://reactormag.com/nintendo-is-officially-developing-a-live-action-legend-of-zelda-movie/ |title=Nintendo Is Officially Developing a Live-Action Legend of Zelda Movie |site=Reactor}}</ref> In May 2024, Ball clarified that his vision for the film had changed from his initial conception. He had originally envisioned the film as being entirely based on motion capture, but his current incarnation was more "grounded" and "real."<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=n an interview with Polygon, Ball said that an entirely mo-capped version The Legend of Zelda “probably isn’t his choice,” with a laugh. Instead, he said the project has to be a little more “grounded” and “real.” |author=Austen Goslin |published=May 1, 2024 |retrieved=March 31, 2025 |url=https://www.polygon.com/24145926/zelda-movie-director-wes-ball-grounded-style |title=Legend of Zelda movie director says film needs to be ‘grounded’ and ‘real,’ versus motion capture |site=Polygon}}</ref> On November 5th, 2025, filming started.<ref>[https://collider.com/the-legend-of-zelda-live-action-movie-filming-start-new-zealand/ Nintendo's Live-Action ‘The Legend of Zelda’ Movie Finally Takes a Massive Step Forward]</ref> On April 14th, 2026, production has wrapped.<ref>[https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/legend-of-zelda-movie-production-wes-ball-release-1236562187/ ‘Legend of Zelda’ Movie’s Team Celebrates as Production Wraps Ahead of 2027 Release]</ref>


===Casting===
On July 16, 2025, [[Bo Bragason]] was cast as Princess Zelda, and [[Benjamin Evan Ainsworth]] as Link.<ref name=leads>[https://variety.com/2025/film/global/legend-of-zelda-movie-cast-bo-bragason-benjamin-evan-ainsworth-1236462236/ ‘Legend of Zelda’ Live-Action Movie Casts Bo Bragason and Benjamin Evan Ainsworth as Zelda and Link]</ref><ref>[https://x.com/Nintendo/status/1945433161124688335 This is Miyamoto. I am pleased to announce that for the live-action film of The Legend of Zelda, Zelda will be played by Bo Bragason-san, and Link by Benjamin Evan Ainsworth-san. I am very much looking forward to seeing both of them on the big screen.]</ref> In November 2025, leaked footage revealed that [[Dichen Lachman]] has reportedly joined the cast as [[Impa]].<ref>[https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2025/11/15/leaked-zelda-movie-footage-shows-link-zelda-and-a-severance-actor-impa/ Leaked ‘Zelda’ Movie Footage Shows Link, Zelda And A ‘Severance’ Actor Impa]</ref><ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20251116125059/https://www.gamespot.com/articles/leaked-footage-from-sonys-live-action-legend-of-zelda-movie-emerges-showing-off-zelda-and-more/1100-6536257/ Leaked Footage From Sony's Live-Action Legend Of Zelda Movie Emerges, Showing Off Zelda And More]</ref> It is not yet known who will play as [[Ganon]].

==Release==
The film's release date was eventually unveiled in a special teaser on March 28, 2025 via the then-newly released phone calendar app [[Nintendo Today!]], alongside the [[wikipedia:Famicom_Disk_System|Famicom Computer Disk System]] version of the [[The Legend of Zelda (Theme)|main theme]] from the original [[The Legend of Zelda|''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'']] game.

==Reception==
Reactions to the movie's announcement were considerably mixed. One of the points of contention within ''The'' ''Legend of Zelda'' fanbase was whether Link would speak in full sentences like in other non-canon media such as [[The Legend of Zelda (television series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' TV series]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=In November 2023, Nintendo announced that a live-action Legend of Zelda movie was in development, which may have lightly alarmed some fans for a variety of reasons, ranging from casting concerns to thinking, as I did, Oh no I don’t want to hear Link talk. |author=Molly Templeton |published=November 8, 2023 |retrieved=March 29, 2025 |url=https://reactormag.com/nintendo-is-officially-developing-a-live-action-legend-of-zelda-movie/ |title=Nintendo Is Officially Developing a Live-Action Legend of Zelda Movie |site=Reactor}}</ref> Former [[Nintendo]] employee [[Takaya Imamura]] also expressed dislike of the idea of Link talking, stating "''The moment Link speaks, I can't help but worry a little that the '''Zelda ''magic' everyone has been nurturing in their hearts might just vanish into thin air.''"<ref>{{cite Web|quote=The moment Link speaks, I can't help but worry a little that the 'Zelda magic' everyone has been nurturing in their hearts might just vanish into thin air |author=Tom Philips |published=April 8, 2026|retrieved=April 8, 2026 |url=https://www.ign.com/articles/former-nintendo-veteran-says-link-finally-speaking-in-the-legend-of-zelda-movie-risks-ruining-the-series-magic |title = Former Nintendo Veteran Says Link Finally Speaking in The Legend of Zelda Movie Risks Ruining the Series' Magic |site=IGN}}</ref>

Another point of contention has been the film's presentation, with some feeling it would be better suited to animation rather than live action like the successful ''[[mariowiki:The_Super_Mario_Bros._Movie|The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Although Zelda fans have longed for a movie for almost as long as the franchise has been around, it’s fair to say that a live-action adaptation was not itself high on anyone’s wishlist for the series—animation was always the seemingly logical choice to take on Zelda and its own penchant for evolving and iterating on its stylistic moods over the course of the games. But… maybe Ball is onto something with Avatar. |author=James Whitbrook |published=November 8, 2023 |retrieved=March 29, 2025 |url=https://gizmodo.com/wes-ball-zelda-movie-old-tweets-mo-cap-nintendo-sony-1851004003 |title=Wes Ball’s Old Tweets About a Zelda Movie Are Fascinating Hindsight |site=i09}}</ref>
