'''Spears''' are recurring objects and weapons in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{ZA|-}}===
[[File:ZA Spear Sprite.png|frame|left]]
Spears in {{ZA}} are obstacles appearing only in the [[Shrine of Water]], where they emerge from the walls periodically. If [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] is hit by a Spear, she takes a [[Heart]] of damage.

==={{HW|-}}===
Spears are a class of Weapons used by [[Lana]] in {{HW}}. It is associated with [[Element|Water]]. The default Spear is the '''[[Deku Spear]]'''. The second form of the Spear is the '''[[Kokiri Spear]]''', followed by the '''[[Faron Spear]]'''.

The Lv.★ Spear is the '''[[8-Bit Magical Rod?]]''', which is based on the [[Magical Rod]] from {{TLoZ}}. It was added to {{HW|-}} with the {{TP|-}} Pack DLC. In {{HW|DE}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the [[8-Bit Magical Rod?]] replaces the [[Sun Faron Spear]] when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.

====Special Attack====
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Lana stabs the Spear into the ground, causing a number of root-like tendrils to emerge. They soon form into a miniature version of the [[Great Deku Tree]], while Lana mysteriously disappears and reappears sitting on a branch. She hops off and waves to it before it vanishes, dropping the foes it collected. This attack damages a circular area ahead of Lana.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
In {{CoH}}, the Spear is used as a weapon by most characters. They deal 1 damage to a single enemy, and have 2 range. Spears deal knockback to the same types of enemies as the [[Dagger]], but deal knockback to less enemies than [[Short Sword]]s and [[Long Sword]]s.
Like all weapons in {{CoH}}, the Spear can be found with or infused into one of 6 materials:
*{{Term|CoH|Titanium Spear|link}}: Deals 2 damage and has 2 range.
*{{Term|CoH|Ruby Spear|link}}: Deals 1 damage and has 2 range. After defeating 5 enemies, restores one half heart.
*{{Term|CoH|Emerald Spear|link}}: Deals 1 damage and poisons the enemy it hits. Has 2 range.
*{{Term|CoH|Obsidian Spear|link}}: Deals 1, 2, or 3 damage. This weapon's damage increases as your combo increases. Has 2 range.
*{{Term|CoH|Glass Spear|link}}: Deals 3 damage to a single enemy in front of the user. If you take damage while this weapon is equipped, it breaks.
*{{Term|CoH|Impa's Naginata|link}}: Deals 1 damage and poisons the enemy it hits. After defeating 5 enemies, restores one half heart. Has 2 range.
