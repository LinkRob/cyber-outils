"{{Term/Store|What's for Dinner?||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|What's for Dinner?}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Lurelin Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can meet {{Term|BotW|Kiana|link}}, who explains that she is from a family of fishermen.<ref>{{Cite|This is Lurelin Village. We're a small fishing town and proud of it! My husband is a fisherman, and I have two sons who will probably grow up to be fishermen too.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> She asks her son, {{Term|BotW|Kinov|link}}, what she should make for dinner.<ref>{{Cite|What should I cook for dinner?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> He suggests {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cook}} her {{Term|BotW|Seafood Paella|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Make your {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}}! It's so good and yummy and great!|Kinov|BotW}}</ref> which she agrees with.<ref>{{Cite|That settles it!<br/>I'll make some {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}}.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref>

Though {{Term|BotW|Kinov}} is happy about this,<ref>{{Cite|Yaaaaay!|Kinov|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} admits to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she is missing the {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}} to make the meal.<ref>{{Cite|We seem to be a bit short on ingredients though. We'll need {{Color|BotW Red|goat butter}} and a {{Color|BotW Red|hearty blueshell snail}}.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> Due to how busy she is with housework, she has not had the time to go shopping for them.<ref>{{Cite|I'm so busy with housework that I don't have time to go shopping for ingredients.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he could retrieve the ingredients for her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I know! Could you go and get me the ingredients we're missing?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> If he agrees, {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} will be happy,<ref>{{Cite|[Sure.] Really? I'm happy to hear that. I'll be waiting!|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Goat Butter}} is sold at {{Term|BotW|High Spirits Produce|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} and {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. In addition, {{Term|BotW|Agus|link}} sells them on his path between {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail}}, on the other hand, are found on the shores of the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Sea|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has acquired both ingredients, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village}}, where {{Term|BotW|Kiana}} will ask for them.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You have the ingredients! Can I have them?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> After obtaining them, she will begin to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cook}}.<ref>{{Cite|All right, I'll take those, then. Now to start cooking.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> Before she does, though, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} to thank him.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a little something...|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> She also invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to eat with her family.<ref>{{Cite|Since you found the missing ingredients, why don't you eat with us?<br/>It'll be done soon.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Cooking}}, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} some of her {{Term|BotW|Seafood Paella}} and encourages him to eat it.<ref>{{Cite|Go on, dig in! It's deeeee-lish!|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> After obtaining the meal, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
