The {{Term/Store|Rona Kachta Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Rona Kachta Shrine}}<br/>Rona Kachta's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Rona Kachta's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Rona Kachta Shrine is in front of the large {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} at the back of the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Temple|link}}, located within the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is protected by a large number of {{Plural|BotW|Decayed Guardian|link}}, which lay dormant within the Temple until {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} arrives. As Link enters the Shrine, he is greeted by the Sits {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rona Kachta|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the Shrine is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Rona Kachta. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Rona Kachta|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Rona Kachta Shrine features no puzzles, simply a direct path to Rona Kachta. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Great Flameblade|link}}. As Link approaches Rona Kachta, the Monk will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the Shrine,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Rona Kachta|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Rona Kachta|BotW}}</ref>
