==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Kings & Queens of the Sky}}" is an event wherein a greater number of winged [[Spirit]]s randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Winged spirits are gliding, soaring and flapping their way to your Spirit Board! Defeat them in battle to earn more Spirit Points than usual.<br/><br/>The Legend-class Wind Fish, Tabuu, Omega Ridley, plus Articuno, Zapdos, & Moltres will also appear at set times. Don't let them get away! |author= Nintendo |published= March 27, 2019 |retrieved= March 27, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/b82c3d5c/index.html |title= Event: Kings & Queens of the Sky - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace Spirits befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
