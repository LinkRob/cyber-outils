{{Term/Store|Shield Surfing||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|Shield Surf|Ability Controls|BotW}}</ref> also stylized as '''Shield-Surfing''',<ref>{{Cite|Hey, maaan! What's that? You're dyin' for some {{Color|BotW Red|shield-surfing}} action, that it?|Fin|BotW}}</ref> is a recurring ability in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
To perform {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can jump on any of his {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} and ride down the slopes of {{Term|BotW|Snow|link}}-, {{Term|BotW|Sand|link}}-, or {{Term|BotW|Grass|link}}-covered mountains and hills as though it were a {{Wp|Snowboard}}. Different {{Plural|BotW|Shield}} have different frictions and speeds, as do different surfaces. Using a {{Term|BotW|Shield}} this way on rough terrain like the rocks on {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}} will drain the {{Term|BotW|Shield}}'s {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}} rapidly, so it can break if this technique is overused. However, {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}} on dirt paths or grass does not damage the {{Term|BotW|Shield}} anywhere near as much,{{Verify|BotW}} and on {{Term|BotW|Sand}} and {{Term|BotW|Snow}}, it doesn't damage the {{Term|BotW|Shield}} at all. {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing}} during {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}} further reduces damage done to the {{Term|BotW|Shield}} and increases speed.

==={{TotK|-}}===
In addition to its previous iteration, Link also has the ability to grind on rail tracks.<ref>{{Cite|You can shield surf along the tracks used for mine carts and other on-rail conveyances.|Loading screen tip|TotK}}</ref> Link can perform a special spin by pressing Y that can attack enemies. The {{Term|TotK|Fuse|link}} ability can be used with shields on certain items to increase shield surfing efficiency. Some items may allow to surf even on rocky surfaces with little durability loss, but some specific items may cause a gradual height gain if used correctly. Others may hinder which will cause Link to bail if such items are surfed on with.
