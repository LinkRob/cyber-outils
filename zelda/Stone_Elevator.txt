The {{Term/Store|Stone Elevator||Series, LA, LADX, FSA}} is a recurring living elevator that acts as an obstruction in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 207 {{Exp Game|LADX}}}}</ref>

In {{LA|-}}, it appears in a sidescrolling passageway near the end of the [[Bottle Grotto]]. At first, it appears identical to other moving elevators in the game (which either move automatically or drop when Link stands on them), but when Link stands upon it, its grimacing face appears as it holds up Link's weight, making it impossible to get past it and progress through the dungeon to reach the [[Nightmare]]. To pass this obstacle, Link has to use his [[Power Bracelet]] and carry one of the large [[jar]]s to be heavy enough to move the Stone Elevator.

In {{FSA}}, a Stone Elevator appears during [[The Mountain Path]] stage, in another sidescrolling area. Unlike its previous incarnation, the face is always visible, and the four Links can move it using their own weight. Rather than blocking Link's path, it instead drops to make it difficult for him to reach a series of paths on one side of the cave.

In both instances, the Stone Elevator is invincible, and does not actually cause any direct harm to Link.

==Trivia==
*This creature's Japanese name, {{Exp|オシン|Oshin}} is a name meaning "patience" or "endurance", and is very similar to that of the [[Mega Thwomp]] ({{Exp|ドシン|Doshin}}), suggesting a relation.
