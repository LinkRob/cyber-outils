"{{Term/Store|Green Is the New Black||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: Green Is the New Black}}<br/>03/13/2019 |author= Nintendo |published= March 13, 2019 |retrieved= March 13, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/37553190/index.html |title= Event: Green Is the New Black - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Green Is the New Black}}" is an event wherein a greater number of green-colored {{Plural|SSBU|Primary Spirit|link}} or {{Plural|SSBU|Support Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Green-colored spirits have made their way to the Spirit Board! Defeat them in battle to earn more gold than usual.<br/><br/>Legend-class spirits Queen Metroid, Celebi, and Link (The Legend of Zelda) will appear at set times. Check it out! |author= Nintendo |published= March 13, 2019 |retrieved= March 13, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/37553190/index.html |title= Event: Green Is the New Black - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace Spirits befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
