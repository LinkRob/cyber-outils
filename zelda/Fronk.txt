{{Term/Store|Fronk||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fronk|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Fronk}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} who can be found at {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. Alongside {{Term|BotW|Ledo|link}}, he is working on repairing the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}} as an apprentice under the smithy {{Term|BotW|Dento|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fronk and I are Master Dento's faithful apprentices. Fronk and I are always striving to better ourselves in the workshop. Master always tells us, "Repairing the domain is part of your training." So that's what we're doing now.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's why my senior apprentice, Fronk, and I are repairing the domain. However, our materials are a bit depleted...|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} initially approaches {{Term|BotW|Fronk}}, he will be distant from him. He may ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to get out of his way.<ref>{{Cite|Pardon me. Could you please get out of the way?|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> He might also be quiet for a moment, before he apologize to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and explains that he was lost in thought.<ref>{{Cite|... My apologies. I was lost in thought...|Fronk|BotW}}</ref>

After the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} is appeased by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} can be found hammering a pillar in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} while conversing with himself.<ref>{{Cite|They say {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} has calmed down, and yet...|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} will apologize and explain that he was talking to himself.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! It is you, Link! I am sorry... I was talking to myself.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} says that after {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|display=Ruta}} started rampaging, his wife {{Term|BotW|Mei|link}} went out to go fishing.<ref>{{Cite|The truth is... My wife Mei left and hasn't come back yet. After the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}} started rampaging, she went out to go fishing like everything was normal.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> Even though the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} is appeased, she still has not returned.<ref>{{Cite|And now {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} has settled down, but she still hasn't returned...|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} wishes he had made her stay and wonders what he should do.<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid the worst has happened... If only I had stopped her from leaving! Oh, what do I do?!|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers to find {{Term|BotW|Mei}}, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} expresses his gratitude and apologizes for placing this burden on him.<ref>{{Cite|Link... You saved Zora's Domain, and now you're going to help me find my Mei, as well? You are a true Champion! I am sorry for placing this burden on you, but please... Please bring her home!|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> This unlocks the "{{Term|BotW|A Wife Washed Away|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} says he will help {{Term|BotW|Link}} however he can to find {{Term|BotW|Mei}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you are going to look for Mei, I will help you however I can.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Mei}}, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} again says she is his wife and the mother of his children, {{Term|BotW|Tumbo|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Keye|link}}. He says she spends her days catching {{Plural|BotW|Fish|link}} and selling them at the {{Term|BotW|Coral Reef|link}} {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|She spends her days catching fish and selling them at the general store.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} describes {{Term|BotW|Mei}} as being very lively and cheerful, believing {{Term|BotW|Tumbo}} gets that from her.<ref>{{Cite|Unlike me, she is very lively and cheerful... I suppose Tumbo gets that from her.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} recalls that she was fishing in the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}}, which began to swell from {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}'s {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The day {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} started raging, she said she was going fishing in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora River}}. {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}}'s rage caused the river to swell. She may have been swept downstream!|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> He believes that the worst case scenario is that she was carried to {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Worst case scenario...she could have been carried all the way to {{Color|BotW Red|Lake Hylia}}.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Mei}} has returned to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} scolds his wife and tells her that she should not go wandering anymore for a while.<ref>{{Cite|After we last met, my darling gave me a good scolding... He said I shouldn't go wandering about anymore for a while...|Mei|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} is overwhelmingly appreciative towards {{Term|BotW|Link}} and thanks him multiple times.<ref>{{Cite|Link! LINK!! My wife, Mei... She came back to me! I cannot possibly thank you enough! But I shall try! Thank you! Thank you! THANK YOU, TRULY!!|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him again, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} calls him a true {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} and thanks him in a more composed manner.<ref>{{Cite| Link! You are a true Champion... Now you have saved my wife, my children, and myself... Thank you, truly.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref>
