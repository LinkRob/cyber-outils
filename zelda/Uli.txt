{{Term/Store|Uli||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TPHD|Uli}} is {{Term|TPHD|Colin|link}}'s mother and {{Term|TPHD|Rusl|link}}'s wife, found in {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}} waiting for her new daughter to be born.<ref name="TPHD Guide 16"/> She tells {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} that she has lost the {{Term|TPHD|Cradle|link}} she made for {{Term|TPHD|Colin}} when he was born.<ref>{{Cite|But I must ask, Link... You have not seen a {{Color|TPHD Red|cradle}} come floating by here, have you? It is a baby's cradle made of finely woven tree bark... Oh, such a misfortune... How far could it have drifted, I wonder... What am I to do? I made that cradle when my first child was born, and I have cherished it all these years...|Uli|TP}}</ref> When {{Term|TPHD|Link}} retrieves the cradle from the {{Term|TPHD|Monkey|link}} that stole it, she gives him the {{Term|TPHD|Fishing Rod|link}} that {{Term|TPHD|Colin}} made for him.<ref>{{Cite|I had nearly forgotten... I am supposed to give you this... All right... Here you go! It is a little...unpolished. My son, Colin, made this under the instruction of his father. You can use it if you like.|Uli|TP}}</ref> {{Term|TPHD|Uli}} can be found on her front porch after {{Term|TPHD|Link}} is transformed into {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} waiting for {{Term|TPHD|Rusl}}, who attempts to search for the monsters that kidnapped the Ordonian children. If {{Term|TPHD|Link}} attempts to approach her, she will yell, causing {{Term|TPHD|Rusl}} to return and attack him.

During the ending credits, {{Term|TPHD|Uli}} can be seen tending to her newborn baby girl as she notices the return of {{Term|TPHD|Rusl}} and {{Term|TPHD|Colin}} from {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village|link}}.
