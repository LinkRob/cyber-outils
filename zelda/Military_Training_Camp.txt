The {{Term/Store|Military Training Camp||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Military Training Camp'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Military Training Camp is the area surrounding {{Term|BotW|Woodland Tower|link}}. The Camp is made up of multiple destroyed wooden towers sinking into a mire. The Camp is situated on a hillside southeast of {{Term|BotW|Lake Mekar|link}}.

The Camp is inhabited by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} that guard {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
The Military Training Camp can be unlocked after [[Link]] gives 5 [[Moblin Trophy|Moblin trophies]], 50 [[Lizalfos Trophy|Lizalfos trophies]], and 200 [[Bokoblin Trophy|Bokoblin trophies]]. The Military Training Camp can be used to level up all of the fighters for a certain amount of [[Rupee|rupees]]. As the character's level gets higher, so does the fee.

One exception in the Military Training Camp is that the place can only level up characters up to the highest leveled character. For example, if Link has the highest level with Level 30, than all the other characters can only be leveled up to Level 30.
{{Noncanon|End}}
