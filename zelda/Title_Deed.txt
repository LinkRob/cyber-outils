{{Term/Store|Title Deed|Title Deeds|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are items in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The four Title Deeds each correspond to one of [[Termina]]'s five main regions, excluding [[Ikana Canyon]], and are each held by a [[Business Scrub]] living in [[Deku Flower]]s. [[Link]] can obtain them by exchanging other Title Deeds, or a [[Moon's Tear]] in the case of the [[Town Title Deed]], while wearing the appropriate [[Mask]]. In {{MM3D}}, the Title Deeds are part of the "[[Business Scrub Scramble]]" [[Event]].

Only the Town Title Deed is required, as it grants [[Deku Link]] access to [[South Clock Town]]'s [[Clock Tower]] during his first cycle. The rest of the Deeds are optional and all yield an opportunity to obtain otherwise unreachable [[Piece of Heart|Pieces of Heart]]. Title Deeds are lost upon playing the "[[Song of Time]]" and returning to the [[First Day]]. After midnight of each night, Link can repurpose the Title Deeds as toilet paper by giving it to {{Term|MM3D|??? (Majora's Mask)|link}} in the [[Stock Pot Inn]]'s bathroom.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|Crisis in the toilet!}}<br/>You helped the mysterious hand in the restroom. It's no good to be out of {{Color|MM3D Red|paper}} in the toilet!<br/>Stock Pot Inn - restroom|The Ghost of the Inn|MM3D}}</ref>
