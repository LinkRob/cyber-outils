"{{Term/Store|Zelda's Lullaby||Series, OoT, OoT3D}}",{{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref> also known as the '''Melody of the Royal Family''',{{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D}}<ref>{{Cite|Hoo hoo! Looks like you've gotten bigger and stronger already, Link! Just ahead lies {{Color|OoT3D Dark Blue|Zora's Domain}}. The Zoras serve Hyrule's Royal Family by protecting this water source. Their door will not open for anyone except those who have some connection with the Royal Family. Let them hear the {{Color|OoT3D Dark Blue|melody of the Royal Family}}! Hooo hoo hoooot!|Kaepora Gaebora|OoT3D}}</ref> and "'''Zelda's Lullaby:OT'''",{{Exp Game|HW}}{{Fact}} is a [[Recurring Music in The Legend of Zelda Series|recurring song]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}. Since its debut in {{ALttP}}, it usually serves as background music whenever [[Princess Zelda]] appears and is thus considered to be her theme song. It appears as a playable melody in {{OoT}} and {{TP}}.

== Locations and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" plays in the background when [[Link]] meets Princess Zelda for the first time and whenever Link rescues one of the [[Maiden|Seven Maidens]].

==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT3D Zelda's Lullaby Icon.png|left]]
"Zelda's Lullaby" is taught by [[Impa]] after initially meeting [[Princess Zelda]] at her [[Hyrule Castle#Princess Zelda's Courtyard|courtyard]]. It is a sacred song with mysterious powers passed down by the [[Royal Family of Hyrule|Royal Family]] for generations, and it also served as a lullaby for the young Princess Zelda.<ref>{{Cite|This is an ancient melody passed down by the Royal Family. I have played this song for Princess Zelda as a lullaby ever since she was a baby... There is mysterious power in these notes.|Impa|OoT}}</ref> "Zelda's Lullaby" plays a major role in solving numerous puzzles in the game, such as raising water levels in the {{Term|OoT3D|Water Temple|link}}, entering [[Zora's Domain]], and opening other hidden passages. The mark that usually signals the need to play "Zelda's Lullaby" is a [[Triforce]] symbol, as seen when opening the [[Royal Family's Tomb]] and changing the water levels in the Water Temple.

According to Impa, only members of the Royal Family are allowed to learn "Zelda's Lullaby"; as such, playing this tune helps prove Link's connection to the Royal Family.<ref>{{Cite|The song I just taught you has some mysterious power. Only Royal Family members are allowed to learn this song. Remember, it will help to prove your connection with the Royal Family.|Impa|OoT}}</ref>  After [[Ganon]] has been defeated and Link and Princess Zelda are talking in the sky, the princess sends the young hero back to his original time by playing "Zelda's Lullaby" on the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}}, further demonstrating the melody's mysterious powers.

==={{MM|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" can be heard as background music during the flashback scene where Princess Zelda gives Link the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}}. In the northern part of [[Termina Field]], "Zelda's Lullaby"'s notes sometimes appear on the wall where Link can play a song for [[Rupee]]s.

==={{TWW|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" is played when [[Tetra]] is revealed to be Princess Zelda. Furthermore, it is heard during the staff credits as part of the ending theme. A darker rendition of the theme was also heard when Ganondorf discovers Tetra's true identity on Forsaken Fortress before transitioning to Ganondorf's leitmotif.

==={{FSA|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" plays after Link rescues Princess Zelda from the {{Term|FSA|Temple of Ice|link}}. It also makes an appearance during the final scene and credits. Unlike {{ALttP|-}},  the song that plays after rescuing each of the Seven Maidens is a different one, although it seems to be loosely based on slowed down version on "Zelda's Lullaby".

==={{TMC|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" occasionally plays when Princess Zelda and Link go to the [[Picori Festival]]. It also appears when Zelda first visits [[Link's House|Link's house]] and during the ending credits.

==={{TP|-}}===
Upon first entering the [[Sacred Grove]], [[Wolf Link]] must howl the lullaby in order to make the [[Skull Kid]] appear. Once Link defeats him, the Skull Kid will open the way to the inside of the grove. He must howl it again after he reaches the inner part of the forest to activate the guardian statues and solve their puzzle to access the path to the [[Master Sword]].

Zelda's theme is also heard on both occasions that Wolf Link meets Zelda inside her chamber in [[Hyrule Castle]], and it's played once again when Princess Zelda receives the [[Light Arrow]]s from the [[Light Spirit]]s and asks Link for his help. A distorted version of "Zelda's Lullaby" can be heard when battling against [[Possessed Zelda#Twilight Princess|Puppet Zelda]].

==={{PH|-}}===
A darker version of "Zelda's Lullaby" is played when Tetra pleads for Link's help as she gets swallowed by the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}. The normal tune is later heard after Tetra returns to normal inside [[Linebeck]]'s [[SS Linebeck|ship]].

==={{ST|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" is heavily featured as background music whenever Princess Zelda is on screen.

==={{SS|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" is played just before Zelda goes into her slumber. This could be the reason the song is called Zelda's "Lullaby". Additionally, the main theme for this game, "[[Ballad of the Goddess]]", is a slightly modified version of "Zelda's Lullaby" being played backwards.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" first plays when Link meets Zelda in [[Hyrule Castle]] to warn her that [[Yuga]] has transformed [[Seres]] and the Captain of the Guard into paintings. A darker version is later played when she is transformed into a painting herself. During the final battle, a piece of it is played when Zelda gives Link the Light Arrows, and finally, Zelda's Lullaby is one of the songs that can be played at the [[Milk Bar]] in [[Kakariko Village]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" is one of the many tunes the [[Lucky Lobby Ball]] will play when hit with the [[Sword]].

==={{BotW|-}}===
A slowed down version of "Zelda's Lullaby" plays while riding a [[Horse]] on the field in {{BotW}}. The motif is also frequently referenced in other tracks relevant to Princess Zelda, such as "A King's Request", "Link's Memories: Slumbering Power", "Link's Memories: Zelda's Awakening", "Do You Remember?", and the interior portion of "Hyrule Castle".

==={{TotK|-}}===
Just like in {{BotW}}, "Zelda's Lullaby" also plays while riding a [[Horse]], inside "Hyrule Castle" and in some other moments related to Princess Zelda. However, it's main appearance comes at the end of the game, when Zelda's draconification is reversed, probably by [[King Rauru|Rauru]]'s power of light and [[Queen Sonia|Sonia]]'s time power (according to [[Mineru]]'s theory). Then, it plays again when Link is approaching the princess in the final dive.
==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSBB|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" is heard at the beginning of the "[[Ocarina of Time Medley]]", and it appears alongside other familiar melodies such as the "[[Song of Storms]]", "[[Epona's Song]]", "[[Saria's Song]]", and the "[[Song of Time]]".

==={{HW|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby:OT" can be heard on the battle results screen. "Zelda's Lullaby" can also be heard as part of "[[Main Theme:1st]]", which plays on the [[Adventure Mode]] screen and in the [[Adventure Map|Adventure]], [[Master Quest Map|Master Quest]], and [[Rewards Map]]s.

==={{SSB4|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" appears at the beginning of the "Ocarina of Time Medley" in {{SSB4}} and "[[The Legend of Zelda Medley]]" track in {{SSB4|Wii U}}.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" appears as part of the "Ocarina of Time Medley" and "The Legend of Zelda Medley" tracks in {{SSBU}}.

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
"Zelda's Lullaby" is played by [[Terrako]] on multiple occasions, and is shown to be used as an actual lullaby for [[Princess Zelda]].
{{Noncanon|End}}
