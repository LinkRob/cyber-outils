{{Term/Store|Fanadi's Palace||Series, TP, TPHD}},<ref name="Mansion">{{Cite|The Fortune-Telling Mansion / FANADI'S PALACE|Sign|TP}}</ref> also known as '''The Fortune-Telling Mansion''',<ref name="Mansion"/> is a location in {{TP}}.

==Features and Overview==
Fanadi's Palace is located in [[Hyrule Castle Town]], where [[Madame Fanadi]] offers to read people's fortunes at the price of 10 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the fooortune-telling house, Fanadi's Palace... The fates swiiiiiirl about you, and only I can tell what they have in stooore... The dooor to the future will ooopen...for 10 Rupees!|Madame Fanadi|TP}}</ref> Despite its name, it is not actually a palace or a mansion of any sort, and instead appears as a small, cluttered room.

Here, [[Link]] can choose to either have Madame Fanadi read his "Career" fortune, which tells him where to go next on his adventure, or his "Love" fortune, which shows him where to find an unclaimed [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|Goooood... So... Which doooor will open?|Madame Fanadi|TP}}</ref> If Link has found all of the Pieces of Heart, Madame Fanadi will return his Rupees should he ask for his fortune in love.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmmm... The ooooonly thing that appears in the crystal is the image of yoooooou... It appears that yooouuuu have already found all the looooooove the crystal can seeeeeee...|Madame Fanadi|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I will return the foooortune fee... Taaaaake care of the hearts that yoooooou have now...|Madame Fanadi|TP}}</ref>
