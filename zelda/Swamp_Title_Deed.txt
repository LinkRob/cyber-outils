The {{Term/Store|Swamp Title Deed||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
<!-- {{Color|MM3D Red|Swamp Title Deed}}<br/>The title deed for the Deku Flower in the Southern Swamp. You traded your Town Title Deed for this. -->
The Swamp Title Deed is a claim to the [[Deku Flower]] located beside the [[Swamp Tourist Center]] in the [[Southern Swamp]]. If Link approaches the Deku Flower, a [[Business Scrub]] will pop out from it. If Link speaks to the Business Scrub as a [[Deku Link|Deku]], the Scrub will inform him that he is selling [[Magic Beans]] to [[Deku Scrub]]s, though he couldn't make the journey to the [[Deku Palace]] because of the [[Big Octo]]s blocking the way.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for stopping by! I'm doing business with a focus on a Deku Scrub clientele. Actually, I wanted to business at the palace, but there was this big octopus...and... Nothing could really be done about it, so I set up shop here. Do you know what {{Color|MM3D Green|Magic Beans}} are, sir? I'll sell you one for {{Color|MM3D Purple|10 Rupees}}.|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> However, if Link speaks to the Business Scrub as a [[Human]], [[Goron Link|Goron]], or [[Zora Link|Zora]], the Business Scrub will mention that he is interested in setting up shop at a more lively place.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for stopping! You're not from here, are you? I sell {{Color|MM3D Green|Magic Beans}} to Deku Scrubs, but I'd really like to leave my hometown. I'm hoping to find some success in a livelier place! But to open a business in another place... You need to go through the proper channels first. Do you know what I mean?|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> If Link presents the [[Town Title Deed]], the Business Scrub will offer his own Deku Flower in return.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! That's a {{Color|MM3D Red|Town Title Deed}}! I wish you'd let me have that. I'll give you this land in return! Deal?|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> If Link accepts, the Business Scrub will give Link the Swamp Title Deed and leave for his new Deku Flower in [[South Clock Town]].<ref>{{Cite|You traded your Town Title Deed for a {{Color|MM3D Red|Swamp Title Deed}}!|N/A|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm going off to town right now! I'll leave the rest up to you!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>

With the Swamp Title Deed in Link's possession, he can freely use the Deku Flower in the Southern Swamp to fly to ledges within range, such as the roof of the Swamp Tourist Center. Link can alternatively repurpose the Swamp Title Deed as toilet paper by giving it to {{Term|MM3D|??? (Majora's Mask)|link}} in the [[Stock Pot Inn]]'s bathroom after midnight each night.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|Crisis in the toilet!}}<br/>You helped the mysterious hand in the restroom. It's no good to be out of {{Color|MM3D Red|paper}} in the toilet!<br/>Stock Pot Inn - restroom|Bombers' Notebook|MM3D}}</ref> Link can also trade the Swamp Title Deed to the Business Scrub outside the [[Goron Shrine]] in [[Goron Village]] in exchange for the [[Mountain Title Deed]].<ref>{{Cite|Ah! That's a {{Color|MM3D Red|Swamp Title Deed}}! I wish you would give that to me! I'll give you this land in exchange!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> However, if he does so, the Business Scrub will move to the Deku Flower in the Southern Swamp and Link will not be able to access it.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going right back to the swamp!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>
