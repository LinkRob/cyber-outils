'''Forge Constructs''' are friendly [[Construct|constructs]] in {{TotK}}.
==Location and Uses==
A Forge Construct is a shop where you can exchange your [[Zonaite]] and Large Zonaite with [[Zonai Charge|Zonai Charges]] and [[Crystallized Charge|Crystallized Charges]]. Only found in [[The Depths]] and the [[Sky]]. There are Forge Constructs at all major abandoned mines in The Depths.
