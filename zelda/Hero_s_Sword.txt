{{Term/Store|Hero's Sword|Hero's Swords|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=127 {{Exp Game|TWW}} }}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWDE}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
The {{Term|TWWHD|Hero's Sword}} is given to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} by [[Orca]] on [[Outset Island]]. While {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can train with Orca using the Sword, Orca will not give it to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} until the [[Helmaroc King]] drops [[Tetra]] over the [[Fairy Woods]]. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} temporarily loses it during his first visit at the [[Forsaken Fortress]], accidentally dropping it after being catapulted right into a wall, but he regains it after reaching the top of the Fortress. The {{Term|TWWHD|Hero's Sword}} is replaced by the [[Master Sword#The_Wind_Waker|Master Sword]] once {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} obtains it.

After {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} embeds the {{Term|TWWHD|Master Sword}} into {{Term|TWWHD|Ganondorf|link}}'s head and goes back to the world above the sea to sail with Tetra, he is seen wielding the {{Term|TWWHD|Hero's Sword}} once again.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW|-}}, the {{Term|HW|Hero's Sword}} appears as the first-tier version of the [[Light Sword]] weapon for [[Toon Link]], in conjunction with the {{Term|HW|Hero's Shield|link}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
