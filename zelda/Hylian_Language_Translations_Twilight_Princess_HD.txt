TITLE : Hylian Language Translations/Twilight Princess HD

<noinclude>{{TLoZ|colon}} {{TPHD}} uses a form of [[Hylian (Language)|Hylian text]] that is a cipher based on the Latin alphabet. It translates directly to English in most cases, and occasionally to Japanese. The form of Hylian text introduced in {{TWW}} also makes an appearance in the {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village Graveyard|link}}.

Most instances of [[Hylian Language Translations/Twilight Princess|Hylian text from the original release]] of {{TP}} are carried over to {{TPHD|-}}. The following text is newly-added or changed.</noinclude>
