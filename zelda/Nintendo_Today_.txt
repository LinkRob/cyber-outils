'''Nintendo Today!''' is a calendar-based phone app that was released on March 27, 2025 as a means to further ensure [[Nintendo]] players got any Nintendo-related news directly to them in the vein of Nintendo Directs. It allows for users to choose activities relating to their favorite franchises, including ''[[The Legend of Zelda (Series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'', and also has animated icons modeled after various franchises, including the ''Zelda'' franchise.

A teaser video was released on March 28, 2025 via the app that announced the release date for the live-action film adaptation of ''[[The Legend of Zelda (film)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' as occurring on March 26, 2025. The video showed the [[Triforce]] slowly coming into view across a black backdrop while the logo for the franchise emerges in a golden light. The BGM playing during the teaser is the Famicom Disk System version of the opening theme song for the original ''Legend of Zelda''

Content on the site includes an Art Museum depicting artwork for various games in the ''Zelda'' franchise. A teaser image for the app also indicated that the Orchestra Concert for the ''Zelda'' series would be involved to some capacity in the app.<ref>https://play-lh.googleusercontent.com/1l3l1bMGwuIlK8jdCrSj5ooay8V3uws4gxeykJ3nRhwnYeQzZG6qqRh-NHRuJwyG9w=w2560-h1440-rw</ref>

*Day 3: A bag rolls down a yellow hill with a [[Korok]] panicking as the bag halts to a rest.
*Day 4: A zoom in of ''Tears of the Kingdom'' Zelda walking with a torch.
*Day 5: A [[Guardian]] firing a beam of energy.
*Day 6: Two figures in a fog before being revealed via lightning to be [[Riju]] and [[Urbosa]], subtly alluding to their ties to the lightning element.
*Day 7: Link riding on Epona with a sunset and ocean in the background.
*Day 8: ''BOTW''-Zelda in a prayer pose while behind Link
*Day 9: An animation from ''BOTW'' with Bokoblins dancing around a campfire.
*Day 10: An animation showing a man bracing and holding onto a sign as a storm passes through and strikes a nearby wooden parts storage area.
*Day 11: ''BOTW''-era Zelda sitting on a hill with Link as a gust blows by with pedals from nearby [[Silent Princess]]es passing by them.
*Day 12: [[Tulin]] flapping his wings and causing a gust of wind as [[Revali]] stands nearby.
*Day 13: A figure is ominously obscured in shadow before it pans out and reveals itself to be ''TOTK''-era Ganondorf. The number 13 is in red.
*Day 14: It focuses on a fairy fountain before [[Tera]] emerges from it.
*Day 15: Panning back to show ''BOTW''-era Link climbing a cliff with the number 15 to his left.
*Day 16: [[Kass]] on a cliffside staring out while holding an accordion.
*Day 17: Link swimming in a body of water with lilypads nearby.
*Day 18: ''TOTK''-era Zelda holding the damaged Master Sword.
*Day 19: A menacing face is shown before zooming out to show Link staring down a Golden Lynel.
*Day 20: [[Sidon]] and [[Mipha]] side by side.
*Day 21: A zoomin on one of the Sky Islands, presumably the Temple of Time, with Link near an edge looking out.
*Day 22: [[Hestu]] alongside three Koroks proceed to dance and teleport in before striking a pose together.
*Day 23: Various explosions are emitted before [[Master Kohga]] emerges, with the number 23 being seen in one of them.
*Day 24: Link caught in a blizzard and using his sword as a means to keep himself stable from the gusting winds.
*Day 25: An animation of the [[Sheikah Slate]] boot up is shown before showing off [[Impa]], [[Purah|Adult Purah]], [[Paya]], [[Josha]], and [[Robbie]].
*Day 26: It zooming out to show Link about to aim a bow and arrow in a pose resembling that of the ''BOTW'' teaser.
*Day 27: It focusing on Daruk before a fireball emerges from behind him and then lands in front of him to reveal Yunobo.
*Day 28: Link running towards a cliff and jumping off as the sun was setting only to use a [[Paraglider]] and glide through the air just as the image freezes.
*Day 29: [[Mineru]] and [[Rauru]] emerges from fog as a subtle reference to their deceased status.
*Day 30: Zelda, Link and the Four Champions descending at a diagonal downward manner.
*Day 31: The [[Ultrahand]] logo is shown on a green pattern, with a Shrine of Light opening up and causing various green font alongside the number 31 to emerge.

*Spring: Lunk walks up to a tree releasing cherry blossoms, looks up from his point of view, then stands near it as the camera shutter occurs.
*Summer: Link in swimtrunks jumping off a cliff into the water in a manner similar to the gameplay trailer for {{BotW}} and then standing afterwards and smiling.
*Autumn: Link riding a [[Deer]] in a deciduous forest with leaves turning orange before cutting to Link carrying a handful of mushrooms with two piles near him and the deer nearby as it shutters.
*Halloween: Link dressed up in a skeleton as several skeletal enemies and Keese emerge before posing and it freezing in place.
*Winter: Link while clad in swimtrunks shivers during a blizzard before diving into an implied hot spring and then surfacing with a refreshed expression.
