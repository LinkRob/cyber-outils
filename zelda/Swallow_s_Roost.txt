The {{Term/Store|Swallow's Roost||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Swallow's Roost'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Swallow's Roost is located in [[Rito Village]].  A standard bed will cost 20 rupees and refill all your hearts.  The Rito-Down bed will cost 80 rupees which will fully recover Link's health and also give him three temporary Hearts and a single temporary Stamina Wheel.
