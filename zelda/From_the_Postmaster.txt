"{{Term/Store|From the Postmaster||Series, ST}}" is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|From the Postmaster|Letters|ST}}</ref> As its name suggests, it is a {{Term|ST|Letter|link}} from the {{Term|ST|Postmaster|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
"{{Term|ST|From the Postmaster}}" is the first {{Term|ST|Letter}} {{Term|ST|Link}} receives in the game. He receives it from the {{Term|ST|Postman|link}} as he passes the {{Term|ST|Castle Town|link}} {{Term|ST|Postbox|link}} on the way to the {{Term|ST|Royal Engineer|link}} graduation ceremony at {{Term|ST|Hyrule Castle|link}}.{{Note|{{Term|ST|Link}} cannot pass through the archway in front of {{Term|ST|Hyrule Castle}} without triggering the delivery event, no matter the angle from which he approaches it.}}

In the {{Term|ST|Letter}}, the {{Term|ST|Postmaster|link}} notifies {{Term|ST|Link}} of the new {{Term|ST|Mail Delivery|link|display=postal system}} and introduces the {{Term|ST|Postman|link}}. It reads,

<blockquote>
''Dear {{Player Name}}, Our new postal system has started running today. If you're reading this letter, it means my new postman is doing his job right.''

''He's as hardworking and efficient as they come! So be nice to him, won't you? We'll be in touch again if anything new comes up. -- The Postmaster''
</blockquote>
