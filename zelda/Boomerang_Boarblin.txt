'''Boomerang Boarblin''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Boomerang Boarblin|Trained in the use of boomerangs, this Boarblin warrior is dangerous both coming and going.<ref name="Boomerblin">{{Cite|Trained in the use of boomerangs, this Boarblin warrior is dangerous both coming and going.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Boomerang Boarblin are a variant of Boarblin known for throwing Boomerangs. A stronger version, [[Boomerang Boarblin Lv. 2]], also exists. They can be summoned with an [[Echo]] cost of 2 when acquired.
