The {{Term/Store|Great Fairy of Ice||Series, FS}} is a character in {{FS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 263 {{Exp Game|FS}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Great Fairy of Ice is the guardian of the [[Talus Cave]].<ref name="Guardian">{{Cite|I am the Great Fairy of Ice. I am the guardian of Talus Cave. You have done well, young adventurers. You have done well to gather so much treasure. I dub you...little eggs, waiting to hatch into heroes. I grant you this {{Color|FS Red|Silver Key}}.|Great Fairy of Ice|FS}}</ref> She appears from her [[Fairy Fountain|Fountain]] at the end of the Talus Cave after the [[Links]] have defeated [[Dera Zol]]. There, she bestows the Links a Key alongside a title befitting their achievements. The type of Key that she distributes is based on the amount of [[Rupee]]s the Links have collected as well as whether they have defeated [[Vaati]] with a given set of Keys. If the Links have collected at least 1,000 Rupees, the Great Fairy of Ice will gift the Links a [[Silver Key]] and dub them "little hero eggs".<ref name="Guardian"/> If the Links have collected at least 3,000 Rupees and have defeated Vaati after collecting all of the Silver Keys, she will dub them "brave heroes" and bestow upon them [[Golden Key]]s.<ref>{{Cite|I am the Great Fairy of Ice. I am the guardian of Talus Cave. Collecting this many Rupees is an incredible feat! I dub you...brave heroes and grant you each a {{Color|FS Orange|Golden Key}}.|Great Fairy of Ice|FS}}</ref> Defeating Vaati and returning to the Great Fairy of Ice's Fountain with at least 5,000 Rupees will prompt her to declare the Links the "greatest of heroes" and grant them [[Hero's Key]]s.<ref>{{Cite|I am the Great Fairy of Ice. I am the guardian of Talus Cave. There is nothing left to be said. I dub you...the greatest of heroes and grant you each a {{Color|FS Orange|Hero's Key}}.|Great Fairy of Ice|FS}}</ref>

The Great Fairy of Ice then cautions the heroes that they must have collected a complete set of Keys in order to open the way to [[Vaati's Palace]].<ref>{{Cite|You all seem to have collected the three {{Color|FS Red|Silver Keys}}. Vaati's palace of winds is a great castle that floats in the sky. Now that you have gathered the keys of the Fairies of Forest, Ice and Flame, the palace shall appear. Onward, young heroes! You must save Princess Zelda!|Great Fairy of Ice|FS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If all of you collect the three {{Color|FS Orange|Golden Keys}} of the Great Fairies of Forest, Ice, and Flame... ...a path to Vaati's Palace shall open! Onward, heroes!|Great Fairy of Ice|FS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If all of you gather the {{Color|FS Orange|Hero Keys}} of the Great Fairies of Forest, Ice, and Flame, a path to Vaati's Palace shall open. Onward, great heroes!|Great Fairy of Ice|FS}}</ref>

If the Links have defeated Vaati after collecting all of the Hero's Keys in {{FSAE}}, the Great Fairy of Ice's appearance and personality will change to resemble the {{Term|MM|Great Fairy of Wisdom|link}} from {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! You have done well to get this far. I am the Great Fairy of Ice! Throughout the ages I have faced off against the ice as the guardian of Talus Cave.|Great Fairy of Ice|FSAE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I see you already have the three {{Color|FS Orange|Hero Keys}}! I wonder if you know about the path that opened to {{Color|FS Blue|Vaati's Palace}}. It has {{Color|FS Orange|12 floors}}... Do you need an explanation? ...No? Then off you go, brave heroes!|Great Fairy of Ice|FSAE}}</ref>
