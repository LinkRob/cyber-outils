{{Term/Store|Minish Portal|Minish Portals|Series, TMC|plural}} are objects in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 80}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal}} can be accessed by {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} throughout {{TMC}}. These {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal|display=Portals}} allow {{Term|TMC|Link}}, with {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}}'s help, to reduce his size to that of a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}. The {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal}} allow {{Term|TMC|Link}} to access different areas, and after swallowing a {{Term|TMC|Jabber Nut|link}}, allows communication with the {{Term|TMC|Minish}}. {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal}} can also be used to return {{Term|TMC|Link}} to normal size. Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} is of {{Term|TMC|Minish}} size, he cannot return to normal size without using a {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal}}.

==Types of {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal}}==
==={{Plural|TMC|Dungeon Entrance}}===
{{Main|Dungeon Entrance}}
{{Plural|TMC|Dungeon Entrance}} are only found inside {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}}.

==={{Plural|TMC|Pot Entrance}}===
{{Main|Pot Entrance}}
{{Plural|TMC|Pot Entrance}} are found throughout {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. To be used, they must be upside down; the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}} is sometimes required to flip them over.

==={{Plural|TMC|Rock Portal}}===
{{Main|Rock Portal}}
{{Plural|TMC|Rock Portal}} are found in the mountainous regions of northwestern {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}.

==={{Plural|TMC|Stump Entrance}}===
{{Main|Stump Entrance}}
{{Term|TMC|Stump Entrance}} are the most common {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|display=Portal}} type in the {{Term|TMC|Overworld|link}}.

==={{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets}}===
The entrance of the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}} is a special type of {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal}} that allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to shrink down and enter the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}.
