==Appearances==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Fragrant Scent}}
In {{MM}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Fragrant Scent|link}} can lead {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} to {{Plural|MM3D|Mushroom|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, {{Plural|TPHD|Scent}} can only be seen and learned by {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} while using his senses. {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}, even in human form, can pick up a visual cue that a {{Term|TPHD|Scent}} can be learned by a colored cloud around the scented item. There are five different {{Plural|TPHD|Scent}} that can be learned, the {{Term|TPHD|Youths' Scent|link}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Scent of Ilia|link}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Poe Scent|link}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish Scent|link}}, and the {{Term|TPHD|Medicine Scent|link}}, each one represented by a different color.<ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TPHD Red|Youths'}} Scent!|N/A|TPHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TPHD Red|Scent of Ilia}}!|N/A|TPHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TPHD Red|Poe}} Scent!|N/A|TPHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TPHD Red|Reekfish}} Scent!|N/A|TPHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TPHD Red|Medicine}} Scent!|N/A|TPHD}}</ref> However, {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} can only remember one {{Term|TPHD|Scent}} at a time, although the already used {{Plural|TPHD|Scent}} are not needed later.<ref>{{Cite|Forget the {{Color|TPHD Red|Reekfish}} Scent and learn a new smell?|N/A|TPHD}}</ref> The first three {{Plural|TPHD|Scent}} cannot be relearned since the things that give them off ({{Term|TPHD|Talo|link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Sword|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Ilia|link}}'s bag, and the {{Term|TPHD|Poe|link}}'s body) disappear. The last two, however, may be relearned and used interchangeably; for the {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish Scent}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can merely catch another {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish|link}} in {{Term|TPHD|Zora's Domain|link}}, and for the {{Term|TPHD|Medicine Scent}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can turn into {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} inside the {{Term|TPHD|Medical Clinic|link}}, move aside the crate that covers the puddle of medicine, and retrieve the {{Term|TPHD|Medicine Scent}}.
