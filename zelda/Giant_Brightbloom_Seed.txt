'''Giant Brightbloom Seeds''' are a material in {{TotK}}. They are used to illuminate dark areas. They can be found in caves and other underground areas. It has a Fuse Attack Power of 1. Compare with the [[Brightbloom Seed]] which is smaller.

<blockquote>
A seed that's found underground. When struck, it will take root and bloom, radiating a large amount of light from a big flower.
</blockquote>
