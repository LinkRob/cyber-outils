The {{Term/Store|Big Octorok||Series, TMC}} is the {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 166}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Big Octorok}} was referred to as '''Giant Octorok''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 62}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It is the guardian of the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}}.

==Background==
Though simply a typical {{Term|TMC|Octorok|link}} to a normal-sized {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, when {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, this mediocre creature becomes gargantuan. When encountered, it is frozen in the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}' central room, in close proximity to the frozen {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}}. After {{Term|TMC|Link}} flips two giant {{Plural|TMC|Switch|link}}, a hatch on the ceiling opens wide, allowing sunlight to thaw both the frozen {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link|display=Element}}. Before {{Term|TMC|Link}} can claim the {{Term|TMC|Water Element}}, the {{Term|TMC|Big Octorok}} sucks it up and retreats into the next room.

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Big Octorok}}
{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must use the {{Term|TMC|Big Octorok}}'s rock-spitting tendencies against it to inflict damage by deflecting the rocks with his {{Term|TMC|Shield|link}} or batting them back with his [[Sword]].  It will also try to suck {{Term|TMC|Link}} up, and will ram the walls, causing rocks to fall from the ceiling.

After a few hits, the {{Term|TMC|Big Octorok}} will freeze itself and all of the water covering the floor, and the budding flower on its back becomes vulnerable. {{Term|TMC|Link}} must run to the {{Term|TMC|Big Octorok}}'s backside and set the flower on fire with his {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}}. Repeating this process a few times will complete the battle; {{Term|TMC|Link}} will receive the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}} along with another {{Term|TMC|Heart Container|link}}.

==Trivia==
*In the British English translation, the Figurine is called "Octorok".
*In the Spanish translation, the Figurine is called "Ferrus negro", the name for {{Plural|TMC|Black Knight|link}}. The actual {{Term|TMC|Black Knight}} {{Term|TMC|Figurine}} is named "Blacknut" instead.
