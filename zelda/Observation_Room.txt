The {{Term/Store|Observation Room||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Observation Room'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Observation Room}} is a surveying spot positioned due north of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Gate|link}}, overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town Ruins|link}}. Initially, it is entirely inaccessible from all sides, as {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}} blocks all apertures connecting to the {{Term|BotW|Observation Room}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} destroys the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Eyeball|link}} in the adjacent {{Term|BotW|Reception Room|link}}, his way into the {{Term|BotW|Observation Room}} will be cleared. The interior of the {{Term|BotW|Observation Room}} is decorated with a lone, avian statue flanked by two stairs leading to an upper level where the viewing platform is. To access the balcony, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must destroy the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Eyeball}} in the {{Term|BotW|Observation Room|display=Room}}.

Prior to the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} would address his subjects from the {{Term|BotW|Observation Room}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 248}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} overtook {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}, all of its windows ended up covered in {{Term|BotW|Malice}}.<ref name="CaC"/>

On the {{Term|BotW|Observation Room}} balcony, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
Two {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found on the balcony of the {{Term|BotW|Observation Room}} through the use of {{Term|BotW|Archery (Mechanic)|link}}. The first will appear if {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots the coat of arms located on the balcony from below. The second is marked by a {{Term|BotW|Pinwheel|link}}, which causes a {{Term|BotW|Balloon|link}} to appear when {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches. Both of these {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}} for finding them.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
