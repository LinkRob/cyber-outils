The {{Term/Store|Roc's Feather||Series, LA, LADX, OoS, OoA, FSA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 136"/>

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Roc's Feather|This legendary treasure has been handed down from olden times on Koholint Island. The magical Feather makes the bearer's body much lighter and allows him to jump very high. The jumping ability bestowed by the Feather is useful for avoiding enemy attacks or jumping over pits.}}
In {{LA}}, the {{Term|LADX|Roc's Feather}} is found inside the {{Term|LADX|Tail Cave|link}}.<ref name="LA Tail Cave"/> {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} can combine it with the {{Term|LADX|Pegasus Boots|link}} to jump over multiple pits at once.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= LADX|page= 19}}</ref>

{{Term|LADX|Link}} needs the {{Term|LADX|Roc's Feather}} to jump over {{Term|LADX|Rolling Bones|link}}' {{Term|LADX|Spiketrap}} to avoid getting hurt.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 29}}</ref>
{{Clear|left}}

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Roc's Feather|Roc's Feather magically makes your body feel much lighter and allows you to jump into the air. Use Roc's Feather to jump over pits and dodge enemy attacks.}}
In {{OoS}}, the Roc's Feather is found in the [[Poison Moth's Lair]]. It is later stolen from Link by the [[Strange Brothers]]. To retrieve it, the hero must [[Stealth|follow them undetected]] in order to find its hiding location. Link can combine the Roc's Feather with [[Pegasus Seed]]s to jump over multiple adjacent pits.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Roc's Feather|Roc's Feather magically makes your body feel much lighter and allows you to jump into the air. Use Roc's Feather to jump over pits and dodge enemy attacks.}}
In {{OoA}}, the Roc's Feather is found in the [[Wing Dungeon]]. Just like in {{OoS|-}}, Link can combine its power with Pegasus Seeds in order to jump over multiple pits at once.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Manual|FSA|Roc's Feather|Use this to jump across holes and chasms. Press the B Button while you're in the air to drop and do a down-thrust attack.<br/>{{Big|Lev. 2}}<br/>You can also do a second jump in the air after your first jump.}}
In {{FSA}}, Roc's Feathers can be found in multiple stages. It allows the [[Links]] to jump over three pits. If the Links give the Feather to a [[Great Fairy]] in order to upgrade it to Level 2, it will give them the ability to jump once more while in midair.
