'''Platboom''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Platboom|An unusual monster that tries to crush anything that approaches. The brave can ride it to high-up places, however.<ref name="Platboom">{{Cite|An unusual monster that tries to crush anything that approaches. The brave can ride it to high-up places, however.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Platbooms are [[platform]]-like monsters that attempt to crush foes beneath them. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Platboom [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of three.
