The {{Term/Store|Cafe Bar||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a location in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gillian / Birthplace: Windfall Island / Workplace: The Cafe Bar|Nintendo Gallery|TWW}}</ref> It is located right next to [[Zunari's Shop]], and it is one of the most popular hang-out places around [[Windfall Island]]. It was previously run by [[Gillian]]'s father, but he has fallen ill and his daughter is now the one that manages it, although she does not seem to enjoy the job.<ref>{{Cite|Gillian manages the cafe in place of her now-ill father.|Nintendo Gallery|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If I look a little worried, it's just because my pop's been sick and bedridden for a long time now. That's why I'm stuck managing this cafe! It's taken me a while, but I think I'm finally getting the hang of running this place.|Gillian|TWW}}</ref> 

==Features and Overview==
Many citizens of Windfall enjoy going to the bar for either dates or even just to sit around and relax after a hard day at work.<ref>{{Cite|I've got it! Maybe I'll invite her to join me at the cafe bar_for coffee, of course!|Anton|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ahhhhhhh! Nothin' better than a cup of refreshment at your local cafe after a long day's work... Nothin'!|Candy|TWW}}</ref> At night, the cafe bar gets filled with sailors such as [[Dampa the Sailor|Dampa]], [[Candy]], [[Loot]], and [[Kane]], but during the day it is a much calmer place.<ref>{{Cite|When night falls around here, this place fills up with all these sour, swarthy sailors.|Gillian|TWW}}</ref> [[Gossack]] can always be found hanging around here, and at some point, various other characters that [[Link]] meets during his journey wind up coming to the bar for one reason or another.

If Link manages to unite [[Linda]] and [[Anton]], the young hero can go to the Cafe Bar to receive a [[Piece of Heart]] as a reward. Furthermore, Link can receive [[Moblin's Letter]] here from the [[Postman#The Wind_Waker|Rito postman]] [[Ilari]] after he witnesses a heated discussion between [[Maggie's Father]] and the said postman.
