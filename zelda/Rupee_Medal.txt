==Location and Uses==
Two Rupee Medals can be found within a couple of the [[Goddess's Treasure Chest|Goddess's Treasure Chests]] found within the [[Sky]]. While [[Link]] has one within his [[Pouch|Adventure Pouch]], [[Rupee|Rupees]] will appear more often. Like other pouch items, this has no effect in the [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round|Lightning Round]].
