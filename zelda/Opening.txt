==Overview==
==={{MM|-}}===
"{{Term|MM|Opening}}" is track 1 of {{Term|MM|The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask Orchestrations|link}}. It is an orchestration of the music played during the opening segment when {{Term|MM3D|Link}} is riding {{Term|MM3D|Epona|link}} through the {{Term|MM3D|Lost Woods|link}} and the music played during the intro animation at the start menu.

==={{BotW|-}}===
"{{Term|BotW|Opening}}" is track 2 in Disc 1 of {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Original Soundtrack|link}}. It plays when {{Term|BotW|Link}} first exits the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}} and looks over the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}.
