==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Zapshroom}}
{{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom}} can be found below trees in the [[Gerudo Highlands]] as well as in forests in [[Deep Akkala]]. They are also sold in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} by [[Ardin]]. When eaten raw, Zapshrooms can restore half of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}. Zapshrooms can also be cooked into meals using a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} Shock Resistance.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> Burning a Zapshroom with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} will turn it into a {{Term|BotW|Toasty Zapshroom|link}}. They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] in [[Hateno Village]] to make Yellow [[Dye]].

Zapshrooms are among the mushrooms that can be used to make [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Fragrant Mushroom Sauté]], [[Fruit and Mushroom Mix]], [[Glazed Mushrooms]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], a [[Mushroom Omelet]], [[Mushroom Rice Balls]], [[Mushroom Risotto]], a [[Mushroom Skewer]], [[Copious Mushrooms Skewers]], [[Salt-Grilled Mushrooms]], and [[Steamed Mushrooms]].

Five Zapshrooms, along with five {{Plural|BotW|Yellow Lizalfos Tail|link}}, are required to upgrade all three pieces of the {{Term|BotW|Rubber Armor (Set)|link}} set to their third tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Rubber Armor (Set)''': 15 (0, 0, 5, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
