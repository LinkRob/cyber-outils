==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Fragile Walls|Walls and floors with cracks in them can be knocked down with bombs. You can also try to knock down such walls with a dash attack. If you knock down a wall, you can sometimes get into the next room.}}
{{Stub|ALttP}}

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Fragile Walls|If you notice cracks in a wall, try setting a bomb next to it. This may destroy the wall, allowing you access to the room on the other side.}}
{{Stub|LA}}

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Plural|OoT3D|Cracked Wall}} appear {{Term|OoT3D|Inside the Deku Tree|link}}, in the {{Term|OoT|Fire Temple|link}}, {{Term|OoT|Water Temple|link}}, and {{Plural|OoT|Secret Grotto|link}}. Both an obviously-cracked and subtle version can be found, and they make a distinct sound when struck with a sword. {{Plural|OoT|Cracked Wall}} can be destroyed with {{Plural|OoT|Bomb|link}}, {{Plural|OoT|Bomb Flower|link}}, or {{Term|OoT|Blue Fire|link}}, revealing small passages, {{Plural|OoT|Treasure Chest|link}}, or {{Plural|OoT|Gold Skulltula|link}}.
