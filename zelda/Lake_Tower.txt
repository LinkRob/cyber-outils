The {{Term/Store|Lake Tower||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lake Tower|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Lake Tower is a [[Sheikah Tower]] that is located in the [[Faron Grasslands]], just south of [[Lake Hylia#Breath of the Wild|Lake Hylia]] and the [[Bridge of Hylia]]. The tower sits on a fractured hill with sunken pathways running through them, containing spiked barricades and various Bokoblins and Lizalfos lurking in wait. A [[Cooking Pot]] sits just south of the base of the tower. Upon activation, it adds data for the {{Term|BotW|Lake Tower Region|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.
