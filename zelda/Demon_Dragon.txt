The '''Demon Dragon''' {{Exp Game|TotK}}, also known as '''Dark Dragon Ganon''', is introduced in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'' as the third and final phase of the [[Bosses in Tears of the Kingdom|Final Boss]] battle. This dark creature is the embodiment of [[Ganondorf]]'s power, as a massive [[dragon]], but holds none of his original personality except a desire for pure destruction. 

==Overview==
[[File:TotK Light Dragon vs Demon Dragon.jpg|left|thumb|262x262px|Link watches the Demon Dragon (Ganon) and the Light Dragon (Zelda) battling each other.]]
Upon realizing [[Link]]'s strength is too much for him to handle, despite his formidable power as the Demon King, as a final resort, Ganondorf desperately undergoes [[draconification]], by swallowing his [[Secret Stone|Secret Stone of Darkness]], with the destruction of everyone now his only desire, turning himself into the Ganon reborn, the Demon Dragon. After transforming, Ganon seizes Link in his jaws and flies upward through the [[Hyrule Castle]] Chasm, breaking through to the surface. However, he is confronted by the [[Light Dragon]], the draconified form of [[Princess Zelda]], who saves the hero from the Demon Dragon's clutches. With the fated trio reunited once more, Link and Zelda prepared to finish off Ganon for good.

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|The form that Ganondorf, the Demon King, attained after swallowing a secret stone and attaining his true power. No trace of his original form remains. Its overwhelming magical strength would shake the skies. The jet-black scales covering it repel any and all attacks, save for those of the one and only Master Sword.|[[Hyrule Compendium]]}}

As the Demon Dragon, Ganondorf is given the title of '''Draconified Demon King''' and becomes Ganon once more, taking the form of an [[Immortal Dragon]] with jet black scales and red spikes adorning its back, along with a fiery red mane and dozens of sharp horns on its head. Curiously and perhaps oddly, both it and the Light Dragon have monocular vision (their eyes are located on the side of their heads rather than at the front), a feature usually reserved for prey animals, while predators and humans tend to have binocular vision.

Similar to [[Dark Beast Ganon]] in {{BotW}}, the battle is relatively simple in execution. In order for [[Link]] to deal damage, he must direct the Light Dragon (Zelda) above the Demon Dragon so he can jump off, landing on Ganon while avoiding the [[Gloom]] blasts fired from its mouth. Upon landing, the Hero of the Wild must attack four weak points - all bubbling pustules of eyes concentrated in pits of gloom on his back. After destroying a weak point, Ganon will attempt to throw Link off, but Zelda will retrieve the hero again to continue fighting. Outside of the four pits where the villain's weak points exists, the entire dragon's body is coated in Gloom, which will cause Link to suffer damage so long as he walks on those scales. Being hit by a Gloom blast will permanently remove one full [[Heart Container]] from Link.
[[File:Link trusting the Master Sword into the Secret Stone..jpg|thumb|231x231px|The final blow]]
The final weak point is the Secret Stone on his forehead, which is moderately more difficult to target due to his Gloom blasts being much closer. Upon reaching the weak point, two swings of the [[Master Sword]] will cut through the rest of Ganon's health bar. After a short scene of Ganon writhing in agony, Link is prompted to deliver the final blow by thrusting the sacred blade onto his forehead, which results in a large vertical explosion of [[Malice]] and [[Light]], finally vanquishing the [[Great Calamity|Calamity]] that has plagued the land of [[Hyrule]] for so long and Ganondorf/Ganon himself being ultimately slain.

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese release, the Demon Dragon is known as '''Black Dragon'''. 
*Upon his initial transformation in the [[The Depths|Depths]], the Demon Dragon bears a striking resemblance to the incorporeal form of [[Calamity Ganon]] from {{BotW|-}}.
*The appearance of the Demon Dragon is similar to [[Volvagia]], the boss of the [[Fire Temple]] from {{OoT}}, as well as the [[Immortal Dragon|dragons]] seen in the game's prequel. 
*The Demon Dragon's snout bears a slight resemblance to that of a boar, a likely reference to the standard form of [[Ganon]] seen in most previous entries of the series.
*Considering [[Ganondorf]]'s large size compared to other characters, the Demon Dragon is significantly larger than the Light Dragon.
*Ganondorf's loss of personality upon a transformation is reused from himself turning into his [[Dark Beast Ganon|beast form]] in {{TP}}. Unlike the Demon Dragon, however, the Dark Beast's defeat from {{TP|-}} reverted Ganondorf to his [[Gerudo]] form upon clashing with [[Midna]]. It's also a homage to when Ganon was revived by [[Twinrova]] in the linked ending of ''[[Oracle Series]]'', where he becomes a mindless beast with destruction only in his mind due to the [[Room of Rites|ritual]] being botched with the wrong sacrifice.
**Coincidentally, the Oracle series also has a male character tied to the Gerudo who transforms into a dragon late into his fight with Link (in this case, [[General Onox]]). Though the only difference is that the Dark Dragon form for Onox was his true form and he merely disguised himself as a human, while Ganondorf forcibly transformed himself via Draconification as a last resort.
*The musical theme used for the scene where Ganondorf engages in [[Draconification]], before the fight against the Demon Dragon, was a remix of Ganondorf's battle theme from {{OoT}}.
*The battle itself of [[Link]] scaling over the otherwise gigantic boss is a homage to a scrapped idea originally planned for Ganon in {{OoT|-}}, before it was transferred to [[mariowiki:Megaleg|Megaleg]] from ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Galaxy|Super Mario Galaxy]]''.
