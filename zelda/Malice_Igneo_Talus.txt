{{Term/Store|Malice Igneo Talus|Malice Igneo Talus|Series, HWAoC|Plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Boss|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}} They are a [[Malice]] infected variant of {{Plural|HWAoC|Igneo Talus|link}}, a fiery variant of the [[Stone Talus]].

Similar to their non-malice counterparts, but unlike Talus enemies in {{BotW}}, the player has the ability of damaging the Malice Igneo Taluses just by hitting the rocks that consists of the Taluses. But for Malice Igneo Taluses, they take much longer to be destroyed due to the Malice on their bodies. Their weak spots are the same, though; to drain the [[Weak Point Gauge]] of said enemy, one must hit the [[Ore Deposit]].
