'''Tickets''' are [[item]]s in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
There are two Tickets, both of which are sold by [[Ticket Seller]]s at ticket booths in the [[Shrine of Strength]]. Both Tickets allow [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] to enter the area's fields and challenge [[Ursore]]'s three [[Knight (Zelda's Adventure)|Knights]]. The first ticket, which depicts "ADMIT," allows Zelda to enter the field with the red Knight, while the patterned ticket allows her to visit the field with the blue and green Knights.
