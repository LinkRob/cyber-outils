The {{Term/Store|Bully's Friend||Series, ALttP}} is a boy transformed into a strange creature due to the [[Dark World]]'s magic in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 388}}</ref>

==Biography==
[[File:BullysFriendCured.gif|25px|left]]
The Bully's Friend was transformed into a ball-like creature when he came in search of the [[Triforce]] with the [[Bully]], who was transformed into a monster. The Bully's Friend states that he took the form of a ball because he was always indecisive while in the [[Light World]], and a round object came to reflect his inner nature.

[[File:BullysFriendInvert.gif|left|25px]]
Link finds him on top of [[Death Mountain]] where the Bully chases after him. He constantly runs away from him, but the Bully follows him and kicks him like a soccer ball. When Link wishes on the Triforce for Ganon's evil to be undone, all of Agahnim's wrongdoings are undone as well. As a result, Bully's Friend is transported back to the Light World. During the end credits, the Bully's Friend can be seen being chased by the Bully just outside of the [[Tower of Hera (A Link to the Past)|Tower of Hera]], both in their normal forms.

==Trivia==
*In the original version of the game, the Bully's Friend says "goro goro" which is a Japanese [[Wikipedia:Onomatopoeia|onomatopoeia]] for the sound of a rolling rock (the name of the [[Goron]] race originated from that same onomatopoeia).
*The Bully's Friend's head is as round as the ball which he was transformed into.
