The '''Might Bell''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Might Bell|A lovely bell made by the Great Fairy. It rings when it senses a might crystal nearby.<ref name="Might Bell">{{Cite|A lovely bell made by the Great Fairy. It rings when it senses a might crystal nearby.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Might Bell is rewarded after completing [[The Great Fairy’s Request]] [[Side Quest]]. Equipping it helps [[Princess Zelda]] detect [[Might Crystal|Might Crystals]] by ringing while in close proximity.
