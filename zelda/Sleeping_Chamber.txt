==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber}} is located on the second level of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, behind the stairs leading up to the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}}. It has four pools, which many {{Plural|BotW|Zora|link}} sleep in at night. Many {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}} also appear at the edges of the water. There are also six {{Plural|BotW|Jar|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can break for supplies.

Two {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} can always be found in the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber}}, these being {{Term|BotW|Torfeau|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Jiahto|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Torfeau}} practices with her {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear|link}}, while {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} will be examining the {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|link}}. After "{{Term|BotW|A Wife Washed Away|link}}" has been finished, {{Term|BotW|Mei|link}} will spend her day swimming in the pool beneath the stairs, though she is bored by this.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh... I'm so bored... But my darling is looking after the kids today... So all I have to worry about is having fun and being fancy-free!|Mei|BotW}}</ref>

Initially, {{Term|BotW|Kapson|link}} is also found inside the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber}}.<ref name="Gaddison"/> Due to his age, he would like one last chance to marry a couple, and so he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to inform him of any engaged couples he encounters.<ref>{{Cite|Link... As I'm sure you are well aware, the afterlife lays claim to all. We never know when it will beckon. If during your travels you meet any {{Color|BotW Red|engaged couples}}, I would like you to introduce them to me. Joining two souls is a true honor, one I shall stake my life on. Just one more wedding...and I shall be fulfilled.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> During the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hudson|link}} will task {{Term|BotW|Link}} with finding a priestly {{Term|BotW|Zora}}.<ref>{{Cite|If we're looking for a people devout enough to have a {{Color|BotW Red|priest-like person}}...I'd suggest the {{Color|BotW Red|Zora}}.|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} is interested in this,<ref>{{Cite|[Get a load of this...] I have been waiting to hear those words. Now, please tell me the specifics...|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> and he decides to leave {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} to officiate {{Term|BotW|Rhondson|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hudson}}'s wedding.<ref>{{Cite|As for the location... Hmmm... {{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}} in {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}}... {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} isn't too far from here. It would please me to hurry there right away.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref>

In the back of the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber}} is the {{Term|BotW|History of the Zora, Part Seven|link}}, one of the 10 {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} scattered throughout the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|H---ory of the Zor-, Part S-ven<br/>The H-ro Who D-feat-ed the Ly--l<br/>As T--d b-y King Dorephan|History of the Zora, Part Seven|BotW}}</ref> As noted by {{Term|BotW|Link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monument}} has been worn down by the passage of time.<ref>{{Cite|Time has taken its toll on this...|Link|BotW}}</ref> After completing the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}}, who will have a request for him.<ref>{{Cite|Hylian Champion Link... Will you hear my request?|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> In an effort to preserve {{Term|BotW|Zora}} history, he wants {{Term|BotW|Link}} to read all of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} and recount their contents to him.<ref>{{Cite|In the few years I have left, my role and joy in this world is to preserve Zora history for future generations... In other words, I'm counting on you! I'd like you to find all {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} around {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} and tell me what they say.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> This begins the "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} gives him a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Link, thank you for taking on such an arduous task! Please, allow me to reward you!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>

Another {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} begins after "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)}}" has been completed, this being "{{Term|BotW|The Giant of Ralis Pond|link}}". {{Term|BotW|Torfeau}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to defeat the {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} that had taken up residence near {{Term|BotW|Ralis Pond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|In fact, I wonder if you can do this, too... Exterminate the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hinox}} by {{Color|BotW Blue|Ralis Pond}}!|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref> Once this has been done, she will give him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Please, take this. Consider it a small token of my immense gratitude.|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref>
