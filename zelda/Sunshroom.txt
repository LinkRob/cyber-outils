==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Sunshroom}}
Sunshrooms are found growing near trees in [[Eldin Canyon]] and the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. They can be purchased from [[The Slippery Falcon]] in [[Rito Village]], and [[Ardin]] in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. They can be eaten as is to restore half a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} in Link's [[Life Gauge]], but can also be [[Cooking|Cooked]] to restore greater health and provide [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"></ref> When {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom}} are burned by {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}, they become {{Plural|BotW|Toasty Sunshroom|link}}. In addition, {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom}} can be used as an ingredient at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to create Red [[Dye]].

Sunshrooms, among other mushrooms, can be used in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Fragrant Mushroom Sauté]], [[Fruit and Mushroom Mix]], [[Glazed Mushrooms]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], a [[Mushroom Omelet]], [[Mushroom Rice Balls]], [[Mushroom Risotto]], a [[Mushroom Skewer]], [[Copious Mushrooms Skewers]], [[Salt-Grilled Mushrooms]], and [[Steamed Mushrooms]].

At the [[Hateno Ancient Tech Lab]], {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can receive the [[Side Quest]] "[[Sunshroom Sensing]]". First, [[Symin]] will ask him to take a photo of a Sunshroom.<ref>{{Cite|First, go take a picture of a {{Color|BotW Blue|sunshroom}} with the {{Color|BotW Blue|Camera}}. There is one {{Color|BotW Red|behind}} the lab.|Symin|BotW}}</ref> After he does so, Symin will ask Link to bring him three sunshrooms.<ref>{{Cite|This seems like a nice opportunity to ask if you'd mind bringing me {{Color|BotW Red|three sunshrooms}}.|Symin|BotW}}</ref>

Another Side Quest, "[[Riddles of Hyrule]]" involves [[Walton]] telling Link riddles and having him bring the corresponding item. For his third riddle, Walton will ask Link to find a mushroom that warms your bones and can be found in warmer areas.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|This sultry shroom will warm your bones}}. It makes its home in warmer zones.|Walton|BotW}}</ref> If Link places a Sunshroom on the leaf in front of Walton, he will cheer that Link figured out the riddle, before quickly moving on to the next one.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Sunshroom!}} That's it! THAT'S IT! You got it!}}|Walton|BotW}}</ref>

Five Sunshrooms, along with eight {{Plural|BotW|Fire Keese Wing|link}}, are required to upgrade each piece of the [[Snowquill Set]] to its third tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Snowquill Set''': 15 (0, 0, 5, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

The default image still exists in the game's files, however.
