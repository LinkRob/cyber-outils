{{Term/Store|Droolik||Series, TFoE}} is a character in {{TFoE}}.<ref name="Droolik">{{Cite|Not many left, Link. There's Droolik, 'round the side of Glutko.|Horgum|TFoE}}</ref> 

==Biography==
Droolik is a man who is found eating and drinking in a cave in the {{Term|TFoE|Glutko|link}} area. [[Horgum]] mentions that he is one of the few remaining left of [[Koridai]].<ref name="Droolik"/>

Droolik asks [[Link]] where he is heading, to which Link responds that he is off to fight {{Term|TFoE|Glutko (Boss)|link}} the cyclops. Droolik gives him a hint by suggesting that instead of fighting, he feeds him "something spicy," referring that Link throws a [[Bomb]] into Glutko's mouth.<ref>{{Cite|Don't fight him, feed him! Something spicy, know what I mean?|Droolik|TFoE}}</ref>
