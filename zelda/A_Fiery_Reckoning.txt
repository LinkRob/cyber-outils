{{Term/Store|A Fiery Reckoning||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|A Fiery Reckoning|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|A Fiery Reckoning}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}}, with {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo|link}} as its pilots. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Opal|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, and 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

Throughout the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to defeat 4,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within six minutes. {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Talus|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} and many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} fill the area, while more {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} spawn as Vah Rudania advances throughout the map. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 4,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
