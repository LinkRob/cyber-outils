==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Southeast Gate}} is the southeast entrance to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Lashley|link}} is posted as guard to this entrance. The {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} can be found near the {{Term|BotW|Southeast Gate|link}}.
