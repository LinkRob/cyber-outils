'''Lizalfos Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Lizalfos Lv. 2|Nimble and lizard-like, this creature is resistant to fire and able to leap into close combat from great distances.<ref name="EoW Lizalfos Lv. 2">{{Cite|Nimble and lizard-like, this creature is resistant to fire and able to leap into close combat from great distances.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Lizalfos Lv. 2 are stronger, orange-skinned versions of the regular [[Lizalfos]], with increased health and damage. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can use the [[Tri Rod]] to learn their [[Echo]].

The Lizalfos Lv. 2 also comes in a [[Dark Echo|Dark variant]] upon coming in contact with a [[Tecuum]].
