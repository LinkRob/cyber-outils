The {{Term/Store|Laundry Pool||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an area in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Laundry Pool|N/A|MM3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Laundry Pool is a small, closed off area attached to [[South Clock Town]]. A pool of water is located in its center, likely where the residents of Clock Town come to wash their laundry. The [[Curiosity Shop]] in [[West Clock Town]] has a [[Curiosity Shop Back Room|Back Room]] in the Laundry Pool that [[Kafei]] uses as his hideout.<ref>{{Cite|From here, I check on the Curiosity Shop's customers.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> While small, it is an important location in the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest. A bell belonging to the Curiosity Shop can be found here for people with business to ring.<ref>{{Cite|Those with business, please ring bell. No solicitors. -Curiosity Shop.|Sign|MM}}</ref>

A bench is situated against the south wall of the Laundry Pool. On the [[First Day|First]] and [[Second Day|Second Night]]s, [[Guru-Guru]] will sit here, playing his portable phonograph.<ref>{{Cite|La-la-la... They said I was much too loud when I practiced in my room. They got mad. Sigh...Now I'm sad. I'll just stay here all night and away from their pad.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> If [[Anju]] does not entrust [[Link]] with her [[Letter to Kafei]], she will come on the Second Day with the intention to meet him herself, but her courage will falter and she will sit on the bench and cry.<ref>{{Cite|I'm...actually...afraid to meet him... and to hear the reason why he wanted to disappear... It might be because of... me... There are only two days until the carnival... Should I wait? Kafei...|Anju|MM}}</ref>

There are three red [[Rupee]]s in the pool itself, totaling 60 Rupees' worth. However, they can only be reached on the Night of the Second Day. This is due to the Rupees being behind a grating that prevents Link from traveling up the stream's flow beforehand. After the Night of the Second Day, they will be swept farther downstream and behind an opposite grating, again out of reach. A crate next to the bench will always hold a blue Rupee.

A [[Frog]] can be found underneath the tree here. If Link has defeated [[Goht]] and returned spring to [[Snowhead]], he can use [[Don Gero's Mask]] to notify this Frog that Snowhead's unusual cold snap has ended and that the [[Frog Choir]] is meeting again.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Don Gero! It has been so long. What has brought you all this way? Could it be... Has spring finally come to the mountains? That look...It is true! Winter was so long that I began to lose all hope. I understand. I shall head for the mountains immediately.|Frog|MM}}</ref>

This is also the location of Clock Town's lone [[Stray Fairy]] during the Day. At nightfall, the Fairy moves to [[East Clock Town]].

== Trivia ==

* In the Japanese release, the Laundry Pool is known as '''Laundry Place'''.
* If [[Link]] were to enter the Laundry Pool as his [[Fierce Deity Link|Fierce Deity]] form, due to his very tall height and long reach to grab ledges, it is possible for him to go out of bounds. Upon reaching the wall by the entrance of the map, he can jump and climb to the top, allowing easy access to the outer area, but care must be taken not to fall into the wide abyss.
