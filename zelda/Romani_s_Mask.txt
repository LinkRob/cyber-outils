{{Term/Store|Romani's Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a [[Mask]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's {{Color|MM Red|Romani's Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>That is a nice mask. It is a sentimental item that exudes a lot of maturity.</p>}}

Romani's Mask is given to [[Link]] by [[Cremia]]. It is obtained through the [[Helping Romani and Cremia|Romani and Cremia sidequest]]s. Link must visit [[Romani Ranch]] before 6:00 PM on the [[Second Day]], and have completed the sidequest to protect the [[cow]]s from the [[Ghost (Majora's Mask)|Ghost]]s on the [[First Day]]. [[Cremia]] will ask if Link wants to ride with her into town while she makes a delivery.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going by wagon into town. Would you like a ride?|Cremia|MM}}</ref> On the way, they are forced to take a detour through the [[Gorman Track]] because of a fence blocking [[Milk Road]].<ref>{{Cite|What? The road... Boy, get your bow ready... First Milk Road is blocked by a boulder and now... We have to take this detour through ugly country...|Cremia|MM}}</ref> Link must defend her [[milk]] supply from the [[Gorman Brothers]], who wish to destroy it.<ref>{{Cite|Are you ready, boy? I'm going to try to get us through here as fast as I can! If any pursuers come from behind, chase them off with your arrows. They may be after my cargo of milk bottles.|Cremia|MM}}</ref> If at least one [[Bottle]] of milk remains, Cremia will reward Link with the mask.<ref>{{Cite|This isn't very big, but accept my thanks! By doing one good deed, a child becomes an adult. That mask is only given to a limited number of adult customers. It's proof of membership. I now acknowledge you as being an adult!|Cremia|MM}}</ref>

The mask signifies that the one wearing it is a member of the [[Milk Bar]], and an adult.<ref>{{Cite|It seems the girl who smells of the ranch has a mask that only adults have...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's Romani's Mask. Only adult customers can get that mask. But "adults" isn't really the right term. It's not based on age, but on a person's way of thinking and acting... Hee, hee. Somewhere, there's a person who thinks of you as an adult.|Cremia|MM}}</ref> While wearing the mask, the members-only Milk Bar will see that Link is a member, and will allow him to enter.<ref>{{Cite|Ah...You are a member. Please come in.|Mr. Barten|MM}}</ref> This is useful many times, and is necessary to obtain valuable items that require access to the Milk Bar, like the [[Troupe Leader's Mask]], [[Chateau Romani]], and a [[Bottle]].

==Trivia==
*This is one of the few duplicated masks in the game. The other masks with this characteristic are the [[Keaton Mask]], which each of the [[Bombers Secret Society of Justice|Bombers]] claim to have, the [[Couple's Mask]], which is a symbol of marriage, the [[Garo's Mask]], which both [[Gorman Brothers]] own, and [[Kafei's Mask]], which Anju also has.
