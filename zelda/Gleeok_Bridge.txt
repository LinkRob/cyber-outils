{{Term/Store|Gleeok Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Gleeok Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Gleeok Bridge is located in [[Hyrule Field]]. It is a small, wooden bridge that crosses the [[Regencia River]].

In [[Master Mode]], a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Gerudo Scimitar]] can be found atop a platform suspended by [[Sky Octorok]]s north of the Bridge.
