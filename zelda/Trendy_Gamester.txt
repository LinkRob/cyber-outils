The {{Term/Store|Trendy Gamester||Series, LANS}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Trendy Gamester|N/A|LANS}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Trendy Gamester}} was referred to as the {{Term/Store|Trendy Game Shop Owner||LA, LADX}} prior to {{LA|NS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{LA|NS|-}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
The {{Term|LADX|Trendy Game Shop Owner}} runs the {{Term|LADX|Trendy Game|link}} in {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village|link}}. For a fee of 10 {{Plural|LADX|Rupee|link}}, he will let {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} play a single round where he may earn one of the shop's prizes. The {{Term|LADX|Trendy Game Shop Owner}} will also give {{Term|LADX|Link}} a short instruction on how to operate the {{Term|LADX|Trendy Game}}'s {{Term|LADX|Crane|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The A and B Buttons move the crane...The rest is just timing! Go over to the buttons to play! Good Luck!|Trendy Game Shop Owner|LADX}}</ref>

If {{Term|LADX|Link}} brings {{Term|LADX|Marin|link}} to the {{Term|LADX|Trendy Game}}, she will ask to play a round, which {{Term|LADX|Link}} will pay for. However, she grabs the {{Term|LADX|Trendy Game Shop Owner}} with the {{Term|LADX|Crane}} instead. The {{Term|LADX|Trendy Game Shop Owner}} compliments {{Term|LADX|Marin}} on her expertise, but then angrily demands that she and {{Term|LADX|Link}} leave.<ref>{{Cite|You're good! You're a pro, aren't you? ... ... ... ... Well, beat it! Pros aren't allowed in here!|Trendy Game Shop Owner|LADX}}</ref>
