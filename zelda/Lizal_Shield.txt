{{Plural|BotW|Lizal Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} commonly found among {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> The sloppy craftsmanship used to make these {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Shield|display=Shield}} leads to their poor {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are wielded by weak {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}}, though {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}} may also use them.

All {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Shield}} are known to be made of metal.<ref name="Inventory"/> Because of this, they conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and will {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. However, {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Shield}} can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, and they will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
