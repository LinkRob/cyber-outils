The {{Term/Store|Deku Pipes||Series, MM, MM3D}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You have them! You do! Just like the princess... {{Color|MM3D Red|Deku Pipes}}! Those will work great!!!|Monkey|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Green|Deku Pipes}}<br/>Loud pipes that sprout forth from your Deku Scrub body. ==Location and Uses==
The Deku Pipes are obtained alongside the [[Deku Mask]] after [[Link]] learns the "[[Song of Healing]]". It replaces the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} when transformed into a [[Deku Link|Deku]]. Link must play them to learn the "[[Sonata of Awakening]]" from the [[Monkey]]. By playing the song on the Deku Pipes in [[Woodfall]], Link can reveal [[Woodfall Temple]].

==Trivia==
*The Deku Pipes existed as an independent [[C-Button]] item in the original {{MM|-}}. Their icon was coded into the game but was not implemented in the final release.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= darkeye14|published= August 25, 2010|retrieved= |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KYdj1DiCgug|title= Japanese Majora's Mask: Exclusive Beta Items|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref> It can be seen in certain [[Unused Content|pre-release]] screenshots. The icon was later used in {{MM3D}}.
