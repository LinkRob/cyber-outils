The '''Tri Rod''' is an [[Items in Echoes of Wisdom|item]] gifted by [[Tri]] in [[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]].

==Location and Uses==
{{hatnote|”Tri loaned you this mystical rod. With its power, you can echo things you've learned.” — [[Key Items in Echoes of Wisdom|Key Items]]<ref name="EoW Tri Rod">{{Cite|Tri loaned you this mystical rod. With its power, you can echo things you've learned.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
After [[Princess Zelda]] is imprisoned by the [[Dark Echo|impostor]] king, the spirit Tri visits her cell and gifts her the Tri Rod, a mystical [[Rod]] capable of creating [[Echo|Echoes]] of objects and creatures to aid her in her escape. Throughout her journey through the process of mending [[Rift|rifts]], Tri gains the power of allowing Zelda to create more Echoes without losing already existing ones.
