{{Plural|BotW|Stone Smasher}} are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} made by the {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Goron Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created and used by the {{Plural|BotW|Goron}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 171}}</ref> Their center of gravity is at their tip, so it uses centrifugal force to deal damage.<ref name="Inventory"/> A {{Term|BotW|Stone Smasher}} has the attack damage of 42. Like other hammer-type {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, the {{Term|BotW|Stone Smasher|display=Smashers}} can break {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}} with one hit. Mining ores in this manner was the {{Plural|BotW|Goron}}' intended purpose when crafting them, as the name suggests. Because {{Plural|BotW|Goron}} are much stronger and larger than {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}, they can wield these {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} one-handed. {{Term|BotW|Link|Link}}, however, needs both hands to use one.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Stone Smasher}} have a hidden property which causes their damage output to be doubled when used on the {{Term|BotW|Ore Deposit}} atop a {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus|link}}. Similarly, they are capable of immediately destroying {{Plural|BotW|Stone Pebblit|link}}.

Though they are commonly found around {{Term|BotW|Goron City}} in {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}, two {{Plural|BotW|Stone Smasher}} can be found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. One of them is wielded by a {{Term|BotW|Black Moblin|Link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall|link}}, and the other is in a hidden chamber connected to the {{Term|BotW|Library|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Stone Smasher}} can also be found when a {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Stone Smasher}} are made from metal, and they will therefore conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will also sink if dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up again using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. Finally, they will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
