{{Term/Store|Steem||Series, ST}} is a character from {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 288}}</ref> He is a member of the [[Lokomo]] race and Guardian of the [[Snow Realm]] who resides in the [[Snow Sanctuary]].<ref name=steem/> Like the rest of the Lokomo people, Steem possesses a sacred instrument, his being the [[Wikipedia:Biwa|biwa]], which generates energy to power the [[Spirit Tracks (Object)|Spirit Tracks]] when played with [[Spirit Flute|another sacred instrument]].<ref>{{Cite|Indeed. We are called the Lokomos. We are servants of the spirits. They have entrusted us with protecting the temples and the Spirit Tracks. By playing our sacred instruments... We generate energy that powers the Spirit Tracks.|Anjean|ST}}</ref>

==Biography==
In order for [[Link]] to access the chamber of the sanctuary in which Steem is located, he must press a switch that opens the way to Steem. However, the young hero must reach the door before it closes without being "seen" by the head statues scattered across the room that are facing in different directions.<ref>{{Cite|When the door opens, head to the far room without being seen... And do it before the time runs out...|Sign|ST}}</ref> Once the young hero successfully gets past the door, he will meet Steem, who will mention that he has heard all about Link and Zelda's journey to restore the Spirit Tracks.<ref>{{Cite|Hoo hoo hoo! I've heard all about you two! You want to restore the Spirit Tracks, correct?|Steem|ST}}</ref> Link, using the [[Spirit Flute]], then performs a [[Lokomo Song#Steem Duet|duet]] with Steem to reveal the Spirit Tracks to the [[Snow Temple]].<ref>{{Cite|I haven't been that nervous about performing in I don't know how long... But now the Spirit Tracks to the Snow Temple have been restored.|Steem|ST}}</ref> Before the young hero heads out to the temple, Steem warns him about the violent blizzards that have been savaging the area around the temple.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, but there is one thing I should warn you of. Ferocious blizzards have been savaging the area around the temple. Be careful on your journey there!|Steem|ST}}</ref>

After Link obtains a freight car for his [[Spirit Train]], Link can bring a [[vessel]] to Steem from the [[Wise One]] in [[Papuchia Village]]. The Lokomo, who had been wanting a vessel to decorate his sanctuary, will give a [[Force Gem#Spirit Tracks|Force Gem]] to the young hero as a sign of gratitude.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, hello. I putt-putted over to meet you when I heard the train! I'm looking to redecorate my sanctuary, and I need a nice vessel. Can you bring me one?|Steem|ST}}</ref>

During the final battle against [[Malladus]], [[Princess Zelda]] sings a duet with Link, and soon after this, all five of the Lokomos, including Steem, join in to form a full orchestrated version of the piece. This melody weakens Malladus and reveals a weak spot in his back, which is necessary to defeat the Demon King once and for all.<ref>{{Cite|Let's combine my sacred power with the power of the Spirit Flute! I think we can use them to weaken Malladus!|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref>

Steem is last seen during the ending credits when [[Anjean]], along with the rest of the Lokomos, departs towards the heavens after the defeat of the Demon King since their protection is no longer needed in the land of [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|But our protection is no longer needed. Even without the spirits' guidance, you will do well. So I think I will return to the heavens... In the company of my old friend Byrne.|Anjean|ST}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*His name is a pun on the word "steam".
*[[Link]] can break Steem's vessel by slicing it with his sword. If he does that, Steem will yell at him and [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] will expect him to get another. Link does not have to, but if he does, he will get a [[treasure]]. Also, the vessel's price will change.
