{{Term/Store|Through the Storm||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Through the Storm|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Through the Storm}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, with {{Term|HWAoC|Revali|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Teba|link}} as its pilots. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Sapphire|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Ruby|link}}, and 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

Throughout the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to defeat 3,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. These {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} include {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Hinox|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Wizzrobe|link}} of each element, {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|White-Maned Lynel|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}}. More of these {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} appear in two waves as the {{Plural|HWAoC|Challenge}} progresses. {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon|link}} appears twice as well, and retreats the first time after losing half of his health. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 3,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
