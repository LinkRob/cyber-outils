{{Term/Store|Rioma||Series, TP (Himekawa)}} is a character in the {{TP|Himekawa}}.{{Name Ref}}

==Biography==

[[File:Boys sparring.png|500px]]

Two years prior to the [[manga]]'s events,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Two years ago...|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 139}}</ref> Rioma lived in a city that guarded the border of a [[Gerudo Desert|desert]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= My hometown was close to the desert-A city that guarded the border.|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 139}}</ref> He and the others boys within the city would practice sword fighting to defend [[Hyrule]]'s borders when they grew up.<ref name="Boy"/>

Rioma is first seen dueling his friend [[Link]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You're shying away!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 139}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Throw yourself into it!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 139}}</ref> [[Zeu]] approaches the boys and suggests stopping for the day.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Let's stop for the day!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 140}}</ref> Rioma complains that fighting Link is difficult because he is left handed,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= It's hard to fight Link! He's left handed!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 140}}</ref> with Link replying he would do the same right handed against him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I'd do the same right handed against you, Rioma!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 140}}</ref> [[Darpa]] confronts Link and asks if he can really defend Hyrule with just a sword and skill against the [[Ganon|demon thief]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You say you'll drive them all away?! One of them is a demon thief! Can you really defend Hyrule with just a sword? Don't get cocky just 'cause you've got some skill!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 140}}</ref> Rioma questions what Darpa plans to do.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= What about you then, Darpa?|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 140}}</ref> Darpa reveals his plans to acquire a sword stronger than magic, the [[Gaurof Sword]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I have a plan. I'm gonna get a sword stronger than magic... The Secret Sword!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 140, 141}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Secret Sword? You don't mean the Gaurof Sword, do you?|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 141}}</ref>. When he leads Rioma, Link, and Zeu to where the Gaurof Sword is kept, Rioma expresses his uncertainty, as they have been told not to go near it by parents and teachers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Let's not do this, Darpa. We've been told not to go near that since we were kids. <small>By parents and teachers...</small>|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 141}}</ref> Darpa snaps at him and says to go back if he is scared of getting in trouble.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= If you're scared of getting in trouble, go back!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 141}}</ref> While Darpa unsuccessfully tries to pull the sword,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Umph! Umph! Urgh! I can't pull it out!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 142}}</ref> Rioma tells him to give it up.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Give it up!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 142}}</ref> Rioma watches as Link tries to pull out the sword next. He succeeds, but the stone that the sword had been stuck in sucks Rioma, the city, and the other boys inside, leaving Link as the sole survivor.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... Everyone...Got sucked into the darkness. Why did I alone survive? I don't know!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 149}}</ref> 

[[Wolf Link]] has a flashback in the manga's second volume where Rioma is shown with Darpa and Zeu.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= "In the future, we'll defend Hyrule's borders!"|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 79}}</ref> He feels ashamed that they were unable to protect [[Princess Zelda]] and laments his own fate as a beast.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Back home...I went through tough training so I'd be able to fight for my country in a crisis... We all...Believed that. But now the threat is real. I was supposed to protect her! But our home has disappeared... Our very lives have disappeared...And I stand before Princess Zelda not as a person...But as a beast!|book= {{TP|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 79}}</ref>
