The {{Term/Store|Down Thrust|Down Thrusts|Series, TMC, CoH}},{{Exp Game|TMC, CoH}}<ref>{{Cite|Those thorny little horn monsters! Your sword doesn't hurt 'em at all! But if you bump 'em with your {{Color|TMC Red|shield}} up, they'll totally flip! There are other ways, though. Flip 'em with a {{Color|TMC Red|bomb}}! Or your {{Color|TMC Red|Cane of Pacci}}! Or with a well-placed {{Color|TMC Red|Down Thrust}}!|Swordsman Newsletter 4|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|CoH Yellow|Down Thrust}} ability! You don't need to assign it to a button. If you drop down onto an enemy, you can hop to another space or on top of another enemy!|N/A|CoH}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Downthrust|Downthrusts|TAoL}},{{Exp Game|TAoL}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 108 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref> and {{Term/Store|Down Thrust Attack|Down Thrust Attacks|FSA}},{{Exp Game|FSA}}<ref>{{Cite Manual|game= FSA |page= 28}}</ref> is a recurring ability in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Down Thrust}} was referred to as the '''Downward Thrust''' in {{A&A}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 386 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It is performed by [[Link]] by jumping into the air, positioning his [[Sword]] underneath him pointing downwards, and landing on enemies sword-first to stab them from above. This technique made its debut in {{TAoL}} and has since become one of the hero's signature special moves, though it isn't as common as the [[Spin Attack]] or [[Jump Slash|Jump Attack]].

The [[Jump Thrust]] technique introduced in the same game is the polar opposite, as Link thrusts his sword straight up as he leaps, instead.

==Overview==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
[[File:TAoL Link Downward Thrust Sprite.png|left]]
In {{TAoL|-}}, the Down Thurst is taught to Link by a [[Knights of Hyrule|Hyrulean Knight]] living in the [[Harbor Town of Mido]]. However, since the main door is locked from the inside, Link must use the [[Spells of The Adventure of Link#Jump Spell|Jump Spell]] to reach the second story of the house and thus enter through its open door. The knight at the end of the basement will teach Link the new skill upon talking to him.<ref>{{Cite|WHEN YOU JUMP PRESS DOWNWARD TO STAB.|Hylian Knight|TAoL}}</ref> This technique proves to be useful when fighting enemies such as [[Daira]], {{Plural|TAoL|Fokka|link}}, [[Moa]]s, or enemies that are too small to attack normally, such as [[Myu]]. When used with the [[Power Glove]], the Down Thrust allows Link to crush rocks inside the many [[Temple]]s of [[Hyrule]].

==={{FS|-}}===
In {{FS}}, the [[Roc's Cape]] is required to use the Down Thrust, as it is they only way for the [[Links]] to jump. 
 
==={{FSA|-}}===
[[File:FSA DownThrust.png|left]]
In {{FSA}}, while the Links are jumping with the [[Roc's Feather]] or falling from ledges, they can perform this technique by pressing down on the Control Stick and the attack button at the same time. Upon landing, the attack will send out shockwaves, although they will not cause any damage to the enemies nearby. Like in {{TAoL|-}}, the Down Thrust can be used to break [[Rock]]s.

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, the Down Thrust is a [[Tiger Scroll]] technique learned from [[Swiftblade]]. It also requires the use of the Roc's Cape. 

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, Link can perform the Down Thrust while using the [[Paraglider]]. When Link lands on the ground, he will send out a shockwave that will damage nearby enemies and knock them of their feet, disarming them. If Link lands on an enemy first, he will damage and disarm them before landing on the ground and creating the shockwave.

==Crossover appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSBS|-}}===
In the {{SSBS}}, Link, [[Young Link]], and [[Toon Link]] can perform the attack as their down aerial attack. This is considered to be one of Link's most powerful moves, as it can deal up to 22% of damage and can be used as a {{ssb|meteor smash}}. However, if Link lands while the move is still in action, he will thrust the sword into the ground and will have to pull it out, leaving him open to other attacks. Also, when using it as a meteor smash near the edge of the stage, Link can self-destruct if he misses his target. In {{SSBB}}, Toon Link's Down Thrust stalls momentarily when it begins, but then it launches him downward extremely quickly.

==={{SCII|-}}===
[[File:SCII Link Using Down Thrust.png|thumb|right|150px|Link performing the Sword Plant in {{SCII|-}}]]
:''How to perform: {{SC2|d}}{{SC2|va}}''
The Down Thrust is one of [[SoulCalibur II/Command List|Link's Commands]] in {{SCII}}, although it goes by the name of "Sword Plant." It is considered to be a mid-attack, dealing up to 40 HP worth of damage. The Sword Plant can be used to attack from a distance, since Link jumps towards the enemy as he begins to perform this technique. Due to its powerful impact, the Sword Plant knocks enemies down to the floor if hit; moreover, if Link hits the enemy on the first strike, he can damage the opponent with this attack up to two more times by continually pressing the vertical attack button. However, like in {{SSB|-}}, if the [[Hero of Time]] misses, he will be left open to attacks while he pulls the Sword from the ground. He can also accidentally Ring Out if he performs the Down Thrust at his opponent and blocks as he continually attempts to land hits at the edge of the arena.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The [[Ending Blow]] taught by the [[Hero's Spirit]] in {{TP}} works very similarly to the Down Thrust, but is only used on enemies that are knocked down, including [[Ganondorf]] (which was used for the final blow). This also applies to the [[Fatal Blow]] and [[Parry Attack]] for the finishing blows of [[Demise]] and Ganondorf, respectively, in {{SS}} and {{TWW}}.
*In {{ALttP}}, Link performs a similar move when using the [[Quake Medallion]].
**Ironically, in the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|GBA remake]], the four [[Dark Link|Dark Links]] during the final challenge in the [[Palace of the Four Sword]] are able to perform a Down Thrust using the Quake Medallion animation, which can prove difficult to dodge.
