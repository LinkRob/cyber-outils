The {{Term/Store|Cursed Rich Man||Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239 {{Exp Game|OoT}} & 244 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> He was cursed by various means into a [[Skulltula]] because of his greed.<ref name=greed>{{Cite|Folks around here tell of a fabulously rich family that once lived in one of the houses in this village... But they say that the entire family was cursed due to their greed! Who knows what might happen to those who are consumed by greed...|Professor Shikashi|OoT}}</ref><ref name=dog>{{Cite|Rrr-ruff! Stupid master... His eyes are always filled with greed and desire...|Dog|MM}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Cursed Rich Man}} and his five sons, who are also cursed, reside within the {{Term|OoT3D|House of Skulltula|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}}. They were once excessively affluent, but they fell to a curse because of their greed and were forced to assume the form of {{Plural|OoT3D|Skulltula}}.<ref name=greed /> Upon {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}}'s visit, the father explains that the only way their curse will be lifted is if all of the {{Plural|OoT3D|Gold Skulltula|link}} around {{Term|OoT3D|Hyrule|link}} are destroyed and their [[Gold Skulltula Token|Token]]s are collected.<ref>{{Cite|We all look like this because of the spider's curse. If every Spider of the Curse in the entire world were destroyed, the curse would be broken.|Cursed Rich Man|OoT}}</ref> 

The man persuades {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} to help them by promising the young hero great riches if the father and his sons are all freed from their curse.<ref>{{Cite|If you break the curse on my family, we will make you very rich...|Cursed Rich Man|OoT}}</ref> {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} must seek out and destroy the 100 {{Plural|OoT3D|Gold Skulltula}} to restore the family back to its former state. {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} earns their grateful rewards each time he destroys the {{Plural|OoT3D|Gold Skulltula}} in intervals of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 100.<ref>{{Cite|The curse has been broken! Thank you! Here's a reward for you!|Hylian|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the Cursed Rich Man was cursed by the [[Swamp Spider House]].<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Help me! I am not a monster! The spider's curse... It made me this way...|Cursed Rich Man|MM}}</ref> According to him, someone told him that he could become rich by visiting this House and gave him the [[Mask of Truth]]. The person said that the instructions to become rich were written somewhere in the Spider House, but as the man went in to go look, he befell a curse and turned into a Skulltula-like creature.<ref>{{Cite|You see, a while back, someone told me I could become rich and he gave me this mask. He said the instructions were inscribed somewhere in here, but when I went to look, I was cursed!|Cursed Rich Man|MM}}</ref> The only method to lift the curse is for the 30 Gold Skulltulas in the house to be killed and their Spirits harvested as proof of their defeat.<ref>{{Cite|If you lift the curse...I'll teach you...something good...Hurry...Please...This is awful... In here...The gold ones...The cursed spiders...Defeat them all...Make me normal...again...|Cursed Rich Man|MM}}</ref>

After all 30 of the Gold Skulltulas are defeated and the curse is lifted, the Cursed Rich Man will give Link the Mask of Truth.<ref>{{Cite|If I had known it would be such an ordeal, I never would have taken it. I'm getting rid of it!|Cursed Rich Man|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, defeating all 30 of the Gold Skulltulas a second time will earn Link a [[Fishing Hole Pass]], and the Cursed Rich Man states that he will get rid of the Mask of Truth.

==Trivia==
*In {{OoT|-}}, the family's fame on account of their wealth brought word of them as far as [[Zora's Domain]].<ref>{{Cite|You say you want to buy a Zora Tunic? That tunic is made of the finest material, and is therefore exceedingly expensive! In fact, I'm afraid only that very rich family in Kakariko Village can afford this fine garment...|Zora|OoT}}</ref>
*It is possible to strike any of the cursed family members with a sword strike in their back, they will cry in pain and be stunned for a moment. When they are no longer stunned they will spin around once, which may hurt Link, and if Link approaches them again they will attack him one time. After that they will become peaceful again. Leaving the house and then reentering also returns any of them back to their normal behavior.
*In {{MM|-}}, the Cursed Rich Man's [[Dog]] is also in the Spider House, and on the [[First Day]], he says his master's eyes are always filled with greed.<ref name=dog/> On the [[Second Day|Second]] and [[Third Day]]s, however, his speech is different; his thoughts indicate that he used to be a racing dog at the [[Doggy Racetrack]] at [[Romani Ranch]].<ref>{{Cite|Rrr-ruff! I've had it with this foolish master... What's a racing dog like me doing in a place like this? Oh, how I miss the ranch...|Dog|MM}}</ref>
*In {{MM|-}}, the man seeking for shelter at the [[Oceanside Spider House]] resembles one of the children of the Cursed Rich Man from {{OoT|-}}.
*Similar to the rich family cursed by the Skulltula, in {{TP}}, Link must save a man named [[Jovani]], who has sold his soul to the [[Poe]]s for untold riches, by obtaining 60 [[Poe Soul]]s.
