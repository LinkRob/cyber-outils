'''Mothula Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Mothula Lv. 2|This powerful Mothula lives in the wetlands. Attacks won't stop this flying juggernaut while it is blasting forward.<ref name="Mothula Lv. 2">{{Cite|This powerful Mothula lives in the wetlands. Mothula Lv. 2 is a tougher variant of the standard Mothula, with higher health and damage. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], and summoning one costs five Echo energy.
