==Biography==
{{Term|CoH|Error Houlihan}} lives alone in a house outside the boundaries of the map.{{Note|{{Term|CoH|Error Houlihan}}'s house is only accessible by going out of the bounds of the {{Term|CoH|Overworld|link}} map. Due to the way the game generates the Overworld, not every file is be able to access the house.}} When talked to by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}, he simply introduces himself.<ref>{{Cite|I AM ERROR.|Error Houlihan|CoH}}</ref> Inside his house, he sells {{Term|CoH|Boots of Speed|link}} for 500 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
His name is a reference to {{Term|TAoL|Error|link}}, a character from {{TAoL}}, and {{Term|ALttP|Chris Houlihan|link}}, a character from {{ALttP}} who is mentioned in a similar secret room.
