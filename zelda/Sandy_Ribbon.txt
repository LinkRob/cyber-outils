{{Term/Store|Sandy Ribbon|Sandy Ribbons|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 137"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Sandy Ribbon}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Dunes|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Sandy Ribbon}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum|link}}''', ''"Avoid falling and quicksand!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Sandy Ribbon}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Desert Temple|link}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Sandy Ribbon}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Desert Temple}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Sandy Ribbon}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Sandy Ribbon}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Gust Garb|link}}.<ref name="E 137"/>
