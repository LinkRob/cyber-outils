"'''Title Theme - The Legend of Zelda'''",{{Exp Game|SSBU}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|SSBU Music|Title Theme - The Legend of Zelda}}<br/>The Legend of Zelda|Music|SSBU}}</ref> also known as "'''Title (The Legend of Zelda)'''",{{Exp Game|SSBB, SSBfWU}}{{Name Ref|SSBB, SSBfWU}} is a recurring [[Song]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Overview==
"Title (The Legend of Zelda)" is an arrangement of the music that plays in the title screen in the original ''[[The Legend of Zelda (Game)|The Legend of Zelda]]''.

==={{SSBB|-}}===
"Title (The Legend of Zelda)" is a default song that plays on the {{Term|SSBB|Bridge of Eldin|link}} {{Term|SSBB|Stage|link}} in {{SSBB|-}}.

==={{SSB4|Wii U|-}}===
"Title (The Legend of Zelda)" reappears in {{SSB4|WiiU|-}}. It is a default Song for the {{Term|SSBfWU|Bridge of Eldin|link}} {{Term|SSBfWU|Stage}}.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
"Title Theme - The Legend of Zelda" reappears in {{SSBU}}, again as a direct copy of its initial appearance. It is a default Song for any {{Term|SSBU|Stage}} related to {{TLoZ|Series}}.
