The '''Librarian''' is a character featured in the {{ALttP|Ishinomori}}. He lives in {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Kakariko Village|link}} and is in charge of the nearby {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Library|link}}.

==Biography==
When {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link|link}} returns to {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Kakariko Village|link}} to find clues about the remaining two [[Pendants of Virtue]],<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Leaving Sahasrahla, Link returns to Kakariko Village to look for clues to the location of the remaining two pendants...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> he finds the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Library|link}} has been set on fire by soldiers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The House of Books is ablaze! The soldiers must have laid torch to it!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} learns that the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Librarian}}, who {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} must speak to according to {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Sahasrahla|link}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He's inside that inferno?! Sahasrahla said I must speak with him!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> is trapped inside the flames.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Mister, you gotta help! The librarian is trapped inside!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref> The young hero pours a bucket of water on him and barges in to successfully save the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Librarian}}. Inside the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Library}}, the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Librarian}} theorizes that the soldiers might have been after the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Book of Mudora|link}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Perhaps their target was the book of Mudora...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> and explains that it is the key to the ancient {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Hylian Language|link}} which mentions that the hero will stand in the desert holding said book.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=We   managed to save it from the flames... This book is the key to the   ancient Hylian language. According to the legends, the hero will stand   in the desert with this book...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=7}}</ref> {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} takes the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Book of Mudora}} and mentions how {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Sahasrahla}} had told him the Pendant of Power was in desert ruins,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Let me see that! After Sahasrahla gave me the Pendant of Courage, he told me the Pendant of Power was in a desert ruin!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=7}}</ref> leading the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Librarian}} to claim {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} as the legendary Hero of {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Hyrule|link}} and calmly leaving the book in the young hero's hands.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Then you...'''you''' must be the legendary Hero of Hyrule! This is a very fortunate turn of events! I will leave the book in your capable hands.|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=7}}</ref>

Later on in the comic, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} learns from the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Librarian}} and the rest of his friends that all approaches to {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Hyrule Castle|link}} are blocked by {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Agahnim|link}}'s soldiers. <ref>{{Cite Book|quote='''Link''': So all the approaches to the castle are '''blocked''' by Agahnim's sentries?<br>'''Boy:''' Yep! Even '''approaching''' the castle means certain death!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=5 }}</ref> The {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Librarian}} then comes up with the idea of making a hot air balloon to carry him into the castle, and he, {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Sahasrahla's Wife|link}}, and {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Sahasrahla's Grandson|link}} begin to build it using the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Librarian}}'s plan.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=That gives me an '''idea'''! I believe that a large bubble...filled with hot hair...will float! But to make such a thing...in '''one day'''...will be...hard work!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page= 5,6}}</ref> The {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Librarian}} is then seen once more when {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}} uses the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Comfork|link}} in the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Dark World|link}} to communicate with {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Sahasrahla}} and the rest of his friends, where the {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Librarian}} comments he is glad to hear that {{Term|ALttP (Ishinomori)|Link}}is safe.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link? I'm glad you're safe!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=7}}</ref>
