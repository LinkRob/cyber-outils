{{Term/Store|Hollo's Forest Potion Shop||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a location in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW |publisher= Prima |page= 154}}</ref> It is located inside a cave within the main area of [[Forest Haven]]. [[Hollo]] heals the Koroks using the ingredients found in the forest<ref>{{Cite|I am Hollo. I make potions from the fruits of the forest.|Hollo|TWW}}</ref> and runs the shop; he is one of two Koroks to remain in Forest Haven after the Korok Ceremony, the other being [[Makar]]. If Link gives him four [[Boko Baba Seed|Boko Baba Seeds]], then Hollo will concoct a [[Blue Potion]] from the spoils.<ref>{{Cite|All right, I'm going to use four of your seeds to create a super-replenishing potion.|Hollo|TWW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* During the cutscene in which Hollo creates the potion, he cries "Onjalla, manjalla, CHOWAY!", which could be a specific spell he uses to combine the ingredients or unleash the magic contained in the seeds.
* There is a poster at the back of Hollo's shop which reads (clockwise from the top left) "Bokobaba Potion," "Bokobaba Seed," "Defeat Bokobaba," "Baba Bud" and "Bokobaba."<ref>{{Cite Web|url=http://www.zeldauniverse.net/forums/4941838-post4.html|title=Source|site=Zelda Universe Forums}}</ref> They appear to be instructions on how to retrieve the Boko Baba Seeds, as they are accompanied by a diagram of the plant splitting into both a [[Baba Bud]] and a Boko Baba Seed.
*Despite Hollo claiming not to have Boko Baba Seeds, there appears to be several of them hanging from the walls throughout the shop. They may simply be decorative likenesses, however.
