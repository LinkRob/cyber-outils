The '''Construct Bow''' is a bow used by [[Soldier Construct]]s. It is most commonly used by the weakest and most common Soldier I variety, but other varieties can use it sometimes as well. It has 5 attack power and one of the most common bows in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''. It is made from the same metal-like material as many other Zonai creations, likely [[Zonaite]], and as such is not flammable.
