{{Term/Store|My Fairy||Series, HWL, HWDE}} is a game mode in {{HW|L}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|My Fairy}}|N/A|HWL}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|My Fairy}}|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> In it, players can befriend '''Companion Fairies''',<ref>{{Cite|Companion Fairies are partners who use powerful magic and skills to help you on your adventure.|Tutorial|HWL}}</ref> which serve as partners for Warriors. To befriend a Companion Fairy, Warriors must find the Fairy hidden in a [[Jar]] in Adventure Mode.<ref>{{Cite|You will meet all kinds of fairies during Adventure Mode battles. These fairies are hidden in various jars in enemy keeps during these battles. If you find fairies in jars, they will become your companions. Gather lots of fairies, and work with them to overcome challenges on your adventures!|Tutorial|HWL}}</ref> Once found, Companion Fairies can be taken into battles and scenarios in Adventure Mode and [[Legend Mode]]. Multiple Fairies can be befriended, but only one per warrior can be taken into a battle at a time. Using [[Fairy Magic]],<ref>{{Cite|Bringing a companion fairy into battle allows you to use Fairy Magic. The effects of the magic will become more powerful as the fairy's level increases.|Tutorial|HWL}}</ref> they are able to conjure powerful attacks. Fairies can be raised and customized in a number of ways. They will level up over time, allowing them to learn new skills and attributes for use in battle. Leveling them up also increases their magic, allowing them to accompany Warriors into harder battles.

==Overview==
===Dining Room===
[[File:HWL Dining Room Icon.png|right]]
Fairies can be fed special food found in Adventure Mode, which can grant or increase certain skills or statistics. There are three kinds of Foods: Bronze, Silver, and Gold varieties. After receiving some Golden Food in a battle, it becomes possible for enemies to randomly drop it. Fairies can be freely raised in any way the player wishes, allowing them to be raised as balanced battlers, or as specialists in a particular stat or skill. 

===Personality Traits===
10 personality traits exist, but each Fairy can only keep up to five. Each time a Fairy is revived (in which it reverts back to level 1 after reaching level 99), it becomes possible to change its name, as well as one personality trait. This is the only manner of maxing out every trait.

These 10 traits are:
*Dizzy
*Dreamy{{Exp Game|HWL}}/Aspiring{{Exp Game|HWDE}}
*Eager
*Flashy{{Exp Game|HWL}}/Valiant{{Exp Game|HWDE}}
*Fleet{{Exp Game|HWL}}/Shrewd{{Exp Game|HWDE}}
*Friendly
*Resolute
*Smiley
*Soft{{Exp Game|HWL}}/Relaxed{{Exp Game|HWDE}}
*Sparkly

Each food gives bonuses and a stat decrease: for example, a Delicious Odd Mushroom gives 10 points in Valiant, but decreases Dizzyness by 5 points. 

*'''Bronze Food''' gives +2 in three traits, -1 in one trait, and nothing to the remaining six.
*'''Silver Food''' gives +5 in two traits, +2 in two, -2 in one, and is neutral for the remaining five.
*'''Gold Food''' gives +10 in one trait, +5 in two, +2 in two, but also -2 in two, and -5 in another. Neutral for the remaining two.

===Food & Fairy type===
Each Food has a type. If a Food and a Fairy share the same type, stat bonuses are increased and decreased stats are reduced by less:

*+10 becomes +12
*+5 becomes +6
*+2 becomes +3
*-1 becomes +2
*-2 becomes +1
*-5 becomes -2

A Fairy cannot lose any points in her personality traits if she eats food of the same type, except if this food is of the golden variety, and gives -5 in one of the trait she is currently carrying.

Like Materials, Foods come in three rarities: Bronze, Silver, and Gold. Unfortunately, in order to for these foods to begin dropping from enemies, the appropriate food and rarity must first be unlocked from Adventure Mode. Captains will only drop Bronze and Silver Food, while playable characters and bosses drop Silver or Gold Foods. The difficulty setting also increases the chances of obtaining better quality food. Additionally, each captain, character, and boss has a specific set of drops.

===School===
[[File:HWL School Icon.png|right]]
In the '''School''', Companion Fairies can change the [[Rental Skill]] they are currently using.<ref>{{Cite|In the {{Color|HW Green|School}}, you can change your companion fairies' Rental Skills.|Tutorial|HWL}}</ref> Some Companion Fairies will have learned one Rental Skill when they are befriended but others will be unable to use a Rental Skill until they learn one. Companion Fairies can also view what personality traits they require to learn particular Rental Skills.<ref>{{Cite|Fairies can learn different Rental Skills depending on which of the five of the different fairy personality traits they have. You can check the personality traits and their current values on the radar chart.|Tutorial|HWL}}</ref> If the Fairy has the correct personality traits, they can see how much the trait's value must be increased to learn the skill but if the Fairy lacks a personality trait, the trait will be displayed in red and show what value the trait will need to be raised to if the Fairy later gains that trait after being refreshed.<ref>{{Cite|Any personality traits that the companion fairy doesn't have will be displayed in red. You can change one of the fairy's personality traits each time you refresh her.|Tutorial|HWL}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*If you obtain another fairy while carrying the maximum amount you can hold, you will be asked to release a fairy. If you release a fairy that is at least level 25, it will release a silver gratitude crystal (+5 to every trait), and if you release a fairy that is at least level 50 or has been refreshed, it will then release a gold gratitude crystal (+10 to every trait). The element of the gratitude crystal will correspond to the element of the fairy who was released, meaning a released water fairy will give a water gratitude crystal.
*On occasion after unknown triggers, fairies can give gifts which can range from materials to weapons.
*Sometimes, fairies will give requests for food, giving the correct category of food will give +2 trust, and giving the exact type of food will give +5 trust.
**'''Fire Fairies''' will say "''I could probably push myself to work a little harder, if only I had something warm to drink!''", requesting a drink category food, specifically Pumpkin Soup, Elixir Soup, or Hot Spring Water. They will also say "''I want something that's been freshly picked.''" requesting the plant-like category of food, specifically Stamina Fruit or Ember Seeds.
**'''Water Fairies''' will say "''My throat is parched...''", requesting the drink category of food, specifically Sacred Water or Bottled Water.
***They can also say "''Some juicy fruits or vegetables, please!''" requesting the plant-like category of food, specifically Magic Beans or Water Fruit. The water fairy Becka will say "''I'm in the mood for something that's tasty yet not quite ripe...''" requesting plant-like category food, specifically Magic Beans, Water Fruit, or Gale Seeds. The water fairy Becka may also ask "''Is it weird that I'm in the mood for something that has whiskers…? Or should I say it's fishy?''" requesting fish-like category food, specifically Greengill, Ordon Catfish, Hyrule Bass, Hylian Pike, Reekfish, or Hylian Loach.
**'''Lightning Fairies''' may say "''You know what I’d love to eat? Any plants that might help me fly farther!''" requesting plant-like category food, specifically requesting Hyoi Pear. Or Lightning Fairies may say "''Let's stir things up a bit, hm? I'd like to try something different today!''", requesting the weird-like category of food, specifically Mushroom Spores, Chu Jelly, or Bee Larvae.
**'''Light Fairies''' may say "''I need to eat something that’ll really fill me up.''", requesting feast-like category of food, specifically Weird Egg, or All-Purpose Bait. Or Light Fairies may say "''All this effort has really parched my throat!''" requesting the drink category of food, specifically Lon Lon Milk, or Great Fairy's Tears. The light fairy Neris may say "''(I hope I get to eat some sparkling fruit soon...) Ah, not to sound picky or anything!''" requesting plant-like category of food, specifically Life Tree Fruit, Light Fruit, Mystery Seeds, or Pumpkin.
**'''Darkness Fairies''' may say "''I want to take some food with mysterious powers and then put it in a salad and eat it.''", requesting plant-like category of food, specifically Odd Mushroom or Scent Seeds. A darkness fairy may also say "''Have you caught any nice fish lately? You know the kind I like!''", requesting the weird-like category of food, specifically Skullfish or Bombfish. The darkness fairy Loyla may say "''Give me something filling that will prepare me for battle.''", requesting feast-like category of food, specifically Ordon Goat Cheese. Or the darkness fairy Loyla might say "''I actually get a lot of power from foods humans can't eat.''", requesting weird-like category of food, specifically Skullfish or Bombfish.
