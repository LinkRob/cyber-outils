==Location and Uses==
Keese Trophies are obtained as post-battle rewards after finishing any given Chapter's [[Scenario]] battle or a [[Challenge]], with a Trophy earned for every [[Keese]] kill (be they regular, [[Fire Keese|Fire]], [[Ice Keese|Ice]], and [[Electric Keese|Electric]]) a Warrior obtains.

Keese Trophies can be used to either complete [[Side Quest#Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|Quest]]s or be used in the Hyrule Blacksmith Guild workshop to de rust Rusted Weapons or remove Seals on a weapon.
