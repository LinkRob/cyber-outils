{{Plural|BotW|Frostspear}} are magical {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}} forged with ancient {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are found not only among those {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|display=Mountains}}, but in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} as well, particularly around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. Due to their elemental nature, they belong to the {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> When their blades glows blue, any attacks will freeze {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> These attacks will immediately defeat {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}-related {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}}. Equipping a {{Term|BotW|Frostspear}} in a in high levels of {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}} will also grant {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Frostspear}} are appear in the following locations:
* North of {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest|link}}, at a {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} base.

One of the {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} {{Term|BotW|Nebb|link}} asks to see during "{{Term|BotW|The Weapon Connoisseur|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Frostspear}}, which his grandfather looked for when he was younger.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|frostspear}}? So...when my grandpa was young, he looked for it but couldn't find it. So please! If you find it, please let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} shows him a {{Term|BotW|Frostspear}}, {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} will be excited and give him a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that the {{Color|BotW Blue|frostspear}}? So cool! Let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks for lettin' me see! Take this as a thank you!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Frostspear}} are made of metal, and as a result, they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, but they are incapable of burning when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Frostspear}} can be manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
