''{{Name|Japanese|The Legend of Zelda: The Count of Black Shadows}}'' is the third in a series of [[w:novel|novels]] published by [[Futabasha]]. It was published on February 5, 1992.

==About==
The super popular action RPG has finally been successfully ported to a novel!
An adventure book that talks about the legend of [[Triforce]] and the activities of Hero [[Link]] is here!
