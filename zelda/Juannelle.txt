{{Term/Store|Juannelle||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Juannelle|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} residing at {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tundra|link}}. She spends her days inside the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}, where she writes in her journal. At night, she sleeps in one of the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}}, she explains that she has been all over {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} in search of information.<ref>{{Cite|I've traveled to the east and the west in my great hunt for information, and now I find myself here in the freezing north...|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> She has come to {{Term|BotW|Hebra|link}} in search of a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse|link}}, but her efforts have been halted by the cold.<ref>{{Cite|I heard there was a {{Color|BotW Red|Stalhorse}} in this area, but I can't take it any longer! It's too cold to look anymore!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} shows interest, {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} shares that she wants to see a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}} with her own eyes.<ref>{{Cite|[A Stalhorse?] {{Big|Beep-beep!}} You just set off Juannelle's intuition sensors! You're interested in the {{Color|BotW Red|Stalhorse}} too, huh? I just wanted to see this creepy {{Color|BotW Red|Stalhorse monster}} once with my own eyes!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> She thinks such a discovery would be a huge scoop.<ref>{{Cite|If it really does exist, it'd be such a big scoop!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref>

Since she is unable to go looking herself, {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he could take a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of one for her.<ref>{{Cite|But... Well, I can handle all kinds of dangers and hazards, but this cold... Brrr, I can't stand it! Can you handle this task for me? I'll even reward you! All I need is a picture! Or some notes!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes her offer, {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} will be pleased.<ref>{{Cite|[I can do it.] Good answer!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> She shares rumors that a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}} can be found with {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|N. Tabantha Snowfield|link}} or near the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a rumor that it can be found with {{Color|BotW Blue|Stalkoblins}} in the {{Color|BotW Blue|North Tabantha Snowfield}}. Some have reported it aaaaall the way on the other side of the snow field to the east, right in front of these bizarre {{Color|BotW Blue|ruins}}.|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> She wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} luck in his search.<ref>{{Cite|Good luck! I'm really counting on you!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> After this, the "{{Term|BotW|Stalhorse: Pictured!|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}} to show it to {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}}. While the sight of a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse}} scares her, she is still pleased to receive a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of it.<ref>{{Cite|Wha—? This is the {{Color|BotW Red|bony horse}}? That {{Color|BotW Blue|Stalhorse}}... It's a monster! The sight of this is going to haunt my dreams for weeks... But the picture is still amazing!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> Now that she has the {{Term|BotW|Picture}},<ref>{{Cite|Yup, that picture's all I need!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} is going to use it as inspiration to write an article.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, thank you! This is a great picture! It'll be perfect inspiration for the article I'm going to write!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's your reward, just as I promised!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> She expresses her desire to work with {{Term|BotW|Link}} in the future, at which point the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} ends.<ref>{{Cite|I hope we get a chance to work together again in the future!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} afterwards, she proclaims that she knows everything before asking if his name is {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're seeking the best information in Hyrule, I, {{Color|BotW Blue|Juannelle}}, know everything there is to know! You're...Link, right?|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms that he is, {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} states that she loves straightforward and honest answers.<ref>{{Cite|[That's me!] I love when I get straightforward, honest answers!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} instead instructs her to call him "Mr. Link," she reprimands him for acting coy.<ref>{{Cite|[That's Mr. Link to you.] Cute... Don't try to act coy with {{Color|BotW Blue|Juannelle}}!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref>

After confirming {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s identity, {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} offers to share information about the area with him.<ref>{{Cite|Soooo, what do you wanna know? I have info on the pitch-black ruins and on what's at the far edge of the Hebra Mountains.|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> He is also able to wake her up at night to ask her questions, though {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} considers this to be rude.<ref>{{Cite|I hope you realize how rude it is to wake me up when I'm trying to sleep... I'll forgive you, though. You clearly are desperate for the kind of info only I can provide!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} explains that they are extremely dark, even during the day.<ref>{{Cite|[Tell me about the ruins.] Light is unable to enter these ruins for some reason. It's said that you can't see an inch ahead, even during the day!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> She theorizes that there is a mysterious power behind the phenomenon.<ref>{{Cite|There must be some mysterious power behind this phenomenon, don't you think?|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> She believes that someone would become famous if they were able to find out what was hiding within the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins|display=Ruins}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can only wonder what's hiding within those ruins... But finding whatever it is would certainly make you famous!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> Finally, she informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that they are located past {{Term|BotW|Mount Drena|link}} in the direction of the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Mountains|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Where is it? It should be past {{Color|BotW Blue|Mount Drena}} if you're heading toward the {{Color|BotW Blue|Eldin Mountains}}.|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the far edge of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} shares he story of an adventurer who attempted to journey to the northwestern corner of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}}.<ref>{{Cite|[The far edge, eh?] There was an adventurer who pledged to journey to the northwesternmost part of Hyrule... To the very edge of the Hebra Mountains.|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> However, before he was able to reach his destination, he was forced to turn back due to the cliffs and {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This adventurer went north of the mountain range as far as possible and then west. However, steep cliffs and {{Color|BotW Red|raging winds}} stood in the way. The adventurer was forced to turn back.|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} asked the adventurer why he went so far out, and he told her it was because he wanted to know what was there.<ref>{{Cite|Upon returning, I posed a question to the adventurer. I said, "Why do so far out there?" "Because I wanted to know what was there!" was the adventurer's reply.|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} is unable to understand his reasoning, as that would not be enough of a reason for her to go out into the cold.<ref>{{Cite|I must admit, I don't think that'd be enough for me. I can't believe someone would want to go into the cold unknown.|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref>
