'''Kirby''' is the main protagonist of the [[Kirby (series)|''Kirby'' series]]. He is one of the playable characters in every installment of the {{SSB|Series}}, like [[Link]] and [[Mario]].

==Trivia==
*Kirby was created by [[Masahiro Sakurai]], who himself is also the creator of the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'']] series.
*In the ''Kirby'' series, whenever Kirby copies the [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Sword Sword ability], he gains a floppy green cap (except for ''[https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Kirby%27s_Adventure Kirby's Adventure]'' on [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]]) and many attack moves in direct reference to Link, such as the [[Down Thrust]] and firing [[Sword Beam|Sword Beams]] when at full health. According to the booklet that comes with the game ''[https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Kirby%27s_Dream_Collection_Special_Edition Kirby's Dream Collection Special Edition]'' released on the [[Wii]], this was done as a way to "return the favor" for Kirby's guest appearance in {{LA|-}}. Plus, his rival, [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Meta_Knight Meta Knight], is also a sword fighter like Link.
**In the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series, when he copies any version of Link, he gains his floppy green hat and uses the [[Bow]] and [[Arrow]], although in the original [[Nintendo 64]] game Kirby uses the Boomerang, instead. The Sword ability itself was also used as his [[Final Smash]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U|SSB4]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|Ultimate]]'', known as the Ultra Sword.
*Although Kirby is canonically male, due to his cute, feminine-looking appearance, some fans have easily mistaken the character as a female.
*Although the word "Kirby" normally refers to the main character of the ''Kirby'' franchise, it is known that [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Kirby_(species) multiple other Kirbies exist as a species], which can be seen as early as ''Kirby's Dream Land''.
*Super Smash Bros. is the first media in which Kirby had a voice actor, contrary to popular belief that it was the ''Kirby: Right Back at Ya!'' anime.
