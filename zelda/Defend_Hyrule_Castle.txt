{{Term/Store|Defend Hyrule Castle||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 58 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defend Hyrule Castle|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Defend Hyrule Castle}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must capture four {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} while defending the two that they already control. These two are found in the east and near the center of the map. When it comes to the target {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}}, there is one in the far west, east, and north, and one in a room accessed through a small path. There are also multiple {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} that assault the allied {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} from the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}. Many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}} are scattered around the map as well.

The northern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}} and has a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Guardian|link}} inside as its boss, while the eastern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}  is captured by defeating a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}} instead. Although, if there is only a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel}}, a larger and stronger {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin}} will spawn after its defeat as the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}'s boss. The western {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos}} and has a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} as its boss, and the last {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} has many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} with two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} that must be defeated to capture it.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

A {{Term|HWAoC|Korok|link}} can be found in a hallway near in the western area of the map, behind a cracked wall that can be destroyed with {{Term|HWAoC|Remote Bombs|link}}. When talked to, it awards the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} with a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}}.
