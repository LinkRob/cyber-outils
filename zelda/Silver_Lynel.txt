==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Silver Lynel}}Silver Lynels make their debut as the strongest variant of Lynels in [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|''Breath of the Wild'']], being Lynels enhanced by Ganon's magic and fight just like the other Lynels, right down to wielding the Savage Lynel gear and teleporting if knocked out of their territory, though one will always wields a [[Flameblade]] in the [[Coliseum Ruins]]. In [[Master Mode]], they are outclassed by [[Golden Lynel|Golden Lynels]].

==={{TotK|-}}===
Silver Lynels reappear in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', acting the same as in ''Breath of the Wild'', but can also attack Link with their new Mace Horns. One is summoned alongside a [[Blue-Maned Lynel]] and a [[White-Maned Lynel]], in addition to some [[Bokoblin|Bokoblins]] and [[Moblin|Moblins]] by [[Ganon|Ganondorf]] after he becomes the Demon King.
