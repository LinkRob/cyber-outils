{{Plural|BotW|Soup Ladle}} are a wooden kitchen implement used for serving soup.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. One in particular can be found by the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Shared Cooking Space|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. As they are made from the wood of sturdy {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}},<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use {{Plural|BotW|Soup Ladle}} to inflict a low amount of damage to {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}.

Due to their wooden composition, {{Plural|BotW|Soup Ladle}} will burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. If they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, they will float on its surface.

{{Plural|BotW|Soup Ladle}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Daily Use Goods|link}} equipment archetype, which consists of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that double as tools used in the daily lives of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link|display=Hyrule}} residents.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>
