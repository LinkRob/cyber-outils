The "{{Term/Store|Sonata of Awakening||Series, MM, MM3D}}" is a [[Song]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96}}</ref>

==Learning Location and Uses==
[[File:MM3D Sonata of Awakening Icon.png|left]]
The "Sonata of Awakening", originally known only by the [[Deku Royal Family]], is a special melody played on the [[Deku Pipes]].<ref>{{Cite|This melody, which only the Deku Royal Family knows... It proves the foolish monkey deceived the princess so he could enter the temple!|Deku King|MM}}</ref> The captured [[Monkey]] in the [[Deku Palace]] teaches it to [[Link]] after the young hero reaches him from the [[Inner Palace Garden]] entrance.<ref>{{Cite|OK, now I'll teach you the melody that opens the temple. The princess taught it to me! We can't let them hear us, so I'll sing it softly...|Monkey|MM}}</ref> By playing the sonata, one has the ability to wake anything that is asleep, such as a [[Deku Scrub]].<ref>{{Cite|This melody awakens those who have fallen into a deep sleep!|N/A|MM}}</ref> 

The "Sonata of Awakening" is needed to enter [[Woodfall Temple]] by making it rise from the depths of [[Woodfall]]. Later on, when Link reaches the [[Ikana Graveyard]], he must play this melody to [[Captain Keeta]] in order to wake him up and race with him to receive the [[Captain's Hat]]. With the exceptions of playing it for Captain Keeta and for the sleeping Deku Scrub in the [[Swamp Spider House]], the song only works if Link is wearing the [[Deku Mask]].

==Nomenclature==
A sonata literally means a piece played, as opposed to a cantata, which is a piece sung.
