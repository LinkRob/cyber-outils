==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|Camera Manual}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} located within the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. It was written by {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Camera Manual<br/>Written by: Purah!|Camera Manual|BotW}}</ref> and it details how to use the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
