The {{Term/Store|Ketoh Wawai Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ketoh Wawai Shrine}}<br/>Ketoh Wawai's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Ketoh Wawai's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an [[Ancient Shrine]] in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai Shrine}} is located in the northeast corner of the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first enters the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins|display=Ruins}}, he will hear the voice of the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai|link}} telling him to come find the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} within the darkness.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Traveler from beyond the woods, you are now faced with a trial... Find the shrine hidden within these dark ruins...}}|Ketoh Wawai|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Shrouded Shrine|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.

At the western entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}} near a bird-shaped {{Term|BotW|Torch}}. By following the bird {{Plural|BotW|Torch}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will eventually come across a {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} with an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} hanging around his neck. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} must be obtained so it can be placed in the nearby {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}, unearthing {{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|When you stole the object from the Hinox hidden in the depths of the ruins and placed it on the pedestal, a shrine appeared!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai}} will greet him, saying he has already proven himself by arriving.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in the shrine... I am Ketoh Wawai. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Ketoh Wawai|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai Shrine}} features no puzzles, only a straight path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Core|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai}}, he will be gifted a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ketoh Wawai|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ketoh Wawai|BotW}}</ref>
