Erla is a minor member of the [[Kikwi]] tribe. He is found hiding among the grass on a cliff in the [[Faron Woods#Skyward Sword|Faron Woods]]. Link must attempt to pick him up in order to speak with him.

==Nomenclature==
Erla's name is derived from {{Wp|Earl Grey tea}}.
