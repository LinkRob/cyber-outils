{{Term/Store|Purple Deadrock|Purple Deadrocks|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Purple Deadrock|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Purple Deadrock}} are the stronger counterpart to {{Plural|CoH|Deadrock|link}}. The only appear in the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}}, where they can move on every beat as they chase {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. When hit, they turn into stone and become vulnerable to explosives. When in rock form, they can be picked up with the {{Term|CoH|Power Glove|link}} and tossed at enemies.
