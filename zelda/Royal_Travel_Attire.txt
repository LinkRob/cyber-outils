==Trivia==

* The attire is said to have been part of the royal family, presumably for female descendants, for a while. However no Zeldas before or after this one have been shown wearing it, furthermore as the time isn't specified it isn't known if it is just for Zeldas of the Fallen Hero Timeline or available to the Zeldas of the timeline before the split.
* While in game it is said to be well suited for a long journey it doesn't provide mobility or travel benefits compared to any other outfit Zelda can wear in this game, nor any benefit at all being comparable to the disguise post name clear and the royal dress.
* This is one of three outfits Zelda gets equipped with without player input throughout the game, it is also those same three that provide no unique benefit when worn.
* Zelda's ponytail when wearing this outfit appears to be her complement to Link's hooded aesthetic, especially the way it hangs down.
