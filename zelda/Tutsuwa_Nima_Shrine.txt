The {{Term/Store|Tutsuwa Nima Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Tutsuwa Nima Shrine}}<br/>A Major Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Major Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Tutsuwa Nima Shrine}} is located behind the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} can only be accessed after completing "{{Term|BotW|The Spring of Power|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. To receive this quest, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must talk to {{Term|BotW|Nobo|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}} and ask her about the surrounding area.<ref>{{Cite|[This area?] Let's see... If you go {{Color|BotW Red|west}} of the stable, there's a sacred place called the {{Color|BotW Red|Spring of Power}}!|Nobo|BotW}}</ref> She will recite a legend saying a "sacred article" must be offered to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|display=Spring}}.<ref>{{Cite|"Offer the {{Color|BotW Red|sacred article}} to the Spring of Power." That's what the legend says.|Nobo|BotW}}</ref> After hearing this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will begin.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power}}, the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} will instruct him to offer {{Term|BotW|Dinraal's Scale|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|display=Spring}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have done well to find this spring. Offer {{Color|BotW Red|Dinraal's scale}}, received from the red spirit, to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Power}}. I, Goddess Hylia, will guide you.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> Doing so will open the door to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|When you carried out the statue's suggestion and dropped Dinraal's scale into the Spring of Power, an ancient shrine revealed itself.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> When he enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will hear the voice of its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Tutsuwa Nima|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Tutsuwa Nima. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Tutsuwa Nima|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Tutsuwa Nima Shrine}} is just like any other "Major Test of Strength," which tasks {{Term|BotW|Link}} with fighting a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}}. This {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} is equipped with a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword++|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear++|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Shield++}}. The floor of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} is covered with a thin coat of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use the {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} to his advantage while fighting his {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}}.

After defeating the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV}} in combat, the portcullis in the back of the chamber will open. Behind it is the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Tutsuwa Nima}}, who will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Tutsuwa Nima|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before disappearing.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Tutsuwa Nima|BotW}}</ref>

Behind the portcullis, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also find a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. He can recover a {{Term|BotW|Flamespear|link}} from inside.
