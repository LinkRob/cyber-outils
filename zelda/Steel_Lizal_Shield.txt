{{Plural|BotW|Steel Lizal Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Shield|link}} that have been reinforced with several metal shells.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Though they offer an incredible defense, their weight makes it so only a skilled soldier can bear them.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Black Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lizalfos|link}}. After {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} tier up for the second time, they are also used by {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}}.{{Verify}}

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Steel Lizal Shield}} equipped, she will make note of it. She explains that their spike makes them worth major style points, and they make their riders look hard-core while {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's a steel Lizal shield! That spike is worth major style points for when you want to look hard-core on the slope!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Steel Lizal Shield}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, they will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
