The '''Great Sky Island''' is a location in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''. This is where [[Link]] begins his journey. It is the tutorial section of this game and the equivalent to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]''<nowiki/>'s [[Great Plateau]].
