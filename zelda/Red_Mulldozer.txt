{{Term/Store|Red Mulldozer|Red Mulldozers|Series, TMC|plural}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 194">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 194}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TMC|Red Mulldozer}} are bug-like monsters that harbor a skull pattern and spikes on their shell. They inhabit areas rich with plant life such as the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} and inside various {{Plural|TMC|House|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can only encounter them while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized. They move quickly and erratically in circles after spotting a target, but can be halted by hitting them with a {{Term|TMC|Shield|link}}, leaving them vulnerable to attacks.<ref name="E 194"/>
