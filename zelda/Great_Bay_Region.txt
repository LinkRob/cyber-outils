It lies in this direction. Be careful.|Guard|MM3D}}</ref> the '''Sea''',{{Exp Game|MM3D}}<ref>{{Cite|Sea|Map|MM3D}}</ref>{{Fact}} is the western area of [[Termina]] in {{MM}}. It is a large shore that makes home to the aquatic race of the [[Zora]]s, who live in the [[Zora Hall]]. Its hot weather, murky waters,<ref>{{Cite|Lately, the seawater has gotten really warm. It hasn’t helped my fishing at all... And what’s worse, this water has gotten murky, so when I ship out, I always lose my way and somehow end up back at shore. We fishermen – and even the fish - are in a real predicament!|Fisherman|MM}}</ref> and population of enemies now plague the Bay.<ref>{{Cite|Woah-oh! In Great Bay now somethin’ is a-hapennin’. Is it now?|Mikau|MM}}</ref> The Great Bay is also home to the [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirates]], who live in their [[Pirates' Fortress|fortress]].

The entrance to the Great Bay is barricaded by fences, requiring that [[Link]] bring [[Epona]] to jump over them.

==Features and Overview==
===Great Bay Coast===
{{Main|Great Bay Coast}}
The [[Great Bay Coast]] is the main part of the region. Found in the coast is the [[Fisherman's Hut]] as well as the [[Oceanside Spider House]]. The coast provides access to the other parts of the western region.

==={{Term|MM3D|Marine Research Lab}}===
{{Main|Marine Research Lab}}
The {{Term|MM3D|Marine Research Lab|link}} is located some meters from the coast. It is where various marine biology experiments are carried out. Link finds here the tank in which he should place the [[Zora Egg]]s stolen from singer [[Lulu]] by the Gerudo Pirates from the northern fortress.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Baby, listen to me. I don’t want to beg. Gerudo Pirates! They stole that girl’s eggs|Mikau|MM}}</ref>

===Fisherman's Hut===
{{Main|Fisherman's Hut}}
The Fisherman's Hut is where the [[Fisherman]] lives, where he used to practice fishing until the coast became unsafe. He has a [[Seahorse]] for sale. The Fisherman wants a [[Pictograph]] of a Gerudo Pirate, and will allow Link to take his Seahorse in exchange. He also offers to play a mini-game which involves [[Fisherman's Jumping Game|jumping between platforms]], where a [[Piece of Heart]] can be obtained.

===Oceanside Spider House===
{{Main|Oceanside Spider House}}
The [[Oceanside Spider House]] is a cursed, abandoned house east from the coast. It is full of [[Gold Skulltula]]s. A man seeking shelter appears here once all of the Skulltulas inside have been slain, and will give Link various awards depending on which Day he had cleared the house out.

===Pirates' Fortress===
{{Main|Pirates' Fortress}}
The Pirates' Fortress is the refuge of the dreaded Gerudo Pirates.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Mikau!  What are you doing in a place like this? This is the ominous Pirates’ Fortress. I don’t think you should get too close.|Zora|MM}}</ref> Having an industrial appearance, it is a stronghold where the stolen [[Zora Eggs]] are held inside pools of water (although only four of the Eggs are actually kept here, since the Pirates lost the other three).

===Pinnacle Rock===
{{Main|Pinnacle Rock}}
[[Pinnacle Rock]] is northwest from the coast. It is a formation of rocks filled with nothing but murky water. Beneath the waves of these waters (deep under the rocks) is home to the [[Deep Python]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Here in the depths of Pinnacle Rock live many dangerous sea snakes…|Seahorse|MM}}</ref>

===Zora Cape===
{{Main|Zora Cape}}
[[Zora Cape]] is south from the coast. It is where a river ends its path towards the sea. It is full of rock elevations, and it is also where Link can find the rest of Great Bay's areas, including a [[Fairy Fountain]].

===Zora Hall===
{{Main|Zora Hall}}
[[Zora Hall]] is the congregation area for the Zoras, found inside a coral reef a few meters from the river. It is also the home of the famous rock band [[The Indigo-Go's]].

===Great Bay Temple===
{{Main|Great Bay Temple}}
The [[Great Bay Temple]] is the only temple in Termina that is not located within a specific area. It is inaccessible to most people and is surrounded by a very powerful cyclone of water.

===Waterfall Rapids===
{{Main|Waterfall Rapids}}
Found east of Zora Cape, the [[Waterfall Rapids]] are accessed from behind the top of the river's waterfall.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, aren’t you Mikau? Were you able to get an empty bottle from the beavers on top of the waterfall? Those guys are quick, so it's pretty tough, isn't it?|Zora|MM}}</ref> It is the home of two {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver Bros.|link}}, who will race Link and, on the event of losing, will give away an empty [[Bottle]] (after two races) and a Piece of Heart (after four).

==Crossover appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:Great Bay.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The Great Bay stage from {{SSBM|-}}]]
==={{SSBM|-}}===
Great Bay appears as a stage in {{SSBM}}. The stage features the {{Term|SSBM|Marine Research Lab}} as the main platform. A rock platform stands to the left and the [[Giant Turtle]] to the right; the palm trees on his back serve as two smaller platforms. After a while, the Turtle will hide underwater, reappearing after a set amount of time.

[[Tingle]] makes a cameo appearance floating on a [[Tingle Balloon|balloon]] that can be used as a platform. If a player stands long enough on the balloon, or it is pierced by an attack, it will burst and drop Tingle on the platform below. Eventually, Tingle will blow up another balloon.

The [[Four Giants]] and the [[Moon]] make background appearances. As the fight progresses, the Moon will come closer to the ground. Eventually, the Four Giants will appear and stop the Moon, returning it to its original position.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
Great Bay returns as a stage in {{SSBU}} with updated graphics to closely resemble its {{MM3D}} appearance. Like every other stage, it also features a flat Omega form and a Battlefield form with three extra platforms.
{{Noncanon|End}}
