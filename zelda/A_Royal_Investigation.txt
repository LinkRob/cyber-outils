{{Term/Store|A Royal Investigation||HWAoC}} is a level 61 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|A Royal Investigation|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|A Royal Investigation}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Terrako Component|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Princess|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}'s objective is to capture three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} within seven minutes. One {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Lynel|link}} spawns inside when {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} approaches. Although, the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Lynel}}'s defeat does not capture the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} has to defeat a large {{Term|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos}} instead. The other two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are just like the first, except that all {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are infused with ice for the second and with electricity for the third. Other {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, like {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Keese|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Keese|link}} are also scattered around the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}}, along with a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok|link}} and many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} near {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}'s starting point. Once the objective is reached, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Lynel|link}} spawn in a new southern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} and the time limit is set to five minutes. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Malice Lynel}} are defeated.
