{{Term/Store|The Breach of Demise||HWAoC}} is a level 15 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Breach of Demise|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|The Breach of Demise}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}} and 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}’s objective is to capture three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}}. The first {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is captured by lowering its health and then defeating a {{Term|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}. The second is captured by defeating a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} that spawns when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, and the third is captured by defeating two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} that spawn when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} does the same. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once all three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured.
