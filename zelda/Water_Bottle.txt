'''Water Bottle''' is an item in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Water Bottle begins as an [[Empty Bottle]], but when [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] fills it with [[Water]] from the [[River Source Pond]], it becomes a Water Bottle. Zelda can give it to a thirsty woman named [[Glebb]] to receive the [[Vial of Winds]].
