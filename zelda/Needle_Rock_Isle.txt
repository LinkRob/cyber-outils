{{Term/Store|Needle Rock Isle||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (E1) is an island in the [[Great Sea]] of {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features==
{{Fishman|You've seen the boats sailing around here with the real huge cannons, right, fry? I bet you those things have their hulls packed with treasure! Of course, if you don't have a cannon yourself there's no way you'll ever sink one of them...}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= <p>Needle Rock Isle</p><p>That's where Needle Rock Isle is. Nature sure creates some odd shapes!</p>
}}

The island has a very tall pillar right in the middle, as well as a switch at the top of it that can be used to shut off the flames surrounding a chest in order to get the [[Piece of Heart]] placed inside it. There is also a cave with its entrance frozen where, once inside, Link must light up six torches using the [[Fire Arrow]] to obtain 100 [[Rupee]]s.

There are also three [[Warship]]s in the surrounding waters. The golden ship contains a [[Triforce Chart]] which is retrieved by sinking the ship and using the [[Grappling Hook]] in the light-ring that appears. Like in other islands (such as the [[Crescent Moon Island]]), big jars on barrels can be found, although they sink when Link is too close to them.

This island is the location the [[Korok]], [[Elma]], chose to plant one of the [[Great Deku Tree]]'s seeds.
