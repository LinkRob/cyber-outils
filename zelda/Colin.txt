{{Term/Store|Colin||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/>

==Biography==
Colin is a timid young boy who lives in [[Ordon Village]]. He is the son of [[Rusl]] and {{Term|TPHD|Uli|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When my son Colin grows up, I want him to be just like you. He really is a sweet boy... He can just be a little...timid.|Uli|TP}}</ref><ref name= "Fishing Rod">{{Cite|My son Colin is making a fishing rod so the two of you can play together.|Rusl|TP}}</ref> He looks up to both [[Link]] and [[Ilia]].<ref>{{Cite|But when I grow up, I'm gonna be just like you, Link!|Colin|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whenever I thought I couldn't go on, I would think of you and Ilia and hold on, Link...|Colin|TP}}</ref> Colin is often taunted and teased by [[Malo]] and [[Talo]], two other children of Ordon Village.<ref>{{Cite|Link. Thanks...Those guys are always teasing me...|Colin|TP}}</ref> He gradually becomes braver and more confident as the story progresses.<ref>{{Cite|I understand what my dad meant when he told me I needed to be stronger, like you, Link... He wasn't talking about strength, like lifting stuff. He was talking about being brave...|Colin|TP}}</ref> Colin made Link's [[Fishing Rod]] early in the game.<ref name= "Fishing Rod"/>

Colin is kidnapped along with [[Ilia]] when the [[Ordona Province]] comes under attack by [[King Bulblin]] and his [[Bulblin]]s. Link later finds him in the care of [[Renado]] in [[Kakariko Village]] along with the other children. Shortly after Link gets [[Epona]] back, the village is attacked by King Bulblin. Colin proves his bravery by pushing {{Term|TPHD|Beth|link}} out of the way of the rampaging monster but is captured in the process. Link rescues Colin, who appears to be sorely wounded. Colin recovers under the care of Beth and {{Term|TPHD|Luda|link}}. Because of his bravery, Colin earns the respect of the other children of Ordon Village.<ref>{{Cite|I can't believe Colin... I mean, that was a really brave thing for him to do, huh?|Talo|TP}}</ref>

During the ending credits, Colin appears in Hyrule Field with the other children carrying a [[Sword]] and [[Shield]], symbolizing his hard-earned courage.
