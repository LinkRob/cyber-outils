{{Term/Store|Pippin||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 210}}</ref>

==Biography==
Pippin is a farmer who resides in [[Lynna Village]]. He appears to have a great disliking for [[Gasha Seed]]s, and will give [[Link]] one when talked to.<ref>{{Cite|Darn Gasha Seeds! Sproutin'! Growin'! Makin' nuts! And all those new nuts got different stuff inside! Hah! Get rid of it for me!|Pippin|OoA}}</ref> Pippin warns Link against planting a Gasha Seed in the soil by his home.<ref>{{Cite|I know there's soil right by my house, but don't go plantin' seeds there!|Pippin|OoA}}</ref> Shocked by this, and his resemblance to [[Bipin|someone else he knows]], Link asks Pippin's name, who obliges, and tells him not to forget it.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? My name? It's Pippin! Don't forget it!|Pippin|OoA}}</ref>
