==Location and Uses==
The Tunic of the Wind is a piece of [[Armor]], which can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the ''Super Smash Bros.'' Toon Link Amiibo '''or''' ''The Legend of Zelda'' 30th Anniversary Toon Link Amiibo. Each Wind the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Tunic of the Wind. It can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Tunic of the Wind, alongside the [[Cap of the Wind]] and [[Trousers of the Wind]], comprise the [[Hero of the Wind Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from ''[[Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]''. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
