The {{Term/Store|Goddess Sword|Goddess Swords|Series, SS, BotW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref><ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Goddess Sword}}<br/>A sword said to have once belonged to a hero from the sky. Its blade houses the fire of the Goddess. When wielded, a strange yet heavenly breeze kicks up around you.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It is a legendary blade that was crafted by the Goddess, [[Hylia]], to aid her [[Hylia's Chosen Hero|Chosen Hero]].<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|SS Gold|Goddess Sword}}! This mysterious sword is bathed in divine light.|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref name="Created">{{Cite|I was created for a single purpose, long before the recorded memory of your people. I must aid you in fulfilling the great destiny that is your burden to carry.|Fi|SS}}</ref> To this end, it is inhabited by a spirit known as [[Fi]]. In order to defeat the [[Demon]]s which threaten [[The Surface]], it is eventually tempered into the [[Master Sword]] by [[Link]].<ref name="Blessing">{{Cite|{{Color|SS Gold|Valiant}} {{Color|SS Red|hero}}{{Color|SS Gold|, you have endured many hardships and journeyed far in your quest to reach this place. Along your travels you have found wisdom, power, and courage, and for this I shall bless your sword with the goddess's power. May it give you and your sword the strength to drive back the abomination that threatens this land!}}|Zelda|SS}}</ref> The Goddess Sword undergoes several stages to reach this ability: first by transforming into the {{Term|SS|Goddess Longsword|link}} with [[Farore's Flame]],<ref>{{Cite|The flames of Farore have improved your sword, making it longer and sharp enough to do twice as much damage!|N/A|SS}}</ref> the {{Term|SS|Goddess White Sword|link}} by [[Nayru's Flame]],<ref>{{Cite|The flames of Nayru have transformed your blade, expanding Fi's powers. She can now help you use your {{Color|SS Red|dowsing}} ability to search for more objects!|N/A|SS}}</ref> the {{Term|SS|Master Sword|link}} with [[Din's Flame]] and ultimately, the {{Term|SS|True Master Sword|link}} with Hylia's blessing.<ref>{{Cite|The flames of Din have imbued your blade with a sacred white light that demons revile.|N/A|SS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
The Goddess Hylia created the Goddess Sword and Fi,<ref name="Created" /> an intelligent humanoid spirit who resides in the Sword, and gave it the purpose of assisting Hylia's chosen hero.<ref>{{Cite|Link, the goddess created Fi and the great blade she's a part of for very specific reasons.|Zelda|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|First, she created {{Color|SS Blue|Fi}}. She made the spirit that resides in your sword to serve a single purpose: to assist her {{Color|SS Red|chosen hero}} on his mission.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> She then enshrined the Sword inside the [[Statue of the Goddess]] before raising it into [[The Sky]] as part of [[Skyloft]].

Preceding the [[Wing Ceremony]], Fi appears to [[Link]] in his dreams, foretelling him of his destiny. After Link and [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]] are attacked by a black tornado, Fi appears again to Link in his sleep, calling for him to realize his destiny.<ref>{{Cite|Link...? I am waiting for you. The time has come for you to awaken. You are vital to a mission of great importance. Link...|Fi|SS}}</ref> After he awakens, Fi appears in the [[Knight Academy]] outside of Link's room to guide him to the  [[Chamber of the Sword]]. Inside, Fi explains his fate as the [[Hylia's Chosen Hero|Chosen Hero]] and encourages him to draw the Goddess Sword from its pedestal so that Fi can formally acknowledge him as her Master.<ref>{{Cite|I was created for a single purpose, long before the recorded memory of your people. I must aid you in fulfilling the great destiny that is your burden to carry. Come, Link. You must take up this sword. As the one chosen by my creator, it is your destiny.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|SS Gold|The youth who draws forth the guiding sword shall be known as the goddess's chosen hero, and it is he who possesses an unbreakable spirit. He shall be burdened with the task of abolishing the shadow of apocalypse from the land. Such is his destiny. With the spirit of the blade at his side, he shall soar over the clouds and plummet below... ...And united with the spirit maiden, shall bring forth a piercing light that resurrects the land.}}|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In the name of my creator, draw the sword and raise it skyward. Recognition complete, Master...|Fi|SS}}</ref>

After Link receives the [[Goddess's Harp]] from Zelda, the young hero must temper the Goddess Sword using the three [[Sacred Flame]]s in order for it to hold the necessary power to open the [[Gate of Time]] at the [[Sealed Grounds]].<ref>{{Cite|Ah, sorry to disappoint you, boy, but for now your sword lacks the power necessary to awaken the gate. {{Color|SS Red|Faron Woods}}..{{Color|SS Red|.Eldin Volcano}}...and {{Color|SS Red|Lanayru Desert}}... A {{Color|SS Red|sacred flame}} is hidden somewhere in each of these lands. Seek them out, and purify your sword in their heat. Only after your blade has been tempered by these three fires will it be fully imbued with the {{Color|SS Red|great power}} for which you search.|Impa|SS}}</ref> Each time Link bathes the Goddess Sword in one of the Sacred Flames, the Sword is both empowered and physically altered. First, Farore's Flame converts it to the Goddess Longsword, which has doubled [[Weapon Strength|attacking power]] and greater reach. Then, Nayru's Flame converts it to the Goddess White Sword, which adds the ability to dowse for [[Rupees]], [[Treasures]], [[Gratitude Crystals]] and [[Goddess Cubes]]. Finally, Din's Flame converts it to the [[Master Sword]], with four times the attacking power of original Goddess Sword, an even greater reach, and the [[Power to Repel Evil]]. However, this Master Sword is not fully capable of defeating [[Demise]], and requires its true power to be unlocked by Hylia's blessing.

The final change to the Goddess Sword takes place in the distant past, before Zelda enters into a deep sleep to keep the seal placed upon Demise intact. Her blessing converts it to the True Master Sword,<ref name="Blessing" /><ref name="Wield">{{Cite|The goddess has blessed your blade, and the Master Sword has at last achieved its ultimate form! The sword is now imbued with the mythical power to drive back demons, and only {{Color|SS Blue|Link}} may wield it!|N/A|SS}}</ref> extending the cross-guard outward as well as restoring the gem and the [[Triforce]] symbol upon it. With this final transformation, the True Master Sword is capable of defeating Demise. Additionally, the time it takes to charge a Skyward Strike is reduced. In [[Hero Mode]], the [[Skyward Strike]] is already at its maximum power and charging speed from the previous playthrough, so upon acquiring the True Master Sword its charge time is upgraded again to become nearly instantaneous.

The Goddess Sword houses a spirit, Fi, who guides Link in many ways, such as giving him helpful advice or actually pointing him in the direction he needs to go. When Fi has something to say, the Sword begins to glow, and Link can speak to Fi at any time by pressing down on the D-Pad. Moreover, the Sword has a treasure-seeking ability called [[Dowsing]]. With this ability, it can sense important people, items and objects to help Link locate them, acting as a sort of compass.<ref>{{Cite|Taking this into account, I calculate an 85% probability that your quest will be aided if you find the lost Kikwis for the elder. To expedite your search for these forest-dwelling creatures, I have added {{Color|SS Red|Kikwis}} as a dowsing option.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Link can choose in the Dowsing menu which specific object or person he wishes to track down. While Dowsing, the Goddess Sword periodically beeps as it searches for the target, beeping with greater frequency the closer Link is to the target.

If Link holds the Goddess Sword up to the sky, it will be charged up by "energy from the heavens" and be able to release a [[Skyward Strike]] once it's swung.<ref>{{Cite|Master Link, I suggest you imbue that crest with the power of your {{Color|SS Red|Skyward Strike}} and then insert the tablet I gave you a moment ago. To gather energy from the heavens and charge up a {{Color|SS Red|Skyward Strike}}, {{Color|SS Green|point your sword skyward}}.|Fi|SS}}</ref> This beam can activate the various objects that the Goddess specifically left for the hero, such as [[Goddess Cubes]], [[Goddess Wall]]s and [[Goddess Crest]]s.<ref>{{Cite|If I remember right... Yup, this is one of the things the goddess was said to have left behind for the hero of legend. Pretty amazing, right? I even thought up a name for these things. I have been calling them Goddess Cubes! Makes sense, right?|Gorko|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|When you struck the crest with a Skyward Strike from your sword, a message from the {{Color|SS Blue|goddess}} awakened deep within my memory. The goddess intended this message for you, Master. These are her words.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Goddess Sword}} is a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} that may be obtained at random within a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that appears after tapping {{TLoZ|-}} series {{SS|-}} {{Term|SS|Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. It has 28 attack power and a durability of 45. The {{Term|BotW|Goddess Sword}} houses the fire of the {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link|display=Goddess Hylia}}.<ref name="Inventory" /> It is said that a "heavenly breeze" kicks up around its wielder.<ref name="Inventory" />

Being made of metal, the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Sword}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if it is equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. It will also sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but it can be picked up and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. If it is exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Sword}} will not burn like a wooden {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} would.

Although it is known as the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Sword}}, it instead resembles the {{Term|Series|Goddess Longsword|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
The Goddess Sword functions the same as it did in {{BotW|-}}, however it is now rewarded upon completing the quest "The Mother Goddess Statue," involving the revival of the statue located in the Forgotten Temple. An amiibo is no longer required, and its implementation is likely canon due to the nature of the quest.

Its name is now referred to as the ''White Sword of the Sky'' and the description of the weapon has been changed to read, "A sword said to have once belonged to a hero from the sky. It's beautiful white blade stands out. When it's wielded, a strange but heavenly breeze kicks up around you." 

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
The Goddess Sword appears as part of [[Fi]]'s moveset in {{HW}}. The three main tiers of the Sword are based on the upgraded versions of it that appear in {{SS|-}}. While the first two tiers are named identically to their {{SS|-}} counterparts, the Goddess White Sword was renamed the True Goddess Blade for its third tier. The star tier of the moveset added in the ''Master Quest'' pack DLC is the 8-bit [[Silver Arrow]].
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*When Link first obtains the True Master Sword, it is stated that he is the only one capable of wielding it.<ref name="Wield" /><ref>{{Cite|This sword, blessed by the goddess herself, can only be wielded by the hero of legend.|N/A|SS}}</ref> Despite this, [[Ghirahim]] is able to disarm Link and wield it himself during the [[Thunder Dragon's Lightning Round]], as the Lightning Round reuses the first Ghirahim fight, and Ghirahim's gimmick in that fight was mockingly stealing and using the Goddess Sword against Link.
*At the end of {{SS|-}}, because of the time travel involved, the Goddess Sword and Master Sword exist simultaneously inside the Statue of the Goddess of the past and the [[Temple of Hylia]] of the present, respectively.
*Despite its name, the Goddess Longsword is in fact an {{Wp|arming sword}}.
