{{Term/Store|Blaino||Series, LA, LADX, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|LADX|Boss|link}} and character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 167 {{Exp Game|LA}} & 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref>{{Note|Blaino is known as '''Might Punch''' in the {{Guide|OoS|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= A Work-Out with {{Color|OoS Versus Red|Might Punch}}<br/>This is the home of the, ahem, legendary champ "{{Color|OoS Versus Red|Might Punch}}." |game= OoS |publisher= Versus |page= 21 }}</ref> "Might Punch" is a direct translation of his Japanese name.}} {{Term|Series|Blaino}} appears as a {{Term|LA|Sub-Boss|link}} in {{LA}} and as a minor character in {{OoS}}. {{Term|Series|Blaino}} is characterized by his boxing gloves and his fitting boxing-style he uses to fight {{Term|Series|Link|link}} in both his appearances. 

==Appearances==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Term|LA|Blaino}} first appears as the {{Term|LA|Sub-Boss|link}} of {{Term|LA|Turtle Rock|link}}, the final {{Term|LA|Dungeon}} of {{LA}}. {{Term|LA|Link}} can defeat him with only his {{Term|LA|Sword|link}}, but {{Term|LA|Blaino}} is capable of blocking {{Term|LA|Link}}'s attacks with his gloves, making it impossible for {{Term|LA|Link}} to attack him head-on. Being punched by {{Term|LA|Blaino}} sends {{Term|LA|Link}} back to the entrance of the {{Term|LA|Dungeon}}. Although there are many {{Plural|LA|Sub-Boss}} in {{Term|LA|Turtle Rock}}, {{Term|LA|Blaino}} is the only one not to have appeared in previous {{Plural|LA|Dungeon}}, and is therefore the {{Term|LA|Dungeon}}'s true {{Term|LA|Sub-Boss}}. As such, a portal to the entrance appears upon his defeat.

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, {{Term|OoS|Blaino}} becomes a regular character capable of speech. He lives within his boxing gym in {{Term|OoS|North Horon|link}}, and will challenge {{Term|OoS|Link}} to a boxing match for 10 {{Plural|OoS|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm da legendary champ, Blaino! You want somt'in'? Den earn it in da ring! Will you bet 10 Rupees?|Blaino|OoS}}</ref> The mini-game is won by knocking him out of the ring (or lost by getting knocked out of the ring) using only fists, as items (or rings) are not allowed.<ref>{{Cite|Lissen up! We trade punches 'til one of us gets knocked outta da ring. And no items!|Blaino|OoS}}</ref> If {{Term|OoS|Link}} attempts to use an item during the minigame, the match will automatically end. As punishment, {{Term|OoS|Blaino}} will raise the price of a match to 20 {{Plural|OoS|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you break da rules once, da next match is for 20 Rupees. And no rings!|Blaino|OoS}}</ref> The prize for defeating {{Term|OoS|Blaino}} is 30 {{Plural|OoS|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hyuh! I lose. You're good! Well, here's yer 30 Rupees.|Blaino|OoS}}</ref>

On his adventures, {{Term|OoS|Link}} must win back {{Term|OoS|Ricky's Gloves|link}} by defeating {{Term|OoS|Blaino}} in a boxing match. The gloves belong to {{Term|OoS|Ricky|link}}, who lost to {{Term|OoS|Blaino}} in a boxing match, consequently losing his gloves to the boxing champion.<ref name="Gloves"/>

==Trivia==
*{{Term|Series|Blaino}}'s gloves are blue in {{LA|DX}}, and red in {{OoS}}.
*In {{OoS}}, {{Term|OoS|Blaino}} takes on the accent of a typical boxer from [[wikipedia:Boston|Boston]].
