==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>I have recorded information on this creature.</p><p>His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Lopsa}}. He is the most cowardly of the herbivorous Kikwi race.</p>
}}

Lopsa is a minor member of the [[Kikwi]] tribe. He is also the most cowardly of the tribe.<ref>{{Cite|His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Lopsa}}. He is the most cowardly of the herbivorous Kikwi race.|Fi|SS}}</ref>  When Link first encounters him, he is hiding on a tree branch from a [[Bokoblin]]. After Link defeats the Bokoblin, Lopsa will ask the young hero for help getting down. Link helps him by rolling into the tree, causing Lopsa to fall face first onto the ground.<ref>{{Cite|See, I've got this problem. My legs have turned to jelly and I can't get down. Think you could help me, kwee?|Lopsa|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ouch! That, uh...was effective. Anyway, thanks to you I'm saved, kee-paleep!|Lopsa|SS}}</ref> After thanking the hero for saving him, he asks Link to find the elder [[Bucha]] and tell him where he is.<ref>{{Cite|My name's {{Color|SS Blue|Lopsa}}. If you see the elder, can you please tell him where I am? Oh kwee... My legs are still shaking.|Lopsa|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Lopsa's name is derived from {{Wp|Lapsang souchong}}, a type of {{Wp|black tea}}.
