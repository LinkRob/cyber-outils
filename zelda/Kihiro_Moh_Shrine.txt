The {{Term/Store|Kihiro Moh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kihiro Moh Shrine}}<br/>Inside the Box}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Inside the Box''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} DLC Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Kihiro Moh Shrine}} is found in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} outside of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}}. It is one of three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} unlocked as part of the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must infiltrate the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout}} and retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb|link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}} stole from the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|In that case, I shall not keep this a secret from you. Our {{Color|BotW Red|treasured orb}} was stolen.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> It is found on the second floor, among a pile of {{Term|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I know one thing for sure... The {{Color|BotW Red|orb they stole from the Gerudo tribe}} is in their second floor storage area.|Mils|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} must sneak past or defeat several {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Blademaster|link}} to reach the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb|display=Orb}}. 

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} sneaks out with the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}}, he must throw it into the massive pit in the center of the arena where he fought {{Term|BotW|Master Kohga|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} loses the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb|display=Orb}} after retrieving it, it will reappear in the final chamber of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout}}. After successfully dropping the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}} into the pit, {{Term|BotW|Kihiro Moh Shrine}} will rise from beneath the ground nearby. Upon entering the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, Link is offered the trial by the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kihiro Moh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kihiro Moh. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kihiro Moh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
In the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} are four rows of {{Plural|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|link}} for {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}. Each row is denoted by a different color. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must place each Orb in the correct slot in the row from one to five, denoted by the amount of lights at the beginning of each row.

To find out which slot to place each {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} in, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must count the amount of each color of sphere inside the gyroscope puzzle on the ceiling of the next room, accessed by a device in the center of the first room. There is one red sphere, two green spheres, two yellow spheres, and four purple spheres. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must place the nearby red sphere on the first row of the red column, the green sphere on the second row of the green column, the yellow sphere in the second row of the yellow column, and the purple sphere in the fourth row of the purple column. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has done so, a gate will open and allow him access to the second room and the end of the Shrine.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} positions the gyroscope device so that the magnetic part faces downward, he can use {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} to release all the Orbs from the cube. On the right side of the second room, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find another small Orb puzzle with three different slots labeled one, two, and three by the lights on the wall. A large metal sphere lies in a compartment in the wall above it and two metal spheres can be found inside the gyroscope device. With the slots of the puzzle being the same size as these large metal spheres, Link can place one in the slot labeled with three lights after counting a total of three spheres. After doing so, a small room will open with a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Thunderspear|link}}.

At the end of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} meets the {{Term|BotW|Kihiro Moh}}, who commends his resourcefulness and rewards him with one of {{Term|BotW|Naboris's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of the hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Naboris's Emblem.}}|Kihiro Moh|BotW}}</ref> He then tells Link to collect all three of Naboris's Emblems to reveal a new challenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Kihiro Moh|BotW}}</ref> If this is the first of the three Shrines Link visits, the Monk tells Link that two Shrines remain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Kihiro Moh|BotW}}</ref> If it is the second Shrine he visits, the Monk will tell him that one remains.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Kihiro Moh|BotW}}</ref> If it is the third and final Shrine he visits, the Monk will tell him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Naboris...}}|Kihiro Moh|BotW}}</ref>
