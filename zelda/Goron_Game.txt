The {{Term/Store|Goron Game||Series, PH}} is a racing {{Term|PH|Mini-Game|link}} that can be played in {{PH}}.{{Name Ref|PH}}

==Location and Rules==
The Goron Game takes place on [[Dee Ess Island]], and becomes available only after [[Link]] clears the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}} and has received [[Gongoron]]'s letter from the [[Postman]]. If Link arrives to Dee Ess Island and speaks with the game's operator prior to clearing the Temple of Ice, the operator will inform him that the preparations are not yet complete, as he has yet to find a part-time employee, and thus, the mini-game is closed. However, some time after Link has cleared the Temple of Ice, he will receive a letter from Gongoron, informing him that he had left [[Goron Island]] and went to work as part-time employee in Dee Ess Island.

The Goron Game costs 50 [[Rupee]]s to play. Link is partnered with Gongoron during the mini-game. The object of the game is to direct Gongoron through the island, breaking floating crystals along the way, in a set amount of time. Successfully clearing the game rewards Link with various prizes; the value of which is determined by how quickly Link and Gongoron clear the game. Common prizes include various [[Treasure]]s, [[Ship Parts]], or [[Rupee]]s. The best prize available, a [[Bombchu Bag]], is rewarded after beating the record of 35 seconds.
