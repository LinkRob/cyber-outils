The {{Term/Store|Round Spiral Staircase||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 240}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Round Spiral Staircase}} connects the garden of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} to its interior. However, its exterior entrance is covered by {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}, blocking this route for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Round Spiral Staircase|display=Staircase}} can be accessed from the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors|link}} via the {{Term|BotW|Water Pump Room|link}}. After defeating the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Eyeball|link}} found there, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pass through the {{Term|BotW|Round Spiral Staircase|display=Staircase}} without any obstacles. 

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Round Spiral Staircase}}. From inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can retrieve three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow|link}}.
