The {{Term/Store|River Devil||Series, TAoL}} is an enemy in {{TAoL}}.<ref name="Name">{{Cite|THE RIVER DEVIL EATS THOSE WHO ARE WEAK.|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref> It is a black creature that blocks the single north-south road of [[Eastern Hyrule]].

==Characteristics==
Though citizens of [[Nabooru (Town)|Nabooru]] speak of the River Devil in fear,<ref name="Name"/> he does not attack, and serves merely as an obstruction. The only way to pass him is to play the [[Flute]] found in the [[Palace on the Sea]].<ref>{{Cite|THE DEVIL DOES NOT LIKE NOISE|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The River Devil has a different design in the Japanese version of the game, directly based on a traditional ''{{Wp|Oni}}''.
