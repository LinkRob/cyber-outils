==={{TWW|-}}===
Following the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Flood|link}}, the vast majority of {{Term|TWWHD|Hyrule|link}} was sunk into the {{Term|TWWHD|Water (Terrain)|link|display=ocean}}. Most of the survivors settled on new islands created by the deluge, discarding their {{Term|TWWHD|Hyrulean}} descent in the process. However, one resident of {{Term|TWWHD|Hyrule}} remained. In spirit form, {{Term|TWWHD|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|link}} remained in the submerged {{Term|TWWHD|Hyrule Castle|link}} for at least some time, before going to the surface in the guise of the {{Term|TWWHD|King of Red Lions|link}} to aid {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} in his quest.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Plural|TMC|Hyrulean}} live scattered throughout the kingdom of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} in {{TMC}}. Most of them are {{Plural|TMC|Hylian|link}}, although a handful of {{Plural|TMC|Goron|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Business Scrub|link}} also reside within {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}. {{Plural|TMC|Hyrulean}} are concentrated mostly in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}}, although a few of them live in the surrounding areas (such as {{Term|TMC|Percy|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Stockwell|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Smith|link}} in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}}).
