==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Crenel Hermit}}
The {{Term|TMC|Crenel Hermit}} resides in a cave high up on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}};<ref>{{Cite|I'm the {{Color|TMC Green|Crenel hermit}}. I left the world to pursue a life of quiet reflection. You seem to be but a child, and yet you climbed this forbidding mountain alone? I must reward your courage with a little information.|Crenel Hermit|TMC}}</ref> his cave is built into the northwestern section of the {{Term|TMC|Crenel Wall|link}}. Upon meeting {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, he notes that specific patterned walls about the face of {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}} are susceptible to {{Plural|TMC|Bomb|link}} explosions,<ref>{{Cite|Have you not seen strange patterned walls as you climbed this mountain? {{Color|TMC Red|Bombs}}! Bombs are the answer!|Crenel Hermit|TMC}}</ref> yielding secret passageways that hide {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}, among other rewards.

He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Of course!|Crenel Hermit|TMC}}</ref>
