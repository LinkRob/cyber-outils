==Biography==
Hamsha is an elderly woman who lives in an igloo in [[Nortinka]]. For unknown reasons, her mobility seems to rely entirely on an unspecified fountain. During [[Ganon]]'s siege over the island of [[Koridai]], he had frozen this fountain, and as a result froze Hamsha as well.<ref>{{Cite|Help! Ganon froze the fountain. I'm stuck!|Hamsha|TFoE}}</ref> [[Link]] can throw a [[Firestone]] at her to thaw her, and she in turn rewards him with some [[Water of Life]]. If Link owns a [[Canteen]], she will refill it for him as well. Even when thawed, she is still not free, and will freeze again shortly after. Link can thaw her again whenever necessary.
