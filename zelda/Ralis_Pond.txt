{{Term/Store|Ralis Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Ralis Pond'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Ralis Pond is located in the [[Lanayru Great Spring]] between the [[Veiled Falls]] and the [[Bank of Wishes]]. The Ralis Pond is not deep enough for [[Link]] to swim in, so he is able to stand upright in the water.

Nearby the Ralis Pond, a [[Blue Hinox]] can be found sleeping in the grass.
