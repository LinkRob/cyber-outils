==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Diary}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} that was used by {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} to document her personal thoughts into. The {{Term|BotW|Book}} can only accessed with the purchase of {{TCB}} DLC Pack. When downloaded, it will appear in {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, on the right arm of the sofa in front of {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Diary|display=''Diary''}} contains passages that tell about {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}'s close relationship with the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule|link}}, her inauguration as a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}, and her concerns for {{Term|BotW|Zelda|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Riju}} about {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}, she reveals that they recently found her {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Diary|display=''Diary''}} and that {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} was once friends with the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|After {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} was tamed, we located {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa's diary}}. Since you're here, I shall tell you something from it... {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa}} had a beloved fried whom she thought the world of...{{Color|BotW Blue|the queen of Hyrule}}.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks further, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} will direct him towards the {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Diary|display=''Diary''}}, saying that {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} trusted him and therefore would not mind if he read it.<ref name="Riju"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa's diary}} is on the sofa. Since she trusted you, I'm certain she wouldn't mind you reading it.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} fights {{Term|BotW|Thunderblight Ganon|link}} again, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} has a chance to mention her {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Diary|display=''Diary''}} afterwards. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever comes upon it, she asks him to show it to {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|Should you happen upon my old diary, please show it to the princess.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> In particular, she wants {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} to see the section about the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a section about a dear friend of mine. Her mother. She left this world suddenly, leaving her sweet daughter behind. How the loss must have wounded her...|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref>
