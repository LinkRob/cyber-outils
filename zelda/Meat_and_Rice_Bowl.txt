{{Term/Store|Meat and Rice Bowl|Meat and Rice Bowls|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Meat and Rice Bowl}} are meals that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. If eaten, they'll replenish several {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Composed of rice and lightly seared meat, these dishes are considered a mainstay throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Additional ingredients can be used when making Meat and Rice Bowls, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. When eaten, they'll refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. These bowls completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. Eating these meals will temporarily increase Link's speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]] if he eats these dishes.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. They will increase Link's defense when eaten.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. If eaten, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} will be temporarily increased.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This dish of rice and lightly seared meat is a mainstay all throughout Hyrule.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding cold ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's [[Heat Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Chilly"/>
