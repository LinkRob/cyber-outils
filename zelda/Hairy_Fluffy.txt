'''Hairy Fluffy''' are docile sheep enemies in {{FPTRR}} that appear in the Deku Forest. They have no visible face.

==Characteristics and Variations==
===Hairy Fluffy===
{{Plural|FPTRR|Hairy Fluffy}} will attempt to avoid {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} if they spot him, and will all keep their distance if he gets into a battle. They tend to drop {{Plural|FPTRR|Twisted Tusk|link}} or {{Plural|FPTRR|Frizzy Carrot|link}} when defeated. As they are so timid yet appear in great numbers, it is useful to use {{Term|FPTRR|Recipes|section=One Love|display=One Love|link}} to gather them towards {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} before attacking.

===Gold Fluffy===
[[File:FPTRR  Gold Fluffy Sprite.png]]
'''Gold Fluffy''' are rare gold enemies that appear in place of normal {{Plural|FPTRR|Hairy Fluffy}}. If defeated, they will drop a large number of {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
The Japanese name for these monsters is {{Romanize|モコモコ|Mokomoko}}, which is onomatopoeia meaning "fluffy."
