{{Term/Store|Zora River Target Practice||Series, LCT}},<ref>{{Cite|Zora River Target Practice|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> also known as '''Stage 4-1''',<ref>{{Cite|Level 4-1|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> is a {{Term|LCT|Stage}} in {{LCT}}. It is the first {{Term|LCT|Stage}} of {{Term|LCT|Level 4|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|LCT|Zora River Target Practice}} takes place in the {{Term|LCT|Zora's River|link}} below {{Term|Series|Zora's Domain|link}}. During {{Term|LCT|Zora River Target Practice}}, {{Plural|LCT|Target|link}} will fall down the many waterfalls and move along the paths behind them.<ref>{{Cite|Take careful aim as the targets fall down the waterfall, and blast away!|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> The {{Term|LCT|Stage}} starts at the entrance to {{Term|Series|Zora River}} looking up at the waterfalls leading to {{Term|LCT|Zora's Domain}}. A {{Term|LCT|Sign|link}} stands at the entrance to the {{Term|LCT|Zora River}}, which {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} can shoot. The camera will then move to the biggest waterfall, with {{Plural|LCT|Jar|link}} on either side of the walls for {{Term|LCT|Link}} to shoot for points. Once the camera reaches the waterfall, four of the {{Plural|LCT|Jar}}, two on either side of the waterfall, will be out of reach of {{Term|LCT|Link}}'s {{Term|LCT|Crossbow|link}}. Only two {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} will be in reach once the camera stops. A {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow|link}} can be seen standing on a platform to the right of the waterfall for {{Term|LCT|Link}} to shoot. After the camera stops moving towards the waterfall, a horn will sound which starts the fall of {{Plural|LCT|Target}} down the waterfall. The first {{Term|LCT|Target}} falls down the middle of the waterfall, followed by one {{Term|LCT|Target}} on the left, and two {{Plural|LCT|Target}} on the right. Then, a {{Term|LCT|Target}} falls down the left side, while a fast-moving {{Term|LCT|Target}} falls down the middle. Another fast-moving {{Term|LCT|Target}} falls down the right side, followed by a fast-moving {{Term|LCT|Target}} to the left of two slow-moving {{Plural|LCT|Target}}. Another {{Term|LCT|Target}} falls down the left side of the waterfall, and then a short break of no falling {{Plural|LCT|Target}} occurs. Afterward, a {{Term|LCT|Target}} falls down the middle of the waterfall with two X-marked {{Plural|LCT|Target}} underneath and above it. After the X-marked {{Plural|LCT|Target}} pass, two {{Plural|LCT|Target}} will fall on the left and right side of the waterfall. One is a normal red {{Term|LCT|Target}}, while the other is a gold {{Term|LCT|Target}}. After the two {{Plural|LCT|Target}} pass or are shot by {{Term|LCT|Link}}, a whistle will sound, making the camera move to the next area.

The camera leaves the largest waterfall and moves to a smaller waterfall to the left of the biggest one. On a path left of the smaller waterfall, three {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} sit side by side. An {{Term|LCT|Orange Rupee|link}} can be discovered if {{Term|LCT|Link}} shoots the leftmost of the three {{Plural|LCT|Jar}}. A {{Term|LCT|Jar}} with a different design sits next to the small waterfall to the right of the three {{Plural|LCT|Jar}}. If {{Term|LCT|Link}} had shot the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}} from the earlier area 8 or more times and then shot the head, a new {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}} will appear in between the {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} on the left and right. Another horn will sound once the camera reaches its stopping point, starting the movement of the {{Plural|LCT|Target}}. Two {{Plural|LCT|Target}} will move along the path behind the small waterfall, starting near the three {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} and moving out of range behind the waterfall. Once these two {{Plural|LCT|Target}} start moving, another {{Term|LCT|Target}} will appear behind the small waterfall, moving towards the starting point of the first two {{Plural|LCT|Target}}. Another {{Term|LCT|Target}} will fall down the small waterfall as these three {{Plural|LCT|Target}} move. Once these four {{Plural|LCT|Target}} pass by or are shot, three more {{Plural|LCT|Target}} will appear exactly the way the first two did, moving from the left of the path to the right. Once these three {{Plural|LCT|Target}} start moving, a small gold {{Term|LCT|Target}} will appear behind the waterfall and move right. Two {{Plural|LCT|Target}} will fall down the waterfall at the same time the gold {{Term|LCT|Target}} starts moving. Three {{Plural|LCT|Target}} appear from behind the waterfall side by side, with the middle {{Term|LCT|Target}} small like the gold {{Term|LCT|Target}} from before. Three more small {{Plural|LCT|Target}} will appear from the left side of the path, with the last one being a gold {{Term|LCT|Target}}.  While all these {{Plural|LCT|Target}} move around the path, another {{Term|LCT|Target}} falls quickly down the waterfall. In the end, one last {{Term|LCT|Target}} falls down the waterfall. The whistle will then sound, signaling the camera to move to the final area.

{{Term|LCT|Link}} will be taken to the largest waterfall again, but this time facing down towards the river below instead of directly facing the waterfall. This allows {{Term|LCT|Link}} to shoot any of the {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} missed from the first area. One final horn will sound, starting the flow of {{Plural|LCT|Target}} down the waterfalls. Nine {{Plural|LCT|Target}} will fall down the middle of the largest waterfall in a 3×3 array, making a square, with an extra {{Term|LCT|Target}} above the square in the middle. The middle {{Term|LCT|Target}} of the top row of {{Plural|LCT|Target}} is an X-marked {{Term|LCT|Target}}, instead of a regular {{Term|LCT|Target}}. While these {{Plural|LCT|Target}} fall down the waterfall, a gold {{Term|LCT|Target}} floats down a smaller waterfall to the right of the largest waterfall. Five {{Plural|LCT|Target}} will then fall the largest waterfall, one after another. The first {{Term|LCT|Target}} falls on the left of the waterfall, the second in the middle, the third on the right, the fourth again in the middle, and the fifth on the left. The fifth {{Term|LCT|Target}} in this pattern is also an X-marked {{Term|LCT|Target}}. Three more {{Plural|LCT|Target}} fall directly above the last target side by side. After these {{Plural|LCT|Target}} appear, another gold {{Term|LCT|Target}} falls down the smaller waterfall on the right. The last {{Plural|LCT|Target}} that fall are another 3×3 square of targets falls down the largest waterfall, with four of them being X-marked {{Plural|LCT|Target}}. The top-right corner {{Term|LCT|Target}}, bottom-middle {{Term|LCT|Target}}, bottom-right {{Term|LCT|Target}}, and the middle-left {{Plural|LCT|Target}} are all X-marked.

If {{Term|LCT|Link}} had shot both {{Plural|LCT|Scarecrow}} eight or more times and then both of their heads, {{Term|LCT|Link}} will be transported to a different final area near the entrance of {{Term|LCT|Zora's Domain}} atop the largest waterfall. Two {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} sit on the left side of the entrance, while another three sit on the right side. A final horn sounds, starting the flow of {{Plural|LCT|Target}}. All {{Plural|LCT|Target}} floating down the river float side by side in groups of three. The first group is two X-marked {{Plural|LCT|Target}} on the left and right sides, with a gold {{Term|LCT|Target}} in the middle. The second group is identical to the first group but inverted with an X-marked {{Term|LCT|Target}} in the middle and two gold {{Plural|LCT|Target}} on the left and right. The third group is composed of two regular {{Plural|LCT|Target}} on the left and right and a gold {{Term|LCT|Target}} in the middle. Like the first and second groups, the fourth group is an inverted version of the third group. The left and right {{Plural|LCT|Target}} are gold {{Plural|LCT|Target}} and a regular {{Term|LCT|Target}} is in the middle. The fifth group is completely made up of X-marked {{Plural|LCT|Target}}, and the sixth group is completely made up of gold {{Plural|LCT|Target}}. The seventh group is the same as the first group, and the eighth group is made up of two gold {{Plural|LCT|Target}} on the left and right, with an X-marked {{Term|LCT|Target}} in the middle. The final group, the ninth group, is two X-marked {{Plural|LCT|Target}} on the left and right, with a gold {{Term|LCT|Target}} in the middle. After this final group appears, no more {{Plural|LCT|Target}} appear and {{Term|LCT|Link}} can shoot the remaining {{Plural|LCT|Target}} until time runs out.
