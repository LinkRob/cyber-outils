The {{Term/Store|Queen of Hearts||Series, TFH}} is an {{Term|TFH|Item|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 135}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Queen of Hearts gives you three extra Heart Containers. I never cease to amaze, do I?}}

The {{Term|TFH|Queen of Hearts}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|TFH|Mystery Jade|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Fairy Dust|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Star Fragment}}, one {{Term|TFH|Exquisite Lace|link}} and 1,200 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. It becomes available for purchase after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Water Temple|link}}, the {{Term|TFH|Fire Temple|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Ice Temple|link}}.

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Queen of Hearts}} increases the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}' total {{Plural|TFH|Heart Container|link}} by three, as made evident by the three {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} on its front.

The {{Term|TFH|Queen of Hearts}} and the {{Term|TFH|Jack of Hearts}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit}} appear to be based on characters from the book ''{{Wp|Alice's Adventures in Wonderland}}''.
