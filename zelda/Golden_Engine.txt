The {{Term/Store|Golden Engine||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Golden Engine|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Golden Engine}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of two {{Plural|ST|Pearl Necklace|link}}, one {{Term|ST|Ruto Crown|link}} and one {{Term|ST|Goron Amber|link}}. It features a stately design with a solid gold body, and its description reads "Even the smoke on this spectacular wonder seems to have some gold in it."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Golden Engine}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Golden Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to eight Hearts; more than is possible with any other combination of {{Plural|ST|Train Car}}. Using the {{Term|ST|Golden Engine}} also changes the {{Term|ST|Whistle|link}} to a hollow, metallic sound with a faint twinkling effect.
