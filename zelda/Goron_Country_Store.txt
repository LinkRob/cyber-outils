{{Term/Store|Goron Country Store||Series, ST}} is a location in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Goron Country Store / Up Ahead →|Sign|ST}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Goron Country Store is a small shop that is operated by [[Tengoron]] and located near the entrance of the [[Goron Village]]. It offers basic items for sell; however, most of the items sold here are more expensive than in other stores found throughout the four realms. For example, the [[shield]] sold here costs 200 [[Rupee]]s, while in the [[Castle Town Shop]] in the [[Forest Realm]] costs only 80 Rupees. Moreover, buying 10 [[Bomb]]s in the [[Papuchia Shop]] is only 150 Rupees, making it cheaper than the Goron Country Store.

Once the Quiver Upgrade has been bought, the shop will sell [[Prize Postcard]]s for 100 Rupees in its place.
