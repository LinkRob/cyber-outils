{{Term/Store|Kane||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a sailor residing on [[Windfall Island]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> Kane will say different things, depending on the [[decorations]] above the gate of Windfall. He rewards Link with 50 [[Rupee]]s if Link places a [[Shop Guru Statue]] atop the gate, and another 50 Rupees if he does so with the [[Postman Statue]] instead. He gives Link 20 Rupees if all six pedestals on the gate contain flags (including the [[Pinwheel]]) and another 20 Rupees if all six pedestals contain [[Fountain Idol]]s, [[Skull Tower Idol]]s, or a mixture.
