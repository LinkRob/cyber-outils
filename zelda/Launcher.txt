==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} featured in several {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. They are piston-like devices that jut outwards periodically. Alternatively, some {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} only move once they have been activated by certain mechanisms, such as a {{Term|BotW|Crystal|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}}, or a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}. Anything that is placed in front of a {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} when it extends itself will be pushed forward, including {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. However, certain {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} are more gentle than others, allowing for a slower ascent. Some {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} point straight upwards, while others are positioned at an angle.
