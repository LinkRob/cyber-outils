{{Term/Store|Imp Poe|Imp Poes|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 186}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TPHD|Imp Poe}} possess a unique doll-like appearance, and carry a large scythe with which they use to attack. Their {{Plural|TPHD|Lantern|link}} are instead attached to their feet. When {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} is a human, only their floating {{Plural|TPHD|Lantern}} can be seen. When an {{Term|TPHD|Imp Poe}} is about to strike {{Term|TPHD|Link}}, it will glow brightly if seen through {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}'s senses. {{Plural|TPHD|Imp Poe}} only come out at night and can only be defeated as {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} by using his senses. Once an {{Term|TPHD|Imp Poe}} has been damaged, it will fall to the ground and its {{Term|TPHD|Poe Soul|link|display= Soul}} will become visible, which can be ripped out and collected by performing the {{Term|TPHD|Ending Blow|link}} on the {{Term|TPHD|Imp Poe}} while still in {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link||display= Wolf form}}.

A man in {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle Town|link}} named {{Term|TPHD|Jovani|link}} was cursed for his greed and had his soul stolen by {{Plural|TPHD|Imp Poe}}, of which there are a total of 60 throughout {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm {{Color|TPHD Green|Jovani}}. I...became consumed by greed long ago, and sold my soul to a dark creature that did this to me... I can't move... I can't go see my girlfriend... My pet cat {{Color|TPHD Animal Green|Gengle}} is frozen on my head... I don't think I could be more miserable... I have to ask you a favor, doggie... Can you find and defeat the {{Color|TPHD Red|20 ghosts that lurk in the dark}} for me? I think if you can free the pieces of my {{Color|TPHD Red|soul}} from the {{Color|TPHD Red|ghosts}} that hold them, I'll be free...|Jovani|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But...my body is still golden... Does this mean my {{Color|TPHD Red|soul}} is still not whole? That's strange... Maybe we need the souls of {{Color|TPHD Red|60 ghosts}}... Can I ask another favor of you, doggie? Can you defeat more {{Color|TPHD Red|ghosts}}?|Jovani|TP}}</ref> Collecting 20 {{Plural|TPHD|Poe Soul}} will break part of the curse on {{Term|TPHD|Jovani}},<ref>{{Cite|Once the rain stops, you can come back in here by digging around the {{Color|TPHD Red|front wall where the cats gather}}… Understand, doggie?|Jovani|TP}}</ref> and he will give {{Term|TPHD|Link}} a {{Term|TPHD|Bottle|link}} full of {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears|link}} as thanks, as well as the {{Term|TPHD|Ghost Lantern|link}} in {{TPHD}}. After all 60 {{Plural|TPHD|Poe Soul}} have been collected, the curse will lift completely, and {{Term|TPHD|Jovani}}'s cat, {{Term|TPHD|Gengle|link}}, will give {{Term|TPHD|Link}} 200 {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}} every time he enters the room thereafter. In {{TPHD|-}}, the number of {{Plural|TPHD|Imp Poe}} defeated and remaining in each region are tracked on the map screen. Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TPHD|Ghost Lantern}}, it will glow if there is an {{Term|TPHD|Imp Poe}} nearby in the general area.
