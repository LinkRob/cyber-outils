{{Term/Store|Toxic Water|Toxic Water|Series, OoT}} is a recurring trap in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= CE |publisher= Nintendo |page= 83{{Exp Game|OoT}} }}</ref> {{Term|Series|Toxic Water}} goes unnamed in {{OoT3D}}, {{MM}}, {{OoA}}, and {{FSA}}.

==Location and uses==
Toxic Water is a poisonous type of [[Water (Terrain)|Water]] that slowly drains [[Link]]'s health while he is in it.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
Toxic Water first appears in {{OoT}}. It drains Link's health slowly and is a murky green color. It is first encountered in the [[Royal Family's Tomb]] which [[Sharp]] and [[Flat]] guard. It also appears in various other areas, such as the [[Bottom of the Well]].

==={{MM|-}}===
Toxic Water also appears in {{MM}}, where it can be found in the some places at the [[Southern Swamp]] area, along with the [[Woodfall]] area, and in the Woodfall Temple. This causes damage to Link's health, but may be safely hopped across with [[Deku Link]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
The polluted [[Zora Seas]] in {{OoA}} were green and lumpy until Link assisted the Great Fairy in cleansing them. If Link tries to swim through the Toxic Water, he will be injured and be pushed back.

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, Toxic Water appears purple in color in [[The Swamp]]. The [[Links]] may pass through Toxic Water, but this takes away their health in the process.
