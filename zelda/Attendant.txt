The {{Term/Store|Attendant||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}},<ref name="CaC"/> introduced in {{TCB}} [[DLC]] pack.

==Biography==
[[File:BotW cutscene attendant.png|thumb|200px|left|Two attendants with [[Princess Zelda]]]]
The {{Term|BotW|Attendant}} was a servant to {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} and the rest of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} before the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref name="CaC"/> Her duty was to accompany {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} when she met with the leaders of other nations.<ref name="CaC"/>

During the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}, she can be seen alongside {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} as she recruits {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} become a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪Recall {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}'s Champion of sand,<br/>before she was lost to the land.♪<br/>♪Chief {{Color|BotW Blue|Urbosa}}'s heart was strong,<br/>so the princess came along.♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} would accept {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} request,<ref>{{Cite|Princess of Hyrule. I accept your proposition without hesitation.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} would thank her for on behalf of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gerudo chief Urbosa... One behalf of Hyrule and its king, I thank you.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>
