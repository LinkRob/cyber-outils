The '''Dolphin Jump''' is a technique performed by {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link|link}} that allows him to jump out of the water onto a piece of land very quickly and efficiently.{{Name Ref|MM}}

==How to Perform==
To perform the {{Term|MM3D|Dolphin Jump}}, {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link}} must dive down and start swimming by holding A. Once {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link}} is swimming, the player must press and hold down on the analog stick. When {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} reaches the surface, he will breach it and leap into the air. This technique makes the fight with the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple|link}} {{Term|MM3D|Boss}}, {{Term|MM3D|Gyorg (Boss)|link}}, much easier and can also save time, as it will allow {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to jump onto platforms instead of climbing up them.

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese version of {{MM}}, {{Term|MM|Link}} must utilize this technique to reach the {{Term|MM|Marine Research Lab|link}}, as the water level is not high enough for him to climb onto it. This was changed in international versions.
