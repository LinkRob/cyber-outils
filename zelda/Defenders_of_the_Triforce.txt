'''Defenders of the Triforce''' is a ''Zelda''-themed live action role playing adventure built in collaboration with the Japanese company SCRAP.

==Overview==
Defenders of the Triforce created teams of six people who collaborate to solve puzzles, collect items, and save the world. The event was held in eight American cities and six Canadian cities beginning January 31, 2017, and ending July 9, 2017. The timing coincided with the release of the [[Nintendo Switch]] and {{BotW}} on March 3, 2017. The live action adventure used the puzzle solving aspect of {{TLoZ|Series}} to immerse players in the event which includes recurring items of the franchise. SCRAP boasted a 10% success rate of players who complete all of the challenges in its promotional material. It was available exclusively in English and French.

===How to Play===
The events lasted one hour each. Although 100 to 240 people played simultaneously, each team of six acted on their own and could not team up with or compete with each other. An MC guided the teams through the story and the goal.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Enjoy the introduction from our talented MC, who will explain the game's story and your goal to escape.|author= |published= |retrieved= February 11, 2017|url= http://scrapzelda.com/how-to-play/|title= How to Play|site= Defenders of the Triforce website|type=}}</ref> Each team had its own table but needed to explore the entire game space in order to find all of the clues.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= During the game, your team will have their own table. This is your base of operations, but you will need to get up and explore the game space in order to find all the clues.|author= |published= |retrieved= February 11, 2017|url= http://scrapzelda.com/how-to-play/|title= How to Play|site= Defenders of the Triforce website|type=}}</ref> Teams which did not escape could choose to receive the solutions at the end of the event.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Hey, listen! to the solution walkthrough at the end - just in case you aren't able to escape.|author= |published= |retrieved= February 11, 2017|url= http://scrapzelda.com/how-to-play/|title= How to Play|site= Defenders of the Triforce website|type=}}</ref> No prizes were given to those who completed the challenge, but all players were invited to an event following the game featuring video trailers and merchandise.
