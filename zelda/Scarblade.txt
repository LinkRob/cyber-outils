==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Scarblade}} is the second youngest member of the {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}. He lives in a waterfall cave on the northeast side of {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}. He will teach {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to release his {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack|link}} more quickly.<ref>{{Cite|Yes! Then I will teach you how to more quickly unleash your {{Color|TMC Red|Spin Attack}}, which you have already learned!|Splitblade|TMC}}</ref> His dojo can only be accessed once {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Grayblade|link}} and can swim to it using the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}.
