{{Term/Store|Furnace|Furnaces|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|You know the unlit {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}} on the wall just outside the laboratory?|Purah|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Furnaces are found outside the [[Hateno Ancient Tech Lab]] and the [[Akkala Ancient Tech Lab]]. They can be lit with a [[Blue Flame]], which will then power the [[Guidance Stone|Guidance Stones]] inside of the labs.<ref name="Guidance Stone">{{Cite|This sweet little {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} will start working once you do that, allowing us to restore your missing {{Color|BotW Red|runes}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Get the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from {{Color|BotW Blue|Tumlea Heights}}, put it in the {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}}, and then {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}...I mean...the ancient oven will be fixed.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> At the beginning of the game, both furnaces will be unlit.

When [[Link]] arrives at the Hateno Ancient Tech Lab, [[Symin]] will notice that Link's [[Sheikah Slate]] is missing its basic [[Rune|Runes]].<ref>{{Cite|Strange... It doesn't seem like you have the {{Color|BotW Red|basic runes}}...|Symin|BotW}}</ref> [[Purah]] offers to restore them,<ref>{{Cite|And to help the one true hero, I, the one and only Purah, will restore the {{Color|BotW Red|basic functions}} missing from the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> but only if Link lights the Furnace outside and activates the lab's Guidance Stone.<ref>{{Cite|Could you please bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} in town? Oh, and also use it to light our {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}}?|Purah|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Guidance Stone"/> Once Link does this, the [[Travel Gate]] for the Hateno Ancient Tech Lab will be activated.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab is now set as a travel gate.}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref> Inside, Purah points out that the Guidance Stone is reacting to the Blue Flame.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} must be starting to react! There's nothing mysterious about it—this is pure science!|Purah|BotW}}</ref>

At the [[Akkala Ancient Tech Lab]], [[Robbie]] offers to provide Link [[Ancient Soldier Gear]], which is made by the [[Ancient Oven]].<ref>{{Cite|And on that note... Sorry for the long preamble, but I'd like to provide you with some {{Color|BotW Red|ancient soldier gear}}. {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}...erm, the ancient oven... creates ancient soldier gear. But I fear she's out of sorts at the moment.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> However, since the Furnace outside is unlit, the Blue Flame needs to be retrieved from [[Tumlea Heights]].<ref>{{Cite|Hm. I'm afraid someone will have to bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} again...|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> When Link successfully lights the Furnace, the Travel Gate outside of the lab will be activated.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab has been added to your map as a travel gate.}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref> Robbie shares his excitement that the Ancient Oven is working once again, believing it was due to the power of his love.<ref>{{Cite|You! Link! Listen up, hm! My darling {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}} is back to normal! Such is the power of love... My love made {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}} well again!|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> Link then informs him that he lit the Furnace, which Robbie thanks him for.<ref>{{Cite|[The furnace is lit.] ... Oh... Well, thank you. Yes, quite.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref>
