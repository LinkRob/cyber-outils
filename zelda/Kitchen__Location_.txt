==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}} is a building located within {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. It features the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}}'s only {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, which rests above a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} in the center of its floor.

{{Term|BotW|Genli|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Amali|link}} both frequent the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}} during the day. When spoken to, she will ask who {{Term|BotW|Link}} is and if he came to stop {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Who are you? Some kind of traveler? Did you come to make that big bird up in the sky stop bothering us?|Genli|BotW}}</ref> At night, he can encounter {{Term|BotW|Amali}} standing by the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}, and she wonders if {{Term|BotW|Link}} has anyone to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}} for.<ref>{{Cite|Traveler...who do you usually cook for?|Amali|BotW}}</ref> She goes on to explain that when she makes dinner for her family, she thinks about their future, but these dark thoughts disappear after she eats.<ref>{{Cite|When I cook dinner for my daughters, I sometimes wonder what the future holds for this village...for my family. My thoughts tend to grow a bit dark then, but they lighten as soon as I satisfy my hunger.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Amali}} encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}} to make {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} for himself.<ref>{{Cite|So go ahead and use the pot. A full stomach will do you well.|Amali|BotW}}</ref>

After completing the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kheel|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest|link}}. She complains that her sisters have forgotten about practicing for their recital, and she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to help retrieve them.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Grrrrrr! My sisters are dumb DUMMIES!}} They promised we'd rehearse together, but NO ONE showed up! We have to perform our song for the {{Color|BotW Blue|elder}} at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}}! We're going to be laughed off the stage... Hey, you... You look like I can use you... Thaaaat is to say...you look like a nice guy who likes to help people in need. Can you go tell my sisters in Rito Village to stop ditching rehearsal? They won't listen to me, but they might listen to a scary-looking Hylian like you.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} believes that {{Term|BotW|Genli}} may difficult to convince, as she is likely in the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}} demanding {{Term|BotW|Salmon Muenière|link}}.<ref name="Kheel"/> This begins the "{{Term|BotW|Recital at Warbler's Nest|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. True to {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}'s prediction, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}}, refusing to sing until she eats {{Term|BotW|Salmon Muenière}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hurry! Hurry! {{Big|I NEED {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} in my belly!}} I know {{Color|BotW Blue|Kheel}} wants me to practice singing with her, but I just can't. Not until I get my {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> Her other three sisters have all left to gather ingredients to make it for her.<ref>{{Cite|My sisters already left to get {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} ingredients. I told them I wouldn't sing until I get it. I didn't expect them to actually make it for me, though!|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} locates {{Term|BotW|Notts|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kotts|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Cree|link}}, they will each give {{Term|BotW|Link}} an ingredient on the condition that he return to the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}} to make {{Term|BotW|Salmon Muenière}} for {{Term|BotW|Genli}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well then...I guess I'll give the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}} to you! Please make the best {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli!|Notts|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please take this {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}} and go make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli. Hopefully she'll feel like singing after she has some.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If I give you this {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}, will you make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli? She should be in the {{Color|BotW Red|kitchen}}.|Cree|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Genli}} with {{Term|BotW|Salmon Muenière}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, she is able to smell it and asks if he would give some to her.<ref>{{Cite|*sniff sniff...* *sniff sniff sniff* That amazing smell... Could it be? {{Big|Sa... Sa... {{Color|BotW Blue|Salmon meunière}}?!}} You're going to let me have some, right? RIGHT?!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> Should he agree, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} will immediately eat it.<ref>{{Cite|[Enjoy!] YAAAAAAAAAAAAY! ♪Time to dig in!♪ *munch munch* Hngh mngh... Hnff! Hurk! Mmmmmm... I'm stuffed!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> After being reminded of her promise, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} will leave the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}} to join her sisters.<ref>{{Cite|...Huh? My promise to my sisters? I suppose I shouldn't break my promise... I really DO like singing! ...But I am too nervous to sing in front of other people. I have SERIOUS stage fright...but maybe practicing with my sisters will help me feel more comfortable. Anyway, thank you for the amazing {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}! It was so soft and flaky and delicious! I'll give this rehearsal my all. ♪Come listen later!♪|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

Once "{{Term|BotW|Recital at Warbler's Nest}}" has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Amali}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}} at all hours of the day. While she is here, she will speak to {{Term|BotW|Link}} about her husband and daughters.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Link. I hope my daughters are not causing you too much grief today.|Amali|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Link! You have brought my husband back to us! I really cannot thank you enough. He is singing with his daughters at his usual place. I am sure they could use an audience if you have some time to spare.|Amali|BotW}}</ref>
