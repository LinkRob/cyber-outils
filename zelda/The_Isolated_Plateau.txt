"{{Term/Store|The Isolated Plateau||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Isolated Plateau}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Great Plateau}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} descends the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man|link}} glides in on his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to talk with him. Seeing the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|display=Tower}}, he notes that the two of them have an enigma in front of them.<ref>{{Cite|My, my... It would seem we have quite the enigma here.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} activated the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower}}, it emerged from the ground along with the rest of the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This tower and others just like it have erupted across the land, on after another.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} likens it to a long-dormant power suddenly awakening.<ref>{{Cite|It is almost as though...a long-dormant power has awoken quite suddenly.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if anything odd happened on top of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you do not mind me asking... Did anything...odd occur while you were atop that tower?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells him that he heard a voice,<ref>{{Cite|[I heard a voice.] Well now! A voice, you say?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> he asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} recognized it.<ref>{{Cite|And did you happen to recognize this mysterious voice?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He is disappointed to hear that {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not know the voice.<ref>{{Cite|[No.] I see. Well, that is unfortunate.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

Changing the subject, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} points out {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} circling {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I assume you caught sight of that atrocity enshrouding the castle.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He explains that, 100 years ago, the {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Calamity}} brought {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} to ruin.<ref>{{Cite|That...is {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon.}} One hundred years ago, that vile entity brought the kingdom of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule}} to ruin.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> It appeared suddenly, destroying many innocent lives.<ref>{{Cite|It appeared suddenly and destroyed everything in its path. So many innocent lives were lost in its wake.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> While it has been just barely trapped in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} for a century, it has been building its strength and will soon be free.<ref>{{Cite|For a century, the very symbol of our kingdom, Hyrule Castle, has managed to contain that evil. But just barely. There it festers, building its strength for the moment it will unleash its blight upon the land once again. It would appear that moment is fast approaching...|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he intends to go to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}.<ref>{{Cite|I must ask you, courageous one... Do you intend to make your way to the castle?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> However, he informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} is surrounded on all sides by sheers cliffs,<ref>{{Cite|Here, on this isolate plateau, we are surrounded on all sides by steep cliffs, with no way down. If you were to try to jump off, well... no death could be more certain. Or more foolish.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> and the only way down would be with a {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}}.<ref>{{Cite|Of course, if you had a paraglider like mine, that would be quite another story.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} notices {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s interest in it,<ref>{{Cite|[Paraglider?] Oho! Piqued your interest, have I? Yes, I didn't come soaring down on my own feathery wings, you know!|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> so he decides to offer him a trade.<ref>{{Cite|Worry not—I will happily agree to give you my paraglider. But not for nothing. Let's see now... How about I trade it for a bit of treasure that slumbers nearby?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> This activates the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} has {{Term|BotW|Link}} follow him to a nearby hill.<ref>{{Cite|Come. Let me show you something.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He shows {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Oman Au Shrine|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Do you see that structure there? The one shining with a strange light?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> saying it began to glow when the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower}} emerged from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|It began glowing at the exact moment those towers rose up from the ground.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He believes there might be a treasure inside and tasks {{Term|BotW|Link}} with retrieving it in exchange for the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}}.<ref>{{Cite|I would think such a place might house some sort of treasure, wouldn't you? Treasure for the {{Color|BotW Blue|paraglider}}. A fair exchange, I believe.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed his first {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} appears to congratulate him for obtaining a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It seems you managed to get your hands on a {{Color|BotW Blue|Spirit Orb}}. Well done!|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} is confused as to how he knows this, he chalks it up to clairvoyance.<ref>{{Cite|[How did you know?!] {{Big|Clairvoance!}} Oho, or perhaps just something similar.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He explains that the older one gets, the more difficult it can become for them to see what it right before their eyes,<ref>{{Cite|As one gets older, it can become more difficult to see what is right before one's own eyes...|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> but things that were once hidden can become crystal clear.<ref>{{Cite|However, that which was once hidden from view can often be crystal clear.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> However, he acknowledges that this may not be true for everyone.<ref>{{Cite|But perhaps that is not true for everyone! Oho ho!|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

According to the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}}, the appearance of the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower}} and the awakening of the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} are connected to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The appearance of those towers and the awakening of this shrine... It is all connected to that {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} you carry on your hip there.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that it has been some time since I last saw the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|It has been quite some time since I have seen that {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}...|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He explains that, long ago, a highly advanced race known as the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} lived in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|display=Hyrule}},<ref>{{Cite|Long ago, a highly advanced tribe known as the Sheikah inhabited these lands.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> and their wisdom saved the kingdom several times over.<ref>{{Cite|The great power of their wisdom saved this kingdom time and time again.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> It is said that their {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} disappeared,<ref>{{Cite|But their ancient technology disappeared long ago... Or so it is said.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> but it is interesting to him that items have survived hidden away in Shrines.<ref>{{Cite|It is interesting, however, to think... how something like that survived all this time, hidden away in a shrine.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} continues, saying there are more {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} hidden all over {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|These shrines are tucked away in numerous places all across this land.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He reveals there are three more {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrines}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}},<ref>{{Cite|On this plateau alone, I believe there are still {{Color|BotW Red|three}} more.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> asking for the treasures in each of them in exchange for the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bring me the treasure from each of those shrines...and I will give you my {{Color|BotW Blue|paraglider}}.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} points out that this was not what he agreed to, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} claims to have changed his mind.<ref>{{Cite|[That wasn't the deal!] Oh? Well, I suppose I changed my mind. I'm sure that won't be a problem for a young go-getter like you!|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks why he needs more now, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} recalls that he never said there would only be one treasure.<ref>{{Cite|[So I need more now?] I said "treasure," but I never said there would only be one treasure.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

Feeling generous, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a tip for finding more {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|Since I'm feeling generous, I will also teach you a trick for finding shrines.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that it is best to survey the area from a high point,<ref>{{Cite|It's always best to survey the area by {{Color|BotW Red|looking around from a high point}}. Let's see here...|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> suggesting he try the top of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower}}.<ref>{{Cite|How about you make your way to the top of that tower, again?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks if he is joking, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} shares another trick with him.<ref>{{Cite|[Are you joking?] Oho ho! I am afraid not. But do not worry! I have another little trick to share with you for your effort.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can open the {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} to instantly travel to {{Plural|BotW|Travel Gate|link}} he has activated.<ref>{{Cite|Take a look at the {{Color|BotW Blue|map}} on your {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}. See those {{Color|BotW Blue|blue icons}}? You should recognize the cave where you woke, the shrine you came from, and the tower. You can travel instantly to any of those places with the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He claims to have heard this some time ago, but he does not know if that is actually how it works.<ref>{{Cite|Or so I heard quite some time ago... I do not know if it actually works as such.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the fourth {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|display=Paraglides}} down to meet him outside.<ref>{{Cite|With this, you have now acquired all of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spirit Orbs}} from the shrines on this plateau.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He decides it is time to tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} everything.<ref>{{Cite|That means...it is finally time. Link, it is finally time for me to tell you everything. But first...|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to imagine an "X", with each of its points being one of the shrines, and to find the spot where the lines intersect.<ref>{{Cite|Imagine an X on your map, with the four shrines as the end points. Find the spot {{Color|BotW Red|where those lines intersect}}.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> There, he will be waiting for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I shall wait for you there.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he understands as he disappears into green lights.<ref>{{Cite|Do you understand? Where two lines connecting the shrines would cross... There...I will...be waiting...|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

The location the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} is referring to is the {{Term|BotW|Temple of Time|link}}. He can be found in the room on top of it, waiting. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} arrives, he congratulates him and says it is time to share his true identity.<ref>{{Cite|Well done there, young one! Now, then... The time has come to show you who I truly am.|Old Man!|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} reveals himself to be {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}}, the last king of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|I was king Rhoam Bosphoramus Hyrule. I was...the last leader of Hyrule. A kingdom which no longer exists.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> With a flash, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}}'s appearance changes to that of {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}}.

When the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}} struck, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} lost his life.<ref>{{Cite|The Great Calamity was merciless... It devastated everything in its path, lo, a century ago. It was then that my life was taken away from me.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Since then, he has remained on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}} in the form of a spirit.<ref>{{Cite|And since that time, here I have remained, in spirit form.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> The reason he kept his identity a secret from {{Term|BotW|Link}} was to not overwhelm him while he memory was still fragile.<ref>{{Cite|I did not think it wise to overwhelm you while your memory was still fragile. So rather than that, I thought it best to assume a temporary form. Forgive me.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He believes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is now ready to hear the tale of what happened 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|I think you are now ready. Ready to hear what happened 100 years ago.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

In an age long past, {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} was once a man, but he has since been transformed into {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|To know Calamity Ganon's true form, one must know the story from an age long past. The demon king was born into this kingdom, but his transformation into Malice created the horror you see now.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Stories of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}} were passed down in the form of legends and fairy tales.<ref>{{Cite|Stories of Ganon were passed from generation to generation in the form of legends and fairy tales.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> There was also a prophecy,<ref>{{Cite|But there was also...a prophecy.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> stating "The signs of a resurrection of Calamity Ganon are clear. And the power to oppose it lies dormant beneath the ground."<ref>{{Cite|"The signs of a resurrection of Calamity Ganon are clear. And the power to oppose it lies dormant beneath the ground."|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

Following the prophecy, excavations began on large areas of land.<ref>{{Cite|We decided to heed the prophecy and began excavating large areas of land.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> In the process, they found the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}, giant machines made to be piloted by warriors.<ref>{{Cite|It wasn't long before we discovered several ancient relics made by the hands of our distant ancestors. These relics, the Divine Beasts, were giant machines piloted by warriors.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> In addition, they found the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}, which coincided with ancient legends.<ref>{{Cite|We also found the Guardians, an army of mechanical soldiers who fought autonomously. This coincided with ancient legends, oft repeated throughout our land.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Their research also informed them of an ancient [[Princess_Zelda#Ten_Thousands_Years_prior_to_Breath_of_the_Wild|princess with a sacred power]] and a [[Link#The_Hero_10.2C000_Years_Prior_to_Breath_of_the_Wild|knight]] chosen by the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link|display=sword that seals the darkness}},<ref>{{Cite|We also learned of a princess with a sacred power and her appointed knight, chosen by the sword that seals the darkness.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> who were able to seal {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} away with the power of the relics.<ref>{{Cite|It was they who sealed Ganon away using the power of these ancient relics.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

100 years ago, a {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link|display=princess}} was set to inherit her sacred power, with a knight by her side.<ref>{{Cite|One hundred years ago, there was a princess set to inherit a sacred power and a skilled knight at her side.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Four {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} were selected and tasked with piloting their {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|We selected four skilled individuals from across Hyrule and tasked them with the duty of piloting the Divine Beasts. With the princess as their commander, we dubbed these pilots Champions—a name that would solidify their unique bond.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Just as the princess, the knight, and the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} were on the brink of sealing away {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}},<ref>{{Cite|The princess, her appointed knight, and the rest of the Champions were on the brink of sealing away Ganon...|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> he responded with his own plan.<ref>{{Cite|But nay... Ganon was cunning, and he responded with a plan beyond our imagining.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Appearing from below {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}, he took control of the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} and {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|He appeared from deep below Hyrule Castle, seized control of the Guardians and the Divine Beasts, and turned them against us.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> The {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} and those residing in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}} lost their lives,<ref>{{Cite|The Champions lost their lives. Those residing in the castle as well.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> and the appointed knight collapsed while defending the princess.<ref>{{Cite|The appointed knight, gravely wounded, collapsed while defending the princess...|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}} was devastated by the {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Calamity}}.<ref>{{Cite|And thus, the kingdom of Hyrule was devastated absolutely by Calamity Ganon.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

The princess survived and faced {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} on her own.<ref>{{Cite|However... The princess survived... to face Ganon alone.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> The princess can be seen using her power on {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}, telling {{Term|BotW|Link}} he is their final hope and the fate of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|display=Hyrule}} rests with him.<ref>{{Cite|Link... You are our final hope. The fate of Hyrule rests with you.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} reveals that the princess was his daughter, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}},<ref>{{Cite|That princess was my own daughter... My dear Zelda.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Link}} was her fallen knight.<ref>{{Cite|And the courageous knight who protected her right up to the very end... That knight was none other than you, Link.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> When he collapsed, he was taken to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}, which brought him back 100 years later.<ref>{{Cite|You fought valiantly when your fate took an unfortunate turn. And then, you were taken to the Shrine of Resurrection. Here you now stand revitalized, 100 years later.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

The voice {{Term|BotW|Link}} has heard since he woke up is that of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}},<ref>{{Cite|The words of guidance you have been hearing since your awakening are from Princess Zelda herself.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> calling out to him as she keeps {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} restrained.<ref>{{Cite|Even now, as she works to restrain Ganon from within Hyrule Castle, she calls out for your help.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Soon {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} power will be exhausted,<ref>{{Cite|However, my daughter's power will soon be exhausted.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> and nothing will be left to stop {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}} from destroying {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|display=Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|Once that happens, Ganon will freely regenerate himself and nothing will stop him from consuming our land.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} feels ashamed to ask for {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s help when he could not save {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|display=Hyrule}} himself,<ref>{{Cite|Considering that I could not save my own kingdom, I have no right to ask this of you, Link...|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> but he recognizes it is the only thing he can do.<ref>{{Cite|But I am powerless here...|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He begs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to save {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} and anhnihilate {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|You must save here...my daughter. And do whatever it takes to annihilate Ganon.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

Even after a century, the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} and {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} are still under the control of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|Somehow, Ganon has maintained control over all four Divine Beasts, as well as those Guardians swarming around Hyrule Castle.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Considering it reckless for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to head straight to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}},<ref>{{Cite|I believe it would be quite reckless for you to head directly to the castle at this point.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} instead directs him to {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, who can tell him more about the path ahead of him.<ref>{{Cite|I suggest...that you make your way east, out to one of the villages in the wilderness. Follow the road out to Kakariko Village. There you will find the elder, Impa. She will tell you more about the path that lies ahead.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

After finishing his story, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}} he promised.<ref>{{Cite|Go on... Here is the {{Color|BotW Blue|paraglider}}, just as I promised.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} receives it, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} will be completed.
