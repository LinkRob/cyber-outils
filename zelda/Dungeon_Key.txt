{{Term/Store|Dungeon Key|Dungeon Keys|Series, LA, LADX|plural}} are {{Plural|LANS|Item|link}} in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Dungeon Keys<br/>These keys open the entrances to the dungeons. ==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|LA|Dungeon Keys|These keys open the entrances to dungeons. If you do not have the correct key for a dungeon, you will not be able to enter it.}}
{{Plural|LADX|Dungeon Key}} are required to open certain [[Dungeon]]s.

===Tail Key===
{{Main|Tail Key}}
[[File:LADX Tail Key Sprite.png|20px]]
The {{Term|LADX|Tail Key}} is found in the [[Mysterious Woods]].
{{Clear|left}}

===Slime Key===
{{Main|Slime Key}}
[[File:LADX Slime Key Sprite.png|20px]]
The {{Term|LADX|Slime Key}} is obtained by returning [[Prince Richard]] his five {{Plural|LADX|Golden Leaf|link}} found in [[Kanalet Castle]].
{{Clear|left}}

===Angler Key===
{{Main|Angler Key}}
[[File:LADX Angler Key Sprite.png|20px]]
The {{Term|LADX|Angler Key}} is dropped by the [[Lanmola]] located in the [[Yarna Desert]].
{{Clear|left}}

===Face Key===
{{Main|Face Key}}
[[File:LADX Face Key Sprite.png|20px]]
The {{Term|LADX|Face Key}} is dropped by the [[Armos Knight]] located in the [[Southern Face Shrine]].
{{Clear|left}}

===Bird Key===
{{Main|Bird Key}}
[[File:LADX Bird Key Sprite.png|20px]]
The {{Term|LADX|Bird Key}} is found deep within a cave located in [[Tal Tal Heights]].
{{Clear|left}}
