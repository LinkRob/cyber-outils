The {{Term/Store|Goma Asaagh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Goma Asaagh Shrine}}<br/>A Major Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Major Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Goma Asaagh Shrine is located in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} directly under {{Term|BotW|Hebra Peak|link}}, surrounded by pillars of ice. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must either melt the ice or find a way to go over the top of the ice to access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. As Link enters the Shrine, he is greeted by the Shrine's {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Goma Asaagh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Goma Asaagh. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Goma Asaagh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Goma Asaagh Shrine features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. As Link approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}} wielding an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe++|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear++|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword++|link}} will rise from a pit to challenge Link. The floor of the Shrine is covered by a thin layer of water, allowing pillars of ice to be created using {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} that can protect Link from the Guardian Scout IV. Link must defeat the Guardian Scout IV to cause a portcullis separating him from the Monk to rise.

Once Link has done so, Goma Asaagh will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Goma Asaagh|BotW}}</ref> and afterwards will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Goma Asaagh|BotW}}</ref>

A [[Treasure Chest]] behind the portcullis contains a {{Term|BotW|Royal Claymore|link}} with a potential supplementary effect.{{Note|The Royal Claymore may come with an effect, such as [[Critical Hit]], or it may appear as a default Royal Claymore.}}
