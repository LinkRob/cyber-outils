{{Term/Store|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vases|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are objects in {{TWW}}.{{Name Ref|TWWHD}}

==Location and Uses==
The {{Plural|TWWHD|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase}} are a set of {{Plural|TWWHD|Jar|link}} collected by [[Mila's Father]].<ref>{{Cite|Those happen to be extremely high-class bone-china vases that run a minimum of 10 Rupees each!|Mila's Father|TWW}}</ref> They are said to be the "kind of vase one wants to break as soon as one catches sight of them."<ref>{{Cite|His hobby was once collecting expensive vases, but unfortunately, they were the kind of vase one wants to break as soon as one catches sight of them.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> Eight of his Vases are displayed on the first floor of the {{Term|TWWHD|Auction House|link}}. If {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} breaks one, Mila's Father will call him a weasel but will let him off with a warning.<ref>{{Cite|You little WEASEL!!! Well, thank you SO much for coming into my home and breaking my high-priced vases! ...If you break any more, I assure you that I shall not let it go lightly! No, sir! Not at all! I shall require compensation!|Mila's Father|TWW}}</ref> Subsequent incidents will result in his being forced to compensate Mila's Father 10 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}} for each one he damages before he can leave,<ref>{{Cite|Wait just one moment! Do you think you can just break someone's vases and leave without paying for them? I must chortle at your naivete! Ho ho! Those happen to be extremely high-class bone-china vases that run a minimum of 10 Rupees each! Most assuredly, I shall now accept compensation for them! Let me see now... That comes to a total of... ''X'' Rupees! I shall accept your payment!|Mila's Father|TWW}}</ref> followed by an explanation of his guilt caused by his "destructive tomfoolery" and a dismissal from the {{Term|TWWHD|Auction House|display=House}}.<ref>{{Cite|...When you break someone else's vases, it's only natural that you compensate them for the loss! You, my boy, are in the wrong because of your destructive tomfoolery! And now, because of you, I'm going to be even richer...and I was quite rich before! The world is a cruel place, isn't it? Don't stand around looking like you've been swindled... Be on your way!|Mila's Father|TWW}}</ref> If {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} doesn't have the means to pay, Mila's Father will be outraged and forcefully remove him from the premises.<ref>{{Cite|...What's this nonsense?! Why, this is ridiculous! You don't have enough money! Be on your way!|Mila's Father|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hrrrgh! Of all the cheekiness! I can't BELIEVE you'd go around breaking people's vases when you so clearly lack the money to pay for them! Such rudeness!|Mila's Father|TWW}}</ref> The {{Plural|TWWHD|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase|display=Vases}} will be replaced before {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} returns to the {{Term|TWWHD|Auction House}}.

When ownership of the {{Term|TWWHD|Auction House|display=House}} passes to [[Maggie's Father]], Link can destroy the {{Plural|TWWHD|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase}} without any fear of consequences, as he doesn't seem to care about the {{Plural|TWWHD|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase|display=Vases}}.

[[Mrs. Marie]] was apparently also fond of these {{Plural|TWWHD|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase|display=Vases}}, as she keeps four at her [[Private Oasis]]. When the [[Cabana Deed]] passes to {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}, he can enter the Private Oasis and break the {{Plural|TWWHD|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase|display=Vases}}, which contain many {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}}. The {{Plural|TWWHD|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase}} will be replaced after {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} breaks them, presumably by the [[Oasis Butler]], but their monetary contents will not. 

==Trivia==
* Despite the supposed high value of the {{Plural|TWWHD|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} is charged the same amount of {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}} to replace the {{Plural|TWWHD|Jar|link}} belonging to [[Sue-Belle]] should he break them.
