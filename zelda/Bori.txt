'''Bori''' is the [[Sub-Boss]] of [[Wrecked Ship]] in {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
Bori is a jellyfish-like Monster found in the Wrecked Ship. Defeating it is the goal of the side quest [[The Zappy Shipwreck]].
====Appearance and Behavior====
Bori is a large, glowing creature that generates electricity. It is protected by swarms of [[Biri]], smaller jellyfish that rotate around it.
====Combat====
Bori’s Biri swarm shocks anything that gets too close. Players must dodge them and find openings to attack Bori directly. Defeating or disabling the Biri can make the fight easier.
====Side Quest: “The Zappy Shipwreck”====
Players must defeat Bori to clear the Wrecked Ship of dangerous electrical surges. Beating it rewards Zelda a [[Fairy Bottle]].

==Trivia==
*Bori fights very similarly to [[Barinade]] from {{OoT}}, and was likely a direct inspiration.
