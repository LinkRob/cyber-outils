'''Ancient Underground Fortress''' is a location in {{TotK}}. The area has large [[monster]] quantities and [[Niuzimod Lightroot]]. All of these Monsters are [[Gloom]]-based and inflcit Gloom damage.

[[Link]] can find the [[Trousers of Twilight]] in this area. There are [[Zonai Devices]] and elevators, which could mean it was operated by [[Zonai]].
