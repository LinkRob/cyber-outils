The {{Term/Store|Mother-and-Child Rocks|Mother-and-Child Rocks|Series, TP}} are a location in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|Reekfish live close to the two stone formations we call the Mother-and-Child Rocks. They've got a distinct smell that some find a little...gamey...but they're packed with nutrients, so they're revered and well cared for.|Zora|TP}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Mother-and-Child Rocks are a notable part of [[Zora's Domain]]. The structures can be found in the central waters near the base of the waterfall, and are the only area where the native [[Reekfish]] congregate. In the game, Link must catch a Reekfish in order to obtain the [[Reekfish Scent]] and thus find his way to [[Snowpeak Top]].
