The {{Term/Store|Phantom Greaves|Phantom Greaves|Series, BotW}} are an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Phantom Greaves}}<br/>Myths abound about armored phantoms that terrorized brave heroes. These greaves look just like the ones they supposedly wore. They're a rather rare find.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They are a piece of [[Armor]] exclusive to [[The Master Trials]] DLC Pack.

==Location and Uses==
Obtaining the Phantom Greaves is one part of the objective of the "[[EX Treasure: Phantasma]]" [[Side Quest]]. The Greaves were stolen from [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] by [[Misko]] and a clue to their location can be found in [[Misko's EX Journal]] at the [[Outpost Ruins]].<ref>{{Cite|I hid the eight priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Armor worn by fierce phantoms, feared even by heroes, is hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in the ruins of Hyrule Field}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at a battleground where brave souls tested their skill}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at ceremonial grounds where royal guards are honored}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where the soldiers of Hyrule gathered}}.|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> The [[Treasure Chest]] that hides the Phantom Greaves can be found buried in the southeast of the [[Hyrule Garrison Ruins]] near a ruined [[Guardian Stalker]]. After obtaining the Phantom Greaves, [[Granté]] will begin selling them for 50 [[Rupee]]s.{{Note|Prior to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild#Updates|Version 1.3.3 Update]], Granté would not sell any Armor that is exclusive to the Expansion Pass or The Master Trials DLC Pack. This meant that the Phantom Greaves could not be replaced if sold, which was noted in the description of the Greaves.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Phantom Greaves}}<br/>Myths abound about armored phantoms that terrorized brave heroes. These greaves look just like the ones they supposedly wore. If sold, this rare find can't be replaced.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>}}

The description of the Phantom Greaves were changed in the Version 1.3.3 Update as the Greaves could now be replaced.<ref name="Inventory"/> According to its description, the Phantom Greaves look just like the Greaves supposedly worn by [[Phantom]]s that tormented heroes.<ref name="Inventory"/> When worn, the Greaves provide one level of Attack Up. The Phantom Greaves cannot be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] nor can they be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Phantom Greaves, alongside the [[Phantom Helmet]] and [[Phantom Armor]], compose the [[Phantom Equipment]].
