==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Gourmet Poultry Pilafs are meals made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Raw Whole Bird]], [[Hylian Rice]], [[Goat Butter]], and a [[Bird Egg]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they restore a few {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Not only are they made with high-quality poultry, but they also flood one's mouth with flavor with every bite.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Additional ingredients can be added to Gourmet Poultry Pilaf, which can result in one of the following variations:

* '''Hearty Gourmet Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Gourmet Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Made with the highest-quality poultry, every bite of this pilaf floods your mouth with flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these pilafs will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Gourmet Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Gourmet Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Made with the highest-quality poultry, every bite of this pilaf floods your mouth with flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. If eaten, they will refill a part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Gourmet Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Gourmet Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Made with the highest-quality poultry, every bite of this pilaf floods your mouth with flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Consuming these will completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Gourmet Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Gourmet Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Made with the highest-quality poultry, every bite of this pilaf floods your mouth with flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. If they're eaten, Link's movement speed will receive a short boost.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Gourmet Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Gourmet Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Made with the highest-quality poultry, every bite of this pilaf floods your mouth with flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. When eaten, they'll grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Gourmet Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Gourmet Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Made with the highest-quality poultry, every bite of this pilaf floods your mouth with flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. Eating them will grant Link on level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Gourmet Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Gourmet Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Made with the highest-quality poultry, every bite of this pilaf floods your mouth with flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Link gains an increase to the power of his attacks when he eats these.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Gourmet Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Gourmet Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Made with the highest-quality poultry, every bite of this pilaf floods your mouth with flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. When eaten, they increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Gourmet Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Gourmet Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Made with the highest-quality poultry, every bite of this pilaf floods your mouth with flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. If eaten, Link will receive a temporary boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Gourmet Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Gourmet Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Made with the highest-quality poultry, every bite of this pilaf floods your mouth with flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding cold ingredients. When they're eaten, Link gains one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
