The {{Term/Store|Lost Desert of Hanman||Series, TSP}} is a location mentioned in {{TSP}}.<ref name="Evil">{{Cite Book|quote= 'Who beat Ganon's forces in the Lost Desert of Hanman? ==Features and Overview==
After the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule|link}} announces that {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|link}} will take the {{Term|TSP|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} to the {{Term|TSP|Fifth Castle of Ancient Hyrule|link}} due to it being dangerous in {{Term|TSP|North Castle|link}}, {{Term|TSP|Link|link}} furiously reminds the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule|display=King}} of all the brave deeds he has done and claims that {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|display=Charles}} cannot be trusted since he is evil.<ref name="Evil"/> One of the accomplishments he mentions is defeating {{Term|TSP|Ganon|link}}'s forces in the {{Term|TSP|Lost Desert of Hanman}}. No other information, such as its location or inhabitants, is given in the book.
