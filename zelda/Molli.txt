{{Term/Store|Molli||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Molli|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Molli}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} child who lives in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. She is the daughter of {{Term|BotW|Harth|link}}, and since she is close in age to {{Term|BotW|Tulin|link}}, their families spend a lot of time together.<ref name="Father"/> During the day, she sits on a ledge on the north end of the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}}. At night, she sleeps in the {{Term|BotW|House|link}} that she shares with her father.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Molli}}, he will find her talking about a {{Term|BotW|Bird|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A birdie!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> If spoken to, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants to hear a story.<ref>{{Cite|Want me to tell you a story?|Molli|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} state that he loves stories, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} reveals that she does as well.<ref>{{Cite|[I love stories!] Awesome! Me too!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> Her story begins with her {{Term|BotW|Grandpa|link}} stopping at a big {{Term|BotW|Cedar Tree|link}} on top of a mountain and looking to the northwest.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* Once upon a time... My grandpa stopped at a {{Color|BotW Red|big tree}} while he was climbing a big mountain and looked below him to the northwest.|Molli|BotW}}</ref> When he did so, he saw a huge white {{Term|BotW|Bird}}, which he started flying towards.<ref>{{Cite|When suddenly he saw {{Color|BotW Red|a HUGE snow-white birdie}} with its wings spread wide! My grandpa took of {{Color|BotW Red|flying after the snow-white birdie}}.|Molli|BotW}}</ref> Drawing close to the {{Term|BotW|Bird}}, he saw something in its stomach.<ref>{{Cite|And as he got closer to the white birdie, Grandpa saw {{Color|BotW Red|something SUPER important}} inside its belly! The birdie had swallowed—!|Molli|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} prompts {{Term|BotW|Molli}} to reveal what the {{Term|BotW|Bird}} swallowed, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} admits that she gets tired during this part of the story and does not remember.<ref>{{Cite|[Well? What was it?!] {{Small|...I always get sleepy around that part of the story...so I don't remember...}}|Molli|BotW}}</ref> However, she states that she knows the location of the {{Term|BotW|Cedar Tree}} where her {{Term|BotW|Grandpa}} stopped.<ref>{{Cite|OH! I know where the {{Color|BotW Red|big tree}} is, though!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> She points out the {{Term|BotW|Cedar Tree}} on {{Term|BotW|Talonto Peak|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's over there! You see it? Grandpa said he saw the {{Color|BotW Red|snow-white birdie}} from THAT tree!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Molli}} tries to recall what the {{Term|BotW|Bird}} swallowed, and she laments not being able to hear the story from her grandfather.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm hmm hmm... Now what did that snow-white birdie swallow? I can't hear the story from Grandpa anymore... *sniff*|Molli|BotW}}</ref> After this, "{{Term|BotW|The Bird in the Mountains|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Molli}} while the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} is active, she asks if he wants to hear the story again.<ref>{{Cite|Do you want me to tell you the story again?|Molli|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} will repeat the story for him. Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} resolves to find out for herself what the {{Term|BotW|Bird}} swallowed when she grows up.<ref>{{Cite|The snow-white birdie's {{Color|BotW Red|important thing}}... When I grow up, I'm gonna go to the {{Color|BotW Red|big tree}} and find it.|Molli|BotW}}</ref>

After locating {{Term|BotW|Mozo Shenno Shrine|link}} and completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} to share his findings with {{Term|BotW|Molli}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I found it.] WHAT?! You met the white birdie?! What did he swallow?|Molli|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reveals that he found an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} is shocked to hear that the {{Term|BotW|Bird}} was actually the {{Term|BotW|Biron Snowshelf|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Well...] A...{{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}? The snow-white bird wasn't actually a bird? So the white birdie was actually Hebra Mountain!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> She then recalls that her {{Term|BotW|Grandpa}} was always a prankster.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Grandpa! You always were such a prankster!|Molli|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Molli}} again, she will thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} for helping her rediscover the ending to her {{Term|BotW|Grandpa}}'s story.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for helping me figure out what the snow-white birdie's important thing was! Even if it wasn't a birdie at all.|Molli|BotW}}</ref> Now, she is capable of reciting the entire story on her own, and she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to hear it.<ref name="Grandpa"/> At the end of her story, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} reveals that her {{Term|BotW|Grandpa}} saw an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} in the {{Term|BotW|Bird}}'s stomach.<ref>{{Cite|It was... A {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}}!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> She then describes how the hero who found the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} defeated the {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link|display=bad guys}} and saved the {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link|display=princess}}, living happily ever after.<ref>{{Cite|The hero who found the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} defeated the bad guys...and saved the princess! And they all lived happily ever after!|Molli|BotW}}</ref> Following this, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} is proud to have told the story all by herself.<ref>{{Cite|I told the whole story by myself! Perfectly!|Molli|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Molli}} while she is walking to her ledge, she tells him that she is busy.<ref>{{Cite|I'm busy right now...|Molli|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, she promises to tell him a story if he follows her.<ref>{{Cite|But if you follow me, I'll tell you a story.|Molli|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Molli}} while she is returning to her {{Term|BotW|House}} for the night, she says that it is time for her to go to sleep and invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to talk later.<ref>{{Cite|I'm gonna go sleepy now...|Molli|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Let's talk later...|Molli|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} wakes {{Term|BotW|Molli}} up while she is sleeping in her hammock, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is going to say "good morning" to her.<ref>{{Cite|...Aren't you going to say "good morning" to me?|Molli|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses to do so, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} states that she is not ready to say it yet.<ref>{{Cite|[Uh... Yeah.] I'm not ready to say "good morning" yet...|Molli|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Molli}} says that she is tired.<ref>{{Cite|[Nope.] Yep... Sleepy.|Molli|BotW}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Molli}} is derived from Molly, with the final letter replaced to comply with the "-li" suffix common among {{Term|Series|Rito}} names. Molly is a diminutive of the name "Mary."<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.behindthename.com/name/molly |site= behindthename.com |title= Given Name MOLLY}}</ref>
