The '''[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun''' is a [[Submachine Guns|Submachine Gun]] in [[Fortnite]] that is currently unreleased.

== Functionality ==
[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun is an extremely fast-firing, low damage ray gun. It has first-shot accuracy and uses infinite ammo.

Firing the item reduces the durability meter. Once the durability meter reaches 0%, the item breaks and is removed from the player's inventory.

== Stats ==
[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun uses infinite {{Ammunition|Energy Cells}} and has a headshot multiplier of 1.5x.
