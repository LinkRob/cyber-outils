''''Copter Fox''' is an {{Uncommon}} [[Emote]] in [[Fortnite]] that can be obtained as a reward from [[Dodger's Rewards]]. 

'Copter Fox is [[Dodger (Sidekick)|Dodger's]] [[Built-In Cosmetics|Built-In Emote]] and can only be used while using it.

{{LEGO Emote|'Copter Fox}}

== Trivia ==
*The way Dodger uses its tail to fly in 'Copter Fox is a reference to [[wikipedia:Tails_(Sonic_the_Hedgehog)|Tails]] from the ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' franchise, who is also a fox that uses his tails like helicopter rotors to fly.
