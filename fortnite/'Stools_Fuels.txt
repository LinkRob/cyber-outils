'''<nowiki>'Stools Fuels</nowiki>''' was a [[Landmarks (Battle Royale)|Landmark]] in [[Fortnite: Battle Royale]] added in [[Chapter 3: Season 3]]  to the island [[Artemis]]. It is a small Gas Station/Outpost owned by [[GAS]] surrounded by decaying fauna, and found northwest of [[Reality Falls]].

== History ==
=== [[Chapter 3: Season 3]] ===
* [[Update v21.00]]
** '''<nowiki>'Stools Fuels</nowiki>''' is added to [[Artemis]].
=== [[Chapter 3: Season 4]] ===
* [[Update v22.00]]
** The decaying of the [[Reality Tree]] has caused much of the [[Reality Bloom Biome]] to die, including the area around ''''Stools Fuels'''.
*[[Fracture]]
** '''{{PAGENAME}}''', like the rest of [[Artemis]], was destroyed by [[The Herald]].
