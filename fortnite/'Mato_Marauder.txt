''''Mato Marauder''' is a {{Rare}} [[Outfit]] in [[Fortnite]] that can be purchased in the [[Item Shop]] for {{V-Bucks|1,200}} or with the [[Early Explorers Bundle]] for {{V-Bucks|2,800}}. 'Mato Marauder was first released in [[Chapter 3: Season 3]] and is part of the [[:Category:Squad Origins Set|Squad Origins Set]].

== Trivia ==
'Mato Marauder's styles are based on Chapter 1 Seasons and things related to them:
*''First Flight'' - [[Pre-Season]] era [[Recruit Banshee]] 
*''Fort Knights'' - [[Black Knight]]
*''Meteor Strike'' - [[Dark Voyager]]
*''Launched'' - [[The Visitor]]
* ''Drifted'' - [[Drift]]
*''Cube Rising'' - [[Cubes|The CUBE]]
*''Iced'' - [[The Ice King (Outfit)|The Ice King]]
*''Peeled'' - [[Peely]]
*''Neo Royale'' - Color Scheme
*''X'ed Out'' - Color Scheme
