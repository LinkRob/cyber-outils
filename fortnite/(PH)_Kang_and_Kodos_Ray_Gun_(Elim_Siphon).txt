The '''[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun (Elim Siphon)''' is a [[Submachine Guns|Submachine Gun]] in [[Fortnite]] that is currently unreleased.

[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun (Elim Siphon) is a {{Mythic}} version of the [[Deodorant Applicator]].

== Functionality ==
[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun (Elim Siphon) is an extremely fast-firing, low damage ray gun. It has first-shot accuracy and uses infinite ammo, and will fire continously before cooling down. [PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun (Elim Siphon) is intended to grant siphon on each elimination, however this function was never properly implemented.

Firing the item reduces the durability meter. Once the durability meter reaches 0%, the item breaks and is removed from the player's inventory.

== Stats ==
[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun (Elim Siphon) uses infinite {{Ammunition|Energy Cells}} and has a headshot multiplier of 1.5x.
