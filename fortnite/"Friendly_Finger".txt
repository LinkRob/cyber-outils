"'''Friendly Finger'''" was an {{Uncommon}} [[Emote]] in [[Fortnite]].<ref>https://www.reddit.com/r/FortniteLeaks/comments/g1ndoo/unfinished_fist_bang_middle_finger_emote_via_hypex/</ref>

==Trivia==
*"Friendly Finger" is a reference to the fist-banging gesture from the American sitcom ''Friends'', which is the equivalent of giving someone the middle finger in the show.
