'''104th Training Corps arc''' is the third [[Story Arcs|story arc]] of the ''[[Attack on Titan (Manga)|Attack on Titan]]'' manga.

== Introduction ==
After witnessing the horror of the [[Titan]]s, [[Eren Yeager]], [[Mikasa Ackerman]] and [[Armin Arlert]] decide to undergo [[military]] training and enroll as members of the [[Training Corps|104th Training Corps]]. There, they get to know the rest of the trainees and slowly begin to understand the hardships of being a soldier.
