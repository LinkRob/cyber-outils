{{nihongo|'''''"Attack on Titan" The Final Season Original Soundtrack Complete Album'''''|進撃の巨人 The Final Season Original Sound Track Complete Album|Shingeki no Kyojin The Final Season Original Sound Track Complete Album}} is the eighth soundtrack album containing music from the anime adaptation of ''[[Attack on Titan (Anime)|Attack on Titan]]'', compiling the previous three into a single album and featuring an extra Blu-ray disc that contains the orchestra concert footage from the ''Attack On Titan The Final Season SPECIAL EVENT 2022''. It is composed by Kohta Yamamoto and {{w|Hiroyuki Sawano}}.
