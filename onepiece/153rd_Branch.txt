==History==
===Past===
After [[Morgan]] was promoted from the rank of Chief Petty Officer to Lieutenant Commander for reportedly capturing the infamous "[[Nugire Yainu|Kuro]]", and later making his way up to the rank of [[Captain (Marine Rank)|Captain]],{{Qref|sbs=6|page=124|Fan Question: Is the man who captured fake Kuro from Chapter 37 Captain Axe-Arm Morgan?}} he became corrupted by his newfound fame. He went on to tyrannize the entire 153rd Branch, along with the town under its jurisdiction, for three years—an abuse of power in which his son, [[Helmeppo]], eagerly took part.

===East Blue Saga===
====Romance Dawn Arc====
Their reign of terror came to an end when [[Monkey D. Luffy|Luffy]] and [[Roronoa Zoro|Zoro]] defeated Morgan. Following his defeat, Morgan was dismissed from his position, and command of the base was handed over to Commander [[Ripper]]. [[Koby]] and [[Helmeppo]] remained part of the staff of the base until the transfer of the imprisoned Morgan to [[Vice Admiral]] [[Monkey D. Garp|Garp]]. During the transfer, Morgan managed to escape, after which Koby and Helmeppo came under Garp's command and began training under his tutelage.

===Fish-Man Island Saga===
====From the Decks of the World====
During the two year timeskip, Ripper became a [[Marines|Marine]] officer and [[Rika]] joined the base as a waitress.{{Qref|cover=616|Rika is shown after the timeskip.}}
