The '''16th Branch Base''' is a [[Marine Base]] and the seat of the 16th Marine Branch in [[East Blue]]. It is commanded by [[Captain (Marine Rank)|Captain]] [[Nezumi]].{{Qref|name=c69|chap=69|page=12-16|ep=31}}

==Appearance==
The 16th Branch is largely hidden in the manga, and its appearance is unclear, though it does have the dashed-line design characteristic of Marine bases. Atop the base sits a large model of a mouse's head; above the head there is a small tower with a surrounding balcony, itself topped with another set of mouse ears and an upwards tail that turns into a Marine flag.

In the anime, the base is depicted as a wide, short, blue dome, with smaller towers around it.

In the PlayStation game ''[[Set Sail Pirate Crew!]]'', it is depicted as a green tower more similar to other bases, with a smaller tower alongside it that a cannon rests upon.

==History==
===Past===
The 16th Branch is responsible for the region of [[Conomi Islands]], which is where [[Cocoyasi Village]], [[Nami]]'s home village, is situated. It was also responsible for turning a blind eye to the activities of the [[Arlong Pirates]] and their tyrannical reign over the island, thanks to [[Arlong]]'s monthly bribes.{{Qref|name=c69}}

===East Blue Saga===
====Arlong Park Arc====
[[File:Nezumi Beaten Up.png|thumb|left|210px|The 16th Branch beaten up by Zoro for trying to spoil the Straw Hats' victory.]]

As part of Arlong's scheme to keep Nami under his power, he tipped off [[Nezumi]] about the money she had gathered to buy her village back from him, allowing the [[Marines|Marine]] [[Captain (Marine Rank)|Captain]] to confiscate all {{B}}93,000,000 that Nami kept buried near her house.{{Qref|chap=80|page=6-15|ep=36|ep2=37}}

Nezumi later attempted to claim Arlong's treasure after the [[Straw Hat Pirate]]s defeated the [[Fish-Men|fish-men]] crew, but was beaten up and then forced by Nami to return the money he had previously confiscated. Humiliated and injured, Nezumi furiously retreated to this base to report [[Monkey D. Luffy]] as a pirate to the [[Marine Headquarters]].{{Qref|name=intro}}{{-}}

{{Non-Canon}}
===Summit War Saga===
====Post-War Arc====
[[File:16th Branch Episode 490 Cameo.png|thumb|200px|The 16th Branch Marines celebrating.]]

After the [[Summit War of Marineford]], the 16th Branch celebrated the Marines' victory over the [[Whitebeard Pirates]] and the deaths of [[Portgas D. Ace]] and [[Edward Newgate|Whitebeard]].{{Qref|ep=490}}{{-}}
