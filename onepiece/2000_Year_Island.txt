'''2000 Year Island''' is a non-canon island rumored to have a hidden treasure of {{Nihongo|"2000"|2千もの|Nisen-mono}} gold pieces, though it's actually just [[Gaimon]]'s {{Nihongo|fake|ニセモノ|Nisemono}} gold tooth.{{Qref|text=[[One Piece Omake|''One Piece Omake'']] 2000 Gold, Luffy and Nami search for the 2000 Gold.}}

==Geography==
2000 Year Island is a small, hill-shaped island with a single palm tree in the middle.

==Citizens==
[[Gaimon]] is, [[Canon#Non-Canon|non-canonically]], the only resident of the island.

==History==
===2000 Gold===
[[Monkey D. Luffy|Luffy]] and [[Nami]] travel to 2000 Year Island, hoping to find the 2000 Gold. They successfully find the rumored chest but, instead of gold pieces, discover that it's just [[Gaimon]]. He informs them that he has a fake gold tooth, angering Nami and causing Luffy to laugh.
