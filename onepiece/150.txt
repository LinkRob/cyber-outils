'''150'''{{Qref|ep=830|150 is named in the captions.}} is a member of the non-canon [[Organ Dealing Assassination Group]].{{Qref|name=debut}}

==Appearance==
150 is a very large man with tanned skin and immensely large muscles in every part of his body. He lacks a shirt, wearing only a pair of tight-fitting black pants, a yellow sash around his waist, bandages around his wrists and forearms, and a small black cap.{{Qref|name=debut}}

==Personality==
Like the other members of the [[Organ Dealing Assassination Group|Assassination Group]], 150 is very violent, wanting to kill all the [[Big Mom Pirates]] to take their organs. He is quite fearless, charging into battle head-on despite being assaulted by multiple enemies.{{Qref|name=debut}}

==Abilities and Powers==
150's body is hard enough to repel bullets that strike him. However, he was still defeated in one blow by [[Charlotte Oven]]'s tremendously [[Netsu Netsu no Mi|hot punch]].{{Qref|name=debut}}

==History==
===Whole Cake Island Arc===
The [[Organ Dealing Assassination Group]] went to [[Whole Cake Island]] with [[Jigra]] to get revenge on the [[Big Mom Pirates]] for killing Jigra's father. The group reached the top of the [[Whole Cake Chateau]] after Jigra had already been killed by [[Charlotte Katakuri]], and 150 rushed toward the [[Tea Party]] venue entrance with several henchman, being undeterred by the volley of bullets striking him. However, Big Mom's sons [[Charlotte Daifuku]] and [[Charlotte Oven]] then arrived, and Oven punched 150 in the chest with a fist heated up by the [[Netsu Netsu no Mi]], defeating him instantly.{{Qref|name=debut}}

==Major Battles==
*Organ Dealing Assassination Group vs. [[Fire Tank Pirates]], [[Charlotte Katakuri]], [[Charlotte Daifuku]], and [[Charlotte Oven]]
