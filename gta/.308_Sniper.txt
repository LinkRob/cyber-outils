The '''.308 Sniper''' (also known as the '''Laser Sighted Sniper Rifle''' and later simply as the '''Laser Sniper''') is a semi-automatic [[Sniper Rifles|sniper rifle]] appearing in {{VC}}, {{LCS}} and {{VCS}}.

==Design==
In ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'' and ''Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories'', the weapon (named '''.308 Sniper''' and '''Laser Sighted Sniper Rifle''', respectively) is based on the {{WP|Heckler & Koch SR9|Heckler & Koch SR9T}}. In ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'', the weapon is modeled after an {{WP|SVD (rifle)|SVD Dragunov}}, and is referred to as the '''Laser Sniper'''.

In all titles, the rifle is equipped with both a telescopic sight and a red [[Laser Sight|laser point sight]], seen when moving the aiming to a side. The laser point sight will leave a trace of red color.

The .308 Sniper is one of the few weapons that used real-life names in the original releases of ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'', appearing as the '''PSG1''' (which the real SR9T is derived from) in the day-one v1.40 release for the [[PlayStation 2]]. It was renamed '''.308 Sniper''' in later releases. [[Rockstar North]] remodeled and renamed the weapon in later versions of ''GTA Vice City'', possibly to avoid copyright and trademark infringements. The remodeled weapon features a smaller magazine and a [[suppressor]] which serves no purpose other than aesthetics

==Performance==
The .308 Sniper has a seven-round magazine, allowing for (brief) rapid firing, in contrast to the bolt-action mechanism of the regular [[Sniper Rifle]]. The .308 Sniper in ''GTA Vice City'' is one of the most powerful weapons in the game; a full magazine will easily set any unarmored vehicles on fire.

==Trivia==
===General===
*Part of the name '''.308 Sniper''' suggests that the rifle uses {{WP|.308 Winchester}} cartridges. However, both the {{WP|Heckler & Koch|H&K}} SR9T and the PSG1 are restricted to use {{WP|7.62×51mm NATO}} cartridges. However, this could be due to the developers not knowing the minute differences between the two calibers, assuming they are the same at the time.

===3D Universe===
*The [[Heads-Up Display|HUD]] icon in ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'' shows a PSG1 instead of an {{WP|SVD (rifle)|SVD}}.
*The [[Sniper Rifle]]'s [[HUD]] icon in ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas'' resembles that of the PSG1.
*The unused {{Manhunt|Heavy Sniper}} (PSG1) from [[Rockstar North]]'s ''[[Manhunt]]'' can be found in the <code>gta_int.img</code> interior prop files for ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''. This interior decoration prop is left unused in-game.
