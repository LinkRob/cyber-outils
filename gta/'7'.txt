''''7'''' is a cut mission in {{SA}}.

==Description==
The mission was designed by [[Neil Ferguson]] at [[Rockstar North]] and is labelled as "<code>WUZI_3</code>"{{Ref|name = gtasa-notusedtext-wuzi3|ref = Line 129<br/>"NF" stands for Neil Ferguson|type = textdata|date = 07/31/2025|filepath   = {{TDE 1.0.0}}\GTASA\Text\notusedtext.txt|filedata   = [WUZI_3] '7'(wuzi3-NF)}}, taking place between [[Amphibious Assault]] ("<code>WUZI_2</code>") and [[The Da Nang Thang]] ("<code>WUZI_4</code>"), after the arrival of the [[Red Gecko Tong]] to discuss a way to stop the attacks from the [[Da Nang Boys]] on the [[San Fierro Triads]].

While there are no leftovers of the mission in the final version, a [[notusedtext.txt|<code>notusedtext.txt</code>]] text file was unintentionally left in the files for the original releases of ''[[Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy - The Definitive Edition]]'' by [[Grove Street Games]], listing old unused text scrapped from the GXT file by [[Rockstar North]] during the game's early development, including mission text for the mission '7'. Prior to that, the only evidence of the cut mission in the original releases was the missing GXT entry between <code>WUZI_2</code> and <code>WUZI_4</code>.{{Ref|name = gtasa-text-AEROPL|ref = |type = textdata|date = 07/31/2025|filepath   = \TEXT\american.gxt|filedata   = [WUZI_1] Mountain Cloud Boys
[WUZI_2] Amphibious Assault
...
[WUZI_4] The Da Nang Thang}}

The mission was cut in an early stage of development, as evidenced by the mission text still using the placeholder "Character: Quote" format and Scottish terms used by Rockstar North.

==Mission==
The mission consisted of a stealth mission set during night time at a heavily guarded Triad complex, occupied by an unspecified Triad gang from the San Fierro Triads.{{Ref|name = gtasa-notusedtext-wuzi3_01|ref = Line 1529|date = 07/31/2025|type = textdata|filepath   = {{TDE 1.0.0}}\GTASA\Text\notusedtext.txt|filedata   = [WUZ3_01:WUZI3] ~s~Sneak into the triad complex and perform the traditional execution.}} Certain objectives suggest this complex is used as a front for a prostitution ring. The mission used certain gameplay mechanics from the mission [[Home Invasion]] ("Noise" meter and "Light" countdown), the ''[[Manhunt]]''-inspired shadow hiding mechanic introduced in [[Madd Dogg's Rhymes]], and a vague "Stealth" HUD indicator.{{Ref|name = gtasa-notusedtext-noiselightstealth|ref = Line 1539|type = textdata|date = 07/31/2025|filepath   = {{TDE 1.0.0}}\GTASA\Text\notusedtext.txt|filedata   = [NOISE:WUZI3] Noise
[LIGHT:WUZI3] Light
[STEALTH:WUZI3] Stealth}}{{Ref|name = gtasa-notusedtext-manhunt|ref = Line 1573|date = 07/31/2025|type = textdata|filepath   = {{TDE 1.0.0}}\GTASA\Text\notusedtext.txt|filedata   = [WUZ3_20:WUZI3] ~s~There are a lot of guards in this building.
[WUZ3_21:WUZI3] ~s~Use dark areas to avoid detection.}}

The context behind Woozie's order to kill a Triad boss and the location of the complex are not specified in the leftover text. There are no locations in [[Chinatown (GTA San Andreas)|Chinatown]] and surrounding neighborhoods that can be identified as a Triad complex, other than the [[Blood Feather Triad Storehouse|storehouse]] used by the defunct [[Blood Feather Triad]], which does not match with the description of the Triad complex of the mission (building with shadows to hide).

===Mission Objectives===
The main objective is to murder the Triad boss by performing the "traditional execution", unspecified in the leftover text.{{Ref|name = gtasa-notusedtext-wuzi3_01}} The player has the ability to liberate a gimp inside the complex and obtain the [[Gimp Suit]].{{Ref|name = gtasa-notusedtext-gimpsuit|ref = Line 1559/1571|date = 07/31/2025|type = textdata|filepath   = {{TDE 1.0.0}}\GTASA\Text\notusedtext.txt|filedata   = [WUZ3_19:WUZI3] ~s~You've freed the gimp.
[WUZ3_13:WUZI3] ~t~ = Wear Gimp Outfit
[WUZ3_14:WUZI3] ~s~You are now wearing the Gimp outfit.}} The costume appears to have been used for a checkpoint system, giving the player the ability to return to the gimp's cell whenever they are detected by a guard.{{Ref|name = gtasa-notusedtext-returntocell|ref = Line 1563|date = 07/31/2025|type = textdata|filepath   = {{TDE 1.0.0}}\GTASA\Text\notusedtext.txt|filedata   = [WUZ3_15:WUZI3] GUARD - 'I see you've broken free again, gimp boy.'

==Possible Leftovers==
Aside from the Gimp Suit repurposed for [[Key to Her Heart]], two unused models appear to have been related to this mission.

===Triad Boss===
A unique pedestrian model for a Triad Boss was left unused in-game, and stored in the <code>cutscene.img</code> file under the name <code>cstriboss</code>, specifically used for motion capture cutscene assets. While this old oriental pedestrian may have been the mission target, other factors suggest this is an early model for the younger Triad gang member internally named "[[The Triad Leader|Triad Boss]]", commonly found during and outside missions.
