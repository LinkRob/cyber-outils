'''''&Action''''' is a newspaper appearing in {{V}} and {{O}}.

==Description==
The newspaper is dedicated to [[Vinewood (HD Universe)|Vinewood]] movie industry–related news stories. An issue of ''&Action'' is found inside [[Solomon Richards' office]] in [[Backlot City]]. It contains several generic headlines such as "Some people happy, some people not happy", "Man does a thing", "Stuff happened yesterday", "Someone gets promoted", "Company does a bunch of stuff" and "Person reads a book novel", followed by illegible text.

Artworks of characters from {{IV}} and {{EFLC}} (including [[Niko Bellic]], [[Johnny Klebitz]], [[Luis Fernando Lopez|Luis Lopez]], [[Little Jacob]], [[Phil Bell]] and [[Vladimir Glebov]]) and a promotional screenshot of ''Grand Theft Auto IV'' featuring Niko Bellic holding a [[Combat Pistol]] are used on the newspaper texture as an [[Secrets and Easter Eggs in GTA V|Easter egg]].

==Influence==
The newspaper appears to be inspired by the real-life ''{{WP|Variety (magazine)|Daily Variety}}'' newspaper based in {{WP|Los Angeles}}, {{WP|California}}, which has ceased publication in March 2013.
