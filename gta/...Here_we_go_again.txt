'''...Here we go again''' is an [[Achievements and Trophies in GTA San Andreas|achievement/trophy]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy - The Definitive Edition|The Definitive Edition]]'' of ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]''.

==Acquisition==
To acquire this achievement, the player must start a new game after completing the game [[100% Completion in GTA San Andreas|100%]].

==Details==
The achievement is twofold, as it is both an achievement/trophy on supported consoles, as well as unlocked in the [[Social Club]].

==Trivia==
*The name of the achievement is a reference to [[Carl Johnson]]'s iconic line ''"Ah shit, here we go again"'', after [[Frank Tenpenny]] throws him out of the [[Police (vehicle)|police car]] in an alleyway in [[Jefferson (neighborhood)|Jefferson]], which is a [[Ballas (3D Universe)|Ballas]] territory, during [[In the Beginning]].
