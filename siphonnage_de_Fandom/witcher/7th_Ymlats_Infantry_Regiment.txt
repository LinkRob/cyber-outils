|appears_games = {{Tw3}}}}'''7th Ymlats Infantry Regiment''' was a military branch of the [[Nilfgaardian Army]] from the duchy of [[Ymlac]]. The unit took part during the [[Second Northern War]] under the command of [[Duke var Attre]].

In {{Year|canon=no|year=1272}}, the unit was sent to [[Skellige]] in order to fight the [[Wild Hunt]]. Under the command of Duke var Attre and [[Geralt of Rivia]], the unit had the order to accost the [[Naglfar]]. All the members of the regiment however (with the exception of Geralt who later managed to break the spell) remained trapped in a ice spell casted by [[Caranthir Ar-Feiniel]], the [[Wild Hunt Navigator|navigator]] of the Wild Hunt. Their fate remained unknown.
