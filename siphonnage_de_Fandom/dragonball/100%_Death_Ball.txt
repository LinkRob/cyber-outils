{{nihongo|'''100% Death Ball'''|100%デスボール|Hyaku Pāsento Desubōru}}<ref>''[[Dragon Ball Z: Budokai Tenkaichi]]'', 2005</ref><ref>''[[Dragon Ball Z: Budokai Tenkaichi 2]]'', 2006</ref> is the full-powered version of the [[Death Ball]] used by [[Frieza]] in his [[100% Full Power]] form.

==Overview==
First, Frieza raises both of his hands up in the air and forms a pink energy sphere. Then, he rapidly charges it multiple times, making it bigger and more powerful. Finally, Frieza throws his hands forward and fires the attack at the opponent, inflicting a huge amount of damage.

==Usage==
Frieza uses this attack during his battle against [[Goku]] after knocking the [[Super Saiyan]] underwater. Fortunately, Super Saiyan Goku manages to catch the 100% Death Ball and punches it up toward the sky, sending it to another planet that explodes immediately. For a brief moment, Goku looks weakened after being able to counter Frieza's 100% Death Ball.

In the ''Budokai Tenkaichi'' series, it is named ''100% Death Ball''' where it is the same color of the normal Death Ball and is Frieza's [[Ultimate Blast]] in his Full Power form. The attack is replaced by [[Last Emperor]] in ''[[Dragon Ball Z: Budokai Tenkaichi 3]]''. 

In ''Dokkan Battle'', it is named '''Full Power Death Ball''' where it is '''Roar of Resentment Frieza (Full Power)'s''' Ultra [[Super Attack]].

In ''Dragon Ball Legends'', Full Power Death Ball appears as the Special Move Arts for Legends Limited Final Form Frieza: Full Power (DBL37-02S). Additionally, he can also teach it to [[Shallot]] (DBL00-01) after reaching Friendship Rank 2.
