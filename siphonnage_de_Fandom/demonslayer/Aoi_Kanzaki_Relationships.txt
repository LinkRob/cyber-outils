== Relationships ==
=== [[Shinobu Kocho]] ===
Shinobu is Aoi's mentor in the field of medicine and likely oversaw some or all of her demon hunter training as well. Aoi thinks highly of Shinobu, like all the other girls in the Butterfly Mansion. Shinobu cares for Aoi and feels sympathy for how her family was killed by demons. She also seems to feel some anger on Aoi's behalf, remarking that none of the girls' families would have been killed if it weren't for demons during her fight with [[Doma]].{{Ref|chap=143}} Furthemore, Shinobu happen to have shown interacting with Kanao, and the Butterfly Girls in the flashbacks, when Tanjiro reminscenes of Shinobu, after receives the news of her death in the battle against Doma.{{Ref|chap=144|pages=3-4}}

=== [[Kanao Tsuyuri]] ===
[[File:Aoi_and_Kanao_training_the_trio.png|thumb|200px|Aoi and Kanao training the trio.]]
Aoi and Kanao get along and seem to have a somewhat sisterly relationship. Kanao attempted to stop [[Tengen Uzui]] from taking Aoi with him at a time when it was highly unusual for Kanao to make her own decisions or go against her orders. As seen in the novel, Aoi previously thought of Kanao as difficult to deal with, since she never showed her own emotions. She also felt inferior to Kanao, since Kanao could fight demons and Aoi could not. However, once Kanao starts to open up and make her own decisions, she and Aoi begin to grow closer. Eventually their friendship grows strong enough that, next to Shinobu, Aoi becomes the closest person to Kanao and one of the people she cares for the most. Kanao in particular comes to enjoy eating every single dish Aoi cooks for her and considers them all her favorites. Aoi, in turn, is shown to truly love Kanao and has come to genuinely see her as a sister. So much so that during the time it was Kanao’s decision to chose a last name for herself, Aoi attempted to discreetly convince her to take her last name to make them as close as genuine sisters.

=== [[Inosuke Hashibira]] ===
[[File:Aoi_giving_food_to_Inosuke_CH204.png|left|thumb|200px|Aoi handing over a tray of food to Inosuke.]]
Aoi and Inosuke met for the first time at the [[Butterfly Mansion]]. At the beginning, he struggled to beat her in all of the tasks due to not having a strong will. Later on, he was finally able to adapt and beat her. In comparison with Zenitsu and Tanjiro, he was harsher against her.{{Ref|chap=48}} Inosuke was one of the slayers who volunteer in Aoi's place to go to the mission to [[Yoshiwara]] with Tengen.{{Ref|chap=70|pages=15-17}} After the fight against Upper Rank 6, Inosuke was carried one more time to the Butterfly Mansion and Aoi was visibly sad about his terrible condition, since he had poison through his body and she thought it was too late to stop the bleeding. Her tears were quickly dispelled however, as he drops off the ceiling. She started to see him as a pain and argued with Inosuke again.{{Ref|chap=100}} After the final battle, Inosuke went out of Tanjiro's room and headed to the kitchen to get some food. He tried to steal food behind Aoi's back, but she noticed him. She handed him over a rounded tray of food that was made only for Inosuke and told him that he could eat how much and whenever he wanted from that tray. After this gesture, he visibly started to see her in a good light.{{Ref|chap=204|pages=19-20}} Eventually, they got married and had a great-grandson named [[Aoba Hashibira]].

=== [[Tanjiro Kamado]] ===
Aoi and Tanjiro seem to have formed a friendship. Tanjiro respects Aoi for her help and care while he, Inosuke, and [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] resided at the Butterfly Mansion. He later intervened for her when she was being kidnapped by Tengen, and defended her when Tengen said she isn't useful. When Tanjiro left the Butterfly Mansion, Aoi said she enjoyed their time together.

=== [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]] ===
Aoi met Zenitsu during [[Rehabilitation Training]]. She generally dislikes him due to his often irritating and lazy attitude. Even so, she has some appreciation for him because of the time he, along with Tanjiro and Inosuke, took her place on the mission with Tengen.
