The '''Anti-Kibutsuji Drug'''{{Ref|databook=databook2|pages=119}} is a unique and extremely powerful drug that was made with the sole purpose of defeating the Demon King, [[Muzan Kibutsuji]].

==Overview==
The Anti-Kibutsuji Drug is a special four-stage drug that was created by the combined efforts and medical expertise of [[Tamayo]], [[Yushiro]] and [[Shinobu Kocho]] with the main purpose of annihilating [[Muzan Kibutsuji]]. 
[[File:Tamayo stabs Muzan with her hand.png|thumb|left|200px|Tamayo drugs Muzan by hiding the compound as he absorbs her impaling hand.]]
It was developed after the attack in the [[Swordsmith Village]] and used in the final [[Demon Slayers vs Muzan Kibutsuji: Part 1|battle]] in the [[Infinity Castle]]. Before [[Kagaya Ubuyashiki]] and his family sacrificed themselves in a suicide attack to stun Muzan, [[Tamayo]] assimilated some of the drug herself, so that if the likely possibility that Muzan would absorb and assimilate her body occurred, the Demon King would also take in the drug on accident.

Every stage of the drug was meant to account for every one of Muzan's strengths, advantages, and abilities.

===Stages===
There are ''four'' stages of this drug, each of which is activated sequentially after the previous stage has taken its effect on Muzan Kibutsuji.

'''Human Regression:''' The first stage of the drug, which was created even before the need arose by [[Tamayo]], which was given to [[Nezuko Kamado]]. This stage effect is to convert Muzan back into a human. This stage started to take effect after Muzan absorbed [[Tamayo]]'s arm, which contained the drug. The components utilized in the making of this stage are quite vague, but Tamayo hinted at the use of the blood of powerful [[demon]]s, which have a high concentration of blood from [[Muzan Kibutsuji|Muzan]], and the blood of [[Nezuko Kamado]]. After she teamed up with [[Shinobu Kocho]], the drug was improved and the effects were drastically enhanced. However, Muzan was able to completely dissolve and nullify it, but in return, accidentally triggered and allowed the other stages of the drug to take effect.
[[File:Muzan realizes he is slowing down.png|thumb|left|200px|Muzan was forced to divert power into combating his rapid aging, protecting Tanjiro Kamado from his full strength.]]
'''Accelerated Aging:''' The second stage of the drug, which Muzan assimilated after absorbing [[Tamayo]], began showing its effects and significantly affecting the Demon King after [[Tanjiro Kamado]] engaged in combat with him in their final [[Demon Slayers vs Muzan Kibutsuji: Part 2|confrontation]]. This stage was proposed by [[Shinobu Kocho]] but the components utilized were unknown. The intended effect of this stage was to age [[Muzan Kibutsuji]] over fifty years in a single minute. While to a [[demon]], especially the Demon King, aging would be meaningless since they can continually regenerate and keep their bodies in optimum condition, the accelerated age instead took advantage of Muzan's need to keep his body in fighting condition during the battle, which caused him to use much of his power just to fight off its effects. The drug caused Muzan to physically age the equivalent of 9,000 years after being in his system for roughly 3 hours. This stage of the drug is arguably the most important stage as it drastically decreased Muzan's previously unparalleled physical capabilities and condition, even nullifying his unlimited and boundless stamina to the point where the injured [[Hashira]] and Demon Slayers could keep up with his attacks and movement speed, albeit barely.

'''Division Prevention:''' The third stage of the drug is created by [[Tamayo]] herself after witnessing [[Yoriichi Tsugikuni]]'s near succession in killing [[Muzan Kibutsuji]], who only managed to barely escape by splitting himself into hundreds of pieces. Thus, Tamayo, through unknown means, created a special compound that would prevent Muzan from splitting himself up and escaping during the final battle. This stage of the drug was always dormant after Muzan absorbed it, but he only realized it after attempting to split himself, which Tamayo anticipated.
[[File:Tamayo's spirit reveals the Anti-Kibutsuji Drug had four parts.png|thumb|200px|Weakened by the drug's last stage, Muzan coughs blood as he is unable to heal.]]
'''Cellular Destruction:''' The fourth and final stage of the drug was meant to finish Muzan by destroying his body at a cellular level after he was weakened by the previous three stages. This stage of the drug was meant to cause Muzan's cells to slowly deteriorate and perish, and was facilitated by the aging stage of the drug that weakened his immortality and grasp on his own body. This stage had an extremely profound and significant effect on his absolute regenerative abilities, to the point that where even an ordinary [[Nichirin Sword]] was able to cause serious damage to his body, as well as his healing process to slow down from healing instantaneously to taking several seconds to heal even the most minor of wounds, even being unable to heal some more serious injuries.

In the end, all four stages combined managed to delay the fight till sunrise arrived while also constantly weakening Muzan, allowing the Demon Slayers to push him to a corner, prevailing against the Demon King after 1,000 years of war and bloodshed.
