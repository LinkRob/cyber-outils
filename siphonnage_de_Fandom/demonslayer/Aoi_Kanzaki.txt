{{Nihongo|'''Aoi Kanzaki'''|{{Ruby|神|かん}}{{Ruby|崎|ざき}} アオイ|Kanzaki Aoi}} is a supporting character of [[Kimetsu no Yaiba (Manga)|''Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba'']]. She is a [[Demon Slayer]] of the [[Demon Slayer Corps]], but works as a helper at the [[Butterfly Mansion]] to help injured Demon Slayers, rather than taking to the front lines and fighting [[demon]]s.

{{TOC Limit|limit=2}}

==Appearance==
Aoi is a young woman of average height and pale skin complexion, with large deep blue eyes that fade to a lighter blue at the bottom, along with white rectangular pupils, straight black hair with a parted fringe, and twin-tails. She has dark, thin, and arched eyebrows which are usually furrowed due to the frown that she often has on her face. She wears two white butterfly ornaments that keeps her twin-tails in place. They have a blue trim on the outer side of the wings, and decorated with light turquoise green speckled spots.

Aoi wears the standard Demon Slayer uniform with the addition of a white nurse dress on top of it, with a blue sash around her waist. Her uniform is tinted slightly blue, along with pair of [https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zori zōri] with blue straps and white [https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabi tabi socks]. The uniform consisted of a button-up jacket with a stand-up collar and long sleeves, featuring silver buttons, a shirt pocket on the left breast, and the kanji for {{Nihongo|'''Destroy'''|{{Ruby|滅|めつ}}|Metsu}} engraved on the back of the uniform. Beneath the jacket, she wore a white long-sleeved collared undershirt, and her outfit was fastened at the waist with the standard white belt. She wore traditional tattsuke-hakama pants matching the blue color tone of her uniform.

==Personality==
[[File:Aoi telling Zenitsu to take his medicine.png|thumb|left|200px|Aoi reprimanding Zenitsu Agatsuma for complaining.]]
Aoi is tough, uncompromising, strict, and serious, displaying an efficient and no-nonsensical attitude nearly at all times. While performing her duties in the Butterfly Mansion, she is effective and proactive, evident when she quickly demands a complaining [[Zenitsu Agatsuma|Zenitsu]] to silence himself and take his medication; going so far as to demand he does not complain to her when he does not heal, despite his reluctance due to its bad taste.

Aoi's personality is somewhat similar to that of a younger [[Shinobu Kocho]], being loud, volatile, and open. She often appears angry, generally displaying how she feels very clearly. However, she does occasionally express more tender emotions such as declaring her enjoyment of the time spent with the trio when they were at the Mansion,{{Ref|chap=53|pages=7}} looking openly surprised at [[Tanjiro Kamado|Tanjiro]]'s reassurance towards her,{{Ref|chap=53|pages=8-9}} and bursting into tears when discussing [[Inosuke Hashibira|Inosuke]]'s serious injuries.{{Ref|chap=100|page=8}}

Aoi harbors insecurity over her inability to be an effective Demon Slayer, stating that her survival of Final Selection was due to luck and deeming herself a coward for her failure to fight in battle due to her immense fear of combat. Despite her somewhat gruff demeanor, she is very kind and caring, doing her best to help the Demon Slayers that come to the Butterfly Mansion for rehabilitation training.{{Ref|chap=53|pages=8}}

Aoi is shown to be rather reserved, as shown when Tanjiro asks what she would do to show [[Giyu Tomioka|someone who thinks he is alone]] that he isn't actually alone, she bluntly states that she wouldn't know and that she would prefer to be left alone.

==Abilities==
===Overall Abilities===
As someone who passed the [[Final Selection]], Aoi has above-average fighting abilities and physical capabilities compared to the average person. However, she is not on par with active Demon Slayers such as [[Tanjiro Kamado]], [[Zenitsu Agatsuma]], [[Inosuke Hashibira]] and [[Kanao Tsuyuri]] as she does not spend time training to fight demons or go on missions, but rather chooses to be a helper in the Butterfly Mansion, helping injured Demon Slayers to recover.

'''Medical Knowledge:''' After training under [[Shinobu Kocho]], Aoi is able to concoct most of the simpler medicines and use them to treat injured patients.

'''Trained Craftsmanship:''' Aoi is knowledgeable and skillful enough to repair [[Nezuko Kamado]]'s box which was damaged by [[Sanemi Shinazugawa]]'s blade.{{Ref|vol=6|text=(Extra Pages).}}

==Trivia==
* {{Nihongo|"Aoi"|アオイ|Aoi}} can have different meanings depending on the kanji used. For example, her name can translate to "mallow", {{Nihongo|"hollyhock"|{{Ruby|葵|あおい}}|aoi}} or {{nihongo|"blue"|{{Ruby|青|あおい}}|aoi}}.
**However, her name is spelled with three [[Wikipedia:Katakana|katakana]] characters, which have no semantic properties. Although since her entire character design revolves around the color blue, it can be presumed that it stands for blue in nature.
* Aoi's surname, '''Kanzaki''', contains the [[Wikipedia:Kanji#Kun'yomi_(native_reading)|Kun'yomi]] reading of the [[Wikipedia:Kanji|kanji]] for {{Nihongo|"deity, spirit"|{{Ruby|神|かん}}|kan}} and {{Nihongo|"promontory, peninsula"|{{Ruby|崎|さき}}|saki}}, the latter is affected by [[Wikipedia:Rendaku|rendaku]].
* Aoi was partially responsible for Kanao's name. During the time in which the naming selection for Kanao took place, Shinobu and her older sister [[Kanae Kocho|Kanae]] placed the names on the floor, allowing Kanao to choose a name at random. After seeing that Kanao was going to choose a paper containing the name {{Nihongo|"Kamasu"|カマス|Kamasu}}, Aoi swiftly swapped the paper for the one containing Kanao.
* Aoi wanted a sister, so she was fiercely pestering Kanao to choose her last name.{{Ref|vol=19|text=(Extra Pages).}}
* Aoi was in the same Final Selection as the [[Hashira|Mist Hashira]], [[Muichiro Tokito]].{{Ref|databook=databook2}}
* Aoi's uniform was supposed to have an open chest design like [[Mitsuri Kanroji]]'s uniform, same with Kanao and Shinobu. She later on burned this uniform with the oil and matches that Shinobu gave her and Kanao.{{Ref|vol=12|text=(Extra Pages).}}
* Aoba shares the Hepburn romaji spelling of the first part of his name with Aoi (however, the "ao" in "Aoi" and the "ao" in "Aoba" are written using different character scripts). Given that [[Sumihiko Kamado]], [[Kanata Kamado]], [[Toko Agatsuma]], and [[Yoshiteru Agatsuma]] share parts of their names with their ancestors, this is also the case for Aoba.
** Due to Aoba's name containing the character {{Nihongo|"blue, azure"|{{Ruby|青|あお}}|ao}}, this is direct evidence that Aoi's name is in reference for blue.
** Aoba works in the field of science, much like Shinobu did back in the [[Butterfly Mansion]] where Aoi lived.
** Aoba has jet black hair rather than hair that fades from black to blue like Inosuke's, the same as Aoi.
* Aoi ranked 24th in the first character popularity poll with 103 votes.
* In [[Kimetsu Academy]], Aoi is a second year high school student of the Persimmon Class and a member of the Flower Arrangement Club, and she is also a volunteer at the school canteen. Her parents own Aozora Diner, where she helps out a lot. However, [[Mitsuri Kanroji|a girl with a big appetite]] has been coming recently and causing her much trouble.{{ref|vol=7|text=(Extra Pages).}}
* Aoi's voice actress, Yuri Ehara, is an in-house animation staff member at Ufotable, the animation studio behind ''Demon Slayer''.
