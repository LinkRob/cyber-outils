{{Nihongo|'''Akito Ubuyashiki'''|{{Ruby|産|うぶ}}{{Ruby|屋|や}}{{Ruby|敷|しき}} {{Ruby|晟|あき}}{{Ruby|斗|と}}|Ubuyashiki Akito}} is a minor character from [[Kimetsu no Yaiba (Manga)|''Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba'']], being exclusive to a 2021 art exhibition.

==Appearance==
As a child, Akito greatly resembled his posthumous nephew [[Kiriya Ubuyashiki|Kiriya]], having a black bobcut adorned with a [[wisteria]] headpiece. He also wore a floral print kimono with the exact same design as his [https://kimetsu-no-yaiba.fandom.com/wiki/Kuina_Ubuyashiki nieces], and a striped [[Wikipedia:Obi_(sash)|obi]] with a circular design, supported with a [[Wikipedia:Obi_(sash)#Accessories_for_women's_obi|obiage]] and [[Wikipedia:Obi_(sash)#Accessories_for_women's_obi|obijime]]. Akito was seen with a gentle, if not reserved and passive expression.

==Personality==
Unlike the kind and understanding [[Kagaya Ubuyashiki|Kagaya]] or resentful and dour [[Senri Ubuyashiki|Senri]], Akito appeared to be meek, quiet, and compliant. Due to his brother convincing him to set their home ablaze, Akito seemed to be unable to stand up to himself and carried out Senri's instructions without question, leading to his death.

==History==
Akito was the youngest child of his generation of the Ubuyashiki family. His elder brother Senri, who resented his illness and his father's suicide as a result of his position as [[Oyakata]], forced Akito to set fire to the mansion, as well as joining him to commit double-suicide. Their mother would die from the fire in an attempt to save them, leaving Kagaya as the sole survivor.<ref>[https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/features/2021/12/7/report-demon-slayer-sits-on-top-of-the-world-in-koyoharu-gotoges-1st-exhibition ''Kimetsu no Yaiba Gotoge Koyoharu'' exhibition 2021] (via [https://www.crunchyroll.com Crunchyroll])</ref>
