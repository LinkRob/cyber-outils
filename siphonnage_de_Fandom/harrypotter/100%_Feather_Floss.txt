'''100% Feather Floss''' was a [[Healing magic|healing]] [[potion]] that was used to treat damaged [[magic]]al [[bird]] feathers. It could be used on [[Augurey]]s, [[Diricawl]]s, [[Fwooper]]s and [[Golden Snidget]]s. Four to five drops were to be applied to the bird's feathers to loosen knots and sticking. It was for external use only. The [[Magizoologist]] [[Newton Scamander]] kept this potion for use on his pet magical birds.<ref name="CB">{{CB}}</ref>
