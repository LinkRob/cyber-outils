'''"Tank" Dempsey''' is a character featured in the [[Zombies (Treyarch)/Story/Dark Aether Saga|Dark Aether Saga]] of [[Zombies (Treyarch)|Zombies]] mode. Dempsey appears in ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops 6]]'' in the ending cutscene of [[Reckoning (Zombies)|Reckoning]] and as a canon playable character in all maps of ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops 7]]''. He is also a playable [[Operators|Operator]] of the [[Joint Special Operations Command|JSOC]] faction in ''Black Ops 7'' Multiplayer and ''[[Call of Duty: Warzone 2.0]]''.

==Biography==
===Early Life===
"Tank" Dempsey, in his original timeline, hailed from the West Virginian town of [[Liberty Falls (location)|Liberty Falls]], and was part of a local biker gang known as the Steel Mountain Riders. Some time later, he enlisted with the [[United States Marine Corps]] and did bootcamp in either {{w|Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune|Camp Lejeune}} or {{w|Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton|Camp Pendleton}}. After leaving bootcamp, he participated in the [[Vietnam War]], which caught the attention of his universe's version of [[Grigori Weaver]] - a [[CIA]] operative who was part of the Office of [[Requiem]].

Weaver told Dempsey to join in an assignment in the deep jungle and they, along with his universe's [[Oskar Strauss]] and another squad, traveled to a jungle where they then found a lost temple. As they approached it, they were suddenly attacked by a horde of [[zombie]]s; they attempted to hold out, but were on the verge of being overrun until Weaver gave Strauss an order to trigger something within Dempsey. Although it is unknown what exactly happened to Dempsey, it was described by the [[Shadowsmiths|Shadowsmith]] [[Veytharion]] as him becoming "the vessel for a ravenous evil, summoned and bound" to him, which nonetheless happened to make it so that he was able to save himself, Weaver, Strauss and the rest of their squad from the horde.

Shortly after this, Dempsey was lauded as a hero by some of his fellow companions, but personally felt he had been traumatized by the events at the temple. He deserted the Army and sought refuge within his lover's arms as a way to distance himself from his traumatic memory, but to no avail.

===Banishment to the Dark Aether===
At some point in 1968, whilst Dempsey was admittedly sitting on a toilet, he was teleported to the [[Dark Aether]] by [[Jack "Warden" McGregor|the Warden]], a mysterious entity who rules over the Dark Aether, due to his previous encounter at the temple having bonded him to a Dark Aether entity. Whilst there, he then allied with three soldiers who had also been displaced from their own worlds: [[Nikolai Belinski (Dark Aether)|Nikolai Belinski]], a [[Soviet Union/Zombies|Soviet]] tank commander who was fighting in the [[Soviet-Afghan War]] in 1984; [[Takeo Masaki (Dark Aether)|Takeo Masaki]], a wandering ronin from Feudal [[Japan]] in 1591; and [[Edward Richtofen (Dark Aether)|Edward Richtofen]], a [[Nazi Germany|Nazi German]] scientist from a timeline where the [[Axis Powers]] won [[World War II]].

===Meeting Requiem===
Shortly after arriving in the Dark Aether, Dempsey and his newfound crew found themselves in a mirrored version of the [[Project Janus Headquarters|Janus Towers]] facility within the Dark Aether. They then found four individuals-Grigori Weaver, [[Elizabeth Grey]], [[Mackenzie Carver]], and [[Maya Aguinaldo]]-teleported into the lobby area, and Richtofen proceeded to greet them. Before both crews could learn about one another, they were interrupted by the Warden, who arrived and used a totem skull to drain their life forces, then left them to fend off the awakened zombie horde. The crews managed to escape the lobby area, as the Warden began to observe them. 

As Dempsey began to get to know the rest of the crew, he found special disdain for Weaver because of the events that transpired at the temple in his universe, despite this version of Weaver being different from the one he knew. In spite of this, Dempsey remained mum about the truth whenever Weaver pressed him, only choosing to maintain a neutral attitude toward the latter. In contrast, Dempsey found himself attracted to Maya, bonding with her over her use of foul-mouthed language. Maya similarly returned the favor with flirtatious banters. He also bonded with Carver over war stories, both having spent time serving in the Vietnam War, while he was less than enthusiastic with Grey, due to her formal manners. Much like his [["Tank" Dempsey (Ultimis)|other]] [["Tank" Dempsey (Primis)|counterparts]], Dempsey got on well with Nikolai and Takeo, but held great resentment against Richtofen because of his Nazi association.

===Confronting Veytharion===
While traversing near a crashed rocket site, Dempsey found his own dog tags, causing him to relive the memories of his encounter in the mysterious temple, while being taunted by Veytharion of his past actions. As he recovered from the vision, Dempsey tossed the dog tags aside, and moved on. As Veytharion continued with suggesting that Dempsey embrace his truth as a survivor and not a hero, Dempsey rebuked that he didn't need no "ghost" to tell him what he already knew. 

Later, he and the others encountered an [[Entity (Black Ops 7)|Entity]], who explained that the Warden sought out each of them after they all had encounters with ancient powers in their own worlds, and that he sought to empower himself with their essences. The Entity further revealed that the Warden controlled a group of ancient beings in the Dark Aether, the Shadowsmiths, who were in possession of their essences; defeating them would be required to regain their souls and memories. The group encountered Veytharion, who was imprisoned in this area of the Dark Aether, and defeated his corrupted form. This allowed the freed Veytharion to restore the memories and souls of Dempsey and Maya. Afterward, the Entity teleported the group away to a pocket dimension for temporary refuge.

===Confronting Caltheris===
Some time after, the Entity sent the crew to outer space, where they landed on a skull-shaped asteroid, housing an observatory at the top. They explored the area in search of the second Shadowsmith, [[Caltheris]]. Dempsey learned that his mind had now been telepathically linked to Veytharion, allowing them to talk to each other without the others being aware (minus Maya, whose mind was also linked to Veytharion).

The crew later found a cryopod containing the body of Dr. [[Silas Thurston]], the astronomer who oversaw the observatory, and carved out his brain to put it in another device, allowing Thurston to assist them. With Thurston's guidance, the team rebuilt a portal that took them to a temple located on the surface of Mars, as Caltheris revealed herself to be taking the form of the skull shaped asteroid itself, and began attacking the crew. Later, after the crew defeated her various forms, Nikolai approached Caltheris and demanded she give his soul back. She obliged and restored his soul as well as Grey's. Their victory was short-lived, however, as the Warden finally took notice of their actions, and quickly dispatched of them, by summoning [[Ravager]]s to drag them underground to an unknown location, while the Entity was summoned elsewhere.

===Escaping the Time Loop===
As punishment for having released Caltheris and Veytharion from the Warden's control, Dempsey and the rest of the crews were thrown into a version of [[Paradox Junction|a Nuketown that had been obliterated by a nuclear detonation]] - with said nuke's mushroom cloud being able to be seen in the distance, but that still had some of its infrastructure resist the blast, as well as some [[Zombie|inhabitants]] still roaming around. Every so often, the town Dempsey was trapped in would travel itself back in time to a pre-nuclear detonation state, where the town would appear as idyllic as any other; and a few moments later, the town would inevitably travel itself forwards in time to the post-nuclear detonation state. As it stood, Dempsey and the crews were stuck in the Nuketown time loop, and did not know how to escape.

However, within the Nuketown there were two little girls - [[the Twins]] - who happened to be already living and carelessly playing within the Nuketown time loop. They helped Dempsey and the crews to complete the necessary steps - amongst them the assembly of the [[Blundergat]] and its variant, the Sundergat - as it would also free them as well. As the crews completed different tasks for the Twins and discovered a way to escape, they prepared to face the entity that was forcing the realm they were in to engage in an eternal loop - the [[The Dark Heart|Dark Heart]].

While spending time in the Nuketown loops, Dempsey contacted Veytharion once more, this time inquiring him about the reason that Dempsey and the others were specifically chosen to be banished to the Dark Aether. Veytharion, similarly, was clueless, but suggested that the Entity know the real reason. Later, Maya and Dempsey both questioned Veytharion on the nature of the various timelines that the crews originated from, to which Veytharion clarified that their worlds existed on planes, rather than a linear path.

Dempsey and the crews fought against the Dark Heart whilst it would send against them different enemies to stop them, but ultimately the crews emerged victorious, and the Dark Heart was defeated. The Dark Heart's essence was siphoned into a magical piece of chalk that fell onto the Twins' hands, and they used it do draw a hopscotch game that would lead into an exit portal. The Zombie Twin, infatuated with Dempsey, shoved Maya aside and hugged him, thanking him for setting the Twins free; Dempsey would then - visibly appalled at the sight of the Zombie Twin - reply with a "you're welcome". The Human Twin then finished drawing a hopscotch game on the pavement, which then created a portal at the end of it. The Twins then said that they would first go to "the house she made", and then off to [[Totenreich|somewhere "cold"]]. The crews then hopscotched their way into the exit portal, leaving the doomed Nuketown behind.

====Confronting Dravakar====
The crews arrived at their next destination, the island of Eidskallen. Upon arrival inside a Giant Robot's inactive head, Richtofen looked outside and immediately recognized the location, given its strong ties to his horrendous actions. Before the crews could settle in, they noticed a gigantic ice creature in the background battling a Giant Robot. Richtofen was quickly pulled out of the robot head by zombies, but was saved in time by Weaver and the others. The giant, who turned out to be the Shadowsmith [[Dravakar]], nearly stomped on the crews, but they managed to dodge. As the crews began to navigate Eidskallen, each of them took turns to question Richtofen on his actions involving this place, to which he refused to give a straight answer. The crews later encountered the spirit of [[Astrid Loven]], who guided them to acquiring the [[Jotunn Star]], a powerful weapon that they needed in order to defeat Dravakar. They also proceeded with repairing the Giant Robot Tyr in order to take on Dravakar.

With Tyr's repairs finished, the crews finally used Tyr to launch them atop a platform, where they battled Dravakar and defeated him. As soon as Dravakar returned to his Shadowsmith form, Richtofen immediately stepped forward and demanded he restore his essence. Before he could finish his sentence however, Dravakar immediately grabbed Richtofen by the neck and hoisted him up in the air. Richtofen momentarily struggled to breathe as Dravakar then restored him before throwing him down onto the ground, calling him a "vile little man." After restoring Weaver's essence, Dravakar claimed the crews' actions were causing the Dark Aether to unravel, and that they needed to restore balance to the dimension if they were to return to their homes. Dravakar tasked the crews with journeying to "the place of fire" to liberate his sister, the final Shadowsmith, and retrieving the World Seed, before they can confront the Warden.

==Trivia==
*Dempsey's "Ravager's Rise" skin is based on the [[Intelligence Support Activity|ISA]] SMG class outfit in ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops II]]'' Multiplayer. 
**Coincidentally, Dempsey's voice actor Steve Blum also voiced an ISA soldier in ''Black Ops II'' Multiplayer.
*When opening the [[Mystery Box]], Dempsey may say he would "spin counter-clockwise and crouch three times" to get a [[Ray Gun]]. This is a reference to a community myth where the player can "guarantee" a Ray Gun with every Mystery Box purchase by doing the aforementioned step.
*Dempsey makes several references to [[Liberty Falls]] in his dialogue, including mentioning that [[Quick Revive]] tastes like something "that bastard Riffle would keep in his shed", referring to Dennis Riffle, the Liberty Falls church groundskeeper who is encountered and killed during [[Bye-Bye, Dark Aether]].
*When selecting Dempsey in the Multiplayer Operators menu, he could be seen holding the [[M10 Breacher]]. Originally, when selecting him in the Campaign or Zombies Operators menu, he was seen holding the [[Marine SP]] instead. This was fixed with the [[Season One (Black Ops 7)|Season One]] update.
*In Multiplayer, when activating the [[Tactical Nuke|Nuke]] killstreak, Dempsey will yell "NUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUKE!", mimicking [[Ultimis]] [["Tank" Dempsey (Ultimis)|Dempsey]] when he acquires a Nuke power-up in Zombies.
*Dempsey's "Bootcamp" skin is based on the appearance of his [[Primis]] [["Tank" Dempsey (Primis)|counterpart]], introduced in ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops II]]''.
*Dempsey's "Artillery" skin is based on the appearance of his [[Ultimis]] [["Tank" Dempsey (Ultimis)|counterpart]], introduced in ''[[Call of Duty: World at War]]''.
