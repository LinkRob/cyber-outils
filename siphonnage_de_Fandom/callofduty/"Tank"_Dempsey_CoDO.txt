'''"Tank" Dempsey''' was an [[United States of America|American]] Marine who fought in the [[United States Marine Corps]]. She is a playable character in the [[Doomsday (Online)|Doomsday Zombies]] mode of ''[[Call of Duty Online]]''.

==Biography==
Dempsey speaks with an American accent. She's the team's demolition expert. Her personality often swings between playful and adorable and noisy and irritable. But her true self is a smart girl. She loves adventure, and even more so, things that can explode.{{MTL}}
