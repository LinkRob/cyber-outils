Master Sergeant '''"Fender" Takacs''' is an [[Operators|Operator]] of the [[KorTac]] faction featured in ''[[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II]]'', ''[[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III]]'' and ''[[Call of Duty: Warzone 2.0]]''.

==Biography==
Takacs was the illegitimate son of an American [[Central Intelligence Agency|CIA]] operative and a revolutionary in Soviet-era {{w|Budapest}}. However, Takacs was raised without knowing who his father was as he died before he was born, but he was told he was "a great American soldier."

After turning 18, he enlisted in the {{w|Hungarian Defence Forces}} and joined the {{w|Structure of the Hungarian Defence Forces|1st Explosive Ordnance Disposal Regiment}} before joining the 2nd Special Operations Brigade, earning the rank of Master Sergeant (''Zászlós'') and becoming an expert in high value targeting, demolitions, and urban warfare.

Takacs spent most of his career in Joint Task Forces alongside {{w|United States special operations forces|American special forces teams}} in [[Iraq]] and the [[United Republic of Adal]].

By 2022, Takacs became a contractor for the [[KorTac]] private military company.
