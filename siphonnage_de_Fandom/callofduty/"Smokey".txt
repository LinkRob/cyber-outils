'''"Smokey"''' is one of the four [[Marines (Zombies)|Marines]] that are playable in the [[Zombies (Treyarch)|Zombies]] map [[Verrückt]] in ''[[Call of Duty: World at War]]''. He and his squadmates were part of the mission to rescue [[Peter McCain]] which failed, leading to two of the four soldiers dying, [[Paxton "Gunner" Ridge|one]] escaping, and [["Tank" Dempsey|one]] being captured and brainwashed.  He is mentioned in two of [[John "Banana"]]'s limericks found by [[Roppen|Oppenheimer]], the first one acknowledging his death as he tries to kill John and continues trying even after being decapitated, showing he had been turned into a zombie. Smokey is mentioned once more in the map [[Revelations (Map)|Revelations]] in John's radio message hidden in the Verrückt section of the map.
