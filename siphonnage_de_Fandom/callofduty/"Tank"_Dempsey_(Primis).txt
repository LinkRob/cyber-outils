Corporal '''"Tank" Dempsey''', also referred to as '''Primis Dempsey''', was an [[United States of America|American]] Marine who fought in the [[United States Marine Corps]], and is a playable main character in the [[Zombies (Treyarch)|Zombies]] game mode, as well as a major character in the [[Portal:Timeline/Zombies (Treyarch)|Aether Story]].

Primis Dempsey was formally introduced in [[Origins]] on ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops II]]'' as a younger version of [["Tank" Dempsey (Ultimis)|his Ultimis counterpart]], hailing from Dimension 63. He reappears in ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops III]]'' as a main playable character in [[The Giant]], [[Der Eisendrache]] (which also features Ultimis Dempsey in a non-playable role), [[Zetsubou No Shima]], [[Gorod Krovi]], and [[Revelations (map)|Revelations]]. 

Primis Dempsey returns as a playable character in the ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops 4]]'' maps [[Blood of the Dead]] and [[Alpha Omega]] (the latter of which also features Ultimis Dempsey as an alternate playable character). He appears in a non-playable role in [[Classified]] and [[Tag der Toten]]. In addition, Primis Dempsey is an unlockable character in the [[Blackout (Black Ops 4)|Blackout]] game mode.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Corporal Tank Dempsey: American Hero. Originally assigned to intercept Dr. Edward Richtofen in Northern France, Dempsey now fights alongside Nikolai, Takeo and Richtofen as they attempt to put an end to the enemy war machine once and for all. Though he's seen more than his fair share of bloodshed, nothing could have prepared him for the [[My Brother's Keeper|events]] of Der Eisendrache…| Dempsey's biography for ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops III]]''}}

===Getting sent to Europe===
On November 19th, 1917, Corporal "Tank" Dempsey of the [[United States Marine Corps]] was sent to spy on the German [[Group 935]] due to reports of "[[Panzersoldat|prototype armored weaponry]]...strange lights in the sky...a mysterious [[Element 115|plague]]...and even [[Giant Robot|giant metal men]]," in [[Origins|Northern France]]. Specifically, he was assigned to grab a member of the organization as a H.V.T., and was given a transport in the form of a [[Mark IV Tank]].

On April 14th, 1918, Dempsey reported, despite ongoing battles, he has fared well in Northern France. In a personal letter, he reveals that both the Japanese and Russian Armies have also sent spies to investigate the activities of Group 935.

===Freeing Samantha===
On June 4th, 1918, Element 115 completely awakened the undead knights from The Great War and quickly consumed the camp. Dempsey, along with Russian spy [[Nikolai Belinski (Primis)|Nikolai Belinski]] and Japanese spy [[Takeo Masaki (Primis)|Takeo Masaki]] unite with German scientist [[Edward Richtofen (Primis)|Edward Richtofen]] on the battlefield and help free a girl named [[Samantha Maxis]] from her imprisonment in Agartha. The four uniting marked the return of Primis from the original Great War, as the group were reincarnated versions of them. Samantha, who had been freed and sent to the House, then sent Primis to their next destination.

===Convincing Ultimis Richtofen: Deceptio Fracture===
Dempsey, along with Nikolai and Takeo, was sent to the original timeline on June 4th, 1943. Upon learning of Richtofen's multi-dimensional travels, the trio attempted to stop him for fearing that he will disrupt the established continuity of the dimensions. After several dimension jumps and failed attempts, they arrived the Der Riese facility in the original timeline, two years later on October 13th, 1945, just moments after [[Edward Richtofen (Ultimis)|Ultimis Richtofen]] trapped Maxis and Samantha in the teleporter with Fluffy. Dempsey, Nikolai and Takeo confronted Ultimis Richtofen, telling him that there is a chain of events that must be carried out, and prompt him to awaken the Ultimis versions of themselves.

As the zombie horde approach them from outside, Richtofen ignored the three's warning and turns on the teleporter, only to find Primis Richtofen inside. Primis Richtofen then shot his Ultimis self in the head, killing him instantly. This triggered a fracturization across the original timeline. While the group expressed disappointment in Richtofen for tampering with history, he defended his action, believing that what he did would be to secure a better future. While the others fended off the zombies, Richtofen pretended to use the [[Summoning Key]] to acquire Ultimis Richtofen's soul, which Dempsey noticed. Eventually, the four activated a [[Fly Trap|beacon]] in the facility, allowing Maxis from another dimension to locate them.

===Killing Ultimis Dempsey: Deceptio Fracture===
After acquiring a Giant Robot from the Der Riese facility, the four pursue the scattered remains of Group 935 to [[Der Eisendrache|Griffin Castle]], codenamed "Eagle's Nest", on November 5th, 1945. The Germans had captured [["Tank" Dempsey (Ultimis)|Ultimis Dempsey]]'s cryo chamber from being experimented on by Ultimis Richtofen and planned to bring him to Griffin Station, with Primis close behind until a German soldier fires a Panzerschreck at the Giant Robot, disabling it before it could retrieve Ultimis Dempsey. After arriving at Griffin Castle, the young four watch as a rocket containing the Ultimis Dempsey launches to the Moon. After making radio contact with Dr. [[Groph]], Ultimis Richtofen's second-in-command, Richtofen masquerades as his Ultimis self in order to fool Groph. However, Groph becomes suspicious of Richtofen's change of personality and announces him as an imposter. Using the Death Ray, the young four managed to bring the rocket containing the Ultimis Dempsey back to the castle, much to Groph's anger, who activated a failsafe so they couldn't touch the chamber.

Using the Vril Device obtained from the crash site, the young four awakened a ghost [[Keeper]] and help it return it to its physical form before it returns the favor and brings the [[Moon Pyramid Device]] from the Moon to the castle. However, the Keeper becomes corrupted, forcing the four to battle it, eventually defeating it. After defeating it, Groph expresses his extreme anger, vowing to destroy them and the castle itself. In order to tie up these loose ends, the four use the Summoning Key to launch several missiles towards the Moon, destroying it, ultimately destroying Griffin Station and killing any Group 935 scientist at the station, including Groph. After walking towards Ultimis Dempsey's cryo chamber, Richtofen activated the Summoning Key, forcing the others to levitate in the air.

Here, Richtofen's plan is revealed to the Dempsey and the rest of the group. Richtofen planned to kill the Ultimis versions of themselves to be put in the Summoning Key like he supposedly did with his own at the Der Riese facility. When Dempsey himself sadly put his older self down, Richtofen briefly comforted him after it was done, showing that he had laid his previous dislike of Dempsey off to the side. Richtofen then captured the Ultimis Dempsey's soul in the Key.

===Killing Ultimis Takeo: Proditione Fracture===
Dempsey and the other three then travel to a different fractured timeline, to an island, arriving on a Japanese vessel heading for a [[Zetsubou No Shima|Division 9 Facility]] on October 18th, 1945, before being caught and the Summoning key being taken away from him. A brief fight ensues with the Japanese soldiers with Richtofen burning one alive before he notices the Key rolling toward the ocean, luckily Takeo had managed to barely catch the Key by his fingertips much to Richtofen's relief. The four then are forced to swim to a nearby island after the ship blows up. Later, Dempsey was present when [[Takeo Masaki (Ultimis)|Ultimis Takeo]] was cured from being a [[Thrasher]] to collect his soul.

===Stopping at Alcatraz===
After Ultimis Takeo honorably sacrificed himself for a better future, Richtofen then preserved his soul. Initially Dempsey suggested going after [[Nikolai Belinski (Ultimis)|Ultimis Nikolai]], but Richtofen said a "chain of events" must be set in motion. Realizing that he wants to save his three comrades from their eventual fate, Richtofen then takes them to Alcatraz on July 4th, 1941, where he meets his younger self and acquires the Victis blood samples.

===Killing Ultimis Nikolai: Agonia Fracture===
Primis then jumps to another fractured timeline, in a war-torn Stalingrad on November 6th, 1945, to kill the Ultimis Nikolai Belinski and place his soul in the Summoning Key. Eventually the four are teleported out of the sky and parachute in front of Ultimis Nikolai's mech while he is distracted with vodka and listening to "The Ace of Spades" on his radio.

Upon seeing them the Ultimis Nikolai attempts to kill them until a dragon throws him into a building. Later, Dempsey and the others make a temporary truce with the Ultimis Nikolai in order to kill the dragon. Afterward, Dempsey and the other three demand Ultimis Nikolai to surrender to which he refuses and betrays them. After the Primis Nikolai kills his Ultimis self, Richtofen opens a portal in the sky with Maxis' help and lights a beacon from the Summoning Key which releases the Ultimis Nikolai, Takeo and Dempsey. After this, he says they will see Maxis soon.

Later, the mysterious voice contacts Dempsey, Nikolai and Takeo informing them that Richtofen can't hear him. He congratulates the crew on their progress of fixing the fracturing universe. However, he informs them that Richtofen had been searching for years for the correct versions of them to close off the dimensions, suggesting that the Ultimis timeline versions of the characters were the ones who determined the universe's disorder and fates of the incarnations of the alternate versions of the characters in other universes, who have all been erased due to the originals being killed off. He also tells them to not trust Richtofen and that's he's been in "The House" for "ages". Finally, the voice introduces himself as [[Monty (Zombies)|Doctor Monty]], claiming it's so nice to see the characters.

===Return to the house and the final battle===
Soon after the events of Gorod Krovi, Nikolai along with the rest of the Primis crew finally arrive at the house. There Richtofen reunites with Maxis, the two then destroy the MTD to ensure the house would remain safe. Letting his guard down Richtofen leaves the Summoning Key on a table unaware of who was trapped within. The voice inside calls out to Maxis and influences him due to his lack of soul making him easy to control. Maxis opens the Summoning Key releasing the [[Shadowman]] inside, exploring his new surroundings, he opens a portal to Agartha and unleashes an Apothicon invasion. [[Monty (Zombies)|Doctor Monty]] enlists the help of the Primis crew to defeat the Shadowman and save his perfect world from destruction.

With the help of Maxis and [[Sophia|S.O.P.H.I.A]], Primis manages to kill the Shadowman and banish the Apothicons to places unknown. As the world returns to normal Monty becomes concerned as Primis remains in front of him. Noticing the empty blood vials he becomes angered claiming that they are putting his realm at risk. Initially he decides to simply wipe them from existence but decides to instead send them somewhere else. The 4 then end up in the year 1299, in northern France just as the Great war comes to an end causing the cycle to continue.

===Breaking the cycle===
In a new iteration of the cycle, Primis enters the pocket dimension of Alcatraz to pick up the blood vials. Ending up far from their intended target, the group enters the underground lab and find another Richtofen. He gives the vials to the group and instructs Richtofen to re-read the Kronorium. Richtofen scans through pages exclaiming that pages have changed and that his blood is needed. He becomes enraged and throws the Kronorium before hearing a horde of zombies. The group then makes their escape but before entering the portal [[Brutus (Zombies)|Brutus]] destroys it trapping the group within the twisted version of Alctraz.

Beside a mysterious machine known as the Dark Mechanism, The Primis crew battle against Brutus, Whom is being resurrected time and time again. Richtofen realized what the Kronorium meant and that the machine was intended to draw his blood, and stepped inside, sacrificing himself to free the others.

With Brutus finally unable to resurrect, the rest of Primis is joined by a post-Revelations Richtofen, who informed them that the future had changed, and that Nikolai had to keep his soul to defeat Doctor Monty. The future Richtofen explained that the cycle was broken, and that he wished he could say he was sorry to his younger self. Taking the Soul key the future Richtofen handed Nikolai the Kronorium telling him he would need to read it to defeat Monty. The remainder of the Primis members and the Dimension 63 Richtofen left the old Richtofen alone and scared, as the machine continuing to drain his blood.

===Recruiting Ultimis===
In order to prepare for the coming battle; Primis makes a stop at Groom Lake to gain some allies. Within Hanger 4 the Ultimis crew were being held seemingly after the events of Moon, With Richtofen also retaining his body. As Ultimis Nikolai expresses glee at being able to drink uninterrupted, Primis Nikolai emerged from a portal asking his alternate self if drowning his sorrows in vodka is the best choice. The Primis crew rallies their alternate selves and recruit them to fight in the coming battle. As the they each face their alternate selves Ultimis Dempsey asks Primis Nikolai "What kind of war?" to which he replies "A war unlike any other that you have seen. A Great war."

===Retrieving the Elemental Shard===
After recruiting Ultimis at the Pentagon, Primis (alongside Ultimis) teleported to the Broken Arrow facility known as "Camp Edward" in order to retrieve the Elemental Shard - an especially powerful piece of Element 115. However, they were refused access to the A.P.D, which contained the shard, by the intelligence program Rushmore, who was assigned to protect the facility from intruders. However, after performing various tasks to gain Rushmore's trust, Primis was finally granted access to the A.P.D. However, the crew would soon realize that the undead was not the only threat within the facility, as they were now tasked with defeating the electrical entity known as the Avogadro. After defeating the Avogadro and sending it to the Hanford site in Washington, Rushmore allowed the crew to take the Elemental Shard. Not long after gaining the Elemental Shard, Samantha Maxis and the child version of Richtofen were teleported from the House within Agartha to Camp Edward by her father, Ludvig, who was killed by Dr. Monty in the process. Grieving the loss of her father, Samantha unleashed a pulse of energy, destroying Camp Edward in the process.

===Resolving the Paradox===
Not long after their mission to Camp Edward, Primis, Ultimis, and the children found themselves within a forest. There, they decided to start a campfire and take a rest before they went to go fight the Great War. The crew celebrated with alcohol and food, with Primis Nikolai constantly begging the question of where the crew wished to go after the war ended. Primis Dempsey responded to Nikolai's question by stating that he wished to be a drill instructor. Not long after, Dempsey, along with his comrades, were killed by a secret poison that was laced by Nikolai in the alcohol that they had been drinking.

==Personality==
{{Dialogue a-b|Primis Dempsey|Don't you go shootin' off wildly now, Tank.|Ultimis Dempsey|Now does that sound like somethin' I would do? *laugh*|Conversation between Primis and Ultimis Dempsey in [[Blackout (Black Ops 4)|Blackout]]}}
[[File:PrimisUltimis Dempsey Conversation Blackout ShootinOffWildly BO4.ogg]]<br>
Compared to his Ultimis self, Primis Dempsey is much more focused on the mission. When playing on solo, he updates his situation with field reports. While his attitude still remains vulgar, he now shows less aggressiveness and profanity, and is more inclined to use other weapons as well. As he never encountered Richtofen before and was just briefed about him, but as the journey continues, he grows suspicious about his intention. However, the relation becomes less tense after the event of Der Eisendrache. He remains neutral with Nikolai and Takeo, and tries to calm them down when they are upset.

==Trivia==
*Primis Dempsey shares many traits with his Ultimis self, including their favorite [[Perk-a-Cola]] ([[Juggernog]]) and least favorite ([[Quick Revive]]).
*Unlike their Ultimis selves, Primis Dempsey and Primis Richtofen are less hostile toward each other, though Dempsey remained skeptical of Richtofen due to his unclear motives.
*Primis Dempsey and Primis Nikolai seem to be on good terms when they first met, although their relationship becomes strained in The Giant. Regardless, they share mutual respect for one another.
*Primis Dempsey's relationship with Primis Takeo is more resembling of a friendship compared to their Ultimis counterparts, where Ultimis Dempsey treats Ultimis Takeo with respect, though the feeling was not mutual.
*Unlike his Ultimis self, Primis Dempsey rarely breaks the fourth wall.
*In Der Eisendrache, Primis Dempsey mentions he was once kicked by a horse.
*Primis Dempsey had a cat.
*In [[Blood of the Dead]], Primis Dempsey states that his family is of Irish descent.
*In a wisp found in Revelations, [[Monty (Zombies)|Dr. Monty]] refers to Dempsey as "Thomas" and "Ringo", jokingly citing them as his first names. This is a reference to {{w|Thomas the Tank Engine}} from the long-running children's TV show {{w|Thomas & Friends}}, and the show's first narrator, {{w|Ringo Starr}}.
*According to Monty, Dempsey holds the universe record of most one armed pushups; even in the universe where everyone only has one arm.
**It is also revealed that Dempsey once made a bet that he could eat fifty hard boiled eggs in fifteen minutes for fifteen dollars. He regretted it almost immediately after winning and feared for his life.
**Monty reveals that once Dempsey had gotten his testicles painfully stuck in bedsprings and lived to tell the tale.
**Further more, Dempsey is able to throw a grenade farther than a professional baseball player.
**Despite being in the [[United States Marine Corps|Marines]], he's often referred to as a soldier.
**His favorite weapons are the [[BAR]], [[M16]], and any type of heavy weaponry ranging from heavy machine guns to [[Wonder Weapons]].
*Dempsey's default outfit introduced in ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops II]]'' appears as the skin, "Bootcamp", for his [[Dark Aether]] [["Tank" Dempsey (Dark Aether)|counterpart]] in ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops 7]]''.
