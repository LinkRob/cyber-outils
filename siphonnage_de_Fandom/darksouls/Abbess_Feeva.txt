TITLE : Abbess Feeva

[[File:Abbess Feeva - 01.png|right|280px]]
'''Abbess Feeva''' is a white [[Phantom (Dark Souls II)|phantom]] appearing in {{DaSIISK}} and {{DaSIIIK|1}}.

==Location==
===Crown of the Sunken King===
She is available for summoning in the fight with [[Sinh, the Slumbering Dragon]]. Her summoning sign can be found in the [[Dragon's Rest]], across from [[Transcendent Edde]]'s summoning sign.

===Crown of the Ivory King===
She is available for summoning in the fight with [[Lud, the King's Pet & Zallen, the King's Pet|Lud and Zallen]]. Her summoning sign can be found at the beginning of [[Frigid Outskirts]], near the statues.

==Strategy==
She only uses [[Miracles (Dark Souls II)|miracles]] such as [[Great Lightning Spear (Dark Souls II)|Great Lightning Spear]] and [[Blinding Bolt]] in combat, can cast [[Sacred Oath]] and will heal the party with [[Soothing Sunlight (Dark Souls II)|Soothing Sunlight]] if a party member's [[HP (Dark Souls II)|HP]] is reduced to a dangerously low amount. Considering Sinh has fairly high [[elemental]] defenses, she doesn't have the ability to do sufficient damage to the dragon, so her strong points are supporting the party and tanking hits.
