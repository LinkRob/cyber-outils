The '''Abyss''' is a [[location]] in ''[[Dark Souls]]'', as well as a [[storyline]] concept featured in all three games in the series.

The in-game location is at the heart of the [[New Londo Ruins]], a featureless expanse where the [[Chosen Undead]] must slay the [[Four Kings]] and can encounter [[Darkstalker Kaathe]]. As a concept, "Abyss" is the name given to both a force that consumes or corrupts most forms of life, and to the areas in which it dwells; it is tied to the [[Age of Dark]], and also gave birth to a twisted offshoot known as [[The Deep]].

==Lore==
The Abyss is a realm of Dark born from the [[Dark Soul]] or its [[Humanity (item)|fragments]]. It's an ecosystem capable of creating life, as generated by one of the core components of the First Flame, and of expanding while slowly absorbing  everything in its path; it is uninhabitable to all beings aligned with [[First Flame|Fire]] including the [[Gods]], as they require special equipment or protection to prevent corruption (both in the spiritual and physical sense). In contrast, any beings aligned with or indifferent to the Abyss (such as [[Humans]]) are able to roam it with no risk of harm or corruption.<ref>''Dark Souls Trilogy Compendium, Universal Concepts: "The Abyss, Manus, and the Fragments of Dark"''</ref>

[[Knight Artorias]] famously traversed New Londo's Abyss and hunted the [[Darkwraith (enemy)|Darkwraiths]] therein.<ref>[[Greatsword of Artorias]] description.</ref> He was protected by the power of a magical ring, which he gained after making a covenant with the beasts there.<ref>[[Covenant of Artorias]] description.</ref> The ring prevented him from being swallowed by the void, but it led to his [[Greatsword of Artorias|sword]] becoming [[Greatsword of Artorias (Cursed)|cursed]].<ref>[[Greatsword of Artorias (Cursed)]] description.</ref> In recognition of his actions, he was awarded one of [[Anor Londo|Anor Londo's]] treasures, a [[Silver Pendant]] that allowed him to repel Dark [[Sorcery (element)|sorceries]];<ref>[[Silver Pendant]] description.</ref> eventually he traveled to [[Oolacile]] to combat the threat of [[Manus, Father of the Abyss|Manus]], the lord of the Abyss. He ultimately failed in his mission he was overtaken by the dark, leaving behind his companion Sif.<ref>[[Greatshield of Artorias]] description.</ref>

==Location ==
In Dark Souls the Abyss is located at the end of the [[New Londo Ruins]]. To reach to the Abyss, the floodgates must be lowered and the [[Covenant of Artorias]] ring, a reward for killing [[Great Grey Wolf Sif]], must be worn. After climbing down a spiral staircase within a tower, the path will abruptly end, making it necessary to descend into the darkness of the Abyss.

The [[DLC]] ''[[Artorias of the Abyss]]'' allows us to explore a more recent [[Chasm of the Abyss|Abyss]], despite being located in a past era, deep within the dungeons of [[Oolacile]].

==General information==
Dropping into the Abyss will immediately trigger the area boss, the [[Four Kings]], to spawn. After the Four Kings have been defeated, a [[Bonfire (Dark Souls)|bonfire]] will appear. At this point, the Abyss will cease being hostile, making the Covenant of Artorias ring useless.

==Notes==
*It is possible to reach and kill the Four Kings by killing [[Ingward]], even if the player hasn't acquired the [[Lordvessel]] yet.
*The bonfire that appears ''will'' allow warping to escape the Abyss, even without the Lordvessel.
**This can be used as a shortcut for far away locations, such as [[Anor Londo]], [[Tomb of the Giants]] or [[Ash Lake]]. If the player avoids resting at other bonfires, the Abyss can be used as a "home base", warping to locations in [[Lordran]], and returning via [[Homeward Bone]].
*Although it appears to be a black void of nothingness, the "ground" of the abyss actually floats a few meters above an unseen cobblestone floor. This can be observed by using a light source, such as the [[Skull Lantern]] or [[Sunlight Maggot]].
*Items that require hitting a surface to activate, such as [[Prism Stone (Dark Souls)|Prism Stones]] and [[Carvings]], will not work in the Abyss as they simply fall through the "floor".

==Characters==
[[Darkstalker Kaathe]] will appear after the boss has been defeated. The player cannot have placed the [[Lordvessel]] under [[Kingseeker Frampt]]'s guidance yet; if they do so before having met Kaathe, Kaathe will not appear.
