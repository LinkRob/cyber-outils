The '''Abyss Greatsword''' is a [[boss soul weapon]] and [[Greatswords (Dark Souls)|greatsword]] in ''[[Dark Souls]]''. It was introduced in the ''[[Artorias of the Abyss]]'' DLC.

{{Description
|First paragraph  = This greatsword belonged to Lord Gwyn's Knight Artorias, who fell to the abyss.
|Second paragraph = Swallowed by the Dark with its master, this sword is tainted by the abyss, and now its strength reflects its wielder's humanity.}}

==Availability==
The Abyss Greatsword is created by [[Weapon Modification|ascending]] any [[Standard path|Standard]] +10 [[Greatswords (Dark Souls)|greatsword]], [[Ultra Greatswords|ultra greatsword]], [[Straight Swords (Dark Souls)|straight sword]] (other than the [[Broken Straight Sword]] or [[Straight Sword Hilt]]) or [[Daggers|dagger]] at the [[Giant Blacksmith]] in [[Anor Londo]] with the [[Soul of Artorias]] and 5,000 [[soul]]s.

==Characteristics==
Compared to its [[Greatsword of Artorias|Greatsword of Artorias']] true version, the Abyss Greatsword has a distinct moveset, lower stat requirements, weighs one unit less, has a heavily deteriorated appearance, and deals purely physical damage. The strong attacks are reminiscent of those performed by [[Knight Artorias|Artorias]] himself. Despite requiring 18 [[Faith (Dark Souls)|Faith]] and [[Intelligence (Dark Souls)|Intelligence]], the Abyss Greatsword does not scale off of these parameters. This should discourage most builds from utilizing this weapon.

Unusual for a weapon, the Abyss Greatsword has fairly good [[magic resistance]], although most [[shields]] are still preferable.

Like [[Chaos (upgrade path)|Chaos]] weapons, the Abyss Greatsword scales with [[Humanity (mechanic)|Humanity]] (up to 10). When fully upgraded, with 10 [[Humanity (mechanic)|Humanity]], and 40/40 in Strength and Dexterity, the Abyss Greatsword deals the most raw physical damage among all of the [[Greatswords (Dark Souls)|Greatsword]] class weapons, beaten only by [[Ultra Greatswords|ultra greatswords]] and Great Hammers/Axes in this regard. This damage difference is not significant and when factoring in that Abyss Greatsword cannot be [[Weapon Augmentation|buffed]], other weapons are capable of vastly out performing this sword without putting points into two different casting stats.

If one does not meet the Intelligence and Faith requirements but still meet the required Strength and Dexterity, they will still be able to do full damage with the sword, but, will get a stagger animation that makes them immune to backstabs. This occurs because since the Intelligence and Faith requirements don't actually affect the damage output, but rather the Strength and Dexterity, they can completely disregard investing to get the Intelligence and Faith stats to 18. However, the drawback to this is that it will not allow one to do any follow ups, as once this sword makes contact, it will bounce off, stopping the user from doing anything for a few seconds.
