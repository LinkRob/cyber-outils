==Trivia==
*An instrumental version of this song was used in [[Pokémon Puzzle League]] as Ash's theme music.
*Fire and Flame were the same thing so it was like Fire was repeated. The type that was left out was Poison.
*The sheet music for this song is included in the Pokémon 2.B.A. Master piano book, Pokémon Five-Finger Piano and Pokémon 2.B.A. Master: Recorder Fun!.
*Although the lyrics sung are "R U ready 2 meet and defeat... The Elite?", the official lyrics have "Elite Four", instead of "Elite" by itself.
**In the Danish and Norwegian versions, the song is more not mentioning "The Elite", but instead they sang about fighting the [[Giovanni|leader]] of [[Team Rocket]].
