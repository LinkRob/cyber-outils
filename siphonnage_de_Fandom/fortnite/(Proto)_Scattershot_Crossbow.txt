The '''[Proto] Scattershot Crossbow''' is a [[Crossbows|Crossbow]] in [[Fortnite]] that are currently unreleased.

== Functionality ==
The [Proto] Scattershot Crossbow fire a projectile that explodes after traveling 100m.

The [Proto] Scattershot Crossbow fire a '''Fast Moving Projectile''' that is affected by gravity and does not have '''First Shot Accuracy''' enabled.

The [Proto] Scattershot Crossbow can be aimed down sights while reloading and be aimed down sights while in midair.

== Stats ==
The [Proto] Scattershot Crossbow use Ininifte {{Ammunition|Crossbow Bolts}} and has a headshot multiplier of 1.5x.
