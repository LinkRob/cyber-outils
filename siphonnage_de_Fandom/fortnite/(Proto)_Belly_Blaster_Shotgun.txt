The '''[Proto] Belly Blaster Shotgun''' is a [[Shotguns|Shotgun]] in [[Fortnite]] that is currently unreleased. 

[Proto] Belly Blaster Shotgun is an early version of the [[Chaos Reloader Shotgun]].

== Functionality ==
The [Proto] Belly Blaster Shotgun is a single shot, longer range shotgun. It has a tighter spread than other shotguns and must reload after each shot.

The [Proto] Belly Blaster Shotgun is a '''Hitscan''' weapon that fires a spread of 10 pellets when both aiming down sights and hipfiring. Aiming down sights tightens the spread of pellets.

The [Proto] Belly Blaster Shotgun can be aimed down sights while reloading.
