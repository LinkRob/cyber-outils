''''Loon Pawntoon''' was a [[Landmarks|Landmark]] in [[Fortnite: Battle Royale]] added in [[Chapter 3: Season 4]] to the island [[Artemis]]. The location is the original [[Pawntoon]] [[Landmarks|Landmark]], with jets attached to the bottom of it, allowing it to float above the water. 

== History ==
=== [[Chapter 3: Season 4]] ===
* [[Update v22.00]]: '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is added to the island, becoming an airborne version of [[Pawntoon]].
* [[Fracture]]: '''{{PAGENAME}}''' was destroyed by [[The Herald]].
