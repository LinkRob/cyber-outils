The '''[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun (Exotic)''' is a [[Submachine Guns|Submachine Gun]] in [[Fortnite]] that is currently unreleased.

[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun (Exotic) is a {{Exotic}} version of the [[Deodorant Applicator]].

== Functionality ==
[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun (Exotic) is an extremely fast-firing, low damage ray gun. It has first-shot accuracy and uses infinite ammo, and will fire continously before cooling down. [PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun (Exotic) is intended to have a special function, however its function was never properly implemented.

Firing the item reduces the durability meter. Once the durability meter reaches 0%, the item breaks and is removed from the player's inventory.

== Stats ==
[PH] Kang and Kodos Ray Gun (Exotic) uses infinite {{Ammunition|Energy Cells}} and has a headshot multiplier of 1.5x.
