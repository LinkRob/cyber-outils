'''100% Island''' is one of 34 islands surrounding [[Whole Cake Island]] as part of [[Charlotte Linlin|Big Mom]]'s territory, [[Totto Land]]. It is run by [[Charlotte Smoothie]], the {{Nihongo|Minister of Juice|ジュース大臣|Jūsu Daijin}}.{{Qref|name=mag4}}

==Layout==
===Kaju Town===
{{Nihongo|'''Kaju Town'''|カジュータウン|Kajū Taun}} is a city situated on the island.{{Qref|name=mag4}} ''Kajū'' (果汁) means "fruit juice" in Japanese.

==History==
===Past===
Less than 62 years ago, 100% Island became part of the [[Big Mom Pirates]]' territory [[Totto Land]], and [[Charlotte Smoothie]] was appointed the minister in charge of this island.{{Qref|name=mag4}}

==Trivia==
*The name of the island is a possible reference to 100% juice or pure fruit juices, those which do not have artificial additives.
