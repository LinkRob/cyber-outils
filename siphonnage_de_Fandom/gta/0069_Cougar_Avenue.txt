'''0069 Cougar Avenue''' is a [[Safehouses in GTA Online|safehouse]] located on [[Cougar Avenue]] in [[Del Perro]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]112,000 and includes a 2-car garage.

==Trivia==
*[[69]] is a clear reference to the sex position, another example of [[Rockstar Games|Rockstar's]] obscene adult humor.
**''[http://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=Cougar Cougar]'' is slang for an older woman who prefers younger men, further adding to the humor.
