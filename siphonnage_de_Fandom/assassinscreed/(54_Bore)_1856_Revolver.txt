The '''(54 Bore) 1856 Revolver''', or properly the '''Beaumont-Adams revolver''', is a [[pistol|revolver]] adopted by the [[British Army]] in 1856 that uses {{wiki|.442 Webley|.442 calibre (54-bore)}} cartridges.

Its relatively small size makes the [[Firearm|gun]] easily concealable, and in 1868, the [[United Kingdom|British]] [[British Brotherhood of Assassins|Assassins]] [[Evie Frye|Evie]] and [[Jacob Frye]] were rewarded with this gun by Sergeant [[Frederick Abberline]] of the [[Metropolitan Police Service]] after they helped him lock up several [[Blighters]].<ref>''[[Assassin's Creed: Syndicate]]''</ref>
