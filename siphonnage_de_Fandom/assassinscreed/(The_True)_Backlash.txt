'''(The True) Backlash''' was an altered representation of a fragmented [[genetic memory]].<ref>''[[Assassin's Creed: Brotherhood of Venice]] – [[Roma (expansion pack)|Roma]]''</ref>

==Intro==
''Finally, the entrance to the [[Colosseum Vault|Temple]] is within [[Italian Brotherhood of Assassins|our]] reach. We have to escort [[Ezio Auditore da Firenze|Ezio]] and help him activate the two parts of the mechanism that will him ''[sic]'' to unlock access to the Temple.<br><br>Then, we will have to wait for Ezio to put the [[Venetian Staff of Eden|Staff]] in safety permanently by preventing the [[Followers of Romulus|Followers]] from locating the Temple's entrance.<br><br>Outraged that the Followers had used him to deceive us, [[Leonardo da Vinci]] sent a copy of his legendary [[tank]]... If we find it, it will help us clean the way.''

==Objectives==
*'''1.''' Escort Ezio to open access to the Temple by accomplishing the 2 [3 Objective token] Objectives. '''Ezio must be present on each square where 1+ [[Assassins]] spend 1+ [action cube] to complete each Objective.'''
*'''2.''' [!] As soon as the 2 [3 Objective token] Objectives are completed, Ezio enters the Temple: Remove his miniature from the game. Take the 4 Special Event cards from envelope R2.4, shuffle them and place them on top of the Event deck. You must now wait for him to come out by getting rid of as many nearby Enemies as possible.
*'''3.''' When Ezio comes back out, escort him to the [[Fast travel station|Fast Travel Station]].

==Debrief==
''Mission accomplished! The [[Staves of Eden|Staff of Eden]] is safe where the Templars will never be able to reach it.<br><br>As Ezio prepares to [[Assassin's Creed: Revelations|leave]] for the [[Middle East|Orient]], his sister [[Claudia Auditore da Firenze|Claudia]] is now well established at the [[Assassin leader|head]] of the Brotherhood. We will finally be able to take a little break.<br><br>The [[Templars]] have missed a master stroke. We shouldn't be hearing from the Followers of Romulus for a long time.<br><br>This could not have been accomplished without our team of Assassins!''
