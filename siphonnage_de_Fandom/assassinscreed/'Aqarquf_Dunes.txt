The '''<nowiki>'</nowiki>Aqarquf Dunes''' was a region of the {{Wiki|Arabian Desert}} located in [[Iraq]], north of [[Baghdad]]. It was named after the ancient {{Wiki|Kassites|Kassite}} city of [[Dur-Kurigalzu]], also known by its modern name of Aqarquf. Sometime during the 860s, the [[Assassins|Hidden One]] [[Basim Ibn Ishaq]] visited the dunes and the ancient city.<ref name="ACM">''[[Assassin's Creed: Mirage]]''</ref>
