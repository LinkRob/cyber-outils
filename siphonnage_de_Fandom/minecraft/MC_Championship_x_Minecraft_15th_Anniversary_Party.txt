The '''MC Championship x Minecraft 15th Anniversary Party''' was a limited-time [[Event servers|event server]] hosted by [[Mojang Studios]] and developed in collaboration with Noxcrew's MCC. The event was open starting on July 22 at 10 AM PST / 7 PM CET and ran until August 4 at 3 PM PST / August 5 at 12 AM CET. The server was accessible only via [[Bedrock Edition]].

The event served as the means to obtain several [[Character Creator]] items that were made for the event, including the MCC Party [[Cape]]. These items could be obtained by playing the four mini-games hosted as part of the event; playing the games allowed players to obtain coins, which could be spent at shops in the Main Lobby to obtain items which were exclusive to the event. Many of these items were needed to complete 15 challenges that involved fulfilling objectives in the four mini-games as well as in other aspects of the event.

== Locations ==
The event took place across five locations, with each being needed to complete the challenges presented at the event. The locations are accessible by walking through portals in the world or by using the Teleport Map.

=== Main Lobby ===
The Main Lobby is the default location where every player starts after joining the server. Its interactive character is '''Jens & Agnes'''. It contains portals that take the player to the mini-game rooms. The '''Ender Cup''' announcement is also present at the Main Lobby.

==== Shops ====
The Main Lobby contains shops, in which the player can spend coins to buy most items - several of which are needed to complete challenges. The shops include: Foods, Hats, Mascot Hats, and Toys.

==== Mysterious Cave ====
This location is found within the Main Lobby. It contains seven pictures that convey hints on what to do to complete the seven mystery puzzles. For each of the first seven days of the event's existence, the interactive character '''Puzzle Helper''' would have a new dialogue further hinting at what must be done to complete one of the seven puzzles.

=== Grid Runners ===
Grid Runners is a mini-game where players must use the limited time to complete objectives in four different areas with various cooperative challenges. Its interactive character is '''Smajor'''. Players had to join the queue to play this game. A set of players would be teleported to the game area when it was ready.

Grid Runners is involved in the completion of the Green Geckos Challenge, the Aqua Axolotls Challenge, the Purple Pandas Challenge, and the Grid Runners Challenge.

=== Sands of Time ===
Sands of Time is a mini-game where players must collect Coins and Chest Keys then escape the dungeon before [[dying]] or allowing the time to run out. Its interactive character is '''HBomb94'''. Players were able to join the game room at any time.

Sand of Time is involved in the completion of the Cyan Coyotes Challenge, the Blue Bats Challenge, and the Sands of Time Challenge.

=== Meltdown ===
Meltdown is a mini-game where players must use bows to freeze monsters and punch the monsters to destroy them, while escaping each room as it starts deteriorating. Its interactive character is '''hannahxxrose'''. Players had to join the queue to play this game. A set of players would be teleported to the game area when it was ready.

Meltdown is involved in the completion of the Yellow Yaks Challenge, the Cyan Coyotes Challenge, and the Meltdown Challenge.

=== Ace Race ===
Ace Race is a mini-game where players must maneuver across a long path while using unique pad blocks that assist the player's movement. Its interactive character is '''Seapeekay'''. Players were able to join the game room at any time.

Ace Race is involved in the completion of the Red Rabbits Challenge, the Orange Ocelots Challenge, the Pink Parrots Challenge, and the Ace Race Challenge.

== Items ==
[[Items]] of different types could be given and used at the event. The items used in the server exist exclusively as part of the event.

=== Inventory ===
These items were automatically placed in the player's inventory after entering a specific location. They serve as extensions of the user interface to allow the player to navigate various aspects of the server. They are:

* '''Challenge List''' - The Challenge List allows the player to see the list of challenges, their objectives, and the progress made on each challenge. It is given to the player after joining the event, and is removed if the player is participating in a mini-game. It is given back when the mini-game is over.
* '''Cosmetic Chest''' - The Cosmetic Chest allows the player to choose to wear one of any Hats or Mascot Hats they own. It also provides the option to remove any Hat or Mascot Hat from the player's head. It is given to the player after joining the event, and is removed if the player is participating in a mini-game. It is given back when the mini-game is over.
* '''Teleport Map''' - The Teleport Map allows the player to go to any of the five server locations without using portals. It is given to the player after joining the event, and is removed if the player is participating in a mini-game. It is given back when the mini-game is over.
* '''Leave Game''' - This item allows the player to leave a mini-game that is in session. If used, the player is taken to that game's lobby.
* '''Blank Paintbrush''' - This item is given to players in the Copy the Painting section of Grid Runners. It can be converted into any Paintbrush item by using it on a paint bucket.

Foods are purchased from the Foods shop in the Main Lobby by spending Coins. They are [[Food|edible]] items that give the consumer status [[effects]]. A description of the item is shown for a few seconds upon purchase. The Foods are:

* '''Cotton Candy''' (10 Coins) - "Cotton Candy! Eat it to get a jump boost!" (Grants [[Jump Boost]] III for 10 seconds.)
* '''Popcorn''' (10 Coins) - "Popcorn! Eat it to get a jump boost!" (Grants Jump Boost III for 10 seconds.)
* '''Tasty Burger''' (20 Coins) - "Burger! Eat it to get a speed & jump boost!" (Grants Speed II for 10 seconds and Jump Boost III for 10 seconds.)
* '''Tasty Hot Dog''' (20 Coins) - "Hot Dog! Eat it to get a speed & jump boost!" (Grants Speed II for 10 seconds and Jump Boost III for 10 seconds.)
* '''Cupcake''' (20 Coins) - "Cupcake! Eat it to get slow falling!" (Grants [[Slow Falling]] II for 10 seconds.)
* '''Cake Slice''' (20 Coins) - "Cake! Eat it to get levitation!" (Grants [[Levitation]] II for 3 seconds and Slow Falling II for 10 seconds.)
* '''Bowl of Chips''' (20 Coins) - "Chips! Eat it to get a speed boost!" (Grants Speed II for 10 seconds.)
* '''Cookie''' (20 Coins) - "Cookie! Eat it to get a jump boost!" (Grants Jump Boost III for 10 seconds.)
* '''Healthy Wrap''' (20 Coins) - "Super Wrap! Eat it to get all the boosts!" (Grants Speed II for 10 seconds, Jump Boost III for 10 seconds, and Slow Falling II for 10 seconds.)

* '''Propeller Hat'''
* '''Cake Crown'''
* '''Birthday Present Hat'''
* '''Jester Hat'''

=== Transformed Items ===
These items are created as a result of using other items. These can either appear in the player's own inventory, or in the inventory of a recipient player, depending on the original item. They are:

* '''Gift Box''' - Created by using a Gifting Box on another player. When used,
* '''Pizza Slice''' - Created by using a Box of Pizza on another player. Grants Speed II for 10 seconds when eaten.
* '''Black Paintbrush''', '''Blue Paintbrush''', '''Brown Paintbrush''', '''Green Paintbrush''', '''Purple Paintbrush''', '''Red Paintbrush''', '''White Paintbrush''', '''Yellow Paintbrush''' - Items that are created by applying a Blank Paintbrush or another Paintbrush item to a paint bucket. When used, a Paintbrush shoots balls of paint of the matching color forward. These are required to complete the Copy the Painting section of Grid Runners.

=== Found Items ===
Found items are obtained from being at specific locations within a world. All examples of these are found in Sands of Time. If unused when the mini-game is over, they are removed from the player's inventory. They are:

* '''Iron Key''' - Obtained from an iron key spot in Sands of Time. These can be used one time to open an Iron Chest.
* '''Gold Key''' - Obtained from a gold key spot in Sands of Time. These can be used one time to open a Gold Chest.
* '''Diamond Key''' - Obtained from a diamond key spot in Sands of Time. These can be used one time to open a Diamond Chest.
* '''Netherite Key''' - Obtained from a netherite key spot in Sands of Time. These can be used one time to open a Netherite Chest.
