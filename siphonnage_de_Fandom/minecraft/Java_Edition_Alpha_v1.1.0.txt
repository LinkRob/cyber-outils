'''Alpha v1.1.0''', also known as '''Seecret Friday 9''',<ref name="blogpost">[[wordofnotch:1098183801|"Seecret Friday 9! OooooOOOoo! And Minecraft Alpha 1.1.0! And Minecraft Server 0.2.0!"]] – [[The Word of Notch]], September 10, 2010</ref> is a version of [[Alpha]], and also the ninth and last [[Seecret Friday]] update, released with [[Alpha server 0.2.0 01|server 0.2.0_01]] on September 10, 2010.

==Additions== 
===Items===
;[[File:Compass JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Compass]]
*Always points towards the world spawn point.

== Changes==
*Experimental switch to turn on monsters in [[multiplayer]].
*The [[inventory]] is now saved on multiplayer servers.
*[[Lava bucket]]s can be used as [[fuel]] to smelt up to 100 items.
*[[Clay]] is more common when a world is generated.
*Servers will now save player locations upon leaving the server.
*Removed the model of a [[mob]] inside [[mob spawner]] due to lag on old computers.

==Fixes ==
;2 bugs fixed
*[[Torch]]es can no longer be placed on [[leaves]], but only with fancy graphics.
* Chests, signs and furnaces now work in multiplayer.

== Reupload==
Alpha v1.1.0 was reuploaded on September 13, 2010,{{verify|edition=java}} to fix a bug where the player could force the camera's movement inside walls. The version found in the launcher is the pre-reupload version, but is recompiled differently and has a different checksum.</onlyinclude>
