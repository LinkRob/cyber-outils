'''MINECON 2013''' was held in Orlando, Florida from November 2 to 3.
Official Tumblr: https://mineconorlando.tumblr.com/.

[[File:One.ogg|thumb|The song "One" from [[C418]]'s soundtrack from the [[Story of Mojang]] documentary was the song embedded in the 2013 site.]]

== Announcement ==
Mojang revealed on June 27 that '''MINECON 2013''' would be held in Orlando, Florida from November 2–3.<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130809010950/https://mojang.com/2013/07/where-in-the-world-is-minecon-2013/|title=Where in the world is Minecon 2013?|author=Owen Jones|website=Mojang.com|date=July 1, 2013}}</ref> It was also said that tickets/hotel deals would later be available for sale in July.

After a lot of mystery, it was officially confirmed by Mojang that MINECON 2013 would be hosted in the '''[[wikipedia:Orange County Convention Center|Orange County Convention Center]]''' (OCCC).<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130806003705/https://mojang.com/2013/07/minecon-tickets-are-here/|title=MINECON tickets are here!|author=Lydia Winters|website=Mojang.com|date=July 31, 2013}}</ref> At one point, it was listed on the OCCC's calendar of scheduled events. Due to popularity directed toward OCCC's website, MINECON was taken off the list for upcoming events. 
Some time after all the tickets where sold, MINECON was added to the OCCC Exhibitor On-line Services calendar<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130909090420/http://www.occc.net/iEBMS/coe/coe_p1_all.aspx?oc=10&cc=COEexorder|title=OCC Exhibitor On-line Services calendar|website=Orange County Convention Center|date=September 2013}}</ref> and the Show Banner Orders calendar.<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130907025632/http://www.occc.net/iEBMS/coe/coe_p1_all.aspx?oc=10&cc=COESMBANNER|title=Show Banner Orders calendar|website=Orange County Convention Center|date=September 2013}}</ref> Also a page for exhibiters to get electrical, lighting and other booth utilities was created.<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140418203106/http://www.occc.net/iEBMS/wbe/wbe_p1_main.aspx?&oc=10&cc=WBE1034905|title=Minecon Event Website|website=Orange County Convention Center|date=Septmeber 2013}}</ref>

== Age restriction ==
Mojang originally intended the age restriction to be without an adult to be 17 years old, (16 and under with an adult) but after further research and consideration, they lowered it to 14 years of age (13 and under with an adult). They offered a refund for this reason, and tickets refunded were put back on sale if accepted.<ref name="Con2013">{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130911074756/https://www.mojang.com/2013/08/minecon-2013-2/|title=MINECON 2013|author=Vu Bui|website=Mojang.com|date=August 6, 2013}}</ref>

== Tickets ==
Early on July 31, Mojang confirmed that tickets would start going on sale later that day. These tickets were said to be released in batches of 3,<ref name="eb"/> each selling 2,500 tickets and at $150 a piece. (7,500 tickets in total).<ref name="Con2013"/> This system was intended to give people who may have school, or a job a chance to get tickets. Also, Mojang announced that 7,500 tickets is the total number, no more, they weren't selling at-door tickets, and they didn't give away tickets to the press for free.<ref name="Con2013"/> They also gave attendees discounted hotel bookings.<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190710064621/https://book.passkey.com/g/20065700|title=MINECON 2013|website=Cvent Passkey}}</ref>

== Cape ==
[[File:MINECON 2013 Cape.png|thumb|The MINECON 2013 cape.]]
On September 5, Mojang posted that attendees who went to MINECON would receive a 2013 MINECON [[cape]].<ref>{{citation|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130819125137/http://help.mojang.com/customer/portal/articles/1246918-minecon-2013|title=Minecon 2013|website=Mojang Support Center|date=August 12, 2013}}</ref> Further details regarding the capes were stated to be "released at a later date."

On October 29, Mojang stated that capes were sent as a redemption URL to the email address registered when the ticket was purchased. The capes were sent out through email with the message: "A popular part of going to MINECON is that you receive an in-game cape for your character to show everyone online that you were there." "This year's edition comes in jade green with a handsome [[piston]] fitted on the center back. Follow the link below and log in to minecraft.net (with your usual credentials) and it will automatically get added to your character." - Lydia Winters{{cn}}

== Controversy ==
Mojang and their respective ticket sales group, [http://www.eventbrite.com/home/ Eventbrite], received controversy for the system of purchasing tickets this year. The three batches were swiftly sold out (the first within three seconds), and while they were purchased, the text "Pending" showed while the website didn't handle well (Eventbrite). Because of this, many tweets of controversy appeared, mostly under the hashtag "Minecon", and a [http://www.change.org/petitions/mojang-ab-minecon-2013-ticket-failure-sell-minecon-2013-tickets-at-the-door?utm_campaign=signature_receipt&utm_medium=email&utm_source=share_petition Change.org petition] was made.

== Contests ==
Minecon 2013 hosted an official costume contest and an official build contest - the last time this occurred.

Contestant fyrflies successfully placed in both the Build Contest and the Costume Contest.

=== Build Contest ===
The build contest was given the theme of "Halloween" due to the close proximity to the event. The build contest actually ran a few weeks ahead of the convention itself - with the winners being announced at the closing ceremony.

Tewkesape/theo_the_ape is the only participant to have placed in the top three for both the 2013 and 2012 official build contests.
