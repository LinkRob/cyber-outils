This page serves as a compact list of all unique [[particle]] textures which have ever existed in officially released versions of ''Minecraft''. For an overview on when each texture was changed, see the appropriate [[History of textures]] subpages.

Note: the textures shown in this page are upscaled tenfold for display purposes.

== Current textures ==

[[Tint]]ed particles have a pink background, untinted particles have a purple background.
