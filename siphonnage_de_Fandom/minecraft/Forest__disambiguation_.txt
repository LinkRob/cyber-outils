A '''{{BiomeLink|forest}}''' is a common biome with many trees.

'''Forest''' may also refer to:

* {{BiomeLink|Flower forest}}, a biome filled with [[oak]] and [[birch|birch trees]], and multicoloured [[flower|flowers]]
* {{BiomeLink|Birch forest}}, a biome with tall birch trees and [[Wildflowers]]
* {{BiomeLink|Dark forest}}, a dense roofed forest biome with thick [[Dark oak|dark oak trees]] and [[Huge mushroom]]s
* {{BiomeLink|Crimson forest}}, a crimson red Nether fungus forest biome with [[Huge fungus|huge crimson fungi trees]] and [[Fungus|crimson fungi sprouts]]
* {{BiomeLink|Warped forest}}, a cyan Nether fungus forest biome with [[Huge fungus|huge warped fungi trees]] and [[Fungus|warped fungi sprouts]]

Other forested area:
* {{BiomeLink|Swamp}}, a biome filled with [[oak|swamp oak]], [[Huge mushroom]] and [[Lily Pad]]s
* {{BiomeLink|Jungle}}, a bright green dense biome filled with tall [[jungle|jungle trees]] and [[Jungle Bush]]es
* {{BiomeLink|Taiga}}, a cold biome filled with [[spruce|spruce trees]] and [[Sweet Berries]]
* {{BiomeLink|Snowy taiga}}, a taiga biome variant, but with [[snow]]
* {{BiomeLink|Old growth taiga}}, a taiga biome variant, but with huge spruce trees, [[Forest rock]]s, [[podzol]], [[Coarse Dirt]] and [[Mushroom]]s
* {{BiomeLink|Windswept forest}}, a taiga biome variant, but generates on hills and consists of few spruce trees and open areas
* {{BiomeLink|Wooded Badlands}}, a dry arid badlands biome variant, but with oak trees and coarse dirt
