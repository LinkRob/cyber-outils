This is the list of all development versions of [[Bedrock Edition 26.20]].

= 26.20.20 =
'''Bedrock Edition 26.20.20''' is the first beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 26.20]], released on March 10, 2026.

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Grass Block]]s
** Now creates tall grass when fertilized with [[bone meal]].
* [[Torch]]es
** Recipe can now be unlocked by acquiring either of the following items:
*** {{ItemLink|Stick}}
*** {{ItemLink|Coal}}
*** {{ItemLink|Charcoal}}
*** Any {{ItemLink|Pickaxe}}

=== Mobs ===
* Baby mobs
** No longer show green growth particles when fed on when the growth is paused.

= 26.20.21 =
'''Bedrock Edition 26.20.21''' is the second beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 26.20]], released on March 17, 2026.

== Changes ==
=== General ===
* [[Recipe book]]
** Added an unlocking recipe system for [[smelting]] recipe.
** Only available under "Furnace Recipe Book" experimental toggle.

=== Mobs ===
* Baby mobs
** Baby spawn sounds now play with a correct pitch on:
*** {{EntityLink|Pig}}
*** {{EntityLink|Cat}}
*** {{EntityLink|Chicken}}
*** {{EntityLink|Horse}}
*** {{EntityLink|Wolf}}
* Baby [[wolves]]
** Hind legs now rotated correctly while sitting.
* [[Rabbit]]s
** Its idle animation has been disabled when leashed with [[lead]].
* Baby [[zombie]]s
** No longer levitate when riding baby mobs.

= 26.20.22 =
'''Bedrock Edition 26.20.22''' is the third beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 26.20]], released on March 25, 2026.

== Changes ==
=== General ===
* Party system
** Added an ability to chat on parties.
*** Shares the same functionality as you chat on in-game worlds.
* [[Realms]]
** Added Realms Hub, a central place for Realm.
*** Replaces Realms stories and all old settings menus.

'''Bedrock Edition 26.20.23''' (for Preview) and '''Bedrock Edition 26.20.24''' (for beta) are fourth beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 26.20]], released on March 31, 2026.

== Additions ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Cinnabar]] Block Set
** A new reddish-hue colored blocks found in [[Sulfur Caves|sulfur caves]].
** These include:
*** {{BlockLink|Cinnabar Stairs}}
*** {{BlockLink|Cinnabar Slab}}
*** {{BlockLink|Cinnabar Wall}}
*** {{BlockLink|Polished Cinnabar}}
**** {{BlockLink|Polished Cinnabar Stairs}}
**** {{BlockLink|Polished Cinnabar Slab}}
**** {{BlockLink|Polished Cinnabar Wall}}
*** {{BlockLink|Cinnabar Bricks}}
**** {{BlockLink|Cinnabar Brick Stairs}}
**** {{BlockLink|Cinnabar Brick Slab}}
**** {{BlockLink|Cinnabar Brick Wall}}
*** {{BlockLink|Chiseled Cinnabar}}
** Like any [[stone]]-related blocks, it can only be mined with a [[pickaxe]].
** Only available under "Drop 2 of 2026" experimental toggle.
* [[Sulfur]] Block Set
** A new sickly yellowish-colored blocks found in [[Sulfur Caves|sulfur caves]].
** These include:
*** {{BlockLink|Sulfur Stairs}}
*** {{BlockLink|Sulfur Slab}}
*** {{BlockLink|Sulfur Wall}}
*** {{BlockLink|Polished Sulfur}}
**** {{BlockLink|Polished Sulfur Stairs}}
**** {{BlockLink|Polished Sulfur Slab}}
**** {{BlockLink|Polished Sulfur Wall}}
*** {{BlockLink|Sulfur Bricks}}
**** {{BlockLink|Sulfur Brick Stairs}}
**** {{BlockLink|Sulfur Brick Slab}}
**** {{BlockLink|Sulfur Brick Wall}}
*** {{BlockLink|Chiseled Sulfur}}
** Like any stone-related blocks, it can only be mined with a [[pickaxe]].
** Only available under "Drop 2 of 2026" experimental toggle.
* [[Potent Sulfur]]
** A new sulfur block variant that emits gas bubbles when placed on underwater.
** Inflicts [[nausea]] when a player or mob gets close to or enters a sulfur pool.
** Like any stone-related blocks, it can only be mined with a [[pickaxe]].
** Only available under "Drop 2 of 2026" experimental toggle.

=== Items ===
* [[Sulfur Cube Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to spawn [[sulfur cubes]].
** Spawns in random size.
** Only available under "Drop 2 of 2026" experimental toggle.
* [[Bucket of Sulfur Cube]]
** Can be obtained by using a bucket on a sulfur cube.
** Spawns sulfur cube when spawned.
** Only available under "Drop 2 of 2026" experimental toggle.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Sulfur Cube]]
** A new passive [[Slime (disambiguation)|slime-like mob]] that mostly spawns in sulfur caves.
** Like [[slime]]s and [[magma cube]]s, they split into two upon death.
*** However, smaller cubes can grow to larger versions.
** Has 9 effects depending on the type of block absorbed:
*** Bouncy - makes it very bouncy
*** Fast Flat - less bouncy, slides more often
*** Fast Sliding - unable to bounce, able to slide
*** High Resistance - unable to bounce, very hard to push
*** Light - lower gravity, makes it float
*** Slow Flat - higher gravity, makes it heavy
*** Slow Sliding - unable to bounce, slides slow.
*** Sticky - unable to bounce or slide
*** Regular - able to bounce and slide often
** When a player drops or uses a [[block]] on a sulfur cube, the mob sucks the block up and turns into a bouncy cube.
*** Can be removed by using [[shears]].
** Unlike most passive mobs, sulfur cubes cannot spawn in peaceful difficulty.
** Only available under "Drop 2 of 2026" experimental toggle.

=== Biomes ===
* [[Sulfur Caves]]
** A new underground biome that mainly consists of sulfur and cinnabar blocks.
** Sulfur pools generate in this biome.
** It is where [[Sulfur Cube|sulfur cubes]] spawn naturally.
** Only available under "Drop 2 of 2026" experimental toggle.

=== Structures ===
* [[Sulfur Pool]]
** Generates as part of sulfur caves.
** Consists of bodies of water and potent sulfur.
** A potent sulfur placed underwater inflicts nausea on nearby players and mobs.
** Only available under "Drop 2 of 2026" experimental toggle.
* [[Sulfur Spring]]
** A new structure that generates on any overworld biomes above [[sulfur caves]].
** Consists of [[sulfur]], [[potent sulfur]], [[water]], and [[magma block]]s.
** Can trigger geysers overtime{{Upcoming|BE 26.30}}.
** Only available under "Drop 2 of 2026" experimental toggle.
