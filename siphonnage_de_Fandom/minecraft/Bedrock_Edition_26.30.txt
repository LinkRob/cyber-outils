'''Bedrock Edition 26.30''', named [[Chaos Cubed]], is an upcoming game drop for [[Bedrock Edition]]. It is set to release on June 16, 2026.

== Additions ==
=== Gameplay ===
* New background music tracks by fingerspit
** Shores
** Memories
** Nightly
** Home
** Ebb

=== Blocks ===
* [[Sulfur Spike]]
** A new pointy and sharpy block that naturally generates in [[sulfur caves]].
** Like [[pointed dripstone]]s:
*** Creates a stalactite when placed above the ceiling, and creates a stalagmite when placed on the ground.
*** Breaks instantly with a thrown [[trident]].
** Unlike pointed dripstones:
*** Does not increase fall damage when fallen on a stalagmite.
*** Does not drip liquid on a stalactite's tip when [[water]] or [[lava]] is placed on the block above where the spike is hanging from.
** Can be used to craft [[sulfur]] blocks.

=== Items ===
* [[Music Disc|Bounce Music Disc]]
** A new music disc consisting of the "Bounce" song remixed by fingerspit.
** Like any music discs, it can also be played on [[jukebox]].
** Can be obtained on [[mineshaft]] [[Minecart with Chest|chest minecarts]] located within a [[sulfur caves]] biome.

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
* [[Furnace]] [[Recipe book]]
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
* [[Menu screen]]
** Changed the [[panorama]] to reflect [[Chaos Cubed]] game drop.

=== Blocks ===
* [[Sulfur]] Block Set
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
** Sulfur blocks can now be crafted with 4 [[sulfur spikes]].
** Changed the blast resistance from 3 to 6.
** Changed the effect when absorbed by [[sulfur cubes]] from regular/football to slow bouncy, like other [[stone]]-related blocks does.
* [[Potent Sulfur]]
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
** Can now be used to create geysers.
*** To create a geyser, the potent sulfur must be placed on top of a [[magma block]] and under 1-4 [[water]]-source blocks.
*** Can also erupt when a [[lava]] is placed underneath.
* [[Cinnabar]] Block Set
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
** Changed the blast resistance from 3 to 6.
** Changed the effect when absorbed by [[sulfur cubes]] from regular/football to slow bouncy, like other [[stone]]-related blocks does.
* [[TNT]]
** Can now be absorbed by sulfur cubes, either by picking it up or interacting with it.
*** This gives an explosive effect when ignited.
* [[Stone]]s
** Can now be absorbed by sulfur cubes, either by picking it up or interacting with it.
*** This gives a new slow bouncy effect when hit.
* [[Magma Block]]s
** Can now be absorbed by sulfur cubes, either by picking it up or interacting with it.
*** This gives a new hot effect, damaging nearby players and mobs similar to walking on a regular magma block.

=== Items ===
* [[Sulfur Cube Spawn Egg]]
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
* [[Bucket of Sulfur Cube]]
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Sulfur Cube]]s
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
** Its spawn egg can now summon its baby when used on like most adult mobs does.
** Added 3 new effects:
*** Explosive - explodes when ignited
*** Slow Bouncy - can bounce, but very slow
*** Hot - makes players and mobs take damage when nearby, similar to walking in [[magma block]]
** Now spawns in peaceful difficulty, like most passive mobs does.

=== Biomes ===
* [[Sulfur Caves]]
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
** [[Sulfur spikes]] now naturally generate in sulfur caves, in a form of sulfur spike cluster.
** Now generates less likely on the following biomes:
*** Under {{BiomeLink|Ocean|Oceans}}
*** {{BiomeLink|Mountain|Mountains}}

=== Structures ===
* [[Sulfur Pool]]s
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
* [[Sulfur Spring]]s
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
** Now with a bigger size, tuff, granite, and more block variations.
** Now only generates in flat terrains.
