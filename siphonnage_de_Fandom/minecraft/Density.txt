'''Density''' is a [[mace]] [[enchantment]] that provides 0.5 additional damage per level for each block fallen with the mace.

== Obtaining==
Density can be obtained from enchanting tables, [[ominous vault]]s, and by [[trading]] with a [[Trading#Librarian|librarian]] of any level.

==Incompatibilities==
Density is mutually exclusive with [[Smite]], [[Bane of Arthropods]], and [[Breach]]. However, if [[commands]] are used to have two or more of these enchantments on the same item, the effects stack.
