'''Diamond chickens''' were a rare joke variant of [[chicken]] present in the April Fool's [[Java Edition 2.0]] that lays [[Lapis Lazuli]]s or [[Diamond]]s instead of [[egg]]s.

== Spawn ==
Diamond chickens spawn under normal chicken spawning conditions, but only with 7% chance.

== Behavior ==
As chickens were modified in [[Java Edition 2.0|2.0]] to display neutral behavior, diamond chickens are also neutral.

Diamond chickens lay [[diamond]]s and [[lapis lazuli]] instead of eggs like normal chickens. They also have a 5% chance to explode instead of laying an item.
