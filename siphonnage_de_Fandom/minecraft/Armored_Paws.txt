The '''Armored Paws''' is a game drop that features [[armadillo]]s, new [[wolf]] variants, and [[wolf armor]]. It was released on April 23, 2024. It is the last [[game drop]] to be released in the 1.20 era.

== Additions ==
=== Mobs ===
* [[Armadillo]]
** A new passive mob that spawns in [[savanna]]s and [[badlands]].
** It rolls up when:
*** Being hit or attacked by a [[player]].
*** A player is sprinting or riding towards it.
*** An [[undead]] mob is nearby.
** Drops armadillo scutes every 5-10 minutes.
*** Can also be dropped by brushing.
** [[Spider (disambiguation)|Spider]]s flee from it, even tho it rolls up

=== Items ===
* [[Wolf Armor]]
** Can be worn on wolves.
** Can be crafted using armadillo scutes.
* [[Armadillo Scute]]
** Can be obtained by brushing an armadillo.

== Changes ==
* [[Wolf|Wolves]]
** Added 9 new variants:
*** Pale Wolf - Default
*** Spotted Wolf
*** Black Wolf
*** Striped Wolf
*** Snowy Wolf
*** Ashen Wolf
*** Woods Wolf
*** Chestnut Wolf
*** Rusty Wolf

* [[Cobweb]]s
** Now has a new breaking sound, rather than the classic breaking stone sound.
