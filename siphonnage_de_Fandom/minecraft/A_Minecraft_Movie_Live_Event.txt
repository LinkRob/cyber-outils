'''A Minecraft Movie Live Event''' was a limited-time [[Event servers|event server]] hosted by [[Mojang Studios]]. The event was open starting on March 25 at 2:30 PM PT / 10:30 PM CET and ran until April 14 at 2 PM PT / August 5 at 11 PM CET;<ref>https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/a-minecraft-movie-live-event</ref> it was originally going to end on April 7, but was extended by a week starting on the day it was intended to end.

The event served as the means to obtain several [[Character Creator]] items that were made for the event, including the Yearn [[Cape]]. These items could be obtained by playing the three mini-games hosted as part of the event; the objectives to fulfill were outlined by 6 quests, which were revealed individually throughout the event's duration. Playing the games also allowed players to obtain "Emeralds", which could be spent to obtain cosmetics which were exclusive to the event.

== Locations ==
The event took place across four locations, with three of these being needed to complete the quests presented at the event. The locations are accessible by talking to characters and accepting their dialog prompts, or by using the Map.

=== Midport Village ===
Midport Village is the default location where every player starts after joining the server. It is where all interactive characters are found, and in turn where players are able to go to the other locations.

==== Diamond Bootique ====
Midport Village contains the Diamond Bootique, in which the player can spend Emeralds to buy cosmetics.

==== Secret Stash ====
Midport Village contains the Secret Stash, in which the player can spend Emeralds to upgrade and enchant their equipment items.

=== Wilderness ===
The Wilderness is accessed by talking to '''Dawn and Natalie'''. In it, players navigate a path alongside a cart, while defeating mobs that appear and try to attack the cart. When players ensure the cart is not destroyed and remain near it when it reaches the hub, they are credited for its successful travel.

=== Mines ===
The Mines are accessed by talking to '''Henry'''. In it, players travel down caves to collect resources from the ore blocks using the Pickaxe. Hostile mobs randomly spawn throughout the caves. The player's resources are only kept if they return to the elevator; upon death or leaving the server, all resources are lost. The elevator allows players to go to two sections: Level 1 and Level 2. Level 1 has [[Coal Ore]], [[Iron Ore]], and [[Redstone Ore]]. Level 2 has these ores in addition to [[Lapis Lazuli Ore]], [[Gold Ore]], and [[Diamond Ore]].

=== Outer Walls ===
The Outer Walls are accessed by talking to '''Garrett'''. In it, players go to the top of Midport Village's walls to defend it from waves of Piglins that emerge from Nether Portals. Players must shoot at all the enemies before they reach the walls' doors and breach them. After a wave ends and the doors remain safe, all participating players are credited for its completion. No mobs will spawn for some time, and then another will begin.

== Items ==
[[Items]] of different types could be used at the event. Some of the items used in the server exist exclusively as part of the event.

=== Equipment ===
* '''[[Sword]]''' - The Sword can be used to attack enemies in the Mines or the Wilderness to deal more damage than by using other items. It is a Stone Sword by default.
* '''[[Bow]]''' - The Bow is required to shoot at enemies in the Outer Walls without letting them breach the doors. It can also be used to shoot at enemies in the Mines and the Wilderness.
* '''[[Pickaxe]]''' - The Pickaxe is required to collect ores in the Mines. It is a Stone Pickaxe by default.
*'''[[Arrow]]''' - Hidden in the ninth slot of the player's inventory, Arrows are used as ammunition for the Bow. The player always has 16 Arrows.
*'''[[Chestplate]]''' - The Chestplate provides [[armor]] points to the player. It uses the Chest slot. It is a Leather Tunic by default.

==== Enchantments ====
* '''+[[Punch]]''' - An enchantment for the Bow that can be applied for 3000 Emeralds.
* '''+[[Thorns]]''' - An enchantment for the Chestplate that can be applied for 3000 Emeralds. If a Gold Upgrade has not been bought, a Leather Tunic is given to provide the enchantment.
* '''+[[Fire Aspect]]''' - An enchantment for the Sword that can be applied for 4000 Emeralds.
* '''+[[Fortune]] 1''' - An enchantment for the Pickaxe that can be applied for 5000 Emeralds.

==== Upgrades ====
* '''+Iron Upgrade''' - Replaces the Stone Sword with an Iron Sword for 1200 Emeralds.
* '''+[[Sharpness]] 1''' - An enchantment for the Sword that can be applied for 1500 Emeralds. Requires the Iron Sword.
* '''+Diamond Upgrade''' - Replaces the Iron Sword with a Diamond Sword for 1700 Emeralds. Requires Sharpness 1.
* '''+Sharpness 3''' - An enchantment for the Sword that can be applied for 2000 Emeralds. Requires the Diamond Sword.

* '''+Iron Upgrade''' - Replaces the Stone Pickaxe with an Iron Pickaxe for 1200 Emeralds. The Iron Pickaxe is required to collect Redstone and Diamonds from their respective Ore blocks.
* '''+[[Efficiency]] 1''' - An enchantment for the Pickaxe that can be applied for 1500 Emeralds. Requires the Iron Pickaxe.
* '''+Diamond Upgrade''' - Replaces the Iron Pickaxe with a Diamond Pickaxe for 1700 Emeralds. Requires Efficiency 1.
* '''+Efficiency 3''' - An enchantment for the Pickaxe that can be applied for 2000 Emeralds. Requires the Diamond Pickaxe.

* '''+[[Power]] 1''' - An enchantment for the Bow that can be applied for 1200 Emeralds.
* '''+Power 2''' - An enchantment for the Bow that can be applied for 1500 Emeralds.
* '''+Power 3''' - An enchantment for the Bow that can be applied for 1700 Emeralds.
* '''+Power 4''' - An enchantment for the Bow that can be applied for 2000 Emeralds.

* '''+Gold Upgrade''' - Replaces the Leather Tunic with a Gold Chestplate for 1200 Emeralds.
* '''+Chainmail Upgrade''' - Replaces the Gold Chestplate with a Chain Chestplate.
* '''+Iron Upgrade''' - Replaces the Chain Chestplate with an Iron Chestplate.
* '''+Diamond Upgrade''' - Replaces the Iron Chestplate with a Diamond Chestplate.

* '''Empty Cosmetic Slot''' - Is exchanged for a cosmetic item that the player purchases in the Shop.

== Quests ==
The 6 Quests are objectives that must be fulfilled to obtain specific rewards; each Quest also rewards the player with 500 Emeralds and 1000 EXP upon completion. Descriptions of their objectives and progress are accessible from the Quests menu. The Quests and their descriptions are shown as follows:

=== Quests menu ===
"Complete all quests to get the '''Yearn Cape'''.

New Quests will unlock every other day."

=== Quest 1 - Defend the Village! ===
"Help each of the characters to get the '''Boots of Swiftness''' Dressing Room item."

- Wilderness: Carts delivered (<nowiki>#</nowiki>/5)

- Mines: Ores retrieved (<nowiki>#</nowiki>/100)

- Outer Walls: Waves defended (<nowiki>#</nowiki>/5)

=== Quest 2 - Ghasts Incoming! ===
"Piglin reinforcements have arrived at the Outer Walls - help defend it to get the '''Battle Cry''' emote."

- Ghasts killed (<nowiki>#</nowiki>/20)

=== Quest 3 - Farm Mobs, not Crops! ===
"The fight is going well. Make sure it stays that way to get the '''Mushroom Hat''' Dressing Room item."

- Enemies killed (<nowiki>#</nowiki>/200)

=== Quest 4 - Protect the Cart! ===
"The wandering trader's route has expanded, defend the cart to get the '''Kadoosh!''' emote."

- Carts with at least 40% health delivered (<nowiki>#</nowiki>/3)

=== Quest 5 - Low on Iron! ===
"Protect the iron golems at the Outer Walls to get the '''Jet Pack''' Dressing Room item!"

- Waves cleared with at least two Iron Golems alive (<nowiki>#</nowiki>/5)

=== Quest 6 - Mine Diamonds! ===
"The village needs more diamonds! Finish all quests to get the '''Yearn Cape'''!"

- Diamonds retrieved (<nowiki>#</nowiki>/60)

- Quests completed (<nowiki>#</nowiki>/5)
