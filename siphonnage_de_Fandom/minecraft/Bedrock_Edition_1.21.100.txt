'''Bedrock Edition 1.21.100''' is a minor update to 1.21, released on August 5, 2025. It contains experimental features for [[The Copper Age]].

== Additions ==
=== [[Block]]s ===
* [[Copper Chest]]
** A new chest variant composed of waxed and oxidized variants.
** Can be crafted with a [[Chest|chest]] surrounded by 8 [[Copper Ingot|copper ingots]].
** Acts as its [[Chest|wooden]] counterpart.
*** Placing it side by side creates a large chest.
** Like normal [[Block of Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper chests undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
*** Unoxidized - Normal stage
*** Exposed
*** Weathered
*** Oxidized
** Only lightning and [[Axe|axes]] can revert the oxidation process.
*** Using a [[Honeycomb|honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper chest prevents the oxidation.
** Can be interacted with by [[Copper Golem|copper golems]].
** Only available under "Drop 3 2025" experimental toggle.

=== [[Item]]s ===
* Copper Armor Set<ref name=":0" group="note">Only available under "Drop 3 2025" experimental toggle.</ref>
** [[Copper Helmet]]
** [[Copper Chestplate]]
** [[Copper Leggings]]
** [[Copper Boots]]
* [[Copper Nugget]]
** Can be obtained by [[Smelting|smelting]] any copper equipment.
** Can be used to craft [[copper ingot]]s.
** Only available under "Drop 3 2025" experimental toggle.
* [[Spawn Egg|Copper Golem Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to spawn copper golems.
** Only available under "Drop 3 2025" experimental toggle.
* Copper Tool Set<ref name=":0" group="note">Only available under "Drop 3 2025" experimental toggle.</ref>
** [[Copper Axe]]
** [[Copper Hoe]]
** [[Copper Pickaxe]]
** [[Copper Shovel]]
** [[Copper Sword]]

=== [[Mob]]s ===
* [[Copper Golem]]
** A new passive mob in which it undergoes oxidation, like normal [[Block of Copper|copper blocks]].
** Can be spawned by placing a [[Pumpkin]] or [[Jack o'Lantern]] block on top of a [[Block of Copper|copper block]].
*** Can also be spawned by using its [[Spawn Egg]] or by its {{cmd|summon}} command.
** Opens up [[Copper Chest|copper chests]].
** Can pick up a maximum of 16 items in a stack at a time.
** Like normal [[Block of Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper golems undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
*** Unoxidized - Normal stage
*** Exposed
*** Weathered
*** Oxidized
** Only lightning and [[Axe|axes]] can revert the oxidation process.
*** Using a [[Honeycomb|honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper golem prevents the oxidation.
** When a copper golem gets fully oxidized, it will turn into a [[Copper Golem Statue]].
** Drops 1-3 [[Copper Ingot|copper ingots]] on death.
** Only available under "Drop 3 2025" experimental toggle.
** Prior to this update it was featured in [[Minecraft Live 2021]]'s [[Mob Vote]], but was lost to [[Allay]].

== Changes ==
=== General ===
* [[Flat]] world presets
** Added [[The Void|"The Void" preset]], completing all 9 flat world presets in [[Bedrock Edition]].
** Prior to this update, it was only exclusive to [[Java Edition]].

=== Gameplay ===
* [[Vibrant Visuals]]
** Weather events now affect the scene illumination and the color of clouds and sky in Vibrant Visuals.

=== [[Mob]]s ===
* [[Happy Ghast]]s
** Now drops 1-3 [[Experience|experience]] orbs when killed by a [[Player|player]].
** Increased the volume of the happy ghast ride sound.

=== [[Item]]s ===
* [[Lead]]
** Leashes now emit a breaking sound from the location of the leash holder.
