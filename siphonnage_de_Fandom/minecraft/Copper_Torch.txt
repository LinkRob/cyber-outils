A '''copper torch''' is a [[torch]] variant that was added in [[The Copper Age]]. Like [[Torch|normal torches]], it gives light when exposed to darkness.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Copper torches can be easily broken with any tool.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Copper Torch|any}}
A copper torch also drop as an item if:
* The attachment block is removed or destroyed.
* [[Water]] or [[lava]] flows into it.
* a [[piston]] tries to push it or moves a block into its space.

== Usage ==
Like [[torch]]es, copper torches can be placed on any solid blocks. It gives a light level of 14.

Unlike most [[copper]] blocks, copper torches do not oxidize.
