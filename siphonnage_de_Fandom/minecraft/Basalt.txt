{{BlockSprite|basalt}} '''Basalt''' is an igneous [[rock]] found in [[the Nether]].

{{BlockSprite|polished-basalt}} '''Polished basalt''' is the polished version of basalt and can be found as part of [[Ancient City|ancient cities]].

{{BlockSprite|smooth-basalt}} '''Smooth basalt''' is an ornamental rock [[smelting|smelted]] from [[basalt]] or found as part of [[Amethyst Geode|amethyst geodes]] and [[Ancient City|ancient cities]].

== Obtaining ==
=== Natural generation ===
Basalt generates naturally as [[basalt pillar]]s, which are found in the {{BiomeLink|soul sand valley}} biome. They also generate in the {{BiomeLink|basalt deltas}} biome as regular terrain and as part of [[basalt columns]]. {{IN|java}}, they also generate in [[blobs]], which attempt to replace [[netherrack]] 75 times per chunk in blobs of radius 3—7, from [[altitude|levels]] 0 to 127, in [[basalt deltas]] biome.

Both basalt and polished basalt can be found naturally as part of [[bastion remnant]]s.

Smooth basalt generates naturally as a part of the outer layer of [[amethyst geode]]s.

Polished basalt and smooth basalt also generate in [[Ancient City|ancient cities]].

=== Breaking ===
Basalt drops as an [[item]] if mined using any [[pickaxe]]. If mined using any other [[tool]], it drops nothing.

=== Post-generation ===
[[File:Basalt generator.gif|thumb|A simple basalt generator.]]
Basalt can also be made by [[lava]] flowing into a space that is on top of [[soul soil]] and adjacent to [[blue ice]]. The flowing [[lava]] is then replaced with basalt.

== Usage ==
Basalt's only current use is as building material. Like [[log]]s and [[quartz pillar]]s, basalt and polished basalt point perpendicular to whatever block face they are placed on.

=== Note blocks ===
Basalt and polished basalt can be placed under [[note block]]s to produce "bass drum" sounds.

== Trivia ==
* Geologically, [[wikipedia:Basalt|basalt]] is a volcanic rock that cools from mafic lava flows. Mafic lava is runny, much like lava in the [[the Nether|Nether]], and under the right conditions it can cool into the stacks of [[basalt columns]] seen in [[basalt deltas]].
** Basalt makes up most of Earth's sea floor, as well as Iceland and the Hawaiian Islands.
* Despite being an extrusive igneous rock, basalt has a lower mining time than [[granite]] and [[diorite]], which are intrusive.
* Basalt is the only rock-like block to have both a polished variant and a smooth variant.
