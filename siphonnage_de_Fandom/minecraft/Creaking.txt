The '''creaking''' is a hostile mob that spawns in the [[pale garden]] from a [[creaking heart]]. It can also be spawned by using a spawn egg or by the {{cmd|summon}} command.

== Behavior ==
A creaking is spawned around an activated [[Creaking Heart|creaking heart]] at night or during thunderstorms. It becomes alerted when a player looks at the creaking while within 12 blocks. It returns to its idle state if no player remains near it, or the player goes into [[Creative]] or [[Spectator]] mode. When a player is looking at it, a creaking will be unable to move or attack unless the player is wearing a [[Pumpkin|carved pumpkin]]. However, if no players are looking at it, it will move towards the nearest{{verify}} player and attack it, dealing {{Hp|3}} damage in Normal difficulty. A creaking will not move beyond 32 blocks from its creaking heart, even while following a player. If the creaking is pushed beyond the heart's radius, it disintegrates (despawns).  

A creaking spawned from a creaking heart cannot take any form of damage and is immune to knockback, but when hit by a player or pet, a trail of orange particles will appear between the creaking and its heart, and 2-3 [[Resin Clump|resin clump]]s will be exuded on the sides of pale oak logs near the creaking heart. Once its creaking heart is destroyed, a creaking will twitch and then disintegrate.

Creakings spawned from spawn eggs or the {{cmd|summon}} command are not connected to a creaking heart and persist through both day and night. They take damage and have {{Hp|1}} health.

[[Illager]]s are afraid of creakings and run away from them. The "Johnny" [[vindicator]] (who normally would attack almost any other mobs) is also afraid of the creaking but does consider the creaking a mob, leading it to get as close as possible to the creaking before running away. Creakings are completely immune to damage from lava, fire, [[Iron Golem]]s, [[warden]]s, [[zoglin]]s, and [[wither]]s, unless spawned from a spawn egg or the {{cmd|summon}} command.

== Trivia ==
* Similar to the Creeper, the Creaking's texture allows it to camouflage with the Pale Oak Trees.
* The Creaking is the first asymmetrical mob added in the game.
* In Java 1.21.5, named Creakings cannot despawn during the daytime.
* The Creaking's eyes is noticeable from the [[Flower|open eyeblossoms]].
* Despite having low health, naturally spawned Creakings are undefeatable by Warden's attacks.
* A group of Creakings is called a 'crunch'.
