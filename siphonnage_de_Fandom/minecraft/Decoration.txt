This is a '''list of decorative blocks and entities'''.

* {{itemLink|Armor Stand}} - Immobile, player-like entity that holds armor
* {{blockLink|Banners}} - Hangable wall block that displays customizable patterns
* {{blockLink|Bookshelf}} - Block that enhances enchanting tables
* {{itemLink|Campfire}} - Block that produces a column of smoke and provides light and cooking when lit
* {{itemLink|Candle}} - Floor block that provides a variable light level when lit; can be dyed
* {{itemLink|Flower Pot}} - Floor block that can hold a plant
* {{blockLink|Heads}} - Hangable wall & floor block that resembles certain mobs and even players; can be worn in the head slot
* {{itemLink|Item Frame}} - Hangable wall & floor entity that holds an item
* {{itemLink|Lantern}} - Hangable ceiling & floor block that provides light
* {{blockLink|Lectern}} - Block that can hold a book
* {{itemLink|Painting}} - Hangable wall entity that can display certain artpieces
* {{blockLink|Sea Pickle}} - Floor block that provides a variable light level when submerged in water
* {{blockLink|Torch}} - Hangable wall & floor block that produces light
