'''Bedrock Edition 1.21.60''' is a minor update to 1.21, released on February 11, 2025. It contains experimental features for [[Spring to Life]].

== Additions ==
=== General ===
* Filter Profanity Toggle
** A new feature where all text and chat contents containing profanities will be filtered for you and others when on.
** Most profanities (with the exception of harmful contents) will be unfiltered when off.
** It is on by default for all players, and cannot be changed for child accounts and players that are not signed in.

=== [[Mob]]s ===
* [[Pig]]s
** Added warm and cold variants.
*** The pre-existing variant before this update is now temperate.
** Only available under "Drop 1 2025" Experimental Toggle

== Changes ==
=== [[Blocks]] ===
* [[Creaking Heart]]
** Now has an additional state called "Dormant state".
* [[Eyeblossom]]s
** Now opens when the moon starts rising and closes when the moon has completely set.
* [[Iron]] ([[Iron Bars]], [[Block of Iron]], [[Trapdoor|Iron Trapdoor]], [[Door|Iron Doors]], and [[Pressure Plate|Heavy Weighted Pressure Plates]])
** Now uses iron sounds when placing, breaking, falling, and stepping on them.
* [[Leaves|Pale Oak Leaves]] and [[Sapling|Pale Oak Saplings]]
** Now uses updated map color to match the actual in-game color.
* [[Flower|Torchflowers]], [[Pitcher Plant]]s, Open and Closed Eyeblossoms
** Now increases the probability of growing a nearby sapling into a tree with a bee nest.

=== Items ===
* [[Resin Clump]]s
** Reduced XP rewarded for smelting resin clumps to [[Resin Brick|resin bricks]] from 0.3 to 0.1
