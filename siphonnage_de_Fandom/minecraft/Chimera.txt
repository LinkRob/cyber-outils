The '''chimera''' is a mythological monster, he is part {{Wikipedia|Lion|lion}} and {{Wikipedia|Snake|snake}} creature. He gives the teams the challenge of beating him in theatrical performances with two [[dryad]]s.

== Appearance ==
The Chimera has a yellow-tan body with a dark brown curly mane, brown and yellow eyes, a white muzzle, and a scaly green serpent tail.

== Personality ==
The Chimera is dramatic and spirited creature with a passion for acting. He likes "stagecraft" in the ''[[Minecraft Mini-Series]]''.

== Trivia ==
* Despite the origins of the chimera in Greek mythology (''Chímaira'' referring to a she-goat<ref>The Greek Myths: The Complete and Definitive Edition: https://books.google.com/books?vid=ISBN9780241983386</ref><ref>https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chimera_(mythology)</ref>) the Chimera only has a lion body and snake tail.
* The Chimera in the ''[[Minecraft Mini-Series]]'' is based on the "Chimera" from Matel which in turn was based on a texture for the ocelot in the [[Mash-up pack#Greek Mythology Edition|Greek Mythology Mash-up pack]].
