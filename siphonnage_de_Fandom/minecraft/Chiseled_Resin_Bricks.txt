'''Chiseled Resin Bricks''' is a chiseled variant of [[Resin Bricks]]. They are either crafted with 2 Resin Brick slabs or using a Resin Brick block on a [[Stonecutter]].

==Trivia==
* Similar to chiseled [[Chiseled Sandstone|sandstone]], [[Chiseled Red Sandstone|red sandstone]], [[Chiseled Nether Bricks|nether bricks]], [[Chiseled Blackstone|polished blackstone]], [[Chiseled Tuff|tuff]] and [[Chiseled Deepslate|deepslate]], chiseled resin bricks have a face/symbol in its texture - in this case, the [[Creaking]].
