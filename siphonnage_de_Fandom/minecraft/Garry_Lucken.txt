'''Garry Lucken''', also known as '''Army of Trolls''', is a Pixel artist notable for his work on the [[Skin pack]]s in ''Minecraft''. Lucken's work includes the City and Town Folk Skin packs in [[Pocket Edition]] and has assisted with various Skin packs in [[Console Edition]].<ref>https://www.mojang.com/2015/05/pocket-edition-getting-skins-chicken-jockeys-more/</ref><ref>https://twitter.com/4JStudios/status/258603048392347648</ref>
