This is the list of all development versions of [[Bedrock Edition 1.21.120]].

= 1.21.120.20 =
'''Bedrock Edition 1.21.120.20''' is the first beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 1.21.120]], released on September 3, 2025.

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
* [[Shelf]]
** Now emits vibrations when adding or removing redstone power to and from shelves.

= 1.21.120.21 =
'''Bedrock Edition 1.21.120.20''' is the second beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 1.21.120]], released on September 9, 2025.

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
* [[Pause menu]]
** Now redesigned with some buttons moved to the bottom with icons.
** Now has a large player skin.

=== Blocks ===
* [[Copper Bars]]
** Changed the texture of weathered copper bars.

= 1.21.120.22 =
'''Bedrock Edition 1.21.120.22''' is the third beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 1.21.120]], released on September 16, 2025.

== Changes ==
=== Mobs ===
* [[Copper Golem]]s
** Now emit scraping and waxing particles at its location.
* [[Wither Skeleton]]s
** Now picks up copper swords.
