'''Air''' is an [[invisible block]] used to define empty space where any other blocks could otherwise exist.

'''Cave air'''{{only|java}} is the underground air found in [[cave]]s and some [[generated structures]].

'''Void air'''{{only|java}} is used internally for blocks above (Y>319) and below (Y<-64) the world, and in unloaded chunks.

== Obtaining ==

{{IN|bedrock}}, air cannot be obtained in the [[inventory]] without editing. {{IN|java}}, the block is completely unobtainable. Air can be destroyed or created only by the placement or removal of other [[block]]s respectively.

Upon world generation, all otherwise empty spaces are occupied by air. {{IN|java}}, [[badlands]] [[mineshaft]]s and [[lava lake]]s generate cave air,<ref>{{bug|MC-125112||Surface mineshafts in mesas use cave_air|WF}}</ref><ref>{{bug|MC-128250||Surface water and lava ponds use cave_air|WF}}</ref> and underground structures generate cave air. Void air is used in place of unloaded chunks and above or below the [[world]]'s buildable area.{{only|java}}

== Usage ==

[[File:Shown Invisible Blocks.png|thumb|250px|Air, shown at left, is represented by blue cubes within structures. Cave air and void air are also shown as such.]]

Air shares some properties with non-[[solid block]]s, including allowing the [[player]] or [[mob]] to move within that space without [[suffocating]], and also to catch their breath if they are [[drowning]] in [[water]].

Air can be visualized with the use of [[structure block]]s in [[Structure Block#Save|save mode]]. With "Show invisible blocks" enabled, the air is represented as small blue cubes. All air types are displayed identically as blue cubes.

Air can be used to clearing blocks with the <code>/fill</code> command, an example is <code>/fill ~-5 ~-5 ~-5 ~5 ~5 ~5 minecraft:air</code> (clears a 11x11x11 cube of space centered on what executed the command.).

Cave air and void air have no special properties; they function the same as normal air.
