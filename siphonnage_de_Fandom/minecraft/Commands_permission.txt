Lists or reloads [[permission]] list.

Can be executed only in [[BDS]] console.
==Syntax==

:<code>permission list</code>
::Prints the current used permissions list.
:<code>permission reload</code>
::Makes the [[server]] reload the operator list from the <samp>permissions.json</samp> file.
