{{For|the full release|Bedrock Edition 26.0}}
This is the list of all development versions of [[Bedrock Edition 26.0]].

= 26.0.23 =
'''Bedrock Edition 26.0.23''' is the first beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 26.0]], released on December 3, 2025.

== Additions ==
=== Gameplay ===
* [[Subtitles]]
** Can be enabled via Accessibility in settings.
** Displays text for in-game sounds.
** Prior to this update, it was only exclusive to Java Edition.

== Changes ==
=== Items ===
* [[Horse Armor]], [[Nautilus Armor]], and [[Wolf Armor]]
** Now displays their toughness, armor, and knockback resistance values if they are greater than 0.
* [[Diamond Horse Armor]] and [[Diamond Nautilus Armor]]
** Now has a toughness value of 2.
* [[Netherite Horse Armor]] and [[Netherite Nautilus Armor]]
** Now has a toughness value of 3.
* [[Spear]]s
** Now rotates when charging in first and third person views.
** No longer picked up by the following mobs:
*** {{EntityLink|Drowned}}
*** {{EntityLink|Skeleton}}
*** {{EntityLink|Stray}}
*** {{EntityLink|Bogged}}
*** {{EntityLink|Parched}}

=== Mobs ===
* [[Donkey]], [[Horse]]s and [[Mule]]s
** No longer gradually grows when they age.
* [[Squid]] and [[Glow Squid]]
** Baby ones no longer drop items when killed by a [[player]] or tamed [[wolf]].
* [[Husk]]s, [[Zombie]]s, [[Drowned]]s, and [[Zombie Villager]]s
** Now drop items when killed by a [[player]] or tamed [[wolf]].
* [[Nautilus]] and [[Zombie Nautilus]]
** Now has swim sounds.
** Now has an alternative ambient, hurt, and dying sounds when on land.
* [[Polar Bear]]s
** Baby ones now attack [[fox]]es.
** No longer hostile towards player on any [[difficulty]] level.
* [[Vindicator]]
** Now attacks non-illager mobs correctly when applied with a "Johnny" name tag.

= 26.0.24 =
'''Bedrock Edition 26.0.24''' is also the first beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 26.0]], also released on December 3, 2025 for Windows.

= 26.0.25 =
'''Bedrock Edition 26.0.25''' is the second beta and preview for [[Bedrock Edition 26.0]], released on December 10, 2025. It is the last beta to be released in 2025.

== Additions ==
=== Gameplay ===
* Party system
** Players can now create and join parties.
** Members doesn't need to be in leader's world
** Each party contains up to 15 players.
*** Leaders can choose what type of privacy setting that a party has:
**** Invited only - requires an invitation sent from a leader to join
**** Open - anyone can join
** Can promote other members to become a leader.
** Party settings can be accessed via settings.

== Changes ==
=== Mobs ===
* [[Camel Husk]], [[Zombie Horse]], and [[Skeleton Horse]]
** No longer panic when hit.
** Baby zombie horses no longer be ridden or equipped with saddle and horse armor.

= 26.0.27 =
'''Bedrock Edition 26.0.27''' is the fourth preview and third beta for [[Bedrock Edition 26.0]], released on January 7, 2026. It is the first beta and preview to be released in 2026.

== Changes ==
=== Items ===
* [[Name Tag]]s
** Can now be crafted with any nugget and a paper.
** Only available under "Drop 1 of 2026" experimental toggle.

=== Mobs ===
* Baby mobs
** The following baby mobs have remodeled the texture:
*** {{EntityLink|Cat}}
*** {{EntityLink|Chicken}}
*** {{EntityLink|Cow}}
*** {{EntityLink|Mooshroom}}
*** {{EntityLink|Ocelot}}
*** {{EntityLink|Pig}}
*** {{EntityLink|Rabbit}}
*** {{EntityLink|Sheep}}
*** {{EntityLink|Wolf}}
** Baby [[Wolf|wolves]], [[cat]]s, and [[pig]]s now has a new sound.
** Armor and [[saddle]] on baby wolves and pigs respectively will no longer render.
** Can pause or resume aging by feeding them with [[Golden Dandelion|golden dandelion]]{{Upcoming|First Drop 2026}}.
** Only available under "Drop 1 of 2026" experimental toggle.
* [[Rabbit]]
** Changed the texture.
** Now has an idle animation.
** Only available under "Drop 1 of 2026" experimental toggle.
