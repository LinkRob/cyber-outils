'''Karl Lowry''' is a brand enforcer at [[Mojang Studios]], known for issuing [[wikipedia:YouTube_copyright_strike|copyright strikes]] to YouTube channels that upload videos of Minecraft. Most notably, the YouTuber [https://www.youtube.com/@robro07 robro07], who was issued a copyright strike for uploading a video of himself playing Minecraft while "Jorking it", leading to the video's deletion, sparking controversy among fans.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7PPBj-fE6eE</ref>
