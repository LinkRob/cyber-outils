A '''fire patch''' is a [[feature]] that consists of [[fire]] or [[soul fire]].

== Generation ==
Fire patch generates only on [[netherrack]] if it is normal [[fire]], while [[soul fire]] patch generates on [[soul soil]].

{{IN|bedrock}}, fire patch feature generates in any biome in [[the Nether]].

{{IN|java}}, normal fire patch generates also in any biome in [[the Nether]]. But soul fire patch cannot generate in {{BiomeLink|Crimson Forest}} biome.
