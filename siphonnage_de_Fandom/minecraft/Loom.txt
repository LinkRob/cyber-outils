A '''loom''' is used to apply [[banner pattern|pattern]]s to [[banner]]s. It also serves as a [[shepherd]]'s [[job site block]].

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
A loom can be mined using any [[tool]] or by hand, but [[axe]]s are the quickest. It always drops as an item.

{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Loom|Axe}}

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting
|A2= String     |B2= String
|A3= Any Planks |B3= Any Planks
|Output= Loom
|type= Decoration block
}}

=== Natural generation ===
Looms can be found in [[village]] shepherd houses.

== Usage ==
=== Applying banner patterns ===
[[File:Loom GUI.png|176px|thumb|right|Interface for the loom {{in|java}}.]]
[[File:Loom GUI BE.png|176px|thumb|right|Interface for the loom {{in|bedrock}}.]]
{{loom|dye=any}}

{{control|Using}} on a loom opens its interface. It has 3 slots for a [[banner]], a [[dye]] and an optional [[banner pattern]] item. When a banner is put in, a list of patterns appears in the center part. 6{{only|java}} or 8 patterns require the following banner patterns: Flower Charge, Creeper Charge, Skull Charge, Thing, Snout, Globe, Field Masoned,{{only|bedrock}} and Bordure Indented.{{only|bedrock}} Putting a banner pattern in its respective slot makes the loom show only that pattern.

Selecting a pattern or using a banner pattern on the list shows the preview of the banner after applying, if a dye is present. To finish, the player must take the banner in the slot beneath the preview. The original banner and the dye disappear from their respective slots, but the banner pattern is not consumed.

=== Changing profession ===
If a [[village]] has a loom that has not been claimed by a [[villager]], any villager who hasn't already chosen a [[job site]] block has a chance to change their profession to shepherd.

=== Fuel ===
A loom can be used as fuel in a [[furnace]] to smelt 1.5 items.

=== Note Blocks ===
Looms can be placed under [[note block]]s to produce "bass" sounds.
