Lessons in Good Trouble is a custom [[world]] released by [[Mojang]] for ''[[Minecraft: Bedrock Edition]]''. It is available to download from the [[Marketplace]] for free. 

==Description==
This world is intended to be played in [[Creative]] Mode, by a single player. The world begins with some introductory dialogue. It tells that the player is going to City Hall, having intended to meet Daria there; the two were working on a civics report, but Daria could not meet the player there due to the streets closing in response to a protest. After the dialogue, the player is seen in the midst of this protest.

The game asks the player to select a control scheme to provide instructions for. The player must then point the [[Heads-up display|crosshair]] at several floating orbs made of yellow star [[particles]]. After this, the player can walk around and interact with protesters and police officers in the crowd. The player is instructed to navigate around them to reach the City Hall and talk to the Police Captain, who deems the crowd too disruptive. The crowd subsequently closes in on the City Hall, and the player is instructed to escape.

Among the crowd is Librarian Maia, who escorts the player to a library to get them away from the crowd. Here, Librarian Maia discusses the concept of “Good Trouble” - speaking out against problematic social dispositions - and how it has occurred throughout history to help change circumstances in human lives.

When the dialogue is finished, the player can interact with one of several "books" to begin simulations of assorted times and places where "Good Trouble" played a part. Each simulation has instructions for the player to complete in-game, as well as several [[NPC]]s that can be interacted with. There are four books in the center shelf, which can be played in any order after the initial sequence: ''Equal Education for All'', ''Anti-Apartheid Movement'', ''Independence for India'', and ''Women's Suffrage''.

In addition to these, there are five additional sections that each play immediately after one of the main sections. These sections subsequently appear on the library wall as books that can be replayed at any time while in the library. ''Bloody Sunday'' immediately follows the introductory sequence.

''Turnaround March'' is played after one main book is completed. ''Rosa Parks & Muddy March'' is played after two main books are completed. ''Funeral Procession'' is played after three main books are completed. On some devices, the world will lock while attempting to load this side section, making it and ''Final Thoughts'' inaccessible, and requiring the player to recreate the world to experience the final main book.

''Final Thoughts'' becomes available after all four main books are completed, but does not play automatically; it must be accessed in the library, where it initially appears on Librarian Maia's desk between the other books. After it is completed once, its book is moved to the center of the library wall.
