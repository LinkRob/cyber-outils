'''1.18.45''' is a minor update to {{el|ee}} released on January 17, 2023, which changes the name and logo, and fixes bugs.<ref>{{cite|url=http://web.archive.org/web/20230118035046/https://educommunity.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/4484636118420|title=What's New in the Mobile, Multiplayer, & More update (version 1.18.45)|website=Minecraft Education Support}}</ref>

== Changes ==
=== General ===
; [[Logo]]
* New game logo with ''Minecraft Education''.

; App
* Renamed ''Minecraft: Education Edition'' to ''Minecraft Education''.
* Updated app icon.

== Fixes ==
* Fixed screen hanging/crashing on red Mojang Studios splash screen on ChromeOS.
