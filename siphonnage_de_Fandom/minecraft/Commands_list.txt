Lists players on the server.

== Syntax ==
* '''Java Edition'''
:<code>list</code>
:: Shows the names of all currently-connected players.
:<code>list uuids</code>
:: Player UUIDs are shown alongside names.

* '''Bedrock Edition'''
:<code>list</code>
:: Shows the names of all currently-connected players.

== Result ==
Always succeeds.
