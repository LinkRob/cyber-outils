A version of '''[[Indev 0.31]]''' was released on '''January 6, 2010'''.<ref>[[wordofnotch:320353436|Level generator updated]] – The Word of Notch, January 6, 2010</ref>

== Additions ==
=== World generation ===
* New Level options.
** Level type (island, floating, flat, original).
*** Island: The world is a large island, sand and gravel are common. The map is surrounded by water.
*** Floating: The world was made of chains of floating islands, under which was void. Water is rare. The map is surrounded by air.
*** Flat: The surface was similar to modern [[superflat]]. Trees and flowers are generated. The map is surrounded by water.
*** Original: The terrain resembles that of [[late Classic]]. The map is surrounded by water.{{verify|edition=java|type=untestable}}
** Level shape (square, long, deep).
** Level size (small, normal, huge). Map sizes looked like the following:

* Blocks in the hotbar get used up in [[Creative mode]].
