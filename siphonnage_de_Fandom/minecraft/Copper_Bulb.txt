A '''copper bulb''' is a light-emitting block and a transparent block. It is made of copper and therefore oxidizes over time.

== Obtaining==

=== Breaking ===
Copper bulbs can be broken with stone [[pickaxe]]s and any pickaxes higher than that. Mining the copper bulbs with anything else would drop nothing.

===Natural generation===
Copper bulbs can be found in a random state (lit on or off, any oxidation) in the [[trial chambers]].

=== Waxing ===

Copper bulbs can be turned into the respective waxed copper blocks by {{Control|using}} a [[honeycomb]] item on them. Waxed copper bulbs do not oxidize and are identical to the non-waxed version.

=== Scraping ===

{{Control|Using}} an [[axe]] on a waxed copper bulb turns it into the respective non-waxed copper bulb. In addition, using an axe on an exposed, weathered, or oxidized copper bulb reverts it one stage to a regular, exposed, or weathered copper bulb respectively.

=== Lightning strike ===

Non-waxed copper bulbs are completely deoxidized when struck by lightning, and other non-waxed copper bulbs nearby are deoxidized randomly.

==Usage==
Copper bulbs are redstone components that emit light while triggered. A [[comparator]] can read the state of a copper bulb. They also work as a T-flip-flop.
