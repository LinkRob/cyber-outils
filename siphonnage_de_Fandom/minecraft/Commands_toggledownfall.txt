Toggles the [[weather]].

== Syntax ==

: <code>toggledownfall</code>

== Result ==

Always succeeds. If weather is currently clear, [[rain]] or [[Snowfall|snow]] will start. If weather is currently rain, snow, or a [[thunderstorm]], it will become clear. Can be overridden by the {{cmd|weather}} command.
