'''''Minecraft: Soothing Synths''''' is an {{w|Extended play|extended play}} released for ''Minecraft'', featuring compositions by [[Aaron Cherof]] and remixed by Monolism. It consists of the songs that were released as part of the [[Minecraft: Trails & Tales (Original Game Soundtrack)|soundtrack]] for [[Trails & Tales]], but remixed in a "{{w|Lo-fi music|lo-fi}}" style. It was released on September 18, 2023.<ref>{{ytl|jAS-GKmQ_5o|Minecraft LoFi: Soothing synths for mining blocks|Minecraft|September 18, 2023}}</ref>
