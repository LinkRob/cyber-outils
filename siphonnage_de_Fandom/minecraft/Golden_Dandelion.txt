A '''golden dandelion''' is a decoration block added in [[Tiny Takeover]]. It has the appearance of a golden version of a normal [[dandelion]], and can be used to feed most baby mobs, which pauses or resumes their aging, i.e., prevents them from growing into adults.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Like any [[flower]]s, golden dandelions can be easily broken with any tool.
{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Golden Dandelion|any}}
A golden dandelion also drop as an item if:
* The attachment block is removed or destroyed.
* [[Water]] or [[lava]] flows into it.
* A [[piston]] tries to push it or moves a block into its space.

=== Trading ===
[[Wandering trader]]s may sell 1 golden dandelion for 2 [[emerald]]s as part of their normal trades.

== Usage ==
Like any [[flower]]s, golden dandelions can be used as a decoration and can be planted on [[flower pots]].

Golden dandelions can be placed on the following blocks:
* {{BlockLink|Grass Block}}
* {{BlockLink|Dirt}}
* {{BlockLink|Coarse Dirt}}
* {{BlockLink|Rooted Dirt}}
* {{BlockLink|Farmland}}
* {{BlockLink|Podzol}}
* {{BlockLink|Mycelium}}
* {{BlockLink|Moss Block}}
* {{BlockLink|Pale Moss Block}}
* {{BlockLink|Mud}}
* {{BlockLink|Muddy Mangrove Roots}}
Unlike other flowers, golden dandelions cannot be composted in the [[composter]], do not attract [[bee]]s, and aren't pollinated by bees.

=== Baby mobs ===
Feeding baby mobs a golden dandelion will lock their aging. Feeding it again will resume its aging.

Golden dandelions, however, do not apply to baby monsters (including [[undead]] ones) and baby [[villagers]].

=== Crafting ===
Like most flowers, golden dandelions can be crafted to yellow dye. They can also be used to craft [[Saturation]] [[suspicious stew]]s as any small flower does.

=== Piglins ===
Like all golden variant items, {{EntityLink|Piglin|piglins}} are attracted to golden dandelions and run toward any golden dandelions on the ground, inspecting it for 6 to 8 seconds before putting it in their inventory.

=== Bee nests ===
Oak, birch, and cherry trees grown from saplings planted within 2 blocks of a golden dandelion have a 5% chance of growing a [[bee nest]] containing 2-3 bees.
