'''Bone''' may refer to:
* {{ItemLink|Bone}}, an item which is dropped by [[fish]]{{only|bedrock}} and [[skeleton]]s, or obtained from [[fishing]].
* {{ItemLink|Bone Meal}}, an item crafted out of bones, dropped by fish{{only|java}} or from a [[composter]] which is used to grow [[crops]] quicker.
* {{BlockLink|Bone Block}}, a block crafted from 9 bone meal and generates naturally as a [[fossil]].
