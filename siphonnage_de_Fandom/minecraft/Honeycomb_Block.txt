'''Honeycomb blocks''' are decorative [[block]]s crafted from [[honeycomb]]s.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Honeycomb blocks can be broken using any [[tool]],<ref>{{bug|MC-176860||Honeycomb blocks do not have an assigned tool}}</ref> and always drop as an [[item (entity)|item]].

{{breaking row|horizontal=1|Honeycomb Block|Any}}

=== Crafting ===
{{Crafting
|A1= Honeycomb |A2= Honeycomb
|B1= Honeycomb |B2= Honeycomb
|Output=Honeycomb Block
|type= Decoration block
}}

== Usage ==

=== Note Blocks ===
Honeycomb blocks can be placed under [[note blocks]] to produce flute sounds.{{only|bedrock}}<ref>{{bug|MC-212976}}</ref>

== Trivia ==
* While real-life [[wikipedia:Honeycomb|honeycomb]]s are based on a [[wikipedia:Hexagonal tiling|hexagonal tiling]], the honeycomb block's texture is more akin to a [[wikipedia:Square tiling|square tiling]], likely to fit with the square theme of the game.
** This makes the square tiling the second [[wikipedia:Regular_polyhedron|regular polyhedron]] to be represented in Minecraft, after the cube.
