'''''Minecraft: Caves & Cliffs (Original Game Soundtrack)''''' is the second soundtrack for ''[[Minecraft]]'' by [[Lena Raine]], in collaboration with [[Kumi Tanioka]]. It was released on Spotify on October 20, 2021, followed by other platforms the following day.

The album includes 10 songs for a total length of over 45 minutes. Most of the tracks were implemented for [[Caves & Cliffs]] biomes in [[Java Edition 21w42a]] and [[Bedrock Edition beta 1.18.0.22]], with the exception of ''otherside'', which is a [[music disc]], and ''Ancestry'', which wasn't implemented until [[Java Edition 22w16a]] as music for the deep dark biome.

== Trivia ==

* Due to an unknown reason, "Left to Bloom", "One More Day", "Floating Dream" and "Comforting Memories" play as normal in-game overworld music instead of being in their specific biomes.<ref>{{bug|MC-241779||Resolved as "Works as Intended"}}</ref>

* Although "Ancestry" was added to this album, it never played in the game until Minecraft 1.19, [[The Wild Update]].
