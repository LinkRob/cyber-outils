The '''enderall''' is a hostile and more powerful variant of the [[enderman]] in ''[[Minecraft Mini-Series]]''. It first appeared in episode 5 in the Ender Cave on Scatter Island.

== Behavior ==
The enderall has telliporting abilities like an [[Enderman#Behavior|enderman]] along with the ability to suction [[mobs]], [[items]], and lightweight [[blocks]] (such as [[ladders]]) into it's portal-like mouth. It can also launch blocks or items it sucked in, back out as a ranged attack. It has a purple weak spot on it's back and drops [[ender pearls]] when killed.

== Appearance ==
The enderall is a big humanoid monster resembling [[endermen]]. It's black with a glowing purple portal-like mouth with black tooth-like spikes, glowing purple eyes, and a glowing purple streak on it's back.

== Trivia ==
* The enderall was seen killing [[endermen]], showing that it's hostile even with other related mobs similar to a [[zoglin]].
* The enderall was based on a [[Mash-up pack#Minifigures based on Mash-Up Packs or Skin Packs|minifigure]] by Matel which is based on a skin with the same name in the "Campfire Tales" [[skin pack]].
