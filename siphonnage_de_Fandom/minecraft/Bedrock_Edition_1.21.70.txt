'''Bedrock Edition 1.21.70,''' named [[Spring to Life]], is a game drop to 1.21, released on March 25, 2025.

== Additions ==
=== Gameplay ===
* [[Pause menu]]
** Now completely freezes when a player enters the pause menu.
** Only works in singleplayer mode worlds.
** Prior to this update, it was only available in beta and preview.
* Filter Profanity Toggle
** Now available in mobile devices.
** Prior to this update, it was only available on Windows.

=== [[Block]]s ===
* Bush
* Firefly Bush
** This block now generates [[Removed features|Fireflies]] as ambient particles
* Cactus Flower
* Leaf Litter
* Short Dry Grass
** Tall Dry Grass
* Wildflower

=== [[Item]]s ===
* Blue Egg
* Brown Egg

=== [[Mob]]s ===
* [[Cow]]s, [[Chicken]]s and [[Pig]]s
** Added warm and cold variants.
*** The pre-existing variant before this update is now temperate.
* [[Wolf|Wolves]]
** Added 6 new wolf audio variants.

== Changes ==
=== [[Mob]]s ===
* Warm and cold [[Pig]] variants
** No longer experimental and it is now available during normal gameplay.

=== [[Blocks]] ===
* [[Lodestone]]
** Can now be crafted with an [[Iron Ingot]] surrounded by 8 chiseled stone bricks, making it renewable.
** Can now be found in ruined portal chests.
* [[Leaves]]
** All leaf blocks now have leaf particles dropping from them, similar to the cherry and pale oak trees.

=== Items ===
[[File:Creeper Spawn Egg New.png|thumb|100px|The newly redesigned [[Creeper]] Spawn Egg]]
* [[Spawn Egg]]s
** Changed the texture of all spawn eggs, except [[NPC]] spawn egg from Education Editon toggle.
*** Each spawn egg now has their own unique visual which captures the personality and character of every mob it spawns.
* [[Bundle]]s
** Can now be found in [[Village]]s.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Donkey]]
** Now spawns in savannas.
* [[Camel]]s
** Now spawns in deserts.
