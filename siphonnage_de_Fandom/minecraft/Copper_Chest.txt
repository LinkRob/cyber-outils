A '''copper chest''' is a [[copper|copper variant]] of a [[chest|normal chest]] that was added in [[The Copper Age]]. It can store various items similar to chests and can be interacted by [[Copper Golem|copper golems]]. It also undergoes oxidation process, just like normal [[Block of Copper|copper blocks]].

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Copper chests drops itself and its contents when mined using stone or a better pickaxe. Otherwise, it only drop contents and the copper chest will disappear.

=== Spawning ===
Placing a [[Pumpkin|carved pumpkin]] or a [[jack o'Lantern]] on top of a copper block generates a copper chest with a [[Copper Golem|copper golem]] spawned on top of it. This also applies to all waxed and oxidized variants.

Copper chests do not generate if a copper golem is spawned using a [[Spawn Egg]] or by a {{cmd|summon}} command.

== Oxidation ==
Like most [[Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper chests undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
* Unoxidized - Normal stage
* Exposed
* Weathered
* Oxidized
Only lightning and [[axe]]s can reverse the oxidation process. Using a [[Honeycomb|honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper chest prevents oxidation.

== Trivia ==
* The top texture of a single Copper Chest is similar to the texture of a [[Trapdoor|Copper Trapdoor]].
* Waxed Weathered Copper Double Chest is now the longest block name in the game, compared to [[Polished Blackstone Bricks|Cracked Polished Blackstone Bricks]].
* Due to a weird bug, normal copper chests sometimes connect to their waxed variants, although they are different and look similar. <ref> https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MC/issues/MC-302468 </ref>
