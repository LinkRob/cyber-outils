'''1.14.70''', the first release of '''[[Camps & Clubs]]''', is a major update of {{el|ee}}, released on May 26, 2021. The update expands eligibility to camps, after-school programs, homeschools, nonprofits and other organizations.<ref>{{cite|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210611002638/https://educommunity.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360061848571-What-s-New-in-The-Camps-and-Clubs-Update-Version-1-14-70-|title=What’s New in The Camps and Clubs Update (Version 1.14.70)|website=Minecraft Education Edition Support|author=Nate Mackie}}</ref><ref>{{cite|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210429162605/https://educommunity.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/community/posts/360077777291-Camps-and-Clubs-Beta-Feedback|title=Camps and Clubs Beta Feedback|website=Minecraft Education Edition Support}}</ref><ref name=changelog>{{cite|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210429042423/https://educommunity.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360049494792|title=Feature Walkthrough Minecraft: Education Edition Camps & Clubs Beta (version 1.14.60)|website=Minecraft Education Edition Support}}</ref><ref>https://education.minecraft.net/en-us/blog/the-camps-and-clubs-update-brings-minecraft-education-edition-to-learners-beyond-the-classroom</ref>

== Additions ==

=== Mobs ===
[[File:NPCConstructionWorkers.png|thumb|300px|The 5 new construction worker NPC skins.]]

;[[NPC]]s
*Added 10 new NPC skins.

=== General ===
;Pause menu
*Added links in the pause menu to a related Flipgrid topic, Forms quiz, or web tool when assigning a lesson from the Library.
*Added an option that enabled hosts of a world to share a link that takes users directly into their world.

;World settings
*Added the ability to add resource links to a Flipgrid topic.

;Settings
*Added a Classroom tab to the Settings menu to access common world settings.

;Launching
*Added the ability to launch the game (including specific worlds) from a Learning Management System.
