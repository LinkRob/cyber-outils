'''Copper''' is a metallic material found in the [[Overworld]]. It spawns naturally in copper ore, which drops raw copper, and then the raw copper can be smelted into copper ingots. The copper ingots can be crafted into blocks, items, tools, and armor.

Unlike most metals, copper blocks oxidize in 4 different stages:
* Unoxidized
* Exposed
* Weathered
* Oxidized

Copper blocks can be waxed with [[honeycomb]], to extend the oxidation over a long period of time. The flooring and the wall designs of the [[trial chambers]] are made of waxed copper block variants.
