The '''Marketplace''' ('''Minecraft Store''' on PlayStation 4) is an in-game store, where [[Player|players]] can purchase access to [[skins]], [[add-ons]] and [[worlds]]<ref>[https://marketplace.minecraft.net/catalog Minecraft Marketplace Catalog]</ref> from community creators, who in turn get reimbursed for their creations. Players can access these by spending Minecoins (or Tokens on PlayStation 4), which can be bought for real currency in amounts according to the item the player intends to purchase, or directly in various amounts.

== Using Minecoins ==
Minecoins are the digital currency used to buy most content available in the Marketplace.

The larger Minecoin packs have an increased Minecoin-to-cost ratio compared to the smallest pack.

== Requirements ==
The Marketplace is a service that can only be accessed by players that meet these requirements:

*The player must have purchased the full version of [[Bedrock Edition]] (not including [[Legacy Console Edition|Legacy Console Editions]] or [[Apple TV Edition|Apple TV]]).
*The player must have a stable internet connection.

== Content ==
There are four different categories of content available on the Marketplace, including [[Skin Packs]], [[Worlds]], [[Texture Packs]] and [[Mash-up Packs]]. All purchased content instantly syncs to the player’s Microsoft account unless they are not signed into an Xbox profile, in which case it will be saved locally to the device in use.

All content is submitted by official ''Minecraft'' partners and is approved by the ''Minecraft'' Content Team.

=== Skin Packs ===
Skins Packs are collections of custom skins that players can use in both singleplayer and multiplayer games. Depending on the specific content, some Skin Packs provide 1 or 2 free skins for you to use without needing to purchase.

=== Worlds ===
Worlds are pre-built maps that offer wide variety of in-game experiences. Not to be confused with Mash-up packs, Worlds can also include custom textures, blocks, items and mobs and be bundled with bonus skins.

==== Adventure Maps ====
Adventure maps are self-contained experiences that focus on exploring and other types of guided gameplay. These types of worlds can range from PvP arenas to simulators and can be designed for singleplayer, multiplayer or both.

==== Mini-Games ====
Mini-games are compact worlds with a specific theme or goal that are designed to be repeatable. These can feature a set of different gamemodes and variants or be designed to reset continuously.

==== Survival Spawns ====
Survival spawns are starter maps that players can explore, gain loot from and expand. Maps of this type can sometimes add an entirely new aspect to the game but will still allow players to experience the world as they normally would in survival mode.

=== Texture Packs ===
Texture Packs allow players to change the visual appearance of worlds. These packs can also customize other in-game elements such as sounds, items, the [[GUI]] and the physical shape of mobs. Texture Packs are only able to alter existing features in ''Minecraft'' and cannot add new entities, blocks or items.

=== Add-ons ===
Standalone add-ons include new entities, items, and blocks. They can be applied to any existing world the player has, but do not include a dedicated world of their own.

=== Mash-up Packs ===
Mash-up Packs are special bundles that combine a world, texture pack and skins. Unlike Worlds, texture packs included in Mash-up Packs can be used across other singleplayer worlds and even servers.

== Trivia ==

*Before the ''Minecraft'' Marketplace was introduced, real money was used to buy the in-game [[Texture Pack|Texture Packs]] and [[Skin|Skins]]. When Mojang did it this way, all of the content had to pass certification on every single platform. The Marketplace and Minecoins were introduced to avoid this.{{Citation needed}}
*Any packs that were bought before the Marketplace was introduced read "Owned (P)" instead of "Owned".
*On PlayStation 4, Tokens are used instead of Minecoins and do not transfer to or from other platforms.<ref>[https://help.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360037725571-Minecraft-for-PlayStation-TM-4-FAQ Minecraft for PlayStation TM 4 FAQ]</ref> They are light, matte blue and have a large T in the middle instead of an M.
* Skins, add-ons and worlds downloaded from the Marketplace are not stored in the of the {{cd|skin_packs}}, {{cd|behavior_packs}}, {{cd|resource_packs}} or {{cd|minecraftWorlds}} of the [[com.mojang]] folder. They are instead stored in the {{cd|premium_cache}} folder and are encrypted.
* If you download a world from 2017-2019 if you spawn a [[Villager]] it will have it's original [[Villager (old)|variants]].
