'''Copper spleaves''' are joke [[block|blocks]] introduced in [[Java Edition 23w13a or b]] which are broken upon any contact with the player.

== Obtaining ==
Copper spleaves can be obtained via the [[Creative]] inventory or with [[commands]], as they are unobtainable by [[Survival]] means, even using a tool enchanted with [[Silk Touch]].

===Breaking===
Copper spleaves cannot be obtained when mined, even using a tool enchanted with [[Silk Touch]].
{{Breaking row|Copper Spleaves|Any|horizontal=1|drop=0}}

=== Natural generation ===
Copper spleaves generates when the structure [[Lunar Base]] on [[The Moon]] is activated.

== Issues ==
'''Copper spleaves''' blocks are an unsupported [[item]] due to being an [[Wikipedia:April Fools' Day|April Fools']] joke, and therefore such issues relating to them will not be fixed.

== Trivia ==

* Copper spleaves is a reference to the Minecraft minigame [[spleef]]. Spleef is a competitive minigame played within Minecraft. When playing spleef, players destroy blocks below other players, allowing them to fall off the playing field and into a pit. The object of the game is to be the last player on the field. Because of this, the name of the Copper Spleaves is a combination of the words "Spleef" and "Leaves".
* The exact y level the player needs to be in to not break the copper spleaves is 0.9999998999999999971244335483788745477795600891113281250000...1 above the block.
*In the [[Tricky Trials]] Update, a similar block called [[Copper Grate]]s are added. However, unlike Copper Spleaves, they are deoxidised with an axe and waxed.
** Weathered Copper Grates shared a similar texture to Copper Spleaves.
