'''Jungle bush''' is a variant of [[Jungle tree|jungle trees]] that generates in [[jungle]] biomes and cannot be grown by jungle saplings. It is one of the rarest plants in the game.

== Generation ==
Jungle bush generates as a [[jungle log]] surrounded by [[oak leaves]]<ref>{{bug|MC-156133|||WAI}}</ref><ref>{{bug|MCPE-164963|||Fixed}}</ref>. Unlike [[jungle tree]]s, jungle bushes can generate in all jungle [[biome]] variants.
