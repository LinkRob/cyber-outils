'''Mounts of Mayhem''' is a game drop that features the [[nautilus]], and [[Zombie Nautilus|zombie nautilus]], [[Nautilus Armor|nautilus armor]], and [[spear]]s. It was released on December 9, 2025. It was revealed in [[Minecraft Wiki:Drafts/Minecraft Live 2025 2nd|Minecraft Live - September 2025]].

It is internally referred to as "'''Holiday Drop 2025'''".

== Additions ==
=== Items ===
* [[Nautilus Armor]]
** A new armor type comes with copper, iron, gold, diamond, and netherite variants.
** Can be obtained in buried treasure chests.
** Can be equipped on a tamed [[nautilus]] or [[Zombie Nautilus|zombie nautilus]].

* [[Spear]]
** A new melee weapon comes with wooden, stone, copper, iron, gold, diamond, and netherite variants.
** Has the same crafting recipe as [[shovel]]'s, but in a diagonal form.
** Has two types of attacks: charge and jab.
** The faster the player moves while charging, the more damage will be done to an entity.
** Has first appeared in [[Minecraft Dungeons]] and as an add-on in [[A Minecraft Movie: Add-on]].

* [[Netherite Horse Armor]]
** Can be obtained by upgrading a [[Diamond Horse Armor|diamond horse armor]] with a [[Netherite Upgrade|netherite upgrade smithing template]] and a [[Netherite Ingot|netherite ingot]] in the [[Smithing Table|smithing table]].

* [[Spawn Egg|Nautilus Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to summon [[Nautilus|nautiluses]].
* [[Spawn Egg|Zombie Nautilus Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to summon [[Zombie Nautilus|zombie nautiluses]].
* [[Camel Husk Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to summon [[Camel Husk|camel husk]]s.
* [[Parched Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to summon [[Parched|parched]]s.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Nautilus]]
** A new tamable mob that spawns rarely in the deep ocean, close to shipwrecks, and sea grasses.
** Can be tamed with [[Pufferfish (item)|pufferfish]], then any type of fish bucket afterwards.
** Like any ridable mobs, it can be ridden on using a [[saddle]].
*** Can also be removed using [[shears]].
** Gives a new effect "[[Breath of the Nautilus]]" when ridden on, which freezes the oxygen bar.
** Might have a chance of spawning as a baby.
** Atrracts drowned.
* [[Zombie Nautilus]]
** A new neutral [[undead]] mob variant of a [[nautilus]].
** Can be ridden on by a [[drowned]], forming a zombie nautilus jockey.
** Like any ridable mobs, it can be ridden on using a [[saddle]].
*** Can also be removed using [[shears]].
** Gives a new effect "[[Breath of the Nautilus]]" when ridden on, which freezes the oxygen bar.
** Like any undead mobs:
*** Are damaged by {{EffectLink|Instant Health}} and healed by {{EffectLink|Instant Damage}}.
*** Are not affected by {{EffectLink|Regeneration}} and {{EffectLink|Poison}}.
*** Take extra damage from [[Smite]] enchantment.
*** Burns in sunlight (when on land).
*** Are not targeted by the {{EntityLink|wither}}.
*** Causes an [[armadillo]] to hide in its shell.
** '''Coral Zombie Nautilus'''
*** A new variant of the zombie nautilus that spawns in warm oceans.
** [[Jockey|Zombie Nautilus Jockey]]
*** A new hostile jockey consists of a drowned riding a zombie nautilus.
* [[Camel Husk]]
** A new tamable undead passive mob that spawns in deserts.
** Can be tamed by killing a [[husk]] or [[parched]] riding it.
** Like any ridable mobs, it can be ridden on using a [[saddle]].
*** Can also be removed using [[shears]].
** Like any undead mobs:
*** Are damaged by {{EffectLink|Instant Health}} and healed by {{EffectLink|Instant Damage}}.
*** Are not affected by {{EffectLink|Regeneration}} and {{EffectLink|Poison}}.
*** Take extra damage from [[Smite]] enchantment.
*** Are not targeted by the {{EntityLink|wither}}.
*** Causes an [[armadillo]] to hide in its shell.
* [[Parched]]
** A new undead hostile skeleton variant that spawns in deserts.
** It wields [[bow]] and shoot players with an [[arrow]], inflicting [[weakness]] when hit.
** Like any undead mobs:
*** Are damaged by {{EffectLink|Instant Health}} and healed by {{EffectLink|Instant Damage}}.
*** Are not affected by {{EffectLink|Regeneration}} and {{EffectLink|Poison}}.
*** Take extra damage from [[Smite]] enchantment.
*** Are not targeted by the {{EntityLink|wither}}.
*** Causes an [[armadillo]] to hide in its shell.
** Like husks, parcheds do not burn in sunlight.

=== Effects ===
* [[Breath of the Nautilus]]
** A new status effect given on a [[player]] when riding a [[nautilus]] or [[Minecraft Wiki:Drafts/Zombie Nautilus|zombie nautilus]].
** Freezes the oxygen bar, preventing a player from drowning.

=== Enchantments ===
* [[Enchantments|Lunge]]
** A new enchantment that is only exclusive to [[spear]]s.
** Has up to 3 levels.
** Consumes hunger points depending of the level of this enchantment.
** It only works when using a jab attack.
** Like any enchantments, it can be used using an [[anvil]], through an [[Enchanting Table|enchanting table]], or by {{cmd|enchant}} command.

=== Achievements/Advancements ===
* [[Advancement|Mob Kabob]]
** Requires hitting 5 [[mob]]s with the same charge attack using a spear.

=== DLCs ===
* [[Mounts of Mayhem: Dungeon Descent Add-on]]
** A new [[Marketplace]] [[add-on]] that can be purchased for free.
** Supports [[Vibrant Visuals]].
** Adds new saddle charms, decorative saddles, and a new structure: Spear Trials.
