'''''Minecraft Monthly''''' is a series of monthly news videos aimed at giving a rundown of newly introduced and upcoming features regarding the [[Minecraft (franchise)|''Minecraft'']] franchise.<ref name="monthly-one">{{Ytl|jQ58UEXrFzs|1.20 name reveal and more &vert; Minecraft Monthly|Minecraft|2 March 2023}}</ref> This series is hosted by [[Vu Bui]].
