'''Caring''' is a joke [[effect]] from [[Java Edition 15w14a]] which replaces the data value of both slowness and poison effects.

==Effect==
Caring causes mobs to change their AI to move toward the nearest mob as if it were to attack it.

The effect can be removed by drinking [[Milk Bucket|milk]].
