A version of '''[[Minecraft Infdev]]''' was released on '''June 25, 2010''', marking the second Seecret Friday update.<ref>[[wordofnotch:735489725|Seecret Friday update 2! OooooOOOoo!]] – The Word of Notch, June 25, 2010</ref>

== Additions ==

=== Blocks ===
;[[File:Spawner JE1.png|32px]] [[Monster Spawner]]
* Found in dungeons.

=== Items ===
;[[File:Saddle JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Saddle]]
* Can be found in dungeons.

=== Generated structures ===
;[[Dungeon]]
* The [[chest]]s may contain [[gunpowder]], string, [[wheat]], [[bread]], [[iron ingot]]s, [[bucket]]s, [[saddle]]s, and rarely [[golden apple]]s.

=== Player format ===
* {{nbt|byte|OnGround}} tag.
** 0 if the player is in the air, 1 if on ground.

== Bugs ==
* Map generation creates "bad chunk" areas of the map in which the player and all [[Mob|NPC]]s stutter and move very slowly through.
* The [[Far Lands]] now lag and have positioning errors, allegedly different from those introduced in [[Java Edition Infdev 20100624|0624]].{{verify}}
* Signs crash the game.
