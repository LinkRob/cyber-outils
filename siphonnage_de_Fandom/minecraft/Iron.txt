'''Iron''' is a material found in the [[Overworld]]. It spawns naturally in [[iron ore]] veins and blobs, which drops raw iron, then the raw iron can be smelted into iron ingots. The iron ingots can be crafted into blocks, items, tools, and armor.

== Blocks and items ==
* {{BlockLink|Iron Ore}}, an ore found underground
* {{BlockLink|Deepslate Iron Ore}}, a variant found in the deepslate layer of the world
* {{ItemLink|Raw Iron}}, an item that drops from iron ore when mined
* {{ItemLink|Iron Ingot}}, an item created by smelting raw iron
* {{ItemLink|Iron Nugget}}, an item obtained by smelting iron tools, weapons, or armor
* {{BlockLink|Block of Raw Iron}}, a block used for compact raw iron storage
* {{BlockLink|Block of Iron}}, a block used for compact iron storage
* {{BlockLink|Iron Bars}}, a block similar to glass panes but is more blast-resistant

* {{BlockLink|Iron Door}}, a door that has to be activated by redstone
