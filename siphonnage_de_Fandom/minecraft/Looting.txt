'''Looting''' is an [[Enchanting|enchantment]] for [[sword]]s and [[Spear|spears]] that can cause [[mob]]s to [[drop]] more items and increase the chances of rare drops. The additional drops do not affect [[experience]].

== Usage ==

Increases the maximum number of items for most [[Drops#Common drops|common drops]] by 1 per level. It increases the chance of [[Drops#Rare drops|rare drops]] by making a second attempt to drop if the original attempt failed. The success chance of this second attempt is {{cd|''level'' / (''level'' + 1)}}. Looting also increases the chance of [[Drops#Rare drops|rare drops]] and [[Drops#Equipped items|equipment drops]] by 1 percentage point per level.

Looting affects any kills while holding the enchanted item in the ''main'' hand, not only to kills with the enchanted item.<ref>{{bug|MC-3304}}</ref> For example, a player can fire a projectile then switch to the Looting item before the projectile reaches the target to get the Looting effect, or can fire a projectile from the off-hand{{only|java}} while holding a Looting item in the main hand. This allows Looting to work on [[arrow]]s or thrown [[trident]]s, as long as the player is holding the Looting item when the arrow or trident lands.

Looting applies to mobs killed with a [[sweep attack]].{{only|java}}

Using [[commands]] to get higher levels than 3 works as expected, adding higher numbers with a weight of 1. High enough numbers however (like level 2500) may crash the game due to too many dropped item entities.

Looting has no effect in certain cases:
* Mobs with no drops, such as [[bat]]s, [[bee]]s, or [[endermite]]s, are not affected by this enchantment.
* [[Wither]]s, [[Iron Golem|iron golems]]<ref name="MC-200707">{{bug|MC-200707}}</ref>, [[cat]]s<ref name="MC-200707"/> and [[Snow Golem|snow golems]] are not affected by this enchantment.
* Looting does not increase the amount of fish dropped by [[fish]] mobs.<ref>{{bug|MC-212795}}</ref>
** [[Guardian]]s and elder guardians are not considered fish mobs, and Looting does cause them to drop more fish.
* {{IN|java}}, Looting does not increase the drop of [[bamboo]] from [[panda]]s.<ref>{{bug|MC-160006}}</ref>
* Looting does not increase the drop quantity when used on a [[fox]], but it does increase the chance for a drop.
* It does not affect the amount of [[wool]] dropped by [[sheep]].
* It does not affect [[sponge|sponges]] or [[Smithing Template|tide armor trims]] dropped by [[Elder Guardian|elder guardian]]s.
* It does not affect [[Totem of Undying|totems of undying]] dropped by [[evoker]]s.
* It does not affect the amount of [[Head|skulls]] dropped by [[wither skeleton]]s, but does increase the chance for them to drop one.
* It does not affect the amount of [[sculk catalyst|sculk catalysts]] dropped by [[warden]]s.
