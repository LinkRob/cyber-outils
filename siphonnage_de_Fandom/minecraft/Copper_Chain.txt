A '''copper chain''' is a [[copper|copper variant]] block that was added in [[The Copper Age]]. It is similar to [[chain]]s and oxidizes like most [[copper]] blocks.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
Copper chains can be broken with any [[pickaxe]]. Otherwise, it drops nothing.

== Usage ==
Copper chains function like [[chains|its iron counterpart]].

=== Oxidation ===
Like normal copper blocks, unwaxed copper chains undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
* Unoxidized - Normal stage
* Exposed
* Weathered
* Oxidized

Only lightning and axes can reverse the oxidation process. Using a honeycomb on an unwaxed copper chain prevents oxidation.
