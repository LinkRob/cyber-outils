'''Copper ingots''' are [[metal]] ingots that can be obtained by smelting up [[raw copper]] or occasionally dropped by [[drowned]] upon death. They are used to craft [[armor]], [[tools]], and other [[copper|decorative copper variant blocks]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===
{{Crafting
 |Block of Copper;Waxed Block of Copper|Output=Copper Ingot,9
 |type=Miscellaneous
}}

=== Smelting ===
Copper ingots can be obtained by smelting [[raw copper]] or [[copper ore]] in a [[furnace]] or [[blast furnace]].

=== Mob loot ===
==== Drowned ====

When killed by a [[player]] or a tamed [[wolf]], a [[drowned]] has a 11% ({{frac|11|100}}) chance of dropping a copper ingot. With the [[Looting]] enchantment, the chance can be increased to 13% ({{frac|13|100}}) with Looting I, 15% ({{frac|3|20}}) with Looting II, and 17% ({{frac|17|100}}) with Looting III.
