The '''block of bamboo''' is a [[log]]-like block made of [[bamboo]].

A '''block of stripped bamboo''' is an alternate variant made by {{control|using}} an axe on a block of bamboo.

== Obtaining ==
=== Breaking ===
They can be broken by hand, but using an [[axe]] speeds up the process.
{{breaking row|link=none|horizontal=1|Block of Bamboo,Block of Stripped Bamboo|Axe}}

=== Crafting ===
{{Crafting|foot=1|showdescription=1

|A3 = Bamboo |B3 = Bamboo | C3 = Bamboo
|Output = Block of Bamboo
|description = Cannot be crafted back into 9 bamboo.
}}

=== Post-generation ===
==== Stripping ====
{{control|Using}} an [[axe]] on a block of bamboo turns it into a block of stripped bamboo.

== Usage ==
=== Crafting ingredient ===
{{Crafting usage|Block of Bamboo,Block of Stripped Bamboo}}

=== Fuel ===
They can be used as a [[fuel]] in [[furnace]]s, [[smelting]] 1.5 [[item]]s per [[block]]. As fuel, it is more efficient to use [[Planks|bamboo planks]] rather than using block of bamboo. 2 bamboo planks can smelt 3 items, but a block of bamboo can only smelt 1.5 items and 9 harvested [[bamboo]] smelts 2.25 items.

Unlike [[log]]s, blocks of bamboo cannot be smelted into [[charcoal]].<ref>{{bug|MC-257428||Bamboo blocks and stripped bamboo blocks cannot be processed into charcoal|WAI}}</ref>

=== Note blocks ===
Block of bamboo can be placed under [[note block]]s to produce "bass" sounds.
