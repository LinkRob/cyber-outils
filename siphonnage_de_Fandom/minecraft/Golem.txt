The term '''Golem''' may refer to several golem or golem like mobs:

== ''Minecraft'' ==
=== Mobs ===
* {{EntityLink|Iron Golem}} - a neutral or passive mob naturally spawned in villages
** {{ItemLink|Iron Golem Spawn Egg}}
* {{EntityLink|Snow Golem}} - a passive mob created from snow blocks and a carved pumpkin or jack o'lantern
** {{ItemLink|Snow Golem Spawn Egg}}
* {{EntityLink|Copper Golem}} - a passive mob created from a copper block and a carved pumpkin or jack o'lantern; interacts with [[Copper Chest|copper chests]] and [[Chest|chests]]
** {{ItemLink|Spawn Egg|Copper Golem Spawn Egg}}

=== Blocks ===
* {{BlockLink|Copper Golem Statue}} a decorative [[copper]] statue of the copper golem; uncraftable and only obtained when a copper golem fully oxidizes.

=== Previous update ===
* {{EntityLink|Shulker}} - a hostile mob found within [[End City|end cities]] that shoots shulker bullets, forcing their victims to [[Levitation|float upwards]]. In the game files, they were referred to as a <code>City_Golem</code> before it was changed in a later update.

=== ''Education Edition'' ===
* {{EntityLink|Agent}} - a passive mob exclusive to the ''[[Education Edition]]''

== ''Minecraft Live'' ==
* {{EntityLink|Copper Golem}}  - a mob in the [[Minecraft Live 2021]] mob vote that would have operated copper buttons.
* {{EntityLink|Tuff Golem}}  - a mob in the [[Minecraft Live 2022]] mob vote that would have displayed items.
