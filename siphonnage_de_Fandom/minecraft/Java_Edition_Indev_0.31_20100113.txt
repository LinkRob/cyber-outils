A version of [[Indev 0.31]] was released on January 13, 2010.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20140427012410/http://notch.tumblr.com/post/332998186/  No more putting it off! Finite water in /indev/!] - The Word of Notch, January 13th, 2010</ref>

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===
; [[File:Water JE11.gif|32px]] [[Water]]
* Is now finite.
** Always drains from highest remove location.
* Water does not move on the surface it is on.
** Will not spread out over unfilled water surfaces, if it has an empty space next to it.
** Will evaporate ({{frac|2|3}} chance) or spread ({{frac|1|3}} chance).

=== World generation ===
* [[Ocean]]s still have infinite water.

== Creative variant ==
[[File:Indev 0.31 2010-01-13 creative.png|thumb|The Creative variant of this version.]]
A variant of the January 13, 2010, version of Indev 0.31 was released with [[Creative]] mode enabled.<ref>[https://www.minecraftforum.net/forums/archive/alpha/alpha-survival-single-player/796501-finite-water-today#c20  Minecraft Forums] - "Notch turned off the health for a little time and i managed to get on the bottom on the floating islands alive."</ref> There are a few bugs with this version due to some Creative mode code that was broken by the time this version came out; for example, blocks are not infinite.

This was one of the few versions from [[Java Edition 0.30 (Creative)|0.30]] to [[Java Edition Beta 1.7.3|Beta 1.7.3]] to get a Creative release, the only other versions being [[Java Edition Indev 0.31 20091223-1|the first]] and [[Java Edition Indev 0.31 20091223-2|second]] December 23, 2009, builds of 0.31.
