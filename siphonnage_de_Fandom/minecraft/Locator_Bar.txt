The '''Locator Bar''' is a feature for the player HUD added in [[Chase the Skies]]. It shows the position of other players and entities through the use of "waypoints". It only works in [[Multiplayer]] mode.

== Usage ==
The Locator Bar appears in place of the [[Experience|XP]] bar, except when gaining or losing XP, or while the [[Anvil|anvil]] or [[Enchanting Table|enchanting table]] GUI is on-screen. By default, it shows the position of other players in multiplayer as colored indicators, referred to as '''waypoints'''.

Waypoints appear on the locator bar when the target is horizontally within 120 degrees of the camera's view.

Players can prevent from being seen in the locator bar, as waypoints disappear if:
* The [[Player|player]] is sneaking
* The player has the [[Invisibility|invisibility]] effect
* The player is wearing a [[Head|mob head]] or a [[Pumpkin|carved pumpkin]]

Waypoint icons can be changed based on the player's distance to its location.
* Square - 0-179 blocks
* Circle - 179-230 blocks
* Small square - 230-281 blocks
* Small circle - >281 blocks

Entities' waypoints are tracked by the game when their transmission range is set to greater than zero.
