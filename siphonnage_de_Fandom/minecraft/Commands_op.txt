Grants [[operator]] status to a [[player]].

==Syntax==

* '''Java Edition'''
:<code>op <targets></code>

* '''Bedrock Edition'''
:<code>op <player: target></code>

== Arguments ==
{{argument|targets|game_profile|player: target|CommandSelector<Player>}}
: Specifies the player to be granted op.
: {{IN|java}}, {{lcfirst:{{arg desc|je=game_profile}}}}
: {{IN|bedrock}}, {{lcfirst:{{arg desc|be=player|type=players}}}}

==Result==

{{Result table}}
{{Result table|the arguments are not specified correctly|unparseable|unparseable}}
{{Result table|<code><targets></code> fails to resolve to one or more player profiles|fail|N/A}}
{{Result table|<code>player: target</code> is not in [[Target selectors#Player type and single type|player type]]|N/A|fail}}
{{Result table|<code>player: target</code> fails to resolve to one or more online players}}
{{Result table|all specified players are already op or higher.|fail}}
{{Result table|succeed}}
{{Result table}}

{{Output table}}
{{Output table|edition=je|On fail|N/A|0|0}}
{{Output table|On success|N/A|1|the number of players who are newly granted op}}
