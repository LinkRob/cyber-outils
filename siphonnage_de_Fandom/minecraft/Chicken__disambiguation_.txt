A '''{{EntityLink|chicken}}''' is a passive mob that lays eggs.

'''Chicken''' may also refer to:
* {{ItemLink|Raw Chicken}}, a food item that restores {{hunger|2}} on the hunger bar
* {{ItemLink|Cooked Chicken}}, the cooked variant of raw chicken
* {{EntityLink|Chicken Jockey}}, a chicken being ridden by a baby zombie or its variants
* {{ItemLink|Chicken spawn egg}}, the spawn egg for the chicken
