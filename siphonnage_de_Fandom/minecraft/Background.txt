The '''background''' is a decorative background of a [[GUI]] displayed in the [[menu screen]]s, during dimension teleporting, and displaying the [[end poem]].

== Backgrounds ==
=== Dirt backgrounds ===
A [[dirt]] textured background is used in the menus and settings.

Despite the texture of dirt being changed in [[Java Edition Beta 1.9 Prerelease 5]] and [[Pocket Edition v0.2.0 alpha]], this texture is still the old texture even in modern game versions, except dirt background when teleporting to [[Overworld]].{{only|BE}}<ref>{{bug|MC-198202}}</ref>

=== Netherrack background ===
{{exclusive|bedrock|section=1}}
[[File:Netherrack background.png|thumb|Netherrack background.]]

A background with [[netherrack]] texture fading from neutral to slightly orange is displayed when teleporting to [[the Nether]].
{{-}}

=== End stone background ===
{{exclusive|bedrock|section=1}}
[[File:End Stone background.png|thumb|End stone background.]]

A background with [[end stone]] texture fading from neutral to slightly darker is displayed when teleporting to [[the End]].
{{-}}

=== Nether portal background ===
[[File:Nether Portal background.png|thumb|Nether portal background. (Bedrock Edition only)]]

A background with unanimated darkened [[Nether Portal (block)|nether portal]] texture is displayed when joining a [[realm]].

[[File:Nether Portal background JE.png|thumb|Nether portal background. (Java Edition only)]]

{{In|JE}}, when moving from the Overworld to the Nether and back, an animated Nether portal texture background is used. However, it is stretched out to fit the window size more.
{{-}}

=== End stone background ===
{{exclusive|java|section=1}}
[[File:End Poem Background JE.png|thumb|End Portal background]]

An animated [[End Portal]] texture is displayed when moving from the Overworld to [[the End]] and back.

This background is also used when moving from the End to the Overworld, after defeating the [[ender dragon]]. It also displays the [[End Poem]] and credits as well.
{{-}}
