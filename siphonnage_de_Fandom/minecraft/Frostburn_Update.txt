The '''Frostburn Update''' is the name for [[Java Edition 1.10]], a major update released on June 8, 2016. It added some new features such as skeleton/zombie variants in snowy biomes and deserts respectively, polar bears, structure blocks for saving/loading structures in worlds, and an auto-jump feature.

The official name for the update, the ''Frostburn Update'', was revealed on June 8, 2016, in a blog post on the Mojang website, the same day the update was released.<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20160608153158/http://www.mojang.com/2016/06/minecraft-110-an-update-of-fire-and-ice/</ref>

Development began very shortly after [[Java Edition 1.9|1.9]] (the [[Combat Update]]) was released, and snapshots began the week after [[Java Edition 1.9.3|1.9.3]] and [[Java Edition 1.9.4|1.9.4]] were released, in mid-May. Only 3 snapshots and 2 pre-releases were released for 1.10, between May 18, 2016, and June 8, 2016.

Mojang released the [[Java Edition 1.10-pre1|first pre-release]] on June 2, 2016, and [[Java Edition 1.10-pre2|the second]] on June 7, one day before the update was released.

1.10 had only three snapshots and two pre-releases, tying it with [[Java Edition 1.1|1.1]] as the version with the least development versions; if only snapshots are considered, it is the smallest outright.

== Notable features ==
{{main|Java Edition 1.10}}
=== Additions ===

* 18 new sound events, plus 2 new ambient sounds.
* 4 new splashes.
* An "auto-jump" option (enabled by default), which automatically makes the player jump when running towards a one-block-tall obstacle.
* 1 new particle: <code>fallingdust</code>.
* 1 new command: {{cmd|teleport}}.
* 4 new NBT tags:
** <code>FallFlying</code>, <code>ZombieType</code>, <code>ParticleParam1</code> and <code>ParticleParam2</code>.
* [[Fossil]]s.
* 5 new blocks:
** [[Structure void]], [[magma block]], [[nether wart block]], red nether brick (now called [[red nether bricks]]) and [[bone block]].
* 3 new mobs:
** [[Polar bear]]s, [[stray]]s (variant of [[skeleton]]s), and [[husk]]s (variant of [[zombie]]s).

=== Changes ===

* {{cmd|tp}} command y-coordinate range increased.
* Several minor changes to world generation.
* [[Structure block]]s can now be used in-game.
* [[Chorus fruit]] and [[ender pearl]]s now teleport players from ride-able horses / pigs.
* [[Mob]]s can now shoot while they are in a boat.
* [[Enderman|Endermen]] now spawn in the Nether.
* [[Magma cube]]s now spawn about twice as often.
* [[Skeleton]]s now shoot the arrow they are holding in their off-hand.
* [[Wolf|Wolves]] no longer despawn.

== Trivia ==

*This update has the fewest amount of snapshots to date, with a mere 3 compared to the 20–30 from previous updates.
*The time span between the releases of [[16w20a]] and [[Java Edition 1.10-pre1|1.10-pre1]] was only 2 weeks, unusual based on [[Mojang]]'s history, as 1.8 and 1.9 both took around a year or so to develop.
*This update's changelog continues the trend of having "Removed [[Herobrine]]" at the very end.
*The update had only 2 further revisions, 1.10.1 and 1.10.2, which drastically shrunk from [[Java Edition 1.7.1|1.7]] (with 7 revisions), [[Java Edition 1.8|1.8]] (9 revisions), and [[Java Edition 1.9|1.9]] (4 revisions).
**This trend of only two revisions continued for two and a half years, up until [[Java Edition 1.13.2|1.13.2]].

== Further revisions ==
*[[Java Edition 1.10.1|1.10.1]] fixed "some of the most annoying reported bugs."
*[[Java Edition 1.10.2|1.10.2]] was released soon thereafter to fix a crash from 1.10.1.
