The Crab is a mob proposed in the 2023 Mob Vote & [[Minecraft Live 2023]]. The Mob was in 2nd place in October 15th, 2023

==Spawning==
Crabs spawn in [[Swamp|mangrove swamps]]. 
==Drops==
Somehow, Crabs drop [[claw]]s. They are likely not killed, but can be farmed without dying.
==Behavior==
Crabs can survive on land or water, like [[Axolotl]]s and [[Dolphin]]s, but probably last longer. They can also climb vines.
