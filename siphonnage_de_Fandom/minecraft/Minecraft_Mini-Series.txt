The '''''Minecraft Mini-Series''''' is a Canadian-American action/adventure animated web series based on [[Mojang Studios]]'s ''[[Minecraft]]''. The series is produced by Mojang Studios and [[wikipedia:Xbox Game Studios|Xbox Game Studios]] in collaboration with [[Wikipedia:Atomic Cartoons|Atomic Cartoons]] and [[Wikipedia:Mattel#Mattel Creations|Mattel Creations]], with Christopher Keenan serving as executive producer, and premiered on October 26, 2017, on the Mattel Action YouTube channel, with the final episode being released on May 10, 2018. 
It was met with mixed reviews.
