'''Bedrock Edition 1.21.40''', named [[Bundles of Bravery]], is a game drop to 1.21, released on October 22, 2024.

== Additions ==
=== [[Game mode]]s ===
* [[Hardcore]]
** A new variant of [[Survival]] mode (after [[Adventure]] mode) in which the world is locked to the hardest difficulty.
** A [[player]] dies cannot respawn, but can continue on via [[Spectator]] mode.
** Prior to this update:
*** From 1.21.0 to 1.21.30, it was only available in beta and preview.
*** Was only exclusive to [[Java Edition]].

== Changes ==
=== Items ===
* [[Bundle]]s
** No longer experimental and is now available during normal gameplay.
