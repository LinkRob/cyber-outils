'''1.2.14''' was an update for [[Bedrock Edition]], which was released exclusively to iOS devices. The update removed the developer commands that were accidentally introduced in '''1.2.13'''<ref>{{Mcnet|ext=feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360004167311-Minecraft-1-2-14-iOS-Only-|Minecraft - 1.2.14 (iOS Only)}}</ref> It is still multiplayer compatible with other devices running version [[Bedrock Edition 1.2.13|1.2.13]]. Although two beta versions ([[Bedrock Edition beta 1.2.14.2|1.2.14.2]] and [[Bedrock Edition beta 1.2.14.3|1.2.14.3]]) were released under the same name as this version, they were actually for [[Bedrock Edition 1.4.0|1.4.0]].

== Removal ==
* Removed the developer commands such as /aigoals, /cleanroom, /destroyblock, etc that were introduced in '''1.2.13'''.
