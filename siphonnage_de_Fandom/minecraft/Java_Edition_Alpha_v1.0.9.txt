'''Alpha v1.0.9'''<ref name=:1/> is a version that made changes to animations and a [[sound]], and fixed a bug.

==Changes==
*The animation of the [[player]] and [[human]]s has been changed so that their arms no longer flail around.
*The [[cow]]'s sound has changed.
*The background of the pause menu has now been darkened.

==Fixes==
;1 bug fixed
*Fixed a stuttering bug.</onlyinclude>
