A '''{{EntityLink|Bee}}''' is a neutral flying mob.

'''Bee''' may also refer to:

* The advancement [[Bee Our Guest]]
* The achievement [[Bee our guest]]
*{{BlockLink|Bee Nest}}, a natural block that is used by bees to produce honey
*{{BlockLink|Beehive}}, a craftable block that is used by bees to produce honey
* The splash [[Splash|The bee’s knees!]]
* The splash [[Splash|Bees, bees, bees, bees!]]
* The update [[Buzzy Bees]]

== ''Minecraft Dungeons'' ==
* {{DuEntityLink|Bee}}, a mob that attacks any hostile mob, is passive unlike in the base game
* {{DuItemLink|Buzzy Nest}}, an artifact that summons bees
* {{DuItemLink|Beenest Armor}}, a type of armor that has a chance to summon a bee when the player takes damage
** {{DuItemLink|Beehive Armor}}, a unique variant of the beenest armor
* [[MCD:Tumblebee|Tumblebee]], an armor enchantment that has a chance to summon a bee whenever the player rolls
* [[MCD:Busy Bee|Busy Bee]], a melee weapon enchantment that has a chance to summon a bee whenever a mob is killed with that weapon
