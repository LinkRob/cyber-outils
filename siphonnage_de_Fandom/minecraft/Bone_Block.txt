'''Bone blocks''' are storage [[block]]s equivalent to nine pieces of [[Bone Meal|bone meal]]. 

== Obtaining ==
=== Natural generation ===

Bone blocks are generated naturally as part of [[fossil]] structures found in [[desert]] and [[swamp biome]]s. They also generate as part of [[nether fossil]]s in [[soul sand valley]] biomes.

=== Breaking ===

Bone blocks can be [[breaking|mined]] using any [[pickaxe]]. If mined without a pickaxe, they [[drops|drop]] nothing.

== Usage ==
Bone blocks are decorative [[block]]s, and can also be used as compact storage for [[bone meal]].

=== Crafting ingredient ===
{{crafting usage}}

=== Note blocks ===
Bone blocks can be placed under [[note block]]s to produce xylophone [[sound]]s.
