'''Fruits''' are consumable items that can be found in trees or planted on the ground.

Some of them are:

== Blocks ==
*{{BlockLink|Melon}} - Found in jungle biomes.
*{{BlockLink|Pumpkin}} - Often found in biomes with grass blocks or in the villages.

== Items ==
====Overworld====
*{{ItemLink|Apple}} - A [[food]] that occasionally comes from oak or dark oak [[leaves]].
**{{ItemLink|Golden Apple}} - A rarer variant of apples that can be used for regeneration.
*{{ItemLink|Melon|Slice}} - The food item that comes from melon blocks.
*{{ItemLink|Sweet Berries}} - Found in berry [[bush]]es.
*{{ItemLink|Cocoa Beans}} - Collected from [[Cocoa]] plants in the jungle.
*{{ItemLink|Glow Berries}} - Grows on cave vines.

====The End====
*{{ItemLink|Chorus Fruit}} - A food from [[the End]] that can be used to teleport, found on chorus trees.
====Crafted====
*{{ItemLink|Popped Chorus Fruit}} - The cooked form of a chorus fruit. It is used as a material rather than a food.
*{{ItemLink|Glistering Melon}} - A form of melon slices with gold nuggets in it that is not edible but can be used in brewing potions.
