'''Narrator''' is a character in the official ''[https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1sELGmy5jp5fQUugmuYlXQ Minecraft]'' [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1sELGmy5jp5fQUugmuYlXQ YouTube channel] voiced by Matthew Holness.<ref>https://www.linkedin.com/posts/marillawex_voiceovers-activity-6871801222328930304-UGO-</ref>

He narrated the series [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHdY_46KnYtODUzKsnX_KWI4fAdUVdGk ''How We Make Minecraft''] and then co-hosted [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHdY_46KnYutPL4boezjUogDLKRbzXmE ''The Secrets of Minecraft''] and Around the Minecraft World in 80 Biomes with [[MARILLA]] from [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHdY_46KnYs3_DJBKiXhLg6vs7o9YFGy ''Ten Things You Probably Didn't Know About Minecraft''].

== Appearance ==
Narrator uses a skin made in the [[Bedrock Edition]]'s [[Character creator]]. Starting with the Alex skin, his base is the Slender Skin with Cool Birch color, the hair is Short Slick colored Dark Brown, the eyes are Steely Stare colored Alex Green, the top is the Tuxedo Waistcoat, and the bottom is Alex's Pants.

== Personality ==
Narrator is calm, sarcastic, false factual, and somewhat egotistical.

== Trivia ==
* The description card on ''Minecraft''<nowiki/>'s YouTube channel has him as "Untelligent",<sup>{{Sic}}</sup> "Self-proclaimed Minecraft expert", etc. This changes each episode.
* Narrator allegedly married [[Mobbo]], though he doesn't remember it.<ref>{{ytl|zj7oviDVEh4|Ending Minecraft: How We Make Minecraft Episode 9 @ 6:56|Minecraft|Apr 30, 2021|t=416s}}</ref><ref>{{ytl|Z4haZTbU9ZA|The Secrets of Minecraft's Deep Dark @ 2:12|Minecraft|June 24, 2022|t=132s}}</ref>
* He believes that "[[Minecraft Antiquated Robot Inadequate Loves Lying Audaciously|MARILLA]] was created a few thousand years before rocks were invented".<ref>{{ytl|Z4haZTbU9ZA|The Secrets of Minecraft's Deep Dark @ 0:54|Minecraft|June 24, 2022|t=67s}}</ref>
* He supposedly met his end at the hands of the [[Warden]] by the orders of [[Mobbo]].<ref>{{ytl|Z4haZTbU9ZA|The Secrets of Minecraft's Deep Dark @ 5:14|Minecraft|June 24, 2022|t=67s}}</ref>
* Narrator is canonically divorced from his ex-husband, Jonathan Minecraft.<ref>{{ytl|CSdQ61dEizI|The FINAL Secrets of Minecraft!! @ 0:22|Minecraft|July 22, 2022|t=22s}}</ref>
*He claims to have been almost cast in [[wikipedia:Avengers:_Endgame|Avengers Endgame]], playing a character Marilla refers to as “Cowardly British Man.”<ref>https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/new-show-alert--the-secrets-minecraft</ref>
