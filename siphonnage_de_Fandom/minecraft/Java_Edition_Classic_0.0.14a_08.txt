'''0.0.14a_08'''<ref>{{ytl|Drmi4EZMBzs}}</ref><ref>{{link|url=https://forums.tigsource.com/index.php?topic=6273.msg205808#msg205808|title=Minecraft (alpha)|website=TIG Forums|author=[https://forums.tigsource.com/index.php?action=profile;u=3311 YagerX]|date=May 28, 2009}}</ref><ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YRmzcrwhY28 Reupload of a lost 0.0.14a_08 video]</ref><ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0IMGwOeTqEM Reupload of another lost 0.0.14a_08 video]</ref> is a version of [[Java Edition Classic]] released on May 28, 2009, at 20:40 UTC.<ref name="release">[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''(22:38:10) <@notch> restart your clients, compare fps plox =D''" [...] "''(22:38:40) <@notch> new client update, check the fps. It should be better''" [...] "''(22:39:21) <@notch> wait.. it's supposed to say _08..''" [...] "''(22:39:25) <@notch> I think I didn't upload it right''" [...] "''(22:40:13) <@notch> ok, now''" (20:38 to 20:40 UTC)</ref> This is the last version of [[Early Classic Creative]] (i.e. the last Classic version without [[multiplayer]] functions).

==Changes==

=== Blocks===
;[[File:Oak Sapling JE2.png|32px]] [[Sapling]]s
* Saplings now appear as full bright when in shade, and turn invisible when selected while in shade.

===World generation===

; [[File:Oak JE2.png|32px]] [[Tree]]s
*Trees now have a (+) shape for the top layer.<ref>[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''[17:06] <justinforasec> notch this is just a style thing, but did u want the trees to be cubes? i found if u take off the corners they look alot more natural''" [...] "''[17:07] <@notch> oooh, that IS nicer, justinforasec'' [...] ''[17:07] <@notch> I'm definitely making it so for the next version, thank you'' [...] ''(22:46:02) <mezo> lol @ new trees''"(17:06 UTC)</ref>

===General===
*Improved framerate.<ref name="release" />
*Shadows now show up on water and lava surfaces.<ref>[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''[14:43] <@notch> a free bonus is that shadows now show up on water surfaces :D''" (20:43 UTC)</ref>
*Fog is now white instead of blue.<ref>[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''[10:35] <justinforasec> i dont get why the fog is blue'' [...] ''[10:35] <justinforasec> wouldnt white or greyish be better?''"(17:06 UTC)</ref><ref>[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''[23:07] <justinforasec> hey the fog is white!'' [...] ''[23:07] <justinforasec> ty notch''"(17:06 UTC)</ref>
*Fog now gets applied to shadow surfaces.

==Trivia==
*The <samp>[[terrain.png]]</samp> file of this version seems to have an early texture for [[wool]], [[cloud]]s,<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20220522024237/https://imgur.com/a/B7Ize8x</ref> or [[snow block]]s.
