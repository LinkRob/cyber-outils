A '''nautilus''' is a neutral [[taming|tamable]] aquatic mob. It can be tamed using [[Pufferfish (item)|pufferfish]], can be bred and fed using any fish or any type of [[Bucket of aquatic mob|fish bucket]], equip with [[Nautilus Armor]], and can be ridden on using a [[saddle]].

== Spawning ==
Nautili usually spawn in all [[Ocean|ocean biomes]]. Normally in groups of 1-2. Also in the ocean biomes, Baby Nautili can also spawn.

== Behavior ==
Nautili are passive to players and other mobs. They use their tentacles to propel forward in water. They cannot survive on land and suffocate unless pushed back into the water.

Nautili are immune to [[poison]]. They attack [[pufferfish]]es and attract [[drowned]]s.

When a player attacks an untamed nautilus, it uses its dash attack to inflict damage on the player.

=== Taming ===
Nautili can be tamed by feeding them [[Pufferfish (item)|pufferfish]] or with [[Bucket of aquatic mob|Buckets with Pufferfish]].

=== Riding ===
Tamed nautili can be equipped with [[Nautilus Armor]] and can be ridden on by using a [[saddle]]. When ridden, it gives the "[[Breath of the Nautilus]]" effect, which pauses the player's oxygen bar and prevents the player from [[drowning]].

=== Breeding ===
Nautili can be bred and fed on by using any fish or any type of [[Bucket of aquatic mob|fish in a bucket]].

== Trivia ==
* The nautilus is the first Minecraft mob to be based on a living fossil.
** They were once referred to as being extinct in a Minecraft article. 
* Despite being immune to the poison effect, pufferfish are still toxic to nautili in the real world.
