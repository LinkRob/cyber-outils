{|{{Simple Guide table|New Items}}
|-
|[[Rabbit skin]]
|[[File:Rabbit Hide JE2 BE1.png|84px]]
|
* Adult [[Rabbit]] will drop 0-1 rabbit skin when killed.
|
* Can be used to synthesize [[Leather]].
|-
|[[raw rabbit meat]]
|[[File:Raw Rabbit JE2 BE1.png|84px]]
|
* Adult rabbits will drop 0-1 raw rabbit meat when killed.
|
* Press and hold the {{Control|Use}} key in the hotkey bar to eat raw rabbit meat. Each piece of raw rabbit meat will restore {{hunger|3}} hunger and 1.8 [[saturation]].
* Raw rabbit meat can be used to [[breed]] or heal tamed [[wolves]], guide them to move around, and also speed up the remaining growth time of tamed wolf pups by 10%.
* Raw rabbit meat can be used to cook cooked rabbit meat.
|-
|[[Cooked rabbit meat]]
|[[File:Cooked Rabbit JE2 BE1.png|84px]]
|
* Adult rabbits will drop 0-1 cooked rabbit meat when they die on fire.

|
* Press and hold the {{Control|Use}} key in the hotkey bar to eat cooked rabbit meat. Each cooked rabbit meat will restore {{hunger|5}} hunger and 6 [[saturation]].
* Cooked rabbit meat can be used to [[breed]] or heal tamed [[wolves]], guide them to move around, and also speed up the remaining growth time of tamed wolf pups by 10%.
* Cooked rabbit meat can be used to craft rabbit meat stew.
|-
