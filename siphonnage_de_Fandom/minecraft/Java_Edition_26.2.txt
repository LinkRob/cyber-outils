* [[Potent Sulfur]]
** A new sulfur block variant that emits gas bubbles when placed on underwater.
** Inflicts [[nausea]] when a player or mob gets close to or enters a sulfur pool.
** Can be used to create geysers.
*** To create a geyser, the potent sulfur must be placed on top of a [[magma block]] and under 1-4 [[water]]-source blocks.
*** Can also trigger geysers when [[lava]] is placed underneath..
** Like any stone-related blocks, it can only be mined with a [[pickaxe]].
* [[Sulfur Spike]]
** A new pointy and sharpy block that naturally generates in [[sulfur caves]].
** Like [[pointed dripstone]]s:
*** Creates a stalactite when placed above the ceiling, and creates a stalagmite when placed on the ground.
*** Breaks instantly with a thrown [[trident]].
** Unlike pointed dripstones:
*** Does not increase fall damage when fallen on a stalagmite.
*** Does not drip liquid on a stalactite's tip when [[water]] or [[lava]] is placed on the block above where the spike is hanging from.
** Can be used to craft [[sulfur]] blocks.

=== Items ===
* [[Sulfur Cube Spawn Egg]]
** Can be used to spawn [[sulfur cubes]].
** Spawns in random size.
* [[Bucket of Sulfur Cube]]
** Can be obtained by using a bucket on a sulfur cube.
** Spawns sulfur cube when spawned.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Sulfur Cube]]
** A new passive [[Slime (disambiguation)|slime-like mob]] that mostly spawns in sulfur caves.
** Like [[slime]]s and [[magma cube]]s, they split into two upon death.
*** However, smaller cubes can grow to larger versions.
** Has 12 effects depending on the type of block absorbed:
*** Bouncy - makes it very bouncy
*** Explosive - explodes when ignited
*** Fast Flat - less bouncy, slides more often
*** Fast Sliding - unable to bounce, able to slide
*** High Resistance - unable to bounce, very hard to push
*** Light - lower gravity, makes it float
*** Slow Flat - higher gravity, makes it heavy
*** Slow Sliding - unable to bounce, slides slow.
*** Sticky - unable to bounce or slide
*** Slow Bouncy - can bounce, but very slow
*** Hot - makes players and mobs take damage when nearby, similar to walking in [[magma block]]
*** Regular - able to bounce and slide often
** When a player drops or uses a [[block]] on a sulfur cube, the mob sucks the block up and turns into a bouncy cube.
*** Can be removed by using [[shears]].

=== Biomes ===
* [[Sulfur Cave]]s
** A new underground biome that mainly consists of sulfur and cinnabar blocks.
** Sulfur pools generate in this biome.
** It is where [[sulfur cubes]] spawn naturally.

=== Structures ===
* [[Sulfur Pool]]
** Generates as part of sulfur caves.
** Consists of bodies of water and potent sulfur.
** A potent sulfur placed underwater inflicts nausea on nearby players and mobs.
* [[Sulfur Spring]]
** A new structure that generates on any overworld biomes above [[sulfur caves]].
** Consists of [[sulfur]] and [[potent sulfur]], [[tuff]], [[granite]], [[water]], and [[magma block]]s.
** Can trigger geysers overtime.

=== Advancements ===
* Uh Oh
** Requires a sulfur cube absorbing a TNT block.

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
* [[Menu screen]]
** Changed the [[panorama]] to reflect [[Chaos Cubed]] game drop.

=== Blocks ===
* [[TNT]]
** Can now be absorbed by sulfur cubes, either by picking it up or interacting with it.
*** This gives an explosive effect when ignited.
* [[Stone]]s
** Can now be absorbed by sulfur cubes, either by picking it up or interacting with it.
*** This gives a new slow bouncy effect when hit.
* [[Magma Block]]s
** Can now be absorbed by sulfur cubes, either by picking it up or interacting with it.
*** This gives a new hot effect, damaging nearby players and mobs similar to walking on a regular magma block.
