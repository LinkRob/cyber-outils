'''1.1''' is a major update to ''Minecraft'' ({{el|je}}) released on January 12, 2012,<ref>{{cite|url=https://www.mojang.com/2012/01/minecraft-1-1-update/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/0/https://www.mojang.com/2012/01/minecraft-1-1-update/|title=Minecraft 1.1 Update|author=Lydia Winters|date=January 12, 2012|website=Mojang.com}}</ref> which adds [[spawn egg]]s, support for multiple languages, the [[Superflat]] world type, and beaches. It is the first update to begin using snapshots, and to add 56 languages, and the first major release that did not introduce any new blocks. There were no minor versions to 1.1.

== Additions ==
=== Items ===
;{{Animate|Creeper Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Spider Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Skeleton Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Zombie Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Slime Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Ghast Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Zombie Pigman Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Enderman Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Cave Spider Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Silverfish Spawn Egg JE2 BE2.png;Blaze Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Magma Cube Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Pig Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Sheep Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Cow Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Chicken Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Squid Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Wolf Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Mooshroom Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png;Villager Spawn Egg JE2 BE1.png|32px}} [[Spawn egg]]s
*Spawns mobs when {{control|used}}.
*20 types of spawn eggs, each spawning their respective mob:
**The types were [[blaze]], [[cave spider]], [[chicken]], [[cow]], [[creeper]], [[enderman]], [[ghast]], [[magma cube]], [[mooshroom]], [[pig]], [[sheep]], [[silverfish]], [[skeleton]], [[slime]], [[spider]], [[squid]], [[villager]], [[wolf]], [[zombie]], and [[zombie pigman]].
*Are a creative-exclusive item.

=== World generation ===

; [[Biome]]s

* {{BiomeSprite|Beach}} [[Beach]]es
** Re-added beaches after being removed in [[Java Edition Beta 1.8|Beta 1.8]].
***Are now separate [[biome]]s instead of generated structures.

* {{BiomeSprite|Desert Hills}} [[Desert Hills]]
** Hills variant of [[Desert]].

* {{BiomeSprite|Mountain Edge}} [[Mountain Edge|Extreme Hills Edge]]
** Generates at edges of [[Mountains|Extreme Hills]].

* {{BiomeSprite|Wooded Hills}} [[Wooded Hills|Forest Hills]]
** Hills variant of [[Forest]]s.

* {{BiomeSprite|Taiga Hills}} [[Taiga Hills]]
** Hills variant of [[Taiga]]s.

;[[World type]]s
*Re-added world types to the Create World menu.
**Types are Default and Superflat.
*[[Superflat]] world type
**Generates completely flat worlds composed of [[bedrock]], [[dirt]], and [[grass block]]s.
**[[Village]]s can generate.

=== General ===
;Languages
*Added 56 new translations.
**Includes constructed and fictional languages such as Pirate Speak, Klingon, and Quenya.

;[[Server.properties]]
*New settings:
**<code>level-type</code>
**<code>spawn-npcs</code>
**<code>generate-structures</code>

== Changes ==
=== Blocks ===

;[[File:Farmland JE3 BE3.png|32px]][[Farmland]]
*Farmland is no longer trampled when walked upon.

;[[File:Oak Fence Gate JE2.png|32px]] [[Fence gate]]s
*The collision box on fence gates now matches that of [[fence]]s.
*Fence gates can now be opened and closed using [[redstone]].

;[[File:Ladder (S) JE2 BE1.png|32px]] [[Ladder]]s
*Removed collision box from ladders.

;[[File:Melon JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Melon]]s and [[File:Carved Pumpkin (S) JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[pumpkin]]s
*Can now grow onto dirt instead of only [[farmland]].

;[[File:Oak Leaves JE3 BE4.png|32px]][[Oak leaves]]
*Oak leaves now have a {{frac|1|200}} chance of dropping apples.

=== Items ===
;[[File:Bow JE2 BE1.png|32px]] [[Bow]]s
*Bows are now [[enchant]]able, with four new possible enchantments specific to bows: ''[[Power]]'', ''[[Punch]]'', ''[[Infinity]]'', and ''[[Flame]]''.

;[[File:Golden Apple JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Golden apple]]s
*Golden apple recipe has been changed to use [[gold nugget]]s instead of [[gold block]]s.<ref group=note name="Release change">This change occurred between the last development version for 1.1, 12w01a, and the full release of 1.1.</ref>
*Golden apples now restore {{hungerbar|4}} and provide [[Regeneration]] I for 5 seconds.<ref group=note name="Release change" />

;{{Animate|Music Disc 13 JE1 BE1.png;Music Disc Cat JE1 BE1.png;Music Disc 11 JE1 BE1.png;Music Disc Blocks JE1 BE1.png;Music Disc Chirp JE1 BE1.png;Music Disc Far JE1 BE1.png;Music Disc Mall JE1 BE1.png;Music Disc Mellohi JE1 BE1.png;Music Disc Stal JE1 BE1.png;Music Disc Strad JE1 BE1.png;Music Disc Ward JE1 BE1.png|32px}} [[Music disc]]s
*All music discs except for disc 11 can now be dropped when a [[creeper]] is killed by a [[skeleton]].

;[[File:Wheat JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Wheat]]
*The distance that wheat attracts mobs has increased from 2–3 blocks to 8–9 blocks.

=== Mobs ===
;Mob AI
*Added the infrastructure for improved mob AI.
**Allowed for more complicated behavior than before.
**These behaviors themselves have not been implemented yet.

;[[File:Magma Cube Revision 1.png|48px]] [[Magma cube]]s
*Can now drop magma cream upon death.

;{{Animate|Orange Sheep JE1.png;Magenta Sheep JE1.png;Light Blue Sheep JE1.png;Yellow Sheep JE1.png;Lime Sheep JE1.png;Pink Sheep JE1.png;Gray Sheep JE1.png;Light Gray Sheep JE1.png;Cyan Sheep JE1.png;Purple Sheep JE1.png;Blue Sheep JE1.png;Green Sheep JE1.png;Brown Sheep JE1.png;Red Sheep JE1.png;Black Sheep JE1.png;|48px}} [[Sheep]]
*Sheep now can eat [[grass]] and tall grass, which regrows their wool.
*Baby sheep now eat faster than before.
*Sheep now drop 1–3 wool per shearing rather than 2-4.

=== Non-mob entities ===
;[[File:Arrow JE2 BE1.png|32px]] [[Arrow]]s
*Arrows that are burning now set [[entities]] on fire when hit.

=== World generation ===
;Biomes
*Biomes have more varied elevations of land in previously flatter biomes.
**These biomes include Desert, Forest, and Taiga.
*Smoothed color transitions between biomes.
**[[Swampland]] grass, foliage, and water smoothly transition into other biomes.
*[[Taiga]]s now contain snow and ice.

;{{EnvLink|Village}}s
*[[Blacksmith]] buildings in villages now have a chest that contains loot.

=== Gameplay ===
;[[File:Brewing Stand (full) JE1.png|32px]] [[Brewing]]
*Reduced brewing time from 30 seconds to 20 seconds.

;[[File:Enchanting Table JE1 BE1.png|32px]] [[Enchanting]]
*Narrowed the various possibility of enchantment levels.

;{{EnvLink|The Void}}
*Removed the void fog in creative mode.

=== General ===
;[[Server]]s
*Non-operator users are auto-kicked in multiplayer servers if they spam in chat too much.
*Operators are not notified of the kick; they must assume that the user was auto-kicked.

== Fixes ==
;3 bugs fixed
*Fixed "Sniper Duel" achievement not working.
*Fixed [[powered rails]] not depowering.
*Fixed [[powered rails]] not updating when placed.
