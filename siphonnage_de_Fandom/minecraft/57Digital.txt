'''''57Digital''''' is a mobile application development company based in [[wikipedia:Sheffield|Sheffield]], [[wikipedia:England|England]].   It is run by Jamie Chapman and Luke Dixon, two software engineers from [[wikipedia:Sheffield|Sheffield]].

57Digital is responsible for creating an official<ref>{{tweet|carlmanneh|182406087545339904|Need a companion app for Minecraft? We recommend this one: http://itunes.apple.com/ng/app/minecraft-explorer-pro/id449354665?mt{{=}}8 …|March 21, 2012}}</ref> ''Minecraft'' companion guide, ''Minecraft Explorer, and [https://www.bedrockexplorer.com/ BedrockExplorer.com], a Minecraft Marketplace exploration tool for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.''
