A '''birch forest''' is a lush [[biome]], usually generating near other woodland biomes. There are two variants in the biome family.

==Description==

Birch forest biomes are almost exactly the same as the regular [[forest]] biome. The most obvious difference is that the trees generated are different. Birch forest biomes feature [[birch]] [[tree]]s across the landscape. They generate as densely as trees in the regular forest biome. And unlike the regular forest biome, wolves do not spawn here.

Birch forest biomes are rarer than regular forest biome, and usually surrounded by regular forests or [[dark forest]]s. Regular birch forests are most commonly separated from old growth birch forests or meadows by rivers.

Like forests, birch forests are an excellent starter biome in a Survival world.

==Variants==
{{for|variants that existed before 1.18|Biome/Before 1.18}}
There are two birch forest biome variants (birch forest and old growth birch forest), with two removed variants in previous versions.

The '''old growth birch forest''' features birch trees, half of which are significantly taller than normal, with their trunks being up to 14 blocks tall. This makes harvesting wood more difficult, but allows more wood to be collected in the long run. [[Trail ruins]] generate here.
