'''Luck''' is a [[status effect]] that makes it more likely to receive better loot from certain loot tables, contrary to [[Bad Luck]].

== Effect ==
Adds 1 × level to [[attribute]] <code>generic.luck</code>, making [[loot table]] entries with a high <code>quality</code> score more likely, and entries with negative <code>quality</code> less likely. It also increases the chances of <code>bonus_rolls</code> occurring. Currently, only [[fishing]] uses <code>quality</code>. The new weight of each entry is <code>floor(base_weight + quality * generic.luck)</code>. Negative levels decrease luck.

Using a [[fishing rod]] enchanted with [[Luck of the Sea]] increases a [[player]]'s <code>generic.luck</code> attribute, but doesn't actually grant the Luck status effect.

== Immune mobs ==
While no mobs, except bosses, are immune to Luck, only players are actually affected.
