A version of '''Indev 0.31''' was released on '''February 2, 2010''', at 23:31 UTC.<ref>{{link |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140427012048/http://notch.tumblr.com/post/367646413/ |title=No functioning mobs today, unfortunately |website=The Word of Notch |date=February 2, 2010}}</ref><ref group="note">While some sources imply that this version was released on February 3, it was actually released at 23:31 UTC on February 2 (which is 00:31 on February 3, Sweden time).</ref>

== Changes ==
=== General ===
; Splash
* Depending on the length of the splash, it changes size in accordance to the default resolution.
=== Blocks ===
; [[File:Water JE5.gif|32px]] [[Water]]
* Generates splash particles when swam in.

; [[File:Lava JE6.gif|32px]] [[Lava]]
* Pushes out items.
* No longer generates on the surface of Normal themed worlds.

; [[File:TNT JE2 BE1.png|32px]] [[TNT]]
* Now hurts mobs.
** Items are still safe from TNT explosions.
* Pushes away entities.

; {{Animate|Coal Ore JE1 BE1.png;Iron Ore JE1 BE1.png;Gold Ore JE2 BE1.png;Diamond Ore JE1 BE1.png|32px}} [[Ore]]s and [[File:Stone JE2 BE1.png|32px]] [[stone]]
* No longer requires a pickaxe to break the block, although a pickaxe breaks it much faster.<ref group="note" name="gameplay_evidence" />
** Once again possible to mine [[stone]]-related blocks with anything that's not a [[pickaxe]].
** Mineral blocks (iron, gold, diamond) are treated similarly to their corresponding ores.

=== Items ===
* No longer destroyed in fire or lava.
* Shadows display under dropped items.
* No longer go into hidden inventory slots once a save is reloaded.

; [[Tools]] and [[Tiers]]
* Harder materials no longer require better tools.<ref group="note" name="gameplay_evidence">This has been verified during gameplay of this page's client jar, dated February 2, 2010 23:31 UTC.</ref>
* Mining underwater or in the air is now slower.
* Swords deal less damage.
* Mining tools can now hit mobs. Their damage depends on the type and tier of tool.

=== Entities ===
; Mobs
* Re-added pigs, sheep, skeletons, creepers, zombies and spiders.
** Monsters are passive due to their hostile AI missing.
** Sheep spawn with no wool due to their AI for eating grass not working.
** All mobs have {{Health|10}} health.
* Removed Humans.</onlyinclude>

; Other
* Changed pickup animation for arrows and dropped items.
** They now get sucked up when the player walks onto them.

== Fixes ==
''' From the previous version '''
* Flowers no longer spawn with dirt under them.
* Standing with the player's head inside non-solid blocks no longer causes extreme fog.

== Trivia ==
* The 23:27 UTC blog post announcing this version's release occurred 4 minutes before this page's client jar was packaged (23:31).
** The blog post features Notch talking about not yet getting mobs to function, yet gameplay of this page's client jar contains all of the old mobs, with animation.
