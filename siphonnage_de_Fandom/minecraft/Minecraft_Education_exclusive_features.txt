{{Exclusive|education}}

This is the list of features that are available exclusively to the {{el|ee}}. This is comparison between the {{els|be|ee}}.

== General ==
;Tutorial worlds
:  Guide players on in-game navigation, crafting, and placing or breaking blocks.
:  Includes the World of Chemistry
;Lessons in the game. Try to use that if you can find it
:  Downloadable from education.minecraft.net
;3D Exporting
:  Allows players to use their creations in the real world and share them via Remix 3D (remix 3D has now been discontinued)
;[[File:Classroom Mode.png|32px]] [[Classroom Mode]]
:  A companion app for ''Minecraft'' that shows a high-level map view of the world, a list of all the students, the ability for a teacher to teleport students, and a chat window to communicate.
:  It is provided for educators who may want to observe student activity without being in the game, and is not needed for students to play.
;[[File:Code Connection.png|32px]] [[Code Connection]]
:  Allows educators and students to explore, create, and play in an immersive Minecraft world – all by writing code. 
;[[Code builder]]
:  An interface used to program agents and other in-game events using editors.
;[[Minecraft MakeCode]]
:  A version of Microsoft MakeCode designed for Minecraft: Education Edition.
;[[Library]]
:  A list of the most popular educational worlds and content to import and use.
;Easy Classroom Collaboration
:  An entire classroom of up to thirty (30) students can play in a world together with no separate server setup required.
:  Students can work together in pairs or groups simply by joining their classmates' world.
;Simple, Secure sign-in
:  Individual student and teacher logins with Office 365 Education accounts ensure data privacy & security while playing Minecraft: Education Edition.

== Blocks ==

;[[File:Border BE1.png|32px]] [[Border]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
: Entities cannot pass over or under border blocks, no matter how far above or below they are.
;[[File:Allow BE1.png|32px]] [[Allow Block]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
: Players can build above allow blocks even in worlds where they cannot build otherwise.
;[[File:Deny BE1.png|32px]] [[Deny Block]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
: Players cannot build above deny blocks even in worlds where they can build otherwise.
;[[File:Chalkboard Slate.png|32px]][[File:Chalkboard Poster.png|32px]][[File:Chalkboard Board.png|32px]] [[Chalkboard]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
: Allows creators to communicate learning goals, provide additional information and give explicit instructions within the game.
: It comes in three different sizes – Slate (1×1), Poster (2×1), and Board (2×3).
;[[File:Element Constructor BE1.png|32px]] [[Element Constructor]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock">These blocks and items also exist {{in|bedrock}}, but only in worlds with "Education Edition" enabled.</ref>
: It allows the creation of [[elements]] based on their atomic structure with sliders to choose the number of protons, neutrons and electrons.
: There are 118 elements from the Periodic Table as well as over 400 stable isotopes.
;[[File:Compound Creator BE1.png|32px]] [[Compound Creator]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
: Add [[elements]] to the grid and the resulting [[compound]] will appear to use.
: There are over 30 compounds that can be created with this tool – from luminol and hydrogen peroxide to charcoal and soap.
;[[File:Lab Table BE1.png|32px]] [[Lab Table]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
: It allows the creation of items by combining [[elements]] and [[compounds]] in a grid.
: For example, adding [[Compound#Water|water]] and [[Compound#Sodium Hypochlorite|sodium hypochlorite]] makes [[bleach]], which a player can use to turn wool white.
;[[File:Material Reducer BE1.png|32px]] [[Material Reducer]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
: It reduces a [[block]] to its component [[elements]], providing a useful way to explore the [[elements]] that make up our environment.
: Since some blocks in ''Minecraft'' do not exist in the natural world, there is a "?" element for these to encourage curiosity of learners.
;[[File:Heat Block BE1.gif|32px]] [[Heat Block]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
: Melts [[snow]] and [[ice]] without producing light.
;[[File:Underwater TNT BE1.png|32px]] [[Underwater TNT]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
: TNT that can damage other blocks underwater.
;[[File:Underwater Torch BE1.png|32px]] [[Underwater Torch]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
: [[Torch|Torches]] that can function underwater.

: Torches that are colored.
;[[File:Hardened Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened White Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Orange Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Magenta Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Light Blue Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Yellow Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Lime Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Pink Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Gray Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Light Gray Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Cyan Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Purple Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Blue Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Brown Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Green Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Red Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]][[File:Hardened Black Stained Glass BE1.png|32px]] [[Hardened Glass]]<ref group="note" name="bedrock"/>
