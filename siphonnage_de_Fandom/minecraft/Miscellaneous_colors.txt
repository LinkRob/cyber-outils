== List ==

===Rendering===

Blocks and items in the Creative inventory have a darkened tint attached to them on ''Java Edition''. If all textures are set to {{color|#FFFFFF}}, then all items, single sided blocks, and the top faces of all other blocks, are rendered slightly darker at about 98.8% brightness {{color|#FCFCFC}}, the bottom left side of blocks at about 64.3% brightness {{color|#A4A4A4}}, and the bottom right side at 39.6% brightness {{color|#656565}}. In full brightness, blocks render at 98.8% brightness {{color|#FCFCFC}} on the top, 79.2% brightness {{color|#CACACA}} on the Z-axis, and 59.2% brightness {{color|#979797}} on the X-axis.

This is due a rendering bug: {{bug|MC-213446}} and {{bug|MC-123703}}. <!--- Prior to 1.15, {{color|#FFFFFF}} 100% for items and the tops of blocks, {{color|#A2A2A2}} 63.5% brightness for the bottom left side of blocks, and {{color|#6F6F6F}} 43.5% brightness for the bottom right side of blocks--->

=== Entities ===

==== Experience orbs ====
{{section needed}}
Experience orb textures are mostly white, gray, and red; a gradient is applied afterward to make them green and yellow.

=== Dyed sheep ===

Sheep colors do not seem to match dye colors or firework star colors directly one to one, they differ slightly.{{info needed}}

=== Wolf and cat collars ===

Both wolf and cat collars use the [[Dye#Color values|dye colors]] directly one to one.

=== Other world elements ===
==== Banners ====

Banners are colored according to default [[Dye#Color values| dye colors]].

==== Beacon Beam ====

Beacon beams are colored according to default [[Dye#Color values| dye colors]]. Without any stained class, it defaults to the color for white dye.

===Miscellaneous color codes===

In the source code of ''Java Edition'', is a third set of color codes. These aren't used for dyes, firework stars, or leather armor; and the values differ from those of the chat colors; so what these values are used for are unknown. They have been in the game since at least [[Java Edition 1.17.1|1.17.1]] through present day versions (1.20.1+).
