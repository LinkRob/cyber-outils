'''''Minecraft: Soothing Scenes''''' is a series of six singles released for ''Minecraft'', featuring compositions by [[Samuel Åberg]], which feature ambience pertaining to the particular theme. The first five were released on December 16, 2022, followed by "Relaxing Cherry Grove" on June 2, 2023.
