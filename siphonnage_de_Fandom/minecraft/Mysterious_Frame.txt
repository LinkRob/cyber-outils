'''Mysterious Frame''' is a [[Bedrock Edition|Bedrock]] exclusive block that is unobtainable in the game. It shares a similar texture of the [[Reinforced Deepslate]], except the top texture has a deep hole in the middle.

== Obtaining ==
This block in unobtainable by normal means but can be obtained through Inventory Editors/ NBT editors and possibly Item Transmutation. It is most likely a placeholder block and/or a test block. According to YouTuber ElementX this block is obtained through editing a "Moving Block"'s NBT data, this is most likely referring to the "Moving Piston" block

=== Breaking ===
The mysterious frame drops nothing when broken.

== Trivia ==
* The name "Mysterious Frame" and "Mysterious Frame Slot" are actually the name for reinforced deepslate in the China Edition.
** And since technically Minecraft Bedrock Edition and Minecraft China Edition are very, very similar  this could mean that this block is a left over.
* The fact that it won't drop an item when broken is commonly shared through other unused/ invalid blocks.
** The reason for this is unknown.
