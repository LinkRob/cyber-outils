'''Curse of Vanishing''' is an [[enchantment]] that causes the item to disappear upon death.

==Obtaining==
Curse of Vanishing is a [[treasure enchantment]]; it can be obtained from [[End City]], [[Ancient City]], [[Jungle Temple]], and Stronghold [[chest loot]], Ruined Portal,  [[fishing]], [[raid]] drops{{only|bedrock|short=1}}, or [[trading]] with a librarian of any level.

==Usage==
When the player dies while an item enchanted with Curse of Vanishing is in their inventory, it does not drop on the ground, but is removed from the world entirely. The item does not vanish if it is {{control|dropped}} manually, or if it is placed in a [[chest]] before death.

The effect can be mitigated by storing affected items inside a [[shulker box]],<ref>{{bug|MC-109053|||WAI}}</ref> or by setting the <code>keepInventory</code> [[gamerule]] to <code>true</code>.

Curse of Vanishing cannot be removed by a [[grindstone]] or a crafting table. However, if the cursed item is a pumpkin or mob head, placing and breaking the head removes the curse.

If an item dropped by a mob (such as a zombie or skeleton wearing armor) has Curse of Vanishing, the enchantment's effect works as normal.

Of all enchantments in the game, Curse of Vanishing is compatible with the greatest number of different item types. It can be added to any type of item which can be enchanted at all, including [[compass]], [[carved pumpkin]] and [[jack o'lantern]].

The effect is irrelevant in singleplayer [[Hardcore]] difficulty because death is permanent, so no items can be retrieved after dying.
