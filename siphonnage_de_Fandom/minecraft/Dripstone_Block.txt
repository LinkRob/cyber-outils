The '''dripstone block''' is a [[rock]] block that allows [[pointed dripstone]] to grow beneath it.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===
Dripstone blocks require a [[pickaxe]] to be mined, in which case it drops itself. When mined without a pickaxe, it drops nothing. It can be blown up by [[TNT]], in which case it also drops itself.
{{breaking row|Dripstone Block|pickaxe|wooden|horizontal=1}}

=== Natural generation ===
{{main|Dripstone (feature)}}
Dripstone blocks can be found in [[dripstone caves]]. They can generate on the floor, the ceiling, as stalactites, stalagmites, or stalagnates (joined stalactite and stalagmite). They can generate underwater.

=== Trading ===
{{IN|java}}, journeyman-level stone mason [[villager]]s have a {{frac|2|7}} chance to [[trading|sell]] 4 dripstone blocks for one [[emerald]] as part of their trades.

{{IN|bedrock}}, journeyman-level stone mason villagers have a {{frac|1|4}} chance to sell 4 dripstone blocks for one emerald as part of their trades.‌

== Usage ==
Dripstone blocks are used primarily for decoration.

=== Natural growth ===
{{main|Pointed Dripstone#Growing}}
If a stalactite is hanging from a dripstone block with a [[water]] source above, it will slowly grow both the stalactite from above and a stalagmite from below.
