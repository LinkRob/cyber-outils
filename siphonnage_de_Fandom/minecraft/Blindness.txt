'''Blindness''' is a [[status effect]] that impairs the player's vision. It can be cast by [[illusioner]]s{{Only|Java}} or by consuming [[suspicious stew]] made with an [[Flower|Azure Bluet]].

== Effect ==
{{See also|Fog#Blindness fog}}
[[File:Blindness View.png|thumb|right|The view from a player who is afflicted by blindness.]]
Blindness impairs the player's vision as if a thick black [[fog]] were surrounding them, with only the immediate area being visible (this means that only a few blocks directly around the player are visible to them). However, the sun and moon can be seen as normal, but it has to be during clear weather.{{only|java}} Additionally, [[liquid]]s do not fade into the fog perfectly. The effect also prevents the player from [[sprinting]] or inflicting [[Damage#Critical hits|critical hits]] but does not stop an ongoing sprint.

The effect can be removed by drinking [[Milk Bucket|milk]]. {{IN|education|bedrock}} when education options are enabled, drinking [[eye drops]] also removes blindness.

Additionally, [[warden]]s are only naturally blind mobs even without requiring the Blindness status effect, and they can be found in [[Ancient City|ancient cities]].

Blindness does not effect [[Mob|mobs]].
