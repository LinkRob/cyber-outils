'''Curse''' may refer to:
* [[Curse of Binding]] - a rare enchantment used on [[armor]] to prevent it from being removed.
* [[Curse of Vanishing]] - a rare enchantment used on [[tools]], [[weapons]] and armor, which causes them to disappear when the wearer [[die]]s.
* [[Bad Omen]] - a [[status effect]] that triggers a [[raid]] when the player enters a [[village]] or a [[Trial Omen|ominous event]] in a [[trial chamber]].
* [[Bad Luck]] - a [[status effect]] that decreases your {{code|luck}} [[attribute]] making chest loot less valuable.
