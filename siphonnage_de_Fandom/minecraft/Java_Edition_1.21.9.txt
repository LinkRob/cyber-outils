'''Java Edition 1.21.9''', named [[The Copper Age]], is a game drop to 1.21 for [[Java Edition]], released on September 30, 2025.

== Additions ==
=== [[Block]]s ===
* [[Copper Golem Statue]]
** A new decorative block composed of waxed and oxidized variants.
** It turns into this state when a [[Copper Golem|copper golem]] gets fully oxidized.
*** It reverts back to a copper golem if the statue is unoxidized when interacted with an [[axe]].
** The block's pose can be changed by interacting with it.
** Like normal [[Block of Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper golem statues undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
*** Unoxidized - Normal stage
*** Exposed
*** Weathered
*** Oxidized
** Only lightning and axes can revert the oxidation process.
*** Using a [[Honeycomb|honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper golem statue prevents the oxidation.
*** Right-clicking an unoxidized or waxed copper golem statue with an axe converts the statue into a copper golem.
** Activated [[piston]]s push them off as an item
*** Also drop as an item when mined.
* [[Shelf]]
** A new decorative block that displays items similar to an [[item frame]].
** These include:
*** {{BlockLink|Oak Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Spruce Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Birch Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Jungle Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Acacia Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Dark Oak Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Mangrove Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Bamboo Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Cherry Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Pale Oak Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Crimson Shelf}}
*** {{BlockLink|Warped Shelf}}
** Can be crafted with six matching [[Log|stripped logs]].
*** Can produce 6 of them.
** Can store up to 3 item stacks.
*** Interacting them swaps the [[player]]'s main hand.
*** Items appear to be placed on the shelf, instead of being placed in the middle.
* [[Copper Chest]]
** A new chest variant composed of waxed and oxidized variants.
** Can be crafted with a [[Chest|chest]] surrounded by 8 [[Copper Ingot|copper ingots]].
** Acts as its [[Chest|wooden]] counterpart.
*** Placing it side by side creates a large chest.
** Like normal [[Block of Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper chests undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
*** Unoxidized - Normal stage
*** Exposed
*** Weathered
*** Oxidized
** Only lightning and [[Axe|axes]] can revert the oxidation process.
*** Using a [[Honeycomb|honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper chest prevents the oxidation.
** Can be interacted with by [[Copper Golem|copper golems]].
* [[Copper Chain]]
** A new chain variant composed of waxed and oxidized variants
** Requires copper nuggets and copper ingots to craft them
** Like normal [[Block of Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper chains undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
*** Unoxidized - Normal stage
*** Exposed
*** Weathered
*** Oxidized
** Only lightning and [[Axe|axes]] can revert the oxidation process.
*** Using a [[Honeycomb|honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper chain prevents the oxidation.
* [[Copper Bars]]
** A new [[Iron Bars|bars]] variant composed of waxed and oxidized variants
** Can be crafted with 6 copper ingots.
*** Can produce 16 of them.
** Like normal [[Block of Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper bars undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
*** Unoxidized - Normal stage
*** Exposed
*** Weathered
*** Oxidized
** Only lightning and [[Axe|axes]] can revert the oxidation process.
*** Using a [[Honeycomb|honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper bars prevents the oxidation.
* [[Copper Torch]]
** A new [[torch]] variant.
** Gives off green light.
*** Gives a light level of 14.
** Can be crafted with a copper nugget, [[stick]] and a [[coal]]/[[charcoal]].
*** Can produce 4 copper torches.
** Unlike normal [[Block of Copper|copper blocks]], copper torches do not oxidize since it uses wood sound when placing and/or breaking them.
* [[Copper Lantern]]
** A new [[lantern]] variant composed of waxed and oxidized variants
** Gives off green light
** Can be crafted with a copper torch surrounded by 8 copper nuggets.
** Like normal [[Block of Copper|copper blocks]], unwaxed copper lanterns undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
*** Unoxidized - Normal stage
*** Exposed
*** Weathered
*** Oxidized
** Only lightning and [[Axe|axes]] can revert the oxidation process.
*** Using a [[Honeycomb|honeycomb]] on an unwaxed copper lantern prevents the oxidation.

=== [[Mob]]s ===
* [[Copper Golem]]
** A new buildable passive mob in which it undergoes oxidation, like normal [[Copper|copper blocks]].
** Can be spawned by placing a [[Carved Pumpkin]] or [[Jack o'Lantern]] block on top of a [[Block of Copper|copper block]].
*** Can also be spawned by using its [[Spawn Egg]] or by its {{cmd|summon}} command.
*** Spawns with a [[copper chest]]
** Only interacts with [[chest]]s, [[trapped chest]]s and copper chests.
** Can pick up a maximum of 16 items in a stack at a time.
** Like all copper blocks, unwaxed copper golems undergo 4 stages of oxidation:
*** Unoxidized - Normal stage
*** Exposed
*** Weathered
*** Oxidized - Statue stage
** Only lightning and axes can revert the oxidation process.
*** Using a honeycomb on an unwaxed copper golem prevents the oxidation.
** When a copper golem gets fully oxidized, it will turn into a copper golem statue.
** Drops 1-3 [[Copper Ingot|copper ingots]] on death.
** Prior to this update it was featured in [[Minecraft Live 2021]]'s [[Mob Vote]], but was lost to [[Allay]].
* [[Mannequin]]
** A new [[NPC]] wearing a slim Steve [[skin]].
*** The NPC's skin can be customised via commands; however, the block and mob textures appear broken.
** Does not have its own [[spawn egg]].
** Only summoned via [[commands]].
** Can hold [[items]], [[blocks]]; wear [[armor]] and [[elytra]]; and have [[attributes]] and [[effects]].
** Has 20 hearts of [[health]] similar to the [[player]].

=== [[Language]] ===
* Now supports the Halychian language.

== Changes ==
=== Gameplay ===
* Menu
** Changed the [[panorama]] to show copper golems wandering around a village.

=== Blocks ===
* [[Lightning Rod]]s
** Now undergo 4 stages of oxidation, like all copper blocks.
*** Unoxidized - Normal stage
*** Exposed
*** Weathered
*** Oxidized
** Only lightning and axes can revert the oxidation process.
*** Using a honeycomb on an unwaxed lightning rod prevents the oxidation.
* [[Trapdoor|Copper Trapdoor]]
** Now requires 4 copper ingots to craft, since 6 copper ingots craft copper bars.
* [[Chain]]
** Now renamed as "Iron Chain" to avoid confusion with copper chains.
* [[Signs]] and [[Signs|Hanging Signs]]
** Has a new object text component
*** Can only be accessible by commands

=== Items ===
* [[Dye]]s
** Changed the texture icons of all color dyes to support colorblind accessibility.

=== Mobs ===
* [[Iron Golem]]
** Sometimes plants a [[flower|poppy]] on the copper golem.

=== Dimensions ===
* [[The End]]
** Now has a new skylight called "end flashes".
*** From time to time, bright purple light flashes in the End.
*** Was a feature introduced in [[Bedrock Edition]], during the development of [[Vibrant Visuals]].
