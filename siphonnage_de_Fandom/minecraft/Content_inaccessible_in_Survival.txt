There are many elements in ''[[Minecraft]]'' that are '''completely inaccessible''' to the [[player]] in [[Survival]] mode. This is due to many factors, such as game balance, or due to the fact that the player accessing certain content would not have any logical sense in the context of playing Survival mode, such as [[barrier]] [[block]]s and [[spawn egg]]s.

The article encompasses all things unavailable to the player in Survival. Note that many - if not all - of the listed content can still be obtained in [[Creative]] mode, with the use of [[commands]], or [[mods]].
