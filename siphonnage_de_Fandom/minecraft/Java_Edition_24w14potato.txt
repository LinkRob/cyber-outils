'''24w14potato''' is an [[Easter eggs#April%20Fools|April Fools' joke]] snapshot, supposedly the first and only snapshot for the "'''The Poisonous Potato Update'''", released on April 1, 2024, which introduces the Potato dimension and all related content. This snapshot is a fork of 24w12a.

*Takes players to a nearby Potato Colosseum.

*Is enchanted with Knockback 10 by default.

*Acts like a grappling hook, but with a potato spud instead of a hook.

; Toxic Resin

=== Mobs ===
; Batato
* A potato variant of a Bat
; Poisonous Potato Zombie
* A potato variant of a [[zombie]]
* Attacks and converts other zombies, [[husk]]s, [[drowned]] and [[zombie villager]]s into Poisonous Potato Zombies
* Says "Potato" instead of making normal [[zombie]] sounds
; Toxic Slab
* a Potato variant of the [[Guardian]]
* Has a flat slab shaped body, instead of a cube shaped body
; Plaguewhale Slab
* a Potato variant of the [[Elder Guardian]]
* Has a flat slab shaped body, instead of a cube shaped body
* Does not inflict Mining Fatigue effect, unlike its in-game counterpart.
; Mega Spud
* A mega Potato Boss mob that only spawns once in a Potato Colosseum.
* has 10 phases during boss fight
** After each phase, its body shrinks smaller until its final 10th phase.
; Non-mob entities
* Potato Boats and their chest variant
* 7 potato-themed paintings
