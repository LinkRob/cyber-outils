A '''bogged''' is a variant of a [[skeleton]] found in [[swamp]] biomes. It is an [[undead]] [[hostile mob]] that can shoot [[Arrow|arrows of Poison]].<!-- Missing Assets -->
==Spawning==
Bogged naturally spawn within the [[swamp]] biome, similar to how [[stray]]s spawn in [[Tundra|snowy biomes]]. Bogged may also be spawned from ranged mob [[trial spawner]]s in [[trial chambers]].

==Drops==

Upon death, bogged may drop:

*{{ItemLink|Bone|Bones}}
*{{ItemLink|Arrow|Arrows}}
*{{ItemLink|arrow of Poison|Arrows of Poison}}
*{{ItemLink|Bow|Damaged bows}}.

==Behavior==
Bogged behave very similarly to [[skeleton]]s. They will attack nearby players by shooting [[arrow]]s tipped with [[poison]] at them. Notably, bogged are much slower to reload their bows than normal skeletons, taking 3.5 seconds rather than 2.0, and also have {{Hp|4}} less health.

==Trivia==

*The Bogged was released for Valentine's Day 2024.<ref>https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/my-boggy-1-21-valentine</ref>
*The name "Bogged" is likely a reference to the idiom "''bogged down''", meaning to be stuck or slowed by something difficult, either physically or mentally, as if stuck in a bog (a type of wetland similar to a swamp, but with grass and shrubs and almost no trees growing in waterlogged peat).
*The name and theme of the mob are likely a reference to [[wikipedia:Bog_body|Bog Bodies]], mummified human cadavers found in peat bogs.
