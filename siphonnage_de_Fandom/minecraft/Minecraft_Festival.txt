'''Minecraft Festival''' is an in-person ''Minecraft'' convention similar to [[Minecraft annual events#MINECON|MINECON]] that is planned to be held in Orlando, Florida, on a yet to be announced date. It was originally set to be held from September 25–27, 2020, but was postponed three times due to the {{w|COVID-19 pandemic}}.<ref name=Postponed2021>{{Mcnet|minecraft-festival-postponed|Minecraft Festival postponed|March 5, 2020}}</ref><ref name=Postponed2022>{{Mcnet|announcing-minecraft-live|Announcing Minecraft Live!|September 3, 2020}}</ref><ref name=Postponed>{{tweet|Mojang_Ined|1477932777559498752|There is no Minecraft Festival happening this year|January 3, 2022}}</ref> The online portion of the event is called Minecraft Live!{{sic}}<ref>[https://www.minecraftfestival.com/Features/Minecraft-Live/ Minecraft Live!] — [https://www.minecraftfestival.com/Home minecraftfestival.com]</ref>

== Announcement ==
At [[MINECON Live 2019]] on September 28, 2019, it was announced that Minecraft Festival would be held in Orlando, Florida, through 25–27 September 2020.<ref>{{Mcnet|everything-we-announced-minecon-live-2019|Everything We Announced at MINECON Live 2019}}</ref>

On March 5, 2020, it was announced that the festival has been postponed until late 2021, due to safety concerns about the ongoing {{w|COVID-19 pandemic}}.<ref name=Postponed2021/>

On September 3, 2020, during the announcement of [[Minecraft Live 2020]], it was announced that the festival has been postponed to a yet to be announced date in 2022.<ref name=Postponed2022/>

On January 3, 2022, a Mojang employee stated that Minecraft Festival would not take place in 2022.<ref name=Postponed/>

== Location ==
Minecraft Festival will be held in the South Hall at the Orange County Convention Center, located at 9899 International Drive, Orlando, Florida 32819.<ref name=FAQ>https://web.archive.org/web/20200402134911/https://www.minecraftfestival.com/About/FAQs/</ref>
