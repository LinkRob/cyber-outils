== Trivia ==

* When the <code>item</code> property displays a block, its model appears rotated and its bottom face is missing.
** The only block that is not affected by this is the [[How did we get here? (block)|How did we get here?]] block, but only when the <code>item</code> property is set to 0.
* Colored blocks like the [[grass block]] and [[leaves]] are not colored when displayed by the block.
* Translucent blocks are opaque when displayed by the block.
* Some block entities don't display by the block, such as chests, beds...
* The enchanted glint does not appear on items that have the enchanted glint when displayed by the block.
** Because of that, enchanted golden apple when displayed by the block looks like normal golden apple.
