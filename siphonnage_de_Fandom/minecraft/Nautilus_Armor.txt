The '''nautilus armor''' is an item added in [[Mounts of Mayhem]]. It is an [[armor]] that can be worn on a [[nautilus]] or [[Zombie Nautilus|zombie nautilus]], cannot be crafted and can only be obtained via [[treasure|treasure chests]], [[shipwrecks]] and [[ocean ruins]].

== Obtaining ==
Nautilus Armor can be obtained via looting [[Buried Treasure]] and ocean ruins. They can also be obtained by shearing them off of a [[Jockey|zombie nautilus jockey]], if the zombie nautilus spawns armored.

Copper nautilus armor does not oxidize, unlike most [[copper|copper variants]].

=== Upgrading ===
Like any diamond equipments, a diamond nautilus armor can be upgraded to a netherite nautilus armor via a [[netherite upgrade]].

== Usage ==
Tamed nautili and zombie nautili can wear nautilus armor, but baby nautili cannot be equipped with nautilus armor.

=== Types ===
Nautilus armor has [[iron]], [[gold]], [[copper]], [[diamond]] and [[netherite]] variants. However, nautilus armor does not have a [[chainmail]] variant.

=== Protection ===
Nautilus armor can be equipped to nautiluses by right-clicking the nautilus with it.

There is a gradual increase in the defense given from a nautilus wearing the different types of armor. Note that golden nautilus armor provides more protection than iron nautilus armor, whereas the opposite is true for [[armor|player armor]].

When a nautilus dies while equipped with any nautilus armor tier, it drops its normal loot, saddle (if it had one), and the nautilus armor.

=== Durability ===
Unlike [[armor|player armor]], nautilus armor does not have durability. This means a single nautilus armor can be used infinitely unless destroyed.

=== Flammability ===
Unlike all nautilus armor tiers, netherite nautilus armor is inflammable. So it floats in lava.

=== Piglins ===
{{EntityLink|Piglin|Piglins}} are attracted to golden nautilus armor and run toward any golden nautilus armor on the ground, inspecting it for 6 to 8 seconds before putting it in their inventory.
