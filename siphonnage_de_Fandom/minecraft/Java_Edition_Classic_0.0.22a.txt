'''0.0.22a'''<ref>[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''P5:21:52 <Rroff> before on just plain 22a sound was fine for me''". (22:21:52 UTC)</ref> was a version of [[Java Edition Classic]] released on June 29, 2009, at 21:56 UTC.<ref>[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''P4:56:47 <akuryo> notch you did an update?''", "''P4:56:55 <AlLnAtuRalX> Notch, server update?''". (21:56 UTC)</ref> This version is not available in the [[launcher]].

0.0.22a was reuploaded at 21:58 UTC to fix players not being able to connect to servers.<ref>[[#logs|IRC logs]]: "''P4:58:33 <08Notch> there we go, the client should work again''". (21:58:33 UTC)</ref>

** No sounds were used for [[water]], [[lava]], [[dandelion]], [[rose]], [[brown mushroom]], [[red mushroom]].
