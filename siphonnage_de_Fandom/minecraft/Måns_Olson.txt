'''Måns Olson''' is a game developer at [[Mojang Studios]], currently working on the launcher for [[Minecraft]] and developing [[Minecraft Dungeons]].<ref>https://twitter.com/mansolson/status/654680140614492163</ref> Previously, he worked as the lead designer for [[Scrolls|Caller’s Bane]].<ref>http://scrollsdev.tumblr.com/post/75801791192/team-changes</ref>

== Biography ==
Olson is a graduate of [[wikipedia:Royal Institute of Technology|Royal Institute of Technology]] in computer science. He previously worked at Fabrication Games from January 2010 to March 2012 before being hired by Mojang.<ref name=linkedin>http://se.linkedin.com/pub/m%C3%A5ns-olson/10/1a5/b47</ref>
