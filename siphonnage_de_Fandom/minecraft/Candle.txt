A '''candle''' is a [[dye]]able [[block]] that emits [[light]] when lit with a [[flint and steel]]. Up to four of the same color of candle can be placed in one block space, which affects the amount of light produced.

== Obtaining ==
=== Natural generation ===
Non-dyed candles and white candles can be found in [[ancient cities]]. Brown, green, purple, and red candles can be found in [[trail ruins]].

=== Breaking ===
Candles drop themselves as items when mined using any [[tool]], or without a tool.

==Usage==
===Light===
[[File:Four Candles (lit).gif|right|150px]]
Candles can be used as a light source, emitting light like a [[sea pickle]]. A single candle emits a light level of 3. Up to four identically colored candles may be placed in one block, with each candle increasing the emitted light level by 3, for a maximum light level of 12. They also emit fire particles. 

Candles are not lit when they are placed, and must be lit using [[flint and steel]], [[fire charge]], or any flaming projectile. {{IN|BE}}, a mob/player that is burning, a [[Fire Aspect]] book or any [[sword]] enchanted with Fire Aspect can also be used to light candles. Candles may be [[waterlogging|waterlogged]], but waterlogged candles cannot be lit. Lit candles can be extinguished using [[water]] (including using a water bottle{{only|java}}) or by the player {{ctrl|interacting}} with the candle.

{{-}}

===Cakes===
[[File:Cake with Candle.png|right|150px]]
A single candle of any color may be {{control|used}} on an uneaten [[cake]], turning it into a candle cake, which acts as a normal single candle. Only one candle can be placed on a cake. If the candle cake is eaten or destroyed, the candle is dropped.

{{-}}

==Trivia==
*Using the command <code>/[[setblock]] ~ ~ ~ minecraft:candle[lit=true,waterlogged=true]</code> allows for a both lit and waterlogged candle. The same thing can be done using the [[debug stick]].
*A single candle has the same width as [[iron bar]] or [[glass pane]].
