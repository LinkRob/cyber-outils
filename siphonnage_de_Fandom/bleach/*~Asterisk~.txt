'''''*~Asterisk~''''' is ORANGE RANGE's tenth single. The title track was used as the first opening theme song for the anime [[Bleach]], and it was featured from [[Episode 1]] through [[Episode 25|25]]. It was also used as the theme for ''[[Bleach: Heat the Soul]]''.
