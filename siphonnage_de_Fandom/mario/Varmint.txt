}}The '''Varmint''' is an unlockable bike that appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.

It is an all-terrain motorcycle and is a bike version of the [[Bolt Buggy]] and the [[Pipe Frame]]. It shares its stats with the [[Pipe Frame]], Wild Wiggler, [[Standard Bike]], [[W 25 Silver Arrow]], and City Tripper. It offers good outside drifting, acceleration, weight, and speed.

The Varmint's stats are altered in ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', now sharing them with the City Tripper, and Pipe Frame.
