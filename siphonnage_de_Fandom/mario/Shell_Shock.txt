==Effects==
To use Shell Shock, the player must play a small game in order to help it grow in size. The player can then kick the shell into other enemies dealing damage. However, they must be careful as it can also hurt the user. The Shell Shock can also be given to [[Saffron]] to cook into a Courage Shell.
