A '''Tail Bullet Bill''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It is a variant of [[Bullet Bill]] with a tail attached to it.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
A Tail Bullet Bill has the appearance of a regular Bullet Bill but with a tanooki tail attached to it.

=== Behavior ===
Tail Bullet Bills are fired from [[Bill Blaster]]s, like with most Bullet Bills, and continue in the direction in which they were launched. After traveling a certain distance, a Tail Bullet Bill spins around using its tail. Defeating a Tail Bullet Bill sometimes yields a [[Super Leaf]], or a [[Statue Leaf]] in [[Special World]]s.
