'''Spinny Flowers''' are an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Description==
Spinny Flowers are multicolored flowers found in certain parts of the overworld, such as in [[Sunbeam Plains]] and [[Doop Doop Dunes]]. A Spinny Flower is closed at first, but if the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] [[dash]] into it using the [[Dash Socks]], the Spinny Flower opens to reveal a [[Coin]]. Even after a Spinny Flower has bloomed, it can still be dashed into, although Coins do not appear from it.
