'''Moe-Eye''' (ミルゾウ) is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

== Appearance ==
Their only known appearance is in the [[Sand Kingdom]] in the [[Moe-Eye Habitat]]. They will not attack [[Mario]]. However, they try to run away from him.

When Mario uses [[Cappy]] to capture a Moe-Eye, he can put on its shades to see invisible paths and other invisible objects in the level. However, doing this will make Mario move much slower.

==Trivia==
*The name "Moe-Eye" is a playword of "[[wikipedia:Moai|Moai]]", the [[wikipedia:Easter Island|Easter Island]] rock sculptures in forms of heads from which this enemy is based from.
*This enemy marks the third appearance of a "Moai" in a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game, after [[Tokotoko]] and [[Hiyoihoi]].
