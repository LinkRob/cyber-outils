'''Katsumi Suzuki''' (鈴木 勝美, born August 5, 1956) is a Japanese voice actor known for voicing [[Diddy Kong]] in the [[Donkey Kong (series)|''Donkey Kong'']] and [[Mario (series)|''Mario'']] franchises. Suzuki took the role in 2004, starting with ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', and replacing [[Chris Sutherland]], who portrayed him in the [[Rareware]] era (although Sutherland's voice clips were used alongside Suzuki's in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]'').

He was formerly an employee at Arts Vision along with [[Takashi Nagasako]] and [[Toshihide Tsuchiya]], but he moved to 81 Produce.
