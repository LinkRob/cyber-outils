The '''Scrapbook''', (called the Sticker Book in the [[Game Boy Color]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''), is, as the name states, a scrapbook holding collected photos obtained throughout the game. It appears in the two remakes of ''Donkey Kong Country'' and the [[Game Boy Advance]] version for ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''.

==Role==
===''Donkey Kong Country'' (Game Boy Color)===
{{Main|Sticker Book}}
[[File:Jungle Hijinxs - Sticker - Donkey Kong Country (Colour).png|thumb|left|[[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] finds a sticker in [[Jungle Hijinxs]].]]
Here, it is named [[Sticker Book]], in this game is different from how it functions in the [[Game Boy Advance]] remakes of ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''. All the photos taken are in grayscale colors and there are miscellaneous photos such as the background photo of the title screen of the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version of ''Donkey Kong Country''. There is also an alphabet album that is part of the scrapbook with all of the numbers and letters included. The Scrapbook doesn't need to be obtained. It comes with the start of a new file. Also, only the stickers appear in adventure mode. Not the scrapbook itself.

===''Donkey Kong Country'' (Game Boy Advance)===
Unlike the [[Game Boy Color]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', this version has the scrapbook function in a different way of the sort. In this game, the scrapbook comes with the game like last time. This time, the images are in full color. The background of it is now jungle-themed.

===''Donkey Kong Country 2'' (Game Boy Advance)===
The Scrapbook acts mainly the same in the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' than it does in ''Donkey Kong Country'' for the Game Boy Advance. Except, it is redone with scroll-like papers and such. It can be obtained at [[Kong Kollege]]. Also, the first to seventh pages have a heart with an arrow in it with the text [[Dixie Kong|DIXIE]].
