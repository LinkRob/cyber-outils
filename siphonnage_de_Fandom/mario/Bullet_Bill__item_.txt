'''Bullet Bill''' is an item featured in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart DS]].'' 

== Description ==
This item can be obtained if the player is at the lower ranks in the race. Upon using the item, the racer temporarily turns into a large [[Bullet Bill]], which moves automatically at a high speed and can help them catch up to nearby racers. Any racers hit by the Bullet Bill (aside from those under the [[Starman]]'s effect) are knocked out briefly before they can continue the race.
