"'''A Toadally Magical Adventure'''" is the thirteenth episode of ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It is centered around [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]]. In syndication, the episode aired alongside "[[Misadventures in Babysitting]]."

== Synposis ==
Toad loses a magic wand to the Koopa Troop.

==Trivia==
*The word "Toadally" in the episode title is a portmanteau of "Toad" and "totally".
*When [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] sees [[Fire Mario]] shooting fireballs towards him & the [[Koopalings]], he shouts out "[[Great Balls of Fire]]" in panic. This could possibly be a callback to ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Fire of Hercufleas]]".
*[[Lemmy Koopa|Hip]] & [[Iggy Koopa|Hop Koopa]] were the only Koopalings to not appear in this episode.
