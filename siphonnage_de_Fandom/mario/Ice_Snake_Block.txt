An '''Ice Snake Block''' is an object in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Description==
Ice Snake Blocks are a variation of [[Snake Block]] made up of [[Ice Block]] segments. Aside from having a slippery surface, Ice Snake Blocks function the same as Snake Blocks, and are used to cross pits. If a character is in their [[Penguin Mario|Penguin]] form, they can move freely on the platform without slipping.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''==
Ice Snake Blocks appear in {{world-link|3|castle|World 3-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)}}. There are only two Ice Snake Blocks: the first one leads to the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] and the second one to [[Lemmy Koopa]].
