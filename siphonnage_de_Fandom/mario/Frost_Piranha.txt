'''Frost Piranhas''' are a recurring enemy in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Frost Piranhas are a sky-blue, ice-elemental variant of [[Piranha Plant]]s, and they are larger than regular Piranha Plants.

===Behavior===
In ''Paper Mario'', Frost Piranhas have the ability to breathe clouds or blasts of ice capable of [[Frozen|freezing]] the target. They can burrow underground and pop up to attack.

In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', a Frost Piranha does not breathe ice, but its bite can potentially freeze Mario or his partner unless they use a [[Guard]] move. Like their Paper Mario counterparts, they can burrow underground and pop up to attack. Unlike in the previous game, they cannot be jumped on in battle.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
Mario first encounters Frost Piranhas on the way to [[Starborn Valley]]. Along with being exceptionally strong, they had the ability to shoot icy blasts of breath that can freeze Mario and his allies (although the blasts themselves won't hurt them). Frost Piranhas were later encountered on Shiver Mountain where they were less abundant.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
Frost Piranhas are encountered in the [[Fahr Outpost]]. Unlike with ''Paper Mario'', a Frost Piranha cannot be [[jump]]ed onto to attack it.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
Frost Piranhas only appear in [[The Underwhere]], where they appear from [[Warp Pipe]]s and retain their ice-breath attack from ''Paper Mario''. A Frost Piranha can be mainly defeated from using [[Luigi]]'s [[Super Jump]] on it from an angle. A Frost Piranha has a chance of dropping an [[Ice Storm]] upon being defeated.
