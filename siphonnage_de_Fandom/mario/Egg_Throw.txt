'''Egg Throw''' is a recurring move usable by [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', and is commonplace throughout the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platformers and {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.

==Description==
Egg Throw consistently involves a Yoshi throwing a [[Yoshi Egg]], but the execution of the move differs between series.

In the ''Yoshi'' platforming titles, the player must hold or press the aiming button for an X-shaped cursor to display. This cursor indicates which direction the Yoshi Egg will launch, once the player releases the aiming button. Upon being thrown, a Yoshi Egg continues in the direction it was thrown in until hitting a wall, ceiling, or the ground, upon which it bounces. When the Yoshi Egg hits an enemy or bounces too many times, it breaks and disappears. At first, a Yoshi Egg used in Egg Throw has green spots, but as it bounces multiple times, the egg changes to [[Yellow Egg|yellow]] then to [[Red Egg|red]], after which it breaks. A majority of ''Yoshi'' bosses can be attacked using Egg Throw, and become defeated after a number of hits.

==In ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''==
The first level, [[Make Eggs, Throw Eggs]], is named for the Egg Throw ability. The second [[Message Block]] in the level gives instructions on how to have a Yoshi use Egg Throw.

==In ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''==
[[File:Yoshi Artwork - Super Mario 64 DS.png|thumb|200px|''Super Mario 64 DS'' artwork of Yoshi about to use Egg Throw]]
Egg Throw is one of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s moves and the only {{series|Super Mario}} installment in which he can use the move.

Unlike the ''Yoshi'' platformers, Egg Throw now automatically hits the nearest enemy, so it no longer has to be aimed. If there are no nearby enemies, the Yoshi Egg is thrown forward. Egg Throw can chain to a few enemies, and doing so causes each enemy to lose two [[Coin]]s instead of one. The move is optional, but can sometimes be beneficial, such as in [[Behind Chain Chomp's Gate|Behind Chain-Chomp's Gate]], in which using Egg Throw on a [[Chain Chomp]] stuns it for a few seconds.

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''==
Egg Throw is an attack used by [[Sunnycide]] during its boss fight, and this is the first time Egg Throw is used by a character other than the Yoshis. Sunnycide's Egg Throw is different because it involves throwing [[Yoob]] eggs containing trapped Yoshis at all four of the [[Mario]] brothers.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
Egg Throw is featured as Yoshi's up special in every ''Super Smash Bros.'' game, starting with the first ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' for [[Nintendo 64]].
