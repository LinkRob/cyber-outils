'''Superball Mario''' is a form of [[Mario]] that only appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. Mario can transform into Superball Mario by collecting a [[Superball Flower]] found in levels.

==Powers and Abilities==
Superball Mario can toss [[Superball]]s at foes defeating them. Superball Mario works similarly to [[Fire Mario]], except Superball Mario's Superballs are shot forward and ricochet off of angles. Superballs are even able to collect coins. Superball Mario is useful in dealing with certain bosses in the game.
