'''Elite Trio X''' is a group of three members that are the stronger versions of the [[Elite Trio]]. The members of the group are stronger clones of [[Private Goomp]], [[Sergeant Guy]] and [[Corporal Paraplonk]]. They appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
As clones of the [[Elite Trio]], they bear the same appearance.
