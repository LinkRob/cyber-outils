}}A '''Dark Growmeba''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Growmebas are darker versions of [[Growmeba]]s that can be found in rooms 35 and 37 of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Dark Growmebas, much like Growmebas, mainly float in the air duplicating themselves. However, trying to figure out the real one from the duplicates may prove difficult due to them all looking alike.
