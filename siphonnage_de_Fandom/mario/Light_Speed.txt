'''Light Speed''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each character is riding a hover-car, and they must use it to drive over tiles to change them to their corresponding color. There are also warp zones that assist the characters in going across the areas easily. Whoever has more tiles of their color when the 30-second [[time limit]] runs out is the winner. If the minigame ends with both characters having the same number of tiles corresponding to their color, it ends in a [[Draw|tie]].

==Ending==
The winner stands in the middle of the tile floor, outside their hover-car, and does their victory animation as the tiles repeatedly change color by row. If the minigame ends in a tie, both characters remain on their respective tiles as they spin in circles in their hover-cars.
