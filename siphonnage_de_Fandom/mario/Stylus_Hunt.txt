'''Stylus Hunt''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'' as '''Pushing Buttons''', and it is part of [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s [[Retro Action]] stage. Stylus Hunt makes its second appearance under its current name in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', as one of 9-Volt's microgames.

==Gameplay==
Both iterations of the microgame are double length, so the player has eight seconds to meet the objective. If the objective is not met on time, the player loses.

In the original version, Pushing Buttons, the player has press a [[Game Boy Advance]], [[Game Boy Advance SP]], or [[Nintendo DS]] button indicated with a red circle. If done successfully, they win, and [[Wario]] appears on the Game Boy Advance screen. Wario's appearance as he flies towards the screen resembles his cover artwork from ''[[Wario Land 3]]'' (except with his hand making the "V" sign instead of three fingers), while his appearance when he hits the screen resembles his [[Bubble Wario]] artwork from ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

In Stylus Hunt, the object is instead to locate a [[stylus]] within a [[Nintendo 3DS]].

===Difficulty levels===
====''WarioWare: Touched!''====
*'''Level 1:''' The button is in plain sight.
*'''Level 2:''' The button is more hidden.
*'''Level 3:''' The button is well hidden.

====''WarioWare Gold''====
*'''Level 1:''' The microgame starts with the front of the system shown.
*'''Level 2:''' The microgame starts with the back of the system shown.
*'''Level 3:''' The microgame starts with a random angle of the system shown.
