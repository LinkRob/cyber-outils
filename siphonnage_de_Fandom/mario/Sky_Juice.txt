'''Sky Juice''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Saffron]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Effects==

It is made from a [[Red Apple]], [[Blue Apple]], [[Yellow Apple]], or [[Pink Apple]]. This juice restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]. It is a good idea to bring all of the Apples to Saffron once they are picked up. It will increase the total HP recovered by 10 since each Apple restores only 5 HP.
