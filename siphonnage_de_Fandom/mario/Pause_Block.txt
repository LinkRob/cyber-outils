'''Pause Blocks''' halt the running of the playable characters in ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' when they step onto one.

== Description ==
If the player stands on the Pause Block, they will stop and the timer will pause. Tapping the screen will make the character [[roll]] and then continue running. If the block is facing diagonally upwards, the player will jump off the block into that direction.
