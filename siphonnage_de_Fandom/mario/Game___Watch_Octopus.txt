'''Game & Watch Octopus''' is one of [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. It takes place within the [[Game & Watch]] game ''[[Octopus (game)|Octopus]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must pick up the [[Wii Remote]] when the diver is next to the treasure to snatch it from the [[Octopus (character)|Octopus]]. If the diver gets snatched by the Octopus, the player loses the minigame. Each difficulty level puts the diver farther from the treasure, and in Level 1, the diver is right next to the treasure.
