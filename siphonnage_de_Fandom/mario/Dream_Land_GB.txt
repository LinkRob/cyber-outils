'''Dream Land GB''' (also referred to as '''Dream Land''') is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. 

==Stage design==
{{sectstub}}

===Features===
After reaching a certain point in each area, the stage would teleports the fighters to a different part of Dream Land.
{{sectstub}}

==Origin==
Dream Land GB is based on the five different areas in ''[[w:c:kirby:Kirby's Dream Land|Kirby's Dream Land]]''.
