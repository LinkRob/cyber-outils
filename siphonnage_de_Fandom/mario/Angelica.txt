'''Angelica''' is an old woman who makes a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[Time Out Luigi]]."


== Biography ==
She works as a salesperson for Weird and Spooky Gadgets Incorporated, and she visits [[Mario Brothers Plumbing]] to try and sell some of her items to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].
