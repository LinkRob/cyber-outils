'''''Super Mario Bros.'' Watches''' are a line of LCD game watches produced by McDonald's in 1990. Three games were released within the line of ''Super Mario Bros.'' Watches: ''[[Mario's Egg Catch]]'', ''[[Luigi's Hammer Toss]]'', and ''[[Princess Toadstool's Castle Run]]'', starring [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] respectively.
