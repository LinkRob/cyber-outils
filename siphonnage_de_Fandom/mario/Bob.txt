'''Bob''' is one of the [[human]] tennis players featured in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]]. He is partnered with [[Curt]] in doubles. Bob is also an unlockable playable character, being of the Power type, and he can be unlocked by completing Singles in Junior Champ.
