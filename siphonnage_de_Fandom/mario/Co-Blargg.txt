'''Co-Blarggs''' (also called '''Red Blarggs''', '''Mini Blarggs''', or simply '''Blarggs''') are a recurring enemy in the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Profile==
A Co-Blargg is a smaller variant of [[Blargg]] that attacks by poking its head out of the [[lava]] and moving slightly toward the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]].

==In {{series|Yoshi's Island}}==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island/Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''===
[[Image:Co-Blargg Encounter.png|thumb|left|200px|A Co-Blargg emerging from lava in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'']]
Co-Blarggs are first seen in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and later return in its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They are commonly found in [[lava]] pits inside several of the [[fortress|forts]], one notable example being [[Burt the Bashful's Fort]]. They are seen resting in the [[lava]] but as soon as [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] approaches, they poke their heads out and attempt to bite. They deal damage upon contact and cannot be defeated however, Yoshi can send one back in the lava by tossing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]], they are commonly seen early in the game and appear less towards the end in favor of the much larger [[Gargantua Blargg]].
{{br|left}}
===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
[[Image:Co-Blargg DS.png|thumb|200px|A Co-Blargg in ''Yoshi's Island DS''.]]
Co-Blarggs return in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' retaining their behavior from before, they are once again seen dwelling in [[lava]] pits and poke their heads out as [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] approaches however, they the lava pits they dwell in are mostly much lower than the platforms so avoiding them shouldn't be much of an issue. Yoshi cannot destroy a Co-Blargg but he can toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at one to send it back in the lava, through this, they can be made to appear in the [[Museum]], here, they can be found in the Subterranean Enemy Exhibit. Co-Blarggs are mainly found in earlier levels of the game and not as much towards the end. Unlike the other Blarggs in this game, Co-Blarggs look almost identical to how they did in the original game.

===''Yoshi's New Island''===
[[Image:Red Blargg Dodge.png|thumb|left|Yoshi jumps over a Co-Blargg.]]
Co-Blarggs return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' retaining their behavior from before however this time, they are somewhat bigger than they were previously. Once again, they attack by poking their heads out of the [[lava]] to attack and can be retreated by tossing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at them. They are also commonly found mainly in the earlier parts of the game.
