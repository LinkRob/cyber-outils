The '''First-Degree Card''' is an key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The First-Degree Card is obtained once after [[Mario]] defeats [[Chan]] at [[The Dojo]]. The First-Degree Card serves no gameplay purpose, and it simply acknowledges that Mario won against Chan.
