The '''Prison Key''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Prison Keys can be obtained at [[Bowser's Castle]] after defeating [[Koopatrol]]s that possess them. The Prison Keys can then be used to unlock certain doors to move on in through the castle.

==Trivia==
*The Prison Key uses a similar sprite to various other [[key]]s, including [[Odd Key]], [[Storeroom Key]], and [[Warehouse Key]].
