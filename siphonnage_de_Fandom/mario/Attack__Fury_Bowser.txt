'''Attack! Fury Bowser''', also known as '''Bowser's Fury Theme''' is a song first featured in the [[Bowser's Fury|adventure itself]], and ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. It is mostly the theme of [[Fury Bowser]].

==Uses==
===Bowser's Fury===
The song is used in three different phases when a rampaging Fury Bowser attacks [[Lake Lapcat]], as well as [[Mario]] and [[Bowser Jr.]], until he retreats. An arrangment was heard in the credits after the adventure.

===The Super Mario Bros. Movie===
The song is used in a dance party at Bowser's ship-like Castle, while [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] reveals his plans to invade the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], after obtaining the Super Star from the Snow Kingdom.
