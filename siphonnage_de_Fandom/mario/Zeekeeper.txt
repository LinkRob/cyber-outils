A '''Zeekeeper''' is a bird-like creature which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They are actually someone inside a full-body suit. The Zeekeeper is a big rainbow colored bird that saves the [[Pi'illo]] every time [[Antasma]] attacked. Zeekeepers are seen in a lot of places on [[Pi'illo Island]]. Their name is based on the word "zookeeper."

Zeekeepers can primarily be found in populated areas on Pi'illo Island such as on the [[Pi'illo Blimport]], inside [[Pi'illo Castle]], or around [[Mushrise Park]], [[Wakeport]], and [[Driftwood Shore]]. Zeekeepers often have vast personalities such as acting as staff and appearing among crowds on the island.


==Appearance==
Zeekeepers vary in color from green, yellow, pink, or purple. They also appear to wear shoes.

==Trivia==
*Near the beginning of the game, there is a Zeekeeper in the crowd who, when [[Mario]] talks to him, will say "I HAVE EXCITEMENT!" This quote is an obvious nod to [[Fawful]], the villain from [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|the previous game]] whether or not this Zeekeeper is Fawful or not is unknown and also quite unlikely too. This Zeekeeper is never seen again once the prologue is finished.
