'''Runaway Pay''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Runaway Pay has a [[Badge Point|BP]] cost of 2. So long as [[Mario]] has Runaway Pay equipped, he still gets the [[Star Point]]s that he would if he defeats enemies in a battle and runs away. However, Mario only gets the points in the bottom-right corner, not those from enemies he has not defeated in battle. Runaway Pay does not tamper with the runaway meter.

Runaway Pay is found in [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]]. In the desert, two sectors northwest of [[Dry Dry Outpost]], are three trees in a triangular formation, and an invisible [[Question Block]] containing Runaway Pay is in the middle of these trees.
