'''Team Sonic''' is a group of ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters in the {{series|Mario & Sonic}}. They compete in the [[Olympic Games]] alongside [[Team Sonic]].

==Trivia==

*[[Nabbit]], [[Dry Bones]] and [[Dry Bowser]] mark the only Team Mario characters to not appear in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''.
*[[Toad (character)|Toad]] and [[Birdo]] are removed from Team Mario. Instead, they appear in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]'' as non-playable characters.
**Birdo does not make a physical appearance, but the red and blue [[Birdo (species)|Birdos]] return as goalies in Football.
