'''4-Player Spaces''' are an object in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
A 4-Player Space is a type of [[space]] which resembles a [[Blue Space]], but with "VS 4" printed on it. The text is dark purple with a white outline. 

==In ''Mario Party 6''==
4-Player Spaces exclusively appear on the boards in Solo Mode. Whenever the player's character ends their turn on a 4-Player Space, they have to play a 4-Player mini-game with the [[Koopa Kid]]s.
