A '''Danger Space''' is a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
A Danger Space is ''Mario Party: Island Tour''{{'}}s equivalent to the [[Red Space]] in other ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' games. Danger Spaces are identical in appearance to Red Spaces, but are functionally very different. Danger Spaces only appear in [[Banzai Bill's Mad Mountain]], and while being on a Danger Space does not yield any immediate effect, any character on a Danger Space on either half of the board are open to being hit by a [[Banzai Bill]] whenever it launches, sending them back to the start or halfway point, for the first and second halves of the board, respectively.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
There are a total of twelve Danger Spaces on Banzai Bill's Mad Mountain, most of which are below a [[Safe Space]]. If a character moves onto a Danger Space next to a Safe Space and has at least one move remaining, they have a choice to either continue forward to the next space or hide in the Safe Space until their next turn.
