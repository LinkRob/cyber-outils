'''Three Door Monty''' is a battle mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this game, a [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]], [[Toad]], and a [[Boo]] will all enter doors labeled Z, B, and A respectively. After all three characters have hidden in a room, the game will then show a picture of the character. The players must try to answer the question as quickly as possible, and correctly. The person who answers the quickest will win the battle Mini-Game.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|a}} - Selects the A Door.
*{{button|n64|b}} - Selects the B Door.
*{{button|n64|z}} - Selects the Z Door.

==Trivia==
*The name of this mini-game is a play on the con card game Three-card Monte.
