'''Honey Shrooms''' are items featured in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It appears to be a red-capped mushroom with yellow honey-glazed on the top. It is made by mixing a [[Mushroom]] or a [[Volt Shroom]] with a [[Honey Syrup]]. In its first two appearances, the Honey Shroom restores 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 5 [[Flower Point|FP]]. In all of its appearances, a Honey Shroom can be sold for 10 [[coin]]s.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:Honey Shroom.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', this item can be acquired by asking [[Tayce T.]]. Honey Shrooms cannot be obtained until after Chapter 4, as [[Mario]] needs to have first obtained the [[Cookbook]]. Tayce T. can create a Honey Shroom by way of cooking a [[Mushroom (Paper Mario series)|Mushroom]] and a [[Honey Syrup]].

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
[[File:HoneyShroomTTYD.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Honey Shroom reappears, albeit with no new characteristics. Like in ''Paper Mario'', the Cookbook is featured in chapter 4, so Mario cannot get this item for some time.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Honey Shroom has a slightly different effect. Mario can obtain a Honey Shroom by giving a [[Shroom Shake]] and a [[Honey Jar]] to [[Saffron]]. Unlike the previous two games, a Honey Shroom instead restores 10 HP.
