'''Blubble Lake''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. It is first explored by [[Bowser]], then later by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. The boss fight against the [[Tower of Yikk]] takes place here.
