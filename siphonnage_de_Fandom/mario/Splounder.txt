'''Splounders''' are enemies which first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. They are fish-like enemies that appear in [[Plessie]] stages. They either remain stationary or move from side to side. If bounced on, Splounders will allow Plessie to jump higher and sometimes get on top of areas or grab items that would otherwise be inaccessible. If Plessie bumps into a Splounder, it will become stunned and bounce Plessie to the side.
