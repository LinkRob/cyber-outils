The '''Quirks''' are a species in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Stars in Their Eyes]]."

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Quirks are humanoid aliens with light-green skin and two trumpet-like noses protruding from their face.

===Habitats===
Quirks are the namesake inhabitants of the planet [[Quirk (planet)|Quirk]].

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
==="Stars in Their Eyes"===
The Quirks have been enslaved by [[Moon Man Koopa]], [[Astro Mouser]], and their [[Koopa Troopa]] minions, who force them to mine the rocks of Quirk and transport them to Moon Man Koopa's [[Quirk Factory]].
