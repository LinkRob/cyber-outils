The '''Warship''' is a kart that first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
It is a variation of the [[Landship]], with dark, dull colors, almost resembling a shipwreck. It is likely intended to be a "spookified" variation of the Landship, as [[King Boo]] and his variants are often seen using the Warship in promotional screenshots. It uses a variation of the [[Wood]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Warship was first made available in the Daily Selects during the [[Halloween Tour (2019)]]. When the kart is used, it increases the duration of [[Mini-Turbo]] boosts and the points earned for them. It ic classified as a Normal kart.
