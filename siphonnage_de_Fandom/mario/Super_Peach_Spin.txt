'''Super Peach Spin''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] of [[Princess Peach]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Peach spins around and hits a curve ball imbued with some heart-like power. Using the move knocks the opponent back a bit before attracting them back to the net, in front of Peach, as hearts trail behind the opponent briefly. With the opponent distracted, Peach has an advantage to score and have them miss a return.
