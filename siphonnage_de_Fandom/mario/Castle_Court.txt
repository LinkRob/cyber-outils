'''Castle Court''' is an unlockable tennis court which appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]].

==Description==
[[File:Luigi vs Baby Mario MT GBC.png|thumb|left|Luigi serving the tennis ball to Baby Mario]]
Castle Court takes place outside of [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. The court is pink with an orange winged heart symbol on each side. It does not feature any gimmicks or hazards.

==In ''Mario Tennis'' (Game Boy Color)==
After completing the [[Island Open]] in singles or doubles, [[Alex]] and [[Nina]] can face [[Mario]] and [[Princess Peach|Peach]] here. The Castle Court can be unlocked by getting over 60 points in the minigame [[Shooting Stars (Mario Tennis)|Shooting Stars]]. The minigame [[Target Shot]] takes place in the Castle Court.
