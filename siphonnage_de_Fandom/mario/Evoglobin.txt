The '''Evoglobin''' is the evolution of the [[Emoglobin]] and a type of creature found in [[Bowser's body]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. These live in [[Rump Command]] and ask the player to board the boats of revival so Bowser can move again and become giant when he gets knocked out. Before Mario and Luigi board the boats the Evoglobin have arms and legs.
