The '''Ding-A-Lings''' are supporting bosses in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are yellow bell-like creatures that appear to be controlling the larger boss [[Count Down]], and they all serve under [[Smithy]] in his [[Smithy Gang|gang]]. The Ding-A-Lings and Count Down are fought together in [[The Factory]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
===Battle===
In battle, the Ding-A-Lings can attack physically (unlike Count Down) but are capable of using strong magic moves such as [[Dark Star (move)|Dark Star]]. They can even use a unique move called [[Spore Chimes]] to turn one of the party members into a [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]].

====Strategy====
Fortunately, like Count Down, the Ding-A-Lings are weak against [[Ice (element)|Ice]] so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] move will come in handy. [[Mario]] can also attack the group with his [[Ultra Jump]] while [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] attacks and heals.
