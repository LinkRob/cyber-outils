'''Multibounce''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. Wearing it allows [[Mario]] to use his [[Boots|jump]] attack on all enemies. Mario can wear Multibounce by paying one [[Badge Point|BP]], but using the attack requires two [[Flower Point|FP]] to be used.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Multibounce_badge.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Multibounce can be bought from [[Rowf's Badge Shop]] for 75 [[coin]]s.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Multibounce is found in a red [[Question Block]] within [[Shhwonk Fortress]].
