'''Mirage Mansion''' is a court set within a haunted mansion. It appears only in ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]''.

== Adventure Mode ==
In [[Adventure Mode]], [[Mario]] and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] visit the third area for the second [[Power Stone]]. The [[Boo]] challenges Mario  in a match. After completing the mission, Boo tells them that there is "true horror" waiting up ahead. Mario must complete the mirror puzzle when he reaches the Reflection Room within the time limit. He then has to face the boss and mansion ruler, [[Madame Mirage]]. There is an optional mission to complete from Boo which he needs to knock down the panels with the tennis ball. Then Boo provides Mario a hint for the puzzle in the Reflection Room. Another optional mission which Mario must return the ball to Boo until he reaches 300 [[point]]s, and he will be rewarded the [[Mirror Racket]].
