'''Music Bashes''' are a species in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. Prior to ''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit]]'', they were named '''Bouncing Notes'''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Music Bashes have the appearance of large, black eighth notes. Their facial features consist of entirely white eyes, an orange, button nose, and a white mouth.

===Behavior===
Music Bashes bounce in tune with the melody of the course's theme. Upon landing, a Music Bash creates waves that lift all racers on their section up a little. Three metronome-like ticks play before Music Bashes can be heard before they land. Bouncing Notes bounce faster during the final lap.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 7]]''===
Bouncing Notes are encountered toward the end of [[Music Park]]. A racer can perform a [[trick]] whenever a Bouncing Note creates waves upon landing. If a Bouncing Note lands on a racer, they are flattened.

===''[[Mario Kart 8]]''/''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
Bouncing Notes retain their role from ''Mario Kart 7''. Aside from them, smaller Bouncing Notes appear near the finish line as spectators of the race.

===''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit]]''===
Music Bashes are encountered in [[Music Broadway]], where they bounce back and forth on the xylophone keys.
