The '''Workshop''' is a room located in [[Old Clockworks]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It has doors leading to the [[Warehouse]] and [[Clock Tower Gate]].

== History ==
After entering the far left door in the Warehouse, [[Luigi]] enters the Workshop, a smaller room similar in materials to the Warehouse. Upon entering it for the first time in mission C-1: [[A Timely Entrance]], Luigi witnesses two [[Greenie]]s with welding masks and hammers working on a [[Giant Robotic Bomb]]. When the bomb is flashed with the [[strobulb]], it explodes; stunning the Greenies and allowing Luigi to capture them with ease. There are no [[Gem]]s or [[Boo]]s in the Workshop. No other [[Ghosts]] appear in the Workshop with the exception of a Greenie and [[Polterpup]] in mission C-4: [[Play Catch]].
