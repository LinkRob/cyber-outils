The '''Special Dice Block Space''' is a recurring [[space]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', in which it is named the '''Dice Block Space''', then in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'' under its current name.

==Description==
Special Dice Block Spaces usually have an identical appearance to a regular [[Blue Space]]. When a Captain lands onto one, they receive either a [[0-1 Dice Block]], a [[1-2-3 Dice Block]], a [[4-5-6 Dice Block]], a [[1-10 Dice Block]], or a [[Slow Dice Block]] from a small, red box. At times, the box also contains a few [[Mini Star]]s or initiates a Free-for-All [[minigame]], which can also start if the Captain lands onto a [[Free-for-All Space]].

Exclusively in ''Mario Party 10'', landing onto a Special Dice Block Space activates a roulette, allowing the player's character to receive a corresponding [[Dice Block]] instead of a random one. The Special Dice Block Space appears in each of the three board game-based modes, but [[amiibo Party]] mode specifically redesigns it to depict a blank Dice Block.
