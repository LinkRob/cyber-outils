The '''Pachinko Game''' is a minicourse in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It takes place in a large {{w|pachinko}} machine set high within the sky. The Pachinko Game is accessed from a hole in [[Delfino Plaza]]. Here, Mario has to collect eight [[Red Coin|red coins]] to earn a [[Shine Sprite]].
