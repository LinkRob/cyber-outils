'''Pennies'''<ref>"''Be careful! When you pinch Wendy's Pennies, they pinch back!''" &mdash; '''[[Mario]]''', ''Hotel Mario''</ref> are enemies that appear in ''[[Hotel Mario]]''. In [[Wendy's Blitz Snarlton Hotel]], [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must be careful when collecting [[Coin]]s, as they may really be Pennies in disguise. When they are approached, Pennies grow eyes, two very heavy eyebrows and a smirking mouth. Once Mario finds the piggy bank in the secret room where Wendy hid the real coins, they no longer become Pennies.
