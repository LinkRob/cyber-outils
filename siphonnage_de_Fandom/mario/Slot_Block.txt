'''Slot Blocks''' are a Block found in the [[Lucky House|Lucky Houses]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. The houses rein in four Slot Blocks with  repeatedly scrolling images of 5 power-ups (the [[Double Cherry]], [[Fire Flower]], [[Boomerang Flower]], [[Super Leaf]], and [[Super Bell]]), which characters stop by hitting them. When all four Slot Blocks are stopped, the player receives a certain number of [[coin]]s depending on how many images are matching. It is doable to hit two Slot Blocks at the same time, though the sequence is very precise.
