The '''Vending Machines''' is a place in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Vending Machines are located in the [[Town Area]] section in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], although they only sell [[Coal]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The Vending Machines have a role during [[Shy Guy (Mario Party Advance)|Shy Guy]]'s quest, [[Locomotionless]], in which the player's character has to acquire Coal for Shy Guy for the trains. During the quest, when the player's character arrives at the Vending Machines, they are given the option to buy some coal, which they can then bring to Shy Guy.

If the player's character visits the Vending Machines outside of the Locomotionless quest, they comment on how coal is sold there but that they do not need any. After stating this, the player's character leaves the area.
