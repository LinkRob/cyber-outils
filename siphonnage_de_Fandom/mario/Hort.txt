'''Hort''' is a minor character in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Hort is a young, green [[Flip-Flopian]].

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Hort can be found in [[Flipside Arcade]] of [[Flipside]] where he is the self-proclaimed "ironman of games." However, is statement is true given the high scores on the arcade games. He may be in a relationship with [[Kerrie]] since she says her true love lives at the arcade.

==Trivia==
*His [[Flopside]] counterpart is [[Mort]] (even though Mort moved to Flipside).
