The {{Nihongo|'''Capsule Space'''|カプセルマス|Kapuseru masu|Capsule Mass}} is a space that can be created by the player by throwing the [[Capsule]] at the [[Blue Space]] or [[Red Space]]. The Capsule Space is a space in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

== Description ==
Appearing in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', the Capsule Space has different roles depending on the color of the space.
* The thrown green capsules create spaces for movement. The spaces take the player to another board section.
* The thrown red capsules create spaces for capsules.
* The thrown blue capsules create spaces for other purposes.
* The thrown yellow capsules create spaces for coins. The spaces either give or take coins away from the player.
