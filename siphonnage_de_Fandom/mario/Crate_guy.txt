The '''crate guy''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Crate guy is a tan [[Pianta]] who wears glasses.

===Behavior===
Throughout most of ''Super Mario Sunshine'', Crate guy is located in his shop. Around the start of [[Mario]]'s adventure, Crate guy can still be seen outside the shop prior to its opening.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Crate guy is the owner of a small shop filled with [[crate]]s in [[Delfino Plaza]], and the shop is found left of the [[Shine Gate]].

When Mario enters Crate guy's shop, he challenges Mario to destroy every [[crate]] in the shop within 30 seconds to a prize. To accomplish this, Mario has to [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] every crate within the [[time limit]], then the Crate guy rewards him a [[Shine Sprite]].

If Mario returns to Crate guy's shop at any point after earning a Shine Sprite from him, the shop features more crates stacked on top of each other in four separate rows, rather than small groupings like before. The Crate guy gives Mario a harder timed challenge, retaining the same 30-second time limit, to destroy all of the crates once more for another Shine Sprite.
