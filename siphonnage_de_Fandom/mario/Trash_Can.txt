'''Trash Can''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]''.

==Description==
A Trash Can is one of the items that can be obtained from an [[Item Box]]. It consists of three small red, green and blue fruit cans strung together. A Trash Can can lock on to a racer and shoot at them.
