The '''Belly Blech''' is a small worm and a minor character in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Profile==
The Belly Blech is a parasite that, if ingested by someone, dramatically increases their strength, but also turns them evil from being so enraged that they attack anything near them.

== History ==
[[Lady Lima]] mentions the Belly Blech as she complains that [[Cackletta]] forced [[Queen Bean]] to swallow the Belly Blech. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] return from [[Chucklehuck Woods]] and have Queen Bean drink the [[Chuckola Reserve]] to cure her.
