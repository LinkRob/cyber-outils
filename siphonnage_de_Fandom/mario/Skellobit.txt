{{InfoboxNeeded}}[[File:Skellobit.jpg|thumb|Artwork of a Skellobit.]]'''Skellobits''' are the minions of [[Bonechill]] and are led and commanded by him. They appear in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

They lay siege to the Overthere and freeze many of the citizens. The Skellobits were banished to the [[The Underwhere|Underwhere]] but escaped and are determined to seek their revenge. They will usually attack by charging at the player and cannot be jumped on when their spear is up unless [[Bowser]] breathes fire on them.
