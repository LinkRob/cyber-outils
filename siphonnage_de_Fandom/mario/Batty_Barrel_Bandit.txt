==Gameplay==
Batty Barrel Bandit is a minigame that involves lining up four pictures of [[Golden Banana]]s on a barrel slot. This has to be done a number of times in order to win. This minigame can be played by [[Diddy Kong]], [[Lanky Kong]], and [[Chunky Kong]]. It can also be played by any Kong in [[Snide's H.Q.]] once all of the [[Blueprint]]s have been found.
