The '''Royal gatekeeper''' is a character that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. As his name states, he serves as the gatekeeper to [[Poobah the Pharaoh]] inside his [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid|pyramid]].

== History ==
[[Wario]] and [[Goodstyle]] soon meet the Royal gatekeeper hoping to get access to see Poobah the Pharaoh himself. However, the Royal gatekeeper refuses due to Wario constantly insulting him. The Royal gatekeeper almost mentions that only those that are deemed worthy are able to see the Pharaoh. After collecting two different medallions, the Royal gatekeeper (reluctantly) lets Wario through.
