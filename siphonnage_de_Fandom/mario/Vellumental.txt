The '''Vellumentals''' are four mythical beings in the [[Paper World]] that have the ability to manipulate the elements, one for [[Earth Vellumental|Earth]], one for [[Water Vellumental|Water]], one for [[Fire Vellumental|Fire]], and one for [[Ice Vellumental|Ice]]. They appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. Early during the game's events, they were folded into Origami, and the Vellumentals are never seen in their standard form.
