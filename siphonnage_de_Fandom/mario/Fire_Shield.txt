'''Fire Shield''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Wearing this badge gives [[Mario]]'s [[boots]] protection from jumping on fire-based enemies such as [[Ember]]s. The Fire Shield also increases Mario's defense against fire-based attacks by one.

Mario can find this item within [[Mt. Lavalava]]. While on a zip-line within the mountain, Mario can fall from halfway along the rope and land on a large, flat spire. Atop this spire is this badge. This badge is rather useful to jump normally on enemies such as [[Ember]]s or the [[Lava Piranha]] during it's second phase, however; the Fire Shield badge is considered inferior to the [[Ice Power]] badge as not only the Ice Power badge also grants protection from fire, but deals extra damage on top of it as well.
