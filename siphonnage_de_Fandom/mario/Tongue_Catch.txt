'''Tongue Catch''' is a move in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Tongue Catch can only be used by [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] while on the field, and it involves him catching the ball with his tongue.
