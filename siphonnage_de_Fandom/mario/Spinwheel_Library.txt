'''Spinwheel Library''' is the seventh level in [[Episode 1 (Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker)|Episode 1]] of ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

== Features ==
The player starts next to a Spinwheel, which is used to rotate the center part of the course. The player simply needs to rotate the Spinwheel twice (180 degrees) to reach the course's Power Star.

== Super Gems ==
*'''Super Gem 1''': Spin the spinwheel 90 degrees counterclockwise from its original position to reveal a Super Gem.
*'''Super Gem 2''': Spin the spinwheel 90 degrees clockwise from its original position (or 180 degrees from first Super Gem) and walk up the stairs to the Super Gem.
*'''Super Gem 3''': Spin the spinwheel until it is at its original position (unless it already is) and walk to a hidden passage leading to a purple pipe. Go in it and find [[Blue Toad]].

== Optional Mission ==
*Grab the Golden Mushroom.
