'''Emmit''' is a character who appears in the game [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']]. He is a young Concordian man who studies dancing under [[Groovemaster Dyode]], and wants to become a skilled dancer.

== Description ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Emmit looks like Dyode, even having similar waxed hair, but his hair is shorter and blue, and his glasses are black-framed and have transparent lenses. His outfit consists of a white jacket and dark blue trousers, with no top underneath the jacket.

== History ==
In a flashback, it is shown that Emmit and his teacher, [[Groovemaster Dyode]], went to the forest that would become [[Twistee Island]] before [[Concordia]]'s [[Zokket|sundering]]. However, Dyode had exhausted his hair wax supply, causing his confidence to wane, and refuses to leave despite Emmit suggesting so. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] reach Twistee in their quest to stitch Concordia's islands back together, and are tasked with finding some hair wax when they speak with Emmit. Upon getting hair wax, Dyode has his confidence restored and begins dancing, impressing Emmit and a crowd, and starts the Brother Groove minigame alongside him and the Bros.

The side quest Stepped into a Slump has Emmit suffer the same situtation that Dyode did earlier, with Emmit having bought Elite Boots and losing confidence. To complete this side quest, the Bros. must craft the Groove Boots, which Emmit eagerly trades the Elite Boots for, restoring his confidence. The side quest becomes available after the player links [[Allsand Island]] and becomes unavailable after [[Prince Chilliam]] and [[Princess Burnadette]] have their wedding.

When the Bros. reach [[Jellyfish Island]], Dyode and Emmit are revealed to have reached the island by paddling on a raft for several days. After dancing with Dyode, Emmit gives the brothers [[Club Dyode Ticket|Club Dyode Tickets]].

After [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|hijacks Concordia]], Dyode and Emmit have to be danced with to get their Bond and break through the [[Soli-Tree]]'s shield.

Emmit makes an appearance during the credits, where he and Dyode can be seen performing on [[Heatfreeze Island]].

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Emmit derives his name from the common name "Emmett," which derives from the female name "Emma." The presence of the I in the name is based on an "emitter," which is a component of a diode (hence [[Groovemaster Dyode]]'s name).

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Emmit is called "Emitto," which sounds like ''ēmittō'', Latin for "I send forth" (first person singular of ''ēmittere'', the source of "emitter," which his English name derives from). His Chinese name, ''Guāngcí'' (written 光磁), literally means "magneto-optical," referencing the magneto-optical drive. Spanish and Dutch translate his name to ''Led'', referencing the acronym for the English term "light-emitting diode." Portuguese refers to him as ''Ledson'', again referencing the light-emitting diode, but also taking inflence from the name ''Edson''. In Italian, his name is ''Leddy'', again referencing the light-emitting diode and incorporating ''Eddy'', a shorthand form of the name "Edward." French gives ''Électroll'', from ''électricuqe'' (which means "electric") and "roll," referencing the "rock and roll" music genre with Dyode's French name ''Électrock''. In German, he is called ''Strahley'', a pun on ''Strahl'' ("beam") and the name "Stanley." Emmit just gets a name transliteration for Korean, resulting in ''Emiteu'' (written 에미트), which could also be merely a transliteration of "emit," the source of "emitter."
