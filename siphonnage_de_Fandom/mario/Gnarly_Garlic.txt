'''Gnarly Garlic''' is an item in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
Gnarly Garlic gives Wario the ability to use his [[Star Skill]], [[Phony Ball]]. It appears only in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge mode]], where it can be bought from the shop for 200 [[coin]]s if either [[Wario]] is the team captain or if he has been recruited to the player's team.
