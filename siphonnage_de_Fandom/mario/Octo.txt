'''Octo''' is a large squid boss who appears in ''[[Wario Land 3]]'' and ''[[Dr. Mario 64]]'', being named '''Skowler''' in the former game.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Octo is a black squid, changed to orange in ''Dr. Mario 64'' possibly due to avoid blackface connotations, although later changed again to its original black design in ''[[Nintendo Puzzle Collection]]''.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Octo is encountered as a boss in [[Sea Turtle Rocks]].
{{sectstub}}

==In ''Dr. Mario 64''==
In the opening of Story mode for either [[Dr. Mario (character)|Dr. Mario]] or [[Wario]], Dr. Mario uses his [[Megavitamin]]s to cure Octo from having a case of the flu. In Wario's story, Octo is the fifth boss fought. Like the other bosses, Octo is a playable character in multiplayer mode.

As a CPU opponent in VS. mode, Octo is one of four characters to have the second-lowest difficulty star rating, tied with [[Silky]], [[Appleby]], and [[Jellybob (Wario Land 3)|Jellybob]].
