A {{Nihongo|'''Supersyrup Jar'''|スーパーシロップ|Sūpā Shiroppu|Super Jar}} is an item in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', in which it is named '''Super Syrup'''. A Supersyrup Jar can be used both during and outside of a battle, and restores a certain amount of [[Bros. Point|BP]] to one player, which can be used to recover stats to launch a special attack.

==In the ''Mario & Luigi'' series==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
[[File:Syrup MLSS sprite.png|frame|left]]
Super Syrup restore 40 BP to either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]. They can be dropped by a several number of enemies. They are sold at the item shop in [[Beanbean Castle Town]]. In the Japanese version as well as the [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', a Super Syrup recovers only 35 BP.

===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''===
Unlike the previous game, Supersyrup Jars can only recover 20 [[Special Point|SP]]. 

===''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''===
''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' returns back with 40 BP with Supersyrup Jars. They can be purchased from item shops all over [[Pi'illo Island]] and a few enemies can drop them by chance.

===''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''===
Supersyrup Jars return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', though they have the same functions as the previous game.
