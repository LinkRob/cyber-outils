'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a course in [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. 

==Layout==
The level heavily features [[Hoppo]]s and is the first level to use the enemy. The level opens by demonstrating how Hoppos work. A Hoppo is placed on top of a series of [[? Block|? blocks]] and [[Brick Block|brick block]]s, encouraging the player to hit a block. When the player hits a block, it will cause the Hoppo to roll and get stuck in a [[pit]]. Above the pit is a column of [[coin]]s, encouraging the player to jump on top of the Hoppo. The rest of the level is filled with Hoppos.

After the third [[10-Flower Coin]], is a series of pits, pipes, Hoppos, and a [[Wow Bud]]. Hitting each wow bud will spawn a new one, and eventually a [[Wonder Flower (item)|wonder flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will trap the player with walls and summon various Hoppos and one massive Hoppo. The [[Wonder Seed]] is above the massive Hoppo.

===[[10-Flower Coin]] locations===
The 10-flower coin is in the air, obtainable by pushing a Hoppo into a hole and jumping on it. Pushing a hoppo between two pipes and jumping on the Hoppo will send you to a special area. The 10-flower coin can be found in the right of the special area. Hitting a brick block in a wall next to a [[pipe]] and pit will summon the last 10-flower coin.
