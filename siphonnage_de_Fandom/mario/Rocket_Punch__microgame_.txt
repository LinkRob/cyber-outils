'''Rocket Punch''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'', being featured as the boss of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s level, Makin' Waves. It uses the Massage form for its gameplay.

== Layout ==
The microgame's protagonist is named KOM-MODE MK. II. He resembles a humanoid toilet and can shoot from his hands when players press the shoulder buttons on their Joy-Con controllers. The first section is a sky-based section where common enemies appear. Afterwards is an outer space segment where a large face with hands holding plungers attacks KOM-MODE MK. II., serving as the "boss."

== Gameplay ==
=== Phase 1 ===
During the first phase of the microgame, the player must help KOM-MODE MK. II. shoot down the enemies. Some of the enemies move around, and some can fire missiles, but KOM-MODE MK. II. can shoot them down as well. If one of the projectiles hits him, KOM-MODE MK. II. will die instantly resulting in a failure.

=== Phase 2 ===
After three waves, the players must recalibrate their controllers by holding them in position. The boss then appears. It fires missiles from its plungers, but KOM-MODE MK.II. can shoot those down with his own bullets. He must also shoot at the screws on the boss's arms. After he destroys the screws on the boss's arms, KOM-MODE MK. II. can shoot at the screws that protect its nose. This is the enemy's true weak point. KOM-MODE MK. II. must shoot the nostrils; two hits defeats the boss, which blows up. If KOM-MODE MK. II. takes too long, however, the enemy will release a devastating sneeze, resulting in a failure.

=== Difficulty Levels ===
==== Level 1 (Easy) ====
During the first phase of the microgame, two stationary enemies are featured per wave, but the third wave has an enemy that moves left and right. The enemies rarely attack. The boss has four screws on the arms (two per arm) during the first phase and two screws protecting its nose.

==== Level 2 (Hard) ====
During the first phase of the microgame, three stationary enemies are featured in the first wave, the second wave has two stationary enemies and an erratically-moving enemy, and the third wave has two enemies that move left and right. Enemy attacks are also somewhat common. The boss's arms each have three screws (six total), and its nose is protected by three screws.

==== Level 3 (Expert) ====
During the first phase of the microgame, four stationary enemies are featured in the first wave, two stationary enemies and a single left-and-right moving enemy appear in the second wave, and the third wave has three stationary enemies, a single enemy that moves left and right, and a fifth enemy that moves erratically. The enemies attack very frequently. The boss's arms each have four screws (eight total), and its nose is protected by four screws.
