'''Excalibur''' is an item in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[King Mario of Cramalot]]."

==Description==
Excalibur is a long, golden plumbing snake located in the [[Forest of Perpetual Night and Terrible Dangers]], where it is hidden in a lake. It is named for the sword of the {{w|Excalibur|same name}} from the {{w|King Arthur|Legend of King Arthur}}.

Excalibur has a few additional abilities, like when [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] touches it to transform into [[Fire Mario|Super Mario]]. Excalibur can be extended and used as grappling line, and it can switch from this flexible shape to being stiff and sword-like.

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
==="King Mario of Cramalot"===
The Excalibur is summoned when a lightning bolt strikes the lake where Excalibur is hidden. The same lightning bolt raises a hand out of the lake, which proceeds to toss the Excalibur to Mario.

Later, Mario uses the Excalibur to duel against [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and his [[Golden Plumber's Helper (King Mario of Cramalot)|Golden Plumber's Helper]]. The Excalibur ultimately proves to be more powerful than the Golden Plumber's Helper, and King Koopa loses against Mario.
