'''Exploritone''' is a character appearing in [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']].

== Description ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Exploritone is a pink-eyed blue [[Fractone]]. Initially, he has a wide body to accommodate the large fossil stuck to his back, but waking him up causes his body to revert to a skinnier form.

== History ==
Long before the events of the game, Exploritone wondered where the [[Fractone|Fractones]] originated from. Curious, he delved deep into the planet to seek answers, eventually finding that Fractones trace their roots to the [[Heart of Gold|heart]] of the [[Mushroom Earth|World]]. His adventures are recounted in the Saga of the Exploritone.

[[Donkey Kong (character)|DK]] and [[Pauline]] can find the gravestone of Exploritone and burrow underneath it. If they do so, they find him sleeping and can provoke him into waking up with an attack. The cave where Exploritone is found has books and crystal figurines of various animal species found in the underground.

== Saga of the Exploritone ==
Exploritone kept logs of his journey stone tablets scattered across the sublayers of the [[Underground World]].


* '''Verse 1''': This verse appears in the Hilltop Layer. In this verse, Exploritone wonders about the origin of his race, and voices his decision to delve deeper: ''"Where did the Fractones come from? What is the origin of Us? Curiosity consumes us. After much research, we think we know where we will find our answers. The road leads underground… so underground we shall go. Into the depths… Deeper than we can imagine… We shall find this origin of Us."''
* '''Verse 2''': Found in the Canyon Layer. Exploritone recounts gathering his courage and jumping down to the next layer. His description of the world around him describes a desert: ''"We worked up our courage and leapt into our first sinkhole… The sensation of landing was jarring but not painful. We recovered quickly. The landscape here is very different than the hilltops we are accustomed to. The dry air and scent of sand on the hot winds is an intriguing change of pace. If the atmosphere of this new land is this exciting, then we cannot wait to meet those who reside here."''
* '''Verse 3''': This verse has Exploritone describing creatures with "long necks, with a distinctive dappled pattern" and flexible, fur-covered bodies, perhaps alluding to giraffes: ''"We met some creatures here unlike any we have seen before… They had long necks, with a distinctive dappled pattern. Their bodies were flexible and fur covered. We observed them experimenting on Banandium Gems to process them as an energy source. They hope to build the Underground World's greatest refinery someday. They have great ambition. They dream of a day in which their long necks rise above the crowded throngs of history. It is time for us to head to the next sinkhole. What sort of creatures will we meet farther down?"''
* '''Verse 4''': Found in the divide between the Canyon Layer and the Freezer and Forest Layers. Exploritone describes the path ahead of him splitting, with one being snowy and the other being leafy, alluding to the Freezer Layer and the Forest Layer, and decides to choose randomly by spinning and taking the path in the direction of his fall: ''"We have reached a fork in the road… One branch is snowy. The other is leafy. We are deeply curious about them both. Either one could lead to the origin of Us. In moments of indecision, we stand on one foot and spin around until we are overtaken by dizziness. Once we fall over, whichever path we point toward will be the one we follow. At least they both point down…''
* '''Verse 5''': Found in the Freezer Layer. Exploritone describes his new environment as covered in white all around with a frigid wind that "chills him to his very core," citing his source as a local resident with "quite fashionable stripes" (perhaps indicating a zebra): ''"Every surface here is covered in white… The frigid wind in this layer chills us to our very core. Some sort of frozen cotton is falling from the sky. We are told it is "snow." Our source is a local resident with quite fashionable stripes."''
* '''Verse 6''': Found on the Freezer Layer. "''The stripey zebras gave us a warm welcome to their cold abode...A sweets artisan in training invited us to a tasting of experimental delicacies. Of all of it, the Choco Banana had the biggest impact on us. Our feedback was that it wasn't very sweet. They say they'll work on that.''"
* '''Verse 8''': Found in Freezer Layer. "''This sublayer is warmer than the icy ones we explored above...The heat down here makes it a perfect environment for producing chocolate. They dip a sweet, soft banana into the molten chocolate, which is only possible in this areas, and the result...Ah! Thinking about it has caused a rumbling in our insides. We must take in more delicious meals on this journey...''"
* '''Verse 9''': Found in the Forest Layer. Exploritone describes the air in this layer as "thick with the pleasant smell of life." ''"The air here is thick with the pleasant smell of plant life… We are surrounded by grass so tall that it tickles as we walk through it. In the distance, we can see a large nest sheltering its eggs amidst the deep blue waters of the lake. All these sights and scents taken together combine into a tableau of incredible beauty. It soothes our spirit."''
* '''Verse 10''': Found in the Forest Layer. "''The tall birds here were very polite in their greetings to us...They call themselves "ostriches." They have an enormous egg in which they host guests to their layer. Inside it are beds crafted from leaves and tree branches, and soft carpeting woven from grass. We saw no reason to decline their gracious offer of lodging for the night.''"
* '''Verse 11''': Found in the Forest Layer. "''After we notified the hotel staff that we were departing, they surprised us...The full staff of the hotel appeared to extend their thanks and give us a proper send-off! Not only that, the manager who founded the place personally escorted us all the way to the sinkhole. Such hospitality! We have high hopes that the next leg of our journey will be just as luxurious."''
* '''Verse 12''': Found in the Junction between the Forest and Freezer Layers and the Groove Layer. Exploritone describes the chill winds of the Freezer Layer and the sweet fragrance of the forest as "having left his heart full," and claims to be singing with joy as he writes this verse: ''"The branched path has become a single road once more… The chill winds of the snowy peaks and the sweet fragrances of the forest have left our heart full. We sing with joy as we write this."''
* '''Verse 13''': Found in the Junction between the Forest and Freezer Layers and the Groove Layer. Exploritone notes the sinkhole ahead leading to the Groove Layer: ''"The clouds stretching into the distance are a sobering sight… We can see a giant hole ahead, as though someone shaved a chunk out of the sea of clouds. We have come to the next sinkhole. It is…quite large… When we jump into it, we will pretend that we are flying through the sky."''
* '''Verse 14''': Found in the Resort Layer: "''There is water as far as the eye can see...It's warm here... The sunlight is strong...It feels nice to dip our feet in the water. We feel saltiness against our stone hide. The sand is damp and claylike, with vibrant fruits buried in it everywhere. After eating our fill, we slept in the shade of a tree. Before we knew it, our nap had lasted several years. Now that we are well-rested, it's time for us to head for the next sinkhole. Onward, to the origin of Us!''"
* '''Verse 17''': Found in the Tempest Layer. "''With umbrellas held in their trunks, the elephants guided to the sinkhole. The weather and scenery has changed constantly since our arrival. Sometimes it snows. Sometimes there is lightning. But when we arrive at the sinkhole, the skies were completely clear.''"
* '''Verse 18''': Found in the Landfill Layer: "''There is something strange about the ground here...We looked closer and made a surprising discovery. Small scraps of debris seem to have settled into the layer here creating an unusual sediment. Strange, wiggly creatures spend their days swimming through these odds and ends. They have long, undulating bodies. When we asked, they told us they are are underground eels. There was no sinkhole, so we didn't know where to go from here. But the eels dug a hole for us, opening a way forward. Thanks tom them, our quest to find the origin of Us continues!''"
*'''Verse 19''': Found in Racing Layer. "''We have come to a dreary and empty clearing... Nothing is her but a breeze blowing through a vast meadow. It reminds us somewhat of home. Are the Fractones of the Hilltop Layer singing and dancing from their hearts as we write this? Will this barren meadow someday be home to lively locals of its very own? We hope so. For now...we head deeper.''"
* '''Verse 21''': Found in Radiance Layer. "''After following a long, snakelike path, the landscape suddenly opened up...Before us was a glowing lake...but not truly a lake. Instead, it was light-refracting rocks that filled the grotto floor. The snakes told us they chisel off pieces of these rocks and use them to make light fixtures. Enlightening!''"
* '''Verse 25''': Found in the Groove Layer. Exploritone notes his discovery of a greater quantity of Fractones, but nonetheless notes that he is disappointed to find that this is not the actual source of the Fractone race. However, the Fractones point him to the Planet Core: ''"Finally! We have arrived at the source of all Fractonium! The origin of Us! At least, this was our hope. But it does not seem to be the case. Despite this, we are thrilled to see a greater gathering of Fractones than we have ever experienced before. And oh, how the music flowing from the floor's center moves us! We asked around as we danced and received important information. Apparently, the origin of Us is still farther below. We seek a place called the Planet Core."''
* Verses 26 and 27 have been lost.
* '''Verse 28''': Found in the Forbidden Layer. Exploritone notes the desolation of the Forbidden Layer despite his certainty of his arrival at the origin of all Fractones. He looks for clues on where to look next: ''"This time, we're certain that we have arrived at the origin of Us… Although…there's no one here. We hoped we'd get the meet the original Fractones still living here, but…nothing. It is a desolate place. Our saga ends here. We will have a look around for any stray clues. If we find none, we will head back home to the Hilltop Layer."''
* '''Verse 29''': Found in the Planet Core. Exploritone notes his success in finding the origin of the Fractones and also notes that the Fractones in the area have difficulty speaking, so he proposes to teach them how to communicate: ''"It wasn't the end after all… We have reached the true Planet Core! This is the birthplace of all Fractones. At last, this is the origin of Us. The Fractones here look like us, but they do not seem capable of speech. No matter how long it takes, we will find some way to communicate with them."''
* '''Verse 30''': Found in the Planet Core. Exploritone notes his success in getting the Planet Core Fractones to communicate, and notes how he managed to entertain them with his tales of underground travels, and reveals his next plan to start a village in the Planet Core and invite creatures from the higher layers: ''"It took many long hours, but we finally taught the local Fractones to speak… Now capable of verbal communication, the Fractones here have enjoyed hearing tales of our underground travels. We have seen so much. We have met so many. Thus, we have a new quest! We are going to start a village here. We will invite creatures from all the upper layers to live and trade with us. This will someday be a thriving village that attracts all kinds of residents. This will be the origin of a new Us. As one saga ends, the next begins!"''

== Behind the Scenes ==
Exploritone is named after the verb "explore," from Latin ''explorāre'' ("to investigate"), referencing his role as an explorer.
