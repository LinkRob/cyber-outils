'''Yellow Mario''' refers to various unidentifiable counterparts of [[Mario]] who wear yellow clothing. One such character first appears in the ''[[Mario Bros. (Game Boy Advance)|Mario Bros.]]'' remake for the [[Game Boy Advance]], being player 3's character in either 3-player or 4-player mode. Just like [[Blue Mario]], it is unknown whether he is just a colored copy of [[Mario]], or if he is a relative.

In the files of ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', there are unused icons of a Mario wearing yellow/orange clothing and the letter S. No connection between it and the Yellow Mario from ''Mario Bros.'' has been established, howewer, the icons are for a "sepperate character" from Mario.
