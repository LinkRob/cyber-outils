The '''SS Tea Cup''' is a pirate ship used by [[Captain Syrup]] and the [[Black Sugar Gang]] in both ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and ''[[Wario Land II]]''; in the former game, the SS Tea Cup is featured as the fifth world. In both of its appearances, the SS Tea Cup has [[Bobo]] as its guardian. In ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]'', Captain Syrup instead uses [[the Sweet Stuff]] as her ship.
