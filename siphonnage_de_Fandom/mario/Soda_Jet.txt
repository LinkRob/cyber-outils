The '''Soda Jet''' is a kart that appears in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appeared in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

== Description ==
As the name states, the kart resembles a jet and a interesting detail is that the engine noise actually makes actual jet sounds. The characters emblem is located on the two tailfins.

In its reappearance in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', the kart is equipped with the [[Sponge]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. 

==In {{series|Mario Kart}},==
=== ''Mario Kart 7'' ===
[[File:MK7 Soda Jet.png|thumb|150px|The Soda Jet as it appears in-game]]
The Soda Jet is an unlockable [[kart]] body part. The Soda Jet offers a boost in [[speed]] and [[acceleration]], whilst lowering [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]] and [[Off-Road|off-road]]. In terms of hidden stats, the Soda Jet offers a boost in air speed and handling, as well as [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]] and [[drift]], whilst lowering sea speed.

This kart can be considered to be [[Koopa Troopa]]'s signature kart. As a computer-controlled racer, he will use this kart body often.

The Soda Jet is used for the Expert [[Staff Ghost]] for [[Music Park]], by Nin★Masa using [[Rosalina]], equipped with the [[Roller]] tires and the [[Parafoil|Paraglider]], with a time of 02:07.511. It is also used for the Expert Staff Ghost on {{Classic course|N64|[[Koopa Troopa Beach]]}} with Koopa Troopa, Sponge tires and the Paraglider by Nin★ueda, with a time of 01:42.909.

=== ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
[[File:Rosalina_Mario_Kart_Tour.jpg|thumb|180px|right|Rosalina peforming a [[Tricks|Jump Boost]] while using the Soda Jet on {{Classic course|SNES|[[Rainbow Road (Super Mario Kart)|Rainbow Road]]}}]]
The Soda Jet returns in ''Mario Kart Tour'', where it is classified as a Super kart. Mini-Turbos will last longer when performed and will earn more points or the player if it is used. The game introduces a blue variation of the Soda Jet, called the [[Blue Soda]].
