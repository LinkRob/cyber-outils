'''Crown City''' is a course from ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. It is the second course in the [[Mushroom Cup]] and the third course of the [[Shell Cup]].

== Layout ==
As with the City Tracks from [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']], Crown City has three routes.

=== Crown City 1 ===
The racers start on a long straightaway, close to the south end, before heading south and taking a U-turn to the left with an upwards slope. They then take a [[Dash Panel]] ramp which leads to a section where they cross several solar-panel roofs either by driving on them normally or using grind rails and Dash Panels. The racers then take a left turn, which leads to a drop down to a segment with a sharp right turn. After the turn is a jump through a hole in the middle of a giant tire via another Dash Panel ramp. The racers then land on a rooftop, then make a few right turns before hitting a [[Glide Ramp]], which the racers use to glide over the straightaway, heading south toward the starting line.

This layout is used in the Mushroom Cup.

=== Crown City 2 ===
The racers start on a narrow straightaway on the beachfront which ends in a right turn, which leads to a split path where they can either turn right or continue straight. Both paths meet up later, after which the racers go on a striaghtaway with a gentle slope upward with a rail in the middle. They then must switch lanes to the right, leading to a quarter pipe that turns 180 degrees to the right. This is then followed by a slight right that winds up for a left turn, then a right turn onto another straightaway. Afterward, the racers turn right slightly past 90 degrees before making a slight left to even it out, leading back to the starting line.

This is used in the Shell Cup. When arriving from [[DK Spaceport]], [[Super Mario Kart|SNES]] [[Koopa Beach 2]], or [[Faraway Oasis]], the racers enter through the road that they finish on in Crown City 4, skipping everything before the split.

=== Crown City 3 ===
This variant is only accessible in Versus during routes from [[Peach Stadium]], [[Mario Kart Wii|Wii]] [[Moo Moo Meadows]], or [[Mario Kart 64|N64]] [[Choco Mountain]]. As the racers enter the course, they make a slight right into an alleyway, before turning left to exit the alleyway onto a road to the left of the bridge. The sidewalk has a [[Dash Panel]] ramp that leads into the ocean, where racers must avoid [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]] who are water skiing before a ramp leads directly onto a dock. The racers make two left turns to exit the dock back onto the beachfront, where the race ends.

The final route of the Ice Rally uses a piece of this variant before transitioning to Crown City 1.

=== Crown City 4 ===
In Versus mode when playing without routes, players can play on a three-section course with pieces of all three variants. The racers start outside the third variant before crossing it and turning right to go along it in reverse. However, they turn left and go through Crown City 2, before turning right at the split path (or continuing straight if they took the outside path of Crown City 2) and switching to Crown City 2 backwards. After the U-turn, they turn right and enter Crown City 1, which they traverse entirely for the second section. The final section begins with a left turn before the racers enter Crown City 3 going the correct way around; however, rather than going into the water, they turn left onto the road that they started on and turn right into the now-functional [[finish line]].
