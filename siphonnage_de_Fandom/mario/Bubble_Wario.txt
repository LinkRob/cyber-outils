'''Bubble Wario''' is one of [[Wario]]'s transformations in the [[Wario Land (series)|''Wario Land'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==Description==
The transformation features Wario encased within a bubble, allowing him to travel his way upward. Wario can become Bubble Wario by touching a [[bubble]] while underwater. As Bubble Wario, he floats upward, allowing to travel up water currents that he is incapable of doing otherwise. Wario can return to his normal form by either hitting a surface or floating high enough until he manages to get some fresh air.
