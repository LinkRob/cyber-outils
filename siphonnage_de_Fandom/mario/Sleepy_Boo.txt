'''Sleepy Boos''' are a variant of [[Big Boo]]s featured in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. Their sole appearance is in [[Mysterious Hotel Delfino|Episode 3: Mysterious Hotel Delfino]] of [[Sirena Beach]], as an obstacle blocking [[Mario]]'s path.

Sleepy Boos are always found asleep, blocking passages, especially in the narrow passages within the attack. [[Mario]] cannot awaken a Sleepy Boo, but if he touches one, he takes one unit of damage. The only way to defeat a Sleepy Boo is by having a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] eat it.
