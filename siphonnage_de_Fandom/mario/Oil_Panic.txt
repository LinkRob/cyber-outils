'''''Oil Panic''''' is a [[Game & Watch]] game released in 1982. While it is not a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game, ''Oil Panic'' is one of the four games included in ''[[Game & Watch Gallery]]'', in which there is a Modern remake starring ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters. Aside from this, ''Oil Panic'' has also been referenced in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] and [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]].
