'''Koopa Dasher''' is a [[Weight Class|lightweight]] [[kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is the signature kart of [[Koopa Troopa]], but can be used by any lightweight character. The Koopa Dasher is has the appearance of a [[Green Shell]] in the form of a kart. Like most other lightweight karts, the Koopa Dasher lacks [[Speed]] but has better [[Acceleration]].

[[Koopa Paratroopa]], being the partner of Koopa, has a similar signature kart to the Koopa Dasher named [[Para Wing]].

== Stats ==
*'''Class''': Light
*'''Speed''': 2/5
*'''Acceleration''': 4/5
*'''Weight''': 2/5
