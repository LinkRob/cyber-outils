The '''Shroom Fry''' is a [[recipe]] made by [[Zess T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
This recipe has about a fifty percent chance of success. It is made by cooking a [[Mushroom]], [[Super Shroom]], [[Volt Shroom]], [[Dried Shroom]], or a [[Poison Mushroom|Poison Shroom]]. This recipe can also be made by mixing a Dried Shroom and a [[Fire Flower]], a Dried Shroom and a Volt Shroom, a Mushroom and a Dried Shroom, a Mushroom and a [[Turtley Leaf]], and a Mushroom and a [[Golden Leaf]].

If [[Mario]] buys as many Mushrooms as he can hold, gives them to Zess T., and sells them at the [[Toad Bros. Bazaar]] next door, he can build up shop points for free.
