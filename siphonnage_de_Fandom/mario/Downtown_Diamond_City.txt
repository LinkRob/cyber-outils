'''Downtown Diamond City''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

== Description ==
Downtown Diamond City is a subarea of the greater [[Diamond City]]. It is possible that it has been integrated into the definition of Diamond City as a whole in other ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' games, as Downtown Diamond City has not been defined as a separate area outside of ''WarioWare: Touched!''.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
In [[Mona]]'s story, [[Cool Cuts]], Downtown Diamond City is where the television station, [[Diamond Broadcasting System]], is located.
