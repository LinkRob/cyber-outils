'''Golf''' is a real-world sport and the name of several subjects within ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media:

*''[[Golf (Nintendo Entertainment System)|Golf]]'', a game for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] and its Japanese counterpart, [[Family Computer]].
*''[[Golf (Game Boy)|Golf]]'', a game for the [[Game Boy]].
*''[[Golf (series)|Golf]]'', a series of golf games, many of which include [[Mario]] as the mascot, and a predecessor to the {{series|Mario Golf}}.
*[[Golf (microgame)|Golf]], a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' based on the identically named Nintendo Entertainment System game.
*[[Golf (event)|Golf]], an event in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' for [[Nintendo 3DS]]
