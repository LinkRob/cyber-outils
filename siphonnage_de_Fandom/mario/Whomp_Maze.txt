'''Whomp Maze''' is a mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[Whomp]]s will be buried under the stage. The objective is for players to navigate through the maze that are made out of the Whomps. The first player who can get through the maze first will win. If a player gets to close to a Whomp, then it will pop out of the ground and doing so will result in the player becoming dizzy for a small amount of time and will be unable to move.
