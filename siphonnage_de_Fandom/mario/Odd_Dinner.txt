==Effects==
Eating an Odd Dinner restores only 2 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]. It can be made by giving [[Dyllis]] a [[Poison Mushroom|Poison Shroom]] and a [[Fried Shroom Plate]]. Making an Odd Dinner is not recommenced as the Fried Shroom Plate by itself restores 15 HP making it a better recovery item. However, one can still make an Odd Dinner to fill the recipes list.
