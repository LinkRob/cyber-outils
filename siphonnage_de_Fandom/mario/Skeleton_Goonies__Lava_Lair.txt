'''Skeleton Goonies' Lava Lair''' is the final level of [[World 6 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 6]] in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.

==Layout==
The level starts with several dropping platforms along with an full line of [[Skeleton Goonie]]s. After a few plans, Yoshi has to jounce on any Skeleton Goonies, stay away any lava geysers  advancing. Subsequently, a lot of more dropping platforms materialize, and Yoshi has to eat a Skeleton Goonie to thrash a [[Winged Cloud]], giving away a dropping plan and show the way to a lot of dropping plans. Then a [[Blargg]] become visible, go after by another group of Skeleton Goonies that has to skipped on. More dropping plans become visible, go after by some [[Shy Guy]]s. A lot more Skeleton Goonies become visible, in a line by more Blarggs, directing to a [[Warp Pipe|warp pipe]], which in sequentially straight to the [[Goal Ring]].

==Collectibles==
===[[Smiley Flower]]s===
#Behind a falling platform just before reaching the Skeleton Goonies that need to be bounced on.
#Just above the group of Skeleton Goonies that needs to be jumped on, just before the first group of lava geysers.
#In a Winged Cloud below the warp pipe that spawns [[Flightless Skeleton Goonie]]s.
#In the right Winged Cloud just above the falling platforms after the first Blargg.
#In a Winged Cloud just before the Goal Ring.

===[[Wonder Wool]]s===
#On top of the lowest platform immediately after the first large group of Skeleton Goonies.
#Near the lava, by the group of Skeleton Goonies that need to be jumped on, just after the second Smiley Flower.
#Just behind the first Blargg. Yoshi must jump on a Skeleton Goonie to safely get it.
#In the left Winged Cloud just above the falling platforms after the first Blargg.
#Behind a falling platform just below the warp pipe leading to the Goal Ring.

Collecting all of the Wonder Wools knits NES Yoshi back together.
