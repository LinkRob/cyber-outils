'''Blooper''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. He is a representative of his species of the [[Blooper|same name]], having the same appearance as them.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Blooper is found at the [[Mushroom Beacon]], where he trains regularly to become a master duelist. He challenges the player's character to the [[Hammergeddon]] mini-game. If the player wins at the mini-game, they get to unlock it.

Later on, at [[Sushi Cliff]], Blooper is listed as one of the suspects who dragged [[Shroomlock]] of the cliff and into the ocean. Between him, [[Cheep Cheep (character)|Cheep Cheep]], and [[Dolphin (character)|Dolphin]], Blooper is discovered to be the culprit, as his tentacles make Blooper the only one out of the three with the ability to drag Shroomlock into the ocean.
