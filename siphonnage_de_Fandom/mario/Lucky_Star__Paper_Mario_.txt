The '''Lucky Star''' is a key item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
This item is originally belongs to [[Princess Peach]] and is given to [[Twink]] to give to [[Mario]]. Received on the [[Path to Shooting Star Summit]] after talking to the [[Star Spirit]]s on top of the [[Shooting Star Summit|Summit]] for the first time, Twink bumps into Mario while rushing to find him. After analyzing Mario's appearance, Twink converses briefly with Mario and then gives him the Lucky Star.

After receiving the Lucky Star, Mario is given a tutorial on how to use the Action Command. The player has the option to skip the tutorial if they already know of the controls. Although the Lucky Star does not appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', the action command is available for use and is a default in the game.
