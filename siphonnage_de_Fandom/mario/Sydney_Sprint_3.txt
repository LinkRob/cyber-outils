'''Sydney Sprint 3''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is the third layout of Sydney Sprint, hence its prefix.

== Layout ==
Racers following this route start facing north in Port Jackson, with the route visiting the Barangaroo Reserve. The racers turn left and cross the water via a ferry (which for some reason stretches across the gap). They go through a winding road through the Barangaroo Reserve. Eventually they reach the city's southwestern corner, turning left and heading northwest. Rather than crossing the bridge from southwest to northeast, they turn right and head southeast, following Sydney Sprint 1 thereafter.

This is used as the first half of the second lap of [[Sydney Sprint]], but the racers continue northeast across the Port Jackson Bridge, switching to [[Sydney Sprint 2]].
