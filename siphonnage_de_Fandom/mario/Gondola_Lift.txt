'''Gondola Lift''' is one of the [[microgame]]s during [[Dr. Crygor]]'s [[High Tech]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to move a gondola along a wire so that it reaches a station atop a cliff. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame, but if the gondola does not make it on time, the player loses at the microgame. When the gondola arrives at the station, the character next to it is seen spinning their flag rapidly.

[[Ashley]] is listed as the most suitable, as she can freely roam around and decide which direction to fire projectiles in. [[Young Cricket]], [[Master Mantis]], and [[9-Volt]] are listed as the least suitable.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The wire goes down toward the station.
*'''Level 2:''' The wire goes up toward the station.
*'''Level 3:''' The wire goes in a wavy pattern towards the station. There is also a pole midway along the wire, and the wire segment near it cannot be moved.

==Trivia==
*This is the only microgame in ''WarioWare: Get It Together!'' in which Master Mantis is a bad fit. It is also the only non-boss microgame in which Young Cricket is a bad fit.
