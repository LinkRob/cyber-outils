==Appearance==
Clip Guys are basically [[Shy Guy]]s that carry large paper clips over their heads.

==In battle==
The Clip Guy only attacks by trying to clip [[Mario]] with its paper clip. The player must press A repeatedly so Mario can block the Clip Guy's attack. If the Clip Guy is successful, then it will clip Mario for a couple of turns then it turns into a regular Shy Guy. Clip Guys are only seen in battle battling alongside other enemies such as [[Snifit]]s and [[Piranha Plant]]s. Clip Guys are pretty similar to [[Paint Guy]]s.
