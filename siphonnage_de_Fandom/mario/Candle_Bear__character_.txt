The '''Candle Bear''' is a character from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''. He is featured as the main protagonist of the [[Candle Bear (microgame)|microgame of the same name]].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Description ===
The Candle Bear is a brown bear. He bears a white and blue birthday candle in each hand.

== In ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' ==
The Candle Bear appears in this game, where he is featured as the main protagonist of [[Candle Bear (microgame)|the Boss Microgame]] of [[Mona]]'s level, Mermaid Meet and Greet. Here, the Candle Bear has to protect his candles as ants try to extinguish them, then use said candles to ignite the frozen fuse of a cannon, allowing him to keep the cake out of their hands. Getting both his candles extinguished causes the player to lose, at which point he sweats as the ants prepare to eat the cake.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The Candle Bear and [[Candle Bear (microgame)|Microgame that he appears in]] derive their names from "candle bearer."
