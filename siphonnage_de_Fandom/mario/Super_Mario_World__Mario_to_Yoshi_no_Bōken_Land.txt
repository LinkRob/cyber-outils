'''''Super Mario World: Mario to Yoshi no Bōken Land''''' is an interactive OVA game made by [[Bandai]] and utilizing their [[Terebikko]] system. It was released in Japan in 1991. ''Super Mario World: Mario to Yoshi no Bōken Land''' is based on ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Synopsis==
It starts in the Mushroom Kingdom. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] receive a letter from Princess Peach. It invites them to come to Dinasour Island, where she is. The letter includes a photo of them with his new Dinasour-friend, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]. Mario and Luigi accept. They go in Airplane. In the middle of the travel, Mario mentions he forgots something...Then he says he should call his friends watching the TV, referencing the [[Terebikko]] User. Luigi takes his Terebikko he carries with him. Mario decides to test it, leaving to the first Terebikko interaction. Mario calls the User. The User must pick it to Continue (for that pick the toy phone in the Terebikko, just like a real phone). At the moment the user picks it, Mario starts to talk with the User about how much excited is about to visit the Dinasour Island. When the airplane is about to land, he leaves the call without any major interaction to show. The airplane lands. Mario asks that to say if there is many people there. When the Airplane finally stops, They open the door and Say Aloha. But Strangely there is no one there. Mario and Luigi are confused since there is no one there. Then an Evil Laughter displays, black clouds appear in the sky, and Bowser shows in that same Sky. Bowser Say the next lines: "Long time no see, Mario! I've taken your Princess Peach. If you want her back, come to my castle! But this island is now full of my minions... I doubt you can even make it!" As he laughts again, he disappears. Luigi, and Mario decide to rescue her again from Bowser. They start in a place called: "Yostar" or something like that. In te middle of the way, they start to be tired. Luigi hits a block with the hope there is a Super Mushroom in there. But instead, a egg appears from the block. Luigi calls the User. The user picks up: Luigi wants the User to figure out what's in the egg, when a tail shows out. The option are: 

#Yoshi (correct)
#Koopa
#Flying Cheep Cheep
#Rex

After the chosse moment, the egg hatches and a Yoshi comes out, and thanks Mario and Luigi for saving him and introduces himself and asks who are they. Mario and Luigi introduces themselfs too. Yoshi mentions that he knows those names because Peach always told about them. Luigi regonises him and checks the results:

*If the User picks Yoshi, he says is correct.
*If other animal is picked, he will say is incorrect.

Mario asks Yoshi where is Peach now. Yoshi answers that he doesn't know, because when he went to the castle up to save his friends, he ended up being sealed in that egg! Koppa (which is Bowser's name in Japan) caught all of his friends and he's worried about them... But Mario and Luigi invites them to come in adventures with him. Yoshi accepts and says Mario and Luigi to get in his back, because he knows the way. They go away. They almost get to Donut Plains when [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]] and his Minions cuts the way. Then they complain about If there are more Rexes, Burus or Nokonokos. Luigi calls the user again. 

==Trivia==
* This is the only ''Mario'', let alone Nintendo, game utilizing the Terebikko system.
* In this 28 minute anime, Peach's hair is brown instead of yellow.

==Errors==
*Bowser spikes are white and his horns are total yellow instead of slight yellow.
*When Mario and Yoshi reach Bowser lair, they are wet, even if Yoshi fears the water and doesn't know how to swim. This error also occurs in Super Mario World.
* The map in this video make a reverse way. In Super Mario World, they start on the right way, but here, they start in the left way.
