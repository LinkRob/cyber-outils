'''Lift Leapers''' is a 4-Player [[minigame|mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
The first character emerges from the [[Warp Pipe]] at the start, then the screen divides into four segments, one for each contestant.

==Gameplay==
Lift Leapers is a 2D platforming minigame in which each character has to [[jump]] from various [[platform]]s to get to the goal at the end. The course is divided into sections, each featuring a [[Warp Pipe]] at the end that leads to the next section. If a character falls down a [[pit]], [[Lakitu]] fishes them out, and the character is forced to restart the section.

The first section has two platforms, one of which moves horizontally while the other moves vertically while zigzagging. The second section consists of two vertically moving platforms and two circling platforms, as well as [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]], which have to be avoided, otherwise the character has to restart the section. The third section only has vertically moving platforms. The fourth and final section is made of cloud platforms and spikes.

The first character to make it to the goal is the winner, but if no one gets there within five minutes, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Ending==
The view zooms into the winner's section of the screen, and they perform their victory animation.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Jump
