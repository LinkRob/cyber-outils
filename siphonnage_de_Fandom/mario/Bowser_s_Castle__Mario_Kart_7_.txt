'''Bowser's Castle''' is a track based on a [[Bowser's Castle|castle with the same name]]. It appeared in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

== Layout ==
It contains elements seen in previous ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' games, such as [[Thwomp]]s and [[lava]]. Like most of the other courses in ''Mario Kart 7'', there is an area eligible for [[Glide|gliding]] and [[Propeller (Mario Kart 7)|swimming]].

== Trivia ==
* The course's music is a combination of ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'s'' Bowser's Castle music and ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'s'' Bowser's Castle music.
