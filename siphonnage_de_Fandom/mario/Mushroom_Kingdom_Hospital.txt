The '''Mushroom Kingdom Hospital''' is the location where ''[[Dr. Mario (game)|Dr. Mario]]'' takes place.<ref>"Now, believe it or not, I work in the virus research lab at the Mushroom Kingdom Hospital." ''Dr. Mario'' game manual for NES, page 3.</ref> The [[Virus]]es spread throughout the hospital after an experiment by [[Dr. Mario (character)|Dr. Mario]] and [[Nurse Toadstool]] went awry,<ref>"One of the experiments has gone out of control. The viruses are spreading quickly!" ''Dr. Mario'' game manual for NES, page 3.</ref> leaving Dr. Mario to fix the mess.

The hospital is seen in the ''[[Super Mario Bros. (Valiant Comics)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' comic "[[The Doctor Is In... Over His Head]]," where it is named the '''Mushroom Kingdom General Hospital'''. [[Dr. Waldo Bloom]] works there, although by the end of the story, he takes up golf and has Mario take his position. In addition to [[Toad (species)|Mushroomland folks]], enemy characters are also treated at the hospital, but are made to sign a contract to be good before they leave, though it sometimes fails.

The hospital appears in various volumes of [[Super Mario (Kodansha manga)|Kodansha's ''Super Mario'' manga]]. In ''[[Dr. Mario (manga)|Dr. Mario]]'', [[Mario]] and [[Princess Peach]] take [[Luigi]] to the hospital after he is infected by a stronger version of the [[Gahaha Virus]]. In the manga, a [[Goomba]] known as [[Kuribō (KC Mario)|Kuribō]] works as a doctor at the hospital. In ''[[Super Mario World! Mario Open Golf]]'', [[Yoshi Kart (KC Mario)|Yoshi Kart]] takes [[Billy]] to the hospital after he breaks his shoulder, who later casts a [[Hurricane Shot]] from the hospital to the [[Game Boy Club|Golf Club]]. After [[Dr. Mario Nakamatsu]]'s debut, he becomes the new head doctor of the hospital, as seen in ''[[Super Mario Kart 1]]'', where he tends to [[Donkey Kong Jr.]]'s injuries after a kart crash.

A hospital similar to the Mushroom Kingdom Hospital makes an incidental appearance in ''[[Dr. Mario World]]'' in the backgrounds for the Clinic Event after all of the tasks are complete and the popups announcing clinic events.
