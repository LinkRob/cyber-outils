'''Big Galoombas''' are an enlarged variant of [[Galoomba]] which appear in a few ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' series installments. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

== In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' ==
These enemies are exceptionally rare, appearing in only two levels. The first is [[Big Galoomba Blockade]], where three Big Galoombas must be defeated in order to gain the [[Green Star]] and finish the level. The second is [[Gargantuan Grotto]], where they appear alongside many regular enemies. Just like the normal Galoomba, they attempt to chase the player's character but slowly.

== In ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' ==
Big Galoombas are available in the ''Super Mario World'' theme. The player can change the size of the Galoomba to be larger. Big Galoombas reappear in ''Super Mario Maker 2'', acting the same as in ''Super Mario Maker''.
