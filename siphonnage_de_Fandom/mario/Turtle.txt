'''Turtles''' are a real-world species that have appeared in the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' series, mostly in the form of [[Koopa Troopa]]s. There are two different types of Turtles in the ''[[Wario (series)|Wario]]'' series:

*[[Turtle (Wario Land series)|Turtles]], a species in the ''[[Wario Land (series)|Wario Land]]'' series
*[[Turtle (Wario World)|Turtles]], an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''
