'''Bowser's Castle''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''.

==Description==
Bowser's Castle is a stone court built over [[lava]], with many volcanoes appearing in the background. The ball speed is set to Fast, while the bounce is set to Strong. [[Mechakoopa]]s appear on the sidelines as spectators. A refereeing [[Toad (species)|Toad]] sits in a cage outside the castle itself, where the normal referee's throne is. The music for this court is a cover of Bowser's theme from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and the Bowser Battle theme from ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==In ''Mario Tennis Open''==
Bowser's Castle is where the [[Shell Cup]] takes place in Tournament mode.
