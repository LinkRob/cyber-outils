'''Silver Chomp Grudge Match''' is the first level of the [[Rolling Masterpiece Galaxy]]. It is unlocked when [[Mario]] gains access to the Rolling Masterpiece Galaxy itself, which he can by getting at least 19 [[Power Star]]s in his possession, including those from the first missions of [[Bowser's Lava Lair]], the [[Cloudy Court Galaxy]], and the [[Freezy Flake Galaxy]]. This mission involves the [[Star Ball Rolling]] minigame from the first game and has Mario roll through an art-related obstacle course with the [[Star Ball]], and its objective has him fight a [[Silver Chomp]].

== How to complete==
Mario starts on a planet that looks like the [[Rolling Green Galaxy]]'s starting planet. He has to make his way north to where [[Bill Board]] greets him and tells him about rolling on the [[Star Ball]]. Mario then has to roll the Star Ball to a [[Cannon]] at the north, which leads him to a spherical planet with paintbrush walls. He then has to find a key that unlocks another cannon, which Mario uses to get to a planet that resembles a palette. If he checks the checkpoint flag here, Mario can return here after he dies, spawning on top of a Star Ball. He then has to cross a gap that has assembly blocks from the [[Space Junk Galaxy]] form a bridge that he uses to get across. Mario then must ascend on a lift, which takes him to a [[Flipswitch Panel]] challenge. Mario has to roll across the Flipswitch Panels with his ball to open up a ruler-styled bridge that leads to a cannon on top of an oversized stack of tape rolls. After Mario loads into the cannon, he will fly over to a wooden bridge that breaks apart as [[Grinder (obstacle)|Grinders]] cut through it, so Mario has to be fast to make it to the ruler bridge. There is a [[Comet Medal]] on this wooden bridge, but Mario has to be quick to get it as two Grinders will destroy a circular section the Comet Medal is on. Mario also has to be careful since some [[Bob-omb]]s will disturb his rolling. Fortunately, those Bob-ombs will not respawn due to no Bob-omb dispensers inhabiting the wooden bridge. Once he uses the ruler at the path's end, Mario will get into a match versus the Silver Chomp. This Chomp will rise from a hole in the centre of his arena and the two will push each other off à la the ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' minigame [[Sumo of Doom-o]]. He will sometimes hug the wall to make the job harder, though. If the Silver Chomp wins, Mario will fall to his doom. If Mario wins, another ruler will be spawned and Mario can roll the ball across that to reach a platform of seven point-down eraser-less pencils that carry the goal in the centre. Once Mario rolls the ball onto the central pencil, the mission's [[Power Star]] will be revealed, spawning at the top of a flagpole.
