The '''Carbo Cookie''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
[[Mario]] can obtain the Carbo Cookie by trading a [[Shiny Stone]] to a mole girl in [[Moleville]]. The Carbo Cookie can then be given to another mole girl who is hungry in exchange for some [[Frog Coin]]s, and the mole also opens a shortcut to [[Midas River]].
