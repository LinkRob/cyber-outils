'''Carni-Kiss''' is a very strong physical move used by certain enemies and bosses in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Carni-Kiss is used, the enemy moves closer to its target and a chomping-like sound is heard when this attack makes contact.

==Effects==
With Carni-Kiss, the enemy can deal a high amount of damage to one of [[Mario]]'s party. This move is very common with the treasure chest enemies.
