'''World 7''' is the seventh world in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. It is a sky-themed world filled with power-ups. The world has some [[Paragoomba]]s and many [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. The boss of this world is [[Ludwig von Koopa]].
