'''Cold Case''' is the first level in the [[Secret Mine]] of ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

== Layout ==
In this mission, [[Professor Elvin Gadd]] pixelates [[Luigi]] into the [[Chalet Approach]] outside of the Secret Mine, and must search through the Chalet for a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] assistant. Luigi then finds that he is trapped in a painting by [[List of Boos in Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon|Boofoon]], in the [[Fishing Hut]]. By freeing the Toad using the [[Dark-Light Device]], they both fall into the bottom of the mine and must find a way to access the elevator in the [[Airway]] back up to the Chalet and pixelate the Toad back to the [[Bunker]]. During Luigi's journey, he'll have to capture [[Greenie]]s, [[Hider]]s, and [[Sneaker]]s.

== Collectible items ==
=== Gems ===
* '''Round sapphire''': In the [[Smokehouse]], Luigi must use the Dark-Light Device to uncover an invisible towel. Luigi must then vacuum the blue and yellow coats, which opens up access to this sapphire.
* '''Triangle sapphire''': In the Fishing Hut, vacuum up a hidden pile of snow with the [[Poltergust 5000]] from the southwest to collect it.
* '''Baguette sapphire''': In [[Under the Ice]], fire Toad into the shaft on the upper balcony so he can break the ice and push the sapphire out to collect it.
* '''Football sapphire''': In the [[High Wires]], Luigi must use the cables to cross the ravine, and collect the sapphire on the other side from the crow's nest.
* '''Drop sapphire''': By accessing the [[Airway]] via the [[Terminal]], Luigi must look into the binoculars and see a bat carrying the sapphire a dropping it into a barrel. Later on, after crossing the unstable bridge, Luigi should open the barrel to grab it.

=== Boo location ===
After coming back to the Terminal after collecting the key from the Airway, there is a barrel that has turned invisible, where the mission's Boo, [[List of Boos in Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon|Boofoon]], is located in.
