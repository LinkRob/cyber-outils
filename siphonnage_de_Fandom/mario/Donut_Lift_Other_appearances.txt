==''Yoshi'' franchise==
===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
[[File:Donut Lifts SMW2.png|thumb|[[Purple Yoshi]] in the level [[Hop! Hop! Donut Lifts]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'']]
Donut Lifts appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' as well as ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They are platforms that often appear in auto-scrolling levels, such as [[Hop! Hop! Donut Lifts]], but they also appear in a variety of other levels. When a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] stands on a Donut Lift, it glows white, and if he is on the Donut Lift for too long, it starts to shake before descending into the pit. Donut Lifts do not respawn after falling.

The game also adds [[Large Donut Lift]]s, which are four times as large.
{{br}}
====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
[[File:Donut Lift Moves.png|thumb|left|Yoshi above some Donut Platforms in ''Yoshi's Island DS''.]]
Donut Lifts (referred to as '''Donut Platforms''' in this game) return in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They are commonly found in sky-themed levels, such as [[Donuts and Eggs]] and [[Goonie Heights]], but Donut Platforms are generally less common than in the previous game. Donut Platforms otherwise function identically to before. They are commonly used as extra platforms, but in some cases, they are the sole platforms, requiring Yoshi to make careful movements.
{{br}}
====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[File:Donut Lift Falls.png|thumb|Yoshi hops to the Donut Platforms in ''Yoshi's New Island''.]]
Donut Lifts (once again referred to as '''Donut Platforms''') also appear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. They now have their usual yellow color rather than the brown seen in the first two games of the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}. Donut Platforms also no longer glow white when stood on but merely shake and turn red as they descend. Donut Platforms otherwise retain their functions from other platforming games. They appear in levels such as [[Bouncy Beanstalk Walk]] and can also be used to obtain collectibles items.

{{br}}
===''Yoshi's Woolly World / Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''===
[[File:Lava Plume Smoke.png|thumb|left|Dount Lifts around the Volcanic Area.]]
Donut Lifts return in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' as well as ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. They appear in several levels, most commonly those in [[World 2 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 2]]. The Donut Lifts appear to be made of sand, and they both disintegrate and fall when stepped on. Donut Lifts do not turn red upon being stood on, unlike ''Yoshi's New Island''.

==''Donkey Kong'' franchise==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''===
[[File:Donut Lift Hut.png|thumb|right|Donut Lifts around the goal in ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''.]]
Donut Lifts appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]'', often within levels of [[Mystic Forest]]. Like in most games, whenever [[Mario]] stands on a Donut Lift, it begins to shake and descend, so Mario must often limit how long he stands on one. Donut Lifts are often placed between platforms, frequently serving as extra ground for Mario to traverse. They can also be lowered to reach certain lower [[platform]]s.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''===
[[File:Donut Lift Toys.png|thumb|left|Donut Blocks around a room in ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''.]]
Donut Lifts (under the name '''Donut Blocks''') appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]'', being introduced in [[Magnet Mania (Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis)|Magnet Mania]]. When a [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Mario]] or other [[Mini]] stands on a Donut Lift, the object turns red and then descends, but the Donut Block does not become red simply from being walked over. To keep a Mini on a Donut Block, the player must either stop the Mini or quickly and continuously move the Mini left and right.

Donut Blocks are often used as platforms that the Minis need to walk across, especially for traversing [[Spike (obstacle)|spikes]] and [[pit]]s. Other times, standing on a Donut Block is required to access lower platforms. Upon being used, Donut Blocks continue to fall until they hit the ground, at which point they respawn in their original position. They can be used to descend from heights that would destroy the Mini if it simply fell down.

During the battle with [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] in Magnet Mania, there are Donut Blocks on the platforms above. The Mini Marios need to be shot up to the platforms and drop the Donut Blocks so they land on DK's head.

Donut Blocks can be placed in the [[Construction Zone]] in the [[Magnet Mania (Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis)|Magnet Mania]] and [[Spooky Attic]] Kits, as well as in [[Special Kit 2]] and [[Special Kit 3]].

==Crossover appearances==
===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
====''Super Smash Bros. Melee''====
Donut Lifts appear in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', in the stage [[Rainbow Cruise]]. Their design is taken from ''Super Mario 64'', but they are smaller in size compared to Mario, and when they fall and break, the blocks regenerate instantly, unlike in both ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' and ''Super Mario 64''.

====''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''====
Donut Lifts reappear in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', in the returning Rainbow Cruise stage.

Unique to the game are [[Drop Block]]s, which are blocks with a down-pointing triangle on them. They function just like Donut Lifts and are only used in the Stage Builder and certain stages of [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]]. Drop Blocks are not explicitly associated with the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]].

====''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Wii U'' / ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''====
Donut Lifts also appear in the [[3D Land]] stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''. They also appear in the downloadable [[Super Mario Maker (stage)|Super Mario Maker stage]].

====''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Donut Lifts appear in various returning stages, retaining the same role as before.

===''Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games''===
In ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'', Donut Lifts make a minor appearance in [[Mario's Figure Skating Spectacular]]. They retain their design and function from ''Super Mario 3D Land''.
