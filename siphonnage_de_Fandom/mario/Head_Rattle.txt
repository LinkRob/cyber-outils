'''Head Rattle''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear this badge if he has one [[Badge Point|BP]], and can use this attack during battle by using two [[Flower Point|FP]]. Using Head Rattle may [[Confused|confuse]] an enemy, causing it to possibly do another action instead of their regular move, including attacking another enemy, missing with their current attack, attacking themselves, or using a different attack. Head Rattle can become more powerful if multiple of them are equipped.

Head Rattle is found in [[Keelhaul Key]], a jungle island to the south of [[Rogueport]]. Another Head Rattle badge is purchased at the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] for a price of 100 [[coin]]s.
