'''Pagosu''' is the spacecraft owned and piloted by [[Tatanga]] throughout ''[[Super Mario Land]]''.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Land''===
Tatanga is always seen piloting his Pagosu. In the end, at [[World 4-3 (Super Mario Land)|World 4-3]], [[Mario]] uses his [[Sky Pop]] while Tatanga uses his Pagosu in one final air battle. Aside from flight, the Pagosu is only capable of firing missiles capable of splitting into smaller ones. To destroy the Pagosu and therefore defeat Tatanga, Mario must fire at the vehicle a total of 25 times.

===''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''===
Although the Pagosu does not return, Tatanga does, and now uses a spaceship in outer space to fight Mario.

==In ''[[Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up]]''==
In the "Astronaut" page, Tatanga makes a cameo appearance riding his Pagosu.
