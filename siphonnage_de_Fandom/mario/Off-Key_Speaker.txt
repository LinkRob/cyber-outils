The '''Off-Key Speaker''' is an item which appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and its two sequels, ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
The Off-Key Speaker is a lock-on item that, upon hitting a racer, causes them to lose speed. Following this, the Off-Key Speaker then proceeds to hover around the racer while blasting obnoxious music and producing colorful waves and musical notes, potentially distracting them from driving properly.
