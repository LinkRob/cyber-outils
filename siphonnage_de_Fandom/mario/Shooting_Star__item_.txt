The '''Shooting Star''' is an item featured in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and later returns in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
The Shooting Star is a multi-hit item that strikes every enemy on the screen for 6 points of damage. It is considered an upgraded version of a [[Thunder Rage]] and can be found rarely in areas. Shooting Stars can be bought in [[Item Shop]]s such as [[Harry's Shop]] and [[Star Haven Shop]].
== In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ==
[[File:ShootingStarTTYD.png|frame|left]]
In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', the Shooting Star also inflicts confusion on top of causing damage, giving it a double purpose and increasing its effectiveness.

== In ''Super Paper Mario'' ==
In ''Super Paper Mario'', the Shooting Star is used by aiming the Wii Remote at the screen and shooting the stars to increase the item's attack power. Once the input is completed, the stars rain down and deliver damage to every enemy visible. The Shooting Star can also be made into a [[Meteor Meal]] via [[Saffron]]'s Restaurant, in which case the item requires no input from the player and delivers an instant 20 HP of damage to every enemy present on the field, making it valuable for boss battles and against tough enemies with sturdy defenses.

== Trivia ==
*The Shooting Star is similar to [[Skolar]]'s ability [[Star Storm]]. However, it is recommended to use a Shooting Star as Star Storm takes away 2 [[Star Power|SP]] from the Star Gauge.
