'''Ball Beamers''' are enemies with nuts on top that debuts in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. One of them also has a screw along with the nut. They pop up on different edge of ball-shaped planets. Ball Beamers debuts in the [[Buoy Base Galaxy]] and the [[Deep Dark Galaxy]]. Ball Beamers are motionless enemy that leak round shockwaves that proceed on all sides of the outline of the planet, damaging [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] if he press themselves. 

==Appearances==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
They only appear in [[Buoy Base Galaxy]] . They shoot circular beams that damage [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] if he touches them. Unlike the [[Bolt Beam]]s, their top is perfectly attached making them completely indestructible.
