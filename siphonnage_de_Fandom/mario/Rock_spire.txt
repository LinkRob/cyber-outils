'''Rock spire'''<ref>''Super Mario Galaxy'': Prima Official Game Guide, page 151</ref> is an object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Description==
Rock spires are [[stalagmite]]s sticking out of the ground in certain [[Galaxy|galaxies]]. Certain rock spires reveal items when broken.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can destroy a rock spire by [[spin]]ning into it. In [[Ghostly Galaxy]], there is a glowing stalagmite that has to be destroyed to summon a [[Launch Star]] and complete the [[mission]] [[Matter Splatter Mansion]].

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[File:Rock spire SMG2.png|thumb|x200px|left|A rock spire in Boulder Bowl Galaxy in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'']]
Rounder-shaped rock spires appear in galaxies like [[Boulder Bowl Galaxy]]. The only way to destroy this type is by crashing into them as [[Rock Mario]].
