The '''Motley Bossblob''' is an antagonist who serves as a recurring boss in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|enhanced Ninento Switch port]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

== Description ==
Motley Bossblob is a jester who wears a red and blue jester outfit, with half of his outfit being red and half being blue. He wears rings around his wrists, wields a silver wand with a red stone, has tan sking, and has two colored eyes, with the left one being red and the right one being blue. He later appears with a green and purple outfit with pinkish skin, and then with a light red and light blue outfit with greenish skin. He emits gelatinous blobs to transform into a large robotic clown-like monster. His monster form appears as silver in the first fight, gold in the second fight, and pink in his third fight.  His name is derived from the word "motley" which refers to an assortment, referring to the many blobs that make up his clown form, but it also refers to a jester's costume, which he wears.

== In ''Super Mario 3D World'' ==
[[File:Motley.png|thumb|left|250px|Motley's base appearance]]
Motley Bossblob first appears as the third/final boss of [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]] where he is fought in the level [[Motley Bossblob's Big Battle]]. He is the main boss of that area and is overall the ninth boss of ''Super Mario 3D World'' and its Enhanced Port on the Nintendo Switch 

In this boss-only course, Motley Bossblob gathers large gelatinous blobs to transform himself into a large clown-like monster. He will then try to jump on the player. On the last jump, Motley will split into many smaller blobs along with his jester form, where he will be vulnerable to attack. The player must defeat him by attacking the fleeing jester when he is defenseless, while avoiding the blobs that hurt the player. The player can also attack or step on the smaller blobs to make his clown monster form smaller when they combine. The more blobs that get attacked, the smaller he gets. Every time the boss splits, a set of [[Double Cherry|Double Cherries]] will appear. After three hits, Motley Bossblob is defeated and the Warp Pipe to the Goal Pole is revealed.

Motley Bossblob is later fought again in [[World Bowser]] in the level [[Motley Bossblob's Encore]], which is one of the two main boss levels that must be completed in order to progress to the final boss level. He is either the second/third-to-last or third/penultimate boss of that world and is overall either the fourteenth or fifteenth boss of ''Super Mario 3D World''. His final appearance is in the level [[Boss Blitz]] as the sixth/final boss of that stage with a similar strategy. His jumps will now create shockwaves which can damage the player.

== Attacks ==
*'''Blob Clown Formation''': Motley will use his wand to form many blobs with him forming a giant clown monster made of blob.
*'''High Jumping''': After being fused with the blobs, Motley will start jumping trying to crush the player. after many jumps he will try to ground-pound the player however if the player dodge the attack the blob clown will dissipate and Motley will return to his normal form and start running.
**'''Shock Waves:''' During the second and third battles, Motley can also created shock waves a pond landing on the ground after jumping. 

== Trivia ==

* Moltey Bossblob shares many similarities to [[Hisstocrat]], they both serves as the main boss of a circus-themed stage, both have a reptile-like design, both have a form of Power-Up that can be used in their respective battles and both have a form of minions that aids them, though in terms of the esthetics and battle strategies, they both have many opposite traits. While Hisstocrat is a gargantuan limbless/serpent-themed monster with one form and fights while stationary, Moltey Bossblob however is a tiny humanoid monster with two forms and fights while moving around.
* Motley Bossblob's based form bares a slight resemblents to a Koopaling, particularly [[Lemmy Koopa]]. 
* Motley is technically the true final boss of the game, since he's the last one fought in the level [[Boss Blitz]].
