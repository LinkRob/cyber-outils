'''Unnoticed Shy Guys''' are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] first appearing in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', they are incredibly rare enemies that hide behind bushes or crystals alongside [[Bandit]]s, if a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] passes through the bush or crystal, a Bandit will snatch the baby from Yoshi's back and the Unnoticed Shy Guys will hop on Yoshi's back, the Unnoticed Shy Guy will not grant Yoshi any abilities or hindrances but Yoshi cannot pass through a [[GOAL! Ring]] with an Unnoticed Shy Guys on his back, Yoshi will have to retrieve the baby from the Bandit that took off with it, which should be done quickly so Yoshi doesn’t lose too many [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]], once the baby is retrieved, the Unnoticed Shy Guy will drop off Yoshi's back and behave as a standard Shy Guy. An Unnoticed Shy Guy can be defeated by swallowing it from the bush/crystal it is hiding behind and after which, if Yoshi is to pass it now, nothing will happen, the bush/crystal can also be jumped over. In ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', Unnoticed Shy Guys are green while in''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', they’re red.
