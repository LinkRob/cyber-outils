'''Lip Sync''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be obtained by completing the quest [[Bowser: Accused!]].

==Description==
Lip Sync provides a close-up view of either [[Mario]]'s, [[Princess Peach]]'s, a [[Boo]]'s, or a [[Goomba]]'s mouth. The player can toggle between any of them by pressing a direction on the {{button|gba|pad}}. They can also get the characters' mouths to move by pressing and releasing the {{button|gba|L}} or {{button|gba|R}} button.

The Gaddget's name is shared with the phrase "{{w|lip sync}}," meaning for a person to move their lips as if they were saying something when the source is actually from elsewhere.
