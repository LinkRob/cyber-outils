'''Flashes''', also known as '''Ice Mapu'''<ref name=Versus>Loe, Casey. Versus Books ''Luigi's Mansion'' Perfect Guide. Page 35.</ref> or '''ice basher''',<ref name=Versus/> are a type of [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Flashes are a variant of [[Purple Puncher]]s, with which they share a base appearance, but Flashes have a white body and a heart protected by ice.

===Behavior===
Flashes act the same as Purple Punchers in that they try to charge up a punch into [[Luigi]] and hit him.

==History==
[[File:LM Flash.png|thumb|left|200px|The Flash found in the Kitchen in ''Luigi's Mansion'']]
Because Flashes are protected by ice, [[Luigi]] must melt them through capturing a [[Fire Elemental Ghost]] and producing flames from his [[Poltergust 3000]], leaving the Flash vulnerable to being captured into the [[Poltergust 3000]]. Like Purple Punchers, Flashes each have 20 [[Heart Point|HP]].

Three Flashes first appear in the [[Hidden Room]], each located in one of the [[Treasure chest (Luigi's Mansion series)|treasure chests]]. One later appears in the [[Kitchen (Luigi's Mansion)|Kitchen]], where it is hiding in the refrigerator and is the only ghost who has to be defeated for the lights to turn on. Two Flashes appear on the [[Roof (Luigi's Mansion)|Roof]], or five in the Hidden Mansion.

During the [[blackout]], two Flashes can optionally be encountered in the [[Butler's Room]], along with two [[Gold Ghost]]s.
