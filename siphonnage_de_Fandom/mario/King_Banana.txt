The '''King Banana''' is an item in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
The King Banana is a large [[banana]] with a crown placed on it.

==In ''Mario Superstar Baseball''==
The King Banana is a permanent item in the [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge Mode]] that enables [[Donkey Kong]] to use his [[Star Skill]], [[Banana Ball]]. The King Banana costs 200 [[coin]]s to buy at the [[Item Shop]] if either Donkey Kong is the team captain or if he is on the player's team.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The name of the King Banana comes from it being a banana, and the phrase "king of the jungle", which Donkey Kong has commonly been called. Coincidentally, [[w:c:earthbound:King Banana|King Banana]] is also the name of a [[w:c:earthbound:Key item|key item]] in ''[[w:c:earthbound:EarthBound|EarthBound]]''.
