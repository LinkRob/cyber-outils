'''Spirit Balls''' are enemies that appear only in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

== Biography ==
Spirit Balls are small in size that are clear in color. Spirit Balls are found in every mansion and they are created by [[Boo]]s. Spirit Balls mainly hide in certain objects making them invisible. [[Luigi]] can reveal them using the [[Dark-Light Device]] and vacuum them all up into the [[Poltergust 5000]]. Luigi must be quick because if he doesn't vacuum them all up in time, they will go back into the object, making it invisible again and they must be revealed and vacuumed up again. Spirit Balls can hurt Luigi by simply touching him.
