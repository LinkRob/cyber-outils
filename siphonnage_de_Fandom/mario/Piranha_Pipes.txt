The '''Piranha Pipes''' is an unlockable Heavy Class kart which appears in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is made of green Pipes, which are best known for warping [[Mario]] into different areas, and for being [[Piranha Plant]]s' homes, where they would pop out and try to bite the player. One pipe has the driver, and the pipe behind that is the character who throws the items. This kart is mainly for [[Petey Piranha]], but can be used by any Heavy Class character. It can be unlocked by earning a gold trophy by finishing the Mirror Mode [[Star Cup]] in first place.
