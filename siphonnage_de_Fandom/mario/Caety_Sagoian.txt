'''Cathleen Michelle Sagoian''' (born September 21, 1984 in Seattle, Washington) is an American voice actress and singer. She is the current voice actress portraying [[Bowser Jr.]] and [[Baby Bowser]] and has voiced them since ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]'', replacing [[Dolores Rogers]] who had voiced them in previous games.

==Voices==
* [[Bowser Jr.]] (2007–present)
* [[Baby Bowser]] (2014–present)
