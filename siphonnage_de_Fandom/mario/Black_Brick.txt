'''Black Bricks''' are an object which appear in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. They are a type of [[block]] that can only be broken by [[Wario]]. Certain missions can only be completed by [[Wario]] due to a key feature, or sometimes even a [[Power Star]] being hidden within a Black Brick.
