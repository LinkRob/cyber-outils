'''Shiver Mountain''' is a location that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is located within [[Shiver Snowfield]] that opens up after the puzzle is solved leading up to it.

==Layout==
Shiver Mountain is mainly a large, snowy mountain. There is also a secret area hidden behind an invisible wall that [[Mario]] can pass through and speak with [[Merlar]].

==History==
Mario and his friends continue their journey to the [[Crystal Palace]] by hiking up Shiver Mountain. Mario runs into problems with the [[Duplighost]]s, who are henchmen of the [[Crystal King]], with one of them even disguising itself as [[Kooper]]. Mario heads further until he reaches a room with Merlar, an ancestor of [[Merlon]]. She tells a long story to Mario and gives him the [[Star Stone]] which allows the stairway to open up to the top. Mario and his friends climb the large stairway and reach the Crystal Palace.
