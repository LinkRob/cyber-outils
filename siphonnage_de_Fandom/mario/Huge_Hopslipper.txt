|name=Huge Hopslipper (Battle Card)}}'''Huge Hopslipper''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Huge Hopslipper is the super-strong version of [[Mario]]'s long [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a huge green slipper with a spring attached to the bottom, with a green/lime spiral background.
