The '''Big Daddy of Blooper-surfing''' is a tan-colored [[Pianta]] character who appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. His name refers to how he owns several [[Blooper Racer]]s that he allows [[Mario]] to ride on for free.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
The Big Daddy of Blooper-surfing only appears in [[Ricco Harbor]] during two episodes, both of which involve Mario riding a Blooper Racer.

The Big Daddy of Blooper-surfing first appears in Episode 2: [[Blooper Surfing Safari]], where Mario has to ride on a Blooper Racer during a timed race. If Mario clears the race fast enough, the Big Daddy of Blooper-surfing gives him a [[Shine Sprite]]. If Mario does not, he loses an [[extra life]] from failing to meet the object.

His next appearance is in Episode 6: [[Red Coins on the Water]]. Here, the Big Daddy of Blooper-surfing leases Mario a Blooper Racer for free, as Mario is required to ride one to collect all of the [[Red Coin|red coins]] within the [[time limit]].
