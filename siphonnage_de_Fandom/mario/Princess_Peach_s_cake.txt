'''Princess Peach's cake''' is a cake that appeared in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It reappeared in ''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]''.

== In ''[[Super Mario 64]]''/''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' ==
Peach baked a cake for [[Mario]] and sent him a letter inviting him to [[Princess Peach's Castle]].

At the end of the game, after Peach is restored back to the kingdom, she kept her word to celebrate Mario's victory.
