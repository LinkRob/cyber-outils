'''Shy Guy''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' and a representative of his species of the [[Shy Guy|same name]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Shy Guy works as the conductor of the [[Train Station]] in [[Shroom City]]. He needs help retrieving coal for his train. If the player character does this, he gives them the [[Cake Maker]] [[Gaddget]].
