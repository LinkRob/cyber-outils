|name=Big Fire Flower (Battle Card)}}'''Big Fire Flower''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Fire Flower is the strong version of [[Mario]]'s fire [[Flower attack.]] It is a big sentient red flower with a green stem, accompanied with a red/pink checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 80/300 coins

Sell Price: 40/150 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Fire Flower is first seen in [[Vortex Island]], but can also found in [[Fortune Island]], [[Sunset Express]], [[Mossrock Theater]], and [[Black Bowser's Castle]].
