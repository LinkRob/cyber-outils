==History==
Hamma Jamma's father is only mentioned a few times by his son, [[Hamma Jamma]]. Hamma Jamma holds high regards for his [[Hamma Jamma's grandfather]] who was a former fighter, but not his father. This is because Hamma Jamma's father dropped out of Hammer Bros. school to fulfill a life in business which, in turn, makes him a "Business Bro." However, Hamma Jamma was upset at his father for not choosing to become a fighter like the rest of his family, which leads to Hamma Jamma rarely talking about his father.
