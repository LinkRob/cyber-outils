The '''Royal Bus Driver''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
The Royal Bus Driver is a [[Lakitu]] that wears large glasses and a green cap.

==History==
The Royal Bus Driver, as his name states, is the official bus driver for [[King Nimbus]]. King Nimbus calls the Royal Bus Driver to drive [[Mario]] to [[Bowser's Keep]], which the [[Smithy Gang]] used as one of their main bases.
