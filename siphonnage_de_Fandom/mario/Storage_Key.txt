The '''Storage Key''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Storage Key can be found in the phone booth in [[Glitzville]] left there by [[Jolene|X]]. With the Storage Key, [[Mario]] can unlock the storage room in the [[Glitz Pit]].

==Trivia==
*The Storage Key resembles the [[House Key (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|House Key]] in appearance.
