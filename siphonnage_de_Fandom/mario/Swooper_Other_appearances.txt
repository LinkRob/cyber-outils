==''Donkey Kong'' franchise==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''===
[[Image:Swooper Toys.png|thumb|right|Two Swoopers guard [[DK]] from the Minis.]]
Swoopers appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''. They only appear in [[Spooky Attic]] during the battle with [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] in [[Boss Game 7]] as an additional obstacle to avoid while aiming at Donkey Kong. During the battle, two Swoopers are roosting on the [[girder]] dividing the two screens. One of the Swoopers emerges and flies left and right in a manner similar to [[Dorakyū]] from the [[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Game Boy ''Donkey Kong'']]. When the [[Cannon]] ends up underneath a Swooper, it wraps itself in its wings and drills downward, trying to hit the [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Mario]], requiring the player to move the cannon out of the way quickly. The Swoopers periodically emerge and rest after Donkey Kong has been hit once. From there, the second Swooper emerges and joins the first, but as time goes by, one may stop to rest, leaving the other alone for some time. Swoopers stop attacking after Donkey Kong has been defeated.
