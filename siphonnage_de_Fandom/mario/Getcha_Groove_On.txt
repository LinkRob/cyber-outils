'''Getcha Groove On''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'' as one of [[Orbulon]]'s microgames, under the [[IQ]] folder, and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Jimmy T]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The player has to observe the instructor's commands and copy them by using the Control Pad to pose in the same directions each. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame, but if unsuccessful, they lose. In the original game, the player controls a canine-like character named [[King of Jam]], who must follow a human's instructions. In ''WarioWare Gold'', both characters are cats.

The first, second and third difficulty levels feature two, three, and four moves to copy respectively. In ''WarioWare Gold'', this was increased to three, four, and five moves respectively.
