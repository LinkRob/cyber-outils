'''Thwacks''' are box enemies with faces that live on [[Thwomp Volcano]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Their name is derived from an onomatopoeia for something being struck, while also being similar to both "[[Thwomp]]" and "[[Block]]," both of which they resemble. Thwacks come in two colors: pink and green. Pink Thwacks have blue eyes while green Thwacks have yellow eyes. Thwacks also make a variety of facial expressions that change on their turn. In the field, Thwacks roll around in their territory, not unlike a [[Tox Box]], but may occasionally spin around.

In battle, Thwacks employ a variety of attacks, with which one of their attacks is being used being telegraphed by the defense icon. Before each attack, a Thwack twirls and either remains the same color or changes into a different color. If green, the Thwack attacks [[Luigi]]; if pink, the enemy attacks [[Mario]]. After twirling, the Thwack may attempt to crush the Bro. of its choice. As the Thwack hovers over the hero, the baby Bro. should counter with a [[Hammer]] strike. Additionally, Thwacks may fly low, attempting to ram into a Mario brother. In this case, Mario or Luigi should [[jump]] to dodge or counter the attack. Thwacks can also summon more Thwacks to join the battle. 

If the battle has gone on for too long, the Thwack may turn yellow (instead of pink or green) with no face. When this happens, the Thwack will attempt a sacrificial attack in a last ditch effort to destroy the Mario Bros. After turning yellow, the Thwack splits into two boulders, and each rock rolls into a brother. This attack can be avoided by jumping. Once all Thwacks have done this, the battle ends if no other enemies are present.

In the battle against [[Bowser]] and [[Baby Bowser]], the former uses a shock wave attack that, depending on the speed of the shock wave, can send all kinds of Thwacks raining down on the quartet. The debris can be jumped over.

Thwacks are similar to [[Wonder Thwack]]s (which have higher stats in all areas except [[Heart Point|HP]]) and [[Thwack Totem]]s (which are formed when multiple Thwacks merge into one entity).

A [[Tanoomba]] and [[Tashrooba]] can also mimic Thwacks in the field, having its face on the front.
