'''Bob-omb Aplomb''', titled '''Kick Kick Boom''' in PAL releases, is a General minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Opening==
A [[Bob-omb]] lands on a rock platform in the middle of the screen, then explodes, causing the rock platform to crumble apart and fall into the [[lava]].

==Gameplay==
The characters have to kick Bob-ombs off their rock platforms. Bob-ombs randomly fall from the sky, and they can be kicked either into the [[lava]] or onto an opponent's platform. After being onscreen for too long, a Bob-omb explodes, destroying whichever rock platform it is sitting on. When only one character is left standing or the 60-second [[time limit]] runs out, the game ends, and any character remaining by the end is ranked the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|3ds|Stick}}: Move

*'''Rules (American English)''': ''Run and kick the Bob-ombs onto your opponents' platforms before they explode.''
*'''Rules (British English)''': ''Move into the Bob-ombs to kick them off your platform.''
