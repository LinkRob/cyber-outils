==Effects==
Block Blocks are rather special items as they are the only item in the game that can make [[Mario]] and the others invincible. The player must put in the commands to make the effects last longer. The only way to obtain Block Blocks is to either purchase them from [[Notso]]'s shop of [[Flopside]] for 50 coins or find them dropped by [[Tileoid]]s or [[Hooligon]]s. Block Blocks are also the sole ingredient of the [[Block Meal]] dish.
