'''Mini-Necky''' is an enemy in the [[Donkey Kong (series)|''Donkey Kong series'']]. They appear to be miniature versions of [[Necky|Neckies]]. In ''Donkey Kong Country''/''Land'', they attack by shooting coconuts while in the sequels, they swoop down at [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong]]. In all four games, they can be defeated when jumped on. They wear red bandanna on their heads in ''Donkey Kong Country 2''/''Land 2''.

A Mini-Necky boss called Krow appears in ''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest'' and ''Donkey Kong Land 2''. He sends out Mini-Neckies in the second battle when he is his form, Kreepy Krow. They also reappear in ''[[DK: King of Swing]]'' and ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]'' where they swoop down or shoot coconuts and also appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Barrel Blast]]'' as an item.
