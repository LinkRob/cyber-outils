'''Birdo''' is a character appearing in the ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie|Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. Birdo is seen shooting eggs at [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]]. 

== Appearance ==
Birdo is a pink, Yoshi-like creature with a big red bow. Birdo has a white belly, four fingers, and three toes, along with a large holed snout to shoot eggs from.

===Trivia===
*In the games [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and [[Birdo]] are partners whereas in this movie they never interact.
