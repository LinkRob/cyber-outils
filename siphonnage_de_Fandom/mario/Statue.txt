A '''statue''' is an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
A statue is composed of light gray stone and resemble [[Wario]]'s head. Statues function similar to [[column]]s, as they can be used against enemies, break after one hit, and Wario can perform any [[Mad Move]] with a statue.
