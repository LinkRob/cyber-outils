The '''Dream Machine''' is a machine used by [[Wart]] during the events of ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its Western ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' conversion, ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It is located in Wart's chamber in [[World 7-2 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 7-2]], and it spits out [[vegetable]]s that the player's character can use to attack [[Wart]].

==In ''Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic''==
More plot involving the Dream Machine was provided in ''Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic'' than in ''Super Mario Bros. 2''. In the former game, the [[Subcon (species)|Subcon]] (here named the Muu people) were revealed to have created the Dream Machine, but Mamu (as Wart is named in Japanese) misused the Dream Machine to produce several monsters (something that the Dream Machine is not shown to do in-game) and form an army called the [[8-bits]] to conquer [[Subcon]]. Although the Muu people were successful in stopping Mamu, two baby twins named Piki and Poki read a storybook about these events and rip out the last page, undoing the Muu people's success. When the babies rip out the page, Mamu's hand reaches in to kidnap them both, prompting the four main characters to rescue the babies and stop Mamu from rewriting the story.
