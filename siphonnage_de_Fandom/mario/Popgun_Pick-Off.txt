|type = Duel Mini-game}}'''Popgun Pick-Off''' is a [[Duel Mini-game]] that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, two players must compete in shooting the Baby Bowsers that appear on the screen. The players can quickly shoot the Baby Bowsers over and over again until its picture disappears. Players must be careful to not shoot Toad or they will lose 10 points. The player that has the most points when the time ends wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Move cursor
*{{button|n64|a}} - Shoot
