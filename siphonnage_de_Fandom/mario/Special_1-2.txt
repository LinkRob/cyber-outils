'''Special 1-2''' is the forty-eighth level of the game ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
The overall layout of Special 1-2, like Special 1-1 was to [[World 2-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-1]], is based nearly entirely on [[World 1-2 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 1-2]]. Though like Special 1-1, the level does include several differences from its initial counterpart &ndash; including tougher enemies and more spikes. Like the previous level, Special 1-2 includes a [[Cosmic Clone]] of [[Mario]] that will follow Mario until it can bump into him to damage him; however, after one attack, it will continue to follow Mario for another.

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] is found atop the large square of blocks near the beginning of the level, however, the blocks break upon Mario's landing on them.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal is being circled around by a large, spiked bar that Mario must jump over to obtain the coin.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The final Star Medal is found shortly after the second. By using the first bouncy platform, Mario can jump over a chasm to collect this coin.
