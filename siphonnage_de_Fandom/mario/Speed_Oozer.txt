{{Infobox_Enemy|name = Speed Oozer|image = Speed Oozer.png|games = [[Luigi's Mansion 3]]|located = [[ScareScraper]]|species = ghost|leader = [[Hellen Gravely]]|attacks = throwing objects}}Speed Oozer is a form of [[Oozer]], however, it is glowing red instead of being yellow. It is a kind of rare ghost in [[Luigi's Mansion 3]] which only appears in [[ScareScraper]]'s ten floors mod, they have 250 HP. Speed Oozers move faster than Oozers.

==Appearance==

Speed Oozers appear to be glowing red Oozers. It has white eyes and mouth, same as regular Oozes. Although these two kinds of ghosts are both Oozers, Speed Oozer can move faster and have more HP than regular Oozers. Speed Oozers are defeated in the same way as Oozers. 

==Battle==

When [[Luigi]] arrives on the fifth floor of [[ScareScraper]] in ten floors mod, the game will choose a kind of rare ghost to appear randomly. When Speed Oozers appear, they will move faster than regular Oozers and hide to throw objects. Luigi and the other players have to defeat Speed Oozers in the same way as they defeat regular Oozers: find them and flash them, then suck them up.
