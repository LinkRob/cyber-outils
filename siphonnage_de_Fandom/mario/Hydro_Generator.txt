|first = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''}}The '''Hydro Generator''' is a room in the [[Haunted Towers]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. As stated by [[Professor E. Gadd]], the Hydro Generator is the main source of power of Haunted Towers powered mainly by water. The Hydro Generator is accessed from the [[Courtyard]].

==History==

During [[Haunted Towers#B-1: A Job for a Plumber|B-1: A Job for a Plumber]], E. Gadd sends Luigi to Haunted Towers to fix the plumbing. As Luigi enters the Hydro Generator, a [[Slammer]] is seen and it flys upstairs. It appears that the Slammer actually broke the main generator forcing Luigi to head upstairs to the [[Water Supply]] for repairs.

During this mission, Luigi can use the [[Dark-Light Device]] to reveal a hidden washer and capture [[Boos in Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon#Haunted Towers|MamBoo]].

During [[Haunted Towers#B-5: Doggone Key|B-5: Doggone Key]], Luigi must chase after the [[Polterpup]] through this room.
