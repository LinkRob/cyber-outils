A '''Blarggwich''',<ref>''Yoshi's New Island'', World 3-S: Ride the Blarggwich</ref> originally known as a '''Support Ghost''',<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 128.</ref> is a monochrome, [[Flatbed Ferry]]-topped [[Blargg]] that first appeared in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''====
Support Ghosts are rare, appearing only in [[Sluggy The Unshaven's Fort]] in their debut game. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can [[jump]] onto and freely move around atop the Flatbed Ferry carried on a Support Ghost's head. Once stepped on, it moves along a set course, occasionally stopping and stretching upward, leading the Yoshi to both traps and helpful [[List of items|items]]. A similar creature known as the [[liquid lift]] appears earlier in [[Salvo The Slime's Castle]], and it is a water spirit that is thinner, lacks eyebrows, has smaller eyes, and moves in a gentle loop in a big room.

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
Blarggwiches reappear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' under their current name. One is featured as the main gimmick of the third [[world]]'s special [[level]], [[Ride the Blarggwich]]. A [[Blarggwich (sandwich)|variant]] between two Flatbed Ferries also appears in [[Count Fang's Castle]].

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
Blarggwiches return in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', where they appear in the stage [[Yoshi's Island (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Yoshi's Island]]. This type perpetually acts as an extra platform over a deep pit, and players can jump onto it to avoid falling. However, it appears only at random moments.
