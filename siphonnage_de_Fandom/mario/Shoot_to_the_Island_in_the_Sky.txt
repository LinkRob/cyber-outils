'''Shoot to the Island in the Sky''' is the third mission of [[Bob-omb Battlefield]], exclusive only for ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. It does not return in the [[Reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', which instead has [[5 Silver Stars!]] as the third mission.

==Description==
The objective is simple, the player must talk to a [[Bob-omb Buddy]] to activate a [[cannon]]. There are also two cannons to use, one at the start of the level, and another one located to the right of the [[Chain Chomp]]. Once entered the cannon, the player should shoot to the floating island. Using the [[Wing Cap]] found in red [[! Block|! Blocks]] can also make this mission easier if you have it unlocked. Once on the floating island, the player must hit the yellow ! Block to obtain a [[Power Star]].
