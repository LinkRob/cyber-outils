==Gameplay==
Stealthy Snoop is a minigame where the player must sneak around and find the goal within a certain amount of seconds. Several [[Kremling]]s known as [[Kop]]s patrol the area and if the player gets caught in one of their flashlights, they lose the minigame. Only [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] plays this minigame as Stealthy Snoop is not available to play at [[Snide's H.Q.]] once the player gets all of the [[Blueprint]]s in the game.
