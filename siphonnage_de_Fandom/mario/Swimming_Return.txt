'''Swimming Return''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] used by [[Waluigi]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
Waluigi plugs his ears, and a pool of water is created on his side of the court. Waluigi then does a breaststroke to wherever the ball is located and returns it. In doing so, Waluigi does a lob shot, and the height in which the ball returns depends on how far Waluigi was from it before activating Swimming Return.
