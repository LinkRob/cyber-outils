'''A Tale of Tails''' is the third level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 30.

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains 2 [[Big Tail Goomba]]s, 2 [[Tail Boo]]s, and 4 [[Tanoomba]]s. The enemy captain is a Big Tail Goomba, and he knows Deny.

The 2nd battle contains 4 Tanoombas and 4 Tail Boos. The enemy captain is a Tail Boo, and he knows [[Sneak Attack]] and [[Deny]].

The final battle contains 2 Big Tail Goombas, 2 [[Spike]]s, 3 Tanoombas, and 1 Tail Boo. The enemy captain is a Big Tail Goomba, and he knows [[Rally]] and [[Pass the Bob-omb]].
