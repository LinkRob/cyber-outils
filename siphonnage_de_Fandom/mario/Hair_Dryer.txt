The '''Hair Dryer ''' (known as the '''Hairdryer''' in Europe) is a [[Stickers#Thing Stickers|Thing&nbsp;Sticker]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. 

== Description==
The Hair Dryer has a blue handle and a silver top. The Hair Dryer is used to burn enemies, like the other fire things. You find the Hair Dryer by unlocking the blue door in [[Ice Flow]]. You can also purchase it from the [[Toad Shop]] for 150 coins. The only use this sticker has is when you fight the World 4 boss. It is a weakness to the World 4 boss.
