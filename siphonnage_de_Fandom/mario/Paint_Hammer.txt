The '''Paint Hammer''' is an item which only appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==History==
When Huey's paint is wrung out of his body, that and his power to contain paint is transferred to a hammer [[Mario]] recently found. Now the hammer has the ability to recolor white spots just by giving it a swing.

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Paint Hammer is available in [[Port Prisma]] at the start of the game. To increase its paint capacity, the player has to defeat enemies and collect Hammer Scraps. The player can press {{button|wiiu|A}} to make it function like a normal hammer. The player can press {{button|wiiu|X}} to make paint splash from the hammer.
