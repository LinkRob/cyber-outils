'''{{world|mushroom|cannonn2}}''' is the Warp Cannon level found in [[World Mushroom (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Mushroom]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. The stage is unlocked by completing {{world-link|mushroom|B|World Mushroom-B}}. 
==Layout==
The level begins with [[Mario]] in front of the Warp Cannon on the world's overworld in a level theme never seen again. Entering the Warp Cannon initiates a countdown, where he is shot into the main portion of the level, dashing without stopping until the end. A [[Crowber]] will first appear, followed by a [[Boomerang Bro]]. A Red Ring can then be found. More Crowbers and Boomerang Bros. follow. After that, the level ends, unlocking {{world-link|flower|1|World Flower-1 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)}}.
