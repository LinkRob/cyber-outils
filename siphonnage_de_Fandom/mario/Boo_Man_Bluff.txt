'''Boo Man Bluff''' is an enemy which first appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Boo Man Bluff is a variant of [[Boo]] that wears a black blindfold, preventing them from being able to see. In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', Boo Man Bluffs are depicted as mid-sized peach-colored Boos with a green mouth and short, stubby arms.

In ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'', Boo Man Bluffs are colored white and were given large pink ears, although they now lack arms and no longer have a green mouth. Their blindfold color was also changed to light-blue.

For ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', the design from ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' was reinstated, but the Boo Man Bluffs are still white and lack a green mouth like in ''Yoshi's Story''.

===Behavior===
Because they are blindfolded, Boo Man Bluffs rely on sound to figure out where a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] is, something that ''Yoshi's Story'' emphasizes by giving it ears. In ''Super Princess Peach'', they appear in swarms.

==In {{franchise|Yoshi}}==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
[[File:Boo Man Bluff SMW2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
There is only one Boo Man Bluff, and it appears in [[Bigger Boo's Fort]]. In the [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', some Boo Man Bluffs appear in the [[Endless World of Yoshis]]. A Boo Man Bluff cannot be defeated.

===''Yoshi Story''===
[[File:Blindfold Boo YS sprite.png|frame|left]]
For this appearance, they are named '''Blindfolded Boos''', or alternatively '''Blindfold Boos'''. Blindfolded Boos exclusively in the [[Ghost Castle]], and two can be seen in the level's associated icon. Blindfolded Boos act similarly as in their first appearance. Much like in the previous game, Blindfolded Boos are invulnerable to attacks, and when a Blindfolded Boo goes after a Yoshi, it can move faster than the Yoshi can run.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
[[File:Blindfold Boo.PNG|frame|left]]
[[File:BLB1.PNG|thumb|Boo Man Bluffs behind Princess Peach in ''Super Princess Peach'']]
Boo Man Bluffs first appear in the level [[Shriek Mansion 3-3]]. They are only encountered as an impenetrable swarm, and their movement causes the screen to move. Despite being blindfolded, a Boo Man Bluff's behavior is affected if [[Princess Peach]] looks at them, in which case they hide, while continuing to chase her. If Peach touches a Boo Man Bluff, she is forced to restart the room she is in. The Boo Man Bluffs stop chasing Peach once she leads them into light, causing them to vanish. In later levels, Boo Man Bluff are much quicker, and Peach can only escape from them using the [[Gloom]] [[vibe]].

Boo Man Bluff are one of the few enemies to not appear in the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]].

== Behind the scenes ==
===Etymology===
Its name is a pun of {{w|blind man's bluff}}, a tag game involving the use of blindfolds.
