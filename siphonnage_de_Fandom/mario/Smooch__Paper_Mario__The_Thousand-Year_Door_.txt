'''Smooch''' is one of [[Ms. Mowz]]'s attacks in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It can be used once she reaches the [[Ultra Rank]]. Smooch requires 10 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Effects==
To use Smooch, the player must alternately press {{button|gcn|a}} and {{button|gcn|b}} to fill up the gauge. If successful, Ms. Mowz will kiss [[Mario]] restoring up to 10 [[Heart Point|HP]]. However, given this move requires 10 FP to use to only restore 10 HP, it is much more recommend to use a [[Super Shroom]] or other items that restore 10 or more HP rather than wasting FP.
