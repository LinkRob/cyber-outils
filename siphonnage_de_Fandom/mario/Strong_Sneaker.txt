'''Strong Sneakers''' are a stronger variant of [[Sneaker]]s in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Strong Sneakers are basically regular Sneakers, but theit bodies are a slight darker shade of pink, and they have light pink cracks all over their bodies.

=== Behavior ===
Strong Sneakers behave the same way as regular Sneakers, scaring Luigi from behind, disrupting him from vacuuming up ghosts, and attack Luigi by shoving him. Although now they have 100 HP, though some in [[Piece at Last|a]] [[Double Trouble|few]] [[Paranormal Chaos|missions]] only have 50 HP.

== In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' ==
A Strong Sneaker first appears in the [[Old Clockworks]]. A few more are found in the [[Secret Mine]], but they are more common in the [[Treacherous Mansion]].

Strong Sneakers also appear in [[ScareScraper]], but only on Expert difficultly or Endless mode. Strong Sneakers are usually fought with other Sneakers, or different varations of Greenies. Strong Sneakers also aid [[Primordial Goo]] and its variations, replacing [[Slammer]]s.
