|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Lulla-Bye''' is a special move used by certain enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Lulla-Bye is used, cyan colored notes will appear on the screen.

==Effects==
With Lulla-Bye, the user is able to inflict the [[Sleep]] status on one of [[Mario]]'s party. This can be countered by wearing a Wake Up Pin.

==Trivia==
*The theme that plays when Lulla-Bye is used is a version of the main theme of ''[[Super Mario World]]''.
