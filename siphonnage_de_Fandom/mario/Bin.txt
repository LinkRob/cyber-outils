'''Bins''', alternatively named '''Garbage Cans''', '''Trash Cans''', or '''Trash Bins''', are a recurring object in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related games. They first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]].

== Description ==
Bins are containers, usually gray in color, that usually contain items discarded as waste, although they can occasionally contain a useful item. Bins are commonly found in urban-themed locations.

Bins have a few animate enemy counterparts, including [[Dust Man]] from ''Donkey Kong'' for Game Boy, [[Bucket Man]] from ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]'', and [[Possessed Bin]] from ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. In ''[[Mario Bros. (Game Boy Advance)|Mario Bros.]]'' for [[Game Boy Advance]], there is a specific type of bin in multiplayer mode known as the [[garbage can]], which a character can be thrown into and obtain an item from.

==In {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}==
===''Donkey Kong'' (Game Boy)===
[[File:Trash Can DK GB sprite.png|frame|left]]
Trash Cans are first found in the [[Big-City]]. [[Mario]] can use a Bin as a small platform to help him reach certain higher areas or pick one up and throw it at an enemy, such as [[Bowbow]]s, to defeat it. Once a Trash Can hits or is hit by another object or enemy, it falls off-screen.

A Dust Man disguises itself as a regular Bin, and reveals its eyes and feet upon being approached by Mario.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''===
Garbage Cans have the same function as in ''Donkey Kong'' for the Game Boy. They first appear in [[Level 1-1 (Mario vs. Donkey Kong)|Level 1-1]]. A Garbage Can's use as a platform is first demonstrated in [[Level 1-3 (Mario vs. Donkey Kong)|Level 1-3]], where Mario can activate a yellow [[Color Switch]] to drop several Garbage Cans on a bed of [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]] and jump from them to move across.

Like Dust Men from ''Donkey Kong'' for Game Boy, Bucket Men disguise themselves as regular Bins, but reveal themselves upon being approached by Mario.

==In {{franchise|Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''===
[[File:SMO Garbage.jpg|thumb|Mario facing a bin and some garbage bags in ''Super Mario Odyssey'']]
Bins are often found in the alleyways of [[New Donk City]] in [[Metro Kingdom]]. Here, garbage bags can be seen next to them. Mario can hit a Bin using either his [[Cap Throw]] move or a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] for a [[coin]].

===''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''===
Trash Bins are commonly found all across [[The Last Resort]]. A Trash Bin contains money that [[Luigi]] can obtain either by searching it, in which can the Trash Bin spits the money out, or by destroying the Trash Bin by pulling on it with a [[Suction Shot]].

===''[[Mario Golf: Super Rush]]''===
Bins make a background appearance in the [[New Donk City (golf course)|New Donk City]] golf course.
