'''World 2-3''' is the third level of [[World 2 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 2]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the sixth level overall in said game.

==Layout==
Throughout the level, many [[Cheep Cheep]]s will be jumping up from the abyss below and falling back down. The level is made up of several broken up platforms, varying in sizes. Several [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s are also placed on the platforms, along with one [[Blooper]] near the center of the level. In the area with the [[? Block]]s, the top-left block holds a power-up. After the second set of tall, tree-like platforms is the [[Goal Pole]].
