The '''police''' are characters in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
They are mask-wearing troopers who serve as guards to the main tennis stadium. They are seen in the opening cinematic and special edition blooper reel outtake compilation. They pursue [[Wario]] and [[Waluigi]] throughout the entrance of the [[Peach Dome]] after the duo used markers to vandalize images on a tournament bracket board until the chase stalls after Wario and Waluigi open a door that leads to a trap.
