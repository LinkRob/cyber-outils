The '''Ancient Minister''' is one of the antagonists who appears in [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. The Ancient Minister is only shown during cutscenes, not gameplay, and most of his cutscene appearances also feature at least one ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' character.

==In ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''==
{{spoiler}}
In the backstory for Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary, the Ancient Minister was once the leader of the [[w:c:supersmashbros:Isle of the Ancients|Isle of Ancients]], named the '''Master Robot''', and he watched over the [[w:c:supersmashbros:World of Trophies|World of Trophies]] along with other [[R.O.B.]]s. When [[Tabuu]] invaded the Isle of Ancients, the Ancient Minister reluctantly joined his side because he did not want to lose all of his R.O.B.s to him.
{{endspoiler}}
