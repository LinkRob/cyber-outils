"'''Will the Real Elvis Please Shut Up!'''" is the ninth live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the animated episode "[[Love 'Em and Leave 'Em]]."

==Plot==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] starts out by singing a song in a mock [[Elvis Presley|Elvis]] voice and pretending to be Elvis. Later, [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] comes in singing an altered version of "{{w|Heart Break Hotel}}." The two argue over who's the better Elvis impersonator, both claiming the other to be a disgrace. Finally, the "real" Elvis shows up to aid the Marios in becoming better Elvis impersonators.
He mocks Mario’s hair and then teaches them some techniques like the “Elvis lip twist.”  He also teaches them some dance steps but Mario knocks down a wall.  Elvis claims that Mario's doing great much to Luigi's dismay.

The Marios claim that working with Elvis was a dream come true but they can't go against each other.  Elvis suggests they play together then leaves
