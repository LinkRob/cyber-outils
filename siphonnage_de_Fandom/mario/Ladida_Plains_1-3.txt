}}'''Ladida Plains 1-3''' is the third level of [[Ladida Plains]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. The music changes back to its normal music. The level starts with Peach on top of the orange platform.

==Features==
*When Perry and Peach jump in the water, [[Perry]] starts to act like a [[Bowlbrella]].

==Toads==
*'''Toad 1''': Climb a bit higher on the plant and go right to the orange platform.
*'''Toad 2''': Go through the pipe going up, then go up and move left with the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy vibe]].
*'''Toad 3''': Use the Joy vibe to shrink the yellow cloud-shaped fluff, then go pass through.

==Puzzle Piece==
*Use the Rage vibe to burn the first bridge.
