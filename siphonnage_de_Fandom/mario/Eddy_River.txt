The '''Eddy River''' is one of the locations featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. While usually peaceful, the Eddy River has been flooded by [[Olivia]], and features fast-moving currents as a result. [[Mario]] and his party have to take the Eddy River at one point during their journey.
