'''Allsand Island''' is a location appearing in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is the eighth major island to be linked to [[Shipshape Island]], and is the last major island found in the [[Color-Full Sea]], appearing to the west, just north of [[Color-Full Sea#Islets|Dinkee Islet]].

During the fly-in animation, while [[Mario (character)|Mario]] rolls a short distance before springing up and posing, [[Luigi]] loses his hat, which gets caught on a [[Cactus]] before he fishes it off.

== Description ==
As can be told from its name, Allsand Island is a desert island with [[Quicksand]] pools that can be traversed with the [[Bros. Ball]] transformation.

== History ==
To begin their assault on the [[Color-Full Sea]]'s [[Great Lighthouse Islands|Great Lighthouse Island]], [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are tasked with getting some Stickem Oil from Allsand Island to fuel a rocket that [[Technikki]] built. Along the way, they find some [[Quicksand]] pools can be traversed with the [[Bros. Ball]] transformation. [[Sharkbone|Sharkbones]] infest many of the pools throughout the island, and are best avoided according to a [[Concordian]] citizen because they are too strong. The Sharkbones are still fightable and killable, however, despite their strength; taking too long, though, allows them to flee.

Mario and Luigi eventually find the Stickem Oil at the far end of a large quicksand pit. [[Luigi]] uses his [[Luigi Logic]] skill to distract the Sharkbones by hanging himself from a tree while Mario crosses over to retrieve the oil. After Mario gets the oil, he and Luigi proceed to connect the island to [[Shipshape Island]]'s [[Uni-Tree]].

Upon relinking the island, [[Connie]] travels there. When found by the Bros., she tells them about a memory that she had when she and [[Cozette]] visited the island before Cozette vanished. In the memory, Connie fell into a quicksand pit, and Cozette saved her. After saving Connie, Cozette says that she may need to be rescued by Connie one day.

The island appears during the credits, where the [[IDLE]] gang poses for someone.

== Layout ==

=== Starting Area ===
This is a small sand platform that has a hut with a [[Mystery Block]] in it at the south where [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] land to begin their mission. A [[Warp Pipe]] leads to the oasis near the centre.

=== Quicksand Pools ===
To bypass the quicksand, Mario and Luigi must use the [[Bros. Ball]] to roll across each pool. If they do not roll quickly, they will get pulled under.

=== The Oasis ===
The island's centre is marked by this pool of water, which is inhabited by a [[Whirlpool Spirit|spirit that manifests as a talking whirlpool]]. Mario and Luigi can talk to the spirit by standing on a small peninsula at the south of the oasis. The spirit demands to be fed several items. If the Bros. succeed, they can get a [[Battle Plug]] that can cause enemies to instantly die, although [[Seedle|Seedles]] will spawn to continue the fight.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Allsand Island is called ''Sunabakka'' (written スナバッカ), meaning "only sand" in the Katakana script. In Chinese, it has its name translated literally from English, resulting in ''Quánshā'' (written 全沙). Spanish refers to Allsand as ''Desertotal'', from ''desierto'' ("desert") and ''total'' ("total"). Italian refers to it as ''Sáblia'', fro ''sábbia'' ("sand") and ''ólio'' ("oil"). French translates its name as ''Cactusia'', from "cactus." Allsand's Dutch name, ''Allessant'', is derived from ''alles'' ("everything") and a devoicing of ''zand'' ("sand"). In Korean, it is called ''Moraeman'' ("sand-only"; written 모래만).
