'''World 4-5''' is a level found in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros.|New Super Mario Bros]]''.

==Overview==
It can be unlocked by clearing {{world-link|4|ghost|World 4-Ghost House (New Super Mario Bros.)}}.

==Layout==
This is an underground level with [[Bob-omb]]s and platforms that act like drawbridges. Some [[Brick Block]]s can only be destroyed by blowing up a Bob-omb, which is needed for Mario to get past some areas. This skill is also used to get the first [[Star Coin]]. To get the second Star Coin, the player must wait until a drawbridge goes up. Next, he must hit the Bob-omb above him, destroying some blocks, allowing him to get the coin. After a few more drawbridges, the player should notice one that has a pipe with blocks covering it. If Mario blows up a Bob-omb on top of the Pipe, he can enter it and, by breaking more blocks with Bob-ombs, he can get the last Star Coin. When Mario leaves the [[Warp Pipe]], he will reach the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].
==Enemies==
* [[Bob-omb]]s
* Red [[Koopa Troopa]]
* [[Spiny|Spinies]]

==[[Star Coins]]==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' To get the first Star Coin, Mario should blow up a Bob-omb near the side of a box of Brick Block.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' When the drawbridge goes up, a Bob-omb can be detonated and the Star Coin can be collected.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' A Bob-omb can be used to destroy blocks that are blocking a pipe. Inside, five Bob-ombs are available to destroy the blocks so Mario can grab the Star Coin.
