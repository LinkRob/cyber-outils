A '''frog'''<ref>''Yoshi's Story'' Player's Guide, pages 10, 90, and 91.</ref><ref>''Nintendo 64 Game Secrets, 1999 Edition'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide, page 111.</ref> is an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==History==
Frogs are found in [[Neuron Jungle]], and they swarm any [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] crossing the lagoon. They may randomly spawn on the ground or in the water, with tiny ones doing so exclusively in the latter. In both cases, they hop in a Yoshi's direction along the surface of either the land or the water. Certain normal-sized ones wait in fixed locations on land, hopping after Yoshis when they catch sight of them. Although any Yoshi can eat them, the Green Yoshi likes them best.
