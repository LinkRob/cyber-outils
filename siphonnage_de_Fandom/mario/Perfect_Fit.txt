'''Perfect Fit''' is an unlockable [[2 vs. 2 Minigame|2-vs-2]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
Two block pieces appear on the screen and rotate and align together in a cube. The cube gets off the screen.

== Gameplay ==
Teams must work together to rotate the two block pieces in order to align into a cube. Teams must connect three pairs to win the minigame.

== Ending ==
The winning team standing on their cube does their victory animation.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|joy-con}} – Rotate

==In-game description==
*''"Fit the block pieces together!"''
*''"Rotate {{button|ns|joy-con-l}} and align the two halves of the block!"''
