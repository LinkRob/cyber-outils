|website       = }}'''Takashi Tezuka''' (born November 17, 1960) is a game designer/director for [[Nintendo]]. He is a graduate of the Design Department of Osaka of University of Arts and is currently a General Manager. 

== Work ==
Tezuka is known for his work with the earlier ''Mario'' games such as ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World]]''. He is also known for helping with some games from the ''The'' [[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda series|''Legend of Zelda'' series]]. Tezuka is also helps create some of the ''Yoshi'' games. Tezuka helped with the [[Wii U]] and is currently helping produce another ''Yoshi'' game known as ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.
