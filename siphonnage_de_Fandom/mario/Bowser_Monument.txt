The '''Bowser Monument''',<ref>''Mario Kart 8'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Page 130.</ref> (also named '''Lava Bowser''' or '''Bowser Golem''') is a character who first appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Bowser Monument is a massive [[Bowser Statue]] composed of molten rock, with visible [[lava]] highlights.

===Behavior===
Unlike Bowser Statues or the comparable [[Mechanical Bowser]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', the Bowser Monument attacks by [[punch]]ing the race course, causing the road to shake, instead of breathing [[fireball]]s. The Bowser Monument can also punch a [[Dash Panel]] on each side of the track.

==In ''Mario Kart 8'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''==
Bowser Monument is found in [[Bowser's Castle (Wii U)|Bowser's Castle]]. It is the centerpiece of the course, where it appears in the middle of the two paths. The Bowser Monument is the first shown during the course's intro. Racers can perform [[trick]]s off of the ripples created by the Bowser Monument.

==In [[Super Nintendo World]]==
Lava Bowser is in the [[Grumble Volcano]] area of [[Mario Kart: Bowser's Challenge]]. The goal of the attendees is to throw [[Koopa Shell]]s at Lava Bowser to eventually destroy it.
