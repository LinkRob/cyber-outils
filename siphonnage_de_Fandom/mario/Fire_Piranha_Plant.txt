A '''Fire Piranha Plant''' is a variety of [[Piranha Plant]] that can shoot [[fireball]]s from its mouth. They are colored black with orange spots. They were introduced in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' as relatives of [[Venus Fire Trap]]s. They are commonly found in caves or sky areas. In more recent games, Fire Piranha Plants have replaced Venus Fire Traps as the common fire-spitting Piranha Plants.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario 3D Land''====
[[Image:Fire Piranha Plant 3D.png|thumb|left|A Fire Piranha Plant as seen in ''Super Mario 3D Land''.]]
Fire Piranha Plants make their debut in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' alongside the [[Inky Piranha Plant]]. They remain perfectly still but as soon as [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) approaches one, they will take aim and spit a fireball at them. Fire Piranha Plants can be found in almost all of the game's worlds commonly in caverns or skies. They can be defeated with basically any attack as well. Upon defeat, a Fire Piranha Plant may drop a [[Fire Flower]].

====''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''====
[[Image:Fire Piranha Plant 3D New.jpg|thumb|right|Two Fire Piranha Plants near the [[Clear Pipe]]s in ''Super Mario 3D World''.]]
Fire Piranha Plants return in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. In these games, they are semi-common enemies appearing in various different themes (mainly skies). Their attacks and ways to be defeated are the same as before however their chances of dropping a [[Fire Flower]] are higher in this game than in ''Super Mario 3D Land''. They return in the game's [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury|port]], where their [[fireball]]s are now more realistic-sounding. The [[Bowser's Fury]] side of the port also introduces [[Cat Fire Piranha Plant]]s.
{{br}}

====''Super Mario Maker 2''====
Fire Piranha Plants return in ''Super Mario Maker 2'' as placeable enemies exclusive to the ''Super Mario 3D World'' style replacing the [[Venus Fire Trap]]s in that style. They behave just as they did in ''Super Mario 3D World'' but in a 2D environment and can be defeated in the same ways. A Fire Piranha Plant can be given a [[Super Mushroom]] to become a [[Big Fire Piranha Plant]] or wings to become a [[Para-Fire Piranha Plant]]. To make a Fire Piranha Plant, you can shake a regular Piranha Plant.
{{br}}

====''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''====
[[File:SMBW Magma Flare-Up Plant.jpg|thumb|Fire Piranha Plants in [[KO Arena Magma Flare-Up]]]]
Fire Piranha Plants reappear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', acting the same as in previous appearances, but can spit multiple fireballs at once. It is their first appearance in a 2D installment of the {{series|Super Mario}}. They appear in several levels of the game such as [[KO Arena Magma Flare-Up]]. They can be defeated in several ways such as with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Bubble Flower]], [[Elephant Fruit]], [[Drill Mushroom]], or the [[Star]]. The [[Big Fire Piranha Plant]] also returns in this game.
{{br}}
===''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''===
[[Image:Fire Piranha Plant in the Dark.png|thumb|left|A Fire Piranha Plant taking an aim at the evasive Toadette.]]
Fire Piranha Plants return in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' and both it's ports on the [[Nintendo 3DS]] and [[Nintendo Switch]]. They behave identically to how they did in ''Super Mario 3D World'', spitting fireballs at the heroes whenever they get close however because [[Captain Toad]] and [[Toadette]] cannot jump, defeating them just got a bit more difficult. The Toads can beat them by either landing on them from above or by using a [[Super Pickax]] on them. They are commonly found in darker levels such as [[Shy Guy Shadow Den]]. Fire Piranha Plants are also useful in the sense that they can be used to light unlit torches.

===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
[[File:Fire Piranha Plant Fire Bro.png|thumb|right|A Fire Piranha Plant has teamed up with a [[Fire Bro]].]]
Fire Piranha Plants appear as enemies in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. They can attack by spitting fireballs thus doing damage. Any variety of attack can be used to bring one down. They can also be added as allies as well.

===''Mario Tennis Aces''===
[[File:Fire Piranha Plant model MTA.png|thumb|left|The playable Fire Piranha Plant in ''Mario Tennis Aces''.]]
Fire Piranha Plants appear in ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]''. They appear in [[Piranha Plant Forest]] as stage hazards where if they catch a ball, they will fire a [[Zone Shot]] at the opposing player. They also appear in the mission [[Rapid Fire]] in Adventure Mode, where they also fire Zone Shots.

One Fire Piranha Plant appears as a playable Technical character, appearing in a pot and holding its racket in its mouth. It was available as a participation bonus in the tournament that took place from June 1, 2019 to June 30, 2019 and became automatically unlocked for all players after July 1, 2019. This marks the first time a Fire Piranha Plant is playable.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
While they do not appear in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Fire Piranha Plants are mentioned briefly by [[Viridi]] during the [[List of Palutena's Guidance conversations|Palutena's Guidance]] dialogue for the [[Piranha Plant]] fighter. Piranha Plant also spits fire from its mouth for its back air attack, referencing both Fire Piranha Plants and Venus Fire Traps.

==Trivia==
*Their colors of orange and black slightly resemble [[Fiery Dino Piranha]]’s colors.
*Their coloration change and name seems to suggest that Fire Piranha Plants are regular Piranha Plants that have donned a [[Fire Flower]] like Mario, this is further proven by the fact that they drop a Fire Flower upon defeat.
*Their colors also slightly resemble those of the [[Inky Piranha Plant]] and the [[Spiky Piranha Plant]].
