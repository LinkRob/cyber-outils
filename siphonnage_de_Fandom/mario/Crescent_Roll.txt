'''Crescent Roll''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'', as one of [[Dr. Crygor]]'s microgames, and reappears in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Ashley]] & [[Red]]'s microgames, part of the Fantasy set.

==Layout==
In ''WarioWare: Twisted!'', the sky in the background is a gradient of green and blue. In ''WarioWare Gold'', the sky is purple and instead has a light-colored gradient at the bottom, making it appear more as if it takes place at sunset, and a few five-pointed stars were added into the background, and pine trees are in the nearby distance.

== Gameplay ==
The object is for the player to tilt the [[Mischievous Moon]] so that any devil that appears falls off. If they do not accomplish this before the timer runs out, they lose at the microgame. Later difficulty settings share this objective, but it adds two children, [[Toby]] and [[Menta]], whom the player must take caution to not have fall off while tilting the moon. The devil never appears in the middle, so as to prevent a [[softlock]] from occurring.

*'''Level 1:''' A devil is on the moon.
*'''Level 2:''' A devil and a boy and girl are on the moon.
*'''Level 3:''' The boy and girl are sitting between the two devils on the moon.

==Behind the scenes==
=== Etymology ===
Crescent Roll's name comes from a croissant (which can alternatively be called a "crescent roll"), while also referencing the crescent moon.
