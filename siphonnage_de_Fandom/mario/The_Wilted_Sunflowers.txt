'''The Wilted Sunflowers''' is the fourth episode of [[Pinna Park]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Objective==
[[Mario]] has to defeat five [[Snooza Koopa]]s at the beach at the start. They are gnawing away at the roots of the [[Sunflower Kid]]s and the [[Great Sunflower]], causing them to wilt. Each time Mario defeats one of the first four Snooza Koopas, a [[Sunflower Kid]] is restored from its wilted state. Defeating the fifth and final Snooza Koopa restores the [[Great Sunflower]], who rewards the [[Shine Sprite]].
