The '''Monster Maniac''' is a minor character from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. He is a Concordian man who can be found in various places in [[Concordia]]. He is also related to a Concordian man named [[Dewey Fightem]].

==Description==
The Monster Maniac is a light grey Concordian with a black moustache. His outfit consists of a light blue coat, dark grey trousers, a gold necklace, and red-rimmed sunglasses.

==Role in ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership''==
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can meet the Monster Maniac in various locations, and each encounter with him has a different kind of monster which is actually an altered, strengthened version of an enemy they fought earlier. 

*[[Merrygo Island]]: This encounter with the Monster Maniac has strengthened [[Krode|Krodes]] called "Krode R's" that have tougher shells (and thus a higher defense). Mario and Luigi can counter this with their Surprise Iron Ball [[Battle Plug]], which drops a [[Spike Ball]] that tears through the shell.
*[[Raynforst Island]]: This encounter with the Monster Maniac has strengthened Hydro [[Seedle|Seedles]] whose healing powers have amplified healing powers. This can be countered by [[Dizzy|stunning]] them with the Dizzifying Attack Battle Plug.
*[[Brrrning Sea#Islets|Petit Islet]]: This encounter with the Monster Maniac has the Curlup R, which is a [[Curlup]] with a disproportionately large amount of health. The player can get around this by applying the Fiery Attack Battle Plug to [[Burn|set the Curlup R on fire]].
*[[Offandon Island]]: During this encounter, the Monster Maniac sics some [[Bob-omb|Bob-ombs]] whose blasts can instantly kill the Bros., who can prevent this by using the Seedlise Battle Plug to turn the Bob-ombs into regular [[Seedle|Seedles]].
*[[Lottacoins Island]]: During this encounter, [[Mettozokettohei|Mettozokettoheitachi]] attack the Bros. with their gimmick being that they have to be slain all at once. If the Bros. fail to kill all of them, the survivors will resurrect the fallen members. The Monster Maniac claims that the Mettozokettoheitachi gorged on a lot of [[1-Up Mushroom|1-Up Mushrooms]], explaining their resurrection ability.
