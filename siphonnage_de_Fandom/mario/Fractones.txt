'''Fractones''' are shape-changing, crystalline denizens of the [[Underground World]] who were introduced in [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']].

== Description ==
Fractones appear to default in appearance as a blue crystal with prominent eyes and stubby legs. Typically friendly and enterprising, they can change their shape as desired and frequently default to a form and name that's indicative of their profession: Constructones wear hardhats, Architectones appear in the shape of a house, Chiptones appear as large [[Banandium Chips]] and so on.

They're indifferent to being broken into pieces, as their bodies restore to normal moments later. Whether for curiosity or for commerce, they enjoy traversing the many sublayers of the [[Mushroom Earth|planet's]] interior.
