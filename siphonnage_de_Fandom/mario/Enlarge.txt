'''Enlarge''' is a move in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Enlarge is a special ability exclusively used by [[Mario]], [[Baby Mario]], and the [[Toad (species)|Toad]] characters (including [[Toadette]] and [[Toadsworth]]) while running toward home base during a close play. If the player quickly presses the right button while any of said characters are running during a close play, their Enlarge ability is activated, in which the character grows larger and knocks away whoever defending the base. As a result, this can also knock the ball out of the base defender's glove and sending it rolling elsewhere on the field, possibly allowing the other runners to advance a base.
