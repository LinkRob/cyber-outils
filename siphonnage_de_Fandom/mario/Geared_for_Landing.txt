'''Geared for Landing''' is [[Young Cricket]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

== Gameplay ==

The plane must go through the gate by the player opening one of the gates turning the level. To open the top gate, the player must rotate the [[Wii Remote]] counterclockwise. The player must rotate the Wii Remote clockwise to opening the bottom gate. If the plane crashes the spikes, the player loses.
*'''Level 1:''' A plane goes through one of the gates.
*'''Level 2:''' A plane goes through one of the gates, the other is the fake plane.
*'''Level 3:''' A chance of a fake plane. The player must open one of the gates the other way.
