'''Chakroads''' are a type of object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. They are portals placed around the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] that allow [[Bowser]] to travel from one Chakroad to another, although only if he has discovered them. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] cannot use Chakroads.
