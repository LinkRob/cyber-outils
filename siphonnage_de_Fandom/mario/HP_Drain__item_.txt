An '''HP Drain''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', in which it can be bought in certain [[Item Shop]]s.

==Effects==
Using an HP Drain deals 5 damage to an enemy and also restores 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] to [[Mario]] or any of his partners who have used this item.
