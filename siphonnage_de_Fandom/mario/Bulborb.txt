'''Bulborbs''', also named '''Red Bulborbs''' are one of the main enemies in the ''Pikmin'' series. They have made a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related crossover appearances, especially in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. Their first ''Mario''-related is in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Their appearance consists of a red, ball-shaped body with white dots and a yellow face with two eye stalks and thin, short legs.

===Behavior===
Bulborbs are one of the main predators of [[Pikmin (species)|Pikmin]].
