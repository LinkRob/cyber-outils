The '''Llama's Temple''' is a minor location in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. It is located within [[Angry Aztec]] and is one of three temples that can be explored alongside the [[Monkey Temple]] and the [[Five Doored Temple]].

==In ''Donkey Kong 64''==
The Llama's Temple is owned by the [[Llama]]. It can be accessed in the second main area of Angry Aztec. Any Kong with their respective weapons can enter the temple and each has at least one [[Golden Banana]] that can be found inside of the temple. The temple is fairly large in size as it requires another set of [[Bananaport Pad]]s just for the inside.
