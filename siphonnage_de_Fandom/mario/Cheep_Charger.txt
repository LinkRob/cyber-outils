The '''Cheep Charger''' is a lightweight kart from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' with good [[acceleration]].

==Information==
The Cheep Charger appears to be [[Cheep-Cheep]] shaped car with the character emblems on the side of the kart.

The Cheep Charger is unlocked by at least getting a star rating on all 50cc [[Retro Stage|Retro Cups]], or by playing 1,800 races.

In [[Mario Kart Tour]] it is an easy kart to unlock. There is also a green unlockable version of the kart called the Green Cheep Charger.

Its relatively weak stats are speed and weight, while it has got good acceleration.

[[Koopa Troopa]] uses a Cheep Charger in the [[Staff Ghost]]s for [[Koopa Cape]] and [[Shy Guy Beach]] (Nin☆Rose and Nin☆Kato).
