'''{{Infobox Enemy|name=Sombrero Guy|image=PMCS Sombrero Guy.png|caption=|first=''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''|games=|homeland=|located=|leader=|species=[[Shy Guy]]|subspecies=|affiliates=|related=|attacks=Throws its hat at Mario.|defeated=|comics=}}'''Sombrero Guys''' are a type of [[Shy Guy]] that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]].''

== Description ==
Their appearance is that of a normal [[Shy Guy]], except they wear a sombrero and play a guitar. Along with having attacks, he acts as a support for other enemies.

== Stats ==
'''HP:''' 30

'''Attacks:'''

* Hat Attack ('''13''')
* Group Attack ('''13''')
* Blowback Attack ('''12''')
* Cheer Power (Power-Up Status)
* Cheer Heal (Heals '''60''' HP to allies)
* Run (Runs from battle)

[[File:PMCS Sombrero Guy Card.png|left|frameless|83x83px]]

=== Price ===
Sell Price: 3 coins

=== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ===
The Sombrero Guy is only seen in [[Sunset Express]], and can still be found in post game.
