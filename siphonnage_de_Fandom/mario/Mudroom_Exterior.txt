The '''Mudroom Exterior''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is located right beside the [[Mudroom]] and can be accessed anytime once Luigi obtains the [[Strobulb]]. The Mudroom Exterior doesn't contain any key items nor it needs to be explored but the small room does contain a bit of money, gold bars, and a Gem.
