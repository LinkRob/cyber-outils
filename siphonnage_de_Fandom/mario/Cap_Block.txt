'''Cap Blocks''' are a type of [[block]] featured in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. The most recurring type of Cap Block is the [[Metal Box]] (originally named '''Green Block'''), which appears as an item throughout the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]].

==In ''Super Mario 64''==
In ''Super Mario 64'', the Cap Blocks come in red, green, and blue colors, and hold the [[Wing Cap]], [[Metal Cap]] and [[Vanish Cap]] respectively. They cannot be used at first because [[Mario]] has to activate the corresponding Cap Switch. The Cap Blocks with a Wing Cap are named Red Blocks, and their corresponding Cap Switch is at the [[Tower of the Wing Cap]]. The Cap Blocks with a Metal Cap are named Green Block, and its corresponding Cap Switch is found in the [[Cavern of the Metal Cap]] within [[Hazy Maze Cave]]. 

==In ''Super Mario 64 DS''==
The Red, Green, and Blue Blocks are all replaced with red [[? Block]]s, which contain a [[Power Flower (Super Mario 64 DS)|Power Flower]] or, in some cases, a [[Wing Cap]], which is only usable by Mario. All ? Blocks become usable upon activating the single Cap Switch in the Tower of the Wing Cap.
