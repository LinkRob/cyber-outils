'''Fire Stalking Piranha Plants''' are a variant of both [[Stalking Piranha Plant]]s and [[Venus Fire Trap]]s. They first appear in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', both as enemies and recruitable allies. Fire Stalking Piranha Plants have the ability to spit out [[fireball]]s.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
[[File:Fire Stalking Piranha Plant sprite.png|frame|left]]
Fire Stalking Piranha Plants are both enemies and allies which exclusively appear in the ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'' campaign, in which they are first encountered in the level [[Hunger Pains]]. Fire Stalking Piranha Plants are Ranged troopers which attack by spitting [[fireball]]s at enemies. A Fire Stalking Piranha Plant's special attack is [[Spitfire Fury]], which allows it to spit several fireballs at a targeted enemy.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''===
Fire Stalking Piranha Plants return as both enemies and allies, retaining exclusivity to the game's campaign, ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'', while remaining absent from the ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' remake. Fire Stalking Piranha Plants act similar as in ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'', and retain Spitball Fury as their special. Fire Stalking Piranha Plants are effective against [[Treevil]]s, [[Beehoss]]es, [[Pokey]]s, and other Fire Stalking Piranha Plants.
