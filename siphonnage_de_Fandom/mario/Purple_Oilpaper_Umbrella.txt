'''Purple Oilpaper Umbrella''' is a glider in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Purple Oilpaper Umbrella is a High-End glider in ''Tour''. Its special skill is [[Mushroom Plus]], which increases the driver's chance of getting a [[Mushroom]] from an [[Item Box]]. The player gains points every time a Mushroom is used.
