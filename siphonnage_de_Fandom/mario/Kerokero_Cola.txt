'''Kerokero Cola''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
[[File:Kerokero Cola SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
Kerokero Cola is often viewed as one of the most useful and expensive items in the game. Drinking a Kerokero Cola restores all [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Flower Point|FP]] for all party members. However, purchasing Kerokero Cola requires [[Mario]]'s party to have the [[Soprano Card]], which can only be received after helping [[Toadofsky]] at [[Melody Bay]].

==Trivia==
*The "kerokero" name is the Japanese onomatopoeia for a frog's croaking.
