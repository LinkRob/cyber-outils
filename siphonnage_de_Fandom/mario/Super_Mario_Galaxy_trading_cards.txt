'''''Super Mario Galaxy'' trading cards''' are official trading cards produced by Enter-Play to promote the release of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. The packs that the trading cards came in are titled "Fun Paks," and they each contain four trading cards, a pop-out standee card, and a tattoo card.
