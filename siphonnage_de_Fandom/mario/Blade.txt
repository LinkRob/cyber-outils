The '''Blade''' is a large [[airship]] utilized by the [[Axem Rangers]] during the events of ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
The Blade is a massive battleship that resembles a giant axe. He has a great eye in the middle of his blade-like nose, and two smaller axes hang from his sides. His main weapon is a massive face-shaped energy cannon that can fire the powerful Breaker Beam. Original artwork portrayed the Blade as somewhat greyer, while the game itself gives him a brown tint.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
The Blade was first mentioned by [[Yaridovich]] where he attempted to escape with the [[Star Piece (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Piece]] from [[Seaside Town]]. However, the Axem Rangers with the Blade were running late and did not appear. It was not until [[Barrel Volcano]] where the Blade and the Axem Rangers made their first appearance. The Axem Rangers stole the Star Piece from [[Mario]] and attempted to escape on their Blade. However, Mario catches up to them and they all fight on board the Blade. After all of the Axem Rangers are defeated, Axem Red hops up higher and can use the [[Breaker Beam]] function on the Blade. Mario's team eventually fought this off as well and the Blade was destroyed for good.
