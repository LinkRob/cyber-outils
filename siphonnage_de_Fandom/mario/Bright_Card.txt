}}The '''Bright Card''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
The Bright Card allows access into [[Grate Guy's Casino]] that is hidden very well in [[Bean Valley]]. However, in order to get the Bright Card, [[Mario]] must head back to [[Booster Tower]] where he will find [[Knife Guy]] in a hallway. If Mario completes Knife Guy's game, he will give Mario the Bright Card. The Bright Card can also be sold to a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] for a number of coins (or Frog Coins).

==Trivia==
*The "Viva" logo located on the artwork of the Bright Card is a reference to the "Visa" card.
