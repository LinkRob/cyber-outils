'''Sean''' is a tennis player featured in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]]. He is partnered with [[Sammi]] in doubles. Sean is a unlockable playable character of the All-Around type, and he can be unlocked by winning against him in the Mario Tour in Doubles in [[Island Open]].
