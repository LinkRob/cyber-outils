A '''Ukiki Capsule''' is a type of [[orb|capsule]] that appears only in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. It can be thrown onto a [[space]] on the [[board]], or used on the player for ten [[coin]]s. Whoever lands on a space containing that capsule summons [[Ukiki]], who is hanging on a vine. The player uses a roulette wheel to decide whom Ukiki should attack. Ukiki then comes to the chosen rival and ambushes them, stealing all their capsules and using them on the board on random spaces. If the player chosen has a capsule that cannot be thrown, such as a [[Miracle Capsule]], the space that capsule lands on will have no effect, whether it had a capsule event on it or not.
