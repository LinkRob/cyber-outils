An '''Escape Spring''' is an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
Escape Springs are small, red, coiled-up springs with a light gray pad on top depicting a smiling face. When [[Wario]] steps onto an Escape Spring, it launches him up into the air.

==In ''Wario's World''==
An Escape Spring appears in every secret [[Trapdoor (Wario World)|trapdoor]] area, and Wario can use them to escape after obtaining all of its items. There is also an Escape Spring in [[Unithorn's Lair]], where it is hidden under a random crate. Wario has to find the correct crate and break it so that he can use its Escape Spring to launch out of the area and back into the main course.
