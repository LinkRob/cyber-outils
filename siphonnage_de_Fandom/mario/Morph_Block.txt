The '''Morph Block''' was a block exclusive to ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

== Description ==
When hit by [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], in morph form, the block will revert him back to regular Yoshi, without being brought back to the [[Morph Bubble]].
