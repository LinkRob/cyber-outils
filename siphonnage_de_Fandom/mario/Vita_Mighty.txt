'''Vita Mighties''' are items in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. 

==History==
Whenever [[Wario]] obtains one, one health point gets restored back to his maximum amount of health. To obtain one, the player must complete a minigame of [[Color Me Happy]], which only appear in some green colored treasure chests. Some of the later levels in the game require more of the bottle to be colored in. Only five Vita Mighties can be obtained in the game and they can be found in: [[S.S. Caviar]], [[Sneezemore Cave]], [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid]], [[Sweatmore Peak]], and [[Carpaccio's Lab]].
