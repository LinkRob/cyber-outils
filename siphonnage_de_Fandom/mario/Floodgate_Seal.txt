The '''Floodgate Seal''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Floodgate Seal is a green key that has a swirly design on it, as [[Wario]] describes it.

==History==
The Floodgate Seal is one of four main keys that can be found in [[Ancient Waterworks]]. This item can be found in the lower left portion of the level that can be accessed by hitting a switch with [[Cosmic Wario]]'s laser.  This key is used to unlock a door to the left that leads to the giant waterfall.
