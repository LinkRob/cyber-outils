The '''Membership Card''' is an item that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is a special card that recognizes members on the [[S.S. Chuckola]]. It also identifies members as [[Bink]]'s sidekick.

==Description==
The Membership Card appears to be a small, gold and orange card.

==History==
The Membership Card is mainly used to help gain access to the room that a [[S.S. Chuckola Crew]] member took the [[Beanstar]] piece into. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can acquire a Membership Card by winning Bink's [[Barrel (minigame)|barrel]] minigame.
