The '''Yoshi Doll''' appears in ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening]]''. It is a doll of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].

== In ''The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening'' ==
Yoshi made an appearance as a doll in ''Link's Awakening''. It is a prize won from the Trendy Village in Mabe Village.
