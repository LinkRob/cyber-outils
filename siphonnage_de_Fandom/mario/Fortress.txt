'''Fortresses''' are a recurring type of location in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

== In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Bros.''===
[[File:Fortress SMB.png|frame|left]]
A fortress is placed behind a [[Goal Pole|Flag Pole]] in most [[level]]s. They are made up of brown bricks. When [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] enter into a Fortress, the remaining [[Time Limit|time]] counts down, adding to the score. They can then progress to the next level.

In the third [[level]] of a [[world]], a [[castle]] instead appears behind the Flag Pole, as the last level of a world takes place within a castle. From the outside, a castle is shown to be made up of the same brown bricks as fortresses, but are a lot larger than them.

===''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''===
Fortresses have the same role as in ''Super Mario Bros.'', but with a slight graphical enhancement.

===''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''===
Specific fortresses known as '''Mini-Fortresses''' appear in several of the worlds, all featuring a boss fight against [[Boom Boom]] at the end.

===''[[Super Mario World]]''===
Fortress levels make an appearance like in ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', but there are fewer of them. Mario or Luigi now have to fight [[Reznor]]s at the end of a fortress level.

===[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' series]]===
Like in ''Super Mario Bros.'', a fortress appears after the Flag Pole at the end of each level. Meanwhile, [[Tower]]s fill in for the fortress levels in ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' and ''Super Mario World''.

===[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]===
{{Main|Fortress (Illumination)}}

A Fortress once again appears in the movie, where [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] finds himself from [[Brooklyn]] to the [[Mushroom Forest]].
