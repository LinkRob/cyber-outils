'''''Golf: Japan Course''''' is a golf game starring [[Mario]] and an installment of the {{series|Golf}}. It was released for the [[Family Computer Disk System]] on February 21, 1987, exclusively in Japan. A few months after the game's release, it was followed by a sequel, ''[[Golf: U.S. Course]]''.
