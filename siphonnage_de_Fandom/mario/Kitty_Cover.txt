'''Kitty Cover''' is [[Kat and Ana]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' in the Nature folder and reappears as [[Mona]] microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player controls the umbrella. The objective is to protect the cat from the rain. The player loses if the cat is under the rain.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The player shades the cat with a wide umbrella.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The player shades the cat with a narrow umbrella.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The player shades the cat with a narrow tilted umbrella. The wind blows the rain to the left.


=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
There were slight design changes to the microgame design.

==''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''==
[[File:SSBB_Kitty_Cover.jpg|thumb|left]]
The microgame appears in the [[WarioWare, Inc. (stage)|WarioWare, Inc.]] stage. When the microgame appears, the characters must shade themselves under the umbrella to get the prize. The cat still remains under the umbrella. The rain causes small damage. The umbrella has a smooth line drawing with a darker colour.
