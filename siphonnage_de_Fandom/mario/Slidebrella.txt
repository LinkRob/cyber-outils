Konbea Kassā|Conveyor Perry}} is one of [[Perry]]'s forms in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Description ==
It gives Perry the ability to slide on ropes upside-down while Peach is riding on him. The player can press {{button|ds|a}} to jump to collect coins or to switch ropes. This ability can be obtained for free at [[Shriek Mansion 3-4]].
