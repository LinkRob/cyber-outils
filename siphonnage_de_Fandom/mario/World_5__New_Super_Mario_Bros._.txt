'''World 5''' is the fifth world in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. This world is unlocked by four methods; by taking the respective [[Warp Cannon]]s of [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|Worlds 1]] or [[World 2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2]], or by completing the [[Castle]] levels of either [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 3]] or [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 4]].

== Layout ==
The world is set in a snowy plain filled with icy mountains and ground, similar to [[Ice Land]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. The boss of this world is [[Petey Piranha]], who has to be defeated by ground-pounding on him three times, allowing access to [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6]], though if the player completes the castle in [[Mini Mario|mini form]], they will gain access to [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]] instead.

This world features 3 [[Mushroom House|mushroom houses]], and a [[cannon]], which leads to [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8]]. This world also features 2 [[Warp Pipe]]s, which connect [[World 5-2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5-2]] to [[World 5-3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5-3]], and [[World 5-B (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5-B]] to {{world-link|5|castlen|World 5-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)}}.

Starting with this world, in reference to ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', [[Hammer Bro]]s can be found on the overworld amongside the flying red [[? Block]]s, and if [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] enters a course with the Hammer Bro on it, they can defeat the Hammer Bro to get any power-up, except the [[Starman]]. And just like the flying red ? Block, Hammer Bros will avoid going to Castles.

== Trivia ==
* World 5 seems to be the snow/ice counterpart to World 2.
** Both worlds feature [[Warp Pipe]]s in the overworld to take Mario/Luigi to other levels.
** The towers and castle levels include environments of the world's theming in some way.
** To unlock secret worlds (Worlds 4 and 7 respectively) Mario/Luigi have to defeat the respective bosses of the castles with the [[Mini Mushroom]] power-up.
** To access the castle, it can either be taken through a Warp Pipe via a secret level [[World 2-A (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-A]] and World 5-B) or complete an auto-scrolling level before the castle ([[World 2-6 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-6]] and World 5-4).
** Elements from World 2 reappear in World 5, such as blocks that extend three times vertically (found in both [[World 2-1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-1]] and World 5-1), [[Swooper]]s (in [[World 2-3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-3]] and World 5-2), [[Hammer Bro]]s (in [[World 2-4 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-4]] and any level of World 5 besides the castle), and [[Spiked Ball]]s ({{World-link|2|castlen|World 2-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)}} and {{world|5|towern}}).
** Both worlds can unlock three other worlds by beating the castle normally, with Mini form, or by Warp Cannons respectively (World 2 can unlock Worlds 3, 4, and 5, and World 5 can unlock Worlds 6, 7, and 8).
* World 5 also shares some similarities to World 3 in terms of overworld layout. 
** Both worlds feature 2 normal levels and an unlockable secret level before the tower level, but unlike in World 3, the first level of World 5 has to be done to play any other level in the world.
** Both towers can be skipped via secret exits in the second normal levels ([[World 3-2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 3-2]] and World 5-2).
** Both worlds have three secret levels, and upon completing the second secret levels ([[World 3-B (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 3-B]] and World 5-B) can unlock the third secret levels ([[World 3-C (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 3-C]] and World 5-C. Both secret levels can also be used as an alternative path to the castle levels, and skipping the world's Ghost Houses.
** Both overworlds also feature one of each type of [[Mushroom House]]s.
