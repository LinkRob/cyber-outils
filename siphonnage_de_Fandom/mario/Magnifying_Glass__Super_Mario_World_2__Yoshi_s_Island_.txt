'''Magnifying Glass''' is an item featured in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. It is one of the items obtainable from winning a [[Bonus Challenge]]. A Magnifying Glass can be used from the Pause Screen, and doing so causes all of the [[Red Coin]]s in the [[level]] to become visible, as they are otherwise disguised as regular [[coin]]s, and causes invisible [[Winged Cloud]]s to become visible.
