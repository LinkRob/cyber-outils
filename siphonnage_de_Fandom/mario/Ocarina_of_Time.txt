'''Ocarina of Time''' is a [[microgame]] found in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', made by [[9-Volt]]. It is based on the [[Nintendo 64]] [[w:c:zelda:Ocarina of Time|game of the same name]]. [[Link]] must pull the [[w:c:zelda:Master Sword|Master Sword]] out of the stone in all three difficulties.

==Gameplay==
*'''Level 1''': The sword is easy to pull out, and after doing so, [[Young Link]] turns into the grown-up Link.
*'''Level 2''': Now, the sword will be somewhat hard to pull out of the stone, and after doing so, Young Link will become older Link with a long, gray beard.
*'''Level 3''': At last, the sword will be hard to pull out, and after doing so, Young Link will now become a [[w:c:zelda:Cucco|Cucco]].

=== ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' ===
This microgame returns as [[5-Volt]]'s microgame for the "Nintendo Classics". The player must keep tapping {{Button|3ds|Padup}}.
