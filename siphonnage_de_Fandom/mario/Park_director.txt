'''Park director''' is an old [[Noki]] who manages the [[Pinna Park]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. He makes an appearance in most of its episodes.

In Pinna Park's first episode [[Mecha-Bowser Appears!]], the park director is the character who has [[Mario]] ride a roller-coaster to fight [[Mecha-Bowser]].
