'''Sprinkler Squirt''' is a move used by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
Sprinkler Squirt is an extension of the [[Spin Jump]] move but involves Mario using [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]] to spray water in all directions. Sprinkler Squirt can prove useful in episodes where large portions of an area are covered in [[goop]], such as [[Sirena Beach]]'s [[Scrubbing Sirena Beach]].
