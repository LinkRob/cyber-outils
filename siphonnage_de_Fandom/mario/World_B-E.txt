'''World B-E''' is a glitched [[level]] in the games ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' as well as its remasters ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario All-Stars + Super Mario World]]''.

== Gameplay ==
World B-E starts in a bonus room that is seen in a few of the letter worlds from earlier or later levels in the game, the bonus room includes both the [[Super Mushroom|Mushroom]] and [[Poison Mushroom]] inside the second and second to last bricks. There are exactly 12 [[Coins]] inside the box of bricks and they can be obtained by bumping the bricks up. In this glitched level you can actually get inside the box of bricks by going inside the pipe which will glitch [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to go upwards into the box making them get stuck inside until you save and quit the game on the [[Super Mario All-Stars|Super Mario all-stars]] remaster, because if you play on the original [[Family Computer Disk System|Famicon]] you will stay stuck inside with no way of getting out of it.

== Transition Screen/Selection Screen ==

=== Transition screen ===
Once you transition to this level by selecting it, you will see a plains map with platforms varying height and length along with a red black gradient wall in the background. The level also shows a bowser although it is not a castle level.

=== Selection screen ===
In your select menu screen, you will see text that says “World Bw->?” Instead of the real level name B-E, the reason for this is because the selection screen doesn’t have the correct sprites to show the real level name which is why it shows a triangle and question mark to represent it.

== Trivia ==
* Speed runner Kosmic has got to this glitched level before, and made a very detailed video explaining these glitched levels.
* The levels are glitched enough to look weird in the selector screen.
*This is the first level in the game to start in a bonus room.
*These levels were discovered after 39 years as of Nov 13, 2025
