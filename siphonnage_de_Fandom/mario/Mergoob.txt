'''Mergoob''' is a type of [[Goob]] which appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. They are only found on the [[ScareScraper]]'s Spectral Catch-themed floor. Mergoobs have 200 HP.

==Physical appearance==
Mergoobs are mermaid-themed Goobs that wear mermaid clothing. They are similar to normal Goobs that both kinds have blue bodies, white eyes and mouth, and one tail. Mergoobs act in the same ways as regular Goobs. 

==Description==
In ScareScraper, Mergoobs might appear if there is at least one [[Luigi]] wears pirate clothing. They can appear in any room, the way to defeat them is to flash them and vacuum them up.
