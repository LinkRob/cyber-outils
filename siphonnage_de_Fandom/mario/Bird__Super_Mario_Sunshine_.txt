'''Birds''' are a species in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Birds are parrot-like creatures either green, blue, gold, or red in color.

===Behavior===
Birds are usually found perched in high areas. Like [[Little Bird]]s in later ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' installments, a Bird flies away if [[Mario]] approaches it. However, a Bird specifically flies in a set path before returning to their original location. Mario can use [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]] to spray a Bird until it releases an item, with green Birds releasing a [[Coin]] each, blue Birds releasing a [[Blue Coin|blue coin]] each, gold Birds releasing a [[Shine Sprite]] each, and red Birds releasing a [[Red Coin|red coin]] each.

===Habitats===
Birds are native to [[Isle Delfino]].
