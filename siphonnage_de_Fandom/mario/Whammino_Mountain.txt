'''Whammino Mountain''' is the fifth level of World 1 in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It is known as World 1-5.

== Layout ==
Unlike all of the previous levels, Whammino Mountain has a little amount of rock to walk on, instead of grass. Though it's main colors are still green and brown.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' ==
After finding the Secret Exit back in [[Water's Edge Way]], [[Mario]] ends up high in the mountains to continue his search for the [[Royal Sticker]]s. Mario, at one point, encounters a [[Lakitu]] that drops many [[Spiny]]s. Fortunately, there is a [[Starman]] that allows Mario to run through each of them with ease. Mario eventually has to [[Paperize]] a White Gate in order to continue with his quest. After fighting many enemies later, Mario manages to peel a Toad that runs a [[Sticker Shop#Whammino Mountain|Sticker Shop]] just ahead. After leaving the shop, Mario triggers a Rube Goldberg machine that results in a clear path to the [[Comet Piece]].
