The '''Scurvy Crew''' are three pirate crabs that act as the second boss in the games ''Donkey Kong Country Returns'' and ''Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D'', as well as the boss to Beach world of Donkey Kong Island in the stage Pinchin' Pirates. They are composed by: a blue crab with a sword-hand, a yellow crab with a fork-hand, and their leader, Captain Greenbeard, a red crab with a hook-hand. They were hypnotized by the Maraca Gang.
