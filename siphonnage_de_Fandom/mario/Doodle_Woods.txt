'''Doodle Woods''' is the third level of [[Topaz Passage]] in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
Doodle Woods is named for its drawing-styled appearance. Despite having the word "woods" in its name, Doodle Woods does not feature any trees. The main enemy featured in the level is [[Hoggus]], which can draw three other pig enemies to life—[[Butatabi]], [[Deburīna]], and [[Denden]]. All four enemies only appear in Doodle Woods.
