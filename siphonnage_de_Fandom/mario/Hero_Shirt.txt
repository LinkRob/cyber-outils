The '''Hero Shirt''' is [[Mario]]'s strongest personal armor in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from [[Croco]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] for 100 [[coin]]s. Equipping it to Mario increases his [[Defense]] by 48 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 24.
