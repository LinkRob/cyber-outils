'''Spear Dance Return''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] of [[Shy Guy]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Like his [[Offensive Power Shot]], [[Lightning Spear]], Shy Guy becomes a [[Spear Guy]] while using the move. Upon initiating the move, Shy Guy does a dance and transforms his tennis racket into a spear, reaching the ball and hitting back a slice.
