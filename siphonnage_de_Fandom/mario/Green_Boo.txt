The '''Green Boo''' is a [[Boo]] who is a racing opponent in the [[You Vs. Boo]] mode of ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. It moves slightly faster than a Boo. Once the Green Boo is defeated, it gets replaced by a [[Red Boo]].

In ''[[Mario Party DS]]'', Green Boos appear in the [[mini-game]] [[Peekaboo]].
