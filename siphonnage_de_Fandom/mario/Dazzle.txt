'''Dazzle''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Physical description==
Dazzle's species is unknown. He has a dark blue jester-like hat with what appears to be a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] dangling from each of the four tips. Dazzle has a dark purple face with blank white eyes and large orange lips. Dazzle's other bodily features include light blue arms and round, black feet. He carries a blue purse that matches the color of his headwear, although there is a visible pink button on the purse.

==History==
Dazzle lives in the [[Rogueport Sewers]]. He is obsessed with [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]], and he can trade rare [[Badge]]s to [[Mario]] for Star Pieces. As such, Dazzle serves the same role as [[Merlow]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. 

==Tattle==
''"That's Dazzle, the Star-Piece collector. He'll trade you badges for Star Pieces. Once you collect a few Star Pieces, you should definitely come talk to him. Hey, but why do you think he collects them, anyway? You think he bathes in them?"''

==Trivia==
* Dazzle's role as the character that purchases Star Pieces is the same as [[Merlow]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
