'''Takechiko City''' is a minor location in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Description==
Takechiko City is a city based on [[Japan]], indicated by its architecture. In front of the city is a red bridge with wooden platforms arching above a river.

==In ''WarioWare: Get It Together!''==
In the intro to [[Orbulon]]'s stage, [[Culture]], Takechiko City is one of the cities where Orbulon steals artifacts from. On his [[Oinker]], Orbulon hovers above a stone path in front of the city's bridge, where he uses the Oinker's tractor beam to abduct a present, a lucky cat, a teapot, a pillow, a table, [[Ms. Munchly]], and [[Ruffington]]. After this, Orbulon warps over to [[Ohlaka Island]].
