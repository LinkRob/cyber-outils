'''World 6-Tower''' is the first [[tower]] level of [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Layout==
In this level, the [[Spike Pillar]]s return. Mario must jump before the Spike Pillar can damage him. After the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], Mario must quickly jump up to avoid the Spike Pillars below him. After the door is another area similar to the previous one. Mario should get to the top before getting the second Star Coin. Through the double doors at the top of the tower is another Bowser Jr. battle. This time, Mario is able to jump on Bowser Jr. This fight takes place on a tilting platform.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Mario can get the first [[Star Coin]] by ducking or ground pounding to avoid a Spike Pillar, and then jumping to it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], Mario must quickly jump up to avoid the Spike Pillars below him. After the door is another area similar to the previous one. Mario should get to the top before getting the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': To get the last Star Coin, the player must time their [[Wall Jump]]s very well or be [[Mini Mario]]. Mario must Wall Jump to a green Warp Pipe, then enter it and avoid the four Spike Pillars to get the last Star Coin.
