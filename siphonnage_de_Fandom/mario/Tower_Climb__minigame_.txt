'''Tower Climb''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''. It can be played through [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020#Story Mode|Story Mode]] or in the [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020#Game Room|Game Room]].

==Gameplay==
[[File:M&S Tokyo 2020 Tower Climb.png|250px|thumb|Gameplay]]
The minigame stars [[Miles "Tails" Prower|Tails]], who must climb [[Tokyo Tower]] to retrieve [[Silver the Hedgehog|Silver]]'s [[entry pass]]es to the [[Tokyo Aquatic Center]] and [[Aomi Urban Sports Park]] that he dropped while climbing Tokyo Tower. Tails uses holds to traverse the tower. There are different types of holds: Tails can stay on blue holds for up to nine seconds, yellow holds for up to seven seconds, red holds for up to five seconds, and rainbow-colored checkpoint holds for 15 seconds. There are also Springs that bounce Tails higher up the tower, [[Bumper (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Bumpers]] that can bounce him off the tower, and spiked balls that can knock him down. Colliding into spiked balls or falling off the tower results in a penalty, causing Tails to lose 15 seconds. Tails retrieves the entry passes once he reaches the top of the tower. If Tails fails to reach the top of the tower within the 3-minute time limit, he fails the minigame.
