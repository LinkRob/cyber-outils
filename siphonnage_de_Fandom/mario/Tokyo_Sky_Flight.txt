'''Tokyo Sky Flight''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''. This minigame can be played during [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020#Story Mode|Story Mode]] or in the [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020#Game Room|Game Room]].

==Gameplay==
[[File:M&S Tokyo 2020 Tokyo Sky Flight.png|250px|thumb|Gameplay]]
The player controls [[Mario]], who is controlling the [[Tornado (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Tornado]], which is flying near {{wp|Tokyo Tower}}. [[Sonic]] stands on the standing area in front of the controlling area of the Tornado. The player can shoot out normal and charge shots to destroy enemies. Some enemies use [[w:c:sonic:Crawl|Crawl]]'s [[Bumper (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Bumpers]] as shields, however, meaning the player cannot shoot those enemies. The player can also use loops, which causes the Tornado to spin. A loop causes a shockwave that can stun some enemies and additionally removes Bumpers; however, there is a cooldown to how much the player can use this due to Sonic losing balance because of the spin. During the stages, there are [[Ring (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Rings]] to collect, but they do not act how they normally do in other ''[[Sonic the Hedgehog (franchise)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' games because there is a heart counter with five hit points. When the player reaches the end of the stage, they must fight a boss. There is one stage in Story Mode and three in the Game Room.
