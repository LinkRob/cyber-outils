'''Superball''' is a projectile which appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. They are projectiles thrown by [[Superball Mario]], a transformation of [[Mario]] granted by the [[Superball Flower]], and are much like the [[fireball]]s thrown by [[Fire Mario]].

Unlike fireballs, which bounce along the ground, Superballs are fired directly off of surfaces and go at a right angle, making it rather difficult to hit enemies with. However, Superballs are capable of collecting [[coin]]s unlike fireballs, which only move through them.

In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', the [[Superball Flower]] course element is unlocked by clearing the [[Spiny Shell Smashers]] job in Story Mode. It is one of two unlockable course elements, the other being the [[Super Hammer]].
