The '''Rainbow Cruiser''' is a vehicle which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

==Description==
The Rainbow Cruiser is a six-winged wooden [[airship]] that is usually located in [[Rainbow Ride]].

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 64''===
The Rainbow Cruiser appears in [[Rainbow Ride]], where [[Mario]] can get to it by using a series of [[Magic Carpet]]s. He first has to take the Magic Carpet to the right of the course's starting point, then ride the Magic Carpet to the right of the spinning [[platform]]s, and lastly the Magic Carpet to the left at the end of the second carpet's path. Once at the Rainbow Cruiser, the player must [[jump]] over platforms and [[Donut Block|Falling Block]]s to climb onto it.

On the Rainbow Cruiser, there is a heavy [[wind]] that blows Mario to the stern. Two [[Bob-omb]]s and a [[Lakitu]] are on the deck, and a [[cannon]] is on the stern. The [[Power Star]] for the first [[mission]], [[Cruiser Crossing the Rainbow]], is on the ship's bow. A [[1-Up Mushroom|1-UP Mushroom]] can be found on the tip of the bowsprit. The ship's mast is in the center, and a 1-UP Mushroom spawns from it when climbed to the top. Once the cannon is opened, by talking to the [[Bob-omb Buddy]] at the start of Rainbow Ride, Mario can use it to reach a platform with a [[Chuckya]] and a [[! Block|yellow block]] containing the Power Star for the sixth mission, [[Somewhere Over the Rainbow]].

===''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''===
The Rainbow Cruiser has some differences in the remake. One of the Bob-ombs is replaced with a [[Goomba]]. The Star and 1-UP Mushroom have switched spots, with the Star on the very end of the bowsprit and the 1-UP Mushroom simply on the bow. A [[Cap Block|red block]] containing a [[Power Flower (Super Mario 64 DS)|Power Flower]] was added near the stern.

==In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''==
Several of the [[level]]s of [[Toad Shot]] take place on the Rainbow Cruiser.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
The [[Rainbow Cruise]] stage introduced in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' includes the Rainbow Cruise as the main platform and stage element.
