The '''Shooting Star''', also known as the '''Twinkle Star''' in Australia and Europe, is a large bike in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. This bike is unlocked by finishing the [[Engine Class|100cc]] [[Star Cup]] in first place or playing 1,500 races. It also may belong to [[Rosalina]] because of the bike's looks and name.

This bike is similar to its kart equivalent, but a little rounded. It has high speed, but low acceleration, and it has outside [[Drift]].

The normal staff ghost for [[Rainbow Road]] ([[Rosalina]], Nin*Konno) uses this bike.
