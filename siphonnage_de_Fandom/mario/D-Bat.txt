A '''D-Bat''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land II]]''. It also makes a cameo in ''[[Picross NP Vol. 7]]'' as one of the character puzzles.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
D-Bats are dark purple bats with yellow ears.

===Behavior===
D-Bats have one of two behaviors. If it is carrying a weight, which a D-Bat constantly drops and lifts, it tries to flatten [[Wario]] with it, in which case he temporarily turns into [[Flat Wario]]. D-Bats that do not carry a weight attempt to drag Wario up, causing him to lose [[coin]]s, but if he is Flat Wario, these D-Bats can stretch him back into his normal state. A D-Bat can be defeated from Wario's [[Dash Attack]].

The [[Omodonmeka]] from ''[[Wario Land 3]]'' would go on to have similar behavior to the D-Bats.
