'''Peaches''' are a type of [[fruit]] which appear in some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related games. It is the namesake fruit of which [[Princess Peach]] is named after. Despite this, Peaches rarely appear within ''Mario''-related media, and they have yet to appear within the main ''Mario'' franchise itself.

== In ''[[Yoshi (video game)|Yoshi]]'' ==
Peaches make their first overall appearance in ''[[Yoshi (video game)|Yoshi]]''. Incidentally, while these are not associated with Princess Peach, ''Yoshi'' was released at a time when she was named "Princess Toadstool" to English-speaking audiences. Peaches first appear after the player clears levels 12 and 13 of the B-Type game, and matching them awards 600 bonus points.

== In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} ==
The only time Peaches are associated with Princess Peach is in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} when she uses her [[Final Smash]], [[Peach Blossom]], upon which several Peaches drop onto the stage. In the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series, Peaches act like the [[food]] items in general in that they restore a character's health. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]'', thirteen Peaches drop onto the stage when Peach Blossom is used, and they restore 5% each. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', three big Peaches are dropped onto the stage instead, and these restore 20% of health each. Peach's Echo Fighter, [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]] has a Final Smash called [[Daisy Blossom]] but instead of peaches, they summon daisies.
