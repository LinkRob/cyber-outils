'''Tako''' are an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]''.

== Profile ==
===Physical description===
A Tako is a small, orange octopus.

===Behavior===
Tako crawl along the sea floor toward [[Mario]] and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].

==In ''Yoshi's Safari''==
Tako appear in the underwater portion of [[Crescent Coast]]. Here, Mario and Yoshi have to fight off an assault of Tako, which can be defeated from being blasted by the [[Super Scope]] a few times. Tako are launched from [[Tsūdebiru]]. A Tako can be defeated by blasting it with the [[Super Scope]] a few times. Once the Tsūdebiru has been destroyed, Tako are no longer spawned.
