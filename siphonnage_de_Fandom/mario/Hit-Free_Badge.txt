The '''Hit-Free Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be obtained from either the [[Star Shrine]] or as an item dropped by [[Elder Shrooboid]], albeit with a 7.5 percent chance. Equipping the Hit-Free Badge allows the wearer to use an unlimited number of [[Bros. Item]]s in a battle up until taking damage.
