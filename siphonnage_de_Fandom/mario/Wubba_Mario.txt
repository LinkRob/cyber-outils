'''Wubba Mario''' is a form that [[Mario]] can take on if he grabs certain [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flowers]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Description==
As Wubba Mario, Mario resembles a regular [[Wubba]], though red. His cap, nose, and moustache are retained in this form.

==Abilities==
As with the Wubbas in the [[Fungi Mines]] overworld map, Mario can stick to walls in his Wubba form. This form does not prevent him from performing [[Wall Jump|Wall Jumps]] and [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounds]]. Mario shrinks to a regular Wubba's size (similar in size to his regular [[Small Mario|Small form]]) upon taking damage, though his abilities are not lost. If he takes a second hit, however, Mario will die.

== Appearances ==
Wubba Mario appears in [[An Uncharted Area: Wubba Ruins]], [[A Final Uncharted Area: Poison Ruins]], the [[Deep Magma Bog Palace]], and [[Operation Poplin Rescue]].

== Trivia ==

* Despite the fact that Mario can climb walls as Wubba Mario, the [[Wubba|Wubbas]] in actual levels cannot.
