'''Birds''' are a recurring enemy in the {{series|Mario vs. Donkey Kong}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Birds are both the toy equivalent and a variant of [[Nitpicker]]s. A Bird has an orange beak, orange feet, and yellow feathers. In ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]'', a Bird is more clearly depicted as a toy, with metallic-looking joints under its wings.

===Behavior===
Birds fly around an area and attack by trying to drop [[egg]]s onto their target. In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'', Birds routinely spawn from nests and perform three complete flights across the level before disappearing off-screen. Once they spot [[Mario]] underneath, they drop an egg. In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis'' and ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Minis March Again!]]'', if a [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Mario]] is underneath a Bird, it swoops at him, and whenever [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] takes damage, the Bird briefly increases in speed and drops eggs toward the Mini Marios.

==In {{series|Mario vs. Donkey Kong}}==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''===
[[File:MvsDK Nitpicker.png|frame|left]]
Birds are encountered in [[Donkey Kong Jungle (world)|Donkey Kong Jungle]]. If Mario gets hit by the egg or the Bird itself, he loses a life. Mario can deflect a Bird's eggs using his [[handstand]], but the Birds themselves are invulnerable to any attack. In [[Fire Mountain (world)|Fire Mountain]], regular Birds are replaced by [[Firebird]]s.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''===
A single Bird appears as an obstacle in [[Jungle Hijinks]], during the battle against Donkey Kong in [[Boss Game 8]]. Whenever Donkey Kong is damaged, a Mini Mario can drop a [[Banana Bunch]] on the Bird to temporarily destroy it, although the Bird reforms shortly afterward. Aside from this, artwork of a Bird can be seen during the game's staff credits.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Minis March Again!''===
Birds appear in the boss levels [[Floor 4 Donkey Kong]] and [[Floor 4+ Donkey Kong]], where they act the same as the Bird in ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''.
