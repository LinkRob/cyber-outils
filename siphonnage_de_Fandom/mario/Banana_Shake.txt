'''Banana Shake''' is a DK mini-game which appears in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

<big>Donkey Kong Shakes The Banana Tree And A Banana Falls Down From a tree and he eats it</big>

==Gameplay==
Each player shakes a tree for 15 seconds to get as many bananas as possible. Either a [[Banana]], a [[Banana Bunch]] (worth five Bananas), or [[Hammer]]s can fall from the tree. The player can have their character dodge a hammer by pressing {{button|gcn|B}}, otherwise their character becomes temporarily stunned.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|a}} - (Press repeatedly) Shake the tree
*{{button|gcn|b}} - Dodge hammers

==Trivia==
*The minigame takes place in [[Delfino Plaza]], a location from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' that has not appeared in any ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' game.
