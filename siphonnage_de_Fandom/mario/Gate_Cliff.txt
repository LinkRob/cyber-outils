==Layout==
Gate Cliff is mainly a giant rock on water which guards Bowser's Castle.

==History==
[[Mario]] and [[Kersti]] arrive to a large stone door which seems heavily closed. Kresti then points out the Royal Stickers which they start to glow on the door. After that and Paperizing the area, [[Bowser's Castle]] is finally shown but it is high up in the skies. Mario and Kersti then head back to [[Wiggler's Tree House]] as their [[Wiggler]] friend is now a full-grown [[Flutter]] and asks for his help. He agrees and takes them to Bowser's Castle ending this very short level.

==Trivia==
*The events of the Gate Cliff are foreshadowed at [[The Enigmansion]] on the Big Bookcase where it mentions the Royal Sticker lights illuminating the wall.
