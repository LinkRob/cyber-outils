'''Papercraft Fire Mario''' is a [[papercraft]] variation of [[Fire Mario]]. He is [[Papercraft Mario]] reborn from the ashes after being destroyed by a [[Papercraft Bowser]]. Papercraft Fire Mario resembles his previous form, but his cap and shirt are white, while his overalls are red. Papercraft Fire Mario's signature ability which was missing in his first form is the power to shoot fireballs from his mouth.

== History ==
After Papercraft Bowser destroys him and the other three [[Papercraft]]s, [[Toadette]] gets the idea to rebuild Papercraft Mario from Fire Ore. [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] find the Fire Ore after having to go through several monsters en route and during obtainment. Using this Fire Ore, Toadette resurrects Papercraft Mario and turns him into Papercraft Fire Mario, allowing him to take on [[Papercraft Bowser]].
