'''Sproings in the Twilight Forest''' is a level of [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
==Layout==
The level takes place at sunset, and nearly everything is pictured as a black silhouette. The layout itself is standard, with a few types of enemies and tree platforms.

Shortly after the level's [[Checkpoint Flag]], there is a [[Sproing]] with a blue aura around its head. Killing the Sproing will drop the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will make the player and enemies stretchable by [[crouch]]ing and getting up. The [[Wonder Seed]] is obtainable when crouching between two trees, crouching in the area will reveal the seed.
==[[10-Flower Coin]] locations==
Breaking [[brick Block|brick blocks]] in front of a tree will make an entrance for the first 10-flower coin.

Hitting a [[Wow Bud]] above a Koopa Troopa will reveal the path to the second 10-flower coin. The Wow Bud is not necessary to obtaining the 10-flower coin, but it does make the path obvious.

During the Wonder Effect, the last 10-Flower coin can be found under a bridge. The player must crouch to obtain it.

== Trivia ==
A [[Talking Flower]] is found hidden at the very start of the course. When interacted with, it will say, "Here I am! I'm impressed you found me."{{SMBWNav}}
