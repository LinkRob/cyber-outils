==History==
The Random House Key can only be obtained from during the very lengthy [[Piccolo]] sidequest. It is also the final item acquired in the quest. The player must give the [[Crystal Ball]] to [[Merlee]] and she will then give the player the Random House Key. This unlocks the locked door in [[Flopside]] which leads to a chest containing Piccolo.
