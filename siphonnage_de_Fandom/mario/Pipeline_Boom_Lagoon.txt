|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''Pipeline Boom Lagoon''' is a level of World Flower in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
This level is a redesigned version of World 3-5, [[Pipeline Lagoon]]. The level begins in the small beach area from the original level, though there are four [[? Block]]s that become [[Cannon Box]]es when hit, and there are [[Madpole]]s in the water that replace the Goombas. Sand sculptures of two [[Goomba]]s and [[Bowser]] are also found. A [[Warp Pipe]] leads to a section of the ground leading to a body of water, and more Madpoles are found on the surface of the water. Some [[crate]]s and a Goomba sand sculpture are found on top of a cliff. A Goomba sand sculpture containing [[Micro Goomba]]s inside and two more Cannon Boxes are found before the underwater section. [[Rammerhead]]s and [[Blooper]]s are encountered in the underwater section, replacing the [[Cheep Cheep]]s and [[Porcupuffer]]s from the original level. A [[Warp Box]] is found on a platform above the water, leading to the [[Checkpoint Flag]].

Another underwater section follows the Checkpoint Flag, where more Bloopers are found. [[Spike Block]]s appear as obstacles in the area. The sunken ship from the original level is found, followed by a section containing a Spike Block and Rammerheads leading to a Warp Pipe. Three sections of Rock Blocks are found on the wall, and the third contains a [[Clear Pipe]] that leads to a section with more Bloopers. A platform is found above the water, where [[Coin Coffer]]s and another Warp Pipe are found. Both Warp Pipes leads to the area with the [[Goal Pole]], with the one located through the Clear Pipe leading to a raised platform.
==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Near the end of the first underwater area, the first Green Star is behind a wall of [[Rock Block]]s that must be destroyed with a [[Cannon Box]].
*'''Green Star 2''': In the second underwater area, the second Green Star is located above the first three Spike Blocks.
*'''Green Star 3''': Deep below the second Green Star, the player must destroy a wall of four Rock Blocks with a Cannon Box and then activate the Green Star Ring behind it, then collect all eight [[Green Coin]]s to get the third Green Star.<br />
