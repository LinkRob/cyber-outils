'''Super Mario Slot''' is a [[Luigi]] single player mini-game in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It's an enhanced version of [[Mario Slot]] where the wheel has more wild Superstars, which in turn means higher chances to win.

This game is unlocked by capturing Luigi's rabbit in the [[Princess Peach's Castle|Mushroom's Castle]] roof, which is accesible by the cannon outside opened when the 150 stars are collected''.''{{Stub}}
