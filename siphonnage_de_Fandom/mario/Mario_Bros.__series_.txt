== Overview ==
'''''Mario Bros.''''' is the very first series of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. Its first installment is ''[[Mario Bros. (Game & Watch)|Mario Bros.]]'' for the [[Game & Watch]], let alone for the ''Mario'' franchise in general. It is a one-off title featuring [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] working at a bottling plant before they were reintroduced as plumbers in the arcade game ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]''.

The series title became the basis for ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', the first installment of the {{series|Super Mario}}, which went on to become the primary series of the ''Mario'' franchise.
