'''Octoguys''' (also called '''Pink Electrogoombas''') are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' that spit rocks to attack. Mario can destroy them by spinning into them and jumping on top of their heads. If Mario gets too close to them, they will run away for a moment, and then resume their attack patterns.

== Profile ==
[[Image:Pink Electrogoomba Mob.jpg|thumb|left|Many Octoguys as seen in the [[Deep Dark Galaxy]].]]
When an Octoguy sees [[Mario]], it throws rocks at him. Mario can avoid the rocks and defeat them by stomping.
[[Rock Mushroom|Rock Mario]] can run over the Octoguy. As opposed to the Octoomba, Octoguy throws his rocks in a verticle arc. Octoguys might not be a menace individually, but in larger numbers the pose a larger threat.
