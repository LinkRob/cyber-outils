'''Mario's Gloves''' are a recurring object in the {{franchise|Mario}}, although their first notable role is in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''.

==Description==
Mario's Gloves are one of the main parts of [[Mario]]'s clothing. Mario wears them in most of his appearances, although generally not in the older games due to graphical limitations. [[Luigi]], [[Wario]], and [[Waluigi]] all similarly wear gloves as part of their respective outfit.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
{{quote|Wh-what is this? If it isn't Mario's Glove! Ah-hah! Still damp with the sweat of the living!|Madame Clairvoya}}
Mario's Glove is one of Mario's five missing items, the other four being [[Mario's Cap|Mario's Hat]], [[Mario's Letter]], [[Mario's Star (item)|Mario's Star]], and [[Mario's Shoe]], all of which must be brought to [[Madame Clairvoya]] so that she can provide Luigi some clues as to where Mario is located. Mario's Glove is found in the [[Projection Room]], where a small [[treasure chest]] containing it appears after Luigi has vacuumed up every [[Grabbing Ghost|Cinema Ghost]].

Even though Mario's Glove is one of Mario's missing items, Mario is shown to be wearing both in his [[Mario's Painting|painting]]. Similarly, Mario is still shown to be wearing his cap in the painting, even though it is one of his missing items. 

In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]]'s [[Hidden Mansion]], Mario's Glove instead appears in the [[Courtyard (Luigi's Mansion)|Courtyard]] after vacuuming up every [[Ceiling Surprise]] and [[Purple Bomber]].
