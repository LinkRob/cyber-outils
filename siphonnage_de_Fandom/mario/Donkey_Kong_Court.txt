'''Donkey Kong Court''' is an unlockable tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]].

==Description==
As the name suggests, Donkey Kong Court is owned by [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]. The court takes place in a jungle, and the background features trees, palms, and [[barrel]]s. On the ground of the tennis court is an artwork image of Donkey Kong holding his tennis racket.

Donkey Kong Court can be unlocked by winning [[Mushroom Cup]] with Donkey Kong.
