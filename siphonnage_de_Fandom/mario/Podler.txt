==Appearance==
Podler is very similar to [[Podley]] in appearance being a [[Beanish]] character that wears glasses and has a mustache.

==History==
Podler is the manager of the [[Fresh Juice Bar]] in [[Glitzville]]. He mentions his love for hammers. During the events of Chapter 3, Podler gives the Super Hammer to [[Mario]] that is from the mysterious [[Jolene|X]].
