'''Chuckleroot''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. He lives at the heart of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] where he guards the way to the [[Chuckola Cola]]. He also has a [[Chuckleroot's Grandaughter|grandaughter]] that lives not too far away from him.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
[[File:Chucklerootmulticolor.png|thumb|left|Chuckleroot when multi-colored.]]
Chuckleroot is a large, blue tree creature. He can also turn different colors as red, white, and purple when he explained to Mario and Luigi that they need to retrieve the three [[Chuckola Fruit|Chuckola Fruits]].

==History==
[[File:Chuckleroot.png|frame|left]]
Mario and Luigi nearly reach the source of the Chuckola Cola when they approach Chuckleroot. He explained to Mario and Luigi that he needs them to collect the three Chuckola Fruit for him before allowing them to pass. He then opens the gates where Mario and Luigi can go to retrieve the Chuckola Fruits: red, white and purple. After Mario and Luigi have done so, Chuckeroot then moves aside allowing Mario and Luigi to pass. However, [[Popple]] and [[Bowser|Rookie]] quickly run past him first.
