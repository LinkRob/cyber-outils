The '''Nomadimice''' is a race of [[Little Mouser]]s who appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Nomadimice are often a light-blue Little Mouser who wear a headdress on his head.

==History==
The Nomadimice can only be found living in the humid town of [[Dry Dry Outpost]]. They can be found living amongst the other residents there, namely a race of nomadic [[Toad (species)|Toad]]s known as [[Dryite]]s. The most notable Nomadimouse living in Dry Dry Outpost is [[Moustafa]], who aids [[Mario]] in his quest by giving him the [[Pulse Stone]] to help him find [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]].

Another wandering [[Nomadimouse (character)|Nomadimouse]] can be found sitting near a tree in [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]] where Mario and [[Parakarry]] can deliver a letter to him which is from Moustafa. Despite being a fellow Nomadimouse, the wanderer isn't familiar with Moustafa and simply grateful for receiving a letter.
