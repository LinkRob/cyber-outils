A '''Ghoul Shroom''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Ghoul Shrooms are stronger versions of [[Zombie Shroom]]s that appear in later levels of the game (starting with the [[Gap of Crag]]). Ghoul Shrooms behave the same as Zombie Shrooms and can only be found in [[Question Block]]s. Ghoul Shrooms move rather quickly requiring timing to defeat them.
