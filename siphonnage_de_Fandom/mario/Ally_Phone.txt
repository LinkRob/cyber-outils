The '''Ally Phone''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Description ==
The Ally Phone is a Toad-like miniature phone with an icon of an ally. The player can obtain the Ally Phone on an [[Item Space]] on any board. The player can use the Ally Phone to summon a random ally.
