'''Prince Kooky von Koopa''' is a character that appears in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World (show)|Super Mario World]]''. He is one of the [[Koopalings (DiC Cartoons)|Koopalings]] and son of [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]. 

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Kooky has very messy hair, is smaller in size, has only one spike on his shell, has a usually twisted or crooked tail, possesses an unusual laugh, his eyes are larger and has a vague Germanic accent. In the ''Super Mario World'' episode "[[The Wheel Thing]]," Kooky's snout is colored the same as the rest of his body. In "[[Rock TV]]," Kooky's snout is a lighter shade, as in ''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''.

===Personality===
Kooky has a mad scientist-like personality, inventing various gadgets for his father [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]].
