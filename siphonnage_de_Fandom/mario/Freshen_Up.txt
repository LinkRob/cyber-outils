}}A '''Freshen Up''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Using a Freshen Up cures all party members of any negative status effects except [[Death]]. Freshen Ups can usually be obtained as items dropped by certain enemies. Freshen Ups are also sold at certain [[Item Shop]]s for 50 [[coin]]s.
