'''Bonus Chance''' is a minigame in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It involves stopping three slots on a slot machine, rewarding the player with [[1-Up|extra lives]], depending on how the slots line up. If there is no match, the player does not earn any lives for that turn. The player gets one turn for each [[coin]] they had collected in the [[level]] they had completed just before starting the Bonus Chance.
