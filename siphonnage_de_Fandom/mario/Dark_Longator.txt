}}A '''Dark Longator''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
These stronger versions of [[Longator]]s are only found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. They attack by stretching out their heads a fair distance away. Their heads can even go through blocks so one must take caution when battling them.
