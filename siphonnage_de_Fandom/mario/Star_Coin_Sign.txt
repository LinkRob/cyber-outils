A '''Star Coin Sign''' is a sign will block a trail on the worlds of ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. They sell for five [[Star Coin]]s to eliminate, and unlocks either an [[Toad House]] or level; the player will search to remind to save after opening a star coin sign.

In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'', a sign blocking the way to a Yellow Toad House in [[World Star (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Star]]; this Star Coin sign only after the player recieves all of them in the game, including [[Moon Coin]]s, and sells for nine coins to eliminate. 

Eventhrough the signs did not be seen in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', same gates are find in [[World 9 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9]] of the game, and the gates can be not locked by recieves all of them from each world to be compatible with the level (for example, [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1]] unlocks [[World 9-1 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-1]], etc.)
