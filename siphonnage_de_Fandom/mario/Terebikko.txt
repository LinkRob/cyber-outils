The '''Terebikko''', known as the '''See 'n Say''' in America was a educational video console manufactured by [[Bandai]] launched in Japan in 1988 and in America in 1989. It used VHS as storage medium. Only one ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'', let alone [[Nintendo]], game made use of this system, ''[[Super Mario World: Mario to Yoshi no Bouken Land]]''.
