|race = [[Flip-Flopian|Flopsider]]}}'''Chip''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Chip is a [[Flip-Flopian]] that is blue in color with a pointy nose.

==History==
Chip lives in [[Flopside]] where he can be found in [[The Overthere (bar)|The Overthere]] coffee bar. Chip loves to drink coffee and spends his days tasting different ones for taste. He loves to drink his coffee with milk but never likes to drink it black since he states it's too bitter for him.

==Trivia==
*Chip's [[Flipside]] counterpart is [[Chap (blue)|Chap]] who loves milk.
