'''Big Shiny Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. The sticker is a more-powerful variant of the regular [[Jump]] sticker.

== Appearance ==
Big Shiny Jump looks exactly like the Shiny Jump sticker, except for the boot itself appears a little bigger in shape.
