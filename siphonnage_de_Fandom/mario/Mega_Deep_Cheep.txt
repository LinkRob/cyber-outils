'''Mega Deep Cheeps''' (also spelt as '''Mega Deep-Cheeps''') are larger variants of [[Deep Cheep]]s that first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. They behave identically to normal-sized Deep Cheeps, pursuing the plumbers if they get close but their larger size makes them harder to avoid. They are commonly seen underwater and like the [[Mega Cheep Cheep]]s, are sometimes seen guiding smaller Deep Cheeps. Mega Deep Cheeps have commonly been seen in many of the recent 2D ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' series installments.

==History==
===''New Super Mario Bros.''===
[[Image:Mega Deep-Cheep Painting.jpg|thumb|left|A Mega Deep Cheep]]
Mega Deep Cheeps make their debut in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' alongside the [[Deep Cheep]]. In this game, they only appear in [[World 6-5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-5]] and are only seen near the end of the level. They behave similarly to the [[Mega Cheep Cheep]]s back in [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 3]] but they pursue [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] if they get near them, if the plumbers move away however, the Mega Deep Cheeps will continue along their path. A Mega Deep Cheep can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Mega Mushroom]], or [[Starman]]. 

===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[Image:Mega Deep-Cheep and Deep-Cheep.png|thumb|right|A Mega Deep Cheep with a smaller Deep Cheep]]
Mega Deep Cheeps return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', in this game, they are found in [[World 1-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1-4]], [[World 8-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-4]], and various levels of [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4]]. They are slightly smaller than they were in the previous game being only slightly larger than a regular [[Deep Cheep]], Mega Deep Cheeps are also seen guiding smaller Deep Cheeps through the waters, they behave as they did previously chasing after the heroes if they come near but they can easily be defeated with a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]]. They can also be frozen with the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]], when frozen they slowly rise to the surface which can be used as a stepping stone for reaching higher platforms.

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[Image:Mega Deep Cheep Emerges.png|thumb|left|A Mega Deep Cheep emerging from the side to attack.]]
Mega Deep Cheeps appear again in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', in this game, they behave as they did in the previous games and just as before pursue the plumbers whenever they get close. Mega Deep Cheeps are commonly found in [[World Mushroom-2 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Mushroom-2]] and various levels of [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]], they also regain their larger size from ''New Super Mario Bros.'' and can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Gold Flower]], [[Starman]], [[Mega Mushroom]], or [[Invincibility Leaf]].

===''New Super Luigi U/New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[Image:Mega Deep Cheep Guider.png|thumb|right|A Mega Deep Cheep guiding smaller Deep Cheeps as The Luigi Crew passes by.]]
While Mega Deep Cheeps (and regular-sized [[Deep Cheep]]s) didn't appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', they do exist unused in the game's files. They ended up appearing in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' in levels such as [[Cheep Chomp Chase]]. They behave as they did in previous games, (chasing the heroes when they come near) and guiding smaller Deep Cheeps. They also return to their size from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''. They can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]] and can once again be frozen with the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]]. 

===''Super Mario Maker/Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS/Super Mario Maker 2''===
[[Image:Mega Deep Cheep Line.jpg|thumb|left|A line of Mega Deep Cheeps in ''Super Mario Maker''.]]
Mega Deep Cheeps appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as well as it's [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS|two]] [[Super Mario Maker 2|sequels]] as placeable enemies exclusive to the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' style. They can be made by either shaking a [[Mega Cheep Cheep]] or by dragging a [[Super Mushroom]] to a a [[Deep Cheep]]. They can also be given wings to become a [[Winged Mega Deep Cheep]]. Mega Deep Cheeps behave the same as they did previously in this game (chasing [[Mario]] whenever he gets near) however they are slightly more relentless this times. Mega Deep Cheeps can also hop around on land-based levels and bounce higher while engulfed in flames (from the [[lava]]) in Castles. A Mega Deep Cheep can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]].
