The '''Airway''' is a location within the mansion [[Secret Mine]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Layout==
The Airway is a very large room consisting of bridges. There is also a [[pit]] with air blowing from it.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
During Secret Mine's first mission, D-1 [[Cold Case]], Luigi must lead the [[Toad (species)|Toad Assistant]] over the large area. However, the bridge is down and Luigi must cross the narrow rope himself then pull away the rope on the other side to bring up the bridge. The Toad Assistant crosses over and the two can finally use the elevator to reach the [[Chalet]].
