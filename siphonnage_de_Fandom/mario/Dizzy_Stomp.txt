The '''Dizzy Stomp''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Wearing the Dizzy Stomp badge gives [[Mario]] a new [[Boots|jump]] attack that has the potential to daze his foes. The badge has a [[Badge Point|BP]] cost of 1, and using the Dizzy Stomp during battle requires Mario to use two [[Flower Point|FP]].

Dizzy Stomp is found in [[Mt. Lavalava]], but only if Mario has the Ultra Hammer. Using [[Parakarry]], Mario can cross a lava river in a room hidden by steel blocks. In this room, atop a rocky slope, Mario can acquire this badge by way of a treasure chest.
