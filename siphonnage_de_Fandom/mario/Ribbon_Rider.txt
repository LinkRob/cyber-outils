The '''Ribbon Rider''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Ribbon Rider is a red, gold and green [[Wikipedia:Christmas|Christmas]]-themed variation of the [[Birthday Girl]]. There is a yellow star located above the character's emblem. The kart uses an orange variation of the [[Roller]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Ribbon Rider is a High-End kart introduced as a spotlight for the [[London Tour]] and 2019 [[Holiday Tour]]. The Ribbon Rider increases the duration of [[Mini-Turbo]] speed boosts and the points earned for performing them. This is the signature kart for [[Daisy (Holiday Cheer)]], alongside the [[Glitter Glider]].
