{{Nihongo|'''Urn It'''|このツボど～れだ?|Kono tsubo do ~reda?|Whiiich one is this vase?}} is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. Its name is a pun on the phrase "earn it."

== Opening ==
Players run towards the temple with a brick pipe, and the pipe starts to glow.

==Gameplay==
The gameplay is similar to ''[[Mario Party 4]]''{{'}}s [[Archaeologuess]]. In order to win, the player has to choose an urn that matches the one that is rotating. If they match you earn points. If not, you lose points. The earlier you choose, the more points you earn. Whoever has the most points wins. Not choosing does not take or give any points.

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules:''' ''Choose the image with a pattern that matches the one on the spinning urn. The faster you choice the correct image, the more points you receive.''
*'''Controls:''' ''Hold the Wii Remote vertically. Point at the image you want to select, and press {{button|wii|a}} to confirm your choice.''
