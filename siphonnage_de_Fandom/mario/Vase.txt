'''Vases''' are a recurring object in the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. In their appearances, a Vase can often be pushed by a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] onto the ground, usually revealing a [[key]] upon breaking.
