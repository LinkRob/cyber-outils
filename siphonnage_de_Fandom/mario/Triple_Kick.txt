|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Triple Kick''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can only be used by [[Jinx]].

==Effects==
When Jinx uses Triple Kick, he rushes at one of [[Mario]]'s party and does a series of attacks followed by a kick dealing high damage. Jinx doesn't really use this move much compared to his other moves such as [[Jinxed]], but becomes more common the more times he is fought.
