A '''Hot-Hot Rock''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. 

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Hot-Hot Rocks resemble sentient blocks of stone, and they can appear in big and small variants. 

===Behavior===
Occasionally, a Hot-Hot Rock can emit magma, which harms the player when stepped on. The magma can be extinguished with water.
