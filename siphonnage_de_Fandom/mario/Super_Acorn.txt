The {{Nihongo|'''Super Acorn'''|スーパードングリ|Sūpā Donguri|Super Acorn}} (also known as the '''Acorn Mushroom''') is a power-up in the {{series|Super Mario}}. It first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', and reappears in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

== Description ==
A Super Acorn is an acorn which resembles a [[Super Mushroom]]. It can turn any character, except [[Nabbit]], into a [[Flying Squirrel Mario|Flying Squirrel]]. There is also a variation of the Super Acorn with a P on it called the [[P-Acorn]] which allows for infinite height, similar to the [[P-Wing]]. It can only be acquired when [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or one of the two [[Toad (species)|Toads]] capture Nabbit and then a Toad rewards them, or very rarely in [[Acorn Plains]] on the overworld.
