'''Double Dip P''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Double Dip P, the partner version of [[Double Dip]], gives Mario's partners the ability to use multiple items in a turn. This badge cannot be used to have multiple attacks, however. This badge costs three [[Badge Point]]s to wear and by wearing two of these, Mario's partners can access the Triple Dip attack, allowing them to use up to three items in one turn. During battle, Double Dip P costs four [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

This badge is found twice. One of these badges is found in [[Fahr Outpost]] where Mario must find a cleverly placed invisible block around a tree near the west side of the outpost, and inside of this invisible block. Another Double Dip P is found in [[Pit of 100 Trials]] on the seventieth level.

==Trivia==
*This badge's hidden location is hinted by [[Wonky]] if Mario pays him for one of the tales that leads to this badge.
