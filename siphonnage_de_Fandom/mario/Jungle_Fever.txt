"'''Jungle Fever'''" is the eleventh episode of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. Its corresponding live-action segment is "[[Dance]]."

==Synopsis==
While venturing through the [[Amazon Jungle]] in search of a witch doctor named [[Dr. Sheldon]], [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] are hit with some of [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s [[Koopa Strength Itching Powder|itching powder]] and it's up to [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] to save the day.

==Plot==
Mario, Luigi, [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], and Toad arrive in the Amazon Jungle in search of a witch doctor named [[Sheldon]], who might be able to rid the Mushroom Kingdom of King Koopa. As the group hacks their way through the Amazon Jungle, Mario calls a lunch break and the group discussing their plans. Unfortunately, a couple of [[Shy Guy]]s listen in on the plans and report to King Koopa. He decides that he'll have to beat them to the punch.

Unaware of King Koopa's plan, the group continues toward a gorge named [[George's Gorge]], where they try to cross a bridge. King Koopa then blocks their way back and has the bridge's ropes cut causing the Marios to have to try and climb the bridge to the top. Knowing they'd try this, King Koopa sends in a group of [[Albatoss]]es to throw in some [[Bob-omb]]s that explode covering everyone in a white powder. Everyone then starts to scratch and King Koopa claims its itching powder. Another wave of Albatosses attacks but Mario grabs one and forces it to save his friends and give them a ride. King Koopa is unhappy about it and chases after the group.

Though it seems the Marios will reach Sheldon first, King Koopa manages to stop their ride by having a [[Birdo]] shoot an egg onto the Albatoss's face. The Marios go down. Somehow the itch powder no longer affects the Princess but her companions are itching so bad they can only scratch. King Koopa arrives in the village first and catches Sheldon, leaving behind his assistant named [[Butterfingers]].

The Princess arrives a moment too late but Butterfingers claims she can make an anti-itch potion herself. She tries but fails. It's decided that only Sheldon can make the potion and since the Marios are out of commission the Princess decides to go alone. Butterfingers goes with her believing the Princess cannot face King Koopa alone.

Meanwhile, King Koopa forces Sheldon to make him a potion, which will turn the Princess into a rock. Sheldon agrees with little fuss and mixes the potion. The Princess and Butterfingers arrive but are captured. Sheldon then takes some of the potion and warns the Princess to duck. King Koopa, who was standing behind the Princess at the time, gets hit with the potion and he starts to itch as well.

Sheldon and his friends return to the Marios where he gives them the "cure" to King Koopa's itch powder, spaghetti.

== Trivia/Goofs ==
* Though King Koopa wears a different costume in this episode he doesn't take on a different alias.
* This is the first time the Princess resolves to aid the Marios alone.
* George's Gorge is deeper than The Grand Canyon at three miles deep, whereas the Grand Canyon goes only 2 miles deep.
* Although Mario is using a plunger to cut the bamboo it still makes clean cuts.
* The Princess was hit by the itching powder and is initially seen scratching but later she inexplicably is freed from its effects.
* "Jungle Love" is briefly played in this episode, and was retained in the DVDs with the episode. This is the only episode to retain the featured song in its later showings.
