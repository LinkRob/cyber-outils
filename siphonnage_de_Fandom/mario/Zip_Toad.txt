'''Zip Toad''' is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Zip Toad is apparently very famous. When [[Mario]] gets on the [[Excess Express]], he is supposedly in Room 1. Later, when Mario finds him with the waitress' [[Shell Earrings]] and [[Toodles]]' [[Gold Ring (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|Gold Ring]], he is revealed as [[Doopliss]] in disguise. It would later be revealed by [[Pennington]] that Zip Toad was found knocked out nearby in [[Rogueport]] which was also when Doopliss snuck aboard disguised as him. After the events of Chapter 6, Mario gets an e-mail from the real Zip Toad, thanking him for revealing Doopliss' masquerade and saying he will continue to make movies. Zip Toad is notable for being one of few characters that Mario never officially meets in person despite playing a major role in Chapter 6.
