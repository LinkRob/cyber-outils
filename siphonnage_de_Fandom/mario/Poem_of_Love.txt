The '''Poem of Love''' is a key item in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
It is a poem that [[Petal Guy (character)|Petal Guy]] wrote for [[Flutter (character)|Flutter]] to confess his feelings for her. The text of the Poem of Love is:

<poem>
Eyes like the ocean,
Nose like a mountain,
Mouth like the shining
Sun, you are my earth!
And I, a lone spaceman,
Crash into your love.
</poem>

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The Poem of Love is obtained at the [[Petal House]], where Petal Guy asks the player's character to figure out where Flutter lives and deliver the Poem to Love to her. The player's character can find Flutter in her home on the [[Mushroom Condo]]'s third floor, which is where the letter must be given to her. When Flutter receives the Poem of Love, she asks who Petal Guy is, only to be interrupted by Petal Guy, when he enters the Mushroom Condo. Petal Guy reads the Poem of Love to Flutter, them he asks her what she thought of it. Flutter promptly says that she needs to leave and flies away, leaving Petal Guy disappointed. Following this, Petal Guy proclaims that he will not give up on his love, and he rewards the player's character with the [[Bead Machine]] [[Gaddget]] for helping him.
