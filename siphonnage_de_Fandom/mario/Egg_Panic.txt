'''Egg Panic''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is available from the start and has no unlock requirements.

==Description==
Egg Panic is used to play a four-player minigame on a single system, which has to be held vertically due to the extended display. Each player has to cooperate to fill a bag with eggs without tearing the bag. This can be accomplished by synchronizing button presses. After a certain point, the eggs hatch, disrupting the synchronization.
