}}'''Cosmic Slalom''' is a Duel minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. It is very similar to the ''[[Mario Party 3]]'' minigame, [[Motor Rooter]].

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the two players are in hovercrafts where they are competing in a race across the large slalom. There are electrical fences around where, running into one, will cause the player to be stunned losing speed. The player also can use speed boosts to give themselves a burst of speed for a short time. However, they are only able to use it three times per race. The player who reaches the end first wins.
