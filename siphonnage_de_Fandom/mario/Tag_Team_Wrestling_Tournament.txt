The '''Tag Team Wrestling Tournament''', or '''Tag Team Tournament''', is a wrestling tournament hosted at an arena by the [[Mushroom Wrestling Federation]] during the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Tag Team Trouble]]." [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] enter as contestants, and they fight two [[Sledge Bro]]s.
