'''Wario Land 3''' is one of [[18-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. It is both named after and based on the game of the [[Wario Land 3|same name]].

== Layout ==
The microgame takes place in the level [[The Tidal Coast]], particularly a vertical water current, but the layout is unique the microgame itself.

== Gameplay ==
The object is for [[Wario]], in his [[Bubble Wario|Bubble]] form, to travel his way up a current to collect a [[Musical Coin]] while avoiding [[Spike (Wario Land series)|Spikes]] along the path. The player can control Wario's movement by tilting the [[Nintendo 3DS]]. If Wario touches a spike or does not reach the Musical Coin within the time limit, the player loses at the microgame. Higher difficulty levels feature more spikes, being harder for Wario to move around.
