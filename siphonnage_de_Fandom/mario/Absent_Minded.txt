'''Absent Minded''' is an unlockable Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The minigame is hosted by [[Toadette]].

== Opening ==
Players are having a conversation during the autumn setting. A player tells them a joke and the other players laugh about it. [[Toadette]] got their attention on the minigame and the minigame starts.

== Gameplay ==
The player must find the character that is not displayed on the screen for each round. It may be blurred images, showing one character per frame, puzzling the characters' tiles. After every chooses the answer or time runs out, the correct answer will show. The player who chose the correct answer 1st earns five points, 2nd earns three, 3rd earns two and 4th earns just one.

== Ending ==
The winner(s) do(es) their victory animation while other players sulk.
