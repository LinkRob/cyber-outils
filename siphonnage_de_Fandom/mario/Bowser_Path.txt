'''Bowser Path''' is a road which leads to [[Bowser's Castle]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|remake]]. It can be accessed by traveling northeast of [[Bumpsy Plains]].  The area contains many conveyor belts and requires the use of the sliding haymaker. [[Bowser]] saved his [[Shy Guy]] minions on Bowser Path and learned the Special Attack [[Shy Guy Squad]]. There are 10 [[bean]]s that can be unearthed on Bowser Path. The road ends in Bowser's treasure vault, located under his castle, which is where Bowser encounters the Fawful Express. This is also the last area where the Bros go to the [[Arm Center]].
