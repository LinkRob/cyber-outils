A '''Large Question Block''' is a giant variation of the [[Question Block]] appearing mostly in the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. A Large Question Block first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', in which it is featured within the [[level]]s of [[Giant Land]]. The main feature of a Large Question Block is its size, and most of them otherwise function the same as regular Question Blocks.
