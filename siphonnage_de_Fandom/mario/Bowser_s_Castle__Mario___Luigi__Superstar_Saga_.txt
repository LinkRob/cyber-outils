'''Bowser's Castle''' or '''Bowser's Flying Fortress''' appears as the last area in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|its]] [[Reissue|remake]]. It is near [[Hoohoo Village]] from the skies of [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. The floating castle bears closely identical to the original [[Bowser's Castle]] the likeness of [[Bowser]], and has the ability to fly, breathe flames shoot bullets.
