'''Cavity Calamity''' is a [[microgame]] which first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' as [[Orbulon]]'s and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as [[Mona]]'s.

== Gameplay ==
The goal is to move to a spot to a safe space. The player controls the germ. The player must examine the top and bottom teeth.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The gap is very noticeable.
* '''2nd level difficulty: '''They are more rugged than the first level.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The ceiling and floor is very uneven.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
The teeth takes place inside the human's mouth. The germ is purple.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' Most teeth are simply even.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' Less teeth are simply even.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The teeth are uneven.
