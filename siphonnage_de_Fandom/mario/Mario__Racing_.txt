'''Mario (Racing)''' is a skin of [[Mario]] who appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', ever since the [[Mario Tour (2021)]]. He is designed after Mario's appearance from ''[[Famicom Grand Prix: F1 Race]]'', even bearing the [[Mario Motors]] sponsor on his racing suit. Mario (Racing)'s special skill is [[Triple Mushrooms]], his rarity is High-End, and his size class is Medium.

In [[Mario Kart World]], Pro Racer Mario’s outfit bears a strong resemblance to the signature racing gear, prominently featuring the Mario Motors branding again.{{stub}}
