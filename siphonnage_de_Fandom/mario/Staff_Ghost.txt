'''Staff Ghosts''' are members from the [[Nintendo]] team (or [[Retro Studios]] team in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'') who race their time in [[Time Trials]]. After beating the Normal Staff [[Ghost]]s by a certain margin of time, you can unlock the Expert Staff Ghosts. The following are the approximated times for unlocking them:

== Description ==
Staff Ghosts debuted in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''; however they lacked a name. Normally Staff Ghosts use racers who are closely associated with the course, otherwise the racers they choose for the course remain unknown why they were chosen. In ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' and onward, the Staff Ghosts have names starting with "Nin★". ''Mario Kart DS'' Staff Ghosts have Japanese names in kanji which was changed in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', they had Staff Ghosts whose name starts with "Ret★" representing [[Retro Studios]]. In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', Staff Ghosts' country are shown in the ghost data.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', Staff Ghosts use racers depending on the speed: [[150cc]] or [[200cc]]. The 150cc has the Staff Ghosts whose racers remain the same as ''Mario Kart 8''. In 200cc, the different Staff Ghosts choose racers who are either counterparts of the racer in 150cc (ex. Baby Peach for [[Sweet Sweet Canyon]] in 150cc and [[Peach]] for Sweet Sweet Canyon in 200cc), also closely associated with the course (ex. [[Pink Gold Peach]] for [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 8)|Rainbow Road]] in 150cc and [[Rosalina]] for Rainbow Road in 200cc), or are selected for unknown reasons.

=== Expert Staff Ghosts ===
''Wii'' introduced Expert Staff Ghosts. If the player managed to succeed a certain time, the Expert Staff Ghosts replace the Normal Staff Ghosts' time. Unlike the Normal Staff Ghosts, their time is faster. Expert Staff Ghosts made their last appearance in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.
