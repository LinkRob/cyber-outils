'''Wish Stars''' are objects that are the physical embodiment of [[wish]]es in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They have the appearance of light-green stars, and appear in [[Star Hill]] after a character's wish has been granted at the [[Star Road (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Road]].
