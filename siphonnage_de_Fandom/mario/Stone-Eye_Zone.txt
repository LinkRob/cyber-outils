'''Stone-Eye Zone''', is the first level of [[Layer-Cake Desert]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It features several [[Stone-Eye]]s and [[Sand Geyser]]s, and various ice cream cones and a large cake are visible in the background.  

Completing this course unlocks [[Perilous Pokey Cave]] and [[Fire Snake Cavern]] as well as a red [[Toad House]]. In addition, it also triggers the first instance of [[Nabbit]] and unlocks the [[Pipe Cannon]]s used to get from world to world.

Stone-Eye Zone is the fourth property on the game board in ''[[Monopoly Gamer]]''. It is one of two properties in the Light Blue Color Set (along with [[Layer-Cake Desert]]).

==Layout==
The level begins with a [[? Block]] containing a power-up, along with a [[Stone-Eye]] moving in a pit of [[quicksand]]. To the left of the starting area is another Stone-Eye. Above it is a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[Super Acorn]]. Some [[Waddlewing]]s are found, along with palm trees that act as platforms. Another Stone-Eye is found, along with a [[? Switch]]. A section with multiple Stone-Eyes with [[Koopa Troopa]]s on them follows, with [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]]s flying above. Another Stone-Eye is found, along with some [[Piranha Plant]]s and [[Goomba]]s. A Koopa Troopa is on a palm tree. More Waddlewings are found, one of which is holding a Super Acorn, and are followed by the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. Moving Stone-Eyes and [[Sand Geyser]]s act as platforms to cross pits. Paratroopas are found in between the platforms. Following a series of large Stone-Eyes that begin to lower, the [[Goal Pole]] is found.
==[[Star Coin]]s== 
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin sits in plain sight, and the player must leap off the Stone-Eye below to obtain it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The player must use the [[Flying Squirrel Mario|Flying Squirrel suit]] or [[Peachette]] (''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' only) to reach a [[Warp Pipe]] on the tree after the [[Checkpoint Flag]]; it leads to the second Star Coin. In multiplayer mode, one player can also jump on another player's head to reach the Warp Pipe.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Within a [[Sand Geyser]], the third Star Coin can be collected by waiting until the geyser comes up, or when the Stone-Eye moves so the player can grab it while the geyser is up.
