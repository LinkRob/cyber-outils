A '''cannon''' is a recurring object in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.

==Description==
A cannon is only featured in a few courses throughout its appearances, such as [[DK Mountain]] or [[Maple Treeway]]. Whenever a racer drives into a cannon, it launches them at a very high speed to another section of the course. Cannons most commonly have the appearance of a Barrel Cannon, but their designs may vary, such as [[Vancouver Velocity]] using a circus cannon in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and [[Sweet Sweet Canyon]] using an ice cream cone in the latter game..

In ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', a racer is not affected by any item while blasting from a cannon, even [[Lightning]] and [[Spiny Shell (blue)|Blue Spiny Shells]], and a cannon is preceded by a [[Dash Panel]]. As of ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', racers are no longer invulnerable to items while blasting from a cannon, and every cannon is preceded by a [[Glide Ramp]] rather than a Dash Panel.
