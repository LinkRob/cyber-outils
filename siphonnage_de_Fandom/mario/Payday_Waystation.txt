'''Payday Waystation''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
It is a short station that is a subsection of the [[board]] [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]]. It can be reached only if the player lands on the board's [[Lucky Space]]. Upon doing so, the train quickly stops at Payday Waystation. Rgw character who landed on the Lucky Space gets off of the train to the Payday Waystation.

The station is five [[Blue Space]]s long, and each Blue Space has three [[coin]]s. At the end of the station is a [[Shy Guy]] conductor, who gives the player a free [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] for reaching him. Immediately afterward, a handcar operated by a few Shy Guys arrives at the station. The character [[jump]]s on and is brought back to the train's caboose.
