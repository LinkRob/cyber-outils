'''Flagpole Leap''' is a [[minigame]] in [[World 1 (Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix)|World 1]] of ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame is somewhat based on ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', as it features a flagpole and a nearby [[castle]] in the background, although the roofs of the castle are all red mushroom caps covered in white spots. 

==Gameplay==
The object is for [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to get as high as possible on the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]]. The player has to rapidly alternate between ← and → on the [[Nintendo GameCube Action Pad]] to have Mario or Luigi run faster. When Mario or Luigi reach the white line, the player must hit ↑ for them to [[jump]] to the flagpole, and faster runs allow for Mario or Luigi to perform higher jumps. If Mario or Luigi does not jump, he runs into the flagpole and does not earn any [[point]]s.
