A {{Nihongo|'''Rubee'''|マネー|Manē|Money}} is a fake, foreign currency in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. Rubees are predominant features in Chapter 2-3 and 2-4 where they were searching for [[Merlee]] in [[Merlee's Mansion]].

== In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ==
[[Mimi]] forces [[Mario]] to work in a sweatshop-like area until he can pay his 1,000,000-Rubee debt, or until he can buy the safe combination of a slave, with the safe holding one million Rupees. Mario pays Mimi at the counter. All of a sudden, she was disappointed that it happened and disappeared. Tippi found out she placed the curse in the mansion. When they came to the [[Merlee's Basement|basement]], they find Merlee who turns out to be Mimi forcing Mario to sign up. Mimi transforms into her true form and chases Mario using the Rubees to attack him.

During boss battles, Mimi uses Rubees to attack Mario and his party, but [[Thoreau]] can grab Rubees while [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can shield herself with her [[Peach's Parasol|parasol]].

== Trivia ==
*The word Rubee is a pun on [[Rupee]]s from ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' series and the fact that Rubees resemble {{w|ruby|rubies}}.
*The combination for the safe is always the same (41262816).
