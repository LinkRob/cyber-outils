'''Game Boy''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''. It is one of [[9-Volt]]'s microgames, categorized under the [[Nintendo Classics]] folder.

==Layout==
A [[Game Boy]] is placed in the center, and a Game Pak moves left and right above it. It does this four times before the microgame timer runs out. The Game Boy is placed lower with each increased difficulty level.

==Gameplay==
The player must press {{button|gba|A}} once the Game Pak is above the Game Boy's cartridge to insert it. If done successfully, they win the microgame, but if the player misses or does not attempt to insert, they lose at the microgame.
