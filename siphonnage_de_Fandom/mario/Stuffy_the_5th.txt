'''Stuffy the 5th''' is an enemy that appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is a statue that honors the actual Stuffy the 5th, the fifth ruler of the Dolphinian empire.

==History==
Stuffy the 5th can be found in a room of [[Blowhole Castle]] where it was guarding the chest to the [[Wandering Soul]] which is needed to progress through the level. Stuffy the 5th attacks by shooting electric balls at [[Wario]] and they can change their direction should Wario try to get behind them. However, they are fixed in place and will not attempt to go after Wario. The recommended way to defeat Stuffy the 5th is to either shoot them with [[Cosmic Wario]]'s lasers or use [[Dragon Wario]]'s fire breath.
