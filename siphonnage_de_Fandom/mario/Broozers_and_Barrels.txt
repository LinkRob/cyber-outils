'''Broozers and Barrels''' is a level in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.
== [[Star Coin]]s ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Jump on the right Broozer of the group of four Broozers on the same platform to land on the platform to the right above. After that, jump on the hidden jump through platform to get the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Over the pipes of the Venus Ice Traps, jump to the platform over the coin and jump over the Broozer on that platform to have it destroy the box blocking the path to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Jump on the top of the box platforms near the yellow wrap pipe before the Broozer destroys the bottom box with a barrel. You can even jump on Broozer before it falls to get the coin.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Create a firework show by reaching the flagpole the timer's at a multiple of eleven. Pass the castle near the Toad giving Luigi an item shows Luigi as a snowman.
