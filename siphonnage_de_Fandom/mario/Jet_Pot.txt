The '''Jet Pot''' is a power-up item featured in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. It is a [[Power Up Pot]] that transforms [[Wario]] into [[Jet Wario]]. The Jet Pot somewhat bears the shape of a rabbit head, but with a pipe on top.
