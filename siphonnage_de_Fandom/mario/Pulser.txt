'''Pulser''' is a character appearing in [[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope|''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'']].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Pulser is a cream-colored Spark. As with other Sparks, a pair of rabbit ears rises from a point straddling the tuft on top of her body, and she has a face similar to those of the [https://rabbids.fandom.com/wiki/Rabbids Rabbids]: bulbous blue eyes and a mouth with teeth. Unlike most Sparks, she has a quadruped build, with the legs positioned more backward and the arms positioned towards the bottom front. Behind her is a heart outline.

== In ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'' ==
Like other [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Sparks]], Pulser used to be an ordinary [[Luma]] before she and the other Lumas who would become [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Sparks]] had their first encounter with the [https://rabbids.fandom.com/wiki/Rabbids Rabbids], turning them into Sparks.

Pulser is first seen in the second [[Darkmess Tentacle]] battle on the planet [[Terra Flora]], where she claims to be stuck, and tells [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and company that to free her, they must win the battle. After the gang wins the battle, she joins the heroic party and can be used in battle.

During combat, Pulser has the ability to revive [[Death (Super Mario Bros. RPG's)|characters who have fallen in battle]], with the range of her reviving ability being 11 metres with a cooldown of four turns. The amount of health she restores when reviving a dead character can be increased if she is fed [[Star Bit|Star Bits]]. Pulser can also amplify healing, which can also be upgraded similarly.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Pulser most likely derives her name from a heart's pulse, reflecting how she helps out with healing and revival.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Pulser is called "QQ Rescue" (or ''Kyū Kyū Resukyu''; written キューキューレスキュ). Spanish translates her name as ''Estelázaro'', from ''estrella'' ("star") and the given name ''Lázaro'' (in reference to the Biblical character Lazarus who was revived by Jesus, referencing how she can bring dead heroes back to life). In Portuguese, she is called ''Pulsar'' ("to pulse"). Italian refers to her as ''Vivistello'', from the verb ''vívere'' ("to live") and ''stella'' ("star"). In French, she is called ''Stellazare'', from Latin ''stella'' ("star") and the given name ''Lazare'' (referencing the Biblical character Lazarus who was brought back from the dead, similarly to how she can revive dead characters). In Dutch, Pulser has the name ''Impulsar'', from "impulse" mixed with "pulsar."  German gives her name as ''Reanimond'', from "reanimate" and "diamond." Her Russian name, ''Pul'sor'' (written Пульсор), is derived from her English name.
