'''Pink Fuzzies''' are a variant of [[Fuzzy]] appearing exclusively in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', they are stronger and faster versions of the Fuzzies.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==


===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Pink Fuzzies appear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', in this game, they act very similarly to regular [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] hopping towards their foes except they’re faster and stronger. Their attack power and HP have doubled, making them one of the stronger Fuzzies. It’s generally recommended to use [[Bowser]] or [[Cudge]] when dealing with Pink Fuzzies. Pink Fuzzies can be found on [[Planet Blobule]] and the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]].
{{br|left}}


==Trivia==
*Pink Fuzzies strongly resemble [[Flower Fuzzy|Flower Fuzzies]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|it’s remake]] however, Flower Fuzzies are a special variant capable of sucking of [[FP]] and preforming devastating attacks with it while Pink Fuzzies are simply a faster and stronger variant.
