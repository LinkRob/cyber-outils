The '''Coin Express''' is a secret level found in [[World 5 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 5]] of ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Features==
There are several [[Coin]]s in the level and therefore several opportunities to earn [[1-Up]]s. However, once the player dies or complete the level, it is gone for 24 hours.
