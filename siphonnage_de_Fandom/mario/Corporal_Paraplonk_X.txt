'''Corporal Paraplonk X''' is a boss in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

== Battle ==
Corporal Paraplonk X has either the choice to use a [[Mushroom (healing item)|Mushroom]] on one of his allies or summon two [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopas]] to place two blocks next to Mario. This attack will continue indefinitely until Mario gets either hit or counters it by jumping on his shell. This will cause him to crash into the line of [[Sub-Goomba X]]'s horizontally before [[Private Goomp X]] and damage them including Private Goomp X. Afterwards Corporal Paraplonk also ricochets to the background damaging the first row of Sub-Goomba X's.
