'''Cudge''' is a [[pixl]] from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Cudge is found in the [[Land of the Cragnons]] at the [[Gap of Crag]], after obtaining the three tablets of Water, Fire, and Earth. [[Mario]] travels through World 5-2 around the mountains, and finds Cudge, after the three tablets are put into place, at the peak of the tallest mountain in the land.

After saved by Mario and his friends, Cudge joins his party. While in tow, Cudge can be used by Mario or his party as a [[hammer]] attack, similar to that of other [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' games]]. The hammer attack is stronger than Mario's regular attack, and can be used to break certain blocks or obstacles.
