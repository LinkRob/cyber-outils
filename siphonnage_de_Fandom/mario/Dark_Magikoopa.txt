A '''Dark Magikoopa''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Magikoopas reside in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]], being the strongest variant of [[Magikoopa]]s encountered. Like with regular Magikoopas, Dark Magikoopas attack with a magical spell. They can also be sometimes found flying around on broomsticks.
