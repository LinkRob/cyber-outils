'''''Game Boy''''' is a series of comics produced by [[Valiant Comics]] between June and September [[1990]], as part of their ''[[Nintendo Comics System]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://valiantarchive.com/valiant/nintendo/nintendo.html|title=Nintendo|publisher=Valiant Firsts|language=English|accessdate=22 June 2024}}</ref> It consists of only four issues. Although the title suggests a focus on [[Game Boy]] games in general, ''[[Super Mario Land]]'' is the game focused on throughout the entire comic.

The comics feature an exclusive character named [[Herman Smirch]], a quasi-hero of the storyline. He was an ill-willed character whom [[Tatanga]], the main antagonist from ''Super Mario Land'', could easily hypnotize into doing his bidding, due to the bitterness in the man's cynical soul. In each issue, Smirch would release Tatanga and his minions into the real world, and a child would then release a tiny [[Mario]] to battle them and try to save [[Princess Daisy]].
