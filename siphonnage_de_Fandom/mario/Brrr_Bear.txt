'''Brrr Bears''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Brrr Bears have the appearance of a light blue bear with a large belly.

===Behavior===
Brrr Bears act similar to the [[Yukimaru]] from ''[[Wario Land II]]''. They attack [[Wario]] by shooting snowflakes at him, and if Wario touches a snowflake, he becomes [[Frozen Wario|Ice Skatin' Wario]].

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Brrr Bears are encountered in several levels, the first time being in [[A Town in Chaos]]. If Wario performs a powered-up [[Ground Pound (move)|Smash Attack]], resulting in a small earthquake, he can stun Brrr Bears temporarily. Wario can defeat a Brrr Bear by either [[jump]]ing onto it or by using his [[Dash Attack]] on it.
