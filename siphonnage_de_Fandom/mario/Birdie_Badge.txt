'''Birdie Badges''' are an item in ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for [[Nintendo 64]].

==Description==
A Birdie Badge is a gold coin depicting [[Mario]]'s face on the front. Players can only earn these in tournaments for each hole they play. The player earns one Birdie Badge for every time they score below [[Par]]. The player can collect up to 108 Birdie Badges.

[[Maple]] and [[Metal Mario (character)|Metal Mario]] can be unlocked by collecting 50 and all 108 Birdie Badges, respectively.
