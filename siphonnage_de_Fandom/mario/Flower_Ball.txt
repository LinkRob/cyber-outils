'''Flower Ball''' is the [[Star Skill]] used by [[Princess Daisy]] in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and her [[Star Pitch]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Flower Ball is similar to [[Princess Peach]]’s [[Heart Ball]] move in that it disguises the baseball when thrown, potentially confusing the batter about the ball’s location, whether Daisy is pitching or batting. Flower Ball specifically conceals the ball within several light orange petals. If Flower Ball is performed while batting, the petals begin to disappear as the ball travels toward the ground. If a fielder does not catch the ball and it hits the ground, it is no longer obscured by petals.

In [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge Mode]] of ''Mario Superstar Baseball'', the player's team has to purchase [[Pretty Flowers]] before Daisy can use Flower Ball.

In ''Mario Super Sluggers'', Daisy only uses Flower Ball for pitching, and was instead given [[Flower Swing]] as her [[Star Swing]] equivalent.
