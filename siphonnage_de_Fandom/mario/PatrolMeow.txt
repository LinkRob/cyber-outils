}}A '''PatrolMeow''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
PatrolMeows are robots created by [[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]] that are found in hallways of [[Fort Francis]]. PatrolMeows patrol the halls until the spot any intruders. When they do, they will raise their alarms and attempt to charge toward the intruder. They can be easily dispatched of, however.
