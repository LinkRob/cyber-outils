'''Bowser Wrestling''' is a one-on-one [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. Normally played against [[Bowser]] in Party Mode or Story Mode at [[Bowser's Gnarly Party]], this minigame can also be played with a friend once it has been played against Bowser.

==Gameplay==
Players stand on a small circular platform, with [[Koopa Kid]] positioned in the center as the referee. When Koopa Kid shouts "Stomp 'em!", the match begins. Players must try to shove each other out of the ring. However, doing so decreases the attacker's [[Heart Point|health]], which is represented by five [[Golden Mushroom]]s displayed above the character. Occasionally, actual Golden Mushrooms fall from the sky. These mushrooms restore stamina and grant temporary invincibility—along with non-decreasing stamina—to the player who collects them. The player who successfully shoves their opponent out of the ring wins the match.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Advance on opponent (used only at the start of the match)
* {{button|gcn|L}} '''→''' {{button|gcn|R}} (alternately) &ndash; Shove opponent
