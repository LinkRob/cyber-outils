|name=Fire Flower (Battle Card)}}'''Fire Flower''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Fire Flower is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s fire [[Flower attack.]] It is a sentient red flower with a green stem, accompanied with a red/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 40/200 coins

Sell Price: 20/100 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Fire Flower is first seen in [[Ruddy Road]], but can also found in [[Indigo Underground]], [[Daffodil Peak]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Marmalade Valley]], [[Kiwano Temple]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Vortex Island]], [[Tangerino Grill]], [[Redpepper Crater]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
