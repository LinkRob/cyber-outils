'''Terror Tennis''' is a [[Minigame|Special Game]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

== Description ==
Terror Tennis takes place in the [[Luigi's Mansion Court]] where players score points by hitting [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] back to their [[portrait]]s. There are [[Gold Ghost]]s, [[Purple Puncher]]s, [[Blue Twirler]]s, and [[Garbage Can Ghost]]s. The player can earn more [[point]]s by hitting a ghost instead of its portrait. If the player either misses the ball or hits it improperly, a [[Bowling Ghost]] throws them a new ball.

The scoring is as follows:

*Portrait - 1 point
*Shaking Portrait - 2 points
*Trapped Ghost - 3 points
*Free Ghost - 5 points
