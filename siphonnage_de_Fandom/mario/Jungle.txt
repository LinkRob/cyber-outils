'''Jungle''' is the first world found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' being located in [[Donkey Kong Island]] where the [[Kong Family]], the [[Animal Buddy|Animal Buddies]], and [[Mugly]] lives at. Mugly is also the boss of this world as [[Kalimba]], a [[Tiki]], hypnotized him to attack [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]]. Donkey Kong's Banana Hoard and Treehouse are also found here.

==Trivia==
*[[Kongo Jungle]]'s [[Jungle Hijinxs]] has the same name as ''Donkey Kong Country Returns'' does and both are the first level of the games respectively.
