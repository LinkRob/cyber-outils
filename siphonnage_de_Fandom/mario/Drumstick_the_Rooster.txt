'''Drumstick the Rooster''' is an unlockable playable character in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]'' who was turned into a frog by [[Wizpig]]. Drumstick is the second fastest driver with [[T.T. the Timer|T.T.]] being in first place. He appears to be wearing a white T-shirt with blue overalls on him.

He can be unlocked by thirty nine balloons and four gold trophies in the [[Nintendo 64]] version and can be unlocked in the [[Nintendo DS]] version by flicking a frog with a red comb on a cliff. Then, the frog with a rooster comb comes down. The player must run over it to unlock Drumstick. His instrument is the violin or the fiddle as shown in the intro of ''Diddy Kong Racing''.
