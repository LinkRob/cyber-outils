A '''Super Drop''' is an item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It has two more effective variations, [[Super Drop]]s and [[Ultra Drop]]s, and all of them use the same inventory sprite. If used, a Super Drop restores fifteen [[HP]] for all party members, effectively taking the role of [[Nut]]s from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. Certain [[Item Shop]]s sell Mushroom Drops for 20 [[coin]]s each. [[Dr. Shroob]]s and [[Pidgit]]s each have a 15% chance of dropping a Mushroom Drop when defeated, while the [[L33t Hamm3r Broz.]] have a 50% chance.
