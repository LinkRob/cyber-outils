The '''Club Dyode Ticket''' is a key item from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']]. They are used to get into [[Club Dyode]] on [[Jellyfish Island]].

== Description ==
Club Dyode Tickets are black tickets with a pink soundwave running along their length and a pair of joined sixteenth notes at their centre.

== History ==
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are given the Club Dyode Tickets, which must be shown to Club Dyode's barman, after they participate in a dancing minigame with [[Groovemaster Dyode]] and [[Emmit]] just before the club. This is necessary to contact [[Adaphne]], who is inside the club.
