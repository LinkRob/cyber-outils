The '''Beauty Badge''' is a type of [[badge]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is only sold at the clothes shop in [[Beanbean Castle Town]].

==Description==
Despite its name, the Beauty Badge does not have any cosmetic effect but rather just increases [[Power|POW]] of its holder. Furthermore, the Beauty Badge has the same icon as the other badges, although the Beauty Badge's in-game description describes it as "sparkling" and "beautiful."

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Beauty Badge costs 200 [[coin]]s to buy, or 250 in the Japanese version. Equipping it increases its user's POW by 28, or 30 in the Japanese version.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Beauty Badge is priced at 250 coins like in the original's Japanese version, and it can be sold for 63 coins. It now increases its user's POW by 44 as well as both their HP and BP by 10 each. Here, the Beauty Badge becomes available to buy after [[Princess Peach|Peach]] has been kidnapped by [[Cackletta]].
