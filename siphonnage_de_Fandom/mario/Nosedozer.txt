'''Nosedozers''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They have white eyes, red forehead, yellow body and a drill.

===Behavior===
A Nosedozer can use its drill to damage [[Wario]] on contact.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Nosedozers have a threat rating of one star. To defeat one, Wario must either execute an [[Earthshake Punch]], [[jump]] on it, or perform a [[Dash Attack]] on their back. While a Nosedozer is stunned, Wario cannot jump onto it, and he instead has to defeat it using either a [[Dash Attack]] or by throwing another enemy at it.
