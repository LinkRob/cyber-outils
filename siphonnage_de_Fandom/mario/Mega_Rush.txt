'''Mega Rush''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. [[Mario]] can wear it by paying one [[Badge Point|BP]], and wearing Mega Rush grants Mario's [[Attack]] by four whenever he is in [[peril]].

== Appearances ==
=== ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ===
[[File:Mega_rush_badge.png|thumb|left]]
In ''Paper Mario'', this badge is in a secret room in a secret room in [[Tubba Blubba's Castle]] on the left side of the castle, blocked by a grandfather clock.

Once the clock is moved, Mario can access a secret bedroom, climb the bookcase in the room and use [[Parakarry]] to fly to the bed, then jump onto the shelf at the left and enter a hidden area with the badge.

=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', Mario can acquire this badge by purchasing this badge from [[Charlieton]] for sixty [[coin]]s. The badge sports its abilities from ''Paper Mario'', and was also given a partner variant named [[Mega Rush P]].
