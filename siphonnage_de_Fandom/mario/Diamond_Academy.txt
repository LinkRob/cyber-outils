'''Diamond Academy''' is an academy in [[Diamond City]] that [[Penny]] attends in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==History==
The auditorium of the school is seen in the game. It was the place where Penny competed in the Great Invent-off against her grandfather to see who was the bad inventor. For that particular event, there was a boxing ring and an audience seemingly made up of [[Mr. Game & Watch]]es.
