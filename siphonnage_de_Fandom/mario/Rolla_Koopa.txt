'''Rolla Koopas''' (or '''Pink Shell Koopas''') are a variant of [[Koopa Troopa]]s and an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They wear a magenta-colored [[Koopa Shell|shell]], but are distinguished from the regular variant by their rollerblade shoes, which allow them to travel more quickly, and can also jump gaps. A Rolla Koopa casually strolls through a level while it rides its roller blades. It is featured prominently in Rolla Koopa Derby, the level named after these enemies. Rolla Koopas often travel in groups.
