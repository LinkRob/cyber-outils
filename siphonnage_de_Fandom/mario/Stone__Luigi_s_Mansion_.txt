'''Stones''' are items in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
There are three types of stones—[[Blue Stone]]s, [[Green Stone]]s, and [[Red Stone]]s—worth 500,000G, 800,000G, and 1,000,000G respectively. Aside from their difference in color and value, there are no other differences between the three stones.

==History==
In ''Luigi's Mansion'', Luigi can find the Stones through various means: watering certain [[plant]]s; examining certain objects, which can include buckets, gutters, and gold chandeliers; catching [[Speedy Spirit]]s; and opening certain green [[treasure chest]]s, from which they are released with other [[treasure]]s. The [[Hidden Room]] is the only room that features all three types of stones.

There are a total of thirty Stones, ten of which are Red Stones, Green Stones, and Blue Stones respectively, totaling 23,000,000G. One more Green Stone was added to the Nintendo 3DS version, making a new total of 23,800,000G. In the PAL version's [[Hidden Mansion]], there are only five of each type of Stone, halving the total to fifteen Stones, because the majority of Speedy Spirits and [[Gold Mouse|Gold Mice]] drop [[Blue Diamond]]s instead.
