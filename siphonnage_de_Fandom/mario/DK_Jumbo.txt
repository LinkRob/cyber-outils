'''DK Jumbo''' (or ''Donkey Kong Jumbo'') is a heavyweight [[kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is [[Donkey Kong]]'s signature kart, in part evidenced by how it is made out of wooden barrels, logs, and has a DK sign on the front. Any Heavy character can use this kart. The DK Jumbo has more weight than [[Parade Kart]] and [[Wario Car]].

While [[Diddy Kong]], who is partnered with Donkey Kong, often rides the DK Jumbo with him, Diddy his his own signature kart, the [[Barrel Train]].
