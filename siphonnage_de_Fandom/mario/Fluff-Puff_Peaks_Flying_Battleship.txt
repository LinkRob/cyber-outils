'''Fluff-Puff Peaks Flying Battleship''' is an autoscrolling level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
==Layout==
The level features many [[Baboom]]s and crates. The level opens with various crates and babooms. Later in the level, the player is encouraged to use a baboom to break [[Hard Block]]s.

Eventually, there is the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will cause [[Castle Bowser]] to try and shoot the player with green explosions. The explosions can break metal blocks. This must be used to obtain the [[Wonder Seed]] blocked off by metal blocks.

At the end of the level, there is a room with a machine spawning [[Bob-omb]]s and metal blocks. Presses can crush the player in the room. To end the level, the player must jump on a  big red button atop the machine.
==Items==
The [[10-Flower Coin]]s can be obtained in the following order:
#There is a 10-flower coin on ground above a few crates.
#A [[pipe]] is blocked off by hard blocks. A baboom must be used to break the hard blocks and enter the pipe. In the room where the pipe brings the player, is a 10-flower coin on the top blocked off by hard blocks. A baboom must be used to break the hard block and drop the 10-flower coin.
#After the second [[Checkpoint Flag]], the player must push a block with a [[cannon]] into a hole to be able to enter a pipe. The room where the pipe sends the player, there is a 10-flower coin next to some [[coin]]s.
