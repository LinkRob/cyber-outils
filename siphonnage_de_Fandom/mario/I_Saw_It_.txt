'''I Saw It!''' is one of [[Mona]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''

==Gameplay==
The object is to cut through the log. To accomplish this, the player has to tap the two hands holding the saw, one after another. If the player only touches one hand, the other one becomes angry and prevents the other hand from moving. When the log is cut, the player wins, and either a sage, a rocket, or the [[Baker (Mario Paint)|Baker]] from ''[[Mario Paint]]'' is revealed. If time runs out and the log has not been completely cut through, the player loses.
