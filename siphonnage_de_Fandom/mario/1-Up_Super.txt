{{Nihongo|'''1-Up Super'''|1UPキノコDX|1UP Kinoko DX|1-Up Mushroom DX}} (also known as a '''1-Up DX''' and '''1-Up Deluxe''') is an [[item]] in every game of the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

== Description ==
1-Up Supers are a more effective variation of a [[1-Up Mushroom]]; aside from reviving a fallen Bro, a 1-Up Super also restores their [[Heart Point|HP]] fully rather than halfway like a 1-Up Mushroom. Because they are more effective, 1-Up Supers are generally more expensive and are less often found in [[block]]s than normal 1-Ups.

In the first two ''Mario & Luigi'' games, they are known as 1-Up Supers, but in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', they were renamed to '''1-Up Deluxes'''. They have maintained that name in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. In ''Paper Jam'', they are referred to as '''1-Up DX'''. In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', they are once again called 1-Up Supers.

== In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', one of these items are dropped by [[Dragohoho]], [[Queen Bean]], [[Cackletta]], [[Clumph]], [[Jojora]], [[Ludwig von Koopa]] and [[Fawful]] even when he is airborne. 1-Up Supers can be bought from certain [[Item Shop]]s for 240 [[coin]]s.
{{sectstub}}

===''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''===
Certain Item Shops sell 1-Up Supers at 120 coins, exactly half the price as in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''.
