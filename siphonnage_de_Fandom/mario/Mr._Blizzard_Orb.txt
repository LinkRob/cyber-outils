The '''Mr. Blizzard Capsule''' is a red [[Orb|capsule]] found in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', where it can be thrown to a [[space]] on the [[board]], or used on the player for ten [[coin]]s. Its effect is that it makes a [[Mr. Blizzard]] fall flat on top of them, making them lose all their capsules. In ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]'', it is known as the '''Mr. Blizzard Orb''', and costs ten coins or five during a flash sale at the [[Orb Hut]]. It has the same effect, however.
