A '''Dark Boomerang Bro''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
These dark versions of [[Boomerang Bro]]s can only be found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Like their counterparts, Dark Boomerang Bros attack by tossing boomerangs at their targets. There isn't anything else special about Dark Boomerang Bros except having more HP and attack than regular Boomerang Bros.
