'''Rudder Madness''' is a 4-player minigame that appears in the game ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
The players are located in a dirty and muddy swamp riding on lily pads. The goal of this mini-game is to reach the finish line, however, there are many obstacles and objects that the player must avoid. If a player bumps and crashes into one, they lose time. The first player to cross the finish line will win the game.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|wiimote}} - Tilt to Steer Rudder

==Trivia==
*The name of the mini-game is a pun on the phrase "utter madness."
