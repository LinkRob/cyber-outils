'''Ba'''-'''Boom, the Boisterous '''is the third boss of the game [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]. He is the boss of [[Bright Savannah]], appearing in the level '''Triple Trouble'''. Before the battle, the Kongs notice a [[Banana Bunch]] in the air by a wooden pole. As they try to grab it, Ba-Boom snatches the bunch while mocking them. He then creates two copies of himself, whom also mock the Kongs, angering them.

== General Information ==

{{Empty section|date=May 2023}}

== Physical Description ==
Ba-Boom is a black baboon with blue cheeks and messy hair. He wears a hat with the [[Snowmad]] symbol as well as blue bracelets on his arms and legs. Ba-Boom's clones look exactly like him except one of them has light-blue cheeks and bracelets while the other has red cheeks and bracelets.

== Attacks ==
Ba-Boom and his copies rely on their speed to attack. They start by swinging on ropes, swinging hammers. Occasionally, Ba-Boom and/or a copy may forget to swing a hammer, rendering them vulnerable. Another attack involves him and his clones jumping down and rolling at [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]; this must be avoided by jumping. Ba-Boom and the clones also become vulnerable upon striking a wall. After Donkey Kong destroys one clone by hitting it thrice, Ba-Boom and his remaining copy move to the second phase, whereupon they start throwing bombs. After Donkey Kong destroys the second clone, Ba-Boom enters his final phase, spawning two more clones. The three Ba-Booms can form a chain while swinging and throwing bombs, which must be thrown back at the Ba-Boom chain. Ba-Boom and his copies can also swing separately on ropes, throwing both hammers and bombs. After he takes three hits (The fake Ba-Booms cannot take damage.) Ba-Boom is defeated and can be punched into the background.

{{Empty section|date=May 2023}}

== Combat ==
To defeat Ba-Boom, the Kongs will have to damage the trio nine times, either by jumping on them or by throwing the watermelon bombs at them.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Ba-Boom seem to be a mix of baboons and mandrills, referencing how the latter were formerly classified as the former. His name is also a pun on "baboon" and the explosion onomatopoeia "ka-boom."

"Boisterous" means noisy and energetic.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japan, Ba-Boom is called "''Babūn Burazāzu''," a Japanese transliteration of "baboon brothers" (written バブーンブラザーズ), referencing his duplication abilities. In Spanish, he is called "Babum el Bombardero," with "Babum" coming from "baboon" and the Spanish onomatopoeia ''bum'', which can be used to represent explosion, and ''bombardero'' referring to someone who uses bombs to attack. His Italian name is ''Babum'', from the same source, with his full name including his title being ''Babum il'' ''babbuiníngia'', from ''babbuino'' ("babboon") and "ninja." In French, Ba-Boom himself is called ''Ba'', with his copies being called ''Da'' and ''Boum''. German similarly considers his clones separate beings, and all three are called "Die Radikalen Rambazambas" ("the Radical Rambazambas").
