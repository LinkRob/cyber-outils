'''Wing Shot''' is the [[Special Shot]] of [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can have Paratroopa perform Wing Shot by tapping out an uppercase "N" twice. The ''N'' presumably stands for "Nokonoko," the Japanese name for [[Koopa Troopa]].

Paratroopa begins the shot by flying in an infinity symbol (∞) shape twice while the ball glows orange and bounces in an ''N'' shape. Paratroopa then flies backward and quickly spirals into the basket as a [[tornado]] and feathers trail him.

If Wing Shot is used outside the three-pointer line, Paratroopa does not fly backwards but does an upwards corkscrew instead before shooting the ball toward the basket. One of Paratroopa's teammates then has to catch the ball to dunk it.
