'''Axem Pink''' is a member and the sole female of the [[Smithy Gang]]'s [[Axem Rangers]], a major antagonist and boss in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its upcoming [[Super Mario RPG|2023 remake]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Axem Pink, along with the other Axem Rangers, managed to steal the [[Star Piece (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Piece]] from [[Mario]] at [[Barrel Volcano]]. The Axem Rangers attempted to flee on their battleship, the [[Blade]], but it was running late and Mario and company managed to catch up. Everyone boarded the Blade where they all engaged in a fight. Eventually, Axem Pink and the other Axem Rangers were defeated.

===Battle===
Axem Pink is the "mage" of the Axem Rangers specializing in healing techniques such as [[Recover]] and [[Mega Recover]]. However, Axem Pink is also capable of using the dreaded [[Petal Blast]] move that can turn all of Mario's party into [[Mushroom (ailment)|mushrooms]].

====Strategy====
Axem Pink is weak against fire techniques meaning Mario's fire moves (especially [[Super Flame]]) will work well against her. Axem Pink should also be targeted first as her recovery techniques can be a nuisance as she can keep herself and the other Axem Rangers healed in battle.
