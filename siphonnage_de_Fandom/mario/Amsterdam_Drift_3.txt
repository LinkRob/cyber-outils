'''Amsterdam Drift 3''' is a race track in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', being variant of [[Amsterdam Drift]]. It made its first appearance in the [[Peach vs. Bowser Tour (November 2022)|Peach vs. Bowser Tour]] in November 29, 2022. The Switch version of ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' has the racers traverse it in Lap 3 of the [[Amsterdam Drift]] track in the [[Booster Course Pass]].
== Layout ==
Focusing on the tulip gardens to the far west of the city (south in ''Mario Kart 8''), this route starts on Damrak Street, just like the other two routes, although the racers are facing in the same direction as Amsterdam Drift 2. After a slight right, they will be lead into a brief [[Propeller (Mario Kart 7)|underwater]] segment before continuing down a dirt road through a garden of flowers, where [[Piranha Plant|Piranha Plants]] will attack. After this section, the racers will get back into the city and take a sharp right, then a sharper left. After this, they will drive past the National Monument of Amsterdam before going through a Tram station, where a Tram can be encountered. If the racers crash into the Tram, they will get a boost. They then take two ninety-degree left turns to get back to the finish line.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', the racers will be sent through the final leg and the Tram section first, then take a right turn onto a side road to enter the underwater section. They then follow the course as they normally would until they get to the side road, which they are forced onto rather than entering the Tram section again, thereby finishing the course in the same direction that they started in. The Tram now causes damage.
