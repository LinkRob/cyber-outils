'''Trapdoors''' are an object in ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''.

==Description==
A Trapdoor is a wooden object with a white target painted on it. If [[Mario]] hits a trapdoor, it reveals a [[cannon]], which shoots a single [[Bob-omb]], then the trapdoor closes.

==In ''Mario Pinball Land''==
They appear in the shipwreck of the [[Frosty Frontier Stage]] and in [[Porcupuffer (boss)|Porcupuffer]]'s rematch room of the [[Fiery Stage]]. In both areas, there is a trapdoor on the left and right sides of the room, respectively. If Mario hits a trapdoor, the player earns 3,000 [[point]]s.
