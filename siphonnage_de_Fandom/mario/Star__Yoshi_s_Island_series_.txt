'''Stars''' are one of the collectible items in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. They make their first appearance in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. Collecting each Star increases the number on the [[Countdown Timer]], which starts at 10 and can go up to 30. At the end of each level, the number of Stars that a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] has is tallied up at the end, along with the number of [[Red Coin]]s and [[Smiley Flower]]s. During this, a Yoshi can receive up to 30 points for the Stars he has collected, specifically the same number on which the Countdown Timer was set. Stars are acquired through a variety of means, such as by breaking open a [[Winged Cloud]] or certain [[crate]]s.
