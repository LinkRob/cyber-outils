'''Athens Dash 1''' is a layout of [[Athens Dash]], debuting in the Spring Tour. Hence its name, it is the first route of [[Athens Dash]].

== Layout ==
Athens Dash 1's route focuses on the Théatron Dionusósio (Theatre of Dionysus) and the Apsída Hadrianósio (Hadrian's Arch, alternatively Arch of Hadrian), therefore being engulfed by the Acropolis, being more concentrated to the south.

The racers start inside the Parthenon, facing east. When they get out, they turn left and head south down the hill. They can drive on the pillars for more coins. When they reach the bottom, the racers turn left, passing a fountain. They make another left and then turn right as they drive on the seats of the Théatron Dionusósio. They head down a dirt road curving left, passing under the Apsída Hadrianósio, passing through a [[Glider]] [[Cannon (Mario Kart series)|Cannon]] to get back to the top of the mountain. They take another left turn and come to a four-way intersection, where they go straight. (The south path leads down a cliff, with the Théatron Dionusósio below it, while [[Athens Dash 2]] involves the north path.) Afterwards, the racers make one last right turn to make their way back into the Parthenon (dodging around a few columns), where the [[Finish line|Starting Line]] awaits them.

In the [[Booster Course Pass]], the racers take this route for the first lap of [[Athens Dash]].
