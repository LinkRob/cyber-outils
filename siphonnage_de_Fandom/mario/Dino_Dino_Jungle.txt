'''Dino Dino Jungle''' is a recurring course in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' as the second course of the [[Special Cup]]. Much like [[DK Mountain]], this course is jungle-themed. This course reappears in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' as a retro course. It is the second course in [[Lightning Cup]]. It has the same background music as DK Mountain.

Just like the title of the course, there are dinosaurs that roam around the jungle. One of them, a bright blue ''{{wp|Brachiosaurus|Brachiosaurus}}'' (named [[Noshi]]) he can hurt the player if he or she hit him, and causes that player to flip out. The blue [[Noshi]] is seen twice in one lap. The first time is at the beginning, when passing by its feet and going under a bridge. The second time is when coming out of a cave full of water geysers and seeing its head and neck right by the bridge the player is crossing. There is a possibility you can slip off the bridge. Also, the water geysers can pick up the racers and make them drag.

==Trivia==
*The red and yellow [[Noshi]]s are present in the original version of this stage. But only the blue [[Noshi]] appears in both the ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' versions (and the red and blue [[Noshi]]s were removed).
