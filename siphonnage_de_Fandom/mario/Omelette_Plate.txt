'''Omelette Plate''' is an item found in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Omelette Meal TTYD.png|frame|left]]
Here, it is named '''Omelette Meal''', which can be found after spending a night in the [[Poshley Heights]] Hotel or from cooking certain ingredients together. [[Zess T.]], the chef in [[Rogueport]], can also make an Omelette Meal by mixing any mushroom and a [[Mystic Egg]]. An Omelette Meal heals only five [[Heart Point|HP]] and five [[Flower Point|FP]].

===''Super Paper Mario''===
The Omelette Plate now restores 20 HP and cures [[Poison (status effect)|poison]]. It no longer restores any FP.
