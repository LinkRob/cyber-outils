'''Pi'illodactyls''' are a species of Pterodactyl native to [[Pi'illo Island]] appearing in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', they are the first instance of a background enemy that can be brought into the foreground with a [[Taunt Ball]]. Pi'illodactyls are extremely dangerous enemies with strength on par with some of the game's bosses. Pi'illodactyls specialize in fire-based attacks and also have control over the wind as well.

==History==
===''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''===
Pi'illodactyls appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', they can be found in [[Mount Pajamaja]], during battles with the various enemies on the mountain, a Pi'illodactyl may have a chance of showing up in a battle in the background, if left alone, they will spit fireballs at [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] requiring them to jump over the fireballs, these fireballs have a chance of inflicting [[Burn]] so they should be avoided at all costs. The plumbers can use a [[Taunt Ball]] to bring the Pi'illodactyl into the foreground, when the Pi'illodactyl enters the battle, it knocks out every enemy currently active bringing the sole focus on the battle to the Pi'illodactyl.

In battle, a Pi'illodactyl has a few very devastating attacks, their first attack is to spit fireballs at the plumbers which they must hop over, even when these fireballs are leapt over, they form into pools of lava which the plumbers must continue hopping over until the lava recedes, if the plumbers are hit by the lava, they may be [[Burn]]ed, another attack Pi'illodactyls possess is the ability to flap their wings at alarmingly powerful rates sending the plumbers backward, as the plumbers drift backward, boulders will bounce toward them, they must then hit them back with their [[Hammer]]s, every bit of damage they take from the boulders will push them back making timing the next few harder, when the attack is finished, the boulders that were successfully hit back will land on the Pi'illodactyl's head for some damage.

As established, Pi'illodactyls are incredibly dangerous and hit HARD, and not only that, their Defense is also much higher than most enemies in [[Mount Pajamaja]], it is highly recommended to use [[Bros. Attack]]s such as the [[Bye-Bye Cannon]] and [[Dropchopper]] to take them out. The battles may be tricky but they are rewarding as Pi'illodactyls, leave behind a lot of [[EXP]], a good grinding strategy is to eliminate the foreground enemies (to get the experience from them) until only one remains and then use the [[Taunt Ball]] on the Pi'illodactyl, if all the foreground enemies are defeated while the Pi'illodactyl is still in the background, the Pi'illodactyl will flee.

If you're going for the "No Hitter" [[Expert Challenge]], you don't need to bring the Pi'illodactyl into the foreground, dodging it's attacks from the background will still count, this is generally an easier strategy as the Pi'illodactyl's attacks from the background are far easier to dodge than when it becomes a foreground enemy.

A much stronger variety of the Pi'illodactyl, the [[Pi'illodactyl R]] is fought later in [[Somnom Woods]].
