'''Wario Wing''' is a [[glider]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is a purple counterpart of the [[Super Glider]] bearing [[Wario]]'s facial features on the top. The glider later made a reappearance in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.
