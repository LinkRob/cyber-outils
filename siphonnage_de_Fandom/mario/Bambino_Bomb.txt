'''Bambino Bomb''' is a [[Microbomb]] featured as an item in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is automatically dropped by [[Croco]] when he is defeated. [[Mario]] and his party use it only once—to break open a passage in the [[Moleville Mines|Coal Mines]].
