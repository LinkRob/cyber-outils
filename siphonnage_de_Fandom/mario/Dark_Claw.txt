'''Dark Claw''' is a special move only usable by [[Hobgoblin]]s in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, a Hobgoblin charges at a single character to attack them. The Hobgoblin has a chance of [[Poison (status effect)|poisoning]] one of [[Mario]]'s party at the same time, dealing damage in the process.
