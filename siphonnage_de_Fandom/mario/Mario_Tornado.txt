'''Mario Tornado''' is one of [[Mario]]'s moves in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, first appearing in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Mario Tornado SSBB.jpg|thumb|left|200px|''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' screenshot of Mario using Mario Tornado on Zelda and Kirby]]
Mario Tornado began as Mario's down special move in ''Super Smash Bros.'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', but since ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', it has become his down aerial, as [[F.L.U.D.D.]] was made his down special instead.

Upon using the move, Mario spins rapidly to hit opponents multiple times and finishes with a full-body stretch that launches them. If the special button is pressed rapidly, Mario can rise up in the air slightly.

Mario Tornado has two variations: [[Luigi Cyclone]] and [[Dr. Tornado]], used by [[Luigi]] and [[Dr. Mario (character)|Dr. Mario]] respectively.
