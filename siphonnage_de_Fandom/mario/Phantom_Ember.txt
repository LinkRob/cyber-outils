'''Phantom Ember''' is an enemy which appears in the [[Palace of Shadow]] and the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They can set [[Mario]] and his partners on fire, as Phantom Embers are a variant of [[Podoboo]] made of fire. They appear to be green fire with yellow demonic eyes. They also have a very similar appearance to [[Lava Bubble]].

==History==
[[Image:Phantom Ember Paper.jpg|thumb|left|A Phantom Ember as seen in the game]]
Phantom Embers are only found in the [[Palace of Shadow]] as well as the lowest floors of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]], they are the toughest variations of the [[Lava Bubble (Paper Mario)|Lava Bubble]]s. These guys are rather tough and pack quite a punch. These guys, like their counterparts will chase after [[Mario]] and his allies and attempt to [[Burn|scorch]] them. Phantom Embers have both the move sets of the [[Lava Bubble (Paper Mario)|Lava Bubble]]s and [[Ember]]s from [[Keelhaul Key]]. Due to being composed of fire, Phantom Embers are immune to jumps or any physical attacks from above. They can only be damaged with Hammers or other Projectiles; Phantom Embers are also known to take critical damage from Ice-Based attacks so that's why it is highly recommended to use such attacks when battling against multiple Phantom Embers.

==Trivia==
*[[Goombella]]'s [[tattle]] says that she has a fear of Phantom Embers.
