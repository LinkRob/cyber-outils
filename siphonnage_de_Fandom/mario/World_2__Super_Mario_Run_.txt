'''World 2''' is the second [[world]] in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. It is the only free world, and completing it unlocks [[World 3 (Super Mario Run)|World 3]].

==Levels==

=== [[Ghost-Door Deception]] ===
A ghost house level that introduces the player to Doors which can be used to travel between rooms.

=== [[Sky-High Lifts and Leaps!]] ===
A sky level that introduces line-controlled platforms.

=== [[Treasure-Hoarding Swoops]] ===
An underground level with many Swoopers.

=== [[Airship Cannons...Fire!]] ===
An airship level containing Ninjis and Cannons, as well as the game's second boss battle, against Boom Boom.
