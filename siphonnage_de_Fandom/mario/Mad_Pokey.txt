A '''Mad Pokey''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Mad Pokey is a variant of [[Pokey]] imbued with the [[Rage]] [[vibe]], indicated by its red body and angry facial expression. A Mad Pokey also has a spiky red flower on its head.

===Behavior===
A Mad Pokey usually has three body segments, but it can extend to five segments to try and prevent [[Princess Peach|Peach]] from progressing.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Mad Pokeys are first encountered in [[Wavy Beach 5-3]]. Peach can defeat a Mad Pokey either by using the Rage vibe or the [[Poundbrella]] attack.
