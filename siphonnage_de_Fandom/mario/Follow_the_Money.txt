'''Follow the Money''' is an unlockable Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
Players run and stop when they approach a course full of enemies and coins. 

== Gameplay ==
In this minigame, players can collect coins that appear on the course. While collecting coins, the camera pans forward which the players cannot come back and get more coins. Different enemies are randomized placements such as [[Grrrol]]s and a [[Mega Grrrol]], [[Whomp]]s, [[Thwomp]]s, [[Super Thwomp]]s, [[Bill Blaster]]s and [[Bullet Bill]]s, and [[Walleye]]s. Players lose three coins if they come in contact with the assigned enemy, or if they fall off the course. 

== Ending ==
Players that collected coins do their victory animations while other players without coins do their losing animation.
