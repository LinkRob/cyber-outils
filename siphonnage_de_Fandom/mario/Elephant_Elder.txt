The '''Elephant Elder''' is a character that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. She is one of the five Elders that can bestow the ancient power of [[Bananza]] upon [[Donkey Kong]] and she rules over the [[Tempest Layer]].

==Appearance==
The ELephant Elder is a very large and elderly elephant that is mainly grey and blue in color. She has a massive trunk that has a yellow, banana-like pattern going down it. Her tusks are also massive in size and they both resemble large, old bananas. She wears a blue cap on her head and blue wristbands on her arms.

==History==
The Elephant Elder is first seen where she is seated on her throne in the Tempest Layer. She informs Donkey Kong and [[Pauline]] about the strange occurrences of the intense weather that have recently been plaguing her layer making note a certain [[Void Kong|villain]] has been seen around causing trouble. Knowing they will need help, the Elephant Elder agrees to give them her Bananza form, [[Elephant Bananza]], if the two could find the pieces of her [[Ancient Record]]. After Donkey Kong and Pauline bring the Elephant Elder the Ancient Record pieces, she then teaches the two the beat and power of Elephant Bananza, an extremely helpful form that can clear through terrain most notably lava.

==Trivia==
*The Elephant Elder is the only Elder in the game that still has her previous occupation alongside her current one as a DJ (as she and others mention she is still the current head meteorologist).
