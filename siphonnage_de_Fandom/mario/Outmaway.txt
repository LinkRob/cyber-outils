The Outmaway is an enemy from ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Outmaway have round, white bodies with two loose pointy "ears" sticking up from the top of their body. Their eyes are surrounded with a red frame. They have red and yellow shoes.
=== Behavior ===
Outmaways can kick Ice Blocks in their way and can also deflect [[Koopa Shell]]s thrown at them with their kick attack. Outmaways often stay still in their spot, but can sometimes be seen behaving like [[Goombrat]]s, walking back and forth and turning at walls and/or ledges.
== In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' ==
The Outmaway is featured prominently in the level [[Outmaway Valley]]. They also appear in [[A Final Uncharted Area: Poison Ruins]]<nowiki/>and [[Break Time! Kick It, Outmaway]].

== Behind the Scenes ==
=== Name ===
The Outmaway was named after the phrase, "Get out of my way!"
