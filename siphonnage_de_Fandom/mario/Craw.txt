'''Craws''' are a bird-like species that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Most of the known Craws are affiliated with the [[Robbo Gang]], led by [[Ishnail]]. The Robbo Gang has an ongoing feud with the [[Pianta Syndicate]]. They are located in the east side of [[Rogueport]]. A similar enemy called [[Dark Craw]] exists.

The term "craw" may be a created term from the word "crow". It may also refer to the Scottish slang term for the word "crow", which is "craw".
