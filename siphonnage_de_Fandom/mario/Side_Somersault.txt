{{nihongo|'''Side somersault'''<ref name="sm64manual">{{cite manual |game=Super Mario 64|page=10 |quote=While running press {{button|n64|a}} Button as you make a U-turn.}}</ref>|横宙返り|Yoko chūgaeri|Side Somersault}} is a high jump in the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. It made its first appearance in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

== Description ==
{{Q|While running press {{button|n64|a}} Button as you make a U-turn.|''[[Super Mario 64]]'' manual, page 10<ref name="sm64manual"/>}}
Side somersault is a high jump that debuted in ''Super Mario 64''. The method benefits the player that cannot reach places with a normal jump. In ''Super Mario 64'', ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', the player can perform a side jump by pressing {{Button|default|a}} as they make a U-turn.<ref name="sm64manual"/>

== In ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' ==
Mario can perform a side somersault according to the ''Super Mario 64'' instruction booklet.

== In ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' ==
Mario can perform a side somersault in ''Sunshine''. In this case, the player must immediately tilt the {{button|gcn|stick}} to a direction. Afterwards, the player must simultaneously tilt the {{button|gcn|stick}} to the opposite direction and press {{button|gcn|a}}.<ref>
{{cite manual |game=Super Mario Sunshine |page=19 |quote=This jump comes in handy when trying to reach places a standard jump can't or for getting above large enemies.}}</ref>

== In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' ==
Mario can perform the side somersault in ''Galaxy''. The player must use the {{button|wii|stick}} and the {{button|wii|a}} like the latter method to perform the technique.

== In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' ==
Mario can perform the side somersault in ''Galaxy 2'' by using the same method in ''Galaxy'' and ''Sunshine''.
