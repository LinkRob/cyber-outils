The '''Awazon River Basin''' is the main setting of ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. One of its notable locations is the [[Aldegara Waterfall]].

==Etymology==
The first two words of the name refer to the {{w|Amazon River}}, but with the M substituted for a W, which is the same way how [[Wario]]'s name was derived of [[Mario]]'s.

==In ''Virtual Boy Wario Land''==
The Awazon River Basin is where Wario went to for a vacation. At one point, he flies his Bulldog to the Aldegara Waterfall, where he notices three [[Mask-Guy]]s taking [[treasure]] behind there. Because of his love for treasure, Wario pursues the Mask-Guys, hoping to steal the treasure. However, the floor collapses, putting Wario in various underground areas set beneath the Awazon River Basin for a majority of his adventure.
