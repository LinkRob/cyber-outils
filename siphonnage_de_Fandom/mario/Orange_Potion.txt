The '''Orange Potion''' is an important item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is a chemical that was created by the [[Secret Society of X-Nauts|X-Nauts]] for unknown purposes.

==History==
During one of the chapter intermissions, [[Princess Peach]] had to use this potion, along with the [[Blue Potion|blue]], [[Green Potion|green]], and [[Red Potion|red]] ones, in order to make herself turn invisible. With the help of [[TEC-XX]], she successfully did so, and was then able to secretly infiltrate [[Sir Grodus]]' office and steal the [[Data Disk]] that TEC-XX requested.
