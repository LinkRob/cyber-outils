'''Fava''', also named '''Old Bean''', is an elderly and wise [[Beanish]] character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. He is the younger brother of the [[Beanbean Castle Town]]'s oldest citizen.

== History ==
[[File:MLSS Beanfruit Cycle.png|thumb|left|Fava explaining the process of a Bean Fruit becoming a Neon Egg]]
Fava appears in the [[Yoshi Theater]], sitting at the far end against the projection screen. He also designed the Yoshi Theater, although Fava is more interested in the [[Neon Egg]]s. Because of this, [[Boddle]] even told [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to ask Fava where to find the Neon Eggs.

When Mario and Luigi talk to Fava, he explains that they have to collect the [[Bean Fruit]] located throughout the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] and feed each one to a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] for it to hatch a Neon Egg, which is visually demonstrated from a slide on the projection screen. To make Mario and Luigi's task easier, Fava even marks where the different Bean Fruits on their map.
