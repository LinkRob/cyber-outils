'''Toadbert's Drawing''' is an item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It consists of crudely drawn sketches done by [[Toadbert]] of his only remaining memories. Toadbert's Drawing is covered in mud, and the game's only use of the Stylus is clearing it off the drawings.
