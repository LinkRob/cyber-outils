|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Light Beam''' is a move used by a few bosses mainly by [[Belome]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Light Beam is used, a large rainbow colored orb flies at the target.

==Effects==
With Light Beam, this move is able to put one of Mario's party to [[sleep]]. This move won't work if Mario or his team is wearing a specific badge.
