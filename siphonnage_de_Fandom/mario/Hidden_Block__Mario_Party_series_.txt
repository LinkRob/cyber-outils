A '''Hidden Block''' is a recurring object in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Description==
A Hidden Block is a [[block]] containing [[coin]]s or a random item, such as a [[Skeleton Key]] or sometimes even a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]. It is invisible, and only appears if a character lands on a certain [[space]]. A Hidden Block's design varies between appearances, with it sometimes appearing as a [[Brick Block]] and other times as a small [[Treasure Chest]].

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''[[Mario Party DS]]''===
There is a one-time use item called a [[Block Sensor]], and using it causes a Hidden Block to appear on the character lands on during that turn.
