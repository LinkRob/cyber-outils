'''Danger''' is a special status condition in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
Only [[Mario]] can get this status effect when his [[Heart Point|HP]] drops to 5. In this state, Mario will hunch over looking exhausted until he is healed by any type of recovery item or Star Power move. When Mario's HP reaches 1, he will be in [[Peril]]. Although it is highly recommended to restore Mario's HP before it drops to 0, certain badges actually aid Mario when he is in Danger. These badges are the [[Close Call]] badge (which sometimes causes an enemy's attack to miss) and the [[Power Rush]] badge (which increases attack power by 2 as long Mario is in Danger).

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
Danger functions exactly the same. However, now Mario's partners can be in Danger because they now each have their own set of HP. Like with Mario, there are badges that can be used with partners when they are in Danger (the [[Close Call P]] and [[Power Rush P]] badges).

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Danger serves no other purpose but to alert the player to use a recovery item to heal (since there are no longer badges in this game).
