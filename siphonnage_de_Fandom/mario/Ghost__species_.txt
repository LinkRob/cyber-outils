'''Ghosts''' are undead creatures which appear in the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. The most recurring type of ghosts are [[Boo]]s. Ghosts themselves are the main enemies throughout the entire [[Luigi's Mansion (series)|''Luigi's Mansion'' series]].
