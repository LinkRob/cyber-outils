'''Order Up''' is a 2-vs.-2 mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' and ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The minigame takes place at a fast food restaurant. The two teams must pick the fast food items that [[Toad (species)|Toad]] orders at the restaurant. This mini-game requires memory as the food items will be shown for a few seconds before being covered up with a box and then rotated around. (Five fast food items appear --a cheeseburger, a corn dog, French fries, an ice cream cone, and a milkshake.) The team that correctly gets the food orders three times wins.

== Controls ==
The controls are the same in both of the minigame's variations.

*Control Stick: Move a character
*A button: Open a box
