'''Snails''' are a real-world gastropod species which sometimes appear in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
Snails are best known for slithering along the ground very slowly.

==In ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''==
Snails do not make any physical appearances, but two snails, one bigger than the other, can be seen on [[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart: Super Circuit)|Luigi Circuit]]'s course select icon. Here, [[Princess Peach]] and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] appear to have their [[kart]]s stopped as they wait for the snails to get across. Aside from having a pair of eyes on their stalk, these snails have little difference in physical appearance to real-world snails.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
In ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', a snail named [[Goma]] stars in the microgame [[Leafy Greens]]. Aside from having a bright, pink shell and two squares for eyes, Goma does not look that different than real-world snails. Goma has the ability to [[jump]], something that snails cannot do.
