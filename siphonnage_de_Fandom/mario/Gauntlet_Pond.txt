==Layout==
Gauntlet Pond is like most of the previous levels being a dense forest filled with poison. The only difference is the black ink spots seen on the ground thanks to the Bloopers.

==History==
[[Mario]] treads further and further into the toxic forest to look for another [[Wiggler Segment]]. He spots it sitting on a lone platform surrounded by the poisonous water. Mario eventually works his way around until he gets to the Wiggler Segment, no fight included. Mario heads further and grabs the [[Comet Piece]] completing the stage.
