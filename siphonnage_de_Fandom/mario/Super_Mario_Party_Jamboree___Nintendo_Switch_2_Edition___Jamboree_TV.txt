|nxtgame =}}'''''Super Mario Party Jamboree – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV''''' is an enhanced port version of ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'' released worldwide for the [[Nintendo Switch 2]] on July 24, 2025. It was first announced during the Nintendo Switch 2 Direct in April 4, 2025. The game adds new game modes along with visual enhancements, improved framerate, and support for CameraPlay and GameShare.
