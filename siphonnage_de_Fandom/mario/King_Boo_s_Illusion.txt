'''King Boo's Illusion''' is the final level/area in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.
==History==
After defeating the [[Tough Possessor]] in the [[Treacherous Mansion]], [[Luigi]] gets pixelated, but back at the lab, when [[Professor E. Gadd]] expects Luigi to come out of the pixelator, only the Dark Moon shard comes out. You then see Luigi's pixels surrounded in a purple aura. Then his pixels bump into each other to make Luigi, then Luigi falls to the ground of a dark room. Then he hears [[King Boo]] and certains open to reveal many windows and a picture of Mario can be seen. Then the battle starts. {{Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon Nav}}
