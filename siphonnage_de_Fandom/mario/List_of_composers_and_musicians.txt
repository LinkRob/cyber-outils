{{Listnotice|''Mario'' composers and musicians}} The ''Mario'' series is notorious for having a unique library of soundtrack, alongside its live-performed music, orchestrated performances, and the famous "Ground Theme" from the original ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' game.

Below is a list of composers and musicians involved in the ''Mario'' series, primarily for the mainline and ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series. This does not include credits from certain spin-off franchises such as the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]]'' games.

==''Super Smash Bros.'' arrangers==
The following is a list of composers, both freelance and studio-employed, that have provided ''Mario'' series arrangements for the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' games. This only includes credits for of the ''Mario'' series and does not include ''Donkey Kong'' credits, as well as arrangers who have not worked in any ''Mario'' game.

Best known for the ''Xenoblade Chronices'' games, the '''ACE''' music group have arranged the "Fortress Boss" track from ''[[Super Mario World]]'' in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''. The band group consists of Tomori Kudo and Hiroyo "Chico" Yamanaka, and sometimes Kenji Hiramatsu (in which a "+" is added when Hiramatsu is included).

====Mario track(s) arranged in====
*''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' (2014) - "Fortress Boss (Super Mario World)"

*Tomori Kudo was a guitarist for ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Musicians==
This list includes musicians who worked on live-performances for the ''Mario'' games, such as performers, conductors and others.

===HIDE+HIDE (Hideki Ishigaki, Hideki Onoue)===
'''HIDE+HIDE''' is the name of the music duo led by '''Hideaki Ishigaki''' and '''Hideki Onoue''' who are known for their work in ancient Japanese music. They have performed in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', while Ishigaki performed alone in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. Though they have worked on the arrangements for ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', none are ''Mario'' series related.

====Mario game(s) performed in====
*''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' (2013)
*''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' (2017) - Ishigaki only

====External link====
*[https://vgmdb.net/album/42692 3D World Soundtrack]

===Koji Haishima===
'''Haishima''' is a conductor who worked as the orchestra conductor for ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' which is his only Nintendo-developed work. He worked in many JRPG games such as ''Final Fantasy'' and ''Grandia'', as well as some entries of the ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario &amp; Sonic]]'' sports games.

====Mario game(s) performed in====
*''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' (2007) - Conductor - Composed by [[Mahito Yokota]] and [[Koji Kondo]]

====External link====
*[https://vgmdb.net/album/5411 Super Mario Galaxy soundtrack]

===Taizo Takemoto===
'''Takemoto''' is an orchestra conductor who worked in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', as well as the ''Smash Bros.'' games. He also conducted the music in the ''SUPER MARIO BROS. 25th Anniversary Special Sound Track PRESS START Edition'' concert.

====Mario game(s) performed in====
*''Dairantou Smash Brothers DX Orchestra Concert'' (2002)
*''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' (2010) - Conductor - Composed by [[Mahito Yokota]], Ryo Nagamatsu, and [[Koji Kondo]]
*''SUPER MARIO BROS. 25th Anniversary Special Sound Track PRESS START Edition'' (2010) - Concert conductor - Composed by [[Koji Kondo]], [[Mahito Yokota]], Kenta Nagata, Ryo Nagamatsu, and Shiho Fujii; arranged by Nobou Kurita, [[Mahito Yokota]], and Takeshi Hama

===Tomori Kudo===
'''Kudo''' served as the guitarist for ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. He is part of the '''ACE''' (see above) music group where he worked in the ''Smash Bros.'' arrangements and ''Xenoblade Chronicles'' games.

====Mario game(s) performed in====
*''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' (2013)
