'''''Super Mario-kun Volume 1''''' is the first volume of the ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' based on ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

== Description ==
'''''Super Mario-kun Volume 1''''' is the first volume of the ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' illustrated by Yukio Sawada and published by Shogakukan. The comic book is based off the ''[[Super Mario World]]'' events.
