'''Boshi''' is a [[Blue Yoshi|blue]] [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Boshi is a blue Yoshi who is somewhat different in appearance from the other Yoshis, as he wears sunglasses, a spiked red collar, and shoes allowing his three toes to poke out, instead of the usual Yoshi shoes. He also has a red crest protruding from the back of his head.

==''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
[[File:Boshi SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
Boshi lives on [[Yo'ster Isle]] with the other Yoshis. He frequently participates in [[Mushroom Derby]] races. Boshi can only speak the Yoshi language, so when Boshi talks, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] translates for [[Mario]].

In the ending, when Croco loses to Yoshi, Boshi meets [[Croco]] and gives him solace as they walk away from the celebrations.

== ''Mario Kart World'' ==
[[File:Boshi (MKWorld).jpg|thumb|[[Blue Yoshi|Yoshi (Biker)]] in ''[[Mario Kart World]]'']]
[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] (Biker) bears great resemblance to Boshi. He also has sunglasses, orange spikes, and a red spiked brace around his neck. However, this Boshi also has a vest, black shoes, and fingerless gloves.  

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
In the Japanese version, his name is Washi (ワッシー Wasshī), translating to "Bad Yoshi." This is both a play on names and words, like [[Wario]] and [[Waluigi]], combining bad (悪い warui) & yoshi (ヨッシー yosshī), which is why he is also known as "Waryoshi".

==Trivia==
*Boshi in particular is one of very few Yoshis to have a distinct appearance from other Yoshis (as other Yoshis often look the same but are different colors). The next would later be [[Village Leader]] who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
