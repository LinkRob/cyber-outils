The '''Pebble''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Pebble makes a very minor appearance, and only one Pebble can be found in one of the three pedestals at [[Shiver Mountain]]. The Pebble is essentially a dud item, as throwing it at an enemy only inflicts one [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage but is able to damage any type of enemy including those that fly and are on the ceiling.

==Trivia==
*The function of the Pebble is similar to the [[Dusty Hammer]], which also deals only one HP of damage to an enemy.
