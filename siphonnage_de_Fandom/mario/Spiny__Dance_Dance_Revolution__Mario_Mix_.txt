'''Spinies''' are a type of obstacle in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Spiny resembles a metallic sphere with spikes, and it has black rims, essentially appearing to be [[mine]]s. They are featured in red, green, and orange color variations, and the orange ones resemble [[Spiny Egg]]s.

===Behavior===
If a Spiny is stepped onto, it explodes, causing the player's dance meter to decrease significantly. In Mush Mode, the red Spinies move faster than the green ones.

==In ''Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix''==
[[File:Coin Collection.png|thumb|left|150px|Luigi [[jump]]ing over a non-explosive Spiny in Coin Collection]]
In the [[Mush Mode]] versions of "[[In the Whirlpool]]" and "[[Step by Step]]," a red or a green Spiny sometimes appears in place of an arrow. There are orange Spinies in [[Banana Storm]] and [[Coin Collection]]. In Banana Storm, [[Ukiki]]s may sometimes throw Spinies instead of [[banana]]s, and [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] have to avoid the Spinies. In Coin Collection, Mario or Luigi must avoid the Spinies while riding on a cloud, and if he hits a Spiny, he falls off.

Spinies have an ice variation, [[Ice Spiny]], which bear a significantly different appearance than regular Spinies.
