A '''coin bag''' are [[item]]s in various spinoff titles of the {{franchise|Mario}}, especially the {{series|Mario Party}}.

==Description==
Coin bags are collectible items that contain a certain number of [[coin]]s. Coin bags are almost always exclusive to [[minigame]]s, where they are less common than singular coins, so players often compete to obtain them for this reason, in addition to them being worth more coins.

==Appearances==
===''Mario Party'' series===
[[File:Dash Flow.png|thumb|Coin bags in [[Cash Flow]] from ''Mario Party 6'']]
In the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]], coin bags are worth five coins each and are usually present in coin-collecting minigames, although they are rarer than the singular coins.

In ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', some coin bags appear in the minigame [[Paratrooper Plunge]], where they give the player five coins each.

In ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', the [[Koopa Bank Capsule]] is represented by a coin bag.

In ''[[Mario Party 6]]'', in [[Castaway Bay]], the Ukiki sometimes throws at least one or even two coin bags for the character to catch among the other [[coin]]s while dodging the [[Spiny Egg]]s. Each coin bag is worth five coins, like in the main minigames.

In ''[[Mario Party 8]]'', [[MC Ballyhoo]] places coin bags onto the board's [[Red Space]]s when the game reaches the [[Last Five Turns Event|last five turns]].

===''Mario Superstar Baseball''===
In ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', coin bags appear only in the minigame [[Star Dash]], and each of them contain ten coins. Characters can touch the coin bags to obtain the coins within, with a chime also playing. If coin bags hit the ground, the coins are spilled.
