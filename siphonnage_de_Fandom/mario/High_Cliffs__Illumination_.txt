'''High Cliffs''' is a location in the [[Mushroom World]]. It appeared in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== Description ==
The High Cliffs is full of tall rock mountains.

== History ==
Mario, Peach and Toad travelled through High Cliffs while journeying to the [[Jungle Kingdom]].

== Trivia ==
* High Cliffs is based off [[Rock-Candy Mines]] from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.
