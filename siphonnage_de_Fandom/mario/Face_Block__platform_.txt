'''Face Blocks''' are a type of [[block]] featured in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' and later returning in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Face Blocks SM3DL screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|Face Blocks in [[World 4-3 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 4-3]] of ''Super Mario 3D Land'']]
A Face Block is a yellowish block with a face on it, generally one with a neutral expression. At first, there is only one Face Block, but each time [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] hits it, another Face Block pops out from below it. Up to four Face Blocks can be out at a time. Face Blocks allow Mario or Luigi to either reach higher platforms or enter [[Warp Pipe|pipes]] facing horizontally. After a while the Face Blocks collapse back into one Face Block.

In ''Super Mario 3D Land'', Face Blocks instead spawn from [[! Block]]s temporarily. Besides this, Face Blocks are otherwise the same as in ''New Super Mario Bros.''.
