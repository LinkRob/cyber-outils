{{Nihongo|'''The Goomba Bros.'''|クリクリブラザーズ|Kurikuri Burazāzu}} is the first team fought in the [[Glitz Pit]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Members==
The Goomba Bros. consists of five ordinary [[Goomba]]s.

==Battle==
Right as soon the battle begins one of the Goombas attack Mario. The main condition for this fight is for Mario to appeal to the crowd at least once.

===Strategy===
It's simple to have Mario appeal to the crowd and then take out all of the Goombas using [[Koops]]' Power Shell move.

==Trivia==
*This is one of two fights of the Glitz Pit that has the same set condition that needs to be achieved first (appealing to the crowd in this case). The second is the fight against [[Rawk Hawk]] where the condition is always to use at least one Special Move in the fight).
