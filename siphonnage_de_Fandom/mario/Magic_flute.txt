The '''magic flute''' is an object in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Pied Koopa]]."

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
[[The Pied Koopa (alias)|The Pied Koopa]] uses the magic flute to lure the [[Toad (species)|Mushroom kids]] of [[Pastaland]] to his [[Bowser's Castle|castle]], where The Pied Koopa holds them ransom in change for the spaghetti crops of Pastaland.

After [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] rescue the children, The Pied Koopa attempts to rush near and play the magic flute again, only for [[Mouser]] to drop him into the castle moat. The Pied Koopa then loses the magic flute in the moat, where it is never seen again.

The Mushroom kids are similarly lured toward Mario and Luigi when the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] play on improvised pipes from The Pied Koopa's castle basement.
