The '''Volcano Shroom''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
It takes the appearance of a [[Mushroom]] crafted into a volcano. Made by mixing [[Hot Sauce]] with a [[Shroom Shake]], this volcanic item briefly doubles Attack. Although similar to Hot Sauce, the Volcano Shroom's effect lasts longer.
