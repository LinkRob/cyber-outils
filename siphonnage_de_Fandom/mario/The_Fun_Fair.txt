'''The Fun Fair''' is the first and main hub area of ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''. It takes place at an amusement park, and consists of only a single screen area. The Fun Fair is the only location to have a blue [[Toad's tent]]. Aside from this, the Fun Fair also features a [[Haunted House (Mario Pinball Land)|Haunted House]], where several [[Boo]]s are encountered. [[Goomba]]s are encountered on the overworld area.
