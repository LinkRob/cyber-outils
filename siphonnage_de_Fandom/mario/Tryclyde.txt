'''Tryclyde''', also known as '''Triclyde''', is a character introduced in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' [[reissue|reskin]] ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Tryclyde Art (Super Mario Bros. 2).png|thumb|x200px|left|Tryclyde's design for the original ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', as seen in the book ''[[Mario Mania]]'']]
Tryclyde is a large, three-headed snake capable of breathing balls of fire. His design is based on the mythical [[Wikipedia:Lernaean Hydra|Lernaean Hydra]], a giant, multi-headed serpent.

Tryclyde is typically established as being red with black stripes or wrinkles. Only the front two heads actually move and spit [[fire]], while the back one stays still and only reacts to getting hit. In the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Advance]]'' ports of the game, Tryclyde was recolored turquoise in the game, yet official artworks still depict him in red like in ''Super Mario Bros. 2''. Similarly, the [[Phanto]] is yellow and golden in its sprite, yet retains its white and red coloration in artwork.

Most official artwork shows Tryclyde with visible bottom fangs, yet in the game, the upper fangs are visible instead. He is also depicted in artwork with plain smooth skin rather than stripes.

==Background==
Not much of Tryclyde's past is revealed, though it is mentioned that he was originally an outcast; he was recruited by [[Wart]] due to his "cunning brain and offensive capabilities which are three times normal strength".<ref name=SMB2>''Super Mario Bros. 2'' instruction booklet, page 27.</ref>

==Main appearances==
===''Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[File:SMB2 World 6-3 Tryclyde Battle.png|thumb|200px|The second boss fight against Tryclyde in ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', set in World 6-3]]
In ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', Tryclyde acts as the guardian of [[World 2 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 2]] and [[World 6 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]]. Upon sighting the player, he shoots [[fireball]]s at them. In order to injure Tryclyde, the player needs to throw three [[Mushroom Block]]s at him, which are resistant to Tryclyde's fire breath. After being hit by three Mushroom Blocks, Tryclyde is defeated.

While Tryclyde reprises his role in the version of ''Super Mario Bros. 2'' included in ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'', he is only the boss of World 2 in the ''[[Super Mario Advance]]'' port, his rematch in World 6 having been replaced by [[Mouser]]'s own (originally from World 3). Upon defeat in ''Super Mario Advance'', Tryclyde can be heard shouting, "Impossible!"

===''BS Super Mario USA''===
[[File:Tryclyde BS SuperMario.png|left|thumb]]
Tryclyde returns with the other bosses from ''Super Mario Bros. 2'' in ''[[BS Super Mario USA]]''. Like in the first game, Tryclyde serves as a boss, and is fought in a similar way as in his battle from ''Super Mario Bros. 2''.

Tryclyde's mugshot depicts him having a green coloration, closer to his in-game sprite reused from ''Super Mario All-Stars'', though the cutscenes in which he appears color him red.

==Other appearances==
[[File:Tryclyde Super Show.jpg|thumb|Tryclyde in the episode "[[Raiders of the Lost Mushroom]]" in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'']]
