'''Coney Island''' is a recurring 4-player [[minigame]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'', and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

Originally, the minigame was named '''Ice-Cream Catcher''', and it is one of the minigames included in the ''Mario Party 5'' demo included on the ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!! Bonus Disc]]''.

==Layout==
It takes place atop a statue of [[Mario]] holding an ice cream cone upward, which plays homage to a [[New York City]] landmark, the [[Statue of Liberty]].

==Opening==
The camera shows the entire Mario statue as it heads up to the surface of the ice cream cone it holds, with the four players standing on it.

==Gameplay==
[[File:MPTT100 Coney Island.png|thumb|left|In ''Mario Party: The Top 100'']]
This minigame's object is to catch the scoops of ice cream that fall from above by moving to where their shadows are. As the player's pile of ice cream becomes bigger, it becomes harder for them to walk and turn. Any player with the biggest ice cream cone at the end of the 30-second time limit (and therefore the one(s) who caught the most ice cream) is the winner. However, if no one has caught any ice cream, then the minigame ends in a draw. There are eight different flavors.

==Ending==
[[File:Coney Island ending.png|thumb|right|The ending to Coney Island in ''Mario Party 5'']]
The four players line up in the center of the area. In ''Mario Party 5'' and ''Superstars'', the winning player's/players' ice cream cone(s) then shrink(s) to a smaller size as they strike a victory pose. Simultaneously, the other players perform their losing poses as their ice cream cones disappear. In ''Mario Party 5'', if all players have caught the same number of ice cream scoops, they all perform their victory poses.
