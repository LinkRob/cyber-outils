The '''Dramalama Plaque''' is an item mentioned in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Dramalala Plaque is only mentioned as being part of [[Luigi]]'s journey. It was a prize for winning the [[Jazzafrazz Town]] Drama Slam. [[Luigi]] needed the [[Marvelous Compass]] piece embedded in the plaque, so with the help of [[Hayzee]], Luigi won the plaque and continued along his journey.
