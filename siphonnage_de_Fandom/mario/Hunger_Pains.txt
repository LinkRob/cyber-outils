'''Hunger Pains''' is the third level of [[Beanbean Kingdom]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 7.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle consists of 3 [[Beanie]]s and 5 [[Piranha Bean]]s. The enemy captain is a Beanie.

The final battle consists of 3 Beanies and 5 [[Fire Stalking Piranha Plant]]s. The enemy captain is a Beanie.

After the final battle, the player unlocks 1 Fire Stalking Piranha Plants.

The battle can be replayed for up to one more Fire Stalking Piranha Plant each time.
