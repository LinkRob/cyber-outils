=== Personality traits ===
Mario is short-tempered and resorts to yelling at his friends for failing or taking uncalled-for actions; Mario himself is not above certain vulgar actions, such as farting to taunt his opponents or urinating on Yoshi, and he also cross-dresses several times. The title itself and the art style of the ''Super Mario-Kun'' manga also depict Mario with a childish personality. «Kun» is a generally masculine Japanese honorific for juniors such as boys or teenagers.

Mario has been shown on various occasions to have an almost overbearing attitude towards his brother, this is shown most often in ''Super Mario-Kun''.

== Biography ==
{{sectstub}}
=== Save Princess Peach!! Start of a new Adventure!! ===
{{Main|Super Mario-Kun Vol 1 1}}
Bowser tells [[Mario (Super Mario-Kun)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Super Mario-Kun)|Luigi]] that he kidnapped [[Princess Peach (Super Mario-Kun)|Princess Peach]] and he intimidates the brothers with a large army. After Mario destroyed the mechanized doll, they found an injured [[Yoshi (Super Mario-Kun)|Yoshi]]. Mario and Luigi cured the Yoshi and it claimed that Bowser made a ruckus and took the eggs. Mario and Luigi helped Yoshi in his adventure and defeated [[Super Koopa]]s, [[Dino-Torch]] and [[Chargin' Chuck]].

=== Boom!! One Shot Kill! A Plan to Join Forces ===
{{Main|Super Mario-Kun Vol 1 2}}
[[File:Super Mario-Kun Vol 1 2 Page 16 Panel 1.jpg|100px|thumb|left|Mario and Yoshi defeated the Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant.]]
Mario and Luigi ride onto Yoshi and they approached a castle. Before they could proceed, [[pipe]]s scattered around them. Bowser told Mario, Luigi and Yoshi that there is no escape because the pipes surrounded them. Bowser summoned a squad of [[Piranha Plant]]s and left. Mario, Luigi and Yoshi plotted escaping Piranha Plants to escape, their plans failed. Mario performed the "SNES Controller Nunchuck" and defeated the Piranha Plants. A Piranha Plant transformed into a [[Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant]]. Mario and Yoshi's "[[Mario & Yoshi's Rolling Attack]]" successfully defeated Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant.
