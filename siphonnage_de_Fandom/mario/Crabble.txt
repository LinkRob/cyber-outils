'''Crabbles''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', in which they are first encountered in the level [[Big Bungee Piranha's Lair]]. They resemble hermit [[crab|crabs]] with shells that world range from red, blue, and green.

==Behavior==
Crabbles move back and forth. Their shell can change color between red, light green, and light blue if hit by an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] or another projectile, such as from having a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] spit an enemy at it. A Crabble can be defeated from having an egg thrown at it three times. If a Yoshi either [[jump]]s on or [[Ground Pound]]s a Crabble, he takes damage, and the [[Countdown Timer]] is activated.
