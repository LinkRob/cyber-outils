'''Mole Mario''' is an ability learned in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It replaces the [[Luigi Dunk]] from the past ''[[Mario & Luigi series|Mario &amp; Luigi]]'' games.
==Usage==
Mole Mario is first learned at [[Dozing Sands]] and is learned at the same time with [[Mini-Mario]]. To use this technique, Luigi must hammer Mario into the ground. Then, Mario can search underground for items mainly [[Bean]]s. Mole Mario is also required to beat the [[Mole Hunt]] game to find the hermit crabs that dig fast underground.
