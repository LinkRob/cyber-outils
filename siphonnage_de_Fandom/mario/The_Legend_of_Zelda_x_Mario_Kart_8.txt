'''''The Legend of Zelda x Mario Kart 8''''' is one of the downloadable content packs for ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. It was released on November 13, 2014. The pack was included in the base game for ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. Most of its content is focused on ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' series, including a [[Hyrule Circuit|new course]], although it also adds the [[Blue Falcon]] as a [[kart]] part.
