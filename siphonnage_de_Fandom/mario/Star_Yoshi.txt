'''Star Yoshi''' is a power-up form of [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] in ''[[Yoshi (video game)|Yoshi]]''.

==Description==
A Star Yoshi resembles a regular [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], but the most notable difference is the yellow star floating to the left side of his body. A Star Yoshi is also bulky in size, and he makes a much lower-pitched than a regular Yoshi.

==In ''Yoshi''==
Combining the lower and upper halves of a [[Yoshi Egg]] causes a Yoshi to slip out of the egg, but if the maximum number of characters are between the two halves (7 in the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] version and 6 in the [[Game Boy]] version), a Star Yoshi emerges, and he awards 500 [[point]]s.
