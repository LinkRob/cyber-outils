'''World 2-Airship''' is the second [[airship]] and the last level of [[Desert Land]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. Completing it allows [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to continue on to [[Water Land]].

== Layout ==
Mario/Luigi first have to avoid [[Bullet Bill]]s coming from two [[Bill Blaster]]s. Then the player will need to jump on the higher part of the airship, where they will have to avoid a cannon blaster and then a few Bullet Bills coming from more Bill Blasters. After getting past the Bill Blasters, the player will then get to a [[Question Box]]. If they are [[Small Mario]]/Luigi then they will get a [[Super Mushroom]], and if they are Big/[[Fire Mario]] (or Luigi), they will get a [[Super Leaf]]. Mario/Luigi will then have to go down some boxes while avoiding some Bullet Bills, and then climb the next few boxes while also avoiding the Bullet Bills on the opposite side. Mario will then come across three [[Rocky Wrench]]es and will have to avoid the wrenches they throw at him (they can be defeated for a short period of time by jumping on them). He will then have to avoid Bullet Bills being shot out from two Bill Blasters and then he needs to go down the gray [[Warp Pipe]] to confront [[Morton Koopa Jr.]].

== Boss Battle ==
Morton Koopa Jr. will jump high and will shoot laser beams from the wand. Mario/Luigi must jump on him three times to defeat him. He then needs to grab the wand and he gives it back to the king. Then the king gives him a letter from Princess Toadstool and Mario/Luigi moves on to [[Water Land]].
