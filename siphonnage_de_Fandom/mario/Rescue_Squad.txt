The '''Rescue Squad''', or '''Rescue Toads''', are a group in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Rescue Squad consists of five color-coded squads of [[Toad (species)|Toads]]: red, yellow, green, blue, and purple. The [[Rescue Squad Chief]] is collectively in charge of all five squads.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''===
The Rescue Squad aids [[Mario]] in overcoming obstacles that he cannot pass otherwise. To gather them all in one area, Mario must find and talk to them in another location. Each one assists Mario once he gathers all the squad members in a certain area. There is one Toad named [[Red Rescue Squad Apprentice Toad]] who wishes to enter the Rescue Squad, and he is eventually becomes part of the red squad.

The Rescue Squad also star in their own mini-series, [[Rescue V: Fearless Color Defenders]], similarly to [[Toad Force V]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. 

===''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''===
All five colors of the Rescue Squad have a minor role, and they are found in [[The Ringer]] at the [[Battle Lab]]. A photograph of the red Rescue Squad's captain can also be seen at [[Shogun Studios]].
