'''Pin enemies''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Pin enemies are gray, thumbtack-shaped enemies with a spike on their bottom. Their clothing consists of a green soldier hat and a yellow parachute.

===Behavior===
Upon hitting the ground, a pin enemy bounces very high several times.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
Pin enemies assist appear only during the boss fight against [[Aerodent]], who throws them at [[Wario]] to try and damage him. Wario can knock a pin enemy to the ground by jumping on its head, then he can throw them upward at Aerodent's balloon to deflate it. If Wario touches the pin side of a pin enemy, he takes damage.
