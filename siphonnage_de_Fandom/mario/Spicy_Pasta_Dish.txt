'''Spicy Pasta Dish''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. In the former game, it is named '''Spicy Pasta'''.

== Appearances ==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:PastaSpicy.png|frame|left]]
In this game, eating a Spicy Pasta restores 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 10 [[Flower Point|FP]] in addition to raising attack power for a short while. To make a Spicy Pasta, one must give [[Hot Sauce]] (which can be bought from the [[Businessman]] from [[Glitzville]] after completing his trouble at the [[Trouble Center]]) and one of three pasta dish items ([[Fresh Pasta Bunch|Fresh Pasta]], [[Koopasta]], or [[Spaghetti]]) to [[Zess T.]] once she learns to cook two dishes.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
Eating a Spicy Pasta Dish has a different effect of doubling [[Attack]] power for 30 seconds. Having [[Dyllis]] cook a Fresh Pasta Bunch and Hot Sauce results in the creation of a Spicy Pasta Dish.
