The '''Quacker''' is a [[bike]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== Description ==
The Quacker has a low top speed and a high acceleration.

The Quacker is unlocked by winning the [[Engine Class|150cc]] [[Star Cup]] or playing 2,400 races.

==NPC Uses==
The Quacker is used as the staff ghost for Nin*Murak racing as [[Toadette]] in [[Sherbet Land (Mario Kart 64)|Sherbet Land]] and Nin*Sakat racing as [[Baby Luigi]] in [[Mushroom Gorge]].

==Trivia==
*It is the lightest vehicle in ''Mario Kart Wii''. The player could also get easily punched aside if ran into.
*If you get hit you automatically get your speed back after you've landed
