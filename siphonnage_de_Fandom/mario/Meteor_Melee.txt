'''Meteor Melee''' is a minigame found in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.
==Gameplay==
In this minigame, you use the Circle Pad to move your character to collect falling stars and avoid [[Meteor]]s as Meteors deduct a point from your total. The player who collects the most points wins.
