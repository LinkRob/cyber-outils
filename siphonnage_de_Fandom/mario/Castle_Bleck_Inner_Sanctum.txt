|first = ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''}}'''Castle Bleck Inner Sanctum''' is a location in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is the fourth and final area of [[Castle Bleck]] and serves as the main setting for Chapter 8-4. It is also the final level in the entire game.

==History==
===Chapter 8-4: Tippi and Count Bleck===
It is just down to [[Mario]] and [[Tippi]] meaning the area is going to make great use of Mario's [[Flip]] ability. Castle Bleck Inner Sanctum is best known for the very large room with different hallways where some have openings to go through. After a lot of traveling, Mario and Tippi eventually reach the room where [[Count Bleck]] himself is waiting for them and Mario and Tippi proceed to fight the Count.
