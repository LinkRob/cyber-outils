'''Pair Pointer''' is a microgame that can be found in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' It uses the Scales form for its gameplay, and [[Wario]] serves as its creator, though the player has to play the level Lava at First Sight.

==Layout==
There are six shoe boxes in a semicircular formation, and [[Wario]] himself stands in the formation's "centroid."

==Gameplay==
The player must move their arms so Wario can point to the correct shoes. If Wario points at the correct shoes, he will wear and admire them, leading to a victory. The player will be defeated if Wario points to the wrong shoes; should this happen, Wario will cross his arms with circles surrounding the correct shoes.
