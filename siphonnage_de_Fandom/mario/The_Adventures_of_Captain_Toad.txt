'''The Adventures of Captain Toad''' are a series of courses starring [[Captain Toad]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. Here, Captain Toad is the only playable character, and he progresses through puzzle-based areas to obtain five [[Green Power Star|Green Stars]] along the way. The courses are single-player only, and can only be playable with the [[Wii U GamePad]] in the original version.

Captain Toad cannot jump, sprint, roll, or use any other moves of the main playable characters: [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Toad (character)|Toad]], and [[Rosalina]]. The courses are unlocked from the start, but [[Enemy Blockade]]s or general map environments could make them inaccessible. The course end automatically once every Green Star has been collected.

Captain Toad Courses are typically found in odd-numbered Worlds to contrast with the even-numbered World exclusive [[Mystery House]]s, except for the last Captain Toad Course, which skips [[World Flower (Super Mario 3D World)|World Flower]] and is placed in [[World Crown]].

In ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury'', multiple people can now participate in these courses. However, every player can also control the camera, which pans with the camera stick if in TV Mode, and the Touch Screen if in Tabletop/Handheld Modes.
