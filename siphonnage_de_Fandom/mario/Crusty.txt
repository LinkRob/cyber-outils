A '''Crusty''' is a large, powerful crab enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They can be found in the [[Sunken Ship]], usually alongside other enemies in battle. Crusters are relatives of [[Star Cruster]]s.

==Battle==
Crusties do not have any special attacks, although they have an incredibly high [[attack]] rating, so their physical attacks can often deal a lot of damage.

===Strategy===
Crusties are weak to both [[Fire (element)|fire]] and [[Thunder (element)|thunder]], so [[Mario]] should use his [[Super Flame]] while [[Mallow]] uses [[Shocker]]. Also, when fought alongside other enemies, the Crusties should always be the first target considering they are bigger threats.
