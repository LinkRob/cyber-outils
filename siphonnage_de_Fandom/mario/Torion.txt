'''Torions'''<ref>''Super Mario Land'' English instruction booklet, page 15.</ref><ref>M. Arakawa. Nintendo ''Game Boy'' Player's Guide. Page 5.</ref> are fast-moving, carnivorous fish in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. They are weak against the [[Marine Pop]]'s torpedoes and are defeated after one successful hit. They award [[Mario]] with a 100 [[point]]s each. Torions are found only in [[World 2-3 (Super Mario Land)|World 2-3]] of the [[Muda Kingdom]], the Marine Pop's level. Torions always swim in groups of three. According to the instruction manual of ''Super Mario Land'', [[Honen]] are undead Torions that were eaten by [[Tatanga]].
