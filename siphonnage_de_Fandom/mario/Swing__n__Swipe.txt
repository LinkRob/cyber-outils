'''Swing 'n' Swipe''' is an Item minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the player must hit one of the many [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowsers]] that appear on the field. Each one of them has a certain item and the player must pick one to hit. This minigame is considered the easiest Item Mini-game and getting the desired item should be easy.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Move
*{{button|n64|a}} - Swing
