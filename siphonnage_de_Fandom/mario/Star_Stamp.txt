A '''Star Stamp''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==In ''Mario Party 3''==
Star Stamps are only found in Story Mode of this game. The idea was bought up by a [[Lakitu]] to [[Mario]] and the gang to see who will become the Superstar. The [[Fake Millennium Star]] gives the player a card that will contain all of the Star Stamps earned in the game.
