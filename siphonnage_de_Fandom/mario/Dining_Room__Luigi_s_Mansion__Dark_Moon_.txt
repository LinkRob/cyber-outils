The '''Dining Room''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. This is a large room and it's located near the [[Kitchen (Gloomy Manor)|Kitchen]]. This room is most notable for Luigi's first encounter with a [[Boo]] in ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
During [[Visual Tracks|A-4: Visual Tricks]], Luigi heads into the Dining Room where he runs into a Boo for the first time. Luigi also learns that it is the Boos that are behind the [[Spirit Ball]]s and they make the table disappear. Luigi reveals the table using the [[Dark-Light Device]] and vacuums up all of the Spirit Balls into the [[Poltergust 5000]]. The Boo reappears and it isn't happy with Luigi's actions and proceeds to fight him. Using the Dark-Light Device, Luigi captures the Boo and E. Gadd decides to [[Pixelator|Pixelate]] out of the mansion to review the Boo.

During [[Sticky Situation|A-5: Sticky Situation]], Luigi must head into the Dining Room again to clean up spider webs. In addition, he must fight [[Greenie]]s.

Luigi can also [[Strobulb|strobe]] the vault on the ceiling in this room to release a [[Gem]].
