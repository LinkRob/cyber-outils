The '''SS Brass''' is a location in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Description==
The SS Brass is a wooden [[airship]] with turbines resembling either a trumpet, a trombone, or a french horn, depending on the player's progress. The airship has the capabilities to be driven underwater like a submarine.

==In ''Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix''==
The SS Brass is used by [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] as a means of transportation around the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] on their journey to collect the [[Music Key]]s, and commonly appears during the story mode cutscenes, as the characters travel between locations. After the player defeats the [[Lakitu|pirate Lakitu]] during "[[Fishing Frenzy]]," the SS Brass's turbines change from being trumpet-like to either trombone-like or French horn-like, depending on the level.

The SS Brass is shown during certain songs, such as "[[In the Whirlpool]]" and "[[Step by Step]]," where its underwater capabilities are shown. The song "[[Always Smiling]]" also takes place on the SS Brass. During these three songs, Mario is seen taking control of the ship's wheel.

There is an unlockable [[minigame]] titled [[Fire Up the SS Brass]], in which the object is to maneuver the SS Brass through tunnels.
