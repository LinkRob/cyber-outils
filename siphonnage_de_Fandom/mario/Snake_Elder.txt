The '''Snake Elder''' is a character that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. He is one of the five Elders that can bestow the ancient power of [[Bananza]] upon [[Donkey Kong]] and he rules over the [[Radiance Layer]].

==Appearance==
The Snake Elder is a very large and elderly snake that is mainly dark green in color with yellow stripes that resemble bananas. He also wears a green hat on his head with large green headphones and has a beard made of a bundle of old bananas.

==History==
Donkey Kong and [[Pauline]] first encounter the Snake Elder after they managed to make it to him on the Radiance Layer. Like with the other Elders, the Snake Elder will be able to bestow the power of [[Snake Bananza]] to them upon retrieving the pieces of his [[Ancient Record]]. After doing so, the Snake Elder then informs Donkey Kong and Pauline about the importance of the [[Banandium Root]] and how every single clumps of gold seen throughout the game are created from it. The Snake Elder says the more they see of it, the closer they are to reaching the [[Planet Core]].
