'''Zappa Mechakoopa '''is a variant of [[Mechakoopa]]s that first appears in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' as part of the 3.0.0 update. Unlike normal Mechakoopas, Zappa Mechakoopas are blue in color and is able to launch a single electrified laser beam that can destroy brick blocks. Just like its normal and red counterparts, Zappa Mechakoopas are usable in every level style except for ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.
