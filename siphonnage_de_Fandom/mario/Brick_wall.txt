A '''Brick wall''', also named '''rock''', is an object that first appears in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its overseas conversion, ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. 

==Description==
Brick walls are a type of [[block]], and despite their name, bricks walls appear to be composed of clumps of hardened dirt or rocks. A few brick walls are found obstructing a path, stacked from floor to ceiling, and they can only be destroyed by placing either a [[bomb]] or a [[Bob-omb]] near it and having it explode.

In ''Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic'' and the original ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', the color of the brick wall sprite varies depending on the area, due to the limited palette of the [[Family Computer Disk System]] and [[Nintendo Entertainment System]]. In the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Advance]]'' [[reissue|remakes]], all brick walls have a light, reddish-brown color. The ''Super Mario Advance'' sprite is more yellowish because the game utilizes a brighter palette.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. 2''==
Brick walls commonly appear in underground areas, as early as [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1-1]]. In [[World 1-3 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1-3]], before [[Mouser]] can be fought, the player's character has to destroy the brick walls obstructing the entrance to his area.
