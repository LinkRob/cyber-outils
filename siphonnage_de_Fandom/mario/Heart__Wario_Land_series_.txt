'''Hearts''',<ref>''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3'' English instruction booklet, page 15.</ref><ref name=Virtual>''Virtual Boy Wario Land'' English instruction booklet, page 18.</ref> also referred to as '''Bonus Hearts''',<ref>''Nintendo Magazine System'' (AU) Issue #16, page 20. "''Wario is all set to pop the next pirate with his bomb in the Bonus Hearts game.''"</ref> are items in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.

Since no other ''Wario Land'' games feature a Heart Point system, these types of hearts have never reappeared. In ''[[Wario Land 4]]'', however, [[Heart (Wario franchise)|hearts]] that replenish health appear.

==History==
===''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''===
[[File:WL Heart.gif|frame|left]]
Hearts can be found in many [[? Block]]s throughout ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''. After appearing, hearts continuously blink and stay in place; they do not disappear until Wario picks them up. When picked up, they award [[Wario]] 10 Heart Points<ref>''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3'' English instruction booklet, pages 8 & 15.</ref> towards an [[extra life]]. In contrast, 1 Heart Point is normally awarded for each defeated enemy unless a [[Super Star|Star]] is active (which changes it to 10 Heart Points per enemy). While [[coin]]s have appeared and had a similar effect in other ''Mario'' games, [[Coin (Wario franchise)|coins]] in ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3'' do not contribute to getting extra lives.

===''Virtual Boy Wario Land''===
Hearts make a reappearance in ''Virtual Boy Wario Land'', where there are two types: a regular heart now worth 1 Heart Point, and a Big Heart<ref name=Virtual/> worth the original amount of 10 Heart Points. While the same methods as its predecessor may be used, regular hearts can now also be obtained by destroying blocks or defeating an enemy via a fire attack from [[Sea Dragon Wario]] or [[King Dragon Wario]] (as opposed to dashing, which instead relinquishes a coin).
