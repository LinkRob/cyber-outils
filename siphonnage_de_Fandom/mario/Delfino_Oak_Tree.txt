'''Delfino Oak Trees''' are a type of tree featured in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. Although they have Delfino in their name, Delfino Oak Trees are not native to [[Isle Delfino]], as they do not appear there.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Delfino Oak Tree consists of a brown trunk with only a single pointed branch for a nose, black eyes, and a large green bush on top decorated with a wavy pattern.

===Behavior===
A Delfino Oak Tree does little else aside from gently swaying from side to side. A Delfino Oak Tree can serve as an obstruction to a racer, as crashing into one briefly puts their [[kart]] to a halt.

==In the ''Mario Kart'' series==
Delfino Oak Trees are featured within the ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'' course [[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Mario Circuit]], including its reappearance in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. Most of the Delfino Oak Trees sit behind the racetrack boundaries. Delfino Oak Trees do not appear in the Mario Circuit found within prototype builds of ''[[Mario Kart DS/Prototype|Mario Kart DS]]''.

Prior to ''Mario Kart Wii'', the Delfino Oak Trees went unnamed, as their name was provided in the game's Prima guide.

[[Peach Beach]] features palm trees similar to a Delfino Oak Tree.

==In the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series==
Delfino Oak Trees make a cameo appearance in the background of the [[Figure-8 Circuit (stage)|Figure-8 Circuit]] stage, originally named "Mario Circuit" in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. Although this is the case, Delfino Oak Trees do not appear in the ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' course [[Figure-8 Circuit]], which the stage is based on.
