The '''Shroom Shop''' is an item shop featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is run by two [[Toad (species)|Toads]] within [[Princess Peach's Castle]] during the present. Various items are sold at the Shroom Shop, including [[Bros. Item]]s, [[Clothing]], and [[Badge]]s.
