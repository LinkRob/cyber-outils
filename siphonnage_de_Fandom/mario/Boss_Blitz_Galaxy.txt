The '''Boss Blitz Galaxy''' is a galaxy in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. In this galaxy, five bosses from the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', the game's predecessor, are fought. This galaxy was brought back in [[Super Mario 3D World]] with the [[Boss Blitz|same name]].

==Missions==
=== Throwback Throwdown===
[[Mario]] must defeat [[Dino Piranha]], [[King Kaliente]], [[Major Burrows]], [[Bouldergeist]] and [[Fiery Dino Piranha]] in succession.
===Throwback Throwdown Speed Run===
Similar to the first mission except for one modification: time is limited to 5 minutes. This mission is only accessible, unless already completed, if a [[Speedy Comet]] is in orbit.
=== Green Star Missions ===
Both of the Green Stars are found in ''Throwback Throwdown''.
====Green Star 1 ====
This Green Star is found beside the tree on Major Burrows's Planet.
====Green Star 2 ====
This Green Star is behind Bouldergeist's Arena. Mario will have to fall off to get it and be careful not to go in the [[Black Hole]].
==Layout==
===Dino Piranha's Planet===
This planet looks exactly like it does in the original ''Super Mario Galaxy''. Dino Piranha is fought how he is in the original too; go behind him and spin on his tail four times.
===King Kaliente's Planet===
This planet also looks like its original. King Kaliente is also fought in the same way; by spinning on the coconuts in a back-and-forth chain. However, the [[Comet Medal]] and a [[Life Mushroom]] are hiding on the bottom of this planet.
===Major Burrows's Planet===
This planet also appears the same as in its predecessor. There are thorny flowers on the bottom of this planet, and Green Star 1 at the top near the tree. Major Burrows is fought the exact same way.
===Bouldergeist's Arena===
Before Bouldergeist are stone platforms that hold a [[Checkpoint Flag]] and a Luma Shop (both removed in the comet mission). Bouldergeist is fought in the same way as the original.
====Rest Meteor====
After Bouldergeist is defeated the Launch Star takes Mario here. There is nothing but the next Launch Star, and a Cosmic Spirit that appears to help with the last battle after a certain amount of deaths.
===Fiery Dino Piranha's Planet===
This, and the boss, appear how they do in the original. Once Fiery Dino Piranha is defeated the Power Star will appear.
==Trivia==

*Boss Blitz Galaxy is the very first level/stage in the ''Super Mario Bros.'' series to be a glorify Boss Rush, [[The Gauntlet]] excluding in that its only becomes a boss rush after the Player(s) has defeat one boss at a time and that they are agents stronger recolored versions of that boss. 
*The music has not changed for the bosses except for the galaxy's main music. All five bosses have their original battle themes.
*The concept behind Boss Blitz Galaxy is very reminiscent to [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Boss_Endurance?so=search Boss Endurance] and the [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Arena?so=search Arena]/[https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/The_True_Arena The True Arena], the-two-to-three Modes from another game series made by Nintendo, ''[[Kirby]]'', in that they are both boss rush modes.
*When [[Starship Mario]] goes near or beside the galaxy on the World Map, it will give off glittering sounds.
*For unknown reasons, [[Topmaniac]] does not reappear in this galaxy, despite being fought twice (Four times including the [[Prankster Comet]]s) in [[Super Mario Galaxy]].
