The '''Arcade''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Description==
The Arcade is a minigame area located in the clouds above any [[Lakitu Info Center]]. It is only accessible via the green pipe to the left of the center's entrance.

At the Arcade, all of the arcade machines are designed to resemble [[Nintendo 3DS]] and [[New Nintendo 3DS]] systems. The Arcade hosts two extra modes: the [[Battle Ring (Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam)|Battle Ring]], where bosses can be fought again, and the [[Attackathon]], which features several endurance minigames that involve [[Bros. Attack]]s and [[Trio Attack]]s. The [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] can also buy [[Lakitea]] for 20 [[coin]]s from a shopkeeper, which will fully heal all members of the party. 

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''==
The Arcade only becomes accessible after visiting [[Sunbeam Plains]] a second time.
