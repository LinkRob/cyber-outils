'''Chain Chomp and Spike Stomp''' is the second level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 28.

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains  2 [[Chain Chomp]]s, 3 [[Lakitu]], and 3 [[Spike]]s. The enemy captain is a Spike.

The 2nd battle contains 6 Lakitu and 2 Spikes. The enemy captain is a Lakitu, and he knows [[Chance]] and  [[Deny]].

The 3rd battle contains 6 [[Para-Beetle]]s, and 2 Spikes. The enemy captain is a Para-Beetle, and he knows Deny.

The final battle contains 3 Chain Chomps, 1 [[Broozer]], 2 Lakitu, and 2 Para-Beetle. The enemy captain is a Chain Chomp, and he knows Chance.
