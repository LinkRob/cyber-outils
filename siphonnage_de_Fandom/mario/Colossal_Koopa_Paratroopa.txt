'''Colossal Koopa Paratroopas''' (also known as '''Colossal Paratroopas'''<ref>''Nintendo Power'' Volume 13, page 8.</ref> or '''Colossal Koopa Para-Troopas'''<ref>Hodgson, David S J. ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide. Pages 54, 58, 59.</ref>) are a larger variant [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s introduced in the film ''[[Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]'' and later the game ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==History==
===''Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!''===
A Colossal Paratroopa appears in the 1986 anime movie ''[[Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]'' She captures [[Mario]], and later [[Luigi]] and [[Haru-ōji|Kibidango]], and takes them to her nest, where she attempts to feed them to her babies, which resemble large green baby birds. Mario avoids being eaten by feeding the babies the laughing mushrooms he had been picking; he saves Luigi and Kibidango by pretending to taste Luigi and saying he has gone rotten. The babies then persuade the mother to go get them some dessert. She is last seen waving goodbye to the heroes with her babies after they acquire the [[Super Mushroom|mushroom]] from the freed [[Toad (species)|Toad]]s.

===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Bros. 3''====
[[File:Colossal KP SMB3 sprite.png|frame|left]]
[[File:GiantParakoopas SMB3.png|frame|right]]
In ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', Colossal Paratroopas appear in [[Giant Land]] alongside [[Grand Goomba]]s, [[Gargantua Koopa Troopa]]s, and [[Piranhacus Giganticus]]. They attack by hopping up and down. Their larger size does make them more troublesome to avoid but despite this, they are no stronger than a regular Paratroopa and a single stomp can knock loose the wings turning it into a Gargantua Koopa Troopa. Additionally, the use of the [[Fire Flower]], [[Hammer Suit]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Tanooki Suit]], and the [[Starman]] can easily destroy a Colossal Paratroopa.
{{br}}

====''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Super Mario Maker 2''====
[[Image:Giant Enemies Super Mario Maker.jpg|thumb|A Colossal Paratroopa riding a Gargantua Koopa Troopa, near them is a [[Grand Goomba]], [[Piranhacus Giganticus]], and a [[big Monty Mole]].]]
Colossal Paratroopas also appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', in this game, they can be produced if the player puts a [[Super Mushroom]] onto normal [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s; another way of creating them is to attach a pair of [[wings]] to a [[Gargantua Koopa Troopa]]. In addition to their original green color, Colossal Koopa Paratroopas now come in [[Giant Red Shell|red-shelled]] varieties, and each behave differently akin to their smaller relatives: green-shelled ones make consequent hops on the ground, heading towards the direction they are facing, and red-shelled ones fly in a straight pattern up and down. However, in the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' style, they do not have their distinct sprite; instead, they look like double-sized versions of ordinary Koopa Paratroopas, as in the other styles. A Colossal Paratroopa can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Star]] just like in other games.

===''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''===
In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', the [[Grand Troopa|Tub-O-Troopa]] enemies that are only found in [[Bowser's Castle#Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars|Bowser's Keep]] bear a resemblance to Colossal Koopa Paratroopas.

===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
In ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]'', there are two variants of Colossal Paratroopas known as [[Big Green Koopa Paratroopa]]s, and [[Big Yellow Koopa Paratroopa]]s.

===''Yoshi's Woolly World''===
In ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', a regular Koopa Paratroopa named [[Knot-Wing the Koopa|Knot-Wing]] is transformed by [[Kamek]] into a giant-sized Koopa as a boss of [[World 2 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 2]], [[World 4 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 4]], and [[World 6 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 6]].
