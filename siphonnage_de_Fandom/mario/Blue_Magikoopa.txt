A '''Blue Magikoopa''' is an enemy and the main color of [[Magikoopa]]s. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==In ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''==
Blue Magikoopa is an enemy which has Water as its element.

==In ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]''==
Blue Magikoopa is an obtainable minion in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]''. His type is Flying, and his [[Special Skill]] is [[Chain Shot]].
