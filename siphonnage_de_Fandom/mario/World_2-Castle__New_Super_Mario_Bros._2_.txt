''World 2-Castle'' is the castle level and final level of [[World 2 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 2]] in [[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]. 

==Layout==
The player starts out on a flat surface, where the [[Switch Lift]] is docked on the [[lava]]. After jumping on the moving platform, a few [[Buzzy Beetle]]s are encountered, moving around on rotating Brick Block structures. When the player approaches the structures, they fold out, dropping the Buzzy Beetles onto the platform. Soon after, Thwomps appear. The player can bait these Thwomps into crashing into lava, destroying them. Shortly after, the [[Checkpoint Flag]] appears along with two ? Blocks, one of which has a Super Leaf inside. Another Switch Lift is docked here. The Switch Lift quickly approaches a quad of fireballs and the second Star Coin rotating above. The level's [[Red Ring]] is located directly after this, floating above a set of rotating Brick Block structures. Shortly after, two sets of fireballs and a red pipe appear on-screen. A few more sets of fireballs is followed by a vertical section of [[coin]]s with [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] in between them. The player can then approach the boss door; a [[Hidden Block]] containing a Super Leaf is next to the door.

=== Boss battle===
The boss is Iggy Koopa, riding around in his [[Chain Chomp]] carriage. The battle plays similarly to that of his [[World 5]] battle in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. The Chain Chomp carriage travels on a rectangular grid layout and can exit the room from the top and bottom. Some sand will appear upon entering in the opposite direction. The player must use the four smaller blue platforms and stomp on Iggy's head three times to defeat him.

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin coin can be seen right above the second set of folding brick block platforms. To reach it, use the platforms that are under the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second coin is swinging back and forth on the ceiling. The player can either use the springboard (from the nearby ? block) or they can try to jump for it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The player will need the springboard, hiding in the ? block near the second Star Coin, to reach a red pipe in the ceiling. In this area, the player should run to the left and hit the [[P Switch]] in the left block. This will allow the player to get the final Star Coin. Alternatively, the player can use a Super Leaf to enter the red pipe. There are also two Hidden Blocks on both sides of the room that have 1-Up Mushrooms.
