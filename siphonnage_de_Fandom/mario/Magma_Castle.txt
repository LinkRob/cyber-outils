'''Magma Castle''' is the final [[level]] of ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'' as well as the fourth and final level of the sixth page, [[P. 6-Finale]]. Here, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] has to travel on a [[dragon]] to safely travel across the [[lava]]. The main part of the level involves a boss fight against [[Baby Bowser]].
