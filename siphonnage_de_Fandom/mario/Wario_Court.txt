The '''Wario Court''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' for [[Nintendo 64]].

==Description==
Wario Court is one of the tennis courts belonging to [[Wario]] Like in the [[Wario & Waluigi Court]] and the [[Waluigi Court]], the background of Wario Court features [[treasure chest]]s and ruins in a desert setting. The ground of the court depicts artwork of [[Wario]] holding his racket. This court has a weak bounce with a slow ball speed.

==In ''Mario Tennis''==
Wario Court is an unlockable court, and to unlock it, the player has to wind the third round of [[Treasure Box (minigame)|Treasure Box]], a minigame in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for [[Game Boy Color]], then transfer the data through a [[Transfer Pak]]. Since none of the game's [[reissue]]s are compatible with the Transfer Pak, Wario Court is exclusively unlockable in the original version.
