'''Hammersaurus Rex''' is a form of [[Hammer (Luigi's Mansion 3)|Hammer]] and a kind of [[Ghost (species)|ghost]] that made their debut in [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']]. They only make appearances in [[ScareScraper]] floors that are based on Unnatural History Museum. They have 300HP, which is more than regular Hammers.

== Appearance ==
Unlike Hammers who have red bodies, Hammersaurus Rexes have dinosaur-themed skin. Their body shape, however, are exactly same as regular Hammers.

== In [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']] ==
In the ScareScraper mode, one of the [[Luigi]]<nowiki/>s must have the paleontoluigist costume on in order to gain access to the Unnatural History Museum themed floor. Hammersaurus Rexes most likely appear in hidden rooms that have to be found by using the [[Dark-Light Device]] to unlock the door and are not required to be captured to beat the floor. Hammersaurus Rexes act and attack in the same way as regular Hammers. Consequently, the way to defeat them is also same, which is flashing them and vacuuming them up from behind with the [[Poltergust G-00]]. Like Regular Hammers, they can also be slammed by two players at once.
