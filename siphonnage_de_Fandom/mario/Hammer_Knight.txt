'''Hammer Knights'''<ref>Musa, Alexander. ''Mario Kart 8'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Page 82. "'''''Hammer Knights''': The final stretch of the track is populated by statues that slam their hammers onto the ground. It’s easy enough to avoid them by staying close to the center of the track, but the best racing lines will take you very close to them.''"</ref> are large obstacles in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. They are statues possessed by [[Boo]]s that inhabit [[Twisted Mansion]].

==In ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''==
Two of them appear on the final leg of the track at the track's side, wielding giant hammers. They continuously swing down their hammers onto the track, blocking the way for racers and potentially flattening them and causing them to stop. The ground underneath their hammers is also partially destroyed and has a [[coin]]. Their hammers can also block incoming items, such as [[Green Shell]]s and [[Red Shell]]s. After the hammer slams the ground, the Boos that are possessing them float out of the statues. Whenever a Hammer Knight is knocked by a [[Spiny Shell (blue)|Spiny Shell]], [[Super Star|Star]], [[Bullet Bill]] or [[Super Horn]], the Boo shouts in pain.
