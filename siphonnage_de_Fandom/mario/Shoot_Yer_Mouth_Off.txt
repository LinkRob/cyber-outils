'''Shoot Yer Mouth Off''' is a Mic mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Shoot Yer Mouth Off.png|thumb|left|Daytime]]
One player gives commands to [[Shy Guy]]s by saying a number from one to nine into the Mic. If the player with the mic says zero, three [[Thwomp]]s will fall randomly onto the map. The one player must use these features to try and knock out the other three players, while the three players must dodge all these attacks.

==Controls==
===1 Player===
*{{button|gcn|Mic}} "1" "2" "3" "5" "6" "7" - Bob-omb
*{{button|gcn|Mic}} "4" "8" "9" - Bullet Bills
*{{button|gcn|Mic}} "0" - Three Thwomps will fall randomly

===3 Players===
*{{button|gcn|stick}} - Move
