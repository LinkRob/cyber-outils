|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Diamond Saw''' is a special move used mostly by bosses and some enemies in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Diamond Saw is used, the screen turns flaky and two saws fly away and then fly through the selected character in [[Mario]]'s party.

==Effects==
Diamond Saw is a rather strong move as it pierces defense.

==Trivia==
*The flakly screen during the attack is similar to the [[Blizzard (move)|Blizzard]] technique.
