The '''garbage can''' is an object in ''[[Mario Bros. (Game Boy Advance)|Mario Bros.]]'' for [[Game Boy Advance]].

==Description==
The garbage can has the appearance of a regular trash can, except it is light blue. It is exclusive to the Mario Bros. Battle mode, in which the garbage can replaces the lower [[POW Block]], but remains in one space and cannot be picked up.

If a character approaches a garbage can while carrying another character, it opens and automatically closes if the character is thrown inside. If a character is trapped inside of a garbage can, they can escape using a [[Charge jump|Super Jump]], capable of dazing another character if they are standing on top, or [[jump]]ing out when the lid opens, which the garbage can does every few seconds. A garbage can contains a random item, including either a [[Yellow Shell|Koopa Shell]], a POW Block, a [[Fish Skeleton]], or an [[Egg]], which itself can contain a [[coin]], a [[Heart]], or a [[Starman]].
