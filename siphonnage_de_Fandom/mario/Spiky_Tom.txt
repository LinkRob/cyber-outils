|race = [[Koopatrol]]}}'''Spiky Tom''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario (game)|Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Spiky Tom is a [[Koopatrol]] working as one of [[Bowser]]'s guards in [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. After the events of Chapter 5, he and his friends, [[Mr. Hammer]] and [[Spiky John]], are preparing to have a quiz show but needed one other person. [[Princess Peach]] happens to walk in on them but, instead of taking her back to her room, Spiky Tom, Mr. Hammer, and Spiky John allow her to participate in their quiz show. Unlike with Spiky John, Spiky Tom often tries to answer questions quickly.
