A '''Bros. Block''' is a type of [[block]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is based on the [[Question Block]]s that yield several [[coin]]s.

==Description==
A Bros. Block is colored yellow and has an arrow pointing either left or right. It becomes a red block with an M symbol after [[Mario]] hits it or green block with an L symbol after [[Luigi]] hits it.

==In the games==
Hitting a Bros. Block causes it to move in the direction of the arrow. It then turns red or green, depending on whether Mario or Luigi hit the block, respectively. A Bros. Block becomes its initial color while moving in a certain direction. After a short amount of time, the Bros. Block becomes an [[Empty Block]], upon which it stops giving out coins. To get the most coins, Mario and Luigi must alternate [[jump]]ing beneath it.

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
Bros. Blocks are found on the [[Koopa Cruiser]] and throughout the [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. A prototype Bros. Block that gives off no coins is found in the Block research area of the [[Woohoo Hooniversity]].
