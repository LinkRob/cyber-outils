The '''Cooking Disk R''' (also known as '''Cooking Disk 2''' in the PAL version) is a red [[Cooking Disk]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Usage==
The Cooking Disk R can be found at [[Flipside]] on the other side of the second floor where the staircase is hidden, [[Tippi]] and [[Tiptron]] can reveal the staircase. It is inside the [[Treasure Chest|treasure chest]] after climbing up the stairs. After inserting the disk, the [[Dining Specializer]] will upload the second page with recipes.
