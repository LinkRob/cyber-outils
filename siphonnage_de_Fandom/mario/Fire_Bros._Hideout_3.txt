{{Nihongo|'''Fire Bros. Hideout #3'''|かくれファイアブロス その3|Kakure Faia Burosu sono 3|Hidden Fire Bros., Part 3}} also called '''World 6-B''' is a level of World 6 in the game ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. 

==Features==
The level takes place in a castle with hexagonal tiles. The level is complementary to [[Fire Bros. Hideout 1|Fire Bros. Hideout #1]] due to its brighter features.

==[[Green Star]]==
*'''Green Star 1''': Defeat the Fire Bro., two Boomerang Bros., and two Hammer Bros. to claim the Green Star.
