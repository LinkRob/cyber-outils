'''Tulipatooies''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Tulipatooies are tall, orange flowers with a circular mouth, a pair of eyes, and a green leaf on its left and right side each.

===Behavior===
A Tulipatooie continuously spits out a [[Glue Globe]] before sucking it back up.

==In ''Wario World''==
Tulipatooies are only encountered in [[Beanstalk Way]]. Wario can neither damage nor defeat a Tulipatooie. To bypass a Tulipatooie, [[Wario]] must cling to the Glue Globe as it comes out and then [[jump]] off immediately after. If Wario is sucked into a Tulipatooie with its Glue Globe, he takes damage.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
A Tulipatooie's name is a portmanteau of "tulip" and "ptooie," an onomatopoeia for spitting.
