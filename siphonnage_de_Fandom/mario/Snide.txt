'''Snide''' is a major character that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. He is an ally to the Kongs and can be found in his [[Snide's HQ|HQ]] in nearly every level.

==Appearance==
Snide is an orange weasel that wears a blue lab coat.

==History==
Snide used to work for [[King K. Rool]] but didn't like his work and decided to help the Kongs instead after getting fired. Snide is working on a machine that can slow down K. Rool's [[Blast-o-Matic]]. However, Snide will need [[blueprint]]s from K. Rool's [[Kasplat|toughest henchmen]] to help slow down the machine. Each of the five Kongs ([[Donkey Kong]], [[Diddy Kong]], [[Tiny Kong]], [[Lanky Kong]], and [[Chunky Kong]]) have a total of 10 blueprints to find in the game and each blueprint adds 1 minute to the final level [[Hideout Helm]]. If all 50 blueprints are given to Snide, the Kongs will have 60 minutes to shut down the blaster (there will always be 10 minutes added in as extra time). In reward for bringing him blueprints, Snide will give the Kongs a [[Golden Banana]].
