The '''Colossus in Suspenders''', titled the '''Dungareed Colossus''' in British English releases, is a [[Treasure (Pikmin franchise)|treasure]] in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Hey! Pikmin|Hey! Pikmin]]''.

==Description==
The Colossus in Suspenders is an [[amiibo]] of Mario. It also has two counterparts, [[Priceless Artifact]] and [[Great Man]], amiibos of [[Gold Mario]] and [[Silver Mario]], respectively.

==In ''Hey! Pikmin''==
[[File:Colossus in Suspenders possible location.png|thumb|left|200px|The Colossus in Suspenders in Secret Spot 5, one of its possible locations]]
The Colossus in Suspenders can appear in a [[w:c:nintendo:Secret Spot|Secret Spot]] if the player scans a Mario amiibo, like with any other treasures of amiibo. The Colossus in Suspenders is valued at 200 [[w:c:nintendo:Sparklium|Sparklium]].

In the [[w:c:nintendo:Treasure Log|Treasure Log]], [[Captain Olimar|Olimar]] says that he feels he shares a "special bond" with the character since he and Mario have a similarly shaped nose.
