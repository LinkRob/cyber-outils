'''Peach's Castle''' is a stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]''. It was also released as a downloadable stage for ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' on July 30, 2015, under the name '''Peach's Castle (64)''' and later in the base game of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

In ''Super Smash Bros.''{{'}}s 1P Game, the player fights against Mario and Luigi on this stage with a random partner. The stage's background music is an arrangement of the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' [[Ground Theme]].

==Layout==
Peach's Castle is a direct reference to [[Princess Peach's Castle]], specifically as it appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', although the stage takes place in the clouds, high above it. This stage is already unlocked from the start making it a starter stage. One of the main features of this stage is its size. Peach's Castle is one of the smallest stages in the game making it less desirable to play on. The main layout of Peach's Castle composes of two platforms: an upper one resemble a bridge and a lower one that is thicker in size. There are also a moving platform at the bottom that moves left and right at a slow pace. This may act as means for recovery but only if the player is fortunate enough to be on the correct side as it moves out. There are also two objects on the side that may save a player from KOs or act as somewhat of recovery platforms. The only hazard on this stage is a single [[bumper]] located at the very top. A player (especially at a high damage percentage) can get knocked away by the bumper which may result in a KO. A [[Lakitu]] also makes a cameo in the background every few minutes.

In ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U'', the ledges on the moving platform can now be grabbed. The Omega form of Peach's Castle is set on a larger form of the base platform, although the platform now lacks the railing in the foreground and background. A red carpet has also been placed on both sides of the tower. A Lakitu no longer appears in the background.
