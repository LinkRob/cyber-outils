'''Sky Dive''' is a move used by [[Parakarry]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Sky Dive is also Parakarry's basic attack that requires no [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

== Description ==
To use the Sky Dive, the player must press the {{button|n64|a}} button just as soon Parakarry hits the enemy. Sky Dive is useful against flying and ceiling enemies thanks to Parakarry's flying nature.
