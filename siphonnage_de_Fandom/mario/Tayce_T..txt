'''Tayce T.''' is a kind [[Toad (species)|Toad]] chef who is willing to cook for [[Mario]] anytime. She only appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', where she lives in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]].

== Physical description ==
Tayce T. is a Toad with yellow spots on her head. She also has white, curly hair that can be seen.

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
Tayce T. can be first seen cooking for [[Princess Peach]]'s party at her [[Princess Peach's Castle|castle]]. However, the castle soon starts to rise into the sky thanks to [[Bowser]] and Tayce T. manages to escape. Tayce T. can be seen in her home at south Toad Toad where she will cook for Mario. During Chapter 4, a [[Shy Guy]] steals Tayce's [[Frying Pan|frying pan]] and she is unable to cook. Mario manages to retrieve it and she awards him with a [[cake]].

If Mario gives Tayce T. the [[Cookbook]] after it was dropped by [[Gourmet Guy]] during the events of Chapter 4, she can cook two items for him simultaneously. This is required for Mario to cook up better healing items (namely the [[Jelly Ultra]]) and complete certain tasks requested by [[Koopa Koot]] that require the use of two items being cooked up.

===Background===
It was revealed by a green Toad found just outside the Odd House that Tayce T. and [[The Master]] are siblings. According to the Toad, Tayce T. used to live at [[Toad Town Dojo|The Dojo]] with her brother before deciding to move out and live at her own house in the south region of Toad Town. [[Russ T.]] also mentioned how there was a "powerful Goomba master" who has a wife that taught Tayce T. how to cook. [[Goompa]] was revealed to be the Goomba master that trained alongside The Master and, putting these facts together, makes his wife [[Gooma]] the one that taught Tayce T. how to cook.
