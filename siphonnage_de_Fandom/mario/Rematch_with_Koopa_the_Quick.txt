'''Rematch With Koopa the Quick''' is the third [[mission]] of [[Tiny-Huge Island]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

== Description ==
As the name suggests, [[Mario]] races [[Koopa the Quick]] for a second time to the [[Flagpole]] for another [[Power Star]]. This mission takes place in the "Tiny" version of the stage.In the race, Mario has to race Koopa the Quick across [[Tiny-Huge Island]] to "Windswept Valley" for a [[Power Star]]. This is the second and last time that Mario races Koopa the Quick, coming after [[Footrace with Koopa the Quick]], a mission in [[Bob-omb Battlefield]].
