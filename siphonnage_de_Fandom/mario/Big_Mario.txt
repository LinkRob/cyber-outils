'''Big Mario''' is a power-up which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

==Description==
Big Mario is a form that [[Mario]] becomes after obtaining a [[Big Mushroom]]. Big Mario has the ability to destroy [[Brick Block]]s and hard [[block]]s by either running into them or [[jump]]ing on them. While Big Mario can instantly defeat most enemies by [[stomp]]ing them, he remains vulnerable to enemy attacks, and reverts to a smaller form upon being hit.

Big Mario is available only in the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', like the Big Mushroom power-up item, which can appear by scanning either the Classic Colors or Modern Colors [[amiibo]] of Mario. Scanning the Classic Colors amiibo of Mario colors him after his original ''Super Mario Bros.'' sprite, while scanning his Modern Colors amiibo gives Mario a red shirt and blue overalls. Regardless, Big Mario's sprites are modeled after [[Small Mario]]'s sprites from ''Super Mario Bros.'', except upscaled. If Big Mario grabs a differently colored Big Mushroom than the one he transformed from, Mario's color changes accordingly. Placing a Big Mushroom in any other style will still place the mushroom, but grabbing it will do nothing but give 1000 [[point]]s.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Maker''===
If Big Mario is hit, he reverts into Small Mario. While Mario is in his Big form, several visual effects change: some enemies sport some of Mario's features, such as his mustache or his [[Mario's Cap|cap]]; some enemies sport an entirely different appearance, such as [[Bullet Bill]]s becoming miniature [[Luigi]]s; and a filter onscreen that mimics the effects of a CRT television.

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
Luigi, [[Toad (character)|Toad]], and [[Toadette]] can also use this form. The Classic Colors iteration of the Big Mushroom do not return, meaning that Big Mario can only appear in his modern colors. Some other differences include Big Mario reverting to [[Super Mario (form)|Super Mario]] if hit, rather than back into Small Mario, and no longer having the ability to destroy blocks from the side, only above or below him.
