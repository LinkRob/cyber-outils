{{nihongo|'''Builder Mario'''|ビルダーマリオ|Birudā Mario}} is the identity [[Mario]] assumes in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' to reflect the game's level-building mechanic, but would not become a power-up until ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

==Description==
===Appearance===
It adds a tool belt and a hardhat and changes their attire to yellow shirts and their trademark colored overalls. It grants Mario troupe the ability to swing a large hammer to slam the environment and enemies alike.

Other characters have taken on the Builder form, such as [[Toad (species)|Toads]], as Builder Toads.

===Usage===
Unlike [[Hammer Mario]], where the hammers are arched projectiles, Builder Mario uses them as a melee weapon, similar to the hammer attacks in the Paper Mario series and the Mario & Luigi series. Builder Mario can also create a maximum of five Builder Boxes at a time to be used as platforms.

==History==
===Movies===
====''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''====
In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', Builder Toads can be seen when Toad and Mario travel across [[Lift|lift]]s while in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

===Games===
===={{series|Super Mario}}====
=====''Super Mario Maker'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]''=====
Builder Mario is one of the many unlockable [[Mystery Mushroom]] costumes, but not in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] version, and he can be unlocked after completing [[Gnat Attack]] for the first time. Upon transforming into Builder Mario, the title shout before the title screen can be heard; if the player clears the course as Builder Mario, the music heard when starting up the game plays. If the player loses a life as Builder Mario, the Game Over theme from Gnat Attack plays. When the player presses , Builder Mario raises his hammer for his pose.

Outside of the game, Builder Mario is most prominent in promotional material, including artwork.

=====''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''=====
A cap and outfit based on Builder Mario's attire (named the Builder Helmet and the Builder Outfit respectively) can be purchased from the [[Crazy Cap]] store in the [[Metro Kingdom]] using regional coins.

=====''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''=====
Mario, Luigi, Toad or Toadette can take a Builder form by collecting a [[Super Hammer]], a power-up found exclusively in the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' style. It adds a tool belt and a hardhat, and, in Mario and Luigi's case, changes their attire to yellow shirts and colored overalls. Builder Toadette also resembles her appearance as the Chief in Story Mode, albeit with a yellow helmet instead of pink. It grants Mario and co. the ability to swing a large hammer to slam the environment and enemies alike. Unlike Hammer Mario, where the hammers are arched projectiles, Builder Mario uses them as a melee weapon, similar to the hammer attacks in the role-playing games. Builder Mario can also create a maximum of five Builder Boxes at a time to be used as platforms.

===In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''===
Builder Mario's helmet can be seen inside the Mario Bros.' House.

====In {{series|Mario Kart}}====
=====''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''=====
Builder Mario, Builder Toad, Builder Luigi, and Builder Toadette are drivers. Builder Mario and Builder Toad debuted in the 2020 Trick Tour, to coincide with the release of the final major update for ''Super Mario Maker 2'', Builder Luigi debuted in the 2020 Mario vs. Luigi Tour, and Builder Toadette debuted in the 2021 Trick Tour. Builder Mario, Builder Luigi, and Builder Toadette's special skill is the Hammer, while Builder Toad's is the Bob-omb Cannon.

Builder Toadette is the only Builder form to not have a unique emblem, as well as the only one with brand new artwork instead of reused artwork from ''Super Mario Maker 2''. Builder Mario and Luigi have yellow backgrounds instead of white and Builder Toad has blue spots on his cap instead of red spots.

=====''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit]]''=====
The Builder Outfit is an unlockable costume for both Mario and Luigi.

===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong (Nintendo Switch)''===
In the opening of the ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (Nintendo Switch)|Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]'' remake on the [[Nintendo Switch]], Builder Toads are seen working at the [[Mario Toy Company]].<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TcM4DUo3hkA&feature=youtu.be</ref>

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Builder Mario is one of Mario's [[Palette swap|alternate costumes]] in addition to also being a fighter spirit that can be bough from the Vault Shop.
