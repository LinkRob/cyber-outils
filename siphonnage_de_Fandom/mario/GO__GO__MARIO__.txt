'''GO! GO! MARIO!!''' is the first [[level]] of [[World 4 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 4]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. Its main feature is the final portion, in which [[Baby Mario]] touches a [[Super Star]] to travel through the last portion to progress through it by himself.

GO! GO! MARIO!! is the only level in which [[Goomba]]s appear. Many other enemies from the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]], such as [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, [[Piranha Plant|Wild Piranhas]], and [[Bullet Bill]], also make an appearance, possibly owing to how Baby Mario is a main focus of the level.
