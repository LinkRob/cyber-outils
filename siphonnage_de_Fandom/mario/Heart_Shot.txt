The '''Heart Shot''' is [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Special Shot]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can activate the Heart Shot by tapping out a triangle on the Touch Screen twice, like for many of the other playable female characters.

Heart Shot begins with Peach dribbling in a triangle, with pink hearts flowing into her. If the ball is shot inside the three-pointer line, she lifts her left hand and jumps into the air, extends her arms and legs, and casts the ball in the hoop magically, hearts trailing behind it. When the ball lands in, two giant hearts made of many smaller hearts appear.

If Peach uses Heart Shot outside the three-pointer line, a teammate needs to catch the ball and dunk it. The animations remain unchanged.
