The '''Main Observation Room''' is [[Hellen Gravely]]'s office, located in the northern section of the [[Master Suite]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. 

== Description ==
The Main Observation Room consists of 14 screens that enable Hellen to monitor the other fourteen floors of the hotel, a spinning throne chair, a desk, a circular cushion chair, a model of the Last Resort, an artwork of [[King Boo]] on the floor, and a ceiling light based on King Boo's crown jewel.
