The '''Shiverian Racer''', referred to as the '''Supersonic Snowman''' by the [[Shiverian Elder]], is a [[shiverian]] in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', making his home in Shiveria City in the [[Snow Kingdom]]. While other Shiverian Racers exist, this specific Shiverian Racer is found sobbing, saying that his opponents take the race too seriously, and that he is not going to compete and that someone else can take his place.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Unlike most Shiverians, the Shiverian Racer does not wear a hat and is shown with a small blue spike on his head. His outfit consists of a red set of trousers, leaving him topless just like his fellow racers. His body is round and his hands are pentadactyl, with each finger having a single claw.

== In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' ==
This Shiverian Racer is found in the Snowline Circuit's waiting room. He tells Mario that he does not want to compete in the Bound Bowl Grand Prix race because he is nervous and believes he does not stand a chance against the other racers. Mario is able to [[Capture]] the racer with his hat (which is inhabited by [[Cappy]]) so he can compete in two races on Snowline Circuit: "Snowline Circuit Class A" and "Snowline Circuit Class S". After the Snowline Circuit Class A race, the Shiverian Racer is shocked over his victory, while his competitors are sweating over their loss.

The racer appears again post-game after breaking the Snow Kingdom's [[Moon Rock]]. This time, he appears outside of the waiting room and must once again be captured to win two different races on Iceburn Circuit: "Iceburn Circuit Class A" and "Iceburn Circuit Class S". In these instances, the Shiverian Racer seems to have gained confidence.
