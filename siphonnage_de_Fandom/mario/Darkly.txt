'''Darkly''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Darkly is an immigrant from [[Twilight Town]] who moved to [[Rogueport]] who lives on the east side, and he is the only known [[Twilighter]] that lives outside of Twilight Town.

==History==
Darkly can be found behind a fake wall right beside [[Merlon]]'s house. During the events before Chapter 4, Mario must find Darkly in order to figure out how to enter [[Twilight Town]] though the red warp pipe. Darkly says that the person must have their name written on an article of clothing and writes down Mario and his partner's names.

After Mario returns from Twilight Town, where he learns that Darkly is the grandson of [[Mayor Dour]], Mario tries to tell Darkly about a curse that turned Twilight Town's inhabitants into [[pig]]s, but Darkly does not believe him.

==Trivia==
*Since he wasn't present in Twilight Town at the time, Darkly is the only Twilighter not affected by the bell that changes others into [[pig]]s.
