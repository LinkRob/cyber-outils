}}The '''Gingerbread House''' is a recipe made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
By the name and look of this recipe, it is simply a well-made gingerbread house. It is made from a [[Chocolate Cake]] and [[Mousse]]. When used, this dessert restores 30 HP and cures poison, twice the HP the other ingredients restore separately.
