The '''Zebra Elder''' is a character that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. He is one of the five Elders that can bestow the ancient power of [[Bananza]] upon [[Donkey Kong]] and he rules over the [[Freezer Layer]].

==Appearance==
The Zebra Elder is a very large and elderly zebra who has the traditional black and white stripes. He has hair and a beard made of large, old bananas and wears light blue headphones and shades. He also wears a dark red necklace and ankle bracelets that are all in the shape of bananas as well.

==History==
The Zebra Elder is first seen once Donkey Kong and [[Pauline]] meet him at his alter. The Zebra Elder informs the two that a female Kong, being [[Poppy Kong]], has been causing trouble and has caused the Freezer Layer to become even more colder than usual. He also says that in order to get to her, Donkey Kong must master the [[Zebra Bananza]] form but must retrieve the pieces of his [[Ancient Record]] first. Once Donkey Kong and Pauline have done so, the Zebra Elder gladly grants them the power of Zebra Bananza that allows them to cross over fragile bridges and water.
