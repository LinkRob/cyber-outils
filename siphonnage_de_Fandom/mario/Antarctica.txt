'''Antarctica''' is a continent on [[Earth]] in the real world. Its first appearance in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' media is the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[7 Continents for 7 Koopas]]."

==Description==
Antarctica is an icy desert continent located at the southernmost point of Earth. Because of its frozen climate, Antarctica is the only continent without any [[human]] civilization, making it autonomous. Antarctica is largely inhabited by arctic wildlife, such as [[Penguin (real world)|penguins]].

==In ''[[Mario is Missing!]]''==
Antarctica has an essential role during the story. [[Bowser]] and the [[Koopalings]] travel to Antarctica, where they construct a [[Bowser's Castle|fortress]]. Bowser's plan is to steal and sell various artifacts so that he can afford enough hair dryers to melt Antarctica and therefore flood the Earth.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
The entirety of Earth, including Antarctica, can be seen on the online racing menus for ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', but does not serve a role in either game.

==In ''[[Mario Party DS]]''==
Antarctica, along with the rest of the world map, appears on the globe in the [[minigame]] [[Globe Gunners]].

==In other media==
===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
In his attempt to conquer Earth, [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] puts [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Big Mouth Koopa Jr.]] in charge of Antarctica. Big Mouth uses his [[Magic Wand]] to create an [[Abominable Snowkoopa]] to scare the penguins. [[Luigi]] later restores peace to the continent.
