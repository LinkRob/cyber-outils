'''Club Sugar''' is a recurring location in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Description==
[[Jimmy T]] and his [[Jimmy's Folks|family]] frequently visit Club Sugar to go dancing. In the earlier ''WarioWare'' titles, Club Sugar was a central setting of Jimmy T's story.
