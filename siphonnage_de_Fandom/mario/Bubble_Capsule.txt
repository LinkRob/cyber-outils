The '''Bubble Capsule''' is a [[capsule]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
The Bubble Capsule can be thrown to a [[space]] on the board, or used on the player for ten [[coin]]s. A [[Podoboo]] emerges and catches the player on fire and bounce them forward ten spaces. The only downsides are that the player also loses any other capsules they may have had, they cannot control the direction that they go in if they approach a junction, and all board events (including [[Capsule Machine]]s and [[Star Space]]s) are ignored.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zIewImOz12Q</ref> If a player is under the effects of this capsule when approaching a shortbread bridge on [[Sweet Dream]], the bridge will not break upon crossing it.

==Trivia==
*The Bubble Capsule is the incorrect name for the capsule as the character inside of the capsule is actually a [[Podoboo]]. This is not the only capsule that was named incorrectly as the [[Magikoopa Capsule]] was also named incorrectly (a [[Toady]] is inside).
**Both their Orb variations in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' corrected these naming errors with the [[Podoboo Orb]] and [[Toady Orb]] respectively.
