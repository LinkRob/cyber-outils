The '''Golden Pyramid''' is a location that serves as both the main setting and hub area in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
[[Wario]] goes to the Golden Pyramid when he learns in a newspaper article about all of the treasure contained within. He follows a [[Black Cat]] there and falls into the [[Entry Passage]], starting his journey.

{{spoiler}}
In the ending, once Wario has defeated the [[Golden Diva]], the Golden Pyramid begins to crumble. Wario takes the treasure and follows the Black Cat out of the Golden Pyramid. After escaping, Wario watches as the Golden Pyramid disappears into the ground. Following this, the Black Cat reveals herself to be [[Princess Shokora]].
{{endspoiler}}
