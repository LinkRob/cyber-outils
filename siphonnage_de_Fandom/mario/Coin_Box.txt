A '''Coin Box''' is an object featured in a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Description ==
Coin Boxes are [[block]]s with an identical appearance to a [[Question Block]], although in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', Coin Boxes have a distinct, shiny glow to it. Whoever uses a Coin Box receives several but limited number of [[Coin]]s as they move, and in the {{series|Super Mario}}, [[Mario]] or another playable character can wear a Coin Box.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
[[File:QuestionBoxSM3DL.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario wearing a ? Box in ''Super Mario 3D Land'']]
Like [[Propeller Box]]es, Mario or Luigi can wear a Coin Box on the upper part of their body, although this prevents them from being able to throw [[fireball]]s or [[boomerang]]s, or turn into [[Statue Mario|a statue]]. While wearing a Coin Box, Mario or Luigi gain coins as they move around. A Coin Box cannot be removed unless Mario or Luigi touches an enemy or if he reaches the [[Goal Pole]] while wearing it, in which case they receive a [[1-Up]]. Coin Boxes only appear in three courses: [[World 8-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 8-1]], [[World 8-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 8-4]], and [[Special 1-3]].
{{br}}
===''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''===
[[File:Toad Coin Box.jpg|thumb|150px|Toad wearing a Coin Box in ''Super Mario 3D World'']]
Coin Blocks function the same as [[Gold Block]]s from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' in that they disappear once the player's character has obtained 100 coins while wearing it. Like in ''Super Mario 3D Land'', the character cannot use any power-ups while wearing a Coin Box, and touching a Goal Pole yields a [[1-Up]] reward.
