A '''Moon Coin''' is an item similar to a [[Star Coin]] except they display a [[3-Up Moon]] instead of a [[Starman]]. Moon Coins first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''==
They are exclusively found in [[World Star (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Star]]. While Moon Coins serve the same gameplay purpose as Star Coins, they do not contribute to the Star Coins needed to open the different [[Toad House]]s blocked by a [[Star Coin Sign]]. Regardless, Moon Coins are one of the requirements for acquiring a star ranking on a save file. Upon receiving every Moon Coin, every Toad House remains open permanently.

==In ''Mario Golf: World Tour''==
Moon Coins reappear in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]'', in which they can be obtained by the player by achieve harder challenges than the [[Star Coin]] versions. In these challenges, the coins and rings are set in positions to be trickier, the challenge in match plays have to increase, the wind is tougher, and the tees are used.
