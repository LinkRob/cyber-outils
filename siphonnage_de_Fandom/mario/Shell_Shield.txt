'''Shell Shield''' is a special move used by [[Koops]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. 

==Description==
Shell Shield can be used once Koops has been upgraded to the Super Rank, and the move requires 4 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

To use Shell Shield, the player must press {{button|gcn|a}} at the right moment to have Koops drop a protective shell onto [[Mario]]. If done successfully, an intact shell will protect Mario from three attacks without taking any damage at all. However, if the A button is pressed at any other moment, it cracks and lasts a shorter time. Shell Shield can be a very useful defensive technique in battle as Mario can still attack with the shell still on him.

==Trivia==
*Shell Shield works similarly to [[Sushie]]'s [[Water Block]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
