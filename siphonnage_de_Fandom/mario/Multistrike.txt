'''Multistrike''' is a special move in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
Multistrike is a move exclusively used by Dodo. Upon using Multistrike, Dodo can peck a single target four times in quick succession to deal rather high damage. It is possible to block each peck, lessening the damage.
