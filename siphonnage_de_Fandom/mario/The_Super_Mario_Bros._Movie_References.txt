==Other media==
*[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' series]]:
**[[Donkey Kong (video game)|''Donkey Kong'']]: When Mario and Luigi are in Punch-Out!! Pizzeria, Giuseppe can be seen playing an arcade game called "Jump Man" that resembles the original ''Donkey Kong'' arcade game, albeit with the main antagonist being a yeti. In the Great Ring of Kong, the platforms Mario and Donkey Kong stand on are the red girders from the level [[25m]]. [[Barrel]]s and blue ladders can also be seen in the same scene. The sound that plays when Donkey Kong obtains a Fire Flower is the same one that plays when a barrel is broken with a hammer in the NES version of the game. Audio from this game is played on the Super Mario Bros. Plumbing website.
**[[Donkey Kong Country (series)|''Donkey Kong Country'' series]]: Numerous members of the [[Kong Family]] from the series can be seen in the movie such as [[Cranky Kong (Illumination)|Cranky Kong]], [[Diddy Kong (Illumination)|Diddy Kong]], [[Dixie Kong (Illumination)|Dixie Kong]], [[Chunky Kong (Illumination)|Chunky Kong]], [[Swanky Kong (Illumination)|Swanky Kong]], [[Funky Kong (Illumination)|Funky Kong]] and [[Kiddy Kong (Illumination)|Kiddy Kong]]. During one point in the film where Mario attempts to use a Fire Flower against Donkey Kong, he quickly blows it out referencing the same move that is seen in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]''.
**''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'': When Donkey Kong is introduced, a shortened version of the [[DK Rap]] with just the lyric: "DK! Donkey Kong!" playing on loop.
*[[Nintendo Entertainment System]]: The Toad at the antique store asks about an NES cartridge, to which the clerk tells them to blow into it.
*[[Wii|Nintendo Wii]] [[Mii]]: The phone call icon is Mii when Luigi got the phone call.
*''[[Wrecking Crew]]'': [[Foreman Spike]] appears in the film.
*''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'':
**The Warp Pipe sound from this game can be heard several times throughout the movie.
**The bridge over a sea of jumping [[Cheep Cheep]]s that Mario, Princess Peach and Toad walk across is very similar to [[World 2-3 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-3]] from this game.
** A screenshot of the exterior of a fortress can be seen inside the antique store and a nearby barrel contains axes. The store's "open" sign is styled after this game's in-game logo.
**The Ground Theme, Underground Theme, and Underwater Theme are arranged in this film's score. When Mario and Luigi are on their way to their first customer, a construction site they run through is identical to the first screens of [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]].
**When the Toad Guards were suspicious of Mario, they joked about the "princess in another castle", a reference to Mario rescuing a Toad in each world.
**All the original power ups are shown.
*''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'':
**When Mario arrives at the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] and is about to touch a blue mushroom, [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] appears and loudly warns that touching the mushroom will kill him. This matches with the purpose of the [[Poison Mushroom]], which was introduced in this game.
*[[nintendo:Duck Hunt|''Duck Hunt'']]: The Duck from ''Duck Hunt'' appears in a photo at Punch-Out!! Pizzeria.
*[[w:c:kidicarus:Kid_Icarus_(game)|''Kid Icarus'']]: After dinner at the Mario household, Mario can be seen playing the game on his NES. He gets a [[Game Over]] and switches to the news.
*''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'': The song in the [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]] Commercial is a remix of the [[Plumber Rap|Mario Rap]] from the show.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pu57M7cjSD4|title=Super Mario Bros. Plumbing Commercial|work=YouTube|accessdate=2023-02-22|date=2023-02-13}}</ref>
*[[Super Mario Bros. (1993 film)|'''Super Mario Bros.''' (1993 film)]]: Like the live-action movie, the brothers are struggling with their plumbing business and have a rivalry with another more successful one.

*[[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]:
**General: Mario's kart half resembles the [[Pipe Frame]]. The [[Koopa General]]'s design is reminiscent of the [[Blue Spiny Shell]]'s design. He even explodes on Mario and Donkey Kong, referencing what it usually does.
**Even the Illumination logo starts like all Mario Kart races. A minion fails to start its kart, which makes its kart backwards off screen, which happens when the user presses accelerate for too long.
**''[[Mario Kart DS]]'': Rainbow Road as depicted in the film resembles Rainbow Road as depicted in that game.
**''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'':
***Mario's kart half resembles the [[Standard Kart M|Standard Kart]] from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.
***Toad drives a vehicle based on the [[Tiny Titan]] kart.
***Peach rides a bike similar to the [[Mach Bike]] and wears her biker outfit from that game.
***One of the Koopa Troopas has a Bike similar to the [[Bullet Bike]].
***Funky Kong's presence in Rainbow Road references him being the most used character for world records.
**''[[Mario Kart 7]]'': Mario, Peach and Toad arrive at the Jungle Kingdom via a kart with a glider.
**''[[Mario Kart 8]]'': The countdown sound from this game is used for the Illumination logo. The menu music from this game is heard in the song "Karts!" A design on the vehicle-building machine references the Wii U GamePad's functionality as a horn. The sound of Toad's horn is directly from this game. Anti-gravity is used in the Rainbow Road scene.
*[https://starfox.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Fox_(series) Star Fox (series)]: The [https://starfox.fandom.com/wiki/Arwing Arwing] can be spotted in Mario and Luigi's room. There’s a road called Wolf Street, as a reference to Wolf O’Donnell.
*''[[Super Mario Adventures]]'': Bowser's main motive is to marry Princess Peach. Mario says "Mushroom Kingdom, here we come!" in the teaser trailer, referencing the final line in the comic.
*''[[Super Mario 64]]'': The penguins appearing in the movie are based on the ones from that game. [[King Bob-omb]] appears at the wedding.
*[[Nintendo GameCube]]: Mario and Luigi's ringtone is the GameCube start-up theme.
*[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|''Luigi's Mansion'']]: During the [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]] Commercial, the brothers make a face similar to the face Luigi makes on the box art of that game. Luigi mentions mansions during the voice message if someone were to call the Plumbing website's phone number in real life. [[King Boo]] appears at the wedding. Luigi's Mansion concept art was released hinting a new film in the works, a cut scene, or both. Charles Day even wants to make one.
*''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'': At the beginning of the Donkey Kong fight, Mario obtains a [[Mini Mushroom]].
*''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'': [[Lumalee (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Lumalee]] makes an appearance. During the end-credits medley, Level Complete, a remix of the [[Gusty Garden Galaxy]] theme is heard.
*''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'': Bowser is shrunken to a small size when Peach feeds him with Mini Mushroom after his defeat, just like in the end credits of ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.
*''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'': In the middle of the fight against Donkey Kong, Mario grabs a [[Super Bell]] and goes into his [[Cat Mario|Cat]] form. Toad traps small Bowser inside the jar after his defeat, just like his fate in the end credits of ''Super Mario 3D World''.
*''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'': Bowser's main motive is to marry Princess Peach. When he practices his proposal he puts on the same hat he wore in the game. He is also wearing the same wedding outfit as he was in the game in a few scenes of the film. The [[Sand Kingdom]] (under the name "Desert Area") is visited. The Piranha Plant bouquet makes an appearance.
*''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'': [[Attack! Fury Bowser|Attack! Fury Bowser]] is performed by some [[Koopa Troopa]]s in Bowser's Castle after the successful defeat of the [[Penguin]]s and the capturing of the [[Starman|Super Star]].

==Later media==
*[[Nintendo Switch]]: An advertisement for several Super Mario games for the Nintendo Switch uses various gameplay footage to mirror several scenes from the film, most notably the Training Course being recreated within Super Mario Maker 2. Mario succumbs to a Fire Bar, Bullet Bill, and falling Donut Block in the recreation, as he does to dummies of these three in the film.
*''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'': Fire Peach has her hair down instead of in a ponytail, like in the movie.
*''Migration'': Advertising for this film, also produced by Illumination, mentions that it is from "...the studio that brought you The Super Mario Bros. Movie", using the film's logo.
*''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'': The ending of the Bowser Kaboom Squad mode enhances the players with Star power and has them rapidly attack Impostor Bowser with punches, similarly to Mario and Luigi's actions against the real Bowser in the climax of the movie.
In the minigame description of Sunset Standoff in the English localization, the Banzai Bill enemy is renamed Bomber Bill, its name from this film.
*''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'': Diddy Kong and Dixie Kong retain the pins on their hats from the film.
*''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2#Nintendo Switch|Super Mario Galaxy 2 (Nintendo Switch)]]'': Mari's design changes to appear identical to a Lumalee, and her personality is shown to be very similar to the Lumalee in this film, with a jaded and nihilistic temperament. Additionally, she mentions having vague memories from one cycle of the universe where she was trapped inside a cage, much like the movie Lumalee.
*''It's Me, Mario! Stop Motion Shorts'': Toad using his mushroom cap to camouflage among mushrooms in "Hide and Seek" is reminiscent of Toad's introductory scene in the film.
