The '''Empty Wallet''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
The Empty Wallet can only be found after [[Mario]] accepts [[Koopa Koot]]'s favor to find it. Mario must head just outside of [[Koopa's Shop]] in [[Koopa Village]] and find the Empty Wallet in the bushes by shaking them. After returning it to Koopa Koot, the elderly Koopa rewards Mario with a [[coin]].
