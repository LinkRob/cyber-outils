'''Bowser Bop''' is a story mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player's character and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] must compete to see who can bop the balloon [[Koopa Kid]]s that appear from different spots. This mini-game is very basic and doesn't require much strategy except to bop more Koopa Kids than Toad (or another player in Party Mode). Whoever bops the most Koopa Kids wins.

== Controls ==
*{{button|gcn|stick}} - Move
*{{button|gcn|a}} - Bop with hammer
