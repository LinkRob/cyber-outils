==Effects==

Drinking a Sap Syrup restores 13 [[Heart Point|HP]] over time and even halves damage taking from enemies for 40 seconds. This item is very useful for later levels. To make a Sap Syrup, one must give a [[Sap Soup]] to [[Saffron]] of [[Flipside]].
