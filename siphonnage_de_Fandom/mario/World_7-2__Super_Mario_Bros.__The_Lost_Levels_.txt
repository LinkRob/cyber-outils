'''World 7-2''' is the second [[level]] of [[World 7 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 7]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the twenty-sixth level overall in said game.

==Layout==
World 7-2 is split between two sections. The first section has a horizontally moving [[lift]] that will loop around the area once the player steps onto it. Above the first pool of water is a [[? Block]] containing a power-up. In order for the player to progress, they need to bounce off of the [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s to reach a [[Warp Pipe]]. The pipe leads to an underground area with [[Coin]]s and a power-up in the far left [[Brick Block]] in the row of Brick Blocks at the top. The Warp Pipe will take the player to the second area.

The second area has [[Lakitu]]s and [[Fire Bar]]s the player needs to avoid as they cross platforms with [[Koopa Troopa]]s on them. The first two Fire Bars spin in different directions. After the third Fire Bar are falling platforms the player needs to cross to reach a stationary one. Then the player has to bounce off of a Koopa Paratroopa to reach a falling platform, followed by quickly jumping to a pipe with a [[Piranha Plant]] in it. After this pipe is a chasm with the [[Goal Pole]] on the other side.
