'''Elite X-Nauts''' are a variant of [[X-Naut]]s and an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Elite X-Nauts are the most powerful out of any type of X-Naut. They are [[Sir Grodus]]'s most powerful minions and the main guards of the [[X-Naut Fortress]] on [[The Moon]]. Elite X-Nauts are also equipped with some of the more powerful items like [[Stop Watch]]es or [[Thunder Rage]]s. In battle, these guards area also aided by more elites or an [[X-Naut PhD]] or some [[Yux]]es.
