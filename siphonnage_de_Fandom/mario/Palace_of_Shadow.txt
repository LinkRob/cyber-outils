The''' Palace of Shadow''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It serves as the main setting for [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door#Chapter 8: The Thousand-Year Door|Chapter 8]] and is the last area that is explored in the game.

==History==
There is only one way to enter the palace, through the [[Thousand-Year Door]]. Only when Mario has the [[Magical Map|Map]] and the seven [[Crystal Stars]] will the door open. Within this area is where many dangerous traps and enemies are found. Some rooms contain trap doors, some contain many spikes, others are filled with deadly enemies like [[Dry Bones]] or [[Chain Chomp]]s. There is also a Riddle Tower within the large palace that has eight different puzzles in which [[Mario]] must solve. After some heavy battles with [[Sir Grodus]] and [[Bowser]], Mario must fight the final boss, the [[Shadow Queen]], in the deepest area within the palace.
