|image = [[File:BowserHighwayShowdown.jpg|250px]]}}'''Bowser's Highway Showdown''' is the final level of [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 1]] of the game ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
Bowser's Highway Showdown is mainly a liner level which is based on Bowser's castle. This all eventually leads to a very long platform where the players do battle against [[Bowser]] for the first time in the game. There are two ways to play the Bowser fight:
*9 hit- Simply kick 9 bombs onto his car.
*3 hit- Use the [[Super Bell]] to scratch just 3 bombs into Bowser's face for a short victory.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Activate the [[Green Star Ring]] at near the first cannon to reveal eight green coins. Get those coins before the timer runs out to get the star.
*'''Green Star 2''': To the right of the fourth cannon, be in Cat form to spin the wheel revealing a high ledge. Climb it to get the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': Kick the soccer bomb to the third goomba tower to get the star.

==Stamp==
[[File:Bowser's_Highway_Showdown_Stamp.png|thumb]]By the third and fourth cannon, kick a soccer bomb on the block wall to get the Bowser stamp.
