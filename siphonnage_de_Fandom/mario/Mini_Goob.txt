'''Mini Goobs''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mini Goobs are miniature [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] that are a smaller variant of [[Goob]]s, but aside from being smaller, Mini Goobs have the same appearance as Goobs.

===Behavior===
Several Mini Goobs are always encountered in groups, much like [[Mini Hammer]]s, which are a smaller variant of [[Hammer (Luigi's Mansion 3)|Hammers]]. Mini Goobs lack any [[Heart Point|HP]], so they can easily be captured with a flash from the [[Strobulb]] and then being vacuumed into the [[Poltergust G-00]], just like with Mini Hammers.
