'''St.GIGA''' was a Japanese satellite radio company that collaborated with [[Nintendo]] to produce software for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super Famicom]]'s [[Satellaview]] add-on, including a share of unique ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, such as ''[[Excitebike: Bun Bun Mario Battle]]'', including variations of Super Famicom games such as ''[[BS Super Mario Collection]]'', a variation of ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]''.
