'''Thieving Slinker''' is a kind of ghost and a variant of [[Slinker|Slinkers]] that made their debut in [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']]. Like the other [https://mario.fandom.com/wiki/Luigi's_Mansion_3#Rare rare ghosts], Thieving Slinkers only appear in the ScareScraper mode on floors that are multiple of five. They have 250HP, which is higher than that of regular Slinkers' by 10. Their special power is to steal Luigi's [[Poltergust G-00]].

== Appearance ==
Thieving Slinkers have dark purple glowing bodies instead of purple. They also have yellow glowing eyes. The rest of their physical features are exactly same as regluar Slinkers'.

== In [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']] ==
When Thieving Slinkers appear, they might get invisible and sneak up on the [[Luigi]]<nowiki/>s like regular Slinkers. However, unlike regular Slinkers that can only grab Luigi from behind and deal damage, Thieving Slinkers can grab Luigi's [[Poltergust G-00]] and pull it off from Luigi's back. The Poltergust G-00 will then be teleported to somewhere in the room. Therefore, the player would have to find the Poltergust G-00 back in order to let Luigi regain the ability of capturing ghosts. If the Thieving Slinker is defeated by another player before the player that lost the Poltergust G-00 finds it, the Poltergust G-00 will get back to the player that lost it immediately. They are captured in the same way as regular Slinkers.
