'''Crosshair Cranky''' is a mode exclusive to the [[Game Boy Color]] version of ''[[Donkey Kong Country (Game Boy Color)|Donkey Kong Country]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[Donkey Kong]] travels to various islets off the coast of [[Donkey Kong Island]], and these are all locations that he cannot visit in the Adventure mode. Despite his name appearing in the title, [[Cranky Kong]] does not appear in the mode, similar to [[Funky Kong]] with [[Funky's Fishing|Funky Fishing]] (though the latter would directly host the minigame in the [[Game Boy Advance]] [[Donkey Kong Country (Game Boy Advance)|version]]).

===Stages===
*'''Kremlings and Kongs''': Shoot fifty [[Kritter|Kremling]]s which hop on [[banana]]s while avoiding the Kongs.
*'''Barrel O' Kremlings''': Donkey Kong must shoot a certain number of Kremlings. The counter begins at 100 but decreases by one if Donkey Kong shoots a green Kremling; two if it is a red Kremling; or five if it is a blue Kremling. If Donkey Kong misses too many Kremlings, he loses.
*'''Kremling Krackdown''': Donkey Kong must shoot all 70 Kremlings.
*'''Buddies Beware''': [[Rambi]], [[Expresso]], and [[Winky]] are chased by a group of [[Gnawty|Gnawties]]. Donkey Kong must shoot [[coconut]]s in the holes so the Animal Buddies do not fall through, similar to the gameplay of ''[[Manhole (Game & Watch)|Manhole]]''. They must do it 25 times to complete the stage.
*'''Wanted''': Donkey Kong must shoot the Kremlings that try to steal their bananas in a dark room.
*'''Galleon Gunner''': Donkey Kong must shoot [[King K. Rool]]'s [[cannon]]s before he shoots [[cannonball]]s at Donkey Kong. After 36 hits, six upon each of the cannons, Donkey Kong wins the stage and the mode at large.

==Controls==
*{{button|gbc|Pad}} Move crosshair
*{{button|gbc|a}} Shoot (max of four shots)
*{{button|gbc|b}} Reload
