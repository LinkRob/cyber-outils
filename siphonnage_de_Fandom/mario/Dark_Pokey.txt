}}A '''Dark Pokey''' is a subspecies of a [[Pokey]] that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', mainly found in the [[Flopside]] area of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Dark Pokeys resemble a black silhouette of a [[Pokey]].
=== Behavior ===
As a defense, they throw segments of their body at an enemy. If someone was hit by its body segment, they would become badly poisoned.
