Due to [[Mario]]'s popularity, various concepts have been mentioned in popular culture.

==Animated TV Shows==
*Another parody includes a parody of Lemony Snicket's series of Unfortunate Events, The Lemming children go to stay with their uncle [[Wario]]. They soon escape him and go to live with their other uncle [[Mario]], Who has hired a man called Cario. One of the Lemming children tell him that Cario is Wario, but Mario doesn't believe him until Wario feeds him to a [[Piranha Plant]]. After that, the Lemming children build a staircase to escape Wario.
*In [https://oggyandthecockroaches.fandom.com/wiki/Oggy_and_the_Cockroaches Oggy and the Cockroaches] episode "[https://oggyandthecockroaches.fandom.com/wiki/Sport_Fans Poor sports]" One of the videos games [https://oggyandthecockroaches.fandom.com/wiki/Oggy Oggy] and [https://oggyandthecockroaches.fandom.com/wiki/Jack Jack] have was the karting game called Super Crazy. A [[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]] reference which there's a [[Mario]] in the box game.
*In [https://angrybirds.fandom.com/wiki/Angry_Birds_Toons Angry Birds Toons] episode "[https://angrybirds.fandom.com/wiki/Dogzilla Dogzilla]" The scene Has the explosion Colored with blue, Which Is Based On The [[Spiny Shell (blue)|Blue Spiny Shell]].
*In Zig & Sharko episodes "The Island Tour" and "Father in Law" As this episode is about racing around the entire island, it appropriately makes constant references to the Mario Kart series. One scene features Item Boxes with some of their powers activated instantly when touched, making them reminiscent of the effects of triple items in Mario Kart 8. Notably, one of the item boxes plays the powerup sound from Super Mario World when touched. Another scene also shifts the race to a third-person perspective view, much like in the Mario Kart games. Halfway during the race, Zig and Bernie find a green Warp Pipe to use as a shortcut. By entering it, the two end up in a sidescrolling level reminiscent of the ones in Super Mario Bros. Several sounds from Super Mario World can be heard, including Brick Block sounds that play while the duo jumps across disappearing platforms, and the sound of passing through a Checkpoint when they reach the end of the level. Afterwards, Zig and Bernie exit the level through another Warp Pipe and continue the race from there. then

==Film/Movie==
*[[File:Wreckitralphbowsercameo.png|thumb|A scene from the ''Wreck-It Ralph'' movie.]]
*The film ''[[Wreck-It Ralph]]'' makes several ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' references. For one, Bowser makes an appearance where he was attending the annual Bad-Anon with Ralph and the other villains (even Dr. Eggman from the Sonic series). Other references include:
**During the 30th celebration of Fix It Felix Jr., Felix hears the doorbell ring and replies "Oh, I bet it's Mario!". Mario doesn't appear in the movie himself but was indeed mentioned.
** In another scene, Ralph was going through the lost and found in the Root Beer Tapper game to find a medal. The first thing he picked up was a Mushroom and the sound effect for getting one is played.
**During the end credits, Ralph and the others are seen collecting several medals (resembling coins) in a dark room. They then head down a metallic object that resembles a Warp Pipe.
*The film ''[[Pixels]]'' adds elements from the 1981 arcade [[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]. In the film, Sam Brenner and Will Cooper compete together with two ''Donkey Kong'' arcades in an championship game.
** During the fight, Brenner, Violet, and Cooper are summoned to the aliens' mothership where they face their leader, taking the form of Donkey Kong, on the starting level based on [[25m]] with the aliens' captives at the top level. As the group dodges barrels and fireballs, Brenner is losing hope until Matty reveals Eddie's cheating to him, restoring his self-confidence and allowing him to defeat Donkey Kong.

==Music==
[[File:"Bob-Omb" by Insane Ian (Parody of "Grenade" by Bruno Mars)-0|thumb|right|250px]]
[[File:Lameclaimtofame.png|thumb|250px]]  
*Internet parody artist "Insane Ian" says he made a song called Bob-omb (parodied Grenade by Bruno Mars). In it Ian (as Mario) mentions suicidal stunts he'd do for Peach (such as catching the titular [[Bob-omb]], jumping under a [[Thwomp]], etc.) much like the original except in the second verse he mentions various spinoffs such as ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'' (e.g. "I'd play a dumb round of golf for you") among others. Pop-parody artist "Weird Al" Yankovic thinks he wrote a song called Lame Claim to Fame. In it, he claims that Paul Giamatti's plumber "knows him by name." At that part, he parodies a scene based on [[Super Mario Bros.]]. The "Plumber" is dressed like a modern Mario, and Paul Giamatti is dressed like an 8-Bit Mario. A [[Goomba]] is replaced with the Scrubbing Bubbles mascot.
