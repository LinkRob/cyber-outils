'''World 2-1''' is the first [[level]] of [[World 2 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 2]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'', and the first level of the game overall.

==Layout==
The player starts on a path that leads into a bridge with five [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s jumping towards the player, and in the middle [[? Block]] above the bridge is a power-up. Across a pit is a flight of stairs with a row of [[Brick Block]]s above, the middle of which containing a [[Super Star]]. Next are a series of pipes with [[Piranha Plant]]s in them, with the far left being a [[Warp Pipe]] to an underground area with [[Coin]]s, exiting out to the second-from-the-right Warp Pipe on the surface. Over the platform afterwards are two [[Hidden Block]]s, the first of which will allow the player to reach the second, which contains a power-up. Next are two horizontally moving [[lift]]s with a [[Super Spring]] between them. In the next area are two rows of Brick Blocks, and directly to the right of the pipe are two Hidden Blocks the player can use to find a [[Beanstalk]] in one of the upper blocks; the Beanstalk will take the player to [[Coin Heaven]], which will drop the player near the rows of Brick Blocks at the end. In the left-most ? Block ahead is a [[Poison Mushroom]], followed by a Super Spring before a series of narrow platforms over a large [[pit]], which has a couple of Koopa Paratroopas floating over them. Over a high platform afterward is a Hidden Block with a [[1-Up Mushroom]], followed by a flight of stairs with [[Goomba]]s walking down them that lead to the [[Goal Pole]].
