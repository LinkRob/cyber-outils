'''Lottery Shops''' are buildings in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' where players can play games of chance to win prizes. Each game costs five [[coin]]s to play. There are two types of games, one of which is randomly chosen each time a player visits the shop. 

The '''Lottery Ball Game''' involves rotating {{button|gcn|stick}} until a colored ball pops out of a machine. A blue ball indicates third prize, a pink one second prize, and a gold one first prize. A white ball wins nothing. The player has an 68% chance of getting a white ball, a 20% chance of getting a blue ball, a 10% chance of getting a pink ball, and a 2% chance of getting a gold ball.<ref name="Decomp">Mario Party 4 Decomp, 1 December 2025 [https://github.com/mariopartyrd/marioparty4/blob/413d1b0529d872cd20a05dac9de06f683726efce/src/game/board/lottery.c#L999 SetBallPrize]</ref> <ref name="Rare_Oddities_7>Mario Party Legacy, February 15, 2019. [https://youtu.be/2rB2ekdluyA Getting Lucky With the Lottery in Mario Party 4 | Rare Oddities #07] Retrieved July 13, 2021.</ref>

The '''Scratch Ticket Game''' involves the player picking one of twelve scratch cards and scratching it to reveal a prize. One card each will win the player first, second, or third prize, indicated by a 1, 2, or 3 on the card, respectively. The remaining nine cards have the text "Try Again", winning nothing, giving the player a 75% chance of winning nothing, and an 8.333% chance of winning any of the three prizes individually (for a total of 25%).<ref name="Rare_Oddities_7"></ref> Computer players will always pick a "Try Again" card.

The prizes are a [[Super Mega Mushroom]] or a [[Super Mini Mushroom]] for third prize, 30 coins for second prize, and 100 coins for first prize. If a player winning third prize already holds three items, they will not be able to collect the prize.

Additionally, if it is a player's first visit to the Lottery Shop and the visit came before the last five turns of the game, the player receives a free [[Lucky Party Ticket]]. Also, if any players enter the shop while under the effects of a [[Mini Mushroom]] or Super Mini Mushroom, a block is provided for them to stand on when playing.
