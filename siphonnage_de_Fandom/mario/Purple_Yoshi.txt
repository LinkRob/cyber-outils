'''Purple Yoshis''' are a variety of [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] that first appeared in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. Purple Yoshis are generally smarter than the other Yoshis and often handle dangerous environments better than to other Yoshis and work best under pressure.

==History== 
===''Yoshi'' franchise===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''====
[[Image:Purple Yoshi SNES.jpg|thumb|left|A Purple Yoshi starting the level.]]
[[Image:Purple Yoshi GBA.jpg|thumb|right|A Purple Yoshi starting the level as seen in the remake.]]
Purple Yoshis make their debut in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', in these games, the Purple Yoshi is playable on the fifth stage of each world. They usually begin the second half of each world directly after the Fort. Purple Yoshis are playable in [[Hop! Hop! Donut Lifts]], [[Watch Out for Lakitu]], [[Jammin' Through The Trees]], [[Chomp Rock Zone]], [[Goonie Rides!]], and [[The Very Loooooong Cave]]. In the GBA [[Reissue|remake]], the Purple Yoshi is a lighter purple than in the original.

====''Tetris Attack''====
[[File:TA4.png|thumb|right]]
A Purple Yoshi make a brief appearance on the title screen in ''[[Tetris Attack]]''. Alongside [[Yoshi]] himself, a [[Blue Yoshi]], and two [[Pink Yoshi]]s.
{{br}}

====''Yoshi's Story''====
[[Image:Yoshi's Story Cover.jpg|thumb|left|A Purple Yoshi seen in ''Yoshi's Story''.]]
Purple Yoshi, with [[Brown Yoshi]], were [[Yoshi's Story/Beta elements|originally intended]] to be in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'', but both were removed in the final version. A Purple Yoshi still appears in the intro of ''Yoshi's Story''. Artwork for the Purple Yoshi was also released alongside the game leading people to believe that they were an unlockable character that could be unlocked by collecting every coin in the game and was covered by numerous websites but this rumor was eventually debunked.

====''Yoshi Touch & Go''====
[[Image:Purple Yoshi Fast.jpg|thumb|right|The Purple Yoshi is running.]]
In ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]'', you can acquire a Purple Yoshi by traveling at least 20,000 yards in Marathon mode. The Purple Yoshi can carry a total of 50 [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] and is the fastest Yoshi in the game, these two factors make the Purple Yoshi the most powerful Yoshi in the game.
{{br}}

====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
[[Image:Purple Yoshi DS.jpg|thumb|left|A Purple Yoshi starting the level.]]
Purple Yoshis reappear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', with an identical role to that of ''Yoshi's Island''. Just as before they are playable in the fifth level of each World, in this game, they’re often the first Yoshi to utilize the abilities of new babies as the first level with the Purple Yoshi is also the first to include [[Stork Stop]]s. Purple Yoshis are playable in [[Catch the Breeze]], [[Boing!]], [[Heeeeeeeeere's Wario!]], [[Friend or Foe?]], and [[The Cave That Never Ends]]. In the [[At Last, Bowser's Castle!|in the final battle]], the Purple Yoshi is [[Baby Wario]]'s signature Yoshi and if the player used [[Baby Wario]] for the final battle, they will have control over the Purple Yoshi.
{{br}}
====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[Image:Purple Yoshi 3DS.jpg|thumb|right|A Purple Yoshi at the level's beginning.]]
Purple Yoshis return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', in this game, Purple Yoshis are again playable on the fifth level of each world, these being [[Heads Up, Hop Up]], [[Lantern Ghost Grotto]], [[Don't Fear the Spear]], [[Metal Eggdozers on a Roll]], [[Cruise the Clouds]], and [[Hotfoot Hurdle]]. Also, in this game, if a [[Grim Leecher]] touches a Yoshi, the Yoshi will turn purple regardless of their color, and the controls will be reversed.
{{br}}
====''Yoshi's Woolly World'' / ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''====
[[Image:Yarn Purple Yoshi.jpg|thumb|left|A Yarn Purple Yoshi.]]
Purple Yoshi is back in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' as a playable character. The Purple Yoshi does not play differently from the other Yoshis in the game. If the game is not played on file two, Purple Yoshi can be unlocked as a playable character in two ways, the hard way is by completing [[King Bowser's Castle (Yoshi's Woolly World)|King Bowser's Castle]] and the easy way is by scanning a Purple Yarn Yoshi [[amiibo]].
{{br}}
====''Yoshi's Crafted World''====
[[Image:Crafted Purple Yoshi.jpg|thumb|right|A Crafted Purple Yoshi.]]
Purple Yoshis return in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]'' retaining the same role as in previous games, they are among one of the selectable Yoshis and can be played as in any level of the game.
{{br}}
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Sunshine''====
[[File:SMS Purple Yoshi Platform.png|thumb|left|Purple Yoshi in ''Super Mario Sunshine'']]
A Purple Yoshi appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. In this game, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can hatch as a Pink Yoshi upon eating a [[Durian]] or [[Pepper]]. A particular [[Yoshi Egg]] in the mission [[Yoshi's Fruit Adventure]] of [[Ricco Harbor]] is craving a Durian which Mario must give him at which point he will hatch into a Purple Yoshi. Whenever a Purple Yoshi spews [[Juice]] at an enemy, it turns into a purple platform which moves horizontally, this can be useful for reaching platforms or ledges that are too far away, especially when it comes to enemies that jump high such as [[Cheep-Cheep]]s or [[Jumping Blooper]]s.

====''Super Mario Run''====
[[Image:Purple Yoshi Run.jpg|thumb|left|Purple Yoshi runs again.]]
Purple Yoshi was made available as a playable character in ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' following the release of the version 2.0.0 update on March 22, 2017. Purple Yoshi can be unlocked after purchasing Purple Yoshi's House from the Kingdom Builder shop, which only becomes available if the player has collected 150 Blue Toads, 150 Green Toads, and 100 Purple Toads in the [[Toad Rally]] mode. The Purple Yoshi, like the other Yoshis has the ability to [[Flutter Jump]] and harmlessly walk along [[Muncher]]s and [[Spike Trap]]s (though Spike Traps are still hazardous from the sides).
{{br}}

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
Purple Yoshis make their ''Super Smash Bros.'' debut in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' as an alternate color for [[Yoshi]]. Like in ''Super Mario Sunshine'', they wear orange shoes. It also reappears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

===''Paper Mario'' series===

===''Paper Mario''===
[[Image:Green Yoshis Village.jpg|thumb|right|Mario and [[Goombario]] near a Purple Yoshi.]]
Purple Yoshis appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' this time commonly being found in [[Yoshi Village]] on [[Lavalava Island]]. In this game, their role isn’t that big but they can be talked to like other common NPCs.
{{br}}

====''Paper Mario: Color Splash''====
[[Image:Paper Purple Yoshi.jpg|thumb|left|A Paper Purple Yoshi.]]
Purple Yoshis appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' in [[The Emerald Circus]], in this game, unlike previous games, the Purple Yoshis are actually wild creatures who like to swallow those who come near, the Purple Yoshis are first found in a cage along with other wild [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]. After [[Lemmy Koopa]]'s disappearance during his performance, the Yoshis break out of their cage, when [[Mario]] battles the enemies in the tent, a Purple Yoshi can show up to attack Mario with its tongue, eating one of his [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]]s if he does not [[Guard|block]] the attack. If Mario successfully blocks it, the Purple Yoshi leaves harmlessly.
{{br}}
===''Mario & Luigi'' series===
====''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''====
[[Image:Purple Yoshi SS.jpg|thumb|right|A Purple Yoshi providing a Neon Egg.]]
Purple Yoshis return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', in this game, they can be found around [[Yoshi Theater]] alongside various other [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]. One particular Purple Yoshi is in need of a [[Bean Fruit]] to placate his hunger. Once [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] provide the Purple Yoshi with a Bean Fruit, the Purple Yoshi will give them a [[Neon Egg]].
{{br}}
====''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''====
[[Image:Purple Yoshi SS DX.jpg|thumb|left|A Purple Yoshi providing a Neon Egg.]]
Purple Yoshis are seen again in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. In this game, they retain their role from the original, just as before, there is a Purple Yoshi in the [[Yoshi Theater]] that is craving a [[Bean Fruit]] which [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] need to find to satisfy his hunger. Once the Purple Yoshi is given a [[Bean Fruit]], he provides the plumbers with the [[Neon Egg]] they need. Additionally, several Yoshis (including Purple Yoshis) are also seen on the touch screen in the cinema chairs during the events of [[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]].
{{br}}
===''Mario Kart World''===
{{main|Aristocrat Yoshi}}
A Purple Yoshi named [[Aristocrat Yoshi]] appears as an unlockable playable character in ''[[Mario Kart World]]''.


==Physical appearance==
[[File:PurpleYoshi.gif|left|frame|]]
In ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', Purple Yoshi has purple skin and wears shoes and a saddle that are both orange. These traits were carried over into ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' and have been the most consistent aspects of its design ever since. However, Purple Yoshi has slight altered appearances for a few games; the Game Boy Advance port of ''Yoshi's Island'' and early artwork of ''Yoshi's Story'' feature Purple Yoshi wearing pink shoes, ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' features it wearing red shoes, and ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' features it wearing light brown shoes.

==Trivia==
*Purple Yoshi is one of the only three Yoshis in the ''[[Yoshi's Island (series)|Yoshi's Island]]'' games that is never shown having the baby passed to him upon level completion mostly due to the fact that he only appears in the levels directly after the Forts where the goal is to beat the boss rather than reach the [[GOAL! Ring]] (the usual area where the Yoshis pass the baby), the other two being the [[Black Yoshi]] and the [[White Yoshi]].
*The Purple Yoshi is one of the only two Yoshi colors that isn’t playable in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', the other being the [[Brown Yoshi]].
