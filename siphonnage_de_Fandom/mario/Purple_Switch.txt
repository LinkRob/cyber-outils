'''Purple Switches''',<ref>''[[Super Mario 64]]'' Nintendo Player's Guide, page 18.</ref> also named '''Purple Exclamation Switches''',<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/19980224211156/http://www.nintendo.com/n64/super_mario64/bowser1.html Bowser in the Dark World]. ''Nintendo: Super Mario 64 Strategy'' (Wayback Machine).</ref> are a type of [[! Switch]] found in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. 

==Description==
Purple Switches are found in a majority of the game's courses. Though their exact effect varies between courses, they usually either cause a set of breakable platforms to appear or make unnavigable terrain passable, such as turning a steep slope into a staircase, as in [[Bowser in the Dark World]]. With very few exceptions, the effect of the Purple Switch is temporary. A ticking sound indicates that the switch is still in effect and becomes more rapid as the effect is about to expire.

In ''Super Mario 64'', their hue is dark violet while in ''Super Mario 64 DS'', they are dark red. Furthermore, the remake introduces a yellow, star-shaped variant called a [[Star Switch]].
