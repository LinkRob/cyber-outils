The '''Star Spin'''<ref>''Super Mario Galaxy'' trading card description for "Mario in Training"</ref> (alternatively '''Star-Spin''',<ref>''Super Mario Galaxy'' trading card "Mario (Star-Spin)"</ref> '''star spin''',<ref>''Super Mario Galaxy'' trading card description for "[[Tweester (Super Mario Galaxy)|Tweester]]"</ref> or simply the '''Spin'''), is a move that [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can perform in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', replacing [[punch]]ing  and [[kick]]ing from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. The [[Co-Star Luma]] can also perform this move in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', and [[Rosalina]] can perform it in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Reissue#Ports|port]], ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It is typically used to attack enemies and interact with objects such as levers; when performed midair, it also gives the playable character extra height, similarly to a [[Double Jump (airborne)|Double Jump]]. The Star Spin creates a sparkly blue trail around the character when performed. Star Spinning near enemies will stun or defeat them depending on the enemy. The move is slightly like a [[Spin Jump]] or [[Mario Tornado]] except that it can also be performed while on the ground.

Star Spinning while in a [[Launch Star]] or a [[Sling Star]] will send Mario flying. Star Spinning can also break certain objects like [[Item Crystal]]s, [[rock]]s and [[crate]]s. It is also used to activate special powers if you have a certain power-ups. If you have a [[Bee Mushroom]] while you star spin, the normal sparkle effects will be replaced with honey. If you [[jump]], star spin, and then [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]], the somersault you do in the air before ground pounding will be at a slight tilt, and Mario will land with a bow. 

Occasionally, the Star Spin move is also used as a means by which to activate or use several power-ups, including the [[Fire Flower]], the [[Red Star]], the [[Cloud Flower]], the [[Rock Mushroom]], and obviously, the [[Spin Drill]]. Although not necessary for it to work, spinning while the [[Ice Flower]] is in effect will cause Mario to skate across any open body of water quickly as opposed to walking, and spinning while the [[Boo Mushroom]] is in effect will cause Boo Mario to become intangible for a short time. However, spinning cannot be performed as [[Spring Mario]]. [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] also possesses his own variation of the Star Spin called the [[Head Shake]].

The Star Spin can be performed in ''Super Mario Galaxy'' and ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' by shaking the [[Wii Remote]], in the ''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]'' version, it can be performed by either pressing Y or by shacking one of the [[Nintendo Switch]] Joy-Cons. In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' as well as ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'', [[Rosalina]] can perform this move by pressing Y or X however this can only be done while she is in her [[Super Mario (form)|Super]] or [[Small Mario|Small]] forms.

[[Image:Star Spin Beta.jpg|thumb|right|Mario performing a Star Spin in the beta version of ''Super Mario Galaxy''.]]
The Star Spin makes its debut in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. Here, [[Mario]] gains this ability after meeting [[Rosalina]]. The guardian of the cosmos herself will entrust Mario with the care of [[Luma (character)|Baby Luma]] who will sit inside [[Mario's Cap]] granting Mario the ability to do a Star Spin. With this move  Mario can deflect certain projectiles flying at him such as [[Coconut]]s, stun or kill enemies, activate [[Lever Switch|switches]], break [[Item Crystal]]s, swim faster, slide across [[Sproutle Vine]]s quicker. and even pick up certain nearby items. The Star Spin can also be used to pick up [[Shell]]s and [[Mario]] can also preform a Star Spin to attract [[Coin]]s when underwater or under the effects of the [[Red Star]] (in the beta stages he could also do this at any time). Mario also ascends very slightly whenever he does a Star Spin in mid-air. Whenever Mario does a Star Spin, Baby Luma pops out of his hat for a second which enforces a delay. Mario can only perform a Star Spin one time in the air. He must touch solid ground before he can use the ability again. Even on the ground there is a slight recharge time for this move. When [[Luigi]] uses this move, a slightly longer recharge time is required so keep this in mind.

An extremely useful application of the Star Spin is the ability to stun, freeze, or make enemies dizzy by Star Spinning into them. This gives Mario a chance to escape or attack the enemies if he is fast enough. Spinning is needed to defeat certain bosses including [[Dino Piranha]], [[King Kaliente]], [[Topmaniac]], [[Bouldergeist]], [[Major Burrows]], [[Baron Brrr]], [[Fiery Dino Piranha]], and the master himself, [[Bowser]]. One of the major applications of this move is the ability to use it to skate on icy surfaces. Mario just has to Star Spin while moving on an icy surface for this to work. Mario can also chain a spin into a [[Double Kick]]. Also, if Mario crouches and Star Spins, he kicks instead of holding his arms out, this can be really useful when attacking enemies whilst attempting to avoid being hit. Additionally, if Mario is to attempt a Star Spin Jump whilst under the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Ice Flower]], the jump will be significantly nerfed nullifying it's true potential.

Aside from these uses for the move, one of the most important uses for it involves using it to activate [[Launch Star]]s and [[Sling Star]]s, both of which shoot [[Mario]] through space to other planets and areas acting as portals for traversing between planets. In this regard, completing the game would not be possible without the presence of the Star Spin move. In addition, if Mario Star Spins in midair and immediately [[ground pound]]s, he will spin and then home in on nearby items, objects, or enemies. Additionally, if he is crouching and then spins, he will do a pirouette similar to a dance move.

[[File:SMG Dark Matter Plant Dark Star Spin.png|thumb|[[Bowser]] uses a spin on [[Mario]].]]
It also appears that after his first defeat in [[Bowser's Star Reactor]], [[Bowser]] took note of Mario's Star Spin, and made his own version during his fight in [[Bowser's Dark Matter Plant]] with the power of the [[Grand Star]] he stole. Mario can counter Bowser's aggressive Star Spin by performing one himself upon collision.

====''Super Mario Galaxy 2''====
[[Image:First Star Spin.png|thumb|right|Mario's first Star Spin in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.]]
The Star Spin ability returns in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' retaining it's properties from the first game. This time, Mario gains the ability after finding [[Luma (character)|Baby Luma]] during the game's introduction. The Baby Luma once again stows himself inside [[Mario's Cap]] granting him the ability. The Star Spin retains it's strengths from the previous game however some new ones have been added as well. Mario can spin near [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] to ride him without having to jump on him, and when riding Yoshi, the spin becomes a [[Head Shake]] which is effectively Yoshi's version of the Star Spin, it has the same powers as a Star Spin and can activate/destroy the very same things however, it doesn't help much when jumping. Star Spins are also used to reverse around the [[Flip-Swap Platform]]s as well as the [[Flip-Out Wall]]s.

Just as before, the Star Spin is also used during boss fights however slightly less bosses possess this weakness, the move is used to defeat the bosses [[Peewee Piranha]], [[Gobblegut]], [[Sorbetti]], [[Squizzard]], [[Prince Pikante]], [[Dino Piranha]], [[King Kaliente]], [[Major Burrows]], [[Bouldergeist]], and [[Fiery Dino Piranha]].

In addition to the returning power-ups utilized by spinning, it is also used to activate [[Cloud Mario]], [[Rock Mario]], and the [[Spin Drill]].

====''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''====
[[File:Rosalina with Cat Bullet Bills SM3DW screenshot.png|thumb|Rosalina using the spin near [[Cat Bullet Bill]]s]]
[[Image:Star Spin Rosalina.jpg|thumb|left|Rosalina preforms the Star Spin.]]
The Star Spin returns in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' as well as ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'', in these games, [[Rosalina]], the fifth playable character in the game, can also perform this move. Like in the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games, the move allows her to gain a bit of extra height when used in mid-air. It is possible to Star Spin multiple times in one jump though only the first Star Spin will give Rosalina the extra height. The delay between Star Spins has also been made shorter. Just like ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', Rosalina is able to stun or kill an enemy with the Star Spin. However unlike other characters, Rosalina is only able to perform the move whilst in her [[Super Mario|Super]] or [[Small Mario|Small]] forms, if she is under the effects of another Power-Up such as the [[Fire Flower]], [[Starman]], [[Super Bell]], or [[Super Leaf]]. Unlike ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', Rosalina's Star Spin cannot activate the [[Flip-Swap Platform]]s as they are now activated by simply [[Jump]]ing.

===''Mario & Sonic'' series===
In the [[4x100m Relay]] event featured across multiple ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' games, Mario's special boost is highly reminiscent of the spin from ''Super Mario Galaxy''. For instance, in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'', Mario spins while creating a translucent blue trail to gain a burst of speed. In the [[Wii U]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Wii U)|Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'', the spin additionally creates sparkles around the character, making the resemblance more prominent. Luigi and [[Amy]] also spin in their 4x100 Relay special boosts, but the trails they leave are green and magenta, respectively, isolating Mario's special boost as a reference to the ''Super Mario Galaxy'' move.

====''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''====
In the [[Dream Ski Jumping]] event of the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'', Mario's special action is a spin that surrounds him with a sparkly blur, similarly to the spin from ''Super Mario Galaxy''.

====''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games''====
In the [[Dream Spacewalk]] event of the Wii version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'', the characters can perform a spin, here referred to as a '''Spin Attack''', to deflect [[Dino Piranha]]'s fireballs.

==Similar attack methods==
In ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' and ''Super Mario 3D World'', the [[Tanooki Mario|Tanooki form]]'s tail whip acts like a Spin, but does not give any extra height in midair, has little delay between spins, and [[Roll|can be used continuously]] while [[crouch]]ing.

In ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'', spinning is done in the same vein as the [[Spin Jump]] from ''Super Mario 3D World'', rather than the Spin that is seen in the ''Super Mario Galaxy'' games. To make [[Captain Toad]] or [[Toadette]] spin around, the player has to quickly rotate the analog stick. While spinning, the characters become dizzy and immobile for a brief moment; however, if timed correctly, the move also enables them to defeat enemies that come into contact with them, serving as an alternative form of attack from stomping, throwing [[Turnip]]s, or using a [[Super Pickax]].

In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' the [[Cap Throw]] partially functions as a Star Spin. In it, Mario spins to throw [[Cappy]] forward. Damage is dealt entirely through Cappy, but the move affects Mario's trajectory in the same way the Spin does. While a Cap Throw can be used more than once per jump, Mario is not able to send out Cappy on further throws, and the spin's properties only apply to the first throw. The [[Cap Throw|Spin Throw]] move has Cappy whirl around Mario to attack, which has a wider radius than the Spin but again is tied to a projectile rather than Mario. If Mario jumps right as Cappy returns from a throw, he twirls while jumping and reaches slightly higher than a standard jump. [[Pokio]] can perform an action similar to a Spin when [[capture]]d, but they can use their beaks with the spin to create an attack. [[Gushen]] can also perform an action similar to a Spin when captured, which shoots out water in all directions as an attack.

==Trivia==
*In the [[Super Mario Galaxy/Beta Elements|beta stages]] of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', especially during E3 2006, [[Mario]] could spin an unlimited number of times in a row, in the game's final build, a small delay is enforced after a Star Spin is preformed. This however doesn't apply whenever [[Mario]] is underwater as he can do his unlimited spins there, in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', this was fixed and his Star Spin delays even apply underwater as well.
*If [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] is to preform a Star Spin near an NPC (such as a [[Toad (species)|Toad]], [[Luma (species)|Luma]], etc.), they will also spin with the plumbers.
**Interestingly the non-playable Luigi (on the [[Comet Observatory]] or [[Starship Mario]]) will always spin whenever Mario/Luigi do even if the plumbers aren't close to him.
