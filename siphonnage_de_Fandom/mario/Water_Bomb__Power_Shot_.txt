The '''Water Bomb''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] of [[Koopa Troopa]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Koopa Troopa absorbs the tennis racket with water particles and hits the tennis ball, covering it in water. The resulting shot is a similar to a 0° drop-shot, making it more difficult to return compared to a normal drop shot. A player that returns the ball without using a [[Defensive Power Shot]] has their movement speed decreased temporarily.
