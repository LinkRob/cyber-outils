'''Grodus Xs''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Summary==
These are the creation of Grodus and his minions. Every once in a while, [[Sir Grodus]] will summon four of these. While four or more exist in battle, Grodus becomes invincible, thus impossible to defeat, but only when these vile creations are in play. These can be one of the most annoying enemies for [[Mario]]. These creatures seem to be much more enhanced versions of other X-Naut creations like the [[Mini-Z-Yux]] and [[Mini-Yux]].
