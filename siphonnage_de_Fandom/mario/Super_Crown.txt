The '''Super Crown'''<ref name="NSMBUD website">Nintendo. [https://newsupermariobrosu.nintendo.com/characters/ New Super Mario Bros.™ U Deluxe for the Nintendo Switch™ home gaming system — Characters] ''YouTube''. 2018. Retrieved December 28, 2018.</ref> is a power-up exclusively found in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. It is a golden crown with a pink [[Mushroom]] as part of its design.

== Description ==
[[Toadette]] is the only playable character who can use this item. In this case, Toadette becomes [[Peachette]], a form strongly resembling [[Princess Peach]] and she will have similar abilities. Peachette has abilities complementary to [[Flying Squirrel Mario]]. She can [[Floating Jump|float]] or perform a [[Double Jump|midair jump]]. Uniquely, Peachette can recover from a [[Pit|bottomless pit]] with the midair jump without the player doing it. However, she cannot cling to the wall.

== In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''==
The Super Crown debuted in ''Deluxe'' as a power-up. This item is exclusively used by Toadette, as anyone other than her may collect a 10 coin bonus. In most levels, the Super Crown replaces the [[Super Acorn]].

==In ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''==
The Super Crown appears in the '''Version 1.20''' update.<ref name="Ninsoup">RRG. [https://nintendosoup.com/captain-toad-treasure-tracker-special-episode-is-a-prequel-to-new-super-mario-bros-u-deluxe/ Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker Special Episode Is A Prequel To New Super Mario Bros U Deluxe] (March 16, 2019). Retrieved March 16, 2019.</ref> Before the end of the game, Toadette appears next to the Super Crown. During the staff credits, the Super Crown is seen hanging onto the mine cart.

==In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''==
The Super Crown reappears as her icon for her Cup in ''Mario Kart Tour'', as on her head.

== Trivia ==
* In the game's description, it apologies to [[Luigi]] that he cannot use the Super Crown.<ref name="NSMBUD website" />
* If Toadette uses the Super Crown and meets [[Princess Peach]] who is behind the window, the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] princess will look confused and will say ''"What?"'', as she believes she is seeing herself.<ref name="NSMBUD Peachette Peach">BeardBear. (January 9, 2019) [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cBYIqzpFaFg What Happens When Peachette Saves Peach? - New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe] ''YouTube''. Retrieved January 11, 2019.</ref>
**At the end of the game, Toadette comes to Peach into her normal form despite defeating [[Bowser]] as Peachette.

* Bowser is shown to dislike the Super Crown in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', as he believes the "Peach Crown" is rather crazy for a crown or [[Mushroom]], and responds that it makes him want to breathe his Flame Breath.
