'''Yoshi Park 2''' is a course which appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''. It is one of two courses in the [[Yoshi Cup]], the other being [[Yoshi Park 1]]. One of its features is a haunted house with [[ghost]]s from ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''.
