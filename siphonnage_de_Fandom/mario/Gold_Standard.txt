}}The '''Gold Standard''' ('''Gold Kart''' in PAL releases) is an unlockable [[kart]] body part in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. It looks identical to the Standard kart except that it has a gold color scheme. The Gold Standard can be unlocked by getting 20,000 coins, or by getting 10,000 VR in [[Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection|Wi-Fi]] races. It has great [[speed]] (the second to fastest kart in the game) and good [[weight]], but lacks good [[handling]], and not very well [[Off-Road|off-road]]. The Gold Standard goes with [[Gold Tires]] and [[Gold Glider]].

== In {{series|Mario Kart}} ==
=== ''Mario Kart 7''/''Mario Kart 8'' ===
[[File:GoldStandard3DS.png|left|thumb|180x180px|The Gold Standard's appearance in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.]]
The Gold Standard returns in ''Mario Kart 8'' and ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' as an unlockable kart body part. Unlike its appearance in Mario Kart 7, the Gold Standard has the 8 emblem on the front of the kart. It now shares its stats with the Sneeker and Master Cycle. The Gold Standard reappears in Mario Kart Tour as an exclusive kart to Coin Rush mode.

=== ''Mario Kart Tour'' ===
The Gold Standard is exclusive to [[Gold Mario]] in [[Coin Rush]].
