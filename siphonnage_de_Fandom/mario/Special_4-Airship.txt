'''{{world|s4|airship}}''' is the [[Airship]] level of [[Special 4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|Special 4]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. 
==Layout==
The player starts out an a small [[Airship]] before jumping onto another Airship that a [[Warp Pipe]] that warps them to a room with a [[Hammer Bro]] and two [[Boomerang Bro]]s. After defeating them, a Warp Pipe spawns, leading to another Airship that has a Hammer Bro and a [[Blokkablok]] on it.

After this Airship is another Airship that has two Boomerang Bros., a Blokkablok, the [[Checkpoint Flag]] and a Warp Pipe on it. The Warp Pipe leads to the [[Boom Boom]] battle, which takes place on two colored sections that have [[Spike Trap|spike]]s come out of them. After defeating Boom Boom, a Warp Pipe appears that leads to an area that has three small platforms and several [[Donut Block]]s on them. One of the small platforms contains a [[+ Clock]] and after collecting it, [[Pom Pom]] appears. After defeating Pom Pom another Warp Pipe appears that leads to the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Medals==
*'''Star Medal 1:''' The player must use an [[! Block]] to spawn several [[Face block (platform)|face block]]s that lead to the Star Medal.
*'''Star Medal 2:''' The player must again use another ! Block to spawn face blocks that lead to the Star Medal.
*'''Star Medal 3:''' Just before the [[Checkpoint Flag]] is another ! Block that leads to the Star Medal.
