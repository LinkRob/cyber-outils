|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Scream''' is a move used mainly by undead enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Scream is used, a sonic wave appears on the target followed by a screeching sound.

==Effects==
With Scream, the user is able to cause the [[Fear]] ailment to one of Mario's party cutting their attack and defense in half.
