'''Honeycombs''' (also referred to as '''Honey Walls''' or '''Honeycomb Walls''') are objects in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Description==
Honeycombs are hexagonal platforms coated in [[honey]]. They are attached to the sides of walls, and can be climbed only by [[Bee Mario]], who must fly to reach different parts of the walls. Honey walls have their own [[gravity]], since Bee Mario can [[fly]] with his feet facing the walls and even [[Ground Pound (move)|ground-pound]] onto them. 

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[File:Honey walls in SMG.png|thumb|left|200px|Bee Mario climbing Honeycombs in ''Super Mario Galaxy'']]
Honeycombs appear in four [[galaxy|galaxies]]: the [[Honeyhive Galaxy]], [[Honeyclimb Galaxy]], [[Gold Leaf Galaxy]], and [[Sand Spiral Galaxy]], the latter having only a single Honeycomb panel.

The Honeyclimb Galaxy has several Honeycombs on what appears to be a flat hive. The only [[mission]] of the galaxy, [[Scaling the Sticky Wall]], has an object where Mario has to climb up the planet using Honeycombs along the way. The second wall of the second planet features [[meteor]]s that fall by, and if Mario is hit, he falls off and into a [[Black Hole]]. Several [[Mandibug]]s appear around the Honeycombs on the third planet.

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
Honeycombs are a lot less common, since they appear only in [[Honeyhop Galaxy]].
