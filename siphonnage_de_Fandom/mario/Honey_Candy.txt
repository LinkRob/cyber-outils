'''Honey Candy''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Honey_Candy_PM.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Honey Candy is made by [[Tayce T.]] after Mario has given her the [[Cookbook]], and [[Mario]] gives her a [[Honey Syrup]] and [[Cake Mix]]. Mario can use this item anytime to restore twenty [[Flower Point|FP]].

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Honey_Candy_PMTTYD.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Honey Candy is made by [[Zess T.]] if given the same recipe as in ''Paper Mario''. The Honey Candy retains the same effects as well.

Mario can use this item in a [[Trouble Center|trouble]] in which Mario must give [[Gob]] a Honey Candy because he cannot speak and a Honey Candy is the only thing that can help heal his voice.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Honey Candy is made by [[Saffron]], who will make this dessert by using a [[Honey Jar]]. Mario can use this item to restore twelve [[Heart Point|HP]] and cure [[Poison (status effect)|poison]].
