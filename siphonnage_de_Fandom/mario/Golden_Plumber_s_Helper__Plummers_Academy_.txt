The '''Golden Plumber's Helper''' is an item in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Plummers Academy]]."

==Description==
It is a plunger with a brown handle and a gold suction cup at the end. The Golden Plumber's Helper has the same name as a different [[Golden Plumber's Helper (King Mario of Cramalot)|plunger]] from "[[King Mario of Cramalot]]."
