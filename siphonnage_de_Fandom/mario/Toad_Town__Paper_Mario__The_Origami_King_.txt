'''Toad Town''' is a location in [[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]. It contains the homes of most of the Toads in The Origami King, and contains Peach's Castle. Toad Town is first seen from the beginning scene. After Mario escapes Peach's Castle, the castle is plucked out of the ground. Many paper macho soldiers have destroyed parts of Toad Town. 

After Mario exits from the [[Whispering Woods]], he enters the path to Toad Town. The 1st [[Not-Bottomless Holes|Not-bottomless hole]] is next to a tree above the path. Farther down the path are 4 [[Origami Goomba|origami goombas]] who have captured 10 [[Goomba|goombas]]. All four of them attack you in a 2 wave battle. Here [[Olivia]] teaches you how to slide panels in battles, called [[sliding]]. Upon defeating the origami goombas, the 10 goombas are rescued. The goombas run off towards the direction of Toad Town. Continuing along the path, you come across the 2nd Not-bottomless hole. Toad Town comes into view and you can see 3 paper macho goombas marching around. Further along is the 3rd Not-bottomless hole, at the top of a series of stairs. Above it is the 1st question block. Inside it is a 100-coin. As Mario continues toward Toad Town you can see the 4th Not-bottomless hole. 

Mario comes across the entrance to Toad Town, where there is the 5th Not-bottomless hole in the upper left corner. To the right of the entrance is the 2nd question block, which contains a bag of confetti. Mario and Olivia come across their 1st paper macho goomba, which they have to fight. The 5th Not-bottomless hole is on the house that the paper macho goomba was chewing on. This house has a yellow roof and is in the main entrance. Inside of the house is a letter that says, "Toad Town is in ruins. Papier-mâché monstrosities rule the streets. For now, I flee. Long live the princess!" Inside of the dresser is a 100-Coin. In the upper right of the main entrance is a Save block and a sign that says, "← Peach's Castle      Toad Town Port →"

Behind the Save block is a hidden area that has a tunnel that leads to under a road. In the hidden area is a chest that contains the No.4 Collectible Treasure Torch from Peach's Castle. To the right of the Save box is the [[Battle Lab]], which is shaped like a mushroom with hammers along the head.  There is a sign that says "Battle Lab Training Tough Toads Tirelessly." The house is locked. Below the house is the 6th Not-bottomless hole. Filling in the hole reveals a bench. To the right of the hole is the [[Musée Champignon]], which is the place where you can view your collectibles, enemies defeated, and it also has a pipe network to explored worlds. To the right of Musée Champignon is an orange rusty pipe. The other side of the pipe is blocked.

The next building on the right is the [[Weapon and Item Shop]]. In front of it is a sign that says "Weapon and Item Shop." The door is locked until you find the [[Toad]] who owns it. To the right is a flight of stairs up onto a raised area. There is another path to the right that leads to the 3rd question block. Inside it is a 100-coin. 

As Mario goes up the flight of stairs, he encounters the 2nd paper macho goomba. To the left of the plaza in which the paper macho goomba stood in, is a raised porch on the roof of the Weapon and Item Shop. On the porch is a pipe that is blocked. It leads to the pipe next to the 6th question block, on the far left part of the Town. On the the left side are two crates, they both contain a coin each. On the far right of the plaza is a blue gate. It leads down a flight of stairs to the docks. There is a sign that says, "The Dock, You're on it." The brown gate that leads to the boats is locked, and has a note on it that says, "Sorry I missed you! I'll be at [[Autumn Mountain]] until the winds change. Sea Captain Toad." 

At the top of the plaza is a red brick warehouse, in which the 6th Not-bottomless hole is found on. Once you fill the hole, you can enter the warehouse. Inside the warehouse, on the upper left is a series of 3 100-coins of a shelf. You can reach them by hammering the 4th question block on the patch of dirt on the floor. This one will remain invisible until you hit it. Inside the block is a coin. On the far right of the warehouse is another shelf. In front of the shelf is a box with a [[Spike]] in it. On the upper left of the shelf is a crate that contains confetti. 

Continuing on from the road from the plaza is the 7th Not-bottomless hole. It is next to the fountain. Behind the warehouse is a secret path that a dock out into the ocean. At the far end is a barrel with a lid on top. If you hit the lid without being on top, a  [[Monty Mole]] will pop out. Latter on in the game, he will sell collectables of the [[Legion of Stationery]]. After Mario defeats the Colored Pencils, the No.7 Colored Pencils Collectible Treasure will appear in his shop for 698 coins.

Past the warehouse is the Tram Station. This area will let you travel to Overlook Mountain, and its area's [[blue streamer]]. On the tram's waiting porch is a journal that says, "Tram hasn't arrived. Snacks are running low. Morale even lower. Send help. And snacks." Next to the station is a sign that says, "Tram to Overlook Mountain." To the left of the sign is a Save box. To the left of that is a broken bridge leading to the other side of Peach's castle. Next to the fountain is the 3rd paper macho goomba. To the left of the broken bride is a abandoned café stand. A blue [[Snifit]] has taken over the stand, and will sell you a [[Rabbit Espresso]], or [[Turtle Tea]]. The first one is free, but afterwards the Rabbit Espresso is 50 coins, and the Turtle Tea is 20 coins.

There are 3 tables in front of the café stand, the far right one has an umbrella, along with the upper left. If you hit the upper left umbrella with your hammer, a [[Magic Circle|magic circle]] will fall out, which you can use to pull off a section of the nearby white house. Mario can then climb onto the café shop, using a staircase of crates, and hop along the opened umbrella, and the pulled down wall. This will bring Mario onto the porch of the building. On the right is a stair case taped with King Olly's Tape. Upon pulling this off, the stair case unfolds, and provides easy access to the second floor. Farther left is a door that leads to the 2nd floor. On the far left is a staircase leading to the roof, where there is the 4th Paper macho goomba. Inside of the paper macho goomba is a yellow Snifit. The Snifit gives Mario the No.6 Collectible Treasure Coins. After rescuing the Snifit, you can buy confetti from him for 300 coins.

Following the path to the left of the café, is the 8th Not-bottomless hole. It is in front of the white house. Going inside the house, you can find the 9th Not-bottomless hole. In the far right corner are two dressers. The left one contains a origami goomba, while the right one contains a handful of confetti. On the 2nd floor is a gap in the, in which you must enter through the door on the porch to get across. Above the gap is a coin. However, the other door is locked until you can find the Toad to open it. Underneath the porch is a tunnel that leads to the 10th Not-bottomless hole. Behind the hole is a red Snifit. To the left of the white house is another white brick house with a red roof. Inside is the 5th question block, above a dresser. You must hit the dresser with the hammer in order to climb up. Inside is a 1000-coin bag. To the left of the red roofed house is a pipe that leads to another pipe at the front of Toad Town. 

The road leads to Peach's Castle, but the bridge is broken and is therefore impassible. As you go near it, Olivia tells you to jump in an underground tube, travel through it somehow, and pop out on the other side. To the left of the castle, is a wooden house. The door to the inside has a red [[Shy Guy]] stuck in it. the Shy Guy tells you take the sewer to get to Peach's Castle. inside the house and to the left is a sign that says, "Graffiti Underground ♦Toad Town Entrance♦" Technically, it's a sewer. In front sign is a sewer lid that leads to the [[Graffiti Underground]]. However, the lid is locked, and the Shy Guy tells you to find a special manhole hook. 

Farther down the path is the 11th Not-bottomless hole. To the right of it is a house. As you approach the house, the 5th Paper macho goomba bursts out of a shed. Inside of the paper macho goomba is a heart that restores 20 HP. In front of the house is a shed with the 12th Not-bottomless hole in it. Inside of the house is a journal that says, "The Origami Festival is almost here! I'm so excited, I'm writing notes to myself. In the upper right corner is a closet with the 10 goombas inside. They give you a heart that restores 20 HP.

To the left of the 11th Not-bottomless hole,  is a house with the 13th Not-bottomless hole on it. Inside the house is another door that leads to the house's lawn. however, the door is won't open because of the 6th Paper macho goomba that stands in front of it. However, there is a window that can be reached by hammering the dresser. A heart that restores 20 HP also pops out. Mario has to hammer the window, and then he can jump out and fight the Paper macho goomba. After defeating the Paper macho goomba, a red Shy Guy falls out. He tells you to go the the red brick building at the port. After defeating the all of the paper macho goombas, Olivia reports that it feels calmer, and you can use the bench at the entrance. 

Following down the road and down the staircase is a save box, and a pipe that leads to the front of Peach's Castle bridge. To the left of the save box is a route that leads to the 6th question block. Inside it is a large bag of confetti. To the upper right is a covered pipe that leads to the pipe above the 

To the right of the save block is a house that has a sign in front of it, which reads. "Fun, Funky, and Functional Accessory Shop." The door is locked until you find the Toad. To the right of the Accessory Shop, is the 7th question block, which is in between the Accessory Shop, and the next house. Inside it is a 100-coin, While there is nothing of importance in the next house, there is a back door that leads to a chest that is behind the Accessory Shop. Inside the chest is the No.5 Collectible Treasure Camp Speaker.

Once you talk to the red Shy guys, you can return to the red warehouse, where the Spike encourages you to hammer the left shelf boxes. As you continue to hit, a box pops out, and drops a [[Magic Circle]]. You can use it to pull of a brick wall and reveal a door. Inside the next room are two origami Goombas. In the bottom left is a crate that contains a 10-coin. Above it are another two crates. The left one contains 3 10-coins, while the right one contains a handful of confetti. Behind these crates is a rolling step ladder that can be moved with you hammer. Mario can use it to get onto the shelfs. To the left is a crate that contains a mushroom, confetti, and the [[Manhole Hook]]. To the right is the 8th question block, that contains a 100-coin. Below it is a 10-coin. Mario can now use the Manhole Hook to open the manhole to Graffiti Underground. 

Once you enter back into Toad Town from the Graffiti Underground, you enter Peach's Castle. To the right is a gate blocked by Luigi's kart, while to the left is the 9th and last question block. Straight onwards is the ruins of Peach's Castle, along with Luigi's hat on top of a rock. To the left of the hat is a cracked wall that can be broken. There is another broken wall on the ground that has a handful of confetti inside. All cracked walls contain confetti. In the bottom left are two 100-coins. To the upper left is the only intact part of Peach's Castle, where there are three jars. The first two on the floor contain confetti, while the one on the ledge can be opened by climbing up the castle's walls. When it is hit, a Magic Circle falls out, that can be used to peel of the paint and save Luigi.

You can use the blocked gate after Luigi moves his kart. Following it, it leads to a save block, and the outer wall of Peach's Castle. Outside of the walls is a Toad trapped inside of a tower. You must hammer the tower until an Origami Goomba falls out. After its defeat, it drops a Magic Circle, which can be used to rescue the blue Toad, who is the owner of the weapons shop. He is the only Toad in Toad Town. However, he still needs your help to unravel the bridge, by pulling off the tape. He gives you a free mushroom. To the right, the path leads to [[Picnic Road]].

Upon returning to the Weapon and Item Shop, the blue Toad shop keeper will unlock the door and allow access to the shop. 

The next time Mario enters Toad Town after rescuing the Toad who runs the Battle Lab, Olivia will want to visit the Battle Lab to get the free gift that was mentioned. The Toad is standing near the Battle Lab and will unlock it when Mario arrives. Inside the Battle Lab, there is a staircase that leads down to the basement. Here, there are multiple training exercises that mimic the attacks and blocking that Mario might have to do in battle.  The 1st machine is the boots training machine. This lets Mario practice jumping on a row of 4 enemies. The training repeats until Mario exits.  The 2nd machine is a hammer training machine, and lets Mario practice swinging his hammer. The 3rd machine is a guard training exercise that mimics Mario blocking from a Origami Goomba. The free gift is a [[1-Up Mushroom ( Paper Mario the Origami King)|1-Up Mushroom]].  

There is also a [[Bossotronic Animated Machine Fighter]], that lets Mario refight boss fights. However, Mario will not get a game over if he loses. There is aw prize if Mario defeats all of the bosses a second time. 

In the upper right corner, there is the ring training machine. There is a basic training, The Ringer, with 10 levels per round, or a Speed Rings mode. This is where Mario has 100 seconds to complete as many lineups as he can. During both trainings, Mario must match the same colored Toads in order to complete the level. If Mario uses up all of his ring moves and gets an incorrect answer then it is a game over for that game.

When Mario approaches the last house to the left on the front horizontal road, then a Toad will unlock his Accessory Shop. This is if Mario has rescued the accessory Toad from the front of the [[Earth Vellumental Temple|Earth Vellumental's Temple]]. 

Inside the shop is the 14th and last Not-Bottomless Hole. The Toad will not sell his wares until the Not-Bottomless Hole is filled in. He sells the [[Confetti Vacuum]] for 2000 coins.
