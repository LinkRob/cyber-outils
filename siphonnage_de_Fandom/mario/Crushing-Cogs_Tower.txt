}}'''Crushing-Cogs Tower, '''also known as '''Acorn Plains-[[File:Fort Icon.png|18px|link=]]''', is the tower level of Acorn Plains and the first tower level to appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. The stage contains various moving stone cogs/gears as obstacles. This is also the first stage to introduce the [[Ice Flower]] and the enemy [[Dry Bones]]. Once the player completes this stage, Miiverse functionality will be unlocked.

==Features==
This tower level consists of several rotating stone cogs. They are placed in such a way to crush the player if they fail to advance. The enemies mainly consist of Dry Bones. The moving cogs can be used as lifts as well as obstacles, so they player must be weary of their placement and movement direction.

This level also contains the first boss of the game, [[Boom Boom]]. The boss battle is relatively straightforward without extra obstacles or dangers.

Steer your landing by using a spin and land on the small platform with the second Star Coin.
