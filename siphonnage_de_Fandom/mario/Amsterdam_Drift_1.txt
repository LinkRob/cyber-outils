'''Amsterdam Drift 1''' is a course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. As its name implies, it is the first of [[Amsterdam Drift]]'s layouts.

== Layout ==
The focus of this layout is the countryside, in the northeastern (northwestern in ''Mario Kart 8'') part of the track.

Racers start on a road that represents Damrak Street, between the Royal Palace of Amsterdam and a building known as "The Beehive" (De Bijenkorf). After they start, the racers take a right turn, followed by a left turn. This leads into a [[Glider]] segment that the racers use to make their way to a rural region of the track. Some windmills appear here, and their blades can block the racers. The racers then make their way to a dirt road where [[Monty Mole]]s accost them. Houses and windmills can be found here. The course leads to a [[Dash Panel]] that the racers use to make their way back to the mainland. A ferry can be seen here. They then head down a road that has a tram that they can hit for a [[Trick|Jump Boost]]. The road makes a left turn to the [[Finish Line]].

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' for the Switch, the racers head down this route for the first lap of [[Amsterdam Drift]] in the [[Booster Course Pass]], but the tram now causes damage rather than [[Trick|Jump Boost]]s.

== Trivia ==
*This is the only [[Amsterdam Drift]] route that has [[Glider|gliding]] instead of [[Propeller (Mario Kart 7)|underwater driving]]. However, in the Do Jump Boosts Challenge, players can go underwater in some parts of the course.
