'''''Rhythm Heaven Megamix''''' is a [[Nintendo 3DS]] rhythm game that is the fourth installment of the ''[[w:c:nintendo:Rhythm (series)|Rhythm]]'' series. It was first released in 2015 in Japan and some other parts of Asia. In 2016, the game was released in North America, although only as a digital download. Later that year, it was released in Europe, Australia, and South Korea, both in cartridge and digital medium.

While ''Rhythm Heaven Megamix'' does not feature any connections to the main [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], the main characters of the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]] all make guest appearances in the dedicated Wario... Where? challenge courses.
