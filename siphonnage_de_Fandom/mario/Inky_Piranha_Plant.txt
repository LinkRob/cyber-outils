'''Inky Piranha Plants''' are [[Piranha Plant]] variants which made their debut in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. In their appearances, Inky Piranha Plants use an an attack in which they spit ink on the game screen, much like the [[Blooper]] item from the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' games.

==In ''Super Mario 3D Land''==
Inky Piranha Plants attack by spitting ink that temporarily obstructs the screen. If the player blows into the microphone, the ink will wash off. They can be defeated in the same way as regular Piranha Plants: by either [[jump]]ing on it, having [[Fire Mario]] shoot a [[fireball]] at it, hitting it with [[Tanooki Mario]]'s tail, or by touching one while under the effects of [[Starman]].

==In ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''==
Inky Piranha Plants are featured in the [[Ink Showdown]] minigame, where they shoot tennis balls and also sometimes ink blobs that can obstruct the screen.

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''==
Inky Piranha Plants are background enemies of the [[Dream World]]. They mainly blind the screen, making it harder for Mario to avoid attacks from other enemies. Mario can defeat them using a [[Taunt Ball]].
