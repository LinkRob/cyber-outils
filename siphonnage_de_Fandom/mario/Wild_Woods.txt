'''Wild Woods''' is a track found in the Animal Crossing x Mario Kart 8 DLC Pack for ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', it is already available as a track like the other ''Mario Kart 8'' DLC tracks.

== Layout ==
The track starts out with the karts sideways, going up a tree. After a turn, you'll go through a fork. The right fork has jumps on it, while the left fork is simpler. Soon afterwards, you'll go through a town populated by Shy Guys (The houses also look sort of like Shy Guys too).

After this you'll enter a log, that's got a path of water you ride down on. The water will pour into a frog pond, with lily pads that have jumps on them. You'll re-enter onto the anti-gravity section taking you back to the finish line.
