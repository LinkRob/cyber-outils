'''Haru-ōji''' is the prince of an unseen land known as Flower-koku. He exclusively appears in the anime movie ''[[Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]''.

==Trivia==
*Haru's Kibidango form strikingly resembles a [[Wiggler]], which would debut in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.
*Haru-ōji shares some similarties to [[Prince Florian]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
**Both of them are rules of kingdoms that has "Flower" in the name. (Flower-koku for Haru and [[Flower Kingdom]] for Florian)
**Prince Florian's design resembles Haru's Kibidango form.
