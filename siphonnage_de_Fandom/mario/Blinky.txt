'''Blinky''' (Japanese: アカベイ ''Akabei'') is a red ghost from the ''PAC-MAN'' series. He has appeared in a few ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' games, including ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''. He makes a cameo appearance in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. Blinky debuted in the same game as [[PAC-MAN]], titled ''{{w|Pac-Man|PAC-MAN}}''.

==Biography==
Blinky is the archenemy of [[PAC-MAN]] and [[Ms. PAC-MAN|his wife]] and the leader of the other ghosts Inky, Pinky, and Clyde, all of whom appear as an [[Assist Trophy]] in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series since ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''. Blinky's special items are Ghost Shield, Monster's Hammer, Petit Monster, Flare Ball and Ghost Morph.
