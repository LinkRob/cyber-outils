A '''Kannonball''' is an object that appears in the [[Donkey Kong (series)|''Donkey Kong'' series]].
==Appearances==
===''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''===
A single, large Kannonball can be found in [[Gloomy Galleon]] of this game. In a small room in the beginning of the stage, a Kannonball can be used with a [[Kannon (cannon)|Kannon]] by [[Chunky Kong]] to fire at targets. He must hit all of the targets within the time limit to earn a [[Golden Banana]].
