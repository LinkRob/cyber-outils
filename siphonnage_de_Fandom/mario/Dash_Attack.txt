The '''Dash Attack''', also named '''Barge''', is one of the main moves used by [[Wario]] in the ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' platforming titles. Its main purpose is to attack enemies, although Wario can also use Dash Attack to either move faster, break fragile objects, or open [[treasure chest]]s. Wario first uses Dash Attack in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.
