'''Nemo''' is a purple shrimp-like enemy found in ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]'' exclusively found in the level [[Nautilus Chase]] in [[Kremlantis]].

== Profile ==
Unlike [[Croctopus|Croctopi]], Nemos follow the Kongs throughout the entire level instead of just a specific pathway. They appear to be sleeping at first, but when [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] pass by, they start chasing them throughout the level. They are quick throughout the level, meaning that the Kongs have to quickly move throughout the level or they get attacked by Nemo's tentacles. They, at some point, quit chasing the Kongs and hide behind the coral.
