'''Eddy the Mask''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a [[Bandit]] who lives in [[Rogueport Underground]].

==History==
Eddy the Mask doesn't play a significant role in the game, but he is knowledgeable about [[Stylish Move]]s. By speaking with Eddy, he will tell how to execute each stylish moves for [[Mario]] and his partners. [[Goombella]] also mentions that Eddy is quite the "health nut" (or "health fanatic in the remake) which may explain why he is interested in Stylish Moves.
