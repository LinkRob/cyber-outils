==History==
The Owl can be found near the big tree in [[Fungi Forest]]. Being that he's is an owl, the Owl can only be encountered at night. He will not talk to anything that doesn't fly forcing only [[Diddy Kong]] to talk with him while using his [[Rocketbarrel Boost]]. The Owl will then task Diddy to follow him around while flying through his rings. If Diddy completes the Owl's game, the Owl will reward him with a [[Bonus Barrel]] disappointed that his game wasn't as tough as he thought it was.

==Trivia==
*The Owl's game is very similar to [[Necky]]'s from [[Angry Aztec]] as they both involve flying through rings using Diddy.
*If Diddy loses to the Owl, the Owl will say that he flies like a "dodo" which is a type of flightless bird.
