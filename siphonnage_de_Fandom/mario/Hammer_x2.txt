|name=Hammer x2 Battle Card}}'''Hammer x2''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Hammer x2 is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s "Hammer x2" attack. It is two of the [[Hammer (Battle Card)|Hammer]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied by an orange/white background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 16 coins (unpainted)

Sell Price: 8 coins (unpainted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Hammer x2 is first seen in [[The Crimson Tower]], but can also found in [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Kiwano Temple]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Plum Park]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fort Cobalt]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Mustard Café]], [[Sunset Express]], [[Redpepper Volcano]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
