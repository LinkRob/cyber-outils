'''Mini Bob-ombs''' are an enemy in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mini Bob-ombs are a small [[Mini]] variation of a [[Bob-omb]], and they have a yellow wind-up key on their back.

==In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''==
Mini Bob-ombs are only encountered in the minigame [[Shy Guy Smash!]], in which the player must avoid tapping them, otherwise they lose five [[point]]s. Mini Bob-ombs also appear in the staff credits.
