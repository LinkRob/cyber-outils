'''Gold Big Boos''' are a variant of both [[Big Boo]]s and [[Gold Boo]]s which appear as an enemy in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Gold Big Boo NSMB2 screenshot.png|thumb|left|A Gold Big Boo leaving behind a trail of coins]]
Aside from having a gold body, Gold Big Boos essentially look the same as regular Gold Boos.

===Behavior===
All Big Boos turn into Gold Big Boos whenever a [[Gold Ring]] has been activated. A Gold Big Boo acts the same as a Gold Boo, but leaves a much larger trail of [[coin]]s behind while floating around due to its larger size.
