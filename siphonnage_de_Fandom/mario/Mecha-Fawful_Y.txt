'''Mecha-Fawful Ys''' are enemies that appear exclusively in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign or the ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' [[Reissue|remake]].

==Physical Description==
Mecha-Fawful Ys are a robotic version of [[Fawful]]. They have a rocket that keeps them propelled in the air.

==Battle==
Mecha-Fawful Ys are a Flying troop, and use [[Cranium Crush]] as their [[Special Skill]]. They attack by ramming into their foes. They only appear in the level [[Believe in Bowser]].

==Flavor text==

*'''In-game description:''' "Mass-produced robots that serve Fawful. Those heads are known to fly about."
