'''Lemons''' are an item which appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The only known location of Lemons is a tree found in the oasis of [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]]. A Lemon replenishes one [[Heart Point|HP]] and two [[Flower Point|FP]] if eaten by [[Mario]]. Lemons are also used in cooking to make a [[Tasty Tonic]]. Mixing a Lemon and [[Cake Mix]] will create the [[Lemon Candy]]. The only way to get rid of the [[Anti Guy]] in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] without defeating him is to give him a Lemon Candy.

Even though the Lemon is used in cooking and for warding off the Anti Guy, those things are only optional. The mandatory purpose of the Lemon is to give to a green [[Nomadimouse]]. Then, the mouse will tell Mario to buy a [[Dried Shroom]] and then a [[Dusty Hammer]] in that order. If Mario does so, he can speak with [[Moustafa]], who will give the [[Pulse Stone]] to him. Since the Pulse Stone is an important item, obtaining a Lemon is required.
