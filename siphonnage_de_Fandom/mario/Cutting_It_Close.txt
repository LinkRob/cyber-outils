'''Cutting It Close''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' as one of the [[WarioWatch]] microgames and later in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]'s microgames. Cutting It Close is included in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'' partially, in the second difficulty level of the [[WarioWare: Twisted! (microgame)|WarioWare: Twisted!]] microgame as well as in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'', but as the first difficulty level instead.

==Gameplay==
The player utilizes a razor and must rotate the system to have it shave the face of a man named [[Shades]]. The second and third difficulty levels each have more facial hair to shave than earlier difficulty levels.
