'''Disco Hammer''' is a ghost in [[Luigi's Mansion 3]] and a form of [[Hammer (enemy)|Hammer]], they have shiny, disco ball style bodies instead of red bodies. They only appear in the [[ScareScraper]]'s Dance Hall themed floor. They have 300 HP.

==Appearance==

Disco Hammers' bodies are styled like Disco balls which causes their body to shine, they have red eyes with red mouth. Unlike many ghosts but like normal Hammers, they have two tails and eyebrows. They are vacuumed in the same way as Hammers.

==In the game==

In order to find them, the green [[Luigi]] in the ScareScraper team must wear the DJ clothing, if the green Luigi doesn't wear the any outfits, it will depend on the second, third or the fourth player. But Disco Hammers are not necessarily has to be catched. They will hide in a random room (usually has a secret door), and players can capture them if they are found.

==Trivia==
*Disco Hammers only exist after the game update in 2020.

*Capturing them are not necessary.
