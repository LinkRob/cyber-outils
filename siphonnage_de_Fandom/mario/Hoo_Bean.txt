'''Hoo Beans''' are a type of [[Bean]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Hoo Beans are the rarest type of Bean, and are mainly acquired by jumping at invisible [[Question Block|? Block]]s. Hoo Beans are also among the rewards for clearing certain minigames, such as the [[Surfing minigame]].

Like the other Beans, the purpose of Hoo Beans is to brew certain drinks at the [[Starbeans Cafe]]. Hoo Beans in particular are used to make [[Hoohoo Blend]] or [[Hoolumbian]], the former having 25 Hoo Beans as its sole ingredient while the latter takes 10 Hoo Beans as well as 15 [[Woo Bean]]s.
