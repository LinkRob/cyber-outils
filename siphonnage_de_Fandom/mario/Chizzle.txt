A '''Chizzle''' is a rock enemy encountered in [[Dreamy Mount Pajamaja]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. A Chizzle typically wanders about in the field, but if the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] encroach upon a Chizzle's territory, it charges at them.

== Description ==
Chizzles have yellow eyes, with both their arms held up to appear like a man flexing. Their legs are stubby.

==Battle==
Chizzles are always found in a very large group, and has multiple attacks. One is the whole group of Chizzle running into the background and hurling a giant dumbbell-like weight at Mario and Luigi, similarly to the [[Grumbell]]. The hammer is used to counter this attack, and, if the Bros. hit the dumbell back with their hammers, the Chizzles will cower in fear while the rocky weight slams into the group, dealing damage to all of them.

Another attack is the group smashing through rocks and ramming at Mario.

The final attack is some of them jumping into four pits of lava above, beside, and below the target. The target must hit them back with his hammer into the pools of lava, which they use like a hot tub. If hit by this attack, it can burn the targeted Mario Bro., so be careful.
