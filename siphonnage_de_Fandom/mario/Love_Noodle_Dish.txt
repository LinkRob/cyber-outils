|damage = 0}}'''Love Noodle Dish '''is a dish cooked by [[Dyllis]] in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is a passionate pasta meal that awakens the romantic in whoever eats it. It briefly doubles attack power, reduces damage taken by half, and paralyzes any enemy [[Mario]] touches. The effect lasts 20 seconds. It is made by mixing a [[Lovely Chocolate]] with a [[Fresh Pasta Bunch]].
