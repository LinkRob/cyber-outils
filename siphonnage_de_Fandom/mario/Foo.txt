'''Foos''' are enemies that appear in the console ''New Super Mario Bros.'' games. They make fog so you won't be able to see where you're going in a certain stage. They act sort of like a [[Fwoosh]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. They are found in three levels in the entire series: 7-5 (''Wii''), [[Snaking Above Mist Valley]] (''U''), and [[Three-Headed Snake Block]] (''Luigi U''). Larger and stronger variants named [[Ty-Foo]]s appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. They can be taken out by jumping on top of them.
