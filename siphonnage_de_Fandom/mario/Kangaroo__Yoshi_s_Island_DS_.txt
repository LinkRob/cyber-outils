The '''Kangaroo''' is an ally of the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] who appears in certain levels of ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. It is used as a vehicle, even being classified as such in the instruction booklet, but is the only one to be a living creature. Kangaroos are only found in a specific area of a level.

The Kangaroo has a large pouch on the front that a Yoshi can climb into. The Kangaroo can bounce around very high, allowing a Yoshi to collect several [[coin]]s that are otherwise unreachable due to how high they are. The Kangaroo can also briefly float in midair if the player holds the {{button|ds|B}} button, similar to [[Princess Peach]]'s ability from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.
