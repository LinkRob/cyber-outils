'''Love Pudding''' is an item and a [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Love Pudding.png|frame|left]]
Love Puddings can be created by [[Zess T.]] by giving her a [[Mystic Egg]] and a [[Mango Delight]]. Using this time is rather risky as it can make the user [[invisible]] or [[electrified]], Love Puddings can also put the user to [[sleep]] as well.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
In this game, Love Puddings are now made by giving [[Dyllis]] a [[Big Egg]] and a [[Lovely Chocolate]]. Unlike the previous game where eating a Love Pudding may result in a useful status condition, eating a Love Pudding in this game causes the player's controls to be reverse. As such, using the item is not recommended, and its only benefit is to fill the recipes list.
