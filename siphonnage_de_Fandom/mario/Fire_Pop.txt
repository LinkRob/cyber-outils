The '''Fire Pop''' is an item and [[recipe]] in the early ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' installments.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:FirePopPM.png|thumb|left]]
[[File:StickOutTongueFrame3.png|alt=Mario sticking out his tongue|thumb|Mario's sprite after using the item in Paper Mario|border]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', [[Tayce T.]] will make this item for [[Mario]] by using a [[Fire Flower]] and a [[Cake Mix]]. Upon using it, Mario is damaged with one point of [[Heart Point|HP]], but restores twenty points of [[Flower Point|FP]].


===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', [[Zess T.]] will make this item for Mario by using a Cake Mix with a Fire Flower like in ''Paper Mario'' or by using a [[Hot Sauce]]. Fire Pop in this game keeps all of its effects from ''Paper Mario''.
