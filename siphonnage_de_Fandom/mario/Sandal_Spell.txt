The '''Sandal Spell''' (also called '''Flip-flop Spell''') is an ability used by [[Kamek]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.
==Description==
Kamek uses his wand to turn all of Mario's Stickers into Sandals (even though they're called flip-flops in the text) making it hard to tell which Sticker is which. After Kamek is defeated, Mario's Stickers will return to normal.
===Users===
*[[Kamek]]: He is the only known user of this ability. He only uses it during two battles (the second and the final) to give him the edge over Mario. Mario can counter this ability by sorting out his Stickers to remember which is which.{{PMSSNav}}
