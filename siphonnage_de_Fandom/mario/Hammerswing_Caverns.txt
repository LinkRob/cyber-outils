'''Hammerswing Caverns''' is the fourth level of [[Superstar Road]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked when the player collects every [[Star Coin]] in [[Frosted Glacier]]. 

==Layout==
The course is based on the course Icicle Caverns from Frosted Glacier. The level begins outside, with a [[Warp Pipe]] leading to the icy underground area. To the left of the Warp Pipe is a hidden area with a [[? Block]] that contains a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. Two [[Huge Icicle]]s fall onto platforms, with a ? Block found above a platform in between. Two [[Swinging Hammer]]s are found, and one can be used to reach a ? Block above an icy platform. Another Huge Icicle is found next to a small one, along with a group of [[Koopa Troopa]]s. More Swinging Hammers are found, and are used to cross above a pit. Another Huge Icicle falls onto a platform, and a [[Buzzy Beetle]] can be found nearby. A group of [[icicle]]s is found above a pit crossed by two Swinging Hammers. A Buzzy Beetle is found underneath [[Brick Block]]s, followed by a Swinging Hammer on a track. Four icicles and a ? Block are found as the Swinging Hammer moves. A slope with multiple Buzzy Beetles and icicles on it is encountered, followed by a two Huge Icicles. A [[Green Ring]] is found before another Swinging Hammer on a track. Two Swinging Hammers are found before another one on a track, and a [[P Switch]] can be used to cause [[Blue Coin]]s to appear. A [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]] and a Warp Pipe are found. The player is led back outside, where a Swinging Hammer and the [[Goal Pole]] are found.

Alternatively, the player can use a [[P-Acorn]] to fly up above the wall before the pipe leading underground, which will lead to the end of the level.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is near the beginning of the level. After the player passes the first set of double swinging platforms, there is an alcove slightly transparent. The player should jump into the hidden alcove in the ceiling.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The player will need to use the [[Flying Squirrel Mario|Flying Squirrel suit]] or [[Peachette]] (''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe|New Super Mario Bros U Deluxe]]'' only) to glide under a ledge and reach a [[Warp Pipe]] that contains the second Star Coin. A [[P Switch]] must be pressed to turn the Brick Blocks blocking it into coins.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The final Star Coin is found at the end of the level. The player can reach the wall beside it with a running start and wall-jump to it. The player can use the Flying Squirrel suit or Peachette to reach it as well.
