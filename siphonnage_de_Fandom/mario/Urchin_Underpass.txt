'''Urchin Underpass''' is a new battle stage found in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is based on the Turf War map of the same name from ''Splatoon''.

Similar to [[Hyrule Circuit]] and [[Animal Crossing (track)|Animal Crossing]], the coins are replaced with the Splatoon currency.
