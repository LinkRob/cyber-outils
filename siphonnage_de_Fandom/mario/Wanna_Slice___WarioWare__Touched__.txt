'''Wanna Slice?''' is one the [[microgame]]s featured in [[Wario-Man]]'s [[Super Zero]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[File:WarioWare Touched Fat Wario cameo.png|thumb|left|The Fat Wario cameo in the third level]]
Wanna Slice? is a double-length microgame in which the player has to cut the pizza into a certain number of slices.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The player must cut the pizza into four slices, while it already starts with one cut.
*'''Level 2:''' The player must cut the pizza into four slices.
*'''Level 3:''' The player must cut the pizza into eight slices. Doing so reveals a variation of the [[Fat Wario]] artwork from ''[[Wario Land II]]'' with an added "P" on his overalls and him eating pizza instead of a [[cake]].
