'''Moving Blocks''' are a type of [[block]] featured in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can turn a Moving Block into a platform by throwing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at it. There are two types of Moving Blocks: pink ones, which move vertically, and blue ones, which move horizontally. If a Moving Block travels too far off-screen, it reverts to its normal.
