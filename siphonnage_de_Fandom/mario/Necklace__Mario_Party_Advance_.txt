The '''Necklace''' is a key item in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
During the [[Bestest Buds]] quest, the Necklace is one of three potential gifts for [[Bowser]], the other two being the [[Bracelet]] and the [[Ring (Mario Party Advance)|Ring]]. All three items become available to purchase at Junk after the player visits Bowser at the [[Bowser Pad]], where he had asked them to figure out which of the three gifts he wants the most. In one of his hints, Bowser says that he likes necklaces and bracelets equally the same. The player's character has to purchase the Ring from Junk because it is Bowser's favorite of the items, not the Necklace. Regardless, Junk's shop owner, [[Paratroopa (character)|Paratroopa]], says that Bowser is going to appreciate the Necklace.

If the player's character brings the Necklace to Bowser, he immediately rejects the gift, and they are forced to restart the quest.
