{{InfoboxNeeded}}[[File:Jetpac_DK64.png|thumb|256px]]'''''Jetpac''''' is game originally for the ZX Spectrum that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.
==History==

''Jetpack'' can be unlocked once the player has 15 [[Banana Medal]]s and can be played anytime in [[Cranky's Lab]]. Playing ''Jetpac'' is required as the [[Rareware Coin]] can be earned and it must be collected in order to complete the game. Even after collecting the Rareware Coin, ''Jetpac'' can still be played at Cranky's Lab for fun.
