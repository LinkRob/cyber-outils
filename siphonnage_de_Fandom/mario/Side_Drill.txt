The '''Side Drill''' is a [[Bros. Attack|Bros. Move]] that is seen in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. This move is taught to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] by [[Big Massif]] and [[Lil' Massif]].
==Usage==
In order to use this move, the player must switch to the Side Drill icon by pressing "R" twice. Then, the player must press "B" to have Luigi hop onto Mario's shoulders. Then, the player must press "B" again to use the move. The Side Drill is mainly used to help cross narrow passageways and destroy rocks in the middle of areas. This move is similar to the [[Spin Jump]] as it can cross gaps but it travels in a much shorter distance.
