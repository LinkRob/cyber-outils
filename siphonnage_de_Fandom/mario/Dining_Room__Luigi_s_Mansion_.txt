'''Dining Room''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Dining Room is a mostly symmetrical room featuring a long table in the center, extending across most of the room. Both a china cabinet and a torch can be found on the left and right sides of the room each. There are two doors on the left wall that lead into the [[Kitchen (Luigi's Mansion)|Kitchen]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Dining Room first becomes accessible during [[Area Two]], after [[Luigi]] uses the [[Key]] from the [[Conservatory (Luigi's Mansion)|Conservatory]] to unlock the door to it. The Dining Room is where [[Mr. Luggs]] and his two [[Waiter]] [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] are fought. However, the battle is optional because the doors to the Kitchen are not locked. There is also a [[Speedy Spirit]] hiding in the left china cabinet and a [[cheese]] containing a [[Gold Mouse]] under a chair next to Mr. Luggs. There are a few discarded [[Banana Peel]]s that Luigi can vacuum up. Once Mr. Luggs is defeated, the lights turn on, and a green [[treasure chest]] containing [[treasure]] and a [[Blue Diamond]] spawns on the right side of the room.

During the blackout, Luigi can encounter one [[Gold Ghost]] and one [[Garbage Can Ghost]] here.
