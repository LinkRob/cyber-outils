The {{Nihongo|'''Lumacomète'''|コメットチコ Kometto Chiko|Comet Luma}}, also known as '''Comet Tico''', is a minor character that only appeared in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]].''

== Profile ==
=== Physical appearance ===
The Lumacomète looks like a gradient purple-colored [[Hungry Luma]] with golden eyes. He holds sticks with stars that have yellow star frames in his hands. His eyes are golden. On top of him is the Hungry Luma's characteristic tuft akin to the top point of a star with a nodule to the side.

=== Personality traits ===
The Lumacomète is always hungry for [[Star Bit]]s, but knows a lot about [[Prankster Comet]]s and is happy to divulge what he knows.

=== Powers and abilities ===
The Lumacomète is able to move [[Prankster Comet]]s at will.

== In ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ==
After Mario collects 31 [[Power Star]]s, Lumacomete will appear near the [[Terrace]]. Mario can ask Lumacomete: "Tell Me More!" or "Move It!". If Mario tells him, "Tell Me More!", Lumacomete will talk about the types of [[Prankster Comet]]s. If Mario says, "Move it!," the Lumacomète will use his power to move the Prankster Comets; if Mario complete some of the Prankster Comets, but not many, the Lumacomète will change the positions of some of the Prankster Comets. If Mario completed the majority of them, the Lumacomète will disable the current Prankster Comets. The latter option comes at a price of 100 Star Bits.
