The '''Houhou''' is an enemy from ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Houhous resemble owls with brown feathers. They have paler feathers on the belly. The nests they emerge from are brown, hanging on the ceiling.

=== Behavior ===
Houhous lurk in their nests in their idle state, but if one spots [[Mario]], it will swoop down from its nest to attack. The Houhou's attack can be dodged, causing them to curve back up and retreat nestward. Any attack can defeat a Houhou, rendering the nest empty for the rest of the level.

== In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' ==
Houhous only appear in the level Pokipede Pass.

== Behind the scenes ==
=== Etymology ===
The name Houhou is derived from the cry of an owl, more specifically the Japanese onomatopoeia representing it, Hō (ホー).
