'''Gold Lakitus''' are a variant of [[Lakitu]] which first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' and reappear in ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Gold Lakitu resembles a regular Lakitu but has a gold-colored shell, a light yellow belly, and wears glasses with a yellowish-brown frame. Gold Lakitus ride around in a golden [[Lakitu's Cloud]].

===Behavior===
In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'', rather than throw [[Spiny Egg]]s like regular Lakitus, a Gold Lakitu instead throws groups of three [[Coin]]s down at [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]. In ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World'', a Gold Lakitu drops golden [[Bead]]s instead.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''==
[[File:Gold Lakitus Galore.png|thumb|left|200px|Two Gold Lakitus in [[World 5-2 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5-2]] of ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'']]
Whenever a [[Gold Ring (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|Gold Ring]] has been activated, every Lakitu transforms into a Gold Lakitu. If Mario or Luigi defeat a Gold Lakitu, they earn 20 [[coin]]s, and the Gold Lakitu's cloud becomes available to ride in. Mario or Luigi can earn coins from riding around in a Gold Lakitu's cloud in a similar manner to a [[Gold Block]]. When the Gold Ring's effects wear off, the Gold Lakitus and their clouds all return to normal.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''==
A Gold Lakitu appears in Gold Rush Mode of the [[Poochy Dash]] minigame. Here, [[Poochy]] collects the gold Beads dropped by Gold Lakitu throughout the minigame as it moves to the right at Poochy's speed.
