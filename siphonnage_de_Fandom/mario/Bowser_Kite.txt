The '''Bowser Kite''' is a glider that appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== Description ==
Following its namesake, it is a kite modelled after [[Bowser]], and there is a seal with the kanji "任天" ("Ninten").

== In ''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' ==
The Bowser Kite must be unlocked by collecting a randomized amount of [[coin]]s. The Bowser Kite boosts [[acceleration]] in both games, but in the original game, it hinders [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]], while in ''Deluxe'', [[speed]] and [[traction]] is hindered.

In the original game, the Bowser Kite shares its stats with the [[Cloud Glider]], [[Peach Parasol (glider)|Peach Parasol]], [[Parachute]], [[Parafoil]], [[MKTV Parafoil]], and [[Hylian Kite]]. In ''Deluxe'', its stats are no longer shared with the Cloud Glider, Parachute, or Hylian Kite.
