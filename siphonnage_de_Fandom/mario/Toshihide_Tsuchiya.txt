'''Toshihide Tsuchiya ('''born November 11, 1963) is a Japanese voice actor. In the ''Mario'' and ''Donkey Kong'' franchises, he is the current voice actor of [[Funky Kong]] and previously provided the voice of [[King K. Rool]]. The latter role would to be taken over by [[Tsuguo Mogami]] in [[Donkey Kong Bananza]].
