'''Dream Platform''' is a [[Dream Event]] in the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]''. It has to be unlocked by completing the Cosmos Circuit first.

==Layout==
The event takes place at the Tropical Reef Diving Area.

==Gameplay==
Players jump from a flying ship 10,000 feet in the air above the ocean. Each player competes not only to reach the end as quickly as they can but also to score as many points as possible during their descent. There are several [[Item Box]]es that give the player power-ups, as well as floating rings that, when fallen through, prompt the player to press a certain sequence of buttons within a [[time limit]] to perform tricks, earning the player points. Obstacles come in the form of [[Brick Block|blocks]] and red flying robots known as [[Egg Flapper|Flapper]]s that subtract 500 points if collided with.

==Items==
There are one of four obtainable items from the Item Box:

*[[Barrier (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Barrier]], which protects the user from obstacles for five seconds.
*[[Dash Mushroom]], which give the user a five-second speed boost.
*[[Bomb|Explosion]], which destroy several blocks under the player
*[[Red Shell]], which homes in on whoever is directly in front of the player.

==Missions==
===Amy===
[[Amy Rose]]'s second mission takes place during the event. Her goal is to pass five rings and enter the water first.

===Luigi===
[[Luigi]]'s third mission takes place during the event. His goal is to destroy six blocks by ramming into them and then enter the water first.

===Sonic===
[[Sonic the Hedgehog]]'s fourth mission takes place during the event. To win this mission, Sonic must reach a speed of 170m per second and be first to enter the water.

==Trivia==
* Dream Platform is the only Dream Event in the [[Wii]] version of ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'' without an equivalent in its [[Nintendo DS]] [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Nintendo DS)|counterpart]].
