'''Noozes''' are an enemy which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. They are only encountered in [[Toad Town Caves]].

==Physical description==
Noozes are birds that bear a resemblance to [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]'s nose, hence their name, as well as mustache. Noozes have a pair of eyes, a comb, and a yellow beak. The part of a Nooz's body resembling Mario or Luigi's mustache serves as their wings. Noozes with wings resembling Mario's mustache each have a red comb whereas Noozes resembling Luigi's mustache each have a green comb.
