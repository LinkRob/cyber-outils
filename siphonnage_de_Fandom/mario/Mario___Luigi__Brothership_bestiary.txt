This is a compilation of enemies from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

* Name: The enemy's name.
* Location: Where the enemy can be found.
* Stats: The enemy's statistics.
** Level: The enemy's level, which measures how strong it is. Note that bosses are typically tougher than enemies with the same level.
** Health: How much health an enemy has, measured in [[Heart Point|hit points]]. Unless stated otherwise, it always starts at maximum health. When its health drops to 0, the enemy dies, unless stated otherwise.
** [[Power|Attack]]: The enemy's attack stat. A higher number allows it to deal more damage.
** [[Defense]]: The enemy's defense stat. A higher number means it takes less damage.
** [[Speed (Initiative)|Speed]]: The enemy's speed stat, which dictates when it can take its turn. Combatants with higher Speed stats take their turn sooner than those with lower Speed stats. For example, a [[Guardarm]] has a Speed of 22 starting from when [[Gorumbla]] goes on his first rampage, so if it somehow was accompanied by the Conductor Island [[Seedle]], which has a Speed of 10, it will go before the Seedle in each combat round.
* Rewards: The rewards for the enemy's defeat.
** Experience: How much experience is gained from defeating the enemy, measured in [[Experience Point|Experience Points]].
** Loot: Items gained from defeating the enemy.
*** [[Coin|Coins]]: How many coins the enemy drops when you defeat it.
*** Loot: Non-coin items gained from defeating the enemy.
* Special Traits: Special things that may happen when fighting the enemy.
