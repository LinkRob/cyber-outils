The '''Cloud 9''' is a [[kart]] that appears in the {{series|Mario Kart}} . It debuted in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

== Description ==
It is in the shape of a [[Lakitu's Cloud]], with two golden exhaust pipes at the back. It is based for [[Lakitu]], though any character can use this kart.

In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', the kart uses the [[Sponge]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''

== In {{series|Mario Kart}} ==
=== ''Mario Kart 7'' ===
[[File:Lakitumk7front.jpg|thumb|250px|left|Lakitu in the Cloud 9 racing on [[Mario Circuit]]]]
The Cloud 9 is an unlockable kart part, serving as the signature kart for [[Lakitu]]. It gives a boost in [[acceleration]] and [[handling]], whilst lowering [[speed]] and [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]]. In terms of hidden stats, the Cloud 9 offers a boost in sea and air speed, sea and air handling, [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]] and [[drift]].

The Cloud 9 is particularly favoured by CPUs [[Princess Peach]], [[Rosalina]] and [[Honey Queen]].

The Cloud 9 is used for the Expert [[Staff Ghost]]s for [[Piranha Plant Slide]] and {{Classic course|SNES|[[Rainbow Road (Super Mario Kart)|Rainbow Road]]}}, using both using Lakitu, and his kart is equipped with the [[Sponge]] and [[Roller]] tires respectively, and with the [[Parafoil|Paraglider]]. [[Toad (character)|Toad]] is also used in the Cloud 9 for the Normal Staff Ghost for {{Classic course|Wii|[[Mushroom Gorge]]}}, equipped with the [[Mushroom (tires)|Mushroom]] tires and the Paraglider.

=== ''Mario Kart Tour'' ===
[[File:MKT Lakitu Waluigi.jpg|thumb|150px|right|Lakitu in the Cloud 9 racing with [[Waluigi]] and [[Bowser]] on {{Classic course|SNES|[[Ghost Valley 1]]}}]]
The Cloud 9 reappears in ''Mario Kart Tour'', where it is classified as a Super kart. Performing [[Tricks|Jump Boosts]] will earn more points if this kart is used. Unlike in ''7'', the Cloud 9 does not display the characters' emblem at the back.

== Other appearances ==
=== ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' ===
[[File:Cloud9Trophy3DS.png|thumb|right|Lakitu in his Cloud 9 as a trophy]]
The Cloud 9 makes an appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' as a trophy, with Lakitu in it. It is also equipped with the [[Slick]] tires and Lakitu's [[Super Glider]].
