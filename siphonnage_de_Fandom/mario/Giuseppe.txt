'''Giuseppe''' is an Italian immigrant and citizen in [[Brooklyn]] who is an acquaintance of [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]]. He appeared in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Giuseppe's appearance is remniscent to [[Mario]]'s debut appearance from ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]''.

=== Speech ===
Giuseppe has an Italian voice that bears an excruciatingly strong resemblance to Mario’s voice in most of the mainline games, as he is voiced by Charles Martinet.

== Biography ==
Giuseppe met Mario and Luigi at the [[Punch-Out!! Pizzeria]], while playing [[Jump Man (fictional game)|Jump Man]], praising Mario and Luigi's Italian accents in their [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]] commercial. After he witnessed Mario, Luigi, Peach, Toad and Donkey Kong overthrow Bowser, he was part of the crowd cheering for Brooklyn's victory.
