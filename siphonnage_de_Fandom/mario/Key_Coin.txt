'''Key Coins''' ('''Pink Coins''') are a type of [[coin]] and an item which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. They also make an appearance in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' and [[Super Nintendo World]].

==Description==
In certain areas, there are a number of Key Coins to collect. If the player's character collects every Key Coin, they are either automatically given a [[key]] to unlock a [[Key Door]] or a nearby [[Key Warp Box]] opens, depending on the area.

In ''Super Mario 3D World'' and Super Nintendo World, the Key Coins are big and kind of a goldish-brownish color, but in ''Super Mario Maker'' and ''Super Mario Maker 2'', the Key Coins are pink, except in the ''Super Mario 3D World'' theme, in which they are instead designed after their original appearance.

In [[Super Nintendo World]], collecting three out of five Key Coins will allow you to traverse Bowser Jr.'s castle and retrieve the [[Golden Mushroom]] from him.
