'''Seer Terror''' is a Rare mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
[[Bowser]] welcomes the player's character into his chamber and says that he can predict their future.

==Gameplay==
Following the intro, the announcer says "Go!", and four ropes fall from above, Bowser instructs the player's character to select one of the four ropes and pull on it within ten seconds. Each rope is accompanied by a random event, most of which harm the player's character.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move cursor
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Confirm

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''': ''Bowser will peer into his crystal ball and predict your future! All you have to do is choose a rope and pull on it.''
* '''Advice''': ''There are good fortunes and bad fortunes... which will yours be?''
