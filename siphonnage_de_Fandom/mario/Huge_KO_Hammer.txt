|name=Huge KO Hammer (Battle Card)}}'''Huge KO Hammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Huge KO Hammer is the super-strong sideways version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer attack]]. It is a huge yellow and blue hammer with a star in the center, accompanied with a yellow/pastel yellow spiral background.
