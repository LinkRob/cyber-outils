[[File:Poisonmove.png|thumb|Poison (move) is a known Poison move.]]'''Poison''' is an reoccurring element that appears usually in the Mario RPG games. It is a fairly common element that certain enemies and bosses can use. Whenever Mario or someone else is hit with an poison-inflicting move, they are [[Poison (ailment)|poisoned]] themselves and can be cured using a certain item.

==''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''==

Poison is fairly common in this game. Certain moves can be used by enemies can cause poison on the spot. [[Smithy]] is known for being the only character in the game that can poison multiple characters at the same time. It should be noted that the Mad Adder cannot use any named poison moves but can still poison with a bite that it uses. Poison, in this game, can be healed with either [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]]'s healing techniques or with [[Able Juice]]s.

==''[[Paper Mario]]''==

Poison returns in this game and it's used by a few enemies and bosses in this game. Enemies that live in the jungle such as of [[Jade Jungle]] can inflict this status ailment. [[Bowser]] is well known for being the only boss in the game that is able to poison Mario with his claw attack. If Mario is poisoned, then he will take 1 point of damage for up to 9 turns unless he heals himself with either [[Refresh]] or by drinking a [[Tasty Tonic]].
