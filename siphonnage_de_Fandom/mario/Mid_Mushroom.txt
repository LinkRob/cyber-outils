The '''Mid Mushroom''' is a healing item in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a more effective variation of the [[Mushroom]] item, as using a Mid Mushroom restores 80 [[Heart Point|HP]] to the user. The Mid Mushroom can be bought from various item shops for 20 [[coin]]s.

Mid Mushrooms share a Japanese name with [[Super Mushroom]], suggesting them to be the same thing. Despite this, Mid Mushrooms have a light green cap like that of a [[1-Up Mushroom]].
