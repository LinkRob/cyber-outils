'''Banana Storm''' is a [[minigame]] in [[World 2 (Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix)|World 2]] in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''. 

==Gameplay==
The player needs to press ← and → on the Dance Pad to have [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] collect as many falling [[banana]]s from the [[Ukiki]]s as he can within 30 seconds. The Ukikis show the player what they will drop before they do so. If Mario or Luigi catches a [[Spiny (Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix)|Spiny]], he will be unable to move for a short time.

==In-game text==
*''"It's raining bananas! Try to grab as many of them as you can! Look out for the Spinies though!"''
*'''How to Play:''' ''"Grab as many falling bananas as you can by using ← and →! But be careful! Catch a Spiny and you won't be able to move for a while!"''
