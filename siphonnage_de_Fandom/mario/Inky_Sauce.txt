}}'''Inky Sauce''' is a [[recipe]] in the [[Paper Mario series|''Paper Mario'' series]].

== Appearances ==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Inky Sauce Sprite - The Thousand-Year Door.png|frame|left]]
Inky Sauce debuted in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', as a rare item which can only be created by [[Zess T.]] of [[Rogueport]]. [[Mario]] can get Zess T. to create an Inky Sauce by mixing a [[Hot Sauce]] with a variety of items. Inky Sauce is also used in a few recipes. Mario can also use Inky Sauce to recover thirty [[Flower Point|FP]].

=== ''Super Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Inky Sauce is used differently than in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''. Instead of recovering thirty FP, Inky Sauce now only recovers three [[Heart Point|HP]], but is still used in several new recipes. Inky Sauce can be obtained from [[Blooper]]s that Mario and his friends defeat on their quest.
