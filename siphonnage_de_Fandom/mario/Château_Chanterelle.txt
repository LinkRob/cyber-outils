'''Château Chanterelle''' is a [[level]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Layout==
The level consists of a [[Toad House]] and a small barn. The main level revolves around using [[Cut-out]] to place the [[Bone Thing]] so that the professor's pet [[Chain Chomp]], [[Princess]], will return. after he gives you the key to [[Kiwano Temple]].
