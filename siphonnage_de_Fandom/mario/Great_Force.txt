The '''Great Force''' is an item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Appearance==
The Great Force looks like a small, yellow, triangular object. Its appearance and name is a parody of the [[w:c:zelda:Triforce|Triforce]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' series. In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Great Force was redesigned to bear a greater resemblance to the complete Triforce.

==Usage==
While equipped, the Great Force allows both Mario and Luigi to deal double the damage to enemies, although at the cost of taking double the damage from enemies themselves. The Great Force should only be worn against strong foes whose attacks are easy to block or evade against. If one brother has the Great Force equipped, it affects both of them.

==History==
The Great Force can be only obtained by making a different type of [[Starbeans Espresso|espresso]] at [[Starbeans Cafe]] for the fourth time. When [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] do so, [[Professor E. Gadd]] makes an appearance and gives them the Great Force.
