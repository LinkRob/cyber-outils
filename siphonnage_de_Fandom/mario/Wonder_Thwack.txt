'''Wonder Thwacks''' are stronger versions of [[Thwack]]s found at the [[Star Shrine]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Unlike regular Thwacks, which come in pink and green, Wonder Thwacks come in red and blue, and they also glow.

In battle, Wonder Thwacks use the same attacks as regular Thwacks, but they move faster. Even though they are more powerful than normal Thwacks, Wonder Thwacks still have the same amount of [[Heart Point|HP]]. Before each attack, a Wonder Thwack twirls and either remains the same color or switches to a different color. If red, it attacks [[Mario]]; if blue, it attacks [[Luigi]]. After twirling, the Wonder Thwack attempts to crush the appropriate Mario brother. However, since they fly higher than normal Thwacks, the Baby Mario Bros. must wait longer until the Wonder Thwack drops within range before countering with their [[Hammer]]s.
Wonder Thwacks can also fly low and ram into a Mario brother. The bros. can dodge or counter this attack by jumping. Additionally, as with their weaker counterparts, they can summon more of their kind to join the battle.

If the battle has gone on for too long, Wonder Thwacks will turn yellow (instead of red or blue) when their turn comes, after twirling and attempt a final resort attack. After turning yellow, they split into two yellow boulders which automatically kills them. Each boulder rolls towards a Mario brother. Mario and Luigi can dodge this attack by jumping. 

Red Wonder Thwacks are resilient to [[Bro Flower]]s and [[Mix Flower]]s, while blue ones are resilient to [[Ice Flower]]s both taking only a single point of damage from the element they’re resilient to. Each color receives more damage from items that its counterpart is strong against.
