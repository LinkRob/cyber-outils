'''The Sharp Trial: Launch to Victory''' is a level of [[Shining Falls]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level features many [[Spike Trap]]s. The player must ride a [[lift]] across the level. Hoppycats will lauch out of the side of the lift when the player [[jump]]s.

The [[Wonder Flower]] is located in a [[? block]] to the right of a row of [[cloud block]]s. The [[Wonder Effect]] will transform the player into a hoppycat. All obstacles on screen aside from the cloud blocks dissapear and a vertical based area spawns for the player to scale. The [[Wonder Seed]] is located at the top of the area.
