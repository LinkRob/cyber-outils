'''Custom Tickets''' are an item in ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]''.

==Description==
Custom Tickets can be redeemed for [[Custom Club]]s at the [[Custom Club Shop]]. There are four different types of Custom Tickets: Custom Ticket A, Custom Ticket B, Custom Ticket C, and Custom Ticket D. Each of the four types have only two to collect except Custom Ticket D, of which there is only one to collect.
