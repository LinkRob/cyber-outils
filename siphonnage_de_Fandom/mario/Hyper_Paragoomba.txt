'''Hyper Paragoombas''' are a hyperactive, green variant of [[Paragoomba]]s in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They appear in both ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

In both of its appearances, Hyper Paragoombas have a powerful effect allowing them to charge their [[Attack]] power by six for their next attack. If a Hyper Paragoomba loses its wings, it becomes a [[Hyper Goomba]].
