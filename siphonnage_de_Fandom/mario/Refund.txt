'''Refund''' is a recurring [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. By using an item in battle while having the Refund equipped, [[Mario]] receive a small refund for the item in the form of [[coin]]s. Refund is not usable outside of battle.

== Appearances ==
=== ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', this badge is found in the [[Koopa Bros. Fortress]]. It can only be obtained after [[Bombette]] has joined Mario's party. In the most eastern part of the Fortress, Mario can command Bombette to blow up the wall with a crack in it to reveal a path leading outside. Outside, Mario will find a treasure chest. This badge only requires one [[Badge Point|BP]] to be used. When equipped, this badge will give Mario half the price of the item after the battle.

=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
[[File:Refund (The Thousand-Year Door).gif|Refund as it appears in Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]
Refund is found in the [[Pianta Parlor]], where it can be bought for of thirty-four [[Pianta Token]]s. Just as before, using Refund costs only one BP. A difference is that a Refund now gives Mario 75 percent of the price of the item instead of half the price.
