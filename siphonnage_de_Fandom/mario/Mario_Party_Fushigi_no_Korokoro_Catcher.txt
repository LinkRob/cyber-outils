'''''Mario Party: Fushigi no Korokoro Catcher''''' is an arcade game based on ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. It was released in 2009, exclusively in Japan, being the third ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]''-related game to only be released there. It featured a variety of minigames, and it had support for up to 16 players, each of whom would play a different minigame. In 2013 a sequel was released, ''[[Mario Party Fushigi no Korokoro Catcher 2]]'', also based on ''Mario Party 8''.
