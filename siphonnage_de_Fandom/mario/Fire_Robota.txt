'''Fire Robota''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Fire Robotas are a variant of [[Spear-bot]] with a very similar appearance except its torso is red.

===Behavior===
Fire Robotas have a fiery torch stored in its chest. If [[Wario]] is nearby, it faces him, extrudes the fiery torch, and begins to move faster. After a while, a Fire Robota retracts its fiery torch and begins to move at regular speed once more. A Fire Robota essentially has the behavior a Spear-bot except the latter extrudes and retracts a spear instead of a fiery torch.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Fire Robotas are first encountered by Wario in [[The Peaceful Village]], where a total of eight are found. If Wario touches a Fire Robota's fire, he temporarily becomes [[Flaming Wario|Hot Wario]].
