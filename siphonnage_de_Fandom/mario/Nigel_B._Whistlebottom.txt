{{Infobox Character|name = Nigel B. Whistlebottom|image = [[File:Nigelbwhistlebottom.gif]]|games = ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''|race = Human|first = ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]'' (2007)|gender = Male}}'''Nigel B. Whistlebottom''' is a cop in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
He guards the [[Smithsnorian Museum]] and when he spots [[Wario]], he will blow his whistle and alert other security guards like [[Officer Kicksworth]]s, [[Sergeant Houndstooth]]s or even [[Dave]]s to enter the room. He will also attack by swinging a baseball bat if Wario comes too close. He cannot be defeated but can be stunned for a while before he can fight again.
