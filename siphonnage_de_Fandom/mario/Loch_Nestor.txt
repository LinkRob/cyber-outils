'''Loch Nestor''' is a round, orange pufferfish enemy featured in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. They only appear in areas where a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] has to take on the form of a [[Submarine (form)|Submarine]]. A Loch Nestor can be attacked by firing a torpedo at it. A Loch Nestor swells up with each hit, and shooting three torpedoes (two in ''Yoshi's New Island'') at a Loch Nestor causes it to explode, defeating it. If Submarine Yoshi touches a Loch Nestor, he gets stunned for about one second.

Its name is a pun on the {{w|Loch Ness Monster}} despite having no apparent similarities to it.
