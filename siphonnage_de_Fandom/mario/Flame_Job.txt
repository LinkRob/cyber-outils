'''Flame Job''' is a [[Blaster (primary weapon)|Blaster]] weapon that is designed after a hand cannon with average power and range. [[Mario]] can use the Flame Job. The weapon appears in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:MRKB Flame Job screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|Flame Job in-game.]]
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Flame Job resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack with a medium range. The Flame Job deals around 30 to 40 damage. The user gains 15% bonus against small enemies.
