'''Eeries''' are ghostly [[dinosaur]]s that first appeared in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. They can fly in either straight or wobbly lines and usually appear in packs, damaging [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] on contact. Eeries are only encountered in [[Ghost House]]s, and they appear in most of them. Like [[Boo]]s, they can only be defeated with a [[Super Star]].

Eeries make another appearance in the [[Nintendo GameCube]] version of ''[[Yoshi's Cookie]]'', where they appear as the fourth opponent in the game's VS. Mode if the player plays on the medium difficulty.
