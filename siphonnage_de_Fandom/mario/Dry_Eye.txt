'''Chicken Pinch''' is [[Mona]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and returns in [[WarioWare: D.I.Y.|''WarioWare: D.I.Y.'']] and ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as her microgame.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to squirt the bottle to the eye. The player wins if the bottle squirts at the woman's eye.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The eye is wide and the bottle is close to the eye. 
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The eye reduces a third and the bottle is a bit further from the eye. 
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The eye reduces two thirds and the bottle is further away from the eye.
===''WarioWare D.I.Y''===
The objective is to squirt the bottle to the woman's eye. However, her eye must be open so the player can squirt it. The player loses if it is squirted somewhere else.
=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
The woman is replaced by a robot which the command says "Squirt?". It sometimes happens on the second and third level that remakes [[Laser Vision]].
