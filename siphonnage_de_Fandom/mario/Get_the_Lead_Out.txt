'''Get the Lead Out''' is a 4-Player and Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. It appears as the fifth minigame in [[Score Scuffle]], coming after [[Study Fall]], with its own successor being [[Whomp-a-thon]].

==Opening==
The characters are seen waving next to some [[mechanical pencil]]s.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Get the Lead Out MPDS duel.png|thumb|left|Duel mode variation]]
In the minigame, the players are pushing graphite out of mechanical pencils as fast as they can in 10 seconds. Every button press increases the graphite length by 0.3 units. The graphite pieces match their respective players' colors, with Player 1's graphite being blue, Player 2's being red, Player 3's being green, and Player 4's being yellow. Once the time is up, the screen moves over to show grid paper. It is at this time that the distances are measured. In the North American English release, the length is measured in inches. Elsewhere, it is measured in centimeters. These units are arbitrary, and the numbers given are the same in all versions of the game. The player with the most graphite pushed out wins the minigame.

The highest score possible is a graphite length of 90.0 inches/centimeters, which is achieved through pause-buffering.<ref>さかみやり (January 22, 2022). "[https://youtu.be/drbAqXhPOPM <nowiki>[TAS]</nowiki>マリオパーティDS たたいてペンシル 90.0cm]". YouTube. Retrieved March 10, 2024.</ref> In Score Scuffle, it is impossible to receive 1,000 points, as a player needs to achieve a score of 90.0 inches/centimeters to earn 1,000 points, and 89.7 inches/centimeters is the maximum score achievable without pause-buffering.<ref>pirahnaplant (September 2, 2010). "[https://youtu.be/WOZ6OiRYd7M?t=219 Mario Party DS Score Scuffle 9999 Points TAS]". YouTube. Retrieved March 10, 2024.</ref> This may be either a programming bug or a measure to prevent any player aiming for the highest score from having their score reset to zero and their placement changed to last in Score Scuffle.

Like other 4-Player/Duel minigames with a record system, if the current record is broken while everyone gets the same score in both variants, it counts as a new record even though no one won, as the screen says "Tie" after the "New Record" fanfare.

==Ending==
The winner does their victory dance on the grid paper. If a [[Draw|tie]] is declared, all the players do their losing animations on the grid paper.

==Controls==
*{{button|nds|A}} &ndash; Press rapidly to extend graphite

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The name of the minigame comes from an idiom meaning "to move faster."
