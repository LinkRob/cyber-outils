The '''Uni-Tree''' is an object from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is the tree that holds [[Concordia]] together. It has a dark reflection known as the [[Soli-Tree]], which serves as the final area of the game. Some time before the events of the game, the Uni-Tree was destroyed during the disappearance of its previous caretaker, [[Cozette]]. [[Connie]] re-planted it on [[Shipshape Island]].

== Description ==
The current Uni-Tree looks different from what it originally looked like. It looks like the sail of a ship, having a straight trunk. At the top of its trunk, a lightbulb can be seen. Its leaves hang out in front of it akin to a drape, since they act as Shipshape Island's sail, and have a trapezoidal appearance when seen from a distance. The Uni-Tree's original design was different, with its branches having a more realistic layout and a cage-like formation in its centre.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The Uni-Tree's name comes from a pun on "unite" (or "unity") and "tree."

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese and Chinese, the Uni-Tree is called the "Connectar Tree" (Japanese: ''konekutaru jumuko'' (written コネクタル樹木); Chinese: ''Kēliánkùtǎlǔshù'' (written 柯連庫塔魯樹)), with "connectar" being a pun on "nectar." It is referred to as "Conectárbol" in Spanish, with its name coming from a pun on ''conectar'' ("to connect") and ''arbol'' ("tree"). The Italian dub makes its name ''Centralbero'', from ''albero'' ("tree") and likely ''centralina'' ("power box"). In French, the tree's name is ''Unicéa'', from ''unir'' ("to unite"). Dutch translates the name to ''Gezameleik'', from ''gezamenlijk'' ("together") and ''eik'' ("oak").
