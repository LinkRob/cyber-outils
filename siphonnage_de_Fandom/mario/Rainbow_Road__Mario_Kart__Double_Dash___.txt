'''Rainbow Road''' is the fourth and final track in the [[Special Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is also the final track in the [[All-Cup Tour]]. Rainbow Road is throttled with [[Boost Pad]]s, and near the end, it hosts a large pipe that launches the player. The number of barriers to prevent the player's downfall is borderline minimal, making the course very difficult. There are several statues of 3D rainbow-colored items and characters floating above the player. It uses a pipe-like cannon that shoots the player straight up, which has later been used for the ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' generation of [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart Wii)|Rainbow Road]].

The staff ghost uses [[Mario]] and [[Princess Peach]] in the [[Red Fire]] kart.

==Trivia==
[[File:Misplaced heart Double Dash.png|thumb|200px|The misplaced heart.]]
*This course takes place above [[Mushroom City]].
*In single-player only, [[Starman|Star]]s will fall on the track due to the fact that falling stars come down on the track a lot (and many times becoming a Star item on the track). Hitting these will make the player invincible (because that is what a Star item does) unless they fall off the course.
*This is not seen in normal gameplay, however, if the player uses cheat codes or Action Replay, and go out of the skybox, there is a misplaced heart that is never seen in normal gameplay. It is unknown why this was placed outside of the skybox, or why it was made in the first place.
* Rainbow Road's music remixes [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 64)|Rainbow Road]]'s music from ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.
* The official website claims that this course is the longest course in the game. This is false, as the actual longest course is the [[Wario Colosseum]]; however, the website may have been referring to the total time.
