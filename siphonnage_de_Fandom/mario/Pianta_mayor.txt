The '''Pianta mayor''' is a character in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Pianta mayor is a tan-skin [[Pianta]] with a mustache. Aside from wearing a grass skirt like other male Piantas, the Pianta mayor also has a pair of glasses and wears a hat on his head.

===Behavior===
The Pianta mayor is the mayor of [[Pianta Village]], although there he mostly hangs around a gold Mushroom in the middle of the area. The Pianta mayor is shown to have bravery during Pianta Village's third [[mission|episode]], [[The Goopy Inferno]], when he stays behind while evacuating the others as the village becomes covered in [[burning goop]].
