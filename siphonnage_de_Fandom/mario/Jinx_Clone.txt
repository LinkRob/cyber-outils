A '''Jinx Clone''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Jinx Clones are identical clones of [[Jinx]], the powerful martial artist of [[Monstro Town]]. They are summoned into battle by a [[Magikoopa]] after hatching up a [[Yoshi Egg]].

== Battle ==
In battle, the Jinx Clones are capable of using nearly all of the real Jinx's attacks. This includes [[Jinxed]], [[Triple Kick]], and [[Silver Bullet]]. The Jinx Clones have lower HP than the real Jinx but overall has his strength from the first fight with him.
