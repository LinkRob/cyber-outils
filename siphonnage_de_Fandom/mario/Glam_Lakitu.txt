'''Glam Lakitus''' are enemies found in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', serving as somewhat rare enemies. They are more powerful versions of [[King Lakitu]]s, as such, also attack faster. In order to be encountered, [[Bowser]] must go into a battle with a [[Beehoss]], with a chance of a Glam Lakitu appearing on the top screen. They can be inhaled, and then fought by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. However, if the bros. do not defeat it quickly enough, they will flee [[Bowser]]'s body.
