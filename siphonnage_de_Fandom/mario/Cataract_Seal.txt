==Description==
The Cataract Seal is a teal key that, according to [[Wario]], has a jagged design.

==History==
The Cataract Seal is one of four keys that can be found in [[Ancient Waterworks]]. This key can be found in a purple treasure chest located on the third-highest floor of the level which can be accessed by using [[Cosmic Wario]]'s laser to activate a switch. The Cataract Seal can then be used on a picture that can be found in the center waterfall.
