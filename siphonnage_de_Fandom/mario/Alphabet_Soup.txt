'''Alphabet Soup''' is one of [[Orbulon]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Gameplay==
A word is given on the top for the player to spell out, although they must switch around two letters. To do so, the player must tilt the system to move the cursor and press the A button once the two correct letters are selected to switch them around.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level adds another letter to the word being spelled, and each one also has a different set of four letters it randomly selects from before starting.

*'''Level 1:''' The player must spell out a three-letter word, either "pen," "top," "hip," or "ton."
*'''Level 2:''' The player must spell out a four-letter word, either "glue," "tape," "note," or "fish."
*'''Level 3:''' The player must spell out a five-letter word, either "plane," "train," "watch," or "plant."
