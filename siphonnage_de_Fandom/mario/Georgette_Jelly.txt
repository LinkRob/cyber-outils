'''Georgette Jellies''' are enemies featured in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They are jelly blobs that slowly move on the ground.

Georgette Jellies only appear in [[Sluggy The Unshaven's Fort]]. Here, they try to hurt [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] if he makes contact with one. If Yoshi is to jump on a Georgette Jelly, it will splatter on the walls for a few seconds before rematerializing. Despite being made of goo, Georgette Jellies are actually quite sturdy as they can't be beaten with [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] tosses or swallows however, Yoshi can still swallow one to spit it back out. With this trick, these creatures make good makeshift projectile as they curl up into a hard ball able to plow through most enemies. The only way to beat a Georgette Jelly is to either use a [[Winged Cloud]] item when near one or spit it at a [[Dangling Ghost]].

==Trivia==
*Georgette Jellies are one of the few enemies from ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'' to not reappear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.
*The way Georgette Jellies reform after being stomped is similar to that of a [[Dry Bones]], a recurring enemy from the mainline ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games.
