'''Funky's Flights II''' is one of the businesses run by [[Funky Kong]] in the [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]]. It first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and reappears in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''.

==Description==
Much like [[Funky's Flights]] from ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', Funky's Flights II is a flight service where Funky allows for [[Diddy Kong]] and [[Dixie Kong]] to use the [[Jumbo Barrel]] to travel between worlds on the main [[Crocodile Isle]] map, as well as levels by extension, they have already visited. In the original [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version, the service costs two [[Banana Bunch Coin]]s the first time, but subsequent uses in that world becomes free afterward.

In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2 (Game Boy Advance)|Donkey Kong Country 2]]'' for the [[Game Boy Advance]], Funky instead focuses on hosting a series of his own [[Funky's Flights (Bonus Game)|challenges]], given that after completing the [[Aerial Loop]] challenge, the [[Gyrocopter]], which replaces the Jumbo Barrel from the original version, can be called up from the world map at any time.
