'''Roiling Roller Isle''' is an island located on [[Lake Lapcat]] in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
It is one of the few islands that surrounds the Wasteland [[Giga Bell]]. The island is a large factory-like level with rolling platforms and [[lava]] below.
