The '''crown'''<ref>''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon Prima Official Game Guide'', page 265.</ref> is a piece of jewelry belonging to [[King Boo]]. It first appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and appears the most prominently in the {{series|Luigi's Mansion}} thereon.

==Description==
In the {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}, the crown holds a diamond at the top, colored red in the first game but colored purple afterward, including for the ''Luigi's Mansion'' King Boo counterpart in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

In most other appearances, the crown is golden, encrusted with jewels like [[Peach's Crown]], with four red ones encrusted around the crown and one blue one in the center. King Boo's crown bears a slight resemblance to the one worn by the [[King Boo (Super Mario Sunshine)|King Boo]] from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}==
===''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''===
King Boo's crown also counts as a [[Red Diamond (Luigi's Mansion)|Red Diamond]], which has the same value as a regular [[coin]], 5,000G. King Boo's crown is the only [[treasure]] required to collect, making it possible yet unlikely to get 5,000G for an entire playthrough.

===''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' / ''[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]]''===
The gem on King Boo's crown was made into a purple diamond-shaped stone resembling an amethyst, and it is powerful enough to shatter the [[Dark Moon]].

===''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''===
The crown retains its appearance from ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''. It now has more visual detail, due to the [[Nintendo Switch]] graphics. After defeating King Boo, Luigi manages to obtain the crown's gem, although it vanishes after [[The Last Resort]] collapses and [[Professor E. Gadd]]'s containment field is breached, and the [[ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] are no longer antagonists.
