'''Galactic Stadium''' is an unlockable stadium in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. It is set somewhere in outer space. Galactic Stadium can be unlocked by acquiring the "Golden Foot" and "Brick Wall" awards in the [[Striker Cup]] tournament.
