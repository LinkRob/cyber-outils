'''Wish Keys'''<ref>''Diddy Kong Racing DS'' manual, page 11</ref> are a set of four keys that appear in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and its [[Nintendo DS]] remake, ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]''. Each Wish Key is hidden in a racetrack of all four worlds, and unlock the Challenge Level door of their respective world.

All four Wish Keys are large, golden keys in the original version. In ''Diddy Kong Racing DS'', they have been slightly repurposed, as each Wish Key instead unlocks a [[Wish Race]] against [[T.T.]]. Each Wish Key was also redesigned after its world theme except for the one of [[Dragon Forest]], as [[Dino Domain]]'s key has a skull design, [[Snowflake Mountain]]'s key is made of ice, and [[Sherbet Land]]'s key resembles a golden anchor.
