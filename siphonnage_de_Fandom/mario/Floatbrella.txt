The {{Nihongo|'''Floatbrella'''|フワフワカッサー|Fuwafuwa Kassā|Floaty Perry}} is [[Perry]]'s form that helps [[Princess Peach]] glide airborne. It appears in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Description ==
Peach can get the Floatbrella technique for 200 [[coin]]s in the shop. Using the technique Peach can stall in the air for a few seconds by holding {{button|ds|A}}, helping her cross over large gaps.

== Trivia ==
* Peach does not float on her own in ''Super Princess Peach''. However, in video games such as ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Run]]'', Peach can stall in the air for a few seconds without relying on a parasol.

=== References to video games and other media ===
* In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Perry's spirit has a spirit battle of Peach in a gold costume using [[Peach Parasol]] to reference Perry's Floatbrella technique.
