==History==

Mega Phil & Mega Lowe were first seen after they were freed from their petrified states. They both are informed by [[Dreambert]] on what to do and, after a song dance, they crushed the gate that was blocking their way. However, Mega Phil & Mega Lowe were confused as "beef lords" by [[Big Massif]] and [[Lil' Massif]]. Mega Phil & Mega Lowe then leave afterwards.
