A '''Mega Urchin''' (also called a '''Big Urchin''' or a '''Giant Urchin''') is a larger variety of the [[Urchin]] found in the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' series. They behave in the same manner as Urchins, however, they cannot be defeated without a [[Starman]]. 

==History==
===''New Super Mario Bros.'' series===
====''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''====
[[Image:World 4-3 - Giant Puffer.png|thumb|left|A Mega Urchin as seen in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.]]
Mega Urchins make their debut in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. In this game, they are commonly found in [[World 4-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4-3]] both lingering underwater and on the water's surface aiming to hit [[Mario]] with their pointy bodies. There is no way to defeat a Mega Urchin without a Starman.

====''New Super Mario Bros. U''/''New Super Luigi U/New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''====
[[Image:Mega Urchin Waterspout Ride.jpg|thumb|right|Mega Urchins riding the Waterspouts.]]
Although Mega Urchins are absent in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', they reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. In these games, they are far more common appearing frequently in [[Sparkling Waters]]. Once again, only the effects of a Starman can destroy them. In this game, the are also seen riding on the [[Waterspout]]s, they are also seen guarding [[Star Coin]]s.

They reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' with the same role.

==Other appearances==
===''Mario Golf: World Tour''===
[[Image:Cheep Cheep Lagoon Golf.png|thumb|left|Mega Urchins in the Lagoon.]]
Mega Urchins reappear in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]'' in the course [[Cheep Cheep Lagoon]], where they appear as obstacles to avoid. They also appear in the background on some other water courses.

===''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS''===
[[Image:Waterspouts Wii U.jpg|thumb|right|A single Mega Urchin riding a Waterspout.]]
Mega Urchins also appear as obstacles in the [[Mushroom Kingdom U]] stage found in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' hurting any players that make contact. They rise on the [[Waterspout]]s.

===''Mario Party 10''===
While Mega Urchins don't appear in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', a similar variety called the [[Big Urchin]] did. They are smaller than a Mega Urchin but larger than a basic Urchin.

===''Paper Mario: Color Splash''===
[[Image:Paper Mega Urchin.png|thumb|left|A Mega Urchin above [[Mario]] and [[Huey]]'s Bubble.]]
A Mega Urchin is also seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' in the [[Snifit or Whiffit]] Game Show. It floats above Mario as he answers the questions, every time Mario gets a wrong answer, the Mega Urchin will lower itself onto Mario's [[bubble]]. If Mario gets too many wrong answers, the Mega Urchin will pop his bubble killing him and resulting in a [[Game Over]].
