'''Papa T.''' is the father of [[Jimmy T]] in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. He first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' along with his wife, [[Mama T.]].

==Physical description==
Papa T. is a tall man who wears a green pinstripe suit, white bell bottom pants, green shoes, a tall, yellow afro wig, and glasses with a white square-shaped frame. Papa T. has a red, bulb-shaped nose.

==In ''WarioWare: Twisted!''==
[[File:Jimmy T and parents dancing WW Twisted.png|thumb|left|Papa T. with Jimmy T. and Mama T. in ''WarioWare: Twisted!'']]
Papa T., along with Mama T., first appear in Jimmy T.'s stage, dancing with their son in [[Club Sugar]]. Later on, Papa T. and Mama T. host the [[Family Scramble]] [[microgame]] set, featuring a mixture of [[Mona]]'s, Jimmy T.'s, and [[Kat and Ana]]'s microgames.
{{br|left}}
==In ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''==
Papa T. and Mama T. no longer host their own microgame set, although they return in Jimmy T.'s stage, [[Dance Club Rub]], where they dance with Jimmy and his two younger siblings, [[James T.]] and [[Jamie T.]].
