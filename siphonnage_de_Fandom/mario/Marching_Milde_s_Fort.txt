'''Marching Milde's Fort''' is the fourth level of [[World 4 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 4]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and the Game Boy Advance version, [[Super Mario Advance 3: Yoshi's Island|''Super Mario Advance 3: Yoshi's'' ''Island'']]. 

==Layout==
The level starts in an exterior area. Heading straight, the player must avoid two [[Incoming Chomp|Incoming Chomps]] that come and fall. Yellow Yoshi should continue and run to the other end of the area on dirt and various platforms, as a [[Chomp Shark]] chases you. On route, the player should collect 4 [[Red Coins|red coins]] and 1 [[Smiley Flower]]. Yellow Yoshi should make his way up by hitting a [[Winged Cloud]] to create a staircase, and another again to reach the pipe that warps to the fortress. 

Inside, the player will be in a lobby with a locked door, and four doors in each diagonal corner of the room. The bottom left door leads you to a room with lava areas. Get some eggs from an [[Egg Block]] below the door and hit the Bucket to float across the first lava pit, dodging the [[Podoboo]] and also collecting a red coin. Cross safely again with the [[Paddle Platform]], and you'll also come across another red coin. Float up to the higher level with the Arrow Lift and traverse right to the other Arrow Lift. Avoid the two [[Hot Lips]] in the area and safely collect the Smiley Flower just below the lava, and head right. Above the last Hot Lips is a red coin. Collect the key from the Winged Cloud. 

The top left door leads you to a room with two [[Spiked Fun Guy (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|Spiked Fun Guys]] and a blue Arrow Lift. Ride it to the left side and defeat the Wild Piranha to reach a box containing Stars and six coins, two of which are Red. Hop to the other side and make your way upwards, avoiding two [[Blow Hard|Blow Hards]]. On the right side is a Winged Cloud containing Stars (coins in the GBA version), as well as one red coin in a row of 4. Continue and use eggs to cut through the dirt, avoiding Spiked Fun Guys and collecting two red coins. The key is behind a Wild Ptooie Piranha and the exit. Above is a Smiley Flower. 

In the bottom right room, first the player must hop on rotating 3D platforms upwards, avoiding Snifits and Grunts running around. Hop down to an Arrow Lift that you can ride to collect some coins above thorns, and two red coins. A Smiley Flower is trapped to the left and can be obtained by throwing an egg. Make your way upwards and collect two red coins in a row of 4 inside a hollow area, but avoid the Grunt. Soon, the player will reach two doors that lead to the same area, with a Paddle Platform below it in an area with an Egg Block and thorns. The room contains a key in a Winged Cloud, above a 3D polygonal block. The door above it takes you back to the room lobby. 

In the top right room, the player must navigate through destroying green blocks, as well as dirt. Some red coins are in the area. Afterwards, Yellow Yoshi must avoid Snifits, Bullet Bills and Bandits. After the area with two Bullet Bill cannons, the player can find a hidden Winged Cloud at the top left corner that contains a Smiley Flower. To find the key in this room, the player must destroy the thorns barricading two Winged Clouds, one of which contains the key (the other contains stars). After getting it, Yellow Yoshi must navigate back to the door at the start by passing the Bullet Bill cannon and breaking the barrier.  

After collecting all four keys, enter the locked center door and go through each room and unlock each door until you reach the Boss Door. 
==Boss Battle==
[[Kamek]] enlarges a regular [[Milde]] to create [[Marching Milde]]. Marching Milde is arguably the easiest battle of the game, as she does not attack. The player needs to Ground Pound her to split her into two, whereas Ground Pounding them again splits them into two again. The next time, she splits into four Mildes. The player can take the rest out however they'd like to win the fight.

== Trivia ==

*At the starting area, the Athletic theme is played. This is the only fort/castle in the game where different music plays in said level.
* '''Level differences:'''
** In the GBA version, the area leading into the fortress pipe contains a [[Flipper]] and a [[Middle Ring]].
** The lobby floor in the GBA version is broken from its continuous mountain shape at the bottom of the middle door, and below it is a Middle Ring. [[File:Milde Compare.png|thumb|250px]]
** In the GBA version, the Spiked Fun Guy below the door is potted.
** The dirt in the bottom left room is slightly differed in the GBA version. It is seemingly shrunk down to fit in the smaller screen environment, while also having a different shape.
