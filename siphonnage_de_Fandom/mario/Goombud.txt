The '''Goombud''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

== Profile ==
It appears to be a combination between a [[Galoomba]] and a [[Goombrat]]; having leaf-like tufts and lacking eyebrows like the latter and the roundness of the former. It behaves like the [[Galoomba]] but has a different appearance. It replaces the [[Goombrat]] in the ''[[Super Mario World]]'' style, much like how the [[Galoomba]] replaces the Goomba. When the player stomps on the Goombud, it will flip over; the player can pick up the Goombud when it is in this position. They resemble tomatoes than mushrooms, persimmons, and chestnuts. It and the [[Goombo]] are some of the only "versions of a Goomba" that does not have a 3d model.
