'''Match 'Em''' is a gambling [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]], Match 'Em is played at the [[Town Game Room B]], during the [[Losing Streak]] quest, where the player has to help [[Amp (character)|Amp]] win at least one [[Coin]] on his behalf. If done successfully, the player unlocks the minigame.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Match 'Em values.png|thumb|left|200px|The combination values]]
The objective is to try to line up all of the symbols to earn coins. After inserting the desired number of coins, the machine starts, and betting more coins increases the player's chances at being awarded more money. Betting one coin is for the middle row; betting two coins is for that row and the bottom row; betting three coins is for those two rows and the top row; betting four coins is for those three rows and the line of items going right diagonally downwards, and betting five coins is for the four aforementioned lines as well as the line of items going right diagonally upwards. Sometimes, a [[Piranha Plant]] comes out of one of the [[Warp Pipe]]s and changes one of the symbols; it targets only symbols in a row.

The potential combinations and their corresponding coin multiplier values include: [[Koopa Shell]] (×1); two Koopa Shells (×5); three Koopa Shells (×15); three red [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]] shells: (×20); three [[Lakitu's Cloud]]s (×30); three [[Yoshi Egg]]s (×50); three [[Mushroom (item)|Mushrooms]] (×100); three [[Fire Flower]]s (×150); and three [[Super Star]]s (×250).
