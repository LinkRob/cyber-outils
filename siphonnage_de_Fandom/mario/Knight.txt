A '''Knight''' is a soldier enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They take on the appearance of a regular knight wearing an armor, a helmet, and a breastplate. A Knight also has a spike on its helmet.

===Behavior===
Whenever a Knight takes damage, it turns to face [[Wario]] and charges to try and stab him with its spear before calming after a while.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
[[File:Knight WLSML3 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Although a Knight is classified as an enemy, only one is encountered, guarding a room in the final [[level]], [[Course No.40]], making it more of a mini-boss. If Wario touches either a Knight's spear or the spike on its helmet, Wario takes damage. A Knight's vulnerable spot is its back, which Wario has to hit with a [[Dash Attack|Body Slam]] three times to defeat it. If Wario is in his [[Tiny Wario|Small]] form, he can instead damage a Knight by producing a [[10-Coin|10 Gold Coin]] and then throwing it at the Knight. Upon taking a final hit, the Knight's visor breaks, revealing a duck-like face similar to [[D.D.]]'s.
