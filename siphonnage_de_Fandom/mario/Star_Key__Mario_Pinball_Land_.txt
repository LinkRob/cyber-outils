'''Star Keys''' are an item which appear in ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''.

==Description==
A Star Key is a collectible [[key]] dropped by a boss upon being defeated. There are four Star Keys, each being guarded by a boss of each of the first four areas. If the player does not get a [[Game Over]], every Star Key collected subsequently is worth 100,000 more points than the previous one collected.

Aside from rewarding 100,000 [[point]]s, the only gameplay purpose that Star Keys serve is unlocking each of the four [[boss statue]]s in front of [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's castle]]. When all four are unlocked, Mario can access the [[Fiery Stage]].
