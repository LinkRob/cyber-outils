'''Extreme Egg''' is the [[Skillshot]] used by the [[Birdo (species)|Birdos]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
The Extreme Egg has the longest charge time of any Skillshot. Once Extreme Egg is fully charged, Birdo launches a giant [[egg]] containing the soccer ball toward the [[Kritter|Goalie]], whom it passes through, allowing Birdo to score. Other characters can block the Extreme Egg if they are in its path, but they still become stunned in the process.
