'''Chuckola Badge''' is a type of [[badge]] item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Physical description==
In the original version, the Chuckola Badge is said to be designed after a [[Chuckola Fruit]]. However, this distinction is never shown since it, like the other badges, use the same badge icon.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Chuckola Badge is obtained as an item drop from [[Gold Beanie]]s, although only if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] have the [[Game Boy Horror SP]] equipped or use the [[Advanced Swing Bros.]] attack. Equipping the Chuckola Badge increases Mario or Luigi's [[POW]] by 28 (or 32 in the Japanese versions).

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Chuckola Badge increases POW at a lower number of 21, although this is compensated for how it now additionally increases [[BP]] by 12, [[Speed]] by 10, and [['Stache]] by 5. It also has the [[Item Grab]] special effect, which increases the likelihood of enemies dropping items.
