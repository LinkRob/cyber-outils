'''Cage Beasts''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Cage Beasts are large, hairy, brown creatures trapped within a cube-shaped cage. The only parts of their body shown is their legs, arms, and yellow eyes from within their cage.

===Behavior===
Upon being awakened, a Cage Beast sticks its large arms out of its cage and begins to swipe at [[Wario]]. If punched, a Cage Beast becomes angry, breaks its legs through the bottom of its cage, and starts moving around.

==In ''Wario World''==
Cage Beasts are only encountered in [[Wonky Circus]]. While a Cage Beast is walking around, Wario can attack it by punching it a few more times and then use one of his [[Mad Move]]s—either the [[Mega Toss]], [[Piledriver]], or [[Wild Swing-Ding]]—to defeat the Cage Beast.
