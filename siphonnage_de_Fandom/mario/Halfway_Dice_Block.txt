A '''Halfway Dice Block''' is an [[item]] that is purchasable in the [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shop]] of ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. As its name suggests, this item has a maximum dice roll of five, half of numbers of a normal [[Dice Block]], which goes up to ten. Also, in [[Kamek's Library]], when the player lands on a [[Event Space|Green Space]] at the very top of the board, [[Kamek]] appears and replaces the player's items with three Halfway Dice Blocks. It only costs one [[coin]].

The Halfway Dice Block has the same effect as the block used when characters in some ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' games are subject to a [[Cursed Mushroom]], though unlike that one, the Halfway Dice Block cannot be used on other players.
