'''Toad''' is [[Princess Peach|Peach]]'s standard special move in all of her playable appearances in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, from ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' onward. It was also made into [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]'s special move in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Description==
Peach or Daisy pull a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] out of {{w|hammerspace}}, the latter of whom specifically gets out a [[Blue Toad]], to hold out for shielding and counterattacking. In ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'' only, Toad counterattacks by absorbing the attack and returning it to the opponent, while in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' onward, the Toad releases mushroom spores that inflict damage to the opponent. If [[Kirby]] uses [[Inhale]] on either princess, he can use the Toad move.

In ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'', while the move functions identically, Peach and Daisy behave differently while using the move; the princess ducks, covering her ears with a terrified expression, and turns away while Toad independently spreads his arms in front of her on his own. If Toad hits an opponent, he releases a spray of spores while Peach or Daisy winces. As the Move List makes apparent, Peach uses the [[Toad (character)|Toad]] character for her attack, rather than a generic member of the species.
