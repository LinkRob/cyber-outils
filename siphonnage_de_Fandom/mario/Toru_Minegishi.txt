'''Toru Minegishi''' is a game composer and voice actor at Nintendo. He has done sound effects and music for various games in the ''Super Mario'' franchise including ''[[Mario Artist: Talent Studio]]'', ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]'', and ''[[Super Mario 3D World]].'' He is also the voice of [[Petey Piranha]] since ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]].''
