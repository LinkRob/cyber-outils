The '''Magical Typewriter''' is an item which first appears in CD-ROM releases of ''[[Mario Teaches Typing]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Teaches Typing 2]]'', both in which it is the main objective.

== History ==
After defeating a [[Koopa Troopa]], [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] find a typewriter that was owned by them. The typewriter was left with a scroll that says it will be used to destroy the Evil Castle, but if you end up typing horribly on it it will explode. Mario then types on the typewriter, but his spelling seems to have quite a bit of sloppiness to it. The typewriter then explodes — as the scroll said — forcing Mario and Luigi to find the pieces, which leads to Mario learning to type properly. Mario then puts together the typewriter and types the command to crush the Evil Castle, only this time, he uses perfect grammar and spelling, ending up with the castle being crushed by an enlarged typewriter.
