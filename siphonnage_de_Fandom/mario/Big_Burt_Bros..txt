The '''Big Burt Bros.''' are the first boss in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They are encountered in 1-4: [[Castle of the Big Burt Bros.]]. The Big Burt Bros. are large [[Burt]]s, but their boss fight is based on that of [[Burt the Bashful]]'s from ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

== Battle ==
The Big Burt Bros. attempt to defeat [[Yoshi]] by jumping around the screen, and sometimes they jump onto one another To defeat them, Yoshi must throw [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] at them. Yoshi can obtain the eggs from an [[Egg Plant]] that shoots them in the middle. The only way Yoshi can properly dodge their attacks is by hiding in a steep end. Once both Big Burt Bros. are defeated, they both deflate like balloons and disappear, similar to how Burt the Bashful is defeated in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''.
