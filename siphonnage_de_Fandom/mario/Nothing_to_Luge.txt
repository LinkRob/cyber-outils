'''Nothing to Luge''' is a 1-vs-3 minigame in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. It is not listed in the Simple Set in the Gallery.

==Layout==
The minigame take places at a snowy hill in the mountains. There is a wooden sled at the very end.

==Gameplay==
The solo character rides a wooden spoon while the team of three are all riding a wooden shoe. Both sides must try to make it to the bottom of the hill first. They have to navigate through curves, and if a side turns in the opposite direction or either left or right while going down a straight path, their movement is slowed. The solo character has an advantage in that the teammates each have to cooperatively turn left or right.

Whichever side crosses the [[finish line]] at the bottom of the hill first wins. In the unlikely occurrence that both the solo player and the team players finish at the exact same time or nobody reaches the end within five minutes, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Controls==
*{{button|ds|L}}/{{button|ds|R}} Turn left/right at curves respectively
