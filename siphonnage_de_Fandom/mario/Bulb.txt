A '''bulb''' is an object in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Description==
Bulbs are an inflatable type of purple berry that allow [[Luigi]] to to hover and float into the air when they are sucked onto the [[Poltergust 3000]]'s nozzle. Luigi can fly by blowing air into the bulb, which lasts until it pops on a sharp object or until Luigi vacuums it into his Poltergust 3000. Bulbs also have a golden variation, named [[gold bulb]]s, which cause a trail of [[coin]]s to temporarily appear.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
Bulbs are found in various rooms, such as the [[Conservatory (Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon)|Conservatory]] of the [[Haunted Towers]] or the [[Ancient Exhibit]] in the [[Treacherous Mansion]].
