'''World 5-3''' is the third [[level]] of [[World 5 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 5]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the nineteenth level overall in said game.

==Layout==
World 5-3 contains a puzzle the player must solve in order to progress through the level. By stepping onto a [[lift]] at the start of the level, it will begin moving across the area horizontally; when the platform moves past the fourth tree platform, it will loop around to directly after the starting platform. Above the tallest tree platform is a [[Hidden Block]] containing a power-up. To progress through the level, the player needs to bounce off of the two [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s to reach the high [[Warp Pipe]], which leads to an underground area with some [[Coin]]s and a [[Coin Block]] before the exit pipe to the next area.

When the player jumps onto the first tree platform in the next area, [[Bullet Bill]]s will begin firing from off screen. After a [[Scale Lift|scale]] lift, the player has to jump onto a horizontally moving lift below and use a Koopa Paratroopa to reach the next platform. Afterward are three lifts that fall when the player steps onto them, followed by another scale lift and a small platform before the [[Goal Pole]].
