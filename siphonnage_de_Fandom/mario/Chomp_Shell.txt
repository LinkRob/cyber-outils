The '''Chomp Shell''' is a ball-and-chain weapon that [[Bowser]] can use in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the shop in the [[Marrymore]] hotel for 60 [[coin]]s, and can be sold for 30 coins. Equipping the Chomp Shell increases Bowser's [[Attack]] power by 9. In battle, Bowser swings the Chomp Shell around before throwing it at an enemy. 

The Chomp Shell is actually of a [[Chain Chomp]] shell, although the English localization mistakenly refers to it as being a [[Kinklink]]'s instead.
