'''Coin Magnet''' is a [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|badge]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It allows the user to make [[coin]]s come to them when they get close, similarly to [[Baby Wario]]'s [[magnet]].
