'''World 0-2''' is the second board and level of [[World 0 (Mario Party: Star Rush)|World 0]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. The board has detailed features on the board: rocks, grass patches and a flower. This board has no allies; however, the player can use an [[amiibo]]. It introduces [[? Block]]s and [[Shy Guy Shop]]s.

== Spaces ==
=== Boss Spaces ===
*'''1st boss:''' The first boss is in front of one of the five rocks on the middle.
*'''2nd boss:''' The next boss is in the middle between the two elevations.
*'''3rd boss:''' The last boss is behind the four rocks on the second level. It is on the third rock from the left-hand side.
