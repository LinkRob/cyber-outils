'''Banana Grab''' is a [[minigame]] from ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]''. It is accessible from the Challenge menu. The player controls both [[Donkey Kong]]'s hands, using the {{Button|nds|L}} button to move the left hand and using the {{Button|nds|R}} button to move the right. 

The goal of the game is to have Donkey Kong catch as many bananas as possible within 30 seconds. There are various kinds of bananas in this minigame: Banana Bunches, simple [[banana]]s and glowing bananas worth multiple points. Spiked balls will also fall occasionally and if Donkey Kong grabs one, the hand he took it with will be temporarily paralyzed.

This minigame, like the other five in this mode, has three levels, the last two of which are unlocked when the previous level is cleared. Level one finishes when eighty bananas are caught, level two with 100 bananas, and level three with 140. Clearing all three unlocks an endless mode.

==Browser game==
[[File:BananaGrab PC.png|thumb|left|Adobe Flash version]]

An {{wp|Adobe Flash}} version of this minigame was playable on the official ''DK: Jungle Climber'' website as well as the [[Nintendo Arcade]] section on nintendo.com, where it was listed among other promotional games as '''''DK: Jungle Climber -- Banana Grab'''''. The browser version is largely the same as the original minigame, although it does not have a goal score to surpass.
