A '''Red block''' is an object that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
Red blocks are rather common objects that are found throughout the game but are first encountered in [[Sneezemore Cave]]. These blocks are fairly big in size with red flames inside of them. Red blocks cannot be destroyed by normal means and can be only destroyed by [[Wario]] transforming into [[Dragon Wario]] once he finds the [[Guise Gem]] for it. After doing so, Wario can use Dragon Wario's fire breath to destroy the Red blocks. Stronger versions of Red blocks also appear called [[Blue block]]s that can only be destroyed after Dragon Wario's breath has been upgraded.
