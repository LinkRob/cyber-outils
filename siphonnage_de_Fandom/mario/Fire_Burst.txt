A '''Fire Burst''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
To use a Fire Burst, the player must rapidly shake the [[Wii Remote]] up and down to fill the meter. When it is filled, it will deal consecutive damage to all enemies on the screen. However, this will not work on all enemies mainly the ones immune to fire. Fire Bursts can also be used as an ingredient in certain dishes but it can be cooked by itself for a [[Spicy Soup]].
