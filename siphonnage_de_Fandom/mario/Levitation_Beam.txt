The '''Levitation Beam''' is an object in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Reptiles in the Rose Garden]]."

==Description==
The Levitation Beam is a machine owned by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] that can levitate objects, and is especially used for very large objects, such as the [[White House]].

==In other media==
===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
===="Reptiles in the Rose Garden"====
The Levitation Beam is used to lift the White House off the ground so that [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] can take it and the [[Mr. President|President]] back to [[Dark Land]] and allow [[Kootie Pie Koopa]] to rule [[United States of America|America]]. King Koopa later uses the Levitation Beam to position the White House over the [[Mushroom Sea]], where he allows it to sink to the bottom.

After [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] raises the White House from the bottom of the sea, he and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] sneak onto the [[Doom Ship]], where Mario takes control of the Levitation Beam, using it to warp the White House back to [[Washington, D.C.|Washington]]. At first, the White House was accidentally placed on top of the [[Washington Monument]], but is then returned to its proper location. Upon returning the White House, Mario then uses the Levitation Beam to send Kootie Pie back to Dark Land.
