'''Dash Pepper''' is a type of fruit that appears in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]],'' and one of three power-up fruits for [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], along with [[Blimp Fruit|Blimp Fruits]] and [[Bulb Berry|Bulb Berries]]. Upon eating a Dash Pepper, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] becomes [[Dash Yoshi]], allowing him to dash up steep slopes and even across water. This power-up has a time limit, but the player can extend the amount of time by having Yoshi eat another Dash Pepper before the time expires.

== Level appearances ==
* '''[[Hightail Falls Galaxy]]:''' In the mission "[[Hot-Stepping Dash Pepper]]."
* '''[[Starshine Beach Galaxy]]:''' In the mission "[[Surf, Sand, and Silver Stars]]."
* '''[[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]]:'''  In the mission "[[Bowser's Fortified Fortress]]."
