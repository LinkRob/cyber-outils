'''Cheep Chomp Chase''' is the secret level in the [[Acorn Plains]] area in [[New Super Luigi U|''New Super Luigi U'']].

==Layout==
The level begins above a body of water with [[Cheep Cheep]]s, many yellow [[Warp Pipe]]s, and one red Warp Pipe that leads to the underwater area. Above it are two [[Hidden Block]]s, with one containing a [[coin]] and the other containing a [[Starman]]. A [[Cheep Chomp]] attacks from the left side of area. A [[Mega ? Block]] containing a Super Star is found in a section to the left. After this, schools of [[Eep Cheep]]s and [[Cheep Cheep]]s are found. Some L-shaped Warp Pipes that create [[current]]s are found, along with some schools of [[Deep Cheep]]s. Some moving current-producing Warp Pipes are then found, along with a [[Red Ring]]. A section with Warp Pipes rotating in a circle are found, along with [[Mega Cheep Cheep]]s, [[Mega Eep-Cheep]]s, and [[Mega Deep Cheep]]. At the end of the area, a Warp Pipe is found. The Warp Pipe leads to an area that contains two groups of spinning pipes, more Cheep Cheeps, and the [[Goal Pole]].

The player can skip the underwater sections by using the [[P-Acorn]] to fly over the wall at the beginning of the level.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': When entering the underwater world through the pipe, turn left and follow the pipe current to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the trail of the eight red-coins, go between the gap of the yellow pipes above where the second Star Coin will be revealed.
*'''Star Coin 3''': At the same gap, swim upward to a hidden area above water where the third Star Coin is.

==Hidden Luigi image==
On the right side of the yellow pipe that blocks the entry to the finish shows a picture of standard [[Luigi]] swimming from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''
