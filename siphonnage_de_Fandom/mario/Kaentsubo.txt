'''Kaentsubo''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Kaentsubo is a living torch with a blazing flame in its body.

===Behavior===
If [[Wario]] goes near a Kaentsubo, it flips and creates a flame on the ground. If Wario touches the flame, he becomes [[Flaming Wario]].

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
Kaentsubo are only encountered in two levels, [[The Curious Factory]] and [[Hotel Horror]].
