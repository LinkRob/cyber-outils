The '''Pot of Gold Coins''' is an item in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Mighty McMario and the Pot of Gold]]."

==Description==
The Pot of Gold Coins belongs to a leprechaun named [[Murphy]]. The coins in the pot are magical and grant good luck as long as they are stored in a safe place. Should the gold coins get lost or stolen, Murphy's good luck instead turns bad until the pot is retrieved.

==Episode appearances==
==="Mighty McMario and the Pot of Gold"===
The Pot of Gold Coins are stolen by [[King O'Koopa]], and Murphy is struck with bad luck, including a rain storm that slowly begins to flood his home of the [[Shamrock Kingdom]]. [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and his friends eventually help Murphy get his Pot of Gold Coins back, bringing good luck back to him and ending the rain.
