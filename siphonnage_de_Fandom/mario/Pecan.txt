'''Pecans''' are pelican enemies associated with the [[Black Sugar Gang]] in the {{series|Wario Land}}. They first appear in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and return in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==In {{series|Wario Land}}==
===''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''===
Pecans only appear in the water, where they spit mines out of their beaks.

===''Wario Land II''===
Pecans return as enemies, and they can now be found in land areas, spitting out spiky fish.
