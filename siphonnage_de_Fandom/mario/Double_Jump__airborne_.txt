A '''Double Jump''' is a type of move which rarely appears in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] but appears throughout the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. The move makes its earliest ''Mario''-related appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]].

==Description==
A Double Jump involves a character being able to perform an extra [[jump]] while in midair. While it is called a Double Jump, most appearances featuring the move allow the character to perform a jump while in midair in general, such as after running off a platform.

== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
[[Princess Daisy]] has the ability to do the Double Jump in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. This can be performed if Daisy is stalling in the air and the player taps on the screen.

== In {{Series|Super Smash Bros.}} ==
All playable fighters can double jump in every series.

Daisy is knowon to have the ability to double jump in ''Super Mario Run''. However, in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', since all fighters can double jump, it is not a unique ability. Instead, she also gains the [[float]]ing ability like Peach as her echo fighter.
