'''Painted Pipeworks '''is a level found in [[Soda Jungle]] in the game ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Layout==
The level starts in a jungle near three [[Piranha Plant]]s, two of which are in moving [[Warp Pipe]]s. A [[? Block]] is also found. Some Warp Pipes are found moving in and out of [[Poisoned Water]], with a [[Red Ring]] found above. A Piranha Plant is found in one of the Warp Pipes. A stationary blue Warp Pipe is found, followed by a group of four rotating pipes. Some moving Warp Pipes are found above the poison, some of which contain Piranha Plants. Some stationary pipes are found, along with two swinging ones with Piranha Plants in them. The player then arrives in an area with the [[Goal Pole]] in sight.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Wait at the tip of the second blue pipe for the Star Coin. When you see it, wait for the plant to go in the pipe (unless in Fire, Ice, Penguin form, or Nabbit)
*'''Star Coin 2''': Avoid the fifth Piranha Plant and jump to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Avoid the last Piranha Plant and jump towards a red pipe below to get the coin and wall-jump away from the poison.

==Hidden Luigi image==
After taking the pipe leading to the secret exit, see a portrait of modern Luigi.

==Secret Exit==
Jump on the last swaying blue pipe to use it as a platform to reach to the red pipe above it to reach to the secret area. The path leads out of the haunted area if found or else it'll lead a path back to the last two levels of the area.
