The '''Shy Squad''' is a specific group of [[Shy Guy]]s that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. They are only featured in the battle with [[General Guy]], where they attack [[Mario]] per General Guy's commands.

==Battle==
In battle, the Shy Squad mainly attacks by charging at Mario, and five Shy Guys will hit him for only 1 HP of damage each. This can be easily guarded against a well-timed [[Power Bounce]], and [[Lady Bow]]'s [[Fan Smack]] will take the Shy Squad in a few turns.
