'''Mountain Mountain''' is one of the [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', appearing under [[Jimmy T]]'s [[Sports]] folder.

==Gameplay==
To beat the microgame you must jump on clouds to reach the top of a mountain. 

===Difficulty levels===
*Level 1: Clouds are stationary, making it easy to jump on them.
*Level 2: Clouds will move left to right a bit, making it a little harder.
*Level 3: Cloud will move left to right faster, and the clouds will be further apart, making it harder.
