'''Horses''' are real-world animals that have sometimes appeared in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise, their first time being in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Great Gladiator Gig]]." While horses are the main steed in the [[Earth|real world]], this role goes to [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] in the ''Mario'' games.

==Game appearances==
In ''[[Mobile Golf]]'', a character named [[Bean (character)|Bean]] is seen riding a horse in his artwork, something that he does not do in-game.

The first time horses actually appear within a ''Mario'' game is in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', within the [[microgame]] [[Rump Roast]]. In it, the player has to tap the horse on the Touch Screen enough times so that it gains enough speed to win the race.

Horses prominently appear in the crossover ''[[Mario & Sonic]]'' series, in which characters ride them in [[Equestrian]] events. Their first appearance in this series is ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' for the [[Wii]]. Here, the player can have their character move on their horse by shaking the [[Wii Remote]].

The Equestrian event would later carry over into the ''Mario'' franchise itself in ''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]'', in which the Horses have more cartoony facial features than in ''Mario & Sonic'' series.
