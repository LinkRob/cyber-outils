'''Sky Dive''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] of [[Koopa Paratroopa]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Paratroopa flies into the air, dives toward the ball, and returns it to the opponent. There is a chance of Paratroopa landing headfirst into the ground, briefly resulting in his head becoming stuck.
