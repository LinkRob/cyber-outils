|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Hammer Time''' is a special move used only by the [[Hammer Bro.|Hammer Bros.]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Hammer Time is used, three hammers will fall upon the target.

==Effects==
With Hammer Time, the Hammer Bro. is able to hurt it's target (in this case, [[Mario]] since he is the only one in the party when this attack is shown) for medium damage. Fortunately, this attack doesn't pose much of a threat and can be blocked against to minimize the damage.
