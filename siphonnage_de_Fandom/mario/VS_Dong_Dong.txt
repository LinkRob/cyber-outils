'''VS Dong Dong''' is an unlockable [[minigame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. It is unlocked after clearing at least one of either [[Orbulon]]'s, [[Dr. Crygor]]'s, or [[Kat and Ana]]'s stage.

==Gameplay==
VS Dong Dong is one of the four two-player minigames played on a single system, with the first player using {{button|gba|L}} and the second player using {{button|gba|R}}. The first player plays as [[Mona]] while the second player plays as [[Dribble and Spitz|Dribble]], on the left and right sides respectively. Mona and Dribble are separated by a gray column in the center with three yellow blocks, and both characters come with an vertically moving bar, colored red for Mona's side and blue for Dribble's side.

Whenever a player presses their corresponding button, their bar moves to the center. If the bar is in line a yellow block at the moment the player presses their button, the bar pushes the block a bit forward to the side of the opponent, then the bar turns back and resumes its vertical movement. Fully pushing a block to the other character's side requires at least three pushes, and accomplishing this causes the block to fall down and squash the opponent's character, earning their opponent a star. A character can undo one push their opponent did to the block by pushing the block again with their bar. Whoever earns three stars is the winner.
