'''Weapon World''' (previously called '''Smithy Factory'''<ref>Pelland, Scott, and Kent Miller. ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' Player's Guide. Page 95.</ref> and also known as '''The Factory''' or '''Gate''') is the last area from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is accessed after defeating [[Exor]], who will then suck Mario into a portal between Mario's World and Weapon World. In the game, the area is simply called "Factory". As the name implies, [[Smithy]], the final boss of the game, is located here.
