'''Daniel Falcone''' (born August 5, 1984) is an American actor. He is best known for voicing Roy Koopa since [[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]. He was the only one of the voice actors for the [[Koopalings]] who was not replaced in ''[[Mario Kart 8]].''
