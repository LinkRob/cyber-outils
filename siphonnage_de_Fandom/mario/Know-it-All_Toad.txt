|desc=Gives [[Mario]] hints about what [[Things]] will help him on his adventures.}}The '''Know-it-All Toad''' is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. This toad gives [[Mario]] hints about what [[Things]] will help him at the moment, and can be talked to throughout the entire game. Before [[Mario]] enters [[The Crimson Tower]] and when he gets a [[Game Over]] from a [[Koopalings|Koopaling's]] special move, Huey will tell [[Mario]] to speak with this toad for guidance.

== Location ==
[[File:PMCS Know-it-All Toad in can.png|left|frameless|117x117px]]He can be found in a trash can in the harbor district of [[Port Prisma]]. When [[Mario]] approaches the can, he pops up and can be talked to.
