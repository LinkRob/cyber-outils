A '''Gritty Goomba''' is a type of [[Goomba]] that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its remake, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Gritty Goombas are found in [[Teehee Valley]], occasionally appearing with [[Spiky Snifit]]s. They appear to be swathed in cloth, resembling a mummy, and they don a pair of sunglasses, with a spear and colored flag protruding from their head.

During the events of the game, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must protect [[Princess Peach]] from these Goombas as she crosses Teehee Valley to get to [[Little Fungitown]]. If they let one get too close, or else leave her offscreen too long, a Gritty Goomba grabs the Princess and carries her down a yellow [[Warp Pipe]] leading to their hideouts in the underground ruins, which Mario and Luigi must enter to save Peach. They are mysteriously replaced by [[Anuboo]]s after gaining the first piece of the [[Beanstar]] but still appear in battles, the Gritty Goombas in the underground ruins are the only ones that remain in the overworld after this point of the game. Additionally, when [[Trunkle]] uses his vacuum breath, Gritty Goombas are one of the things that can be pulled in.

Gritty Goombas have a stronger variant named [[Elite Gritty Goomba]]s, which were also named Gritty Goombas in the original [[Game Boy Advance]] version. Elite Gritty Goombas resemble Gritty Goombas, except their bandages are brown and they have a green flag wrapped around their spike. 


==Battle==
Gritty Goombas attack by ramming into the Mario Bros., similar to normal Goombas; however, they will summon sandstorms and use them as a cover in order to obscure which brother they are targeting. The Bros. must counter the attack with their [[Hammer]]s.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', Gritty Goombas appear as enemies in ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser''. They are melee troopers, and attack by charging into minions. Their [[Special Skill (Mario & Luigi series)|Special Skill]] is Rocket Headbutt, which allows them to jump headfirst into a minion. Due to the spear on their heads, attacking them from above damages minions.
