'''Cramalot Castle''' is a location in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[King Mario of Cramalot]]."

==Description==
Cramalot Castle is a large, light blue castle with purple, cone-shaped roofs. It is located on a hill above the village of [[Cramalot]]. Cramalot Castle's design makes it appear as if the upper part is leaning right. Cramalot Castle has typical features of a castle, including a throne room, a dining room, and a drawbridge. It has a dungeon where the ceiling is covered in [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]] as well as holes in the wall where water can pump in from. The spiked ceiling has the ability to lower.

Cramalot Castle is surrounded by a moat with several [[Trouter]]s and the [[Forest of Perpetual Night and Terrible Dangers]].
