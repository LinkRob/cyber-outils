The '''Tasty Melon'''<ref>''Donkey Kong Barrel Blast'' instruction booklet, page 17.</ref> is an item in ''[[Donkey Kong Barrel Blast]]''.

==Description==
A Tasty Melon can be obtained from a [[Balloon (Donkey Kong Barrel Blast)|red balloon]]. When used, it makes the player temporarily invincible, much like the [[Super Star|Star]] from the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series, but unlike the Star, touching a player while invincible does not hurt them. The effects of the Tasty Melon last longer for the [[Kong]]s than the [[Kremling]]s.
