'''W Emblem''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Zero [[Badge Point|BP]] are required for [[Mario]] to wear the W Emblem. This badge gives him [[Wario]]'s clothing, including a yellow hat and shirt with purple overalls and green shoes. If the W Emblem is worn with [[L Emblem]], Mario instead wears [[Waluigi]]'s clothing, including a purple hat and shirt with black overalls and orange shoes.

The W Emblem can be obtained by purchasing it from [[Charlieton]] in [[Rogueport]] for 360 [[coin]]s.
