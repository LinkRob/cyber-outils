The '''Yummy Meal''' is an item and a [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Yummy Meals can be created in numerous ways but the simplest is by having [[Tayce T.]] cook a [[Whacka Bump]]. However, Yummy Meals restore 20 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 20 [[Flower Point|FP]] while the Whacka Bumps restore 25 HP and 25 FP, so it's recommended using two other items to create a Yummy Meal. [[Ultra Shroom]]s are the most common ingredient for a Yummy Meal. It is recommended using another item as Ultra Shrooms restore 50 HP alone, so the best item to use is the [[Iced Potato]] since it alone restores 5 HP.
