'''Dark Mechawful.5''' is an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. They are only encountered in [[Peach's Castle]], and a Dark Mechawful.5 is created from a [[Dark Mechawful]]'s head as a new body if it has been ejected from its former body and left on its own for a few turns.
