'''Swirly Spinners''' are an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
A Swirly Spinner is a yellow disk depicting a row of red arrows pointed either left or right. Swirly Spinners are always found partially inside walls.

To activate a Swirly Spinner, [[Wario]] has to perform a [[Wild Swing-Ding]] either clockwise or counterclockwise, depending on if the arrows are pointed right or left respectively. When Wario activates a Swirly Spinner, a nearby gate is opens temporarily, so Wario has only a short time to go through the gate and continue further into the course.
