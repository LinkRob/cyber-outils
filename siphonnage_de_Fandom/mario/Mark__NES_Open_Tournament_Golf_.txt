'''Mark''' is a character in ''[[NES Open Tournament Golf]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mark is a kid with black hair and blue eyes. He wears a blue baseball uniform, the back of which has a large "1," and a matching blue hat.

==In ''NES Open Tournament Golf''==
[[File:NESOpenTournament-Mark.png|thumb|left|200px|A screenshot of Mark playing golf in ''NES Open Tournament Golf'']]
Like with [[Steve (NES Open Tournament Golf)|Steve]], Mark exclusively appears in the North American version. He is an amateur-ranked golfer who can be challenged in match play.
