'''Pair-a-Gone''' is one of [[Luigi]]'s [[minigame]]s in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and a Table minigame in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. In the former game, there is an endless variation named [[Pair-a-Gone and On]]. 

==Gameplay==
The minigame features identical gameplay to {{w|Nestor (solitaire)|Nestor}}. There are fifty cards, each depicting a certain type of icon, and the player must match any two cards adjacent to each other either horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. To earn [[coin]]s, the player has to match all of the cards in the set, which also earns them a Star. If the player is unable to match all of the pairs, they lose a coin for each remaining card and a Star.

There are a total of ten card types, six of which are available from the start and four of which are added as the minigame progresses. The six starter types include [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]], [[Fire Flower]], [[Starman|Star]], and [[Lakitu's Cloud|Cloud]]. The four types added later on include [[Goomba]], [[Bowser]], [[Boo]], and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].

==Trivia==
*The name Pair-a-Gone not only appears to mention the minigame's base objective, but it could also be a pun on "paragon."
