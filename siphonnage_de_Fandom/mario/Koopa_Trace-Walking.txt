'''Koopa Trace-Walking''' is a minigame from ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It appears in the [[Sand Kingdom]], the [[Snow Kingdom]], and the [[Moon Kingdom]]. As its name implies, this minigame's objective has the contestants follow a set path. The more accurate their path is, the more points the contestants earn. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] has to earn above a certain score (80 the first time and 90 the second time) to complete certain objectives.

The [[Koopa Trace-Walking Host|host]] is a [[Koopa Troopa]]. If he is approached by an enemy, regardless whether [[Mario (character)|Mario]] is [[Capture|possessing]] the creature in question or not, the host will cower and the minigame becomes unplayable until the enemy either leaves the area, or is killed/destroyed.
