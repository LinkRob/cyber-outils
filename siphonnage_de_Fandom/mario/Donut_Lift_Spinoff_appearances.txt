==''Mario'' game appearances==
===''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''===
[[Image:Donut Lift Hop.gif|thumb|Mario hops the Donut Blocks in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'']] 
In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', they are named '''Donut Blocks''' and have a 3D isometric appearance. In this game, Donut Blocks can be found during various platforming sections, whenever [[Mario]] stands on one, it will descend downward, requiring the heroic plumber to be quick about his movements, they appear less commonly towards the end of the game however.
{{br|left}}
===''Mario & Luigi'' series===
====''Mario & Luigi Superstar Saga''====
[[File:Guffawha Ruins Donut Lifts.png|thumb|left|Luigi hops across the Donut Lifts.]]
Donut Lifts appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', where they are only found in [[Guffawha Ruins]]. They appear when [[Luigi]] hits the [[! Block]], rising to the staircase's level and allowing Luigi to hop across the Donut Lifts. The Donut Lifts fall as Luigi steps on them, so he must be quick while hopping across. If Luigi slips and falls, must hit the ! Block again to reattempt the challenge.

In [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|the remake]], the Donut Lifts are replaced with [[Guffawha Lift]]s, which function similarly.
{{br}}
====''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''====
[[File:Donut Lifts Flab Zone.png|thumb|right|Donut Lifts in the Flab Zone in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''.]]
[[File:Donut Lifts Flab Zone DX.png|thumb|left|Donut Lifts in the Flab Zone in the remake.]]
Donut Lifts make a brief appearance in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' as well as ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. They appear in only one area: the [[Flab Zone]] inside [[Bowser's body]].

When [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are chasing [[Alpha Kretin]] (who has kidnapped [[Princess Peach]]), Luigi accidentally stands on the Donut Lifts and falls into a pit of [[mucus]], but he is pulled out by [[Princess Lipid]] and returned to Mario. She also gives them some [[Badge]]s to use in battle against enemies.
{{br}}
====''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''====
[[File:Dreamy Donut Lifts.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] and [[Dreamy Luigi]] below Donut Lifts.]]
Donut Lifts are seen again in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' appearing in area B of [[Dreamy Neo Bowser Castle]]. In this game, they are much slower than they are in most games as they shake and descend much slower than usually.  They retain their colors from the ''New Super Mario Bros.'' games, but are much thinner, more stretched and rounder. They function similarly to ''Super Mario 3D Land'', where, once they are stepped on, they turn red and fall after a short time. Donut Lifts are often used for climbing across platforms and reaching higher ledges.
{{br}}
===''Super Princess Peach''===
[[File:Ladida Plains 1-3.png|thumb|Appearance in [[Ladida Plains 1-3]] of ''Super Princess Peach'']]
[[Image:Peach Donut Lift House.png|thumb|left|Peach hopping across Donut Lifts in ''Super Princess Peach''.]]
Donut Lifts are seen again in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', where they appear in several levels. Whenever [[Princess Peach|Peach]] stands on a Donut Lift, it shakes, and if she is on a Donut Lift for too long, it falls quite rapidly. Donut Lifts take slightly longer to descend than in the ''Super Mario'' series. Donut Lifts can respawn if Peach leaves and re-enters the area.

The game also introduces blue variants called [[Blue Donut Lift]]s, which fall immediately when stood on, requiring Peach to use her [[Gloom]] [[Vibe]] to quickly move across them.
{{br}}

===''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''===
[[File:Toad's Donut Lift Run.png|thumb|Captain Toad running across the Donut Lifts in ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''.]]
Donut Lifts appear in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' and its Nintendo Switch and Nintendo 3DS [[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker (Nintendo Switch / Nintendo 3DS)|ports]]. They can be found in several levels, typically those that take place in the sky. Donut Lifts retain their appearance and function from ''Super Mario 3D World''. When [[Captain Toad]] or [[Toadette]] stands on a Donut Lift, it will turn red and then start to descend. The [[Toad (species)|Toads]] cannot [[jump]], so they must keep running across the Donut Lifts until they reach safe ground.

==In other media==
===''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''===
[[Image:Princess Peach Donut Lift Slide Movie.png|thumb|left|Princess Peach sliding along the Donut Lifts with swift agility.]]
[[Image:Mario Donut Lift Fall.png|thumb|right|Mario celebrates too early and falls from a Donut Lift.]]
Donut Lifts make a return once again in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' where they can be seen as one of the many different types of platforms found on the [[Training Course (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Training Course]]. Here, they can be seen as one of the last obstacles/platforms found on the course. When [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]] makes a demonstration on the course to show [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] how it's done, she eventually reaches a mass of Donut Lifts and manages to run across them without an issue as they fall. Mario eventually tries it out for himself, he initially doesn't have much luck at one point getting too cocky after clearing a set of [[Lift]]s without realizing he's standing on a Donut Lift as it falls with him standing on it. On another attempt, Mario runs into a Donut Lift from the side after a mistimed jump. Mario eventually gets the hang of it and runs across each of the Donut Lifts without falling thus joining Princess Peach and [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]] on the adventure to stop [[Bowser (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Bowser]]. In this movie, the Donut Lifts retain their mainstream 3D-aligned appearance used since ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' but with outer metal layers as well.
