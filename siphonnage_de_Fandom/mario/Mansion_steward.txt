The '''Mansion Steward''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

== Profile ==
The mansion steward is a Paper Magenta Toad. He also speaks in a very polite manner.

== Biography ==
Mario and [[Kersti]] found the mansion steward sitting outside of his mansion shivering in the cold. He told Mario that many "[[Boo|terrors]]" have been released into his mansion from the [[Book of Sealing]]. He requested [[Mario]] to help him and gave him the Book and Mario heads inside. As [[Mario]] was searching for the Boos, he exited the Mansion and found the Mansion Steward by a fire, the Steward stated that he was feeling chills up his spine and they won't go away (even by the fire), thinking quickly, [[Mario]] puts out the fire with his [[Hammer]] and the Boo then reveals itself, [[Mario]] dispatches of the Boo and the Steward's chills then recede, after this the Steward will tell [[Mario]] how many Boos are left hiding (as well as potential locations) but he won't get the exact number until most of them are caught. Mario saw the Mansion Steward after he got rid of all of the Boos and gracefully thanked Mario for his help; he allowed him to return anytime.

Mario saw the mansion steward after he completed [[Whiteout Valley]]. Mario talked with the original owner of the mansion and then the Mansion Steward handed Mario a [[HP-Up Heart]].
