'''Snaking Above Mist Valley''' is the eighth level of [[Meringue Clouds]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Spinning Spirit House]], [[Bouncy Cloud Boomerangs]], or [[A Quick Dip in the Sky]], and its own completion unlocks [[Ludwig's Clockwork Castle]].

== Layout ==
Like [[Bouncy Cloud Boomerangs]], the clouds are pinkish-peach instead of white. The level begins near a [[Snake Block]] platform and a [[Foo]] on a row of [[? Block]]s. The Snake Block acts as the main part of the ground in the section, and the level immediately begins to autoscroll when it is touched. [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]]s and Foos are encountered in the area. A [[P Switch]] is found, and a section of ground follows. More Paratroopas and Foos then follow. A [[Pipe Cannon]] leads the player to the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. Another Snake Block is found, which continues above a pit. A Paratroopa, a [[Fire Bro]], and a Foo can be found. A [[Green Ring]] is located beneath a section of ground. Another Foo is found, along with a section with Paratroopas and clouds that cover portions of the level. Fire Bros and Foos are found near the end of the area, where a Warp Pipe is found. The [[Goal Pole]] is found.

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The player should activate a [[P Switch]] to reveal [[Blue Coin]]s and turn the [[Brick Block]]s barricading the first Star Coin into [[Coin]]s.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is found in plain sight. The Snake Blocks or [[Super Acorn]]/[[Peachette]] can help the player reach it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In the second area, the third Star Coin is in plain sight. However, the Snake Block goes above it. The player should either use the Super Acorn or Peachette, or throw a [[Koopa Shell]] to collect it for the player.
