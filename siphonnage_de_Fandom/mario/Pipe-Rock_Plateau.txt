'''Pipe-Rock Plateau''' is the first [[world]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It contains 35 [[Wonder Seed|Wonder Seeds]], 11 levels, and 8 Challenges. This world has 3 secret exits, 3 secret [[Captain Toad]] locations, 3 [[Badge Challenge|badge challenges]], 1 [[Wiggler Race|wiggler race]], 1 [[KO Arena|KO arena]], and 3 [[Break Time!|break time stages]].
