The '''Boomerang Ball''' is the [[Star Skill]] of [[Diddy Kong]] in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''. It turns the baseball into a [[banana]] that curves wildly while either pitching or by batting it into the field. In Challenge Mode, Diddy cannot use Boomerang Ball until the [[Chimp Banana]] has been purchased.
