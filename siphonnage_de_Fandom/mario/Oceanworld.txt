'''Oceanworld''' is a one-time location in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[20,000 Koopas Under the Sea]]."

==Description==
It is a large, water-filled location that has a port town titled the [[Happy Harbor]], which is inhabited by [[Toad (species)|Mushroom People]].
