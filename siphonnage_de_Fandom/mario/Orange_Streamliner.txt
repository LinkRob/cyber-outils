The '''Orange Streamliner''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The kart is an orange and teal variation of the [[Streamliner]]. It has the Mario Motors, PTT and Princess Orange sponsors embroidered on the kart. It is equipped with the [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. The livery of the kart seems to be inspired on the [https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazda_787B Mazda 787B].

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Orange Streamliner is a High-End kart. It increases the duration of [[Rocket Start]] speed boosts and the points given for performing one.
