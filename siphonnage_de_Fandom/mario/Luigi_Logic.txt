'''Luigi Logic''' is a special power that [[Luigi]] gains in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. With this power, he uses his unique mind to think up beneficial solutions, allowing him and [[Mario (character)|Mario]] to solve certain puzzles. It can also be used in boss battles to interact with a background element.

== Instances ==

* The first instance of Luigi Logic happens when the Bros. are confronted by a gate and Mario activates a switch on one side to open gaps. Luigi squeezes through the gap to get to the other side and hits the switch on that side to open it.
* In the field, there are certain areas where Luigi can automatically perform solo actions such as uprooting Plug Radishes, break boxes with hammer strikes, and collect [[Sprite Bulb|Sprite Bulbs]].
* Luigi Logic is also used in certain boss fights.
** [[Gorumbla]]: After the Gorumbla performs his stone rain move, there will be a stone on top of a statue that Luigi can dislodge. When he does so, he will send it flying at the Gorumbla, who will send it at Mario. Mario must use his hammer to stop the stone, after which Luigi uses his own hammer to send it at the Gorumbla, [[Dizzy|stunning]] him. The Gorumbla will take increased damage during this timeframe. This also applies to his [[Glohm]]-contaminated form.
** [[Gobblick]]: There is a vent to the north of the arena where this boss is fought, and when it is active, he can be grabbed by his tongue by Luigi, who must throw him into the vents to stun him. This will weaken the Gobblick's defense stat for several turns.
** [[Snaptor|Mega Snaptor]]: Mega Snaptors can send tornadoes at the Bros. with one of their attacks, though in the battle at Lottacoins Island, this causes the water pipes to rotate. The key to the Luigi Logic here has Luigi fix the pipes with his hammer, after which he activates the valve, sending a torrent of water that stuns the Mega Snaptor and forces the creature to the ground. In this state, the Mega Snaptor's defense is weakened.
** [[Sharpcask]]: After he drops to 75% HP, the Sharpcask retreats into his shell. Luigi can manipulate the conveyor belt to position the creature under some falling crates to stun him. As with the first three examples, the Sharpcask's defense lowers until his stun wears off.
** [[Pipegunk]]: [[Extension Corps|Shun]] uses his remote to move the arena to a lava chamber, strengthening the Pipegunk. Luigi can attack Shun for the remote, then shift the arena to the icy chamber. This done, the Pipegunk will be stunned and suffer a penalty to defense, though it will eventually acclimate, so Luigi will have to shift the arena back to the lava chamber.
** [[Glohm Bowser]]: [[Bowser]] does not have this during his first battle, but in the second fight with Glohm Bowser controlling the Koopa King's body, a [[Shy Guy Airtub]] will sometimes fly in. Luigi can use his Luigi Logic to hijack the Airtub and bring Mario aboard. This leads to a shmup segment where the Bros. attack Bowser with their vehicle as the Koopa King attacks with fireballs and punches. Bowser must be stunned to use this, as if Luigi tries to use the Airtub, the Koopa King will attack it, cancelling the Luigi Logic.
** [[Reclusa]]: There is a mandatory Luigi Logic segment where [[Snoutlet]] creates a plug craft upon being freed from a [[Doom Bloom]] and the Bros. ride up Weeping Reclusa's trunk to enter the beast's maw. This is similar to the first phase, but the obstacles are thorny walls rather than Doom Blooms, though Reclusa still attacks with fireballs and ice balls. Near the end, Reclusa fires a ball of dark energy, which the Bros. can repel with the Bro Bomb. Failure results in the second phase having to be started from the beginning, but if the Bros. repel the dark energy ball, the Weeping Core phase starts.
