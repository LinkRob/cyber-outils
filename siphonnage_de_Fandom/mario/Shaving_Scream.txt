'''Shaving Scream''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. It is one of [[Wario]]'s games. Its name is a pun on "shaving cream". Like [[Cutting It Close]] from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'', the gameplay of Shaving Scream features shaving hair from a man's face, in this case Wario's.

==Gameplay==
In Shaving Scream, the player will have to use the [[Wii Remote]] pointer and shave Wario's face. Each level of difficulty involves the player shaving off more and more hair. After all of Wario's facial hair is cut off, he will blush.

*'''1st level difficulty''': Wario has a sideburn or a mustache, which have a little hair.
*'''2nd level difficulty''': Wario has a beard or sideburns, which have more hair.
*'''3rd level difficulty''': Wario has sideburns and a beard or a circle as his beard, which have a lot of hair.
