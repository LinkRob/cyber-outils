'''Azalea''' is a female human character and an experienced golfer featured as a playable character in some of the earlier [[Mario Golf (series)|''Mario Golf'' series]] games. Azalea made her first appearance in ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]], although her technical debut is in ''[[Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]], where she can only be transferred into via using the [[Transfer Pak]]. She also appears in ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]''. Azalea is a playable character in all three of these appearances. She is the champion of the [[Dunes Course]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Azalea wears an orange shirt with white sleeves, yellow shorts, orange sneakers, white socks, and a visor (replaced with a brown hat in ''Mario Golf: Advance Tour''). Azalea's eyes are blue, and she has burnt orange hair.
