'''Seesaw Shrooms''' is the second level of [[Meringue Clouds]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Land of Flying Blocks]] and its own completion opens the path to [[Slide Lift Tower]].

==Overview==
Like [[Mushroom Heights]], this course is an athletic course with many mushroom platforms; these include stationary platforms, expanding/shrinking ones, and seesaw mushrooms similar to the ones seen in an [[Acorn Plains]] course not seen in the final game. [[Yoshi]] makes an appearance in this course as well.

==Walkthrough==
Like [[Mushroom Heights]], this course is an athletic course with many [[Mushroom Platform]]s. The level begins with a row of [[Seesaw Shroom]]s, as well as a [[Fuzzy]]. A [[? Block]] containing a [[Yoshi's Egg]] is found above the third Seesaw Shroom. Some Mushroom Platforms follow, and [[berry|berries]] can be found on bushes. More Seesaw Shrooms are found, along with a Fuzzy and some [[Paragoomba]]s. [[Warp Pipe]]s containing [[Piranha Plant]]s are between some of the Seesaw Shrooms. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] is then found. A row of Mushroom Platforms follows, and [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Spiny|Spinies]], and Fuzzies are encountered. A [[Lakitu]] also appears in this section of the level. Some Seesaw Shrooms are located above Mushroom Platforms. A section containing [[Stretch Shroom]]s and Fuzzies on tracks leads up to a final Seesaw Shroom and the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
* '''Star Coin 1''': Underneath a row of Brick Blocks. Yoshi's Flutter Jump is required to reach it.
* '''Star Coin 2''': In the middle of a group of spinning coins. Lakitu's Cloud is required to reach it.
* '''Star Coin 3''': Above a shrinking/expanding mushroom is a green Warp Pipe that leads to the area with the third Star Coin. The player needs either Yoshi's flutter jump or a Super Acorn to reach the pipe and the coin.
