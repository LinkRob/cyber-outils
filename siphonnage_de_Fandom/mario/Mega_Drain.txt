|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Mega Drain''' is a special move used by a few enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Mega Drain is used, a pinkish beam is shown on the screen.

==Effects==
With Mega Drain, the user is able to inflict a fair amount of damage to one of Mario's party. Wearing certain items like the Safety Badge will cut the damage in half and wearing a Lazy Shell cancels the attack out altogether.
