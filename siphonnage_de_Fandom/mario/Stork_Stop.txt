'''Stork Stops''' are an object featured in several [[level]]s in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can stop on one to alternate between [[Baby Mario]] or another baby he has encountered, the earliest being [[Baby Peach]]. The Yoshis are often required to use Stork Stops to make use of a baby's special abilities during a part of a level.
