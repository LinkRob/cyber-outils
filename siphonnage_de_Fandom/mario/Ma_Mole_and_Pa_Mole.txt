'''Ma'Mole and Pa'Mole''' are mole characters who appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==History==
Ma'Mole and Pa'Mole live in [[Moleville]] with their children, [[Dyna and Mite]]. As [[Mario]] and the others make it into Moleville, Ma'Mole and Pa'Mole were frantically trying to get Dyna and Mite back as they were lost in [[Moleville Mines]]. Ma'Mole stays at home while Pa'Mole tries to dig his way inside. He was able to get some distance in but was unable to get further because of the entrance being too high. Fortunately for him, Mario is able to easily get up there and later rescues Dyna and Mite.
