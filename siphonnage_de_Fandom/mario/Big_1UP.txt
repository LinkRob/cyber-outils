'''Big 1UP''', titled '''Big 1-Up''' in British English releases, is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. This sticker is extremely rare and can only be found in one level, [[Drybake Desert]].

==Description==
The Big 1-UP sticker is colored very similarly to [[1-Up Mushroom]]s. However, instead of reviving [[Mario]], the Big 1UP gradually heals his [[HP]] over turns instead.
