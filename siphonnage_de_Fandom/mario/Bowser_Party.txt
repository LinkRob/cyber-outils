''Bowser Party'' is a mode in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

== Description ==
It can be played with five players. While the characters try to get to the other side of the board to get a [[Star]], The person who has the [[Wii U]] Pad plays [[Bowser]], who tries to stop them from achieving their goal. [[Haunted Trail]] and [[Airship Central]] are not included with the mode.
