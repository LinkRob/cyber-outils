The 2.5 Main Waves [[Jakks Pacific]] figures line debuted in 2014 and is sold in waves at a variety of different stores, such as Walmart, Target, Khols, ToysRus and GameStop. Product releases include a figure alongside an item.

Note: This page does contain other franchises such as Zelda, Splatoon Pikmin etc...

If you want to see every figure of the mario 2.5 please checkout [[Jakks 2.5 figures]]
