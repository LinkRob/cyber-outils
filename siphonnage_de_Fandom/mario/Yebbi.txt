}}'''Yebbi''' is a character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. Yebbi is one of the sages of [[The Overthere]] and is the most cowardly and cautious of them.

==History==
During [[Bonechill]]'s attack of The Overthere, Yebbi has locked himself inside of a restroom refusing to come out due to the monsters around. When [[Mario]] and the other heroes knock on the door, Yebbi refuses to come out thinking they're one of the monsters. They must reply they are monsters which will have Yebbi come out saying a real monster won't admit to being a monster. Even then, he refuses to give up his [[Orb (Super Paper Mario)|Yellow Orb]]. [[Luvbi]] then threatened to tell the others that he hid in the restroom forcing Yebbi to give up his orb.
