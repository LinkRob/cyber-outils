'''Giddy Sky '''is the seventh world of [[Super Princess Peach|''Super Princess Peach'']]''. ''It is an aerial, sky-themed world located in [[Vibe Island]]. It's a world which takes place high in the skies of Vibe Island, there are many clouds, Balloons, and spinning platforms which are used to traverse the world. The inhabitants of this world are [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopas]] and [[Amazing Flyin' Hammer Bro.|Amazing Flyin' Hammer Bros.]]. 

The boss this world  is [[Giant Kamek]]. Additionally Giant Kamek doesn't kidnap a Toad but [[Luigi]].
