The '''Attack Piece Blocks''' are a type of [[block]] in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

==Description==
Attack Piece Blocks resemble pieces of a puzzle joined together to form a block, and a white uppercase "A" is shown on them. Whenever [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] jump at one, they obtain an [[Attack Piece]], ten of which allow them to learn a [[Bros. Attack|Special Attack]]. Not all Attack Pieces are obtained from an Attack Piece Block.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story / Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''===
[[File:Attack Piece Block M&LBIS sprite.png|frame|left]]
Attack Piece Blocks first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and later go on to appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. In these games, can be found both throughout [[Bowser's body]] and in the overworld. The Attack Pieces in Bowser's body have a two-dimensional appearance while the ones outside of him are three-dimensional. [[Bowser]] cannot activate an Attack Piece Block, only Mario or Luigi can.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''===
Attack Piece Blocks return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' in an identical fashion, except instead of [[Special Attacks]], they produce [[Bros. Attacks]] when completed, exclusive to Mario or Luigi in the [[Real World]], and also produce [[Luiginary Attack]]s for Mario and [[Dreamy Luigi]] in the [[Dream World]] when found and completed there.

==Trivia==
*Attack Piece Blocks are one of the only instances in the ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' series where [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] need to find certain items to create [[Bros. Attack]]s, in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', they unlock Bros. Attacks as they learn new moves, in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' Bros. Attacks and [[Trio Attack]]s are obtained by catching [[Nabbit]], and in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', Bros. Attacks are [[Bros. Item|consumable items]].
*Although the sprites in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' are dervived from the sprites that originated in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', the Attack Piece Blocks have been redesigned to more closely resemble their appearance from the original ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.
