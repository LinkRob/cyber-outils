'''e-Coins''' are an item in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
e-Coins only appear in certain [[level]]s of [[World-e]]. They are collectible items which only contribute to the [[completion]] rating. A total of eight e-Coins are available, only three of which are available in the original North American version due to only a select number of World-e levels being released there. In digital [[reissue|ports]] of ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3'', all eight e-Coins can be obtained in all regional releases because the entirety of World-e was implemented into the base game.

Each e-Coin depicts the [[e-Reader]] "e" on one side, as seen in the levels, and an image of a character or power-up on the other side. After [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] collect an e-Coin and complete a level, they can find it on display in the [[e-Coin Castle]], within its own frame, depicting a unique image and listing the level in which they are found underneath. If Mario or Luigi obtain an e-Coin in a level in which it is found within a [[block]] or under a [[grass|grass tuft]], the e-Coin is replaced by a [[1-Up Mushroom]] if they revisit the level.
