The '''Yellow Berry''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
This item restores three points of [[Mario]]'s [[Flower Point|Flower]] and [[Heart Point]]s. It can only be acquired by smacking a single tree in [[Flower Fields]] with a [[Hammer]]. This tree is located behind the red gate in Flower Fields.

In addition to being a healing item, Yellow Berries can be given to the Yellow Flower guarding the yellow gate in Flower Fields. This path leads Mario to [[Lily (Paper Mario)|Lily]], a kindhearted plant on the southeastern side of Flower Fields.
