'''Rock Candy''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Its icon is a [[bomb]].

This item deals 200 [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage to all enemies. If one uses Rock Candy on a character boosted by [[Geno]], it deals 300 HP of damage instead. If the player uses Rock Candy, white stars go across the screen, while a sound of a magic plays in the background.

This item can be found in [[Item Shop]]s or in the [[pipe]]s of [[Bean Valley]] and [[Bowser's Castle]]. As this item is powerful, the X button can be pushed for a free item to be gained. It is highly recommended to use this item on boss fights.
