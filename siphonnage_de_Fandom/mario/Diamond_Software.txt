'''Diamond Software''' is a business featured in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''. Similar to [[WarioWare, Inc.]], Diamond Software specializes in the production of [[microgame]]s. In addition to this, Diamond Software also produces [[comic]]s and [[Record (WarioWare: D.I.Y.)|records]], so they are more of a company which produces entertainment media. It is one of several locations run by [[Joe]] throughout the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. Prior to the game's events, some employees of WarioWare, Inc. quit their job to go and work for Diamond Software.
