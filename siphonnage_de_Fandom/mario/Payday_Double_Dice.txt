The '''Payday Double Dice''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Description==
It is a [[Double Dice]] completely plated in solid gold. The character starts by rolling two [[Dice Block]]s, but when the total roll is revealed, the character receives [[coin]]s equal to the total number rolled, much like the [[Plus Block]] from the first ''[[Mario Party]]''. The player can potentially get 2-20 free coins with the Payday Double Dice, along with an additional 10 coin bonus for matching numbers similar to the [[Double Dice]]. It has a [[Triple Dice]] variant in the [[Payday Triple Dice]].

[[Mario]]'s [[Jamboree Buddy]] ability dice roll also adds to the total amount of coins for a maximum of 38 coins (with a roll of matching tens for 20 coins, the 10 coin bonus, and an 8 from Mario).

==In ''Super Mario Party Jamboree''==
This item is purchasable in [[Rainbow Galleria]]'s Golden Shop, or occasionally in a regular [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)| Item Shop]] for 10 Coins (5 during a flash sale or if the player has [[Daisy]] as a Jamboree Buddy, 20 if the player is under the effect of a [[Markup Sticker]], and 2 if the player has Daisy as an ally during a flash sale). It can also be obtained from an [[Item Space]].

If bought for the regular price of 10 coins, the Payday Double Dice have a 68% chance of paying for themselves — a 64% chance of rolling at least a 10 (as noted on the [[Double Dice]] page) and a 4% chance of rolling a double 1-4 for a 10 coin bonus.
