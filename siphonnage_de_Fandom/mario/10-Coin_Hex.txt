The '''10-Coin Hex''' is a type of [[Hex]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Description==
10-Coin Hexes are one of the more common Hexes picked up at a [[Hex Area]]. Whoever has a 10-Coin Hex can place it on any [[Blue Space]] or [[Red Space]] up to five [[Space]]s away from where they are. As the name suggests, if an opponent ends their turn on a 10-Coin Hex, they lose ten [[Coin]]s, which is distributed to the player's character or team. If the character who placed the 10-Coin Hex (or someone in the same team as whoever placed it) ends their turn on it, they earn five Coins. Like any other Hex, after the 10-Coin Hex is activated, the Space that it was on returns to normal.

The 10-Coin Hex has an equivalent named [[20-Coin Hex]], which takes away twenty Coins from an opponent instead of ten. Furthermore, ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'' has its own equivalent to the 10-Coin Hex titled the [[10-Coin Steal Trap]].
