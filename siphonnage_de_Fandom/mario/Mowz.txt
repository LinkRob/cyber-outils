'''Mowz''' are [[Little Mouser]]s that have become thieves. They have two important roles in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

Their first big appearance is in [[King Boo's Haunted Hideaway]]. If a [[? Space]] is landed on inside the mansion, a Mowz will let the player spin a wheel with a pinball inside. Depending on which one of three holes on the wheel the ball lands in ([[Coin]]s, [[Power Star|Star]], or [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]]), it will steal an item from a random opponent for the player.

Another appearance of a Mowz is in [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]]. This Mowz stole [[Holly Koopa]]'s candy and when landing on a [[? Space]] (it's one of the ones above the train) a [[Shy Guy]] will come and tell the character about the incident. Then he'll tell the character who the suspect is ([[Bandit]] can also be the one who stole the candy). If the player correctly remembers which car the Mowz was on, they will be rewarded with 20 coins (10 in Duel Mode).

A Mowz can be seen in the [[Star Carnival]] in the menu screen. Other characters walking in the Star Carnival are famous enemies such as [[Shy Guy]]s, [[Koopa]]s and [[Chain Chomp]]s.
