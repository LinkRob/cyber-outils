The '''Konga Dunk''' is [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]'s [[Special Shot]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can have Donkey Kong perform the move by tapping out the letter "M" twice, despite the letter being associated with [[Mario]], whose [[Fire Shot]] is also activated by tapping out the letter M twice.

DK initiates Konga Dunk by dribbling the [[basketball]] in a rough ''M'' shape as it glows yellow. He then throws the ball straight up, [[Hand Slap|slams his hands]] on the ground three times, and then catches the ball in midair. Donkey Kong then does a flip and dunks the ball into the hoop. When the ball is dunked, a small, yellow explosion is briefly shown.

If performed outside the 3-point line, Donkey Kong throws the ball toward the basket while a yellow trail follows from behind. One of his teammates has to dunk the ball, and by doing so, a yellow explosion is shown.
