'''Cloudy Capers''', is seventh level of [[Superstar Road]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by collecting all the Star Coins in [[Meringue Clouds]]. 
==Layout==
The player starts the level in a cloudy area with some blocks and platforms up ahead. A [[Lakitu]] will come up, and the player must destroy it and use its cloud to fly over the gap. Many [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] will soon swarm the area. After some distance, more platforms will appear, along with another ? Block and a Lakitu. After avoiding some more enemies, the player will reach the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Near the beginning, some Fuzzies will be in a circle, encasing the Star Coin. The player must use Fireballs to destroy the Fuzzies, opening up access to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the second Lakitu, the player needs to use its cloud to float up to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': After the third Lakitu, the player needs to use its cloud to get the Star Coin which is encircled by two Fuzzies.
==[[List of Luigi sightings in New Super Luigi U|Luigi Sighting]]==
*When traveling through the last set of Fuzzies, use a Fireball to hit the second to last Fuzzy on the bottom right to reveal an 8-bit Luigi.
