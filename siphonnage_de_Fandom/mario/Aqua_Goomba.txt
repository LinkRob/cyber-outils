'''Aqua Goombas'''<ref>[https://www.nintendo.co.jp/character/mario/archives/land2/?lang=en English ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'' entry on the official Mario Portal]. ''nintendo.co.jp''. Retrieved August 13, 2022. ([https://archive.ph/xw7hA Archived] August 13, 2022, 13:51:45 UTC via archive.today.)</ref> are enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. They are [[Goomba]]s wearing {{wp|diving helmet}}s. In the [[Turtle Zone]]'s [[Whale Course|final stage]], several Goombas are found in the belly of the giant [[whale]]. Despite their name and appearance, they generally appear free from the water and behave largely like regular Goombas, with the only difference being that they are not flattened when jumped on.
