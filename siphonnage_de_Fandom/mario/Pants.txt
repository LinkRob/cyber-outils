'''Pants''' is a basic armor only usable by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the [[Item Shop]] in [[Toad Town|Mushroom Kingdom]] for seven [[coin]]s. Equipping the Pants increases Mallow's [[Defense]] by 6 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 3.
