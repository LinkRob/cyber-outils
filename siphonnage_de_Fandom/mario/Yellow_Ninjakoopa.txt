The next in line of the [[Koopa Bros.]], '''Yellow Ninjakoopa''' specializes in traps as he set up a fake question block which caused Mario to fall into the cell.

== ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ==
Yellow Ninjakoopa is initially encountered in [[Toad Town]] disguised as a [[Toad]] with a the mushroom part of his head black and with yellow spots.
