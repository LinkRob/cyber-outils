The '''Three Musty Fears''' are a trio of characters who appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are only seen while Mario is asleep in [[Monstro Town]]. They urge Mario to find their flags in order to receive a prize.

==Description==
The Three Musty Fears are composed of a [[Big Boo]], a [[Greaper]], and a [[Dry Bones]].

===Dry Bones' Flag===
Dry Bones said he hid his flag on a bed. Mario must head back to his house, [[Mario's Pad]], and check under his bed to get one.

After Mario finds all the flags, he must return to Monstro Town and fall asleep again and The Three Musty Fears will award him with the Ghost Medal.

==Trivia==
*The Three Musty Fears is an obvious parody of ''The Three Musketeers''.
