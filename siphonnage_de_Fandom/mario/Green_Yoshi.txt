'''Green Yoshis''' are the most common color variant of the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. They can be found residing in [[Dinosaur Land]] and [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. The titular [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] who acts as a sidekick to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] is a Green Yoshi. Dozens of other Green Yoshis have also appeared alongside or separately from the main [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario World''====
[[Image:Green Yoshi SNES.png|thumb|left|A Green Yoshi with Mario.]]
Green Yoshis make their debut in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' (alongside the main [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]), where they can be hatched from a [[Yoshi Egg]] whenever [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] finds one. In this game, Green Yoshis can eat enemies to defeat them, including enemies Mario and Luigi can't normally defeat on their own, such as [[Spiny]]s. The Green Yoshi can also use this ability to receive different powers depending on which color of [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]] shell he is holding in his mouth. These abilities are:

*Green Shell &ndash; Spit
*Red Shell &ndash; Spitting Fireballs
*Blue Shell &ndash; Flying
*Yellow Shell &ndash; Ground Pound
*Rainbow Shell &ndash; Flying, Ground Pound, and Spitting Fireballs

Green Yoshis can also eat [[Berry|Berries]] in this game, each of which have different effects. If a Green Yoshi is to eat ten red berries in the same level, he will lay an egg which hatches into a [[Super Mushroom]] for the plumbers to collect. If he eats two pink berries in the same level, he will lay an egg that hatches into a cloud which tosses [[Coin]]s. This cloud will also provide a [[1-Up Mushroom]] once all the [[Coin]]s are collected. [[Green Berry|Green Berries]] will add 20 seconds to the timer.

If Mario or Luigi beats a level while riding a Green Yoshi, the Yoshi will remain with him and join him in future levels. However, Green Yoshis are rather timid and are scared of [[Ghost House]]s and [[Castle]]s, and will wait for Mario and Luigi outside until they've finished up business in there.

In the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]] version of the game, [[Yellow Yoshi|Yellow]], [[Red Yoshi|Red]] and [[Blue Yoshi|Blue]] Yoshis can only be hatched from eggs in the [[Star World]], and only the Green Yoshi can be found in [[Dinosaur Land]]. However, in the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake (''[[Super Mario Advance 2: Super Mario World]]''), the different colored Yoshis can also appear at random in Dinosaur Land after they are discovered in the Star World allowing one to use their effects in any level of the game.


====''Super Mario Advance''====
[[Image:Yoshi Menus.png|thumb|right|Yoshi's Challenge]]
Green Yoshis make an appearance in the ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' remake, ''[[Super Mario Advance]]'' along with some other colored [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] in the exclusive "[[Yoshi's Challenge]]" mode. Their eggs can be found in the first level of each world and can be collected.
{{br}}
====''Super Mario Sunshine''====
[[Image:Green Yoshi Beta SMS.jpg|thumb|left|A Green Yoshi as seen in ''Super Mario Sunshine''.]]
Green Yoshis make small appearances in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', in this game, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] will start out green upon hatching from the [[Yoshi Egg]] but will turn into a different color ([[Purple Yoshi|purple]], [[Pink Yoshi|pink]], or [[Orange Yoshi|orange]] depending on the fruit) immediately after, also runs out of juice, the Yoshi will turn green if they are about to die from not eating another Fruit for a long time. While green, Yoshis spit water instead of juice. Yoshis can also turn green upon hitting water before they evaporate.

In the game's [[Super Mario Sunshine/Beta elements|beta files]], it is shown that Green Yoshis were planned to appear as a basic color for the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]s rather than only appearing briefly each time.
{{br}}
====''Super Mario All-Stars / Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3''====
[[Image:King Green Yoshi.png|thumb|right|The King has been transformed into a Green Yoshi.]]
While absent from the original ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', a Green Yoshi makes a cameo appearance in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. In this game, the [[Kings|King]] of the [[Pipe Land]] was turned into a Green Yoshi by [[Ludwig von Koopa]] (in the original, he was turned into a [[Venus Fire Trap]]).
{{br}}
====''Super Mario 64 DS''====
[[Image:Green Yoshi and Red Yoshi DS.png|thumb|left|A Green [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in a VS game.]]
In ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', the main [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] is a playable character in the adventure mode. In the game's VS Mode, a Green Yoshi (alongside a [[Red Yoshi]], [[Blue Yoshi]], and [[Yellow Yoshi]]) is among one of the four playable characters. The Green Yoshi is the playable [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] for Player 1.
{{br}}
====''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''====
[[Image:Yoshi Plains Ride.png|thumb|right|A Green Yoshi being ridden by [[Blue Toad]].]]
Green Yoshis appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' (alongside various other [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] colors including [[Pink Yoshi]]s, [[Yellow Yoshi]]s, and [[Cyan Yoshi]]s). In this game, the Green Yoshi is identified as [[Mario]]'s primary Yoshi and retains similar behaviors to ''[[Super Mario World]]'', they can once again beat enemies by swallowing them and if a Green Yoshi is to eat five [[Berry|Berries]], Mario will be granted with a power-up or a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. The Yoshis cannot be brought into other levels, once the heroes tag the [[Flagpole]], the Green Yoshi remains behind and waves goodbye to the heroes as they enter the castle. It is possible that the Green Yoshi from this game is the titular [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] but this is unclear.
{{br}}
====''Super Mario Galaxy 2''====
The Green Yoshi that appears in some galaxies of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' is [[Yoshi]] himself. Yoshi has a traditional moveset, but he can also use some special power-ups like the [[Bulb Berry]]. Upon eating one of these power-ups, Yoshi's color changes until it’s effects end, reverting him back to green.

====''New Super Mario Bros. U / New Super Luigi U / New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''====
[[Image:Green Yoshi Many.png|thumb|left|The Mario Crew riding four Green Yoshis.]]
Green Yoshis also appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. In this game, they retain their role from previous games, and are once again rideable allies that can beat many enemies and eat [[Berry|Berries]], eating five rewards a power-up or [[1-Up Mushroom]]. The Green Yoshis are the only color of [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] to appear in this game as in multiplayer, multiple Green Yoshis can be seen. Just as before, the Yoshis only stay with the heroes until the end of the level.
{{br}}
====''Super Mario Maker / Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS / Super Mario Maker 2''====
[[Image:Green Yoshi Maker.png|thumb|right|Two Green Yoshis in the SMW style.]]
Green Yoshis appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as well as it's [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS|two]] [[Super Mario Maker 2|sequels]] as rideable, allies. In these games, the Yoshis are exclusive to the ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' styles (as they are replaced by [[Goomba's Shoe]]s in the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' styles, Yoshis hatch from placed [[Yoshi Egg]]s, they Green Yoshis function identically to how they do in the mainline games and can easily beat enemies. Unlike most games, only two Yoshis can be on screen at a time. If a [[Yoshi Egg]] is given a [[Super Mushroom]], two Green Yoshis will come out, though they start running around like crazy immediately. Also, in the ''[[Super Mario World]]'' style, knocking on a [[Door]] theme may cause [[Weird Mario]] to appear riding a Green Yoshi. Green Yoshi is the only color of Yoshi to appear in this game, unlike the other ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' games, Yoshi actually enters the end-level Castle with [[Mario]] rather than staying behind.


===''Yoshi'' franchise===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''====
[[Image:Two Green Yoshis.png|thumb|left|Two Green Yoshis seen at the end of the extra course.]]
Green Yoshis appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' as well as ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. In these games, the titular [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] appears as a playable character in the first level of each world while the final world has another Green Yoshi playable in the first level while the main [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] is playable in the world's final level, [[King Bowser's Castle]] (replacing the [[Blue Yoshi]] who was playable in the castles of the previous worlds). Additionally, Green Yoshis are also playable in the secret and extra levels of the game. In these levels, when the Green Yoshi jumps through a [[GOAL! Ring]], [[Baby Mario]] is passed onto another Green Yoshi. Green Yoshis are the most used Yoshis in the game being playable in [[Make Eggs, Throw Eggs]], [[Visit Koopa And Para-Koopa]], [[Welcome to Monkey World!]], [[GO! GO! MARIO!!]], [[BLIZZARD!!!]], [[Scary Skeleton Goonies!]], [[King Bowser's Castle]], [[Poochy Ain't Stupid]], [[Hit That Switch!!]], [[More Monkey Madness]], [[The Impossible Maze]], [[Kamek's Revenge]], and [[Ultimate Castle Challenge]], the remake adds six more levels all of which a Green Yoshi is also playable in, [[Exercise in the Skies]], [[Mystery of the Castle?]], [[Go! Go! Morphing!]], [[Fight Toadies w/ Toadies]], [[Items are Fun!]], and [[Endless World of Yoshis]].

In the remake, if the player manages to get 100 points in all levels, a cutscene will play which shows a Green Yoshi along with a message that says:

<blockquote>"What an amazing job!

You are the Xth Yoshi to adventure on Yoshi's Island!"
</blockquote>

The number (shown as "Xth") in the message is the amount of lives the player lost plus 1.

[[Image:Green Yoshi Push.png|thumb|left|A Green Yoshi pushing the Chomp Rock.]]

In this game, the titular [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] is seen residing on [[Yoshi's Island]] alongside numerous other [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] including other Green Yoshis, they were all eaten by [[Yoob]] when he was let loose on [[Yoshi's Island]] and from here, placed in [[Yoob Egg]]'s in the monster's factory-like [[Yoob's Belly|belly]] to be reborn as mini Yoob-like monsters. In this game, they can be seen vacationing on [[Pi'illo Island]] from the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and [[Yoshi's Island]], they are shown to snack on fruits and other snacks in this game, the Yoshis can be seen around [[Pi'illo Castle]], [[Mushrise Park]], [[Wakeport]], and [[Driftwood Shore]]. A Green Yoshi is also seen as one of the guests for the [[Mount Pajamaja]] tour hosted by the [[Big Massif|Massif]] [[Lil' Massif|Bros.]] but immediately rejects it (alongside all the other tour guests except [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]) after hearing how dangerous it is, Green Yoshis can also be seen around the game's various shops.

====''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''====
[[Image:Green Yoshi Mountain.png|thumb|left|The [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] meet a Green Yoshi.]]
A Green Yoshi appears in [[Mount Brrr]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' as a racing opponent. In this game, he challenges [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] to a race after they defeated his [[Yellow Yoshi]] friend from [[Gloomy Woods]]. The heroes must race the Green Yoshi along the course but he is very fast and may cheat by using [[Yoshi Egg]]s and [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounds]] to slow down the heroes if they're ahead. If the heroes win, the Green Yoshi is a good sport and gives them some [[Speed Gloves]].

==== ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'' ====

==Appearance== 
In ''Super Mario World'', Green Yoshi has green skin, but orange gloves, brown shoes and a brown saddle. In ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]'', Green Yoshi's gloves were removed to match its current appearance.

In ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', Green Yoshi looks about the same as he does in ''Super Mario World'', but with orange shoes and a red saddle. He also doesn't wear his orange gloves, and instead he has bare green arms. In the [[Game Boy Advance]] port, Green Yoshi wears red shoes.

From ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'' to ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', Green Yoshi has red shoes and a red saddle. From ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' and on, Green Yoshi has orange shoes and a red saddle, except for ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', where the shoes are again red.

==Trivia==
The Green Yoshis have retained the same general appearance in recent games but early on, they did go through a couple changes.
*In ''[[Super Mario World]]'', Green Yoshis have green skin, but orange arms, brown shoes and a brown saddle.
*In ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', Green Yoshis look about the same as they do in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', but with orange shoes and an orange saddle. However in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', Green Yoshis wear red shoes.
*In ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'', Green Yoshi have red shoes and a red saddle.
*As of ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', Green Yoshis wear orange shoes and a red saddle.
