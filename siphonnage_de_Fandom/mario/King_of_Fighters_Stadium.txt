'''King of Fighters Stadium''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. Its first and only appearance is in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', where it appears as a DLC stage along with [[Terry Bogard|Terry]] in the 4th challenger pack of the first Fighter's Pass. It is from the ''Fatal Fury'' universe.
