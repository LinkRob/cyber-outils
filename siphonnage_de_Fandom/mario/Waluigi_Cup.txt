The '''Waluigi Cup''' is a [[cup]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2''===
The Waluigi Cup is the first normal cup. It contains two [[Waluigi]]-themed courses, both of which take place in the same general area and are similar to other stadium tracks in the series. The layout of the two courses in this cup would later be reused for [[Aerial Road]] and [[Sky Arena]] in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
The Waluigi Cup is introduced in the [[Halloween Tour (2019)|2019 Halloween Tour]], alongside Waluigi as a playable character. Here, courses featured in this cup are treated as Waluigi's favored or favorite courses.
