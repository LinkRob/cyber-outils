The '''Double Card''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Description==
Double Cards are an item exclusive to [[Toad Scramble]] mode, and using one doubles how many [[coin]]s and [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]] are obtained from defeating a boss. A Double Card is stackable, so characters can earn up to eight Stars if they defeat the last boss of any board. Unlike other items, the Double Card activates automatically as soon as a character faces a boss.
