'''Feeling Fine''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It protects [[Mario]] from suffering status ailments such as [[Poison (ailment)|poison]] or [[Dizzy|dizziness]].

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:Feeling_fine_badge.png|thumb|left]]
In ''Paper Mario'', Feeling Fine is found in [[Shooting Star Summit]]. It is one of the many badges that [[Merlow]] sells to Mario in exchange for [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]]s. Merlow prices this badge for 5 Star Pieces. This badge is very helpful during chapter 5 as there are a variety of enemies that can [[Poison (ailment)|poison]] Mario. [[Hammer Bro|Hammer Bros.]] in the final chapter can also shrink [[Mario]], which this badge will help prevent. Feeling Fine is actually glitched to provide extra protection from nearly every status ailment. This badge has a BP cost of 3 to wear in this game.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Mario can find the Feeling Fine badge by using the [[Cog]] item found in the [[X-Naut Fortress]] on the [[Moon]]. In this fortress, Mario uses the cog to fix a crane that will grab this item, [[Feeling Fine P]], a Star Piece, and some [[coin]]s. This badge now sports a BP cost of four, but has the same effect as in ''Paper Mario''.
