'''Congo Canopy''' is a golf course in ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]''.

==Description==
It is a high layer of foliage located on [[Donkey Kong Island]]. The course is used in the [[Birdie Challenge]] and Near-Pin modes. To unlock it for Stroke Play, the player must finish the "Back 9" mode in Birdie Challenge. It is located in a canopy area of a jungle, which has very large trees since a lot of characters can stand on it and the forest even has a lake. It is very easy to make the ball miss its mark and fall off the course. This course can be played in the One-On, One-Putt and Speed Golf modes. Unique to Congo Canopy, the course has an intertwined layout, and certain greens can be seen from other tees. The tee markers are miniature barrels with the letters "DK" on the front.
