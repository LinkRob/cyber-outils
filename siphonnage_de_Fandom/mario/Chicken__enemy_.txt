A {{Nihongo|'''Chicken'''<ref>{{cite manual|game=Super Mario Land|page=17}}</ref>|チッキン<ref>{{cite manual|game=Super Mario Land|language=Japanese|page=20}}</ref>|Chikkin|Chicken}} is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. They are only found in the final kingdom, the [[Chai Kingdom]].

== Profile ==
=== Behavior ===
[[File:Chicken SML sprite.png|frame|left]]
A Chicken is found soaring through the air meaning Mario can only take them down by shooting missiles from his [[Sky Pop]]. The player earns 400 [[point]]s for each Chicken defeated. If Mario misses or does not shoot down the Chickens, they will attempt to fly toward him dealing damage. Chickens are also present during the fight with [[Biokinton]] where the giant cloud attacks by sending a flock of them toward Mario.
