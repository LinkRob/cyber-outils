'''Darkmess Edge''' serves as the second boss at Cursa's Stronghold in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''. She is encountered in the level Deep, Dark Secret, which is unlocked by destroying [[Darkmess Bowser]] in King of Darkmess. Hence her name, Darkmess Edge takes the form of the real [[Edge]], having been created by [[Cursa]] due to the latter being Edge's creator.

== Description ==
Darkmess Edge looks like her "real" counterpart, but is black all over save for her eyes and hair, which are orange. She also wears a completely black version of Edge's clothes.

== Combat ==
As with how [[Bowser]] is a pre-set party member while players can choose his allies in the level King of Darkmess, Edge is required to use in Deep, Dark Secret while the player can choose whom she will bring into the battle. Darkmess Edge has Edge's moves, as she uses a sword boomerang ability which allows her to strike an enemy once as well as anyone between them twice and can also use a counter move to protect herself against anyone who gets too close to her. She is resistant to the [[Burn]] [[Status Effect]]. Darkmess Edge is aided by Scopers (two of which appear immediately on the islands at the north side of her battlefield), Medicians (one of which is on the central island where she spawns), Oozers (one of which is on the central island where she spawns), Spellraisers (which do not appear immediately but can be summoned by Darkmess portals), and Stooges (which do not appear immediately and have to be summoned by either Spellraisers or Darkmess portals).

Darkmess Edge is Level 26 with 12,964 hit points if her Enemy Threat level is low. At medium, she is of Level 30 with 26,290 hit points. At a high Enemy Threat level, she can be either of Level 29 with 31,421 hit points or Level 30 with 34,177 hit points. She can perform up to three Dash attacks.

== Aftermath ==
After Darkmess Edge is destroyed, [[Edge]] reveals her past as a former [[Spark Hunter]] who was created by [[Cursa]] as the group's leader. However, Edge was too smart to fall for her would-be master's demands and attempted to turn [[Bedrock]], [[Midnite]], and [[Daphne]] on Cursa, only to be sold out by them and driven out. Edge tried to save as many [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Sparks]] as possible after she left. Meanwhile, [[Kanya]] was created to replace Edge, but did not like her position, fleeing to the Melodic Gardens to create a plot to overthrow Cursa and rule the galaxy herself.
