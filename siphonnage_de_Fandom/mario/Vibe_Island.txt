'''Vibe Island''' is an island where the events of ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' take place. The vibes on this location effect [[Princess Peach]] and gives her new powers. This is where [[Bowser]] heard about it and built [[Bowser's Villa|his villa]]. [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] and [[Toad (character)|Toads]] get kidnapped by Bowser and the [[Hammer Bros.]]
