The '''Shake Meter''' is a gameplay mechanic in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Description==
The Shake Meter is a small meter shown on the top-center of the HUD. It gradually fills in light yellow, and has the signature 'W' [[emblem]] in the center. When the Shake Meter is full, [[Wario]] can perform an [[Earthshake Punch]], although in doing so, the Shake Meter empties, and Wario can only perform an Earthshake Punch once it fills back up.

In the Japanese version, the Shake Meter instead depicts the word "!SHAKE!" across the bar.
