'''Cat Boomerang Bros.''' are a variant of [[Boomerang Bro]] featured in the [[Bowser's Fury]] mode of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. They are among the cat enemy variants, and Cat Boomerang Bros. resemble a Boomerang Bro but have cat ears and a tail.
