'''Halloween Tour''' is a tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] that originally occured on  October 19, 2022 and ended on November 1, 2022 as the eighty-first tour. It also is a rerun of 2023, 2024 and 2025.

Like other Halloween Tours it features ghost themed tracks including a new track: [[Boo Lake]] from [[Mario Kart: Super Circuit|''Mario Kart: Super Circuit'']]. [[Donut Plains 3 (track)|Donut Plains 3]] also returns from ''[[Super Mario Kart|Super Mario kart]]'' and [[Twilight House]] from [[Mario Kart DS|''Mario Kart DS'']] returned as a  battle track. There are also R/T variants introduced for [[Ghost Valley 2]]. The [[Streetle]] from [[Mario Kart 8]] also returns as a kart introduced as a kart.

The Autumn Celebration event that started in the previous tour continued into this tour. This tour introduced the second set of Autumn Challenges cards, which rewarded [[Captain Toad]] for completing all challenges on the card, in addition to finishing the Autumn Challenges 1 card from the previous tour. In addition, players could earn Petey Piranha (Gold), the newly introduced Red Vampire Flyer kart, and a gold pipe as part of reward for placing high in the ranked cups.
