'''Trottin' Piranha Plant''' is a variant of a [[Piranha Plant]] and an enemy which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', appearing in the second level, [[Piranha Plants on Parade]]. While they walk around as a threat to the heroes, activating the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]] will result in singing and dancing Piranha Plants.

==Trivia==
*This is the third subspecies of Piranha Plants with the ability to walk, the first two being [[Stalking Piranha Plant|Stalking Piranha Plants]] and [[Ptooie|Ptooies]].
