'''Flashing Eggs''' are a type of [[Yoshi Egg]] featured exclusively within the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. They make their overall debut in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and later return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. Flashing Eggs are very uncommon, and only appear in a few [[level]]s due to each of them containing one of the [[Red Coin]]s of that level. To release the Red Coin, Yoshi has to break open the Flashing Egg by throwing it at an enemy.

==Description==
The spots on a Flashing Egg rapidly flashes between different colors, and while they are never shown to not alternate between colors, the standard color of the spots of a Flashing Egg is purple, as suggested from their artwork for ''Yoshi's New Island''.
