The '''Star Tourney''' is the third tournament in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. It is unlocked by completing [[Flower Tourney]]. It consists of four courts: [[Wario Factory (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|Wario Factory]], [[Jr. Street]], [[Bowser's Castle (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|Bowser's Castle]], and the unlockable [[Glare Desert]]. Completing the Star Tourney unlocks the [[Rainbow Tourney]].

==Unlockables==
Various content can be unlocked by completing Star Tourney on a certain difficulty or by obtaining a bronze, silver, or gold prize at minimum, depending on the item. 

*[[Bowser]]: Complete on normal difficulty with at least a bronze prize.
*[[Moogle]]: Complete on normal difficulty with at least a silver prize.
*[[Spiked Ball]]: Complete on normal difficulty with a gold prize.
*[[Fly Guy]]: Complete on hard difficulty with at least a bronze prize.
*Fly Guy alternate costume: Complete on hard difficulty with at least a silver prize.
*[[Goomba]] Ball: Complete on hard difficulty with a gold prize.
