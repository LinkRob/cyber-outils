'''Leafy Greens''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', featured as one of the microgames under [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[Nature]] folder.

==Gameplay==
The microgame stars a snail named [[Goma]], who has to go from the left platform to the right platform and then up at the leaf. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame, and Goma grows to a gigantic size. However, attached to the right of the first platform and the left of the second platform are thin platforms that collapse when Goma slithers on them, leading into the [[pit]] below. As such if Goma either falls down the pit or does not eat the leaf within the [[time limit]], the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
*Level 1: Goma does not have to jump to get to the leaf.
*Level 2: Goma has to jump once to get to the leaf.
*Level 3: Goma has to jump twice to get to the leaf.
