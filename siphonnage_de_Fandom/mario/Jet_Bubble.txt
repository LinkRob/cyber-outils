|advantages=[[Speed]], [[Drift]]|disadvantages=[[Weight]]|class=Small}}The '''Jet Bubble''', or '''Bubble Bike''' in the Japanese and European releases, is a small size [[Bikes|bike]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. An average bike, the bike is good for beginners. It can be unlocked by finishing the [[Leaf Cup]] on [[Mirror Mode]] in first place, or by playing 3,900 races. Its kart counterpart is the [[Blue Falcon]]. [[Toad]] and [[Koopa Troopa]] are best known for using this bike in [[Time Trial]]s as Nin*Misa and Nin*morimo ([[Toad's Factory]] and [[Koopa Cape]]).

== Stats ==
The Jet Bubble is a fairly balanced bike; its two strengths are its speed and drift. Its weight is low, although it is the heaviest small bike in the game. Its other stats are average.{{MKW Kart Stats/Jet Bubble}}
