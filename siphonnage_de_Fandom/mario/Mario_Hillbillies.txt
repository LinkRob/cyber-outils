"'''Mario Hillbillies'''" is the twenty-third live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the animated episode "[[Do You Princess Toadstool Take this Koopa...?]]."

== Plot ==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] are ready to go on a fishing trip and even have a worm pizza for bait but have to wait for their cousins, [[Mario Joe and Luigi Bob]]. Impatient to get going, the Marios leave a note for their cousins and leave. Their cousins arrive just as the Marios leave and raid the refrigerator.  

Later [[Donna Douglas]] comes looking for the Marios so that they can sell her a faucet assembly. Luigi Bob and Mario Joe tell her that they are the Mario Bros.  She invites them to a possum dinner and they leave with her.  Mario and Luigi return glad that they didn't have to take their cousins to the plumber's hoedown.

== Trivia/Goofs ==
* The Marios themselves never meet the guest star.  Even in the final segment where they say "Do the Mario" it is their cousins that say it.
* When Luigi Bob reads the note, it is addressed to "Mario and Luigi Bob" rather than "Mario Joe and Luigi Bob".
