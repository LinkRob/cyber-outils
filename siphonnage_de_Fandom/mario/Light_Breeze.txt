}}'''Light Breeze''' is a 2-vs.-2 mini-game found in the game ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each team must quickly press the L and R buttons alternatively to spin the turbines. The meter is shared by a team, but is filled up on opposite sides. The first team to fill the electricity meter wins the mini-game.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|L}} → {{button|gcn|R}} - Move the fan. Press alternatively
