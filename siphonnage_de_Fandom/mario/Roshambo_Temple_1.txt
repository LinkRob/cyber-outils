'''Roshambo Temple #1''' is a special location in the game ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'' ==
Here, Mario can participate in a [[Super Roshambo]] tournament, which is three rounds of Rock-Paper-Scissors. The other contestants consist of a [[Goomba]], a [[Shy Guy]], and a [[Koopa Troopa]].
