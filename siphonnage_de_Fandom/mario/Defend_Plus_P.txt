'''Defend Plus P''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can equip this badge by using five points of his [[Badge Point|BP]]. While wearing this badge, Mario's partners gain an extra point to their defense.

Defend Plus P can be found inside of the [[Pirate's Grotto]] where Mario must sail through a thin waterfall and then through a small path leading to this badge. In addition, when Mario has the Spin Jump, he can go to [[Rogueport Sewers]] and find this badge by using the spin jump to latch onto a metal pipe and using [[Admiral Bobbery|Bobbery]] to find this badge. Mario can steal these badges from the help of [[Ms. Mowz]]. [[Chain Chomp]]s, [[Bob-ulk]]s, and [[Bulky Bob-omb]]s possess this badge on occasion.

==Trivia==
*The Defend Plus P badge found in the [[Pirate's Grotto]] was originally a [[Damage Dodge P]] in the Japanese version.
