'''Mad Red Paragoombas''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mad Red Paragoombas are a variant of [[Paragoomba]]s imbued with the [[Rage]] [[vibe]]. They have a red body and sport an angrier facial expression than the average Paragoomba.

===Behavior===
Mad Red Paragoombas are more aggressive than Paragoombas, as whenever a Mad Red Paragoomba notices [[Princess Peach|Peach]], it begins to charge in her direction. If a Mad Red Paragoomba falls off a ledge, it can still fly back up to the surface.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Although Mad Red Paragoombas first appear in the [[level]] [[Shriek Mansion 3-8]], they are most commonly encountered in [[Fury Volcano]]. In their [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] entry, Mad Red Paragoombas have their name formatted as '''M. Red P-Goomba'''.
