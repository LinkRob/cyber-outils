'''Ghost''' (幽霊, or ゆうれい, ''Yūrei'') is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. It is a large pirate ghost that roams around outdoor areas of the level [[Crescent Moon Village]] in search of treasure. It flies around pursuing [[Wario]] and cannot be defeated, nor can it damage Wario. It will steal any coin it can get in greed. Also if Wario has a [[Keyzer]] with him, the Ghost tries to snatch it away as well. If the Ghost succeeds, it laughs, and all doors are locked until Wario gets Keyzer back.
