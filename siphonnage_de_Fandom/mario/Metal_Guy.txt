'''Metal Guys''' are an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Metal Guys are a variant of [[Mega Guy]]s made up of metal, not unlike [[Metal Mario (character)|Metal Mario]].

===Behavior===
Metal Guys act the same as Mega Guys, except they spawn from giant metal [[Warp Pipe|pipe]]s, 

==In ''Yoshi's New Island''==
Metal Guys appear in the [[level]]s [[Koopa Canyon]], [[Rise of the Nasty Nep-Enuts]], [[Slime Drop Drama]], [[Lose the Lunge Fish]], and [[Make Way for King Bowser!]]

A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can swallow a Metal Guy if the player rapidly presses {{button|3ds|A}} to turn it into a [[Metal Eggdozer]].
