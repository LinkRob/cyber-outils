'''Bonds''' are a central element in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They are born from powerful emotional connections between multiple persons, born from mutual care and understanding. Bonds can be strengthened and formed to produce Connectar, which allows the [[Uni-Tree]] to flourish and guide [[Shipshape Island]] to further their creation. The power of exceptionally strong Bonds, can serve as a direct counter to influence from the overarching antagonist of the game, [[Reclusa]]. Throughout the game, [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must reestablish Bonds to save [[Concordia]]. A toxic substance called [[Glohm]] which causes victims to seek isolation also appears as an antithesis to Bonds.

== History ==

=== Background ===
[[Zokket]], a follower of [[Reclusa]] and the leader of the [[Zokket Army]], destroyed the [[Uni-Tree]] around the time that the tree's original caretaker, [[Cozette]] reportedly vanished, and his desecration of the tree sundered the land of [[Concordia]], rendering connections between the Concordians substantially more difficult and severing many Bonds. The [[Great Conductor]], in his efforts to fight the apocalypse, summoned persons with especially strong Bonds in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] to various corners of Concordia, which is how the Mario Bros. got abducted by the vortex in the sky above the Mushroom Kingdom. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are two of these, reuniting on Shipshape Island despite getting separated during their arrival. [[Bowser]] and his [[Koopa Troop|minions]] are another group, while [[Princess Peach]] and her [[Mushroom Retainer|Mushroom Retainers]] are the third.

=== In ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'' ===
Mario and Luigi have to re-establish Bonds between the inhabitants of Concordia. These Bonds would then empower and strengthen the Uni-Tree on Shipshape Island, reconnecting closed currents and guiding the Bros. to restore more of Concordia.

Near the end of the game, [[Reclusa]] takes over Zokket's fortress and creates the [[Soli-Tree]] from it (which it uses as its own fortress), and [[Cozette]] (who was rescued at the cost of Zokket's life) tasks the Bros. and her pupil [[Connie]] to take her Bonding Can and fill it with seven powerful Bonds in order to penetrate the barrier surrounding the fortress. When the Bros. infiltrate the Soli-Tree, Connie uses the Bonding Can on the Bros. to give them the power necessary to punch through the barrier before she lets them use the cannon. The required Bonds are gathered from the following:

* [[Connie]] and [[Cozette]] give their Bond right after the latter explains that Bonds are necessary to break through the Soli-Tree's barrier.
* [[Prince Chilliam]] and [[Princess Burnadette]] are found hiding within the caves beneath [[Skorcheen Island]], their romance giving the second Bond.
* [[Chief Willma]], her [[Maykit and Billdit|children]], and the [[Gorumbla]] give theirs on [[Rumbla Island]] after the latter overcomes his [[Glohm]] poisoning and Willma decides he can be part of her family.
* The [[IDLE]] gang gives theirs after being found on various islets.
* [[Groovemaster Dyode]] and Emmit give theirs when they and the Bros. perform on [[Twistee Island]], [[Merrygo Island]], and [[Jellyfish Island]].
* [[Bowser Jr.]] and [[Furen]] give theirs when reunited on [[Offandon Island]] after the Bros. fight through Glohm-infected enemies.
* The [[Extension Corps]] gives theirs in their laboratory on [[Slippenglide Island]].

After the first phase of the battle versus Reclusa, the Bros. fall victim to Weeping Reclusa's Glohm fog and are unable to fight any further. However, Connie restores their power with the Bonding Can, allowing them to continue fighting.
