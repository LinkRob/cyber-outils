'''World 4-1''' is the first level of [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Overview==
This level introduces [[Mega Urchin]]s and is mostly submerged in water.

==Walkthrough==
This level begins on land, near a platform above the water with two [[? Block]]s above it. The player must then travel through a section of water, where [[Urchin]]s, [[Cheep Cheep]]s, [[Mega Cheep-Cheep]]s, and a [[Piranha Plant]] are encountered. A piece of land with a [[Koopa Troopa]], a [[Red Ring]], and two ? Blocks are in the section. Afterward, two currents coming out of [[Warp Pipe]]s are found, along with a wall that leads underwater. A group of [[Spiny Cheep-Cheep]]s follows along with a platform with a ? Block containing a [[Starman]]. The [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] then follows. The player must then go into the water, where more Urchins and Warp Pipe currents are located. A [[Roulette Block]] is hidden in the wall above. A section with [[Mega Urchin]]s follows, with [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s flying above. After a Big Urchin with two Urchins, a structure made of [[Brick Block]]s and ? Blocks is encountered. Underneath the last Big Urchin, there is a Warp Pipe leading to an area with three [[Clampy|Clampies]]. After a section with more Urchins and currents, a Warp Pipe leading to an area with a Koopa Paratroopa and the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]] is found.

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Freeze the Pirahna underwater with the Penguin Suit, then jump on the frozen Pirahna to get the first Star Coin. This is located near the beginning of the course.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Freeze the Urchin underwater with the Penguin Suit, then jump on the frozen Urchin to get the first Star Coin. You've got to hurry before[[File:World 4-1 Last Star Coin.png|thumb|293x293px|The last Star Coin in World 4-1.]] it unfreezes! This is located slightly after the midway point.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Belly slide on the water that has Urchins and pipes underneath. Then jump on the ledge. There is a pipe, but don't enter it yet! There is the third Star Coin waiting for you.
