'''Dry Bones Burnout''' is a course from [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']], featured in the [[Leaf Cup]] as the last course.

== Description ==
Dry Bones Burnout is set in the north section of the map, more specifically in the west part of the Bowser area. It is set around a Day of the Dead theme, featuring a sepia-tinted section with giant bones that can be driven on. There is also lava which can be boosted on with a mushroom.
