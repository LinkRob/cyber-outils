'''Sylvia''' is one of the [[human]] tennis players featured in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. She is of the speedy type, and her partner is [[Sasha]].

==Physical description==
Sylvia has long, dark purple hair and gray eyes. She wears a white shirt sporting a red bowtie, a red skirt, a white headband, and tennis shoes.
