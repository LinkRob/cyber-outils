'''Cure Badge A''' is a [[badge]] item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be bought from [[Peach's Castle]] for 700 [[coin]]s. Equipping it restores ten [[HP]] to its user each turn. Cure Badge A is a more effective variation of [[Cure Badge]], which instead only restores five HP per turn.
