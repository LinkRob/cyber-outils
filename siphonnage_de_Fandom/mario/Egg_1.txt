The '''Egg 1''' is kart that appears in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart series]]''. It made its debut in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

== Description ==
It is in the shape of a green [[Yoshi Egg]], with a yellow rim around the top, two grey exhaust pipes at the back and a grey chassis at the lower front, resembling the chassis on the [[Pipe Frame]]. It is usually equipped with small tires.

== Game appearances ==
===  ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' ===
[[File:Egg1MKDS.png|thumb|left|[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in his Egg 1]]
The Egg 1 debuted in ''Mario Kart DS'', as Yoshi's default kart, along with his [[Standard YS]], and his unlockable kart, the [[Cucumber]]. It has the fourth best acceleration and handling stats, average speed, and low drift and items. It is also the lightest kart within the entire game. It is also the fastest kart within the lightweight class. However, the Egg 1 and [[Mario]]'s [[Shooting Star (kart)|Shooting Star]] are the lightest karts in the entire game.

The Egg 1 is one out of the four karts in ''Mario Kart DS'' that are the best for snaking, with the other three being [[Dry Bones]]' [[Dry Bomber]], [[Luigi]]'s [[Poltergust 4000]] and [[R.O.B.]]'s [[ROB-BLS]].

[[Princess Daisy]] and R.O.B. (if either of them have been unlocked yet) can use the Egg 1 once the player completes all cups in the 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix, and all racers can use the Egg 1 once all cups in the Mirror Mode have been completed. Until then, only Yoshi can use this kart.

Yoshi in the Egg 1 is used as the staff ghosts for courses [[Yoshi Falls]], with a time of 0:57:677, and {{Classic course|SNES|[[Koopa Beach 2]]}}, with a time of 0:54:847.

=== ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' ===
[[File:Egg_1_Metal_Mario.png|thumb|[[Metal Mario (character)|Metal Mario]] in the Egg 1 equipped with [[Slick]] tires]]
It returns in ''Mario Kart 7'' as an unlockable [[Retro Kart]] body. It gives a boost in acceleration, whilst hindering speed and weight. In terms of hidden stats, the Egg 1 has the best sea speed in the game, tied with [[Tiny Tug]], and also gives a boost in air speed, mini-turbo and drift.

A computer-controlled [[Princess Peach]] and [[Wario]] particularly favor in using the Egg 1 kart body.

The Egg 1 is used for three [[Staff Ghost]]s; the Expert Staff Ghost for {{Classic course|DS|[[Luigi's Mansion (track)|Luigi's Mansion]]}} with Luigi, [[Monster (tires)|Monster]] tires and the [[Parafoil|Paraglider]], then used for both the Normal and Expert Staff Ghosts at {{Classic course|GCN|[[Dino Dino Jungle]]}}, with Yoshi, the [[Roller]]/[[Monster (tires)|Monster]] tires respectively, and the Paraglider. 

Equipping the Egg 1 with Roller tires will resemble the original ''Mario Kart DS'' version, with the only difference is the center of the tires is silver instead of orange.

=== ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
[[File:MKT_Egg_1.jpg|thumb|left|Yoshi powersliding in his Egg 1 on {{Classic course|GCN|[[Yoshi Circuit]]}}]]
The Egg 1 makes an appearance in ''Mario Kart Tour'', and is classified as a Super kart. It also has two variants: a gold variant called the [[Gold Egg]], and a variant with a dreamlike aesthetic called the [[Dreamy Egg]], which are both High-End karts. 

==Crossover appearances==
===''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' ===
The Egg 1 appears as a trophy in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''. It uses the ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' rendition, equipped with the [[Slick]] tires and Paraglider.
