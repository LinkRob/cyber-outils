The '''Bowser Game Hall''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Bowser Game Hall is located in the [[Seaside Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. However, it can only be accessed through the [[Pipe House (Mario Party Advance)|Pipe House]]'s orange [[Warp Pipe]]. It features a green stage that [[Bowser]] rests on, with [[Koopa Kid]] at his side. There are a few stacks of [[coin]]s in front of him, and two decorative volcanoes in the background erupting gems. Above all of the decorations is a purple curtain with a golden trim.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Bowser stands in the location as the self-proclaimed game king. When the player's character visits Bowser he assumes that they want to challenge him for the title, initiating the [[Game King]] quest. Bowser has the player's character play the gambling minigame, [[Watch 'Em]], and the reward for winning is not only the title of "game king," but also the minigame itself and the [[Jewelry Case]] [[Gaddget]].
