'''''Wario''''' is a spinoff franchise of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. It stars [[Wario]], who usually acts more as an anti-hero rather than a true one due to his greedy nature. The game's first installment is ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'', released in 1994, but the first game to have Wario's name in its title is ''[[Mario & Wario]].''
