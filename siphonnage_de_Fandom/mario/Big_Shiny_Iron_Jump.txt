'''Big Shiny Iron Jump''' is a Battle Sticker seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. With this, Mario can stomp on spiked enemies for great damage. These stickers are ideal for fighting Bowser in the final fight since Bowser himself acts like a spiked enemy. These stickers can be bought at the [[Sticker Shop]] in [[Rumble Volcano]].
==Description==
Big Shiny Iron Jump stickers are similar in color to Shiny Iron Jump stickers except for the large size. They take up four sticker spaces in the [[Sticker Album]].
