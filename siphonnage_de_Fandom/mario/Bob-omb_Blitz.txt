'''Bob-omb Blitz''' is a [[Special Attack]] used by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''

==Description==
Bob-omb Blitz can be used in battle for 12 [[Special Point|SP]]. When using the move, the player must use the [[Stylus]] to line the [[Bob-omb]]s up with the enemies. If they all hit the same enemy, the enemy may become [[dizzy]]. If the player fails to line them up in the right places, the Bob-ombs run past the enemies, dealing no damage.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
Bob-omb Blitz becomes usable once Bowser frees the [[Bob-omb]]s from a cage in [[Bowser Castle]].
