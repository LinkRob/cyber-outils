'''Whimps''' are tiny form of [[Whomp]]s. They are summoned by the [[Whomp King]] in [[Super Mario Galaxy 2]] when he stomps his feet. They are also a tad faster than a basic Whomp. After the Whomp King is hit, he will summon three (five after the second hit) Whimps to attack [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]). The Whimps will chase after Mario (or Luigi) and when they reach him, they will jump and fall like a standard Whomp self destructing in the process. If a Whimp does manage to hit Mario (or Luigi), it will do one damage rather than taking all of it. Whimps can also be defeated by [[Star Spin]]ning into them or with ground pounds, additionally, they disappear when the Whomp King makes his move to attack.

==Trivia==
*"Whimp" is a portmanteau of Whomp and [[Thwimp]], which are small and weaker versions of [[Thwomp|Thwomps]]. (Just as Whimps are to Whomps)
**Notably, "Thwimp" is itself a portmanteau of Thwomp and wimp. "Wimp" and "whimp" are pronounced the same, making the pun come full circle.
*They are about the same size as [[Mario]].
*The Whimp's sound is a high-pitched version of the Whomp's.
*This may be hard to notice while playing, but the back of a Whimp looks just like the tileset of the battle arena. This implies the arena is made of Whimps, and they get kicked up by the Whomp King during the fight.
