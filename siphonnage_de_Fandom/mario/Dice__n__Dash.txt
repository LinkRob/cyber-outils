}}'''Dice 'n' Dash''' is a Free-for-All minigame found in the game ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Gameplay==
The goal of the minigame is to light up the icons on the door across from the player: in order to do so, they must hit the die above them. When the player hits an hits an icon on their die, the corresponding icon on the door will light up. Once all three icons are lit up, the player will complete the minigame. However, if a [[Bowser]] icon is hit, the player will have one icon on the door unlit.
