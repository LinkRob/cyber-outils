}}A '''Sap Soup''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It can only be found from defeating [[Floro Sapien]]s.

==Effects==
Eating a Sap Soup only restores 3 [[Heart Point|HP]] making it a rather poor recovery item. However, it can be cooked with itself or a [[Cake Mix]] to make a better recovery item.
