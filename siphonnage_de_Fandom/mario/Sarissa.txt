'''Sarissa''' is the thirteenth boss faced by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in the VS. COM mode of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sarissa is a short witch with long light green hair extending to the ground and blue eyes. She wears an indigo magician's outfit, with two yellow buttons on her shirt and a yellow star pattern on her hat. She also wears long shoes. Sarissa carries a dark golden staff taller than herself, and the hook of the staff contains a red orb. Sarissa has never been shown using her staff.

===Personality traits===
Sarissa has some characteristics comparable to a {{w|feminist}}'s, telling [[Toad (character)|Toad]] that she should win, citing "ladies first," and that she likes players who respect women. Sarissa despises being called a little girl despite appearing as one.

If Sarissa loses against Toad, she uses her hat to cover her face in shame, presumably crying. If she wins, Sarissa jumps up and down happily.
