'''Local News''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'' as of [[Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s, and it returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Young Cricket]] & [[Master Mantis]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The object is to keep the microphone in front of the interviewee's mouth while they are being interviewed. When the text gets smaller, player has to readjust the microphone. If the microphone is consistently held in front of the interviewee's mouth, the player wins. However, if the letters get too small, the interviewee gets angry, and the player loses at the microgame. In ''WarioWare Gold'', Local News is a double-length microgame.

===''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''===
*'''Level 1:''' The player has to interview a baseball player, who does not move, If the player loses, the baseball player is only show to be angry.
*'''Level 2:''' The player has to interview a polar bear, who moves its head left or right. If the player loses, the polar bear roars at the camera.
*'''Level 3:''' The player has to interview an old lady, who moves around constantly. If the player loses, the old lady performs a fighting pose.

===''WarioWare Gold''===
*'''Level 1:''' The player has to interview a baseball player, who only moves once.
*'''Level 2:''' The player has to interview a gorilla in a jersey, who moves his head frequently. He is the only different character than in the microgame's original iteration.
*'''Level 3:''' The player has to interview an old lady named [[Chatty Kathy]], who moves back and forth while getting faster. Like in the original microgame, if the player loses, Chatty Kathy does a fighting pose.
