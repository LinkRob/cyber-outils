'''DVD for Me''' is a quest in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The quest is initiated at the [[Horror Condo]]'s first floor after the player's character agrees to listen to [[Ninji (character)|Ninji]]'s favor. Ninji explains that he wants a limited-edition [[DVD]] of [[Toad Force V]], and he asks the player's character to get one from [[Mushbert]], who is on the first floor of the [[Mushroom Condo]].

At the Mushroom Condo, Mushbert offers to explain what Toad Force V is. If he is prompted, Mushbert can also gush about the robot superhero show. Mushbert also owns so many copies of the DVD that he does not mind giving one away to the player's character.

Back at the Horror Condo, when the player's character gives the DVD to Ninji, he gets very excited and rewards the player with the [[Tile Trial]] [[Gaddget]]. After exclaims Ninji exclaims that he wants to watch the DVD immediately, the quest ends.
