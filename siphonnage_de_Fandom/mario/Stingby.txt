'''Stingby''' is an enemy which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Stingby is an airborne enemy resembling a bipedal bee. It has a yellow egg-shaped body with brown stripes all covered in fur. It has huge, cartoony eyes with a single stinger on its “nose”. It has two small legs, and small wings sitting on the top of its body.

=== Behavior ===
They chase after [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] when they are encountered and the Bros may defeat it by jumping on top of it or by any power-up. These enemies are located mainly in sky levels and can only fly straight unlike other bees which can fly up and/or down.

== In {{series|Super Mario}} ==
===''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''===
Stingbies are first encountered in [[World 2-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-4]]. They are common in levels taking place in the sky or over large [[pit]]s. Stingbies fly at a certain height and cannot move up or down. When a Stingby spots [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], it slowly flies after them angrily. Despite their aggressive nature, Stingbies aren’t very durable and can be defeated by any form of attack including stomps or the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Boomerang Flower]], [[Statue Leaf]], [[Star]], or an [[Invincibility Leaf]].

===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' / ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''===
Stingbies return in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'', in this game, they are first seen in the [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 1]] level, [[Switch Scramble Circus]]. They go on to appear in several later levels such as [[Switchboard Falls]], just as before Stingbies will attempt to attack [[Mario]] and friends if they come near, Stingbies are once again weak enemies and can be defeated with any attack including a stomp or the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Bell]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Boomerang Flower]], [[Star]], [[Lucky Bell]], [[Invincibility Leaf]], or [[Invincibility Bell]].

The ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign features a cat variation, aptly named [[Cat Stingby]].

===''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''===
They can be placed as enemies in the ''Super Mario 3D World'' style. They can also be given a [[Super Mushroom]] to turn them into [[Big Stingby|Big Stingbies]]. Similar to ''Super Mario 3D Land'', Stingbies cannot move horizontally, this time always horizontally, due to the 2.5D perspective.

== In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' ==
[[File:Honey_House.png|thumb|Metal Mario near some Stingbies in ''Mario Kart 7'']]
Stingbies are obstacles in the battle course [[Honeybee Hive]]. They fly around, knocking out any [[kart]]s upon contact.

== In ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''==
Stingbies are enemies exclusively within the [[Wii U]] version, where they appear in [[Conkdor Canyon]]. Stingbies chase after [[Captain Toad]] upon encountering him, but he can defeat a Stingby by either landing on it or from a [[spin]].

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''==
While normal Stingbies do not appear, there is an unused paper variant named [[Paper Stingby]].

==In ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''==
Stingbies appear in the boss minigame [[Mega Stingby Stompers]], where they assist [[Mega Stingby]], a giant Stingby who appears for the first time. Stingbies specifically appear when Mega Stingby is at half-health, but the characters can defeat a Stingby by stomping it, as usual.

== Trivia ==
*A similar enemy called an [[Urban Stingby]] appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. They at first appear in a [[Urban Stingby Larva|larval form]], but then grow to their adult form once [[Mario]] comes nearby. At that point, they charge. If they hit something, be it friend or foe, the Urban Stingby will self-destruct, dealing damage to anybody near the impact.
*The Stingbies slightly resemble [[Digga]]s from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.
