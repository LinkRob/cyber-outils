'''Lucky ? Blocks''', titled '''? Lottery''' in PAL releases, is an unlockable minigame in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. It is hosted by a [[Purple Toad]] in a [[Toad House]]. To unlock it, the player has to clear {{world-link|3|mortonair|World 3-Airship (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|World 3-Airship}}.

==Gameplay==
Lucky ? Blocks lets the player try their luck at winning items, with each attempt costing 100 [[coin]]. At first, the prizes include [[1-Up Mushroom]]s, [[Transformation Item]]s, and [[Skill Item]]s, and a larger variety of prizes are added as the player progresses in their journey. After the player has completed every normal [[world]], rare Skill Items and [[Star Gem]]s are added to the prizes. By completing {{world-link|sp3|mortonair|★World 3-Airship (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|★World 3-Airship}}, the player has a better chance of winning rare Skill Items, and completing {{world-link|sp6|royair|★World 6-Airship (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|★World 6-Airship}} further increases their chance at winning a rare item.
