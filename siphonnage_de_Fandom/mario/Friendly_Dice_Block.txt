The '''Friendly Dice Block''' is [[Princess Daisy]]'s unique [[Dice Block]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

== In ''Mario Party: Star Rush'' ==
In [[Toad Scramble]], if a character has Daisy in the party, her Friendly Dice Block can roll either a 3, a 4, a 5, or a number corresponding to how many allies the character has to add to their total roll.

The Friendly Dice Block and [[Rosalina]]'s [[Wondrous Dice Block]] are the only Dice Blocks to have a side with a variable number dependent on the character's current status.
