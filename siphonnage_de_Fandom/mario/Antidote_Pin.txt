'''Antidote Pin''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
The Antidote Pin is an item that "prevents poison damage". When any user equips the item, the player is immune to poison during battle. The player can buy the item in [[Mushroom Kingdom]], [[Rose Town]] and [[Seaside Town]]. The item costs 28 coins and the player can sell the item for 14 coins.
