A '''Sipping Stalk''' is an object that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
[[File:WMODSippingStalkWatered.png|left]] Sipping Stalks can be only found throughout [[Sneezemore Cave]]. They appear as small weeds when unwatered but when they do get watered, the become very large stalks that [[Wario]] can climb up on. In order to water a Sipping Stalk, Wario must lure a [[Snow Globe (enemy)|Snow Globe]] or [[Blow Globe]] directly over the unwated stalk and defeat it. Doing so will cause a water droplet to fall and land on the Sipping Stalk allowing it to grow in size insantly.
