'''All Fired Up''' is a Battle [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. The name comes from a term that describes someone who is either really excited or angry about something.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, players must attempt to dodge the [[Lava Bubble|Podoboo]]s as long as possible. Podoboos gather at the center and then create a circle, a cross, or a wave-like shape, and they repeat forming and un-forming until the time is up. The number of Podoboos is not constant. If a player makes contact with a Podoboo, they lose. The last player standing, or anyone still standing when time is up, wins the minigame. If everyone gets hit by a Podoboo, the minigame ends in a draw.
