'''Penguru''' is an old [[penguin]] who debuts in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and reappears in its sequel, ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

== History ==

=== ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ===
[[Mario]] encounters Penguru in four different [[galaxy|galaxies]].

Penguru is first seen in [[Drip Drop Galaxy]], where he and some other [[penguin]]s appear on an island during the galaxy's sole mission, [[Giant Eel Outbreak]]. Here, Penguru and the Penguins are horrified of how the [[Gringill]]s have escaped and are threatening underwater life. Penguru asks Mario to get rid of the Gringills, and in doing so, Mario receives a [[Power Star]] from Penguru.

Penguru makes his next appearance in [[Bigmouth Galaxy]]. Here, Penguru tells Mario that it is his hideaway that is perfect for fishing and being left alone. At the start, Penguru directs Mario on where to go.

His third appearance is in the [[Sea Slide Galaxy]]. Penguru's actual home is here, specifically on an egg-shaped island called [[Penguru's Place]]. Here, during the [[The Silver Stars of Sea Slide]] mission, Penguru guards one of the [[Silver Star]]s here.

Penguru makes one last appearance in the [[Grand Finale Galaxy]]. Like in Bigmouth Galaxy, Penguru has only a minor appearance here, and he is shown to be looking at [[Peach's Castle]].

=== ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' ===
Penguru has more of a minor appearance, and he only appears in only two galaxies, [[Hightail Falls Galaxy]] and [[Cosmic Cove Galaxy]].

In Hightail Falls Galaxy, Penguru congratulates Mario and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] if they manage to get onto the big ramp. Penguru has a more notable appearance in [[Cosmic Cove Galaxy]], where he stands on a lone platform in every mission. In the first mission, [[Twin Falls Hideaway]], Penguru provides a big hint to Mario on how to get the Power Star, which is by [[Wall Jump|wall jumping]] between two frozen waterfalls. In the second mission, [[Exploring the Cosmic Cavern]], Penguru gives Mario some background information on the frozen waterfalls.

==Physical description==
Penguru is a black penguin. He is old, which is shown from his thick white eyebrows and a long white beard.
