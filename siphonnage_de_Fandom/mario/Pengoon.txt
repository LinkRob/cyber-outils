A '''Pengoon''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Pengoon exist in two varieties, blue and pink, the former being the species' main color.

===Behavior===
Pengoons tend to hide in certain areas. If [[Wario]] gets near a Pengoon, it emerges from its hiding place and begins to spit chunks of ice at him. Blue Pengoons only spit one ice chunk out at a time, while the pink Pengoons spit out three at a time.

===Habitats===
Pengoons are encountered in two icy [[level]]s, [[Slipshod Slopes]] and [[Freezing Fields]]. The blue variety is more common than the pink one.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Pengoon's name is a portmanteau of "penguin" and "goon."
