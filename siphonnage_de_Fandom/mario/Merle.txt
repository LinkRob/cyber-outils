'''Merle''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is [[Merlon]]'s son who lives along with the [[Ninji]]s and [[Star Kid]]s at [[Starborn Valley]]. As revealed by [[Goombario]], he is also the father of [[Merluvlee]] and [[Merlow]].

== Appearance ==
Merle greatly resembles his father Merlon except that his beard is yellow in color instead of white.

== History ==
After hearing about [[Mario]]'s quest to find the [[Star Spirit]]s, Merle sends a Ninji out to him to invite him to Starborn Valley. Merle eventually meets up with Mario and explains the Star Kids [[Monstar|actions]] earlier. Merle then tells a story to Mario before giving him the [[Scarf]] which is needed with another item to get to the [[Crystal Palace]]. Merle then recommends Mario to speak with [[Mayor Penguin]] in [[Shiver Town]] for the [[Bucket (Paper Mario)|other item]].
