'''Bullet Bill Bullies''' is a 1-vs-Rivals [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Gameplay==
The solo character is on an [[Arrow Platform]], and the rivals must shoot [[Bullet Bill]]s at them. If the solo character gets hit by a Bullet Bill, they lose one heart. The solo character has two hearts and three hearts for if there are a total of two or three rivals respectively. If the solo character loses all of their hearts before the 20-second [[time limit|timer]] expires, the rivals win, but the solo player has at least one heart remaining when the minigame ends, they win.
