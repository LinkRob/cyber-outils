'''Mario Matrix''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place inside of a computer inside of a laboratory.

==Opening==
The four players walk into four capsule-like containers, all connected to a computer. The players all get transported into the computer.

==Gameplay==
There is a large hexagon made of 35 smaller [[hexagon]]s, and the players must click on as many illuminated hexagons as they can by pointing at the TV screen and pressing {{button|wii|A}} or {{button|wii|B}} on their [[Wii Remote]]s. The hexagons are illuminated for only a few seconds. The player who clicks on the most hexagons wins. If nobody clicks on any hexagons, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Ending==
[[File:TheMarioMatrixEnd.png|thumb|[[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], [[Waluigi]], and [[Wario]] left in the computer in the ending.]]
Winning players are transported back out through the capsule, performing a victory dance, while the losing players remain hovering on the computer screen. It is possible for none of the characters to remain. The other three players do nothing (only in Free Play). If the minigame ends in a tie, the camera scrolls right to the computer screen and shows all four players hovering.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Its name is a reference to the 1999 science-fiction movie ''{{wp|The Matrix}}''. Like this minigame, it the theme of a cybernetic world inside the computer; the green background of the minigame even resembles the green rain of codes from the movie.
