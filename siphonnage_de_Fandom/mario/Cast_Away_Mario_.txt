'''Cast Away Mario!''' is a minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. Playing it requires scanning the game's corresponding [[Princess Peach]] card via an [[e-Reader]] device.

==Gameplay==
There is an underwater creature in Peach's thought bubble (either a [[Cheep Cheep]], an indigo [[Blurp]], a green [[Rip Van Fish]], or a [[Sidestepper]]). Mario has to lower the fishing line and catch whichever underwater creature is depicted in the thought bubble.
