==History==
Chunky makes a few cameo appearances alongside [[Diddy Kong (Illumination)|Diddy Kong]] and [[Dixie Kong (Illumination)|Dixie Kong]] where they was watching the battle between [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] and [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]]. Chunky was enjoying the matches between the two and even when Mario becomes victorious at the end, Chunky was seen applauding for Mario alongside the other Kongs. He then joins the Kongs in fighting Bowser's army on [[Rainbow Road]].
