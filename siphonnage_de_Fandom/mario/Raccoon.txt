'''Raccoons'''<ref>''Super Mario Sunshine'' [[Prima Games|Prima]]'s Official Strategy Guide, page 40.</ref> are a species in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Raccoons are plump creatures directly based on the real-world {{w|Japanese raccoon dog}}s, known as tanukis in Japanese. Raccoons wear a yellow sash, a sun hat, and blue pants. The design of Raccoons in ''Super Mario Sunshine'' is inspired by tanuki statues seen across Japan; the sun hat, shirt/coat, tail, and sash are all trademarks of the statues. 

=== Habitats ===
Raccoons live on [[Isle Delfino]]. They are presumably the rarest species found there, given that [[Mario]] only encounters three of them in total.

==History==
===''Super Mario Sunshine''===
[[File:Blue Coin Shop SM3D All-Stars.png|thumb|The father and son Raccoon in the Boathouse in ''Super Mario Sunshine''{{'}}s enhanced port for ''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]'']]
In ''Super Mario Sunshine'', Mario first encounters two Raccoons in [[Delfino Plaza]], where a father and son Raccoon run the [[Boathouse]] together. Here, Mario can trade in ten [[Blue Coin]]s at once for a [[Shine Sprite]]. The father only accepts Blue Coins in increments of ten, and if [[Mario]] tries to ask for a Shine Sprite for less than ten Blue Coins, the father exclaims that he cannot sell the Shine Sprites at such a low price.

In [[Noki Bay]], there is another adult Raccoon who owns a small rowboat shack. A sign near the shop says that the boats can be rented for 90,000 [[coin]]s, a number which Mario cannot obtain, but after Mario completes the [[Uncork the Waterfall]] episode, the shack owner allows Mario to use his boats for free. 

The father's voice clips are provided by [[Scott Burns]].<ref>[[:File:Scott burns sms 2.jpg|Confirmation from Scott Burns via e-mail.]]</ref>

In the Super Mario Mash-up in ''[[Minecraft]]'', [[w:c:minecraft:Wandering Trader|Wandering Traders]] are replaced by Raccoons.
