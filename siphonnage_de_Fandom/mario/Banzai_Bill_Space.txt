The '''Banzai Bill Space''' is a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
Banzai Bill Spaces only appear in [[Banzai Bill's Mad Mountain]]. If a character lands on a Banzai Bill Space, a [[Banzai Bill]] gets launched out of a [[Banzai Bill Blaster]], traveling straightforward, and whoever is hit is sent back to the start, or the halfway point if it has been activated.
