'''Deny''' is a [[Captain Command]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Description ==
If the user uses Deny, they shout and stop any enemies who are using a Special Skill.

==Flavor text==
*In-game description: "''When an enemy is about to use a Special Skill, I shout to stop them!''"
