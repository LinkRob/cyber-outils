'''Ants''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
Ants are a basic enemy walk back and forth. Like most other enemies, if [[Mario]] touches an Ant, he takes damage. He can defeat an Ant by either [[jump]]ing onto it or by shooting a [[fireball]] at it as [[Fire Mario]]. 

=== Variants ===
Ants have a few of their own variants:

*[[Chikunto]]: An ant covered in spikes
*[[Dokanto]]: Has a cannon on its head
*[[Goronto]]: Wears a miner's helmet

=== Etymology ===
Ants are both named after and based on [[Wikipedia:Ant|ants]] from the real world.

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
Ants most prominently appear in [[The Ant Monsters]] along with their variants.
