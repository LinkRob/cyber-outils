A '''Big Bone Piranha Plant''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' and later returns in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. It doubles as a variant of both a [[Super Piranha Plant]] and a [[Bone Piranha Plant]].
