'''Lava Blubbles''' (originally named '''Fireballs''') are blue [[Sparky|Sparkies]] found in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. They are a blue variant of [[Lava Babble]]s found in [[Booster Tower]], in the floor of the mine room. They ambush [[Mario]] when walked over.

==Battle==
In battle, Fireballs tend to float up in the air and attack with a dash for physical damage. They are capable of using [[Drain]] and [[Mega Drain]] as special moves in battle. Fireballs have high attack, magic attack, and evade, making them strong and hard to attack, but not durable. They can deal damage with moves like [[Hot Shot]] and [[Fire Saber]]. 

===Strategy===
Fireballs have very low physical defense and [[Heart Point|HP]] so taking them down with a physical attack isn't difficult. They are weak to both Jump and Ice but since [[Mallow]] most likely hasn't learned [[Snowy]] during that point in the game yet, Mario's [[Super Jump]] is effective on them (though it's still recommended to use just physical attacks to save on [[Flower Point|FP]]).

==Trivia==
*Enemies that resemble Lava Blubbles appear in the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series, specifically in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' (as well as its [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]] on the [[Nintendo Switch]]). These are cyan-colored [[Lava Bubble (Paper Mario)|Lava Bubbles]] called [[Ember]]s. It's possible that Embers were inspired by the Lava Blubble's design, given that both enemies are essentially Lava Bubbles that have been given a blue hue and differing stats from their parent species.
**Both are also similar to the [[Blue Podoboo]]s that appear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' as well as the [[Lava Bouncer]]s from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.
