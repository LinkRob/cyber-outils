The '''Frosty Frontier Stage''' is a world which appears in ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''.

==Overview==
Hence the name, it is a frosty land with icy floors, snow, [[Mr. Blizzard]]s, and several ice-skating [[Shy Guy]]s. At the start of the level is [[Toad's Tent]].

Mario will encounter four Penguins, along with a big [[Freezie]]. His objective is the bash the Freezie into an icehole. Following this, Mario must enter to find a cold, watery level with a sunken ship. Here, he must battle against a [[Porcupuffer]] and its brethren with the help of [[Bob-omb]]s as artillery weapons. Once the enemy is defeated, Mario takes the [[Star Key]] and presses on. Also present here are some [[Monty Mole]]s with parka clothing that are difficult to defeat.
