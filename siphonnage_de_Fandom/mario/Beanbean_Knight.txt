'''Beanbean Knights''' are [[Beanish]] who serve as guards of the [[Beanbean Castle]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Physical description==
[[File:Beanbean Guard.png|frame|left]]
Beanbean Knights have green skin and hold a green, white shield with a happy expression.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
The Beanbean Knights first appeared in [[Hoohoo Village]], assuming that they have been lying and kidnapped [[Prince Peasley]] until [[Tolstar]] explained that they were finding [[Cackletta]] with Princess Peach's lost voice. They appeared again in front of [[Fawful]] begging to return them Prince Peasley. They also guarded the castle, allowing the Bros. to enter the Beanbean Castle learning they were friends with the prince.
