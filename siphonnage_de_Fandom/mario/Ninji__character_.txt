'''Ninji''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' and a representative of his species of the [[Ninji|same name]]. Ninji lives on the first floor of the [[Horror Condo]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Ninji is one of several fans of the show [[Toad Force V]]. He wants a copy of the limited edition DVD and says someone in the [[Mushroom Condo]] possibly has it. Once the player character obtains the DVD there from [[Mushbert]] and returns to Ninji, he is very happy. As a reward, he gives the player the [[Tile Trial]] [[Gaddget]].
