The '''Hit-POW Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be obtained from the [[Koopaseum]] or as an item dropped by [[Mrs. Thwomp]]. Equipping it increases the wearer's [[Power|POW]] in battle up until they take damage.
