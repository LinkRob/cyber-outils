A '''Starslap''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are starfish enemies encountered throughout [[Rose Way]]. On the overworld, Starslaps can be found usually sleeping but when woken, they will hover in the air. They have stronger relatives known as [[Zeostar]]s.

==Battle==
Starslaps mainly attack by dancing at a party member. Starslaps can also use [[Recover]] to heal themselves and other enemies.

===Strategy===
Starslaps are weak to fire and thunder attacks meaning [[Mario]]'s [[Fire (element)|fire]] techniques and [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunder (element)|thunder]] techniques are effective against them.
