'''Shriek Mansion 3-5''' is the fifth level of [[Shriek Mansion]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. The level teaches you a technique of quietly going pass by enemies by pressing {{button|ds|L}} and {{button|ds|R}}.
==Toads==
*'''Toad 1''': Bounce on the springs to the highest peak, ignore the purple pipe and go down to the right for the right landing.
*'''Toad 2''': Go through the door on the top-middle corner of the blue blocks and hopefully will lead you to the bottom right corner of the door under the blue blocks.
*'''Toad 3''': Find the ninth door from the left and hopefully, next to you is a ! Box.
==Puzzle Piece==
Fall off the brown or blue falling platform where the two Big Mad Boos are found and find the Puzzle Piece close to the right.
