'''Candies''' are recurring items in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}.

==Description==
Candies are orange star consumable items that restore approximately 40% of [[Mario]]'s or [[Luigi]]'s [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Bros. Point|BP]]. They have three variants that restore more health, from least to most: [[Super Candy|Super Candies]] (60%), [[Ultra Candy|Ultra Candies]] (80%), and [[Max Candy|Max Candies]] (complete); all of these upgrades have the same appearance as candies. They appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]],'' ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. In the former two, Candies share their design with the [[Star Candy]], but ''Brothership'' gives them a spherical design with a pink and yellow wrapper.

Candies are functionally identical to [[Golden Mushroom]]s and [[Star Candy|Star Candies]] from previous ''Mario & Luigi'' games, functioning identically to them with the exception that a Star Candy can cure status effects and always restores health and special points fully, whereas this Candy has different healing levels for each variant and cannot cure status effects.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
At the start of the game, Candies can be bought from [[Item Shop|item shops]] for 25 [[coin]]s each. [[Capnap]]s and [[Propellion]]s have a 2% chance and 10% chance of dropping a Candy, respectively.
