'''Mega Smash''' is a [[badge]] which only appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
This badge can be worn by [[Mario]] if he pays three [[Badge Point|BP]], and wearing the badge gives Mario a new [[hammer]] attack requiring six [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

Mario finds this badge on the upper left path in the center of [[Flower Fields]], near the [[Sun]]'s tower. Using [[Bombette]] to blow up the rock above, Mario can fall into a hole in the top of a small, ruined building and find this badge.
