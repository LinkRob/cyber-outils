A '''Slow Shroom''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is a [[Mushroom (Paper Mario series)|Mushroom]] that is pink and purple in color.

== Description ==
Eating a Slow Shroom restores 2 [[Heart Point|HP]] over the course of ten turns. Slow Shrooms can be purchased at the [[Deepdown Depot]] for 15 [[coin]]s each. Slow Shrooms can be used in some dishes including with a [[Point Swap]].
