}}'''Sunset Wilds''' is the final course in the [[Lightning Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]].'' It takes place at dusk in the desert. By the third lap, the sun sets and the sky turns starry and dark in the desert. Hazards here are [[Shy Guy]]s, which dwell in tents and can grab onto a player's kart and take [[coin]]s from the player if not careful. They also cause slowdown of players' karts. The track returns in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', first appearing in the [[Sunset Tour]]. It also returns in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' as the second course of the [[Cherry Cup]] in the [[Booster Course Pass]].
