'''Cheese Chicken''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. It can be used by [[Bowser]] and replaces the function of a [[Star Candy]] for him.

==Usage==
Cheese Chickens are used pretty much the same way as Star Candies but can be only used by Bowser. They restore a gigantic amount of HP and BP.
