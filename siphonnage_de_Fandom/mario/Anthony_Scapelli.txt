Anthony Scapelli is a minor antagonist in the [[Super Mario Bros. (film)|Super Mario Bros movie]] (1993) Who is played by [[wikipedia:Gianni Russo|Gianni Russo]]. He is the owner of a larger rival plumbing company to the Mario Bros. When he finds out Dasiy is digging for fossils in his construction site, he implies  that he is responsible for the disappearance of young women around the city(which is eventually revealed to be false)when that fails to intimidate her, he instead floods the passages. In the Koopahari desert, Iggy and Spike reveal that Scapelli was the one who accidentally unearthed  the Meteorite during his  construction. Near the end of the movie [[Bowser|King Koopa]] Uses his Devo gun to Devolve him to a Chimpanzee.

=== Trivia ===

* He is a parody of the italian mob boss stereotype that was popularized by the real life gangster [https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Capone Al Capone].
* When king koopa exclaims "Monkey!" This is actually a false statement due to Chimpanzees being apes, not monkeys. The confusion likely stems from King Koopas ignorance of human evolutionary history.
