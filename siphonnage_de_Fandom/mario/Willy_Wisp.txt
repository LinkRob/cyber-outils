|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}{{Nihongo|'''Willy Wisp'''|ウィルオーウィスプ|Wiru Ō Wisupu|Will-o-Wisp}} is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used by certain enemies and bosses in the game.

==Effects==
When Willy Wisp is used, a red energy ball is tossed into the ground and appears over the targeted character. This attack deals rather high damage to a single character and cannot be blocked against.
