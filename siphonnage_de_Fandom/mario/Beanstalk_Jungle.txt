'''Beanstalk Jungle''', or '''{{world|Waters|leaf}}''', is the secret course of [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by reaching the secret exit of [[Haunted Cargo Hold]], and its own completion will unlock [[Mount Fuzzy]].

==Layout==
The level starts on a platform next to a rapidly growing [[beanstalk]]. [[Luigi]] must jump on the [[Leaf Platform]]s, as the area autoscrolls. A [[? Block]] containing a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] is found nearby. A [[Lakitu]] throwing [[Piranha Plant Egg]]s is found, along with a [[Green Ring]]. The area then begins to scroll horizontally, and Luigi must jump across multiple beanstalks. Some [[Piranha Plant]]s and a [[Big Piranha Plant]] are found on the [[Bean Platform]]s. At the end of the area, a group of [[Waddlewing]]s and a red [[Warp Pipe]] that takes the player to an area with the [[Goal Pole]] are found.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': On the second beanstalk, wait for a leaf to appear under the Star Coin or use Latiku's cloud to fly to the coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Wait for a leaf to appear on the final beanstalk to jump to the Star Coin without dying.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Over the second to last beanstalk is a hidden area with a pipe in it; jump up there (jumping on a Waddlewing might help) and go in the hidden pipe revealing a hidden area with the Star Coin. If you went in the obvious red pipe shown, use the beanstalk after the pipe to get to the hidden area although you can't get the hidden 1Up Mushroom.
