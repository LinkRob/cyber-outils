'''Ice Bros. Feel the Heat''' is the seventh level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 23.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 2 [[Limbo Bro]]s. and 6 [[Ice Bro]]s. The enemy captain is an Ice Bro.

The final battle contains 3 Limbo Bros, and 5 Ice Bros. The enemy captain is a Limbo Bro, and he knows [[Chance]].

== Potential recruits ==
* Ice Bro (Lv. 23-24)
