'''Jellopus''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Jellopuses are featured in either cyan, purple, or yellow variations.

===Behavior===
A Jellopus's behavior varies between color variants. Blue Jellopuses swim around in a carefree manner, yellow ones swim in a wavy pattern, and purple Jellopuses discharge four balls of electrical energy that can damage [[Wario]] if he touches one.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Jellopus only appear in underwater levels that feature the [[Subwarine]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
A Jellopus's name is a portmanteau of "jellyfish" and "octopus," as they have characteristics of both.
