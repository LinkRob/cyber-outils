'''Liftoglobins''' are a type of [[Emoglobin]] that are non-talking platforms that inhabit [[Bowser]]'s body.

== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''/''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' ==
They first appear in [[Airway (Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story)|Bowser's lungs]]. Liftoglobins have a golden look in their natural state and turn blue in their frozen state. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] have to freeze them before they can stand on them. Sliding on the Liftoglobin causes it to move. Holding it causes it to stop.
