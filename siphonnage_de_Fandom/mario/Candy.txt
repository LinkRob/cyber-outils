*[[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candies]], items in ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' that are similar to the [[Orb|capsules]] of previous [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'']] games and have a variety of effects on the consumer or other players.
*[[Candy (Mario & Luigi series)|Candies]], consumables that restore [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Bros. Point|BP]] in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}.
*[[Candy Pop]]s, "creatures" found in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
*[[Candy Kong]], a character from the ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' franchise and the love interest of [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]].
**[[Candy Clone]], a robotic double that appears in the [[Donkey Kong Country (television series)|''Donkey Kong Country'' television series]].
*[[Star Candy]], a consumable that fully recovers all HP and [[Special Point|SP]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.
