'''Skeleton Ghosts''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]]. Skeleton Ghosts are encountered in three locations: [[Boneyard]], [[Graveyard]], and the [[Telephone Room]]. They sometimes do not get stunned by [[Luigi]]'s [[flashlight]]. Their attack consists of throwing a bone. After doing so, the Skeleton Ghost collapses shortly before rebuilding itself again afterward.

A Skeleton Ghost called Mr. Bones eventually wakes up in the Boneyard when [[Luigi]] is chased by [[Spooky]] whom is barking. In a while, Mr. Bones wakes up being disturbed by Spooky's barking. Skeleton Ghosts appear in the Graveyard, having to be defeated to encounter the area's boss [[Bogmire]].

==Trivia==
*Skeleton Ghosts are the ghosts aside from [[Boo]]s and [[Portrait Ghost]]s with the ability to talk.
