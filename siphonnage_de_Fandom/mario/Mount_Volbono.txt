{{Nihongo|'''Mount Volbono'''|ボルボーノ|Borubōno|, lit. Volbono}}, also known as ''The Bubbling Burg that Heats the Meat'', is a location in the [[Luncheon Kingdom]] in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. This location, inspired by Italy (or more specifically by [[wikipedia:Mount Vesuvius|Mount Vesuvius]]), is a combination of lava/volcano and food themes. The city's main inhabitants are notably [[Volbonans]].

==Layout==
Mount Volbono is surrounded by strange pink lava, its a colorful and vibrant palace that is famous for its cuisine, with dishes simmered over the volcano, and chock-full of the local produce that grows to enormous size thanks to the volcanic climate. On your way to Mount Volbono, you'll pass through the Old Town, ringed by the ruined wall that once enclosed the town. Atop the walls are little mounds of salt, carried by the wind and piled up naturally. The sights here are very different from the colorful scenery of Peronza Plaza, but equally breathtaking.

==Culture==
The highlight of Mount Volbono's year is the Cooking Carnival, where visitors from all over the world come to sample the famous Stupendous Stew. While cooking on a volcano is of course a grand spectacle, the dish itself has a surprisingly delicate flavor, described most frequently in reviews as a "melty deliciousity."''

==Notable Locations==
*Peronza Plaza: a town-like area
*Meat Plateau: a high hill that houses a giant slab of meat that can be captured.
*Top of the Peak Climb: an area close to the top of Mount Volbono.
*Salt Pile Isle: a small island resting in a sea of pink lava.
*Floating Sky Island: a little pink floating isle where the player appears after having used the portrait from the [[Wooded Kingdom]] or the [[Lake Kingdom]].

==History==
===''Super Mario Odyssey''===
When Mario and Cappy first visit this kingdom, Bowser and the Broodals have stolen the Stupendous Stew as food for the wedding reception. They must then defeat [[Spewart]]. Once successful, they must make way to the remaining Stupendous Stew to defeat [[Cookatiel]]. However, Mario and Cappy are blown off after the volcano erupts. Therefore, they must reach the pot a different way. After defeating Cookatiel, Mario and Cappy may visit Bowser's Kingdom, but their journey is interrupted by Bowser and the [[Ruined Dragon]], sending them crash-landing in the [[Ruined Kingdom]].

===''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''===
A new level inspired by the Luncheon Kingdom appears in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] and [[Nintendo Switch]] versions of ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''. [[Cookatiel]] appears an enemy in the level, spitting [[durian]]s at the player. [[Double Cherry|Double Cherries]] also appear in the level.

Due to its elevation, Mario should long jump/dive from the Top of the Peak Climb Checkpoint or from where the rolling pepper is launched

*'''68: Flying Over the Lava Islands:''' In the area with the "Treasure of the Lava Islands" Power Moon, the player must capture a Podoboo and jump into the first lava cannon seen there.
