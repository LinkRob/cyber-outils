'''Bros. Rock''' is a type of [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is rewarded for collecting all ten pieces of the [[Beanstar]].

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', Bros. Rock grants 20 [[Bros. Point|BP]] and 20 [[Power|POW]] to its user. In the Japanese version, the former was reduced to 16 BP.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', Bros. Rock has nearly identical stats to the original's Japanese version, except its POW is at 21, only one number higher than in both the Japanese and international releases of the original game. It also grants the [[HP-Up Jump]] special effect.
