The '''Gold Flower''' is a power-up that only appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Obtaining it turns [[Mario]] into [[Gold Mario]] and [[Luigi]] into [[Gold Mario|Silver Luigi]]. 

==History==
===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
Whenever a player visits any [[Rainbow Course]] except for World 1's, they can collect 8 red coins to receive a Gold Flower, which is immediately pocketed for later use. When the player uses it as a power-up, they become Gold Mario or Silver Luigi, and can throw gold/silver fireballs that turn everything they touch into coins. The amount of coins given depends on the enemy or object and whether a [[Gold Ring]] is active or not.

== Use ==
If you die or get to the end of a level or get hit you will lose the Gold flower and instead have a Normal [[Fire Flower]] instead. [[World 2-1 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 2-1]] is the first level in which the Gold Flower appears.
