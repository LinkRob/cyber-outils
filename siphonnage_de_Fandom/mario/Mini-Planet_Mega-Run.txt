'''Mini-Planet Mega-Run''' is the first [[mission]] of [[Battle Belt Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Objective==
[[Mario]] has to defeat every enemy on each planet to proceed. As Mario progresses, the enemies become increasingly harder to defeat. After all enemies are defeated, a [[Sling Star]] to the next planet appears. The last enemies that Mario has to defeat are four [[Silver Chomp]]s, and by doing so, he can collect a [[Key]] to unlock a [[Cage]] with the mission's [[Power Star]].
