}}'''Rome Avanti''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. As its name implies, it is set in [https://wikipedia.org/Roma_(city) Roma], the capital of [https://wikipedia.org/Italy Italy] (Italia in Italian). Rome Avanti's debut appearance occurred in the Night Tour, making it one of the few city courses from ''Mario Kart Tour'' to appear first in a tour not named after the location.

== Layout ==

=== Rome Avanti 1 ===
Rome Avanti 1 is the first layout of Rome Avanti, putting more focus on the Colosseum. It starts at a rocky path the leads to a [[wikipedia:Colosseum|Colosseum]]. In the Colosseum, it leads up to the top where the racers fly from the [[Glide Ramp]]. The next path includes pillars that act like ramps. There is also an arch off-road where the players can take the shortcut. The next section leads to a town square where there are arrow signs that guide the racers the direction they should take until they reach the finish line.

=== Rome Avanti 2 ===
Rome Avanti 2 is the second layout of Rome Avanti, putting more focus on the city streets, the Spanish Steps, and the Villa Borghese gardens.

=== Rome Avanti 3 ===
This route was released for the 2023 Anniversary Tour.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Rome Avanti's first two routes debuted in ''Mario Kart Tour'' in the [[Night Tour (2023)|Night Tour]] along with [[Moonview Highway]].
