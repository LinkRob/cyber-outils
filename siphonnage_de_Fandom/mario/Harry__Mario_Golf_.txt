'''Harry''' is a [[human]] golfer in ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]]. This is the only game that he appears in, thus making it his only playable appearance. He has a long drive and a shot that draws, similar to Mario. He has a maximum drive of 260 yeards and High Draw shot. Harry wears a blue shirt, blue hat and white pants with brown shoes. He also has a blonde goatee and hair, and his name may even be a pun on the term "hairy." He can be unlocked by beating him in the 5th Match of the Get Character mode.

Harry is also a very serious golfer, as he gets angry with [[bogey]]s, grunts in a disappointed way when he gets [[par]]s, and is only "okay" with [[birdie]]s. Additionally, if he loses a hole against another character, his icon is shown bending his club in anger.

Although Harry does not make an appearance in ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]], he is mentioned on the tournament board. There is a separate character named [[Harry (Mario Tennis series)|Harry]] featured in the Game Boy Color ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.
