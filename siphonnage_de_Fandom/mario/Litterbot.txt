'''Litterbot''' is one of [[Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
Using the [[Wii Remote]], the player must control the robot and navigate it to pick up the [[barrel]]s. If either time runs out or the robot hits a van, the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' There are three barrels on the ground.
*'''Level 2:''' There are two stationary barrels and one rolling barrel.
*'''Level 3:''' There are two stationary barrels, one rolling barrel, and a moving van.
