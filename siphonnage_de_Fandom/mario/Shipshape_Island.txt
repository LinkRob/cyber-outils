'''Shipshape Island''' is a location that appears in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Super Mario'' series]]. It is a part island and part ship traveling around seas of [[Concordia]]. It is the home to most [[Concordian]] residents and where [[Cozette]], [[Connie]] and [[Snoutlet]] live. Due to its role as the hub, Shipshape Island lacks its own [[Lighthouse (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Lighthouse]]; however, other islands' Lighthouses can connect to it. Connie owns the ship, but she temporarily gives it to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] as they take residence by the events of [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']] until the end.

==Description==
The Shipshape Island is a ship island used by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] in order to navigate over the waters of [[Concordia]]. The island features trees, grass, sand and several hills, waterfalls and landmarks.

A large tree serves as a mast, while the leaves serves as its sail. The island seems to be powered by some kind of energy that allows it to move, as supposed with the electric bulb on top of the mast. This tree is actually the new replacement [[Uni-Tree]] that [[Connie]] has been taking care of after [[Zokket]] desecrated the original. On the front of the island there is the [[Shipshape's Cannon]], that can be used by the protagonists in order to access the various island of Concordia.

[[Maykit and Billdit|Maykit]] sets up a [[Shipshape Island's Workshop|shop]] on this island after she is rescued on [[Twistee Island]] courtesy of [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] reconnecting the island's [[Lighthouse (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Lighthouse]].

[[Connie]] and [[Snoutlet]] seem to be residents of the island.
