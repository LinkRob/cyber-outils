'''Mario Star''' (known as '''Marioro''' in Japan) is a sponsor in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart 64'' ==
Mario Star made several appearances as trackside banners and signs in different courses.

=== List of appearances ===
* Trackside banners in [[Luigi Raceway]]
* Signs in [[Moo Moo Farm]]
* Signs in [[Kalimari Desert]]
* Trackside banners and signs in [[Mario Raceway]]
* Signs in [[Royal Raceway]]

== In ''[[Mario Party]]'' ==
Mario Star appeared in [[Slot Car Derby]] as a sign.

== Etymology ==
Marioro is based on [[wikipedia:Marlboro (cigarette)|Marlboro]]. Interestingly, they both contain the same red polygon.
