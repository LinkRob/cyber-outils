'''Mini-Game Park''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Description==
The Mini-Game Park is a park located within [[Mini-Game Land]]. It appears to be controlled by a [[Piranha Plant]] who constantly chases a [[butterfly]]. This park is where [[List of minigames in Mario Party 2|Minigame]]s purchased from [[Woody]] can be played freely. The Park dwells five different colored [[tree]]s, with one assigned for each type of minigame, while the [[apple]]s found on them each represent a different minigame.

==In ''Mario Party 2''==
Three of the trees are default while the other two can be unlocked via completing the [[Mini-Game Coaster]].

Additionally, the minigame [[Dungeon Dash]] will be unlocked when every 4-player, 1 vs 3, and 2 vs 2 Minigame is bought. After the player clears the [[Mini-Game Coaster]], on normal and hard difficulties, the player will unlock the Item and Battle Mini-Game Trees. Once there are three Item and Battle Mini-Games to purchase, the player is able to play the new minigame [[Rainbow Run (minigame)|Rainbow Run]] in the park. Once all minigames are unlocked, the games can be played when desired.
