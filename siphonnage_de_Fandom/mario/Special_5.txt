'''Special 5''' (also referred as '''Special World 5''') is the fifth special world and the thirteenth world overall in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. The overworld theme is a brown desert during nighttime, with cacti and visible brown dust in the air. This world has 6 levels, 5 main levels, and a [[Castle]], as well as an additional [[Mystery Box]]. The overworld theme is a remix of [[World 5 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 5]]'s overworld theme.
