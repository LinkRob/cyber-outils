A '''Dizzy Dial''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==In ''[[Paper Mario]]''==
[[File:Dizzy Dial.png|thumb|left]]
The Dizzy Dial in ''Paper Mario'' is a basic and somewhat useful item. These dangerous devices allow [[Mario]] to target his foes with a deadly status known as [[Dizzy|Dizziness]]. When an enemy is under the Dizzy status, they attack and sometimes miss. An item like this works well combined with badges such as [[Pretty Lucky]]. Mario can easily find this item through means of purchasing them through [[Item Shop|shops]]. [[Harry's Shop]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and [[Igor's Shop]] in [[Boo's Mansion]] sell this item.

==In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''==
The Dizzy Dial works the same as in ''Paper Mario''. It is a powerful item when used against stronger opponents that deal hefty damage to a lower leveled player. These items can be purchased at [[Westside Goods]], a shop in western [[Rogueport]]. The Dizzy Dial must also be purchased after the [[Dried Shroom]] in order to see [[Don Pianta]].
