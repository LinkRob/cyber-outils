==Profile==
Ball 'n' Chains are a type of [[Spiked Ball|spiked ball]] on a chain. Ball 'n' Chains are usually found in [[castle]]s and similar environments and are often indestructible. Their behavior varies depending on the game, switching between spinning around in a circular motion and swinging repetitively in a pendulum motion. The chain also changes, with most games allowing [[Mario]] to safely pass through it unharmed.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario World''====
[[File:Ball 'n' Chain SMW GBA.png|thumb|Some Ball 'n' Chains in Ludwig's Castle in ''Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2'']]
Ball 'n' Chains first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', where they are found in [[castle]] and [[fortress]] levels, such as [[Ludwig's Castle]]. They continuously rotate from a [[Hard Block|Gray Block]]. While the spiked ball damages [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], he can still pass through the chain harmlessly. Mario or Luigi can perform a [[Spin Jump]] to harmlessly bounce off the spiked ball.

In regular playthroughs, Ball 'n' Chains are indestructible. While they can be destroyed through the effects of the [[Star|Super Star]], this power-up item is never located near a Ball 'n' Chain. However, through hacking, it is shown that if [[Invincible Mario]] were to destroy a Ball 'n' Chain, its sprite changes into a defeated [[Bony Beetle]].
{{br|left}}

====''New Super Mario Bros.''====
[[Image:Ball 'n' Chain Sky Castle.png|thumb|right|Mario grabbing a Star Coin on a Ball 'n' Chain's block.]]
The Ball 'n' Chain object also appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' in the [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7-Castle]]. In this game, The Ball 'n' Chains rotate around the block quickly in this level. A [[Star Coin]] even appears on one of the blocks a Ball 'n' Chain is attached to requiring for some quick maneuvers to obtain it. Just as before, the Spiked Ball is harmful to [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] but the chain isn't, additionally their blocks can be stood on. A Ball 'n' Chain cannot be destroyed in this game.


====''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''====
[[Image:Ball 'n' Chain Secret.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] in a secret room where a Ball 'n' Chain is found.]]
The Ball 'n' Chain object then reappears in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', however in this game, they are rather rare obstacles with only two appearing in the whole game. in the secret areas of [[Lemmy]]'s [[World 3-Tower (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|Fortress]] and [[Wendy]]'s [[World 4-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|Castle]]. In the former, the Ball 'n' Chain is seen guarding one of the level's [[Star Coin]]s within some large moving blocks and in the latter, it is seen in an underwater section attached to a spinning cross-shaped pendulum platform guarding a [[Red Ring]]. Unlike the previous game, the Ball 'n' Chain can be destroyed using a [[Starman]] and unlike previous games, the chains are now attached to nails in walls rather than blocks. Just as before, Ball 'n' Chains are seen rotating and only the ball is harmful.


====''Super Mario 3D Land''====
[[File:Ball'n'Chain3D.png|thumb|left|A Ball 'n' Chain in ''Super Mario 3D Land'']]
Ball 'n' Chains make their 3D debut in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. They are depicted purple with yellow spikes, and the chain is also yellow. Ball 'n' Chains do not have shackles attached to them, unlike ''Super Mario World''. They sway back and forth like a pendulum and no longer spin. Ball 'n' Chains first appear in [[World 1-2 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 1-2]] and often appear in cave and castle levels. Ball 'n' Chains can be destroyed with the effects of a [[Starman]], [[Invincibility Leaf]], or [[Statue Leaf]].

====''New Super Mario Bros. U'' / ''New Super Luigi U'' / ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''====
[[Image:Ball 'n' Chain Powerful.png|thumb|right|The Mario Crew evade the dangerous Ball 'n' Chains.]]
Ball 'n' Chains appear again in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', in this game, they are referred to as <b>Pendulums</b>. They are more hazardous than before, possessing spikes all around, even their chains, making those a hazard as well. As a result, Ball 'n' Chains can only be passed under. Ball 'n' Chains only sway and do not spin unlike before. They appear in only two levels, [[Pendulum Castle]] and [[Impossible Pendulums]]. Ball 'n' Chains are also indestructible and cannot be destroyed, even from a [[Starman]]. If [[Nabbit]] touches a Ball 'n' Chain, he gets knocked back a bit within taking damage. They also have a giant counterpart, [[Giant Ball 'n' Chain]]s.

The Prima Guide for the game generically refers to Ball 'n' Chains as <b>Spinners</b>.

===''Super Princess Peach''===
{{main|Bowser-Ball 'n' Chain}}
In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', pendulum-style Ball 'n' Chains called [[Bowser-Ball 'n' Chain]]s appear in [[Bowser's Villa]], where they swing back and forth between the background and foreground, similar to pendulums. The chains resemble their appearance in ''Super Mario World'', but the spiked balls are colored green and textured to resemble [[Bowser's Shell]].

===''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''===
Motionless Ball 'n' Chains make a minor reappearance in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', where they are seen hanging from the ceiling as decorations in [[Baby Bowser's Castle]].
{{br}}

===''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''===
[[File:Ball n ChainMK8.png|thumb|200px|A Ball and Chain in Bowser's Castle in ''Mario Kart 8'']]
Ball 'n' Chains (now spelled '''Ball and Chains''') return in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', here, a single Ball and Chain appears as an obstacle in [[Bowser's Castle (Mario Kart 8)|Bowser's Castle]]. The obstacle acts virtually the same as the [[pendulum]]s in [[Tick-Tock Clock (race course)|Tick-Tock Clock]], except that players spin out upon hitting it, also causing them to drop [[Coin]]s in the process. The Ball and Chain moves faster during the final lap of the race. Unlike in previous games, the spikes on the ball appear to be blunt, similarly to [[Thwomp]]s. Racers can phase through a Ball 'n' Chain without an issue while under the effects of a Star or [[Bullet Bill]].

===''Mario Party 10''===
[[Image:Ball 'n' Chain Spiked Ball Scramble.png|thumb|right|Ball 'n' Chains in Spiked Ball Scramble.]]
Pendulum-style Ball 'n' Chains appear in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'' in the minigame [[Spiked Ball Scramble]]. Ball 'n' Chains hang from the ceiling and swing across the path, and they appear in standard and larger varieties. Players are required to time their movements to avoid being hit while staying on track to avoid the [[lava]]. Players should generally choose paths with fewer Ball 'n' Chains to avoid them, with the paths ahead being revealed as the screen scrolls. If a player touches a Ball 'n' Chain, they get eliminated.
{{br}}

===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''====
[[File:Ball 'n' Chain Fort.png|thumb|200px|Yellow Yoshi near a Ball 'N' Chain in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'']]
Rotation-style Ball 'n' Chains appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. They are colored orange instead of black, and their chains are replaced by spiked bars, some of which are double-sided. They, along with similar objects such as [[Ball and Chain (windup)|Windup Ball and Chain]]s, are operated by certain [[Boo Guy]]s. As usual, Ball 'N' Chains typically appear in [[fortress|fort]]s, such as [[Burt The Bashful's Fort]].

Ball 'N' Chains, known as '''Spinning Spikes''',<ref>Black, Fletcher. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Page 132. They typically appear in levels such as [[Castle of the Big Burt Bros.]], but they can also be found in cave areas.
{{br}}
====''Yoshi's New Island''====
[[File:YNI Spinner.png|thumb|left|A Ball 'n' Chain in ''Yoshi's New Island'']]
[[File:Ball 'n' Chain Larger.png|thumb|200px|Purple Yoshi near a Ball 'n' Chain in ''Yoshi's New Island'']]
Ball 'n' Chains appear again in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. In this game, they are now slightly larger than previous ''Yoshi's Island'' games. They are seen in Forts and Caves and just as before, are indestructible and spinning as they are controlled by the [[Boo Guy]]s. [[Mace Guy]]s once again use the Ball 'n' Chain as their primary weapon.
{{br}}
===''Yoshi'' platforming series===
====''Yoshi's Story''====
[[File:Ball 'n' Chain Up.png|thumb|200px|[[Cyan Yoshi]] near a Ball 'N' Chain in ''Yoshi's Story'']]
Ball 'N' Chains appear in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'', where they swing in a manner similar to pendulums for the first time. Unlike ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', their chains are harmless. They are found alongside [[ball and chain (windup)|windup]] iron balls, which share the same graphics. Both appear in the stages [[The Tall Tower]] and [[Magma Castle]].

====''Yoshi's Woolly World'' / ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''====
[[File:Ball 'n' Chain Gray.png|thumb|left|200px|Two Ball 'n' Chains near Yoshi in ''Yoshi's Woolly World'']]
Ball 'n' Chains also appear in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. This time, they look closer to their ''New Super Mario Bros. U'' redesign, but retain their fully-rotating behavior from the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. Ball 'n' Chains are commonly found in forts and castles and are indestructible.
