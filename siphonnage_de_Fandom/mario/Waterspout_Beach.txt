'''Waterspout Beach''', or '''Sparkling Waters-1''', is the first level of [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Morton's Compactor Castle]], and its own completion unlocks [[Tropical Refresher]]. Waterspout Beach is based off Course 3 of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Mii]]''.  
==Layout==
The level begins near a pair of palm trees, with a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] located above the taller tree. A [[Waterspout]] is found, along with a [[Huckit Crab]]. Another Huckit Crab follows, and is located on top of a Waterspouts. A section of the level above a pool of water follows, with Waterspouts and [[Warp Pipe]]s acting as platforms. A [[? Block]] containing a power-up is found above a pair of Waterspouts. A Huckit Crab is found on a Warp Pipe, and is followed by Waterspouts with [[Piranha Plant]] in between them. A section of ground with a Huckit Crab and a ? Block above palm trees follows. Bridge platforms and Waterspouts are used to cross the water, and a [[Red Ring]] is located above one of the Waterspouts. A row of Waterspouts with Piranha Plants is followed by the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. More Waterspouts and Huckit Crabs are located past the Checkpoint Flag. A Warp Pipe leads to an area containing a [[Clampy]] with a 1-Up Mushroom in its mouth. More Waterspouts and Piranha Plants are found in the section leading to the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In plain sight, players should jump off a Waterspouts to reach the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Shortly after the first Star Coin, players should freeze a Huckit Crab with the [[Ice Flower]] and toss it through the Waterspouts to reach the second Star Coin. Otherwise, players can freeze a nearby [[Cheep Cheep]] and use it as a platform to reach it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Players should use the [[POW Block]] when the Waterspouts rise to safely collect the third Star Coin.
