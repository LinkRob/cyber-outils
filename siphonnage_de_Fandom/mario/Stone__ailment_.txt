==History==
When under the effects of Stone, [[Mario]] is completely immune to all damage for a number of turns. However, he will be unable to move only allowing his partners to attack. Wearing a [[Stone Cap]] causes the Stone ailment. The Stone ailment can be very useful when dealing with tough opponents having only Mario's partners to do damage as they aren't targeted by most bosses.
