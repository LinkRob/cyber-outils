The '''Gossips''' are characters appearing in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They appear on [[Shipshape Island]], where they give hints as to side quests.

==Description==
The Gossips are rotund women, and their getups consist of sleeveless dresses. One gossip has pale blue skin and blue hair and a necklace, the second gossip has pink skin and red hair, and the third gossip has yellow skin and hair and an orange dress.

==Role in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''==
The role of the Gossips is to provide hints to the player on side quests. The pink Gossip's husband is the central character for the side quest An Invitation to the Spa; after [[Concordia]] [[Zokket|split apart]], he could not go to his job on [[Lottacoins Island]], so the pink Gossip decided to help him find a new job on [[Raynforst Island]].

All three get captured by [[Reclusa]]'s flowers when it [[Reclusa World|takes over Concordia]] shortly after hatching from its egg in [[Fortress Zokket]] when [[Zokket]] meets his demise.
