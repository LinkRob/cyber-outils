'''Pokey Mummies''' are a poisonous variant of [[Pokey]] featured as an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', where they are encountered in [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]]. Pokey Mummies would later be replaced in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' with a different poisonous variant, aptly named [[Poison Pokey]]s.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Pokey Mummies have mostly the same appearance as regular Pokeys except their body is a blueish-green color. 

===Behavior===
Behavior-wise, Pokey Mummies are virtually the same, just with the ability to occasionally [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] Mario upon landing a successful attack.
