'''''Greenhouse''''', alternatively spelled '''''Green House''''', is a dual-screen [[Game & Watch]] game released on December 6, 1982. While it is not a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game, ''Greenhouse'' is the first appearance of [[Stanley the Bugman]], the game's main protagonist, who would shortly after become a ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' character with the release of ''[[Donkey Kong 3]]''.

''Greenhouse'' was one of several Game & Watch games to be [[reissue]]d as part of the [[Game & Watch Gallery (series)|''Game & Watch Gallery'' series]]. It was first [[reissue|ported]] to ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 2]]'' as an unlockable Museum title, then as one of the Game & Watch games in ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 3]]'', which has a "Classic" version aiming to be faithful to the original release and a "Modern" remake with ''Mario'' characters.
