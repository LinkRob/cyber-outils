'''Mona's Monkey''' is a character who first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. He is a part of the [[Yum Yums]], being a pet of [[Mona]]'s.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mona's Monkey is a monkey with light brown fur and light skin. He wears a dark blue shirt with a red "3" on it.

===Behavior===
Mona's Monkey generally assists Mona when she gets in roadside conflict. Mona's Monkey attacks by throwing a [[Banana Peel]] onto the road, causing the vehicle trailing Mona to tumble over.
