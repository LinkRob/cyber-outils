The '''Super Blooper''' is a giant [[Blooper]] who resides in [[Toad Town Tunnels]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
===''Paper Mario''===
Super Blooper is one of the many [[Blooper]]s whom [[Mario]] comes across in ''Paper Mario'', specifically after Chapter 5. [[Sushie]]'s ability is needed to reach the Blooper. It is the strongest and largest of the three variations of Bloopers in Toad Town Tunnels; the other two Bloopers that are found in Toad Town Tunnels are the [[Electro Blooper]] and the regular Blooper, both encountered before Super Blooper.

During battle, the Super Blooper will occasionally glow red and [[POW-Up|raise its attack power]]. Unlike the Electro Blooper, when the Super Blooper is charged, the effect remains even when it is damaged. Also during battle, the Super Blooper will spawn two [[Blooper Baby|Blooper Babies]] that attack Mario similarly to [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] or [[Swoopula]]s; they latch onto him and drain his health, and he must repeatedly tap the {{button|n64|A}} Button to remove them. When Mario wears a [[Zap Tap]] badge, Blooper Babies cannot damage him.

===''Super Mario-kun''===
[[File:SMKun 25 Super Blooper.png|thumb|The Super Blooper in ''Super Mario-kun'']]
The Super Blooper also appears in volume 25 of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' during the ''Paper Mario'' arc. In it, it is portrayed as a mother to a [[Baby Blooper]] and the wife to [[Electro Blooper]]. After the Super Blooper sees a wanted poster for Mario, she decides to attack him, [[Goombario]], and [[Kooper]], but the family ultimately loses. Like in the game, the Super Blooper easily gets mad.
