|games = ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''}}'''K. Lumsy's Prison''' is a location that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==History==
K. Lumsy's Island is a rather small prison located right beside [[Crocodile Isle (Donkey Kong 64)|Crocodile Isle]]. This is where [[King K. Rool]] kept [[K. Lumsy]] locked up after the latter refused to destroy [[DK Isles]]. There is a Bananaport 1 pad located here where the Kongs can warp right from DK Isles to here and vice versa. The Kongs can also come here whenever they receive a [[Boss Key]] and K. Lumsy will unlock the way to the other areas. There are also two [[Golden Banana]]s that can be earned here. Both as [[Lanky Kong]] as one is located outside of the prison that requires his [[Grape Shooter]] to get into and the other is inside that requires his [[OrangStand Sprint]].

Once after the Kongs get all of the keys required to free K. Lumsy, the excited Kremling is finally able to go outside and helps in chasing down K. Rool before he can escape. K. Lumsy's Prison is also seen for the final time after [[King Krusha K. Rool]] is defeated and K. Lumsy sends him flying over and across the prison and Crocodile Isle into the distance.
