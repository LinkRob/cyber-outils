'''Cannonball!''', titled '''Wind Me Up!''' in PAL releases, is a General [[minigame]] from ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Opening==
The camera zooms into a cliff, where the characters are. Each of the characters perform an animation to show they a ready, then they turn to look at their respective [[cannon]].

==Gameplay==
The object is for the characters to be launched from their cannon farther than their opponents. At the start, the player must crank their cannon handle as fast as they can for four seconds. After that, each character jumps into their cannon, which fires them out a far distance, depending on how fast they cranked their cannon at the start. Whoever traveled the farthest distance is the winner.

==Controls==
*Rotate {{button|3ds|Stick}}: Crank the handle

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''Wind your cannon with {{button|3ds|Stick}} as fast as you can. Whoever flies the farthest wins.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name refers to a jumping technique where one leaps into a pool of water while hugging their knees.
