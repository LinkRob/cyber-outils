'''Not-Bottomless Holes''' are obstacles and collectibles in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. If [[Mario]] falls into Not-Bottomless Holes, he losses 7 health points. Mario can repair Not-Bottomless Holes with confetti from his confetti bag. Each area in The Origami King has a set amount of Not-Bottomless Holes and a progress percent that tells how the percent of how many Not-Bottomless Holes have been filed.
