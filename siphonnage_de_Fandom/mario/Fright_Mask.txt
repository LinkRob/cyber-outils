The '''Fright Mask''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is an uncommon item that can bought in certain shops or found in certain areas. The Fright Mask itself resembles [[Bowser]]'s face. The Fright Mask is the successor to the [[Fright Jar]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]'', with which it functions identically to.

==Effects==
Using the Fright Mask may possibly scare off all enemies from the battle. If it works, the enemies that fled yield only one [[Star Point]], although it may not work on others. Fright Masks are ineffective against bosses.
