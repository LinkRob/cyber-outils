'''The Sweet Smell of Soda''' is the second level of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 10.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 4 [[Buzzy Beetle]]s and 4 [[Boo]]s. The enemy captain is a Buzzy Beetle.

The final battle contains 5 Buzzy Beetles and 3 Boos. The enemy captain is a Boo.

After the final battle, the player unlocks a Boo. 

The level can be replayed to unlock up to one Boo and or Buzzy Beetle.
