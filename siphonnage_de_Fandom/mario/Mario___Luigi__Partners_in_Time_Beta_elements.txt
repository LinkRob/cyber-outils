== Unused Enemies ==

=== Scoot Bloop ===
An unused enemy named Scoot Bloop, which turns out to be almost entirely functional compared to the others within the game's files. It's located among the enemies in [[Yoshi's Island]] and [[Yoob's Belly]] and has similar stats to the used enemies there, which likely dictates that it would've been placed in one of those two areas. Some pre-release material shows Scoot Bloops in the Vim Factory, but it's possible that they were at one point supposed to be fought there. Even so, they were then moved around before ultimately being left unfinished and scrapped from the final game. They can also be seen in the E3 Demo of the game.

The Scoot Bloop has two attacks. The first one simply involves them ramming into either their enemies (dealing damage) while other one has it stop indefinitely in front of Mario or Luigi, which then initiates a soft-lock and requiring a game reset. This implies that the battle script had yet to be completed. Both of these attacks allow a jump to counterattack. By changing the battle script of the Scoot Bloop to that of certain enemies (such as the Goomba's), it works without any issues. Even though the enemy goes unused, its name was translated into several different languages in the European version of the game.

|TestMAP Test 1 (Fujioka Value 5)
|Using the debug menu, found in the game's files, setting all the numbers (in '''Fujioka''') to zero will warp the player (with a hammer sound being played afterwards) to a variant of '''Room''' '''0x07''' as adult Mario and Luigi. The player will spawn, standing between two Toad NPCs from ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''.


If the player talks to the Toad at the top, the screen will scroll and an arrow made out of Yoshi Eggs will spawn on the right. The Yoshi egg graphics are used, but the behavior is not as touching them will make a little hop and a hammer sound. The eggs seem to make use of some sort of primitive physics engine as they will bounce endlessly on top of characters' heads and will move ever-so-slightly after being disturbed. The latter will result in the hammer sound looping infinitely.


If the player continues to disturb the eggs, the game will lag and eventually have the sprites on-screen flicker or disappear. The second Toad at the bottom does nothing other than show a talk icon. The 4-bro platform is gone, as is the trampoline. On the top screen, Room '''0x09''' is displayed in the player's view.
