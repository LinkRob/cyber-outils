The '''Green Kiddie Kart''' is a [[kart]] appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Green Kiddie Kart takes off the [[Red Kiddie Kart]]'s design, but the shirt and overalls design are now based of [[Luigi]]'s color scheme. However, it still uses the [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Green Kiddie Kart first debuted in the [[Valentine's Tour]] during 2020. This kart helps to increase the duration of [[Rocket Start]]s and the points earned for landing them.
