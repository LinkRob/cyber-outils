The '''Double Kick''' is a ability in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' that allows [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to kick two times. Double Kicking near enemies will stun or defeat them depending on the enemy. The move is slightly like a [[kick]] or a [[Star Spin]] except that it can also be done on the ground. Mario or Luigi must first sprint an enemy and [[Star Spin|spin]] when he nigh to the enemy, and then he be required to perform a [[Long Jump]] by cower fast and [[jump]]. Double Kicking can also break certain objects like [[Item Crystal|Item Crystals]], rocks, and activate [[Chance Cube]]s, [[Lever Switch]]es, [[Arrow Switch]]es. However, it cannot break some [[Crate]]s. Mario does his [[Long Jump]] but he has a couple sparkles throughout him and he is prepared to hurt the most enemies that they are in his plan. This offensive does harm only when he is rising, when he is in the falling down energy, the double kick transform a regular Long Jump and it doesn't have a damage effect. However, he can trip when spin when  protecting himself from getting injured.
