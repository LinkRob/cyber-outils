The '''Standard''' ('''Normal''' in European/Australian languages) wheels are the default wheels for a [[kart]] in the games ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== Description ==
'''Standard''' wheels are originally combined with the Standard Kart, though they can be combined with any kart body part. As the name applies, these wheels are standard, and all stats are set to zero.
