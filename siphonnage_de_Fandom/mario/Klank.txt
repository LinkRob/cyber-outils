'''Klank''' is an enemy appearing in the two games, ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''. They appear in the two [[Krazy Kremland]] levels, [[Rickety Race]] and [[Target Terror]]. They are the equivalent to [[Krash]]es, a similar enemy riding in a Mine Cart, while they appear to be [[Kutlass]]es riding in [[Skull Cart]]s.

In the two levels, they do different effects. In Target Terror, ten different colored Krashes must be jumped on so [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] can obtain a [[DK Coin]]. While in Rickety Race, they throw barrels to the Kongs as an attack.
