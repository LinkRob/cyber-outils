'''Goomba's Worth''' is the eighth level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 32.

==Layout==

Prior to the start of the level, [[Ludwig von Koopa|Ludwig]] has [[Captain Goomba (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Goomba]] summon the other [[Koopalings]]. As a test to prove his loyalty to the minions and [[Bowser]], Ludwig makes him and his squad fight all seven Koopalings at once.

The 1st battle contains 6 [[Mechakoopa]]s, 1 [[Lakitu]], and 1 [[Green Magikoopa|Magikoopa (Green)]]. The enemy captain is the Magikoopa, and he knows [[Chance]].

The 2nd battle contains 5 Mechakoopa, 2 [[Fly Guy]]s, and 1 [[Red Magikoopa|Magikoopa (Red)]]. The enemy captain is the Magikoopa, and he knows [[Rally]].

The 3rd battle contains 6 Mechakoopas, 1 Lakitu, and 1 [[White Magikoopa|Magikoopa (White)]]. The enemy captain is the Magikoopa, and he knows [[Call for Backup]] and [[Deny]]. Call for Backup calls for a [[Para-Beetle]], or a [[Koopa Paratroopa]].

The 4th Battle contains 6 Mechakoopas, 1 Fly Guy, and 1 [[Blue Magikoopa|Magikoopa (Blue)]]. The enemy captain is the Magikoopa, and he knows Rally, [[Parry]], and Deny.

The final battle contains [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Morton]], [[Roy Koopa|Roy]], [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]], [[Lemmy Koopa|Lemmy]], [[Wendy O. Koopa|Wendy]], [[Larry Koopa|Larry]], and Ludwig. The enemy captain is Ludwig, and he knows Rally, [[Thwomp Thump]], and Call for Backup. Call for Backup calls for a Magikoopa (Blue).

After the battle, Ludwig believes the test was a success, so the Koopalings rejoin the Goomba Sqaud to defeat [[Fawful]] and his army.
