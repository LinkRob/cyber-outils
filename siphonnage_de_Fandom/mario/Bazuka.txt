'''Bazuka''' is an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]''. It is a red [[Kremling]] that wields a bazooka, and it attacks by shooting barrels in a direction.
