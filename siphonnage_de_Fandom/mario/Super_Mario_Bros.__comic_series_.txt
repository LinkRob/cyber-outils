'''''Super Mario Bros.''''' is a line of comics part of the ''[[Nintendo Comics System]]'' from [[Valiant Comics]], and published between April [[1990]] and June [[1991]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://valiantarchive.com/valiant/nintendo/nintendo.html|title=Nintendo|publisher=Valiant Firsts|language=English|accessdate=22 June 2024}}</ref> Its content is based on the three ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' titles for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]]: ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.
