'''Mini Ztar Spaces''' are a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Description==
Mini Ztar Spaces act as the opposite of [[Mini Star Space]]s. Upon landing on a Mini Ztar Space, the Captain receives the same number of [[Mini Ztar]]s as the number imprinted on the space, therefore losing the same number of [[Mini Star]]s as well. Landing on a Mini Ztar Space causes the word "Unlucky!" to display as if the Captain had landed on an [[Unlucky Space]].

These spaces (along with [[Mini Star Space]]s) do not appear on [[DK's Jungle Ruins]]. That is because [[Mini Star]]s and [[Mini Ztar]]s are instead replaced with [[Banana]]s and [[Z-Banana]]s. However, they have the same function as Mini Stars and Mini Ztars.
