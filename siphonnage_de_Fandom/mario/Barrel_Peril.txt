'''Barrel Peril''' is a single-player [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player in the [[barrel]] must reach the goal line while being bumped by [[Chain Chomp]]s as few times as possible. The player's character can avoid being bumped by one of the Chain Chomps by hiding in the barrel. Alternatively, if the player's character is far enough from the Chain Chomp, it cannot reach them. If the player's character does not hide in the barrel when the Chain Chomp charges at them, they are knocked backwards and momentarily stunned, being unable to move for three seconds.

The player has 60 seconds to clear the minigame in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] and [[Mini-Game Attack]]. The course is largely different between the aforementioned two modes, with the Mini-Game Attack version containing fewer Chain Chomps than the Shroom City version. In [[Play Land]], the course is longer, and the player must instead make it to the goal as quickly as possible.
