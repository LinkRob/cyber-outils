The '''Hanabihei''' is an enemy from ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They are a variation of the [[Bob-omb]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Hanabiheis (alternatively with the plurals "Hanabiheitachi," "Hanabiheira," or merely "Hanabihei") have a red body with grey shoes. They are covered in a yellow cap with a blue cap on top of it, with the blue cap having a flatter grey cap on top with a hump in the middle.
{{TBA}}

=== Behavior ===
Hanabiheis patrol back and forth, turning when an obstacle gets in their way. Stomping on them causes them to release firecrackers from their cap. Up to three firecrackers can be released; for every firework that releases, the Hanabiheis lose one of their caps. Once they lose every cap, Hanabiheis stop launching fireworks.

== In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' ==
Hanabiheis appear in the Fluff-Puff Peaks Flying Battleship level and Magma Flare-Up.

== Behind the scenes ==
Hanabiheis are based on firecrackers, with their name coming from Japanese ''hanabi'' ("firework"; written 花火). The final "hei" syllable comes from ''hei'' (written 兵), Japanese for "soldier." This means that their name is the Japanese translation of "firework soldier."

== Names in other languages ==
Due to "Hanabihei" being ripped from Japanese. Hanabiheis have the same name in that language. Their Chinese and Korean names are translations of the Japanese name, with the former language calling them "''Yānhuǒ Bīnɡ''" (written 煙火兵 with ''yānhuǒ'' meaning "firework" and ''bīng'' meaning "soldier") and the latter language calling them "''Pokjuk Byeong''" (written 폭죽병 with ''Pokjuk'' meaning "firework" and ''Byeong'' meaning "soldier").
