Badge''' is a badge that [[Luigi]] obtains after both brothers achieve Level 40 in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. This badge allows excellent hits to boost the meter. But if the player does not receive an Excellent hit, then the built up power in the meter will break.
