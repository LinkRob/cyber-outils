The '''Flaming Tender''' is an item which first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in both ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
The Flaming Tender is a spicy red pepper with an angry expression. In ''Mario Kart Arcade GP'' and ''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2'', it is one of [[Bowser]]'s [[Special Item (Mario Kart series)|special items]], and upon using the item, he starts to [[Fire Breath|breathe fire]] that sweeps back and forth. Anyone close to Bowser gets hit by the flame and is brought to a stop. In ''Mario Kart Arcade GP DX'', the Flaming Tender can be used by anyone, and doing so temporarily sets the user's kart ablaze, causing them to damage any rival karts they come into contact with.
