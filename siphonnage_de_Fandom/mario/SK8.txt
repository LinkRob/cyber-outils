'''SK8''' is a recurring object in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Description==
The SK8 is a skateboard invented by [[Dr. Crygor]] and used by [[9-Volt]] to travel between areas. The SK8 has a rocket booster, allowing 9-Volt to also ride it in midair like a hoverboard. The SK8 is usually depicted as having a light blue board, but in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'', the SK8 is depicted with a yellow board.
