'''Urban Stingbies''' are mosquito-like enemies found in the [[Metro Kingdom]] of ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. Despite being named after [[Stingby|Stingbies]], Urban Stingbies bear few similarities to them. Urban Stingbies appear across [[New Donk City]], both in fly and larva forms.

== Description ==
They are found in [[New Donk City]] alleyways. While in their larva form, if they spot Mario they will evolve into their adult form, and then charge forward. Urban Stingbys will be destroyed if they charge into a wall. Urban Stingbies are also among the few enemies that cannot be captured.
