'''Carver''', also named '''Hassan''', is a character from the ''[[Dragon Quest]]'' franchise. He made his first overall appearance in ''[[w:c:dragonquest:Dragon Quest VI: Realms of Revelation|Dragon Quest VI: Realms of Revelation]]'', and appears alongside ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters in ''[[Itadaki Street DS]]'' and ''[[Fortune Street]]'', both in which he is a playable character.
