'''Jungle Japes''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', then later reappeared in the next installment, ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. It then reappeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It is from the ''Donkey Kong'' universe.
