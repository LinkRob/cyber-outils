The '''Awesome Snack''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing a [[Fresh Veggie]] and [[Cake Mix]]. A favorite of the town [[Flopside]], the Awesome Snack restores 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
