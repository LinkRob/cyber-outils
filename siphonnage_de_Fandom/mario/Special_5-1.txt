'''Special 5-1''' is the first level of [[Special 5]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It is based off [[World 2-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-4]]. 

== Layout ==
The player starts out on a set of platforms that lead to a set of [[Flip-Swap Panel]]s with several [[Boo]]s on them, at the end of the set of Flip-Swap Panels the player must drop down and go onto the next set of Flip-Swap Panels that leads to the [[Warp Box]].

In the next area is the [[Checkpoint Flag]] and another set of platforms that lead to more Flip-Swap Panels which have [[Spiked Ball]]s launched onto them. After this is another set of Flip-Swap Panels that lead to some elongated Flip-Swap Panels. After the elongated platforms are several platforms, that have Spiked Balls being launched onto them, that lead to the [[Goal Pole]].

== [[Star Medal]]s ==
* '''Star Medal 1:''' Floating above the [[Flip-Swap Panel]]s next to the second set of [[Brick Block]]s.
* '''Star Medal 2:''' On the platform next to two [[Spiked Ball]] generators.
* '''Star Medal 3:''' At the end of the elongated Flip-Swap Panels.
