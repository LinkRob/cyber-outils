The '''Party Cube''' is a giant white box in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. In the game, [[Toad]], [[Shy Guy (Mario Party 4)|Shy Guy]], [[Boo (Mario Party 4)|Boo]], [[Koopa (Mario Party 4)|Koopa]], and [[Goomba (Mario Party 4)|Goomba]] send invitations to [[Mario]] and his friends, allowing them to enter the Party Cube. Inside it are a lot of game [[board (Mario Party series)|board]]s and [[minigame]]s.

The Party Cube is rather mysterious, first appearing inside a floating bag over [[Peach's Castle]]. Bystanders Mario, [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Yoshi]], [[Daisy]], [[Donkey Kong]], [[Wario]], and [[Waluigi]] are initially frightened by the presence of this bag, which comes in an almost intimidating aura including dark clouds, though it eventually bursts to reveal the Party Cube. At this point, the characters are permitted to travel into the Party Cube, and the events of ''Mario Party 4'' begin. 

During ''Mario Party 4''{{'}}s end credits, the story of how the game's [[guide|guide characters]] found the Party Cube is slowly revealed through a nostalgic-looking slideshow. Apparently, they came across it as a big blank [[block]] trapped in a [[Warp Pipe]], later carving pictures of themselves in it with unknown intentions. This led to a series of events that slowly morphed them into their ''Mario Party 4'' forms, often through other characters appearing and rewarding them with their thematic costumes that they wear in the game. A [[Lakitu]] also gave them the invitations seen in the game's opening while they were working on the Party Cube, encouraging them to put it to its use. The Party Cube is last seen escaping into the stars, being chased by the guide characters.
