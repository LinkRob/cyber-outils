'''Goal in One''' is a recurring [[microgame]] of [[Jimmy T]]'s in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player has to score a goal by kicking the soccer ball into the goal. The goalie moves around from left to right. There are three arrows on the ground: one pointing northwest, one pointing north, and one pointing northeast. When an arrow is tapped, the ball flies in that direction. If the ball goes through the goal, the player wins. However, if the goalie blocks the ball or time runs out, the game ends in a loss.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', a rotating arrow instead indicates the direction, which the player must lockdown with {{button|3ds|a}}. Afterward, the arrow turns into a meter, which the player must stop to set the strength of the kick. A regular goalie appears in the first difficulty level, but it is still different than the one in ''WarioWare: D.I.Y.''. In the second difficulty level, the goalie is the old master from [[Self-Defense]], who levitates and teleports himself left and right. In the third difficulty level, the goalie is a clown named [[Clownkeeper]], who stretches his legs and arms outward with every move.
