'''Clone Luigi''' (also called '''NPC Luigi''') is the non-playable version of [[Luigi]] found in the ''Super Luigi Galaxy'' side of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. Clone Luigi is noticeably taller than the playable Luigi and his clothes are a darker green. This is because the playable Luigi is actually a "reskin" of [[Mario]] from the main game. Clone Luigi has the same role as the Luigi in the main game. He is first seen in the [[Ghostly Galaxy]] imprisoned in a cage and after he is saved there, he returns to the [[Comet Observatory]] with Luigi and goes on to find [[Power Star]]s in the [[Good Egg Galaxy]], [[Honeyhive Galaxy]], and [[Battlerock Galaxy]]. After this, he remains on the Comet Observatory for the rest of the game.

==Trivia==
*Everytime Clone Luigi talks to Luigi, he humorously refers to Luigi as "me".
*Upon being brought back to the [[Comet Observatory]], [[Rosalina]] refers to Clone Luigi as Luigi's "twin".
*Possibly the reason for Clone Luigi's appearance is due to the fact that it would be out of character to have Mario in the place of Luigi where he is afraid of [[Flipbug|bugs]] and [[Boo]]s and gets himself trapped quite easily as Mario is usually portrayed as being fearless.
