The '''Predictotron''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is unlocked by completing the quest [[True-Blue Boo]].

==Description==
The purpose of the Gaddget is to predict the most likely outcome of the next move played in a game of {{w|Rock-paper-scissors}} against the Gaddget itself. For the first turn, the list is blank, meaning that there is no guess to the opponent's move. After a turn, what the opponent chose in the preceding round is recorded by the Gaddget, which then calculates the probability of each outcome and tells the player which of them is most likely to occur.
