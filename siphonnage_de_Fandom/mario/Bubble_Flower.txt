A '''Bubble Flower''' is a power-up item which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Description==
When a character picks up a Bubble Flower, they turn into their [[Bubble Mario|Bubble]] form, giving them the ability to shoot bubbles, like with [[Baby Yoshi]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. A Bubble Flower is cyan in color, while the head is white, violet, and pink with the typical black eyes.
