'''Brinstar Depths''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It is from the ''Metroid'' series.

==Origin==
Brinstar Depths is based on the level of the same name from ''Metroid'' and ''Super Metroid''. Brinstar Depths is a fairly large area that is located below [[Brinstar]]. The boss of the area is Kraid.
