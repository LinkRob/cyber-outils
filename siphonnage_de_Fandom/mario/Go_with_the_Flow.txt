'''Go with the Flow''' is a [[Co-op Minigame|Co-op]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. It originates from "go with the flow."

== Opening ==
The camera pans from the inflatable cloud with a inflatable rainbow to the Toad's air balloon.

== Gameplay ==
In the gameplay, the player must hold the Joy-Con horizontally. The player must move the Joy-Con in a circular motion. the faster the player moves it, the stronger the fan blows and the faster the hot air balloon will be pushed in the fans direction. The players must work together for the Toad to claim as many balloons as possible. Some balloons are worth one point and grouped balloons are worth three points. The three Toads goes on a hot air balloon for each round until they reach the top.



== Ending ==
Depending on the ranking, the players will do their victory or losing animations.
