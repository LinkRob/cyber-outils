A '''Mini Hammer''' is an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mini Hammers are a type of [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] that is smaller variant of a [[Hammer (Luigi's Mansion 3)|Hammer]], similarly as [[Mini Goob]]s are to [[Goob]]s. Aside from being a smaller size, Mini Hammers are no different in appearance from one another.

===Behavior===
Mini Hammers, like Mini Goobs, are significantly weaker than their regular counterpart, and they often appear in groups. A Mini Hammer attacks by swinging their arms while charging at Luigi. However, a Mini Hammer can be easily defeated from being stunned by [[Luigi]]'s [[Strobulb]] and then vacuumed into his [[Poltergust G-00]], as Mini Hammers have no [[Heart Point|HP]].
