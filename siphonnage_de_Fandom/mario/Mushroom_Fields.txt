'''Mushroom Fields''' is a track in ''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit]]''. It is the first track of the [[Banana Cup]].

==Description==
Mushroom Fields takes place in a baseball stadium, and it is named for how there are several [[Dash Mushroom]]s that provide the driver bursts of speed, often trailing sets of [[Coin]]s. A dimmed, raining effect is present throughout the track. Mushroom Fields uses the same musical theme as ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''{{'}}s [[Wuhu Town]].

The course's icon shows a rear view of [[Mario]] wearing his [[Baseball Uniform]] and [[Batting Helmet]] from ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' while driving in the [[Fastball]], and a Dash Mushroom placed on the grass on the left side.
