'''Speed Hockey''' is a [[2 vs. 2 Mini-game]] that appears in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. It is just like ''[[wikipedia:Pong|Pong]]'', the classic arcade game.

==Gameplay==
In this game, both teams simply move up and down to hit the [[Koopa Shell]]. Like real-life hockey, the players must score by hitting the shell into the other team's goal. As the game goes on, the shell gets faster.

The first team to get three points wins.
