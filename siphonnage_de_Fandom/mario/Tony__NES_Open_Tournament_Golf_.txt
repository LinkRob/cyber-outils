'''Tony''' is a character in ''[[NES Open Tournament Golf]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Tony wears an orange striped shirt, orange pants, and black shoes. Tony also has orange hair.

For his artwork, Tony wears orange, collared shirt and matching pants, and on top of his shirt is a red and white striped sweater vest. Tony's hair is also given a more orange shade than how it appears in-game. He is also shown to have blue eyes.

==In ''NES Open Tournament Golf''==
[[File:Tony NESOTG screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|A screenshot of Tony playing in ''NES Open Tournament Golf'']]
Tony is a non-playable golfer who can be played against in match play. Tony is ranked as semi-professional, and he is the second hardest character to beat after [[Billy]].
