'''Adult Shine Blocks''' are a type of [[block]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' on a few occasions.

==Description==
[[File:Adult Shine Block green MLPIT sprite.png|frame|left]]
They are a somewhat wide [[block]] with a [[Shine Sprite]] symbol on it, along with either an M or an L centered within the symbol. The color of an Adult Shine Block depends on which letter is shown, and Adult Shine Blocks with an M are colored red whereas those with an L are green.

[[Mario]] is the only character who can hit red Adult Shine Blocks, whereas only [[Luigi]] can hit green Adult Shine Blocks. Neither [[Baby Mario]] nor [[Baby Luigi]] can use an Adult Shine Block, as its title would suggest, but if either Mario or Luigi hits his corresponding Adult Shine Block, a [[small Shine Block]] appears and goes over to the babies, who can then hit it to illuminate the room temporarily. When the room goes dark again, an Adult Shine Block has to be activated again to illuminate the room again.

If Mario jumps under a green Adult Shine Block or Luigi under a red one, a [[fireball]] comes out, and there is a possibility of it landing on either of the babies. If this happens, the group is forced to restart the room.
