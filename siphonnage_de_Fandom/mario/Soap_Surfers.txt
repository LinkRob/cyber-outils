{{Nihongo|'''Soap Surfers'''|せっけんアタック|Sekken Atakku|Soap Attack}} is a 4-Player and Duel minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera shows the number of players on the surface of a bathroom sink, standing on their respective colored soap bars (blue, red, green, or yellow).

== Gameplay ==
The aim of the game is to survive 30 seconds on the bathroom sink and push other players off by surfing on their soap bars, using the {{button|ds|Pad}} to move in all directions. Players are eliminated from the game if they are knocked off the sink either by themselves or by other players.

== Ending ==
The players stand on the blue glass next to the next. The winning players stand up in front and do their victory dance, while the eliminated players stand back and do their losing poses. If the game ends in a tie, the players remaining in the sink will simply stay stationary.

== Description ==
*'''Rules''': ''"Knock all rivals out of the sink as you surf on a soap bar. Avoid getting knocked out before the time runs out, or be the last one standing."''
*'''Tips''': ''"Stay on the move and change direction often as you try to stay in the middle area."''

== Trivia ==
* This minigame is not included in the Simple set.
