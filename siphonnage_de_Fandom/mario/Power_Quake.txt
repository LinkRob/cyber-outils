'''Power Quake''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Power Quake is a useful badge as it is not only easy to find but it is also a more powerful version of the [[Quake Hammer]] Badge. This badge is used to attack all enemies on the ceiling and ground, and both it and the Quake Hammer are one of the few ways to attack enemies on the ceiling. Wearing the Power Quake costs two [[Badge Point|BP]]. Its strength is based on the [[hammer]] used by [[Mario]] during the battle, but using Power Quake requires four [[Flower Point|FP]] per use.

Power Quake has a more powerful variation named [[Mega Quake]].

==In ''Paper Mario''==
This badge is found in the [[Jade Jungle]]. By using [[Sushie]], Mario can access a small island, where a lone [[Spear Guy]] battles [[Mario]] if he gets the chance. The Power Quake can be found inside of the red [[Question Block]].
