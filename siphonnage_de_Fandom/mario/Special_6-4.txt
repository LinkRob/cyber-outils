'''Special 6-4''' is the fourth level of [[Special 6]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It is based off of [[World 4-3 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 4-3]], but this time a [[Cosmic Clone]] chases the player. 
==Layout==
The player starts off on a small grassy platform in front of some rotating blocks that lead to a small platform, with some [[? Block]]s that contain [[coin]]s on it, after this are more rotating blocks that lead to a platform with a [[Mushroom Trampoline]] on it. After this is another rotating block segment that leads to the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Medal]]s==
* '''Star Medal 1:''' On the first grassy platform after the rotating blocks.
*'''Star Medal 2:''' Near the bottom of a red rotating block, before a grassy platform with a Mushroom Trampoline.
*'''Star Medal 3:''' On the blue rotating platform at the beginning of the large rotating block segment.
