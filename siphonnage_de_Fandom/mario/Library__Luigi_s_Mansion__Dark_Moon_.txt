The '''Library''' is a room in the [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. Along with the [[Foyer]], the Library is one of the biggest rooms in Gloomy Manor. It's also the room where Luigi has his first encounter with [[Poltergeist]]s. The Library is also one of the most visited rooms in Gloomy Manor as well.

== History ==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-3: Quiet Please!|A-3: Quiet Please!]], Luigi was sent by E. Gadd to investigate the piano signals heard from the Library. Luigi fights his way to the Library where he encounters the big-headed Poltergeists for the first time. Luigi fights all three of the Poltergeists and captures them all into the [[Poltergust 5000]]. E. Gadd then [[Pixelator|pixelates]] Luigi from the mansion.

Luigi can head back to the Library once he obtains the [[Dark-Light Device]] during [[Gloomy Manor#A-4: Visual Tracks|A-4: Visual Tricks]] to collect a [[Gem]].

Luigi can visit the Library for the final time during [[Gloomy Manor#A-5: Sticky Situation|A-5: Sticky Situation]] to clean up spider webs and capture [[Boos in Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon|Ooga Booga]] the [[Boo]].
