A '''Star Cruster''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Star Crusters are large, crab-like enemies that can be found in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]]. Humorously, even though Star Crusters resemble crabs, they deny being crabs, and even appear to be offended whenever referred to as such.

==Battle==
In battle, Star Crusters are known for their very high attack and defense. They can attack physically with their claws as their main attack. However, whenever a Star Cruster is attacked directly, they will counterattack with a small ball of energy that can put someone to sleep.

===Strategy===
Star Crusters are weak to ice moves meaning [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] or [[Ice Bomb]]s are effective against them.
