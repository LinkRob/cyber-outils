'''Go, Goomba, Go!''' is the first level of [[Hoohoo Mountain]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 1.

Before the battle, [[Captain Goomba (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Goomba]] angers a Beanie. This causes the sub sequential fight.

After the battle, Captain Goomba finds a couple of [[Goomba]]s, who convince him to be their captain due to his Minion spirit.  

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains 1 [[Beanie]]. The enemy captain is the Beanie.

The final battle contains 1 Beanie. The enemy captain is the Beanie.

After the final battle, some Goombas are recruited. They are only recruited for the first time the level is played through.
