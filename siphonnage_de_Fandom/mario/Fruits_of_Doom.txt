'''Fruits of Doom''' is a Bowser [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.  It involves [[Bowser]] commanding the four players to bring him [[fruit]].

==Gameplay==
At the beginning of the minigame, Bowser says a list of ten or eleven fruits he wants. The list of fruits speeds up to the point where the final fruits on Bowser's list are visible for only about four frames, making the end of the list barely understandable. After Bowser finishes his list, the players take turns bringing him a fruit. If a player brings a fruit that is on the list, the participants keep playing.

The eight fruit choices shown in the minigame are in order from left to right as follows: a [[strawberry]], a [[melon]], an [[apple]], an [[orange]], a bunch of [[grape]]s, a pair of [[Cherry|cherries]], a bunch of [[banana]]s, and a [[pineapple]]. Despite their appearances, the fruits do not have to be brought in any specific order; rather, each successful offering removes a corresponding fruit from the list Bowser gave at the beginning. As the minigame progresses, Bowser may occasionally show second thoughts before eating the fruit that he is given regardless of whether he is given a correct fruit or not, but it is simply intended as a bluff; if Bowser consumes the fruit, it is correct. He may sometimes bluff on a fruit twice. The minigame continues until someone brings a fruit that is not on Bowser's list or that has been eliminated from his list, which causes that player to get burned by Bowser and lose the minigame.
