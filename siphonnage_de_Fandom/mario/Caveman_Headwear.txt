The '''Caveman Headwear''' appeares in Super Mario Odyssey for the Nintendo Switch. The Caveman Headwear can be purchased for 5 purple coins in the Cascade Kingdom. This comes with the Caveman Outfit at a combined price of 15 purple coins.

It is based off of the head of a [[Dry Bones]] enemy.
