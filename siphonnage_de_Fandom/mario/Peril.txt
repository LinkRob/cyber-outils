==History==
First appearing in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Peril is a condition where [[Mario]] has only 1 [[Heart Point|HP]] left. This can also occur for Mario's partners in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Peril is a step down from [[Danger]] and is recommended for Mario to use a recovery item to stay alive. In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', the game will prompt the player to heal when they are about to lose all HP. Some [[Badge|badges]] can only be used when in Peril

== Strategy ==
In [[Paper Mario]] and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', the badge "[[Mega Rush]]" the badge will make Mario do 4 more damage in Paper Mario, and 5 more damage in The Thousand-Year Door. A common strategy is to get Mario to 1 HP and use [[Power Bounce]] to do massive damage.{{PMNav}}{{PMTTYDNav}}{{SPMNav}}
