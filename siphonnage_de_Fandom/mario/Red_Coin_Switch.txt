'''Red Coin Switches''', also titled '''Red Switch Plates''', are objects in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
A Red Coin Switch is resembles the upper half of a red sphere. It can be activated if [[Mario]] [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]]s it, causing eight [[Red Coin]]s to appear and an onscreen timer. If the resulting timer [[Time limit|runs out]], Mario loses a [[1-Up|life]].

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Red Coin Switches are located on the first platform of secret areas.
