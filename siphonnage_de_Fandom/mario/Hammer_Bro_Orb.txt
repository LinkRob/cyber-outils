The '''Hammer Bro Orb''' is an [[orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. It replaces the [[Spiny Orb]] from ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
The Hammer Bro Orb is the successor to the [[Hammer Bro. Capsule]] from ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. It functions almost identically to the Hammer Bro. Capsule: a Hammer Bro arrives and throws two hammers at the target to take away ten coin, except those coins are now given to the character who placed the Orb. The mechanics of the Hammer Bro Orb are identical to the Spiny Orb from ''Mario Party 6'', so when the Spiny Orb returned in ''Mario Party 7'', it received a different function.

If an opponent lands on a [[space]] with the Hammer Bro Orb, a Hammer Bro arrives to throw two [[hammer]]s at them, costing the target ten [[coin]]s, which are then given to the player whoever placed the Orb.

Another difference from the Hammer Bro. Capsule is that the Hammer Bro Orb cannot be applied to the user. This is because ''Mario Party 7'' follows the mechanic introduced in ''Mario Party 6'', where characters are prevented from using Orbs with negative effects on themselves directly and are not affected by landing on a space where they themselves placed an Orb.
