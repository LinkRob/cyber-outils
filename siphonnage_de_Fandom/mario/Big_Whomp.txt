A '''Big Whomp''' (also called a '''Large Whomp''') is a bigger version of a [[Whomp]] that first appeared in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. They act very similar as the regular Thwomps and can be defeated by using the [[Ground Pound]] on their backs; even they are enemies who will cause damage to the plumbers on contact. 

==History==
===''New Super Mario Bros.''===
[[Image:Whomp Large.png|thumb|left|A Larger Whomp.]]
Big Whomps (under the name '''Whomp (Large)''') first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. In this game, only one is seen in the whole game, it is located in [[World 3-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 3-Castle]] after a rope-swinging section. Unlike the other [[Whomp]]s in the Castle, this one actually moves around and will attempt to flatten [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] on sight, like the regular Whomps however, this one slams it's head down allowing for a ground pound to finish it off, the Big Whomp also drops more [[coin]]s than a regular Thwomp.

In the game's files, there exists an unused variety of the Big Whomp which doesn't move (similar to the smaller [[Whomp]]s (which also have an unused moving variety)). This Whomp would've behaved identically to the smaller Whomps however it would've been larger.

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[Image:Whomp Chaos.png|thumb|right|Mario near a larger Whomp.]]
Big Whomps make a return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. In this game, they are seen in [[World Mushroom-Castle]] alongside several smaller [[Whomp]]s, they retain their behavior from their previous appearance however there are also still varieties which appear. A Big Whomp can be defeated by ground pounding it after it topples or with the effects of a [[Gold Flower]], [[Invincibility Leaf]], or a [[Starman]]. 

==Trivia==
*The [[Whomp King]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' is sometimes mistaken as a Big Whomp due to lacking a crown, this error was fixed in [[Super Mario 64 DS|the remake]] as he is now given a crown.
