'''Stick to It''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is one of the Gaddgets that is available from the start.

==Description==
The Gaddget offers help with decision-making by letting a stick fall randomly into one of 2 to 4 directions, the number of which can be set by the player manually. After the stick has fallen, there is no option to reset the Gaddget, meaning the player instead has to exit and re-enter the Gaddget before they can use it again.
