The '''Factory Chief''' is a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and [[Super Mario RPG|its 2023 Remake on the Nintendo Switch]]. He is a purple [[Ninja (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Ninja]] who is second highest ranked member of the [[Smithy Gang]], falling only behind [[Smithy]] himself.

Alongside the Gunyolk, the Factory Chief serves as the third/penultimate boss of the Factory, and is overall the twenty-fourth/penultimate boss of the game. And it was the last obstacle standing between [[Mario]]'s party and [[Smithy]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
===Battle===
In battle, the Factory Chief operates his invention the [[Gunyolk]] that can use the deadly [[Breaker Beam]] move. The Factory Chief himself can attack with strong physical such as [[Thornet]] and can even turn one of [[Mario]]'s party into a [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]].

====Strategy====
It is very wise to take down the Factory Chief before going after the Gunyolk as his [[Funguspike]] technique can be troublesome. The Factory Chief is weak to [[Ice (element)|Ice]], so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] is effective against him. Mario should attack with his moves with [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] heals.
