'''Cricket Jam''' is an item featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a jar of jam found in [[Kero Sewers]], and the Cricket Jam has to be given to [[Frogfucius]], who gives [[Mario]] ten [[Frog Coin]]s in exchange for it.
