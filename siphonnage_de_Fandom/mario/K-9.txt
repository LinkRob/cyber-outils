'''K-9s''' are dog-like enemies featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. K-9s are found on the field in [[Bandit's Way]], where they move around very slowly. They have stronger variants known as [[Chow]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, K-9s often attack by biting [[Mario]] or another party member. They can also [[Fangs]] for more damage or [[Howl]] that causes the [[Fear]] status halving attack and defense.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
K-9's name is a pun on the word "canine."

==Trivia==
*According to the [[Monster Guide]] in the remake, the K-9's official name is '''"Blueish-Tinged Gray K-9"'''. However, due to character count limitations, it was shortened to just "K-9."
