'''Play Nintendo''' is a website from [[Nintendo|Nintendo of America]] that aims to appeal to younger audiences by promoting their products and provide free online activities involving ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' and other Nintendo franchises alike, sometimes also third-party releases on Nintendo platforms. The website was launched on October 1, 2014.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.nintendo.com/whatsnew/detail/fDFU_xSPg70DC4laXkgruUB9g-rMP93w |title=Find fun and surprises at Play Nintendo |accessdate=2023-05-08 |date=October 1, 2014|publisher=Nintendo of America |language=English |archiveurl=http://web.archive.org/web/20150106065955/http://www.nintendo.com/whatsnew/detail/fDFU_xSPg70DC4laXkgruUB9g-rMP93w |archivedate=2015-01-06}}</ref> The website is divided into five sections: "Friends," "Activities," "Media," "Printables," and "News & Tips." A dedicated YouTube channel of theirs was launched around the same time.

Because [[Mario]] is Nintendo's mascot, the website design features a ''Mario'' theme, such as how clicking the search bar reveals [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], whose tongue elongates, revealing a search bar. Randomly placed [[Question Block]]s can be found while randomly scrolling any of the sections, and clicking it causes a brief random effect to occur, known as "surprises," such as a graphic of a [[Bullet Bill]] traveling across the screen, an upscaled sprite of [[Super Mario (form)|Super Mario]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' walking across the screen while the beginning part of the [[Ground Theme]] plays, or [[Kirby]] launching into the computer screen before slowly sliding his way back down.

In general, several Play Nintendo activities and videos are focused on Nintendo all-stars or major Nintendo releases, so Mario often appeared alongside other Nintendo protagonists such as [[Link]] and [[Samus Aran]].
