'''Wendy's Shifting Castle''', or '''{{world|Frost|castle}}''', is the [[Castle]] of [[Frosted Glacier]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. This level contains swinging girders, [[Dry Bones]], and [[Thwomp]]s. The boss is [[Wendy O. Koopa]]. It can be unlocked by finishing [[Swaying Ghost House]], and competing this level opens the path to [[The Mighty Cannonship]]. 

==Layout==
The level begins with some swinging girders that must be jumped across, with [[Dry Bones]] located on platforms in between them. Some [[Thwomp]]s, a [[Super Thwomp]], and a [[Thwimp]] are found in the next section, which leads to the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. Following the Checkpoint Flag, two girder platforms are found with a Thwomp in the middle. A Thwimp is found with a [[? Block]] containing a power-up, and is followed by more girder platforms and Dry Bones. More Thwimps are found, with a hidden area filled with [[coin]]s in a section of the ground. More girders follow, as well as Thwimps. A section with multiple Super Thwomps is found, with the boss door located at the end of the area.

The boss door leads to a red [[cannon]]. After being launched out of the red cannon into Wendy's [[airship]], the player proceeds to fight [[Wendy O. Koopa]]. She uses her [[Death Ring|Rings]] to knock [[icicle]]s from the ceiling onto the player and, at the same time, skates across the area to avoid the player. After three hits, she is defeated and the path to [[The Mighty Cannonship]] is unlocked.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is behind the last Thwomp in a long line of falling Thwomps immediately before the [[Checkpoint Flag]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': Between two Thwimps is a [[Brick Block]]. Above the Brick Block is a [[Hidden Block]] that can be used to reach a pipe that leads to the second Star Coin. A row of Thwimps can be found in the room with the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In a section with moving girders, the player must make it to a pipe at the bottom of the course that leads to the final Star Coin. The Star Coin is hidden behind a Big Thwomp, with lifts on either side of it.
