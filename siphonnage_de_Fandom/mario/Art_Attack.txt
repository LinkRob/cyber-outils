|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Art Attack''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. [[Mario]] can learn this technique after getting the [[Crystal Stars|Ruby Star]] from Chapter 4 and requires 4 [[Star Power|SP]] to use.

==Effects==
With Art Attack, the player can use the {{button|gcn|stick}} to draw circles around enemies to deal damage. This move is very effective in attacking hordes of enemies especially since it can also pierce through enemy defenses.
