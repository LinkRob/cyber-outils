The '''''Game Boy Advance''''' book series, also known as the '''''Game Boy Color''''' book series, is a series of interactive {{wp|gamebook}}s published by [[Scholastic]] that are based on games for the [[Game Boy Advance]] and [[Game Boy Color]].<ref name="Amazon"/> They also serve as gameplay guides, due to their strong relevance to the games. The book series lasted from 2001 to 2002.<ref name="Amazon"/><ref name="Warioland 4">[https://www.gamebooks.org/Item/1178/Show Item - Warioland 4]</ref> All books in the series were written by Craig Wessel.<ref name="Amazon"/>
