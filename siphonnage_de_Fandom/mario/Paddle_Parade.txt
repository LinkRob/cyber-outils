'''Paddle Parade''' is one of [[Mona]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. It is a remade variation of the [[Penguin Shuffle]] minigame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place on a pond. The ducks have brown-and-white feathers while the duckling controlled by the player has yellow feathers. The fisherman is in the background.

==Gameplay==
Like the baby [[Penguin (real world)|penguin]] from Penguin Shuffle, the player has to move the duckling in unison with the other ducklings. If the duckling bumps into one of the others, the player loses at the microgame.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The ducks take a brief pause before they move left.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' Similar to level 1, they stop and continue moving left.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The ducks take a brief pause before they move left, then pause and move right.
