'''Paper Petey Piranha''' is a boss in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
He looks like the regular counterpart of [[Petey Piranha]] except for his paper-like proportions.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''==
Paper Petey Piranha is the second boss fought in [[Sunbeam Plains]].
