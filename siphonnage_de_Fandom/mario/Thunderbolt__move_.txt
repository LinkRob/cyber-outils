'''Thunderbolt''' is a move used by [[Mallow]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a [[Thunder (element)|Thunder]]-based move that requires only 2 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use. Mallow already knows this move without having to level up.

==Effects==
With Thunderbolt, Mallow can attack every enemy on the screen for electrical damage. This is useful when dealing with a group of enemies (especially those with a weakness to Thunder such as the [[Shyster]]s). However, the move itself is a tad weak. A stronger version of Thunderbolt is called [[Shocker (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Shocker]] but it only strikes a single enemy for high damage.
