'''Amsterdam Drift 2''' is a track in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. As its number implies, it is the second layout of [[Amsterdam Drift]].

== Layout ==
The focus of Amsterdam Drift 2 lies mainly in the canals at the southern edge of the city.

Racers start on the same straightaway that they started the [[Amsterdam Drift 1|first layout on]], although they face the opposite direction (to the west; south according to ''Mario Kart 8'') much like in [[Amsterdam Drift 3]]. After a slight right, they will [[Propeller (Mario Kart 7)|take a dip into the canal]]. They then make a left and make manoeuvres between many [[Warp Pipe]]s with currents coming out of them. The racers then make a serpentine turn before a straightaway. A fork in the road follows, and the racers can choose to go straight for a chance to ride an air current onto a boat before driving back in or take a 90 degree left and then a right. Both routes meet up later, and the racers get out of the water via a ramp. This is followed by one left turn back to the finish line.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''{{'}}s [[Booster Course Pass]] for the Switch version, the racers take this route in reverse, they are locked onto the straight path in the canal section, and the boat is on the opposite side of the current so they can still access it.

== Trivia ==
*This is the only [[Amsterdam Drift]] variant to be driven completely in reverse in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.
