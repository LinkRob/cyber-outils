'''Trappers''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Trappers are orange [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] with two blue tongues.

===Behavior===
Trappers wait in front of certain doors or against the wall in narrow hallways while invisible. If [[Luigi]] or [[Gooigi]] comes near a Trapper, it reveals itself and grabs either of them with one of its tongues.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3''==
Trappers have 0 [[Heart Point|HP]]. It is possible to reveal a Trapper beforehand using the [[Dark-Light Device]]. A Trapper can be defeated from either Luigi or Gooigi stunning it with the Strobulb, then each pulling on a tongue and slamming it simultaneously. If Gooigi bursts next to a Trapper attacking Luigi, it drops Luigi and returns to being invisible.

In the [[Hallway (Master Suite)|Hallway]] of [[Master Suite]], there is a Trapper that, if not vacuumed after being flashed by the Strobulb, will become invisible and move elsewhere on the floor.
