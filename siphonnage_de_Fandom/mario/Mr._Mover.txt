'''Mr. Mover''' is a [[Duel Mode]] board that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Layout==
Mr. Mover has a unique layout to it. First of all, there are two conveyor belts which can change color from red to blue every turn. That means a player must carefully plan out their moves in order to get back to their starting area. Because of this, Mr. Mover has been known for being the most difficult board to move around on. [[Belltop]] is also located in the center of the stage right before the conveyor belts.

===Story Mode===
In [[Story Mode]], the player must duel against [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]].
