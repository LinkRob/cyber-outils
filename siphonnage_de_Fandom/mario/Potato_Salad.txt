'''Potato Salad''' is an item and a [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
The Potato Salad can be made by having [[Tayce T.]] simply cook an [[Iced Potato]] which can only be obtained from sleeping at the [[Inn]] in [[Shiver City]]. The Potato Salad can restore 10 [[Heart Point|HP]].
