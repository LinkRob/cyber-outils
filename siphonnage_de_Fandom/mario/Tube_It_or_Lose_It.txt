'''Tube It or Lose It''' is a recurring 1 vs. 3 mini-game in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' and later returns in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
===Solo===
The single player's goal is to simply try to pop each of the other players' tubes within the time limit. The single player may move slower than the three players but they have an advantage of being able to break through obstacles to go after players. They can also very easily catch players in mid-air. The single player wins if they manages to pop all 3 tubes.

===Team===
In turn, the trio must try to avoid being popped by the single player. The three player's have much more maneuverability than the single player. They must try to spread apart to force the single player into attacking one at a time. They must also watch out for the obstacles and the single player being able to pop them when going off of a slope in the air. Only 1 and 2 players of the team needs to survive at the end in order for them to win this minigame.

==Controls==
The controls are the same in all three iterations of Tube It or Lost It.

*Control Stick: Move
