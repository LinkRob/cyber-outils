'''World 3-3''' is the third [[level]] of [[World 3 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 3]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the eleventh level overall in said game, taking place in the heights. In the original and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]'' releases, this level is snow themed, though it uses grassland tiles in ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]''.  This level reappears in ''[[All Night Nippon: Super Mario Bros.]]'' as World B-3.

==Layout==
The player begins facing a high platform they need to jump onto before coming to a row of [[Koopa Troopa]]s and a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] on the next couple of platforms. After the first pipe with a [[Piranha Plant]] is a [[scale]] lift that leads into a couple more platforms with additional Piranha Plants in pipe, after the third of which is a [[Super Spring]]. Over the next tall platform is a [[Hidden Block]] containing a power-up, after which is another scale lift. Beyond the Koopa Paratroopa afterward a platforms that start to fall when the player lands on them with a pipe with a Piranha Plant between them. Afterward is a platform with Hidden Blocks above it that can be used as a platform to make jumping across the next segment easier. After the next Koopa Paratroopa is the [[Goal Pole]].
