The '''Mystery House''' is a special area in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. These areas make their debut in World 2 and appear in every even-numbered world.

==Summary==

Within these Mystery Houses are challenges that award the player a [[Green Power Star]] upon completing each challenge. Their appearance is almost absolutely identical to the [[Mystery Box]], except it has a triangular roof on top of it and a small red door on its side. The [[Mystery House Host|host]] is a generic [[Sprixie]].
