The '''Soreboar''' is an enemy from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They inhabit [[Twistee Island]].

== Description ==
Soreboars are boars with yellow fur. Their face is framed by a short, brown mane.

== Behaviour ==
Soreboars can attack by charging at their target, which must be jumped over. Unless the target stomps on them, the attack is performed again from behind. The second attack is a rolling charge that must be jumped over, targeting one brother and then the other. As the Bros. defeat enemies in the fight, Soreboars may bring in a larger Soreboar.

The regular Soreboar is of Level 3 with 24 hit points, an attack power of 13, a defense of 8, and a speed of 16.  When slain, it gives 3 experience points and 3 coins, and may drop a Mushroom (3%), a Nut (1%), or a Twistee Leaf (18%).

== Variants ==

=== Angry Soreboar ===
Attacking a Soreboar may cause it to transform into an Angry Soreboar.  Angry Soreboars look like their regular counterparts, but are red with blank yellow eyes; their stats are also the same, right down to the rewards. They have the same attacks as the regular version, but performed even faster.

=== Big Soreboar ===
Big Soreboars are bigger specimina of Soreboars, brought into combat by smaller versions. Their attacks are the same, but they are stronger. A big Soreboar is of Level 4 with 45 hit points, an attack power of 16, a defense of 9, and a speed of 20, giving 8 experience and 6 coins when slain, along with possibly a Mushroom (5%), a Super Mushroom (2%), or a Twistee Leaf (36%).

=== Glohm Soreboar ===
After Bowser pauses his sortie on the [[Zokket Army]] and disconnects Twistee Island by shooting [[Shipshape Island]], certain Soreboars are infected with [[Glohm]]. These Soreboars fight like their regular counterparts, but can summon a shield that can break in a few hits. A Glohm Soreboar has a Level of 29, 312 hit points, an attack power of 128, a defense of 94, and a speed of 150. When destroyed, it gives 370 experience and drops 41 coins along with possibly a Super Mushroom (3%), an Ultra Nut (1%), a Twistee Leaf (18%), a Glohm Crystal (5%), some Glohm Dust (9%), or a Glohm Fragment (9%). Bigger specimina are of Level 30, with 500 hit points, an attack power of 139, a defense of 100, and a speed of 158, and their destruction gives 604 experience, 65 coins, and possibly a Super Mushroom (5%), an Ultra Nut (2%), a Twistee Leaf (36%), a Glohm Crystal (8%), some Glohm Dust (13%), or a Glohm Fragment (13%).

After [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|takes over Concordia]], Glohm Soreboars gain a new ability when destroyed, causing a [[Ring Beamer]]-esque shockwave of fire that must be jumped over to erupt as they die. At this point, Glohm Soreboars are of Level 39 with 577 hit points, giving 794 experience and 120 coins when destroyed, with bigger specimina having 866 hit points and giving 1191 experience and 180 coins when destroyed.
