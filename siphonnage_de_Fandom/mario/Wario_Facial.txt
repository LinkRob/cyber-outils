'''Wario Facial''' is one of [[Wario]]'s [[microgame]]s under the [[Anything Goes]] category in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to insert three parts of [[Wario]]'s face in the correct order, including the hat, the eyes, and the nose with a mustache. Each part's incorrect options are [[Mario]]'s and [[Luigi]]'s respectively. If all the correct parts are added to Wario's face, the player wins, but if either the objective is not met on time or an incorrect part is selected, the player loses.

*'''Level 1:''' Only one face part has to be selected for Wario.
*'''Level 2:''' Two of Wario's face parts have to be selected, one of which is set to the correct option, while the other one is the wrong option.
*'''Level 3:''' Three face parts have to be put on Wario, two of which are the correct option while one of them is set to the wrong option.
