==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Jump on the first Stone Spike and use the moving platform to get the first coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Jump on the fifth Stone Spike and move right to get the second Star Coin. If you didn't collect the coin, use Luigi's Super Jump to collect the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Stay on the last moving platform until getting the third Star Coin. Jump right after to the flagpole to avoid death.

==Hidden Luigi image==
When almost getting the third Star Coin, jump on the final Spike and move left to the high wall to see A picture of Super Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' jumping.
