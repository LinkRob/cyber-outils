An '''Ultra Mushroom''' (also known as an '''Ultra Shroom''' or '''Max Mushroom''') is a healing item found in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]], and the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]].

==History==
===''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''===
[[File:Yellow Mushroom.png|frame|right]]
In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', Ultra Mushrooms are known as '''Max Mushrooms''', have yellow caps, and completely refill one party member's HP when used. An Ultra Mushroom can be bought at [[Smithy Factory]] for 39 [[coin]]s.

===''Paper Mario'' series===
In the ''Paper Mario'' series, these [[mushroom]]s, known as '''Ultra Shrooms''', have the same green caps as [[1-Up Mushroom]]s (which are absent in the first three ''Paper Mario'' games).
==== ''Paper Mario'' ====
[[File:Ultra Shroom.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Ultra Shrooms are rare items that restore fifty [[Heart Point|HP]]. Ultra Shrooms are not sold in any shop, but many can still be obtained from the [[Li'l-Oink Farm]]. Ultra Shrooms are dropped by [[Li'l-Oink|Golden Li'l-Oinks]] in non-Japanese versions of the game. A few Ultra Shrooms can be found throughout the game, namely in the latter levels. [[Shiver Snowfield]] has a hidden block containing an Ultra Shroom while [[Shiver Mountain]] has one in a pedestal that can be traded out for another item.

==== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''====
[[File:Ultra Shroom (TTYD).png|frame|left]]
[[File:Ultra Mushroom PMTTYDNS icon.png|thumb]]
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', very few places sell Ultra Mushroom (originally called Ultra Shrooms); however, they may be purchased for 200 [[Coin]]s each at the [[Deepdown Depot]], the shop in [[Rogueport Underground]], and they can also be found in the [[Pianta Parlor]] for 67 (80 in the Nintendo Switch remake) [[pianta token]]s. [[Charlieton]] occasionally sells Ultra Mushrooms for 120 coins.

==== ''Super Paper Mario'' ====
{{Main|Ultra Shroom Shake}}
[[File:Ultra Shroom SPM.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Ultra Shrooms retain their function of restoring 50 HP of the player character. Ultra Shrooms are rare, but Mario can generally find them before boss battles during the later chapters. Ultra Shrooms are also hidden within [[? Block]]s.

Their carry-on equivalent is the [[Ultra Shroom Shake]], which Mario can use to hold to use whenever he needs it.

===''Mario & Luigi'' series===
{{multiframe|[[File:Super Mushroom MLSS.png]] [[File:MaxMushPiT.png]] [[File:Mushroom BiS sprite.png]] [[File:M&LDT Mushroom.png]]}}
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' Ultra Mushroom restores 120 HP to either Mario or [[Luigi]]. In the Japanese version, it is at a lower number of 75 HP. Ultra Mushrooms can be bought from certain [[Item Shop]]s for 100 [[coin]]s.

===''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''===
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', an Ultra Mushroom restores 80 HP to one of the brothers. Ultra Mushrooms are now sold at less expensive price of 40 coins at certain Item Shops.

===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''===
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', they restore 120 HP, as in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''.

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', the Ultra Mushroom restores 80 HP like in ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''; this number is only slightly more than how much it restores in the original's Japanese version.

===''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''===
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', Ultra Mushrooms restore 100 HP.
