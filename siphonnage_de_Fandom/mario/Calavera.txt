'''El Calavera''' is a rabbid that appears in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.


== Profile ==
El Calavera is a Rabbid with a body stashed inside of a wooden coffin. His limbs are made out of thorny vines. He has taken [[Rabbid Yoshi]] as a hostage. He resembles ‘Day of the Dead’ symbolism.

== Attacks ==
Grenaduck: El Calavera forces Rabbid Yoshi to launch a grenaduck from his mouth, dealing 100 damage to heroes. The grenaduck has a chance to push heroes, dealing 105 damage.

== Trivia ==
*The name El Calavera in Spanish means ‘The Skull’ in English.
