'''Hookbill the Koopa''' is an enlarged [[Koopa Troopa]] in the game ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|remake]]. He is the [[List of bosses|boss]] of [[World 4 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 4]] and resides atop the [[Hookbill The Koopa's Castle|castle of that world]].

A similar boss called [[Knot-Wing the Koopa]] appears in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy and Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', as a recurring middle boss in even-numbered worlds. Knot-Wing is essentially a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] enlarged in the same fashion as Hookbill. Additionally, [[Kent C. Koopa]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]'' resembles a bespectacled version of Hookbill.
==History==
===''Yoshi's Island'' series===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''====
[[File:Hookbill-SMW2.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Hookbill battle SMA3.png|thumb|Boss fight in ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'']]
When [[Yoshi (species)|Blue Yoshi]] and [[Baby Mario]] reach the bridge on which Hookbill is fought, a fog envelops the area, dissipating on the other side. Blue Yoshi and Baby Mario then encounter a normal Koopa Troopa and [[Kamek]], who casts his magic on the Koopa. The Koopa Troopa retreats into his shell, which grows.

After the transformation is complete, Hookbill re-emerges from his shell. Walking on all fours, he creeps towards Blue Yoshi to attack him and Baby Mario. After walking on all fours for some time, he stands up and runs in Blue Yoshi's direction and lunges at him. Blue Yoshi then must dodge the attack in order to avoid getting hurt. To defeat Hookbill, Blue Yoshi must first throw [[Yoshi's Egg|egg]]s at his head or arms. Jumping on Hookbill's shell or head will cause Hookbill to cough up eggs for Blue Yoshi to use in case his supply gets depleted. After four hits while on all fours or after three hits while on two legs, Hookbill will fall backwards and will be briefly vulnerable to attack. Blue Yoshi must jump on and then [[Ground Pound|ground pound]] Hookbill's exposed belly, delivering damage to him. After this (or after a certain amount of time passes), Hookbill will recover, retreat into his shell, and begin pounding the ground. He will then re-emerge, and the process will have to be repeated. Once his belly has been ground pounded three times, Hookbill is defeated. His shell explodes and disappears, and Hookbill (with no shell and reverted to normal size) falls from the top of the castle.

====''Yoshi's Island DS''====
During the introduction to ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', Hookbill the Koopa makes an appearance alongside the other castle bosses in the game as the events of ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' are recapped.

===''Tetris Attack''===
[[File:TA SNES Hookbill.png|frame|left]]
Hookbill The Koopa reappears in ''[[Tetris Attack]]'' as one of [[Bowser]]'s allies in the game and speaks with a stutter. Here, he takes Phoenix's place of the original ''[[Panel de Pon]]'' at Stage 9, and unlike other characters, Hookbill does not have a personal ally of his own.

==Trivia==
*The design of the turtle boss had several revisions during the development of ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', earlier depicted as a shelled creature somewhat resembling [[Birdo]].
*Hookbill the Koopa is the only boss in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' other than Baby Bowser whose explosion effects are a different coloration from the magic spells cast upon by Kamek (shared by a Yellow Yoshi post-boss battle in Prince Froggy's Fort).
