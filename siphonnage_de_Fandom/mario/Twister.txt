'''Twisters''' are wind-enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. This enemy doesn't directly deal damage to the players. It emits a gust of wind which will propel the player upwards, either helping them reach taller areas or sending them into a trap.

==Trivia==
*Twisters slightly resemble [[Weighdown]]s, a similar enemy from ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.
