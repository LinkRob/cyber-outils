}}|rivals=[[Captain Goomba (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Goomba]]}}'''Private Goomp''' is a character that first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. He is one of [[Bowser]]'s elite troops and he is mainly seen with [[Corporal Paraplonk]] and [[Sergeant Guy]]. Private Goomp and the others eventually return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. He also has a large main role in two side stories: ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' and ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.
==Appearance==
Private Goomp is a [[Goomba]] that appears to wear blue pajamas. He also has long eyelashes. He seems to be the defensivest of the [[Elite Trio|Trio]], because he has multiple Goombas at battle. In ''Minion Quest'', he wears a black pijama hat with a Bowser's mark.

==History==

===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''/''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' + ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey''===
In the original/[[Nintendo DS|DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', Private Goomp, along with Corporal Paraplonk and Sergeant Guy, find their real leader Bowser, where he needs a Banzai Bill to help break into his castle, after Fawful had taken over it. Private Goomp and the others said that one was around and Bowser has to go and search for it. Bowser succeeds in finding one from a Wiggler and launches the bullet at Fawful's Castle. However, the castle ends up crushing Bowser which makes Private Goomp and the others worry. However, Bowser soon turns giant and defeats his castle. Private Goomp isn't seen again until after Bowser comes to Blubble Lake near the Tower of Yikk, then Bowser follows him and he tells Bowser that the next Star Cure is in his castle. After Bowser goes to his castle, defeats the train and remembers the combination, he gets into the safe to get the Star Cure. However, Private Goomp and the others betray Bowser, pushes him into the safe, and lock it. Private Goomp then says that they will get paid greatly by Fawful. However, once Fawful is defeated, Private Goomp and the others beg Bowser for forgiveness. Bowser eventually forgives them and yells them to help fix back up his castle.

Private Goomp and the others have been promoted at some point before the events of the game, where they assist Bowser and [[Antasma]] in taking over [[Pi'illo Island|the island]] and [[Mushroom Earth|the world]]. Private Goomp was part of an ambush after Mario was fooled by a [[Kamek|fake Princess Peach]]. Private Goomp, along with Corporal Paraplonk and Sergeant Guy, then fight Mario with their many arsenal of attacks. However, Private Goomp accidentally tells Mario that the only way to defeat them is to knock them out all at the same time. An angry Sergeant Guy hits Private Goomp for telling that information, which ends them defeated. After he and the others are defeated, Private Goomp has been demoted to scrubbing the [[Neo Bowser Castle|new castle]] by Kamek. As Mario & Luigi infliltrate their way to Bowser after Antasma's defeat, Private Goomp and his comrades attempt to defend Bowser by rolling lots of [[Mega Bob-omb|Mega Bob-ombs]] down the path to him only to fail and fall down. After Bowser is defeated and the castle completely destroyed, he, Kamek, Private Goomp, Sergeant Guy and Corporal Paraplonk decide to retreat. Although Bowser is mad that his plan failed and blames Antasma, he tells his three minions that they did pretty well by their usual standards, much to their joy.
