}}'''Blazing Lassos''' is a Duel minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. This minigame's name is a pun of the game ''Blazing Lazers''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the two players must use their lassos in order to bring in barrels with numbers on them. The numbers determine how many points the player gets. There are also barrels with zeros on them and they should be avoid. The player who has the most points within the time limit wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}} (Twirl) - Throw lasso, Yank back
