The '''Mushroom Pond''' is a location in the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]], in [[Illumination]]'s, ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series.

==History==
While Mario and Luigi are taking care of the kingdom, during Peach's abscence, A [[Mega Cheep-Cheep]] was seen consuming a Blue Toad riding a fishing boat on the pond before being rescued by [[Frog Mario|Luigi]].
