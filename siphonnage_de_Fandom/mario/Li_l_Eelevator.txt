'''Li'l Eelevators''' are eel-like creatures in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''.

== Description ==
Li'l Eelevators are blue eel-like creatures with crystalline skin, golden lips and white buck teeth. On their head is a mowhawk-shaped fin, and two small fins can be seen at the sides of their mouth. They are implied to be the older form of the [[Eeling]]s, who look more like an infant. Both of them are the children of the [[Eelevator]] and the [[Great Harmoneel]].

== Behaviour ==
[[File:Teeleport.png|left|thumb|DK and Pauline teeleporting]]
Li'l Eelevators are a friendly people who quickly befriend [[DK]] and [[Pauline]]. They can be used to transport the pair to various locations on the same layer by jumping into their mouth and being taken there. They can be found in almost every layer of the [[Underground World]], and are good friends of another crystal-like species, the [[Fractone]].

== Trivia ==
*The transportation process is referred to as ''teeleporting'', a pun on teal (their color), eel (the species they resemble) and teleportation (the process of moving between two locations quickly or instantly).
*They claim to be able to transport the user between different layers as well, but comment that it would worry their mother if they moved too far from her. One is later seen briefly in [[New Donk City]].
