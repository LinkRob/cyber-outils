'''Magnet Carta''' is a 2 vs. 2 [[coin]]-collecting [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. It costs 150 coins to purchase from [[Woody]].

==Gameplay==
The players have thirty seconds to pick up [[coin]]s in the center and bring it to their team's hole. There are also [[coin bag]]s worth 5 coins, and [[treasure chest]]s worth 10 coin.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Move

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The title of the minigame is a pun on "Magna Carta", meaning "great charter".
