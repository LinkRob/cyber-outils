A '''Wereturtle''' is a creature that appears in the Halloween episode of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. Their name is a combination or werewolf and turtle. His original appearance is a Koopa Troopa but when Bowser says to them, "Behold, the full moon," they turn into wereturtles.
