|name=Blazehammer (Battle Card)}}'''Blazehammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Blazehammer is the fiery version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer Attack|Hammer attack]]. It is a brown hammer with a flame in the center, accompanied with a red/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: N/A

Sell Price: 8/16 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Blazehammer is first seen in [[Indigo Underground]], but can also found in [[Daffodil Peak]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Marmalade Valley]], [[Kiwano Temple]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Violet Passage]], [[Sunset Express]], [[Redpepper Volcano]], [[Redpepper Crater]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
