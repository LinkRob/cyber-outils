'''Gold Digger''' is [[Mona]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. It is also [[9-Volt]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'' and ''[[Game & Wario]]'' (in the minigame [[Gamer]]).

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to pick the finger through the nostrils by pressing {{button|gba|a}} at the right time.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The pinky finger must pick the nose.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The index finger must pick the nose.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' Two fingers (index and middle) must pick through both nostrils.

=== ''WarioWare: Touched!'' ===
Gold Digger returns as 9-Volt's microgame but all level difficulties are the same.

=== ''WarioWare: D.I.Y.'' ===
The microgame's objective is the same, but the player must use the stylus to pick the nose.

=== ''Game & Wario'' ===
The microgame appears in ''[[Game & Wario]]'' in [[Gamer]]. The nose is replaced with a mouth in [[Fake Out]] that the player must not pick.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
Despite it originally from ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', it has a strong resemblance to ''Game & Wario''. There is a possible chance that the player must pick the camel's nose. There is also a possible chance that a pierced nose appears that the player must not pick. The level difficulties are same with ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

=== ''WarioWare: Get It Together!'' ===
This version of the microgame doesn’t involve fingers picking the nose, but the player’s character must jump inside the nose’s nostril or shoot it with an attack form. 

* '''Level 1:''' The nose stays still.
* '''Level 2:''' Same as Level 1 but the nose breathes within time.
* '''Level 3:''' The nose moves from side to side.

In two-player, the player to plug the nose will earn an effort point, and the player to hit the nose will get an effort point taken away.

==== Mission ====

* '''Pick Your Battles''' - Using all crew members, the player must complete the microgame 18 times in a row by picking the left and right nostrils 9 times each.
