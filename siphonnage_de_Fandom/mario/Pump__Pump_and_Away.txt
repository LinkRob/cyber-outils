|type = 2 vs. 2 mini-game}}'''Pump,  Pump and Away''' is a 2 vs. 2 mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the two teams must pump air into their rockets to power it. The players of the two teams must work together and fill the rocket with much air as they can before the time ends. After that, the rockets are launched into the air and whichever is higher, that team wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|a}} - Pump up
*{{button|n64|b}} (or {{button|n64|z}}) - Pump down
