'''Flame Runner '''is an unlockable bike in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. It resembles a classic Harley-Davidson chopper with a fire design on the side. The character's logo is found on the headlights, but it seen on the rear of the bike as well. The engine has a [[Fire Flower]], a V-twin cylinder with [[Mushroom]] pistons, and a [[? Block|? mark]] engraved on it. The Flame Rider offers a small acceleration boost and a large handling boost at the cost of weight and grip. It shares its stats with [[Pipe Frame]], [[Wild Wiggler]], [[Standard Bike]], [[W 25 Silver Arrow]], [[Varmint]], and [[City Tripper]]. Advantages about this bike is the handling and advantages, however, the traction and weight is poor. This bike is only unlocked by collecting 950 coins.
