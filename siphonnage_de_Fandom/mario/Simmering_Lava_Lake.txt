|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''}}'''Simmering Lava Lake''' is a level of [[World Castle]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Layout==
This level starts on a platform where lava rises and sinks. After navigating past some [[Parabones]] and [[Splorch]]es, the player comes upon a [[Clear Pipe]] which transports them to an area with the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. This area houses some [[Lava Plume]]s,  of the aforementioned enemies, and a Clear Pipe at the end. The clear pipe brings the player to an area with a few [[Charvaargh]]s and the [[Goal Pole]].


==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': The first green star is not hard to spot, as it is floating over a pool of lava. To retrieve it, you must use a boomerang flower.
*'''Green Star 2''': To collect the second star, touch the star-shaped coin ring and, in the few seconds with which you are provided, collect all eight coins.
*'''Green Star 3''': A little while after the stamp, you will see a platform sinking and reemerging from the lava. When it is above the lava, hop on it to get the last star.

==Stamp==
[[File:Simmering_Lava_Lake_Stamp.png|thumb]]

After the checkpoint, there is a platform rising and falling into the lava. This is where the stamp is. Wait for the lava to lower and snatch the stamp.
