'''Jarvis, the Jar Collector''' is an antique jar-collecting [[portrait ghost]] of unknown age in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'' and [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|its remake]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Jarvis is a purple ghost with only a head, a little hair, yellow eyes and big lips making for a rounded mouth.

===Personality===
Jarvis speaks in a stereotypical Scottish accent. He loves [[jar]]s so much that he lives inside one.

==History==
[[File:Jarvis LM3DS encounter.png|thumb|Luigi's first encounter with Jarvis in the Nintendo 3DS remake.]]
[[Luigi]] first finds Jarvis in the [[Ceramics Studio]] on the third floor of [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|Luigi's Mansion]]. Although Luigi does not need to capture Jarvis to save [[Mario]], he can earn a [[Blue Diamond]] and two [[Gold Bar]]s if he succeeds in capturing him.

After Luigi examines the area around the far left wall, Jarvis appears from one of the jars and challenges Luigi to a ''[[Wikipedia:Whac-A-Mole|Whac-A-Mole]]''-style game. If Luigi can hit Jarvis with blasts of ice seven times as he warps to different jars around the room, the ghost allows Luigi to fight him. If Luigi fails to hit Jarvis seven times, he gets teleported outside of the [[Armory (Luigi's Mansion)|Armory]].

Angered by losing the game, Jarvis levitates ceramic jars and tosses them at Luigi, dealing five [[Heart Point|HP]] damage for every hit. Luigi can counter by sucking up Jarvis with the [[Poltergust 3000]], while trying to not hit the floating jars, otherwise Jarvis will escape the Poltergust's pull. Jarvis has 100 HP.

Jarvis' portrait number in the [[Gallery (Luigi's Mansion)|Gallery]] is 12.

==Flavor text==
*'''Game Boy Horror profile:''' ''He loves his antique jars so much that he now lives in one. He can hardly contain himself!''
*'''Game Boy Horror quote:''' "''Go back to the woods, boyo!''" (Nintendo 3DS version only)

== Trivia ==
*Jarvis is very similar to [[Roger the Potted Ghost]], who appeared in the game ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.
*Jarvis is the only non-boss portrait ghost whose heart can not be checked at all. If the player tries to scan his heart, nothing occurs. This does not happen in the 3DS version, as Jarvis has a heart quote.
*Jarvis has an appearance similar to [[Ty-foo]]s from ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.
*In the Japanese version, the description for Jarvis notes that it is a mystery what he uses for a toilet.
