A '''Mad Mecha-Spike Top''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mad Mecha-Spike Tops are a variant of [[Mecha-Spike Top]]s imbued with the [[Rage]] [[vibe]], and they differ in appearance from having a yellow spike on their back instead.

===Behavior===
As with most other enemies, Mad Mecha-Spike Top moves around, and to attack, it stops moving to extend out its spike, revealing to to be over twice the size of its body.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Mad Mecha-Spike Tops are first encountered in [[Fury Volcano 4-4]]. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can only defeat them using the Rage vibe. In their [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] entry, Mad Mecha-Spike Tops have their name formatted '''M. M-Spike Top'''.

==Flavor text==
*[[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]]: ''A dangerous foe that can stretch its spines.''
