'''Ultra-Hand Return''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] used by [[Wario]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Wario gets out an [[Ultra Hand]], extends it toward the ball, and hits it. The tennis ball then moves over the net with little power and in the form of a straight shot.
