'''Bunny Springs''' are an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
A Bunny Spring is a large springing platform designed after a bunny. [[Wario]] can [[Ground Pound (move)|ground pound]] a Bunny Spring to have it launch him high up into the air, allowing him to reach certain higher areas.

==In ''Wario World''==
Bunny Springs are first found in [[Greenhorn Forest]] and then later in [[Shivering Mountains]]. In the latter level, they are colored white.
