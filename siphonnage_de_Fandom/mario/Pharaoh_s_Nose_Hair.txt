The '''Pharaoh's Nose Hair''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''

==Description==
The Pharaoh's Nose Hair is a long strand of brown nose hair belonging to [[Poobah the Pharaoh]].

==History==
The Pharaoh's Nose Hair can be found in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]] inside of a purple chest in an outdoor area behind the [[Sphinx (character)|Sphinx]]. [[Wario]] was tasked by the Sphinx to find three of the Pharaoh's treasures in order to know important information. The Pharaoh's Nose Hair is one of those and the other two are the [[Pharaoh's Pillow]] and the [[Pharaoh's Underwear]].
