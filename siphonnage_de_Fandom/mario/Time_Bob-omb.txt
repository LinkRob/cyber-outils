A '''Time Bob-omb''' is a type of [[Bob-omb]] species, which first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They are only present in [[Bowser Castle (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Bowser Castle]]

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Time Bob-ombs resemble a normal bob-omb, but flash red and have a counter on their face, similarly to the ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' variant of [[Bob-omb]].

=== Behavior ===
The Time Bob-omb's gimmick is that the player has to defeat a boss within 8 turns (the turns vary in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', 9 for [[Roy Koopa|Roy]], 7 for [[Wendy O. Koopa|Wendy]], and 5 for [[Larry Koopa|Larry]]). If the Time Bob-omb reaches zero, it with explode on [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], causing an instant Game Over. Only Roy, Wendy, and Larry use them.

[[Cackletta|Bowletta]] later uses this to begin her third phase during her battle.

==Trivia==
*The Time Bob-omb does a total of -398HP, which if the player has more than that, they will not get knocked out, and the player can have unlimited turns to defeat a [[Koopalings|Koopaling]]. This was changed to an automatic KO in the 3DS version.
*When [[Bowletta]] uses the Time Bob-omb at the end of the battle and the Bros. should survive, they will not get knocked out. However, Bowletta still thinks they are unconscious and regardless, still inhales them in her belly.
