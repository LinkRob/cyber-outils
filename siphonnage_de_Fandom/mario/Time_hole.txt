'''Time holes''' are an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.

==Description==
Time holes are tears in the spacetime continuum that can be used to time-travel between the past and the present. A time hole has the appearance of a circular swirl with pink and yellow patterns.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''==
Time holes are essential for the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] to use during their journey, as time-traveling is a key mechanic. If one pair of the Mario Bros. jumps through a time hole alone, the other pair follows soon after, regardless of the terrain outside the time hole they went through.

There are a few different ways that a time hole can be created. The first time hole is opened when the damaged [[E. Gadd's time machine|time machine]] returns from the past, but it closes after being used by [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Stuffwell]]. Most of the other time holes are caused by reactions from the [[Cobalt Star]] shards. During her boss fight, the [[Elder Princess Shroob]] can open time holes, although this is to attack Mario and Luigi.

[[Professor E. Gadd]] later uses the time holes' energy to power his time machine after the Cobalt Star is destroyed, bringing Mario, Luigi, and [[Princess Peach]] back into the present.
