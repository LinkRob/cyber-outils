'''The Lonely Koopa Troopa''' is the eighth level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 22.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 3 [[Koopa Troopa]]s, and 5 [[Beanerang Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Beanerang Bro.

The 2nd battle contains 3 Koopa Troopas, and 5 Limbo Bros. The enemy captain is a Limbo Bro.

The 3rd battle contains 3 Koopa Troopas and 5 [[Fire Stalking Piranha Plant|Fire Stalking Piranha Pl.]] The enemy captain is a Fire Stalking Piranha Pl.

The final battle contains 5 Koopa Troopas, and 3 [[Koopa Paratroopa|Koopa Paratroopas (Red)]]. The enemy captain is a Koopa Troopa, and he knows [[Shell Shock ( Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions)|Shell Shock]] and [[Deny]].

After the final battle, the player recruits [[Captain Koopa Troopa]] (Lv. 23).

== Possible recruits ==
* Koopa Troopa (Lv. 22)
* Koopa Paratroopa (Red), (Lv. 22)
* Fire Stalking Piranha Pl. (Lv. 22)
