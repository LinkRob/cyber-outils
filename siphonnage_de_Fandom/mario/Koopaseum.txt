The '''Koopaseum''' is a large, colosseum-like structure in the middle of the [[Gritzy Desert]] that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Its name is a portmanteau of "[[Koopa (species)|Koopa]]" and "[[Colosseum]]". In the past, the [[Mario]] [[Luigi|brothers]] and the [[Baby Mario]] [[Baby Luigi|brothers]] came to the Koopaseum looking for [[Princess Peach]]. Once the brothers entered through the secret entrance, they saw the Princess Shroob giving a victory speech to celebrate conquering the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] which the Shroobs cheered to. At the same time, Peach was fed to [[Petey Piranha]] on [[Princess Shroob]]'s commands, with [[Kylie Koopa]] being kidnapped by the [[Shroob]]s after.

In the Koopaseum, the brothers enter the arena to a large audience of a million Shroobs and fight four Shroobs on the stage with Princess Shroob, three of which the brothers (except Luigi) destroy, before fighting the [[Shrooboid Brat]]. After the brothers defeat it, they are taken down by many Shroobs with [[hammer]]s. The brothers are then dropped to [[Gritzy Caves]], which is directly below the Koopaseum.

It is unknown who really owns the museum but because of the name of the museum, it is very possible that the [[Koopa]]s once owned the museum but Shroobs later took over it. It is also possible that when the Shroobs came to Gritzy Desert, the Koopaseum was already abandoned.
