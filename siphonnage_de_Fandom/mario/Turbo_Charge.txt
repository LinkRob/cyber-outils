|textcolor = ffffff}}'''Turbo Charge''' is a special move used by [[Watt]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It can be used once Watt is powered up to the [[Super Block#Super Rank|Super Rank]] and it requires 3 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Usage==

To use Turbo Charge, the player must press the {{button|n64|a}} button at the right moments to have Watt increase Mario's attack power. If this is done correctly, Mario's attack power will be increased by 1 up to four turns. This can be useful against strong and lengthy boss fights and against [[The Master]] as well.

==Trivia==

*Turbo Charge is one of four partner moves in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' that affects Mario directly. The others being [[Lady Bow]]'s [[Outta Sight]], [[Sushie]]'s [[Water Block]], and [[Lakilester]]'s [[Cloud Nine]].
