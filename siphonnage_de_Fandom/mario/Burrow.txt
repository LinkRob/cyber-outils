'''Burrow''' is a move in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Burrow can only be used by [[Monty Mole]] during a close play. As such, it can only be used when the appropriate button, either {{button|wii|A}} or {{button|wii|B}}, is pressed during a close play. If done successfully, Monty Mole tunnels underneath where the character at the base is standing and reaches it without getting out.
