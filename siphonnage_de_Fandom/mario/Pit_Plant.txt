The '''Pit Plant''' is a giant green [[Piranha Plant]] who only appears as one of the main obstacles within the course [[Dry Dry Desert (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Dry Dry Desert]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.

==Physical description==
Only the head of the Pit Plant is ever shown. The Pit Plant has a dark green head with white spots, large, light green lips resembling [[Petey Piranha]]'s, and maroon spikes protruding from various corners of its head.

==In ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!''==
The Pit Plant hides in the center of a quicksand pit in Dry Dry Desert. Here, it waits for racers to drive into there. If a pair of racers is pulled into the quicksand, the Pit Plant emerges to swallow them. A few seconds later, the pair of racers are returned onto the race course by [[Lakitu (referee)|Lakitu]].

Although Dry Dry Desert returns in both ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', the Pit Plant does not, because if a racer falls into the quicksand, Lakitu instantly arrives to rescue them.

==Trivia==
*The Pit Plant may be a reference to the [[wikipedia:Sarlacc|Sarlacc]] and the Great Pit of Carkoon from the 1983 film ''[[wikipedia:Star Wars Episode VI: Return of the Jedi|Star Wars Episode VI: Return of the Jedi]]'', both being large, carnivorous plants inspired by [[wikipedia:Antlion Larvae|antlion larvae]]. Both creatures are also found in desert regions at the bottom of a sloped pit.
