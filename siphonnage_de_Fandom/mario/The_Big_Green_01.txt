==History==
During his journey to find the next piece of the [[Marvelous Compass]], [[Luigi]] finds one as the grand prize of a race on [[Circuit Break Island]]. Luigi then uses the Big Green 01 which is his partner's [[Torque]]'s kart in the race. However, the Big Green 01 ended up going in reverse and crashes into all of the other karts destroying them. Luigi wins the race by default and is awarded with the piece. However, Torque continues to follow Luigi around until he pays off for the damages of his kart.
