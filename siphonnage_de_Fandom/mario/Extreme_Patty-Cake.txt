'''Extreme Patty-Cake''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', as one of [[Kat and Ana]]'s microgames, and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', as as one of [[Jimmy T]]'s.

== Gameplay ==
There will be a person in front of the player. The player must quickly and forcefully push their hands, using the {{button|wii|wiimote}} in ''Smooth Moves'' or {{button|3ds|A}} in ''Gold'', to make them fall down. The victims cannot be pushed if their hands are moved in.

=== In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'' ===
*'''1st level difficulty:''' The victim is a girl who is standing. When she falls down, two mushrooms bounce.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The victim is a boy who moves his hands slowly in and out. When he falls down, two flowers bloom and the volcano on the background erupts.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The victim is a man who moves his hands quickly in and out. When he falls down, he gets sent flying into the background. There, a giant cat eats him and a giant dog shakes its backside.

=== In ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
*'''1st level difficulty:''' The victim is a man who rarely moves his hands.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The victim is a man who moves his hands slowly in and out. The player must push him into the sea.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The victim is a boy who moves his hands constantly, and must be hit twice to give in. He is then captured by a giant flying rooster.
