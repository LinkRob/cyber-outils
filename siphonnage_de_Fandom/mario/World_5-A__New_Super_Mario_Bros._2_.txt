World 5-A or World 5-Airship Is a Stage In [[New Super Mario Bros. 2]].

== Layout ==

== Star Coins ==

* '''Star Coin #1 -''' Bounce of the nearby [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]] to reach It.
* '''Star Coin #2 -''' After the Checkpoint there are a few ways to reach It.
** '''1.''' You can bounce of the to reach the nearby ledges
** '''2.''' You can use the Spring that you grabbed from the [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]].
* '''Star Coin #3 -''' Like with the previous you can either reach It with a running jump or by bouncing off one of the [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopas]].

== Secret Exit ==
The player needs to grab the spring just before the [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]]. Then they must hold on to It till the end of the level. They must hit the right brick block just before the to reveal a hidden beanstalk. Then they must climb It to find another area. As well as the [[Goal Pole|Secret Exit]] .
