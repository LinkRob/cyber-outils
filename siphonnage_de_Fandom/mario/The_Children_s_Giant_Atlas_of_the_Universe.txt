'''''The Children's Giant Atlas of the Universe''''' is a book by {{wp|Ian Ridpath}} that appears in the ''[[King Koopa's Kool Kartoons]]'' episode "[[Kids Rights Day]]." The book is given by [[Bowser|King Koopa]] to the [[Bowser's Minions|Troopa]] in the "Magic Seat" (Red 7), the Troopa turning out to be [[Billy (King Koopa's Kool Kartoons)|Billy]]. The book's eponymous size is remarked on by King Koopa as he hands the book off to Billy.
