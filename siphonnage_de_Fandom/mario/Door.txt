'''Warp Doors''' (or simply '''doors''') are objects appearing in several games of the {{franchise|Mario}}. They take the playable character to next phase of the level. Most Doors are already unlocked, unlike [[Key Door]]s, which require a key to unlock. Some games featured [[P Warp Door]]s, which they are blue doors that debuts while the [[P Switch]] is in action.
