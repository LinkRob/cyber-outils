'''Isaac Marshall''' is a former game tester for [[Nintendo|Nintendo of America]] who is credited in several games in the ''[[Donkey Kong Country (series)|Donkey Kong Country]]'' series. He also portrayed [[Toad]] in the English version of ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'' and lent his voice for ''[[Tetris Attack]]''.

==Works==
*''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' &ndash; Game Testing (credited as '''I. Marshall''')
*''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'' &ndash; Game Testing (credited as I. Marshall)
*''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'' &ndash; Nintendo Product Testing (credited as I. Marshall)

==Portrayals==
*''[[Tetris Attack]]''
*''[[Mario Kart 64]]'' (international version) &ndash; [[Toad]]
