'''Arrowhead''' is a [[Duel Mode]] board that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Layout==
Arrowhead is pretty linear board that that is easy to navigate through. There is an area in the center of the stage where players can actually go endless around in a circle eventually lowering the counter on the [[Bellhop]] for a mini-game. This makes it easy for players to earn coins quickly.

The red player area is located on the top right corner of the board while the blue player area is located on the bottom left.

===Story Mode===
In [[Story Mode]], the player must duel [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] or [[Luigi]] on this board.
