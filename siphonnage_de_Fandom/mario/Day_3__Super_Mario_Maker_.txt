'''Day 3''' is the third day of ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' when you are about to create a course. A few moments later, you will hear a truck coming. A message will come up and it will ask you: "Do you want to play a sample course". You could either say "Create" or "Play". If you press Create, you will get the new items.
