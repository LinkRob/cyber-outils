|games = ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''}}'''Sweatmore Peak''' is a location that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Layout==
Sweatmore Peak is a large, volcanic mountain area that is home to a literal sea of magma where [[Wario]] must obtain objects called [[Sweatmore Hotpants]] in order to move through the magma unharmed. As he gets deeper within the area, he'll need [[Sweatmore Superhotpants]] to get down further but must be careful of enemy fish called [[Snackeral]]s that can steal the hotpants upon contact.

==History==
===Episode 7: Fouling Up the Whole Works!===
Wario makes it to Sweatmore Peak after being told by [[Count Cannoli]] to meet him there to find out more about the [[Wishstone]] pieces (still trying to get his wand [[Goodstyle]] back). Wario finds himself navigating through the hazardous magma sea using a combination of [[Captain Wario (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Captain Wario]] and the Sweatmore Hotpants until making it to the end. He defeats a large monster called [[Barfatronic Lavachomper]] which turns out to been created by [[Carpaccio]] in order to stop Wario. Seeing Wario as a major threat, Count Cannoli and Carpaccio decide to team up.

===Special Episode Part 4===
Count Cannoli makes it back to Sweatmore Peak hoping to get some "stuff." Wario himself is interested in the "stuff" and decides to take them for himself.
