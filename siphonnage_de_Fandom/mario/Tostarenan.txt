'''Tostarenans''' are the native of the Sand Kingdom introduced in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. They are the warm and friendly people who live in the [[Tostarena Town|Tostarena area]] of the Sand Kingdom.

==Description==
Tostarenans are based on the typical candy [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calavera sugarskulls], candy coming from a Mexican tradition called "[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_of_the_Dead day of the dead]" the candy has a skull shape, and [https://humanbreeds.com/2015/08/25/mexican-stereotypes/ Mexican stereotype] (people with sarape, mustache, and sombrero). As from their appearance, they're in all Mexican clothing, holding maracas, which are an instrument in Mexico.
