The '''Forbidden Pop-Up Book''' is an item in ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]''.

==Description==
The Forbidden Pop-Up Book is a book filled with spirits, and it is owned by [[Hongo]].

==History==
[[File:Forbidden Pop-Up Book screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|The Forbidden Pop-Up Book's in-game appearance]]
In the beginning of ''Yoshi Topsy-Turvy'', Hongo uses his magic to seal [[Bowser]] and his armies, who were causing chaos at [[Yoshi's Island (Yoshi franchise)|Yoshi Island]] at the book. However, Hongo fails and accidentally seals the entire island in the book. In the Forbidden Pop-Up Book, [[Yoshi]], helped by the [[Yoshi Island Spirits]], goes on a quest to defeat Bowser and bring Yoshi's Island back to normal.
