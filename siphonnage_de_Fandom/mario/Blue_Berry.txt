The '''Blue Berry''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
The Bubble Berry restores five of [[Mario]]'s [[Flower Point]]s. It can only be obtained by smacking a single tree in [[Flower Fields]] with the [[Hammer]]. This tree is located behind the yellow gate in Flower Fields.

In addition to being a healing item, Blue Berries can be given to the Blue Flower guarding the blue gate in Flower Fields. This path leads Mario to [[Rosie]], a pompous flower on the western side of Flower Fields who is in the possession of the [[Water Stone]] which is required to help out [[Lily (Paper Mario)|Lily]] by filling her pond with water.
