'''Tilt-a-Kart''' is a [[Battle Stage]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', and is one the toughest battle stages to accomplish.

There are no rails in this course and the stage tilts under even the slightest weight. The stage is made up of an 8-bit [[Mario]] sprite. [[Item Box]]es bounce up and down, and sometimes fall off the stage when the stage tilts. The white blocks on the edge of the stage slow the player down, helping them stop if they accidentally fall to the edge. The music that plays is a remix of [[Baby Park]]'s music.

Tilt-a-Kart is the second and final unlockable Battle Stage. To unlock it, the player has to finish [[Flower Cup]] on [[Engine Class|Mirror Mode]] in first place.

== Trivia ==
* The background of the course is like that of the Secret Areas in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.
* This is the only Battle Stage in the game where the players can drive off the edge.
