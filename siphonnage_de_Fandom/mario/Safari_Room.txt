The '''Safari Room''' is a room found in [[Area Three]] of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
[[Luigi]] has to defeat all of the [[ghost]]s to obtain the [[key]] that unlocks the door to [[Balcony (3F)]], where [[Boolossus]] is fought.
