'''Boo Balloons''' are a variant of [[Boo]]s featured in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They have the appearance of a light blue balloon with a Boo's face. They appear in two levels: [[Bigger Boo's Fort]] and [[Don't Look Back!]]. Boo Balloons float around in midair like a regular balloon, and if a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] touches one, he takes damage. If a Boo Balloon is hit with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]], it inflates, and Yoshi can defeat a Boo Balloon by hitting it with an egg three times, causing it to pop.
