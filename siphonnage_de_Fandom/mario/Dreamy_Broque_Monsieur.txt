|forms = }}'''Dreamy Broque Monsieur '''is a character featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' that appears in [[Dreamy Pi'illo Castle]]. He assists [[Mario]] and introduces him to [[Dreamy Luigi]] for the first time.

Dreamy Broque Monsieur explains that Dreamy Luigi looks a bit more handsome than in the real world, therefore providing Mario with his first good explanation of happenings in the [[Dream World]]. He guides Mario where to go in some areas and gives him his first info about Dream World battling.

Later in the game, Dreamy Broque Monsieur is seen trying to catch a [[Gold Beanie]] and says that Mario and Dreamy Luigi will get a lot of wealth and/or items if they catch one.
