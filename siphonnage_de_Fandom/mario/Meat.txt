==Appearances==
===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
Meat can only be found in [[Bowser]]'s levels of this game. Meat act very similar to [[Mushroom]]s as eating Meat allows Bowser to grow bigger. When he eats enough Meat, he'll turn into Giant Bowser and is able to smash through anything (and acts like a [[Starman]]). Giant Bowser wears off in about 10 seconds before Bowser turns back to normal. Meat can be found in blocks or by roasting [[X-Naut]]s.
