'''Mama''' is a character who appears in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'', released overseas as ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Her husband is [[Papa (Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic)|Papa]], her eldest son is [[Imajin]], and Mama has toddler twins named [[Poki and Piki]] respectively.

==Physical description==
Mama is tall, and she wears Arabian clothing consisting of a dark blue cap, a blue dress, and blue pointy shoes.

==In ''Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic''==
Mama is one of four playable characters, the others being Imajin, Papa, and [[Lina]]. With her family, Mama has to rescue Poki and Piki from [[Wart|King Mamu]], who dragged them into a storybook. Mama has the ability to [[jump]] higher and lightly hover at the top of her jumps.

Mama is replaced by [[Luigi]] in ''Super Mario Bros. 2''.
