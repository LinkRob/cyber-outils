'''Yoshi''' ('''Yoshi 1''' in Japan) is a sponsor in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'' ==
Mario Star made several appearances as trackside banners and signs in three courses.
=== List of appearances ===
* Trackside banners in [[Luigi Raceway]]
* Signs in [[Kalimari Desert]]
* Signs in [[Choco Mountain]]
* Signs in [[Mario Raceway]]
* Trackside banners in [[Royal Raceway]]

== Etymology ==
'''Yoshi 1''''s logo appears to be based on [[wikipedia:Mobil 1|Mobil 1]].
