The '''Zapperator''' is a new weapon that is seen in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is used for the [[Special Attack]] of the same name which [[Luigi]] seems to have access to, and he has to spend 7 [[Special Point]]s for its use.

==Description==
The Zapperator manifests as a cyan dome with two levers connected to its main part and a clock with a yellow frame, akin to the levers of a handcar. One of its levers is green with its other lever being red.

==Procedure==
The Zapperator attack starts when Luigi pulls the Zapperator out of his pocket and tosses it between himself and [[Mario]]. The brothers have to build up electricity for the cloud above the enemies to do more damage by pushing their respective levers, done when the player alternates between A and B quickly  (Each brother pulls his lever up when the other pushes his own lever down.), then the player must press X before the timer turns all red to activate the cloud and shoot [[Thunderbolt|thunderbolts]] at the enemies. When the cloyd flashes yellow that means the attack is ready. It is advised to go before the time runs out and to crank fast to ensure maximum power.
