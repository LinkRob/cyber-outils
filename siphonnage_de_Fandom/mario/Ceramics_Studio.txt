The '''Ceramics Studio''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Ceramics Studio is accessed from the door in the northern side of the [[Armory (Luigi's Mansion)|Armory]]. It has many ornate jars and vases, nine of which can be interacted with. Many of the jars and vases contain [[treasure]], and one of them even has a [[Red Stone]]. There are four jars placed against the north and south walls each. The jar near the center of the room is frozen and contains an [[Ice Elemental Ghost]]. Some crates and chairs are stacked behind the crafts. In the top-right corner is the chimney for the fireplace in the [[Study (Luigi's Mansion)|Study]], blocked by wooden boards.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Ceramics Studio is an optional room accessed in [[Area Four]] of [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|Luigi's Mansion]]. When Luigi enters the Ceramics Studio, he has to defeat [[Jarvis]], who spawns after walking up to the vases. After Luigi defeats Jarvis, a [[Treasure Chest|green treasure chest]] is spawned, and it contains various treasures, with a [[Blue Diamond]] being among them.
