The '''Mystery Egg''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and its two sequels, ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
The Mystery Egg is one of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Special Item (Mario Kart series)|Special Items]] in each of its appearances. Upon being used, the Mystery Egg is dropped behind the [[kart]] in the manner of a [[Banana]], and if a racer drives into the Mystery Egg, they become affected by a random status condition.
