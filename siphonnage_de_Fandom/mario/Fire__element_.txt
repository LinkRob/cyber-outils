'''Fire''' is a reoccurring element in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' role-playing games. It is, by far, the most common element that has first appeared in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Fire, in other games, tend to cause the [[burn]] ailment that ranks upon damage on each turn.

==''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
In this game, [[Mario]] is the main user of the Fire element on his team. Mario can utilize the [[Fire Orb]], [[Super Flame]], and [[Ultra Flame]] moves that can damage his enemies. Fire tends to be the weakness of insect and plant-related enemies especially those in the [[Forest Maze]].
