'''Mario Key''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
The Mario Key is a gold key with an uppercase "M" in its ring and [[Mario's Cap]] sitting on top.

==In ''Super Mario 64 DS''==
[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can obtain the Mario Key by defeating [[Goomboss]] in [[Goomboss Battle]]. With the key, Yoshi can unlock the door that [[Mario]] trapped behind, therefore unlocking Mario as a playable character.
