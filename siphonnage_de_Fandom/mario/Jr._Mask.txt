'''Jr. Mask''' is an item in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
Jr. Mask is a permanent item in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge mode]] that gives [[Bowser Jr.]] the ability to use his [[Star Skill]], [[Killer Jr. Ball]]. Jr. Mask has the exact appearance of Bowser Jr.'s bandana, although Bowser Jr. is still shown wearing it, even without the bandana.

Jr. Mask costs 200 [[coin]]s to purchase at the [[Item Shop]] if either Bowser Jr. is on the player's team or has been recruited to theirs.
