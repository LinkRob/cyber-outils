{{nihongo|'''Dotty'''|ドッティ|Dotti}} is a ladybug-like enemy featured in [[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|''Donkey Kong'']] for the [[Game Boy]].

==Profile==
===Behavior===
Dotties just circle around the environment. They do not harm [[Mario]] but will push him. He can also use them as stepping stools but he can't pick them up.
