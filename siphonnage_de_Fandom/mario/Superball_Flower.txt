A '''Superball Flower''' is an power-up that debuted in ''[[Super Mario Land]]'' and reappeared in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

==Description==
When [[Mario]] touches a Superball Flower, he transforms into [[Superball Mario]], giving him the ability to toss [[Superball]]s. The Superball Flower is similar to the [[Fire Flower]], and is ''Super Mario Land''{{'}}s equivalent of it. Touching a Superball Flower also earns the player 1,000 [[point]]s.

==In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''==
The Superball Flower is an unlockable item for editing mode. Using it changes Mario's appearance to be monochromatic, just like his sprites from ''Super Mario Land''.

The item can be unlocked in Story Mode. Here, when [[Toad (species)|Purple Toad]] uses the [[Spiny Shell]], which can be obtained by clearing [[Spiny Shell Smashers]], to break the Hard Blocks, Mario can hit the formerly obstructed [[? Block]].
