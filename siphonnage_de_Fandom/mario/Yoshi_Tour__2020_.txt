'''Yoshi Tour''' was the fiteenth Tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']]. The Tour introduced an easter-themed variant of Yoshi in a half snatched egg along with his kart [[Egg 1]] that returns from [[Mario Kart DS]] en [[Mario Kart 7]]. It also introduce similair-looking gliders, being the [[eggshell glider]] and [[Bright Glider]].

This tour featered [[Yoshi Circuit|''GCN Yoshi Circuit'']] and a Yellow Birdo as a new driver. It was also the first Tour not to introduce new Tracks besides R/T variants for [[RMX Mario Circuit 1]] and [[Donut Plains 1 (track)|SNES Donut Plains 1]].
