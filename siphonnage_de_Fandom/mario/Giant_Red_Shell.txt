'''Giant Red Shells''' are a recurring object in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
Aside from being a larger size, Giant Red Shells are the same as a [[Red Shell]]. Giant Red Shells have a [[Green Shell]] counterpart known as a [[Giant Green Shell]], which they function very similarly to. 

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''===
Giant Red Shells are worn by red [[Gargantua Koopa Troopa]]s and red [[Colossal Koopa Paratroopa]]s, both of which are only encountered in [[Giant Land]]. Like regular [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can [[jump]] on one of these enemies to have it retreat into its shell. Like Red Shells, Giant Red Shells can be picked up and [[kick]]ed.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' / ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' / ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
Giant Red Shells retain their functionality from ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', but they can now break through [[Hard Block]]s without bouncing off.

===''[[Super Mario Maker]]''/ ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''===
A Giant Red Shell is once again worn by each red Gargantua Koopa Troopa, which can be created by giving a [[Super Mushroom]] to a [[Red Koopa Troopa]]. Unlike earlier ''Super Mario'' installments, Giant Red Shells cannot be picked up and thrown.

==In {{series|Mario Strikers}}==
Giant Red Shells, along with Red Shells, appear as an item in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'' and ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. A Giant Red Shell essentially functions the same as a Red Shell, but with the added advantage of being larger. Upon being used, a Giant Red Shell homes into an opponent, squishing and stunning them temporarily, after which the Giant Red Shell continues to roam around temporarily before disappearing.
