'''Pillars''' are large, wide indestructible columns featured in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==In ''[[Super Mario World]]''/''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''==
Pillars only appear in three levels, each taking place in either a [[castle]] or a [[fortress]]: [[Iggy's Castle|#1 Iggy's Castle]], the [[Forest Fortress]], and the [[Front Door]] of [[Bowser's Castle]]. A Pillar is not seen at first, as it hides within either the floors, ceilings, or walls. As [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] approaches a Pillar, it sticks out slightly before quickly extending down. After this, the Pillar retracts into the surface it is in before repeating the cycle. If Mario or Luigi touch a Pillar, he takes damage.
