The '''Record''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Record can only be obtained in [[Boo's Mansion]] after agreeing to play a game with a bunch of [[Boo]]s. One of the Boos will have the Record and [[Mario]] must hammer or jump on the one that has it. The Record can then be used on the phonograph to play a jazzy arrangement of [[Ground Theme]] to distract the Boo that is guarding it. The player has to time the music to play it perfectly and get the Boo to come over and dance. After the Boo is distracted, Mario can then get the [[Weight (Paper Mario)|Weight]] in the chest. After doing so, the Boo will disappear fearing [[Lady Bow]]'s anger. The phonograph can still be played afterwards.
