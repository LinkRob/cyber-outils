'''Chaos Kamek''', named '''Switch-All Kamek''' in PAL releases, is an item in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
Chaos Kamek is the more powerful variant of the [[Crazy Kamek]] item. When used, [[Kamek]] appears and switches all characters' positions with each other.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
Chaos Kamek can be obtained in the [[Perilous Palace Path]] board, either from [[Yellow Toad]] giving it out to characters as they approach the [[finish line]] or from winning first place in a minigame and selecting the item as a reward.

==Flavor text==
*Description: ''Switch everyone's places.''
