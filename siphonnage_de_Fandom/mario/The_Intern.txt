'''The Intern''' is one of [[Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. 

==Gameplay==
The object is to straighten the stack of papers or books. To accomplish this, the player has to hit the [[Wii Remote]] against their palm, although at a certain speed, because doing it too quickly or unevenly does not straighten the paper or books and results in even more work. Once the objective is fulfilled, the player wins, and the papers or books are put in a file or bookshelf respectively. If this objective is not fulfilled, the player loses at the microgame.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels require the Wii Remote to be hit two, three, and four times, respectively.
