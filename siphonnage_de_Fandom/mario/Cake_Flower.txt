'''Cake Flower''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'', being part of [[Kat and Ana]]'s set used for the level The Grand Parfait Adventure. It uses the Hand Model form for its gameplay.

== Layout ==
The protagonist is a man who stands close to the camera, facing the middle of the room where a woman wears a cake costume with varying amounts of candles, strawberries, and flowers.

== Gameplay ==
During the first phase of the microgame, the woman spins so players can count the number of candles, strawberries, and flowers on her costume. (She takes around seven beats to make a full rotation.) During the second phase, the player will be asked for the number of a certain element on the cake (candles, strawberries, or flowers), which they must give by holding the appropriate number of fingers up; the man holds up the same number on his hand. (The maximum number of the correct type of element on the cake is five.) If the player gives the correct number, they win the microgame and the characters celebrate. The player will be defeated if they give an incorrect number, in which case the woman cries.

=== Difficulty Levels ===

# '''Level 1 (Easy)''': The cake has just one layer, with either only flowers, only strawberries, or only candles.
# '''Level 2 (Hard)''': The cake has two layers, with two of the countable elements. The player will be asked for the quantity of one element.
# '''Level 3 (Expert)''': The cake has three layers, with the three countable elements all being present. The player will be asked for the quantity of one element.

== Nomenclature ==
In the Japanese version, this microgame's name is ''Kēki Toppingu'' (written ケーキトッピング), from English "cake topping." The Korean name is ''Keikeu Toping'' (written 케이크 토핑), from the same source. Spanish translates the name as ''Decoración de Pastel'', literally "decoration about/of cake." In French, the name is ''C'est du Gasteau''. (This is a phrase that is translated as "It's a piece of cake.") Chinese gives the name as ''Dàngāo Zhuāngshì'' (written 蛋糕装饰), which means "cake decorations."
