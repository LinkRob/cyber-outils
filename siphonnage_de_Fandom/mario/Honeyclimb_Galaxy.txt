The '''Honeyclimb Galaxy''' is a galaxy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It is located in the [[Bedroom]]. This galaxy tests [[Mario]]'s skill with the [[Bee Mushroom]]. It only contains a single [[Power Star]], and its icon portrays all three honeycomb walls.

== Missions ==
=== Scaling the Sticky Wall ===
The aim of the mission is to climb up the walls as [[Bee Mario]]. Several [[Mandibug|Mandibugs]], a rain of meteors, and moving honeycomb platforms will be in the way on the climb up. The Power Star is on the very top of the last of the three honeycomb wall planets.
