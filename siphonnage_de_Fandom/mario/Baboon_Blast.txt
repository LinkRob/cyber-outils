'''Baboon Blast''' ''('''Barrelum Perilous''')'' is a special technique used by [[Donkey Kong]] in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. It can be obtained from [[Cranky Kong]] at [[Jungle Japes]] for three [[Banana Medal]]s.

==History==
Like the other special moves taught by Cranky, Baboon Blast is an essential move. It is mainly used to help Donkey Kong unlock certain areas. In order to use Baboon Blast, he must find a [[Cranky's Kong Pad|Donkey Pad]]. By using Baboon Blast on it, Donkey Kong blasts off into the sky and must shoot himself between [[Barrel Cannon]]s and [[Blast Barrel]]s in order to reach the end. A [[Golden Banana]] or a [[Bonus Barrel]] often appears at the end, but in some cases, Donkey Kong goes through a [[DK Star]] that unlocks a certain event, depending on the location.

==Trivia==
*For the majority of the level, the theme song for the Baboon Blast courses is a different version of the main level theme it's taking place on. However, this isn't the case for [[Frantic Factory]] where it's main theme remains the same during the Baboon Blast course there.
