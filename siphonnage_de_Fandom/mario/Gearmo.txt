A '''Gearmo''' is a robotic creature that appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

== In {{series|Super Mario}} ==
=== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' ===
Gearmos usually appear on planets with a large amount of trash. In ''Super Mario Galaxy'', they mostly inhabit the [[Toy Time Galaxy]], the first mission of the galaxy centering around rescuing a number of them. Two specific Gearmos each have a job for [[Mario]]. In these jobs Mario throws [[Bob-omb]]s at garbage, thus cleaning it up. If Mario does this for both Gearmos he can acquire a [[Power Star]] from each. A Gearmo can also be found in the Engine Room of the [[Comet Observatory]].

=== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' ===
Similarly in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', Mario is also put in charge of burning up trash with Bob-ombs or flames as [[Fire Mario]] within 20 or 30 seconds. Gearmos are made as original type, or they can be gold or silver. The gold and silver Gearmos let Mario know that they want a certain enemy type brought to them, and the Gearmo will reward him with a Power Star. Gearmos are brought to [[Starship Mario]] when Mario does a job for them in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.

== Notable members ==
* [[Garbage Gearmo]]: He takes care of the [[Battlerock]]'s/[[Dreadnought]]'s Ammunition Depot in the [[Battlerock Galaxy|Battlerock]]/[[Dreadnought Galaxy]] and serves as a host to the [[Bob-omb Blasting]] mini-game.
* [[Gold Gearmo]]: He has a softness for [[Goomba]]s, and has set up his own Goomba-trap in the [[Boulder Bowl Galaxy]].
* [[Silver Gearmo]]: He has an affection for [[Topman|Topmen]], and has set up his on Topman-trap in the [[Space Storm Galaxy]].
* [[Purple Comet Host (Battlerock Galaxy)]]: [[Purple Comet]]s are her thing, and she will reward him who grabs 100 [[Purple Coin]]s.
* [[Purple Comet Host (Dreadnought Galaxy)]]: He has a penchant for Purple Comets like his sister does. Completing the [[Purple Coin]] challenge really completes him. Getting no Purple Coins gets on his nerves.
*Crazy Gearmo: This Gearmo appears in the [[Chompworks Galaxy]], where he gets mowed down by rolling [[Chain Chomp]]s, and his sibling is getting irritated by him doing this, asking Mario, ''"What's he doing?"'' A second one appears in Spring Into the Chompworks getting ran over by the [[Chomp Pup]] that pop out of another pipe.
