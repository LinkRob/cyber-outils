'''Social Climbers''' is an unlockable Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The minigame's title refers to a people that is distressed to gain a higher social status.

== Opening ==
The player waves, turns around and grips onto the pole. The screen started to split into four

== Gameplay ==
The players must continuously climb until they reach the top. The player who reach the top first wins.

== Ending ==
The winner's screen becomes wide and the winner remains standing on top. The clouds and balloons are in the background.

==How to Play==
*{{button|ns|joy-con}} + {{button|ns|L}}/{{button|ns|R}} – Climb Up

==In-game description==
*''"Climb to the top!"''
*''"Swing {{button|ns|joy-con-l}} down while pressing {{button|ns|L}}/{{button|ns|R}} and then release while swinging {{button|ns|joy-con-l}} back up, until you reach the top!"''
