{{Redirect|Kamek|the character|[[Kamek]]|the movie villain|[[Kamek (Illumination)]]}}{{Infobox Item|name=Kamek Crystal Ball|first=''[[Mario Kart World]]'' (2025)|effects=Summons [[Kamek]], who spawnsenemies or turns racers into obstacles/NPCs.|games=''[[Mario Kart World]]''|image=MKW Kamek Roulette.png}}'''Kamek''' is an item from [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']].

== Description ==
The Kamek item appears as a purple crystal ball. The stands are gold and resemble the face of a [[Magikoopa]] (like [[Kamek]] himself), and spell symbols swirl inside.

== ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' ==
When the player uses Kamek, the character [[Kamek]] will use his magic to summon many of a certain type of enemy and/or turn other racers into enemies.

Some enemies, like [[Nabbit]], cannot be summoned on the track, but can still be what the victims are turned into. Likewise, there are other enemies, like [[Chain Chomp|Chain Chomps]], that can be summoned on the track, though racers cannot turn into them. Occasionally, non-enemy species can be chosen as what the racers become, though these species cannot be summoned on the track; an example is [[Pianta]].
