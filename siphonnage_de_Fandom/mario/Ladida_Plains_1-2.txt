'''Ladida Plains 1-2 '''is the second level of [[Ladida Plains]] of ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. The level starts with an orange plain field with a Koopa Troopa and multiples of Goombas. The song also starts to change.

==Toads==
*'''Toad 1''': Jump on the pink block platform and use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] to go up to the left.
*'''Toad 2''': Fall of the bumpy orange block platform next tot the brown platform.
*'''Toad 3''': Attack the [[Paratroopa|Koopa Pararatroopa]] next to the [[! Block]].

==Puzzle Piece==
* Patiently stay on the blue falling platform, then move left.
