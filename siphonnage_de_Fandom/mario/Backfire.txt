'''Backfire''' is a special move used only by [[Stumpet]]s in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
When using Backfire, the Stumpets will launch small fireballs at [[Mario]]'s party for moderate damage.
