The '''Lucky Ribbon''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Equipping it grants the [[Lucky Attack]] effect, similar to the [[Lucky Bros.]].

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Lucky Ribbon is dropped by [[Hermie III]] upon being defeated. Equipping it to either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] increases his [[Bros. Point|BP]] rating by 22. In the Japanese version, the Lucky Ribbon is instead dropped by [[Birdo]] upon being defeated, and it instead increases Mario or Luigi's [[Power|POW]] rating by 45.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Lucky Ribbon is more closely based on its appearance in the original game's Japanese version, as it is again dropped by Birdo and increases its user's POW rating, which has been slightly increased to 52. In addition, the Lucky Ribbon can be sold for only three [[coin]]s.
