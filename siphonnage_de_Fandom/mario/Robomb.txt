A '''Robomb''' is an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Robombs are spherical, red explosives with retractable arms and legs.

===Behavior===
At first, a Robomb is dormant, but if [[Luigi]] uses the [[Strobulb]] on a Robomb, it grows out its arms and legs and runs after Luigi, exploding a few seconds later. Some Robombs malfunction, which is indicated by a different startup sound, red electric sparks around them, and their behavior. Malfunctioning Robombs first run into place before charging into Luigi. 

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
Robombs are first encountered in the [[Old Clockworks]]. If Luigi is hit by a Robomb, he takes 20 [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage, although the explosion can stun any [[ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] caught in it. Some [[Greenie]]s use Robombs to hide from the Strobulb flashes.

Robombs also have a golden variant, [[Strobomb|Gold Robombs]], which, like other gold enemies, releases some [[treasure]] when destroyed.
