'''World 4-Fortress''' is a level from [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4]] which appears in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Overview==
This is a secret exit, which takes the player to [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]].

==Walkthrough==
This tower begins with [[Conveyor Belt]]s that causes four [[Iron Block]]s to fall, allowing the player to progress. A [[Dry Bones]] and a [[? Block]] are found on the right side of the area. A [[Warp Pipe]] is at the top of the area, with two Dry Bones with ? Blocks on either side. The next area contains a pipe that creates Iron Blocks, which are used to reach the platform above. More Conveyor Belts follow, with a large group of [[coin]]s above one. The [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] follows some Conveyor Belts. Following this is a [[Red Ring]], as well as [[Propeller Block]]s and [[lift]]s that allow the player to reach the top of the area. Dry Bones are found on platforms next to the walls.

The next area contains Conveyor Belts, as well as more Iron Blocks that act as obstacles as the player climbs the area. At the top of the area, the pipe that creates the Iron Blocks is found, along with a Warp Pipe. There is a hidden section behind a wall to the left of the area containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]], which can only be accessed by [[Mini Mario]]. Through the Warp Pipe, Conveyor Belts drop a large amount of Iron Blocks, which act as platforms to reach the top of the area. The boss door is found at the top, with coins and a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[Fire Flower]] in the wall to the left.

[[Wendy O. Koopa]] is fought here, at the end. Wendy attacks Mario by shooting [[Candy ring|rings]] that bounce off the walls (they eventually dissolve) out of her wand and jumping. She spins around in her shell attempting to hit the player when hit. After being hit the third time, Wendy is defeated.
==Enemies==

* [[Dry Bones]] - 14
* [[Wendy O. Koopa]] (boss) 

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is at the start of the second area, floating high-up. Mario and co. should get on top of an incoming Iron Block and jump from it to reach the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Directly below the midway point is a hidden area that conceals the second Star Coin. It can be reached with the conveyor belts.
*'''Star Coin 3''': When entering the area with Wendy's door, the third Star Coin is floating to the high-left. To get it, Mario and co. must jump onto every Iron Block as it falls down, working up to the Star Coin. Once the second-last one falls, Mario and co. should hurry and grab the Star Coin before the last Iron Block falls.

== Secret Exit ==
*In the last room, there are many Iron Blocks which are about to drop.  The player should dash for the left wall before the [[Iron Block]]s fall. They should then jump into a hidden alcove in the wall with a [[Warp Pipe]]. The pipe leads to the tower's rooftop which contains the red flagpole. This exit takes the player to the {{world|4|cannon}} Warp Cannon, which takes the player to [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]].
