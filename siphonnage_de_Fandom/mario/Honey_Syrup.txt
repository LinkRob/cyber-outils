'''Honey Syrup''' is an item which first appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' then in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

== Appearances ==
=== ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' ===
In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'', Honey Syrup is a basic item that will restore [[Flower Point|FP]]. Anyone in [[Mario]]'s party can use a Honey Syrup, although the amount of FP it replenishes for its user is determined by their level and Flower Points. Honey Syrups can be bought for ten [[coin]]s apiece in the item shops in [[Toad Town]] and [[Rose Town]] as well as the food store in [[Seaside Town]].

=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Honey Syrup restores five of Mario's FP. This item is very basic and has better versions in [[Maple Syrup]] and [[Jammin' Jelly]]. This item can be purchased from the shop in northern [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] for a small price around five coins, although the price varies per location. Honey Syrup is used to make [[Honey Shroom]]s.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Honey Syrup retains its effects and properties as its ''Paper Mario'' counterpart. The game also adds new recipes that require the Honey Syrup and its recipes to be used. The Honey Syrup is purchased from the [[Toad Bros. Bazaar]] in [[Rogueport]].

=== ''Super Paper Mario'' ===
{{Main|Honey Jar}}
''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' doesn't actually have the Honey Syrup, which has been replaced with [[Honey Jar]], an item that gives Mario ten [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (status effect)|poison]]. The Honey Jar is lesser effective that the Honey Syrup.
