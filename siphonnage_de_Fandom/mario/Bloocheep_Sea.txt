'''Bloocheep Sea''' is one of the basketball courts in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. It is the second court played during the [[Rainbow Tourney]].

==Description==
[[File:Bloocheep Sea icon.png|thumb|left|200px|Preview icon]]
Bloocheep Sea takes place underwater, and as such, the characters move around slower while playing here. The court plays an arrangement of the [[Underwater BGM (Super Mario Bros.)|Underwater BGM]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', and includes 8-bit [[Blooper]]s from said game that slowly move their way around the court. If the ball hits a Blooper, it flies off at an angle relevant to how it hit the Blooper. Each basketball hoop has an orange anchor as a backboard. There is a [[Sushi]] [[swim]]ming back and forth in the background, but it has no effect on gameplay. Bloocheep Sea is the only court that has the [[Mimic (treasure chest)|Mimic]] item.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The first word, "Bloocheep," is an etymology of "[[Blooper]]" and "[[Cheep Cheep]]."

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'', level 2 of the minigame [[Harmony Hustle]] contains a piece of music, titled "Bloocheep Ocean". Despite different names, the music on the basketball court is the same as the music in the minigame.
