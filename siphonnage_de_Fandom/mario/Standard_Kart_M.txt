'''Standard Kart M''' is a [[kart]] from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. As it name says, it is a standard kart for medium weighted characters. The normal staff ghosts for [[Moonview Highway]] ([[Princess Peach]], Nin*KOZ*) and [[Bowser's Castle]] ([[Bowser Jr.]], Nin*GASK2) use this kart.

==Characteristics ==
As for all karts, this kart has good points and bad ones. The majority of its stats are evened out (though not very impressive) and it excels in speed, but it has generally bad off-road ability.
