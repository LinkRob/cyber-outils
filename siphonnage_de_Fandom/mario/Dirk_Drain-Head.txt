'''Dirk Drain-Head''' is a comic book superhero who appears in various ''[[Nintendo Comics System]]'' stories. He is [[Mario]]'s favorite comic book star. Dirk Drain-Head has the appearance of a stereotypical superhero, and appears to carry around a plunger as his weapon of choice. Dirk Drain-Head resembles [[Luigi]], although mainly because of his mustache.

Dirk Drain-Head is first mentioned in the story "[[A Mouser in the Houser]]," but is first physically shown in the story "[[The Adventures of Dirk Drain-Head]]," featured around Dirk Drain-Head and Mario's obsession with him.
