The '''Giant Cannonball''' is an obstacle which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It is the same as a [[cannonball]] but is larger in size. Giant Cannonballs make a reappearance in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.
