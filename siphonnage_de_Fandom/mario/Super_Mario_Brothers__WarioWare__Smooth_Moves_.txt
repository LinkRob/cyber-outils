'''Super Mario Brothers''' is a [[microgame]] which appears in [[9-Volt]] & [[18-Volt]]'s stage in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. The microgame is based on ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', directly reusing assets from said game except upscaled, but does not spell "Bros." in shortened form.

== Layout ==
The microgame takes place in an overworld [[level]] in ''Super Mario Bros.'', and the display is more zoomed in than the regular game. The HUD is intact except for the time limit. Both the score and number of [[Coin]]s start at zero. According to the display, the level takes place in [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]]. Collecting a Coin adds to the Coin counter, and each Coin is worth 200 [[point]]s, just like in the actual ''Super Mario Bros.'', although this is only for aesthetic. Whenever [[Mario]] [[jump]]s, he holds up a [[Wii Remote]] while doing so.

In Level 1, Mario stands underneath a [[Coin Block]] resembling a [[Brick Block]]. Levels 2 and 3 feature a series of Brick Blocks and [[Question Block]]s, and the way in which they are laid out is not seen in the original game. Level 3 features the same layout as Level 2 but with an added [[Goomba]] or [[pit]] at the start.

== Gameplay ==
The goal is for Mario to collect at least two Coins from a [[Block]]. The player can have Mario jump by swinging up the Wii Remote. If Mario does not collect at least two Coins, the player fails at the microgame.

In Level 1, Mario, in his [[Super Mario (form)|Super]] form, stands in a fixed position, and he has to jump least twice to obtain Coins from the Coin Block. Up to six Coins can be obtained from it before it becomes an [[Empty Block]].

In Level 2, Mario remains in his Super form, but now moves automatically as he moves by the Brick Blocks and Question Blocks. Some of the Brick Blocks contain Coins.

Level 3 is mostly the same as Level 2, but Mario is now in his [[Small Mario|Small]] form and has to avoid either a Goomba or a pit at the start.
