'''Floating Bandineros''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Floating Bandineros are a variant of [[Bandinero]]s sporting the same base appearance, except Floating Bandineros have a light blue body and a dark bandana wrapped around the top of their head. Floating Bandineros also have yellow eyes, which can be seen on their bandana.

===Behavior===
On the ground, Floating Bandinero act the same as regular Bandineros. A Floating Bandinero has the ability to inflate itself to float in the sky, where they can obstruct [[Wario]]'s path. A Floating Bandinero can make itself temporarily invincible by charging itself with electricity.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
A Floating Bandinero has a threat rating of one star. Wario can send a Floating Bandinero to the ground by jumping on it, or he can defeat a Floating Bandinero by jumping at it after swinging on a pole.
