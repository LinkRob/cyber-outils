'''Where's Wario''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', as part of [[Wario-Man]]'s [[Super Zero]] stage, and later returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Wario Deluxe]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
Similarly to [[Stylus Hunt]], the microgame is double-length and requires the player to rotate a 3D model. The goal is to roll the globe to find a picture/caricature of Wario. If done successfully, they win, but if the task is not accomplished on time, the player loses.

===Difficulty levels===
====''WarioWare: Touched!''====
*'''Level 1:''' Wario's picture is big.
*'''Level 2:''' Wario's picture is small.
*'''Level 3:''' The player has to find a small ''[[Mario Bros. (game)|Mario Bros.]]''-style Wario amongst sprites of [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Shellcreeper]]s from ''Mario Bros.''

====''WarioWare Gold''====
*'''Level 1:''' Same as in ''WarioWare: Touched!'', but winning causes the background to turn pink with starry patterns.
*'''Level 2:''' Same as in ''WarioWare: Touched!'', but there are also [[Mushroom]]s and [[Fire Flower]]s decorated with Wario's nose and mouth on the globe.
*'''Level 3:''' A handsome-like caricature of Wario must be found amongst pictures of Mushrooms and Fire Flowers with faces on the globe. If the player wins, the background sparkles.

==Trivia==
*The name is a pun on the famous spotting book series ''{{w|Where's Wally?|Where's Waldo?}}''.
