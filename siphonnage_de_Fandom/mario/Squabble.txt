A '''Squabble''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They are seagulls that attack [[Wario]] by swooping down and biting him.

==History==
Squabbles can only be found on the [[S.S. Caviar]] where they lurk in the skies ready to swoop down onto Wario. They can be taken out by any attacks, but is recommended to take them out using [[Cosmic Wario]]'s lasers.
