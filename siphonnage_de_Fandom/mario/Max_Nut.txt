A '''Max Nut''' is a healing item from the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]]. They restore [[Heart Point|HP]] to both [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] at the same time, more than the other [[Nut]] items. Max Nuts first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. The only ''Mario & Luigi'' game in which Max Nuts do not appear is ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
Max Nuts heal all of Mario and Luigi's health.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''===
Max Nuts heal only up to 160 HP for both Mario and Luigi instead of always healing all HP in the previous game. In the ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' remake, this was changed to always heal all of Mario and Luigi's HP.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''===
Max Nuts heal only up to 80 HP, half of what it was in in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''===
Max Nuts act the same as in ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''.
