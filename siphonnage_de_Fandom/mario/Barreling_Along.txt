'''Barreling Along''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The players slowly roll the [[barrel]] while they are standing on top of the barrel.

== Gameplay ==
Players must tilt the Joy-Con to roll the barrel. Tall grass and puddles slows down the player while [[Dash Panel]]s speeds the player. The first player who crosses the finish line wins the minigame. The last player sulks since he/she did not reach the finish line. Their time is also recorded in this minigame.

== Ending ==
The winner does her/his victory animation on top of the barrel while other players sulk and are not standing on their barrels. The barrels are either lying on the ground or standing.
