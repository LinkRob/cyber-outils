The '''Junior Koopa Clown Car''' is a miniature version of [[Bowser]]'s [[Koopa Clown Car]] commonly used by [[Bowser Jr.]] as is transportation, but smaller. It first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Description==
Like the Koopa Clown Car, the Junior Clown Car is a helicopter-like vehicle with a smiling clown face on the front, hence the name. It has the ability to change its expression, but with a smaller smile. The Junior Clown Car's rim and propeller are colored the same shade of green as Bowser Jr.'s head.

Compared to the Koopa Clown Car, the Junior Clown Car is shown to be more versatile, especially in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, boasting more weapons and functions in its arsenal, including boxing glove-covered mechanical arms, wheels and thrusters, and built-in [[Grinder]]s, drills and wrecking balls. The Junior Clown Car's facial expressions are slightly different from the original's, since it cannot shed tears when it is sad, and it has a more expressive mouth.
