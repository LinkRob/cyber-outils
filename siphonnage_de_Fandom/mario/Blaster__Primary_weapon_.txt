'''Blaster''' is a type of [[List of weapons in Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|primary weapon]] used by [[Mario]] and [[Rabbid Peach]].

== Description ==
All Blasters are all-around weapons with average power and range. The Blaster resembles a hand cannon.
