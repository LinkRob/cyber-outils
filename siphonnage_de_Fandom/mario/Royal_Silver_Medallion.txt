The '''Royal Silver Medallion''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Royal Silver Medallion, as it's name states, is a medallion that is silver in color. According to [[Wario]], it has an image of the king's face on it.

==History==
The Royal Silver Medallion can be found inside [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid]]. After being disrespected by Wario, the [[Royal gatekeeper]] forces Wario to retrieve the Royal Silver Medallion along with the [[Royal Gold Medallion]] in order to see [[Poobah the Pharaoh]] himself. The Royal Silver Medallion is located in a dark room with spikes in it inside of a purple treasure chest.
