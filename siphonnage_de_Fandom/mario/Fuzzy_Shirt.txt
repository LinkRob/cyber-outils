'''Fuzzy Shirt''' is a type of armor used by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from a shop in [[Nimbus Land]] for 70 [[coin]]s. Equipping it increases Mario's [[Defense]] by 36 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 18.
