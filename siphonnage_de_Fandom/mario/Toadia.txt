'''Toadia''' is a character in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Profile==
Toadia is a female [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who lives in [[Poshley Heights]]. She is a huge fan of [[Luigi]].

==''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Toadia can be found standing near the fountain in Poshley Heights talking about Luigi. Toadia has a [[Trouble Center|trouble]] that involves meeting up with Luigi in person. [[Mario]] can do this by getting the [[L Emblem]] and wear it and talk to Toadia. Toadia will become excited to see Luigi but when she sees another Luigi (the real Luigi), she think he is an impostor and yells him to leave. Toadia will then give "Luigi" (Mario) a [[Choco Cake]].

When [[Bowser]] and [[Kammy Koopa|Kammy]] arrive at Poshley Heights and talks to Toadia, she gets very scared.

==Trivia==
*Toadia is the second character in the {{series|Paper Mario}} who is a big fan of Luigi, the first being [[Koopa Koot]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
