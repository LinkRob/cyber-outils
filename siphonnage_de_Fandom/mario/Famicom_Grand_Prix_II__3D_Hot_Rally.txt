{{nihongo|'''''Famicom Grand Prix II: 3D Hot Rally'''''|ファミコングランプリII　スリーディーホットラリー}} is a racing game for the [[Family Computer Disk System]]. It was released exclusively in Japan on April 14, 1988. The game serves as the sequel to ''[[Famicom Grand Prix: F1 Race]]'' but is significantly different in gameplay, being closer to ''[[w:c:nintendo:Rad Racer|Rad Racer]]''. ''Famicom Grand Prix II: 3D Hot Rally'' is one of eight games to be compatible with the [[Famicom 3D System]].

''Famicom Grand Prix II: 3D Hot Rally'' had little of an impact on the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], although it is the first appearance of the [[Monster (vehicle)|Monster]] vehicle, which the [[Tiny Titan]] [[kart]] from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' is designed after, and the [[Monster (tires)|Monster]] tires introduced in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' are also based on that of Monster's.
