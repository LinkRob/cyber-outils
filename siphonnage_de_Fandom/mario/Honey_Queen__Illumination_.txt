The '''Honey Queen''' is a supporting character in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''.

==Description==
Honey Queen wears a brown bonnet that covers her whole head except for her eyes, mouth and nose (the latter of which is purple). She has long eyelashes, black beady eyes with pink eyeshadow, and plump, pink lips. On top of her head, she wears a crown bejeweled with blue gems all around.

==Origins==
The Honey Queen made her debut in the 2007 Wii game ''Super Mario Galaxy'', going by the name 'Queen Bee'. She is the ruler of the Honeyhive Galaxy, and aids Mario and Luigi in some of the galaxy's missions that take place there. She also appears in its sequel, ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' and serves a similar role.

She has subsequently appeared in other ''Mario'' series spinoff games, often as a minor character.
