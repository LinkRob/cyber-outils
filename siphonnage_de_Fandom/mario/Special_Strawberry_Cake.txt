'''Special Strawberry Cake''' is an item made in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. The cake is made by [[Princess Peach]] and [[Twink]] during the interlude between Chapter 4 and Chapter 5.

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
{{Spoiler}}
After talking with [[Gourmet Guy]], the princess learns of a kitchen on the first floor. Gourmet Guy, who was going to report Princess Peach to [[Bowser]], promises that if Peach makes him something tasty that he will not report her. Once the cake is made, Princess Peach needs to bring it to Gourmet Guy.

Once the cake is given to Gourmet Guy, he thanks her and then indulges in the cake. If the cake is made incorrectly, Gourmet Guy will spit it out and tell Peach to go back to the kitchen to make another cake. Once the cake is made correctly, Gourmet Guy exclaims it's extremely delicious and to show his gratitude he tells Peach a secret regarding one of the [[Star Spirit]]s being that it's guarded by the [[Lava Piranha]] in [[Mt. Lavalava]] on [[Lavalava Island]].

{{Endspoiler}}
