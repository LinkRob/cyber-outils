The '''Roast Shroom Dish''' is a [[recipe]] which first appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. In the former game, it is named '''Shroom Roast'''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
[[File:ShroomRoast.png|frame|left]]
Eating a Shroom Roast restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 5 [[Flower Point|FP]]. The only way to make a Shroom Roast is by cooking either a [[Slow Shroom]] or a [[Life Shroom]].

{{br|left}}
==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
It is made by [[Saffron]] from a [[Super Shroom Shake]] or a [[Life Shroom]]. This meal restores 30 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (status effect)|poison]].

When mixed with a [[Fruity Hamburger]], the [[Dyllis Special]] is created. It would be recommended to use a Life Shroom to make the Roast Shroom Dish rather than the Super Shroom Shake, if the player wants to spend less money. The Life Shroom costs fifty [[coin]]s in [[Yold Town]], whereas the Super Shroom Shake costs 80 Coins when purchased from [[Notso]]'s shop in [[Flopside]].
