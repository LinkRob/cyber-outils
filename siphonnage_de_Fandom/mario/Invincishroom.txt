An '''Invincishroom''' is an item that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its remake, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==
In the original ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Invincishroom resembles a [[Poison Mushroom]], although these items require both a [[1-Up Mushroom]] and a [[Star]] for their creation. In the remake, the actual Invincishroom was changed to look very similar to the [[Invincibility Mushroom]] from ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''.

==History==
[[File:Invincishroom.PNG|thumb|x220px|left|Three of the Captains with the new Invincishroom and a Poison Mushroom in Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]
The Invincishroom can only be obtained from the [[Game Arcade]] in [[Little Fungitown]]. It is said to either give the user great powers or to act just like a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. The Invincishroom is a plot item, so it cannot be used in battle. When [[Mario]] tries the Invincishroom, he turns green and gets a rare disease known as [[Bean Fever]]. According to Dr. Toad, Mario gets Bean Fever because he is not used to eating different Mushrooms from different regions. Mario was going to turn into a bean in a few days due to his illness but [[Luigi]] got some [[Crabbie Grass]] for him, saving his brother in the process.

In the remake's ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign, [[Captain Goomba]] and the minions were tasked by a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] in [[Teehee Valley]] to find the Invincishroom. But when they do, they instead give the Toad a [[Poison Mushroom]] because Captain Goomba thought it looked cooler. It's possible that the same Toad was responsible for Mario's Bean Fever by making the Poison Mushroom a prize in the Game Arcade.
