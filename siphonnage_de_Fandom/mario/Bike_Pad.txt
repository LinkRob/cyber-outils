'''Bike Pads''' are an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Description ==
Bike Pads are a pair of yellow slabs used for transportation. When the Mario Bros. alternate their jumps, it propels them forward through the air, similar to that of a bicycle. They first appear in the [[Stardust Fields]], but later appear in [[Bowser's Castle]]. Bike Pads resemble the [[Rotating Block]]s from ''[[Super Mario World]]''.
