'''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' It is based on the [[Nintendo 3DS]] game of the same name, and uses the Ba-KAW form.

==Layout==
The protagonist is a Cucco, and the scene takes place in a farm. Link chases behind the Cucco, using the design he used as a child in the game. Link has a blue shield on his back with the Triforce symbol near the top.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of a [[Cucco]], who has to avoid being caught by child [[Link]]. If the Cucco avoids being caught until the timer expires, the player wins, but if Link catches the Cucco, the player loses. The second and third difficulty levels each increase the number of obstructions as well as Link's speed. The path in Level 3 may sometimes be narrower.
