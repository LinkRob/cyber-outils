'''''WarioWare: Snapped!''''' is a ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' game which served as a launch title for the [[Nintendo DSi]]'s [[DSiWare]] service. It was later reissued to the [[Nintendo 3DS]]'s [[Nintendo eShop]], also on the day of its respective launch.
