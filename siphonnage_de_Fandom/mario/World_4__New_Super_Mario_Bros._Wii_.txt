'''World 4''' is the fourth world of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. It is a beach world and consists of solely a plethora of platforms and an enormous tank of water. Surprisingly, this is the first world to host an [[Airship]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

The [[Penguin Suit]] is of paramount use here, as it will allow [[Mario]] to glide over the water and will allow Mario's swimming to improve dramatically.
