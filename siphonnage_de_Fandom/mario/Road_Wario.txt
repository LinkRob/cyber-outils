'''Road Wario''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', found in [[Wario]]'s Twist League introduction stage as the boss Microgame. It shares its gameplay with [[Driver's Ed (WarioWare: Smooth Moves)]] from ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Differences from [[Driver's Ed (WarioWare: Smooth Moves)|Driver's Ed]]==
All of the differences are aesthetics. Wario now drives the car, which has been changed to his personal [[Wario Car|one]]. Wario's car has no review mirror, so we do not see his smile. The top screen also shows the track's layout, with Wario's nose and moustache marking your position.

==Rules ==
"Steer smoothly so I stay on the road! I can't afford another car!"
