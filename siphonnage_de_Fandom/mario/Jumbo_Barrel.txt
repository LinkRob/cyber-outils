The '''Jumbo Barrel''' is an object appearing primarily in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''. It made its only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' appearance in an issue of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''.

==Description==
The Jumbo Barrel is a [[barrel]] owned by [[Funky Kong]], who allows [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] to use it to travel around any previously visited locations on [[Donkey Kong Island]].

==In other media==
===''[[Super Mario-kun]]''===
[[File:Jumbo Barrel SMkun.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Mario, Yoshi, Donkey Kong, and Diddy Kong riding the Jumbo Barrel]]
In ''[[Super Mario-kun Vol 14]]'', Funky Kong allows not only Donkey Kong and Diddy Kong but also [[Mario]] and [[Yoshi]] to ride the Jumbo Barrel. However, the plane's flight goes awry and turns upside-down, dumping the four characters into the ocean.
