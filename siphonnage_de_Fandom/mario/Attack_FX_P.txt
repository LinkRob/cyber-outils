}}'''Attack FX P''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
As the name may imply, this badge is not a [[:Category:Partner Badges|partner badge]], and thus only affects [[Mario]]. Mario can wear this badge for zero [[Badge Point|BP]], and wearing the badge makes some of his attacks make a loud roar sound.

It is obtainable from the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] later on in the game, around the completion of Chapter 6. It is on sale for the steep price of 100 [[coin]]s and sixty if it is discounted.

In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], the Attack FX P Badge now has a giggle sound effect while the roar sounds were instead given to the [[Attack FX R]] badge which in the original was the badge that provided the cricket sound effects necessary for beating [[Hooktail]], in the remake, this was given to the [[Attack FX G]] badge owing to Hooktail's new weakness being frog sounds. The giggle sounds this badge possesses in the remake were originally seen on the Attack FX G in the original.
