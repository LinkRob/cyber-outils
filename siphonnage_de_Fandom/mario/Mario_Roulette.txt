'''''Mario Roulette''''' is an [[arcade]] medal game both developed and manufactured by [[Konami]] and released on December 13, 1991, exclusively within Japan. Its aesthetic is heavily based on ''[[Super Mario World]]'', the latest {{series|Super Mario}} installment at its time of release, including the sprites, title screen, and logo. The gameplay in particular is based on the [[Bonus game (Super Mario World)|Bonus game]] from ''Super Mario World''.
