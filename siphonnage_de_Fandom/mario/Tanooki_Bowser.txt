A '''Tanooki Bowser''' (also known as a '''Fake Tail Bowser''' or '''Tail Bowser''') is a type of [[Fake Bowser]].

== In ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' ==
In Super Mario 3D Land, 2 Tail Bowsers appears in [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 1]] as a [[Goomba]], in [[World 5 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 5]] as a [[Magikoopa]], and then the real [[Bowser]] appears in [[World 8 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 8]].

== In ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'' ==
A Tail Bowser appears in [[Dream Figure Skating]], it chases the players from a [[castle]] and then when defeated, stops, and then reverses back into a goomba with a super leaf.

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' ==
While never actually in the game itself, it is a [[Battle Card]].
