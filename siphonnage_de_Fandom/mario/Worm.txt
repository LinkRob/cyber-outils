The '''Worm''' is a character in [[The Bird! The Bird!|The Bird, The Bird]] who appears and the end of the episode.

== History ==
after saving [[Cheepy]], [[Cheepy's mother|Cheepys mother]] gives [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] food. When he sees what he thinks is Pasta he happily tells how much he loves it. The Pasta than reveals that he is a Worm much to the surprise of Mario.
