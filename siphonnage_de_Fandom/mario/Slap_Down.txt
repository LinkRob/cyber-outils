'''Slap Down''' is a [[Battle Minigame]] that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.
==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the players must watch the panels on the ground and slap down the hand as soon the picture shows up that matches the larger picture in the middle. Reflexes are key to winning this match and players must be careful not to slap the wrong picture or they will be eliminated from the game. The player that slaps down the correct picture first wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|a}} - Slap

==Trivia==
*This is a mini-game where an active player can actually cheat in. If one repeatedly presses the Start button, the pictures will show up slowly giving the player the chance to tell which picture is right and wrong.
