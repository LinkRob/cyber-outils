'''Jojora's Friends''', are a group of friends called '''Chucklissa''', '''Oholina''', '''Hoohoolia''' and '''Teeheena''' who are mutually friendly characters with the same appearance. They appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
[[Jojora]] asked who do they want to meet, it was one of Jojora's Friends. Whichever name they chose, she called them. Jojora's Friend introduced herself, now starting the battle against [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. Jojora even teamed up with her. After defeated, Jojora's Friend lied down on the ground and melted away.

==Trivia==
*This is the only character in the game to have many names, even when choosing a name.
