'''Mini Yuxes''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Mini-Yuxes act as protectors of [[Yux]]es. during battle. During battle, these enemies must be defeated before [[Mario]] or his partners can damage the Yux. Up to four Mini-Yuxes can be in combat per regular Yux.
