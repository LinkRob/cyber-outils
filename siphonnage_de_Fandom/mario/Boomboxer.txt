A '''Boomboxer''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]].'' It is considered the weakest of the species. Its head, which looks similar to a boombox, shoots out soundwaves that can injure Mario. It also makes a strange face when it attacks. When [[Boomer (Pixl)|Boomer]] is used, any Boomboxer on-screen will be stunned for a while.
