'''Nintendo 64 - Nintendo Classics''', formerly known as '''Nintendo 64 - Nintendo Switch Online''', is an application for the Nintendo Switch and the follow-up to Nintendo Entertainment System - Nintendo Classics and Super Nintendo Entertainment System - Nintendo Classics. It features a library of Nintendo 64 games, some of which support local and online multiplayer, and, alongside Game Boy Advance - Nintendo Classics and a similar application for Sega Genesis games, it is exclusive to active Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack subscribers. It was announced in a Nintendo Direct on September 23, 2021, and was released in the Americas on October 25, 2021, most other regions on October 26, 2021, and in Taiwan on September 9, 2025.

The European versions of the games are available on the North American version of the app, with certain titles available in European French, German, and European Spanish. The iQue Simplified Chinese versions of the games are not available.

For the application on the Nintendo Switch 2, a CRT filter and a rewind function was added, while button remapping was added to both versions. The application also now runs at a resolution of 1080p in handheld mode and 2160p (4K) in TV mode, though games are slightly scaled up in the 1080p resolution to fill the top and bottom edges of the screen.

Games that supported the Rumble Pak on the Nintendo 64 have rumble support in this application, such as ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', unlike the Virtual Console for [[Wii]] or [[Wii U]]. There is, however, no Transfer Pak or Controller Pak support for games that originally supported them.

As of the v4.0.0 update, in-app button remapping is enabled for each game. The menu where this is accessible shows what each button does in each game (such as  being used for items in ''Mario Kart 64''). Button remapping only works when using using two Joy-Con, a Pro Controller, or a Nintendo 64 controller. Button remapping is not available when playing with a singular Joy-Con.
