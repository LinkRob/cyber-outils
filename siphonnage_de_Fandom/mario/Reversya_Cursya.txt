A '''Reversya Cursya''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
Reversya Cursyas is a yellow [[Cursya]] with two eyes and purple hand-like appendages, which all Cursyas have.

=== Behavior ===
Reversya Cursyas is one of the [[Cursya]]s encountered throughout the game. Reversya Cursyas are mainly found in [[Floro Caverns]] and [[Overthere Stair]]. Like with the other Cursyas, Reversya Cursyas inflict a curse whenever they are directly touched. As their name states, Reversya Cursyas inflicts the reverse curse which reverses controls when touched. The curse wears off after time but the best way to avoid being cursed is to take the Reversya Cursya out from a distance.
