'''Cars''' are a recurring [[traffic]] obstacle in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.

==Description==
Cars are the main component of traffic in courses taking place in a city, such as [[Toad's Turnpike]], or courses with a busy road, such as [[Shroom Ridge]]. If a character drives their [[kart]] into a car, they spin out as if they had driven into a [[Banana]]. A car cannot be destroyed, but certain items can send them flying into the air, most commonly a [[Starman|Super Star]] or, as of ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', a [[Bullet Bill]]. The drivers are not visible in most cars, but sound of them make a honking sound upon being hit. [[Bus]]es have a similar function to Cars.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 64]]''===
White-colored cars are featured as the main obstacles of [[Toad's Turnpike]], along with buses. The cars make the same engine sound as [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s kart.

===''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''===
Cars are encountered as obstacles in [[Mushroom Bridge]] and [[Mushroom City]]. Here, the three standard types of cars found are red, yellow and blue, all of which have "POW" as a license plate, and the only difference between all of them is their color. There are also the more hazardous [[Bob-omb Car|Bomb-cars]], and driving into one sends the racer flying upward in an explosion, just as if they were caught in the blast radius of an exploding [[Bob-omb]] item. Cars also have a unique variation, [[Mushroom Car]]s, which can drop a [[Dash Mushroom|Mushroom]] onto the course upon being bumped into but do not spin out the racer, making them the only helpful obstacle in traffic.

===''[[Mario Kart DS]]''===
Cars prominently appear throughout [[Shroom Ridge]] and the returning {{classic course|GCN|Mushroom Bridge}}, but with a more realistic design. The red, yellow and blue cars are based of a 1960s Prince Skyline (the predecessor of the modern Nissan Skyline), but each with minor differences. 

A red car is the focus of Mission 4-3, in which Mario has to race one on Shroom Ridge. The red car moves a lot faster than the other cars.
