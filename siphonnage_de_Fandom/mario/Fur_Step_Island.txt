'''Fur Step Island''' is a location in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It mean is the first island when [[Mario]] is walking around a grassy field in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] when a puddle of black [[Goop]] is formed from the logo of [[Shadow Mario]], also made of black Goop, which then grows. Mario falls in, and lands in a rainy Lake Lapcat, falling head-first into a large footprint likely left by [[Bowser]].

You need 100 [[Cat Shine|Cat Shines]] and unlocked all 3 islands to unlock this island after beated [[Fury Bowser]] (phase 1 and 2) and [[Giant Bowser]]. And Fury Bowser became to Eternal (phase 3) with his's white hair, but if you beated him, he still Giant Bowser{{SM3DWNav}}
