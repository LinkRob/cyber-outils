'''Laser Jigglefish''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Laser Jigglefish are floating jellyfish-like creatures with a red eye hidden inside of their body.

===Behavior===
When a Laser Jigglefish spots [[Wario]], it shoots a laser from its eyeball-like undersides and slowly turns over until being upside-down. After a while, the Laser Jigglefish gets back up.

==In ''Wario World''==
Laser Jigglefish are encountered in [[Pecan Sands]]. Whenever a Laser Jigglefish is flipped over, it is vulnerable to attack. To stun a Laser Jigglefish, Wario has to [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] its soft underside before it flips back over. He can then defeat it using a [[Mad Move]].
