'''Pix Fix''' is a 1-vs.-Rivals minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. It involves having to rotate panels of a scene depicting a location from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' so that it is facing correctly. Whichever side is the first to complete their scene is the winner.

The solo player has to flip 6 panels, and a team of two must fix 9 panels, a team of three must fix 12 panels.
