'''Sneakers''' are ghosts that appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Sneakers are purplish pink ghosts. They also usually have a smug-like facial expression.

There is also a stronger form of the Sneaker, the [[Strong Sneaker]], which first appear in the Old Clockworks. There is also a larger yellow Sneaker, the [[Terrible Teleporter]], which is one of the bosses found in the [[ScareScraper]].

=== Behavior ===
Sneakers have 25 HP. They have a habit of disappearing during battle and popping behind Luigi scaring him. Sneakers are also very fast. They only attack by pushing Luigi when his guard is down. If Luigi is trying to vacuum up other ghosts at another time, Sneakers will immediately scare Luigi breaking his grip from the other ghosts. It is best for Luigi to capture groups of Sneakers at the same time so he doesn't have to worry about losing his grip. Sneakers are usually found in trios, or appear with other ghosts such as [[Greenie]]s.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
Sneakers appear throughout most missions in the [[Haunted Towers]], [[Old Clockworks]], [[Secret Mine]] and the [[Treacherous Mansion]].
