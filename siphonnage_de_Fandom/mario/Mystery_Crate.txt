A '''Mystery Crate''' is a type of object featured in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. They usually contain a [[Melon]], and a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can open a Mystery Crate by [[Ground Pound]]ing it. It is also possible for Mystery Crates to be pushed around.
