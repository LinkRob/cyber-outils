{{DISPLAYTITLE:List of glitches in ''Mario Kart Wii''}}
This page lists the various glitches in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' for the [[Wii]].

==Everyone as the Same Character Glitch==
*To apply this glitch, enter a multiplayer match. Player 1 chooses a character, presses B, then moves their cursor off of that character. Player 2 does the same. Repeat with Players 3 and 4. Then, Player 1 presses B to return to the mode select screen, then presses A to go back to the character select screen. All of the players' cursors will be on that character.
**Using the "same characters" glitch can break the character riding animations when choosing a vehicle (ex. Daisy still wearing the biker outfit In Standard Kart M) however this will not be pulled into actual gameplay.
**Once you complete any race, on the next racetrack select screen, all players besides Player 1 will have their Kart or Bike invisible.
**Using this glitch, it's possible to put a Mii's head on any character except for Mii at the podium
**Using this glitch, it's possible to get a princess character to keep her biker outfit on the podium

==Courses==
*In ''[[Coconut Mall]]'', if a player runs up against the Elevator wall, they will do a back flip.
*In one of the [[Bowser's Castle]]s, the player lines up to face one of the walls and a ramp. If done correctly, the player will shot through the wall and have an accident.
*In [[Toad's Factory]], if the player does a wheelie before one of the moving ramps, he will rollover and get stuck on his back for a while.
*In [[Mushroom Gorge]], if the player uses a mushroom on the second mushroom near the turning bridge, He will drive up the mountain for a short while, and fall into the hill.
*In Coconut Mall, if the player turns on the upper ramp leading outside of the mall, he will land on the first sign leaving the store.
*In [[DK's Jungle Parkway]], the player can jump ahead to the ramp that will take them across the river by doing a wheelie.
*In [[DK Mountain]], if the player goes in reverse on the blue panels, they will fall into the mountain, and causing them to crash.
*It is possible to crush the [[Lakitu]] using a Thwomp.
*In [[DK Summit]], if the player uses one of the blue panels near where they land after coming out of the cannon to go back, they will fall through one of the snow drifts and crash.
*In [[Grumble Volcano]], if the player uses a boost up to the rock to the left of the start line and goes round it, they can skip the full three laps and land back down at the finish line to finish the race. This is impossible using karts.
