'''Waddle Dees''' are an enemy from the ''Kirby'' franchise. They made their first overall appearance in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby's Dream Land|Kirby's Dream Land]]'', but they have appeared in some of the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} installments.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Waddle Dees are not too differently shaped from [[Kirby]], as they have a round, ball-shaped body, two stubby arms, and oval-shaped feet. Waddle Dees have an orangeish-brown body, round, yellow feet, a light-colored face, and no mouth.

===Behavior===
Waddle Dees are the weakest and most basic of enemies encountered in the ''Kirby'' games. They only move around slowly, and are the ''Kirby'' games' equivalent of [[Goomba]]s in the {{franchise|Mario}}.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''===
A Waddle Dee makes a cameo as a collectible [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]].

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''===
[[File:King Dedede entrance SSBB.png|thumb|200px|''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' screenshot of four Waddle Dees carrying in King Dedede for his stage entrance]]
Four Waddle Dees appear during [[King Dedede]]'s stage entrance, in which they carry him on stage. Waddle Dees are the main feature of King Dedede's side special, [[Waddle Dee Toss]], and his [[Final Smash]], [[Waddle Dee Army]]. For Waddle Dee Toss, King Dedede throws a Waddle Dee at his opponent, and after it hits, the Waddle Dee continues to walk around the stage. For Waddle Dee Army, King Dedede summons an army of Waddle Dees to attack the other fighters.

A Waddle Dee makes a cameo in the form of two [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|stickers]]: one of them depicts its artwork from ''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby 64: The Crystal Shards|Kirby 64: The Crystal Shards]]'', while the other sticker, titled Waddle Dee Ball, shows Waddle Dee's ''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby: Canvas Curse|Kirby: Canvas Curse]]'' artwork, depicting its ball form.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''===
Both Waddle Dees and [[Parasol Waddle Dee]]s appear as enemies in [[Smash Run]]. When a Parasol Waddle Dee drops its [[Parasol (Kirby franchise)|parasol]], it falls to the ground and becomes a Waddle Dee.

==In ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' / ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''==
[[File:Waddle Dee Yoshi.png|thumb|left|200px|The Waddle Dee Yoshi pattern in ''Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World'']]
Waddle Dee Yoshi is one of many unlockable [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] designs. To unlock it, the player has to scan a Waddle Dee [[amiibo]].

==In other media==
===[[Play Nintendo]]===
Waddle Dees are in a few Play Nintendo activities with ''Mario'' content, including [[Spring Break Quiz: Which Nintendo Switch Game Location is best?]] and [[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World Fun Personality Quiz]]. The last two photos of the [[Nintendo Characters Valentine's Picture Gallery]] show Kirby and Waddle Dee paired together.
