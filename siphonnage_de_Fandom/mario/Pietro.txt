'''Pietro''', also called by his fake full name '''Pietro Mario''', is the titular "main antagonist" of [[Fake Bro]].

== History ==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] are busy working on an unknown project. While the Marios are working, a strange man enters the basement. When questioned about his identity he claims that he's the long lost Mario Brother: Pietro.

Pietro reveals that he was never mentioned because he was the black sheep of the family and ran off when they were young. However, now that he's returned, he wants to take over the family business and sell it with him taking seventy percent of the profits. Luigi quizzes him on a few little known facts but he answers the questions perfectly.

Just as Pietro has gotten his estimate on the business, Luigi receives a call from the IRS. The family owes nearly a million dollars in taxes. Pietro reveals that he's not really their brother and just wanted the money. He then leaves. Luigi reveals that the call was not from the IRS but the pizza parlor saying their pizza was ready.
