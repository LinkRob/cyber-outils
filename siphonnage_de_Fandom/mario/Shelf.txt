'''Shelves''' are solid objects appearing only in the second section of [[Shifty Boo Mansion]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. When a player stands on a shelf, it either retracts into the wall or extends out of it permanently until the [[level]] is reloaded. One of the shelves (the one that retracts to the left) on the left side of the room contains a [[Green Star]] behind it.
