'''Dribble Taxi''' is a taxicab owned and used by [[Dribble and Spitz]] throughout the {{series|WarioWare}}. It also has the former character's name in the title. Dribble Taxi first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.
