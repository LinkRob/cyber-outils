The '''Juke Box''' is an item from ''[[Mario Party]]'' available in the [[Option House]].

The Juke Box allows players to listen to any song from the game. The only requirement is that the player must have heard the song in the game itself first for it to appear in the Juke Box. The Juke Box becomes available for use after the player buys the [[Record (Mario Party)|Record]] from the [[Mushroom Shop]] for 50 [[Coin]]s.
