'''Goomez''' is a [[Goomba]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]].

==History==
This wool-capped Goomba lives in the western side of [[Rogueport]], and is one of the few Goombas on that side of town. There's not much information provided on Goomez, but according to RDM, a news cast in the game, he was once caught eating some of the flowers in the flower bed on the western side of town, around the [[Warp Pipe]]. That is also the area he hangs around most of the day. [[Goombella]] also mentions in her [[Tattle]] that Goomez seems to always be tired and likes to take naps in the flower bed.

==Trivia==
*In the original version of the game, the RDM reveals Goomez's age being 40.
