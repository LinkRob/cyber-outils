'''World 5-3''' is the third [[level]] of [[World 5 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 5]] as well as the twenty-fifth overall level in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
World 5-3 takes place in the clouds and sports several moving platforms and spiked bars. [[Flophopper]]s are found throughout the level as well. After a few paths of moving platforms, Mario crosses a chasm with two parallel platforms, dodging [[Paragoomba]]s and [[Venus Fire Trap]]s. Afterward, Mario finds the level's [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]]. Past this checkpoint are pendulums that carry Mario to the last leg of the level. This last part is similar to the previous part with moving platforms, only the two platforms begin to move up and down. Clever movement can allow Mario the final [[Star Medal]] in this part. At the end of this path is the [[Flagpole]] of the level.

== Star Medals ==
*'''[[Star Medal]] 1''': The first Star Medal doesn't appear until Mario reaches the Checkpoint. It is found on a block at the first pendulum.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal is found in Coin Heaven, a location accessed by a nearby [[Rainbow Note Block]] past the pendulums.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The final Star Medal can be obtained by jumping from the left moving platform near the end of the level. After the jump, Mario must hastily run up the falling stairs to re-reach the aforementioned platform to safety.
