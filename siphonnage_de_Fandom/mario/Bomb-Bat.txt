'''Bomb-Bats''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
As shown from their artwork, Bomb-Bats are indigo bats, and the insides of their ears and front of their wings are both light yellow.

===Behavior===
A Bomb-Bat is seen carrying a red spiked [[bomb]] with its feet. At first, a Bomb-Bat is shown in the background, but if a Bomb-Bat notices [[Wario]], it flies to the front, where Wario is, but remains toward the ceiling. A Bomb-Bat attempts to drop its spiked bomb onto Wario, and if he is hit, Wario takes damage from the explosion. The Bomb-Bat then returns into the background to get another bomb.

==In ''Virtual Boy Wario Land''==
[[File:Bomb-Bat VBWL sprite.png|frame|left]]
Bomb-Bats are common enemies that are encountered in several of the levels. Although Wario cannot stun a Bomb-Bat, he can defeat it by either [[Dash Attack|Barging]] into it or from breathing flames at it while in either his [[Sea Dragon Wario|Sea Dragon]] or [[King Dragon Wario|King Dragon]] form.
