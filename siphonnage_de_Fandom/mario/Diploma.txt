The '''Diploma''' is a key item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
[[Mario]] can only earn the Diploma by defeating [[The Master]] three times at [[The Dojo]]. The Diploma serves no other means but to serve as a certificate proving that Mario has defeated The Master.
