'''Cat Piranha Plants''' are enemies in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. They are variant of [[Piranha Plant]]s native to [[Lake Lapcat]]. Cat Piranha Plants have some of their own variants, such as [[Cat Piranha Creeper]]s and [[Cat Fire Piranha Plant]]s.

== Physical description ==
Their appearance is similar to [[Piranha Plant|Piranha Plants]], but they have red fur with white spots on their flowers & white-tipped ears. 

== In ''Bowser's Fury''==
Like normal Piranha Plants, Cat Piranha Plants appear in various areas, and attack [[Mario]] when he is nearby. A Cat Piranha Plants occasionally drops a [[Super Bell]] upon being defeated.
