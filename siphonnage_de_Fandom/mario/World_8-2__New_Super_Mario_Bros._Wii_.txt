'''World 8-2''' is a level found in the game, ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

== Overview ==
This level contains more falling debris and spinning wheels on the ground. As soon as Mario and co. enter the pipe, they end up in an underground area filled with more lava. Goombas and Paragoombas will appear many times on this stage, along with the spiked balls. The team have to dodge the obstacles to get through. This level also has a secret exit.

== Walkthrough ==
This level begins above ground, in a section with [[Lava Fireball|Lava Rock]]s and rolling hills. A [[Warp Pipe]] leads the player to an underground area filled with more lava. Multiple rolling hills are found, with a [[? Block]] containing a power-up above it. There is a section with [[Paragoomba]]s on rolling hills, with some rolling [[Spike Ball]]s following. [[Hefty Goomba]]s are found climbing up rolling hills, along with more Spike Balls and a [[Giant Spiked Ball]]. A spot that infinitely spawns Spike Balls is found as well. The [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] is found as well, followed by rolling hills over pits of lava. A [[Red Ring]] is found, along with Goombas and Hefty Goombas on rolling hills. A [[Starman]] is in a Brick Block. Two Warp Pipes that spawn Goombas are also found. More Spike Balls are found, along with a Giant Spiked Ball. A Warp Pipe takes the player to the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].

== Star Coins ==
* '''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is high up in the area where the first Giant Spiked Ball appears. Mario and co. must use the platforms to reach it.
* '''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is hidden in the wall to the bottom-right of the two Pipes from which Goombas emerge.
* '''Star Coin 3''': At the end of the level, there is a hidden area under the exit Pipe. The third Star Coin is in this area.

== Secret Exit ==
Directly after the second Star Coin, there is an area with several spinning wheels and Spiked Balls. At the end of it, turning left reveals a hidden area that leads to a Green Pipe. This Pipe leads to an area with more, faster spinning blocks that can only be traversed by jumping. Once at the end, avoid the Goombas and jump up to the Pipe which leads to the secret flagpole.{{New Super Mario Bros. Wii}}
