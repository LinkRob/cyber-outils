'''Fright Jars''' are an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. They can be obtained in various locations but are mainly purchased in [[Item Shop]]s. A similar item called the [[Fright Mask]] appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can use a Fright Jar to scare some enemies away. Upon using a Fright Jar, a large spirit resembling [[Bowser]] comes out from it, possibly causing the enemies to be scared away. The Fright Jar is ineffective against bosses and using it against enemies doesn't result in earning [[Star Point]]s.

The [[Star Power]] move known as [[Up & Away]] effectively replaces the Fright Jar as it does the exact same thing in wiping off weaker enemies on the screen.
