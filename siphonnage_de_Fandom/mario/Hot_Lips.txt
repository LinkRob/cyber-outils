'''Hot Lips'''<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 127.</ref> are [[lava]]-dwelling creatures that first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They later appear in both ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. Hot Lips have a similar appearance to [[Gargantua Blargg]]s but are much smaller and completely stationary.

==History==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''===
[[File:Hot Lips SMW2.png|thumb|left]]
Hot Lips live completely inside lava, being permanently stationed in one place. To attack, Hot Lips peek out of their basin and spit a stream of lava in the direction of [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] and [[Baby Mario]], in a manner similar to that of the [[Spray Fish]]. If the Yoshi is hit with the lava stream, it instantly loses a life. Hot Lips can only be defeated with a [[POW Block]], as many other attacks are completely ineffective and [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] only temporarily stun them.

===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
[[File:HotLips YIDS.png|thumb]]
Hot Lips later appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They still attack by spraying lava at Yoshi, and if Yoshi touches the spray, he loses a life. They appear in levels such as [[Yikes! Boiling Hot!]], [[Six-Face Sal's Fort]], and [[Find the Number Ball!]].

===''Yoshi's New Island''===
Hot Lips reappear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' in the level [[Gargantua Blargg Attacks!]] Their lava streams no longer defeat Yoshi on the spot. Instead, they cause him to drop Baby Mario as most other enemy attacks.
