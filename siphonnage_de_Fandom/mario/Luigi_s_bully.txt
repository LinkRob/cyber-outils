'''[[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]]'s bully''' is a minor character in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' during one of Luigi's flashback in the film from when he was a baby. Within that flashback, the bully can be seen knocking over his block tower, before [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] steps in to defend his brother. He then promptly runs off afterwards.
