'''Slots Shot''' is one of [[Wario]]'s [[minigame]]s in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Gameplay==
Similarly to [[Bingo Ball]], the player must launch a ball, but with a different aim and themed pinball-style board. To launch the ball, the player has to use their [[stylus]] to touch the [[Flipper (Pinball)|flipper]] and pull back for a ball to roll on, then they must release to launch it. The speed at which the flipper is released determines where the ball flies. The player has to aim to get the ball into a pipe that activates a slot machine. They first have to shoot the ball into two [[Piranha Plant]]s to remove barriers blocking the pipe, then they can shoot the ball into the pipe. After the player is awarded 3,000 [[point]]s, the ball hits a switch that activates a small slot machine. Lining up three symbols on the slot machine awards extra [[point]]s: three [[Fire Flower]]s for 5,000 points; three [[Starman|Starmen]] for 8,000 points; and three lucky 7 symbols for 10,000 points. If the player misses the pipe, the ball bounces on some [[mushroom]]s, each rewarding 300 points, and can land in one of four warp holes, both returning the ball to the player and earning them 2,000 points. The minigame ends once all eight balls have been launched.
