'''FP Drain''' is one of the [[badge]]s featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear FP Drain for one [[Badge Point|BP]], and wearing it causes Mario to restore [[Flower Point|FP]] when taking damage.

This badge is found in the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] where it is sold for one hundred [[coin]]s. There is a similar badge called the [[HP Drain (badge)|HP Drain]] that drains [[Heart Point|HP]] instead of FP.
