'''Throwing It All Overboard''' (or '''World 6-3''') is the third level of [[World 6 (Super Mario Run)|World 6]] in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. 
==Layouts==
<tabber>
|-|Pink Coins = The level begins with a single [[Rocky Wrench]]. It follows with a [[trampoline]] which the player must bounce on, then spin in midair to get the first Pink Coin. More Rocky Wrenches appear, as well as a few [[Ninji]]s. The second Pink Coin is just above another Rocky Wrench, within a jump's reach. Immediately afterward, the player must use the next trampoline to obtain the third Pink Coin. Many more Rocky Wrenches appear afterward, as well as a few more Ninjis. Afterwards, the fourth Pink Coin appears just behind a Rocky Wrench. A few more Rocky Wrenches appear, followed by the fifth and final Pink Coin, just below a Rocky Wrench. After a few more Rocky Wrenches, a [[Warp Pipe|warp pipe]] appears, leading to the boss battle with [[Boom Boom]].
|-|Purple Coins = The level begins with a single Rocky Wrench. It follows with a trampoline which the player must bounce on to hit the first ? Block that has the first Purple Coin inside it. After another Ninji appears, the player must bounce on the next trampoline to get the second Purple Coin. Almost immediately afterward, the player must jump directly on the next Ninji on a ? Block to obtain the third Purple Coin. Many more Rocky Wrenches appear afterward, as well as a few more Ninjis. Afterwards, the fourth Purple Coin appears just in front of a Rocky Wrench in a "trench" just after a Ninji. A few more Rocky Wrenches appear, followed by the fifth and final Purple Coin, on the edge of a platform with a Ninji on it. After a few more Rocky Wrenches, a warp pipe appears, leading to the boss battle with Boom Boom.
|-|Black Coins = The level begins with a single Rocky Wrench. It follows with a row of s and a Ninji that the player must jump on, then jump again to get the first Black Coin. Immediately afterwards, the player must drop down and grab onto the next ledge to obtain the second Black Coin. After a few more Rocky Wrenches, there is a wall that the player must wall jump on to obtain the third Black Coin. The player must then evade two more Rocky Wrenches and jump onto another row of ? Blocks, then jump again to collect the fourth Black Coin. Afterwards, the player must evade some more Rocky Wrenches, then jump off of the highest Rocky Wrench to collect the fifth and final Black Coin. After a few more Rocky Wrenches, a warp pipe appears, leading to the boss battle with Boom Boom.
</tabber>
