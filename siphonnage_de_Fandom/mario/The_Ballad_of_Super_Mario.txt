'''"The Ballad of Super Mario"''' is a song performed by [[Jack Black]] to promote ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. Its music video was aired on April 1, 2026 during The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon and features Black wearing a Bowser costume while performing the song in Tokyo. [[Chris Pratt]], [[Charlie Day]], [[Anya Taylor-Joy]], [[Keegan-Michael Key]], [[Benny Safdie]], [[Brie Larson]], [[Glen Powell]], and [[Donald Glover]] provide backing vocals and also appear in the music video. The song's lyrics are written from the perspective of Bowser, who sings about the plot of the film.
