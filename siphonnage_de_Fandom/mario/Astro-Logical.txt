'''Astro-Logical''' is a Battle minigame in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to find a symbol on the board that matches the card featured at the top. There is a container that contains many cards featuring astrological symbols. This container will open up, allowing all of the cards to fly out. Players must then take stamps and guide them around the board and find a matching astrological sign. The picture at the top will be distorted at the beginning, but will slowly become clearer as players progress.

Players will need to be quick in this game, as there is one less symbol than there are players, meaning whoever is the only one to not find a symbol is eliminated. Players will also be eliminated if they don't find a symbol within a time limit of ten seconds. The last remaining player wins the game. It is also possible for the game to come to a draw if all players fail to find a symbol within the time limit.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|stick}} - Move the cursor/stamp
*{{button|gcn|A}} - Confirm choice

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a portmanteau of "astrology" and "logical."
