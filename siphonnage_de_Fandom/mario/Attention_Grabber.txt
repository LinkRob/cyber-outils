}}'''Attention Grabber''' is a Duel minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the two players are competing to see who can get the attention of the boat first. This is accomplished by waving the Wii Remote to signal for help. The player who manages to get the boat to pick them up first wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}} - Wave for help
