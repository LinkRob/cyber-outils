The '''Giga Bell''' is a power up exclusive to the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' mode of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It transforms [[Mario]] into [[Giga Cat Mario]], although its power can only be accessed by collecting five [[Cat Shine]]s. Giga Bells make their first appearance near [[Lake Lapcat]]'s lakeside.

Mother Calico [[Cats (Bowser's Fury)|Cats]] can often be found near them.

==Description==
By default, Giga Bells are the standard [[Super Bell]], but their size is much larger and the Giga Bell has cat ears, whiskers, green eyes, and a more orangish coloration. It also rests on a golden, Sphinx-like pedestal with a short staircase leading up to the top, sometimes with a few stacks of coins resting beneath. When it has been corrupted by [[Fury Bowser]], however, the bell becomes black and spiky, with a dark purple aura surrounding it, and it appears to be asleep, or sad. When awake, it will start making ringing noises, the sound similar to a massive bell, and make low meowing noises.

The Giga Bell can only be used by [[Mario]]. The user of it gains a komainu-like Cat costume and has all of the abilities of [[Cat Mario]]. But it makes Mario huge and even if he is damaged and isn't a cat anymore, he will still be a giga-sized Mario, more giant than if he takes the [[Mega Mushroom]], and if he takes more damage from there, he’ll become his small form, but also giga-sized. This is the only way to fight Fury Bowser off.
