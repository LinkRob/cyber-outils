'''Gold Mario''', or '''Silver Luigi''' when used by [[Luigi]], is a power-up which first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Their move set allows them to throw golden fireballs that explode which turns obstacles into [[coin]]s, and defeats enemies, which also turn into coins. The fireballs also make [[Brick Block]]s and [[Question Block]]s release their items. The player loses the power-up after they finish a level or gets hurt. For Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS and [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]], when a player grabs 100 coins in the stage ''Golden Plains'' their character will turn Golden: this includes [[Mario]] himself. This power-up is similar to [[Metal Mario (character)|Metal]] [[Metal Mario (form)|Mario]].

Gold Mario made several playable appearances in other games, which include ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]'', ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'',  ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' and [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|''Paper Mario: The Thousand Year Door'' remake]].

==Trivia==
[[File:Gold_mario_oreo.jpg|thumb|100px|left]] 
*In the first ''Animal Crossing'' game, there are two furniture items based on Mario and Luigi, the Mario Statue and the Luigi Statue. Interestingly, the Mario Statue is golden, and the Luigi Statue is silver.
*As a promotion for ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'' in 2012, Nintendo teamed up with Oreo to create an advertisement that featured Gold Mario shooting golden Oreos.
