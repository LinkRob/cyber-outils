'''Crooks''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are thieves encountered at [[Rose Way]] and in [[Moleville Mines]], where they assist [[Croco]] in stealing all of [[Mario]]'s [[coin]]s. Crooks hide and run away before they are fought in battle. Crooks have a stronger variant named [[Sackit]].

==Battle==
In battle, Crooks possess no special attacks and only attack by punching and throwing daggers. However, they also tend to run away from battle.

===Strategy===
To compensate for their high [[Attack]] rating, a Crook has low [[HP]] and [[defense]], so they should be taken out with a timed attack.
