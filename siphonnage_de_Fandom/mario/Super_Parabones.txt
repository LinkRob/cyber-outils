A '''Super Parabones''' is a larger variation of a [[Parabones]] and a winged varitey of the [[Super Dry Bones]], they behave very similarly to Parabones but their larger size makes them harder to avoid. Super Parabones are commonly seen flying about and sometimes attempt to fly at [[Mario]] upon sight, Super Parabones are rather durable and merely rattle their teeth upon being stomped, this can be used to get better positioning to reach higher platforms, due to their durability, a Super Parabones can only be destroyed with the effects of an invincibility power-up such as a [[Starman]].

==History==
===''Super Mario Maker/Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS/Super Mario Maker 2''===
Super Parabones appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as well as it's [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS|two]] [[Super Mario Maker 2|sequels]] as placeable enemies. A Super Parabones can be made by applying wings to a [[Super Dry Bones]] or by giving a [[Super Mushroom]] to a [[Parabones]], Super Parabones behave much like regular-sized Parabones but like the Super Dry Bones, they are very durable and stomping on them merely makes them rattle their teeth, they wings also remain intact from stomps. If a Super Parabones is groundpounded it will lose it's wings and become a regular Super Dry Bones. Super Parabones can be stomped on continuously for better positioning to reach higher ledges or crossing dangerous chasms, a Super Parabones can only be destroyed with the effects of a [[Starman]].
