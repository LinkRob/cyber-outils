'''Patty''' is an orphaned girl who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live action segment "[[Day of the Orphan]]." She shows up at [[Mario Bros. Plumbing]] as a guest, and [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] allow her to have a stay at their place. However, Patty's parents show up at the end and force her to confess that she is not an orphaned child.
