'''Gold Bar x3''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. As the name suggests, it is three [[Gold Bar]]s.

== Appearances ==
=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
[[File:Gold_Bar_x3.png|frame|left]]
Gold Bar x3 has no particular purpose, but it is primarily similar to Gold Bars. [[Mario]] can purchase a Gold Bar x3 at [[Deepdown Depot]] for 350 [[coin]]s or at the [[Pianta Parlor]] by paying 234 [[Pianta Token]]s with the [[Silver Card]]. Another way for Mario to obtain a Gold Bar x3 is by buying 300 items from a store. A Gold Bar x3 can be sold for 300 coins.

=== ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' ===
Gold Bar x3 reappears as an item. Mario can now purchase them from the shop in [[Flopside]], but the Gold Bar x3 otherwise has little difference from its previous appearance.
