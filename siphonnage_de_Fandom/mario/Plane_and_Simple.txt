'''Plane and Simple''' is [[Young Cricket]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. "Plane and Simple" is a pun of ''plain and simple''. The same music shares with [[Logged In]].

==Gameplay==
The goal of the microgame is to pull the [[Wii Remote]] towards yourself many times. When the microgame is won, it replaces it with something else.
*'''Level 1:''' It takes place in the snow. Plane the wood 300 units (inches) for the skis, the person continues skiing.
*'''Level 2:''' It takes place in the plains. Plane the wood 6000 units (yards) for the train tracks. The small train continues over it.
*'''Level 3:''' Plane the wood 9999 units (miles) for Saturn's ring. The saucer appears flying past.
