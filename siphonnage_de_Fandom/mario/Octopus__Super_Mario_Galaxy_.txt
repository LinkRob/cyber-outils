'''Octopuses''' (also called '''Aliens''' or '''Roctos''') are a variant of [[Octoomba]] which appear as an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. In their debut appearance, Octopuses appeared in [[Space Junk Galaxy]] during the [[Kamella's Airship Attack]] mission. They reappeared in [[Bowser Jr.'s Lava Reactor]].
