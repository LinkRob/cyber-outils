The '''Projection Room''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Projection Room has a projector and a projector screen, hence the room's name, and the projector screen is flanked by two oddly-shaped speakers resembling violin cases without necks. There is also a large cabinet placed against the eastern wall and two lights hanging from the ceiling.

If Luigi tries to suck the projection screen with the [[Poltergust 3000]], it turns into the picture of a [[Boo]] with the text "Get out of here!". The same poster makes a reappearance on the second floor's [[Washroom (2F)|Washroom]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Projection Room first becomes accessible in Area Three, and it accessed from the right door of the [[Billiards Room]]. The only ghosts encountered in the Projection Room are seven [[Cinema Ghost]]s, which only begin to appear once Luigi taps a projector on a table, also affecting the room's lighting in the process. Once every Cinema Ghost is vacuumed up, the lights turn on, and a [[treasure chest]] containing [[Mario's Glove]] appears.
