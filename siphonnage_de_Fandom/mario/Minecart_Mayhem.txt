==Gameplay==
Minecart Mayhem is a minigame that involves being inside of a minecart and avoiding [[TNT Barrel]]s for a number of seconds. The player will be able to speed up, slow down, and change tracks in order to avoid being blown up and losing the minigame. [[Chunky Kong]] is technically the first Kong to play this minigame as his Minecart Mayhem game involves avoiding only one TNT Barrel. [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] will have to avoid two TNT Barrels during their race. Avoiding the TNT Barrel(s) results in a [[Golden Banana]]. This is another minigame that cannot be played at [[Snide's H.Q.]] after getting all of the [[Blueprint]]s.
