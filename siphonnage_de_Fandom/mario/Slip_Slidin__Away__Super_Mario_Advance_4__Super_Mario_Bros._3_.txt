'''Slip Slidin' Away''' is a [[level]] of [[World-e]] in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It was originally only available to Japanese releases, but would later become available in the [[Wii U]] [[Virtual Console]] and [[Nintendo Switch Online]] [[Reissue|port]]s.

==Description==
Slip Slidin' Away is an ice-themed level where [[Ice Block]]s compose a majority of the platforms, and there are snow-capped trees in the background. The level uniquely features one [[Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brother]] and a number of [[Bumpty|Bumpties]], two enemies not in the main ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It is also one of the World-e levels with [[Stretch Block]]s, along with [[Rich with Ropes]].
