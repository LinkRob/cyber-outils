A '''Nutty Cake''' is a type of [[cake]] baked by [[Tayce T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
A Nutty Cake can be made by mixing both a [[Goomnut]] and a [[Cake Mix]]. Using a Nutty Cake restores 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] making it a decent healing item. Obtaining the ingredients for this [[recipe]] requires going to [[Goomba Village]] and [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]].
