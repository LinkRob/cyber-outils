'''Pipesqueak''' is a [[Duel Mode]] board that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Layout==
As hinted in the title, Pipesqueak mainly revolves around using Warp Pipes to warp to a random location making this board based mainly on chance. There are four Warp Pipes that are located on the four corners of the board and [[Belltop]] is located near the bottom of the board.

===Story Mode===
In [[Story Mode]], the player must duel against [[Donkey Kong]] or [[Luigi]].
