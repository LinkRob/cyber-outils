'''Origami Mechakoopas''' are an origami variant of [[Mechakoopa]]s which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. One Mechakoopa can appear in random battles as well as flee if it is not defeated in one turn. Defeating an Origami Mechakoopa earns [[Mario]] a 1,000 Coin bag upon returning to the overworld.
