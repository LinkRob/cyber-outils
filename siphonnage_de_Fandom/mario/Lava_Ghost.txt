'''Lava Ghosts''' are types of ghosts made out of fire in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. They are very similar to [[Podoboo]]s except that they have an appearance as a ghost with flames surrounding it. Lava Ghosts are only encountered in one [[level]], [[Blargg's Boiler]]. Lava Ghosts cannot be defeated.
