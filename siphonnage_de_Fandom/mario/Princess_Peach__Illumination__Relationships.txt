'''[[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]]''' is a charismatic, capable and a confident leader. When a threat to her kingdom such as Bowser arrives, she will stop at nothing to defend it. Despite her qualities of a leader, she relies and helps her allies such as giving [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] the access to [[Super Bell]] to beat [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]]. In return, her allies help Peach when needed such as Toad giving Peach the [[Ice Flower]] to freeze [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]].

== Friends/Allies ==

=== [[Cranky Kong (Illumination)|Cranky Kong]] ===
Before Cranky Kong agreed to Peach's request, he wanted Mario to defeat his son, Donkey Kong. Seeing Mario was in trouble, she assisted him defeating Donkey Kong.

Donkey Kong came to Peach's aid and assisted sabotaging the wedding and rescuing the prisoners.

=== [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] ===
{{Q|'''Mario:''' See? I told you I'd get you a pet Turtle.<br>'''Peach:''' [giggles] Not bad, mustache.|Mario and Peach.}}
[[File:Mario Movie Screenshot 21.jpg|thumb|right|Mario meets Princess Peach in her castle.]]
First meeting each other in [[Princess Peach's Castle]], Peach was amazed to see another human. Knowing he is new to the Mushroom World, she acts as a mentor and ally of Mario being supportive of him in his struggles.

Struggling to complete her obstacle course. Despite Mario nearly reaching the end after many attempts, Peach focused on his improvements and rewarded him. In the [[Jungle Kingdom]], Mario struggled to fight Donkey; Peach helped him by giving him a [[Super Bell]] to transform into [[Cat Mario]] helping him defeat Donkey Kong.

While Peach was forced in a wedding with Bowser, Mario came to her aid crashing the wedding.

=== [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]] ===
{{Quote|I said I wouldn't let anyone hurt you.|Toad}}
[[File:Mario Movie Screenshot 11.jpg|thumb|left|Peach and Toad.]]
Peach has known Toad since she was a princess of the Mushroom Kingdom. She took him to their travels to the [[Jungle Kingdom]]. When Bowser forced a marriage on Peach while torturing Toad, Peach reluctantly agreed to Bowser's proposal for Kamek to stop torturing Toad. Before Bowser's wedding started, Toad helped Peach smuggle an [[Ice Flower]] in a boquet to surprise Bowser when she transforms into her [[Ice Mario|ice form]].

Movie)|Princess Peach]] through an interactive 3D map to that Bowser and his army have taken over the Snow Kingdom. Toad General wished Peach luck before she headed off to her journey.

=== Toads ===
{{Q|I will not let [[Bowser (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|him]] hurt you. We are going to stop Bowser.|Princess Peach to [[Toad (species)|Toads]], ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]}}
Peach has been close to the Toads ever since she was a child when she entered a pipe to the Mushroom World. The Toads raised her by teaching her fishing and completing an obstacle course before she became the princess of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] ruling for the Toads. When she returned driving from [[Rainbow Road]], Peach convinced the Toads that they will fight back Bowser despite their doubts.

=== [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]] ===
Donkey Kong is one of Peach's friends, although, Mario fought and beat him and won the Kong army, to defeat Bowser. Donkey Kong tends to be very polite and respectful towards the princess throughout the movie even stopping him from teasing Mario over his attempt to flirt with her when Peach says enough.

=== [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] ===
{{Quote|'''Peach:''' Luigi, you're so brave.<br>'''Luigi:''' Oh, thanks. Yeah, it's kinda my thing.|Peach to Luigi.}}
Peach cared about Mario's brother, Luigi, because Mario needed Peach's help on rescueing her. Luigi told the princess that bravery is his thing, after defeating Bowser and saving Broklyn with the help of his brother together.

Peach and Bowser had a one-sided relationship, Bowser loves Peach as he expresses his love for her in "[[Peaches]]" but she does not love him back.

Peach had a goal to stop Bowser as she also has a cardboard cut-out that looks like Bowser in her obstacle course as a target.

Despite Peach not loving him back, Bowser intervened the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] forcing a proposal to Peach. Knowing Toad was tortured, Peach reluctantly agreed to be his "wife" in his wedding. Aware that he was planning to publicly burn the prisoners in the lava, she confronted Bowser telling him that she will not marry a "monster" like him.

Before Peach shrank Bowser with a [[Mini Mushroom]], Bowser begged Peach to give him a chance which she refused. Toad then followed up with putting Bowser inside the jar.

=== [[Kamek (Illumination)|Kamek]] ===
Kamek as Bowser's informant, Peach dislikes Kamek. When Kamek has the role of the wedding officient as he pronounced Peach and Bowser's wedding, Peach punched him in the face before he finished his speech much to Bowser's surprise.
