'''Mushroomy Kingdom''' is one of many [[Mario]] themed stages that are playable in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

Mushroomy Kingdom is a distorted copy of [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]] of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' All [[Question Block|item boxes]] will grant the player a random item (based on which ones are turned on) and all the other boxes can be broken with any attack. The background of this stage is very much a desert and not too friendly looking. This stage scrolls to the left on its own, and once the end of the level is reached, it goes back to the start of it. Note that all the boxes are back to the original state. This stage also has a chance of being [[World 1-2 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-2]], which acts just like World 1-1 only this time a different level.
