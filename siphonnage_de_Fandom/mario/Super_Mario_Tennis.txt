'''Super Mario Tennis''' is one of four special games in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''. It is named after ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', which it reuses several assets from.

==Gameplay==
The minigame involves the player's character having to hit the tennis ball at enemies, [[coin]]s, power-up items, and [[Brick Block]]s on a screen, which also earns them [[point]]s. The object of each level is to hit the [[Goal Pole|Flagpole]] at the end with the ball. The minigame has a [[time limit]] that can be raised by earning points and coins. If the ball hits a [[Warp Pipe]], either a sub-area or a different level is accessed. The lives are represented by how many tennis balls the character has left, so if they miss hitting a tennis ball, the character loses that tennis ball.

Playing the minigame can earn a few unlockables. [[Baby Mario]] can be unlocked by completing level 1-3. A [[Wario]] costume can be unlocked by completing level 1-4 with at least 800 coins, and a [[Waluigi]] costume can be unlocked by completing level 2-4 with at least 800 coins.
