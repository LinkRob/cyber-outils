'''''Super Mario-kun''''' is a manga series based on the ''Mario'' games written by [[Yukio Sawada]] and published by [[Shogakukan]]. The comic is printed monthly in [[CoroCoro Comic]]. They contained many of the characters and scenarios of various ''Mario'' series games, including ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
