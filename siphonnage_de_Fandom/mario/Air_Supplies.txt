'''Air Supplies''' is a [[microgame]] of the [[Strange]] genre in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place on a flat surface with a flatbed truck driving by. The top of the screen has a helicopter, whose sprites are reused from ''[[Parachute (Game & Watch)|Parachute]]'' for the [[Game & Watch]].

==Gameplay==
The objective is to make the helicopter successfully drop the cargo onto the truck, by pressing the A button at the right time. The cargo must land on the back of the truck, even if partially. If the cargo is not dropped onto the truck or it hits the cab, causing it to off, the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
The width of the truck bed differs in each difficulty level.

*'''Level 1:''' The truck bed is wide
*'''Level 2:''' The truck bed is slightly shortened.
*'''Level 3:''' The truck bed is the same width as the cargo.
