'''Terminal Velocity''' is one of the [[microgame]]s under [[Mona]]'s [[Strange]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place at night. A train goes down a railroad track from the right, and there is a train station with some passengers on the left. Some clouds can be seen in the sky, one of which resembles a whale.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to get the train to stop at the train station. To do so, the player must press {{button|gba|A}} with the right timing to gradually slow the train to stop at the station so that the passengers can climb aboard. In doing so, they win at the microgame. If the train does not reach the passengers, as a result of either bypassing the station and hitting a pile of rocks or by stopping too early, the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level increasingly decreases the width of the train station.

*'''Level 1:''' The station is wide, taking up almost a quarter of the in-game screen's length.
*'''Level 2:''' The station is shorter.
*'''Level 3:''' The station is even shorter.
