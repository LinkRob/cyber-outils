'''Blargg's Boiler''' is the second level of ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'' for World 2. This is a lava-like place marking the first appearance of featuring [[Blargg]]s and [[Podoboo]]s along with their species [[Lava Bubble]]s and [[Lava Ghost]]s. [[Puffer]]s also appear in this area. There are giant bones used as platforms to avoid the lava which can be found all across the entire level.

As the page turned, an ocean of boiling hot lava bubbled out.  If the Yoshis didn't cross, they would never find the Super Happy Tree. Blargg and the lava were scary, but the Yoshis bravely fought their way through.
