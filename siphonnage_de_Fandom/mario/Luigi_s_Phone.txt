'''Luigi's Phone''', that matches [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]]'s colors of his hat, and shirt is an object that appears in [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros. film series]].

==History==
{{Quote|'''Spike:''' Tell me: have you even gotten '''ONE''' call since you left me to start your dumb company?<br>'''Luigi:''' As a matter of fact, Spike, we have!<br>'''Mario:''' Wow! Really?<br>'''Luigi:''' Ah, Yeah! [[Mario and Luigi's mother|Our mom]] called and she said, "Aw, boys, that's the best commercial I've ever seen!" And I said, "Thank you very much, mother! We're very proud of it!" So BOOM! (accidentally drops his phone)|Luigi bragging Spike about the brothers' call from their mother.}}
After Luigi, and his brother, [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] advertised their [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing|plumbing commercial]], their mother called and told them how much she loved it like it was cinema, and Luigi replied "Thank you very much, mother, we're very proud of it." After bragging [[Foreman Spike (Illumination)|Spike]] about it, since they left him to start their company, Luigi accidentally slammed his phone on the ground when he said "So, Boom!", causing its screen to break. After Spike left [[Punch-Out!! Pizzeria]], they got a call for their first job.
