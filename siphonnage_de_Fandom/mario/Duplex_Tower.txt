The '''Duplex Tower''' is a major location from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']]. It is the main building on [[Lottacoins Island]], and is located at the for north side.

== Description ==
The Duplex Tower consists of two square buildings connected by sky bridges. It has square windows and a design resembling outlets in the outer walls. Its floors consist of yellow-brown square tiles, with the exception of the fourth floor in its eastern half, which have grey tiles. The buildings have blue cameras in there interiors, and these cameras can be viewed and used to get hints for codes to get past gates that block off paths.

On the bottom floor of the west building is a reception centre, with the second floor immediately to the east consisting of little more than a short hallway. The third floor has a few desks, while the fourth floor has a north-south hallway with a vent at the south end that [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can traverse with their [[Bros. Ball]] ability leading to an area above the third floor. The area above the third floor has another vent that leads to the rooftop, where a giant breed of [[Snaptor]] called "Mega Snaptor" is fought. Another vent leads to [[Lottacoins Island]]'s [[Lighthouse (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Lighthouse]].

The east building's bottom floor is closed off, but the second floor remains accessible, with a radio through which a [[Mettozokettohei#Variants|Togezokettohei]] who calls himself "Zok Trooper" gives a hint for the code that unlocks the door to the third floor of the Duplex Tower. The fourth floor has a grey-tiled room where [[Princess Peach]] is held captive.

== History ==
The Duplex Tower is the main business location of [[Concordia]], located in Lottacoins city, which would later become [[Lottacoins Island]] when [[Zokket]] sundered the [[Uni-Tree]] at the behest of his "god," [[Reclusa]]. It was conquered by the [[Zokket Army]] and used as a waystation to deliver [[Concordian]] prisoners to the [[Great Lighthouse Islands]] in the [[Color-Full Sea|Color-Full]] and [[Brrrning Sea|Brrrning Seas]].
