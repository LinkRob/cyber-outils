'''The Vinery''' is a location in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It consists of several floating, curly [[beanstalk]]s in varying colors, and they can be used as a path from [[Bean Valley]] to [[Nimbus Land]]. Two enemies are encountered here, [[Birdy]]s and [[Heavy Troopa]]s.
