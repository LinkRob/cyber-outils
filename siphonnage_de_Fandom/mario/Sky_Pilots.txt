'''Sky Pilots''' is a 2-vs-2 racing [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to reach the goal before the other team. One team member must steer the [[Cheep Cheep]] plane, while the other must flap the wings. Going through a rainbow ring causes a plane to accelerate. However, colliding with a [[cannonball]] or a [[Bowser]] balloon knocks the plane backward. The team that reaches the goal first wins. 

In ''Mario Party 2'', the minigame can be purchased from [[Woody]] for 200 [[coin]]s.
