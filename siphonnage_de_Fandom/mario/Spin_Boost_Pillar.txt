'''Spin Boost Pillars''' are an object in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

==Description==
Spin Boost Pillars are featured in [[anti-gravity]] areas within several courses. Whenever a racer drives into a Spin Boost Pillar, they perform a [[Spin Boost]], which can also occur if two racers bump into one another while in an anti-gravity area. A few other courses feature objects with the functionality of a Spin Boost Pillar, such as the [[submarine]] wheels in [[Water Park]] or the [[Wario's Gold-Filled Mine Cart]]s in {{classic course|Wii|[[Wario's Gold Mine]]}}.

In [[Hyrule Circuit]], driving into all three Spin Boost Pillars on the castle's stairs within a short period of time reveals a [[Dash Panel]] ramp in the next room that takes racers over the central platform with the [[Master Sword]], which itself has the functionality of a Spin Boost Pillar upon being passed through.
