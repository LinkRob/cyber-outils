==Trivia==
*The Mushroom Cup in this game is called Kinoko Cup.
*Luigi, Peach, Wario, and Toad use Japanese actors over their American voice actors. These voices are reused in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' in 2001.
*The billboards where puns on real life companies in the Japanese Version.
*Some of the sounds in the 4th place or lower ending are different.
