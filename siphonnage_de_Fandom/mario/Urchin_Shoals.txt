'''Urchin Shoals''', or '''Sparkling Waters-4''', is the fourth level of [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Haunted Shipwreck]], and its own completion unlocks [[Dragoneel's Undersea Grotto]]. It is the location of the third [[Nabbit]] chase. 

==Layout==
Like [[Waterspout Beach]] and [[Above the Cheep Cheep Sea]], this level contains many [[Waterspout]]s. The level begins with a Waterspout with a [[Mega Urchin]] on it. A [[Goomba]] is found, along with another Mega Urchin on a Waterspout, found under a [[? Block]] containing a power-up. A group of five Waterspouts follows, some of which have [[Urchin]]s on them. [[Koopa Troopa]]s are also found. A row of [[Donut Block]]s is found above a Mega Urchin, and causes them to fall allows access to a [[Brick Block]] containing a [[Mini Mushroom]]. A section of ground with a Koopa Troopa and a Goomba on it, with Mega Urchins below, is followed by a Waterspouts. More Mega Urchins on Waterspouts are found, followed by the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. A platform containing a long row of Mega Urchins on Waterspouts, and is followed by a platform with a Koopa Troopa on it and a row of Urchins on Waterspouts below. Another section of Mega Urchins on Waterspouts follows. A Waterspout that leads to a platform above is found, along with a [[Red Ring]]. More Mega Urchins on Waterspouts follow, with two lifted above rows of Donut Blocks before the [[Goal Pole]].

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Underneath an orange bridge, a [[Mini Mushroom]] is required to reach the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Immediately after the first Star Coin is a Waterspouts that reaches up to the [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the area with the second Star Coin. Like before, a Mini Mushroom is required to reach it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Below an orange bridge is a series of Urchins and Waterspouts that creates a path to the Warp Pipe that leads to the area with the third Star Coin.
