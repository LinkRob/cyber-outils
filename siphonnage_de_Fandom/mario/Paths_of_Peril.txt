'''Paths of Peril''' is a [[Battle Minigame|Battle mini-game]] appearing in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. It later returns in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
This mini-game has players who need to navigate throughout a canyon amongst narrow pathways within 60 seconds. Players have to move carefully, but quickly in attempt of winning the mini-game. There are two ways of players to navigate throughout the course; the first is by going left, which leads to a short winding road and the other is right, which leads to paths which go in straight lines in all directions. Both paths lead to the same ending area.

If the player falls off a cliff into an endless abyss, a [[Klepto]] (''[[Mario Party 4]]'') or a [[Lakitu]] (''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'') carries them back to where they stopped. If a player reaches the finishing area first, that player wins the mini-game.
