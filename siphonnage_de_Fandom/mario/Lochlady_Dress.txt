The '''Lochlady Dress''' is a treasure of [[Lake Lamode]] appearing in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It is a white replica of [[Princess Peach]]'s dress. [[Bowser]] stole the Lochlady Dress from the Water Plaza Display Window in [[Lake Kingdom]] for Princess Peach to wear. After Peach is rescued from [[Bowser]], she returns the Lochlady Dress to the Water Plaza Display Window.
