|name=Worn-Out Hammer x5 (Battle Card)}}'''Worn-Out Hammer x5''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Worn-Out Hammer x5 is the weakest version of [[Mario]]'s "Hammer x5" attack. It is five of the [[Worn-Out Hammer (Battle Card)|Worn-Out Hammer]]'s scattered across the card. There is an orange/white striped background.
