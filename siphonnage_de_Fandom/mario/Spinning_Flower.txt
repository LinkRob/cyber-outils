A '''Spinning Flower''' is a type of flower which appears as an object in both ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Physical description==
A Spinning Flower's design vastly differs between ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' and ''Paper Mario''; in the former game, Spinning Flower with four pink petals and a yellow head with two black eyes, whereas the latter removes a Spinning Flower's head and changes its petals to purple.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
[[File:Spinning Flower SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
Spinning Flowers appear in only three areas, [[Mushroom Way]], [[Bandit's Way]], and [[Land's End]]. Whenever Mario stands on a Spinning Flower, he spins around, and by [[jump]]ing off, Mario is launched into a single direction; this is necessary to reach certain areas.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
[[File:PM_Spinning_Flower.png|frame|left]]
Spinning Flowers are only found on [[Lavalava Island]]. When [[Mario]] stands on a Spinning Flower, the player can press the {{button|n64|z}} button to have Mario hover even higher. This is usually done to collect some coins. However, using a Spinning Flower is required to find one of the missing Yoshi Kids of the [[Fearsome 5]].
