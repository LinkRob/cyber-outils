'''Wario's Bluff''' is a [[minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party-e]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame puts [[Wario]], who is driving the [[Wario Car]], in a desert area, a possible reference to him doing so in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''{{'}}s intro and end credits.

==Gameplay==
Wario has to drive his car and stop as close to the edge without falling off, otherwise the player loses the minigame. After the first player has their turn, the second player does the same. Whoever stops Wario closest to the edge is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|A}}: Accelerate
*{{button|gba|B}}: Brake
