'''Tryclyde''' is an antagonist in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. He is the show counterpart of the [[Tryclyde]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Tryclyde is a red three-headed snake. He is depicted with a pale yellow belly instead of white like his game counterpart.

=== Personality traits ===
Tryclyde is somewhat unintelligent. Since [[Wart]] does not appear in ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'', Tryclyde's allegiance is [[King Koopa (DiC cartoons)|King Koopa]] as a member of his [[Koopa Troop|Koopa Pack]]. Tryclyde often helps King Koopa, [[Mouser (DiC cartoons)|Mouser]] and [[Koopa Troopa (DiC cartoons)|Koopa Troopa]] in their plots. He most often battled by [[Princess Toadstool (DiC cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], who was usually able to defeat him.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Tryclyde has the ability to speak from all three heads, but he rarely uses his middle.

== Episode appearances ==
In "[[The Great Gladiator Gig]]", Tryclyde assumes the alter-ego of "Tryclydius" to fit with King Koopa's [[Emperor Augustus Septemberus Octoberus Koopa|Roman motif]].

In "[[Koopa Klaus]]", Tryclyde assumes the moniker of "Randolph the Red-Nosed Tryclyde", going with King Koopa's [[Koopa Klaus (alter ego)|Santa Claus motif]].

==Trivia==
*The series' writer's bible mentions that there is more than one Tryclyde.
