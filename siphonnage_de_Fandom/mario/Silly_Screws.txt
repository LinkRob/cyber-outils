}}'''Silly Screws''' is a Duel Mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the two players must get to the finish by running on screws. They must run either clockwise or counterclockwise to line up the screws and reach the finish. The player that reaches the finish first wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|a}} (Repeatedly) - Spin Counterclockwise
*{{button|n64|b}} (Repeatedly) - Spin Clockwise
