'''Get a Hand''' is the third [[mission]] of [[Tick Tock Clock]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Objective==
[[Mario]] (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] in ''Super Mario 64 DS'') needs to climb to the platform where the first clock hand spins across. Mario has to [[jump]] onto the hand and ride it as it turns around the large chasm. Once the clock hand reaches a small alcove on the other side of the clock, Mario has to jump into it to collect the [[Power Star]] while avoiding the [[Amp]] moving nearby. Alternatively, the Star can also be obtained by falling from the platform above the small alcove, which is the only reliable way to reach it if Mario has entered Tick Tock Clock with all the mechanisms disabled. The [[Heave Ho]] can also launch Mario into the alcove, but this is very unreliable and more likely to occur by accident.

In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', there are two more methods to reaching the alcove, by either carefully using Luigi's [[Backward Somersault]] from a higher area or Yoshi's [[Flutter Jump]].

==Trivia==
*The mission's name is a pun on a phrase that means "to receive help."
