The '''Endless stairs''' is an obstacle that is encountered in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It is to prevent the heroes from reaching the final level, [[Bowser in the Sky]], before collecting enough [[Power Star|Power Stars]].

== Description ==
The endless stairs is a room with a long red staircase, with portraits of [[Bowser]] and [[Princess Peach]] on either side, and at the top of the staircase is a corridor with a larger portrait of Bowser with a hole leading to the final level in the game, Bowser in the Sky. Unlike other rooms where the player is required to have a certain number of Power Stars to enter, the room containing the Endless Stairs allows [[Mario]] to enter while having fewer, but will not be able to get past the obstacle.

In ''Super Mario 64'', the whole room has an ominous dark red lighting. In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', the lighting is normal, but if the player comes into this room with less than the required 80 Power Stars or with any character other than Mario, the room has a very dark lighting when trying to climb the stairs.

== In ''[[Super Mario 64]]''/''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''==
If Mario has fewer than 70 [[Power Star]]s (80 in the remake), he can still enter the [[Big Star Door]], although the endless stairs will be in effect, which can be identified by an ominous music playing. The illusion of the endless stairs is created by looping Mario back when he reaches a certain point. In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', the player can easily see this on the map. Once the Power Star requirement has been met, Mario can freely climb the top of the stairs. In the remake, if [[Luigi]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], or [[Wario]] attempt to open the Star Door, they will climb the endless stairs, regardless of the number of Power Stars, as they hear Bowser's voice telling them to scram; While they are still able to enter, the endless stairs are put into effect, even if the required Power Stars are met. This is because the [[Nintendo DS]] version is scripted to have only Mario defeat Bowser and the Koopa King only views the red plumber as his true rival.

== Trivia ==
* In ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', the minigame [[Growing Up]] remixes the song while the players are climbing up.
* The Endless Stairs has become an infamous meme among fans on social media.[[Image:SM64DS Endless Stairs (Oldschool) Glitch|thumb|Performing the BLJ glitch in ''Super Mario 64 DS''|227x227px]]

* In ''Super Mario 64'', it is possible to bypass the Endless Stairs with less than 70 Power Stars, thanks to the infamous Backwards Long Jump (or "BLJ") glitch, and thanks to the continuous loop, the player can practice as many times trying to master it until they eventually skyrocket up to the top. However, it should be performed on the right side of the stairs, because if the player does rocket up the stairs on the left corner, Mario will clip through the stairs and end up in [[Wet-Dry World]] instead. In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', only Mario can reach the top of the endless stairs but the Backwards Long Jump glitch allows any character to reach the top. More information on this glitch can be found in the above video. This glitch also allows for players to beat the game without playing as Mario.
** Should the player try to access Bowser in the Sky with anyone other than Mario via this glitch, and number of oddities can occur.
*** When Luigi, Yoshi, or Wario ultimately defeat Bowser and grab the [[Jumbo Star]], they'll sprout wings, but will be frozen in place as Mario spawns behind them, and the camera moves.
**** This is possibly due to the fact that, because the Jumbo Star is programmed to give Mario wings, the game cannot program any other character using the ability in single-player, only multiplayer.
*** The ending plays out as normal afterwards, but during the credits roll, the character that the player used to defeat Bowser with that is not Mario will spawn in some of the scenes featuring Mario, like the hut in [[Cool, Cool Mountain]] and inside the volcano in [[Lethal Lava Land]].
*** Then, after the scene featuring the [[Rainbow Ride]], the credits cut abruptly and the character spawns outside of the Mushroom Castle with a life lost (or a [[Game Over]] if on their last life). Most sound effects are also gone, and the second floor is locked again. The only ways to resolve both issues is to restart the game, and complete [[Bowser in the Fire Sea]] again respectively.
**** The reason why the second floor is locked again is because there is a code reverted which tells the game that Bowser in the Fire Sea has not been completed yet, because one of the scenes from [[Dire, Dire Docks]] shows Mario standing on Bowser's submarine, and for some reason, the [[Plunder in the Sunken Ship|first star]] in [[Jolly Roger Bay]] gets added to the Star list if it has not yet already been obtained, because to show that scene, it requires that Star to be obtained. Because of these oddities, it would make sense as to why the credits cut halfway and the player loses a life, as none of these would occur if the player did the final level with Mario. However, the cause for the missing sound effects are still unknown.
*** However, it makes more sense as to why the final [[Big Star Door]] does not require any Power Stars to open in this version of the game, and due to all the glitches stated above on what happens when using anyone other than Mario, this explains why the endless stairs are permanently put in effect for them, because of programming issues, to prevent the player from having to unlock the second floor again should they actually manage to beat the game without Mario.
**** It is also worth noting that, none of these glitches occur if the player has less than 80 Power Stars, but uses Mario to enter Bowser in the Sky, and everything will play out as normal afterwards.
