'''Sworms''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are black worms with big orange lips. They can be found in the [[Beanbean Fields]].

==Battle==
Sworms accompany [[Sharpea]]s during battle. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can attack one by either stomping it or by whacking it with their [[hammer]]. Sworms attack by burrowing into the ground and pop out under Mario or Luigi. They also attack by burrowing into the ground and popping out to bite either Mario or Luigi.

=== Strategy ===
Mario and Luigi can counterattack by jumping over the Sworm. When it pops out, Mario and Luigi can whack its head with their hammer.
