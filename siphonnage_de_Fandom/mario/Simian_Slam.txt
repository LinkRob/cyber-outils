|games = ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''}}The '''Simian Slam''' ''('''Buttus bashium''')''' '''''is a technique used by the Kongs in the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. The main function of the Simian Slam is that it allows the certain kong to slam a switch with their face on it. The Simian Slam is first learned by [[Donkey Kong]] after completing [[Cranky Kong]]'s [[Cranky's Training Mini-games|training]]. When the Simian Slam is used, the kong generates a small, green shockwave upon impact. The Simian Slam can only pound green switches.

==Other Versions==
===Super Simian Slam===
The Super Simian Slam is a stronger version of the Simian Slam and it can be learned from Cranky in [[Fungi Forest]]. When the Super Simian Slam is used, a medium-sized shockwave generates upon impact. The Kongs can then pound blue switches with faces on them. The Kongs can still pound green switches.

===Super Duper Simian Slam===
The Super Duper Simian Slam is the strongest version of the Simian Slam and it can be learned from Cranky in [[Creepy Castle]]. When the Super Duper Simian Slam is used, the Kong can generate a large, red shockwave that generates upon impact. The Kongs can now pound red switches but can still pound blue and green switches.

==Trivia==
*The Simian Slam is arguably similar to the [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] using in the main Mario series.
