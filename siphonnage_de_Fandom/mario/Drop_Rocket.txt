'''Drop Rocket''' is the [[Super Strike]] used by [[Waluigi]] in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''.

==Description==
Upon activating the move, Waluigi does a karate-like pose while being surrounded by a purple nebulous halo. Waluigi then kicks the ball using both feet, causing the ball to zoom to the goal, leaving behind a nebulous trail in the process. The Robotic Nightmares on the [[Super Team]] perform a similar Super Strike, given that they are modeled after Waluigi.
