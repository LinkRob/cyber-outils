'''Beespies''' are an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong 3]]'' and its sequel ''[[Donkey Kong 3: Dai Gyakushū]]''.

==In {{series|Donkey Kong}}==
===''Donkey Kong 3''===
Starting from round 3, Beespies begin to appear from the hives that [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] shakes loose. Some Beespies are escorted by [[Buzzbee]] guards that increase their point value significantly. If a Beespy is sprayed once, it turns blue (green in the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] version), and spraying a Beespy again defeats it. Even after the second shot, Beespies split into four small stingers that can damage [[Stanley]].

===''Donkey Kong 3: Dai Gyakushū''===
Beespies return as enemies. They take the form of [[Attacker]]s when first sprayed, and can be defeated by spraying it a second time.
