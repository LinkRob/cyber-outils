The '''Drill Bit''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Profile ==
The Drill Bit is built by [[Smithy]] of the [[Smithy Gang]] and more was seen telling Smithy not to pound too much in his [[Smithy's Factory|factory]].

== Notes ==
* The Drill Bit originally would have appeared as a boss enemy of [[Land's End]] in the beta version of this game.
** It was hinted that they were associated with [[Yaridovich]] as their [[Psychopath]] message was going to be ''"This is for Yaridovich!"''.The Drill Bit could also use a special attack called [[Multiplier!]] which is also used by the Yaridovich [[Machine Made]]s.
