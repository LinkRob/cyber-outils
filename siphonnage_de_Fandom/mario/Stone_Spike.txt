'''Stone Spikes''' (also referred to as '''Blue Stone Spikes''' or simply '''Blue Spikes''') are a blue-colored variant of [[Spike]]s that debuted in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. They also appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', and their remake ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

== Physical appearance ==
Stone Spikes have swapped color placement compared to normal Spikes (who have blue hair and green skin), with green hair and blue skin. They keep their large lips, black shell, and big eyes.  

== Appearances ==

=== In the ''Super Mario'' series ===
In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]], [[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' (and [[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe|its remake]]), and ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', Stone Spikes usually stand on high platforms, where they throw a [[boulder]] downward every few seconds, similar to how a Spike throws a [[Spiked Ball]] every few seconds. Stone Spikes have green hair, a blue body and an orange mouth. They can be defeated by a stomp, a [[fireball]], an [[Ice Ball]], the [[Star]], or a rolling object like a [[Koopa Shell]] or a [[Barrel]]. 
=== In ''Paper Mario: The Origami King'' ===
{{Main|Origami Stone Spike}}
[[File:PMTOK Stone Spikes.png|thumb|180x180px|An [[Origami Stone Spike]] and a [[Paper Macho Stone Spike]]]][[Origami Stone Spike|Origami Stone Spikes]] appear as enemies in ''Paper Mario: The Origami King'' in [[? Island]] and [[Bowser's Castle#Paper Mario: The Origami King|Bowser's Castle]]. They use [[ground pound]] attacks, Ground Pound and Super Ground Pound, along with rock throwing attacks, Rocky Up Chuck and Hurly Burly. 

On [[? Island]] and [[Scuffle Island]], there are also [[Paper Macho Stone Spike|Paper Macho Stone Spikes]], the one in Scuffle Island being named the Rocky Rogue.  

=== In the ''Mario Tennis'' series ===
[[File:King Hisstocrat MTF.png|thumb|Stone Spikes assisting [[King Hisstocrat]]]]
In both ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis Fever]]'', Stone Spikes make cameos in the audience. Stone Spikes also appear as enemies in ''Fever''<nowiki/>'s Story Mode, assisting [[King Hisstocrat]] in his boss battle.

== Trivia ==
*Stone Spikes have a color scheme resembling both [[Tubba Blubba]]'s and [[Grubba]]'s.
*Stone Spikes make cameo appearances in ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis Fever]]'', as spectators within the crowd.
