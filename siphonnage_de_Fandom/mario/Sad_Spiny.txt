'''Sad Spiny''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sad Spinies are a variant of [[Spiny|Spinies]], which they resemble, except Sad Spinies are constantly crying due to being imbued with the [[Gloom]] [[vibe]].

===Behavior===
A Sad Spiny can move around a lot faster than regular Spinies.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
A Sad Spiny first appears in [[Ladida Plains 1-7]]. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] has to either quickly dodge or attack a Spiny to defeat it.
