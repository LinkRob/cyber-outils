'''The Bursh Off''' is [[Mona]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' in the "Strange" folder and returns as her microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player controls the toothbrush that the objective is to clean the teeth.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The player should brush four times.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The player should brush six times. The brush moves faster.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The player should brush eight times. The brush even moves faster.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
The microgame returns in ''WarioWare Gold'' as Mona's microgame again. The microgame is updated which the character is a boy or a girl that is holding a cup. The hand is also holding the toothbrush. If the player wins, the plain background appears.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The character is a brown-haired boy holding a blue cup. The player should brush four times.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The character is a long brown-haired girl holding a pink cup. The player should brush six times. The brush moves faster.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The character is a blonde girl holding a yellow cup. The player should brush eight times. The brush even moves faster.
