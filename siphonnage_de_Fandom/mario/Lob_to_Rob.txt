'''Lob to Rob''' is a Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the two players are competing to shoot down wooden panels. There are two types to score on: the [[Mushroom]] panels that reward 10 [[point]]s and the [[1-Up Mushroom]] panels that reward 30 points. However, there are [[Goomba]] panels where shooting one deducts 10 points from the player's score. Whoever gets the most points within the 30-second [[time limit]] is the winner.
