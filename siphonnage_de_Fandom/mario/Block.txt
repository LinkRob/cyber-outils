A '''Block''' is a type of object found in several ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. There are several different types of Blocks, each serving a specific purpose such as granting various powers or items or being used as platforms. The first type of Block to appear in the ''Mario'' franchise are [[POW Block]]s, which debuted in ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]'', but two of the most recurring Blocks—[[Question Block|? Blocks]] and [[Brick Block]]s—first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.
