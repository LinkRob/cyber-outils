'''Accordion Guy''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It is only fought alongside a [[Sombrero Guy]] and the [[Maraca Guy]]. Only one appears in the entire game and that is on the level [[Yoshi Sphinx]].

==Appearance==
The Accordion Guy looks like a regular [[Shy Guy]]. It carries an accordion. Like the Sombrero Guys and Maraca Guys beside it, it uses its musical powers to heal himself or increase his power.
