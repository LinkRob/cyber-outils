'''Bullet Bill Speed Trial''' is a sponsor based on a [[Bullet Bill]]. It debuted in ''Mario Kart 8'' and reappeared in its remake and ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It made cameo appearances as patches on two outfits in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''/''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ==
Bullet Bill Speed Trial made most appearances on courses.

=== List of appearances ===
* Trackside banners and signs in [[Mario Kart Stadium]]
* Trackside banners and signs in [[Mount Wario]]
* Signs in N64 [[Toad's Turnpike]]
* Signs in DS [[Wario Stadium]]
* Signs in Wii [[Grumble Volcano]]
* Trackside banners and signs in [[Excitebike Arena]]
* Cans, local information map, and Exit B and D3 in [[Super Bell Subway]]
== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The sponsor made plenty appearances as signs on tracks. It made two appearances on a kart and a glider. The Bullet Bill Speed Trial logo appears as a badge including gold badge and "hot shot" badge.
=== List of appearances ===
* Signs in [[DK Summit]]
* Signs in [[Kalimari Desert]]
* Baseball stadium wall banner in [[Los Angeles Laps 3]]
* Signs in [[Tokyo Blur]]
* Signs in [[Tokyo Blur 2]]
* [[Decal Streamliner]]
* [[Bullet Bill Parachute]]
* Badge 006
== In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' ==
The sponsor made two appearances on outfits in ''Odyssey''.
