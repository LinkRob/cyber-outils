'''Spooktastic World''' is the second [[world]] in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Spooktastic World exterior.png|thumb|left|200px|As shown from Treasure Square]]
Spooktastic World is a spooky/horror-themed area located inside a manor portal at [[Treasure Square]]. It has an evening setting, complete with dried, leafless trees and sharp end fences. The location contains a small castle, from where [[Wario]] has access to the [[Ghost House|haunted house]] known as [[Horror Manor]], and to the left is a circus tent that serves as an entrance to [[Wonky Circus]]. The swamp entrance on the right with giant green vines leads to [[Dual Dragon|Dual Dragon's Showdown]].

The world boss is Dual Dragon. There are heart symbols on each end of the gate, referencing the Dual Dragon's Japanese name.
