'''Lucy''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Lucy is a [[Flip-Flopian|Flopsider]] that is pink in color with a purple pony-tail.

==History==
Lucy can be found on in [[Flopside]] where she often acts as a rebel making her own rules and doing things her own way. She calls the player "babe" when the player talks to her.
