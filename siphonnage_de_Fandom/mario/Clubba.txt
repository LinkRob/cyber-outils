'''Clubba''' is an enemy found in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', appearing in [[Tubba Blubba's Castle]] and [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] in the former and in [[Gap of Crag]] in the latter. They use their maces to attack. Clubbas are members of the [[Koopa Troop]].

In the events of ''Paper Mario'', Clubbas guard Tubba Blubba's Castle and can be snuck past if they are sleeping. In ''Super Paper Mario'', they have similar traits as they did in the first game.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Clubbas are mainly green in color and have white hairs. They also have black saddles on their backs and are always seen carrying clubs. They resemble [[Spike (enemy)|Spikes]] because mainly green in color, the black saddle, and both hold something spiky.

===Known Clubbas===
*[[Tubba Blubba]]: A much larger and pale blue Clubba with a spiked shell. He was known for eating [[Boo]]s being invincible until Mario and company damaged his Heart and the two reunited. Many Clubbas also live in his castle.
*[[Grubba]]: A large blue Clubba who is the same color and much smaller than Tubba Blubba in his regular form. Grubba also used to run the [[Glitz Pit]]. Grubba also had another form known as [[Macho Grubba]] who is much bigger in appearance being orange in color mainly and  in this form he is slightly taller than Tubba Blubba . He was eventually defeated by Mario and his friends.
*[[White Clubba]]s: Stronger versions of Clubbas that are only found in the [[Shiver Region]].
