The '''Fried Shroom Plate''' is a [[recipe]] made by [[Saffron]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
It resembles the [[Fried Shroom]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and the [[Shroom Fry]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Unlike those two, the Fried Shroom Plate restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (status effect)|poison]]. It is made from a [[Shroom Shake]]. This meal can be used in four different recipes to create new ones.
