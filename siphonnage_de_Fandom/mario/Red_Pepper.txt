'''Red Peppers''' are items which first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They went on to reappear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==
Red Peppers resemble real life red peppers, but in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]], they are mainly used to increase the power of Mario or Luigi for a few turns. However, it also increases their weight make it harder for them to jump and dodge attacks. However, if a Red Pepper is used followed by a [[Green Pepper]], then the weights are canceled out and Mario or Luigi's power and defense will be increased for a certain amount of turns.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'', certain [[Item Shop]]s sell Red Peppers for 80 [[coin]]s.
