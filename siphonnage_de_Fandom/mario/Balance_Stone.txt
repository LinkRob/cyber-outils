The '''Balance Stone''' is an important item in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Description==
The Balance Stone is based on the [[Nunchuk]], and it is paired with the [[Form Baton]], which itself parodies the [[Wii Remote]]. The Balance Stone, like with the Form Baton, is composed of rock.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
The Balance Stone is the focus of [[Orbulon]]'s story, [[The Secret of the Balance Stone]]. Here, the Balance Stone crashes onto the [[Oinker]], which then falls near the [[Temple of Form]]. The Balance Stone lands right next to the Form Baton inside the building, then both items begin to glow. When Orbulon grabs them, the Temple of Form suddenly starts to rise. Orbulon's microgames use the form [[The Diner]], which not only utilizes the Wii Remote but also the Nunchuk, which is intended to correspond to the Balance Stone.
