'''Hoo's Wood 2-2''' is the second level of [[Hoo's Wood]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. The music changes from a F major into a D major music.

==Puzzle Piece==
In the second area, in front of the pipe jump to the deepest end to receive the Puzzle Piece.

==Toads==
* '''Toad 1''': Use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] vibe, go under and fly up under.
* '''Toad 2''': Rage to burn the bridge. Go to the left to fall of to the ground. Jump over the spikes and hit the ! Box.
* '''Toad 3''': After jumping on two grass ground levels, jump on the plain brownish-green platform.
