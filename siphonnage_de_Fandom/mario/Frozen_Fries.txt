The '''Frozen Fries''' is a [[recipe]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
[[Tayce T.]] can make them by frying up a mix of an [[Iced Potato]] and a [[Fire Flower]]. Iced Potatoes can only be obtained in [[Shiver City]] after sleeping at the [[Toad House|Toad House]]. This frozen snack restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and sells the best for 18 [[coin]]s in Shiver City.
