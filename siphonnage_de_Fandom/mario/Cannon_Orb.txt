A '''Cannon Orb''' is a special [[Orb]] that appears only in [[Pagoda Peak]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. Players can use it on themselves, causing a [[cannon]] to appear, which the player then enters. The cannon fires the player to a higher level of the mountain. Sometimes the player travels far, while other times the firing range is short. The number of [[space]]s traveled when using this Orb does not count toward the [[Bonus Star (Mario Party series)|Running Star]]. If the player uses the Orb on the last space of the board, the cannon will shoot them into the air before exploding as they fall down.
