'''Cross Lifts''' are objects in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and its [[Reissue|enhanced port]], ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

==Description==
Cross Lifts are [[platform]]s that expand and contract horizontally, and also conversely expands and contracts vertically. They appear only in the level [[Shifting-Floor Cave]], where they are extremely common throughout the [[level]], though they also reprise their role in the challenge [[The High Road]].
