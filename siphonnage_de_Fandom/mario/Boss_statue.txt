The '''boss statues''' are a series of four objects in ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''.

==Description==
Every boss statue is golden, and each of them depict one of the first four bosses, including [[Petey Piranha]], [[Egyptian Koopa]], [[Porcupuffer (boss)|Porcupuffer]], and [[Big Boo]] respectively.

==In ''Mario Pinball Land''==
[[File:Fiery Stage start.png|thumb|left|200px|The four boss statues in the first area of Fiery Stage in ''Mario Pinball Land'']]
The boss statues are located at the entrance of the [[Fiery Stage]]. Each of them can be unlocked by [[Mario]] using one of the [[Star Key (Mario Pinball Land)|Star Keys]]. In doing so, the boss statues turn in the direction of the gate, which opens, allowing Mario to continue further on into the Fiery Stage.
