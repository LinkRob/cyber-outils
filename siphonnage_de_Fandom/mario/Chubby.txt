'''Chubby''' is a [[Monty Mole]] residing on [[Moo Moo Farm]] in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''. He can often be seen hopping out of holes in the ground just like the other [[Monty Mole]]s, coming into contact with Chubby will knock racers about so be careful, players can knock Chubby away with the use of [[Shell]]s or by being under the effects of a [[Star]].

==Trivia==
*[[Moo Moo Farm]] returns in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' however it’s unknown if any of the Monty Moles in this game are Chubby.
*It is widely assumed that "Chubby" was simply the name of the Monty Mole species in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'' however the Instruction Booklet itself was actually referring to a specific Monty Mole named Chubby rather than that being the name of the species as a whole, the Player’s Guide for the game calls them Monty Moles.
