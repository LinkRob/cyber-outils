}}The '''Beetle Mania Toad''' is a character that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Appearance==
The Beetle Mania Toad is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with blue spots on his head.

==History==
The Beetle Mania Toad can be first found in the [[Inn]] of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] playing games on his [[Game Boy]]. If [[Mario]] tries to talk to him, the Beetle Mania Toad will yell at him to leave. However, once Mario completes [[Marrymore]] after dealing with [[Booster (character)|Booster]], the Beetle Mania Toad will sell him the Game Boy for 500 coins. Mario can now play the Beetle Mania minigame. Talking with the Beetle Mania Toad again will allow him to give instructions on how to play Beetle Mania.
