A '''Blue block''' is an object which appears in a few ''[[Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' platforming games. Blue blocks first appear in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
{{sectstub}}

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
[[File:WMODblueblock.png|thumb|left]]Blue blocks appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]'' with a different design and function. They are the counterpart of the [[Red block (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Red block]]s that can be broken with [[Dragon Wario]]'s fire breath attack. However, the Blue blocks cannot be destroyed by the normal breath. It is until Wario gets the [[Mastery Gem]] for Dragon Wario where he can then upgrade his fire breath and be able to destroy the Blue blocks.
