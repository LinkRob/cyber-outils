'''Green Spaces''' are a recurring object in the {{series|Mario Party}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
Green Spaces are a type of [[space]] that, unlike most other spaces, do not cause any event to occur whenever a character lands onto one. Just like with landing on any other space, landing on a Green Space ends the character's turn, followed by the character starting theirs.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party 5''===
Green Spaces exclusively appear in [[Card Party]] mode.

===''[[Mario Party 9]]''===
Unlike their debut appearance, Green Spaces appear on every regular boards, although their effect is retained.

===''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''===
For the most part, Green Spaces retain their usual effect. An exception to this is in [[Bowser's Peculiar Peak]], where landing on a Green Space causes whoever landed there to perform their losing animation, as the object of the board is avoid getting to [[Bowser]] first.

===''[[Mario Party 10]]''===
Green Spaces retain their usual effect, although like the [[Special Dice Block Space]], landing onto a Green Space can sometimes trigger a [[minigame]].
