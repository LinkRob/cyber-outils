'''Big Amps''' are a recurring enemy in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Big Amps are large black spheres with electricity surrounding them. They are a larger variant of [[Amp]]s. In most games, Big Amps have the same face as normal Amps, but in the ''Super Mario Galaxy'' series they don't have a face at all.

===Behavior===
Big Amps usually remain stationary, waiting for [[Mario]] or another character to walk into it, therefore causing it to electrocute the character. Some Big Amps move along a path and generally rotate on the spot.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
====''[[Mario Party 5]]''====
Big Amps first appear in [[Shock Absorbers (Mario Party 5)|Shock Absorbers]], along with regular Amps. Aside from being larger, Big Amps act identically to regular Amps, as if a character comes into contact with either type, they become electrocuted.

===''[[Mario Party 6]]''===
A Big Amp makes a cameo during the intro of [[Circuit Maximus]], being noticeably larger than the Big Amps in ''Mario Party 5''.

===''[[Mario Party 10]]''===
Big Amps appear in [[Kamek's Rocket Rampage]], where after [[Kamek]] loses half of his health, he uses Big Amps and [[Banzai Bill]]s to attack, the former of which deals two points per hit.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''===
Big Amps are commonly seen in Space or Tech [[galaxy|galaxies]] such as the [[Space Junk Galaxy]], [[Battlerock Galaxy]], [[Toy Time Galaxy]], and the [[Dreadnought Galaxy]]. They usually spin around a set path or around a small planet. Their larger size makes them harder to avoid but they are also much smaller. Avoid them at all costs because there is no attack that defeats them.

===''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''===
The Big Amp can be seen in galaxies such as the [[Sky Station Galaxy]] and the [[Space Storm Galaxy]]. They behave identically to how they did in previous game. They are once again indestructible however [[Co-Star Luma]] can stun them allowing Mario to get past easier.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' / ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
Big Amps are commonly found in [[Snake Block Tower]] and [[Stone-Snake Tower]]. For the first time, they have been given a face like their smaller counterparts. In this game, they hang idly in one location hoping [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) will hit them. In this game, the effects of a [[Starman]] can defeat them. Iceballs can also freeze them.

==Trivia==
*Coincidentally, Big Amp has a somewhat similar name to the [[Mega AMP]], a boss in ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]''.
