The '''Fire Statue''' is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and reappears in the [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]].

== Description ==
Fire Statues appear on [[Hoohoo Mountain]] and Fire Statues appear a few times.  These statues can be extinguished by Mario and Luigi. Mario can get water from the fountain and Luigi squirts it at the Fire Statue. Thus the Fire Statue extinguishes and the [[Mask (object)|Masks]] activate and produce [[Whirlwind|Whirlwinds]]. The Whirlwinds can be performed by Mario Bros. by the [[Spin Jump]].
