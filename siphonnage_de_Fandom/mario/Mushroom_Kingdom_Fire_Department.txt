The '''Mushroom Kingdom Fire Department''' is an organization only appearing in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Reign Storm]]."

==Description==
It is a regular fire department, except part of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], and all of its members are [[Toad (species)|Mushroom People]].

==In ''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''==
In "Reign Storm," [[Kooky von Koopa]]'s [[Robot Princess]] has ordered the fire department to extinguish every [[Fire Flower]] in the Mushroom Kingdom. The Mushroom Kingdom Fire Department are seen carrying out the job. unaware that the Robot Princess is an impostor.
