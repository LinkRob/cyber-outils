'''Fury Cat''' is an enemy in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. They appear only when [[Fury Bowser]] starts his active phase at night.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Fury Cats are shadowy versions of the [[kitten]]s in [[Lake Lapcat]]. They have a jet black body, red eyes, and spiky fur. They appear in big, medium, or small sizes.

=== Behavior ===
A Fury Cat remains stationary until [[Mario]] gets too close to it during his travels, at which point it enters its active phase. If Mario touches a Fury Cat, it slashes at Mario, inflicting damage. After Mario defeats Fury Bowser, the Fury Cats vanish and the kittens return. Fury Cats can also be defeated by being pushed into water.
