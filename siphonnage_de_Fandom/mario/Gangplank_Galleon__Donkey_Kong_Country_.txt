'''Gangplank Galleon''' is the final and fortieth area appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

==Summary==
In the game, Gangplank Galleon is visible from [[Donkey Kong Island]] in a little part of the distance after [[Kongo Jungle]] is finished. It comes closer and closer to Donkey Kong Island the further progress on the worlds [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]]. Though, this area can only be accessed once the boss battle with [[Master Necky Snr.]] is completed, the boss battle being the last area of the final world, [[Chimp Caverns]].

Mainly, this location is a major location as it is where the [[banana]]s from the Banana Hoard was taken from during the storyline of the game. Here, [[King K. Rool]] is battled for the bananas to be taken back. Once he is defeated, the [[Kong]]s talk to [[Cranky Kong|Cranky]] in his cabin (all the Kongs on the ship in the [[Game Boy Advance]] version). Also, the credits take place on this area in the Game Boy Advance remake rather than Donkey Kong's Treehouse from the original.
