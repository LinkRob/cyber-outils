'''Sumo of Doom-o''' is a Duel [[minigame|mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Layout==
The characters use the same vehicles as in [[Tricky Tires]], and gameplay takes place on a quadrilateral fighting platform. A [[Thwomp]] is positioned in the upper center of the stage.

==Opening==
The characters emerge inside their vehicles from doors on top of the arena. A Thwomp then pounds the ground, causing the ramps that lead to the exits to collapse.

==Gameplay==
Each character has to try and knock the other character off the quadrilateral fighting platform. The player can move their character's vehicle by using both Control Sticks, with the {{button|gcn|Stick}} controlling the left set of tires while the {{button|gcn|C}} controls the right set of tires. Every few seconds, the Thwomp pounds the floor, causing the rim of the platform to fall off, and the Thwomp stops pounding if only the middle part of the platform is remaining.

Whoever knocks the other character into a [[pit]] is the winner. If neither character knocks the other one off when the 30-second [[time limit]] is up or both characters fall off simultaneously, the minigame ends in a [[Draw|tie]].

==Ending==
The winner stands outside their vehicle and performs their victory animation.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Control left wheels
* {{button|gcn|C}}: Control right wheels

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Use your vehicle to push your opponent off the crumbling platform. Whoever knocks the other player off first wins!''
* '''Advice 1''': ''Tilt {{button|gcn|Stick}} and {{button|gcn|C}} to steer your vehicle. Tilt both upward to move forward.''
* '''Advice 2''': ''The stage will crumble away and get smaller each time the Thwomp lands.''
