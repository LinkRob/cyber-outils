The '''Piranha Plant Orb''' is an [[orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Piranha_Plant_Orb.jpg|thumb|left|''Mario Party 6'']]
The Piranha Plant Orb is the successor to the [[Piranha Plant Capsule]] from ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. It functions almost exactly the same, as opponents who land on a [[space]] with a Piranha Plant Orb are chewed on by a [[Piranha Plant]] and spat out, causing them to lose half their coins, which are then transferred to the space's owner. The main different from the Piranha Plant Capsule is that the user cannot use the Piranha Plant Orb on themselves, due to the resulting negative effects.

The Piranha Plant Orb costs fifteen [[coin]]s to purchase at an [[Orb hut]].
