The '''Cookbook''' is a key item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
[[File:Cookbook PM sprite.png|frame|left]]
[[Tayce T.]], the cook in this game, will only make recipes that require one item to make. In Chapter 4, [[Mario]] can an item that allows her to "spice up" her cooking. After feeding Gourmet Guy some [[cake]], he flies up into the air and drops his cookbook. Mario can pick it up and obtain this item. Later on, he can give it to Tayce T. in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and she can make new items for Mario that requires two ingredients.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
The Cookbook is found in [[Creepy Steeple]], a location in the fourth chapter. Mario makes his way here and notices a small hole by the front door on the inside. Mario can use his ability to become a cylinder and roll under the hole thus entering a secret room. In one of the chests is this item. Mario can then give this item to [[Zess T.]] anytime he meets her. Zess T., so long as she is in possession of this item, can create dishes that need two ingredients to be made.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
''Super Paper Mario'' does not actually have a cookbook as an item, but the first cook whom Mario meets cannot make items that need two ingredients to make. [[Saffron]], a cook in [[Flopside]], will perform this instead. She, like the cookbooks in the last games, cannot be accessed until after chapter 4. Mario needs [[Fleep]] to access Flopside the first time.
