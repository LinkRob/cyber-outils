'''World 6''' is the sixth world of ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. It is a volcanic-themed world with nine levels. Like in the previous world, eight levels are compulsory but there is [[Skeleton Goonies' Lava Lair|one optional level]] after all 40 [[Smiley Flower|Smiley Flowers]] in the previous levels of World 6. This is the final world of the game.
