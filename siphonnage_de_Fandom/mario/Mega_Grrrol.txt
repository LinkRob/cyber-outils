}}The '''Mega Grrrol''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. They look like normal [[Grrrol]]s, except that they are bigger, have slightly smaller eyes and mouth, and have puffy cheeks. In ''New Super Mario Bros. U'', they appear in [[Grinding-Stone Tower]], while in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', they appear in [[Smashing-Stone Tower]], [[Underground Grrrols]] and in [[Stone-Snake Tower]].
