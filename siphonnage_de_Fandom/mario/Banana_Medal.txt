'''Banana Medals''' are special items that appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==History==
Banana Medals can be obtained after collecting 75 regular bananas for a Kong in each level (except for [[DK Isles]] and [[Hideout Helm]]). That means that 5 Banana Medals can be obtained per stage. Although it seems they serve no purpose at first, it is revealed that at least 15 Banana Medals is needed to play [[Cranky Kong]]'s [[Jetpac]] game in order to get the [[Rareware Coin]] which is needed to compete the game. At Hideout Helm, Banana Medals can be obtained after a Kong completes his/her mini-games.
