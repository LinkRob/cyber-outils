|textcolor = ffffff}}'''Time Out''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. This move can be used once [[Mario]] defeats [[Huff N. Puff]] at [[Cloudy Climb]] and frees [[Klevar]]. It requires 2 [[Star Power|SP]] to used.

==Description==
Klevar appears and chants a spell that may [[Immobilized|immobilize]] enemies for a number of turns. However, this move is useless against most bosses and only tends to work on weaker enemies. Time Out is similar to the [[Stop Watch]] item and the [[Clock Out]] technique from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
