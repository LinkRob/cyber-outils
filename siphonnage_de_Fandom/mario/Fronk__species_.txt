'''Fronks''' are a species in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', and have since appeared in every game of the series. The most notable Fronk is a pet owned by [[9-Volt]] of the [[Fronk|same name]].

==Physical description==
Fronks are small humanoids with short black stick-shaped arms and legs and a disproportionately large head. Their general appearance appears to be based on that of sprites from older video games or {{w|pixel art}} in general. Fronks are usually yellow, but they have also been colored red or blue. 9-Volt's pet Fronk has the same appearance as yellow Fronks in general.

==In the ''WarioWare'' series==
===''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''===
Fronks appear in three [[microgame]]s, including [[Mona]]'s [[Sole Man]], [[Orbulon]]'s [[Find My Behind]] and [[Kat & Ana]]'s [[Blow Hole Bridge]].

===''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''===
Up to four Fronks appear in the 4-player microgame [[Allergies Return]], where they have to stay away from a woman who tries to trap them with the mucus from her runny nose.

===''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''===
[[File:Tread Carefully WWTouched screenshot.png|thumb|x200px|Gameplay of the Tread Carefully microgame]]
A small group of Fronks appear in one microgame titled [[Tread Carefully (microgame)|Tread Carefully]], which only appears in stages with microgames that involve blowing, such as some of the games in 9-Volt's and [[Wario-Man]]'s sets, as well as all of [[Mike]]'s games. The microgame serves as a trap to those who blow into the microphone as soon as a microgame begins, so if the player blows at all, the Fronks fall and they lose the microgame.
