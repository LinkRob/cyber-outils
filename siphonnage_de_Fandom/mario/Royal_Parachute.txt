The '''Royal Parachute''' is a High-End glider sponsored by [[Kingdom Café]] in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The Royal Glider made its debut in the first [[Winter Tour (2019)|Winter Tour]] in 2019. is a High-End glider with a special skill called [[Red Shell Plus]].
