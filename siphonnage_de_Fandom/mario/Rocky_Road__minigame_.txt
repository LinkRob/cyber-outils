'''Rocky Road''' is a 2-vs-2 minigame introduced in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and returning in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

== Layout ==
The minigame takes place with each team's car driving on the road near the seaside. In ''Mario Party 6'', the minigame can take place during daytime or nighttime, depending on the setting. In ''Mario Party: The Top 100'' and ''Mario Party Superstars'', the minigame always takes place during daytime. In ''Mario Party 6'', each team is driven by a [[Shy Guy]], but in the minigame's reappearances it is [[Toad (character)|Toad]], who meets up with [[Toadette]] at the end.

==Gameplay==
The objective for each team is to have their car reach the end first. They come across five sets of [[boulder]]s along the way, encountered in the order of: two small ones at the start, one small boulder, three small boulders, two medium boulders, and lastly one large boulder. Because the boulders are blocking the way, the car stops moving whenever it reaches them, and the teammates have to cooperatively destroy them so that their car can continue down the road. A boulder can be destroyed using several [[punch]]es and [[kick]]s, the latter of which is only usable while [[jump]]ing into the air. Larger boulders require more hits to break. Once a set of boulders have been destroyed, the teammates enter the car and continue onward. The first team to make it to the end is the winner.

If a character hits their teammate, they are briefly slowed. In ''Mario Party 6'', hitting a teammate additionally stuns them for a few seconds, and even when the road is cleared, the car does not move forward until the stunned character also enters the car.

==Ending==
Both characters on the winning team continue down the road in their car, waving at the screen.

==Controls==
*Control Stick: Move
*A button: Jump
*B button: Punch
*B button (in the air): Kick
