The '''Dark Dark Boo''' is a dark sub-species of a [[Boo]] which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
It has an attack power of four and appears in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. They attack by sneaking up on Mario and he can counter this by doing a [[flip]] to avoid the Dark Dark Boo.
