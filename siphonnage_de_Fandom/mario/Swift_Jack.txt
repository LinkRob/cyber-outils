Greatly increases Slipstream boost time and points earned for performing one.|locked = {{y}}|first=''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' (2019)}}The '''Swift Jack '''is an obtainable spotlight kart in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It was added in the London Tour and could only be obtained by subscribing to the Gold Pass, if you had 310 [[Grand Star]]s.



==Special attributes==
Its color comes from the British flag. Its model comes from the [[Wild Wing]], but unlike the [[Wild Wing]] its advantage is Slipstream Plus.
