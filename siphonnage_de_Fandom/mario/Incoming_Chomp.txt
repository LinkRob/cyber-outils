'''Incoming Chomps''' are a large [[Chomp]] variant with a high [[jump]]ing ability. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', in which they are first encountered in the level [[Watch Out Below!]]. 

==History==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''===
[[File:WatchOutBelowGBA.jpg|thumb|left|[[Pink Yoshi]] and Baby Mario narrowly avoiding an Incoming Chomp.]]
Incoming Chomps first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and it's [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|remake]], in this game, they primarily appear in the second level of [[World 1 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 1]] [[Watch Out Below!]]. The Incoming Chomps are first seen on an island in the background, but as a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] approaches, one will [[jump]] high into the air to the foreground where Yoshi is and will try to land on him. In doing so, the Incoming Chomp destroys a large chunk of the earth creating a pit. Despite the large size of an Incoming Chomp, a Yoshi does not lose [[Baby Mario]] if he touches it, although the impact of the Incoming Chomp's fall tries to forcefully push the Yoshi down with it. Thankfully, it is easy to gauge where an Incoming Chomps will land as there are signs with an Incoming Chomp's face imprinted on it, these signs indicate where the Incoming Chomps will land. Incoming Chomps are completely indestructible and cannot be destroyed in any way. Incoming Chomps also appear in [[Marching Milde's Fort]].

===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
[[Image:Incoming Chomps DS.jpg|thumb|right|A [[Green Yoshi]] and [[Baby DK]] jumping out of the way for an Incoming Chomp.]]
Incoming Chomps return in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. In this game, they behave the same as they did previously however this time, they are much larger (possibly to distinguish them from the smaller [[Stompin' Chomp]]) meaning they can destroy larger chunks of the floor, in some cases, they can even knock [[Stork Stop]]s down into the pits rendering them unusable. An Incoming Chomp once again cannot knock a baby off his back but they can shove him into the pits below, most of these pits have wind gusts, if Yoshi has [[Baby Peach]], he can fly back out of the pits. Unlike the previous game, there are no signs to indicate where the Incoming Chomps will land however they always descend down the same pits they create. Just as before, Incoming Chomps are indestructible and cannot be destroyed however tossing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at one will cause it to appear in the [[Museum]]. Incoming Chomps are found in the Giant Enemy Exhibit.

===''Yoshi's New Island''===
Although Incoming Chomps don't appear in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' a similar type of [[Chomp]] called the [[Flaming Chomp]] does. Flaming Chomps behave identically to Incoming Chomps except they emerge from volcanoes, are engulfed in flames, and will instantly kill a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]].

==Trivia==
*Incoming Chomps are one of the few enemies from both ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' to not return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', instead being replaced with the more dangerous [[Flaming Chomp]].
*The Japanese name for the Incoming Chomp is "Wanwan Dosun", Dosun is also the Japanese name of the [[Thwomp]] which is a nod to the Incoming Chomp's behaviors being similar to the Thwomp's.
