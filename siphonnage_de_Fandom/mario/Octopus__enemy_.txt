'''Octopus''' (オットー) is an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]]. It only appears in [[Stage 7-2]], where it wanders underwater attempting to grab [[Mario]] with its tentacles. If Mario is caught it will enable him to only walk and jump and cannot pull switches until the Octopus releases him.
