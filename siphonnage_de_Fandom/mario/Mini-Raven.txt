'''Mini-Ravens''' (also spelt as '''Mini Ravens''') are smaller versions of [[Raven]]s found in games of the ''[[Yoshi (series)|Yoshi]]'' series. Debuting in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and later appearing in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', they are rare enemies only found in a few levels such as [[Shifting Platforms Ahead]], [[Raphael the Raven's Castle]], and [[Beware the Spinning Logs]], they walk along platforms at a quick speed and can also walk along the walls and up ceilings too like their large cousins. Running into a Mini-Raven will cause damage however, they can be easily defeated by jumping on them, swallowing them, or by tossing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at them. Unlike most enemies, swallowing a Mini-Raven will not produce an egg. 

Mini-Ravens return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' behaving as they did previously however this time, Yoshi can produce an egg by swallowing a Mini-Raven, the ways to defeat a Mini-Raven are the same as before and they are only found in one level, [[Eggstraordinary Terrain]]. 

==Trivia==
*Mini-Ravens may be baby versions of Ravens.
*Mini-Ravens don't appear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', however, the regular Ravens are about the same size as a Mini-Raven but are considered regular Ravens by the game.
