'''Vamp''' is a Status Effect that can occur in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. While it is active, characters who hit the victim will gain [[Heart Point|hit points]]. [[Luigi]]'s [[Rabbid Luigi|Rabbid Self]] can inflict the condition through his Bworbs and Vamp Dash, while [[Mario]]'s [[Rabbid Mario|Rabbid Self]] can inflict the condition through his Boom Shots and Melees. Peek-a-boos, Smashers (only in [[Spooky Trails]]) and the [[Lava Queen]] can inflict the condition.

== Trivia ==
*This condition's name is rather ironic: the inflicted character takes damage while giving health to enemies, whereas real Vamps parasitically sap the life from others.
*A similar condition called [[Lifesteal]] can be found in [[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope|''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'']], but while Vamp lasts for a few turns, Lifesteal only counts for the attack that triggered it.
