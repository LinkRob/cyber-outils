'''Balloon Fight''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and reappears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It is based off of the game of the same name.

==Stage design==
You can go over to the blast zone on the bottom platform and go to the other side of the stage.
{{sectstub}}

===Features===
*'''Big Fish:''' A very large fish that is found in the waters of the Balloon Fight stages. When a player is near the water, the fish will leap up and attempt to drag them into the water resulting in a KO. A player has a chance to fight off the fish by mashing the buttons but the success rate drops the more they are damaged.

==Origin==
As stated above, Balloon Fight is a reference to the game of the same name and the different stages are the first three stages of the game.
