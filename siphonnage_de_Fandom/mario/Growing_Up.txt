'''Growing Up''' is a Free-for-All mini-game featured in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each player must push the button on their [[Wii Remote]] that matches the one shown on the block, after the block spins through some like a roulette. If they push the correct button, they can hit their block and a [[vine]] will grow out of it. Whoever press the correct button the fastest gets a greater height increase for their vine than others who correctly hit the same button afterward. There are eight rounds, giving each player eight chances. When the game is over, the players climb their vines and stop when the reach the top of each one. Whoever grew the longest vine is the winner.
