The '''Fare Flier''' is a High-End glider in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Fare Flier made its debut in [[New York Tour]] in 2019. It is a High-End glider with a special skill called [[Super Horn Plus]].
