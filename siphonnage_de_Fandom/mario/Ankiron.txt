'''Ankirons''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
An Ankirons has the appearance of large, armored, red turtle with green cannons built into its shell. They are among the enemies to have several variants, and the standard Ankirons only appear in [[Excitement Central]].

===Powers and abilities===
An Ankiron can shoot out an explosive [[bomb]] with a human-like face. Because of their weight, an Ankiron can only turn in place. It can retreat into its shell to protect itself from [[Wario]]'s attacks. If Wario attacks an Ankiron while it is in a retreated state, the Ankiron comes out and shoots out ammunition.) in all directions.
