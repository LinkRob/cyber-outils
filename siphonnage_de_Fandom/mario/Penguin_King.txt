'''The Penguin King''' is a supporting character of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.<ref name="trailer">{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KydqdKKyGEk |title=The Super Mario Bros. Movie | Official Teaser Trailer |accessdate=2022-10-07 |work=YouTube |date=2022-10-07}}</ref> He was first seen in the opening of the film along with his soldiers, who follow him into battle and fight to protect his kingdom. He is one of the distressed characters.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
The Penguin King is a blue [[penguin]] that wears a golden crown and a furry black robe.

=== Personality traits ===
The Penguin King is shown to be good. He is a great leader and fights alongside his men. The trailers showing him and his people captured show his distrust for [[Lumalee (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Lumalee]].

== Biography ==
Penguin king rules the Snow Kingdom. His kingdom guarded the [[Super Star (Illumination)|Super Star]].

He and his army fought against [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]], [[Kamek (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Kamek]], [[Koopa General]] and his army. However, Bowser destroyed their castle while Koopa General restrained the Penguin King. Bowser stole the Super Star.
Penguin King along with the penguins were imprisoned in Bowser's Castle. He also met [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]].

Meanwhile, after [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]] agreed to "marry" Bowser. Peach was alarmed to see the prisoners along with Penguin King being planned to be publicly burned in the lava during the wedding. In response, Peach used the Ice Flower she hid in the bouquet to fight Bowser and froze the lever that brings the prisoners to the lava. However, the lever freed from the ice after Peach reverted back to her normal form. [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] and [[Donkey Kong (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Donkey Kong]] intervened the scene and saved the prisoners including the Penguin King from death. It is unknown what happened to him and the other penguins after, but its possible they rebuilt the Snow Kingdom or relocated to live somewhere else.
