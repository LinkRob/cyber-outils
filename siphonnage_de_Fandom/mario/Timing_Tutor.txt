'''Timing Tutor''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear this badge by using one [[Badge Point|BP]]. In doing so, Mario can easily find the timing for stylish commands. [[Charlieton]] sells the Timing Tutor to Mario for 120 [[coin]]s.
