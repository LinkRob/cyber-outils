'''Fast Thwomp'''s are enemies appearing in the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' series. These enemies are basically [[Thwomp]]s infused with the power of a [[Prankster Comet|Fast Foe Comet]].

==Behaviour==
Fast Thwomps make their only appearances in the [[Beach Bowl Galaxy]] (on the Cyclone Stone planet in the mission [[Prankster Comet|Fast Foes on the Cyclone Stone]]) of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and the [[Stone Cyclone Galaxy]] of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. Their behaviour is nearly identical to normal cousins, but the Fast Foe Comet has made them twice as fast as their normal counterparts. They cannot be destroyed. In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', Fast Thwomps have their speed increased to quadruple the standard Thwomp, making them much harder to deal with. However, Mario can flip a nearby switch to turn a Fast Thwomp into a "Slow Thwomp", whose speed is 1/8 that of the standard Thwomp.
