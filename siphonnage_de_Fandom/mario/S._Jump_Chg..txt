'''S. Jump Chg.''' (short for '''Smash Jump Charge''') is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
When S. Jump Chg. is equipped, [[Mario]] can charge his [[Jump]] attacks to deal extra damage on the next turn. This badge is a stronger version of the [[Jump Charge]] badge, and using the S. Jump Chg. allows Mario to deal three extra points of damage from his Jump attack. However, using S. Jump Chg. costs four [[Flower Point|FP]] per charge. S. Jump Chg. can even be used alongside the Jump Charge badge, giving Mario even more attack power. The S. Jump Chg. badge can be found on a cloud on [[Cloudy Climb]] to the left side. This badge can be useful in the upcoming [[Huff N. Puff]] fight.
