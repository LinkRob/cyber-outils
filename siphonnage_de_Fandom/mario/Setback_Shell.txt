A '''Setback Shell''', named '''Knockback Shell''' in Europe and Australia, is an item in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
A Setback Shell has the exact appearance of a [[Green Shell]], and it is exclusively found in [[Perilous Palace Path]]. Upon using a Setback Shell, a character can select an opponent, who is then knocked back two [[space]]s.

==In ''Mario Party: Island Tour''==
A character has a chance of obtaining a Setback Shell from either landing on an [[Item Space]] or winning a minigame and selecting the item as a reward.
