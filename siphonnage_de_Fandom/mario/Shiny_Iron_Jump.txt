The '''Shiny Iron Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

== Description ==
Shiny Iron Jump stickers look similar to [[Iron Jump]] stickers except the boot is now silver in color. It also has a silver background. They only take up on sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]].

== Notes ==
Shiny Iron Jump stickers are very helpful when fighting strong spiked-enemies such as [[Spiny]]s. They are found commonly laying around in levels of [[World 4 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star|World 4]] and [[World 5 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|World 5]].
