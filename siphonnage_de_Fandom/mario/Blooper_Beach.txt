'''Blooper Beach''' is a [[board]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. It is the fourth map in Story mode. For the final time in Solo mode, the player will go up against either [[Shy Guy]] or [[Kamek]] and the possibility of one or two other characters. If the player is paired up with either [[Shy Guy]] or [[Kamek]], this ensures the player must become the superstar or tie to move on to the next board.

== Layout ==
It takes place at the seaside. In the first segment of the map, the [[Dolphin]] is in front of the vehicle which is marked with a star on a space. The player who catches up to the dolphin earns five [[Mini Star]]s. Afterwards, a [[Sushi]] will approach and it can move four spaces behind the vehicle. If it catches the captain, she/he lose half of her/his Mini Stars.

In the second segment after the fort, the players can participate a Captain Event to grab Mini Stars. Approaching a Blooper statue, if the player lands on an Unlucky space, the Blooper statue will create a whirlpool and trap the vehicle. The captain can escape by landing on [[Dash Space]]s. However, she/he can land on unlucky spaces which grants her/him [[Mini Ztar]]s.

In the final segment after the flag marked by [[Green Toad]], Bowser will give the player a choice if the player wants [[Bowser Space]]s If the player were to say "yes", he will transforms plenty of spaces into Bowser Spaces. If "no", he will transform a few spaces into Bowser Spaces. If a player happens to land on a Lucky Space, everyone can participate an event where they go to a star-shaped island and grab as much Mini Stars as they want.

In Story mode, the two default bosses are [[Cheep Cheep]] at the fort and [[Blooper]] beyond the Bowser gate.
