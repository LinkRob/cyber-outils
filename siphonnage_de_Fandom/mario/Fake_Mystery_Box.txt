A '''Fake Mystery Box''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
A Fake Mystery Box has an appearance of a regular [[Mystery Box]], but when the player's character approaches a fake Mystery Box, it turns into several [[Boo]]s, summons one [[Big Boo]] behind the player's character, and disappears.
