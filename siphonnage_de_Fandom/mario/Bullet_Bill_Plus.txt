The '''Bullet Bill Plus''' is a special skill in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is a special skill that is available on certain gliders granting the chance of obtaining a [[Bullet Bill]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The Bullet Bill Plus is available on certain gliders as a special skill. While the gliders grant chances of getting a Bullet Bill, the rarity of the glider affects the amount of points for the racer.
