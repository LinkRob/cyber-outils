'''Mastah Mosquitos''' are giant mosquito enemies that appear in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.

==Behavior==
A Mastah Mosquito tries to attack [[Wario]] by stinging him. Wario can mainly defeat a Mastah Mosquito by either perform a [[Dash Attack|Barge]] or a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] onto it, except for at its stinger because this damages him. Alternatively, a Mastah Mosquito can be defeated from [[Sea Dragon Wario|Sea Dragon]] or [[King Dragon Wario]]'s flames.
