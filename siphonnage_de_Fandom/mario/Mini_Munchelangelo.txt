'''Mini Munchelangelos''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
Mini Munchelangelos are smaller versions of [[Munchelangelo]]s that can only be found in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]] near the [[Sphinx (character)|Sphinx]]. Like with the larger Munchelangelos, Mini Munchelangelos attack by biting at [[Wario]]. They can be beaten with any damaging attack.
