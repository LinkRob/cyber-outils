'''Toad''' is one of the two tritagonists (alongside [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]]) of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]], serving as the tritagonist of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KydqdKKyGEk |title=The Super Mario Bros. Movie | Official Teaser Trailer |accessdate=2022-10-07 |work=YouTube |date=2022-10-07}}</ref> and the secondary tritagonist of ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. He uses a frying pan as paraphernalia.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Toad's appearance resembles to that of his [[Toad (character)|video game counterpart]], but far more detailed, like his pants having laces on the front and having more defined (yet stubby) legs. He also carries a backpack. In the [[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie|sequel movie]], he wears a red bandana making him resemble more with [[Captain Toad]].

===Personality traits ===
Toad is adorable and cheerful. Despite this light-hearted demeanor of his, he can also have a biting wit and isn’t afraid to go to battle to defend the people he cares about.

== Biography ==
Toad lived in the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] and is a friend of [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]].

Toad met [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] when he arrived at the [[Mushroom Forest (Illumination)|Mushroom Forest]]. Mario told Toad about [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]], who was trapped in the [[Dark Lands]], much to Toad’s shock, who explained the place to the plumber. He then brought Mario over to the Mushroom Kingdom, alerting the citizens about Mario’s situation.

Toad and Mario visited Princess Peach in her castle. Surprised to meet another human, she brought him to the [[Training Course (Illumination)|training course]] teaching him how to complete it before he completes it. If Mario completes the course, Peach will bring him along. After Mario's many failed attempts, he nearly reached the end but Peach brought him along with Toad to their journey.
