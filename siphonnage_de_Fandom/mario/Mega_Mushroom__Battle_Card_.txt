|name=Mega Mushroom (Battle Card)}}'''Mega Mushroom''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Mega Mushroom is the super-strong version of [[Mario]]'s healing ability. It is a huge red and white speckled mushroom with an orange/tan spiral background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 150 coins (painted)

Sell Price: 75 coins (painted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Mega Mushroom is first seen in [[The Emerald Circus]], but can also found in [[Black Bowser's Castle]].
