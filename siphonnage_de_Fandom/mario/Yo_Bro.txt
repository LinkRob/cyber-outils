'''Yo Bro''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are encountered in [[Woohoo Hooniversity]], although only in battle among other enemies such as [[Laser Snifit]]s.

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
It is a variant of a [[Hammer Bro]] that has two dangerous spiked yo-yos, a red yo-yo and a green yo-yo. They wear a purple bucket-like hat, green skin, yellow long sleeve shirt, pants and a gold vest.

=== Etymology ===
Yo Bros. are types of "Hammer Bros." and have two "yo-yos" mixed together "Yo Bro".

== Battle ==
In the battle, they are healed with Mario's [[Firebrand]] and critically attacked with Luigi's [[Thunderhand]]. When it is their turn to attack, they bounce the red or the green yo-yo and at the second bounce, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] should jump.
