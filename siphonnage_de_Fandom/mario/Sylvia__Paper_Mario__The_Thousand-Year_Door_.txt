'''Sylvia''' is a female [[Bob-omb]] character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Appearance==
Sylvia has a silver body and white eyes, both appearing to have an eyelash within them. Sylvia's clothing items consists of a dark pink hat and shoes, with the former also having two light pink feathers extended from it.

==Biography==
Sylvia is married to [[Goldbob]], and the two eventually have a son named [[Bub (character)|Bub]]. All of them live at [[Poshley Heights]].

==History==
Sylvia and her family are first seen on the [[Excess Express]] where Sylvia and Goldbob are arguing what to get Bub for his birthday. Bub simply wants an [[autograph]] from the train conductor and giving him it stops the two from arguing. During Day 3 on the Excess Express, Sylvia is one of many people captured by the [[Smorg]]. After the Smorg has been defeated by [[Mario]] and company, Sylvia, Goldbub, and Bub make it back to Poshley Heights back to their home.
