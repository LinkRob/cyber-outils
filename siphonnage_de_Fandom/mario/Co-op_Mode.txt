'''Co-op Mode''' is a mode only in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is when 2 players team up to race on [[Grand Prix]]. The 1st player drives, while the 2nd player throws items back and in front. Press 'Z' to switch from driving and throwing. No more than 2 players can play in Co-Op Grand Prix. Other than having two human players in your kart, the mode is the same as regular GP. The special ability for playing co-op mode is the [[Double Dash!!]], which can be used at the start of the race.
