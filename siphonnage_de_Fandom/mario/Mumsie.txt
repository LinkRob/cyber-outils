'''Mumsies''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They are typically found in desert levels.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mumsies are mummy-like enemies that wear a very tall headgear made of bandages and have greenish-blue feet and hands. Mumsies also have a loose wrapping on their backside, and the player's character can pull it to defeat them.

==Trivia==
*Mumsies strongly resemble [[Chincho]]s, a mummy species from ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.
