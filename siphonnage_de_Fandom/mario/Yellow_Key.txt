The '''Yellow Key''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Yellow Key, as the name states, is a yellow key that has a square-shaped design on the end.

==History==
The Yellow Key is one of three keys that can be found in [[Allergia Gardens]] (along with the [[Red Key (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Red Key]] and the [[Blue Key (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Blue Key]]). The Yellow Key can be found in a purple treasure chest in a room with [[Ratfael]]s in it and it can be used on two different yellow doors.
