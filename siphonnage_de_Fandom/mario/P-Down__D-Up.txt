}}'''P-Down, D-Up''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. [[Mario]] can wear this badge at the cost of two [[Badge Point|BP]], and in doing so, Mario gains one point of defense at the cost of a point of his attack power.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:P_down_D_up_badge.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', this badge is found in the [[Crystal Palace]]. Inside of the upper area of the palace, Mario can find this badge under the wooden 'X' near the very beginning of the dungeon.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', P-Down, D-Up reappears, and is found in the [[Pirate's Grotto]] on [[Keelhaul Key]]. This badge can also be obtained by using [[Ms. Mowz]]. Ms. Mowz can steal these badges from [[Dark Craw]]s and [[X-Naut PhD]]s.
