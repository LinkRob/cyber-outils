|first = ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''}}The '''King Kruiser II''' is a vehicle that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. It also serves as the location for the final boss in the game.

==History==
The King Kruiser II was under the ownership of [[King K. Rool]]. After the Kongs managed to deactivate the [[Blast-o-Matic]], K. Rool attempted to escape in his vehicle, the King Kruiser II, that emerged from within his fortress of [[Crocodile Isle (Donkey Kong 64)|Crocodile Isle]]. Unfortunately for him, [[K. Lumsy]] was recently freed and chases K. Rool in his Kruiser around [[DK Isles]]. K. Lumsy was able to knock down the King Kruiser II which landed to the side of DK Isles. The Kongs can then enter inside of it to do battle with K. Rook as [[King Krusha K. Rool]].
