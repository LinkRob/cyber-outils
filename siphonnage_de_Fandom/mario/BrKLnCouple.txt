'''BrKLnCouple''', initially called '''BrKlyn Couple''', is the username of two unnamed customers of Super Mario Bros. Plumbing who appear in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

=== Personality traits ===
The couple seem friendly with the Mario Bros. offering their call to help fix the bathroom, however it was short when they're shocked to see Mario pulls Frances out of the toilet. It is unclear how they reacted next, though in SMB Plumbing Testimonials, the couple blame the plumbers as they would've given one star only to give two stars due to Frances love for them. The couple would then change their minds as they cheered Mario and Luigi for stopping Bowser and saved Brooklyn. 

== Biography ==
The couple was [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]]'s first client to call the [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]] service. The couple members are African-American and own a dog named [[Francis (Illumination)|Francis]], who ends up getting mad at Luigi for accidentally stepping on and breaking his dog bone. After Mario and Luigi quickly repair the couple's sink, Francis attacks them in the bathroom, causing the pipes they repaired damaged, eventually ruining and flooding everything the bathroom. They grab Francis, who almost falls down on the ground down, unaware the couple not seeing them. The couple members then enter the room in time to see Mario pulling Francis out of the toilet with a plunger, much to their shock and horror.

One day later, both the couple and their dog are later seen in the crowd of people cheering for Mario and Luigi, after the final battle with [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]].
