'''Capture Kongs''' are a recurring enemy in the {{series|Mario vs. Donkey Kong}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Capture Kongs resemble [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], except they have gray fur, dark skin, red eyes, a purple necktie with a red "C," and a large, golden sack on their back. In ''[[Mario and Donkey Kong: Minis on the Move]]'', Capture Kongs instead have a "W" on their tie, a barrel on their back instead of a sack, and larger, red eyes. In ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars]]'', this design was reverted.

===Behavior===
A Capture Kong carries a sack on its back, where it stuffs [[Mini]]s into if they come into contact with a Capture Kong. Capture Kongs walk back and forth across level terrain, including [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]]. Any Minis captured by Capture Kong cannot be used until a different Mini either throws a [[fireball]] at Capture Kong while in its [[Fire Mario|Fire]] form or hits it with a [[Hammer]]; in either case, this temporarily stuns the Capture Kong. There is no limit as to how many Minis a Capture Kong can capture, and if it captures all Minis in a level, this results in losing at the level.
