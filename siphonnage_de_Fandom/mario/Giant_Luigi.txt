'''Giant Luigi '''is a character and a protagonist in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

== Appearance ==
He first appeared when the battle started with [[Robo-Drilldigger]], affecting his hat symbol to shine and [[Starlow]] should touch the "L" on his cap to start the battle, when Starlow touches the "L" on the cap, the [[Luiginary|Luiginaries]] start to gather on Luigi and enlarges him.

== Personality ==
His personality is similar to Luigi, when he starts the battle, he gets pins and needles, however, he was confident battling with Giant [[Bowser]]. He is even strong when very huge.
