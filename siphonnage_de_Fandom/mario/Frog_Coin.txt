'''Frog Coins''' are items in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
Frog Coins are a rare type of [[coin]] generally only used amongst [[frog]]s and [[tadpole]]s. They are green coins depicting a frog on the side. Whenever a Frog Coin is picked up, it plays a deep, musical clunking sound. 

==History==
[[File:SMRPG Tadpoles explaining Frog Coins.png|thumb|left|200px|Tadpoles explaining to Mario what Frog Coins are in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'']]
Frog Coins appear in a variety of locations, such as in various [[Surprise Box]]es. There are two shops that sell rare items in exchange for Frog Coins: the "Frog Coin" emporium at [[Tadpole Pond]] and a small shop run by [[Frogfucius' Student]] in [[Seaside Town]].

Frog Coins are a reward for performing well in certain minigames. In [[Sky Bridge]], completing the Expert course rewards the party with a Frog Coin. In [[Beetle Race]], collecting a Golden Beetle and giving it to the [[Snifit (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Snifit]] at [[Beetles Are Us]] earns the party with a Frog Coin. At [[Grate Guy's Casino]], the party can earn one Frog Coin by lining up three symbols in the slot machine. In the same location, they can also earn an additional Frog Coin in Blackjack by betting a Frog Coin and winning the game.

On [[Midas Waterfall]], four Frog Coins appear outside of the caves. Sixty coins from the waterfall and [[Midas River]] can be exchanged for one Frog Coin. If 100 coins are collected in one session, the party earns five Frog Coins.

Aside from regular Frog Coins, there is a [[Rare Frog Coin]] that [[Croco]] steals from [[Mallow]], and the party has to retrieve it from him.
