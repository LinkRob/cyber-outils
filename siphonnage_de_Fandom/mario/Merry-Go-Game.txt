'''Merry-Go-Game''' is a Mini Mini-Game featured on [[Toad's Midway Madness]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. It is located on the eastern side of the [[board]], past the roller-coaster track.

As the player walks by, a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] talks to them and gives them the opportunity to play. The player rides a horse spinning clockwise, and four different items are shown on a disc in the center, spinning counterclockwise. The player is then given five seconds to stop their horse, and the item they stop their horse on becomes theirs.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The [[minigame]]'s name is a pun on {{wp|carousel|merry-go-round}}.
