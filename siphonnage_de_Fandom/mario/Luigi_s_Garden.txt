'''Luigi's Garden''' is a mini-golf course in ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]].

==Description==
Luigi's Garden is one of only two Mini-Golf courses in the game, the other being [[Peach's Castle (Nintendo 64 golf course)|Peach's Castle]]. Due to its status as a Mini-Golf course, all eighteen holes have a par of 3. [[Bob-omb]]s and [[Bob-omb Buddy|Bob-omb Buddies]] appear as spectators on the course. Giant mushrooms and [[Warp Pipe]]s are in the background.

The putting area has a checkered pattern. The first nine holes are shaped as the digits 1 through 9, while the last nine holes resemble the letters A through I.
