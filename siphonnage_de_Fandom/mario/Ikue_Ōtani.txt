'''Ikue Ōtani''' is a Japanese seiyuu at [[wikipedia:Mausu Promotion|Mausu Promotion]] best known for her voice acting as [[Pikachu]] from the ''[[Pokémon]]'' series. Other roles of hers include Candy from Smile Precure, Tony Tony Chopper in "[[wikipedia:One Piece|One Piece]]", Sachiko Shinozaki in the ''{{w|Corpse Party}}'' franchise, and Hana-chan/Hana Makihatayama in the last two seasons of ''{{w|Ojamajo Doremi}}''.
