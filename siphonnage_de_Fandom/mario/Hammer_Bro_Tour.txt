'''Hammer Bro Tour''' was the fourteenth tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] that began on March 24, 2020 and ended on April 7, 2020.

This tour features [[Hammer Bro]] who is playable for the first time in the [[Mario Kart (series)]] along with [[Boomerang Bro]], [[Fire Bro]] and [[Ice Bro]] as Super drivers. This tour also added [[Donut Plains 1 (track)|Donut Plains 1]] from [[Super Mario Kart]] and [[Bowser Castle 2 (Mario Kart: Super Circuit)|Bowser Castle 2]] from [[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]].
