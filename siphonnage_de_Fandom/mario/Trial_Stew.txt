'''Trial Stew''' is an item which appears in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:Trial_Stew_TTYD.png|thumb|left]]
At first, the Trial Stew is seemingly useless, as it lowers the player's [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Flower Point|FP]] to basically nothing. However, to compensate, Trial Stew is the only item to fill up the [[Star Power]] entirely. A Trial Stew is created by mixing both the [[Couple's Cake]] with a [[Poison Mushroom|Poison Shroom]]. In the Japanese version, it can also be created by mixing both the [[Thunder Bolt]] with a [[Thunder Rage]].

===''Super Paper Mario''===
This item reappears in ''Super Paper Mario''. Trial Stew has a different disadvantageous effect, mainly because FP and Star Power do not return, and uses a unique power to automatically [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] [[Mario]]. However, Mario gets 100 [[Heart Point|HP]] whenever he takes damage from this special poison, but the poison can still end up killing Mario if not treated soon enough.
