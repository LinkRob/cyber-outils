The '''Fleet Glide Galaxy''' is a galaxy in [[World 5 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|World 5]] in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It takes place on an enormous rocket-shaped spaceship with lots of artillery on board.

== Missions ==
=== Fluzzard's Wild Battlefield Glide ===
On the starting in this mission, the player needs to talk to the [[Jibberjay]] in the center of the platform, who will ask him if they want to fly with Fluzzard and tells them to reach the giant sun gate at the end. While flying with [[Fluzzard]], the player has to keep away from particular  obstacles and enemies. First, the player has to avoid [[Magmaargh]]s, fly through large holes then the exterior, then [[Bat (Super Mario Galaxy)|bat]]s, [[Thwomp]]s, and [[Banzai Bill]]s. In the large area after, the player has to keep away from falling pillars while  fraught to beat a gate before it closes. The final area of this mission has particular [[Airship]]s and [[Undergrunt Gunner]]s before the goal. If the player flies through the goal, they will return to the starting platform where the Jibberjay will reward with the [[Power Star]].
=== Fastest Feathers in the Galaxy ===
On the starting planet, the player will find the black Jibberjay that sent them the letter. Talking to him will have him challenge the player to a race, and accepting will have the player hang from [[Fluzzard]] and will be placed against Jibberjay. The player then has to race him through the obstacle course, the layout of which is mostly the same as how it was in the last one. However, some enemies are differently, such as additional [[Undergrunt Gunner]]s. If the player manages to beat Jibberjay to the gate at the end of this mission, he will reward them with a [[Power Star]].
=== Green Star 1 ===
The player should redo the first mission. Right when the race begins, however, the player should quickly plunge to get the [[Green Star]].
=== Green Star 2 ===
The player should redo the last mission. If the player get to the [[#Area 4|fourth area]], the player will move to the left where the stanchion is about to fall. Behind the stanchion is the Green Star.{{Super Mario Galaxy 2 Galaxies}}
