'''Diamond City Stadium''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==In ''WarioWare Gold''==
Diamond City Stadium is where [[Wario]] hosts his [[Wario Bowl]] tournament. All of Wario's tutorial stages and cutscenes, as well as [[Wario Deluxe]]'s story in the Ultra League, take place here.
