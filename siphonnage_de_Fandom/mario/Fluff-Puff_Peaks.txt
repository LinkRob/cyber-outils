'''Fluff-Puff Peaks''' is the second [[world]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It contains 30 [[Wonder Seed]]s, 18 levels, and 8 challenges. This world has 3 Badge Challenges, 1 KO Arena, 1 Search Party, and 3 Break Time Stages.
