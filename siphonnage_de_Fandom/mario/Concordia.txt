'''Concordia''' is the general location and main setting in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It used to be a single continent on its [[Concordia's World|world]], but got ripped apart, with its resultant islands drifting off. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] end up transported here at the start of the game, and they, [[Connie]], and [[Snoutlet]] have made putting it back together their mission.

==Description==
So far, Concordia is composed of several small islands and is surrounded by a massive water throughout the [[Concordia's World|world]]. Mario and others use the [[Shipshape Island]] ship to navigate over the waters and each of the islands are accessed through the use of the [[Shipshape's Cannon]].

Similar to the [[Mushroom Earth|planet]] on [[Mushroom World|Mario's World]], Concordia has several [[? Block|? Blocks]] covered with [[coins]], [[List of items|items]] and clothes.

== Trivia==
*In reality, Concordia's pieces would have been rooted to their place by their connection to the ocean floor, and even if the process involved plate tectonics, the gaps would have taken many years to grow to a noticeable size, and an earthquake would have caused damage to certain structures like [[Rumbla Island]]'s Rumbla Dish and the buildings on [[Lottacoins Island]]. However, it is possible that [[Zokket]] severed the pieces' connection to the ocean floor during/not long after/via his desecration of the original Uni-Tree.
