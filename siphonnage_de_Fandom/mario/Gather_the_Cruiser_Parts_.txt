'''Gather the Cruiser Parts!''' is the second level of [[Joke's End]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 24.

==Layout==
The 1st battle contains 3 [[Clumph]]s, and 5 [[Ice Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Ice Bro, and he knows [[Chance]] and [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 5 [[Chuck Guy]]s, 2 Ice Bros, and 1 Clumph. The enemy captain is a Clumph, and he knows Chance and Deny

The final battle contains 2 Clumphs, 3 Chuck Guys, and 3 Ice Bros. The enemy captain is a Chuck Guy, and he knows Deny and Chance.

*Ice Bro (Lv. 24)
