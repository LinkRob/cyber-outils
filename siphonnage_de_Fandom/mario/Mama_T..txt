'''Mama T.''' is the mother of [[Jimmy T]] in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. She first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' along with her husband, [[Papa T.]].

==Physical description==
Mama T. wears a red shirt covered by a yellow apron and a light pink afro wig.

==In ''WarioWare: Twisted!''==
Mama T., along with Papa T., first appear in Jimmy T.'s stage, dancing with their son in [[Club Sugar]]. Later on, Mama T. and Papa T. host the [[Family Scramble]] [[microgame]] set, featuring a mixture of [[Mona]]'s, Jimmy T.'s, and [[Kat and Ana]]'s microgames.
{{br|left}}
==In ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''==
Mama T. and Papa T. no longer host their own microgame set, although they return in Jimmy T.'s stage, [[Dance Club Rub]], where they dance with Jimmy and his two younger siblings, [[James T.]] and [[Jamie T.]].
