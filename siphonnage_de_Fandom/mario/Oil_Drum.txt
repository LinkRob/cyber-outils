}}'''OiI Drums''' are enemies found in the ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' arcade game and act as enemies in that game that spew out fireballs as an attack. The Oil Drums are harmless in the original ''Donkey Kong'' unless a [[barrel]] with fire is thrown at it, making it produce fire.

OiI Drums later reappear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]'' as enemies/obstacles. There are two different types in the games: One spews out fire and has the words "OiI" on it, while the other species spews out enemies mainly [[Slippa]]s and [[Gnawty|Gnawties]] and have the Skull marking with two crossbones on it. OiI Drums can be defeated by [[TNT Barrel]]s in these games. OiI Drums majorly appear in [[Oil Drum Alley]] where many appear on where [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong]] must hop on these TNT Barrels when they are not in process of spewing fire. They have the same traits in ''Donkey Kong Land'' s they did in ''Donkey Kong Country''.

OiI Drums reappear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]'' as obstacles in the final battle against [[Donkey Kong]]. They have the same traits and personality as they did in the original ''Donkey Kong'' arcade game.

Similar items called [[Oil Barrel]]s appear in ''[[DK: Jungle Climber]]'' as collectible items instead of enemies or an obstacle.
