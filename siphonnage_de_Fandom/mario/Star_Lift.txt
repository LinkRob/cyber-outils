'''Star Lifts''' are objects which first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and return in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

==Description==
Star Lifts are star-shaped platforms appearing in either one of two sizes. They spin a certain direction, depending on which side the player is weighing down on. Star Lifts produce glockenspiel noises while spinning.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
They appear only throughout [[Spinning-Star Sky]] in ''New Super Mario Bros. U'' and in [[Broozers and Barrels]] in ''New Super Luigi U'', also showing them to be spun by a [[Broozer]]'s punches.
