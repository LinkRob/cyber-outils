'''Screamy''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is an odd-looking character that joins [[Luigi]] on his [[Super Luigi|journey]] in the [[Waffle Kingdom]]. His design is an early design of the [[Smorg]]s.

==Biography==
Screamy ran into Luigi while he was looking for a piece of the [[Marvelous Compass]] in [[Rapturous Ruins]]. It is not stated why Screamy joined Luigi's side, although but Screamy says that he must deliver something.
