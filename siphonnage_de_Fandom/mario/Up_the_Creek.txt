'''Up the Creek''' is the first level of [[World 3 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 3]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', a [[Green Yoshi]] takes control in this level. This level takes place on a flowing river on the border between a jungle and a beach (containing themes from both worlds), the level also contains two waterfalls as well and is filled with aquatic enemies such as [[Nep-Enut]]s, [[Flopsy Fish]], and the ever-dangerous [[Lunge Fish]] which make attempts to eat Yoshi. 

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, jump to the walkway to the right and move left to tag the [[Stork Stop]] above the starting point, switch to [[Baby DK]] if you haven't already as you will be needing him for the entirety of this level. From here, advance to the right and swing from the ropes to reach the ledge to the right, next, advance to the right and pound the [[Stake]] for a [[Red Coin]] and then jump to the vines above and climb the vines but watch out for the [[Snap Jaw]]s coming down from the vines, hop to the left and grab the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]] here, from here, advance to the right along the platforms and jump to the tall [[Stake]], from here, hit the block above with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] to grab all the [[Coin]]s around it ([[Baby DK]]'s egg tosses explode on impact and the blast radius can collect the Coins), next ground pound the [[Stake]] all the way down to reveal a hidden [[Winged Cloud]], hit it to earn a 1-Up, from here, move to the left and pound the [[Stake]] here for a [[Red Coin]], then traverse to the right and hop across the [[Teeter Totter]]s while avoiding leaping [[Cheep Cheep]]s and continue hopping as you reach the higher ones to the left, from here, climb the vine (while avoiding the [[Snap Jaw]]s, next move to the right and jump across these [[Teeter Totter]]s and grab the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]] then toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the [[Smiley Flower]] above the bushes then use the small [[Flatbed Ferry]] to move along the track collecting [[Coin]]s and from here, exit the area through the right.

In the next area, advance to the right and hop to the small palm tree, from here, flutter to the right to reveal a hidden [[Winged Cloud]] underneath a branch, hit the [[Winged Cloud]] to get some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and from here, hop up the branches until reaching the [[Stork Stop]], advance to the left to tag the [[Middle Ring]] and then move to the right, use the [[Hanging Blow Hard]]'s rope to reach the vine (but watch out for the [[Needlenose]]s it spits out), from here, climb across the vines and collect the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]] before dropping in the opening to get a [[Smiley Flower]], next, climb back up the vine and return back to the main path below the [[Hanging Blow Hard]], from here, drop down and collect the [[Smiley Flower]] and collect the [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s here but watch out for the [[Egg-enut]] that attcks from the water pretending to be an egg, jump to the plam tree and climb up the brown tree and from here, use the [[Hanging Blow Hard]]'s rope to reach the vertical-moving rope and grab onto the vines and use the next rope to zip-line down as you collect the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]] along the path, upon dropping onto the ledge with the [[Platform Block]], advance to the left and toss a [[Snap Jaw]] into the [[Tulip]] for some extra [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], from here, move to the left and hop across the [[Stake]]s and the [[Teeter Totter]] (ground pound the [[Stake]] to the left of the [[Teeter Totter]] as there is a [[Red Coin]] inside) and move to the left grabbing the [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s on the palm trees but watch out for the [[Egg-enut]] posing as an egg, from here, move to the left, hop into the water to get the two [[Coin]]s and a [[Red Coin]] and hop to the palm tree and then the tree branch to get two more [[Coin]]s and another [[Red Coin]], from here, advance right back to where you were and hit the [[Platform Block]]s to create some moving platforms, from here, use the platforms to reach the [[Hanging Blow Hard]]s' ropes and swing from them (while avoiding the [[Needlenose]]s spat) to reach the ledge with a [[Spring Ball]] hop on the [[Spring Ball]] and use it to reach a hidden area.

In this secret area, you will find a long rope and a hoard of [[Coin]]s around it, use the rope to swing to grab all the [[Coin]]s, once you're done, exit the are through the bottom, you will land on a [[Teeter Totter]], drop down to the left to land on a [[Stake]], pound it for another [[Red Coin]], hit the [[Platform Block]]s to create platforms (and grab the three [[Coin]]s on the [[Teeter Totter]] to the left), as you traverse to the right, watch out for the [[Nep-Enut]] in the water that tries to attack you, from here, pound the [[Stake]] and grab the [[Coin]]s underneath the bridge as one of them is a [[Red Coin]], once done, advance to the right to exit the area.

In the next area, tag the [[Middle Ring]] and switch your baby at the [[Stork Stop]] if you want to (although it is highly recommended to remain with [[Baby DK]]) and hop onto the boat, the boat will start sailing as you're on it (one thing to remember is do not fall into the water, the waters are infested with [[Lunge Fish]] and one is even following your boat to signify this, if you fall into the water, you will immediately be eaten by a hungry [[Lunge Fish]] so remain on the boat at all costs). As you travel down the river, toss your eggs at the [[Fly Guy]]s as one of them carries a [[Red Coin]] (and be sure to hit the blocks above surrounded by [[Coin]]s as [[Baby DK]]'s egg explosions can net all the [[Coin]]s within the radius), as you continue sailing down the river, [[Flopsy Fish]] will start hopping out of the water as well attempting to knock you off your raft and into the [[Lunge Fish]]-infested waters, so either avoid or eat these nuisances, from here, hop onto the island and launch an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the [[Fly Guy]] carrying a [[Red Coin]], fill up your egg supply with the [[Egg Block]], toss an egg at the [[Smiley Flower]] above, and beat the [[Piranha Plant]] blocking you're path (you will have to do all this quickly as the boat will not wait for you, if you're too slow, it will leave you behind and you'll have nowhere to go but into the river where a shoal of hungry [[Lunge Fish]] await). Once done, hop back onto the raft and ride it into the next area.

In the next area, the boat will continue sailing down the waterfall (the waters here are free of [[Lunge Fish]] but the screen is scrolling now) and be in a free fall, abandon ship and hop in the air to grab all the [[Coin]]s and a [[Smiley Flower]], four of the [[Coin]]s here are [[Red Coin]]s, once done, the boat will hit the bottom of the river and sail itself to the island at the end, from here, advance to the right to exit this small area, you should have 17 [[Red Coin]]s by this point.

In the level's final area, tag the [[Middle Ring]] atop the palm tree and advance to the right, you should see a waterfall here, your goal is to climb to the top, jump to the first ledge and once you stand on the set of [[Donut Lift]]s above, the screen will start scrolling, hop across ledge and [[Donut Lift]]-set as you to escape the vertical-scrolling screen (if you fall below the screen, you die so watch out) watch out for [[Crazee Dayzee]]s on the ledges too as they will try to impede your progress, one of the ledges has a [[Stake]] so be sure to pound it for a [[Red Coin]], hop across the next set of ledges, [[Donut Lift]]s, and palm trees and reach the ledge with the [[Stork Stop]], if you're not using [[Baby DK]] at this point, switch to him (with the [[Crazee Dayzee]]s, it is possible to reach the upper ledges without [[Baby DK]] but you won't be able to get the [[Baby Coin]] if you do), hang off the [[Hanging Blow Hard]]s rope (while slurping any [[Needlenose]]s coming down) and hop to the [[Donut Lift]]s and the palm trees, from here, hop to the ledges and beat the [[Crazee Dayzee]] and [[Piranha Plant]] here, on the [[Piranha Plant]]'s ledge there are three [[Coin]]s and one of these [[Coin]]s is a [[Red Coin]] so be sure to grab it, from here, hop to the palm trees and across the [[Teeter Totter]]s, the upper [[Teeter Totter]] has a [[Red Coin]] at the right end so be sure to grab it, from here, jump to the left ledge and to the one above, bounce off the [[Spring Ball]] to reach the higher path and move to the right, quickly drop below into the water to get the [[Baby Coin]] below the path and watch out for the [[Cheep Cheep]]s here as you hop towards the ledge with the [[GOAL! Ring]], jump through it to finish the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
You will need [[Baby DK]] for this [[Baby Coin]], at the level's end, run to the right of the screen as it scrolls and then as you drop into the water at the waterfall's top, quickly move to the left and grab the [[Baby Coin|Baby DK Coin]] before the scrolling screen completely moves past it.
