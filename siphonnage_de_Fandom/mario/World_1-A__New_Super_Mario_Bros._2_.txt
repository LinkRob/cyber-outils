'''World 1-A''' is a secret level of [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1]] from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]], ''and it's themed around [[Warp Pipe]]s. It has many [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Piranha Plant]]s, and [[Flipper]]s. In order to access this level, you have to buy it after completing [[World 1-4 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1-4]].

==Layout==
This level takes place in a maze of pipes, making it similar to [[Pipe Land]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It contains one-way flippers from ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', pipes that launch Mario and a few [[Gold Ring]]s to grab many coins from golden enemies. At the end of the stage is the Flag Pole, which is set past four [[Piranha Plant]]-infested pipes that can be used to grab the tip of the pole.

Completing this level allows Mario to unlock a red [[Toad House]] and access {{world-link|1|castle|World 1-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. 2)}}, skipping [[World 1-5 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1-5]].

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Not too far into the level, you'll see a [[Flipper]] blocked off by [[Brick Block]]s. [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] these [[Brick Block]]s ([[Small Mario]] is unable to access this), and it'll reveal this [[Star Coin]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': At the part where you go down through [[Warp Pipe]]s while dodging [[Piranha Plant]]s, this [[Star Coin]] will be in plain sight, though it will be blocked off by a [[Flipper]]. Move a little bit to the left, but not past the [[Flipper]] under you, to open the [[Flipper]] blocking off the [[Star Coin]]. Then grab the [[Star Coin]].
*'''Star Coin 3''': Right before the [[Goal Pole|Flagpole]], hit a [[Question Block|Hidden Question Block]] in between the [[Warp Pipe]] staircase and the floating [[Warp Pipe]]s. Then go into the [[Warp Pipe]] to the left of the [[Question Block|Hidden Question Block]]. This will lead you to a room with a [[Big Piranha Plant]], a red [[Koopa Troopa]], and the [[Star Coin]]. Use the red [[Koopa Troopa]] (or [[Fireball]]s) to defeat the [[Big Piranha Plant]], and reveal the way to the [[Star Coin]].
