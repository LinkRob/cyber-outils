'''Cork and Cask''' are characters in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They are the owners of the [[Chateau de Chucklehuck]].

==Appearance==
Cork is the shortest of the brothers and he is wearing a red shirt who resembles [[Mario]]. Cask is meant to reference [[Luigi]], as he is taller and wears a green shirt.

== History ==
=== ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' ===
They are first seen after Mario and Luigi freed them from a barrel where [[Popple]] and [[Bowser|Rookie]] trapped them in. The two duos teach them how to do techniques, [[Mini-Mario]] and [[Luigi Dunk]]. They request Mario and Luigi to find the [[Green Goblet|Green]] and [[Red Goblet]], the Mario Bros. then return with the two goblets, pleasing them and allowed them to enter the [[Chucklehuck Woods]].

=== ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'' ===
In ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'', they encounter [[Captain Goomba (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Goomba]] and his [[Elite Captain Quartet|squad]] in [[Reclaim the Red Goblet!]]. They ask Captain Goomba to reclaim the Red Goblet, which has been stolen from them by a [[Koopa Paratroopa]]. When the Red Goblet is returned to them, Cork gives Captain Goomba a map to [[Mother Chuckalola]] to help them find [[Iggy Koopa]].
