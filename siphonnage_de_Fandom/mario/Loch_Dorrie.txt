'''Loch Dorrie''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Loch Dorrie is one of the locations in the [[Jungle Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. Its name is a portmanteau of the real-life {{w|Loch Ness}}, located in [[Scotland]], and [[Dorrie]], who lives here. Loch Dorrie is a swamp filled with tall trees and tangled green plants, and it has several land masses that surround a small body of water at the center.

== In ''Mario Party Advance'' ==
For the [[Dino of Mystery!]] quest, Yoshi is required to visit Loch Dorrie because Dorrie is very lonely, and he is looking for another dinosaur friend. The [[Soil Sonar]] [[Gaddget]] can be obtained here if Yoshi agrees to befriend Dorrie.
