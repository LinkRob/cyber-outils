'''Countdown Platforms''' (also referred to as '''One-Jump Platforms''') are a type of object featured in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming games, in which they debut in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. They are floating white platforms with large, red spots and a preset number on them, ranging from as low as 1 and generally as high as 4 in their appearances.

Whenever a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] lands on a Countdown Platform, it gets pressed down, then by moving or [[jump]]ing off, the Countdown Platform goes down a number. If a Yoshi goes off a Countdown Platform with the number 1, the Countdown Platform vanishes.
