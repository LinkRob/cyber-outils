'''Moblins''' are a recurring enemy species in the {{series|The Legend of Zelda}}. They made their first overall debut in ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|The Legend of Zelda]]'', and their first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance is in ''[[Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up]]'', in the form of a cameo. Moblins have a design resembling either a bulldog or a pig, depending on their appearance.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up''==
[[File:SMB Friends When I Grow Up - Teacher.png|thumb|left|200px|A Moblin (left) in the Teacher page of ''Super Mario Bros. & Friends: When I Grow Up'']]
A Moblin and a [[Daira]] make a cameo on the Teacher page, where they are being taught some basic addition and subtraction from [[Luigi]].
{{br|left}}

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''===
Moblins, using their sprites from ''[[w:c:zelda:Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|Zelda II: The Adventure of Link]]'', sometimes appears in Level 3 of [[The Legend of Zelda (WarioWare: Touched!)|The Legend of Zelda]] microgame, in which the object is to rescue two moving [[Fairy (The Legend of Zelda)|Fairies]] must be rescued from them.

==In ''[[Tetris DS]]''==
An enlarged sprite of a Moblin from ''The Legend of Zelda'' sometimes appears on the Touch Screen during ''The Legend of Zelda''-themed challenges.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''===
There is a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Sticker]] of a Moblin, which depicts its ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker]]'' artwork. It can only be equipped by one of the ''The Legend of Zelda'' fighters—[[Link]], [[Princess Zelda]], [[Ganondorf]], or [[Toon Link]]—and doing so increases their attack by 15 in [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]].

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
There is a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|primary Spirit]] of Moblin depicting its ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time]]'' artwork.

==In ''[[NES Remix]]''==
[[File:NES Remix Stamp 025.png|thumb|''NES Remix'' stamp]]
The Secret Moblin appears in Stage 3 of the ''The Legend of Zelda''-themed challenges. In Stage 6, the object is to fight and defeat spear-wielding Moblins on the overworld.

One of the [[stamp]]s, numbered 25, is of a Moblin, and it specifically depicts its sprite from ''The Legend of Zelda''. Unlocking it requires that 7,000 Bits be collected.
