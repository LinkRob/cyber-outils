'''Gene Yuss''' is a character in the ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'' series. He first appears in ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]], and has returned in ''[[Mobile Golf]]'' as an unlockable character and in ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]'' as a playable character. Gene Yuss has an arrogant and self-absorbed personality, hence why his name is a pun on the word "genius."
