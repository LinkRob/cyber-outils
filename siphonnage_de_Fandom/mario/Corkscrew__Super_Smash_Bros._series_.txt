'''Corkscrew''' is the up special move of [[Wario]] in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', and later returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Description==
[[File:WarioCorkscrew-Brawl.png|thumb|left|250px|Wario using Corkscrew on Luigi in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''']]
Corkscrew is Wario's recovery move that involves him propelling straight upward while spinning, damaging anyone whom he comes in contact with him. Wario can spin into an opponent a few times while performing the move. If Wario executes Corkscrew while in midair, there is a distinction in which he is surrounded by a blue swirl while making his way upward.
