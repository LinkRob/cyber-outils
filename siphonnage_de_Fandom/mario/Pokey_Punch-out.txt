'''Pokey Punch-out''' is a 4-Player [[minigame|mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Layout==
[[File:Pokey Punch-out MP6 night.png|thumb|left|Nighttime variation]]
The background of the area is similar to [[Shifting Sand Land]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', which is mainly noticeable from the [[pyramid]] and two pillars near it as well as [[Klepto]] flying around. The sky reflects whether it is daytime or nighttime, but this does not affect the gameplay of the mini-game.

==Opening==
The characters are standing idly. They become shocked as several [[Pokey]]s emerge from the ground behind them.

==Gameplay==
The characters have to [[punch]] and/or [[kick]] at the Pokeys' body segments to knock them out. Characters can earn [[point]]s by attacking Pokey segments. Attacking a Pokey's head destroys all its body segments at once, , adding the appropriate number to the player's score. Characters can also stun each other by [[jump]]ing on and punching each other. Whoever gets the most points within the 30-second [[time limit]] wins.

Pokey Punch-out is the sixth minigame in [[Decathlon Park]]. Here, the player's objective is to attack as many Pokey segments as possible to earn as many points as possible, up to 1,000. The default record for this minigame in Decathlon Park is 15 points.

==Ending==
Any Pokeys left on the field retract back into the ground. The characters remain put, with the winners performing their victory animation whilst the losing characters do their losing animation.

==Controls==

* {{button|gcn|B}} &ndash; Punch
* {{button|gcn|A}} '''→''' {{button|gcn|B}} &ndash; Kick

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Punch and kick Pokeys that emerge from the sand to score points. Whoever scores the most points wins!"''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"Attack a Pokey's head to topple it completely and earn points for every one of its segments."''

==Trivia==
*The [[Miracle Book]] has a page based on Pokey Punch-out, "Prickly Brawl," which depicts [[Waluigi]] and [[Koopa Kid]] punching Pokeys.
