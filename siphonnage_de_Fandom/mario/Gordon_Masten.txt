'''Gordon James Masten''' (August 30, 1950 - July 26, 2020) was a Canadian voice actor. He provided the voice of [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Big Mouth Koopa]] in the animated series ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World (television series)|Super Mario World]].'' He was one of the singers in the show as well as some of the cavemen.
