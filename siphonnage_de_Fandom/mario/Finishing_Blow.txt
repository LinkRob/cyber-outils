Watch closely, and then deliver full feline force at just the right moment!}}'''Finishing Blow''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' It uses the Archer form for its gameplay, and [[Jimmy Thang]] acts as its creator, with his level, Surfin' Surprise, having a chance to have the player play it.

== Layout ==
The microgame is set on a dirt path with grass and trees to the side. The protagonist is a cat, presumably Sameow from Mewtroid. A creature will charge at the protagonist from a distance.

== Gameplay ==
The player must punch with their dominant-hand fist when the enemy gets close enough to the cat. If the player punches with the wrong timing, the cat (and therefore, the player) will be defeated.

=== Difficulty Levels ===

* Level 1 (Easy): The enemy moves at a constant speed.
* Level 2 (Hard): The enemy may pause before suddenly running quickly.
* Level 3 (Expert): The enemy jumps or flies toward the cat.
