'''Rainbow Road''' is a secret passage that can transport anyone from one Kingdom to another, and is a minor location in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' based off the [[Mario Kart (series)]].

==History==
Princess Peach makes it known that in order to bypass Bowser's army and get to the Mushroom Kingdom from the Kong Kingdom, the Rainbow Road was the fastest way to get there. However, the Koopa General finds out about it and warns Bowser of this plan.

As Mario, Peach, Toad, and the Kongs drive on the Rainbow Road, they have to avoid every Koopa's attacks that slow them down.

Eventually, both Mario and Donkey Kong are able to defeat the general, before the latter in response, transforms into a Blue Shell and destroys part of the road, leading to the two falling into the ocean below, and allowing the rest of the Koopa Troopas to capture Cranky Kong and the Kong Army.
