'''Silver Bullet''' is one of Jinx's special attacks in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is an [[Instant Death Attack]]. [[Mario]] and his party can block it, but the character will last with only a single point of HP left. It is very rarely seen before Jinx does [[Vigor Up!]].
