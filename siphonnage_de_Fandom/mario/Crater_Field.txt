'''Crater Field''' is a stadium in both ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'' and ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. In the former game, it is the [[Super Mushroom Cup]]'s special field.

==Description==
Crater Field is located on [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]]. The playing field consists of grass, and other plants also grow in the stadium. The structure is furnished with a heatproof material, possibly to protect against the warmth within the crater. The spectators sit in glass-covered, egg-shaped sections instead of normal seats.
