Yuki Kodaira (born January 28, 1981) is a Japanese voice actress. She is the voice of [[Hariet]], [[Madame Broode and Chain Chompikins|Madame Broode]], [[Tiara]] and [[Cookatiel]] in [[Super Mario Odyssey|''Super Mario Odyssey'']].
