'''A Plumber's Lament''' is a work of art presented by [[Garro]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is actually [[Mario]] disguised as a golden statue as part of Garro's plan to sneak him into [[Nimbus Castle]], after it had been occupied by [[Valentina]]. At first, when A Plumber's Lament was presented to Valentina, she did not like it, but when Garro says that it is a sculptural ode to the struggle of the working class, Valentina changes her opinion.
