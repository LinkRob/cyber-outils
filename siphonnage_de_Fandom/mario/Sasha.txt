'''Sasha''' is a young girl and a tennis player in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. Her Doubles partner is [[Sylvia (Mario Tennis: Power Tour)|Sylvia]].

==Physical description==
Sasha is a brunette with a bobcut and very large dark brown pupils. She wears a light purple shirt which has a white collar, a red mini-skirt (orange in-game), and white socks with light pink tennis shoes bearing the same color as her shirt.
