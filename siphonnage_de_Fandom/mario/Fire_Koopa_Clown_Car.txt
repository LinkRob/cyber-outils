The '''Fire Koopa Clown Car''' is a variation of the [[Koopa Clown Car]] capable of breathing [[fireball]]s. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. In the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' style, it has a slightly different name, '''Fire Junior Clown Car'''.
