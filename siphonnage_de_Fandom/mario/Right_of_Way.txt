'''Right of Way''' is [[Mona]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player controls the walker that is moving ahead on the path.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The walker on his phone quickly moves onto one side of the sidewalk.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The walker quickly moves onto one side of the sidewalk, and sometimes will quickly move onto the other side of the sidewalk.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' During the night, a motorcyclist with headlights speeds in a straight line. The motorcyclist can be coming from behind or front.
