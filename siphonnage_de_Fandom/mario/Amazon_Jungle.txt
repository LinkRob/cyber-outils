'''Amazon Jungle''' is a location in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It only appears in one episode, "[[Jungle Fever]]."

==Description==
The Amazon Jungle majorly consists of a vast jungle, including rivers and [[George's Gorge]]. The only civilization of the Amazon Jungle is a small village inhabited by some [[Toad (species)|Mushroom People]] and [[Human]]s, such as [[Dr. Sheldon]]. The Amazon Jungle also has some old ruins and stone structures, but it is not made clear as to who created them.

==Behind the scenes==
The Amazon Jungle is based on the real-world {{w|Amazon Rainforest}}.
