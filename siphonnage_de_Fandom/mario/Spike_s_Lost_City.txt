==Features==
{{sectstub}}

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': At the top of the first long slope, go in the nearby [[Warp Pipe]] leading towards a secret area. In that area, activate all the switches to get the star.
*'''Green Star 2''': After climbing the long slope past the checkpoint, be in Cat form to climb the curve wall by the Spike enemies to find a hidden path leading towards the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': Go to the third swinging platform past the checkpoint and jump towards another swinging platform on the upper left to get the star.
