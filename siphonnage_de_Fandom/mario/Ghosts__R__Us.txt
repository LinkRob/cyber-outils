'''Ghosts 'R' Us''' is the fourth episode of the [[Super Mario World (television series)|''Super Mario World'' television series]].

==Plot==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] are searching for [[Oogtar]], who has been apparently been wandering around the Forest of Illusion (called "the Enchanted Forest"). They then encounter a [[Magikoopa]] named [[Wizardheimer]] (everyone pronounces his name wrong), who is infamous for being the most cruel Magikoopa in [[Dinosaur Land]]. Yoshi chickens out and runs away, while Wizardheimer commands the rest to go down a warp pipe to his lair, the [[Ghost House]], or to be blown apart by a [[Bob-omb]].

Yoshi then encounters Oogtar when the caveboy scares him by announcing himself as the "King of the Jungle", but he eventually gets stuck in a [[Piranha Plant]], which Yoshi eats. Oogtar tells Yoshi that he's not afraid of anything, even though they encounter a [[Wiggler]], which Oogtar ''is'' afraid of. Yoshi then finds a pair of wings, Oogtar hops on, and the two head to the Ghost House in which Wizardheimer is residing.

When they reach the Ghost House, Oogtar is kidnapped, leaving only Yoshi let to save them all. He then goes inside, and after being chased by [[Boo]]s, finally reaches the dungeon where his friends are being held. Yoshi eats Wizardheimer's wand and uses the Magikoopa's own magic against him, freeing the others. Oogtar then tries to break down the door, only to find a key, which sends them back to Dome City.

==Trivia/Goofs==
* This episode's title is in no doubt a reference to the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toys_%22R%22_Us Toys "R" Us] store, just like the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Crimes R Us]]."
*This is Oogtar's first appearance.
*This is the first of three episodes with the Wings, a superpower exclusive to Yoshi.
*When Yoshi flies around to eat the [[Wiggler]], the background moves, but the "?" Block in the background does not.
*When Wizardheimer is leaving, the "L" on Luigi's cap turns white on a black background and the "M" on Mario's cap turns red on a black background.
*When Wizardheimer says "You green-faced glutton," his mouth isn't moving.
*After Oogtar and Yoshi find the warp pipe, Oogtar says "This warp pipe leads to Wizenheimer's digs", but nowhere in the episode does it show Yoshi telling Oogtar the name of the person who captured Mario, Luigi, and Princess Toadstool.
