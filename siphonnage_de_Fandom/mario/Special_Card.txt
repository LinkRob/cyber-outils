The '''Special Card''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Special Card allows [[Mario]] to play the Plane Game at the [[Pianta Parlor]]. Mario receives it when he completes [[Koopook]]'s [[Trouble Center|trouble]] titled "Try to find me!".
