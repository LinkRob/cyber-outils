'''Lethal Slinker'''s are a kind of rare ghost that made their debut in [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']]. Like all the other [https://mario.fandom.com/wiki/Luigi%27s_Mansion_3#Rare rare ghosts], Lethal Slinkers only appear in the [[ScareScraper|ScareScraper modes]] (with the exception of the 5 floors mode)  on floors that are the multiple of 5. Like [[Lethal Goob|Lethal Goobs]], [[Lethal Hammer|Lethal Hammers]] and [[Lethal Oozer|Lethal Oozers]], Lethal Slinkers can knock out [[Luigi]] in one attack.

== Appearance ==
Like the other Lethal rare ghosts, Lethal Slinkers have black bodies that are glowing orange, red eyes and red mouths. The rest of their physical features are identical to regular Slinkers.

== In [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']] ==
When Lethal Slinkers appear, they might get invisible and sneak up on Luigis like regular Slinkers. Before they attack, their hands will appear and glow orange, indicating the players of their location. All players must battle the Lethal Slinkers with extreme caution as one grab from them will instantly knock out Luigi. Therefore, it is recommended to get the [[Dark-Light Goggles]] before battling the Lethal Slinkers. In this case the the player can always know their exact locations even without using the [[Dark-Light Device]]. The [[Hyper Strobulb]] is also a great choice against Lethal Slinkers since it can stun every single ghost in the entire room with a powerful flash, which includes all invisible ghosts. Lethal Slinkers are vcuumed and captured in the same way as regular Slinkers. Like all forms of Hammers and Slinkers, they can also be vacuumed and slammed by two players at once since they all have two tails. If a Luigi gets knocked out by a Lethal Slinker, he will have to wait for his teammates to wake him up. Or else the entire team will face a game over if all Luigis are knocked out.
