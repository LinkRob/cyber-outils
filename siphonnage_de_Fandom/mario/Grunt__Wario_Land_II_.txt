'''Grunts''' are helmet-wearing apes encountered as enemies in ''[[Wario Land II]]''. They attack by throwing [[Spiked Ball|iron balls]] at [[Wario]], although he can defeat a Grunt using his [[Dash Attack]]. If an iron ball hits Wario, he loses coins. Grunts are heavy enemies given that Wario is slowed while carrying them around.
