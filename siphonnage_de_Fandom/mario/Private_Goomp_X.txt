'''Private Goomp X''' is the X version of [[Private Goomp]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They both share the same appearance.

== Battle ==
Private Goomp X has the ability to call more [[Sub-Goomba X]]'s if there are few on the field. If there are enough Sub-Goomba X's on the field Private Goomp can also use his secondary attack which involves stockpiling the Sub-Goomba X's and sending them to Mario. This attack can be countered by jumping on the Sub-Goomba X formation, sending them back to Goomp. After a few times the pile will hit Goomp X. If Mario gets hit by the attack the Sub-Goomba X's will immediately return to Goomp requiring Mario to jump over them in order to prevent getting hit again.
