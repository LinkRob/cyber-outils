The '''Steel Driver''' is a recurring [[kart]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.

==Description==
The Steel Driver is a submarine-like vehicle with a dark blue top and a red underside. It is based on the submarine used in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] game ''[[w:c:nintendo:Steel Diver|Steel Diver]]'', except in the form of a kart. When the Steel Driver goes underwater, the propeller appears in the rear guard, similarly to a submarine itself. The [[emblem]] is located on the front sides of the Steel Driver.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart 8''===
The Steel Driver is an unlockable kart body. It boosts [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]] and [[traction]], but lowers [[acceleration]] and [[handling]]. The Steel Driver has identical stats to the [[Badwagon]], the [[Tri-Speeder]], the [[Standard ATV]], and the [[GLA]].

===''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
Unlike the original ''Mario Kart 8'', the Steel Driver is available from the start. It instead boosts [[speed]] and weight, but lowers acceleration and handling. Just as before, the Steel Driver shares its stats with the Tri-Speeder, but the only other kart body that its stats are the same as is the [[Bone Rattler]].

As computer-controlled opponents, [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]], [[Koopa Troopa]], [[Dry Bones]], and [[Wiggler]] sometimes use a configuration of the Steel Driver with the [[Metal]] tires.

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
The Steel Driver is a High-End kart which uses the [[Pirate Sushi Racer]]'s tires. The Steel Driver was added at the start of the [[Mario vs. Luigi Tour (2020)|2020 Mario vs. Luigi Tour]]. Later on, during the [[Snow Tour]], the Steel Driver became the signature kart of [[Penguin Luigi]]. The Steel Driver increases the duration of [[Slipstream]] boosts and the number of points earned from performing them.
