'''Spiders''' an enemy in the {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}. They first appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' and return in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Spiders are based on their {{w|Spider|real-world counterparts}}. In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'', Spiders are red in color, but they have a gold variant named [[Gold Spider]]s. In ''Luigi's Mansion 3'', Spiders instead resemble {{w|black widow}}s.

===Behavior===
Spiders mainly crawl down from the ceiling and onto the ground. When they spot Luigi, they let out a hiss before going after him and biting him. Like [[Bat]]s, Spiders mainly attack in groups and it is best to deal with them with the [[Strobulb]]. The Spiders seem to obey over the [[Spider Queen]] even when she was possessed by a [[Grouchy Possessor|ghost]].
