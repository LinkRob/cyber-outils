''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'' has special crafting materials that can be obtained from enemies in certain locations.

== Materials gained in the [[Lushgreen Sea]] ==

* Rumbla Stone: Dropped by enemies on [[Rumbla Island]]. Can be used to craft the First-Round Belt at the workshop (two required) or the Groove Boots (one required along with a Twistee Leaf). Can be sold for ten [[Coin|coins]].
* Twistee Leaf: Obtained on [[Twistee Island]]. Can be used to craft the Luck Charm (three required along with three Raynforst Vines) and or the Groove Boots (one required along with a Rumbla Stone)
* Raynforst Vine: Dropped by enemies on [[Raynforst Island]]. Three can be used along with three Twistee Leaves to craft the Luck Charm.

== Materials gained in the [[Color-Full Sea]] ==

* Desolatt Scrap: Dropped by enemies on [[Desolatt Island]]. Can be used to craft the Repeater Belt (three required alongside four Merrygo Bolts and two Lottacoins Levers). Can be sold for twenty coins.
* Merrygo Bolt: Dropped by enemies on [[Merrygo Island]]. Can be used to craft the Repeater Belt (four required alongside three Desolatt Scraps and two Lottacoins Levers). Can be sold for twenty coins.
* Lottacoins Lever: Dropped by enemies on [[Lottacoins Island]]. Can be used to craft the Repeater Belt (two required alongside three Desolatt Scraps and four Merrygo Bolts). Can be sold for twenty thirty coins.

== Materials gained in the [[Brrrning Sea]] ==

* Bulbfish Lantern: Dropped by enemies on [[Bulbfish Island]]. Can be used to craft the Miraculous Belt (three required along with seven pieces of Heatfreeze Ore and seven Offandon Bricks) or sold for 40 coins.
* Heatfreeze Ore: Dropped by enemies on [[Heatfreeze Island]]. Can be used to craft the Miraculous Belt (seven pieces required along with three Bulbfish Lanterns and seven Offandon Bricks) or sold for 40 coins.
* Skorcheen Lava: Dropped by enemies on [[Skorcheen Island]]: Can be used to craft the HP Bros. Bangle (six samples required along with three Slippenglide Snow samples and three Offins) or sold for 50 coins.
* Slippenglide Snow: Dropped by enemies on [[Slippenglide Island]]. Can be used to craft the HP Bros. Bangle (three samples required along with six samples of Skorcheen Lava and three Offins.

== Materials gained in the [[Gulchrock Sea]] ==

* Offandon Brick: Dropped by enemies on [[Offandon Island]]. Can be used to craft the Miraculous Belt (seven required along with three Bulbfish Lanterns and seven samples of Heatfreeze Ore).

== Other ==

* Wayaway Chain: Dropped by enemies on [[Wayaway Island]]. Can be used to craft the Escalation Belt (eight required along with three Egobies).
* Zokket Alloy: Dropped by [[Mettozokettohei|Mettozokettoheitachi]]. Can be used to craft the Adaphne Boots or Adaphnikki Boots.
* Glohm Dust: Dropped by enemies infected with [[Glohm]]. Can be used to craft the Overcharged Boots (five samples required along with five Glohm Fragments and five intact Glohm Crystals).
* Glohm Fragment: Dropped by enemies infected with Glohm. Can be used to craft the Overcharged Boots (five required along with five Glohm Dust samples and five intact Glohm Crystals).
* Glohm Crystal: Dropped by enemies infected with Glohm. Can be used to craft the Overcharged Boots (five required along with five Glohm Dust samples and five Glohm Fragments).
* Offin: A fish that can be caught anywhere on [[Shipshape Island]]. Three are required to craft the HP Bros. Bangle alongside six Skorcheen Lava samples and three Slippenglide Snow samples.
* Egoby: A fish that can be caught anywhere on Shipshape Island. Three are required to craft the Escalation Belt alongside eight Wayaway Chains.
