The '''Scoreboard''' (also known as '''Player Panel''') is a recurring gameplay mechanic in various games of the {{franchise|Mario}} and related media.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
The scoreboard shows how many [[Star (Mario Party series)|star]]s, [[coin]]s and the type of items the player has during the match. It also shows the player's current face and ranking. The panel color changes when the player lands on a space (red, blue, DK, [[Bowser]], character or green). The Scoreboard also determines what type of [[mini-game]] everyone plays at the end of the turn. The design changes from game to game.

Counting the home console games, in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 10|10]]'' --the first two future home console games after ''[[Mario Party|Mario Party 1]]'' through ''[[Mario Party 8|8]]''-- [[Star (Mario Party series)|star]]s and [[coin]]s are replaced by [[Mini Star]]s and [[Mini Ztar]]s (or [[Banana]]s and [[Z-Banana]]s --with a similar function to [[Mini Star]]s and [[Mini Ztar]]s-- in the ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' board [[DK's Jungle Ruins]]). [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]s and [[coin]]s, they do not return until the home console games ''[[Super Mario Party]]'', ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.
