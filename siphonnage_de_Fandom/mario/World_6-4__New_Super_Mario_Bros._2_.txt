'''World 6-4''' is the fourth regular level in [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It is one of the only levels in the game taking place in a haunted forest, without being a [[Ghost House]], the other level being {{world-link|star|3|World Star-3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)}}. It is similar to [[World 8-1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8-1]] from the original ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' game. This level focuses on [[Peepa]]s, which move platforms by shaking them. 

==Layout==
[[Mario]] begins the level near two [[? Block]]s, one of which contains a power-up. A [[Peepa]] carrying a [[lift]] vertically is then found, followed by a Peepa moving diagonally and a Peepa below a ? Block. A [[Crowber]], two [[Boo]]s, and more Peepas are then found. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] is then found, with two ? Blocks nearby. A lift that is carried by a Peepa when Mario touches it is found, below a ? Block with a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] found above it. A Boo and a group of three Peepas holding lifts are found with a [[Red Ring]]. After a few platforms, another lift that Mario must ride on is found. [[Crowber]]s and a circle of [[Peepa]]s are encountered while Mario rides the lift. Two more lifts are found, both leading to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' As soon the level starts, the player must head left to get the first [[Star Coin]].
*'''Star Coin 2:''' Once [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] see the first spinning [[Brick Block]], they must jump on top to reveal a hidden block with a [[Vine]]. They must climb it, then time their jumps over the [[Stone Block]] correctly to get the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' The third Star Coin is being circled by [[Peepa]]s towards the end of the level. The player must [[Wall Jump|wall kick]] to obtain the third [[Star Coin]] of the level.
