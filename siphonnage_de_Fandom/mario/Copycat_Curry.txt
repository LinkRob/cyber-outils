'''Copycat Curry''' is a [[Rhythm minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''. It is featured in the Rhythm Kitchen mode.

== Gameplay ==
The aim of Copycat Curry is to perfectly spice the curry standing front of you in time. You do this by pressing 'A' in time with the music. The pose that your character performs will be demonstrated by Waluigi, who stands in front of the curry dish. This game is very similar to [[Super Mario Party]]'s [[Time To Shine|Time to Shine]].
