'''White Turbo Birdo''' is a High-End kart in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It made its first tour appearance in [[Yoshi Tour (2022)|Yoshi Tour]] in 2022.
== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
White Turbo Birdo debuts in Yoshi Tour in 2022 as a High-End kart. Its special skill is [[Rocket Start Plus]].
