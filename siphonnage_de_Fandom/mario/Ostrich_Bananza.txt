'''Ostrich Bananza''' is a transformation of [[Donkey Kong]] that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. It is one of the many Bananza forms he can transform into and can either be his second or third transformation, depending on whether the player goes to the Forest Layer or the Freezer Layer first, being granted to him by the [[Ostrich Elder]] of the [[Forest Layer]] after finding the [[Ancient Record]] for her. 

==Physical description==
As Ostrich Bananza, Donkey Kong is, naturally, a giant ostrich who has grey fur and black feathers. Uniquely, despite being a bird, Donkey Kong in his Ostrich Bananza form still has fingers, but has talons for feet. A bunch of bananas also stick out at the end of the feathers resembling wings as well as having a bunch of them around his neck.

==Powers and abilities==
Ostrich Bananza's main ability is simply flight. Donkey Kong will be able to fly through the air for a short time before he falls down. However, thanks to upgrading skills through the skill tree, Ostrich Bananza will gain the ability to glide through the air for a short time after tiring out from flying. These abilities together make it extremely useful for platforming sections and traveling around in large, open areas in general. One more unique ability that Ostrich Bananza can gain through the skill tree is the ability to drop explosive eggs. These eggs can be dropped down on enemies allowing Donkey Kong to damage them while he is flying in midair. The number of eggs can be upgraded up to three eggs. Upon completing the Ostrich Bananza Rehearsal after the events of the game, Donkey Kong will be able to use the Golden Egg Bomb to drop explosive eggs that turns the impact area into gold.
