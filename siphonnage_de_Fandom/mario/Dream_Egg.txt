'''Dream Eggs''' are items that appear ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They can be made by the mysterious [[Seadring]]s in the [[Dream World]]. They are only found in [[Dreamy Driftwood Shore]].

==Appearance==
Dream Eggs are crystal-like eggs that are multi-colored.

==Usage==
Dream Eggs are mainly used to help [[Seadric]] expand Dreamy Driftwood Shore in order to hide [[Princess Peach]] deeper in the dream world. Once [[Mario]] and [[Dreamy Luigi]] have obtained the three Dream Eggs from Sedrics siblings [[Seabelle]], [[Seatoon]], and [[Seabury]], Seadric can expand Dreamy Driftwood Shore to its limits.
