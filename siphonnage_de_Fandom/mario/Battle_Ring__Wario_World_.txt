'''Battle Rings''' are an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
Battle Rings are small fighting arenas set on certain circular pieces of land. When [[Wario]] enters a Battle Ring, a force field surrounds the area, preventing Wario from being able to leave. Numerous enemies make an appearance in the Battle Ring, and Wario has to defeat as many as possible within sixty seconds. Certain Battle Rings have [[column]]s that Wario can use against an enemy.

Once the battle ends, a [[wooden chest]] containing [[coin]]s appears, and the number of coins it contains increases the more enemies Wario has defeated. Once a Battle Ring has been completed, it cannot be revisited.

==In ''Wario World''==
Battle Rings first appear in [[Greenhorn Ruins]], and every level afterward has one Battle Ring of its own except [[Shivering Mountains]] and [[Beanstalk Way]], which feature two.
