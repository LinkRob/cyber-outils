==Features==
This level takes place on a mountain. [[Toadette]] rides a mine cart to the [[Power Star]], while throwing [[Turnip]]s to defeat enemies and collect items.
==Super Gems==
*'''Super Gem 1''': The first Super Gem is located to the left of a high cliff after the first three [[Piranha Plant]]s.
*'''Super Gem 2''': The second Super Gem is under a stone arc above the track near the first descent.
*'''Super Gem 3''': The third Super Gem is behind a wall of bricks that can be broke by throwing a Turnip at the [[POW Block]].
