'''Hermetic Pants''' is a clothing item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It can only be equipped by [[Mario]].

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Hermetic Pants can be bought from the clothes shop in [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 300 [[coin]]s, or 340 in the Japanese version. Equipping it increases Mario's [[Defense]] by 45. It also grants him the [[60% Poison Dodge]] ability.

In the remake, the Hermetic Pants is instead found at [[Gwarhar Lagoon]]. Its Defense rating was increased to 59, and it instead has [[Emergency DEF]] as its special effect. Hermetic Pants can be sold for only three [[coin]]s.
