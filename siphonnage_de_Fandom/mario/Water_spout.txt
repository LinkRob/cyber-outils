'''Water spouts''', alternatively named '''water spigots''', are a type of object in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. 

==Description==
These are objects that shoot streams of water straight upward in timed intervals. A water spout can briefly carry [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] into the air if they move into one. In their [[Ice Mario|Ice]] form, either Mario or Luigi can freeze the surface of the water to form temporary ice platforms. If Mario or Luigi touch a water spout stream while in their [[Bee Mario|Bee]] form, they revert to normal, just as with any body of water.
