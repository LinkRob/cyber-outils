}}'''GBA Bowser Castle 3''' is the third Bowser Castle in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' and the final course in [[Star Cup]]. The course takes place outside of [[Bowser's Castle|the castle]], the background proving that. At the end of the course, near the finish line, [[Kamek]] can be seen swooping back and forth. This course includes lava as a hazard and sharp turns. Other hazards in the course include [[Thwomp]]s and [[Podoboo]]s. This course reappears in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. In the ''Mario Kart Wii'' version, the graphics are updated and 3D instead of 32-bit 2D, an the road is pale-grey with a slight pink tint instead of sky blue. Also, Kamek does not return, a goal gate taking his place at the starting line.

The course makes a return in ''Mario Kart Tour'' with more ramps on the course and having a similar environment to the first [[Bowser Castle 1 (Mario Kart: Super Circuit)|Bowser Castle]].
