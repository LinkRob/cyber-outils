A '''Falling spike''' is a hazardous object which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario World]]''===
[[File:FallingSpikeSMW.png|left]]
Falling spikes appear commonly in the [[castle]] levels. Falling spikes are often found beside bunch of other spikes and they can be told apart from their darker color scheme. Falling spikes will often fall just before [[Mario]] reaches one of them.
