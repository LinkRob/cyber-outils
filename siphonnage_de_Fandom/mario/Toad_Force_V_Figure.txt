The '''Toad Force V Figure''' is a key item in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Toad Force V Figure is an action figure based on the [[Toad Force V]] television show. According to [[Lantern Ghost (character)|Lantern Ghost]], every "real fan" of the show has one. A Toad Force V Figure is also featured as part of the [[Toad Force V (Gaddget)|Toad Force V]] [[Gaddget]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
When the player's character visits Lantern Ghost in the [[Horror Condo]], Lantern Ghost is annoyed that they do not have a Toad Force V Figure. The [[Nerd Force V]] quest involves having to purchase a Toad Force V Figure at [[Junk]], where they can be bought, according to Lantern Ghost.

At Junk, there is only one Toad Force V Figure in stock, as [[Paratroopa (character)|Paratroopa]] says that they are selling very quickly due to the show's popularity.

When the player's character returns to Lantern Ghost with the Toad Force V Figure, he notices the figure's mint condition, and promptly takes it from them. Now deeming the player's character to be an official Toad Force V fan, Lantern Ghost gives them the [[Poochy Pal]] [[Gaddget]] as a reward.
