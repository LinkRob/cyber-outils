'''Charred''' is a status effect in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
Charred is a stronger form of the [[Burn]] status effect, and it can be inflicted only on enemies by having [[Mario]] use the [[Super Attack|Advanced Command]] form of his [[Fire Bros. (move)|Fire Bros.]] move. Enemies inflicted by Charred have a small flame icon above them, and any enemy under the effects of Charred takes a lot damage each turn for the remainder of the battle.
