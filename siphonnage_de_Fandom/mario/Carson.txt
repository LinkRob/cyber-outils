|race = [[Flip-Flopian|Flopsider]]}}'''Carson''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Carson is a [[Flip-Flopian]] that is red in color and has a large mustache.

==History==
Carson is the owner of the bar, [[The Overthere (bar)|The Overthere]], that resides in [[Flopside]]. Carson is well known for being very knowledgeable and, for 10 coins, will tell any of his stories. Carson has fifteen stories in total.
