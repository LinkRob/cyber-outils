'''Mushbert''' is a character who appears only in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
He is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with a green-spotted cap, and is a big fan of [[Toad Force V]].

===Personality===
Mushbert is a known fan of the hit TV show ''[[Toad Force V]]'' and enjoys finding collectible memorabilia from the show. However, in the epilogue, it is said that Mushbert outgrew ''Toad Force V'' and started watching ''[[Koopa Quest]]''.

==History==
He can be found on the first floor of the [[Mushroom Condo]]. Once the player enters his apartment without being on a quest, he forces them to leave, saying that he is busy.

The quest [[DVD for Me]] involves going to Mushbert's apartment to get a rare ''Toad Force V'' DVD for [[Ninji (character)|Ninji]]. However, he did not mind giving one away; he had forty-nine more. After completing the quest, Ninji states that only fifty were made, meaning Mushbert had bought them all.

The quest [[What's That Line?]] involves going to his apartment to learn the famous quote from episode 28 for [[Lakitu (character)|Lakitu]].
