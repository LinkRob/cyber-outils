'''Fire Bros. Hideout #2''' is a level of World 5 in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The Blockade can be accessed via a Warp Pipe near Bob-ombs Below. The arena is similar to Big Galoomba Blockade, but contains one Fire Bro on a Goomba Tower and stone platforms which alternate between rising and lowering.

==Green Star==
Defeat the [[Fire Bro]]. by using the rising platforms or by destroying the [[Goomba]]s on the tower to get the Star.
