'''Banana Bunch''' is a [[minigame]] which appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]].

==Gameplay==
[[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] is the playable character, and he has to hit panels of [[banana]]s and [[Banana Bunch]]es using a tennis ball. Each time Donkey Kong hits a panel, he earns [[point]]s, with a Banana panel rewarding one point while Banana Bunch panels reward three. The minigame ends once Donkey Kong misses hitting the ball.

The game has a default high score of 60, and clearing it unlocks the [[Jungle Court]].

The music includes arrangements of the opening theme and the jingle for the How High Can You Get? screen from the original ''[[Donkey Kong (game)|Donkey Kong]]'' arcade theme, as well as the title theme from the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] version. Its music is also shared with the Jungle Court.
