'''Assist Mode''' is a mode in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. When the player uses Assist Mode, it modifies the gameplay to make the video game easier for the player to complete.

== Description ==
Assist Mode is available in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. If the player uses Assist Mode, the words will appear on the bottom-right corner of the screen. Assist Mode gives the player benefits such as the following:
* The player has six health points by default, and can gain up to nine by finding a [[Life-Up Heart]].
* The player will heal by standing idly.
* If the player falls into deadly hazards (i.e. poison, abyss), the player will lose one heart and float back to the hazardless ground.
* In the story, arrows will guide the player.
* The player will never drown in water.
* Longer timers
