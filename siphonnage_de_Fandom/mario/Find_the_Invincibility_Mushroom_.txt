'''Find the Invincibility Mushroom!''' is the fourth level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 20.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 8 [[Gritty Goomba]]s. The enemy captain is a Gritty Goomba.

The 2nd battle contains 4 Gritty Goombas, 3 [[Limbo Bro]]s, and 1 [[Hammer Bro]]. The enemy captain is a Gritty Goomba.

The final battle contains 7 Limbo Bros, and 1 Hammer Bro. The enemy captain is a Limbo Bro.
