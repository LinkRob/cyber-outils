A '''ByeBye Balloon''' is an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
A ByeBye Balloon is a [[Glue Globe]] with several regular balloons attached to it; the balloons have colors varying from red, blue, yellow, and green. A ByeBye Balloon is always found near the entrance of a course's boss, and [[Wario]] can use the ByeBye Balloon return to the start of the course he is in, allowing him to more easily collect any missing [[treasure]]s and other collectibles.

==In ''Wario World''==
If Wario has completed a course and defeated its boss, a ByeBye Balloon appears the start of the course. Wario can use this ByeBye Balloon to be take to the middle of the course he is in.
