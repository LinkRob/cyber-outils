'''Gooper Blooper Court''' is a gimmick court which appears in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
The court takes place on top of the yellow platform in [[Ricco Harbor]]. The spectators are [[Blooper]]s. In a standard match, the floor is semi-translucent blue glass, and several [[Cheep Cheep]]s can be seen swimming.

In gimmicks mode, the floor features different platforms, four on each side. When the yellow or green platforms move either left or right, depending on the arrow, the characters are exposed to some of the [[water]], which they cannot walk on. If a ball lands on a movable tile, it slides in the direction of the arrow. If the ball lands onto the water, it is an out. The orange platforms do not move.

==In ''Mario Party Tennis''==
The Special Game that takes place on Gooper Blooper Court is [[Gooper Blooper Volley]]. Here, the player's character has to do a tennis match with [[Gooper Blooper]], a homage to Gooper Blooper being the boss of Ricco Harbor in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.
