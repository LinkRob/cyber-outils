'''Machine Mades''' are creations of [[Smithy]] that are enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are only fought in [[Smithy Factory|The Factory]], and comprised [[Shyster]]s, [[Drill Bit]]s, and copies of the previously defeated higher-ups of the [[Smithy Gang]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Machine Mades are more distorted versions of the regular bosses of the Smithy Gang. They include [[Mack]] along with a [[Shyster]], [[Bowyer]], [[Yaridovich|Speardovich]], and each of the [[Axem Rangers]].

==Trivia==
*Out of all the Machine Mades, only the Machine Made Speardovich is required to defeat in battle in order to proceed through the game.
**This one also has the same [[Psychopath]] message as the real Speardovich.
