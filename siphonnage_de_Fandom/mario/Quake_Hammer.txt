'''Quake Hammer''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
In all of its appearances, the Quake Hammer gives [[Mario]] a new [[hammer]] ability. It can be worn by him for two [[Badge Point|BP]] and used in battle using two [[Flower Point|FP]].

== In {{series|Paper Mario}} ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
Quake Hammer is a [[hammer]] badge that deals damage equal to [[Mario]]'s equipped hammer to all ceiling and ground enemies. This badge's attack also flips all turtles and beetles on the ground as well. Mario can obtain more powerful variants of this badge with [[Power Quake]] and [[Mega Quake]]. This badge is found on [[Mt. Rugged]]. On the mountain, this badge is in the area of one of the letters. Using [[Parakarry]] to get onto the area with a letter, then using him again to get onto the next ledge will result in obtaining this badge.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
Quake Hammer features the same abilities it has in ''Paper Mario'', but can now pierce defense and can use the powers of Power and Mega Quake by equipping multiple Power Quake badges. This badge is found at the [[Boggly Woods]]. This badge is in ? Block hidden behind a tree on the way to [[Madame Flurrie]]'s home. This badge can also be obtained at the [[Pianta Parlor]], where it costs 67 [[Pianta Token]]s to acquire.
