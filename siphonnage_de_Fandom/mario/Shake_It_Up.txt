'''Shake It Up''' is a 4-Player and Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in some sort of city.

==Opening==
A character goes over to a cooler beside soda cans, grabs a can on it, and walks back to where the other players are. The minigame starts shortly after that.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Shake It Up duel.png|thumb|Duel version]]
The characters have to shake their soda cans (with the [[Wii Remote]]) as fast as they can for five seconds. The characters then open the cans, and soda comes flying up like a fountain. The character who shook their can the most makes the soda produce the biggest soda fountain of the two or four characters and wins the minigame. However, if no one shakes the cans, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Ending==
The winning character has a glass of soda in their hand and then does a winning pose while still holding the soda. The losing characters stand behind them, doing their losing animations. If the minigame ends in a tie, the cans disappear and the players do their losing animations.

==Controls==
*'''Wii Remote:''' Shake can
*'''Buttons:''' None

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Shake your can of soda pop. After 5 seconds, everyone will open their cans. Make your can spew highest."''
* '''Hint''' &ndash; ''"Don't get too crazy when shaking your can. Small, fast shakes do the trick better than big ones."''
