The '''Jumbo Ray''' (not to be confused with [[Manta Ray]] and other ray-like enemies) is an ally that appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. They appear only in [[World 5-5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5-5]]. [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and the [[Toad]]s can use them as platforms to get themselves across the level. They are considered as an ally, because they do not harm the player purposefully.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Jumbo Rays are like manta rays in appearance. Their skin tone is commonly green, with lighter green spots on their backs. Their undersides are a very light yellow in appearance, with light purple-gray spots as well. The have orange-yellow lips and have goggles that are green and yellow, with light blue visors.

Some images on the internet show that Jumbo Rays can also appear in blue and purple, but is not true for the main game. They do appear in other colors in mods like [https://newermario.fandom.com/wiki/Newer_Super_Mario_Bros._Wii Newer Super Mario Bros. Wii].

=== Behavior ===
[[File:NSMBW World 5-5 .webp|left|thumb|Jumbo Rays in the level 5-5.]]
Jumbo Rays act as moving platforms that Mario and others can stand on. They fly across the level as the level progresses. They are not semi-solids, but solid.

Jumbo Rays cannot get defeated by any means. They are immune to the [[POW Block]] found in the levels, and are immune to the [[Super Star|Starman]].

=== Differences With Other Ray Enemies ===
Surprisingly, there are multiple Ray enemies in the Mario franchise. Here are some differences:

The [[Manta Ray]], unlike the Jumbo Ray, is a water-dwelling ally found in [[Super Mario 64]] and other games (like [[Mario Kart World|Mariokart World]]), whilst the Jumbo Ray has only been seen in the sky (and thus assumed to be a aerial-dwelling ally). The [[Stingray]] is found in Wario World, and are land-dwelling. Again, the Jumbo Ray is an aerial-dwelling ally. The Stingray is also an enemy.

== Other Game Appearances ==
The Jumbo Ray has appeared in multiple other games as a side detail, mostly in backgrounds of games.

* In [[Mario Party: Island Tour]], the Jumbo Ray appears in backgrounds of minigames like "[[Hot-Air Hijinks]]" and "[[Point 'n' Shoot|Point n' Shoot]]".
* In [[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX|Mariokart Arcade GP Deluxe]], the Jumbo Ray appears in the track "[[Tropical Coast Circuit]]".
* In [[Mario Golf: World Tour]], the Jumbo Ray appears in the course "[[Sky Island]]".
* In [[Mario Party 10]], the Jumbo Ray appears in backgrounds of minigames like "[[Bouncy Brawl]]".
