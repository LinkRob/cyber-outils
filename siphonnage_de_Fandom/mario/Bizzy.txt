'''Bizzy''' is minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Appearance ==
Bizzy is a [[Flip-Flopian]] that is blue and white in color. He wears a blue suit with a green tie and glasses.

== History ==
Bizzy can be found in the bar in [[Flipside]]. He is known to work as a magazine editor but often gets sidetracked. His other hobbies include fishing.

== Trivia ==
*Bizzy's name is a pun of the word "busy" which describes his character.
*Bizzy has a [[Flopside]] counterpart in [[Ledger]].
