'''Rumble in the Castle!''' is the sixth level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 30.

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains  6 [[Tail Boo]]s and 2 [[Hammer Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Tail Boo, and he knows [[Sneak Attack]] and [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 3 [[Broozer]]s and 5 [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. The enemy captain is a Broozer, and he knows [[Chance]], Deny, and [[Call for Backup]]. Call for Backup calls a [[Lakitu]] or a [[Para-Beetle]].

The 3rd battle contains 2 Broozer, 5 Lakitu, and 1 [[Big Boo]]. The enemy captain is the Big Boo, and he knows [[Rally]] and Deny.

The final battle contains 1 Broozer, 4 [[Fly Guy]]s, 2 Hammer Bros, and 1 [[Blue Magikoopa|Magikoopa (Blue)]]. The enemy captain is the Magikoopa, and he knows Sneak Attack, Call for Backup, and Deny. Call for Backup calls a Broozer.

==Possible recruits==

*Magikoopa (Blue), (Lv. 31)
*Koopa Paratroopa (Lv. 30-31)
*Boomerang Bro (Lv. 30)
