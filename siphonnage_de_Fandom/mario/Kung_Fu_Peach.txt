'''Kung Fu Peach''' is a power-up in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''

==Overview==
Kung Fu Peach involves [[Princess Peach]] being dressed as a martial artist and performing various kung fu moves. This form is comparable to [[Ninja Peach]].

===Physical appearance===
In her Kung Fu form, Peach wears a gold-lined, long purple kung fu jacket with a gold seigaiha-like pattern on the bottom, a dark teal fabric belt, calf-length kung fu pants of the same color, and traditional black slip-on shoes with yellow soles and two crossed loops of strings wrapping her calves, which are covered in white stockings. Her hair is worn in a high braid held by a wooden stick with her brooch hanging from it; her earrings are red instead of her usual blue ones. Additionally, she wears smudged red eyeshadow applied in a circle around her eyes.

== Powers ==
Peach moves quickly and can punch and kick to break through enemies and obstacles like doors. She can also easily jump onto stumps and swing on poles and bars to spin and kick nearby enemies.
