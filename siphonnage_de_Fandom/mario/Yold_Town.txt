'''Yold Town''' is a town located on [[Mount Lineland]] in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. The town's name is based after "mold town" as there is mold on the roofs of the buildings.

== Description ==
The mayor of Yold Town is [[Old Man Watchitt]]. The population at the end of the events of ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' is a grand total of nine people making it one of the smallest towns in the game. [[Red & Green]] are guards for the mountain town that operate the two bridges to Yold Town. Thousands of years before the events of the game, the [[Tribe of Ancients]] settled here. They were advanced and took part in helping the heroes to save the world. The [[Pixl]], [[Thoreau]], is also found within the undersides of a building heavily implying that the Tribe of Ancients sealed him in there until the hero finally arrived.
