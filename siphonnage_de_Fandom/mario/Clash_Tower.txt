The '''Clash Tower''', also named '''Clash House Tower''',<ref>''Mario Clash'' English instruction booklet, page 8.</ref> is the setting of the [[Virtual Boy]] game ''[[Mario Clash]]''. Little is known about it, but from what is known it appears to be a tower-like building filled with [[Warp Pipe]]s and platforms surrounded by empty space. During the events of ''Mario Clash'', the building is overrun with numerous kinds of enemies, which emerge from the Warp Pipes and attack [[Mario]] as he tries to curb the infestation. Falling off the ledges or hitting the [[Icicle]]s hanging from the ceiling are other hazards Mario faces in the Clash House Tower.

Some artwork from ''Mario Clash'' shows the tower having a large amount of candles attached to the walls, or just floating in the empty space.
