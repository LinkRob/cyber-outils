The '''Fire Cracker''' is an item which first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in both ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
Upon being used, the Fire Cracker will drop behind the racer's [[kart]], similarly to a [[Banana Peel|Banana]]. If an opponent drives into a Fire Cracker dropped behind, they lose speed. Since each Fire Cracker consists of five balls, a single Fire Cracker can hit up to five times.
