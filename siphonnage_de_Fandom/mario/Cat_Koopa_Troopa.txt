'''Cat Koopa Troopas''' are a cat variant of [[Koopa Troopa]] native to [[Lake Lapcat]]. They appear in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

== Physical description ==
Their appearance is similar to Koopa Troopas but they have yellow fur, white-tipped ears and a yellow cat tail.

== In ''Bowser's Fury'' ==
Like a normal [[Koopa Troopa]], Cat Koopa Troopas appear almost everywhere in Lake Lapcat.
