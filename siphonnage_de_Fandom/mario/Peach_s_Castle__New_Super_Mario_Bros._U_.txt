'''Peach's Castle''' is the lava world in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. The world lies north of [[Meringue Clouds]] and [[Rock-Candy Mines]] and east of [[Frosted Glacier]]. The world can be accessed by beating the level [[Boarding the Airship]] or [[Bowser Jr. Showdown]].

This world contains six levels, three normal, one leads to a secret stage, and two castle levels.
