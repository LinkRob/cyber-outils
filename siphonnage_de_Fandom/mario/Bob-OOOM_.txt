'''Bob-OOOM!''' is a single-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Layout==
The player's character stands on a thin platform held up by three bronze [[Bowser Statue]]s on the left and right sides each. The Bowser Statues themselves are standing on a thin platform of an identical type.

==Opening==
A Bob-omb drops into the hands of the player's character from above. After a second, the character drops the Bob-omb, which explodes onto the bottom two Bowser Statues, destroying both of them.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to drop Bob-ombs with the right timing so that they explode onto at least one Bowser Statue, thereby cracking and destroying it. The player earns [[point]]s for destroying the Bowser Statues. If a Bob-omb is flashing, it indicates that it will soon explode, and if the player holds the Bob-omb too long, the resulting explosion temporarily stuns them. Destroying two Bowser Statues at once adds four seconds to the timer; destroying three at once adds six seconds; and destroying four at once adds eight seconds. When an entire set of Bowser Statues is destroyed, more appear to replace them.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the player must earn 1,500 points to win the minigame.

==Controls==
* {{button|gba|Pad}}: Move left or right
* {{button|gba|A}}: Drop Bob-omb
* {{button|gba|B}}: Dash

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
*The minigame name is a portmanteau of "Bob-omb" and "boom."

==Flavor text==
*'''Instructions:''' ''Time your Bob-omb drops so that you can blast away all the Bowser statues!''
