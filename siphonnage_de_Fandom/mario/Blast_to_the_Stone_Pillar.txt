'''Blast to the Stone pillar''' is the fifth mission (fourth in DS) of [[Jolly Roger Bay]] in [[Super Mario 64]] and it's [[Reissue|remake]]: [[Super Mario 64 DS]].

== Description ==
To collect the fifth [[Power Star]] of [[Jolly Roger Bay]], (fourth in the DS version) [[Mario]] must shoot to a stone pillar at the top of the level for a Star. At the start of the level, there will be a small stone platform at the surface of the water with a [[Bob-omb Buddy]] on it. When Mario talks to him, he will open up a [[cannon]] for him to use. When Mario goes into the cannon, he needs to aim for a stone pillar located at the top of the level that he can grab on to. Mario can then jump off the pillar to a small platform with a box on it. When Mario breaks the box, a Star will appear and he can collect it.
