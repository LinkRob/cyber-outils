The {{nihongo|'''Comet'''|スーパーコメット|Sūpākometto|Super Comet}} is an unlockable bike part in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', it is available from the start.

== Description ==
The Comet is a superbike with the emblems located on its sides. It has a turbine-shaped fairing. In the ''Mario Kart 8'', it has great acceleration, water handling, air handling and ground handling. In ''8 Deluxe'', its statistics have changed with air speed, acceleration, ground handling, air handling and mini-turbo as its strengths.
