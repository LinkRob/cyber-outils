'''Hip Hop Drop''' is a Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each character rides on their pogo stick as they cross a large bridge made of square tiles, and the goal is to reach the end first. The characters must press the button shown on the tile in front of them. If the player presses the wrong button, a tile drops out below their character, which then falls, losing a few seconds as they return to their previous spot. Whoever reaches the end first is the winner. If the minigame goes on for five minutes without anyone reaching the end, the minigame ends in a tie.
