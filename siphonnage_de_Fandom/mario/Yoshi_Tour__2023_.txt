'''Yoshi Tour''' was the ninety-third tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']], that began on April 4, 2023 and ended on April 18, 2023. This Tour was a rerun that began on April 2, 2024 to April 16, 2024 and April 1, 2025 to April 15, 2025. 

This tour mainly features the [[Yoshi (franchise)]] theme which added many items from [[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]] including [[Yoshi's Island (Mario Kart series)|Yoshi's Island]] that made it's first debut in the [[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]], [[Booster Course Pass|Booster Course Pass Wave 4]]. It also added [[Poochy]] who becomes a playable character for the first in the [[Mario Kart (series)]]. It introduced [[Yoshi's Cookies]] which is a new glider added.
