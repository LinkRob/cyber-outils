'''Mario & Yoshi's Rolling Attack''' is a move performed by [[Mario (Super Mario-kun)|Mario]] and [[Yoshi (Super Mario-kun)|Yoshi]]. Mario and Yoshi dubbed and performed the move at ''[[Super Mario-kun Vol 1 2]]'' to defeat the [[Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant]].

== Description ==
[[File:Super Mario-Kun Vol 1 2 Page 16 Panel 2 and 3.jpg|thumb|150px|left|Mario and Yoshi getting their bodies tangled after using '''Mario & Yoshi's Rolling Attack'''.]]
From a further distance, Mario and Yoshi dash towards the weak point and curls into a ball to attack the enemy's weak point as seen after defeating the [[Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant]]. However, Mario and Yoshi tangled their bodies to each other. Yoshi said that they will never use the move again.
