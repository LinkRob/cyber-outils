'''Jumpin' Rope''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'' under [[Jimmy T]]'s [[Sports]] folder. Jumpin' Rope was later remade for ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', also including it as one of [[Jimmy T]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to [[jump]] over a jump rope held by two characters up until the [[time limit]] expires. If successful, the player wins at the microgame, but if the character trips on the jump rope, the player loses at the microgame.

Every difficulty level features a different playable character, with each later level having one who jumps lower than in lower difficulty levels: Level 1 features a small human as playable; Level 2 has a bird; and Level 3 has a Moai.
