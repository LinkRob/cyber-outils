'''Ōsama''' is a character whose only sole appearance was in the [[Satellaview]]-broadcast title ''[[BS Super Mario USA]]'', in which he is solely referred to by his kingly title in both dialogue and the closing credits, with no proper name ever being given. 

Ōsama is king of [[Subcon]] rules the kingdom peacefully along with many of the fairies also known as [[Subcon (species)|Subcons]], although he himself is human, as is [[Shireikan|his right-hand man]]. His world was attacked by the [[8 bits]], led by [[Wart]], in during both [[Super Mario Bros. 2|''Super Mario Bros. 2'']] and ''BS Super Mario USA'', although Ōsama only appeared in the latter game.

==History==

===''BS Super Mario USA''===
After their defeat in the [[Super Mario Bros. 2|Subcon Incident]], Wart and his cronies escaped to another dream realm and laid low for some time, before returning to Subcon once again. This prompts Ōsama to call [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]] and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] back to Subcon using the power of the stars.

While Mario and the others battle Wart's army, images of the main characters, both heroic and villainous, appear on screen, delivering commentary and assisting the player on their quest. Like the other characters, Ōsama can either cause earthquakes like a [[POW Block]], allow characters to make heavy stomps on the ground, transform Mario into his [[Invincible Mario|invincible form]], destroy all enemies of the screen's range, or give [[Bomb|bombs]] to him.

==Trivia==
*His royal fanfare is the first bar from "[[w:c:March of the Volunteers|March of the Volunteers]]", China's national anthem.
*Ōsama was voiced by the late [[w:c:Chikao Ōtsuka|Chikao Ōtsuka]], who also did the voice of [[Wario]] in his Japanese commercials.
