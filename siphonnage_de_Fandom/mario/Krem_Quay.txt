'''Krem Quay''' is a swampy area located upon [[Crocodile Isle]] in the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]]'' series. The area itself also has a sunken ship (implying that, like Crocodile Cauldron, this area could've once been a harbour), brambles and polluted water.

==Games==
===''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest''===
Krem Quay first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''.

In the game, Krem Quay is the third world in the game, during [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]]'s quest to save [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] from the clutches of [[King K. Rool|Kaptain K. Rool]]. Here, there are primarily swampy levels, a gangplank level, an underwater level, and a mast level. The boss in this world is [[Kudgel]], a related species to [[Klubba]].

At the end of the game, K. Rool is knocked into the power source for powering up [[Crocodile Isle]], with his bulk blocking the energy flow. Then, the energy has excess build-up, causing it to overflow and shoot off [[K. Rool's Keep]] and the [[Lost World (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Lost World]] (its location). Then, all the build-up causes the entire Crocodile Isle, including Krem Quay to sink underwater.

In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of the game, Krem Quay is relocated to the left side of Crocodile Isle, rather than the right side.

===''Donkey Kong Land 2''===
While Krem Quay doesn't physically appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'', the resurrection of [[Crocodile Isle]] caused Krem Quay and [[Crocodile Cauldron]] to form a new world, [[Krem Cauldron]].
