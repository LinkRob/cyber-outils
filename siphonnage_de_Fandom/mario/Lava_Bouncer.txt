'''Lava Bouncers''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Lava Bouncers are a blue variant of [[Podoboo]]s, much like [[Blue Podoboo]]s themselves.

===Behavior===
Lava Bouncers move around by bouncing. They attack by hopping towards [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] and ramming him. However, the creatures will vanish about ten to twenty seconds after their emergence from a lava pool, meaning Mario can destroy them by waiting them out. Lava Bouncers can only be destroyed directly by launching a [[Star Bit]] at them, also releasing one [[coin]] in the process.

== Appearances ==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
In ''Super Mario Galaxy'', Lava Bouncers first appear in the [[Good Egg Galaxy]] during the battle against [[King Kaliente]] on the [[Good Egg Galaxy#Magma Planet|Magma Planet]]. King Kaliente summons them as allies during his boss battle only after Mario knocks off King Kaliente's crown with the second hit. They surround the Magma Planet and simply bounce around it, increasing the difficulty of avoiding them and defeating King Kaliente. This is because the player must watch out for King Kaliente's fireballs and wait for him to shoot [[Coconut]]s while also avoiding the Lava Bouncers bouncing around the planet. If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] comes in contact with a Lava Bouncer, they will be burned and react as if they have touched [[lava]] by jumping and running around uncontrollably for a few seconds.

Lava Bouncers also appear when fighting [[Black King Kaliente]] in [[Bowser Jr.'s Lava Reactor]], where King Kaliente spawns them sooner than before. In this galaxy, Lava Bouncers do bounce around before the boss shows up, making their appearance in the pre-boss area after Mario makes his way to the Lava Reactor.

Lava Bouncers are lastly found in the [[Melty Molten Galaxy]], in which they are regular enemies. Some of them guard a [[Silver Star]] in the [[mission]] [[Burning Tide]], while others guard [[Star Chip]]s in [[Through the Meteor Storm]].

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
Lava Bouncers appear in the [[Boss Blitz Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', once again aiding King Kaliente during his battle. When he is defeated, all Lava Bouncers will disappear, just as in the previous game.

== Behind the scenes ==
=== Etymology ===
Lava Bouncers are named since they come from lava pools and bounce around to move.
