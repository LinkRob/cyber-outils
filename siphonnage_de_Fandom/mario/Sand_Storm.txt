|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Sand Storm''' is a deadly attack that is used by certain enemies/boss found in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description and Effects==
Whenever Sand Storm is being used, the entire screen turns into a faded brown and a shaking noise is heard. Sometimes whenever Sand Storm hits, it could cause [[Fear]] to a character. A [[Fearless Pin]] is a great defense  item against it.
