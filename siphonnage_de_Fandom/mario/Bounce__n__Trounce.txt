'''Bounce 'n' Trounce''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'', and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''. 

==Layout==
[[File:Bounce 'n' Trounce BG.png|thumb|Background in ''Mario Party 3'']]
The minigame takes place on a midair 3×3 playing field over a [[pit]] in a circus tent. The purple starry background has trees and floating stars in ''Mario Party 3'' and ''The Top 100'' and curtains with starry patterns and glowing lights in ''Superstars''.
{{br}}
==Gameplay==
[[File:BouncenTrounce.png|thumb|left|Appearance in ''Mario Party 3'']]
[[File:Bounce 'n' Trounce 3D.png|thumb|''Mario Party: The Top 100'']]
Players on bouncing balls have to knock other players off the platforms by [[jump]]ing and spin-attacking. Later in the minigame, the square-boxed platforms fall off one by one. The last player standing wins. In ''Mario Party 3'', if multiple players survive after time runs out, the minigame ends in a draw. In ''Mario Party Superstars'', if everyone falls off, the last player to do so wins.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YmMy3-ZZ0PY</ref>
{{br}}
