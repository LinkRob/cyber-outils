'''Jumping Sudden Death''' is a 1-on-1 minigame exclusive to the Vs. Battle mode in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

== Layout ==
Like [[Bounce and Pounce]] from ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', the minigame takes place in a sky, with clouds and rainbows in the background. The only difference are hazardous spikes on the bottom of the screen.

== Gameplay ==
[[File:Jumping Sudden Death icon.png|thumb|right|Icon]]
The aim of the game is that the player has to avoid the spikes and survive the longest. Player 1 controls two [[Mario]]s at a time, while Player 2 will control two [[Luigi]]s at a time. Both players get three lives, and landing on the spikes will take out a Bro. Players can tap on the Touch Screen to bounce their Bro up to avoid the spikes. Victory goes to the Bro that survived the longest.
