The '''Leaf Tires''' are ''[[Animal Crossing]]''-themed [[tire]]s introduced in the ''[[Animal Crossing x Mario Kart 8]]'' downloadable pack, and are included in the base game for ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. They are associated with the [[kart]] [[City Tripper]] and the [[glider]] [[Paper Glider]].
