A '''Kriffid''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are plant-like enemies encountered in [[Land's End]] and [[Bean Valley]], and are relatives of [[Artichoker]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, Kriffids often attack with special moves such as [[Blast]]. They can even use the powerful [[Sand Storm]] move as well.

===Strategy===
Kriffids are weak against [[Ice (element)|Ice]], so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] technique is very effective against them.
