'''''Nintendo Today!''''' is a news application by [[Nintendo]]. It was shown during the [[Nintendo Direct]] on March 27, 2025, and was released on the same day.

==Description==
''Nintendo Today!'' delivers information on Nintendo products, including their games, software, and events for ''[[Nintendo Switch Online]]''. It also functions as a calendar, and an animation with the current date is shown whenever they launch the app. The theme of the application can be based on various series, also affecting the interface and date animation.

The user is required to have a [[Nintendo Account]] before they can start using ''Nintendo Today!''.
