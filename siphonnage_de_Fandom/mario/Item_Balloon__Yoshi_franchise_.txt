'''Item Balloons'''<ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' Player's Guide. Pages 15, 36, 55, 96, and 98.</ref> (or '''Items Balloons'''),<ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' Player's Guide. Pages 6 and 9.</ref> also known as '''Balloons''',<ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'' Player's Guide. Page 12.</ref> are different-colored balloons with smiley faces that appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]'', ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', and ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''. Item Balloons carry harmful or helpful [[list of items|item]]s ranging from [[coin]]s and [[extra life|extra lives]] to [[Shy Guy]]s, which are dropped if the balloons are popped with an [[Yoshi's Egg|egg]] ([[yarn ball]] in ''Yoshi's Woolly World''), with a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s tongue, or by direct contact. A trio of Item Balloons also carries [[crate]]s together. They also help in the final battle against [[Baby Bowser]] by dropping [[Giant Egg]]s. In ''Yoshi's Crafted World'', these balloons can also be popped by [[Go-Go Yoshi]]'s punches in the [[Go-Go Yoshi (level)|level named after it]] and the ''Triceratops'' skull in [[Dino Smash]].
