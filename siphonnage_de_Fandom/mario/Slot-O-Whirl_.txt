'''Slot-O-Whirl!''' is a single-player Bowser [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
The character arrives on a moving platform. On that platform, a giant mechanical slot machine constructs itself in front of the character.

==Gameplay==
The player must push {{button|gcn|A}} for the three individual slots and have them land on the [[key]] and not the colored [[Bowser]] faces. If a slot wheel lands on a key, the next wheel spins faster. If the player can get the key to appear on all three slots before the 30-second time limit expires, the player wins.

==Ending==
[[File:Single Player Bowser MP7 Endings.png|thumb|The ending to Slot-O-Whirl!]]
The slot machine gets disassembled. If the player wins, they get their hands on the key, use it to escape through a door at the north, and celebrate their victory on their way out. If the player loses, Bowser crushes them, laughs, and raises one of his fists in triumph.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Stop the slot

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Line up 3 key symbols on the slot machine to get a key of your own and win!"''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"Watch the slot's timing and press {{button|gcn|A}} when the key flies by. It moves fast, so you may have to guess!"''
