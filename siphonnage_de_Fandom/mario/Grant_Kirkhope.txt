'''Grant Kirkhope''' (born July 10, 1962) is a Scottish video game composer, film composer, and voice actor. He is best known for his time working at Rare for composing the music for the Donkey Kong game. He was also the voice of Donkey Kong for various games. He was then later replaced by [[Takashi Nagasako]] in [[Mario Power Tennis]].
