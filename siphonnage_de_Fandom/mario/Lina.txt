'''Lina''' is an Arabian girl and a protagonist in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'', released overseas as ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. She is the girlfriend of [[Imajin]].<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KL9o9zzCUsQ&t=8m38s</ref>

==Physical description==
Lina is a dark-haired girl who wears a purple veil fastened by a band with a heart in the center, a used shirt, pink pants, and red pointy shoes. Some artwork shows her with a magic wand which has a star attached to the end.

==In ''Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic''==
Lina is one of the four playable characters, along with Imajin, [[Mama (Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic)|Mama]], and [[Papa (Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic)|Papa]]. Lina joins Imajin and his parents to rescue [[Poki and Piki]] from [[Wart|Mamu]]. Lina can [[jump]] and float in the air for a short time, but she is lacking in both speed and strength.

In ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', Lina was replaced by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]].
