'''Thunder Bolt''' is a recurring item in the {{series|Paper Mario}}, and it first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
[[Mario]] can find this item in various locations or at [[Item Shop]]s, which sell Thunder Bolts for eight [[coin]]s each. A Thunder Bolt can deal 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage to an enemy. Like other attack items, Thunder Bolt pierces through enemy defenses. Because Thunder Bolt is an electric-based item, electrical enemies are not affected by this item.

There is a similar item named [[Thunder Rage]], which has the same effect except it is inflicted on all enemies making it the more effective item.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
[[File:Thunder Bolt.png|Thunder Bolt]]
Thunder Bolt is found in the shop in Western [[Rogueport]] and in some other shops. The item sells for ten coins.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Thunder Bolt can be found early on in the game. Depending on Mario's performance with the action command, this item deals five to seven hits of damage to enemies.
