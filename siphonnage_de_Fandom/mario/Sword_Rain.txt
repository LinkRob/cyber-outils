|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Sword Rain''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can only be used by [[Smithy]] and [[Shyper]]s during the final battle.

==Effects==
When Sword Rain is used, a bunch of tiny swords rain on the screen. Sword Rain deals rather high damage to all members of [[Mario]]'s party.
