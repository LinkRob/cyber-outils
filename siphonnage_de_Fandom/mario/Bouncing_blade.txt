The '''bouncing blade''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Advance]]''.

==Description==
The bouncing blade is a pointy light-blue object that lies on the ground, expanding and contracting repeatedly.

==In ''Super Mario Advance''==
The bouncing blade is a rare object only found inside the second [[jar]] of [[World 5-2 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5-2]]. If a playable character touches the bouncing blade, they lose a heart point. The bouncing blade cannot be taken out regularly, as it is unaffected by the nearby [[vegetable]] and [[POW Block]] in the same jar. A character can only take out a bouncing blade by touching it while under the effects of the [[Starman]], although this does not earn them any [[point]]s.
