|name=Hammer x3 Battle Card}}'''Hammer x3''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Hammer x3 is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s "Hammer x3" attack. It is three of the [[Hammer (Battle Card)|Hammer]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied with an orange/white checkerboard background.
