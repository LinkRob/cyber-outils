'''Kind Goomba''' is a quest in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Objective ==
The quest can be completed when the player enters [[Goomba House]] in the [[Town Area]]. [[Goomba (Mario Party Advance)|Goomba]] wants to get a ticket from the [[Train Station]]. The player must head to the Train Station, left of the Goomba House and ask for a ticket from [[Shy Guy]], then he will give the [[Ticket]]. The player must return back to Goomba House and he will give the [[Egg Roll]] [[Gaddget]].
