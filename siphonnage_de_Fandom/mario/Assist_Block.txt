'''Assist Blocks''' are a type of [[block]] featured in certain [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
An Assist Block is a golden block containing either an [[Invincibility Leaf]] or a [[P-Wing]], usually the former. An image of the contained power-up is displayed on the sides of the Assist Block. An Assist Block appears in a [[level]] if either [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or another character has lost a life in that level at least five times. It appears at either the start of the level or near a [[Checkpoint Flag]], if it has been activated, depending on the playable character's progress and therefore the area they restart from after losing a life.

The power-up within an Assist Block always assists the player's character complete a level, such as how the Invincibility Leaf makes the character invincible. In the case of P-Wings, which are only featured in an Assist Block in ''Super Mario 3D Land'', one takes Mario or Luigi to the end of the level. An Assist Block with a P-Wing only appears in a level if the character has lost a life there at least ten times.
