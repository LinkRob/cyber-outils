'''Elite Troopeas''' are a stronger variant of [[Troopea]] and enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They can be found in [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] and [[Joke's End]]. Elite Troopeas can heal their allies just like a normal Troopea, but they can also revive defeated enemies like the [[Soul Bubble]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Elite Troopeas also have a flying variant called the [[Scaratroopea]], which themselves are a stronger variant of [[Paratroopea]].

== Physical description ==
Elite Troopeas have a similar physical appearance to Elite Troopeas, with the main difference being that they wear a red shell like red [[Koopa Troopa]]s.

== Battle ==
Elite Troopeas are found on the map in [[Gwarhar Lagoon]]. Mario and Luigi can stomp or latter Elite Troopeas with a hammer. Elite Troopeas are faster than [[Troopea]]s. Elite Troopeas are accompanied by [[Beanerang Bro|Beanerang Bros.]]. Elite Troopeas can revive themselves, enemies or defeated enemies.

=== Strategy ===
Elite Troopeas are less vulnerable to hammer and hand techniques. However, Elite Troopeas are weak to Luigi's [[Thunderhand|Thunder]] attack despite their defense.
