The '''Concordians''' are a plug-shaped humanoid species living in [[Concordia]]. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

==Description==
Concordians are of humanoid shape, and their heads have eyes resembling type B regional outlets (though [[IDLE]] is an exception) and a small mouth. Their skin tones and proportions vary. ([[Connie]] and Ireen have relatively wide faces and are short, while [[Technikki]], Dulles, and [[Cozette]] are taller, with narrower, taller heads). Each one has wires coming out of the top of the head, acting as "hair," though some have typical facial hair and eyebrows, such as [[Mayor Leyden]] of [[Bulbfish Island]] and the [[Monster Maniac]].
