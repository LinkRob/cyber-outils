'''Goomba Spotting''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. It makes a reappearance in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
Your aim is to get the same number or the closest number of [[Goomba]]s. Clicking once is worth one. [[Buzzy Beetle]]s can also be seen running with the Goombas but players should avoid clicking for them as they won't count, only Goombas are worth counting. The player with the closest or exact number of Goombas is the winner. Player with the farthest number is placed last.

==Controls==
{{System Buttons|Wii|A}} - Click once per Goomba
