A '''Grand Gold Mushroom''' is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
A Grand Gold Mushroom is a Skill Item that raises the Skill level of a teammate whose Skill level had been increased to level 4 using [[Gold Mushroom]]s, such as [[Toad (species)|Toads]], [[Goomba]]s, and [[Piranha Plant]]s as well as the latter two's respective variations.

A Grand Gold Mushroom can be obtained either from opening a [[? Block]] at random, winning it from the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] game in [[Toad House]]s, or from defeating either a [[Goomba Tower|3-Goomba Tower]], [[Big Paragoomba]], and "[[Bullet Bill]] & Goomba" with a 10+ Combo attack.
