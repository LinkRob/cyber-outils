'''Ice Puffs''' are enemies which first appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
These creatures are, as the name points out, an ice variant of [[Ruff Puff]]s. Ice Puffs are found in the small snow field located due west of [[Fahr Outpost]]. similar to a Ruff Puff's lightning attack, Ice Puffs have a deadly ice attack that can freeze [[Mario]] and his partners.
