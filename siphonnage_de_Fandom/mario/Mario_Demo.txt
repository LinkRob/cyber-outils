'''Mario Demo''' is an untitled [[tech demo]] for the [[Virtual Boy]]. It was the start-up screen of the Virtual Boy prototype that was shown at Shoshinkai 1994. The sequence shows a rendered [[Mario]] under the Virtual Boy logo, which flies into the viewer one letter after the next.
