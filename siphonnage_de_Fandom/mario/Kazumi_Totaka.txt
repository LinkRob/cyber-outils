'''Kazumi Totaka''' (born August 23, 1967) is a Japanese video game composer, sound director and voice actor, who has worked on various Nintendo video games as a composer for the game music, he is also the current voice of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and [[Birdo]].
