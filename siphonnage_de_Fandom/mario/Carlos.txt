'''Carlos''' is an audience member in the ''[[King Koopa's Kool Kartoons]]'' episode "[[Good Golly, Miss Molly!]]" During the episode, [[King Koopa (DiC cartoons)|King Koopa]] announces he will be giving away four tickets to the [[List of implied locations#Los Angeles Zoo|LA Zoo]]. The [[Bowser's Minions|Troopa]] who would get tickets will be determined by the seat labeled on the card [[Ratso]] has. After pulling the card away from [[Ratso]], Koopa calls on the Troopa on the seat labeled "Blue 5," who is revealed to be Carlos. King Koopa asks Carlos to do the "Koopa Salute," which he refuses to do. After King Koopa gives Carlos the tickets, King Koopa asks Carlos if he is excited, to which he responds by saying he is bored.
