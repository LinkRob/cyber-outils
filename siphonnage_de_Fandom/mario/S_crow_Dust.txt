'''S'crow Dust''' is a special move used by [[Fink Flower]]s and [[Piranha Plant]]s in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When S'crow Dust is used, a purple light is shown on the target.

==Effects==
With S'crow Dust, the user is able to inflict the [[Scarecrow]] status onto a character, forcing them to only use special attacks, although this can be countered using a [[Trueform Pin]].
