The '''Thunder Stick''' is an item which first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the Thunder Stick, the racer swings it for about five seconds. If an opponent gets too close to the racer using the Thunder Stick, the opponent gets zapped, spins out, and becomes temporarily immobilized. Exclusively appearing in ''Mario Kart Arcade GP'' is a variation named Thunder Stick+, which extends the duration of the attack for about two seconds.
