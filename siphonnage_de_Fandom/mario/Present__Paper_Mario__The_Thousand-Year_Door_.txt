The '''Present''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Description ==
The Present itself has light pink wrapping and a darker pink bow on top.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
The Present can only be obtained from [[Bub (character)|Bub]] during his [[Trouble Center|trouble]] "Help me make up." Bub's mother, [[Sylvia]], was still upset from her argument from [[Goldbob]] and Bub, feeling responsible since his parents were arguing about what to get him for his birthday, decides to give her a present to help make up. The present can then be taken to Sylvia for a reward.
