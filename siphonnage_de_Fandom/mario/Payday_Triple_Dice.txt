The '''Payday Triple Dice''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Description==
It is a more powerful variant of the [[Payday Double Dice]], being a set of three solid gold dice. This item allows the player to roll three [[Dice Block]]s (much like a [[Triple Dice]]), with the player receiving coins equal to the total amount rolled (much like the [[Plus Block]] from the first ''[[Mario Party]]''). The player can potentially get 3-30 free coins with the Payday Triple Dice, along with an additional 20 coin bonus for matching numbers or 50 coin bonus for matching sevens similar to the Triple Dice.

[[Mario]]'s [[Jamboree Buddy]] ability dice roll also adds to the total amount of coins for a maximum of 79 coins (with a roll of matching sevens for 21 coins, the 50 coin bonus, and an 8 from Mario).

==In ''Super Mario Party Jamboree''==
Payday Triple Dice can be purchased from the Gold Shop in [[Rainbow Galleria]] for 15 coins (7 during a Flash Sale or if the player has [[Daisy]] as a Jamboree Buddy, or 30 if the player has a [[Markup Sticker]]) and from Kamek's Shop in [[Goomba Lagoon]] during Pro Rules. Outside of that, it can rarely appear from an Item Bag or Item Space.

==Flavor text==

==Trivia==
*If bought for the regular price of 15 coins, the Payday Triple Dice have a 65.2% chance of paying for themselves — a 64.8% chance of rolling at least a 15 (as noted on the [[Triple Dice]] page) and a 0.4% chance of rolling a triple 1-4 for a 20 coin bonus.
