The '''Slick''' wheels are unlockable wheels in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. They give the best [[speed]] in the game, and are only tied with the [[Gold Tires]]. Although having a great amount of speed, they lack good [[Off-Road|off-road]], also like the Gold Tires, and they lack good [[handling]], and in fact, have some of the worse handling in the game, tying with [[Monster (wheels)|Monster]], [[Red Monster]], and Gold Tires.

The Slick Tires also appears in [[Mario Kart 8]] and Mario Kart 8 Deluxe.
