The '''Delicious Banana''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
The Delicious Banana is one of [[Donkey Kong]]'s [[Special Item (Mario Kart series)|special items]]. It is a recovery item that, when used, removes all status conditions from the user's kart, such as a [[Thunder Cloud]]. Additionally, using the Delicious Banana provides a shield for [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]'s [[kart]] for about half a second.
