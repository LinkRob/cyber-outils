'''Putts''' is a human character in the ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'' series. He first appeared in ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]], and reappears in ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]'', in which he is a playable character.

==Profile==
===Personality===
One of Putts's characteristics is his intelligence and use of advanced vocabulary words.

===Etymology===
Putts's name is derived from the golfing term "putt," meaning to gently hit a golf ball so that it rolls across the green and into the hole.
