'''Dark Wizzerds''' are a variant of [[Wizzerd]] and an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
As the name suggests, these guys are magical masters. These purple mechanic organisms were built by the [[Shadow Queen]] long ago and thus are found in the [[Palace of Shadow]]. These powerful minions have a variety of attacks and can mean serious trouble if unprepared. They also occasionally possess items such as the [[Stop Watch]] or the [[Thunder Rage]].

Their green laser does a random amount of damage, and most of its attacks are difficult to [[Superguard]], so players should take care around them.
