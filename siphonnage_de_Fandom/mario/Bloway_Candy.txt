The '''Bloway Candy''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
The Bloway Candy is a yellow [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] with a picture of a tornado on the wrapper, and when a player uses it, their torso downward becomes a small tornado. This makes them a "Bloway" character, and as they move along the board, any opponents they run into will be blown back to the start. This Candy normally costs 15 [[coin]]s, but this is reduced to five coins if the player's rank is low, which causes the shop to sell its candies at discounted prices. It can be found only on [[DK's Treetop Temple]] and [[Goomba's Booty Boardwalk]].
