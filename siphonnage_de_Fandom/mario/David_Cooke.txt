'''David Robert Cooke''' (born August 30, 1967) is an American voice actor, singer and musician who portrays [[Morton Koopa Jr.]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', replacing [[Lani Minella]] from that game onwards (aside from Lani Minella's reused clips for ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'').
