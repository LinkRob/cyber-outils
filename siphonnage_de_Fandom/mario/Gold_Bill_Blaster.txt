'''Gold Bill Blasters''' are an object in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==Description==
Gold Bill Blasters are a variant of [[Bill Blaster]]s that shoot [[Gold Bullet Bill]]s. They are only present while the effects of a [[Gold Ring (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|Gold Ring]] are active, during which every Bill Blaster becomes a Gold Bill Blaster, just as how all [[Bullet Bill]]s fired from them become Gold Bullet Bills.
