'''Pity Flower''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It can be found on the 40th floor of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].

==Effects==
Pity Flower can be worn by using three [[Badge Point|BP]]. While Pity Flower is equipped, [[Mario]] can recover [[Flower Point|FP]] when he takes damage.
