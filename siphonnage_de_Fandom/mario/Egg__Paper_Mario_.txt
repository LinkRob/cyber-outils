'''Eggs''' are a type of item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Eggs are relatively uncommon. [[Mario]] can obtain one at the base of [[Mt. Rugged]] by searching various bushes. If Mario leaves the main area of Mt. Rugged, he can return to where the station is located to find another Egg. Each Egg gives Mario five [[Heart Point|HP]]. The Egg's main usage is being used in a variety of recipes. More Eggs can be found in [[Jade Jungle]] by pulling down a vine.

After the events of Chapter 4, [[Princess Peach]] and [[Twink]] also can acquire eggs to help them bake a [[cake]] for [[Gourmet Guy]]. The eggs must be added alongside [[Flour]] before mixing them together and baking in the oven.
