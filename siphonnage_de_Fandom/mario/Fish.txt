'''Fish''' are the primary underwater creatures on [[Earth]]. In the {{franchise|Mario}} and related media, they are most commonly ambient components of the underwater-themed levels, particularly in 3D [[Super Mario (series)|mainline]] titles. Fish usually have no interactable elements, passively schooling in the [[water]]. However, they occasionally serve more substantial gameplay purposes, such as in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' and ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]'' Sometimes their placement within levels indicate there is a nearby secret, similar to the [[small bird]]s and [[small animal|animal]]s found on land.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario World'' / ''Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2''===
[[File:SMWSodaLakeFish.png|left|78px]]
Ambient fish first appear in the foreground of some underwater courses of ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its remake ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]'', such as [[Soda Lake]] and [[Star World 2]]. Rather than being individual sprites, all fish are part of a background layer that scrolls at a constant speed; as such, all fish are always at the same distance from each other.

===''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''===
Schools of fish swim around the map of [[Turtle Zone]] in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

===''Super Mario 64'' / ''Super Mario 64 DS''===
[[File:Red fish SM64.png|thumb|left|200px|A fish resembling a red-bellied piranha in ''Super Mario 64'']]
[[File:Blue fish SM64.png|thumb|200px|A blue fish in ''Super Mario 64'']]
Ambient fish appear in the underwater courses of ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. Most fish are blue with black stripes, but some are red instead. Amongst the main courses, fish appear in [[Jolly Roger Bay]] and [[Dire, Dire Docks]]. The blue fish occur in the tanks of the room that holds the [[painting]] for Jolly Roger Bay as well. However, they are most numerous in [[The Secret Aquarium]], accessed within the same room. In the Japanese version, this secret course is named after them. Schools of blue and red fish occur here together.

In any areas where the water is shallow, such as [[Whomp's Fortress]] or the basement of the [[Peach's Castle|Mushroom Castle]], there is a small chance that a fish will leap from the water when [[Mario]] [[jump]]s.
{{br}}
===''Super Mario Sunshine''===
[[File:SMS GelatoBeach BlueCoin 14.png|thumb|200px|Mario approaching a school of blue fish in [[Gelato Beach]] in ''Super Mario Sunshine''.]]
[[File:Red fish SMS model.png|thumb|left|200px|A red fish in ''Super Mario Sunshine'']]
In ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', fish relay the location of [[coin]]s in underwater areas, a role somewhat comparable to [[Bird (Super Mario Sunshine)|birds]] and [[butterfly|butterflies]]. Like ''Super Mario 64'', there are blue and red types. The blue ones are oblong with yellow fins and school near [[Blue Coin]]s in [[Ricco Harbor]] and [[Gelato Beach]]. The red fish are taller and occur only in the [[mission|episode]] [[Red Coins in the Coral Reef]], where they surround hidden [[Red Coin]]s around the reef.

Additional species appear in the seafood markets on [[Isle Delfino]], which is known for its fisheries.
{{br}}
===''Super Mario Galaxy'' / ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
{{quote|Ever since those [[Gringill|big eels]] showed up, we haven't caught ANY fish!|[[Penguru]]}}
[[File:Blue fish SMG model.png|thumb|200px|left|Model of a blue fish in ''Super Mario Galaxy'']]
[[File:Red fish SMG model.png|thumb|200px|Model of a red fish in ''Super Mario Galaxy'']]
The fish in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' lack any interactive components, but the specific types that appear depends on the type of course they are found in. In the beach-themed [[galaxy|galaxies]] whose bodies of water are under the direct sky, such as the [[Beach Bowl Galaxy]] and [[Starshine Beach Galaxy]], five types of fish appear. In underwater caves like the ones found in the [[Deep Dark Galaxy]] or [[Slimy Spring Galaxy]], two different types of dark-scaled fish appear, being either turquoise or purple. Fish always school with members of the same type.

The fish in these games are purely ambient, like the [[seagull]]s also found in these games. The [[penguin]]s state that they eat fish, and a comment from [[Penguru]] implies that [[Gringill]]s do as well.

===''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''===
[[File:Yellow fish in SM3DW.png|thumb|200px|Yellow fish in ''Super Mario 3D World'' resemble angelfish.]]
In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'', fish characterize the beach and underwater-themed courses in the game. These fish are more caricatured than the ones in previous games. There are four types: a [[wikipedia:Fusiform|fusiform]] red fish; an oblong blue fish; a broad green fish; and an angelfish-like yellow fish. They do not provide any gameplay functions.
{{br|left}}
===''Super Mario Odyssey''===
[[File:Koi fish in Super Mario Odyssey.png|thumb|left|200px|Koi fish in ''Super Mario Odyssey'']]
With the exception of the [[Cap Kingdom]], the [[Cascade Kingdom]], and the [[Snow Kingdom]], all of the kingdoms with bodies of water in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' contain fish. Like most elements in the game, the type of fish are thematically tied to the environment of each kingdom. The Lake Kingdom contains many small colorful fish. A pool near the beginning of the Iron Road in the [[Wooded Kingdom]] has small schools of [[wikipedia:Rainbow trout|rainbow trout]] in it. The waters of the [[Seaside Kingdom]] are filled with shimmering gold-colored fish that swim around subjects of interest, like [[bubbler]]s, [[8-bit pipe]]s, and [[glowing spot]]s. The moat of [[Bowser's Kingdom]] contains [[wikipedia:Koi|koi]]. Like the birds and [[small animal]]s found in ''Super Mario Odyssey'', all of the these fish appear in the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Super Mario Odyssey)|Mushroom Kingdom]] once [[Princess Peach]] and [[Tiara]] return to [[Peach's Castle]].
{{br}}
==In ''Virtual Boy Wario Land''==
[[File:Thorny Fish VBWL screenshot.png|thumb|Wario near a Thorny Fish, accompanied by a nearby school of fish.]]
Schools of fish appear in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. One school accompanies each [[Thorny Fish]] seen in [[Stage 3 (Virtual Boy Wario Land)|Stage 3]], swimming back and forth, with regular pauses. They appear to speed up if [[Wario]] is swimming ahead of them. These fish appear to be in the background of the stage, and cannot harm Wario in any way. 

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', fish appear as an obstacle at the [[Midas River]] course, where Mario must jump over them while riding a [[barrel]]. One appears in a cave in the previous Midas Waterfall section, where it flops around after escaping a family of [[Goomba]]s.

==In ''Donkey Kong 64''==
Fish also appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'' as ambient components in the underwater areas of [[Gloomy Galleon]].
{{sectstub}}

==In ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''==
The red fish from ''Super Mario 3D World'' return in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'', retaining the same aesthetic purposes. They can be found in beach and underwater-themed levels such as [[Floaty Fun Water Park]] and [[Briny Bowl Swimming Hole]].

==In ''Princess Peach: Showtime!''==
Fish appear in all the mermaid-themed stages of ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]'' [[Mermaid Peach]] can control them with her singing voice and use then to gather collectibles or pry open [[Clam|clamshells]].

==List of real-life fish==
[[File:Blue fish SMS.png|thumb|The blue fish in ''Super Mario Sunshine'' are similar to the yellow-tail acei.]]
Unlike [[Cheep Cheep]]s and other fish-based characters, ambient fish typically have [[wikipedia:Realism (arts)|realistic]] designs derived from real-world species. These are the real-life fish with incidental or deliberate design similarities to generic fish in ''Mario''-related media.

*[[wikipedia:benthic zone|Benthic]] species: The underwater caves of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' have two different types of dark-scaled fish: turquoise ones resembling [[wikipedia:Sternoptychidae|hatchetfish]] and purple ones resembling [[wikipedia:Viperfish|viperfish]].
*[[wikipedia:Discus (fish)|Discus]]: The taller red fish in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' are similar to them.
*[[wikipedia:Koi|Koi]]: They appear in the moat of [[Bowser's Kingdom]] in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.
*[[wikipedia:Littoral zone|Littoral]] species: The outdoor water environments in the beach-themed [[Galaxy|galaxies]] of ''Super Mario Galaxy'' and ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' include five types of fish that look like them: the red fish look like [[wikipedia:Anthiadidae|anthias]]; the yellow fish look like the butterflyfish of the ''[[wikipedia:Chaetodon|Chaetodon]]'' genus; and the white fish look like [[wikipedia:Moorish idol|Moorish idol]]s and butterflyfish in the genus ''[[wikipedia:Heniochus|Heniochus]]''. The blue fish do not have any reminiscent counterpart in particular.
*Neotropical [[wikipedia:Poeciliidae|poeciliids]]: Many of the small, colorful fish in the [[Lake Kingdom]] of ''Super Mario Odyssey'' look like them.
*[[wikipedia:Pterophyllum|Freshwater angelfish]]: The blue fish in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' are vaguely similar to them. The orange fish in ''Super Mario 3D World'' look like them.
*[[wikipedia:Rainbow trout|Rainbow trout]]: Small schools of them appear in a pool near the start of the Iron Road in the [[Wooded Kingdom]] of ''Super Mario Odyssey''.
*[[wikipedia:Mikrogeophagus|Ram cichlid]]: There are also small colorful fish in the Lake Kingdom of ''Super Mario Odyssey'' that resemble these fish.
*[[wikipedia:Red-bellied piranha|Red-bellied piranha]]: The red fish in ''Super Mario 64'' resemble them. [[Bitesize]]s from ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' are based on them directly.
*[[wikipedia:Pseudotropheus sp. "acei"|Yellow-tail acei]]: The blue fish with yellow fins in ''Super Mario Sunshine'' are similar to them.
