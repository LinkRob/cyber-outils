'''Tamao''' is a creature which appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is an indestructible, ball-shaped, bouncing life form encountered during the boss fight against [[Dragonzamasu]] in the [[Muda Kingdom]].

==Battle==
Tamao is the guardian for Dragonzamasu, whom it assists by blocking the torpedoes fired from [[Mario]]'s [[Marine Pop]]. Tamao is completely invincible, so Mario has to shoot around Tamao in order to damage Dragonzamasu. After Dragonzamasu is defeated, Tamao disappears as well.
