'''Bomb Boos''' (also called '''Dark Boos'''{{Citation needed}} or '''Black Boos'''{{Citation needed}}) are a variant of [[Boo]] which appears in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise, with their debut being in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They are explosive, charcoal-black colored Boos. This enemy is typically a fusion of a [[Bob-omb]] and a Boo, due to its traits of a Bob-omb and its species being a Boo.

== In the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[Image:Bomb Boo Brawl SMG1.png|thumb|left|Mario swinging six Bomb Boos.]]
Bomb Boos make their debut in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. In this game, appear in the [[Ghostly Galaxy]] in the missions [[Beware of Bouldergeist]] and [[Bouldergeist Daredevil Run]]. They first appear inside the mansion and can be tossed at the [[Bowser Statue]]s to destroy them for either items or progression. During the [[Bouldergeist]] battle, Bomb Boos only appear when Bouldergeist throws a small black stone.

They also appear when Bouldergeist loses his rock body and floats lazily around, having Bomb Boos appear from its trail. [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] can use them to deal damage to Bouldergeist. Mario can defeat these Bomb Boos by using his [[Star Spin]] to grab the Bomb Boo by the tongue and fling it around until defeated. If held for too long, it explodes on Mario, injuring him.

=== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' ===
[[Image:Bomb Boo Swing.png|thumb|right|[[Mario]] grabs hold of a Bomb Boo.]]
Bomb Boos reappear in the [[Boss Blitz Galaxy]] once again aiding [[Bouldergeist]] in battle. They act the same way as they did in the previous game and are once again summoned by Bouldergeist's black rocks, they also spawn during his critical phases as well, once again a Bomb Boo can be picked up with a [[Star Spin]] and can damage [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] if held for too long.

==In the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]]==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
[[Image:Boo and Bomb Boo Brawl.png|thumb|left|Two Bomb Boos in battle with several other Boos.]]
Bomb Boos make their ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' series debut in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' appearing exclusively in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' side story as recruitable allies. Bomb Boos are classed as Flying Troops and typically possess the same attacks as a regular Boo, their Special Attacks involve them exploding on their foes for extra damage, they are strong against [[Ice Snifit]]s. Their in-game description is, "A rare type of Boo that explodes! They are especially strong against Ice Snifits."
{{br}}
===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''===
[[Image:Bomb Boo G-Force Agent Data.png|thumb|right|A Bomb Boo]]
Bomb Boos make a return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' this time appearing as recruitable allies in the [[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]] side story aiding [[Bowser Jr.]] and the [[Koopalings]]. Bomb Boos retain their behaviors and attacks from before once again possessing the same abilities as the [[Boo]]s but more explosive. Just as before, they are classed as Flying Troops and their special attacks involve them exploding near any foes.
{{br}}

==Trivia==
