'''Skewer''' is a move used by certain enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Skewer is basically a stronger move that enemies with spears uses. Unlike with [[Pierce]], Skewer can be a little tricky to block against due to the nature of the attack.
