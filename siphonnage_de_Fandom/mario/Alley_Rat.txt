'''Alley Rats''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are a stronger variant of [[Rat Funk]]s fought in the [[Sunken Ship]] and in [[Bowser's Keep]].

==Battle==
In battle, the Alley Rats mainly attack by swinging their tails. They can also use their sole special move, [[Poison (move)|Poison]], that may [[Poison (ailment)|Poison]] one of [[Mario]]'s team. However, this can be countered with either an [[Able Juice]] or [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]]'s healing techniques. Alley Rats are unusually weak and tend to run away from battle. However, they also tend to fight in groups making them deadly.
