'''Halberd''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', then reappeared in one of the subsequent titles, ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''. It later reappeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It is from the ''Kirby'' universe.

===Features===
