'''Bedsmith''' is a character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. He is the one who helps create the [[Ultibed]], being the only one who can do so.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Bedsmith is a yellow [[Pi'illo]] that wears glasses. Also, unlike other Pi'illos, his pillow-shaped head is sideways rather than the usual way.

=== Personality traits ===
Bedsmith has a rather erratic personality as he keeps saying he wants to nap on [[Dreambert|Prince Dreambert]]. He also takes great pride in his work and thinks highly of his artistic skills. Bedsmith also has a habit of speaking in the third person.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' ==
Bedsmith makes his first appearance after Mario and [[Dreamy Luigi]] freed him from a [[Nightmare Chunk]] after defeating [[Earthwake]]. Everyone then head's back to [[Pi'illoper]]'s house where Prince Dreambert tries to convince him to make the Ultibed. Starlow then says that they already gathered the five Zee Parts. After hearing that, Bedsmith then decides to go to [[Somnom Woods]] for his shop in order to help create the Ultibed.
