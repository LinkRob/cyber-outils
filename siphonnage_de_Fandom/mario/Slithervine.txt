'''Slithervines''' are enemies in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Slithervines are rather large carnivorous plants that consist of a purple head and a green vine made from spike ball segments.

===Behavior===
When a Slithervine spots [[Wario]], it attempts to either bite or eat Wario when he gets nearby. Slithervines cannot extend themselves too far, making it sometimes hard for them to reach Wario.

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Slithervines are only encountered in [[Sneezemore Cave]] and [[Ancient Waterworks]], and they are often encountered near other enemies.
