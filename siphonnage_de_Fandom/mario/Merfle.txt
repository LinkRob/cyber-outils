'''Merfles''' ('''メルフル''', ''Merufuru'') are a major fairy-like species in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''. They live in the [[Shake Dimension]], under [[Queen Merelda]]'s rule, and their clothing is featured in a variety of colors such as blue, orange, and brown. A single [[Merfle (character)|light green Merfle]] is featured as a major character.
