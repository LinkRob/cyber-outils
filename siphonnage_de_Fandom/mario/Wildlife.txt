}}'''Wildlife''' is the title of one of [[Donkey Kong]]'s three [[Kart]]s in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. His other two are [[Standard DK]] and [[Rambi Rider]]. It resembles as one of a muscle car jeep without a top hod. It is unlocked by completing all 100cc Nitro Grand Prix cups.

The Wildlife features great speed, average acceleration and weight, poor handling, very good drift and a poor [[Items (stat)|items]] stat.

Its acceleration is tied with [[Wario]]'s [[Brute]] and [[Standard WR]], as well as [[R.O.B.]]'s [[Standard RB]] and [[ROB-LGS]]. Its weight is equivalent with [[Mario]]'s [[Standard MR]] and [[Luigi]]'s [[Standard LG]], [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Royale]], Wario's Brute and [[Waluigi]]'s [[Gold Mantis]]. It also shares its poor handling with [[Princess Daisy]]'s [[Power Flower (Mario Kart DS)|Power Flower]].

Luigi and Princess Peach can use the Wildlife once the player completes all 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix cups, and all racers can use it once all cups in the Mirror Class have been completed. Until then, only Donkey Kong use the Wildlife.
