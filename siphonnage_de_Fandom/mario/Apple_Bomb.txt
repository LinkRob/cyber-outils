'''Apple Bomb''' is an item which appears in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
An Apple Bomb is an explosive that resembles an [[apple]], but the lower part is rainbow-colored. An Apple Bomb has a bomb hidden inside of it.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
An Apple Bomb is one of the items that [[Wario]] can purchase from the [[shop owner]] to deal damage to the boss right before starting the battle. An Apple Bomb is one of the weaker items sold by the shop owner, and an Apple Bomb costs two [[Medal (Wario Land 4)|medals]] to purchase, or three medals in Hard mode and four in S-Hard mode.

When Wario purchases an Apple Bomb and accesses the boss level, the shop owner appears there. Just before the battle begins, the shop owner breaks open the Apple Bomb, revealing a bomb, which she then throws at the boss to deal some damage to it. She then leaves, commencing the battle between Wario and the boss.
