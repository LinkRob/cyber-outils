'''Reverse Minigame''' is one of the minigame types in ''Mario Party 9'' and ''Mario Party 10''. It is a minigame gimmick.

== Description ==
The minigames objective is to be the last player to complete the game, first player to lose or the lowest score in the minigame. It is hosted by Bowser in the Bowser Roulette. The player who wins the reverse Minigame will get 10 [[Mini Stars]] in [[Mario Party 9]],  20 Mini Stars in [[Mario Party 10]]).

== Trivia ==
* In the Italian version of ''Mario Party 9'', instead of calling the minigame "Reverse minigame", it is spelt "!ocoiginiM".
