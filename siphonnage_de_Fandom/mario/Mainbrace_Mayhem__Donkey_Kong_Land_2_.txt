'''Mainbrace Mayhem''' is the second level appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'' and in [[Gangplank Galleon]]. It comes after [[Pirate Panic (Donkey Kong Land 2)|Pirate Panic]] and before [[Gangplank Galley (Donkey Kong Land 2)|Gangplank Galley]].

==Overview==
This level takes place in the high rigging parts of [[Gangplank Galleon]]. This area also has the inclusion of ropes needing to be climbed by the two monkeys in order to reach the top of the level. There are also crow's nests in the area.

Enemies in the level include eight [[Klinger]]s, five [[Click-Clack]]s, seven [[Neek]]s, three [[Zinger]]s and two [[Kruncha]]s.
