'''Poppy Kong''' is one of the main antagonists of the game ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. She is a high-ranking member of [[VoidCo]] who works under her boss, [[Void Kong]], and alongside her fellow associate [[Grumpy Kong]]. Poppy Kong serves as the information gatherer for VoidCo providing them crucial intel to help aid in her company's plans to reach the [[Planet Core]] for the wish of untold riches.

==Appearance==
Poppy Kong is a very tall, female Kong who has purple and black fur with a long tail. She also has long, white hair and wears fancy clothing including a dress shirt, flashy paints, and golden heels. She also wears golden glasses.
