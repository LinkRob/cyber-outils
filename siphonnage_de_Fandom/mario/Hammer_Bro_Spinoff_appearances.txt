==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
[[File:Hammer Bro SMRPG art.jpg|thumb|A Hammer Bro, in ''Super Mario RPG''.]]
{{quote|HEY! Are [[Mario|YOU]] lookin' at me? My hammer and I don't like you! Now ya got my hammer angry! Ya shouldn't have done that!|Hammer Bro|Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars}}
In ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|its remake]], Hammer Bros. are slightly different, with hammers made entirely of wood and bodies that are significantly larger than typical depictions. These Hammer Bros. also speak fondly of their hammers, as if they are sentient, living beings.

The Hammer Bros. appear at the end of [[Mushroom Way]], and [[Mario]] must battle them to proceed to [[Toad Town|Mushroom Kingdom]]. If [[Toad]] has been previously rescued from [[Goomba]]s and [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]]s, the Hammer Bros. also capture him in his rush to get to the castle, and needs to be saved by Mario. During the battle, the Hammer Bros. pound Mario with their hammers, and even used the special move [[Hammer Time]], by throwing multiple hammers at him. When Mario defeats one of the brothers, the remaining brother uses the move [[Valor Up]] to increase his defense. When defeated, they yield a [[Flower Jar]].

Later in the game, a single, green-colored Hammer Bro named [[Dr. Topper]] appears in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]], which has been taken over by the [[Smithy Gang]]; Dr. Topper is not an enemy, per se, but he does block Mario's progress through Bowser's Keep unless Mario solves his various puzzles and riddles.

After drumming, a [[musical note]] travels straight toward the player's character, who must copy the beat to send the musical note at Hammer Bro, harming him. After three hits, Hammer Bro is defeated.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
{{Quote|Nyuck yuck yuck... What took you so long, Mario? Ready to taste my hammer? Here it comes!!!|Hammer Bro|Paper Mario}}
Hammer Bros., along with [[Koopatrol]]s and [[Magikoopa]]s, are the elites of [[Bowser]]'s forces. In this game, Hammer Bros. attack Mario by either throwing one hammer at him or several, weak hammers at him. Additionally, Hammer Bros. occasionally throw a hammer at Mario which can cause him to [[Tiny (ailment)|shrink]] in size and diminish his attack power. Hammer Bros. have a defense point which nullifies a point of damage done to it, so [[Mario]] is often required to use his stronger moves to bring down a Hammer Bro quicker.

A Hammer Bro can be first encountered in the [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]], if [[Princess Peach]] tells [[Bowser]] and [[Kammy Koopa]] that one of the things that Mario fears most is a Hammer Bro,[[Kammy Koopa]] later warps a Hammer Bro into the [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] to guard a treasure chest. Hammer Bros. are also encountered throughout Bowser's Castle, where they act as guards, attacking Mario if he approaches. Additionally, a Hammer Bro by the name of Mr. Hammer runs a quiz called the 64th Trivia Quiz-Off, in which [[Princess Peach]] participates. They are also seen in [[Bowser's Castle]] as semi-common enemies.

===''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', Hammer Bros. return as enemies. They, like several other returning enemies, received an updated design to match their appearance in the {{series|Super Mario}}. It is the first ''Paper Mario'' title where Hammer Bros. do not have a defense point.

Hammer Bros. appear in many [[world]]s. They are first seen in [[Yoshi Sphinx]] and from there, go on to appear in [[Sandshifter Ruins]], [[Drybake Stadium]], [[Shy Guy Jungle]], and [[Chomp Ruins]]. They can also be found in [[Bowser Jr.'s Flotilla]] and assist [[Bowser]] in [[Bowser's Sky Castle]]. Boomerang Bros., Fire Bros., and [[Ice Bro|Ice Bros.]] also appear, the latter making their first appearance in a ''Paper Mario'' game.

Hammer Bros. toss hammers on the map, which damages Mario on contact. In battle, Hammer Bros. attack by throwing hammers, either in the air to hit Mario from above or by throwing them straight at Mario. The Hammer Bro can throw off timing by either waiting for at least two seconds or by throwing the hammer before Mario can even block for one second. Hammer Bros. are one of four enemies that can throw off timing, the other three being [[Snifit]]s, [[Spiny|Spinies]], and [[Scaredy Rat]]s. A Hammer Bro can typically be defeated by using a shiny [[sticker]].

There is a glitch causing one Hammer Bro in [[Shy Guy Jungle]] to stand in mid-air.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
[[File:HammerBroMLSS.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', the first Hammer Bros. encountered by Mario and Luigi are the two [[Border Bro]]s, who act as border patrol between [[Beanbean Kingdom]] and [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. Hammer Bros. are semi-common enemies encountered in [[Bowser's Castle]], only alongside [[Gunner Guy]]s and [[Boo]]s. Like the rest of the [[Koopa Troop]], the Hammer Bros. have been duped by [[Bowletta]] into defending the castle and believing that she is the real [[Bowser]].

In battle, Hammer Bros. attack Mario and Luigi by throwing hammers at them, almost identically to how [[Rookie]] threw them earlier on, except a bit faster. The Hammer Bro [[relay]]s whether he will attack Mario or Luigi depending on his movement: If the Hammer Bro jumps before tossing, he is targeting [[Luigi]], but if the Hammer Bro remains still before throwing the hammers, then [[Mario]] is the target. The brothers can use their own hammer to counter the hammers as they are about to hit them. Upon losing half of its [[HP]], a Hammer Bro starts to attack by tossing two hammers at once. Hammer Bros. are healed by [[Fire (element)|fire]] attacks whilst being weak to [[Thunder (element)|thunder]] attacks.

Hammer Bros. have a somewhat non-standard appearance consisting of white pupil-less eyes, missing shoes, and appearing more hunched over and slightly portly, though not to the extent of a Sledge Bro. In later ''Mario & Luigi'' titles, including the remake ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', Hammer Bros. utilize their standard design, unlike [[Dry Bones]] and [[Spiny|Spinies]], which use designs invoking the off-model designs from the original game.

Besides the Border Bros., the game also introduces various more Hammer Bro variants, including [[Beanerang Bro]] (named "Boomerang Bro" in the original game), [[Yo Bro]], the [[Limbo Bro]] and their [[Elite Limbo Bro|Elite]] counterparts, and the [[Chomp Bro]].

===''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''===
{{Main|L33t Hamm3r Broz.}}
Only one pair of Hammer Bros. appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. The [[Shroob]]s brainwash them with mind-control helmets and order them to guard the [[Vim Factory]]. While mind-controlled, the Hammer Bros. introduce themselves as the [[L33t Hamm3r Broz.]] and speak in the Internet "language" known as "[[Wikipedia:Leet|1337-speak]]" (pronounced "leet speak"), as well as with capital letters. Once the Hammer Bros. are defeated, [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] destroy their mind-control helmets, thus returning the Hammer Bros. to their normal state. Free from Shroob influence, the Hammer Bros. begin to speak normally again (and even comment on the strange manner of speaking they were just using). Grateful for being released, the Hammer Bros. give the babies [[hammer]]s, and teach them how to use the hammer and [[Baby Drill]].

When the babies go into the vents and encounter a [[Spiny Shroopa]], the Hammer Bros. teach them how to use the hammers in battle. Later, when the babies rescue Mario and Luigi, the Hammer Bros. return and show how to use the hammer while piggybacking in battle.

===''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''===
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', Hammer Bros. appear as enemies once again. They also have a paper variant, [[Paper Hammer Bro]]s., and a shiny paper variant named [[Shiny Paper Hammer Bro]]. Fire Bros. and Boomerang Bros. also appear, each also having their own paper and shiny paper variants.

Hammer Bros. first and mainly appear in [[Twinsy Tropics Dungeon]]. They act as guards of the dungeons, where they are working the imprisoned [[Toad (species)|Toads]] to mine [[Fire Ore]] for [[Bowser]] and [[Paper Bowser]]. When [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] are captured by the [[Koopalings]], the Hammer Bros. secretly [[Stuff Retrieval|take away]] their [[Command Block]]s. Mario and Luigi find out about this upon escaping their cell and chase the Hammer Bros. around the caverns. Before the Mario Bros. get the [[Flee]] command back, they can only counterattack the Hammer Bro and not directly. Once they get the Flee command, the player has no choice but to press {{button|3ds|R}} for "Idle" to skip their turn. Upon battling the Hammer Bros., the Mario Bros. manage to successfully earn their Command Blocks back and then search for a way out of the dungeons.

Stronger Hammer Bros. are also seen during Mario and Luigi's third trip to [[Sunbeam Plains]], on the clouds above. One Hammer Bro is also fought alongside a Paper Hammer Bro, a [[Spike]], and a [[Paper Spike]] as one of Bowser and Paper Bowser's last lines of defense in [[Neo Bowser Castle]], and it has the same stats as the Hammer Bros. in Twinsy Tropics Dungeon.

In battle, Hammer Bros. walk up to the targeted [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario brother]] and throws hammers at him. If the Hammer Bro throws a hammer while grounded, the Mario brother must jump over the hammer, and if the Hammer Bro jumps before throwing a hammer, the bro must not jump because the hammer bounces over him. The Hammer Bro then charges at the targeted bro, who can counter the attack by jumping and then landing on the Hammer Bro. Their only other attack involves them throwing a hammer in the air with another Hammer Bro or a paper counterpart; the foes may even swap hammers occasionally before throwing. These hammers must be countered with the Mario Bros.' hammers. If a Paper Hammer Bro participates, their hammer falls after the regular hammers, but if both of the Hammer Bros. involved are regular, their hammers both fall at the same time.

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
[[File:Hammer Bro 3DS sprite.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', Hammer Bros. do not have any major differences in the main story from ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''. Hammer Bros. also have a role in the exclusive ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' side-story, where they are one of the first recruitable allies, aside from [[Goomba]]s. In addition, [[Boomerang Bro]]s., [[Ice Bro]]s., and [[Fire Bro]]s all appear exclusively in the side-story.

In the story of ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'', Hammer Bros. are first encountered in the mission [[For the Love of Hammers]]. Here, two Hammer Bros. are seen teaming up with the [[Hammerhead Bro]]s. on [[Hoohoo Mountain]] and plan on moving in with them. Upon the arrival of [[Captain Goomba (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Goomba]] and his squad, they convince them to join them in their mission to save [[Bowser]] from [[Fawful]]. The Hammer Bros. are conflicted and decide to fight Captain Goomba and his Goomba squad to determine which side they join; if the Hammer Bros. win the fight, they remain with the Hammerhead Bros., but if they lose the fight, they join Captain Goomba. Upon joining Captain Goomba's squad, the Hammer Bros. say farewell to the Hammerhead Bros. they believe that they can push their hammer crafting to the limits by creating the [[Ultra Hammer]], which the Hammerhead Bros. give to Mario and Luigi before [[Joke's End]].

During battles in ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'', Hammer Bros. are Ranged troopers who attack by throwing hammers. Their [[Special Skill (Mario & Luigi series)|Special Skill]] is Rain of Pain, which involves the Hammer Bros. throwing multiple hammers at surrounding foes. Hammer Bros. are very useful against many flying troopers, so deploying many Hammer Bros. against flying enemies is a good strategy.

===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''===
Hammer Bros. appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' as enemies and recruitable allies. They have much of the same role as in ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'', also retaining Rain of Pain as their Special Skill, but they are also weak against [[Parabones]].

===''Mario Superstar Baseball''===
[[File:Hammer Bro Baller.png|thumb|Hammer Bro batting in ''Mario Superstar Baseball'']]
In ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', Hammer Bro is an unlockable playable character, along with [[Fire Bro]] and [[Boomerang Bro]], although both of them unusually serve as [[palette swap]]s while still retaining some differences in stats, much like the palette swaps of [[Noki]] and [[Pianta]]. Hammer Bro, Fire Bro, and Boomerang Bro are all three members of the [[Bowser Monsters]] in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge mode]], and all three of them are unlocked by clearing the mode as Bowser. While Hammer Bro and Boomerang Bro use a hammer and boomerang respectively for batting, Fire Bro uses a standard bat with painted flames on it.

===''Mario Super Sluggers''===
[[File:Hammer Bro. Baseball Castle.png|thumb|left|A Hammer Bro prepares to bat in ''Mario Super Sluggers'']]
In ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'', Hammer Bro is a playable character, along with Boomerang Bro and Fire Bro. Unlike the previous game, Hammer Bro and his variations are available from the start. All three have better fielding because their special move [[Hammer Throw (Mario Super Sluggers)|Hammer Throw]] involves them throwing their weapons out a distance to catch the ball. All three of them also use the same type of baseball bat as before, and each of them exhibit more varied stats than before. Hammer Bro and Boomerang Bro have slightly weaker batting skills, but Fire Bro's remains unchanged.

Only Hammer Bro and Boomerang Bro use their bats for fielding, while Fire Bro relies solely on flames. Notably, all three characters swing their bats with a single hand. Additionally, the trio retains their standard voice introduced in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''.

In Challenge mode, Hammer Bros. are on [[Bowser]] and [[Bowser Jr.]]'s team alongside the [[Magikoopa]]s, [[Dry Bones]], and their various variants.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
[[File:Hammer Bro SPP sprite.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Hammer Bro. Beach.png|thumb|[[Peach]] facing a Hammer Bro.]]
Hammer Bros have a large role in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. They storm Peach's Castle and kidnap Mario, Luigi, and the [[Toad (species)|Toad]]s, under the leadership of the [[Army Hammer Bro]].

Hammer Bros. also appear as regular enemies around midway through. They act the same as before, which is by throwing hammers in an arc while hopping. Hammer Bros can be defeated by whacking them with [[Perry]] or with the use of the [[Rage]] Vibe. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can also [[stomp]] on a Hammer Bro. and from there pick it up to toss it. Hammer Bros. are first seen in [[Wavy Beach 5-2]], and they commonly appear in later [[world]]s. Hammer Bros. also act as Bowser's soldiers in [[Bowser's Villa]].

There is only one returning Hammer Bro variety, [[Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brother|A. F. H. Bro]]s. As a result of the [[Vibe Scepter]], Hammer Bros. have a [[Calm]] variant named [[Calm Hammer Bro]] and likewise A. F. H. Bros, which have the [[Calm Amazing Flyin' Hammer Brother|C. A. F. H. Bro]] variant.

==In {{series|Mario Strikers}}==
===''Super Mario Strikers''===
[[File:Hammer Bro Strikers.png|thumb|left|200px|Hammer Bro in ''Super Mario Strikers''.]]
[[File:Hammer Bro Entrance.png|thumb|Some Hammer Bros. enter with [[Mario]] as their team captain in ''Super Mario Strikers''.]]
In ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'', Hammer Bros. appear as teammates and sidekicks to the captains. Although the Hammer Bros. are classified as offensive teammates, they still play no differently than the other teammates. This is the first game that Hammer Bro is given a standard voice as opposed to his computer voice in ''Mario Party'' and in ''Mario Superstar Baseball''. They typically exhibit a very cheery and positive attitude.
{{br|left}}
===''Mario Strikers Charged''===
[[File:Hammer Bro Orange Yoshi MSC.jpg|thumb|x250px|A Hammer Bro in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.]]
[[File:Hammer Bro. Strikers.png|thumb|left|Three Hammer Bros. waiting for their captain [[Petey Piranha]] in ''Mario Strikers Charged''.]]
Hammer Bros. also appear as offensive teammates in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. Here, they are very good at shooting and passing the ball, but are unable to tackle very well. Hammer Bro is also very slow in this game, possibly because he carries several hammers strapped to his back. Every time he shoots or tackles, he uses the hammers from the sling on his back.

Hammer Bro also pulls out these hammers when hitting, faking, and using his skillshot. His [[Deke]] allows him to pull a giant hammer out of nowhere, and smash it on the ground to blast anyone he hits away. Despite not having a large range, it is nonetheless a decent defense as it temporarily paralyzes players that come in contact with it.

His [[Skillshot]] is the [[Hammer Throw (Mario Strikers Charged)|Hammer Throw]], which, once fully charged, causes Hammer Bro to leap into the air and toss hammers at the [[Kritter|Goalie]], nailing the Goalie to the ground and immobilizing him for a few seconds as well as leaving the goal open for a few seconds.

==In ''Mario Golf'' series==
===''Mario Golf: World Tour''===
Hammer Bros appear in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]'' as NPC characters in the [[Castle Club]] mode.

===''Mario Golf: Super Rush''===
Hammer Bros appear as NPCs scattered around [[Bonny Greens]]'s practice area in ''[[Mario Golf: Super Rush]]''.

==In ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''==
Hammer Bros return in ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' and its Nintendo Switch and Nintendo 3DS [[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker (Nintendo Switch / Nintendo 3DS)|ports]], appearing in [[Drift-Along Canyon]] and [[Battle Tower Blitz]] (one of them standing in this level on two [[Goomba Tower]]s, jumping from one to the other). In the [[Nintendo Switch]] and [[Nintendo 3DS]] versions of the game, Hammer Bros are also found in the bonus level, [[Uptown, Downtown]].

If the Hammer Bros are interacted with using a pointer/touch screen, they jump to a new position immediately once they are no longer stunned. They cannot be stunned while jumping.

==In ''Mario Sports Superstars''==
Hammer Bro appears as a selectable sidekick for the soccer and baseball sports of ''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]''. Similar to the ''Mario Baseball'' series, Hammer Bro is classified as a Power type. [[Boomerang Bro]]s. appear as alternate colors in soccer and baseball while [[Fire Bro]]s. are exclusive to baseball.

==In ''Mario Tennis Aces''==
Hammer Bros appear in ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]'' in the final world of the game, [[Inferno Island]]. They appear in two missions: [[Sure Shot Challenge (Advanced)]] where two Fire Bros and one Hammer Bro pitch the ball at the player, and the mission at Castle Gates, [[Defeat Bowser's Minions!]], where the player must defeat ten Hammer Bros by reflecting their hammers that they throw at the player and, once the ten are defeated ten Fire Bros appear and the player must do the same, except with the Bros.' fire. Hammer Bros. are also found as audience members in various stages.

==In ''Dr. Mario World''==
Hammer Bros. appear in ''[[Dr. Mario World]]'' as assistants. Their stage mode skill increases the float time of floating objects such as [[capsule]]s, [[bubble]]s and [[key]]s by a multiplier. Their versus mode skill grants a chance that a column is cleared in the player's stage when their opponent uses a skill.

==In other media==
===''Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!''===
[[File:AnimeHammerBro.png|thumb|A Hammer Brother preparing to throw a hammer in ''Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]''
Although no Hammer Brothers appear amongst Bowser's invasion force at the beginning of the ''Super Mario'' anime film ''[[Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]'', an individual one does appear later. This Hammer Brother (which, depending on the selected scene, is inconsistently depicted as both rather large and completely to-scale) is assigned to guard a cave where a recurring pair of [[Goomba]]s trap Mario, Luigi, and [[Haru-ōji|Kibidango]]. However, Luigi (who was searching for [[coin]]s at the time) digs an escape tunnel, and they escape from the Hammer Bro without being caught. In a last-ditch attempt to retrieve the prisoners, the Hammer Bro throws hammers at a cliff that Mario, Luigi, and Kibidango are trekking across, and causes the top of the cliff that Kibidango and Mario are on to fall, smashing the area that the Hammer Bro is on and burying him beneath several rocks.

Robed, book-carrying Hammer Brothers are later seen acting as singers during Bowser and Peach's wedding towards the end of the film, and another Hammer Brother is seen applying makeup to Princess Peach for the said event.

===''Amada Anime Series: Super Mario Bros.''===
[[File:Mario riding.png|left|thumb|Ojīsan and Obāsan]]
In ''[[Super Mario Momotarō]]'' of the ''[[Amada Anime Series: Super Mario Bros.]]'', two elderly Hammer Brothers play the role as the adoptive parents of [[Mario]] and the parents of [[Princess Peach]]. They are called [[Ojīsan and Obāsan|''Ojīsan'' and ''Obāsan'']], the Japanese words for ''grandfather'' and ''grandmother''.

===Nintendo Comics System===
In the [[Nintendo Comics System]], several Hammer Brothers appear in the crowd of monsters chasing Mario in the story "[[The Fish That Should've Gotten Away]]". Several Hammer Brothers (although they do not wield any hammers) also appear to pester Toad, who had become a reporter, in the comic "[[The Kingdom Enquirer]]"; aside from these two appearances, Hammer Brothers also appear in several of the false advertisements that are featured in the comics.

===Nintendo Adventure Books===
In ''[[Koopa Capers]]'', two Hammer Brothers bring Luigi to Bowser after a [[magic carpet|flying carpet]] deposits him outside Bowser's Castle. If Luigi decides to look around the castle for clues about [[Wendy O. Koopa]]'s disappearance, Bowser tries to call in a Hammer Brother to act as an escort for him, but Luigi declines the offer.

While traveling through the [[Koopahari Desert]] in ''[[Pipe Down!]]'', the Mario Brothers bring up a previous close call with some Hammer Brothers during a past visit to the region. 

In ''[[Dinosaur Dilemma]]'', two Hammer Brothers can be found sleeping in Bowser's Castle by Mario and Yoshi, and are woken up by their cuckoo clock as the two try to sneak out of the room. Depending on how the reader solves the accompanying puzzle, the Hammer Brothers either knock the heroes out and make a meal out of them, or their hammer blows keep missing, and weaken the floor to the point that they go falling through it while Mario and Yoshi escape.

In ''[[Flown the Koopa]]'', if the reader chooses to have Luigi be the head of a Brachiosaurus costume the Mario Brothers don, they run into a Tyrannosaurus Rex. Depending on how the accompanying puzzle is solved, the T-Rex can turn out to be two disguised Hammer Brothers, who see through the Mario Bros.' costume, use some rubber cement to seal them in it, and capture them with the intent of making a meal out of the two.

===''The Super Mario Bros. Movie''===
Hammer Bros. appear in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' as a part of [[Bowser]]'s army.
