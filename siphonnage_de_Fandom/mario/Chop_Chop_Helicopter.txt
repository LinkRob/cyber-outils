The '''Chop Chop Helicopter''' is a red helicopter in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. It allows the player to play [[Minigame#8-Player minigames|8-Player minigame]]s. The Chop Chop Helicopter is on the outer part of the [[MSS Sea Star]], and it already has all the 8-Player minigames unlocked on it. The figure costs 1,000 [[Cruise Mileage Point]]s.
