'''Mario's Letter''' is an item in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
{{quote|Wh-what is this? If it isn't Mario's Letter! Give it to me! I will read it at once! Erm... Sure it's OK, I'm a licensed letter-reader! What what? Hm... Let's see... L-o-o-k--o-u-t--f-o-r--B-o-o-s,--L-u-i-g-i-! Is that all? Not a novelist, your Mario. This must have been written in quite a hurry.|Madame Clairvoya}}
Mario's Letter is one of [[Mario]]'s five missing items, along with [[Mario's Cap|Mario's Hat]], [[Mario's Glove|Mario's Glove]], [[Mario's Star (item)|Mario's Star]], and [[Mario's Shoe]], and must be taken to [[Madame Clairvoya]] to help her figure out Mario's whereabouts. Mario's Letter is located inside a little birdhouse in the [[Courtyard (Luigi's Mansion)|Courtyard]].

In the Nintendo 3DS version's [[Hidden Mansion]], Mario's Letter is instead located inside of the desk in [[The Twins' Room]].

==Flavor text==
*'''In-game description:''' (3DS version): ''It's a letter from Mario, but it's nearly impossible to read. He must have been in a hurry writing it.''
