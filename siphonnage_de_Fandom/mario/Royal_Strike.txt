'''Royal Strike''' is [[Princess Peach|Peach's]] [[Super Strike]] from ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''. After charging, the screen is surrounded by a pink and light blue halo where Peach fires out the ball toward the goal. During the strike she drill-kicks the ball which marks the ball with a heart-shaped trail.
