'''Screen Clean''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''Mario Party Advance''. It is obtained by completing the [[Bowser Toy Shop]] quest.

==Description==
The Gaddget involves a screen-cleaning gimmick for the player's [[Game Boy Advance]] screen. The screen shown in this Gaddget references the Game Boy Advance BIOS's boot sequence, but only the "[[Game Boy]]" portion of the title is shown, without "Advance".

The screen is blurry at first. When the player presses {{button|gba|A}}, water is sprayed all across the screen. Then, a window wiper comes in and wipes off the water, cleaning up the screen in a style similar to a car wash. Nothing else can be done afterward.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|A}} &ndash; Clean
