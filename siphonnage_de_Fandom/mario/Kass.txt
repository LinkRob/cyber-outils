'''Kass''' is a [[Kremling]] (possibly [[Kritter]]) child and a playable character in ''[[Donkey Kong Barrel Blast]]''. She was made to rival [[Dixie Kong]], and as such she has the same racing stats as her. Kass is also the first known Kremling to be explicitly female. She is one of the few Kremlings, a trait that she shares with [[Kalypso]] and [[Kasplat]].

Kass also makes an appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', in which she is one of the trophies. Her trophy states that she wants to grow up and has a fear of frogs, especially [[Winky the Frog|Winky]].
