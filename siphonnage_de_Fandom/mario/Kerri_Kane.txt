'''Kerri Kane''' is an American voice actress known for her voice portrayal of [[Rosalina]], the first time being in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', taking over the role of [[Mercedes Rose]]. Eventually, [[Laura Faye Smith]] took over the role of voicing Rosalina.

== Voices ==

* [[Rosalina]] (2011-2018)

== Roles ==

* [[Mario Kart 7]] – [[Rosalina]]
* [[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]] – Rosalina
* [[Mario Golf: World Tour]] – Rosalina
* [[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]] – Rosalina
* [[Mario Sports Superstars]] – Rosalina (with Laura Faye Smith)
* [[Mario Tennis Aces]] – Rosalina (with Laura Faye Smith)
* [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]] – Rosalina
