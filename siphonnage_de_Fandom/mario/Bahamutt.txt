'''Bahamutt''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are very large, red dragon enemies that are only encountered in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]]. They are only summoned by [[Magikoopa]] or a [[Chester]] and can be dangerous in battle. Defeating Bahamutt results in it dropping 200 [[coin]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, Bahamutts can attack physically by biting but they can also use special moves such as [[Drain]] to inflict damage. Their signature move, [[Iron Maiden]], may cause the [[Fear]] ailment.

===Strategy===
Bahamutts are weak against [[Ice (element)|Ice]], so it is heavily advised that [[Mallow]] use his [[Snowy]] technique against it.
