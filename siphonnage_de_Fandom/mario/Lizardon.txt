'''Lizardon''' is the twelfth boss in VS COM mode of the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version of ''[[Wario's Woods (Super Nintendo Entertainment System)|Wario's Woods]]''. He is found in the mountains and is obsessed with crushing [[Toad]]'s apparent dreams of fame. He is a chubby and purple creature resembling a [[dinosaur]], and has a horn sticking out of his nose, along with a short but long spine on his back. He looks somewhat like a combination of a {{wp|Ceratosaurus}} and an {{wp|Acrocanthosaurus}}.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
His name is a portmanteau of "lizard" and "don", a Greek suffix meaning "tooth" frequently used for naming dinosaurs.
