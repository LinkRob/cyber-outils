'''Inns''' are locations which appear in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' role-playing games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Whenever [[Mario]] rests in an Inn, all of his [[Heart Point|HP]], [[Special Point|SP]] or [[Flower Point|FP]] fully heal. In the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' games, they all fully heal his [[Star Power]].

==''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''==
Inns first appear in this game. Inns can be found in most places. Inns can be identified with a little star in the front and they are usually found right near [[Item Shop]]s. The Inn in [[Marrymore]] is notable for being the most expensive and if Mario doesn't pay the coins, then he must work it off. The Inn at [[Rose Town]] is also where [[Geno]] is brought to "life," and is also where [[Link]] is found sleeping before a certain point in the game. If Link is jumped on he makes a certain sound from the ''The Legend of Zelda'' series.

=={{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
{{Main|Toad House (Paper Mario series)}}
Here, they are named Toad Houses, and can be found in several locations. Mario can go here to fully restore his HP, FP, and Star Power. Toad Houses usually have a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] Innkeeper, except for the toad house in [[Star Haven]] having a [[Star Kid]] as an Innkeeper. Toad Houses in this game are also free of charge and in some areas, Mario is left with an item before he leaves.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
Inns were given a few differences. For once, Inns now cost a certain amount of coins in order to use it. However, there are new items called [[Inn Coupon]]s where Mario can use it to stay at an Inn free of charge. Many times, Mario is left with an item after he stays at an Inn. The Inn routine is different in [[Glitzville]] as Mario stays either in a locker room or the Champion's room and the resting is free of charge.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Inns in this game function about the same as they do in Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door. Although they're are only two inns in the game, in [[Flipside]] and [[Flopside]].
