The '''Atari 7800''' (also known as the '''Atari 7800 Pro System''') is a video game console released by Atari in North America on May 15, 1986 and in PAL regions in 1987, and was one of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]]'s greatest competitors. It had instant backwards compatibility with the [[Atari 2600]], its predecessor model. Three ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related games had been released for this system: ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'', ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|Donkey Kong Jr.]]'', and ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]''. It was discontinued on January 1, 1992 along with the [[Atari 2600]].
