|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Appeal''' is a type of move that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
Using Appeal simply adds more energy to the Star Meter allowing it to fill up faster. [[Mario]]'s partners can also use Appeal. In the [[Glitz Pit]], Mario sometimes has to Appeal in matches in order to move on to the next match.

==Trivia==
*Appeal replaces Focus from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
