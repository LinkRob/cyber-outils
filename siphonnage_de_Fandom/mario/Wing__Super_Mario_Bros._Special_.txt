The '''Wing''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Special]]''.

==Description==
When [[Mario]] collects a Wing, he can temporarily [[fly]] through the air. The Wing functions similarly to the [[P-Wing]], which would later appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. Special''==
A Wing exclusively appears in [[World 3-2 (Super Mario Bros. Special)|World 3-2]] and [[World 4-1 (Super Mario Bros. Special)|World 4-1]], hiding inside a [[Hidden Block]]. While the Wing's effects are active, the player can have Mario move through the air by using the game's [[swim]]ming controls. When the Wing's effects run out, there is the risk of Mario falling into a pit or an obstacle.
