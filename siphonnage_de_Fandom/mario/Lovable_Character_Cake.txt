{{Quote|Lovable Character Cakes provides a full color photograph of each finished cake, step-by-step color photographs and clear instructions to guarantee that each cake is a complete success. Some designs are aimed at the beginner and use the minimum of equipment, while other projects are more challenging for the accomplished cake decorator, so there is a fabulous cake for everyone to make, and receive!|Synopsis}}

Lovable Character Cakes is a book by Debbie Brown. It shows the reader/baker photographs of different franchise-themed cakes and step-by-step photos with instructions so that they can make the same cake as shown. ''Super Mario'' is among one of the themes.[[File:Lovable Character Cake Cover.png|thumb]]
