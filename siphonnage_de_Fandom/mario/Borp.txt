'''Borps''' are an enemy which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Physical description==
Borps are small purple bush-like creatures with a mouth sporting two upper teeth and three light yellow flower-like patches on its body, two of which give somewhat the impression of a pair of eyes.

==In the games==
Borps are only encountered in [[Cavi Cape]]. They can be fought by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] but not [[Bowser]], who automatically knocks a Borp aside when walking into it on the field, earning him a [[coin]].
