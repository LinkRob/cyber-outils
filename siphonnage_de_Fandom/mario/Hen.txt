The '''hen''' is [[Wario]]'s pet [[chicken]] in ''[[Wario Land II]]''. She is notable for being one of the few things that Wario expresses love for other than money.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The hen does not bear much physical difference from real-world hens, although she has long eyelashes to indicate that she is female.

===Behavior===
The hen is described in the the ''Wario Land II'' manual as being non-hostile when Wario states that she "wouldn't hurt a flea," and urges the reader to be nice to her. The hen is easily scared, as she flies off when the [[Pirate Goom]]s approached her, and during one part of ''Wario Land II'', the hen tries to fly away as Wario tries to return her to her nest as they pass by several aggressive [[Rooster]]s.

==In ''Wario Land II''==
[[File:Hen WLII.png|thumb|left|Hen in ''Wario Land II'']]
The hen appears in the first story of Chapter 2 (the [[SS Tea Cup]]): [[Return the hen to her nest]]. The cutscene before this stage depicts the three [[Pirate Goom]] soldiers of the [[Black Sugar Gang]] dashing away with Wario's [[treasure]]s and startling the hen. Wario follows closely behind in pursuit of his loot. However, when Wario sees the hen fly away, he temporarily stops his quest to go searching for her.

During the story, the player must guide Wario to the hen in the outskirts of his lawn, pick her up, and return the hen to her nest. Wario encounters some enemies along the way, notably [[Rooster]]s, who are very protective of the hen and charge at Wario whenever he holds her. Back at her nest, the hen lays an [[egg]], and Wario completes the story.
{{br|left}}

==In ''Game & Watch Gallery 4''==
[[File:Hen G&WG4.png|thumb|The hen in ''Game & Watch Gallery 4'']]
The hen reappears in the Modern version of the [[Game & Watch]] game ''[[Fire Attack]]'' in ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 4]]''. The hen climbs up the stairs into Wario's fort. If Wario stands behind the hen, she lays an egg worth five [[point]]s. If Wario attacks her, he does not get any points, being an action that he must do to the [[Bob-omb]]s instead.

The hen is also featured in a sequence on the Game Select screen that appears if the player leaves the cursor inactive over ''Fire Attack''. It shows the hen strutting around a tomato field as Wario is tilling the land. The hen flies away after seeing a Bob-omb roll towards Wario from behind, but Wario fails to notice and is sent flying by the explosion.
