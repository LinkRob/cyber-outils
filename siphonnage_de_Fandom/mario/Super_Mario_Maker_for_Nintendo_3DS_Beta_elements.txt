|Mystery Mushroom
|Although the [[Mystery Mushroom]] was officially removed from this port, the item is still obtainable in versions prior to 1.03 using a couple of overlooked methods:

* A big Magikoopa can still randomly turn a Brick Block into one using it's magic attacks.
* One will appear in the Make mode if the user places a [[Weird Mushroom (item)|Weird Mushroom]] in a[[Question Block]] and then switches the style to any other style, runs several screens away, restores the style back to ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and returns.

Both of these methods of obtaining the Mystery Mushroom were patched in version 1.03, but the power-up still remains (mostly) functional if hacked into a level.


When the Mystery Mushroom is collected, Mario turns small but retains all of the abilities of Super Mario (where he can break brick blocks, can crouch, Fire Flower combined with Super Mushroom will appear as a Fire Flower, etc.). When Mario gets hit in this form, a Super Mushroom flies off of him, which is likely a remnant of the animation for the currently-worn costume flying off that appeared when getting hit in the Costume Mario form in the Wii U version.

The interesting part about this specific graphic is that this file also appeared in the Wii U version (albeit at that game's higher resolution), which means that this may have just been carried over as a result of the conversion process. The image also has a resolution of '''32x32''' with in the final game having '''16x16'''. The same goes for the Wii U version: the original image is '''64x64''', while the unused variant is '''128x128'''.

== Unused Costumes ==
The Nintendo 3DS version of ''Super Mario Maker'' has an unused Amiibo table (/Mush/BossCharaMarioTable.byaml). As the Mystery Mushroom was scrapped from the game, this table of Costume Mario forms consecutively goes unused as well. However, it not only lists the "Golden Retriever" costume from the original game, but it also lists three additional costumes (Rayman, Roy and Ryu).

* GoldenRetri
* RayMan
* Roy
* Ryu
