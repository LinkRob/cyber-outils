'''Ironsider''' is a character in ''[[Wario World]]''. It is the main level boss of [[Pecan Sands]], is the second/penultimate of the three bosses of [[Sparkle Land]] and is the overall eleventh/third-to-last boss of the game, as well as the last level boss to be fought prior to [[Black Jewel]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Ironsider is an iron statue of a head of some beast-like creature. Disconnected from it are two giant hands made of stones.

===Powers and abilities===
Ironsider's main attack is breathing out green [[fire]] at [[Wario]], although Ironsider can also generate shockwaves at him when jumping on one of the nine pades. Despite their massive size, Ironsider's hands do not damage Wario but rather attempt to hold him in place while Ironsider attacks.

== In Wario World ==
The battle agents Ironsider takes place on a square-shape arena held on by chains on all four coners of the sides, hanging above what appears to be sand inside a castle. When Wario enters the arena, Ironsider will crash down in one of the nine square-shape patters on the arena as two large hands also crash down on the sides, which will start the boss. 

In order to deal damage to Ironsider, Wario must damage one of Ironsider's two hands until they stun, after which Wario can pick up one of the stun hands and throw it at Ironsider to deal damage, as much like Dual Dragon and Red-Beef J, Ironsider is immune to Wario's attacks, after which Ironsider will send in two more stone hands to deal with Wario.

== Trivia ==

* Ironsider is the only level boss in Wario World to be immune to Wario's attacks.
* The design of Ironsider, being an entity that is a levitaing head(and body) while having flying dis-embodied hands that it controls, is a homage to the trope of a floating head(with a body) while having flying dis-embodied hands, also known as the classic severed head(sometimes with a body) telepathically controlling two(or more) hands, most of which have been used many times for bosses by Nintendo(the same company that made ''Wario World'').
