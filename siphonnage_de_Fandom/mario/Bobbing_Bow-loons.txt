'''Bobbing Bow-loons''' is an Item mini-game which appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this game, a player is attached to a balloon in the air while several balloons in front of them bob up and down with pictures of items on them. The items range from average items (on the far right), with the better items being on the far left. The player is given ten seconds to shoot down one of the balloons successfully. The player must also time their shot, because if the player shoots an arrow that misses every balloon, it will ricochet off the tower behind the last balloon and bounce back to pop the balloon attached to the player.
