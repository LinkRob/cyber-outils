The '''''Princess Peach''''' series is spin-off, platformer, video game series that star [[Princess Peach]] instead of [[Mario]]. This series starts off with ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' and has recently been revealed at the Nintendo Direct on June 21, 2023 that a  sequel is being made  called ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]'' It will be released March 22, 2024. Thanks to this game's reveal, ''Princess Peach'' has been officially confirmed as a series.
