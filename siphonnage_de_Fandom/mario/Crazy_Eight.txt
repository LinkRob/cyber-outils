The '''Crazy Eight''' is an item in [[Mario Kart 8]]. The item is a blue '8'. As it's name implies, it gives the player eight items, such as a [[Mushroom]], [[Banana]], [[Green Shell]], [[Red Shell]], [[Blooper]], [[Bob-omb]], [[Star]] and a [[Coin]]. The items circle around the player.

== Trivia ==
*CPU players cannot get this item.
*The Crazy Eight is similar to the [[Lucky Seven]] in [[Mario Kart 7]], except the [[Coin]] is added.
