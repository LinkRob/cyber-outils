'''Lava Cheep Cheeps''' are a variant of [[Cheep Cheep|Cheep-Cheep]] featured in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Lava Cheep Cheep's scales are dark mahogany, and they have asterisk-shaped pupils. Lava Cheep Cheeps are undead Cheep-Cheeps. And they are always on fire due to them always appearing from [[lava]].

===Behavior===
A Lava Cheep Cheep swims a short distance in a single direction before leaping in an arc back to their initial position.

===Habitats===
Lava Cheep Cheeps inhabit the lava in the lower area of [[Corona Mountain]].

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
While exploring Corona Mountain, [[Mario]] can spray water at a Lava Cheep Cheep to stun it temporarily.
