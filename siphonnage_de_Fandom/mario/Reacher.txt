A '''Reacher''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are large, skeletal creatures encountered in [[Sunken Ship]], but only during battles and not on the field.

==Battle==
Reachers are capable of a couple of tricks in battle. The Reacher's main attack involves reaching out toward a party member, hence their name. Reachers can also toss bones at a party member, dealing more damage.

===Strategy===
Reachers are weak to [[Thunder (element)|thunder]]-elemental attacks, so [[Mallow]]'s thunder techniques are effective against them. A Reacher can also be instantly defeated using [[Pure Water]], as it is an undead enemy.
