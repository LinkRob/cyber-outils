'''Robo-Mouse''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Robo-Mouse is a giant mechanical mole with a pair of wheels and claws. Its in-game sprite depicts it as red.

===Behavior===
Upon noticing [[Wario]], Robo-Mouse chases him. It also attacks using its claws to throw Wario to another area.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Robo-Mouse is an invincible enemy encountered in two [[level]]s: [[The Stagnant Swamp]] and [[The Peaceful Village]].
