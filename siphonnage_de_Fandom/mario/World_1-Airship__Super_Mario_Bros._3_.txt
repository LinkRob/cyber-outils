'''World 1-Airship''' is the eighth and final level of [[Grass Land]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It is the first [[airship]], and features a boss fight against [[Larry Koopa]]. Completing the level allows [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to continue on to the next world, [[Desert Land]].

== Layout ==
The level starts with [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] jumping on the airship. There are two cannons and a [[Bill Blaster]]. Mario must dodge the cannonballs and the [[Bullet Bill|Bullet Bills]]. Then, there are more cannons and Bill Blasters that try to harm Mario/Luigi. The player must avoid the hazards and get to the [[Question Box]]. Where the player can decide whether to hit it or not. Going forward, there is a four-way cannon that shoots cannonballs in all sides. Mario/Luigi must avoid them and get past the next two cannons and Bullet Blaster. After that, there is a gray [[Warp Pipe]]. Mario/Luigi must enter the Warp Pipe to face Larry Koopa.

== Boss Battle ==
Larry Koopa will move towards Mario/Luigi and will try to harm them by shooting magic beams from the wand, Mario/Luigi needs to jump on Larry Koopa three times to defeat him. Shooting fireballs at Larry Koopa (if the player is [[Fire Mario]]/Luigi) can also harm him. After Larry Koopa is defeated, Mario/Luigi must get the wand and give it back to the king. Who then gives Mario/Luigi a letter from [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] and Mario/Luigi moves onto [[World 2 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 2]].
