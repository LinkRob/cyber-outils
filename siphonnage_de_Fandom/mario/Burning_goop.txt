'''Burning goop''', also known as '''Fire goop''', '''Fire''', '''Red gunk''',<ref>{{cite book|last=|first=|title=''Super Mario Sunshine'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide|publisher=[[Prima Games]]|page=12|isbn=}}</ref> '''hot gunk''',<ref>{{cite book|last=|first=|title=''Super Mario Sunshine'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide|publisher=Prima Games|page=152|isbn=}}</ref> '''Lava''',<ref>{{cite book|last=|first=|title=''Super Mario Sunshine'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide|publisher=Prima Games|page=153|isbn=}}</ref> or '''Molten goop''',<ref>{{cite book|last=|first=|title=''Super Mario Sunshine'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide|publisher=Prima Games|page=159|isbn=}}</ref> is a [[lava]]-like [[goop]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
Burning goop is red-orange in color, and it releases a small burst of fire periodically. Burning goop burns [[Mario]] and sends him flying upward, just like lava or [[fire]] would. If Mario gets caught in the middle of a large quantity of burning goop, he likely loses an [[extra life]] or sustains heavy damage, as he flies upward before falling back down, only moving slightly in the process.

Unlike Mario, Piantas do not get burnt from burning goop, although it does intoxicate them like regular goop.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Burning goop is only featured in [[Pianta Village]]. In the first episode, [[Chain Chomplets Unchained]], burning goop is produced by feverish [[Chain Chomplet]]s. In the third episode, [[The Goopy Inferno]], burning goop covers nearly the entire course, and Mario has to save the [[Pianta mayor]] from it. [[Coo Coo]]s fly around and drop burning goop during this episode. In the sixth episode, [[Piantas in Need]], Mario has to rescue ten [[Pianta]]s who are stuck in small puddles of burning goop. In the seventh episode, [[Shadow Mario Runs Wild]], [[Shadow Mario]] leaves a trail of burning goop behind him while running.

== Trivia ==
* If Mario touches burning goop, the "Bogen Multicom 2000 bell tone #2" sound effect is played.
