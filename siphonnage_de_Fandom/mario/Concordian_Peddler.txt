The '''Concordian Peddler''', also simply called the "'''Peddler'''" in the game, is a character from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. He is a Concordian man who can be found in various locations, usually close to where the Bros. start.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Concordian Peddler is a rotund Concordian man who carries a backpack. His skin is pink and his clothing red.

== Role in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'' ==
The Concordian Peddler appears in various places, typically at the start of a level when there is no shop nearby. His shops have items of both consumable and gear varieties. Mario and Luigi can encounter the Peddler in the following locations:

** [[Florall Island]]
** Lushgreen Sea's [[Great Lighthouse Islands|Great Lighthouse Island]]
