'''''SSX on Tour''''' is a High Definition snowboarding game created by [[Electronic Arts]] in 2005. The [[Nintendo GameCube]] version of the game features both [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Princess Peach]] as unlockable playable characters, like in ''[[NBA Street V3]]''.
