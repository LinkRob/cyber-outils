'''Bumper Ball Maze''' is a single-player mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party]]''. It can be purchased from the Mini-Game House.

==Gameplay==

The player must navigate through a maze on a bumper ball to reach the end of the goal. If the player either falls off or fails to make it to the goal in time, they will lose.
