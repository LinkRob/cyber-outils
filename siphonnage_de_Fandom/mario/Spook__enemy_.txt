{{nihongo|'''Spook'''|マイコニン|''Maikonin''}} is an enemy featured in ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for both the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. They are mushrooms of varying colors, the standard being dark blue as suggested by their artwork. Several more color varieties of Spook appear in the latter version. [[Toad (character)|Toad]] can defeat a Spook by throwing two [[bomb]]s at it, the first of which leaves it temporarily vulnerable.
