'''Hammer''' is one of the [[ghost]] enemies featured in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''. It appears in [[The Last Resort]] and [[ScareScraper]]. Hammer is the second kind of ghost fought in the game. They look, behave, and have a similar voice to [[Slammer]]s in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. Hammers have 200 HP.

==Physical description==
Hammers are red ghosts with square bodies, they have white eyes and mouth. Unlike most other ghosts, Hammers have two tails and eyebrows.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion 3''==
Like [[Goob]]s, Hammers work for [[Hellen Gravely]] in the game, and both kind of ghosts were brainwashed by [[King Boo]] They attack by slamming their face on the ground and using shoulder bash. Hammers will charge before using shoulder bash, while charging they can't be flashed. The way to defeat Hammers is different to the way to defeat Goobs, [[Luigi]] can't suck Hammers from the from the front, he can only flash them and suck them from behind.
