'''Orbit Ball''' is a [[souvenir]] in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The gameplay is identical to the [[microgame]] [[Galaxy Bounce]], as the player has to draw trampoline-like lines so that [[Orbit Ball (character)|Orbit Ball]], except that the gameplay is indefinite until the player loses. Like in the microgame Galaxy Bounce, there are clouds along the way that slow Orbit Ball's ascent. A random souvenir can be unlocked by bouncing Orbit over 1,000 miles upward.

==Trivia==
*Coincidentally, the minigame features very similar gameplay to the [[iOS]] and [[Android]] game ''{{w|Doodle Jump}}'', and is like an indirect predecessor of it.
