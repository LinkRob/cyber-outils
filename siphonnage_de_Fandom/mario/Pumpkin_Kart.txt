The '''Pumpkin Kart''' is a kart appearing in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Pumpkin Kart combines similarities to both the [[Trickster]] and [[Apple Kart]]. It is a [[Wikipedia:Jack-o'-lantern|jack-o'-lantern]]-themed kart like the Trickster, whilst the kart is stylized and modeled after the Apple Kart. The kart is equipped with the [[Wood]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Pumpkin Kart is a High-End kart introduced as a spotlight in the 2020 [[Halloween Tour (2020)|Halloween Tour]], but is also available as a spotlight in the 2021 [[Halloween Tour (2021)|Halloween Tour]]. The kart increases the duration of [[Mini-Turbo]] speed boosts and the points earned for performing them. This kart is the signature kart for [[Mario|Mario (Halloween)]].
