'''[[File:Bros._Points_M&L_Supestar_Saga_GBA.png|thumb|215px|Bros. Point system in ''Superstar Saga'']]Bros. Points''', also known as Special Points in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]],'' are a special statistic featured in that game, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its 3DS remake, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', as well as ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. Their abbreviation is often BP.

==Overview==
In ''Superstar Saga'' and its remake, Bros. Points appear on a meter within an image featuring red and green balls. To regain lost Bros. Points, Mario or Luigi must drink from a [[Syrup Jar]] or use a similar item.

Bros. Points do not appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', as they were replaced with [[Bros. Item]]s. They are present in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', albeit grouped into Special Points (see there for details). [[Bowser]] utilizes a [[Special Attack]] that involves his minions with another variation of Special Points called [[Brawl Point]]s.

In ''Dream Team'', Bros. Points serve the exact same purpose as before, only this time, they are also used for [[Luiginary Attack]]s when in the [[Dream World]]. In this game, Bros. Points are represented by the [[Green Shell]] icon, which is also used to represent Special Attacks (SP) in ''Bowser's Inside Story''.

Finally, Bros. Points are used in ''Paper Jam'', serving the same function as before in the previous two installments. Additionally, they can be used for [[Trio Attack]]s controlled by [[Paper Mario]].
