The '''Whomp Glove''' is a weapon only usable by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought for 72 [[coin]]s either at the shop in [[Marrymore]]'s hotel or from the [[Shaman]] merchant at the [[sea]] or [[Sunken Ship]]. The Whomp Glove has an [[Attack]] power of 40.
