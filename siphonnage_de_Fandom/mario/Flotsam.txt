'''Flotsam''' is a species of manta ray appearing in the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]]'' series.

==Games==
===''Donkey Kong Land''===
While not appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]'', Flotsam's notable member [[Wild Sting]] made an appearance at the end of the first world being the boss in that area and the first in the game. It was the inspiration of the Flotsam species.

===''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest''===
Flotsam makes its first official appearance in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''. It first appears in [[Lockjaw's Locker (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Lockjaw's Locker]] and its final is in [[Animal Antics (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Animal Antics]].

In the game, Flotsam is simply not a threat to [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] nor [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] at all as it does is simply swim back and forth or diagonally without turning. When the two monkeys come in contact with this foe, they are injured. With [[Enguarde the Swordfish|Enguarde]]'s sharp bill, he can defeat the foe by stabbing it. In this game, its colors are blue and green. In the remake, it is yellow and purple.

===''Donkey Kong Land 2''===
Flotsam reappears in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''. In the game, there is no significant changes from the semi-sequel and has exactly the same behavioral traits.
