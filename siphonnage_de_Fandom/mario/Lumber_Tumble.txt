'''Lumber Tumble''' is one of the 4-Player [[minigame]]s in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

== Opening ==
The intro starts when 4 characters walks in the waterfall. But suddenly, a Yellow Shy Guy pulls the lever that made the log appear which made the 4 characters move up quickly.
==Ending==
Winners do their victory pose while losers sulk in defeat.
