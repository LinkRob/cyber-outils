The '''Anuboo Jeans''' is a [[clothing]] item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is named for the fact that [[Anuboo]]s sometimes drop it.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', only [[Luigi]] can wear the Anuboo Jeans. Wearing it increases his [[defense]] by 40, or by 10 in the Japanese versions.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Anuboo Jeans increase Luigi's defense by 42 and his HP by 10. The Anuboo Jeans merit little value, as they can be sold for only 5 [[coin]]s.
