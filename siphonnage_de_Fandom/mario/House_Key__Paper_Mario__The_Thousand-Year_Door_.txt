The '''House Key''' is an orange [[key]] and a key item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The House Key only appears if [[Mario]] takes on [[Garf]]'s "Need a Key!" trouble at the [[Trouble Center]]. The House Key can be found just outside [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] in the main area of [[Rogueport]]. The House Key will then permanently unlock Garf's house which is the easternmost house of [[Rogueport]].
