'''Mach 8''' is a [[kart]] body part in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== Appearance ==
The Mach 8 resembles a jet airplane mixed with a 1950s formula one race car. It has a turbine engine in the front of its body, as well as having a yellow racing stripe and tail fin.
