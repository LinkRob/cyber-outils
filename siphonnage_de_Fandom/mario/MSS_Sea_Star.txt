The '''MSS Sea Star''' is a location that appears in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Super Mario]]'' series. Its a cruise ship featured in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
[[Toadsworth]] invites [[Mario]] and the other playable characters use the MSS Sea Star to travel [[Mushroom Earth|around the world]] and access the different [[board]]s. The playable characters agree, but [[Bowser]] is jealous that he is not invited. The MSS Sea Star serves as the hub. The [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Ukiki]]s are shown to work on the ship.

==Cruises==
[[File:Duty-Free Shop.png|thumb|The Duty-Free Shop, one of the areas of the MSS Sea Star.]]
===Party Cruise===
The player can freely play a party board with four players, either CPU players or other players using a [[Nintendo GameCube]] controller or even play with two or four teams (note that the controls are different with eight players).

===Solo Cruise===
The player can play against a single player using a GameCube controller or a CPU player. Playing against a computer player acts as the story mode of the game, where the player can unlock the [[Bowser's Enchanted Inferno!]] board.

===Deluxe Cruise===
The player can use the [[Chop Chop Helicopter]] to play 8-Player Minigames with other players or CPUs. When playing with eight players, the controls are different.

===Minigame Cruise===
The player can play [[minigame]]s via the [[Free Play Sub]] with CPUs or other players, or play special games such as Waterfall Duel. A single player can also play [[King of the River]] if it is purchased and the player has the required Minigames.

===Duty-Free Shop===
{{main|Duty-Free Shop}}
The player can use [[Cruise Mileage Point]]s to buy certain items or listen to the game's soundtrack.

===Control Room===
The player can change or adjust their settings anytime in here, like enabling the Microphone or setting the controller to vibrate.

==Trivia==
*The top of the ship bears a resemblance to [[Peach's Castle]].
