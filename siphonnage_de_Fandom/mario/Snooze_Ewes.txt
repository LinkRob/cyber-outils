The '''Snooze Ewes''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Snooze Ewes is stated in the description to be for helping the player fall asleep. The player can turn on the Gaddget to have it play lullaby-esque music while [[sheep]] puppets move across the screen and jump over a fence, i.e. [[wikipedia:Counting sheep|counting sheep]]. The background changes between a nighttime setting and a daytime setting. The sheep and music can be paused.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
It is obtained by completing the [[Goal Tenderizer]] quest.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|a}} &ndash; ON/OFF
