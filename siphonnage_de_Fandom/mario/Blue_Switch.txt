'''Blue Switches''', or '''! Switches''', are items in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. They are used to open up hidden parts of the current location [[Mario]] is in.

Mario first encounters one of these switches after he defeats the [[Goomba King]] and the [[Red and Blue Goombas|Red Goomba and Blue Goomba]]. The switch is located to the side of the [[Goomba King's Fortress]] and is hidden in the bush to the side. This switch is used to open the bridge that was previously in place between [[Goomba Road]] and [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]].

In addition to Blue Switches, [[Red Switch]]es appear. These switches are activated to solve more intricately designed puzzles.
