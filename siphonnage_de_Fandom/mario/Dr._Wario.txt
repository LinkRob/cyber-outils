'''Dr. Wario''' is an unlockable minigame which appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. It essentially serves as a [[reissue|remake]] of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game ''[[Dr. Mario (video game)|Dr. Mario]]'', sharing a similar aesthetic and gameplay, but the main difference is that [[Wario]] is the character played as instead of [[Dr. Mario (character)|Dr. Mario]].

Dr. Wario can be unlocked by completing twenty mails on [[Jimmy T.]]'s first stage.
