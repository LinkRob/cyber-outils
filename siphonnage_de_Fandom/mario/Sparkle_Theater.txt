The '''Sparkle Theater''' is the main location of ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''. It is a theater where [[Princess Peach]], along with some of her [[Toad (species)|Toads]], went to see a show over there, why one of them presented a flier advertising it. [[Theet]]s are featured as the theatre's main employees. Suddenly, the wicked sorceress [[Grape (character)|Grape]], and the Sour Bunch took over, center stage, having making their grand enterance at the theater. Princess Peach then sets out with the theater's guardian [[Stella]] in order to defeat them and save the theater.

Inside, the Sparkle Theater is spacious and elegant, and it features numerous plays. Each play can be accessed through its respective door, and is advertised through posters both inside and outside the theater. The shows being performed at the Sparkle Theater are:
* A kung fu play where Peach transforms into [[Kung Fu Peach]] in order to save a martial arts school taken over by the Sour Bunch.
* A ninja play where Peach transforms into [[Ninja Peach]].
* An underwater play where Peach transforms into [[Mermaid Peach]].
* A heist play featuring a thief using a glider, where Peach transforms into [[Dashing Thief Peach]].
* An ice skating play where Peach transforms into [[Figure Skater Peach]].
* A fantasy play set in a medieval town and castle overtaken by purple spiky plants, where Peach transforms into [[Swordfighter Peach]].
* A sweet-themed play where Peach transforms into [[Patissiere Peach]] and bakes various desserts after they were stolen from a sweets festival.
* A mystery play where Peach transforms into [[Detective Peach]] and must find out who stole valuable artifacts from a museum.
* A Wild West play where Peach transforms into [[Cowgirl Peach]].
* A superhero play set in a town invaded by aliens, where Peach transforms into [[Mighty Peach]].
