A '''Para-bomb''' is a [[Bob-omb]] variant which first appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. They slowly descend from the sky using a [[parachute]], and upon landing, a Para-bomb becomes a regular Bob-omb.

==In ''Super Mario World''==
[[File:Para-bomb SMW sprite.png|frame|left]]
Here, a Para-bomb is one of two enemy variants to use a parachute, the other one being [[Flying Goomba]]. Defeating a Para-bomb earns the player 200 [[point]]s.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''==
Para-bombs only appear in one level, [[World 8-5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-5]]. They retain their behavior from ''Super Mario World''.
