'''Ring Leader''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Gameplay==
*Jump your Dolphin through the floating rings. Red rings are worth one point, and the higher gold rings are worth three points.
*The highest score you can get is 40, getting this score you will surely get the first place against COM Master.

==Controls==
Hold the {{Button|Wii|Wiimote}} vertically. Swing down to go down, swing up to jump.{{MP9Nav}}
