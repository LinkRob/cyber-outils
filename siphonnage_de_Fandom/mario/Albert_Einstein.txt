'''Albert Einstein''' was a German scientist in real life who is famous for his discoveries of {{w|modern physics}}, especially the formula for {{w|mass-energy equivalence}}, E=mc^2. He first appeared in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[9001: A Mario Odyssey]]" and later returned in ''[[Mario's Time Machine (Nintendo Entertainment System)|Mario's Time Machine]]'' for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]].

==In ''Mario's Time Machine'' (NES)==
Einstein's [[Physics Equation]], which holds his famous ''E=mc<sup>2</sup>'', is one of the items that [[Bowser]] had stolen. After retrieving the item, [[Mario]] has to time travel back to the year 1905 and return the Physics Equation to Einstein.

There is a paradox, possibly a programming oversight, in which Einstein tells Mario that he moved to the [[United States of America]] in the 1930s even though Mario traveled back earlier than that.

==In other media==
===''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''===
In the live-action segment "9001: A Mario Odyssey," Einstein invented the [[HAL 9001]] computer and pizza machine for [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]]. After Einstein leaves, the computer starts to malfunction.
