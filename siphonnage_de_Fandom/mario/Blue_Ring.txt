'''Blue Rings'''<ref>''New Super Luigi U'' internal filename (<tt>R_blue_ring</tt>)</ref> are objects found only in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Description==
When Blue Rings are [[jump]]ed through, [[Blue Coin]]s appear for [[Luigi]], [[Yellow Toad]], [[Blue Toad]], and/or [[Nabbit]] to collect, and all [[coin]]s turn into [[Brick Block]]s (and vice versa), making them very similar to [[P Switch]]es that also appear in the ''New Super Mario Bros.'' series. If the character(s) collect(s) all the coins before they disappear, the audience will applaud. Unlike with the [[Red Ring]] and [[Green Ring]], in addition to the ticking sound effect that plays while the coins appear, the switch tune also can be heard.

==In ''New Super Luigi U''==
Blue Rings appear in the levels [[Dragoneel Depths]] ([[Sparkling Waters]]-5), [[Ice-Slide Expressway]] ([[Frosted Glacier]]-5), and [[Rainbow Skywalk]] ([[Meringue Clouds]]-3).
