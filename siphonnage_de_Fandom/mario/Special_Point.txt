'''Special Points''' (abbreviated as SP) are a statistic featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. They serve a similar function to [[Bros. Point]]s from other ''Mario & Luigi'' installments, being required (hence their name) for [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] and [[Bowser]], in order for them to execute [[Special Attack]]s of [[Bros. Attack|both]] [[Brawl Attack|types]]. Their maximum amounts of SP increases as the three gain levels and obtain better [[List of clothing in Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|clothing]]. As a benefit, wearing certain pieces of special clothing reduces the SP cost of Special Attacks. Players can refill SP by using [[Syrup Jar]]s or the effects of the [[Bro Badge]].

Unlike the Bros. Points from other ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' games, Special Attacks are not specific to a certain bro, instead every Special Attack can be used by both Bros.

==Trivia==
*[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story/Beta elements|Originally]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' was going to feature the traditional [[Bros. Point|BP]] system from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' however it was later renamed to SP due to [[Bowser]] also being playable.
**In the [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|remake]], the BP system from most other games replaces the SP system with Bowser's being renamed to [[Brawl Point]]s. Despite this however, both bros are able to use all Bros. Attacks as in the original.
