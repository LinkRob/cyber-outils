|name=Eekhammer (Battle Card)}}'''Eekhammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Eekhammer is the fast version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer Attack|Hammer attack]]. It is a red hammer with a red/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 5/20 coins

Sell Price: 3/10 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Eekhammer is first seen in [[Indigo Underground]], but can also found in [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Daffodil Peak]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Marmalade Valley]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Plum Park]], [[Sacred Forest]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Mustard Café]], [[Toad Trainworks]], and [[Tangerino Grill]].
