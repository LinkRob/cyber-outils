'''Call of Nature''' is one of [[Mike]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', in which it is part of the [[Mic Rocking]] stage.

==Layout==
The microgame utilizes both screens, with an animal appearing on the top screen, while the Touch Screen shows a few horns, each of a specific animal. Both screens have a light pink background with a white animal silhouette.

==Gameplay==
Call of Nature is a double-length microgame in which the player has to blow into the sound horn of the same animal as the one on the upper screen. If done correctly, the player wins the microgame. If the wrong horn is blown, or the player has not blown into a horn when the microgame timer expires, they lose at the microgame.

Each difficulty level adds one more horn on the Touch Screen than the level prior: Level 1 has two horns; Level 2 has three horns; and Level 3 has four horns.
