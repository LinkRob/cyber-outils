'''Shining Ghosts''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Shining Ghosts only appear in the [[Observatory]]. Here, they rain down on [[Luigi]] once he looks through the telescope and notices the [[moon]]. It is possible for a Shining Ghost to damage Luigi by falling onto him. Luigi can vacuum a Shining Ghost onto the nozzle of his [[Poltergust 3000]], and he can then shoot the Shining Ghost into the moon, which explodes as a result. However, doing so reveals a path to [[Mario's Star (item)|Mario's Star]].
