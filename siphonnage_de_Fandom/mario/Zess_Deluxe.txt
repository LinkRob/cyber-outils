A '''Zess Deluxe''' is an item and [[recipe]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It one of the dishes named after [[Zess T.]] herself, along with other examples such as [[Zess Cookie]] and [[Zess Dynamite]]. Zess Deluxe is made by Zess T., but it is a rather tricky dish to make, requiring rather specific ingredients. The easiest method to making a Zess Deluxe is to mix a [[Golden Leaf]], which can be found in the [[Creepy Steeple]], and a [[Whacka Bump]], which can be found on [[Keelhaul Key]].

==Effects==
Eating a Zess Deluxe replenishes 40 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 40 [[Flower Point|FP]] making it one of the most effective recovery items, second only to the [[Jelly Ultra]].
