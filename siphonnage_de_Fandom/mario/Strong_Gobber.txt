}}'''Strong Gobbers''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. Their first appearance is in the fourth mansion, [[Secret Mine]]. They are the stronger version of the [[Gobber]], and this is due to the power crystals found in the Secret Mine.
 
== Appearance ==
Strong Gobbers have similar appearances to the normal Gobbers, yellow and still containing their large, round shape, but they seem to have white line-like cracks running over the surface of their body. There are also several sections where the yellow on their body is tinted darker than other spots.

== Battle ==
These Gobbers are much more stronger than the normal Gobbers. They will attack by shooting out slimy gross gelatin-mucus like substance all over the floor, causing the player to slip. To defeat the Gobber, Luigi must use the [[Poltergust 5000]].
