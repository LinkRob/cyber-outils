'''Energy Ball''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] of [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Upon initiating the move, Paratroopa flies backward, then spins forward again, igniting the ball with a shiny purple energy and darkening the court. When the ball is hit, it travels high in the air in the manner of a lob shot. Whoever hits the ball briefly spins in a tornado, after which they become briefly stunned.

It is possible to counter Energy Ball using [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Rainbow Flutter]], [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]'s [[Boomerang Banana Return]] or [[Diddy Kong]]'s [[Barrel Jet]].
