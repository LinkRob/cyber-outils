'''Fury Volcano 4-2''' is the second level of [[Fury Volcano]].

== Layout ==
Princess Peach starts standing behind the tiki statue and two [[Podoboo]]s. Ahead, there are plenty of [[Goomba]]s and a [[Glad Red Koopa]]. After sliding, when climbing up the ladder, there is a [[Mad Goomba]]. A [[Fiery Tiki|flaming Tiki head]] is vulnerable to Peach's [[Gloom]] vibe, it blocks the three [[Giant Coin]]s worth 5 coins. There is another Mad Goomba, there is a [[Warp Pipe]] ahaed.

Beneath the platform is another Warp Pipe that leads to the Toad. The statue is blocking the way, the [[! Switch]] must be pressed down to break the statue. After going through the other pipe, there is a [[Heart]] and a [[Crystal (Super Princess Peach)|Crystal]]. To reach the five Giant Coins, Peach must use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] vibe to reach them.

Going back to the first area, Peach must use to Joy vibe to go through the warp pipe. Otherwise, throw the stone platform on the lava plume.
== Toads ==
* '''Toad 1:''' The [[Rage]] vibe can be used to pound the [[! Switch]] which breaks the statue. Going through the pipe, Peach must glide over the lava and rescue the Toad.

* '''Toad 2:''' Use the Rage vibe to pound the stone platform and rescue the Toad.
* '''Toad 3:''' Use the Rage vibe to pound the ! Switch and rescue the Toad.

== Puzzle Piece ==
The [[Puzzle Piece]] can be obtained by jumping on the green platforms.
