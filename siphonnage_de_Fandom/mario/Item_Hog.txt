'''Item Hog''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Description ==
[[Mario]] can wear the Item Hog by using three [[Badge Point|BP]]. While he is wearing it, Mario has a greater chance of obtaining an item after battle.

Mario can find this badge in [[Rogueport Sewers]] where [[Dazzle]] sells this badge for five [[Star Piece]]s.
