'''Shooting Star Summit''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Though, it is actually [[Star Hill]], from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', only renamed.

==History==
Shooting Star Summit is located to the east of the remains of [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. It is here that [[Mario]] will find the [[Star Spirits|Seven Star Spirits]] who give him advice. Before Mario makes the walk up the mountain, he can go around to the left side to find a [[Star Piece]].
