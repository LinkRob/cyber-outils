|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Funguspike''' is a special move used by a few enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Funguspike is used, the enemy tosses a spike at the selected character. It is inflicted in fungi which cause the [[Mushroom (ailment)]] on the victim.

==Effects==
With Funguspike, the user is able to inflict the [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]] status on the victim. Wearing a Trueform Pin will prevent this. Funguspike can still cause regular damage neither-less.
