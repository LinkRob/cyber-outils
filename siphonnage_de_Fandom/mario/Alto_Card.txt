The '''Alto Card'''<ref name="SMRPG">{{cite video game| title = Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars
| developer = [[Square Enix]] | publisher = [[Nintendo]] | date = March 9, 1996 | platform = [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] | level = [[Toadpole Pond]]}}</ref> is a [[Special Item (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Special Item]] featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.<ref name="SMRPG" />

== Description ==
The Alto Card is a white card with the pink outline including the name mentioned. The orange "A" and the yellow note is on the card. The card can be obtained by playing the minigame, [[Tadpole Pond]]. Specifically, the player may play "Frogfucius' Suite #18". After completing the minigame, the player will talk to [[Toadofsky]]; he will reward the player the card.<ref name="SMRPG" /> When the player uses the Alto Card, he/she may buy merchandise at the [[Juice Bar]].
