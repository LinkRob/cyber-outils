'''Freeze Frame''' is a 4-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

== Opening ==
[[File:MP6 FreezeFrame Daisy.jpg|left|thumb|202x202px|Daisy's artwork in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.]]
The camera shows the player viewing the area exposed by [[Goomba]]s, then they hide and the camera zooms into the player.

== Gameplay ==
The player has ten seconds to take a many photos of Goombas as possible. During day, the [[Shy Guy]] gives extra points while at night, the [[UFO]] gives extra points. The player with the most points wins.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} - Navigate the viewfinder
* {{button|gcn|A}} - Take a photo
