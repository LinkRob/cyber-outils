'''Popple's Partner''' is the tenth level of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 15.

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains 2 [[Pestnut]]s, 2 [[Fuzzbush]]s, and 4 [[Piranha Bean]]s. The enemy captain is a Pestnut.

The 2nd battle contains 2 Pestnuts, and 6 Fuzzbushes. The enemy captain is a Fuzzbush.

The final battle contains 3 Pestnuts, 2 Piranha Beans, 1 Fuzzbush, and [[Popple]]. The enemy captain is Popple, and he knows [[Rally]].
