'''Jimmy's Cell''', named '''Jimmy's cell phone''' in earlier appearances, is a recurring object in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Description==
Jimmy's Cell is a mobile phone constructed by [[Dr. Crygor]] for [[Jimmy T]], who has used it in several of his appearances. In earlier appearances, it had the appearance of a flip-phone, but since ''[[Game & Wario]]'', it is changed to a smartphone, reflecting the updates of phone technology in the real world. Since the smartphone redesign, Jimmy T has not been shown to use his phone as frequently.
