'''Zombie Guys''' are an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Zombie Guys are undead red [[Shy Guy]]s that can be distinguished by their dirt-tainted clothing.

===Behavior===
Zombie Guys usually do a staggered, sluggish walk in a single direction, turning the other way upon encountering a wall, an edge or another enemy. One Zombie Guy stays in place at the start, but proceeds to shuffle toward the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] when he comes close, damaging him on contact. While most Zombie Guys are encountered normally on Yoshi's path, others spawn from underground burrows, and if a Zombie Guy from an underground burrow is defeated, another one spawns in its place.

==In ''Yoshi's Crafted World''==
Zombie Guys are first encountered at the start of [[Haunted Maker Mansion]], when they come out of the ground during a lightning strike, and Zombie Guys appear frequently throughout outdoors portion of the level. The only other time they appear is in the level [[Be Afraid of the Dark]].

A Zombie Guy can be defeated from either being [[jump]]ed on or having an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] thrown at it. Yoshi can also eat and swallow a Zombie Guy to produce an egg.
