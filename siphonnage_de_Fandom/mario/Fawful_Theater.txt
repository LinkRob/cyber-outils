'''Fawful Theater''' is an area of [[Bowser's Castle]] during [[Fawful]]'s occupation of it in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. It is where another boss fight against [[Midbus]] occurs.
