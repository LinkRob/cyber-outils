|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Gulp''' is a default special move used by [[Yoshi (partner)|Yoshi]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
When successfully timed, Yoshi will use his tongue to grab and hold an enemy in his mouth, and then spit it out at the next available enemy. This move is extremely useful against spiked opponents as it negates any defense. This move is required to defeat the [[Armored Harriers]] at the [[Glitz Pit]].
