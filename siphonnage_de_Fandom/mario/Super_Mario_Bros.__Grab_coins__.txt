'''Super Mario Bros.''' is one of [[9-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', appearing as part of the [[Retro Action]] stage. It takes place in the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[Super Mario Bros.|same title]]. ''WarioWare: Touched!'' also has another microgame based on ''Super Mario Bros.'' and sharing the same title, [[Super Mario Bros. (Stomp!)|Super Mario Bros.]]

==Gameplay==
The player has to earn a certain number of [[Coin]]s before time runs out. They can do so by tapping the [[? Block]]s, or even by tapping a hidden [[Coin Block]] to earn Coins as well. When [[Mario]] appears, the player can tap him to earn Coins too. If the player collects the required number of Coins, they win, but if they do not fulfill this goal on time, they lose.

Levels 1, 2, and 3 involve the player having to collect two, four, and six Coins, respectively.
