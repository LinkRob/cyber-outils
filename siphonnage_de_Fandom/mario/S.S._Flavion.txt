'''S.S. Flavion''' is a vehicle in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
It is a ship owned by [[Flavio]] of [[Rogueport]], and it is his favorite ship in his fleet.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Flavio used the S.S. Flavion to traverse the ocean to [[Keelhaul Key]]. However, it was sunk during the "OO" of the star month by ferocious pirate-ghost embers. The crew ended up washed up on the island of [[Keelhaul Key]], with several of them separated. They made a small settlement on the island's shore.

After the S.S. Flavion had sunk, [[Mario]] uses [[Cortez]]'s haunted ship, the [[Black Skull]], to go to and from Keelhaul Key.

==Other appearances==
The S.S. Flavion briefly appears in the [[Paper Mario (stage)|Paper Mario]] stage of ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''.
