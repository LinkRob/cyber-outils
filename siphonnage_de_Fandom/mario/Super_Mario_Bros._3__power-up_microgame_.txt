''' Super Mario Bros. 3''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' It is part of [[9-Volt]]'s [[Spintendo Classics]] stage, and the microgame is based on the original [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] version of the [[Super Mario Bros. 3|game of the same name]].

==Gameplay==
The player has to select the correct power-up for [[Mario]]; this can be either [[Fire Mario]], [[Hammer Mario]], [[Frog Mario]], [[Tanooki Mario]], or [[Statue Mario]], randomized each time the microgame is played. The correct power-up must be selected quickly, before Mario falls between several other Marios in the same power-up. If done successfully, the player wins, indicated by all of the Marios doing the peace sign. However, if Mario lands with an incorrect power-up, the player loses, indicated by the group of Marios glaring at Mario.

Levels 1, 2, and 3 feature three, four, and five power-ups to choose from, respectively.
