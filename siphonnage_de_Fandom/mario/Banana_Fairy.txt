}}The '''Banana Fairies''' are characters that appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==History==
The Banana Fairies serve under the [[Banana Fairy Princess]] on [[Banana Fairy Island]]. However, the Banana Fairies were scared off by the appearance of [[King K. Rool]] and his [[Kremling Krew|crew]]. The Banana Fairy Princess then tasks [[Tiny Kong]] and the other Kongs to retrieve all of the Banana Fairies by using the [[Banana Fairy's Camera]] and [[Banana Camera Film|film]] to capture them in.
