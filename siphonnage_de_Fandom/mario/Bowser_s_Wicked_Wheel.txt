'''Bowser's Wicked Wheel''' is a [[Bowser]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Layout==
The team characters are inside of an {{w|hamster wheel|exercise wheel}} with a pair of [[Amp]]s at the corner. Electrical pylons can be seen in the background.

==Gameplay==
The minigame has a [[time limit]] of 20 seconds. The object for [[Bowser]] is to try and get the characters to hit the Amps, which produce electricity, by spinning the wheel, while the other characters have to avoid being harmed. Whoever touches the electricity gets shocked, costing them a heart. Bowser can stop the wheel, causing the characters go on to the right Amps, or spin it fast enough so that they finish on the left Amps. Whoever touches an Amp loses a [[heart]], and if a character loses all of their hearts, they are eliminated.

==Controls==
