'''Floating Fuzzy Time Mine''' is a level of World Flower in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The layout is similar to that of [[Fuzzy Time Mine]], except that the level has floating water (hence the name). At the end of the level, another Fuzzy Horde appears.
==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': On a wall near the start of the course. It is recommended the player be [[Cat Mario]] or [[Lucky Cat Mario]] to grab this Green Star.
*'''Green Star 2''': Inside a cloud, blocked by a giant [[Brick Block]].
*'''Green Star 3''': Obtained by collecting 8 Green Coins near the end of the level.
