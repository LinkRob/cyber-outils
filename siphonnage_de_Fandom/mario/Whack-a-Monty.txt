'''Whack-a-Monty''' is an Action minigame in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Gameplay==
The minigame features gameplay akin to {{w|Whac-A-Mole}}, but it involves the player having to tap [[Monty Mole]]s as they pop in and out of the holes on a 5×4 grid. Whenever the player taps a Monty Mole, they earn a [[point]]. Each time a Monty Mole is tapped, its body gets stacked on the top screen. While tapping Monty Moles, the player has to avoid tapping the [[Luigi]]s that also pop in and out of the holes. If the player taps a Luigi, they lose three points. Ultimately, the player has to tap as many Monty Moles within the 30-second [[time limit]] as possible while avoiding the Luigis.

The minigame has a multiplayer variation that supports between two to four players. It plays similar to its single-player counterpart, except each player competes to tap the most Monty Moles within the time limit. Multiplayer mode also has three difficulty settings: Easy has no Luigis; Normal has some Luigis; and Hard features several quickly moving Luigis. In the latter two difficulty settings, it is possible for a player's score to fall below zero.

==In-game instructions==
*'''Single player:''' ''"Whack Monty Mole whenever he pops out of a hole! WHACKITY WHACK!!! You'll lose points if you hit poor Luigi by mistake. Be careful! How many Monty Moles can you whack?"''
*'''Multiplayer:''' ''"Whack Monty Mole whenever he pops out of a hole! WHACKITY WHACK!!! You'll lose points if you hit poor Luigi by mistake, so be careful! How many Monty Moles can you whack?"''

==Trivia==
*The background music is an arrangement of the [[Underground Theme]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. The same arrangement is also used in [[Coincentration]], [[Intense Coincentration]], and [[Hide and Boo Seek]].
