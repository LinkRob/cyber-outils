'''Super Sushi''' is an item in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Mario Meets Koop-zilla]]."

==Description==
The Super Sushi is an edible power-up that grows whoever eats it to twice the height. It is created by [[Dr. T. Garden]]. Super Sushi has to be used in a microwave-like device. The effects of a Super Sushi can be undone from a [[Super Sushi Shrinker]].

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
[[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] has stolen Super Sushi so that he could eat it and become gigantic. Later on, to counter this, [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] eats Super Sushi to become gigantic and battle King Koopa.
