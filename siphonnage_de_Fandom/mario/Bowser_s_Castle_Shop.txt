|caption = [[Mario]] in Bowser's Castle Shop.}}'''Bowser's Castle Shop''' is a location that appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is an [[Item Shop]] run by a [[Spiked Goomba]] in [[Bowser's Castle]]. This is also the very last Item Shop visited in the game and it mainly sells items from the past areas but at higher prices. The Spiked Goomba only sells items to [[Mario]] because he doesn't recognize Mario (though, even though he thought it was Mario, he was going to sell items to him anyway).
