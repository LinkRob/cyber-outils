'''Hermie III''' is a boss in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
He is a giant, lisping hermit crab who has a conical shell that resembles a Christmas tree, bearing the appropriate decorations. He resembles the Yadotsurī enemy from ''Tomato Adventure'', another game created by [[AlphaDream]], except Hermie III is much larger.

===Personality===
Hermie III is short-tempered, as seen when the inhabitants of Gwarhar Lagoon are focused on [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] instead. It can also be seen in battle when Hermie III is attacked by either Mario's [[Firebrand]] or [[Fire Bros. (move)|Fire Bros.]], as Hermie III shouts that the Mario brother is ruining his decorations.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
[[File:MLSSHermieIIIOverworld.png|frame|left]]
[[File:MLSS-Hermie Beach Screenshot.png|thumb|Hermie III in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'']]
Hermie III is found at [[Gwarhar Lagoon]]. Some time after the [[Beanstar]] broke into four pieces, Hermie claimed one piece and placed it at the top of his shell. With some help from fellow residents, Hermie has his shell is decorated until it eventually looks like a Christmas tree.

When Mario and Luigi arrive, Hermie III refuses to give up his piece of the Beanstar. Hermie III's helpers started paying more attention to the well-known [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] than his prized decorations, infuriating Hermie III, who then proceeds to battle both Mario and Luigi.

After Hermie III is defeated by Mario and Luigi, the two explain the situation involving the Beanstar, then Hermie III reluctantly gives them the Beanstar piece.

The Mario Bros. can optionally make amends with Hermie III by giving him [[Spangle]] to place on his shell. After receiving it, Hermie III gratefully rewards them with the [[Soulful Bros.]] [[badge]].

In the ending sequence, Hermie III is seen waving to [[Toad Express|Peach's plane]] as Mario and Luigi proceed to return to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

==Battle==
Hermie has one main attack: pinching at the Mario Bros. with his claws, which can be dodged only by [[jump]]ing. If the claw starts to vibrate, it hits either Mario or Luigi, depending on whom it is closest to. If it does not, Hermie III attacks the other Mario Bro. Hermie III has another attack in which he spits pink bubbles capable of [[Poison (status effect)|poisoning]] Mario or Luigi unless he destroys the bubbles with his [[Hammer]].

After taking some damage, Hermie starts hiding inside of his shell, where he begins to heal himself. The player must have either Mario use [[Firebrand]] or both Mario Bros. use the [[Fire Bros. (move)|Fire Bros.]] attack on Hermie III to get him out of his shell.

[[Sidestepper]]s from ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]'' can also be seen as part of the background.

In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] remake, if Mario or Luigi avoid Hermie III's pinching attack perfectly, Hermie III increases the speed at which the attack moves, and [[stomp]]ing Hermie III's pincers stops him from using them. The Crabs in the background are also replaced by [[Huckit Crab]]s.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Hermie III's first name is derived from the real-world [[wikipedia:Hermit crab|hermit crab]]s. The "III" is the [[wikipedia:Roman numerals|roman numeral]] for "three," which sounds similar to "tree," referencing how his shell resembles that of a Christmas tree.

==Nomenclature==
In the Japanese version of the game, Hermie III is called "Yadofuru Sansei" (written やどふる さんせい in Hiragana and ヤドフル サンセイ in Katakana), which literally means "Yadolph III," with "Yadolph" being a pun on ''yadokari'' (Japanese for "hermit crab;" written やどかり (Hiragana script) or ヤドカリ (Katakana script)) and Adolph III.

In Chinese, he is merely called "Christmas Crab III" (''Shèngdàn Xiè Sānshì''; written 圣诞蟹三世).

In the French dub, he is called Crustassov III (with Crustassov being a pun on ''crustacé'' which means "crustacean," which crabs belong to) and the Russian name suffix "-ov."

His German name, "Paguri," comes from the scientific name for hermit crabs, "paguridae."

The Italian dub calls him "Grácchio III," which is a pun on ''gránchio'' (crab).

In Korean, his name is ''Soralpeu Samse'' (written 소랄프 삼서 in the Hangul script, with ''Soralpeu'' coming from ''sorage'', which means "hermit crab" and is written 소라거, and Adolph III.

In Spanish, he is called Cigatrón III, with Cigatrón coming from ''cigala'', which refers to a nephrops.

==Trivia==
*Similar to [[Bowser]] from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', [[Cortez]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', and [[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]] from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Hermie III becomes friends after his battle.
*Hermie III is the only boss in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' who can be easily defeated just with the use of Mario's [[Firebrand]] (which burns his shell) and Luigi's [[Thunderhand]] (which deals critical damage to him).
*Due to the Christmas decorations on his shell and the appearance of Mario (wearing red and being a little chubby), Hermie III thought that Mario was [[Santa Claus]]. ("C-could you be... SANTA CLAUS!?").
