The '''Wario Cup''' is a [[cup]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It appears as the third of the six cups in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and the third and final normal cup in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''. The cup contains two [[Wario]]-themed tracks, shared between the two games. The tracks themselves take place in [[Diamond City]], the main setting of the {{series|WarioWare}}.

The Wario Cup reappears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', starting with the [[Tokyo Tour]] when Wario became available in the game as a playable character. Courses featured in this cup are treated as Wario's favored or favorite courses.
