[[File:SuperMarioGalaxyStarChip.png|thumb|200px]]'''Star Chips''' are items which first made an appearance in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. Star Chips are hidden across the galaxy. After collecting all the Star Chips, a new [[Launch Star]] will be born to propel [[Mario]] to the next galaxy. These items later appeared again in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.
