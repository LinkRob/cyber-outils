'''Bumper Crop''' is a 2-vs-2 minigame in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a round garden enclosed by short logs sticking from the ground. The garden is separated into three areas: one for carrots, one for turnips, and one for potatoes, each marked by a sign that shows where each type of vegetable is grown. There is an opening on the upper-left and upper-right sections, each leading out to a dirt trail, but during the minigame, these paths are blocked off by a red truck and a blue truck, parked on the left and right paths respectively. Each truck carries a wooden crate in the back to store the vegetables collected by the respective team.

==Gameplay==
The object for both teams is to collect two of each vegetable, including carrots, turnips, and potatoes, and put them into the small trucks. Each team has their own [[Shy Guy]] holding a sign with an "O" in their left hand and a sign with an "X" in their right hand. If a team's Shy Guy holds up its "O" sign, the characters can throw vegetables into their truck, but if their Shy Guy holds up its "X" sign, the team cannot throw vegetables into their truck. The first team to put every required vegetable into their truck in the winner, but if neither team accomplishes this within the 45-second [[Time Limit]], this results in a draw.
