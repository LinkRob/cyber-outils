The '''Flipside Token''' is a token that is used to exchange prices in the [[Flipside Arcade]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It resembles an orange-colored [[Rubee]]. The Flipside Token can be used to exchange prices in the [[Flipside Arcade]] and play minigames. The Flipside Token functions like the [[Pianta Token]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
