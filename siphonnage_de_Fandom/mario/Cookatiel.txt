{{Nihongo|'''Cookatiel'''| コック鳥|Kokku-chō|Cook Bird}} is the second boss of the [[Luncheon Kingdom]] and is overall the thirteenth boss in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. She slightly resembles [[Wingo]] from [[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]. As the name suggests, the bird likes to cook.

The bird made her first appearance when [[Mario]] made his way to the top of Mount Volbono. After Mario captures a giant steak at Meat Plateau and Cookatiel carries it up to the stew pot, furious by Mario being in her stew Cookatiel will start to try to her wings to blow Mario off the volcano, just as a volcanic eruption tossed Mario off back to the [[Odyssey]].

Mario later encountered Cookatiel after capturing a Podoboo by using a cannon to go to the top of the volcano.

== Appearance ==
Cookatiel is a large bird, more specifically resembling a hen, dressed in chef's attire. Her natural body consists of hot pink skin with white, roughly square-shaped feathers that are arranged to look like a coat or shirt. Despite having the ability to fly, her wings are small compared to her body, also covered in feathers. She has a curved, mostly yellow beak with an orange tip.

The talons have two toes on the front and one on the back, following the previous color pattern along with having cyan nails. Hanging under the beak is a red wattle. Similar material surrounds the eyes, and the eyelids are a different shade of pink than the skin with eyelashes and dark purple eyeliner. The eyes themselves are crossed, with the position of each pupil changing only as she moves. Her tail has identical feathers to her torso but five select individuals are extremely elongated, simulating the sickle feathers of a rooster.

The bird also wears a white chef's hat on her head and a red neckerchief around her neck; the exact same clothing of most [[Volbonan]]s.

==Battle==
The battle begins when [[Mario]] captures a Podoboo with his hat and is sent to the top of [[Mount Volbono]] via cannon. When Mario encounters him, Cookatiel will start throwing bell pepper-like rocks, and durians into the strew.

In order to damage Cookatiel, Mario must use the [[Podoboo]]'s ability of lava swimming to swim up Cookatiel's lava barf (Cookatiel will shoot durians at Mario to stop this), and to jump on top of her head to damage her. Cookatiel will then spit out more fruits in the second phase, and gush out the pink liquid in a zigzag fashion. In the third phase, Cookatiel will spit even more fruits, and gush out the pink liquid in small bursts which Mario must leap from one by one.

Like most bosses, the Cookatiel is defeated after being hit three times. Once defeated, Cookatiel will fall into the stew and explode, revealing the [[Multi Moon]].

In the rematch in [[Peach's Castle]], the fight is mostly the same, but there are now three [[Moonsnake]]s in the stew, which Mario must avoid.

==Trivia==
*The Cookatiel's name is a portmanteau of "cook" (which is showing her cooking outfit) and "cockatiel" (species of bird).
*While not the final boss, the Cookatiel does serve as the last non-major antagonist of ''Super Mario Odyssey'' to serve as a boss, as the next six bosses (Lord of Lighting, Topper, Harriet, Mecha Broodal, Madame Broode/Chain Chompikins and Bowser) are the primary villains of the game.
