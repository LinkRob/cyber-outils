The '''Dark Stone''' is a key object that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It was once used for granting nightmarish or malefic wishes.

== History ==
The Dark Stone, like the [[Dream Stone]], was kept guarded by the [[Pi'illo]]s in [[Pi'illo Island]]. The bat king, [[Antasma]], stole the Dark Stone, which provoked the Pi'illos to fight him to get it back. They imprisoned Antasma in the [[Dream World]], but before they were finished, Antasma crushed the Dark Stone, and [[Nightmare Chunk]]s were scattered throughout [[Pi'illo Island]], turning the Pi'illos into petrified stone and trapping their spirits in the Dream World.
