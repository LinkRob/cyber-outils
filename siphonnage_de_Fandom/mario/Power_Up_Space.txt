The '''Power Up Space''' is a rare [[space]] found in [[board]]s of [[Duel Mode]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Description==
If a player lands on this space, any [[Mario Party 3#Partners|partner]]s they have will have their attack, stamina, and salaries doubled. They return to normal after the player either returns to their starting area or gets a "Power Down" event from a [[Event Space|Happening Space]].
