'''Gritzy Caves''' is a cavern location featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', and is located near [[Gritzy Desert]]. It has light blue sand, but its walls are dark green. [[Petey Piranha]] is the boss fought in Gritzy Caves.
