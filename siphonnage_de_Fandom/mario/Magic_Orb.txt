The '''Magic Orb''', titled '''Surprise Orb'''<ref>''Mario Party 7'' instruction booklet, page 21.</ref> in the instruction manual, is an [[orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
The orb is usable exclusively by [[Boo]] and [[Dry Bones]]. When used, it doubles the number rolled on the [[Dice Block]] and turns the character invisible, allowing it to bypass all roadblock traps on the board. This effect lasts for two turns.

The item bears [[Professor E. Gadd|E. Gadd]]'s emblem.
