'''Fire Breath''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] used by [[Bowser]] in both ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
Upon activating the move, Bowser uses his actual [[Fire Breath]] to put the ball on fire, then returns the ball as it dashes over to the receiver, covered in flames. In doing so, the receiver gets pushed back a distance.
