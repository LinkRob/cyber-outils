The '''Genie''' is the seventh and final boss of ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. He is named for being a genie, which is his species. The Genie is a neutral character, and only fights [[Wario]] per the wish of [[Captain Syrup]]. After defeating the Genie, Wario can trade his treasure to wish for a new building, with birdhouse being the lowest ranked reward and a planetoid being the highest ranked reward.{{WLSML3Nav}}
