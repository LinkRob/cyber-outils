'''Bowser Jr.''' is the secondary antagonist of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]], serving as the main antagonist of ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''.

He is the son of [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] and also heir of the Koopa Army who plans to exact his vengeance on the Mario Bros. for his father's defeat by seeking to restore him to his normal size and destroy the universe with Rosalina's cosmic powers.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Bowser Jr. is relatively similar in appearance to his [[Bowser Jr.|video game counterpart]], except with some differences, such as having proper eyes with red irises rather than beady eyes, and the mouth on his bandana seemingly being more neatly coloured.

When using the power of his [[Magic Brush (Illumination)|magic brush]], he turns into a form similar to that of his video game counterpart’s [[Wonder Bowser Jr.|Wonder]] and his new [[Fury Bowser|Fury transformations]], only in his wonder form his body is darker and his bandana’s mouth is yellow instead of green. Unlike in Wonder where the bandana fully opens and closes like a mouth, here it appears to be animated in 2D, though it seemingly becomes fully modelled when roaring.

===Personality===
Similar to his mainstream counterpart, Bowser Jr. is a spoiled, bratty, malicious, arrogant, spiteful and narcissistic individual with grand ambitions of conquest and destruction, like his father. He desires to create a world that he and his father can rule together and will crush anyone who stands in the way of that dream.
Bowser Jr. has a way of manipulation, as seen when he was able to make his father turn evil again simply by using the past of himself and his father, as well as his old ambitions of wanting to rule the universe, showing that Junior could not care if Bowser was friends with the Bros., as Junior merely sees this as weakness and doesn't care about change. This could have been because of how Bowser Jr. was raised by his father when he was a toddler.
Though rarely Bowser Jr. is able to show sadness, as when he thought Mario had killed Bowser, he went out of his way to mourn him by kneeling down by the edge of the broken bridge. However: this is quickly broken when Bowser comes back as Dry Bowser.

== Biography ==

=== Past ===
Before the events of ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'', a much younger Bowser Jr. was being read a bedtime story by his father, Bowser, who tells Bowser Jr. that the two were desperate for a place to call home, unsatisfied by their results, Bowser would tell Junior that they could build their own planet together under the alias of 'Planet Bowser', using a makeshift puppet show to demonstrate. Bowser Jr. became extremely inspired, determined to make the world that he and his father would rule over.

Years later, Bowser Jr. would begin construction on Planet Bowser, presumably during the events of [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]. However after hearing about his father's capture by the titular plumpers, he would set a course to the Comet Observatory, already beginning his plan of universal domination.

=== ''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie.'' ===
Rosalina raises the star-like species of Lumas as their mother on the Comet Observatory, however soon arrives Megaleg, a tripod-like mech piloted by Bowser Jr. who captures her as well as a brave Luma aboard his UFO. He then takes said UFO through the universe and right to Planet Bowser, he uses his clown car to carry the caged Rosalina and Luma to his base within Planet Bowser, gloating to his subjects how he has captured her and will use her powers to destroy the universe. Rosalina, now filled with maternal protection, uses her powers to eviscerate her restraints and Luma's before Junior makes an inky Piranha Plant to restrain her again as he plans to go to the Mushroom Kingdom to rescue his kidnapped father.

The Luma who escaped captivity crash-lands in the kingdom and asks for Peach's help; she and Toad accept. In the Gateway Galaxy, Peach and Toad fight Wart, the king of its casino. Mario, Luigi, and Yoshi protect the Mushroom Kingdom in Peach's absence, which includes rehabilitating Bowser. Peach's castle is lifted into space by Bowser Jr.'s UFO, where he descends down and demands to have his father be realized, after a quick denial from Mario, Junior fights the brothers and Yoshi. The castle is accidentally dropped into the Honeyhive Galaxy after Megaleg malfunctions, flies up, and crashes into the tractor beam within the UFO. In the Honeyhive Galaxy, Bowser sacrifices his liberty to allow Mario, Luigi and Yoshi to enter the Gateway Galaxy, where they reunite with Peach and Toad. Bowser is kidnapped by the UFO, where he reunites with his son.

Peach and Toad hire pilot Fox McCloud, hailing from another universe, to transport the group to Planet Bowser, but his spaceship crash lands into a Prehistoric Galaxy after an attack from Bowser Jr., who turns Mario and Luigi into babies with the Super Scope from the UFO. Bowser feels guilty, having bonded with the brothers, but Bowser Jr. convinces him to return to his role as King of the Koopas. Yoshi finds a Super Scope and rescues the brothers and Toad, who also got turned into a baby, from a Tyrannosaurus Rex. The group is then picked up by the Comet Observatory by the Lumas after Luigi sent a distress call over to them. Fox manually flies the Observatory straight to Planet Bowser, where he drops off Mario, Luigi, Peach and Yoshi. Mario and Peach attempt to sneak in, but are caught and sent to a booby-trapped trench, which they escape from using Peach's parasol. They confront Bowser, whom Mario drops into the lava moat below after he cuts the chains of the bridge with one of Bowser's axes. Junior is shocked, thinking that his father had died. And then, he rushes out to the edge of the broken bridge, peering down at the lava below. Suddenly, Bowser is still alive, coming out as a skeleton, now named Dry Bowser.

Luigi and Yoshi assist Mario in fighting the Bowsers. During the fight, Luigi was able to get a hand of Junior's paintbrush, now able to summon creatures to fight for them. However, due to Luigi being bad at drawing, he was only able to create Mr. Game and Watch, who proves to be of use as he was able to quickly shatter Dry Bowser into pieces with a hammer. Bowser Jr., shocked by this, screams in anger and throws Dry Bowser's separated shell at Mr. Game and Watch, who quickly gets bisected by the shell, and before Luigi could move out the way, the shell hits him too, knocking the paintbrush from his hands. Bowser Jr. reclaims the paintbrush and uses it to summons a dragon. But in a surprising turn, he gets swallowed whole, and he has to be rescued by Mario. Using their powers, Peach saves and reunites with Rosalina after freeing her. Sometime later, Peach's castle is rebuilt, and Fox sends the Bowsers to prison, where they are confronted by Lumalee, who is the warden of the prison and threatens them. So much so, that the two scream in horror at Lumalee's dark threats.
