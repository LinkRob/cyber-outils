'''Omodonmeka''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Omodonmeka are flying robots which appear in either blue, green, or red varieties.

===Behavior===
The color variants vary in behavior. The green and red Omodonmeka carry a weight known as an [[Omodon]], and attempt to attack [[Wario]] with it by dropping the Omodon onto the ground before lifting it back up, similar to a [[D-Bat]] from ''[[Wario Land II]]''. Being hit by the weight temporarily turns Wario into [[Flat Wario]]. The blue Omodonmeka do not attack Wario, but it tries to grab him, and it can revert Wario to normal if he has been flattened by it.
