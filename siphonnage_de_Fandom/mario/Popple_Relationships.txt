This article is about '''[[Popple]]'''{{'}}s relationships with other characters that resonated with his sneaky and backstabbing personality since ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Enemies==
===Mario and Luigi===
Mario and Luigi were walking inside [[Château de Chucklehuck]] to find the [[Chuckola Reserve]]. Suddenly, Mario/Luigi spooked Popple behind his back. Luigi/Mario returned to Mario/Luigi to continue searching. The brothers approached Popple, who introduced himself. Popple told Bowser to defeat Mario and Luigi. While Bowser tried to remember Mario and Luigi, Popple interrupted him and understood that Mario and Luigi also came to search for the Chuckola Reserve. After battle, Popple blamed Bowser for losing to Mario and Luigi. Popple along with Rookie decided to continue to the sea.
===Cork and Cask===
{{Q|We were stoofed into zese barrels by two tres bizarre characters. Are you zee ones 'oo saved us?|[[Cork and Cask|Cask]], [[Château de Chucklehuck]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''}}
Popple had interacted with [[Cork and Cask]] off-screen. Popple and Bowser trespassed [[Château de Chucklehuck]], attacked and trapped Cork and Cask inside the barrels before searching the [[Chuckola Reserve]]. After Mario and Luigi finished their battle against Popple and Bowser, they broke the barrel realizing they rescued the duo. Cork and Cask told Mario and Luigi that Popple and Bowser attacked and trapped them inside the barrels.

==Minions==
===Bowser===
{{Q|Ok, [[Rookie]]! Its your turn, see? I want you to squash those {{color-link|Mario|red|red}} and {{color-link|Luigi|green|green}} drips there!|Popple, [[Château de Chucklehuck]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''}}
Bowser had amnesia after [[Captain Goomba]] unintentionally stomped on his head at [[Hoohoo Mountain]]. As Bowser teamed up with Popple, he considered himself as "Rookie". Popple and [[Bowser]] were first seen interacting with each other in ''Superstar Saga'' at the [[Château de Chucklehuck]]. Before that, Popple and Bowser trapped [[Cork and Cask]] inside the barrels to fable the [[Chuckola Reserve]]. As they both encounter [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], Popple demands Bowser to battle against the Mario brothers. While Bowser tried to remember Mario and Luigi, Popple discourages Bowser to continue remembering. During battles, Rookie immediately attacks Mario and Luigi if they attempt to attack Popple. After battle, Popple blamed Rookie for losing to Mario and Luigi. Popple and Bowser agreed to not battle against them and continue finding the Chuckola Reserve.
