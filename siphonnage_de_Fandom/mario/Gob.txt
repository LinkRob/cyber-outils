|race     = [[Bob-omb]]}}'''Gob''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. He is a [[Bob-omb]] that lives at [[Fahr Outpost]].

==History==
Gob can be seen standing right outside the [[Inn]] of Fahr Outpost. Gob wishes to become bigger and even builds a large [[snowman]]. Gob would later a trouble at the [[Trouble Center]] after Chapter 7 has been completed. Gob says he has trouble speaking and that a [[Honey Candy]] will help. Honey Candies can be made by having [[Zess T.]] cook together a [[Honey Syrup]] and a [[Cake Mix]] (the latter can only be bought at the [[Pianta Parlor]] in [[Rogueport]]). If Mario gives him the Honey Candy, Gob will then reward Mario with 20 coins.
