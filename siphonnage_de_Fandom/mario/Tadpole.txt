'''Tadpoles''' are real-world amphibians that are [[frog]]s in early stages of development. They appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', in which Tadpoles live in [[Tadpole Pond]]. The location has an area named [[Melody Bay]], where Tadpoles act as musical notes that [[Mario]] can jump on to play one of a few songs.
