'''Sort Stack''' is a single-player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Opening==
The character [[jump]]s onto a cart in front of a bookshelf. A sweat drop appears inside a text bubble above them, suggesting that they are overwhelmed.

==Gameplay==
Many of the books are misplaced on a bookshelf, and the player's character has to take whichever books are misplaced and put them into the appropriate shelves. The books must be sorted by color: red books go on the top shelf; blue books go on the third shelf; yellow books go on the second shelf; and green books go on the bottom shelf. The player's character can only hold two books at a time, and the player earns [[point]]s whenever a shelf is cleared.

In the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the player's character has to organize only one shelf in 30 seconds to win. In Mini-Game Attack, the object is the same, but the [[time limit]] is halved to 15 seconds.

In Play Land, the player's character must organize as many shelves as they can within the time limit. Here, completely organizing a shelf adds a certain number of seconds to the time limit, depending on how many sections a shelf has, each fitting three books. Three seconds are rewarded for clearing a shelf with only one section, six for a shelf with two sections, and nine for a shelf with three sections.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|Pad}}: Left/Right
*{{button|gba|A}}: Right hand
*{{button|gba|B}}: Left hand

==Flavor text==
*In-game description: ''Put away each book where it belongs. You can only hold two at a time!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on "short stack."
