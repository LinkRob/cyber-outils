*''[[Super Mario 3D All-Stars]]''}}'''Blast Away the Wall''' is the sixth [[mission]] of [[Whomp's Fortress]] in [[Super Mario 64]] and it's [[Reissue|remake]]: [[Super Mario 64 DS]].

== Description ==
This level is unchanged between ''Super Mario 64'' and its remake. For the sixth Power Star, Mario, Yoshi, Luigi, or Wario has to use the cannon on the left of the starting point from Star 3 again and blast himself against the acute wall edge on the far right. The wall will crumble away and release the star then climb up and grab it. If he hits the wall on the left, the wall will crumble like the other one but nothing else will happen.

==Trivia==
*In <i>Super Mario 64 DS</i> the Star will reveal itself with the usual Star appear cutscene however in the original, the Star is already there upon the wall being blasted and no cutscene plays.
