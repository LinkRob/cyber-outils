'''Cranky's Kong Barrels''' are special types of barrels that appear in the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

== History ==
Cranky's Kong Barrels are found throughout levels of [[DK Isles]] and jumping into these barrels allows the Kongs to use their transformations or powerups. However, in order to use one of Cranky's Kong Barrels, one must visit [[Cranky Kong]] in his [[Cranky's Lab|lab]] first. After paying for a potion, the Kong will be able to use the barrels (they will become solid instead of intangible. All use of powerups from Cranky's Kong Barrels require [[Crystal Coconut]]s to use.

=== Types of Cranky's Barrels ===
*'''[[Strong Kong]]:''' This is used by [[Donkey Kong]]. Donkey Kong will become invincible and will be able to cross over hazards.
*'''[[Rocketbarrel Boost]]:''' This is used by [[Diddy Kong]]. Diddy will be able to take to the skies reaching areas that the other Kongs cannot access. Diddy can also use his [[Popgun|Peanut Popguns]] in this mode.
*'''[[OrangStand Sprint]]:''' This is used by [[Lanky Kong]]. Lanky will move at superhuman speeds allowing him to get to areas quicker and keep up with fast characters.
*'''[[Mini-Monkey]]:''' This is used by [[Tiny Kong]]. Tiny will shrink to small size allowing her to fit into small openings.
*'''[[Hunky Chunky]]:''' This is used by [[Chunky Kong]]. Chunky will grow to a large size and will be able to pick up large bolders and press large switches.
