'''Door Shy Guys''' are a rare variant of [[Shy Guy]] appearing in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. Hence their name, they are seen carrying doors, when a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] approaches a [[Door]], the Shy Guy will emerge from the ground and lift the door attempting to take it to the nearest edge. If Yoshi is not quick enough to catch the Door Shy Guy, it will leap off the edge with the door rendering it inaccessible, while the doors they carry aren’t required for level progression, they often lead to secret rooms containing [[Red Coin]]s and [[Smiley Flower|Flowers]] which may force a restart if the player is trying to attain 100 Points. A Door Shy Guys can be defeated with basically any attack be it a stomp, swallow, or [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] toss, when the Door Shy Guy is beaten, the door it was carrying is dropped allowing Yoshi access to the room it leads to.

==Trivia==
*Door Shy Guys are among one of the few Shy Guy variants that don’t directly interact with Yoshi rather just walking towards the edge with the door.
*The doors the Door Shy Guys carry don’t really fit with the environment as Yoshi can still access the room the door leads to regardless of where the Door Shy Guy drops it upon defeat.
