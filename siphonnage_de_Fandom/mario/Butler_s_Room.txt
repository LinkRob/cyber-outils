The '''Butler's Room''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Butler's Room has large tool cabinet in the northwest corner. On the left side of the room, there is an ironing board and a small bucket. In the exact center, there is a table with three stools. A sewing machine and an ironing board are north and south of the table, respectively. Along the east wall is a bucket of water, a stack of toilet paper, and a mouse hole.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Butler's Room is a room which first becomes accessible in [[Area Two]] of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]]. It is accessed from a door on the southern wall of the [[Laundry Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Laundry Room]]. At first, only [[Blue Mouse|Blue Mice]] are found here. However, after [[Luigi]] lights [[Shivers]]' candles, he has to go to the Butler's Room, where Shivers also goes to. After Luigi vacuums up Shivers, the [[Key]] to the [[Conservatory (Luigi's Mansion)|Conservatory]] appears. Luigi can optionally use his [[Game Boy Horror]] to examine the mouse hole, causing it to create a wind-like vacuum to the [[Hidden Room]].

During the [[blackout]], Blue Mice can be found here, just as before, along with two [[Gold Ghost]]s and two [[Flash]]es.

In PAL releases' and Nintendo 3DS version's [[Hidden Mansion]], some [[Flying Fish]] appear alongside the Blue Mice in the Butler's Room.
