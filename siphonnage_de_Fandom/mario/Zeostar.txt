'''Zeostars''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Zeostars are similar to [[Starslap]]s, and they are encountered at the [[Sea]] and in the [[Sunken Ship]] as well as in [[The Trial Course]] of [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]].

==Profile==
===Behavior===
Zeostars are not aggressive in battle as they spend most of their time sleeping. However, if they do wake it, they tend to [[Recover|heal]] themselves or attack using [[Viro Plasm]], causing the target to become [[Poison (status effect)|poisoned]].
