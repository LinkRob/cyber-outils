A {{Nihongo|'''1 UP Heart'''|1UPのハート|1UP no hāto|1UP Heart}} is an item which appears in a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, and first appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. 1 UP Hearts act the same as the more recurring [[1-Up Mushroom]]s in that they grant [[Mario]] or another character a [[1-Up]].

The reason for 1 UP Hearts appearing in a few ''Mario'' games for the [[Game Boy]] instead of a 1-Up Mushroom is likely because of the system's monochromatic palette, as 1-Up Mushrooms are generally distinguished from [[Super Mushroom]]s from having a green coloration.

== In [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] ==
===''[[Super Mario Land]]''===
[[File:SMLHeart.png|frame|left]]
{{Nihongo|'''1-Up'''|1UPハート|1UP hāto|1UP Heart}} make their first appearance. They can be found after the minigames. When [[Mario]] collects a 1-Up Heart, he will receive extra life.

===''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''===
[[File:SML2 1-Up Heart.png|frame|left]]
The 1-Up Heart returned in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'' with the same function but different sprite design. The artwork differentiates the monochromatic sprite. The sprite of the 1-Up Heart has a lighter color inside the sprite.

==In ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' ([[Game Boy]])==
1 UP Hearts reappeared in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for the [[Game Boy]]. Hence the name, "1 UP" appears on the sprite.
