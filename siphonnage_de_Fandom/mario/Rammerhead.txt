'''Rammerheads''' are hammerhead shark-like enemies from Super Mario 3D World.

==Profile==
Rammerheads aren’t hostile, as they just swim about in whatever pattern they are assigned.

However, touching a Rammerhead's [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammerhead_shark#Cephalofoil cephalofoil] will damage the player, so the player must traverse around it in the levels it appear in. There is no way to destroy it other than using a [[Starman]] or a [[Golden Leaf]]. Rammerheads often swim in the same pattern, so memorizing their movements is okay.

==In ''Super Mario 3D World''==
A Rammerhead is first encountered in [[Captain Toad Makes A Splash]], where there is one leaping over a wooden bridge that [[Captain Toad]] must walk over. Rammerheads are notably featured in [[Rammerhead Reef]] in [[World 7 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 7]], where they take up most of the spotlight in the underground water levels.
