'''Suction Catch''' is a move in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', which names it '''Suction''', and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'' under its current name.

==Description==
Suction Catch can only be used by [[Birdo]] while on the field. It allows Birdo to catch a ball a distance away from her. If Birdo uses Suction Catch to catch a flying ball, she gets a Big Play.
