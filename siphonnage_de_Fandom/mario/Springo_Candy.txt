The '''Springo Candy''' is a green [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. It has a picture of a spring on the wrapper, and when a player uses it, their torso downward morphs into a spring. This makes them a "Springo" character and allows them to bounce to an opponent's space chosen by the dart wheel. This is similar to landing on the [[Warp Space]] from ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' or using the [[Klepto Orb|Klepto Capsule]] from ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

Springo Candy normally costs 20 [[coin]]s, but this is reduced to 10 coins if the [[Candy Shop]] is selling candy at discounted prices. The Candy is found on [[DK's Treetop Temple]], [[Goomba's Booty Boardwalk]], [[King Boo's Haunted Hideaway]], and [[Koopa's Tycoon Town]].

If Springo Candy is used during a Tag Battle, the dartboard will show only the opposing team, as players cannot jump to their corresponding teammates. If the Candy is used in Duel Battle or Star Battle Arena mode, the dart wheel does not occur as the opponent is the only player to whom the user can jump.
