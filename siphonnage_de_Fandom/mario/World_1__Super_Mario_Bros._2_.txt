'''World 1''' is the first world [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], and [[Toad]] venture through in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It is divided into three levels and inhabited by [[Tweeter]]s, [[Shy Guy]]s, [[Snifit]]s, and [[Pidgit]]s. At the ends of the first two levels, the heroes have to face the [[Birdo Egg|egg]]-spitting [[Birdo]], and [[Mouser]] at the end of the last level, before [[World 2 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 2]]. This world tends to have waterfalls with log bridges.

==Level information==
===World 1-1===
World 1-1 starts with the chosen character dropping down from a floating door. At the bottom of the screen, there is another doorway, which leads outside. It's a simple level with enemies like [[Shy Guy]]s, [[Tweeter]]s, and [[Ninji]]s. There is an extra [[1-Up Mushroom|1-Up]] disguised as a plant next to a [[POW Block|POW]] on top of a hill. There is a shortcut to Birdo that can be found in the cave area halfway through the level. After climbing up the vine, instead of exiting to the right to the normal exit, jump across the waterfall and use [[bomb]]s to blow up blocks. After going through the door on the other side, proceed to the right and [[Super Jump]] over the wall on the right side of the screen, where [[Birdo]] is faced in. Birdo is fought at the end.

===World 1-2===
At the start of the level, the player has to cross a very long chasm with the help of [[Pidgit]]. Afterwards there are two [[vase]]s - the first has a 1-UP at the bottom, and the second has a [[key]] for opening the locked door. Beezos will try to knock player off the Magic Carpet in this level. Inside the cave, the player has to crumble walls. When leaving the cave, there are a lot of [[Vegetable|veggies]]. After the player goes through the door, they will find a pink Birdo that spits only eggs.

===World 1-3===
The level features a hidden warp to [[World 4 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 4]]. Right before the castle, there will be a cluster of plants, one of which is a [[Potion|magic potion]]. The player must take the magic potion past the far right of the screen, past the castle, to a vase. Then the player needs to use the magic potion to create a door, enter the door, enter the vase while still in dreamland, and the player will be warped to World 4.

For the normal exit which will lead to [[World 2 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 2]], the player needs to play the castle as normal and reach the end to face [[Mouser]].
