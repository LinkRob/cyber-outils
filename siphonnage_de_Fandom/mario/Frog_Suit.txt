The '''Frog Suit''' is a [[list of power-ups|power-up]] item that debuted in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. The Frog Suit transforms [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] into [[Frog Mario]] or Frog Luigi. It grants Mario or Luigi the power to [[jump]] higher, swim faster, and resist water currents better, but they can only hop while on land. When Frog Mario or Frog Luigi is hit by an enemy, they revert back to [[Super Mario (form)|Super Mario]] or Super Luigi.

The [[Penguin Suit]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' has somewhat similar swimming abilities to those of the Frog Suit.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Bros. 3''====
[[File:Sea Side.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Mario swimming through [[Water Land]] with a Frog Suit]]
[[File:Frog Suit SMB3 sprite.png|frame|right]]
[[File:Frog Suit SMAS SMB3.png|frame|right]]
The Frog Suit first appears in ''Super Mario Bros. 3''. It can be found inside [[Giant ? Block]]s in certain bonus rooms, and can be obtained from [[Toad House|Toad's House]]s from [[Water Land|World 3]] and beyond; a Toad's House in a secret part of [[Desert Land|World 2]] also has one. The suit, as stated above, lets Mario swim faster and better, jump higher, swim through water currents, and hop while on land.

The Frog Suit is a handy item to use in underwater levels. Without one, Mario and Luigi will sink underwater and can only swim up and/or left/right. Frog Suits allow them to remain stationary in the water, and swim in any direction. However, Frog Suits do not provide much help on dry land. When out of water, Frog Mario and Frog Luigi move very slowly, as they can only move by hopping (instead of running). On the positive side, Frog Suits do allow the Mario Brothers to jump higher than they can normally, and it is possible to run on dry land in Frog form if the player is carrying an object, like a [[Koopa Shell]].

If Mario or Luigi defeats a Koopaling while in Frog form, the restored [[Kings|king]] will say, "Oh me, oh my! You've been transformed! Shall I change you back with this wand?"

====''Super Mario Maker / Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS''====
Although the power-up itself does not appear, ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' features a [[Mystery Mushroom]] [[Costume Mario|costume]] based on the Frog Suit for the ''Super Mario Bros.'' theme. In addition, in ''Super Mario Maker'' and at the start of ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', knocking on a [[Warp Door]] in the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' theme in Edit Mode may cause [[Weird Mushroom (item)|Weird Mario]] to appear in a Frog Suit.

====''Super Mario Maker 2''====
The Frog Suit was added to ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' in the version 3.0.0 update released on April 22, 2020, appearing as an item in the ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' style. It allows the player to move more efficiently underwater, along with being able to run on the surface of water when holding an item along if the [[P-Meter]] is maxed-out. In the nighttime ground theme, Frog Suits jump back and forth, rather than jumping in a single direction. Since the nighttime castle theme makes players swim in the air, the Frog Suit accordingly grants the same mobility here as it does underwater.

===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
{{quote|In my Frog Suit!<br>I can dive down deep!<br>In my Frog Suit!<br>I can lunge and leap!|[[In My Frog Suit]]}}
[[File:FrogSuitCartoon.jpg|thumb|Mario and Luigi, each holding a Frog Suit from ''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3'' episode "[[Super Koopa (episode)|Super Koopa]]".]]
The Frog Suit also appears often in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

In "[[Reptiles in the Rose Garden]]," Mario uses a Frog Suit to get the [[White House]] back on the surface of the [[Mushroom Sea]] after [[Bowser|King Koopa]] sinks it. 

In "[[Oh, Brother!]]", Luigi uses a Frog Suit to swim through a flooded [[Pipe Land|Pipe Maze]] in order to rescue Mario from King Koopa and [[Ludwig|Kooky]].

In "[[Mush-Rumors]]", Mario and Luigi don Frog Suits in an attempt to save [[Norman]]'s family from the Koopas, but are unsuccessful in the end. The episode's song is also dedicated to the Frog Suits.

The most prominent appearance of the Frog Suit in the cartoon is in the episode "[[The Ugly Mermaid]]," because Mario, Luigi, [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] and [[Toad]] are all using Frog Suits (and thus creating Frog Princess and Frog Toad), and they keep those suits through the whole episode.

In the beginning of "[[7 Continents for 7 Koopas]]," Mario and Luigi can be seen crossing a bridge wearing Frog Suits. They are planning to relax in the water with Princess Toadstool and Toad, when [[Bowser|King Koopa]] attacks, thus starting problems for the Mario Bros.

In "[[The Venice Menace]]" Mario suggests for Luigi and himself to take their Frog Suits with them on their trip to [[Venice]]. This piece of advice helps them greatly throughout the course of the episode. 

In "[[Super Koopa (episode)|Super Koopa]]", Bowser has access to a [[Power Pendant]] that gives him the same powers that Mario and Luigi normally used, including a Frog Suit. Mario and Luigi later wear Frog Suits, which they use to leap away from Super Koopa's attacks.

Additionally, Luigi is seen wearing a Frog Suit during the show's introductory sequence, in which he rescues Toad from [[Roy|Bully]] and [[Morton|Big Mouth Koopa]] after they capture him while underwater. Artwork of Frog Luigi also appears on the cover of Shout! Factory's Complete Series DVD set.

One distinct feature of the Frog Suits in the show is that the eyes on the suit mimic those of its wearer, a trait which would later be used in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

===''Super Mario Bros.''===
In the ''[[Super Mario Bros. (Valiant Comics)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' comic "[[Love Flounders]]", Mario wears a Frog Suit to help him swim underwater on his quest to retrieve [[Chuckberry|Chuckberries]], but loses it after being kissed by [[Bertha]].

===''Nintendo Adventure Books''===
Early on in the [[Nintendo Adventure Book]] ''[[Leaping Lizards]]'', Luigi can acquire a Frog Suit, which he needs if he encounters a swarm of Bloopers in a body of water in [[Iggy|Iggy Koopa]]'s secret lab; the Frog Suit allows Luigi to deflect some Sleepy Ink they spray at him. In a later book, ''[[Unjust Desserts]]'', Mario mentions he should have brought a Frog Suit while tumbling through some of the fluid in [[Yoshi]]'s body.

===''Super Mario-kun''===
In the first volumes of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'', beginning since the very first chapter of the series, Mario often pulls out a Frog Suit to cross various bodies of water, but never with good results with someone (mostly his friends) tearing it.

In the first chapter from the [[Super Mario-kun Volume 1|first volume]], Mario pulls a prank on Luigi, pretending to drown and then revealing to be wearing a Frog Suit.

The Frog Suit is also used when the power-ups from ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' are brought up, even when there is no water: for instance, in [[Super Mario-kun Volume 12|volume 12]], one of the [[San Mario]] uses one and later is fused to Mario and the other clones into [[Super Power-Up Mario]], where the Frog Suit makes for the shoes of the costume.

===''Paper Mario'' series===
====''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''====
After an approximate 20-year absence, the Frog Suit returns in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' as a [[Sticker#Frog Suit|sticker]] that Mario can wear. For one turn, Mario becomes able to avoid enemies' attacks by jumping over them; his next Jump attacks also become stronger.

It was originally designed to act like the [[Multibounce]] [[Badge]], but was ultimately replaced in that role by the Line Jump sticker. It can be found in [[Sandshifter Ruins]] and purchased at [[Outlook Point]], and it is placed as #62 in the [[Sticker Museum]].

====''Paper Mario: Color Splash''====
The Frog Suit returns as a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)#Frog Suit|card]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]'', acting as it did in the previous game. However, Mario now becomes [[Frog Mario]], his attack power remains the same, and he loses the form upon taking damage (this physically removes the Frog Suit, as in ''Super Mario Bros. 3'') or winning, rather than when his turn is over. It can be found from a [[? Block]] in [[Sunglow Ridge]], a [[Cutout]] in [[Fort Cobalt]], or purchased for 80 coins at [[Prisma Cardware]].

===''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie''===
In ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'', [[Luigi]] uses the Frog Suit to rescue a blue [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who was eaten by a [[Big Cheep Cheep]].

===Other appearances===
In the Super Mario Mash-Up Pack of ''[[Minecraft]]'', Iron Armor is replaced by the Frog Suit.
