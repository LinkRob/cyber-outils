{{Mentioned}}{{Infobox Group|name = Hoohoo Civilization|onlyappear = ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' (2003)}}'''Hoohoo Civilization '''is an old tribe who used to live in [[Hoohoo Village]] in [[Hoohoo Mountain]]. They were mentioned in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

== History ==
They were mentioned that they invented a type of writing called [[Hooroglyph]]. It was mentioned the "great people" by the [[Hoohooligans]].
