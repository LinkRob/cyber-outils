|race = [[Noki]]}}The '''Noki Elder's grandson''' is the grandson of the [[Noki Elder]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==History==
He resides in [[Noki Bay]] along with his grandfather. Whenever Mario and [[F.L.U.D.D.]] arrive at Noki Bay, this young [[Noki]] is usually found near the beginning of the level. He tells [[Mario]] where to find his grandfather and gives him a [[helmet]] for an increased air supply in water in certain missions. He also has a wife and a child, as seen in Noki Bay's [[Noki Bay#Episode 8: The Red Coin Fish|Episode 8]]. If Mario talks to him at the beginning of this Episode, he tells him that he retrieved them from their hiding place after the pollution in Noki Bay disappeared.
