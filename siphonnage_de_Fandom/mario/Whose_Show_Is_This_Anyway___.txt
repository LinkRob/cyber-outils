'''Whose Show Is His Anyway?!!''' is the first episode of ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. The episode takes place on [[S.S. Caviar]].

== Plot ==
The plot begins with the Silver Zephyr on the dock of S.S. Caviar, about to steal all the treasures on the cruise ship. Then, [[Wario]] drops from the sky and lands on the Silver Zephyr, making him drop his wand, [[Goodstyle]] and reverting him back to his original self, [[Count Cannoli]]. Wario picks up the wand as Cannoli recovers from the collision, demanding Wario to return his wand. Wario refuses to and waves the wand, unaware of its powers. Goodstyle is activated, and claims Wario to be his new master. With his powers, Goodstyle teaches Wario to become a master thief. Wario then gives himself a new name, the Purple Wind.

Wario then enters the ship. Inside the ship, he soon runs into a green treasure chest, which completing the minigame gets Wario a [[Guise Gem]], which Goodstyle eats to give Wario new disguises. This chest contains the Cosmic Guise Gem, which turns Wario into Cosmic Wario. Wario then runs into Cannoli once again, demanding him that he returns Goodstyle back to him, as it has served for generations in his family, but Wario refuses to once again, and shames Cannoli for his former fame. Wario then returns into a red treasure chest, which by completing the minigame earns him the "I Love You Lunchbox", worth $298. He'll also run into a purple treasure chest, which will earn him a map of how the S.S. Caviar is laid out. Cannoli will also appear, and if he catches Wario, he will have to go back to the start of the room. Wario will also run into a bag of money and a [[Munchelangelo]]. Cosmic Wario should shoot lasers at the platforms to be able to climb up the ladder to reach an upper area. He encounters a [[Squabble]]. Wario pushes the box in front of the door to gain access into the room, where he encounters [[Galley Cat]] and [[Pushy Cat]]. He moves forward, past a lift, and encounters Cannoli who his standing on a chandelier, warning him that he has booby-trapped the ship.
