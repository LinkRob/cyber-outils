|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}The '''Sea''' is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
The Sea is a fairly large place with water around. There is also [[Shaman]]s here that can sell items to [[Mario]].

==History==
After getting word from the "[[Yaridovich|Elder]]" that the Star Piece fell into the Sea, Mario and his team head into the body of water. After fighting through the many underwater enemies, Mario gets sucked through a whirlpool into the [[Sunken Ship]].
