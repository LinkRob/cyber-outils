'''''Ball''''' is the first ever game for the [[Game & Watch]] line of systems. It was first released in 1980.

that got a [[Mario]] edition in ''[[Game & Watch: Super Mario Bros.]]'' The game plays identically original game, but has a cosmetic difference where the protagonist's head is replaced with [[Mario]]'s. Regardless, the object for the player character is to juggle balls, with Game A providing two to juggle while Game B provides three. The standard version of Ball also appears in ''[[Game Boy Gallery]]'' and ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 2]]''.

== Trivia ==
* This isn’t the first time Mario has starred a juggling game, the first game would [[Mario the Juggler|''Mario the Juggler'']], which was also on a Game & Watch system.
* You can play as [[Luigi]] in the ''Game & Watch: Super Mario Bros.'' version by holding A after selecting a game mode.
