'''Bumbleprods''' are enemies in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Bumbleprod sideways.png|thumb|left|200px|''Wario Land: Shake It!'' artwork of a Bumbleprod from the side]]
A Bumbleprod is a yellow, turtle-like enemy with a metallic, navy-blue, helmet-like shell with an orange stripe in the center. A few spikes are lined on the orange line, and there is a large, sharp horn on the left and right sides each.

===Behavior===
Bumbleprods move back and forth without attacking [[Wario]], but if he jumps onto a Bumbleprod, Wario takes damage because of the spikes on its shell.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Bumbleprods have a threat rating of two out of three stars. Bumbleprods are encountered in various boss battles, such as [[Bloomsday Blowout]]. Wario can only attack a Bumbleprod by using an [[Earthshake Punch]] to flip it over temporarily. While the Bumbleprod is flipped, Wario can then [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] the Bumbleprod to defeat it.
