'''Parachute Cap''' is a [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|badge]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It allows the character to slow their fall and use a [[cap]] as a parachute. It can be activated by pressing R or shaking the controller while midair.
