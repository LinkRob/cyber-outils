'''Big Goombrat''' is an enemy in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They are a variant of both [[Goombrat]]s and [[Grand Goomba]]s. Big Goombrats first appear in ''[[Super Mario Run]]'', since the version 3.0.4 update, and return in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Big Goombrats have the same appearance as a Goombrat, but are at a larger size.

===Behavior===
Like Goombrats, Big Goombrats walk straightforward on the ground, and upon reaching an edge, a Big Goombrat starts walking the opposite direction. Big Goombrats do not actively try to attack [[Mario]], but they can still hurt Mario by touching him. [[Stomp]]ing a Big Goombrat splits it into two Goombrats, similarly to how [[Hefty Goomba]]s divide into two [[Goomba]]s upon being stomped.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Run''===
Big Goombrats are only encountered during certain levels in the Remix 10 mode. In the [[Lakitu's Revenge|World 5-1]] Special Remix Course, Big Goombrats can also split if knocked with a [[? Block]] from below. Mario can instantly defeat a Big Goombrat by touching it while [[Invincible Mario|invincible]].

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
Big Goombrats act the same as in ''Super Mario Run'', and they can be created by adding a [[Super Mushroom]] to a Goombrat.
