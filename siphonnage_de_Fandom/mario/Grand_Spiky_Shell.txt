A '''Grand Spiky Shell''', named '''Grand Bowser's Shell''' in PAL releases, is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
A Grand Spiky Shell is a [[Skill Item]] that can raise the Skill level of a teammate whose Skill level had been increased to level 4 using [[Spiky Shell]]s; this includes all [[Magikoopa]] variations, [[Boom Boom]], the [[Koopalings]], each Koopaling's evolved form, [[Bowser Jr.]], all three forms of [[Bowser]], and [[Rock Luigi]].

A Grand Spiky Shell can be obtained either by randomly opening up a [[? Block]], as a prize from the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] minigame, or from defeating Boom Boom in {{world-link|8|bowserp|World 8-Bowser's Castle (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)}} with a 10+ Combo attack.
