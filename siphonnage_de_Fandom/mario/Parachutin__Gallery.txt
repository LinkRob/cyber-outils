'''Parachutin' Gallery''' is a 4-Player minigame in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. The minigame's name is a pun and combination of "shooting gallery" and "parachute".

== Opening ==
The players jump off a beansteak and fall through the sky, passing through a panel of shapes that match on the top screen.

== Gameplay ==
When a colored shape and pattern appears on the top screen, players will need to hit that same one through five different panels, and the first one to get to five stars wins. It is possible for all 4 players to win the minigame.

The types of shapes that will appear are listed below;
*'''Square''' - ''Blue'' background
*'''Circle''' - ''Pink'' background
*'''Triangle''' - ''Green'' background
*'''X''' - ''Brown'' background
*'''Tear Drop''' - ''Dark blue'' background
*'''Diamond''' - ''Lilac'' background

== Ending ==
In the end, the winner(s) activate(s) a parachute to ground level, and they perform their victory dance(s).

== In-game description ==
*'''Rules''' - ''"Skydive through the panels that match those shown on the top screen."''
*'''Tips''' - ''"Each type of target has a unique color. You may be able to react faster if you watch for colors, rather than patterns."''
