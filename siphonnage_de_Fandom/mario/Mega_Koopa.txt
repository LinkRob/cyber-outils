'''Mega Koopa''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
It is a variant of [[Koopa Troopa]] with the appearance of an enlarged [[Koopa Troopa]] sprite from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. Despite this, the Mega Koopa is actually 3D, which can be seen when flipping.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
The Mega Koopa can only be encountered in [[The Bitlands]] of Chapter 3-1. A regular Koopa Troopa sees the heroes coming and manages to find a [[Mega Star]] to transform into the Mega Koopa. The Mega Koopa is then able to take out anything in its path. However, despite this, the Mega Koopa can be easily taken out in one hit when one of the heroes use a Mega Star of their own.

== Trivia ==
* Even though the Koopa who got the star was bipedal, he turns quadrupedal with the Mega Star.
