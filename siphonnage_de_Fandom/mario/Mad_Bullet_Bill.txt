A '''Mad Bullet Bill''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Mad Bullet Bills are a variant of [[Bullet Bill]]s, which they resemble, except Mad Bullet Bills are red and engulfed in fire.

=== Behavior ===
Mad Bullet Bills move a lot faster than regular Bullet Bills, similarly to how their variant, [[Mad Banzai Bill]]s, travel a lot faster than [[Banzai Bill]]s. Both Mad Bullet Bills and Mad Banzai Bills do not have a corresponding [[Bill Blaster]], so they are always fired from offscreen.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Mad Bullet Bills are first encountered in [[Hoo's Wood 2-8]].
