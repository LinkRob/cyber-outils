'''Hyper Bald Clefts''' are a variant of [[Cleft]] featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
These strange enemies are encountered only in the [[Glitz Pit]]. After the fight with [[Cleftor]] and his men has concluded, [[Mario]] cannot fight Hyper Bald Clefts again unless he loses the later battles. Like all of the "hyper" enemies, a Hyper Bald Cleft can charge its attack by 5.
