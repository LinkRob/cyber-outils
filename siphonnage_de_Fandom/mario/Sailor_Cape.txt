The '''Sailor Cape''' is an armor used by [[Geno]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from the [[Shaman]] at either the [[Sea]] or the [[Sunken Ship]] for 50 [[coin]]s after [[Yaridovich]] has been defeated. Equipping the Sailor Cape to Geno increases his [[Defense]] by 18 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 9.
