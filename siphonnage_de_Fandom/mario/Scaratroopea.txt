'''Scaratroopeas''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] equivalent of [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s.

== Profile ==
Scaratroopeas are almost identical to [[Paratroopea]]s except they wear a red [[Koopa Shell]]. In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', Scaratroopeas are bipedal, giving them closer similarity to Koopa Paratroopas, and have a green sprout added on their head as well as red shoes.

== History ==
=== ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' ===
Scaratroopeas are enemies only encountered in [[Joke's End]].

==== Battle ====
They attack by swooping down at their target. After they take enough damage, they lose their wings and fall, at which point they become normal [[Elite Troopea]]s. They have the ability to heal and revive other members.

=== ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'' ===
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''{{'}}s side story ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'', Scaratroopeas appear as an enemy. They are of the Flying type, and their [[Special Skill]] is [[Cranium Crush]].
