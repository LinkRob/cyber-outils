'''Invisible''', also known as '''Transparent''', is a recurring status effect in the {{series|Paper Mario}}.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
Invisible is a rather rare status condition. One of the only ways for [[Mario]] to become invisible is to use the [[Repel Gel]] which is a rare item itself in the game. [[Lady Bow]]'s [[Outta Sight]] also functions as a one-turn Invisible status. [[Gray Magikoopa]]s can also use their magic to turn their own allies or themselves Invisible. When someone is Invisible, they are immune to all damage until it wears off making it an extremely useful status when fighting against tough foes.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
[[File:InvisibleTTYD.png|thumb|left]]
Invisible is slightly more common than in the previous game, because it is caused mainly by using a [[Boo's Sheet]], which can be purchased in the [[Item Shop]] on the [[Excess Express]]. Certain other items such as [[Love Pudding]] and [[Kooky Cookie]] can cause the Invisible effect as well. Sometimes, a [[Boo]] from the [[Audience]] may fly on the stage and turn either Mario, his partner, or one of the enemies Invisible rendering them unable to take damage.
