'''Maykit''' and '''Billdit''' are characters from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They are residents of Rumbla Island's village, being the children of the village's chief [[Chief Willma|Willma]]. The duo love their mother, but do have their limits, as shown after [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] cleanse the [[Gorumbla]] of [[Glohm]] poisoning.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Maykit and Billdit both have lime green skin and dark green hair, a trait that their mother shares with them. Each sibling as a dark green cord coming from their head.

Maykit is a young Concordian girl. She wears a blue shirt with a light blue apron that has a white [[Hammer]] printed on it, orange-rimmed goggles, and dark green trousers.

Maykit's brother, Billdit, is taller. His getup consists of a black shirt and a red apron. A white hachimaki is tied around his head.

=== Personality ===
Maykit likes writing poetry and often speaks in verse, though there are moments where she does not do this, often when speaking seriously. Billdit likes to act tough, but his tough exterior belies a tendency to get worrisome.

== Role in ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'' ==
Maykit and Billdit have a chance to run one of the shops on [[Shipshape Island]]. Additionally, Maykit got lost on Twistee Island, and needs to be rescued and brought back to the Rumbla Village. Billdit also got separated, landing on Raynforst Island. The sibling on the island that gets connected first will run a shop on Shipshape Island and craft [[Hammer]]s. The rewards for the shop sidequest will change to fit the siblings' respective expertise. The sibling who is rescued second stays by their mother.

The duo appear prominently in the cutscene that plays after defeating the [[Gorumbla]] a second time after [[Zokket]] or [[Reclusa]] has infected him with toxic [[Glohm]] and [[Chief Willma]] tries to stop him. They tell her that the rage that she held against the Gorumbla is misplaced. It is also revealed that the Gorumbla looked after them when they were young. This leads to his assimilation into the family, after which he forges a bond with the three official members.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Maykit's name is a pun on "make it" and the name "May." Billdit takes his name from the name "Bill" (a form of "William") and "build it."
