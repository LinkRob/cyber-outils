'''Broggy Bonker''' is a Special Attack that can be only used by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

== Description ==
The attack uses twenty SP to be able to be used. This attack is unlocked once all 15 [[Blitty|Blitties]] are collected and returned to [[Broque Monsieur]]. Bowser has to clean Broggy. Depending on how well the [[Action Command]] for the attack is performed, the attack's damage will either be better or worse:
*OK &mdash; Broggy will charge into the enemy once from the front.
*Good &mdash; Broggy will charge into the enemy twice, one from the front, then from the back.
*Great &mdash; Broggy will charge into the enemy once from the front, then slam down on it from above. In the remake, Broggy charges at the foe, then bounces up and stomps them on his way down. He then turns around and pounces the foe.
*Excellent &mdash; Broggy will charge at the enemy, jump up for a slam, doubling damage, continue on, turn around and charge into the enemy, trailed by the 15 [[Blitty]]s, who also cause some damage on the enemy.
