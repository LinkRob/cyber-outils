{{Nihongo|'''Yoshi's Fruit Cart'''|ヨッシーのフルーツカート|Yosshī no furūtsukāto|Yoshi's Fruit Cart}} is a minigame found in ''[[Nintendo Land]]''. The game revolves around a [[Mii]] riding a [[Yoshi]]-Cart (that resembles the Yoshi-Bots in [[Mario Chase]]) and finding fruit. The player must use both the TV and the Wii U GamePad in order to complete it. The TV is the only way to see the fruit, and the player must use the GamePad to draw a path for the Mii to follow to collect them. The doors in the level will only open if the player collects all the fruit.
