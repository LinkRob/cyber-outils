'''Koopa Kappa''' is a [[Bowser]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's character must drop capsule lids on [[Koopa Kid]]s to trap them as they travel on one of two conveyor belts; the top one always has Koopa Kids sitting in an open capsule inscribed with either an {{button|gba|L}} or an {{button|gba|R}}, while the bottom one always has Koopa Kids sitting in a capsule inscribed with either an {{button|gba|A}} or a {{button|gba|B}}. For each conveyor belt, the player must press the button matching the capsule that Koopa Kid is sitting in the instant he reaches the green arrows. Whenever a capsule is dropped, the player's character must wait for a moment before they can drop another. A Koopa Kid is not trapped if the player drops a capsule lid which does not match the button inscribed on the capsule that Koopa Kid is sitting in.

If a Koopa Kid makes it to the end of a conveyor belt without being trapped, he goes into the room the player's character is in and presses one of five plungers. If all five are pressed, the barrier protecting the player's character rises entirely as they are blown up, and the minigame ends in a loss. 

In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the player must cap 40 Koopa Kids in total to win. In Play Land, they can select how many Koopa Kids to cap.

===Controls===
*{{button|gba|A}} / {{button|gba|B}} / {{button|gba|L}} / {{button|gba|R}}: Drop capsule; each button corresponds to a different type

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In Shroom City mode, Koopa Kappa is played at [[Bowser Hideout]] during the quest [[Boss Bowser]]. In it, [[Bowser]] challenges the player to complete Koopa Kappa after they win his card game. Winning Koopa Kappa unlocks not only the minigame but also two [[Gaddget]]s, [[Faux Flame]] and [[Hourglass (Gaddget)|Hourglass]].

==Flavor text==
''Press the right button to drop capsule lids on each of the Koopa Kids.''
