'''Chiritorie''' is a [[microgame]] which appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', under [[9-Volt]]'s [[Nintendo Classics]] folder, and later returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[18-Volt]]'s microgames. ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' also features a full minigame named [[Chiritorie (minigame)|Chiritorie]], based on the microgame, in which two players compete to have their Chiritori collect the most crumpled pieces of paper lying around.

''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]'' features a similar boss minigame titled [[Dustpan]], which has similar gameplay and graphics as Chiritorie.

==Layout==
In ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'', the Chiritori is on a pitch black background, surrounded by a rectangular light green frame, and is shown from an overhead perspective. To the right is a Chiritori remote control with an A on the white button, indicating that {{button|gba|A}} has to be held to move the Chiritori around.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', the Chiritori is on a hardwood floor. The perspective is more zoomed in, and the scene is instead surrounded by a curved pink frame. The Chiritori remote is no longer shown onscreen.

==Gameplay==
The object is collect all of the discarded crumpled paper lying on the ground before the timer runs out. By default, the Chiritori spins around, with a yellow arrow pointing out the direction it, and holding {{button|gba|A}} causes the Chiritori to continue straight in the direction it faces when it stops spinning. The Chiritori can bounce off walls. In Level 1, there is one piece of trash to pick up, in Level 2, there are four pieces of crumpled paper to pick up, and in Level 3, there are instead seven pieces of crumpled paper to pick up.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', the Chiritori is instead controlled by tilting the system. Level 1 features only a single crumpled paper as before, although Levels 2 and 3 instead only have two and three crumpled papers for the Chiritori to vacuum, respectively.
