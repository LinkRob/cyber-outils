'''Secret Scroll 1''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Description ==
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] may go to the first room to the right of the [[Hammerhead Bros.]]'s new home. A [[Thwomp (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Thwomp]] requests the Mario Bros. to break one of the three stones. The scroll appears after winning the minigame. After reading the scroll, the brothers learn to use the [[Swing Bros.]] technique.
