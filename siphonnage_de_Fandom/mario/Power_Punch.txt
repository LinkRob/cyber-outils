'''Power Punch''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
The Power Punch makes [[Mario]] or a partner [[Huge]], and it increases their [[Attack]] power by 2. The Power Punch's effects wear off after two turns. It can be dropped from various enemies or purchased at the [[Souvenir Shop]] in [[Glitzville]] for 15 [[coin]]s. A Power Punch can also be made by [[Zess T.]] mixing a [[Mini Mr. Mini]] and [[Point Swap]]. Sometimes [[Charlieton]] is carrying one, but it is quite expensive at times.
