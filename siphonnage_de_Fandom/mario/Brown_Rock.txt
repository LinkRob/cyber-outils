'''Brown Rocks''' are obstacles in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. There found throughout the [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can demolish them using a [[hammer]]. Brown Rocks are first introduced at [[Hoohoo Mountain]], in the home of the [[Hammerhead Bros.]], who forge a hammer out of [[Hoohoo Block]] for [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. Brown Rocks are mostly used early on, serving as a tutorial for using hammers.

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', they appeared as cracked gray rocks.
