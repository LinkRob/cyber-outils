The '''Arrow Field''' is an object that only appears in [[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]] on the [[Nintendo Switch]] and is a holographic sign either shown in red or green and shows the player which way they should turn.

== Description ==
As their name implies, Arrow Fields are insubstantial fields of chevron shapes that point in the correct direction. Their main purpose is to dissuade someone from going down the wrong path. The chevrons are usually red, though depending on the course, their colours may vary, usually for visuality purposes.

== In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' ==
The Arrow Field only appears in [[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe|Mario Kart 8 Deluxe's]] [[Booster Course Pass]] and was never included in the [[Mario Kart 8|Wii U version]].

Courses from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' imported to the [[Booster Course Pass]] that take place in real-world cities have more than one route being traversed, so the racers will have to deal with Arrow Fields on their way through. Some courses in ''Mario Kart Tour'' that were not based on cities might also have Arrow Fields.

As ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' has a single route per layout per city course, routes that are not taken are blocked with fences rather than Arrow Fields.

The following courses have Arrow Fields, coming from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' unless otherwise specified:

*[[Tokyo Blur]]
*[[New York Minute]]
*[[Sydney Sprint]]
*[[Mario Kart 64|N64]] [[Kalimari Desert]] has green Arrow Fields.
*[[London Loop]]
*[[Berlin Byways]]
*[[Mario Kart DS|DS]] [[Peach Gardens]]
*[[Amsterdam Drift]]
*[[Bangkok Rush]] has green Arrow Fields.
*[[Singapore Speedway]]
