== Effect ==
Just as with other Slaphammers, Mario must time the hit perfectly. Upon an Excellent execution of the attack, the enemy will go flying around the screen. The hammer is really powerful, and makes opponents really dizzy. 

== Trivia ==
* It is the strongest [[Slaphammer]] type in the game.
