|defeated = Un-defeatable}}'''Suits of Armor''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (video game)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. They attack mainly by swinging their swords at Luigi. They cannot be defeated and must be avoided.
==Appearances==
===''[[Luigi's Mansion (video game)|Luigi's Mansion]]''===
Suits of Armor are actually rare enemies that appear in this game only appearing in one hallway. There is a Suit of Armor that actually uses a mace to attack Luigi with.
===''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''===
Suits of Armor play a much larger role in this game. Suits of Armor appear in the mansions [[Gloomy Manor]] and the [[Treacherous Mansion]]. In Gloomy Manor, Suits of Armor are mainly seen in the [[Guard Hall]] where they all attempt to damage Luigi with their swords. There are also two Suits of Armor in the [[Entrance]] but they are not alive. In the [[Treacherous Mansion]], in some rooms, [[Greenie]]s will sometimes take over a Suit of Armor and try to damage him. The [[Tough Possessor]] takes over a body of a large Suit of Armor and even has others to aid him in the boss fight.
