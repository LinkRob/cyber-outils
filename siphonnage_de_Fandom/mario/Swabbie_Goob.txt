'''Swabbie Goob''' is a type of ghost in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', it is a kind of [[Goob]]. They only appear in the [[ScareScraper]]'s Spectral Catch-themed floor. Swabbie Goobs have 200 HP.

==Appearance==

Swabbie Goobs are Goobs wearing pirate-themed clothing. Same as regular Goobs, both kinds of ghost have white eyes and mouth. Swabbie Goobs act in the same way as Goobs.

==Description==

Swabbie Goobs might appear in a room if [[Luigi]] wears pirate clothing, if they are found, Luigi has to defeat them in the same way as defeating Goobs.
