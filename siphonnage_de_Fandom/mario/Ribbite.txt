'''Ribbite''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Ribbites are large, muscular frog enemies that can be found in [[Land's End]] and [[Bean Valley]]. They are a stronger variant of [[Frogog]]s.

==Battle==
In battle, Ribbites often fight alongside [[Beezo|Shy Away]]s. Ribbites mainly attack by biting, but they can also use special move attacks such as [[Venom Drool]] and [[Sleep Sauce]].

===Strategy===
Ribbites are vulernable to [[Ice (element)|ice]], so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] is very effective against them, as well as using an [[Ice Bomb]].
