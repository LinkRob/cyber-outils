'''Dash Food''' is an item exclusive to ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. This item gives the user a speed boost (similarly to a [[Dash Mushroom|dash mushroom]]) and has a chance to change their character's costume. 

== Description ==
Dash Foods vary in their designs, which depend on the region, but are always obtained inside food bags. These bags have simple dot eyes with a mouth below their eyes. One end of the mouth has a loop to represent a tongue, forming a licking lips face.

== Location ==
Dash Foods are located at [[Yoshi's]] restaurants around the world. They have different items inside depending on the design of the restaurant and where the restaurant is located. 

They can also be found in Dash Food Trucks that can be found driving along roads.
