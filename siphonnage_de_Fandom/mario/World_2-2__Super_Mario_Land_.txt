'''World 2-2''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the second level of World 2, the [[Muda Kingdom]].

==Overview==
World 2-2 is mainly a coastal level with water always seen at the bottom. There are new enemies to this level called [[Mekabon]]s who are unique to this level. Mekabons can be simply defeated by jumping off their heads. There are areas in the level where [[Mario]] can access that lead to many coins.
