'''Frogogs''' are large frog enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They only appear at [[Bandit's Way]], specifically during a battle alongside [[Spikey (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Spikey]]s and [[Goomba]]s. Frogogs are considered the strongest enemies on Bandit's Way.

==Battle==
In battle, Frogogs are not capable of using any special attacks but they make up for it for their very high physical attack. They also very high [[Heart Point|HP]] which may require more hits to defeat.

===Strategy===
As hinted by their [[Psychopath]] thought, Frogogs cannot come into contact with sunlight, meaning that [[Fire (element)|fire]] is its weakness. If [[Mario]] has learned it, he should use [[Fire Orb]] on them. Frogogs are also weak to [[Thunder (element)|thunder]] so [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]] is effective as well.
