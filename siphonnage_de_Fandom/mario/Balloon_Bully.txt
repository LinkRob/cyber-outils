'''Balloon Bullies''' are a supporting species in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Profiles==
===Physical description===
[[File:BalloonBullyYS.png|frame|left]]
Balloon Bullies are large, light blue inflatable balloons found in mechanical crates and [[? Bubble]]s.

===Behavior===
If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] fires an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at a Balloon Bully, it expands into a balloon-like creature with a log on its head.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Yoshi's Story''==
Three Balloon Bullies are encountered; two in [[Surprise!!]] and one in [[Lift Castle]]. An expanded Balloon Bully can allow a Yoshi to reach otherwise unreachable areas or items. While a Yoshi is standing on a log on the Balloon Bully, the player can control the Balloon Bully's movement using the Control Stick. If a Yoshi leaves a Balloon Bully's lift, it remains stationary for a few seconds, then slowly sink to the ground.

==In ''[[Mario Artist: Paint Studio]]''==
A Balloon Bully makes a cameo as one of the character stamps, specifically its artwork for ''Yoshi's Story''.
