'''''Kirby''''' is a video game franchise consisting mainly of platforming games. It stars a pink blob of the [[Kirby|same name]]. The franchise was co-created by [[Nintendo]] and [[HAL Laboratory]], the latter developing most of their games. ''Kirby'' has crossed over multiple times with the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]].

==List of ''Mario''-related references==
===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby's Dream Land|Kirby's Dream Land]]'' ([[Game Boy]])===
*Many areas in [[w:c:nintendo:Castle Lololo|Castle Lololo]] feature blocks resembling [[? Block]]s. Instead of a [[coin]] or power-up, however, a [[w:c:nintendo:Chuckie|Chuckie]] ([[w:c:nintendo:Hurly|Hurly]] in the [[w:c:nintendo:Extra Game|Extra Game]]) repeatedly comes out instead.

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby's Adventure|Kirby's Adventure]]'' ([[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]])===
[[File:KA Spiny Artwork.png|100px|thumb|Togezo artwork from ''Kirby's Adventure''.]]
*A stylized form of the [[Spiny]] known as the [[w:c:nintendo:Togezo|Togezo]] (the Spinies' Japanese name) appears as a regular enemy, appears as a regular enemy, and is the sole representative of the [[w:c:nintendo:Needle|Needle]] ability. They walk normally but periodically stop to curl up in a ball, resembling a [[Spiny Egg]] in the process, and roll quickly along the ground. In the [[Game Boy Advance|GBA]] remake, ''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby: Nightmare in Dream Land|Kirby: Nightmare in Dream Land]]'', they were replaced by the original enemy [[w:c:nintendo:Needlous|Needlous]], having been previously functionally replaced by [[w:c:nintendo:Punc|Punc]] in ''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby 64: The Crystal Shards|Kirby 64: The Crystal Shards]]''.

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby's Dream Course|Kirby's Dream Course]]'' ([[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]])===
[[File:Spiny's Dream Course.png|frame]]
Spiny reappears as an obstacle with the Needle ability. It now has a red shell making it further resemble Spinies of the ''Mario'' franchise.
{{br}}

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby's Dream Land 2|Kirby's Dream Land 2]]'' ([[Game Boy]])===
The second stage of [[w:c:nintendo:Ripple Field|Ripple Field]] features tubes that emit [[current]]s similar to the [[Warp Pipe|pipes]] in certain underwater courses in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby Super Star|Kirby Super Star]]'' ([[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]]) / ''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby Super Star Ultra|Kirby Super Star Ultra]]'' ([[Nintendo DS|DS]])===
*Mario, [[Toad]], [[Luigi]], [[Birdo]], [[Bowser]], and [[Princess Peach]] are in the audience of the [[w:c:nintendo:Megaton Punch|Megaton Punch]] sub-game and the [[King Dedede]] battle in [[w:c:nintendo:Spring Breeze|Spring Breeze]]. Bowser and Peach are only visible in Spring Breeze, and are hidden off-screen (though Peach is still partially visible). In the remade version of Spring Breeze, [[Wario]] replaces Birdo and Bowser, Peach is moved to a more visible position in the audience, and there are additional [[Toad (species)|Toad]]s.
*The map structure of [[w:c:nintendo:Dyna Blade (main game)|Dyna Blade]] is similar to the map structure of ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', with the "E" containing a single ambush room with an [[w:c:nintendo:Iron Mam|Iron Mam]] being similar to the roaming [[Hammer Bro]] [[Enemy Course]]s.
*A picture of Mario appears in the credits of [[w:c:nintendo:Milky Way Wishes|Milky Way Wishes]] and, in the remake, [[w:c:nintendo:Revenge of Meta Knight|Revenge of the King]].
*Several of the items in [[w:c:nintendo:The Great Cave Offensive|The Great Cave Offensive]], such as the [[Green Shell|Turtle Shell]] ([[Koopa Troopa|Koopa Shell]] in the remake), [[Bucket]], and [[Donkey Kong|Kong's Barrel]] (depicted as a regular [[barrel]] in the original and a [[DK Barrel]] in the remake) are from the ''Super Mario'' franchise. In the remake, the Zebra Mask is also referred to as the [[Phanto|Phanto Mask]].
*In the original ''Kirby Super Star'', the second room in [[w:c:nintendo:??? (Kirby)|???]] resembles [[Subspace]] from the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] version of ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.
*When Kirby activates his [[w:c:nintendo:Stone|Stone]] ability he will (very rarely) turn into a gold statue of Mario. The statue also appears in the credits for Revenge of the King in ''Kirby Super Star Ultra''. In the DS version, the statue's raised hand is changed from a V-sign to a fist pump, keeping in-line with the prohibition of the former in official depictions of Mario at the time.
*In the remake, [[w:c:nintendo:Kabula|Kaboola]], renamed Kabula, now has a [[Bullet Bill]]-like appearance. One of Kabula's special attacks involves shooting giant Bullet Bill-like missiles at Kirby.
*Also in the remake, one of the [[w:c:nintendo:Stone|Stone]] forms debuts, which resembles a [[Brick Block]] as it appeared in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' From ''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby: Triple Deluxe|Kirby: Triple Deluxe]]'' onwards, the form now resembles how Brick Blocks have looked since ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''
*The recurring enemy [[w:c:nintendo:Scarfy|Scarfy]] is given an alternate behavior in this game identical to [[Boo]]s, becoming threatening and chasing when Kirby's back is turned toward them and holding still when faced.

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby's Dream Land 3|Kirby's Dream Land 3]]'' ([[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]])===
[[File:KDL3 Spiny art.jpg|100px|thumb|Togezo artwork from ''Kirby's Dream Land 3''.]]
*Togezo also appears as a regular enemy in this game. Instead of performing their rolling attack from ''Kirby's Adventure'', they withdraw and extend their spikes when the player is in proximity, similar to a [[w:c:nintendo:Spikey|Spikey]] from ''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby's Dream Land 2|Kirby's Dream Land 2]]''. Unlike previous appearances, they are not the only source of the Needle ability.
*One of the [[w:c:nintendo:Heart Star|Heart Star]]s can be obtained in [[w:c:nintendo:Cloudy Park (Kirby's Dream Land 3)|Cloudy Park]] from an NPC that resembles a [[Fire Flower]]. Its Japanese name even translates to "Pseudo-[[Fire Flower]]".
{{br}}

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby Tilt 'n' Tumble|Kirby Tilt 'n' Tumble]]'' (GBC)===
*The game's level structure is similar to that of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', with every fourth level taking place in a castle. There are even two areas similar to [[Warp Zone]]s.

===[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby 64: The Crystal Shards|''Kirby 64: The Crystal Shards'']] (N64)===
*An enemy known as [[w:c:nintendo:I³|I³]] appears, a living stone cube with an identical attack pattern to a [[Thwomp]] (similar to the aforementioned obstacles in ''Kirby's Dream Land 3''). Their Japanese name,「ドネン」(''Donen''), plays off that of Thwomps,「ドッスン」(''Dossun''), and associated onomatopoeia.
*Scarfy again has its Boo-like behavior, which is also given to another recurring enemy, [[w:c:nintendo:Mumbies|Mumbies]]; for the latter enemy, this becomes standard to their behavior in later games.

===[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby: Canvas Curse|''Kirby: Canvas Curse'']] (DS)===
*Another Thwomp-like enemy appears, this one called [[w:c:nintendo:Thudd|Thudd]] (thus keeping the connection in English). The original Japanese name is「デンドン」(''Dendon''), additionally playing off「ドッスン」(''Dossun'').

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby's Return to Dream Land|Kirby's Return to Dream Land]]'' (Wii) / ''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby's Return to Dream Land Deluxe|Kirby's Return to Dream Land Deluxe]]'' (Switch)===
*The [[w:c:nintendo:Stomper Boot|Stomper Boot]] shares similarities with the [[Goomba's Shoe]].
*Red-colored [[w:c:nintendo:Scarfy|Scarfies]] and [[w:c:nintendo:Degout|Degouts]] called Hunter Scarfies and Hunter Degouts, respectively, have a behavior similar to [[Phanto]]s in the fact that they only become hostile whenever [[Kirby]] picks up a [[Key]].

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby: Triple Deluxe|Kirby: Triple Deluxe]]'' ([[Nintendo 3DS|3DS]])===
[[File:Stone Kirby Brick Block KTD.jpg|thumb|The Brick Block transformation in ''Kirby: Triple Deluxe'']]
*One of the possible forms of Stone Kirby is a [[Brick Block]].
*One of the moves [[w:c:nintendo:Wheel|Wheel]] Kirby can perform is the Rocket Start, which is a reference to the [[Rocket Start|starting boost of the same name]] in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series.
*When the [[w:c:nintendo:Ace Phanta|King Phanta]] possesses a piano, it resembles the [[Mad Piano]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and [[Super Mario 64 DS|its remake]].
{{br}}

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby and the Rainbow Curse|Kirby and the Rainbow Curse]]'' ([[Wii U]])===
*Two promotional comics for this game on [[Play Nintendo]] either reference Mario or have Mario make a direct appearance.
**In "Saved by the Rainbow", after [[w:c:nintendo:Elline|Elline]] breaks Kirby's fall with a rainbow, the announcer rebukes the two, saying that Elline cannot be used in ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Smash Bros.]]'', and Mario and Yoshi are seen looking down at them with an expression of disgust.<ref>https://play.nintendo.com/news-tips/comics/kirby-and-rainbow-curse-presents-saved-rainbow/</ref>
**In "An Evening Out", Elline sprays a trail of Italian sauce in the air.  Kirby, wearing a mustache and bow tie, rolls down the trail and says "It's-a me!", to which [[w:c:nintendo:Bandana Waddle Dee|Bandana Waddle Dee]] replies, "Aah!! Mario!!"<ref>https://play.nintendo.com/news-tips/comics/kirby-and-rainbow-curse-presents-evening-out/</ref>

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby: Planet Robobot|Kirby: Planet Robobot]]'' ([[Nintendo 3DS|3DS]])===
[[File:Wheel Kirby Rocket Start KPR.jpg|thumb|The Rocket Start in ''Kirby: Planet Robobot''.]]
*The Brick Block and [[Gold Mario]] statue are some of the forms that Stone Kirby and [[w:c:nintendo:Smash|Smash Bros.]] Kirby can use.
*The [[w:c:nintendo:Doctor|Doctor]] ability that debuts in this game is based on the moveset of [[Dr. Mario]] in the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series. Appropriately, scanning in a Dr. Mario [[amiibo]] also gives this ability.
*The Wheel ability's Rocket Start move returns from ''Kirby: Triple Deluxe'', referencing the starting boost in the ''Mario Kart'' series.
{{br}}

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby Star Allies|Kirby Star Allies]]'' ([[Nintendo Switch|Switch]])===
*The Brick Block form of Stone Kirby makes an appearance in this game.
{{br}}

===''[[w:c:nintendo:Kirby and the Forgotten Land|Kirby and the Forgotten Land]]'' ([[Nintendo Switch|Switch]])===
*The laughter used by the [[w:c:nintendo:Surprise Alien Boards|Surprise Alien Boards]] is similar to the original laughter of the [[Boo]]s from earlier games such as ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.
*The [[w:c:nintendo:List of Present Codes in Kirby and the Forgotten Land|Present Code]] found in [[w:c:nintendo:Waddle Dee Cinema|Waddle Dee Cinema]] is "KIRBYSTORY", which refers to one of the film posters in the original version of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.
