'''Rolling Brush Return''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] of [[Bowser Jr.]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Bowser Jr. jumps and rolls with his [[Magic Paintbrush]] out and uses it to hit the ball along the way.
