'''Thugly''' is the boss of the [[Cliff (world)|Cliff world]] of the [[Donkey Kong Island]], as well as the sixth boss in the games ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'', its [[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D|3DS]] and [[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD|Nintendo Switch]] [[Reissue|ports]]. He is possessed by the [[Tiki Tak Tribe]] member [[Xylobone]]. Thugly is encountered in the level bearing his name, Thugly's High Rise, and his intro features him eating bananas only for one of the Tiki Tak Tribe generals, namely Xylobone (a xylophone-based Tiki Tak Tribe member) to get his attention.

== Appearance ==
﻿Thugly looks similar to [[Mugly]], an earlier boss from the same game, in that he is a gigantic creature that appears to be an amalgamation of a rhino, a toad and a porcupine, except Thugly has hot plates on his back (similar to those on an armadillo) and his body is purple, and his teeth are sharper, making him look more fearsome than his yellow counterpart. His eyes are also smaller and red, and he has sharper horns. Thugly has markings on his arms and back, that look like thunderbolts. He has large, burly eyebrows, and a small snout. The front part of his head overlaps his jaw. Thugly also has a white underbelly, with a small marking underneath. Another large factor is his huge, red horns, which are his main means of attacking.

== Combat ==
In battle, Thugly attacks by charging, much like Mugly. However, Thugly cannot be attacked until his plates slide off his back, at which point [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] can strike him. He can also jump high into the air and slamming into the floor, creating a shockwave. This is telegraphed by him scraping his back paws over the ground. After the second and third charge, Thugly looks around for a few seconds before covering his back. He can also dash at Donkey Kong before crashing into a wall, causing boulders to fall down, although Thugly will collapse on the floor and become dazed.

After three hits, Thugly becomes enraged and destroys the floor, and gains a new attack where he blasts a fire stream which must be dodged by ducking. He also dashes more quickly and becomes vulnerable for a shorter time. Additionally, Thugly becomes hazardously surfaced during some of his attacks, including his fire breath. After another three hits, Thugly destroys the floor of his level again, and starts shooting fireballs that split into smaller fireballs, which also causes his body to become hazardously surfaced. Upon defeating Thugly, Xylobone will become dazed and expose himself to a finishing blow from Donkey Kong.
