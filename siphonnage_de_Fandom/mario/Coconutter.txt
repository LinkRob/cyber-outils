'''Coconutters''' are a type of enemy fought at [[Yoshi's Island]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. They resemble a coconut but have legs that resemble grass. Coconutters have a skeletal mouth and their eyes are empty holes.

== Behaviour ==
Coconutters throw their spears at anyone who enters their territory in the field.

==Battle==
In battle, a Coconutter can either shoot a [[poison (ailment)|poison]] dart or throw his spear at its target. Both of these attacks can be dodged by jumping. The spear attack can be determined by whichever eye of the Coconutter twinkles.

A Coconutter can also change the position of his spear to be vertical, making himself a [[Spiny (Enemy type)|spiked]] enemy. The next turn, a rain cloud will then appear, healing up to 30 HP to every enemy on the field.

Coconutters have a stronger variant found later in the game, called the [[Red Coconutter]].

==Trivia==
*Etymology: Coconutters earned their name from the term "nut" (crazy person) and "coconut".
*[[Cocoknight]]s from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' bear a strong resemblance to Coconutters.
