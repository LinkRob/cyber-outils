'''The Wind Waker''' is one of [[9-Volt]] & [[18-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. It returns as one of 18-Volt's microgames in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. The microgame is both based on and takes place within ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker]]'', a [[Nintendo GameCube]] game.

== Layout ==
The minigame takes place somewhere within the [[w:c:zelda:Great Sea|Great Sea]], between the [[w:c:zelda:Forest Haven|Forest Haven]] and the [[w:c:zelda:Forbidden Woods|Forbidden Woods]]. The background music during the microgame is the theme for when traveling the Great Sea.

== Gameplay ==
As [[Link]] is riding his [[Deku Leaf]], being guided by the [[wind]], the player has to make him land on a tree stump platform. In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'', each higher difficulty level places Link farther from the platform. In ''WarioWare Gold'', the platform becomes increasingly smaller for each difficulty setting.

== Trivia ==
*Like the microgame [[Super Mario Sunshine (microgame)|Super Mario Sunshine]], each difficulty of The Wind Waker plays a different portion of the same song.
