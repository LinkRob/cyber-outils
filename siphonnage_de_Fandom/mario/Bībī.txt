{{Nihongo|'''Bībī'''|ビービー}} is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. They are bees which live in honeycombes. Whenever Mario approaches a Bībī, it comes out and tries to attack Mario by stinging him. It is immune to fireballs but can be defeated with jumps. Bībī also has relatives named [[Skeleton Bee]] and [[Būichi]].

== In ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'' ==
They are found on the walls with eight holes. Every hole houses has a different one, which will exit their hole after the previous one is defeated. They try to attack by exiting its holes and slowly homing [[Mario]]. Bees can be defeated by a [[jump]], but [[fireball]]s will have no effect. After defeating all every Bee or defeating one with a [[Spin Jump|spin jump]] or while [[Invincible Mario|invincible]], they will not appear from the wall.
