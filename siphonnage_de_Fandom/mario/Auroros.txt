'''Auroros''' are enemies which appear in [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. A majority of the levels in which they appear feature either [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] or [[Diddy Kong]] as playable characters the first time.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Auroros are white birds with a rainbow pattern on their wings and tail.

===Behavior===
Auroros act like flying spears who dive bomb at enemies. If an Auroros gets its beak stuck into the ground, it can be picked up and used as a thrown item.

===Etymology===
The name Aururos is derived from "aurora," referring to the colorful tips of its feathers.

==In ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''==
Auroros are first encountered in [[The Lake]] by both [[Diddy Kong]] and [[Fox McCloud]].
