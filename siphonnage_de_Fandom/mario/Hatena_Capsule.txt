The '''Hatena Capsule''' is an item from [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder|''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'']].

== Description ==
Hatena Capsules are yellow capsules adorned with white question marks. In essence, they look like rounded [[Mystery Block|Mystery Blocks]]. They resemble Gashapon toy capsules.

== In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' ==
Hatena Capsules appear on rope of some handles that can be broken if the player pulls hard enough, at which point the capsules fall off and drop to the ground, breaking open upon impact to release their contents. Occasionally, [[Condrop|Condrops]] can carry Hatena Capsules, dropping them similarly. The capsules cannot otherwise be interacted with directly, and they only release their contents when something causes them to hit the ground.

In the [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park|Nintendo Switch 2 remake]], [[Plucky]] may help [[Mario (character)|Mario]] in the [[Koopalings]]' stages by flying in Hatena Capsules in his claws, dropping them to release a power-up that Mario can get his hands on.

== Behind the Scenes ==
The name "Hatena Capsule" comes from Japanese ''Hatena'' (written はてな), which refers to the question mark (?).
