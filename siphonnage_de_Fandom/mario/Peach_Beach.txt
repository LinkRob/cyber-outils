'''Peach Beach''' is the second race of the [[Mushroom Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' and is one of only two tracks to commence on [[Isle Delfino]] in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. The second is [[Delfino Square]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash]]'' ==
[[Peach]] Beach's main features are the tide - low tide can result in a sub-shortcut - the [[Cataquack]]s, which launch racers into the air upon contact and make them drag and having the [[Daisy Cruiser]] in the background. It is also notable for hosting the awards ceremony.

Towards the finish line is the Pinata Statue, which also appeared in [[Delfino Plaza]].

There is a shortcut found at the beginning,  by turning left where the player can see a pipe. Entering it will give a player the [[Double Item Box]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' ==
The track reappears in Mario Kart Wii as the first race of the Shell Cup. The layout stays the same and the graphics change very little. [[Cataquack]]s chase after racers if they approach nearby. In [[Time Trials]], Cataquacks do not chase racers.

== Shortcuts ==
* On water you can speed up not to slow you down.
* You can use the mushroom to go across the tree.

There are multiple different paths in this track.

== Trivia ==
* This course was used for the success of victory (or the results theme) in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''
* In this race, you can see the [[Daisy Cruiser]] when going on sand.
