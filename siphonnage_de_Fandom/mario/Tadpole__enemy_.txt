A '''Tadpole''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Tadpole has a red body with a light yellow underside. Although they bear a resemblance to real-world tadpoles, these various official sources describe them as being fish.

===Behavior===
A Tadpole jumps out from either the water or [[lava]] to try and hit [[Wario]], causing him to roll backward until hitting a wall. This is the same behavior as [[Tobī]] from ''[[Wario Land II]]'' have except they only bounce Wario back a slight distance. Tadpoles are not present if Wario is underwater.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Wario first encounters three Tadpoles in [[The Peaceful Village]] and later five more in [[The Big Bridge]]. Tadpoles are typically positioned to knock Wario away from the objective, and he can either avoid the Tadpoles or defeat them by [[jump]]ing onto them.
