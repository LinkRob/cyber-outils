The '''Cursed Mushroom Orb''' is a [[Orb|Capsule]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', containing a [[Poison Mushroom]]. It can be thrown to a space on the board or used on the player for five [[coin]]s. Its effect is still the same as from ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. In ''[[Mario Party 6]]'', it becomes an Orb that players can only use in Solo Mode, where they can only roll 1-3 on the [[Dice Block|dice]].
