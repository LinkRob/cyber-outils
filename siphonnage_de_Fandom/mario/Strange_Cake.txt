A '''Strange Cake''' is a [[recipe]] item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It would later be replaced by the [[Love Pudding]] item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
This item is another one of [[Tayce T.]]'s recipes. To create this dish, [[Mario]] must need both a [[Strange Leaf]] and a [[Cake Mix]]. This item doesn't restore HP or FP like its ingredients. However, this item is very unique where it, instead, gives Mario one of three different status ailments: [[Invisible|Invisibility]], [[Electrified|Electrification]], or [[Sleep|Sleepiness]].
