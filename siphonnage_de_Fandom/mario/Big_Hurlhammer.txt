|name=Big Hurlhammer (Battle Card)}}'''Big Hurlhammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Hurlhammer is the strong line version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer Attack|Hammer attack]]. It is a big blue-gray hammer with a blue/cyan checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 50/250 coins

Sell Price: 25/125 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Hurlhammer is first seen in [[Toad Trainworks]], but can also found in [[Sunset Express]] and [[Redpepper Crater]].
