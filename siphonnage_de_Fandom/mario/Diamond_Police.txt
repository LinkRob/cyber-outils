The '''Diamond Police''' are unseen characters who drive police cars, serving as the police force of [[Diamond City]]. They appear in [[Mona]]'s story, where they chase after her after she speeds by one while rushing on her way to work. As the player completes the [[microgame]]s, Mona pushes back against the police cars chasing her.
