'''Fires''' are an enemy from the [[Donkey Kong (series)|''Donkey Kong'' series]]. They first debut in the [[arcade]] game of the [[Donkey Kong (video game)|same name]] as one of the fire-based enemies, the other one being [[Fireball (Donkey Kong)|Fireballs]]. Fires are similar to Fireballs, but have a different design and are only encountered in [[100m]].

Despite appearing in the very first ''Donkey Kong'' game, Fires have made few reappearances, with all of them referencing their debut appearance in the original ''Donkey Kong'' game. Fires' lack of reappearances is likely because they had been overshadowed by Fireballs.
