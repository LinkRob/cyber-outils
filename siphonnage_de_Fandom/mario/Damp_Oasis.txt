'''Damp Oasis''' is the fourth [[level]] of [[World 2 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|World 2]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Layout==
Damp Oasis is mainly a sand based level. It has rocks and cactus and is similar in appearance to Drybake Desert. Once [[Mario]] has used the [[Faucet]] Thing sticker, Damp Oasis now has lush, green grass and water.

==History==
As Mario first heads into Drybake Ruins, he is met with a vicious sandstorm.

[[File:Pmssguide72-275x165.png|thumb|273px|Damp Oasis brought to life.]]
Mario heads toward the gust of the sandstorm west until he sees a tornado. Using a combination of [[Paperize]] and the [[Vacuum]] Thing Sticker, Mario is able to suck away the tornado clearing the sandstorm. Mario then helps out a Toad near the dried oasis using Paperize and the Faucet Thing Sticker. The Toad is pleased and floats away as Mario enters the nearby house and grabs the third Tablet Piece. After doing this, Mario heads back west, grabs the [[Comet Piece]], and clears this extremely short level.
