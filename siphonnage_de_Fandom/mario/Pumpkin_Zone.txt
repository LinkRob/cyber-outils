The '''Pumpkin Zone''' is a haunted-themed zone which appears in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. It features three regular [[level]]s, a boss level in which the world's boss [[Witch (Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins)|Witch]] is fought, and two secret levels. Several [[Boo]]s appear in the levels as well as in the background of the world map.
