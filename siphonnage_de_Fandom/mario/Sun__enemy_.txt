The '''Sun''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
It is a round, red star with a huge mouth, black, bushy eyebrows, and small eyes.

===Behavior===
The Sun is out during daytime in certain areas. It attacks by flying around after [[Wario]] and spitting fireballs at him.

invulnerable enemy that appears in ''Wario Land 3''. It appears in the daytime.

== History ==

=== ''Wario Land 3'' ===
A sun appears in two [[level]]s, [[The Vast Plain]] and [[The Colossal Hole]], although only during the daytime, with [[Mr. Moon]] taking its place at night instead. If Wario touches the sun or one of its fireballs, he briefly becomes [[Hot Wario]]. In The Colossal Hole, if Wario is below the sky, the sun does not chase him. A sun cannot be defeated.
