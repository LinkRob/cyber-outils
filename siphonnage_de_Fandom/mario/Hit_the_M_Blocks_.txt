'''Hit the M Blocks''' is the second level of [[World 1 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 1]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. A [[Pink Yoshi]] takes control in this level, the level takes place in a grassy field (similar to that of the previous level) with a nice view of the lustrous sea however this level takes place at night. A portion of the level also takes place in a cavern, levelwise, the level introduces [[M Block]]s which can only be hit while [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] is carrying [[Baby Mario]]. [[Stork Stop]]s also start appearing in this level after [[Catch the Breeze!]] is completed.

== Layout ==
Starting off, move to the right while defeating the [[Crazee Dayzee]]s travelling along the path, also, hit the [[M Block]]s for some coins and grab the Red Coin (located atop an [[M Block]] above two aligned [[M Block]]s). Continue along the path and hit the end [[M Block]] in the row of three which contains a [[Flower Plant]] which grows to the height of a platform, the platform contains a [[Smiley Flower]], a couple [[M Block]]s, and a [[Fly Guy]] holding a [[Red Coin]]. After collecting the goodies, move to the right and jump to a "cheese block" platform which holds an [[M Block]] containing two [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], and a [[Red Coin]]. After this, continue along the path, hit the [[Winged Cloud]] to score some more [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and then continue onward, shoot some [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s at the hovering coins as one [[Coin]] in either lot is a [[Red Coin]], this will score you two [[Red Coin]]s right there. Hit the two [[M Block]]s to get some more [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]], then enter the [[Pipe]].

After entering the Pipe, you will end up in the underground section, flutter across to the stone platform and hit the invisible [[Winged Cloud]] above to get some more [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]]. Then advance back to the left and hop along the toadstools collecting the [[Red Coin]]s on them whilst beating the [[Lantern Ghost]]s continue along the path and hop on the toadstools and collect some more [[Red Coin]]s, launch an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] up at the [[Smiley Flower]] to collect it and continue along the path, hit the [[M Block]] there to reveal a [[Spring Ball]] and hop up to the ledge to get a stash of [[Coin]]s, there is also a [[Baby Coin]] here but [[Baby Peach]] is required to obtain it. Drop back down and continue hopping across the toadstools whilst avoiding the [[Nipper Plant]]s, hit the [[Fly Guy]] above carrying the [[Red Coin]] and tag the [[Middle Ring]] before hitting the [[M Block]]s which causes another [[Spring Ball]] to appear. Hop up and grab the [[Coin]]s and the [[Red Coin]]s amongst them, after this grab the [[Key]] from the [[Baron von Zeppelin]] and use the [[Key]] to unlock the locked door which leads to the [[Flutter Challenge]] minigame, after this, advance back to the right and use the [[Spring Ball]] to return back aboveground.

Upon returning back aboveground, hit the [[M Block]] to make a [[Flower Plant]] bloom from it and climb the Flower Plant to reach the higher platform, high above, there is a [[Winged Cloud]], hit the Winged Cloud to create a staircase which leads to a Pipe, grab the [[Red Coin]] underneath and enter the Pipe, the pipe takes you to a room high in the clouds with a [[! Switch]], dash get all the [[Coin]]s that appear and the [[Smiley Flower]] at the end, you better run because the stone blocks rise after the switch is activated so if you're too slow, the final stone block will block the ledge with the Flower requiring some tough manuevers to have a shot at reaching it. After getting the goodies, exit  through the pipe to return to the main level, advance to the right and you should find some [[Pointey]]s, jump on the Pointeys or toss [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] at them and grab the [[Red Coin]]s and the [[Smiley Flower]]. After this, jump on the [[Flatbed Ferry]] to reach the higher ledge, hit the [[M Block]]s here to score some more goodies, watch out for the [[Shy Guy]]s here as you hop onto the [[Ferris Wheel]]s to earn some more items, the last of the [[Red Coin]]s are located along the largest Ferris Wheel and the final [[Smiley Flower]] is inside a [[Winged Cloud]], hit the [[Winged Cloud]] then obtain your prize, another [[Winged Cloud]] here also contains some more [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] if you're short so hit this one too then advance to the right and jump through the [[GOAL! Ring]] to finish the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
During your first, you are unable to grab this [[Baby Coin]] as it requires [[Baby Peach]] but once you complete [[Catch the Breeze!]], be sure to return to this level, there is a [[Stork Stop]] in the cavern, use [[Baby Mario]] to hit the lone [[M Block]] containing the [[Spring Ball]] and after that, switch to [[Baby Peach]] to hop up and obtain the [[Baby Coin]].
