|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Migraine''' is a self-destructing special attack used only by [[Cluster]]s and [[Pulsar]]s in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Migraine is used, the user explodes and orange sparkles appear on the screen.

==Effects==
With Migraine, the user may be able to automatically knock out one of Mario's party. However, the user, in turn, destroys itself.
