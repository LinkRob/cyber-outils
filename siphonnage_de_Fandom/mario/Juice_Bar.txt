The '''Juice Bar''' is a location in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Super Mario RPG]]''.

==Description==
The Juice Bar is an area in [[Tadpole Pond]] where the player can buy stuff. You can also unlock more items by getting stronger membership cards like the [[Alto Card]]. You can get those at Melboy Bay (located on the right path of Tadpole Pond.
