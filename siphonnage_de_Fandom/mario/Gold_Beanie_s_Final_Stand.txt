'''Gold Beanie's Final Stand''' is the seventh level of [[Bowser's Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 30.

==Layout==

The 1st battle contains 2 [[Buzzy Beetle]]s and 6 [[Bandit]]s. The enemy captain is a Bandit, and he knows [[Chance]].

The 2nd battle contains 4 [[Bob-omb]]s and 4 [[Hammer Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Hammer Bro, and he knows [[Deny]].

The 3rd battle contains 4 Buzzy Beetles and 4 Bob-ombs. The enemy captain is a Buzzy Beetle, and he knows [[Rally]] and Deny.

The final battle contains 2 Bob-ombs, 1 Buzzy Beetle, 4 Bandits, and 1 [[Gold Beanie]]. The enemy captain is the Gold Beanie, and he knows Chance.
