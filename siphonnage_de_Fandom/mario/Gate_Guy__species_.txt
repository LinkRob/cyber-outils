'''Gate Guys''' are a species in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Gate Guys are toll gate barriers. The central vertical support structure features an A-frame shape adorned with thick alternating horizontal stripes of bright yellow and dark blue. Attached to this base is a horizontal boom arm that extends outward to the left, pivoting on a dark grey circular joint with a metallic silver center. This horizontal arm is decorated with alternating vertical stripes of vibrant green and dark blue. The far left of the arm features large white cartoonish gloved hand shaped into a fist with a prominent thumb. A Gate Guy's eyes, which are two tall narrow black oval shapes, are on the palm of its glove.

== History ==
Gate Guys appear only in one [[board]] in [[Duel Mode]], the namesake [[Gate Guy]] board. There are only two of them, one positioned at the northern end of the board and the other at the southern end. Both of them block a shortcut and require a character to pay a toll of five [[coin]]s if they wish to pass. The Gate Guys can be used to return to one's player space or go after their opponent.

Both Gate Guys appear to be right hand gloves, although their speech bubble icons depict them both as left hand gloves.
