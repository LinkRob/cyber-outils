'''Raynforst Island''' is a major location from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', being one of the major islands that must be connected to the [[Uni-Tree]]. It is found in the [[Lushgreen Sea]], and is unlocked after rescuing [[Maykit and Billdit|Maykit]] and reconnecting [[Twistee Island]] (after which she sets up her crafting shop on [[Shipshape Island]]), and its own linking causes a development in [[Rumbla Island]] where the [[Guardarm|Guardarms]] cause more trouble as their boss, the [[Gorumbla]], seeks revenge after being starved of attention. Billdit is trapped here.

== History ==
After they reconnect [[Twistee Island]], Maykit sets up a shop on [[Shipshape Island]], crafting some hammers which the Bros. take. Mario and Luigi then head to Raynforst Island, where Billdit is trapped, having gotten separated from his sister and their mother [[Chief Willma]]. When the Bros. reconnect Raynforst Island, Billdit uses the Warp Pipe that spawns close to the landing area to get to Shipshape Island.

The [[Monster Maniac]] can later be found here, siccing some Hydro [[Seedle|Seedles]] whose healing powers have been enhanced.
