The '''Headmaster''' is a character who first appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]] and returns in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. He is the head of the [[Royal Tennis Academy]] and heads the [[Island Open]].
