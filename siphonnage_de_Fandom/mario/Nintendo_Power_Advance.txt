'''''Nintendo Power Advance''''' is a spinoff of the ''[[Nintendo Power]]'' that consists of four issues centered entirely around the [[Game Boy Advance]] and its games. It began in early 2001 and ended in early 2002, with the last issue being a dedicated strategy guide of ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. There is ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related content in every issue, and [[Mario]] appears on all of them except the third issue, which has a panel with [[Wario]] instead.
