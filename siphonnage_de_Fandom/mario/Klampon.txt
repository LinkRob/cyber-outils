'''Klampons''' are an enemy in the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}. They first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''. A Klampon makes its only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' appearance in an issue of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Klampons are a quadrupedal variant of [[Klaptrap]]s with blue scaly skin with red spots. Klampons also have a big head, large jaws, and bulging eyes.

===Behavior===
Like Klaptraps, Klampons clamp their jaws while moving back and forth.
