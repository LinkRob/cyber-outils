A '''Togemaru''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Togemaru is a round creature with a black upper half and a light underbelly. Several spikes stick out of the upper half of a Togemaru's body.

===Behavior===
Togemaru attempt to bounce onto [[Wario]] to damage him. It can also bounce off of the walls, floor, and ceiling to try and interfere with Wario's progress.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
[[File:Togemaru sprite.png|frame|left]]
Togemaru are only encountered in [[Course No.02]]. If Wario either [[jump]]s onto or [[Dash Attack|Body Slams]] a Togemaru, he takes damage because of the Togemaru's spikes. The only way Togemaru can be defeated is if it lands onto Wario while he is using a Body Slam. A Togemaru releases a [[coin]] upon being defeated.
