|effects = Allows Tiny to warp to a different area.}}'''Monkeyport''' ('''''Warpum Craftious''''') is a special ability used by [[Tiny Kong]] in the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. Tiny can use this ability after buying a potion from [[Cranky Kong]] in [[Crystal Caves]] for 7 [[Banana Coin]]s.

==Usage==
To use Monkeyport, the player must locate a pad with Tiny's face on it and press {{button|n64|z}} to have Tiny warp to another location with a Tiny pad. This ability is most notable for being the only way to access the highest part of Crystal Caves to defeat the [[Giant Kosha]] to prevent him from dropping stalagmites throughout the caves. It is also needed to help access the final level in the game: [[Hideout Helm]].
