{{nihongo|'''Backward Somersault'''|バック宙|Bakku chū|Back air}} is a high jump in the {{series|Super Mario}}. It first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|The Game Boy version of Donkey Kong]]''.

==Description==
{{Q|Crouch {where you are} and jump.|''[[Super Mario 64]]'' manual, page 10<ref name="sm64manual">{{cite manual |game=Super Mario 64|page=10 |quote=Crouch {where you are} and jump.}}</ref>}}
Backward somersault made its debut in ''Super Mario 64''. The method benefits the player that cannot reach places with a normal jump. Compared to side somersault, the backward somersault moves slightly backwards while the latter moves further. In most games, the player presses {{button|default|z}}, then {{button|default|a}}.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario 64]]''/''[[Super Mario 64 DS|DS]]''===
The ''Super Mario 64'' instruction booklet calls the move, '''Back somersault'''. Mario can perform a backward somersault according to the ''Super Mario 64'' instruction booklet. The player must crouch with {{button|n64|z}}. While crouching, the player must press {{button|n64|a}}.

===''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''===
Mario can perform the backward somersault in ''Super Mario Galaxy''. The player must press {{button|wii|z}} then {{button|wii|a}} to perform the technique.

===''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''===
Mario can perform the backward somersault in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' by using the method in ''Super Mario Galaxy''.
