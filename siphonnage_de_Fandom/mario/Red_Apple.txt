A '''Red Apple''' is an item in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Effects ==
[[File:Redapplepapermariotree.jpg|thumb|left|Red Apples on a tree]]
Red Apples can be found on a tree in the [[Overthere Stair]]. They can be used to create a [[Sky Juice]]. Eating a Red Apple restores 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] and giving this to [[Princess Peach]] turns her big.
