The '''Snow King''' is the main antagonist of the Adventure mode in ''[[Mario Golf: Super Rush]]''.

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
The Snow King has a body made up of ice. His eyes are black, he has a red glove with a red crystal, a green glove with a green crystal, a crown, a blue cape, and a blue spike.
