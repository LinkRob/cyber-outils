A '''Big Fire Piranha Plant''' is a larger version of the [[Fire Piranha Plant]] found in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. They behave similarly to a standard [[Fire Piranha Plant]] with the major difference being that they can spit larger fireballs. 

==History==
===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
Big Fire Piranha Plants are first seen in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. In this game, it is only found in the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' style replacing the [[Big Venus Fire Trap]]s in that style. They can be made by either shaking a [[Super Piranha Plant]] or by giving a [[Super Mushroom]] to a [[Fire Piranha Plant]]. They shoot larger fireballs than a regular [[Fire Piranha Plant]] however despite their larger size, Big Fire Piranha Plants are no more durable than their smaller counterparts and can be defeated with basically any attack, be it a stomp or the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Bell]], or a [[Starman]].

==Trivia==
*Besides the winged varieties, Big Fire Piranha Plants are the only new [[Piranha Plant]] variety introduced in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.
