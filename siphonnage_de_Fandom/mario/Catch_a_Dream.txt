'''Catch A Dream'''<ref name="SPM tattle">'''''Tippi's'' tattle:''' ''"That's Catch a Dream, the card shop... You can buy and sell Catch Cards there..."'' {{Cite video game|title=[[Super Paper Mario]] |developer=[[Intelligent Systems]], Nintendo SPD Group No.3|publisher=[[Nintendo]]|date=2007-04-09}}</ref> is a [[Catch Card]] shop in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
[[Hoodin]] owns '''Catch A Dream''' in [[Flipside]]. The customer can buy or sell [[Catch Card]]s.<ref name="SPM tattle"/> After the customer buys the Catch Card, it is placed in the [[Card Bag]]. The Card Bag costs ten [[coin]]s each. After the customer sells the Catch Card, [[Hoodin]] judges its rarity. He gives more coins to the customer if he/she gives the rarer card, vice versa.
