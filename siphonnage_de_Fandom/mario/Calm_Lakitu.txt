'''Calm Lakitu''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Calm Lakitus are a variant of [[Lakitu]] imbued with the [[Calm]] [[vibe]], and Calm Lakitus have the same appearance as a Lakitu.

===Behavior===
Like with other Calm enemies, a Calm Lakitu starts off sleeping but as soon as [[Princess Peach|Peach]] approaches a Calm Lakitu, it wakes up. Like with regular Lakitus, a Calm Lakitu attacks by throwing [[Spiny Egg]]s down.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
[[File:Calm Lakitu Beach.png|thumb|left|200px|Peach near a Calm Lakitu]]
Calm Lakitus first appear in the level [[Hoo's Wood 2-7]], and they commonly appear in worlds such as [[Wavy Beach]] and [[Giddy Sky]]. Peach can easily pass a Calm Lakitu by sneaking by it, and a Calm Lakitu can easily be defeated from any attack.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', [[Giga Lakitu]] will fall asleep when [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] leaves the battle arena, similarly to a Calm Lakitu.
