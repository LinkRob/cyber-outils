|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Somnus Waltz''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is only used by [[Puppox]]s.

== Description ==
When Somnus Waltz is used, the Puppox preforms a dance that puts the target to [[sleep]]. This move can be negated by wearing certain accessories that prevents sleep. This move is considered an upgraded version of [[Eerie Jig]].
