'''Zebra Bananza''' is a transformation of [[Donkey Kong]] that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. It is one of the many Bananza forms he can transform into with the help of [[Pauline]] and can be his second or third transformation, being granted to him by the [[Zebra Elder]] in the [[Freezer Layer]] after finding the [[Ancient Record]] for him.

==Physical description==
As Zebra Bananza, Donkey Kong is now a giant zebra who is very muscular. His fur is slightly different from the regular [[Zebra (Donkey Kong Bananza)|Zebra]]s being a darker color with black and tinted stripes. Zebra Bananza also has unique hair with each strand resembles a banana and the colors alternate between yellow and brown. He also wears jewelry around his ankles that resemble a bundle of bananas.

==Powers and abilities==
Zebra Bananza's main power is being able to run at high speeds allowing him to cross over the crumbling snow bridges of the Freezer Layer. This form is also very useful in running across water and catching up to fast enemies (such as the nimble [[Poppy Kong]]). While he is in this form, Donkey Kong cannot be slowed down by mud or fresh snow. His Charge Dash technique is also very useful for getting a quick burst of speed allowing him to plow through enemies and obstacles. Thanks to the hooves that are gained with this form, Donkey Kong is also able to run across slippery terrain such as ice and mud without losing any speed or traction. Upon completing the Zebra Bananza Rehearsal during the post game, Donkey Kong will now be able to preform the Golden Dash to transform the terrain around him into gold after charge dashing through them.
