'''Front Door''' is the final level in [[Valley of Bowser]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. On the other side, the [[Back Door]] is also the final level and they are both inside [[Bowser's Castle]]. 

==Walkthrough==
===First Section===

The first part of the level leads to a bright hallway. And there are four areas (numbered 1 through 4). 

The paths vary with what door was selected: 

====Door 1 ====
An auto scrolling level with wooden smashers similar to [[Iggy's Castle]].
====Door 2====
A climbing fence with climbing koopas with spikes below.
====Door 3 ====
A maze with [[Mechakoopa]]s.
==== Door 4====
This room has [[Hothead]]s and [[Li'l Sparky]]s.

===Second Section===

The second part leads to a second bright hallway. And there is another four areas (this time numbered 5 through 8). 

The paths again vary with what door is selected: 

====Door 5====
A long stretch of spiked pillars and [[Thwomp]]s. The player must sprint as soon as possible to avoid being crushed.
====Door 6====
This is underwater with [[Fish Bone]]s, [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s and [[Dry Bones]] along with spikes.
====Door 7====
Contains Bowser Statues. Some Gold and some silver. The silver ones shoot fire; the golden ones jump around.
====Door 8====
This room is filled with [[Chargin' Chuck]]s.

===Back Room===
After going through one of the four rooms the player is in the [[Back Door]]. The Back Door can be directly accessed with a secret exit, skipping the eight doors. The back door room is dark and has [[Ninji|Ninjis]]. A [[Red ? Block]] in the middle will trigger a disco ball, which slightly illuminates the room.

==[[Bowser|Battle]]==
After entering the boss door, the player will be faced with the final boss, [[Bowser]]. 

=== Part 1 ===
He floats around the arena in his Koopa Clown Car, occasionally stopping to drop Mechakoopas. To hurt Bowser, you must stun the Mechakoopas and throw them at Bowser, stunning and hurting him. If you miss and hit the propeller on the Clown Car (which also hurts you), it will disappear, but he will throw more for you. There will never be more than two Mechakoopas on the screen at one time; if there is one, he will only throw one, and he will not throw them at all if there's still two.

After the second hit, Princess Toadstool pops out of the [[Koopa Clown]] screaming for help and throws a Mushroom. (She does this again after the fourth hit as well.)

After being hit twice, Bowser leaves the area and drops fire onto the area. It can be avoided by standing between the flames or spin-jumping off of them. Bowser will then return, and the second part of the fight begins.

=== Part 2 ===
Instead of simply floating back and forth, Bowser will now hover directly above the player. He will occasionally stop to drop [[Big Steely|Big Steelies]]. After two Big Steelies, he will launch Mechakoopas again. Hitting him is trickier due to his hovering above you.

After being hit two more times, he will leave and launch flames again before coming back for the final part of the fight.

=== Part 3 ===
Bowser is furious now. He attacks the player in a much more direct manner, attempting to stomp them with the Clown Car. Kick the Mechakoopas into him when he launched him. 

After two more hits, the clown car will malfunction, turning upside down and releasing the princess before floating off into oblivion, spinning out of control all the while. You have now defeated Bowser and completed the game - congratulations!
