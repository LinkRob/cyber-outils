'''Cosmic Clone Wall Jumpers''' is the second [[mission]] of the [[Flip-Out Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It can only be played if the [[Comet Medal]] is collected in the previous mission, [[Wicked Wall Jumps]], and while a [[Prankster Comet]] is in orbit.
