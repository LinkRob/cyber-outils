A '''Rhythm minigame''' is a type of minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. Four players compete against each other in Rhythm minigames, and the player earns points by following the beat. Rhythm minigames' openings start with a metronome. The players can wave in order to play an easter egg after the minigames if in a game that you can play random minigames. CPU players will not activate the easter egg though. These minigames are not available when the player plays online.
