The '''Sports Coupe ('''known as''' Sports Coupé '''in Europe and Australia''')''' is an unlockable [[kart]] body in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. It resembles a yellow sports car. Unlike most vehicles, the Sports Coupe has its exhaust pipes in the back of its trunk.
