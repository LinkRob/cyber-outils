'''Dire-POW Badge''' is a type of [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can either be bought from [[Gritzy Caves]] for 620 [[coin]]s or found within a [[? Block]] in [[Toad Town]]. If [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], or [[Baby Luigi]] are equipped with the Dire-POW Badge, their [[Attack]] power grows to 250% if their [[HP]] falls below 25%.
