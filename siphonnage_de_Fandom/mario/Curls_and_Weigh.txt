It comes from [[Wario Weigh-In]] from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!|Twisted!]]'' and shares its gameplay with the latter.

==Gameplay==
The player has to exercise to lose weight. The higher the difficulty, the more stringent the requirements. Twisting is required to lose weight. The character who appears is a man who has yet to be identified.

==Rules==
''"Stand on the scale and pump those muscles! But don't overdo it, or you might just find yourself in worse shape than when you started.''

==Trivia==
*The name of the microgame comes from "curds and whey," which refers to the byproducts of making cheese.
