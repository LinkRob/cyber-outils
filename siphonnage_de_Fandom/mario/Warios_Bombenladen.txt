'''Warios Bombenladen''' is a location in the ''[[Club Nintendo (German magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' comic "[[Warios Weihnachtsmärchen]]."

==Description==
The location is owned by [[Wario]], as is shown from a yellow "W" on the front of the building. The only other employee is [[Bomberman]], who works as the clerk. The location sells [[bomb]]s of various types and has had customers such as [[Kid Klown]], [[Bowser]], and a [[Kremling]].

==In "Warios Weihnachtsmärchen"==
At one point, when Bomberman requests to have off time for [[Christmas]], he is fired by Wario. By the end of the story, after Wario has been visited by the Ghosts of Past, Present, and Future, the first thing that he does to make amends is go to Warios Bombenladen, where Bomberman is, and rehires him.
