'''Strobombs''', originally named '''Gold Robombs''', are an enemy species in the {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}. They first appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'', as an enemy, and return in ''[[Luigi's Mansion Arcade]]'' as an item.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Strobombs are a golden variant of [[Robomb]]s.

===Behavior===
In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'', Strobombs function like malfunctioning bombs, except they run much quicker and explode in contact with furniture. Like with other golden enemy variants, a Strobomb releases [[treasure]] when stunned by [[Luigi]]'s [[flashlight]], more specifically 25 [[bill]]s or five [[Gold Bar]]s.

==In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}==
===''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''===
In the [[ScareScraper]], they are occasionally released from inspecting furniture or using the [[Dark-Light Device]] on [[painting]]s.

===''Luigi's Mansion Arcade''===
Strobombs are items that can be used to stun all [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] onscreen. Each player gets three Strobombs to use at the start of each level,

==Game data==
*'''''Luigi's Mansion Arcade'' description:''' ''If you run into a lot of ghosts up ahead, don't be afraid to use your Strobombs!''
