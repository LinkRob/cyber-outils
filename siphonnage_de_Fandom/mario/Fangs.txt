|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Fangs''' is a special, physical move used only by [[K-9]]s in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
With Fangs, the K-9 using targets one of [[Mario]]'s party dealing a little less than twice the normal damage.

==Trivia==
*[[Claw]] can be considered Fangs' counterpart as it's also a physical move used by the K-9's stronger counterpart [[Chow]].
