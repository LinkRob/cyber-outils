'''Elite Octoombas''' are green [[Octoomba]]s with two antenna and yellow spots. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. Unlike other Octoombas, Elite Octoombas can shoot two rocks without stopping. There are many Elite Octoombas in the [[Shiverburn Galaxy]] in [[World 5 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|World 5]].
