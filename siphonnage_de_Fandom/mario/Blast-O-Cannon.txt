'''Blast-O-Cannons''' are an object in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Description==
A Blast-O-Cannon can blast [[Wario]] in a certain direction, allowing him to break through hard [[block]]s along the path. Blast-O-Cannons on the ground can be entered like a [[Warp Pipe|pipe]], but a Blast-O-Cannon on a wall or ceiling can only be entered by shooting into it from another Blast-O-Cannon. Some Blast-O-Cannons are fixed, while others move on tracks from side to side. After being blasted, Wario bounces off surfaces encountered at sharp angles until he hits a surface perpendicular to his line of movement.

While Wario is inside a Blast-O-Cannon, the player can tilt the [[Wii Remote]] to adjust its angle, hold {{button|wii|2}} to charge the Blast-O-Cannon, and release the button to shoot. The player can press {{button|wii|1}} to have Wario exit a Blast-O-Cannon without being fired.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Blast-O-Cannons are common in desert-themed levels. They first appear in [[Whoopsy Desert]]. In certain sections, Wario has to launch between multiple Blast-O-Cannons to climb a long tunnel and reach the top.
