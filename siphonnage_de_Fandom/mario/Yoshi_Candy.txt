A '''Yoshi Candy''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Using a Yoshi Candy on a character restores 100 of their [[Heart Point|HP]]. To obtain a Yoshi Candy, a character must first use a [[Yoshi Cookie]] to get a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] to eat an enemy. If he fails to do so, this could instead result in a Yoshi Candy.
