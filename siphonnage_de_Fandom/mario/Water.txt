'''Water''' is a recurring object in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media. It first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|Donkey Kong Jr.]]''.

==Description==
Water is a liquid inhabited by aquatic creatures in the real world and ''Mario''-related media alike. It varies between light blue, dark blue, or clear in color. Vast amounts of water can generally be [[swim|swum]] through by [[Mario]] and other characters, with certain [[level]]s being dedicated to swimming underwater, but certain games such as ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' features water as a hazard which has to be avoided. Certain 3D {{series|Super Mario}} installments only allow for the player's character to swim through water for a short amount of time, as when the character's air meter is depleted, they drown.
