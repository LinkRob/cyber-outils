Toads are a major species that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]] and its spin-offs. It is a They are peaceful and ruled by [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]].

== Appearance ==
Toads are mushroom like creatures that have white mushroom caps on their heads. They wear diaper-like bottoms, a vest the color of the spots on their heads, and brown shoes.

== History ==
=== Past ===
At long time ago, a young girl named, [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]] came through a [[Mysterious Pipe]]. Although the Toads don't know where she came from, and neither doesn she, they adopted and raised her in the Mushroom Kingdom like one of their own, and as time passes on, they eventually made her their [[:Category:Royalty|princess]].

=== ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' ===
All the Toads in [[Toad Town (Illumination)|their town]], at [[Mushroom Heights (Illumination)|the Heights]] are busstling with fun and buisness, although they have no idea, who [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] is and where he came from, since [[Toad (Illumination)|one of them]] was bringing him to Peach's castle, when he found out that his brother [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] is lost in the [[Dark Lands (Illumination)|Dark Lands]]. After Peach decided to Take Mario with her to the [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]], she reminded to Toads that everyone will stop Bowser at all costs, with the Kong Army's help. But when she returned with the army captured, they were forced to evacuate. With the Mushroom Kingdom and [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]], [[New York City (Illumination)|New York City]] both saved, the Toads returned home, and built a [[Mario Bros. House (Illumination)|new house for the Super Mario Brothers]].
