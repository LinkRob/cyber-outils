'''Origami Black Shy Guys''' are a type of [[Shy Guy]] which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. Here, they are enemies in the [[Temple of Shrooms]]. A Black Shy Guy sometimes comes out from behind certain pillars in the walls and attacks Mario with a paper fan, dealing a single point of damage to him. Mario can First Strike the enemy to defeat it, but by not doing so, it cannot be refought from there. Several Black Shy Guys are later faced in combat.

As of version 1.0.1, Black Shy Guys can also be fought in [[Scorching Sandpaper West]].
