'''''Super Mario 3D World Power-Up Quiz: Throw Fireballs''''' is a skill quiz featured on the [[Play Nintendo]] site. It features eight basic questions about power-ups in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'', and the quiz was created to promote said game.

==Questions==
{{Play Nintendo quiz/Super Mario 3D World Power-Up Quiz: Throw Fireballs}}

==Results==
*Getting 0–2 questions correct: '''You got POWER-UP PUPIL'''
*Getting 3–4 questions correct: '''You got POWER-UP PRO'''
*Getting 5–8 questions correct: '''You got POWER-UP POWERHOUSE'''

"''You'll need plenty of special items to power Mario and his friends through the [https://supermario3dworld.nintendo.com/ Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury] game for the Nintendo Switch system. From the classic Super Mushroom power-up in the Super Mario 3D World adventure to the mighty Giga Bell power-up in the Bowser's Fury adventure, there are a ton of power-ups for you to explore.''"
