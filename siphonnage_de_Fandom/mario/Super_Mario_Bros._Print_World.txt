'''''Super Mario Bros. Print World''''' is a productivity computer software released in 1991. It allows the user to design their own stationery, such as calendars and banners, using ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters. A lot of the graphics in particular are characters, enemies, and items from the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]], and some are from ''[[Dr. Mario (video game)|Dr. Mario]]''.
