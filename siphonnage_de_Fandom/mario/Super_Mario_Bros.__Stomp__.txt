'''Super Mario Bros.''' is one of [[9-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', appearing in the [[Retro Action]] stage. The microgame is based on and reuses assets from the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[Super Mario Bros.|same title]]. Another microgame titled [[Super Mario Bros. (Grab coins!)|Super Mario Bros.]] also appears in ''WarioWare: Touched!''

==Gameplay==
The player has to defeat every enemy before time runs out. [[Goomba]]s need to be stomped only once, although enemies such as [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Buzzy Beetle]]s need to be tapped once for them to retreat into their shells, and once more for them to slide across the ground. [[Spiny|Spinies]] cannot be defeated from being tapped, and only from being hit by either a [[Koopa Shell]] or a [[Buzzy Shell]]. Once every enemy is defeated, the player wins, but if not all enemies are defeated on time, they lose.

Super Mario Bros. is among the double-length microgames, having a length of eight seconds, to give the player additional time to remove the enemies.

[[Mario]] also slides in from the left when the microgame is cleared. On level 1, he is small and riding on a cloud. Level 2 has [[Super Mario (form)|Super Mario]] on a cloud. Level 3 has a giant Mario riding on a [[Bullet Bill]].

Levels 1, 2, and 3 involve the player having to poke three, four, and five or more enemies, respectively.
