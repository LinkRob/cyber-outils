An '''Energizer''' is an [[item]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Drinking an Energizer increases the attack of all party members by 100%. This item is very useful for boss fights and if [[Bowser]] in an active party member.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
The Energizer can be purchased only at the [[Frog Coin Emporium]] in [[Tadpole Pond]] for two [[Frog Coin]]s.
