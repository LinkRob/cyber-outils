==Appearance==
Ledger is a [[Flip-Flopian]] that is swampish in color. He also wears glasses and a business suit.

==History==
Ledger lives in [[Flopside]] where he is a very successful magazine editor. He often brags about how successful his articles are. It is also noted that he married his boss' daughter.
