Ghostzilla makes his ''[[Luigi's Mansion (series)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' series debut as a boss in Luigi's Mansion 3, being located on the eighth floor, [[Paranormal Productions]].

== Profile ==
Ghostzilla is a [[Goob]] that is in a Monster suit based on the Monster Godzilla. The suit has dark green, scaly, skin. His horns are long and curve forward. His nature as a ghost prevents him from having legs. However, he can still fly about.

== Appearances ==
===''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''===
Ghostzilla makes his first appearance when [[Director Morty]] summons him to fight [[Luigi]] as part of a movie he is making. If Luigi wins, Morty will surrender the button he has in his possession. When he makes his entrance, Ghostzilla starts stomping around before roaring.

====Combat====
Ghostzilla attacks by breathing fireballs from his mouth. The fireballs vary in their size.  The red ones cannot be pushed back, but can be avoided due to their relatively slow speed.  The Blue fireball can be pushed back by the [[Poltergust G-00]], causing it to hit Ghostzilla. After being hit once, he will spit out green fireballs. These ones are more powerful, requiring Luigi and [[Gooigi]] to both push it at the same time. After being hit by the fireball a second time, Ghostzilla will spit out a bluish green fireball. This is the most powerful one, and will require the player to rapidly press the A button in order for Luigi and Gooigi to push it back. Once his fireballs hit his face three times, the suit get burned off a the [[Goob]] is exposed. In this form, he is vulnerable to being sucked up (He can't be captured while in the suit.) Once his 100 hit point stamina is drained, the boss is captured and Morty, true to his word, gives Luigi an elevator button.
