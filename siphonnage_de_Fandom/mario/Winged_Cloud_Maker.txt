The '''Winged Cloud Maker''' is a [[Special Item]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. Using one turns each enemy onscreen into a [[Winged Cloud]].
