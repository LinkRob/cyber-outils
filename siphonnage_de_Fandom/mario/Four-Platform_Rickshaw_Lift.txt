'''Four-Platform Rickshaw Lifts''' are a type of platform found in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', they are a large elevation platform that constantly spins around slowly (either in a clockwise or counterclockwise direction). They are commonly used to reach higher platforms or ledges, they can also whisk a walking [[Dry Bones]] on board. Four-Platform Rickshaw Lifts are very rare only appearing in [[World 2-Tower (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-Tower]] with only five appearing in the whole game.
