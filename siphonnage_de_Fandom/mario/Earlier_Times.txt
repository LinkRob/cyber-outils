'''Earlier Times''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Earlier Times is a very rare item that has the unique ability to restart the battle from the beginning. It can only be bought from [[Frogfucius' Student]] in [[Seaside Town]] for 15 [[Frog Coin]]s.
