'''Merloo''' is one of the Four Sages in the [[Tribe of Ancients]]. The other three sages are [[Merlight]], [[Merlimbis]], and [[Merlumina]]. Not much is known about Merloo because you do not see the character in-game. There is a stain-glass picture of the four sages in the [[Mirror Hall]] connecting [[Flipside]] and [[Flopside]]. [[Tippi]] or [[Tiptron]]'s tattle reveals that Merloo was a very wise man. Merloo is thought to have written the [[Light Prognosticus]] long ago before the Tribe of Ancients collapsed. Merloo is not proven to be distantly related to anyone in either Flipside or Flopside.

Merloo is also said by Tippi and Tiptron that he is a powerful, dimension-controlling magician. Because [[Dimentio]] is also a powerful, dimension-controlling magician, some believe that Merloo and Dimentio might be related.
