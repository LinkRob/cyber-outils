The '''Courage Shell''' is a type of [[armor]] usable by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It resembles Bowser's regular shell but appears to be more reflective. The Courage Shell increases Bowser's [[Defense]] by 12 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 6. The Courage Shell can be bought from the shop in [[Monstro Town]] for 60 [[coin]]s.
