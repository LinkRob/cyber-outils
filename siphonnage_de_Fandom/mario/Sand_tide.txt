'''Sand tides''', also referred to as '''sandbars''', are a type of hazard in the ''Super Mario'' franchise that moves up and down.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario World''===
Sand tides first appeared in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', debut in [[Donut Plains 2]] and [[Valley of Bowser 2]]. They are sandy yellow platforms that and be of different sizes.

===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', they reappear in [[Dusty Dune Galaxy]]. In the Pyramid Planet, they reappear in the form of walls that either move up or down when they are activated. Once all five [[Silver Star]]s are recollected, they will sink and disappear. On the Sand Tide Plane, it moves up and down and have [[stump]]s, [[note]]s, and [[coin]]s unseen in it.
