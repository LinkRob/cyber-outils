'''Mechakoopa Stomp''', originally titled '''Bowser Crush''', is a special attack used by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|remake]].

==Description==
When Mechakoopa Stomp is used, a giant [[Mechakoopa]] hops through the screen, and Bowser can use it attack every enemy on the screen for high damage. Although Mechakoopa Stomp can be paired with [[Psych Bomb]] or [[Geno Flash]], Mechakoopa Stomp is considered a big deadly attack to enemies with low [[HP]], and it is also considered a stronger version of [[Crusher]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Bowser learns Mechakoopa Stomp once he reaches [[level]] 18. The attack requires 16 [[Flower Point]]s to use.
