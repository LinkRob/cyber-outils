'''Phil''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. He is a [[Bob-omb]] apart of the [[Bob-omb Blitz]] group. His name is only revealed when [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] talk to them before [[Bowser]] frees the group.
