'''''"Super Mario RPG" Original Sound Version''''' is an official two-disc soundtrack release of ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It was released exclusively in Japan on March 8, 1996. The soundtrack was composed by [[Yoko Shimomura]].
