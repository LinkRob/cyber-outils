'''The Maze That Pays''' is a [[microgame]] featured under [[Wario]]'s [[Intro Games]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place in a maze with some [[coin]]s in it. There are several intersecting horizontal and vertical paths. Paths that exceed the edge of the screen connect to the opposite side.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of [[Wario]], who has to collect every coin in the maze, in a somewhat similar manner to the ''{{w|Pac-Man|PAC-MAN}}'' games. Coins appear in groups of either two on a vertical path or four on a horizontal path, although this varies each time the microgame is played. If Wario collects every coin in the maze before the timer expires, the player wins at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level adds another group of coins and changes the color of the walls, the latter of which has no effect on gameplay.

*'''Level 1:''' There is only one group of coins, and the walls are green.
*'''Level 2:''' There are two groups of coins, and the walls are blue.
*'''Level 3:''' There are three groups of coins, and the walls are red.
