'''Michael Williams Stark''' (born April 6, 1955) is a Canadian voice actor. He provided the voice for [[Ludwig von Koopa|Kooky von Koopa]] in both ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and the ''[[Super Mario World (television series)|Super Mario World]]'' cartoon.
