The '''Sugarscoot''', also known as the '''Bon Bon''' in the European version, is a medium bike in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. It is available from the start and is the staff ghost bike for [[Coconut Mall]] ([[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], Nin*SiMO). It is the slowest and lightest medium vehicle, but has great [[Acceleration]], decent [[Off-Road]], good [[Mini-Turbo]], and the best [[Handling]] of all the medium vehicles.
