'''Denden''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Denden are pig-like creatures that wear a snail-like shell with a few large spikes on it.

===Behavior===
When a Denden lands onto the ground, it begins to crawl from side to side. If [[Wario]] is near a Denden, it turns to him, curls up into its shell and rolls toward Wario, but disappears if it hits a wall.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
Denden are only encountered in [[Doodle Woods]], where they are creations brought to life by [[Hoggus]], along with [[Butatabi]]. While a Denden is rolling, Wario can perform a [[Ground Pound (move)|ground pound]] nearby to have the Denden roll into a different direction. Wario can defeat a Denden by performing a [[Dash Attack]] on the front, although if Wario touches the spikes on a Denden's side, he takes damage. Wario can alternatively defeat a Denden by first flipping it upside down from either a nearby ground pound or by hitting a platform it is on from below. In doing so, Wario can then ground pound the upside-down Denden to defeat it. Dendens can respawn indefinitely, and are one of the enemies to not yield any [[Coin]]s upon being defeated.
