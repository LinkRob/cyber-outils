'''Smokey the Dragon''' is a winged fire dragon. He is the fourth boss found in [[Diddy Kong Racing]] and [[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]. He resides in [[Dragon Forest]]. [[Wizpig]] brainwashed him and gave him the final piece of the [[Wizpig Amulet]].

After the player completes all of the races in Dragon Forest, Smokey challenges the player to a race through grasslands for half of the time and in caves the other half. Smokey also sets puffs of fire behind him as an attack while attempting to beat the player while making it more challenging for the player to beat them. When he is beaten, he gives the player the final piece of the [[Wizpig Amulet]] and opens the Silver Balloon challenges for the player to do.
