The '''Koopa Pilaf''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It takes the appearance of a [[Koopa Shell]] with three different foods inside. Made by mixing a [[Turtley Leaf]] with a [[Horsetail]], this food temporarily halves damage taken from enemies.
