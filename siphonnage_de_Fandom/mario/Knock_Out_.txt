'''Knock Out!''' is an unused enemy spell hidden in the code of ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Its sprite and sound effects are the same as [[Drain]], an attack that every type of [[Shy Guy]] can do. The only difference is that this is an [[Instant Death Attack]] as it does 9,999 damage to all party mambers.
