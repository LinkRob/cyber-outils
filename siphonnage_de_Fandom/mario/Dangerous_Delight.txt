}}'''Dangerous Delight''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Saffron]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
It is made from a [[Poison Mushroom|Poison Shroom]]. This recipe should not be eaten, as it takes Mario's abilities away. Mario cannot flip, has low jumping, slow running, and reverse movements.
