'''Rosalina's mother''' is a character who first appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Rosalina's mother has a very similar appearance to [[Princess Peach]], she has peach-colored defined lips, and long strawberry-blonde hair that reaches to her waist.

Her primary outfit consists of a turquoise long-sleeved gown that bares her shoulders, similar to her daughter's, along with sapphire blue pearl-shaped earrings.

==History==
in Chapter 4 of [[Rosalina's Storybook]], after young [[Rosalina]] has a dream that is also an omen about her losing her mother back on Earth.
