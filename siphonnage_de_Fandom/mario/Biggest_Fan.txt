'''Biggest Fan''' is the boss [[microgame]] of [[Wario]]'s stage, [[Mysterious Form Baton Found!]], in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. It is the first boss microgame of the game overall.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place on top of a mountain, with a few more cliffs being visible in the background. The sky is mostly cloudy, but a little bit of blue sky is visible in the upper portion. The fan used in the microgame has a bird on it, but in the Japanese version, the fan has the kanji「祭」(''matsuri'') printed on it, which means "festival."

==Gameplay==
The player has to rapidly shake the [[Wii Remote]] as a fan to blow away miniature [[Wario]]s and later Wario himself piloting a giant Wario robot named [[Robo-Wario]]. The player wins if they manage to blow everything away. If the fan is hit by any of the Miniature Warios or Robo-Wario, the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
In later levels, Robo-Wario defends itself from the wind created by the fan, and the player must wait for Robo-Wario to lift its arm to strike the fan to blow it away.

*'''Level 1''': There is only one group of miniature Warios and a black Robo-Wario that has to be blown off once to fall.
*'''Level 2''': There are two groups of miniature Warios and a yellow Robo-Wario that can protect itself from the fan and also has to be blown twice to fall.
*'''Level 3''': There are three groups of miniature Warios and a red Robo-Wario, also capable of protecting itself from the fan and also has to be blown thrice to fall. The level has another losing criteria where if the red Robo-Wario reflects too many wind gusts, the player loses.
