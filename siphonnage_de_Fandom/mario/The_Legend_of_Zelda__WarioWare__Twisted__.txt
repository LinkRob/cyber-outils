'''The Legend of Zelda''' is one of [[9-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''. It is based on the game of the [[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|same name]], although the overhead display and graphics differ from the actual game itself.

==Gameplay==
[[Link]] has to use his [[Shield (The Legend of Zelda)|Shield]] to deflect the rocks spit at him by [[Octorok]]s. In Level 1, Link has to deflect attacks from two Octoroks, and in Level 2 he has to deflect attacks from three of them. In Level 3, Link is surrounded by several Octoroks, although only a few of them spit rocks at him.
