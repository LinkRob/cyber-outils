'''Vancouver Velocity 1''' is a race track from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is the first of the three layouts for [[Vancouver Velocity]] and is the first layout of the course, making its debut in the Vancouver Tour.

== Layout ==
Vancouver Velocity 1's route focuses on the forest to the south of the city, where the Capilano Suspension Bridge Park is located. Facing east at the [[Finish line|starting line]], the racers start on a street next to the Vancouver Convention Centre, with the Vancouver Olympics Caudron up ahead on the right. They take a ninety-degree right turn that goes beneath it and enter the Capilano Suspension Bridge Park with a little concessions building on the left. They drive onto the Capilano Suspension Bridge, where numerous [[Swooper]]s can be seen. After the racers make a sharp right turn onto the bridge, they must make an S-curve to get through the forest, after which they find a [[Glide Ramp]], which they must use to [[Glider|fly over the water]] back to the mainland. Upon making their way back to the mainland, the racers must take a right turn that leads to the starting line. There is a small shortcut in the form of a small corner with some decorations; the grass will slow racers down, but a [[Dash Mushroom]] can allow them to get through unimpeded.

In the [[Nintendo Switch]] version of ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', this is the first lap of [[Vancouver Velocity]]. The suspension bridge is in anti-gravity, meaning that collisions between racers will give boost. After the completion of the first lap, the racers will be redirected onto [[Vancouver Velocity 2]].
