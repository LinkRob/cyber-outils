'''POW Space''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Description==
The POW Space is a [[space]] with the appearance in [[POW Block]].

==In ''Mario Party 9''==
POW Spaces only appear in the [[minigame]] mode [[High Rollers]]. It becomes is only available to use after the first turn of the mode. If a character rolls over the POW Space, the number of [[point]]s on all of the number spaces decrease by 10. If the POW Space is used when 0-point spaces are present, they change back into 30-point spaces. The POW Space can be useful for characters if there is a shortage of spaces worth a lot of points. Once the POW Space is used, it cannot be used again until the end of the turn, before the next minigame, when it reactivates. Additionally, after a character gets knocked off the board by an opponent's move item rush attack and when it is that character's turn again, they start their turn on the POW Space and automatically activate it.
