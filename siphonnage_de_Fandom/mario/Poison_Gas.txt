|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Poison Gas''' is a move used by [[Bowser]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Poison Gas is used, a green Mokura appears on the screen and spreads a large green smoke over the enemies.

==Effects==
With Poison Gas, Bowser is able to [[Poison (ailment)|poison]] enemies on the screen. However, this move doesn't work on all bosses and is useless against enemies that are of the poison element such as [[Alley Rat]]s.

==Trivia==
*This is the first poison move that Bowser uses in the whole entire series. The next will be his poison bites and poison claws from ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
