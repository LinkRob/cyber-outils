'''Mario Kart Slot Cars''' is a series of toy cars based on ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

==Description==
It comes with two cars, one of which is always [[Mario]] on his [[Standard MR]] and the other being either [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], or [[Wario]] on their respective [[Standard Kart]]s. Each vehicle has a personalized controller, and the track is about nine feet long. Overall, the game is similar to {{w|Scalextric}}.
