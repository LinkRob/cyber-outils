{{Nihongo|'''Captain Goomba'''|クリボー隊長|Kuribō Taichō|Captain Goomba}} is the protagonist in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]''. Before gaining his title, he was called '''Goomba'''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
'''Captain Goomba''', with the name '''Goomba''', is a regular [[Goomba]] identical to other Goombas. While recruiting the crew, Goomba had a new appearance with a red flag on his head. The red flag has a yellow outline, yellow ornament and a metal pole connected to a yellow collar. The collar sticks onto Goomba's head.

=== Personality traits ===
Captain Goomba is an enthusiastic and determined leader. At the start of ''Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser'', its fellow minions mention to Captain Goomba with the "aura of stupidity". Throughout the story, Captain Goomba gained trust from Bowser's minions. After rescuing Bowser, [[Ludwig von Koopa]] gained trust from Captain Goomba.

=== Powers and abilities ===
==== Normal abilities ====
* '''Charge''': As a Melee troop, Captain Goomba charges into his foes.
* '''Rocket Headbutt''': His Special Skill allows him to jump up and land head first onto an enemy to deal great damage to them.
* '''Leadership''': Throughout the entirety of the game, Captain Goomba's leadership has been demonstrated and tested amongst his fellow companions. Usually, whenever Captain Goomba sets a plan in motion, his other captains, like [[Captain Shy Guy (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Shy Guy]] and [[Captain Boo]] either don't take him seriously or believe his idea will only get them into unnecessary trouble. Nevertheless, Captain Goomba has always had a focused mindset on rescuing his leader Bowser, which further drives his motivation and leadership skills. Towards the quest at [[Bowser's Castle]], it seems that Captain Goomba's leadership skills have evolved to the point of where he has gained the respect of not only the minions of Bowser's army, but also the higher-ranking generals such as the [[Elite Trio]], the [[Koopalings]], and even by Bowser himself. However, it seems that, as of his demotion towards the end of ''Minion Quest'' and subsequent moments featuring the four captains (plus [[Captain Koopa Troopa]]) in ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'', Captain Goomba's leadership skills have seemingly faltered due to his anxiety of upsetting Bowser again. His leadership and courage however, have not gone unnoticed, as proven by characters like [[Morton Koopa Jr.]], the other three minion captains, and Ludwig von Koopa.

==== Captain commands ====
* '''[[Deny]]''': Like with all captains in the game, Captain Goomba can deny an enemy of using their Special Skill, which takes up 1 CP.
* '''[[Chance]]''': Captain Goomba can hit a Block which causes a random event to happen, such as status increase for his team, or status decrease for the enemies' team, etc. Takes 3 CP to use.
* '''[[Rally]]''': Captain Goomba can rally up his troops to temporarily raise their SPIRIT stats and allow them to excute their Special Skills more frequently. Costs 4 CP to use.
* '''[[Clash of Captains]]''': Exclusively to ally captains such as Captain Goomba, he can challenge the enemy captain to a one-on-one showdown. He'll take damage from any incoming enemies as he charges through, but gains a fiery aura that allows him to perform his Rocket Headbutt move whenever he gets to attack. Some other enemy Melee captains use this ability as well.
