|effects = Used to supply the [[Banana Fairy's Camera]].}}'''Banana Camera Film''' is an item that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

== Description ==
Banana Camera Film can be found throughout levels and can be used to help supply the [[Banana Fairy's Camera]] that is used to capture [[Banana Fairy|Banana Fairies]]. Whenever a Kong successfully captures a Banana Fairy, the [[Banana Fairy Princess]] appears and will restore and raise the supply of almost all of their items with the film being one of them.
