|first = ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' (2017)}}'''Steam Gardeners''' are the natives of the Wooded Kingdom introduced in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. They are the Machine Gardeners who live in the [[Steam Gardens|Garden area]] of the Wooded Kingdom. They have inhabited the Wooded Kingdom since time immemorial and always have gardening on their minds.

Steam Gardeners need power to continue their work. This is shown as several steam gardeners are lined up at a charging station. One is already at the station, recharging his power, while the one at the back of the line (who waited too long) has passed out since he ran out of energy. One of the silver steam gardeners look silver and contain some seeds. One can be used to get to the obsevation deck, another for a climbable zone to reach somewhere higher, and the last one is in the [[Deep Woods]] used to escape the Deep Woods.
