'''Iwao''' (岩男) is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Behavior==
These tough, pudgy creatures attack by throwing large rocks at [[Wario]]. Iwaos are also invulnerable to most of Wario's attacks, and they can only be defeated by [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]] onto them.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
Iwaos only appear in [[Fiery Cavern]]. Here, when Wario jumps on the [[Switch (Wario Land 4)|switch]] in the level and the temperature radically changes, the Iwaos become [[Yeti]]s which implies that these enemies change with the climate.
