'''Goronto''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Goronto are a variant of an [[ant]] that wear a miner's hat and carry a shovel around with them.

===Behavior===
It attacks by digging up rocks and rolling them at Mario, very similar to the [[Chargin' Chuck|Diggin' Chucks]] from ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
Goronto are only encountered in the [[level]] [[The Ant Monsters]]. [[Mario]] can defeat a Goronto by either [[stomp]]ing it or by throwing a [[fireball]] at it as [[Fire Mario]].
