The '''Mellow Kitty''' is an enemy in [[Wario: Master of Disguise|''Wario: Master of Disguise'']].

==History==
The Mellow Kitty is a rather odd cat-like enemy that is shaped like a bubble. There is only one Mellow Kitty in the entire game and it is found in a room in [[Carpaccio's Lab]]. It attacks by consuming [[Wario]] and, the longer he is inside it, the more the levels of his disguises drop. However, the Mellow Kitty cannot harm Wario in any way making this more of an inconvenience. Any non-physical attack can defeat it such as [[Cosmic Wario]]'s lasers or [[Sparky Wario]]'s electric balls.

==Trivia==
*It's name is a pun of "Hello Kitty" who is a popular character in animation.
