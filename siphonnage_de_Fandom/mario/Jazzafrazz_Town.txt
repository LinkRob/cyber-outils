|first       = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Jazzafrazz Town''' is a mentioned-only location in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
It's one of the places [[Luigi]] visited on [[Super Luigi|his quest]] for [[Princess Eclair]] during the events of ''Paper Mario:The Thousand Year Door''. Luigi describes this place as hip and full of [[Crazee Dayzee|Dayzees]]. This is where Luigi met [[Hayzee]], his fourth partner, a Crayzee Dayzee with green petals. Jazzafrazz town's major event, it seems, is the Drama Slam competition held annually, the prize being a [[Marvelous Compass]] piece.
