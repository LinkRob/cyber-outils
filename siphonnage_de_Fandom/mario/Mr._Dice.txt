==History==
A couple of Mr. Dices can be found in the Testing Room of Frantic Factory and are usually seen running around alongside [[Sir Domino]]s. When they spot a Kong, they will run quickly toward the Kong attempting to damage them on contact. However, Mr. Dice are very weak enemies and can be taken out with any attack. Mr. Dices can also be fought in the R&D Room with [[Chunky Kong]] and one eventually forms together with other toys to form the [[Toy Monster]].

==Trivia==
*It is assumed that Mr. Dice works with [[King K. Rool]] since they are found in Frantic Factory and that some are used for the [[Toy Monster]] that was guarding a [[Golden Banana]].
