'''Dorguy the Second''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
Dorguy the Second appears in Chapter 7-2 during the hero's trek through [[Underwhere Road]]. He is the second of the three Dorguys working under [[Queen Jaydes]]. The heroes must answer his questions that require memory to do so. Answering them correctly will allow Dorguy the Second to let the heroes pass.

== Trivia ==
*Dorguy the Second's questions are a reference to the game ''Brain Age'' where even Dorguy the Second makes a reference to the game himself.
