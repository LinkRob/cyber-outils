'''Yellow Bonus Game House''' is a building that appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
The '''Yellow Bonus Game House''' is a white Toad house with blue stars.

== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' the Yellow Bonus Game House is a Special building with no rarity. The player can buy the house at the Shop. If the player touches the house, the game will ask the player to play the Bonus Game. After the player completes the bonus game, the player cannot play the minigame until eight hours pass.

=== Bonus Game ===
The player has 30 seconds the complete the bonus game. The player must tap the touchscreen to make Mario jump while he runs. The player can grab [[Purple Coin]]s, [[coin]]s and [[Rally Tickets]]s. If Mario hits the fireball, the game ends. After a fireball touches Mario or the timer ends, the game determines the number of coins and Rally Tickets.
