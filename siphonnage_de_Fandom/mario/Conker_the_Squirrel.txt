|fullname=Conker the Squirrel|species=Squirrel|alias=Conker|status=Alive}}'''Conker the Squirrel''' is a character created by [[Rare]] that first appeared in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]] as one of the playable characters in the game. He was called by [[Timber the Tiger|Timber]] to help him save the island from the evil [[Wizpig]]'s control. He mostly appears exclusively in his own series of game titles created by Rare for the [[Game Boy Color]], the Nintendo 64, and the Xbox game systems. Originally created as a harmless children's character, Conker's personality was drastically altered for the game ''[[w:c:conker:Conker's Bad Fur Day|Conker's Bad Fur Day]]'' in an effort to appeal to a more mature audience. These changes included making Conker be a bad-mouthed, alcoholic, and perverted squirrel. The other characters in the game were also made out to be incredibly inappropriate for younger audiences, such as the Great Mighty Poo, a boss entirely made out of fecal matter who uses balls of poo as his attacks.

==''[[Mario Artist: Paint Studio]]''==
Conker makes a cameo appearance as a stamp. This is the only game in which Conker appears alongside [[Mario]].

==Trivia==
* Conker goes ''Call of Duty'' and ''Halo'' style in the game, ''Conker's Bad Fur Day'', and its remake, ''Conker: Live & Reloaded''. Both are rated M for Mature.
* After ''Conker's Bad Fur Day'', he no longer appeared on any [[Nintendo]] game system, ''including'' any reissues.
