==Layout==
Tree Branch Trail goes back to the main setting of World 3: A dark, dense forest. However, there are spiderwebs in this level similar to [[Rustle Burrow]].

==History==
After escaping the home of the [[Scuttlebug]]s, [[Mario]] ventures further into the forest looking for another [[Wiggler Segment]]. He finds one on trapped on a spiderweb but must make his way around the toxic forest to retrieve it. Mario battles through more Scuttlebugs and other enemies and eventually reaches the Wiggler Segment. He battles with it and wins and it joins his side. Mario collects the [[Comet Piece]] completing the level.
