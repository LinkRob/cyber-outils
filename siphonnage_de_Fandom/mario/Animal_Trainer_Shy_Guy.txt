An '''Animal Trainer Shy Guy''' is an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Animal Trainer Shy Guys are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] with the exact same appearance as regular green ones.

===Behavior===
Outside of battle, Animal Trainer Shy Guys train [[Wild Dino Rhino]]s for circus performances.

During battle, an Animal Trainer Shy Guy attacks [[Mario]] by flailing its arms at him, just like normal Shy Guys. If an Animal Trainer Shy Guy is defeated before the Wild Dino Rhinos, they become angry and attack several times. The next turn, a different Animal Trainer Shy Guy arrives to fill in for the previous one.
