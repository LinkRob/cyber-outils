The '''Huge Treasure Chest Key''' is an item in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
The Huge Treasure Chest Key is a [[key]] that [[Wario]] needs to unlock the [[huge treasure chest]] containing the [[Black Jewel]] in [[Treasure Square]]. The Huge Treasure Chest Key has four pieces, each guarded by the main boss of a world and designed after the respective one.

==In ''Wario World''==
Wario receives a piece of the Huge Treasure Chest Key after defeating the main boss of a world. In order, they are obtained from [[DinoMighty]] at [[DinoMighty's Showdown]], [[Dual Dragon]] at [[Dual Dragon's Showdown]], [[Red-Brief J]] at [[Red-Brief J's Showdown]], and lastly [[Captain Skull]] at [[Captain Skull's Showdown]].
