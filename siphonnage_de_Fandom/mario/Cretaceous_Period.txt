The '''Cretaceous Period''' is one of the real-world periods of time from when [[dinosaur]]s lived on the [[Earth]]. In the {{franchise|Mario}}, the Cretaceous Period is represented as a location in every version of ''[[Mario's Time Machine]]'', including its [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] [[Mario's Time Machine (Nintendo Entertainment System)|version]].

==In {{series|Mario Discovery}}==
===''Mario's Time Machine''===
In PC versions, [[Mario]] ends up in the Cretaceous Period if he enters an incorrect date in the [[Timulator]]. Mario can only escape by catching some falling eggs from a [[Pterodactyl]] while dodging [[Bullet Bill]]s. After catching enough eggs, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] appears, and Mario hops onto him to somehow return back to the present.

The Cretaceous Period also appears in the good endings for the PC and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] versions. If everything is done correctly, [[Bowser]] attempts to use the [[Timulator]] to escape Mario, but it malfunctions and sends him back to the Cretaceous Period. Bowser is soon squished by a large dinosaur. In PC versions, this is followed by Bowser's crushed form being thrown away like a flying disc.

===''Mario's Time Machine'' (Nintendo Entertainment System)===
[[File:Cretaceous Period MTM NES.png|thumb|left|''Mario's Time Machine'' (NES) map of the Cretaceous Period]]
The Cretaceous Period has a larger role, now being an entire level, taking place about eighty million years ago. Bowser time-travels back to here to steal a [[Dinosaur Egg]]. After retrieving the Dinosaur Egg, Mario eventually time-travels back to here to return the Dinosaur Egg to its original location. However, Mario has to avoid [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]]s and [[Bodyslam Koopa]]s along the way. There is a cave at the rightmost part of the Cretaceous Period, where Mario drops off the Dinosaur Egg before returning to the present.
