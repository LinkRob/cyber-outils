'''Penkoons''' are enemies in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

== Profile ==
===Physical description===
The Penkoon is a hybrid between a penguin and a raccoon.

===Behavior===
A Penkoon attacks by popping a [[Spiked Ball|spike ball]] out of it stomach and kicking it toward [[Wario]].

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
A Penkoon is unaffected by Wario's [[Dash Attack]], but despite Penkoons wearing a horn helmet, Wario can use a [[stomp]] to defeat them.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Penkoon's name is a portmanteau of "penguin" and "raccoon," the two species it is based on.

== Flavor text ==
*'''Instruction booklet''': ''This pirate is part penguin and part racoon. Watch out for its spiked stomach ball.''
