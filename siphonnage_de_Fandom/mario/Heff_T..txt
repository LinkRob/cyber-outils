|race = [[Toad (species)|Toad]]}}'''Heff T.''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. He is a very large [[Toad (species)|Toad]] that is always seen with a knife and fork in his hand.

==History==
Heff T. plays a somewhat major role during Chapter 6 of the game. Heff T.'s room is two away from [[Mario]]'s on the [[Excess Express]]. Heff T. is also the culprit of the stolen [[Galley Pot|pot of stew]] case. He apologizes for his actions saying he couldn't wait for the food any longer and wanted to eat right then and there. Heff T. also gets captured by the [[Smorg]] later in the chapter until Mario defeats it. After the events of Chapter 7, despite many others that were on the Excess Express such as [[Toodles]] have left, Heff T. remains behind. Heff T. says that he has eaten too much, he couldn't fit outside his door. However, he is content saying he'll just live on the train for awhile longer.

==Trivia==
*His name is a pun of the word "hefty" which describes his overweight appearance.
*Heff T. may be a reference to [[Gourmet Guy]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]'' as they both are overweight characters, they love to eat, and that they are always seen holding utensils (Heff T. holds a spoon and a fork while Gourmet Guy holds a knife and a fork).
