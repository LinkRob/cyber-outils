'''Toad Shot''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player has to shoot at enemies ([[Bowser]]s, [[Hammer Bro]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, and [[Goomba]]s) with their [[stylus]] to gain points under a certain [[time limit]] while avoiding [[Luigi]]s and the [[Mario]]s, which take away points. [[Bob-omb]]s do not reward any points.

Toad Shot has ten levels of difficulty, each requiring an increasingly higher point total. To compensate, higher difficulty levels have more higher-scoring targets but still include more Marios and Luigis to avoid. [[Coin]]s are rewarded for clearing the minigame.
