'''Cat Bully''' is a variant of [[Bully]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It is one of several cat variants of enemies. Aside from being covered in blue fur, a Cat Bully also has white ears and a tail matching its body color but with a white tip at the end.
