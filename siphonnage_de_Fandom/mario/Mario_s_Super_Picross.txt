'''''Mario's Super Picross''''' is a ''Picross'' game for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super Famicom]] featuring ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' elements. It is a sequel to ''[[Mario's Picross]]''. The game was originally released on September 14, 1995, exclusively in Japan.

The game was ported to the [[Virtual Console]] for the [[Wii]], [[Wii U]], and [[New Nintendo 3DS]] respectively, as well as [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System - Nintendo Switch Online]]. For all three iterations of the Virtual Console, ''Mario's Super Picross'' was released in Europe and Australia as well. It was also included in these countries in the Nintendo Switch Online release as well as in North America for the first time. Despite not receiving any translation in any of its digital re-releases, the Virtual Console (New Nintendo 3DS) and Nintendo Switch Online ports of ''Mario's Super Picross'' released in English-speaking countries before Japan.
