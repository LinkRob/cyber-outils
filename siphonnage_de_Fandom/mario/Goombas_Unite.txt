Goombas Unite is the first level of [[Joke's End]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 23.

==Layout==
The 1st battle contains 8 [[Goomba Tower|Goomba Towers]]. The enemy captain is a Goomba Tower.

The 2nd battle contains 4 Goomba Towers and 4 [[Fly Guy|Fly Guys]]. The enemy captain is a Fly Guy.
