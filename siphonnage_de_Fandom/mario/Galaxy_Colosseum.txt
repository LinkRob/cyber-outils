'''Galaxy Colosseum''', named '''Galaxy Arena''' in Europe and Australia, is an arena that exclusively appeared in various online tournaments in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. It takes place on a round, spinner-shaped arena in outer space. Galaxy Colosseum mainly features [[Spiky Topman|Spiky Topmen]], which had varying roles between each of the three online tournaments taking place in Galaxy Colosseum.

Since the discontinuation of Tournaments, let alone [[Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection]] on May 20, 2014, the battle arena can no longer be accessed without using a cheating device such as Wiimmfi.
