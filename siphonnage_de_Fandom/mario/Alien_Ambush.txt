'''Alien Ambush''' is a [[microgame]] of the [[Sci-Fi]] genre in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to trap an incoming octopus-like alien into a cage on the left. When the alien reaches the cage, the player must press the A button at the right timing to trap the alien. If the alien either avoids the trap or if the cage is closed too early, the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level exhibits a difference in the alien's behavior.

*'''Level 1:''' The alien walks straight through.
*'''Level 2:''' The alien briefly pauses and sometimes jumps before walking into the cage.
*'''Level 3:''' The alien pauses shortly, then curls up and tries to roll through the cage at twice the speed.
