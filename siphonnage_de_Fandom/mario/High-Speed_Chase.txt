'''High-Speed Chase''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' It appears in the Spandex Challenge level, which [[Wario]] hosts as the final stage.

== Layout ==
High-Speed Chase has a different layout for the difficulty levels, with its "easy" level taking place on a farm, its "hard" level taking place in a parking lot, and its "expert" level taking place in a factory. In each setting, [[Wario]] will be chased by enemies.

== Gameplay ==
The player has to tilt the [[Game Boy Advance]] so [[Wario]] can escape his opponent. The "easy" level uses a boar who moves slowly and has poor targeting, the "hard" level uses a police car that moves fast and is more accurate, and the "expert" level features a turtle robot that moves even more quickly and is very accurate. If he gets caught, Wario will hit the screen and break it (and thusly the [[Fourth wall]]).
