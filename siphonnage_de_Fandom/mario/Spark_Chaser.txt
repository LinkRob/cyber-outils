'''Spark Chasers''' are an object which exclusively appear in ''[[Super Mario Advance]]''.

==Description==
Spark Chasers are found only inside [[jar]]s, and can be used to defeat all [[Spark]]s on the ground and the wall. A Spark Chaser can stick to any surface, upon which it begins traveling around fast. [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] [[Princess Peach]], or [[Toad (character)|Toad]] can jump off a Spark Chaser as well. Over time, as the Spark Chaser moves around, it gradually shrinks until it disappears.
