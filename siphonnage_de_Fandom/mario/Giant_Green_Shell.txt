'''Giant Green Shells''' are a recurring object in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
Aside from being a larger size, Giant Green Shells are the same as a [[Green Shell]]. They have a [[Red Shell]] counterpart known as a [[Giant Red Shell]].

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
Giant Green Shells are worn by [[Gargantua Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Colossal Koopa Paratroopa]]s, both of which are only encountered in [[Giant Land]]. Like regular [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can [[jump]] on one of these enemies to have it retreat into its shell. Like Green Shells, Giant Green Shells can be picked up and [[kick]]ed.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' / ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' / ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''===
Giant Green Shells retain their functionality from ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', but they can now break through [[Hard Block]]s without bouncing off.

===''[[Super Mario Maker]]''/ ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''===
A Giant Green Shell is once again worn by each green Gargantua Koopa Troopa, which can be created by giving a [[Super Mushroom]] to a green [[Koopa Troopa]]. Unlike earlier ''Super Mario'' installments, Giant Green Shells cannot be picked up and thrown.

==In {{series|Mario Strikers}}==
Giant Green Shells are an item in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'' and ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. Giant Green Shells function similarly to [[Bowser's Shell]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' in that they can be launched and slide wildly around the stage. A Giant Green Shell spins into opposing team members, squishing and stunning them temporarily, and continues to roam around temporarily before disappearing.
