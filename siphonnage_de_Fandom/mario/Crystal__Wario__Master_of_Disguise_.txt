The '''Crystals''' are key items that appears in the game ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
Each of the Crystals resemble a bird and come in four colors: red, blue, yellow, and green.

==History==
The Crystals can only be found in [[Sneezemore Cave]]. After [[Wario]] meets [[Carpaccio]] for the first time, the thief blocks off the area leading to the [[Wishstone]] piece forcing Wario to retrieve the Crystals throughout the cave.

===Locations===
* The Red Crystal can be found in a room after getting the [[Dragon Wario|Dragon]] Guise Gem.
* The Blue Crystal can found in a room in the center of the cave near some ice resembling chandeliers.
* The Yellow Crystal at the very top right corner of the cave.
* The Green Crystal can be found in a room after using Dragon Wario to drain some water in order to get to it.
