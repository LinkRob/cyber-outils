The '''Tape''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Apparently, [[Koopa Koot]] lent this item to [[Goompa]] a long time ago and wants it back. One of Koopa Koot's favors tasks [[Mario]] to retrieve the Tape back from Goompa's house and deliver it to Koopa Koot. If Mario goes to Goompa in [[Goomba Village]] and asks, Goompa will give it back abet reluctantly. Mario is rewarded with a coin after he has finished this favor. It is unknown what is on this tape.

==Trivia==
*This Tape is comparable to [[Toadstool's ???]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', the [[Box]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and the [[You-Know-What]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. All four of these items show or contain mysterious things that the hero cannot know about.
