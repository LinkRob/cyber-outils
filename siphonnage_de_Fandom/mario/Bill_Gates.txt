{{nihongo|'''Bill Gates'''|ビルゲイツ|Birugeitsu}} is a character in the fifth broadcast of ''[[Shitamachi Ninjō Gekijō]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
In his only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' appearance, Bill Gates takes on the form of a plush toy, like the other characters in ''Shitamachi Ninjō Gekijō''. Bill Gates has dark brown hair, dark red blushing cheeks, and a tongue sticking out.

==In ''Shitamachi Ninjō Gekijō''==
Bill Gates specifically appears in the fifth broadcast, which took place on February 20, 1996. When [[Mario]] and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] argue over who gets to ride the [[Windows 95]], which they believe to be a vehicle (when in actuality, the Windows 95 is a real-world computer). Bill Gates shows up to correct both of them, stating that the Windows 95 is something to wear on the head. 

==Behind the scenes==
Bill Gates is actually a real person who founded {{w|Microsoft}}, but he is also known for his contributions to philanthropy.
