'''Dark Goomba''' is an enemy found in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. These enemies are found in the first, second, third, eighth, sixteenth, and fifty-sixth floors of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]].

== Appearance ==
They are black silhouette-like [[Goomba]]s. 

== Abilities ==
They attack by trying to ram into Mario and Co. They will always inflict two points of damage if the attack connects.

== Other possible appearances ==
Although not named, Goombas with black-and-purple coloring appear in the final battle against [[Dark Bowser]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. When Dark Bowser is revived by [[Dark Fawful]], he throws a ball containing different dark enemies at [[Bowser]]. Dark Goombas will dash into Bowser; they can be defeated using Bowser's punch.
