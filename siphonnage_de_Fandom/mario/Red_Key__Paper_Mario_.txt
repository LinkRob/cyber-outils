The '''Red Key''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
The Red Key is a [[key]] found in the [[Crystal Palace]], where it is used to unlock the red door in the very first room. The Red Key is located to the south of the entrance room, much like with the [[Blue Key (Paper Mario)|Blue Key]]. To obtain the Red Key, [[Mario]] first has to go to the north side and break a wooden floor using the [[Tornado Jump]]. After doing this, a hole appears in the floor, and the Red Key is in a [[treasure chest]] at the end of the path down the hole.
