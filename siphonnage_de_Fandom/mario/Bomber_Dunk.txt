'''Bomber Dunk''' is the [[Special Shot]] of [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can have Bowser perform Bomber Dunk by tapping out an uppercase "B" twice. The "B" represents the first word of Bowser's name.

Bowser begins Bomber Dunk by dribbling the ball four times, creating a small burst of flame with each one. He then spins around and leaps into the air. The ball gets a large flame trail behind it. When Bowser dunks the ball in, a very large column of fire appears.

If executed outside the 3-point line, Bowser will hurl the ball with one hand toward the basket. As usual, a teammate needs to jump and dunk the ball in for the shot to be complete. The column of fire is still shown afterward.
