A '''spring platform''' is an object which appears in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
A spring platform is made up of five green segments. It can propel [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] upward in the manner of a [[Springboard|Jumping Board]] or a [[Jump Block]]. As such, using a spring platform plays the same sound effect. A spring platform can always be found attached to the side of an object. Mario or Luigi can bounce higher from a spring platform the closer they stand toward the end of one. Spring platforms use different frames of animation depending on if it is jumped on either closer to the object or toward the edge.

==In ''Super Mario World''==
Spring platforms can be found in three different [[level]]s: [[Vanilla Secret 1]], [[Vanilla Dome 4]], and [[Gnarly]].
