'''World 4-1''' is the seventeenth level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
The level begins in a small plain with a few enemies at the base of a giant rock spire. Using a pair of binoculars near-by, Mario can zoom in on a [[Toad]] and receive a [[1-up Mushroom]]. There is a tree to the far-right corner of the plain with a hidden [[Propeller Block]]. Mario must scale the towering mountain. Along the way are several enemies, including [[Koopa Troopa]]s [[Goomba]]s. and at the top is the level's [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]]. There is also a music box that will take Mario to the level's "Coin Heaven." On the other side of the checkpoint is a tall tree that is guarded by a [[Chain Chomp]] at its base. Along the path up the tree are several [[Biddybud]]s and [[Piranha Plant]]s, as well as time bonuses. At the top of the tree is the [[Flagpole]] which as five Para-Biddybuds flying around it.

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': At the base of the mountain is a small block that is breakable with the [[Tanooki Suit]] and after it's broken, Mario can crouch through and obtain the first [[Star Medal]].
*'''Star Medal 2''': At the end of the level's "Coin Heaven," Mario will find the second Star Medal.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The third and final Star Medal is found via the cannon near the checkpoint. This cannon, if aimed at the Star Medal, will allow Mario to take it mid-air.
