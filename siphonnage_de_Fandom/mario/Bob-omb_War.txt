'''Bob-omb War''' is a war that took place in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', and was featured in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

[[King Bob-omb]] was apparently the king of the land of [[Bob-omb]]s. He was the commander of the black Bob-omb military, and was directing them in the civil war against red [[Bob-omb Buddy|Bob-omb rebels]]. It is possible that the red Bob-omb leader was a generic red Bob-omb or already killed by a black Bob-omb or King Bob-omb. Cannons, [[Goomba]]s (likely supplied from [[Bowser]] to the black army), and many Bob-ombs were present in the battle.

After [[Mario]] came to the Bob-omb Battlefield, he helped the red Bob-ombs defeat King Bob-omb. The battle is not yet finished, however, as it rages on into ''Mario Party'' where it is in the stage [[Wario's Battle Canyon]].
