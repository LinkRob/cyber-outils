'''Bowser's Space Race''' is a [[Boss Battle]] minigame featured in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Gameplay==
The four players have to all try to damage [[Bowser]] by throwing a [[bomb]] at him. You get to speed up 3 times per cycle to give yourself an advantage. There are [[Mega Mushroom]]s placed along the way, allowing for even greater damage. At half health, Bowser gets into a mech and starts shooting fireballs. Once Bowser loses all his health, the minigame ends, and whoever dealt the most damage to them (shown from their [[point]]s) is the winner.

== In-game description ==
"Transport an explosive surprise to Bowser!"

== Controls ==
*Control Stick: Move character
*B button: Boost
