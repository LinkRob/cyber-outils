The '''Gold Card''' is a key item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Gold Card allows [[Mario]] to play the [[Tube Mode]] [[Mini-game|Minigame]]. It is received after Mario solves [[Frankie]]'s [[Trouble Center|trouble]] titled "Important Thing!".
