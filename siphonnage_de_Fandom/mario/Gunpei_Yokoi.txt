'''Gunpei Yokoi''' (born September 10, 1941 in Kyoto, Japan, and died October 4, 1997<ref name="Wikipedia">http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunpei_Yokoi#Death</ref><ref name="Virtual boy">http://www.virtual-boy.org/gunpei%20yokoi.htm</ref>) is the erector of, and voice actor for, the character [[Wario]], and has also created the [[Game Boy]]. He is also the designer of the long-running [[Metroid]] series.
