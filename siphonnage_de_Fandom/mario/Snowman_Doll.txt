The '''Snowman Doll''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
When the Snowman Doll is used, a large snowman appears and deal 4 points of damage to all enemies onscreen, and it may possibly also [[Frozen|freeze]] them. The Snowman Doll is very useful against fire-based enemies such as [[Lava Bubble]]s, [[Ember]]s, and the [[Lava Piranha]]. One Snowman Doll can be found on [[Shiver Mountain]], and more of them can be bought at the [[Item Shop]] in [[Star Haven]] (being the [[Star Haven Shop]].
