'''Mask''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
The Mask has a face resembling that of a [[Koopa Clown Car]]'s.

==In ''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2''==
A Mask is an offensive item which can be locked onto an opponent and then thrown at them. Upon being hit, the Mask temporarily halts the opponent and covers their face. One the Mask's effects has worn off, it disappears.
