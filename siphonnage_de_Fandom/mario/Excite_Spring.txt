The '''Excite Spring''' is an item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is a small red spring that can only be obtained by making a different type of [[Starbeans Espresso|espresso]] at [[Starbeans Cafe]] a third time. In doing so, [[Professor E. Gadd]] appears and gives the Excite Spring to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

==Usage==
When equipped, the Excite Spring can let Mario or Luigi stay in midair a bit longer if the player holds either {{button|gba|A}} (for Mario) or {{button|gba|B}} (for Luigi) when they are jumping. This is especially helpful in dodging ground attacks from enemies.
