'''Sizzling Steaks''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

==Opening==
The player walks to the stove and the camera zooms in. 

==Gameplay==
The objective is to cook all sides of the steak cube. There is a record system on the middle top of the screen. The player controls the pan and the winner is the first player to cook all sides. After cooking five sides of the steak, it mentions "One more left!". It takes place in a kitchen with the colors depending on the player's color.

The player must flick the Joy-Con to flip the meat cube on the pan. The player can tilt the Joy-Con to slide the meat cube that must be on the sauce to cook one side. the player must be the first to cook all sides of the meat cube to win. After cooking all sides, the meat cube is already plated with the vegetables.

==Ending==
The camera zooms out, the player's dish is on the cart and he/she does his/her victory pose.
