'''Graffiti Shot''' is the [[Special Shot]] of [[Bowser Jr.]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can have Bowser Jr. perform Graffiti Shot by tapping out an uppercase "B" on the Touch Screen twice, just like with [[Bowser]] and his [[Bomber Dunk]], with the "B" representing both of their names.

Bowser Jr. initiates Graffiti Shot by dribbling the ball in a "B" shape. The ball takes on a dull green glow, and Bowser Jr. tosses it straight up. In the air, Bowser Jr. uses his [[Magic Paintbrush]] to paint the ball three times, the third time hitting it into the basket with a trail of multi-colored paint behind it.

If Bowser Jr. uses Graffiti Shot outside the three-pointer line, the visuals remain the same, except one of his teammates has to catch the ball to dunk it.
