'''Mii Brawler''' is one of the many playable characters in the ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' series.

==''Super Smash Bros. 4''==
Mii Brawlers are one of the three different types of Mii Fighters that appear in ''Super Smash Bros. 4'' along with Mii Swordfighters and Mii Gunners and share the same character slots.

It a formidable fighter who combines speed and power with impressive combo-ability in order to take out opponents.

==''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''==
The Mii Brawler returned as a playable character in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. They were confirmed as a playable character on June 12, 2018 along with the other Mii Fighter types, the Swordfighter, and the Gunner. The Mii Brawler is classified as fighter #51.
