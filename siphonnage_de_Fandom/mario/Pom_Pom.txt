'''Pom Pom''' is a character who first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. She is the female counterpart of [[Boom Boom]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Pom Pom has pinkish skin, a ponytail, and a polka dotted shell. Like [[Boom Boom]] her feet are clawed and padded, but the pads are not white but are pink. Pom Pom has shorter arms than Boom Boom's.

===Attacks===
*'''Boomerang Blitz''': Pom Pom starts throwing boomerangs continually until becoming hit.
*'''Shell Stomp''': Pom Pom retreats into her shell and starts stomping on the floor, trying to home on Mario.
*'''Shuriken Throw''': Pom Pom starts to throw ninja stars at Mario.
*'''Koopaninja Clone Concealment''': Pom Pom vanishes in a smoke puff and reappears after being cloned. Mario has to figure out who is the real Pom Pom, and it is the one who is holding a pink star.
*'''Zone Change''': This power is not seen very much, but Pom Pom changes the zone in [[World 3 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 3]], while at [[World Bowser]], Pom Pom's zone turns into pink.
*'''Confusion Justsu''': She always make the enemy with her clones and shuriken.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
[[File:Pom Pom SM3DL screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|Pom Pom throwing a boomerang at Mario in ''Super Mario 3D Land'']]
Mario battles Pom Pom in the [[airship]] levels of [[World 4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 4]], [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D Land)|6]], and [[World 7 (Super Mario 3D Land)|7]] each. It is in a pink room where she is battled, in world six, she has fire in her room. Her last battle is alongside the [[Boom Boom]]. She throws a maximum of two boomerangs. When she is defeated by jumping on her three times, she releases several [[coin]]s, like [[Boom Boom]].
{{br|left}}
===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''===
Pom Pom returns as a boss. Her attacks involve throwing Ninja stars and duplicating herself as a Ninja. She and Boom Boom have ninja attacks combined with their abilities in the sequel of ''Super Mario 3D Land''.

===''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''===
Pom Pom only appears in the ''Super Mario 3D World'' setting. She attacks by making duplicates and throwing ninja stars at the players.

==In ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' ==
Pom Pom is an unlockable playable character, and it is her first appearance in a {{series|Mario Party}} installment, let alone as a playable character. Despite this, Boom Boom does not appear alongside her. Pom Pom can be unlocked by completing [[Salty Sea]], the fifth world of [[Challenge Road]].

Pom Pom's personal [[Dice Block]] has a "3" on four sides, a "0" on one side, and an "8" on one side.
