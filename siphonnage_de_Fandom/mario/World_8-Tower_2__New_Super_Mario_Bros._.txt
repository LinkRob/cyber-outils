'''World 8-Tower 2''' is the second [[tower]] level of [[World 8 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 8]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Layout==
This level is based around [[Snake Block]]s, similar to the design of {{World-link|7|castlen|World 7-Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)}}. There are also [[Spiked Ball]]s and [[Fire Bar]]s found throughout the level to impede the player's progress.

The level starts off on solid ground. Walking forwards will reveal the Snake Block, which will then travel in a staircase-like pattern through a group of Fire Bars. The Snake Block will then pass through a narrow corridor covered in spikes, also containing Fire Bars; as the spikes will not allow the player to jump, they must instead stall with good timing to avoid the Fire Bars and also not fall off the Snake Block onto the spikes below. Afterwards, the Snake Block travel upwards, heading into another spiky narrow corridor. Past this second corridor is the level's [[Checkpoint Flag]].

After the midway point, the Snake Block will continuously loop around over a floor covered in spikes. There is a [[Red Ring]] available here. Eventually the Snake Block will head upwards in another staircase pattern, and then head right through another corridor with Fire Bars. The Snake Block will then turn sharply upwards, with a Spiked Ball suddenly rushing out and hitting the Snake Block as it ascends. The Snake Block will then turn left and loop again, along with a second Spiked Ball landing on them. They will then ascend upwards again, and the player must be careful to avoid additional Spiked Balls and spiked walls as they do so. After one last steep ascension, the Snake Block stops in front of the boss door.

Beyond the boss door is [[Bowser Jr.]], in his last solo fight. He is fought on a standard arena, though [[Podoboo]]s will continuously rise up and fall down on both ends. He cannot be jumped on, though he will take damage from [[shell]]s and [[fireball]]s from  [[Fire Mario]]. After three shells or ten fireballs, he will retreat for the last time. Upon his defeat, another bridge is built outside of the castle; this will lead to {{World-link|8|bowsern|World 8-Bowser's Castle (New Super Mario Bros.)}}, the final level of the game.

==Enemies==
* [[Fire Bar]]s
* [[Podoboo]]s
* [[Spiked Ball]]s
* [[Bowser Jr.]] (boss)

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' In the spiked corridor right before the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]]; the [[Star Coin]] is in a gap between the spikes on the ceiling.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' Above the Fire Bar corridor past the Red Ring; it is found in its own corridor, and the player must jump towards it from the Snake Blocks.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' Near the end of the level; found on the ledge where the last Spiked Ball drops from.
