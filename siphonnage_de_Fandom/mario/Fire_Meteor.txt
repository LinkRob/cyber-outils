'''Fire Meteor''' is a [[Skillshot]] used by the [[Toad (species)|Toad]]s in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Upon using the move, Toad kicks the ball, somehow covering it in fire, as it rapidly shoots over to the goal. Any characters in the way of the Fire Meteor gets caught on fire including the [[Kritter]] goalie if he blocks it. Any characters caught on fire run uncontrollably, during which they are unusable. There is a small chance of Fire Meteor making it into the goal.
