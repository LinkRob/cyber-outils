'''Jewelry Land''' is a kingdom serving as the main setting of ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]''. It is ruled by [[Prince Pine]] and [[King Fret]], and is named for its magical gems that maintain peace and stability there.

==In ''Yoshi's Safari''==
[[Bowser]] and the [[Koopalings]] steal the magical gems, causing Jewelry Land to split into a [[Light Realm]] and a [[Dark Realm]]. They also take Prince Pine and King Fret as their prisoners. As such, [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] asks [[Mario]] and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] to save Jewelry Land, wherein they use a [[Super Scope]] throughout.
