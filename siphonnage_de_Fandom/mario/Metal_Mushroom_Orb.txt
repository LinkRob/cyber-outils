A '''Metal Mushroom Orb''' is an [[orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
As with many orbs, it contains an object to use as an item; in its case, it has a '''Metal Mushroom'''. It has the effect of turning the user [[Metal Mario|metal]], making them invulnerable to [[Trap Orb]]s put on [[space]]s.

The appearance and effect combined makes a Metal Mushroom Orb resemble a [[Heavy Mushroom]]. It also has the same effect as the [[Metal Cap]].

==Appearances==
===''Mario Party 6''===
In ''Mario Party 6'', the Metal Mushroom Orb can be obtained on all boards except [[Towering Treetop]] and [[Faire Square]], though it can only be obtained from the Great Mushroom Spirit on [[Castaway Bay]]. "[[Invincibility BGM]]" plays while a player uses a Metal Mushroom Orb in [[Star Sprint]].

===''Mario Party 7''===
In ''Mario Party 7'', the Metal Mushroom Orb can be obtained on [[Pagoda Peak]], [[Windmillville]], and [[Bowser's Enchanted Inferno!]]. The cost of a Metal Mushroom Orb in ''Mario Party 7'' ranges from five to thirty [[coin]]s.
