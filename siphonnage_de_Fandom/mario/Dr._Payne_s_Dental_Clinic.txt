'''Dr. Payne's Dental Clinic''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. It is a dentistry located in [[Diamond City]], and it is run by [[Dr. Payne]].

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
The location appears during the first stage, [[Touch Training]]. In the opening cutscene, [[Wario]] gets cavities from eating too much sweets, so he hops onto his [[Wario Bike|bike]] and goes to Dr. Payne's Dental Clinic. Here, Dr. Payne then drills Wario's cavities, with a short gameplay demo of the player tapping them with the touch controls, making Wario's teeth healthy again. After Wario has his teeth cleaned, Dr. Payne tells him to avoid eating sweets.

In the ending, Wario deliberately breaks the warning, gets cavities from eating pie at [[The Sweet Spot]], and automatically skyrockets back to the reclining seat in Dr. Payne's Dental Clinic, where Dr. Payne says that he knew Wario would return.
