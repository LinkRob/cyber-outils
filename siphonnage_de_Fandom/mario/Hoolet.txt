A '''Hoolet''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
A Hoolet hatches from its egg once it touches the ground, after which it waddles toward [[Princess Peach]] to attack her. If a Hoolet's egg breaks before hitting the ground, a Hoolet does not spawn from it.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Hoolets only appear in [[Hoo's Wood 2-6]], where they hatch from eggs spawned by [[Hoo]] during the first part of the boss fight. A Hoolet can be defeated with one hit from [[Perry]].
