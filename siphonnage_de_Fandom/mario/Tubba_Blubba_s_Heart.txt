'''Tubba Blubba's Heart''', as its name implies, is the heart belonging to [[Tubba Blubba]]. It appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

Tubba Blubba's Heart is red and hairy, and is the body part that [[Bowser]] removed from Tubba Blubba in order for him to become invincible. Tubba Blubba's Heart is one of the main villains of Chapter 3.

== History ==
Hiding in the [[Windy Mill]], the heart dictates the actions of Tubba Blubba as though he was a puppet. However, [[Mario]] discovers Tubba Blubba's secret - his heart -  thanks to the key he finds in [[Tubba Blubba's Castle]]. After fleeing from the castle and making his way to Windy Mill, Mario found the hiding heart under the windmill. The heart accuses Mario of exposing his secrets, and attacks him since he could care less about Mario's attempts of disapproval. However, he was severely hurt by Mario in the battle...

After the Heart's [[Heart Point|HP]] reaches five or lower, he flees the battle, yelling "Owwwweeee! Oh, I'm hurting! I have to get out of here!" Mario subsequently chases the Heart, and upon his exiting of Windy Mill, Tubba Blubba, who have apparently broken through the boos' attempts to block him inside his mansion, and his Heart reunite, but makes the main body lose the invincibility this way.

Tubba Blubba's Heart is seen in the parade at the end of the game, chasing his body when three [[Boo]]s carry [[Tubba Blubba]] away.

== In ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ==
In battle, Tubba Blubba's Heart only attacks in two ways. It's first and main attack is to simply charge into Mario that deals 6 damage. His second attack involves him charging up then unleashing tiny hearts onto Mario that will squeeze him and deal a massive 12 damage.

Due to the heart's lack of defense, [[Bow]] can deal high damage to the heart suing Smack without wasting FP and use [[Outta Sight]] to help Mario dodge Tubba Blubba's Heart's Swarm Squeeze attack. Since Tubba Blubba's Heart is on a small pedestal, Mario can hurt it with Jump or Hammer Throw attacks.
