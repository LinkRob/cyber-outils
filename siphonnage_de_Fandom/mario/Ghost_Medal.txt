'''Ghost Medal''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When any user equips the Ghost Medal, the item increases their [[Defense]] and [[Magic]] power by 2. The player can obtain the item after finding the [[Three Musty Fears]]' fangs.
