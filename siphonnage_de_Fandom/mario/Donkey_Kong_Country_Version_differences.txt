This article is a subpage for the game ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

==Version differences==
===North American===
====Version 1.0====
This is the original version.

====Version 1.1====
*The [[Star Barrel]] in [[Coral Capers]] has been moved up by ten pixels.
*A bug involving trading [[Rambi the Rhinoceros|Rambi]] for a different [[Animal Buddy]] in the [[Bouncy Bonanza]], [[Orang-utan Gang]] and [[Misty Mine]] [[Bonus Level]]s have been fixed.

====Version 1.2====
*The track platforms no longer drop when reaching the end of their path.
*The first [[Klaptrap]] in [[Loopy Lights]] can no longer be defeated by the roll attack as the bug has been fixed.
*The air roll bug in [[Mine Cart Carnage]] and [[Mine Cart Madness]] has been fixed.
*The bug for walking right through a [[Mini-Necky]] and [[Necky]]'s nuts has been fixed.
*When a level is completed, the completed level animation can no longer be skipped.
*The [[Kong]]-swapping bug in the [[Barrel Cannon]] has been fixed.
*The warp glitch to a level far beyond [[Kongo Jungle]] has been fixed.
*A new bug has been added; when the game pauses, the swinging ropes don't.
*The bug where Diddy jumps high up when losing a [[Bonus Level]] has been fixed.
*The two [[Slipslide Ride]] bugs have been fixed; no longer being able to over the ice in the ceiling or crawl in the wall.

===European===
====Version 1.0====
This is the original version. The North American version 1.0 differences and bugs have all been fixed.

====Version 1.1 (Virtual Console only)====
Fixed the bugs that the 1.2 version of the North American ''Donkey Kong Country'' has.

===Japanese===
====Version 1.0====
This is the original version. The North American version 1.0 differences and bugs have all been fixed along with the [[Mini-Necky]] and [[Necky]] nut bug.

====Version 1.1====
Fixed the bugs that the 1.2 version of the North American ''Donkey Kong Country'' has.
