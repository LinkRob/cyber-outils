'''Bone Appetit''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. The name is a pun of ''bon appétit'' which is to tell people to enjoy their meal; it is French for ''good appetite''.

== Gameplay ==
The objective of the game is to grab the bone without the dog or two dogs noticing you doing it. If they see you picking up the bone, the player loses in the microgame. If completed successfully, the boy will hold a bone with his mouth and two fingers up. If the Wii Remote is not in "The Discard" form, the dog will not drop it until it is in that form.
*'''Level 1:''' The dog is slowly walking in circles.
*'''Level 2:''' The dog is quickly walking in circles.
*'''Level 3:''' Two dogs are walking in circles.
