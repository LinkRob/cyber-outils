'''Hippo''' is an ally in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Hippo SML2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Hippo resembles a real-world hippopotamus, but it often sits on the ground.

===Behavior===
When [[Mario]] approaches Hippo, it releases [[Bubble|soap bubbles]] from one of its nostrils. By going into a bubble, Mario is able to swim in midair.

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
Hippo is encountered in its own [[level]] of the [[Hippo (level)|same name]].
