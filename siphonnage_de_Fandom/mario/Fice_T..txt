'''Fice T.''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who guards the entrance to [[Forever Forest]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]].

==Personality==
Fice T. often tries to describe himself as a brave Toad, but he has a major fear of ghosts (mainly [[Boo]]s). Whenever he sees a ghost, he shivers in fear.

==History==
In Chapter 3 he asks [[Mario]] to help him, he is seeing a ghost everywhere and it has just gone into the Forever Forest. The ghost turns out to be [[Bootler]], [[Lady Bow]]'s butler. After defeating [[Tubba Blubba]], Bootler sends Fice T. a letter saying that he will scare him making the Toad even more paranoid. Despite this, Fice T. still gives Mario a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] for delivering the letter to him.

If Mario talks to Fice T. with [[Lady Bow]], he will get scared, start shaking, and tell Mario that there is a ghost behind him. He will continue to do this until Bow is removed from Mario's active party. Fice T. continues to maintain his post throughout the game even when the [[Shy Guy]]s were rampaging throughout Toad Town in Chapter 4.
