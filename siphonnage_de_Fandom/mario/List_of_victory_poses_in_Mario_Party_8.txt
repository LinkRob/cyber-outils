==Information==
Unlike the previous game, every character is now limited to one winning and losing pose. The characters that win now have an echo in their voice clips.

== Trivia ==
* Wario, Daisy, Toad and Toadette are the only characters that are shown smiling in their losing animations.
* Dry Bones' losing pose is contradictory to his Mario Party 7 losing pose. In Mario Party 7, he fully collapses when losing while in Mario Party 8, only his head falls off and he tries to get it back on him.
