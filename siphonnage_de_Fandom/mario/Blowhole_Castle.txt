|games = ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''}}'''Blowhole Castle''' is a location that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Layout==
Blowhole Castle is a large, haunted castle populated by ghostly dolphins and other creatures. There is also numerous of green liquid found in areas that appears to be acid, but it doesn't harm [[Wario]] when he enters it.

==History==
===Episode: When Dolphins Rule the Earth!===
[[Wario]] continues his search for the [[Wishstone]] pieces but finds himself inside of the haunted Blowhole's Castle. Wario fights through the many ghostly dolphins and other enemies until he makes it to the final room. [[Count Cannoli]] is more eager than ever to get [[Goodstyle]] back and has aligned himself with [[Stuffy the 64th]], the ruler of Blowhole Castle. Wario defeats Stuffy the 64th and makes it past the castle into [[Carpaccio's Lab]].
