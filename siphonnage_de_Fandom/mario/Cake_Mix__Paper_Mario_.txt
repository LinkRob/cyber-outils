{{Nihongo|'''Cake Mix'''|おかしのもと|Okashi no Moto app|Confectionery Base}} appeared as an ingredient in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== Locations ==
*'''[[Shy Guy's Toy Box]]:''' The [[Shy Guy]] can be found holding the Cake Mix in the playground in the west of the blue area. This is, notably, the only area in the game to find Cake Mixes requiring frequent visits to Shy Guy's Toy Box should one is needed for a recipe.
