==History==
The Llama is only seen in the level [[Angry Aztec]]. As soon [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] enter the first main area of Angry Aztec, the Llama is seen trapped inside of a cage asking for help. The Llama can only be freed by Donkey Kong by heading to his [[Baboon Blast]] pad in the second main area and completing the course after going through the DK Star releasing the Llama. The pleased Llama leaves behind a [[Golden Banana]] before running off.

The Llama is later seen in his own [[Llama's Temple|temple]] where he can be used to help cool off the pool of lava into water which is needed to get to [[Lanky Kong]].

==Trivia==
*The Llama is very similar to Gobi from the ''Banjo-Kazooie series'' as they are both llamas that were trapped in a desert world that can be freed by the heroes.
