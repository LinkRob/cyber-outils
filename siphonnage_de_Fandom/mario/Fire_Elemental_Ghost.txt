'''Fire Elemental Ghosts''' are little floating orange-yellow flame balls with glowing yellow eyes that appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (video game)|Luigi's Mansion]]''. These little ghosts can be found near heat sources such as lamps or fireplaces. [[Luigi]] can suck up these ghosts, and then be able to shoot fire out of his [[Poltergust 3000]]. He can use these to make the Flash ghosts vulnerable, and suck them up. He can also use them to suck up a Spark ghost. If Luigi touches one of the ghosts in full size, it will set him on fire, causing damage and making him take off his hat and shake it around. The only area where Luigi can come in contact with a full size one, is in the Graveyard.

==Trivia==
*Fire Elemental Ghosts slightly resemble [[Podoboo]]s with yellow eyes, they also resemble [[Li'l Cinder]]s from the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games.
