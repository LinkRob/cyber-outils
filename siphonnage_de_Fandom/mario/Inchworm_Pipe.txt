'''Inchworm Pipes''' are a species in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Inchworm Pipes are normal [[Warp Pipe]]s brought to life during [[Wonder Effect]]s.

===Behavior===
They move by arching and straightening themselves, and players can ride on them.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder''==
Inchworm Pipes appear in the levels [[Welcome to the Flower Kingdom!]], [[Swamp Pipe Crawl|Swamp Pipe Crawl,]] [[The Final Battle! Bowser's Rage Stage]], and [[The Final Test Wonder Gauntlet|The Final Test: Wonder Gauntlet]].
