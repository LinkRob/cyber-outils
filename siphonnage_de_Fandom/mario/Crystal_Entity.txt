'''Crystal Entities''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Crystal Entities are a gem-bodied species with the appearance of a large crystal with a torso, two arms, and a ball-shaped head. Crystal Entities are featured in three color variations, red, yellow, and blue, changing the color of their torso and facial marking accordingly. Each color variant has a differently shaped facial marking of a celestial object: red Crystal Entities have a sun-like design on their face; blue Crystal Entities have a a moon design on their face; and yellow Crystal Entities have a five-pointed star design on their face.

===Behavior===
[[File:WW Pecan Sands Crystal Entity yellow.png|thumb|left|200px|A yellow Crystal Entity being fought in [[Pecan Sands]]]]
A Crystal Entity remains in one position while being fought, and it summons smaller [[Crystal Creature]]s to fight him. After damaging a Crystal Creature a few times, Wario can then throw it at the Crystal Entity to damage it. Crystal Entities are unaffected by any of Wario's regular attacks.

Upon taking damage, a Crystal Entity flashes for a few seconds, during which they are invulnerable, followed by an attack. Each Crystal Entity uses a different attack: the red ones emit a laser beam that sweeps upward; the yellow ones shoot several purple energy blasts in a straight line; and blue ones shoot three homing lasers at Wario. Aside from Wario, these attacks are also capable of damaging the Crystal Creatures.
{{br|left}}

==In ''Wario World''==
A Crystal Entity first appears in the level [[Greenhorn Ruins]]. Either one or two Crystal Entities appear in all subsequent levels except [[Mirror Mansion]]. A Crystal Entity either drops from the sky or rises from the ground, depending on the level. Wario cannot continue further until he defeats the Crystal Entity, as the Crystal Entity has formed a barrier around her area to.

Once a Crystal Entity has taken three hits from a Crystal Creature, causing it to become dazed, Wario can pick up the Crystal Entity, which ends the battle and causes the barrier to disappear. Wario can then use any attack to defeat the Crystal Entity.
