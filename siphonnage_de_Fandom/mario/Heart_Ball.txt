'''Heart Ball''' is [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Star Skill]] in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

== Usage ==
=== ''Mario Superstar Baseball'' ===
During her pitching, Peach will cause the ball to disappear momentarily into scattered hearts. Just before it crosses the home plate, it reappears, making hitting it a challenge. When Peach is batting, and the ball is hit as a high fly ball, this skill also has the same effect. The ball reappears shortly before landing on the ground, making it a challenge for fielders to catch it.

In [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge Mode]], the player must purchase the [[Lovely Heart]] before Peach can learn how to use the move.

=== ''Mario Super Sluggers'' ===
This game marks the second appearance of this Star Pitch, and it retains the same functions as it did in the predecessor. Upon usage, Peach scatters hearts around the ball, causing confusion to the batter. It is still possible to get a ball using this move, instead of a strike.

== Nomenclature ==
* In Japanese, this move is called ハートボール, which translates to ''Hāto Bōru'', meaning "Heart Ball".
* In Spanish, this move is called "Bola Corazón", which also means "Heart Ball".
