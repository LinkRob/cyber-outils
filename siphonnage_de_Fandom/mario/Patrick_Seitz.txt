'''David Patrick Seitz''' (born March 17, 1978) is an American voice actor and voice director. In the Mario series he serves as the voice director for the games at [[Cup of Tea Productions]] beginning with [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]].  He is also the voice of the [[Talking Flower]] since ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree: Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV|Super Mario Party Jamboree – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV]]'' replacing [[Mick Wingert]].

* ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''
* ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''
