'''Fries With That?''' is [[Mona]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as her microgame.

== Gameplay ==
[[File:WWIMM Fries With That?.png|thumb|left|The microgame in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'']]
The objective is to squirt tomato sauce on food and drinks that appear.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The player must squirt on a hamburger.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The player must squirt on a hamburger and fries.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The player must squirt on a hamburger, fries and a soft drink.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
Additionally, there is a label on the tomato sauce. The background has changed with colors, pink, purple and white; the patterns have horizontal stripes and polka dots.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' The player must squirt on a hamburger/fries.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The player must squirt sauce on two items. They can be a soft drink and omurice that comes up and down. They can be a donut and cupcake that comes diagonally.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The player must squirt sauce on three sushi that move horizontally.
