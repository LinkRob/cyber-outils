'''5 Silver Stars!''' is the third [[mission]] of [[Bob-omb Battlefield]], exclusive only for ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. This mission replaces [[Shoot to the Island in the Sky]] from the [[Super Mario 64 DS|original game]]. It is unlocked upon completing [[Footrace with Koopa the Quick]]. 

== Objective ==
The player's objective is to collect all five Silver Stars. This mission can be played with any character in the game. The locations for each Silver Star are listed below.
* At the top of the tree near the first bridge.
* Within a [[Goomba]] at the set of four stumps and some trees.
* Within a Goomba on the climb up the mountain nearest to the slope with the [[Red Coin]].
* In the middle of a patch of flowers near the mountain's base.
* Attached to the end of the [[Chain Chomp]], whom is roaming freely around the grassy area just east of its post.

Upon collecting all 5 Silver Stars, the [[Power Star]] will appear in its [[Star Sphere]] at the beginning of the course. But if the player gets hit by an enemy while having a Silver Star, the character will drop it, and by losing one of the collected five, the Power Star will disappear until they lost Silver Stars have been recollected.

Completing this mission unlocks [[Big Bob-omb's Revenge]].
