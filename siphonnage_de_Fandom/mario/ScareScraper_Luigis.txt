The ScareScraper Luigis are a group of four identical Luigis that are playable in the [[ScareScraper]] mode of [[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD|Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon/Luigi's Mansion 2 HD]] and [[Luigi's Mansion 3]].
[[File:Scarescraper Luigis.webp|center|thumb|448x448px|Four Luigi's standing next to each other.]]

== Description ==
They all act just like and are completely identical to [[Luigi]] with the exception of the outfits they wear. One wears a green hat with green overalls, one wears a blue hat with blue overalls, one wears a pink hat with pink overalls, and one wears a blue hat with blue overalls. They all wear green shirts underneath their overalls, and each have unique colored [[Poltergust 5000 (object)|Poltergust 5000s]] corresponding to the color of their hat and overalls. In Luigi's Mansion 3, the Luigis can wear different outfits depending on whether or not the multiplayer DLC pack is downloaded. These outfits can change the themes of the floors depending on which outfit the green Luigi wears.
[[File:Luigi Costumes.png|thumb|365x365px|The Costumes for the Luigis|left]]

== Costumes ==
The costumes that the Luigis wear can affect the theme of the floor, but the theme is only based on what the green Luigi is wearing. The primary color of the costume also depends on what the main color of the Luigi is. The images shown all show green costumes, but if the blue Luigi were to sport any of these, they would be blue as well.
