'''Psychic Safari''' is the Duel [[minigame]] of [[Mystery Land]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==
Players must power up their relics by using some brand of psychic energy and destroy the other player's relic by alternately pressing {{button|n64|A}} and {{button|n64|B}} until the time is up. The player who destroys the other player's relic wins the other player's [[Coin]]s. 

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|A}} '''→''' {{button|n64|B}} (alternately) &ndash; Power up relic

==In-game text==
''Press the {{button|n64|A}} and {{button|n64|B}} Buttons alternately to psychically power up the ancient relic. Whoever powers up his or her relic the most in 5 seconds wins.''
