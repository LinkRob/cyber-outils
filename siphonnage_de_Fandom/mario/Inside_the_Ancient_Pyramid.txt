'''Inside the Ancient Pyramid''' is the third [[mission]] of [[Shifting Sand Land]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]]: ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

== Description ==
To collect the third [[Power Star]] of [[Shifting Sand Land]], [[Mario]] has to go inside the Pyramid found in the middle of the level. When he goes inside the pyramid, He must ride an elevator down to the bottom of the level. Once he has reached the bottom of the pyramid, he needs to traverse an obstacle course to climb up to the Star.
== Trivia ==
*There is a known skip in the level in which if the player jumps out of the elevator at the top, you can get the star without going through the obstacle course
