'''Bowser Crashers''' is a Boss Battle minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]].'' It pits players against [[Fake Bowser|Impostor Bowser]], and is the final mandatory minigame of Party-Planner Trek and Boss Rush.

This boss minigame is unique from the others in the game in that it has a time limit of five minutes rather than three.

==Opening==
The player characters stand on their jets. Impostor Bowser stands at the top of a castle (resembling Bowser's Castle) before he jumps down to the ground and then breathes fire as he is approached by the players.

==Gameplay==

=== Phase 1 ===
Impostor Bowser stands with the castle to his back on the end of the bridge, and the player characters start on the opposite side of the screen to his position. To defeat him, [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and company must charge their jets and attack with ramming attacks. Impostor Bowser can attack by shredding with his claws or firing out four fireballs.

Once his health reaches 2/3 (recorded as half in the life bar) and when the progress reaches 30%, Impostor Bowser falls onto his back, only to be revived and enlarged by [[Kamek]], which restores Impostor Bowser's health to full. The life bar's 0 point shifts so that the "maximum health" is at 2/3. Unlike most boss battles, this fight features a rigid damage gate, meaning any overkill damage to the first phase is not carried over to the second. This means any extra points given during the first phase will still be carried over, bringing the theoretical final maximum score from 200 to 208 if done in single-player mode, or 310 to 318 if the number of players is at least two.

=== Phase 2 ===
From this point onward, the cameras shift from a side-angle to a front-facing one. Impostor Bowser's attacks will be paused as Kamek occassionally creates [[Bob-omb|Bob-ombs]] that fall from the sky or [[Bullet Bill|Bullet Bills]] that fire from the sides of the screen. Once the Bob-ombs or Bullet Bills disappear, Impostor Bowser resumes his attack pattern, but only attacks by firing out three fireballs. The gameplay is otherwise similar to phase 1.

Interestingly, Impostor Bowser will also primarily focus on breathing fireballs as his main attack, which excludes his claw-shredding attack before phase 3.

When reaching 45% progress, Kamek will repeat the hindering of Bob-ombs or Bullet Bills at least once.

=== Phase 3 ===
When Impostor Bowser reaches 1/3 max health (recorded as half in the life meter again due to the meter shifting) and when progress reaches 65%, he jumps closer to the players, can act more quickly, and he can also deal a continuous stream of fire. He'll still attack with firing three fireballs at a time until the progress reaches 80%. Kamek will still create either Bob-ombs or Bullet Bills, although this does not happen very often as Impostor Bowser's attack pattern number had already increased by the time he reaches half health again. Impostor Bowser's claw-shredding attack resumes at the start of this phase, similar to phase 1.

When the progress reaches 80%, Impostor Bowser's fireballs will increase from three to six and his stream of fire's attack range is larger and longer. His attack pattern range will also increase as well.

When his health is fully depleted, Impostor Bowser is defeated, awarding ten additional points to the player who dealt the finishing blow, except for Party-Planner Trek, as no bonus points are given to the player who delivers the final hit to the boss.

=== Scoring ===
Impostor Bowser has 200 hit points in total (60 for the first phase, and 70 between the second and the third phases) only if the player does not recruit any allies, or 300 hit points (90 for the first phase, and 105 between the second and third phases) when fighting with 2 players or more.

The amount of points gained from a successful attack goes as follows:

* Super-weak charge: +1
* Weak charge: +2
* Strong charge: +3
* Max charge: +5

==Ending==
Once the final hit is delievered, Impostor Bowser roars and falls onto his back in defeat, and the players perform their victory animations in front of him. If five minutes pass in the Mario Party or Free Play mode, the player with the most points wins, regardless of whether the boss is defeated or not. In Party-Planner Trek and Boss Rush, the player team has to defeat the boss within five minutes to advance.
