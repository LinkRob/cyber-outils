The '''Pipe Buggy''' is a kart debuting in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Pipe Buggy is a dark purple dune buggy. It bares some resemblance to the [[Pipe Frame]] and [[Bolt Buggy]] kart parts from the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It comes equipped with bright blue versions of the [[Monster (tires)|Monster]] tires.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Pipe Buggy, which was first made available in the Daily Pipe Selects in the [[Winter Tour (2019)]], increases the duration of [[Drafting|Slipstream]] boosts and the points earned for getting them.
