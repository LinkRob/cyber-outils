'''World 6-2''' is the third normal level in [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. 
==Layout==
The level starts in a volcano-themed overworld; on a ledge above are some coins, and the area underneath leads to a pit that takes the player underground, to the main portion of the level.

After falling down into the area, there is a slope that leads onto a [[Bone Lava Lift]] floating in lava, the segments rising and lowering as it floats along through the level. As the player travels through this area, there are several platforms and blocks overhead containing [[Goomba]]s that drop down onto the Bone Lava Lift. After a while, the Bone Lava Lift stops at a part of land with a [[Warp Pipe]]; up above is an alcove with another Warp Pipe leading to a bonus room with a [[Gold Ring]], some [[POW Blocks]], and several [[Piranha Plant]]s.

On the other side of the Warp Pipe is the [[Checkpoint Flag]] and another Bone Lava Lift after that, this time with a [[Dry Bones]] on it; as the coaster travels along, several [[Podoboo]]s also leap out of the lava. After the Bone Lava Lift reaches a wall, the lava starts to rise; [[Parabomb|Para-bomb]]s also start to fall down. After the lava rises to the top, there is a Warp Pipe nearby leading to the outside, to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' The first Star Coin is found directly to the right of the first two [[Micro Goomba]]s the player sees.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' The second Star Coin is surrounded by [[Brick Block]]s. The Brick Blocks can easily be broken so that the player can collect it.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' The third Star Coin is blocked with stone blocks. The player needs to use the Para-bombs to destroy them.
