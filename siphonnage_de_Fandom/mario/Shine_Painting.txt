'''Shine Paintings''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
A Shine Painting is an outline of a [[Shine Sprite]], usually found in the sand, and can vary in color and size. It starts off invisible, but if [[Mario]] sprays the Shine Painting with [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]], it is gradually revealed. Despite their name, most Shine Paintings release a [[Blue Coin|blue coin]] upon being revealed. An exception to this is the Shine Painting in [[Delfino Plaza]], which reveals a hidden Shine Sprite, and spraying it instead gradually fills and colors in the Shine Sprite aside from its outline.
