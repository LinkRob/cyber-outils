'''Wizakoopa''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Although he technically first appears in this game, he isn't officially named as "Wizakoopa" until the remake, ''[[Super Mario RPG]]''. Wizakoopa is a [[Magikoopa]] that later turns red and also appears as a boss later in the game.

==History==
Wizakoopa was first seen at [[Rose Way]] alongside [[Bowser]] as part of [[Koopa Troop|Bowser's Minions]] where Wizakoopa was the leader of the Magikoopa Division. Though originally tasked to help Bowser take down [[Smithy]] and reclaim his [[Bowser's Castle|Keep]], Wizakoopa and the Magikoopa Division disappeared once [[Mario]] reached [[Moleville]]. It would later be revealed that Wizakoopa was brainwashed into working for Smithy and fights Mario and the rest. After his defeat, Wizakoopa comes to his senses and apologizes for his behavior to Bowser explaining he made it back to the Keep but got brainwashed somehow wondering if he did anything wrong. Bowser decides to put the incident behind them and Wizakoopa, grateful, creates a treasure chest that will never run out of coins. He also offers to fully heal Mario's team of HP and magic at anytime.

===Battle===
In battle, Wizakoopa mainly attacks using powerful spells such as [[Water Blast]] or [[Blast]]. When his health gets low, Wizakoopa will summon a stronger enemy, mainly [[Bahamutt]]s, to aid him in the fight while he stands to the sidelines. He will only get back into the battle once his summoned foe is defeated.
