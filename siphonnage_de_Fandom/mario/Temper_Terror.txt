'''Temper Terrors''' are enemies in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Temper Terrors are light-blue [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] and a variant of [[Gold Ghost]]s with a heart protected by the element of [[fireball]]s.

===Behavior===
Temper Terrors act identically to Gold Ghosts, and their sound effects are even shared. A Temper Terror tries to punch Luigi, and if successful, it laughs at him.

==History==
In ''Luigi's Mansion'', a Temper Terror can have its heart exposed by [[Luigi]] if he has [[water]] in his [[Poltergust 3000]] and shoots it at the Temper Terror. As a result, the fire surrounding the ghost's heart is doused, leaving the ghost vulnerable to the Poltergust 3000. A Temper Terror has 10 [[Heart Point|HP]].

Temper Terrors appear in the second floor of [[Washroom (2F)|Washroom]] and the [[Sitting Room]]. During the [[blackout]], a few Temper Terrors are encountered in the [[Bathroom (1F)|Bathroom]] and [[Boneyard]]. They are one of the few regular ghosts that are optional to encounter.
