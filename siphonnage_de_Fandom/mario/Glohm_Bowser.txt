'''Glohm Bowser''' is a boss enemy from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is fought at the top floor of [[Fortress Zokket]] during the first invasion, and was created when [[Bowser]] stormed the fortress to rescue his son, [[Bowser Jr.]], and [[Zokket]] activated his Glohmatic Quad-Ray to take advantage of Bowser's raw power.

== Description ==
Being created from Bowser's body getting blasted by the Glohmatic Quad-Ray, Glohm Bowser takes its design from the Koopa King himself. Its eyes glow yellow, and its body has a red aura. The monster's feet have jet boots that let it hover, but these jets are not active in its dizzy state. Glohm Bowser's personality is hostile and violent. When it sees the Bros., it immediately registers them as enemies and attacks them. Despite its violent nature, however, Glohm Bowser still has some intelligence, shooting down the [[Shy Guy Airtub]] should [[Luigi]] try to [[Luigi Logic|commandeer it]].

== History ==
When [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] get to the top floor of [[Fortress Zokket]] during their first assault, [[Bowser]] is seen confronting [[Zokket]] and the [[Extension Corps]], attempting to rescue his son [[Bowser Jr.]] [[Zokket]], seeing the potential in the Koopa King's raw power, activates his Glohmatic Quad-Ray, blasting Bowser and creating Glohm Bowser, which attacks Mario and Luigi.

After the battle, Glohm Bowser dies, with Bowser regaining control of his body and giving a Power Tap along with the Ouch Charge [[Battle Plug]]. He later escapes to his [[Wayaway Island]] fortress, where [[Starlow]] can be seen giving him a piece of spicy chicken (a nod to how [[Drumstick|chicken drumsticks]] heal him in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'') in a side quest.

== Combat ==
Since it has jet boots, Glohm Bowser starts the battle floating, dodging ground-based weapons such as the standard hammer, the 3D Green Shell, and the Flash Clock. It has seven attacks:
*'''Death Shell''': Glohm Bowser tucks its limbs and head into its shell and performs a [[Shell Dash]] around Mario and Luigi, closing in to hit one of them either laterally or by jumping onto them. The Bros. have to counter its Shell Dash with their hammers. Unlike Bowser, who flips when the Bros. repel him, countering Glohm Bowser merely forces it to retreat.
*'''Death Punch''': Glohm Bowser approaches a brother to punch him; this must be jumped over.
*'''Glohm Pound''': Glohm Bowser does a [[Ground Pound]], with the target having to jump a quick shockwave. Slower [[Glohm]]-infused shockwaves follow and must be jumped over as well. This attack comes from an attack that Bowser himself used during his boss fight in his [[Wayaway Island]] fortress.
*'''Cold Flames''': Glohm Bowser [[Fire Breath|breathes fire]] at a Bro. from up close. This attack must be jumped over. Failure to dodge will [[Frozen|freeze the target]]. This attack comes from an attack that Bowser himself used during his boss fight in his Wayaway Island fortress.
*'''Death Pipes''': Glohm Bowser summons two large pipes that hide the Bros. entirely and flies around them to disorient the player. It then shoots a fireball into each pipe, and the Bros. must counter this with their hammers. The player has to memorise which Bro. is in which pipe. If it created a Glohm puddle, the player can use that to tell which Bro. is in which pipe; if the Glohm puddle appears in the background, Mario is on the left, with Luigi on the right.
*'''Cold Flame Stream''': Glohm Bowser breathes a stream of fire that it sweeps across the arena at alternating altitudes. It follows this with Charge. Getting hit with this attack can freeze the target.
*'''Charge''': Upon finishing its Cold Flame Stream attack, Glohm Bowser will fly into a rage and charge. This attack must be countered by jumping onto it.

Getting hit by Glohm Bowser's attacks (barring the quick shockwave from its Glohm Pound attack) can cause the victim to contract [[Glohm]].

Throughout the battle, Glohm Bowser's side has a Glohm puddle that expands (courtesy of its fist slamming the floor after it attacks). The puddle is ignored if Mario tries to use his Flash Clock attack. If the puddle reaches their side of the battlefield, [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] take damage every turn. [[Mettozokettohei|Togezokettoheitachi, Būmeranzokettoheitachi, Ringuzokettoheitachi, and Jettozokettoheitachi]] can enter the fight on the beast's side, powering up with the substance if the puddle is big enough. [[Shy Guy Airtub]]s may occasionally interfere, firing cannonballs from their cannons on their turn.

Glohm Bowser's flight can be destabilised via damage. If its flying is destabilised enough, it will fall to the ground, dazed, typically recovering after one turn, though in rare cases it may take longer to recover. During this time, [[Luigi]] can use his [[Luigi Logic]] to commandeer a Shy Guy Airtub if there is one, after which Mario climbs on as Glohm Bowser chases after their new ride. This leads to a shooter segment where it breathes fire from its mouth and attacks with punches, and the Bros. have to retaliate with cannon fire with their stolen vehicle's cannon. The Airtub will break down if Glohm Bowser scores a hit or after a certain amount of time. When the Airtub breaks down, it will crash in a different location, voiding any Glohm puddles that Glohm Bowser set up in its old arena. If the Bros. attempt to bring the Airtub under their control outside of its stun time, the Airtub grabs Glohm Bowser's attention, and it immediately destroys the vehicle. Snoutlet then wonders if the Luigi Logic failed because "Bowser [[Glohm|lost each and every one of his marbles]]." Successfully doing this Luigi Logic twice will prevent any more Shy Guy Airtubs from spawning.

After half its health is depleted, Glohm Bowser's [[POW-Up|attack power]] and [[DEF-Up|defense are increased]], and it executes its attacks slightly more quickly.

=== Statistics ===
Glohm Bowser is a Level 36 monster who has 8700 [[Heart Point|hit points]], an [[Power|attack power]] of 182, a [[Defense|defense]] of 138, and a [[Speed (Initiative)|speed]] of 366. When destroyed, it gives 14000 [[Experience Point|Experience]] and drops 1500 [[Coin]]s, a Power Tap, and the Ouch Charge [[Battle Plug]].
