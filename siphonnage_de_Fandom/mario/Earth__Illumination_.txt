'''Earth''' is a location that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]] and its spin-offs. It is a planet located separate from [[Mushroom Earth (Illumination)|another world]] and it is the home of [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]].
