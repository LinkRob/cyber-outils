'''Dark Tortoises''' are a bipedal tortoise-like enemy featured in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. They only appear in [[Stage 6 (Virtual Boy Wario Land)|Stage 6]].

Dark Tortoises wear a shuriken-like object on their head, and attack [[Wario]] by picking it off and throwing it at him. The object spins for a while before returning to the Dark Tortoise in the same manner as a boomerang. Wario can defeat a Dark Tortoise by either [[Ground Pound]]ing its head or by doing a [[Dash Attack]] on its side.
