'''Beijing''' is a real-world location serving as the capital city of [[China]]. It made its first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' appearance in ''[[Mario is Missing!]]''.

==In ''Mario is Missing!''==
[[Luigi]] has to return artifacts from famous landmarks in Beijing: the [[Hall of Good Harvest]] from the [[Temple of Heaven]], a [[Stone from the Great Wall|stone]] from the [[Great Wall of China]], and the [[Gate of Heavenly Peace]] from the [[Forbidden City]]. Additionally, in PC releases, Luigi also has to return the [[Monument to the People's Heroes]] in [[Tian An Men Square]].

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]''===
The regular Olympic events take place in Beijing, as the game is based on the {{w|2008 Summer Olympics}}.
