'''Happy Flower''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. 

==In ''[[Paper Mario]]''==
[[File:Happy_flower_badge.png|thumb|left]]
[[Mario]] gains a point of [[Flower Point|FP]] every turn. Equipping two Happy Flowers increases this rate. This badge, like its [[HP]]-based equivalent [[Happy Heart]], costs three [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear. This badge is also purchased from [[Merlow]] for eight [[Star Piece]]s. The other Happy Flower badge is found in [[Flower Fields]]. By hitting the central tree in an area with three aligned trees first, Mario can find the code for the trees. Mario then hits the right tree with his hammer. After that, hit the left tree to reveal this badge.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
Happy Flower makes a reappearance, now having a slightly lower BP of 2. Happy Flower is found in [[Dazzle]]'s inventory, where it can be purchased for four [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]]. The [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] sells the Happy Flower for 150 [[coin]]s when it is not on sale.
