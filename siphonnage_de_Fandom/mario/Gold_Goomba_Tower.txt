'''Gold Goomba Towers''' are a type of [[Goomba]] combining the characteristics of both a [[Gold Goomba]] and a [[Goomba Tower]]. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''==
Gold Goomba Towers are Goomba Towers under the effect of a [[Gold Ring]]. They act the same as Goomba Towers in that they walk back and forth. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can earn a few [[coin]]s if they defeat Gold Goombas within the Gold Goomba Tower, but Mario or Luigi can earn more coins per each one defeated if they do so in succession.

==In ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''==
As of version 2.0, Gold Goomba Towers are teammates featured in two variations, '''Gold 2-Goomba Towers''' and '''Gold 3-Goomba Towers'''. A Gold Goomba can first be turned into a Gold 2-Goomba Tower by spending four [[Goomba Coin]]s, then into a Gold 3-Goomba Tower by spending six more Goomba Coins.
