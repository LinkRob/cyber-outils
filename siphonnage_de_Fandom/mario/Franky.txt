'''Franky''' is a [[Boo]] and a minor character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He has the same appearance as regular Boos.

==History==
Franky lives in [[Boo's Mansion]] and he can be found near the entrance in the living room. Franky does show affection for [[Lady Bow]] and tells [[Mario]] he can shop at the [[Boo's Shop|item shop]] in the basement. Franky is also involved in one of [[Koopa Koot]]'s favors where he has an [[Old Photo]] of Koopa Koot as the elderly [[Koopa Troopa]] wants it back. After getting the Old Photo, Franky warns Mario that Koopa Koot is a selfish Koopa that often takes advantage of people for doing his favors and only paying them with a single coin. Though this is partly true, Koopa Koot also offers better rewards such as [[Star Piece]]s and the credits.

Franky is also involved in the chain letter side-quest. After Mario delivers the letter to Franky from the [[Little Mouser (character)|Little Mouser]] of the [[Little Mouser's Shop|shop]] in [[Dry Dry Outpost]], Franky mentions that Little Mouser is known for getting him the best deals and useful items from around the world. Franky then asks Mario to deliver a letter to [[Dane T.]] of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] who likes to hang around the train station.
