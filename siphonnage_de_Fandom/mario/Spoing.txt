'''Spoings''' are spider-like enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. Whenever they see [[Mario]], they hop in the air and then back down. Spoings earned their name from "spider". These enemies can easily be defeated by jumping on their head or spinning into them. They are commonly found in the [[Space Junk Galaxy]] (where their leader [[Tarantox]] is fought) and in the [[Tall Trunk Galaxy|Tall Trunk]] and [[Flip-Out Galaxy|Flip-Out]] [[Galaxy|Galaxies]]. Whenever they successfully hit Mario, they snicker upon contact. They have a web-hanging equivalent called [[Spangler]]s.
