'''Bob-omb Blasting''' is one of the four mini-games found in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' along with [[Bubble Blowing]], [[Manta Ray Surfing]], and [[Star Ball Rolling]]. It appears in the [[Battlerock Galaxy]] and the [[Dreadnought Galaxy]], and involves the use of multiple [[Bob-omb Dispenser]]s. When [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) arrives on the small planet that the mini-game takes place on, he is told by a furious [[Gearmo]] that people have been dumping trash in the ammo depot, which is supposed to be clean. Mario (or Luigi) therefore has to use [[Bob-omb]]s to blow up all the trash in under thirty seconds. The Bob-ombs take a full 10 seconds to explode, and the small yellow lights on the ground mark a good place to throw them.
