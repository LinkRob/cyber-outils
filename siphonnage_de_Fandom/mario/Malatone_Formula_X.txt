'''Malatone Formula:X''' is a item that only appears in the ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' side-story of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. It was made by [[Best Fitness Friends|Kaley & Dieter]] by combining [[Skeletone Formula:D]] & a [[Malodorous Fibban]]. It has the ability to turn victims hostile. was first used on [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Morton]], to turn him against [[Bowser Jr.]] & [[Kamek]].

==In ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey''==
Malatone Formula:X looks like [[Skeletone Formula:D]] but with a purple-and-green color scheme. Dieter and Kaley of the Best Fitness Friends created the item in order to brainwash any minion that eats it, giving them a purplish aura and extra power. Its effects are so powerful it even sways a Koopaling (Morton) to Fawful's side.
