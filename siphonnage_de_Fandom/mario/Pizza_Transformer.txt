The '''Pizza Transformer''' is an item that exclusively appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Cher's Poochie]]." 

==Description==
The Pizza Transformer is a large device that can switch two bodies by zapping them with blasts of energy. Among the components of the Pizza Transformer are two computer screens, two keyboards, various wires, and a large switch.

==Episode appearances==
In "Cher's Poochie," the Pizza Transformer switched Mario's body with and [[Cher]]'s dog, [[Poochie]].
