'''Baby Mario and Baby Peach: Dynamic Duo''' is the seventh level of [[World 1 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 1]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. A [[Red Yoshi]] takes control in this level. Like [[Mario's Fleet Feet]], this level takes place in a forest of colorful pine trees. The level has many [[Stork Stop]]s and primarily revolves around switching between [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Peach]] and using their abilities to overcome the obstacles ahead. This level also marks the debut of the [[Super Star]], a powerful item that turns [[Baby Mario]] into an invincible superhuman that can fly and run up walls and ceilings. There are also vines located throughout this level which [[Baby DK]] can use once he is unlocked.


==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, advance to the right and tag the first [[Stork Stop]], assuming you're using [[Baby Peach]], switch to [[Baby Mario]] to make the nearby [[M Block]]s solid and hop onto them to reach the ledge upahead. On this ledge, grab the two [[Red Coin]]s above the M Blocks and hop to the nearby ledge with the [[Winged Cloud]], hit the [[Winged Cloud]] with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] for some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and drop back down to the main path defeating the [[Shy Guy]]s and [[Crazee Dayzee]]s along the way, hop onto the ledge and you will see some [[M Block]]s blocking the path down, advance to the left using the nearby set of [[M Block]]s as a platform to reach the ledge where the [[Stork Stop]] is and switch to [[Baby Peach]], after this, the [[M Block]]s should disappear and you should be able to obtain the [[Smiley Flower]] as well as hit the [[! Switch]] to temporarily activate the wind to soar to the high ledge above (if you fail to reach the ledge in time, don't panic, just hit the [[! Switch]] again for another attempt) and continue along the path grabbing the [[Coin]]s and two [[Red Coin]]s here, after this, drop back down, switch to [[Baby Mario]], hit the next [[! Switch]] to activate the switch blocks, run across them before time runs out whilst collecting the [[Coin]]s and three [[Red Coin]]s here, at the path's end, hop down the [[Pipe]].

In this secret room, you will come across a [[Super Star]], a special item that only [[Baby Mario]] can obtain (other babies are unable to use it's effects), in this form, Yoshi will briefly transform into a [[Giant Yoshi Egg]] and [[Baby Mario]] will become a invincible caped baby capable of gliding, climbing up walls and ceilings, and running at extremely fast speeds. There are heaps of [[Coin]]s in this room and even a [[Smiley Flower]] but no [[Red Coin]]s, Baby Mario can collect multiple [[Super Star]]s to prolong the effects of his invincibility, he can also run around the spherical platforms in an endless loop. Once you've obtained all the goodies and the effects wear off, Yoshi will return and you can exit through the [[Pipe]].

Upon returning to the main level, advance to the right and beat the [[Piranha Plant]] in your way, in the next area, you will come across a staircase of [[M Block]]s which are blocking a [[Pipe]], here, you will find some transparent [[M Block]]s, hit them (as [[Baby Mario]]) to make them appear (when [[M Block]]s are hit, they remain solid even when you switch out to [[Baby Peach]]) and climb them to reach the [[Stork Stop]], switch out to [[Baby Peach]] and advance back down (to the left, there is a secret area that only [[Baby DK]] can typically access due to his vine-climbing abilities but by bringing a [[Shy Guy]] to the ledge (with Yoshi's tongue) and stomping on it for some extra oomph, Yoshi can reach the ledge, crack the block there with ground pound to reveal a hidden [[Winged Cloud]] which contains a 1-Up), enter the previously blocked-off [[Pipe]] to reach a secret area with a [[Smiley Flower]], grab the flower and return back to the main level advance to the right and through the transparent [[M Block]]s and tag the [[Middle Ring]], hop across the transparent [[M Block]]s (if you fall, use [[Baby Peach]]'s umbrella to glide out of the abyss) and switch to [[Baby Mario]], the [[M Block]]s block off the pits and hit the transparent [[M Block]] staircases to solidify them before switching back to [[Baby Peach]] and climbing them, soar across the winds and grab some [[Coin]]s and three [[Red Coin]]s here, and use the wind at the end to fly higher.

In the next section, you may seem trapped with nowhere else to go but advance to the right through the wall and a path is revealed, continue down the path (whilst avoiding some [[Tap-Tap]]s here) and continue to the right, drop down and you will find an area with some vines (keep these in mind for later when you unlock [[Baby DK]]), there is a [[Smiley Flower]] in the air above, an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] should be able to secure it for you, there is also a [[M Block]] here (which [[Baby Mario]] can hit for two [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]]) and in the niche to the left, there are two invisible [[Winged Cloud]]s, either use [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] or the two [[Shy Guy]]s in the area to open these clouds for [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and a 1-Up. After this, use the [[Spring Ball]] to return to the main path, hop to the upper path, you can make the [[Tap-Tap]]s more manageable by knocking them into the pits with either Yoshi's tongue or an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]], after this, run past the [[Stork Stop]] and hop through the transparent [[M Block]] staircase and jump through the hidden path on the ledge, you will get [[Coin]]s on your way down, there is also a [[Stork Stop]] here, move to the right and grab the two [[Red Coin]]s at the end, upon returning back to the [[M Block]] staircase, switch to [[Baby Mario]] and climb the [[M Block]]s, hop to the nearby ledge to reveal a hidden niche with two [[Red Coin]]s, continue along the path (with accurate flutter jumps, you can hit these [[M Block]]s to solidify them and use a [[Shy Guy]] to propel up to the upper platforms to clear this area with [[Baby Peach]]), upon entering the tunnel, you will come across your second [[Morph Bubble]], the [[Yoshi Mole Tank]], you can use this ability to squeeze through the tunnel and plow through the dirt blocking it, there are several [[Coin]]s stashed here and there are also three [[Red Coin]]s located near the end, once you're done, hit the [[Yoshi Block]] at the end to switch back to regular Yoshi and drop down and grab the [[Coin]]s before entering the [[Pipe]].

Upon reaching the next area, advance to the left to find a secret nook with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] and an [[Arrow Cloud]], hit the Arrow Cloud with an egg to launch the egg in that direction along the track of Arrow Clouds grabbing the [[Coin]]s in the path and the [[Smiley Flower]] at the end, from here, continue to the right entering more niches in the small hill-like structures, one of these contains a [[Red Coin]]. Enter the large structural niche to the right (by hopping on the floating block) and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] to make a [[Flower Plant]] appear. Climb up the Flower Plant and hop on the moving [[Flatbed Ferry]] and hit the hovering [[Fly Guy]] above (it is carrying a [[Red Coin]] and return from whence you came and take the path down, advance to the left and grab some [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s from the [[Egg Block]] and toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the [[Arrow Cloud]]s so they follow the track into the [[Tulip]], upon tossing an enemy or egg into the Tulip, you get 8 bonus [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], continue to the right and switch out to [[Baby Peach]] get rid of the [[Shy Guy]] and [[Tap-Tap]] here and hit the [[! Switch]] to activate the wind, grab the [[Key]] from the floating [[Baron Von Zeppelin]] and soar up to the [[Flatbed Ferry]] (where you hit the [[Fly Guy]] for a [[Red Coin]] and enter the niche to the right and enter the door at the end to play a minigame ([[Speed Eating]]) after the minigame is done, advance to the left and return to the level's main path, you can finish the level with whichever baby you choose, advance to the niche to the right, avoid the [[Tap-Tap]]s, use the [[Spring Ball]]s and grab the [[Coin]]s and [[Red Coin]] here, upon reaching the top, you will reach an area with several [[Shy Guy]]s, [[Woozy Guy]]s, and [[Tap-Tap]]s, (the right platform here has a hidden [[Winged Cloud]] with some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], hit it if you are short) and trek towards the end, as you exit the hut a [[Fly Guy]] holding the final [[Red Coin]] soars into view, be sure to hit it with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] or else it's going to fly away, after this, jump through the [[GOAL! Ring]] to finish the level.

==Baby Coin location==
You will need [[Baby DK]] for this [[Baby Coin]], after completing [[Baby DK, the Jungle King!]], return to the area with the vines and the [[Smiley Flower]], use [[Baby DK]]'s climbing skills to climb the rightmost vine to a hidden niche where the [[Baby Coin]] is stashed.
