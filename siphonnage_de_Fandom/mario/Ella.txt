'''Ella''' is a character in the ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'' series. She first appears in ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]'', along with [[Neil]], as one of [[Kid]]'s two students, although Ella makes her technical debut in ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]'', in which she can become playable if using the Nintendo GameCube Game Boy Advance Link Cable to transfer her over from ''Mario Golf: Advance Tour''.
