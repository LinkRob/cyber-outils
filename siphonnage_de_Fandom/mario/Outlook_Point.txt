'''Outlook Point''' is the sixth level of World 3 in the game  ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.
==Layout==
Unlike all of the previous levels of World 3, Outlook Point is the only one, during the current events of the game, that wasn't polluted with poison. It has lush green grass and bright blue skies. This level approaches more on being a rest point level than a level that focuses on gameplay.

==History==
[[Mario]] takes a little break from battling enemies and avoiding poisonous gunk. He appears at a [[Sticker Shop#Outlook Point|shop]] that sells [[Secret Door]] Stickers. Mario then finds a [[Toad]] looking at a shipwreck and talks to him. Mario then leave this level.
