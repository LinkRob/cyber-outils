'''Kalypso''' is a female [[Kremling]] and a racer who appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Barrel Blast]]''. She is an unlockable character and the rival of [[Tiny Kong]]. The player can unlock her by clearing the Sapphire Cup as a Kremling.

Kalypso also makes an appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as collectibles.
