The '''Golden Apple''' is a type of food in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. The apple cannot be claimed in the game.

== History ==
At the [[Overthere Stair]], Mario and company found [[Princess Peach]] sleeping next to the Golden Apple. Luvbi threw away the apple and stated it as the "forbidden fruit", Peach will sleep for one hundred years if they do not make her eat the taboo fruit. [[Tippi]] questioned Luvbi about the taboo fruit, but Luvbi refused to help Mario and company. Leaving Luvbi, they searched for different types of apples, with several comedic things happening if you pick the wrong fruits. Fortunately, Peach woke up disgusted with the taboo fruit, [[Black Apple]]. Princess Peach rejoined Mario's party.
