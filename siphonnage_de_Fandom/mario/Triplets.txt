The '''Triplets''' are characters who appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Appearance ==
The Triplets are three [[Micro Goomba]]s who all have the same appearance as one another.

== History ==
The Triplets can be found in [[Monstro Town]] where they along with their father a [[Goomba]] open up a shop. They fled out of the [[Koopa Troop]] due to fear of fighting [[Smithy]] and his [[Smithy Gang|gang]]. However, they will offer to sell useful items to [[Bowser]] in order to get his [[Bowser's Keep|keep]] back. The Triplets only sell a defective type of [[Mushroom (healing item)|Mushroom]] that turns its user into a [[Mushroom (ailment)|mushroom]], although it recovers 30 [[Heart Point|HP]] and can steadily recover more until the mushroom status effect wears off.
