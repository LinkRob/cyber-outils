'''Wario-Man''' is the superhero alter-ego of [[Wario]] that first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''. In his ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' appearances, Wario-Man hosts the final set of [[microgame]]s played through by the player.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===  ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' ===
When Wario was checking up on the video game devices that he asked [[Dr. Crygor]] to make for him he sat down to play. Then all of a sudden he fell into Crygor's [[Gravitator]]. When he returned, he was Wario-Man. He decides to become more powerful by merging himself with the Gravitator. Wario-Man then flies out to space only to be hit by [[Orbulon]]'s [[Oinker]]. Orbulon thought Wario was an invader but Mona recognized him from his mustache. When Wario falls to [[Mushroom Earth]], Dr. Crygor was fishing and reeled in Wario.

=== ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'' ===
Wario fell sick. When in bed he thought that Garlic would make him feel better. But when he ate some Bad Garlic he transformed into Wario-Man. After his [[microgame]] mix, he thought he could crush a train, but his cold affected his powers and the train collided with him. He flew high in sky and when fell down into the sewers he had reverted back to his normal self again. The Sewer Guru then hit him on the head as revenge from the fight Wario and the [[Sewer Guru]] had earlier. Meanwhile, as a [[Breaking the fourth wall|fourth wall]] reference, ''WarioWare: Touched!'' proved to be a smash hit in the stores.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
{{Infobox_Misc|name=Wario-Man (Super Smash Bros.)|image=Wario-Man SSBB.png|caption=Wario-Man as he appears in [[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]|first=''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' (2008)|latest=''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' (2018)}}
Wario-Man also appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]'' as the [[Final Smash]] performed by Wario. With Wario-Man, he is able to run fast, make stronger hits, and he can be invincible. In ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', there is an obtainable [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|Trophy]] of Wario Man, and it is unlocked by beating All-Star mode with Wario.

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', when Wario eats his garlic, he turns into Wario-Man and runs into an opponent in front of him. When that opponent is hit, Wario suddenly does many attacks to the opponent and ends with a waft.

== Weakness ==
* Catching a cold can affect his powers.
* Using Wario Bike creates an excess of speed which hosts a danger of falling off a stage.
* A strong enough hit can turn Wario-Man back to Wario, like when he got hit by a train in ''WarioWare: Touched!'' or when he got hit by Orbulon's Oinker in ''WarioWare: Twisted!''.

== Trivia ==
* ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' is the only game where Wario-Man's mustache is red. In all the other games it is black.
* When Wario transforms into Wario-Man his nose turns red instead of the usual pink. Although this only happens in some of the games.
* When Wario transformed to Wario Man in ''WarioWare: Touched!'', he ate Bad Garlic, but in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' he transforms from eating regular [[Garlic]].
