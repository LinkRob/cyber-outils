'''Pastaland''', alternatively spelled '''Pasta Land''' is a location in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It first appears in the episode "[[The Pied Koopa]] and returns in "[[Mario and the Red Baron Koopa]]".

==Description==
Pastaland is an area where pasta can grow out of the ground, hence its name, and many areas of the ground resemble a red and whited checkered tablecloth. In the center of Pastaland is a village inhabited by [[Toad (species)|Mushroom Folk]], who make a living harvesting their local crops, and Pastaland also has its own mayor, [[Mayor Fettuccine]].

The [[Pied Koopa]]'s castle is located in another area of Pastaland, and the castle is surrounded by a moat with [[Trouter]]s.
