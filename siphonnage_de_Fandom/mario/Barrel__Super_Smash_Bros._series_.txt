'''Barrels''' are a recurring container item in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Bowser holding Barrel SSB4 Wii U.jpg|thumb|left|250px|''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'' screenshot of [[Bowser]] about to throw a Barrel]]
A Barrel is a container item containing between one to four items, and it can roll along inclines. Certain Barrels also explode upon being broken. As of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', Barrels bear a different appearance depending on which stage they appear in, such as the [[Yoshi's Island (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Yoshi's Island]] stage featuring barrels in light green present wrapping or [[Lylat Cruise]] having metallic barrels. The barrels all function the same, regardless of their appearance. The wooden and metal Barrels in the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series also have the series' emblem, and its placement on the wooden barrel varies between its appearances. 

Barrels are possibly based on their [[Barrel|equivalent]] from the ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' and ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. Aside from being a container item and having an emblem, another distinguishing factor with the Barrels from the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series' is that their [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] descriptions list ''Super Smash Bros.'' games instead of a ''Mario'' or ''Donkey Kong'' game.
