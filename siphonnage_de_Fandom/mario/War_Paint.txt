The '''War Paint''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the War Paint.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', War Paint resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has medium cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The War Paint deals around 105 to 115 damage.

It has a 50% chance of triggering the Super Effect. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Honey (status effect)|Honey]] dealing 144 damage.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the War Paint as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it after the player defeats [[Calavera]].
