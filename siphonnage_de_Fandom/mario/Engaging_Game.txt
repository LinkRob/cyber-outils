== Objective ==
The player can find the quest if they enter the [[Ice Game Room]] where they find [[Penguin (character)|Penguin]] and accept their quest to help him.

He explains to the player that he is trying to propose to a girl named [[Pengwen]]. However, he is broke and cannot afford the wedding ring. The player has to play [[Drop 'Em]] by winning at least 1 coin more than what they started with. The player starts with 30 coins, means he needs to gain 31 coins or more in total.

After completing the quest, Penguin thanks the player and he said he can buy Pengwen a ring. The player can keep the Drop 'Em minigame as well.
