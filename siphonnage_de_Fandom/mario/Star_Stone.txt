The '''Star Stone''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Star Stone can be obtained from [[Merlar]] of [[Shiver Mountain]]. [[Mario]] must walk behind a hidden wall in order to speak with her and, after listing to her long story, is given the Star Stone. The Star Stone can then be used on a pedistal which makes a large stairway appear allowing access to the [[Crystal Palace]].
