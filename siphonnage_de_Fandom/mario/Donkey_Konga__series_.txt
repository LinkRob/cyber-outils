'''''Donkey Konga''''' is a spinoff series of rhythm games in the ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' franchise. It consists of three installments: ''[[Donkey Konga]]'', ''[[Donkey Konga 2]]'', and ''[[Donkey Konga 3 JP]]'', the latter of which never released outside of Japan. All three games are for the [[Nintendo GameCube]], and they were developed by [[Namco]]. The main controller is the [[DK Bongos]], also used for ''[[Donkey Kong Jungle Beat]]'', although the games can also be played using a standard [[Nintendo GameCube Controller]].

''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters make cameo appearances in all three ''Donkey Konga'' games, but they do not physically appear among the ''Donkey Kong'' cast.
