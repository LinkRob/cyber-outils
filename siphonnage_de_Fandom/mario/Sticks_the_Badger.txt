'''Sticks the Badger''' is a character from the ''[[Sonic the Hedgehog (franchise)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' franchise, the ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Boom (series)|Sonic Boom]]'' series in particular. She made her first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sticks is a female jungle badger.

===Personality===
Sticks is energetic, wild, and very friendly, though she is sometimes paranoid and crazy.
