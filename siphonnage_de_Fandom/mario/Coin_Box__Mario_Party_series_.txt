The '''Coin Box''' is an item from ''[[Mario Party]]''. This is the first item given when the game begins. Its purpose is to store [[coin]]s and [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]]s that players acquire at the end of a board game (or [[Mini-Game Stadium]]). They are similar to [[Casino Box]]es and [[Lucky Box]]es.

In ''[[Mario Party 2]]'', if the player maxes out the Coin Box's capacity at 99,999 coins, the player receives a secret message from the developers upon checking it.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l-ObRV-m_Ig ''Mario Party 2'' 99,999 Coins in the Coin Box]. YouTube. Retrieved July 13, 2024.</ref>
