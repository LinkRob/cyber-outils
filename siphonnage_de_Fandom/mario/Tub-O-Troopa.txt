'''Tub-O-Troopa''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Tub-O-Troopas are very large [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s encountered in [[Bowser's Keep]]. They have a variant named [[Heavy Troopa]].

==Battle==
In battle, Tub-O-Troopas mainly attack by charging at one of [[Mario]]'s party members. They are well known for their high HP and attack.

===Strategy===
Tub-O-Troopas are weak to thunder meaning [[Mallow]]'s [[Shocker (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Shocker]] will be very effective against them.
