}}A '''Poison Cherbil''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Poison Cherbils are a species of [[Cherbil]] that are found in different locations of the game (first seen in the [[Dotwood Tree]]). Poison Cherbils attack by expelling out poison gas that can inflict [[Poison (ailment)|Poison]] requiring caution around them.
