'''Very Gnawty''' is the first boss found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' who resembles a giant [[Gnawty]]. He is the boss of [[Kongo Jungle]]. Very Gnawty can be attacked by [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] by simply jumping on him. He progressively becomes quicker and jumps further after each time Donkey and Diddy jumps on him. Donkey and Diddy must then counter Very Gnawty's attacks and jump on him four more times to defeat Very Gnawty.

Very Gnawty has the same appearance as [[Really Gnawty]], except that one is green and the other is brown. This was switched in the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake.
