'''Nuts''' are consumables used by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'']] series. They restore [[Heart Point|HP]] to both of them in one turn. In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', they are replaced by [[Mushroom Drop]]s.  They look similar to {{wp|peanut}}s.

==History==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
[[File:MLSuperStarSagaNut.png|frame]]
Nuts first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. Regular Nuts heal {{hover|20 [[Heart Point|HP]]|25 HP in the Japanese version of ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''}} for both brothers. It can be bought from [[Castle Town Item Shop]] after [[Mom Piranha]] is defeated for fifty [[coin]]s, as well as from [[Little Fungitown Item Shop]] as soon as it opens for the same price. A few enemies have a chance of dropping it: [[Bob-omb]]s have a 19.35% chance, undamaged [[Mecha-Chomp]]s have a 12.9% chance, and [[Teehee Valley]]'s [[Gritty Goomba]]s have a 9.68% chance.

===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''===
[[File:Nut.png|frame|left]]
Nuts make a return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. Just like in ''Superstar Saga'', they are able to restore twenty HP to both Mario and Luigi. [[Protobatter]]s have a 20% chance of dropping a Nut after a battle, and [[Calorite]]s have a 25% chance.
{{br|left}}

===''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''===
[[File:MLDT Nut.png|frame]]
Nuts reappear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. While they function the same, they now restore fifteen HP instead of twenty. They cost twenty coins at any of the shops. [[Thorb]]s have a 5% chance of dropping one after defeating them, and [[Bloatula]]s have a 4% chance.
{{br|left}}

===''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''===
Nuts in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' have the same look and function as they did in ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''.
