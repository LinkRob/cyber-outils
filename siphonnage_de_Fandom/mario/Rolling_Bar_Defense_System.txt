The '''Rolling Bar Defense System''' is an obstacle in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
The Rolling Bar Defense System is a series of spiky tubes that roll between platforms indefinitely. Two of these systems are placed just before the red and green [[Pearl Bean]]s in order to protect them. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] and bypass them only in certain circumstances; if either character comes into contact with one of the bar's spikes, they are sent flying into the air and back to the start.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
The Rolling Bar Defense System is found in the caves under the [[Relaxation Room]], serving as a tutorial for Mario's [[Dash (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Dash]] ability and [[Luigi]]'s [[Tickle]] ability. It is later found outside in [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] and at [[Joke's End]] and [[Bowser's Castle]].
