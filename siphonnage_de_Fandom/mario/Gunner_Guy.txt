'''Gunner Guys''' are enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] encounter them in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser Castle]], which [[Cackletta]] had brought under her control at the time, also bringing [[Bowser]] under her control himself.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Gunner Guys have long cannons poking from their masks and army camouflage gear. Gunner Guys use their cannons for artillery weapons. In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', Gunner Guys wear the same red robe as a regular [[Snifit]].

== Battle ==
[[File:MLSS Gunner Guy.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario and Luigi battling two Gunner Guys, one of which is assisted by a Shy Guy, in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'']]
In battle, Gunner Guys are aided by [[Shy Guy]]s with camouflage-patterned robes, being pushed up to their target and firing when their Shy Guy tells them to. The projectile that Gunner Guys shoot from their gun-like nozzles must be jumped over. The Shy Guys will give a clue to the projectile's trajectory with their behavior: if they hop, the projectile will fly quickly at the target; otherwise, it will roll slowly along the ground towards him. The projectiles deal damage despite lacking spikes or other dangerous things on their surface should they be jumped on. Gunner Guys are healed by [[Firebrand|fire]] damage, but [[Thunderhand|electrical]] damage is their weakness.

Gunner Guys are treated as Level 40 enemies that have 60 HP, a Strength of 124, a Defense of 120, and a Speed of 90 as well as immunity to the Burn and Dizzy effects. Their defeat nets Mario and Luigi 90 Experience Points apiece and 25 coins on top of an Ultra Mushroom in 387 out of 2000 cases.
