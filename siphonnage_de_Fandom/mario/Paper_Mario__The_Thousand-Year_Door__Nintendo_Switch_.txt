'''''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''''' is a role-playing game for the [[Nintendo Switch]]. It is a [[reissue|remake]] of the [[Nintendo GameCube]] game of the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door|same name]]. The release date was announced on [[Mario Day#2024|Mario Day 2024]] and released on May 23, 2024.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vfjmQlWRHgQ Mar10 Day 2024]</ref>

==Changes==
===Overall===
*Most assets have been updated.
*New soundtracks have been added, some of which weren’t included in the original, additionally, the [[HP]] and [[FP]] restoration sound effects have been updated to match the sound used in games since ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', the item-use sound effect has also been updated 
*Characters now have a "cardstock" appearance to them.
*Certain characters will have different sounds while dialogue is showing, such as [[Koops]] having [[Koopa]]-like noises.
*Partners and other characters can now face away from the camera.
*New [[Battle Master]]<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eTnX8KtICnQ&t=672s 15 Minutes of PAPER MARIO The Thousand-Year Door REMAKE - Exclusive NEW GAMEPLAY 🍄 (Nintendo Switch)]</ref> Toads helping the player practice battle skills.<ref>[https://www.nintendo.com/jp/topics/article/a37c0bd2-647f-4547-9d74-a2ec35d46faf 個性豊かな仲間たちと、ペラペラマリオが大冒険。Nintendo Switch『ペーパーマリオRPG』の紹介映像を公開] on [[Nintendo]]'s Japanese website.</ref> 
*The Partners have now been given extra dialogue and animations.
**Mario and the other characters (other than the partners) have more expressions and extra animations as well.
*Mario no longer loses coins whenever he runs from battle.
* Mario earns coins from defeating bosses or battles in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] known as a Clear Bonus, a feature that first appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.
*The fast travel system in [[Rogueport Underground]] has been completely redone. Instead of it being two rooms with warp pipes in them, it is now one giant room where the Warp Pipes appear after Mario has visited the location that has one. This also helps cuts down on the backtracking of the search for [[General White]] twice.  
** In addition, there is a Warp Pipe that leads directly from the main plaza of Rogueport to the room with the Warp Pipes.
**The player can now fast travel to [[Glitzville]], [[Twilight Town]], and [[Fahr Outpost]], which previously couldn't be done in the original game.
* A fast travel system has now been added to Chapter 4 cutting down the infamous backtracking from the original game. There is a Warp Pipe located inside of a house beside the Item Shop in Twilight TOwn where it can be only accessed by destroying a large block blocking access to the Warp Pipe in the [[Creepy Steeple]]. However, the player must wait until they get into the room with Doopliss' pet parrot to utilize it.
*Both [[Prince Mush]] and [[Whacka]]-who were originally just background characters-are both now hidden super bosses. Prince Mush can be fought anytime after [[Jolene]]'s trouble is completed, while Whacka can only be fought by whacking him 7 times in [[Keelhaul Key]], beating both the [[Shadow Queen]] and [[Bonetail]], and doing the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] a second time.

===Gameplay mechanics===
*The player can now see whenever Mario and his partners are guarding allowing them to visually see when they guard (a trait that has been adopted from the later ''Paper Mario'' games starting with ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'').
*During the fight with [[Doopliss]] on [[Twilight Trail]], Doopliss can now damage Mario while Mario cannot damage him back (in the original, Mario and Doopliss were unable to damage each other at all).
*When Mario [[Level Up|levels up]], all of his partners get their [[Health Point|health]] refilled, instead of just the active partner.
* When a partner faints in battle, their HP will be set to 1 after the battle if they haven't been revived.
*Mario no longer shakes the bushes to inspect them. Instead, Mario must whack them with his hammer.
*Partner hints have been added. By pressing the {{button|switch|ZL}} button, one of Mario's partners will now give the player advice on what to do next. During the main story, these hints will usually be given by [[Goombella]] unless there is a certain puzzle requiring a specific partner. During the postgame, partner hints will have various partners give Mario ideas on what to do next.
* The [[action command]] for [[Power Bounce]] is harder to execute, with the fourth bounce being frame perfect. <ref>"[https://www.gamesradar.com/games/super-mario/its-not-just-you-speedrunners-confirm-an-essential-paper-mario-the-thousand-year-door-move-is-way-harder-in-the-rpg-remake/ It's not just you - speedrunners confirm an essential Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door move is way harder in the RPG remake]" on GamesRadar.</ref>

===Other=== 
*At the end of a chapter, the game will say "CLEARED" instead of "END OF CHAPTER" a nod to the original Japanese script.<ref>[https://mynintendonews.com/2024/04/25/preview-paper-mario-the-thousand-year-door-for-nintendo-switch/ Preview: Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door for Nintendo Switch]</ref>
* When hitting a [[Save Block|save block]], the game will no longer ask you if you are sure you want to save and will just save right away.
**Also, after completing a chapter, the game no longer gives the player the option to save before going to Princess Peach and Bowser's sections. It, instead, autosaves between the interludes.
* When [[guard|guarding]] / [[superguard|superguarding]], the game will no longer say 'Nice' and 'Great", but instead 'Guard' and 'Superguard'
*A few locations, characters, etc. have been renamed:
**[[Rogueport Sewers]] is known as "Rogueport Underground."
**The [[Shadow Sirens]] are now known as "The Three Shadows."
**[[Riddle Tower]] is now called the "Palace of Shadow Tower."
**[[Squeek]]s are now referred to as "Little Mousers".
**[[Sky-Blue Pipe]]s have been renamed to "Sky-Blue Spiny Eggs", addressing a mistranslation from the original.
*A few characters that had multiple names in the original are now consistently referred to by one name.
**The [[Armored Harriers]] (also referred to as the "Iron Adonis Twins") are now consistently referred to as "The Iron Adonis Twins".
**The [[Red Spike Buzzy]] (also referred to as a "Red Spike Top") is now consistently referred to as "Red Spike Top".
**The [[Spiky Goomba]] (once referred to as a "Spiny Goomba" by [[Professor Frankly]] during the tutorial battle) is now consistently referred to as a "Spiky Goomba".
*In the RDM forms, it no longer lists the ages of the characters that were involved in a certain article.
*The player can no longer name their save file, much like every ''Paper Mario'' game since ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''.
*The dialogue was translated to be more faithful, matching the script from the Japanese version. For example, the Japanese dialogue implied that [[Vivian]] is transgender during a playthrough, but this statement isn't seen in the German and English releases. Despite that, the translators for the game included this implication in the Nintendo Switch version internationally.
*The Goomba Trio in [[Rogueport Underground]] (the [[Goomba]], [[Spiky Goomba]], and [[Paragoomba]]) no longer initiates a battle with [[Mario]] and [[Goombella]] by catcalling Goombella like in the original, instead they now pick a fight over their resentment towards "above ground" people entering their underground turf.
*Bonetail's gender has been changed from being male in the original version to now being female in this version.
*[[Hooktail]] is consistently referred to as a female in the remake, in the original, she wasn’t revealed to be a female until [[Mario]] and his friends met [[Gloomtail]] and prior to then, all the citizens of [[Petalburg]] (along with Mario’s partners) referred to Hooktail as an "it" or a "he". 

==Trivia==

*The game was released on May 23, 2024, a Thursday, while most Nintendo games release on Fridays; the last Mario game to not release on a Friday was [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']], which released on October 31, 2019.
**[[Luigi's Mansion 2 HD|''Luigi's Mansion 2 HD'']], a game which had its release date revealed in the same video, was also released on a Thursday.
* Much like ''[[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|Super Mario RPG]]'', the cover art is based off the original Japanese box art.
*This is one of very few Mario games where the title screen changes throughout the game (as it updates whenever Mario gains a new partner). Another Mario game that has notably done this is ''[[Mario Party]]'' where the title screen changes depending which character won the previous board.
*An error message "A System Update is required to use this card." bricks the switch cartridge game by default preventing the game ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' from working on the offline Nintendo Switch consoles not connected online and can only work if the game is connected to the Eshop store to unlock the bricked game.
