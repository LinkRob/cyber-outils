The '''Dream World''' is a major location that appears in [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]]. It is a dream area equivalent to the [[Mushroom Earth|real world]] and is home to dreamy-like residents from [[Pi'illo Island]].

==Layout==
Much like [[Bowser's Body]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', the Dream World is shown from a 2D perspective.

One of the areas is [[Dreamy Pi'illo Castle]], the Dream World equivalent of [[Pi'illo Castle]]. It can be accessed by the pillow located in the large room of Pi'illo Castle. The boss of the location is [[Dreamy Mario]].
{{Sectstub}}

== History ==

=== ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' ===
[[File:Dream World area.png|thumb|left|An area within the Dream World]]
The Dream World can be accessed by [[Mario]] while [[Luigi]] sleeps on a special type of [[pillow]], causing a [[Dream Portal]] to appear. This way, Mario (or another character) can jump into the Dream Portal to enter the Dream World equivalent of the current location. Mario is assisted by [[Dreamy Luigi]] whenever he is in the Dream World.
