The '''Ribbit Stick''' is a weapon used by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a stronger variation of the [[Froggie Stick]], which it even shares a sprite with. The Ribbit Stick can be bought from [[Seaside Town]] for 86 [[coin]]s, but only after it is no longer occupied by [[Yaridovich]]. The Ribbit Stick has an [[Attack]] power of 50.
