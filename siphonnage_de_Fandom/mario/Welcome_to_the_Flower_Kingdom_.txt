'''Welcome to the Flower Kingdom!''' is the first [[level]] of [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch 2]] port ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park]]'', let alone the game itself.

==Layout==
It starts with some [[Brick Block]]s and some [[? Block]]s. Then after that is a tree with 2 [[Coin]]s and a [[Flower Coin]], below the tree is a [[Pipe]] with a [[Goomba]] on top. While those are there, there are 2 [[Goomba]]s sitting next to each other, above the 2 [[Goomba]]s is a [[1-Up Mushroom]] in a [[Hidden Block]]. Next to that is 2 [[Coin]]s above some more [[Brick Block]]s with an [[? Block]] with the new [[Elephant Fruit]]. To the right is some coins and a [[Goomba]]. Then there’s a water fountain that deposits the Elephant Fruit. After that is a Goomba and a tree with some more coins. Then there’s some water pipes also with a Goomba and a tree. To the right is 3 Goombas and a lot of pipes but in the middle of the pipes is the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]]. Along the way are some [[Flower Coin]]s, then you come across the first [[Wonder Seed]]. Some pipes lead you to the [[Goal Pole]] and you save the [[Poplin]].

== [[10-Flower Coin]] locations ==
The first coin is found under an arch made of [[Brick Block|Brick Blocks]], directly below a [[? Block]]. The player must use an [[Elephant Fruit]] to break said Brick Blocks and obtain the first 10-flower coin.

The second coin is easily accessible on a tree next to a slope and some [[Pipe|Pipes]]. 

The last coin is found during the [[Wonder Effect]] of the level. The player must ride an [[Inchworm Pipe]] and jump off it onto a tree. From there, the player can jump over and collect the coin. If the Wonder Effect is not activated, the player must activate [[Wow Bud|Wow Buds]] in order to obtain the coin.{{SMBWNav}}
