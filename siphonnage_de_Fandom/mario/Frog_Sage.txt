The '''Frog Sage''', also named {{Nihongo|'''Frogfucius'''|カエル仙人|Kaeru Sennin}}, is a [[frog]] character in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He is the adoptive grandfather of [[Mallow]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Frog Sage is an elderly frog with a large, white beard. He also wears a strange, purple hat.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
The Frog Sage plays a rather important role. Prior to the events of the game, the Frog Sage discovers a young Mallow floating across the river of [[Tadpole Pond]]. Not wanting to let the baby go, the Frog Sage takes in the young Mallow and raises him as his own grandson.

During the current events of the game, the Frog Sage gave Mallow a [[Frog Coin]] and tasked him with buying a [[Cricket Pie]], which the Frog Sage planned to eat. However, Mallow ran into issues as the Frog Coin was soon stolen by the thief [[Croco]]. Thanks to [[Mario]], Mallow was able to get back the Frog Coin and purchase the Cricket Pie for the Frog Sage. After making their way through [[Kero Sewers]] and down [[Midas River]], Mario and Mallow make it back to Tadpole Pond to speak with the Frog Sage. However, the Frog Sage then decided to tell Mallow the truth about his heritage and that he isn't really a frog and he found him floating down Midas River one day in a basket. This revelation upsets both the Frog Sage and Mallow, and the Frog Sage urges Mario to help Mallow find his real parents. the Frog Sage will remain in a depressive state until Mario gives him the Cricket Pie which cheers him back up.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Frogfucius' name is a play on that of the famous Chinese philosopher, {{w|Confucius}}, but with "frog" added as a prefix as a reference to his species.
