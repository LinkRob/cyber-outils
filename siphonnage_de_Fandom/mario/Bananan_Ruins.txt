'''Bananan Ruins''' is a course which appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Layout==
The course begins within a jungle. Following a sharp turn to the left, racers will be lead under a overhang, which then leads to an area within the entryway into the ruins. After being lead through the ruins, racers will drive drive up some stairs, which then brings them to a hairpin curve that leads out of the ruins.

The route then leads through a high area above ground. Following this, racers will then descend into some more ruins. Inside these ruins are water puddles that will temporarily slow racers down. From here, racers will then be led into the jungle again, where they'll approach another hairpin that returns racers to the starting line.

==Trivia==
*This course shares the same layout as [[Omatsuri Circuit]] from ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.
*In rounds 3 and 4, rain will occur, and racers will have access to different items.
