'''Fattingtons''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Fattingtons are obese, round enemies resembling a two large scoops of ice cream and a small, white scoop on top showing their face. A Fattington's face consists of two pink eyes and a pink mouth that somewhat appears to be smiling.

==In ''Wario World''==
Fattingtons are only encountered in [[Shivering Mountains]]. Whenever a Fattington drops onto the ground, it shakes. Because of a Fattington's round body, [[Wario]]'s punches are ineffective against them, and punching or even touching a Fattington bounces Wario back, putting him at risk of falling off the stage. Wario can stun a Fattington by grabbing either a [[column]], a [[statue]], or an enemy, such as an [[Icicle Mite]], and then throw it at the Fattington. Wario can then pick up the Fattington and defeat it using a [[Mad Move]].
