'''Stamina Juice''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing [[Inky Sauce]] with a [[Honey Jar]]. This power-up drink gradually restores [[Heart Point|HP]] over time.
