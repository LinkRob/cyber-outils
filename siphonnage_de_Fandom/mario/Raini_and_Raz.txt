'''Raini and Raz''' are a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] couple in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Raini has fuchsia spots on her head and wears a blue dress, while Raz has blue spots on his head. Together, the two of them live in the [[Toad Town|Mushroom Kingdom]].

==History==
When [[Mario]] first comes to the Mushroom Kingdom, Raini tells Mario that she and Raz are going to get married. Unfortunately, the peaceful city gets invaded by [[Mack]], a member of the [[Smithy Gang]].

After Mario and his newly obtained partner, [[Mallow]], defeat Mack, Raz and Raini tell Mario that they are going to get married in [[Marrymore]].

During their wedding in Marrymore, [[Booster (character)|Booster]] comes in and kicks everyone out so that he and [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] can get married. After Mario saves the Princess, Raz and Raini finally get married. You can see them on their honeymoon on [[Yo'ster Isle]].
