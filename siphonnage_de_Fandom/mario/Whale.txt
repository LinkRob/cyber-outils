'''Whales''' are real-world animals which sometimes appear in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'', serving as both an enemy and obstacle, and retains this role in its overseas version, ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== Profile ==
They appear to be purple Sperm Whales. They blow sprouts of air every few seconds. Whales only appear in [[World 4-2 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 4-2]] after you go through the first door. Whales are harmless, although their sprout of air can harm players. If you land on the blowhole (where the sprout comes up) you won't get hurt, but you will be sent upwards. Only if you touch the sprout from the side you can get hurt. Whales tails are hard. Trying to get an [[Ace Coin]], [[Mushroom]], or [[1-Up]] on its tail could harm the player if they are not careful.

=== Attacking ===
If you use a [[Starman]] on the Whale's sprout, the sprout will stop. A [[Vegetable]] does not affect the sprout. Whales cannot be defeated, nor do they go back in the sea. It is probably because they are like platforms.
