'''Bangkok Rush 2''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is the second of the three layouts of [[Bangkok Rush]], making its debut in the 2022 Winter Tour alongside [[Bangkok Rush 3]]. The locations visited are a parking garage known as the Esplanade Ratchadaphisek and a night market.

== Layout ==
Bangkok Rush 2's starting position matches [[Bangkok Rush 1|the first route]], starting in the same place and facing the same direction, but the racers make two right turns down a street, followed by a left turn down a marketplace. Afterwards, they make a curve to the right, coming across a fork in the road. The racers must turn left into the parking garage, making a 270-degree left turn as they head up to a higher floor. At that floor, the racers encounter a drop and can either bounce on the tents in their path or drive underneath. A right turn down the Ratchadaphisek Road leads back to the Bangkok Rush 1 route, which the racers take a right onto the road beneath the sky train to align their route with. There is a staircase at the second right turn involving this road. A left turn followed by a right turn leads them back to the [[Finish line|starting line]].

''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' has this route for the final lap of [[Bangkok Rush]], but the racers align themselves for [[Bangkok Rush 3]] (the focus of lap 2) by going down this route until the market.
