A '''Crown''' is an object featured in a few [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' games]] in which it denotes whoever is in the lead. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', particularly in multiplayer mode, in which whoever scores the most [[point]]s in a [[level]] earns a Crown afterward.
