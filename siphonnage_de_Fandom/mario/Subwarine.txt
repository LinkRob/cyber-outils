The '''Subwarine''' is a vehicle in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Description==
The Subwarine is used by [[Wario]] in underwater levels. It has the ability to fire missiles, which can be done by pressing {{button|wii|1}}, but only three can be present onscreen at a time. To move the Subwarine up and down, the [[Wii Remote]] must be titled diagonally, and using left or right on the D-Pad moves it back and forth. The machine starts to break down if Wario loses half his hearts.
