A '''P-Acorn''' is a power-up item which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and its [[reissue|enhanced port]] ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. It is the [[P-Wing]] equivalent to the [[Super Acorn]], and touching a P-Acorn turns [[Mario]] into [[P Flying Squirrel Mario]], which is similar to the [[Flying Squirrel Mario|Flying Squirrel]] form except Mario can do an unlimited number of midair jumps in succession.
