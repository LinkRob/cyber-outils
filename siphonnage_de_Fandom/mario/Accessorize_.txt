'''Accessorize!''' is a quest in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Description ==
The quest begins at [[Goombob Manor]], after the player's character agrees to fulfill a favor for [[Goombob]]. He proceeds to explain that he is looking for a [[Lovely Seashell]], which he wants to give as a gift for [[Goombetty]], and offers a reward if the player helps him. He suggests that they look for the seashell at a beach. More specifically, the player's character can obtain the Lovely Seashell at the [[Sandy Beach]].

Once the player's character returns to Goombob with the Lovely Seashell, Goombetty unexpectedly shows up, and Goombob gifts it to her. Goombetty agrees to Goombob's proposal to go on a date, and the quest ends with him rewarding the player with the [[Dessert Menu]] [[Gaddget]].
