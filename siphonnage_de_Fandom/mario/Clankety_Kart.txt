'''Clankety Kart''' is a [[Kart]] that apears in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']].

== In [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] ==
The kart was first introduced in [[Exploration Tour (2020)]] as a High-End kart. Clankety Kart is one of the [[Clanky Kart]] models that is coverd with silvers. Unlike the Clanky Kart, this kart increases the duration of [[Mini-Turbo]] speed boosts and the amount of points earned for performing Mini-Turbos.
