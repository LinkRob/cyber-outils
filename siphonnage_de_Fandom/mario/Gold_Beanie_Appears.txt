'''Gold Beanie Appears''' is the sixth level of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 13.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 6 [[Chuck Guy]]s and 2 [[Fly Guy]]s. The enemy captain is a Chuck Guy.

The 2nd battle contains 7 Chuck Guys and 1 Fly Guy. The enemy captain is the Fly Guy.

The final battle contains 4 Chuck Guy, 3 Fly Guys, and 1 Gold Beanie. The enemy captain is the Gold Beanie.

The player recruits 2 Fly Guy after the final battle.

The battle can be replayed to recruit up to 2 more Fly Guys each time.
