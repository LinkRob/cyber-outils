'''''Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park''''' is an enhanced port of ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' released worldwide for the [[Nintendo Switch 2]] on March 26, 2026, as part of the [[Super Mario Bros. 40th Anniversary|''Super Mario Bros.'' 40th Anniversary]] celebration. First announced during the September 2025 Nintendo Direct, the port includes new multiplayer co-op and competitive game modes along with the appearance of characters such as [[Rosalina]] and [[Co-Star Luma]], the [[Koopalings]], the [[Baby Yoshi|Baby Yoshis]], and the [[Toad Brigade]].

== New content ==

===Playable characters===
[[File:SMBW 14 Characters.png|left|thumb|All 12 original characters return, along with [[Rosalina]] and [[Co-Star Luma]]]]
Two new playable characters have been added, [[Rosalina]] (along with her [[Luma]]) and [[Co-Star Luma]]. Rosalina plays the same as the other normal characters ([[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], [[Yellow Toad]], [[Blue Toad]], and [[Toadette]]), whereas Co-Star Luma does not take damage from enemies  (similarly to the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] and [[Nabbit]]), can freely fly around the screen, spin to damage enemies, and can only be played as if there are two or more players in the game. Co-Star Luma can also be controlled using the Joy-Con 2 Mouse Controls, allowing for easier flying across the level.

=== Bosses ===
The Koopalings return as bosses. Upon entering Bellabel Park, the Koopalings steal the seven Bellabel Flowers from Bellabel Park, and Captain Toad and the Recruit Poplin help the player find them. Each Koopaling hides in a different world of the Flower Kingdom, which now features a new level type, called Brigade Tent. These levels are remixed versions of older levels where the Koopalings use Wonder powers to transform into different forms, and the player must proceed through the level while constantly being attacked, before battling the corresponding Koopaling at the end. Upon defeating them, they are rewarded with a Bellabel Flower.

After Ludwig is defeated, harder versions of these bosses become available as challenges in the Toad Brigade Training Camp.

=== Other changes ===

==== Gameplay ====

* An Assist Mode has been added to the game. It prevents the player from taking any damage and allows them to float with a Propeller Flower after falling into a pit.
* The functionality of amiibo when scanned in a course is altered, with the Poplin & Prince Florian amiibo and general ''Super Mario''-series amiibo (including Mario Sports Superstars cards) now providing a Super Flower Pot and other amiibo now providing a Bubble Flower.
* Hidden Character Block placements in levels are adjusted to accomodate the addition of Rosalina.
* The staircase made of Hard Blocks, the Goal Pole, and the Poplin House at the secret exit of Piranha Plants on Parade have been moved to the right.
* The range for pulling handles has been increased.
* The Settings menu received more options:
** "Special Move Controls" can be toggled between "{{Button|ns|zr}}" and "{{Button|ns|r}}/{{Button|ns|zr}}". It controls which button(s) are used for Spin Jumps, Midair Spins, and certain Badge abilities.
** "Local Multiplayer Crown" can be toggled between "Swap Between Players", which is how it is in the Nintendo Switch edition, and "Prioritize Player One", which always gives player one the crown unless they are a ghost.
** Local Multiplayer Off-Screen Behavior" can be toggled between "Revive at a Player" and "Become Ghost".
** "Local Multiplayer Camera" can be toggled between "Normal", which is how it is in the Nintendo Switch edition, and "Wide". It controls the screen extents when playing in local multiplayer.
** "Online and Local Wireless Camera" can be toggled between "Normal", which is how it is in the Nintendo Switch edition, and "Wide". It controls the screen extents when playing online, in single player, and local wireless.
*** Enabling this allows a new block type to be viewed in Search Party An Empty Park? and Timer-Switch Climb.

==== Aesthetic ====

* Rosalina and Luma are added to the base game's opening and ending cutscenes.
** The Starshroom that they crashed can now be seen in the background of the opening cutscene as well as Pipe-Rock Plateau's world map.
* Several levels in the base game now have an Easter egg where Pikmin and pixel Prince Florians can be found in the background.
* World map:
** The Koopalings' crashed Koopa Clown Car can be seen in Pipe-Rock Plateau's world map after defeating them in their first encounter.
*** A cloud that would obscure it was removed.
** The locations in the world map where Captain Toad could be found in the base game are instead populated by various members of the Toad Brigade.
** The loading screen when traveling between levels now shows the currently used character(s) riding the Propeller Flower, as opposed to always showing [[Mario]].
*** The sprites of the characters on the Propeller Flower are less animated than Mario's in the Nintendo Switch Edition.
** The world map now features tiny details relating to the Recruit Poplin's reports.
* Kingdom map:
** The entrance to Bellabel Park was added to the Kingdom map.
** A paper map of Bellabel Park can now be seen above [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]].
* Characters have a new animation for remaining idle with the Jet Badge equipped, which plays when either on a stand-still at the beginning of the level, running into a wall, or landing on the ground from a pipe without moving.
* The UI for the character selection screen has a few changes:
** The background that the characters sit on is now entirely gray, rather than having the Yoshis and Nabbit's portion of it being dark teal.
** The text box that originally said ''"Yoshis and Nabbit won't take damage, but they also won't transform with power-ups."'' now says ''"Yoshi and Nabbit do not take damage and will not transform with power-ups."'' Additionally, the text box has been moved to the bottom of the screen rather than right underneath the Yoshis and Nabbit's character icons.
** Player number icons are now color coded by character instead of P1, P2, P3, and P4 being red, blue, green, and yellow respectively.
* The icon for "Player Options" is now four horizontal Joy-Con; in the Nintendo Switch edition, it is a single Joy-Con oriented diagonally.
* Several minor decorations in courses have either been added, removed, or relocated.
* A visual oversight in the level Missile Meg Mayhem has been fixed; the upper-left clouds in the area above where the Wonder Seed is obtained were originally abruptly cut off. This was fixed by adding a layer of pink clouds in front of it, though it is likely that this means the original clouds are still cut off but it is now just no longer visible.
* The text in the opening and world clear cutscenes are now narrated by the Talking Flower.
* The sound that plays when stomping an enemy that cannot be damaged from the stomp, like Mumsies and Revvers, now only plays every second bounce.

==== Miscellaneous ====

* The game is now translated to Polish and Thai, two languages the original was not available in, as at the time, Nintendo of Europe did not do Polish translations, and Nintendo Singapore and Nintendo Thailand did not yet exist. However, the Talking Flowers have not been dubbed in those languages, instead defaulting to English audio if "Match system language" is selected in the Talking Flower voice language menu.
* The Talking Flowers' English and Russian voice lines have been rerecorded by Patrick Seitz and Stanislav Shapkin, respectively, their voice actors since ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV]]'', replacing the original lines by Mick Wingert and Anton Kobylko, likely to keep consistency with the new dialogue added. However, it is possible to listen to the original voice lines by booting up the original Nintendo Switch Edition of the game from the main menu.

==== Technical aspects ====

* The game now runs at a resolution of 1080p in handheld mode and 2160p (4K) in docked mode. Most of the fonts and UI elements, however, are still 1080p.
* The game also now supports high dynamic range (HDR).

== Bellabel Park ==
A new location in the Flower Kingdom, Bellabel Park, appears in this game.

=== Attraction Central ===
Attraction Central holds a slew of multiplayer attractions that can be played both locally and online. The left side of the map, called the '''Local Multiplayer Plaza''', contains attractions for local multiplayer, while the right side of the map, called the '''Game Room Plaza''', contains attractions for multiplayer across multiple systems. Between both attraction plazas is the '''Tour Plaza''', where the Attraction Tour can be played.

==== Local Multiplayer Plaza ====
The Local Multiplayer Plaza contains 17 attractions, which can be played with up to four players on the same system or through GameShare (except for the Captain Toad attraction, which can only be played with two players). The top half of the Plaza contains seven Co-op Attractions, where players work together, while the bottom half contains ten VS attractions, where players compete against each other in both free-for-all and teams format. Some attractions share the same level marker with other similar ones on the World Map, and some attractions contain multiple levels that can be selected, including modified versions of levels from the main game as well as some original stages. Some attractions also allow for certain badges to be equipped, and unlike the main game, each player can equip their own individual badge instead of sharing one.

==== Game Room Plaza ====
The Game Room Plaza contains six attractions which can be played with up to eight players via local wireless, or twelve players via online and LAN play, with every player on their own system.

==== Tour Plaza ====
The Tour Plaza contains the Attraction Tour, which has the players compete in a series of attractions back-to-back, similar to the mode of the same name from ''Nintendo Land''. Before starting a Tour, the format (either Co-Op, VS (Free-for-All), or VS (Team), the difficulty, and the number of rounds played can be set.

=== Camp Central ===
[[File:1600px-SMBW NS2E Camp Central.png|thumb|251x251px|Camp Central]]
Camp Central is another explorable area in Bellabel Park. It consists of a single circular area and features the Toad Brigade Training Camp, as well as a large archway which holds seven Bellabel Flowers, along with a larger central one.

==== Toad Brigade Training Camp ====
At the Toad Brigade Training Camp, players can talk to Brigade Blue Toad to take on extra challenges set in courses from the main game. Completing challenges awards the player with a new currency named Bellabel Water and patches which represent the player's rank in the Toad Brigade. As the player completes challenges, they will rank up, unlocking additional challenges. Some challenges start with the player character in a specific form or equipped with a specific badge.

== amiibo ==
[[File:SMBW amiibo.png|right|frameless]]As with the base game, scanning any amiibo while in a course will provide the player with a power-up. While the Elephant Mario and Captain Toad & Talking Flower amiibo still provide an Elephant Fruit and Drill Mushroom respectively, the power-ups received for all other amiibo differ from those of the base game. Scanning the Poplin & Prince Florian amiibo, all other amiibo in the ''Super Mario'' line, or ''Mario Sports Superstars'' amiibo cards now provides a Super Flower Pot instead of a Bubble Flower, with a Bubble Flower now being given for scanning any unsupported amiibo instead of a Fire Flower.

==== amiibo decorations ====
[[File:1600px-SMBW NS2E Camp Central Amiibo None.jpg|thumb|215x215px]]
By speaking to Florist Poplin, amiibo can be scanned to adjust certain decorations in Camp Central. Using most ''Super Mario'' character amiibo will display a relevant character's emblem on the flower bed at the park's entrance, the player's backpack, flags, and banners, and apply a matching color scheme to elements such as the Toad Brigade tent and the Poplin Band stage. Scanning in an unsupported amiibo will instead randomly theme the decorations to either a Bellabel Flower, Wonder Flower, 1-Up Mushroom, Super Mushroom, Fire Flower, Super Star, Bubble Flower, Drill Mushroom, or Super Flower Pot.

==Trivia==
*The introduction to the Switch 2 edition of Super Mario Bros Wonder consists of the same character group used in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' and [[Super Mario 3D World|''Super Mario 3D World'']]: [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]] and [[Blue Toad]].
*[[Phanto]] returns since its first debut in [[Super Mario Bros. 2|''Super Mario Bros. 2'']].
*This is the first appearance of [[Rosalina]], [[Luma]], and of [[Co-Star Luma]] in a 2D Mario platformer.
