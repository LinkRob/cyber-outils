'''Dashell''' is one of the [[Pixl]]s in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dashell gives the team a great boost in speed. He is unlocked after defeating [[Wracktail]] in the Flipside Pit of 100 Trials, and his [[Catch Card]] is found on the tenth floor of the Flopside Pit of 100 Trials. Dashell thinks fighting is useless and revealed that when he gets in a fight, he flees. When he causes his user to dash, he flashes red.
Dashell, like [[Barry]] and [[Piccolo]], is an optional Pixl, and is not required to beat the game. If the player beats the Flipside Pit of 100 Trials and gets Dashell, while [[Peach]] and [[Francis (Super Paper Mario)|Francis]] are on the [[Swoon.exe]], Dashell is not listed in the Pixl section.

==Etymology==
Dashell's came from a word "dash," since he has a boost of speed.
