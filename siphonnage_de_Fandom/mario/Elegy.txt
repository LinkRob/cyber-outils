'''Elegy''' is a special move used by a few enemies and bosses in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Elegy is used, cyan-colored music notes fly on the screen in a similar fashion to [[Lulla-Bye]].

==Effects==
With Elegy, the enemy is able to inflict the [[Mute]] status on its victim.
