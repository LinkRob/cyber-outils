'''''Wario Ware: Smooth Moves''''', (otherwise known as '''''Odoru Made in Wario''''' in Japan), is the fifth game in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It was developed by [[Nintendo SPD]]<ref name="IGN Interview">[http://wii.ign.com/articles/710/710610p1.html IGN: ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'' Interview]</ref> and was released in Japan on December 2, 2006, and in Europe and North America on January 12, 2007 and January 15, 2007 respectively. The game was the first game in the Mario franchise to be released on the [[Wii]].

This game takes full advantage of the [[Wii Remote]].
