'''Mandibugs''' are a common enemy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They first appeared in the [[Honeyhive Galaxy]] and can also be found in [[Honeyclimb Galaxy]]. They also appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' in the [[Puzzle Plank Galaxy]]. They are lead by their leader [[Bugaboom]].

== Description ==
Mandibugs appear as purple stag beetle-like creatures, with a white explosion mark on their back (which serves as a hint as to how to defeat them). Their head is yellow with a mouth with prominent mandibles which can retract when the Mandibug is in their "peaceful state" and can extend for a nasty bite in their "aggravated" state.

==Strategy==
When they see Mario approaching, Mandibugs run in a straight line towards Mario, trying to run into him and bite him. Their attacks can be easily avoided by running to the side. To defeat them, Mario must use his [[Ground Pound]] on top of them to strike the explosion mark. In both games there is a special Mandibug. That Mandibug has a smaller Mandibug on the top of it which is protecting the big Mandibug. That means you have to defeat the smaller Mandibug first and then defeat the big one. After that a star will be revealed.

== History ==

=== In ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ===
Mandibugs make their debut in the first [[Super Mario Galaxy|''Super Mario Galaxy'']], where Mario can encounter them in the [[Honeyhive Galaxy]] in all major missions except Bee Mario Takes Flight. A bee who lives there describes the Mandibugs as invaders from another planet in Trouble on the Tower. In the third mission, Big Bad Bugaboom, Mandibugs overrun the kingdom, and Mario has to find and defeat their leader to stop the invasion.

Mandibugs make a return in [[Bowser Jr.'s Airship Armada]] in which only one is on the bridge near the cannon gauntlet, several Mandibugs are seen again in the [[Honeyclimb Galaxy]] these Mandibugs are gravitationally clinging to walls.

=== In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' ===
Mandibugs return in [[Super Mario Galaxy 2|''Super Mario Galaxy 2'']], retaining their strategy from the original game, but only one of them appears in the [[Puzzle Plank Galaxy]]. It drops one [[Coin]] when defeated. A green-shelled Mandibug also appears as an engraving on the titular block in The Puzzling Picture Block. Their leader also appears in the secret mission.

=== In ''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'' ===
A few friendly Mandibugs appear in [[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie|''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'']], acting as steeds and laborers for the bees. Their elytra is also shown to be able to open in order to fly.

==Behind the Scenes==
Mandibugs are based on stag beetles. Their name is a transfusion of "mandible" - the scientific name for the jaw - and from "bug".
