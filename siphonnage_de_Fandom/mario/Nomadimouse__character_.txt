==History==
He is an unnamed [[Nomadimouse]] that can be seen in [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]] on a path sitting under a tree. He does this to shelter from the blazing heat of the desert. A [[letter]] can be delivered to him which can be found on a roof in [[Dry Dry Outpost]]. This letter is addressed from [[Moustafa]], although the Nomadimouse states that he has never heard of him before. Regardless, the Nomaidmouse is grateful for receiving the letter and gives Mario a [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]] for his troubles.
