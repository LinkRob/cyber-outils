The '''All Cup Tour''' is a special, unlockable fifth cup in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' It is unlocked by placing first in the 150cc [[Special Cup]].

Unlike the other four cups, the All Cup Tour has the player racing on all sixteen tracks of the game (the cup's logo, which consists of each of the other cups' logos inside a circle, reflects this). Each track that the player races on is chosen in a random order, though always starting with [[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Luigi Circuit]] (the first course of the game) and always ending with [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Rainbow Road]] (the final course of the game).

Completing the All Cup Tour in 150cc unlocks [[Mario Kart#Modes of play|Mirror Mode]]. Beating the All Cup Tour in Mirror Mode unlocks the [[Parade Kart]]. Completing it in 50cc and 100cc do not unlock anything specific, but they are both necessary in addition to the other engine classes to unlock the special title and ending screens.
{{br|left}}

==Other games==
In ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', the player can mimic the properties of the All Cup Tour in VS mode by setting the course selection to Random and the number of races to 32 (48 in ''Mario Kart 8'' if both DLC packs were purchased, or if the base game of ''8 Deluxe'' is played). However, with the lack of a 96-race option for the ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe – Booster Course Pass|Booster Course Pass]]'', this is no longer the case for ''8 Deluxe''.
