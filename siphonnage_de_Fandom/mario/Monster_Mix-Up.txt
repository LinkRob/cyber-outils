'''''Monster Mix-Up''''' is the third book of the ''[[Nintendo Adventure Books]]'' series. It was published some time in July 1991. It involves [[Bowser]] using one of his inventions, the [[Monster Mixer]], to create enhanced [[Koopa]]s by combining them with various other creatures.
