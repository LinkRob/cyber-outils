Shake your soda and share it with them!"|forms = [[The Thumb Wrestler]]}}'''Spray It, Don't Say It''' is one of [[Ashley]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to shake the bottle the amount of times expected. When the bottle is about to blow, point the [[Wii Remote]] to the people.
*'''Level 1:''' Two shakes. Three people are standing on the podium.
*'''Level 2: '''Three shakes. Four people are team players.
*'''Level 3:''' Four shakes. Five people are on the ground, which they become musclemen afterwards.
