'''The Cave Of Chomp Rock''' is the third level of [[World 1 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 1]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and the Game Boy Advance version, [[Super Mario Advance 3: Yoshi's Island|''Super Mario Advance 3: Yoshi's'' ''Island'']]. Its name is possibly because of the [[Chomp Rock|rocks]] that look like [[Chain Chomp]]s.

== Layout ==
The player starts in an overworld location with a Piranha Plants on each of the two sides. The player can hit both of them with a [[Yoshi Egg]], but only the left Piranha Plant will give the player a [[Coin|gold coin]]. The player needs to go down the Green Warp Pipe. After going down the Warp Pipe, they will be in an underground location. A Chomp Rock will roll until it hits a post. Next to the post there is a box, if the player ground pounds on it, 5 stars will come out. The player must continue moving until they get to the second post. They must get the two coins which are red coins. They must then ground pound on the post twice to get another red coin. They must then go further to where four Nipper Plants are on the ground and there are lots of coins (some are red). The player then needs to go down a pit, and keep going until they get to one flower and two red coins. The player must then get ot of the pit and go further to where the second Chomp Rock is. They must roll it, and follow where it rolls. Then it will hit the third Chomp Rock so the player has to follow the third one. Until it gets to a place where there are four coins (some are red), a question box and a Nipper Plant. The player needs to get the coins and hit the question box with an egg to make 5 stars come out. They must go left, go inside the bottom tunnel, and get two red coins and one flower. The player must then go back up and get the Middle Ring. This will give them 10 extra stars. They must then continue and go to the place where Nipper Plants will start parachuting down. If they go a little further, they will see floating balloons with a box. If the player throws and egg at the box and then ground pounds the box, 5 stars will come out. There will be a balloon with a key, so the player can throw and egg at it, if they want. The key will then unlock the locked door. The player must then continue to where three poles are. The player must ground pound the middle pole three times to get a red coin and the right pole need three ground pounds too. If the player gets the six coins a little further, they will get two red coins. The player must continue, there will be a Warp Pipe which spawns Lantern Ghosts, if Yoshi turns them all into eggs, they will stop spawning. The player can go down the Warp Pipe, but there will be nothing there. The player must go right and continue until they get to a flower, the player can thow an egg at it to get it, and somewhere in the white blocks, there will be a question cloud that the player can get by throwing an egg at it. The player must go to where the next pole is and ground pound it two times. There will be a question cloud that the player can hit. The player can then go down where the red arrow is (after hitting the red switch). They will go to a room with lots of coins and can exit by jumping on the bouncy arrows. The player (after exiting if they went to the coin room) must continue and roll the Chomp Rock and jump on it so that they can reach the high place. The last flower and eight coins (some are red) will be there. The player must then go past the red arrow and they will be overworld again. If they continue and avoid the Piranha Plants, they can get to the goal. Orange Yoshi will catch Mario and if they get lucky (if they got one or more flowers) they will get a flower on the goal which will give them the bonus game.
