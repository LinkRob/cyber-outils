A '''Lucky Mini-game''' is a special mini-game type that appears in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. When this occurs, the player will play the mini-game like they do normally except that when they win, they will earn double or possibly triple the winnings for a mini-game (including Bonus mini-games). But if it is on 1x, then the player gets to play the mini-game normally.
