'''Bulbers''' are angler fish enemies in the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]] and'' are encountered in [[World 8-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-4]]. They also appear in the water regions of ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. 

== In [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' series]] ==
Bulbers are encountered underwater, where they use their angler to light up dark areas.

== In ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' ==
Bulbers appear as bouncy obstacles in the water regions the player must navigate around.
