'''Teehee Butterflies''' are a butterfly-like enemy which appear in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. 

==Behavior==
A Teehee Butterfly flies around, changing from pink to blue and vice versa. Eating a pink Tehee Butterfly restores a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s health, but eating a blue one depletes it which, according to a [[Message Block]], is due to its bitter taste. A Yoshi can also bounce off a Teehee Butterfly, effectively using it as a [[springboard]].
