{{Mentioned}}The '''Blorb Mushroom''' is a mushroom said to be created by [[Fawful]] during the events of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]. ''Not much is known about this item, or if it even exists. However, during a meeting at [[Peach's Castle]], it was mentioned by [[Toadbert]] that he had interviewd some blorbed [[Toad (species)|Toads]] and they all said they had bought blorb mushrooms. Later in the game, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] collected every [[Star Cure]] and created the Miracle Cure, and restored every blorbed Toad to normal.
