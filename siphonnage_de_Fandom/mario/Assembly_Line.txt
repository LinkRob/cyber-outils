'''Assembly Line''' is a [[microgame]] within the [[Reality]] genre in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to move the falling car body so that it falls onto the wheels on the ground. Once landed on to, the car drives away, and the player wins at the microgame. If the car body fails to land properly onto the ground, the wheels bounce off, and the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
* '''Level 1:''' The player must assemble a green jeep to a pair of big car tires.
* '''Level 2:''' The player must assemble a blue car to a pair of tires smaller than the jeep's.
* '''Level 3:''' The player must assemble a white van with very small tires.
