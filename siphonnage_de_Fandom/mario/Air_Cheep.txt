'''Air Cheeps''' are a variant of [[Cheep Cheep]] which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. They are under the influence of the [[Dark Star (character)|Dark Star]]. Air Cheeps are only encountered in the [[Airway (Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story)|Airway]] of [[Bowser's body]], so they are only fought by either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]].

==Physical description==
As their name implies, Air Cheeps are purple Cheep Cheeps with a light purple, round, inflated body. The creature has wings in place of its fins and a yellow cork on its head to keep the air from flowing out of the body. It has a black star-shaped pattern around its eyes to indicate its loyalty to the Dark Star.

== Combat ==
Air Cheeps cannot be hurt by the regular [[Hammer]], the [[Green Shell (move)|Green Shell]] [[Special Attack]], or the [[Snack Basket]]'s shockwave due to their ability to fly in their normal form. Other attacks, like the standard [[Jump]], can hit them, however. In the event that it loses its cork, the Air Cheep can be hit by just about any attack.

Air Cheeps can fly above their target to fall on him or puff up and roll backwards into him; this must be countered by smashing them via the [[Hammer]]. The Air Cheep's first attack can be performed multiple times, especially if their target fails to counterattack. If their cork is smashed during their second attack, the creatures deflate. When this happens, they fall to the ground and get the [[Dizzy]] status effect, which wears off when the creatures replace their cork.

An Air Cheep is a Level 27 monster with 236 [[Heart Point|hit points]], an [[Power|Attack Power]] of 126, a [[Defense]] of 88, and a [[Speed]] of 121, with double chances of getting [[Dizzy|stunned]] and regular chances of being [[Burn|burned]] or [[Stat-Down|getting weakened attributes]] and taking normal damage from fire. When slain, it gives 600 [[Experience Point|Experience]] and 75 [[Coin|Coins]]. Its loot has a 3 in 20 chance to have a [[Mushroom (item)|Max Mushroom]] and a 3 in 10 chance to have a [[Syrup Jar|Max Syrup Jar]].{{stub}}
