The '''Jungle Court''' is an unlockable tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]].

==Description==
The court takes place in a jungle-type setting. It is owned by [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], which is especially indicated from a white "DK" (short for [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] on each player's side. The referee sits on a [[barrel]] while spectating the match. Jungle Court has its ball speed set to Normal and its bounce strength to Strong.

Jungle Court can be unlocked by surpassing the high score in the challenge mode of [[Banana Bunch (minigame)|Banana Bunch]], with which it has the same background music.
