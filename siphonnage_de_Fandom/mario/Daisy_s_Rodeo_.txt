'''Daisy's Rodeo!''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party-e]]''. It can be played by swiping the game's associated [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]] card using an [[e-Reader]].

==Layout==
The minigame features Daisy riding atop a [[Bowser]]-like mechanical bull in the middle of a large stadium.

==Gameplay==
Throughout the minigame, the mechanical bull pushes Daisy in one of three directions. The player must press the button indicated on the screen, each corresponding to a certain direction: {{button|gba|L}} for swinging left; {{button|gba|R}} for swinging right; and {{button|gba|B}} for swinging up. To win, the player has to correctly press whichever button is indicated three times within ten seconds.
