'''The Extras''' are a special menu in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' as well as it’s [[reissue|ports]] on [[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D|the Nintendo 3DS]] and [[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD|the Nintendo Switch]], the Extras include various pieces of content for the game that can be viewed at any time by the player, they are usually unlocked by collecting [[Puzzle Piece (Donkey Kong series)|Puzzle Piece]]s or by defeating bosses. The extras contain four different Galleries, the '''Image Gallery''' which contains concept art of various characters, items, locations in the game as well as [[Donkey Kong Country Returns/Beta elements|certain concepts that were planned for the game]], the '''Music Gallery''' which contains Music from each of the eight worlds in the game, the '''Diorama Gallery''' which includes a model of elements from each of the eight worlds, and the '''Movie Gallery''' which is exclusive to ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD]]'' and includes the major cutscenes for the game.

*Jungle Waterfalls (Image Gallery) - Collect all the Puzzle Pieces in World 1-3 [[Tree Top Bop]].
*[[Screaming Pillar]] (Image Gallery) - Collect all the Puzzle Pieces in World 1-4 [[Sunset Shore]].
*[[Awk]] (Image Gallery) - Collect all the Puzzle Pieces in World 1-5 [[Canopy Cannons]].
*Jungle Statues (Image Gallery) - Collect all the Puzzle Pieces in World 1-6 [[Crazy Cart]].
*[[Jungle]] Music - Complete World 1-B [[Mugly's Mound]]
*[[Jungle]] Diorama - Collect all the Puzzle Pieces in World 1-K [[Platform Panic]] and complete World 1-B [[Mugly's Mound]]

==Trivia==
*The Music Gallery seems to have it's music selection arranged based on the world theming, for example, every world that had an intro on the first level ([[Beach]], [[Ruins]], [[Cliff]], and [[Volcano]]) have four tracks while the worlds that didn’t have intros ([[Jungle]], [[Cave]], [[Forest]], and [[Factory]]) have three tracks.
[[Category:Features in Donkey Kong Country Returns]]
*In most worlds, the image extra unlocked in the first level is always the boss of the previous world, possibly showing that the boss's name is revealed to players only after it has been beaten.
