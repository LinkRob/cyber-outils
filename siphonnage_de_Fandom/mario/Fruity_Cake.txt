}}A '''Fruity Cake''' is a recipe made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

It is made by cooking a [[Peachy Peach]] and a [[Mushroom Crepe]]. When used, it heals 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures poison.
