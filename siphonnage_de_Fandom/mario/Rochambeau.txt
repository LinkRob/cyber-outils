'''Rochambeau''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is obtained by completing the [[Flowers Are a Blast!]] quest.

==Description==
Rochambeau allows the player to play [[wikipedia:Rock paper scissors|rock paper scissors]] with the CPU, useful especially if there is no one for them play it with. When used, the screen randomly shows a hand displaying the sign of a rock, paper, or scissors.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|L}} '''/''' {{button|gba|R}} &ndash; Change hand

==Flavor text==
*"''Got no one to play with? Need to train up? Play a round with your GBA!''"
