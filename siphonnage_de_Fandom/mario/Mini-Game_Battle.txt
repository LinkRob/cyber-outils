'''Mini-Game Battle''' is a game mode in ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' and a sub-mode of [[Mini-Game Stadium]]. The mode is unlockable in [[Mini-Game Land]]. It is unlocked by buying at least three [[Minigame#4-Player_minigames|4-Player]], [[Minigame#2_vs._2_minigames|2-vs-2]], and [[Minigame#1_vs._3_minigames|1-vs-3]] Minigames.
[[File:Mini-Game Battle Win.png|thumb|left|200px|[[Mario]] being declared as the winner.]]
Here, the player's character can battle other characters in multiple minigames. At the start, the player can choose the match rules (3-Win, 5-Win, or 7-Win) and the category of the minigames (4-Player, 2-vs-2, 1-vs-3, or [[Minigame#Battle_minigames|Battle]]). A random minigame from the specific category will be chosen, and all the players will start the minigame. After every minigame, the winner is rewarded with a cup. The first player to get the specified number of cups wins, and is put under the spotlight and declared the winner.
