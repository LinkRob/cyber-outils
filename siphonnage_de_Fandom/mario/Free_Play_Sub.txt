The '''Free Play Sub''' is a submarine that docks on the [[MSS Sea Star]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. Here, the player can play any [[minigame]]s they want.  There are also ways to play collections of minigames, such as Decathlon Castle and Waterfall. There is also a figurine of it in the [[Duty-Free Shop]]. The sub's interior has a window showing clips from the currently selected minigame, or if a minigame the player does not have is selected, it shows underwater scenery.
