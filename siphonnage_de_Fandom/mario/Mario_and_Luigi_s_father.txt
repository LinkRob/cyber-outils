'''Papa Mario''' is a minor character in [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]. He is voiced by [[Charles Martinet]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
His appearance resembles his sons, [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]] with brown hair, eyebrows and moustaches.

=== Personality ===
At first, he isn't supportive of his sons' decision to forego their high-paying job at the construction company to start their own business. He also shows some protectiveness towards Luigi, saying that Mario's decisions to start their own business was dragging him down with him. This might be because Luigi used to be bullied back when he was a child and Mario always stood up for him. He also lacks self-awareness, not knowing that he had unintentionally hurt Mario's feelings after he leaves the dinner table until everyone gives him disapproving looks for it.

Despite his criticism towards Mario, Pio genuinely cares for both of his sons, as he demonstrates this when Mario and Luigi save [[Brooklyn]] from [[Bowser (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Bowser]] and his army, commenting how proud he was of him. Additionally, he and his wife reacted in horror when they were briefly eaten by a Piranha Plant. He later praised his sons after they saved Brooklyn.

== Biography ==
He is the father of Mario and Luigi residing in Brooklyn.

== Trivia ==
* Papa Mario's voice is modeled after the voice that [[Charles Martinet]] used when crashing his audition.
* His appearance resembles [https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/Talon Talon]'s appearance from ''The Legend of Zelda'' franchise.
* Papa Mario and Mama Mario's design in the film was provided by Nintendo.
* He is the fourth member of Mario and Luigi's family to be hugged by [[Donkey Kong (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Donkey Kong]], followed by [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]], [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]], and [[Mario and Luigi's mother]].
