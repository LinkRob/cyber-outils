'''Va Va Voom''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can only be used by [[Stumpet]]s.

== Description ==
When Va Va Voom is used, the Stumpet charges the attack for one turn. On the next turn, the Stumpet unleashes a bunch of embers that can deal massive damage to one of [[Mario]]'s party members. Defeating the Stumpet is the only way to prevent this powerful attack from happening.
