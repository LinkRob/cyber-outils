The '''Bouncy Bloom''' is an object from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They can be found in several different locations. They are first seen in a secret area on [[Raynforst Island]] but are not used until [[Florall Island]].

== Description ==
Bouncy Blooms are flower platforms suspended by green propellers. Their central core is yellow and surrounded by pink petals.

== Use ==
Stepping on a Bouncy Bloom will cause the character to be sent flying into the air. The character then spins down and can drift from side to side, akin to what happens in the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' [[Super Mario Galaxy 2|games]] if Mario jumps off [[Spring Robot|Spring Robots]] or [[Spring Topman|Spring Topmans]].

A blue-petaled variant on [[Shipshape Island]] appears later, letting the user move more quickly laterally, at the cost of flight time. The Bouncy Blooms on Slippenglide Island have snow coverings.
