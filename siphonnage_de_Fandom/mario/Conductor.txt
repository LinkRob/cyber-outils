A '''Conductor''' is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==
Conductors are blue spheres that can only be powered using [[Luigi]]'s [[Thunderhand]]. They have a counterpart named [[Torch (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Torches]], which can similarly only be powered using [[Mario]]'s [[Firebrand]].

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
Conductors are first found in [[Oho Oasis]], but they can be throughout [[Beanbean Kingdom]]. Powering the conductors in the repaired [[Koopa Cruiser]] can reveal hidden [[? Block]]s containing several [[Hee Bean]]s.
