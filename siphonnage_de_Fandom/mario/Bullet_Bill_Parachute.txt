'''Bullet Bill Parachute''' is a glider sponsored by [[Bullet Bill Speed Trial]]. It appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' as a High-End glider making its first appearance in [[New York Tour]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The Bullet Bill Parachute starred in New York Tour as a High-End glider. It grants [[Bullet Bill Plus]] to the racer when equipped.
