The '''Vital Paper''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Vital Paper can be found in Room 001 of the [[Excess Express]] during the Day 2 mystery. [[Mario]] finds this paper in [[Zip Toad]]'s room and he must give it to the [[Businessman]]. The Businessman explains it came from his stolen [[Briefcase]] where the [[Nitro Honey Syrup]] recipe is in.
