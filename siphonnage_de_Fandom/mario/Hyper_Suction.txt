'''Hyper Suction''' is a Special Move in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
Upon using Hyper Suction, Wario opens his mouth wide and uses it as a vacuum, in a similar manner to [[Kirby]]. The Hyper Suction specifically allows Wario to draw in nearby [[coin]]s. While using the move, Wario cannot attack, and he moves very slowly. To have Wario perform Hyper Suction, the player must press {{button|gcn|L}} or {{button|gcn|Z}}.
