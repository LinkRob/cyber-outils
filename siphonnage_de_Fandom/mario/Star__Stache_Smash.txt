'''Star ‘Stache Smash''' is a minigame in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. [[Geno]] makes his only appearance in the ''Mario & Luigi'' series here.

== Description ==
It takes place in [[Little Fungitown]]. When [[Mario|the]] [[Luigi|bros]]. go into the arcade, they find an arcade machine. If they use it, they will be taken to a minigame which, in the original version, is hosted by a mechanical [[Geno]]. Two [[Piranha Plant]]s with open “mouths” are on each side. The objective is to get the power-ups into the piranha plants to get a score of 200 or more. [[Bob-omb]]s will close the mouths. Upon completion, the bros. are rewarded with an [[Invincishroom]]. After [[Mario]] eats it, he gets sick with [[Bean Fever]]. As a result, his brother has to go to [[Guffawha Ruins]] to find a [[Crabbie Grass|cure]] for him.

== Controls ==
Mario uses the {{button|gba|A}} button and [[Luigi]] uses the {{button|gba|B}} button to tilt the board upwards.
