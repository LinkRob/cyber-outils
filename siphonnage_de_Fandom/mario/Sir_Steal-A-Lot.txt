'''Sir Steal-A-Lots''' are robot enemies that appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They are versions of [[Sir Lance-A-Lot]]s that are gold in color and carry large, brown sacks.

==History==
Sir Steal-A-Lots are first encountered in [[Carpaccio's Lab]] and later in [[Allergia Gardens]]. They most likely aid their creator, [[Carpaccio]], in his theft of items given after defeating them, they release a lot more gems than other enemies. Sir Steal-A-Lots are notable for being one of few enemies in the game that doesn't try to attack [[Wario]] and, instead, attempt to flee from him. They can be taken down with any attacks.
