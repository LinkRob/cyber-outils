'''Leaflitter Path''' is the first level of World 3 in the game ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Layout==
Leaflitter Path takes place in a very dark and dense woods. Thanks to the pollution, the color scheme is mainly green and purple.

==History==
After defeating the [[Tower Power Pokey]] and earning the second [[Royal Sticker]], [[Mario]] trends into the very dangerous Leaflitter Path. He first uses his Trumpet Thing Sticker to wake up a [[Wiggler]] who was blocking his path and moves on. Mario fights through many new enemies and avoids poisonous pits until he reaches the end. But before grabbing the Comet Piece, two Boomerang Bros. reveal themselves and do battle with Mario. Mario grabs the [[Comet Piece]] and finishes the level.
