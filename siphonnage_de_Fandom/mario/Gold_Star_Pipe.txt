'''Gold Star Pipe''' is a Special building that appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
The '''Gold Star Pipe''' is a gold [[Pipe]] with a [[Star]] engraved on the pipe. The pipe's rim is shaped like a star.

== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' the Gold Star Pipe is a Special building with no rarity. The player can buy it at the [[Shop (Super Mario Run)|Shop]] after collecting all the [[Black Coin]]s in all levels in [[World Star (Super Mario Run)|World Star]]. If the player touches the Gold Star Pipe, the game will ask the player if they want to play the special course, but colored coins will not be counted on the records.
