'''Ice Climber''' is a microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' As its name implies, it is set in the NES game of the same name. [[9-Volt]] has this microgame in his Spintendo Classics level.

== Layout ==
[[Ice Climbers|Popo]] is standing in a room with a black background and a green floor over a layer of ice. He will be attacked by enemies from the sides of the room.

== Gameplay ==
For the player to win this microgame, Popo must swing his hammer to his sides to repel the enemies. If one catches Popo, it will kill and freeze him, causing a loss with the death jingle from ''Ice Climber''.
