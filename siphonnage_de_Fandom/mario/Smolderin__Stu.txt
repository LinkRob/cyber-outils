==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Smolderin' Stu is a fiery variant of [[Strollin' Stu]] with bright, red skin and fire emitting from its body. If the flames are removed, its bright red skin becomes a dark charred brown.

==History==
===''Super Mario Sunshine''===
In ''Super Mario Sunshine'', Smolderin' Stus are uncommon enemies only encountered in the [[Pinna Park]] episode [[Mecha-Bowser Appears!]] as well as the [[Sirena Beach]] episode [[King Boo Down Below]], during the [[King Boo]] boss fight. [[Mario]] can quickly defeat a Smolderin' Stu by having [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]] spray water at it. If Mario touches a Smolderin' Stu, he gets burned, even if he attempts to [[stomp]] it.

===''Mario Golf'' series===
Smolderin' Stu's name is also seen as one of the default high scores in ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]'' and ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]''.

==Trivia==
*The "Bogen Multicom 2000 bell tone #2 sound effect is played whenever Mario gets burned from a Smolderin' Stu.
