'''White puffs''' are an enemy in the earlier {{series|Wario Land}} installments. They first appear in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' and return in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
White puffs are small blobs capable of inflating. In its small form, only a White puff's eyes are seen, but in its inflated form, a White puff is shown to have a mouth, and its eyes are both closed and slanted. 

===Behavior===
A White puff remains in a single position. If ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3'', if Wario runs into a White puff, it inflates, thrusting him upwards.

==In {{series|Wario Land}}==
===''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''===
White puffs only appear in [[Course No.02]]. Depending on its placement, a White puff can possibly thrust Wario up to the ceiling, and can serve as a hazard if the ceiling is lined with spikes. Wario can only defeat one as [[Dragon Wario (Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3)|Dragon Wario]], by [[Fire Breath|breathing fire]] directly onto the White puff.

===''Wario Land II''===
White puffs are first encountered in the story [[Turn off the giant faucet!]]. Wario can now defeat a White puff by either throwing another enemy at it or by touching it as [[Fat Wario]].
