'''The Final Battle! Bowser's Rage Stage''' ("''The Final Battle''" or "''Bowser's Rage Stage''" for short) is the final level of ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]].'' It has ''a lot'' [[Wonder Effect|Wonder Effects]], but not any that transform the player. Because of how [[Bowser]] activates his "[[Bowser's Epic Wonder|Epic Wonder]]" in this level, there are total of 13 different Wonder Effects, each from different levels. The only level after this one (not counting the [[Special World (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|Special World]]) is [[Credits (Level) (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|the credits level]]. After you beat this level, the [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|badges]] "All Fire Power", "All Elephant Power", "All Drill Power", and "All Bubble Power" become available at the [[Poplin Shop|Poplin Shops]].  In the boss battle the ''"Ninji Jump Parade"'' Wonder Effect is active.

== Trivia ==

* Though the level is called "''The Final Battle''," the true final level is ''"The Final-Final Test Badge Marathon"''.
