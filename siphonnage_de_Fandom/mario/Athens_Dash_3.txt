'''Athens Dash 3''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', that made it's debut in the [[Anniversary Tour (2023)|Anniversary Tour of 2023]], acting as a layout of [[Athens Dash]]. Unlike the other routes, it first appears during the second lap of ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''<nowiki/>'s version of the course. Its route includes the Odeîon Herōdèsio Attikósio (Odeon of Herodes Atticus), another theatre on the Akrópolis of Athens. alongside the Théatron Dionusósio (Theatre of Dionysus).

== Layout ==
The racers start facing east like they do in [[Athens Dash 2]]. They start by turning right at the four-way intersection and heading south, dropping down into the Théatron Dionusósio, exiting through the back. They then turn right and drive down [[Athens Dash 1]] in reverse, deviating into the tunnel that leads to the Odeîon Herōdèsio Attikósio. They make a slight right and go through the split between the stairs, turning right onto [[Athens Dash 2]] and making their way back up the stairs to the Parthenon where the [[Finish line|starting line]] awaits them.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', this route is used for the second lap of [[Athens Dash]].
