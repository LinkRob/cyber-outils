'''Nintendo Selects''' was a label given to [[reissue|re-releases]] of certain best-selling [[Wii]], [[Nintendo 3DS]], and [[Wii U]] titles. The label was first introduced on May 15, 2011, as the successor of the [[Player's Choice]]. A Nintendo Selects re-release was sold at a lower price, specifically about half the amount as the original.
