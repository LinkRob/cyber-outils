'''Jellyfish Island''' is an island in [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']]. It is the eleventh (disconnecting when [[Bowser]] disconnected [[Twistee Island|Twistee]] and [[Heatfreeze Island|Heatfreeze]] during his sortie on [[Shipshape Island]]) major island to be connected to [[Shipshape Island]], and the last major island to be connected to it. Jellyfish Island is found in the [[Stormstar Sea]].

== History ==
As with the other major islands, Jellyfish Island was one of the resulting fragments of [[Concordia]] when [[Zokket]] sundered the [[Uni-Tree]]. It is renowned for its club, [[Club Dyode]], whose shape gave the island its name. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] are sent to the island because [[Adaphne]] is staying there on vacation, and her help could be useful in bypassing the barrier that Zokket is using to fence off his fortress. She is found inside the island's dance hall, and after being provoked, she asks to be fed pizza. Mario and Luigi make pizza for her to eat and are provided access to the island's lighthouse, resulting in its connection and allowing Adaphne to get to [[Shipshape Island]].

[[Groovemaster Dyode]] makes the island's dance hall his last stop in his tour to help improve the Concordians' morale after [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|hijacks Concordia]]. After a dance-off, he and [[Emmit]] share a moment, filling some of the Bonding Can with one of the [[Bond (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Bonds]] the Bros. need for their invasion of the [[Soli-Tree]].
