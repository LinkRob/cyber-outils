The '''Bean Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured as an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Bean Badge is obtained from [[Lady Lima]] after [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] defeat [[Queen Bean]]. The Bean Badge is the first badge obtained overall. It increases its user's [[BP]] by 6 and [[Power|POW]] by 8. In the Japanese version, the Bean Badge instead increases its BP by 4.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Bean Badge is instead rewarded by a [[Pea]]. It increases BP by 4, like the original version's Japanese version, but now increases POW by 10.
