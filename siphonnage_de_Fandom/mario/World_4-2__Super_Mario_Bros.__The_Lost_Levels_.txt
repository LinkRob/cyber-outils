'''World 4-2''' is the second [[level]] of [[World 4 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 4]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the fourteenth level overall in said game.

==Layout==
The player starts facing five [[Bill Blaster]]s with a [[? Block]] containing a power-up between them. After the Bill Blasters is a [[Super Star]] within the [[Brick Block]], followed by a [[Springboard]] after some gaps and [[Buzzy Beetle]]s the player can use to reach a higher platform. On this platform, the player will encounter a [[Koopa Troopa]] and a [[Lakitu]], the former of which the player can kick to defeat a row of enemies underneath. After a tall pipe with a [[Piranha Plant]] is a row of ? Blocks over some Bill Blasters, the left-most block contains a power-up and the right-most block contains a [[Poison Mushroom]]. After a [[Coin Block]] suspended over a pit is a [[Hammer Bro]]. before the path splits. At the end of the upper path is another Hammer Bro., and the first pipe at the end of the bottom path is a [[Warp Pipe]] to an underground area with [[Coin]]s and [[Hidden Block]]s containing a power-up and a Poison Mushroom. After the pipe at the end of both paths (which is the exit pipe from the underground area) are scattered platforms with a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] above them before the [[Goal Pole]].
