==Profile==
===Physical description===
Gold Mice look almost identical to the [[Blue Mouse|Blue Mice]] and [[Purple Mouse|Purple Mice]], except they are yellow. 

===Behavior===
A Gold Mouse is either hiding in the wall or a block of [[cheese]] in certain rooms. If revealed, a Gold Mouse quickly moves in a fixed path over to the other side to try and escape. If vacuumed into the [[Poltergust 3000]], a Gold Mouse releases lots of [[treasure]], something that [[Speedy Spirit]]s also do. If a Gold Mouse is defeated using an [[Elemental Ghost|element]], it does not release any treasure.

==History==
Ten Gold Mice appear throughout [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|the mansion]], and they appear in a location from either one of two conditions. The five '''cheese golden mice'''<ref name=GM>Loe, Casey. Versus Books ''Luigi's Mansion'' Perfect Guide. Page 78.</ref> (or '''cheese mice''')<ref>Loe, Casey. Versus Books ''Luigi's Mansion'' Perfect Guide. Page 19.</ref> emerge from a hidden piece of [[cheese]] if [[Luigi]] scans it with the [[Game Boy Horror]], while the five '''random golden mice'''<ref name=GM/> (or '''Random Mice''')<ref>Loe, Casey. Versus Books ''Luigi's Mansion'' Perfect Guide. Page 42.</ref> have a 20 percent chance of appearing in a predetermined room or hallway. When one appears, the "puzzle complete" jingle plays, followed by the mouse squeaking. Luigi must capture a Gold Mouse before it escapes into the wall. However, unlike with Speedy Spirits, Luigi has another opportunity at catching the Gold Mouse if he reenters the location later, which includes during the [[blackout]], as long as the lights are not turned on.

Gold Mice release a variety of treasure, including [[coin]]s, [[bill]]s, [[Gold Bar]]s, and even a [[Stone (Luigi's Mansion)|Stone]]. Every Gold Mouse releases 30 coins, ten more than the number released by Speedy Spirits. In the PAL [[Hidden Mansion]], each Gold Mouse relinquishes 30 coins, 20 bills, a Gold Bar, and a [[Blue Diamond]].
