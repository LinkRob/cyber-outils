Like the other Infinijump stickers, the Flashy Infinijump is very rare and   only a few can be found in the entire game: two in [[The Engimansion|The Enigmansion]], and one in Chain Chomp Ruins, that respawns a few times. With this sticker, Mario can jump on enemies up to 100 times for damage.
==Description==
The Flashy Infinijump has a red boot in the middle. It also has a rainbow background. It takes up  one space in the [[Sticker Album]].
