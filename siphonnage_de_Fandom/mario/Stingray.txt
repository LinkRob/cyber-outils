'''Stingrays''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Stingrays are land-dwelling {{w|manta ray}}s with a blue body.

===Behavior===
Stingrays that move through tall grass as if they were swimming through it. A Stingray tries to attack [[Wario]] by dashing towards him. Stingrays behave similarly to [[Big Scorper]]s.

They "swim" very close to the ground, so they can only be hit by a [[Ground Pound]] or [[Wild Swing-Ding]]. After three Ground Pounds, they are stunned, allowing Wario to use a Mad Move of his choice. However, Ground Pounding is dangerous in areas that they appear, as missing will result in Wario getting stuck in the grass for a few seconds.

==In ''Wario World''==
Stingrays are only encountered in [[Beanstalk Way]]. Because Stingrays move around very close to the ground, Wario can only attack them using a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] or a [[Wild Swing-Ding]]. After Wario performs a Ground Pound onto a Stingray three times, it becomes stunned, allowing Wario to defeat the Stingray using any [[Mad Move]].
