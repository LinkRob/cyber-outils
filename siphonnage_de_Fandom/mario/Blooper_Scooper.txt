'''Blooper Scooper''' is a 4-player minigame in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective for each character is to swim away from a whirlpool created by the [[Giant Blooper]] to avoid being drawn in by it. If a character gets hit by an obstacle or the Giant Blooper's tentacle, they become stunned temporarily. If a character gets too close to the Giant Blooper, it grabs them with its tentacle and takes them underwater, eliminating that character.

The winning criteria is determined by either who is the last character remaining or whoever manages to outlast the Giant Blooper within the 30-second time limit. If all four characters either outlast it or are dragged into the water, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Controls==
*Control Stick: Move
*A button: Swim
