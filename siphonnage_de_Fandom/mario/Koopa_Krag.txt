'''Koopa Krag''' is an elderly [[Koopa Troopa]] who appears in the Story mode of ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. He is the grandfather of [[Koopa (Mario Party DS)|Koopa]].

== Description ==
Koopa Krag has bown skin and a chartreuse shell. His eyes are covered by silver hair, and his chin has a silver beard.

==''Mario Party DS''==
In Story Mode, when the heroes make their way to [[Kamek's Library]], Koopa asks for the heroes' help in freeing his grandfather, whom [[Kamek]] trapped into one of his books.
