'''Luigi's Mansion Court''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Description==
The court takes place outside the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]] from ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''. There are several [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] spectating the match. The normal version of Luigi's Mansion Court takes place on a concrete field, with no real gimmicks in it.

In the gimmick version, there are five panels depicting a ghost symbol. Hitting one summons a [[Gold Ghost]], [[Purple Puncher]], [[Blue Twirler]], or [[Bowling Ghost]] to the court; these ghosts can slow characters down, interfering with their movement and swing timing. Alternatively, a [[Garbage Can Ghost]] may appear to throw a [[Banana Peel]] on the ground. However, if a player hits the mid-back panel depicting a light bulb, all ghosts on the other side of the court disappear.

==In ''Mario Power Tennis''==
The Special Game [[Terror Tennis]] takes place in Luigi's Mansion Court. Here, the player's character has to try and stop the ghosts from escaping their portraits. The ghosts can slow the character's movements, much like if a tennis game is played here.
