'''Kannons''' are an enemy from the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}. They first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'', and their only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' appearance is in an issue of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Kannons are a pirate alias of [[Klump]]s, according to ''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest''{{'}}s instruction booklet. Kannons have a purplish-pink skin. They are dressed in a pirate outfit, mainly noticeable from their black hat with a white skull-and-crossbones symbol on it. Kannons always carry an object of the [[Kannon (cannon)|same name]] with them.

===Behavior===
Kannons always remain in one spot, firing [[kannonball]]s from their kannon.
