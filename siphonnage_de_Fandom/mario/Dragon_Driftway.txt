'''Dragon Driftway''' is a course found in the [[Egg Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. The course starts out by sending you into the mouth of a dragon (That loosely resembles [[Gobblegut]] from the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy series|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games), and along a spiraling track, that takes you into a town that resembles an Asian town.

== In ''[[Super Smash Bros. series]]'' ==
Dragon Driftway's music can be played on stages from the Mario universe.
