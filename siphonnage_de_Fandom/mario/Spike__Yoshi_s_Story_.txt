'''Spikes''' are a type of enemy featured in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. They are encountered in the levels [[Bone Dragon Pit]] and [[Magma Castle]]. Spikes either jump back and forth or periodically jump straight upward. They are among the enemies that cannot be defeated, so a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] has to avoid them.
