==Effect==
The hammer does minimal damage and temporarily puts enemies to sleep if timed properly. While the enemies are asleep, they heal a few HP over time. Regular enemies fall asleep for around 3 turns while bosses fall asleep for one. 

==Trivia==
*The hammer has an icon of sheep similar to that of the [[Sleepy Sheep]] from previous ''Paper Mario'' games.
