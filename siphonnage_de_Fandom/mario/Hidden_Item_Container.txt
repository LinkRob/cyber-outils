A {{nihongo|'''Hidden Item Container'''|がちやがちやカプセル<ref>{{cite web|url=https://21010.net/club/bn/agb/amaj/pdf/p006_009.pdf|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20190509010638/https://21010.net/club/bn/agb/amaj/pdf/p006_009.pdf|title=Shogakukan guide preview|work=Shogakukan|archivedate=2019-05-09|accessdate=2023-05-17}}</ref>|Gachagacha Kapuseru|Clattering Capsule}} is an object which appears exclusively in ''[[Super Mario Advance]]''.

==Description==
Hidden Item Containers can be randomly found under [[Mushroom Block]]s or behind destructible walls. A Hidden Item Container scrolls between a [[Bomb]], a [[Small heart|heart]], and a [[Starman]]. Either [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], or [[Toad (character)|Toad]] can pick up a Hidden Item Container and throw it to break it open and therefore obtain whichever item was shown on the Hidden Item Container the moment it broke open.
