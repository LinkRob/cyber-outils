'''Marumen''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Marumen have a round body that is either purple or red in color, and their face is yellow. Marumen have round feet, with the red ones having green feet and the purple one having orange feet.

===Behavior===
Marumen are like [[Wanderin' Goom]]s from earlier ''[[Wario Land (series)|Wario Land]]'' games in that they are weak enemies incapable of harming [[Wario]]. A Marumen can be easily defeated from any attack. If Wario does a [[Ground Pound (move)|Smash Attack]] near a Marumen, it changes from red to purple or vice versa.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
Like [[Spear-Mask]]s, Marumen are one of the earlier enemies encountered. [[Wario]] first encounters them in the [[Hall of Hieroglyphs]]. The only difference between a purple and a red Marumen is that defeating a purple one causes it to release two [[Small Coin]]s while defeating a red one causes it to release a [[Bronze Coin]].
