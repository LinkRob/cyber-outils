'''[[Mario]]''' is a plumber who, among many of his other appearances, is the main character of the ''[[Super Mario Bros. (comic series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Game Boy (comic series)|Game Boy]]'' comic series by [[Valiant Comics]], as well as the ''[[Nintendo Adventure Books]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Mario is a short overweight Italian man with a thick black mustache, black eyebrows and black hair with blue eyes. He wears a red cap with white gloves and a long-sleeved blue shirt and red overalls, matching his original appearance in ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' and his appearance in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' as well as the manual artwork for ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. The colored cover artwork for the Nintendo Adventure Books show him having switched to the modern "blue overalls/red shirt" look from ''[[Dinosaur Dilemma]]'' onward.

In "[[It's Always Fair Weather]]", Mario's hair briefly turns brown when he becomes Raccoon Mario. This happens again when he puts on the doctor's coat in "[[The Doctor is In... Over His Head]]", a fact that he even lampshades at the end of this story.
