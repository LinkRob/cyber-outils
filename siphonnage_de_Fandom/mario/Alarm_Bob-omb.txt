An '''Alarm Bob-omb''' is an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] version, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
An Alarm Bob-omb is a variety an alarm clock, their nose resembles clown noses and a drill protruding from their heads.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
Alarm Bob-ombs can be fought by either both [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] or by [[Bowser]]. They commonly appear in battle with [[Naplock]]s.

While fighting Bowser, an Alarm Bob-omb's behavior involves it running up and down the combat area ringing their alarm to try and awaken the Naplock, though this occurs only if the Naplock is damaged. After much ringing, the Alarm Bob-omb eventually tries to charge into Bowser, exploding, but this can be countered using a [[punch]] attack. It is possible for an Alarm Bob-omb to have its alarm shut off by Naplock.

If an Alarm Bob-omb is fought by Mario and Luigi, it behaves similarly except without a Naplock to shut it off.
