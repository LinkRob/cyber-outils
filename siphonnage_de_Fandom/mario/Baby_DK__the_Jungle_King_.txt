'''Baby DK, the Jungle King!''' is the first level of [[World 2 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 2]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', a [[Green Yoshi]] takes control in this level. This level takes place in a vast jungle with many trees, vines, statues, and ruins populated by many [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]]. This level introduces [[Baby Donkey Kong]] and his abilities of climbing vines and more. Once this level is completed, [[Baby DK]] joins the team as a permanent member and [[Stork Stop]]s appear in this level allowing you to switch between babies at anytime. 

==Story==
At some point after defeating [[Gilbert the Gooey]], a group of [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]] overwhelm the team and make off with the babies. As [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] gives chase to the thieving monkeys, [[Baby Donkey Kong]] drops down and offers to help [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], the two of them then set out to find [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Peach]].

After the level's completion, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and [[Baby DK]] finally find the thieving [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]], they then preform a [[DK Dash Attack]] which wipes out all but one of of the [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]] thus freeing [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Peach]], the [[Stork]] retrieves both the babies and [[Baby DK]] joins the team as a permanent member.

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, advance to the right and climb the vines, swallow the [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]] here and continue climbing the vines and grab the [[Coin]]s and three [[Red Coin]]s in a vertical line, from here, continue climbing to the right and drop down, swallowing any [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]] or [[Nipper Plant]]s in your way, launch an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] upward to obtain the [[Smiley Flower]] in the nook above, continue to the right drop down the pits on either side to swallow foes to fill your [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] supply and then swing across the swinging ropes, from here, advance to the right and beat the [[Nipper Plant]]s and [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]] as you collect the [[Coin]]s and two [[Red Coin]]s in this vine-filled area, beat the [[Piranha Plant]]s in the way as well and continue climbing to the top, from here, advance to the right and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] for some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and slurp up the [[Shy Guy]]s that emerge from the [[Pipe]], there are four [[Baron Von Zeppelin]]s here, two of them are carrying [[Needlenose]]s and [[Grenade]]s however one is carrying a [[Key]] avoid the weapon-carrying Barons and pop the one holding the [[Key]], at the path's end, there is a box which can be used as a platform (and effective alternative) to reaching the upper vine but breaking it will reveal five [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] (the box respawns if you leave the area through the nearby Pipe or Door and then return (but it won't give [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] a second time)). Move back to the left and climb the long vine and climb across the vines to the left and drop down on the platform, collect the [[Coin]]s and toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the [[Winged Cloud]] above the hut to score a 1-Up, once done, use the [[Key]] you snagged from the [[Baron von Zeppelin]] to enter the door and play a minigame ([[Egg Toss]]), after the minigame is done, climb across the vines and continue climbing to the right snagging the [[Coin]]s and two [[Red Coin]]s, after this, climb up the tall vine to the top to reach the next area.

After reaching the next area, tag the [[Middle Ring]] and swing across the large rope collecting the [[Coin]]s (if you fall off, don't sweat it, just advance to the left (slurping up any [[Nipper Plant]]s in your path) and bounce of the [[Spring Ball]] back to the main path), swing with the rope to the right and build up enough swings to leap and grab the [[Smiley Flower]] and grab onto the vine where three [[Red Coin]]s are lined up, grab the [[Red Coin]]s and drop down, grab the [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] here and beat the [[Nipper Plant]]s and [[Piranha Plant]]s and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] for some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], next, use the vine to swing to the upper ledge and hop on the robot statues to reach the vines, launch an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the dirt blocking the [[Smiley Flower]] to obtain it, climb up the vine and climb across the smaller vines to the left collecting the [[Coin]]s and hit the [[Egg Block]] to fill your egg supply and climb up to the vine to the platform and toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the dirt chunks to obtain the [[Smiley Flower]], there are [[Coin]] stashes to the right with two of these [[Coin]]s beind [[Red Coin]]s, kill the [[Piranha Plant]] here and then drop down the side to the right grabbing the [[Coin]]s, then drop down to the main ground and beat the enemies to the left (if you want to) and then move to the right for the next area.

In the next area, you will find some [[Pointey]]s blocking your path, wipe out these guys with a [[DK Dash Attack]] and advance to the right, you will see some more [[Pointey]]s and a Box at the end, these can be taken out with a set of [[DK Dash Attack]]s, after this use the rope above to swing to the vine to the left, climb up the vine, there are two [[Red Coin]]s dangling below the vines, (you can grab these with either an egg toss or by dropping down onto them) climb across the vines collecting the [[Coin]]s as you go, you will come across some blowing wind which is used to reach the upper ledge (ordinarily, you would need [[Baby Peach]] for this but you can also swallow a [[Nipper Plant]] and spit it out here and bounce off it's head to reach the upper platform, there are [[Winged Cloud]]s and [[Coin]]s here, hit the [[Winged Cloud]]s for some items and snag the [[Coin]]s but watch out for the falling [[Nipper Spore]]s from above (these turn into [[Nipper Plant]]s upon landing), there is an outlined [[Baby Coin]] at the path's end but you will need [[Baby Peach]] for this one, drop down back onto the main path and there are three [[Red Coin]]s here, grab them and swing from the rope collecting the [[Coin]]s and the [[Smiley Flower]] here, there are also three [[Red Coin]]s in the center so climb up the rope to get these, you can swing to the level's end but if the rope swinging loses momentum, drop down and climb the platforms to reach the ledge and jump through the [[GOAL! Ring]] to finish the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
After completing the level, return to the wind-blowing area at the third section, there is a [[Stork Stop]] here, switch to [[Baby Peach]] and glide up to the platform with the wind and advance to the left and grab the [[Baby Coin]] at the path's end.

==Trivia==
*The [[Grinder (species)|Grinder]]'s baby-thieving habits in the cutscenes of this level are a nod to how the [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]] would steal [[Baby Mario]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'' however in the actual game, the [[Grinder (species)|Grinders]] don't steal the babies and merely just run away from [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] upon sight.
*The level's name is a nod to how [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] is often referred to as the King of the Jungle.
