The '''Water Vellumental''' is a boss in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'' that has the power to manipulate water to it's will. It is a [[Vellumental]] who makes it's lair in the [[Water Vellumental Shrine]] found at [[Autumn Mountain]]. It was folded by [[King Olly]] into Origami in the early events of the game.

After defeating it, [[Olivia]] can learn it's folding techniques, and will be able to transform into her version of the vellumental.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
The '''Water Vellumental''' is an origami dragon. It has four pointy dark blue legs, a cyan head, and a long cyan tail. It's body is cyan on top, with light blue on it's belly. It has light blue wings, and a row of five spikes on it's spine.

Usually, the dragon flies. However, once attacked, it must stay low.

It's head, legs and tails are it's weaknesses, which Mario can attack with hammers dealing massive damage when it's grounded.

Defeating the Vellumental drops a Water Bibliofold, which allows Olivia to learn how to fold into a Water Vellumental.

=== Powers and abilities ===
==== Before Spin ====
* '''Summon Spouts''' - Summon a number of water spouts to the Inner Circle, each targeting an angle of the battlefield. The number of spouts varies, from 2 to 8. They will stay until it uses Wash Away at the end of it's turn.
** '''Wash Away''': When Mario finishes spinning the battlefield, it uses the Wash Away ability. The spouts go on their respective lines, wiping out tiles on their way.
==== On its turn ====
* '''Bite'''- If the Vellumental is grounded and Mario is facing it, it will bite him, dealing damage once.
* '''Flying bite''' - If Mario is on high ground (risen by [[Olivia]]'s [[Earth Vellumental]] power), and the Vellumental is Flying, it will use Flying Bite. The Vellumental will also use this move whenever it is flying at any time. Deals damage once.
* '''Water Pistol''' - If the Vellumental is grounded and Mario is at its front, far away from it, the Water Vellumental will blast a column of water at Mario, dealing damage once.
* '''Wing Ding''' - If the Vellumental is not flying and Mario is at its side, it uses its wing to hit Mario, dealing damage once.
* '''Wyvern Whip''' - If the Vellumental is not flying and Mario is behind it, it uses its tail to strike Mario, dealing damage once.
* '''Big Wave''' - If the Vellumental's belly is full (after using Soak Up 3 times), it uses Big Wave. It throws the water out, deals big damage to the player. Mario can dodge if he is on high ground. 
** The second time it uses Big Wave, it turns into Mega Wave, then Giga Wave, then Ultimate Wave, dealing more damage with every use. After Ultimate Wave, the move goes back to Big Wave.
* Skip turn - It does nothing on it's turn. (Only when Drained)
==== End of turn ====
* Soak Up (unofficial name) - When it is not drained, it uses Soak Up. It will suck up water from the water spouts into its belly, getting bigger each time. After 3 times, it will then use Big Wave.
* Fly (unofficial name) - After Drained or 2 turns in Ground phase, it uses Fly to return to its Flying phase.

=== Hints ===
* When the Water Vellumental takes flight, bring it down with a jump, then follow up with your hammer!
* When the Water Vellumental’s belly is full, it’s about to unleash a powerful move! Use the Earth Vellumental’s power to stay high and dry!
* The best time to use the 1,000 Fold Arms is after the Water Vellumental uses its Big Wave move!

==Biography==
{{Sectstub}}

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'' ==
The Water Vellumental makes it's lair in the [[Water Vellumental Shrine]] found on [[Autumn Mountain]]. By defeating it, [[Olivia]] can learn it's folding techniques, allowing to transform into her version of the vellumental.

===Combat===
==== Belly states ====
During combat, the Water Vellumental will Soak Up water from the spouts, filling it's belly with water at the end of it's turn. There are a total of 4 states: Empty, 1/3 filled, 2/3 filled, and Full.

When Mario begins combat, it begins with Empty phase. It will Soak Up after any attack, except for Big Wave, where it becomes Drained instead. Pay close attention to its belly to indicate how full the Water Vellumental is before using the Earth Vellumental's power, as you can very well waste it if not careful.

After three Soak Ups, it reaches Full state. During this state during Mario's turn, it cannot be forced to change from Flying phase to Ground phase using Jump attacks. During its turn, it will also use the Big Wave ability, forcing it to Drained Phase and Empty belly state.
==== Flying phase ====
The Water Vellumental is partially invulnerable in it's flying phase. Mario cannot hit it with [[Olivia]]'s [[Earth Vellumental]] attack, hammer attack or [[1,000-Fold Arms]] attack, and jumping does very little damage but forces it to the ground, where it is then vulnerable to all of the above.

==== Ground phase ====
After Mario jumps on it, it will drop to the ground, becoming vulnerable to other attacks. It can not be grounded when Full. After it's turn, it will rise again into the air, unless Drained.

==== Drained phase ====
The Water Vellumental is very vulnerable in its drained phase. It would skip one turn after entering this state. After skipping it's turn, it enters flying phase, repeating the process until defeated.
