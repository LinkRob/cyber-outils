'''Petal Isles''' is the 7th [[world]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It contains 34 [[Wonder Seed|Wonder Seeds]], 14 levels, and 6 challenges. This world has 3 Badge Challenges, 1 KO Arena and 2 Wiggler Races.
