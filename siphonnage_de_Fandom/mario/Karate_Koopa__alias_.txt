'''Karate Koopa''' is an alter-ego assumed by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Karate Koopa]]". It is his only alias in which his skin is pale white instead of green. Karate Koopa is often assisted by [[Ninji]]s.

==Physical description==
Karate Koopa is a ninja dressed in yellow and red, and he wears a pair of sandals. Karate Koopa has a black ponytail, black shell, black nails and teeth. He is found wearing sandals.
