'''Fawful Guys''' are [[Fawfulized]] [[Shy Guy]]-like enemies that wear orange robes and a [[Fawful]] face mask. In the overworld, they can be defeated by one of [[Bowser]]'s punches for a small reward of coins. They have a dark variation named [[Dark Fawful Guy]].
