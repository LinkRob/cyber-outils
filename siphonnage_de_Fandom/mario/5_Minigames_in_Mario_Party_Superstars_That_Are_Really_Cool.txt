"'''5 Minigames in ''Mario Party Superstars'' That Are Really Cool''' (formatted as "'''5 Minigames in Mario Party Superstars that are REALLY COOL'''") is an episode of ''[[Fun Lists! Lists! Lists!]]'', released on [[Play Nintendo]]'s official YouTube channel. This episode is about winter-themed minigames in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Overview==
Narrator explains the mechanics of five minigames and shows footage from them. During one of the minigames, a player is tasked with guessing whether a [[snowball]] will knock [[Mario]] out or not based on a still from the footage.
