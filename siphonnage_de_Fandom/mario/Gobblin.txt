'''Gobblins''' are enemies in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Gobblin is a large, yellow ball with one pink cheek. It has two beady eyes and a smiley face.

===Behavior===
Gobblins sit on [[Warp Pipe]]s to block a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]'s path. Unlike most enemies, a Gobblin does not damage Yoshi if he touches it. To defeat a Gobblin, Yoshi has to throw six [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] at it, as the Gobblin grows bigger from eating each egg thrown at it, up to the point of exploding.

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
Gobblins are first encountered in [[Big Bungee Piranha's Lair]]. Two Gobblins can be found in the subterranean-enemy exhibit of the [[Island Museum]], and are one the few enemies there that can be defeated.
