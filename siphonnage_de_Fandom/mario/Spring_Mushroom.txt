A '''Spring Mushroom''' is a item in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They are metallic mushrooms that turn [[Mario]] into [[Spring Mario]], in which he is enveloped within a spring. In his Spring form, Mario bounces instead of running and, as such, can reach much higher areas. It is also possible to bounce off walls in this form, which is required to collect a [[Power Star]] in [[Toy Time Galaxy]]. Later in the game, the Spring Mushroom is found in [[Matter Splatter Galaxy]]. In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', the Spring Mushroom is only found in [[Chompworks Galaxy]]. Taking damage, touching water, or entering a [[Launch Star]] will revert Mario to his original form.
