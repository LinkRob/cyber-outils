'''Chuckola Pants''' are an unused [[clothing]] item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. Similar to the [[Chuckola Badge]], their description states that they are made in the likeness of [[Chuckola Fruit]]. [[Mario]] would have been the only character capable of equipping the Chuckola Pants, and it would have increased his [[Defense]] by 45 and grant him the [[60% Poison Dodge]] special effect.
