'''Don-chan''' is the main character from the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiko_no_Tatsujin ''Taiko no Tatsujin''] series. He makes his first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Don-chan is a living Taiko (太鼓), a type of Japanese drum sort of reminiscent to a conga. He has a light blue cylindrical body with a black beam running across the middle. A white design is printed on the side of his base, and his bottom is also colored white. Nearing Don-chan's face, the blueish tint gets a little darker. Don-chan's face is on where the batter head of a drum would normally be, with a white trim going around the reddish-orange cover. Don-chan has two gazing, black eyes and usually has a happy smile on his face. Arms and legs extend from the edges of the racer/drum, each ending in a light ball for hands and feet. A white cord is tied onto Don-chan's back, making him somewhat resemble a keychain.

Since 2004, Don-chan has been voiced by Miki Narahashi (楢橋 美紀), a Japanese professional voice actress.

===Powers and abilities===
In terms of size, Don-chan is the smallest character in any {{series|Mario Kart}} installment, even more than [[Toad (character)|Toad]]. Don-chan's short stature makes his [[Speed]] meager, but [[Acceleration]] and [[Handling]] greatly benefit from it.

== Game appearances ==

=== ''Mario Kart Arcade GP DX'' ===
In [[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX|''Mario Kart Arcade GP DX'']], Don-chan is a default character, playable from the start. He has two other usable forms, [[Strawberry Don-chan]] and [[Hero Don-chan]].

=== {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} ===
Don-chan is one of the characters in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' whom [[Pac-Man]] can randomly summon during his Namco Roulette taunt.
