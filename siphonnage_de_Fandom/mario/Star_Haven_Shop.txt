The '''Star Haven Shop''' is a location which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
The Star Haven Shop is an [[Item Shop]] run by a [[Star Kid]] and is located within [[Star Haven]]. The Star Haven Shop is known for selling high quality items for very low prices including [[Life Shroom]]s for only 25 [[coin]]s. The [[Super Soda]] is only sold here and practically replaces the [[Tasty Tonic]] from the other Item Shops. This makes stocking up this shop recommend before [[Mario]] goes into his final confrontation with [[Bowser]].
