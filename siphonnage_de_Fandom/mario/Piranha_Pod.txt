'''Piranha Pods''', also named '''Piranha Plant Eggs''' or '''Piranha Plant's Eggs''', are an alternative to the usual [[Spiny Egg]]s that [[Lakitu]]s throw. They resemble Spiny Eggs (complete with the spikes) but they also include a Piranha Plant mouth. They behave identically to a Spiny Egg upon being dunked onto the ground but they transform into [[Piranha Plant]]s rather than hatching into [[Spiny|Spinies]] upon landing, they also roll around less as well. Piranha Pods otherwise can be destroyed in the same ways as a Spiny Egg. Piranha Pods first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. U'' / ''New Super Luigi U'' / ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''==
[[Image:Piranha Plant Egg Toss.png|thumb|left|Lakitu tossing Piranha Pods at the playable characters]]
Piranha Pods first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and later appear in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', in these games, they are used as weapons by [[Lakitu]] serving as an alternative to the usual [[Spiny Egg]] weapons they throw. Piranha Pods however are much more rare. They behave similarly to Spiny Eggs but they become [[Piranha Plant]]s upon hitting the ground rather than [[Spiny|Spinies]], Piranha Pods also roll around less as well. Piranha Pods can be destroyed with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]]. They can also be frozen with the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]]. Piranha Pods can be found in [[Blooming Lakitus]], [[Spinning Sandstones]], and [[Beanstalk Jungle]].

==In ''Mario Party Superstars''==
[[Image:Piranha Plant Egg Party.png|thumb|The Icing on the Cake, Lakitu drops a Piranha Plant Egg to bloom into a Piranha Plant.]]
Piranha Pods (referred to as "strawberries") reappear in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''. In this game, they can be found in [[Peach's Birthday Cake]]. Whenever players land on the [[Happening Space]]s found throughout the board, [[Lakitu]] will show up and for five [[Coin]]s, Lakitu will place a Piranha Pods in the icing by the Happening Space and a Piranha Plant will bloom from the icing below. Players that land by the Happening Spaces will lose [[Coin]]s and the lost Coins will be given to the player that deployed the Piranha Plant. If a player is to land on a Happening Space after having placed a Piranha Plant there, he/she will be rewarded with 10 Coins and Lakitu will give the player the option to upgrade their Piranha Plant to a [[Super Piranha Plant]] for 30 Coins, if the player accepts, Lakitu will drop a [[Big Piranha Pod]] in the icing thus revealing a [[Super Piranha Plant]], Super Piranha Plants are far more dangerous as they can actually steal [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]] from rivals and give them to the placing player.
