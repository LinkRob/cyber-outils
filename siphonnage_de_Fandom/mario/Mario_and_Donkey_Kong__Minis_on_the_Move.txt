'''''Mario and Donkey Kong: Minis on the Move''''' is a [[Nintendo 3DS]] Nintendo eShop download-only game, as part of the ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series. In this game, [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Pauline]] join to run a carnival.

==Gameplay==
In ''Mario and Donkey Kong: Minis on the Move'', the player must place blocks on a checkered square in order to make a path for the Minis so they can reach the goal of every level. There are 180 levels overall in the game.
