'''Ice Flow''' is the second level of World 4 in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

== Layout ==
Ice Flow is quite similar in appearance to the previous level, [[Snow Rise]]. This level is also mainly a large, ice cavern that has shades of a darker blue in color.

== History ==
[[Mario]] continues his hunt for the [[Royal Sticker]] as he treads further into the snow. Mario must be careful while walking on the slippery ice and dodging [[Cooligan]]s. Mario eventually finds the Comet Piece to finish this cold level (and notices a mansion in the background).
