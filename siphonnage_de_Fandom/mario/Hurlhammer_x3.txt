|name=Hurlhammer x3 (Battle Card)}}'''Hurlhammer x3''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Hurlhammer x3 is the line version of [[Mario]]'s "Hammer x3" attack. It is three of the [[Hurlhammer (Battle Card)|Hurlhammer]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied with a blue/white checkerboard background.
