The '''Snowmad Tribe''', also individually called "'''Snowmads'''," is a villainous faction that serves as the main antagonists of [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze|''Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze'']]. They are a hostile tribe of seafarers based on stereotypical depictions of vikings (and are sometimes simply refer to as such) as well as being anthropomorphic animals.

== History ==

=== In ''Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze'' ===
The Snowmads set the events of [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze|''Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze'']] in motion when their leader, [[Lord Fredrik]], summons an ice dragon with his magic horn. The creature blows the [[Kong Family|Kongs]] away from [[Donkey Kong Island]], which the Snowmads take over, setting their command ship on the volcano and using it as their fortress.

The Kongs make it back to their island and storm the Snowmad command ship, where [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] engages Lord Fredrik and defeats him, sending the Snowmad King to his seeming demise.

==== In the game ====
Snowmads are found on every island that the Kongs go to.

== Members ==

* [[Lord Fredrik]], the Snowmad King, is the leader and is faced on [[Donkey Kong Island]].
* [[Pompy]], the Presumptuous is a seal who attacks with circus tricks. His base is in [[Lost Mangroves]].
* [[Skowl]], the Startling is an advanced [[Hootz]] who attacks by throwing feathers and claw swipes. His base is in [[Autumn Heights]].
* [[Ba-Boom]], the Boisterous, is a baboon who can clone himself and attacks with hammers and bombs. His base is in [[Bright Savannah]].
* [[Fugu]], the Frightening, is a pufferfish who can change size and suck Donkey Kong in by inhailing water and, if that fails, push him into the hermit crabs with an exhale attack. His base is in [[Sea Breeze Cove]].
* [[Bashmaster]], the Unbreakable, is a polar bear who wields a mallet. His base is in [[Juicy Jungle]].

== Behind the Scenes ==
The Snowmads take their designs from various arctic and antarctic animals, and are also based on stereotypical vikings. Their name is a pun on "snow" and "nomad," the latter referring to someone from a society/class who herds animals from pasture to pasture with no fixed home, from Greek ''nomás'' (written ''νομᾰ́ς''), which comes from ''némesin'' ("to have a flock graze"; written νέμεσιν).
