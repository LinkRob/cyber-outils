'''Peach's Castle Grounds''' is a golf course in ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]''.

==Description==
Peach&#39;s Castle Grounds takes place outside of [[Princess Peach's Castle]], which is visible in both the first and last holes. Peach's Castle Grounds features a variety of settings, such as forests, grassy fairways, islands, and clifftops above the clouds. Some holes feature [[Warp Pipe]]s, and if the player's character hits a golf ball into one, it transports out of another side. Several tall, bouncy mushrooms appear throughout. If the player's character hits a golf ball onto a mushroom cap, they are given the "tree hazard" penalty.

Peach's Castle Grounds takes inspiration from ''Super Mario 64'', as Hole 8 features a spiraling mountain similar to the one from [[Bob-omb Battlefield]], and holes 2 and 12 feature [[Chain Chomp]]s. If a ball lands near a Chain Chomp, this is registered as out of bounds. [[Toadsworth]], who debuted in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', has a statue of him in hole 7.
