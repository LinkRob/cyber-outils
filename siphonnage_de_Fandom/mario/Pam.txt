'''Pam''' is a tennis player of the all-around type in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]]. She is partnered with [[Beth]] in doubles. Pam is also an unlockable playable character, and she can be unlocked by completing Singles in Junior Champ.
