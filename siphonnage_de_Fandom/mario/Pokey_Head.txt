'''Pokey Heads''' are a sub-type of [[Pokey]] that appear as enemies in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They resemble [[Pokey Sprout]]s but they're green and lack any spikes.

== In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' ==
Pokey Heads appear as common enemies in Desert Galaxies, they attack by hopping slowly towards [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]), they can damage Mario by running into him. To defeat them, you will need to either stomp on their heads or spin into them.

Five of them will attack you at once in one of the scenarios of Dusty Dune Galaxy. You have to beat them to make a Red Pokey spawn, and you have to beat that one for a [[Launch Star]].

== In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' ==
In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', they are seen in the [[Slipsand Galaxy]]. Despite them appearing, regular Pokeys are absent from the game.

== Trivia ==
*Pokey Heads behave very similarly to Pokey Sprouts from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.
