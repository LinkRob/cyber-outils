'''Carlton''' is the 16th boss faced by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in the VS COM mode of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. He wears steel armor, bearing the appearance of a typical medieval knight wearing one, and he also wears a red cape with it.
