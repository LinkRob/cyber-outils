'''Tornado Jump''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear Tornado Jump by using two [[Badge Point|BP]], and in doing so, Mario can use a certain [[boots|jumping]] attack for three [[Flower Point|FP]] in which a large twister attacks all midair enemies.

Tornado Jump is found inside of the well in [[Creepy Steeple]]. By going deep in the well, Mario can find Tornado Jump in a room filled with [[Buzzy Beetle]]s. Tornado Jump can also be obtained by purchasing it at the [[Pianta Parlor]] for 67 [[Pianta Token]]s.
