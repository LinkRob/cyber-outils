A '''Bubble Baby Yoshi''' is a variant of [[Baby Yoshi]] appearing mainly in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bubble Baby Yoshis have the exact appearance of a regular blue [[Baby Yoshi]].

===Behavior===
While a Bubble Baby Yoshi is being held by a character, it can blow out [[bubble]]s that can trap enemies and can be bounced on by the characters.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. U''==
Bubble Baby Yoshis can be seen on the [[world]] map in [[Frosted Glacier]] and [[Rock-Candy Mines]], and can be met and taken to any other course except for [[castle]]s and [[tower]]s. A Bubble Baby Yoshi also adds a chorus to the current music, one he himself creates.

In the [[Wii U]] games, the player can have a Bubble Baby Yoshi release bubbles by either shaking the {{button|wii|Wiimote}} or pressing the {{button|wiiu|zr}} button on {{button|wiiu|GamePad}}, while in ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe'', this can be done by tapping {{button|ns|r}}.

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''==
A Bubble Baby Yoshi appears on a collectible [[Battle Card]] alongside Blue Toad.
