|time=600 seconds}}'''{{world|coin|3}}''' is the third level of [[World Coin]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Layout==
This level is a [[Ghost House]] level, which takes place on a descending platform with [[Boo]]s. It is very similar to the elevators of other Ghost House levels in the game. 

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In the beginning of the level, to the right. It is very easy to see.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Halfway through the level, all the way to the right.
*'''Star Coin 3''': At the end of the elevator ride, all the way to the left.
