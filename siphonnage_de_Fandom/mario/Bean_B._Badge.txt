The '''Bean B. Badge''' is one of the [[badge]]s featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. It is only sold at the clothes store in [[Beanbean Castle Town]]

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', it costs 78 [[coin]]s to buy (or 100 in the Japanese version). It increases [[BP]] by 6 and [[Power]] by 10.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Bean B. Badge becomes available at the clothes shop once the [[Chuckolator]] has been defeated. It costs 100 coins like in the original version's Japanese version. It instead increases BP by 15 and POW by 22. The Bean B. Badge can be sold for 25 coins.
