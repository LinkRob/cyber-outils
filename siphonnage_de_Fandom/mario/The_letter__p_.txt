'''The letter "p" ''' (『ン』のもじ ''"n" no moji ''in [[wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]) is a special item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
It is needed by [[Mario]] and [[Vivian]] to guess [[Doopliss]]' name. The item can be found in the basement of the [[Creepy Steeple]] where Mario must head down the well in order to get to it. In the room with The letter "p", there is also Doopliss' pet [[Parrot (character)|Parrot]] that will reveal his name after [[Veil|spying on it]]. With both The letter "p" and Doopliss' actual name revealed, Mario can finally defeat the ghoul to get his body back.

==Trivia==
*The letter "p" is in the same gradient color as the title for ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''.
