}}'''Grabby Gridiron''' is a 2 vs. 2 minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the two teams are competing in a football match where they must toss footballs into the goals. The footballs are fired from a cannon where they will bounces around on the field where someone can grab it by running into it. The players can also punch other players that are holding footballs to make them drop it. The team to score five points first wins.
