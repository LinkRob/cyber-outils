'''Bug Bite''' is one of [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[microgame]]s, categorized under the [[Nature]] folder, in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of a mosquito, who has to avoid the clouds of insect repellent and bite the baby. Once the mosquito does this, the player wins the microgame.

The number of insect repellent clouds differs between each difficulty level, each adding two more clouds than the previous level; Level 1 features two clouds, Level 2 features four clouds, and Level 3 features six clouds.
