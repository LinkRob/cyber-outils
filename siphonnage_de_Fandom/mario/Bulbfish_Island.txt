'''Bulbfish Island''' is a part of [[Concordia]], visited during the events of [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']]. It is the first island to be found drifting in the [[Brrrning Sea]], and the ninth one connected to [[Shipshape Island]]'s [[Uni-Tree]]. The island is named for its signature delicacy, Bulbfishes, a glowing species of fish raised on the island. A large cloud hovers above Bulbfish Island, rendering it always dark regardless the time of day.

== Layout ==
Bulbfish Island is divided into two major areas: the village and the underground tunnel. It has yellow-brown sand as most of its ground.

=== Starting Area ===
Mario and Luigi land to the south of the village.

=== Bulbfish Village ===
The island's most notable location is the village that dominates its area. There are many buildings here. [[Chief Leyden]] lives in the large house to the north.

==== Bulbfish Pen ====
At the village's southwestern corner is the Bulbfish Pen, where the island's inhabitants raise Bulbfishes.

=== Underground Tunnel ===
This area is a cellar with [[Foopfly|Foopflies]] and [[Capnap#Variations|Capnap R]]'s. The southern entrance is at the southeastern corner of the village, while the northern entrance leads to the large house where Leyden lives.

=== Lighthouse ===
Bulbfish Island's [[Lighthouse (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Lighthouse]] is at its far north end with purple steps.

== History ==

=== The Bulbfish Egg Theft ===
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] set foot upon Bulbfish Island after their arrival in the [[Brrrning Sea]]. Upon arrival, they hear about a Bulbfish egg theft, with many citizens claiming that [[Chief Leyden]] had stolen them. He vehemently denies it, and tasks the Bros. with clearing his name and finding the real thief. Mario and Luigi also find that many citizens are refusing to associate with others, distinguished by a blood red aura around their body. Mario and Luigi find a lead that suggests that Leyden's assistant may be responsible, but a [[Bulbfish Island Guard|guard]] forbids entry to the underground area. However, he agrees to let the Bros. pass on the condition that the Bros. answer his questions. Answering all three questions correctly allows them to enter the underground tunnel.

=== Investigating The Crime ===
During their investigation of the underground region, the Bros. find the assistant's diary, where he rants about his grievances with Leyden, further implicating the assistant. However, when the Bros. return outside, they discover that the assistant had blamed [[Prince Chilliam]] of [[Slippenglide Island]] and [[Princess Burnadette]] of [[Skorcheen Island]]. This prompts them to talk to the Guard. He tasks the Bros. with finding his brother in exchange for the key. The Bros. give away the diary to someone called the Secret Snatcher, who reveals that the Guard's brother is inside the Bulbfish restaurant. The brother explains that the thief wore glasses, leading the Bros. to conclude that the assistant had stolen the eggs. The Bros. return to the Guard with their information, but a [[Mettozokettohei#Būmeranzokettohei|Būmeranzokettohei]] attacks him, poisoning him. The Guard runs away, but the Bros. manage to get their hands on the key, springing Chilliam and Burnadette and unlocking the rest of the underground tunnel.

=== Confronting The Perpetrator ===
Mario and Luigi make their way through the underground tunnel, and spot [[Extension Corps|Ecks]] underground, where Ecks reveals that [[Zokket]]'s [[Zokket Army|goons]] have been spreading a toxin called [[Glohm]], which has been causing the aloofness of the Concordians with the blood red aura. Mario and Luigi end up in the house and head up to the roof, where they catch the assistant in the act of disposing of the eggs. Leyden stops the assistant, who is revealed to be his son. The assistant admits to having resented his father and plotting to replace him, and Leyden admits to his poor parenting and that he raised his assistant poorly. However, they get into a fight, with one accusing the other of  The assistant is ready to be put in the slammer for his crime, but the villagers instead propose that he does Leyden's work as its mayor. Mario and Luigi can then finish their business by connecting Bulbfish Island's lighthouse to [[Shipshape Island]]'s [[Uni-Tree]].

== Profile ==
Bulbfish Island's in-game description reads, ''"An island that's dark and cloudy all year round. Famous for its Bulbfish."''
