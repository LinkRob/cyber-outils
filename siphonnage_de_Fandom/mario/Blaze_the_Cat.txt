'''Blaze the Cat''' is a cat princess from the ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' franchise. introduced to the series in ''Sonic Rush''. Blaze first appears alongside ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'' for the [[Wii]].

==Profile==
===Powers and abilities===
Blaze has can spawn and control fire, a skill known as pyrokinesis.

===Personality===
Blaze was originally withdrawn and aloof until she met [[Cream the Rabbit]], whom she learned the meaning and value of friendship from, along with [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] and [[Miles "Tails" Prower|Tails]].

Typically, Blaze is serious, quiet, and not too good at making friends. She takes her job very seriously, to a somewhat unhealthy extent. However, Blaze has a strong sense of justice.

She is also featured as a playable character in every installment in the ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' series, and she is of the All-Around type. Blaze is fast and has the most power and the lowest skill of any female character in ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games''. Blaze has also made cameo appearances in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] since ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

==Background==
Blaze is the princess of an alternate dimension and the guardian of the [[w:c:sonic:Sol Emerald|Sol Emeralds]], her world's equivalent to the [[Chaos Emerald]]s. Blaze first met Sonic in ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Rush|Sonic Rush]]'', when she traveled to his dimension to recover the Sol Emeralds.

==In {{series|Mario & Sonic}} ==
{{Sonic Wiki Zone|Blaze the Cat/History and appearances}}
=== ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'' ===
Blaze is a playable character of the All-Around type in both the Wii version and the Nintendo DS version. Blaze is fast and possesses the most power but lowest skill of any female character.

=== ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' ===
Blaze retains her role of being a playable character in both the Wii version and the Nintendo DS version of the game. She is once again a Power type excelling in Speed.

In [[Adventure Tours]], Blaze has a minigame called [[Move the Blocks!]], where she must use her fire powers and move the signed blocks to the same signed floor. If the player makes a mistake, they can move Blaze to the red button and reset the puzzle by pressing it. She then joins the team.

=== ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' (Wii) ===
Blaze reappears as a playable character of the All-Around type. She is now voiced by Laura Bailey, reprising her role from 2010's ''Sonic Free Riders''. In this game, Blaze wears her athletic wear from ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'' for most Events, her winter gear for Equestrian events and a new outfit for Gymnastics and Aquatics events. In the opening, Blaze appears at the start with all of the other athletes, as well as competing in Synchronized Swimming and Dream Uneven Bars. An updated version with the athletic wear of the Mii outfit appears, and Blaze is also a character that can be spoken to in order to play minigames and earn stickers in London Party mode.

=== ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' (Nintendo 3DS) ===
Blaze is a playable character in the Girls group, so she is only playable in certain events involving that group. A badge of Blaze can also be obtained from the badge machine.

In the Story Mode, Blaze first appears protecting a group of [[Toad (species)|Toads]] with [[Princess Peach|Peach]] from some [[Fog Imposter|Fog Imposters]], which they manage to defeat and then save [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]] and [[Amy Rose|Amy]]. The group then find [[Rouge the Bat|Rouge]], and after recovering from her brainwashing she leads them to Hyde Park's Fog machine, which Blaze destroys. Blaze decides to stay behind in Hyde Park with the other girls, Rouge and Toads to keep an eye on things there. She later calls [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] and [[Miles "Tails" Prower|Tails]] to support them when they face [[Bowser]] and [[Dr. Eggman]], and appears at the opening ceremony for the games. In the bonus episodes, Blaze appears volunteering to help with the invitations with Peach, the Toads and Amy, and challenges Amy to an Olympic Event to try to stop her when she asks to deliver Sonic's invitation personally. Although Amy wins, Blaze manages to accidentally knock the invitations onto the floor, so Amy decides to stay anyway to help pick them up. Blaze later helps Princess Peach when she tries to stop Daisy from taking Amy on a shopping trip. Blaze helps to mail the invitations out, and after the group concludes that Bowser and Dr. Eggman got their invitations, Blaze tries to head back to the hotel, but is convinced to go on a shopping trip with Peach, Daisy, Amy and the Toads instead.

=== ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'' ===
Blaze is a playable character. She is classified as an All-Around character, as usual, and retains her winter outfit from ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''. In [[Legends Showdown]], Blaze is partnered with [[Mario]], [[Princess Peach]], and [[Silver the Hedgehog|Silver]]. There is another [[Mii]] costume of Blaze, which has a specific unlock criteria of having to complete [[Short Track Speed Skating 1000m]] with all three laps having a lap time of at least below 11.5 seconds.

=== ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' ===
Blaze returns as a playable character. Another Mii costume of Blaze was added in the Wii U version. It can be obtained by completing Blaze's Carnival Challenge, although in two separate parts.
{{sectstub}}

=== ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]'' ===
Blaze returns as a playable character. She is among the characters who wear outfits suited for the event are event-appropriate. Her voice actor was changed to Erica Lindbeck.

==In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' ==
Blaze appears in the form of a support spirit. A fighter equipped with it gains the "Mouthful of Curry" effect, allowing them to breathe a stream of constant fire as if they were under the effects of [[Superspicy Curry]].
