An '''L Block''' is a [[block]] complementary to [[M Block]]s in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}.

==Description==
L Blocks are block similar to [[Question Block]]s in which when bumped with a characters head from below, an obtainable such as a [[Coin]] or a power-up comes out of it, the leading to the block turning brown and becoming useless. The difference with the L Block is that only [[Luigi]] (and [[Baby Luigi]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''). This is similarly the case to the block's counterpart, [[M Block]], which is only usable by [[Mario]] (and [[Baby Mario]] in ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''). As with other blocks, the further Luigi progresses, the more treasures can be obtained from L Blocks along the way, such as [[Mushroom (healing item)|Mushroom]]s. In some cases, L Blocks can contain multiple [[50-Coin]]s.
