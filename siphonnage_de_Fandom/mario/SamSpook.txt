'''SamSpook''' is the fourth boss encountered by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in the Versus mode of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'', or the first boss on Medium difficulty.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
SamSpook is humanoid character with a green pumpkin for a head. His mouth is jagged and he has semicircular eyes with the flat sides on the bottom. SamSpook's clothing is comparable to [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]], especially the latter due to his size, in that he wears a pair of overalls atop an orange shirt as well as white gloves. SamSpook also wears a pair of round shoes.
