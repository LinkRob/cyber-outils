'''Lucky Stars''' is one of [[Luigi]]'s [[minigame]]s in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

== Objective ==
The objective of the game is for the player to guess which card features the most stars. The player will be given ten [[coin]]s to begin with, meaning they have ten chances. If the player happens to pick a card that has more stars than the following card, more coins will be rewarded. If the card the player select has less coins than the following one, coins will be deducted. If all coins are lost, then it is [[Game Over]].
