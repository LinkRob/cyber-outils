The '''Boo's Portrait''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
The Boo's Portrait can be found in the library of the basement of [[Boo's Mansion]]. It is required to be obtained as it is needed to access the third floor in order to see [[Lady Bow]]. The Boo's Portrait can talk and it askes Mario to give Lady Bow his best regards.

== Trivia ==
*Despite being an item, the Boo's Portrait can actually talk. This is the first item in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' that can talk with the other being [[Yakkey]] (who is found later in the same chapter being Chapter 3).
