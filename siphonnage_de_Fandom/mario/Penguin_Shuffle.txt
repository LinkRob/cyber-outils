'''Penguin Shuffle''' is one of [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''. The microgame was later remade in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as [[Paddle Parade]], but it features ducklings instead of [[Penguin (real world)|penguins]].

==Layout==
The minigame takes pla+ ce in an arctic area. The penguins have black feathers while the baby penguin has blue feathers. There is a polar bear in the background, but it does not have a gameplay purpose.

==Gameplay==
The player controls the baby penguin, who must walk in unison to prevent bumping another penguin. If the baby penguin bumps into another penguin, the player loses at the microgame.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The penguins take a brief pause before they move left.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' Similar to level 1, they stop afterwards.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The penguins take a brief pause before they move left, then pause and move right.
