{{Nihongo|'''Clearing the Table'''|ひっぱれテーブルクロス|Hippare tēburukurosu|Hit Parade Tablecloth}} is an unlockable [[Rhythm Minigame|Rhythm]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The players position their hands to pull the tablecloth and the camera zooms closer to which the players' hands are visible. The metronome ticks and the red arrow points where the player must pull the tablecloth.

{{clr}}
== Gameplay ==
[[File:SMP Clearing the Table.gif|thumb|left|200px|Gameplay of Clearing the Table.]]
The player's aim is to earn many points by pulling the tablecloth at the right time. The player must depend on the rhythm of the objects falling onto the tablecloth (glassware or Thwomp). If timed correctly, the player earns three points; the objects stay in place. If the player pulls the tablecloth late or early, they earn one point; the objects move but do not fall off the table. If the player pulls the tablecloth when not all of the objects appear on the table, the player earns no points, and the objects collapse.

{{clr}}
== Ending ==
The winner(s) do(es) their victory pose while other players sulk defeated.
