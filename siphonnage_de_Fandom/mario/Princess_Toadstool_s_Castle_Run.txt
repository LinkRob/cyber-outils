'''''Princess Toadstool's Castle Run''''' is a [[Super Mario Bros. Watch]] released by [[Nintendo]] in 1990.

==Gameplay==
The game involves playing as [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] as she is running to her [[Princess Peach's Castle|castle]]. Her objective is to dodge any [[Koopa Troopa]]s along the way.

==Trivia==
*This is the first game to feature Princess Peach (then known as Princess Toadstool) as the main protagonist.
