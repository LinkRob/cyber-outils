'''Throw Down''' is a [[Microgame]] from the game ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''. It uses the Massage form for its gameplay, and [[Wario]] has it in his second set, which is featured in the level Lava at First Sight, hosted by [[Volcano Wario]].

==Layout==
Wario is depicted in a blue Martial Arts Gi, wearing his biker helmet on his head and yellow, fingerless gloves on his hands, and his opponent varies by level (a man in a blue-colored Gi on Level 1, a bald-headed man in a green-colored Gi on Level 2, and a cyan-colored Wario clone on Level 3). The scene is a nondescript bluish-green dimension with light cyan streaks speeding by.

==Gameplay==
The player must swing their Joy-Cons the moment [[Wario]] and his opponent grab each other's hands. If the player succeeds with good timing (There is some room for error.), Wario performs a suplex on his foe and raises his fists in triumph as the dimension becomes dots. Failure causes the opponent to throw Wario in a suplex instead. If the player swings their Joy-Cons too quickly, Wario and his opponent look at the camera, confused. No tricks are performed on the first level, while on the second level, the opponent can grab more quickly. On level 3, the enemy tries to fake the player out.
