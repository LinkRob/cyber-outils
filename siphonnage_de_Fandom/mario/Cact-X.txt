The '''Cact-X''' is an unlockable [[kart]] part which appeared in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' and returned in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
Its name is a pun on "cactus", explaining its cactus-like size on the kart. In its reappearance in ''Mario Kart Tour'', the kart is equipped with the [[Sponge]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== In {{series|Mario Kart}} ==
=== ''Mario Kart 7'' ===
[[File:ShyGuyrideCactx.png|thumb|left|Shy Guy driving a Cact-X in {{Classic course|N64|[[Kalimari Desert]]}}]]
The Cact-X offers a boost in [[handling]] and [[Off-Road|off-road]], but lowers [[speed]] and [[acceleration]]. In terms of hidden stats, the Cact-X offers a boost in sea speed, but lowers air speed and [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]].

A CPU-controlled [[Toad (character)|Toad]] can be seen using the Cact-X kart body often.

The Cact-X is also used for the Normal [[Staff Ghost]] on {{Classic course|N64|Kalimari Desert}} using [[Shy Guy]], equipped with the [[Wood]] tires and the [[Swooper (glider)|Swooper]] glider, and is used the Expert Staff Ghost for {{Classic course|Wii|[[Koopa Cape]]}} using [[Koopa Troopa]], equipped with the [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires and the Flower Glider.

=== ''Mario Kart Tour'' ===
The Cact-X first debuted in the [[Wild West Tour]], where it is classified as a High-End kart. When used, it increases the duration of [[Drafting|Slipstream]] boosts and the points earned for getting them. 

The game introduces several new variants of the kart, such as the [[Cact-Ice]], which first appeared in the [[Snow Tour]], the [[Cact-R]] which first appeared in the [[Toad vs. Toadette Tour]], and the the Cact-B which first appeared in the [[Bowser Tour (2022)|2022 Bowser Tour]].
