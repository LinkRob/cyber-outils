The '''Sunken Ghost Ship''' is a [[level]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''.

==Description==
The Sunken Ghost Ship is a sunken ship on the bottom of the sea. It is located outside the [[Valley of Bowser]]. It is the only ghost level where [[Yoshi]] accompanies [[Mario]].

After defeating [[Wendy O. Koopa]], Mario or [[Luigi]] are required to swim through the Sunken Ghost Ship before they can make the doorway to the Valley of Bowser emerge from the sea.

==Layout==
[[File:Sunken Ghost Ship.png|thumb|left|200px|Mario swimming between several Disappearing Boo Buddies]]
Mario or Luigi begins by coming out of a [[Warp Pipe]] underwater next to the sunken ship. There is a [[? Block]] containing a power-up as well as some [[Bill Blaster]]s just ahead. After passing the Bill Blasters, Mario or Luigi has to enter a yellow Warp Pipe.

Upon entering the following area, [[Eerie]]s and a large swarm of [[Disappearing Boo Buddy|Disappearing Boo Buddies]] can be found. After passing these, Mario or Luigi come across [[Circling Boo Buddies]] and two [[Boo Buddy|Boo Buddies]]. Afterward, they can enter another Warp Pipe at the end.

The third area begins with a drop, and the player can optionally grab a [[Super Star|Starman]] during their descent. Falling [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s are also encountered on the way down. Halfway down, Mario can find a ? Block that contains another Starman, and even further down are lots of [[coin]]s and falling [[Floating Mine|Mines]]. Mario or Luigi can fall right to avoid entering the water with the Mines. On the right is a green [[? Ball]], which Mario or Luigi must touch to finish the level.
