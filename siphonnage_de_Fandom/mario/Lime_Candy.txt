The '''Lime Candy''' is a [[recipe]] and an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
The Lime Candy can be created by having [[Tayce T.]] cook together a [[Cake Mix]] and a [[Lime]]. The Cake Mix can be found in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] while the [[Lime]] can be found at the oasis in [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]]. The Lime Candy can restore 20 FP which is pretty useful throughout the game.
