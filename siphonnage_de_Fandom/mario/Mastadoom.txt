'''Mastadoom''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Mastadooms are large, skeletal elephants that can only be found on [[Star Hill]]. They are never seen on the overworld and can only be encountered in battle alongside other enemies.

==Battle==
Mastadooms mainly attack with their large trunks dealing rather high damage to a single member of [[Mario]]'s party. They are also capable of using powerful spells such as [[Drain Beam]] and [[Blizzard (move)|Blizzard]].

===Strategy===
Mastadooms are weak against fire attacks, so Mario's [[Fire Orb]] or [[Super Flame]] is very effective against them.
