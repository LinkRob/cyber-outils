The '''Red Berry''' is a type of [[berry]] and an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
The Red Berry restores [[Mario]]'s [[Heart Point|HP]] by 5. It can only be acquired by smacking a single tree in [[Flower Fields]], in the area with [[Petunia]], with a [[Hammer]].

In addition to being a healing item, Red Berries can be given to the Red Flower guarding the red gate in Flower Fields. This path leads to [[Posie]], a plant who has fused her roots with a [[Crystal Tree]].
