'''Calorites''' are a fire-elemental enemy encountered in the [[Flab Zone]] of [[Bowser's body]] by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. Their name and fire capabilities is based on {{w|calorie}}s, a unit of energy involving heat.
