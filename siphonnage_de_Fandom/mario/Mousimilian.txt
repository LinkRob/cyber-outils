|race = [[Little Mouser|Squeek]]}}'''Mousimilian''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a [[Little Mouser|Squeek]] that is always seen standing outside of the [[Item Shop]] in [[Rogueport]].

==History==
Mousimilian would commonly smell [[Mario]] and tell him about his various of scents whenever he completes a Chapter. Mousimilian also has a [[Trouble Center|trouble]] that involves hitting him many times until he remembers what he had to do. After hitting him enough times, Mousimilian will remember that he can buy a [[Sleepy Sheep]] at the nearby Item Shop and sell it to the one in [[Petalburg]] for a 2-[[coin]] profit. Although Mousimilian doesn't really reward Mario for helping him out, one can use Mousimilian's plan to make a profit each time for themselves.

==Trivia==
*Mousimilian's name is a combination of "mouse" and "Maximilian".
