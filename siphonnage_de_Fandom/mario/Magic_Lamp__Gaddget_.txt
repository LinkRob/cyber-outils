'''Magic Lamp''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be obtained by completing the quest [[Bowserstein!]].

==Layout==
The Gaddget shows a golden lamp on an empty black background.

==Gameplay==
The player can rub the magic lamp using a cursor to summon a genie and make a wish. In order for the genie to emerge, the player has to rub the lamp in a circular motion while rubbing the star designs.
