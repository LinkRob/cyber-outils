'''Wendy's Blitz Snarlton Hotel''' is a location in ''[[Hotel Mario]]''.

==Description==
Wendy's Blitz Snarlton Hotel is one of seven [[Koopa hotel]]s. It is specifically owned by [[Wendy O. Koopa]]. Wendy's Blitz Snarlton Hotel resembles a casino, and it is named after the {{w|The Ritz-Carlton Hotel Company|Ritz-Carlton Hotel}}.

Enemies in Wendy's Blitz Snarlton Hotel include [[Bellhop Goomba]]s, [[Z Koopa]]s, [[Mechakoopa|Mecha Koopa]]s, [[Rhinestone Goomba]]s and [[Penny (enemy)|Pennies]]. There are nine regular stages and a boss stage named [[Wendy's Sweet Suite]]. There is a second screen for each stage, so once [[Mario]] closes every door, an extra elevator at the top floor takes him to the next half of the area.
