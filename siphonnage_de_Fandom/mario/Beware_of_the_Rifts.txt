'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is the fourth course of the [[Fungi Mines]] world in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It is unlocked after [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and company inspect the [[Loyal Poplin]]'s house and find a note the Poplin left behind at his house, then collect the [[Wonder Seed]], and its own completion unlocks Break Time! Tumble House. The main enemies are [[Shadow Rift|Shadow Rifts]] that move in various directions and damage players on contact. Two [[Wonder Seed|Wonder Seeds]] are present in the course, which is given a difficulty rating of three stars.

== Layout ==

=== Starting Area ===
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] starts in an area where [[Shadow Rift|Shadow Rifts]] move through the ground and ceiling; up ahead is a red [[Koopa Troopa]] on patrol. After the Koopa Troopa, he will come across another red Koopa Troopa and a gap where some rifts move up and down. Mario can hit some close-by [[Mystery Block|Mystery Blocks]] with [[Wow Bud|Wow Buds]] that cause pipes to spawn to form a bridge, which he uses to cross the gap. This is followed by more shadow rifts that Mario must avoid and more Koopa Troopas. He can also find the first Big Flower Coin here by activating a Mystery Block with a Wow Bud, but needs to use a nearby Koopa Troopa's shell at it to get it. 

=== Area 2 ===
This section starts with a [[Checkpoint]] that is followed with a Mystery Block that causes a red [[POW Block]] to appear. One of the two Mystery Blocks afterward contains a Wow Bud. There is also a [[Hidden Character Block]] that Mario can hit to get [[Coin|Coins]] and [[Flower Coin|Flower Coins]] There is a Mystery Block below the bonus room, and hitting it causes a [[Beanstalk]] to grow, leading to the bonus room. After those, there are two Mystery Blocks. One contains a Wow Bud that causes red pipes to rise from the upcoming pit; Mario can use them as a bridge. The other summons Coins and a Big Flower Coin to fall down.

=== Bonus Room ===
If Mario grew the Beanstalk, he can climb the Beanstalk akin to a ladder and go to a small room. There are many Shadow Rifts near the top of the room, and two Brick Blocks close to the ground with a space between them. This space contains a [[Hidden Block]] that activates a Wow Bud, which removes the rifts, causing Coins, Flower Coins, and the second Big Flower Coin to fall down. There is also a Hidden Character Block that only [[Princess Peach]] can hit; if she does, she gets a single Flower Coin.

=== Shadow Rift Mario Zone ===
After the pit, Mario finds the second Checkpoint. He will then come across a [[Wonder Flower]], which must be collected to start a [[Wonder Effect]]. When the Wonder Effect starts, a ghostly monster named [[Rift Mario]] enters the stage, which Mario finds to his horror, beginning a boss battle. Rift Mario chases Mario throughout the room, and can be stunned if Mario strikes a POW Block. Rift Mario is also aided by [[Shadow Koopa Troopa|Shadow Koopa Troopas]]. He must be defeated by grabbing all five [[Wonder Token|Wonder Tokens]] that spawn throughout the room and then grabbing the [[Wonder Seed]] that appears; once Mario grabs the Wonder Seed, Rift Mario and his Shadow Koopa Troopas get disintegrated, leading to their defeat and allowing Mario to continue to the final area.

=== Final Area ===
This area is a small room that contains the [[Goal Pole]], which Mario must grab to clear the level.

== Big Flower Coin Locations ==

# Right before the first [[Checkpoint]], Mario can throw a Koopa Troopa's shell to the Big Flower Coin to get it.
# Hidden in the Bonus Room, spawned when Mario hits the Invisible Block.
# Falls from the ceiling near the gap before the second Checkpoint when Mario strikes one of the Mystery Blocks with Wow Buds. The other spawns pipes that catch the Big Flower Coin.
