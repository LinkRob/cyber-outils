'''Gold Draggadon''' is a gold-colored [[Draggadon]] who appears in the game ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' and its Nintendo Switch and Nintendo 3DS ports.

==''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker''==
In [[Secret of the Golden Realm|Episode 3-13: Secret of the Golden Realm]], [[Captain Toad]] must battle Gold Draggadon. The boss fight is similar to the one with [[Draggadon]], but with faster [[fireball]]s and the [[lava]] rising quicker. After its defeat, it plays a crucial role in reuniting Captain Toad with his fellow explorer [[Toadette]]. 

In the Nintendo Switch version, Gold Draggadon is also ridden on during the bonus level [[Draggadon's Gold Rush]] in the [[Special Episode]]. When controlling Gold Draggadon, its fire works similarly to [[Gold Mario]]'s fireballs, turning [[brick block]]s into [[coin]]s.

When facing Gold Draggadon for the first time, it lacks a bump on its head, indicating that it is a different Draggadon from the one that Captain Toad and Toadette encountered earlier.
