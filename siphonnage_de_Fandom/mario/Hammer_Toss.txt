'''Hammer Toss''' is one of the [[microgame]]s under [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]]'s [[Sports]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
There is a man standing in a light gray circle surrounded by a wide, dark green area presumably meant to represent grass. To the right is a yellow area separated by white lines.

==Gameplay==
The man is swinging a ball and chain, and the player must press {{button|gba|A}} to have him throw the ball while it is lined up with the yellow area. The target area becomes smaller with each increasing difficulty level. If the ball is thrown within the yellow area, the player wins, but if it lands elsewhere, they lose.
