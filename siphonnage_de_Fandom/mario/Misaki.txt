'''Misaki''' is a professional martial artist from [[Sayonara]] who appears in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Karate Koopa]]." He helps [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] to fight off [[Karate Koopa (alias)|Karate Koopa]] ([[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s alias for the episode) and his [[Ninji]]s.

==Physical description==
Misaki is an Asian man whom wears his hear in a ponytail. He wears an indigo robe, which has an orange stripe around the collar, and a pair of black sandals.
