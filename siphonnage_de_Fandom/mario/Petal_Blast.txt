|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Petal Blast''' is a deadly special move used by certain bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

=Description=
When Petal Blast is used, the screen becomes filled with pink petals that last about five seconds.

==Effects==
With Petal Blast, the user is able to cause rather high damage to all characters and even inflict the [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]] status onto them. The mushroom side affect can be nullified by wearing a Trueform Pin, the Lazy Shell armor, or the Safety Pin.
