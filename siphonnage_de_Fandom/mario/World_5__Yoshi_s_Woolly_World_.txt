''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''|levels=9|prewor=[[World 4 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 4]]|nxtwor=[[World 6 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 6]]}}'''World 5''' is the fifth world of ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. It is an ice-themed world that has nine levels. Eight are compulsory but there is [[Yoshi's Curtain Call|one optional level]] which is unlocked after collecting all 40 [[Smiley Flower|Smiley Flowers]] in the other levels of World 5. Completing this world unlocks the final world of the game, [[World 6 (Yoshi's Woolly World)|World 6]].
