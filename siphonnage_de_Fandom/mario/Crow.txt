'''Crows''' are a real-world animal which appear in some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, most commonly the {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}. They first appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' for [[Nintendo GameCube]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
In ''Luigi's Mansion'' and ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', Crows are black-feathered like real-world crows, they are tall and stocky, and they have eyes on each side of their head. In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'', Crows are instead purple-feathered, and they are quite short and lean and have eyes on the front of their head.

==In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}==
===''Luigi's Mansion''===
Crows make a cameo during the opening cutscene, as Luigi travels through [[Boo Woods]] on his way to the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]].

===''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''===
Crows are one of the non-ghost enemies, and most of them are encountered in [[Haunted Towers]]. A Crow can damage [[Luigi]] upon contact, but he can instantly vacuum it into his [[Poltergust 5000]] or flash the [[Strobulb]] to defeat a Crow, causing it to drop a [[bill]] or some [[heart]]s. Crows also received their own variant, [[Gold Crow]]s.

===''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''===
Both Crows and Gold Crows return as enemies from ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'', retaining their behavior. Both types also utilize a swooping attack similar to [[Crowber]]s from the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' sub-series.

==In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''==
Crows appear on various trees in [[Twilight Town]]. At first, if [[Mario]] talks to a crow, it just caws, but if Mario uses [[Vivian]] and hides under a tree, they say other things. According to Vivian, the Crows only pretend to be ignorant when humans are around. Crows have the ability to talk about a variety of subjects, and a few even provide tips.
