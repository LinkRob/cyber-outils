'''World 3-3''' is the third [[level]] of [[World 3 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]] in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its overseas version ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. A second battle against [[Mouser]] takes place at the end, although in ''[[Super Mario Advance]]'', he is replaced with [[Robirdo]].
