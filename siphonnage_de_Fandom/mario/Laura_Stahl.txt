'''Laura Megan Stahl''' (born August 12, 1991) is an American voice actor and voice director, In the [[Super Mario (series)|''Mario'' series]], she is the current voice of [[Toadette]], beginning with [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']], succeeding [[Samantha Kelly]]. <ref> @laura_stahl (July 22, 2025) "[https://x.com/laura_stahl/status/1947741373853274181 peeking out of a social media hiatus to share that I am the voice of Toadette in #mariokartworld! grateful to  and   for the opportunity! im still in disbelief, and so, so grateful thanks, and happy racing]!" Retrieved September 3rd, 2025

 
</ref>
