'''Card Trick''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be obtained by completing the [[Probably a Robbery?]] quest.

==Description==
The Card Trick is named for and based on the card tricks done by magicians. It involves the player having to think of a card and then selecting the row of each card three times. The player is then shown which card the Gaddget believes they have selected.
