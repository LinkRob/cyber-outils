'''Max Milliplatt''' is a character in the ''[[Club Nintendo (magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' comic "[[Super Mario: Coole Klänge]]."

==Comic appearances==
==="Super Mario: Coole Klänge"===
Max Milliplatt is the boss of the record company [[Koopa Records]], which he signs [[Mario]] on, despite Mario not being able to sing well. Max has a secretary called [[Mona (Club Nintendo)|Mona]].
