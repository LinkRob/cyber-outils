==Features==
In this Mystery House, each room contains fighting a certain type of enemy in 10 seconds. There are 5 stars and 12 enemies.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': In Section 1, defeat the two [[Galoomba]]s and grab the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 2''': In Section 2, defeat the two [[Koopa Troopa]]s and grab the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 3''': In Section 3, defeat the two [[Cat Goomba]]s and grab the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 4''': In Section 4, defeat the four [[Conkdor]]s and grab the star within ten seconds.
*'''Green Star 5''': In Section 5, defeat the two [[Fire Bro.|Fire Bros]]. and grab the star within ten seconds.
