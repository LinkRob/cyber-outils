'''Togēba''' are enemies in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Togēba are spiky blue blobs.

===Behavior===
Togēba are invincible enemies, capable of shrugging off enemies thrown at it and even [[Fat Wario]]'s attacks. Performing a [[Ground Pound (move)|Smash Attack]] near a Togēba causes it to fly up into the air with its spikes withdrawn while slowly floating its way back down. Performing a Smash Attack on a Togēba as it floats downward causes its eyes to bulge out, but it is otherwise not affected.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Togēba are encountered in five [[level]]s. They first appear in [[A Town in Chaos]], where three of them are encountered, followed by two more in [[The Steep Canyon]], one in [[Cave of Flames]], three more in [[Above the Clouds]], and lastly one in [[Castle of Illusions]]. While a Togēba is in midair with its spikes withdrawn, Wario can either run underneath it or use it as a temporary platform.
