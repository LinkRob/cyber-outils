'''Z-Yuxes''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They can summon [[Mini-Z-Yux]]es to guard them.

==History==
Z-Yuxes can only be found on the [[Mushroom Moon]] and inside [[X-Naut Fortress]]. They are the semi-strongest of the Yux family with the regular [[Yux]] being the weakest and the [[X-Yux]] being the strongest (even though Z-Yuxes have more attack, X-Yuxes have more HP and a point of defense). Like with the other two main Yuxes, the Z-Yux can attack from a distance with an energy beam that can [[First Strike]] [[Mario]] if it should connect.
