|race = [[Flip-Flopian|Flipsider]]}}'''Chap''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Chap is a [[Flip-Flopian]] that is blue in color.

==History==
Found in [[The Underwhere (bar)|The Underwhere]] bar in [[Flipside]], Chap spends all of his time drinking milk. Chap appears to have an obsession of milk as that all he ever talks about.

==Trivia==
*Chap has a [[Flopside]] counterpart in [[Chip]] who, instead having a milk obsession like Chap, has a coffee obsession.
*Chap is referred to as "the Map Chap."
