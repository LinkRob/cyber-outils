==Description==
When Flame Stone is used, a large flaming stone rains down on a character.

=== Effects ===
With Flame Stone, the enemy can damage on character on Mario's team for rather minor damage.

==Trivia==
*Despite being made of fire, Flame Stone isn't a fire-type move and it remains neutral.
*This move has been darkened in the Nintendo Wii Virtual Console version of this game to avoid the risk of seizures.
