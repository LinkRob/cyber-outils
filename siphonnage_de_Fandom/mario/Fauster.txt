{{nihongo|'''Fauster'''|アクマン|Akuman}} is the first boss whom [[Toad (character)|Toad]] fights in the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] version of ''[[Wario's Woods]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:Fauster WW NES sprite.png|left]]
Fauster is a devil who wears a dark red jester outfit, has purple bat-like wings, light skin, and two pointed teeth on his lower lip. Fauster carries a trident around with him.
