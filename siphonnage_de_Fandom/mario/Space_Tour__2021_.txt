'''Space Tour''' was the fifthy-seventh tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] that began on November 16, 2021 and ended on November 20, 2021. 

This Tour is mainly space themed in which it introduced [[Mario (Satellaview)]] from [[Super Mario Odyssey|''Super Mario Odyssey'']] as a driver. It also added space items like [[Lunar Cruiser]] and [[meteor Balloons]]. This tour also also featured [[Rainbow Road]] from [[Mario Kart 7|''Mario Kart 7'']].
