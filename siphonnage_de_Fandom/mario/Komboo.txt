'''Komboos''' (also called '''Seaweed Walkers''') are walking seaweed creatures found in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. They mainly attack by running into [[Mario]] while he's underwater, a flick of [[Cappy]] can easily take care of them however, upon defeat, Komboos may drop some [[Coins]]. 

== In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' ==
Komboos are commonly found underwater in the [[Lake Kingdom]] and the [[Seaside Kingdom]]. They're found in underwater sections and cannot leave the water.


==Trivia==
*Their name sounds similar to [[Podoboo]], an unrelated [[Lava]] enemy.
