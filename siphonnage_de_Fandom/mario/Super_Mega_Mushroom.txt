A '''Super Mega Mushroom''' is an [[List of items|item]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Description==
The Super Mega Mushroom turns its user giant-sized, similarly to a normal [[Mega Mushroom]]. This item has the same effect as a Mega Mushroom, but the differences are that the color of this type of mushroom is translucent blue, and the user gets to roll three [[Dice Block]]s instead of two (for a total of 3-30 spaces). This replaces the [[Golden Mushroom]] from ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 3|3]]''. If the player rolls the same numbers, they get a bonus 10 coins (30 coins for three "3's" and 50 coins for three "7's"). A Super Mega Mushroom can be obtained during either the [[Minigame#Mini minigames|Mini Mini-Game]]s on the board, the [[Lottery Shop]], or through the [[Item Shop (Mario Party series)|Item Shops]]. Unlike a [[Mega Mushroom]], this item --along with the [[Super Mini Mushroom]]-- does not appear on a [[Mushroom Space]] nor can its effect be inflicted on opponents (with a [[MiniMega Hammer]]).
