The '''Oho Ocean Seabed''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. It is an underwater network of platforms which is connected with [[Oho Ocean]] and [[Oho Oasis]].

==History==
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] found themselves on Oho Oasis, after being taught how to use the [[Firebrand]] and [[Thunderhand]] techniques, they went to the Oho Ocean Seabed to continue their journey. They swam to the other [[Warp Pipe]] to return to the  [[Beanbean Kingdom]].
