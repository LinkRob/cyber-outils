'''Pi'illoper''' is a character that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. He is very knowledgeable about the [[Pi'illo]] folk and kepts several treasures of their artifacts in his basement at his house at [[Wakeport]]. [[Popple]] attempts to rob him twice, but fails both times, the latter time killing off his plans to stay on the island for more treasure after he incurs the wrath of [[Wiggly]].

==Appearance==

Pi'illoper is a [[Beanish]] character that wears Pi'illo mask. The sleeves of his sweater cover his hands.

==History==

Pi'illoper was mentioned several times by [[Popple]] who attempted to steal his treasure but couldn't find the way to the basement. [[Mario]] and the rest eventually finds him in the attic and was surprised to see Mario and the group with the five pieces of the [[Ultibed]]. Pi'illoper then takes the group down to his basement where he presents them with [[Bedsmith]]'s [[pillow]]. Mario and [[Prince Dreambert]] then enter the [[Dream World]] where they eventually find and save Bedsmith (after having a harrowing fight against a [[Earthwake|guardian whose presence Pi'illoper attempted to give warnings about]]). Mario and the rest then leave for [[Somnom Woods]] leaving Pi'illoper in shock and awe as he didn't know what had happened.

==Trivia==
*Pi'illloper is the only significant Beanish character that didn't appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' or [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|it's remake]].
