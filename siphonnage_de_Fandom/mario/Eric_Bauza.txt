'''Eric Adrian Bauza''' (born December 7, 1979) is an Canadian-American voice actor. His notable roles include [[w:c:looneytunes:Bugs Bunny|Bugs Bunny]], [[w:c:looneytunes:Daffy Duck|Daffy Duck]], [[w:c:looneytunes:Tweety|Tweety]], [[w:c:looneytunes:Marvin the Martian|Marvin the Martian]], [[w:c:looneytunes:Pepé Le Pew|Pepé Le Pew]], [[w:c:looneytunes:Speedy Gonzales|Speedy Gonzales]], [[w:c:looneytunes:Henery Hawk|Henery Hawk]], and [[w:c:looneytunes:Barnyard Dawg|Barnyard Dawg]] in ''[[wikipedia:Looney Tunes Cartoons|Looney Tunes Cartoons]]'', [[w:c:woodywoodpecker:Woody Woodpecker|Woody Woodpecker]] in ''[[wikipedia:Woody Woodpecker (2018 web series)|the 2018 reboot]]'', [[w:c:renandstimpy:Stimpy|Stimpy]] in ''[[wikipedia:Ren & Stimpy "Adult Party Cartoon"|Ren & Stimpy "Adult Party Cartoon"]]'', [[w:c:fairlyoddparents:Foop|Foop]] in ''[[wikipedia:The Fairly OddParents!|The Fairly OddParents]]'', [[w:c:eltigre:Rodolfo Rivera|Rodolfo]]/[[w:c::eltigre:White Pantera|White Pantera]] in ''[[wikipedia:El Tigre: The Adventures of Manny Rivera|El Tigre]]'', [[w:c:turtlepedia:Hamato Yoshi (Rise of the TMNT)|Splinter]] in ''[[wikipedia:Rise of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles|Rise of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles]]'', and [[w:c:starwars:Luke Skywalker|Luke Skywalker]] in ''[[wikipedia:Lego Star Wars|LEGO Star Wars]]''.

Bauza voiced a [[Koopa Troopa]] soldier, [[Diddy Kong (Illumination)|Diddy Kong]], and the [[Toad General]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.
