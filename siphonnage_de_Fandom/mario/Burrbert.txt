'''Burrbert''' is an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Burrberts have an orange body surrounded by spikes.

===Behavior===
Because of its spiked body, a Burrbert can hurt [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] if he touches one. Burrberts can be found stuck to the moving Velcro conveyor belts or walls, some of which are generated from [[Warp Pipe|pipes]].

==In ''Yoshi's Woolly World''==
Burrberts are only found in World 6-6, [[Feel Fuzzy, Get Clingy]]. Yoshi can hit a Burrbert with a [[Yarn Ball]], which temporarily prevents Yoshi from taking damage from it. Unlike most enemies, they cannot be destroyed by a Yarn Ball nor Yoshi himself.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Burrbert's name is derived from "burr," referring to the spiked seeds that inspired {{w|Velcro}}.
