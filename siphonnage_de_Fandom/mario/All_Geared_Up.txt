'''All Geared Up''' is a 4-Player and Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place inside of a grandfather clock.

==Gameplay==
The characters start at the bottom, and their goal is to climb to the top. The characters can ascend by [[jump]]ing onto various [[platform]]s that may be moving, sticking out and in (like a [[Bomp]]), and rotating. The players can jump on opponents to hinder them. The first player who climbs to the top wins. Like other minigames without a [[time limit]], if none of the characters reach the end by five minutes, which is only possible with human players, it is a tie.

==Controls==
*{{button|ds|Pad}} &ndash; Move
*{{button|ds|A}} &ndash; Jump

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''' &ndash; ''Climb the gears and platforms to reach the goal. Some platforms may rotate or disappear after some time.''
*'''Tips''' &ndash; ''Head for the goal by the least crowded path you can find. If you get stuck near someone, you'll waste time trying to make your way upward.''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The minigame title refers to the phrase "all geared up," referring to when a person is ready to go.
