'''Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection''' is one of the features for the [[Nintendo DS]] and [[Wii]]. As the name applies, it uses Wi-Fi Connection, so that players can play certain games via online connection. The first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game to make use of Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection, let alone in general, is ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

The [[Nintendo 3DS]] and the [[Wii U]] received their own successor titled [[Nintendo Network]].

As of May 20, 2014, Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection has been discontinued, making it no longer possible to do regular online play on any Nintendo DS or Wii game without the use of external software.
