'''Big Shiny Line Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. These stickers are more powerful versions of the [[Line Jump]] sticker, a sticker that allows [[Mario]] to attack all enemies with a repeating jump attack.

== Appearance ==
Big Shiny Line Jump stickers look similar to [[Shiny Line Jump]] stickers except the boot is much bigger in size. It takes up nine sticker spaces in the [[Sticker Album]].
