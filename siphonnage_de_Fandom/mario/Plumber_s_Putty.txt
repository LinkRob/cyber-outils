'''Plumber's Putty''' is an item in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
The Plumber's Putty is a tubed substance that one can insert into their ears to protect themselves from the effects of the [[Doom Dancer Music Box]].

==Episode appearances==
The Plumber's Putty only physically appears in "[[Do the Koopa]]." When [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] uses the [[Doom Dancer Music Box]] to force everyone to dance to "[[Do the Koopa (song)|Do the Koopa]]," [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] takes out his Plumber's Putty and sticks some in his ears, preventing him from being affected by the Doom Dancer Music Box.

In "[[Oh, Brother!]]," Plumber's Putty was briefly mentioned by [[Kooky von Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|Kooky von Koopa]], who states that Mario, who was brainwashed by the [[Lame Brainer]], was "Plumber's Putty in his hands."
