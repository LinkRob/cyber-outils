|games = ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''}}'''Creepy Castle''' is a location in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. It serves as the seventh world and can be accessed from a large cannon located beside [[Crocodile Isle (Donkey Kong 64)|Crocodile Isle]]. It is a large, haunted castle located in the skies and falling off will lead to instant-death, a first in the game (as the other levels don't have bottomless pits from the start). All of the [[Kong Family|Kongs]] play an equal role on this level. The boss of this level is [[King Kut Out]] that can be fought with all of the Kongs (but [[Lanky Kong]] is the only one that can be used to fight it).
