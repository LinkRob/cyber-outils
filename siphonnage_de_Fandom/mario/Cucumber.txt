The '''Cucumber''' is a kart appearing in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]. It first appeared in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

== Description ==
In ''Mario Kart DS'', the Cucumber takes the shape of its namesake, painted green with metal rails on the sides, and a brown seat for the driver. It has four large wheels with a yellow rim and red outline. There is also a circle with metal bars on the front of the kart, and the character's emblem is located on the hood.

In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', the Cucumber gains no major changes, besides extra detailing, although the seat is a dark tan color, and the kart uses the [[Off-Road]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== In ''Mario Kart DS'' ==
[[File:Cucumber with Yoshi - Mario Kart DS.png|thumb|200px|right|Yoshi in his Cucumber kart on the kart selection menu.]]
The Cucumber first appears in this game as Yoshi's unlockable kart. It is unlocked by completing the Nitro Grand Prix cups in 100cc.

The Cucumber has great [[Acceleration]], [[Handling]] and [[Items (stat)]], whilst [[Speed]] and [[Drift]] is slightly hindered. It has an average [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|Weight]] boost. In terms of hidden stats, the Cucumber features good [[Off-Road]] speed and [[Mini-Turbo]], only being weaker to the Egg 1 and Standard YS.

Once the Nitro Grand Prix cups in 100cc are completed, only Yoshi can use this kart. Once the 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix cups are completed, [[Luigi]] and [[Dry Bones]] can use this kart. Once all cups in Mirror Mode have been completed, all characters can use this kart.

It is one of the few karts in the game that doesn’t share any of its stats with any other kart, apart from Items, which is shares with all other characters' heavier karts.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Cucumber returns in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' after 16 years of absence, debuting in the [[Berlin Tour]].

== Trivia ==
* The Cucumber shares a few similarities to future karts in the series, in terms of kart design.
**The [[Classic Dragster]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' in both appearance (specifically Luigi's color scheme) and stats.
**The [[Zucchini]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', though it is a lot faster, heavier, and has less acceleration.
** The [[Tri-Speeder]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. While it has an identical color scheme and body style, the kart is a three-wheeler.
