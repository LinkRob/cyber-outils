|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''|backcolor = 9900cc}}'''Veil''' is a move used by [[Vivian]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is similar to [[Lady Bow]]'s [[Outta Sight]] technique and also serves as Vivian's field move.

==Usage==

In battle, Veil only costs 1 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use. The player must press all of the buttons ({{System Buttons|gcn|A}}/{{System Buttons|gcn|B}}) shown on the screen in order to activate it. It allows Vivian to hide Mario underground avoiding an enemy's attack. This technique, however, costs Vivian her next turn leaving only Mario to attack for that turn. This move is useful for avoiding the [[Shadow Queen]]'s Dark Wave attack and [[Gloomtail]]'s and [[Bonetail]]'s Megabreath attack.

Outside of battle, Vivian can hide Mario underground allowing him to spy on certain characters and avoid traps such as the spike trap in [[Rogueport Sewers]]. Veil is also needed to find [[Zip Toad]], really [[Doopliss]] at the time, in Mario's room and [[Ghost T.]] in his room aboard the [[Excess Express]].
