}}'''Fruit Picker''' is a  minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this unique minigame, the player must memorize the fruit that appears on the wheel.  The player must pick the fruit in the correct order in order to win. If they miss even one, they will lose the minigame on the spot. The player also cannot view the images if they press the Start button or hit the Home button as this is to prevent cheating.
