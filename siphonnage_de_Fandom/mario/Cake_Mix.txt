'''Cake Mix''' is an item in the ''[[Paper Mario series]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
{{Main|Cake Mix (Paper Mario)}}
[[File:Cake Mix.png|thumb|left]][[Mario]] obtains this item at one single location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Inside of the Pink Station's area in [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]], Mario can find a large room with a few [[Shy Guy]]s holding items. Defeating them will allow Mario to obtain the Cake Mix item. This item only restores one FP when consumed, deeming it useless on its own. However, as one should expect from cake mix, it is used in many, many recipes in the game from regular cakes to [[Sweet Shroom]]s.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
{{Main|Cake Mix (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)}}
[[File:Cake_Mix_PMTTYD.png|thumb|left]]This item appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' as well. Since it reappears, it has almost the same purpose. There are different, but less, recipes with the Cake Mix items. There are some really powerful items too like [[Jelly Candy]] and [[Shroom Crepe]]. Although, this item is easier to obtain in the last game, there are less recipes with this item. The [[Pianta Parlor]] in [[Rogueport]] sells this item for 6 Piantas. So, this item is priced at 18 [[coin]]s, but has horrible sell value.

===''Super Paper Mario''===
{{Main|Cake Mix (Super Paper Mario)}}
[[File:Cake_Mix_SPM.png|thumb|left]]In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', this item restores little HP, now that FP was removed from the game. This item is used similarly to how it was used in the last game. Mario can take to the cook and make many fine products. Mario can find this item at the [[Flipside Arcade]] and the [[Itty Bits]] shop in [[Flipside]]. This item is required for creating unique items like [[Shroom Cake]] or [[Koopa Dumpling]]s.
