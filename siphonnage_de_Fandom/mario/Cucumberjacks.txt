'''Cucumberjacks''' is a both a 2-vs.-2 and a duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Intro==
One player is seen cutting a slice off a cucumber.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Cucumberjacks MPDS duel.png|thumb|left|150px|Duel mini-game]]
Each team member goes on opposite ends of a [[Cucumber (vegetable)|cucumber]] and uses a [[slicer]]. To win, the cucumber must be cut into slices as quickly as possible. The two players on a team slice from either end by sliding their [[stylus]]es horizontally back and forth along the cucumber. Bigger strokes will cut bigger pieces of the cucumber off. The team that fully slices their cucumber first is the winner. Cucumberjacks has no [[time limit]], so if five minutes pass without either side winning, it is a tie.

===Etymology===
Its name is a portmanteau of "cucumber" and "lumberjacks".
