'''Beanbean Castle Town''' is the central location in the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] in [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga|''Mario &amp; Luigi: Superstar Saga'']]. It immediately surrounds [[Beanbean Castle]], and in the north part of the town, [[Queen Bean]] lives with her son, [[Prince Peasley]], her ambassador, [[Lady Lima]], and a plethora of advisers.


==History==
It contains [[Starbeans Café|a café]] owned by [[Professor E. Gadd]], and four other miscellaneous shops (one for items, one for clothing/badges, one for information, and one for photography where the player can have their photo taken).

When the Mario Bros. arrive in the town, they discover that it has been destroyed by [[Cackletta]]. She stole the [[Beanstar]] and poisoned Queen Bean with the [[Belly Blech]]. After Mario and Luigi obtain the [[Chuckola Cola|Chuckola Reserve]] and return from [[Chucklehuck Woods]], the town has been repaired. However, after the Mario Bros. defeat [[Popple]] and [[Birdo]] in [[Teehee Valley]], the town was destroyed again by [[Bowletta]] after Luigi stole the Beanstar from the [[Koopa Cruiser]]. As the game resets to the last point that Mario and Luigi saved at when it is completed in the remake, the town will remain in a ruined state for the remainder of the game.
