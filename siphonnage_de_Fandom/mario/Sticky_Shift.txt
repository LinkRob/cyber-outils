{{Infobox Microgame|name = Sticky Shift|image = [[File:WWSM Sticky Shift.png|thumb|250px]]|games = ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''|genre = [[Ashley and Red|Ashley]]|info = ''"Driving a stick shift can be kind of sticky, so put a little oomph into it."''|forms = "The Thumb Wrestler"}}'''Sticky Shift''' is one of [[Ashley]]'s set of microgames in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
To player must change gear before time runs out by following the lighted path on the gearbox. If time runs out, the player loses in the microgame.
*'''Level 1: '''Two shifts to complete the level.
*'''Level 2: '''Three shifts to complete the level.
*'''Level 3: '''Four shifts to complete the level.
