==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the single player will be in a hovercraft and must try to run over the three players within the 30 second time limit. The three players must of course survive for 30 seconds in order to win. The single player's hovercraft is always moving and they must turn in order to keep moving. The three players must also avoid holes in the ground as falling will result in being eliminated.
