'''Hammer Mario''' is a transformation of [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. A character can turn into their Hammer form by using a [[Hammer Suit]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
While a character is in their Hammer form, they wear a black helmet and shell.

===Powers and abilities===
Hammer Mario can throw an endless amount of [[hammer]]s that can strike from a distance. He can also hide in his shell that can provide moderate defense. The hammers can defeat many enemies that can't be defeated by [[fireball]]s, like [[Thwomp]]s, [[Boo]]s, and other enemies. The hammers can also defeat [[Bowser]].
