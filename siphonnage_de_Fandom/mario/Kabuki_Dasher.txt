The '''Kabuki Dasher''' is a kart that appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Kabuki Dasher is a cream-colored variation of the [[B Dasher]], with [[Wikipedia:Kabuki|kabuki]] paint on the body. The engine block and exhaust pipes are black, green and red.

== In ''Mario Kart Tour'' ==
The Kabuki Dasher debuted in the [[Tokyo Tour]], as a High-End kart. It increases the duration of [[Dash Panel]] speed boosts and the points earned for hitting one.
