'''Pick Me Up''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. This item has the power to resurrect a dead character and restore their HP to its maximum. The item appears to be a red glass with a [[star]] picture on it. This item's effects are very similar to [[Life Shroom]]'s effects. [[Princess Peach]]'s move, [[Come Back]] has the same effect as the Pick Me Up.
