'''Toilet Training''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', in which it is [[Mona]]'s boss microgame, and later returned in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', now as [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]'s boss microgame.

== Layout ==
In its original incarnation, Toilet Training took place outdoors. There is a straight path intersected by a curving path which both start at a pool, cross near the middle, and meet just before a T intersection with the shacks containing bathrooms to the sides. The protagonist, dressed in a suit with blue on the left and pink on the right with the dividing line going down the middle, stands in the centre holding a white block (resembling a Wii remote) in his hand.

When she took over the microgame, [[Penny Crygor]] redesigned it and put the protagonist, who now appears as a disembodied hand holding a flag, in the theatre close to the back. The bathrooms are no longer seen due to their off-screen positions, and the pink bathroom is now yellow.

== Gameplay ==
To win, the player has to guide the persons that come out of the pool (leave the seats in ''WarioWare Gold'') by tilting their Wii remote in the direction of the bathroom of the same colour. (Blue and pink on ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'' and blue and yellow in ''WarioWare Gold''.) If one goes into the wrong bathroom, they will get booted into the correct bathroom, causing a loss.

==== Difficulty Levels and Complications in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' ====
The number of persons who have to be led to the bathroom is given by the following function:

<math>p = 6 + 6d</math>

Here, p represents the number of persons who have to be led to the bathroom, while d is the current difficulty level (1 through 3). If a blackout occurs, the persons will have their clothing colour obscured, but their eyes are visible.
