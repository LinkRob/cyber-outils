'''Watches''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Watches are birds with a long, pointed beak. In their artwork, Watches are shown to have blue feathers, a yellow beak, and red talons. They are about the height of [[Wario]].

===Behavior===
Watches are first seen hovering in the air. Whenever Wario is in range, a Watch swoops down to try and attack him with its sharp talons.

===Habitats===
Watches are commonly found in areas where they act as a proper interference, such as above [[pit]]s or near platforms that fall if Wario stands on them for too long.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
[[File:Watch WLSML3 sprite.png|frame|left]]
A Watch is first encountered in [[Course No.07]]. Wario can counter its attack by either jumping on top of the Watch or by dodging the attack.
