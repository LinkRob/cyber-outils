'''Advance Wars''' is one of [[18-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. It is based on the [[Family Computer]] game ''[[w:c:nintendo:Famicom Wars|Famicom Wars]]''. Despite this, the microgame shares a name with ''[[w:c:nintendo:Advance Wars|Advance Wars]]'' for the [[Game Boy Advance]], although the microgame's Japanese name matches the actual game of origin.

==Gameplay==
Two armies are battling with tanks, and some of the infantry are destroyed. There is a question mark button on each team's side, and the player must press the question mark on the side that has more soldiers.
