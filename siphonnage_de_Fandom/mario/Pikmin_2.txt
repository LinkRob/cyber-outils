'''Pikmin 2''' is the name of a [[microgame]] from two different ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' games:

*[[Pikmin 2 (WarioWare: Smooth Moves)|Pikmin 2]], a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', later returning in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''
*[[Pikmin 2 (WarioWare: Get It Together!)|Pikmin 2]], a microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''

==Etymology==
Both microgames are named after and have the same name as ''[[w:c:pikmin:Pikmin 2|Pikmin 2]]'', the second ''[[Pikmin (series)|Pikmin]]'' installment, released in 2004 for the [[Nintendo GameCube]].
