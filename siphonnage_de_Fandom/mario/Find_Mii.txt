'''Find Mii''', titled '''StreetPass Quest''' in PAL releases, is a recurring stage in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Description==
The stage is based on the game of the [[w:c;nintendo:Find Mii|same name]] from the built-in [[Nintendo 3DS]] application [[StreetPass Mii Plaza]], specifically the top of the ruins of its final stage, [[w:c:nintendo:Castle of Darkness|Castle of Darkness]].
