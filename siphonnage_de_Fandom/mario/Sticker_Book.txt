The '''Sticker Book''' is a five-page scrapbook that holds and displays the collected [[DK Sticker Pack]]s in the [[Game Boy Color]] remake of ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''. It serves as the predecessor to the [[Scrapbook]] in the [[Game Boy Advance]] remakes of ''Donkey Kong Country'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest|Donkey Kong Country 2]]''. The player can view the Sticker Book from the selection menu option "STICKER BOOK", categorized under "PRINT". The player can even print the DK Sticker Packs of the Sticker Book from the Game Boy Printer.

Each page of the Sticker Book has its own border: the first page has a green border with [[Bitesize]]s, the second page has a purple border with [[Rambi]]s, the third page has a blue border with [[Zinger]]s, the fourth page has a pink border with [[Croctopus]]es, and the last page has a yellow border with [[Army]]s.
