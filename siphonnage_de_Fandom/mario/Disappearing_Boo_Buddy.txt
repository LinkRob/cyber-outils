'''Disappearing Boo Buddies''' are a variant of [[Boo]] featured in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

== In ''Super Mario World'' ==
Disappearing Boo Buddies are always encountered in groups, and only appear in one [[level]], [[Sunken Ghost Ship]]. They vanish in and out simultaneously, making it fairly difficult for [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to avoid them. A tip for avoiding them is to swim slowly.
