A '''Spikester''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are a variant of [[Spikey (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Spikey]] and a common enemy encountered on [[Booster Pass]].

==Battle==
In battle, Spikesters normally attack by charging at a party member. However, they can also use [[Thornet]] which may [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] a party member or [[Funguspike]] to turn someone into a [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]].

===Strategy===
Spikesters are weak to [[Ice (element)|ice]] moves, so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] is effective against them. However, during the point when Spikesters are encountered, Mallow will most likely not have learned Snowy, so using other special moves, such as [[Mario]]'s [[Super Flame]], will work as well.
