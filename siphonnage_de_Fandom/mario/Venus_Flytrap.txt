'''Venus Flytraps''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. They are first seen in the mansion [[Haunted Towers]].
==Appearance==
Venus Flytraps resembles actual venus flytraps except they have yellow eyes.
==Battle==
Venus Flytraps mainly attack Luigi by biting at him then chewing on him. Luigi must shake himself free to avoid damage.
===Tips===
Since they are not actual ghosts, Venus Flytraps cannot be sucked up into the [[Poltergust 5000]]. Instead, Luigi must locate a Spike Ball and vacuum it up. Then Luigi must blow it into the Venus Flytrap which it will eat it and then die. Fortunately, Venus Flytraps have limited bite range due to them being trapped in pots and the Spike Balls are always locked onto them.
