|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Star Rain''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used by [[Mallow]] once he reaches level 18 and requires 14 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Effects==
When Star Rain is used, a large Star appears on the screen and damages all enemies on the screen for high damage. This move is very similar to the move [[Dark Star (move)|Dark Star]] as it acts exactly the same.
