'''Hanenbow''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'', then later reappeared as DLC in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''. It also appeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It is from the ''The Legend of Zelda'' universe.
