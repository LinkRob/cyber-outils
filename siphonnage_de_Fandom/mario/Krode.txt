The '''Krode''' is an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They are common on Merrygo Island.

== Description ==
Krodes have silver shells with a hole in the front where their black, green-eyed face is visible. The enemy is supported by eight legs.

== Behaviour ==
Whenever it attacks, a Krode will summon a metal pipe to throw at its target after relocating to another spot on the field. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must use their [[Hammer|Hammers]] to counter the attack. The target can be telegraphed by the Krode's location after it shifts its position; Krodes that move to the north half of the battlefield will attack Mario, while Krodes that move to the south half are gunning for Luigi.

A Krode can also summon a metal ball to throw at a Bro., with the target being telegraphed by the direction of the spin. A clockwise spin indicates that Mario will be the target. If it spins counter-clockwise, the Krode will send its ball toward Luigi.

The Krode is a Level 14 monster with 173 hit points, an attack power of 52, a defense of 45, and a speed of 37. When slain, it can drop a [[Mushroom (item)|Super Mushroom]], a Super Candy, or a Merrygo Bolt.

== Variants ==
Stronger variant of the Krode can be encountered later in the game.

=== Krode R ===
Krode R's are encountered in the side quest Monster Mania as an optional boss. These strengthened Krodes have dark blue shells and yellow eyes, and their defense is much higher, though they are vulnerable to [[Battle Plug]] effects. A Krode R is of Level 22 with 141 hit points, an attack power of 97, a defense of 3000, and a speed of 60. When slain, it gives 219 experience and 110 coins.

=== Glohm-infused Krodes ===
During the sidequest Technikki's Past, certain Krodes can be found contaminated with the [[Glohm]] virus. These Krodes attack more quickly than their non-corrupted counterparts and can increase their stats. When the player gets to [[Technikki]]'s workshop on Merrygo Island, a single Glohm-infused Krode can be found accompanying a Glohm-infused [[Snaptor]], and both monsters serve as an optional boss. Upon their defeat, Technikki's certificate can be brought back to Shipshape Island, whereupon she explains her past.

Later, when [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|hijacks]] [[Concordia]], all Krodes become infected with Glohm, and the infected Krodes become even stronger, with a Level of 40, a health maximum of 1148, an experience drop of 1271, and a coin drop of 152.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Creation and Design ===
Although it resembles a [[Treevil]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', the Krode actually takes its design from corroded electrical conduits.

=== Etymology ===
Krodes are named after the verb "corrode," reflecting their corroded electrical conduit design.

== Nomenclature ==
In the Japanese version of the game, Krodes are called ''Magarakuta'' (written マガラクタ), their name being a portmanteau of ''Magatsuhi-kami'' ("god of misfortune"; written 禍津日神) and ''garakura'' ("trash," "garbage," "junk"; written 瓦落多). Chinese calls them ''Mózá'' (written 魔杂), with the source being unknown. In Spanish, their name is ''Despantojo'', from ''espanto'' ("dread") or ''espantar'' ("to spook") and ''matojo'' ("bush"). The Portuguese dub calls them ''Calamilixo'', presumably from ''Calamidade'' ("calamity") and ''lixo'' ("trash," "junk," "garbage"). Italian translates their name as ''Spazzator'', from ''spazzatura'' ("junk," "trash," "garbage") with the Monster suffix -''or''. French translates their name as ''Âmonceau'', from a portmanteau of ''âme'' ("soul") and ''monceau'' ("heap") In Korean, they have the name ''Mondongsani'' (written 몬동사니), from ''japdongsani'' ("junk"; written 잡동사니) merged with a transliteration of the first syllable of the English word "monster."
