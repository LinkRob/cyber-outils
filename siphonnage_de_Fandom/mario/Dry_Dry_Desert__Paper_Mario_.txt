'''Dry Dry Desert '''is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is a fairly large desert that is found beyond [[Mt. Rugged]], and [[Mario]] ventures here to search for the next [[Star Spirit]].

== History ==
In this game, the desert contains only few trees, but has an oasis in the southern part of it. If Mario continues on the path that comes from [[Mt. Rugged]], and go east you'll reach the town of [[Dry Dry Outpost]]. [[Mario]] can find [[Kolorado]] and his research team in the first area he enters. Each area has a path that leads to the south, east, north, and west. Each area is shaped like a block that contains 49 areas total. Amongst this dry land is [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]] where a Star Spirit was kept.

==Trivia==
*Although Dry Dry Desert itself isn't seen in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', there is a very similar location that replaces it: [[Riverside Station]]. This can be confirmed since Pokey Mummies (called [[Poison Pokey]]s in this game) make an appearance here and the background has nothing but sand. This may state that Riverside Station may be located near Dry Dry Desert (though, there is another [[K64|train station]] back in ''Paper Mario'' that leads to Dry Dry Desert as well).
