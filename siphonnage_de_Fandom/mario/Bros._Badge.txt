'''Bros. Badge''' is a [[badge]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Bros. Badge can be bought from the clothes store in [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 150 [[coin]]s, or 250 coins in the Japanese version. Wearing it increases [[Power|POW]] by 25 and [[Bros. Point|BP]] by 8.

In the Nintendo 3DS version, the Bros. Badge becomes available at the same location after completing [[Oho Oasis]]. It costs 250 coins like in the original's Japanese version, and the Bros. Badge can be sold for 63 coins. The Bros. Badge has been made slightly more effective, as it increases POW by 32 and BP by 10.
