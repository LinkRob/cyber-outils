'''Ice Storms''' are an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]], in which they appear in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances ==
===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
[[File:Ice_Storm.png|frame|left]]
Ice Storms are standard offensive items that can potentially freeze [[Mario]]'s opponents. Using this item in battle deals three points of damage to all of Mario's foes. It deals extra damage on [[Fire (element)|fire]]-related enemies, such as [[Ember]]s.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Ice Storms work similarly as in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''. The damage Mario deals using this item depends on the success of the action command. Like in ''The Thousand-Year Door'', Mario can freeze enemies using the item.
