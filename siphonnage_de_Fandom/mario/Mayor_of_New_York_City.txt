The '''Mayor of New York City''' is a one-time character in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', having specifically appeared within the episode "[[Recycled Koopa]]." His job title is being the mayor of [[New York City]].

==Episode appearances==
In "Recycled Koopa," [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] ask for the mayor's help to clean the garbage dumped on [[Brooklyn]], only to find the mayor was among the people who had been transformed into a [[Koopa Zombie]].
