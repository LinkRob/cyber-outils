'''Super Flags''' are located in [[Port Prisma]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. These are the equivelant of achievements in this game. There are only six flags to obtain. When the player completes an achievement, its corresponding flag will unroll and be displayed in the credits parade. If the player manages to complete all six achievements, an alternate ending is unlocked after the end credits, with [[Huey]] falling from the sky into the [[Prisma Fountain]].
