'''Mini Urchins''' (also called '''Small Urchins''') are a smaller subspecies of [[Urchin]] first appearing in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. They behave similarly to regular Urchins but are smaller, they are often found in underwater oceans however they aren’t particularly aggressive.

==History==
===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[Image:NSMB2-W3-A.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] swimming amidst several Urchins and Mini Urchins.]]
Mini Urchins appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', in this game, they can be found in underwater levels most commonly in [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]] as well as some alternate worlds like [[World Mushroom (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Mushroom]]. They are smaller variations of [[Urchin]]s and just like their bigger cousins aren’t very aggressive but can still damage [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] upon contact, Mini Urchins typically appear in clusters which can make getting around them slightly challenging, despite this, a Mini Urchin is far from invincible and can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Starman]], [[Mega Mushroom]], or a [[Gold Flower]], the blast radius from a [[Gold Fireball]] can clear out multiple Mini Urchin clusters at once which makes it very useful when dealing with large numbers of them.

==Trivia==
*While Mini Urchins appeared in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', their larger counterparts, the [[Mega Urchin]]s did not, likewise the Mega Urchin appeared in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' all of which the Mini Urchins did not appear in.
*It could be possible that Mini Urchins are baby versions of regular Urchins, this is further proven by the fact that unlike regular Urchins, Mini Urchins don’t have mouths.
