'''Melee EXP Beans''' are an obtainable item in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Locations==
Melee EXP Beans can be gained by completing levels, or by dismissing troops. Better Melee EXP Beans are gained in harder levels, or by dismissing troops with a higher level.

*Melee EXP Bean S can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 11 or higher, or by dismissing Melee troops that are level 1-13.
*Melee EXP Bean M can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 16 or higher, or by dismissing Melee troops that are level 14-24.
*Melee EXP Bean L can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 25 or higher, or by dismissing Melee troops that are level 25-29.
*Melee EXP Bean DX can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 30 or higher, or by dismissing Melee troops that are level 30 or higher.

==Types==
===Melee EXP Bean S===
The Melee EXP Bean S gives one recruited Melee troop 10 EXP, or it can be used on a Flying or Ranged troop to give them 5 EXP.
===Melee EXP Bean M===
The Melee EXP Bean M gives one recruited Melee troop 100 EXP, or it can be used on a Flying or Ranged troop to give them 50 EXP.
===Melee EXP Bean L===
The Melee EXP Bean L gives one recruited Melee troop 1000 EXP, or it can be used on a Flying or Ranged troop to give them 500 EXP.
===Melee EXP Bean DX===
The Melee EXP Bean DX gives one recruited Melee troop 3000 EXP, or it can be used on a Flying or Ranged troop to give them 1500 EXP.

*The Melee EXP Bean M in-game description is: "A Bean that gives 100 EXP to Melee troopers. Effect is halved if eaten by other unit types."
