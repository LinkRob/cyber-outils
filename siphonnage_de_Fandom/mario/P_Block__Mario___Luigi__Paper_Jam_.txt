'''P Blocks''' are an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Description==
A P Block is a type of [[block]] similar to the [[M Block]]s, [[L Block]]s, and [[? Block]]s, except they can only be activated by [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]]. Certain items, such as [[Mushroom]]s, can emerge from a P Block.
