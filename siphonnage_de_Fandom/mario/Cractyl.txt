'''Cractyl''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Cractyl has the appearance of a purple pterodactyl, but with eyes that are always swirling.

===Behavior===
A Cractyl attacks by swooping down, carrying [[Wario]] into the air, and dropping him from a height. A Cractyl can also attack by dropping a bomb at Wario.

==In ''Wario World''==
Cractyls are encountered throughout [[Excitement Central]].
