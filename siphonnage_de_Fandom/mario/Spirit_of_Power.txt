The '''Spirit of Power''' (also known as '''The Spirit Who Loves Might'''<ref>''Yoshi Topsy Turvy'' instruction booklet, page 24.</ref>) is the third of the [[tiny spirits]] seen within the [[Forbidden Pop-Up Book]] in the game ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]''. This spirit likes mighty people, and only likes to associate himself with those who have proven their worth and strength. Therefore, to acquire the [[Happiness Medal]], [[Yoshi]] must defeat a certain number of enemies in a level, exactly the opposite of the task set up by the [[Spirit of Kindness]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Spirit of Power resembles a genie. He has purple skin, and a huge belly. Underneath his belly he appears to be wearing red underwear with a white lining, which has a hole cut for his wispy tail. His face is happy, and he wears a headdress with three feathers colored green, yellow and red. He has a goatee on his chin and has only three fingers on each hand.
