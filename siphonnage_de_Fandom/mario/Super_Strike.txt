'''Super Strikes''' are a type of special move used by team captains in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''. A Super Strike is worth two points if scored instead of just one.

The player can have their character perform a Super Strike by pressing the B button. In doing so, the character jumps into the air, and a small meter is shown; the power behind a Super Strike depends on where the two white lines land on the meter, which has a dark green zone in the center and wider light green zones on the left and right side each. The best possible timing is if both white lines land within the dark green zone, causing the character to execute an unstoppable Super Strike that can push the [[Kritter|goalie]] backward into the soccer goal it is guarding. 

Super Strikes do not return in any of the ''[[Mario Strikers (series)|Mario Strikers]]'' games, as ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]'' replaces them with [[Mega Strike]]s, which in turn is replaced in ''[[Mario Strikers: Battle League]]'' with [[Hyper Strike]]s.
