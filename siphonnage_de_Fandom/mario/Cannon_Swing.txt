'''Cannon Swing''' is [[Birdo]]'s [[Star Swing]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It replaces her [[Weird Ball]] move from ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', which itself is replaced with the [[Suction Ball]].

==Description==
Cannon Swing works similarly to [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Egg Swing]]. Upon initiating Cannon Swing, Birdo spits out an [[Birdo Egg|egg]] with bow silhouettes on it and then hits it. The egg flies in front of the ball, stunning any character whom it hits. After a while, the ball falls behind, leaving it open for a diving catch. Most characters with a [[Super Dive]] ability can catch the ball from behind the egg.
