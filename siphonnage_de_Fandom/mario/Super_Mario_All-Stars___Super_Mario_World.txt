'''''Super Mario All-Stars''''' '''+ ''Super Mario World''''' is a compilation game for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. It is the same as ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'', but adds ''[[Super Mario World]]'' alongside the [[reissue|remakes]] of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'', ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. The inclusion of ''Super Mario World'' resulted in an updated title screen, in which [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can be seen amongst the other characters. A difference in the ''Super Mario World'' port itself is that [[Luigi]]'s sprites were updated so that he no longer appears as a palette swap of Mario.

* ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''
