'''World 2''' is the second [[world]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It is a [[Desert theme|desert-themed world]]. This world has four Toad Houses, two are red, one is green and the last is yellow. It is also the first world with a Ghost House. This world consists of eleven levels. Eight of them are on the main path, two levels are optional and the last is the world's Rainbow Course. This world has a total of twelve exits. This world is also optional as it can be bypassed by completing World Mushroom.

The boss is [[Iggy Koopa]]. To unlock this world, the player must complete [[World 1-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1-Castle]]. Its completion unlocks [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]].
