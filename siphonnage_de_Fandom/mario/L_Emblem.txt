'''L Emblem''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
At a [[Badge Point|BP]] cost of zero, the L Emblem allows [[Mario]] to dress up as [[Luigi]], donning a green hat and shirt with the same blue overalls and brown shoes. Wearing both L Emblem and [[W Emblem]], a badge that dresses Mario up in clothing like [[Wario]]'s, will result in Mario wearing clothing like [[Waluigi]]'s, wearing a purple hat and shirt with black overalls and orange shoes. ﻿

The L Emblem is found in [[Poshley Sanctum]]. Here, this badge is at the top of the second portion. Mario must climb up all of the way using his [[Spin Jump]] technique. L Emblem is used to complete the [[Trouble Center|trouble]] "I wanna meet Luigi."
