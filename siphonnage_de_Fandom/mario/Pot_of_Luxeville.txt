The '''Pot of Luxeville''' is a plot-essential object in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==History==
The pot was originally from [[Luxeville]], where it was the only toilet used by all of the residents. One day, [[Wario]] stole the Pot of Luxeville, thinking it would be valuable. [[Lulu]] hopes to be recognized as a local hero in her area, so she tries to retrieve the Pot of Luxeville back from him. Later on, the Pot of Luxeville transforms Wario into [[Wario Deluxe]].
