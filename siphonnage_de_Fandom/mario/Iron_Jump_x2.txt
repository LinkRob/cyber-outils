|name=Iron Jump x2 Battle Card}}'''Iron Jump x2''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Iron Jump x2 is the iron version of [[Mario]]'s "Jump x2" attack. It is two of the [[Iron Jump (Battle Card)|Iron Jump]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied by a blue/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 20 coins (unpainted)

Sell Price: 10 coins (unpainted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Iron Jump x2 is first seen in [[Plum Park]], but can also found in [[Fort Cobalt]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Mustard Café]], [[Toad Trainworks]], [[Redpepper Volcano]], [[Mossrock Theater]], and [[Black Bowser's Castle]].
