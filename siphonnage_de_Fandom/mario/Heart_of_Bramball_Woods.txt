==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Jump on the ninth Bramball and hit the P Switch to reveal a below and jump on the other Bramballs to get the coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the first Star Coin, get the Star Coin close to it while not getting hit by the Bramball close to it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Run and duck (if not in standard form) to the Star Coin while also avoiding the Bramball in the poison.

==Hidden Luigi image==
On the platform by the first Star Coin shows a picture of modern Luigi's head.
