'''Best Badges''' are badges that are won in [[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]], and help to unlock new courses and characters in [[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]. They are color coded by the score:

Birdie Badge - Blue Eagle Badge - Green Albatross Badge - Red Hole-in-One Badge - Yellow

The best badges can be used to unlock [[Wario]], [[Luigi]], [[Waluigi]], and [[Bowser]] in Mario Golf: Advance Tour. This can be done using the GameCube Gameboy Advance Link Cable.
