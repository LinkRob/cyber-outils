{{nihongo|'''Brighton'''|ソルル|Soruru}} is a host in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' during the day. In most courses, when Brighton is the host, he turns the course into an area with positive items, such as the [[Bowser Space]] becoming a [[DK Space]]. He also hosts the mini-games at daytime.

== Biography ==
=== Brighton vs. Twila ===
Brighton and [[Twila]] hosted the Mario Party world. One day, Brighton determined who is more impressive than Twila. Mario's party watched Twila and Brighton argue seeming that they could not end their feud. [[Mario]] planned "to throw a Mario Party to fill the Star Bank!". Mario's party set to stockpile [[Star]]s to end their feud.

=== Ending the feud ===
Mario's party successfully filled the [[Star Bank]] expecting Brighton and Twila to end their feud. Pleasing the two, they promised to Mario and his cohorts that they promise to get along. They released the stars in the Star Bank.

== In ''[[Mario Party 6]]'' ==
Brighton and Twila are hosts in ''Party 6''. On boards, Brighton hosts during daytime while Twila hosts during nighttime.
