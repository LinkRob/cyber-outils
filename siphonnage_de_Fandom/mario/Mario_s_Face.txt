'''''Mario's Face''''' is a tech demo for the [[Nintendo DS]] that allowed the user to stretch out parts of [[Mario]]'s face, an iconic feature of the title screen in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', or [[Wario]]'s face. The tech demo only came to fruition in the lower screen of ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''{{'}}s title screen, in which users could stretch and customize either Mario's or [[Yoshi]]'s face, albeit in doodle form.
