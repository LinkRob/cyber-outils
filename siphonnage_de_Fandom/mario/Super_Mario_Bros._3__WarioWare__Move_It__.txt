'''Super Mario Bros. 3''' is one of [[9-Volt]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' It is based on the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game of the [[Super Mario Bros. 3|same name]], and its gameplay uses the Ba-KAW form.

==Layout==
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] is in [[Raccoon Mario|Raccoon]] form. The location depends on the level. In Level 1, he is outside, and an infinitely-long grass platform appears eventually. At Level 2, Mario is inside a Fortress level, with lava below, and the platform is finite in length. [[Podoboo|Podoboos]] appear on the lava on the other side. Level 3 is in a similar location to Level 1, but no platform appears, so Mario must use his power to prevent the [[Pit (obstacle)|abyss]] below him from consuming him.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of [[Raccoon Mario]], who slowly ascends when using his tail and quickly descends when stationary. If Raccoon Mario either falls off or gets hit, the player loses.

* '''Level 1:''' Raccoon Mario only has to fly, and landing on a platform counts as a win.
* '''Level 2:''' Raccoon Mario only has to land on a platform. Flying past the platform results in a loss, as Mario is shown to be attacked by [[Podoboo]]s, resulting in him falling to his demise in the [[lava]].
* '''Level 3:''' Raccoon Mario must avoid being hit by [[Bullet Bill]]s, although he can still [[jump]] on them.
