!Super Mushroom
----
[[File:NSMBU Mushroom Artwork.png|125px]]
|The [[Super Mushroom]] is a small, red [[Mushroom]] with white spots. It is the most common type of Mushroom found throughout the ''Mario'' series. The Super Mushroom has the ability to make whatever it touches grow in size, sometimes increasing said being's strength. Mario and Luigi have commonly used Super Mushrooms throughout their many adventures in order to help them survive. In ''[[Mario]]'' [[platformer]]s, the Super Mushroom generally increases the size of the Mario Bros. by a minimal amount; this change in size is just enough to allow the Bros. to break [[Brick Block]]s. In ''Mario'' [[role-playing game]]s, Super Mushrooms generally heal Mario or his partners by partially refilling their HP.
|<center>''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''</center>

!1-Up Mushroom
----
[[File:NSMBU 1-Up Mushroom.png|125px]]
|The [[1-Up Mushroom]] is an item that is similar in appearance, but entirely different in effect to the Super Mushroom. 1-Up Mushrooms are green in color, with white spots. They are the same size as Super Mushrooms, and share the same facial features. 1-Up Mushrooms grant Mario and others to gain an [[extra life]] when they touch or eat it. 1-Up Mushrooms can be found hidden in blocks, behind items, guarded by enemies, within chests, or any number of other locations. 1-Up Mushrooms are much less common than Super Mushrooms, appearing roughly once per level, whereas Super Mushrooms appear several times in a level. In ''Paper Mario'' games, 1-Up Mushrooms don't exist; rather, there are [[Life Shroom]]s, which are kept in the inventory and automatically revive Mario or his partners when they run out of [[HP]] in battle. 1-Up Mushrooms do appear in the Mario & Luigi series with a similar function, but they don't work automatically. They have to be used on the brother that ran out of HP by the active brother.
|<center>''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''</center>

!Poison Mushroom
----
[[File:PoisonShroom.png]]
|Debuting in ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels'', [[Poison Mushroom]]s are Mushrooms that, as their name suggests, harm whatever they touch. In platformers such as ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels'', ''Super Mario 3D Land'', and ''Luigi's Mansion'', Poison Mushrooms will shrink the player if he or she comes in direct contact with one. In ''Super Mario Kart'', Princess Toadstool and Toad could throw these at opposing racers, shrinking them drastically in size; when an opponent shrank, they could easily be run over and thrown off-track. In the ''Mario Party'' series, Poison Mushrooms appear once again; however, here they affect how many spaces the player can move. In ''Mario'' role-playing games, Poison Shrooms harm Mario or his partners by decreasing their HP greatly.
|<center>''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''</center>

!Mini Mushroom
----
[[File:NSMBU Mini Mushroom artwork.png|125px]]
|Debuting in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', [[Mini Mushroom]]s were pink and shrunk the player and decreased the dice from 1-10 to 5-10, and using this item the player could gain access to certain areas of the game boards, These areas allowed the player to play special [[Minigames]] usually for [[Coin]]s or [[Items]]. In ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]'', Mini Mushrooms were used to squeeze inside small locations to retrieve [[Power Star]]s. In ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', finishing worlds 2 and 5 with [[Mini Mario]] allowed access to worlds 4 and 7. In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', their color scheme changes drastically from the original pink, to the new blue with white spots. Mini Mushrooms held the same effect, allowing Mario or Luigi to fit in tiny [[Warp Pipe]]s to retrieve certain items. Their latest appearance was in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.
|<center>''[[Mario Party 4]]''</center>

!Mega Mushroom
----
[[File:Mega Mushroom Artwork - New Super Mario Bros 2.png|125px]]
|First appearing in ''Mario Party 4'', [[Mega Mushroom]]s were pale green in color with white spots; they were slightly larger than most other Mushrooms, but still had similar facial features. Mega Mushrooms would grant anyone who used them the power to grow enormous in size, as well as use two [[Dice Block]]s. If the player crushed an opponent while walking across the board, he or she would receive ten coins for doing so. Mega Mushrooms later appeared in ''New Super Mario Bros.'' with a new design; they were large, yellow mushrooms with vibrant red spots. Here they granted Mario or Luigi the ability to be huge in size and invincible from all harm, (with the exception of falling into an [[abyss]]). This is the one of the two items from New Super Mario Bros., along with the [[Blue Shell]] that was not reused for ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', its successor. A [[Goomba]] also touched a Mega Mushroom in the game, becoming the boss of the [[World 4 (NSMB)|fourth world]]: [[Mega Goomba]]. In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', Mega Mushrooms acted similar to the way they did in ''Mario Party 4'', only the player would not receive coins for running over opponents. The Mega Mushroom also appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', making its first appearance in a 3D Mario platformer.
|<center>''Mario Party 4''</center>

!Fire Flower
----
[[File:Fire Flower NSMBU artwork.png|125px]]
|Debuting in ''Super Mario Bros.'', [[Fire Flower]]s are a type of plant that grant whoever touches one the ability to throw [[fireball]]s. When Mario or Luigi would touch a Fire Flower, they would turn into their [[Fire Mario|fire form]], with the traditional white shirt and hat, and red or green overalls respectively, being able to sling fireballs at incoming enemies. Fireballs are sometimes the most effective way to eliminate enemies, as very few are immune to them; [[Buzzy Beetle]]s are one of these exceptions. In ''[[Super Mario World]]'' Fire Flowers went through a design change, appearing to be more like orange [[wikipedia:Tulip|tulips]] with happy eyes. In the ''Paper Mario'' series, Fire Flowers can be used both as weapons against enemies, and as an ingredient into several different recipes, (including the [[Spicy Soup]]).
|<center>''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''</center>

!Ice Flower
----
[[File:NSMBU Ice Flower Artwork.png|125px]]
|The [[Ice Flower]] debuted not in a normal platformer, unlike most items, but in a RPG, being ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', as a Bros. Item. It allowed [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] to shoot ice balls to the enemies, opposing to the Fire Flower. In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', however, Mario turns into an ice sculpture of himself, being [[Ice Mario]], with the ability to freeze water and lava when stepped on, instead of throwing ice balls. The Ice Flower also made a more prominent appearance in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', being similar in purpose to ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'', by throwing ice balls again. Because of this, Mario will not turn into ice, but instead get a light blue shirt and hat, and overalls become red, to fit his Fire Mario appearance.
|<center>''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''</center>

!Star
----
[[File:Starman NSMBU.png|125px]]
|The [[Star]] (also called a Starman), is a powerful celestial object found commonly throughout the ''Mario'' series. Stars debuted in ''Super Mario Bros.'' Whenever Mario or Luigi touch a Star, they become almost entirely invincible — the only things that can still [[Game Over|end their game]] is to either fall into an [[abyss]], or to get squished. While using the Star, they can defeat any enemy instantly with one touch. However, the effect of the Star lasts for a relatively short time. The theme music of the effect eventually was used in most ''Mario'' games, though sometimes the theme would get remixed. ''[[Super Mario Land]]'s'' Star theme music was the famous Can-Can music, but ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'' used an entirely different melody. In ''Super Mario Kart'' and other ''Mario Kart games'', this item was one of the rarest ones received, which of course gave the user invincibility, as well as the ability to knock other racers aside. This item was substituted with the [[Rainbow Star]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and returned in ''Mario Kart Wii'' and ''Super Mario 3D Land''.
|<center>''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''</center>

!Banana
----
[[File:Banana (Mario Kart Wii).png|125px]]
|The [[Banana]] is a fruit that has commonly appeared throughout the ''Mario'' and ''Donkey Kong'' series. It is the favorite food of [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]], but its most prominent appearance is in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series. Here, it appears as an item that can be used to spin out other players and slow them down. The bananas in the ''Mario Kart'' series have a more anthropomorphic appearance than the bananas in the ''Donkey Kong'' series.
|<center>''[[Donkey Kong Jr. (game)|Donkey Kong Jr.]]''</center>

!Super Leaf
----
[[File:Super Leaf NSMB2.png|125px]]
|The [[Super Leaf|Super Leaves]] are special power-ups whose effect vary per game but is almost exactly the same. On their first appearance in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', when Super Leaves are grabbed, Mario or Luigi grow a pair of raccoon ears and a tail, this last being the crucial part of the transformation as the tail can be swung to defeat enemies, destroy brick blocks, slow the descent, and even fly for a short period of time. In this appearance, Super Leaves looked like common dried brown leaves. After years of absence, Super Leaves return with a new appearance in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' where they gained a wider appearance with a pair of eyes and a bite on their left sides. Instead of transforming into a Raccoon, this time, these leaves would grant Mario the [[Tanooki Suit]], turning him into [[Tanooki Mario]]. Virtually, there was almost no changes, except that Mario can no longer fly. For ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', Super Leaves would again turn Mario into [[Raccoon Mario]], including the ability to fly again, while retaining the appearance introduced in ''Super Mario 3D Land''. Before ''Super Mario 3D Land'', in the ''Mario Kart'' series, the Super Leaves were used to be the emblem of the [[Leaf Cup]], but an actual Super Leaf would not become an item until ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' where it makes the kart to grow a Tanooki Tail that can be used either defensively and offensively. This item was removed in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', but it remains as the emblem of the Leaf Cup.
|<center>''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''</center>

!Coin
----
[[File:Coin - New Super Mario Bros 2.png|x105px]]
|First appearing in the arcade game, ''Mario Bros.'', [[Coin]]s are a form of currency in the ''Mario'' series, primarily in the Mushroom Kingdom. In the ''Super Mario Bros.'' series of platformers, when collected, they will award the player with a total of one-hundred points per coin; also, after collecting one-hundred coins, the player would earn an extra life. In ''Super Mario 64'', there were three different types of Coins: [[Yellow Coin]]s, [[Blue Coin]]s, and [[Red Coin]]s. Yellow Coins played a much more vital part in completion of the game. By collecting coins, the player's HP meter will refill by one notch; also, if the player collects one-hundred coins on a stage, he or she will be awarded with an secret [[Power Star]], which is vital to entirely completing the game. In the ''Mario'' RPGs, Coins act as a form of currency to purchase items, weapons, badges, and other things that are useful along Mario's journey.
|<center>''[[Mario Bros. (game)|Mario Bros.]]''</center>

!Star Coin
----
[[File:NSMB2 Star Coin.png|x105px]]
|[[Star Coin]]s are big Coins that first appeared in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' Three of them appear in every levels. In the game, they are required to go to [[Toad House]]s, buy new backgrounds, and complete the game by collecting all of them, which is the same in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. However, in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', they are used to buy hint movies at Princess Peach's Castle. Star Coins are also useful in unlocking levels in the secret star worlds of ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', ''New Super Mario Bros. 2'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. In ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', they are redesigned as Star Medals, like the [[Comet Medal]]s from ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''. They are used to unlock other courses, serving the purpose of [[Power Star]]s from past 3D platformers.
|<center>''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''</center>

!Red Coin
----
[[File:Red Coins parachute NSMB2.png|125px]]
|[[Red Coin]]s are a type of coin appearing mostly in the 3D ''Mario'' platformers, though they have lately appeared in some ''Mario'' sidescrollers. Red Coins appear in groups of eight, and collecting them all will result in the player receiving a reward. They first appeared in ''Super Mario 64'', where collecting all eight of them would result in a Star to appear over a [[Star Marker]]. They were worth two regular coins. In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', they would only appear in certain missions, which could only be completed by collecting them and Yoshi Eggs. In ''Super Mario Sunshine'', they served the exact same purpose, though they did not affect the player's amount of coins, giving the indication that they were an entirely different currency. In ''New Super Mario Bros.'', ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', and ''Super Mario 3D Land'', Red Coins would only appear if the player ran through a [[Red Ring]]. By collecting all of them, the player would receive a power-up (a 1-Up Mushroom if they already had a power-up beyond a Mushroom and every time in ''Super Mario 3D Land''). In ''Super Mario 3D Land'', only five Red Coins appear in a group. They are replaced with [[Green Coin]]s in ''Super Mario 3D World''.
|<center>''[[Super Mario 64]]''</center>

!Yoshi Egg
----
[[File:Yoshi Egg NSMBU.png|x125px]]
|[[Yoshi Egg]]s are items used in the ''Mario'' and ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' franchise. Each egg hatches a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. They are also items in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series. There are different colored eggs that hatch different colored Yoshis. For example, a light blue egg, will hatch a [[Light Blue Yoshi]]. Ever since their first appearance in ''Super Mario World'', Yoshi eggs have made many more appearances. Their latest appearance is in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' as the emblem of the [[Egg Cup]].
