'''Firewheels''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Firewheel is a small, circular stone face surrounded by four [[fireball]]s.

===Behavior===
A Firewheel attacks by spinning while bouncing around rooms diagonally.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Firewheels are only encountered in two [[level]]s of [[Fury Volcano]], [[Fury Volcano 4-3|4-3]] and [[Fury Volcano 4-5|4-5]]. Firewheels are invulnerable to any attack, so [[Princess Peach]] cannot defeat them. They are one of the enemies to not have a [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] entry.
