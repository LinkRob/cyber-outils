{{Nihongo|'''Bowlbrella'''<ref>{{cite video game| title = [[Super Princess Peach]]| developer = Nintendo SPD, TOSE| publisher = Nintendo| date = February 26, 2006| platform = Nintendo DS}}</ref>|どんぶらカッサー|Donbura Kassā|Bowl Perry}} is one of Perry's forms in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Description ==
When Peach touches the water, Perry transforms into a "bowl". Peach can float on Perry and move across the boat.
