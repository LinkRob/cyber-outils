{{Nihongo|'''Aqua Man'''|アクアマン|Akuaman|Aquaman}} is an enemy which appears in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for the [[Game Boy]].

== Profile ==
===Physical description===
Aqua Men are humanoid creatures who wear diving suits.

===Behavior===
An Aqua Man has a tendency to walk back and forth. If [[Mario]] touches an Aqua Man, he loses a life.
