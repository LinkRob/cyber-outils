The '''Evil Forces''' are the overarching antagonists of the [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros. film series]].

They are a group of miscellaneous aliens who wanted Rosalina's and Peach's powers for themselves. They are the reason why Peach became the princess of the Mushroom Kingdom, why Rosalina became the 'mother of the stars' to the Lumas and is the reason both her and Rosalina are separated.

==History==
While they are mentioned briefly in a flashback shown within one of Rosalina's storybooks, they are seemingly a large group of different or the same alien species that seek cosmic power, mainly to do unspecified evil doings. They set their sights onto two siblings made of stardust that lived on a small planet, both named Rosalina and Peach, who had powers that could create live.

Eventually the Evil Forces found the two, Rosalina, seeing the horizon turn a sharp purple, grabbed and ran with Peach and brought her to a warp pipe nearby as the light grew thicker and closer, Rosalina decided to take Peach away from these evil forces and be brought to safety using the aforementioned warp pipe. Rosalina set Peach on the edge of the pipe is it began to suck Peach into it. Rosalina and Peach gave each other a final look as Rosalina let go of Peach's hand, despite Peach's pleas to not be away from her sister. It is unknown what happened to these Evil Forces after this.
