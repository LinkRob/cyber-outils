'''Mona Pizza''' is a recurring song in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It is sung by [[Sal Out]], as an extended advertisement jingle for the restaurant of the [[Mona Pizza|same name]]. However, the second half of "Mona Pizza" features the restaurant's main competitors, [[The Dinosaurs]], from [[Pizza Dinosaur]], who disparage Mona Pizza and brag about their pizzas selling more because they have more locations.
