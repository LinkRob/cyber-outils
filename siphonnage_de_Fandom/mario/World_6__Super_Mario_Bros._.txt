'''World 6''' is the sixth world in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''.

== Description ==
It is set once again at nighttime. It consists of two ground levels, one level at heights, and one castle level. No new enemies are introduced in this world.

On the map in ''Deluxe'', the land elevation is low and there are no [[pipe]]s. The player will follow a more simple path from west to east to reach the castle.
