'''Wario Palace''' is a baseball stadium owned by [[Wario]] in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''. In the sequel, ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'', Wario instead has [[Wario City]] as his baseball stadium.

==Description==
Wario Palace takes place within a golden palace in the desert. In Challenge Mode, Wario Palace is shown to be near the [[Bowser Castle (baseball stadium)|Bowser Castle]] stadium. The field has two gimmicks: two [[Chain Chomp]]s, tethered at the upper-left and upper-right ends of the stadium respectively, and two [[Cyclone Generator]]s, which originally appeared in ''[[Wario World]]''. The Chain Chomps are usually asleep, but if they are awake, the Chain Chomps attempt to lunge at any fielder next to it. If an fielder is hit by a Chain Chomp, they get stunned for a few seconds. If a baseball goes flying toward a Chain Chomp, it also moves toward it. If a baseball goes flying toward a Cyclone Generator, a whirlwind surrounds the Cyclone Generator for a few seconds, during which the baseball moves around within it before being launched into a random direction.

Aside from Chain Chomps and Cyclone Generators, Wario Palace also has some star-shaped dunes in the sand, and whenever the batter hits a baseball onto one, their team earns a [[Team Star]].

Given its architecture, the palace just outside the boundaries of stadium itself is possibly based on the {{w|Taj Mahal}}. The palace's landmark feature is a giant gold statue of Wario's head, which has two light green gemstones for eyes and a [[Wario Cap|cap]] that more closely resembles a turban than Wario's usual cap. The giant Wario head is placed on a rectangular structure with a large 'W' on the front, surrounded by purple flags and a smaller statue of Wario made entirely from gold. The structure is surrounded by four pillars placed near each corner, and the two pillars in the front have a flag depicting Wario's emblem near the tip. Two giant torches are next to the pillars, each having a flame on the top. The background also has a few small palm trees and some purple flags depicting a golden Wario head.

Wario Stadium is where the minigame [[Chain Chomp Sprint]] takes place. Here, characters run around the home plate to collect [[Jewel]]s while avoiding a Chain Chomp in the center.
