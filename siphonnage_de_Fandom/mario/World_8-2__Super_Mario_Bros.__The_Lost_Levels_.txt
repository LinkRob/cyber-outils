'''World 8-2''' is the second [[level]] of [[World 8 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 8]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the thirtieth level overall in said game. The level is unique in that the end-of-level flagpole is actually hidden in the sky, and requires making a beanstalk appear to complete said level. The fortress walls in the background appear in the original and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]'' versions of the game with items being found in [[Brick Block]]s, but the walls are absent in the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' version, with items being found in [[Hidden Block|invisible block]]s.

In ''[[Nintendo World Championships: NES Edition]]'', completing this stage as [[Luigi]] is a Legend-difficulty challenge titled Lost Legend.

==Layout==
The player begins on a stretch of land ending at a [[Warp Pipe]] with a [[Piranha Plant]]. Above the gap afterward is a platform that falls when the player jumps onto it, and above that is a Brick Block containing a power-up. Afterward is a row of [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Buzzy Beetle]]s, a [[Koopa Paratroopa]], and Piranha Plants ending at a [[Trampoline|Springboard]]. Using the Springboard, the player can get over a tall pillar and jump across a pool of water. To progress further, the player needs to jump off of a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] and hit the Brick Block above it, producing a [[Vine|Beanstalk]] that leads to the [[Goal Pole]]. If the player defeats the Koopa Paratroopa without hitting the block, or misses the block entirely, they will have to continue to the right through some [[Hammer Bro]]thers, blocks with [[Poison Mushroom]]s, and an underground [[bonus]] room to return to a Warp Pipe before the Springboard and try again.
