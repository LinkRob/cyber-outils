'''Boomerang Banana Return''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] of [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The move involves Donkey Kong turning around, turning his racquet into a boomerang-shaped [[banana]], throwing it and knocking the off-course ball back over the court.
