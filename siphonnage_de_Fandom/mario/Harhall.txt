'''Harhall''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. He is a clothes designer and is known for being harsh toward his employees. Harhall owns his own studio, aptly named [[Harhall's Studio]], and can always be found there.

==Appearance==
Harhall is a very slim Beanish who wears glasses and, like most Beanish people, has blonde hair. He also wears a camera around his neck. He wears pink lipstick and pink horn rimmed glasses.

==History==
Needing a [[Beanstar]] piece, Mario and Luigi follow the map to Harhall's Studio where they notice people leaving complaining about drinking too much water. Harhall then notices Mario and Luigi and agrees to hand over the Beanstar piece if they can design three different clothing designs for him. The Mario Bros. do so successfully, and Harhall hands over the Beanstar piece. If Mario and Luigi return later on, they can design more clothes in exchange for [[bean]]s and [[Harhall's Pants]].
