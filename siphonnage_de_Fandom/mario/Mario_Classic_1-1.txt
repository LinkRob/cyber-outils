'''Mario Classic 1-1''' is a location in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Layout==
[[File:Mario Classic 1-1 screenshot.png|thumb|left|x250px|Ninja in Mario Classic 1-1.]]
Mario Classic 1-1 is based on [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]] of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', as both the background and scenery depict various 8-bit-style models and textures based on the environment of said [[level]].

The course is fairly simple, with no structural hazards. Up to eleven total [[? Panel]]s are scattered across the course at a time, all releasing items that can used to get coins from the opponent. There are a total of four [[Warp Pipe]]s that teleport the player directly across the map. The player can only enter into the pipes at the top of the map and at the right, as the other two pipes are where these two pipes lead to. Items are unable to travel through the Warp Pipes, giving the player protection while inside of one.

==In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''==
Mario Classic 1-1 is the first course of the multiplayer-exclusive Coin Hunter mode in ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''. Whoever collects the most [[coin]]s here within the 200-second [[time limit]] wins.
