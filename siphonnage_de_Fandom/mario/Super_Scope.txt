The '''Super Scope''', known as the '''Nintendo Scope''' in European languages, is a {{wp|light gun}} peripheral for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. The only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related entry to utilize it is ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]'', in which it is used by [[Mario]] in-game.

==Description==
It fires an infrared beam that is picked up by a sensor placed on top of the television, and where on the screen it is aiming is determined by the sensor after calibrating the scope by shooting a test target screen.

The [[Devolution Gun]] used to cause [[de-evolution]] in the [[Super Mario Bros. (film)|''Super Mario Bros.'' film]] is a modified Super Scope.

==History==
===''Yoshi's Safari''===
[[File:Yoshi's Safari title screen.png|thumb|''Yoshi's Safari'' screenshot of the title screen, showing Mario hold up the Super Scope and a Turbo option on the bottom.]]
In ''Yoshi's Safari'', Mario uses the Super Scope throughout his journey to shoot at enemies. On the title screen, he can be seen holding the Super Scope upward while mounted on [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
Super Scopes also make a cameo as used by [[Sniper Bill]]s in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|its remake]].

===''Super Mario-kun'' Volume 8''===
In [[Super Mario-kun Volume 8|Volume 8]] of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'', [[Mario (Super Mario-kun)|Mario]] uses a Super Scope (sent by [[Princess Peach (Super Mario-kun)|Princess Peach]]) to defeat the [[Kurokyura]].
{{br}}
===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
{{Smashpedia}}
[[File:Peach with Super Scope SSB4 Wii U.jpg|thumb|left|''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'' screenshot of [[Princess Peach]] holding a Super Scope]]
The Super Scope is featured as an item in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] starting with ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', allowing players to fire energy balls at their opponents, either in dozens of small bullets or several large charged-up shots (three in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and five in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''). When a player repeatedly shoots small blasts in a line, the opponent is unable to move away when hit, but the player also cannot move while firing; starting with ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', though, the player can walk and [[jump]] while firing the item.
{{br|left}}
===''WarioWare'' series===
====''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''====
[[File:9-Volt opening WWIMM screenshot.png|thumb|A Super Scope, as seen in the background of 9-Volt's bedroom]]
In ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', a Super Scope can be seen in the background of [[9-Volt]]'s bedroom.
{{br|left}}
====''WarioWare: Touched!''====
In ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', the Super Scope can appear in the [[microgame]] [[Gunslinger]] as a possible [[Nintendo]] accessory to be released from destroying a barrel drum.
