'''Bills'''<ref>''Luigi's Mansion'' instruction booklet, page 18</ref><ref>Sinfield, George. ''Luigi's Mansion'' Player's Guide. Page 95.</ref><ref>''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' digital instruction manual, page 10</ref> (known as '''Notes''' in British and Australian English)<ref>''Luigi's Mansion 2'' digital instruction manual, page 10</ref> are a kind of money in the [[Luigi's Mansion (series)|''Luigi's Mansion'' series]] that adds to the players "G" (gold) counter. They can be sucked up and collected using the various [[Poltergust]] models seen throughout the games.

==History==
===''Luigi's Mansion''===
[[File:LM Foyer chandelier Bills.png|thumb|left|[[Luigi]] collecting some bills in ''Luigi's Mansion'']]
In ''Luigi's Mansion'', bills are collectible items usually found in groups by examining various furniture or by using the [[Poltergust 3000]] to vacuum at certain chandeliers, such as the one in the [[Foyer (Luigi's Mansion)|Foyer]]. Bills are among the treasures to contribute to [[Luigi]]'s Gold ("G") counter. Luigi can collect Bills by either touching them or vacuuming them into his Poltergust 3000. Because they are light, Bills fall in the air slower than other treasure, and can take slightly longer to vacuum into the Poltergust 3000 than [[Coin]]s.

Each Bill is worth 20,000G, being the second-least valuable form of money, after Coins. Bills are colored either green or orange, but this does not affect their value.

===''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''===
Bills appear next in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'', where they take the same role as they do in ''Luigi's Mansion''. However, instead of being worth 20,000G, they are worth 5G and are colored green, making them more resemble American currency.

===''Luigi's Mansion 3''===
Bills return in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', where they act as they did in ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''. Sucking on money bags with the [[Poltergust G-00]] destroys the bags and releases several bills into the air, and destroying a piggy bank releases a few bills as well as several coins.
