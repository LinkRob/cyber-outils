'''Mr. Hoggle''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is an anthropometric [[Pig]] who runs the [[Hot Dog Stand]] in [[Glitzville]].

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Mr. Hoggle is commonly seen running his hot dog stand, where he charges ten coins per [[Hot Dog]]. At one point, Mr. Hoggle tried to make a hot dog recipe with eggs on them, called the Southern Fried Egg Dog of Tastiness, but his only [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] starts bouncing around. The egg lands onto of his stand and starts "sleeping." [[Mario]] arrives on the scene and, upon further inspection, reveals that it isn't really an egg to eat and decides to take it with him. Mr. Hoggle understands and allows Mario to take the egg away, saying it was too dirty from bouncing around anyway and that people do not like eating food that bounces all over the place.
