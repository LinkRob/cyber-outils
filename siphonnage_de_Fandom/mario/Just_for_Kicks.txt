'''Just for Kicks''' is a [[Team Minigame|Team]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
Players walking in a line into the soccer arena while the crowd cheers. Then the screen shows two teams preparing before starting the minigame.

== Gameplay ==
Players must shoot the ball into the goal to earn a point. Players can tackle the ball from the player, shoot the ball to their team player or shoot it to the goalie. The team with the most points wins the minigame.

== Ending ==
The winning team poses their victory while the other team poses their loss on the center of the arena.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} - Move
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} - Pass/Tackle
*{{button|ns|jc-top}} - Shoot
