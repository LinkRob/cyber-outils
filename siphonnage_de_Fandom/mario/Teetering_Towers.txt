'''Teetering Towers''' is a 1-Player [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must [[jump]] across the tops of tilting towers. The towers tilt in a different direction depending on where the player lands. When a tower teeters over, the player must jump to the next one. Some of the towers have either a [[Coin]] or a [[coin bag|Coin Bag]]. The minigame is cleared if the player reaches the final platform at the end. If the player falls into the [[pit]], the minigame ends in a "Miss!" and they lose five Coins.

The minigame can be purchased from the [[Mini-Game House]] for 100 Coins when it is available for purchase.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|Stick}} – Move
*{{button|n64|A}} – Jump
