The '''River Piranha Plant''' (also referred to as a '''Water Lily Piranha''') is a [[Ptooie]] variant which first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. They are only encountered in [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5]] and make their only appearance in rivers.

River Piranha Plants blow their [[Spiked Ball|spike ball]] over them to hit anyone who passes by. Eventually they lower their spike ball right over them, giving others a chance to pass. A River Piranha Plant can be defeated by either the [[Ice Flower]], [[Fire Flower]], or [[Starman]].

They later appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' in which they utilize the same technique as before.
