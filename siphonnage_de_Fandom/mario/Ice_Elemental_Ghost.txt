'''Ice Elemental Ghosts '''are little ghostly balls of ice with yellow eyes and a blue-white color that appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''. These ghosts clearly represent the ice element and can be found around cold places, such as freezers or buckets of ice. [[Luigi]] can suck these up with the [[Poltergust 3000]] and can use them to defeat ghosts and solve riddles throughout the mansion. Sucking these ghosts up you can defeat ghosts like [[Miss Petunia]] and can freeze the water in the Pipe Room. Luigi can be hurt, losing 1 HP by these ghosts. The only place he can be hurt by them is in the Tea Room, while walking on the ceiling. They are related to Water Elemental Ghosts and Fire Elemental Ghosts. They may be lead by Sir Weston.

==Trivia==
*Ice Elemental Ghosts slightly resemble [[Brrr Bit]]s from the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games, they also share a resemblance to [[Lava Bouncer]]s from the same series however Lava Bouncers are made of blue fire rather than ice.
