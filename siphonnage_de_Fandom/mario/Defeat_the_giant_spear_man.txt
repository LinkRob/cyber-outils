'''Defeat the giant spear man''' is the name for six different stories in ''[[Wario Land II]]'', all in which [[Wario]] has to defeat the titular [[Giant Spear Man]]:

* The [[Defeat the giant spear man (Maze Woods)|second story]] of [[Maze Woods]].
* The [[Defeat the giant spear man (Invade Wario Castle)|third story]] of [[Invade Wario Castle]].
* The [[Defeat the giant spear man (Go to the cellar!!)|first story]] of [[Go to the cellar!!]].
* The [[Defeat the giant spear man (Ruins at the Bottom of the sea)|second story]] of [[Ruins at the Bottom of the sea]].
* The [[Defeat the giant spear man (Uncanny Mansion)|first story]] of [[Uncanny Mansion]].
* The [[Defeat the giant spear man (Mysterious Factory!)|first story]] of [[Mysterious Factory!]].
