'''Captain Goomba Trial''' is the tenth level of [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 35.

==Layout==

Is this level, the only ally on your team is Captain Goomba.

The 1st battle contains 3 [[Beanie]]s, and 1 [[Gritty Goomba]]. The enemy captain is the Gritty Goomba.

The 2nd battle contains 2 [[Rex]]es, and 1 [[Elite Limbo Bro]]. The enemy captain is the Elite Limbo Bro.

The final battle contains 5 [[Paratroopea]]s, and 1 [[Clumph]]. The enemy captain is the Clumph.
