'''Harry''' is one of several [[human]] tennis players featured in [[Camelot]]'s ''[[Mario Tennis (series)|Mario Tennis]]'' games.

==Physical description==
Harry wears a dark purple and white tennis shirt, dark purple shorts, and white sneakers. Harry has black hair with four strands pointed upward, and he has a goatee of the same color.

==In the ''Mario Tennis'' series==
===''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' ([[Game Boy Color]])===
Harry is [[Alex]]'s doubles partner during Story mode.

===''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' ([[Nintendo 64]])===
Harry can be transferred to the [[Nintendo 64]] ''Mario Tennis'' by using the [[Transfer Pak]].

===''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''===
Harry is one of the coaches at the [[Royal Tennis Academy]].
