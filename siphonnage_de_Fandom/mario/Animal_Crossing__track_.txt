'''Animal Crossing''' is a track in the ''[[Animal Crossing x Mario Kart 8]]'' downloadable pack in in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. which is based on the Nintendo franchise of the [[Animal Crossing|same name]]. It reappeared in the base game of ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is the fourth and final course of the Crossing Cup.

==Layout==
The track starts out in Town Square and then heads out towards a bridge over a river. It then heads out into an area with trees around it and hitting the trees with a [[Kart]] or an item will cause the fruit to fall from the trees.

The player passes a jump and glide ramp that takes the player over a fountain. Around this area, there are item boxes attached to balloons. After this, the player will pass under an archway made out of balloons and enter a beach. Shells are located around here, so be careful. After this, the player will pass through a small, populated area, and over a bridge. The museum, found in most Animal Crossing games, is also passed by. Near this area, is [[Mr. Resetti]] ([[Snowboy]] during the winter), who will possibly take the place of [[Monty Mole]]s in the track. Rounding the curve after all this takes the player back to the starting line.

==Trivia==
* Coins are replaced with [[Bell (Animal Crossing)|Bells]] in this track.
* The course changes seasons, taking place during either spring, summer, fall, or winter, much like how ''Animal Crossing'' games change seasons.
