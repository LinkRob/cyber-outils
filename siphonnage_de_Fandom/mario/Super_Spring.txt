A '''Super Spring'''<ref>''Super Mario All-Stars'' Player's Guide, page 28.</ref> is an object in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''.

==Description==
A Super Spring is an upgrade of the [[Springboard]] that launches [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) offscreen, allowing him to skip portions of a [[level]]. With the right timing, Mario can [[dash]] before [[jump]]ing onto a Super Spring to travel even farther before landing. After some time, Mario or Luigi falls back down, but with improper timing, he is at risk of falling into a [[pit]].

==In ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''==
A Super Spring first appears in [[World 2-1 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 2-1]], followed by [[World 3-1 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 3-1]], [[World 3-3 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 3-3]], [[World 7-3 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 7-3]], [[World B-1]], [[World B-3]], [[World C-2]], and [[World C-3]].

In the original [[Family Computer Disk System]] version only, Super Springs are colored teal in World 7-3.
