A '''Sapphire Star''' is an object that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
Sapphire Stars are small, glowing star blocks that can be found throughout levels in the game. These stars can only be activated by shooting at them using [[Cosmic Wario]]'s lasers. Doing so will cause some coins and gems to be produced until the Sapphire Stars stop glowing. Once they do, they cannot be shot at again.

According to [[Goodstyle]], [[Wario]] can pace himself between shots in order to produce more coins and gems to collect.
