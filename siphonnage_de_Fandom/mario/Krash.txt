'''Krash''' is an enemy found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. They appear to be [[Kritter]]s riding [[Minecart]]s. They are only found in the two levels [[Mine Cart Carnage]] and [[Mine Cart Madness]]. Krashes attack by trying to drive into the [[Kong]]s and the Kongs can simply avoid them by jumping over them.

==Appearances==
In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]'', equivalents of Krashes called [[Klank]]s appear instead. They appear to be [[Kutlass]]es riding [[Skull Cart]]s. In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]'', Krashes have no equivalent.

In ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'', Krashes reappear. They act in the same habit as they did in ''Donkey Kong Country'', but only use clubs to try to smack the [[Kong]]s with so they can lose coins. Krashes can now be defeated by crashing into rocks or the Kongs may use a lever to drop something that block the Krash's path.
