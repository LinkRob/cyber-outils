'''Doc Freud''' is a character who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[A Basement Divided]]."

==Profile==
===Personality traits===
Doc Freud is a family psychotherapist who speaks in a German accent and walks around with a cane.

==Episode appearances==
==="A Basement Divided"===
Doc Freud is called in to help [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] over their conflict of Mario's sloppiness.

==Trivia==
*Doc Feud is based on and named after the famous psychoanalyst {{w|Sigmund Freud}}.
