'''Bowser's Multiplying Toads''' is an event in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' ==
If Bowser's space hits this event, a Toad clone will appear. If the player lands on the "Toad"'s space and buys the [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]], he reveals himself as [[Koopa Kid]]. He grants the player the [[Ztar]] despite it not affecting their star count.
