'''Spring Mario''' is one of Mario's forms in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. After obtaining a [[Spring Mushroom]], Mario will become spring Mario. He will be encased in a spring. While Mario will no longer be able to walk, meaning he has to bounce and controls are more slippery, Mario will be able to jump much higher than normal. This form can be obtained while playing levels in the [[Toy Time Galaxy]] and the [[Matter Splatter Galaxy]] in ''Super Mario Galaxy'', and the [[Chompworks Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.
