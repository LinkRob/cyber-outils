'''Mushroom Roulette''' is one of [[Luigi]]'s minigames in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

== Gameplay ==
Mushroom Roulette is a Mario-themed version of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roulette Roulette], but it doesn't have the green section. You get 5 coins, and can bet those 5 coins on different colors and pictures.

If your bet is wrong, you lose those 5 coins. If you bet on a color and it's correct, the number of coins you bet will be multiplied by 2. If you bet on a picture and it's correct, the number of coins you bet will be multiplied by 12.{{Super Mario stub}}
