'''Mayor Fettuccine''' is a character in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. He first appears in the episode "[[The Pied Koopa]]."

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mayor Fettuccine is a [[Toad (species)|Mushroom Person]] with a long, white mustache. He has the exact same appearance as [[Mr. Mayor]].

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
==="The Pied Koopa"===
Mayor Fettuccine and the other residents of Pastaland face a problem in which [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] kidnapped all of the children by playing his [[magic flute]].

The kids of Pastaland are being held ransom over the spaghetti crops, so Mayor Fettuccine decides that Pastaland has to give all of their spaghetti crops to King Koopa to get the kids back. [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and his friends get the kids back so that Mayor Fettuccine and the residents of Pastaland do not have to surrender their crops. To thank them, Mayor Fettuccine individually awards Mario, [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] with the Order of the Golden Meatball, a golden medal in the shape of a meatball.

==="[[Mario and the Red Baron Koopa]]"===
Mayor Fettuccine makes a cameo during the Pastaland picnic.
