'''Sim City''' is a 1989 city building game, developed by [[Will Wright]] for home computers. The [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version has a [[Mario Statue]], and the city can be stamped by [[Bowser]]. The 1961 Tokyo scenario is based on a Bowser Koopa attack. The 1991 [[Freeland]] bonus scenario has the trees in the forest, where the city is to be built, having formed to look like the shape of Mario from above.
