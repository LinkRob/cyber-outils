A '''Cymbal Bush''' is a species of plant in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Cymbal Bushes are only found on [[Lavalava Island]] where they are commonly found throughout [[Jade Jungle]]. When [[Mario]] stands on one, the Cymbal Bush closes onto him, flattening Mario; although, this does not damage Mario. Cymbal Bushes serve no other purpose.
