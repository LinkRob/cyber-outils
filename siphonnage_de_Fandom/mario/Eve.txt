'''Eve''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. She is a [[Twilighter]] who lives in [[Twilight Town]] along with her three children.

==History==
If Eve receives a visit from [[Mario]] during the beginning of Chapter 4, her children will be starving of food. If Mario gives her children food, she will be grateful but the bell soon rings and Eve transforms into a pig. After the [[Doopliss|cause of the bell ringing]] has been taken care of, Eve will turn back to normal. If Mario does give her children food, she will give him [[Shooting Star (item)|Shooting Stars]] back.

Eve also had posted a trouble at the [[Trouble Center]]. Her trouble mainly involves speaking with her past love [[Podley]]. However, issues lead to Podley to not have the same feelings for her and Eve decides to move on and focus on raising her children. Eve rewards Mario with the [[Meteor Meal]] item which is food that requires Shooting Stars to make hinting Eve makes them herself (though she also mentioned purchasing them from [[Zess T.]]

==Trivia==
*Eve's name is a reference to "evening" which fits the darker theming of Twilight Town.
