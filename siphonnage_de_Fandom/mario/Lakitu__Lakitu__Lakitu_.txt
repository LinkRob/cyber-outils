'''Lakitu! Lakitu! Lakitu!''', is the seventh level of [[Superstar Road]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked when the player collects every [[Star Coin]] in [[Meringue Clouds]]. Slide Lifts also return. This level is also used for the Don't. Touch. Anything. challenge.  

==Layout==
The level begins near a [[? Block]] on a circular track, along with a [[Koopa Troopa]]. A row of green [[Slide Lift]]s follows. Some sections of the ground follow, and [[Lakitu]]s begin to appear when blue Slide Lifts are reached. More blocks on tracks are found, as well as some orange Slide Lifts. [[Coin]]s that can be reached using [[Lakitu's Cloud]] are found above the area. Some more sections of the ground are found leading up to some pink Slide Lifts and another ? Block on a track. This is followed by a [[Hidden Block]] with a [[Starman]]. A section of fast-moving Slide Lifts is found, and multiple Lakitus can appear on screen at the same time. A [[P Switch]] is located above the third Star Coin, and causes [[Blue Coin]]s to appear, leading to three [[Brick Block]]s containing [[1-Up Mushroom]]s located in a section of the ground. Another green Slide Lift is found, along with the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin sits between two Slide Lifts.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is suspended in mid-air after a series of horizontal Slide Lifts.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is located in the ground after another series of horizontal Slide Lifts. It can only be obtained with the use of [[Lakitu's Cloud]].
