'''Anchor Man''' is [[Young Cricket]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to win the match of tug-of-war. The line indicates the players goal for the ribbon to go pass. When the team cries out, "Pull!", pull the [[Wii Remote]]. The player wins as the other team falls down and the referee holds up the white flag.

*'''Level 1:''' It takes a tug to win the match.
*'''Level 2:''' It takes two tugs to win the match.
*'''Level 3:''' It takes more than three tugs to win the match.
