The '''Boo Moon Galaxy''' is a galaxy in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' located in [[World 5 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|World 5]]. It takes place in a haunted setting, and features the poison swamp substance which [[Mario]] will die if he falls into, as well as [[Snake Block]]s. The Galaxy is represented by a crudely-made model of its own Starting Planet with three Boos. Mario can access this galaxy with 42 or more [[Power Star|Power Stars]] in his possession, including the Power Star from the first mission of the [[Space Storm Galaxy]], Follow Me, Bob-omb!

==Missions==
===Silver Stars Pop-Up===
[[Mario]] has to make his way through the boo maze, traverse through a poison swamp by using [[Snake Block|Snake Blocks]], then blast off to the moon and then go to a Pop-up Book to find [[Silver Star]]s to recover the [[Power Star]].

===Haunting the Howling Tower===
Mario needs the [[Boo Mushroom]] to [[Boo Mario|transform into a ghost]]. This allows him to climb the Howling Tower.

===The Star in the Sinking Swamp===
In Mission 1: Silver Stars Pop-Up, [[Mario]] must deviate from the normal path in the swamp and take a fast [[Snake Block|Snake block]] to the secret [[Power Star]].

===[[Green Power Star]]s===
These only appear in Mission 1: Silver Stars Pop-Up.

====Green Star 1====
In the Sinking Swamp, take the secret path and perform a [[Long Jump]] to the star.

====Green Star 2====
Jump off the Moon's left side and perform a [[Spin Jump]] to get the star.

====Green Star 3====
Perform a [[Long Jump]] off the Pop-Up Planet.

==Planets==
True to the Boo Moon's name, its planets have a haunted theme.

===Starting Planet (Ghost Maze Planet)===
This small planet is a small ball and the only place that appears to have Standard [[Gravity]]. It is visited in every mission. It is about 40 metres in diametre. When [[Mario]] lands here here, he will be given  a "lantern". Mario has to use this lantern to navigate his way through the maze and reach the [[Launch Star]] at the end. [[Boo]]s infest the planet's corridors, and will ambush Mario if they sense him nearby.

===Sinking Swamp Planet===
This flat planet is a swamp whose surface at first appears to be flat, but it eventually folds up. It is named as such in the Secret Star mission. Mario has to use the [[Snake Block|Snake blocks]] in order to make his way to the next area. The snakes do not return to their starting point, so if Mario will die if the snakes leave him.

===[[The Moon]]===
While it isn't the real thing, a crescent moon appears as a planet in this galaxy. Standing on its sides will cause it to tilt to Mario's weight, and standing on one side will cause him to fall into deep space and die of the lack of oxygen. In Mission 1, Mario has to collect five [[Star Chip]]s to form a [[Launch Star]], which appears on the planet's right side. Mario later returns here during his trip home after [[Bowser]] is defeated.

===Rise Picture Book Planet===
This graveyard-themed planet folds up like a book. It is about 80 metres on a side, bigger than the Starting Planet. To activate the planet's folding mechanism, Mario must use his [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] to hit a pink switch at the south end. Five [[Silver Star]]s are scattered here.

===Howling Tower Planet===
This planet earned its name from the fact that it is a tower infested with winds. The Howling Tower is visited only in Mission 2, where it is the mission's namesake and "end zone". Mario has to use a [[Ghost Mushroom]] to transform into his [[Ghost Mario|Ghost]] form, which he can use to fly up the tower and phase through certain walls. [[Spiny Hermit|Spiny Hermits]] and [[Octoboo|Octoboos]] infest the tower.

==Soundtrack==

* The Galaxy's theme is identical to the [[Haunty Halls Galaxy]]'s theme, being a remix of the [[Ghost House]] theme from ''[[Super Mario World]]'', which in turn is remixed from the game's overworld theme.
