'''Allergic''' is a status ailment in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
Allergic is a rather special status ailment. When [[Mario]] or his partners is Allergic, they will not be able to receive any other status effects. This means if someone is Allergic, they won't be able to get stat changes in attack, defense, etc. This means they cannot use items such as [[Power Punch]] to raise attack, [[Courage Shell]]s, to raise defense, or even the special move [[Power Lift]] that raises both. The only ways to get the Allergic ailment is to either eat [[Space Food]] to get hit by the [[Shadow Queen]]'s breath attack. Allergic will go away over time or when the battle is over.
