'''Ladida Plains 1-1 '''is the first level of [[Ladida Plains]] from ''[[Super Princess Peach]]. ''The level has [[Perry Block]]s, whenever that are hit, [[Perry]] will teach the player how to use to controls and the two vibes; [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] and [[Gloom (vibe)|Gloom]].

== Toads ==
* In the second area of the level, when the player goes straight and find the second Perry Block, the player will find the [[! Block]] which [[Princess Peach|Peach]] must whack.
* After going pass the brown platform, fall off to find the Toad.
* Jump on top of the gold platform with four squares.
