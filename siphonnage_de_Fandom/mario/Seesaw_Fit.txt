'''Seesaw Fit''' is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Description==
Seesaw Fit is a panel located in the underground area of [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser Castle]]. It is connected to a track on the room wall and functions like a seesaw.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
Seesaw Fit is used by [[Bowser]] to access higher areas of the underground area of Bowser Castle. Bowser can use [[Body Slam (Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story)|Body Slam]] on the Seesaw Fit to add to its potential energy, and it goes higher after a U-turn.
