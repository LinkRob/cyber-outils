'''Remix 10''' is a gameplay mode for ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. It appears at the pipe next to the [[World Tour]].

== Description ==
The player's objective is to collect 3 [[Bonus Medal]]s in each course. Players may obtain special rewards via collecting coins to unlock the chest at the lower right corner. Each special reward is set by players blocked through every 10 areas. At Area 30, the player can rescue [[Princess Daisy]]. As you play on, [[Bonus Medal|Bonu]]s [[Bonus Medal|Medal]]s will be useful if you get enough. When you do, you unlock the Super Bonus Game.

== Courses ==
Most courses are shortened versions of [[World Tour]] and [[Toad Rally]] courses. The mode is endless and courses are randomly selected.
