'''Pearl Square''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
Pearl Square is where the multiplayer minigames are hosted, but it only becomes accessible once the player has completed [[Tiny Wario]]'s stage, [[Forever Form Baton]].
