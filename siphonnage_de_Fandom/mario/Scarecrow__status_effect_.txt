'''Scarecrow''' is one of the status effects featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It turns [[Mario]] or another party member into the likeness of a scarecrow, limiting that character to either using a Special move or defending. Scarecrow is caused by moves such as [[S'crow Funk]] and [[S'crow Bell]]. Each character has different colored clothing as a scarecrow matching that of their actual clothing, such as how Mario wears red clothing as a scarecrow.

This status can be prevented by equipping a character with a [[Trueform Pin]].
