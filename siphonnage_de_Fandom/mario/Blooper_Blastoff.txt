'''Blooper Blastoff''', spelled '''Blooper Blast-Off''' in PAL releases, is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each of the characters are in their own [[Blooper]]-shaped submarine, and their object is to propel to the surface. The main obstacle is [[Torpedo Ted]]s, and if a character hits one, their submarine spins out for one second. [[Current]]s produced by [[Warp Pipe]]s are another obstacle, as they can push the submarine away. Whoever makes above the dotted line first is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}}: Change angle
*{{button|wii|2}}: Propel
