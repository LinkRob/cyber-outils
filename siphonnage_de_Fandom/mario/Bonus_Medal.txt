The '''Bonus Medal''' is a collectible in [[Remix 10]] in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
Bonus Medals are 
== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
Bonus Medals appear in Remix 10. If the player collects three coins each level, they will receive "Perfect". Collecting the coins will be determined by the game filling up the gauge to obtain a new item.
