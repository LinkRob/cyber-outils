'''Cliffside Crisis''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame features ten [[Bomp]]s in a cliff with grass on the top.

==Gameplay==
During the minigame, five of the Bomps can be used as platforms, and the characters have to avoid either being pushed off by the other five Bomps or letting a Bomp slide back from beneath them. Any character who falls off is eliminated. The winners are determined by either who is the last character remaining or whoever is still remaining when the 30-second [[time limit]] runs out.

The minigame has an associated challenge named Defying Gravity, in which all four characters have to avoid falling off the Bomps until time runs out.
