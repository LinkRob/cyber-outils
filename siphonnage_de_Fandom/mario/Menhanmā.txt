'''Menhanmā '''(メンハンマー, meaning "'''hammer face'''") is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

== Appearance ==
He is a small warrior who wears a yellow mask and has tuft green hair. He wields a hammer which he uses to flatten [[Wario]]. If he hits [[Wario]], he will turn into [[Spring Wario]].
