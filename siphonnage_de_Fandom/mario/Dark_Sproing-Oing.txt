}}A '''Dark Sproing-Oing''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Sproing-Oings reside in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]] and are only found in rooms 32 and 44. Dark Sproing-Oings are darker and stronger versions of [[Sproing-Oing]]s. Attacking a Dark Sproing-Oing will cause it to split into three mini-versions of itself.
