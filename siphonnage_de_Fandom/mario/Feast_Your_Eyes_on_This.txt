'''Feast Your Eyes on This''' is one of [[Ashley]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Gameplay==
There are three plates, and each one carries either one item (usually either a food or drink) or nothing. Each plate is then covered with a cloche and shuffled around.. At first, while the shuffling occurs, the player's character is contained within an upside-down wine glass to prevent them from interfering with the cloches. After this, the wine glass lifts, and the player's character is tasked to find the item (or a specific item in Levels 2 and 3) hidden within a specific cloche, during which the command changes to "Find it!". If the character chooses the correct cloche, they win the microgame, but if they choose a wrong one, they are knocked out by the cloche and lose at the microgame.

In two-player mode, there are instead four cloches, and the losing criteria is if neither player selects the correct cloche. Anyone who selects the correct cloche gains an effort point. There are three separate difficulty settings, just like in single-player.

[[5-Volt]] is listed as the most suitable character because she can aim her spirit on the correct dish to more easily keep track of it.

===Difficulty levels===
;Single player
*'''Level 1:''' Only one cloche has an item, and every cloche are shuffled once.
*'''Level 2:''' Two cloches each have an item, but every cloche is shuffled twice.
*'''Level 3:''' Every cloche has its own item, and every cloche is shuffled twice.

;Two players
*'''Level 1:''' Only one cloche has an item, but the cloches are shuffled twice rather than once like in single-player's Level 1 difficulty.
*'''Level 2:''' Every cloche has its item, two of which have the same one, and the cloches are shuffled three times.
*'''Level 3:''' Almost the same as Level 2 but with a different possible item combination of either three identical items and only one different item or four different types of items.
