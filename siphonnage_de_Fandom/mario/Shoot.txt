'''Shoot''' is a purple rabbit who plays soccer and the fourth boss of ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. Shoot is encountered in the world [[A Town in Chaos]]. He uses a tortoise as his goalkeeper, and an audience also spectates the battle. [[Wario]] must attack Shoot by turning him into a soccer ball and kicking him into the goal the tortoise is blocking. Shoot also does the same thing and Wario must quickly do it before he does. Wario must kick Shoot three times into the goal to defeat him.

==Trivia==
*Shoot is possibly related to [[Dunk]] as they are both sport-playing rabbits named for an action within said sport, except Dunk is blue and is a basketball player.
