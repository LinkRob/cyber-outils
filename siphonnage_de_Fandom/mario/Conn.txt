|homeland = [[Merlee's Mansion]]}}'''Conn''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Conn is one of many hostages in [[Merlee's Mansion]] under [[Mimi]]'s command. Conn can be found in a room where he says he hates being lonely. However, if one of the heroes gives him 100 [[rubee]]s, he will give them the passcode for the vault.

==Trivia==
*His name comes from the word "con" which means to scam.
