'''{{world|3|cannonn2}}''' is the Warp Cannon level of [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. 

To unlock this level, the player needs to locate the secret exit in {{world-link|3|towern2|World 3-Tower (New Super Mario Bros. 2)}}. Completing this level unlocks {{world-link|flower|1|World Flower-1 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)}}.
==Layout==
The player begins on jungle grounds in front of the Warp Cannon. Entering the Warp Cannon will initiate a countdown, then the player is blasted off. The player will not be able to stop running during the level. First, [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] will come across a [[Scuttlebug]], followed by a [[Wiggler]]. Then a [[Red Ring]] can be found in between a Scuttlebug and a Wiggler.  If all the [[Red Coin]]s are collected, the player will receive a [[Gold Flower]] in their storage. The level continues with more Scuttlebugs and Wigglers. After a few of them are encountered, the level ends.
