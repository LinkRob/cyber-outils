The '''Weapon and Item Shop''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. It is owned by a blue [[Toad (species)|Toad]] and is located within [[Toad Town (The Origami King)|Toad Town]]. The owner can be rescued at the end of Toad Town and is trapped inside of a tower. Oddly enough the owner is one of the few Toads required to be rescued to proceed.
