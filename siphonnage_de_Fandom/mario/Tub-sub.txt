'''Tub-subs''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Tub-subs are submarines and the variant of [[Shortfuse|Shortfuses]] appearing to be made up of [[barrel]]s. Aside from the standard brown wooden type with navy blue hoops, there are also Tub-subs made of light gray wood and purple hoops. A Tub-sub has yellow eyes on the front, with very thin pupils stretching from the top to bottom of its eyeballs.

===Behavior===
A Tub-sub can attack by firing missiles. Unlike light gray Tub-subs, a brown one moves vertically to try and attack Wario after taking its first hit. Tub-subs cannot move diagonally or turn, and they only launch missiles in a horizontal line.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Tub-subs have a threat rating of three stars. They appear in underwater levels, such as [[Wavy Waters]]. [[Wario]], in the [[Subwarine]], can defeat a Tub-sub by firing a missile at it three times. A Tub-sub drops three small [[coin]]s upon being defeated.
