The '''Mandibug Stack''' is a mini-boss in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It is a large [[Mandibug]] carrying a smaller Mandibug. 

== In {{series|Super Mario}} ==
=== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' ===
[[File:Luigiencounteringthestack.png|thumb|left|Luigi encountering Mandibug Stack in ''Super Mario Galaxy'']]
It is first encountered in the [[Honeyhive Galaxy]] on top of the tower during the end of the [[mission]] [[Trouble on the Tower]]. It attacks by charging towards [[Mario]] until it reaches a ledge, in which it will crawl backwards to the center of the arena. It can be defeated by ground pounds. If the smaller Mandibug is defeated, the larger Mandibug is angered and will move faster. If the bigger one is ground pounded, it explodes and releases a [[Power Star]].

=== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' ===
The Mandibug Stack reappears, this time in the [[Puzzle Plank Galaxy]] during the mission [[The Puzzling Picture Block]]. It appears after the player fixed the final planet that resembles a Mandibug. It drops from the air. Like with the previous ''Super Mario Galaxy'' installment, it uses the same attack strategy.

== Trivia ==
* It may be the servant of [[Bugaboom]] as he is the boss who is fought before Bugaboom.
* His way of attack is similar to the original Mandibug, but it turns angry.
