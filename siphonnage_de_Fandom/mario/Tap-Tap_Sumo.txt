'''Tap-Tap Sumo''' is a [[Gaddget]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is given as a reward from [[Pokey (character)|Pokey]] for completing the [[Mysterious Riddles]] quest.

==Description==
It is based on the children's game of [[wikipedia:Kamizumo|kamizumo]], otherwise known as paper wrestling. Tap-Tap Sumo is a single-player minigame played on one handheld that involves two [[Galoomba|Goombas]] fighting one another, one of which is blue while the other is red. The player on the left controls the blue Goomba using the {{button|gba|Pad}}, while the player on the right controls the red Goomba using {{button|gba|A}} and {{button|gba|B}}. A Goomba can tilt into a different direction by pressing {{button|gba|L}} or {{button|gba|R}}.

Each player has to tap the buttons on their side to try and knock their opponent's Goomba off-balance. Once either Goomba has fallen, making the other one the winner, a fan depicting a sun and moon goes over the screen and resets the game.
