A '''Fire Bomb''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
[[File:Fire Bomb SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
Using a Fire Bomb in battle deals 120 points of damage to all enemies on the screen. This item has double the effect on enemies that are vulnerable to fire, although enemies with fire resistance are unaffected by it. There are some ways to get a Fire Bomb, such as buying them in [[Seaside Town]] for 200 [[coin]]s while [[Yaridovich]] is in control of the town.
