The '''Waterfall Area''' is a water area located in the [[Mushroom World]]. It appeared in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== History ==
Before the film began, Peach who was raised by the Toads would go fushing until she grew up.

[[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]], [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]] and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] travelled through Waterfall Area while heading to [[Jungle Kingdom (Illumination)|Jungle Kingdom]].

== Trivia ==
=== References in video games and other media ===
* Waterfall Area is based off [[World 2-3 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 2-3]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', a level known for its jumping [[Cheep Cheep]]s on a bridge.
