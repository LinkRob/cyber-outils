'''Daisy's third eye''' is a glitch found in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''.

== Description ==
The glitch can be found on the [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]] trophy. The third eye is behind her head, under the hair on the right. If the player positions the camera and zooms closer to the trophy on the American and Japanese versions, the glitch will be seen. The glitch was fixed in the PAL and NTSC versions on '''Patch 1.2'''.
