'''Burn''' is an status aliment that appears in several ''Mario'' role-playing games.

== Description ==
'''Burn''' is a term used to refer to the victim inflicting damage from fire-based attacks. Depending on the game, the player can cure the ailment in different methods.

== In ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario series]]'' ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
Burn is first seen in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' where it is usually caused by fire-based enemies such as [[Ember]]s. The Burn status is similar to [[Poisoned]] except it lasts fewer turns. This ailment can be cured with either Refresh or a [[Tasty Tonic]].

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
[[File:BurnPMTTYD.png|thumb|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Mario can inflict burn if a fire-based enemy comes in contact. A [[Tasty Tonic]] can cure the effect.

{{clr}}

=== ''Super Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', like its game predecessors, Mario can inflict burn if a fire-based enemy comes in contact. The player must shake the {{button|wii|wiimote}} to get rid of the status.

=== ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', if Mario is burned by an enemy, he loses the turn.

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi series]]'' ==
[[File:BurnBIS.png|250px|thumb|right|Mario with burn in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''.]]
In the ''Mario & Luigi'' series, the Burn condition causes Mario, Luigi, Baby Mario, Baby Luigi, or Bowser to lose their turn. This condition can be healed with a [[Refreshing Herb]].

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', there is a harmful version of Burn called '''Char'''. Victims of Char lose 30 HP as opposed to losing 10 HP inflicted from Burn. The status effect also appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

The effect returns in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', though the victim is now able to choose an action while on fire.
