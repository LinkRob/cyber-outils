'''Anger Management''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] used by [[Wiggler]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''. Its name is shared with the phrase "anger management."

==Description==
Upon activating the move, Wiggler turns red and angry, then hits the ball to the receiver. Upon returning the ball, the receiver spins inside of a tornado.
