'''Coursebot''' is the robot companion of the player in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. It allows the player to save their [[level]]s and download levels from other players.

== Profile ==
Coursebot allows the player to save and download levels in the ''Super Mario Maker'' games. His icon resembles the robot in ''[[Mario Paint]]'' when a picture is being saved to the console. 

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS'' ===
In both [[Super Mario Maker]] and [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]], Coursebot only appears when the player is saving a level, downloading a level or loading a level. It is introduced to the player in the tutorial when first starting the game.

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
Coursebot acts the same as before. He also appears in the Story mode as a bonus for completing the game, and Coursebot is used to replay cutscenes from earlier after the player frees him from being stuck in the ground.
