'''Chopper Bros.''' is a [[Bros. Attack]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The move can be activated during [[Mario]]'s turn. It costs five [[Bros. Point|BP]] in Mode 1 and Mode 2, but three BP in Mode 3.

In Chopper Bros., the move involves Mario hammers [[Luigi]] into the ground with {{button|gba|A}}. They will then automatically jump to the enemy selected, but {{button|gba|B}} must be pressed at the right time to make Luigi jump underneath Mario, throwing him into the air. Mario can then swing the Hammer at will by pressing {{button|gba|A}} repeatedly, allowing him to chop off as much enemy HP as desired until he lands. If not done properly, Mario will fall without hitting.

It takes 32 uses of the Chopper Bros. before they can learn the [[Advanced command]]. In the advanced command, Mario hangs on the position a bit longer, Luigi pops up underneath the enemy, dealing damage, Mario hammers him back down, and the process repeats. The Advanced commands are {{button|gba|A}} to pound Luigi into the ground, {{button|gba|A}} again as the brothers near their target, and finally [[File:GBA B.png]] to make Luigi pop out and cause damage. The player should then alternate pressing {{button|gba|A}} and {{button|gba|B}} to make Luigi pop out and have Mario hammer him down again.
