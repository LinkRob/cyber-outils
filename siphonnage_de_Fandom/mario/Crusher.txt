|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Crusher''' is a special move used by Bowser in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He can learn this move at level 15 and it requires 12 [[Flower Point]]s to use.

==Description==
When using Crusher, a large stone pillar appears and strikes the enemy.

==Usage==
With Crusher, Bowser can strike a single enemy for high damage. A stronger version of this move is called [[Bowser Crush]].

== Trivia ==

* Bowser mastered this power due to his jealousy of Mallow's ability to control the weather.<ref>''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' Nintendo Player's Guide, page 13.</ref>
