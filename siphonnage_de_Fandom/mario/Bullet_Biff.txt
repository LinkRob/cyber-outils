'''Bullet Biffs''' (originally known as '''Giras''' and '''Ginas''') are enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. Bullet Biffs are [[Bullet Bill]]s that can be found in different levels in the game mainly in the last two kingdoms: the [[Easton Kingdom]] and the [[Chai Kingdom]].

==History==
Bullet Biffs, like with Bullet Bills, are often fired from cannons attempting to crash into [[Mario]]. Bullet Biffs can be easily taken down by [[jump]]ing on them.
