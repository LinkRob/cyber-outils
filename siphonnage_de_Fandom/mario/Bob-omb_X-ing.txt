'''Bob-omb X-ing''' is an [[Extra Room]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Opening==
The player's character is on a street surrounded by [[Bob-omb]]s. The camera zooms out, and the game begins.

==Gameplay==
The player's character must run around an intersection while avoiding exploding Bob-ombs. They have to survive as long as possible before being blasted off the course. The Bob-ombs on the course either walk around casually or chase after the main character; in either case, they do this until they explode. As Bob-ombs explode, more arrive from each of the four streets outside the arena. As time goes on, the Bob-ombs move increasingly faster, and there are more to avoid in the square. The minigame only ends once the player's character is blown out by a Bob-omb, although they can properly win by outlasting ten minutes in the intersection without a Bob-omb exploding on them.
