The '''Miracle Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The Miracle Badge grants the [[HP-Up Jump]] special effect in both appearances.

== In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Miracle Badge can be bought from [[Beanbean Castle Town]]'s clothes shop for 120 [[coin]]s, or 150 coins in the Japanese version. Equipping it to either [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] increases their [[Power|POW]] by 22.

In the remake, the Miracle Badge can be bought from the same location after returning from [[Oho Oasis]]. It costs 150 coins like in the original's Japanese version. The Miracle Badge now has a higher POW rating of 34, with the addition of [[Bros. Point|BP]] by 15. The Miracle Badge can be sold for 38 coins.
