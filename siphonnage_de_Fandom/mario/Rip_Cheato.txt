'''Rip Cheato''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
[[File:Rip Cheato screenshot.png|thumb|left|Screenshot]]
Rip Cheato can be found in the lower areas of [[Toad Town Tunnels]], where he can only be reached once [[Mario]] acquires [[Sushie]] in his party, allowing him to swim to the lower areas of the sewers where Rip Cheato lives. Rip Cheato's house is also revealed to the locked blue house found in the southwestern area of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. There is a nearby [[Odd Key|key]] that allows Mario to unlock the house from the other side to more quickly access Rip Cheato.

==Items==
Mario can by items from Rip Cheato, but he will not say what the item is before buying it, just telling Mario it is better than the last. He will sell Mario these items in this order:
*[[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]]
*[[Life Shroom]]
*[[Bump Attack]]
*[[Repel Gel]]
*Star Piece
*[[Super Shroom]]
*[[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]]
*[[Dried Shroom]]
*Dried Shroom
*Star Piece
*Dried Shrooms infinitely

==Trivia==
*Rip Cheato's name is also a pun of the words "rip-off" and "cheat" referencing that he is a con man.
