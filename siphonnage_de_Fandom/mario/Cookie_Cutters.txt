'''Cookie Cutters''' is a 1-vs-3 minigame found in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Opening==
The solo character cuts three remaining cookies on their plate before it is moved to the left. The screen is then divided to show each team.

==Gameplay==
The object for each side is to cut cookies into a certain shape, which is either a star, a square, or a [[Mushroom]]. The side to cut the most cookies within 30 seconds is the winner.

==Ending==
The cookies are finished baking then counted up: The star-shaped cookies are designed after [[Super Star]]s; the square-shaped cookies are designed after [[Brick Block]]s; and the Mushroom-shaped cookies are designed after red Mushrooms. Following a white transitional effect, the screen shows the characters in the kitchen, where each side performs their winning or losing animation, depending on their performance.

==Flavor text==
*'''Description:''' ''Cut out as many cookies as possible. The solo player is responsible for three cookie cutters, while the players on the team side are responsible for one cookie cutter each.''
