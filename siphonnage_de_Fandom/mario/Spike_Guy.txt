|affiliates=[[Spiny]]}}'''Spike Guys''' are a type of [[Shy Guy]] that appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. A Spike Guy can either have a red, green, blue, yellow, or pink color.

== Description ==
Their appearance is that of a normal [[Shy Guy]], the difference being that they wear a green, sharp-spiked hat. When [[Mario]] jumps on them, he takes damage.

'''HP:''' 16

'''Attacks:'''
*Attack ('''3''')
*Group Attack ('''3''')
*Spike Attack ('''9''')
*Blowback Attack ('''12''')

== Red Spike Guy ==
[[File:PMCS Red Spike Guy Card.png|left|frameless|89x89px]]

=== Price ===
Sell Price: 3 coins

=== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ===
The Red Spike Guy is first seen in [[Ruddy Road]], but can also be found in [[Cherry Lake]], [[Indigo Underground]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Violet Passage]], [[Toad Trainworks]], and [[Mossrock Theater]]. The ''best location'' to find this card is [[The Golden Coliseum]], as this level contains 5 Red Spike Guys in both the playthrough and the post game.
