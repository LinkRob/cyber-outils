'''Bumpers'''<ref>''Sonic the Hedgehog'' instruction booklet, page 7.</ref> are objects from the ''[[Sonic the Hedgehog (franchise)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' franchise that knock the player back after contact with it. Bumpers make one ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]'' within three [[minigame]]s: [[Tower Climb (minigame)|Tower Climb]], [[Tokyo Sky Flight]] and [[Bullet Train Blast]]. In Tower Climb, Bumpers are either used as springs to help [[Miles "Tails" Prower|Tails]] jump higher, or they knock Tails, possibly leading him to fall. In Tokyo Sky Flight, [[w:c:sonic:Crawl|Crawl]]'s Bumpers are used as shields used by enemies to deflect shots fired by the [[Tornado (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Tornado]], and in Bullet Train Blast, they are obstacles that knock [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] backwards, slowing him down.

==Names in other languages==
