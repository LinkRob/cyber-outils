'''''Mario Strikers: Battle League''''' (known as '''''Mario Strikers: Battle League Football''''' in Europe and Australia) is a game for the [[Nintendo Switch]] that released on June 10, 2022. It is the third installment of the {{series|Mario Strikers}}, following ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]'', a game released for the [[Wii]] 15 years beforehand.

==Gameplay==
''Mario Strikers: Battle League'' has a 4-vs-4 gameplay based around football/soccer. It introduces a new mechanic named [[Hyper Strike]], which ensures its user makes a goal.

==Characters==

== Trivia ==
*This was the only official ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game to be released in 2022; though it was one of the two games released that year to feature Mario in it overall, the other title being ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.
*These are Rosalina's and Pauline's first appearances in the ''Mario Strikers'' series.
*One stadium named "Mushroom Hill" is a reference to a zone from ''[https://sonic.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_3_&_Knuckles Sonic the Hedgehog 3 & Knuckles]''.
*If not counting the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' franchise, this is Daisy's first absence in the base roster of a Mario spinoff title since ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', however, she did make an appearance when Nintendo announced that a free update will give the player Daisy along with Shy Guy for free.
*One of Shy Guy's victory animations involves him flying in the air with a red, blue, and yellow-colored balloons. This is a direct reference to the [[Sky Guy]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
*One of Donkey Kong's goal animation became a meme.
*One of Daisy's goal animations parodies the Bully Maguire dance meme from the 2007 film ''[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_3 Spider-Man 3]''.
*This would be the last game in the franchise to have [[Charles Martinet]] as the voice of both Wario & Waluigi before the announcement in August 2023 that Martinet will be retiring from doing voice acting in ''Mario'' games.<ref>America, Nintendo of. “[https://twitter.com/NintendoAmerica/status/1693624002982314108 We Have a Message for Fans of the Mushroom Kingdom]. Please Take a Look.” ''Twitter'', Nintendo, 21 Aug. 2023,</ref>
**Charles Martinet would later serve his final role as both Mario & Luigi in ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope''.
**This is also the last game in the franchise to have [[Deanna Mustard]] as the voice of Daisy.
