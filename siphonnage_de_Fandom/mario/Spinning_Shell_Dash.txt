'''Spinning Shell Dash''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] used by [[Bowser]] in both ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
Using the move involves Bowser getting inside his shell, spinning over to the ball and hitting it, similar to Koopa's Water Shell Dash. Bowser can only activate the move if the ball is at ground level.

==Trivia==
*The act of Bowser whirling around in his [[Bowser's Shell|shell]] is much like what he does with his [[Whirling Fortress]] attack in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.
