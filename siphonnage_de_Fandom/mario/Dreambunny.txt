'''Dreambunny''', nicknamed '''Fluffykins''' by [[Eldream]], is a character in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. He is found in [[Dreamy Mushrise Park]]. Dreambunny protected the [[Nightmare Chunk]] that imprisoned Eldream. After [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] caught the Bunny, Dreambunny gave up the Nightmare Chunk and upon freeing Eldream, Dreambunny was revealed to be his pet.
