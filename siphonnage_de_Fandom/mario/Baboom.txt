'''Babooms''' are firework-like enemies in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Babooms are cylindrical [[Bob-omb]]-like creatures composed of a red segment on the bottom, a yellow one in the center, and a blue one on the top. They sport a pair of blank white eyes on the bottom segment. They have round, black feet and a black lid on top.

===Behavior===
Babooms attack by releasing three [[firework]]s if they are either [[stomp]]ed or hit with a [[fireball]].

== Appearances ==
Babooms first appear in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder|Super Mario Bros. Wonder']]<nowiki/>s [[Fluff-Puff Peaks Flying Battleship]], but they also make appearances in [[KO Arena Magma Flare-Up,|KO Arena Magma Flare-Up]], in the third battle area, where they are needed to defeat 16 [[Piranha Plant|Piranha Plants]].

Their English name is the onomatopoeia of an explosion.{{SMBWNav}}
