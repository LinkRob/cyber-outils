A '''Flatbed Ferry''' is a type of [[platform]] which is from the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Description==
A Flatbed Ferry functions like a [[lift]] in that it follows a set path made up of individual dots. If there is a red dot at the end, the Flatbed Ferry begins moving back the opposite direction upon reaching it. Some trails that a Flatberry Ferry moves on does not have a red dot at the end, so the Flatbed Ferry falls off when reaching there.

The speed in which a Flatbed Ferry moves depends on the color of its zigzag pattern, with green ones moving the slowest, yellow ones moving slightly faster, and red ones moving the fastest.
