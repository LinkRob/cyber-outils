'''Drain Badge A''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is a more powerful variation of [[Drain Badge]] in that it restores [[HP]] equal to twice as many times they damage an enemy each turn. A Drain Badge A can be bought either from buying it at [[Peach's Castle]] for 800 [[coin]]s or at the [[Shroob Mother Ship]].
