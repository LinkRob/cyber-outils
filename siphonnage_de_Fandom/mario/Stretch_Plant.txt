'''Stretch Plants''' are rubber bulb-shaped plants which appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They are also named '''Rubbery Bulbs''' due to their bulb-like appearance.

==Description==
A Stretch Plant can be [[spin|spun]] into, upon which it stretches out in the opposite direction, either to break stuff or to attack a boss or an enemy. After flinging forward, the Stretch Plant flings back into place. If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] gets hit by the rebound, they get knocked over and lose about 13 [[Star Bit]]s, although they do not take damage. However, if an enemy collides with a Stretch Plant, Mario will not lose Star Bits upon getting beaned.
