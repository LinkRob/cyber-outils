A '''Sweep Kick''' is one of [[Mario]]'s moves introduced in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Breakdance.gif|thumb|left]]
A Sweep Kick involves Mario swinging his legs around in a circular motion. It can be performed by pressing {{button|n64|B}} while holding {{button|n64|Z}}. This kick can damage any surrounding enemies or breakable objects. In ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', Sweep Kick can be used by the other playable characters: [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], and [[Wario]]. Because Yoshi has low attack power, certain enemies, such as [[Boo]]s, are unaffected by the attack.

In the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, Mario, Luigi, and [[Dr. Mario (character)|Dr. Mario]] use the Sweep Kick as their down smash attacks in each of their respective playable appearances,
