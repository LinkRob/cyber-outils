'''Wooden Bridge''' is a [[Stickers#Scraps|Scrap]] found in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' in the level [[Warm Fuzzy Plains]]. The Wooden Bridge is first turned into a Scrap after [[Bowser Jr.]] paperizes it away just after losing to [[Mario]]. Mario can only access the Wooden Bridge after getting five [[Toad]]s to help him out. Mario and [[Kersti]] can then [[Paperize]] the Wooden Bridge back into its spot.

== Description ==
The Wooden Bridge looks like a regular bridge. It is mainly brown and light brown. The Wooden Bridge is also medium-sized, not taking that much in the [[Sticker Album]].
