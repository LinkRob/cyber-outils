|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Meteor Blast''' is a special move used by a few bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Meteor Blast is used, stars will flash on the screen.

==Effects==
With Meteor Blast, the user is able to inflict moderate damage to all of Mario's party. This move is considered a weaker version of [[Meteor Swarm]].
