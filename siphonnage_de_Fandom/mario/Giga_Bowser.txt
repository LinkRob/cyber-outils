'''Giga Bowser''' is a form of [[Bowser]] appearing only in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''===
Giga Bowser is fought as a boss at the end of Adventure Mode if completed quickly enough.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''===
Bowser's transformation, [[Giga Bowser (Final Smash)|Giga Bowser]], involves him transforming into Giga Bowser.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Giga Bowser is fought as a boss in both [[Adventure Mode: World of Light]] and in some characters' [[Classic Mode]] routes.
