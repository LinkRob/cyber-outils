'''Boing!''' is [[Wario]]'s boss [[microgame]] for the [[WarioWatch]] mode in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''

==Gameplay==
There is a heavy ball attached to a person's hand via a string, and the player has to tilt the system until the ball goes down and smashes a vase.

In the Spindex, unlike other boss microgames, the player must clear Boing! ten times to get a gold crown. The microgame also starts off with ten seconds in the mode.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The vase is close by, so the system left and right.
*'''Level 2:''' The vase is down lower, and smashing it causes a spider-type creature to appear and suck on the ball.
*'''Level 3:''' The vase is even lower, so if the spring misses the vase, it immediately goes right back up to the hand.
