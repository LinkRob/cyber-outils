The '''Mailbox SP''' is an important item [[Mario]] carries throughout ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It serves as a virtual mailbox, being able to send and receive messages from other people. Despite having it from the start, Mario does not receive a message until he reaches [[Petalburg]]. The design of the Mailbox SP resembles the ''[[Game Boy Advance SP]]''.
