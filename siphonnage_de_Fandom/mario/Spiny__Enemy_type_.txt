[[File:Thorb.png|thumb|Since it has spikes on its head, the [[Thorb]] is considered to be spiny.|43x43px]]Some enemies in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'']] games cannot be jumped on. These enemies are designated as "spiny." Spiny enemies have something on the top of their body to prevent a character from jumping on the enemy safely (usually spikes, though other objects can be atop the enemy's body as well such as fire); should one attempt the attack anyway, the attack will be voided and the attacker takes a point of damage.

== Examples==

=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''===
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', spiny enemies can be jumped on safely with an advanced [[Splash Bros.]] or the [[Badge|Spiny-Killer Jump Badge]].

*[[Sharpea]]
*[[Spiny]]
*[[Queen Bean]] has a crown that can be knocked off by disabling her arms. While wearing the crown, she is considered to be a spiny enemy.
*[[Pestnut]]
*[[Piranha Plant]]
*[[Spiky Snifit]]
*[[Gritty Goomba]]
*[[Puffer-Cheep|Puffer-Cheeps]] puff up when stomped; their spikes repel jump attacks and will retract when struck with a hammer, stripping the creature of the spiny trait.
*[[Cackletta]] is able to enter a dark form when she drops to low health during the final battle; in this state, she is hazardously surfaced.
*[[Flaret]]

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''===  

*[[Spiny Shroopa]]
*Green [[Gnarantula]]
*[[Blazing Shroob|Blazing Shroobs]] are spiny with their heads set on fire, and jumping on the flame extinguishes it, allowing the Blazing Shroob to safely be jumped on. The spiny trait can also be weaponised versus the [[Shroob UFO]] that can occasionally show up to aid the Blazing Shroob; as the Bros. bounce off the flame, they knock the Shroob UFO away.
*[[Skellokey]]
*While he is standing upright, the [[Elder Shrooboid]] cannot be jumped on safely due to the spikes on his head. Countering his spiked ball orbit attack causes him to fall onto his face; he can be jumped on safely then.

*[[Thorb]]
*[[Flibbee]]s and their [[Mount Pajamaja]] variant become spiny when grounded.

*When he enters his car form, the [[Robo-Drilldigger]]'s drill arms stick up, preventing jump attacks from working.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''=== 
*[[Spiny|Spinies]] can block jump attacks with their spiky shells if not flipped over.
*[[Big Pokey]] 
*[[Tower Power Pokey]]
*[[Urchin]]
*[[Spear Guy|Spear Guys]] can point their spears skyward to block jump attacks.
*[[Pokey]]
*While [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] can stomp regular [[Venus Fire Trap|Venus Fire Traps]] (called Fire Piranha Plants here), paper Venus Fire Traps point their fangs skyward, foiling jump attacks.
*[[Ant Trooper]]s can wear helmets to block jumps, though sufficient damage will strip their helmets away, removing the Spiny status.

*[[King Bob-omb]]
*[[Bowser]] and his paper counterpart can retract into their shells, blocking jump attacks.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''===
Spiny enemies take increased damage from the Blunt Hammer and the Anti-Spike Specialist [[Battle Plug]].
*[[Spikely]]
*[[Drillby]] (including Drillby R)
*[[Mettozokettohei|Togezokettoheitachi]] can point their jack plug swords skyward to block jump attacks when at low health.
*[[Magmad]]
*Spiked [[Icead]]
*Certain [[Glohm]]-infused enemies can generate spiked shields that cannot be jumped on safely.
*When it powers up, the [[Pipegunk]] gains something hazardous on top of its "head," with that dangerous thing being a flame in fire form and spikes in ice form. This hazardous object is removed when [[Luigi]] [[Luigi Logic|hijacks]] [[Extension Corps|Shun]]'s remote. Ditto for the Pipegunk's [[Glohm]] doppelgänger.
*[[Millton]]
*[[Sharpcask]]'s [[Glohm]] doppelgänger can create spiky shields that blocks jump-based weaponry.
*[[Spiny]]
*The [[Gorumbla]]'s [[Glohm]]-infused form can create spiky shields that block jump-based weaponry
*[[Weeping Trooper|Weeping Troopers]] have a form where they have jack plugs as arms. In this state, Weeping Troopers have a spike protruding from their head, protecting them from jump attacks.
*[[Reclusa]] will transform into the Weeping Core when the Bros. enter Weeping Reclusa's maw. In this form, it is spiked. Reclusa will change into a flying monster when the Bros. destroy the Weeping Core's outer shell.

==Trivia==
*While it can safely be stomped, the [[Alarm Bob-omb]] managed to fool the ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' strategy guide's writers at Prima Games into thinking that its bell counts as a spike that counters jump attacks with the bell's conical shape.
