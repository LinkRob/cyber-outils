'''Fruit Shoot''' is one of [[Jimmy T]]'s [[microgame]]s, located under the [[Sports]] folder, in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

==Layout==
A bow with an arrow in it is shown at the top-right corner, and to the left is a statue with an apple on its head. The ground is green and grassy, and there is a tree left of the statue. Some grassy green hills also appear in a distant part of the background. A body of water is placed between the grass and the hills.

==Gameplay==
The player must hold {{button|gba|A}} until the crosshair appears over the apple. Once the crosshair is aligned, the player must release the button to shoot the arrow. If the player does not fulfill the objective when the timer runs out, they lose. During the microgame, if the player misses, they are immediately given another arrow.

*'''Level 1:''' The statue is the {{w|Venus de Milo}}.
*'''Level 2:''' The statue is {{w|Mannekin Pis}}, which is shorter than Venus de Milo.
*'''Level 3:''' The statue is of a dog, and it is even shorter.

==Trivia==
*Fruit Shoot is based on the tale of {{w|William Tell}}, who supposedly used his bow and arrow to shoot an apple off of his son's head.
