'''Twist Dunk''' is the [[Special Shot]] of [[Waluigi]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The move can be activated by tapping out an uppercase "W" twice, like for [[Wario]]'s [[Move-It Dunk]].

Upon using Twist Dunk, Waluigi dribbles the ball in the shape of a "W," then a "Γ" symbol appears. Waluigi then shoots the ball toward the basket, a [[cyclone]] trailing behind it. Waluigi [[swim]]s through the air and dunks it in. From this, Twist Dunk is likely based on Waluigi's two [[Power Shot]]s from ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', [[Whirluigi]] and [[Swimming Return]].

The visuals are identical if Waluigi uses Twist Dunk outside the three-pointer line, but a teammate needs to [[jump]], catch the ball, and dunk it into the hoop.
