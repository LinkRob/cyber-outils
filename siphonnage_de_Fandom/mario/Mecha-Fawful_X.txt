'''Mecha-Fawful Xs''' are enemies that appear exclusively in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign or the ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' [[Reissue|remake]].

==Physical Description==

Mecha-Fawful Xs are a bigger, robotic version of [[Fawful]]. They have a large metal ball that they use to damage opponents. 

==Battle==

Mecha-Fawful Xs are a Melee troop, and use [[Charging Champ]] as their [[Special Skill]]. They deal damage by charging into their foes.

==Flavor text==

*'''In-game description:''' "Mass-produced robots that serve Fawful. They charge into foes with their giant bodies."
