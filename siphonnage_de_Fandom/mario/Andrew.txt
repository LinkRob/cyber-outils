'''Andrew''' is a common boy name. Four people involved with ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' have Andrew as their first name:

*[[Andrew Iverson]], a director, designer, and writer at [[The Software Toolworks]].
*[[Andy Heyward|Andrew Heyward]], an animation producer and the former CEO of [[DIC Entertainment]].
*[[Andrew Sabiston]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s voice actor in the [[Super Mario World (television series)|''Super Mario World'' television series]].
*[[Andrew Trego]], the host of ''[[Mario Kart 8 From the Pit]]'' and ''[[The Play Nintendo Show]]''.
