'''Natsuko Yokoyama''' is a Japanese sound designer who has been working for [[Nintendo]] since 2012. She was also the voice actress for [[Nabbit]] up until [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]] where she was replaced by [[Dawn M. Bennett]].
