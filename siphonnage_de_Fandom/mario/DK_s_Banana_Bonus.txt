'''DK's Banana Bonus''' is a boss minigame in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. It and [[Diddy's Banana Blast]] are the two boss minigames of [[DK's Jungle Ruins]].

==Opening==
[[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] is seen pounding his chest. As the camera zooms out, Donkey Kong slams the platform he is on, and the [[banana]]s fly in.

==Gameplay==
The objective for each character is to collect as many bananas as possible before the time runs out. The bananas fly in from both sides of the screen, and the characters can launch out of [[Barrel Cannon]]s to collect them. Donkey Kong also throws in a golden Barrel Cannon, which moves across the top of the screen. If a character enters the golden Barrel Cannon, they are shot into a higher area with [[Banana Bunch]]es worth three bananas and a massive gold Banana Bunch worth ten.

The amount of Bananas collected by each player are added to their total in the DK's Jungle Ruins board map.

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''Blast out of the Barrel Cannons, and grab as many bananas as you can! Launch yourself into the moving Barrel Cannon to reach even greater heights.''
* '''Controls''' &ndash; ''Hold the Wii Remote sideways. Press {{button|wii|2}} to launch yourself out of the Barrel Cannon. Press {{button|wii|Pad}} to move left or right while in midair.''

==Trivia==
*Despite DK's Banana Bonus and [[Diddy's Banana Blast]] being categorized as boss minigames, they have more similarity to bonus minigames.
