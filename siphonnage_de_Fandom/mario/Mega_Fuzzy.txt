'''Mega Fuzzies''' are larger [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] found in the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros. series]]''. They have the same moves as a Fuzzy and just like them, follow set tracks however their larger size makes them harder to avoid. Despite their large size, they can be destroyed in the same way as a standard Fuzzy. Fireballs and Shells can instantly destroy one while iceballs can freeze a Mega Fuzzy to be used as platforms for reaching high ledges and getting [[Star Coin]]s.

==''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario series]]''==

=== ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' ===
[[Image:Mega Fuzzy Snow Peaks.png|thumb|left|The Mario Crew near a Mega Fuzzy.]]
Mega Fuzzies are first seen in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', they move in the same way as a standard Fuzzy (moving along set tracks) except the fact that they are larger. They are found in [[World 7-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7-3]], [[World 9-5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-5]], and [[World 9-8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-8]]. Despite their larger size, Mega Fuzzies are no more durable than a regular Fuzzy and can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]]. A Mega Fuzzy can also be frozen with the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]] and from there, the plumbers can use the frozen Mega Fuzzy as a platform for reaching higher ledges and [[Star Coin]]s.

=== ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' ===
[[Image:Mega Fuzzy Caverns.png|thumb|right|A Mega Fuzzy navigating across the wires.]]
Mega Fuzzies later appear in ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''. In this game, they are much larger than they were previously and also move at a slightly slower pace, they are commonly found in caverns such as [[World 4-C (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 4-C]]. The use of the [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Gold Flower]], [[Mega Mushroom]], [[Starman]], or [[Invincibility Leaf]], can also be used to defeat them in this game. Mega Fuzzies can also be seen moving around niches as a way of indicating secret nooks with [[Star Coin]]s.

=== ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]/[[New Super Luigi U]]/[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' ===
[[Image:Mega Fuzzy Coasters.png|thumb|left|Several Mega Fuzzies in [[Superstar Road]].]]
Mega Fuzzies also appear in ''New Super Mario Bros. U'' as well as ''New Super Luigi U'' and ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe'' behaving as they did before however this time, appearing much more commonly and also moving faster than before. In these games, they are commonly found in various levels of [[Rock-Candy Mines]], [[Meringue Clouds]], and [[Superstar Road]] alongside the smaller Fuzzies. In this game, the Mega Fuzzy can also stay in one place and float in the air as an idle obstacle. Mega Fuzzies can be beaten with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]]. They can once again be frozen with the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]] as well. Mega Fuzzies return to their size from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' in these games.

===''[[Mario Party 10]]''===
[[Image:Mega Fuzzy Minigame.png|thumb|right|Several Mega Fuzzies lingering by the Tower.]]
Mega Fuzzies are seen yet again in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', in this game, they are found in the minigame [[Fuzzy Fliers]] as obstacles. During the minigame, players are flying up the tower with their [[Lakitu's Cloud|Clouds]] amidst the scrolling screen and must avoid the Fuzzies and Mega Fuzzies along the way whilst collecting smaller Clouds to sustain their time. Some Mega Fuzzies are moving along wires while others linger idly in the air, if players are hit by a Mega Fuzzy, they lose three points which can bring them closer to their cloud losing it's puff. The player that reaches the top first is the winner.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', despite technically being larger than regular Fuzzies, Mega Fuzzies still possess the same threat level and can dock the same number of points from players.
