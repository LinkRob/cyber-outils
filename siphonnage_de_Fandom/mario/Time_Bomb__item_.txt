The '''Time Bomb''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
A Time Bomb can be thrown at another character if they are targeted; otherwise, the player's character uses it themselves. If used, the Time Bomb starts at 15 as it ticks down. The player with the used Time Bomb may pass it to other players by hitting one of them. If the Time Bomb reaches 0, the bomb explodes as the player is stunned at the moment, leaving other players to pass by.
