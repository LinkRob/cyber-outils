A '''Jump Guarder''' (also called a '''Springy Robot''') is a green Topman. It appears as an undefeatable enemy in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

== History ==
=== ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ===
Only one Jump Guarder appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', in [[Buoy Base Galaxy]]. It will summon [[Topmini]]s. [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] cannot defeat it, but they can stun it by stomping on its head, allowing the plumbers to bounce off it like a spring.
