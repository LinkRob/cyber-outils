A '''Boulder''' is a reoccurring object that appears in the series. They made their debut in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' as an obstacle.

==Appearances==
===''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''===
Boulders reappear has help objects in this game. Only [[Chunky Kong]] can lift the boulders in this game. Boulders usually block [[Music Pad]]s that usually lead to [[Golden Banana]]s (they're mainly found on [[DK Isles]]). In some cases, boulders can also be used to keep switches pressed down.

=== ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' ===
In ''Mario Kart 8'', the Boulders were obstacles in the game for [[Bowser's Castle (Mario Kart 8)|Bowser's Castle]].
