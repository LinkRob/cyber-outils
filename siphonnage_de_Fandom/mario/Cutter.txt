'''Cutter''' is a surfer in Hawaii who met [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]]. He made made a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Reign Storm]]." 

== Biography ==
When Princess Toadstool gets washed ashore during her vacation in [[Hawaii]], Cutter helps her up. Both Cutter and Princess Toadstool go on to do a variety of beach activities together, such as surfing and hang-gliding. Princess Toadstool is enjoying herself so much that she questions whether she wants her vacation to end.
