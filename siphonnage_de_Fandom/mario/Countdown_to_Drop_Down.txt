'''Countdown to Drop Down''' is a level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''
==Layout==
The level features many countdown platforms and [[Melon Piranha Plant]]s. The level demonstrates how countdown platforms work by having a [[Talking Flower]] fall into the first platform, making the platform go down by one.

After the first [[Checkpoint Flag]] is a [[Wonder Flower]] floating over the air. This can be obtained by stealing a [[Lakitu's Cloud|cloud]] from [[Lakitu]] and flying over there. The [[Wonder Effect]] will scroll the screen down and put the player into a free fall. During the free fall, the player will obtain [[Super Star]]s and kill [[Smogrin]]s. The player will obtain the [[Wonder Seed]] during the fall.

==Items==
The [[10-Flower Coin]]s can be obtained in the following order:
#Going down a green [[pipe]] between two melon piranha plants will lead the player to a secret area with a 10-flower coin.
#Hitting a [[Wow Bud]] by launching a block will summon a row of [[Cloud Block]]s, allowing the player to enter a green pipe. In this area, the player must throw a [[POW Block]] up to drop a 10-flower coin.
#After the wonder effect, there is a 10-flower coin floating. It can be obtained by either using lakitu's cloud or timing breaking a platform with the arrival of the lift.
==Trivia==
*This is the only level in ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' to not have a dark gradient indicating a [[pit]] the player cannot go in without dying. This is because the wonder effect scrolls the screen past the pit.
