'''P Switch Peril''', is the second level of [[Superstar Road]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked by collecting every [[Star Coin]] in [[Layer-Cake Desert]]. 
==Layout==
The player starts in a desert, with a [[? Block]] and a skeleton in the background. Then, three [[Iron Block]]s with Bowser's face fall. After that, the player comes across a [[P Switch]] and several [[Brick Block]]s blocking the way. When the player hits the P Switch, the Brick Blocks turn into [[Coin]]s, and the Iron Blocks start to fall. After some higher platforms and the first [[Star Coin]], the player encounters another P Switch and some Brick Blocks, acting as platforms above a pit. Afterwards, more Iron Blocks fall with some [[Spiny|Spinies]] in the way. After the second Star Coin, a [[Trampoline]] is found, leading the player to a few [[Waddlewing]]s and more Iron Blocks. The player then reaches the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Near the beginning of the level, the player must jump from the second high platform after the first P Switch.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the fourth P Switch, the player must jump over Spinies on Brick Blocks to obtain this Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Near the Goal Pole, the player can either bounce off a Waddlewing or come back on top of the Iron Blocks to receive the Star Coin.
