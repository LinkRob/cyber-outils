Found in the [[Brrrning Sea]], it can be accessed after the Bros. link [[Heatfreeze Island]]. As implied by its volcanic nature (and how they are both in the Brrrning Sea), Skorcheen Island may have been directly next to Heatfreeze Island before [[Zokket]] sundered [[Concordia]] with his desecration of the [[Uni-Tree]].

==History==
After the connection of [[Heatfreeze Island]] to [[Shipshape Island]], [[Prince Chilliam]] is ready to propose his vows to [[Princess Burnadette]], only to be sabotaged when [[Extension Corps|Ten]] injects a large dosage of [[Glohm]] into Chilliam's body. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] must head to Skorcheen Island and [[Slippenglide Island]] to find a cure.

When the Bros. arrive, they are told by a resident that the [[Skorcheen King|king of Scorcheen]] might know where [[Doctor Vulko]] is. The king reveals that Vulko had set up his lab to the north, but when the Bros. head in that direction, they are blocked by the Connectar Flowers failing to bloom. They are then tasked with connecting the island to Shipshape Island to make the flowers bloom.

Mario and Luigi eventually meet a group of [[Bob-omb|Bob-ombs]] that they are forced to fight. Upon defeating the group and entering the cave, the Bros. find their path blocked by magma, prompting them to learn the UFO Vacuum to continue on. Upon stopping the magma flow, the Bros. find a [[Hammer Bro]], [[Fire Bro]], and [[Ice Bro]]. The three [[Koopa Troop]] soldiers allow the Bros. to continue on, only to ambush them nearly immediately afterward, leading to a fight. Upon defeating the Koopa Troop soldiers, the Bro. access the lighthouse and reconnect Skorcheen Island to Shipshape Island.

Prince Chilliam and Princess Burnadette later retreat to Skorcheen Island when [[Reclusa]] goes on a rampage. The two are found praying to the sky to save their peoples, who have been trapped in the fiend's dream-inducing flowers. Burnadette expresses how worried she is that her prayers might not be heard, but Chilliam reassures her, creating a [[Bond (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Bond]] that the Bros. swipe. Mario and Luigi also get the [[Mix Tornado]] [[Bros. Attack]] from the couple.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Skorcheen Island's name comes from a deliberate misspelling of "scorching."

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Skorcheen Island is called ''Fureīmu-jima'' (written フレイーム島), meaning "Fureīmu Island"; ''Fureīmu'' comes from English "flame" transliterated to Japanese. In Chinese, it is called ''Chìhuǒ'' (written 熾火), meaning "Fierce fire." Korean translates its name into ''Hwalhwareu'' (written 활화르), from a phonetic of ''hwal-hwal'' (the ideophone for blazing fiercely; written 활활). Its Spanish name is "Flama," meaning "flame." Italian refers to it as ''Ardea'', from the verb ''ardere'' ("to blaze"), presumably with the Latin suffix -''eus'' ("made of"; inflected into -''ea''). French calls it ''Insolinsul'', from "''insolation''" ("sunstruoke") and ''insulaire'' ("insular"). Its Dutch name, ''Loejeet'', comes from ''loeiheet'' ("glowing hot"). It goes by ''Flammaika'' in German, from "flame" and the island Jamaica.
