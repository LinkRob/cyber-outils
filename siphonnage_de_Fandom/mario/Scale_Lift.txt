'''Scale Lifts''', alternatively named '''Balance Lifts''', are an object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
A Scale Lift is a mechanism featuring two platforms to stand on. Scale Lifts usually appear above pits, and using them is required to travel across. If [[Mario]] or another character stands on one platform, the other one rises. If a character stands on either platform for too long, both platforms fall off, putting them at risk of falling down the pit and losing a life.
