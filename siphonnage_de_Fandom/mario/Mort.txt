==History==
Mort used to live in [[Flopside]] but moved to [[Flipside]] where he can be found in [[Flipside Arcade]]. At first, Mort doesn't have much luck on the games losing even more than coins. However, after the events of Chapter 7, his luck starts to turn around even earning high scores on some of the games.
