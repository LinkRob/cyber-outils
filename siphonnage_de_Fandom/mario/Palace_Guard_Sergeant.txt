The '''Palace Guard Sergeant''' is a blue Toad, and one of the [[Palace Guards (Illumination)|Palace Guards]] of the Mushroom Kingdom in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. After hearing that Mario is trespassing the castle, just by the time he reached Peach, he with his [[Palace Guard Captain|captain]], his [[Palace Guard Liutenant|liutenant]], his [[Palace Guard Ensign|ensign]] and their [[Palace Guard Corporal|corporal]], and tried to arrest him, but Peach orders them to release him. And then she recognized and fell in love with him as another human, and not a monster.
