'''Micky R. Wingert''' (born July 4, 1974) is an American voice actor and voice director. In [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]] he is the English voice of the [[Talking Flower]]. He was replaced by [[Patrick Seitz]] in [[Super Mario Party Jamboree: Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV|''Super Mario Party Jamboree – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV'']].
