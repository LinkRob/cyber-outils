'''Big Bang''' is a move used only by [[King Bomb]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is only seen and used in the battle with [[Magikoopa]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]].

==Effects==
With Big Bang, the King Bob-omb explodes leaving a massive explosion that deals very high damage to all of [[Mario]]'s team. It is highly recommended to take down the King Bob-omb before it uses this move.
