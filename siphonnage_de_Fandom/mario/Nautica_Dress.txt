The '''Nautica Dress''' is an armor only usable by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be fought for 50 [[coin]]s either from the [[Shaman]] merchants at the [[sea]] or [[Sunken Ship]] or at the shop in [[Seaside Town]] once [[Yaridovich]] has been defeated. Equipping the Nautica Dress increases Princess Toadstool's [[Defense]] by 30 and her [[Magic Defense]] by 15.
