'''Wanderin Goom''' are basic enemies which first appear in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. They are members of the [[Black Sugar Gang|Brown Sugar Pirates]], but have no ability to attack [[Wario]]. Given that a Wanderin' Goom is defenseless, Wario can defeat it from using any type of attack.

Wanderin' Gooms have a variant named [[Pirate Goom]]s, which are dressed in pirate gear and wield a weapon. Although Wanderin' Gooms do not appear in ''[[Wario Land II]]'', Pirate Gooms do.

In ''[[Game & Watch Gallery 2]]'', the Modern version of [[Ball]] features a Wanderin' Goom as one of the objects that Wario has to juggle.
