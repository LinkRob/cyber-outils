'''Foul Balls''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' Being one of [[Mona]]'s microgames, it can be encountered in the Mini Spin level.

== Layout ==
Foul Balls takes place in a white void room. Its protagonist, manifesting as a stick figure with a green head, stands in the middle of the screen, facing the camera (and, by extension, the balls that he is to dodge).

== Gameplay ==
To win, the player has to tilt their Game Boy Advance so the protagonist can move his head out of the way of baseballs. The number of baseballs that have to be dodged is 1 on the first level, 2 in the second level, and 3 in the third level.

Getting hit will cause the protagonist's face to change colour and have a more negative expression, with the first ball giving him a purple face, the second ball giving him a blue face, and the third ball giving him a red face.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Foul Balls' name comes from the term "[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foul_ball foul ball]," which refers to when a batter fails to send the ball into the outfield within a pair of white lines that meet at the Batter's Box and go along the stands.
