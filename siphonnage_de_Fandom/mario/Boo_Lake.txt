'''Boo Lake''' is a course in the game ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''. It is the second course in the [[Flower Cup]]. It is the prequel to [[Broken Pier]]. It also reappears in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']].

== Layout ==
Its background looks like a bunch of dead trees and a mansion, which could be [[Luigi's Mansion]]. [[Ghost Valley 1]] and [[Ghost Valley 2]] use it. This tends to be a hard and complicated course with a dangerous shortcut, which is necessary to collect all the [[coin]]s.

This track is almost identical to ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''<nowiki>'</nowiki>s [[Ghost Valley 1]], [[Ghost Valley 2|2]], and [[Ghost Valley 3|3]]. They both feature [[Boo]]s floating around the track, but have no affect on the player. They also both have walls that break off if the player hits them. They also have bridges that flicker, disguising the pits.
