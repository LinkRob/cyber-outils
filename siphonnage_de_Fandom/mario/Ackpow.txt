'''Ackpows''' are a species in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are sphinx-hat wearing servants that work for [[Mimi]].

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
They appear inside [[Merlee's Mansion]] in Chapter 2-3. They are responsible for making sure all of the other servants are working and paying them [[Rubee]]s once they finish. Also, if [[Princess Peach|Peach]] flies towards the door above the level start, a Question Block containing a [[Mushroom (Paper Mario series)#Super Paper Mario|Mushroom]] will be found, with an Ackpow watching it. Whenever [[Mario]] or Peach strike the Question Block, they have to pay ten Rubees to the Ackpow. According to [[Tippi]], they act as though they are important, but they are actually in debt to [[Mimi]] just like the rest of the servants.

==Trivia==
*Their system of work is similar to what the Egyptians used, hence their appearance.
