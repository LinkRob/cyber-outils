A '''Mistake''', also referred to as '''Cooking Mistake''', is an item and [[recipe]] which appears in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', then went on to also appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Mistakes can only be made by having the chef ([[Tayce T.]] or [[Zess T.]]) cook an item or two items together that won't make a good recipe. Mistakes can only heal 1 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 1 [[Flower Point|FP]], and is considered the worst healing item in the games aside from the [[Dried Shroom]]s.

==Ingredients==
Mistakes can be created by either cooking an item or items that don't go together. For example, cooking using a [[Thunder Rage]] or cooking a [[Tasty Tonic]] with a [[Dizzy Dial]].

==Trivia==
*In ''Paper Mario'', if Mario eats a Mistake, he will show disdain for it's taste by sticking out his tongue.
*In ''Super Paper Mario'', if the player answers one of [[Mimi|"Merlee"]]'s questions for a Cooking Mistake at [[Castle Bleck]], a bunch of Cooking Mistakes will appear in the next room.
