'''Palkia''' is a Legendary Pokémon from the ''Pokémon'' franchise. It made its first overall appearance in ''[[w:c:pokemon:Pokémon Diamond and Pearl|Pokémon Diamond and Pearl Versions]]'', being the mascot of the latter version, but the first time Palkia appears alongside ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters is in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
In general, Palkia is known for its ability to manipulate space, in contrast to [[Dialga]], a Legendary Pokémon who can manipulate time.

In the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, as a stage element, Palkia has several abilities, which include destroying sections of the stage, shooting a powerful beam either vertically or horizontally, decreasing the stage's gravity, or affecting the camera display to become either askew, horizontal, or rotated upside-down. After doing so a few times, Palkia disappears from the stage. As a [[Poké Ball]] Pokémon, Palkia's main action is using [[w:c:supersmashbros:Spacial Rend|Spacial Rend]], one of its signature moves from the ''Pokémon'' games. Upon performing the move, Palkia slashes across the screen and flips the game camera. In either case, this is brief, and Palkia disappears after performing its move.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''===
Palkia is one of the three Legendary Pokémon to sometimes appear on the [[Spear Pillar]] stage, the other two being [[Dialga]] and [[Cresselia]]. If the stage has a purple tint, Palkia has a chance of appearing.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]''===
[[File:Palkia SSB for Wii U screenshot.jpg|thumb|250px|In ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'']]
Palkia is introduced as a Poké Ball Pokémon. Because it is a Legendary Pokémon, Palkia can be summoned from either a Poké Ball or a [[Master Ball]]. If a fighter comes into contact with Palkia, they take minor damage.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Palkia returns on both the Spear Pillar stage and as a Poké Ball Pokémon.
