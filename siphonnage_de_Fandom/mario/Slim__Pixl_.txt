'''Slim''' is a [[Pixl]] from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', found in [[Merlee's Mansion]] of Chapter 3-3.

==History==
Slim enables the player to flip to their side and avoid enemies or obstacles, similar to [[Lady Bow]] and [[Vivian]]. He also enables the player to go through thin passageways.

==Trivia==
*Slim's ability also replaces Mario's paper thinness ability from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
