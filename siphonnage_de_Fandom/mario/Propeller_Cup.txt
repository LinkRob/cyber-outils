The '''Propeller Cup''' is a cup based on a [[Propeller Mushroom]]. Making its appearance in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', it is a downloadable cup that is part of the [[Booster Course Pass]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ==
The Propeller Cup can be accessed through the Booster Course Pass.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xBly7QHQkYA|title=Mario Kart 8 Deluxe — Booster Course Pass - Wave 2 Release Date - Nintendo Switch|author=Nintendo of America|work=YouTube|date=2022-07-28|accessdate=2023-05-07}}</ref>
