'''Para-Wing''' is an unlockable Light Class kart which appears in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is [[Koopa Paratroopa]]'s kart, the kart looking like his shell, plus having a "P" on the front. Like Paratroopa, the Para Wing is a Paratroopa-shell-looking kart, having the shell textures and a red color just like Paratroopa shell color. The kart is related to the [[Koopa Dasher]]. This kart is one of the slowest in the game, like the [[Goo-Goo Buggy]]. Although it lacks speed, it has some of the best acceleration in the whole game, also like the Goo Goo Buggy. This kart can be unlocked by finishing the 50cc [[Star Cup]] in first place.

==Stats==
*'''Class''': Light
*'''Speed''': 1/5
*'''Acceleration''': 5/5
*'''Weight''': 2/5
