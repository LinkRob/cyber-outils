'''Hammerama''' is a quest in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance==
Hammerama takes place in [[Mushroom Field]]. It begins when the player selects an option to further ask [[Hammer Bro (Mario Party Advance)|Hammer Bro]] about the "Hammer Camp." Hammer Bro proceeds to explain that others train at Mushroom Field to improve their [[hammer]]-throwing skills. The scene transitions to a hammer-throwing competition. Before starting, Hammer Bro gives the player instructions on how to throw a hammer, which is by pressing {{button|gba|a}} and {{button|gba|b}} rapidly. After this, a competition to see who can throw their hammer the farthest begins. If the player's character throws their hammer farther, the quest ends with Hammer Bro rewarding them the [[Mini B-Ball]] [[Gaddget]], as a trophy. However, if Hammer Bro's hammer lands farther away, he compliments the player's skills while urging them to try again, then the game exits back to the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] map.
