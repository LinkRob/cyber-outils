'''Power''' (often shortened to '''POW''' and also referred to as '''Attack Power''' or simply attack '''Attack''' in the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series) is a recurring stat in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' role-playing games. It is first seen in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It represents the strength of [[Mario]] and other characters' attacks.

==Appearances==
===''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''===
Here, it is known as '''Attack'''.
{{sectstub}}

===[[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]===
Power works the same for the majority of this series. In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Mario's base power starts at 1. However, once he gets the [[Lucky Star (Paper Mario)|Lucky Star]] and is able to use the [[Action command]], he will be able to deal 2 points of damage per turn. Mario's partners are rather different when it comes to starting attack. For example, [[Goombario]] starts off at 1 but [[Sushie]] starts off at 3 attack. There are a number of ways to raise attack. The most known was is the [[Super Boots]], [[Ultra Boots]], [[Super Hammer]], and [[Ultra Hammer]] for Mario that permanently raises his attack for his jump and hammer. Mario's partners can get stronger by leveling them up with either [[Super Block]]s or [[Shine Sprite]]s to not only give them more attack but more moves as well. This applies to the first two ''Paper Mario'' games but changes a bit in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. Mario and the others can still level up but attack is one of two things that is automatically risen with the other being HP.
