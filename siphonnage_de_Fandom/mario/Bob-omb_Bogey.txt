'''Bob-omb Bogey''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each character has to swing a golf club and hit ten golf balls. The faster the ball is hit, the more [[point]]s the character gets. However, it is possible for a character to miss a swing. Whoever hits their golf ball first gets 50 points, followed by 30 points for second, 20 points for third, and lastly ten points for fourth. [[Bob-omb]]s appear as obstacles, and if a character swings their golf club at one, they lose 50 points. The minigame ends when each character hits all ten of their golf balls, so whoever has the most points by the end is the winner.

The minigame has an associated challenge named Aced It!, in which the player's character has to hit all ten balls before the other players without hitting any Bob-ombs.
