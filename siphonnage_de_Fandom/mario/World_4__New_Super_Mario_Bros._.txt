'''World 4''' is the fourth world in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. This world is only unlockable if [[Mummipokey]], the boss of [[World 2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2]] is defeated while [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] are in [[Mini Mario|mini form]]. Defeating the boss of this world allows [[Mario]] to progress to the snowy [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5]].

The boss of this world is [[Mega Goomba]] that can be defeated either by using elevators to reach its head or by using 27 fireballs.

== Layout ==
It is a jungle-themed world with toxic purple water seeping through the ground. The world is largely inhabited by [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Goomba]]s, with [[Scuttlebug]]s dropping down from the boughs of the trees. As aforementioned, a large swamp of toxic purple water flows through the world. If Mario or Luigi were to touch the water, they would die instantly.
