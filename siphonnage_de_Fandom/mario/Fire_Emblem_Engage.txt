'''Fire Emblem Engage''' is a [[Microgame]] from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'', based on the Nintendo Switch game of the same name. It uses the Hand Model form for its gameplay. [[9-Volt]] hosts the Microgame, which can be encountered in his level, Quest in the Dark.

== Layout ==
Alear serves as the Microgame's protagonist, sporting his design from ''Fire Emblem Engage'' and holding his hand with one finger raised. Emblem Rings will fall from above (one per round), their linked characters being telegraphed by design:

* Marth is summoned with a blue-jeweled ring.
* Celica is summoned with a blue-jeweled ring.
* Corrin is summoned with a silver ring, which is the ring Ring of the Crux of Fate.
* Byleth is summoned with a purple-jeweled ring, which is the Ring of the Instructor.

== Gameplay ==
To win, the player must move their hand to position Alear's so the falling Emblem Rings are caught on it, summoning their respective character on a success. Failure causes Alear to look down disappointedly.

=== Difficulty Levels ===
The rings are thick in the first and third level, but in the second level, the ring is very thin. Level 3 also features a coin created as a fake-out.
