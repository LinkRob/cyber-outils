==Super Gem==
*'''Super Gem 1''': Raise the blue platform and then shift the purple one to reveal a hidden passage to the first Super Gem.
*'''Super Gem 2''': Go to the other side of the blue platform and then reveal a hole. Go in the hole and then raise the blue platform to reach the Super Gem.
*'''Super Gem 3''': After getting the second Super Gem, lower the blue platform and then pluck a key out. After that, keep walking parallel from the barrier of the blue platform until you fall off the purple platform to find a locked door. Use the key to unlock it.
