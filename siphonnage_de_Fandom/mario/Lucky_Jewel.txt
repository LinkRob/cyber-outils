A '''Lucky Jewel''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. A Lucky Jewel can be purchased from the [[Treasure Hunting Toad]] for 100 [[coin]]s.

==Effects==
A Lucky Jewel is a rather unique item as using it always causes the next [[Bonus Flower]] drop to be a LUCKY! Flower every time, allowing the player to play the [[Wheeere's Yoshi?!]] minigame after the battle. The Lucky Jewel must be used before the enemies are defeated in order for it to be effective. A Lucky Jewel can only be used a few times before disappearing from the inventory.
