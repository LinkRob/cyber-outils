'''Elephant Mario''' is a power-up in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. Mario can turn into this form when he grabs an [[Elephant Fruit]].

== Description ==
As Elephant Mario, [[Mario]] becomes a bipedal elephant, retaining his facial features and his clothing, with the exception of his shoes. His trunk can be used to whip enemies to attack them.

== Trivia ==
* It is the first animal-based form to actually turn Mario into an animal, instead of putting him in a suit that looks like the animal.
