The '''Prisma Fountain''' is a structure and a key item in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. The Prisma Fountain resides in the middle of the Red District in [[Port Prisma]]. There are six different sections, each with their own unique [[File:Red Big Paint Star.png|frameless|34x34px]] [[Big Paint Star]] residing within. These paint stars produce infinite paint, and the center section mixes all of the colors, giving [[Mario]] full health when submirged.
== History ==
In the beginning of the game, the Emercency Recovery Unit (ERU) appears in the [[Prisma Fountain]] as a recovery program for instances of disappearence. After the player is given a set of instructions, [[Huey]] is revealed from the depths.
[[File:Prisma Fountain MD.png|thumb|373x373px|''Mario seeing the memories of Big Paint Stars'']]
This fountain houses all 6 [[Big Paint Star|Big Paint Stars]] of every color. Once a Big Paint Star is recovered, you can restore your [[HP]] in the fountain everytime you visit Port Prisma. Hitting specific [[Big Paint Star|Big Paint Stars]] will grant that color of paint infinitely.
