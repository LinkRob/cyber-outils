'''NES Open Tournament Golf''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' as one of [[9-Volt]]'s microgames and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[18-Volt]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The microgame features a scenario from the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game ''[[NES Open Tournament Golf]]'', in which the object is to help [[Princess Peach]] putt the ball into the hole by rotating the golf course. If she scores, the player wins at the microgame, but if she does not, the player loses.

The distance of the hole increases with each difficulty level.
