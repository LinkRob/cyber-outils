A '''Leaf''' is a sticker in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. It strongly resembles a [[Super Leaf]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. This Sticker is relatively useful as it automatically preforms all subsequent attacks so they're excellent and every [[Guard|block]] successful however it doesn't ensure 100 stomps when using an [[Infinijump]] sticker but rather only does 50 Stomps. However, the Leaf can be sold at any sticker shop for a price higher than the average sticker. The leaf comes in three rarities. The rarer the sticker, the more the leaf sells as.

==Trivia==
*In Mario's first visit to [[Rustle Burrow]], he loses all the [[Sticker]]s in his [[Sticker Album]] and they all end up scattered across the [[Scuttlebug]] lair. While Mario can recover most of the Stickers he had, any Leaf stickers in his album at that point are lost for good.
