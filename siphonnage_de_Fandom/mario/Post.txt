'''Posts''' are a recurring object in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Description==
A post has a wooden peg-like appearance. A character can [[Ground Pound (move)|ground-pound]] a Post to push it slightly downward. A Stump can be ground-pounded more times until it sinks into the ground. Pounding certain Post into the ground causes something to occur.

In ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', Posts were replaced with [[Nail]]s, which function the same as Posts. As of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', Posts have been replaced by [[Stump]]s, which also function the same. Stumps have a similar appearance to Posts, particularly from ''Super Mario 64'', except they are wider.
