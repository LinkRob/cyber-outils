'''Drombas''' are enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Drombas are a variety of [[Gromba]]s, with which they share a very similar appearance, except Drombas have a purple body and yellow eyes.

===Behavior===
Drombas behave similarly to Grombas. They always fight in group of ten through fifteen, like most other enemies in the Dream World. Drombas attack mainly with five lining up in the front row and a line behind one. As fewer Drombas appear in battle, they can attack by charging at their target, like with Grombas.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
Drombas are the [[Dream World]] equivalent of Grombas. Drombas are found in [[Dreamy Pi'illo Castle]], and they are the first enemies within the Dream World, much like how Grombas are in general. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can counter a Dromba's attacks by [[jump]]ing. As Drombas form a line and go towards Mario, it is possible for him to jump on several of them in succession.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The name "Dromba" is a portmanteau of "dream" and "Gromba".

Drombas are named '''Schlumbas''' in the pre-release European releases.
