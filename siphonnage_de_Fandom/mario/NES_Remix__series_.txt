'''''NES Remix''''' is a [[Nintendo]] crossover series featuring snippets of gameplay from various first-party [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] games in the form of brief playable challenges. Among these are several ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, including its predecessors ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|Donkey Kong Jr.]]''. 

Because of their short length and quick objective, challenges within the ''NES Remix'' series have been comparable to [[microgame]]s from the {{series|WarioWare}}, especially [[9-Volt]]'s and [[18-Volt]]'s, as their microgames often feature scenarios from classic Nintendo games.

The series features two main installments, ''[[NES Remix]]'' and ''[[NES Remix 2]]'', both of which only had a standalone digital release via the [[Nintendo eShop]] for the [[Wii U]], in 2013 and 2014 respectively. The ''NES Remix'' series also has two compilations, ''[[NES Remix Pack]]'' and ''[[Ultimate NES Remix]]'', both released on a physical medium in 2014 for the Wii U and [[Nintendo 3DS]] respectively. ''NES Remix Pack'' is a proper compilation of both ''NES Remix'' and ''NES Remix 2'' whereas ''Ultimate NES Remix'' features only some challenges from ''NES Remix'' and ''NES Remix 2''.
