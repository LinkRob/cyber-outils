'''Fawflopper''' is a grasshopper-based enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Fawflopper has a green body, a yellow belly, spring-loaded hind legs, and bear [[Fawful]]'s trademark smile and glasses.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' / ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''==
Fawfloppers among the enemies that can only be fought by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], as [[Bowser]] can easily defeat a Fawflopper on the overworld just by walking into it.

During battle, a Fawflopper can use two different [[jump]]ing attacks. It first looks at Mario or Luigi, depending on whom the Fawflopper plans to attack. A Fawflopper's main attack is lunging at either Mario or Luigi, and the targeted brother can counter the attack by jumping onto Fawflopper. There is the possibility of Fawflopper laughing before performing this attack, in which case it plans to jump over either Mario or Luigi, neither of whom must jump otherwise the Fawflopper damages them. A Fawflopper's other attack includes somehow hopping into the air a few times while making its way above either Mario or Luigi, whom it then tries to land onto. This attack can be countered using a [[hammer]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Fawflopper's name is a portmanteau of "Fawful" and "grasshopper."
