The '''Green Potion''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
The Green Potion is found in [[X-Naut Fortress]] and it can be found in the lab alongside the [[Blue Potion]], the [[Orange Potion]], and the [[Red Potion]]. During a point in the game, [[TEC-XX]] wanted [[Princess Peach]] to retrieve a [[Data Disk|special data file]] from [[Sir Grodus]]' room but she can only get in there by turning invisible. Princess Peach must use the four potions while following TEC's instructions. After making the invisibility potion and retrieving the file, Princess Peach can drink the Green Potion by itself to turn back visible.
