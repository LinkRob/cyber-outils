'''Gobbers''' are enemies that appear in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. Their first appearance is in the second mansion, the [[Haunted Towers]]. They are considered one of the toughest ghosts to deal with in the game.

== Profile ==
Gobbers are very large, yellow, obese ghosts. Their head is a bit smaller compared to the rest of their body. They have white eyes, along with a distinctly visible purple tongue. They have two large flabby arms with very large palms but rather thin spread out fingers. After the [[Dark Moon]] was restored, they gain dark yellow eyes and white irises.

Other counterparts include [[Strong Gobber]]s, Gobbers with a crystalline appearance and with stronger attacks and HP, and [[Creeper Launcher]], a boss variety of the Gobber exclusive to the [[ScareScraper]].

== In ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' ==
Gobbers make their only appearance in the Dark Moon Quest in various missions throughout the Haunted Towers, however they do appear in the ScareScraper of this game. There is another variant of Gobber called the [[Strong Gobber]] which do appear in other mansions, but they are listed as their own separate ghost.

=== Battle ===
Gobbers have a massive 100 HP requiring Luigi to take some time to defeat them. Being the large ghost they are, cannot move very fast and usually stay in one spot during the entire fight with Luigi. They attack mainly by hurling sludge at Luigi. If Luigi gets too close to them, they will bump him away (Dealing damage) with their belly. In the ScareScraper mode, Gobbers can also block doors with sludge, preventing Luigi from entering said door. The only way to remove the sludge is by defeating the Gobber.

==== Tips ====
Since the Gobbers have 100 hp, it is recommended to have the [[Poltergust 5000]] upgraded to the second level for better results. Though it's easy to stun these large ghosts with the [[Strobulb]], Luigi must also be careful as while he is sucking up Gobbers, they still hurl sludge at him, causing Luigi to slip. The Gobber often is accompanied by other ghosts, and it's recommended to capture them first to make it easier to capture the Gobber.

During the ending credits, one of the illustrations shown is Luigi eating food with a Gobber, after the Dark Moon was restored.

== In ''[[Luigi's Mansion Arcade]]'' ==
{{Noncanon}}
Gobbers return in ''[[Luigi's Mansion Arcade]]''. One Gobber first appears during Luigi's quest at the [[Old Clockworks]], launching attacks at him from the roof of the clock tower. It eventually ambushes him at the [[Transportation Hall]], bringing several other ghosts like [[Greenie]]s, [[Slammer]]s, [[Creeper]]s, [[Strong Greenie]]s and [[Strong Slammer]]s, but the Gobber is eventually defeated, allowing Luigi to access the [[Belfry Clock]] and fight [[The Three Sisters]].

Another Gobber attacks Luigi along with several other ghosts within the [[West Corridor]] at the [[Treacherous Mansion]]. The Gobber encountered at the Old Clockworks has 70 HP, although this one has only 50 HP.

== Trivia ==
* Gobbers share similarities to [[Mr. Luggs]] from ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'', as both are round, rotund ghosts that spit out projectiles and have other ghost that carry food for them.
