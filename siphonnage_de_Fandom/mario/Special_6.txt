'''Special 6''' is the sixth of the special worlds appearing in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', and the fourteenth world overall. The world takes place in a sky-based area at night, with a grey checkered ground and various sparkles, hills, and clouds in the background. Its theme is a remix of the normal [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 6]]. There are six levels and one [[Toad House]].
