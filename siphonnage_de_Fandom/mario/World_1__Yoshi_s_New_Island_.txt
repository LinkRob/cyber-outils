'''World 1''' is the very first world in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. It is located in peach-pink grasslands. [[Kamek]] and [[Big Beanie]] are the bosses of World 1. 

==Levels==
*World 1-1: [[Little Eggs, Big Eggs]] &ndash; The first level in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. It introduces [[Mega Eggdozer]]s.
*World 1-2: [[Chomp Rock 'n' Roll]] &ndash; This level introduces the player to Chomp Rocks and [[Mine-Cart Yoshi]].
*World 1-3: [[Cave of the Nipper Plants]] &ndash; This level is the first cave level in the game and it introduces [[Nipper Plant]]s.
*World 1-4: [[Fort Bucket Booby Trap]] &ndash; The first fort of the game, containing the first boss, Kamek.
*World 1-5: [[Heads Up, Hop Up]] &ndash; The first level of the game centered around moving upwards.
*World 1-6: [[Bouncy Beanstalk Walk]] &ndash; The introduction of beanstalks.
*World 1-7: [[Ground Pound Rebound]] &ndash; The introduction to ground pounding.
*World 1-7: [[Big Beanie's Castle]] &ndash; The first castle of the game containing the second boss, Big Beanie.
*World 1-S: [[Diagonal Valley Rally]] &ndash; This special level is centered around moving platforms.
