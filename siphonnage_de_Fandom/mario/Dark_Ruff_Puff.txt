}}A '''Dark Ruff Puff''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Ruff Puffs are darker and stronger versions of [[Ruff Puff]]s that are only encountered in certain rooms of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Dark Ruff Puffs attack with dark thunder that can damage from a distance. They can be easily dispatched of by jumping on them when they are not charging up their attacks.
