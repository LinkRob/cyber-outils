{{Infobox Kart|name = Standard Bike S|image = [[File:Download-2.jpg|200px]]|first = [[Mario Kart Wii]] (2008)|games = [[Mario Kart Wii]]|locked = No|caption = The Standard Bike S with [[Baby Mario]]}}'''The Standard Bike S''' is a Bike that appeared in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. In ''Mario Kart Wii'', bikes were the big, new thing. This Standard Bike S is, as you'd expect, a very standard bike.

== Mario Kart Wii ==
This bike can be ridden by small weight characters. Including [[Baby Mario]], [[Baby Peach]], [[Toad]], [[Koopa Troopa]], [[Baby Luigi]], [[Baby Daisy]], [[Toadette]], and [[Dry Bones]].
