'''Darkmess Bowser''' serves as the first boss at Cursa's Stronghold in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''. It takes on the form of [[Bowser]] himself. The monster is faced as a boss in the level King of Darkmess. He was created by [[Cursa]] due to the [[Megabug]] possessing [[Bowser]] when the heroes fought it during the events of ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== Description ==
Darkmess Bowser resembles his "real" counterpart, but his body is completely black, his bands have black spikes, his horns are orange, and his eyes are yellow.

== Combat ==
[[Bowser]] is a preset party member during this fight, while players can choose his three allies. Darkmess Bowser has his own Bowzooka, which functions the same as Bowser's, firing a projectile that explodes, damaging the target and everyone around them. His special technique summons three [[Mechakoopa|Mechakoopas]] made from Darkmess. His only weakness is him being able to dash only once. [[Magikoopa|Magikoopas]] and [[Goomba|Goombas]] assist him in combat, and his arena is designed after [[Bowser's Castle]].

By default, Darkmess Bowser is a Level 30 enemy with 21,030 hit points.

== Trivia ==

* Unless one counts the Darkmess Mechakoopa copies he summons as Rabbids, Darkmess Bowser is the only boss not to have any Rabbid helpers.
