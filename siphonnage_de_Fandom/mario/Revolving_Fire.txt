'''Revolving Fire''' is a 1 vs. 3 minigame in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Gameplay==
===Solo===
In this minigame, the single player has control of the machine that spouts out three flamethrowers on the side. The single player can have the flames move left and right in an attempt to burn the three players. Once the machine lights up, the player will be able to ground pound it, releasing a wave of lava which can also burn and knock out the players. This can all be controlled at the same time. If the single player manages to knock away the three players within the time limit, he or she will win.

===Team===
For the three players, they must survive being burned until the time ends. However, the three players will have a rather difficult time due to the amount of ways they can get eliminated.  They are able to move freely and jump over the flames but they must be careful of the edge of the arena as they can slip into the lava. The best strategy is to stay alert and moving until the time ends.
