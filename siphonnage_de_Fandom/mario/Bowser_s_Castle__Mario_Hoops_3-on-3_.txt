'''Bowser's Castle''' is a court in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. It is the third court of the [[Star Tourney]].

==Description==
The court takes place just outside the drawbridge to [[Bowser's Castle]], and a pit of [[lava]] surrounds the court.

There are [[Podoboo]]s that jump out from the lava. If a character touches a Podoboo, they catch on fire and run around uncontrollably, like what [[Bomb (species)|Bombs]] do. Another obstacle is a [[Thwomp]], which can sometimes appear from a [[? Panel]] instead of an item. Upon appearing from a ? Panel, a Thwomp instantly falls, crushing anyone beneath it.

The court features some references to ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', including an arrangement of the [[Castle BGM (Super Mario Bros.)|Castle BGM]] playing in the background and [[axe]]s for hoop poles.
