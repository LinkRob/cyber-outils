'''Spiny Hermits''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They are spiny-shelled mollusks that slowly crawl around, usually within dark areas, and usually act as moving obstacles for [[Mario]] to pass. Spiny Hermits have the ability to stick to ceilings.

Spiny Hermits are uncommon enemies in the [[Starshine Beach Galaxy]] and common enemies in the [[Boo Moon Galaxy]]. They are invincible and cannot be defeated, although Spiny Hermits are not particularly aggressive and can be easily avoided, such as from being stunned by [[Co-Star Luma]].

Spiny hermits have conical, light blue shells covered in red spikes.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Spiny Hermits are called "Sharp Points" (''Tongari''; written トンガリ). Their Spanish name, ''Moluspica'', comes from ''molusco'' ("mollusk") and ''picar'' ("to sting"). In Italian, their name is ''Paguspino'', from ''paguro'' ("hermit crab") and ''spina'' ("thorn"). French calls the creature ''Hermite Piquant'', literally "spiked hermit" (''Hermite'' is an alternative variation of ''ermite'', a synonym of ''reclus'' ("hermit").) with influence from ''bernard l'ermite'' ("hermit crab"). German refers to them as ''Stachelkrebs'', meaning "spine crab."
