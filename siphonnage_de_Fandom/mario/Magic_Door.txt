'''Magic Doors''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
Whenever [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] enters a Magic Door, the enemies and objects such as [[Block]]s and [[Warp Pipe]]s change back into their regular size. Likewise, entering a Magic Door while the enemies and objects are normal-sized turns them back to giant.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. 3''==
Magic Doors only appear in one [[level]], [[World 4-6 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 4-6]]. Using the Magic Doors is optional, because the only difference between regular and giant-sized variations other than size is where the [[Hidden Block]]s and [[1-Up Mushroom]]s are located.
