'''Belly Blast''' is [[Wario]]'s [[Super Strike]] in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]''.

==Description==
Upon using Belly Blast, Wario hops to the air and stretches his arms while the background becomes purple and shows Wario being surrounded by lightnings. Wario then uses his stomach then to shoot the [[Soccer Ball|soccer ball]] towards the goal, leaving a trail of electricity contained on the ball.
