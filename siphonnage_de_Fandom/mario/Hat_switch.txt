A '''Hat switch''' is an object that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
Hat switches are small switches shaped like [[Wario's Cap]]. Hat switches are used to help save [[Wario]]'s progress, and many can be found throughout various [[level]]s, most notably before boss fights. In a pinch, Wario can transform into [[Arty Wario]] and draw a magic door that will take him directly to the previously used Hat switch allowing for an easier time to save his game whenever he desires.
