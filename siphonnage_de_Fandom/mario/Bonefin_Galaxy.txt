}}The '''Bonefin Galaxy''' is a galaxy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It is a large galaxy covered mostly by water, and its icon depicts the planet where the [[Kingfin]] makes his lair. It is the only bonus galaxy where Mario fights a boss. In order to unlock this galaxy, [[Mario]] must collect at least 55 [[Power Star]]s and complete the [[Drip Drop Galaxy]].

== Missions ==
Due to its nature as a bonus galaxy, the Bonefin Galaxy has only one mission: Kingfin's Fearsome Waters.

=== Kingfin's Fearsome Waters ===
Mario starts on top of one of the [[Toad Brigade]]'s [[Starshroom]]s where he can use a [[Sling Star]] to jump down to the main planet.

Mario must defeat the boss of this galaxy, [[Kingfin]], a skeletal fish that roams the hollow circular planetoid. Mario has to grab some of the [[Koopa Shell]]s lying around the planet and use them to damage Kingfin by tossing them at him. Note that Kingfin will summon a special kind of [[Fish Bone]] called a [[Bonefin]] (or Skeletal Fish Guard) to come to his aid every time he takes a hit. Once Kingfin is defeated, he drops the [[Power Star]], which Mario can obtain.

== Planets ==

=== Starting Planet (Derelict Starshroom Remains Planet) ===
The Starting Planet is an abandoned [[Starshroom]] that was presumably left to rot here. It has regular [[Gravity]], meaning it can be traversed entirely.

=== Kingfin's Lair ===
The planet where Kingfin appears is separated into two parts: a central water ball with a stone core and an inaccessible black shell. It has regular gravity, and Mario will go through a hole in its shell to get to the central water ball. This is the planet that is featured in the Galaxy icon.

==Trivia==
*Bonefin's Boss music is a slight remix of the Bouldergeist's Battle Theme. The noticeable part that was changed is when you are close to Kingfin, the percussion beat that starts whenever you reveal Bouldergeist’s true form starts in the background. If you will swim away, the beat will fade to eventually disappear.
*The Bonefin Galaxy is the only bonus galaxy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' in which Mario has to fight a boss.
*This galaxy features the exact same background as the [[Ghostly Galaxy]] and the [[Boo's Boneyard Galaxy]].
*For unknown reasons, the [[Starshroom]] at the beginning of the level is empty, and no [[Toad]]s are found in this galaxy. This could be a derelict Starshroom.
