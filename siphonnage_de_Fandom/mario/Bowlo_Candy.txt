The '''Bowlo Candy''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
The Bowlo Candy is a yellow [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] that has a picture of a beach ball on the wrapper. It usually costs 15 [[coin]]s, except when the [[Candy Shop]] is selling Candies at discounted prices; in these cases, the Bowlo Candy will cost only five coins. When a player uses a Bowlo Candy, they transform into a rubber ball, similar to [[Mario]]'s appearance from ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''. This makes them a "Bowlo" character, and as they move along the board, they will steal ten coins (20 in [[Star Battle Arena]] and Duel Battle mode) from any opponent they run into. In Tag Battle, a player cannot steal coins from a teammate, as a team shares its coin and [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] total. Bowlo Candy appears on [[DK's Treetop Temple]], [[Goomba's Booty Boardwalk]], [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]], and [[Koopa's Tycoon Town]].
