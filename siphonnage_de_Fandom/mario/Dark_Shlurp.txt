A '''Dark Shlurp''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
Dark Shlurps are stronger versions of [[Shlurp]]s that are only found in the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Like with Shlurps, Dark Shlurps move around very slowly on the ground and will swallow anyone that gets too close. Dark Shlurps have a tough exoskeleton which makes outside attacks useless. The only way to defeat them is to have them swallow [[Boomer]].
