The '''Thunder Sass''' is a boss that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. He is one of the [[Beef-Off#Hooraws|Hooraws]] that must be fought in order to obtain a [[Hooraw Medal]] in order to see [[Big Massif]].

==Physical description==
Thunder Sass is a small Hooraw that wears yellow clothing. He also has an angry expression on his face.

==Battle==
The Thunder Sass, like [[Heavy Zest]] and [[Sorrow Fist]], summons Hooraw minions to his side. His battle has a condition where Mario can only win by counterattacking and can only heal during his turn.

===Attacks===
*'''Hooraw Charge:''' Thunder Sass will bounce off of the group of Hooraws and into Mario. Mario must have his hammer ready to counter this attack, AND he has to face in the right direction. The [[Big Massif]] also uses this move during his battle.
*'''Charging Fist:''' Thunder Sass will dash at Mario attempting to punch him. Mario must jump at the right moment in order to damage him.

===Tips===
Since Mario can only heal and counterattack, Mario must try his best to counterattack all of Thunder Sass' attacks and heal when necessary.

=== Stats ===
During his battle, the Thunder Sass is the least balanced (in contrast to the Heavy Zest), having the highest speed and power (in contrast to the Sorrow Fist; speed 138 and power 176), but the lowest defense and stamina (140 hit points with a defense of 53).
