'''Red Magikoopas''' are a recurring enemy in the {{franchise|Mario}}, and they first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
Red Magikoopas are a color variety of [[Magikoopa]] having the standard appearance of one, but they also wear a red robe and a red hat.

== In {{series|Paper Mario}} ==
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Red Magikoopas appear as rare enemies. They are similar to regular Magikoopas, but only can increase the attack power of their allies.

== In ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'' ==
Red Magikoopas reappear as basic characters on teams. This character is a member of the [[Bowser Jr.]] Rookies team and the Bowser Monsters team. When he is up to bat, he uses a really long magic wand. Like most of the other Magikoopas in this game, he has good pitching.

==In ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]''==
Red Magikoopas are obtainable minions. They are Ranged troopers, and their special skill is Power Up, which increases the POW stat of allies around them.

===[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]===
In [[Mario Superstar Baseball]], a Red Magikoopa can be chosen as an alternate costume of a Magikoopa on the [[Wario]] Greats. It has a point higher in batting compared to the standard Magikoopa, but a point less in running, making it one of the slower characters in the game.
