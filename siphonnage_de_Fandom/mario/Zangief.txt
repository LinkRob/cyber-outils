'''Zangief''' is a Russian wrestler and a character from the ''{{wp|Street Fighter}}'' series.

==History==
===''Super Mario Klemp-Won-Do: Muskeln sind nicht alles!''===
Zangief's first appearance in any ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media is in the ''[[Club Nintendo (German magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' comic "[[Super Mario Klemp-Won-Do: Muskeln sind nicht alles!]]" In this story, Zangief is [[Mario]]'s first opponent on his way to become "''Street Fighter II'' grand master." Mario is able to defeat Zangief by just running around him, making him feel giddy and causing him to fall down.

Zangief is the only one of Mario's opponents not to appear in the comic before the actual fight begins.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
Zangief reappears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as a Master [[spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]]. In [[Adventure Mode: World of Light|World of Light]], he is the third spirit encountered on the World Tour sub-world. The Zangief spirit battle is a stamina battle against [[Incineroar]] (with the opponent having more powerful throws and physical attacks) on the [[Boxing Ring]] stage with the music "Zangief Stage Type A" from ''[[wikipedia:Street Fighter II: The World Warrior|Street Fighter II]]'' playing.

Once the battle is won, Zangief will become available in the Dojo to teach the Overthrow Style to the player's spirits. Said style increases throw power, grab range, and grab power, but decreases attack power, special move power, and item attack.
