'''Egg Shot''' is the [[Special Shot]] performed by [[Birdo]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
To activate Egg Shot, the player must tap out a triangle on the Touch Screen twice, just like with the Special Shot for several other female characters.

Birdo begins Egg Shot by spinning around twice while purple hearts surround her; the hearts are slightly darker than those seen in [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Heart Shot]]. After spinning, Birdo catches the ball in her snout, jumps, and spits it out toward the basket as an [[Birdo Egg|egg]], with broken hearts trailing it. A puff of purple smoke appears after the egg lands in the basket, followed by several broken hearts.

If shot outside the three-pointer line, the visuals do not change, but as usual, a teammate needs to catch the ball (egg) and dunk it in the basket.
