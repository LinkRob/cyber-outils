'''Dust 'til Dawn''' is a 1-vs-3 Mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
The characters all run into a house. The solo character runs into a small room they have to clean, while the three characters stay in the larger room they entered.

==Gameplay==
The object for each side is to clean the dust off of every dust-covered items in their respective room. The solo character only has to dust a few items in a smaller room. The teammates are in a large room with more household objects needing to be dusted off, and must do so cooperatively. The first side to dust off every item in their room is the winner. If neither side finishes dusting their room within five minutes, the mini-game ends in a tie.

==Ending==
The camera zooms in on either the solo character or the team, depending on who won, and whoever is on the winning side performs their victory animation.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Dust

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''One player has to clean a small room while the other three team up to dust a huge room!''
* '''Advice''': ''Use teamwork to clean the huge room efficiently!''

==Trivia==
*The mini-game's title is a pun on the phrase "dusk 'til dawn."
*There is a page in the [[Miracle Book]] titled "Spring Cleaning," which features a scenario based on Dust 'til Dawn. In it, [[Boo]] and [[Koopa Kid]] are seen cleaning the house. If the page is activated, [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] emerges from the dust cloud, the picture of [[Brighton]] changes to [[Twila]], a [[Koopa Troopa]] looks through the window, and the television gets a clear picture of [[Princess Peach's Castle]].
