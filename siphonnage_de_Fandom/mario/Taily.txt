A '''Taily''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It tends to hang from the ceiling, and drop spike balls onto [[Mario]] and friends, in a similar fashion to what [[Spike]]s do. [[Mario]] and other playable characters can grab onto it, and swing on it like a vine. The Taily is seen in the levels "[[Taily's Toxic Pond]]", and "[[KO Arena Fungi Funk]]".
