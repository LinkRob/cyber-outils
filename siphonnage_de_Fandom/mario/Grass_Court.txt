The '''Grass Court''' is a recurring court in {{series|Mario Tennis}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]]. Aside from the ''Mario Tennis'' series, the Grass Court also appears in ''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]''.

==Description==
The Grass Court is a regular tennis court, although with a grass surface on which the characters play. The Grass Court has a ball speed of Fast and a bounce strength of Weak. It does not feature any gimmicks, being one of the three main regular tennis courts throughout the ''Mario Tennis'' series, along with the [[Clay Court]] and [[Hard Court]].
