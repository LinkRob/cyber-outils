=== Unused Autoscroll End Command ===
The autoscroll end command ('''FC''') is a duplicate of the autoscroll end command ('''FF''') <code>'''FF'''</code>, which (as mentioned) is used to stop the autoscrolling and locks the screen at the last area the autoscroll moved at.
