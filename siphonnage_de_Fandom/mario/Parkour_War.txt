'''Parkour War''' is a [[Free-for-All minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera pans from the top to the bottom of the underground.

== Gameplay ==
The player's objective is to carry three jewels to the top. The player can only hold one jewel at a time. The player has to rely on the [[Mushroom Trampoline (Super Mario 3D World)|Mushroom Trampoline]]s to reach to the top; the jewel will automatically insert itself on the missing hole for the jewel. Then the player has to go to the bottom of the ground to grab another jewel until all three jewels are gathered on the top.
