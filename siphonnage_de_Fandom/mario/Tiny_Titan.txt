The '''Tiny Titan''' (known as the '''Rally Romper''' in PAL regions) is a small unlockable [[kart]] that appears in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]], [[Mario Kart World]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Tour|Mario Kart Tour.]]'' 

== Description ==
It is based on the [[Wikipedia:Monster (vehicle)|Monster]] from ''[[Famicom Grand Prix II: 3D Hot Rally]]'', and it has a bike counterpart called the [[Magikruiser]]. This is further shown in its Japanese name, which is a direct reference to the game.

== ''Mario Kart'' series ==

=== ''Mario Kart Wii'' ===
The Tiny Titan can be unlocked by unlocking an expert staff ghost in Time Trials, by winning 50 WFC races online (now impossible) or playing 1,200 races. 

The Tiny Titan's strongest stat is [[Off-Road]], whilst all other stats are either balanced, above or below average.

The expert Staff Ghost (Nin*solami) for [[Desert Hills]] uses a [[Dry Bones]] driving a Tiny Titan.

{{MKW Kart Stats/Tiny Titan}}

=== ''Mario Kart Tour'' ===
The Tiny Titan appeared as a kart in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']], specifically during the [[List of tours in Mario Kart Tour|Sunshine Tour (2025)]], where it debuted in a [[Mario Kart Tour X Mario Kart World|special event crossover]] with Mario Kart World. It appeared with three other remixes, the Desert Tiny Titan, Rainbow Tiny Titan and Cool Tiny Titan, all of which were High-End rarities. Its special skill is [[Rocket Start|Rocket Start Plus]].

=== ''Mario Kart World'' ===
The Tiny Titan appears as a kart in [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']].
