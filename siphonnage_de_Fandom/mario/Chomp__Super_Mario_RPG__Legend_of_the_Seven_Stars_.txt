{{Nihongo|'''Chomp'''|モンスター アタック|Monsutā Atakku|Monster Attack}} is a special move used by [[Croco]] and [[Lakitu]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Croco only uses this move during his second fight with [[Mario]]'s party in [[Moleville Mines]].

==Description==
Croco reaches into his page and throws an enemy at one of Mario's party members.

==Effects==
With Chomp, Croco tosses an enemy at one of Mario's party for high damage.

==Trivia==
*A version of this move is used later in the series by another crook named [[Popple]].
