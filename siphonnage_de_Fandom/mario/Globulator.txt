The '''Globulator''' is an item in ''[[Mario is Missing!]]'',

==Description==
On either the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] or [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version, the player can press the Start button to bring up a map of [[Earth]] on the Globulator, showing all of the lands that have been completed up to that point. [[Luigi]] can use the Globulator to summon [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] to assist him or even to exit an area.
