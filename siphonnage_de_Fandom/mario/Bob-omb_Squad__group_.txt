|games   = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}The {{Nihongo|'''Bob-Omb Squad'''|ボンバーズ|Bonbāzu}} (also known as the {{Nihongo|'''Big Bomb Boomers'''|セクシーダイナマイツ|Sekusī Dainamaitsu|Sexy Dynamites}}) is a team that is fought in the [[Glitz Pit]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. The team consists of four [[Bob-omb]]s with one of them being their leader, [[Master Crash]]. They are ranked 11th in the tournament are fairly weak, but are somewhat strong compared to the rest of the minor league.
