'''Find the 8 Red Coins''' is the fourth [[mission]] of [[Bob-omb Battlefield]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario 64]],'' its [[Nintendo DS|NDS]] [[Reissue|remake]], ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and its 2021 enhanced port, [[Super Mario 3D All-Stars|''Super Mario 3D All-Stars'']].

== Description ==
The goal for the playable character to collect the eight [[Red Coin]]s hidden throughout [[Bob-omb Battlefield]], allowing to collect the [[Power Star]]. This is the first Red Coin mission.

== Locations ==
* One atop the [[Lift]] near the [[Chain Chomp]].
* One on top of the [[Stump]] that holds the [[Chain Chomp]].
* One on top of the small stone hill near the mountain and the [[Chain Chomp]].
* One on the steep hill at the entrance to the mountain.
* One atop of the tree on the island from [[Shoot to the Island in the Sky]].
* Two at the area near the stumps where this mission's star spawns.
* One hidden behind the caged gate with the [[Bob-omb]]
