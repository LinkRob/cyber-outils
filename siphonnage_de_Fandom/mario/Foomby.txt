'''Foomby''' is a character who appears in the ''[[Nintendo Adventure Books]]'' story ''[[Monster Mix-Up]]''. She is [[Goomby]]'s twin sister and the secondary [[Amalgamates|Goomba Troopa]] leader.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Foomby resembles any other [[Amalgamates|Goomba Troopa]]. She is basically a [[Goomba]] whose head has been replaced by that of a [[Koopa Troopa]].

=== Powers and abilities ===
Like [[Goomby]], Foomby can summon [[Goombomb]]s to trick foes.

== Biography ==
The Goombada twins and their lackeys storm [[Mario]]'s gang. Mario kidnaps the twins themselves, but the duo summon Goombombs into the mix to stop his assault, creating a puzzle. Mario must find the twins, lest the Goombombs explode, their flash blinding Mario. The Mario brothers and Toad will then be left on their own if the latter happens.
