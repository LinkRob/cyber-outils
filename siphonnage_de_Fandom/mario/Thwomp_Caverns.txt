'''Thwomp Caverns''' is the interior location of [[Thwomp Volcano]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] and their [[Baby Mario|infant]] [[Baby Luigi|selves]] can enter here by riding down on [[Mr. Thwomp]]. 

The level begins with [[Bowser]] meeting his infant self, [[Baby Bowser]], but they never realize they're the same person. There is also an early location where the Baby Bros. can glide in midair and collect a large amount of coins. In a secret area, a Thwomp hosts an arcade minigame for the babies to play.

Bowser and Baby Bowser, are fought as bosses here. The level is finished after they get defeated.
