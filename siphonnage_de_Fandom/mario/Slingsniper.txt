The '''Slingsniper''' is [[Luigi]]'s fourth available [[Bros. Attack]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. Like the [[3D Green Shell]], it is mandatory to obtain, as its [[Attack Piece]]s are given to Luigi by [[Big Massif]] and [[Lil' Massif]] after completing the [[Ball Hop]] puzzle on [[Mount Pajamaja]]. 9 [[Special Point|BP]] is required to use this attack.

== Procedure ==
When the attack is initiated, a large slingshot is planted in the foreground of the battlefield. Once Luigi climbs inside the slingshot, the player will have to hold down on the {{button|3ds|stick}} to allow Mario to stretch it further into the foreground. Once the slingshot is fully stretched out, the player will have to hold down the {{button|3ds|B}} button while tilting the 3DS to aim the slingshot. Letting go of the {{button|3ds|B}} button will automatically launch Luigi in the direction the aiming reticle was pointing. The attack will fail if the player takes too long to aim or if Luigi misses the enemies. If the player lets go of the {{button|3DS|stick}} before pressing the {{button|3ds|B}} button to fire Luigi, it will automatically fire and will deal a minimal amount of damage to a random enemy. It is possible to hit several enemies in a row with the attack.

Slingsniper does critical damage to [[Pi'illodactyl]]s, [[Wiggler]], [[Pi'illodactyl R]]s, [[Pi'illodium]], [[Grobot X]], [[Torkscrew X]], [[Mammoshka X]], [[Dreamy Bowser]] and [[Pi'illodium X]] if it strikes them where a star mark appears.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The Slingsniper's name comes from the word "slingshot" mixed with "sniper."

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, the Slingsniper is called the ''Sunaipu Pachinko'' (literally "Sunaipu Slingshot"; written スナイプパチンコ), with ''pachinko'' (written パチンコ) meaning "slingshot" and ''Sunaipu'' coming from a transliteration of "snipe."

== Trivia ==

* This attack is one of two [[Bros. Attack|Bros. Attacks]] to not use the A button in the ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' games, the other being the non-Advanced Command version of the [[Thunder Bros.]] attack from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.
* It is also one of two Bros. Attacks throughout the series to have only one word in their name in the English dub (four if one counts [[Bros. Item|Bros. Items]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' due to the [[Cannonballer]] and the [[Trampoline]]), the other being the [[Dropchopper]], which also appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.
