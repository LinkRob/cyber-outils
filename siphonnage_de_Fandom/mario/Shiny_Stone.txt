The '''Shiny Stone''' is an item that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
The Shiny Stone can be used to unlock the locked door in [[Monstro Town]] that leads to [[Culex]]. In order to get the Shiny Stone, one must head to [[Moleville]] and first purchase [[Fireworks]] for a pricey 500 [[coin]]s. They then must give the Fireworks to a mole girl in exchange for the Shiny Stone. The Shiny Stone can also be traded for a [[Carbo Cookie]].
