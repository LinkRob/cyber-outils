'''Bombshell Koopas''',<ref>''Super Mario Land'' Virtual Console digital manual, page 14.</ref> originally known as '''Nokobon'''<ref name="English instruction booklet">''Super Mario Land'' English instruction booklet, page 13.</ref> or '''Nobokon''',<ref>''Club Nintendo'' (UK) ''Classic'', page 7.</ref><ref>M. Arakawa. Nintendo ''Game Boy'' Player's Guide. Page 5.</ref> are enemies introduced in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bombshell Koopas are a variant of [[Koopa Troopa]]s with a [[bomb]] for a [[Koopa Shell|shell]].

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Land''====
[[File:Bombshell Koopa SML sprite.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Bombshell Koopa Alt.png|thumb|Alternate artwork for ''Super Mario Land'']]
Bombshell Koopas are the most common enemy in ''Super Mario Land'', appearing in all levels except for [[World 2-3 (Super Mario Land)|Worlds 2-3]] (the [[Marine Pop]] level) and [[World 4-3 (Super Mario Land)|4-3]] (the [[Sky Pop]] level). Bombshell Koopas are not invulnerable to [[Superball]]s, and they explode shortly after being [[stomp]]ed. They do not explode if a Superball is used on them. They are somewhat similar to the recurring [[Bob-omb]]s, except they cannot be moved and their blast radius is small and cannot take out other enemies and obstacles. Like [[Red Koopa Troopa]]s, Bombshell Koopas do not walk off edges, but merely pace back and forth on the platform.

====''Super Mario Land 2 - 6 Golden Coins''====
In ''[[Super Mario Land 2 - 6 Golden Coins]]'', normal Koopa Troopas appear as enemies, and Bombshell Koopas themselves are succeeded by a [[Buzzy Beetle]]-like variant, [[Noko Bombette]], whose name is a reference to the original Nokobon. (Bombshell Koopas were only referred to as such in the 2011 port of ''Super Mario Land'' to the [[Virtual Console]], almost twenty years after ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins'' was released for [[Game Boy]].)

===''Super Mario Land'' manga===
In the ''[[Super Mario Land (manga)|Super Mario Land]]'' manga, Bombshell Koopas are the second enemy type met by [[Mario]] and [[Mekakuribō]] (after the [[Goombo]]s). Mario stomps on them and they turn into bombs, confusing Mario enough to get hit by their explosions. In the last chapter, a Goombo steals a magical seal from the [[Chai Kingdom]] temple that prevented it from being attacked by [[Pionpi]], and tosses it to an exploding Bombshell Koopa, destroying it, in the hope that the Pionpi bite the heroes sheltered in the palace.

===''Super Mario Bros. Print World''===
[[File:SMBPW Nokobon.png|thumb|''Super Mario Bros. Print World'' graphic]]
In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Print World]]'', a Bombshell Koopa is one of the printable graphics.

==''Wario'' appearances==
===''WarioWare Gold''===
In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', a Bombshell Koopa appears in the second level of the [[microgame]] [[Super Mario Land (WarioWare Gold)|Super Mario Land]].
