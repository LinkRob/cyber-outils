'''Chao''' (both singular and plural) are a species from the {{franchise|Sonic the Hedgehog}}. They made their first overall appearance in ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic Adventure|Sonic Adventure]]'', but the first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance of Chao is ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'' for [[Wii]]. Chao are voiced by Tomoko Sasaki.

==In {{series|Mario & Sonic}}==
===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'' (Wii)===
Chao, along with [[Hero Chao]], make a cameo appearance as audience members in various events. [[Hot-air balloon]]s in the shape of Chao's heads also appear in the background of some outdoor [[Event]]s, including [[Archery (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games for Wii)|Archery]] and [[Single Sculls]]. A Chao appears alongside [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in the message that reminds players to take break after playing for a while.

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'' ([[Nintendo DS]])===
Like in the Wii version, Chao appear as audience members, and Chao-shaped hot-air balloons appear in the background of both the [[Dream Long Jump]] and [[Archery (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games for Nintendo DS)|Archery]] events.

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' (Wii)===
[[File:M&SOWG audience.png|240px|thumb|left|''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''' (Wii) screenshot of various audience members, with Chao being among them]]
Like in both versions of ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'', Chao return as audience members, and various Chao-shaped balloons appear in the background of certain events. Aside from Hero Chao, [[Dark Chao]] now appear in the audience as well. Like in ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'' for Wii, a Chao and Toad appear in the message that reminds players to take a break after playing for a while. In Festival Mode, in either Singles or Team mode, when training for the [[Ski Cross]] event, the player's character has to race a Chao to the end. Aside from this, the only other role a Chao has is occasionally sending mail to the player.
{{br|left}}
===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' (Nintendo DS)===
Chao retain their role as audience members and hot-air balloon cameos. In [[Curling (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games for Nintendo DS)|Curling]], a pair of Chao form part of the team, taking on the role of sweeping the path for a character's curling stone. A Chao's color corresponds to whichever team it is on.

In [[Adventure Tours]], several different varieties of Chao, including Hero Chao and Dark Chao, appear in the different areas of [[Winter Island]], where they host events and set challenges. Some Chao appear on the unlockable photo of non-playable characters.

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' (Wii)===
[[File:MSL2012 Sticker Chao.png|thumb|A sticker of Chao in ''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games'' for Wii]]
Chao and their varieties return as audience members. In [[London Party]] one of the obtainable stickers is of a Chao. A Chao character named [[Cheese (character)|Cheese]] appears alongside Cream to host the mode. In some Bonus Games, the player must count the number of Chao flying around on the screen.
{{br|left}}
===''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' ([[Nintendo 3DS]])===
[[File:Chao Omochao M&S London 2012 3DS.png|thumb|left|200px|Chao and Omochao in Story mode in ''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games'' for Nintendo 3DS]]
Chao, along with Hero Chao and Dark Chao, retain their usual role of being audience members. Unlike the Wii version, the Chao-shaped hot-air balloons now appear in certain events. A pair of Chao also form one of the teams that the player competes against in events requiring a pair of characters, even though the Chao themselves never participate in the events. Regular Chao, along with Hero Chao and Dark Chao, each have their own obtainable badge.

In Story mode, a Chao appears at the opening ceremony for the Olympic Games. The same one appears in the bonus episode [[Junior, Super Hero?]], in which [[Bowser Jr.]] inadvertently saves the Chao during its confrontation with an [[Egg Pawn]] and an [[Egg Flapper]].

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]''===
As usual, Chao appear among the audience members, alongside Hero Chao and Dark Chao. Hot-air balloons shaped like Chao's head appear in the background of certain events, to the extent of also appearing on the game's key artwork. In [[Groove Pipe Snowboard]] and [[Roller Coaster Bobsleigh]], a Chao is seen sitting on the [[Lakitu (referee)|Lakitu]]'s starting lights. Chao also appear as referees and camera operators in certain events, such as in [[Ski Jumping Large Hill]].

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' (Nintendo 3DS)===
Chao, along with Hero Chao and Dark Chao, appear as audience members. In [[BMX (Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games for Nintendo 3DS)|BMX]] and [[Table Tennis Plus]], Chao appear as referees and camera operators. In [[Football (Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games for Nintendo 3DS)|Football]] and [[Football Plus]], Chao also appear as additional teammates for [[Sonic the Hedgehog (character)|Sonic]] or the player's [[Mii]] if their opponent is [[Mario]].

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' ([[Wii U]])===
Unlike in the Nintendo 3DS version, only regular Chao appear as audience members. Chao retain their role of being referees and camera operators, specifically for the events [[Table Tennis (Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games for Wii U)|Table Tennis]] and [[Rugby Sevens (Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games)|Rugby Sevens]]. Chao also appear as a part of Sonic's float, which occasionally appears on [[Copacabana Beach]].

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''===
Chao return as audience members, and there are various hot-air balloons in the shape of a Chao's head.

In Story mode, Chao are non-playable characters who can be spoken to in various areas of the map. However, the Chao only speak their own language, consisting of multiple uses of "Chao."

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
Chao only make a few cameo appearances.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''===
A group of five Chao make a cameo as a [[trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]], while three Chao are featured as a [[sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]]. Both collectibles are based on their appearance in ''[[w:c:sonic:SONIC ADVENTURE DX: Director's Cut|SONIC ADVENTURE DX: Director's Cut]]''.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]''===
[[File:SSB3DS trophy Chao.png|thumb|left|''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' trophy]]
Similarly to ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', another trophy with three Chao was added.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Chao is featured in the form of a primary [[spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]], which can be enhanced into a Hero Chao. The battle involves facing off against four [[Squirtle]] on the [[Windy Hill Zone]], and they become increasingly powerful after eating.
