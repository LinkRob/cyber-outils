'''Ick Magnet''' is a [[microgame]] found in the [[List of WarioWare: Touched! microgames#Dance Club Rub|Dance Club Rub]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective of the game is to use the stylus to rub across the magnet to generate static electricity to suck up all the things on the floor. This microgame has twice the time to complete, as it takes time to suck up all the things on the floor.

*'''1st level difficulty:''' The pad is close to the floor.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The pad is far from the floor.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' There are two pads: one is close and one is far.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The name is a pun on the term "Chick Magnet", which refers to a man who is good at getting the attention of women.
