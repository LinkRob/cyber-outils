'''Boom-Brella''' is [[Princess Peach]]'s default weapon in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
The Boom-Brella appears in ''Sparks of Hope'' as Peach's weapon. The player can change the skins of the Sharpshooter. Princess Peach will be unlocked once Rabbid Peach is saved from the Darkmess Manta, since Peach owns this weapon.

== Statistics ==
The Boom-Brella can attack multiple opponents in front of Peach, the closer the target is to her, the more damage Peach does.
