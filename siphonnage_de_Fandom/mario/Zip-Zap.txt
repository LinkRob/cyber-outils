'''Zip-Zaps''' are enemies with yellow electric spikes from Super Mario Bros. Wonder. They only appear in the level [[Cruising with Linking Lifts]] and are seen jumping from clouds. Zip-Zaps can only be defeated by [[Bubble Mario|Bubble Mario.]] Zip-Zaps hurt the player on contact by electrocution.
