==Appearance==
Iron Jump stickers are similar to regular [[Jump]] stickers except they're grey in color. They only take up one sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]].
==Notes==
Iron Jump stickers have the same power as regular Jump stickers except for the fact they can be used on spiked-enemies. Because of this. Iron Jump stickers act like the [[Spike Shield]] badges from ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door|Paper Mario: The-Thousand Year Door]]''. Iron Jump stickers are very common early in the game and can be bought in the [[Sticker Shop]] in [[Decalburg]].
