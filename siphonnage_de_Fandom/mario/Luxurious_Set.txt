The '''Luxurious Set''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing a [[Dyllis Dinner]] with a [[Fried Shroom Plate]]. This rich meal restores 30 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
