A '''Condart''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Condart is a yellow bird with a red beak. The tip of a Condart's beak is black.

===Behavior===
When a character approaches a Condart, it launches itself in a direction like a dart until hitting a solid surface, causing the Condart's beak to become stuck in the surface. Condarts take a while to free their beaks, leaving themselves vulnerable to attack.

== Trivia ==

* The Condart's behaviour is similar to the Auroros from ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''.
