'''Coin Bag''' is an item in [[Coinathlon]]. The Coin Bag appears in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. If the player users the item, five [[Red Coin]]s will drop onscreen.

== Description ==
The Coin Bag is exclusive to [[Coinathlon]]. The player can obtain the item if they are not in the first place. If the player has a [[Mario]] [[amiibo]], the player will gain the Coin Bag. When the player uses the Coin Bag, the item drops five [[Red Coin]]s onscreen, allowing the player to move three spaces each.
