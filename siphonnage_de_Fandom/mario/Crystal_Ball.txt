The '''Crystal Ball''' is an object featured in some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. It is often used by psychics and fortune-tellers to foresee one's future. The Crystal Ball first appears in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'', and was inducted into the ''Mario'' franchise with its overseas conversion ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Bros. 2''==
A Crystal Ball is usually guarded by a [[Birdo (species)|Birdo]], who releases it upon being defeated. [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]], or [[Toad (character)|Toad]] can pick up the Crystal Ball to open the [[Mask Gate]]'s mouth, marking the end of a level.

==In the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]==
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', the Crystal Ball is only needed to help complete one of [[Koopa Koot]]'s favors. [[Mario]] needs to obtain an [[autograph]] from [[Merluvlee]] of [[Shooting Star Summit]], but she needs her Crystal Ball back from her sister [[Merlee]] who lives in [[Dry Dry Outpost]]. After Mario retrieves the Crystal Ball, he can give it to Merluvlee to get her autograph which then must be brought to Koopa Koot to receive three [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Pieces]].

In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', the Crystal Ball only appears as an unobtainable object under the ownership of [[Merlon]]. It can later be upgraded with the Ultra Stone allowing Mario to rank up his partners to the Ultra Rank.
