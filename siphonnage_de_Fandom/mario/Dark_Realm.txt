The '''Dark Realm''', or the '''Underworld''', is the second of the two parts of [[Jewelry Land]], following after the [[Light Realm]], in ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]''. It contains five levels, which have a darker, more mystical theme to them. Completing every level causes [[Bowser's Castle]] to appear.

==Levels==
*[[Cornice Cave]] (Boss: [[Magikoopa]])
*[[Ghost Mansion]] (Boss: [[Big Boo]])
*[[Float Castle II]] (Boss: [[Chargin' Chuck]])
*[[Dark Sea]] (Boss: [[Mecha Nokonoko]])
*[[Bowser's Castle (Yoshi's Safari)|Bowser's Castle]] (Boss: [[Bowser]])
