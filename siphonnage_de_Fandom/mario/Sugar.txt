'''Sugar''' is a food and key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
Sugar can be found in the kitchen of [[Princess Peach's Castle]] where [[Princess Peach]] and [[Twink]] must bake a [[Special Strawberry Cake|cake]] for [[Gourmet Guy]] after the events of Chapter 4. Given it's a cake, Sugar is a key ingredient as it helps make the cake sweet. If the cake is correctly made, Gourmet Guy will be pleased and give Peach and Twink information on the next [[Star Spirits|Star Spirit]].
