'''Red Toads''' are the most notable color variant of the [[Toad (species)|Toad]] species. They typically have natural red spots that match the red vests they commonly wear. One notable exception is [[Toad (character)|Toad]], the prominent character, who usually sports a blue vest, though he has occasionally appeared in a Red Toad form in some mario games. Red Toads often are known as loyal helpers who appear as NPCs in various ''Mario'' games.

== History ==

=== In [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario (series)'']] ===

==== ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' ====
There are seven [[Toad (species)|Mushroom Retainers]], portrayed as Red Toads whose red vests match the red spots on their caps. They appear in the first seven worlds at the end of the [[castle]], each encountered after Mario defeats a [[Fake Bowser]]. Upon meeting him, they famously deliver the line: ''"Thank you, Mario! But our [[Princess Peach|Princess]] is in another castle."'' Red Toads frequently show up as NPCs in various Mario games, where they cheer on the heroes, provide helpful items, and support any efforts to rescue Princess Peach from the clutches of King Bowser, who’s notorious for kidnapping her time and again. The mushroom retainers return in [[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]] with the same role and quote allong with the [[Super Mario All-Stars|Remake]] version of both these games.

==== ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' ====
A Red Toad joins [[Mario]], [[Yellow Toad]], and [[Blue Toad]] on a courageous adventure to rescue [[Princess Peach]] from [[Bowser]]. He’s notably seen wearing a [[Tanooki Suit]] and sporting a red tie during the end credits, as he returns to the Mushroom Kingdom alongside the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] after their successful mission. Additionally, several Red Toads appear throughout the game’s levels, often seen cheering on Mario and [[Luigi]] during their quest.

==== [[Super Mario Maker 2]] ====
A Red Toad apears as one of the seven construction Toads led by [[Taskmaster]] and [[Toadette|Chieff]]. The Red Toad's job is constructing the middle part of [[Princess Peach's Castle]] in order to complete the tasks. There where two occasions where Red Toad was kidnapped during a break moment which led [[Mario]] on a mission to rescue him twice.

=== In [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party (series)'']] ===
Red Toads are recurring characters in the ''Mario Party'' series. Known for their enthusiastic cheering, they often appear in the background of various minigames, providing support and adding atmosphere. In the ''[[Toad Scramble]]'' mode of [[Mario Party: Star Rush]], one Red Toad becomes a playable character, allowing players to take control of the iconic crimson-capped mushroom and participate directly in party-style gameplay in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree: Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV]],'' Players must escort two Red Toads through a maze filled with electrified obstacles. If a player accidentally pushes a Red Toad into an electric trap, it becomes encased in a bubble, the bubbled Toad is then transported back to the start or last checkpoint flag.
=== Other Media ===

==== [[Mario Strikers (series)]] ====
In the Mario Strikers series Red Toads can be chosen as sidekicks to help the Captain win the soccer game. They can perform winning animations after they scorred a point. In [[Mario Strikers: Battle League]] Red Toads can be chosen as one of the Toad variant color to play with.{{stub}}
