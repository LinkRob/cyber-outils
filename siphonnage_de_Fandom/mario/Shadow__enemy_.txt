'''Shadow''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', in which they only appear in [[Kero Sewers]]. Shadows are never found on the field, but they accompany other enemies such as [[Rat Funk]]s in battle.

==Battle==
In battle, Shadows mainly attack by either clawing at someone or shooting an energy blast. They can also use [[Endobubble]] to inflict the Fear status on a character.
