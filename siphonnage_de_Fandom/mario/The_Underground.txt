'''The Underground''' is a soccer field in the {{series|Mario Strikers}}. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario Strikers]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
The Underground is a playing field made out of metal. It was funded by [[Wario]] and [[Waluigi]]. As its name implies, it is located several stories underground. The capacity is 35,000, and it is the [[Star Cup]]'s special field.

In the store demo mode of ''Super Mario Strikers'', The Underground is the only unlocked field.
