'''Shaved-Ice Cave''' is the fourth [[level]] of [[World 4 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|World 4]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Layout==
Shaved-Ice Cave is more of a rest stop than a full level. It contains a [[Toad]] in-cased in ice (but is somehow able to 'talk' to Mario) running a [[Sticker Shop]].

==History==
Mario takes a well-deserved break after exploring the horrors of [[The Engimansion|The Enigmansion]]. He buys [[stickers]] from a Toad who is frozen then heads on out back into adventure.

==Trivia==
*This is the second level in the game that is mainly a rest stop. The first is [[Outlook Point]] from World 3.
