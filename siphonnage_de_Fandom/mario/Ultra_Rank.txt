The '''Ultra Rank''' is the final rank that Mario's partners can reach. This was first introduced in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. With the Ultra Rank, Mario's partners will learn their best moves and their attack power increases. In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', once Mario gives the [[Up Arrow]] to Merlon, Mario can start to rank up his partners to the Ultra Rank. Also in this game, Mario's partner's HP increases at every time they level up.
