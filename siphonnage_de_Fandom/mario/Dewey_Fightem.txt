'''Dewey Fightem''' is a character from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Dewey Fightem is a lime green [[Concordian]] man who wears a green outfit and light blue rimmed sunglasses. He has a yellow wire with a leaf, implying he is from [[Raynforst Island]].

=== Personality ===
While Dewey Fightem is fascinated by monsters, he is also scared by them.

== History ==
Dewey Fightem makes his debut when [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Snoutlet]], and [[Connie]] enter the room just before the [[Gobblick]]'s lair during their invasion of the [[Great Lighthouse Islands|Great Lighthouse Island]] of the [[Lushgreen Sea]]; they find him close to the staircase, just before the gate blocking off the path forward. Dewey introduces himself and praises the Bros. for being heroes and warns them about the Gobblick. Mario and Luigi can later find him on [[Shipshape Island]]. Talking to Dewey is required to start the Glohm Gobblick sidequest. When the player activates the sidequest, Dewey reveals that he spied on some [[Mettozokettohei#Variants|Togezokettoheitachi]] at the Lushgreen Sea's Great Lighthouse Island and learned that a Glohm-infused copy of the Gobblick is there. Too scared to research it on his own, Dewey rewards the Bros. with the Gobblick Boots DX and a [[Battle Plug]] that staves off the effects of the bond-destroying substance [[Glohm]]. The process repeats with him watching the [[Zokket Army]] at the Great Lighthouse Islands of the [[Color-Full Sea]] and the [[Brrrning Sea]], learning of Glohm doppëlgangers of the [[Sharpcask]] and the [[Pipegunk]]. Destroying those monsters has Dewey Fightem reward the Brow. with the Sharpcask Hammer DX and the Pipegunk Boots DX, respectively. Upon the defeat of all three Glohm bosses, Dewey Fightem calls Mario and Luigi the actual toughest monsters.

Dewey Fightem claims that he is related to someone who is involved in the Monster Mania craze, perhaps referring to the [[Monster Maniac]]. If what he says is true, it is unknown what familial relationship they have.

When [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|hijacks]] [[Concordia]], Dewey is one of those who manage to escape [[Reclusa Dream Flower|getting trapped in a fantasy]].

== Nomenclature ==
In the Japanese and Chinese dub, Dewey Fightem is called "Lundes," with no known etymology; the former has his name rendered "Rundesu" (written ルンデス) due to Japanese sound laws, which ban the use of the L sound at all and consonant sounds except /n/ at the end of a word, while the latter has his name rendered "Lúndésī" (written 倫德斯). Spanish refers to Dewey as Lucho Lid, from ''lucha'' ("battle") and the Spanish given name Lucho and ''lid'' ("fight"). Portuguese has him called "Edu Vido," from the name "Eduardo" and ''atrevido'' ("bold"). In Italian, he is called ''Mostromane'', from ''mostro'' ("monster") and ''mánia'' ("mania"). French gives him the name ''Lucas Stanieur'', from ''castagneur'' ("brawler"). In German, Dewey has his name translated as ''Kamfred'', from ''Kampf'' ("battle") and the given name "Fred." In Dutch, he is called "Willem Poffe," from ''wil hem poffen'' ("want to thump him"). In Korean, he is called ''Yo-u'' (written 요우), of unknown etymology.
