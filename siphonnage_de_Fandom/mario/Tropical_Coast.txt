'''Tropical Coast''' is a track in the [[Mario Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

This level takes place on a beach-themed course, along with an underwater stage. As you drive down the beach, when you see a water drop on your screen, then you'll know when you are going underwater. The first underwater part of the stage starts with a "Welcome" sign above and you drive through a coral reef with bloopers and cheep cheeps in the distance. Then you will head to an underwater cave and then make your way back on the beach, and then repeat the process for 3 laps.
