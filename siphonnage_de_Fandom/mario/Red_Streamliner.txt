The '''Red Streamliner''' is a [[Kart]] that appeared in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Red Streamliner is a red color variation of the [[Streamliner]], swapping the azure blue for red. Like the Streamliner in the game, both use the [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The Red Streamliner is a Super kart that made its debut in the [[Holiday Tour (2019)|2019 Holiday Tour]]. The Red Streamliner increases the duration of [[Drafting|Slipstream]] boosts and the points earned for them.
