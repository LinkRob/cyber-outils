'''? Island''' is one of the eleven islands of [[The Great Sea]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. It is located in the B-5 Tile on the [[Sea Chart]]. The island consists of [[Origami Stone Spike]]s, [[Origami Sidestepper]]s, many [[Question Block]]s, and a [[Paper Macho Stone Spike]] on top of the hill. A [[Sensor Lab]] can be located here.

== Layout ==
The island as the name says it is shaped like a question mark. It is a grassy island with slopes where the [[Origami Stone Spike]]s throws rocks. In the center area are a floor made out of [[Question Block]]s where the Sidestepers are located. There is a little entrace at the side who leads to a underground part with chest that contains a Collectible and a block that releases a [[POW Block]] who can be used to hit the ? Blocks.
