The '''Cutie Dice Block''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Description==
The Cutie Dice Block is the special pink [[Dice Block]] of [[Toadette]]. It has three sides with a 3, and the other three have a 4. Unlike other character-specific Dice Blocks, the Cutie Dice Block rolls from the pool of median numbers rather than from high or low rolls. The Cutie Dice Block is the only Dice Block to always roll at least a 3.
