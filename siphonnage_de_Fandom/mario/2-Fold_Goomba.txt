A '''2-Fold Goomba''' is an enemy seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', which only appear in [[Bouquet Gardens]].

==Description==
2-Fold Goombas are basically two regular [[Goomba]]s folded together.

==Battle==
2-Fold Goombas mainly attack Mario by crawling to him then closes their body on him. Being two Goombas put together, their HP and Attack power are double from single Goombas. Mario can use any Sticker to take down 2-Fold Goombas.
