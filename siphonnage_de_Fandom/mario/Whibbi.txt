|race = [[Nimbi]]}}'''Whibbi''' is a character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Appearance==
Whibbi is an older [[Nimbi]] that has a very long mustache. He also wears a light blue robe.
==History==
Whibbi is first seen in-cased in ice from [[Bonechill]]'s attack in [[The Overthere]]. After freeing him, Whibbi reveals he is one of the sages and tells the heroes that they will need the orbs from the other sages in order to gain access into [[King Grambi]]'s palace.
