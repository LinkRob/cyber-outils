A '''Cactus''' (plural: '''Cacti''') is a real-world prickly plant often found in desert areas. Their first appearance in a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game is ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', carried over from ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]''. [[Pokey]]s and their variants are designed after cacti.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Bros. 2''===
Cacti appear as platforms in some of the desert [[level]]s. Although cacti are prickly, neither [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]], nor [[Toad (character)|Toad]] take damage from standing on one.

===''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''===
Cacti are featured as obstacles in [[Slipsand Galaxy]], during the mission [[Sailing the Sandy Seas]], where they appear throughout the trail on the Sandy Sea Planet. Whenever the [[Sand Bird]] touches a Cactus, the parts that touched it are temporarily broken off.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
[[File:Cactus MK64 sprite.png|frame|left|''Mario Kart 64'' sprite]]
[[File:Cacti in Dry Dry Ruins MKWii.png|thumb|Yoshi near some round cacti in Dry Dry Desert in ''Mario Kart Wii'']]
Cacti are hazards found in several desert-themed courses throughout the series. Cacti are among the hazards to hinder the movements of anyone who drives into them.

Cacti make their series debut in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'', in which they appear in [[Kalimari Desert]]. They are retained in the course's reappearances in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' [[Booster Course Pass]] (Wave 2).

Cacti make their second appearance in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', where they appear in two courses: [[Sunset Wilds]] and [[Yoshi Desert]], the former returning in ''Mario Kart Tour''.

The third ''Mario Kart'' game in which Cacti appear is ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', in which they appear in [[Dry Dry Desert (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Dry Dry Desert]], and are retained in the course's reappearance in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''.

In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', Cacti make their fourth appearance, and are located in both [[Dry Dry Ruins (Mario Kart Wii)|Dry Dry Ruins]] and [[Thwomp Desert]].

There is a [[kart]] designed after a cactus named [[Cact-X]], which first appears in ''Mario Kart 7''.
