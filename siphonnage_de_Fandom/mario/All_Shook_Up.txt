'''All Shook Up''' is a microgame belonging to [[Ashley]] in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player must open all soda bottles. If the player do not open all bottles on time, the player loses in the mircogame. If the player wins, the bottle or bottles will shoot out soda. 
*'''Level 1:''' Open one soda bottle.
*'''Level 2:''' Open two soda bottles.
*'''Level 3:''' Open three bottles. The last bottle open will shoot out confetti.
