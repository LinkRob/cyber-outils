A '''Fake ? Panel''' is an item in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

== Description==
Fake ? Panels are based on [[Fake Item Box]]es from the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]] in that they are an item imitating the appearance of an object granting an item, which in a Fake ? Panel's case is a [[? Panel]]. At first, a Fake ? Panel has the appearance of a red [[Question Block|? Block]], although with a horizontally flipped question mark. When a Fake ? Panel is thrown onto the ground, it has nearly the same appearance as a ? Panel, but its only distinguishing factor is a horizontally flipped question mark. If a character walks onto a Fake ? Panel, they trip over and lose [[coin]]s.
