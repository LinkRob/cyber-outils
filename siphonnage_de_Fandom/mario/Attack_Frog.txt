'''Attack Frog''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is one of the Gaddgets available from the start of the game.

== Gameplay ==
The Gaddget requires four players to play the game. Each of them controls one of the frogs. The player must aim to gain three points at the end of game. The empty square will show a fly or a snake at a random time. The first player who takes the fly that appears, gains a point. The first player who takes the snake loses a point. If none of the players take the fly or the snake, the square will become empty again.

=== Controls ===
*{{Button|gba|L}} - Controls upper-left frog
*{{Button|gba|R}} – Controls upper-right frog
*{{Button|gba|Pad}} – Controls bottom-left frog
*{{Button|gba|A}}/{{Button|gba|B}} – Controls bottom-right frog
