'''Towering Treetop''' is a [[board]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Towering Treetop night.jpg|thumb|left|Night variation]]
Towering Treetop is mainly a forest-themed board with several flowers placed along the ground. It is centered around a large talking tree, alluding to [[Woody Woods]] from ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. Towering Treetop is surrounded by a few other trees and a lake behind it. Towering Treetop follows the traditional format of the characters making their way to the [[Star Space]] and purchasing a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] for 20 [[coin]]s.

===? Space events===
The [[Event Space|? Space]] behind the start forces the character to use a big feather to tickle the giant tree's nose in order to have it sneeze. If successful, the giant tree lets it out, causing other characters to drop to lower parts of the board. If the giant tree is unsuccessful, nothing occurs.

The ? Space left of the beehive gives characters the choice to ride one of three [[fluff]]s for free. Whatever fluff they choose takes them to another part of the board.

Landing on one of the three ? Spaces in front of the [[beehive]] triggers [[bee]]s to come out and sting the character. The number of bees determines how many coins the character loses, with each bee taking away two [[coin]]s.

The ? Space at the top of the board has two functions, depending on what time of day it is. If it is day, the character sees [[Woody]], who showers coins on all the spaces and characters. If it is night, he is replaced with his counterpart, the [[Evil Woody]], who causes [[Spiny Egg]]s to rain down on all spaces and characters, who lose coins in the process.

===Other events===
Other events happen depending on the time of day as well. During the daytime, the pink flower at the left of the board is in bloom, allowing characters to reach the following spaces that they would not be able to reach by night. At night, that flower closes up, and the purple flower at the right side of the board blooms, allowing characters to use the path that is inaccessible during the daytime.

Towering Treetop is one of two boards to feature [[Red Boo|Pink Boo]], the other being [[Castaway Bay]]. Pink Boo appears at her house only at night, where she offers the character passing by her to steal either coins or a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] from an opponent.
