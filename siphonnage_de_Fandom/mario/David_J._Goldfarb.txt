'''David James Goldfarb''' (born April 17, 1964) is an American voice actor who has voiced [[Ludwig von Koopa]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', replacing [[Mike Vaughn]] from that game onwards (aside from Mike Vaughn's reused clips for ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'').

== Voices ==

* [[Lemmy Koopa|Ludwig von Koopa]] (2014-present)

== Roles ==

* ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' - [[Lemmy Koopa|Ludwig von Koopa]] (uncredited)
* ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' - Ludwig von Koopa (uncredited)
* ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' - Ludwig von Koopa
* ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' - Ludwig von Koopa (uncredited)
* ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' - Ludwig von Koopa
* ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' - Ludwig von Koopa
* ''[[Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit]]'' - Ludwig von Koopa
* [[Super Nintendo World]] - Ludwig von Koopa (Mario Kart: Bowser's Challenge)
