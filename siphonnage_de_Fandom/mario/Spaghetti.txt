'''Spaghetti''' is a well-known Italian [[pasta]] dish. It is also [[Mario]]'s favorite food in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', also confirmed officially by [[Nintendo]].

In ''[[Hotel Mario]]'', [[Luigi]] says he hopes the [[Princess Peach|Princess]] made a lot of spaghetti, as he and [[Mario]] are on their way to have a picnic with her. His line has become viral in YouTube Poops and internet memes, especially during the late 2000s and early 2010s.

In ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', if Mario is left idle long enough he will take a nap and can be heard talking in his sleep, during which time he mention the Italian foods spaghetti and ravioli, leaving one to assume he is dreaming about them).

In the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]], [[Spaghetti (Paper Mario series)|Spaghetti]] is featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' in the form of an item, also being titled Spaghetti Plate in the latter game.
