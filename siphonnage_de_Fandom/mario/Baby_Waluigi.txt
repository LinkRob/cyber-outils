'''Baby Waluigi''' is a character that first appears in [[Mario Tennis Fever|''Mario Tennis Fever'']]. He is an infant form of [[Waluigi]] after having been transformed by a curse during the events of the Adventure mode<ref name="trailer">Nintendo of America (January 16, 2025). [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=myC8ZlDiulo&ab_channel=NintendoofAmerica ''Mario Tennis Fever'' – Nintendo Direct 9.12.2025.] YouTube. Retrieved September 12, 2025.</ref>, but also appears alongside his adult counterpart in the normal gameplay modes.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSyoMsWRL9I ''Mario Tennis Fever'' Overview Trailer</ref>

== Profile ==

=== Physical appearance ===
He appears like a cross between the other baby characters ([[Baby Mario|Mario]], [[Baby Luigi|Luigi]], [[Baby Peach|Peach]], and [[Baby Wario|Wario]]) and adult [[Waluigi]], with Waluigi's purple cap, yellow "Γ" logo, and (in Story Mode) his black overalls with yellow buttons. Like Baby Wario, his shoes become baby sneakers.
