'''World 1-6''' is a level from [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. 
<br />
==Layout==
The level starts with three rolling hills with [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopas]] on them, followed by a larger platform. The center [[Brick Block]] on this platform contains a [[Beanstalk]], which takes the player to a bonus area containing a [[Roulette Block]]. The next section has a pair of floating platforms guarded by Paratroopas and a [[Piranha Plant]] in a pipe below. This is followed by another rolling hill with a pair of pipes containing Piranha Plants and a larger platform on which the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] sits.

There is a hidden [[Warp Pipe]] located below and to the left of the midway point. To access the Pipe, the player must drop onto the next rolling hill and backtrack with a [[jump]] into the hidden area. The Warp Pipe sends the player to an underground area with a very fast-rolling rock hill. Beside the entrance pipe are two more pipes that spawn [[Goomba]]s onto the platform. The exit pipe is located to the left of the entrance pipe.

Back on the surface, the next rolling hill also has a Goomba-spawning pipe above it. Beyond the pipe is a Brick Block that yields a [[P Switch]] when hit. The P Switch exposes small [[Blue Coin]]s. At the lower end of this same rolling hill, there is another Brick Block. This block has a [[1-Up Mushroom]] inside. After the next platform with a Koopa Troopa, [[Mario]] must jump over three larger rolling hills to reach the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''' ''(pictured)'': The first [[Star Coin]] is floating high over the third rolling hill. To get it, Mario and co. must jump off one of the nearby Koopa Troopas and Paratroopas or use a [[Propeller Mushroom]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is floating over a small platform in midair next to four Paratroopas. It can be easily jumped to.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Directly below the midway point is a hidden area with a green Warp Pipe. This pipe leads to an underground area with a huge rolling hill and two green pipes from which Goombas emerge. Mario and co. must use these Goombas to jump up to the platform on the right to access the Star Coin.
