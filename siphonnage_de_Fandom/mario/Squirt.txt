|textcolor = 3399ff}}'''Squirt''' is a move used by [[Sushie]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Sushie starts out with this move, which she already learned, and it requires 3 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

==Usage==
To use Squirt, the player must hold the {{button|n64|a}} button and time the holding do deal 1 point of damage up to 5 points of damage. This attack is very useful against fire enemies and, more specifically, against the [[Lava Piranha]] during the second phase.
