The '''Magical Bean''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Magical Bean can only be obtained after defeating all of the [[Monty Mole]]s in [[Petunia]]'s garden. Afterward, [[Mario]] can use the Magical Bean along with the [[Fertile Soil]] and the [[Miracle Water]] to grow a giant [[beanstalk]] in [[Flower Fields]] and access [[Cloudy Climb]] to defeat [[Huff N. Puff]] (only after the [[Puff Puff Machine]] is destroyed and the [[Sun (character)|Sun]] recovers his strength).

==Trivia==
*The Magical Bean is a parody of the Magic Beans from the fairy tale ''{{w|Jack and the Beanstalk}}''.
