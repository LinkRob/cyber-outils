'''Pulsar''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Pulsars are light green, crystal-like enemies mainly encountered on [[Star Hill]], although they also appear in [[The Trial Course]]. Pulsar are a stronger variant of [[Cluster]]s. 

==Battle==
In battle, Pulsars are capable of using powerful electric spells such as [[Static E!]] and [[Bolt]]. However, Pulsars are rather infamous for using a kamikaze attack known as [[Migraine]] which can instantly knock out one of [[Mario]]'s party if not guarded against. Pulsars will always use this attack when they are defeated.

===Strategy===
Pulsars are weak to fire attacks meaning any of Mario's fire techniques are effective against them. However, one should be careful when dealing with a Pulsar as they will use Migraine in attempt to take a party member down with it. A Pulsar has a chance of dropping a Pick Me Up upon being defeated.
