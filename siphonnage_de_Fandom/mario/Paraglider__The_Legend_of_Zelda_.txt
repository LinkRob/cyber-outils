The '''Paraglider''' is an item from ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild]]''. It was added in the version 1.6.0 update of ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' alongside its associated [[kart]], the [[Master Cycle Zero]], and its wheels, the [[Ancient Tires]].
