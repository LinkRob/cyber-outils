'''Flower Saver P''' is a [[badge]] found in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
Flower Saver P is a variation of [[Flower Saver]] that can be used by [[Mario]]'s partners, hence the "P" in the name. Wearing it allows Mario's partners to use attacks with one less point of [[Flower Point|FP]].

Mario can find the Flower Saver P in [[Rogueport Sewers]] by way of [[Dazzle]], who sells it for ten [[Star Piece]]s. Flower Saver P is also an occasional item dropped by [[Crazee Dayzee]]s which are found on [[Twilight Trail]]. If [[Ms. Mowz]] is the party, Mario can have her use [[Kiss Thief]] to obtain the Flower Saver P from a Crazee Dayzee.
