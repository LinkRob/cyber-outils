'''Torny''' is an enemy in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Tornies are small, black tornado creatures with a pair of eyes.

===Behavior===
Tornies are found spawning from the sky, and they take shape upon hitting the ground. If Wario tries to [[jump]] on a Torny before it has fully formed, he is briefly lifted into the air.

==In ''Virtual Boy Wario Land''==
Tornies are only encountered in the [[level]] [[Stage 7 (Virtual Boy Wario Land)|Stage 7]]. [[Wario]] can ride on a Thorny, allowing him to safely travel across several [[Plant Chomper]]s without taking damage. A Thorny is also used to defeat the [[Big Blob]], a mini-boss encountered later into the level.
