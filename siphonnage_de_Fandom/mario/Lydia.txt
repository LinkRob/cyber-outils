'''Lydia''' is a [[portrait ghost]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'' and the [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|remake]]. She is [[Neville]]'s wife and the mother of [[Chauncey]] and the twins [[Henry and Orville]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Lydia has a slender body with light blue skin. Her head is balloon-shaped, and she has blonde hair, yellow-green eyes, pink eye shadow, pink lipstick, and green earrings. She is dressed in a pink bathrobe.

===Personality===
She is rather vain and is very proud of her well-groomed blonde hair. To keep it in that condition, she spends a large portion of her time primping her hair in front of a [[mirror]]. She gets upset very easily when something threatens the condition of her hair.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Lydia is the second portrait ghost encountered by [[Luigi]] in the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|location]]. Lydia is found in the room [[Master Bedroom (Luigi's Mansion)|Master Bedroom]] in area 1, which becomes accessible to Luigi after he has caught Neville into his [[Poltergust 3000]]. She is sitting in front of her dresser, combing her hair. She is so immersed in her hair that she is completely unresponsive to Luigi's [[flashlight]]. To make her vulnerable, Luigi has to open the curtains to her left with his Poltergust 3000. This causes a breeze to enter the bedroom through a hole in the window. Lydia, distracted by the breeze, then gets up to close the curtains again, at which point she can be stunned by the [[flashlight]] and re-captured. She has 100 [[HP]]. If Luigi fails to capture Lydia the first time, Lydia will disappear, reappear at the back side of the bedroom and casually close the curtains, and return to her mirror, forcing Luigi to repeat the process. After catching her with the Poltergust 3000, Luigi obtains the [[key]] leading to the [[Nursery]].

Lydia also has a book named [[Lydia's Child-Care Diary]], which can be found in the [[Study (Luigi's Mansion)|Study]] in a book shelf behind the desk. Luigi can read the book to be provided with information on how to lure Henry and Orville out of hiding in [[The Twins' Room]].

Lydia's portrait number in the [[Gallery (Luigi's Mansion)|Gallery]] is 2.

In the E3 2001 build, Lydia was fought differently; after looking behind her after a few seconds, she would become vulnerable, similar to Neville pausing his reading.
