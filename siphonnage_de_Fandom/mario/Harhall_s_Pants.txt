'''Harhall's Pants''' are a clothing item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can obtain Harhall's Pants at [[Harhall's Studio]] in exchange for helping him design clothes. Harhall's Pants can affect gravity during battle.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', Harhall's Pants can only be equipped by Mario. Doing so increases his [[HP]] by 60 (100 in the Japanese version) and his [[Defense]] by 10.

In the remake, Harhall's Pants can be equipped by either Mario or Luigi, and instead only increase Defense, this time by 62. Although they can only be obtained once, Harhall's Pants can be sold for only three [[coin]]s.
