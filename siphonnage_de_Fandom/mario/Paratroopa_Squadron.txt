The '''Paratroopa Squadron''' (also called the '''Shell Shock Paratroopas''') are a trio of [[Paratroopa]]s that appear to be agents. They are seen patrolling the area around the [[Yoshi Sphinx]], the group is made up of three Paratroopas named '''Shell Shock 1''', '''Shell Shock 2''', and '''Shell Shock 3'''. Whenever they talk, they always end their sentences with "over" like they're using walkie talkies, their dialogue also makes them sound more digitalized.

== In ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star'' ==
[[Image:Paratroopa Squadron Battle.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] battles the Paratroopa squadron.]]
The Paratroopa Squadron makes an appearance in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. In this game, they appear as a fightable mini boss in the [[Yoshi Sphinx]], as [[Mario]] approaches the [[Bowser Tape]] near the Sphinx's top to open the way to the [[Comet Piece]] (and various other items and collectibles such as a [[HP-Up Heart]], the [[Things|Lightbulb]] thing, several rare [[Stickers]], and a tablet piece), the Paratroopa Squadron arrives and plan an attack on [[Mario]] (whilst talking like they're using walkie talkies), once done, they launch themselves at [[Mario]]. In battle, the Paratroopas each have 14 [[HP]] (which is slightly higher than an average [[Paratroopa]] which has 10 [[HP]]). During the fight, [[Mario]] should use [[Line Jump]]s, and [[Shell]]s (once they're grounded from the jumps) to strike down the Paratroopas. The use of [[Things]] such as the [[Scissors]] also work, once the Paratroopa Squadron is beaten, they disappear allowing [[Mario]] to peel the [[Bowser Tape]] to progress deeper in the [[Yoshi Sphinx]].

==Trivia==
*The Paratroopa Squadrons' walkie talkie-like speech could be a reference to ''[[wikipedia:Star Trek|Star Trek]]''.
*If [[Mario]] flees the fight with the Paratroopa Squadron, they will mock him by calling him a "wuss" (whilst maintaining their walkie talkie way of speaking) and knock him back into the room from whence he came. When Mario returns, they reengage him.
*Before battling them, the Paratroopa Squadron can be seen patrolling the [[Yoshi Sphinx]] in [[Drybake Desert]], they can also be seen at the entrance to the [[Yoshi Sphinx]] as well, after their defeat however, they disappear from the area.
*When the Paratroopa Squadron first sights [[Mario]] in the American version, they immediately, clearly say "...Target Acquired!..." however in the PAL (European and Australian) version of the game, they sighting sounds more distorted with them saying "...bzzt...target ac...ed...bzzt..." to better fit their walkie talkie way of talking. 
*The group is referenced again in [[Shy Guy Jungle]] where amidst the trash, there is a paper apparently written by Shell Shock 1 (to Shell Shock 2) stating that Shell Shock 3 is feeling down, they also use their "over" terms in the letter.
