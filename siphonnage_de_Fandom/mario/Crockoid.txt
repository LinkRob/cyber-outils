The '''Crockoid''' is an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''.

== Description ==
Crockoids are enemies encased in various materials with crocodilian carvings. The shells they wear vary in material, and when exposed, the Crockoids appear as spindly golden crocodile humanoids. Crockoids tend to wield clubs as weapons.

== Behaviour ==
Crockoids attack [[Donkey Kong (character)|DK]] by swinging their clubs and must be defeated by destroying their shell and then attacking the enemy itself underneath. Some of them wear concrete or rubbery shells, which must be destroyed with an explosive or sturdy terrain as Donkey Kong cannot break concrete or rubber with his normal attacks.
