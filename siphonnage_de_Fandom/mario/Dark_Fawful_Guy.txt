'''Dark Fawful Guys''' are an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Dark Fawful Guys are a dark purple variant of [[Fawful Guy]]s that wear a blue Fawful mask.

===Behavior===
Dark Fawful Guys can carry and drop [[Dark Fawful Bomb]]s as a means of attack.

==Battle==
To attack a Dark Fawful Guy, Bowser can [[inhale]] it. If Bowser does not, the Dark Fawful Guy drops a Dark Fawful Bomb onto him. The Dark Fawful Guys have the same attacks as [[Fly Guy]]s, except they are a lot stronger and move quicker.

Defeating a Dark Fawful Guy causes it to drop a [[Max Nut]] or a [[Lucky Charm]].
