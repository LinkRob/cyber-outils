The '''Power Flower''' is one of [[Princess Daisy]]'s standard [[kart]]s in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. Her other karts include the [[Standard DS]], and her unlockable kart, the [[Light Dancer]]. Once Princess Daisy is unlocked, only she can use it. However, [[Princess Peach]] and [[Donkey Kong]] can use the Power Flower once all cups in the 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix have been completed. All racers can use the Power Flower once all cups in the Mirror Class have been completed.

== Description ==
The Power Flower resembles a [[Wikipedia:Triumph TR3A|Triumph TR3A]], colored two-tone orange, to suit Princess Daisy's color scheme.

== In ''Mario Kart DS'' ==
It has average speed and acceleration, high drift and items, but [[Weight Class|medium weight]] and low handling.

The Power Flower shares its speed with [[Mario]]'s [[B Dasher]] and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Egg 1]], its acceleration with [[Waluigi]]'s [[Gold Mantis]] and [[Standard WL]], its and its weight with Yoshi's [[Cucumber]] kart and [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s [[4-Wheel Cradle]].

Princess Daisy in the Power Flower kart is used for the staff ghost for [[Sky Garden|<small>GBA</small> Sky Garden]] with a time of 1:44:400.

== Spiritual successors ==
The [[Bloom Coach]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' can be considered to be the Power Flower's predecessor. The Power Flower's successors can be considered to be the [[Daytripper]] from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', the [[Birthday Girl]] from ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' and the [[Cat Cruiser]] from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', due to their feminine designs and some of these karts can be considered to be based for Daisy, but also for other female racers such as Cat Peach.
