|game = ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''|image = [[File:Faster_Fort_Fire_Bros..png|250px]]}}'''Faster Fort Fire Bros.''' is a level of World Flower in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
The level is a [[Fire Bro.|Fire Bros.]] fort, that has Fire Bros., [[Goomba Tower]]s. It also features the blue [[lava]] on the ground.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': The first Green Star is atop a [[Goomba Tower]] made of three [[Goomba]]s at the start of the level.
*'''Green Star 2''': After exiting the first [[Warp Box]], the player needs to climb onto the first [[Thwomp]]. Once it rises, the player must jump off onto the platform and run to the left to collect the second Green Star. Alternatively, the player can climb up a series of invisible long [[? Block]]s to the left of the Thwomp.
*'''Green Star 3''': There is a light coming from a small circle in the ground after coming out of the [[Clear Pipe]]s that dip into the lava. The player needs to [[Ground Pound]] the spot to make the third Green Star appear.
