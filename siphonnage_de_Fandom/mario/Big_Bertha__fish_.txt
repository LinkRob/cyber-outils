The '''Big Bertha''' is an enemy that is first seen in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It is a female version of the [[Boss Bass]] and a large [[Cheep-Cheep]] that is seen in the deep seas. All Big Berthas seem to be mother fish, as she always carries [[Baby Cheep]]s in her mouth to protect them from any predators, occasionally letting them out. Her male counterpart, [[Boss Bass]], is an aggressive hunter that swims on the water's surface, never seen below in the deep water. While nowhere near as aggressive as a Boss Bass, Big Berthas are still carnivorous and can swallow [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] whole.

==History==
===''Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[File:Big Bertha NES.png|thumb|left]]
The passive Big Bertha is found swimming back and forth underwater in [[World 3-5 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 3-5]], [[World 4-1 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 4-1]], [[World 6-9 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 6-9]], and [[World 7-4 (Super Mario Bros. 3)|World 7-4]]. Here, she carries [[Baby Cheep]]s in their mouth, and occasionally letting them out. After a few seconds, the parent gets its baby back in its mouth, though occasionally, the Big Bertha may not retrieve its baby. The Big Bertha can be defeated with the use of the [[Fire Flower]], [[Starman]], [[Hammer Suit]], [[Super Leaf]], or [[Tanooki Suit]]; in doing so, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] earn 100 [[point]]s.

Unlike the [[Boss Bass]], the Big Berthas remain underwater. Big Berthas aren't as aggressive as the Boss Bass however they may try to swallow Mario or Luigi if he gets too close, resulting in an instant death, although the Big Bertha will ignore the plumbers entirely if they're wearing a [[Frog Suit]]. 
{{br}}
===''Super Mario 64 DS''===
[[Image:Big Bertha DS.png|thumb|right|A Big Bertha as seen in ''Super Mario 64 DS''.]]
The Big Bertha returns in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' replacing the [[Bubba]] from the [[Super Mario 64|original]]. Big Berthas reside on the huge side of [[Tiny-Huge Island]] and behave the same as a Bubba. They will constantly make attempts to eat you however, the Big Bertha can be defeated with the use of the [[Mega Mushroom]], unlike ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', Big Berthas are not seen with [[Baby Cheep]]s this time. 
{{br}}
===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
A Big Bertha named [[Bessie Bass]] appears in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' as a mini boss, she was originally an ordinary Big Bertha living below the waters of [[Bessie Bass's Battleship|her battleship]] before being enlarged by [[Kamek]]'s magic. Upon being enlarged, Bessie Bass gained the abilities of a [[Boss Bass]] as well in addition to swimming below the water like a regular Big Bertha.
{{br}}
==Trivia==
*The Boss Bass is one of the first two carnivorous [[Cheep-Cheep]] varieties to be introduced in the series, the other being the [[Boss Bass]].
*Unlike most carnavirous [[Cheep-Cheep]]s, Big Berthas only attempt to swallow [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] if he is close otherwise, they don't relentlessly pursue them, in addition, they can be faked out by wearing a [[Frog Suit]] (something that can't be done with most carnivorous fish).
