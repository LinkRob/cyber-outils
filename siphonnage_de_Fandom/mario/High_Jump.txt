The '''High Jump''' is one of the first two [[Bros. Attack]]s learned in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', the other being the [[Spin Jump]]. It was taught by the [[Starshade Bros.]] in the [[Stardust Fields]].

It can be performed with [[Mario]] in the front and [[Luigi]] in the back. Luigi will jump on Mario's head and start bouncing. If {{button|gba|B}} is pressed, Mario will hold onto Luigi and jump high. If {{button|gba|A}} is pressed, Luigi will jump back behind Mario, cancelling the move.

If the [[Mini Mario]] ability is used, then Luigi attempts to use the High Jump, he will act as though the A button was pressed normally. However, if Mini Mario jumps while Luigi is above, he will hit Luigi like a [[Coin Block]]. This will create one [[coin]] per hit, but at the cost of a single drop of [[Heart Point|HP]] of Luigi's health per hit. If Luigi is at a single Health Point, there will be no effect, and Luigi will yell in pain.
