'''Pompon Flowers''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. They are moving plants that are only encountered in [[World 4-2 (Super Mario Land)|World 4-2]] of the [[Chai Kingdom]].

==History==
Pompon Flowers are often found moving around on the ground slowly. When [[Mario]] is in the area, the Pompon Flowers will attempt to shoot poisonous spores up at him. Mario can defeat a Pompon Flower by either [[jump]]ing on it or by shooting [[Superball]]s at it. Defeating a Pompon Flower earns the player 800 [[point]]s.
