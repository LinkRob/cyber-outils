'''Cheep Cheep Lagoon''' is a golf course in ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]''.

==Description==
Unlike any other golf course in the [[Mario Golf (series)|''Mario Golf'' series]], Cheep Cheep Lagoon takes place underwater. As such, various sea creatures can be seen and water hazards are absent. The main feature of this course is the effect being underwater has on the ball, permanently putting it through rain conditions, but putting is even more difficult than putting on a raining condition. There are also pipes that partially send the ball in the direction of the pipe's current as well as coral and [[Urchin]]s that serve as obstacles. Its music is a cover of the [[Underwater BGM (Super Mario Bros.)|Underwater BGM]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' Despite being called Cheep Cheep Lagoon, [[Cheep Cheep]]s are only present on the second and ninth holes.

==In ''Mario Golf: World Tour''==
Cheep Cheep Lagoon has to first be unlocked by collecting 21 [[Star Coin]]s. If the player collects every Star Coin for this course's challenges, they are awarded with a [[Waluigi]] costume, and the player can complete a Character Costume Challenge in Cheep Cheep Lagoon to unlock costumes of [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]], [[Petey Piranha]], and a [[Piranha Plant]].
