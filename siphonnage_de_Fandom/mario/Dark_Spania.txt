}}A '''Dark Spania''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Spanias are found in a few rooms of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Dark Spanias are stronger versions of [[Spania]]s and also attack with a charging spin. Due to the spikes on their heads, it is recommended to take out Dark Spanias with any non-jumping attacks such as [[Bowser]]'s fire breath or a [[Pixl]] such as [[Boomer]] or [[Cudge]].
