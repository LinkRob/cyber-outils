'''Bowser's Castle''' is the third track in the [[Special Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.

== Layout ==
Just like every other Bowser's Castle, this one also has [[Thwomp]]s and [[lava]]. This castle debuted the signature [[Mecha-Bowser]], which shoots out [[fireball]]s. The racers start outside the castle and drive into a 2-way split, with Thwomps on either side. After coming out of the split, they turn into an area filled with lava and fireballs firing out. The track splits and joins back at the launch ramp, while in the middle there is a pit of lava. There is a room with large fireballs spinning counterclockwise in a circular room. Hitting these will cause one to spin out. Towards the end of the room, there are [[Boost Pad|boosts]] to take the racers back outside the castle over some more lava back to the starting line.
