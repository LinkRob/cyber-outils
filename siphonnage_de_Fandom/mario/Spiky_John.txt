|race = [[Koopatrol]]}}'''Spiky John''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario (game)|Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Spiky John is a [[Koopatrol]] that guards [[Princess Peach's Castle]] during the events of the game. He has two other friends: [[Mr. Hammer]], a [[Hammer Bro]] that hosts a quiz show, and [[Spiky Tom]], another Koopatrol. He and Spiky John compete against [[Princess Peach]] in Mr. Hammer's quiz show. However, Spiky John rarely answers questions compared to Spiky Tom allowing Princess Peach to answer them first.
