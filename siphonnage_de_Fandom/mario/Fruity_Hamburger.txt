The '''Fruity Hamburger''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing a [[Power Steak]] and a [[Keel Mango]]. It is said to be a fruit-filled [[hamburger]], but it looks plainly like a hamburger patty with a mango behind it and three berries around it. When used, the Fruity Hamburger restores 25 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
