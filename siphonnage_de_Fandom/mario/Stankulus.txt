'''Stankulus''' is an enemy from ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. He is a statue of a naked man with yellow hair and the rest of his body bronze. 

==History==
Stankulus can be found underneath a staircase in the lobby of the [[Smithsnorian Museum]]. He attacks by shooting electric balls from his armpit hair. Stankulus can be taken out by any attack from [[Wario]].

==Trivia==
*Stankulus' name is a pun of "Spartacus," a famous gladiator.
