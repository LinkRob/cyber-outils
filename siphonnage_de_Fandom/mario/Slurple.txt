'''Slurples''' (also called '''Swoopin' Poinks''') are health-sucking space leeches that appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' as common enemies. They attack by attaching themselves to [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) and slowly sucking at their health. They can easily be defeated with a shake of the Wii Remote. Defeating them will release 1 [[Star Bit]]. They appear to be related to [[Poink]]s and [[Swoopin' Stu]]s.


==Trivia==
*In the beta showings of ''Super Mario Galaxy'', Swoopin' Poinks were originally Purple.
