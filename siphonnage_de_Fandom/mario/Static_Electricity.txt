}}'''Static Electricity''' (formerly known as '''Static E!''') is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and the [[Super Mario RPG|remake]]. It can be used by many enemies and bosses in the game.

== Effects ==
When Static Electricity is used, a flash of lightning appears on the screen damaging [[Mario]]'s entire party with moderate damage. Static E! is one of the most common special moves seen in the game. This is also [[Bowyer]]'s signature move being the first known user of this move.

== Trivia ==
*Static Electricity has been toned down on the Virtual Console version of this game to avoid the risk of seizures.
*E in Static E! stands for electricity. In the [[Super Mario RPG|remake]], the full name is now spelled out.
