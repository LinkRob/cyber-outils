{{Nihongo|'''Vivian'''|ビビアン|Bibian}} is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. She was once a member of the villainous [[The Three Shadows|Shadow Sirens]], which consists of herself and her older sisters, [[Beldam]] and [[Marilyn]]. She eventually abandoned them and became [[Mario]]'s fifth partner.

==Profile==
=== Abilities ===
Vivian's abilities include conjuring and wielding powerful fire attacks. She can infatuate her enemies to leave them [[Confused]]. In one of [[Goombella]]'s tattles, she says that Vivian may be cuter than her. Outside of battle, Vivian can pull Mario into the shadows where he cannot be seen by enemies; this ability comes especially handy in [[Twilight Town]], where Mario and Vivian must eavesdrop on [[crow]]s in the trees to learn the whereabouts of [[Doopliss]]. 

==Biography==
At an unknown point in the past, the Shadow Sirens came to the [[Mushroom World]] in order to free their mistress, the [[Shadow Queen]], and eventually met the [[Secret Society of X-Nauts]], who used the three for their own goals.

Vivian and her sisters served [[Sir Grodus]] in hopes of freeing the Shadow Queen and came into conflict with Mario on several occasions in the search for the [[Crystal Stars]]. Throughout the course of the story, Vivian suffered through an inferiority complex, due to the fact that she was constantly mistreated by Beldam. She joined Mario when he helped her find the [[Superbombomb]] her sister accused her of losing. She decides to repay Mario by helping him defeat [[Doopliss]] and regain his body and name. When she loses in a battle over [[Madame Flurrie|Flurrie's]] necklace, she decides to officially and permanently join Mario's team, effectively abandoning her allegiance to her sisters, the Shadow Queen and the X-Nauts. Eventually she gains confidence and learns to respect herself and becomes close friends with Mario and his team.

===Gender===
Similar to [[Birdo]], there has been a lot of controversy concerning Vivian's gender. In the [[Japan|Japanese]] version of ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', Vivian was portrayed as a [[:Category:Transgender characters|transgender]] woman. This is supported by the fact that Beldam referred to her as a "man" (which was changed into her being called "plug-ugly" in the [[United States of America|US]] release), much to Vivian's disdain. In addition, [[Goombella]]'s Tattle information on her, as well as her description in the game menu, referred to her as male. Despite this, Vivian refers to herself and her sisters as "three sisters", and uses feminine pronouns for herself. All masculine descriptions of Vivian were removed in the North American and German releases of the game, although Vivian's portrayal as a trans-woman is retained in all other versions.

However, in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake for ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'']] she was officially confirmed to be transgender, as she says this line during her first conversation with Mario while Doopliss has stolen his identity: "Truth is, it took me a while to realize I was their sister...not their brother. Now their usual bullying feels heavier."

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
When Mario encounters the Shadow Sirens in Twilight Town, they are arguing over who was supposed to bring the [[Superbombomb]]. Beldam blames Vivian, and she leaves with Marilyn to take a nap while Vivian has to search for the missing explosive device in the grass. Mario offers to help, and Vivian offers to join him as a thanks.

Vivian accompanies Mario as he searches through [[Creepy Steeple]] to find whoever stole his identity. When Mario finds that it is [[Doopliss]] and decides to face him in battle, Vivian initially refuses. Eventually, Vivian comes around to admit that Mario has been nicer to her than anyone else she has met, so she joins the battle at Mario's side, becoming a full companion of his henceforth.

By the ending, Vivian bids farewell to Mario, and it appears that she and her sisters have learned to get along.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Although Vivian does not physically appear, she does make a cameo in the form of a [[Catch Card]].

==Other Appearances==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
Vivian appears as a primary spirit in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate. When equipped, she lowers the weight of the player. She can be combined with Gengar to obtain the [[Shadow Queen]] spirit.
