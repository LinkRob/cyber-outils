The '''Grand Pianta Statue''' is a landmark which first appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It has made various background appearances in spinoff games.

==Description==
The Grand Pianta Statue is a monument consisting of an unidentified [[Pianta]] standing on two blocks atop a pedestal. It is similar in appearance to the large Pianta statue found near the [[Pianta Hot Spring|hot spring]] in [[Pianta Village]].

==History==
==={{series|Super Mario}}===
====''Super Mario Sunshine''====
In ''Super Mario Sunshine'', the Grand Pianta Statue is located directly in the middle of [[Bianco Square]] in [[Delfino Plaza]]. When [[Mario]] first arrives at Delfino Plaza, the statue is buried under a brown [[Generator]]. After Mario and [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]] defeat the [[Polluted Piranha Plant]], all the [[goop]] disappears, making the Grand Pianta Statue appear. However, when it appears, [[Shadow Mario]] is on top of it. Shadow Mario jumps off the statue, runs up to [[Princess Peach]], and kidnaps her. After Mario chases Shadow Mario and gets Peach back, Shadow Mario paints a [[Rainbow M]] on the statue, which leads to [[Bianco Hills]]; this Rainbow M stays on the Grand Pianta Statue for the remainder of the game.

The statue is also seen in the end credits; in the last photo, Mario and Peach are posing in front of the statue.

====''Super Mario Galaxy 2''====
The Grand Pianta Statue also appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' in the [[Starshine Beach Galaxy]], a [[galaxy]] based on elements from ''Super Mario Sunshine''. This statue does not look exactly like the one in Delfino Plaza, though. For example, this statue does not have a cloak.

==={{series|Mario Kart}}===
The Grand Pianta Statue appears in the background of two courses: [[Peach Beach]] and [[Delfino Square]], first appearing in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' respectively.

In the North American [[Mario Kart DS/Prototype|kiosk demo]] of ''Mario Kart DS'', the statue was instead of a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] fountain with water emerging from its mouth.

===''Mario Power Tennis''===
In ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', the statue is present in the [[Delfino Plaza Court]], which takes place in Bianco Square.
