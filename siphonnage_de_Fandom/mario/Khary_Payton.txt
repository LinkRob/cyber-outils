'''Khary Payton''' is an American actor who voiced the [[Penguin King]] in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. Outside of ''Mario'', he is best known for playing [[w:c:walkingdead:Ezekiel Sutton (TV Series)|King Ezekiel]] in ''[[w:c:walkingdead:The Walking Dead (TV Series)|The Walking Dead]],'' [[w:c:dc:Victor Stone (Teen Titans TV Series)|Cyborg]] in ''[[w:c:dc:Teen Titans (TV Series)|Teen Titans]]'' and Rafiki in ''The Lion Guard.''
