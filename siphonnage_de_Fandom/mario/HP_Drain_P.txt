'''HP Drain P''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is the partner-based variation of [[HP Drain]].

==Effects==
This badge can be equipped by using one [[Badge Point|BP]]. While equipped, [[Mario]]'s active partner restores a single point of [[Heart Point|HP]] each turn, although it also lowers their [[Attack]] power by one.

Near the end of Chapter 6, Mario goes to [[Poshley Heights]], where he can find the HP Drain P inside of [[Goldbob]]'s house. Here, the HP Drain P is found in a [[treasure chest]] within a secret area accessed through a thin hole in the wall.
