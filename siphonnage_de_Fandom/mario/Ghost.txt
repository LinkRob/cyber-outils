*[[Ghost (Mario Kart series)|Ghost]]s, recordings of the player's performance in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]].

*[[Ghost (Wario Land II enemy)|Ghost]], an enemy in ''Wario Land II''.
*[[Ghost (Wario Land 4)|Ghost]], an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.
