'''Excitebike Arena''' is a course in the [[Egg Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. The track has many jump ramps and a jump panel near the end. This course is also believed to change every time it is played. Bikes work better on ramps than karts. Hence the name, the course is based on the classic NES game ''[[w:c:nintendo:Excitebike|Excitebike]]''.
