'''World 8-1''' is the first level of [[World 8 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 8]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''. The player is given a time limit of 300 seconds. It is the longest level in the game



== Layout ==
This is an overworld course in which the player encounters a multitude of enemies such as [[Little Goomba]]s, [[Buzzy Beetle]]s, and [[Koopa Troopa]]s, as well as a bunch of [[pipe]]s that contain [[Piranha Plant]]s. This level also contains a series of large and small pits, thus adding an additional challenge to jumping, as it causes some platforms to appear very narrow.

Quite a bit of [[coin]]s are seen placed in the sky throughout. There is one secret area, which can be entered through a [[Warp Pipe]] not far from the starting position. Here, the player can collect some coins, plus strike a Ten-Coin Block.

== Challenges in ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'' ==
In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', there are five [[Red Coin]]s and a hidden [[Yoshi Egg]] to collect. The player will also obtain a medal based on their score. The target score for this level is 40000.

=== Red Coin locations ===
* Red Coin #1 is located near the beginning, over a pit.
* Red Coin #2 is located inside a new [[Coin Block]] in the secret underground area. Within the row of [[Brick Block]]s, it is placed at the block farthest to the left.
* Red Coin #3 is located inside the Coin Block placed above the Koopa Paratroopa.
* Red Coin #4 is located within the row of Brick Blocks that contains the [[Starman]]. It is inside the block farthest to the right.
* Red Coin #5 is located between the two stone platforms with a Koopa Troopa. It is just before the narrow platform within the large pit.

=== Yoshi Egg location ===
* The Yoshi Egg is found inside a Hidden Block over the narrow platform within the large pit.
