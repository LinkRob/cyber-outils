'''Big Beanie''' is a large Chilli Pepper-like [[Beanie (Yoshi's New Island)|Beanie]] residing on [[Egg Island]] in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', he started out as a normal-sized Beanie before being enlarged into a giant [[Beanstalk]]-like creature by [[Kamek]]. He is the boss of [[World 1 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 1]] and the game's first major boss. Big Beanie is fought in the Clouds high above [[Big Beanie's Castle|his castle]]. Defeating Big Beanie will unlock [[World 2 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 2]].

==History==
After [[Blue Yoshi]] and [[Baby Mario]] survive [[Big Beanie's Castle]] and reach the large room, a small Beanie will descend from the ceiling and [[Kamek]] will appear and taunt Yoshi before using his [[Magic Hammer]] to make the Beanie grow into a massive [[Beanstalk]]. Yoshi can climb the Beanstalk out of the Castle and into the clouds where he finds the Beanie at the top has grown into Big Beanie.

===Battle===
Big Beanie starts the battle by dangling from his Beanstalk, Yoshi can hit the Big Beanie in the head with an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]], Big Beanie will then get angry and a prickly vine will temporarily shield him from any more attacks, during this time, prickly vines will emerge from the cloudy ground periodically to attack Yoshi, these vines can be annoying to avoid but keep an eye on the ground below to gauge where they will emerge, Big Beanie will then replace the prickly vines with a normal vine, during this time, Big Beanie will spit [[Beanie (Yoshi's New Island)|Beanies]] at Yoshi (these are more of a help than a hindrance as the Beanies work as an egg supply if Yoshi is running low on eggs), hit the vine with an egg, this will leave Big Beanie exposed, hit him again with an egg, following the second hit, he will shield himself again with a thorny vine and several more will periodically emerge from the cloudy floor below Yoshi which he must avoid (or he may lose [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]] thereby making it tricky to get 100 Points). When the attack is done, Big Beanie will bring back the regular vine and resume spitting out his [[Beanie (Yoshi's New Island)|Beanie]] underlinings, hit the vine with an egg again to leave Big Beanie exposed then hit Big Beanie once more to finish him off and complete the World.

==Trivia==
*Big Beanie marks a departure from the previous ''[[Yoshi's Island (series)|Yoshi's Island]]'' Castle bosses as previous games had some sort of blob-like creature as the first major boss, [[Salvo the Slime]] for ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|it’s remake]] and [[Gilbert the Gooey]] for ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', Big Beanie is also the only one if the three to not have a common name.
