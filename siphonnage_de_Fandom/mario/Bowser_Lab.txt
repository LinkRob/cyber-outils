'''Bowser Lab''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Bowser Lab is located in the [[Jungle Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. Its tiles and the glass in the background are tinted teal, while the laboratory equipment is colored purple. A few flasks with colorful liquids are scattered around, and one piece of equipment contains a large tube with green liquid constantly bubbling through it.

==History==
Bowser Lab can only be accessed through the [[Pipe House (Mario Party Advance)|Pipe House]]'s purple pipe. [[Bowser]] and [[Koopa Kid]] create their experiments here, which include [[Naval Piranha]] and [[Huffin Puffin]], the former of whom has to be fought during the [[Bowserstein!]] quest, which pits the player against Bowser's creation at the laboratory to win a Duel [[minigame]], [[Koopa Kurl]].
