==Appearance==
Mini T. is a young [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with olive-colored spots on his head.

==History==
Mini T. lives in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] where he can be found near the train station beside his best friend, [[Dane T.]] Mini T. only plays a role in the chain [[letter]] delivery sidequest. [[Mario]] and [[Parakarry]] must take his letters to the red [[Yoshi Kid]] of [[Lavalava Island]] and his uncle, [[Frost T.]], of [[Starborn Valley]].
