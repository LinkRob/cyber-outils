'''Pie Hard''' is a 2-vs-2 minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
A player throws a cream pie to its opponent that startles the team's opponents. All players grab a cream pie before the game starts. The [[Toad (species)| Toads]] place pies on the empty spots of the table.

== Gameplay ==
The players must throw cream pies at their opponents to gain points. There is a line where players cannot crossover. Players can grab one or two cream pies. The team gains one point when the pie splatters their opponent. Pies may leave a mess on the floor during the minigame.

== Ending ==
The pies disappear from the players' hands. The winning team position themselves and does their victory animations. The losing team stays on their spots and does their losing animation, however, you can't really see since the camera will zoom in to show the winning team.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} – Throw

==Etymology==
The name of this minigame is a pun on the 1988 film [[Wikipedia: Die Hard|Die Hard]].
