The '''Storage Room''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The rightmost area of the Storage Room has a stack of red chairs and several wooden crates. A bucket and mop is nearby, and a [[mirror]] is placed between them on the wall. A red button can be seen in the reflection of the mirror, and pressing it causes the left wall to slide back a distance, revealing another part of the Storage Room. Before the wall is moved, the pole of the broom leans against it, but after the wall moves, the broom lies on the ground.

The other portion of the Storage Room, revealed after the wall moves, has a closed hatched, and on the wall behind it is a red button covered by a poster.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
[[File:Storage Room LM Freed Boos.png|thumb|left|200px|The Boos are freed from the Storage Room in ''Luigi's Mansion'']]
The door to the Storage Room is on the right end of the [[Ball Room]], but [[Luigi]] has to first obtain the [[key]] to unlock it by defeating the [[Ghost Guy]]s and [[The Floating Whirlindas]] in the Ball Room.

In the Storage Room, Luigi has to vacuum up a [[Purple Puncher]] and two red [[Grabbing Ghost]]s. He can optionally also reveal a [[Speedy Spirit]] by examining a chair placed against the wall on the right side. Once the lights turn on, Luigi can press the button shown in the mirror for the left wall to move back. The button only activates when the light turns on, and pressing it again does nothing. At the left side, Luigi has to vacuum a smaller poster into the [[Poltergust 3000]] and press the red button behind it. By doing this, a group of fifty [[Boo]]s and [[King Boo]] are all released from the hatch and escape to different rooms of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]]. Following this, Luigi gets a call on his [[Game Boy Horror]] from [[Professor E. Gadd|E. Gadd]], who asks him to briefly return to the [[Professor E. Gadd's Lab|Lab]], mainly to talk about the [[Boo Radar]] function that can detect the location of any Boo present in the same room as Luigi.

During the [[blackout]], the Storage Room has two Purple Punchers and two [[Gold Ghost]]s. Luigi is also given another chance to capture the Speedy Spirit if he had not done so prior.

In the PAL [[Hidden Mansion]], there are three Gold Ghosts, four [[Garbage Can Ghost]]s, and two [[Blue Twirler]]s.
