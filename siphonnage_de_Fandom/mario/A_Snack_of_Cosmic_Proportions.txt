'''A Snack of Cosmic Proportions''' is the second mission of [[Good Egg Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Objective==
The objective is to feed 100 [[Star Bit]]s to a [[Hungry Luma]] on the [[Yoshi Egg]] planet so that it transforms into a tower-shaped planet, after which is the final planet where the [[Power Star]] can be collected. Star Bits appear all over the [[galaxy]], and [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can travel around the planets as many times as desired so that he can get enough Star Bits to feed the Hungry Luma.

In the final area, Mario and Luigi have to collect five blue [[Star Chip]]s to reveal five [[Pull Star]]s. In the center of the Pull Stars is the Power Star.
