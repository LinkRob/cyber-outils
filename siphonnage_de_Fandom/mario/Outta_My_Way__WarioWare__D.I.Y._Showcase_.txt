'''Outta My Way''' is one of [[Wario-Man]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''.

==Layout==
The microgame is essentially a successor to ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''{{'}}s [[Hectic Highway]] as both involve [[Wario]] driving around vehicles on a straight highway consisting of three lanes.

==Gameplay==
For eight seconds, [[Wario]] has to use his [[Wario Bike]] to dodge all the [[car]]s on the highway. Using the [[Wii Remote]], the player can move Wario left or right to a different lane by clicking on a large, yellow left arrow and right arrow respectively. If Wario avoids hitting the vehicles, the player wins at the microgame, but if Wario crashes into one, they lose.
