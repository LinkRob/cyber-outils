'''Spiked Question Blocks''', also spelled '''Spiked ? Blocks''', are a variation of [[Question Block]]s which appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
They have the appearance of red Question Blocks with three yellow spikes above the question mark symbol. Spiked Question Blocks repeatedly rotate 180 degrees. If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] hit or [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] any side without the spikes, the Spiked Question Block turns into an [[Empty Block]].

==In ''New Super Mario Bros.''==
Spiked Question Blocks only appear in one level, [[World 6-3 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6-3]]. There are only four Spiked Question Blocks, three of which contain a power-up item.
