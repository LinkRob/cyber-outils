'''World 3-1''' is the first level of [[World 3 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 3]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''.

== Layout ==
At the beginning of the level, there are some [[? Block]]s, with the farthest to the right containing a power-up. There are also some [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s leaping towards the player. Next up is a gap and some [[pipe]]s, the second one leading down into a secret underground area where the player can collect some [[coin]]s and another power-up. Upon exiting, the player will exit out of the fourth pipe.
[[File:SMB World 3-1 NES 2.png|thumb|left|150px|The Hammer Brothers section.]]
Following this gap is a bridge with some [[Little Goomba]]s and a [[Hidden Block]] above containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]]. Next, a [[Starman]] power-up lies within the block furthest to the left in the formation of [[Brick Block]]s. This power-up can come in handy when approaching the Hammer Brothers up ahead. Within the Brick Block formation the Hammer Brothers are on is a block (the second one) that contains another power-up. Right after, the player will come across a [[springboard]] that will get the player over the Brick Blocks up ahead. On top row of Brick Blocks, the right-most block holds a [[beanstalk]] that leads to another [[Coin Heaven]].

Further on are some Little Goombas walking down the set of stone stairs, followed by a group of blocks and enemies. The bottom-right ? Block contains another power-up. What lies ahead is a row of blocks, with a [[Coin Block]] found in the second block from the left. Finally, the player will approach a stone pillar and some Koopa Troopas walking down the stone stairs before the [[flagpole]].

== Challenges in ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'' ==
In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', there are five [[Red Coin]]s and a hidden [[Yoshi Egg]] to collect. The player will also obtain a medal based on their score. The target score for this level is 45000.

=== Red Coin locations ===
* Red Coin #1 is located in the middle of the first row of Brick Blocks.
* Red Coin #2 is located in the question block opposite the power-up in the secret underground area.
* Red Coin #3 is located in the ? Block left of the Hammer Brothers section.
* Red Coin #4 is located in the ? Block in the top left in the group of ? Blocks.
* Red Coin #5 is located right after the stone pillar.

=== Yoshi Egg location ===
* The [[Yoshi Egg]] is found inside a Brick Block beneath the beanstalk.
