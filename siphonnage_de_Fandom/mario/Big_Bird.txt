The '''Big Bird''' is the boss of the [[Tree Zone]] in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'' as well as the first boss overall. It is fought in its level [[Final Boss: The Big Bird]], set within a large nest at the top of Tree Zone. To defeat the Big Bird, [[Mario]] has to either [[jump]] on it three times or hit it with six [[fireball]]s.
