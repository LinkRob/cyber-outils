'''New York City''', is were [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] were born in the television shows and movies, specifically in [[Brooklyn]]. 

In the games, ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island|Yoshi's Island]]'' showed that Mario and Luigi were dropped off in [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]] as babies. ===''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]''===
''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' is set in [[Donkey Kong (video game)#Inspiration|1930s]] New York City.<ref name="Dream"/>

===''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]''===
''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]'' is set in New York City's underground sewer system.<ref name="MB"/><ref name="MBB"/><ref name=MBBB>[https://web.archive.org/web/20091022053804/https://www.popularmechanics.com/technology/industry/4334387.html Popular Mechanics: Exclusive Interview With Nintendo Gaming Mastermind Shigeru Miyamoto]</ref>

===''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''===
[[New Donk City]] from ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' is very similar to New York City and is [[New_Donk_City#History|implied]] to have been [[w:c:Tropedia:Merged Reality|built upon]] it.

===''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
