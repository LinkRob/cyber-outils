The '''Red Power Star''' is one of the [[Power Star]] variations featured in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. Aside from its distinctive red color, the Red Power Star does not differ from regular Power Stars.

== Description ==
[[File:RedPowerStar-1-.webp|thumb|left|Mario obtaining a Red Power Star]]
[[Mario]] obtains this star in the second mission of the [[Gateway Galaxy]], [[Gateway's Purple Coins]]. Mario will talk to [[Rosalina]] and a [[Red Luma]], who will give Mario a new challenge of collecting a hundred [[Purple Coin]]s. Mario must use [[Red Star]]s to transform into [[Flying Mario]] to collect the Purple Coins in the air. Once this challenge has been completed, Red Stars appear in the [[Comet Observatory]].
