'''Para-Goombas''' (alternatively '''Paragoombas''')<ref>M. Arakawa. Nintendo ''Mario Mania'' Player's Guide. Page 50.</ref> are [[Galoomba]]s with [[parachute]]s that first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==History==
===''Super Mario World''===
[[File:SMW Para-Goomba Sprite.gif|frame|left]]
In ''Super Mario World'', Para-Goombas are one of two enemies to use parachutes, the other being [[Para-bomb]]. Para-Goombas descend slowly from the sky, and once they land, they become regular Galoombas. Defeating a Para-Goomba earns the player 200 [[point]]s.

===''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3''===
[[File:SMA4 SMB3 Para-Goomba (SMW).png|frame|left]]
Para-Goombas also make a minor appearance in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', where they are found at the end of the [[World-e]] level [[Rich with Ropes]].

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
Para-Goombas make a reappearance in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. They can be made only in the ''Super Mario World'' style by placing a parachute on a Galoomba. Their descending animation is changed to resemble the Galoombas' swimming animation from the first ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''.
