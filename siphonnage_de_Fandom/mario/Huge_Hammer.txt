|name=Huge Hammer (Battle Card)}}'''Huge Hammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Huge Hammer is the super-strong version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer Attack|Hammer attack]]. It is a huge brown hammer with an orange/tan spiral background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 52/250 coins

Sell Price: 26/125 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Huge Hammer is first seen in [[Cobalt Base]], but can also found in [[Green Energy Plant]] and [[Black Bowser's Castle]].
