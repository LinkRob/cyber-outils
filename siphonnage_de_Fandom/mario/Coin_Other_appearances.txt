Coins appear in ''[[Yoshi (game)|Yoshi]]'', after the player clears levels 25 through 27 of the B-Type game. They reward the player 1,200 points.

The ''Yoshi'' games would later have their own type of [[Coin (Yoshi franchise)|Coin]], introduced in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==''Wario'' franchise==
{{main|Coin (Wario franchise)}}
===''Wario's Woods''===
In ''[[Wario's Woods]]'', coins appear whenever [[Toad]] manages to clear a round against Wario's monsters. The amount of coins being dropped from the top of the hollow tree depends on how fast Toad had cleared the round or how much bonus points he has acquired during the round. The coins add up to the amount of points Toad gains throughout the game.

A loose installment of the [[Wario (franchise)|''Wario'' franchise]], coins are not based on those collected by Wario during his adventures.

===''WarioWare Gold''===
In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', Coins from the {{series|Wrecking Crew}} appear in the [[microgame]] [[Wrecking Crew (WarioWare Gold)|Wrecking Crew]].

==''Donkey Kong'' franchise==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong'' series=== 
[[File:M&DKMotM Coin.png|thumb|20px]]
Coins from the ''Mario'' franchise appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]'', ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Minis March Again!]]'', ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!]]'', ''[[Mario and Donkey Kong: Minis on the Move]]'', and ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars]]''. They are collected to raise the player's score. A large coin is worth five small coins. In ''Minis March Again!'' and ''Mini-Land Mayhem!'', by collecting 100 coins, the player gets an [[M-Token]].

==Crossover appearances==
===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
The [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] features its own set of [[Coin (Super Smash Bros. series)|coins]], although ones of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] have still been featured in some capacity. A coin is also featured as a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and as a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', the Coin description claims that it is unclear as to whether or not Coins are the official currency of The Mushroom World, even though they were used as such in both ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' and ''Paper Mario''. 

Coins are produced from [[Mario]]'s, [[Dr. Mario]]'s (in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'') and [[Luigi]]'s attack (when sourspotted only), [[Super Jump Punch]], using their design from ''Super Mario 64'' up until ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', where their design is based on the [[Star Coin]]s from ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its sequels. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', when [[Mario Cap|Mario's cap]] becomes [[Cappy]], the coins are replaced by [[regional coin]]s from the [[Metro Kingdom]] in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' (though this is exclusive to Mario), which use the same sound effect from the original game.

They also appear in the [[Golden Plains]] stage in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS''. Collecting 100 of these turns the character [[Gold Mario|gold]] and increases their attack power and launch resistance for a period of time.

===''Mario & Sonic'' series===
====''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games''====
Coins appear in the [[Dream Canoe]] event in the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'', where the objective of the event is to collect as many of them as possible within the time limit. They initially appear scattered around the area, but can also be stolen from other players as the event goes on.

====''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''====
Coins appear in the [[Deluxe Halfpipe]] [[Dream Event]] in the Nintendo DS version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'', where several appear scattered along the course which the player can collect. When they collect five or more, they can use them to perform a special trick, and will lose five if they successfully perform one or half of their total for a failed attempt, with the number rounded up for halves of odd totals.

In the [[Wii]] [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|version]], coins appear in the [[Dream Ski Cross (Individual)|individual]] and [[Dream Ski Cross (Team)|team]] versions of Dream Ski Cross, where five of them can be collected to allow the player to use their special action again.

====''Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games''====
In ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'', coins appear in [[Groove Pipe Snowboard]], where collecting them allows the player to perform faster boosts in groove zones. Coins are also collected from [[? Block]]s and wave sections in [[Mario's Figure Skating Spectacular]], though only for aesthetic effect. In [[Snow Day Street Hockey]], the characters can collect coins on the Coin Street rink by using the P Switch to turn Brick Blocks into them. Collecting eight coins will cause a bonus goal to appear, which is carried around by [[Lakitu]] on the same side of the rink as the opponent's goal.

===''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle''===
[[File:CoinMRKB.jpg|thumb]]
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'', coins can be collected in the overworld or by achieving a "Golden Shot" from defeating an enemy. They are used to purchase new weapons. Additionally, the Hog Wild weapon has a coin on each side.

==Guest appearances==
====''Animal Crossing'' series====
Coins appear in the ''[[Animal Crossing (series)|Animal Crossing]]'' series as obtainable furniture. In installments prior to ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing: New Leaf|Animal Crossing: New Leaf]]'', they use their ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' design, while from ''New Leaf'' onward, they use their modern design, with ''New Leaf'' using a model similar to those in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. In ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing: New Horizons|Animal Crossing: New Horizons]]'', they can be passed through and "collected", causing them to disappear for a short time.

===''Minecraft''===
In the Super Mario Mash-up in ''[[Minecraft]]'', [[w:c:minecraft:Gold Ingot|Gold Ingots]] are replaced by Coins. There is also a painting of a Coin.

====Club Nintendo====
In [[Club Nintendo (rewards program)|Club Nintendo]], Nintendo's defunct rewards program, coins were used as currency to purchase downloadable games, content, and [[Nintendo]]-themed merchandise.

====''Pikmin Short Movies''====
In the [[w:c:nintendo:Pikmin Short Movies|''Pikmin'' short]] "[[w:c:nintendo:Occupational Hazards|Occupational Hazards]]," a [[Red Pikmin]] exits a green pipe that contained coins. The Red Pikmin also has dust on his face that makes him resemble Mario.
