== Description ==
The Pharaoh's Underwear are a pair of underwear that are gray in color and is described by [[Wario]] to be very smelly.

== History ==
The Pharaoh's Underwear is one of three items belonging to [[Poobah the Pharaoh]] that must be found and given to the [[Sphinx (character)|Sphinx]] in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]]. The Pharaoh's Underwear can be found in a room on the top floor in a purple treasure chest beyond some moving platforms.
