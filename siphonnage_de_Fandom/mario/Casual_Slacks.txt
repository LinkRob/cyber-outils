'''Casual Slacks''' is a [[clothing]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', Casual Slacks are sold at the [[Beanbean Castle Town]] clothes shop for 550 [[coin]]s. It increases [[HP]] by 20 and [[Defense]] by 70. In the Japanese version, the Casual Slacks are at a higher price of 900 coins, owing to how it now increases HP by 30.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', Casual Slacks becomes available at the clothes shop after defeating [[Popple]] and [[Birdo]]. It is priced at 1,000, closer to its price in the original's Japanese version than the international versions. Casual Slacks now increases Defense by 79 and [[Bros. Point|BP]] by 10, but it no longer increases any HP. Whereas Casual Slacks did not have any special effect in the original game, it received the [[Guard Guardian]] as its special effect in the remake.
