'''Space Mines''' are obstacles which first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
Space Mines are a counterpart of [[Floating Mine]]s and [[Mikey Mine]]s located in outer space environments. Space Mines are shaped like green [[Spiked Ball]]s but are colored similarly to Mikey Mines, with which they even share a Japanese name as of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. Space Mines are not sentient and do not have any facial features, unlike Mikey Mines, making Space Mines a type of obstacle instead of an enemy.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Space Mines are first encountered in [[Battlerock Galaxy]] and lastly in [[Bubble Blast Galaxy]]. Most Space Mines do not move around, but some move along purple streaks of light either horizontally or vertically. A Space Mine explodes on contact, damaging [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], though a Space Mine can be more safely destroyed by shooting a [[Star Bit]] at it.

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[File:Space Mine SMG2 artwork.png|thumb|''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' artwork]]
Space Mines are first encountered in [[Bowser Jr.'s Fearsome Fleet]] and lastly in the [[Grandmaster Galaxy]]. They act the same as in ''Super Mario Galaxy'', but now release three Star Bits upon exploding.
