'''Thomas Spindler''' is a former Nintendo of Europe employee of German nationality involved with translation work. Additionally, he was the voice of [https://www.mariowiki.com/Wario Wario] and the [https://www.mariowiki.com/Thwomps Thwomps] in the Japanese version of ''[https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Kart_64 Mario Kart 64]'' (the latter using a slowed down version of the former's 'laugh' voice sample). His voice clips for Wario were reused for both ''[https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Party Mario Party]'' and ''[https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Party_2 Mario Party 2]'' (albeit pitched down) and ''[https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Kart:_Super_Circuit Mario Kart: Super Circuit]''.
