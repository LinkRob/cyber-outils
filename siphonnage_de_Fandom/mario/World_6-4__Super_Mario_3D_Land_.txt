'''World 6-4''' is the thirty-first level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
The level begins with [[Mario]] crossing a pair of [[Beep Block|beat block]] sets. After passing a couple of [[Prongo]]s and [[Wallop]]s, Mario finds a door leading to the inside of the level. The inside features several more beat blocks. Once Mario makes his way to the exit of this inside portion, he finds the [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]] of the level. There are several more beat blocks that lie past the checkpoint, found in arrangements of climbing, running, and very clever jumping. After a winding path around two large spiked bars, Mario finds himself near a wall. This last area sports more spiked bars the rock in a "u" formation. Past these final beat blocks, Mario makes his way to the [[Flagpole]].

== Enemies ==
Enemies that appeared in World 6-4:
* [[Wallop]]
* [[Prongo]]

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] is found atop the entrance to the inside portion of the level.
*'''Star Medal 2''': Like the first one, the second Star Medal is found atop the exit of the inside portion of the level.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The third Star Medal is found on the block of the first spinner with spiked balls.
