The '''Veggie Set''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It does not look much different than the [[Healthy Salad]], which is mixed with a [[Big Egg]] to create this healthy recipe. Eating a Veggie Set restores 20 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
