'''World 3-Fortress''' is a level from [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3]] that appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]].''

==Overview==
It is the only level in the entire game to feature [[Amp]]s.

==Walkthrough==
This level features brightly colored rotating blocks that must be used to climb to higher locations. Rolling [[Spiked Ball]]s, [[Dry Bones]], and [[Amp]]s are some of the hazards that must be avoided as the floor scrolls upward. Near Lemmy's door, there is a squared-shaped area with a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[Penguin Suit]] in the center of it. The last half of this level resembles Castle Crush, a level found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''. At the end of the tower, [[Lemmy Koopa]] is found balancing on a yellow [[Circus Ball]] in an icy arena. The player must attempt to defeat him while avoiding the Circus Balls that Lemmy conjures otherwise they have the risk of being pushed off the level. Lemmy also spins in his shell trying to hit the player when hit. Like most other bosses, Lemmy must be hit three times to be defeated.

== Star Coins ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': To the top-right of the second rotating block, there is a hidden area with a Warp Door. After getting there by jumping from the top of the block, Mario and co. are led to an area with another rotating block, a [[Ball 'n' Chain]], a circle of [[Coin]]s, and the first Star Coin. Mario and co. must jump into the block and jump up to the Star Coin while avoiding the Ball 'N' Chain.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Right near the second [[POW Block]], the second Star Coin is to the left and blocked off by many blocks. Mario and co. must let the [[Spiked Ball]]s destroy the blocks, use the aforementioned POW Block to get the Spike Balls out of the way, then get the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is floating just below [[Lemmy Koopa|Lemmy]]'s door. To get it, Mario and co. should get on the final rotating block, jump from it to the right path, then get the Star Coin while avoiding the [[Dry Bones]].
