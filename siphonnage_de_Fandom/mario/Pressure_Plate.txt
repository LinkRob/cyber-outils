'''Pressure Plates''' are objects in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
Whenever [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] steps on a Pressure Plate, a nearby [[Wall Face]] fires a projectile blast at that character. If a character is inside of a [[Barrel]], the blast is reflected back.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
Pressure Plates are found at [[Chucklehuck Woods]], [[Woohoo Hooniversity]], and [[Joke's End]] alongside the [[Wall Face]]s, with each location changing its appearance.
