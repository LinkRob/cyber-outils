'''Ring Burners''' are robotic enemies in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. They appear in only three levels: [[Cakewalk Flip]], [[Rainbow Run]], and [[Champion's Road]]. Ring Burners are a variant of the [[Ring Beamer]].

== Description ==
Ring Burners are red, with the skull symbol found on [[Bill Blaster|Bill Blasters]] appearing on their top side.

== Behaviour ==
In their calm state, Ring Burners do not move. However, provoking them causes them to launch a fiery red ring outwards, either in a circular or square shape. Their plasma rings also have a finite range, evaporating upon reaching their maximum range. Like the Ring Beamer that they were based on, Ring Burners cannot be destroyed, though they can be stunned by standing on their head.
