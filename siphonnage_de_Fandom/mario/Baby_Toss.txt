'''Baby Toss''' is a move in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.

==Description==
When using Baby Toss, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] throw their baby counterparts, [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]], off of their respective back when they are riding piggyback. Baby Toss helps the babies reach higher platforms and go down cylindrical objects, but [[Baby Spin]], a move learned later on, allows the babies to go even higher.

If Baby Mario and Baby Luigi are thrown into battle when Mario and Luigi use Baby Toss, only the babies are used during the battle.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''==
The Mario Bros. learn the move in [[Bowser's Castle]].
