'''Koopasta''' is both a [[recipe]] and an item which appears in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
=== In ''Paper Mario'' ===
[[File:Koopasta PM sprite.png|frame|left]]
Koopasta can only be created by having [[Tayce T.]] cook together a [[Dried Pasta]] and a [[Koopa Leaf]]. When used, Koopasta restores both [[Mario]]'s [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Flower Point|FP]] by 7, which is considered useful early on, but practically useless later in the game. Koopasta must also be made as one of [[Koopa Koot]]'s favors. Giving him Koopasta results in a reward of a single [[coin]].

=== In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
[[File:KoopastaPMTTYD.png|thumb|left]]
A Koopasta can be cooked by [[Zess T.]] by giving her a [[Turtley Leaf]] and either a [[Fresh Pasta Bunch|Fresh Pasta]] or [[Spaghetti]]. It restores the same amount of HP as from the previous game.

=== In ''Super Paper Mario'' ===
Koopasta returns with the name '''Koopasta Dish'''. In this game, Koopasta Dish now halves the damage taken instead of restoring health.
