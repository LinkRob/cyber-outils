'''Toad Circuit''' is the very first track in the [[Mushroom Cup]] in the game ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', making it the first track in the entire game.'' ''The track is relatively easy, having boosts, simple turns, and [[coin]]s. This track celebrates the whole entire [[Toad (species)|Toad]] species. The track also has an audience watching, a large yellow [[Toad (species)|Toad]] balloon, and many other features. There are two ramps that can move into position that causes karts to activate their super gliders. A shortcut can be taken after the player exits the tunnel and uses a [[Mushroom (Mario Kart series)|Mushroom]] on the ramp to the right. It has a really simple shape: Figure 8 with a "heart turn" and a ramp.

== In [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] ==
Toad Circuit was imported to ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''  which is the first track the player starts off in practice mode either with [[Toad (character)|Toad]] or [[Toadette]]. The track made it's first debut in [[New York Tour]] and is a flagship course of the game as players can drive around it in every Tour.

This track can also apear in three variants called: Toad Circuit R in which the driver races in reverse around the track, Toad Circuit T where ramps are displayed on the course and Toad circuit R/T where trick ramps are displayed in reverse of the track.
