The '''Toad Kart''' is a lightweight [[kart]] which appears in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is the signature kart of [[Toad]]. Toad Kart is slow but has good [[acceleration]]. This Kart was made for [[Toad (character)|Toad]] since it has a blue color scheme like ''Toad's'' shirt and has a [[Mushroom]] logo on it. This kart was the basis for the Standard Karts in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. It can be unlocked by getting first place on the [[Special Cup]] in 100cc. It is a favorite for Time Trials at [[DK Mountain]].

The Toad Kart has inspired the idea for the [[Standard Kart]]s in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' and later installments, notably the [[Standard TD]].
