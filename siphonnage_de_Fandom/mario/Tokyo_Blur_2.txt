'''Tokyo Blur 2''' is a race track that was featured in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. Hence the prefix, it is the second layout of [[Tokyo Blur]]. Tokyo Blur 2 made its debut during the New Year's Tour of 2020.

== Layout ==
The racers on Tokyo Blur 2 start on the Tōkyō Kō Renkakukyō, which is placed on the south side of the city, facing west, just like on [[Tokyo Blur 1]]. After the [[Lakitu (referee)|referee]] says "Go!", they continue west and turn right before hitting the southwest corner of the map, heading north a short distance before meeting a three-way intersection. However, unlike in Tokyo Blur 1, the racers make a right turn, heading northeast for a bit until they reach the second three-way intersection, turning left. The road goes into a 180-degree right turn so the racers head back south into the city, then turn left at the third three-way intersection. It continues as Tokyo Blur 1 from there.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', the racers switch to this route when they finish the first lap, and spend the second lap on this route before switching to [[Tokyo Blur 3]] on the final lap.

== Trivia ==
*This is the only route of [[Tokyo Blur]] to visit all three intersections.
