'''''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''''' is an enhanced remake of the 2009 [[Nintendo DS]] game, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' that was released in Japan on December 27, 2018, and in North America on January 11, 2019 on the [[Nintendo 3DS]]. The game was originally announced in a Nintendo Direct on March 8, 2018. Similarly to ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', this game features a side story named [[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]], where [[Bowser Jr.]] is the main character, with the gameplay also being similar to [[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]], except with more content and more polish. It was the last ''Mario & Luigi'' game to be released before [[AlphaDream]]'s closure in 2019 due to bankruptcy.

==Gameplay==
*In the game, some enemy attacks (as well as some [[Special Attack]]s) are slower.
*There is also a new [[Cheese]] item that recovers [[Bowser]]'s BP (Brawl Points).
*A giant boss gauntlet for [[Bowser]] was made as well.
*Easy Attack Mode returns from [[Minion Quest]].
*Easy Mode and Assistance return as well.
*You start with 3 gear slots, just like in later entries in the series.
*The Battle Guide is now replaced with the Guide Block.
*The finishing move for the [[Green Shell]] returns from Dream Team.
*Bros. Attacks have different costs.
*2 Badge Effects can be stored, like in [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team|Dream Team]].

==Reception==
''Mario & Luigi: Bowser Inside Story + Bowser Jr's Journey'' received generally positive reviews, as demonstrated by 84% in Metacritic.

==Trivia==
*This is the final ''Mario'' game to be released on the Nintendo 3DS.
*This was the second-to last Nintendo 3DS game to be published by Nintendo, the overall last 3DS game to be published by Nintendo in North America & Europe was ''[[w:c:kirby:Kirby's Extra Epic Yarn|Kirby's Extra Epic Yarn]]''.
*This is the last ''Mario'' game released in 2018 in Japan and was one of the first two games to be released in 2019 Internationally, the other being ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.
