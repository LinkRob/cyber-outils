The '''Dinohattanites'''<ref>June 1993. Nintendo Magazine System (AU) Issue #3. Page 13.</ref> are a human-like race that evolved from dinosaurs and other prehistoric reptiles featured in the 1993 ''Super Mario Bros. film'', inhabiting the parallel dimension of [[Dinohattan]].

==History==
===''Super Mario Bros.'' film===
{{main|Dinohattan#History}}During the [[Wikipedia:Cretaceous–Paleogene extinction event|extinction event about 65 million years ago]], in which an asteroid collided with Earth and caused the extinction of dinosaurs and other prehistoric reptiles and provided the conditions necessary for the evolution of humanity, the impact was so powerful that it created a parallel dimension called Dinohattan. This alternate dimension to Earth, conversely, caused the extinction of mammals and provided the conditions for the evolution of dinosaurs and other prehistoric reptiles into intelligent, human-like creatures. The dinosaurs and other prehistoric reptiles that lived at the site of the meteorite impact were exposed to its geomagnetic energy, gradually evolving into the cult of the Gatekeeper. The Gatekeepers were devoted to worshiping the monolith and maintaining the gateway between worlds, ruling secretly through the Monarchy. They harnessed its energy into the grid that powers their great city.
