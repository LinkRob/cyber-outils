'''Coffee''' is a type of real-world drink found in a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games.

==In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' / ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''==
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can make coffee at [[Starbeans Cafe]] using [[Bean]]s and drink it. Different stats go up depending on which type of beans are used to brew coffee. 

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
There is a food called the [[Mushroom Full Course]] served in the [[Excess Express]], and it has a type of coffee called "[[Mushroom]] Coffee."

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
[[Carson]] sells coffee at his bar, the [[The Overthere (bar)|The Overthere]].

==In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''==
There is a type of coffee item named [[Tough Coffee]], which increases [[Princess Peach]]'s health meter by half a [[heart]].
