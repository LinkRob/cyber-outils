}}'''World 4-3''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the third and final level of World 4, the [[Chai Kingdom]]. It is also the final level in the entire game where [[Mario]] fights [[Tatanga]] in order to rescue [[Princess Daisy]].

==Overview==
World 4-3 is a sky level, and Mario will be on his plane, the [[Sky Pop]], for the whole level. Mario will be able to shoot down air enemies mainly [[Chikako]]s, [[Chicken]]s, and [[Roketon]]s. Some point, Mario will enter an area with walls meaning he must carefully navigate through the area. There are also some new enemies called [[Genkotsu]]s, which replace the [[Piranha Plant]]s. Eventually, Mario reaches a room where he must do battle with a large cloud named [[Biokinton]] first. Biokinton attacks by launching Chickens at Mario. Mario must shoot down Biokinton in order to defeat him, and Tatanga himself soon enters the battle. After a long battle, Mario defeats Tatanga and rescues [[Princess Daisy]].
