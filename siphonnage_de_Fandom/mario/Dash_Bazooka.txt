The '''Dash Bazooka''' is an item which first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
The Dash Bazooka is an metal bazooka utilizing the same bazooka as with the [[Banana Shot]] and [[Bullet Bill]], except it is equipped with a [[Dash Mushroom|Mushroom]]. Upon being fired, the Dash Bazooka launches its Mushroom at whoever it is locked onto. While this is generally advantageous for the targeted racer, the sudden burst in speed can cause them to crash into a wall.
