{{Nihongo|'''Peach Blossom'''|ピーチブラッサム|Pīchi Burassamu|Peach Blossom}} is [[Princess Peach|Peach]]'s [[Final Smash]] that debuted in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. The Final Smash reappeared in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

== Description ==
When Peach activates her Final Smash, she dances, and flower pedals and peaches falling to the ground.<ref name="SSBU"/><ref name="SmashBrosDOJO">{{cite web |url= https://www.smashbros.com/wii/en_us/characters/peach.html|title= Peach|accessdate=2019-06-27 |author= Masahiro Sakurai|date= 2007-10-19|work= Smash Bros. DOJO!!|quote= Somehow, Princess Peach always seems go at her own pace. Her Final Smash will lure you into her world. The closer you are to Peach, the deeper you sleep. And all around you is a mountain of peaches that replenish your health when you eat them. The flowers bloom. The peaches pour down. Yes. It’s so elegant.}}</ref> Opponents will sleep longer when the closer they to Peach.<ref name="SSBB manual">''"Pull foes into Peach's world, where they fall asleep. The closer they are to Peach, the deeper their slumber. Eat the fallen peaches to recover damage."'' –''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' instruction booklet, page 27</ref> Additionally, opponents take damage depending on their distance from her.<ref group="note" name="FS damage">The closer the opponent is to Peach, the more damage they take.</ref> The player can recover his/her health by eating peaches.<ref name="SSBU">'''Peach's Move List''': ''"Giant peaches descend from above, and nearby fighters fall asleep. The closer a fighter is to Peach, the longer they stay snoozing, giving Peach plenty of time to launch foes or recover health by eating the giant peaches."'' {{cite video game| title = Super Smash Bros. Ultimate| developer = Bandai Namco, Sora Ltd.| publisher = Nintendo| date = 2018-12-07| platform = [[Nintendo Switch]]}}</ref><ref name="SmashBrosDOJO"/> Additionally, aerial opponents can be KO'd<ref group="note" name="ko">KO is the abbreviation for ''knockout''. KO'd is the informal past tense for ''knockout''.</ref> above bottomless pits when Peach performs the Final Smash.

In ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'', the range for the Peach Blossom has shortened and opponents fall asleep for less time. However, the Peach Blossom can now be used on opponents while on midair. The game uses different artworks of Peach were used.

In ''Ultimate'', the Peach Blossom has a dark pink background when Peach uses the Final Smash. Prior to patch 2.0.0, there was a bug on Peach Blossom and Daisy Blossom, the borders display incorrectly in scrolling stages. After patch 3.0.0, it reduced opponents' sleeping time with [[Daisy Blossom]].

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''===
[[File:Peach Blossom SSBB screenshot.png|thumb|left|140px|Peach Blossom in ''Brawl'']]
Peach Blossom is Peach's Final Smash in ''Brawl''. Peach Blossom also appeared as a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]].
===''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U''===
[[File:SSB43 Peach Blossom.jpg|thumb|right|160px|Peach Blossom in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS''.]]
Peach Blossom is Peach's Final Smash in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS''. Peach can activate the Final Smash while on midair. Aerial opponents are affected by the Final Smash. Peach spawns three large peaches and thirteen regular-sized peaches.<ref group="note" name="SSB4 peach fruit">The following peaches heal different damages.
*Each large peaches heal 20% damage.
*Each regular-sized peaches heal 5%.</ref> Images of Peach replaced ''Brawl's'' Peach illustrations. Additionally, Peach Blossom appeared as a trophy in the ''Wii U'' version.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
[[File:SSBU Peach Blossom Skill Preview.png|thumb|left|150px|Skill Preview of Peach Blossom.]]
Peach Blossom is Peach's Final Smash in ''Ultimate''. Although there were no mechanical changes during ''Ultimate's'' release date, the dark pink background was added.

Prior to 2.0.0, the borders displayed incorrectly in scrolling stages. After patch 3.0.0, it reduced opponents' sleeping time. Overall, it has been nerfed during its update history.

{{see|Daisy Blossom}}

Due to Daisy's presence in ''Ultimate'', she also has a [[Final Smash]] that functions like Peach Blossom. Instead, it has an orange background and Daisy illustrations. Daisy summons edible daisies from the sky. Like Peach Blossom, it has been nerfed overall.

=== Etymology ===
[[File:Thyatira batis.jpg|thumb|right|130px|The moth's wings resembles peach blossom.]]
The name is an obvious pun, including Peach's name. ''Peach blossom'' is a moth that is scientifically called ''Thyatira batis'', a common species in the British Isles. Its brown forewrings has five pink and white blotches resembling peach blossom petals.

== Trivia ==
*The ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' Premiere Edition Guide incorrectly stated that Peach Blossom does not inflict damage to opponents. Although it correctly stated that it puts opponents to sleep.
*Peach Blossom is one of the two [[Final Smash]]es to inflict sleeping status effects. The other being [[Negative Zone]].
**With Negative Zone, no longer used by [[Luigi]]. Peach Blossom was the only Final Smash to put opponents to sleep in ''Super Smash Bros. 4''.
***However, Daisy has a new Final Smash called Daisy Blossom in ''Ultimate'' making it one of Final Smashes to put opponents to sleep.
*Peach Blossom does not KO opponents. However, it can be used as a set up to KO the opponent.
