[[File:Snapshot - 43.jpg|300px]]'''"Almost Unreal"''' is the main theme song of the [[Super Mario Bros. (1993 film)|''Super Mario Bros.'']] film during the end credits. It is a pop rock song by Swedish pop duo Roxette released on May 10th, 1993 as the lead single of the official ''[[Super Mario Bros. Original Motion Picture Soundtrack]]''. The song peaked at number 94 on the Billboard Hot 100 in North America, <ref> "[https://www.billboard.com/artist/roxette/chart-history/hsi/ Roxette Chart History (Hot 100)". Billboard.]</ref> and reached 29 in Canada.<ref> [https://www.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/discover/films-videos-sound-recordings/rpm/Pages/image.aspx?Image=nlc008388.1007&URLjpg=http%3a%2f%2fwww.collectionscanada.gc.ca%2fobj%2f028020%2ff4%2fnlc008388.1007.gif&Ecopy=nlc008388.1007 Canada Top Singles] ''RPM''. Library and Archives Canada.</ref> It is featured in the Super Mario Bros. film as the first song played during the credits, and is the first song on the official soundtrack's album.

Reportedly, the song was written by [[Per Gessle]] intended to be used as the theme song to the Walt Disney Pictures film ''Hocus Pocus''. The song was transferred to be used in the ''Super Mario Bros.'' film instead, and the lyrics were slightly reworked to remove most references to Hocus Pocus.
