'''Killer Ball''' is the [[Star Skill]] used by [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and his [[Star Pitch]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

When Bowser uses the move, the baseball turns into a [[Bullet Bill]]. If Killer Ball is used while pitching, the Bullet Bill slowly moves toward the home plate, does a loop, then zooms by the batter. If Killer Ball is used while batting, the Bullet Bill dashes across the field. If a fielder catches the ball, they are pushed a distance back.

==In ''Mario Superstar Baseball''==
In Challenge mode, Bowser can only start using Killer Ball once the [[Bullet Bill (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Bullet Bill]] item has been purchased. His son, [[Bowser Jr.]], has a Star Skill that is a variation of the Killer Ball, named the [[Killer Jr. Ball]]. It is the same as the Killer Ball except Bowser Jr.'s Bullet Bill is shown wearing his bib.
