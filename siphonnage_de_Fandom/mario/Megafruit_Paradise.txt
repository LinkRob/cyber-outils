{{Nihongo|'''Megafruit Paradise'''|発見！巨大フルーツの島|Hakken! Kyodai Furūtsu no Shima|A Discovery! Islands of Large Fruit}} is a food-themed board in ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' that takes place at an island.

== Description ==
Megafruit Paradise takes place on a tropical island along the beach with structures made of fruit: watermelon and pineapple.
