'''Gloombas''' are a type of enemy in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They first appear in the original ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Gloombas are a variant of [[Goomba]] with a blue body and red feet. In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', their blue coloration is revealed to stem from them receiving no sunlight.

===Behavior===
Gloombas have the same behavior as Goombas, but are stronger.

===Habitats===
Gloombas live in underground areas, an intentional homage to the underground blue palette used on [[Goomba]]s in the original ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
Gloombas are only found in [[Toad Town Tunnels]], where they are the weakest type of enemy encountered. Gloombas attack [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] by jumping towards him and [[Headbonk|drilling their head into the plumber]], dealing 2 points of damage, though Paper Mario can use his [[Guard]] technique to reduce the damage to 1 just as he is about to get hit by the Gloomba's head. Gloombas also have two variants that debuted alongside them, [[Paragloomba]]s and [[Spiked Gloomba]]s.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
Gloombas return as enemies, along with Paragloombas and Spiked Gloombas, and they now inhabit the early floors of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. This time, if the Gloombas managed to strike without getting intercepted by Guard, they deal 3 points of damage.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Gloombas are first encountered in the underground portion of World 3-1, [[The Bitlands]], but they later appear in [[Castle Bleck Foyer]] and the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Unlike the first two ''Paper Mario'' games, Paragloombas and Spiked Gloombas do not appear alongside Gloombas.
