A '''Revver''', internally named '''Chorochu''', is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Revvers have a grayish white head that mouse driving similar to a cross between that of the [[Skeleton Koopa]] and [[Chain Chomp]]. A pink tongue can be seen hanging out of their mouth, and they have a red dog collar and an orange body that has a wheel. A tail with a grabbable handle comes out the back akin to a leash.

=== Behavior ===
Revvers normally roll around on their wheel, but [[Mario]] can grab their leash and pull it, sending the enemy driving forwards uncontrollably, causing them to die upon impact with a wall. Revvers will break through blocks of stone in their revved-up state. Revvers do not die from one hit, but instead need to be [[Ground Pound|Ground Pounded]] to die.

== In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' ==
Revvers make their debut in [[Rolling Ball Hill]] and are last seen in [[Hot-Hot Hot!]]. They also appear prominently in [[Break Time! Revver Run.]]

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Revver is derirved from the verb "to rev up," while the internal name Chorochu is from Japanese Choro-choro (darting about; written チョロチョロ) and chū (written ちゅう; referring to a mouse's squeaking noises).{{SMBWNav}}
