'''''Punch-Out!!''''' is a game released for the [[Wii]] that, like previous games in the ''Punch-Out'' series, is a boxing game where the player takes on various challengers to achieve title belts. The particular version features [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] as one of the challengers.

In one of [[Doc Louis]]'s quotes, after [[Little Mac]]'s second round with Donkey Kong, Doc Louis references [[Mario]] saying, "''Listen up, son! If you're havin' trouble...maybe I should call my plumber! Ha ha ha!''".
