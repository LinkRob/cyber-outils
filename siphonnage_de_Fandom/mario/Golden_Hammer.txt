The '''Golden Hammer''' is an item introduced in ''[[Wrecking Crew]]''. It is a stronger version of the [[hammer]] the player normally carries that the player can swing faster, can break [[wall (Wrecking Crew series)|wall]]s in a single hit, and can also attack enemies to send them to the bottom of the screen. It also appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series as a stronger version of the Hammer item. 

Some other hammers in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] resemble the Golden Hammer, namely the hammers used in the ''[[Mario Party]]'' [[minigame]] [[Bash 'n' Cash]], the hammers used in the ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' minigame [[Lights Out]], and the [[Ultra Hammer]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
===''Wrecking Crew''===
[[File:WC Golden Hammer.png|left|frame]]
In ''Wrecking Crew'', to obtain a Golden Hammer, the player must swing their hammer a number of times so that the number of swings plus the stage number divided by eight leaves a remainder of one. In addition, the player must also strike a certain [[bomb]] to obtain the hammer, and it must be the third bomb struck. A level with fewer than three bombs will not have a Golden Hammer, so the earliest a Golden Hammer can appear is [[Phase 4 (Wrecking Crew)|Phase 4]]. While using the Golden Hammer, the player can also temporarily hover in midair by pressing {{button|NES|A}} and {{button|NES|B}} fast enough. The player holds onto the hammer until a life is lost or if the player is trapped and has to restart the phase. It can be taken into the [[Bonus Stage (Wrecking Crew series)|Bonus Stages]].

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
{{Smashpedia}}
[[File:SSBB - Peach Bowser Golden Hammer Screenshot.png|thumb|A screenshot of Peach using the Golden Hammer on Bowser in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
The Golden Hammer is a extremely powerful battering item in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. When a Golden Hammer spawns, initially only its pedestal is present. Once the pedestal lands on solid ground, a sound cue plays and the Golden Hammer slowly grows out of the pedestal to full size. The Golden Hammer cannot be [[carry|picked up]] until it reaches full size, and doing so makes the pedestal disappear. When a player has a Golden Hammer, they automatically start swinging the hammer up and down until the item's timer runs out and it vanishes, just like the regular [[Hammer]] item. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', hitting opponents with a Golden Hammer creates same the blue pixelated hit effect that the Hammer does. Also like the normal Hammer, players swinging a Golden Hammer cannot attack, dodge, shield, drop the Golden Hammer, throw the Golden Hammer, [[Double Jump (airborne)|midair jump]], and grab the edges of [[stage (Super Smash Bros. series)|stage]]s. The Golden Hammer is swung faster than a normal Hammer, each hit does 30% damage, and it launches characters at a more horizontal angle than a regular Hammer. In addition, repeatedly tapping the attack, jump, or grab buttons will allow the player to float in mid-air while holding this item, like with the Golden Hammer in ''Wrecking Crew'' and unlike the normal Hammer. This helps counteract the inability to perform a midair jump. While floating, the player cannot turn around, as per usual ''Super Smash Bros.'' aerial movement rules. If the player is spinning after being launched, the grab and shield buttons can be used to drop a Golden Hammer, just as with a normal Hammer. If a Golden Hammer touches the ground after being dropped, either through this method or through the user being launched by a strong attack, the Golden Hammer vanishes.

The head of a Golden Hammer cannot pop off like the regular Hammer, but a Golden Hammer can instead become the identically appearing '''Squeaky Hammer''' (also known as a '''Golden Squeaky Hammer''') after being picked up.<ref>"''Golden Hammer - This sometimes turns into a Squeaky Hammer. It doesn’t hurt much, but that sound sure is cute, eh?''" - ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Wii U'' Tips</ref> The Squeaky Hammer squeaks if it hits someone instead of doing damage, meaning the player has all the disadvantages of a Golden Hammer with almost none of the advantages. However, Squeaky Hammers still allow the player to float.'

The Golden Hammer can also be used to open most challenge windows in Challenges. In ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', they can be earned by completing challenges that have windows containing Golden Hammers; in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'', they can be earned by completing a certain number of challenges. There are five Golden Hammers in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' and ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'', three per panel in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'', and nine in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''. Golden Hammers cannot break windows that pertain to specific challenges in the NTSC versions of ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' and all versions of ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Wii U'' and ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''.
