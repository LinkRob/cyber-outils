'''Agility''' is a special move in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It is used by [[Pichu]]. Similarly to [[Pikachu]]'s [[Quick Attack]], when performed, Pichu will make a quick teleport-resembling action in any direction. The move will not deal damage, however.

== Trivia ==
* Agility is one of the four attacks in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'' to not have direct effect on opponents. The other three attacks are Teleport, PSI Magnet and Transform.
