{{Nihongo|'''Half the Battle'''|ひろってメダル合わせ|Hirotte medaru awase|Gather and Match Medals}} is an unlockable [[Team Minigame|Team]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The team gathers the crest halves to form sun-like pieces.

== Gameplay ==
The team's objective is to gather all half crests to gain the most points. Each player in their team must guide their crew to gather half crests. The team must meet their allied team. Depending on the number of half crests gathered, the team will earn points. The team must avoid hitting the [[Amp]]s; it may result the team to drop the pieces.

== Ending ==
The winning team pose their victory while the losing team pose their loss.

*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move
