'''Chain-Chomp Challenge''' is a [[Minigame|Special Game]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[New Play Control! Mario Power Tennis]]''.

==Layout==
Chain-Chomp Challenge takes place in [[Wario Factory Court]]. The four characters are separated into their own lanes, each having a moving [[Conveyor Belt]] and a [[Chain Chomp|Chain-Chomp]] at the end. There are four [[Monty Mole]]s sitting in their own [[Cannon (Super Mario Sunshine)|cannons]] on top of a machine, facing the respective lanes.

==Gameplay==
The four characters have to feed tennis balls to Chain-Chomps as a way to score [[point]]s. The tennis balls, [[Water Ball|water balls]], and [[Bob-omb]]s are shot out by Monty Moles from their cannons. While a Chain-Chomp is in its normal state, a character earns a [[point]] for each ball it consumes. A [[Bob-omb]] causes a Chain-Chomp to enter an enraged state, except each [[tennis ball]] becomes worth two points instead. If the red Chain-Chomp is hit by another Bob-omb, it chases the character. In such an occurrence, the player must press {{button|gcn|a}} (or shake the [[Wii Remote]] in the ''[[New Play Control!]]'' version) so that their character reaches a [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]]-like nozzle to calm the Chain-Chomp with a spray of [[water]]. Otherwise, if the character is hit, they lose points. It is possible for a character to affect an opponent's progress by hitting objects at the Chain-Chomp corresponding to them.

Water balls have the ability to calm an angered Chain-Chomp. However, a drawback is that if a water ball hits a Chain-Chomp in a regular state, it falls asleep and stops eating tennis balls. A player can use a Bob-omb to wake up a sleeping Chain-Chomp, returning it to normal.

Once the [[time limit|timer]] runs out, the character who finishes with the most points wins.

===Difficulty levels===
The original [[Nintendo GameCube]] version has a condition for each difficulty level:

*Peckish Chomp (Easy): All characters start with zero points.
*Hungry Chomp (Normal): Opponents have a ten point advantage.
*Famished Chomp (Hard): Opponents have a thirty point advantage.
