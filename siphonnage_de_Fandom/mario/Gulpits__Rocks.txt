'''Gulpits' Rocks''' are an object in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Gulpits' Rocks are featured in the enemy battles against a [[Gulpit]]. To attack, a Gulpit picks up a stone in its mouth and then spits it out. Gulpits' Rocks come in big and small sizes, dealing 7 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 5 HP worth of damage, respectively. When there are no Gulpits' Rocks left, the Gulpit can no longer use them, and it instead attacks by licking [[Mario]].

While they are not enemies, Gulpits' Rocks have one [[Health Point|HP]] and their own [[tattle]].
