The '''Turbo Yoshi''' is a kart which appears in the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]], and it first appears in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. The kart resembles [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]].

== Appearances ==
=== ''Mario Kart'' series ===
==== ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'' ====
[[File:MKDD_Birdo_and_Yoshi_Screenshot.png|thumb|185px|The Turbo Yoshi in action on [[Yoshi Circuit]].]]
Any medium weight character can use the Turbo Yoshi. It has the same stats as [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Heart Coach]] but more weight than it. Its weight is actually shared with [[Waluigi]]'s [[Waluigi Racer]], and its acceleration is smaller.

==== ''Mario Kart Tour'' ====
[[File:MKT_Turbo_Yoshi.jpg|thumb|180px|Yoshi in the Turbo Yoshi, racing with [[Toad (character)|Toad]] and [[Toadette]] on <small>GCN</small> Yoshi Circuit.]]
The Turbo Yoshi returns in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It is classified as a Super kart. The Turbo Yoshi increases the duration of speed boosts. Notable differences with the kart is that it has only one handlebar, and the handlebar joints were moved at the back, so room of the driver's emblem can be placed there. Also, because from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' onward, the series uses only one character per kart, the second platform is no longer present, and instead is replaced with a part to resemble Yoshi's tall. Also, the kart uses black tires instead of red. There are also three variants of this kart; a red variant, simply named the [[Red Turbo Yoshi]], and a blue variant, named the Blue Turbo Yoshi, which are also Super karts. It also has a white variant, called the White Turbo Yoshi, which is a High-End kart.

=== Non-canon appearances ===
==== ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' ====

[[File:Turbo Yoshi SSBU spirit.png|thumb|150px|right|The Turbo Yoshi as a spirit in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''.]]
The Turbo Yoshi makes an appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', as a Advanced-class support spirit.
