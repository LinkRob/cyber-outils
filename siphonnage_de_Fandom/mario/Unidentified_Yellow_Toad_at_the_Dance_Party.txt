The '''Yellow Toad at the Dance Party''' is a minor ''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie''. He is seen wearing a birthday party hat, watching [[Penguin]]s dance. During the dance party, Mario asked the Toad if he's seen Peach, but he just answered "no". Mario saw Peach outside missing out on her party.
