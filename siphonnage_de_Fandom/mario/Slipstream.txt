'''Slipstreaming''', alternatively named '''Drafting''', is a recurring technique in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.

==Description==
If a racer either trails (or tailgates) behind another racer for a certain period of time, their vehicle receives a speed boost, similarly to the [[Mini-Turbo]]. This gives the racer who is slipstreaming the advantage to ram the vehicle behind and race ahead of them.

Some of the Slipstream's other appearances include ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. Slipstreaming also makes its first non-''Mario Kart'' appearance in ''[[Mario Golf: Super Rush]]''.

==In ''Mario Golf: Super Rush''==
During Speed Golf or Battle Golf, if a character runs behind an opponent, blue streaks begin to form around them, followed by them eventually gaining a burst of speed.
