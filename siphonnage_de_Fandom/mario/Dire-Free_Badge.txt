The '''Dire-Free Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be either bought from the [[Gritzy Caves]] for 500 coins or found at the [[Star Shrine]]. Equipping it to either [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], or [[Baby Luigi]] allows him to use an infinite amount of [[Bros. Item]]s if his [[HP]] is below 25%.
