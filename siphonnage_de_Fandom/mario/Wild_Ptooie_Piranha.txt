'''Wild Ptooie Piranhas''' are a variant of [[Piranha Plant]]s and a type of enemy in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming games. Wild Ptooie Piranhas first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. Wild Ptooie Piranhas have a light green head.

==Behavior==
A Wild Ptooie Piranha attacks a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] by spitting three [[Needlenose]]s at him. Just like [[Piranha Plant|Wild Piranha]], if a Yoshi touches a Wild Ptooie Piranha, it catches him in its mouth for a few seconds, triggering the [[Countdown Timer]], before spitting Yoshi back out. A Wild Ptooie Piranha can be attacked by throwing an object at it three times; upon its first hit, Wild Ptooie Piranha turns yellow, then red if hit a second time. A Yoshi can instantly defeat a Wild Ptooie Piranha by rolling a [[Chomp Rock]] onto it. In ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] thrown by [[Baby Donkey Kong|Baby DK]] can also defeat a Wild Ptooie Piranha instantly.
