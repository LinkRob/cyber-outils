<b>Donkey Kong</b> is a character that appears in <i>[[Super Mario Odyssey]]</i>

== Description ==
This 2D version of Donkey Kong acts as a mid-boss in Super Mario Odyssey. He shares the same design as the original [[Donkey Kong]] in the [[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong arcade game]]. He appears twice, once being in [[New Donk City]] during the festival and once in the [[Darker Side]]. In order to defeat him, Mario must go through an obstacle course to reach him. Donkey Kong will attack by throwing barrels at him. These barrels will appear in every section of the obstacle course. Once Mario reaches him, he must hit the four [[? Block|? blocks]] that Donkey Kong is standing on, which will damage him.  Once all four are hit, Donkey Kong will be defeated.

== Trivia ==

* Donkey Kong is the only boss in the game that is fought in a 2D section.
* He is the only enemy in the game that has no 3D variant and only appears in a 2D form.
* He and [[Yoofoe]] are the only two bosses in the game that do not have a cutscene.
* Donkey Kong and [[Yoofoe]] are also the only two bosses that don't have a unique boss theme.
* The fight in [[New Donk City|New Donk City's]] festival resembles the obstacle course in the original [[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong arcade game]].
