'''Captain Shy Guy Trial''' is the tenth level of [[Beanbean Castle]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 35.

==Layout==

Is this level, the only ally on your team is [[Captain Shy Guy]]. However, the player can use Call for Backup to gain more allies. 

The 1st battle contains 3 [[Chuck Guy|Chuck Guys]], and 1 [[Parabeanie]]. The enemy captain is the Parabeanie.

The 2nd battle contains 4 Chuck Guys, and 1 Parabeanie. The enemy captain is the Parabeanie.
