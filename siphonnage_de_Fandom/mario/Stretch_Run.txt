'''Strech Run''', titled '''Stop or Drop''' in the European and Australian versions, is a general mini-game in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

== Opening ==
The [[Toad (species)|Blue Toad]] demonstrates the game as they pull back their carts and release. The Blue Toad falls off the edge while the Yellow Toad stayed far behind the edge.

== Gameplay ==
The player who reaches the furthest earns higher points. If the player stretches it too far results the player receives no points but fall off the edge. It starts with 1, 2, 4 and 6 on Round 1. On Round 2, The numbers change to 1, 3, 6 and 9. The player with the highest points wins the game.

== Controls ==
{{Button|3ds|Stick}} Up/down - Stretch rubber band
