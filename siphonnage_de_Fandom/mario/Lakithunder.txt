{{nihongo|'''Lakithunder'''|ボスジュゲム|Bosujugemu}} is a [[Lakitu]] who rides a black storm [[Lakitu's Cloud|cloud]]. He is the seventh boss of ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', plus serving as the game’s overarching antagonist.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Lakithunder wears black shades with yellow trimmings and a yellow lightning symbol above it in the center, and lacks the three lines of hair on his head that most Lakitus have. His Lakitu Cloud is dark gray with yellow eyes and mouth, having a evil-looking facial expression.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Lakithunder possesses the standard abilities of normal Lakitus, such as hovering about the skies in his cloud and throwing [[Spiny Egg]]s at [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]. Lakithunder, however, gains the ability of electrokinesis, being able to conjure yellow lightning bolts and try to shoot them down at Mario or Luigi.

==In ''New Super Mario Bros.''==
[[File:Lakithunder NSMB sprite.png|frame|left]]
Lakithunder appears as the boss in [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]], meaning that either he or [[Monty Tank]] of [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 6]] must be defeated in order for access to [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 8]], unless the cannon from World 5 is taken.

He attacks by shooting lightning bolts at the player or dropping [[Spiny|Spinies]]. He also tries to attack Mario or Luigi by swooping downwards. This is the chance the two get to pounce on Lakithunder and attack him. The more pounced on, the more aggressive his lightning bolt attacks get, along with a larger amount of Spinies thrown down at Mario or Luigi. Lakithunder must either be jumped on three times or hit with 15 [[fireball]]s to be defeated and therefore surrender his [[key]]. If by chance Mario or Luigi have a [[Mega Mushroom]] in their inventory, they can instantly defeat Lakithunder by touching it while in their [[Mega Mario|Mega]] form.

Lakithunder is also seen at the intro of the game, attacking the [[Princess Peach's Castle|castle]] in order to distract Mario/Luigi so that [[Bowser Jr.]] can abduct [[Princess Peach]].
