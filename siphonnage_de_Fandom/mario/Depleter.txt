The '''Depleter''' is an enemy from [[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope|''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'']]. They first appear on [[Palette Prime]].

== Description ==
Depleters wear red cloaks that cover their entire bodies (sans the face) and dark blue hair that covers their eyes, and their skin is pink. Their icon depicts them with light blue skin. A Depleter moves by floating above the ground as though it were a ghost, much like a [[Peek-a-Boo]].

== Behaviour ==
In battle, a Depleter attacks by throwing a projectile (called Dart of Draining) that inflicts [[Lifesteal]]. It also has its own version of [[Rabbid Mario]]'s Counter Blow, allowing it to counter the heroes' weapons with its Lifesteal projectiles; this ability has a two-turn cooldown. A Depleter can take additional damage from the [[Shock]] [[Super Effect]] but resists [[Splash]].

== Bios ==
A Depleter's Memory bio reads as follows: ''"The Depleter is a curious study - rather than keeping its distance, it gets in close where it can deplete Heroes of their vitality while strengthening itself in the process. When attacked, a special ability allows it to counter immediately. Water bothers them little - indeed, it seems to have an answer for everything, save for Shock-based electrical attacks."'' When analysed with the Tacticam, it yields with the following text: ''"Aggressive opponent that gets in close and restores some of its health at the expense of its opponent's with each successful assault. Its Technique allows it to immediately respond to attacks with a counterattack."''

== Variant: Giant Depleter ==
A giant version of the Depleter can be found on Palette Prime, encountered in the side quest Giant Surprise. It is a larger version of the Depleter covered in toxic [[Darkmess]]. Giant Depleters fight exactly like their smaller brethren, but have higher health and deal more damage with their attacks, resist all [[Super Effect|Super Effects]] except [[Lifesteal]], and cannot be tripped with a Dash attack, and a Giant Depleter's Darts of Draining explode, affecting every character caught in their explosion.

A Giant Depleter has the following Memory bio: ''"Cursa's power is truly incredible, as evidenced during this attack by an enemy that had been enlarged to Giant-sized proportions - and not just due to their tremendous size. A battle analysis reveals that they are immune to ALL Super Effects as well. The amount of energy involved, along with the delicate nature of such amplification would suggest that these oversized enemies are rare."'' When scanned with the Tacticam, it has the following text: ''"Oversize and aggressive opponent that gets in close and restores some of its health at the expense of its opponent's with each successful assault. Its Technique allows it to immediately respond to attacks with a counterattack."''
