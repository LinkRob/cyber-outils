A '''Rainbow M''',<ref>{{cite book|last=|first=|title=''Super Mario Sunshine'' Player's Guide|publisher=[[Nintendo Power]]|page=12|isbn=}}</ref> or '''"M" Teleport''',<ref>{{cite book|last=|first=|title=''Super Mario Sunshine'' Prima's Official Strategy Guide|publisher=[[Prima Games]]|page=12|isbn=}}</ref> is a multicolored [[M Graffiti]] which appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
Rainbow M's are used to teleport [[Mario]] or [[Shadow Mario]] to a specific part of [[Isle Delfino]]. They are a subset of the [[Level Gate]]. To activate a Rainbow M, Mario has to squirt [[water]] on it using [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]], after which it becomes a portal to its corresponding location.

Mario can use a Rainbow M by [[jump]]ing into it. In doing so, he is fragmented into multiple particles before entering, after which he is reassembled at the start of the level. This feature is similarly implemented into a few later ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' titles, particularly ''[[Mario Party 8]]'', whenever a playable character uses [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]], or ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'', when [[Luigi]] is transported through the [[Pixelator]]. 

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
There are three Rainbow M's, all of which are found only in [[Delfino Plaza]]. The first was painted by Shadow Mario to escape Mario after he foiled the former's first attempt to kidnap [[Princess Peach]] in Delfino Plaza. This one was painted on the base of the [[Grand Pianta Statue]], and it leads to [[Bianco Hills]]. Another Rainbow M can be found on the side of the [[boathouse]], leading to [[Ricco Harbor]], and another on the front of the [[lighthouse]], leading to [[Gelato Beach]]. These Rainbow Ms appear only after each [[Polluted Piranha Plant]] covering the buildings have been defeated, respectively.

The [[goop]] that comprises the Rainbow Ms is also found inside two red [[Warp Pipe]]s: one on top of a building located behind the Grand Pianta Statue, where it starts out being plugged off by a large [[pineapple]], and another on top of [[Shine Gate]]. These Warp Pipes provide access to [[Sirena Beach]] and [[Pianta Village]], respectively.
