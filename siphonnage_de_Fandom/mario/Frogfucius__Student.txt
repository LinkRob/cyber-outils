}}'''Frogfucius' Student''' is a character that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Appearance==
Frogfucius' Student is a frog that wears a purple hat and a purple scarf.

==History==
Frogfucius' Student is a disciple of [[Frogfucius]] and he can be found in [[Seaside Town]] on the second floor of the [[Elder]]'s house. He used to live on [[Tadpole Pond]] but decided to leave to explore the world. He can be visited anytime to buy rare items from him in exchange for Frog Coins.
