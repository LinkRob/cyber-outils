'''Signposts''' are objects that appear in various Mario games. They first appeared in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. They give you various information, such as how to do moves like [[Wall Jump|wall jumping]]. Signposts also have names, like Phil Board and Bill Board in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. Signposts also make appearances in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' games]] and help out [[Mario]] by way of giving information on the basics of the game, directing to various locations, or hints on where to go next.

''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' introduced the TV signposts, which give a video tutorial on how to navigate a certain section of a galaxy or level.{{SM64Nav}}
