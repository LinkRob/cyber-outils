== Appearance ==
False Mario is the same appearance as [[Mario]], but he is evil and is green all over.

== Biography ==
The Great Maze is populated by dark clones of the fighters. There are clones for almost every character in the game. They must all be defeated including False Mario, along with every one of the Subspace Emissary's bosses, in order to unlock the door leading to Tabuu.
