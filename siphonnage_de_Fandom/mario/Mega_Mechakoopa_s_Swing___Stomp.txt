'''Mega Mechakoopa's Swing & Stomp''' is a boss minigame featured in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', on the board [[Chaos Castle]]. The minigame involves the characters having to collaborate to defeat a giant [[Mechakoopa]] aptly named [[Mega Mechakoopa]]. Like other boss minigames, whoever has the most [[point]]s by the end is the winner. Whoever deals the finishing attack gains three points.
