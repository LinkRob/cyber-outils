The '''Nerve Cluster''' is an area in [[Bowser's body]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. It can be accessed after going through the [[Pump Works]] area. It is where [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] fight [[Durmite]]. It is also where they find a trapped [[Toadbert]] and cause [[Bowser]] to gain the [[Sliding Haymaker]].
