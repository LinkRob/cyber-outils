A '''Mad Move''' is a term that refers to [[Wario]]'s three most powerful moves in ''[[Wario World]]'': [[Mega Toss]], [[Piledriver]], and [[Wild Swing-Ding]]. Wario can only use a Mad Move when he has picked up an enemy, and several of the bosses can only be damaged from a Mad Move.
