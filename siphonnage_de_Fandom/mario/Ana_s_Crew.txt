'''Ana's Crew''' is a group of five ninjas who assist [[Ana]] during a cutscene of ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. Here, when [[Boneheads]] took over [[Diamond City Castle]], Ana's Crew attempt to attack Boneheads, but all of them were quickly defeated.
