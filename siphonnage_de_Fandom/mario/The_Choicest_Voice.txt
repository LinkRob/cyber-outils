'''The Choicest Voice''', titled '''Soundalike Star Show''' in PAL releases, is an Extra minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[File:The Choicest Voice judges.png|thumb|left|200px|The five judges in The Choicest Voice]]
The Choicest Voice is very similar to [[Utter Nonsense]], as both minigames involve the player recording their voice to imitate a character. Once the player has done so, the character is judged by a [[Goomba]], a [[Koopa Troopa]], [[Shy Guy]], [[Birdo]], and a [[Dry Bones]]. If there are four players, a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] appears as another judge, making for a total of six judges. If a judge likes the recording, the character receives ten points for each sign held. There are a total of three rounds.

The winning criteria depends on how many are playing. In single-player, the character needs at least ninety points to win, and in multiplayer, whoever ends with the most points is the winner.

==Controls==
*Built-in mic: Imitate character

==Flavor text==
*'''Single player rules''' (American English): ''Listen to the character's voice, and then perform your best impression. Score 90 total points after 3 rounds to win.''
*'''Single player rules''' (British English): ''Listen to the characters' voices and do your best impressions! Get a score of over 90 points across three rounds.''
