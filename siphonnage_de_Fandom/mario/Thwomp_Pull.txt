'''Thwomp Pull''' is a 1-vs-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
[[File:MP3 Thwomp Pull Intro.png|200px|thumb|left|The Thwomps at the start of the minigame]]
The minigame takes place in a wintertime forest. One player on a sled has to press the correct buttons to move the three [[Thwomp]]s, while the other three players on sleds are each assigned a button to press and do the same thing together. [[Mr. Blizzard]]s throw large [[snowball]]s at the lane. If either side gets hit by one, its member(s) will be stunned. The first side to reach the [[finish line]] wins. However, if neither side makes it within 60 seconds, the minigame will end in a draw.
{{br|left}}

==Controls==
* '''{{button|n64|A}} / {{button|n64|B}} / {{button|n64|Z}}''' &ndash; Move sled

==In-game text==
* '''Game Rules''' &ndash; ''"Press the buttons the Thwomps tell you to with good timing to move your sled forward."''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"If you press the wrong button, the Thwomps will get confused and pause. Be careful not to make mistakes."''

==Trivia==
*Thwomp Pull and [[Ridiculous Relay]] are the only minigames in the {{series|Mario Party}} where the pause screen controls occupy two pages.
