'''Porcupuffer Falls''' is the second level of [[Rock-Candy Mines]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

==Layout==
The level begins on a cliff platform, with a section of the area submerged in water. A [[Cheep Cheep]] is found, and a [[Warp Pipe]] in the water leads to the next area. The second area contains a [[Porcupuffer]], and begins submerged in water. A platform with a [[Koopa Troopa]] is found above the water, and is followed by an underwater section with yellow Warp Pipes that create [[current]]s. Cheep Cheeps are found, along with a red Warp Pipe creating a current, along with another section of land. In the next underwater section, a [[Red Ring]] is found, along with another row of current-creating Warp Pipes. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] is then found on a platform above the water. More platforms follow, as well as [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]]s flying above the water. A group of [[coin]]s is in the water below a row of [[Donut Block]]s. A section of the water containing multiple [[Urchin]]s follows, and a [[Roulette Block]] can be found. A green Warp Pipe is found at the end of the area, leading to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Near the beginning of the level, the player must hit a [[Hidden Block]] on the left side of the wall or do a [[Wall Jump]] to reach the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the [[Checkpoint Flag]], the second Star Coin is found after a series of [[Urchin]]s.
*'''Star Coin 3''': On top of the [[Roulette Block]], the player must hit a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[Beanstalk]] over the [[Roulette Block]] to reach the third Star Coin.
