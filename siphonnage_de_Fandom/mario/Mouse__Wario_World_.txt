'''Mice''' are enemies in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are large, gray mice about the size of [[Wario]].

===Behavior===
Mice roll left and right in pink wheels. An indefinite number of them appear out of narrow doorways in walls in lowered areas inaccessible to Wario. Because the back of a Mouse's wheel is faced toward the screen, Mice can only be seen from the mirror.

==In ''Wario World''==
Wario only encounters Mice in a few areas of the [[Mirror Mansion]]. If a Mouse's wheel rolls over Wario, he takes damage. Wario can defeat a Mouse by punching it, although to little effect because the defeated Mice are quickly replaced by other ones. Some of the wheels do not have Mice on them, yet they roll on their own.
