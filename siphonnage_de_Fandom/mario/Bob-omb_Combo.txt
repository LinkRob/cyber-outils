'''Bob-omb Combo''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a factory environment with 80 [[Bob-omb]]s moving around.

==Gameplay==
Each character has only one Bob-omb, and their objective is to detonate as many Bob-ombs as possible by throwing their Bob-omb at them. Upon doing so, a chain reaction occurs depending on how many Bob-ombs are in a certain area. During their turn, a character has only ten seconds to throw their Bob-omb, and the timer becomes visible when they have five seconds remaining. Once every character has had their turn, whoever detonated the most Bob-ombs out of all of them is the winner.

This minigame has a [[Reverse Minigame]] variation. Here, the player who detonates the fewest Bob-ombs wins.

In the Chain Reaction challenge, all four characters must get a chain reaction of at least ten Bob-ombs.

*{{button|wii|A}} − Throw Bob-omb
