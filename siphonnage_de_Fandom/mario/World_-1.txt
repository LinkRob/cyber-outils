'''World 36-1''', commonly known as '''World -1''', is a glitched level in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. It takes place in a neverending underwater level. It is impossible to beat, so if you enter, you die. The terrain of the level is the exact same as the one in [[World 2-2 (Super Mario Bros.)|2-2]], but more difficult.

The level can be accessed by clipping into the final pipe in [[World 1-2 (Super Mario Bros.)|1-2]], then going into the pipe on the left without going too far right.
