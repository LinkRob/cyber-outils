'''Obi-Wan Toadi''' is a character who makes a single appearance in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show]]'' episode "[[Star Koopa]]." He is a bearded [[Toad (species)|Mushroom]] space colonist, who appears to run the [[Mushroom Colony]] on the [[Mushroom Earth (DiC Cartoons)|Mushroom Planet]].

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
When [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] arrive at the Mushroom Colony to try and save it from [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|Darth Koopa]]'s [[Birdo Ray]], Princess Toadstool explains to Obi-Wan Toadi that they only had fifteen minutes left before the Mushroom Planet would be destroyed. Although Obi-Wan Toadi concludes that there is not enough time to fight back, Princess Toadstool begs for Obi-Wan Toadi to find a way. Obi-Wan Toadi explains that Mario's group has to penetrate [[Koop Star]] in [[Mushroom Starfighters]] so that someone can disable the Birdo Ray. Mario agrees, under the condition that he will be fed lunch afterwards.

Before Mario takes off, Obi-Wan Toadi can sense that Mario is nervous, and he presents him with a pizza to bolster his spirits (as well as his appetite). After saying "May the pasta be with you" to Mario, Obi-Wan Toadi watches Mario and his friends off, as they fly into space to take down Koop Star.

After Mario and his friends complete their mission, Obi-Wan Toadi, along with the rest of the Mushroom space colonists, congratulate them all upon their return. Mario, however, is more concerned with the lunch that Obi-Wan Toadi had promised him, causing everyone to laugh.

==Trivia==
*The idea of Mario rescuing a space colony was revisited in the game ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' in the "[[Space Land]]" game, though in this case the colony was inhabited by [[Koopas]]. 
*He is a spoof of {{w|Obi-Wan Kenobi}} from the ''{{w|Star Wars}}'' franchise alongside another character, [[Obi-Wan Cannoli]] from the live-action segment, "[[Zenned Out Mario]]".
**One of his quotes, ''"May the pasta be with you,"'' is a pun on the quote ''"May the Force be with you"'' from ''Star Wars.''
