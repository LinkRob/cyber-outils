'''Berlin Byways 1''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. Although its label bears a "1," it is technically the second layout of [[Berlin Byways]].

== Layout ==
Racers on Berlin Byways 1 start next to the Berlin Cathedral, this time facing away from the archway known as the Brandenburg Gate. (According to the map, they face northeast.) After a right turn, they pass by the Berlin Wall, which has gaps in it. [[Whomp]]s fill these gaps and will fall flat on their faces to attack when the racers pass by on their way. The track then takes them down a straight road, one side of which goes under the Oberbaum Bridge. This road then turns to the right and then to the left, with a fork in the road redirecting racers around the Schiller Monument. Both branches meet up aftewards. After the racers go down a short, narrow staircase, they navigate a small dirt path through the Tiergarten, south of the Straße des 17. Juni, before reuniting with it from the south and driving down [[Berlin Byways 2]] in reverse.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', the racers are diverted from [[Berlin Byways 3]] into this road near the end of the second lap of [[Berlin Byways]]. They drive down this layout fully for the final lap. [[Thwomp|Thwomps]] are added to the Brandenburg Gate replacing the [[Glide Ramp]] that was there while the racers drive down Berlin Byways 1, and the Whomps leave cracks in the road.
