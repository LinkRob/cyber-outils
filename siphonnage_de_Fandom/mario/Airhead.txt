'''Airhead''' is one of [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''. 

==Gameplay==
The microgame involves briefly playing air hockey, and the player has to defend their goal from the pucks. If the opponent scores, the player loses.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The player must prevent one puck from entering their goal.
*'''Level 2:''' The player must prevent two pucks from getting entering their goal.
*'''Level 3:''' The object is instead to shoot a puck into the opponent's goal.
