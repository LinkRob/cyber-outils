A '''Zess Cookie''' is an item and recipe that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is named after [[Zess T.]] herself, and she can create it by combining a [[Cake Mix]] with either a [[Gradual Syrup]], a [[Maple Syrup]], or a [[Mystic Egg]].

==Effects==
Eating a Zess Cookie restores 15 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 15 [[Flower Point|FP]], making it a useful recovery item.
