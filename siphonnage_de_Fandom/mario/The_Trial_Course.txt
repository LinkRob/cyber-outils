'''The Trial Course''' is a series of corridors located in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It consists of six doors, each hosting a different challenge: two are obstacle courses; two involve battling enemies; and two are quizzes.
