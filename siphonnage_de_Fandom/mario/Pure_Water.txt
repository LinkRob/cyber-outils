'''Pure Water''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used on ghost enemy to instantly defeat it, although Pure Water has no effect on any other enemy.

For its artwork, Pure Water appears as a blue bottle with a [[Boo]] crossed out on it. In the game, Pure Water has the appearance of blue liquid within a glass jar.
