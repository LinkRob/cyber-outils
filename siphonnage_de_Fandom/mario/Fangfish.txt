'''Fangfish''' is an underwater enemy found in many levels of the world [[Kremlantis]] in ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]''. Its name appears to be a pun on "fang" and "fish." Fangfish also appears to be a purple large sized fish. Like [[Bitesize]]s, [[Chomps]], etc. they only swim back and forth waiting to attack [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]]. Fangfishes, however, are slightly larger than Chomps.
