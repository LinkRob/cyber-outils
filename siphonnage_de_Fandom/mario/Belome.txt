'''Belome''' is a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Belome appears to be a large, dog-like creature with four eyes. He is also yellow in color and has a long tongue that is always shown out.
{{br|left}}
===Personality traits===
Belome has a very large appetite as he always suffers from hunger.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Belome is first seen guarding the exit to [[Kero Sewers]]. Belome is a big threat in the first fight as he always eats [[Mallow]] during a certain point of the fight temporary making a one on on fight with Mario. He can also use a move called [[S'crow Funk]] capable of turning Mario into a [[Scarecrow]]. Fortunately, Belome is weak to Thunder-based attacks so Mallow's Thunderbolt move works well. After Belome is defeated, he activates a switch which causes water to gush out and sends Mario and Mallow onto [[Midas River]].

Belome is seen a second and final time in [[Belome Temple]], guarding the way to [[Monstro Town]]. After solving a brief puzzle, Belome appears again for a battle. This time, Belome is much more deadlier as he can now eat anyone and turn them into clones that fight by his side. He also as a habit of using sleep-themed moves such as [[Aurora Flash]] and [[Lulla-Bye]]. Belome is eventually defeated again and disappears hoping to find more food.
