}}A '''Dark Striker''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Dark Strikers are stronger versions of [[Koopa Striker]]s and can only be found in certain rooms of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Like with Koopa Strikers, Dark Strikers mainly attack by kicking Koopa shells. They are very powerful and their shells can bounce off of walls. However, they can also damage other nearby enemies with their shells.
