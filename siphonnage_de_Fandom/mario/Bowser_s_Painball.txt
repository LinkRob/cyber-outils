'''Bowser's Painball''' is a [[Bowser]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Layout==
The Team [[Mario]] players are in a pinball game controlled by [[Bowser]]. There are also overhead bumpers and a moving wall.

==Gameplay==
The object for Bowser has 30 seconds to shoot [[Spiked Ball]]s to harm the team members. Meanwhile, the members of Team Mario have to avoid Spiked Balls within that time limit. If a character gets hit by a Spiked Ball, they lose a [[heart]]. At first, there is only one Spiked Ball, and after about five seconds, a second ball appears from the left. If a ball falls into the center, the same one reappears after a few seconds. Whoever is controlling Bowser can actually shake the Wii U GamePad to briefly stun the other players, leaving them more open to being hit by Spiked Balls.

==Trivia==
*The "painball" portion of the name is a portmanteau of "pain" and "pinball," with the former word referring to how Spiked Balls are used in place of pinballs.
