[[File:The_Void.png|thumb]]'''The Void''' is an ever consuming force of destruction controlled by the [[Chaos Heart]] which was foretold by the [[Dark Prognosticus]] that it would destroy all of reality. It was summoned by [[Count Bleck]] during the events of ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. In the game, Mario has to obtain several hearts to counter the magic of the Chaos Heart and the Void.{{stub}}
