A '''Courage Shell''' is one of the items in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It specifically appears in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
[[Mario]] or his partners can use this item in battle to increase their defense to a few turns.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Mario can use this item on himself to increase his defense for several seconds depending on how well the action command is performed. Mario can give [[Saffron]] the [[Shell Shock]] to create the Courage Shell.
