'''P-Up, D-Down P''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can use the P-Up, D-Down P to increase the attack power of his party members but at the expense of lowering their [[Defense]] rating 1. Mario can wear this badge by using two [[Badge Point|BP]].

P-Up, D-Down P is found much later in the game. This badge is found in the [[Palace of Shadow]], in the room before the room that follows a pattern and is the only way to proceed.
