'''Micro Golf''' is one of [[Jimmy T]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''

==Gameplay==
The gameplay simply involves having to sink the golf ball into the hole. To accomplish this, the player has to tap anywhere on the Touch Screen once the red arrow is aimed at the hole. If done successfully, the player wins. However, if either time runs out or the player misses, they lose.
