'''Huckit Beach Resort''' is the first [[level]] of [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.

==Features==
The level is a beach area, with huts the player can run on top or inside of. It also features some water spouts that shoot the player up to higher areas. Huckit Crabs are also found here. They normally are placed outside of the beach huts, or on the sides of some walls, making it mildly hard to continue through the course.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Go in the first hut to get the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Go in the pipe under the sixth Huckit Crab revealing a hidden area with a Star Coin in it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In Super form, ground-pound the fourth to last and fifth to last Huckit Crab along with the Brick Blocks to reveal a Star Coin.

==Hidden Luigi Image==
In the area where the second Star Coin, there is a giant picture of a standard Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' swimming.
