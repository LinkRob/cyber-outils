|race = [[Bumpty]]}}'''Mayor Penguin's Wife''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. She is the wife of [[Mayor Penguin]] and they both live on the far west side of [[Shiver City]].

==Appearance==
Mayor Penguin's Wife is a female [[Bumpty]] who is purple in color. She also wears a white apron around her body.

==History==
Mayor Penguin's Wife is first seen right as [[Mario]] and company enters her house. Mayor Penguin's Wife is happy to see visitors and gladly allows them to see her husband. However, she soon walks in onto her husband knocked out on the floor and she suspects that Mario murdered him. She then calls the [[Penguin Police Chief]] to investigate the scene. Even though Mario manages to bring in [[Herringway]] to the scene, Mayor Penguin's Wife still suspects Mario that is the culprit. However, Mayor Penguin suddenly wakes up and a terrified Mayor Penguin's Wife believes he is a ghost. Mayor Penguin explains everything and Mayor Penguin's Wife is relieved. She apologizes to Mario for falsely accusing him and invites him to come back anytime.
