'''Sort of Fun''' is a [[Co-op Minigame|Co-op]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
[[Toad (character)|Toad]] throws any one of the sports balls to the players and they pass it to the other player and the player drops it in the basket.

== Gameplay ==
Players stand on stools, there are baskets that are sorted with four balls. Toad will throw either: a baseball, volleyball, soccer ball, or a basketball to any player and the players must pass it to the player that stands under the matching basket. If the player drops the ball in the wrong basket, the team loses a point.

== Ending ==
The players do their victory animation if they succeed. If they fail, they will do their losing animation.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} (↔) – Pass/Take
*{{button|ns|stick}} (↓) – Drop

==In-game description==
*''"Pass around the balls, and place them where they belong!"''
*''"Any mistakes will cost you a point!"''
