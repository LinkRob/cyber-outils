'''The Final-Final Test Badge Marathon''' is a [[level]] you unlock in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' after achieving a 100% completion rating on every world. It adds to the special world, and after completing it you unlock a [[Poplin House]] with the [[Sound Off?]] Badge which is your final completion award for 100% the game. Afterwards your save file will have a check mark by it, and your game's title screen will be black instead of white.
