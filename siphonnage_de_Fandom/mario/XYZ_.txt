'''XYZ!''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', in which it is one of [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]'s microgames, and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', in which XYZ! is instead one of [[Kat and Ana]]'s microgames.
