'''Winged Strollin' Stus''' are a variant of [[Strollin' Stu]] and an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Winged Strollin' Stu resembles a Strollin' Stu, except it has peach-colored skin, a blue bottom, a pair of wings that rapidly flutter, and no feet.

===Behavior===
At first, a Winged Strollin' Stu hovers idly, but whenever [[Mario]] comes near a Winged Strollin' Stu, it begins to fly towards him. When a Winged Strollin' Stu is above Mario, it swoops down to try an attack him. If hit, Mario loses one segment of his [[Power Meter]]. In secret courses, a Winged Strollin' Stu flies back and forth along a fixed trajectory.

Winged Strollin' Stu have a larger green variant named [[Swipin' Stu]], which is capable of stealing [[Mario's Cap]].

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Winged Strollin' Stus are found only on [[Pinna Park]] beach during the episode [[Red Coins of the Pirate Ships]], in [[Hotel Delfino]]'s [[The Hotel Lobby's Secret|secret course]], and on the [[Delfino Airstrip]] after the flood. Like other Strollin' Stus, a Winged Strollin' Stu can be defeated from either being squirted by [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]] or [[stomp]]ed on.
