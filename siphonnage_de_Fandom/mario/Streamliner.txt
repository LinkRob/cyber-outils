The '''Streamliner''' is a recurring [[kart]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

== Description ==
A Streamliner is blue with a white stripe in the middle, equipped four large tires and two dual gray exhaust pipes at the back. A Streamliner resembles a 1970s {{w|Can-Am}} prototype

The Streamliner went on to receive two lookalikes, the [[Jetsetter]] from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' and the [[Blue Seven]] from ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', but each of them are heavier than the Streamliner and have different stats.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart DS]]''===
[[File:MKDS Streamliner.png|thumb|left|Luigi in his Streamliner]]
The Streamliner is one of [[Luigi]]'s three signature karts, along with the [[Poltergust 4000]] and the [[Standard LG]]. The Streamliner is the heaviest of Luigi's three karts, but it has a higher [[Items (stat)|Items]] stat. As with all of Luigi's karts, the Streamliner has an excellent top speed, but poor drifting.

The Streamliner shares its speed with the [[Zipper]], its weight is equivalent to the [[B Dasher]], and its handling is shared with the [[Mushmellow]] and [[ROB-BLS]].

The Streamliner can be unlocked once the player completes all cups in the 100cc Nitro Grand Prix, or the 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix if the player has not completed 100cc. It can also be used by [[Dry Bones]] and [[R.O.B.]] that way, but only if they are unlocked. All characters can use this kart once all cups in the Mirror Class have been completed.

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
[[File:MKT Toad Pit Crew.jpg|thumb|[[Toad (Pit Crew)]] tricking off the Streamliner]]
The Streamliner makes an appearance in ''Mario Kart Tour''. It is classified as a Super kart. The game also introduces three variants of this kart: a red variant simply named the [[Red Streamliner]]. It is also classified as a Super kart. The second variant is a dark blue variant with decals known as the [[Decal Streamliner]], which is classified as a High-End kart. The third variant is an orange variant, decals, called the [[Orange Streamliner]], which is also classified as a High-End kart.
