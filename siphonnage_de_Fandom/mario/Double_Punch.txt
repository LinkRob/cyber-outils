The '''Double Punch''' is one of [[Geno]]'s personal weapons in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are rocket-propelled fists that can be launched at a target. It can be bought from the weapon shop in [[Seaside Town]] for 88 [[coin]]s. Equipping the item increases Geno's [[Attack]] power by 35.
