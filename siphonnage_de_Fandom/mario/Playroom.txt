The '''Playroom''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
In order to access the Playroom, [[Mario]] must head to the far southern area of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and hammer a tree to make a [[Warp Pipe]] appear. After entering the warp pipe, Mario is greeted by a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who tells him about the room. In order to play the games in the Playroom, Mario must have the [[Silver Credit]] and the [[Gold Credit]]. Both of these can be obtained by completing certain favors for [[Koopa Koot]].
