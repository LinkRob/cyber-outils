The '''Bitsize Candy''' is a yellow [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Bitsize Candy.png|thumb|left|In-game graphic]]
The Bitsize Candy depicts a bit-sized [[Mario]] on its wrapper. When a player uses it, their character transforms into a pixelated rendition of themselves. Some of the characters are designed after their appearance in an early 2D title of the {{series|Super Mario}}: Mario, [[Blooper]], and [[Hammer Bro]] resemble their sprites from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''; [[Dry Bones]] is based on its look in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''; and [[Boo]] takes its appearance from ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

While in "Bitsize" form, the character hits a [[Coin Block]] containing three [[coin]]s for every [[space]] they move. Consequently, a single die roll can yield anywhere from three to 30 coins.

Functionally, the Bitsize Candy is similar to the [[Flower Orb]] from ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Mario Party 8''==
The Bitsize Candy can be found on [[DK's Treetop Temple]], [[King Boo's Haunted Hideaway]], [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]], and [[Koopa's Tycoon Town]]. It cannot be bought at any [[Candy Shop]]. The Bitsize Candy is also the only yellow Candy that appears in King Boo's Haunted Hideaway.

==Behind the scenes==
Even though the characters look like sprites in their Bitsize form, they are actually rendered as models.

===Etymology===
The candy's name is a pun on the term "bite-sized" and its function of turning characters into "8-bit" versions.
