|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Iron Maiden''' is a special move used by a few enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Iron Maiden is used, a few transparent circles appear at the victim.

==Effects==
With Iron Maiden, the foe is able to inflict the [[Fear]] ailment onto one of Mario's party. This move can be canceled out by wearing a Fearless Pin.
