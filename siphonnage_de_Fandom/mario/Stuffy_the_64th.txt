'''Stuffy the 64th''' is a boss that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. He is the 64th ruler of the Dolphinian empire and serves as the main boss of [[Blowhole Castle]]. He eventually formed a truce between [[Carpaccio]] and [[Count Cannoli]] in order to bring down [[Wario]].
