'''Mario Bros.''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. It is featured as part of [[18-Volt]]'s stage. It is set within the game of the same name, ''[[Mario Bros. (game)|Mario Bros.]]''

== Gameplay ==
In this game, the player has to flip over the onscreen enemies, which are [[Shellcreeper]]s and [[Sidestepper]]s. This is simply done by having [[Mario]] strike them from underneath the platforms, just like in ''Mario Bros.'' As usual, Shellcreepers are flipped over once, while Sidesteppers take two strikes to be flipped over. If the player fails to flip all of the enemies, the game ends in failure.
