'''Bombard King Bob-omb''', titled '''Bombard Big Bob-omb''' in PAL releases, is a Stage Boss battle mini-game in ''​[[Mario Party 9]]''. ​In 'Solo' mode, [[King Bob-omb]], named Big Bob-omb in the PAL version, appears in [[Bob-omb Factory]] as the boss.

==Intro==
When the minigame starts, [[King Bob-omb]] will ride a platform, which stops at such a height that his moustache is at the same level as the platform the players are standing on.

==​Gameplay==
This game has probability; in this game, you should select a Bob-omb with {{Button|Wii|Pad}}, ​{{Button|Wii|A}}​, {{Button|Wii|1}}, or {{Button|Wii|2}}. Each Bob-omb is worth a different number of points:

*Large Bob-omb is worth +3 points.
*Medium Bob-omb is worth +2 points.
*Small Bob-omb is worth +1 point.

Be careful not to choose the same Bob-omb! This consequence will be the player bumps to another player or more; all of you receive no points. If all players bump into each other, King Bob-omb jumps up and down in victory.

===​​Rage===
When Big Bob-omb rages, whenever you choose the same Bob-omb as another player, you end up bumping a player and he tosses the [[Bob-omb]] and you both or more of you loses -1 point.

=== With only two players ===
In the case where only two players are in the heroic party, King Bob-omb can choose his own bomb, removing it from play if nobody picks it. If his pick matches that of a player, King Bob-omb simply steal it from them. During the second phase of the battle, choosing the king's pick will cause him to throw the bomb onto the player who chose it, effectively reflecting the damage back to them. The chosen Bob-omb is random.

== Ending ==
Once King Bob-omb is defeated, he falls onto his back and explodes, revealing several Mini Stars in Party Mode and Solo Mode.
