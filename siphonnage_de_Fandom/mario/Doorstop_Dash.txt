'''Doorstop Dash''', (called シャッターをかけぬけろ ''Shattā o Kakenukero'' in Japan), is the second level appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'' and [[Lake Orangatanga]]. It comes after [[Lakeside Limbo]] and before [[Tidal Trouble]].

==Overview==
In this level, [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] and [[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] find a lot of doorways with metallic blocked passages. The two must lift it by pulling onto a lever to advance. Though, this is limited and thus the two monkeys have to be quick with good timing. The enemies here are eleven [[Sneek]]s, sixteen green [[Buzz]]es and a lone [[Koin]].
