'''Cliff Spring Cave''' is a secret area found in [[Bianco Hills]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', specifically on the northeastern side of the lake. Two of Bianco Hills's episodes take place here, [[The Secret of the Dirty Lake]] and [[Red Coins of the Dirty Lake]], and both involve [[Mario]] having to jump from several [[Rotating Panel]]s.
