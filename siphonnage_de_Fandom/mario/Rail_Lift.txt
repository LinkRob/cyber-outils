'''Rail Lifts''' are an object which first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. They are also featured in the [[microgame]] of the [[Super Mario Bros. 3 (lift microgame)|same name]] in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Description==
Rail Lifts are wooden [[lift]]s that move along a [[track]]. By [[jump]]ing on a Rail Lift, [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can have it move them around while standing on it.
