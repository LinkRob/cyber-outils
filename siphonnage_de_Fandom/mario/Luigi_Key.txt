The '''Luigi Key''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
The Luigi Key is a golden [[key]] with an "L" [[emblem]] in the center of the ring as well as a smaller version of [[Luigi Cap|Luigi's Cap]] on top. It is used to unlock the [[door]] to Luigi's [[switching room]] in the [[Princess Peach's room|Princess's room]] and therefore Luigi as a playable character.

==In ''Super Mario 64 DS''==
The Luigi Key was used by [[King Boo|Big Boo]] to trap Luigi behind a switching room in the Mushroom Castle after he had captured Luigi. The Luigi Key is the second character key that is obtained. To obtain the Luigi Key, [[Mario]] has to defeat Big Boo in [[Big Boo Battle]].
