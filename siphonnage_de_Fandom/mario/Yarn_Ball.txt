[[File:Yarn_Ball.jpg|thumb]]'''Yarn Balls''' are the main type of projectile found in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. They serve as a replacement for the [[Yoshi Egg]]s found in many other games in the Yoshi series. They can only be obtained by eating an enemy or obtaining them from [[Yarn Block]]s. A larger version of the Yarn Balls, called the [[Big Yarn Ball]], can also occasionally be obtained in levels.
