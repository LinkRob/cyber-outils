'''Spin Smash''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
Spin Smash is given to [[Mario]] by way of [[Rowf's Badge Shop]], where it can be bought for 75 [[coin]]s. While wearing the Spin Smash, when Mario succeeds in a [[hammer]] attack on a target, the others take minor damage because the target is hit backward. To wear the Spin Smash, Mario must pay [[Badge Point|BP]], but to attack he must use up two [[Flower Point|FP]].
