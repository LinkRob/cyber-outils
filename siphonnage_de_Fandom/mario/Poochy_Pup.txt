'''Poochy Pups''' are three younger variants of [[Poochy]]. They first appear in [[Yoshi's Woolly World]].

==Trivia==
* Poochy Pups can also be found all over the Yoshi's Crafted World [https://yoshiscraftedworld.nintendo.com/ website]. They are sometimes at the bottom of the page and can award you 50 Platinum Coins.
