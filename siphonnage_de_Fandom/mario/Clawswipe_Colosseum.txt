'''Clawswipe Colosseum''' is one of the islands located at [[Lake Lapcat]] in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
Clawswipe Colosseum is one of the few islands surrounding the Ruins [[Giga Bell]]. It is a large colosseum that grows two stories if the player collects the first and second [[Cat Shine]]s.

Clawswipe Colosseum features boss fights against [[Cat Boom Boom]] and [[Cat Pom Pom]]. As for regular enemies, it has [[Cat Bob-omb]]s, [[Cat Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Baddie Box]]es, and [[Mega ? Block]]s. In addition, it contains Cat variations of [[Bob-omb]]s and [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Baddie Box]]es, and expanding [[Mega ? Block]]s.
