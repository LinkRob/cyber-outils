'''Red Switches''', or '''Red ! Switches''', are recurring items in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Unlike [[Blue Switch]]es, which are used to open up hidden parts of the current location [[Mario]] is in, Red Switches are used to solve more intricately designed puzzles.
