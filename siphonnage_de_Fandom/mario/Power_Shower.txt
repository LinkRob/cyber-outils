The '''Power Shower''' is an object in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Oh, Brother!]]"

==Description==
The Power Shower is a system of pipes and shower heads designed to create an artificial rainstorm, particularly in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. It was created by [[Kooky von Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|Kooky von Koopa]]. The top of the Power Shower is situated above the Mushroom Kingdom and is supplied water through a long pipe extending from underground.

==Episode appearances==
==="Oh, Brother!"===
The Power Shower was activated by Kooky two weeks prior to the events of the episode. His intention behind using the Power Shower is for rain to pour through the Mushroom Kingdom and down through a [[Warp Pipe]] to the [[Pipe Land|Pipe World]], where any loose [[Coin|Gold Coins]] would be flushed out and washed into a basin in one of [[Bowser]]'s dungeons.

When a pipe in the dungeon that supplies water to the Power Shower has burst, Bowser ordered Kooky to fetch [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] so that he can fix it. After being put under the influence of the Lame Brainer, Mario willfully fixes the leak in the Power Shower. [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] eventually comes along to save Mario, and the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] dismantle the Power Shower.
