The '''Batting Helmet''' is an item introduced in the version 1.2.0 update of ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

==Description==
The Batting Helmet is a red baseball helmet with [[Mario]]'s "M" [[emblem]] on the front, indicating that it is an outfit that can be worn by him. The Batting Helmet is frequently paired with the [[Baseball Uniform]].

==In ''Super Mario Odyssey''==
The Batting Helmet becomes available to purchase at [[Crazy Cap]] for 1,500 [[coin]]s after completing the main game.

==In ''Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit''==
The Batting Helmet makes a cameo appearance in the course icon of the [[Mushroom Fields]], which shows a rear view of Mario wearing it and the Baseball Uniform while driving through the course.
