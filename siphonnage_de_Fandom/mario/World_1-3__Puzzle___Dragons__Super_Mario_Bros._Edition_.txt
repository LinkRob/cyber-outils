'''World 1-3''' is the third [[level]] in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. This level introduces [[? Block]]s to the player, as well as branching paths. These require you to clear certain orbs in order to go your desired path. As such, this level can be a little bit more complicated.

== Encounters ==
Note: Enemies listed in bold will always appear, while enemies that are italicized only sometimes appear.

*Encounter 1: '''1 [[Paragoomba]]''', ''1 Red [[Koopa Troopa]], 1 Green Koopa Troopa'' (only one of the of the latter two options appear)
*Encounter 2: Branching Path: Go around, (Water) Pipe (Fire)
*Encounter 3: (Pipe) ? Block. 
*Encounter 3: (Around) '''2 Red Koopa Troopas'''
*Encounter 4: (Pipe) Pipe Exit.
*Encounter 4: (Around) '''2 Green Koopa Troopas'''
*Encounter 5: (Boss) '''1''' '''Red Koopa Troopa,''' '''1 Paragoomba, 1 [[Goomba]]'''

== Items ==
*[[Orb (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|Orbs]]: Fire, Water, Wood and Heart.
*? Block Items: [[1-Up Mushroom]] or 10 [[Coin]]s.
