'''Bowser Fang''' is a [[badge]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is dropped by [[Roy Koopa]] upon defeating him in battle. It increases [[Bros. Point]]s by 50, although Japanese versions instead increase [[POW]] by said number. In the Nintendo 3DS version, Bowser Fang increases POW by 64.
