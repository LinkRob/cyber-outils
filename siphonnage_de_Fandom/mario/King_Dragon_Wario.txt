'''King Dragon Wario''' is a power-up of [[Wario]]'s which appears in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''.

==Description==
Wario can become King Dragon Wario after finding both a [[Eagle Statue]] and [[Dragon Crystal]]. As King Dragon Wario, he can fly a far distance and shoot a flame projectile capable of surpassing most obstacles. Wario is heavier in this form, and he cannot [[Dash Attack|Barge]].
