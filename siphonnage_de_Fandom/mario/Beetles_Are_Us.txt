'''Beetles Are Us''' is a store in [[Seaside Town]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is run by a [[Snifit (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Snifit]] who buys [[Beetle (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Beetles]] from [[Mario]]. For 50 [[coin]]s, Mario can buy a [[Beetle Box]], which is required to play the [[Beetle Race]] [[minigame]] on [[Booster Hill]].

==Trivia==
*The name is a reference to the American retailer 'Toys "R" Us.'
