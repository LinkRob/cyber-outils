'''Wii Music''' is a 2008 video game created by [[Nintendo]] for the [[Wii]]. It allows users to create arrangements of various songs by mimicking the use of instruments using a [[Wii Remote]] and [[Nunchuk]].

==Connections to ''Mario''==
The [[Ground Theme]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' is one of the songs featured in the game.
