'''World 8-7''' is a secret level in [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. This level can only be accessed if [[World 8-2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-2's]] Secret Exit is completed. 

== Overview ==
This level features the [[Bonecoaster]]s' first and only appearance, and is populated by several [[Lava Plume]]s. There are no enemies in the level. Unlike [[World 8-2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-2]] and [[World 8-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-3]], this level uses an edited version of the default underground background.
== Walkthrough ==
[[Mario]] starts the level in a lava-filled cavern where he must jump on a Bonecoaster, which later leads to another Bonecoaster. The Bonecoaster leads Mario to a [[Warp Pipe]], which leads to an above-ground area with the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] and to another Bonecoaster. After three more Bonecoasters, the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]] awaits at the end. The Bonecoasters often make risky maneuvers, passing through Lava Plumes and the lava itself.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': After boarding the second [[Bonecoaster]], the first Star Coin is floating just above the [[lava]]. Mario and co. must obtain it by jumping at just the right time.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]], the second Star Coin is floating next to two Lava Plumes, and must be obtained by jumping over them.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin is floating in between the two tracks of the two Bonecoasters that are ridden simultaneously. Mario and co. must ride the lower Bonecoaster to it, then immediately jump up to the higher one, obtaining the Star Coin and avoiding the Lava Plumes.
