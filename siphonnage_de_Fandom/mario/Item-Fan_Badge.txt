The '''Item-Fan Badge''' is a [[badge]] item featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be obtained either from [[Peach's Castle]] or as an item dropped by [[Bowser Jr.]] during his battle in the [[Thwomp Volcano]]. Equipping the Item-Fan Badge increases [[Bros. Item]] power, although lowers [[Jump]] and [[Hammer]] power.
