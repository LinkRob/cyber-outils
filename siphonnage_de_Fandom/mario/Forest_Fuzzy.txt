A '''Forest Fuzzy''' is an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Profile==
Forest Fuzzies are a green variant of [[Fuzzy]] native only to the [[Forever Forest]]. They are slightly faster than regular Fuzzies, and are usually accompanied by [[Piranha Plant]]s. Like their regular counterpart, Forest Fuzzies attack [[Mario]] by sapping his health. The main difference between a Forest Fuzzy and a regular Fuzzy is [[Speed]] and [[HP]]. Forest Fuzzies have the ability to divide when their HP is low.
