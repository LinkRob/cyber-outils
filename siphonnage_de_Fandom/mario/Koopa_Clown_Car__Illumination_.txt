'''Koopa Clown Cars''' are flying vehicles that appear in [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|''The Super Mario Bros Movie'' film series]], used by [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]'s army to capture the Kong Army, after [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]]'s and [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]]'s presumed death as part of the [[Rainbow Road (Illumination)|Rainbow Road]] collapsed. They were later used as defense cannons to attack from [[Bowser's Castle (Illumination)|Bowser's Castle]]. [[Bowser Jr. (Illumination)|Bowser Jr.]] Eventually used on to intrude [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Peach]]'s castle and rescue his father, from Mario and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]].
