'''Dual Dragon''' is the [[boss]] of [[Spooktastic World]] in ''[[Wario World]]'' as well as the second boss overall.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Dual Dragon is a large, red, two-headed dragon.

===Behavior===
[[File:Dual Dragon Glue Globes.png|thumb|left|250px|One of the heads spitting Glue Globes.]]
Dual Dragon has three attacks. One of its attacks is to spew out [[Glue Globe]]s; a few seconds after one head spits out the Globes, the opposite head inhales them. Another attack of Dual Dragon is attempting to hit [[Wario]] with its tails if he gets too close. Its third attack is [[Fire Breath|breathing fire]], which covers the entire top half of the arena. Normally, when one head breathes fire, the other moves out of the way. Later into the battle, embers begin to erupt from the fire stream and land on the bottom half of the arena.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Wario World''==
[[File:Dual Dragon fire.png|thumb|Wario fighting the Dual Dragon.]]
The battle takes place in the third and final stage, '''Dual Dragon's Showdown''', a swamp located inside a dark forest, unlocked after completing [[Wonky Circus]]. [[Wario]] must defeat Dual Dragon in order to earn a piece of the [[Huge Treasure Chest Key]] and unlock [[Thrillsville]].

While battling Dual Dragon, Wario must avoid being stuck to any of the Glue Globes so that he is not dragged into the harmful [[water]]. To damage Dual Dragon, Wario must wait for one of its head to breathe fire then knock the other unconscious by attacking it repeatedly; as a result, the Dual Dragon gets hit by its own fire, removing one skull from its health meter. After five hits, Dual Dragon is defeated, sinking into the water as a result, and Wario obtains the second piece of the Huge Treasure Chest Key.
