'''''Mario Party: Star Rush''''' is a game in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]] on the [[Nintendo 3DS]]. The game is announced to include new gameplay changes to make the game more portable-friendly, including all the players moving at once instead of having to wait for their turn. The game also puts emphasis on the character [[Toad (character)|Toad]].

It was released in Europe on October 7, 2016, in Australia on October 8, 2016, in Japan, China, Hong Kong, & Taiwan on October 20, 2016, in North America on November 4, 2016, and in South Korea on April 27, 2017.

==Trivia==
*This is the first ''Mario Party'' game that their 4 colors correspond to each player, (which is Player 1 is {{color|red|red}}, Player 2 is {{color|blue|blue}}, Player 3 is {{color|green|green}}, and Player 4 is {{color|yellow|yellow}}) since, ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.
* This is the third Mario Party game to not feature 1-vs-3 or 2-vs-2 minigames, the first being ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.
*This is the second of the three ''Mario Party'' games for the 3DS. The first is ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'' and the next is ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.
*This is the only ''Mario Party'' game where there is a mode where your character is default. In ''Mario Party: Star Rush'', that mode is Toad Scramble.
*Not counting [[Mario Party|the]] [[Mario Party 2|first]] [[Mario Party 3|four]] [[Mario Party 4|installments]] --in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party series]]''-- this is the last later installment in the series (after ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 6|6]]'') to have [[Toad]] and [[Toadette]] as playable characters until ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.
*This is the first (and so far only) ''Mario Party Game'' to not feature any [[Whomp]]s.
*This is the last installment in the series to introduce a protagonist newcomer as a playable character ([[Diddy Kong]]). 
**[[Rosalina]] was introduced in the home console game ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.
