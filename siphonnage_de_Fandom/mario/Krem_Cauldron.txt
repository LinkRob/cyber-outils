'''Krem Cauldron''' is the second world which appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''. It appears to be a fusion between the two worlds [[Crocodile Cauldron]] and [[Krem Quay]]. The boss of the world is [[Kleever]], meaning [[Kudgel]] doesn't appear in the game itself.

This place appears to have the sunken ship from Krem Quay on the right side and the swampy marshes on the left side of the world. The volcanic part is on the right side with some volcanoes.

This world has five levels from Crocodile Cauldron and five from Krem Quay, tallying up the total amount of levels to ten and a boss battle with Kleever. This makes this world have the most levels in the game itself. The level [[Bramble Blast (Donkey Kong Land 2)|Bramble Blast]], though, has been relocated to [[Krazy Kremland]] as the third level in that world.

It is unknown how the two worlds merged, but the fanbase mainly speculate that after Crocodile Isle rose from the water, the two worlds seemed to have somehow merged. Or, this is because of the many limitations of the [[Game Boy]] itself.
