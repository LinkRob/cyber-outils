'''Unithorns''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Unithorns are light purple, partially transparent, rhinoceros-like floating heads.

===Behaviors===
A Unithorn tries to bite [[Wario]] and steal his [[coin]]s. Unithorns have a fear of [[bomb]]s, and they become stunned for a fraction of a second whenever either Wario uncovers one or when the bomb explodes. One of a few icons can appear above a Unithorn's head to indicate its mood: a note, if it successfully bites Wario; a sad face, if Wario uncovers a bomb; an exclamation mark, if stunned by a bomb; and a question mark, if Wario dodges one of its attacks.

===Habitats===
Unithorns are only encountered in [[Unithorn's Lair]], an underground area where [[Wario]] lands if he falls down a [[pit]] in one of the courses.

==In ''Wario World''==
Wario cannot defeat Unithorns, and he instead has to avoid them and eventually escape from their lair by breaking the [[crate]]s until he finds the one with an [[Escape Spring]].
