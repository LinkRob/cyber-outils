'''World 2''' is the second world of the game ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', following [[World 1 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 1]]. The world has a desert and pyramid theme to it, which is later revisited in [[World 6 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]]. It is in this world that the player meets for the first time the [[Cobrat]] [[Pokey]] and [[Panser]] enemies, as well as their first boss fight against the red [[Birdo]] the can spit fireballs as well as [[Birdo Egg|eggs]]. The main boss of the world, fought at the end of World 2-3 and before [[World 3 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]], is [[Tryclyde]], the three-headed snake.

==Levels==
===World 2-1===
This level is divided into two segments. In the first, the chosen character has to walk through the sandy and silvester desert as he or she avoids traps like quicksand and new enemies, such as [[Pokey]] cacti, [[Cobrat]] snakes, and numerous [[Panser]]s. After that, a pyramid is explored; the character has to dig through sand until reaching the bottom. [[Birdo]] is fought in a chamber full of chasms, so caution is the [[key]] in the battle.

===World 2-2===
Very similar to the first level, outside exploration is first, followed by an underground cavern. There is also an additional cavern where an extra life can be gained. In the first cavern, the player can get a [[Potion|magic potion]]. After the cavern there are six vases. Five of them are filled with red turtle shells and the other vase has a [[Small Heart|heart]] and a [[POW Block|POW block]]. Then there is a large pit filled with [[Shy Guy]]s. After going down the large pit there is a door that leads to a Birdo that shoots both fireballs and eggs. This is the first time Birdo can do that (although [[Mushroom Block]]s can be used instead to battle her here).

===World 2-3===
If the player plays as [[Luigi]], there is a cave right at the beginning of the level, that only Luigi can jump high enough to get to, that leads to a magic potion and a bunch of [[coin]]s. The rest of the level is not too different from its two predecessors. The difference is that, once inside the pyramid, the chosen character must first fall through a hole. Then dig to the bottom to find a key that opens the door showing the path that leads to the world's boss, [[Tryclyde]], before advancing to [[World 3 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]].
