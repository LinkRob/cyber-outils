'''Lee''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. He is a [[Duplighost]] that is found in the [[Toad Town Dojo|Dojo]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]].

==History==
Like [[Chan]], Lee can use the abilities of his species in general. He can transform into one of [[Mario]]'s partners, making him a tricky enemy to come by. If Lee uses [[Goombario]]'s abilities, he can use [[Tattle]] on Mario, and uses it to mock Mario. When he is using the abilities of Mario's partners, he can even succumb to their weaknesses even.

After Lee has been defeated in battle, [[The Master]] gives Mario the [[Second Degree Card]].
