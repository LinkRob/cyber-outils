'''Cheep Cheep Falls''' is a golf course in ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]''.

==Description==
Cheep Cheep Falls is a basic golf course with no exaggerated gimmicks, similar to [[Lakitu Valley]], but includes more bunkers and water. Pine trees appear throughout the golf course, a waterfall can be seen in a few holes, and there are snow-capped mountains in the background. Despite its name, Cheep Cheep Falls does not feature any [[Cheep Cheep]]s, a similar situation to Lakitu Valley, in which there are no [[Lakitu]]s.
