'''Magic carpets''' are objects in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. They only appear in [[Flying-Carpet Cruise]] where there are a variety of carpets by color and size. All kinds have arrows on them pointing left and right and standing on one side causes the carpet to move in that direction, similar to [[Switchboard]]s. If [[Yoshi]] jumps off of it, it floats upwards, and if he crouches while on it, it floats downwards. One carpet requires a [[yarn ball]] to be thrown at it to be usable.
