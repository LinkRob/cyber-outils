A '''Scuttle''' is a recurring move used by [[Luigi]] in various {{series|Super Mario}} games as well as later {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} installments. The move first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==Description==
Scuttling involves Luigi kicking his legs while in midair, and it can slow his descent.

Since ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] have used a similar move in their appearances named [[Flutter Jump]], but the main difference is that this move propels them slightly upward.
