'''Sugar Rush''' is a [[microgame]] in [[Ashley and Red]]'s [[Total Drag]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to drag and pull the child's tongue to the candy pieces to the right. Releasing it, the player snaps the candies into the child's mouth. If this objective is not fulfilled, the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The child has to eat three pieces of candy.
*'''Level 2:''' Same as Level 1, but the pieces of candy move.
*'''Level 3:''' The child has to eat five pieces of candy.

==Trivia==
*The microgame name is shared with the phrase "sugar rush," which refers to a person becoming hyperactive as a result of eating too many candy.
*The child in Sugar Rush appears in Level 2 of the ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'' microgame [[Wayward Water]], where it drinks the water from the pipes after the player clears it.
