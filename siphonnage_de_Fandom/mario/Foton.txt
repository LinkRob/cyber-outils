}}A '''Foton''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Fotons are found throughout [[Outer Space]] and [[Outer Limits]] as very common enemies. Fotons are very strange enemies allowing themselves to shrink and grow at will. They mainly attack by firing small particles at the heroes. They can be easily dispatched of with [[Squirps]]' lasers. Fotons may drop [[Shooting Star (item)|Shooting Stars]] upon defeat.

==Trivia==
*During the early production, Fotons were going to appear in [[Fort Francis]].
