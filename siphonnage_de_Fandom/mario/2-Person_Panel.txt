The '''2-Person Panel''' is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.

==Description==
The 2-Person Panel is a yellow ground switch depicting two squares. It is a rare variation of the [[4-Person Panel]] that only requires two characters to stand on it to activate, specifically either the adults, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], or the babies, [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]]. When a 2-Person Panel is activated, a nearby door is opened.

They are only found in the [[Vim Factory]]. They have two squares on them, meaning that either [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] or [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] must stand on the panel, in order to open a nearby door. They are a variation of the blue [[4-Person Panel]]. 

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''==
While 2-Person Panels only appear in [[Vim Factory]], they have a more common [[Trampoline]] variation, first found inside the hollow trunks of [[Toadwood Forest]]. These trampolines can bounce Baby Mario and Baby Luigi into a different area, but because of their small size, these trampolines deflate if Mario and Luigi jump on them.
