The '''Travel Diary''' is an object in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
The Travel Diary is a book in the [[Duty-Free Shop]] that records game statistics. There are four types of records: Party Cruise Records, MiniGame Records, Decathlon Castle Records, and Staff Records. The Party Cruise Records keep track of the total play time and how many times each [[board]] has been played. The MiniGame Records keep track of the new records in certain mini-games. The Decathlon Castle Records keep track of records in the Decathlon Castle and how many [[point]]s have been obtained in the mini-games. Finally, the Staff Records keep track of records held by the game's staff.
