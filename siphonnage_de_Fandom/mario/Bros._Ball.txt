The '''Bros. Ball''' is a move learned by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It is taught to the Bros. when the player returns to [[Princess Peach's Castle]] after the defeat of [[Sunnycide]] inside [[Yoob]]. The move makes a return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', though its appearance is referenced in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' with the [[Ball Hop]].

==History==
The move is taught to the Bros. and by [[Toadsworth]] and [[Toadsworth the Younger]] in an attempt to calm down [[Baby Peach]] in [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. The move requires the Bros. to combine together in such a position where they resemble a ball. In their combined state, the Bros. look like a beach ball, with white spots on each side and sections in red, blue, green, and brown. The Bros. Ball allows them to move faster, enter passages once inaccessible, and hit certain switches. Another one of its functions is to [[Baby Cakes|flatten]] [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]], allowing them to bypass walls as long as there is a very small gap.

''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' introduces the [[Ball Hop]], which seems to be an offshoot of the Bros. Ball used to jump up drops that cannot be surmounted with a regular jump.

The move returns in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', where the Bros. learn it on [[Lottacoins Island]] and need an escape route. It can now brake and move slowly, and is used to travel along rails. Additionally, the Bros. and their copies merge into a giant wheel that seems to be a bigger version of the Bros. Ball and rips through all enemies in combat in the new version of the [[Yoo Who Cannon]] used in this game.

== Ball Blitz ==
The Bros. Ball later gets upgraded with the Ball Blitz technique. This is executed by holding X while using the Bros. Ball, and ten releasing the button. This done, the ball dashes quickly. This can be used to climb slopes that cannot be climbed easily and is learned in [[Slippenglide Island]] where the Bros. are stopped by a powerful gust of wind that acts as a wall from one direction.
