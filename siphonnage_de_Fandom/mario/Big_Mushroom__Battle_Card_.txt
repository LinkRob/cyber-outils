|name=Big Mushroom (Battle Card)}}'''Big Mushroom''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Mushroom is the strong version of [[Mario]]'s healing ability. It is a big red and white speckled mushroom with an orange/tan checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 70 coins (painted)

Sell Price: 35 coins (painted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Mushroom is first seen in [[Plum Park]], but can also found in [[Cobalt Base]], [[Violet Passage]], [[Mustard Café]], [[Sunset Express]], [[Green Energy Plant]], and [[The Emerald Circus]].
