The '''Aldegara Waterfall''' is a location in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. [[Wario]] uses his [[Bulldog]] to travel here while on a vacation at the [[Awazon River Basin]], and it is where he notices three [[Mask-Guy]]s carrying treasure behind the Aldegara Waterfall. This occurrence in particular is shown from artwork in the instruction booklet, in which the Bulldog is seen parked on the riverbed just outside the waterfall.
