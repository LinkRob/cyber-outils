'''Bowser's Brother''', also known as '''Blue Bowser''' is the secondary villain of ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''.{{Citation needed}} He is a blue-colored version of [[Bowser]] and a recurring boss throughout the game. He is stated to be the youngest of the "Bowser twins" and has made almost no appearances ever since.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bowser's brother appears identical to his older brother, though with blue skin and a brown shell. In the ''Super Mario All Stars'' remake of ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels'', he appears fully identical to his brother, though an unused sprite shows him with his blue color albeit with magenta hair.

===Personality===
His personality is very similar to Bowser, they both ambitious, temperamental, and willing to fight with Mario and Luigi.

==History==
===''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''===
[[File:Blue_Bowser_Attacks.png|thumb|200px|left|Bowser's Brother fighting Mario]]
Bowser's Brother serves as the penultimate boss, appearing before the fight with Bowser in World 8-4, where he fights Mario and Luigi by throwing fireballs and hammers at them. After being defeated, he later reappears in World 9-3, but does not breath fire.

In [[World D-4]], a fake version of Bowser's Brother fights Mario or Luigi, being revealed as a Spiny upon being defeated.

===''Super Mario All Stars''===
In the ''All-Stars'' remake of ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels'', Bowser's Brother reprises his role as the penultimate boss, before the fight with Bowser in World 8-4, where he fights Mario and Luigi by throwing fireballs and hammers at them. After being defeated, he later reappears in World 9-3, but does not breath fire.
