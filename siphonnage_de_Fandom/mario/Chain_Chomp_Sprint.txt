'''Chain Chomp Sprint''' is a minigame in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''. While the minigame does not return in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'', there is a similar minigame named [[Blooper Baserun]].

==Layout==
Chain Chomp Sprint takes place around the four bases in [[Wario Palace]], with a [[Chain Chomp]] sleeping in the center.

==Gameplay==
There are four participants, and they have to collect [[Jewel]]s placed all across the home plate while avoiding being hit by a Chain Chomp. The Chain Chomp spends most of its time sleeping, but whenever it wakes up, the characters must stop moving, otherwise the Chain Chomp lunges at them. Whenever a character is hit by the Chain Chomp, they lose half their collected gems. A red Jewel sometimes appears on one of the bases, and collecting it is worth ten individual Jewels. A [[Dash Mushroom|Mushroom]] sometimes appears on the home plate, and if a character collects it, they can run around the home plate faster. While under the effects of a Mushroom, it is more difficult for the character to stop moving, potentially making them vulnerable to the Chain Chomp. Whoever collects the most Jewels within the [[time limit]] wins.

As with the other minigames, Chain Chomp Sprint comes in four difficulty levels: Mushroom, Flower, Star, and Special. The first three difficulty levels have the time limit set to 60 seconds whereas the latter has it at 90 seconds. Furthermore, the first three difficulty levels have a minimum requirement of Jewels that has to be collected: 20 for the Mushroom level; 30 for the Flower level; and 40 for the Star level. In the Special difficulty, there is no minimum requirement, and the winner is whoever has the most Jewels at the end.

==In ''Mario Superstar Baseball''==
Chain Chomp Sprint is also featured in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge Mode]], like the other four minigames. Here, the player's character has to compete against three [[Magikoopa]]s.
