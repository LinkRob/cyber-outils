The '''Markup Sticker''' is an item in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Description==
True to its name, it is a purple sticker that has a red "200%" icon on it and a [[wikipedia:barcode|barcode]] on the bottom. When selected, the player can choose whom to use it on; when the victim's turn starts, items and [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]] will have their cost doubled. This means that Stars will cost 40 coins instead of 20 for players who are affected by this item. This also means that if a player has a [[Jamboree Buddy]] and wants to buy 2 Stars, they will have to pay 80 coins instead of 40.

The effects of a Markup Sticker combine with the effects of a Flash Sale and/or the effects of having [[Princess Peach|Peach]] or [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]] as a Jamboree Buddy, so either being active will cancel out the markup, and having both active results in a total effect of the price being halved.

==In ''Super Mario Party Jamboree''==
The Markup Sticker can only be used in [[Rainbow Galleria]] and costs five [[coin]]s.

==Flavor text==
*'''Description:''' ''Use on an opponent to double the prices of items and Stars until their next turn ends.''
