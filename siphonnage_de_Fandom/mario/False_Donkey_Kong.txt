'''False Donkey Kong''' is a false character who appears in The Great Maze in [[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]].

== Appearance ==
False Donkey Kong is the same as [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], but he is evil and is green all over.

== Biography ==
The Great Maze is populated by dark clones of the fighters. There are clones for almost every character in the game. They must all be defeated including False Donkey Kong, along with every one of the Subspace Emissary's bosses, in order to unlock the door leading to [[Tabuu]].
