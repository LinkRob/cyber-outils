'''Balloon Panic''' is a [[Minigame|Special Game]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''. It takes place in [[DK Jungle Court]].

==Gameplay==
The player's character must stop [[Klaptrap]]s that are climbing on a wooden board from popping any DK balloons. Klaptraps appear in green, red, and blue colors, being fastest to slowest in that order. Every Klaptrap follows an electric current of the same color. To change it, one must hit a vine panel to divert a Klaptrap to an empty space. If the character hits a Klaptrap three times, it falls off of the board, earning the player's character a [[point]]. The minigame ends once the player has knocked out the required number of Klaptraps.

===Difficulty levels===
The following are the difficulty levels and their conditions in the [[Nintendo GameCube]] version:

*Panic (Easy): Protect two red balloons from 10 Klaptraps.
*Super Panic (Normal): Protect three blue balloons from 20 Klaptraps.
*Ultra-Hyper Panic (Hard):  Protect four yellow balloons from 30 Klaptraps.
*Balloon Panic Challenge (Bonus): Protect four green balloons from a horde of Klaptraps. The minigame continues only until a Klaptrap pops a balloon.

In the ''[[New Play Control!]]'' version, red and yellow balloons switch difficulty levels, and the player must protect four balloons regardless of difficulty setting.
