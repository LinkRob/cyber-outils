The '''Hand Aura''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. In both of its appearances, it increases [[Power|POW]] and grants the [[HP-Up Hand]] special effect.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', a Hand Aura is sometimes dropped by [[Starkiss]]es. Equipping it to [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] increases their POW by 20 (30 in the Japanese version).

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', aside from sometimes being dropped by Starkisses, Hand Auras alternatively become available in [[Little Fungitown]]'s shop once the [[Beanstar]] has been restored. There, a Hand Aura can be bought for 300 [[coin]]s or sold for 75 coins. Equipping a Hand Aura increases POW at an even higher number of 46.
