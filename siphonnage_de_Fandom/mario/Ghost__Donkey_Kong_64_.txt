|defeated = Any attacks}}'''Ghosts''' (also called '''Kreepas''') are enemies that appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. They are [[Kremling]]s that wear sheets and pretend to act like ghosts. They are also one of the weakest enemies in the game.

==History==
Ghosts can only be found in [[Creepy Castle]], making them rare enemies to encounter in the entire game. Ghosts walk around and when they spot a Kong, they will move quickly toward them. Larger Ghosts can be found in the basement of the museum that only [[Chunky Kong]] can access. Ghosts also appear during the boss fight against the [[King Kut Out]] but they can be used to restore any lost melons.
