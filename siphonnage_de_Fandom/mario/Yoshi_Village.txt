|games = ''[[Paper Mario]]''}}'''Yoshi Village''' is a peaceful village inhabited by [[Yoshi]]s featured in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is situated near the coastline of [[Lavalava Island]]. After Mario has [[Sushie]] in his party, he can access a warp pipe in [[Toad Town Tunnels]] that leads to Yoshi Village which may imply that the island has proper plumbing.

== Inhabitants ==
The Village consits of many Yoshis of varying colors that are found throughout the village. The plump [[Yoshi Chief]] is the leader of the village and is in contact with [[Raphael the Raven]], and a small number of [[Raven]]s live in a towering tree on the island. In addition to the mayor, the [[Fearsome 5]], a group of young Yoshi pranksters that play Hide-and-go-seek with [[Mario]] within [[Jade Jungle]]. In town, there is also shop and [[Toad House]].
