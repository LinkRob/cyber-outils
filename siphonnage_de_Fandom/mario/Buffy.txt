==Appearance==
Buffy is female [[Flip-Flopian]] that is pink in color and has a purple ponytail.

==History==
Buffy lives in the [[Flopside]] on the first floor. She often talks about [[Dyllis]]' food and replies that her food is the best around. Buffy herself loves to eat, especially her favorite, being rice food.
