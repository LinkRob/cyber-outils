{{Nihongo|'''Bronze Stars'''|ブロンズスター|Buronzu Sutā|Bronze Star}} are types of [[Power Star]]s found in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

== ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''==
[[File:Bronze Star Sprite.png|thumb|left]]
[[File:Bronze Comet.png|thumb|left]]

The Bronze Star can only be received if the [[Cosmic Spirit]] helps Mario complete the level. The player can find the Bronze Star in any galaxy with a [[Cosmic Spirit]].
