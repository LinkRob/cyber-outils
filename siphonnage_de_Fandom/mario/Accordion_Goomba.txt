An '''Accordion Goomba''' is a variant of [[Goomba]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]].''




==Battle==
The Accordion Goomba appear to be stacked with ten Goombas that are connected to each other like an accordion. Accordion Goombas make their only appearance in [[The Crimson Tower]]. They appear along with normal Goombas and [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s in the staircase leading to the [[Big Paint Star]]. In the battle, they launch Mario away if he tries to use the jumping technique on them. If the player uses a card that summons an enemy that attacks from above, the enemy will be launched away and will never come back.
