'''Lovely Chocolate''' is an item in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
This item restores no hit points or flower points for [[Mario]] or the active hero. Instead, this gives the person a new status ability. Not only does this item double the attack of Mario, it also halves the damage he takes. This item does more as well: anytime an enemy makes direct contact to Mario, the enemy suddenly gets shocked, dealing minor dammage. This item sells for around forty [[coin]]s and is created from a single recipe made by [[Dyllis]]. The two necessary items are [[Mild Cocoa Bean]] and [[Fire Burst]].
