This article pertains to the beta elements of ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

== Early Toad Road ==
An early version of Toad Road stage, which can be found in '''/common/bd00'''. Unlike the course maps in the final version of the game, this is all loaded in one chunk where it's counterpart has separate chunks for each section of the level.

=== Map ===
{| class="fandom-table"
!Image
!Description
|-
|[[File:MarioParty9Map00 1.png|center|thumb]]
|There's only one branching path in the stage, which is the fork in the road by the large star in the west portion of the map.
|-
|[[File:MarioParty9Map00Start.png|center|thumb]]
|At the start of the board, the star marker was changed in the final game to look like it's growing out of the dirt instead of just being slapped onto the ground. A ditch, where a bridge would've originally be located, is replaced by two tree stumps in the final game.
|-
|[[File:MarioParty9Map00Bridge.png|center|thumb]]
|The prototype version of Toad Road originally had three mountains instead of two. The third mountain, where the vine grows, was replaced with a hill surrounded by a spiraling staircase. The bog standard bridge was replaced with lily pad platforms later on in the final game.
|-
|[[File:MarioParty9Map00Ruins.png|center|thumb]]
|These ruins do not appear at all in the final game. Interestingly, they were part of the main path at one point during development. In the final game, it was replaced with a small island that's completely separate from the main area of the map.
|-
|[[File:MarioParty9Map00SpecialArea.png|center|thumb]]
|The lower-left corner of the map is where a special Mini-Star filled area can be found. In the final game, this is on an island that's in the center of the main path, but it's early counterpart is completely removed from the main path and is landlocked for the player to access to. The strange mushroom platforms (which are not scaled correctly) that lead to the boss room are completely absent from the final game, along with the the space ditch where a bridge would've been located at originally.
|-
|[[File:MarioParty9Map00BossRoom.png|center|thumb]][[File:MarioParty9hanachan.png|center|thumb]]
|A picture of Wiggler marks where the Boss Gate is found in the final game. Even in this early model, it seem that the [[Wiggler]] was intended to be the boss for this board. As expected, the actual Bowser Gate is not present at all in the early version of the map. The image used as placeholder for the Wiggler is the official artwork from ''[[Super Mario World]]''.
|}

== Unused model ==
[[File:Mario Party 9 Pentaro Early.png|left|thumb|126x126px]]
A very basic placeholder model of the penguin, which can found in the game's files for the [[Polar Extreme]] minigame. It has no animations assigned to it.

== Unused animations ==
Bowser Jr. has unused animations for using a vehicle. These were probably either used for testing purposes or he was going to be playable at one point of development. In the final game, he remains as a mini-boss for the player(s) to fight,
