'''Super Sushi Shrinker''' is an item in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Mario Meets Koop-zilla]]."

==Description==
The Super Sushi Shrinker is a liquid-filled injection that functions the opposite of the [[Super Sushi]], reducing its user from gigantic down to normal size. The Super Sushi Shrinker was created by [[Dr. T. Garden]], like the Super Sushi.
