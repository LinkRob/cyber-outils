The '''Turtle Zone''' is one of the zones featured in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. It takes place underwater, and a large [[whale]] is found asleep on the bottom-left corner of the map.

==Levels==
There are four levels, two being regular ones, one being a boss level, and lastly a secret level.
