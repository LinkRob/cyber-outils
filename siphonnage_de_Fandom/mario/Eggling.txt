'''Egglings''' are a species in ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are egg-like creatures with varying appearance. There are at least 100 Egglings; they come in three sizes and twenty varieties, including Gold, Silver, Pearl, Zebra, Panda, Tiger, Leopard, Giraffe, Squirrel, Cow, Snake, Turtle, Bee, Deer, Dove, Flamingo, Raccoon, Owl, Angel, and Bear. Each individual Eggling also has a name; their names are puns based on their type.

==In ''Yoshi Topsy-Turvy''==
Egglings are the natives of [[Eggland]]. They were attacked by the [[Koopa Troop]] during the game's events and managed to escape to [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]], only to become [[apple]]s from one of [[Bowser]]'s spells and be trapped inside the [[Forbidden Pop-Up Book]], which Yoshi's Island had become with everything else. When prompted by the [[Spirit of Cuteness]], [[Yoshi]] ate the apples and returned the Egglings to their old selves.
