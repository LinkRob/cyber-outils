'''Gangplank Galleon''' is the first world appearing first in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and later in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''.

==Role in Game==
===''Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest''===
In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'', Gangplank Galleon serves as the first world in the game. It is [[King K. Rool]]'s pirate ship from ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'', but expanded to act as a pirate ship. Here, there are [[Neek]]s, [[Klomp]]s, [[Kaboing]]s, etc. to try to stop [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] as they are on their quest to free [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] from K. Rool. K. Rool also has his own cabin in this world at the start of [[Pirate Panic (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Pirate Panic]]. Though, he is never seen in it. At the end of this world, [[Krow]] is seen to be the boss and is fought with eggs needed to be thrown at him in order for him to be defeated so the monkeys can venture on to [[Crocodile Cauldron]].

At the end of the game, the [[Lost World (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Lost World]] had energy build-up when Kaptain K. Rool fell into the power source of the area (and the island in general). This caused Crocodile Isle to sink, with K. Rool and the Kremling Krew escaping into Gangplank Galleon to flee the isle.

In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version, Gangplank Galleon has been relocated to the right side of [[Crocodile Isle]] instead of the left in the original version.

===''Donkey Kong Land 2''===
After Crocodile Isle resurrected from the water, Gangplank Galleon too was resurrected. Enemies including [[Neek]]s, [[Klomp]]s, etc. scavenged the area once more with all of the same levels appearing, including [[Krow]] reprising as the boss in the area atop the mast with his nest and eggs. When he is defeated once more, [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] and [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] may venture onwards to [[Krem Cauldron]], the merged area for [[Crocodile Cauldron]] and [[Krem Quay]].

At the end of the game, K. Rool's bulk once again causes energy build-up in the power source of the island (more specifically, the [[Lost World (Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest)|Lost World]]). As the island sunk, the [[Kremling Krew]] escaped into Gangplank Galleon and fled the island when the island sunk once more.
