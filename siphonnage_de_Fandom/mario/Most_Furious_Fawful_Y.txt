'''Most Furious Fawful Ys''' are enemies that appear exclusively in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign or the ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' [[Reissue|remake]].

==Physical Description==

Most Furious Fawful Ys are [[Cackletta]] minions that use their arms to fly in the air and have a bulb on top of their head.   

==Battle==

Most Furious Fawful Ys are a Flying troop, and use [[Dive Attack]] as their [[Special Skill]]. They deal damage by ramming into their enemies from above.

==Flavor text==

*'''In-game description:''' "Cackletta's faithful underling. Fights as a Flying trooper."
