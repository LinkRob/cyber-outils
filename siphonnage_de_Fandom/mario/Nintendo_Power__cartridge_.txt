'''Nintendo Power''' was a video game distribution service introduced in 1996, exclusively within Japan. It allowed users to download Super Famicom or Game Boy titles onto a flash memory cartridge, as a more cost-efficient way of obtain a game that from retail packaging. At first, Nintendo Power only offered this service for Super Famicom games, but since 2000, it was also made available for [[Game Boy]] and [[Game Boy Color]] games.

One ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' game, ''[[Wrecking Crew '98]]'', was first released as part of the Nintendo Power service before receiving an official retail release shortly after. In Japan, ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]'' was only made available through the service, and it did not receive a proper retail release like with North America, Europe, and Australia in 1999.

The Nintendo Power service was discontinued in 2007.
