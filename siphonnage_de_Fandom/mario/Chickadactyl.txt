'''Chickadactyls''' are a species which appear in the ''[[Super Mario World (show)|Super Mario World]]'' episode "[[King Scoopa Koopa]]." They are used by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] for his fast-food eatery in an attempt to profit, only to have his scheme thwarted by [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]].

== Etymology ==
The named "Chickadactyl" is a portmanteau of "chicken" and "pterodactyl," being the two species that they are based on.
