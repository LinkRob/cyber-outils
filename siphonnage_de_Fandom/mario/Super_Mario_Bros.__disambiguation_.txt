'''Super Mario Bros.''' is the title used for several different subjects thoughout the series.

==Media==
*''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', a 1985 game for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] and both the origin and most common use of the phrase "Super Mario Bros.".
*''[[Super Mario Bros. (Game & Watch)|Super Mario Bros.]]'', a [[Game & Watch]] game based on the original Nintendo Entertainment System game.
*''[[Super Mario Bros. (Nelsonic Game Watch)|Super Mario Bros.]]'', a [[Nelsonic Game Watch]] game based on the original Nintendo Entertainment System game.
*''[[Super Mario Bros. (pinball)|Super Mario Bros.]]'', a pinball game.
*''[[Super Mario Bros. (film)|Super Mario Bros.]]'', a live-action film based on the ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' games, released in 1993.
*''[[Super Mario Bros. (activity books)|Super Mario Bros.]]'', a series of coloring and activity books published by Golden Books.
*''[[Super Mario Bros. (manga)|Super Mario Bros.]]'', a one-shot [[manga]] by [[Yukio Sawada]].
