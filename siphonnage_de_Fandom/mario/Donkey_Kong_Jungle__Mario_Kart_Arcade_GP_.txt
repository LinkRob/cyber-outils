'''Donkey Kong Jungle''' is a course in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''. It is a simple track, in the shape of an oval, like that of [[Baby Park]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is rainy, and [[Piranha Plant]]s are found around the track.
