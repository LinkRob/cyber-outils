'''World 6-B''' is the second special level in [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. The player needs five [[Star Coin]]s to unlock this level. Once the player completes this level, it unlocks {{world-link|6|towern2|World 6-Tower (New Super Mario Bros. 2)}} and skip [[World 6-3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6-3]]. 

==Layout==
The player starts off on a safe platform, and must get across three [[Elevator Lift]]s ahead. Throughout the first half of the level, three [[Flame Chomp]]s appear at different locations. Once the player makes it to the end of the room, a pipe takes them to the next room. Alternatively, the player could find a hidden vine that leads to the second Star Coin, as well as the next room. In the second half of the level, the player encounters several [[Bullet Bill]]s and [[Banzai Bill]]s. Below are two [[Gold Ring]]s which turn the Bullet Bills and Banzai Bills to gold, and above is a [[Red Ring]] and a Star Coin. There are also [[Flying ? Block]]s in some areas. When the player reaches the end of the level, the last platform flashes and disappears, after which the [[Goal Pole]] appears.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': When the player reaches the third Elevator Lift, they must take the lower path to find the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Before the green pipe halfway through the level, there is a hidden block with a [[Beanstalk]] that is surrounded by three ordinary [[Coin]]s. The beanstalk leads to a secret area, where the player must use an Elevator Lift the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Not too far after the Red Ring, the third Star Coin is seen floating above a [[Bill Blaster]].
