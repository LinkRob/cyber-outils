'''Above the Cheep Cheep Seas''', or '''Sparkling Waters-3''', is the third level of [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Haunted Shipwreck]], and its own completion unlocks [[Dragoneel's Undersea Grotto]].

==Layout==
The level begins near a [[? Block]]. A [[Scale Lift]] is above a pit of water containing [[Cheep Cheep]]s, and next to a tall [[Waterspout]], which takes the player into another area. The more ? Blocks are found, the second of which contains [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. Another scale is then found. Cheep Cheeps then begin to jump out of the water in an area leading to more scales, ? Blocks, and some [[berry|berries]]. [[Donut Block]] platforms with [[Brick Block]]s above them are found, followed by a platform with the [[Checkpoint Flag]] on it. An area below the checkpoint has a ? Block, some Donut Blocks, and a Waterspout. After more ? Blocks and Waterspout, a [[Green Ring]] appears above Donut Blocks. A Waterspout leading to a group of scales is found, and a ? Block is found above a bush containing two berries. A ? Block is found, along with large Waterspouts and a large group of jumping Cheep Cheeps. The [[Goal Pole]] is then found.

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin can be found on a floor of [[Donut Block]]s.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Underneath a platform, the second Star Coin is periodically guarded by a Waterspout so time it carefully.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Beneath a grass platform, a [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the area with the third [[Star Coin]] can be found.
