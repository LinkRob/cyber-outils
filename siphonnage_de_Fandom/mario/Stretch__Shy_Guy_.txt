'''Stretches''' (also called '''Stretch Guys''' or '''Melon Guys''') are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] which only appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. They resemble Shy Guys, and even wear the same red, yellow, green, or purple as they do, except their body is stretched upward. A Stretch cannot be defeated from a [[jump]] attack, as doing so only causes them to spit out seeds from a [[watermelon]].
