The '''Super Mario Bros. Plumbing''' is a family-owned plumbing service owned by [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]].<ref name="smbplumbing">{{cite web|url=https://www.smbplumbing.com/|title=Super Mario Bros. Plumbing|work=Super Mario Bros. Plumbing}}</ref>

== Description ==
Super Mario Bros. Plumbing is located in [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]] where Mario and Luigi run their business. The customer can call the service through the number 929-55-MARIO (929-556-2746) which can be found on their website. They have received average-to-good reviews, according to the testimonials. However, in the film, the phone number is (917-555-0186)<ref name="smbplumbing"/>

== Trivia ==
*There is a greeting voicemail from [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] that follows: ''Thank you for calling Super Mario Brothers Plumbing. It's-a me, Luigi! Eh, if you need service, please, uh, text us at the same number you just called! 929-55-MARIO! That's 929-556-2746! Message us about any issues, wherever you live. House, condo... [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]]? And we'll be sure to text you back, right away because at Super Mario Brothers Plumbing, we don't say 'Let's-a wait', we say 'Let's-a go'! Oh, and uh, check our website; [https://smbplumbing.com SMBPlumbing.com]. We're still working on it, so, more updates to come. Bye-bye!''
**Luigi mentioning "mansion" in the voicemail is a reference to his own series: ''[[Luigi's Mansion (series)|Luigi's Mansion]]''.
*The theme that plays throughout the commercial is the main theme rap from [[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]].
