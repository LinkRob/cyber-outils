A '''Mad Boo''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Mad Boos are a variant of [[Boo]], which they resemble, except they have entirely orange eyes.

=== Behavior ===
A Mad Boo is under the effects of the [[Rage]] [[vibe]]. Mad Boos have the opposite behavior of regular Boos in that they only chase [[Princess Peach|Peach]] if she is facing them, a type of behavior also present in their variant, [[Mad Big Boo]]s. Coincidentally, in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'', the Boos in the nighttime Ground theme have the exact behavior of Mad Boos.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Mad Boos are first encountered in the level [[Shriek Mansion 3-5]]. Peach can only defeat a Mad Boo from the effects of the Rage vibe.
