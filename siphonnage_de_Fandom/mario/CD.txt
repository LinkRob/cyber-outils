'''CDs''' are an item in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
[[File:WL4 CD Crescent Moon Village screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|Wario discovering the CD of [[Crescent Moon Village]] in ''Wario Land 4'']]
A CD is a collectible item featuring a musical tune that can be unlocked for play in the [[Sound Room]]. CDs appear in every [[level]] except the [[Hall of Hieroglyphs]] and the [[Golden Passage]], so there are a total of sixteen CDs. A CD is contained within a [[CD box]], often well-hidden in a level. Unlike the [[jewel piece]]s and the [[Keyzer]], the CDs are not required to complete the game.

CDs come in four different colors, each matching the color of the corresponding Passage where it appears: green CDs are in [[Emerald Passage]]; red CDs in [[Ruby Passage]]; yellow CDs in [[Topaz Passage]]; and blue CDs in [[Sapphire Passage]].
