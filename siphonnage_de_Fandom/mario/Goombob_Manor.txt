'''Goombob Manor''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
It is a two-story building with a brown brick exterior and an interior with lavish decorations, including a gaudy chandelier and a red carpet covering the floor and stairs.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Goombob Manor is located in the [[Town Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. It is a setting featured during [[Goombob]]'s quest, [[Accessorize!]]. Here, the player's character has to obtain a [[Lovely Seashell]] from [[Sandy Beach]] and return with it to Goombob Manor. In doing so, they can give the Lovely Seashell to Goombob, who in turn gives it as a gift to [[Goombetty]] and asks if they can go on a date. Goombetty accepts the offer, making Goombob happy, and she then runs out of Goombob Manor. Goombob then rewards the player with the [[Dessert Menu]] [[Gaddget]].
