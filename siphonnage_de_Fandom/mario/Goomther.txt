|forms        = }}'''Goomther''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. He is a [[Goomba]] that lives in [[Rogueport]].

==History==
Goomther is a rather unfortunate character as he often gets scammed out of his money. Goomther has a trouble posted at the [[Trouble Center]] where he needs help in tracking down [[Larson]] the [[Bandit]] who has recently conned him out of his money. After [[Mario]] corners Larson. Goomther appears, pays Mario 20 [[coin]]s, and takes Larson away.
