'''Fuzzy Pants''' is a personal armor used by [[Mallow]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be bought from a shop in [[Nimbus Land]] for 70 [[coin]]s. Fuzzy Pants increases Mallow's [[Defense]] by 36 and his [[Magic Defense]] by 18.
