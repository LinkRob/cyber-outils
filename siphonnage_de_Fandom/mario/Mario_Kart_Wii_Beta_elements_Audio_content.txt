== Sound Effect ==
An early trick sound effect. Compared to the final version's, it has a higher pitch and it is also slightly longer. A similar sound (if not the same) can be heard in a prerelease trailer. Curiously, a variant of this sound was used in a few competitions/tournaments, more specifically the ones that had the player race a [[Wiggler]], when the said character does a trick ('''SE_CC_HANACHAN_JUMP_ACTION'''). However, other than sounding slightly different than the unused variation, it is also sequenced with both the early and the final trick sounds being streamed. This sound would later be used in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' when the player does a trick in both the [[Rainbow Road]] tracks.
