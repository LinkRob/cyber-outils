'''Puniper''' is a large [[Puni]] who lives in the [[The Great Tree|Great Tree]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Personality==
Puniper is a very rude Puni as, even after he was saved by Mario and Punio, he didn't want to go with them further into The Great Tree. However, he was set straight by the [[Puni Elder]] and Puniper temporary joined Mario's side.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
When [[Mario]] first meets Puniper, Puniper doubts that he can save the Punis. Even so, Puniper eventually develops respect for Mario, and recognizes him as both a hero and leader.

==Trivia==
*Puniper is the only Puni of the group who has unique-shaped pupils in his eyes.
