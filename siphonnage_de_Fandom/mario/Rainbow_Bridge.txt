'''Rainbow Bridge''' is a Special building that appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
Hence the name, '''Rainbow Bridge''' is shaped like an arc with various colors (red, yellow, cyan, blue and magenta).

== In ''Super Mario Run'' ==
The Rainbow Bridge is a Special building with no rarity. The more Toads the player has, the more bridges the player can buy it at the Shop. However, they are only limited to five bridges. Once the player bus the object, the player can access more areas to place objects.
