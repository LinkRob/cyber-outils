The '''Guardarm''' is an enemy from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They are common on Rumbla Island. According to the locals there, Guardarms get their kicks from destroying the local artwork on the island. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] fight two of them as a miniboss after arriving on the island.

== Description ==
The Guardarm is a yellow ball-shaped creature with long purple-handed arms and stubby legs. It wears stone bracelets on its hands.

== Behaviour ==

=== Combat ===
In combat, Guardarms use their arms as shields. To damage the creatures, Mario and Luigi have to attack them in the direction that is not covered.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dJddN0i5vW4</ref>

To attack, Guardarms charge arms-forward. Their charge attack can be avoided by jumping over them, and their target is telegraphed by where the creature puts its arms before it charges. If their arms are pointed northwest, the target is [[Mario (character)|Mario]], but if their arms are pointed southwest, their target is [[Luigi]]. If their body is landed on, they take damage. Landing on their arms will not deal damage.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dJddN0i5vW4</ref>

Guardarms are a Level 1 enemy with 20 hit points, an attack power of 9, a defense of 2, and a speed of 10. Their defeat nets 2 [[Experience Point]]s, 3 [[Coin]]s, and possibly a [[Refereshing Herb]] and/or [[Candy]].

After [[Gorumbla]] begins his first rampage, Guardarms become stronger. They can throw stones as the Bros., who have to repel these stones with hammer strikes. At this point, Guardarms are of Level 6, with 55 hit points, an attack power of 23, a defense of 4, and a speed of 22. Their defeat nets 11 experience points and 9 coins, with the possibility of not just the [[Refreshing Herb]] and [[Candy]] from the first visit to Rumbla Island, but also a Rumbla Stone.

== Variants ==
=== Friendly Guardarm ===
Friendly Guardarms appear during the sidequest Gorumbla Gone Good, where the [[Gorumbla]] and his Guardarm squadron dedicate themselves to rebuilding [[Rumbla Island]].

=== Glohm Guardarm ===
After [[Reclusa]] conquers [[Concordia]] following the second visit to [[Fortress Zokket]], Guardarms become infected with a friendship-destroying substance or virus called [[Glohm]], becoming more aggressive than their regular state. These "Glohm Guardarms" attack like their regular counterparts, but will automatically launch an attack of their own in retaliation to an attack they survive. The Glohm Guardarm is also able to spread the infection to the Bros. should it hit them.

A Glohm Guardarm is of Level 39 with 637 hit points, an attack power of 20, a defense of 48, and a speed of 163, giving 953 experience, 144 coins, and the same pre-[[Reclusa World]] non-coin loot drops in addition to the possibility of a Glohm Dust sample, a Glohm Fragment, or a Glohm Crystal when destroyed.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The name "Guardarm" is taken from how the Guardarm's arms behave as shields for the creature's body.
