'''Big Eekhammer x2''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Eekhammer x2 Battle Card is the strong and fast version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer x2]]. It is two of the [[Big Eekhammer]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied with a red/pink checkerboard background.

==Price==
Buy Price: 40 coins (unpainted)

Sell Price: 20 coins (unpainted)

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
The Big Eekhammer x2 is only obtainable in [[Redpepper Volcano]].
