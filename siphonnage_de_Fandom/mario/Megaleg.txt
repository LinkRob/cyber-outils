'''Megaleg''' is a boss in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It is a gigantic mechanical construct with a vague resemblance to a [[Snifit]]. It is found in [[Bowser Jr.'s Robot Reactor]], and is owned and possibly commanded by [[Bowser Jr.]]

==Profile==
===Physical description===
[[File:SMG Megaleg Moon.png|thumb|Megaleg standing on the Robot Reactor.]]
Megaleg is incredibly large and lives on a planet just big enough to hold it. It has three long legs, like a tripod, and each leg has various tricks and traps designed to trip [[Mario]] up. It has fly-like eyes with huge lights beaming out of them. On top of Megaleg's head is a red cage with a rotating lighthouse-like light on top that contains a [[Grand Star]]. Megaleg is much bigger than [[Digga-Leg]], a similar boss encountered in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

The earliest version of Megaleg was labeled "BossCrab", which had a different body design, no nose or mouth, four legs, and a half oval shaped head. In the E3 2006 demo of ''Super Mario Galaxy'', there was nothing in Megaleg's cage.

===Powers and abilities===
Megaleg's attacks consist of firing [[Bullet Bill]]s from its underbelly, which consists of various cannons. Megaleg fires them at Mario while he attempts to climb up its legs. It also attempts to stomp on Mario while he runs around Tech Planet.

==History==
===In ''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[File:SMG Megaleg Head Bullet Bills.png|thumb|left|Mario being followed by a Bullet Bill on Megaleg's head.]]
When Mario enters Bowser Jr.'s Robot Reactor, Megaleg is positioned on the bottom of the [[Bowser Jr.'s Robot Reactor#Robot Reactor|Robot Reactor]]. When Mario uses the [[Launch Star]] on the [[Bowser Jr.'s Robot Reactor#Starting Planet|Starting Planet]] to reach the Robot Reactor, Bowser Jr. appears and orders Megaleg to attack Mario.

In the battle, Mario must run toward one of Megaleg's three feet and wait until they stomp down, then [[jump]] on them and climb up. If one of Megaleg's feet stomps Mario, he loses a life. Mario has to climb up Megaleg's leg until he reaches the top. As Mario is climbing, he has to avoid some obstacles, including a row of rotating gears that are easy to slip off of and a triple cannon that shoots Bullet Bills. 
[[File:SMG Megaleg Destroyed.png|thumb|Megaleg explodes after Mario frees the Grand Star.]]
Megaleg has a large cage on the top of its head; this is its weak point. Several cannons scattered around its head continuously fire homing Bullet Bills. There are about six [[coin]]s to find on the edges of the head. To defeat Megaleg, Mario must have a Bullet Bill home in on him as he is near the cage on Megaleg's head and duck out of the way. With proper timing, the Bullet Bill cannot turn to hit Mario. Once the Bullet Bill crashes into the cage, both it and the cage are obliterated. Once that cage is annihilated, several barrier emerge around it and start to spin to protect the flashing CPU that controls Megaleg through the [[Grand Star]] inside it. Furthermore, more Bullet Bills are fired than before. Mario must then have another Bullet Bill break the walls and ram into the CPU, thus obliterating the tremendous Megaleg and freeing the Grand Star.

The concept of climbing atop and moving on a large being was originally intended to be used in the final fight against [[Ganon]] in ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time]]'', but this was scrapped due to the fact that one would be unable to see Ganon while atop him. However, it was later reused in ''Super Mario Galaxy'', much to the excitement of the development team.<ref>''[[Nintendo Power]]'' Issue 225, page 64.</ref> Megaleg underwent several changes prior to the release of ''Super Mario Galaxy''.

===In ''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie''===
{{main|Megaleg (Illumination)}}
The Megaleg is featured in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'', where in one trailer, it is shown confronting [[Rosalina]]. She uses her powers to summon [[Launch Star]]s around its legs to destroy it.

==Trivia==
*Megaleg’s beta name was '''Snifit Bot''' which paid homage to his [[Snifit]]-like appearance before he was assigned a more unique name.
*In a ''[[Nintendo Power]]'' preview for ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', it was stated that Megaleg would return.<ref>''Nintendo Power'' Issue 251, page 37.</ref> This was later proven false, however, with Digga-Leg appearing instead.
*There is a [[Super Mario Galaxy/Glitches|glitch]] whereby the player can access Megaleg's planet without triggering the cutscene or battle.<ref>A+Start. (March 6, 2015). [https://youtu.be/qC-ypi33wMY?t=133 Super Mario Galaxy Glitches - Son Of A Glitch - Episode 39]. ''YouTube''. Retrieved February 15, 2020.</ref>
*Megaleg's design and functions has some similarities to the original design of the Tripod Walkers from H.G. Wells' novel, "War of the Worlds", such as having search-lights for eyes and three legs.
*All of the other bosses resemble another enemy in the game to some extent. Since Snifits don't appear in ''Super Mario Galaxy'', Megaleg is the only boss that does not resemble another enemy in ''Super Mario Galaxy''. However, [[Digga-Leg]] from ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' looks almost identical to Megaleg, though different in size. 
*Megaleg was in the [[Good Egg Galaxy]] in the beta version; however, he was moved to Bowser Jr.'s Robot Reactor. He also had nothing in his cage and only left behind a regular [[Power Star]] upon defeat rather than a [[Grand Star]].
*Megaleg is the only Grand Star boss that requires one hit to be defeated.
*The earliest beta design of Megaleg appeared on some concept art for the game, here he was known as "CrabBoss" and had a different body design.
