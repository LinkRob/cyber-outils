'''Shiny-Cone Goombas''', spelled '''Shiny Cone Goombas''' in PAL releases, are an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Shiny-Cone Goomba is a variant of [[Paper-Cone Goomba]], which it resembles, but with an added sheen.

===Behavior===
A Shiny-Cone Goomba attacks by lunging itself [[Mario]] in the manner of a paper airplane. While this attack is also used by Paper-Cone Goombas, their variation is slower.

==In ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''==
Shiny-Cone Goombas are only encountered in [[Ice Flow]]. Like their regular counterpart, Shiny-Cone Goombas cannot be stomped onto unless an [[Iron Jump]] [[stickers|sticker]] is used.
