'''Peach's Crown''', or '''Tiara''', is an object worn by [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]] in [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|The Super Mario movies]]. When the time was right, for Peach to complete the [[Training Course (Illumination)|Training Course]], the [[Toad General]], crowned her as princess. Her tiara was seeen during [[Anya Taylor-Joy]]'s credit part during the credits.
