The '''Megaflash Iron Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. This is the strongest Iron Jump sticker and should be used for the final fight with Bowser. This sticker is extreamly rare as it is only found in one level in the entire game: Rugged Road.

== Description ==
The Megaflash Iron Jump looks similar to Flashy Iron Jump stickers except it's very large. It takes up over four sticker spaces in the [[Sticker Album]].
