<b>Small Goombas</b> are a small variant of [[Goomba]]s that have been shrunken by [[Kamek]]’s magic. They first appear in <i>[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]</i>

==History==
===<i>Paper Mario: Color Splash</i>===
Small Goombas appear in <i>[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]</i> in this game, they can primarily be found in the [[Sacred Forest]]. Several [[Small Goomba Gang]]s scatter the blue [[Mini Paint Star]] of that level into five pieces.

In battle, small Goombas are incredibly weak and a single hit is enough to finish them off however they usually fight in groups, whenever they do, they usually attack at once which makes them more threatening.

Five Small Goombas can band up to form [[Small Goomba Wheel]]s. Small Goomba Gangs are more dangerous, and can pummel Mario down a large chunk of his health if they remain together. During battle, the Small Goomba Gangs become increasingly smaller as Mario hits and scatters them, making them significantly more manageable.

One of the Mini Paint Star pieces is guarded by a huge army of Small Goombas, these will kill Mario instantly if he comes anywhere near, to clear them away, Mario will need to find a [[Star]] hidden in a tree off to the side of the clearing.

==Trivia==
*Small Goombas are similar to the [[Micro Goomba]]s found in the mainstream <i>[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]</i> games however they’re not the same as Micro Goombas are a naturally small species while Small Goombas are regular Goombas that have been shrunken by [[Kamek]].
