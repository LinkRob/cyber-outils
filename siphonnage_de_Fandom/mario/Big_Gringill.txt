'''Big Gringills''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Profile==
As directly stated by their name, Big Gringills are giant variants of Gringills. Unlike their regular counterpart, Big Gringills are only found beating in caves, usually in the dark. Similar to the normal one, they are a darker brown in color with dark stripes in their bodies, and two feelers on their lower lips. They go slower and make a straining sound when they coming out in their holes and they give a [[1-Up Mushroom]] when they are defeated.

In ''Super Mario Galaxy'', Big Gringills appear only in [[Beach Bowl Galaxy]], the [[Sea Slide Galaxy]] and the [[Deep Dark Galaxy]], each having only one of them. In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', two of them appear in the [[Starshine Beach Galaxy]].
