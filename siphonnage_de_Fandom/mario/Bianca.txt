'''Bianca''' is a character from the ''[[Dragon Quest]]'' franchise. She appears with ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' characters in the crossover games ''[[Itadaki Street DS]]'' and ''[[Fortune Street]]'', both in which she is a playable character. She is a childhood friend of the hero from ''[[w:c:dragonquest:Dragon Quest V: Hand of the Heavenly Bride|Dragon Quest V: Hand of the Heavenly Bride]]''.
