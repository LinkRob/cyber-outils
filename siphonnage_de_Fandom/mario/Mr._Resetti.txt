'''Mr. Resetti''' is a mole who appears throughout the ''[[Animal Crossing]]'' games since its first title, ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Dōbutsu no Mori|Dōbutsu no Mori]]''. His job is to make sure the players don't reset and save.

==In {{franchise|Mario}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
Mr. Resetti is an obstacle in the [[Animal Crossing (track)|Animal Crossing]] track, acting in a similar manner to [[Monty Mole]]s from other courses.

===''[[Super Mario Maker]]''===
Mr. Resetti is featured as one of several [[Mystery Mushroom]] [[Costume Mario|costumes]].
