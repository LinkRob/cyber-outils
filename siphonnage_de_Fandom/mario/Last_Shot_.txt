|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''|image=Lastshot.png}}'''Last Shot!''' is a move used only by [[Jabit]]s in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
With Last Shot!, the Jabit is able to self-destruct itself in order to automatically knock out one of Mario's party. This can be avoided by wearing a Safety Pin or the Quartz Charm.
