'''World 4-Castle''' is the castle level of [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 4]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== Layout ==
The castle's prominent features are [[Spiked Ball]]s and [[Snake Block]]s. The player begins under some Spiked Balls trapped in Snake Blocks, and two [[? Block]]s. After that is a small series of Spiked Balls and Snake Blocks, with another ? Block, a [[Brick Block]] and a [[Fire Bar]]. There are some openings above which will drop an infinite amount of Spiked Balls. A [[Dry Bones]] is also on one of the platforms. After that lies a [[Roulette Block]], two Fire Bars and some sets of stairs. Then, there is a large platform with Snake Blocks that carry an infinite amount of Spiked Balls, as we as a Dry Bones and a Fire Bar. After that lies a [[Checkpoint Flag]], another ? Block, and some [[Big Spiked Ball]]s with a regular sized Spiked Ball, which are being moved by more Snake Blocks. After that, there is a set of ? Blocks, a platform with Snake Blocks and a Dry Bones, and a platform above that drops an infinite amount of Spiked Balls. After crossing that, there are Fire Bars and some sets of stairs, followed by red and green [[Warp Pipe]]s. Finally, there are three Big Spiked Balls with Snake Blocks, and the boss door leading to Morton Koopa Jr.

=== Boss battle ===
[[File:NSMB World 4-Castle Morton Battle.png|thumb|200px|right|The [[Mario Bros.]] battling Morton Koopa Jr.]]
In the battle, [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Morton]] jumps out of a large Warp Pipe, and uses his wand to cast Spiked Balls. He can also pound the ground which stuns [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] if they are on the ground. The Bros. simply jump on him three times or fire 18 fireballs if they are [[Fire Mario]] or Fire Luigi. After each time he is hit, Morton spins off screen on the bottom ground, and re-appears via the large Warp Pipe on the ceiling, back on higher ground. After defeating him, the Bros. collect the keys to open the gate, where [[Princess Peach]] and [[Ludwig von Koopa]] await them. As they reach them, Ludwig taunts before running off with the princess, and the Bros. chase them to [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5]].

== [[Star Coin]] Locations ==
*'''Star Coin 1''': At the section with two Fire Bars and sets of stairs lies the first Star Coin. To collect it, avoid grabbing the four [[Coin]]s located near it and hit the [[P Switch]] to turn the coins into [[Brick Block]]s, allowing the player to collect the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Shortly after the Checkpoint Flag, with the section with some Big Spiked Balls and a regular sized one with some Snake Blocks lies the second Star Coin. Let the Big Spiked Ball on the right move away from the Star Coin first to collect it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': At the platform with some sets of stairs and a Fire Bar lies a red Warp Pipe. Enter it to find the final Star Coin. Traverse through the Snake Blocks and Spiked Balls to collect it.
