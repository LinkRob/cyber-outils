'''Lakitu''' is a playable racer in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series. 
== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Lakitu has a red shell (to distinguish him from the referee who has a green shell), he wears goggles and three strands of black hair sticking out of his head. The expressions he makes shows his personality being bubbly and chill.
=== Powers and abilities ===
In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', he is classified as Featherweight. Lakitu has high [[acceleration]], [[handling]], and [[Off-Road|off-road]] stats while giving him drawbacks such as low [[speed]] and [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]]. His cloud-shaped kart ([[Cloud 9]]) also has an advantage in acceleration, and handling, but it also has drawbacks such as speed and weight.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' & ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', he is classified as Lightweight. Lakitu's stats show he is the best in handling and [[traction]].

In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', he is classified as a Super Driver. He was given the special item [[Triple Green Shells]], which is shared with [[Iggy Koopa|Iggy]], [[Koopa Troopa]], and [[Dry Bones]].
==In the ''Mario Kart'' series==
===''Mario Kart 7''===
This was '''Lakitu's''' first playable appearance, along with the [[Honey Queen]]. He is the staff ghost racer for [[Piranha Plant Slide]] and [[Rainbow Road (SNES)|SNES Rainbow Road]] and is the most common CPU along with [[Toad]]. He was given a kart based on him, called [[Cloud 9]], which is in the shape of a [[Lakitu's Cloud]].
===''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''===
'''Lakitu''' makes a reappearance in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. He is the staff ghost racer for [[Donut Plains 3|SNES Donut Plains 3]]. However, his kart, Cloud 9, does not make a reappearance for unknown reasons.

=== ''Mario Kart Tour'' ===
'''Lakitu''' was added as a playable character during the [[Tokyo Tour]], and his cloud-shaped kart makes a reappearance. The [[Lakitu (referee)]] in the New Year's costume debuts in the [[New Year's Tour (2021)|New Year's 2021 Tour]] as a playable character.

=== ''Mario Kart World'' ===
As of ''[[Mario Kart World]]'', red-shell Lakitu has been replaced by [[Lakitu|a green-shelled Lakitu]].
