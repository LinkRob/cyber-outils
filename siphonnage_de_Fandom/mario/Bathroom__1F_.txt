The '''Bathroom''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Bathroom consists of a sink with a [[Mirror (object)|mirror]], a bathtub, and a shelf on the upper left side.

==In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}==
===''Luigi's Mansion'' ([[Nintendo GameCube]])===
The Bathroom is the first accessible room of [[Area Two]]. It features two [[Grabbing Ghost]]s, and this is the first room where they appear. When both are captured, the [[Key]] to the [[Ball Room]] is spawned onto the shelf, and because of how high it is, [[Luigi]] must use his [[Poltergust 3000]] to retrieve it, just as how the key to the [[Wardrobe Room]] is retrieved using this method. After collecting the key, Luigi gets a call from [[Professor E. Gadd]] on the [[Game Boy Horror]], and E. Gadd provides Luigi with advice on fighting Grabbing Ghosts, regardless of whether Luigi struggled or not.

In the [[Hidden Mansion]] in PAL releases, there are three red Grabbing Ghosts to fight instead.

===''Luigi's Mansion'' (Nintendo 3DS)===
The Bathroom is mostly the same as in the original version. However, the Hidden Mansion now includes a [[Speedy Spirit]] hidden in the sink, and capturing it releases twenty [[coin]]s, ten [[bill]]s, and two [[Gold Bar]]s.

==Ghosts==
*Grabbing Ghosts (lavender): 2
*Grabbing Ghosts (red) (PAL version's Hidden Mansion only): 2
*[[Speedy Spirit]] (Nintendo 3DS version's Hidden Mansion only): 1
*[[Temper Terror]] ([[blackout]]): 3
