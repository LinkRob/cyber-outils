'''Lantern Ghost''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. He and a few other [[Lantern Ghost]]s live in the basement of [[Horror Condo]], where they have the [[TFV Fan Club]] as they all like the show [[Toad Force V]]. Lantern Ghost has the same appearance as the species, namely the other Lantern Ghosts in the club alongside him.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Lantern Ghost asks the player character if they are a fan of Toad Force V. Even if they say yes, Lantern Ghost does not believe that they look like one and asks them to show a Toad Force V action figure for proof. Because the player's character does not have one, Lantern Ghost instructs them to go get one from the [[Junk]]. If they do this and return to Lantern Ghost, he allows them to be a member of the fan club, and the Lantern Ghost gives the player the [[Poochy Pal]] [[Gaddget]] as a reward.
