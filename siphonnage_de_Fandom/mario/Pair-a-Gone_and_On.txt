'''Pair-a-Gone and On''' is one of [[Luigi]]'s [[minigame]]s in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It is an endless variation of [[Pair-a-Gone]], but unlike said minigame, Pair-a-Gone and On does not return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. 

==Gameplay==
The minigame is largely identical to Pair-a-Gone, given that it is a variation of it. The player has to match any two cards adjacent to each other either horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Whenever all cards in a set are matched, Luigi deals out more to replace them. The minigame only ends once the player cannot match any more cards, in which case it is a [[Game Over]], and the player's score is recorded.

There are a total of ten card types, half of which are available from the start and the other of half are added as the minigame progresses. The five starter types include [[Mario]], [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]], [[Fire Flower]], [[Starman|Star]], and [[Lakitu's Cloud|Cloud]]. The five types added later on include [[Luigi]], [[Goomba]], [[Bowser]], [[Boo]], and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]. The only difference between this configuration and Pair-a-Gone is that the Luigi card type is not available from the start.
