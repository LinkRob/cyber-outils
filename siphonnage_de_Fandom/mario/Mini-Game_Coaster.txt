'''Mini-Game Coaster''' is a special mode in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. Here, the player can compete in mini-games to reach the end and get rewards. Each higher difficulty setting features more mini-games to compete in than the previous ones. All 4-player, 2 vs. 2, & 1 vs. 3 mini-games, except [[Dungeon Dash]] and [[Rainbow Run (mini-game)|Rainbow Run]] are featured here.
