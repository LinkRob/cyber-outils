A '''Magic Growing Potion''' is an item in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Princess, I Shrunk the Mario Brothers]]."

==Description==
The Magic Growing Potion is a red concoction that can enlarge its user several times their original size. It has the opposite function as the [[Magic Shrink Potion]], of which it serves as an antidote to.

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
[[Waldo the Wizard|Waldo]] creates a flask of Magic Growing Potion some time after [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] accidentally shrinks [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] with the Magic Shrink Potion. Toad takes the Magic Growing Potion and inserts it into the spray gun that [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] initially used to spray pesticide at Mario and Luigi. King Koopa corners Mario and Luigi in Waldo's greenhouse and sprays them, unaware that his spray gun contains the Magic Growing Potion, as well as some potted [[vegetable]]s. This not only results in Mario and Luigi growing back to their original size, but the nearby vegetables also grow large as well, so the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] use the enlarged vegetables to scare off King Koopa and his [[Koopa Troopa]]s.
