The term "Moon" can refer to:

== Locations ==

* The [[Moon Kingdom]], the place where [[Mario]] and [[Cappy]] invade [[Bowser|Bowser's]] forced wedding with [[Princess Peach|Peach]] in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]].''
** The [[Dark Side|Dark]] and [[Darker Side|Darker Sides]] are also locations on the Moon in the same game.
* [[X-Naut Fortress]], the place where the [[Secret Society of X-Nauts]] come from in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]].''
* [[Mushroom Moon]], more commonly known as the Moon, the place where it orbits around the [[Mushroom Earth|planet]].

== Items ==

* A [[3-Up Moon]], a rare item with three times the power of a [[1-Up Mushroom]], first appearing in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.
* A [[Power Moon]], the main collectible in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]].''
** A [[Multi Moon]], a rarer collectible in the same game, with three times the power as it's singular counterpart. Typically earned from defeating [[Boss|bosses]].
