'''Poltergust 5000''' is the first mission of the [[Gloomy Manor]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Objective==
[[Professor Elvin Gadd]] has left the [[Poltergust 5000 (object)|Poltergust 5000]] in the [[Garage]] of the [[Gloomy Manor]], so he sends [[Luigi]] to retrieve it. After being pixelated outside of the manor, Luigi first has to chase down a key from a mouse to gain access into the mansion. He then enters the Garage and retrieves the Poltergust 5000. He finds a key in a treasure chest, and after vacuuming some dust blocking the door, he heads to the [[Mudroom]] and collects a key. He uses the keys to gain access to the [[Guard Hall]] and [[Foyer (Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon)|Foyer]].

He finds two [[Greenie]]s who place the Strobulb on a chandelier, but Luigi rushes up the stairs to retrieve it. He uses the flash to open the door to the [[Coatroom]], and finds a Greenie showering. As Luigi tries to leave, he is forced to fight two Greenies. Once he leaves the room, he'll have to capture four more Greenies to complete the mission.

==Description==
*''If we're to restore the Dark Moon, we'll need to get you properly equipped. I've got just the device for the job, but I left it in the Gloomy Manor! Mind fetching it for me, sonny?''

==Goals==
;Mission Goal:
''Find the Poltergust 5000 in the Garage, and capture some ghosts for E. Gadd.''

*Search the Garage.
*Capture any nearby ghosts.

==Boo location==
*This Boo cannot be captured until after completing [[Visual Tricks]], where Luigi gets the [[Dark-Light Device]]. In the Garage, use it to reveal a hidden wheel in the far right corner to expose it.
