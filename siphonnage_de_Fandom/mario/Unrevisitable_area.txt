An '''unrevisitable area''' is any location which is inaccessible after a certain point. Unrevisitable areas are primarily found in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' role-playing games where the player is automatically taken to such places during the storyline and may contain items or accessories that [[Missable content|can be missed]]. Some platformer games that allow the player to roam on world maps may contain stages that can only be accessed once on the world map, or near the beginning of the game.

Games with a linear and irreversible stage progression, such as ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', are not listed on this page.

==''Super Mario'' series==
===''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''===
In the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' version, World 9 can no longer be played again for the current save file if the player used any Warp Zone while completing Worlds A-D.

===''[[Super Mario World]]''===
The four switch palace levels, [[Yellow Switch Palace]], [[Green Switch Palace]], [[Red Switch Palace]] and [[Blue Switch Palace]], cannot be accessed again after Mario steps on the respective switches. In the Japanese version of the game, [[castle]]s and [[fortress]]es cannot be revisited once Mario has destroyed them.

===''[[Super Mario Land 2 - 6 Golden Coins]]''===
[[Gate Course|Mushroom Zone]] cannot be accessed again through standard means after completing it.

Stages containing bosses cannot be accessed again after defeating the respective bosses in each zone [[Game Over|unless the player runs out of lives]], which results in losing the [[Golden Coin]]s they have collected.

===''[[Super Mario 64]]'' / ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''===
Bowser's Sub in [[Dire, Dire Docks]] cannot be boarded again after completing [[Bowser in the Fire Sea]] which makes replaying the mission slightly more difficult due to the [[Power Star]] now floating in midair without any solid ground below it. In ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', the Submarine is permanently gone once [[Bowser in the Fire Sea]] is completed however in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', the Submarine can be brought back by preforming a glitch to beat [[Bowser in the Sky]] without [[Mario]].

===''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''===
The short side-scrolling stage for the [[Star Festival]] at the beginning of the game cannot be replayed after using the first [[Launch Star]].

===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' / ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''===
The [[Sprixie House]]s where [[Stamp]]s are obtained cannot be revisited after collecting their Stamps.

===''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''===
The short introductory course before Bowser becomes [[Castle Bowser]] cannot be replayed.

==''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''==
[[Course No.10]] in [[Mt. Teapot]] is replaced with [[Course No.13]], an altered version of itself, once the player hits the giant [[! Block]] in [[Course No.12]].

==''Yoshi's Island'' series==
===''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''===
The tutorial level [[Welcome To Yoshi's Island]] cannot be visited again after completing the level or exiting it from the menu. In ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', however, this level appears again at the end of the secret stage [[Endless World of Yoshis]].

===''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''===
Similar to Welcome to Yoshi's Island in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', the tutorial level, [[Welcome Back to Yoshi's Island!]] cannot be visited again after completing the level.

==''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''==
The alcove of the [[Peach's Castle|Mushroom Castle]] can only be visited once in the SNES version, during the first visit, right before meeting the [[Chancellor]]. After Mario goes to the Chancellor's room, the alcove becomes inaccessible.

The vault in [[Peach's Castle|Mushroom Castle]] can only be visited twice; the first time right after meeting the [[Chancellor]], and the second time during the [[Shymore]] invasion. After defeating [[Claymorton]], this room cannot be accessed again.

After defeating [[Belome]] in [[Kero Sewers]], the room where he is fought is flooded and cannot be visited; entering the pipe that previously led to that room will transport Mario directly to [[Midas River]].

After racing [[Booster]] in the [[Booster Hill]] track, the race cannot be replayed.

After defeating [[Culex]] in [[Monstro Town]] in the SNES version, the room where he is fought cannot be visited.

[[Count Down]]'s room in the [[Weapon World]] cannot be revisited after defeating it and leaving the area, as the springboard that takes Mario to this area now takes him to another area instead.

==''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''==
Once the first seven bosses are defeated, [[Troff 'n' Scoff]]'s area is no longer accessible as the portal vanishes once that level's boss is defeated.

All of the [[Bonus Barrel]] [[Bonus room#Donkey Kong 64|minigames]] not playable at [[Snide's H.Q.]] cannot be replayed after all instances of them have been completed. These include [[Busy Barrel Barrage!]], [[Splish-Splash Salvage!]], [[Minecart Mayhem!]], [[Speedy Swing Sortie!]], [[Stash Snatch!]], [[Mad Maze Maul!]], and [[Stealthy Snoop!]]. The [[Training Barrel]]s cannot be reentered after completing them, and the ten level-specific minigames in [[Hideout Helm]] cannot be replayed after shutting down the [[Blast-o-Matic]].

==''Paper Mario'' series==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
After defeating the [[Koopa Bros.]] in Chapter 1, the large jail cell in [[Koopa Bros. Fortress]] where Mario and his team meet [[Bombette]] has its hole patched up and it cannot be entered again.

After the Chapter 3 intermission, the interior of the secret passageway is inaccessible manually. While it can still be entered, the player cannot control the movement of Peach in the interior of the passageway after this point, as Peach automatically moves to the exit of the passageway immediately upon entering it.

After defeating [[Fuzzipede]], the inside of the [[Whale (character)|whale]]'s body can no longer be navigated.

After the eruption of [[Mt. Lavalava]] at the end of Chapter 5, the bottom of the room just before [[Lava Piranha]] becomes unsuitable for Mario and his team to walk on, preventing the chambers beyond (three chambers) from being accessed (namely, the room where the Lava Piranha is fought, the room connected to the Lava Piranha's chamber where Bombette blows the crack and the room where the treasure of the final seed is found by [[Kolorado]]).

In Chapter 6, after talking to [[Lily (Paper Mario)|Lily]] and retrieving the [[Water Stone]] for her, her basin as well as the lower part of the previous area cannot be traversed again.

After the Chapter 6 intermission, the secret passageway cannot be entered again altogether, especially when Mario and his team reach Peach's Castle.

[[Madam Merlar]]'s cavern in [[Shiver Mountain]] becomes inaccessible after Mario places the [[Star Stone]] in the pedestal outside, causing flights of stairs to rise up and block the entrance.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
[[Bowser's Castle]] is inaccessible after finishing the cutscene (after Chapter 1 intermission), which is exclusive to Bowser.

In the GameCube original, the red minor and major league rooms in the [[Glitz Pit]] are always accessible at any time as Mario can rank up and down. In [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|the remake]], Mario can no longer rank down, only up, making these rooms inaccessible after Mario climbs up the ranks a second time.

The interior of the [[Black Skull]] cannot be revisited once it leaves the [[Pirate's Grotto]] at the end of Chapter 5. 

The baggage car and the rooftops of the [[Excess Express]] cannot be revisited after defeating [[Smorg]] in Chapter 6, outside of using a glitch.

If the player has executed the glitch that allows them to keep [[Flavio]] permanently in the original, they will never be able to access the [[Excess Express]] again once Chapter 6 is beaten as the glitch requires skipping obtaining the [[Train Ticket]] to work.

In the [[Palace of Shadow]] lies a hidden room connected to [[Gloomtail]]'s chamber by a passageway that can be accessed by blowing up the eastern wall with [[Admiral Bobbery]], which contains an [[Ultra Mushroom]] and a [[Jammin' Jelly]] (a [[Gradual Syrup]], a [[Shooting Star (item)|Shooting Star]] and a [[Mystery]] in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]). Once Mario inserts all eight [[Palace Key (Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door)|Palace Key]]s in the [[Palace of Shadow Tower|Riddle Tower]], the floors of the four hallways leading to Gloomtail's chamber give way, preventing these rooms from being accessed again.

None of [[Bowser]]'s platforming stages can be revisited once they are cleared.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
The generator rooms with the [[Ackpow]]s in [[Merlee's Mansion]] cannot be revisited once 1,000,000 [[Rubee]]s are collected, and given to [[Mimi]].

The [[Fort Francis]] dungeon room that contains a single [[Chain Chomp]] cannot be revisited once [[Thoreau]] is used to carry the block on the button switch that reveals a Warp Pipe, which requires the heroes to go back up to the basement room.

During the events of the game, [[Sammer's Kingdom]] is destroyed by [[The Void]], leaving a blank area called the [[World of Nothing]]. After the game is completed, Sammer's Kingdom is restored and World of Nothing cannot be accessed again.

After the [[Duel of 100]] is completed post-game, all of Sammer's Kingdom ([[Sammer's Early Duels]], [[Sammer's Proving Grounds]], and [[Sammer's Endgame]]) after Gate 1 is sealed off and cannot be accessed again.

===''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''===
The ledge where the [[Wooden Bridge]] scrap is obtained in [[Warm Fuzzy Plains]] is inaccessible after Mario collects it.

===''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''===
The airship near the entrance to [[the Crimson Tower]] cannot be revisited after Mario hits the airship's self-destruct switch with his [[hammer]].

While sailing through the [[Violet Passage]], Mario must use the [[Compass]] to navigate past an area with purple fog, which brings the [[Legendary Captain]]'s ship to the purple [[Mini Paint Star]]. This particular area is inaccessible when sailing through on repeat visits, except with the use of a [[Paper Mario: Color Splash/Glitches|glitch]] to clip past an invisible wall to enter and exit the cabin while in the area with the Mini Paint Star.

The majority of [[Black Bowser's Castle]] (namely, the room where [[Roy Koopa]] is fought, the conveyor belt room, the room after passing through the conveyor belt, the same room after the black paint factory is destroyed and the room where [[Mario]] has chase with the black paint) cannot be revisited after Mario destroys the black paint factory and escapes through the pipe to the entrance of the castle.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''===
The dungeon of [[Peach's Castle]] cannot be revisited after escaping from it at the beginning of the game. Falling into the trapdoor that led to this area in Peach's Castle on the second visit would cause [[Mario]] to [[Game Over|lose all of his HP]].

The area where the [[Earth Vellumental]] is fought cannot be revisited after Olivia raises the dirt platform they are sitting on.

After the lake in [[Autumn Mountain]] is flooded by [[Olivia]], its basin and the canyon to the left of it cannot be accessed again.

The stages of the [[Big Sho' Theater]] cannot be revisited after clearing each of their challenges, with the exception of the first one. However, Mario can watch the Shy Guy ballet again if he talks to a Shy Guy in the lobby.

None of the underwater sections in [[the Great Sea]] can be revisited after collecting their respective treasures and clearing the purple streamer.

The top of the shelf (reached via a high jump from [[Luigi]]), where the forbidden Fold of Life book is obtained in the [[Origami Craftsman]]'s room of his house, is inaccessible upon pulling the [[Olly Tape]] and retrieving it.

The trial temples in [[Diamond Island (Paper Mario: The Origami King)|Diamond Island]] cannot be entered again after completing their trials.

The airship shooting section cannot be replayed after completing it.

[[Hotfoot Crater]] cannot be accessed again after using the [[airship]] cannon to launch out of it.

The entrance hallway of [[Peach's Castle]] is visited only twice: first in the beginning of the game and then after escaping [[Hotfoot Crater]], after the castle transforms into [[Origami Castle]], the entrance hallway cannot be accessed again.

==''Mario & Luigi'' series==
===''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' / ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''===
[[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s [[Mario Bros.' House|house]] cannot be revisited after [[Toad]] reaches the bathroom, or [[Peach's Castle]] grounds (also known as [[Toad Town|Toadtown Square]]) after talking to [[Bowser]] to leave for the [[Beanbean Kingdom]].

The [[Koopa Cruiser]] can only be visited twice: the first visit is during the flight to the Beanbean Kingdom, which becomes inaccessible upon battling [[Fawful]]; the second visit is after [[Joke's End]], where Luigi navigates through the repaired ship in reverse order before collecting the [[Beanstar]].

The Beanbean Borderlands on the Mushroom Kingdom side of [[Stardust Fields]] cannot be revisited after crossing the border to the Beanbean Kingdom.

The Training Area where the [[Starshade Bros.]] teach the Mario Bros. their techniques after learning the [[High Jump]] and [[Spin Jump]] cannot be revisited after completing the tutorial. In the remake, this area can be briefly revisited by practicing the High Jump and Spin Jump moves in the guide.

The underground caverns beneath [[Chucklehuck Woods]], visited after defeating [[Chuckolator]], cannot be revisited after Luigi interacts with the barrel of [[Chuckola Reserve]] with Mario inside it.

The gates to [[Guffawha Ruins]] are sealed after [[Bowletta]] kidnaps [[Princess Peach|Peach]], preventing the interior of the area from being accessed again, especially after the Mario Bros. obtain the [[Ultra Hammer]]s from the [[Hammerhead Bros.]] in their cave. The ledge with the yellow pipe in [[Little Fungitown]] is too high for Mario and Luigi to reach without certain glitches.

The interior of the [[S.S. Chuckola]] cannot be revisited once it heads out to sea and sinks in the [[Oho Ocean Seabed]].

The caverns where the [[Jellyfish Sisters]] teach the Mario Bros. their techniques cannot be accessed again after collecting both [[Pearl Bean]]s. In the remake, this area can be briefly revisited by practicing the [[Dash (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Dash]] and [[Fire Tickle]] moves in the guide.

The stairway that leads to the four big candles on the fourth floor of [[Joke's End]] and the small place where [[Mario]] lights the candles become inaccessible once Mario lights all the candles and returns to the third floor, with the door closing shut behind him.

In [[Beanbean Castle Town]], the northwestern, southwestern, and southeastern houses in the western half of the town are inaccessible when the player visits it for the first time, and become inaccessible again after the town's second destruction when the Mario Bros. return after defeating [[Popple]] and [[Birdo]] in [[Teehee Valley]]. At that point in the game, the northern double-entranced house and southeastern house in the eastern half cannot be entered again as well.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''===
The non-transformed Peach's Castle in the past is not revisitable after [[Baby Bowser]] is defeated. 

The player cannot revisit the [[Koopa Cruiser]], where they first learned how to control [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]], after its crash landing into [[Bowser's Castle]]. 

The interior of [[Young Professor Elvin Gadd]]'s lab on [[Thwomp Volcano]] cannot be entered again after the fire is doused. 

The [[Shroob Mother Ship]] cannot be revisited after the four bros. are ejected from it. 

None of the tutorial courses can be revisited after completing them.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' / ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''===
The non-transformed Peach's Castle at the beginning of the game cannot be revisited after [[Bowser]] eats the [[Vacuum Shroom]] to inhale everyone in the [[Conference Hall]]. 

The areas with the dirt piles in both the [[Toad Town Caves]] and the [[Underground Tunnel]] cannot be revisited once [[Project K]] is completed when Bowser arrives in the [[Treasure Chamber]] in [[Bowser's Castle]]. 

In the ''Bowser Jr.'s Journey'' mode, the first level in the game, [[First Battle!]], cannot be replayed after beating it.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''===
When Mario first enters the [[Dream World]], he appears in a rift and moves in a similar manner to [[Luiginary Antigravity]] to reach the portal to [[Dreamy Pi'illo Castle]]. In subsequent visits to the Dream World, this rift is not visited again.

In [[Mushrise Park]], north of the entrance to [[Somnom Woods]], there is a secluded area which is not connected to Mushrise Park's main map. However, after [[The Zeekeeper]] shoots down the [[Neo Bowser Castle]] after Somnom Woods visit at the game's climax, it becomes the first section of [[Neo Bowser Castle]], and the original version (the area which was part of [[Mushrise Park]]) cannot be revisited again.

The last two rooms of Area G in [[Dreamy Neo Bowser Castle]], where Mario and [[Dreamy Luigi]] fight [[Giant Bowser|Bowser]], become inaccessible after defeating Bowser. The cannon that leads to the first area stops functioning, marked by a green X above it. The pipe used to exit the second area ends in the ceiling of another area, in a game where pipes can only be entered while grounded. No objects are available to access the pipe otherwise.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''===
The storage room in [[Peach's Castle]] cannot be accessed again after the paper characters are released from the book.

There is a mandatory tutorial mission in Peach's Castle at the start of the game, in which several [[Paper Toad]]s are found. This mission cannot be replayed after it is completed, as it does not appear in the [[Lakitu Info Center]] mission list.

After defeating [[Bowser Jr.]] and [[Paper Bowser Jr.]], Bowser's Castle in its regular state becomes inaccessible once it rises from its pool of lava and becomes [[Neo Bowser Castle]]. 

Before Neo Bowser Castle is accessed for the first time, the Mario trio must manually climb the [[Paper Toad]] stairway from [[Mount Brrr]] to the castle. On subsequent visits, the Paper Toad stairway cannot be climbed manually as the Mario trio will automatically move between each location.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''===
[[File:M&L-B Closed Bowser's Castle.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Bowser's Castle when the door is closed]]

In the [[Lushgreen Sea, Great Lighthouse Island|Lushgreen Sea Great Lighthouse]] and [[Color-Full Sea, Great Lighthouse Island|Color-Full Sea Great Lighthouse]], several passageways cannot be revisited after the player inserts both plugs into the sockets in front of the big gate, since the gate that opens upon inserting one of the plugs remain closed (in both lighthouses). Even if one of the plugs is inserted, it can still be removed from a socket and be placed elsewhere, but both cannot be removed once they are inserted.

[[Fortress Zokket]] can only be visited twice: first in the [[Gulchrock Sea]], and next in the [[Stormstar Sea]]. After the second visit and defeating [[Zokket]], Fortress Zokket is destroyed and absorbed into the [[Soli-Tree]] and cannot be accessed again.

The illusory Mushroom Kingdom Mario and Luigi are trapped in cannot be revisited after the Bros. respond with "no" to the fake Peach three times when she confronts them in the castle hallway.

The main area of [[Bowser's Castle]] on [[Wayaway Island]] cannot be revisited by normal means after [[Bowser]] blasts away [[Reclusa]]'s minions in the [[Soli-Tree]], since the front gate is locked. However, the player can use a glitch to bypass the door<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ATmQPNCdZI</ref>, and the hangar area on the right where the airship part was found remains accessible.

==''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' / ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'' / ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD]]''==
After pressing the rocket switches in [[Cog Jog (level)|Cog Jog]], [[Switcheroo]], and [[Music Madness]], the rooms where they are found cannot be accessed again even if the levels are replayed. In ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'', only [[Switcheroo]] remains inaccessible, the other two can be accessed again.

[[File:CloudGateway.jpg|thumb|250px|The short segment before the Golden Temple gateway in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'']]
In ''Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D'' and ''Donkey Kong Country Returns HD'', after using the [[Rare Orb]]s to grant access to the [[Golden Temple]] and, by extension, the [[Cloud (world)|Cloud]] world, the Golden Temple will become the "Cloud Gateway" on the world map, and the player will not be able to replay the segment just before entering the temple. Instead, Donkey Kong is immediately transported to the Cloud world upon selecting the Cloud Gateway on the world map.

==''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]''==
The areas visited during cutscenes in [[Episode 1]], [[Episode 2]], and [[Episode 3]] cannot be accessed again after completing the respective cutscenes.

==''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''==
After defeating Bowser in {{world link|8|bowserp|World 8-Bowser's Castle (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)}}, the player faces [[Bowser Jr.]] and Bowser and [[Koopalings|His Minions]] in a multicolored void for the final battle. Once Bowser joins the player's team, the latter area cannot be revisited.

==''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]''==
In the [[Paranormal Productions]], the cameras in each of the four sets cannot be viewed after obtaining the elevator button from [[Morty]], preventing [[Gooigi]] from being able to revisit the alternative versions of those sets while [[Luigi]] is viewing the cameras.

The [[Pit Chamber]] and the [[Hidden Passageway]] of the [[Tomb Suites]] cannot be revisited after escaping from them.

==''Mario + Rabbids'' series==
===''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''===
None of the bonus rooms in both the main game and the [[Donkey Kong Adventure]] DLC can be revisited after completing their challenges and obtaining the weapons found in them.

===''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''===
The [[Peach's Castle]] courtyard cannot be revisited after Mario and [[Rabbid Peach]] are taken by the [[Darkmess Manta]], which, in turn, cannot be revisited as it consists only of several tutorial battles, and thus not explorable at all. As a result, neither the main quest ([[An "Overall" Nice Day]]) nor its tutorial battles ([[From Cover to Cover]], [[Tough Egg to Crack]], [[Shot in the Darkmess]] and [[Headlong into the Unknown]]) can be replayed after completing them.

After defeating [[Cursa]], [[Cursa's stronghold]] disappears from the world map and can no longer be freely explored, though the Quest battles can be played again from the Quests menu.

==''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''==
The inside of the [[Great Harmoneel]]'s stomach in [[Groove Layer]] cannot be accessed again after [[Pauline]] is rescued.
