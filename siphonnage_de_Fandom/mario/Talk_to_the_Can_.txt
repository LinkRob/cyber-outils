'''Talk to the Can!''' is one of [[Dribble and Spitz|Dribble & Spitz]]'s microgames in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

== Gameplay ==
The player has to use a repellent to spray away any pest. If done successfully, the player wins the microgame, but if unsuccessful, they lose at it.

===Difficulty levels===
The type of pest differs between difficulty levels, and each increasing difficulty level requires more repellent to be sprayed.

* '''Level 1''': The pests are flies, which can be killed from a little bit of insect repellent.
* '''Level 2''': The pests are cockroaches, which can be killed from an average amount of insect repellent.
* '''Level 3''': The pest is a bear, which can be killed by spraying lots of insect repellent at its eyes and following its movement.

== Trivia ==
* The microgame name is a pun on the idiom "talk to the hand", referring to a rude way of dismissing or interrupting a person.
