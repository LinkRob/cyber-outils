'''Boo Cinema''' is a course from [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']], featured in the [[Leaf Cup]] as the third course.

== Description ==
Boo Cinema is set in the north section of the map, more specifically in the east part of the forest area. It is set around a ghostly theatre, featuring a sepia-tinted section with giant film strips that can be driven on. It is accessed by driving through various [[Ghost Valley 1|Ghost]] [[Ghost Valley 2|Valley]] [[Ghost Valley 3|courses]]. Its movie screen features a movie titled ''Boo Castle'', which stars [[Princess Peach]] in her vacation outfit.

Boo Cinema's position is to the northwest of [[Starview Peak]], the east-by-southeast of [[Acorn Heights]], the northeast of the amalgamation of the [[Mario Circuit 1|first three]] [[Mario Circuit 2|Mario]] [[Mario Circuit 3|Circuits]] from [[Super Mario Kart|''Super Mario Kart'']], and the north of [[Dandelion Depths]].

The top of the map faces to the northwest.

== Layout ==

=== Starting Area ===
The racers start at the entrance.

=== Gaps in the Seats ===
As the racers drive through the seats, they pass between the seats via gaps which would be used by theatre patrons to get from seat to seat.
