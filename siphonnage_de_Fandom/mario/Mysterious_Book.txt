The '''Mysterious Book'''<ref>[https://play.nintendo.com/media/pics/mario-luigi-paper-jam-story/ Luigi’s at it again | Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam Story - Play Nintendo]. ([https://web.archive.org/web/20210810004458/https://play.nintendo.com/media/pics/mario-luigi-paper-jam-story/ Archived] August 10, 2021, 00:44:58 UTC via Wayback Machine)</ref> is a key plot element in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. It contains the characters from the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' universe]], as well as its paper world.
== In [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi series'']] ==

===''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''===

[[File:MLPJ story01.webp|thumb|[[Toad (species)|Toad]] and [[Luigi]] see the mysterious book glowing.]]

The origins behind the book creation are unknown, however it was left in the attic of [[Princess Peach's Castle]] at some point prior ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]].'' While [[Luigi]] and a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] search for the source of a draft, they discover the book on the bookshelf. Startled by a [[Scaredy Rat]], Luigi knocks the book down, causing it accidently to open and release various Paper characters into [[Mushroom Earth|the real world]]. [[Mario]], Luigi and [[Starlow]] team up with [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] to track down the various [[Paper Toad|Paper Toads]] that have been released, while [[Bowser]] unites with [[Paper Bowser]].

After the heroes make their way through [[Doop Doop Dunes]], they lose the book to [[Bowser Jr.|Bowser Jr]]. and [[Paper Bowser Jr.]], who were supposed intend to destroy it so they can stay together, but they ultimately decide to keep it. Bowser and Paper Bowser discover the book themselves and intend to trap the Mario Bros. in the book and then destroy it. However, prior to that, the Bowsers plan to betray each other afterward, trapping the other in the book along with the trio, which they fail in doing.

After defeating [[Shiny RoboBowser]], Mario, Luigi, and Paper Mario trap Paper Bowser back in the book and ultimately use the book to return all of the Paper characters and Paper terrain back to their own world.

==Trivia==
*The storybook that tells the events of ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', as shown in the game's opening, resembles identical to the mysterious book, the main difference being that the former depicts a [[Sticker Comet]] on its cover instead of a [[Mushroom]]. 
*What happened to the Mysterious Book afterwards is unknown. It is possible the book was kept safety secured in the attic without ever to fall down again.
