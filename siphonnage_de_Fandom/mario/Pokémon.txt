'''Pokémon''' are a group of creatures that are the central focus of the highly successful [[w:c:pokemon:Pokémon (franchise)|''Pokémon'' franchise]] (which made its debut with ''Pokemon Red/Green'' in 1996 on [[Game Boy]]). There is a wide variety of Pokémon, specifically over a thousand, each taking inspiration from a variety of living things, including animals, insects, plants, and mythical creatures such as dragons. A Pokémon has at least one elemental association, known as a [[w:c:pokemon:type|type]], which often influences its moveset and abilities. The most well-known Pokémon and mascot of the franchise is [[Pikachu]], a electrical mouse. Despite ''Pokémon'' being one of [[Nintendo]]'s flagship series alongside ''[[Mario]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', Pokémon in general have sometimes, although rarely, made appearances within ''Mario''-related media, not having anywhere near as many appearances as [[Link]] from ''The Legend of Zelda'' or even [[Samus Aran]] from the ''[[w:c:metroid:Metroid (series)|Metroid]]'' franchise.

==In [[Game Boy Camera]]==
This is the first time that Pokémon appear in media with ''Mario'' content, although both are referenced in the form of customizable graphics. The first time that Pikachu and other Pokémon physically appear in a game with ''Mario'' characters is ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]]. The [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] in general is the most notable instance of Pokémon appearing alongside ''Mario'' characters, let alone several other video game franchises in general.

==In [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]==
Pokémon have only made a few cameos.

===''[[Mario Artist: Paint Studio]]''===
Artwork of all 151 [[w:c:pokemon:Generation I|Generation I]] Pokémon are each available to place as stamps while designing a picture.

===''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!! Bonus Disc]]''===
Pokémon appear in the ''[[Pokémon Colosseum Promo]]'' trailer.

===''[[Super Mario Maker]]''===
Certain Pokémon such as Pikachu, [[Lucario]], and [[Charizard]] appear as [[Mystery Mushroom]] [[Costume Mario|costumes]]. Most of the Pokémon costumes are the same as the Pokémon fighters from ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]'', with the exception of the Generation I [[w:c:pokemon:Starter Pokémon|Starter Pokémon]]: [[Bulbasaur]], [[Charmander]], and [[Squirtle]].

==In other media==
===[[Wario's Warehouse]]===
Pokémon are mentioned by Wario in the few articles centered around ''[[w:c:pokemon:Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire|Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire Versions]]''.
