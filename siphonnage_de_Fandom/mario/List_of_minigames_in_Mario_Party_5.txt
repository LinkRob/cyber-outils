{{DISPLAYTITLE:List of minigames in ''Mario Party 5''}}
This is a list of mini-games in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. ''Mario Party 5'' has a total of 77 mini-games, including some of the extra mini-games found in Bonus Mode, having in total nine classifications of the mini-games. Due to Donkey Kong becoming his own space, this game introduces DK mini-games to the series, where players can collect bananas in those mini-games to exchange for coins. Duel mini-games also return from ''Mario Party 3'', whereas dueling is absent in ''Mario Party 4''. Mini-game titles are often wordplays, alliterations, and assonance, localized differently in other regions. ''Mario Party 5'' has 23 4-Player mini-games, 12 1-vs-3 mini-games, 12 2-vs-2 mini-games, six Battle mini-games, 15 Duel mini-games, three Bowser mini-games, three DK mini-games, one Story mini-game, and two Bonus mini-games. Mini-games marked in gold are coin-collecting mini-games.
