An '''Orb User''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Orb Users are strange, magical staff-like enemies only encountered in [[Booster Tower]]. They have a more powerful variant named [[Orbison]], which is encountered later on.

== Battle ==
In battle, Orb Users are capable of using spells such as [[Flame Wall]] for offense and [[Recover]] to heal. However, Orb Users are well known for their very low HP but extremely high defense as most attacks deal 1 point of damage to them.
