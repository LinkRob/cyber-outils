The '''4-5-6 Dice Block''' is a type of [[Dice Block]] that debuted in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. It reappears in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Description==
The 4-5-6 Dice Block is a dark purple [[Dice Block]] having exclusively 4, 5 and 6 on it's sides. The Dice Block is used for having a guaranteed chance of getting 4, 5 or 6. This Dice Block appeared in two [[Captain Event]]s of [[Blooper Beach]].
