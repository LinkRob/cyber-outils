Also of note is that several characters mentioned have only been seen in the {{series|WarioWare}} and the other majority are from ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' sports titles. This list also includes humans from the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} and other crossovers. Note that this list differs from the one on the template as that one contains only canon humans rather than ones from all the ''Mario'' media.
