The {{Nihongo|'''Vacuum Helmet'''|すいとりマシーン|Suitori Mashīn|Absorbing Machine}} is an item that belongs to [[Fawful]]. It made its debut appearance in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. Fawful uses this headgear to attack his foes or fly.

== In the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]] ==
=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''/''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|Bowser's Minions]]'' ===
Fawful used his headgear to suck up [[Princess Peach]]'s voice when he and [[Cackletta]] pay a visit. (The latter impersonated the Goodwill Ambassador, who resembles and may have been [[Lady Lima]].) When [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] and [[Bowser]] encountered Fawful, he used his headgear to injure Bowser, forcing the brothers to battle him. Fawful used his headgear to attack the brothers, which can be jumped over (landing on the laser counts as not jumping at all). After he loses his headgear, Fawful attempts ramming into the brothers. After battle, Fawful used the headgear to destroy the [[Koopa Cruiser]].

In [[Woohoo Hooniversity]], Fawful used the helmet to suck [[Cackletta]]'s soul to recover her and meanwhile he let Cackletta enter Bowser's body to become [[Bowletta]].

=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''/''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' ===
Fawful used the headgear to sunction the [[Dark Star]]'s power, transforming himself into [[Dark Fawful]], which allowed him to fawfulize [[Princess Peach's castle]]. Fawful used his headgear during battle and the Vacuum Helmet is absent in the rest of the game.

== Stats ==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Vacuum Helmet has a speed of 1, meaning Mario and Luigi always attack first. It has 18 [[Heart Point|hit points]], an [[Power|attack power]] of 13, and a [[defense]] of 13, and its defeat nets 2 coins and no additional loot, but no [[Experience Point|Experience]] is gained. Nothing special happens from striking the Vacuum Helmet with a [[Firebrand|fire-based]] or [[Thunderhand|electrical]] attack, but the basic [[Hammer]] and Luigi's standard [[Thunder Bros.]] ([[Advance Command|Advancing]] the latter allows a hit.) do not work.

''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' portrays the Vacuum Helmet with 1,200 hit points, an attack power of 180, a defense of 123, and a speed of 130 (1,800, 450, 185, and 195 respectively if either Bro. has the Challenge Medal equipped). [[POW-Down|STAT]]-[[DEF-Down|Downs]] have their likelihood halved, but [[Dizzy|stunning]] and [[Burn|burning]] the Vacuum Helmet cannot be done. Defeating it nets 220 experience, but no loot is gained.
