A '''Lucky Clover''' is an item in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Description==
The Lucky Clover resembles a four-leaf clover, hence its name. If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] uses a Lucky Clover before a dance, he gets up to four misses without being penalized on the dance meter.

==History==
In Story Mode, the Lucky Clover can be bought at any shop run by a [[Lakitu]] for 50 [[coin]]s. Mario or Luigi can carry up to three Lucky Clovers at a time.
