==Appearance==
Harry T. is similar in appearance to the other [[Toad]] shopkeeper at [[Shroom Grocery]].

==History==
In the game, Harry T. runs an [[Item Shop]] named after [[Harry's Shop|himself]] and is the only clerk there. During the events of Chapter 4, a [[Shy Guy]] has stolen the [[Storeroom Key|key]] to his storage room and Harry T. asks [[Mario]] to bring it back. After Mario does so, Harry is excited to have it and unlocks the room for Mario allowing him to take anything that is inside.

==Trivia==
*If Mario opens the door to the storage room without giving the key to Harry T. first, Harry T. will say that he is happy Mario has found the key, but preferred Mario asked for permission first before opening it.
*Harry T. is one of two shopkeepers in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' to have confirmed names with the other being [[Igor]] of [[Boo's Shop]].
