The '''Snow Kingdom''' is the location in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== History ==
The Snow Kingdom is ruled by the [[Penguin King]]. It concealed a [[Starman|Super Star]] before [[Bowser (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Bowser]] and the [[Koopa Troop]] destroyed the kingdom and claimed (stole) the Super Star, which he planned to use to help him marry Peach.
