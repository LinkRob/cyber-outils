'''''Mario Artist: Communication Kit''''' is the third installment of the [[Mario Artist (series)|''Mario Artist'' series]] for the [[Nintendo 64DD]]. It was released exclusively in Japan on June 29, 2000. Users could use the application to connect to RandNetDD and share their creations from any of the released ''Mario Artist'' titles with others.
