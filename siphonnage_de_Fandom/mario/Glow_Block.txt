A '''Glow Block''' is a type of [[Block]] which first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Description==
Glow Blocks are bright, white Blocks found in dark areas. [[Mario]] or another playable character can pick up a Glow Block to illuminate some of their surroundings.
