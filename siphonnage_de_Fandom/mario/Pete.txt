==History==
Pete is a [[Flip-Flopian]] that lives in [[Flopside]] and can be found near Flopsid Tower. Pete is known for being a huge fan of towers and often talks about them nonstop. Pete spends all of his time standing outside Flopside Tower admiring it. Pete is an interior designer but due to looking at Flopside Tower a lot, his job requests often pile up.
==Trivia==
*Pete's [[Flipside]] counterpart is [[Skeet (character)|Skeet]] .
