'''Snowflake Lake''' is a [[board]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Snowflake Lake night.jpg|thumb|Night variation]]
Snowflake Lake is a winter-themed board that is similar to [[Chilly Waters]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. Some background details include conifers, dog houses, and a few snowmen, along with a ski lift at the top right corner, and there are ice sculptures of a [[penguin]], [[Mario]], and his 8-bit form. In the center of the board is a small frozen pond that contains what appears to be the skeleton of a [[Bone Dragon]]. A [[Whomp]] blocks a path in front of an [[Orb Shop]] and charges a character 10 coins to pass. Going this way will take the player to the right side of the frozen pond.

To get [[Star (Mario Party series)|Stars]], players steal them from other players by riding [[Chain Chomp]]s (by seeking them from Chain Chomp dog houses). Each player starts with 5 Stars (or more depending on the number of Star handicaps). In Team Mode, each team starts with 10 stars instead. When a player reaches a doghouse, they can pay coins to ride a Chain Chomp. During the daytime, a ride costs 20 coins, and the player determines the spaces they move with one [[Dice Block]]. At night, players can purchase up to three Dice Blocks per ride, with each one costing 10 coins. Chain Chomp riders that reach other players squish them, including teammates, and steal their Star. Likely due to this unique ruleset, the board has many [[Duel Space]]s. It is the only board in ''Mario Party 6'' where a Duel Space can be reached on the first turn.

Despite the fact this board doesn't have any [[Star (Mario Party series)|star]]s, [[Donkey Kong]] via [[DK Space]]s is the only way where new stars can be received.

During the night, large [[Freezie]]s appear, blocking the paths to the middle of the board, forcing characters to take longer routes to get there. When it is daytime, the Freezies melt into the ground.

The board [[Pyramid Park]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' also involves using Chain Chomps to steal Stars from other characters. ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' has a board named [[Bowser's Warped Orbit]] that involves a similar mechanic, except Stars are stolen by using the [[Bullet Candy]] and [[Bowser Candy]].

===Events===
Landing on a [[Event Space|? Space]] next to the frozen pond activates a [[coin]] game. The rules are different depending on the time of day. During the daytime, [[Brighton]] tosses coins down to the player characters, who grab them while skating, until time runs out. If the characters run into each other, they become stunned temporarily. At night, the characters have to throw [[snowball]]s at each other to knock coins out of them for the taking. During the day, there are two ? Spaces near the frozen pond; at night, there are four.

If a character lands on one of the two [[Event Space|? Space]]s at the top of the board, a [[snowman]] throws a large snowball that begins to roll along the path. As the giant snowball rolls along, it captures any characters in the way and returns them to the [[Start Space]]. Overall, the event is similar to the big [[Mr. Blizzard]] from Chilly Waters, except the giant snowball cannot be avoided.

==Trivia==
*Out of all the boards in ''Mario Party 6'', this board has the highest amount of spaces, with a total of 72 spaces.
*If a player passes by a Chain Chomp Doghouse while at night and does not have at least 10 coins, the game erroneously says that they need 20 coins to ride a Chain Chomp. The message does not take the price change at night into account.
