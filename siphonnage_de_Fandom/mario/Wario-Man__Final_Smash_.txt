}}'''Wario-Man''' is [[Wario]]'s Final Smash in that first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. It reappears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. 3DS/Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.

==Effects==
As Wario-Man, Wario gets massive boosts for each stat, with defense up to the point of being invincible. When a fully charged Wario Waft is used in this state, it will KO opponents at even 0% damage. However, one shouldn't use the Wario Bike as Wario will speed off at a point where he may KO himself. In [[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]], the Wario-Man transformation lasts much shorter than the one from ''Brawl''.

Wario-Man returned in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' with a completely different attacking style. Now, Wario-Man will attack his opponent multiple times before sending them flying off stage. This was done so that the pace of the fighting is fast and straight to the point.
