'''Red Blurps''' are a type of enemy which appears in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Profile==
===Behavior===
Red Blurps behave very similarly to [[Boss Bass]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' in that they try to jump out at the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]. Red Blurps constantly watch from the water as they swim in place and frequently [[jump]] out to attack. If a Yoshi has fallen into the water, a Red Blurp attempts to eat him.

Compared to Boss Bass, Red Blurps jump within a tall and wide arc, higher than what Boss Bass is capable of. To compensate, Red Blurps move slower, including during their descent within the air.
