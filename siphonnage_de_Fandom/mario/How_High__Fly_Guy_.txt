'''How High, Fly Guy?''' is a Mini Mini-Game in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. It is hosted by a [[Shy Guy]] at [[Shy Guy's Jungle Jam]].

There is a [[Mecha Fly Guy|Fly Guy]] and a totem pole with five Shy Guy faces, each with its own coin amount (the bottom one has one, the one above it has five, the middle has 10, the second one from the top has 15, and the top one has 20). The player has five seconds to repeatedly press {{button|GCN|A}} to make the Fly Guy fly high and get as many coins as they can.
