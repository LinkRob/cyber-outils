'''Hat's Off''' is one of [[Dr. Crygor]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''. It was later recreated in level 3 of the [[WarioWare: Twisted! (microgame)|WarioWare: Twisted!]] microgame in both ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'', although the latter game sometimes features [[Trash Day]] instead.

==Gameplay==
The player must tilt the system upside down to flip over the man's head so that his accessories fall of his head.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level adds another accessory that has to be removed.

*'''Level 1:''' The man's construction hat has to fall off.
*'''Level 2:''' Both the man's construction hat and sunglasses have to fall off. 
*'''Level 3:''' The man's hat, his sunglasses, and his wig must fall off.
