The '''Mustard Café chef''' is a character in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Mustard Café chef is a green [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who wears sunglasses.

===Behavior===
The Mustard Café chef is famous and the owner of the Mustard Café, although he is lazy, causing the waiting list for the café to be long.
