'''Pop Quiz''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Gameplay==
In each of the three rounds, the characters have to observe ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' enemies and their animations for several seconds. After that, they have ten seconds to answer the question by pressing the right button. Whoever answers correctly first receives 4 [[point]]s, followed by the second character receiving 3 points, the third one receiving 2 points, and the last one receiving 1 point. However, if a character answers incorrectly, they do not receive any points for the current round. After the three rounds, whoever has the points by the end is the winner.
