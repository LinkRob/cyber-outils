'''Fay''' is a female tennis player featured in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]]. Her partner in Senior doubles is [[Allie]] whereas in Junior doubles it is [[Brian]]. Fay is a Power type playable character who can be unlocked by clearing Singles in Senior Champ.
