The '''Sand Bird''' is a character in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
The Sand Bird is a large avian composed of [[sand block]]s. The Sand Bird has a thin head and neck that is only one column in length, and its wings are wide in comparison

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
At first, the Sand Bird is inside of an egg that is being incubated in a tower called the [[Shine Tower]] at [[Gelato Beach]]. In the second episode, [[Mirror Madness! Tilt, Slam, Bam!]], the egg cannot hatch because a large green [[Wiggler (Super Mario Sunshine)|Wiggler]] is sleeping on top of the tower, and [[Plungelo]]s are standing on the round solar panels, preventing the sunlight rays from pointing at the device the Wiggler is atop and preventing it from having access to sunlight.

By the fourth episode, [[The Sand Bird is Born]], the Sand Bird hatches, as indicated by a cracked eggshell in the Shine Tower. Mario must teleport himself to the Sand Bird by entering a hole in the roof of the tower, created presumably  when the Sand Bird hatched and broke out of the tower, and then ride the Sand Bird around an area resembling the [[Tower of the Wing Cap]] to acquire [[Red Coin|red coins]], seven of them being on the Sand Bird itself. The Sand Bird eventually takes Mario to the eighth and final Red Coin, which is on a tower.

==Trivia==
*There is a different yet similar [[Sand Bird (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|Sand Bird]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', in which Mario has to ride it during the mission [[Sailing the Sandy Seas]] of [[Slipsand Galaxy]]. Unlike the Sand Bird in ''Super Mario Sunshine'', it is used to travel across [[quicksand]].
