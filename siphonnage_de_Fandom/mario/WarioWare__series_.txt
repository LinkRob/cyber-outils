'''''WarioWare''''' (メイド イン ワリオ, ''Meido in Wario''), sometimes known as '''''WarioWare, Inc.''''', is a series of video games starring [[Wario]] and his friends. Unlike the ''[[Wario Land (series)|Wario Land]]'' games, which are platformers like the main [[Mario (series)|''Mario'' series]], ''WarioWare'' is a series of minigame compilations. ''WarioWare'' is unique, however, in that the minigames are very short, most only lasting several seconds. These "[[microgame]]s" make up the basis of the series and are strung together in quick succession with differing control schemes, encouraging frantic action by the player. ''WarioWare'' has also been known for incorporating a gimmick in each game, mostly taking advantage of the latest Nintendo hardware.
