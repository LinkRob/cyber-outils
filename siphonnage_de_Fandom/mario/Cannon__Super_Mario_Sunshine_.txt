'''Cannons''' are a type of object in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Description==
Cannons are a specific type of [[cannon]] that is large, red, and features three turrets. These turrets can be used to either fires a character out for quick transportation or to shoot various projectiles, most often [[Bullet Bill]]s. A few of these cannons are operated by a [[Monty Mole]].

==In ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]''==
Cannons appear as an obstacle in [[Bowser Badlands]], where they shoot out Bullet Bills, which can possibly blocking the player's character's shot from traveling any further.

==In ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''==
Cannons operated by Monty Moles appear in the [[Chain-Chomp Challenge]] minigame. There are four in total, one in each character's lane. A cannon shoots out either [[Bob-omb]]s or tennis balls at a character within their lane.
