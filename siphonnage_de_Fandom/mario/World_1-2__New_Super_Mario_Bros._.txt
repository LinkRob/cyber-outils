'''World 1-2''' is the second level of [[World 1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1]] in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (DS)|New Super Mario Bros]]''.

==Overview==
This is the second level in the game as well as the first level to take place entirely [[Underground theme|underground]] (if counting the secret area in [[World 1-1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]] which is made up of a cave). [[Piranha Plant]]s make their first appearance in the game here. It is also the first level to have a secret exit. World 1-2 is a cave level, similar to [[World 1-2 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-2]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.|Super Mario Bros]]''. It has many [[Brick Block|blocks]] which [[Mario]] can break.

==Walkthrough==
[[File:World1-2NSMB.png|thumb|160px|right|Mario in World 1-2.]]
At the beginning of the level, [[Mario]] will fall down a hole into a cave. Three [[Brick Block]]s which are on top of nine [[coin]]s will then appear. [[Mario]] should then go down a short rocky stairway and up another. After this, he will find a few [[Question Block]]s and [[Brick Block|brick blocks]], covering [[coin]]s. Immediately after this, [[Mario]] can [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] through numerous [[Brick Block|blocks]] to go further underground and find a large number of [[coin]]s. In order to leave this area, [[Mario]] can either [[Wall Jump|wall jump]] through the shaft he came through or [[jump]] up using a spring. The first [[Star Coin]] in the level is to the right of the spring. It can be reached by [[jump]]ing on top of some [[Brick Block|blocks]] and going into the rock alcove. Just past this, the first [[Koopa Troopa]] in the level will appear. Also, by [[jump]]ing on top of some [[Brick Block|brick blocks]] in this area, breaking some on top of the [[Koopa Troopa]], and [[Wall Jump|wall jumping]] off the wall, [[Mario]] can walk on top of the [[Brick Block|brick blocks]] on the top of the level. Although this way is one way to get past the wall of [[Brick Block|brick blocks]] in this area, the main way is simply breaking them. After that, there is a log that will tip back and forth based on where [[Mario]] is walking. Right after the [[Midway Point]], [[Warp Pipe|pipes]] with [[Piranha Plants]] will appear and [[Mario]] will have to avoid them. After the [[Piranha Plant]]s, there are two [[Warp Pipe]]s which [[Mario]] can [[jump]] on to proceed, and several enemies. Then there is another log. [[Mario]] can use this log to get to the secret exit, which will allow him access to a red [[Mushroom House]] and [[World 1-Fortress (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-Tower]]. The final [[Star Coin|star coin]] can only be found this way. If [[Mario]] chooses not to do this, then he will come across several [[Brick Block|brick blocks]], a pit, and the [[Warp Pipe|warp pipe]] which lets him leave the cave. [[Mario]] will then go through the [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] and surface in an area similar to [[World 1-1 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]]. He will then slide down the [[flagpole]] and exit the level. The secret exit is the same, except the stairs to let [[Mario]] get to the [[flagpole]] are floating in the air, and the [[flagpole]]'s flag is red instead of black.

==[[Star Coins]]==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Near the beginning of the level, on a ledge that [[Mario]] needs to use [[Brick Block]]s to get.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Many [[Brick Block|blocks]] surround it. [[Mario]] must break the [[Brick Block|blocks]] to get it. (Does not work with small or [[Mini Mario]])
*'''Star Coin 3''': Next to the secret exit, in plain sight.

==Secret Exit==
'''World 1-2''' features the first secret exit of ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''. This secret exit will lead you to a [[Mushroom House|Power-up Mushroom House]] and [[World 1-Fortress (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-Tower]]. Right before the green pipe that leads to the end of the level, us the seesaw log platform to reach the top of the level. Run right, and this will lead you to the third [[Star Coin]] and a secret [[Warp Pipe]]. Simply go through the secret [[Warp Pipe]], then it'll lead you to the secret exit.
