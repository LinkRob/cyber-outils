'''Jibberjays''' are multi-colored birds which appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They are usually orange, but can also be black, green, yellow, and red. Jibberjays are first encountered in [[Wild Glide Galaxy]] as non-playable characters who divulge information regarding how to control [[Fluzzard]]. After a while, the Jibberjays will send a letter to [[Mario]] to challenge him to a race against their species. Later they are also found in [[Fleet Glide Galaxy]]. After completing the race on Wild Glide Galaxy, a Jibberjay appears on [[Starship Mario]] as well.
