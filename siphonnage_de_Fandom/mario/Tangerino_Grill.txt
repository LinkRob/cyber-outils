'''Tangerino Grill''' is an orange level in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. One orange Mini Paint Star can be found here. This area houses a restaurant, and its business relies on Toads visiting whenever the Sunset Express stops there. The mini-boss Steak is also fought here.
