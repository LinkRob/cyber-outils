'''World 1-2''' is the second level of [[Grass Land]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. The player is given a [[Time Limit]] of 300 seconds to complete it.

== Layout ==
This level is set within a grassy environment. The player begins at the base of a hill, which has a sideways pipe at the top that infinitely spawns [[Goomba]]s. A [[? Block]] lies on the other side, and it holds a power-up. By using the block as a platform, the player must leap over the two pipes that form a "T" shape. The top pipe also spawns Goombas continually.

A group of [[coin]]s lie at the bottom of the next hill, along with a [[Switch Block]] inside a [[Brick Block]] underneath the floating [[Warp Pipe]], with a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] above. This pipe leads to a secret area, where the player can collect twenty coins. They will then be lead outside through a pipe a few inches back.

Pushing ahead, the player will find two [[Jump Block]]s, the second one containing a power-up. Another hill follows, leading down to a pipe, followed by three more Jump Blocks, which will help the player avoid the small pit below. The third and highest Jump Block contains a [[Starman]] power-up. Ahead is another short hill with two more pipes. One last hill awaits, leading the player to the goal.
