|name=Huge Eekhammer (Battle Card)}}'''Huge Eekhammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Huge Eekhammer is the super-strong and fast version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer Attack|Hammer attack]]. It is a huge red hammer with a red/pink spiral background.
