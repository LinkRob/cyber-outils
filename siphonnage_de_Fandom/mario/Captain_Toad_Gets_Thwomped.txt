'''Captain Toad Gets Thwomped''' is a [[Captain Toad Courses|Captain Toad course]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. It can be found in [[World Castle]].

==Features==
The level features conveyor belts, that surround the level. And Thwomps, that not only serve as enemies, but also as platforms to help reach other areas in the level. The upper areas have Bullet Bills, that are shot down the path, meaning the player will have to be careful.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Go behind the first Thwomp from the start of the level into an alcove with a star in it.
*'''Green Star 2''': After passing the two other Thwomps in the first floor of the tower, go into a hole where another Thwomp is hidden. Wait for the Thwomp to lower all the way so you can go on it to rise to the next floor and get the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': Find the second Thwomp from the first floor and wait for it to lower so you can go on it and rise to the third floor. Once there, ride the conveyor belt to the second tile on the left while avoiding the Bullet Bills to get the star.
*'''Green Star 4''': After getting the third star, go on the first Thwomp from before to get the star.
*'''Green Star 5''': After going in a tunnel, wait for the Thwomp to lower to get on it and rise to the final floor. Once there, go on the narrow path while avoiding Bullet Bills to drop down a hole and get the star.
