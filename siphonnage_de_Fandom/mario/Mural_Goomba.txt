A '''Mural Goomba''' is an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

== Appearance ==
Mural Goombas look similar to [[Goomba]]s except they appear a little taller in size. They are dark red in color and appear to not have any eyes.

== Battle ==
Mural Goombas are only fought in one room in [[Drybake Stadium]]. There is also only three of them in the entire game and they always fight in groups. Their only attack is to ram into Mario which is easy to block.
