The '''Kamek Space''' is a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
A Kamek Space is colored green and presents a sideways view of its light-blue hat and glasses. Kamek Spaces exclusively appears in [[Kamek's Carpet Ride]]. If a character lands on a Kamek Space, [[Kamek]] arrives and uses magic to switch each character's positions, with a roulette deciding their fate, i.e. how their positions are affected. The fate can range from taking a character closer to the goal to taking them behind.
