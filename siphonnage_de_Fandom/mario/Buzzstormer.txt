'''Buzzstormer''' is a 2-vs-2 minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
Both teams are riding a large bee-like aircraft through an oversized meadow filled with several large dandelions. The aircraft has two seats, one for each character. At the end, following after all of the dandelions, is a large cliff and an open field.

==Gameplay==
Right before the gameplay starts, the first team is seen flying their aircraft toward a dandelion before turning right to dodge it. The screen then splits in two to show the other team, then the minigame starts.

The object for both teams is to be the first to cross the finish line at the end. If a vehicle hits a dandelion's stem, it is stunned for a few seconds, giving the other team a brief advantage. The first team to cross the finish line is the winner, although if neither side has reached the finish line within five minutes, the minigame ends in a tie.

The ending shows both members of the winning team waving as they continue flying in their aircraft.

==Controls==
*Control Stick: Move
