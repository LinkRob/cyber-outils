The '''Swap Card''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Description==
When a Swap Card is used, the player can switch and steal all items between players that the wheel stops on.

==Trivia==
*This item replaces the [[Treasure Chest]] and [[Barter Box]] (from [[Mario Party 2]] and [[Mario Party 3|3]]). This was succeeded by the [[Weeglee Candy]], [[Snag Bag]], and [[Plunder Chest]] in later installments ([[Mario Party 8]], [[Mario Party DS|DS]], and [[Mario Party Superstars|Superstars]]).
