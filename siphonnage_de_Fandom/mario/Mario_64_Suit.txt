The '''Mario 64 Suit''' (Sometimes referred to as the N64 Suit) is clothing option in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' that gives [[Mario]] a design resembling his appearance in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. The Mario 64 Suit can be unlocked in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].

It has a separate variant called the "Metal Mario Suit" which is just a more expensive version but its all shiny silver. The [[Crazy Cap|suit can be purchased at the shop]] in the Mushroom Kingdom for 20 [[Regional Coin]]s found in the Mushroom Kingdom. [[Mario]] will have no facial expressions or finger movement due to the lack of them which is similar to the 8Bit Mario suit. 

When putting on the [[Nintendo 64|N64]] Suit Mario can access one special area in the Mushroom Kingdom. Mario's eyes still have eyelids so he is able to close his eyes but not actually move them. When wearing this suit at talking to [[Luigi]] he will say "I feel like I recognize that outfit [[Super Mario 64|somewhere]]... Anyway looking good bro" this is because the suit is from Super Mario 64 which is a very old game.
