'''Blockhead Bowser''' is the form [[Bowser]] takes when he and the Mario Party crew face off in the minigame [[Bowser's Block Party]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. It serves as the final boss of the game.

== Description ==
Bowser becomes Blockhead Bowser when he puts on and activates his [[Megamorph Belt]], turning him into a sentient square block cluster consisting of a golden Bowser block that serves as his weak spot and can shoot fire, blocks with Bowser's emblem that can shoot fire, and regular grey blocks that do nothing except form his body. Throughout the battle, Blockhead Bowser stacks the blocks forming his body into the forms of a top, a cube, and a worm-/snake-like form.

== Combat ==
As with the other bosses in the game, Blockhead Bowser is fought in three phases, with his life bar being represented with three hearts, which each take five hits to deplete; thusly, he has a total of 15 hit points. The battle takes place in a circular stone arena surrounded by lava and a thunderbolt scrawled in the centre.

=== Phase 1 ===
During the first phase of his battle, Blockhead Bowser transforms into a top that spins around, flinging fireballs. Eventually, the boss's body will light on fire before he gets dizzy and falls over. If his weak spot is close to the ground, Blockhead Bowser can be attacked just by punching his weak spot. Otherwise, the player has to jump up and kick it to deal damage. After five hits to his weak spot, Blockhead Bowser falls apart.

=== Phase 2 ===
Blockhead Bowser starts his second phase by stacking his blocks into a cuboid formation, and has two attacks, with his first attack being a fireball shot. His second attack doubles as a means of transportation, and involves him rolling à la a [[Tox Box]]. His weak spot can be attacked when it comes close to the ground, with his cuboid body falling apart after five hits.

=== Phase 3 ===
Blockhead Bowser starts his final phase by laying the blocks into a snake-like formation, with the golden Bowser Block as his head and the normal blocks and Bowser emblem-studded blocks forming his "tail." He then crawls around the outside, with the Bowser emblem-studded blocks shooting fireballs that must be avoided. Blockhead Bowser shields his "head" in flames, but can occasionally fire them off to attack, rendering the Golden Bowser Block vulnerable for a few seconds; the player can attack him at that time, but must retreat before Blockhead Bowser shields his head again unless they are absolutely sure they can get the fifth hit in on this form. After he takes the last hit, Blockhead Bowser reverts back to his regular Koopa form and collapses before getting smashed by a large cube.

== Figure ==
Blockhead Bowser is shown in his cube form in his figure, which can be obtained by earning 50 thousand Mario Party points. The description implies that Bowser came up with a special belt to get an edge against Mario: ''"After years of research, Bowser came up with a special belt that would give him the edge against Mario. And now he'll be harder to beat than ever!"''

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Blockhead Bowser is named after the word "blockhead," which refers to someone stupid; his name also alludes to his body being made of blocks in this form.

== Nomenclature ==
Blockhead Bowser is not given a name for the Japanese version, with his figure being referred to as "Post-transformation Bowser" (''Henshin-go Kuppa''; written へんしんごクッパ), implying that the block motif in his names was invented for him outside of Asia. His Spanish name, ''Báuser Bloque'', means "Block Bowser." In Italian, he is called "Cubic Bowser," or ''Báuser Cúbico''. In French, he is called "Concrete Bowser" (''Baüser Béton'').

== Trivia ==
*Blockhead Bowser is the only boss to not be fought when the characters are small.
