The '''Polka Dress''' is the most basic form of armor usable by [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Princess Toadstool has the Polka Dress equipped from the start, when joining [[Mario]] as a party member, and the Polka Dress increases her [[Defense]] by 24 and her [[Magic Defense]] by 12.
