The {{Nihongo|'''Birthday Girl'''|バースデーガール|Bāsudēgāru|Birthday Girl}}, also known as the '''Royal Ribbon''' in the British English releases of ''Mario Kart 7'', is a recurring [[kart]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

== Description ==
The Birthday Girl is a kart body. The kart body is a shoe with feminine features including: spots and a ribbon. The Birthday Girl is simple to accelerate and handle, but difficult to control speed and off-road. However, its weight is easy for heavy karts to push.

In ''Mario Kart Tour'', the Birthday Girl no longer changes its colorization.

=== Color schemes ===
The Birthday Girl can change color depending on the character. Its default color scheme is a pink top and red underside with light pink spots. The Birthday Girl uses the default color scheme when [[Princess Peach|Peach]], a Mii, and male characters use the kart body. However, the Birthday Girl changes color scheme when either three female characters use it:
*[[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]'s Birthday Girl has an orange top and yellow underside with yellow spots.
*[[Rosalina]]'s Birthday Girl has a cyan top and dark blue underside with light blue spots.
*[[Honey Queen]]'s Birthday Girl has a purple top and dark purple underside with light purple spots.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart 7]]''===
[[File:Peach7Flag.png|200px|thumb|right|Artwork of Princess Peach waving a pink-checkered flag next to her Birthday Girl.]]
The Birthday Girl is a standard kart body in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. Like the Peach Parasol, it changes color schemes when Daisy, Rosalina or Honey Queen uses it. The Birthday Girl offers a boost in [[acceleration]] and [[handling]], whilst hindering [[speed]], [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]] and [[Off-Road|off-road]]. In terms of hidden stats, the Birthday Girl offers a boost in sea and air speed, as wells as sea and air handling, [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]] and [[drift]].

The Birthday Girl is a favourite for the CPU [[Princess Daisy]]. On an interesting note, computer-controlled male or indeterminate racers will never use the Birthday Girl, but can ironically choose from the [[Peach Parasol (glider)|Peach Parasol]], which goes with this kart.

Princess Daisy and [[Princess Peach]]'s Birthday Girl karts are used for the [[Daisy Hills]] and [[Cheep Cheep Lagoon]] [[Time Trial]] [[Staff Ghost]]s respectively, save for the expert Staff Ghost for the latter course.

=== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
[[File:MKT Heart Balloons.jpg|thumb|200px|right|[[Toadette]] on the Birthday Girl, using the [[Heart Balloons]] whikst racing on [[Choco Island 2]].]]
The Birthday Girl returns in ''Mario Kart Tour'' as a kart body. Like the Birthday Girl, all kart bodies, gliders and tires from previous installments will return to the game.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://twinfinite.net/2019/05/mario-kart-tour-kart-glider-guide/ |title=Mario Kart Tour: How to Unlock Karts and Gliders |author=Omar Banat |date=2019-05-23 |accessdate=2019-07-13 |work=Twinfinite |quote=''Mario Kart Tour'' brings in familiar karts from all generations of the franchise.}}</ref> The Birthday Girl will ise its default colorisation, no matter which driver uses it. There is also the '''Birthday Girl Rosalina''' which is Rosalina's blue color swap for her Birthday Girl from ''7''. It was added in the [[London Tour]]. There is also a [[Wikipedia:Christmas|Christmas]]-themed version of it called the '''[[Ribbon Rider]]''', which is an unlockable spotlight kart alongside [[Daisy (Holiday Cheer)]] and the [[Glitter Glider]]. Daisy's orange color swap for her Birthday Girl appears in the game, titled '''Birthday Girl Daisy'''. There is also another variant of the Birthday Girl, the '''[[Festival Girl]]''', which is available during the [[Summer Festival Tour]].

== Crossover appearances ==
=== ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' ===
[[File:SSB43 Peach + Birthday Girl.png|thumb|Princess Peach in her Birthday Girl as a trophy in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS''.]]
The Birthday Girl made a cameo appearance on the '''Peach + Birthday Girl''' trophy. Princess Peach, Peach Parasol and [[Mushroom (tire)|Mushroom tires]] appeared along with the Birthday Girl.

== Trivia ==
*All ''Mario Kart 7'' female characters' artwork shows them beside their Birthday Girl, except [[Honey Queen]].
*The [[Peach Parasol (glider)|Peach Parasol]] shares the same trait as the Birthday Girl, by changing color schemes.
*The [[Heart Coach]] and [[Bloom Coach]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', through [[Royale]] and [[Power Flower (kart)|Power Flower]] from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' and the [[Daytripper]] from ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' can be considered the Birthday Girl's spiritual predecessors, possibly due to its "feminine" designs. The [[Cat Cruiser]] from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' can be considered to be the Birthday Girl's successor.
