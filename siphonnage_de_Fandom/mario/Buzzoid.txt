The '''Buzzoid''' is an enemy in [[Donkey Kong Bananza|''Donkey Kong Bananza'']]. They are introduced in the Lagoon Layer level.

== Description ==
Buzzoids appear as golden wasp-like creatures. Their abdomens are large and sport both spikes and a propeller, allowing the Buzzoid to fly.

== Behaviour ==
Buzzoids attack by tearing up terrain chunks with their jaws and throwing those chunks as projectiles. The Buzzoids' spiked abdomens protect them from direct melee attacks. However, Donkey Kong can defeat Buzzoids by throwing a chunk of terrain at them as a projectile. Buzzoids can also be defeated by detonating certain explosive rocks in their vicinity.

Buzzoids appear in the Lagoon Layer's fourth Battle Challenge, and the second Battle Challenge of the Forest Layer, Resort Layer, and Forbidden Layer apiece.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Buzzoids are named after the word "buzz," reflecting how they are based on wasp. The "-oid" suffix in their name comes from ''-oid'', which is a suffix that meant "like" but is often used for robots, and is shared by many enemies created by the [[VoidCo|Void Company]].

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Buzzoids are called ''bazo'' (written バゾ), from "buzz." In Spanish, they are called ''Lanzabejoide'', from ''lanzar'' ("to throw"), ''abeja'' ("bee"), and the suffix ''-oide'' ("-oid.") In Portuguese, their name is ''Marimboide'', from ''marimbondo'' ("wasp"). Italian calls them ''Ronzoide'', comes from ''rónzio'' ("buzz").
