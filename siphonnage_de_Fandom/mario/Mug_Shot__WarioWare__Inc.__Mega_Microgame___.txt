'''Mug Shot''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', under [[Wario]]'s [[Intro Games]] folder, and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Wario Deluxe]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The objective is for [[Wario]] to catch his mug while it is sliding across the table. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame. If it slides past Wario, the player loses at the microgame.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', a mushroom sometimes appears instead of a mug; in this case, Wario has to instead catch the mushroom.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The mug always comes from the left.
*'''Level 2:''' The mug sometimes appears from the right.
*'''Level 3:''' Two mugs sometimes come in opposite directions, one of which stops before another mug suddenly appears from the opposite direction, which is the one Wario has to catch.

==Trivia==
*The microgame's name is derived from the word "mugshot."
