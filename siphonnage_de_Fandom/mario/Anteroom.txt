The '''Anteroom''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Anteroom resembles a short hallway, nearly empty aside from two chandeliers and three cabinets, each with a blue vase on top. It is accessed from the door on the northern wall of the [[Parlor (Luigi's Mansion)|Parlor]], and the Anteroom's left wall has a door leading into the [[Wardrobe Room]]. 

==History==
[[File:Anteroom GCN.png|thumb|left|250px|Luigi traveling through the Anteroom, which has its lights turned on]]
The Anteroom is accessed in [[Area One]], and is the third overall room visted by [[Luigi]]. Luigi can access the Anteroom from a door on the northern wall of the [[Parlor (Luigi's Mansion)|Parlor]]. The first time Luigi enters the Anteroom, the doors are blocked by spiky barriers, and Luigi has to vacuum up four [[Gold Ghost]]s and two [[Purple Puncher]]s. The Anteroom is the first room with Purple Punchers. After Luigi captures every [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|Ghost]] into his [[Poltergust 3000]], the lights turn on and the spiky barriers vanish, enabling Luigi to either exit back into the Parlor or continue on to the Wardrobe Room.

After the [[Boo]]s are freed from the [[Storage Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Storage Room]], Luigi can find a Boo named Bootha in the Anteroom, hiding in one of the three cabinets.

During the [[blackout]], Luigi is required make his way through the Anteroom, after the Parlor, to continue into the Wardrobe Room, where [[Uncle Grimmly]] is fought. Only one Purple Puncher and four Gold Ghosts appear in the Anteroom during the blackout, and Luigi is not required to vacuum them up.

In European and Australian versions of the original [[Nintendo GameCube]] version, the [[Hidden Mansion]] has three Purple Punchers, one [[Blue Twirler]], and several [[Blue Mouse|Blue Mice]]. A [[Speedy Spirit]] can also be found in the northwest cabinet.
