==Effects==

Eating a Gorgeous Steak restores 25 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]] making it an effective recovery item. In order to make a Gorgeous Steak, one must bring a [[Power Steak]] and a [[Hamburger]] to [[Dyllis]]. A Gorgeous Steak can also be used with a [[Shroom Steak]] to make a [[Dyllis Deluxe]].
