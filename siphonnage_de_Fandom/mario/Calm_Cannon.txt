'''Calm Cannon''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Calm Cannon is a variant of [[Kaboomba|Cannon]] imbued with the [[Calm]] [[vibe]].

==Behavior==
Like with most other enemies imbued with the Calm vibe, a Calm Cannon is found sleeping at first, but if [[Princess Peach|Peach]] quickly approaches a Calm Cannon, it wakes up and begins to attack. A Calm Cannon attacks in the same manner as regular Cannon, by shooting [[cannonball]]s.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Calm Cannons first appear in the level [[Shriek Mansion 3-8]].
