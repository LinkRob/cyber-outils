The '''Ancient Poltergeist''' is a sub-boss that is fought in the [[Old Clockworks]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
The Ancient Poltergeist looks similar to regular [[Poltergeist]]s, except the Ancient Poltergeist is cobalt blue with a cyan brain.

=== Behavior ===
The Ancient Poltergeist behaves in the same manners as regular Poltergeists, using telekinesis to throw or control nearby objects. The Ancient Poltergeist appears to be one of the most powerful Poltergeist in the game, due to being very "ancient", yet he's still weaker than the [[Strong Poltergeist]] and [[The Brain]]. He also has the ability to control sand.

== In ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' ==
[[File:Ancient Poltergeist introduction.gif|thumb|200x200px|right|The Ancient Poltergeist's introduction, scaring Luigi and running off with the minute hand.]]
The Ancient Poltergeist first appears in the mission, [[Underground Expedition]]. When [[Luigi]] opens the tomb, he finds the missing minute hand, only to have the Ancient Poltergeist scare him and run off with it, soon leaving Luigi alone to deal with three mummified [[Greenie]]s.

In [[Roundhouse Brawl]], Luigi can see the Poltergeist toying around with the minute hand in the [[Roundhouse]], so Luigi needs to find another way across to retrieve the minute hand from the Ancient Poltergeist. After doing so, the Poltergeist taunts Luigi before the battle begins.

=== Battle ===
Battling the Ancient Poltergeist is similar to battling the other Poltergeists with the distinction of this one being able to control sand. He will create a tornado of sand in the middle of the room, use telekinesis to bring bombs to it, and launch the bombs at Luigi from the tornado. After a certain amount of time, the Ancient Poltergeist will stop using his sand control, revealing himself. Luigi should use the [[strobulb]] to reveal and stun him and use the [[Poltergust 5000]] on him until Luigi captures him. During the battle, he will toss bricks at [[Luigi]] and summon [[Sneaker]]s to scare him, allowing himself to be freed from being captured. Once defeated, he drops the minute hand.
