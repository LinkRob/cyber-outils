The '''Spear Guy Orb''' is a special [[orb]] appearing only in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. It can be found on [[Pagoda Peak]], [[Pyramid Park]], [[Windmillville]], and [[Bowser's Enchanted Inferno!]]. The Spear Guy Orb can be placed on a chosen space on the board. If a player who is not the owner of the space steps on it, a [[Spear Guy]] appears and steals an amount of [[coin]]s determined by a [[Dice Block]] from the one who has stepped on the space. The Dice Block can roll a 5, 10, 20, or 30. The Spear Guy then gives the coins to the space's owner. This orb is the replacement for the [[Goomba Orb]] from ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Names in other languages==
*'''German:''' Speer-Guy-Kapsel, meaning "Spear Guy Capsule"
*'''Japanese:''' ヤリホーカプセル (''Yarihō kapuseru''), meaning "Spear Guy Capsule"
