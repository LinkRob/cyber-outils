'''Pearls''' are valuable treasures appearing in the [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]]. They first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' then ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]'', although in the form of recurring projectiles spit out by [[Clambo]]s in a straightforward line. Pearls later return in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]'', where [[Oyster]]s in [[Gloomy Galleon]] have stolen pearls from the [[Mermaid]], and [[Tiny Kong]] must defeat the Oysters to return the pearls and receive a [[Golden Banana]].
