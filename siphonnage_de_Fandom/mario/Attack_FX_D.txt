'''Attack FX D''' is a [[badge]] that appears only in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Attack FX D, like all other Attack FX badges, costs 0 BP and gives [[Mario]] a special sound upon the impact of his attacks.

To obtain Attack FX D, Mario must first find the [[Master Poet|poet]] responsible for writing music for the [[Club 64]] in southwestern [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. In [[Dry Dry Outpost]], a [[Toad in the Desert|lone musician]] capable of writing brilliant sheet music lives in a small home. Mario asks around for the songwriter who will give Mario the lyrics to give [[Chanterelle]] at Club 64 her singing talent back, and for his efforts, Mario is rewarded the Attack FX D badge. With her a singing career back, Chanterelle will sing the theme song for ''Paper Mario'' if Mario talks to her again.
