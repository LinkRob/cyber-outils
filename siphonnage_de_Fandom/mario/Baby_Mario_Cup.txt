'''Baby Mario Cup''' is a cup with [[Baby Mario]] representing as an icon or name. It appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Baby Mario Cup has an icon representing for itself. Like all cups, the cup contains four courses per tour depending on its appearance on each tour.
