{{Nihongo|'''Daisy Blossom'''|デイジーブラッサム|Deijī Burassamu|Daisy Blossom}}, is [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]'s [[Final Smash]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]''. This Final Smash is complementary to [[Peach Blossom]].

==Description==
This Final Smash can be performed after Daisy is the last fighter to hit the [[Smash Ball]]. When performed, Daisy dances while the models of Daisy appear moving their arms, the orange border is also filled with daisies. The background changes to orange and falling daisies are seen in the background.

Similar to Peach Blossom, it puts opponents to sleep, and Daisy can revive her health with edible daisies (Peach revives her health with peaches). The closer Daisy is to the opponents, the longer they sleep. It also gives Daisy time to launch foes. Players above the bottomless pit or grabbing the ledge onscreen will fall down resulting an instant KO. The player has to be aware sleeping victims can mash the buttons to free themselves from sleeping. However, the higher the damage the foe has, the harder it is for them to wake up.

=== Update history ===
On Patch 2.0.0, the camera for the Final Smash has been adjusted making the borders scroll correctly on scrolling stages.

On Patch 3.0.0, opponents caught upon the Final Smash will have a shorter duration of sleep.
