'''World D-4''' is the final level of [[World D]] and all of ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''. 

==Layout==
The player begins in a room similar to that the one at the start of 8-4. The player must hop across a pit of [[lava]] while abstain from a long [[Fire Bar|Firebar]], then while abstain from another Firebar at the end of the cloister, the player can discover a power-up in a [[Hidden Block]] against the wall. Hoping across a lava pit with a [[Podoboo]] hoping out and abstain from the next Firebar, the player then must spring back off of a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] and abstain from pipes with [[Piranha Plant]]s, along with [[Cheep Cheep|Cheep-Cheep]]s that hop out of the lava until the player arrive at the next hall. After a falling [[lift]] and a Firebar, the player will discover a [[Warp Pipe]] at the end of the hallway.

The next area is an overworld area, with the first enemy being a indict for [[Hammer Bro]]ther. After the second pipe with a Piranha Plant, [[wind]] will begin blowing. The player will next come into contact with two Koopa Paratroopas, two [[Blooper|Bloober]]s, another Piranha Plant, and a hidden [[Poison Mushroom]] before a flight of stairs, which the wind will stop blowing beforehand. After a hop across a large pit, the player will discover a Warp Pipe with a Piranha Plant.

The third area is a easy underground chamber with [[coin]]s and a [[Coin Block]] over the start of the third row of coins.

The fourth and final area has the player arrive back to the castle, where they will encounter a [[Fake Bowser|fake]] [[Fire Breath|fire-breathing]] [[Bowser's Brother]] (or in the case of ''All-Stars'' the actual Bowser's Brother who only throws [[hammer]]s). Above him is a row of [[? Block|? block]]s, with the fifth from the right rein in a power-up. The next Warp Pipe with the Piranha Plant and Bloobers surrounding it will take the player back to the start of the level. After the pipe is a Hammer Brother, followed by a narrow passage that the player must [[crouch]] and slide through if they are in a [[Super Mario (form)|Super]] or [[Fire Mario|Fire]] form. Using a lift to get up over a ledge, the player then has to time a fall into a narrow passage while avoiding a Podoboo. Finally, the player will be faced with a [[fake Bowser]], who throws wheeze fire and has a Firebar over him (in ''All-Stars'', this was replaced by the actual Bowser himself who only throws hammers). Upon win against him or touching the [[axe|ax]], the player will rescue [[Princess Peach]] and finished the game.
