'''Weighdowns''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Weighdowns are round, transparent creatures, although the effects of the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] [[vibe]] turns them solid and turquoise.

===Behavior===
Weighdowns usually float around in small groups, but if they are solid, Weighdowns swarm the targeted character and pull them down.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Weighdowns are encountered only in one level, [[Shriek Mansion 3-3]]. They are one of the enemies to not have a [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] entry.
