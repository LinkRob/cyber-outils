'''World 2-2''' is the second level of [[World 2 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 2]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the sixth level overall in said game. This level was a part of ''[[VS. Super Mario Bros.]]'' as World 3-2 and was imported into ''Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels''. The only difference is that the arcade version is snowy while the ''Lost Levels'' version is not.

==Layout==
The level starts with a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] coming after the player character. After a [[Warp Pipe|pipe]] with a [[Piranha Plant]] is a row of [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, and more Piranha Plants. On the other end of the first gap is a staircase facing to the right that can make jumping onto it difficult. After another row of Troopas is a larger staircase, again facing away from the direction the player is coming from. Across a large gap with a Piranha Plant in a pipe in the center and past a group of enemies and pillars is a [[Brick Block]] containing a [[Super Star]], which is followed by a row of Koopa Troopas. Through the pipe afterward is an underground area with [[Coin]]s and [[Hidden Block]]s containing either a power-up or a [[Poison Mushroom]], and the pipe exits through the pipe immediately after the one the player entered from; if the player does not take the pipe, they can find a [[Coin Block]] and a power-up in the Brick Blocks after it. Above the platform with the pipe and the Koopa Troopa are two Hidden Blocks that lead to a floating pipe, which can be used as a platform to help the player over the next large gap. After a row of enemies, pillars, and platforms are stairs with Goombas walking down them leading to the [[Goal Pole]].
