'''Peasley's Rose''' is an key item which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. It was owned by the famous [[Prince Peasley]].

==Physical description==
The Peasley Rose is mainly a rose that is yellow in color.

==Usage==
After [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] return Prince Peasley from his transformation as [[Dragohoho]] to normal, Prince Peasley gratefully gives over his rose to allow Mario and Luigi to enter [[Beanbean Castle]], and then flies away.
