'''Directional Blocks''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

==Description==
A Directional Block is a purple [[block]] with a white arrow on each side. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can hit a Directional Block from below to have it move a certain distance in the direction it is pointed, which usually allows Mario or Luigi to reach otherwise inaccessible blocks or collectibles, such as the first [[Star Medal]] of [[World 3-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 3-4]]. Each Directional Block's position in a [[level]] cannot be reset unless the level is re-entered.
