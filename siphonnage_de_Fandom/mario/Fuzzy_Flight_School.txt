''' Fuzzy Flight School''' is a Free-for-All minigame in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
Players fly on their vehicles and the Joy-Con appears on the screen behind the players' vehicle.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to be the last player to not get hit by three [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]]. The last player(s) standing is the winner. The player must tilt the Joy-Con to dodge the Fuzzies.

== Ending ==
The players are standing at the end of the tunnel. The winner(s) vehicle is fine, the winner(s) do(es) their victory pose. The losers' vehicles have been damaged, the players do their losing poses.

==Controls==
