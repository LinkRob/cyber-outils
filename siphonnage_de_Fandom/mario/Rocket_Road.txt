'''Rocket Road''' is a board in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''. It is based on the [[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|''Super Mario Galaxy'' series]], and the objective is to be the first to reach the end. It is much shorter than [[Perilous Palace Path]], which features a similar objective.
