'''Castle Crawl''' is a Special [[Challenge Mode|Challenge]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. Its setting is based on the [[Superstar Road]] level, [[Pendulum Castle]].

==Objective==
The player must complete the challenge by walking on top of multiple [[Donut Block]]s while avoiding [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s and [[Bony Beetle]]s. To add difficulty to the challenge, the player must make it through the entire challenge without being able to dash.

==Difficulty==
