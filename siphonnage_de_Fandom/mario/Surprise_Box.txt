'''Surprise Boxes''' are a type of [[Treasure chest|Treasure Box]] featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. A Surprise Box has the same appearance as a Treasure Box except it is hidden. To reveal a Surprise Box, [[Mario]] can [[jump]] under the location where it is at, and in this sense, Surprise Boxes act like [[Hidden Block]]s.

If Mario wears a [[Signal Ring]], a noise is made whenever he is in an area with a Surprise Box.
