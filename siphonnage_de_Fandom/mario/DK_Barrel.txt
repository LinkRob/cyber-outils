'''DK Barrels''' are a recurring object in the {{series|Donkey Kong Country}} and {{series|Donkey Kong Land}}. They first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''. DK Barrels have made various minor appearances in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], the first time being in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==In ''Mario'' franchise==
==={{series|Mario Party}}===
====''[[Mario Party 6]]''====
Two DK Barrels make a background appearance in [[Tally Me Banana]], on a bridge behind where the mini-game takes place.

====''[[Mario Party 7]]''====
[[File:DK's Tree House MP7.png|thumb|DK Barrels in the background of DK's Tree House in ''Mario Party 7'']]
Three DK Barrels make a cameo appearance in front of [[DK's Tree House]] in the background of the [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] minigames [[Jump, Man]], [[Peel Out]], and [[Vine Country]].

====''[[Mario Party 8]]''====
Donkey Kong's intro animation involves him bursting out of a DK Barrel, something that usually occurs when a character lands on a [[DK Space]], but in [[DK's Treetop Temple]], this occurs when reaching a cannon.

==={{series|Mario Kart}}===
====''[[Mario Kart 7]]''====
In [[DK Jungle (Mario Kart 7)|DK Jungle]], DK Barrels appear on the sides of the track in the jungle portion. A DK Barrel can break open by either driving into it or throwing a [[Green Shell]] at it. In doing so, either a [[Banana Peel]] or a [[Starman|Super Star]] launches out of the DK Barrel and onto the race course.

====''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''====
DK Barrels return as an obstacle of {{classic course|3DS|DK Jungle}}, acting the same as before.

===''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]''===
A DK Barrel is featured during Donkey Kong's [[Double Bogey]] animation.

===''[[Mario Sports Superstars]]''===
In the Golf mode, like ''Mario Golf: World Tour'', a DK Barrel appears in Donkey Kong's Double Bogey animation.

===''[[Dr. Mario World]]''===
A DK Barrel was featured as Donkey Kong's Skill Effect.

==Crossover appearances==
===''Kirby Super Star'' / ''Kirby Super Star Ultra''===
In the [[w:c:nintendo:The Great Cave Offensive|The Great Cave Offensive]] game, one of the items that [[Kirby]] can obtain is a '''Kong's Barrel''', worth 1,500 Gold. It is guarded by two [[Gordo]]s in the [[w:c:kirby:Garden Area|Garden Area]].

==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
DK Barrels make minor appearances throughout the series. Donkey Kong's stage entrance involves him bursting out a DK Barrel in every installment except ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', in which stage entrances do not appear. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', there is a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]] of a DK Barrel depicting its ''Donkey Kong Country'' artwork. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'', a DK Barrel appears as a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]].

===''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''===
DK Barrels make a cameo appearance in the level [[Do U Stack Up?]], where they can be seen floating in the ocean.

==In other media==
===''[[Nintendo Monopoly]]'' (2006)===
A DK Barrel is one of six usable tokens.
