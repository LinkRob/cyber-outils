'''Royal Seeds''' are an item that can defeat the [[Cloud Piranha|Cloud Piranhas]]. Each of the Royal Seeds is simply different color that are corresponded to each of the world, Blue: [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]], Turquoise: [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]], Red: [[Shining Falls]], Yellow: [[Sunbaked Desert]], Green: [[Fungi Mines]], Purple: [[Deep Magma Bog]], and Orange: [[Petal Isles]].

== Appearance ==
It's pretty much a larger [[Wonder Seed]] and a crown shell on the bottom of it.
