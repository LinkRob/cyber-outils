{{Nihongo|'''Poncho'''|ポンチョ}} is a '''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''' [[Clothing|clothing item]] obtained by paying 10 [[Regional Coin|Purple Coins]] in the [[Tostarena]] [[Crazy Cap]] located in the [[Tostarena|Tostarena Town]] in the [[Sand Kingdom]]. It also goes with the [[Sombrero]] [[Clothing|clothing item]], which can be obtained by paying 5 [[Regional Coin|Purple Coins]] at the [[Tostarena]] [[Crazy Cap]]. Needed with the [[Sombrero]] to enter the [[Tostarena]] locked room with contains the [[Power Moon]]: [[Dancing With New Friends]]- [[Sand Kingdom]] [[Power Moon]] #53.

==Appearances==
* '''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''' ([[Tostarena]]-[[Sand Kingdom]])

==Trivia==

* The '''Poncho''' is only obtainable in [[Tostarena]]- [[Sand Kingdom]]. 
* The '''Poncho''' has some controversy which leads to being accused of 'racism & cultural appropriation', and [[Nintendo]] also removed Mexican [[Mario]] from the early box art in 2017.
