'''Belcha's Barn''' is the first boss level of ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''. It is located at the end of [[Lake Orangatanga]], in a large barn southwest of the lake. [[Belcha]] is the boss who is fought in Belcha's Barn.

== Layout ==
The player starts on an upper level of the barn. Near the start is a trapdoor, which the player must fall through to get to the boss room. There the player encounters Belcha, who will start spitting barrels at [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] and [[Kiddy Kong|Kiddy]] upon sight.                      

In order to defeat Belcha, the player must throw the enemies inside the barrels into it's mouth to make it belch and move backwards as a result of the recoil. If done quick enough, Belcha will belch and fall down a hole at the end of the linear corridor it is fought in.
