'''''Super Mario All-Stars Limited Edition''''', titled '''''Super Mario Collection Special Pack''''' in Japan and '''''Super Mario All-Stars - 25th Anniversary Edition''''' in Europe, is an emulation of the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] game ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' for the [[Wii]] with no changes from the original game. It was released on October 21<ref>[http://www.nintendo.co.jp/schedule/1010.html Nintendo.co.jp: Release Calendar for October, 2010 (Japanese)]</ref> in Japan, December 2nd in Australia, December 3 in Europe, and December 12th in North America, all during the year 2010.

It was bundled with a book named ''Super Mario History 1985-2010'' and a soundtrack with many different ''Mario'' songs for a limited time. The game is a tribute to 25 years of ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''
