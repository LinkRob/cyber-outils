'''Gravity Arrows''' are a type of object featured in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They affect the flow of [[Gravity]] within their vicinity, and [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can use the [[Star Spin]] on a Gravity Arrow to alter the flow of gravity and change the color of the Gravity Arrows. A similar type of object appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]''.

== Appearances ==
===''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''===
Gravity Arrows appear in the following sections:

*[[Battlerock Galaxy]]: Can be flipped to change the [[Gravity Field (Super Mario Galaxy)|Gravity Fields]]'s direction within the [[Battlerock]]'s gravity corridor. Note that here, the Gravity Fields have their direction reversed in relation to their color: blue for up and red for down.
*[[Gusty Garden Galaxy]]: Within the Gravity Scramble Planet's area, Gravity Arrows can be used to change the gravity near the next area. This is necessary to complete the third mission, [[Gusty Garden's Gravity Scramble]].
*[[Dreadnought Galaxy]]: Within the [[Dreadnought]]'s gravity corridor (visited during the first mission, [[Infiltrating the Dreadnought]], these can be found where Mario cannot pass with the current gravity.
*[[Deep Dark Galaxy]]: Within the [[Boo Box]] is a single gravity arrow that changes the box's gravity in the [[Boo in a Box|secret mission]]. It starts pointing down, but rotates 90 degrees counterclockwise in relation to the camera when flipped.

===''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''===
Gravity Arrows appear within the following sections:

*[[Clockwork Ruins Galaxy]]: During the first mission, which is titled [[Time for Adventure]], a single Gravity Arrow appears on the Pushing Wall Planet. When it is flipped, parts of the planet fall apart, leaving behind gaps. This is necessary to reach the [[Ground Pound Switch]] that opens the gate and frees the [[Launch Star]].
