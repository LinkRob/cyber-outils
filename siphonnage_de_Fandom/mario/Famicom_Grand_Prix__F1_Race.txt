'''''Famicom Grand Prix: F1 Race''''' is a racing game released exclusively for the [[Family Computer Disk System]] in Japan on October 30, 1987. It features [[Mario]] racing in Formula One cars. The game would later receive a sequel, ''[[Famicom Grand Prix II: 3D Hot Rally]]'', also released for the Family Computer Disk System. The game was the subject of a tournament, in which one of the prizes was a ''[[Super Mario Bros. (Game & Watch)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' [[Game & Watch]] handheld.
