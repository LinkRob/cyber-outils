'''Stroke of Genius''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', as part of [[Dr. Crygor]]'s [[Slightly Unscrewed]] stage, and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Young Cricket]] & [[Master Mantis]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The player has to get their swimmer to swim to the very end of the pool and win in first place. The swimmer occasionally swims with different strokes. Stroke of Genius is one of the double-length microgames, which have twice the length of other microgames.

===Difficulty levels===
In ''WarioWare: Touched!'', the difficulty settings include the following differences:

*'''Level 1:''' The swimmer doesn't need to bypass another swimmer.
*'''Level 2:''' One swimmer is in the lead.
*'''Level 3:''' The swimmer must bypass several swimmers who swim fast.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', there are no opponents, and the swimmer's starting position differs between difficulties. The player sometimes has to make a hamster in a ball to reach the end of the pool. The changes between difficulty levels are also different from before:

*'''Level 1:''' The swimmer starts close to the goal.
*'''Level 2:''' The swimmer starts at the far end from the goal.
*'''Level 3:''' Retains the base setup from Level 1, except there is a shark chasing the swimmer, and it gradually speeds up as the swimmer gets closer to the goal. If the shark catches up to the swimmer, the player loses.
