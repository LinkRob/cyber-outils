'''Rhythm Maker''' is one of [[Orbulon]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object is to follow the rhythm of the beat. The microgame starts with a finger demonstrating the order of the buttons. After this, the player has to copy the movement to the beat of the music. If successful, the player wins at the microgame, but if they either go too fast or take too long to press a button, the player loses at the microgame.
