'''Home on the 'Rang''' is a [[Co-op Minigame|Co-op]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. It is a pun of a wild western song, "Home on the Range".

== Opening ==
A [[Pokey]] hops out of the sand and four player hit it with a boomerang.

== Gameplay ==
Players have thirty seconds to work together by flying Boomerangs at Pokeys. Players earn a point for each segment of a Pokey they hit. Hitting the head is worth three points.

== Ending ==
Players does their winning or losing animation depending on their rank.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|joy-con-l}} / {{button|ns|joy-con-r}} – Throw Boomerang
