|games = ''[[Paper Mario]]''}}'''Yoshi's Village''' is a location that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is a small settlement found on [[Lavalava Island]] and is home to the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]].

==Layout==
Yoshi's Village, as stated above, is a small village with two areas. The first area is where the [[Village Leader]] resides along with a few other areas. The second area is much larger and is where the [[Item Shop]] ([[Yoshi's Cabana]]) and [[Toad House]] can be found in.

==History==
Yoshi's Village is accessed once [[Mario]] makes it through a small section of Lavalava Island. Yoshi's Village, naturally, is populated by the Yoshi's and a few [[Raven]]s in a tree in the second area. [[Kolorado]] also visits this island on his quest to make it to [[Mt. Lavalava]] for treasure. However, when Mario briefly leaves Yoshi's Village to follow Kolorado due east towards Mt. Lavalava, the both find the path inaccessible forcing them to head back to Yoshi's Village to plan out their next move. However, by this time, Yoshi's Village is in a state of panic due to the Yoshi Kids, a group named the [[Fearsome 5]], have gone missing in the dangerous [[Jade Jungle]]. Mario and his friends go on to find the Yoshi Kids while Kolorado stays behind in Yoshi's Village. Eventually, Mario (with the help of [[Sushie]]) manages to find the Yoshi Kids bringing peace to Yoshi's Village and the Village Leader gives Mario the [[Jade Raven]] in order to seek the help of [[Raphael the Raven]] in order to get to Mt. Lavalava.

==Trivia==
*Yoshi's Village is one of few areas in the game that undergoes a temporary music change due to a certain event happening in the town (in this case, it's the Yoshi Kids gone missing). The others are [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] when it's invaded by [[Shy Guy]]s during Chapter 4 and the other is [[Koopa Village]] when the [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] are running amok in the village.
