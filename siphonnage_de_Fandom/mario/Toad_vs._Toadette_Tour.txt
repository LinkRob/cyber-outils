'''Toad vs. Toadette Tour''' is a Team up event released in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' on November 2, 2021 and was ended on November 16, 2021. This is the fifth team rally featuring [[Toad (character)|Toad]] against [[Toadette]]. In this Tour, the player can chose between Team [[Toad (character)|Toad]] or Team [[Toadette]].  

This Tour featured R/T variant courses like previous tour events from [[Trick Tour (2020)]] , [[Trick Tour (2021)]] and the [[Mario vs. Luigi Tour (2020)]]. Even tough there where no new tracks introduced, the R/T variant makes his first debut in [[Ghost Valley 1|RMX Ghost Valley 1]] and [[Kalimari Desert 2|N64 Kalamari Desert 2]]. 

The tour event also featured a new Pit Crew Toad color variant called Yellow Toad (Pit Crew).

Team Toad finished 52% of the tokens and thus won the team event.
