A '''Mini Star Space''' is a [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Description==
Whenever a Captain lands on a Mini Star Space, they receive a few [[Mini Star]]s, specifically the number printed on the space, which can be either 1, 2, 3, or 5. In addition, the word "Lucky!" is displayed as if the Captain had landed on a [[Lucky Space]]. The exact opposite of a Mini Star Space is a [[Mini Ztar Space]], which instead gives the number of [[Mini Ztar]]s printed on the space. 

These spaces (along with [[Mini Ztar Space]]s) do not appear on [[DK's Jungle Ruins]]. That is because [[Mini Star]]s and [[Mini Ztar]]s are instead replaced with [[Banana]]s and [[Z-Banana]]s. However, they have the same function as Mini Stars and Mini Ztars.
