'''Big Scorpers''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Big Scorpers are large, red scorpions possessing a long tail, small legs, and blue eyes. Unlike regular scorpions, Big Scorpers do not have claws.

===Behavior===
Whenever [[Wario]] approaches a Big Scorper, it appears from the sand, where only their eyes and tail are visible. A Big Scorper attacks by trying to slam Wario using its tail.

==In ''Wario World''==
Big Scorpers are only encountered in [[Pecan Sands]]. Wario cannot punch a Big Scorper, although he can stun one by [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]] its head three times. In doing so, Wario can then pick up the Big Scorper and defeat it from one of his attacks.
