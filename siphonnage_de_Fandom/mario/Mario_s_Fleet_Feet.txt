'''Mario's Fleet Feet''' is the third level of [[World 1 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 1]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. A [[Cyan Yoshi]] takes control in this level. The level takes place in large tunnels and houses located in a forest of colorful pine trees. Many gimmicks are utilized in this level including the usage of [[Baby Mario]]'s dash ability, using [[Yoshi Egg]]s to clear the path forward, as well as using a [[Chomp Rock]] to clear out a path of enemies. [[Stork Stop]]s will appear in this level after [[Catch the Breeze!]] is completed. 

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, advance to the right and climb up the ledges. As you start walking along the rocky flooring, the monstrous [[Super Big Tap-Tap]] will emerge and attempt to run you down destroying the rocky flooring as it goes. Don't stop for even a second, keep running from the colossal monster collecting [[Coin]]s, [[Red Coin]]s, and even a [[Smiley Flower]]. After this, jump through the [[Flipper]]s, you're safe now as the [[Super Big Tap-Tap]] cannot fit through and will descend into the pit below. Tag the [[Middle Ring]] and enter the "house", use your [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s to bust open the blocks of sand blocking the way forward (or use the [[Shy Guy]]s which emerge from the pipe if you're low on or out of [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s. Upon destroying the sand blocks above, a [[Spring Ball]] should drop down for you to use, bounce upward and advance to the left hitting the [[M Block]]s for [[Coin]]s and [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], advance to the right and defeat the [[Piranha Plant]], [[Shy Guy]]s, and [[Crazee Dayzee]]s. Hop up on the platforms and launch an egg at the diagonal [[Coin]] lines, they should ricochet off the wall and bounce into the diagonal [[Coin]] line above (which includes two [[Red Coin]]s) as well as the [[Smiley Flower]] from here, advance to the right and launch an egg at the large sand chunks diagonally to create a path to the platforms above, which contains a [[Red Coin]]. Drop back down to the main level and get some eggs from the [[Egg Block]] and use the [[Chomp Rock]] to shred through the enemies in your way. After dealing with the pests, be sure to grab the [[Coin]]s at the path's end as two of them are [[Red Coin]]s and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] to get some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]]. After this, move on to the next area.

In the next area, you should see some outlined blocks, hit the nearby [[! Switch]] to make these blocks briefly solid and run across to the next area, take care of the [[Piranha Plant]]s and [[Shy Guy]]s there and hit the [[! Switch]] to make more blocks temporarily solid and run across them whilst collecting the [[Coin]]s, reach the end of the path to grab the [[Smiley Flower]], from here, return back to where the [[! Switch]] is and hit it again. Run across the blocks and jump atop the platforms, hit another [[! Switch]] atop the platform "staircase" and keep running as you collect [[Coin]]s whilst avoiding foes such as [[Shy Guy]]s and [[Pointey]]s, hit the [[Winged Cloud]] to get a 1-Up and continue through the [[Flipper]]. Hit another [[! Switch]] and run across the blocks and take the upper path to snag three [[Red Coin]]s. Return back and hit the [[! Switch]] again, this time, run across the blocks to the path's end to reach the final section.

Entering the next section, ground pound the nearby [[Box]] for some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]] and a [[Stake]] for a [[Red Coin]], from here, run up the hill and pound another [[Stake]] for another [[Red Coin]], then advance up the hills to your left and grab the [[Coin]]s and the [[Red Coin]] atop the [[Stake]] in the corner, pound the [[Stake]] to reveal a [[Winged Cloud]], hit the [[Winged Cloud]] with an egg to make some stone blocks emerge from the ground, use them to reach and enter the [[Pipe]] above on the roof and you will be taken to a canyon with a [[Kangaroo]]. Hop in the Kangaroo's pouch and go hop, hop, hopping across the road collecting [[Coin]]s as you go. The Kangaroo has extraordinarily high hops and can also float in mid-air briefly, keep hopping across the area collecting [[Coin]]s and the [[Smiley Flower]] at the end and then hop out of the Kangaroo's pouch and exit through the pipe at the end to return to the main level. Destroy the Box and the sand chunks below it and grab the [[Red Coin]] above the [[Stake]] and pound the [[Stake]] to get another [[Red Coin]] inside it, next, ground block the cracked block to reach the [[Smiley Flower]] and exit the niche through the [[Flipper]]s, after this, avoid the [[Piranha Plant]]s and [[Shy Guy]]s (or beat them), grab the [[Baby Coin]] (if you can), and jump through the [[GOAL! Ring]] to complete the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
During your first visit, you are unable to grab the [[Baby Coin]] as it requires [[Baby Peach]] but after completing [[Catch the Breeze!]], return to this level and switch to [[Baby Peach]] via the nearby [[Stork Stop]] if you haven't already and then advance to the end where the [[Piranha Plant]]s are and flutter amidst the wind and use [[Baby Peach]]'s parasol to fly up to grab the [[Baby Coin]].
