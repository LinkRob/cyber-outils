'''Bowser's Sourpuss Bread''' is an object in ''[[Hotel Mario]]''.

==Description==
Bowser's Sourpuss Bread is being toasted by several toasters in [[Roy's HardBrick Hotel]], resulting in electrical flickering. It appears to be regular loaves of bread, and a pack of Bowser's Sourpuss Bread is light green, displays the product's name in large, green text, and a picture of [[Bowser]]'s head in the bottom-right corner. While not outright stated, Bowser's Sourpuss Bread is likely created by the [[Koopa Troop]].

==In ''Hotel Mario''==
Bowser's Sourpuss Bread is only shown during a cutscene. When Mario holds up a bag of Bowser's Sourpuss Bread, he says, "All toasters toast toast!".
