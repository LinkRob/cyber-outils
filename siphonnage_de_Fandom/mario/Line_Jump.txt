'''Line Jump''' is a [[Stickers#Battle Stickers|Battle Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. Using Line Jump in battle attacks all enemies in a row for normal damage. If [[Mario]] jumps on a spiked enemy, however, the jumping sequence stops. Also, the damage gradually decreases as you stomp more foes.

== Appearance ==
Line Jump stickers are mainly blue in color. They have an orange background and take up only one sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]].
