|latest = }}'''Wicked Wario''' is a form of [[Wario]] that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. This form can be used after Wario gets the Wicked [[Guise Gem]] from [[Carpaccio's Lab]]. To use Wicked Wario, the player must draw a triangle from Wario's back (like a wing). Wicked Wario is also the last transformation that is acquired in the game.

==Appearance==
Wario as Wicked Wario dons a red, skintight devil-like costume with wings which have purple webs. Like Sparky Wario, his gloves make a change in which they turn purple. He wears blue pants with a thin tail ending in a triangular tip coming out. 

Wicked Wario wears the "W" emblem in the middle of his torso.

==Powers and Abilities==
Wicked Wario main ability is the power of flight. At first, he can only fly up and down slowly but after getting the Wicked [[Mastery Gem]], Wario can fly in any direction. This makes Wicked Wario the most useful disguise in the game when it comes to maneuvering quickly through levels. However, this disguise is obtained very late in the game and therefore isn't used as often as the other disguises (this is most likely done as Wicked Wario's free flight would've made the game much easier if it was obtained early on).

Wicked Wario's main weakness it that he cannot attack at all. And before getting the Mastery Gem, his movement in his flight was very limited making use of him rather tricky. Wicket Wario is only used in a few moments of the game, most notably, during the final leg of the race against [[Carpaccio]] where Wario must quickly fly up in order to beat Carpaccio to the piece of the [[Wishstone]].

==Trivia==
*Wicked Wario is the only disguise in the game that has no methods of attack at all.
