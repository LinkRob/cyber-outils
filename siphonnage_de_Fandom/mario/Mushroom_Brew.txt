'''Mushroom Brew''' is the Duel [[minigame]] of [[Horror Land]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==
Characters have to press buttons as they appear on the screen. Pressing the correct button places a [[mushroom]] into the brew. In the end, the player with more mushrooms in their pot mixes them to create a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]], winning the duel. If both players have the same number of mushrooms in their pots when time runs out, no Star is created and the minigame ends in a draw.

==Controls==
* {{button|n64|A}} &ndash; Blue mushroom
* {{button|n64|B}} &ndash; Green mushroom
* {{button|n64|Z}} &ndash; Gray mushroom

==In-game text==
''Stew the best mushroom brew by putting the proper ingredients into the pot. There are 3 types of mushrooms. Add them in the order [[Goomba]] tells you, and don't delay! Press the {{button|n64|A}} Button for blue, the {{button|n64|B}} Button for green, and the {{button|n64|Z}} Button for gray mushrooms.''
