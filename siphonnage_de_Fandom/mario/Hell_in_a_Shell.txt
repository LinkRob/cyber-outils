The '''Hell in a Shell''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario is the only character that can use the Hell in a Shell.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Hell in a Shell resembles a hand cannon. Its design is strongly resembling [[Bowser]]. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack with a medium range. The Hell in a Shell deals around 25 to 35 damage. When dealing damage to foes, it can inflict [[Bounce (status effect)|Bounce]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Hell in a Shell as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it after the player unlocks the Battle HQ.
