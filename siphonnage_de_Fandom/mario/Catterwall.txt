'''Catterwall''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', part of [[Orbulon]]'s [[IQ]] folder, and later returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', instead as one of [[Mona]]'s microgames. The name is a pun of "caterwaul" an idiom for a person or animal making a terrible sound.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to find the cat among the cats. The picture is at the bottom-right corner for the targeted cat. It takes place outside during night. Cats are idle.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' There are six cats.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' There are eight cats.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' There are ten cats.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
The microgame belongs to [[Mona]] in the '''Mash League''' in the '''''That's Life''''' theme. The place is changed that it takes place inside a store. Some cats are active.
