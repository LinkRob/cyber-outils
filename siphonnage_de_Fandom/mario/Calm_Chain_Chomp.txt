'''Calm Chain Chomp''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Calm Chain Chomp has the same appearance as a [[Chain Chomp]], but it is imbued with the [[Calm]] [[vibe]].

===Behavior===
Like with other Calm enemies, Calm Chain Chomps usually start out sleeping, but if a Calm Chain Chomp hears [[Princess Peach|Peach]], it wakes up, after which it behaves like a regular Chain Chomp.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Calm Chain Chomps first appear in the [[level]] [[Hoo's Wood 2-8]]. In the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]], its named is spelled '''C. Chain Chomp'''.
