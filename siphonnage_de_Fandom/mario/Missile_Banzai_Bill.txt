'''Missile Banzai Bills''' are a variant of [[Banzai Bill]] and [[Missile Bill]] first appearing in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', they are a homing variant of Banzai Bill that locks in on [[Mario]] and friends, they’re also quite durable and only very few attacks can defeat them.

==History==

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''===
[[Image:NSMBW Bull's-Eye Banzai Screenshot.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] near a Missile Banzai Bill.]]
Missile Banzai Bills first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', in this game, they are incredibly rare enemies and are only seen towards the end of [[World 9-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-3]], unlike regular [[Missile Bill]]s, Missile Banzai Bills are never seen being fired from a Banzai Bill Cannon only emerging from the [[Abyss]] below; however, an unfinished setting exists suggesting some cannons would have fired them. Anyway, the Missile Banzai Bills will emerge from the pit below and the second they’re right in front of [[Mario]], they will charge straight at him, Missile Banzai Bills can be dangerous as the abyss they emerge from along with the limited space on the moving platforms makes dodging them very tricky, the key, however, is to fake them out and jump so they zoom above Mario, one Missile Banzai Bill is required to get the final [[Star Coin]] so be sure to align Mario correctly, Missile Banzai Bills are very durable and are immune to [[Fireball]]s, likewise, [[POW Block]]s are useless against them and [[Ice Ball]] only hold them for a second before they instantly break free. A Missile Banzai Bill, however, can be beaten with Stomps or the effects of a [[Star]].

===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
Missile Banzai Bills (under the name '''Bull's-Eye Banzai''') return in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' as placeable enemies. A Bull's-Eye Banzai emerges from a [[Red Banzai Bill Cannon]], Bull's-Eye Banzais now have a new behavior actively following [[Mario]] just like their [[Missile Bill|smaller counterparts]], a Bull's-Eye Banzai can be stomped to reach higher areas and they can still be defeated with Stomps, the effects of a [[Super Leaf]], [[Cape Feather]], or a [[Star]]. Bull's-Eye Banzais appear in the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', ''[[Super Mario World]]'', and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' styles, they are replaced with [[Cat Banzai Bill]]s in the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' style which behave almost identically.

==Trivia==
*Missile Banzai Bills are similar to [[Mad Banzai Bill]]s from ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', both are aggressive, and homing variations of [[Banzai Bill]]s and both are primarily found in special levels.
*In the American Prima Guide for ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', Missile Banzai Bills were sometimes erroneously referred to as [[King Bill]]s, the name of a much larger [[Bullet Bill]] species, this error was corrected in the European version of the Guide.
*Missile Banzai Bills are among one of the few enemies from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' to not return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', or ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.
