==History==

Docks are found throughout the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] mostly on [[Lavalava Island]]. The Docks sole purpose is to allow [[Mario]] to swim on [[Sushie]] on the water. This is required many times and even helps lead to [[Super Block (object)|Super Block]]s.
