'''Nintendo Switch Online''' is an online service which launched on September 18, 2018 for the [[Nintendo Switch]] and [[Nintendo Switch 2]] as a paid subscription. Then in 2021, the Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack was released.

== Description ==

The Nintendo Switch Online service allows subscribers of the service to play online, get exclusive rewards among other things.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nintendo.com.au/nintendo-switch-family/online-service|title=Nintendo Switch Online - Nintendo|work=Nintendo|accessdate=2023-05-19}}</ref>

Subscribers also have the ability to play retro games on the consoles respective "- Nintendo Switch Online" app.

== Retro Services ==
Certain [[Nintendo]] consoles have been put on the service as a way for people to play older Nintendo games.
