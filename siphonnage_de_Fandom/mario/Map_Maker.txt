'''Map Maker''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be obtained by completing the quest [[Treasure of Mystery!]].

==Layout==
The map is created on a tattered, old paper background with a grid.

==Gameplay==
Map Maker can be used to create a treasure map, hence its name, and the player can mark a spot somewhere on the map for someone else to find. A variety of symbols can be used, including a [[treasure chest]], a star, a crescent, a square, a house, a plus sign, and lines, but only up to thirty symbols can be placed.
