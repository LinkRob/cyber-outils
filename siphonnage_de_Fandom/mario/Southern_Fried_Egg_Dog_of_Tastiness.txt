==History==
This food item was mentioned by [[Mr. Hoggle]] in [[Glitzville]]. Originally, he was going to make this item by combining his [[Hot Dog]]s with a Southern [[Yoshi Egg]]. Supposedly, the Southern Fried Egg Dog of Tastiness was going to make the fighters even stronger but since [[Mario]] rescued the Yoshi Egg earlier, he was forced to cancel making the product.
