A '''Catch Card SP''' is an item in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Catch Card SPs are upgraded versions of regular [[Catch Card]]s. Catch Card SPs can be bought in the [[Fondest Hopes]] shop in [[Flopside]] but are sometimes found in other levels hidden or dropped by enemies when they are defeated. Catch Card SPs are able to capture an enemy's soul and turn them into Catch Cards. Catch Card SPs are very effective on weaker enemies such as [[Goomba]]s but will never work on stronger enemies such as [[Muth]]s. Catch Card SPs are also ineffective against all bosses as well.
