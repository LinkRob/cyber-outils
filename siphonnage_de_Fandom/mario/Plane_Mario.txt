'''Plane Mario''' is one of [[Mario]]'s four transformations in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Plane Mario is the first transformation that can be used in the game. It can be used after being cursed by the [[Black Chest Demon]] in [[Rogueport Sewers]]. Then, the player can activate Plane Mario by standing on one of the Plane Panels and pressing {{button|gcn|y}} to morph Mario into a paper airplane. The player must tend tilt back on the control stick to pilot Mario. Plane Mario is mainly used to help cross over large gaps or hazards. Pressing {{button|gcn|b}} will bring Mario back to normal and this can even be done mid-flight.
