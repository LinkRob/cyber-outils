'''Beep-O''' is a primary character from ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' that acts as a tutorial to the player. The player controls him and the playable characters follow him in movement.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Beep-O is a circular-shaped robot with blue eyes. When he goes to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], he grows ears like the [[Rabbid]]s. When he is angry, his brow furrows and he becomes a dark shade of red.

=== Personality traits ===
Beep-O seems to be the smart one of the group (probably since he’s a robot) and often wisecracks to most remarks and happening events. He also seems clueless at times, especially with the [[MegaBug]]. He appears to be full of himself and has a short temper, but is otherwise a good guy.

== In ''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle'' ==
Beep-O appears in ''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle''. He is first seen with the Tech Girl in her room as a non-talking robot. After she leaves, he shuts down. Soon the Rabbids teleport into her room and he awakens. The Rabbids and Beep-O are sucked into the Washing Machine and taken to the Mushroom Kingdom. Beep-O lands with [[Mario]], [[Rabbid Luigi]], and [[Rabbid Peach]]. He then teaches them battling techniques to destroy the opposing [[Ziggy|Ziggies]]. He then accompanies them throughout the entire adventure.

== In ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'' ==
Beep-O appears in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''. His lines are fully voiced in the game, complete with a british accent.

== Trivia ==
*Beep-O is the only playable character that can talk in the game.
*Beep-O can communicate with the Rabbids.
