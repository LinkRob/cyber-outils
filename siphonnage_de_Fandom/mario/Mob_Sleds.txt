{{Nihongo|'''Mob Sleds'''|氷の上で追いかけっこ|Kōri no ue de oikakekko|Tag on the ice}} is one of the 1-vs.-Rivals minigames featured in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. The name of the game is a play-on-words version of "bobsleds." This minigame acts as the opposite of Ruins Rumble, where the solo player controls a vehicle while the team players evade the solo player.

== Gameplay ==
The solo player's objective is the survive without the rivals' [[Cooligan]] bobsleds hitting them until the time runs out.

The rivals' objective is to come in contact with the solo player before time runs out.

The track is smaller in the 1 v 2 version.

== Etymology ==
Mob Sleds is a pun of "bobsleds".
