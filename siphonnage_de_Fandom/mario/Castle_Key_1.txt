'''Castle Key 1''' is a special item featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a one-time item used to unlock a room in [[Nimbus Castle]] where [[Shelly]] is held. [[Mario]] receives Castle Key from a [[Nimbus]].
