{{Nihongo|'''Horror Harriers'''|オバケにぶつかるな|Obake ni butsukaru na|Do Not Run into the Ghosts}} (or '''Jeepa Peepas''' in British version) is a General minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

== Opening ==
From the haunted house, the camera pans towards a circular area. In the meantime, the players run into the area and the gates close much to their surprise.

== Gameplay ==
The player's objective is to be the last standing player to not get touched by the [[Peepa]]s. Peepas move in different formations. If the player touches the Peepa, the Peepa will grab onto the player and carry them up into the sky eliminating them from the game, whoever is the last standing or whoever is still remaining when time is up is the winner.
== Controls ==
*{{button|3ds|Stick}} - Move
*{{button|3ds|A}} - Jump
