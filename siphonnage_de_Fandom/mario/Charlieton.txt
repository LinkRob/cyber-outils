'''Charlieton''' is a character and a salesman who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== History ==
He is found in the central plaza in [[Rogueport]]. He often sells rare items and badges to those who he meets. Charlieton says that he comes from a far away place out of the Rogueport area. His inventory changes every time [[Mario]] leaves Rogueport for a long time. After his return, Charlieton has new items in his inventory and restocks the previous ones later on. His items vary from the weak [[Dried Shroom]] to the unique but seemingly useless [[W Emblem]] badge. He is very similar to that of [[Rip Cheato]] from the first ''[[Paper Mario]]'' game, though Charlieton lets Mario know what he's paying for beforehand.

Mario also find Charlieton in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. Charlieton sells many items such as [[Mushroom (Paper Mario series)|Mushrooms]], [[Fire Flower]]s, [[POW Block]]s, [[Thunder Rage]]s, [[Super Shroom]]s, and [[Honey Syrup]]. The further in the pit, the higher the cost of these items.

In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], Charlieton can now be found in [[Boggly Woods]] right near the warp pipe that leads to [[The Great Tree]]. As always, Charlieton can sell up to four items at a time to Mario and can be quite useful if Mario requires items to help him out in Boggly Woods (most notably before the upcoming fight against [[The Three Shadows]]).

== Etymology ==
His name is a play on the word "charlatan."
