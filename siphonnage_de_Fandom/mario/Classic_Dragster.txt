The '''Classic Dragster''' (known as '''Nostalgia 1''' in the PAL release) is a default [[kart]] used by medium-sized characters in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

== Description ==
[[File:MKW_Luigi_Classic_Dragster_screenshot.png|thumb|200px|right|Luigi [[Mini-Turbo|powersliding]] in his Classic Dragster on [[Mario Circuit 3|<small>SNES</small> Mario Circuit 3]].]]
The Classic Dragster resembles a late-1930's Grand Prix racecar, which resembles [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Cucumber]] from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. Also, just like the Cucumber, the characters' emblem is located on the hood. This is the only kart in the medium-sized class not to have a [[bike]] counterpart. The [[Zucchini]] (Gherkin in the British English version) from ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', could be considered to be the Classic Dragster's successor.

== Statistics ==
Its stats are quite similar to the [[Cheep Charger]] and the [[Quacker]], offering good [[acceleration]], [[handling]], [[drift]] and [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]], but also offers below average [[speed]] and [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]]. Its [[Off-Road|off-road]] stat is evenly balanced.

== Color schemes ==
*[[Mario]]: White body, red stripes and hub caps, gold dual exhaust.
*Luigi: White body, green stripes and hub caps, silver dual exhaust.
*[[Princess Peach]]: White body, pink stripes and hub caps, light yellow dual exhaust.
*[[Princess Daisy]]: White body, orange stripes and hub caps, light dual yellow exhaust.
*Yoshi: White body, lime green stripes and hub caps, bronze dual exhaust.
*[[Birdo]]: White body, violet stripes and hub caps, silver dual exhaust.
*[[Diddy Kong]]: Beige body, dark brown stripes and hub caps, black dual exhaust.
*[[Bowser Jr.]]: White body, gold yellow stripes and hub caps, brown dual exhaust.
*Male Miis: White body, navy blue stripes and hub caps, dull gold dual exhaust.
*Female Miis: Rose pink body, creamy white stripes and hub caps, silver dual exhaust.
