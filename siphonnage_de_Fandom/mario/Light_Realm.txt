The '''Light Realm''', or '''Gem World''', is a region in ''[[Yoshi's Safari]]''. It is the first of two parts in [[Jewelry Land]], followed by the [[Dark Realm]]. It contains more cheerful, positive, and brighter levels than the Dark Realm. The Light Realm was formed as a result of a large earthquake caused by the removal of the twelve gems. [[King Fret]] and seven of the gems are captive in the Light Realm. It contains seven levels, each guarded by a [[Koopalings|Koopaling]]. After clearing the Light Realm, [[Mario]] and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] save King Fret and travel to the Dark Realm.
