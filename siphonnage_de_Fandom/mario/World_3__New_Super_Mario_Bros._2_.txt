'''World 3''' is the third [[world]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. Since [[World Mushroom (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Mushroom]] and [[World Flower (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Flower]] replaced some of the worlds, the game could not have all of the standard world themes, so they decided to mix the [[coastal theme]] with the [[Forest theme|jungle theme]] into one world, which is the first instance that this had occurred.
