'''World Star''' is the special world of ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' as of version 3.0.4. Although its exact terrain is unknown, World Star contains nine levels that consist of the jungle, airship, mansion, desert, sky, and tower themes. 

==Levels==

===[[Scuttlebug Forest]]===
A jungle level with Goombrats and Scuttlebugs. To unlock this level, you must defeat Bowser with Princess Peach in [[Bowser's Bob-ombing Run|World 6-4]].

===[[Airship Armada]]===
An airship level with Ninjis, Burners, and Cannonballs. To unlock this level, you must clear [[Up and Over|World 1-1]] with 5 coins or less.

===[[Fishing Boo Taunts You]]===
A haunted-themed level with Fishing Boos. To unlock this level, you must win Toad Rally three times in a row.

===[[Desert Fuzz]]===
A desert level with Fuzzies and Pokeys. To unlock this level, you must collect 30 or more medals in a single round of Remix 10.

===[[Dark and Stormy]]===
A dark haunted-themed level with Boos as well as thunderstorms that strike them. To unlock this level, you must clear [[Cutting-Edge Spire|World 4-1]] with 60 or more left on the timer.

===[[Wild Slopes]]===
A sky level with Munchers, Big Spinies, Piranha Plants, and Koopa Paratroopas. To unlock this level, you must clear [[Wall-Kicking It Underground|World 1-2]] with 400 or more coins.

===[[Scaredy Rat Race]]===
A tower level with Scaredy Rats. To unlock this level, you must clear [[Slope to Success|World 4-2]] without defeating a single Goomba.

===[[Spike Bar Jungle]]===
A jungle level with Goombrats, Poison Bubbles, River Piranha Plants, and Spike Bars. To unlock this level, you must clear [[Lakitu's Revenge|World 5-1]] with 300 or more coins.

===[[Airship Plunder]]===
An airship level with Bob-ombs, as well as a boss battle against two Boom Booms. To unlock this level, you must defeat 20 or more Ninjis in a single run in [[Airship Cannons...Fire!|World 2-4]].
