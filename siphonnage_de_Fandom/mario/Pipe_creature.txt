The '''pipe creatures''' are a group of two living creatures who make a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[A Toadally Magical Adventure]]." Here, they started out as regular pipes, but [[Toad (species)|Toad]] brings them to life using the [[Wizard King of the West]]'s magic wand.
