'''''Donkey Konga 2''''' is a [[Nintendo GameCube]] game and the second game within the ''[[Donkey Konga (series)|Donkey Konga]]'' series, following after the [[Donkey Konga|titular game]]. ''Donkey Konga 2'' was released in 2004 in Japan and in 2005 in other countries. Like the other ''Donkey Konga'' titles, ''Donkey Konga 2'' is compatible with the [[DK Bongos]], and it was marketed as being compatible with said controller.

''Donkey Konga 2'' is noted for its Teen (T) rating in North America, which is because of some of the song's lyrics. The European version maintains a 3+ rating from PEGI from having an entire line-up of family-friendly songs. Some songs in the North American version such as "{{w|Headstrong (Trapt song)|Headstrong}}" from {{w|Trapt}} are believed to have contributed to the Teen (T) rating.
