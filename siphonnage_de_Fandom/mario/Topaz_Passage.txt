'''Topaz Passage''' is the third world of ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
The Topaz Passage features a consistent toy theme, and its [[level]]s consist of various toys such as toy blocks or dominoes. The four levels of Topaz Passage include [[Toy Block Tower]], [[The Big Board]], [[Doodle Woods]], and [[Domino Row]]. The boss fought at the end of Topaz Passage is [[Aerodent]], a mouse-like ghost on a giant inflatable teddy bear balloon who throws needle-like enemies and spits fire. The [[jewel piece]]s of Topaz Passage appear as a topaz, and the [[CD]]s found here are yellow.
