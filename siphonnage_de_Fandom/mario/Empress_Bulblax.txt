The '''Empress Bulblax''' is a giant [[Bulborb]] and a recurring boss within ''[[w:c:pikmin:Pikmin 2|Pikmin 2]]''. It has a starring playable role in the ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'' [[microgame]] [[Pikmin 2]], in which the Empress Bulblax's goal is to roll over and squish some [[Pikmin]] within a time limit. The Empress Bulblax retains its starring role in the microgame's reappearance in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'', the Empress Bulblax reuses its model from ''Pikmin 2''.

Empress Bulblax made two crossover cameos in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series, the first time as a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|trophy]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and the second time as a [[spirit]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
