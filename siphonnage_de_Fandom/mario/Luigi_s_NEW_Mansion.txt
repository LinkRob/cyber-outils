'''Luigi's NEW Mansion''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
Luigi's NEW Mansion is a home of [[Luigi]]'s that briefly appears in the ending of ''Luigi's Mansion''. Its appearance depends on how much [[treasure]] that Luigi has collected during his journey. This is very similar to the ending of ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'', in which the new home that the [[Genie (boss)|Genie]] gives [[Wario]] during the ending depends on how much treasure Wario had collected during his journey.

==In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}}==
===''Luigi's Mansion''===
[[File:LM Rank A Mansion.png|thumb|200px|left|The Rank A mansion, as seen in the epilogue of ''Luigi's Mansion'']]
After Luigi defeats [[King Boo]], followed by the ending credits, an epilogue is shown where it says that [[Professor E. Gadd]] delightfully decided to build a new house for Luigi using all of the treasure that Luigi had founded during his journey. If Luigi earns Rank A, the mansion becomes a large manor with the letter "L" marking its front. Each lower rank provides progressively smaller and smaller houses, culminating in a tent for Rank H. After Luigi's NEW Mansion is shown, the scene transitions into a painting within a framed portrait. The total amount of money collected is briefly shown at the bottom-right, right after a small, red "The End" notice is shown. The mansion ranks, the amount of money required, and their corresponding message includes:
{{br|left}}
*'''Rank A:''' Finish the game with 100,000,000G to 129,999,999G. (Message: "''Congratulations! Your mansion is complete!''")
*'''Rank B:''' Finish the game with 70,000,000 to 99,999,999G. (Message: "''Welcome to Luigi's NEW Mansion!''")
*'''Rank C:''' Finish the game with 60,000,000 to 69,999,999G. (Message: "''Welcome to Luigi's NEW Mansion!''")
*'''Rank D:''' Finish the game with 50,000,000 to 59,999,999G. (Message: "''Welcome to Luigi's NEW Mansion!''")
*'''Rank E:''' Finish the game with 40,000,000 to 49,999,999G. (Message: "''Welcome to Luigi's NEW Mansion!''")
*'''Rank F:''' Finish the game with 20,000,000 to 39,999,999G. (Message: "''Welcome to Luigi's NEW Mansion!''")
*'''Rank G:''' Finish the game with 5,000,000 to 19,999,999G. (Message: "''Welcome to Luigi's NEW Mansion!''")
*'''Rank H:''' Finish the game with 5,000 to 4,999,999G. (Message: "''The haunted mansion disappeared without a trace...''")

The lowest amount of money that Luigi can have by the end is 5,000G, as [[King Boo's crown]] is the only required collectible, automatically obtained upon defeating King Boo.

In PAL releases, the total amount of money required for Rank A is increased to 150,000,000G. It is only possible to achieve an A Rank in the [[Hidden Mansion]], as it contains more money than the original Mansion in the PAL version.

===''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''===
[[File:Luigi's House LMDM opening.png|thumb|200px|''Luigi's Mansion 2 HD' screenshot of the Rank D mansion in the intro]]
The Rank D Mansion appears during the opening, having a nearly identical appearance as in ''Luigi's Mansion'' except for some wear in the outer walls and a different fence.
{{br|left}}
===''Luigi's Mansion'' (Nintendo 3DS)===
[[File:LM3DS Luigi's NEW Mansion Rank S.png|thumb|left|200px|''Luigi's Mansion'' (Nintendo 3DS) screenshot of the Rank S mansion]]
The remake features differently designed paintings of Luigi's NEW Mansion than in the original ''Luigi's Mansion''. Aside from retaining the original ranks, the game also adds another rank:

*'''Rank S:''' Finish the game with 130,000,000G or more. (Message: "''Congratulations! Your mansion is complete!''")
{{br|left}}
==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
The Rank A Mansion is seen in the background of {{classic course|GCN|[[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Luigi Circuit]]}} in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. In the former game, the mansion is modeled, whereas in the latter, it is only stored as a texture. This may be due to graphical/hardware limitations.

==Trivia==
*A feature similar to Luigi's NEW Mansion is used in the ending for ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', after [[The Last Resort]] collapses and is rebuilt.
