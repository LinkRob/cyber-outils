The '''Purple Comet Host''' of the [[Battlerock Galaxy]] is a [[Gearmo]] character who made her only appearance in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. As her name suggests, she hosts the [[Purple Comet]] of the Battlerock Galaxy. [[Purple Coin Host (Dreadnought Galaxy)|Another Gearmo]] hosts the [[Purple Coin]] challenge in the [[Dreadnought Galaxy]].

== Profile ==
In ''Super Mario Galaxy''{{'}}s Battlerock Galaxy, [[Mario]] needs to grab 100 Purple Coins while keeping his distance from the electric wires and the cannonballs. At the end, the Purple Coin Host will tell him how well he did. If it is 100, he will get his [[Power Star]]. If it is 99 or less, he will lose a life.

In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''{{'}}s [[Tall Trunk Galaxy]], the Purple Coin Host dropped 140 Purple Coins on the Trunk Slide. She asks if Mario will go down the slide and grab at least 100 of them on his way down. Once that has been done, [[Purple Coin Host (Dreadnought Galaxy)|her brother]] (at the end) awards Mario with a Power Star.

==Trivia==
*In her rant when she sees that Mario gets no [[Purple Coin]]s, the Purple Coin Host refers to herself as an "old iron lady," indicating that Gearmos actually are gendered separately.
