The '''Shy Guy Galleon'''<ref>Hodgson, David S. J. ''Mario Kart Wii'' Premiere Edition. Page 122.</ref> is a pirate ship manned by [[Pirate Shy Guy]]s. Its mast features a pastiche of the {{wp|Jolly Roger}} commonly used by pirates, with a [[Shy Guy]] mask in the center instead of a skull. It first appears in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'' in the background of the level [[Shy Guy's Ship]], where it is used to launch [[Bob-omb|Bob-bomb]]s at the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] from the background. It later appears in the background of [[Shy Guy Beach]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' with some design differences, such as the sail's colors being inverted. In ''Mario Kart: Super Circuit'', it steadily circles the track and occasionally launches [[cannonball]]s that disappear in a puff of smoke when they hit the track. If a cannonball hits a racer, they lose some of their [[coin]]s. In ''Mario Kart Wii'', the Shy Guy Galleon stays moored on one side of the track. It again launches cannonballs at the racers, but here, the cannonballs remain stuck in the ground after landing. They then begin blinking and, after a few seconds, blow up with a big explosion.
