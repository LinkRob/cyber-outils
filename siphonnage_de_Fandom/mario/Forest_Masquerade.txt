'''Forest Masquerade''' is the third level of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 11.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 8 [[Spear Guy]]s. The enemy captain is a Spear Guy.

The 2nd battle contains 2 [[Fly Guy]]s and 6 [[Shy Guy]]s. The enemy captain is a Shy Guy. The enemy captain can use [[Chance]] and [[Deny]].

The 3rd battle contains 3 Fly Guys and 5 Spear Guys. The enemy captain is a Fly Guy. The enemy captain can use Deny.

The final battle contains 3 Fly Guys, 2 Shy Guys, 2 Spear Guys, and [[Sergeant Guy]]. The enemy captain is Sergeant Guy, and he knows the [[Captain Command]]s [[Call for Backup]] and Deny. He will either call a [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopa]], [[Buzzy Beetle]], or Shy Guy,

After the final battle, the player recruits Sergeant Guy.

The player can replay the battle to recruit up to one Shy Guy, Spear Guy, and or Fly Guy.
