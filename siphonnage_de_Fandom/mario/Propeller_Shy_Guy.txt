'''Propeller Shy Guys''', also named '''Flying Shy Guys''', are variants of [[Shy Guy]]s which first appear in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Propeller Shy Guys resemble regular [[Shy Guy]]s, except they wear a helicopter-like pack on their back.

===Behavior===
Propeller Shy Guys fly via a helicopter-like pack that they carry on their back consisting of a two-blade propeller (on top of the pack), which spins on a horizontal axis, and an eight-blade propeller (at the back of the pack), which spins on a vertical axis.

==''Yoshi'' appearances==
===''Yoshi's Story''===
[[File:Propeller Shy Guy YS sprite.png|frame|left]]
Propeller Shy Guys fly above [[Baby Yoshi]] carrying [[fruit]]. They will continue to fly back and forth across the area where they appear in, but will immediately fly off-screen if Yoshi eats the fruit they are carrying. Like normal Shy Guys, they change color when a Baby Yoshi performs a [[Ground Pound]] near one.

Propeller Shy Guys may appear alone or in a swarm. When swarming, they will alternate between moving across the background and the foreground in a loop. They can be defeated by Baby Yoshi like any other Shy Guy (with the exception of the [[Black Shy Guy]], which has its own characteristics).

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
====''Super Smash Bros.''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'', Propeller Shy Guys appear rarely in the background of [[Super Happy Tree (stage)|Yoshi's Island]] stage, where one sometimes flies across the screen, going either fast or slow. Occasionally, a slow one will be struggling to carry a [[melon]].

====''Super Smash Bros. Melee''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', Propeller Shy Guys appear in the form of both a stage element and a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]]. Propeller Shy Guys are encountered on the [[Yoshi's Story (stage)|Yoshi's Island: Yoshi's Story]] stage, where they carry [[food]]. When attacked softly, or if an object is thrown at them, the Propeller Shy Guy drops his food and flies off the screen; if they are knocked out with an attack, they drop the food they are carrying, and spin downwards off the screen. If food is disabled in the item switch, they do not bring food. They also sometimes appear in the background of the Yoshi's Island stage from ''Super Smash Bros.'', renamed Past Stages: Yoshi's Island.

====''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', a Propeller Shy Guy appears in the form of a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Sticker]].

====''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''====
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Propeller Shy Guys reappear in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', where they return as stage elements in the Yoshi's Story stage from ''Super Smash Bros. Melee''.
