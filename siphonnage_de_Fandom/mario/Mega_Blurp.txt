'''Mega Blurps''' are a larger sub-species of the [[Blurp]] appearing in a few games. Like Blurps, they wear goggles and are seen swimming in the waters but their larger size makes them a touch harder to avoid. Mega Blurps are mainly seen underwater.

==History==
===''Super Mario Maker/Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS/Super Mario Maker 2''===
Mega Blurps appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' as well as it's [[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS|two]] [[Super Mario Maker 2|sequels]] as placeable enemies. They can be made by giving a [[Super Mushroom]] to a [[Blurp]] in the ''[[Super Mario World]]'' style, Mega Blurps are replaced with [[Mega Green Cheep-Cheep]]s in the ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' styles and [[Mega Deep Cheep]]s in the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' style). Mega Blurps behave almost identically to Blurps (slowly swimming in the waters waiting for contact) however their larger size makes them harder to avoid, Mega Blurps can also be given wings to become a [[Winged Mega Blurp]]. Mega Blurps are commonly seen underwater however when placed on land, they can hop around, in the Castle theme, they are engulfed in flames from the [[lava]] below which can make them hazardous to touch. A Mega Blurp can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]].

==Trivia==
*Mega Blurps are the only large-sized [[Cheep-Cheep]] to only appear in one style of the game.
