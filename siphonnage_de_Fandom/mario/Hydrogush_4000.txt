The '''Hydrogush 4000''' is one of [[Professor Elvin Gadd]]'s inventions which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It resembles [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]]'s head, but with a large hose attached to its mouth. Because of this, the Hydrogush 4000 can produce lots of water, enough to put out any volcanic eruption.
