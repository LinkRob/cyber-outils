'''Dark Star''' (ダークスター) is a special move used by [[Exor]], [[Count Down]], [[Culex]], and [[Smithy]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
When Dark Star is used, a large, black angry [[Super Star]] appears on screen. It then bounces around while laying down for a few seconds; this repeats a few times until the evil star jumps off-screen, inflicting great damage. Despite its large size, the Dark Star can only target one character.

==Users==
*[[Exor]]
*[[Count Down]]
*[[Smithy]]
*[[Culex]]

== Trivia ==

* It is the enemy version of the [[Star Rain]] special move used by [[Mallow]]. Unlike the Dark Star, however, this attack hits all opponents.
* The main antagonist Smithy in his Wizard form can use this move, which is especially dangerous, as he can occasionally perform the Dark Star twice in a row.
* The Dark Star in ''Super Mario RPG'' is likely the inspiration of the [[Ztar]] in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]] and the [[Dark Star (character)|overarching antagonist of the same name]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', giving its very similar appearance.
