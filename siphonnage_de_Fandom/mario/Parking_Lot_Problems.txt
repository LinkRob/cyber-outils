'''Parking Lot Problems''' is [[Mona]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

== Gameplay ==

The person has the car it wants inside the speech bubble, the player must press {{Button|3ds|a}} at the right time when it reaches the car.
*'''1st level difficulty:''' The cars all look drastically different from one another.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The cars all have the same paint color, but otherwise look drastically different from one another.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The cars all have two different paint colors, and all are the same car type (excluding one).
