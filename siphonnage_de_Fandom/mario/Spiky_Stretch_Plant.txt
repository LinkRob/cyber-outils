'''Spiky Stretch Plants''' are a variant of [[Stretch Plant]]s and an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Profiles==
===Physical description===
Spiky Stretch Plants have the appearance of a light green bulb with red spikes.

===Behavior===
Spiky Stretch Plants, unlike regular Stretch Plants, have the ability to physically move. They attempt to damage [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] by touching either of them with their spikes.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
Spiky Stretch Plants are enemies encountered in [[Honeybloom Galaxy]], most prominently during the mission [[Bumble Beginnings]].
