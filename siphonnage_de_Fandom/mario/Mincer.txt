'''Mincer''' is an enemy found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]''. They appear to be spiky sphere-shaped spike balls that cannot be defeated, not even by [[Animal Buddy]] attacks. They appear to act like [[Zinger]]s, except they are spike balls and cannot be defeated in any way so therefore must be avoided. They appear in [[Manic Mincers]], [[Poison Pond]], and [[Torchlight Trouble]] in ''Donkey Kong Country''. It appears only in [[Spiky Tyre Trail]] in ''Donkey Kong Land''. They may move in various patterns.
