A '''classic course''', named '''Retro Course''' in earlier appearances, is a term in the {{series|Mario Kart}} that refers to courses in a ''Mario Kart'' game that returns from an earlier ''Mario Kart'' installment. Classic courses are preceded by a prefix of the course's console of origin. For example, [[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|<small>GCN</small> Mario Circuit]] is from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', which is a [[Nintendo GameCube]] game, and the system's recognized abbreviation is GCN. This is likely done to differentiate between other identically-named classic courses, such as [[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart DS)|<small>DS</small> Mario Circuit]] from ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.
