The '''Big Buzzy Beetle''' is a mini-boss that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. They also appear in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', only appearing in one level, [[Hither Thither Hill]].

==Appearance==
The Big Buzzy Beetle is basically a giant version of smaller [[Buzzy Beetle|Buzzy Beetles]].

==Battle==
[[Mario]] fights the Big Buzzy Beetle just as he is about to leave the mines of [[Hither Thither Hill]]. Mario must use the [[Battle Spinner]] technique in order to do some damage on the Big Buzzy Beetle since it always flips itself over right on the next turn. [[POW Block]]s and [[Boots|Jump]] stickers are helpful in this fight. After the Big Buzzy Beetle is defeated, the way is cleared and Mario is awarded with a [[HP-Up Heart]].
