A '''Meteor Meal''' is an item and [[recipe]] which appears in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:MeteorMealTTYD.png|frame|left]]
In this game, Meteor Meals restore 7 [[Heart Point|HP]] and an additional 2 HP for three more turns making it a rather effective recovery item. It requires a [[Shooting Star]] and a [[Shroom Fry]] to make and be given to [[Zess T.]] of [[Rogueport]]. This item can also be obtained from [[Eve]] as a reward upon completing her trouble at the [[Trouble Center]].

===''Super Paper Mario''===
Meteor Meals can now be made by [[Saffron]] with just a single Shooting Star. However, Meteor Meals are now battle items as using one inflicts a set amount of damage to enemies.
