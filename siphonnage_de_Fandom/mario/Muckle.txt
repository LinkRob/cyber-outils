A '''Muckle''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are large jellyfish first encountered in [[Nimbus Land]], where they serve as guards for [[Valentina]]. Muckles are related to [[Leuko]]s.

==Battle==
Muckles are rather strong enemies when encountered in battle. Unlike Leukos, Muckles specialize in [[Ice (element)|ice]] attacks, including [[Crystal]] and [[Blizzard (move)|Blizzard]].

===Strategy===
Muckles are weak to fire meaning [[Mario]]'s fire techniques such as [[Super Flame]] are very effective on them.
