A '''Scratch card''' is a bonus feature in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey|remake]].

== Description ==
Scratch cards allow [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or [[Bowser]] to receive a refund for some of their [[coin]]s spent while shopping. Having a higher 'Stache or Horn statistic will affect the player's chance of winning. After purchasing an item from the shop, the player may be given the option of scratching a scratch card. A [[Mushroom]] refunds 10% of spent coins, a [[Fire Flower]] refunds %20, and a [[Starman|Star]] refunds %40. Scratching a [[Fawful]] will not reward anything.

===Types===
There are three different types of scratch cards, depending on the [['Stache]] and Horn statistics. The 'Stache number represents Mario and Luigi's combined score for the statistic, as indicated below.
