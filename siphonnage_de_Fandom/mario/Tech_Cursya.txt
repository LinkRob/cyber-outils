}}A '''Tech Cursya''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Tech Curysas are a species of [[Cursya]] that can be found in the [[Gap of Crag]], [[Castle Bleck Foyer]], and the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Touching a Tech Cursya will cause the victim to be inflicted with the [[No Skills]] status disabling certain moves for a time. This means [[Mario]] cannot use his [[Flip]] ability, [[Princess Peach]] cannot use her parasol, [[Bowser]] cannot breathe fire, and [[Luigi]] cannot use his Super Jump. Tech Cursyas should be taken out from a distance to avoid being cursed.
