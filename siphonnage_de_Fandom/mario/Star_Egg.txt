The '''Star Egg''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
The Star Egg can be won from [[Grate Guy]]'s "Look The Other Way" after playing it 100 times. It can be used as many times as desired, and using the Star Egg deals 100 damage to all enemies on the screen each time. If one is wearing the [[Quartz Charm]] when using the Star Egg, the damage increases to 150.
