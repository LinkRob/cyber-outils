'''Blown Away''' is a downloadable [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object to escape a wind storm by reaching a manhole. There are pieces of trash flying around, and two in particular have to be avoided by the player's character. If the player does not move forward, the [[wind]] pushes them back, sometimes a requirement for completing the microgame. The trash always flies in the same place simultaenously: a can and a fish skeleton fly upward from the center, and later a log flies left of the starting point. If the character reaches the manhole without getting hit, the player wins the microgame, otherwise they lose.
