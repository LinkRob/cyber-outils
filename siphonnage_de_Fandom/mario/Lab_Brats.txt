'''Lab Brats''' is a Rare [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''. Its name is a pun on "{{wp|laboratory rat|lab rats}}," which are rats used for scientific research.

==Opening==
In the minigame's introduction, [[Professor E. Gadd]] states that he invented a maze-making machine called the Maze Manufactory. He taunts the player, saying that they cannot solve the mazes before time is up, then he wishes the player good luck in solving them.

==Gameplay==
The aim of the minigame is to reach the end of five mazes in five minutes. It is one of the three one-player minigames available in ''Mario Party 6''. On every level, the player can also find another character wandering around: The characters include a [[penguin]], a [[Ukiki]], a [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]], a [[Goomba]], and a [[Shy Guy]]. Only one appears on each floor, and they appear in a random order. Though they do not help the player directly, finding them contributes to the player's rank. A [[trampoline]] propels the player to the next maze. At the end, E. Gadd gives the player a rank, as high as 16 and as low as 1.
