'''Blue Badwagon''' is a High-End kart in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. The Blue Badwagon is based on the [[Badwagon]] from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
Blue Badwagon is a High-End kart released on [[New York Tour]] in 2019. It contains a special skill, [[Mini-Turbo Plus]] allowing the player to gain more points when performing [[Mini-Turbo|Mini-Turbos]].
