'''Bean Hollows''', formerly named '''Beanholes''', are a recurring object in the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They are represented by X-shaped symbols into the ground to show that a [[bean]] is located underneath. A bean can typically be unearthed from a Bean Hollow if [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] use their [[Drill Bros.]] move.
