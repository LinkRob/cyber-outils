The '''sled-mounted shooter''' is a vehicle in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Description==
It is a one-man sled featuring an adjustable turret at the front, from which small [[bomb]]s can be thrown. There is a meter on the side of the turret that shows how much the sled-mounted shooter has heated up.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
The sled-mounted shooter is used by [[Luigi]] only once, in [[Secret Mine]]'s mission [[Chilly Ride]], in which Luigi rides it down the [[Frozen Pit]] to throw bombs at the icy armor surrounding the [[Shrewd Possessor]]. To use the sled-mounted shooter, the player must aim the cursor with the gyroscope or with {{button|3ds|Stick}} and fire bombs by pressing {{button|3ds|A}}. Each time Luigi fires a bomb or takes damage from the Shrewd Possessor, the sled-mounted shooter heats up, as shown on the meter. If the shooter overheats, the player receives a [[Game Over]].

In the ending credits, there is a graphic showing [[Professor E. Gadd]] and two [[Greenie]]s happily riding the sled-mounted shooter.
