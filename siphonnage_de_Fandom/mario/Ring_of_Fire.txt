A '''Ring of Fire''' is a fiery ring featured as a hazardous object in various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.

==Description==
A Ring of Fire usually consists of fireball segments that spin around, in a similar manner to a [[Fire Bar]], but a Ring of Fire can appear as a complete ring in certain cases. If a character touches a Ring of Fire, they get burned, which in the {{series|Super Mario}} causes them to power down or in the {{series|Mario Kart}} briefly slows their [[kart]].

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!''===
[[Image:Ring of Fire Stadium.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] and [[Waluigi]] approach a Ring of Fire.]]
Rings of Fire can be found in [[Waluigi Stadium]], [[Wario Colosseum]], and [[Bowser's Castle]]. In this game, they are depicted as a ring of fireballs spinning around at varying speeds and are usually seen after jumps with [[Dash Panel]]s. Players should stay in the center of the Ring of Fire as they will spin out upon touching a fireball. Rings of Fire also commonly have [[Item Box]]es in the center of them.

===''Mario Kart DS''===
[[Image:Ring of Fire Biker Stadium.png|thumb|right|[[Wario]] ready to jump through the Ring of Fire.]]
Rings of Fire return in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. In this game, they retain their role and appearance from the previous game and scorch racers upon contact. Rings of Fire can commonly be seen after the jumps from the [[Dash Panel]]s and sometimes have [[Item Box]]es in the center of them. They are commonly found in [[Wario Stadium (Mario Kart DS)|Wario Stadium]].

===''Mario Kart Wii''===
[[Image:Ring of Fire Gauntlet.png|thumb|left|[[Waluigi]] going through a Ring of Fire.]]
Rings of Fire are back in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' where they are now found in [[Bowser's Castle]] and the returning [[Waluigi Stadium]]. In this game, their spinning speed is consistent with each and they can once again spin out those who touch them. A Ring of Fire can commonly consist of multiple fireballs but sometimes can also consist of three. Ring of Fire once again house [[Item Box]]es in the center of them.

===''Mario Kart 8/Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''===
[[Image:Ring of Fire Upgrader.png|thumb|right|[[Mario]] nearing a Ring of Fire.]]
After an absence from ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', Rings of Fire appear again in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. In these games, they are seen in the returning course [[Wario Stadium (Mario Kart DS)|Wario Stadium]] where they appear after the various jumps and house [[Item Box]]es in the center. Once again, running into one will cause a scorching spin. They are commonly seen spinning more quickly in these games as well.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[Image:Ring of Fire Power.png|thumb|left|A [[Pull Star]] in a Ring of Fire.]]
Rings of Fire make their mainstream debut in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. In this game, they appear in [[Bowser's Lava Lair]] and the [[Slipsand Galaxy]]. Rings of Fire can either be formed of fireballs spinning around in a ring formation or the fireballs can be around a planet as serve as an obstacle like the [[Firebar]]s. They will scorch [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] upon contact. Rings of Fire cannot be destroyed but avoiding them isn't too challenging. 

===''Super Mario Run''===
[[Image:Ring of Fire Jump.jpg|thumb|right|91px|[[Mario]] makes the jump for a Ring of Fire.]]
Rings of Fire return in ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' this time as a complete ring of fire rather than spinning fireballs. They can be found in various Castle-levels such as [[Fire Bar Castle! Youch!]] and [[Rings of Fire!]]. Rings of Fire will power-down [[Mario]] if he touches the fire of one requiring him to jump through. They can either be seen in place or moving around. Rings of Fire commonly have items floating in the center of them as well.
