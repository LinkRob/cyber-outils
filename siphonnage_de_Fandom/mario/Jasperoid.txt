'''Jasperoid''' is a character who appears in the fifth chapter of ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. He is a [[Cragnon]] who lives in [[Downtown of Crag]].

==History==
[[File:Jasperoid.jpg|thumb|left|In-game screenshot]]
Jasperoid's main role in the game is to tell Mario and co. the order of blocks to hit when he comes to a set of three blocks. The first code he tells Mario is '''Left''', '''Right''', then '''Middle'''.
