The '''Wallet Badge''' is a type of [[badge]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can be bought at [[Princess Peach's Castle]] for 200 [[coin]]s, but there is also one found in [[Toadwood Forest]]. If the Wallet Badge is a equipped on a [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bro]], they drop little to no coins if they decide to [[flee]] from battle.
