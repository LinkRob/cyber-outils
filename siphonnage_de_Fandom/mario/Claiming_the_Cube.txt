'''Claiming the Cube''' is a mini-game from ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''. It is a General minigame.

== Instructions ==
Jump and ground pound to color the white panels as they appear on the cube.

== Controls ==
*{{Button|3ds|stick}} - Move
*{{Button|3ds|A}} - Jump
*{{Button|3ds|a}} then {{Button|3ds|a}} - Ground Pound
