A '''Drumstick''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. They make a cameo in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

==Description==
As their name implies, Drumsticks look like real-world chicken drumsticks, but their "meat" is tilted to one side, implying that their design comes from a chicken leg.

Only [[Bowser]] can use Drumsticks. He can consume them to restore his health in the same manner as [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] use [[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]]s to restore theirs. The Hot Drumstick is the most common drumstick, giving 80 [[Heart Point|hit points]] to him. The next level up is the Fiery Drumstick, which serves as the "Super" version, restoring 150 hit points. The "Ultra" version is the TNT drumstick, which restores 280 hit points. There is no drumstick counterpart for the Max Mushroom. The 3DS remaster adds a "cheesy" variant that can be used in lieu of the [[Star Candy]], as Bowser can only use the Star Candy in the DS version. Eating either one, causes him to be healed completely and regain all lost [[Special Point]]s.

In the 3DS remaster, if Bowser falls below half HP when fought in [[Doctor Toadley]]'s [[Toadley Clinic|Clinic]] and the [[The Gauntlet|Gauntlet]], he will eat a Drumstick to heal completely unless he took enough damage. He doesn't do this at the start of the game in [[Princess Peach's Castle]].

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', Bowser is shown being given a drumstick by [[Starlow]] during his recuperation after [[Glohm Bowser]]'s destruction. The drumstick eventually drops on a [[Goomba]], who is told by Bowser to give it to him.
