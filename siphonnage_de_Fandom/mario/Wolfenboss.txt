'''Wolfenboss''' is a boss in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. It is the third boss overall, and is encountered in [[The Pool of Rain]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Wolfenboss is a kitsune who wears a purple sorcerer's robe. During its battle, Wolfenboss at first appears as a small blue and white wisp.
