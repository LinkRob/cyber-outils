The '''Master Poplin''' is a character from ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. He is a [[Poplin]] living in [[Shining Falls]].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Master Poplin looks like other [[Poplin|Poplins]]. He has a hijab and white poofy trousers and yellow shoes. He has a yellow flower bud.

=== Personality ===
Despite his acknowledgement of [[Prince Florian]]'s royalty, the Master Poplin is not one to break rules, despite skipping his trials being a potential way to get a [[Royal Seed]] more quickly. However, his sense of honour allows him to give those who pass his trials his Royal Seed.

== History ==
The Master Poplin makes his debut when [[Mario (character)|Mario]], his friends, and their new companion [[Prince Florian]] enter the Master Poplin's house. The Master Poplin, upon finding visitors, tells them to complete his trials, even though Prince Florian asks for the [[Royal Seed]] immediately. When someone completes his trials, the Master Poplin congratulates the person who did so. When the gang completes all his trials, the Master Poplin brings Mario and company to his house, where he gives them the Royal Seed, before leaving to either [[Sunbaked Desert]], [[Fungi Mines]], or [[Deep Magma Bog]]. If the player returns to his mansion after they get the Royal Seed, the Master Poplin will open a path to a [[Propeller Flower]], which leads to [[Shining Falls Special Triple Threat Deluge]] in the game's [[Special World (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|Special World]].

The Master Poplin appears along with several more of his species (including the [[Angler Poplin]] and [[Loyal Poplin]]) to celebrate Mario and company's victory after the defeat of [[Bowser]] and the destruction of [[Castle Bowser]].
