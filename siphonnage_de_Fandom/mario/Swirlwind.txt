'''Swirlwind''' is an obstacle, but sometimes considered an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]''.

== Description ==
They appear in the two levels named [[Snake Charmer's Challenge]] and [[Swirlwind Storm]] where they appear as the main obstacles. These obstacles chase after [[Donkey Kong|Donkey]] and [[Diddy Kong|Diddy]] until falling down an abyss. This obstacle can also help the monkeys reach higher areas when jumped on the top. These obstacles also injure the monkeys when touched.
