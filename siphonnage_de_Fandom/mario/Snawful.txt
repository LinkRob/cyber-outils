'''Snawfuls''' are a species of [[Fawfulized]] [[Snowman]] that appear as enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
Snawfuls are creatures that can be summoned from [[Blizzard Midbus]]. [[Fawful]] leads Snawfuls in attack. [[Bowser]] must inhale them with the vacuum block power or they will hide under ground during an attack. The only way to stop them is to have [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] fight them. They can shoot ice blocks that can freeze enemies but, it can easily be avoided by jumping. They have 105 HP each and recommended attack against them would be the [[Fire Flower]].

==Trivia==
*Its name is a pun of "snowfall".
