'''Toy Dream''' is a [[board]] from the [[Dream Depot]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
[[File:ToyDreamStory.jpg|thumb|160px|The Story Mode version of the board|left]]
Toy Dream is one of the boards where players must defeat the [[Koopa Kid]] trio in Story Mode, although the board there is smaller and simpler. It is created from dreams of toys. Built on a light-blue, checkered platform amongst the clouds, Toy Dream is surrounded by a wall of building blocks. It is built out of toys, including a toy train, toy ship, presents, a robot, toy soldiers and other such objects. One of the toys is based on [[Mecha-Bowser]]. It shoots any player that stops on the [[Green Space|Happening Space]] in its mouth from a cannon back to the start. If players land on a Happening Space on top of a wall of blocks, toy soldiers blast down the blocks, knocking them to a lower section of the board. Landing on the Happening Space on top of a present in the middle of the board causes a [[Jack-in-the-box (object)|jack-in-the-box]] to catapult whoever landed on it to another part of the board. Landing on the Happening Space in front of either train station gave players the opportunity to ride the train one full circuit, collecting [[coin]]s released from the engine along the way.

The background music of this board is called "Lots of Toys". A rearrangement, titled "Cabin Fever", appears as a song in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.
