'''Knockback Bros.''' is a [[Bros. Attack]] used by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] during the events of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. The move costs five BP on Modes 1 and 2, and three BP on Mode 3. Luigi learns this move. Luigi smacks Mario with his hammer, making him a ball, and hits him like a golf ball. The buttons pressed are {{button|gba|B}} to whack Mario, {{button|gba|B}} again to send him shooting toward an enemy, and {{button|gba|A}} to do damage to the enemy.

After successfully executing the attack forty-three times, Mario and Luigi learn the Advanced version. In the Advanced version, more controls are added. The controls for the standard version are the same, but to execute the Advanced version, {{button|gba|A}} must be pressed immediately after the first {{button|gba|B}} press. Then the controls are {{button|gba|B}} to hit Mario, {{button|gba|A}} to do damage, {{button|gba|B}} to hit Mario, {{button|gba|A}} to do damage, etc.

This move is also used by the [[L33t Hamm3r Broz.]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. They perform the move the same way as the Hammer Bros. did in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''. The L33t Hamm3r Broz. are only able to perform it together with both of them standing. Occasionally, their hammers will break, causing the other L33t Hamm3r Bro to bounce towards [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]]. To dodge the attack, the babies must jump over the L33t Hamm3r Bro who targeted themn.
