'''Dr. Freezegoods'''<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 126.</ref> are snowmen enemies from the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Dr. Freezegood has the appearance of an ordinary snowman, but with an added red bucket on its head and a green scarf wrapped around where its two body segments meet. A Dr. Freezegood is always seen riding a pair of orange skis.

===Behavior===
A Dr. Freezegood is usually seen riding down a snowy hill on their skis. In the ''Yoshi's Island'' series, several Dr. Freezegoods ride a ski lift at first before dropping off to start skiing.

==In {{franchise|Yoshi}}==
==={{series|Yoshi's Island}}===
====''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''====
[[File:Doctor Freezegood SMW2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' and its remake ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]'', Dr. Freezegoods are only encountered in [[World 5 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 5]]. Similarly to [[Bumpty|Bumpties]], a Dr. Freezegood does not injure a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], but it pushes him as it moves downhill. If a Dr. Freezegood is not sliding downhill, it remains idle. A Yoshi can defeat a Dr. Freezegood by [[jump]]ing onto its head, also causing the Dr. Freezegood to release a few coins, but he cannot eat a Dr. Freezegood.

====''Yoshi's New Island''====
In ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', Dr. Freezegoods return as enemies, particularly within the level [[Ski Lift Leap]]. Here, Dr. Freezegoods are found skiing down slopes in the first area, while in the second area, one is seen riding a ski lift. A Dr. Freezegood crumbles on contact, and they do not spawn any coins, unlike in ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''.

===''Tetris Attack''===
In ''[[Tetris Attack]]'', a Dr. Freezegood is seen assisting [[Bumpty]] during his round.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Dr. Freezegood's name is likely derived from ''{{w|Dr. Feelgood (album)|Dr. Feelgood}}'', an album from the real-world heavy metal band ''{{w|Mötley Crüe}}''.
