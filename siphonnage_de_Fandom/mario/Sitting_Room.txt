The '''Sitting Room''' is an optional room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The center of the Sitting Room has a table with an easy chair and a sofa around, and the top of the table has a candle from which a [[Fire Elemental Ghost]] can be spawned. In the southwest corner, there is a small drawer with a vase on top, which a [[Water Elemental Ghost]] can appear from. In the northwest corner, there is a plant that can be watered for a [[Gold Bar]]. On the eastern wall is a door that leads into the [[Guest Room]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Sitting Room becomes accessible in [[Area Four]], after Luigi obtains the room's [[key]] in the [[Sealed Room]]. As soon as Luigi enters the Sitting Room, vines seal both doors. [[Luigi]] has to use the effects of a [[Fire Elemental Ghost]] to light some sconces on the north wall so that five [[Gold Ghost]]s and two [[Temper Terror]]s to appear. Once Luigi captures the ghosts, both doors are unsealed.

In the PAL releases' and Nintendo 3DS version's [[Hidden Mansion]], two [[Temper Terror]]s and three red [[Ghost Guy]]s appear in the Sitting Room instead.
