'''Poison Bloopers''' are an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Profile==
=== Physical description ===
Poison Bloopers are a [[Poison (element)|poison]]-elemental variant of [[Blooper]] with which they have the same base appearance as, but are light purple in color. Poison Bloopers have three darker purple spots on their heads.

==In ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''==
Two Poison Bloopers appear along with a [[Wiggler Segment]] in [[World 3 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|World 3]].
