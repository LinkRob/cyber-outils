'''Chicken Pinch''' is one of [[Mona]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''. The microgame made a reappearance in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', once again as part of Mona's set of microgame.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to capture the chicken with tweezers. In ''WarioWare Gold'', the chicken randomly has hair, and if the player pinches the hair as the chicken flees, they lose at the microgame.

* '''1st level difficulty:''' The chicken jumps thrice. The last jump is a backflip. 
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' The chicken jumps twice. The last jump is a backflip. 
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' The chicken is idle for a while, then it does a backflip. In ''WarioWare Gold'', a few chickens sometimes appear in the level.
