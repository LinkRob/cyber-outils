A '''tell''' is a gameplay mechanic in every game of the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}, first appearing in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
A tell is an indicator that enemies use when at least two [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] are in battle to relay which character they intend to attack during their turn. This way, the player can have the targeted Mario Bro react accordingly, by either dodging or doing a counterattack, depending on the move from the enemy. Because every enemy has a different tell, the player often has to figure it out themselves, except during a tutorial at the start of the game. Some enemies have a red or green color to indicate whether [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] is the target, respectively, such as [[Nooz]]es in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''===
[[Starlow]] introduces the tell mechanic to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] in their first battle together, against three [[Gromba]]s in one of the dark rooms of [[Pi'illo Castle]].
