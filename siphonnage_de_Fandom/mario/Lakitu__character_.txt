'''Lakitu''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' and a character representative of his species of the [[Lakitu|same name]]. He lives in the [[Lakitu House]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Like the various other ''Mario Party Advance'' characters who are a representative of their species, Lakitu has the same appearance as his species. One distinct difference is that Lakitu carries a Toad Force V action figure on his fishing pole to indicate he is a fan of the show.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Like many other characters, Lakitu is a big fan of [[Toad Force V]]. He asks for the player character's help in remembering an iconic quote from episode 28. By visiting [[Mushbert]] in the [[Mushroom Condo]], he states that the line is "I'm an iron shroom!". When the player character goes back to tell Lakitu, he thanks them, and the player is given the [[Bait 'n Wait]] [[Gaddget]] as a reward.
