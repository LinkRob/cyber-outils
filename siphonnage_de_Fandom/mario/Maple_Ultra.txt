==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:MapleUltraPM.png|thumb|left]]In ''Paper Mario'', Maple Ultra is a [[recipe]] [[Mario]] can make by giving a [[Maple Syrup]] and an [[Ultra Shroom]] to [[Tayce T.]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. This item has the same effects as its ingredients respectively, and this item restores fifty points of [[Heart Point]]s and ten points of [[Flower Point]]s.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Maple Ultra is created by [[Zess T.]] with the same ingredients required in ''Paper Mario''. This item has effects as its ''Paper Mario'' predecessor as well.
