The '''Red Shell Plus''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
The Red Shell Plus increases the player's chance of getting a [[Red Shell]], this can only be accessed depending on the glider equipped. With the Red Shell Plus glider, the player also gets points obtained by using the Red Shell./

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
It made its first appearance at the launch of the game, ''Mario Kart Tour''.
