'''Putrid Piranha''' is a variant of [[Piranha Plant]] which only appears in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. It first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''[[Paper Mario]]''===
Purtrid Piranhas are found mainly in the [[Jade Jungle]]. They mainly attack [[Mario]] by biting at him, which has the possibility of inflicting the [[Poison (status effect)|Poison]] status effect on him.

===''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''===
Putrid Piranhas are found on [[Keelhaul Key]] and the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. In this game, the Putrid Piranhas can now attack with a poison breath attack that not only hits both Mario and his partner but may also cause poison. However, this attack is weaker than their usual bite attack.

===''[[Super Paper Mario]]''===
Putrid Piranhas can be found in the [[Land of the Cragnons]]. Putrid Piranhas are commonly found in warp pipes and attack by breathing poisonous gas at it's target. Putrid Piranhas often drop items such as [[Horsetail]]s when defeated.
