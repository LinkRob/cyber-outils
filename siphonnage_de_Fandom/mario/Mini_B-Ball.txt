'''Mini B-Ball''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be obtained by completing the quest [[Hammerama]].

==Description==
Mini B-Ball involves playing a simple basketball-like [[minigame]], although playing it requires the system to be rotated upright. Like with basketball, there is the simple objective of having to shoot the basketball into the hoop. The power of the shot can be adjusted by holding left on the D-Pad, and releasing it causes the basketball to be shot.
