'''Giga Cat Mario''' is a [[Power-Up]] in ''[[Bowser's Fury]]''. Mario temporarily takes on this form after collecting a [[Giga Bell]] in order to fight [[Fury Bowser]].

His appearance is similar to Cat Mario, although he has a large orange-yellow mane, and a tuft of hair of the same color on his tail, similar to that of a lion's.

After collecting all 120 [[Cat Shine]]s, Cat Mario will have an appearance similar to this form.
