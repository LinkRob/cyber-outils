'''Wubbas''' are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They are featured prominently in the level bearing their name An Uncharted Area: Wubba Ruins, although the character can turn into them with [[Wonder Effect|Wonder Effects]] in many different levels.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Wubbas are light green blob enemies made out of a slimy goo-like substance. They are about as tall as [[Mario (character)|Mario]] is in his [[Super Mario (form)|Super form]]. Their eyes consist of black sclerae and yellow pupils.

=== Behaviour ===
Wubbas pace back and forth in their territory and swim through goo.

== In ''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'' ==
Wubbas make their debut appearance in the level [[An Uncharted Area: Wubba Ruins]], and the level [[KO Arena Fungi Funk]] is the last level to feature them.

[[Mario (character)|Mario]] can transform into a Wubba via a [[Wonder Effect]], looking like them albeit red in colour and a nose with his signature moustache beneath his eyes and with his cap intact. In this form, he can stick to walls.

== Trivia ==

* The Japanese name of the Wubba matches the [[Purupuru]] from the [[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|''Donkey Kong'' Game Boy]] game.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Wubbas are called ''Purupuru'' (written プルプル) means "jiggly," their name matching that of the [[Purupuru]] from the [[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|''Donkey Kong'' game for the Game Boy]]. In Chinese, they are called ''Tántánjiāo'' (written 弹弹胶), which means "slime." They are called ''Gélatine'' in French, meaning "gelatin," and in German, they are called ''Blobub'', derived from the sound of a bubble. In Italian, they are called ''Gelatino'', derived from ''gelatina'' ("gelatin"), while in Korean, they are called ''Taeng'geul-taeng'geul'' (written 탱글탱글) which is an ideophone for "bouncy." Wubbas have ''Gelatino'' for their Portuguese name, from Portuguese ''gelatina'' (gelatin), while their Russian name is ''Zhel'yenovy'' (written Жельеновы), and the Spanish dub calls them ''Blublú'', derived from the sound of a bubble.
