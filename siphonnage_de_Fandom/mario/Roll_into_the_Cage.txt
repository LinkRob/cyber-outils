'''Roll into the Cage''' is the first [[mission]] of [[Tick Tock Clock]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

== Layout ==
[[Mario]] has to first go past the first swinging [[pendulum]] before the [[conveyor belt]]. To the left is a [[flipping block]], which Mario has to [[jump]] on. He must take a short path and then jump from three more flipping blocks. Afterward, Mario has to pass by the large plane with a [[Clock hand|moving hand]]. Mario must jump from a few more platforms and continue upward to find a cage-like grid with the [[Power Star]]. Mario must jump through the wide opening to obtain the Power Star.

== Objective ==
Mario has to collect the [[Power Star]] in a cage-like grid.
