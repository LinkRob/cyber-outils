A '''Poplin Shop''' is a location in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It is a shop run by [[Poplin]]s where one can purchase things with [[Flower Coin]]s.

== What You Can Buy: ==
In most Poplin Shops, you can buy these things:

* [[Wonder Seed|Wonder Seeds]] - 100 Flower Coins 
** You can only buy one of these at each shop.
* Badges
* [[Standee|Standees]] - 10 for Random
** There is a Poplin shop in the Special World where you can choose which character standee and you will get ones you don't have every time.
