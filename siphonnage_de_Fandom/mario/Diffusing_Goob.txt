'''Diffusing Goob''' is a type of rare [[Ghost (species)|ghost]] that makes debut in [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']] version 1. 4. 0. They can only be found in the [[ScareScraper|ScareScraper's]] 10 or 20 floors mode. They have 250 HP, which is more than twice that of regular [[Goob]]'s HP. They can be captured in the same way as regular Goobs.

== Physical Appearance ==
Like [[Diffusing Hammer|Diffusing Hammers]], Diffusing Goobs are Goobs that have glowing orange body with yellow eyes and mouth.

== In ''Luigi's Mansion 3'' ==
Unlike Regular Goobs but similar to Diffusing Hammers, Diffusing Goobs do not directly chase after [[Luigi]] and punch him. After appearing, they simply stay where they are and start spinning. As they spin, lots of [[Mini Goob|Mini Goobs]] will be spawned from their location and quickly surround them, leaving more enemies for Luigi to deal with. They will then disappear and reappear at another place to spin again. Like Diffusing Hammers, they can't be flashed while spinning, and before appearing at a spot, sparks of orange will appear at that spot, indicating their location.
