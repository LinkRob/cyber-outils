'''Kaboomer''' is [[Rabbid Rosalina]]'s default weapon in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
The Kaboomer appears in ''Sparks of Hope'' as Bowser's weapon. The player can change the skins of the Kaboomer. Rabbid Rosalina will be unlocked during the Spark Hunter, [[Midnite]]'s battle, since Rabbid Rosalina owns this weapon.

== Statistics ==
The Kaboomer can attack multiple opponents from a distance, and anything it hits results in an explosion, damaging or destroying any cover the enemy is hiding behind.
