Figure out which one it is through rigorously honed observation and decision-making skills."''}}'''Muscle Road''' is a [[Microgame]] from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'', serving as the fifth Boss Microgame. It uses the Lifter form for its gameplay. [[Orbulon]] hosts the Microgame, which appears in his level, Sweet Dreams, Sweeter Fruit.

== Layout ==
The setting is a curved path that the protagonist, a blue man, has to navigate. At the end is a straight path where purple men pose, their number being 4 * the difficulty level.

== Gameplay ==
During the first phase, the player gets two cards in their hands and must play a card so the protagonist must not land on a space with a trap (which comes in the form of a bear trap); the protagonist moves a number of spaces equal to the number on the card, and there will always be at least one card with a number not equal to any of the distances to spaces with traps. If he lands on a space that has a trap, the protagonist takes damage as he gets bitten by the trap. If he lands on a space that has a heart, he gets an extra health point. If the player takes too long to play a card, they will be forced to play the card with the number equal to the distance to a space with a bear trap, if there is such a card. If the protagonist loses all of his health, the player loses.

During the second phase, the player has to play cards that match the poses that the warriors are striking to move the protagonist forward. Playing the wrong card damages him. Matching the poses four times (not necessarily in a row) will result in a win.

=== Difficulty Levels ===

* '''Level 1 (Easy)''': During the first phase, there are few traps (often three). During the second phase, one purple man appears in each wave. The first two are unobscured, the third one is hidden by clouds but passes between them (He is visible while moving between clouds.), and the fourth one moves over a balance beam and a hurdle.
* '''Level 2 (Hard)''': During the first phase, some more bear traps appear. During the second phase, two purple men pose per group. The men in the first group show up immediately, while the men in the second group rotate. In the third group, the men move around a column. The fourth group appears from multiple vases.
* '''Level 3 (Expert)''': During the first phase, many bear traps plague the spaces. Three purple men pose in each wave for the second phase, and the third group is obscured by columns while trucks carrying pictures of random poses appear to obscure the fourth wave.

== Trivia ==

* Even though the description says, "You get two cards, and only <u>one</u> of them is right," there can be situations where both cards lead to safety.
