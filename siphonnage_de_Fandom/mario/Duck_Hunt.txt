'''''Duck Hunt''''' is a light gun shooter game first released in 1984 by [[Nintendo]] for the [[Family Computer]], then later released for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] overseas. A handful of subjects in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media are named Duck Hunt:

*[[Duck Hunt (character)|Duck Hunt]], a duo of the [[Dog (character)|Dog]] and a [[Duck (species)|Duck]] from ''Duck Hunt'', first appearing in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]''.
*[[Duck Hunt (stage)|Duck Hunt]], a stage in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U'', later returning in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
*[[Duck Hunt (WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!)|Duck Hunt]], a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.
*[[Duck Hunt (WarioWare: Twisted!)|Duck Hunt]], a microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.
*[[Duck Hunt (WarioWare: Touched!)|Duck Hunt]], a microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.
*[[Duck Hunt (WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase)|Duck Hunt]], a microgame in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''.
*[[Duck Hunt (WarioWare Gold)|Duck Hunt]], a microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.
