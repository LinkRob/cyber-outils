'''Fire Bros. Hideout #4''' is a level of [[World Castle]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Features==
Fire bros. Location is across the line from Boiling Blue Bully Belt in some rubble. The arena is similar to the Hideouts 1 and 3, but the back of the room is raised and it contains seven Fire Bros. to battle.
==Green Star==
*'''Green Star 1''': Defeat the seven Fire Bros. to get the Star.
