'''World 3-2''' is the second level of [[World 3 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 3]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and its remakes ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]''.

== Layout ==
The player starts along a path with several [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Little Goomba]]s. A [[? Block]] containing a power-up lies over the stone pillar ahead, followed by more Goombas and a Koopa Troopa. Ahead are two [[Brick Block]]s above a Koopa Troopa. The bottom one holds [[coin]]s, while the top one contains a [[Starman]]. Next up is a gap, followed by a [[Koopa Paratroopa]], a Koopa Troopa, and a trio of Little Goombas. Ahead is a separated platform with a pillar and a Brick Block, and beyond this is a row of Koopa Troopas, followed by a [[pipe]] with a [[Piranha Plant]] inside. Lastly, there is Koopa Troopa and a row of Little Goombas, shortly before the stairs leading to the [[flagpole]].

==Challenges in ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe''==
In ''Super Mario Bros. Deluxe'', there are five [[Red Coin]]s and a hidden [[Yoshi Egg]] to collect. The player will also obtain a medal based on their score. The target score for this level is 85000.

=== Red Coin locations ===
* Red Coin #1 is located on the path with the first row of enemies.
* Red Coin #2 is located in the air right beside the ? Block.
* Red Coin #3 is located next to the isolated stone pillar.
* Red Coin #4 is located in the air, along the path following the stone pillar. The player must use [[Hidden Block]]s to the right of the Red Coin to collect it.
* Red Coin #5 is located in the air to the right of the pipe.

=== Yoshi Egg location ===
* The Yoshi Egg is found inside the top block within a pyramid of Hidden Blocks next to the fourth Red Coin.
