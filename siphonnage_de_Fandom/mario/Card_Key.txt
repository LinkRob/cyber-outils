The '''Card Key''' is an important item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Description==
In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', the Card Key is required to access certain areas in [[X-Naut Fortress]] in ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''. There are three different colors of Card Keys in this game.

[[File:CardKey_SPM.png|thumb|left]]
In ''Super Paper Mario'', a single Card Key can be found in [[Floro Caverns]]. It can be used in order to access the processing room eventually leading into an encounter with [[Dimentio]] and [[O'Chunks]]. [[Dottie]] can shrink the party to go through the tiny hole that leads the Card Key.
