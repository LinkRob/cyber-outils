'''E. Gadd's Bunker''' is a location which appears in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is the main area where [[Professor E. Gadd]] transports Luigi in and out of during missions. This is also where E. Gadd stays in for the majority of the game as it is protected from any hostile ghosts.

==Description==
E. Gadd's Bunker resembles E. Gadd's lab from the original ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'' except it appears to be much more advanced inside.

===Features===
*'''[[Pixelator]]''': A device that E. Gadd uses to transport Luigi from the Bunker to the other mansions.
*'''[[Parascope]]''': A device that E. Gadd uses to find the exact location of the [[Dark Moon]] pieces.
*'''[[E. Gadd's Vault]]''': A place where E. Gadd keeps data on all of the captured ghosts that Luigi has caught on his journey.
*'''[[Scarescraper]]''': A special mode where a player can battle with friends in sucking up ghosts. This is also used as helpful training.
