'''Socially Awkward Piranha Plant''' is a character whose only appearance is in a variation of the [https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/socially-awkward-penguin Socially Awkward Penguin] image macro uploaded to [[Nintendo of America]]'s Facebook page on July 16, 2014. Despite its name, Socially Awkward Piranha Plant is a [[Venus Fire Trap]], not a [[Piranha Plant]].

In the image macro associated with Socially Awkward Piranha Plant, it is said that he spat [[fireball]]s at everyone in a conversation at a summer party only after building up the courage to join.
