'''Hammer Throw''' is the name of several subjects within the {{franchise|Mario}}, many being different iterations of the Hammer Throw event in the Summer Olympics installments of the {{series|Mario & Sonic}}:

*[[Hammer Throw (badge)|Hammer Throw]], a badge in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]] and the first ''Mario'' subject to be named this.
*[[Hammer Throw (Mario Strikers Charged)|Hammer Throw]], the [[Skillshot]] of [[Hammer Bro]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.
*[[Hammer Throw (Mario Super Sluggers)|Hammer Throw]], the special move of Hammer Bro, [[Fire Bro]], and [[Boomerang Bro]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''
*[[Hammer Throw (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games for Wii)|Hammer Throw]], an Event in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'' for the [[Wii]]. 
*[[Hammer Throw (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games for Nintendo DS)|Hammer Throw]], an Event in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]''{{'}}s [[Nintendo DS]] version.
*[[Hammer Throw (Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games for Wii)|Hammer Throw]], an Event in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' for the Wii.
*[[Hammer Throw (Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games for Nintendo 3DS)|Hammer Throw]], an Event in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]''{{'}}s [[Nintendo 3DS]] version.
