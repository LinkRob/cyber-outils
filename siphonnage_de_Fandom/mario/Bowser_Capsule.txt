A '''Bowser Capsule''' is a unique [[orb|capsule]] that appears only in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. If a player gets a Bowser Capsule from a [[capsule machine]], [[Bowser]] suddenly lands on the player and uses his dark magic to turn a random [[Red Space|minus space]] into a [[Bowser Space|Bowser space]] for the rest of the game. This effect cannot be undone or overridden.
