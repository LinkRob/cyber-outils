'''Fuzzies''', also called '''Fuzzles''',<ref>''[[Nintendo Power]]'' Volume 28, page 15.</ref> are parasitic enemies that debuted in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Profile==
Fuzzies are small, black-furred creatures with unusual, spiky bodies. They possess two strabismus eyes and large teeth, and are often found on [[track]]s. Despite displaying some level of intelligence and the ability to speak. Fuzzies have the ability to secrete webbing-like strands from their bodies, though this ability has largely been phased out in modern times.

==Main appearances==
===''Super Mario World''===
[[File:FuzziesSMW.png|frame|left]]
[[File:Fuzzy Duo.png|thumb|right]]
In ''[[Super Mario World]]'', Fuzzies are particularly rare enemies found circling [[block]]s or on [[track]]s. They first appear in the level [[Donut Plains 3]], which also introduces the [[ON/OFF Switch]]es needed to control the tracks they are on, but they are also common in levels of [[Forest of Illusion]]. Fuzzies do not directly attack [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], only damaging him if they somehow intersect. The Fuzzies circling around blocks can be defeated, either from being [[swallow]]ed by [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] or by hitting them with a [[Koopa Shell]]. [[Cape Mario|Caped Mario]] can also use his Body Press against Fuzzies.

Fuzzies are among the few enemies not shown during the game's end credits.

Although absent from the first ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'', Fuzzies, along with bigger versions called [[Mega Fuzzy|Mega Fuzzies]], reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. Fuzzies can also be found on tracks, usually in big groups, and they do flips when they jump from one wire onto another. Fuzzies can be defeated by either freezing them with an [[Ice Ball]] as [[Ice Mario]] or [[Penguin Mario]] and then shattering the block of ice they are trapped in or by shooting a [[fireball]] at them as [[Fire Mario]]; neither method is possible in ''Super Mario World''. Fuzzies tend to stay frozen longer than most other enemies; this can be advantageous to the playable characters, because frozen Fuzzies can be used as platforms for reaching higher ledges.

Fuzzies are mostly in sky levels. They appear in the levels [[World 7-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7-3]], [[World 9-5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-5]], and [[World 9-8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-8]].

The Prima Guide oddly simply refers to Fuzzies as '''Enemies'''.
{{br}}

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[File:Fuzzy Artwork - Super Mario Galaxy 2.png|thumb|100px|left|Artwork from ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.]]
[[File:Fuzzy Pole.png|thumb|120px|A Fuzzy moving on a pole.]]
Fuzzies return as enemies in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', their first appearance in a 3D ''Super Mario'' title. They look very similar to how they did in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', though they leave behind black smoke clouds as they move.

Fuzzies first appear in the [[Honeybloom Galaxy]], moving up and down the orange [[pole]]s that Mario or Luigi can climb. They are also found in the [[Flipsville Galaxy]], where they can be defeated with a [[Spin Drill]].
{{br}}

===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
[[File:Fuzzy Tightropes.png|thumb|left|Fuzzies in ''Super Mario 3D Land''.]]
Fuzzies return in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', where they are now commonly encountered in sky-themed levels and snow levels such as [[World 3-4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 3-4]]. Fuzzies move along [[tightrope]]s, so Mario must usually have precision to avoid them. Fuzzies are typically found in groups and can only be defeated with the effects of a [[Starman]], [[Statue Leaf]], or an [[Invincibility Leaf]].

===''New Super Mario Bros. 2''===
[[File:W4-C NSMB2.png|thumb|A group of Fuzzies in a room of World 4-C in ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''.]]
Fuzzies and Mega Fuzzies are both featured as enemies in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. They move along tracks in the same manner as in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', but they can now move at varying speeds. Fuzzies appear in [[World 2-A (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 2-A]] and [[World 4-C]], the latter being their first appearance in an underground level within the ''Super Mario'' series. Furthermore, World 4-C only has Fuzzies and Mega Fuzzies. Unlike most enemies, Fuzzies are not affected by a [[Gold Ring]]. They can be defeated by shooting them with a [[fireball]], using a [[Star]], using a [[Super Leaf]] tail whack, or using a [[Gold Flower]].

===''New Super Mario Bros. U'' / ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[File:Fuzzy Cliffs.png|thumb|left|Mario and Yoshi bouncing on Fuzzies in ''New Super Mario Bros. U''.]]
Fuzzies also appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. In these games, they can be found in several levels, such as [[Fuzzy Clifftop]] and several levels of [[Meringue Clouds]]. Fuzzies also appear in the [[Enemy Course]] of [[Meringue Clouds]]. Fuzzies retain their usual behavior from before, moving along tracks and hopping from them, but some of them float idly in midair, sometimes even in [[Water Bubble]]s. [[Mega Fuzzy|Mega Fuzzies]] also reappear, and just as before, both can be beaten with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]] or [[Starman]] and can also be frozen with the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]] to use as a makeshift platform. Additionally, the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] can bounce on Fuzzies without taking damage.
{{br}}
===''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''===
[[File:Fuzzy SM3DW Prima.jpg|thumb|200px|A Fuzzy in ''Super Mario 3D World'']]
[[File:3D Fuzzy Logic.png|thumb|left|Many Fuzzies circling Clear Pipes in ''Super Mario 3D World'']]
Fuzzies reappear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] port ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. In this game, they have the ability to move without the aid of a track or pole. They are generally found in [[Shadow-Play Alley]] and moving through and around [[Clear Pipe]]s. Like in previous games, they are almost invincible, as they can only be defeated by [[Lucky Cat Mario]], a [[Potted Piranha Plant]], or the [[Invincible Mario|invincibility]] effects granted by a [[Star]], an [[Invincibility Leaf]], or an [[Invincibility Bell]]. [[Fuzzy Horde]]s also appear in [[Fuzzy Time Mine]], and they instantly defeat the player's character upon contact, even if they have are under the effects of an invincibility power-up item.

In the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign, Fuzzies are one of several enemies with a regional variant in [[Lake Lapcat]], [[Cat Fuzzy|Cat Fuzzies]], which can be found in [[Risky Whisker Island]].

===''Super Mario Run''===
Fuzzies appear in ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' as enemies moving in trails.

===''Super Mario Odyssey''===
[[File:Fuzzy Icon SMO.png|100px|left|thumb]]
[[File:Fuzzy Transition Odyssey.png|thumb|200px|Fuzzies transitioning between the 3D and 8-bit themes in the Lost Kingdom in ''Super Mario Odyssey''.]]
Fuzzies reappear once again in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', where they can be found in the [[Metro Kingdom]], [[Lake Kingdom]], [[Wooded Kingdom]], [[Lost Kingdom]], [[Mushroom Kingdom (Super Mario Odyssey)|Mushroom Kingdom]], [[Moon Kingdom]], and the [[Darker Side]]. They can travel on electrical wires and tracks, including those of [[spark pylon]]s. In the Lost Kingdom, Mushroom Kingdom, and Moon Kingdom, they can be found in 8-bit segments, using their sprites from ''Super Mario World''. They can also come out of the wall, connected in a line. This makes them the only enemies in the 8-bit segments that do not originate in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' Fuzzies cannot be defeated.
