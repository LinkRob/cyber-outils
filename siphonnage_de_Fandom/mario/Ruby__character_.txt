'''Ruby''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Ruby is a [[Flip-Flopian|Flopsider]] that lives in [[Flopside]]. She was known for being a beauty queen in her younger days. Ruby now lives her life with her granddaughter [[Mitsy]].

==Trivia==
*Ruby's [[Flipside]] counterpart is [[Pearl (character)|Pearl]].
