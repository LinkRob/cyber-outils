'''Chained Kongs''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are only encountered in [[Barrel Volcano]].

==Physical description==
Chained Kongs are a stronger variant of [[Guerrilla]]s They bear a great resemblance to [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], except they wear a gray, spiked collar on both wrists and ankles as well as their head. A Chained Kong has a steel ball attached to the collar on its left wrist.

==Battle==
In battle, Chained Kongs only attack physically as they possess no special attacks but their attack power is very high. They also have very high HP.

===Strategy===
Chained Kong's only weakness is [[Ice]], so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] move is very effective against them.
