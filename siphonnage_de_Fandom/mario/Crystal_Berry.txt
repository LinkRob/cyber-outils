The '''Crystal Berry''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Crystal Berry is given to [[Mario]] by [[Posie]], who is attached to the [[Crystal Tree]] by her roots. It is tasked to Mario to obtain the [[Water Stone]] for [[Lily (Paper Mario)|Lily]] at the far east end of [[Flower Fields]]. Upon finding the stone, it was in the possession of [[Rosie]], a pompous rose at the northwest edge of Flower Fields. Mario convinces Posie that if he obtains an item that is even shinier than the stone, he can trade it to her. He then sets out for Posie down south. After he talks to Posie, he obtains this item and later trades this Crystal Berry for the Water Stone with [[Rosie]].

==In-game description==
''A glittering berry made of crystal.''

==Trivia==
*If Mario tries multiple times to obtain a Crystal Berry before asking Posie for one (even ignoring her warnings), Posie will kick Mario out of the area requiring him to ask her directly to obtain one.
