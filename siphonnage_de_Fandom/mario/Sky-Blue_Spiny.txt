'''Sky-Blue Spinies''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
They are launched by [[Dark Lakitu]]s, and are a much more powerful and dangerous variant of [[Spiny|Spinies]]. Sky-Blue Spinies can also charge up their attack to twelve making them even more deadly. With a high defense value, a Sky-Blue Spiny can seem a little unbeatable. A Sky-Blue Spiny also can curl up into a [[Sky-Blue Pipe|ball]] to increase its defense to an infinite amount for a while, but it cannot attack while in this state.
