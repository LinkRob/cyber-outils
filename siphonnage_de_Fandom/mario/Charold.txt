'''Charold''' is a character in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''Super Paper Mario'' ==
Charold lives in the [[Underwhere]]. He charges four [[coin]]s to ferry [[Mario]] across the [[River Twygz]]. Despite, Mario able to swim across the River Twygz. Although it is slightly safer to ferry with Charold, because there is no chance for an [[Underhand]] to damage the player.

== Etymology ==
Charold is named after Charon, the boatsman of the River Stix in the Underworld as told by Greek Mythology.
