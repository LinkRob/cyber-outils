The '''Super Suit''' is a powerful armor featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be obtained from the [[Chow]] in [[Monstro Town]] after performing 100 consecutive [[Super Jump]]s. The Super Suit can be equipped by any character, and doing so increases their [[Attack]], [[Defense]], [[Magic Attack]], and [[Magic Defense]] by 50 each and their [[Speed]] by 30. It also protects them from any status ailment and element attack. The Super Suit can be sold for 350 [[coin]]s, but since there is only one Super Suit, it cannot be replaced afterward.
