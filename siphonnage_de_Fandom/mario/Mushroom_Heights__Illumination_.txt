'''Mushroom Heights''' is the location where the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] is established.

== Description ==
Mushroom Heights consists of hills and platforms. Toads' houses and shops are established on hills. [[Princess Peach's Castle]] is established on the highest hill of Mushroom Heights.
