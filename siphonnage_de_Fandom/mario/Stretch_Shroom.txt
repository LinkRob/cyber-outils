'''Stretch Shroom''' is a type of [[Mushroom Platform]] which appears in every game within the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' sub-series]]. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
A Stretch Shroom has a behavior of repeatedly expanding and contracting. Whenever a Stretch Shroom contracts, it pulls anything from its sides to the center. A Stretch Shroom can serve as an obstacle because if [[Mario]] or another playable character jump from one to the next with imprecise timing, they can fall into the pit below. In the game that they appear in, Stretch Shrooms only appear in either one or a few [[level]]s.

While the behavior of a Stretch Shroom remains consistent throughout its appearances, its design differs between its appearance. A Stretch Shroom's cap has varied between blue and pink, depending on the game, and the pattern on the cap also varies.

==In the ''Super Mario'' series==
In ''New Super Mario Bros.'', Stretch Shrooms only appear in [[World 5-A (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 5-A]].

In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', Stretch Shrooms only appear in [[World 4-5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4-5]].
