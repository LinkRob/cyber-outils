'''Peach's Extra Dress''' is a key item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It is a pink dress bearing an identical appearance to [[Princess Peach]]'s main dress.

== History ==
At [[Beanbean Castle]], before continuing their journey to [[Joke's End]], [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] receive Peach's Extra Dress from a concerned [[Toadsworth]], who worried that Peach may have damaged her dress.

[[Mario]] came over to [[Luigi]] who seemed upset about the real [[Beanstar]], which was taken by [[Fawful]] who knew that the other one was a [[Fake Beanstar]]. Mario searched for Peach's Extra Dress inside the [[Suitcase]], and Luigi looked skeptical about that idea. Mario soon caught up to [[Bowletta]] wanting her to free Peach, but she refused and laughed. Mario was furious, but laughed and Bowletta had a feeling there is something wrong. She thought she saw two princesses, the fake princess said that they tricked Bowletta to kidnap the wrong princess and was about to leave them. Instead, Bowletta dropped Princess Peach and chased after the fake princess, and kidnapped the fake princess, who was then taken up to the [[Koopa Cruiser]].

At the Koopa Cruiser, Bowletta and [[Fawful]] were pleased to kidnap the "real" princess and get the Beanstar. However, Bowletta suspected that the fake princess was covering their mouth. Fawful asked the fake princess if they are hiding something, and they denied this. Bowletta fooled the fake princess into thinking that there was a "humongous cockroach" behind them. The fake princess jumped with surprise and looked behind, revealing a moustache. The fake princess paused and hid their face, but it was too late, and Fawful verified to Bowletta that, like her, he had also seen a moustache. Bowletta instructed Fawful to fire at will. The fake princess turned out to be Luigi, who was disguised using the extra dress. Bowletta was shocked and furious that he had tricked them. Fawful tried to attack and chase after Luigi, but Luigi managed to escape and hit a [[! Block]], which caused some crates to fall and block his foes’ way through. Luigi took off the dress and continued his journey to recover the Beanstar and escape the Koopa Cruiser, using the dress as a make-shift parachute - on the way down, the dress-parachute was ruined by a bird, and Luigi ended up falling into the desert.
