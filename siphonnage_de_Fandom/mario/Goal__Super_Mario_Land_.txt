The '''goal''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''.

==Description==
A goal is a tower located at the end of most [[level]]s. A goal has two entrances: one at the base that sends [[Mario]] directly to the next level, and one at the top that allows him to play a [[bonus game (Super Mario Land)|bonus game]] for a [[Superball Flower]] or [[extra life|extra lives]] first. Mario has to enter either of a goal's entrances to complete the level. The lower entrance can always be reached easily, being preceded by a screen-width of flat ground with no obstacles or enemies, although the higher entrance requires more effort to reach.

In ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'', there is a similar yet different type of [[Goal (Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins)|goal]], which replaces the higher entrance with a [[Bonus Bell]].

==In ''Super Mario Land''==
In [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Land)|World 1-1]], reaching the higher entrance involves navigating two [[lift]]s, one vertical and one horizontal.

Various levels involve individual [[dropping lift]]s that fall instantly when Mario touches it, giving only one chance to reach the upper entrance. This is noticeable in [[World 3-2 (Super Mario Land)|World 3-2]], where the single-chance [[jump]]s required extends beyond the goal screen itself.
