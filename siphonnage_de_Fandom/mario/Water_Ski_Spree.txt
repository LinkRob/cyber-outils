'''Water Ski Spree''' is a 4-Player [[coin]]-collecting [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Opening==
A [[Shy Guy]] drives a motorboat through the water, with the first player riding on waterskis behind it. Another Shy Guy throws a [[Spiny Egg]] behind the motorboat, and the player dodges it. The screen then splits into four sections, each with a player riding on waterskis behind a motorboat, and the minigame begins.

==Gameplay==
The objective is to collect as many coins as possible. There are coins on the ramps, and the player must move left and right by turning their [[Wii Remote]]. Sometimes there is a ramp on each side, and there is a coin on only one of them. The Shy Guy piloting the boat sometimes throws Spiny Eggs behind it; hitting these stuns the player and renders them unable to move for a few seconds. The maximum number of coins possible in this minigame is 17.

==Ending==
[[File:Water Ski Spree end 8.png|thumb|The ending to Water Ski Spree]]
The four players are all hooked on to the same boat, all waving their hands in the air while the camera is circling around the players. If a player does not collect any coins, they do a losing pose instead. However, if no one collects coins, the four players stay hooked on their boats while the screen stays split as they do their losing poses.

==Controls==
*'''Wii Remote:''' Steer and veer
*'''Buttons:''' None

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Bonus minigame! Catch Shy Guy's coins as you water ski."''
* '''Hint''' &ndash; ''"Go up the ramps whenever you can to grab more coins!"''

==Trivia==
*The rules and hint of the minigame imply the Shy Guy throws coins. However, coins are simply found on ramps.
