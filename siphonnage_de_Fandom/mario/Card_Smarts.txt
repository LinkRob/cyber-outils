{{Nihongo|'''Card Smarts'''|かけひきカードバトル|Kakehiki kādo batoru|Strategy Card Battle}} is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera pans across the table. The camera changes scene to face the player whose cards show onscreen.

==Gameplay==
In this game, each player is given a set of eight cards: one '1', two '2' cards, two '3' cards, two '4' cards and one '5' card. Players must choose a card within five seconds. The player with the highest number card receives one win in the round. The first player to get three wins is the winner. The game ends when the three player gains three wins, all of the remaining players run out of cards or any players earn three points after the first player scored three points. The remaining players that have not completed their three wins, will be determined a place depending on the number of wins.
