'''Sunshine Road''' is a location in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
Sunshine Road takes place on a wooden path, and on both the left and right sides are stands where an [[audience]] spectates the player's character attempt to conquer the mode. The only obstacle of the course are the six rotating tire racks placed along the path, each mounted with eight tires. If the player's character touches a tire rack, they are temporarily flattened and lose some of their coins. Like [[Peach's Castle (Dribble Race course)|Peach's Castle]], the [[? Panel]]s in Sunshine Road either move back and forth slowly in a vertical or horizontal direction or not at all.

==In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''==
Sunshine Road is the second course of Dribble Race. It has a time of 55 seconds to beat. If the player clears the default record of both Sunshine Road's and [[Peach's Castle (Dribble Race course)|Peach's Castle]]'s, they unlock the [[Rainbow Road (Dribble Race course)|Rainbow Road]] course.
