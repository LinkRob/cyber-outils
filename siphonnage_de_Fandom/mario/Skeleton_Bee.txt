{{nihongo|'''Skeleton Bee'''|ガイコツバチ|Gaikotsu Bachi}} is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''. It is an undead skeleton bee that attacks in a similar way of [[Bībī]]. However it is much tougher than it's non skeletal counterpart because when Mario jumps on it, it revives after a few seconds, similar to a [[Dry Bones]]. A Skeleton Bee can only be permanently defeated by Mario if he either does a [[Spin Jump]] on it or touches it while under the effects of a [[Starman]].
