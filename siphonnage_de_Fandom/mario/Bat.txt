'''Bats''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. They are one of few non-ghost enemies as well as the most common, given that they appear in every mansion.

==Behavior==
Bats stay in their place until they spot Luigi. Once a Bat spots Luigi, it will swoop down quickly at him and bite him for 5 HP of damage. The best way for Luigi to deal with Bats is to use the [[Strobulb]]. Bats always appear in groups and Luigi can only vacuum up one of the Bats at a time leaving him open to another Bat attack. There also also Gold Bats that fly more quickly and can be strobed for a Gold Bar.
