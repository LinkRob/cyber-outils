A '''Stumpet''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are large, stump-like enemies only encountered in the [[Barrel Volcano]]. Stumpets are very rare and very powerful enemies to be encountered in battle.

==Battle==
In battle, Stumpets mainly use powerful special moves such as [[Va Va Voom]] and [[Backfire]] that can deal a lot of damage to the party. However, Stumpets must always recharge when using these moves.

===Strategy===
Stumpets are weak to [[Ice (element)|ice]], so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] is effective on them. It is also recommended to use [[Ice Bomb]]s on a Stumpet.
