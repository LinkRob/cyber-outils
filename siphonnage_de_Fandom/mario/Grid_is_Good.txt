'''Grid is Good''', titled '''Points-Pile Up''' in Europe and Australia, is a General minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

== Opening ==
The game demonstrates it by choosing it from a box and putting it on the grid. The player's grid as shown being highlighted and the other player's grid taking one from the other grid. The players nod that they understood the game and the game begins.

== Gameplay ==
The player has to choose a shape from the box and drags it to the grid and press 'OK' if it is where they want to place it, unless they change their mind. After all players choose their shape on the grid, it reveals the grid placement from other players. They sulk if their grid is taken out. The players are awarded the points left. After the three rounds, the player with the most points wins.

== Controls ==
* {{Button|3ds|stylus}} - Move shape/claim 'OK'

== Etymology ==
Grid is Good is a pun of "greed is good".
