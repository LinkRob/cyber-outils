'''World 6''' is the sixth world that appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. Its ruler is the cyan [[Sprixie Princesses|Sprixie Princess]]. It succeeds [[World 5 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 5]] and precedes [[World 7 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 7]].





==Layout==

World 6 is mainly a cloud-based world.
