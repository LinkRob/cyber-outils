The '''Palace Key''' is an item which appears in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==In ''[[Paper Mario]]''==
[[File:Palace Key PM sprite.png|frame|left]]
The Palace Key can be found in the [[Crystal Palace]], where it is used to open locked doors. Palace Keys cannot open the red and blue doors in the initial room of the Crystal Palace, as they are instead opened with the [[Red Key]] and [[Blue Key]] respectively. The Palace Key is blue in color with a red dot in the middle.

==In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''==
The Palace Key, now both larger and silver, is used for the [[Palace of Shadow]] in this game. Different Palace Keys can unlock different doors in this place (except for those at [[Riddle Tower]] which has their own set of keys).
