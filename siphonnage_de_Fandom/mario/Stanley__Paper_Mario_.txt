'''Stanley''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Stanley is a [[Boo]] that lives in [[Gusty Gulch]] and is one of the Boos that greet [[Mario]] and [[Lady Bow]] when they first enter. Stanley warns them that [[Tubba Blubba]] has been eating Boos including one named [[Herbert]]. However, Tubba Blubba himself soon appears and Stanley is eaten after warning Lady Bow to [[Outta Sight|hide]] Mario. Stanley is later freed after Tubba Blubba has been defeated and can be talked to after returning to Gusty Gulch.
