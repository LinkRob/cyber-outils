'''Ty-Foos''' are a variant of [[Foo]] and an enemy which first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Behavior==
They blow air out of their mouths, which will blow the characters off the path.

== Game appearances ==

=== In ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' ===
Ty-Foos are found in [[Shiveria]]. They can be captured and can blow objects around. They can also float over poison and icy water. In this game, Ty-Foos wear Santa Hats.

=== In ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' ===
They appear in the arctic region as obstacles that can blow you off the track. 

=== In ''[[Mario Tennis Fever]]'' ===
They have their own racket, the Ty-Foo Racket, which blows opponents off the court.
