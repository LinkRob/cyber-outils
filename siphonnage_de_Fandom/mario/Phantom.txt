The '''Phantom''' is a large [[bike]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

==Description==
The Phantom resembles a {{w|Honda Gold Wing}} with a stylized boot and fin attachments.

The Phantom is unlocked by winning the [[Special Cup]] on [[Mirror Mode]], or by playing 4,500 races.

Just like the [[Zip Zip]], the Phantom does not have a kart counterpart.

==Statistics==
The Phantom has the best [[Off-Road]] among all large vehicles in the game, tying with the [[Dolphin Dasher]] and [[Bit Bike]], but it also has the worst [[drift]]. It also features good [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]] and [[handling]], as well as exactly average [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]]. Its [[speed]] and [[acceleration]] are above average.
