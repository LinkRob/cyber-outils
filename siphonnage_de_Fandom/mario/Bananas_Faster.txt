'''Bananas Faster''' is a [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
Donkey Kong jumps onto a platform in the middle, where he eats a [[banana]] before throwing its [[Banana Peel|peel]] onto the wheel, which starts spinning.

==Gameplay==
There is a rapidly moving roulette-style wheel consisting of bananas, [[Banana Bunch]]es, and banana peels. The characters have to [[jump]] onto the wheel to collect bananas, each worth a coin, similarly to Donkey Kong minigames. However, a character jumps onto a banana peel, they slip and fall, becoming stunned temporarily. The minigame ends when either every banana is collected or when the 15-second [[time limit]] runs out.

==Ending==
The characters are on the middle platform with Donkey Kong. The characters who obtained at least one banana perform their respective victory animation, but characters who did not collect any bananas perform their respective losing animation.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Jump

==Game data==
* '''Rules''': ''Hop on the roulette wheel to collect bananas! You can jump as many times as you want before time runs out.''
* '''Advice''': ''Get the bunches of bananas, but watch for the peels! If you hit one of those, you'll slip and fall.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's title is a pun on "{{w|Bananas Foster}}," a type of dessert mainly created from ice cream and bananas.
