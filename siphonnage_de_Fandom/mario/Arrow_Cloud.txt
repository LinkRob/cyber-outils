'''Arrow Clouds''' are a type of object featured exclusively within the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Description==
Throughout its appearances, an Arrow Cloud has had a consistent purpose of firing a [[Yoshi Egg]] in the direction its arrow is pointing. Some Arrow Clouds rotate, requiring precise timing before the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can shoot an egg at it. Arrow Clouds are often used to obtain an item, such as a [[Smiley Flower]]. They are sometimes found in groups, so if a Yoshi Egg is launched from one Arrow Cloud to another pointed in a different direction, the trajectory of the Yoshi Egg is changed accordingly.
