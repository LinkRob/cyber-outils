A '''Spiky Shell''', named '''Bowser's Shell''' in PAL releases, is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
A Spiky Shell is a Skill Item that can raise the Skill levels of certain teammates, including all [[Magikoopa]] variations and evolved forms, [[Boom Boom]], the [[Koopalings]] and their evolved forms, [[Bowser Jr.]], all three forms of [[Bowser]], and [[Rock Luigi]]. A Spiky Shell can only raise a Skill level up to level 4, but it has an upgraded variation, [[Grand Spiky Shell]], that can further increase any of the aforementioned teammates' Skill level.

A Spiky Shell can be obtained either from opening a [[? Block]] at random or as a reward from the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] minigame.
