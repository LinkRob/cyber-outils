'''Super Mario!''' is [[Mario]]'s [[Super Ability]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''. He can make himself giant and stomp on others. It can also boost his shooting with the ball. Despite its name, Super Mario! is essentially the same as the [[Mega Mario]] form rather than the [[Super Mario (form)|Super]] form.
