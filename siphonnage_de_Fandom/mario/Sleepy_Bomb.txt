A '''Sleepy Bomb''' is an type of [[bomb]] and an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Using a Sleepy Bomb puts all enemies onscreen to [[Sleep]]. However, it does not work on bosses or larger enemies (or those immune to status effects such as [[Sleep]]). Sleepy Bombs can be purchased at the shop in [[Tadpole Pond]] for one [[Frog Coin]], but certain enemies sometimes drop a Sleepy Bomb upon defeat as well.
