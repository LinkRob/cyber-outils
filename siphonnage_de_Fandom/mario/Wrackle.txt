}}'''Wrackle''' is a white version of a [[Frackle]] that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Behavior ===
Its purpose is the same: [[Mario]] can use Thoreau to toss it at [[Wracktail]]'s antennae. The only differences between [[Frackle]]s and Wrackles are that the appear on Wracktail, live at the bottom of the Pit of 100 Trials, and are white.
