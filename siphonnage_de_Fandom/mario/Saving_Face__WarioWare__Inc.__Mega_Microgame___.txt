'''Saving Face''' is a [[microgame]] featured under [[Wario]]'s [[Intro Games]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''. While it is primarily classified as being one of the intro games, Saving Face can also appear in three other stages: [[Sci-Fi]], [[Nature]], and [[Anything Goes]].

In ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', there is a similar microgame titled [[Up for Grabs (WarioWare: Touched!)|Up for Grabs]], which is similar except it involves using the [[Stylus]] to catch the falling stick.

==Layout==
The scene depicts a black background. At the top of the screen is a hand holding a purple stick, and one of [[Wario]]'s hands is directly below.

==Gameplay==
The hand at the top drops the stick below, and the player has to press {{button|gba|A}} with correct timing for Wario's hand to catch the stick. He has only one chance to grab it. If done correctly, the player wins at the microgame, but if Wario's hand does not grab the stick, they lose. If Wario's hand grabs too early, the stick bounces off the top of his hand, and if he either grabs too late or not at all, the stick falls right through.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The stick is long and easy to catch.
*'''Level 2:''' The stick is slightly smaller and somewhat harder to catch.
*'''Level 3:''' The stick is even smaller, roughly about the size of Wario's hand, and is even harder to catch.
