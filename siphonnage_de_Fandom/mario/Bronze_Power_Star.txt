A '''Bronze Power Star''' is a type of [[Power Star]] that appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. These stars serve as a replacement for regular Power Stars, and are only obtained when [[Mario]] uses the [[Cosmic Spirit]]'s [[Super Guide]] service, the [[Cosmic Guide]].

== Description ==
Hence the name, the Bronze Power Star is a bronze-color. If [[Mario]] dies eight times on an incomplete mission, the [[Cosmic Spirit]] will show up, if [[Mario]] talks to her, she will offer to lead him to the [[Power Star]], if Mario is to accept her help, she will take control of him and will show players how to complete the level, the Cosmic Spirit will not go after every collectible including [[Comet Medal]]s. When the Cosmic Spirit reaches the Power Star, she will tell Mario that he will get a Bronze Star and if he wants an actual Power Star, he must complete the mission himself, Mario will be able to get the Bronze Power Star himself and it will be added to his total Star count but this will prevent Mario from unlocking [[Green Power Star]]s, even after he has collected all 120 stars. To remedy this, he must complete the levels he collected a Bronze Power Star from without the Cosmic Guide and attain the Power Star normally.
