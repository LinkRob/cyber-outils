'''Mice''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical descriotion===
Mice are small, gray rodents with a tail.

===Behavior===
Mice appear from mouse holes. and they tend to run from [[Luigi]]. Mice do not actively attempt to attack Luigi, but touching one can inflict minor damage onto Luigi, taking away 5 [[Heart Point|HP]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
Mice appear in every mansion except the [[Treacherous Mansion]]. The Mice can be easily vacuumed up into the [[Poltergust 5000]] or defeated with the [[Strobulb]], the latter usually causing it to drop a [[coin]].

There are also Gold Mice that appear in certain areas, and they must be strobed in order to obtain the [[Gold Bar]] from them.
