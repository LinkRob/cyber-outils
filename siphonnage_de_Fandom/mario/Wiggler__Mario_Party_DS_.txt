'''Wiggler''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''. He is a [[Wiggler]] who owns, cares for, and needs help in saving a [[Wiggler's Garden|garden]].

== Description ==
The Wiggler is a friendly Wiggler who looks no different to the other [[Wiggler|Wigglers]]. His body is yellow and segmented, with orange spots. His Figurine description paints him as loving the garden and all of the plants in there, with the exception of the Piranha Plant boss that tore it up.

== History ==
The Wiggler makes his debut in the intro cutscene to the [[Wiggler's Garden]] board, where [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and company bump into him on their way to [[Bowser Castle]]. The Wiggler tells them that his garden is being destroyed by a [[Piranha Plant]]. After Mario and company defeat the Piranha Plant, the Wiggler gives the gang a [[Sky Crystal]], claiming he found it after it fell from the sky.

== Figurine ==
The Wiggler has a figurine that can be obtained by winning 18000 Mario Party Points. His figurine is number 17, and has the following description: ''"Wiggler loves the garden and all the wonderful greenery that lives there. Except for that Piranha Plant, which makes Wiggler stomping mad!"''

== Trivia ==
* The Wiggler was not going to be in the game, with [[Petey Piranha]] being the original host of the board, which would have been called "Petey Piranha's Greenhouse." Additionally, the Piranha Plant tearing up the garden would have been a "traitor."
