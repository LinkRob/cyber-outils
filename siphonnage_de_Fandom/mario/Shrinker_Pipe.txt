'''Shrinker Pipes''' are a type of [[Warp Pipe]] found in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' in [[Tiny-Huge Island]]. When entered, instead of transporting our heroes to a different location, they instead change their size making them come out either bigger or smaller (depending on which Island size they were on). This also affects the enemies on the Island (such as the [[Micro-Goomba]]s and [[Grand Goomba]]s as well as the [[Venus Fire Trap]]s and [[Big Venus Fire Trap]]s). 

Entering a Shrinker Pipe also causes events to reset (such as certain things respawning and the [[Koopa the Quick]] race resetting allowing you to redo the race if you lost). Some enemies are also affected differently than others (such as one Micro-Goomba becoming a [[Chuck-Ya]]).
