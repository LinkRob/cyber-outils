==Appearance==
The Trading Event Toad is a pink [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with white spots on his head.

==History==
The Trading Event Toad sponsors contests on the radio station. In order to participate in his contests, [[Mario]] must head into the third house of [[Koopa Village]] and switch to the Information Station on the [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]]'s radio. The Trading Event Toad will sometimes talk on there and request that someone brings him a certain type of item within a time limit. If Mario manages to make it to him in time, the Trading Event Toad will give him a better item in return. The Trading Event Toad then disappears in a cloud of smoke whenever an item has been delivered to him.
