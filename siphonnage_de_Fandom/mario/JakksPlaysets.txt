The '''[[Jakks Pacific]] Playsets''' debuted in 2014 and are sold at a variety of different stores: Walmart, Target, Macy's, Kohl's and GameStop. Product releases include a scene with figures. Many of the sets are based on New Super Mario Bros. U.
