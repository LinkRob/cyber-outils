The '''Gator Train''' is a boss encountered in the world [[Rumble Jungle]] of [[Yoshi's Crafted World]]. It is fought in the level Gator Train Attacks!, where it starts out as a standard train, only to be enchanted by [[Kamek]] as the behest of [[Bowser]], gaining a gator head and boxes with [[Shy Guy]] emblems.

== Description ==
Before it is enchanted, the Gator Train simply appears as an ordinary train. In its boss form, the Gator Train gains an alligator/crocodile head with a pink upper jaw and a yellow lower jaw and yellow eyes with dark green pupils, and behind it are pink box carriages with [[Shy Guy]] mask symbols.

== Battle ==
The battle takes place on a second train that [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and his friends ride. The Gator Train deploys [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]] that toss [[Spiked Ball|Spiked Balls]] and [[Train Heyho|Train Heyhos]] onto Yoshi's train. It then slows down to align its head with Yoshi and attack with a lunging bite, leaving its weak spot (the engine in its "nose") exposed, at which point Yoshi can throw his eggs at the engine to deal damage.

After enough damage, the Gator Train's cars derail, but it rides by, releasing two Shy Guys, before proceeding to derail Yoshi's train. Yoshi must jump onto another train that is coming up.

On the second cycle, the Gator Train deploys ruler walls carried by propellers; these fly to one side of the screen to shove Yoshi off the train. Yoshi must avoid them to avoid getting knocked onto the tracks. After this, it will bite at Yoshi's train again, and Yoshi must attack its engine to deal more damage, prompting it to enter its enraged phase once more to derail Yoshi's train. The third cycle has the Gator Train shoot out colored pencil missiles that fly in from above (in a manner not unlike the [[Colored Pencils]] boss in [[Paper Mario: The Origami King|''Paper Mario: The Origami King'']]) and then roll in from the sides, followed by the Gator Train aligning itself with Yoshi, allowing it to bite him. Yoshi must throw some more of his eggs at the engine block, after which the Gator Train is destroyed, revealing the magenta Dream Gem for the [[Sundream Stone]].
