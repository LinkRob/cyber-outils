The '''Bwah Blaster''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Bwah Blaster.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Bwah Blaster resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has low cover damage and the user can attack with a medium range. The Bwah Blaster deals around 25 to 35 damage. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Bounce (status effect)|Bounce]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Bwah Blaster as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it when the player completes the Red Coin Challenge near the cannon to [[Ancient Gardens]].
