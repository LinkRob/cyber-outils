'''Monstermama''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
Monstermama is an elderly [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with brown spots on her head. She also wears a brown robe.

==History==
Monstermama lives in [[Monstro Town]] where she serves as the leader of the small town. Monstermama is very kind to all of the reformed monsters that live there and makes sure they don't go back to their malicious ways.  Monstermama also offers assistance to [[Mario]] and the others by summoning [[Sergeant Flutter]] to help the heroes get to [[Bean Valley]] from [[Land's End]].
