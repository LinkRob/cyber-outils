'''Yellow Toad''', also known as just '''Toad''' in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]] Deluxe'', is a character who appears in [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' series]]. He is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who joins the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] along with [[Blue Toad]] to rescue [[Princess Peach]].

== Profile ==

=== Physical description ===
Yellow Toad has the appearance of a generic colored Yellow Toad with yellow spots on his mushroom cap, as his name would suggest. Despite this, he and other generic Toads are still labeled as "[[Toad (species)|Toad]]" in various Mario games.

=== Personality ===
Unlike the other [[Toad (species)|Toads]] in the game (with the exception being [[Blue Toad]]), Yellow Toad is shown to be an extremely noble character as seen in the intro of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' where he and Blue Toad ran right after the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] to help them on their quest to save [[Princess Peach]]. Yellow Toad is also kind and compassionate when he and Blue Toad happily invite [[Luigi]] to ride in their hot air balloon after he accidently had been left abandoned by [[Mario]] and Peach. He even went on to fighting the several bosses in the game including [[Bowser]] himself at the end (something that most Toads would rarely do considering their fear over Bowser).

Unlike Blue Toad, Yellow Toad is shown to be the more bravery and courageous of the two as shown in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'', where he immediately jumps in front of Princess Peach to try and protect her from Bowser.

==Super Mario series ==
===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
Yellow Toad first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. He attends [[Princess Peach]]'s birthday party at [[Princess Peach's Castle|Peach's Castle]] along with [[Mario]], [[Luigi]] and [[Blue Toad]]. The party is later interrupted by the [[Koopalings]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] disguised as a large birthday cake in order to kidnap the princess. Yellow Toad along with Mario, Luigi and Blue Toad ran to rescue her. Together they travel through eight different [[World|worlds]], stopping [[Bowser]] and [[Koopa Troop|his minions]] and saving Peach. After Bowser is defeated and the princess is saved, the two Toads go on a hot air balloon, they also invite Luigi to join their balloon after his balloon was left with Mario and Peach onboard.

Yellow Toad can be a playable character in multiplayer and appears in the ending cutscene and the credits.

===''New Super Mario Bros. U/New Super Luigi U/New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[File:Yellow Toad NSMBU Deluxe.png|thumb|x200px|left|Yellow Toad artwork, from ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.]]
Yellow Toad appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. In these games he acts the same. In the opening cutscene, the Toads are seen with the Mario Bros. and Princess Peach attending a lunch at Peach's Castle. However, their peaceful together is soon interrupted by the sudden appearance of multiple [[Airship|airships]] consisting of Bowser and the Koopalings. Bowser use a giant mechanical arm, he pulls away Peach's chair from harm and instead gave a mighty punch at the heroes, causing them to be knocked out. 

Bowser used the mechanical arm to scoop up Yellow Toad, and his friends and threw the heroes far across the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. Upon crashing into the [[Acorn Tree]] consisting of [[Super Acorn|Super Acorns]] and falling back to the ground, the four heroes soon get up and see Peach's Castle getting invaded by Bowser's forces in the far distance. Deciding to take action, Yellow Toad and his friends run across the plains in the start of a new adventure. 

==='''''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'''''===
Yellow Toad appears as playable character with a new style and a higher-pitched voice.

==Other appearances==

==='''''Mario Sports Superstars'''''===
Yellow Toad appears in the form of two different collectible cards, [[Penguin Suit|Yellow Penguin Toad]] and [[Super Acorn|Yellow Flying Squirrel Toad]].

==='''Nintendo 3DS'''===
Yellow Toad and the other ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' heroes were shown as some of the featured characters in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] tech demo. He was shown wearing the [[Propeller Mushroom|Propeller Suit]] in the demo as like in the original game's box art. The 3DS tech demo screen of ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' (featuring Yellow Toad and his friends) is later an obtainable puzzle in the Puzzle Swap mode of StreetPass Mii Plaza.

==='''''New Super Mario Bros. Mii'''''===
Yellow Toad, along with Mario, Luigi, Blue Toad and [[Mii]]s, are also seen as playable characters in the [[Wii U]] tech demo ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Mii]]''.

==='''''Taiko no Tatsujin Wii: Minna de Party Sandaime'''''===
Yellow Toad alongside Blue Toad, Mario, Luigi, the Koopalings, and various other characters from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' made cameo appearances in the Wii game ''[[Taiko no Tatsujin Wii: Minna de Party Sandaime]]'' where the characters can be seen dancing to the various tracks featured in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.

==='''''Super Mario 3D World'''''===
While Yellow Toad is absent from ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', when a [[Blue Toad]] grabs a Fire Flower in that game, he uses the same color-scheme as [[Fire Flower|Fire Yellow Toad]].

==='''''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'''''===
Yellow Toad appears as cameo on two Battle Cards in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==='''''Luigi's Mansion 3'''''===
Yellow Toad appears alongside [[Blue Toad]] and the main [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', it’s unknown if the pair of [[Toad (species)|Toads]] seen throughout the game is actually him.

==='''''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'''''===
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', there is a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]] of Yellow Toad's Flying Squirrel form.

=== ''Mario Tennis Fever'' ===
Yellow Toad appears alongside [[Blue Toad]] appear as support characters. In Adventure mode, he and Blue Toad help [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] on their quest to the island.

==In other media==
===''Super Mario-kun''===
Propeller Yellow Toad on the cover of the forty first issue of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''.

Yellow Toad along with Blue Toad are featured in the forty first and forty second issues of ''Super Mario-kun''. As the story is based on ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', the two Toads are introduced as Mario's new accomplices. Throughout the adaption of the two issues, Yellow Toad plays an important role by journeying alongside Mario, Luigi, and Blue Toad on their quest to rescue Princess Peach from Bowser and his forces.

Both Yellow Toad and Blue Toad are seen in their propeller forms on the cover of issue 41.

=== ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' film series ===
In ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', a [[Yellow Toad and Blue Toad (Illumination)|yellow and blue toad guard]] stops [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] from entering [[Princess Peach's Castle (Illumination)|Princess Peach's Castle]]. Due to their coloration, occupation, and having spotlight than other Toad guards, it is possible that them together are considered references to Yellow Toad and Blue Toad respectively.

== Trivia ==
*It is unknown if Yellow Toad was meant to be playable in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' alongside [[Blue Toad]], as Nintendo stated [[Princess Peach|Peach]] was originally not intended as a playable character. However, Blue Toad’s Fire form in the game references Yellow Toad , suggesting a possible connection.
*Unlike Blue Toad, Yellow Toad does not appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' as a playable character.
*Yellow Toad is labeld as [[Toad (species)|Toad]] in the [[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe|New Super Mario Bros U Deluxe]] roster naming.
*Despite his debut, [[Toad (species)|Yellow Toad]] has appeared in other previous installments. Though, they're different and don't have the same experience as main Yellow Toad.
*There is an unnamed [[Yellow Toad (Toad Brigade)|Yellow Toad]] who is a member for the [[Toad Brigade]] that resembles closely identical to the main Yellow Toad, but he is a separate character.
