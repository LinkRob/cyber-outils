'''Amanitas''' are a type of enemy featured in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are strange, mushroom-like creatures that mainly appear in the [[Forest Maze]].

==In battle==
Outside of battle, Amanitas disguised themselves as regular mushrooms that spring alive once [[Mario]] gets close to them. In battle, Amanitas can use a troublesome move called [[Sporocyst]] that can turn one of Mario's party members into a [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]].

===Strategy===
Amanitas weak point is [[Thunder (element)|thunder]] so [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]] comes in handy for wiping them out. Wearing a [[Trueform Pin]] will prevent any mushroom transformations.
