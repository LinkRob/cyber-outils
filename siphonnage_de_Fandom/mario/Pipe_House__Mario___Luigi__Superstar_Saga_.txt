The '''Pipe House''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
The Pipe House is where the [[pipe man]] lives. It is located in a house west of [[Beanbean Castle]]. The Pipe House has three red [[Warp Pipe]]s and a green Warp Pipe. The green pipe takes [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to any previously visited location; the first two red pipes have a purposeless role of warping the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] between them, and the third red one houses the pipeman.
