The '''Standard ATV''' ('''Standard Quad''' in European languages) is a type of [[ATV]] featured in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is the ATV equivalent of both the [[Standard Kart]] and the [[Standard Bike]].
