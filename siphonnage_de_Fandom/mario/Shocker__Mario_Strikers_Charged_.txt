The '''Shocker''' is [[Dry Bones]]'s [[Skillshot]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Upon initiating Shocker, Dry Bones kicks the ball with electricity, which temporarily electrocutes the [[Kritter|Goalie]] and knocks the ball back to Dry Bones. While the Goalie is still shocked, Dry Bones has a chance to headbutt the ball into the goal. The effect of the move is very similar to [[Koopa Troopa]]'s [[Shell Smash (move)|Shell Smash]], except the ball always returns to Dry Bones.

Another character can block Shocker if they stand directly in front of Dry Bones, but just like with the Goalie, this temporarily electrocutes them. An opponent can counter Dry Bones' use of Shocker by either tackling or otherwise stunning Dry Bones while the ball rebounds toward it.
