'''Ludwig von Koopa (Dark)''' is the dark clone of [[Ludwig von Koopa]] appearing in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'', and later returning in ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

== Profiles ==
=== Physical description ===
Ludwig (Dark) is the exact copy of the real Ludwig, this sprite has a darker tone, with yellow eyes.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Ludwig (Dark) can use [[Chain Shot]], [[Lightning (move)|Lightning]], [[Chance]]/[[Mystery Box]], [[Thwomp Thump]], [[Rally]] and [[Stop and Drop]].
