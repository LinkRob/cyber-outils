'''Cooligans''' are penguin enemies which first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
They are similar looking to a penguin wearing black sunglasses. They are originally located in icy areas.

== In the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] ==
=== ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' ===
Cooligans appear in various levels in [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3]]. They would continuously slide on the ice, bouncing off obstacles. It takes two jumps to defeat a Cooligan. If [[Ice Mario]] uses an [[Ice Ball]] to freeze Cooligan into an [[Ice Block]], he stand on it, although the frozen Cooligan breaks upon colliding with a solid object, defeating the Cooligan in the process.

=== ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''/''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' ===
Cooligans act the same as in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''. However, Cooligans occasionally block the path on the world map. If Mario or another playable character bumps into a Cooligan here, they have to defeat the Cooligans in an [[Enemy Course]], and in doing so, they are rewarded a [[Rainbow Star]].

== In ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' ==
Cooligans appear in the [[Snow Rise]] and [[Ice Flow]] levels of [[World 4 (Paper Mario: Sticker Star)|World 4]]. In Snow Rise, Cooligans are seen sliding down a snow-covered slope. In Ice Flow, Cooligans are seen waddling down the ice path before sliding; this is the first instance in which Cooligans are shown walking. Mario can not face Cooligans in battle, but he takes two [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage if he touches one.

== In ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' ==
[[File:Mob Sleds.png|thumb|Cooligan snowmobiles in Mob Sleds in ''Mario Party 9'']]
Snowmobiles resembling Cooligans in a minigame called [[Mob Sleds]]. When defeating the player on the ice, it is a game over for them.
