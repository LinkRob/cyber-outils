|name=Iron Jump x3 Battle Card}}'''Iron Jump x3''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Iron Jump x3 is the iron version of [[Mario]]'s "Jump x3" attack. It is three of the [[Iron Jump (Battle Card)|Iron Jump]]'s scattered on the edges of the card, accompanied by a blue/white checkerboard background.
