The '''Egyptian Koopa''' is a boss that appears in ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''. He is encountered inside of the [[pyramid]] in the [[Shifting Sands Stage]]. The Egyptian Koopa has a yellow and blue-green Egyptian headress with a snake on it and a white long-sleeved gown and two yellow and blue-green canes in his hands, and his eyes are more akin to a [[Blooper]].

The Egyptian Koopa does not appear immediately in his room, but when Mario enters the room to confront him at the end of his journey through the Shifting Sands Stage, a statue in the Egyptian Koopa's likeness appears instead. When Mario presses the floor panel, the Egyptian Koopa performs a swap-teleportation with the statue, commencing his battle.

The Egyptian Koopa's attacks involves the use of magic pillars that he can shrink Mario with via a magic bullet, most likely resulting in him falling out of the stage. He is defeated by having [[Mario]] run into the pillars. This causes Mario to grow into mega form, allowing him to bash the Koopa. This process must be done three times to beat him; 10 thousand points are awarded with the first two hits, and the last hit grants 500 thousand. Upon defeat, the boss shrinks away, leaving behind his headdress and canes, which disappear as well, leaving behind four yellow coins, four blue coins, and the third [[Star Key (Mario Pinball Land)|Star Key]].

== Other Appearances ==
There is a [[Microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' that is [[Mario Pinball Land (microgame)|based on ''Mario Pinball Land'']]. The Egyptian Koopa is featured in the second level, and must be hit three times. However, he does not attack, as if he shrinks Mario, that could [[Softlock|render the microgame unwinnable]], but he is harder to hit than [[Petey Piranha]] as unlike Petey, the Egyptian Koopa can move around. If Mario falls out of the level, the player also gets softlocked until the timer runs out.

== Nomenclature ==
* In Japanese, his name is ボスノコノコ, which translates to ''Bosu Nokonoko'', meaning "Boss Koopa Troopa". The second Japanese name is ファラオノコノコ, which translates to ''Farao Nokonoko'', meaning "Pharaoh Koopa Troopa".
