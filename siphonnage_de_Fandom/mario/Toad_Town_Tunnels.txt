|games = ''[[Paper Mario]]''}}'''Toad Town Tunnels''' is a location that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is a large, sewer system that can be accessed by taking the Warp Pipe near the [[Bub-ulb]] south of [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]].

==History==
Toad Town Tunnels isn't necessary to visit that much for the storyline (except toward the end of the game) but does play a big role in reaching areas quick to make doing [[Koopa Koot]]'s favors much easier. In one room, there are three blue Warp Pipes where Mario must fight a type of [[Blooper]] in order to open. The blue Warp Pipes in the highest room leads to [[Goomba Village]], [[Koopa Village]], and [[Dry Dry Outpost]]. There are other blue Warp Pipes that leads to [[Boo's Mansion]] and [[Lavalava Island]], but [[Sushie]] must be present since her ability is needed to cross the water.

There are also several [[Super Block (object)|Super Blocks]] that Mario can use to help upgrade his partners. Also, when [[Kent C. Koopa]] is blocking the path. Mario can actually take one of the blue Warp Pipes to go directly to Koopa Village to avoid paying Kent C. Koopa or fighting him altogether. However, Mario will miss out on [[Star Point]]s if he does this. It is also assumed that [[Kolorado]] took the blue Warp Pipe to get past Kent C. Koopa. [[Chet Rippo]] can also be found living down here and Mario can use [[Bombette]]'s ability to get into his secret room. This will also unlock the blue house close by Trace T.'s house so that Mario can access Chet Rippo at any time.

Mario must then actually go through Toad Town Tunnels to do a few things: get the [[Ultra Boots]] and access the Warp Pipe that leads to the [[Shiver Region]]. Mario can get to the Ultra Boots by using [[Lakilester|Lakilester's]] ability to get to a Warp Pipe that is on the other side of a bed of [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]]. Several enemies, such as [[Gloomba]]s and [[Dark Koopa]]s, inhabit these tunnels and was stated by [[Goombario]] as they were [[Goomba]]s and [[Koopa Troopas|Koopa]]s that have adapted to the underground environment.

==Trivia==
*Toad Town Tunnels has another version of it in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' called [[Rogueport Sewers]].
**Coincidentally, Toad Town Tunnels is also known as '''Toad Town Sewers'''.
