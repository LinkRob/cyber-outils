'''Neville, the Bookish Father''' is the first [[portrait ghost]] that [[Luigi]] encounters in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|remake]]. He is the husband of [[Lydia]] and father of [[Chauncey]] and the twins [[Henry and Orville]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Neville is tall and lean with light blue skin. He has an oval-shaped head, yellow-green eyes, red hair and eyebrows, and a red pointed mustache. He wears a deep blue robe.

===Personality===
Neville loves reading books. The book that he reads is titled "Mario Story", which is the Japanese name of the first ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
In ''Luigi's Mansion'', Neville is the first [[portrait ghost]] in Area 1 of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|Mansion]]. Luigi finds him in the [[Study (Luigi's Mansion)|Study]], where he is reading a book while sitting in a rocking chair. His only attack is sending a single levitating book after him. Like most portrait ghosts, Neville cannot be stunned by the [[flashlight]] normally. However, he occasionally yawns and stretches, at which point he becomes vulnerable to light and can be stunned to be re-captured by the [[Poltergust 3000]]. He has 100 [[HP]]. Upon defeat, a [[Treasure Chest]] appears in the Study, containing the [[key]] to the [[Master Bedroom (Luigi's Mansion)|Master Bedroom]].

The player can also have Luigi read [[Neville's Big Baby Care Diary]] in the bookshelf behind Neville's rocking chair. The diary provides hints on how to win Henry and Orville's game of hide and seek, which is by using elemental power on them while they hide in a [[box]].

Neville's portrait number in Professor E. Gadd's gallery is 1, given that he is the first portrait ghost encountered by Luigi.

In the E3 2001 prototype, Neville has only 50 HP and no yawning sound effect.
