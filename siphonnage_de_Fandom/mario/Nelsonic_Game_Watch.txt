'''Nelsonic Game Watch''' is a line of multipurpose wristwatches produced by Nelsonic Industries. They feature an LCD display. Among the line of Nelsonic Game Watches are a few ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, the first being ''[[Super Mario Bros. (Nelsonic Game Watch)|Super Mario Bros.]]'', and lastly a ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' game.

==''Mario''-related games==
*''[[Super Mario Bros. (Nelsonic Game Watch)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' (1989)
*''[[Super Mario Bros. 3 (Nelsonic Game Watch)|Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' (1990)
*''[[Super Mario World (Nelsonic Game Watch)|Super Mario World]]'' (1991)
*''[[Donkey Kong (Nelsonic Game Watch)|Donkey Kong]]'' (1994)
