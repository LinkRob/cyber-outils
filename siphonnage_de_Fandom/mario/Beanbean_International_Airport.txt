*[[Toadsworth]]|final state=Intact}}'''Beanbean International Airport''' is a location that appears in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Super Mario]]'' series. It is where [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] meet [[Princess Peach]] as she enters the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] for the first time.

==History==
=== ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' ===
After hearing from [[Queen Bean]] that Princess Peach will be arriving in the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] through the airport, Mario and Luigi head down over there. However, the [[Pea|employees]] tell Mario and Luigi that the airplane cannot board due to a giant egg and many [[Piranha Plant]]s on the airport. Mario and Luigi eventually find the source of the infestation, [[Mom Piranha]], and defeats her. Soon after, Peach arrives from the [[Toad Express|plane]] and she is about to speak but Mario and Luigi quickly hide thinking she will talk in explosive language. To their surprise, Peach talks normally and Mario and Luigi want to know what happened. Queen Bean then talks the group away from the airport to discuss the situation back at [[Beanbean Castle]].

After [[Cackletta]]'s defeat and [[Bowser's Castle (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Bowser Castle]] destroyed. Mario, Luigi, Princess Peach and [[Toadsworth]] bids farewell to Queen Bean and [[Lady Lima]]. [[Prince Peasley]] gives them his present as the plane departs, unaware the present inside turns out to be [[Bowser]].
