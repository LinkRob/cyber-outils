'''Gunslinger''' is the boss [[microgame]] of [[9-Volt]] & [[18-Volt]]'s microgame set [[Retro Action]] in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Layout==
Gunslinger takes place in the setting of a mode of ''[[w:c:nintendo:Hogan's Alley|Hogan's Alley]]''. Most of the assets are reused from ''Hogan's Alley'' except the blue barrel drums, which are 3D models, and the graphic released from tapping open the barrel.

==Gameplay==
At first, the player must use their [[Stylus]] to tap a few cans so that they land in one of the platforms; from highest to lowest, they reward 300, 800, and 5,000 [[point]]s respectively. While the points add to the score, this is just for style and does not affect gameplay. After this, the player has tap a barrel drum repeatedly to crunch it up and destroy it, upon which a graphic of a [[Nintendo]] item is released, either a [[Nintendo Entertainment System]], [[R.O.B.]] (specifically the [[Family Computer]] model in all versions), a [[Power Pad]], an [[w:c:nintendo:NES Advantage|NES Advantage]], an [[NES Zapper]], or a [[Super Scope]], the only one of these items to be associated with the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] and not the Family Computer or the Nintendo Entertainment System.

If the player fails to hit a can on to a platform or does not break open a barrel drum, they lose at the microgame.

The difficulty levels affect the portion of the minigame in which the cans are shot. In the first difficulty level, the player has to shoot three cans, while in the second and third difficulty levels, they have to shoot five. The third difficulty level also blocks off the topmost 300-point platform.
