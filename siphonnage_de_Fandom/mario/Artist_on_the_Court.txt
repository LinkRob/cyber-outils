'''Artist on the Court''' is a [[Minigame|Special Game]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and its ''[[New Play Control!]]'' release.

==Layout==
The Special Game takes place at the [[Grass Court]] variation of [[Peach Dome]]. In the middle of the court, there is a mural of a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' character, which is colorless at first, but a fully colored reflection of it is displayed on the ground.

The HUD consists of a paint percentage at the upper left showing how much of the picture is colored in and the countdown timer at the upper right.

==Gameplay==
The player has five minutes (ten in the ''[[New Play Control!]]'' version) to color in the mural by hitting it with paint balls that appear from [[Warp Pipe]]s. [[Paint ball]]s come in varying colors, which becomes increasingly lower as more of the mural is colored in. Once the player fills all areas of the mural corresponding to a certain color, paint balls of the same color stop appearing from the Warp Pipes. The player can use the mural reflection as a guide on where to try and hit the paint balls. Some portions of a mural cannot be filled in without doing a lob shot. The Special Game ends once the entire mural has been colored in.

In single-player, the player has to fill the entire mural within a [[Time limit|limited time]] of five minutes in order to unlock the next stage of difficulty and make a time record.

In multiplayer, players must finish the mural painting to set a time record.

In the ''New Play Control!'' version, Artist on the Court has been made easier, mainly because the color of whichever paint ball is hit fills in every portion of the mural corresponding to that color automatically. The time limit has also been made twice as long.

==List of levels==
There are five difficulty levels, each having different characters to color in:

*Novice: [[Mario]]
*Easy: [[Luigi]]
*Normal: [[Diddy Kong]]
*Hard: [[Bowser]] and [[Bowser Jr.]]
*Expert: [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]]

==Trivia==
*An arrangement of "[[Inside the Castle Walls]]" from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' plays during this Special Game.
