The '''Power Star''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Power Star is the most expensive Gaddget, as it has to be bought for 100,000 [[coin]]s. It begins with a purple gradient background, and it changes to a blue gradient background when a [[Power Star]] appears. The Power Stars moves up and down with eyes blinking. The sparks from the Power Star and the background appear as well. There are no controls listed for this Gaddget, and there is no way to interact with it.
