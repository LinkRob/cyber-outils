'''Pianta Tokens''' are special type of tokens that only appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== History ==
Pianta Tokens can only be used and earned at the [[Pianta Parlor]] in [[Rogueport|West Rogueport]]. There are many ways to earn Pianta Tokens and the best way is to win them from the slot machines. Pianta Tokens can be then traded into [[Lahla]] the [[Boo]] for badges or items that can only be found there (such as [[Cake Mix]]es). Pianta Tokens can also be used to play the different games in there with the chance of earning even more if [[Mario]] wins. Whenever Mario tries to leave the Pianta Palor while he still has some Pianta Tokens left, Lahla will warp to where he is and hold onto his Pianta Tokens until he comes back.
