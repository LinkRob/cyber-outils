'''Strong Hiders''' are stronger variants of [[Hider]]s which first appear in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]'' and return in ''[[Luigi's Mansion Arcade]]''. 

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
They resemble normal Hiders, but are recolored dark cyan with sky blue cracks all over their bodies. 

=== Behavior ===
They behave just like Hiders, however. They are a bit more difficult to find and they have 75 HP, instead of 15.

== In {{series|Luigi's Mansion}} ==
=== ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' ===
Strong Hiders only appear in [[Treacherous Mansion]], where they appear through most missions, usually alongside [[Strong Greenie]]s.

In the [[ScareScraper]], Strong Hiders only appear in Expert difficultly or Endless mode, usually with [[Greenie]]s with helmets and [[Creeper]]s. When the [[Creeper Launcher]] or its variants are fought, Strong Hiders assist them in place of normal Hiders and [[Slammer]]s.

=== ''Luigi's Mansion Arcade'' ===
Strong Hiders appear in the [[Old Clockworks]] and Treacherous Mansion. They have 25 HP.
