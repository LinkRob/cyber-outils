A '''Coin Block Capsule''' is a [[orb|capsule]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Description==
It can be thrown onto a [[space]] on the [[board]], or used on the player for five [[coin]]s. Whoever lands on a space containing this capsule obtains ten coins. This is one of the most basic capsules in the game, and it has a similar appearance to the [[Event Block]] from the first ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Names in other languages==
*'''French:''' Capsule Bloc Pièces, meaning "Coin Block Capsule"
*'''German:''' Münzblock-Kapsel, meaning "Coin Block Capsule"
*'''Japanese:''' コインブロックカプセル (''Koin Burokku Kapuseru''), meaning "Coin Block Capsule"
