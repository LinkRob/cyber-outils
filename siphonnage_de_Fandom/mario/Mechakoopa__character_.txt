'''Mechakoopa''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' and a representative of the species of the [[Mechakoopa|same name]]. Mechakoopa is a mathematician who works at the [[Mushroom Library]], and he boasts how he discovered Mechakoopa's Theorem. He gives the player character three math questions, and if all three are answered correctly, Mechakoopa rewards them with the [[Castle Night]] [[Gaddget]].
