''Not to be confused with [[Super Luigi Bros.]].''

'''''Luigi Bros.''''' is a remake of the arcade title ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]'' and a bonus game mode in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and [[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury|''Super Mario 3D World'' + ''Bowser's Fury'']],  it can be unlocked by completing the main adventure, and in the [[Wii U]] version, having [[New Super Luigi U]] save data.

== Characters ==

=== Playable characters ===

* [[Luigi]]

=== Enemies ===

* [[Fighter Fly|Fighterfly]]
* [[Shellcreeper]]
* [[Sidestepper]]

==Gameplay==
the player takes control of [[Luigi]] with the modern colour palette, while player 2 takes control of Luigi with the colour palette of [[Super Mario Bros.]] Luigi / Fire Luigi. Luigi goes through various stages, getting harder after every stage. Game B is generally faster and more difficult compared to game A. In order to play, players have to click on the Luigi icon on the bottom right corner. At the title screen, it does not say "MARIO BROS." like before, instead saying "LUIGI BROS."
