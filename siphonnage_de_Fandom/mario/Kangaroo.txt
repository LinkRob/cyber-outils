'''Kangaroos''' are real [[Australia]]n mammals who have made a few appearances in ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' media. Kangaroos first appear in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[7 Continents for 7 Koopas]]."

==Appearances==
In ''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3'' episode "7 Continents for 7 Koopas," when [[Cheatsy Koopa|Cheatsy]] was assigned by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] to take over Australia, Cheatsy uses his [[Magic Wand]] to transform the [[human]]s into Kangaroos. This effect was later undone by [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] when she retrieved Cheatsy's Magic Wand.

In ''[[Mario is Missing!]]'', Kangaroos are mentioned by a tourist in [[Sydney]], Australia. The tourist states that he is interested in seeing the kangaroos and that they also are Australian mascots.
