'''Dashing Thief Peach''' is a power-up in ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]''

==Overview==
Dashing Thief Peach involves [[Princess Peach]] being dressed as a caped thief and using equipment like a grappling hook.

===Physical appearance===
In her Dashing Thief form, Peach wears a dark red ensemble made of a short coat, a cape with a scarlet interior, tight pants, knee-length laced-up boots, and a fedora with a scarlet trim. She wears a dark red mask on her face, small chartreuse round earrings, short white gloves and a scarlet shirt with thin diagonal stripes under the coat; on the coat are six golden buttons and a belt, as well as her brooch pinned to the right lapel. Her hair is tied up in a bun.
