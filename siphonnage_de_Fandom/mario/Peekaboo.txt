'''Peekaboo''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. By wearing a Peekaboo, [[Mario]] can see his enemies' [[Heart Point|HP]] without having to use the [[Tattle]] ability.

== In ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ==
[[File:Peekaboo_badge.png|thumb|left|19px]]
Peekaboo is a badge that [[Merlow]] sells to Mario for ten [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]]s. Peekaboo requires 3 [[Badge Point|BP]] to use.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
[[Dazzle]] sells Peekaboo to Mario for seven Star Pieces. Peekaboo instead requires 2 BP to use.
