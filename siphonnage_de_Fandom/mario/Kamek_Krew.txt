The '''Kamek Krew''' is a rap group consisting of five [[Toady|Toadies]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. They are found on the second floor of the [[Mushroom Condo]]. Although they are called the Kamek Krew, they are not associated with [[Kamek]] in the game, so it draws reference to how Toadies are minions of Kamek since ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The Kamek Krew are the subject of the mission [[Kamek Krew Live!]]. They want to perform at the [[Mushroom Stadium]] in the [[Desert Area]]. The stadium's owner, [[Bullet Bill (character)|Bullet Bill]], is a big fan of the Kamek Krew, so he allows them to play there for free. As a reward for helping the Kamek Krew, the player receives the [[Bull's-eye]] [[Gaddget]].
