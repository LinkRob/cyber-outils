The '''Luigi Block''' is an object in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] included within ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

==Description==
The Luigi Block is a [[block]] that, contrary to its name, is modeled after [[Mario]]'s signature "M" instead of [[Luigi]]'s "L." After [[Bowser]] has been defeated, a Luigi Block appears at the start of [[level]]s, and hitting it switches to the physics of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', with lower [[jump]]s and better traction. The Luigi Block does not affect [[Nabbit]], and in the original [[Wii U]] version, the Luigi Block does not appear if he is used in single-player mode. If a Luigi Block is hit for the first time in a play session, a message appears, describing the block's effects.
