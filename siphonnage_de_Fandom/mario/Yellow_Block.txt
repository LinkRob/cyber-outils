'''Yellow Blocks''' are an object in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
Found in various locations, these blocks often block [[Mario]]'s path until he finds the [[hammer]]. There are three different various types of blocks found in the game: Yellow Blocks, which can be broken by the Sandard Hammer and upgrades. Stone Blocks, which can be broken by the [[Super Hammer]] and upgrades. However, in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', these blocks can be broken by the [[Ultra Hammer]]. And finally, the Metal Block. The Metal Block can be broken only by the Ultra Hammer. But, In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', these blocks cannot be broken at all. In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', the Yellow Block appears, and is broken only by [[Cudge]].

== Trivia ==
*Yellow Blocks share a very similar appearance to [[Flip Block]]s in ''[[Super Mario World]].''
