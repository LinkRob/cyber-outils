'''That's How I Roll''' is [[Young Cricket]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. It is a pun of the phrase, "That’s how I roll".

==Gameplay==
All balls must roll in the holes. The microgame takes twice as much time as other microgames. If all balls roll into the holes, the microgame is won. With "The Waiter" form, the player must tilt the [[Wii Remote]] to tilt the object.

*'''Level 1:''' A ball must be rolled in three holes. A snowman comes out of the hole after winning the microgame.

*'''Level 2:''' Two balls must be rolled in any two holes. A rainbow appears from the hole to the other after the microgame is won.

*'''Level 3:''' Three balls must be rolled in any three holes. Three fish jump out the holes and go through another hole after winning in the microgame.
