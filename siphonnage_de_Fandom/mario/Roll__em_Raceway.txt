'''Roll 'em Raceway''' is a board that appears in [[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]. It takes place on a circuit race course with a pit stop, surrounded by grasslands. Players travel on this board by driving in a race car, as opposed to by foot as in other boards. This board features an exclusive item known as the Turbo Dice, which allows players to roll four Dice Blocks, giving them a range from four to forty spaces to travel in a single turn.
