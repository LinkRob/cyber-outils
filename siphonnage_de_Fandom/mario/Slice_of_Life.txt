'''Slice of Life''' is one of [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. 

==Gameplay==
The objective is to shred the object with the shredder by twirling the [[Wii Remote]]. If done successfully, the player wins, but if the object is not shredded on time, they lose.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level features a different object that has to be shredded, a different sound effect constantly plays while shredding the object, and winning results in a different effect.

*'''Level 1:''' A small piece of paper has to be shredded, during which a voice saying "paper" constantly plays. Fulfilling this objective causes the paper to be eaten by a goat, whose bleating can be heard.
*'''Level 2:''' A medium pasta sheet has to be shredded, during which a voice constantly saying "pasta" constantly plays. Fulfilling this objective turns the pasta sheet into spaghetti, and an oven timer can be heard.
*'''Level 3:''' A block of ice containing a fish has to be shredded. Fulfilling this objective turns it into sushi, and a Japanese percussive sound effect can be heard.

==Trivia==
*The microgame's name is a pun on the phrase "slice of life."
