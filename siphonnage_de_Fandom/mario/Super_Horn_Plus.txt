'''Super Horn Plus''' is a special skill in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. A glider that grants chances to a player has higher chances of using [[Super Horn]] from [[Item Box]]es. [[Fare Flier]] was the first glider to introduce the Super Horn Plus.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The '''Super Horn Plus''' appears as a special skill. It allows the player to use the [[Super Horn]]. Depending on the rarity of the glider, the rarer the glider, the more points awarded to the player. Granted that gliders grant the players points if they hit the Super Horn Plus on their opponents.
