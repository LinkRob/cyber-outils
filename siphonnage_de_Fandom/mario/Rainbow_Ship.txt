'''Rainbow Ship''' is a special court in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The Rainbow Ship court is set on a giant [[airship]] moving high above the clouds. The ship looks very ornate overall, and the backboards of the hoops are adorned in the shape of a [[w:c:finalfantasy:Chocobo|Chocobo]]. Despite being the final court, Rainbow Ship neither has any gimmicks nor hazards.

There is a similar court in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'' called [[Star Ship (court)|Star Ship]].

==In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''==
Rainbow Ship is the fourth and final court in the [[Rainbow Tourney]]. Unlike the fourth courts listed under other tourneys, the characters actually play on Rainbow Ship during the tourney, after completing [[Pirate Ship]]. The opponents on the Rainbow Ship during the Rainbow Tourney are always the same: [[Ninja (character)|Ninja]], [[Black Mage]], and [[White Mage]].

Rainbow Ship is one of the only three ''Final Fantasy''-based courts, the other two being [[Malboro Garden]] and [[Glare Desert]].

==Behind the scenes==
The first time that an [[w:c:finalfantasy:Airship|airship]] appears in a ''Final Fantasy'' game is in the first ''[[w:c:finalfantasy:Final Fantasy|Final Fantasy]]''. The stage's cloudy background might be a reference to the opening cinematic to the [[Nintendo DS]] remake of ''[[w:c:finalfantasy:Final Fantasy III|Final Fantasy III]]'', which starts with an airship sailing through a sea of clouds.
