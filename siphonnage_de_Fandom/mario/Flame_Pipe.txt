The '''Flame Pipe''' is an area in [[Bowser's body]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. It can be accessed after going through the [[Pump Works]]. A giant [[Scutlet]] is blocking part of the pipe, defeating it will allow [[Bowser]] to breathe fire again.
