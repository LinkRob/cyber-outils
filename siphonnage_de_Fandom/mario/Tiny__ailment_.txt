==Appearances==
===''[[Paper Mario series]]''===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Tiny can happen in a certain amount of ways. [[Mario]] can shrink enemies using the [[Shrink Stomp]] badge. Certain enemies such as [[Bzzap!]]s and [[Hammer Bro]]s can cause this ailment sometimes as well. When Tiny, Mario's or the foe's attack power is lowered. This ailment can be cured by certain items.

[[File:TinyTTYD.png|thumb|left]]In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Tiny returns but now, Mario's attack power is dropped by two making it a rather troublesome ailment. The Shrink Stomp badge returns in this game as a way for Mario to shrink enemies. [[Yoshi (partner)|Yoshi]] can use his [[Mini-Egg]] technique to shrink enemies as well. The [[Mini Mr. Mini]] serves the purpose of shrinking enemies as well.

In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Tiny appears as a form of field move by using the [[Pixl]] [[Dottie]]. This can be used to access small doors. This status effect reappears in [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]] (known as Mini in this game) replacing the [[Poison (ailment)|Poison]] status effect from past Mario & Luigi games. The enemies that cause this effect are [[Spritzbloom]]s, [[Virus]]es, [[Krubbish]]es, and [[Antasman|Antasmen]]. This effect once again deals less damage to enemies and can be cured with a [[Refreshing Herb]]. The status returns in ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'', where [[Ludwig von Koopa]] can inflict this on [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] with one of his Battle Cards.
