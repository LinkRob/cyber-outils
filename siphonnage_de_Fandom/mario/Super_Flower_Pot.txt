The '''Super Flower Pot''' is a powerup available in [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park|''Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park''.]] 

== Usage ==
The Super Flower Pot transforms the user into their Flower form. They gain lime green clothing and a bud on their head of their signature color, making them greatly resemble [[Poplin|Poplins]].<gallery>
