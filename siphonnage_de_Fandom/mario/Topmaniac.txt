'''Topmaniac''' is the leader of the [[Topman]] tribe and a recurring boss in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. He makes his debut appearance in the [[Battlerock Galaxy]], and [[Mario (character)|Mario]] later encounters him in the [[Dreadnought Galaxy]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Topmaniac looks very different from his minions, being a bigger Topman with spikes coming out of him. His head has a red light, and he has two orange eyes.

== In ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ==
Topmaniac is encountered by [[Mario]] in both [[Battlerock Galaxy]] and [[Dreadnought Galaxy]]. In Battlerock Galaxy he serves as the main boss of that Galaxy, is the third/penultimate boss of the Fountain area and is overall the seventh (eight if counting the [[Mandibug Stack]]) of the game. In Dreadnought Galaxy, Topmaniac is also the main boss of that Galaxy and is also the third boss of Garden area, but this time he's the third-to-last boss of that area and is the twenty-first(twenty-second if again, counting the Mandibug Stack)/third-to-last boss of the game.

Topmaniac is first seen flying above his saucer in an erratic pathway in each of his appearances; to fight him, Mario has to jump onto the raised platform on the top side of the saucer. Doing so causes Topmaniac to descend down to his saucer and activate his spikes.

=== Battle ===
When Topmaniac kicks off his battle, he will summons three [[Topmini|Topminis]] to his side. If his minions fall, Topmaniac will charge at Mario, attempting to chop him up with his spikes. Mario must first need to [[jump]] on top of him, causing the boss to be stunned. Afterwards, Mario must use his [[Star Spin]] to knock the monstrous top into the nearby electrical fence. However, there is a time limit to this, as the light on Topmaniac's head will flash. Around four seconds after the flashing starts, Topmaniac will regain his spikes and resume his original attack pattern. The second and third rounds are the same, but Topmaniac summons red Topmans rather than Topminis upon recovering from the second round's electric shock for the third round. After Topmaniac gets knocked into the electric walls of his arena one last time, he explodes and rewards Mario with the [[Power Star]].

In the [[Battlerock Galaxy]], the electrical wire has no gaps. In the [[Dreadnought Galaxy]], the electrical wire at the sides of Topmaniac's arena has gaps, so Mario will have to aim his spin to send Topmaniac into the wall in order to damage him; if Topmaniac lands in the gaps in his arena's electric fence, no damage will be dealt. Additionally, all three of Topmaniac's phases involve red Topmans.

==Behind the scenes==
=== Etymology ===
Topmaniac's name is a portmanteau of ''Topman'', a species of sentient top in the game, and ''maniac'' explaining why he is going wild.

== Nomenclature ==
Topman is known as the "Topman King" in Japanese (''Bēgoman Kingu''; written ベーゴマンキング; ''Bēgoman'' means "topman."), Korean (''Paeng-i King''; written 	팽이킹; ''Paeng-i'' means "topman."), and German (''Begomankönig''; ''Begoman'' means "topman.") His French name is ''Begoman'', sharing the name of his species. In Spanish, he is called ''Rey Trompo'', meaning "King Top." (''Trompo'' is a synonym of ''peón'' ("top").) His Italian name is ''Robobosso'', from ''Roboto'' ("robot"; presumably based on ''Robopunta'' (the name of the common [[Topman]])) and ''Bosso'' (synonym of ''guardiano in videogioco'' ("boss")), most likely due to ''Robopunta'' being the Italian name of the Topman species.

== Trivia ==
*Strangely, Topmaniac is referred to as ''Robopunta Regina'', which means "Topman Queen," in the Italian ''Super Mario Bros. Encyclopedia'', even though his Japanese, Korean, and German names (all meaning "Topman King") and his Spanish name (''Rey Trompo'', meaning "King Top") imply his programmed gender to be masculine.
*Topmaniac's weakness (being thrown into barriers) is similar to the Mega Titan from ''Kirby and the Amazing Mirror''.
*In both areas Topmaniac is fought in, he is always fought as the third mission's boss.
*If counting the strategy of jumping on his head as part of a hit-point, then Topmaniac would have six-hit points. This makes Topmaniac one of the six non-Bowser bosses to have more then three hit-points, the others being [[Tarantox]], [[Bouldergeist]], [[Heavy Metal Mecha-Bowser]], [[Kingfin]] and [[Bowser Jr.]]
*Originally, Topmaniac was going to have his second battle happen in the [[Melty Molten Galaxy]], but the final version has him fought in the [[Dreadnought Galaxy]] instead for that battle. Here, his body was grey with lighter grey stripes rather than blue arrows. He also had a rounded head, with his head rising up like a regular topman.
