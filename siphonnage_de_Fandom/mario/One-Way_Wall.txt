A '''One-Way Wall''' is an object which appears in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''.

== Description==
One-Way Walls are designed so that if [[Mario]] passes through one, he cannot go back through it from the other side, much like the [[Flipper]]s from the {{series|Yoshi's Island}} series. In the {{series|Super Mario Maker}} One-Way Walls are available to use in every style except the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' style in ''Super Mario Maker 2''. One-Way Walls are typically seen pointed to the right, but some instead point left, up, or down. One-Way Walls pointed upward can be used as a platform.
