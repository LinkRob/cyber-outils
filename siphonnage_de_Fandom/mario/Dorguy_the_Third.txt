'''Dorguy the Third''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Dorguy the Third is found on [[Underwhere Road]] where he is one of three guardian doors that works for [[Queen Jaydes]]. Dorguy the Third is the final Dorguy encountered in the game and, unlike his brothers who had quiz questions, has the heroes fight the [[Underchomp]]. The fight itself is in RP format and, after defeating the Underchomp, Dorguy the Third lets the heroes through which is the end of Underwhere Road.
