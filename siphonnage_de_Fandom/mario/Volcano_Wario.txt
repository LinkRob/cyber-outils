The '''Volcano Wario''' is a character from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' that acts as the "final host" in the game. It is encountered in Lava at First Sight after [[Wario]] removes the Form Stone God's statue and gets abducted by a fiery spirit's arm during his raid on the shrine.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
The Volcano Wario is the living, sentient, malevolent form of the Perspiration Peak Volcano on Caresaway Island. It has Wario's face, albeit with jagged lips, no teeth or tongue, and blank orange eyes.

=== Personality traits ===
While it is only seen for short time, the Volcano Wario is shown to be very destructive.

=== Powers and abilities ===
The Volcano Wario is able to launch magma balls while it erupts.

== Biography ==
The Volcano Wario is created when Wario enters the shrine of Perspiration Peak Volcano in his search of treasure. He grabs a large, golden statue of the Form Stone God, but is attacked by a lava hand that lunges out of the now-exposed opening. The volcano comes to life and gains a face that resembles Wario's. Now sentient and out for revenge, the volcano, now referred to as the "Volcano Wario," erupts, endangering everyone on the island. The Woods Watcher Elder instructs everyone to dance in order to calm its wrath, which [[Jimmy Thang]] enthusiastically agrees to do, his companions joining in. With their dancing, Wario's employees defeat the volcano and save the vacationers, causing it to become dormant again.

After the Volcano Wario's defeat, Wario is ejected from its crater, landing back in the shrine where he is mistaken for a god and worshipped. Even after the Volcano Wario's reversion back to an inanimate state, its face still remains, though whether it gets its face removed or not after the ending is unknown.

== In ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' ==
<!-- Replace "game" with the game they appear in, if they appear in more than one game, add headings with "In <game>"-->
The Volcano Wario is the game's final "host." It has the following [[Microgame]]s, in which [[Wario]] himself is involved, either in person (i.e. Balloon Buster, where he appears as a manager) or as a cameo (i.e. Camera Ready, where his moustache and nose are the basis of a mask that a girl wears):
*Balloon Buster, in which the player has to pop balloons by sitting on them via the Squat form.
*[[Camera Ready]], where a girl has to pose for the camera; when the photo is taken, players can see that she is wearing a mask resembling Wario's nose and moustache. The player helps the girl using the Fashionista form.
*Chopsticks Champ, where Wario has to split chopsticks without breaking them in order to give them to customers at his restaurant, with the help of the player using the Choo Choo form.
*Dial-a-flex, where players have to help the character with the Wario mask has to pose like in the picture via the At Attention form.
*Fishin' Hole, where players have to help Wario catch a fish with the help of the player using the Archer form.
*Fresh Squeezed, where a hen-like creature wearing a Wario mask over her beak has to lay an egg with the help of the player using the Lifter form.
*Garlic Grab, where Wario in a frog suit has to jump to get some garlic with the help of players using the Squat form.
*Giant Swing, where players must use the Big Cheese form to make the gymnasts do flips on a bar that two giants have tied to their waists.
*Junction Jumble, where players must use the Massage form to switch the tracks so the trains can pass by.
*Laser Gazer, where players have to help the Mecha Wario zap some targets with eye beams.
*Match Cut, where a girl must tilt her head so Wario can cut one of her pigtails to make it the same length as the other with the help of players using the Lovestruck form.
*Pair Pointer, where Wario has to get matching shoes with the help of players using the Scales form.
*Party of Question Mark, where Wario has invited some folks to his party and the player must count all of them, including him, via the Hand Model form.
*[[Pin Drop]], where Wario has to dodge some spikes on his way down via parachute with the help of players using the Scales form.
*Plain Dealer, where Wario has to deal some cards to others via the Crocodile Form.
*Puppet Patter, where the player uses the Hand Model form to make a puppet in Wario's image speak.
*Rock Paper Scissors, where the player must use the Hand Model form to beat Wario in a game of Rock Paper Scissors.
*[[Rubber Stamp]], where the player must stamp some postcards while dodging bacon via the Knight form.
*[[Throw Down]], where Wario must throw his foe with the help of players using the Massage form.
*Waste Disposal, where Wario has to get some garbage into the trash can with the help of players using the Squat form on the first two difficulty levels and the Ba-KAW form on level 3.
*'''Boss''': Super Wario Dance Company, where the player has to dance with the Wario Dance Troupe à la in Wario Dance Company from ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'' via the At Attention form.
When the player plays Lava at First Sight, the Boss Microgame is reached when the round number is a multiple of 21, and the life bar is represented with god statues. If the player clears a microgame when they play Lava at First Sight, the Volcano Wario calms down, but if the player fails, it threatens the crowd. When the player completes the sixth and thirteenth microgames in each loop, the Volcano Wario will attack the player by launching a magma ball from its crater, and the player must dance to power the shield blocking the monstrous mountain's projectile, following an on-screen instructor. If the player fails, a life will be lost as though they lost a microgame. Speed picks up after each attack.
