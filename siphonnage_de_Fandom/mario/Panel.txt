'''Panels''' are an object which appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==Description==
Panels are located on the map of each world, representing the different locations. The main types of Panels are Action Panels, each leading to a specific [[level]]. Every world begins with a Start Panel, which only marks the starting point on the map. Completing the level within an Action Panel turns it into a Cleared Panel, which depicts either an "M" or an "L," depending on if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] completed the level, respectively.

There are two types of Panels where Mario or Luigi can play a minigame: a [[Spade Panel]] for a slot machine-like minigame and a [[N-Mark Spade Panel]] for a card-matching minigame.
