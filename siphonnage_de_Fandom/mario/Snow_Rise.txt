|caption = [[Mario]] dodging enemies in Snow Rise.}}'''Snow Rise''' is the first level of World 4 in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==Layout==
Snow Rise, like the name, is mainly covered with snow. The skies are in a dark blue color.

==History==
Mario steps out of the harsh, poison atmosphere of World 3 and into the cold of World 4. Mario encounters many new enemies like [[Cooligan]]s (although, they're more of an obstacle than a true enemy since Mario can't battle them) and [[Shiny Goomba]]s on this arctic level. Mario eventually braves the cold and finds the Comet Piece to end the level.

==Notes==
Mario must go to [[Surfshine Harbor]] first before he can even gain access to Snow Rise. He must first fight the [[Big Cheep Cheep]]. Then before he enters Snow Rise, he must have the Radiator Thing Sticker in order to process through the level.

==Secret Exit==
Mario must use the Radiator thing and place it on a brick structure on the large snowy hill. The whole thing melts and reveals a new cave. Mario proceeds further collecting Stickers along the way and the Comet Piece heading toward the next level: [[Ice Flow]].
