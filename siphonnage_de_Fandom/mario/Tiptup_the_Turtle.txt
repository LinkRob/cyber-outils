'''Tiptup the Turtle''' is a green turtle-like creature from the famed ''Banjo-Kazooie'' series that appears in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]''. Tiptup is also a ridable character in the game along with one of the characters who helps the other playable characters stop [[Wizpig]] and send him back to [[Future Fun Land]].

It later reappeared in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]'', the only difference from its predecessor is that Tiptup is recounted with a higher-pitched voice.
==Banjo-Kazooie History==
Tiptup later was featured in the video game series ''Banjo-Kazooie''. He played a minor role in the first installment, being the leader of the Tiptup Choir Group, and in ''Banjo-Tooie'', he resides at the beaches in Jolly Roger's Lagoon.
