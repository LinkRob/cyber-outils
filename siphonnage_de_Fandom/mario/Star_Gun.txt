The '''Star Gun''' is both the final and strongest weapon used by [[Geno]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be found in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]]. Equipping the Star Gun increases Geno's [[Attack]] by 57. The Star Gun can be sold for 60 [[coin]]s, although it cannot be replaced afterward.
