'''Lava Babbles''' (originally named '''Pyrospheres''') are variants of [[Sparky|Sparkies]] found in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|remake]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
They are located in [[Barrel Volcano]], as well as [[Bowser's Keep]]. Lava Babbles usually appear with stronger creatures in battle, such as [[Chained Kong]]s or [[Stompillar]]s. While they appear redder than regular Lava Bubbles in the remake, they share the same palette in the original game.

In battle, Pyrospheres mainly attack by leaping into the air and then trying to burn one of [[Mario]]'s party members. They can also use special moves such as [[Drain]] and [[Flame Stone]]. Pyropspheres are weak to [[Ice (element)|ice]] moves, so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] or an [[Ice Bomb]] are effective against them.

When Mario and his allies reach the volcano's main chamber, a large group of Lava Babbles are flying around. The swarm spots the group and quickly coalesces into [[Czar Dragon]].
