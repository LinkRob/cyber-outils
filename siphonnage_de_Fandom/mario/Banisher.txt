The '''Banisher''' is an unlockable [[kart]] of [[Dry Bones]]' in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. It is unlocked by completing every Nitro Grand Prix [[Engine Class|50cc]] cup. Dry Bones has other two karts being [[Standard DB]] and the [[Dry Bomber]].

== Description ==
[[File:Banisher - Kart Select (Dry Bones) - Mario Kart DS.png|thumb|160px|right|Dry Bones in his Dry Bomber.]]
It has the lowest top [[speed]] out of all karts, extremely high [[Acceleration]], high [[Handling]], low [[Drift]], and is [[weight|light]], but is heavier than all of the other karts Dry Bones, himself has and also high items. It looks similar to the [[Turbo Yoshi]] and [[Turbo Birdo]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'', except replicating Dry Bones.

The Banisher's weight is equivalent to [[Luigi]]'s [[Poltergust 4000]] and [[Toad (character)|Toad]]'s [[Standard TD]]. It shares its items stat with all other characters' heavier karts.

[[Mario]] and Yoshi can use the Banisher once all Nitro or Retro Grand Prix cups in 150cc are completed, and all racers can use this kart once all cups in Mirror Mode have been completed.
