'''DK Jungle''' is a tennis court part of the [[Banana Cup]] in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''.

==Description==
The court takes place inside the canopy of a dense jungle. Various trees and [[DK's Tree House]] can be seen in the background, and are based on their appearance from ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]''. The [[Banana Hoard]] is not seen but is instead represented by a pile of [[banana]]s sitting atop a wooden platform just under DK's Tree House.

The flooring of the court itself is made of wood. There are some wooden chairs around the court boundaries as well as the umpire's chair, which is also made of wood. [[Toad (character)|Toad]] is seen sitting on the chair while a toucan is perched on it. Several other [[Toad (species)|Toads]] can be seen spectating on the sidelines.

The music for DK Jungle plays an arrangement of "[[DK Island Swing]]" from ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

==Flavor text==
*In-game: ''A rugged court in the heart of the jungle.''
