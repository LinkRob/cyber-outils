'''Big Blue''' is a track which appears in the ''[[Animal Crossing x Mario Kart 8]]'' downloadable pack for ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' as well as in the base game for ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is the fourth and final track of the [[Super Bell Cup]]. Big Blue is based on the ''F-Zero'' series.

==Layout==
Similarly to [[Mount Wario]], it is comprised of one large track made up of 3 sections instead of the normal 3 laps around the track. The Coins have been replaced with the recharge strips from ''F-Zero''. Its sections are divided with [[Glider]] areas that span hundreds of feet long.
