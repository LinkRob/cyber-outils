[[File:Sprixie_House.jpg|thumb|A close up of a Sprixie House]]The '''Sprixie House''' is a house in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. They can be accessed at any time, where a [[Sprixie]] (the [[Stamp Collector (Super Mario 3D World)|Stamp Collector]] to be more specific) present have a new [[Stamp]] waiting for the player. However, they can only be accessed once. After their respective level is completed, Sprixie Houses will disappear and cannot be accessed again.

==Locations==
*[[World 2 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 2]]: The [[Sprixie Princess]] embossed on the stamp is the green one, with a happy/awed expression.
*[[World 3 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 3]]: The Sprixie Princess shown here is the yellow one, albeit crying.
*[[World 4 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 4]]: Shown here is the blue Sprixie Princess, who is apparently angry at something...
*[[World 5 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 5]]: On this stamp, you can see a scene where the orange [[Sprixie Princess]] is laughing at something off-screen.
*[[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]]: The purple [[Sprixie Princess]] is shown thinking on this stamp.
*[[World Castle]]: The cyan [[Sprixie Princess]] is shown getting startled by something off-screen.
*[[World Bowser]]: In contrast to her personality, the red [[Sprixie Princess]] is shown here being frightened.
*[[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]]: [[Cat Mario]] is shown climbing on this stamp.
*[[World Crown]]: The stamp given here shows the 2-D sprite [[Luigi]] used when he appeared in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' for the NES System, albeit in pure black and white.
