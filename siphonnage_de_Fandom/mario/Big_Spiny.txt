The '''Big Spiny''' is a larger variant of [[Spiny]] that appears throughout the [[Mario (franchise)|''Super Mario'' franchise]], debuting in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]''. 

==History==
===Movies===
====''[[Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!]]''====
Big-sized Spinies appear in the 1986 anime movie ''Super Mario Bros.: Peach-hime Kyūshutsu Dai Sakusen!''.

===Games===
====In ''[[Mario Party 9]]''====
Big Spinies appear in the minigame, [[Sock It to Lakitu]].

====In ''Super Mario'' series====
=====''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''=====
They reappear in ''Super Mario Maker'', ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS'', and ''Super Mario Maker 2''.

=====''[[Super Mario Run]]''=====
Big Spinies are also found in ''Super Mario Run'', first appearing in World 3-1: [[Big Spiny Blitz]].

====In ''[[Mario Golf: World Tour]]''====
In the ''Mario Golf: World Tour'' course [[Wiggler Park]], several Big Spinies appear as obstacles on Hole 5.

====In ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''====
{{main|Big Spiny (mini-boss)}}
A single Big Spiny appears in ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' as a mini boss in [[Kiwano Temple]], where it guards a [[Mini Paint Star]].
