Pump Mario is a version of Mario that appears in the Mario and Luigi series.
==History==
===Mario and Luigi: Superstar Saga===
If Mario and Luigi approach a water spout, Mario will drink a lot of it and become bloated. Luigi can jump on his brother to spit the water out in a stream, hammer him to shoot up to three water balls, or tickle him to water [[Piranha Plant]]s.
