'''{{world-link|5|castlen2|World 5-Castle (New Super Mario Bros. 2)}}''' is the castle level of [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 5]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

== Layout ==
The level starts with some [[Fence]]s with [[Climbing Koopa]]s. The Fences on the track can move up by pressing B. This one can be used to jump through the platforms above. Followed by that, there is a [[Dry Bones]] with some orange [[Question Block]]s and another Fence, which is above some spikes. As they jump off the Fence, they will have to be careful of the [[Podoboo]], [[Dry Piranha Plant]] and another Dry Bones. There is a set of Question Blocks, and a Fence used to traverse through parts of the castle. The Bros. will need to jump over some spikes and avoid Bone Piranha Plants and Podoboos. After jumping of the Fence as it sinks into the lava, there is a platform with more Question Blocks, [[Coin]]s and the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. There are also more Fences with some [[Climbing Koopa]]s. As they reach another Fence on a track, more Bone Piranha Plants and Podoboos will need to be avoided. A [[Red Ring]] can also be found here. As the Bros. traverse through more platforms, Coins, Question Blocks, Fences, Climbing Koopas and Dry Bones, they will need to climb more Fences on tracks whilst avoiding more Podoboos, Bone Piranha Plants, as well as well as a [[Big Bone Piranha Plant]]. After that, they reach the boss door, leading to [[Ludwig von Koopa]]. In the corridors, they blast through a [[Cannon Pipe]], destroying some Bone Piranha Plants, and reach the arena to confront the Koopaling.

=== Boss battle ===
[[File:NSMB2 World 5-Castle Ludwig Battle.png|thumb|200px|right|The [[Mario Bros.]] fighting Ludwig.]]
Ludwig begins the battle by [[Flutter Jump]]ing to a chain above, shooting blue magic blasts from above. The Bros. will need to blast through a Cannon Pipe to whatever chain Ludwig is hanging onto. This causes him to fall and temporarily stun him, and the Bros. will need to jump on him three times, and spins in his shell after the first two jumps. When down to his final hit, Ludwig will start swinging on his chain, making it harder to hit him using the Cannon Pipes. So, the strategy here is to jump on him before he can Flutter Jump to a chain after jumping on him the first time. Once he is defeated, the Mario Bros. collect the key to open the gate, and find [[Princess Peach]] trapped in a cage aboard the [[Koopa Clown Car]], which drives off to [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]].

== [[Star Coin]] locations ==
[[File:NSMB2 W5-Castle Red Pipe.png|thumb|185px|right|[[Fire Mario]] at the Red [[Warp Pipe]].]]
*'''Star Coin 1''': At the platform with two Bone Piranha Plants and a Dry Bones, there are some orange Question Blocks, and some [[Hidden Block]]s can be found above them. This allows the Mario Bros. to reach a higher platform to a Red Warp Pipe. Enter it to find the first Star Coin.
[[File:NSMB2 W5-Castle Star Coin.png|thumb|185px|right|Mario climbing towards the second Star Coin.]]
*'''Star Coin 2''': Right after the Checkpoint Flag, the second Star Coin is located beneath a Fence with two Climbing Koopas. The Fence it is on sinks into the lava, so the Mario Bros. must be careful not to fall into it.
[[File:NSMB2 World 5-Castle.png|thumb|185px|right|Mario near the final Star Coin.]]
*'''Star Coin 3''': Before jumping off to the smaller Fence on a track near the end of the level, the Star Coin is above the lava. The Mario Bros. will need to make sure the the taller Fence is on the higher portion of the track to collect it, and must get off before they fall into the lava.

== Trivia ==
*World 5-Castle is somewhat remiscient of [[Ludwig's Castle]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', as both of those castle's most prominent features are Fences and Climbing Koopas, and Ludwig serves as the boss in both of those castles.
