'''Bumper Brawl''' is a [[Team Minigame|Team]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The players inside the capsules attempt to hit each other and the allies inside the capsules drop to the square arena.

== Gameplay ==
The team must push their rivals off the arena, also stay in the arena. Players can charge up and slam the players. Players will hit the border thus be on midair which they may chant when they fall off or still stay in the arena. The last team standing wins the minigame.

== Ending ==
The winning team performs their victory while the losing team performs their loss on the arena.

==Controls==
*{{button|ns|stick}} – Move
*{{button|ns|jc-right}} – (Hold) Charge

==In-game description==
*"''Bump your rivals out of the arena!''"
*"''Hold {{button|ns|jc-right}} to charge up power, and then release it to slam it into your rivals!''"
==Trivia==
*Bumper Brawl is similar to Bumper Balls as both minigames involves pushing opponents out of the arena while inside/on top of balls.
