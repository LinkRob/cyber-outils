The '''Annual Plumbers' Dance''' is a social gathering of plumbers mentioned in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' live-action segment "[[Dance]]."

For [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]]'s birthday, [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] buys a gift certificate for both Luigi and himself to learn a dance lesson from [[Shabba-Doo]] in hopes of becoming the best dancers at the Annual Plumbers' Dance.
