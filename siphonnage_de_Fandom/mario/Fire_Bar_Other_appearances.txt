==''Yoshi'' appearances==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' / ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''===
[[File:Spinning Fireballs Yoshi.png|thumb|200px|A Fire-Bar in ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'']]
Fire-Bars appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its remake ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They are depicted as swinging three-dimensionally, rotating horizontally between the background and foreground. Fire-Bars appear in later forts and castles, such as [[Tap-Tap the Red Nose's Fort]].

===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
[[File:Podoboo Cog Sky.png|thumb|left|200px|A Fire Bar in ''Yoshi's Island DS'']]
Traditional Fire Bars appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They function more in line with their appearances in the 2D platforming titles of the {{series|Super Mario}}, though some Fire Bars can rotate in the air without being attached to a block or other surface. Fire Bars are commonly found in various levels of [[World 5 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 5]] such as [[Find the Number Ball!]], [[Superhard Acrobatics]], and [[At Last, Bowser's Castle!]].

==''Donkey Kong'' franchise==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''===
[[Image:Fire Rod Fire Stick Lava Dome.png|thumb|A Mini Mario near a Fire Stick/Rod.]]
Firebars (under the name '''Fire Rods''' in the USA version and '''Fire Sticks''' in other regions) appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''. They are commonly found in various levels of [[Lava Dome]] alongside [[Jetpack Guy]]s and [[Podoboo]]s. [[Mini Mario]]s have to time their movements around Firebars, as one can burn it instantly on contact. Firebars can be placed in the Construction Zone in the [[Lava Dome]] Kit as well as [[Special Kit 2]] and [[Special Kit 3]].

==''Wario'' franchise==
===''Wario: Master of Disguise''===
[[File:Fire Spinner Wario.png|thumb|left|A Fire Bar in ''Wario: Master of Disguise'']]
Fire Bars appear as obstacles inside [[Sweatmore Peak]] in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They consist of several small fireballs which rotate around a larger fireball.

==Crossover appearances==
===''The Legend of Zelda'' series===
Fire bars,<ref>''The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past TOP SECRETS - Messages from Sahasrahla'', page 2. "''The Moon Pearl is in the big chest on the fourth floor of the tower. To reach it, you need to jump into a hole on the fifth floor. The hole you need will appear when you step on the star tile near the fifth floor fire bar. Use the map to find the correct hole.''"</ref> or '''Guruguru bars''',<ref>''Playing With Super Power: Nintendo Super NES Classics eGuide'', ''The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past'' 16 Bits Tab. "''The rotating Guruguru bars that appear in ''A Link to the Past'' originated in ''Super Mario Bros.'', where they’re called Fire Bars. However, they were originally intended for ''The Legend of Zelda''; the developers felt they looked better in a ''Mario'' game and passed them between titles.''"</ref> appear in ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past]]'', ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time]]'', ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures]]'', ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap|The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap]]'', ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass|The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass]]'', ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks|The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks]]'', ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend w:c:zelda Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds]]'', and ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes|The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes]]''. As in the ''Mario'' franchise, they rotate continuously and are indestructible. However, some games play with this concept, such as with switches that change the direction of the bar's rotation. While 2D titles depict the center as a block, 3D titles redesign the center.

Fire bars were first implemented early in the development of ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|The Legend of Zelda]]'', where they rotated from the center of the screen; however, a developer suggested that it would be a better fit for ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', and it was agreed to transfer them across games.<ref>[https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/wii/nsmb/1/3/ Iwata Asks - New Super Mario Bros: Volume 2]</ref>

===''Mario & Sonic'' series===
====''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''====
[[File:DreamFigureSkating WinterGames.png|thumb|Fire Bars in ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'']]
In the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'', a pair of Fire Bars appear during the second segment of the Mario World routine in [[Dream Figure Skating]], where the characters jump over them.

====''Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games''====
Fire Bars appear during the [[Bowser's Castle]] portion of [[Mario's Figure Skating Spectacular]] in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'', where the characters jump over them at various stages during the routine.

===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
{{Smashpedia}}
[[File:SSB4 WiiU FireBar.jpg|thumb|left|200px|A Fire Bar in ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'']]
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'', ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Fire Bars appear as an item. Once picked up, characters can attack others by swinging them, similar to the [[Beam Sword]], although it is fairly powerful. However, every two hits from a Fire Bar causes it to lose a fireball, including blocked hits, shortening it in range. The fireballs closer to the base, however, deal more damage.
{{br}}

==Guest appearances==
===''Animal Crossing'' series===
The Fire Bar appears in the [[Animal Crossing (series)|''Animal Crossing'' games]] as a [[w:c:animalcrossing:Super Mario Set|furniture item]]. It plays the Castle theme from ''Super Mario Bros.'', but this can be turned off.

===''Minecraft''===
In the Super Mario Mash-up in ''[[Minecraft]]'', Blaze Rods are replaced by Fire Bars, though the Fire Bar is not connected to a block.
