'''Count Fang''' is the leader of the [[Fang]]s and a boss in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''. Count Fang was originally a normal-sized Fang before being enlarged by [[Kamek]]. Count Fang is the boss of [[World 2 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 2]] and the game’s second major boss. Defeating Count Fang will unlock [[World 3 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 3]].

==History==
After the [[Blue Yoshi]] and [[Baby Mario]] survive [[Count Fang's Castle]], they come face to face with Count Fang himself, [[Kamek]] then emerges to taunt Yoshi before using his [[Magic Hammer]] to enlarge Count Fang making him massive.

===Battle===
Count Fang begins the battle by flying to the ceiling of the chamber and roosting (when he does this he is immune to any attacks), this move calls several [[Purple Fang]]s who fly to their master, Count Fang then descends and his Purple Fang underlings will fly circling him as Count Fang flies towards Yoshi, Yoshi should aim an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] so it flies by the Purple Fangs and hits the Count himself, if it hits a Purple Fang, it will be eliminated leaving Count Fang open (the Purple Fangs can also be swallowed for eggs if Yoshi is running low), if Yoshi waits too long, Count Fang will roost again and call more Purple Fangs, upon being hit, Count Fang will descend to the ground and spin trying to ram into Yoshi, Yoshi should avoid this as being hit will cost him some [[Star (Yoshi's Island series)|Stars]] which will prevent a 100 Point score. After Count Fang is done with his spin attack, he will roost again and call more Purple Fangs, Count Fang will descend again with his minions, Yoshi should avoid them and hit Count Fang again, he will repeat the same process only faster this time before roosting again and calling even more Purple Fangs, hit Count Fang once more to finish him off.

==Trivia==
[[Image:Count Fang Beta Boss.jpg|thumb|right|Beta screenshot of a [[Green Yoshi]] facing Count Fang.]]
*In the game's trailer, Count Fang was shown being fought by a [[Green Yoshi]] rather than the [[Blue Yoshi]], the background of his room was also different resembling a cave more. The level he was fought in was also [[Cave of the Nipper Plants|World 1-3]] in the demo suggesting that Count Fang was originally going to be one of the first bosses.
*Count Fang's name may be a nod to [[wikipedia:Count Dracula|Count Dracula]], the vampire from the 1958 film ''[[wikipedia:Dracula (1958 film)|Dracula]]''.
