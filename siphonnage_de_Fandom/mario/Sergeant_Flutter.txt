'''Sergeant Flutter''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Physical description==
Sergeant Flutter is a grey-shelled [[Koopa Paratroopa|Sky Troopa]] who wears a helmet and goggles.

==History==
Sergeant Flutter is a reformed Sky Troopa who is summoned by [[Monstermama]] to help [[Mario]] scale a mountain in [[Land's End]] so that he can access [[Bean Valley]]. Being a good friend of Monstermama, Sergeant Flutter is more than willing to help out Mario. Sergeant Flutter calls up other Sky Troopas, and Mario has to jump off their shells in order to make it up the mountain.
