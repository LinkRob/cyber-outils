'''Snaggles''' (also called '''Nibbles''') are Shark enemies appearing in the ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' franchise first appearing in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]''. These Sharks live in the water below and will regularly hop out of the water attempting to chomp at anything crossing above, Nibbles can be quite a handful as getting past them requires timing however they aren’t invincible as they can be beaten by certain projectiles such as [[Barrel]]s.

==History==
===''Donkey Kong Country Returns / Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D / Donkey Kong Country Returns HD''===
Snaggles make their debut in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' and later go on to appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD]]''. In these games, Snaggles are primarily found in the [[Beach (Donkey Kong Country Returns)|Beach]] world and also in the [[Ruins (Donkey Kong Country Returns)|Ruins]] they will attack by leaping out of the water periodically in attempt to bite [[Donkey Kong]], [[Diddy Kong]], and [[Dixie Kong]], Nibbles are immune to most normal attacks but can be knocked away with [[Barrel]]s, however, it’s usually best to avoid them altogether. In the level [[Peaceful Pier]], there are two particular Snaggles that leap out of the water frantically attempting to hit DK while on the [[Rocket Barrel]], two subspecies of the Snaggles also appear in this game called [[Super Snaggles]], a yellow variant that can jump higher, and [[Mega Snaggles]], a much larger variant. In ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns HD]]'', Snaggles also appear in the new level [[Gushin' Geysers]].

===''Donkey Kong Country Tropical Freeze''===
Snaggles return in ''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]'' and it's [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze (Nintendo Switch)|Switch port]] this time appearing on several of the islands [[Donkey Kong]], [[Diddy Kong]], [[Dixie Kong]], and [[Cranky Kong]] traverse across on their way back to [[Donkey Kong Island]], they retain the same behaviors as before, leaping out of water in attempt to hit the Kongs and can once again be beaten with the use of [[Barrel]]s, alternatively, a Snaggles can be beaten if DK is with either Diddy, Dixie, or Cranky by preforming the [[Kong POW]] which turns them into [[Extra Life Balloon]]s, [[Yellow Heart]]s, or [[Banana Coin]]s (depending on who DK has as his partner at the time). In these games level [[High Tide Ride]], some Snaggles will take high leaps out of the ocean water below to hit the Kongs even taking off some of the Mine Cart tracks. In certain levels of [[Secret Seclusion]], some Snaggles even emerge from the clouds below. Additionally, when the Kongs are underwater and a Snaggles spots them, it will swim after them to attack instead, during these instances, [[Cranky Kong]] can beat them with a swipe of his cane, in this game, Snaggles are more common than they were previously appearing in levels of [[Lost Mangroves]], [[Bright Savannah]], [[Sea Breeze Cove]], [[Juicy Jungle]], and [[Secret Seclusion]]. The [[Super Snaggles]] also returns in this game.

==Trivia==
*Even though the Prima Guide for ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'' refers to Snaggles as Nibbles, they’re still called Snaggles in the [[Extras (Donkey Kong Country Returns)|Extras]] for the game.
*Snaggles are one of the few ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' enemies to return in ''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]''.
*The Prima Guides for ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'' group [[Jellybob]]s with Snaggles mostly due to their similar behaviors.
