'''''The Super Mario Challenge''''' was a game show that aired on The Children's Channel in the United Kingdom from Sptember to 20 December 1991.

==Format==
Each night, the series had two guest players who would have to play various games from the Super Mario franchise such as ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. The two players would compete to see who could get the most coins in a level or who could finish a level in the fastest time.
