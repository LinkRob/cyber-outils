The '''Minus Block''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party]]''. It resembles the red [[! Block]]s from ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. It can be bought at the [[Mushroom Shop]] for 100 [[coin]]s and is the opposite of the [[Plus Block]]. During a game, it will randomly appear on a turn. When it appears, the player hits the [[Dice Block]] to get a number. Whatever that number equals up to, the player loses in coins.
