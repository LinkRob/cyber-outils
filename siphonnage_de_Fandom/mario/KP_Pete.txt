'''KP Pete''', also commonly goes by his ring name '''King K''',  is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a competitor at the [[Glitz Pit]] in [[Glitzville]] and one of the first people to become friends with [[Mario]] there. He is also the leader of the [[KP Koopas]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
King K is a KP Koopa which is a yellow version of a [[Koopa Troopa]].

=== Personality traits ===
Unlike most characters in the Glitz Pit who are usually rude and competitive, King K is very friendly and doesn't show any hostility towards Mario. King K is also a good sport who isn't bothered by his loss to Mario unlike most of the others. He even offers advice to Mario whenever the latter comes from a fight. King K, however, does question his own strength at times and considered retiring. However, after he is rescued by Mario, King K decided not to retire anymore and becomes passionate about fighting again.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
When Mario, under the name of The Great Gonzales, first entered the minor league of the Glitz Pit, King K greeted him and introduced Mario to the other competitors. King K talked to Mario after winning every match he participated. Right before Mario fights the [[Punk Rocks]], King K tells him that he is thinking about retiring. After Mario defeats [[Master Crash]] and the [[Bob-omb Squad]], King K disappeared with the others, except [[Cleftor]], missing him.

Eventually, it turned out that King K saw [[Grubba]] using the [[Power-draining machine|draining machine]] and Grubba drained his energy from him. King K was later restored to himself and competed in the Glitz Pit again. Bandy Andy of the Hand-it-overs has the same happen to him.

==Trivia==
*In the original version of the game during the scene where everyone of the Glitz Pit was encouraging Mario, KP Pete's sprite was incorrectly replaced with that of a [[Shady Koopa]]'s. However, this was fixed in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]].
