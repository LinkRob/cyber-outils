'''Icicle Caverns''', or '''{{world|Frost|5}}''', is the fifth level of [[Frosted Glacier]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked via completion of [[Prickly Goombas!]] alongside [[Scaling the Mountside]]. Its own completion opens the path to [[Swaying Ghost House]]. It is also the only level with [[Ice Bro]]s.
==Layout==
The level begins above ground, with a [[Huge Icicle]] that is used to reach a [[Warp Pipe]] leading to an underground area. A row of [[icicle]]s is found through the Warp Pipe, along with a [[Buzzy Beetle]] and a [[lift]] with a [[? Block]] containing a power-up over it. A slope with Buzzy Beetles is found, as well as more icicles. More lifts are found above a pit, and a row of [[Brick Block]]s allows hidden [[coin]]s to be reached. A Huge Icicle is found, along with some more icicles and a [[Red Ring]] above a lift. A section with more lifts, icicles, and Huge Icicles follows. Some Buzzy Beetles are found, along with the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. More lifts are found, followed by an [[Ice Bro]] on a row of Brick Blocks. Another section with lifts and icicles follows. Another Ice Bro and more Huge Icicles are encountered. A Warp Pipe is found at the end of the area, leading to the [[Goal Pole]].
==Shortcut==
At the beginning of the level, just before the pipe, when the giant icicle falls, the player can use a [[P-Acorn]] or a [[Balloon Baby Yoshi]] to fly or float to reach a platform above the area. The player can continue to the right, where a group of coins is found. At the end of the section, the area with the [[Goal Pole]] is found, and the platform with the third Star Coin can be reached.
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''' ''(pictured)'': Near the beginning of the level, the player must find a small hidden area containing the Star Coin. The player must be quick; a giant icicle will block the player from reaching it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Not too far after the [[Checkpoint Flag]], the player must bounce off the first Ice Bro in order to reach the green [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Near the end of the level, the player must take the lower path before the giant icicles fall. The player can then use the Warp Pipe to take the player to the third Star Coin. If the giant icicle falls and not in time, the player could use a [[Super Acorn]], a [[Propeller Mushroom]], a [[Balloon Baby Yoshi]] or [[Peachette]] to reach the top path.
