'''Sunset Beach''' is a court in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
Sunset Beach is essentially [[Koopa Troopa Beach (court)|Koopa Beach]] during a sunset, as the only differences are that a [[Red Shell]] is the backboard for both teams' basketball hoop, not a [[Green Shell]], and no pirate ships appear as obstacles.

==In ''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''==
Sunset Beach is an unlockable court that can be unlocked by playing an exhibition match between the hours of four and six PM, according to the time set on the system's internal clock.

Sunset Beach is classified as part of the [[Mushroom Tourney]], despite never being played in said tourney.
