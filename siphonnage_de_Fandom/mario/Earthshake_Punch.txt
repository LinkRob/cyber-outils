'''Earthshake Punch''' is a move used by [[Wario]] in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Description==
Earthshake Punch can be used by shaking the [[Wii Remote]], although only when the [[Shake Meter]] is full. In doing so, Wario performs a powerful punch onto the ground, releasing a shockwave that can stun ground enemies temporarily, activate [[Shake Pillar]]s, or cause unsteady blocks to fall. After Wario uses the Earthshake Punch, the Shake Meter empties.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
In the boss fight against the [[Shake King]], he uses a move similar to Earthshake Punch, although the Shake King instead slams both of its fists onto the ground. Wario has to jump over the shockwave, otherwise he becomes temporarily shocked and defenseless.
