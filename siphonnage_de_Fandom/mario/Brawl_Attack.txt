'''Brawl Attacks''' are a genus of [[Special Attack]] that appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', though they are only named as such in the 3DS remaster.

Like their [[Bros. Attack|counterpart]] used by the [[Mario (character)|Mario]] [[Luigi|brothers]], Brawl Attacks require [[Special Point]]s to perform. These are labelled as SP for the legacy version and BP (most likely "'''''<u>Brawl</u>''''' Point") in the remaster. Only King [[Bowser]] can use this type of attack, and the command block he uses is orange with a [[Goomba]] pictured on it.

[[King Bowser|Bowser]] obtains six of these attacks through his journey to save his castle and minions from Lord [[Fawful]]. Some of these are also used by him during his boss battles.

==List of Brawl Attacks==
*[[Goomba Storm]]: Found in [[Dimble Wood]]. Bowser has to create a bridge from a [[Wiggler]] statue to reach the island. This is the only Brawl Attack that is mandatory, as if Bowser tries to leave the area, he has second thoughts: "Y'know, I really oughta help those [[Goomba]]s..."
**Procedure: The Goombas will charge from the west. Bowser has to use his fire breath to increase their power. To help him, tap the stylus, During the second phase, Bowser looks up as the flaming goombas stomp down toward his target. 18 are required for an Excellent! rating.
**Special Cost:  6
**Targeting:  Single (serial)
*[[Shy Guy Squad]]: Bowser can obtain this attack en route to [[Bowser Castle|his castle]] after going through [[Bumpsy Plains]] by powering through a treadmill in the fourth room to free his [[Shy Guy]] unit.
**Procedure: Bowser retreats into his shell which [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]] push into a rope that they use as a slingshot. The farther back he is pushed, the more damage he deals. Pushing back farther will lead to better ratings and more damage; this attack cannot end in a "Bad!" rating.
**Special Cost:  8
**Targeting:  Single
*[[Koopa Corps]]: After Bowser sneezes and sends a ship crashing through a wall, he can free the [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa Troopas]] trapped behind said wall in [[Blubble Lake]].
**Procedure: The Koopa Troopas charge at the targeted foe, retreating into their shells, and Bowser has to line up with the oncoming shells via the stylus to kick them back.
**Special Cost:  10
**Targeting:  Random
*[[Bob-omb Blitz]]: Bowser can find the [[Bob-omb]] squad trapped within the basement of his castle en route to his treasure vault.
**Procedure: Six Bob-ombs walk onto the screen. The player has to use the stylus to adjust their formation. After some time, the Bob-ombs will charge due east, blowing up on any enemy at their latitude. If all six strike the enemies, the player will receive an Excellent! rating.
**Special Cost:  12
**Targeting:  Choose
*[[Magikoopa Mob]]: Bowser can find his [[Magikoopa]] unit trapped within the [[Fawful|corrupted]] [[Princess Peach's Castle]] by mugging the [[Fawfulcopter]] who carries the red key for said key and using it on the red door.
**Procedure: The Magikoopas raise their wands in formation and the player must trace the formation to power each one up. After four attack rounds, the Magikoopas give up; if the player traced 21 or more wands successfully, an Excellent! rating is earned.
**Special Cost:  15
**Targeting:  Random
*[[Broggy Bonker]]: Bowser can gain this attack by saving every single [[Blitty]] that vanished and talking to [[Broque Monsieur]] in [[Dimble Wood]] Forest after he does so.
**Procedure: Broque Monsieur's [[Broggy]] emerges with his body covered with mud and dirt that Bowser has to scrub off. If the Broggy is cleaned completely, the player gets an Excellent! rating, and the Broggy will charge at the enemy he was sicced on, bouncing off the enemy in question, stomping on it, and charging at it from behind with the Blitties in tow.
**Special Cost:  20
**Targeting:  20
The targeting descriptors have the following meanings:

* Single: The attack targets one enemy.
* Single (serial): Same as Single except the game selects another random enemy to redirect remaining strikes into if the attack defeats the enemy before it concludes.
* Random: The attack hits enemies randomly.
* Choose: The player has to manually direct the attack into an enemy.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The name "Brawl Attack" most likely comes from a pun on the term "[[Bros. Attack]]" (another type of [[Special Attack]]) and the word "brawl."

== Trivia ==

* Strangely, the [[Chuboomba]] enemies are missing their lollipops in the Brawl Attack instructions.
