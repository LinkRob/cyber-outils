'''The Artist's Studio''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Artist's Studio has eight paintings lined up in a row along the back wall as well as a painting of a [[treasure chest]] on the right. Behind the easels are two-tiered shelves of art supplies, among which are sculptures of both [[Lydia]] and [[Biff Atlas]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Artist's Studio is the penultimate room of [[Area Four]]. Here, Luigi has to fight several waves of ghosts that [[Vincent Van Gore]] individually brings to life from the paintings, each spawning three [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] of a certain type. Luigi first has to fight three [[Gold Ghost]]s, followed by three [[Purple Puncher]]s, three [[Blue Twirler]]s, three [[Red Grabbing Ghost]]s, three [[Ghost Guy]]s, three [[Garbage Can Ghost]]s, and lastly three [[Purple Bomber]]s. Once Luigi has vacuumed up every wave of ghost, he can then vacuum Vincent Van Gore into his [[Poltergust 3000]]. After this, the spade-shaped [[Key]] to the [[Secret Altar]] spawns out of a painting of a treasure chest.

In PAL releases' and the Nintendo 3DS remake's [[Hidden Mansion]] mode, each wave instead consists of five ghosts.
