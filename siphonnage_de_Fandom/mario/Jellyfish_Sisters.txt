The '''Jellyfish Sisters''' are a duo of sisters named '''Gigi''' and '''Merri''', who appear minor characters in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The Jellyfish Sisters are users of the [[Firebrand]] and [[Thunderhand]] techniques, and they help [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] further the use of the techniques after discovering that they can also use it. They run a hand massage shop on [[Gwarhar Lagoon]]. The pairing of the Jellyfish Sisters are based on the Mario Bros., Mario and Luigi.

==Characters==
===Gigi===
[[File:Gigi.png|left]]
Gigi is the older of the two sisters. She will teach Mario how to use the Firebrand technique on Luigi. Gigi demonstrates this technique on Merri on how this new technique makes the duo run faster. Gigi then tells Mario and Luigi to use that technique to retrieve the red pearl for her. They do so and she is excited.

Her outfit is a red dress that covers her entire lower body. She is quite tall, about double Luigi's height, making her as tall Merri is. Gigi is comparable to Mario from being the oldest and wearing red clothing.

===Merri===
[[File:Merri.png|left]]
Merri is the youngest of the two sisters. She will teach Luigi how to use the Thunderhand technique on Mario. Merri demonstrates this technique on Gigi on how this new technique makes the duo walk together at the same time in the same sequence. Merri then tells Luigi and Mario to retrieve the green pearl for her. They do so and she is excited.

Her outfit is a green dress that covers her entire lower body. She is double Luigi's height, which makes her as tall as Gigi. Gigi is comparable to Luigi from being the youngest and wearing light green clothing.
