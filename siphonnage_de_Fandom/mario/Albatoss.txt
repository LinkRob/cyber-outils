'''Albatosses''' are bird enemies that appear in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its Western counterpart, ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. The enemy's name is a portmanteau of "{{wp|albatross}}" and "toss," which refers to its attack method.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario Bros. 2''====
[[File:Albatoss Sprite - Super Mario Bros. 2.png|frame|left]]
In ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', Albatosses used to be normal residents of [[Subcon]] until [[Wart]] took control.<ref>"''He used to only be a resident in the world of dreams. Now, by order of Wart, he works as a carrier of Bob-Ombs.''" - ''Super Mario Bros. 2'' instruction booklet, page 25.</ref> Albatosses carry [[Bob-omb|Bob-Omb]]s and drop them, attempting to hit one of the playable characters: [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]], or [[Toad]]. An Albatoss can be defeated if the player's character throws a [[vegetable]] at it or touches it under the [[Super Star|Starman]] effects. Unlike most other enemies, Albatosses cannot be picked up and thrown.

If an Albatoss flies low enough, the character can [[jump]] on its back and ride on it. [[World 6-2 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6-2]] and [[World 7-1 (Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 7-1]] require the player's character to ride Albatosses to reach otherwise inaccessible parts of the [[level]]; these Albatosses do not carry Bob-Ombs.

====''Super Mario Bros. 3'' reissues====
In the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]] and [[Game Boy Advance]] versions of ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', the [[kings|king]] of [[Sky Land|The Sky]] is turned into an Albatoss (in the original version, he is turned into a regular bird).

===''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''===
[[File:AlbatossCartoon.jpg|thumb|Three Albatosses carrying Bob-Ombs in the episode "[[The Bird! The Bird!]]" of ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'']]
{{quote|Albatoss leader to squadron, Bob-Ombs away!|Albatoss|"[[Jungle Fever]]"}}
Albatosses make a few appearances in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' They are usually used as bombers by [[Bowser|King Koopa]], and they sometimes capture Mario and company in nets. Albatosses are also seen in the show's intro. In this show, they have a dark-brownish head, instead of matching their bodies' red color.

In the episode "[[Jungle Fever]]," Mario and his friends ride an Albatoss to get off the bridge King Koopa sliced. The one in this episode is shown to be able to speak, as shown after getting a [[Birdo's Egg|Birdo egg]] on its face. [[Koopa's rocket]] also slightly resembles the head of an Albatoss. Additionally, the character [[Hooded Robin]] somewhat resembles an Albatoss.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'', Albatosses move at two frames, while in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', they move at seven frames; an eighth frame exists, showing the wings moving back upwards, but is unused due to a [[glitch]].<ref>[https://tcrf.net/Super_Mario_Bros._2_(NES)#Eighth_Animation_Frame The Cutting Room Floor]</ref> The ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Advance]]'' remakes restore the missing frame.
*In the ''[[Perfect Edition of the Great Mario Character Encyclopedia|Perfect Ban Mario Character Daijiten]]'', Albatosses are erroneously referred to as pterosaurs and simultaneously listed as "dinosaurs" for their species category.<ref name=Daijiten/> While modern scientific consensus is that birds {{wp|Origin of birds|are indeed a subspecies of dinosaur}}, pterosaurs were phylogenetically separate reptiles.
