A '''Grand Star Coin''' is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
A Grand Star Coin is a [[Skill Item]] that can raise the Skill level of a teammate whose Skill level had been increased to level 4 using [[Star Coin]]s, including most forms of [[Mario]] and all variations of [[Hammer Bro]]s., [[Coin Coffer]]s, [[Thwomp]]s, and [[Whomp]]s respectively.

A Grand Star Coin can be obtained either from opening a [[? Block]] at random, as a prize from the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] minigame, or from defeating either a "Thwomp & [[Thwimp]]s" or a "Whomp & [[Boo]]" with a 10+ Combo attack.
