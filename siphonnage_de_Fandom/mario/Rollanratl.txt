'''Rollanratl''' is a boss in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Physical description==
Rollanratl is a giant, dark green, living stone statue.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Rollanratl is fought as the first boss. [[Wario]] fights it in [[Rollanratl Battle]], located in [[Ratl Ruins]], where Rollanratl can be found holding up the ceiling.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Rollanratl's name is derived from the phrase "roll and rattle."
