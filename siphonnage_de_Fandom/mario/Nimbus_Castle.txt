'''Nimbus Castle''' is a location within [[Nimbus Land]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is the home of [[King Nimbus]] and [[Queen Nimbus]]. During the plot, the castle is taken over by [[Valentina]], and [[Mario]] and his party have to return control of it to the [[Nimbus]] royalty.
