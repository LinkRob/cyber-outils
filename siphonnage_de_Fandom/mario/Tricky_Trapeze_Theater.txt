==Features==
{{sectstub}}

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Swing left while holding on the third trapeze to reach the invisible cloud leading towards the star.
*'''Green Star 2''': After the checkpoint, head to the [[Warp Pipe]] at the far right while passing some Piranha Plants. After warping, hit the [[P Switch]] and collect all the blue coins to reveal the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': After exiting the clear pipe, go on the left most trapeze to swing to the Green Star Ring. After getting it, collect all the eight coins to reveal the star.

==Stamp==
[[File:Tricky_Trapeze_Theater_Stamp.png|thumb]]After bouncing away from the spinning platforms, go on the left side of the stairs to get the Luigi Stamp.
