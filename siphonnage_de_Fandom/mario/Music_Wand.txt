A '''Music Wand''' is an item in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Description==
When used, a Music Wand lowers the difficulty of a song by one level. Naturally, it is not available in the Easy difficulty level.

==History==
In Story Mode, the Music Wand can be bought at any shop for 200 [[coin]]s.
