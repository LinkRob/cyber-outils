'''Lakitea''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.

==Description==
Lakitea is a healing item that, when consumed, fully restores [[Mario]]'s, [[Luigi]]'s, and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]]'s [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Bros. Point|BP]].

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam''==
Lakitea is only found in the [[Arcade (Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam)|Arcade]]. It can be purchased from the [[Lakitu]] running the Arcade café for 20 [[coin]]s. Lakitea takes immediate effect upon being purchased, so it cannot be stored in the inventory.
