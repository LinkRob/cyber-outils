The '''Red-Hot Pepper Detector''' is an object in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Love 'Em and Leave 'Em]]."

==Description==
The Red-Hot Pepper Detector is a red, pepper-shaped device owned by [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], and its function is to alert whenever a large patch of hot [[pepper]]s is nearby.
