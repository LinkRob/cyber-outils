'''Dark Spiky Skellobit''' are a variant of [[Spiky Skellobit]]s and an enemy featured in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
Dark Spiky Skellobits are found in a few rooms of the [[Flopside Pit of 100 Trials]]. They are stronger versions of Spiky Skellobits. They attack with a charging spear attack. Using jumping attacks is not recommended due to the spikes on their unless but [[Carrie]] can be still used to take them out from jump attacks.
