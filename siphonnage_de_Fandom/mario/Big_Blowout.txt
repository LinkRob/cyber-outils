'''Big Blowout''' is both a 4-player and a [[Duel Minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Layout==
Each of the four characters stand in front of their own row of lit [[candle]]s on a table with a red tablecloth. In the Duel variation, since there are only two players competing, this means that there are only two rows of candles.

==Gameplay==
Players must blow on the Mic to blow out lit flames on the candles ahead. The more air that is blown by the player, the more flames they can extinguish at once. Up to three candles can be blown out at the same time. However, players can only blow out candles ahead while standing on an unlit candle, not while jumping. The first player to extinguish all their candles and make it to the end wins. Like other minigames without a [[time limit]], if none of the characters reach the end within 5 minutes, something that can only occur if all of the human players are idle, it is a tie.

==Ending==
The winner performs their victory animation in front of a bunch of lit candles. The losing players are not seen, unless a tie occurs, in which case every character does their losing animation in front of the candles.

==Controls==
*{{button|ds|mic}} &ndash; Blow out the candles
