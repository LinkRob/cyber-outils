'''Balloon''' is the name for several subjects in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media:

*[[Balloon (Pipe Down!)|Balloon]], an item in ''[[Pipe Down!]]''.
*[[Balloon (platform)|Balloon]], a platform used in various ''Mario''-related games, namely the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}
*[[Balloon (Mini Battle)|Balloon]], an object featured in [[Popping Balloons]] and [[Throwing Balloons]] in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.
*[[Balloon (Mario Kart series)|Balloons]], an item in the {{series|Mario Kart}}, particularly within battle modes
*[[Balloon (minigame)|Balloon]], a minigame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.
*[[Balloon (Donkey Kong Barrel Blast)|Balloon]], an object that grants an item in ''[[Donkey Kong Barrel Blast]]''.
*[[Balloon (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Balloon]], an item in the {{series|Mario & Sonic}} which originated from the ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' franchise.
*[[Balloon (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|Balloon]], an object in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' that yields five or six [[Star Bit]]s upon breaking.
*[[Balloon (Thing)|Balloon]], a [[Thing]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.
*[[Balloon (Mario Kart Arcade GP VR)|Balloon]], an object that acts similarly to [[Item Box]]es in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP VR]]''.
