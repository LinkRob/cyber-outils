'''Bullet Train Blast''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''. It can be played through [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020#Story Mode|Story Mode]] or in the [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020#Game Room|Game Room]].

==Gameplay==
[[File:M&S Tokyo 2020 Bullet Train Blast.png|250px|thumb|Gameplay]]
[[Dr. Eggman]] is sitting inside a bullet train, and [[Sonic]] must race it to its destination. At the start of the minigame, the player must press and hold {{button|ns|A}} for Sonic to charge up a [[Spin Dash (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Spin Dash]]. Once released, the player must repeatedly press {{button|ns|A}} for Sonic to run. While running, Sonic must avoid [[Boulder (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020)|boulders]], [[Spike (Sonic the Hedgehog)|spikes]], and [[Bumper (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Bumpers]], which all slow him down. Along the way, he can collect two items from monitors: [[Shield (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Shield]] and [[Invinciblity (Sonic the Hedgehog)|Invinciblity]]. If the bullet train reaches its destination first, Sonic fails the minigame.
