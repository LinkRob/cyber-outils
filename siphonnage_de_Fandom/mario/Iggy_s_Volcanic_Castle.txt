'''Iggy's Volcanic Castle''', is the castle of [[Soda Jungle]], in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is filled with [[Dry Bones]] and [[lava]]. This is the only level in the game where the lava creates waves, a method reused from [[World 8-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-3]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. 
==Level overview==
The level begins on a platform above [[Lava Wave]]s. A section of the ground that moves up and down is found, along with a [[Dry Bones]] and a [[? Block]] containing a power-up. Some pits of lava are found in between moving platforms, and more Dry Bones are found. Another ? Block containing a power-up is found, and is followed by a row of Dry Bones with a [[Super Dry Bones]] at the end. More moving platforms are found, leading to a stationary platform with the [[Checkpoint Flag]] on it. More platforms that raise up and down are found, along with a [[Red Ring]]. Dry Bones and Super Dry Bones are also encountered. A section of moving platforms with [[Podoboo]]s in between them is found leading up to the boss door.

The boss door leads to a cannon. After being launched out of the cannon, the player proceeds to fight [[Iggy Koopa]]. During the battle, Iggy uses Warp Pipes to travel around the room, and is able to run on the ceiling. He uses his wand to not only shoot green fireballs, but red skeleton orbs as well. These orbs will spawn [[Magmaargh]]s as soon as they touch the lava. After being jumped on three times, Iggy is defeated.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Just before the [[Checkpoint Flag]], the player must freeze the nearby Dry Bones with an [[Ice Flower]] and toss it at the first [[Star Coin]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': Following the section with the Red Ring, three [[Warp Pipe]]s are found above platforms. The middle Warp Pipe can be accessed by either bouncing off a Dry Bones or freezing one and using it as a platform. Inside that pipe is [[POW Block]] that is used to bring down the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The player can go down a Warp Pipe hidden in the lava. The Warp Pipe leads to a room in which the player can bounce off the Super Dry Bones walking on the lava to get the third Star Coin.
