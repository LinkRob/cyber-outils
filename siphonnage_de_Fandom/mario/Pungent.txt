'''Pungent''' is the [[Puni]] shopkeeper of the hidden [[Item Shop]] found inside [[The Great Tree]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
During the [[X-Naut]] invasion of The Great Tree, Pungent was one of the few Punies not to be captured by the extraterrestrial team of villains. He hid in his shop, until the entrance was blown away by [[Mario]]'s partner, [[Flurrie]]. Recognizing that Mario was there to help, Pungent aided Mario by selling him rare items such as [[HP Drain]]s or [[Ice Storm]]s.
