'''Royal Rumpus''' is a Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
Both characters have to [[Ground Pound]] as many [[Goomba]]s as they can within a [[time limit]] of 15 seconds. If a character only [[stomp]]s on a Goomba, they do not earn any [[point]]s. Besides regular Goombas, which are each worth one [[point]], there are the less common [[Gold Goomba]]s, which are not only worth three points but also run faster than regular Goombas. It is possible to Ground Pound more than one Goomba at once. A character can [[jump]] onto their opponent to stun them temporarily. The character with the most points by the end of the minigame wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
*{{button|gcn|A}}: Jump
*{{button|gcn|A}} (while in midair): Ground Pound

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''Ground Pound the Goombas to earn points! The player with the most points wins!''
*'''Advice''': ''Gold Goombas are worth 3 points each. They move quickly, but are well worth the effort.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on the phrase "royal ruckus."
