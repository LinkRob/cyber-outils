==History==
The Cheep Blimp is the main form of transportation to take to [[Glitzville]] as it is located in the skies. However, one must have [[Blimp Ticket]]s in order to board the Cheep Blimp. [[Mario]] can get some Blimp Tickets after helping of [[Don Pianta]] in [[Rogueport|West Rogueport]]. The Cheep Blimp is operated by a [[Cheep Cheep]] named [[Stewart]].
