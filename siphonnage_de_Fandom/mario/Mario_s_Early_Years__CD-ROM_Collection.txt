'''''Mario's Early Years! CD-ROM Collection''''' is a compilation disc consisting of three {{series|Mario Discovery}} games developed by [[The Software Toolworks]]: ''[[Mario's Early Years! Fun with Letters]]'', ''[[Mario's Early Years! Fun with Numbers]]'', and ''[[Mario's Early Years! Preschool Fun]]''. The former two games are identical to their original releases, although the ''Mario's Early Years! CD-ROM Collection'' version of ''Mario's Early Years! Preschool Fun'' adds another world, [[Time World]].
