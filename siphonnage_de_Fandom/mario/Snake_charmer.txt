'''Snake charmers''' are enemies in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==In ''Wario World''==
[[Wario]] only encounters snake charmers in [[Wonky Circus]]. Here, a snake charmer can always be found riding either an [[Elephant]] or a [[Cobra]]. Snake charmers themselves are weak, and can be defeated from any attack. Before a snake charmer can be attacked, Wario must lift the enemy they are riding so that the snake charmer falls off.
