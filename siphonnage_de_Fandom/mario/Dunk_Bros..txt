'''Dunk Bros.''' is a Rare [[minigame|mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''. It is unlocked in solo mode by clearing the board [[Thirsty Gulch]]. 

==Gameplay==
The gameplay essentially consists of a two-on-two basketball game, making it like a 2-vs.-2 mini-game in this regard. Before starting, the player has the option to choose the duration of the minigame, which can range from 60 seconds to five minutes. The object for each team is to score as many [[point]]s as they can before [[time limit|time]] runs out. There are a few moves that can be performed, including passing the basketball to an ally, stealing the ball from an opponent, and shooting the ball into the hoop. A character can also [[jump]] onto an opponent, temporarily squishes the opponent and making them unable to jump. Like in regular basketball, scoring a hoop earns the player's character two or three points, depending on how far the ball is thrown from the basket. Whoever scores more points when time runs out wins the match.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move while dribbling
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Shoot or jump
* {{button|gcn|B}}: Pass to a teammate or steal
* {{button|gcn|X}}: High pass
