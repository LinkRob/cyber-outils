'''King Windbag''' is a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Life's Ruff]]." He is the ruler of [[Ice Land]] who lives in a palace made of ice. He was first seen yelling at a servant of his for cooking the wrong meal. He then gets an unexpected visit from Luigi, who was sent by Princess Toadstool.
