==History==
Paper can be only obtained during the [[Piccolo]] sidequest from [[Old Man Watchitt]]. The Paper is used to get an autograph from [[Merlumina]] for Watchitt. Afterwards, Watchitt will give the heroes the [[You-Know-What]] which then needs to be given to [[Bestovius]].
