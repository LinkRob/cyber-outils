'''Twilight House''' is one of the six [[Battle Stage]]s in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. It is in the middle of a forest, but has many areas where racers are at risk of falling into a [[pit]]. In [[Mission Mode]], one of the missions takes place here, Mission 3-8, in which the player takes control of [[Mario]], who has to collect 20 [[coin]]s within 65 seconds. Changes to the layout include barriers and [[Banana]]s scattered around the map.

Twilight House returns in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' as a Retro Battle Stage, and it is titled {{classic course|DS|Twilight House}}. The course has been graphically updated with very little changes other than that.
