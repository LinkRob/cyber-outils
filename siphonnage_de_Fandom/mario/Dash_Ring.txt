The {{Nihongo|'''Dash Ring'''|ダッシュリング|Dasshu Ringu|Dash Ring}} debuted as the special item from several drivers in [[Mario Kart Tour]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
[[File:Dash_ring.jpg|thumb|right|[[Rosalina]] right before a Dash Ring.]]
This item allows three Dash Rings appear which can be driven through by anyone, even by opponents.

* [[Blue Yoshi]]

* [[Penguin Toad]]
* [[Mario (Aviator)]]
* [[Rosalina Mii Racing Suit]]
==Mario Kart World==
There are a variety of Dash Rings in this game. The nature-themed ones are hung by butterflies whose thick bodies are tied in green vines.
