A '''Jar''' is an obstacle featured on the [[Shy Guy Bazaar]] course in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', later returning in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart World]]''.

==Description==
Jars are light green, are decorated with a zigzag stripe and diamonds both colored dark green, and have a lid on top. In ''Mario Kart Tour'', some of the Jars contain a snake.

If a character drives into a jar, it breaks, and an item flies out, usually a [[Banana]]. The resulting crash also slows the racer briefly.
