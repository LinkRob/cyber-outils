Dexter "Dex" Manley is an American actor. In the Mario series he voiced [[Lakitu]] from [[Mario Superstar Baseball]] to [[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]. He was later replaced by [[Tadd Morgan]] in [[Mario Kart 7]].

== Voices ==

* [[Lakitu]] (2005-2013)
* [[Charlie]] (Mario Golf)
