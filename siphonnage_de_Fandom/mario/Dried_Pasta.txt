'''Dried Pasta''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
This item can only be found at [[Little Mouser's Shop]] at [[Dry Dry Outpost]], where it is sold for five [[coin]]s. By itself, the item restores both [[Mario]]'s [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Flower Point|FP]] by 3. However, item gives Mario some effective [[recipe]]s. When cooked with an [[Ultra Shroom]] or a [[Dried Fruit]], Mario gets the [[Yummy Meal]]. [[Koopasta]] is created by mixing this item with a [[Koopa Leaf]]. By cooking this item by itself, Mario is given a [[Spaghetti]] item. Many other items can be cooked with this, three of them are the colored berries from [[Flower Fields]], and get the [[Bland Meal]] item.
