A '''Green Star Coin''' is an item in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''.

==Description==
A Green Star Coin is a [[Skill Item]] that can raise the Skill level of either [[Luigi]], any of Luigi's forms, [[Spiny|Spinies]], and all variations of [[Bullet Bill]]s, [[Wiggler]]s, [[Chain Chomp]]s and [[Flame Chomp]]s. However, Green Star Coins can only upgrade up to level 4, and their variation, [[Grand Green Star Coin]]s, are required to increase the any of the aforementioned teammates' Skill level beyond 4.

Green Star Coins can be obtained by either winning the [[Lucky ? Blocks]] minigame in a [[Toad House]] or by opening a [[? Block]] in certain levels.
