A '''Spiny Fish''' is a pink fish enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. It has the same Japanese name as a [[Spiny Cheep-Cheep]]. Spiny Fish only appear in one level, [[Lots O'Jelly Fish]], and because of a Spiny Fish's spiked body, if a Yoshi eats one, he loses some health.
