The '''Plunger''' is a [[Thing]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. When Mario first speaks to the [[Wringer]], it is given for free. After the free one is given, It can be purchased from the [[Wringer]] for 150 [[Coin|coins]].

=== Know-it-All Toad's Hint ===
"You must seek a Thing that goes "plorp." You keep it in your bathroom, and you hope you never need it."

== Battle Card ==
If the Plunger [[Battle Card]] is used in battle, the setting transforms into a nighttime city with jazz music. It plunges the ground as it approaches enemies, flying upwards and lowering itself onto them. The cup covers enemies of all sizes, and the plunger moves up and down to drain paint out of them. This weapon is only effective against one enemy at a time. Once all enemies are plunged it flies off screen, causing the setting to return to normal.
[[File:PMCS Plunger battle.png|center|frameless|420x420px]]

== Indigo Underground ==
The Plunger is used at the Indigo Underground to free a [[Rescue Squad]] member and enter the stage. 

=== Use ===
When Mario first attempts to go down the [[Warp Pipe]] in the beginning of the stage, he is blocked by the Green [[Rescue Squad]] Leader. Any attempt to pull him out will not work. Mario must use [[Cutout]] and place the Plunger card in the given slot. If the card is correctly entered, a plunger will lower over the pipe and plunge out the Green [[Rescue Squad]] Leader. This will free him and give access to the rest of the level.
