'''Gold Paragoomba''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It is a variant of both [[Gold Goomba]] and [[Paragoomba]].

==In ''New Super Mario Bros. 2''==
Whenever a [[Gold Ring]] is activated, all Paragoombas become Gold Paragoombas. They act the same as Paragoombas, by hopping on the ground slowly, but yield more [[coin]]s upon being defeated.
