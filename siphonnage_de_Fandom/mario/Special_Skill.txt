A '''Special Skill''' is a special move that minions can use in ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' and ''[[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. The frequency at which the minion uses this move is determined by their SPIRIT stat. All Special Skills can be denied for 1 CP.
