'''Capnaps''' are enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They can commonly be found in [[Mushrise Park]]. Capnaps return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Capnaps appear to have a full body of feathers with three different colors, from top to bottom: yellow, yellow-orange, and orange. This coat covers their whole body, making them appear somewhat like a bush. Capnaps also have two eyes right in front of their yellow feathers. At the top of their bodies, the enemies have two green pieces of grass, at the bottom are brown feet under their feathers.

===Behavior===
In the field, like any other enemy they will to chase after [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] once they spot them. However, Capnaps also appear to be drowsy due to their habit of sleeping for a few seconds (with a snot bubble coming from beneath their eyes for a comedic effect). If Mario and Luigi get closer around this enemy, it will wake up, heading on their tail.

Capnaps can attack in combat by simply charging straight towards both Mario and Luigi in the foreground or the background, with the camera moving to get a better view of their charge. The Bros. must both jump to evade the attack. When jumping they can counter on the Capnap. If one of them manages to counter, it will basically stop and return to its post. Another attack is when, Capnap gets drowsy, falls, and sleepwalks towards a bro. It will start teetering when it towards a bro and then walk straight forward hurting Mario or Luigi. The targeted bro must time the Capnap's teetering and hammer the Capnap before it hurts him. (If the target swings his hammer too quickly, the Capnap has a chance to wake up and stop the attack early.)

==History==

=== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' ===
Capnaps are located in [[Mushrise Park]] south below [[Pi'illo Castle]]. Their [[Dream World]] equivalent is [[Dreamcap]]s, which have a different behaviour in combat; as such, Dreamcaps have their own page.

=== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'' ===
Capnaps make a return in this game, though their Dream World equivalent does not return. Mario and Luigi encounter them at the [[Lushgreen Sea]]'s [[Great Lighthouse Islands|Great Lighthouse Island]]. In this game, Capnaps retain their behaviour from ''Dream Team''. They are also one of the projectiles that can be spat out when the [[Gobblick]] performs his spit attack, others including [[Spikely|Spikelies]], [[Coin|Coins]], and [[Mushroom (item)|Mushrooms]]. Later in the game, Capnaps may get infected with a toxic hatred-generating substance called [[Glohm]], buffing their stats.

==== Variations ====
Stronger, blue Capnaps called Capnap R's can be found in the underground tunnels on [[Bulbfish Island]]. They have the same attacks as their yellow brethren, but with a fourth technique that involves them healing their allies. As with the yellow Capnaps, they contract Glohm later in the game; however, this happens when [[Reclusa]] awakens to [[Reclusa World|conquer Concordia]].

== Statistics ==

=== In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' ===
The Capnap is an enemy of Level 5 with 16 hit points, an attack power of 40, a defense of 38, and a speed of 23. When slain, it gives 15 Experience, with its loot drops including 5 coins and having a 5% chance to include a regular [[Mushroom]] and a 2% chance to include a regular [[Star Candy]].

=== In ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'' ===
This game has the Capnap buffed slightly in some areas, due to the creature appearing slightly later than in ''Dream Team''. This Capnap is of Level 11, with 73 hit points, an attack power of 38, a defense of 18, a speed of 35. When slain, the Capnap gives 32 Experience and drops 15 Coins along with a chance of a Mushroom or a Nut (3% and 1% chances respectively). A Glohm Capnap is of Level 34 with 648 hit points, an attack power of 167, a defense of 87, and a speed of 160. When destroyed, it gives 356 experience, 61 coins, and possibly the following items:

* Super Mushroom (3%)
* Ultra Mushroom (1%)
* Glohm Dust (4%)
* Glohm Fragment (8%)
* Glohm Crystal (9%)

The Capnap R is of Level 19 with 164 hit points, an attack power of 71, a defense of 34, and a speed of 61, giving 121 experience and 34 coins along with possibly a jar of syrup (4% chance; 75% for regular syrup and 25% for Super Syrup out of all syrup jar drops for totals of 3% and 1%) or a Bulbfish Lantern (21%) when slain. In their Glohm-infected state, their level is 40, with 918 hit points, and they drop 814 experience and 111 coins when destroyed.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Capnaps are named after the word "catnap," which describes a brief, light sleep (which is ironic considering their drowsiness). The change from T to P comes from the word "cap."

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Capnaps are known as ''Nemurudake'' (written 	ネムルダケ in Katakana), from ''nemuru'' ("to sleep"; written 眠るin Kanji and ねむる in the Hiragana script) and ''take'' (a translation of "mushroom"; written 茸 in Kanji and たけ Hiragana). Their Chinese name, ''Hūnhūnyùshuì Gū'' (written 昏昏欲睡菇), means "sleepy mushroom." Spanish translates their name as ''Modorrón'', which is a masculine noun derived from ''modorra'' ("drowsiness") alluding to them being drowsy. Portuguese calls them ''Sonomelo'', from ''sonolento'' ("sleepy," alluding to their drowsiness) and ''cogumelo'' ("mushroom"). In Italian, their name is ''Gapibapo'', referring to the Italian television mascot ''Gabibbo'', who has a vaguely similar expression and is derived from a Genoese ironic-depriciative expression. Their French name, ''Roupignon'', is a pun on ''roupiller'' ("to sleep") and ''champignon'' ("mushroom"). In German, Capnaps are called ''Nickerling'', from ''nickerchen'' ("to sleep"). They are called ''Kkeubeoksong'i'' (written 꾸벅송이), from ''kkeubeok-kkeubeok'' (an onomatopoeia for dozing off, written 꾸벅꾸벅) and ''song'i'' (written 송이), which refers to the matsutake mushroom.
