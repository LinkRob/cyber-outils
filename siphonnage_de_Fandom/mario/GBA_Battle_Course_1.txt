'''Battle Course 1''' is one of the four [[Battle Course]]s in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''. It is very simply themed as not much is in it. It is typically themed like the Donut Plains-related courses. There is multicolored walls in the area with sand in the middle of the course. It is simply a smaller version of [[SNES Battle Course 4]].

It reappears in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' as a retro Battle Course. [[Rocky Wrench]]es have been added to amp up the difficulty of the course, along with a speed ramp where tricks can be performed upon in the center of the course where the sand is.
