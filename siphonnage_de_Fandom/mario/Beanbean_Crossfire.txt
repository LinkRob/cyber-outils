'''Beanbean Crossfire''' is the seventh level of [[Beanbean Kingdom]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 9.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 7 [[Shy Guy]]s and 1 [[Parabeanie]]. The enemy captain is the Parabeanie.

The final battle contains 7 [[Fire Stalking Piranha Plant]]s and 1 Parabeanie. The enemy captain is the Parabeanie.

Replaying the level can allow the player to unlock up to one Fire Stalking Piranha Plant, and or Shy Guy.
