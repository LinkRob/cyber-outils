A '''Needle Bomb''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

==Description==
The Needle Bomb involves using a red fish resembling [[Porcupuffer]]. After aiming on to an opponent, the Needle Bomb travels toward the opponent, latches on their vehicle, and pops both tires on the right side of the [[kart]]. travels to its opponent when it is homed correctly. It latches onto the opponent and pops two of the opponent's tires on the right side of the [[kart]]. The initial strike causes the kart to stop briefly, and when the tires are popped, the opponent loses both control and speed. After a while, the tires recover, wearing off the effect.
