'''Mad Goombas''' are a variant of [[Goomba]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. They are a type Goombas that is native to [[Vibe Island]] and due to its magic, are permanently angry. Their outside appearance is the same as a normal Goomba, but they are reddish with an angry expression. When they stomp their feet, an earthquake follows, stunning [[Princess Peach|Peach]] temporarily if she is nearby. Mad Goombas aren’t very strong enemies however and virtually any attack can destroy them. The blue [[Sad Goomba]]s are the opposite of the Mad Goombas.
