'''Big Ape City''' is the fourth and final world of ''[[Donkey Kong Land]]''.

==Summary==
While most areas in the ''[[Donkey Kong (series)|Donkey Kong]]'' series lack modern civilization, this area does with skyscrapers, office building and other buildings. Although, it is said to take place in the area where the original ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' took place in the game such as [[25m]]. There is also a blimp saying "DK" on it where [[King K. Rool]] is fought to wrap up the game.

== Etymology ==
Big Ape City's name parodies [[wikipedia:The Big Apple|The Big Apple]], a landmark in New York City.
