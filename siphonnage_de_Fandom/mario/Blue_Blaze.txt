'''Blue Blazes''' are [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] enemies in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Blue Blazes are a variant of [[Blue Twirler]]s with a semi-transparent white body and a heart protected by an orb-shaped [[water]] element.

===Behavior===
Blue Blazes have a massive ground-slamming attack that costs [[Luigi]] ten [[Heart Point|HP]] if he is caught within range, as with Blue Twirlers.

==History==
They are normally found only in the [[Safari Room]], in the east wing of the third floor of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]]. Specifically, the Blue Blazes and two [[Garbage Can Ghost]]s appear after Luigi shakes the middle deer head. One Blue Blaze is encountered in the center of the [[Tea Room]] during the [[blackout]] and two in the [[Telephone Room]] in the PAL [[Hidden Mansion]].

Blue Blazes are vulnerable to [[Ice (element)|ice]], so if Luigi can release any from the [[Poltergust 3000]], this exposes the Blue Blaze's heart. Afterward, Luigi can proceed to vacuum the Blue Blaze into the Poltergust 3000. Blue Blazes have 30 HP.

==Flavor text==
===''Luigi's Mansion'' (Nintendo 3DS)===
*'''In-game description:''' "''A Blue Twirler with the Water Element. It's weak to ice attacks.''"

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The term "blue blazes" is a euphemism for Hell, used in expressions such as "What in blue blazes?"
