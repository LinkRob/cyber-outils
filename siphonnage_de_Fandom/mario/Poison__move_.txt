|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Poison''' is a move used by certain enemies in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Poison is used, the target is hit by poisonous green spores.

==Effects==
With Poison, it is able to inflict the Poisoned ailment onto one of Mario's party. This attack can also deal moderate damage. However, this can be nullified by wearing a certain badge to prevent poisoning.
