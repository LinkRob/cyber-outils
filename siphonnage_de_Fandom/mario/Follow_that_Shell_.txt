'''Follow that Shell!''' is the final level in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is also the ninth and final level in [[Superstar Road]].

==Layout==
The course is based off of the sky levels from [[Meringue Clouds]]. Throughout the level, [[Koopa Shell]]s can be thrown to defeat enemies and break blocks, allowing the player to progress and collect Star Coins. A Koopa Troopa is found at the beginning of the level, as well as some [[Goomba]]s and a structure of Brick Blocks. Another Koopa Troopa is found, along with a series of Goombas and Koopa Troopas on platforms. Another Koopa Troopa appears in the next section, where [[Hidden Coin]]s and a Brick Block containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] are found. The Koopa Troopa's shell can travel through a section with Brick Blocks and slopes, where Koopa Troopas and Goombas are found. A [[Bouncy Cloud]] is found, followed by a slope with Goombas on it. A structure of Brick Blocks is then found, and the [[Goal Pole]] is located after it.

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': When the stage begins, the player needs to throw the nearby Koopa into a cluster of Brick Blocks so the path to the Star Coin can be revealed.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Near the end of the stage, the player needs to throw a shell on to the brick platform. Mario can use a [[P Switch]] before the shell reaches the point where the Star Coin can be collected by the shell.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Directly after the second Star Coin, the player needs to hit a block right below the final Star Coin. The block will release a [[Vine]] which will take the player to the coin.
