'''Grace''' is a female golfer in the ''[[Mario Golf (series)|Mario Golf]]'' series. She first appears in ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]]

==In the ''Mario Golf'' series==
Grace is a playable character in all of her appearances. Aside from being playable in ''Mario Golf'' for the Game Boy Color, Grace is also playable in ''[[Mobile Golf]]'' and ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]''. In ''Mobile Golf'', Grace is an unlockable character, and she can be unlocked by winning against her in Club Slots.
