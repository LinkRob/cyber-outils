The '''Starshade Bros.''' are a pair of army brothers that worked in the [[Stardust Fields]]. The red one is General Starshade and the green one is Sergeant Starshade. They only appear in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==History==
The Starshade Bros. teach [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] how to perform a [[High Jump]] and [[Spin Jump]] as well as [[Bros. Attack]]s.

Later, upon the defeat of [[Tolstar]], Sergeant Starshade shot [[Bowser]] at him out of a cannon.
