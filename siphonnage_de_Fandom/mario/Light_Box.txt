The '''Light Box''' is a type of block which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. They are most commonly found in [[Ghost House]]s, but can be found elsewhere. 

==Description==
When hit, they are worn over the player's body, shining a spotlight out from the front. This light can defeat [[Boo]]s and [[Peepa]]s when held in their faces for a short amount of time. Additionally, when worn, the Block and its light change color to match that of the corresponding character.

==In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''==
The character that wears the Light Box changes color (red for [[Mario]], green for [[Luigi]], blue for [[Toad (character)|Toad]], pink for [[Princess Peach|Peach]] and cyan for [[Rosalina]]). The Light Box can harm [[Peepa]]s, [[Boo]]s and [[Big Boo]]s.

==In ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''==
Light Boxes appear in the boss minigame, [[King Boo's Light Smite]]. The player holds the Light Box that deals damage to [[King Boo]] with a point, the bigger Light Box deals more damage with three points. However, the player can lose the Light Box when they fall off the platform or collide with a [[Peepa]].
