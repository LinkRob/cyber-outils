'''''Super Mario 64 2''''' (alternatively known as ''Super Mario 64 II'') was a cancelled title for a [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'']] game in development that served as the direct sequel to [[Super Mario 64|S''uper Mario 64'']], intended for release in 1999. It was going to be for the [[Nintendo 64DD]], but was cancelled early in production due to lack of progress and the commercial failure of the 64DD add-on.
