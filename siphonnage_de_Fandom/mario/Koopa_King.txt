The '''Koopa King''' is a heavyweight [[kart]] featured in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is the signature kart of [[Bowser]], being somewhat designed in his likeness and being named after his role of leading the [[Koopa Troop]], let alone the [[Koopa]] race in general. The Koopa King is available from the start. It has maximum [[Speed]], higher than that of any other kart, but at the same time has the lowest [[Acceleration]] out of any other kart.

==Stats==
*'''Class''': Heavy
*'''Speed''': 5/5
*'''Acceleration''': 1/5
*'''Weight''': 5/5
