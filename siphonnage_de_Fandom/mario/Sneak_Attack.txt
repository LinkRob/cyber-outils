'''Sneak Attack''' is a [[Captain Command]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Description ==
If a player uses Sneak Attack, they can attack the enemy captain from behind.

== Flavor text ==
* '''In-game description''': ''Exclusive to Boos. A stealthy attack from behind an enemy captain. Can it be pulled off against types you're weak to?''
