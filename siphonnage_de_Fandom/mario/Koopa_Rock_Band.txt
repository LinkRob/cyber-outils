The '''Koopa Rock Band''' is a trio of Koopa Troopas musicians that perform inside Bowser's fortress constantly in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. Here, they can be seen performing the [[Attack! Fury Bowser|theme to Fury Bowser]], and eventually stop upon hearing word about Bowser's plan to marry Princess Peach.
