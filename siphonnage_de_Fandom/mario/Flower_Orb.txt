The '''Flower Orb''' is an [[Orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. It can only be used by [[Princess Peach|Peach]] and [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]. When used, the player receives three [[Coin]]s for each space they pass on their roll of the [[Dice Block]], and bypasses any roadblock traps for that turn.

The effects of this orb are a combination of the [[Metal Mushroom Orb]], and the later-introduced [[Bitsize Candy]] from ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. However, the Metal Mushroom Orb activates roadblock traps and deflects their attacks instead of bypassing them completely.
