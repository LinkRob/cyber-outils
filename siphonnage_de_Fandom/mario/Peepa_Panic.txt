'''Peepa Panic''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in the hallway of a [[Ghost House]].

==Gameplay==
The objective for each character is to avoid being caught by one of the [[Peepa]]s. Every character has three hearts, and if a character gets caught by a Peepa, they lose a heart and are carried upward by the Peepa. If a grabbed character has any hearts remaining, their player must shake the [[Wii Remote]] for the Peepa to drop the character, otherwise they are eliminated. Whoever either reaches the end of the Ghost House first or is the last character remaining wins the minigame. 

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|pad}}: Move
*{{button|wii|Wiimote}}: Shake off a Peepa
