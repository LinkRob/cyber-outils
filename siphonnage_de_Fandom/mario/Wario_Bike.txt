The '''Wario Bike''' is an object in various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Description==
The Wario Bike is a yellow motorcycle owned by [[Wario]] and one of his signature vehicles throughout his appearances, the other one being the [[Wario Car]]. The Wario Bike is based on the real-world {{w|chopper}} motorcycles. Wario is most often seen using the Wario Bike in the {{series|WarioWare}}, when traveling around [[Diamond City]], and the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, which includes it as part of his moveset. In the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series, the Wario Bike has flames painted on certain parts of its body, and Wario's signature "W" [[emblem]] also appears on it.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''===
[[File:Wario Bike Intro MMG.png|thumb|left|200px|Wario driving the Wario Bike in the opening cutscene of ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'']]
Wario is seen driving the Wario Bike in certain cutscenes. The Wario Bike is first seen in the opening cutscene, where after Wario watched a report on television about the success of ''[[Pyoro (game)|Pyoro]]'', he proceeded to put on his biker outfit, mount the Wario Bike, and drive off to obtain a [[computer]] to start his own business, [[WarioWare, Inc.]].

The [[microgame]] [[Unexcite Bike]], a parody of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game ''[[w:c:nintendo:Excitebike|Excitebike]]'', features Wario driving the Wario Bike in place of [[Excitebike (character)|Excitebike]] driving his own bike.

The Wario Bike is featured in another ''Excitebike'' parody, this time being the [[Wario Bike (souvenir)|Wario Bike]] minigame.

==In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''==
[[File:Wario Bike MKWii.png|thumb|250px|Wario on his Wario Bike in ''Mario Kart Wii'']]
Here, the Wario Bike is featured as a heavyweight [[Bike]] with good [[acceleration]] and [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]], and it has the lowest top speed of the large [[Kart]]s/[[Bike]]s. It is Wario's personal bike, although any character can use it. Like with any other vehicle, the Wario Bike's color scheme differs depending on who uses it. Despite its beginner status, the Wario Bike can be used for tracks with tight turns, such as [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart Wii)|Rainbow Road]]. [[King Boo]] is one of the best drivers to use the Wario Bike with.

The Wario Bike is the vehicle used by four [[Staff Ghost]]s, each of a different track:

* [[Wario's Gold Mine]] (Nin*morimo, Wario)
* {{classic course|DS|[[Delfino Square]]}} (Nin*iwaco, Wario)
* {{classic course|GCN|[[DK Mountain]]}} (Nin*msk, [[Funky Kong]])
* {{classic course|GCN|[[Waluigi Stadium]]}} (Nin*msk, [[Waluigi]])

==In ''[[Mario Party 10]]''==
In the [[Wario Board]] of [[amiibo Party]], a toy-like Wario Bike appears as a background element on the southwest part of the board.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
====''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''====
[[File:Wario Bike SSBB.jpg|thumb|250px|left|The Wario Bike in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
Since Wario's debut as a fighter in the game, Wario Bike has appeared as his side special move. Upon using the move, Wario rides on the Wario Bike, and he can damage any fighter whom he drives into. Wario can still [[taunt]] while riding the Wario Bike. If the player tilts the Control Stick in the opposite direction that Wario is facing, he performs a 180-degree turn. If the Control Stick is tilted up, Wario performs a wheelie, and tilting the Control Stick down resets the position; either these attacks can can deal even more damage if timed right. If [[Wario-Man]] uses Wario Bike, it drives forward even faster, but this puts the Wario Bike at risk of self-destructing. Only one Wario Bike can be on-screen at a time, so if the move is used again, Wario looks around for the Wario Bike. If Wario attempts to use the Wario Bike move while next to the vehicle, he picks it up and begins to ride on it again.

Wario can jump off of his bike if the player either presses an attack or grab button, has Wario jump, or if he hits a wall. The Wario Bike can be stopped by other characters with a timed hit. When Wario jumps off the bike, he can pick it up and throw it. The Wario Bike can be destroyed, and the pieces can be thrown around. Wario can also use [[Chomp (Super Smash Bros. series)|Chomp]] to eat the Wario Bike or any pieces from it. After the Wario Bike is destroyed or disappears, Wario has to wait at least six seconds before he can spawn another one.

====''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U]]''====
[[File:Wario Bike SSB4 Wii U.jpg|thumb|250px|Wario riding the Wario Bike in ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'']]
Both games retain Wario Bike as Wario's side-special move. When riding the Wario Bike, it is now possible for Wario to repeatedly perform a 180-degree turn. Since both games, it is no longer possible to throw around the pieces of the Wario Bike after it had been destroyed. The move was also given two custom variants: Speeding Bike and Burying Bike.

====''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''====
Wario Bike returns as Wario's side special, though it is more similar to its appearance from ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''.

==Trivia==
*Prior to the Wario Bike's debut, Wario has used a different motorcycle in ''[[Wario Blast: Featuring Bomberman!]]'' named [[Moto]], and one of Wario's obtainable [[Present Room|presents]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'' is [[Wario's Motorbike]], a different vehicle than his motorcycle.
