'''Tables''' are interactive objects in a few of the ''[[Mario Kart]]'' games.

==In ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''==
Tables appear in the dining room section, sliding as the cruise ship moves around. If racers drive into tables, their vehicle is slowed temporarily.
