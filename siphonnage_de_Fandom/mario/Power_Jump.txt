'''Power Jump''' is a recurring [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It allows [[Mario]] to deal more damage when [[jump]]ing on an enemy. Power Jump first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and returns in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. In both games, the [[Badge Point|BP]] cost is one and the [[Flower Point|FP]] cost is two. The Power Jump can be sold for 25 [[coin]]s.

== Appearances ==
=== ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ===
[[File:Power Jump Icon (Paper Mario).png|The icon for Power Jump during battle]]
Power Jump is the first badge that Mario receives in ''Paper Mario''. He receives it from [[Goompa]] after safely returning him to [[Goomba Village]]. Goompa also explains to Mario how the badge works. While the Power Jump is a simple badge, it gives Mario a new attack that deals additional damage to enemies.

=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
Power Jump can be bought at the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] for fifty [[coin]]s. It can also be found at the [[Pianta Parlor]] where it can be purchased for 34 [[Pianta Token]]s.
