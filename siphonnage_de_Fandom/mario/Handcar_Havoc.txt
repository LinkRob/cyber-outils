'''Handcar Havoc''' is a [[List of mini-games in Mario Party#2-vs-2 mini-games|2 vs. 2 mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party]] ''and ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==

===''Mario Party'' and ''Superstars''===
The game has the characters split into teams of two. Each team must make their way down a set of tracks in a [[wikipedia:Handcar|handcar]] to reach the finish line. Players must be careful not to tilt too far, lest their cart falls into the [[Abyss|void]]. The characters lose if that happens.

When on [[Mini-Game Island]], this mini-game serves as World 4-3. The player must clear the game by winning this race.
