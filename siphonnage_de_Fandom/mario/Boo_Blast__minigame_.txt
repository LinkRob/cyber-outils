'''Boo Blast''' is a minigame in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]].

==Description==
For this minigame, the player plays as [[Mario]], whose objective is to hit [[Boo]]s as the rally continues with [[Luigi]]. There are three rounds; the goal for the first two are 30 and 60 [[point]]s respectively, while the third round is endless but has a minimum requirement of 60 points. With each round, Luigi hits the ball faster than before.

After winning the third round, the player can copy this data through a [[Transfer Pak]] over to ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]] to unlock [[Super Mario Court]].
