A {{Nihongo|'''Wind Spirit'''|カゼくん|Kazekun|Wind-kun}} (also called a '''Wind Duppy''') is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''

==Profile==
===Behavior===
They form in high areas where [[Mario]] is located. They can vanish temporarily by [[F.L.U.D.D.]], but can only be defeated if [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] swallows it. They can knock Mario off the edge resulting Mario to fall down a bottomless pit. They release a [[coin]] after their defeated. In [[Pianta Village]], three Wind Spirits can be found on the bridge that Mario goes through when entering [[Pianta Village]] only in Episode 3: [[The Goopy Inferno]], and Episode 5: [[Secret of the Village Underside]]. Wind Spirits appear one more time in Pianta Village in Episode 7: [[Shadow Mario Runs Wild]]. They are seen on the east side of the Village.

===Habitats===
Wind Spirits are encountered in [[Bianco Hills]], [[Ricco Harbor]], and [[Pianta Village]].

==Trivia==
*[[Madame Flurrie]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and it’s [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|Switch remake]] is also referred to as a Wind Spirit however she is not the same type of Wind Spirit as these enemies as she is a humanoid genie-like ghost that has wind powers while these Wind Spirits are harmful creatures that fly at [[Mario]].
