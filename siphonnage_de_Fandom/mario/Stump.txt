'''Stumps''' are a recurring object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
A Stump is essentially a regular tree stump. A character can [[Ground Pound (move)|ground-pound]] a Stump to push it slightly downward. A Stump can be ground-pounded more times until it sinks into the ground. Pounding certain Stumps into the ground causes something to occur. Stumps essentially replace [[Post]]s from the {{series|Yoshi's Island}} and ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' as well as [[Nail]]s from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', both of which function the same as Stumps.
