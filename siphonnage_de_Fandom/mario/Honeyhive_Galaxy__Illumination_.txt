'''Honeyhive Galaxy''' is a planet located in the universe seen in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. It is inhabited and ruled by Bees lead by their [[Honey Queen (Illumination)|Honey Queen]].

==History==
After Bowser Jr. broke his father out of prison at [[Princess Peach's Castle (Illumination)|Peach's Castle]], ubducted by a UFO, the castle was dropped and crashed into mthe grounds of the galaxy.

== Trivia ==

* The Honeyhive Galaxy in the movie may have had some inspiration from Terra Flora from ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope with aspects such as it's spring aesthetic, tall flowers and floating debris.''
