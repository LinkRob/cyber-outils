'''Sleepy Sheep''' is an item featured in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It made its first appearance in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', and it would later reappear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' then ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
The Sleepy Sheep is found in shops and [[Question Block]]. Using the item causes enemies to [[sleep]] for a little while. The first Sleepy Sheep is found right before [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] in a Question Block. A Sleepy Sheep can also given to [[Koopa Koot]] as one of his many favors.

In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', this item when cooked, will give [[Mario]] a special version of [[Mistake]]. This special version has no other qualities than its counterpart, but it is a different recipe on Mario's recipe list.
