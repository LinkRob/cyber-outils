'''Yoshi Racing''' is a video game for the [[Nintendo 64]] that was suggested by an employee of Argonaut Games.

==History==
After Argonaut Games and [[Nintendo]] successfully made ''Star Fox 64'', Argonaut Games created a prototype of a game featuring [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] as the main character. However, Nintendo turned them down, and an Argonaut Games employee speculated that Nintendo declined the offer due to them not wanting any third parties using their characters. Afterward, Argonaut Games then rebooted ''Yoshi Racing'' as ''Croc: Legend of the Goggos'' for the PlayStation.

==Trivia==
*If this game was accepted, this would've been the last Nintendo 64 game before ''[[Dr. Mario 64]]''.
