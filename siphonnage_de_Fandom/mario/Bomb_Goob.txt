'''Bomb Goob''' is a kind of [[Luigi's Mansion 3#Rare_Ghosts|rare ghost]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', it is a form of [[Goob]]. Bomb Goobs are glowing yellow Goobs with blue eyes and white mouth. They have 250 HP and can throw bombs to attack. Like other rare ghosts, Bomb Goobs only appear in the [[ScareScraper]]'s ten floor mod.

==Appearance==

Bomb Goobs are glowing yellow instead of being blue like regular Goobs. Bomb Goobs are killed in mostly the same way as Goobs, the only difference is that [[Luigi]] can not flash them when they are still holding their bombs, they can be only flashed after throwing bombs.

==In ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'' ==
When Bomb Goobs appear on the fifth floor in [[ScareScraper]], they will hold a bomb which prevents Luigi from flashing them, they will throw the bombs when it's shining red which means it's about to explode, now Luigi can flash them. While Luigi is sucking the Bomb Goobs, they will throw more bombs to attack. They are defeated in the similar way as Goobs.
