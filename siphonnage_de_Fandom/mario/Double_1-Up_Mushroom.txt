[[File:Double1UpMushroom.png|thumb]]The '''Double 1-Up Mushroom''' is an item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. They are released by [[Paper Bob-omb]]s in [[Doop Doop Dunes]]. When used, they revive two Bros, instead of one like a regular [[1-Up Mushroom]].
