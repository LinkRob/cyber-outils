A '''bramble''' is a spiky vine-like plant which mainly appears in the [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]], but has also appeared in some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, the first being ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. Overall, brambles make their first appearance in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''. Several [[Piranha Plant]]s have bramble-like stalks, such as [[Prickly Piranha Plant]]s and [[Piranha Creeper]]s, and an enemy named [[Bramball]] has long bramble-like legs.

==In the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]==
===''Super Mario Sunshine''===
A few large brambles can be found surrounding the [[Big Windmill]] in [[Bianco Hills]] whenever Petey Piranha is on top of the windmill, which is during the first two episodes [[Road to the Big Windmill]] and [[Down with Petey Piranha!]]. Unlike most of its other appearances, these brambles are red with green spots, and they twitch slightly. They disappear after Petey's first defeat.

===''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''===
Brambles are obstructions featured on the [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5]] map. This is also the debut appearance of the recurring enemy Bramball.
