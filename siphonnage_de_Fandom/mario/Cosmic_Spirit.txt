'''Cosmic Spirit''' is a character in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' with a female human shape just like that of [[Rosalina]].

When [[Mario]] talks to her, Cosmic Spirit will ask if he needs to be shown the way to any [[Star]]s. If the player agrees to do so, the Cosmic Spirit will transform into a blue orb that will encircle and gain control of [[Mario]]. This blue orb will guide [[Mario]] to the [[Power Star]], but will not collect any Star Bits, coins, comet medals, or 1-ups and health mushrooms, all on purpose. This downside is most obviously demonstrated on the level Tall Trunk's Big Slide. While there is the option of regaining control of [[Mario]] by use of the + button, doing so is virtual suicide as this will make the [[Power Star]] degrade to a [[Bronze Star]], in which you have to replay the level to get the [[Power Star]]. She was formerly voiced by Rosalina's very first former voice actress [[Mercedes Rose]].

Note that while the Cosmic Spirit assists the player, [[Cosmic Mario]] and [[Cosmic Luigi]] antagonize players instead of helping them. The cosmic spirit also turns out to be Rosalina.
