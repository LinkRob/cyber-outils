'''Berlin Byways 3''' is a race track from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. As its name implies, it is the third layout of [[Berlin Byways]].

== Layout ==
Racers on this course start on the Straße des 17. Juni, on the other side of the Brandenburg Gate from the [[Berlin Byways 2|first]] [[Berlin Byways 1|two]] layouts (west according to the map). When the racers are given the green light, they drive west towards the Berlin Victory Column. They take the south exit at the roundabout, taking a right as they approach the Tempodrom. They then drive past numerous buildings. This is followed by a wide right U-turn, after which they enter the roundabout again, but this time from the west exit. They then follow the [[Berlin Byways 2]] route for a while (taking the north exit), diverting when they reach a side road called the Scheidemannstraße. They turn right onto this road, then drive up to the Brandenburg Gate. There, they take a right turn, driving along the Straße des 17. Juni again until they reach the finish line.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', the racers will be forced to follow this route on the second lap of [[Berlin Byways]] starting from the roundabout. Near the end of said lap, they will go straight, crossing the Straße des 17. Juni right in front of the Brandenburg Gate (behind from the point of vista of someone who is on the same side as the finish line) to get to a side road that ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' hadn't used before. From there, they follow the [[Berlin Byways 1]] route.
