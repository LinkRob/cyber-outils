{{DISPLAYTITLE:''Mario Party 9''/Game references}}
This is a list of '''game references''' in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

== Music ==
* ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'': The overworld theme was remixed in the music, "Freshen Up!" that was only played in [[Speeding Bullets]]. The overworld theme was also remixed for the Museum background and for "Super-Duper Mario Bros." that is a pun of the game. The underworld theme was remixed for the Minigame mode '''Choice Challenge'''.
* ''[[Super Mario World]]'': The overworld theme was remixed in the Results section.
