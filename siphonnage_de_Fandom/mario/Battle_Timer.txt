The '''Battle Timer''' shows how much time is left during [[Balloon Battle]]/[[Coin Runners]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. It works the same way as the [[Race Timer]] but instead, you have three minutes to score as many points as you can during the battle (two in ''Mario Kart 7''). For ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', the timer range between one and five minutes and it only applies to Balloon Battle.
