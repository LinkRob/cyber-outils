'''Rift Mario''' is an antagonistic character from [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder|''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'']]. He can be fought as a boss in [[Wonder Effect|Wonder Effects]] that are triggered in [[Color-Switch Dungeon]] and [[Beware of the Rifts]].

== Description ==
Rift Mario appears similar to [[Mario (character)|Mario]] himself, but his body is black save for white spots and his eyes are red similarly to a [[Shadow Koopa Troopa]], and his lower body is a wispy tail, akin to a ghost.

== Battle ==
The battle always begins when [[Mario (character)|Mario]] gets his hands on a [[Wonder Flower]] in [[Color-Switch Dungeon]] and [[Beware of the Rifts]], after which Rift Mario manifests, much to Mario's horror.

=== Colour-Switch Dungeon ===
During his first battle in Color-Switch Dungeon, Rift Mario will be aided by [[Li'l Sparky|Li'l Sparkies]] and [[Hothead|Hotheads]]. To defeat him, Mario has to hit ON/OFF Switches whilst dodging these Li'l Sparkies and Hotheads and get to the end of the hallway and grab the [[Wonder Seed]], forcing Rift Mario to demanifest.

=== Beware of the Rifts ===
The second battle with Rift Mario happens during the Wonder Effect in Beware of the Rifts. Rift Mario is this time aided by [[Shadow Koopa Troopa|Shadow Koopa Troopas]]. [[POW Block|POW Blocks]] litter his arena during this battle. He is this time defeated by grabbing five [[Wonder Token|Wonder Tokens]] that summon the [[Wonder Seed]], then grabbing it, after which he demanifests.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Chinese, and Korean, Rift Mario is referred to as "Shadow Mario" (Japanese: ''Kage Mario'' (written 影マリオ); Chinese: Yǐngzi Mǎlì'ōu (written 影子瑪利歐); Korean: ''Geurimja Mario'' (written 그림자마리오)); this is not to be confused with the actual [[Shadow Mario]], as they are not related; Shadow Mario himself is called "Fake Mario" in those languages. However, Rift Mario is referred to as "Dark Mario" or "Shadow Mario" in his files.
