The '''Fashion Model''' is a minor character from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'', making her only appearance in the microgame [[Camera Ready]].

== Description ==
The Fashion Model has white circles with black outlines for eyes, and her mouth and nose are covered by a [[Wario]] mask (since Wario himself tends to be present, mentioned, and/or involved somehow in microgames). Her hair and dress are different depending on the difficulty. On the first level, she has brown hair in a ponytail and wears a white dress. The second level gives her grey hair (tied in a ponytail as well), light brown skin, and dark blue dress. When the player plays at the hardest difficulty level, the model goes back to having fair skin, and she has white hair with pigtails similar to [[Ashley]] and a black dress with a gold trim.

== In ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]'' ==
In [[Camera Ready]], the Fashion Model has to turn towards the camera to get her picture taken for the player to win.
