'''Star Candy''' is an item found in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''.

==Description==
Star Candies, as their name implies, have a star shape. They are orange and have a shiny look.

==Usage==
The strongest item is used to restore 999 [[HP]] and [[Special Point|SP]]. [[Dark Mechawful]]s have a chance dropping it. When the [[Mighty Meteor]] is executed perfectly, it can be rewarded. This is found in [[Blizzard Midbus]], [[Bowser Memory ML]] (only in Bowser Memory M), [[Dark Fawful]], [[Dark Star (character)|Dark Star]], [[Shroob]] and [[Wisdurm]].

In [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]], this item is replaced by [[Cheese Chicken]] for [[Bowser]] use.

== Other Appearances ==
Star Candies make a return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', but their healing values have been divided into four levels. The weakest variant restores 40%, the Super variant heals 60%, the Ultra variant heals 80%, and the Max variant is essentially the original version from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' but without the ability to cure status ailments. This is retained in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.{{Stub}}
