The '''X-Ship''' is a vehicle in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
The X-Ship is a large battleship belonging to [[Lord Crump]].

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Lord Crump summoned the X-Ship after [[Mario]] gets the fifth [[Crystal Star]] from defeating [[Cortez]]. Lord Crump starts blasting on [[Keelhaul Key]] demanding the Crystal Star or he will sink the island. Fortunately, [[Flavio]] manages to convince Cortez to use his ship, the [[Black Skull]], to do battle with the X-Ship. Mario and his partners fight Lord Crump and his army of [[X-Naut]]s until they are defeated.
