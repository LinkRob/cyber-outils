'''Pink Eye''' is [[Young Cricket]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

== Gameplay ==

The objective is to zap all the screaming heads. The kids say "Thank you!" after winning. In "The Elephant" form, moving the [[Wii Remote]] directs the player where they want to aim.

*'''Level 1:''' A line of screaming heads. A kid is rescued.
*'''Level 2:''' Two lines of screaming heads. Three kids are rescued.
*'''Level 3:''' Three lines of screaming heads. Five kids are rescued.
