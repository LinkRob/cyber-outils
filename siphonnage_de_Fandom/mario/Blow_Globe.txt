'''Blow Globes''' are enemies in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Blow Globes are mutated [[Snow Globe (enemy)|Snow Globe]]s resembling a moon, and they have a pirate eye-patch and mustache.

===Behavior===
When [[Wario]] approaches a Blow Globe, it reveals itself and shoots snowflakes at Wario.

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Blow Globes are only encountered in [[Sneezemore Cave]], where there are only four of them. Wario can defeat a Blow Globe using almost any attack. Defeating a Blow Globe causes a single water drop to fall usually on a [[Sipping Stalk]] so that it can grow, allowing Wario to climb it.
