A '''Giant Pillar''' (also called a '''Screeching Pillar''') is a much larger version of the [[Screaming Pillar]] found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' and ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]''. It only appears in the levels [[Canopy Cannons]] and [[Crushing Columns]], Giant Pillars have the same attacks as a Screaming Pillar, (falling down on [[DK]] and [[Diddy]] as they approach) but they are also indestructable and will instantly kill DK and Diddy upon contact. 

Giant Pillars also appear in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''<nowiki/>'s [[DK Jungle (Mario Kart 7)|DK Jungle]] and it's ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''/''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' counterpart and  replaces the Screaming Pillars. Their functionality remains the same as the Screaming Pillar and is unchanged.
