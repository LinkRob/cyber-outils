{{Infobox Microgame|name = Pool Boy|image = [[File:WWSM Pool Boy.png|thumb|250px]]|games = ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''|genre = [[Ashley]]|info = ''"Hit the white ball with the cue stick to sink at least one ball."''|forms = [[The Remote Control]]}}'''Pool Boy''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. The name refers to a boy who serves in a swimming pool. The microgame shares the same music to [[You're Fired!]].

==Gameplay==
One of the balls have to go through the hole. The player loses if the cue ball goes through the hole or none of the balls make it to the hole.
*'''Level 1: '''A ball is right next to a hole.
*'''Level 2:''' There are two balls.
*'''Level 3:''' Several balls which the player must ricochet the cue ball.
