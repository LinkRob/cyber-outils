'''Blooper Bay''' is the fourth course and third unlockable course in ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]''. It is unlocked by winning the [[Shifting Sands|Sands Classic]].

==Description==
The course has a tropical resort theme, as seen by the abundance of water, sand, and bamboo shacks throughout. Blooper Bay also seems to be based on [[Gelato Beach]], an area of [[Isle Delfino]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

There are many hazards laid throughout the course, mostly shaped as sea creatures and food items. Many of the fairways are lined with fast fairways, which allow the ball to have less traction. The vast amount of water in this course makes it more likely to launch the ball into a [[Out of Bounds#Water Hazard|Water Hazard]]. This course also features faster winds, and many bunkers. To the player's advantage, however, there are less trees, allowing the ball more space to breeze through. Although the course is named Blooper Bay, no [[Blooper]]s appear on the course. Many bunkers throughout take their shape. 

Once the player has beaten the tournament for this course, they will unlock [[Peach's Castle Grounds]].

==Tournament leaderboards==
When playing in the Blooper Open, 29 other competitors will appear in the leaderboard at the end of each Hole. The names consist of other playable characters, ''Mario'' characters, or generic nicknames. When playing in the Blooper Star Open, more names will be from ''Mario'' characters, characters from other ''Mario'' series such as ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' will appear, as well as characters from other [[Nintendo]] franchises such as ''[[The Legend of Zelda (franchise)|The Legend of Zelda]]''.
