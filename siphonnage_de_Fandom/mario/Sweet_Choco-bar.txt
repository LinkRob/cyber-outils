{{Infobox PM Item|name = Sweet Choco-bar|games = ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''|hp = 5|fp = 0|image = [[File:SweetChocoSPM.png]]|caption = The sprite of a Sweet Choco-bar from ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''}}'''Sweet Choco-bars''' are items found in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]].''
==Effects==

It restores 5 HP upon use, and cures poison. They can be found at Twinkle Mart, and must be fed to [[Squirps]] in order to progess. Other choco-bars can be used as well, such as a [[Shroom Choco-bar]] or a [[Golden Choco-bar]]. They can also be made by [[Dyllis]] via mixing a Mild Cocoa Bean with [[Honey Jar]].
