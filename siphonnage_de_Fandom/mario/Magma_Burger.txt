|activated by=Riding a [[Draggadon]]}}The '''Magma Burger''' is a special item in [[Paper Mario: Color Splash|''Paper Mario: Color Splash'']]. This item costs 200 coins, and gives [[Mario]] the item in his inventory. It can be purchased in the [[Prisma Café]] located in [[Fort Cobalt]]. Buying this item for the first time will grant Mario with a free special item, the [[Fishing Pole]]. The Magma Burger is used to ride a Draggadon in the [[Redpepper Volcano]], allowing Mario to reach unreachable areas in the lava. When Mario finds the [[File:PMCS Red Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|27x27px]] [[Mini Paint Star]], a choice is given to either give the Magma Burger to the Draggadon, or return to riding it longer. If Mario decided to give the Magma Burger, the Draggadon will eat the burger and descend into the lava. This allows you to obtain the [[File:PMCS Red Mini Paint Star.png|frameless|27x27px]] [[Mini Paint Star]].

In [[Fort Cobalt]], red [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]] can be seen eating Magma Burgers. These burgers cause them to breathe fire, making [[Mario]] take 5 damage when stood infront of the flame. Some ashes with dialogue can be seen on the chairs, seemingly burnt alive by either eating one or sitting infront of one.
