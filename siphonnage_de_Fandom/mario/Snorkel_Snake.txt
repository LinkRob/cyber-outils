A '''Snorkel Snake''' is a long serpentine enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Physical description==
A Snorkel Snake has a long, white body with a long spout for a mouth, green spikes all over its body, a green tail fin, and red eyes.

==In ''Yoshi's Story''==
A Snorkel Snake makes an appearance in the level [[Lots O'Jelly Fish]]. Upon noticing a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], a Snorkel Snake circles around him, attempting to trap the Yoshi. A Snorkel Snake's body is not long enough to form a complete circle, so it is possible for the Yoshi to swim out through the opening.
