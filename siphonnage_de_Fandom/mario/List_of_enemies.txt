The list includes the game or other type of media where each enemy first appeared. Depending on the media, they are also known as '''minions''', '''baddies''', '''bad guys''', '''foes''', '''cronies''', '''moppets''',<ref>[[Nintendo Power issue 1|''Nintendo Power'' Volume 1]], page 11.</ref><ref>''Super Mario All-Stars'' Player's Guide, page 88.</ref> '''henchmen''',<ref>''"ROY AND HIS HENCHMEN ARE PREPARING FOR YOUR ARRIVAL"'' - ''[[Hotel Mario]]''</ref> '''flunkies''',<ref>Multiple dialog labels, ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''</ref> '''monsters,'''<ref>''"Bowser's scaring the monsters!"'' - ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''</ref> '''toadies''',<ref>[[Fight Toadies w/ Toadies]]</ref> and '''meanies'''.<ref>[[Meanie Match]]</ref>

Bosses can be found on their own [[List of bosses|list page]].
