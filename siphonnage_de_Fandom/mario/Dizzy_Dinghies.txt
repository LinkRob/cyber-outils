'''Dizzy Dinghies''' is a Rare Mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
This is a very special mini-game. There are three different courses which all has different turns. There are also three different boats to choose from: one that is red, one that is green, and another that is blue. One of them can accelerate fast but drives slow, another one has normal stats, and another has fast speed but poor turning. The goal of this mini-game is for the player to try and reach the finish and break the record. The player can try different boats for the different tracks to see how fast he/she can finish the courses.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|stick}} - Move
*{{button|n64|a}} - Accelerate
