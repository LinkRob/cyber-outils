|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''}}'''Herb T.'s Place''' is a location that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. It is a small bar owned by [[Herb T.]] whom the bar is named after.

==History==
Herb T.'s Place can be found in [[Rogueport Sewers]] and it is a location where people usually talk about various of information. One of the known visitors is [[Wonky]] who tells a various amount of secrets for a price. Despite being located in the less populated Rougeport Underground, Herb T.'s Place is said to be doing rather well as a business.
