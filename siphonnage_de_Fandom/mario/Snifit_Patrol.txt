The '''Snifit Patrol''', also referred to as '''Shy Guy Cops''', is a police force which appears in the [[Space Land]] board of ''[[Mario Party 2]]'' and in ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''. In the former game, it consists of [[Snufit]]s, referred to as "Snifits" within the game, whereas in the latter they consist of actual [[Snifit]]s.

==In ''Mario Party 2''==
Players can pay the Snifit Patrol to set up a "speed trap" when they come across a unit standing by. They chase after speeding Whomps and Thwomps to ensure the safety of the players playing on the board when a player lands on a Happening Space; when a speed trap is set, the chase lasts longer.

== Trivia ==
* Despite being called Snifits, they are shown without lower bodies in their icons, and additionally lack the black masks Snifits usually wear. The naming inconsistency is likely due to both Snifits and Snufits having the same name in Japanese at the time, resulting in an overlooked translation.
