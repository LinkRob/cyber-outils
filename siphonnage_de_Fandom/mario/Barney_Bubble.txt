A '''Barney Bubble'''<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide. Page 126.</ref> is an enemy that appears only in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its [[reissue]], ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. Despite the fact that its sprites depict them as purple with blue feet, artwork depicts them as white with red feet.

Two Barney Bubbles appear in the level [[Monkeys' Favorite Lake]]. They quickly run back and forth. The [[Yoshi (species)|Red Yoshi]] cannot eat a Barney Bubble, and he can defeat it only by [[Egg Throw|throwing an egg]] at it. Whenever the Red Yoshi [[stomp]]s on a Barney Bubble, it produces [[bubble]]s, which the Red Yoshi can eat and spit out for a variety of uses. [[Bubble Dayzee]]s produce the same type of bubble.

In ''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'', two more Barney Bubbles appear in [[World 6 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 6]]'s Secret Stage, [[Endless World of Yoshis]], in the forest area, making a total of four Barney Bubbles.
