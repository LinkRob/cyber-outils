'''Big Ben''' is a real-world building on [[Earth]].

==Description==
It is a famous bell in the Houses of Parliament, located in [[London]], [[England]]. However, the name "Big Ben" often refers to the clock tower housing the bell, possibly because of the tower's iconic look, when the tower itself is actually named '''Elizabeth Tower''', renamed from '''Clock Tower''' in 2012.

==In ''Mario is Missing!''==
[[File:Big Ben MIM.png|thumb|left|200px|Luigi's photograph of Big Ben in ''Mario is Missing!'']]
[[Luigi]] visits Big Ben when he travels to London. [[Koopa Troopa]]s have stolen [[Big Ben's minute hand]], and Luigi has to find and the minute hand and return it to the Clock Tower.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
Big Ben is one of several landmarks seen throughout [[London Loop]], although Big Ben is only for aesthetic and does not serve any gameplay purpose. In the first two routes, Big is located right next to the road, directly in front of the [[finish line]] on the left, but in [[London Loop 3]], it is behind the finish line. The clock of Elizabeth Tower always shows the hour hand on eight and the minute hand on one minute past, and the clock hands do not move.

===''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ([[Booster Course Pass]])===
In the third wave, with the inclusion of {{classic course|Tour|London Loop}}, Big Ben also makes a returning appearance.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Mario & Sonic}}===
====''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' ([[Wii]])====
Because the London 2012 Olympics takes place in England, the Clock Tower is seen several times. It is first seen throughout the opening cinematic, which concludes with [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] standing on top of the Clock Tower at the end.

The tower is a landmark in [[London Party]], but it also serves as a countdown timer until the next [[Event]]. The clock's face is on the lower-right corner of the HUD while leading up to the event with a ticking sound, and the clock strikes every sixth hour (12am, 6am, 12pm, and 6pm) to indicate when the next Event begins.

Fences themed to Big Ben appear in [[Equestrian - Show Jumping]].

An image of the clock face and top of the tower appears on the sticker earned for completing the [[Collect Shy Guy's Coins!]] minigame.

====''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' ([[Nintendo 3DS]])====
Like in the Wii version, the Clock Tower is featured prominently, especially in Story Mode, where Big Ben has a role. [[Dr. Eggman]] even set up his lab in the interior of the Clock Tower.
