'''Krubbishes''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. They are only fought in [[Dreamy Wakeport]]. Krubbishes and [[Cocoknight]] R's can be fought together.

==Physical description==
Krubbishes are pink-colored trash cans. They are also similar to [[Junker]], a boss from the previous game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. Their eyes are yellow in color, sticking up like [[Robo-Drilldigger]]'s eyes do, and their trash can lid has white studs around the rim, making them appear to have teeth. Their "feet" are tread-wheels.

==Battle==
It should be noted that when Krubbishes are defeated, they will explode hitting other enemies. Their explosions also instantly kill any Cocoknight R's inside the creature.

===Attacks===
*'''Enemy Shot''': The Krubbish will suck in some of the Cocoknight R's and spit them at Mario and Luigi. The target must hammer them back at the Krubbish to avoid damage and deal damage to the Krubbish.
*'''Poison Gas''': The Krubbish will look in a direction and then shoot poison gas that must be jumped over. If it looks straight ahead, both Bros. should jump at the same time. If the Krubbish looks to its right (north), Mario must jump first, and then Luigi. If it looks to its left (south), Luigi must jump first, and then Mario. Getting hit runs the risk of [[Poison (status effect)|poisoning the target]].

=== Statistics ===
Krubbishes are treated as a Level 25 monster with 83 hit points at maximum. Their attack power, defense, and speed measure 180, 152, and 58, respectively. When killed, they give 160 experience and 45 coins and may drop a refreshing herb (1 in 10 drops) and the Rookie Gloves (1 in 20 drops).
