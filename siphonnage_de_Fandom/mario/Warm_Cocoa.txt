'''Warm Cocoa''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Saffron]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Effects==

Made simply from a [[Mild Cocoa Bean]], this hot beverage restores 10 [[Heart Point|HP]], double the amount of one Mild Cocoa Bean, and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
