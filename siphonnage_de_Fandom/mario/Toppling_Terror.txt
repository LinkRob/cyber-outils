'''Toppling Terror''' is a 2-vs-2 and a Duel minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Gameplay==
There is a [[Whomp]] standing between two teams, and the objective for each side is to get Whomp to collapse onto the opposing side. Each character is holding a funnel to blow air at the Whomp. At the end, Whomp topples back and forth, landing on whichever side blew the least amount of air on him. Whichever side Whomp lands on loses the minigame, making the other side the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|ds|mic}}: Blow
