'''Billy''' is an audience member in the ''[[King Koopa's Kool Kartoons]]'' episode "[[Kids Rights Day]]." During the episode, [[Bowser|King Koopa]] announces he will be giving away a copy of ''[[The Children's Giant Atlas of the Universe]]'' to the [[Bowser's Minions|Troopa]] in the selected seat, which is labeled on a card [[Ratso]] has. After pulling the card away from Ratso, Koopa calls on the Troopa on the seat labeled "Red 7," who is revealed to be Billy. Koopa gives the book to Billy and tells him to wave at [[List of implied characters#Billy's parents|his parents]].
