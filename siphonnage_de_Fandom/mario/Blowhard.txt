'''Blowhard''' is one of the [[board]]s in [[Duel Mode]] of ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Description==
[[File:MP3 Blowhard Artwork.jpg|frame|left|Artwork of Blowhard]]
The gimmick is the giant fan in the middle (named Blowhard). Every time players come here, Blowhard blows a gust of wind upward, switching the positions of the player's partners. The four junctions also provide easy access to the opponent or the start spaces. This is the only Duel Board map to not have any [[Back Space (random)|Reverse Spaces]], although they still appear on the board results screen.

In [[Story Mode]], the player has to duel [[Princess Peach|Peach]] (or [[Luigi]] if the player is Peach) on this board for the Love [[Star Stamp]].
