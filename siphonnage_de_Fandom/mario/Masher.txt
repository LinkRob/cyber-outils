'''Masher''' is a [[hammer]] used as a weapon by [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be found in a certain [[Treasure Chest|treasure box]] in [[Booster Tower]]. Equipping the Masher increases Mario's [[Attack]] power by 50. The Masher can be sold for 80 [[coin]]s, although it cannot be replaced afterward.
