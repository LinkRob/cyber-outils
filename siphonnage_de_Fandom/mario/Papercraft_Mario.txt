'''Papercraft Mario''' is the first [[Papercraft]] used in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. It is used to defeat [[Megacrinkle Goomba]] near the end of [[Sunbeam Plains]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Papercraft Mario resembles Mario himself, but he is square in form. For his getup, Papercraft Mario wears Mario's signature red cap and shirt, blue overalls, and brown shirt. After Papercraft Bowser destroys him and the heroes rebuild him, Papercraft Mario is reborn as '''Papercraft Fire Mario''', with a white shirt and cap and red overalls.

===Powers and abilities===
In his first form, Papercraft Mario cannot do much. Rebuilding him as Fire Mario gave him the ability to shoot fireballs from his mouth.
