==Layout==
Strike Lake looks extremely similar to [[Leaflitter Path]] except it's a little more darker in color. There are multiple secret places and a scrap for a secret exit. There are also two leaves that the player have to knock down to continue

==History==
[[Mario]] heads further into the poisonous forest to search for the [[Wiggler]]'s missing [[Wiggler Segment|body segments]]. Mario finds one of the segments, but it runs away from him. He follows the segment further and further on a while goose chase while battling enemies and avoiding the poisonous substances. He eventually catches up to the segment and it agrees to go with Mario. With the segment in tow, Mario grabs the [[Comet Piece]] to exit the level.
