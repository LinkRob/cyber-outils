}}'''Fire Bar Cliffs''', is the sixth course of [[Superstar Road]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is unlocked when the player collects every [[Star Coin]] in [[Rock-Candy Mines]].

==Layout==
The course is based off courses from Rock-Candy Mines, most notably [[Waddlewing's Nest]]. The level begins near a [[Huge Fire Bar]] underneath a platform with a [[? Block]] above it. A Huge Fire Bar is then found above a pit with [[Donut Block]]s above it. Another Huge Fire Bar is found, followed by a smaller path with another Huge Fire Bar. After the player avoids the Huge Fire Bar, they reach two platfroms with [[Koopa Troopa]]s on them. A ? Block is found, followed by another Huge Fire Bar above Donut Blocks. A group of four Huge Fire Bars are found on platforms above a pit, with the fourth one moving in the opposite direction. A [[Warp Pipe]] leading to an area with a ? Block and more Huge Fire Bars is then found. Mario must climb up platforms while avoiding Huge Fire Bars, where he encounters a [[Buzzy Beetle]] and more Donut Blocks. A Koopa Troopa is also found next to a Huge Fire Bar. Mario must continue climbing, and more Huge Fire Bars and Buzzy Beetles are found. At the top of the area, a Warp Pipe is found. The Warp Pipe leads to an area with a Huge Fire Bar and the [[Goal Pole]]. 

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin sits in a crevice guarded by the third Huge Fire Bar.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Before a series of Fire Bars, there is a wall with a [[Warp Pipe]] hidden within. The pipe leads to a underground area where the second Star Coin resides on a fence in between Huge Fire Bars.
*'''Star Coin 3''': In the last section of the course before the exit is a wall to the left with a Warp Pipe. The pipe leads to another area where the third Star Coin is held above a Huge Fire Bar.
