Throughout the ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' franchise, certain enemies have the ability to chase the Bros. This is what is called a '''Chase Attack'''.

==Examples==
===''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''===
Chase Attacks make their debut in this game. When it is [[Vacuum Block|inhaled]] by [[Bowser]], the [[Dark Star]] has an attack where [[Fawful]] turns into a Pac-Man-esque creature that chases the Bros. while it rams them from overhead, attempting to force them into his jaws. Bowser himself also has this in the form of his fireball attack, where the Bros. have to dodge the flames as he [[Super Bowser|supersizes himself]].

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''===
Chase Attacks are more common in this game, where bosses can use them when their health runs low:
*The [[Grobot]] fires water bubbles from his hands. After some time (unless he manages to take out both Bros.), he will trip over a rock, taking damage.
*[[Bowser]] tucks into his shell and chases the Bros. through a field of flames. If neither one of them gets hit by the flames, the attack will end with Bowser slipping; otherwise whoever gets hit twice will get smashed.
*[[Big Massif]] can stack his [[Hooraw]] minions together and create shockwaves to knock the Bros. over. If neither one escapes, Big Massif poses with his Hooraws, with all of them returning to the battlefield. Otherwise, Big Massif's Hooraw minions will collapse and he will be forced to fight alone.
*The [[Mammoshka]] attempts to suck the Bros. into his trunk as he chases them down the mountain, getting tuckered out after around 15 seconds. If his trunk catches either Bro., the boss will spit that Bro. into the one who didn't get caught.
*[[Sergeant Guy]] gets a giant bomb from [[Corporal Paraplonk]]. He uses this bomb to chase the Bros., though [[Private Goomp]] gets swept up along with his minions, whom the Bros. have to avoid or stomp. If either Bro. trips, Sergeant Guy will drop his bomb, causing massive damage to the Bros. If both of them escape, the bomb detonates in Sergeant Guy's hands, damaging him, Paraplonk, and any Goombas (Private Goomp included if the Bros. prevented him from escaping) that are still on-screen.
*[[Wiggly]] the Wiggler charges [[Popple]] into the foreground, though the Bros. get swept up. His charges must be jumped over. Popple may occasionally attempt to ram the Bros., but this can be jumped over.
*The [[Pi'illodium]] turns into a fighter jet-like form that shoots missiles that must be jumped over. He can also ram into the Bros. Stomping on his backside causes him to crash and end the attack early.
*[[Antasma]] gathers his Antasmunchies into a ring and forces the Bros. to jump over them, lest the Antasmunchies blow the Bros. up.
*[[Dreamy Bowser]] spits fire from his mouth, with the Bros. having to jump every time he shifts. He will then stomp onto the battlefield, creating a shockwave that must be jumped over. This repeats once more, after which Dreamy Bowser stops the chase sequence.
Antasma's [[Dreamy Mario]] clone and [[Torkscrew]] have reverse variations where the heroes are the one doing the chasing. Dreamy Mario and his clones will turn invincible and attempt to ram the real Mario and Luigi while the Bros. chase Antasma. This must be avoided by jumping the clones. Torkscrew can stab the Bros. from below with his horn, and if he catches one, he will abduct them forcing the Bro. whom he did not catch to run into the background where the Bro. who did not get caught has to avoid bombs. Coins will also be lost constantly, but the Bro. who did not get caught can get these coins back.

=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' ===
All bosses that would later be fought in the battle ring have a chase attack:

* [[Petey Piranha]] chases Mario and Luigi south, spitting goop from his mouth. Mario and Luigi will have to grab onto [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]], who turns himself into a paper plane. Petey Piranha's paper counterpart sends his clones forward, with the Bros. jumping over them. Some of them are positioned close, so the Bros. will need to grab Paper Mario to glide over them.
* The [[Big Pokey]] and [[Tower Power Pokey]] can charge from the background. While the Big Pokey rolls his segments and head into the Bros., necessitating Paper Mario's airplane transformation to avoid getting trampled, the Tower Power Pokey folds himself and slides, needing to simply be jumped over.
* [[Wendy O. Koopa]] and [[Roy Koopa]] have an attack where Roy chases Mario and Luigi with a giant iron ball with his face on it. Wendy will throw fireballs at Paper Mario. Paper Mario must position himself so Wendy gets hit when Roy tosses the ball, after which she and Roy can be hit.
* The possessed [[Wiggler]] attacks by trying to run the Bros. over with a giant ball summoned by Paper [[Kamek]]. Paper Mario folds his body into the form of a boomerang, and must be thrown at the ball to destroy it.
* The [[Chargin' Chuck|Chargin' Chucks]] charge at the Bros. from the background, bowling their footballs or throwing them overhead. Paper Mario uses his airplane form, carrying the Bros. to help them dodge the footballs thrown along the ground, but has to avoid the overhead-thrown footballs himself.
* [[Larry Koopa]] and [[Ludwig von Koopa]] can have their [[Shy Guy]] minions carry a bomb, which the Koopalings hide behind. Paper Mario must be thrown at the Shy Guys in his boomerang form; defeating enough will cause the bomb to explode.
* [[Bowser Jr.]] and his paper counterpart can have their Shy Guy minions trap the Bros. into barrels, after which the two blast Mario, Luigi, and Paper Mario with their fireballs.
* [[King Boo]] gathers flames in his mouth, and Mario and Luigi must throw Paper Mario in boomerang form at Paper [[Boo|Boos]], then wait for King Boo's mouth to open and throw Paper Mario in. If they succeed, King Boo regurgitates his fireball and Paper Mario, the former of which explodes in King Boo's face to deal damage to him.
* [[Kamek]] and his paper counterpart can chase the Bros. as the former charges up a magic blast and the latter turns into a massive wall that serves as a shield. Mario and Luigi must throw Paper Mario's boomerang form at the wall to destroy segments, then hit Kamek himself to make him blow himself and Paper Kamek up with the magic fireball.
