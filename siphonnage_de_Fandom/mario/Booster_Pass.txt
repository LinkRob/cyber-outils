|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''|ruler=Unknown}}'''Booster Pass''' is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Appearance==
Booster Pass is mainly a rough and grassy area.

==History==
[[Mario]] and his friends head through Booster Pass fighting many enemies along the way. Eventually, they reach the end and they finally arrive at [[Booster Tower]].

In the [[Super Mario RPG|remake]], there is now a semi-hidden spot in the second area where an unseen character is selling entries for unread [[Thought Peek]] entries. This individual charges 3 [[Frog Coin]]s per entry, but allows Mario a freebie on his first visit.
