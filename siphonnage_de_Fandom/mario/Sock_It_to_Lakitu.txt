'''Sock It to Lakitu''' is a boss mini game in the game ''[[Mario Party 9]]''. In "Solo" mode, it appears as a mid-boss in [[Toad Road]].

== Gameplay ==
The players must defeat [[Lakitu]] with the cannons above by hitting the blocks. Lakitu throws [[Spiny|Spinies]] that can subtract the players' points. Players may earn one, two or three points if they shoot Lakitu with three [[Bullet Bill]]s. Hitting the slot with the Spiny, a Spiny will appear. Spinies will take away one point from the player if they come in contact. After losing half of his lives, Lakitu will enrage and start to throw a [[Big Spiny]], the player can lose two points if he/she comes in contact. Despite the player falling off the arena, the player will not lose points but will be brought back to the arena. The player who defeats Lakitu with the last bullet bill will earn a final attack bonus for extra points.

== Ending ==
Once the last Bullet Bill hits Lakitu, he is flung into the sky with his cloud, turning into a star in the distance, as he releases a bunch of Mini Stars in Party Mode and Solo Mode.
