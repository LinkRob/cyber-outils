'''Peach's Parasol''' is an umbrella that first appeared in the merchandise of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'', later officially appearing in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. It was eventually a gift [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] gave to her after he and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] found [[Yoshi (Illumination)|Yoshi]].
