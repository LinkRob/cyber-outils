|name=Big Hopslipper (Battle Card)}}'''Big Hopslipper''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Hopslipper is the strong version of [[Mario]]'s long [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a big green slipper with a spring attached to the bottom, with a green/lime checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 20/60 coins

Sell Price: 10/30 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Hopslipper is first seen in [[Mondo Woods]], but can also found in [[Cobalt Base]], [[Lighthouse Island]], [[Fortune Island]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
