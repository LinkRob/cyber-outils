Layout of the [[Lushgreen Sea]]'s [[Great Lighthouse Islands|Great Lighthouse Island]]. Note that area names are unofficial.

== Starting Area (Entrance) ==
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] land to the south of the entrance gate. There are some crates and [[Mystery Block|Mystery Blocks]] scattered around. The front gate is locked, which prevents entry. The east path curves north and leads to an underground cave. The west path leads to a small secluded area where the peddler appears.

== Sub-Level 1 ==

=== Spikely Path ===
This area has a path connecting to the entrance from the east. It starts heading west and then curves south. There are some [[Spikely|Spikelies]] encountered here. The southern terminus leads to the The Floors Have Fans area. The Stairs to the Basement lie just to the west, blocked by a wall.

=== The Floors Have Fans ===
When the Bros. enter this area, [[Connie]] will appear, explaining what the red spiked lightbulb creatures that haunt the area are: Spite Bulbs, a hostile variant of [[Sprite Bulb|Sprite Bulbs]].

There are a few fans on the floor as well as some Spikelies and [[Capnap|Capnaps]]. To get past, Mario and Luigi have to activate the fans, allowing their UFO Spin to cross large distances. They will eventually reach a switch, which they must hit to lower so Connie can make her way forward.

=== Stairs to the Basement ===
This area lies just to the west of the Spikely Path The stairs lead to the Great Lighthouse's basement.

== Sub-Level 2 ==

=== The Gobblick's Growl ===
When the Bros. enter this area, they hear loud growling noises coming from the east. A man named [[Dewey Fightem]] can be found here, explaining that his presence is due to his drive to research monsters. Past the gate lies the path to the [[Gobblick]]'s arena.

To bypass the gate, Mario and Luigi have to take plugs to the taps. Doing so opens the gate.

=== Pre-Boss Save Platform ===
Just before the area where the Gobblick lives lies a platform [[Save Block]] and a [[Recovery Block]].

=== The Gobblick's Lair ===
The Gobblick lives here, and is faced immediately after a cutscene when the Bros. enter the area. When it dies, the elevator leading up to the main part of the lighthouse becomes functional and a note from [[Arc]]'s father, Patriarc, can be found.

== Top Floor ==
When the gang reaches the top floor, [[Connie]] will activate the Great Lighthouse with her Wattanist powers. This opens the path to the [[Color-Full Sea]].
