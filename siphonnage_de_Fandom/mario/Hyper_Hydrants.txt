'''Hyper Hydrants''' is a 2 vs. 2 mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

== Gameplay ==
In this mini-game, the teams must try to put out the numerous of [[Podoboo]]s that appear. One teammate must pump the water while the other sprays the water with the hose. Teamwork is greatly needed here as one of the teammates must pump the water fast enough to have the other player spray easily. The team that puts out more Podoboos by the end of the time wins. A draw can occur if both teams put out the same number of Podoboos.

== Controls ==
=== One Teammate ===
*{{button|n64|a}} then {{button|n64|b}} (or {{button|n64|z}}) - Pump water

=== Other Teammate ===

*{{button|n64|a}} - Spray water
