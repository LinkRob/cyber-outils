'''Pi'illo Temple''' is a location that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It is a sacred area that dates back to ancient Pi'illo times and is located deep within [[Somnom Woods]].

== History ==
[[File:Pi'illodium awakened.png|thumb|left|Mario and the rest accidentally awaken [[Pi'illodium]] at Pi'illo Temple.]]

[[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Dreambert|Prince Dreambert]], and [[Starlow]] all head into Pi'illo Temple in order to place the [[Ultibed]] in the safe location. Prince Dreambert notices a strange object in the middle and has Mario hammer it. However, the object then awakens as [[Pi'illodium]], a defense weapon created by the anicent [[Pi'illo]] folk. Mario and Luigi fight and destroy the strange monster and they place the Ultibed down. Mario then heads into the [[Dream World|dream world]] in order to help contact [[The Zeekeeper]].
