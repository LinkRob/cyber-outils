'''Can the Eel Come Out to Play''' is the second mission of [[Jolly Roger Bay]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. Completing this mission unlocks [[Treasure in the the Ocean Cave|Treasure in the Ocean Cave.]]

== Layout ==
The ship from the [[Plunder in the Sunken Ship|previous mission]] will now have risen above the surface.

== Objective ==
The player needs to swim to where the ship is, and swim below it. There will be a hole deep below the surface, where [[Unagi the Eel]] is. It will poke its head out for a bit, before swimming out. The [[Power Star]] will move behind its tail, so the player needs to collect it quickly before it goes back in its hole, forcing the player to lure it out. If the player stays very close to the hole on either side, but not too close to where Unagi can bite the character, and when it swims out, collect the Power Star immediately.
