A '''Crusher''' is a obstacle featured in [[Toad's Factory]] in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

==Description==
Crushers are hydraulic machines found in a certain portion of Toad's Factory. They move forward on where the conveyor belts are, and crush down every several seconds. Crushers are similar to [[Thwomp]]s in the sense that they can crush whoever drives under them. If a racer drives into a Crusher while it is pressed down, their movement is hindered.

Crushers are shown to be able to create [[Item Box]]es; they appear to have a form of colorful liquid within them, and when a Crusher presses down onto a conveyor, an Item Box is formed.
