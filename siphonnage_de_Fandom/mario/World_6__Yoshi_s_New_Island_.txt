'''World 6''' is the final set of stages in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' and takes place at a fiery volcano. [[Kamek]], [[Baby Bowser]], and [[Bowser]] are the bosses of this world. 
==Levels==
*World 6-1: [[Chomp Rock Challenge]] &ndash; This level features [[Chomp Rock]]s.
*World 6-2: [[Bandit Valley]] &ndash; This level features [[Bandit]]s.
*World 6-3: [[Gargantua Blargg Attacks!]] &ndash; This level features [[Gargantua Blargg]]s.
*World 6-4: [[Spray Day Mayday]] &ndash; The sixth fort of the game.
*World 6-5: [[Hotfoot Hurdle]] &ndash; This level auto-scrolls.
*World 6-6: [[Pyro Guy Peak]] &ndash; This level features [[Pyro Guy]]s.
*World 6-7: [[Crumble Rock Rumble]] &ndash; This level features crumbling rocks.
*World 6-8: [[Bowser's Castle Break-In]] &ndash; The final castle of the game.
*World 6-Bowser: [[Make Way for King Bowser!]] &ndash; The final boss battle of the game.
*World 6-S: [[Leapin' Lava Meltdown]] &ndash; This special level is centered around [[! Switch]]es.
