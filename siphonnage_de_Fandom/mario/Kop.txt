A '''Kop''' is a large [[Kremling]] that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==Appearance==
As stated above, the Kops are large Kremlings that wear a white shirt with a red tie on it. They also wear blue pants with a black belt, white shoes, and a white cap on their heads.

==History ==
Kops only appear in the bonus stage game, [[Stealthy Snoop]]. They patrol around with their flashlights pointing in the direction they are looking at. A Kong must avoid being spotted by the Kops at all times or they will lose the minigame and must try again.
