'''Beanbean Castle''' is a location that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Reissue|remake]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==
Beanbean Castle is a large [[castle]] at the center of [[Beanbean Kingdom]] with similarities to [[Peach's Castle]]. It is where [[Queen Bean]], [[Lady Lima]], and [[Prince Peasley]] live as well as the place from which they rule the kingdom. It is possible that some other royalty and servants live at Beanbean Castle. The [[Beanbean Castle Sewer|Beanbean Castle Sewers]] is located under the castle, and [[Beanbean Castle Town]] is a town nearby that has many useful shops. The Mario Bros. go to the Beanbean Castle several times in between tasks to talk to Queen Bean and the others to gain information.

==History==
When [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] first enter Beanbean Castle, the town is destroyed and the front gate was blocked. The two [[Beanbean Troop|Beanbean troops]] almost refuse to let them enter until Luigi shows the [[Peasley's Rose]], so the guard acknowledges them as friends of Prince Peasley and lets them in. As Mario and Luigi enter, the ambassador [[Lady Lima]] appears and wants to know who they are. Even after Mario and Luigi explain, Lady Lima still does not believe them. She then knocks the Mario Bros. down into the basement and tells them if they could fix the [[Beanbean Castle Sewer|plumbing under the castle]], then she will believe that they are the true Mario Bros.

Once they fix the plumbing, Mario and Luigi come across the real Lady Lima. It is revealed that the other Lady Lima was actually [[Cackletta]], who had tricked Mario and Luigi to fixing the plumbing to actually deactivates the security on [[Beanstar]]. Mario and Luigi confront her, but Cackletta soon presents them with a powered-up Queen Bean and she flies away. Mario and Luigi defeat the Queen Bean and Lady Lima reveals that she must have ingested a Belly Blech Worm and only the [[Chuckola Cola|Chuckola Reserve]] could cure her. Mario and Luigi head out to find some.
