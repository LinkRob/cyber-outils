'''Attack Scarf''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When any user equips the Attack Scarf, the item protects him from instant death. The Attack Scarf can be obtained by [[Super Jump|super jumping]] thirty times in a row, then talking to [[Chow]]. The item is exclusive to [[Mario]].
