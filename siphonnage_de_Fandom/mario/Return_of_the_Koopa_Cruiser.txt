'''Return of the Koopa Cruiser''' is the seventh level of [[Joke's End]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 27.

==Layout==
The 1st battle contains 6 [[Broozer]]s and 2 [[Ice Snifit (Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga)|Ice Snifits]]. The enemy captain is a Broozer, and he knows [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 5 Broozer, 2 [[Ice Bro]]s, and 1 [[Blue Magikoopa|Magikoopa (Blue)]]. The enemy captain is the Magikoopa (Blue), and he knows Deny and [[Parry]].

The 3rd battle contains 7 Broozers and 1 [[Big Boo]]. The enemy captain is the Big Boo, and he knows [[Chance]].

The final battle contains 4 Broozers, 2 Ice Bros, 1 Ice Snifit, and [[Roy Koopa|Roy]]. The enemy captain is Roy, and he knows [[Rally]], Deny, and [[Shell Shock]].

After the final battle, the player recruits Roy (Lv.27).

*Magikoopa (Blue) (Lv. 27)
