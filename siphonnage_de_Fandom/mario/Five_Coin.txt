'''Five Coins''' are a recurring item in the {{franchise|Mario}}, mainly the {{series|Mario & Luigi}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==Description==
Five Coins are a larger variation of [[coin]]s worth five coins, as is directly stated in their name. Like other coins of incremented values, Five Coins are less common than regular coins.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
[[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can sometimes find a Five Coin from a [[? Block]] while out on the field.

==In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''==
[[File:Five Coin SPP sprite.png|frame|left]]
Five Coins are items which resemble coins, except they are notably less common. Upon buying a [[Courage Soda]], every enemy [[Princess Peach]] whacks with [[Perry]] gives out a Five Coin, but the number of coins dropped increases by 2.5 for every two enemies when whacked in rapid succession.
