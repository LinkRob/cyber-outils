A '''Sphinx Simulator''' is an object that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. 

==History==
Sphinx Simulators are machines that can be found in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]] in two different areas. These machines are encountered when [[Wario]] is trying to look for the Pharaoh's missing treasures. Whenever Wario encounters a Sphinx Simulator, he must use [[Sparky Wario]]'s powers in order to activate them. After doing so, Wario is then tasked to answer riddles in order to proceed. When the riddles are answered correctly, Wario will be able to to move on but if he fails, he will get dropped down a trap door.
