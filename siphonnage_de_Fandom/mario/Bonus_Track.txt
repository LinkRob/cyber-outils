'''Bonus Track''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Bonus Track plays an alternate version of the title screen theme, made to sound as if it were being played on an original [[Game Boy]].

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Bonus Track can be bought in Challenge Mode for 2,000 [[coin]]s.
