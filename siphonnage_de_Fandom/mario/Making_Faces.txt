'''Making Faces''' is a [[2 vs. 2 Minigame|2-vs-2]] [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The team positions the mouth onto the face of [[Mario]] or [[Bowser]], then the camera zooms out.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to position facial features to accurately look like either Mario or Bowser, pretermined by the game at random. Each facial feature is placed down before ten seconds. The facial features start with the eyes then the mouth. The minigame finishes and their accuracy of the character's face is rated by percentage. Whichever team has the highest percentage is the winner.

== Ending ==
The winning team does their victory animations.

*{{button|ns|stick}} – Alternate between buttons.
