'''''Donkey Kong Country Returns HD''''' is a [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Reissue]] of the [[Wii]] game ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' released worldwide on January 16, 2025.

First announced during the [[Nintendo Direct]] on June 18, 2024, ''Donkey Kong Country Returns HD'' is the second reissue of ''Donkey Kong Country Returns'', following the [[Nintendo 3DS]] port, ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]'', released in 2013. It retains every [[level]] from the original game and the extra [[Cloud (world)|Cloud]] levels included in the port.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kX3BkD5PVUM|title=Nintendo Direct 6.18.2024 – Nintendo Switch|author=[[Nintendo of America]]|work=YouTube|accessdate=June 18, 2024|date=June 18, 2024}}</ref>

It is the second reissue in the [[Donkey Kong Country (series)|''Donkey Kong Country'' series]] to be released for the Nintendo Switch, following the [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze (Nintendo Switch)|port]] of the [[Wii U]] title ''[[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze]]'', and the seventh reissue in the series overall.

Near three weeks after the game release, on February 5, 2025, a demo was later released on the [[Nintendo eShop]].<ref> @Wario64 (February 5, 2025). "[https://x.com/Wario64/status/1887310626914996701 ''Donkey Kong Country Returns HD'' demo available on US eShop]". X. Retrieved February 6, 2025.</ref>

One year after the game release, on January 20, 2026, Nintendo announced a free update. It includes [[Dixie Kong]] as playable character, the Turbo Mode, and enhancements for the [[Nintendo Switch 2]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://youtu.be/sl2YybDiluQ?si=Ab7iI-5rNWR8Wp7K|title=Donkey Kong Country Returns HD – Update Overview – Nintendo Switch & Nintendo Switch 2|author=[[Nintendo of America]]|work=YouTube|accessdate=January 20, 2025}}</ref>

==Gameplay==

===Changes to ''Donkey Kong Country Returns''===
New Mode has been replaced to Modern Mode, and the player can now hold up to nine items in one level instead of three.

Motion controls return from the [[Wii]] version, however they are optional, while the controls have been altered and the alternate movement control scheme has been removed.

==Trivia==
*This is the last ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related game to be released before the [[Nintendo Switch 2]] does.
*Despite this being only a port, this was the last game to feature Donkey Kong's design by [[Rare]]. He ends up getting a redesign in ''[[Mario Kart World]]'', which was initially seen in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.
*The game released on the same day that the Nintendo Switch 2 was officially revealed.
