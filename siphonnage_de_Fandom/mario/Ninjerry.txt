}}A '''Ninjerry''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Ninjerry can only be found sometimes in [[Sammer's Kingdom]] during fights and in a few rooms of the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. They are the second strongest of the "Ninja" enemies of this game. The Ninjerry attacks by tossing ninja stars at great speed. Attack the Ninjerry may also end up being a clone and will explode upon contact.
