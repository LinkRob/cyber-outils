The '''Hollijolli Mayor''' is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. He is the leader of [[Hollijolli Village]]. During the [[Shroob]] invasion of the village, the mayor attempts to hide from the aliens, but ends up getting stuck in his chimney. However, [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], who have traveled through time via [[time hole]]s to the past, help the mayor out of the chimney by jumping under him. Unfortunately, as soon as he is saved, the mayor of Hollijolli Village is abducted by Shroobs and presumably taken to the [[Toadwood Forest]] to extract the [[vim]] out of him and transfer it to the [[Vim Factory]]. His ultimate fate is unknown.

The Hollijolli Mayor has a white beard and wears a big, red coat. As such, the mayor resembles [[Santa Claus]], a fitting allusion for the Christmas-themed Hollijolli Village. Additionally, his quirk of getting stuck in his chimney was likely a reference to Santa Claus's association with going down chimneys to deliver gifts.
