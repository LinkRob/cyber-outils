'''World 5-1''' is the first [[level]] of [[World 5 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 5]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the seventeenth level overall in said game. It is the longest level in this game. This level introduces hanging pipes and the use of [[wind]]. In the original and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]'' versions, the level has a standard grassland palette, though the ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' version is snow themed. This level reappears in ''[[All Night Nippon: Super Mario Bros.]]'' as World B-1 but without the [[Warp Zone]] and the wind.

==Layout==
The player begins in front of a row of [[Buzzy Beetle]]s beneath a row of [[? Block]]s that contain [[Coin]]s. Across the gap after the first low hanging pipe with a [[Piranha Plant]], there are four more hanging pipes with Piranha Plants the player must avoid as they are jumping across the gap below. Next is a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] followed by a row of [[Koopa Troopa]]s and two [[Bill Blaster]]s. Through the next [[Warp Pipe]] is an underground area containing coins and the only power-up in the stage. After the pipes are a set of stairs, which wind will be blowing on when the player jumps about halfway up; there is also a hidden [[1-Up Mushroom]] in one of the [[Hidden Block]]s against the back of the stairs. The player must jump with the wind to cross the large gaps ahead and will end up in front of a Bill Blaster. Through both a hanging and a ground pipe with Piranha Plants are a row of enemies leading up to a large gap before a flight of stairs. Across the gap after the stairs are Koopa Paratroopas and rows of [[Brick Block]]s, with the top-right-most block containing a [[Beanstalk]] to [[Coin Heaven]], which will drop the player at a [[Warp Zone]] to [[World 6 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 6]]. If the player does not take that route, they will have to find two Hidden Blocks to use as platforms to reach the top of the ledge above. After the long hill is a pipe with a Piranha Plant followed by the [[Goal Pole]], though the player can also reach the Warp Zone to World 6 by jumping over the pole.
