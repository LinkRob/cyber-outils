'''Treasure buttons''' are a type of object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
Every course features eight buttons, each of a different color: red, yellow, chartreuse, green, cyan, blue, purple, and pink. [[Wario]] can activate a treasure button through various means, including [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]], [[punch]]ing, or even throwing an enemy at it. In doing so a [[treasure chest]] appears on a panel of the same color in the same course. Once Wario has collected the [[treasure]] within the chest, both the treasure button and its corresponding treasure chest turn gray.
