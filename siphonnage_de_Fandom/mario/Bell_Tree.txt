'''Bell Trees''' are an object introduced in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Description==
Bell Trees are a type of [[tree]], and climbing to the top of it releases an item, usually a [[Super Bell]], hence the name. A Bell Tree has a white trunk and a yellow and orange-striped crown of leaves in the shape of a Super Bell. Bell Trees vary in height, with some being short while others are very tall, even more in comparison to [[Mega Mario]].

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''===
[[File:Bell Tree SM3DW.png|thumb|left|x250px|A Bell Tree in ''Super Mario 3D World'']]
Bell Trees are found in various [[level]]s, primarily in [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 1]], but seldomly appear afterward. Like with regular trees, a Bell Tree can be climbed onto for the character to reach higher areas. A Super Bell is usually released when climbing to the top of a Bell Tree. When climbed on, Bell Trees emit a bell jingling sound, something that also occurs if touching it with the [[Wii U GamePad]] in the original ''Super Mario 3D World''. Like with many other objects, a Bell Tree can be destroyed if a character walks into it while in their Mega form, granted by a [[Mega Mushroom]].
{{br|left}}
===''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''===
Bell Trees are featured as a course element in the ''Super Mario 3D World'' style. Similarly to [[Warp Pipe]]s, the player can adjust the height of a Bell Tree. It is possible to place an item inside of a Bell Tree, and if the player's character climbs to the top of the Bell Tree, the item pops out.

==In ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' ([[Wii U]])==
[[File:Super Bell Hill CTTT.png|thumb|left|200px|Bell Trees, as seen in the distant background of Super Bell Hill in ''Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker'']]
Bell Trees appear in [[Super Bell Hill]] of the [[Bonus Episode]]'s chapter, Expedition to the Sprixie Kingdom.
