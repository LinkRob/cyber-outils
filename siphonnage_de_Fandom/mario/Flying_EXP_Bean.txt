'''Flying EXP Beans''' are an obtainable item in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Locations==
Flying EXP Beans can be gained by completing levels, or by dismissing troops. Better Flying EXP Beans are gained in harder levels, or by dismissing troops with a higher level.

*Flying EXP Bean S can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 11 or higher, or by dismissing Flying troops that are level 1-13.
*Flying EXP Bean M can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 16 or higher, or by dismissing Flying troops that are level 14-24.
*Flying EXP Bean L can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 25 or higher, or by dismissing Flying troops that are level 25-29.
*Flying EXP Bean DX can be gained in levels where the enemy squad is level 30 or higher, or by dismissing Flying troops that are level 30 or higher.

==Types==
===Flying EXP Bean S===
The Flying EXP Bean S gives one recruited Flying troop 10 EXP, or it can be used on a Melee or Ranged troop to give them 5 EXP. 

===Flying EXP Bean M===
The Flying EXP Bean M gives one recruited Flying troop 100 EXP, or it can be used on a Melee or Ranged troop to give them 50 EXP.

===Flying EXP Bean L===
The Flying EXP Bean L gives one recruited Flying troop 1000 EXP, or it can be used on a Melee or Ranged troop to give them 500 EXP.

===Flying EXP Bean DX===
The Flying EXP Bean DX gives one recruited Flying troop 3000 EXP, or it can be used on a Melee or Ranged troop to give them 1500 EXP.

==Flavor text==
*The Flying EXP Bean S in-game description is: "A Bean that gives 10 EXP to Flying troopers. Effect is halved if eaten by other unit types."
*The Flying EXP Bean M in-game description is: "A Bean that gives 100 EXP to Flying troopers. Effect is halved if eaten by other unit types."

*The Flying EXP Bean DX in-game description is: "A Bean that gives 3000 EXP to Flying troopers. Effect is halved if eaten by other unit types."
