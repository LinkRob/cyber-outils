'''Wario's Woods''', formerly named the '''Peaceful Woods''', is the main and titular setting of ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for both the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]]. All of the battles in VS COM mode take place here.
