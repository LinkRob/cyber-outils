'''Coin Swap Hex''' is a [[Hex]] featured in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Description==
Like the other Hexes, a Coin Swap Hex can be obtained as a random pick-up from a [[Hex Area]]. A character can place a Coin Swap Hex on a [[Blue Space]] or a [[Red Space]] up to five [[space]]s from where they are.

If an opponent lands on a Coin Swap Hex, all of their [[Coin]]s are traded with whoever threw it. If the character who threw the Coin Swap Hex or their teammate lands on the space, the effect of the hex is canceled out, although they receive five [[Coin]]s.
