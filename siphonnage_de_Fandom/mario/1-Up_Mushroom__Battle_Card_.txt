|name=1-Up Mushroom (Battle Card)}}'''1-Up Mushroom''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The 1-Up Mushroom restores [[Mario]]'s paint upon use. It is a green and white speckled mushroom with a green/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 300 coins

Sell Price: 150 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The 1-Up Mushroom is first seen in [[Indigo Underground]], but can also found in [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Marmalade Valley]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Mustard Café]], [[Toad Trainworks]], [[Redpepper Crater]], [[Mossrock Theater]], [[The Emerald Circus]], and [[Black Bowser's Castle]].
