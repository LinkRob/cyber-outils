'''Dorguy the First''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''Super Paper Mario'' ==
Dorguy the First is one of three guardian doors for [[Queen Jaydes]] on [[Underwhere Road]]. When the heroes make it to Dorguy the First, they must answer his questions in order to pass.

==Trivia==
*Dorguy the First's and the other Dorguy's role is similar to the [[Guard Door]]s from ''[[Paper Mario (game)|Paper Mario]]''. Their faces also resemble those of the [[Black Chest Demons]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
