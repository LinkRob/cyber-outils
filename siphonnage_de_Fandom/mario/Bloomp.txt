'''Bloomps''' are pufferfish-like enemies in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Bloomp is an pufferfish-like creature with an inflatable body. [[Bullet Bill]] cannons are used to inflate Bloomps. There are both Green and Purple Bloomps; purple Bloomps travel faster than green ones.

Bloomps may also contain [[Coin|coins]] when jumped on.

===Behaviour===
Bloomps move in one direction through the air. Their bodies can be used as platforms. When jumped on, the Bloomp shrinks slightly. They move by flying straight, and when the player steps on them, they bounce. If touched from the side, they won't take any damage, but they'll bounce back. If the player steps on them, they'll lose air and deflate. They are defeated by being hit from below.

These Bloomps can grow huge under a wonder effect. In [[Bloomps of the Desert Skies]] the player's character will become alike to a balloon.{{SMBWNav}}
