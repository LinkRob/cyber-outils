'''Ingot Isle''' is a location that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. It is an isle that sits atop the [[Underground World]], which serves as the main setting of the game. The world below is ruled by beings known as the [[Giant Elders]]. The Void Company tunnels through the planet at the isle to reach the [[Banandium Root]] at the [[Planet Core]], where one's wish can be granted.

==Description==
Ingot Isle appears to be the only surface location with the newly discovered Banadium Gems. It hosts a large mining town digging up gold and the Gems. Once the isle was plunged underground it resembled a disorganized construction zone, with girders and signs strewn about.

Near the top of the underground form of the isle, there's an enormous pit leading to the Underground World the rest of the game takes place in.

==History==
Donkey Kong has visited Ingot Isle in search of the recently discovered Banandium Gems. However, a villainous mining company known as [[VoidCo]] slammed a giant meteor through Ingot Isle to tunnel deep into the planet. The leader of VoidCo, [[Void Kong]], plans to drill into the earth to reach the Planet Core and make a wish for infinite gold.
