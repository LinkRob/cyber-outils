'''Color Me Happy''' is a minigame that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player must use the touchscreen in order to color in a certain image on this screen. Color Me Happy is a rather special minigame as it is only playable from green treasure chests and contain [[Guise Gem]]s, [[Mastery Gem]]s, and [[Vita Mighty|Vita Mighties]]. After the player colors in the certain image, they can press the "OK" button to see if they have at least made it close to the percentage needed to complete the minigame and acquire the item. If they player feels like they have messed up in areas, they can use the eraser icon to fix their mistakes.
