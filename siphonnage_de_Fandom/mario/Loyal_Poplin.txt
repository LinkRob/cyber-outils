The '''Loyal Poplin''' is a character from [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder|''Super Mario Bros. Wonder'']] who lives near the [[Fungi Mines]].

== Profile ==

=== Personality ===
The Loyal Poplin is loyal to the Poplin mine exploration crew, and when he gets the news of them missing, he is eager to request help in finding them. At first, he chides [[Mario (character)|Mario]] for being so reckless in going to the mines, but he comes to admire Mario's bravery. His Japanese and Chinese "names" paint him as someone who cares for his friends. His Spanish "name" indicates that he is brave, but French and Dutch give him a "worried" disposition. According to his "name" in the German dub, the Loyal Poplin is generally friendly.

=== Appearance ===
The Loyal Poplin resembles a standard poplin, but with a green bud, hijab, and shoes. He carries a pickaxe with a grey head. His icon shoes this same pickaxe next to him.

== History ==
The Loyal Poplin makes his debut when [[Mario (character)|Mario]] makes his way to the fifth world of the game, the [[Fungi Mines]]. When Mario enters the Loyal Poplin's help, he finds a note instead of the Poplin himself. After Mario gets the [[Wonder Seed]], the Loyal Poplin will appear on the world map, requesting aid to find his missing friends, who travelled deep within the mines to obtain a [[Royal Seed]] for [[Prince Florian]]. In exchange for ten [[Flower Coin|Flower Coins]], he can make a bridge to the next area, allowing access to the first course, [[Upshroom Downshroom]], and also provides the Sensor Badge, which helps with finding certain items nearby such as [[Wonder Flower|Wonder Flowers]] and big Flower Coins.

== Nomenclature ==
In the Japanese and Chinese dubs, the Loyal Poplin is unnamed, but is said to care for his friends in the former and value them in the latter. Spanish refers to him as a "brave Poplin" (''Poplin Valiente''). In Portuguese and Russian, the Loyal Poplin is called "Loyal Poplin" (''Poplím Leal'' and ''Vernyy Poplin'' (written Верный поплин). Italian emphasises his role as a miner with ''Poplìn Minatore'' ("Miner Poplin"). In French and Dutch, he is referred to as a "worried Poplin" (''Poplin inquiet'' and ''Bezorgde Poplin''). He is called "friendly Poplin" in German (''Freundlicher Poplin'') and "affectionated Poplin" in Korean (''Jeong Maneun Popeullin''; written 정 많은 포플린).
