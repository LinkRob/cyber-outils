'''Zappy Path''' is a minigame that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the player must use the touchscreen to guide the blue dot from the start and into the end of the goal without touching the walls within the time limit. If the player touches the walls three times or the time goes up, then they will fail this minigame. Zappy Path can have either a drawing of [[Wario]]'s hat or his shoe.
