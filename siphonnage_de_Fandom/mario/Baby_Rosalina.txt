'''Baby Rosalina''' is a character in the [[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart series]]. She makes an appearance in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]],'' [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] and [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']].



== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
With an outfit based on [[Rosalina]]'s design, Baby Rosalina has fair skin, platinum blonde hair, rosy cheeks, and bright blue eyes with four eyelashes (unlike her adult counterpart, which has six). Her hair is similar to the way Rosalina droops a large bang over her right eye as an adult. Her dress appears to be turquoise with a cyan skirt section. Her lower potion consists of a turquoise ruffled diaper cover over white tights and sapphire blue Mary Jane shoes with copper brown soles. Just like Rosalina, she wears a silver crown. She is equipped with a yellow pacifier.

Baby Rosalina's appearance contradicts [[Rosalina|her adult form]]'s backstory. Despite [[Rosalina's Storybook]] showing Rosalina with red or strawberry blonde hair as a child, Baby Rosalina has the same color hair as her adult incarnation.

Like [[Baby Daisy]], [[Pink Gold Peach]] and [[Toadette]], Baby Rosalina first appears in a ''[[Mario Kart]]'' game. She is a light-class racer but has the stats of feather racers, including baby characters and [[Lemmy Koopa]]. Her only rival is [[Toadette]].

===Alternate forms===
In ''Mario Kart Tour'', Baby Rosalina is introduced in an alternate form for the first time as a detective, which was introduced during the Baby Rosalina Tour on March 10, 2020. Her detective form has a brown hat replacing her crown and a brown scarf attached to her neck. In addition, she holds a magnifying glass in the shape of a star.

In ''Dr. Mario World'', Baby Rosalina is equipped with a standard white lab coat overlapping her standard appearance, but her Mary Jane flatwear is colored teal instead of sapphire blue.

=== Personality and traits ===
Baby Rosalina is depicted quite like her adult counterpart. Like Rosalina, she has a more mysterious air about her. She is quieter than most of the other babies and normally acts calm, even when excited. Baby Rosalina's emblem is a [[Grand Star]] inside teal bonnet frills.

== Appearances ==
===''Mario Kart series''===
==== ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''/''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ====
Baby Rosalina is a newcoming racer in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. She reappeared in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' as a playable racer. The player must win any Grand Prix cup to unlock Baby Rosalina. However, she's a random unlockable character.

====''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''====
Baby Rosalina reappears in Mario Kart Tour as a Common character, being very easy to unlock. Her special item is the Boomerang Flower. There is also a detective version of her and she had an own tour, the [[Baby Rosalina Tour]].
