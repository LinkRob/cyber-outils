The '''Cave of Wickedness''' is a location featured in both the [[Game Boy]] and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] versions of ''[[Tetris Attack]]''. It is a subterranean area featured in Vs. Mode, and the location where [[Hookbill the Koopa]], [[Naval Piranha]], [[Kamek]], and [[Bowser]] are fought.
