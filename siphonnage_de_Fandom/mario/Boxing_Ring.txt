'''Boxing Ring''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS and Wii U ]]'' and returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. It is from the ''Punch-Out!!'' universe.

==Stage design==
Boxing Ring, as the name states, is a large boxing ring around a large crowd of people. Because of this, Boxing Ring few stages in the series to not have any bottomless pits as KOs are strictly done on the side or in the sky.

==Origin==
Boxing Ring is based off of main fighting stages of the ''Punch-Out!!'' series. [[Little Mac]] will always have to fight his opponents in a boxing ring to ensure his victory.
