'''Chombones''' are a skeletal bug-like species which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. A few are encountered by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] within the [[Pump Works]] of [[Bowser's body]].
