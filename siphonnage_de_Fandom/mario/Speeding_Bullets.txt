'''Speeding Bullets''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' and ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]''.

==Gameplay==
*Pilot Bullet Bill to the finish line as fast as you can. Pass over dash zones to boost your speed. Hitting obstacles will lower your speed.
*To defeat COM (Very Hard) and COM (Master) in this minigame make sure you pass over every dash zone and not hitting any obstacles of any kind.

==Controls==
Hold {{System Buttons|Wii|Wiimote}} sideways.

* {{System Buttons|Wii|Wiimote}} (Tilt left) - Turn left

* {{System Buttons|Wii|Wiimote}} (Tilt right) - Turn right
