'''Dark Trashures''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. They are encountered in [[Princess Peach's Castle]] after [[Fawful]] fawfulized it. Like their more colorful counterparts, they will chuck [[cannonball]]s, golden [[hammer]]s, [[coin]]s, and [[Mushroom]]s that heal 100 HP. After a considerable amount of damage done, they will open up, revealing their coins. Unlike the regular [[Trashure]]s, who release (10 coins) at max, these guys can release (100 coins). In the original game, while [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] cannot normally fight Dark Trashures, it is possible for them to battle one with cheats enabled. Most of its attacks function normally, with the objects being thrown at either of the Mario Bros., and they can be dodged by jumping; however, the cannonball is thrown at such a rapid speed that it is very difficult to predict and dodge beforehand.
