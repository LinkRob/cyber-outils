'''P Flying Squirrel Mario''' is a power-up in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''.

==Description==
[[Mario]] or another character can take on their P Flying Squirrel form by using a [[P-Acorn]]. The form gives all of the abilities of [[Flying Squirrel Mario]], but with the added benefit of being able to glide for as long as desired and perform Flying Squirrel Jumps continuously without landing. The [[P-Meter]] can be heard beeping while a character glides in this form. A character loses their P Flying Squirrel form if they either take damage, lose a life, or completes the [[level]]; in the latter case, the character changes back into their standard Flying Squirrel form.

The power-up is based on [[Raccoon Mario]] or [[Tanooki Mario]] being able to fly indefinitely from the effects of the [[P-Wing]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', as it similarly maxes out the P-Meter.
