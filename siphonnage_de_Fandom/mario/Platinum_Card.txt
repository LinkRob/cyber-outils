The '''Platinum Card''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Platinum Card allows [[Mario]] to play the Boat Mode [[Minigame]]. It also adds more prizes to the list ([[Money Money]], [[HP Plus P]], and [[Gold Bar x3]]) in the [[Pianta Parlor]]. Mario receives it when he completes [[Toodles]]' [[Trouble Center|trouble]] titled "I must have that book."
