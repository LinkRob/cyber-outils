The '''Frozen Pit''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Description==
The Frozen Pit is a deep frozen cavity in the depths of the [[Secret Mine]]. It has three antechambers, each adorned with crystals and covered in snow, although the last antechamber has less snow.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
The Frozen Pit is the last room accessed by [[Luigi]] in the Secret Mine, exclusively during the boss mission [[Chilly Ride]]. After the [[Shrewd Possessor]] takes control of the frozen ground in the [[Workshop (Secret Mine)|Workshop]] and sinks into the ground, prompting Luigi to follow it into the Frozen Pit and initiating the boss fight against the Shrewd Possessor.
[[File:Frozen Pit last antechamber.png|thumb|left|200px|Luigi in the last antechamber of the Frozen Pit]]
Each time Luigi destroys the ice armor possessed by the Possessor, the two of them fall into one of the antechambers. After Luigi vacuums a layer of the Shrewd Professor into his [[Poltergust 5000]], the Shrewd Professor possesses another wall of ice and continues further down the Frozen Pit. The Frozen Pit's third and final antechamber is where Luigi captures the Shrewd Professor.

Because of its depth, the Frozen Pit has no visible map, and Luigi's [[Dual Scream]] has low reception while taking calls from [[Professor E. Gadd]].
