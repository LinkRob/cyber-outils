A '''Battle Salmon''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Profile==
Battle Salmon are are thin, blue, mechanical fish.

===Behavior===
According to the in-game description, they move with the speed of 10,000 winds. A Battle Salmon attacks [[Wario]] by crashing into him and then self-destructing on him.

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Battle Salmon are found in an underwater section of the [[Smithsnorian Museum]] and certain areas of [[Sweatmore Peak]]. Battle Salmon can only be defeated using [[Captain Wario (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Captain Wario]]'s missiles. Battle Salmon also have similar counterparts named [[Snackeral]]s.
