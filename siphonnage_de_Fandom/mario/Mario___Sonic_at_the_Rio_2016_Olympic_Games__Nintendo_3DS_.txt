'''''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games''''' is a game for the [[Nintendo 3DS]], developed by [[Sega]] and produced by [[Nintendo]]. It is the fourth handheld installment of the {{series|Mario & Sonic}}, following after ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' for the same console. The game has fifty new sports and twenty characters, none of which are newcomers to the series. The game takes place in [[Rio de Janeiro]], [[Brazil]], home of the [[wikipedia:2016 Summer Olympics|Rio 2016 Olympics]].

This is the first handheld ''Mario & Sonic'' game to release before its [[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games (Wii U)|home console counterpart]], which released a few months later for the [[Wii U]].

''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games'' features an exclusive adventure mode titled [[Road to Rio]], following in a somewhat similar vein to [[Adventure Tours]] from ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for [[Nintendo DS]].

==Trivia==
*This game has a handheld version after ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'' lacked one.
*This is the first ''Mario & Sonic'' game to not have [[Dream Event]]s, although these would return in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''.
**A similar role is fulfilled by the duel events, however, these take place in locations in Rio rather than locations from the ''Super Mario'' and ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' franchises.
*This is the last ''Mario & Sonic'' game to have Knuckles voiced by Travis Willingham as he is voiced by Dave B. Mitchell in the ''Mario & Sonic'' series starting with ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]'' as his voice actor was replaced in ''[[w:c:sonic:Team Sonic Racing|Team Sonic Racing]]''.<ref>https://www.behindthevoiceactors.com/characters/Sonic-the-Hedgehog/Knuckles-the-Echidna/</ref>
