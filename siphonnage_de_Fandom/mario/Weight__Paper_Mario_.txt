The '''Weight''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Weight can be found in a [[treasure chest]] guarded by a [[Boo]] in [[Boo's Mansion]]. The Boo will only leave the chest after [[Mario]] gets the [[Record (Paper Mario)|Record]] from a different room and starts to play it on the nearby phonograph causing the Boo to dance and be distracted. The Weight can then be used on the chandelier in the main room after Mario has bounced off the couch. This will keep the bookshelf from blocking the door that leads down into the basement allowing Mario to proceed on forward. The Weight remains in place on the chandelier for the remainder of the game.
