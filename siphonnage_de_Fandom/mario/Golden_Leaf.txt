'''Golden Leaves''' are one of the items featured in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''==
[[File:GoldenLeaf_PM2.png|thumb|left]]
Golden Leaves debuted as items used in several [[recipe]]s. In this game, they are acquired by [[hammer]]ing a tree in the upper area outside the [[Creepy Steeple]]. To get to this tree, there is a secret pass [[Mario]] can take if he turns into Paper Mode and fits through a small patch of grass.

In this game, the Golden Leaf restores 10 [[Flower Point|FP]].

==In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''==
The effect of Golden Leaves were changed to restore one [[Heart Point|HP]] and cure any character inflicted with the [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] status effect. Golden Leaves are also quite rare, as they are dropped by [[Amazy Dayzee]]s. If a Golden Leaf is cooked, it can be turned into [[Gold Bar]]s, and when two are cooked, they turn into [[Gold Bar x3]], which can be sold for a lot of coins.
