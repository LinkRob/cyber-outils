'''Arty Wario''' is a form of [[Wario]] that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. This form can be used after Wario gets the Arty [[Guise Gem]] from the [[S.S. Caviar]]. To use Arty Wario, the player must draw a square onto Wario.

== Description ==
=== Physical appearance ===
Wario in his Arty Wario disguise wears a red beret with glasses and an apron. He is also holding a paint brush.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Arty Wario can paint items on his easel and create blocks acting as platforms. Blocks can be used to defeat enemies and press switches from a distance. By drawing a square with a cross inside, Arty Wario can create [[Warp door (Wario: Master of Disguise)|warp doors]] that allow him to warp back to the last hat switch making it useful to backtrack to save. After getting the [[Mastery Gem]], Arty Wario can create up to three blocks and create hearts allowing him to restore his health.

However, Arty Wario cannot move in this form. This includes running and jumping. It also takes time to create an object, leaving him vulnerable to enemy attacks. If the player fails to correctly draw an object, Arty Wario will create a movable fecal matter that will run away; although, it can still damage most enemies.
