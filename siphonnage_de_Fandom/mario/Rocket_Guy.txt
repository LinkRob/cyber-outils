'''Rocket Guys''' are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] which first appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' and return in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''. They are essentially regular Shy Guys that hold on to a [[rocket]] while traveling around in outer space.

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
[[File:Rocket Guy YIDS.png|frame|left]]
Rocket Guys first appear in [[The Fort of Moltz the Very Goonie]], both during the rocket ship segment and in the boss fight against [[Moltz the Very Goonie]]. Rocket Guys only appear in one other level, [[At Last, Bowser's Castle!]]. During the rocket segments, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] rides a rocket, like Rocket Guys, which are invincible during these segments. If the Yoshi's rocket crashes into a Rocket Guy's, the Yoshi instantly loses a life.

The only opportunity that the Yoshis have to defeat a Rocket Guy is during the boss fight against Moltz the Very Goonie, who is fought by [[Yellow Yoshi]] in particular. Here, whenever Moltz the Very Goonie gets hit, Rocket Guys begin falling from the sky. When this occurs, Yellow Yoshi can use his tongue to eat a Rocket Guy and convert it into an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]. In doing so, eight Rocket Guys are added to the [[Island Museum]], where they appear under the airborne enemy exhibit.

==In ''Yoshi's Crafted World''==
Rocket Guys appear in two boss levels, [[The Great King Bowser]] and [[Kamek Kerfuffle]]. Unlike in ''Yoshi's Island DS'', if a Yoshi eats a Rocket Guy, its rocket also disappears rather than continue traveling on without the Rocket Guy.
