'''#4 Ludwig's Castle''' is the last level of [[Twin Bridges]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. This castle is owned by [[Ludwig von Koopa]]. 

==Layout==
The player goes inside the castle and starts in a narrow room, they have to jump down and either dodge the [[Bony Beetle]] or destroy it if the player has a cape feather. They then have to go through lots of [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s without being hit. After that the player has two options, they can either jump over the secret ceiling, enter the pipe so they can play the [[Bonus game (Super Mario World)|bonus game]] and then start in the spike room. Or they can simply enter through the door and grab a [[Super Mushroom]] (if they want to) that is next to the door. They will then start in the spike room and the player will have to run and avoid the [[Podoboo]]s until they get to the ON and OFF Switch. The player must hit it once and once again run left. They will encounter several Lava Bubbles so they will need to avoid them and then there is a lot of space between the door and the player so the player must run before the spikes on the wall crush down on them. If they succeed, they will have to enter the door. Then they will to jump up and grab onto a net. If they go up and right into the first gap, they can get a powerup. But if they keep going up and go left the will find the boss door. If they go in it, then the player will need to fight [[Ludwig von Koopa]]. The player first has to go to the end of the room, where Ludwig is, to start the fight. Ludwig will breathe fire three times and will go into his shell if the player doesn't stomp on him. He will then jump and when he touches the ground he starts breathing fire again. There are two ways to defeat him. You can either jump on him three times, or you can throw two fireballs at him, with a [[Fire Flower]], and stomp on him once. Both will be defeat him. Then Mario rescues one of Yoshi's friends, destroys Ludwig's castle and then moves on to the next world, [[Forest of Illusion]].

== Ending Message ==
Mario rescues a Yoshi egg and pressed the bomb detonation switch. But instead of exploding, it launched the castle like a rocket and crashed into a hill off in the distance.

''Ludwig von Koopa's days of composing Koopa symphonies in castle #4 are over. The Forest of Illusion lies ahead. Mario must use his brain to solve the puzzle of this perplexing forest.''
