'''Koopa Shepherdesses''' are female [[Koopa Troopa]]s who appear in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==In ''Mario Party 7''==
[[File:Koopa Shepherdess MP7 speech icon.png|thumb|left|Speech icon]]
Koopa Shepherdesses are encountered at [[Windmillville]] where, aside from watching over their [[sheep]], they run the [[Orb Shop]]s and host some other events.

In the [[Duty-Free Shop]], a Koopa Shepherdess [[trophy]] can be bought for 500 [[Cruise Mileage point]]s.

== Physical description ==
Koopa Shepherdesses wear traditional women's clothing and have a pink flower on the left and right side of their head each.
