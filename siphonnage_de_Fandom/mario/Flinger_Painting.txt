'''Flinger Painting''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Opening==
The camera focuses on a canvas as it zooms out, then the characters enter the area.

==Gameplay==
The characters have shoot paintballs at the canvas, which they have to try and cover as much of with their color as possible. To shoot a paintball, the player must aim the [[Wii Remote]] at the screen and either press or hold {{button|wii|A}}. If the button is held, the player's character can charge a shot that covers a larger area of the canvas with paint. At the end, once the 15-second [[time limit]] is up, the character whose color covers most of the canvas is the winner.

In [[Time Attack (Mario Party 9)|Time Attack]], the player's character has to cover at least 90% of the canvas. There is also a bonus objective in which they must do ten shots or fewer.

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''Launch paint blobs at the canvas. Cover the most space with your color to win!''
*'''Controls''': ''Point the Wii Remote at the area you want to hit with your paint. Press {{button|wii|A}} to shoot. Hold {{button|wii|A}} to charge up a large paint shot. Release {{button|wii|A}} after charging to fire.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on {{w|Fingerpaint|finger painting}}, a type of art in which one paints using their finger.
