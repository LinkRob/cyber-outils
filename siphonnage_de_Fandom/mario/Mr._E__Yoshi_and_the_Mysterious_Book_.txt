'''Mister Encyclopedia''', who goes by the shortened name '''Mr. E''', is a character appearing in ''[[Yoshi and the Mysterious Book]]''.

== Appearance ==
He is a large encyclopedia with eyes and a white mustache on his cover. He contains information on many unusual creatures, although he is unable to read his own pages. He falls from the sky onto an island that has Yoshis, and in the process forgot the data within him about the creatures. Yoshi jumps into Mr. E's pages to help Mr. E by learning about the creatures.<sup>[2]</sup> It appears that the game's levels occur within Mr. E's pages.
