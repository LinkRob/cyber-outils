The '''Hammer Bro. Capsule''' is a [[capsule]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' A similar item called the [[Hammer Bro Orb]] also appears in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
Whenever a character lands on a [[space]] containing a Hammer Bro. Capsule, a [[Hammer Bro]] arrives on a platform and throws two [[hammer]]s at them, making them lose ten [[coin]]s. In Story Mode, Hammer Bro steals five coins instead. The player can also use the Hammer Bro. Capsule on themselves, costing five coins, but still causing Hammer Bro. to take away their coins. Therefore, the total cost the player must pay is fifteen coins in Party Mode or ten coins in Story Mode.

There are a few strategic instances when a character can use a Hammer Bro. Capsule, such as they anticipate exchanging coins from a [[Goomba Orb|Goomba Capsule]]. Alternatively, if the charactrer is low on coins, they can self-activate the Hammer Bro. Orb effect and land on a [[Bowser Space]] to receive 10 coins back.
