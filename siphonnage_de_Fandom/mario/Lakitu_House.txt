The '''Lakitu House''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
Lakitu House is a simple house located in the [[Seaside Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. It has a blue-and-green-background, a television, and an assortment of toys. It is where [[Lakitu (character)|Lakitu]] lives, as the name suggests.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
At Lakitu House, Lakitu is worried because he cannot remember a certain line from [[Toad Force V]], but agreeing to help him initiates the [[What's That Line?]] quest. When it starts, Lakitu tells the player's character to leave in search of a Toad Force V fanatic who lives in [[Mushroom Condo]]. When the player's character learns the line, they can return to Lakitu's House and tell it to Lakitu, who thanks them by giving them the [[Bait 'n Wait]] [[Gaddget]].
