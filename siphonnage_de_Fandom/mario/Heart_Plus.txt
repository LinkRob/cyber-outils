'''Heart Plus''' is one of the accessories featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. It appears as a ring with a heart attached to it. By using Heart Plus, Mario can increase his maximum [[Heart Point|HP]] for a battle. The weakest type of Heart Plus has a bronze border surrounding the heart, but there are stronger variations instead bearing a silver or a gold border.
